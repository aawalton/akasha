Recompute a day's `wisdomWords` and `intelligenceWords` from the git history and write them onto the daily-tracking page. Both counts are rolled up for one ESO day, and each is reported beside the outcome its rollup reached; `--json` answers an envelope carrying the commit shas each count was drawn from as well. Defaults to today's ESO day; pass `--date` for a past day. A `--date` that is not `YYYY-MM-DD`, and a day earlier than the counts begin, both exit 1.
