Recompute each Health persona's cumulative total (the sum of HER OWN persona-day pages' pillars AND her points source) and high-water write it onto her persona page. Forward-only by default: the same source-agnostic high-water guard the commit-points pass uses skips a non-increasing persona write, so re-running is an idempotent no-op once the materialized total is current. Use this after a LIVE health-economy rate change (a property-definition expression edit — pure data, no commit) to refresh the materialized cumulative without waiting for an unrelated daily-tracking commit to fire the CI pass. Pass `--force` to bypass the guard for a downward recalibration (a currency re-denomination that lowers the lifetime total below the stored high-water mark). A value's daily stoplight reads its personas' persona-day pages directly, so there is no value-total rollup here. THE POPULATION IS EVERY PERSONA WHOSE VALUE IS HEALTH EXCEPT THOSE WHOSE POINTS_SOURCE IS `unavailable` — a points source declaring that nothing computes her, whose rows sum to whatever strays landed on them, so writing that total would state an instrument's silence as a measured fact; her exclusion is also what lets a cleared total stay cleared, since an absent total reads to the high-water guard as a bootstrap state rather than a resting one. Pillar ownership does NOT narrow the population: a persona earning through her points source owns no stored Health pillar either, and this pass is the only thing that turns her per-day figures into a lifetime total. `--slug` scopes the write to the personas you name and `--dry-run` reports what each write would be without making it; reach for both before `--force`, since an unscoped forced run re-derives every Health persona at once, including ones another seat is working on.
