"Did you get a message just now?" You turn to her — she is just inside the shut door, where you drew her. "I just got a... a quest. To close the distance between us."

She looks where you're looking — you watch her do it — to the patch of air a foot off your face where the letters hang for you. Her eyes track across it and catch on nothing.

"I got no message." You watch her face wake right up — and she does not step back. "So it talks to you, and not to me," she says, quiet, working it as she goes. "And the very first thing it wants —"
