"I guess I need to explore the open door, then." You are already turning, drawing her gently by the hand toward it. "Would you come with me?"

She comes. That is the answer, ahead of any more words: she moves with it, her weight going easy, matching your step down the short soft-lit stretch of hall.

"The open door it is." A beat, two, her hand loose and willing in yours. "I did say the adventure was the other way. I notice you're taking me the interesting direction regardless."

The door stands open on the room that was waiting for you, and you cross into it — and the strangeness of the place lands on you sideways: it is furnished like somewhere you already live. The bed is made, and made to your taste. The light sits where you would have set it. A chair stands angled to a reading-lamp, put exactly where your hand would reach for it — the placement of someone who had watched you read a hundred evenings, except no one has, and there were no hundred evenings, and you have never once stood in this room.

Behind you, without a sound, the door eases itself shut on the hall.
