Astra lies back and pulls you down over her. She guides you in with one hand, the other at the small of your back drawing you the rest of the way, and then you're deep in her — and she makes a sound you've never heard her make: low, undone.

Your rhythm breaks and comes back rougher than you mean it, all the patience you'd been running on gone out of it, and you press your mouth to her throat to keep quiet — and don't.

Nova's come back to herself enough to push up on one arm and watch the two of you with open delight — and then she can't just watch. She tucks herself against Astra's side, gets a hand into her hair, and sets her mouth to Astra's ear to tell her, in detail, exactly how good she looks taking you.

Astra goes first. You feel it happen — the clench, the shake, your name this time where the instruction used to be — and it takes you with her, the ache finally paying out, and you're gone too, forehead down on her collarbone, wrung right out. And it's Nova who finds words first, soft and wrecked and pleased with you both. "There it is," she says. "That's the whole thing, right there."
