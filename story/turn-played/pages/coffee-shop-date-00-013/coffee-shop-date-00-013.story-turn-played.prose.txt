And she stayed exactly where she was, her hand resting warm and unmoving in his, the join unbroken. She let the day's turning pass over them unremarked — no word for it, no glance spared to the dark gathering and beading at the windows — and let the deepened lamplit warmth come into her smile instead, a shade warmer for it, a shade surer for the night drawn close outside the glass. She met his eyes, soft and unhurried, and let her breath go slowly out. The silence went on being the whole answer between them; she only stayed there at the center of the lit warmth with him, content, in no hurry, while the evening settled gentle around them.
