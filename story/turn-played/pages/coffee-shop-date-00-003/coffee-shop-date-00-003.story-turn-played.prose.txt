Abby held his gaze and let her smile deepen, answering him plainly and without hedging. "It really is." A beat, and then she tipped her head toward the rain-streaked glass, the city softened to nothing beyond it. "Best decision we've made all week." She didn't reach for a question to fill the space, didn't turn it anywhere — she only settled further into the worn give of the chair, meeting his ease with her own and letting a little more of her gladness show than she usually let herself, content to let the whole open afternoon be a thing they were simply in, together.
