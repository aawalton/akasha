Alan let the quiet sit one more beat, settling back into the worn give of the chair. Then he turned and met her eyes — an easy, unguarded smile, nothing held behind it — and tipped his head faintly toward the rain streaking the glass, the warmth wrapped close around them. "This is good," he said. "Made it just in time." And then he let it hang there, the whole open afternoon, for her to take wherever she liked.
