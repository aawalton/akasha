Aura kisses all-in, no pacing left in her now — the slow gone, the fast back but aimed — both hands going up into Awen's hair and working the pin loose until the whole dark weight of it comes down, and she laughs against Awen's mouth, the pin still shut in her fist like she won it.

"That was a good pin," Awen says.

"I'll build you a better one." A lie; she couldn't build a sandwich; they both know it and neither cares. She's already walking Awen backward — past the dark cabinets, toward the stairs at the back — the bag left on the floor, the key cold at her hip. "I'll build you a whole—"

Awen kisses her quiet before she has to finish lying about it.

They go.
