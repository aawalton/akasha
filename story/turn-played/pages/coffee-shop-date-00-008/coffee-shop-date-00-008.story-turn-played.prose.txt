And around them the afternoon eased a faint degree warmer. The gold lying along the floorboards thinned toward amber as the grey beyond the fogged glass dimmed a shade — the room making its first slight, unnoticed lean toward late afternoon. The rain held steady and softened against the windows, the machine idle behind it, and the warmth folded on unbroken around the two of them, settled and joined in the back corner, while the hour went quietly on without asking anything of either of them.
