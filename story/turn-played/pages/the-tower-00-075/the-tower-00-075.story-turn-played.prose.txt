You stand, eyes shut, and step across.

The warm gold that has pressed at you this whole time is simply gone now -- not dimmed, not fought, just shut out behind your own lids, irrelevant to a man who has stopped looking. What's left is what you can feel, and on a floor this cold that turns out to be a great deal. The heatlessness is everywhere, a flat dead evenness with no hearth in it and no living warmth anywhere, and against that nothing the real things stand out like coals on snow. Off to one side, where your eyes would have shown you a tall kind host with its hands held open, your ember-sense finds only a cold hollow -- a hole in the warmth shaped like a welcome, a void with nobody home. You walk past the picture of a host without slowing. It was never the thing in this room.

You go slow, the maul low and banked and ready across your hands, its chain ember-bright, your fire held coiled behind the head and metered out to a measured load -- a hard knock, not an emptying. And as you move deeper into the cold the other thing rises to meet you: that skin-crawl, the wrong oily prickle you've felt at the edge of this whole floor, only here it isn't at the edge anymore. It's real and it's close and it's moving -- a low rough bearing sliding through the dark with a hunter's patience, drawn to the blind man who walked willingly in, certain of its room and its trick and its easy meat.

It comes. The crawl swells against your senses, near and rising and about to spring --

-- and you don't wait to be sure. You loose the burst you've held the whole way in, straight through Burning Anger and out along the bright chain at the one real thing in the room, the maul throwing its banked fire in a single hard pulse of heat. You feel it land. You feel the skin-crawl FLARE -- a sick bright spike as your fire finds it and bites -- and then recoil, and then go out, snuffed in one blow like a candle pinched cold, the oily presence that filled the dark simply ending. The cold hollow where the false host stands does not so much as flicker; it never had anything in it to hurt.

The maul rides lighter and cooler in your hands, its deep fire poured down to a low banked coal. Your focus sits lower in you, spent but nowhere near dry -- you measured it, and the measure was right. The hall is quiet around you, gone still in a way it was not a moment ago, the watching weight of it lifted. You stand in the cold dark with your eyes still closed, the false gold pressing uselessly at lids you have not opened, and somewhere past this room the floor goes on down into a deeper dark you have not yet reached. The way you came in is still open behind you.
