She stops. She was about to say she never gets around to it, and it isn't even true — she likes the door exactly that stubborn. But Aura has closed her hand around the key, all the way, the wind rose vanishing into her fist, and she's looking at Awen, gone still on both feet for once, and a sentence about a sticking door is the least interesting thing in the building.

Aura looks down at her own closed hand, then back up. "You know I've got every record in this place." Her thumb moves once across the top of her fist. "Every machine, every tag. This is number one now. I'm not standing it on a shelf."

She's got a carabiner of keys at her hip — arcade, back room, the roll-gate out front — and she threads Awen's onto it right there, brass ringing once against steel.

"Okay." The ring drops back against her hip. "Put the bag down."

Awen puts the bag down.
