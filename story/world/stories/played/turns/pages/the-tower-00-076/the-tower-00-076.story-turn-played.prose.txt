The quiet doesn't last, and you didn't expect it to.

You go on into it, eyes still shut, the maul low and banked in your hands -- its fire a small thing now, a coal where there was a furnace, but enough to read by. The dead hall opens out ahead of you into something longer and colder, a wider dark you feel more than picture, the heatless evenness of the floor running on and on. And the skin-crawl you came looking for is there to meet you. You wanted one more. You find more than one.

It is the same wrong essence the burst snuffed a room behind you, that oily predator-prickle -- but doubled now, or more than doubled, a low moving wrongness that is not in any one place. There is something off to one side of you and something else further round, and the sense of them slides and shifts, real and alive and patient, beginning to open apart the way two hunters open apart around one animal: one to hold its eye, one to come in from where it isn't looking. You feel them start to move around you in the cold.

And then the cold itself betrays you.

Heat. Warmth, blooming up out of the dark where there has been no honest warmth on this whole floor -- the haven has been cold gold and heatless light from the first step, and now, here, all at once, it is throwing heat at you, soft and bright and everywhere, washing in against the one sense you have been walking by. For half a breath it nearly turns you. And then the harder thing in you, the part that has not stopped reasoning behind your shut eyes, goes cold and certain: this heat is a lie. It is too sudden, too convenient, too exactly aimed at the sense you reached for when you closed your eyes. The place felt you cross the last room blind, by warmth and the lack of it -- and here it has learned to throw false warmth, to flood the very compass you switched to. The way of seeing that let you walk in the dark is being taken from you, deliberately, right here. That it is failing you is itself the answer: you have been understood, and met.

You reach for them through it anyway, and the fine edge is gone -- the false heat fills the dark the way smoke fills a room, and the real wrong things moving in it blur, present and unpinnable, more than one, you cannot say how many or quite where. They are closing. You stand undercharged at the mouth of a colder dark with your eyes still shut and two-or-more patient, hungry things drawing apart to take you from the sides, your blind way of seeing turned against you in the span of a single room. Behind you the cleared hall is quiet, and the way you came is still open.
