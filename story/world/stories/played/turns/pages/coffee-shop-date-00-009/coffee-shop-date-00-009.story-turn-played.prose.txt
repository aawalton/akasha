And then the song that had cradled the hush came to its end — the warmer melody thinning to its last notes and gone, a bare half-second where there was only the soft wash of rain on the glass. Then the next one rose into the space it left, a shade brighter, a half-beat quicker, its melody opening softly beneath the low murmur of the room. The change asked nothing. The warmth in the back corner held; the rain kept steady on the windows; the amber light leaned another faint degree toward late day, and the shelter stayed whole over their joined hands. The room had only turned the song over, as it does, on its own quiet rhythm — and the afternoon went on around them, a little lighter in the air for it.
