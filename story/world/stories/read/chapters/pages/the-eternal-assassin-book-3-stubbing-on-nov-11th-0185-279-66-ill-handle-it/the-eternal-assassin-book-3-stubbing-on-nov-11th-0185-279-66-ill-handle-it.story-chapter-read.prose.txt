An endless space devoid of anything and everything stretched out all around him. For the third time, Asher found himself drifting around without his mortal coil, not knowing how long he’d already been there or how long he would remain. Just like the previous two times he’d awoken within this space, he felt the attention of something larger than any human could possibly comprehend. A presence that dwarfed galaxies and embodied the entire universe. According to Samantha, he was most likely floating before the deity of Eternity. The being who powered the element and embodied it directly. The first time he’d shown up here, he’d sensed curiosity from the supposed deity. The second time, familiarity. This time…

…Entertainment?

Asher blinked as he opened his eyes, finding himself staring up at the night sky. Based on the position of the moon, at least an hour or two had passed since his surprise fight against the three unknown assailants. For the first time, he didn’t wake up from his vision in a cold sweat, or screaming at the top of his lungs. He didn’t know if the repeated exposure to such a colossal entity was simply dulling his reactions to it, or if the fact that it seemed entertained to see him of all things was the cause. But either way, he was just more confused than anything.

Putting the matter of a potential deity finding his repeated deaths amusing aside for the moment, Asher pushed himself to his feet, looking around and realizing he’d been lying halfway across the city from where he’d fought his three attackers. Based on the broken shingles beneath him and the furrow in the roof from where he’d impacted, it looked as though his corpse had been blown quite a good way away from the site of the battle.

Huh. That was some deadman’s switch…

Honestly, Asher wasn’t really all that surprised. The fact that the three attackers had gone to such lengths to hide their identities meant that whoever had sent them after him really didn’t want their identity known. They hadn’t been holding any shards, hadn’t had any equipment on them beyond those weird metal spikes that exploded, and hadn’t said so much as a single word during the entire fight. Asher didn’t even know if he’d been attacked by men, women, or a combination of the two. They’d clearly been professionals, and even in failure, they’d carried out their jobs to the letter.

Glancing down at himself, Asher grimaced at the state his sea serpent leathers were in. While they’d done an excellent job keeping his body in one piece from the force of the explosion, they were severely burned and a few pieces were hanging off in tatters. After making a mental note to bring them back to the tailor in Poltar who was probably trying to figure out how to fake his own death by now just to get out of that free repair policy he’d boasted about, Asher focused on the royal palace and Recalled back to his room.

“Asher?” Samantha asked, rubbing her eyes as she woke up. The moment she got an actual look at him, she gasped, any trace of sleep that had been on her face vanishing instantly as she sat up in bed. “Realms! What happened?!”

“I was attacked by a trio of masked assailants,” Asher shrugged, giving her a reassuring smile. “I’m fine. You can go back to sleep if you want.”

“As if I could go back to sleep after seeing you like that,” Samantha said, getting out of bed and laying a hand against his cheek. “You sure you’re okay?”

“They’re dead and I’m not. It’s about the best outcome I can hope for,” Asher shrugged, pulling her into a reassuring hug. “I’m going to go tick off some royal guards by waking Donvath up. I have a feeling he’s going to want to know about this.”

“I’m coming with you,” Samantha said, quickly pulling on some clothes and grabbing the bow she kept by the bed at all times during the night.

Asher knew better than to argue, and honestly, he felt better having Samantha by his side. While he didn’t think anyone would risk going after those close to him instead of his own head, the possibility wasn’t completely off the table. Once she was dressed and ready to go, they headed over to Donvath’s actual bedroom. Asher had never had any need to wake the High Prince from his sleep before, but there was a first time for everything, and Donvath had told him where to find him in the event of an emergency.

As per usual, there were two intimidating figures standing outside Donvath’s bedroom door. Each one was decked out in a full suit of armor with halberds in hand, and each possessed the royal guard class. The one on the left had an aura that felt like a gleaming scythe cutting through shadows, and the one on the right had an aura that reminded Asher of a tsunami, a sensation of overwhelming force that seemed to sweep out and around his being. Each aura was strong and deadly, and Asher didn’t doubt both of the two royal guards were more than capable of fighting back against any would-be assassin who dared go after Donvath in his own palace.
