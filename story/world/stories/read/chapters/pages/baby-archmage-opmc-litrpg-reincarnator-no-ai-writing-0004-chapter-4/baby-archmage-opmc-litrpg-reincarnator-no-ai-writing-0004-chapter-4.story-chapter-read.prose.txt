[Reward: Perk — Unique]

Description: All Skill Experience related to Mana Manipulation boosted by 15%

What on earth was this? The Extra Skill Experience was clear, naturally, and much appreciated.

But what the hell was an Unique Skill? I contemplated it and decided on a theory.

Since Mana Manipulation was an Uncommon Skill, many people practicing magic must have it. But maybe, since I had the Mythic Skill, which allowed me to see the mana particles, my Mana Manipulation gained special properties through that?

If so, that was awesome. Further testing would be required, but I couldn’t do that before I met someone with a normal variant of the Skill.

Overall I was exuberant about my progress! I was starting to accumulate an interesting and coherent set of Skills and Attributes. I felt a strange satisfaction from having overlapping Attributes in Perception and Mana Flow. I idly wondered what an optimized build looked like. Right now I could only guess, but I trusted my approach because it had one goal: Learn magic. I was doing that organically and step by step, and just accepting the skills that came with it.

And while I had no other reference points, I had a feeling the build was slowly becoming powerful. Much of that obviously had to do with having obtained a Mythic-tier Skill.

As my mother sang to me and I played with her fingers, I thought about the implications. Was Mana Sight an exclusive ability? Couldn’t other people see mana? Surely then, there had to be other ways to perceive it, because Elder Piraka had used magic.

But it was possible that I was in possession of an advantage that could make me a magical powerhouse!

What would I make of this advantage? The village seemed benign enough, even if the men were very competitive. There was a general sense of harmony and wellbeing between the villagers. I might even be safe sharing my secrets in this insulated location. But I needed to think further, and that actually presented a problem I had been considering.

This was a remote location. As much as I loved my parents and was growing fond of the village, my primary goal was the pursuit of magic. I had known from my first visit outside that I couldn’t stay here forever.

If I ventured out into the world as was my plan, I would face powerful people who might not appreciate me having special capabilities. I could be forced into servitude or outright killed. I would need to temper what I was willing to share and with who.

But that was a worry for another day. What was important now was that I had a new Skill to train.

Training Mana Manipulation turned out to be arduous. I mostly focused on a simple task: making a single particle stop for as long as I could.

It took enormous mental effort to impose my intent on the mana particles. Whatever hunch I had in my moment of Magical Insight was now gone. Like I had done something impressive in a moment of inspiration, that I couldn’t reproduce.

Well, I never expected things to be easy. It didn’t matter. I had decided who I was going to be and what I wanted.

I returned to this thought when the training got hard.

After months of meditation, my mind was sharpening. I could stay focused for long periods of time and my recovery from mental strain was fast. That was likely a combined effect of Meditation and the Focus Attribute. This helped me train, and as the days passed, I managed to find enough success imposing my intent on the mana particles.

[+1 Magic Power]

The Attribute bonuses were very noticeable. Either way, each level made imposing my intent much faster, much more accurate and I could make the particles stop for longer periods of time.

My Mana Sight was also improving, although the rate of advancement had clearly slowed down.

[Mana Sight level 7!]

[+4 Perception]

[+2 Mana Flow]

I wondered how slow it would be without my Achievement Perks and Elder Piraka’s buff giving me a 30% Skill Experience increase. Magical Insight helped too, because sometimes it would give me a nudge in how to impose my will on the particles.

Eventually I would get Mana Sight to level 10, which would give me a new Perk. I wondered what it would be. Mana Sight was naturally a much narrower skill than Meditation. Many simple Skills would likely have streamlined upgrades, but I’d see that soon. Because Mana Sight was a Mythic Skill I was hoping the Perk would be a big one. Naturally, that motivated me to train even harder. I would leverage my boundless baby excitement to its fullest!

In this manner two months passed. Alongside my System Skills, I was also improving in two normal life skills — mobility and speech. Rather important ones at that. I wondered if I would have those in my Skill Slots if they weren’t full. Probably not. Apparently not every ordinary ability counted as a System Skill. I was deliberately practicing my speech and motor control, but the System hadn't offered me anything for those.

Either way, my matured mind and already knowing the language made the learning fast. I was babbling constantly when I was with my parents and moving my body around. By now I had enough fine motor control in my mouth that I could already speak some words. However, I had been holding back my true abilities, because I didn’t want to alert my parents. Now I had been alive for a bit over six months, and it was time to move forward.

We were sitting at the table. Mother had just fed me and I was in her lap while she and father ate.

“Ma-ma,” I uttered.

Father dropped a spoon and a wide grin of surprise and delight spread on both of my parents’ faces.

“By the spirits. My little gem! What did you just say?” mother said.

“First words! And early too! My boy!” father said. Then added. “But entirely the wrong word. Say papa, Luka.”

I decided to indulge him. “Pa-brlll”

Father was so excited he stood up. “I’ll take it!”

My mother laughed and hugged me. “What a smart little boy we have. Say it again for mama. Say mama.”

“Ma-ma,” I said and smiled at her.

She giggled and kissed my forehead.

I started repeating the word, which made them ever more excited.

“Look at him! He can see we liked it,” father said. “Say papa. Try it once more.”

“Pa-phh,” I said. Knowing two words at this point was a tad too much.

“My word…” father said. “I can’t wait to gloat to the boys. My son is a genius.”

“He surely is a fast learner,” mother said and then turned to father with a sly smile. “I thought you’d be more upset he said mama instead of papa.”

My father laughed. “I bet you’ve been secretly nudging him to say that when I’m out fishing. I can’t win this one.”

It was absolutely true.

My mother blushed. “Who are you and what happened to my immature husband?”

Father only laughed. “He became a father and had to grow up.”

“Oh, you just know how to say all the right things, huh?”

“C’mere.”

Mother and I sat in father’s lap, while they kissed and father stroked my hair. I loved these moments. I had been initially surprised by the intensity of these childlike emotions. But I wanted to lean into them, so I would strive to continue making my parents proud. I fell asleep feeling loved.

When I woke up, my parents were having a half-whispered conversation.

“About that genius thing,” mother said. “You think it’s true?”

Father shrugged. “I don’t know. He said mama pretty early, but nothing too unusual, right?”

“It’s… very early, love,” mother said.

“Really?” father asked. “But they’re out there wrestling and talking about tactics at four years old. Basically tiny adults.”

“Those are kids with the System already unlocked,” mother said. “That’s different. Kids get more lucid when they unlock it.”

“Do you think he…?”

Mother snorted. “Of course not. How could he?”

Oh, mother. If you only knew.

“Hah, so our boy is simply a genius?”

“I suppose so… Some children are early talkers, others are early walkers,” mother said in thought. “I don’t know… It’s how… easy this is, right?”

“Yeah, I guess? I don’t know how hard it should be. I do like that he doesn’t wake us up at night.”

“That’s really strange,” mother said. “You should see the other mothers. They’re a mess. Piraka and his new apprentice are busy with potions.”

Ah, that was an interesting piece of knowledge. Piraka had an apprentice. Did that mean I could also become one?

As far as I knew, he was the only person with magic-related skills in the village. I would need to learn from him in the future.

Father smiled. “Heh, so it wasn’t just me who thought you’re the prettiest bird in the flock.”

“I’m the only one getting proper sleep,” mother chuckled. “He only wakes me up when he’s hungry.”

“The spirits have blessed us,” father said. “Do you think the second one will be as easy?”

“The second one, huh?” my mother said with a smirk. “Love, I’ve barely recovered from the first one.”

A sibling, I mused. That would be nice. I hoped it would be a sister. I wanted to be a cool protective big brother.

At this point it was clear to me that my father was a big shot at the village. Not a chief or anything like that, but one of the best fishers, which was the most prestigious vocation, at least for men. As proud as he was of me, I feared I might disappoint him by not following in his footsteps. I had no interest in picking up a skill related to wrestling or spearplay. That would take up a precious Skill Slot that I needed for magic!

With Meditation at level 10, Mana Sight at 7 and Mana Manipulation at 2 my total level was at 19. I wondered when I would unlock my next Skill Slot. I didn’t need it now, because I didn’t know what I should even spend it on. I just wanted to learn more about the System. Surely I would gain a new Slot at 20 or 25. If it was any higher, it would be really hard to get Slots, and I even had an extra Skill Slot from Sage. For someone without my advantages, gaining enough Total Levels could be difficult.

Either way I threw myself back to training. I wanted Levels, and I needed to get a handle on talking and crawling. I was so close to Mana Sight level 10!

But most of all, I was just eager to play with magic. Even though I was hardly a master of cosmic forces — merely stopping and moving mana particles from my crib, there was something immensely satisfying about doing it. And I was only getting better at it. Watch out world!

Damn, being a baby was busy work.

Weeks turned into months. After countless hours of concentration, I finally gained another Skill Slot at Total Level 25. It felt nice to have one in reserve, so that I could spend it immediately on a Skill I liked. However, the exciting news happened when I was eleven months old. In the middle of the night, after hours of focus, I leveled up my Mana Sight.
