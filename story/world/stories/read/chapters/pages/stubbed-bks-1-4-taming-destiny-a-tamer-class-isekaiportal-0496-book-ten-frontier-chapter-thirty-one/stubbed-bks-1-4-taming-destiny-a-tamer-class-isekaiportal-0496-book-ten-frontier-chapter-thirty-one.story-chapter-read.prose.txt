The night is dark but, thanks to my enhanced vision, even a minimal amount of moonlight is enough for me to see almost as well as in the day. The guards were concerned about me leaving the settlement alone, but I pointed out that with two Tier threes around and a Tier four regularly coming to visit, it was unlikely that other beasts would dare come too close.

Whether that convinced them or they just recognised that I can handle myself well enough, they didn’t raise any more objections. I could have stayed inside the settlement, of course, but I just felt the urge to work on my internal matrix outside. The night feels pregnant with possibilities – a good time to make some progress.

I find myself savouring the walk – though I’ve agreed to delegate more of the settlement’s minutiae, I’m finding redirecting my thoughts from obsessing over it is another question.

Most of the walk from the command centre to the gates was spent noticing things about the settlement that I want to improve, change, or fix. Many, like how there are still people with no homes, huddling under tents, I noted down for Sarran and Loran to check on – the Earth Mages were probably just busy fighting beasts, and will now return to the task of building, but it’s just as well that the Stewards follow up on it. Some I note as something worth contemplating myself – like when I overheard someone complaining about nothing to do in the evenings.

Perhaps I should introduce board games, I muse, recalling how much even my companions enjoyed playing them. Or at least set some space and resources aside for creating any Moriaxan games that already exist.

It’s a tame kind of entertainment, but one that is likely to have nothing but good effects on my people – unlike other options like the Games in the Colosseum – and requiring less preparation than a theatre troupe or music group.

As I walk towards the farm fields through the peaceful night, I find my thoughts slowing and calming in response. Instead of thinking about what needs to change about the now, I begin to have visions of what this place could be in the future.

The cavern I’ve just exited has far more potential than has been used – the buildings that have been erected so far don’t even take up a fifth of the area that we’ve allowed for. I imagine what it might be in five years time – with buildings and roads filling the space, bustling with new settlers we’ve attracted from the mainland. I envision children playing on the roads, lights making the inside of the cavern feel like broad daylight, and a general sense of happiness and wellbeing.

Casting my gaze out to the harbour area, I imagine it bustling with ships, guarded and guided by beasts of the sea in harmony with the sailors. The path leading up from the harbour, I picture it paved and thick with carriages capable of carrying heavy loads trundling over it, bringing goods to and from our settlement from visiting merchant ships.

Looking onto the other side of the raised road on which I’m standing, I imagine the whole basin-like area cradled between the forest and the settlement to be filled with crops of all different kinds, growing more than enough food for the local population and with parties moving over it into the forest to explore and harvest its riches – without exploiting it.

I sigh, the smile fading from my face as I continue walking. It’s a dream. Aim for the stars in the hopes of achieving a moderately high hill. Even if we achieve only some of that in the next five years, I’ll be content.

I don’t think it too loudly, but I’m kind of hoping that Murphy might be taking a break after all the overtime he’s been putting in since we arrived on this continent. Heck – since we left Moriax.

Stopping by one of the large stones demarcating the road, I gaze down at the small patch of land currently used for farming. Even at this time, there’s movement down below.

Though Elarion is taking a well-deserved rest right now, I know that he’s organised shifts of his mages and farmers so there are enough on the field at all times to keep the wheat growing. He’s told me he will ease off on them after we’ve built up enough stocks of the grain to be able to experiment a bit – I understand his desire to provide for an uncertain future far more since he told me about the famine he witnessed. As long as he takes care not to exhaust either his people or the land too much in his enthusiasm, I’m all for it.

Most of my companions are sleeping soundly – as they deserve to after working hard all day. Fenrir is one of them and I don’t want to disturb him, but I feel a sudden longing for his steady presence. I decide to go for something almost as good – an echo, like I did before.

Though I could summon Fire Fenrir again, I decide to do something different this time – in the dark night, the flames of my fire guardian would probably draw more attention than I’d like. Concentrating on Fenrir’s form once more, I press my hands to the large stone, pouring in my mana.

It takes longer than with the flames, but the earth eventually responds to me, shifting into Fenrir’s shape. Unlike the flame guardian, however, this one doesn’t immediately show any signs of life.

I eye it thoughtfully. Did I do something different here? Or is it just down to the differences between earth and fire? It’s not like earth doesn’t have life – and it moves perfectly well, even if not as easily as fire does – so that shouldn’t be the issue.

Following a hunch, I press my hands to the perfectly formed statue of Fenrir again. Closing my eyes, I pour in my mana again, but this time include my memories of Fenrir and the sense of his unique Bond.

When I’ve packed him full enough of mana that any more would just require me to actively compress it, I pull my hands away, opening my eyes.

The statue is still with no apparent change.

Disappointment claws at me.

And then the statue turns his head towards me and opens his eyes. Where stone orbs should have existed, warm bronze glows instead. They have no pupils which makes them a little odd, but the recognition in them makes my breath catch.

“Earth Fenrir?” I ask quietly.

The statue shifts fluidly with a faint sound of grinding stone, stepping forward and pressing its head to my shoulder – this statue is a lot bigger than the real Fenrir and doesn’t even need to crane its neck upwards to complete the motion.

I laugh quietly and scratch at the statue’s head as if it were the real lizog.

“Alright, will you keep watch over me while I meditate?” I ask it. It nods with just as much eagerness as Fenrir would have responded through the Bond, and lies down, gesturing with its head in a very obvious manner.

Sitting down in the cradle of its body and leaning against it as it clearly wants, I pat it once more, feeling more comforted than I thought I would by its solidity and the affection it radiates. Then I close my eyes and sink into my Core space.

Zooming through my Core space, I feel satisfaction as I look at where the channels had been completely destroyed. The multi-tasking effect I gained with Meditation’s rank up to Master has proven itself in the last few days – even while I was negotiating with the merchants, I was still capable of healing my channels, if at a much slower rate than normal. And now, with those channels as good as new, I can concentrate on figuring out what to do with Bond Teleport.

That is far less simple. I move through my Core space until I’m facing the blackened Skill pattern directly.

At various moments, I’ve been trying different things. Directing Energy, directing mana, doing my best to direct stamina, though with limited effect. I even shifted into Deep Meditation at one point and inspected my Soul to see if I could spot any damaged areas – I couldn’t. Whatever is wrong with Bond Teleport has nothing to do with my soul.

None of it worked. No form of Energy makes it past the dividing line between golden and black and nothing I’ve tried seems to affect Bond Teleport at all. If it didn’t still appear in my status screen – though greyed out – I would wonder whether it had disappeared completely and the black was merely the charred outline of where it used to be. As it is, I sense that there’s still something there, but not anything I can affect. This is clearly a very different kind of injury than I’ve dealt with before in my own Core space and I’m running low on ideas of how to deal with it.

However, while I was in the meeting with the other lords and looking at the map Gulnis had drawn, I had an idea.

If I can’t affect the Skill pattern that’s there…what if I could redraw it?

I’m nervous about the idea – though I’ve created channels in the past, it’s always been about regrowing channels that were always there. Even when I’ve given myself stat points in the past, I did that by pulsing Energy through my internal matrix in the pattern I learned from levelling up with that stat alone – not by creating new channels directly.

And a mistake could have serious effects – I’ve seen the impact that poorly grown internal matrices can have on preventing beings from Evolving. I don’t even have any data about what happens with a damaged internal matrix for a Classer when they level up.

As an educated guess, at best, it would just create an inefficiency in the system that would mean less Energy goes towards increasing my stats – not a great idea. At worst, it could introduce a catastrophic error that causes my whole internal matrix to collapse.

It’s almost enough to make me not want to even try. But at the same time, the current state is no better – I have no idea what might happen if I level up while Bond Teleport is impenetrable.

Damned if I do, damned if I don’t. And between the two, I’d prefer to at least try to improve the situation.

But I don’t intend to do it stupidly. The first thing I do is to inspect the whole of Bond Teleport very closely. I observe the twists and turns of the matrix, memorising the moments where it suddenly turns back on itself, or it looks like it continues straight when in fact it dives down and shifts somewhere else before returning and continuing along the original vector. I plan for the spot where one thread passes through a loop and then splits into two which then become the loop – that one, in particular, feels very important.

When I feel that I’ve almost memorised the entire design, I start.

Pulling mana from my Core, I pull it towards where the thread enters.

I hit my first snag. Redrawing channels isn’t too difficult – it requires concentration, but the threads almost seem to know where to go and don’t resist me much. This is another question. Making that first fraction of an Energy channel takes more effort than redrawing one of the channels that had disintegrated – in its entirety!

What puzzles me most about how difficult it is to draw the channel is that I’ve done it in others’ Core spaces and it’s never been as bad as this. Heck, Loran didn’t report it being anywhere near this difficult to work on his own internal matrix. I wonder briefly if the Class system is just naturally more rigid than the natural kind of Evolution Loran has shown, or if it’s a built in security feature to stop fools from messing themselves up completely. I cross my fingers and hope that I’m not one of those ‘fools’ in this scenario.

Finally, I manage to convince both my Energy channel and the luminous blackness around it that is the representation of my soul that yes, I do want to do this, and a small nub of a channel grows out at a gentle angle from the healthy Energy channel. Since the only sharp bends are found inside actual Skill patterns, and the rest of my internal matrix is characterised by circular movement, I decided that a sharp right-angle wouldn’t be a good idea here.

Now I’ve broken through the initial resistance, it’s much easier to continue growing the Energy channel. I trace the lines of the blackened Skill as closely as I can, though it isn’t long before I hit a little snag – in the section ahead, the blackened lines twist so tightly together that there’s no space for me to slip my golden thread in between.

I pause there for a moment, hesitating over what to do. Draw the golden lines identically above the knot, introducing more of a curve there than originally…or change it even more?
