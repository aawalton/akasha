Alexander awoke with Emily lying on his arm. As he looked at her sleeping form, he felt an overwhelming guilt as he ruminated over the events of the past few hours. He had absolutely no desire to move, not because he found the situation particularly romantic, but because his leg was throbbing with pain. In addition, he had a splitting headache—a familiar occurrence from the last time he had used up all of his mana. The familiar feeling of his soul being sucked dry lingered in him. Thinking of his fall, he didn’t remember how he had escaped from the battle. Where were Flick and Johnny?

A sense of panic set in as he wondered if they had come out of the chaos unharmed. Flick could shadow jump as long as he hadn’t exhausted himself. Johnny had implied he had stealth abilities as well; he would have to trust that they could make do for now. Looking back at Emily, he wondered how she had gotten into this bizarre world.

Alexander sat up slowly, trying not to disturb Emily. He pulled out another sandwich from his fanny pack and ate it slowly. Looking around, he saw his surroundings were brighter than before. He guessed he had retreated from the dusk side of the world, hopefully outside of the Widow's influence. He wasn’t sure if that was a good thing. How would they rescue the party? He groaned at his next thought: he had broken the contract, hadn’t he?

A notification was in the corner of his vision, and he mentally clicked on it.

^

Quest Complete!

Save the damsel in distress!

A young maiden has fallen into another world and been kidnapped!

Objective: Survive the perilous new world, find Megan Dyer, and rescue her!

Quest rewards: 2000 XP

Crystalized Essence of Light

2 gold

Congratulations! You are now level 10!

Well, that’s one question answered. The party managed to escape with Megan.

A few weeks ago he would have been overwhelmed by all of the unanswered questions he had. He wasn’t overwhelmed by it anymore. A small blessing, he admitted. He could still think clearly and move forward logically.

Emily groaned and stretched like a cat as she awoke. Her hair was a mess and her eyes were still shut as she sat up and muttered, “Fooood.”

Despite her unruly hair and just waking up, she was still startlingly attractive to Alexander.

“Do you want a sandwich? I also have some coffee I could heat up for you.” Alexander offered.

Emily’s eyes slowly opened and got progressively wider as she realized where she was and what had happened.

“Holy crap! Alexander? Are you ok!?” Emily asked, crawling closer to him.

“I am a little banged up, but I will manage with a bit of rest,” he said as he looked at his shin brace and adjusted it before he rewrapped it. “I do have a few questions for you, but let me get you some breakfast first.”

Alexander handed Emily a sandwich and some trail mix in a to-go box. Then he pulled out a thermos of mundane coffee. He opened it and stuck his finger in the liquid and smiled apologetically to Emily before he said, “Prestidigitation.”

Steam instantly came out of the top of the thermos as he heated it to a fresh-from-the-pot temperature.

“Prestidigitation? I’d love to hear the origins of that word. It sounds like someone is trying to cheat in Scrabble.”

To Emily’s surprise, Alexander belly laughed. “I had the same thought. Believe it or not, this was my first ability.”

“Heating cold coffee?” Emily asked incredulously.

“Actually, yes. That was exactly why I chose it.”

Emily had to laugh this time.

Alexander handed her the thermos, and she took a slow testing sip.

“So… what are you doing here? How did you end up as a leader of a leshykin uprising?”

“Is that what they are called?” Emily asked. “This is all going to sound weird, but I kind of convinced Bru-haha to tell me where you went, and after getting worried about you, I just kind of snapped and had to find you.”

“Ok… I am surprised Bru-haha broke the NDA we signed. You came through the stump portal at the school? That should have been well guarded,” Alexander asked with a raised eyebrow.

“I kind of convinced the guards to let me through and not tell anyone about it?”

“You must be very persuasive…” Alexander said with some suspicion.

“So then Flick and I went into the stump and found these delightful woods. These trees sing! Some of them are creepy, but not the ones towards the daylight. So I followed one of the more pleasant voices and met the tree giants, or leshy-kin as you called them?”

Alexander nodded.

“The voice led me to them, and they all seemed kind-hearted. I could tell even though I couldn’t understand them. They surrounded me and walked with me as I tried to follow you. I didn’t realize that there were such terrifying creatures that would attack them! I hope they are alright.”

“So they weren’t there to overthrow the Widow of the Woods? Was she really only trying to… un-bond us?”

Emily went silent.

It was getting plain to even Alexander that Emily was trying to avoid talking about her sudden ability to convince people, hear trees, and track him. He didn’t press, though, as he had kept his secret.

Alexander started ripping up the thick moss beneath their feet. He had to pull out a knife to cut away sections as the moss was so thick and interwoven. When he finally managed to get to the moist dirt, he smoothed it out, flattening it, then drew a perfect circle about two feet in diameter.

“What are you working on?” Emily asked while he worked.

“Well, I have no sense of direction right now except the perpetual day and night in this place. So I am going to try and find something that might give us directions.”

He pulled out a worn, antique book he had regrettably little experience with. He had taken it from the semi-evil, mostly just selfish summoner, Karen. He had left her webbed up in a control tower. When the battle of Congo finished, he had informed Brian of her and explained her supernatural crimes. Alexander assumed she was imprisoned afterwards and would likely stay there for a time.

When he had previously opened the book, he had read the basics of the summoning arts but knew it would take time to get good at the craft. Presently he flipped to a chapter he had in mind and skimmed it. He looked around and found a smooth stone about the size of his fist. He placed it at the center of the circle and chanted a short verse.

“Spirit of Earth, send me a helper to guide me through these dark paths. Summon Gnome.”

He assumed Karen had a special ability that let her bypass these steps, so using her actions as a guide, he went for the smallest and simplest option. He felt a small amount of mana drain into the ground from his feet. They watched as the stone sank into the ground and the dirt swirled up into the form of a skinny little figure with a close-trimmed beard.

The gnome looked around at his surroundings. Emily’s eyes went wide at the creature. Once it had its bearings, it looked at Alexander with a pompous smirk and hopped on its feet.

Alexander got on a knee before the gnome and asked it, “Can you guide us to a portal to get us back to my planet?”

The gnome shook his head no, then made his fingers move in a way that showed walking legs and shook its head yes.

Alexander scratched his head. “I’m not sure what you mean, but you can take us somewhere we can find someone who knows?”

The gnome shrugged then nodded its head yes.

Alexander broke the ring around the gnome, and it hopped and scampered off. It moved in the direction of the light, fortunately. Alexander had gotten the impression from Judah that the closer to the darkness they got, the more malevolent the beings they encountered would be.

“Well, shall we follow?”

Emily nodded, and they started moving in the direction the gnome was running. After an hour of brisk walking, they paused for a break. Emily didn’t seem tired, but Alexander noticed her adjusting her shoes. She wore a pair of athletic joggers.

“Would you like to change your socks? I have a pair of moisture-wicking alpaca socks here.”

“That would be great, thank you. I should have brought some better socks, but I didn’t expect I’d be out so long.”

He handed her the socks and a bottle of water.

“So…” Emily started. “At this point, would you mind telling me about the nature of your unique abilities?” she asked.

“Well, it does seem the secret is out.” He grinned self-deprecatingly. “Emily… I am a wizard. That is the class that was given to me before all of the commotion with the orcs. I have been told it will be dangerous for this information to get out too quickly. That I would be targeted. That is at least part of the reason that I didn’t inform you sooner. I was told there are likely only a handful of us known. It seems this class is one of great versatility. I can attest to that.”

“A wizard. So you can shoot fire like that woman from New York?”

“I can now. She was actually here on the mission with us. I assume they found a way to save the girl we were sent here for as the quest—that’s what the screens we get call it.”

“Well, thank you for sharing that with me, Alexander. I’m glad to know.” Emily smiled.

“And you, Emily, you have an ability as well, don’t you?” Alexander asked.

“I…” Emily stammered. “I have a bloodline… I don’t know if that means I’m not human, or if I’m a threat to everyone around me… some kind of monster….” Emily stopped herself and took a breath. “I’m sorry, Alexander. I didn’t mean to control you.”

Control me? Alexander thought. He didn’t remember being controlled by Emily.

“I got a red notification that said I had awakened a bloodline. I have no clue what that even means!” Emily said exasperatedly as she combed her fingers through her hair. She began to put it into a self-contained bun.

“Well, I may have a solution to that, and if you are scared of controlling me, I already have the ability to make an item to prevent some influence to my mind. So everything should be fine,” Alexander said comfortingly.

“I think this bloodline is what created this bond. You were gone so long that I started feeling intense anxiety… As if you were gone too long, a piece of me would be destroyed. I had to find you.”

“Long? But I have only been gone for at most three days?”

“What!? Alexander, you’ve been gone for over a month!”
