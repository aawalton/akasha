Freddy appeared in a small clearing within a beautiful forest.

Despite knowing with absolute certainty that this was just a simulation, every single sense in his body was telling him otherwise. This was real. So real, that even his insanity sense worked here. And while he couldn’t see the other guy… sheesh. Whoever was on the other side of those trees was more than a little kooky, that was for sure.

He looked around, feeling the warmth, the smells, the tug of gravity keeping him anchored.

This is just straight up magic.

He had heard that the arena was “just like real life,” but he hadn’t fully believed it. It was one of those things that couldn’t help but sound like it came with a catch, but no, it seemed that those rumors were pretty spot on.

Given that he was in the middle of a match, he focused. First, he moved to a nearby tree, pressing his back against it and going still.

He had a pretty massive problem.

A lot of his abilities were quite distinctive, and there was no doubt enough footage of “Freddy Cliff” fighting to blow his cover if he used the wrong thing.

That made things complicated.

Really, he only had… five or so abilities he could use. Hydraulic Flex was perfectly safe. Hydraulic Throw also shouldn’t be a problem. He could use Create Water, but that wasn’t going to be accomplishing much—same as Dissipate Wave. As long as he still had his gauntlets on, the explosive nature of Tsunami Strike would be mostly hidden, so that was on the table as well.

That was it.

Rather than five, it was more like three.

Power wise, he was confident in holding his own even with just that much. But where’s the flash?

His opponent made their move, and Freddy put those thoughts aside. “Halt right there!” He shouted somewhat awkwardly. This is so much harder in action. Putting his doubts aside, he leaped out, sword pointed at the man with tattered black cloth covering every inch of his body and a rusty sawblade slung over his shoulder. “Foul villain! Prepare to meet your end!”

Now, given the nature of the anonymous category, Freddy had expected his opponents to be a bit… out there. But this guy was straight up billowing madness.

The man chuckled. “What a joke. Aren’t you ashamed of yourself?” Cocking his head back, the man’s eyes betrayed the smile beneath his mask. “Wearing daddy’s armor and cosplaying as a… What’s this even supposed to be; some kind of superhero?”

This was a pivotal moment for the persona Freddy was trying to build. He decided to play it straight. No matter what his opponents said, even if it broke the “fourth wall” so to speak, he would ignore it. “Cease your mad rambling, vile criminal!”

The guy laughed. “God, you’re so annoying. Just shut the fuck up and fight.” With a sudden thrust of his arm, the man skillfully launched a string of darkness with some kind of curse attached to it.

Freddy leaped to the left to dodge, using just a bare touch of Hydraulic Flex to redirect himself forward and lunge at the man. His soul winced at the raw use of an ability without the support of a ring, but the pain wasn’t enough to hinder him. And ultimately, once he left this match, even soul damage would be undone as if it never happened.

He was holding back immensely to make sure the fight wasn’t too one-sided.

Deciding to start off with a light attack, Freddy swung the sword down. The man frantically rushed to block it with the saw. The sword cleaved right through the weapon, sank into the man’s shoulder, and sliced right through his entire body, all the way to and through his crotch area.

The man released a strange shriek and fell to the ground in two pieces.

Whoops.

Freddy appeared back in the sac. Reaching up, he undid the meat zipper and experienced what it was like to be born before a small audience.

The first thing he noted was Madame’s raucous laughter echoing through the room.

Freddy sighed. “This sword is way too good, huh?”

“And so is your armor,” Madame added. “To be perfectly fair, I did point it out.”

“You certainly did.”

There were two things Freddy learned in that first match. First, he would have to hold back a lot more than initially thought. And second… he needed some cheaper gear.

He ended up swapping his overpriced equipment for an extremely barebones set instead. The only exceptions were his gauntlets, which he kept from the Milliure set, to make sure he could actually use Tsunami Strike, and the weapon, which he replaced with an old heavy sword with a one-star imbuement and a durability enchantment to make sure it wouldn’t crumble when he swung it.

Then, without further ado, he queued up for a second match.

This time, the setting was inside an old, abandoned town in a desert. The yellow-walled infrastructure coupled with the sweltering heat made Freddy’s reunion with the black-clothed man that much warmer.

“Son of a bitch!” The man groaned. “Not this motherfucker again…”

Now, this was another pivotal moment for Freddy’s persona. How straight exactly should he play it?

Raising his sword, he shouted, “Abominable monstrosity! You have been felled once already, and yet, through dark magicks, you rise once more!”

“Dude…” The man stared at him flatly. “Are you deadass?”

But he was already committed. “Prepare to meet your end once and for all!”

Rather than use any serious amount of power whatsoever, Freddy opted to just kind of lightly jog towards the man.

He flinched pretty hard at the sight of Freddy lunging at him, but as he realized how much slower Freddy was moving, he adjusted his guard. It was evident that he noted the change in armor as well, which allowed a degree of confidence to return to the man’s posture. “You’ll regret taking me lightly.” He took a deep breath. “Terminal Bloodlust.”

A pulsating red light flashed across the surface of the man’s body. An immense power enveloped him, and he began to transform. The cloth across his body seemingly fused into him, becoming part of a morphing, monstrous form. The rusty saw melded into his arm, and he grew another half a meter in height.

I’m guessing that trades his life to turn him into a pseudo-monster for a while? Pretty cool, honestly.

Freddy continued his relatively ponderous march forward.

The transformation didn’t last long. As soon as it was done, the man pointed his arm towards Freddy. A large mass of coiling darkness flung out. It wrapped around Freddy’s armor, with numerous tentacles branching out to tie him to nearby rocks and walls to slow him down.

The man-monster screeched, swinging the rusty sawblade down on Freddy’s tied-up form. The saw cut pretty cleanly, likely aided by a talent, but even then, no matter how easily it shredded the armor, all it left across Freddy’s body was a relatively shallow gash. “Gah!” Freddy shouted dramatically. “I must rid the world of this evil at once!”

A freaky voice sounded from the beastly performer. “And what exactly will you—” The man’s words caught as he watched Freddy tear the coiling dark mass like it was paper mache. “What the—”

Freddy jumped. He reached the performer in the blink of an eye, and with a light application of Hydraulic Flex, swung the giant sword. The chunk of metal slashed across the performer’s chest with an impressive degree of force, flinging him back and making him screech something otherworldly.

He scurried to his feet as quickly as he could manage. “Who the hell are you, dude!?”

“I am he who brings order!” Freddy spouted some random bullshit that came to mind as he flourished the sword.

“That’s cute. You elite fuckers must be really bored of life if you spend your time like this. I haven’t seen you around, so I’m guessing you're new.” A shimmering layer of unholy essence enveloped the man. “Let’s see how long that performance of yours holds up.” Bringing his head down, he whispered, “My heart.”

Power raged across the man’s body in a tell-tale display of an unholy blessing that was too powerful for the user’s good. He lunged forward, and this time, he was fast enough that even Freddy had to treat the attack seriously.

Raising his heavy sword, he blocked the incoming slash. The sword held up, but he heard a nasty crack that indicated it wouldn’t last much longer. Opting to keep it in one piece, he dodged around, using his Hydraulic Flex to the best of its ability without two-starring it.

On the surface, the fight was quite dramatic. Freddy kept leaping outside of range, dodging the unceasing attempts to tie him down with dark bindings. He took a number of hits, including a few carrying curses with them. Partial blindness cloaked his vision, and a pain curse left his muscles twitching involuntarily. The attacks themselves shredded his armor like tissue, flinging him around the stage and through a number of buildings.

The buildings were flimsy, as if they were made of actual cardboard, likely to facilitate a more dramatic show as they crumbled at the lightest touch.

However, the actual state of the fight was quite different from what it appeared to be.

Frankly, he was barely injured, and the few slashes he slipped in ensured he was at the very least patched up with first-aid-quality healing. On top of that, he hadn’t tapped into his essence reserves at all, seeing as his satellite could easily cover the cost of his Hydraulic Flex.

His soul wasn’t exactly loving the liberal use of Hydraulic Flex, but other than some very minor tearing, he wasn’t suffering too badly. There was a problem, however—

What’ll happen if I use Tsunami Strike?

That was a real essence-guzzler, and if he double-starred it… well, it wouldn’t kill him outright, but it would suck.

Losing wasn’t a huge deal for him. But breaking the persona would stink. I’ll have to find out eventually.

Deciding to finally break the flow of the fight, he shouted, “Enough!” Swinging the heavy sword in a downward chop, he managed to take a couple of fingers off his opponent.

The man-turned-beast screeched in pain, evidently quite angry and more than a bit confused as to how Freddy wasn’t a pile of mincemeat. “The fuck are you made of?”

“The inviolable essence of justice!”

“Jesus, your lines are atrocious!”

“Silence, vermin!” Freddy flourished the sword. He lunged forward, testing his limits with a double-starred Hydraulix Flex. It stung, but it wasn’t too bad. He reached his target, and put some actual effort into an attack. His opponent blocked the incoming attack with a raised arm, which nearly got cut off, but the sword’s shoddy quality finally caught up with it, and it broke into pieces.

Seeing an opening, the man thrust the saw forward, leaving a rather wicked slash down Freddy’s torso and throwing him back.

Freddy allowed himself to bounce back, playing up the exhaustion he felt as he ragdolled across the scorching sand. He made it look like he could barely drag himself up to his feet, and with some help from a touch of blood essence, his minor wounds were bleeding like an open faucet.

“I can tell you’re going hella easy on me,” the man said. “The fact you swapped your armor tells me everything I need to know. It stings a bit, knowing that even if I trade my life, I can barely hold my ground against an elite. Actually, you’re just not just anyone, are you? I don’t think I’ve seen you use anything other than what I’m guessing to be Hydraulic Flex.” He laughed sardonically. “You piss me off.”

The man gritted his teeth, and then twisted his mouth in a hideous smile.“You think this is going to be easy, don’t you? That just because you can throw some weight around, you’ll get attention?” He laughed. “That might be true in the outside world, but in the arena, there is nothing more boring than a performer like you. Zero flash, and a dogshit persona to boot. It won’t be long before you realize this is a waste of time.”

Sitting down on the ground, he released a sigh. “I have no interest in making you look good.” Putting the saw to his neck, he began cutting. “Go die in a ditch.” The man’s head sagged forward as the muscle was severed, and his arm stopped moving.

A moment later, Freddy appeared inside the sac.

Wooooow…

Leaving the sac, he was met by a shrug from Madame. “That’s just how it is in the anonymous category. The trolls only like to play by their own rules. If it doesn’t go their way, they take the ball and go home.”

Freddy snorted. “I can see that.” He took the helmet off, revealing the mask beneath, all while the viscous sac fluid sloughed off of him. “What do I do then?”

“To be honest with you, I think you’re already on a good track here. I like that you’re playing it completely straight; it provides a neat contrast to the trolls’ lack of seriousness. You should keep it up. As long as you solely focus on your gimmick, the reality behind who you are will inevitably shine through.”

“I believe I have some things to add,” Henry said.

“Just a moment,” she said, raising a finger at him as she faced Freddy. “By the way, I’ve briefed Henry on your identity while you were fighting. You don’t have to worry that he’ll reveal your secret to anyone.”

Freddy nodded. “I assumed as much. I’m guessing Henry over here knows quite a number of secrets, given his job description.”

The man chuckled awkwardly. “Indeed, I do…”

“You’ve had something to say?” Freddy reminded.

Henry eyed Madame, who gestured for him to go ahead.

“Thank you, Madame.” The man faced Freddy. “From what I can see, your greatest strength is that you are inherently a fascinating individual. The simulated arena might not be real, but it still reflects reality in many ways. For starters, simply participating in many fights would show that you have an immense mental fortitude.”

“And a lot of money,” Madame added. “By the way, I’ve sponsored these two matches, but you will have to pay for them with your own tokens going forward.”

“Duly noted,” he said with an awkward chuckle.

“But hey, you’ve recovered around half a percent of the investment with the views you’ve gotten so far.” She grinned.

“A dud, huh?”

“Not necessarily.” Madame smiled. “Some matches get reruns on smaller channels, and people can pay for recordings of specific matches. That being said, I don’t think these two will be paying themselves off any time soon.”

Freddy chuckled. “I’d be shocked if they ever do.” He sighed, turning to Henry. “You were saying?”

“Right,” the man coughed. “There are other things that have become apparent in your previous match. For starters, given the beating you endured, your physical fortitude is clearly at an elite level, and your pain endurance is astonishingly high. You are also no doubt hiding your actual skills, given how much you’ve restricted your use of abilities. These things aren’t just true of your persona, they’re true for you, the man behind it.”

“So what you guys are saying is that I should just keep playing up this goofy persona while letting people slowly pick up on the fact that there’s more to me than just a terrible act?”

“Yes! I believe that would be an excellent approach.”

“Good to know.” He grinned. “Since that’s exactly what I was planning to do since the very start.”

***

Madame left soon after their talk, given she had more work to do, while Freddy remained with the advisor for another while. They went through the footage, with the man noting a huge number of small things Freddy should be doing differently.

For starters, letting a troll ramble on while standing there in silence had been a bad idea. Freddy had let him speak because he thought it would add to the drama, but clearly, all it did was give the man an opening to poke holes in Freddy’s act and ruin the whole thing.

He corrected a bunch of smaller mistakes as well, and warned Freddy about some things that might happen in the future.

It was crucial that he remained in control of the show, not just the fight.

Deciding to leave it there for the day, Freddy headed home, his patience nearing its end.

The brunt of his manic episode was already fading, but he was still in an aggressively emotional state. But he’d already lost two days, and if he kept missing classes at this pace, he’d fall too far behind to catch up.

The next day, as soon as he woke up, he chugged an extra large dose of sedatives and headed out.

His walk, which he usually just suffered through, irked him something fierce. He usually only kept the fifth-rank Trooper medal on his chest, which was enough to get people to acknowledge him, but not enough to stop them from being annoying.

And not enough to get them out of the way.

Despite the fact he was somewhat well known in certain circles, he was nowhere near famous in Starhold at large. The vast majority of pedestrians had no clue who he was, and that showed in how they treated him.

Badges were only a shorthand for one’s actual power. It wasn’t uncommon for people who excelled at one thing to leverage it to make themselves look better. And those who wore just a single high-ranked badge got plenty of stink-eye from passersby making nasty assumptions.

How did I ever tolerate this crap?

For the first time ever, he pulled his full set of badges out, slamming them on his chest in broad daylight.

The change was instant, and drastic.

Like a certain old-world religious figure parting the red sea, he split the crowd.

Those at the bottom rungs of Starhold were incredibly sensitive to high-ranking badges. Even a single spot of violet was enough to make most people flinch violently. A chest full of white? Nobody even dared look him in the eye.

This wasn’t the university, which had rules to protect the students, who were mostly rich elites to begin with. These were the streets. It wasn’t uncommon to find someone with low-ranking badges beaten to near-death because they had looked at someone more powerful than themselves the wrong way.

Nobody was going to be calling bullshit on him around here.

And that felt really, really good.

He made it to the university with a big smile on his face. And, of course, as soon as he stepped inside, went right back to being in a horrible mood.

The people mostly ignored him there, which he was fine with, but there was still the occasional spoiled prick who thought they could take cheap shots at him. And why did they believe that? Because they thought he was toothless. Hell, he had proven it to everyone by failing to stand up for himself.

When his emotions had been muted, he didn’t have the clarity of mind to notice what was happening.

He was being bullied.

Looked down upon.

Humiliated.

By a bunch of spoiled, rich brats who had gotten too comfortable with talking shit and not getting punched in the face.

His teeth were gritted so hard that his neck looked like it had roots growing under his skin.

“Hey there, geezer!” a particularly slappable-looking guy jeered. “Stop straining. You might shit yourself”

Freddy came to a sudden halt. He took a deep breath in an attempt to calm himself down. He failed miserably.

“What?” The jackass continued. “You wanna fight or something?”

“University rules for conflict among students,” Freddy recited, “in case of no injury, the offender owes the victim a lump sum of one million dollars; in case of light injury, the offender owes fifteen million dollars; in case of severe injury, the offending party faces expulsion.” Looking the annoying bastard dead in the eye, Freddy tapped his S-ranked Entrepreneur badge. “I can afford many fifteens of millions of dollars. How many light injuries can you take before you have no future as a combatant?”

At this point, a small crowd had gathered around them, whispers flying about.

The dumbass scoffed, his smile barely disguising his anger. “Are you threatening me right now?”

“Yes.”

“Oooh”-s and laughs sounded through the small audience.

The guy in question bared his teeth. “If you need money that badly, you could have just said so.” His fist suddenly lit up with earth essence, and a sneaky, quick punch headed straight for Freddy’s chin. It came to a sudden stop as Freddy caught him by the wrist.

In the blink of an eye, the guy flew back into the wall, his nose bleeding a river. He lost balance and tripped, falling face-first into the ground.

An all-too-happy Freddy walked up to him, grabbing him by the hair and lifting him as if he weighed no more than the clothes on his back. “Well… I wouldn’t mind a million dollars since you’re already offering.”

Squealing like a pig at the slaughterhouse, he swung a second attack at Freddy’s forearm. The Tectonic Strike landed, and regardless of Freddy’s toughness, that was a pretty heavy attack to take on undefended flesh. His skin was ruptured, muscle torn, and even his bone cracked.

Yet, he didn’t let him go. “Fifteen million?” Freddy grinned. “How generous.” Pulling his fist back, Freddy failed to restrain a wild laugh. “Say ‘oof’!” Freddy’s fist landed on the man’s stomach, despite his best attempt at defending himself. He released a strangled groan before getting thrown on the ground, where he parted with his lunch.

Freddy squatted beside him. At this point, the man didn’t even dare raise his head to look at him.

With complete composure, Freddy pulled his sleeve back, showcasing a pretty nasty bruise on his forearm. “I think that counts as a light injury.” He nodded. “Fifteen million bucks it is. You have a week. If I don’t have my money by then, I’ll be owing you fifteen million every single day until you stop showing up.”

Nodding his head, he muttered, “Okay… okay… Just let me go…”

Freddy got back up, kicked the man over into his own vomit for good measure, and then said, “Get out of my sight.”

Scrambling to his feet, the student fled the scene as if his life depended on it. And from Freddy could see, few judged him for it.

“What are you all staring at? Do you need some pocket money, too?”

The gathering dispersed pretty quickly.

Slowly raising his hand, he touched his cheek. His mouth was spread in a wide smile, one that suddenly died as he sobered up. What am I doing? He looked down at the mess of blood and puke on the ground. Why am I beating random kids up?

“No,” he asserted. That wasn’t the right question to ask. That was a grown man, and he needed to learn a lesson. What he really wanted to know was—

Why am I causing trouble for myself?

Clicking his tongue, he got moving. That wasn’t it, either. He had allowed things to go too far. It was one thing to keep his power hidden, and another to let himself get bullied by anyone and everyone. He had more self-respect than that. He needed to have.

But he couldn’t deny the roiling disgust at himself as he slowly made his way to the medical ward. He didn’t need healing, of course, but he’d prefer others thought he did.

Just as he was about to make his way in, he spotted the guy who he’d just beaten up make his way out. The dude looked utterly petrified as he turned and ran down the hallway.

And Freddy smiled. “Ah, shit…

“Why was that so much fun?”
