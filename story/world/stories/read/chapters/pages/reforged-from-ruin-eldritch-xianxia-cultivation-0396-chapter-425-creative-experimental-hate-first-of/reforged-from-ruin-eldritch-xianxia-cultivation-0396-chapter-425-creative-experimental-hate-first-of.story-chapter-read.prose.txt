The horde works pretty damn well as a quick, improvised tool.

Malformed, barely-functional, spliced together with Flesh Qi and blind stitching, each of them is little more than an amalgamation of limbs and pieces. Bits and surgically severed pieces of hundreds of different spiritual organs festoon each of them, harvested from the wounds that have been suffered by those in her body, added to by the keratinous growths that she’s implanted into them. They won’t survive more than an hour, and probably won’t be able to move themselves very much in the latter half of that time, but thirty minutes is plenty for suicidal bruisers designed to launch themselves at the enemy and then detonate.

Most of them scream while they do it. Comes with having misshapen lungs and adrenal glands the size of cats.

They only have as much neural tissue as is needed to throw themselves bodily at the Feng, no ability to suffer, no higher thoughts. No time to make them and no need.

They still scream as they die.

A cluster of explosives herds the leftmost killer into a fresh body, wrapping a dozen limbs around the Warrior realm cultivator to try and grapple him. His sheer existence is enough to rebuff the creature, the defenses on his cloak never even needing to activate to keep it from touching him, but it doesn’t need to. Before the Feng can dedicate the focus needed to cast it aside, it’s self-destructed. A dozen meridians purposely misaligned all flex at once, shooting discordant, chaotic Qi into reactive keratin crystals that multiply the discord.

The explosion also doesn’t hurt the higher-realm cultivator. It’s not supposed to.

It keeps them in place. Forces them to push aside the sudden flood of nonsensical concepts that’s trying to drown them, lest it affect their techniques. Only takes a half-second or so to wipe the concepts clean and replace them with violence and forestry, but that’s alright.

A half-second is enough time for the bombs.

Mutagenic super-matter, compressed beyond natural limits by Daos of Flesh and Space, explodes with the force of a miniature sun.

The echoing boom of the detonations is loud enough to crack stone and send some of the distant clouds a bit further back. It superheats the air to plasma, ignites the oxygen around it, and mutates further afterwards, Flesh Qi transforming each detonation into a steam of veins and roots and muscle made of energy that grabs and stabs and rips at its target.

And by the time it’s begun to fade, there’s already a half-dozen more amalgamated constructs launching themselves at her target.

It’s wonderful.

It feels like catharsis. Like screaming with desperation and anger and hate, letting it all out in a big burst that doesn’t stop. It relieves something deep down further than she can even remember she goes, scratching at a long-unresolved itch.

Some of the screaming mouths are laughing.

Their song gets louder, outputting enough volume to liquify parts of herself and crack the stone of the mountain all around. Each voice pursues a specific pitch, and there’s a sort of tug that she’s following. Like she can tell, instinctively, what hurts the most, and then follow the pain of her targets into a louder, more specific pitch. Against well-armored, high-realm foes, it’s not enough to kill, but against any lessers, she’s pretty sure she could find the right sound to shout someone out of existence.

And she can do the same for the bombs, and for the amalgamations. She has to feel for it, which is hard to focus on, but she can tell what hurts more, and then follow that down. More amalgamations are born, the combinations of spiritual organs getting more calculated based on responses.

And they carve and they cut and they slash at her, and the forest keeps trying to grow, but it’s in her body so it’s a part of her now and fuck the Fengs.

The battle stops being two-on-one as the Feng that fell earlier returns. His robes have transformed, going from cloth to something more like a liquid. They’ve fused with the advanced arrays on the armor-plating to become a set of midnight-black defenses, leaving the Feng featureless save for the family crest on his robes.

A slash that cuts the world severs a part of herself completely, turning the tip of her mountain-form into a falling meteor of flesh.

She makes more.

In the instant it takes, he’s arrived, landing on her surface like it’s horizontal beneath him and not a roiling mass of ever-changing landscape. She tries to expand around him, to absorb him, to know his Flesh as she knows her own- but the armor makes a slight hissing sound. Each attempt falls to nothing, and where he touches her, she turns grey, falling apart, lifelessly.

He kneels on her surface as a hundred tendrils and a dozen bombs fall at him, and punches.

The blow sends the entire structure of Raika back, rocking her hard enough to slam into the mountain. It cracks further, the hundreds of anchor-points she has coming apart as the mountain crumbles. The pillar, crawling up into the sky, cracks, a seam opening like a vertical canyon.

The fold in space stays right where it’s been, though it feels like she has to clench.

The other Feng, the one trying to be a forest inside her, takes the opportunity to advance. More of her turns from Flesh to Soil, from which sprout more trees in a continuous cycle. She devours and absorbs them while their roots dig through her defenses, churning meat and bone into dirt.

A fresh set of lungs opens and she screams.

Half of the trees disintegrate, and there’s a cry of frustration from the shadow hiding among them.

The armored Feng rears back and goes to hit her again-

A half-dozen bombs she’s moved beneath the patch of her he’s kneeling on explode, ejecting that part of her forcing back the night-black figure. He doesn’t move as far as he should, inertia and momentum somehow sliding off him, but it buys a few feet of space.

She is singing and laughing and there are so many things crawling from her.

She’s used up most of her harvested spiritual organs, though. Running low on those. She could just… harvest the little cultivators inside of her?

Hmm.

No mental blocks in that direction. No safeguards for shutdown. Makes it safer to think about, but… still. Something’s a bit uncomfortable about the thought.

She could figure it out, given a moment to think, but her enemies don’t allow her that.

The lead Feng, the one whose sword is vibrating hard enough to sing sharpness everywhere, enters the fray. His blade Cuts, its nature aligned further with the concept it’s using, and a rush of Qi floods the world as a hundred sword-edges cut down from a hundred directions.

His Jian warps, a screech of twisting metal cutting a few nicks into it from the attack, but the mountain that is Raika is torn open.

The blood alone is powerful enough, rich enough in Qi, that the stones of the mountain start to change. Supernatural capital-C Cuts are harder to heal, and might take minutes instead of milliseconds, but they will heal. His own blade has caused more damage to him than she’s managed to hit all three of them with.

The catharsis is starting to wear off. Not as fun, beating someone you hate to death, if they’re beating you harder.

Dao bends the world.
