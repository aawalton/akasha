Two weeks became three. I said life was tough, so here is the update,

I'll try to keep this as short as possible. A lot of authors hope to one day have being an author as their major source of income, most wish for financial freedom through it. I had that dream and have not yet achieved it. Might not. In my last post for those who read it, I stated that life is tough. It's simple. About five months ago, I got laid off, which means that for the last four months, writing has been my only source of income, which is--considering how much it makes--not so good. I have lived on it while looking for a job these past few months with no positive outcome. I am a person running low on funds at an alarming rate and such a thing ruins the mind slowly but surely. And, unfortunately, writing is heavily influenced by the state of mind, at least for me. And my books are doing quite poorly on Amazon, so that's another thing to think about. My apologies if this just sounds like unnecessary venting. It kind of feels like one.

After much consideration, I will be taking a one month break while I focus all my attention on finding a steady source of income. I will be back at the end of the month. I need money now more than ever. Unfortunately, that is the pattern of life. I'll focus on what I have to do now and hope my mental health survives the stress.

I will be back. August Intruder and Rise of the Living Enchantment will be back. I will drop another update before I resume the billing. I can only hope that you will all be willing to wait for me.

Thank you all for your understanding.
