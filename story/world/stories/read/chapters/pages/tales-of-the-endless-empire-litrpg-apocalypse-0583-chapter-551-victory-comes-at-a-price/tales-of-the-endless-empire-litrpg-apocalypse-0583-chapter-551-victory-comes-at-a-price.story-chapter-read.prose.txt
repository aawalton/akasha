Wer'ti had wanted to help Ter'tu as everything spiraled out of control, but there was this crazy dude with a chainsaw running after him while constantly screaming. The man wore a suit of armor that boosted his speed far beyond the norm. Normally, Wer'ti was a stone-cold fighter who never lost his composure. Well, there was a first time for everything, and for Wer'ti it came when Lucan, after seeing the leader shout a command at him, chose Wer'ti as his very first target.

The Chainsaw 6000 was Lucan's completely new design. Three meters long and with a chain speed of over one hundred meters per second, the Chainsaw 6000 was an utter monster, surpassed only by its twin brother, the Chainsaw 6000 Fire Edition. It looked exactly the same and had identical stats, except that it was constantly engulfed in flames. Lucan wore something akin to an iron suit, and a crystal-powered jet engine propelled him after the now panic-stricken Wer'ti.

Thanks to the special mufflers he had built into each chainsaw, they were extra loud and sounded absolutely terrifying. The fact that Lucan was also screaming like a maniac while chasing Wer'ti was simply too much for the second-in-command. Wer'ti completely forgot to use any skills and just sprinted along the wall while Lucan eagerly chased after him. Chasing the alien was so much fun that Lucan also forgot to activate his booster and movement skills to catch up.

For Ter'tu's base, it wasn't exactly a good look that the co-leader was running around half the fortress screaming, crying, and with eyes as big as saucers. After circling the base once, Wer'ti finally managed to gather his thoughts and turned around. As he did, he first saw one of his favorite D-grade mages being swallowed by a Sky Turtle. It was as if time itself had stopped as he watched his screaming leader—already covered in gruesome wounds and looking more like a mummy than a living being—being torn apart by countless razor-thin streams of water.

"Ter'tu, NOOOOO!" Wer'ti roared, only to be interrupted by a chainsaw slamming into the side of his shoulder. The Chainsaw 6000 also weighed several hundred kilograms, pinning Wer'ti against the stone wall while flesh exploded in every direction as the spinning chain carved its way downward.

"YEYEYEYE! OHHH! TAKAKAKAK!" Lucan screamed in a high-pitched voice over the deafening roar of the chainsaw and Wer'ti's agonized screams, which quickly came to an end.
