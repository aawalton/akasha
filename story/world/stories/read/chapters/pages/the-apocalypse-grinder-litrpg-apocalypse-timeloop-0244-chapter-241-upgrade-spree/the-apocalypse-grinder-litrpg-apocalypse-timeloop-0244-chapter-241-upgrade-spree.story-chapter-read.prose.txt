The great thing about where he’d chosen to start the branch path was that he had not yet had the meeting with the princess. It meant that he could make another specific request of her if he wanted to. He wasn’t sure he had anything particular in mind, though.

Actually…

Ronan felt he had a better idea than getting a bunch of new skills. He decided to ask the princess for more skill upgrade shards, and she was able to comply. However, she did mention that they were fairly rare and she wasn’t sure how many she would be able to give.

After checking, she realised she could give Ronan a total of 13 epic skill upgrade shards, which was far more than he’d expected. It put the total number of shards he had at 23, meaning he might even be able to push stupendous stats up to legendary rarity—something he couldn’t have imagined possible in the first iteration he possessed the newly alloyed skill.

She actually noted that his choice was a good one, as the chance of getting a useful new skill from a tome was pretty low, whereas upgrading an existing skill that one was experienced in using was a direct increase in power. Ronan felt that her logic was sound, though he was sure if she knew the sheer quantity of skills he possessed she might have a heart attack—or whatever the equivalent was for her species.

Ronan realised he had never actually used blueprint manual to check what it did. He’d read the description, of course, but that was different to using a skill. He quickly tested it out, thinking it might be related to the blueprints for buildings he’d received for his faction.

It turned out not to be the case. The skill actually saved blueprints for items to be crafted, which could be adjusted by the user. These blueprints, once created, would allow the crafter to perfectly replicate the listed item. The only faults would be caused by those existing in the original blueprint, and caused by the materials being low quality.

That would be an interesting skill to include in his alloyed crafting skill when he made it, and Ronan considered making it one of his candidates to upgrade. There were plenty of other more powerful skills to choose from, though, so it would be a tough decision.

In the end Ronan opted to upgrade some of his most-used and most powerful skills, choosing the others to ensure that he consumed every single one of the skill upgrade shards.

Ronan found himself sitting in the exact same spot as he had been the moment before he killed himself. Almost nothing had changed, with the exception of a 10 in brackets beside his level on his status. His level was the same, which meant that it would be the next 10 levels he earned that were taken as the penalty. It was harsh, but honestly a better price to pay than losing a skill or trait, as had happened in previous branch paths.

There was still about a week and a half to go until Ronan had to start the second step of the Path of the Gladiator. All that remained now was to use up the gladiator shards and see how far he could push himself.

This would also be the first opportunity he had to use his cultivation art analysis skill. It wouldn’t work for his soul cultivation art or his mind cultivation art due to their rarity being too high, but he might be able to improve the Nine Mouths Delta Mystic Energy Art or the Diamondback Gorilla Tempered Body Art. If that would speed up his cultivation even further it would be fantastic.

Ronan started with soul cultivation. It was the best one to begin with if he wanted to maximise his overall gains. He also wanted to try out his new legendary cultivation art. The Endless Wellspring Prismatic Soul Art wasn’t as complex as the legendary mind art had been.

Rather than requiring much conscious effort on his part, it seemed to fundamentally change the way he cultivated his soul. Rather than simply drawing the shard energy into his soul and using it to gradually empower it, the art did something rather unique.
