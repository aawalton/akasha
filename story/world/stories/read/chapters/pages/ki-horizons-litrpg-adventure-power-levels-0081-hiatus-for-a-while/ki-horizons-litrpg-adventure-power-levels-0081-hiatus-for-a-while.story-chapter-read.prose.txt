Hello everyone. I'm going to be going on a hiatus for a while. Basically, the reasons I had for writing (largely financial) have kinda, vanished. Not really in a positive way but that and a few others reasons that I write at all have, vanished. For one reason or another. Which has made writing feel like trying to fight through a thick fog and made me realize just how exhausted I am, of, many things but especially of writing. Like, why am I doing this to myself?

Long ago, the answer used to be "Because I really, truly, enjoy writing at least somewhat." and that, at least, hasn't been fully true in quite a while. So couple that reasoning being gone, with my other actual reasons for being gone, and I just really don't want to write anymore. For now. I'm just going to take a really strong, long, solid break.

Not just from this but from a whole variety of different things going on in my life. I'm putting a whole lot of different projects, goals, and responsibilities down for a while. Hopefully, one day, I will come back not exhausted by everything and everyone and even breathing. None of my stories are dead. I'm not going to stop for good. But there's simply no point in continuing if I am not actually happy with doing so. And it might take a long while to get back to that point.
