Myka P.O.V

There was obviously some nascent intelligence to the creatures as they launched their attack.

The town's defenders had gotten themselves into the best formation they could manage. So far, they'd done well enough to rotate out the injured. Speed wasn't exactly the forte of the lumbering beasts. However, it was increasingly clear this clash would leave casualties.

One of them was charging up the central path, the funnel the townspeople had created for such situations. The other had come in behind the barricade, where it would soon have access to everyone sheltering there.

Just as the first creature was reaching the front lines, the second lillow had begun its attack. A viney fist shot out from the torso of the creature behind the barricade, heading straight towards a group of people.

No, its aim wasn’t the people, it was the food.

Myka could feel the mana wafting off it and couldn't help but wonder. Were they actually seeking out people because they were aggressive, or perhaps territorial, or were they just heading for the highest concentrations of mana, and attacking humans was a sort of unfortunate byproduct.

Myka couldn’t dwell on the thought for long. The beast was upon them.

People dodged out of the way, Ms. Maggie stood her ground, erecting a barrier of dense mana Myka couldn't help but admire.

The fist struck the barrier once, twice.

The second creature reached the juncture of the road, close enough to attack the defenders. Its own fist lashed out, and the obvious melee fighter rolled out of the way.

Both fists came careening down towards their targets.

And stopped dead.

End of Myka P.O.V.

<-…->

The creatures were as stunned as the rest of the combatants. For a moment, the battlefield was quiet. The creatures had even forgotten for a moment to continue screeching in that awful warbling way of theirs.

It was like the whole town was waiting for what came next. Then.

"Hello, Ms. Margaret," came a voice.

That's when they heard the click, click, clicking of a cane against asphalt.

"Oh, my darling." Ms. Maggie grinned, turning to face the south entrance, where Scarlet was approaching at a leisurely pace. "How have you been?"

"Could be better, dear. Could be better."

As though offended they were being ignored, the creatures finally screamed once more.

Only, they didn't move.

Rather, they couldn't move.

They tried to pull their arms back, but while their bodies could move backwards, their vines simply continued extending from the point at which they were held. Like a particularly ugly rubberband, the fists they'd lashed out with remained locked in place.

Scarlet finally reached the familiar older woman. Ms. Maggie was standing by an enormous pot of something that smelled delicious. But more than that, Scarlet could tell it was absolutely packed with mana.

Scarlet offered Ms. Maggie a small but sincere smile.

Two warm hands immediately reached out and cupped Scarlet’s cheeks.

“Haven’t I told you to call me Ms. Maggies?” Ms. Maggie asked.

Scarlet just shook her head, the soft, warm fingers against her cheeks strangely comforting in the way she imagined family was supposed to be.

“I knew that boy was full of it. Comin’ in with that fancy truck full of people and weapons, and you nowhere in sight.”

“Apologies for the delay. I had a spot of business to sort out, and you seemed like you were handling the situation well enough.”

“Folks gotta handle their own business, else they won’t know how to grow.”

“Fair’s fair.” Scarlet nodded.

Ms. Maggie nodded back.

Scarlet wasn’t surprised at all. Ms. Maggie was one of the most capable people she’d ever met, and she ran her diner with the same military precision Scarlet could see around her now.

The stomping of the beasts and another lash of vines pulled Scarlet away from the moment. She glanced at the creatures, then at the defenders, who were now regrouping in confusion.

“Let me clean up here,” she said to Ms. Maggie, gesturing to the group of people and the two very irate beasts.

A quick Investigate had already informed her that the food Ms. Maggie had been defending was well worth it. It carried minor regeneration buffs, and whatever Skill or Skills she had used must have been excellent, as the regeneration worked for mana, stamina, and health recovery.

“We have quite a bit to talk about, you and I. And I have a gift I believe you’ll appreciate.”

A mental poke had Maus crawling up from inside Scarlet's shirt.

She gave him a few instructions before wandering out onto the active battlefield. She'd seen Myka, seen what he'd done, and her mind was blossoming with ideas and potential applications.

The steady clack, clack, clack of her cane announced her arrival onto the battlefield proper. Space opening around her as she moved.

She had the two creatures firmly locked down now, and most of the defenders were watching as they slammed themselves against the invisible walls of the telekinetic cages Scarlet erected around them.

Scarlet mapped the emotions of the battlefield as she made her way towards Myka. Gratitude, confusion, relief, attraction, fear – overwhelming amounts of fear – and so much more.

After the Training World, Scarlet found it almost trivial to read surface emotions, especially when those she was reading had almost no defences against her.

Ignoring Kain's very loud, though well concealed annoyance, Scarlet focused on Myka's clean happiness. There were traces of other emotions mixed in the background, sublayers in his aura. She could read frustration, a small sense of inadequacy, that lingering attraction she'd sensed the first time she'd met him. But above all, he was happy. Happy about what? She didn't know.

It didn’t matter as she made her way to him and smiled. Sparing a nod for her half-brother as she went.

"Hey." Myka’s voice broke. He cleared his throat and tried again. "Hi, Scarlet. Did you– Is that,” he pointed at the caged lillows. “Are you doing that?"

"I am," she said. "And you did that." She pointed at the mutated corpse of the dead lillow.

"Yeah."

"Do you believe you could take these ones down as well?"

Myka looked at the pair and shook his head. "No, that's... I think that might be a bit beyond me," he admitted.

"Come now. You don't lack power."

"I'm tapped out."

"Easy enough to remedy,” she said. May I?"

She lifted her hand and, when he nodded, placed it against his back. It was a technique she'd first experienced in the Training World. She’d requested the Historian teach her. He’d been agreeable, as it was an excellent training exercise, but he had been unwilling to allow Scarlet to practice on himself. While Maus was an excellent training partner, they had very similar mana signatures.

Working with Myka would give her a chance to practise her fine control while hopefully giving him a chance to learn something new.

"I’ll need you to relax,” she said. “Are you okay for me to help?” she asked. When he nodded, she began gently rubbing circles on his back, the gesture obscuring what she was truly doing. She began pushing soothing signals outward. Manipulating her psionics, she coaxed him slowly, feeling his aura fluctuate as it interacted with her power.

Once what was left of his internal energy was docile, and flowing smoothly, Scarlet began the mana transfer.

Trickles of her mana slowly flowed into him. She started with the bare dregs so as not to overwhelm his much less developed channels and Mana Heart, slowly increasing her output as she felt for his limits.

"That's..." he grunted. "That's not the most comfortable..."

Ignorance, in his case, truly was bliss. He had no idea just how much access he’d given her. The damage she could do to him was catastrophic.

She reached out to his aura again, intending to help, only, his reaction was not what she’d expected.

"This is actually kind of nice," he said, voice hoarse.

That was interesting. The times the Historian had done this for her had been unpleasant at best, and more often than not, deeply invasive. Perhaps it was less a matter of what was happening and with whom it was happening. That, or the disparity between her and the Historian was simply so wide that the mana transfer had been uncomfortable to process, like scouring your skin to get clean or using rubbing alcohol to cleanse a wound.

Scarlet filed the thoughts away for a future experiment.

It didn't take long before Myka was topped up, and Scarlet wondered what his Mana Stat was. He had more in reserve than she'd initially anticipated, and now that he was back at full capacity, it was time to try out her idea. If it worked, Myka might end up being one of the most terrifying people present.
