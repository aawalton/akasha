Don immediately used Devour on Morshoff. He was literally inside of it and bleeding dry, with under a minute to spare. His vision swam and his body was in unadulterated agony.

[500 Evolution Points acquired!]

[Evolution trait “Boss”: Consumed]

[Health at 100%]

[Energy at 100%]

[Debuffs: N/A]

Then one of the notifications changed: [Evolution trait “Boss”: Evolved!]

Don drank in Morshoff’s essence. It was potent, almost dangerous. It wasn’t like the energy bucked as Don drank it. But there was a sinister edge to it. Don could appreciate that. Morshoff had been sinister as fuck.

The innards turned gray and the shell shriveled and dried up. Morshoff was now brittle and white as chalk, bereft of all life.

Don crawled out of the monster and grabbed the shining crystal casket inside. Then he jumped on the edge of the swamp.

He put the casket down and just breathed it in. Breathed in the fact that he was alive. He had done the impossible. Don wasn’t sure there had been any other way to defeat the monstrosity. It had been strong, sporting an almost impenetrable defense. But it hadn’t expected someone to be crazy enough to dive deep into the swamp.

It had been really goddamn stupid. But Don’s instincts had paid off.

Don sat down by the edge of the pool and watched the ruined body of his enemy.

His heart was still hammering. Even though his mind was getting used to the risk and pain, his body wasn’t. He figured that was probably good. It would learn in time, but the body’s job was to keep Don alive. It was telling him that was stupid.

Don shrugged to himself. He would probably do it again.

He found something almost spiritual in fighting until his legs gave out. Taking no backsteps. Going absolutely all in.

Yes, it was stupid. But it was what had made him. Only through this absolute, reserveless intensity, had he grown so fast and come so far.

He breathed in the wet air. It tasted like victory.

Don’s racing mind was already thinking of the next moves.

Get out of the dungeon, go fight Blazeclaw. He managed a chuckle. He couldn’t imagine any other result to his next fight. Absolute domination.

If Jacques hadn’t killed the bear already, Don would make short work of it.

And if Jacques had beaten him to the punch… Well, Don would have to kill him.

He didn’t particularly care about the psycho’s stupid games right now. Sure, Mal’asha wanted them to make sport with each other. But Don was done playing.

If he had to relinquish his hard earned System points to that asshole, there was going to be a problem. Either Mal’asha would have to force Don or he would kill the hunter.

Don observed his thoughts. The shift in mindset was interesting. He had been much more wiling earlier to negotiate and see where things went.

No longer.

He had no incentive to. The strong don’t negotiate with the weak. The strong just take what they want and do what they want.

Sure, Don wasn’t the strongest. He would still need to seek out stronger foes, so that he could grow and evolve.

But he was damned if he listened to a single goddamn thing some weak human had to say.

Don was done with humans. They were weak, frail and stupid. Maybe he would find some worthy people in his journeys. But he would be discerning.

He decided he would check on Charlie. If the girl had managed to pull herself together, Don would teach her a thing or two. He wasn’t going to babysit anyone, but it didn’t hurt to have powerful allies.

As for the hunter… A mad dog that needed to be put down. While Don was mostly indifferent about humans, he wouldn’t suffer someone making sport with their lives. It disgusted him.

The weak should be left to their own devices, not to be abused. Abusing the weak was a waste of time. Not to mention it would soften his mind.

True power came from challenging stronger foes. True strength came from overcoming, not indulging.

I’ve come such a long way… The weak wishy-washy Don has sloughed off. Now I am something else. I feel powerful, confident, ready for anything. Challenges do not scare me, they fill me with thrill.

Don looked at Morshoff’s withered shell. This had been a milestone. But now, Don was sort of directionless. Sure, he would go deal with the Jacques-situation. But that was no threat. There was no challenge in there. Don seriously doubted the hunter had managed to outpace him. Don had become monstrously strong in a very short time. If the humans were still trying to survive in the forest, they were playing a different game entirely.

Don had to ask himself the real question.

What do I want?

Easy. To get stronger. Find new enemies. Surpass himself. Keep evolving.

First he would deal with Blazeclaw and get Jacques’s points. Depending on what he could acquire from the dispensers, he would plot his next move. Finding another dungeon could be fun. Maybe he could traverse through the tutorial zone to see if there was anything worthwhile there. Maybe the tutorial was larger than he thought, and there were monsters and bosses beyond level 20.

He doubted anything at or below level 15 could pose a challenge to him currently. He just knew he would cut through Blazeclaw with his immense strength like paper.

Mal’asha probably had some bullshit planned for Don too. But he would deal with that. He seemed to have a good relationship with the ancient eldritch monster god. Besides, it was his strength, the deity respected, not his winning smile. So as long as Don kept finding ways to increase his strength, he would keep that monkey off his back.

It was simple. Find fights, get stronger.

Don sighed contently and got up. He liked it simple. He really liked this new reality and who he had become in it. It was time to go find new adventures.

“But before that…” Don muttered and his mouth spread into a feral grin. The crystal box at his feet illuminated the dim swamp. It was beckoning Don. And Don would indulge.

“It’s time to check out the loot and upgrades!”

He had so many. The Boss evolution trait notification was waiting for him. There was an evolution choice from level 15, and he could most likely upgrade something with the infusion of 500 Evolution points. Not to mention the loot box seemed larger than the previous two he had opened.

Don felt like a kid on Christmas. He opened the crystal lootbox and his jaw dropped.
