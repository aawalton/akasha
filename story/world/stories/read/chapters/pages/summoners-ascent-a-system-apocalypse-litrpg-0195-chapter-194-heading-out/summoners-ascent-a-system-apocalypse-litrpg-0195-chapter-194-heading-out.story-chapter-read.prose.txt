The heavy wooden gate was pushed open with a loud creak. Even if it seemed sturdy enough to hold out against monster attacks, it was clear that building something like this, particular at this scale, was out of everyone's area of expertise.

But it was getting better already. And this would only keep getting more and more impressive. Ashton was positive that by the next time he came here, he was going to be met with something even sturdier. Hell, once the next quest started, every single camp would change rapidly anyway.

Ashton stood there, in front of the gate, waiting for Millie to say goodbye to her friends a few steps away. She was hugged tightly by Sean and Joey, and the three of them even cried a little. Mostly Sean, actually.

"Oh come on, we'll see her again soon!" Joey said, slightly hitting the boy with her elbow.

"Yeah, but..." Sean sniffled, "... we don't know when... And it's really scary out there..."

"It's fine, I'm with Ashton. You know how strong he is," Millie reassured him as well.

"But I heard there's giants out there! And a dragon!" Sean wiped the snot from his nose as he turned to Ashton. "Can you really fight things like that?"

Ashton grinned lightly and shrugged. "I've faced a couple of those in my time."

Of course, this was in Lumia and Ashton had no chance in hell to currently face off against either a giant or a dragon, but he figured he didn't need to say that right now.

"Wait, really?" Sean let out, and the other two were also looking at Ashton excitedly.

"Yeah, they were a little tough though, I guess. I had my eyebrows fully scorched off by a fire dragon once," Ashton said, covering his brows with his hand.

"Really? Then why do you still have them?" Sean asked with a suspicious frown. "My brother said that his friend Billie shaved his eyebrows off for a bet and they never grew back!"

"No, eyebrows grow back all the time!" Joey protested, shaking her head. "They're like a moustache for eyes."

"Hm... I don't know about that." Sean sighed. "I think Ashton's just lying."

"No way, if Ashton says that he fought a dragon, then he definitely did," Millie insisted, pushing her hand onto her hip.

With a slight sigh, Ashton dropped his hand onto Millie's head. "Don't start fighting now. I'll prove it with time, alright?"

Sean narrowed his eyes and slowly nodded, not letting go of his suspicious gaze. "If you say so."

"Mm-hmm," Ashton smiled, ruffled up Millie's hair, and then turned. "Come on, it's time to go."

Millie nodded and gave her friends another hug. A long hug, that none of them wanted to let go of. But the moment it was over, Millie turned and ran over to Ashton's side, excited to go.

She turned around one more time when they got to the other side of the gate, waving at Joey and Sean.

Rory stood next to them, and briefly nodded at Ashton right as the gate began to be shut. They had already spoken about everything they needed to, so this was enough.

And then, the heavy wooden gate was closed.

For the first time since the start of all this, Millie was outside the schoolgrounds, and it was almost unrecognisable compared to before. There were leftovers from the monster camp that had blocked the school's entrance a while ago as well, but it must have been a while since Millie was able to see anything else. Especially once the walls had been raised.

"Poppy," Ashton said, pulling out a small piece of candy from the small pouch on his belt, "could you cast the speed buff on Millie?"

The pixie immediately nodded. She held her hands forward and cast her spell. Petals of a large flower raised up from the ground and briefly enveloped Millie. She was slightly startled, but she knew that this was on purpose, so she calm down quickly.

"Woah... that feels weird," Millie pointed out, looking down at herself. "So I'm faster now?"

"Mm-hmm. It boosts your 'speed' buff. It improves reaction speed, muscle retraction and extension, and really anything related to how 'fast' you are," Ashton explained. "Speaking of, what level are you with your class right now?"

"Uhm... 2? I only got it because you told me to, it's not like I actually want to be a 'Gardener'," Millie shuddered. "It's fun and all, but no thanks."

Poppy, who was sitting on Ashton's head by now, lowered her head sadly.

"Ah! I do really like flowers though! They're so pretty! I was the one taking care of the poppies in the garden before too!" she pointed out, rushing to cheer Poppy up immediately. Ashton smiled a little and softly knocked a knuckle onto her head.

"Don't worry, she's just playing around. Don't worry about your class, this was just to avoid getting you weakened. The good thing about kids' classes is that they don't lower the requirements to get other classes, so we can just test out your ability and find the right thing for you," Ashton suggested as he began to stretch a little. Seeing what he was doing, Millie seemed to try and do the same. "You have a pretty big talent for magic, so we'll work on that first and foremost. And as your magical talents blossom, I'm sure your innate ability is going to sprout some new leaves as well."

Millie looked up at Ashton, slightly narrowing her eyes. "Why do you talk like that?"

"Hm?"

"Your innate ability is going to sprout some new leaves, who says stuff like that?" Millie asked, shaking her head with a sigh. "Zoomers," she added in the way that only a child could judge an adult.

Ashton felt a slight twitch in the corner of his mouth. "Shut up and let's just get going."

He began to walk forward, and Millie followed behind. Usually, Ashton would be running right now, but he obviously couldn't do that right now. There was no way that Millie could actually catch up to him if he did. So he was just walking a relatively casual pace that she could easily keep up with using Poppy's speed buff.

That didn't mean that it wasn't going to take a while to get back to Calibur, though. Maybe that was for the best, though. There were a lot of things that Ashton had to introduce Millie to.

For one... Monsters. The children of the primary school had been relatively sheltered, even if they had to directly face the zombies not even that long ago. He was sure that Millie would be fine, but being surrounded by tons of monsters could be scary for adults, let alone a young child.

In the future, all of that would likely change anyway. It would be unavoidable to come in contact with monsters in some way, even children that had been relatively safe from the start.

And for Millie, that 'future' was coming today. Just about twenty seconds from now, actually, considering the sound that one of those orcs was making. Those pig-like grunts really echoed through these streets considerably well.

Ashton clicked his finger and called on the gnome and the salamander. They were the best for defence. The gnome by letting Millie be enveloped by rock if necessary, and the salamander since almost every being is scared of fire to some degree. Monsters would hesitate to approach Millie if Ashton placed flames near her.

"Stay close to those two," Ashton instructed her, and she slowly nodded, tightly clutching the strap of her bag.

Ashton stood there with the metal bar that he was using as a weapon for the time being. It was weak and wouldn't be a permanent solution, but he was pretty sure that the weapon left behind by the Mask Maker and the others was too expensive to buy right away.

Then again, Ashton hadn't actually checked yet. He wanted to be back at Calibur when he called the Salesman again, so that he could maybe, potentially, borrow a little extra money from his dearest best friend Sam to bridge some gaps.

By now, since Calibur had quite a lot of people going out to hunt regularly, they must have a pretty solid amount of money. Sam didn't need Ashton to provide any more money for the community posts, either.

Well, whichever the case, for now, Ashton just had to make his way there, and this time with a kid in tow. It would take a while, especially since they couldn't move that fast, but at least the monsters could be taken care of easily.

Ashton cycled his internal energy through his body, particularly focusing on the muscles that he would need for simple strike attacks. He coursed the energy through himself and into his staff. For now, he was going to try and fight without the elementals, so that he could get used to this.

Not that he actually needed the support from either the elementals or his techniques just to beat a measly orc anyway.

He spun his staff around a few times to build up momentum, as the group of four orcs approached. They had started picking up the pace the moment they noticed that there were people here. Ashton couldn't see them yet, but the noise of their equipment and their footsteps was truly far too loud.

Ashton had also infused his internal energy into his inner ear so that he could pick up on even subtle sounds far more easily. It was maybe a bit unnecessary, but he should really get used to this sensation as much as he could. The sensory improvements caused by stats was pretty comfortable, but it could be glaring when it came through techniques or magic. Especially if Ashton planned on increasing the grade of his internal core as fast as he could, he had to make sure something like this didn't bother him.

Before long, the first Orc stepped into his range. It was fairly large, but not threatening. It was more fat than tall, anyway, though you could still see the bulging muscles come out through those log-like arms and legs and the rounded belly.

If it managed to hit Ashton, it would probably hurt him quite a lot, but it wasn't as though that would happen anyway.

The moment the monster got into range, without Ashton even needing to move from his spot, he struck upward at the orc's arm. It broke in a pretty ugly way. Since at this point, Ashton's energy was so low, he wasn't able to concentrate it enough to cause a clean break, so the orc's bone must have shattered.

Before the monster's weapon could even fall to the ground, and before it could realise that it had been injured, its skull was already completely smashed in with a single strike to the nose. The internal energy strengthened the impact and invaded the monster's brain through the injury, completely scrambling it and killing it instantly.

And as the front-runner of the group, the seemingly strongest, fell, the other three orcs stared at Ashton with even more fury in their eyes.

But even that would be extinguished without Ashton needing to take even a single step forward.
