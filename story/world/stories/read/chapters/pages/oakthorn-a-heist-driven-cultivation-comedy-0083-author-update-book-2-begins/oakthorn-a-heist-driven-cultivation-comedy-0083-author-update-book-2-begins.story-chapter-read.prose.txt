Oakthorn Book 2

SOME SECRETS ARE WORTH DYING FOR.

Between sneezing lava and becoming a wanted fugitive, master thief Cade Stormhollow somehow ended up as a babysitter to a giggling goddess.

Suffice to say, the heist did not go according to plan.

Now, he’s on the run. The goddess of Destruction is hunting him, bounty hunters want his head, and even his own magic is trying to kill him. The explosive power tearing through his veins grows stronger every day, and he's running out of time before it rips him apart completely. Worse yet, he's hiding the truth about their newest immortal teammate from everyone—including her.

The Clover Clan offers him a way out of this mess: steal the legendary Soulkeeper's Crown from the nightmare-infested Blightmarshes. The crown's power could stabilize his magic, the payout could buy his freedom, and the job could get him off every divine radar.

Of course, the Blightmarshes are quarantined for a reason—and Cade’s crew isn’t the only one who wants the prize.

When ancient horrors wake up and their competitors become something far worse than a rival, Cade faces an impossible choice: reveal the truth about his divine companion and watch the realms burn, or keep lying and lose everything he's trying to protect.

Chaos was supposed to be his greatest weapon. Now it might be his undoing.

OAKTHORN 2 continues the pulse-pounding saga with higher stakes, deadlier enemies, and secrets that could shatter everything Cade holds dear. This explosive second installment delivers magical progression, devastating betrayals, and the kind of impossible choices that define true heroes. Perfect for readers who love heists, complex anti-heroes, evolving magic systems, and found family bonds tested by forces beyond imagination.
