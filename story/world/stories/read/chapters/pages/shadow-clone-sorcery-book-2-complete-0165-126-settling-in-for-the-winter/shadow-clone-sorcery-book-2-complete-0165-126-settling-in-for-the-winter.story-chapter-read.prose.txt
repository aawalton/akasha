But the regular idiots on the street can’t tell the difference between a masterwork—” He threw up air quotes. “—and a true master’s work. We capitalize on that.

“I don’t know anymore, but they’re buying the new Flukas,” the Stalker answered. “He’s drawn away a good deal of the spies watching us. Things are going to be challenging until we get more clones.”

The Stalker was correct. Lukas now only had room for three ordinary clones. He had one Stalker keeping an eye on the property and another following the new Flukas around. Then there was a Stormtrooper training in the basement. Clones with unique shard abilities took up the rest of the slots.

The Aquila guild was now extra wary of Lukas, but they hadn’t yet caught onto Morph. In fact, the clone had been promoted since the recent incident. Several of their high-ranking and key members had been arrested or were currently being interrogated. They needed more field teams to keep taking jobs and protect their interests in the surrounding region. Because of his brilliant service and skill, Morph, now a provisional tier-four member, was putting together a new team from a pool of low-ranking members and new recruits. Following Ogden’s instructions, Lukas had Morph approach the Aquila council and ensure there were no more people tied to Ephram.

Ace had finally been given the freedom to practise his luck-stealing and burning abilities in the Gray’s gambling dens and was making a tidy profit. He was pleased with his new enchanted snow goggles and wide-brimmed hat. They, apparently, made him feel like a modern cowboy. Ace used Shade’s Mantle to shape himself a beard and long shaggy hair, making himself somewhat unrecognizable. Lukas couldn’t complain.

Not much had changed for Eins. The clone appeared happy to be off the battlefield and setting up his new, much bigger, and better-equipped workspace. He appeared excited to get back to space, but also had started to complain about the lack of helping hands. He wanted a clone assisting him at all times.

Next came the new Zwei. The name was no longer attached to a mage with the Spellweaver ability. Instead, he had a new, unique ability. Shard of the Chronicler’s Mind ability had proven perfect.

Arcane Chronicling: The clone will gain the power to perfectly memorize all text, inscriptions, runescripts, and physical knowledge. He will gain the power to recall and perfectly reproduce them at a moment’s notice or imbue another with the knowledge. The clone will also gain access to all attunements and familiars connected to his soul and may call forth copies of them at any time.

The Body and Heart abilities didn’t compare. The first focused on generic physical improvements and on channeling theoretical knowledge into practical skills. Unfortunately, the ability didn’t function with magic. Meanwhile, the product of the Shard of the Chronicler and the Heart Pillar resulted in powers related to learning, seeking knowledge, a grimoire familiar, and the ability to manipulate its ink and pages.

Arcane Chronicling appeared to do far more than either. Perfect memorization and recall of knowledge, and sharing it with others on its own, felt too weak for a shard that had come from a quest of its caliber. However, the ability to access all attunements and copy familiars made it too potent to ignore. Lukas’s suspicions had proven correct. The clone had no trouble reproducing the life attunement and even the same energy generated by Joybringer’s aura. When Lukas and the new Zwei summoned a pair of Assistant Spindles simultaneously, he knew the decision was the correct one.

It appeared the spies tailing Lukas tied the familiars’ presence to the original him. As a result, Spellweaver and now the Chronicler could pretend to be him. The clone lacked metamagic, but could very well walk the path of wizardry on its own.

Currently, Lukas had the clone functioning as an enchanter and student of wardcraft, but that would eventually change. He suspected the new Zwei would eventually become one of his best assets. But it would take time and more clones.

Finally, there was the new Flukas. He no longer had access to Spellweaver and Assistant Spindle, but Joybringer more than made up for it. The clone was more annoying than Ace, but his aura and eighty years of experience with people had taught him how to command a room, holding attention and charming people. Lukas hoped that eventually he or Zwei would gain the power to grant Assistant Spindle to other clones. It would help sell the lie better.

Recent events have resulted in countless invitations, ranging from simple dinners to military ceremonies and even balls. Lukas could neither attend them all nor intend to attend them all. He mostly accepted summons from the city’s best and most powerful and sent Flukas to most of them.

Attention from potential amorous partners had also significantly improved. Lukas had no interest in anything so soon after his meeting with Minarv, but he didn’t stand in the way of the clones having fun.

“Forever short on clones.” All the clones frequently grumbled about the shortage. Lukas was nowhere near the next tier but had gained a rank the previous evening after creating an Illusionist specialization.

Creating new clone specializations became Lukas’s new focus. He was aware that rank growth slowed down significantly after the third tier, but the next rank of Empowered Copy felt incredibly close. He had no idea what it would offer, but each of the previous offerings was a substantial power-up.
