Night settled over the desert, the temperatures dropping low. Caspian, who had been dying from the heat, looked relieved for about half an hour, but once it got really cold, he began shivering and scuttled closer to Liora. Cee and Smokey soon began to shiver too and got closer to her.

"Am I a heater or something?" Liora asked in annoyance, her eyes locked onto the tent.

She wanted to know what was going on inside. Renir and Aenir seemed to be talking quietly among themselves. Liora stretched her neck, trying to hear their words, but they were too quiet. Could she perhaps get a skill to improve her hearing?

She always felt nervous when Renir wasn't directly within her grasp, but she felt even more nervous now. Not because Aenir would do anything to Renir. No, she believed that Aenir was only trying to look out for his younger brother. He appeared to do his best with the methods available to him.

But Renir clearly didn't agree with those methods. He was a perfectionist; Liora was sure of that, so Renir always sought the most optimal solution. Except when it came to battles. Then he just burned everything to hell with his magic.

"You are the only one with feathers!" Cee pointed out, rubbing against Liora's chest feathers. "Share your warmth with us!"

"Fine, fine! Just stop pushing against me so much!" Liora said, opening her wings.

Caspian instantly pressed himself against her left side, as did Cee, somehow positioning herself beneath the merman. They both sighed with relief once she settled her left wing over them. Smokey pressed himself against her right side, and she covered him with her right wing as much as she could.

The wyvern's scales were incredibly warm, and Liora felt the desert cold retreat a little. This was actually pretty cozy. At that moment, Liora felt relieved to be together with everyone again.

This is my family now, isn't it?

The thought surprised her. After all, what kind of weird family even were they? But all they had in this world was each other. Only Renir was missing from their little huddle group?

Was he part of their little family too? Liora would have liked to think yes, but she wasn't sure what the man himself believed. After all, he wasn't quite the same as everyone else. They were the rejects of this world, not quite belonging anywhere except with each other.

But Renir was different. He was the Pillar of Lost Magic. He was one of the rulers of Velithra. He had Elites working for him and people who needed him. Sure, he wasn't the most liked Pillar, but he seemed to take his duties quite seriously.

Why am I even worried about this? How silly!

Liora shook her head and settled to wait. That was all she could do at the moment. The warmth radiating from Caspian, Cee, and Smokey slowly lulled her to sleep.

"AAAAAAH!" A horrifying scream startled Liora and everyone else awake.

"What's going on?" She jumped to her feet, looking around frantically.

Flashes of light came from the tent, as well as powerful gusts that made the tent flap madly and sent sand everywhere.

"Renir!" Liora called out.

Another scream, this time so loud, so pained, Liora felt like her own heart was being stabbed. Had Aenir done something after all?

Against the sand and wind, Liora pushed herself closer to the tent. As the fabric flapped, she could briefly glimpse what was going on inside. Just like it had been during the previous ritual, Renir was shining so brightly it was impossible to look at him directly.

Aenir was standing in the tent, his arm raised, eyes squeezed shut as he continued chanting something louder and louder. Light flashed and crackled. Liora forced herself to move closer.

Renir was in so much pain. Even more than he had been before. Liora felt incredibly useless at that moment, because she knew that she could do nothing to help him. Nothing to make him feel less pain. All Liora and the others could do was watch the process.

Aenir was clearly struggling too. Sweat beaded on his forehead, and magic seemed to crackle around him, almost as if trying to resist. But he persisted and finished his incantation. Magic burst out from Renir in waves, throwing everybody back.

"Renir?!" Liora called out, jumping to her feet and rushing inside the tent.

Aenir had been thrown on the ground, groaning in pain, not quite able to pull himself up to his feet.

"Oww… Why is it that it hurts more every time, but I still can't feel anything else afterwards?" Renir groaned.

Liora looked at him, taking in his new appearance. He once again became larger. He was likely larger than Liora's magpie form now. His body was also taking on a more serious appearance. If previously Renir appeared like an adorable little gem monster, now he was more like a mini gem golem.

His body and head were more decently proportioned to his limbs. His legs especially were bulkier, although they were still rather short. His hands had five full fingers, and he even had a proper elbow joint. Although Liora had no clue how these joints worked if his whole body was made from hard material. Must be magic.

His face wasn't just a glowing pair of eyes on the same prism, but resembled a rough face carved into a gem. His eyes had sockets filled with glowing crystal, and his mouth moved in a strange manner. As if thousands of small gem pieces were shifting to align with his speech. But he had no nose or ears or anything resembling hair, of course.

"Are you okay?" Liora asked, although she was wondering how heavy he was. Carrying him around her neck would likely be impossible.

"Yeah, I think I'm fine…" Renir muttered. His voice actually sounded deeper and slightly distorted. "Ugh, but breaking two seals in such quick succession is actually quite taxing. My body has changed a lot."

"You didn't even have to do anything," Aenir groaned, as he tried to pull himself up to his feet. Rather unsuccessfully, as he slumped back into the sand. "By the magical weave, those seals are insane… I had to use all of my mana for that. I don't remember the last time I ran out of mana. It's been decades."

"Yeah, each seal is more powerful than the next," Renir nodded, then shook his head. "I don't like how my voice sounds now."

"Me neither. How come it changed?" Liora asked, carefully examining his body. Although he was more monstrous, his body was still made up of the same amber crystal as before, making him shine and glimmer even in the darkness. Imagine how beautiful he would be in the sunlight.

"I suspect it's because previously my voice was produced magically, because I didn't actually have vocal cords or anything that could naturally produce sound. Now I am more akin to a golem. They are still beings made up mostly of magic, but there are some mechanical parts involved in the process. Hmm… I think this might be worth studying."

"I don't think we have time for that," Liora said. "We need to go up that mountain and solve the issue with the river."

Aenir grunted again. "Not today. Everyone needs to rest. Renir, you need to get used to your new body. More of your magic should be unlocked now, but… I don't know if it'll be enough. Remember that you won't be able to use external magic in the Titanspike Mountains."

"Yes, yes. I know! You don't have to remind me," Renir grumbled, clearly peeved.

"Is there any chance you could remove more of Renir's seals?" Cee asked, scuttling into the tent. She and Caspian had been hanging back until the process was finished.

"Not anytime soon…" Aenir muttered. "It'll take some time for me to recover my mana. Also, from what I gleaned, my incantation method only worked on a single seal. The remainder are going to require different methods."

Liora found herself to be filled with relief. That meant that they would still travel together for a good while.

"We'll worry about the remaining seals after we solve this issue. We have to stop the war from breaking out between the antkin and Azhar Sultanate. Tomorrow, we leave at dawn."

Aenir hobbled out of the tent soon after, promising to come the next morning, while Liora and the group remained awake for a bit longer, looking at Renir. He was walking around, trying to adjust to this new body.

It was far more mobile than before. While he was still clumsy and painfully slow, he could walk without toppling over. Given a bit of adjustment time, he would likely be able to walk faster than Caspian. Which admittedly wasn't a high bar to beat.

The biggest change was that he wasn't as defenseless as before. He could fight back both physically and magically. A punch from a monster made entirely of rock was bound to hurt even if the monster was relatively small. Of course, Renir didn't miss the chance to boast that his magical power was on par with Liora's at this point.

That would be great and all if they weren't headed to a place where external magic didn't work. Still, it meant that in the long term their team had gained a powerful magic user. Monsters like those gateclaw scorpions wouldn't be a problem anymore if Renir could overwhelm them with magic.

They didn't remain up for long, as soon everyone cuddled up to Liora again, eager to remain warm in the desert night. The tent did keep some heat inside, but it wasn't much.

"Hey, you! Come here!" Liora announced, watching Renir pace around the tent.

"I still don't require any sleep," Renir muttered. "I wish to learn the limits of this—"

"Come. Here." Liora said firmly.

Renir rolled his eyes—the best way a rock monster could—and walked up to Liora.

"What is it?" He asked, seeming exasperated.

Liora lifted herself slightly off the ground. "Join us."

Renir looked at her, clearly flabbergasted.

"You are not serious."

"I am quite serious."

"I am not getting in your underfluffies."

"You most certainly are."

"If he's not taking the spot, can I?" Cee chimed in. "It looks very warm down there."

"No, it is Renir's spot!" Liora shook her head. "Now get in the underfluffies."

"I am NOT getting in the underfluffies."

They glared at each other, neither willing to budge.

"You won't be able to fit down there for much longer," Liora said sadly.

Renir's rocky expression seemed to soften. Well, not soften, but rather become less severe. It was really hard to tell with that face.

"Okay, okay. Just for tonight. I think this body is a little cold," Renir said, finally shuffling underneath Liora. Although unlike before, he kept his head out.

She knew that he couldn't feel the cold yet, but Liora still felt content. Tomorrow they would have to journey up the mountain and face new perils. The monsters would be strong, their magic would be severely limited, and the Order of Night King seemed to be doing something truly big there.

It would be a mess for sure. Some of them would likely get injured. It would be a difficult journey.

But for this night, they were all together. Even as the cold nipped at Liora's feathers, she felt very warm surrounded by her friends. It was perfect.

"Do any of you have any water? The timer on my Landwalker skill is about to run out," Caspian said.

They all groaned.
