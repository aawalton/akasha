After the initial ambushes, Liu Shu's tricks stopped being so effective. Now that their entire group was alert and walking faster, picking them apart was almost impossible. Only those at the back were sometimes dragged out, but the killing pace was much slower.

By the time they were approaching the two-kilometer distance from the outskirts, the number of deaths on the army's side had nearly reached 70. For an army of 1000, the losses were not negligible.

General Tharn squinted. "We are advancing at a good enough pace. Vaya, please keep an eye around us, and if the ambush group attacks, release an arrow at them."

Vaya nodded and prepared her bow and arrow. "Understood."

"Torran!"

"Yes, general?"

Torran shook her head. "I've already tried a few times. However, it is impossible to link myself with the nature around me. The World Tree completely overpowers my presence and influence."

Liu Shu giggled. "Of course I do. You are in my forest, in my domain. You shouldn't try to steal what's mine. That's something very, very bad."

Torran snorted. "Once we are closer to your main body, you'll understand what 'something very bad' truly is."

Liu Shu's smiling voice spread around. "And what tells you that I haven't already? What tells you that this is not a dream? What allows you to be so sure of your reality~?"

Greth snorted. "Don't listen. We aren't under a mind attack."

Greth shouted. "Don't listen! She has no power over us!"

"I have it, and you know it."

Liu Shu's voice was vague as to where it came from. Therefore, it felt as if she was speaking individually to each of them. So, when she referred to someone in particular, it made a few already unstable people doubt everything.

Between them, the female soldier who Liu Shu manipulated before felt her heart beating loudly as if it were being asphyxiated.

Liu Shu could feel the group's overall emotions—not deeply, but enough to know that their unrest increased the more she spoke. 'Let's see~. We are almost above the ant tunnels, so I need to distract them'

Her gaze focused on the female soldier, and she giggled. 'Isn't that right? So many friends are dying~. It's such a shame.'

"G-Get out of my head!"

Vaya looked behind her and frowned at the female soldier who disrespected her. Liu Shu whispered to Vaya. 'Kill her. She is so disrespectful.'

Vaya's eyes sharpened, and she pointed her bow exactly toward the Golden Sap Town. "Shut it."

BOOM!

When she released the arrow, a shockwave occurred around her. The projectile pierced through the canopies of the trees, whistling through the air and accurately falling toward Liu Shu.

Liu Shu looked at the arrow, stunned. 'How did she detect my exact position?'

The Level 24 Serpent resting on Liu Shu's body reacted and looked back. She opened her mouth and released a powerful water column that impacted against the arrow. The arrow pierced through the column of water for a short while until it stopped.

Then, the water fell like rain over the terrain.

Vaya squinted. "No, sir. I didn't. Something probably blocked my shot."

"Oh? How about the chance that you might've missed?" Greth asked.

Vaya snorted. "Impossible."

Right when they were discussing, Liu Shu realized that they were right above the ant nest, and her gaze chilled. 'Time to send those little ones a few toys.'

She warned Ignatia, and when a soldier stepped over one of the small holes, Liu Shu used a root and dragged that person underground while closing the ground. Each of the holes would be one time use, after all…

Tharn shouted. "Dig! Get him out of the ground!"

An Earth Aether Mana user used his spell and tried opening the ground. However, Liu Shu's domain over the forest and her <Land Modifying> skill were strong enough to block it. Checking around the army, she opened another three of the holes they were standing on and dragged more people underground. Tharn looked at that and growled. "You are full of surprises, aren't you?"

"Do you like them~?" Liu Shu asked, giggling.

Tharn snarled. "I like them enough that I want to meet you soon to repay my thanks."

Liu Shu exclaimed. "Oh! Then you are in luck! You are almost here, so don't give up! Come on, team~, you are almost near my town!"

Everyone in the army felt like digging the ground and chewing on the World Tree roots. However, that was one of the most useless things you could do against World Trees. Their roots spanned so wide and deep that wasting resources on anything that wasn't the Tree's core was futile and idiotic.

"Move out! Don't let her catch you in the pitfalls!"

Liu Shu looked at them speeding up, and she couldn't help but smile. 'They forgot about my other type of little trap~.'

As they ran quickly, Liu Shu's roots surfaced from the ground and tripped around ten people at the back. Tharn shouted. "Wait!"

However, the moment they stopped, several holes opened, and Liu Shu dragged people down toward the ant nest, where a mass of giant ants would swiftly devour them.

Tharn cursed aloud. "Shit!"

In that single second of hesitation, the ambush people rushed and dragged those people that Liu Shu tripped out into the forest. Vaya was prepared this time around and quickly shot an arrow.

BOOM!

However, a muscular man appeared in the way of the cannon-like arrow, using a giant ax to strike the attack.

Clang!

The five Second Energy Cycle powerhouses squinted, and Greth commented. "That person is at our level. We need to be careful if we follow them."

"AH!"

The shout of a soldier being swallowed into the ground snapped them out of their daze. Greth spoke aloud. "You need to make a choice, General. Do we mind the pitfalls more or the tripping? Torran can't counterattack due to her lack of affinity with this forest's plant life."

General Tharn was quick to answer. "Mind the pitfalls more. If we stop each time someone trips, we will be in trouble."

With that in mind, the army quickly moved out and maintained an excellent speed, covering terrain quickly.

Of course, Liu Shu and the ambushing group were ruthless and began picking up those at the back more often. Tharn only looked forward while Vaya kept firing arrows behind their army, determined to get at least one kill.

Sadly for her, the ambushers were too agile and swift, knowing the forest really closely. Her arrows were either deflected or hit trees. Each time the arrows hit a tree, though, the tree's trunk would explode, almost one-shotting them.

Below the ground, Ignatia was moving with her army of ants at the same pace as those above, catching the prey that were swallowed and delivered to them. "Today is a great day~. So much food and experience! Liu Shu, Food!"

"Okay~, two more incoming!"

Ignatia saw two of the tunnels widen very slightly, and then, two people fell from them, slamming against the hard ground. Those people rubbed their heads and looked around. The second they did so, their face completely drained of blood. "A-Ants!?"

Ignatia pointed at them and giggled. "Eat them, my children!"

The two soldiers looked at Ignatia, and their eyes went even wider. "Humanoid A-Ant Queen!?"

They quickly stood up, with zero intention of fighting and with fleeing as the only viable option. However, a massive Level 20 Ant Princess tackled one of them while a Level 19 Ant Soldier slammed the other, pushing them back down. Then, the smaller ants rushed forward and began spitting their fire at them.

"AHH!!"

The burning attacks slowly killed them while the rest of the ants moved forward. Ignatia approached them, breaking their legs with two well-placed kicks, and followed her army of children toward the next prey while the little ones ate the soldiers alive.

Unaware of the horrible fates waiting for them under the pitfalls, the army above kept marching and losing people. By the time they were around one kilometer away from the Golden Sap Town's walls, their numbers had diminished by 130 people.

Greth clicked his tongue. "We are losing too many good soldiers, Tharn."

Tharn answered back. "If you have a better idea, I would love to hear it. However, for now, we don't have any other choice."

Torran was looking at the surroundings when she felt a strange pulse of energy that came from the distance. "Tharn, something's wrong!"
