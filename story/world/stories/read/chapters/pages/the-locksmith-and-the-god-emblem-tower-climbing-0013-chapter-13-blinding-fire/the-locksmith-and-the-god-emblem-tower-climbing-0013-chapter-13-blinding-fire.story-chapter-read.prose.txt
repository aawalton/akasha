Besides the Tower event, there was a reason for why the Locksmith hadn't been caught back in the capital. Like a persistent salesman, the young thief had seemingly mastered the art of knocking on doors. In this case, jumping through them. Yes, he was the only one who had such a skill. Still, it was a commendable talent.

"...Is this worth it?" Said a cultist with a black eye and a few slashes around his left arm.

"If the Shepherd finds out about it..." A second cultist answered as they saw a man jumping from magical doors.

"...We can just say we were protecting the flame."

"We said that last time." But after proper thought, the second cultist added. "Fuck it. Let's go."

They fled.

Huh... Harry added inwardly as he saw the two cultists running away. Nice.

The Locksmith, at the moment, was fighting three armed men. With their red robes, they scowled at Harry with an expression that was the mesh of dreadfulness and... Actually, that was their only expression. Harry noticed they weren't the fighting type, and almost felt bad for putting them to sleep. Turning around, he then saw Vela vigorously holding onto dear life. He punched the cultists even harder a second time.

Painting a few attacks of little importance, on the other side of the battlefield, Madeline was helped by Brun, who could sometimes be of use in that situation. The old woman wasn't as strong or durable as she once was, but being the daughter of a viking, she knew how to handle herself. Her look, however, kept pointing at the church. She would often look at her Emblem and read the name in her mind. Dracno. Former conqueror. The first ever recorded user of two Emblems. She then shook her head as if to wash the thought away. He'll be fine. I know he will. But she didn't, and that's what made it scary.

"Shit!" The woman with gracious red hair puffed. "I'm getting tired... We need to wrap this up before Vela becomes fuel to the flame!"

Almost as if he was reading her mind, Harry appeared next to her, kicking a man in his leg, making it bend in the... well, who's to say what's the right way or not? Maybe the cultist's secret wish was to turn into an ostrich.

"Harry!" Madeline's smile quickly turned into a frowned. "Why didn't you come sooner? We're almost—"

Reflecting the light of the mother flame, a dangerously sharp dagger came close to her neck. Behind Madeline, the cultist with his knee bent back seemed to want a reckoning. Before Harry could react, a familiar shadow pulled the knife away and hit the man in the face with his own fist, promptly ceasing his obnoxious screams.

"I...!" Jumping away in surprise, she gently caressed her throat. "By the gods... Did you do that?"

"Manes!" Harry added with a smile as he saw a boy with around 15 years old retract his black stripes. "I thought you were harmless!"

"I..." Clearly affected by the mocking, he took a deep sigh and added. "Like to prove people wrong."

"...Harry." Madeline deadpanned him.

"What?"

"Why did you adopt a child?"

"I'm not a child!" Manes took a step forward. "And he didn't adopt me."

"You heard the child; I didn't adopt him." Harry said.

The Locksmith looked back and saw Vela, who still wore those silent eyes, which, even closed, said more than any false prophet on the streets of New Cavebrook. Harry declared his act of saving the former monk a selfish thought, for he wasn't saving him out of altruism, but to see the reflection of himself in those eyes once again. Which then begs the question; is it a selfish thought to seek redemption?

The young thief jumped through countless doors until the voices of both Manes and Madeline were no longer audible. With a blind dagger, he narrowly cut down the ropes tying the monk to the stick and freed Vela, who looked awful.

"Hey!" Harry let the man on the fluffy grass, far away from the flame. "Are you okay!?"

After a few moments of not answering, the young thief added. "Vela? Are you with me? Fuckin' hell... say something, man!"

"...Something." Vela blurted a response which brought a sincere smile to the Locksmith's face.

"Wow." He said. "You were a real comedian back in your day, weren't you?"

"Back on my day?" Vela replied. "We are the same age, Mr. Harry."

"Yeah, I bet if you added a double digit number to my age, we absolutely would be."

Where's Prism... Was one of Goldman's many questions at the moment; the sly woman had a knack for sneaking out even before she had an Emblem. Perhaps she could've been kidnapped by a cultist, or maybe even fighting a whole group by herself! Although something told Goldman this wasn't the case, and his guts were almost always right.

"Focus, G-Man!" Daniel yelled as he narrowly avoided a hit from one of Conrad's logs.

The church, in that state, was similar to a sawmill than a place for worship. Dozens of logs were stretched around the place like strings on a spider web. And just like the arachnid, the Shepherd could feel each vibration coming from them. Call it an Emblem Intuition, but after so many years in a blinded state, one starts to trust their others senses. And, in a way, Emblems were like an extension to the body.

Similar to an infuriated ape, Conrad was summoning logs from the ground, granting him leverage, and using them to hang on superior logs. Jumping from one to the other, the living armor, now focused, could detect the exact location of Goldman and Daniel solely by his hearing.

Argh! Daniel groaned to himself as he took a step back, unsure how to proceed. Looking back, he saw that Vela, in the distance, had been rescued. Harry, as Goldman had told him, was safe as well. At least it worked...

The viking then nodded to Goldman with utmost trust. He then shook the serious expression out of his face and began yelling in a berserk state. It wasn't the most efficient strategy to gain attention, but it seemed enough.

Goldman picked a broken pew from the ground and, with his enormous strength, threw it over his head. The object was as large as a small table, but seemed dense enough to make it work. As the object fell, Goldman jumped in front of it and applied his Emblem. As it worked with magnetic poles, with positive and negative areas, the gentle giant applied the same negative area in the metal plate behind his back and on the pew. Being close enough, he was ejected into the air as two negatives meant repulsion. It was a dangerous and reckless strategy if done in proximity to others, but, for the first time, Goldman felt relieved there wasn't anyone around him.

Still in the air, he aimed himself to hit Conrad. With the Shepherd attacking Daniel, it gave him plenty of margin to take him out from behind. Clang! A sharp sound of metal colliding with metal could be heard as Goldman grappled him and took him to the ground.

"Aye! That's right!" He had a bright smile as he compressed Conrad like an old man would grab onto his last bit of virility. Even though he was smaller than the colossus, Goldman still had technique.

And for a few moments, it seemed as Conrad was caught, but then...

"...Wait, what?" The gentle giant felt himself being risen from the ground as he strengthened his grip; the texture of dense and old wood could be felt beneath him.

From Daniel's perspective, another log could be seen going down, straight from the ceiling. ...Shit.

"Get down!" He yelled and tried to catch up with Goldman with the propulsion of his arrows, only to fail. "Goldman! Get down, you stupid..."

And before the gentle giant could realize what was happening, he felt reduced to a tiny pebble against two raging ships. The living armor tearing through his skin as he was being squished between the two logs, no longer being able to breath.

"Your spirit is too tainted by sin to serve the Tower..." Conrad said. Even though his face was a mystery, Goldman was sure he was smiling.

Not... yet! The former pirate forced a small gap between the metal plate in his chest and the back of the Shepherd. In it, he used his Emblem and created two areas with the same positive pole. And as Goldman expected, the logs broke with the force of the same subject they wished to erase. Like a star, constantly fighting against its own gravity, Goldman had beaten Conrad in his own game. Using his Emblem, Goldman was floating midair, slowly levitating as the Shepherd was left confused.

"...What? You're not the only one with tricks." Goldman said as he floated in front of Conrad, who in turn, was looking slightly above him.

He then looked at Daniel who simply nodded and cracked his knuckles. "I'm sure you're only still alive because of that armor."

"...Oh, really?" The Shepherd replied as he looked around. "Well, you'll need to kill me to know that."

And so they began, once again.

"Wait!" Brun said to Harry, holding an empty piece of paper. "He's not Conrad."

Alongside the Locksmith, Madeline also stopped to turn at the woman, who wore a worried look. The young woman, in response, tightened the grip in her brush. In the distance, Manes could be seen taking care of Vela, who had his candelabrum. Brun still didn't totally trust the young boy, but Vela insisted he could take care of himself... at least at that moment.

"What are you talking about?" Harry asked.

"I mean, he is going by the name Conrad now, but just like I could see your name through the alias of Locksmith, I could see his; it was Dracno. Conrad is just an anagram. I'm surprised how I didn't recognize this earlier... He was supposed to be dead for over a decade now..."

Decade!? Harry added inwardly as he took a glance from the entrance of the church, seeing the three man battle with all their hearts. Oh, right, Brun is from a different time... Still, this is Dracno? And Harry's heart started to pound faster. The name Dracno was so feared around the continent that it had been prohibited. To be honest, who would ever name their children "Dracno"? Either way, it was a simple evidence on how the madman had changed the world.

"...Are you serious?" Madeline could be seen wide eyed. "H-He's fucking Dracno? The armor guy? The Shepherd—"

"Yes!" She finally concluded. "Look, my Emblem isn't wrong. I know it. He's Dracno, and he has the Log and the Armor Emblem. Judging by his appearance, the Armor Emblem was probably the second. That would explain why he can't take it out. The good thing is that he's blind, but that fact doesn't seem that much more advantageous as of now..."

"What do you mean two Emblems?" Madeline asked, still confused. "That's not possible."

"It is..." Harry answered, almost muttering to himself. "But each of the forms takes something back from you. In the Quadrangular Form, it takes your control. That's why he's blind; he can't take the armor off."

Madeline looked at the Shepherd, with his strange movements. With sudden bursts of movement, the man would often strike with dangerous precision, but most of the time, he would just swing his head to one side or another, almost in a lethargic manner.

"That's gotta be rough." Madeline replied, unsure of how to feel. "Can't imagine being in his shoes. Anyone would go crazy with that."

"...He chose it." Harry answered without looking at her. "He chose this destiny. In search of power, he lost a part of himself. And he's not crazy because of the Emblem. The Quadrangular Form just steals your body, not your mind. No, that's... that's something else. We can't kill him."

"What!?" Both Brun and Madeline were dumbfounded.

"He's seen the top. Dracno has seen the God Emblem, I know it. I can feel it in his... Well, I know that he probably knows something. And I need to get that information, one way or another."

"Are you crazy?" The old woman added, taking a step forward. "You can't beat him with your simple doors and broken daggers. I'm sure not even Vela's precise jets could melt through that dense layer of metal."

"Relax." Harry said with an unassuming smile as he entered the church. "In the worst case scenario, I can just keep teleporting him to the skies and see what happens."

"...You're not an eagle, Harry." Madeline said.

"I can be whatever I want!" He replied.

"Who said that, your mom?"

"...Yeah." He entered the church. Behind him, in the distance, three figures kept fighting. "And she was right. She was always right."

Fuckin' hell... He added inwardly. Dying in the second floor would be a shame. Let's hope I'm more special than that.

"Let's hope there's still something inside to kill... Funny how, without an Emblem, he's just a man inside a suit." Brun said.

A man inside a suit... Then Harry felt a gale of wind and looked up. The sky was getting slightly darker.

"...We're near the end of a cycle?"

"Yes."
