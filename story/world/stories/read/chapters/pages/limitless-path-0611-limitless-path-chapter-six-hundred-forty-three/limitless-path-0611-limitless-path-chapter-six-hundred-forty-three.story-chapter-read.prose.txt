Even after adapting to it and wringing a couple skill levels out of the challenge, they’re still a good bit slower than we are.”

Not that she didn’t trust the World Walkers, but she didn’t trust anyone apart from her small group, Baelvyr’s group, Veren, Mortaine, Zane, Fallon, and Serena and Alex. Laselle she still didn’t really know all that well and their interactions had had a ‘this is business’ feel to them that didn’t inspire closeness or warmth. Not only were they naturally very tough with good natural regeneration without even adding in any skills, they had massive physical strength. Beth had done just a little research on the members of Del’s team after bumping into them again, and the stuff she had found about behemoths was pretty surprising.

They were one of the few species that didn’t get the standard stat allocation at a rebirth without either doing something special or being a mutant version of their species being the biggest shocking item she had found. Instead of the normative distribution, behemoths got more Strength than their other stats, though they got less Intelligence and Wisdom. It wasn’t a massive difference, but if Red did a True Rebirth, instead of getting twenty percent retention in every stat, he would get thirty percent in Strength and fifteen in Intelligence and Wisdom. It might seem like a big change, but if he invested heavily in Strength early, and pumped Dexterity to make sure that stat kept up, his raw punching power would be horrifying. Even without doing True Rebirths, he still got a lopsided spread which favored Strength, leading to his physical baseline power being through the roof.
