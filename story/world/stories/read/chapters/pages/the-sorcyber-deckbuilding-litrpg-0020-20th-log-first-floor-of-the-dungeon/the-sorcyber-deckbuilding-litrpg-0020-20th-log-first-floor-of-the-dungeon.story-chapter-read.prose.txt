The battle between Zoe and the golem was intense, as they exchanged blows and tried to outmaneuver each other. Zoe's agility and speed allowed her to dodge many of the golem's attacks, but the golem's strength and skin made it difficult for her to deal any significant damage.

In front of them stood four statues, each holding a bowl in front of it. Zoe realized that this room must be some kind of puzzle, and she began to inspect the statues closely. Each of them had a symbol etched into it, and she thought that she had to light the bowls in a specific order to solve the puzzle.

She spent some time studying the symbols, looking for any patterns or similarities. She also checked the surrounding walls and the floor for any clues that might help solve the puzzle.

Upon further inspection, Zoe discovered an inscription on the wall that read: "From new beginnings to the height of summer, to the richness of fall, to the stillness of winter." This inscription provided additional support for Zoe's deduction that the symbols on the statues represented the four seasons.

After careful observation, Zoe deduced that the symbols represented the four seasons, with one symbol representing spring, another representing summer, and so on. She believed that the correct order to light the bowls was spring, summer, autumn, and winter.

She shared her theory with Roman, and they both agreed to test it. Zoe took the torch from the wall and began to light the first bowl, which corresponded to the symbol of spring. They both waited to see what would happen. When nothing occurred, they moved on to the next bowl, representing summer, and so on.

After successfully lighting the fourth bowl, a secret door opened, that revealed a hidden room hopefully filled with treasure and valuable artifacts. Zoe and Roman entered the room, feeling relieved and grateful for solving the puzzle and avoiding the deadly trap.
