Callie didn't know what to do. Tristan was looking at the unmistakable glow coming from her forearm. Even the tunic wasn't enough to block its intense light.

"I…" Callie faltered, and the glow subsided as did the voice she had heard before.

In that instant, Callie realized that she had two choices. She could either give up on the voice and pray that she might be able to come later to find the egg. Perhaps in this case, she could convince Tristan that what he had seen was nothing more than a light reflected from somewhere.

But what if the egg wasn't there later? What if the thief noticed two kids coming near his hiding spot and decided that it was too dangerous to leave it where it was? Or worse yet—destroy it.

No, Callie couldn't take that chance. She resolutely turned away from Tristan and focused on the weak voice, the feeble creature that was filled with fear and longing.

Where are you? Let me help!

The glow on her arm intensified again as she focused.

"C-Calluna?" Tristan called out nervously.

"Shhh! I'll explain everything later," Callie said.

It wasn't that she wasn't afraid of what Tristan might do. In fact, part of Callie was absolutely terrified that the boy witnessed her mark glowing, her mind reeling with possibilities, most of which led to her being separated from her beloved family.

But if the egg wasn't returned to the glauxis, the consequences might be worse still. Villagers might die, the glauxis would undoubtedly get killed, and dragonbloods might be called to the village, which might lead to Callie's discovery.

She would much rather take her chances with Tristan.

Following the voice wasn't easy, however. It felt she was using energy to follow the voice of the hatchling. Maybe it was thanks to magic that Callie was able to "hear" it at all.

Callie went deeper into the forest, and to her surprise, Tristan, after some faltering, followed after her.

"I-Is that magic?" He asked.

Callie didn't answer, still focusing on the voice.

"What are you trying to do?" He continued.

"Shhh! Give me a moment," Callie said, mildly annoyed by his interruption.

Tristan made a grimace, but went quiet nonetheless and followed after her. Maybe it was his curiosity keeping him at bay.

"I'm close…" Callie muttered, feeling that the owner of the voice was just within reach.

"Close to what?" Tristan asked, then pointed at Callie's arm. "It's not glowing as much anymore."

Callie realized that whatever magical energy she had within her was running out. Her grasp on the voice was getting weaker, but she knew that she was close. She ran towards its direction, trying to hold onto her magic for as long as possible, but before long, she ran out of strength.

"Now, it's not glowing at all," Tristan said. "Can you finally explain what is going on?"

Callie looked around in frustration. The egg was somewhere nearby, but she couldn't hear its voice anymore. Neither could she see it. The place didn't look like anything special, trees and bushes around them just like in any other part of the forest.

"Can you help me?" Callie asked Tristan, stepping closer to him. She was determined to find the egg.

"Help?" He asked, taking a step back. He likely wasn't used to seeing Callie being so assertive.

"There's a large egg of a creature somewhere here. Hidden somewhere. Help me find it."

"What? How do you know that? Why would I even help you in the first place?"

"Please! I'll make it up to you!" Callie begged.

"Why would I? You won't even explain what this whole weirdness is! Is this one of your monster things again that will cause trouble for everyone?" Tristan crossed his arms over his chest stubbornly and glowered at Callie.

"Arcane beast!" Callie snapped. "If we find this egg and return it to the glauxis, then the village would be safe. I'm trying to help everyone here!"

Tristan looked skeptical. "That's what you say, but you always end up in trouble. I don't want to be punished alongside you."

Callie was starting to get frustrated with the boy. She just wanted to find the egg already. But getting his help here would be very helpful, while his running and snitching on her might make matters worse than they already were.

"Please, Tristan! This is the only thing I'll ever ask you. Whatever chores, anything you might want from me, I'll do it. Just help me this once. Please!" She stepped closer to the boy and made the same face she did when she begged Callen to do something for her. It was very effective on her brother, although she had no confidence that it would work on Tristan.

"Anything?" Tristan asked, his cheeks tinging a bit red.

"Anything!" Callie nodded.

Tristan looked to the side for a moment while scratching the back of his head. "O-Okay. Fine. But don't forget your promise. And I'll want an explanation as to why your hand was glowing."

Tristan said and extended his tiny pinky finger towards Callie. "Pinkie promises cannot be broken!"

Callie hooked her smaller pinky around Tristan, and they shook on it.

"So you say we need to find an egg? What kind of egg are we looking for?"

Callie opened her mouth to answer, and then she realized that she didn't know. What did a glauxis egg look like? She had no clue whatsoever.

"A large one?" Callie said.

"You don't know?!"

"I… I'm sure I would recognize it once I saw it," Callie said. "It definitely should be larger than chicken eggs. I think…"

"You were walking so confidently before when your arm was glowing. What happened to all of that? Can't you do the arm glowing thingy to find the egg?" Tristan questioned.

"I ran out of energy, I think," she replied sheepishly.

Tristan narrowed his eyes at her and pursed his lips. "You think?"

"I don't know how it works, okay? I just know that the egg is somewhere nearby. Likely hidden."

Tristan sighed. "You're such a pain in the butt, you know that!"

"It's rude to say that!" Callie protested, remembering how Mom had chastised Callen once he had used a similar phrase. Although he used a different word instead of butt.

"Nobody else is here besides us. Let's just search and be done with this already. I'll check up from a tree," the boy said and nimbly climbed up on one of the nearby pines.

Callie, meanwhile, looked through the bushes, in between roots, and tried to find some hidden caves. They kept searching for a good while, yet they couldn't find anything.

"We can't stay here for much longer. Adults will start to worry," Tristan noted. "Maybe that egg of yours isn't even here."

"No, it must be here! It must be…" Callie said, but she wasn't feeling so confident anymore.

The fearful voice from the egg had definitely come from somewhere within this area, but they hadn't seen any signs of it. Nor have they found any traces that somebody might have been here. No footprints or anything that looked like humans had touched it. Just an undisturbed part of the forest.

"Sure… But this place looks like any other place in the forest. Albeit it is odd that there are no birds or squirrels nearby," Tristan said casually.

"What?" Callie looked at the boy in surprise and listened to the surroundings.

How could she have overlooked this? There truly were no birds in nearby trees. The only birds she heard were from a good distance away. This part of the forest was uncharacteristically silent and undisturbed. As if the animals were avoiding the place.

"It must be here! I'm not wrong!" Callie said, her confidence reignited.

"Please do tell where? I've even looked through the tree hollows. Either that egg is so tiny that we would never find it, or it's buried deep underground!" Tristan snapped.

"Underground…" Callie mulled it over. She remembered her lesson with Mister Blackthorn. How she had asked whether creatures could dig their dens underground, and he had snapped at her, ultimately not answering her question. It must also be on an incline to prevent the rainwater from gathering at the bottom.

"That's it! It must be buried in a place where rainwater can't gather. An incline or someplace protected from rain."

"Knowing that doesn't help us. How can we find something that is buried?" Tristan asked.

"It still needs air. If we look carefully, we should find a loose patch somewhere," Callie said, her hopes reignited.

"Fine, but we're only searching for a bit longer. If we can't find the egg, we're heading home," the boy snapped in annoyance.

Callie nodded and dropped to her knees, carefully looking through the bushes and roots that seemed to be higher than the rest, touching the ground, feeling for a looser spot or something that might signify that something might be buried somewhere.

Tristan also searched, although half-heartedly, rummaging lazily through the bushes. The more time passed, the more she heard him grumbling. Callie feared the boy might just run out of patience, when suddenly he let out a yelp.

"What's wrong?" Callie looked up, only to see that Tristan's foot got stuck between some tree roots.

"My foot is stuck!" He complained, trying to pull his foot free.

Callie ran up to him and helped him pull his foot free. It got stuck real tight because of how the roots were intertwined. Looking at the formation, Callie thought there were roots where one could squeeze the egg through; it would be a perfect hiding spot.

"Search between roots," Callie instructed, and began looking for places where roots were intertwined and on an incline so rainwater wouldn't gather between them.

Before long, she found what she thought was perfect. Two pines growing closely together with their large roots growing down an incline. She began touching the soil between roots, and soon came upon soil that felt quite loose when compared to everything else. Could this?

"I think I found it!" Callie called.

"Really?" Tristan came running up to her, peering at the spot Callie began digging.

"Help me!"

Tristan grumbled something but crouched down next to her and began digging. Soon, Callie's hand grabbed at a piece of buried fern. Tristan got some moss. They shared a look and dug more intently.

Before long, they unearthed a hole in the ground, lined with clay, ferns, and moss. Among these sat a white egg with light grey spots. They both gasped when they saw it. It was, without a doubt, the biggest egg Callie had ever seen.

"I-I can't believe it… There really is an egg," Tristan muttered.

Callie reached down and tried to lift it, but found that she didn't quite have the strength to do it. Her hands were already throbbing from all the digging she had done.

"Let me," Tristan shooed her aside.

"Just be careful. Don't break it!" Callie warned.

Tristan reached down and gingerly lifted the egg out of the hole. "Wow… I-It's warm."

Callie's heart raced. They had actually done it. They had found the glauxis's egg. She nervously reached her hand out and touched the egg. Just a fingertip at first. The shell was thick and firm, so she dared to press her whole hand against it.

"It really is warm," Callie noted. As she held her palm against the egg, she felt the lingering bits of magic stir within her.

As if in response, something stirred within the eggshell, ever so slightly.

"D-Did you feel that? It moved!" Tristan noted. Callie was surprised to see that his eyes didn't reflect fear but rather wonder, perhaps even excitement. "Do you think it's going to hatch soon?"

"I-I don't know…" Callie admitted. The thought that the egg might hatch never occurred to her.

On one hand, she would love to see a glauxis hatching. How many people have witnessed that? Callie was willing to bet that it wasn't many. But on the other hand… What would she do with a glauxis hatchling on her hands?

"Okay, we found the egg. Now what do we do with it?" Tristan asked.

"We have to return it to its parent," Callie said.

"Return it? But how are we supposed to do that? Can you summon it somehow with your weird glowing arm?"

"Um… No. At least I don't think so. We'll have to find it. Last time I saw it in the swamp near the training grounds."

"You've actually seen the adult?! You're insane!"

Callie remained quiet. She didn't want to admit that she had hoped that she would convince it to leave by talking to it. That was admittedly a rather stupid idea, even if it did help her discover the truth about the egg.

"Setting your weirdness aside for a moment, we can't go to the swamp now. Look, the sun is starting to set. I bet the adults are already looking for us," Tristan noted.

Looking up, Callie realized that the boy was right. Oh, no! Callie might end up in trouble again.

"Let's go back!" Callie urged.

"Wait? What do we do about the egg? We can't just bring it home for everyone to see! No way anybody is going to believe that this is just a normal egg. And once they figure out that it's a glauxis egg, well… I don't know about your parents, but mine would likely destroy it on sight," Tristan said, and actually looked worried.

Callie thought about it for a moment, then smiled. "We can take it to Granny Ethel. She'll hide it."

"We would have to go around the village to avoid the adults," Tristan noted.

Looking at the sun, it was getting dangerously low, but perhaps they would make it.

"Tristan!" A voice called from somewhere afar. It sounded like Tristan's mom.

The two of them shared a look. There was no time to head to Granny Ethel. They had to find a hiding spot for the egg, and they had to find it now.
