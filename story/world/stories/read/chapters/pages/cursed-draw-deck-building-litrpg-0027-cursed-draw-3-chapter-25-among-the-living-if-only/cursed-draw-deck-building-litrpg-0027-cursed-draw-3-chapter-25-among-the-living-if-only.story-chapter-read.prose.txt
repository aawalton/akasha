Molten sunlight dripped into Alley’s eyes as awareness of his surroundings slowly crept into his mind. He was in a medical tent just outside the stadium where he had fought his match with beams of gold from the setting sun pouring through the tent’s entrance.

‘Did I win?’ Alley wondered to himself as he blinked to acclimatize. In a flash, it all came back to Alley. He had defeated the old man who used the Relic-based Deck and then brought himself here to be treated. He had been in a daze at the time, courtesy of poisoning himself and then letting his cloak drain most of his blood.  He had managed to overpower the older man and his terracotta army, however. With how much Alley’s head felt like it was full of sloshing liquid that filled him with nausea every time he moved, he wasn’t quite sure if the win had actually been worth it. At the time, Alley had been so determined to not allow himself to lose, no matter the cost. Looking back at it through the lens of someone who felt like he was going to wretch every time he moved even a little, Alley's former attitude seemed less like proud determination and more like idiotic stubbornness.

“Hey, not dead after all,” came a cheerful call Alley would recognize under any circumstances. So he slowly turned away from the deep golden rays of the sun that were blinding him.

“You are one to talk,” Alley grunted back as he grimaced from his own movements.  Sitting across from him was a mass of bandages inside a frostdragon scale cloak doing its best Darius impersonation.

“You are more bandage than man.”

The hunter raised a finger on his right hand, which incidentally was also covered in bandages except for the fingers.

“Ahh, but these are from micro-spears launched at me by my opponent. You are in the doctor tent because you were willing to kill yourself to get the win against someone you don’t even know.”

Alley narrowed his eyes for a few seconds, until the effort made his head hurt and his nausea flare.

“How’d you know I did that?” Alley replied with the ghost of a grin haunting his words.

“There isn’t a scratch on you, but you were drenched in sweat and shivering,” Darius answered while doing a sort of half shake of his head.

“Plus, that healer over there told me.” Alley slowly turned to look at where Darius pointed. There was an olive-skinned woman tending to other hurt competitors on the other side of the large cot-filled tent.

“Can you get me something to eat?” Asked Alley when he turned back.

“Sure, what do you want?”

“I don’t care, just lots of it.”

By the time Darius returned with an entire steaming pot of thick meat and vegetable stew, Alley had consumed two entire jugs of water. He understood now why people warned him off Resonance sickness so heavily. This had been only a small amount of Resonance that his body couldn’t process, and he felt as alive as the Cards in his Deck. Of course, there was also the matter of The Cloak of the Bloodless one. Alley might be durable enough now to make use of the Cloak without it killing him, but it was still a risky proposition.

Honestly, the more he thought about it, the more he found the idea of the artifact strange. The point was to generate Vials by sacrificing one’s own blood. That made some sense, except for the fact that you needed Vials to heal yourself of that same blood loss. You wouldn’t have the Vials to spend that way if you intended to use the cloak.

Either the Dealer was simply not meant to be activated unless your rank was so absurdly great that blood was an expendable resource, or there was some secondary or other mechanism Alley wasn’t aware of. He had spoken to Anaten about the cloak, though it had only been a brief moment among countless topics the pair of linked enemies had discussed during their last round of negotiations. Alley was beginning to wonder if Denderra’s brother had left a few things out.

Alley looked down at the oversized iron pot that had clearly been adorning some store’s kitchen until very recently. Alley and Darius weren’t out of funds anymore ever since their trial, but this still had probably cost a lot of Chips…Assuming Darius hadn’t just stolen it.

“ Thank you so much.” Was all Alley managed to get out before he took a spoon and began doing his best to drown his nausea in a sea of earthy northern  vegetables and broth.

After eating two-thirds of the stew and drinking another jug of water, Alley felt if not human, at least something among the living. The pair of them had discussed the ups and downs of their matches and retold the events with only a little exaggeration for flavor.

“So, any idea how Kyera’s match went?” Asked Alley as he put the mostly empty pot aside with the sigh of a man who knows he has eaten too much.

“No idea, no one was at our inn, so I tried here.”

Alley frowned a little at that as he slowly eased himself off of his cot. Every movement no longer made him feel like he was going to throw up, though he was still weak enough that he risked being knocked off his feet if there was even a particularly persistent breeze.

“So you came right to the medical tent? Not really a vote of confidence.”

The hunter snorted.

“Come on, Alley, this far into the tournament, everyone is a monster. Even if you win, no one is getting through matches unharmed…of course, I generally expect it to be damage from the opponent, not impromptu suicide attempts.”

Alley did an exaggerated and obviously false set of laughs in response. “You’re hilarious, now let's just go find Kyera.”

After Alley thanked the healers and assured them he was feeling much better, the pair set off towards the northern stadium that the Framer’s match had taken place in. To get from one arena to another, Alley had to push himself more than he was willing to admit to either his friend or the ghost in his head. His body was already beginning to process Life Essence, but it would be consumed to heal him rather than refill his Vials for at least the remainder of today, and possibly some of tomorrow.

​

“See, he is actually targeting himself. The Cards are giving him a sudden spike of increased authority .” Alley went on explaining Authority Resonance as they closed in on the stadium. “It only works if you already have some degree of real authority over someone, but as the head of law enforcement for Skalean, so long as Korshei stays in the city, he never has to worry about that.”

All of this was just based on what he had read since Alley had never encountered Authority Resonance actually in use before.

“Yeah, I’m pretty sure I get it now. I’d have to consider myself a criminal here in order to actually be immune to those Authority Cards when he plays them. “

Suddenly, Denderra appeared in front of the pair of conversing men.

“I cannot believe you two are still talking about this one, Resonance. It is genuinely like I am stuck floating around a scribe-monk. Anyway, you were right, Darius, she's in the tent for this stadium, and she does not look good.”

Alley and Darius sped up their pace to a near jog until they were at the entrance to the northern arena medical tent. There were fewer injured competitors in this tent than there had been in Alley’s. Whether that was due to there having been less injuries in this stadium, or them being the kind of injury that could be more easily treated.

Alley was more than a little grateful that there were fewer patients filling the cots here, as Kyera had obviously required a great deal of effort from multiple of the healers manning the tent.

As it turned out, Darius wasn’t actually that bandage-covered after all, at least not when compared to Kyera, who was still asleep in her cot. The healers had used Cards to render her unconscious while they worked on her wounds. Alley couldn’t see the full extent of the Framer’s injuries under the layers of gauze, but from what he could tell, she had been absolutely mauled.

Siddling up to Alley, who was standing over Kyera’s sleeping form, the hunter made a hissing noise at her injuries.

“Man, she really took a beating, huh? At least it wasn’t because of your Doom this time.”

Alley shot his friend a disbelieving look.

“Are you fucking kidding me?”
