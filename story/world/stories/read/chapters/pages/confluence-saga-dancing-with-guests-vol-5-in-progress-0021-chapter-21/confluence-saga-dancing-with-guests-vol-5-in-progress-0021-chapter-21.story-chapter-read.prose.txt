Extended Diving expeditions depend on six things: Food, water, a way to repair equipment, medical supplies or a healer, a willingness to go on and most importantly trust in your Diving partners.

If you can't trust them you will end up making a mistake, and mistakes are often fatal in the Depths.

In the end it took nearly another week for Seb to get his Husk to the point where it was acceptable. Anya suspected he would keep refining it for several more weeks, she knew from experience that she would find small things to adjust as she grew used to hers. It did reach the point of being usable in private a couple days after they inventoried her Vault, which meant their new training and exercise routines included getting used to the differences between True Self and Husk.

Although the week should have felt monotonous and boring with its focus on meditation and basic training, Anya enjoyed every minute of it. She was free to be herself, to spend time with someone who looked at her and saw her for who she was. She got to teach him how to stretch, how to fall properly, how to punch a mannequin without injuring himself. She even showed him what little grappling she knew. It wasn't much and she doubted it would have any use in the Depths, but tripping him and ultimately pinning him down, she was significantly stronger and faster than he was when in her Husk, had actually seen her lose once or twice when he cheated. Tickling was not part of their training. She might have gotten even with him when she was showing him the difference in capabilities a Husk provided though. She'd had him pick her up in his True Self then repeat the action in his Husk. When he stumbled from how much easier it was the second time she hooked an ankle behind his knee, landing lightly on her toes with her hands on either side of his head while he fell flat on his back beneath her. Before he could do more than realize what had happened she'd pushed herself back upright and was offering him a hand up.

Their evenings were just as enjoyable, whether it was cooking together and then settling in on the couch to watch whatever struck their fancy, going out for dinner and losing track of time, or sharing popcorn while watching some stupid Comedy in a Movie Theater, being able to use their band to communicate meant no one got upset that they spent half the movie arguing about whether it was funny because it was funny or funny because it was stupid. They were spending almost every waking moment together and yet she never got bored.

The only downside was that despite it all Seb still seemed intent on playing the protective Retainer. She knew he was interested, between him slipping an arm around her while they sat on the couch and the emotions underlying their band communication she had no doubts. Even when they weren't connected she imagined she could feel it, feel his overall mood and well-being. She kept telling herself to be patient, that once they started leveling they would have all the time in the world, but that only went so far. In her mind she kept picturing the Diving tent in her Vault, it could handle three adults in a pinch but even with only two they would be living on top of one another, she'd be patient until they went into the Depths.
