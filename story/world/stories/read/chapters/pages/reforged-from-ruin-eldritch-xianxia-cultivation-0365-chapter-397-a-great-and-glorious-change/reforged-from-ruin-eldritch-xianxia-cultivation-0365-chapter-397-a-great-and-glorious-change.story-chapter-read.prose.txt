Alterations to Causality are a tricky thing. I mean really tricky. It’s not just adding new properties or rules to something pre-existing, it’s actually undercutting the whole process directly to achieve a desired result. Qi is a gateway to something that seems Paracausal, but truly isn’t- you’re interacting with an additional layer to how the Will of Heaven, and thus Physiks, works, modifying pre-existing formulas with the addition of new variables. Paracausality, on the other hand, is the outright removal or side-stepping of those formulas.

If you add fire Qi to a movement, then the movement has some aspect of fire and Qi to it. If you perform a movement and it makes fire, that’s friction and energy igniting chemical consequences in other aspects around you. If fire simply appears, independently of any and all other forms of causes and effects that might normally elicit it, that’s Paracausal.

It’s almost impossible to accurately ascertain, obviously. There are so many variables, hidden and not, in literally every possible aspect of all of existence that it’s nearly guaranteed that there is some sort of more logical and prolonged chain of events occurring, even if it’s beyond one’s ability to perceive. Furthermore, I’d argue that true Paracausality is impossible to consciously create or experience, based on what little we know about the nature of the birth of existence and sapience. Even our current frame of reference for it is flawed: losing parts of the chain of cause and effect does not mean its total absence, and partial coherence is only partially Paracausal. True Paracausality could see events happening before the actions that brought them about, only for those actions to then never take place, could generate effects without rhyme or reason or even according to standard progressions of linearity as we understand, and would, by its definition, lead to a perfect collapse of what we know of as “real” and “logical”, removing both words from the realm of the possible.

However. Having said all of this. I have nevertheless experienced this phenomenon directly.

True Paracausality is impossible so long as choice and existence as we know exist. Partial Paracausality can be born, taking willpower directly around whatever process is normally required for an effect to take place to reach that effect directly. Not through Qi, though its presence, I believe, allows for the possibility of Paracausality in the first place. Not through the Will of Heaven, which dreams existence into a specific shape and decries all that attempts to supersede or alter it.

I do not know what allows one to be able to (rather ironically) cause such an effect. I only know that it occurred at the hands of beings whose existence is defined more by its own ontology than by surrounding contexts.

I once saw a Titan wish someone away.

They were inept and spoke out of turn at a tea service. And then… they didn’t. They hadn’t. They simply were not. They had been, and now they hadn’t been. Their parents hadn’t been. Their siblings, uncles, aunts, cousins, grandparents, hadn’t been.

They were. And then they weren’t.

I researched this event for almost three decades, in absolute and painstaking detail. I tried to track down the exact batch of cloth that was used to make their clothing, the history of the seamstress that brought it about, the slight bacterial imprints they left where they walked and breathed, peering through time itself to track atmospheric and temperature irregularities at points where they had been.

They were remembered. Not much, and as if through a fog, but vaguely. The places where their family had been were found empty, but weren’t memetically contagious or dangerous to perceive, and evidence of people having lived in them until the exact instant that the Titan willed it not be so remained.

But they just… weren’t. Not anymore.

No chemical reaction, no transfer of energy, no fluctuations of Qi, nothing that three decades of study from the most well-funded and advanced scientific mind outside the first ring could detect. I retroactively calculated the movement of the air in the room the servant was in and couldn’t even find a point where a vacuum came into being from their disappearance.

They were. And then they weren’t. And the world kept going without a single further effect from it.

I have no words for how such an act came to be, save that it transcended existence as I understand it.

-Grandmaster Errath, Head Researcher of the Division of Altered Cultivation and the Division of Science. Excerpt found in a discarded set of private journals by a member of a bandit clan acting on the orders of the Missing Man (Hao Nera).
