Dear Diary,

The trap yesterday is absolutely what I did not need.

Seriously, it was one of those things that I hadn't really thought about, not seriously, and it popped off and injured not me, but one of the people nominally under my protection. They're supposed to lead me to the threats, then I'm supposed to eliminate them. That's how this is supposed to go, but I've been increasingly worried that the Spartans wouldn't follow the script. Or that the Fates had relapsed and given in to their pernicious crack habit. As I went on about at length yesterday.

Then that fuckin' trap almost killed Panther.

Not like I'd let him stay dead. I mean, if he died and didn't want to stay dead. For all I know, he'd have gone directly to whatever afterlife his people believe in. Unless it's someplace like Tartarus, I can't see where it could be significantly worse than here and now. Okay, Saffron and Tallulah and the Grand Council and... Okay, a not inconsiderable number of the most competent people in the Alliance are working to make here and now better for everybody, not just a short list of 'special people'. I guess that's one Change that I can fully get behind.

But if he wanted to stay dead, because his people's afterlife is wall to wall hookers and blow or whatever gets his rocks off...

Sonofabitch, Panther is definitely a guy, and Siobhan isn't into guys, so I'm really not fuckin' sure how I'd deal with both of them in my Bedroom for the remainder of eternity. Okay, I do know, and the answer is 'as gently as I could until they asked me to stop being gentle' or something similar, but housing two Souls in a fairly small space seems, I dunno, cruel. I made space for Isnomi's new Swamp Dragon in the stables, I sure as shit ought to be able to make space for two quiet thirsty thirst traps in my Bedroom.

Now I feel a little guilty about comparing the Souls of actual Human Adjacent people to fuckin' Dragons of dubious sapience.

Now that I think about it though, both of them are kind of, y'know, quiet. He's not the sort to force himself on a woman who wasn't interested. Shit, I kinda had to set down big flashing signs to convince him it was okay. I mean, yeah, I thought he was a little bonkers to want me at my most scarred up and unhealed when he could have Saffron or Marie, but he was polite about the whole thing. Meanwhile Siobhan's got nothing against men as, like, conversational partners. I don't think. Maybe she's sort of shy with them? it's not like she's really physically capable of defending herself, although between 'Being An Archmage' and 'Weaponized Stabilize', I'd be a little disappointed if she didn't tase the fuck out of some asshole trying to lay hands on her.

But if they both want that as their afterlife, maybe I'll steal some more board games, or another DVD player or something. I am, after all, easily distracted, and wind up with completely reasonable distractions on the regular.

Like missing my fuckin' Revel last night.

Remembered that a little after midnight. As we've been doing, we kept going a few hours after dark, right up until midnight. Marie marked a tree, we stepped to the Homestead, scarfed down some protein heavy, easy to eat stuff, which today meant sort of soft meatballs in beef gravy over rice, then hit the Bath for a soak before bedtime.

At the door to the hunter's bedroom, I took Panther by the shoulders and pressed my forehead to his. "I'm sorry about today, man."

He smiled grimly up at me. "Do not sorrow, Goddess. Those who have lost themselves to such bestial acts must be put down, are worth risking life and limb to stop."

I kissed him on the forehead. Then shrugged, tilted his head, and kissed him on the lips. "I'm so proud of you, Panther. I'm a little in awe that you chose me as Patron," he blanched a little and his two buddies gave him a bit of side eye. Which I responded to immediately, saying, "even though it's not the normal thing for your people to do something like that. You took that risk for me, and I will do everything I can to be a worthy Patron for you."

He smiled shyly, then sighed. "I need sleep."

"You do." Then I leaned my mouth next to his ear. "You're gonna need even more when we're done celebrating catching and ending these fuckers."

With that I spun him around, propelled him into the hunter's guess room with a gentle shove on the tush, and stepped to the bedroom.

Saffron looked up at me, smiling muzzily. "To the Bedroom with you, Goof."

I leaned down. "After this." I kissed her, wrapping her in arms and tentacles and gently lifting her up until I could just stand there, lip to lip, tongue to tongue. hands and tentacle tips exploring her topography from the nape of her neck to the crack of her ass.
