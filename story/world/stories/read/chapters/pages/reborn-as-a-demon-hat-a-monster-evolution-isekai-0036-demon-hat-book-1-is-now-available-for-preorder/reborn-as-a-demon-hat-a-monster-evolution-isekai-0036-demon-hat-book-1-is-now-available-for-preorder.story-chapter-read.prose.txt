I have a couple of big notes/milestones today which I'll be sharing with you.

Demon Hat is officially finished (over on Patreon)

On Monday, Demon Hat's last chapter was written.

It's odd to write those words, because this represents the first series I've written to completion. It will certainly not be the last. It also doesn't really seem like an ending due to the vast editing I've been doing with my publisher and on my own - which will be continuing for the next month at least before launch. I've even got the notion that there's at least 3/4 extra chapters I could slot into book 4 before the end (even though its already the largest entry in the series by far!)

The amount of fun I've had bringing Ethan's story to life is reward in itself, and has taught me a great deal about serialization, LitRPG, and genre expectations in general. And so it's fitting that today I give you the details on its KU release.

Reborn as a Demon Hat - all formats available on Kindle Unlimited and Audible

The ebook, audiobook and paperback are all now available for preorder at this link: Reborn as a Demon Hat - all formats.

Audio: Adam James Conner has done an incredible job at bringing all of the main cast of characters to life, and if I can recommend one format in particular it would be the audiobook. One thing I love about audio is how one performance can completely change the nature of a book - heightening certain aspects of character personalities that simply reading the words on the page can't do.

The cover artist, Preston Asevedo, has also done a fantastic job at bringing the right vibe to the artwork for the story. He has been involved in the graphic novel adaptation of the Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver series - one of my favorite video game franchises of all time - which is one of the main reasons I went with him.
