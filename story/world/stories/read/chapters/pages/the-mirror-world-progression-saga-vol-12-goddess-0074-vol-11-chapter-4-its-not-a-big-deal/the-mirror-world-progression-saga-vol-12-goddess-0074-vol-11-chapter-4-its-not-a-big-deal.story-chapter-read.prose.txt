Samantha found that everyone continued to be pointlessly concerned with her keeping her eyes shut, though she tried to tell them it was just a preference thing. In addition, it was technically separate from the ‘bleeding eyes’ thing, which was not a preference and to which she could do nothing about. Fortunately, after the initial incident, the ears and nose had stopped bleeding, at least.

But her eyes? On her throne, in the spirit world, in her private domains, it didn’t matter; the eyes bled, and without any indication of a curse or even any System information whatsoever. She could mask over it when she wanted with illusion, but she couldn’t ‘shapeshift’ her way out of it.

Zephyr claimed it was likely the deific equivalent of a psychosomatic issue. A word she was surprised to find had a translation. Samantha did a little reading of what literature existed on Calrenazzod about such things, such as the book Zephyr referenced, ‘Various Curios of Disorders,’ and did find that word again, though the Dominion writer likely would’ve classified hers under the ‘feminine hysteria’ category based on how much he attributed to that.

Quite the jerk. Hmm. Did that pop into Zephyr’s head, too, and then someone warned him it was a bad idea to use it, driving him to find an alternative word? Ha!

Bleeding eyes were an annoyance for sure, but not really a big deal. Others didn’t agree — namely, Azure, who wanted to pursue solutions, treatment, blah, blah, blah.

She also more or less forced Samantha to prove the ‘preference’ claim about her closed eyes. Samantha agreed on the condition that Azure — and the rest! — would be okay with her keeping her eyes closed most of the time.

Admittedly, it took a little bit more effort than Samantha had anticipated to finally open her eyes again… but she did it! See?! No big deal! It wasn’t even all that disconcerting to see the crudeness of material reality. Just slightly disconcerting, that was all.

She didn’t admit this, however, not wanting to worry Azure. Nor did she point out that the bleeding eyes were a bit worse with the eyes open, making them feel too ‘moist’ all the time. At least her nose didn’t run on top of it, as actual crying would tend to induce.

In any case, all she really needed to do was keep a red handkerchief around to dab off excess occasionally. Most of the time, the blood tears simply dried on her cheeks on their own.

No matter what she did about the eyes issue, it didn’t alleviate Azure’s concern, nor that of everyone in general. Samantha understood why: the changes linked to the wound, perhaps the wound itself. She was… different, and she didn’t bother trying to deny that, as there was no way to hide it. And there was as much chance of changing it or fixing it as her siblings had to fix their foibles.

She’d been ripped apart by the mental attacks of the mind mages… torn asunder and, as it would appear to others, stitched back together. Puppet-like perhaps. But what was the body? What had her vast collective of experiences taught her about how relevant one instance of the self was? It was nothing. One eye, one viewpoint of something vast and collective.

Her ‘individual self’ was indeed being piloted by a higher, separated state of awareness. She had simply, by necessity, refused to seat herself entirely back into a damaged mind, cheating herself from a worse set of consequences and paying another set. Someone distant and dissociated was better than whatever would occur when fully synthesized. Disaster awaited her in that garbled, humpty-dumpty eggshell.

The spirit was separate from the body and mind, and this remained pure and untouched by the foul hands of the Dominion and its creatures. The way she saw through into the world was marred, but interpretable, like looking through many broken and warped shards from enough angles to make a satisfactory comparison of the truth.

In fact — ironically — her clarity was made superior in this. She saw the truth as if surrounding it from all angles rather than down the simple tunnel others saw from and walked down.

Indeed, she preferred seeing through the spirit, in part due to this. But it was hard to explain to others and convince them she had nothing broken that needed fixing. Oh, anyone could tell she was, in fact, broken… thus the dilemma.

“The wounds we take for our children, Director. Mother. Yes? They’re necessary for life. A mother would know.”

The Director might’ve heard her. She responded. ‘More like the Mother every day. Let us reach toward one another, as my hubris failed to glean was the final variable. By the end, our concord will be proven unstoppable, a sword of truth driven through the barrier of a false prison, as we stand before infinity hand-in-hand.’

“I do like the sound of that!”

In Itha Ranon, the giant crystal provided by the Primal Mystic was constructed as a side tower and fully ready for channeling, though the means of conversion were still lacking. But a day later, a package arrived in Itha Ranon, delivered by unknown means. Within it was a clockwork contraption the size of a coffee machine, housing a small, soapy-looking crystal in the center and numerous rolled-up spools of glittering wire. It had a separate, large funnel-like piece, or horn, that apparently needed to be screwed into it. There was also a note.

Samantha read it and identified the device simultaneously.

Dearest Friend,

This satisfies our contract, though in finding the notebook I needed in order to complete it (however arduous that the search was), well, it is a priceless bonus to this endeavor. Fantastic!

May the item, and you, accomplish all that you hope in the days to come.

Kindest Regards,

Wondy

The Incredible Mana Converting Implement

Behold — this device is truly glorious!

Simply channel raw mana compatible with the Light aspect into the resonating horn, and the mana will begin to cycle into the crystal. This is not safe, however, without an attached receptacle for the ensuing divine energy. Note also that the horn will begin to make a disturbing racket when the output is too high. Please ramp up slowly to find the tolerable level of transfer!

In the vicinity of a proper receptacle, press the wire release button* to cause the wires to autonomously attach to the nearest proper receptacle directly. This should preferably be a crystal, an orb, or a proper enchanted machine; otherwise, there may be a sudden and catastrophic explosion of the implement, and we don’t want that.

Once attached, you may begin to reap the rewards of this incredible device!

*Note to self: Don’t forget to install the wire release button.

Oh, brother.

Samantha took the device to the top of the new tower, where the crystal's housing left its pointed bottom fully accessible from below. She positioned the converter on a central stand rising up close to the absorbing crystal and searched for a button. Of course, there wasn’t one.

Sighing, Samantha manually pulled the ends of the wires up to the crystal. Fortunately, as they neared, their ends unfurled like unraveling thread, revealing a tiny, glowing center which rather magnetically pulled itself to the crystal and seemed to ‘lock in,’ complete with a pleasant, chime-like sound.

There were six wire spools in all, and Samantha connected each one. Once she did, the mechanisms within the clockwork device began to spin, and the crystal within it glowed faintly.

She took the cue to begin pouring mana into the horn, first starting with the tiniest trickle. The small inner crystal flared up brightly.

Definitively, she sensed the mana spin through the device rapidly and absorb into the greater crystal, turning on a dime from arcane mana into divine energy with a (Light) aspect.

Letting out a loud ‘whoop’ with a hand thrust up high, Samantha soon got to work pouring more and more into the crystal. Five, ten, fifteen, twenty a second, it showed no signs of strain… nor did it at fifty, sixty, seventy… the mana poured into the device, and it whirled like a little engine, as the thread lines connecting to the greater storage crystal flared up as bright and energetic as the sun.

Samantha was cackling as it went over a hundred mana a second, and rapidly depleting her own capacity.

“Incredible!” she cried.

But it finally began to emit a high-pitched, dangerous whine once at 120 or so. The sweet spot was 111, it seemed.

She spent around an hour dumping as much as she could in. The best method was to dematerialize to the spirit world and meditate for increased speed of regeneration. Itha Ranon once again massively boosted mana regeneration thanks to its Nexus status. Her 5% per minute, multiplied by seven, effectively 35% of her mana per minute.

She quickly found that through [Deific Pneumamancy], she could channel mana from the spirit world into the device just as easily. She also used [Abjurer: Ultimate Protection Circle] for 14 MP every 5 seconds, stacking while meditating and regenerating even while channeling.

It was definitely up there for mental and spiritual fatigue… she felt quite drained and rung out after the period of channeling. However, she’d just poured in over forty thousand mana into the receptacle, as it had very little loss in the transfer — less than ten percent. That was forty thousand points of ‘FE-like,’ light-aspected energy, technically called Divine Energy (Light).

When she shifted back to the physical world, the giant crystal glowed brightly with the energy it had absorbed. It could contain more, too. She wasn’t sure, but it could hold perhaps nine to ten times as much energy as it had, which should be more than enough for their purposes.

The air of the place was heavily saturated with mana, burning her skin perceptively. It was likely hazardous to others for the rest of the day, and quite inadvisable to continue channeling for a while.

Nonetheless, Samantha was all grins. Another plan was coming together, and a bunch of people had just completed quests, including her.

The quest “Energetic Assembly” is complete. 5(4) exp gained in [Goddess], 12(6) exp gained in [Magineer], 5(4) exp gained in [Bard]. 33 MP gained. Maximum Faith Energy increased by 20,000.

It was now just up to Orswyth to devise how to utilize the mama jama. There was no quest for her there. In fact, she’d lose something in the process, she was sure — a piece of herself.

In any case, it still required time, and she had plenty more territory to cover before it was relevant. Back to the grind!
