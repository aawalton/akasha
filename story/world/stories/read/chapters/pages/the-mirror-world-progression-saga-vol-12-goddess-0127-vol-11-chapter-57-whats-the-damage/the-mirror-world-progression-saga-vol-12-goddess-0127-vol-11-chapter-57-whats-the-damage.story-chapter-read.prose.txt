Taking advantage of the distraction, Samantha moved on to the final three things to review. First up was the other [Spellfyr: Alter Spell] addition.

Nice. I can only imagine how many times this would’ve been handy.

Samantha’s brows knitted together at ‘Entropy’ being in there. It probably didn’t belong, but she was happy to see it.

Ozzy’s shenanigans again. I’ll take it.

The final change was a doozy: Foolmaker had been altered. Echostrike, Spell Absorption, and Cone of Sound were removed, which was fine, because she’d mostly transcended them already. Knockout and Magi’s Rapport remained, the former upgraded. New shit was added.

Samantha gasped and touched her heart. “Ozzy, you shouldn’t have — my very own pocket shaman! Oh, it’s just what I always wanted!”

Samantha flopped sideways on the couch and sighed in contented satisfaction at all her obtained awesomeness. She summoned Foolmaker and hugged it, even kissed it, ignoring Azure’s annoyed frown.

She spent some time just daydreaming about the mix of magnificent creation and terrible destruction of her enemies that she would inevitably be dishing out.

New Quest unlocked: “Final Gift” — Snuff out the Silver Candelabra.

What the hell is the Silver Candelabra?

Some time later, Samantha was hanging out on a balcony, overlooking the gorgeous sunrise as she juggled various ‘realm things.’ For this, her eyes were fully open, too. Azure was resting as usual after a sexual session, and Samantha had taken the opportunity to finally retrieve her crown, which was now back on her head where it belonged. Despite the recent ‘enterprise,’ she was fully dressed and publicly available, which she knew would mean inevitably being told on by Jeeves and getting ‘accessed.’

Indeed, it wasn’t long before someone showed up. Orswyth.

Samantha smiled as he walked over. “Hey there, young man.”

“Hey there, old girl,” he replied with a faint smirk.

Samantha shifted to a playful glare. “Now, that’s hardly fair. Take it back, or I’ll have to revoke your flattering nickname privileges. You don’t even want to hear the unflattering ones.”

Orswyth smiled as he held up his hands in surrender. “I’m sure I don’t. I take it back, young lady. And I’ll even add the appropriate: Your Majesty.”

“That’s more like it!” She looked back out at the sunrise, and Orswyth did so with her for a spell. Finally, she broke the silence and asked, “What’s the damage, then? You’ve got bad news written all over you.”

Orswyth sighed and replied, “Well, it’s good news for you and the realm, practicality wise. Quite tolerable for you, personally, even. It’s just that Azure won’t like it.”

“That’s never good. Well, out with it, then. Lay it on me. Is it about the new artifact you’ve been selling your soul to fashion?”

Orswyth nodded. “Yes, it is. For it to work at its best?” He met her eyes soberly. “You’ll need to replace a certain part of yourself, as a technical sacrifice.”

Samantha suddenly smiled. Somehow, she knew it would be weird and that she’d like it. “Technical, you say?”
