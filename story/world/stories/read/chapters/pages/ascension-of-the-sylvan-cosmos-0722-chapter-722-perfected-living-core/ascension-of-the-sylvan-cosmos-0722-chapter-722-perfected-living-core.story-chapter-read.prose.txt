After Liu Shu poured her energy into the skill, World Tree Heart began a terrifying transformation.

The jump from A+ onward in the major tier would always be a qualitative jump. So, as Liu Shu felt the changes, her body began adapting and transforming. A single S Rank skill, if it were combat- or defense-related, could completely overhaul the capabilities of any existence.

For instance, Liu Shu’s World Tree Heart had always boosted her passive regeneration. If another World Tree would usually need 1 week to fully recover, Liu Shu’s main body could easily recover in a little over a day.

Such recovery speed was something that would leave even regeneration-based creatures somewhat befuddled.

However, boosting her regeneration further was not the path that her skill was taking.

Because of her <Realm>, the commotion was not large enough to cause ripples outside. However, the changes were extremely comprehensive.

[Congratulations! <World Tree Heart (A+ Rank Level MAX)> has evolved into <Perfected Living Core (S- Rank Level 1)>!]

Liu Shu felt an extremely painful sting as her core transformed. If she were a human, she would’ve undoubtedly bent over while clutching her stomach due to pain.

Thankfully, the pain was short-lived, and soon, she recovered. “That hurt a lot… It has been a while since I sensed something that bad. Let’s check the new skill.”

***

<Perfected Living Core (S- Rank Level 1)>: The World Tree Heart has managed to evolve into a Perfected Living Core. Finally, you’ve gained a new trait: <Adaptability>.

Liu Shu blinked many times, her face showing pure bewilderment. “Isn’t this the trait that Dungeon Bosses of the Mythical and above tiers have?”

A <Dungeon Boss>, as a living creature, could naturally be controlled by paralyzing and concussing skills that left it helpless. However, if you repeated your control skills or spammed them, the Boss would gain resistance to the effects.

So, if the group could paralyze the boss for 5 seconds on the first try, it would be reduced to 2.5 seconds on the next, 1.25 seconds on the following, and so on.

Moreover, as far as Liu Shu knew, <Adaptability> would linger for several minutes at a time!

‘So… if an army attacks my main body, trying to apply status control abilities like soul daze and such, I will eventually become immune to them?’

Skills of that kind, which forced the other party into a daze, paralysis, or similar effects, were extremely scarce. Still, they existed, and whoever had one would become quite important in any group.

Liu Shu, however, read one word that made her doubt her sight. “Here, it also says attacks, doesn’t it?”

She knew that <Adaptability> would have a cap in that regard. No matter how much she adapts, she would naturally be unable to fully become immune. At least, Liu Shu believed so. Moreover, the rate of decrease would not be as powerful as with control effects. “Even then… This is just another independent damage reduction effect, is it not?”

She had her passive defensive percentage over 90%, the immunity Warlya had shared with her, and, on top of that, her own body was incredibly solid and sturdy. Now, beyond all of that, she had gained the ability to adapt to continuous damage.

“Wait… Doesn’t this mean that the main cannons of the ships are now much worse?”

The cannons' power was a continuous beam of raw essence that would tear at a surface. However, that continuous damage would, in turn, trigger her <Adaptability>, making her semi-immune within just a few seconds.

“Wow. So strong! (✧0✧)!”

Liu Shu was very satisfied. She felt that the 15 million Experience Points were extremely worth it!

“Now!” Liu Shu giggled, and her face gained a mischievous smile. “Hehehe. I can leave my <Core Room> with my <Avatar>. Hehehehe.”

She had been extremely eager for this! While she didn’t mind having her <Avatar> in her <Core Room>, as it was just a very small part of herself, Liu Shu knew that Flor and Ignatia had been extremely eager to see her since the time they saw her when she was almost corrupted.
