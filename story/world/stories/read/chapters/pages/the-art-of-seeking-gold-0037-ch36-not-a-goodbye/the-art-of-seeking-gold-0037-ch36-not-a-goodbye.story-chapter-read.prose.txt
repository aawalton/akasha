She opened it, unsure what she expected to see. The pages stayed blank. Yet as she kept looking, she began to sense something through their bond. Possibilities, though she couldn't have pointed to any drawing or words on the paper.

Then she caught a glimpse. It was gone before she could examine it properly, and she drew in a sharp breath. The book was warm again.

"I'm gonna end this." She said it quietly, in case Libris could still hear her, then tucked it into her satchel and got up.

What she remembered from the manga hadn't been much use today. She couldn't quite give up on it, though. There had to be something she could still use, especially now that she had this ability as well...

Lyra noticed her getting up. "Amy? Are you alright?" She was looking at her like that again.

"You wanted to talk, right?" Amy was pleased she'd managed to say it without her voice shaking. "We can do that on the way."

"On the way where?"

"There's somewhere we need to be before Abaddon gets here."

"But Lain and Iris aren't back yet. Amy, we can't just—"

"We can't wait, either." She went over to Crow, who was still unconscious, breathing steadily while Lyra healed him. "We need time to put up defenses. That's only the start of it."

Zayd opened his eyes so abruptly that Amy nearly jumped. Had he been listening the whole time? "Would you like me to come with you?"

"No. Stay here with Ash. When the others get back, tell them where we've gone." Amy held his gaze. "Can I trust you with that?"

He took a while to answer, studying her face as if he hoped to catch her out. "Very well. Though I'd like to know how you found somewhere to go. I don't even recognize this part of the Academy."

"Good sense of direction." Well, it wasn't completely a lie.

Lyra got up too, rubbing her hands together. "I can't leave Crow like this. He's still healing. If something happens while I'm gone—"

"Then we'll bring him."

"Bring him? Amy, he's in no condition to be moved! Besides, he'd never agree to—"

"He owes me," Amy said. "A pretty big favor, actually. It's time he paid it back."
