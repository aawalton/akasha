The scream echoed along the dunes and out over the waveless sea. Even as far away as the source was, it sent chills down my spine.

I knew who it was.

Calliope found Allium.

Nuzzle scurried up to my shoulder and cuddled against my neck, sensing the distress.

I was grateful I wasn't there, that I'd hidden my tracks. I couldn't see her face. I couldn't feel that guilt even more than I already did. Maybe that was cowardly.

No, it certainly is.

It didn't matter.

I had no idea how she'd found him so fast. I'd keep going south before turning back into the Meadow to avoid crossing paths.

She'd know it was me who took him. I'd probably made an enemy today, but she and Jericho couldn't go where I was going anyway. They were content in the Meadow. I needed to advance and enter the inner zones to accomplish my goals.

I wished it hadn't been like this, but it was ending one way or another.

I'm on my own now.

My clothes weren't dry yet, but I had no intention of taking chances waiting around here so close to the lighthouse. I dragged the wet shirt and pants on, grimacing as they suctioned to my skin and kept getting stuck. I still had no shoes.

Finally clothed, I propped Nuzzle on my shoulder and prepared to head south. Away from the lighthouse. Away from Allium's remains. Away from Calliope and Jericho.

There's no going back now.

I could've used the Notice of Resignation at this time, but it seemed too valuable to waste, especially after realizing I could've avoided battling the high rank Unchained Sins on the beach altogether.

I took one last glance in the direction of the scream, which I knew had turned to sobs. Then I turned to head south, only to find I was no longer alone.

Standing in my way, scythe already summoned and ready, was the god of thunder himself…

"Zeus," I said.

"Zephyr," he replied. "Fancy meeting you here."

He eyed the pillar near me. His scythe glowed with a dim Amber aura.

"Were you following me?" I asked.

"Believe it or not, this is just a happy coincidence. I was in the area for a job."

What the hell kind of job would bring him way out to the coast? And how is it that I come out here to escape people and EVERYONE I KNOW is suddenly here? It had to be a coincidence, right?

I already knew I couldn't trust a word Zeus said so I took his explanation with a grain of salt. More likely than not, he was stalking me.

He stepped toward me, twirling his scythe, straight through the edge of the pillar's cold area making his breath fog. His posture was somehow casual, off-hand in his pocket, with a hint of disguised aggression. My scythe was in my hand a moment later.

"Can't we play nice?" he crooned.

"We don't need to play at all," I offered, subtly sliding my Notice of Resignation out of my exoskeleton inventory in a way Zeus wouldn't be able to see it. Maybe now is the time to use it after all. Sibyl had said there were different ways to activate different items. The scroll was sealed with wax, so obviously that meant…

I tried to peel off the wax. It wouldn't budge.

[Notice of Resignation cannot be used during battle.]

Oh, for fuck's sake. Now you tell me? And you call this a battle? That's a pretty subjective interpretation, stupid System.

Zeus had started walking in an arc around me. Nuzzle hissed at him.

"I see you still have your little slug."

"Lizard," I corrected.

He leaned forward, a disinterested expression on his face. "Whatever." His expression changed as he growled and swung his scythe at my head.

I leaned away from the wide arcing weapon, but Zeus already had a flurry of sparks dancing around his other hand. He flung them crackling through the air at me. I Aero Shifted a few feet to the left, dodging the sudden attack.

We both paused, reassessing.

"You've learned a few things since we first met in the Meadow."

"And you're still the same asshole," I countered.

I couldn't see Zeus' Health or Volition, but if he had recently battled a Sin—as it appeared from his glowing scythe—then he'd be at a disadvantage compared to my fully replenished Volition. Then again, he'd presumably been here a lot longer and been gaining stat augmentations with each Will he relinquished. I also had no idea what his second Affinity was.

Realizing that, my confidence waned.

I need to end this fast.

The Syringe I'd earned from one of my first battles crawled from my carapace and I grabbed it in my off hand, plunging the needle into my leg.

[Syringe - 5cc Activated

Effect: Small boost to Strength and Stamina for 30 seconds]

Adrenaline coursed through my veins. My heart pounded. Damn, that's strong. I didn't expect such a visceral response.

The syringe disintegrated into nothing after use.

I just hoped that Strength boosted damage from all attacks and not just physical ones. There didn't seem to be a magic-associated stat aside from Volition, which was more about capacity than damage.

I cast Life Drain at Zeus. The smokey tendrils shot from my palm. They stretched out to him, searching for his eyes and mouth. Then, the smoke took a sudden turn toward the ground at his feet. It latched onto a small bearded figurine with a red cone hat sitting in the sand. A… A garden gnome?

Seriously, what the fuck is with this place?

"You're not the only one who can use items, Death Reaper."

I tried to cut off my Life Drain, but it wouldn't stop.

"Oh, did I mention my little friend draws an ability toward it for a full ten seconds? I watched you brawl in the arena. I know your weaknesses."

I panicked as my Volition continued to drain like a toilet that won't stop running.

Zeus cast another lightning bolt at me, but I Shifted away. Even that didn't interrupt my Life Drain.

But maybe…

I Aero Shifted right up to the gnome and sliced through it with my scythe. The figurine shattered, breaking its hold on the skill. Smoke dissipated. I'd probably only saved myself two or three seconds, but every bit of Volition counted. Especially considering how much Life Drain used up.

I turned to face Zeus, but he was already upon me. How'd he move that fast?!

Sharp energy jolted through my abdomen as he punched me with an electrified fist. I flew backwards, sliding several feet in the sand. The rough grains went all down my shirt and pants, sticking to my wet back and clothes. Steam rose off my stomach.

My health dropped nearly a quarter from that single blow.

Zeus gave me no respite, jumping at me with scythe overhead. I Aero Shifted into a roll, just in time, as the blade stuck into the sand right where my head had been.

My feet found their place under me, as I got up. I had no time to brush the sand off, probably looking like a crazy abominable sand monster. Instead, I Aero Shifted forward, fist coiled for a punch. And…

I missed.

Instead, a searing pain radiated from my leg. The blade of Zeus' scythe pierced completely through my thigh, sticking out the other side, all the way to the shaft. I dropped to a knee, my Health followed. Blood poured from the wound and my Health continued to decrease.

He'd moved to the side even faster than I had in my Aero Shift. He'd…

Holy shit…

His second Affinity.

He wasn't just moving faster. He was Aero Shifting too.

I thought I had a chance in this fight, but he was so far above me, even though we were both Ambers. He really had been bolstering his stats before advancing. I'd been disillusioned by my brawl in the arena, by the battles with the Sins.

This was an entirely different game.

And I was outmatched.

Zeus removed the scythe from my leg and retracted it, blood spraying in the air as it twirled into his carapace arm.

I could hardly move. Nausea bubbled in my stomach. My Health and Volition were low. I tried to summon my Silver Engagement Ring shield, but Zeus kicked my hand and the item tumbled away.

Nuzzle jumped out from his hiding spot under the folds of my cloak, latching onto Zeus' face. He clawed and bit, leaving read streaks.

Zeus howled and ripped him off, throwing him to the side. But Nuzzle returned with a vengeance, gnawing at his ankles, tearing through skin. His retaliation didn't last long though, as Zeus' boot caught him full force. He yelped as he launched through the air.

He didn't come back after that.

Zeus smirked, blood dripping from the scratches on his face. He placed both hands on my ears and grinned. Then he sent a zap of electricity through my skull, frying the neurons in my brain as the bolt travelled from one hand to the other via my skull. I felt my hair standing on end, smoking.

My body slumped, still sitting, but barely.

Why? Why did he hate me so much? Why was he willing to kill me—actually kill me? Did he… Does he not realize?

I looked at his feet. His shadow was barely visible. The sky was still gray… In the cloud cover it wasn't as obvious, he hadn't even noticed I didn't have one.

He picked me up by the neck with his carapace hand.

"Wait!" I grunted, my voice hoarse. "If you kill me, I'll actually die." A last plea. I knew it sounded desperate. Too desperate to be believable. I wiggled my legs pathetically as he lifted me higher into the air, clutched in the armored hand. Sparks crackled around it, stinging my neck and ears. "I have no shadow. I won't go to Vitria." What had Valciferous said would've happened to Kaiju if he'd killed me in the arena? "You'll be Marked."

Zeus didn't avert his eyes from mine. "Nice try."

Oh, shit. Shit shit shit.

"No! It's—"

His lightning zapped through my chest and he threw my limp body to the damp sand at the waters edge.

I knew with certainty, laying there—hopeless, wet, and bloody, as Zeus' boots stepped closer in the sand… that I was about to die.
