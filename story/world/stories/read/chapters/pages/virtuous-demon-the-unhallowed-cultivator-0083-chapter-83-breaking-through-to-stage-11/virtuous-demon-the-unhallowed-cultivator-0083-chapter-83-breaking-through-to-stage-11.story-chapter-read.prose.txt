Over ten days had passed since then. The time of the duel was coming.

“Tell me Shuren, are you an idiot?” Yan was telling him off. “Why aren’t you focusing on reaching Stage 11 instead of this nonsense? You think you’re gonna become a Core Disciple wasting time like this?!”

When Shuren opened the door from his room, the last thing he expected was to be greeted by Yan.

“A small matter, just a duel with some runt,” Shuren answered with a dry tone, his eyes unfazed and sunken.

Yan crossed her arms with a stern face, but she eased her tone seeing the tiredness in Shuren’s face. “Listen to me. Someone’s spread the news, and now everyone on the Seven Clouds knows of your stupid duel. They’re all saying why would someone from the Liu Clan almost at the level of Path Foundation challenge a Stage 6 orphan.”

“He’s not Stage 6. That disciple you mentioned injured my cousin to the point he’s still unconscious,” Shuren retorted, slightly raising his voice. “His conditions aren’t even to defeat me, it’s only making me fall on my knees, or did they purposely spread that information without mentioning it? Either way, if you saw my cousin’s state, you would understand.”

“You should’ve just left it to the administration. They don’t take these matters lightly,” Zhou Yan grumbled before facing the room. She went for the boiling kettle, inscribed to keep the water always near boiling temperature. She ignored Shuren’s presence and walked inside to pour herself a cup of spiritual tea.

Shuren had gotten her addicted to it long ago. Yan also brought out a small letter from her robes, opening it up to read as she drank.

Shuren went back inside to grab a robe. He only had a simple shirt on during his training. “Stop worrying. I just need that brat to apologise in public, and accept his wrongdoings.”

“Yes, yes, I understand, young master of the tea clan,” Yan snapped back before sighing as she finished the contents of her letter. “To think Celes is leaving soon… At least she left a contact point for me.”

“A friend of yours is leaving? A disciple of the sect?” Shuren asked, as he cycled Qi to observe his internal body.

“You could say that. Some family issues popped up so she had to leave. It sounded pretty bad.”

“I see.”

Shuren observed his rather empty Spirit Meridians. Though I dropped to the beginning of Stage 10 during that mission, I managed to climb up quickly since then. After all, I almost made it to Stage 11 before. To reach the next stage of Qidense Realm, not only would you need basic understanding of Qi control, but the accumulation of Qi in one’s Spirit Meridians was vital the further you proceeded.

By Stage 9, he had learnt to store Qi inside his Spirit Meridians, control the flow of Qi in his body, learn Qi Tether by binding it to objects outside of his body, and reinforce parts of his body at will with but a thought. He even learnt how to transform his Qi to gain aspects of the winds; controlling Wind Qi with some techniques.

Stage 9 to 12 was all about transmuting Qi into a technique, one that made use of the laws of the world. Whether that be reinforcing his Ruyi Staff and transmuting bursts of Qi into Wind techniques, Shuren had understood them all.

“Yan, has your family managed to secure any Candeseal leaves? ” Shuren changed the subject.

She placed the cup down with a clink. “No, someone else has already harvested them all. We sent people to investigate and still don’t even know who did it, and it’s not even popping up on the market.”

Shuren sighed. He wanted to use those leaves to make spiritual tea, for its mind clarifying effects could’ve been further strengthened.

“I’m heading off to the waterfall behind the fourth. The next time you’ll see me, I’ll be at Stage 11,” Shuren announced as he walked towards the door. It took him a breath to realise he’d gotten far too used to her presence, and he had even given her cloudmulet access to his personal room.

She’s also getting to know my father well, and he plans to invite her family to the banquet held in my name. Don’t tell me he already told her…

Shuren looked back at his room, staring at Yan. The woman who was sitting at his desk, drinking from one of his cups. Unfortunately, her family head was a Rank 5 Nascent Soul, not a single Immortal Grand Pathstrider existed in her family, unless they had some hidden ancestor.

Yan held her eyes closed, enjoying a cup of spiritual tea, a new recipe she made. A flowery fragrance enveloped the room soon enough, with a slight minty touch.

“Since when did we get this close?” Shuren bluntly said.

When she faced him, she gave a look of utter contempt. “You know the answer, surely. Yet you ask me this stupid question?”

Shuren chuckled. “We’ll talk about this after the banquet.”

With a smile finally adorned on her face, she joked around. “Sure, just don’t lose your silly duel.”

The room went silent for a moment, with Shuren giving a dead pan expression for a while. His laughter broke that silence. “Hahahaha, now you’re the fool saying stupid things. It’s impossible for me to lose.”

As she continued to drink in his room, Shuren left.

He stepped on the flying nimbus, and took in the air as he headed towards the route Haze had once shown him. To the flourishing region hidden behind the mountain, where a waterfall fell across half its height.

But along the way, amidst the flora and fauna of the Seven Clouds; the butterflies flocking to the earthly gourds blooming from horizontal trees, the white-furred squirrels gathering ice from the height of the waterfall, and the beetles that scoured the mountain, breaking off tiny pieces to eat as their food, Shuren held a complicated expression.

While he looked towards his destination, his half-lidded eyes observed his own future.

I’ve been so focused on my goals, I didn’t expect to get close to Yan in the past year. The question is if I’m willing to pursue this or not?

The road to becoming patriarch of the Liu Clan was no simple matter. And a twisted fear crept up into Shuren’s heart. He could only shake it off with the following thought.

I must stand firm on my goal, with complete focus. I must become a Core Disciple as soon as possible. And I must not forget the humiliation I faced, to reforge my own path!

“Enough flying around, I need to warm up my body.” With a single spark of thought, the cloud dissipated and Shuren fell straight down, jumping between trees and the side of the mountain. Even as he weaved and landed between thin branches, rather than breaking under his pressure, Shuren was easily able to shift his weight to keep it untouched.

There was a strict rule in the Seven Clouds Sect when Shuren first joined. To keep the premises outside of the training grounds as preserved as possible. You were considered a mindless buffoon if you couldn’t use the mastery of your Qi to minimise your damage on sect grounds, while at the same time expressing the same level of power.

If some idiot just went out and destroyed a large amount of the natural environment behind these mountains, they’d be forced to pay everything off no matter how long it would take. This was the basic etiquette as a member of the sect.

And if you wished to collect whatever you found within the Seven Clouds Sect, like if Shuren took some honey persimmon, he’d have to ensure to only grab a very few amount from the resource point, using his common sense. There were enough tales of greedy disciples taking everything in sight, only to end up in a sorry sight themselves when investigated.

The preservation of vital resource points was the most important factor. There were only certain portions where a faction would take what they needed, while leaving a good amount as reserves, and in the meanwhile continuing to expand and grow the resource point. The vocations of Spiritual Gardeners, and Beast Tamers, were very well rewarded for these duties.

Shuren adeptly landed on a boulder in the river where the waterfall landed, leaping off it to land on the ground nearby. Taking off his ornate, sky-blue robes, and folding them in a small bag, Shuren wore a simple shirt and airy pants. With a clang of his Ruyi Staff on the floor, he cracked his neck to the side and stretched his shoulders, while using a simple muscle tensing exercise emboldened with his Qi.

The Ruyi Staff stood perfectly still on the ground, as Shuren tethered his Qi to it with ease.

“To think reaching Stage 11 even after my set back would be fast when I focused on the staff. Hah.”

With a crack of his knuckles, Shuren zoned in. The very first thing he had to do was bring himself to the brink of collapsing, by releasing all of his martial power.

At the same time, he wanted to keep the sliver of Qi attached to the Ruyi Staff as is. If he couldn’t even keep the Ruyi Staff straightened on its own, while exerting his own powers, then he clearly wasn’t ready for the next stage.

Facing exactly towards the waterfall from the riverbank, Shuren entered a stance, with his feet shoulder width apart, his left foot slightly forward, and his arms raised to his chest, clenched into fists. Ensuring his hips and shoulders were exactly aligned against the waterfall in the distance, Shuren tightened and engaged his abdominal muscles.

And then, his right fist tight and straight, he prepared to forge himself towards the next stage.

“Now then. It’s about time I made it through to Stage 11.”
