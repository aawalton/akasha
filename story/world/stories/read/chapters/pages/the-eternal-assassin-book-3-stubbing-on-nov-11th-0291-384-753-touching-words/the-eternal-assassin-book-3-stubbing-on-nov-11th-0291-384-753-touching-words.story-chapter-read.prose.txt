Asher could do little more than stare at the Knuckle trapped within Clare’s chains. Owl’s sudden reappearance had been just about the last thing he’d expected to happen today, doubly so with his mind so preoccupied with the Council of Death and taking down all the liches. The Head of the Assassin’s Guild had been silent for months now, not making even the slightest of moves since Asher wiped out all ten of their Knuckles in one fell swoop. Why they chose now of all times to reveal themselves was beyond him.

“Help?” Asher repeated, shaking his head as he stared at the shadow man. “How long have you been tailing me in my own shadow?!”

The sole reason he hadn’t already thrust a dagger into Owl’s head and destroyed the skill a second time was because he remembered the brief conversation he’d had with the Knuckle before his original demise. Asher had tried to explain to Owl how their fight was with the Head and not him. It wasn’t exactly the same situation, but elemental kin such as Wilna and Helpha were living examples that when a person’s skill involved the creation of semi-sentient constructs, they could become their own people. If there was even the slimmest chance they could turn one of the Head’s skills against them, it was worth making the attempt.

“Only for the last minute or so,” Owl said, his voice continuing to echo from their surroundings. “I was about to make my presence known before you teleported away as I can’t follow that, which was when Clare grabbed me.”

“Ohh the freaky shadow man knows my name?” Clare asked. With a dangerous grin, she gave Owl a more thorough scan from head to toe. “Asher, is your friend here single? He is really my type.”

For once, Bordan didn’t even bother to admonish his apprentice. The truthseeker’s face was hard and his eyes practically glowed while he watched the Knuckle. Slowly, Bordan nodded.

“He’s telling the truth. About coming to ask for your help I mean.”

“Alright,” Asher said, more than a little surprised Bordan hadn’t already tried to cleave through the shadow man himself. As a truthseeker, Bordan knew all about the Head and their Knuckles. The fact that he didn’t seem to have a ‘kill on sight’ order was curious. “Just to satisfy my own curiosity, what exactly do you need my help with?”

“The Head is in danger, and I need your help to save them,” Owl said simply, causing Asher and Bordan both to blink in surprise. “If nothing is done, they will be wiped out. And if they die… I don’t know what will happen to me.”

“Hold on, you seriously expect me to go out of my way to save the Head of the Assassin’s Guild?” Asher asked, looking at Owl like he was insane. “Do you not remember me specifically explaining to you the last time we spoke that our fight wasn’t with you but with the Head themselves? Why would I help them of all people? After the Council of Death, who I’m a little busy killing today, for the record, the Head is probably my greatest enemy.”

“What sort of danger is the Head in?” Bordan asked. His brow furrowed and his gaze intensified, as if he already suspected what answer he was going to get.

“The capital of the Noala Kingdom is going to be destroyed,” Owl stated. “Everyone within its walls, the Head included, will be wiped out before the day’s end.”

For the second time in as many minutes, Asher could do nothing more than stare at the Knuckle trapped within Clare’s chains as his mind worked to process the words that had just left Owl’s nonexistent mouth. After a tense moment of confused silence, Asher barked one word.

“Explain.”

“Wiping out the ten Knuckles dealt the Head a far more grievous wound than you realized,” Owl said, surprising Asher by the admittance. “They were forced to pull back from the Guild entirely while waiting for their tritessence to recover. This took a significant amount of time, and once it was finished, they required even more time to manifest the ten Knuckles once again. We are the product of the Head’s ultimate skill. The one blending all three of their elements together.”

“That makes sense. Your existence is incredibly powerful for a skill,” Asher muttered, thinking back to the only other ultimate skill he’d witnessed. Donvath had a skill that pulled from his Time, Mind, and Knowledge elements that was capable of restoring his mindless father back to coherency and even allowed him the use of his own powerful elements again. Asher didn’t know much about these ultimate skills beyond their existence, but he wasn’t surprised to hear that was how Owl had been created.

However, he was surprised at the fact that Owl was telling them all this. As if seeing that confusion on his face, Owl inclined his head slightly, the shadows across his body rippling faster.

“After I was recreated… I recalled the conversation the two of us shared in the astral realm while my body was torn apart piece by piece. Severing me from the constant connection I had with the Head for my entire existence was eye opening, and I realized that I didn’t actually have to follow their orders. I wanted to, I could understand that much. But I did not have to. This level of self awareness was concerning and confusing and without knowing what else to do, I ran away.”

“The hells? How can you just run away?” Clare asked, looking even more invested in Owl’s story than either of the two of them. “I mean, if I’m following along correctly, you’re a part of his skill. Doesn’t that make you, like, a part of his soul?”

“Yes. Which is why this is all so confusing,” Owl admitted. “The Head did not take me abandoning my post lightly, but as I understand it, they are unable to destroy me without destroying all the other Knuckles as well. We are one, in a way, even if we have our own thoughts. As I said, recreating the ten of us is an exhausting feat that takes quite some time, so they were not willing to end the skill just to destroy me and bring me back again.”

“They still have nine functioning Knuckles, so that makes some sense,” Asher muttered, peering curiously at Owl. “Alright, so you ran away. What have you been doing all this time? And maybe speed it up a little? As interesting as your life story is, I want to know why you think the capital is going to be destroyed.”

“Whikoga has been all I’ve known my entire life,” Owl said simply, ignoring his last remark. “Almost without thinking, I found myself drawn to this city once more. These past few weeks I have hid in the shadows, watching the city breathe while lost in thought. I am still connected to the Head and could feel their rage and commands for me to return to them, but for once, I chose to ignore them. Things had been quiet until today, when the zombies attacked. I still am not certain what drove me to do so, but I decided to intervene and help with the defense of the city. My time was spent dispatching the zombies that tried to gain entry to the city via the sewers.”

“The sewers!” Asher gasped, realizing he’d completely forgotten about them. There was a massive system of tunnels running underneath the city that led far beyond the outer walls. He’d spent a decent amount of time down there hunting down Eight before eventually killing him. Lethinal could have directed quite the force to stride through the sewers and attack Whikoga’s defenders from behind. It would have been a devastating blow, and quite possibly resulted in the fall of the whole city.

“When the zombies shifted from acting like a coherent fighting force to their more natural state, I left the sewers and returned to the surface. That is when I discovered you had joined the battle,” Owl explained. “Out of curiosity more than anything, I decided to watch you. I was still undecided as to whether I wanted to speak with you or not, when I felt it. My connection to the Head has been severed once more.”

“What do you mean ‘severed’?” Asher asked. “You’re not drawing tritessence from them anymore?”

“Correct. Just like during our last fight, I am once again relying on my own personal reserves to maintain my physical form. However, as we are not within the astral realm where even the slightest of movements cost tritessence, so long as I am careful, I am not at risk of deconstruction.”

“So why does you being severed from the Head make you think the capital is going to be destroyed?” Bordan asked. “What if they just found a way to cut loose the malfunctioning portion of their skill without damaging the other nine Knuckles?”

“As I explained, the other Knuckles and I are one. You have witnessed our ability to emerge from one another’s shadows. It would seem while the connection between the Head and myself was capable of being severed, the connection between me and my fellow Knuckles is too strong. While I cannot currently travel to them, I can still see out of their shadows, similar to a scrying skill. It is what I am looking at right now that made me decide to reveal myself and ask for your help.”

“And what do you see?” Asher asked, holding his breath as he waited for the reveal. He had a pretty good idea of where Owl was leading with this, but he was hoping beyond hope he was wrong.

“A sphere of pure tritessence has enveloped the entire capital of the Noala Kingdom. The people inside are panicking and doing whatever they can to attack the sphere, but it appears unbreakable. Power is coalescing around the sphere with every passing minute, slowly building to a critical point. When that point is finally reached, it is believed the sphere will implode upon itself, wiping out the entire capital in the blink of an eye.”

Before Owl had even finished his explanation, Asher tried to Recall to his Eternal Mark placed in his and Samantha’s bedroom within the royal palace. While he could tell that the skill was still there, just as Owl had said, it was as if there was an invisible wall blocking him from actually reaching it. He couldn’t scry the mark with the second-tier effect of the skill either, and Asher frowned at the confirmation that at the very least, something was wrong.

“I can’t get a hold of any of the truthseekers within the capital,” Bordan said, lowering his sending stone. “Not within the palace or the academy.”

Brian! Asher’s blood ran cold as he realized despite being in the safest place in the entire kingdom, Brian had inadvertently gotten swept up in his battle against the Council of Death after all. Sure enough, he couldn’t Recall or scry Brian either, and he clenched his fists while he tried to figure out what to do.

“Owl, how do you know what’s going on with the sphere? And how much time do we have?”

“Wolf is currently spying on the palace wizard, who is analyzing the sphere from the inside and trying to figure out a way through it. According to him, whatever ritual was constructed to create the sphere is far beyond his own abilities, but he can at least tell what it is currently doing. As to the time, he estimates a little under an hour before the sphere reaches critical mass.”

“Lethinal took advantage of us spreading our forces out to deal with all the different attacks,” Asher muttered, quickly putting the pieces together. “He has to know that my Recall was tied to locations within the capital. While we dealt with the liches, he pretended to be in Whikoga and Juhstain with the control nodes, while in reality, he was setting up this ritual around the capital. He played us! I knew he had to be planning something bigger than this!”

“Erasing the capital of the kingdom that dared spearhead the attack against the Council certainly sends a message,” Bordan said, seeming strangely calm about all this. “Owl, how did you expect Reaper to help?”

“He is capable of attacking tritessence directly. Something that should not be possible, as far as I understand it,” Owl explained. “He should be able to cut a hole in the sphere surrounding the capital, which the Head can use to escape.”

“There’s nowhere near enough time to evacuate everyone, and I can’t even make it to the capital in under an hour in the first place,” Asher said, starting to pace as he tried to come up with a solution. He’d been in plenty of bad situations before, but he’d never been outmaneuvered in such a way. “Donvath is probably still unconscious from straining himself so much to help with Ploryn, so we can’t rely on him. Whatever the case, I need to get to the capital, and I need to get there now. Samantha’s not fast enough, even with her skills. Bordan, how fast can your griffin get you there?”

“It would take a few hours,” the truthseeker admitted. “But there is one way to make it in time. Whikoga happens to have one of the fastest men in the entire Noala Kingdom living within its walls. He can get you there.”

“Perfect!” Asher said. He let out a sigh of relief as a single spark of hope ignited within his chest. “Where is he?”

“He’s not a fighter, so I’d assume he’s hunkered down within the Frent family estate,” Bordan said, pointing toward the fourth ring. “If you want to save the capital, your only chance is to have Theorn the pegasus courier fly you there.”
