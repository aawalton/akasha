This is going to be rough, Seth thought while gritting his teeth.

Colossus charged forward, Desert Aegis' shimmering layer forming over the already-damaged Quartz Armor. But even with his improved defenses, facing all three elementals would be suicidal. Seth and Nightmare followed, knowing they would need to take some of the hits.

This was no time to hold back, so Seth cast Beastmaster Rage.

His vision turned crimson, and a surge of power flooded him. His muscles bulged; each stride propelled him farther. The artificial rage flowed through Link, and Nightmare, now also cloaked in the red aura, cast Mantle of Terror. The first spell seemingly amplified the other, causing a suffocating wave to wash over the cavern. All three Dark Elementals froze in place for half a second despite their resistance to fear.

And it was all Seth needed to strike the closest enemy. Iron Fists materialized around his gauntlets as he buried his fist into the Elemental's head. Before the Rank-44 creature could even stumble, Nightmare sprang from the shadows and sunk his teeth into its arm with Shadow Bite. While they focused on their target, Colossus slammed into the other two Elementals, holding their attention with his bulk alone since his taunt spell was on cooldown.

Seth and Nightmare moved as one in a barrage of punches and bites to take out their target as quickly as possible. Seth's gauntlets bashed into the Dark Elemental, then blocked the retaliating slashes of its claws so the direwolf could counter. Just as he prepared another attack, Seth realized Colossus' Quartz Armor had begun to crumble under the assault of the other two Elementals, leaving the scorpion exposed beneath the aether of Desert Aegis.

Activating his Swift Eagle Bracelet and casting Huntbound Rush, Seth crossed the distance in an instant, feet flying above the cave's floor. He then used Shadow Step to appear in the scorpion's shadow, just in time to cross his arms and block the scythe-like claws about to cut through his partner's shell.

With the Agility boost from the artifact, Huntbound Rush, and Beastmaster Rage, Seth for the first time had the upper hand in terms of speed against the Dark Elementals.

His core pulsed and flooded his body with its instincts as he deflected the claws once again while he countered with a flurry of jabs. Nightmare cast Chains of Darkness, black tendrils ensnaring the Rank-48 to help them from a distance while still handling his own enemy.

Seth whirled around and, as the other Dark Elemental of the duo dissolved into a streak of shadow to evade his strike, began channeling aether into Phantom Punch. The moment the creature reformed, Seth was ready for it. The skeletal fist of the Undead spell arose around his own and crashed into the Elemental's head, obliterating it in an explosion of teal energy and shadows.

A wave of aether-debt nausea washed over Seth, which forced him to draw on the reserves from his Fine Storing Ring. The energy flooded his Well, so he turned to face the other Elemental—but then he noticed a pair of claws heading toward his torso.

He attempted to activate his Protecting Belt, yet he was a fraction of a second too slow. The attack raked across his chest and tore three deep gashes. Seth grunted in pain and punched the creature. Hissing with apparent anger at Seth's injury, Colossus tried to get the beast's attention.

It was then that Seth caught sight of Nightmare behind the scorpion.

The direwolf twisted away from his opponent's claws, but just as Seth, he wasn't quick enough. The shadowy talons struck his flank, tearing through fur and flesh and sending him stumbling across the stone. The Dark Elemental surged after him.

'Colossus, help Nightmare!' Seth shouted through Link.

The scorpion's gaze shifted to the direwolf before returning to Seth and his own injuries. The hesitation was obvious.

'Go! I’ve got this one,' Seth insisted.

Colossus screeched and charged at the Elemental that glided toward Nightmare, leaving Seth alone with the other Elemental.

The creature lunged almost immediately, and Seth met it head-on. Its claws swept toward him, and he dodged to the side before driving a gauntlet into its ribs. The Elemental’s body rippled around the impact before one arm solidified again and slashed for his throat.

Seth ducked beneath it.

Across the cavern, Colossus crashed into Nightmare’s attacker with enough force to hurl it away from the direwolf. The Elemental dissolved into a streak of shadow before the impact could hurl it into the wall, only to reform several paces away.

Nightmare pushed himself upright, blood running down his flank.

'You good?' Seth asked through Link as he blocked another swipe from his own opponent.

'I'll rip that thing apart,' Nightmare growled. 'Focus on the one before you.'

Good enough.

Seth’s Elemental suddenly disengaged, and its body flowed backward before its arm swept through the air. A crescent of pure darkness erupted from its claws. Seth’s eyes widened.

He threw himself aside as the attack screamed past him and carved a deep trench into the cavern wall. Before the creature could cast the spell again, Seth pushed himself up and poured aether into Huntbound Rush.

He crossed the distance in a blur. His gauntlet crashed into the Elemental’s chest, driving its half-solidified body backward.

On the other side of the cavern, Colossus and Nightmare had turned their own fight around. The scorpion kept the Elemental occupied from the front while Nightmare circled around it, waiting for an opening. Chains of Darkness erupted around the creature, thorny tendrils slowing its shifting form just long enough for Colossus to pierce it with its tails.

Seth couldn’t spare them any more attentio—his own opponent dissolved.

Shit.

His core pulsed a warning, and Seth spun just as darkness gathered behind him. The Elemental reformed with both claws already descending, and Seth barely managed to bring his gauntlets up in time.

As the impact rattled his arms, something trembled inside his Well.

Beastmaster Rage, Seth realized.

The spell was beginning to wane.

Seth could feel the power flickering through his body, becoming less stable with every passing second. He had thirty seconds at best, perhaps less, then the spell would end, and his Toughness would plummet. Against something this strong, that could be a death sentence.

Seth gritted his teeth and pressed on. He already knew the creature’s weakness. Its body became more physical whenever it struck, so rather than wasting energy attacking when it was in its fluid form, Seth waited for the brief moments of solidity while it attacked.

Claws swept toward his ribs. He twisted aside and countered, driving his fist into the Elemental's side. The creature shifted before his second punch could land.

The exchange continued, Seth slipping past attack after attack and punishing the Elemental whenever he could, but the openings were too brief for him to finish it. Seconds slipped away faster than he could wear the creature down.

Then the crimson haze in his vision faded.

Shit, he thought as the shadow-wreathed claws came for his face, forcing him to duck.

The attack passed over his head, barely a few inches away from going straight through his skull. A fraction of a second late and he would have been dead.

The Dark Elemental glided back a dozen feet and turned toward him, causing Seth to swallow. I could actually die here.

There was no protective barrier, no professor or anyone waiting to intervene. Nightmare and Colossus were still occupied with the other Elemental, leaving Seth alone against something that could now likely kill him with a single clean strike.

The creature slowly advanced, and Seth instinctively took a step back. His boot scraped against the stone, and something about the movement sent a flash of anger through him.

No.

Fear still twisted in his stomach, and every reasonable instinct told him that fighting an opponent like this while weakened was a terrible idea, but Seth refused to let that fear dictate what happened next. He had faced many beasts and men that others had expected to crush him because they were stronger.

Why should this creature be any different?

Seth stopped and planted his feet as the Elemental approached. His heart was still hammering against his ribs, but he raised his gauntlets and looked directly into the creature's hollow eyes.

Come on.

His core throbbed. The danger had dramatically increased, and with that danger came an undeniable thrill. To thrive and overcome any challenge. Don't worry. I'll crush it, he thought as if answering the Legacy in his chest.

Then the Elemental attacked.

Seth slipped beneath its claws and retaliated with a punch in its gut. Every sensation became sharper: the scrape of his boots against the cave's floor, the rush of air from the creature's claws, the shifts of shadow before its attacks.

The danger was still there. If anything, Seth understood it even more than before. Yet with that understanding came something else.

Excitement.

A grin spread across his face as his core pulsed again, sending a surge of power through his body. There it was—that exhilaration he had felt so many times without ever truly understanding it. The feeling of standing before something that should overwhelm him. To face the danger and thrive despite all the odds.

The next second, Seth became a phantom. He flashed past the Dark Elemental and wove through every slash before countering with his own fists, exploiting the injuries he'd already inflicted on it. Even without the rage spell, he still had the speed from Huntbound Rush and the last reserves of aether from his Swift Eagle Bracelet.

Furthermore, Agility wasn't the only facet tipping the scales. Seth could read the creature—its rhythm, its fear, all of its twitches. Predators? he scoffed inwardly. No. They were prey all along.

He was the hunter, and they were just insects scrambling to survive.

The moment the next swipe came, Seth slipped past it and drove his fist into the Dark Elemental's chest. The creature's shadowy body burst apart in silence, leaving only a glimmering black beaststone to clatter onto the ground.

Quickly, he then turned to assist Colossus and Nightmare, but halted—both of them were looking at him, both seeming rather relaxed. Their opponent was already gone, and a black crystal lay on the cave's floor next to their paws.

"Why didn't you join in?" Seth asked between heavy breaths.

'You were smiling,' Nightmare answered. 'And you seemed to have it under control. Why would we deny you that joy?'

Seth opened his mouth to argue, then closed it. The direwolf wasn't wrong. Danger was the one thing he sought as a Primalist. "You're right."

His fingers brushed against the three gashes on his chest; the pain there was nothing more than a distant ache. Each fight seemed to be forging a new kind of hunger within him—and he was now craving the next one, even though he was fully out of aether.

From the corner of his vision, he noticed a river of small, gray figures scurrying away from the mine in the distance. With all five of the nearby Dark Elementals now dead, the moles were finally free.

Before he could say anything, a wave of excitement surged from Colossus. 'Can we fight another one?' the scorpion asked, his eyes darting left and right as he looked for any sign of other mines.

Seth smiled. "In twenty minutes."

The aftermath of Beastmaster Rage was still weighing on his body, and the Toughness debuff made any fight way more dangerous. Charging in before then would be reckless, especially when they had no idea how strong their next opponent might be.

So he forced them to wait.

Seth used the time to drink a healing potion and recover as much aether as possible, while Nightmare and Colossus rested nearby. The scorpion spent most of those twenty minutes impatiently shifting his legs and staring into the darkness, clearly counting down every second until Seth was ready again.

Once the lingering weakness finally disappeared, Seth rolled his shoulders and pushed himself back to his feet. 'All right. Let’s find another one.'

Colossus perked up right away, while Nightmare rose beside him with a low growl. Through Link, Seth could feel the same hunger thrumming within both of his partners.

'I hope the next one is even stronger,' Nightmare said.

Seth grinned. 'So do I.'
