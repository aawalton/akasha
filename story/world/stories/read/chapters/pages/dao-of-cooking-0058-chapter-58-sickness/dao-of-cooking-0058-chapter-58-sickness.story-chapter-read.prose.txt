They had a fairly rich cuisine here, and while Lei was adamant about introducing new dishes, he also wished to sprinkle in some familiar tastes. Chicken soup was one of those familiar dishes.With winter closing in on Jiangzhen, it was almost universally accepted that chicken soup warmed both body and soul. Lei's soup would be just that, with a hint of spirituality in the mix.
