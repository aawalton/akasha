We have officially reached the end of Book 1 of Strength Based Wizard!

First and foremost, I wanted to thank all of you, the readers. Like so many other writers on this platform, I shared my story with little to no expectations of it finding an audience. Anyone taking a shot and on this story and reading it would be amazing. Luckily, I have so many awesome readers that not only take the time to read the story, but take additional time to write and share a comment (whether it's simply thanking me for the chapter, reacting to something funny or ridiculous, or theory-crafting...). These comments mean so, so much to a writer such as myself. They fuel me and only slightly give me a glimpse of what a specific brand of insanity may feel like (screaming into the void, only for it to yell back at you: "Thanks!"). You guys continue to be the true rockstars and have stuck with me and my not-ideally-smart (and often times dumb) protagonist, who is a work-in-progress. We can't all be Jelly Boy, unfortunately.

I would like to share some updates and additional details.

What's Next for STRENGTH BASED WIZARD?

Book 2, of course! But I'll be taking a brief pause from normally scheduled chapters (approx. 1-2 weeks) to compile the Book 1 manuscript and do an editing pass. What some of you may have gleaned from following along with Book 1: I publish on the fly, as I go. I don't have advance chapters on my Patreon (yet, possibly never) because you get everything I have, often times as soon as I have it (e.g., bonus chapters). My release schedule is consistent but not precisely so, as I am often jamming out chapters to get them to you all (often resulting in a number of dumb mistakes, which you guys are very helpful at suggesting the edits for). I was lucky enough to draw the attention of a publisher and sign a contract. That contract has obligations, starting with me delivering a properly formatted and decently polished manuscript for their editing team. As you may have also learned: I don't write fast. Especially compared to Royal Road standards. Two or three chapters a week is my max limit of writing given I have a full-time job that requires 60+ hours from me many weeks (then you factor in health & wellness, family, etc., the time for writing, let alone editing is thin).

I'm going to spend this time beginning Book 2, but that will be in the background as I edit Book 1. Revisions will be reflected on Royal Road as I complete them. Nothing in the story will change, but I may brush up the writing in places.

Book 2 will eventually commence on the same 2-chapters-per-week cadence as Book 1. In the meantime, I would love to hear from you all: What do you want to see more of in Book 2? What aspects of Joseph's world do you want explored more (where possible)?
