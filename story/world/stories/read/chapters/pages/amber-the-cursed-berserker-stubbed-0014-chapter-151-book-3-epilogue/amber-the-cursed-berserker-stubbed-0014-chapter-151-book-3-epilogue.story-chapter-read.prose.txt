Unlike normal cores which will be strained by something as simple as this, awakened ones will not. For such strain will just affect your body instead.

[As a champion you became a hero, and as a hero you became a legend. As a legend the Shattered Tower grants you access to the third floor of its treasury that is beyond the portal. A great reward awaits you.]
