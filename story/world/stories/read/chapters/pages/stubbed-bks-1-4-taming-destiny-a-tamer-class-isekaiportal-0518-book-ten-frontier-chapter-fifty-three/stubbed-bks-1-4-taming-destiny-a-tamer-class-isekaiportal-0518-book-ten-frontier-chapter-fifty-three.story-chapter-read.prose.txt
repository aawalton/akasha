Insight

Wow, I think to myself, blinking as I see the differences between my status now compared to what it was less than ten minutes ago. Where do I even start? The Class evolution that happened whether I wanted it to or not? The extra point per level that has now made getting levels at all even harder? The new Skills? The complete rearrangement of my status? The rather disturbingly conversational note at the end of the Class message?

I shake my head, excitement and trepidation making a heady mixture in my chest. Would this have happened if I’d chosen one of the merged Skills? I wonder.

Thinking about it, probably not – the first line in the notification talked about the ‘heavy weight’ in my internal matrix. Given that I didn’t suddenly gain a new Class just because I’d earned my ninth Shaping Skill, the number of Skills can’t be the ‘weight’.

No, the only thing that could have caused this is the way the array completely rearranged my internal matrix. I saw for myself how the array and my Class Skills – at least those in my previous Class – now sit on either side of my Core, roughly equal in position. Before, only my Class Skills had sat near my Core, surrounding it on all sides – the rest were placed further away.

Well, it’s done now. For better or for worse, I have to live with it. I wonder how Nicholas will take this, I think with a wry smile. Technically, I don’t carry the family Class anymore.

Then my smile fades. That…actually might be a problem. The heir almost always carries the family Class – that’s one reason why Nicholas chose not to give the position to his niece, because she’d inherited her father’s Class rather than her Titanbend mother’s. Though not exactly common, from time to time a child with two Classer parents will have a dormant Class that activates at their majority. Another reason why nobles tend to only marry other nobles – and preferably those with rarer Classes than their own.

So what does that mean for me now that I have a hybrid Class? Thankfully, I haven’t lost any Skills – the thought that I might have accidentally lost Dominate and its Subskill Companion Bond, or Tame and Alliance…. I shiver. Too many of my strategies are based on those to make me happy at the thought of losing them.

I want to think that Nicholas will take this in stride, as he has everything else I’ve revealed to him – if with some cursing, some of it aloud. I like to hope that we’ve built enough of a bond in the first few months I spent in this world, that his gratitude for my healing is enough that he’ll accept this new Class. I don’t even know if I’m capable of having children the normal way – it might be that I’ll have to choose an heir like Nicholas did anyway. And in that case, I can just give them the Titanbend Class from one of the stones in the treasure room – no issue.

But it would be irresponsible of me to not consider that he might react badly.

And if he does?

It’s unlikely that Nicholas would choose to disown me right now – that would land him back in the same boat he was in when he decided to pull me over in the first place. Except worse, because we’re already almost a year into the competition and anyone he pulled over would be far behind.

A more likely possibility is that he would choose to disown me at the end of the competition, and claim everything I developed within it for his House.

My expression firms at the thought, my lips pressing together. Well, he can try, I challenge, if only to myself. I have put far too much effort into building this settlement and these people to be willing to just step aside so easily.

Hopefully it won’t come down to that, though. The distance alone is a significant obstacle to any attempt to claim this colony by force. Sending an army by sea is, as we experienced, a risky prospect. The only real option would be to send fighters through the teleportation gates that we’ll establish as soon as we’re allowed to – at the end of the first year of the competition. And high-level fighters, at that, since the cost of sending more than a handful of people would be prohibitive.

Well, we’ll just have to make sure we have an answer to that – high level people of our own who could counter anyone who attacked. That’s a good strategy anyway – there will always be people who want to take or destroy what we have, if only so that we don’t have it anymore.

Of course, that also means making sure that those people are loyal to me, not to Titanbend. Elarion, Layton, Valence, Josiah…who would they stand behind if it came down to Nicholas or me?

I sigh and scrub my hands over my face.

It’s pointless to worry too much over something that may never happen. Take precautions, sure, but not give into paranoia.

Though I do have to decide who to tell about my new Class. My companions are obvious – they’re loyal to me. But my allies? Sarran?

Telling Sarran is the equivalent of telling Nicholas, and I’m not sure I want to do that yet, not when our messaging capacity only allows us to send messages to him, not receive replies in return.

Really, it would be best for Nicholas and I to talk about this face to face – which will only be possible when the teleportation gate has opened. But should I tell him ahead of time? On the one hand, it gives Nicholas warning and time to cool down if he becomes angry as a result. On the other…it also gives him time to put measures into place to…get rid of me.

Sighing heavily, I decide to think more about it later. For now, I look at the other details of my new Class transition.

It’s hard to feel enthusiastic about the changes when I have the nagging worry at the back of my mind about what they mean for my companions and me – we’d only just started feeling settled in this world.

Buck up, Markus, I tell myself sternly. Stop mourning what you haven’t even lost yet.

Swallowing, I close my eyes, then run my flesh-magic through my body, removing the traces of stress that my body has produced in reaction to my thoughts. Immediately, I feel better – calmer and more centred. There we go.

The first thing I do is request descriptions for both Element Sense and Elemental Awareness, Skills that, like Insight, don’t seem to increase in rank. Because they’re passives?

The System obliges, to my relief.

Element Sense (passive)

Effect 1: You have an instinct for what elements are present and their concentrations within a range determined by Wisdom. You also know instinctually whether any area within your range has a chance of spawning an elemental and any that are spawned immediately alert you.

Effect 2: You have an instinctive understanding of how a new element encountered works. If the element is derivative of one or more of the elements you are capable of Shaping, this effect is enhanced further. This effect increases with your Wisdom.

So it does scale, but only with one of my stats rather than independently, I muse, then look at the next description.

Elemental Enhancement

Bind an elemental to one of your Shaping Skills and enhance its effects. Choose whether this relationship is temporary or permanent.

Temporary relationships offer growth for both parties and the connection may be broken at any time by either Binder or elemental. The higher tier the elemental, the more enhancement it offers the element. Higher Wisdom allows more power to flow between elemental and Binder.

Permanent relationships consume the elemental and offer the Binder a greater power boost than temporary relationships. Multiple elementals can be Bound consecutively to a single element, though elementals may fight back and there is a risk of the Skill being damaged in the struggle. Higher Willpower reduces the chances of damage during the absorption, and reduces the length of time it takes the Skill to process the elemental and apply the boost.

That’s definitely been inspired by Tame and Dominate, I think grimly, looking at the second option. One that I doubt I’ll use. The thought of consuming Aingeal just to gain a power boost…yeah, no.

I’ll never say never – if an elemental attacks and won’t listen to reason and I’d have to kill it anyway…well, then maybe. But it certainly won’t be my first choice.

Still, the first part is exciting, and gives me a reason to search out more elementals. Given how Aingeal was created, I’m not sure I’ll try to make another one, but with Element Sense, I’ve got a better chance of just finding one spawning in the wild. I notice that it doesn’t say one way or another whether I can have multiple temporary bonds attached to the same element.

This new Class is definitely intriguing – the way elements have been incorporated with my existing Tamer framework.

Noticing that my existing Skill, Elemental Bonding, appears to have been enhanced, I request a description for that.

Elemental Bonding (Enhanced)

Elementals sense that you have a deep understanding and appreciation of the elements and are more likely to be curious than hostile at your presence, assuming a neutral relationship to begin with. Using the elements that you have learned to Shape, you are capable of communicating with any elemental from the same or a derivative element. The greater your skill in the element, the easier it is to communicate. This also increases with Wisdom.

Once a conversation has begun, positive or negative, you are capable of offering a Bond to an elemental – or forcing one upon them. Bonds are easier to create with elementals from the same elements that you have learned to Shape. Due to your Fire affinity, you are almost guaranteed to succeed in creating a Bond with a Fire elementals unless the Fire elemental is Superior or higher in rank. Increased Willpower increases the chances of a Bond being successful, offered or forced.

Unfortunately, it doesn’t appear to want to give me a comparison between the old and the new, but I think I can identify the differences well enough. The old Skill was mostly about getting the elementals’ attention, being able to Bind them, and increasing the chance of successful Bond with Fire and Fire-adjacent elementals.

This Skill still includes all of that, but now there’s a Tame or Dominate flavour to the text – it talks about both offering a Bond and forcing it upon an elemental – and it’s easier for me to create a Bond with any of the elements I’m able to shape. On that note, Lightning and Lava appear to be derivative elements, rather than ‘combined’. I’m not sure I like the term, but I guess I’d better go along with it for now.

The additional parts to the Skill are that it seems that my relationship ranking with elementals in general has gone up a notch – elementals that would normally have been hostile will now be curious, unless I’ve actually done something to anger them.

I close the description. I’m about to return to looking at my status screen when the hairs stand up on the back of my neck.

I’m not alone. And it’s not one of my companions come to visit.
