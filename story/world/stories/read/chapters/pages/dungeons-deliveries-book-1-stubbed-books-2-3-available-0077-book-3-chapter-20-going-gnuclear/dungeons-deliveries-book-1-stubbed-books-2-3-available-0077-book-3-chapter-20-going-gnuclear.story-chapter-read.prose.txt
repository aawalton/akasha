***On Clifton Hill in Niagara Falls***

At first no one watched Pimple Pete’s videos. Then, thanks to Alex’s speed and snappy one-liners caught on film, not to mention his knack for jumping off soaring heights, or Pete’s habit of zooming in on Francesca’s rear, he’d garnered a small following. He’d hit 2,000 subscribers, and 28 of them even tuned into his live stream during the Tax Guild Stone hunt.

Pete read the chat as he jiggled and huffed downhill on the empty street. His Skill, [Got a Tipoff], newly leveled, worked overtime. It made his eyes burn something fierce now that it was more powerful.

Rubbing at them, he scowled as his small fanbase made fun of him in the comments. They just didn’t believe him.

Why couldn’t his followers understand that he had to get to Niagara Falls? [Got a Tipoff] was never wrong. If anything, it scared the crap out of him with how well it worked these days.

“No, buttopia,” Pimple Pete yelled at a regular commenter through the phone. “Alex is NOT at Pearson Airport. Everyone’s got it all wrong. Even if he was spotted there, I’m telling you guys, they got away. They always get away! This is the place! This is where it—”

“Watch it!” A man in a clown costume yelled as Pete bumped him.

“S-sorry!” Pete stammered back, letting his hand holding his phone drop and coming back to the real world.

“Fucking Hunt made all the families hide inside, not making shit,” the Clown looked at him strangely as balloon animals pranced around him. “The hell are you doing out? Word is the Stone is somewhere by the—”

“Gotta go, bye!” Pete yelled back, already running again.

Typically stuffed with sticky fingered children and sore-backed parents, Clifton Hill was unusually empty. Not that it made running any easier for Pimple Pete. He went back to scanning his chat as his eyes began to glow from within.

“Hey!” The Clown yelled at Pete’s back. “Your eyes OK? Fucking weird fucker.”

Nestled east of the Niagara Falls on a curving hill, Clifton hill was famous for its family friendly activities, even in the post System World. Families packed in by the hundreds to eat sugar and food sure to bloat from stands, buy souvenirs, visit Ripley’s Believe It Or Not Museum, still somehow standing and still somehow perpetually greasy, or visit the wax museum where the replicas really did yawn when you weren’t looking. Great place for kids, with only a kilometer walk to witness the greatest waterfall by flowrate on the globe, and unofficial 8th wonder of the world, before the world was filled with countless wonders, Niagara Falls.

And now, with the Tax Guild Stone alarm sounded, there wasn’t a family or a bloated-bellied father wielding a massive camera in sight. Most hid indoors within the relative safety of an arcade or Dracula’s Haunted House until the hunt was over.

Pete’s heart soared as he saw his live viewers tick up to 30. He even attempted to lengthen his downhill stride just as Alex would.

“Chat,” his voice took on a strange, serene tone. “Trust me when I say he’ll be here. He’ll be at the Falls.”

A name of a chatter he hadn’t seen before commented. A new viewer!

“If you can’t trust me,” Pete said sagely. “Trust in [Got a Tipoff].”

“For all new viewers,” Pete preached with a new voice, sweaty and red-faced, into the camera.

“You’re watching Pimple Pete, and I am stating the truth. I receive visions from my Skill. And I tell you now what I have Seen,”

He skid to a halt in a not unbecoming way at the bottom of the hill and cast his seeing gaze at the Falls. Like a roar of water or an endless trickle, he saw the action unfold in his mind as a flash. The viewers flowed in as [Got a Tipoff] settled into every crack and space. Led by a droplet of hunch, it was the reason he even rushed to the Falls in the first place. The Skill, unstoppable now, dumped even more visions into Peter Salindiskinski, forever branded Pimple Pete by his fans.

Finally, he opened his eyes and cast ethereal light upon the empty space. He’d been right. His Skill had been right. And what he’d seen scared the crap out of him.

Gulping at what was to come, Pete looked across the empty field next to the Falls. Usually, it was full of families getting some shade before posing for a photo every family had. Standing sunburnt and squinting with the massive waterfall behind them.

“It is here,” He foretold, voice crystal clear despite how dry his mouth was. “The Toronto Tax Guild Stone is here. It’s all going down here, so spread the word! But I think you long time viewers know who’s going to get it.”

***Over Lake Ontario***

“One more time for good luck!” Dandelion yelled over the rushing wind.

Tank didn’t need to be told twice. He pressed his head over Alex’s shoulder and let out another massive [Big Chomp]. This little toy looking creature and its coating sure could take a lot.

Like a metal snapping turtle, Tank’s jaw unlatched as his head rushed forward in the blink of an eye. His teeth made of hardened steel went right to crush and inhale Acorn’s [Indominable Plastique Shell] all at once.

SNAP

THUNK

CRASH

“Yikes. You OK, Acorn?” Alex shouted as the wind flapped his cheeks. “You just smashed onto what’s left of the roof!”

Tank’s head bounded back, his [Big Chomp] not finding purchase. Rather than shattering, crunching, tasting, and swallowing all in one, the little barrier the toy had sent Tank tumbling back without being affected. With the force being so large, it sent Tank’s heavy metal body backwards.

“Ung.” Brody grunted as he caught Tank.

CAW CAW! Sounded from behind.

Tank’s tail wagged at his master catching him again. It was a fun game! He didn’t understand why his master kept grunting when his tail hit him in the ribs, though.

“He’s fine!” Dandelion laughed, righting Acorn back onto his feet, and peering over the top of The Expediter towards Alex in the driver seat.

“Twelve big ones!” She howled, wind throwing her hair in every direction. “I think that’s enough. They’re right on us too! Woohoo!”

“Why are you excited about this?!” Alex yelled back. “The whole front cage and wind protector of The Expediter is gone!”

“Cost of doing business,” Dandelion beamed. “Did you see how much punishment he took? Wow!”

“Why are you excited about that?!”

“Oh, just you see what he can do. I’ll get him ready. You focus on getting us there in one piece.” She pulled her head back and stood on the roof, and Alex could still here her giggles.

Alex just shook his head and focussed on driving the flying motorcycle.

Twelve times Acorn had hung by a pinky where the front window should have been and hid behind his shield to use [Twice Returned Damage] and [Unrelenting Block]. Twelve times Tank had used [Big Chomp], swallowing the front windshield and flinging Acorn in a tight circle to smash into the roof. Over and over to charge up the mouthless Gnome.

The Expediter was missing it’s front. There were two psychotic, masochistic gnomes on the roof, with one giggling and smacking the other on the ass. There was a pack of massive [Gluttonous Seagull]s cawing and shooting warning shots towards them as they gained. And at Tank’s mental direction to Brody of where the Tax Guild Stone was, they were headed toward the mouth of a river that led inland, and apparently, right towards the Falls.

Nerves roared in his gut.

This is where I die. Bottom of the stinky lake will be my grave!

A warm hand clapped him on the shoulder, causing Alex to turn his head around.

“We…got it…buddy. Together. Jobs…not done. We need… our tip.” Brody grumbled out, grinning viciously at him.

The wind didn’t whip his hair around. Alex peered over Brody’s shoulder and paled at the incoming birds. Brody’s hand tightened.

SLURP

Tank’s heavy, wet tongue lapped at Alex’s face, causing him to laugh.

“Alex…,” Brody said as Dandelion was heard laughing above.

“We…figure it out. Tank says Falls…And you…get us to Falls. That’s where…Stone is. The rest…we wing it.”

SLURP

It was hard to feel so down when a massive, loving pitbull was licking your face. Alex nodded and finally smiled back at Brody.

“Alright, fuck it. We got it. We’re just going to get to the Falls and get the Stone and that’s actually my tip and holy fucking shit…You know what? We fucking got—”

“They’re gaining on us!” Dandelion called from the roof.  “The big bloody bastards are gaining on us! I’m attaching some rope to Acorn! Alex! Get us to somewhere with space!”

Tank licked Alex’s face one more time.

“Falls? That’s where Tank says it is?” He asked.

“Yup. Or near.” Brody grinned back, placing a hand on his happy dog.

“Then it’s time to see how fast we can our damn tip.” Alex gave back his own grin, and activated [Alternative Goggler], hopeful that the glasses and speed buff from wearing them would extend to their ride.

“Cool glasses, right?” Alex waggled his eyebrows at Brody and turned, revving the throttle.

CAW CAW!

“Faster, Alex!” Dandelion yelled. “We’re almost ready.”

“Not…that cool…” Brody grumbled, lying to himself. The glasses were cool. By sheer luck, the ones that popped onto Alex’s face were a pair of reflective aviators with gold frames.

“Wooohooo!” Alex yelled as The Expediter shot off towards the mouth of the river. Towards the Falls.

The 4.51% buff to speed did apply to the ride. The cawing of the murderous birds got just a bit quieter as Alex put on the speed and let his instincts take over.

They flew at top speed into the river. Quaint cottages with boathouses dotted the shores, churning in the fast-moving water.

CAW CAW!

“Alllllllmost!” Dandelion roared. “ALMOST ACORN. HOLD!”

Alex pulled off the speed as they approached a corner, dipping within inches of the waves, only to rev as hard as he could around the cliff face. Glittery fire roared from the exhaust as he shot off perfectly out of the bend.

He was a natural at driving The Expediter. With his eyes protected behind aviators pulled from somewhere else, he could focus onto on the drive.

In between boulders jutting from the churn they flung with the pack of murder birds chasing. Over a bridge he wove and took a tight right. As they peeled out, the gushing sounds of the Falls came.

“It’s close!” Alex yelled, eyes still forward. “The Falls are just ahead! I can hear them!”

“HOLD, ACORN! Not until we’re there!” Dandelion yelped, squeezing the quiet gnome’s rump in preparation.

CAW CAW!

Alex yanked on the handlebars, and the bike flew upwards two hundred feet as they took the last bend. Finally, the gang was a straight shot toward Niagara Falls.

Tumbling 40 million gallons of water a minute, Niagara Falls was a wall of gushing white. It stretched two thirds of a kilometer wide and sent water violently down two hundred feet to crash into gnarled boulders below. The sound was overwhelming, all crashing waves, and thick mist rained in the pitted valley river it had carved over the years.

The Heroic Hawk Core, unsettled within him, bubbled and churned in Alex’s chest. According to the Title, he needed to Aid the Other in his want, help to secure the power of the one he was companion, become family with the Lich, and deliver that which filled the Taking of the Boss. Nothing to do with the Tax Guild Stone.

Yet it still bubbled and sent a trickle of it’s true power.

He whooped and flew them at top speed right towards the crest of the Falls without a single fear in his heart.

What a way to see the Falls for the first time!

“Acorn!” Dandelion’s laughing voice could still be heard over the water. “Remember who we are. Remember who you are!”

CAW!

Alex only had eyes for the waterfall. It was amazing, to race towards it, to aim right where the water began to fall. So much so that he didn’t notice the hordes of people fighting in the field next to it.

Dandelion tightened the plant rope around Acorn’s waist one final time as he positioned himself on the back lip of the roof. Glancing over her shoulder, she saw what Alex aimed for, realized she only had time for a quick send off, and made sure to fill her voice with laughter. She knew it was Acorn loved most, and she was happy to give it to him.

“From garden soil we come, Acorn! Oft to protect and oft forgotten over years to mildew and stain and be buried in moss!”

Then she leaned forward, over the open air and met his cold, serious eyes with her own.

“But that’s what gave us our way! So do as you should, as all Garden Gnomes can, and remind these beasts. Remind them of where we all return. To dirt! To mud! To clay! Where only our porcelain hearts may remain!”

The bike was a dozen feet from the Falls, and Alex pulled them upwards.

Acorn began to nod but stopped as Dandelion pecked a kiss onto his forehead.

His eyes softened as she laughed and gave him one final ass smack.

“Go, Acorn Turtlecorn, my love,” She giggled in his ear. “I’ll be watching and waiting for you.”

Alex felt The Expediter get slightly lighter as he curled away from the crest of the falls. All he heard was the gushing water and Dandelion’s laughter as he took them soaring into the sky.

***Not two hundred feet away from the Falls***

“—And a pack of [Gluttonous Seagull]s, as foretold! Rushing into the Falls!” Pimple Pete yelled frantically as he swung the camera back towards Alex on his flying motorcycle. “I told you! I TOLD ALL OF YOU! Now comes the next bit!”

His footage caught the horde of people on the field next to the Falls fighting like mad in his panning, but Pete was trying to zoom in on Alex. Now that he’d proven himself, and his Skill, he just needed to catch the finale.

“Shit,” Pete said, searching the sky with his camera for some Relic Alex must have dropped in the sky. “There’s supposed to be an explosion next. Where the hell is the bo—”

***Acorn Turtleshell***

Acorn Turtleshell fell toward the flock. They were massive. Far larger than he, and with many in their number. His shield was planted in front of him, he waited, and he prepped his Skill.

[Twice Returned Damage] burned. Never had he taken and absorbed so much damage. He’d been saving it for weeks and taken on hundreds of strikes prior to today. The ghastly fire bubbled within, Tank’s bites felt potent, and he felt ready to burst apart for how much energy he had within.

Truth be told, he was scared.

He wasn’t sure if he would survive the release. But he’d never let Dandelion know that. He’d do anything for her. Silently and happily.

Anything.

“Now, Acorn! BRING THEM BACK TO THE MUD!” Her voice called. He loved that he could always hear her.

If Acorn could smile, he would.

With a simple flick of his essence, and right before he passed the birds, he activated [Twice Returned Damage] and returned weeks of damage, explosive hellfire, and a dozen bites from an absurdly high-level pitbull. All multiplied by two at once.
