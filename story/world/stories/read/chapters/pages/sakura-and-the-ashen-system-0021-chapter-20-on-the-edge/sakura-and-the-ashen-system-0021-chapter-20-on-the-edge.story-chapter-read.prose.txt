Sakura caught only movement between the trees, brief red flashes in the darkness, and the wet gleam of layered jaws. Then the obsidian creatures stepped into the open, and a chill ran straight down her spine. There were three of them. Three creatures, all level 11.

They looked like centipedes, only monstrously distorted. Their long segmented bodies seemed to be made from coal and living flesh. Each segment was armored with heavy obsidian plates edged in jagged ridges. They had far too many legs, and they moved with fast, coordinated precision. But that was not the worst part.

The heads. One creature had two, another had three, and the largest had a whole crown of elongated muzzles, each set one after another on short, flexible neck-like growths. Every head moved separately, jerking in its own rhythm, clacking its teeth and hissing. Dark vapor leaked from their open mouths.

Gilbert was beside Sakura at once. In a single motion, he drew the sword with the emerald hilt from his Inventory. The blade flashed in the last of the dim light.

“There’s no choice,” he said shortly. “We won’t be able to run. We’ll have to fight.”

Sakura was surprised that these creatures were acting in coordination, like a family, without trying to kill each other. She had gotten used to predators in Veyr reacting aggressively to one another. But apparently, that rule had exceptions too.

Pampli had already stepped forward. A low, rumbling sound rolled out of his chest. He did not charge right away. On the contrary, he froze, staring at the creatures as if answering them with his own growl. One of the multi-headed beasts suddenly crouched low. Several of its heads jerked forward and hissed, as if arguing. Pampli growled louder.

Then the creature lunged.

Sakura did not even have time to understand which one it was. The black body launched forward so fast it blurred into a streak. Pampli slammed into it head-on. They collided with a crash like two rockslides meeting. Obsidian plates screeched against fangs, and claws tore up the earth.

“Pampli!” Sakura shouted.

She started toward him, but in the same instant another obsidian creature rushed her from the side. The blow hit her shoulder and ribs at the same time. It literally knocked Sakura off her feet. The world spun. The ground slammed into her back so hard it drove the air out of her lungs.

Somewhere nearby, Gilbert was already fighting the third centipede-like creature. The sword with the emerald hilt left quick green flashes in the dark. He struck cleanly, without wasted motion, slipping past jaws and long legs, but the beast was too fast. And Gilbert had no magic at all. The heads of the obsidian creature whipped around him, trying to come at him from several angles at once.

Sakura rolled over, dragging air into her lungs with difficulty, and then movement flashed again behind the trees.

A fourth creature came out of the forest. It was the largest of all. Four heads, thick front segments. It moved more slowly than the others, but that only made it look worse.

“You’ve got to be kidding me,” Sakura rasped.

The new creature did not wait. One of its heads snapped sharply toward Sakura, having caught her scent, and the whole massive body immediately turned.

For a moment, Sakura had time to think that maybe she and Gilbert had been unlucky enough to end up somewhere near a whole nest of these creatures in the forest and disturb them, and now they were paying for it. But there was no time to think about that.

Sakura pushed herself up on one elbow, clenching her teeth. Pain pulsed dully in her side and shoulder. She snatched up the blade Gilbert had given her, as if it were her only weapon. As if she did not have that terrible magic of the Obsidian Hierophant.

The creature came at her again. Faster than something that big had any right to move.

Sakura slashed wildly. The blade scraped across one obsidian segment with an ugly shriek and left almost no mark.

“Don’t forget what kind of power you have!” Gilbert shouted, beating back the attack of two heads at once. “Now is exactly the time to remember it! There’s no other way!”

Sakura jumped aside, barely avoiding the next lunge. She forced herself to focus on the creature. It was hard to make herself use magic that terrified her so much... hard to accept it inside herself and try to release it again. Sakura was afraid of herself with that magic. And if there had been any way not to use it, she would not have used it. But now there was no choice.

She instinctively reached for the skill [DEVOURER], directing her intent at the creature just as she had once done back in Crowlis Castle. Only now, consciously.

But she was too late.

The creature was faster. Its long body arched sharply, one head striking first to distract her, and then the next lunge crashed into Sakura with its full weight. It threw her aside like a rag doll. The world flipped again. Something hard drove into her ribs. This time the pain was brighter, sharper, more dangerous.

Sakura gasped, not immediately understanding that she was lying on her side, fingers clawing into the wet ground.

Her vision went dark for a second. It was hard to breathe. Air came in ragged pulls, as if everything in her chest had tightened and refused to open again. Something in her side throbbed nastily. Sakura did not even try to act heroic. She instantly reached into her Inventory, grabbed a vial of healing potion, and yanked it out.

She poured the contents into herself in several greedy swallows. The potion tasted awful, but after only a few seconds the pain weakened a lot. And that happened just in time.

Because the creature was already charging at her again.

There was no time to recover.

Sakura forced herself to her feet on sheer stubbornness, threw one hand out, and this time did not let fear throw her off. Her gaze locked onto the crimson eyes of the nearest head.

“Come on...”

[DEVOURER]

This time, the creature froze.

Everything happened instantly and at the same time felt agonizingly slow. Its movement stopped half a step short. The crimson glow in its eyes flickered. A ripple passed over the black plates, as if the power inside it were being yanked outward all at once. The air around Sakura vibrated. She felt that terrible sucking pull again, just like last time.

The obsidian creature convulsed all over. One head snapped its jaws in a spasm, the second hissed, and the third jerked sharply backward.

The creature turned into obsidian ash, and that ash, together with its power, rushed into Sakura.

The pain hit at once. Sharp, like a blow directly to her nerves. As if molten metal had been poured into her and was now spreading through her veins. Sakura’s knees nearly gave out. She barely stayed upright. But at the same time, she felt how sweetly that power glowed inside her. How the strength called to itself, how it soothed her.

A system window flashed before her eyes.

LEVEL UP.CURRENT LEVEL: 3.

A second panel opened beneath it almost immediately.

PHYSICAL ATTRIBUTES UPDATED.Strength: 5Endurance: 7Agility: 7Constitution: 3Perception: 7

Sakura barely registered the numbers. Strength, endurance, agility, perception. Yes, good, wonderful. She was marginally less killable now. Later she could think about that. Later she could think about what it meant.

Right now Gilbert was still fighting. His sword was moving fast, but the creature kept pressing him back. Gilbert would not be able to keep defending forever without landing a real attack.

Pampli was fighting two at once. He locked his fangs into obsidian segments, tore, threw them back, but even for him it was difficult. His growl had turned harsher. Real strain was in it now.

Sakura made her decision instantly. Help Gilbert first. She turned toward the creature pressing him and tried to call [DEVOURER] again.

Nothing. The skill did not answer.

Sakura froze for a fraction of a second, unable to believe it. She tried again.

[DEVOURER]

No response. A new system warning flashed sharply before her eyes:

[DEVOURER] UNAVAILABLE.REPEATED USE IS IMPOSSIBLE IN THE IMMEDIATE FUTURE.

“Ah, no! Not that!”

Her shock was brief. Because almost at once, one of the creatures that had broken away from Pampli wheeled around and lunged at her.

Sakura darted aside. Clawed legs slammed into the ground where she had been standing a second earlier.

Fine. If not [DEVOURER], then something else.

[SCARLET ASH TAMER]

She reached for the skill almost desperately. For a moment, it even seemed to her that the familiar scarlet force had stirred inside.

But the system hit her again with a dry line.

[SCARLET ASH TAMER] RESTRICTED.LIMIT REACHED.

Sakura felt everything inside her go cold. This was bad. Very bad.

The creature lunged at her again. Sakura ducked, rolled, and felt a rush of air slide over her back as a head swept past.

But then one thought flared bright and clear.

Scrolls.

Gilbert had literally just taught her. External magic. Sakura bolted toward the case lying near the tree.

Pampli slammed into the creature from the side, not letting it reach Sakura that instant. The impact was so strong that both black bodies went crashing across the ground. Sakura stopped.

“Pampli!”

But he did not hear her anymore. Pampli clamped down on its neck, if that could even be called a neck, and tore so hard that even Sakura heard the crack of obsidian plates. The creature thrashed, tried to strike him with its side segments, but Pampli only drove his fangs in deeper.

Then he killed it. The black body collapsed beneath him.

And in the next second, Pampli began to change. He became larger, more terrifying. His muscles seemed to swell with new strength. His paws grew heavier. Obsidian-black energy, nearly pure black, rippled over his body. Pampli threw back his head and roared. The sight was truly terrifying, even for Sakura.

She could not keep watching, because she had to get to the scrolls, grab them. And... hope that one of them held magic strong enough to help against the obsidian creatures.
