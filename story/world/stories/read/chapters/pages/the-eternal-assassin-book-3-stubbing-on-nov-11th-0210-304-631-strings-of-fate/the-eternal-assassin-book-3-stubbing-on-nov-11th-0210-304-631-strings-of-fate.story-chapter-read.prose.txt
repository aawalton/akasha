As worried as he was for Moxy and Kroult, Asher wasn’t about to leave Rulfar to his fate and come back for him. Seeing as the bloodhound was calmly sitting rather than flying about the roof of the cavern, it was a far simpler task for him to hop around and use his void dagger to cut away all the hairs desperately trying to find an entry point into his body, even if he was down a leg at the moment. The second he wasn’t at risk of being taken over, Rulfar dropped his blended skill, his body shaking slightly from the strain of maintaining his skill for so long as he grabbed Asher’s hand.

“Good luck,” Rulfar said before vanishing into his rift.

“Thanks,” Asher muttered. Shoving his dagger back into its sheath, he took a tired breath as he yanked a random spear from his rift and used it like a crutch. His Personal Rift was filled with so many random odds and ends at this point, it was honestly starting to feel a bit cluttered, despite how large the skill had become.

You know, I bet I could reattach my leg easily enough… Razor Wing makes one hell of a clean cut.

Liking the sound of having both his legs attached for his upcoming battle against the lich, Asher went to hobble over to his severed limb, only to frown as he realized it was nowhere to be found. Based on the splatter of blood, it was easy enough to spot where it should have been.

“Damn it, Moxy,” Asher grumbled, realizing she had to have grabbed it before she ran. Despite the fact that he knew she wasn’t in control of her own actions, he couldn’t help but feel like his annoyance was fair.

Shaking his head, Asher sat on the ground and threw up a Spatial Lock around himself just to be safe. The reaching hairs from the ceiling had long since given up on going after him, but the last thing he needed was for them to try again while he was distracted. That done, he threw his focus over to Kroult, watching through his Eternal Mark to see what the lich was up to. Moxy had refused to let him replace his mark on her, but at least Kroult was more reasonable.

Similar to the cavern with the farm they’d been fighting in for the past couple of minutes, wherever Kroult was, it appeared to be an area of the cave far more developed than the seemingly endless miles of tunnels they’d spent the last day searching through. The lich appeared to be standing in an actual jail cell of all things, albeit one with bars made from what were clearly woven strands of human hair. Asher wasn’t sure why The Lost Lady had bothered placing him in a cell when he was still completely under her control, but he highly doubted she’d offer up an answer if he asked.

Unable to see anything more than the cell and Kroult’s stationary form, Asher sighed. He didn’t really have any practice trying to run around and fight whilst missing a leg, and this was one hell of a trial by fire. That said, he also couldn’t afford to wait the day or two it would take him to regrow the missing limb, which meant he was going to have to risk it anyway. If he could just get a single lucky slash off on The Lost Lady with his void dagger…

Hold on. The void dagger ate my Distortion skill earlier. Does that mean it would do the same to Sever the Thread?

Asher frowned as he realized taking the lich down might be tougher than he’d first thought. Originally, he’d planned to simply use his void dagger to cleave straight through whatever defenses she had in place, relying on the power of the void to finish her off and his own Sever the Thread to ensure she stayed that way. However, if that same all-encompassing hunger of the void prevented Sever the Thread from working…

He wouldn’t actually kill the lich for good. She would simply manifest once more wherever the hell Zygrul was hiding out within the void. While she would almost certainly be his problem at that point, Asher still had no idea what exactly Zygrul was trying to do. The last thing he wanted was to give him a weakened lich to potentially take over and force to do his own bidding, which meant he couldn’t risk killing her with the void dagger. There was a chance the void itself was powerful enough to strike at her soul directly like his skill did, but Asher had a hunch that wasn’t the case. If it were, there wouldn’t have been any reason for Zygrul to go through all this effort to allow him to kill the other members of the Council. The lich clearly had a deadly mastery of its Void element, based on how easily it had erased Asher’s arm and ripped a chunk out of his Spatial Lock. If truly killing off the rest of the Council was that easy for it, it probably would have done so by now.

At the very least Asher could still use the void dagger to cut through The Lost Lady’s defenses. He just needed to be careful that the killing blow was made with any one of his other daggers.

Plan in place, Asher pushed himself up with the help of the spear. Even if he didn’t know where Moxy had gone off to, he knew where to start looking. Preparing himself for anything, he Recalled over to Kroult, popping into existence directly beside the trapped lich.

The cell was small enough that there was barely space for him to stand, and Asher shivered as he felt the thousands of strands of hair stretching out of Kroult’s body as they brushed up against him. Not a fan of that sensation in the slightest, Asher ripped out his void dagger and cut the friendly lich free with a few quick slashes. The moment the last strand fell away, Kroult shuddered, his flaming eyes flaring brightly as he sagged against the cell wall.

“That… was remarkably unpleasant. I do not even feel pain or discomfort in this body, but the experience reminded me of being trapped once more. Thank you for saving me.”

“You’re part of the team now, we’re not just going to let you get kidnapped and dragged away,” Asher said, looking through the cell bars and noting that Kroult had been moved to some sort of small prison. There were dozens of identical cells lining the small cavern, some of them complete with long-dead bodies by the looks of things. “Did you see Moxy?”

“Moxy has been taken as well? No, I have not seen any of the others. The Lost Lady kept my head angled up the entire time. She even froze my vocal cords.”

“She was probably able to be a lot more invasive seeing as you’re already an undead,” Asher muttered, shuddering at the thought of what that had to have felt like. It was a good thing Kroult didn’t feel pain or discomfort, because he had a feeling if he did, this rescue would have been filled with a lot more screaming. “I know she got the drop on you, but do you have some way to protect people from her hairs? I could really use the teams’ help for this final showdown.”

“Unfortunately, I do not. I do in fact have a skill taken from one with a Hair element, but it is designed to strengthen and reinvigorate hair, which is the opposite of what we need. I used to use it on the daughters and wives of village chiefs before large events. It was a very popular one.”

“Good to know you can function as a hair stylist in a pinch,” Asher drawled, realizing he was on his own for this one. There was a chance Moxy might be able to help him out once he’d freed her, and he knew without a shadow of a doubt she’d be angry enough she might refuse to hide out in his rift anyway after what The Lost Lady put her through. “Here, take my hand and head into the rift. Though if you have anything that will help improve my balance, I’ll happily take that first. Fighting on one leg isn’t going to be easy.”

Thankfully, Kroult did have a spell to improve balance, and he cast it on him before vanishing into his Personal Rift. Now able to hop around on one leg more easily than before, Asher slipped into the astral and hopped through the cell of hair, doing a quick sweep of the prison to ensure nobody else was in need of rescue.

Grant hadn’t been among the eight men they’d taken down back by the farm, which meant at least one minion was still unaccounted for. At the very least he was down an arm, which made him a bit less dangerous.

Find Moxy, kill The Lost Lady, rescue Grant and the other two men who still have auras, Asher thought to himself as he hopped over to the cave exit. He felt a little bad about leaving those two minions lying on the farm without being able to do much more than squirm around, but he was a little busy.

Hopefully they’d forgive him once he freed them from their captor.

The entrance to the prison didn’t have any sort of door, so Asher did his best to peer around the cavern’s exit, quickly spotting yet another one of The Lost Lady’s minions heading his way. Based on the white hair and lack of aura, this was another one she had to have kidnapped quite some time ago, and Asher gritted his teeth at the sight. It didn’t surprise him that she was able to keep her minions ‘alive’ for so long with her Flesh element and the strange runestones she embedded into them, but it was still unnerving. According to what they’d heard, The Lost Lady only kidnapped new men every couple of years. The fact that she had so many minions still functioning and serving her meant her success rate for this terrifying procedure had to be incredibly high.

Seeing as the minion was free of any aura, Asher didn’t hesitate. Shoving an Eternal Mark into his dagger, he hurled it down the tunnel. The blade clattered against the stone wall beside the blank-faced man rather than hit him, but before it had even connected, Asher was there, separating the minion’s head from its neck with a clean slice from Crescent.

Landing nimbly on one leg, he quickly sliced the head apart as well, ensuring the man was truly put to rest. Heading in the direction the minion had come from, Asher made sure he had his assassin class equipped to keep his movement silent and his footing steady.

It didn’t take him long to find what he imagined was The Lost Lady’s main workshop, and he froze as he took it all in from around the bend in a tunnel.

The cavern was actually smaller than the farm, but far more menacing. There were tables covered in runestones in various stages of carving scattered here and there, as well as slabs with restraints made of hair where he could only imagine she performed her magical surgeries. Three more minions stood motionlessly off to the side, and Moxy stood beside them, continuing to shout some rather creative threats that Asher had never heard come from her mouth before. Based on the veins popping out of her neck and the purple color of her face, he didn’t think she’d stopped straining against the hairs from the moment they’d ensnared her.

Though far more important than that, was the woman standing in the center of the room. Naked as the day she was born, The Lost Lady moved about her workshop with clinical precision, walking with that strange, confident grace that Asher had noticed during the vision of her kidnapping Grant three years ago. Despite being an undead, The Lost Lady didn’t look it in the slightest. Her skin was pale, but it looked healthy and hale. Rather than the thin, decrepit frame of the liches he’d seen up until now, she looked completely normal, even beautiful, in a way. The only tell-tale sign that she wasn’t what she seemed were the flickering fires in her eye sockets. While she’d had regular, human eyes in the vision he’d witnessed from Eternal Knowledge, that was no longer the case. The fires dancing within her eye sockets burned white, matching the long, tendrils of hair flowing from her scalp.

Yet as strange of a sight a healthy-looking lich was, Asher only spared her a single glance, his eyes going wide as he spotted what she was currently doing. There, on one of her work tables normally reserved for bodies and clearly the focus of her current attention, was a single limb.

His own severed leg.
