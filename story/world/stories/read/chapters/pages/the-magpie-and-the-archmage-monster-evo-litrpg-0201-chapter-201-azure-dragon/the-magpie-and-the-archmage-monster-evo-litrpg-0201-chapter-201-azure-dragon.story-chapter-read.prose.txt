While Caspian was busy begging everyone to reconsider and not fight a dragon, Liora, Renir, and Cee were discussing various plans to deal with attacks the dragons could use.

Admittedly, after hearing what these creatures were capable of, Liora wasn't so sure it was such a great idea anymore. First, just like Smokey, they could breathe fire. Actual real fire, not a magical one, and it would cling to their body just like Smokey's flammable liquid.

Second, they had scales much tougher than Smokey's. Forget any magical attacks, because those wouldn't do anything. Actually, physical attacks wouldn't do much, not at the level their team had them at. Those scales were just too tough for them to deal with.

Third, they could fly, and they were good at it. Renir said that Liora likely had better mobility due to her ability to switch forms, but when it came to speed, the dragons would certainly be faster.

"Okay, so to me it sounds like they are quite unbeatable. Why are we attempting to fight them?" Cee asked nervously.

"Exactly! Listen to Cee! This is suicide!" Caspian shouted so loudly, Smokey cocked his head at him.

"If we were a normal party of adventurers, then yes, I agree. It would be impossible. Humans especially are ill-equipped to deal with such threats. But our group is different," Renir said, crossing his crystal arms over his chest and humming deeply. "We have the one advantage we need the most, and great supplementary powers to boot."

"What advantage?" Caspian asked.

"Listen closely," Renir said, and they all leaned closer.

Liora couldn't believe her ears. Could it really be so easy? There was no reason for Renir to lie about something like this. If everything went as Renir said, then they would not only gain plenty of experience, but also a bunch of treasure.

She was excited.

Once they had discussed plans A, B, C, and a few other unlucky contingencies, Liora moved out to scout the mountain. This was the first phase of their plan. Scouting.

Shrouded with her Nightveil and muffled by Featherlight Flight, Liora flew around the mountain, searching for the spots where her Treasure Sense went wild. There were many such spots, actually, and the fact that the mountain itself was a treasure was throwing her off a little bit.

But she did find them. The dragon caves. Their openings were actually quite obvious. Colorful crystals were littered at the entrance as if to say "I'm a rich dragon and I live here". For most creatures, it would likely be a warning beacon.

Liora had different plans. She chose the smaller entrance, which didn't have quite as many crystals. A sign that this was a smaller dragon. It didn't mean that it was weak. It was still a dragon, but it should be easier for them to deal with this particular one than a large, experienced dragon.

With the target chosen, Liora brought Cee and left the spider by the entrance of the cave. Cee then snuck inside. Using her Shadow Walk, she could effectively disappear, although, according to Renir, the dragon inside shouldn't pay much attention to anything entering its cave unless it touched its treasure.

Well, a party of humans would obviously be turned into a roast barbecue, but the dragon shouldn't mind creatures that it would consider "vermin" unless they annoyed the dragon on purpose.

Liora would be lying if she said that she wasn't worried for Cee. She fluttered by the entrance, ready to dive in and pull the spider out at a moment's notice if necessary.

But half an hour later, Cee came out, looking a bit shaky but otherwise fine.

"How did it go? What's its level?" Liora asked once Cee jumped on her back.

"Level 85 Azure Dragon. It was thankfully sleeping and didn't notice me weaving my webs or traps at all, but… I dunno. It was huge! Like at least ten times as big as Smokey. It could squish us all with a single swipe of its paw and then go back to sleep, not even noticing what had happened. Maybe we should keep grinding on those bats?" Cee suggested.

"And how long will that take? We might as well leave and forget this problem ever existed!" Liora huffed.

"You know this isn't something we're obligated to deal with…" Cee muttered. "Since when did we become some kind of heroes trying to save the world?"

"I don't know about heroes, but I can't stand by when those Night Order assholes get to do whatever they want. Who else is supposed to stop them? It was Renir's job, but he can't exactly do it alone, can he?"

Cee sighed. "He is more trouble than he's worth."

"I know. But hey, I think we're all more trouble than we're worth," Liora said. "Besides, dealing with these punks might be easy when compared to that demon we accidentally unleashed."

"Oh, don't even remind me of THAT!" Cee shuddered. "Okay, okay. I really hope Renir's plan works."

"One of them is bound to work."

Liora and Cee returned to the place where Smokey, Caspian, and Renir were hiding.

"So?" Renir asked.

"All the traps are in place," Cee confirmed.

"Okay. Let's proceed with the plan!" Renir announced.

"Do I have to come?" Caspian said with a trembling voice. "I don't want to be a roast merman…"

"Of course you have to come! If something goes wrong, then you are the only chance we have. Our only healer. Our protector!" Cee urged the merman on.

He scratched the back of his head sheepishly. "I-I'm not really that important…"

"What do you mean? The healer is always the most important member of any good party! Especially you, who can heal using songs. Any other healer would be pretty much useless in these mountains!" Cee praised.

Caspian was actually blushing, looking quite embarrassed. This merman seemed to thrive on praise, and Cee was quite good at telling him exactly what he wanted to hear.

Better get to work before he could come to his senses.

"Okay, let's go then! I'm going to place Caspian and Renir in position. Once I give the signal, Cee, you should bring over Smokey."

"A-Are you sure we need Smokey? I'm afraid that the dragon will hurt him…" Cee patted the wyvern's snout.

"Don't worry! Fire isn't going to hurt Smokey, remember? We also have Plan B if things go wrong," Liora consoled.

"Are you worried more about Smokey than about me?" Caspian asked, narrowing his eyes at the spider.

"You have a good head on your shoulders, and Renir will remain by your side to guard you. Smokey… Well…" Cee muttered, looking at the dopey wyvern.

His tongue lolled out of his mouth happily. Smokey was none the wiser about things going on around him, so long as his belly was full.

"It'll be fine. If everything goes as it should, it'll be over before Smokey can be in any real danger," Liora consoled.

"When do things ever go to plan?" Cee countered.

"We have several plans. Don't worry. It'll work," Renir assured. "Let's get going before the dragon wakes."

Liora withdrew her saddle, letting Renir get in it, and then snatched Caspian while Cee flew on Smokey's back.

Of course, as soon as they reached the entrance to the dragon's cave, they quickly changed the arrangement. Caspian remained together with Renir. These two would stay out of sight, and only appear if their help was necessary.

Well, mostly if Caspian's help was necessary. Renir would act as an amplifier and as a shield if necessary since his body was rather sturdy. Actually, Liora was quite convinced that he couldn't even break.

Nonetheless, they weren't planning to test the theory today. The main attacker would be Liora, with support from Cee's traps and Smokey acting as a distraction. Yes, the wyvern would be the distraction.

He was tanky and completely fire-proof, so if worse came to worst, he should be able to endure a hit or two from the dragon. Caspian had already applied a defensive buff on him, just to be safe.

"Alright, Smokey. You're up. Remember to avoid all of my webs!" Cee said firmly.

Smokey let out a soft growl. It was… not very convincing. But when it came to avoiding Cee's webs, the wyvern had actually grown quite good at it. According to Cee, it was an actual skill he had gained—Web Maneuvering.

Not only did this make it easier for the wyvern to spot the webs, but it also helped him navigate them. Cee had deliberately left a path for Smokey to navigate, just large enough for him. The dragon shouldn't be able to avoid the web.

As Smokey crept into the cave, Liora followed after him using her Magpie Disguise. She should pretty much be invisible to the dragon.

Looking at the thing for the first time, she realized just how small she was. The thing was massive. She had thought that Cee was exaggerating when she said that it was ten times larger than Smokey. She was not, and the thing was still curled up and sleeping. Just how much bigger would it be once it got up?

Its scales were a thing of beauty, shimmering with a blue, slightly magical glow. Boy, was it pretty! A treasure in its own right.

But Liora's eyes quickly locked onto the pile the dragon was sleeping on. Armor, jewelry, precious gems, shimmering crystals, rare ore, gold coins. And glimmerstones. So many glimmerstones!

Oh dear…

Liora wanted it. She wanted it all. This was a proper treasure hoard. Admittedly, the pile looked kind of small when compared to the dragon itself, but it was definitely more than what Liora had.

She had been slacking. How could she call herself a treasure hoarder if she didn't even have half of what this dragon had? No, this had to be remedied right now.

Without even thinking, Liora flew towards the dragon, while avoiding Cee's masterfully woven traps. She couldn't help it, even if that tiny voice in the back of her mind was screaming at her to stick to the plan. Her Treasure Sense was louder.

"Cee, now!" Renir shouted.

"Smokey!" Cee shouted, and it was followed by a loud growl from Smokey.

The growl woke up the dragon, although it wasn't very quick about it. It opened its eyes lazily, looking around bleary-eyed. It seemed to register Smokey's presence, but wasn't worried about him in the slightest. Instead, the dragon yawned, taking its sweet time to get up and stretch out its limbs.

Somewhere in her mind, Liora realized that Renir had swerved away from the original plan. Had he anticipated that Liora would lose her mind at the sight of so much treasure? Yeah, he likely had.

Smokey let out another growl, this time louder, clearly trying to challenge the dragon. Although honestly, the wyvern didn't look too intimidating. Not by Liora's standards, so most likely, not by the dragon's standards either. It merely continued stretching out its limbs, as if nothing at all was wrong.

But the dragon hadn't anticipated Liora. She, driven completely mad by treasure, could only barely comprehend the plan. She flew up to the creature's head and turned into the dracoryx form.

The dragon jolted awake by Liora's sudden appearance, but it was too late. Her talons were already deeply buried in its eye socket, tearing the sensitive organ apart.
