Veronica had expected that there would be some kind of major fight once they reached the sanctuary. The ambiance of the valley just didn’t feel right with any of them. That was why it was important for both her and Gwen to complete their training.

Gwen had finished hers. And Veronica—she finished her quest as well. And it was here that she finally understood how the reward she obtained worked.

[You have completed a quest: Recite a story while casting randomly instructed spells][Reward: Tier-3 Revelations Focus Enhancement]

[Spell: Flow State][Effect: By clearing your mind and focusing on the task at hand, you reduce the delay between recognition, decision, and action. You can act at the earliest point of a detected action, allowing you to intercept and react immediately.]

The moment Veronica’s eyes spotted the cracks snaking their way over to Dusk, her body reacted. As the fissure widened, mana shot all throughout her body as she activated her Ascended Body perk.

She forced the mana around her legs first, before kicking off the ground. Her body launched through the air toward Dusk just as the crack reached his feet. As the ground parted, and right before Dusk could lose his footing—Veronica’s arms wrapped around his chest, pulling him close. Both of them sped past the fissure, landing several meters away on solid ground before he could even begin falling.

Veronica blinked a few times, looking down at herself.

The perk essentially allowed her to react and make decisions in an instant. The moment she noticed something, her body would be able to react at nearly the same exact time. With good timing, she could likely intercept an arrow as it was released from the bowstring, even before it cleared the archer’s hand.

“Are you okay?” Veronica asked, looking at Dusk.

He nodded in her arms.

Veronica looked toward the large divot that was opening in the center of the sanctuary. Something rumbled deep within, and it was growing louder. Like a monster trying to escape an abyss.

Dusk took a step back, eyes wide, trembling in her arms. “It’s still here…?” he whispered.

Veronica’s gaze snapped down toward him. “What? What’s here?”

The ground split open wider before anyone could ask more. Massive slabs of stone lifted from the platform, rising into the air one by one. The pieces didn’t fall. They hung there, suspended by a faint blue glow pulsing from the darkness below.

Then, slowly, they began to gather.

Stone crashed against stone, each piece locking into place. The sound echoed through the sanctuary like thunder. Larger sections rose from beneath the platform, pulling themselves free from the earth. First came two enormous legs, each made of carved stone and broken foundation. Then a torso formed above them, broad and towering, covered in cracks that shone faintly with blue light. More stone gathered around its shoulders and arms, thicker than several tree trunks tied together.

Both Gwen and Nasha quickly came over.

Gwen’s face went pale as she stared at the giant golem. “That’s… not good.”

The creature rose higher.

Fifteen meters… no, twenty.

At the center of its chest, stone plates shifted apart, revealing a large blue core pulsing with light. The glow spread through the cracks across its body, filling the gaps between broken stones like veins of mana. Its head formed last, a heavy, angular piece of stone with two hollow eyes burning the same pale blue.

The golem lowered its gaze toward them.

Dusk’s voice came out strained. “Why is it activating now?”

Veronica looked down at him, hands clenching on his shoulder. “You know what that thing is?”

Dusk swallowed, still staring at the giant stone creature. “It’s the guardian of the sanctuary.”

“The guardian?” Nasha repeated.

He nodded slowly. “It protects this place. It was made to stop humans who came here to cause harm to Selene.”

Gwen’s grip tightened around her staff. “But we’re not here to cause harm!”

The guardian took one step forward. The entire platform shuddered beneath its weight.

Veronica’s eyes narrowed as her mana rings began to spin faster. “It’s too late now,” she said. “It already sees us as enemies.”

The golem raised one massive arm. Stone scraped against stone as its fingers curled into a fist large enough to crush all four of them at once.

“Everyone get ready!” Veronica shouted.

The fist came down fast.

Nasha moved first, grabbing Gwen by the back of her cloak and pulling her aside. Veronica seized Dusk in her arms and leapt in the opposite direction, using Ascended Body.

The stone fist slammed into the platform. A shockwave tore across the ground, cracking the carved floor and sending broken tiles flying in every direction. Veronica landed hard near one of the pillars, one arm wrapped around Dusk as debris struck her mana barrier in rapid bursts.

Gwen yelped as Nasha pulled her behind a fractured column.

“That thing is supposed to protect the sanctuary?” Gwen shouted. “It’s destroying it!”

Veronica set Dusk down and raised her palm. Flames coalesced in front of her, shrieking with a high-pitched whine as they condensed into a small bead of red-hot fire. Before the golem could turn to fully face her, she launched the spell.

The impact rang out a moment later, smothering the air in intense heat. The blast exploded across the golem’s head, sending a cone of flames past its broad shoulders and into the air.

But Veronica could still see its legs. It was still moving.

Through the large fire, the golem swung another fist down at her and Dusk.

Veronica clicked her tongue and picked up Dusk again, dashing to the side as the ground exploded where she had just been standing.

“That thing is tougher than it looks,” Veronica muttered. “Does it have any sort of weakness?”

“T-the core in the center. It’s really strong, but if you break it, the guardian will stop. And don’t destroy its head—it will explode.” Dusk said meekly in her arms.

“Alright.” Veronica dashed as far as she could and set Dusk down. “Go hide somewhere. We’ll deal with the golem.”

Before he could respond, Veronica rushed back into the fight.

The golem turned around again, winding back another fist. It punched down, sending more debris and stone flying. Veronica burst out of the rising dust, narrowly avoiding the blow.

“Where is its weakness?” Nasha shouted, leaving Gwen behind a pillar.

“The blue core in the center!” Veronica shouted. “Don’t destroy the head!”

She stopped and raised both palms to cast another spell. However, the golem’s head turned to face her.

[Warning! High concentration of energy detected!]

Veronica stared up at its hollow eyes. They began to glow, a spark lighting up within them. The exact moment she noticed it, her body reacted immediately due to the Flow State perk.

As energy built within its eyes, Veronica canceled her spell. At the exact same time, she formed another one at near-instant speed.

A second later, the golem fired a concentrated blast of power from both eye sockets. However, the beams only traveled a few meters before being intercepted by a magic circle that appeared above them. A condensed burst of compressed mana exploded directly in front of the attack.

Her spell shattered from the force of it, scattering fragments of light as the disrupted beams split wide and carved twin trenches into the platform beside her. Heat and pressure washed across her barrier, making it flicker for a moment before stabilizing again.

[Mana: 570/900 MU]

Veronica growled in annoyance. Disrupting the attack had taken more mana than she wanted.

The golem’s head lowered, its hollow eyes still glowing faintly as if preparing to release another attack.

Veronica’s ears twitched. She turned.

Nasha stood several dozen meters away, one foot braced against the cracked stone. Her bow was drawn to its limit, the string pulled tight against her cheek. A faint layer of aura wrapped around the arrow, twisting and condensing until the shaft looked like it was trembling under the pressure.

For a brief second, the air around the arrow warped.

Then Nasha released her fingers. The arrow vanished in an instant.

A thunderous crack erupted from the golem.

It smashed directly into the golem’s blue core, sending a deep, resonating boom across the sanctuary. The golem staggered, its massive body tilting backward a moment later before crashing into the ground, shaking the sanctuary hard enough that everyone nearly fell over. Dust billowed out in a thick wave as everyone covered their eyes and began coughing.

Veronica’s eyes pierced through the dust, only to see that the blue core on the golem was still intact. And it was already getting back up.

Nasha landed next to her a moment later, breathing harder than usual.

“That bow I got from the viscount’s vault,” Nasha said quickly. “It lets me imbue my arrows with condensed aura, but it tires me out quickly.”

Veronica glanced at the fallen golem, then back to her. “That was one of them?”

Nasha nodded, her expression tightening. “And it barely scratched the core. I can probably only use it two more times before I collapse. I’m sorry I can’t do much more than this.”

The golem’s arm slammed into the ground, dragging its enormous body upright.

Veronica narrowed her eyes. “Don’t worry about it. We just have to play within our limits.”

“Do you have something stronger?”

“Who do you think I am?” Veronica asked, tilting her head down. “But I’ll need time.”

Veronica’s mana rings began to spin faster, drawing more power from her core. “Distract it. Keep its attention away from me. Don’t let it focus its eye beams. If it tries to fire, make it turn. And try to avoid going near Gwen or Dusk.”

Nasha nodded.

With both of them in agreement, they split up. Veronica to the left. Nasha to the right.

By now, the golem was fully standing once again. Its eyes focused on Nasha, who was running straight toward it. Nasha approached from the front just as the golem raised its foot, preparing to stomp down on her. But with a sudden burst of energy, her speed increased, blinking forward just in time to slip beneath and past it. The golem’s leg smashed into the ground, causing a miniature earthquake that shook the sanctuary.

“Come here, big guy! Let’s have some fun!” Nasha taunted from behind him.

Off to the side, Veronica stood still near a pillar, condensing her mana and forming a spell circle in front of her.

[Mana: 291/900 MU]

“Spirals of light, from the sparks that grow and burn, come the echoes of hell,” she whispered.

The golem launched another punch into the sanctuary, missing Nasha as she dodged away from the blow. Light condensed once more in its head as it turned its gaze toward her.

Nasha noticed and immediately pushed her aura through her body, fleeing as quickly as possible. Shortly after, she heard the sharp, high-pitched hum reach her. The ground exploded behind her as she ran, the searing destruction of stone being obliterated at her heels. She leapt over the gap in the center of the sanctuary, sliding to a stop on the other side.

However, her expression immediately turned grim. The golem wasn’t paying attention to her anymore.

“Oh, fuck,” Gwen said, hiding behind a pillar. The golem was now staring at her. And worst still—its eyes were charging up for another blast.

“Gwen!” Nasha shouted, trying to run over to her as quickly as possible.

Gwen backed up a few steps before turning into a full sprint. As she ran, she poured magic into her staff, casting a spell on herself. “Oh, please let this be strong enough!”

The golem fired another concentrated beam from its eyes, aiming straight toward Gwen. And unlike Nasha, she was too slow to fully avoid it.

Gwen turned just as she noticed a bright flash of light coming toward her. She yelped, raising an arm over her head and closing her eyes as the beam descended.

“No, you don’t!” Nasha yelled.

She stopped mid-sprint, drawing back another aura-enhanced arrow. Just as the beam came, she released it. However, she wasn’t quick enough to completely stop the attack.

Lorence’s cloak activated, forming a mana barrier over Gwen just as the beam impacted. The light split, washing over the entire barrier. The energy that struck the ground exploded, destroying the stone beneath her and sending her flying back several meters.

Her scream turned into a grunt as her body rolled across jagged stone.

After a couple of moments, she slowly pushed herself up, getting back onto her knees. “Augh…” she groaned.

She looked down at her arms. There were cuts and bruises all over her body from the fall, but they were already beginning to heal due to her magic. As she looked around, she saw the beam’s devastation sheared away from her, curving into the ground.

Nasha’s arrow smashed directly into the golem’s core, sending it tumbling down once more. If she hadn’t done that, its beam might have stayed focused on her.

“Get down the stairs!” Nasha yelled.

Gwen looked over at her.

Nasha was also on her knees, breathing heavily. Sweat dripped down her face as she forced herself to stand, despite the wobble in her legs. She was in no condition to keep fighting.

“I—I’ll heal you!” Gwen shouted back, raising her staff.

Before the golem could stand again, Gwen began casting Revitalize, a stamina-restoring spell. It slowly began to take effect, but it wouldn’t be fast enough.

The ground rumbled as the golem once again pushed its body upright. It stood on its two bulky legs, its gaze staring down at them.

“Veronica! Are you—” Gwen’s voice cut off as a sudden flash appeared to her left.

Both Nasha and Gwen peered over. So did the golem.

“Through cloud, through ash…” Veronica voiced. “Comes… the first rays of Ra!”

Veronica’s chant rang across the entire sanctuary. As Nasha and Gwen looked over, all they saw was pure light. The valley was already dark, with most of the sunlight blocked by the two mountains. And yet Veronica’s spell made it shine as if a sunbeam had broken through the leaves of a tree overhead.

A bright ray shot through the sanctuary, smashing directly into the golem’s blue core. The light refracted but continued forward, splitting apart and finding its way through every crevice of stone clinging around the orb.

The golem tried to raise its arms and block the attack, but its hands couldn’t even reach the beam. Despite being unable to defend it—even after a few seconds, the core showed no signs of damage. It was too durable.

However, perhaps they didn’t need to destroy the core directly.

Gwen. Nasha. Dusk.

Argh… Sage… I need you to…
