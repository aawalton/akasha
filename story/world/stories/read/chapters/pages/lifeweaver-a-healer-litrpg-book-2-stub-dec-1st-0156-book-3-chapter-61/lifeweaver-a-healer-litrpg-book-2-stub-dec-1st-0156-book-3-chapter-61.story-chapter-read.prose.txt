After some more coaxing, footsteps sounded from what Luke assumed was the kitchen. A woman with unruly brown curls and thick-rimmed glasses, wearing a plaid skirt and checkered blouse, peeked into the living room. When she saw Luke, she held up a cup of coffee, as if it were a peace offering.

Ray appeared behind her, holding the biggest rifle Luke had ever seen. "Got any for me?"

She jumped with a shriek but managed to keep the coffee from spilling. Shaking a little, she handed one cup to Ray, then walked into the living room, glancing at the three unconscious guys and the one who looked like he might pass out from sheer fright.

"Thanks," Luke said, accepting a cup. "How did you make coffee without electricity?"

"Gas stove."

Luke nodded, smelling the dark brew. It was strong, just the way he liked it. Tasting it, he nodded in appreciation before addressing her again. "So why did you try to kill me?"

His normal tone of conversation took her off guard, and her eyes jumped from Luke to her compatriots, then toward the door, as if considering just running for it.

"Well?" Luke asked.

Sandra cleared her throat and put on what looked like a practiced smile. "Well. To be fair, I didn't do anything."

"That argument is likely to work," Ray scoffed from over in the corner, where he was examining the big rifle.

Both Lee and the bouncer were beginning to stir, so Luke took some precautions. Threads of Mana didn't hold much physical power, but with enough of them wrapped around arms and legs, they would serve well enough in keeping the bastards subdued, especially when Luke kept them drained, with mana hovering around ten percent. From experience, he knew it was difficult to put up much of a fight in that state.

Ministrations complete, he turned back to Sandra. "You're Luminous Lark."

"Sure."

He gave her a long look, but when she didn't give even the beginning of an explanation, he let out an exasperated sigh. "What the fuck? You tried to kill me, and you were fucking with the plaque to my guild. Why?"

Sandra just looked at him, assuming a mask of calm. It was all bullshit, of course. Through Weaver's Perception, he could feel the racing of her heart and even sense the synapses in her brain firing so fast it was obvious her mind was going about as fast as a race car. The twitch at the corner of her eye and the way her gaze kept jumping to the guys on the couch were more clues to her state. She was not calm.

"I can hurt her a little, if you want," Ray said from the corner of the room. His voice was subdued and flat, lacking emotion. Unlike Sandra, his visible calm was a reflection of stillness within. If Luke had said yes, his friend wouldn’t have hesitated.

"No," Luke decided. "I've got a better idea."

Two more threads of mana rose from the palm of his left hand, like wispy threads of light blue, heading for Sandra. She flinched back when one entered her mana channels, burrowing in through the elbow crease, like an IV, but she didn't dare move away. Through that thread, Luke took from her, draining mana until he knew she was on the brink of collapse.

Only then did he move the second thread to hover in front of her face. Affixing Needle of Life to its end, he waved it back and forth, the tip of his most innate weapon glinting in the sun peeking in through the large windows.

"Explain," Luke said, his voice just as cold as Ray's had been. "Or I'll start by taking one of your eyes."

"You're supposed to be a healer and a doctor, aren't you?" she asked, looking angered rather than scared. "You wouldn't."

"Healer, yes. Doctor no. Oaths aren't my thing," Luke said, steeling himself as he plunged the needle straight through her eye, puncturing it. Before she could even react, he pulled back and healed her, restoring the eye.

Even so, she screamed, bringing a hand up to her face, tears streaming down her face. There was nothing about this that he enjoyed, but these people had brought it on themselves with their actions. Time was running out, and this joke of a guild was a thorn in his side that needed removing before bigger threats arrived.

Luke stopped cold, ignoring her wailing. That was it. Wasn't it?

"The Intruders," he said. "The Singers. You're working for them."

Sandra froze. It was a reflexive thing, and only for the briefest of moments, but there it was, all the confirmation he needed.

"You are traitors to every Integrated on Earth."

"Holy shit," Ray breathed.

At that moment, the one unbound guy, Sandra's supposed boyfriend, threw himself off the sofa and lunged toward Luke with a roar. It was a pitiful sound, even coming from someone with a broken nose, but there was no compassion or pity left in Luke, who sliced through a forming spellweave and elbowed the guy in the face. He crumpled to the floor, prompting new cries from Sandra, who also began forming spellweaves.

Amazed at the number of them, eight in total, Luke still tore them apart with ease. A strong enough mage could push back his effort to destroy their spells, but Sandra wasn't strong. At least not in that way. Casting those spells ate what little mana she still had available to her, and she fell unconscious where she stood.

Luke didn't catch her when she tumbled to the floor to end up in a heap on top of Lee and her boyfriend.

The room was silent for a while, then Ray sauntered over and hunched down to rest his arms over his knees, poking them with a finger before looking up. "Do we kill them?"

Drained after the assault and jumping through the weave twice, Luke seated himself on the couch's armrest.

"No," he said. "I don't think so."

"Why not? They tried to kill you. It's only fair."

"I'd like to know why they're doing this."

Ray laughed. "Why does it matter? If they're working for the Singers, that's all we need to know. The why of it all is as useless as these fuckers. It's money or power, always is. Or they were threatened. That's an option too, I guess."

"I guess," Luke conceded, getting to his feet. "Let's go."

"That's still a no on the killing?"

Not wanting to take any chances on the people in the room hearing him, Luke sent his reply as a message.

Luke: "I've healed all of them a little, so I can sense where they are. Better to let them go and see what they do. If they lead us to their leader, we can cut the snake off at the head."

Ray chuckled as he followed. "Cut the snake off at the head. You're so bad at trying to sound cool."

"Shut up," Luke grumbled, heading out of the apartment. The four Larks would wake soon enough, so he sent a message to Milla asking her to make sure they left without causing any more trouble, then headed out of the apartment. At least the sniper was dealt with. No more bullets to the head. At least not from her. Checking his interface, Luke saw he'd received messages from Hannah and Lemira during his brief stay in the apartment. Checking on Hannah first, he descended the stairs.

Hannah: "Meeting done. Took them a while to get back into it after you covered the room in your blood."

Luke: "That's how I roll. What did you decide?"

Hannah: "I'll let Milla tell you the specifics, but honestly, not much. A lot of bickering and posturing. We'll all get far away from NY before the countdown finishes, in case it explodes or something like that."

Luke: "Fair enough. I think I'll stay to see what happens."

Hannah: "I'll stay too."

Luke: "Could be dangerous."

Hannah: "First dates should always be dangerous."

Hannah: "I'm heading to practice now. My friend's garage still has power."

Luke: "See you later."

Exiting the building with Ray at his side, he checked the other message.

Lemira: "Luke, are you available? They said something about you sustaining injuries?"

Luke: "I'm fine. What's up?"

Lemira: "That is well, then. I'm afraid I have some bad news."

Luke sighed.

Luke: "Let's hear it."

Lemira: "The system event is coming. I am certain of it."

Luke: "The Singers?"

Lemira: "I do not think so. The event is coming soon. Today, I believe."

Luke: "How do you know?"

Lemira: "My companions told me. Sort of. And I have a feeling. Part of me stirs, as if excited."

Luke: "A demon thing?"

Lemira: "...Yes."

Luke: "Are we talking hours? I'm pretty spent."

He looked back up at the tall building, shaking his head. So many things were going on at the same time. Luminous Larks, Singers, countdown, and now this. He had considered the possibility of them all being connected, but he thought it was a little too soon for the Singers to arrive. Why he felt that way, he couldn't tell, but he did.

Lemira: "Soon. Gather your friends. We do not have much time."

As soon as she sent that last message, the ground beneath their feet rumbled.

"Earthquake?" Ray asked.

Luke shook his head. "Worse, I think. Much worse."
