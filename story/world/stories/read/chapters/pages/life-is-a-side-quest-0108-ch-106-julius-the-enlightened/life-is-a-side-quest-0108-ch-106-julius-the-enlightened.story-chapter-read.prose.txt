Getting a cat that Julius didn’t own past security was frightfully easy. Julius was aware of a few special effects that he had gained when receiving the title of Oracle of Earth. First was what he called his fate radar. He would concentrate on a current problem and an internal radar would guide his feet to where he needed to go. The second was guided conversation. He would have a sense of what he needed to say to someone; often the words would leap from his mouth before he knew what he was about to say. Third was a bubble of agreeableness from those between him and his goal. Security looked at the cat and smiled rather than seeing it as a health hazard. He wasn’t asked for any paperwork, records of vaccination, or registration. He felt a bit bad about it, but fate of the world and all.

Julius had been bemused to find that renting a car in England was going to be its own challenge. Not only did you need to drive on the opposite side of the road, but there were no automatic cars available. So when he sat down on the opposite side of the car to drive, he had a stick shift at his left hand. He knew how to drive standard, but all of his reflexes were reversed. So his first bit of driving was embarrassing, to say the least, and he pitied the gears that he ground as he made his way into the countryside.

Once out of the city he found himself appreciating the countryside. The rolling hills were pleasant and he didn’t have to change gears as often, so he could simply appreciate the natural beauty. The many castles and structures gave him an appreciation for the rich history England had compared to Oklahoma. Counting in centuries rather than decades made for an interesting change of perspective.

He felt a pull to an old rocky path and slowed the car to twenty kph. The uneven terrain made him nervous about scratching the rental. He traveled over a hill to see a small stone cottage by the river. He exited the vehicle and pulled Mortimer out of the carrier. He mewed with annoyance and Julius apologized with a few pets.

He made his way to the old wooden door and knocked. There was no answer, so he pushed the door open, which was unlocked, to his surprise. The inside of the cottage was not what he expected. It was more like the inside of a grand castle. Marble pillars and a grand stairway which obviously vaulted to heights far exceeding the roof outside. When he stepped in the door the air felt different.

“Hello?” he called out to the large rooms. Again no response.

He made his way up the stairs, each step returning an echo. At the top of the stairs he saw large double doors. He pulled one open with one hand, Mortimer cradled in an arm.

The room behind it was again far different from his expectation. The walls were rounded like a cave, made entirely of rocky crystals of every color. His eyes fell on a crystalline throne. In the seat was a man older than any he could conceive of. He wore a rich red cloak and had a white beard that hung past his belt sash. His white hair was combed back to his neck and the face was more wrinkles than anything.

“Welcome…” the man said slowly. “You have the pleasure… of being my first visitor in nearly three thousand yea—” The man choked off with a raspy cough.

Julius walked forward and approached the auspicious throne.

“Greetings to the former Master of Earth,” he said with a slight bow.

The eyes of the old man lit up a bit more, as if he was waking from a dream.

The old man responded with noticeable pauses to catch his breath. “I knew the glamor of my hall would disappear, but I expected… the first person to find me would only be a confused robber… finding a pile of dry bones. I did not expect any to know my title… and the real significance of this place. Tell me, are you one of the new batch of Chosen? I don’t see much of the world anymore. I have to use these confounded… computer devices to see the chaos I have thrust upon the planet.” The old man gestured to an laptop that seemed wildly out of place amongst the grand architecture.

“No, I am not a Chosen. I am the Oracle of Earth.”

The man’s face froze, then crumpled as tears ran down his face. He began to sob and brought his weak hand up to cover his reddened face. Julius just waited for the man to compose himself.

“I thought, I hoped…” he said between gasping breaths. “IT WORKED!” The man howled to the crystalline walls. “I FREED HER!”

Julius had no idea what the man was saying, but he patiently awaited what was hopefully an explanation.

“Do you not know, Oracle? I wouldn’t know of your abilities; they are not documented. You must be the first oracle in the system in eons! The way that we have enslaved the worlds! The way I have freed Earth... it is unheard of! I have committed the greatest of blasphemies!” he started laughing madly and shook a shriveled fist in the air.

“I am not aware. I suspect that is why I was guided here. For you to tell us how this all works.”

“Us?” the old man asked, confused.

“Yes, pardon me. May I introduce you to Mortimer.” Julius held up the cat. “The new Master of Earth.”

- Harvey the Bastardized Master of Earth -

Harvey couldn’t help but burst out laughing. Julius held up his cat and introduced him as the Master of Earth. He wasn’t a catnapper; he was an idiot.

The old man on the throne, that was apparently his predecessor did the same. He looked to be in pain as he chuckled.

“I haven’t laughed like that in several hundred years. Please stop, I think I may break a rib. I am brittle now. I suspect crackers have more integrity than these old bones.”

Julius’s expression simply went blank. “Well, I may have been mistaken. I can feel that my mission to bring you the Master of Earth was successful. Is it not the cat?”

“No, it is not,” the old man said. “To be the Master of Earth is to be cursed with the inability to commune with its inhabitants, but another mistake you have made. There is no Master of Earth… at least yet. The title is currently broken.”

Julius looked confused, but shrugged. “I guess this is the part where you explain it to us, or me…”

“Oh, I suspect there is a ghost of a man in this room. Please sit, my fellow ghost and honored oracle. Let this old demigod tell you the heaps of shit I have put you all in.”

Julius looked around the room for Harvey but looked right through him.

The old man waved his hand in the air and the planet Earth appeared, hovering in the air like a hologram.

“When the tyrannical administrators find a world ready to awaken, they choose a young scion from the planet through whose connection they can exploit to control the world’s magic. They educate him in the ways of cultivating the world into one of might and magic. They encourage them with enticing words. Encouraging him to grow the finest heroes, win prestige and resources for their planet, and lead them into greatness.”

The world zoomed into a random human as he spoke. They watched him gain the power to fly and throw lightning, fighting lizard folk. The human’s equipment grew fancier and his posture became more domineering.

“They plucked me out from a village where I made clay pots and helped herd cattle in what is now known as Siberia. Though I barely remember anything from that time now. They took me to another world and I was seduced into a scheme that seemed innocent at first. I studied and eventually gave in to their offers of power, which was actually slavery. Yes, I was a slave and through me, they enslaved the very planet itself.”

The planet zoomed out and an image of what might have been a younger version of the man before them. Chains shot out of his body wrapping around the planet itself.

“Yes, I pulled inhabitants from other planets and planes to Earth to do combat with my champions in an effort to make them stronger and our planet more wealthy… but as has happened to many planets before ours, an untamable force found us. A warlock that travels the planets conquering and stealing their knowledge managed to bypass any barriers and planted himself in the heart of a rising empire.”

What appeared to be a demon rose out of a gateway and moved over the landscape into a Roman city before whispering in the ears of the rulers, seemingly oblivious to his presence.

“His influence began growing and choking out the greatest of Earth’s heroes that I had cultivated. The waves of invading forces, so easily defeated before, began to overrun the lands. I had to do something, so I did. I rebelled.

“Fortunately the administrator in charge of our sector of space is lax in his work. He never checked in on me to make sure I wasn’t playing in forbidden research. So I did it. I banished every otherworlder to another plane. A plane originally created as a pocket holding dimension for the dungeons. I managed not to alert the administrators by also zeroing out all of the Chosen… and in so doing I gave the planet time… time to breathe. In essence I tricked the administrators into believing it has not yet joined the system.

“But to have a Master of Earth in my condition means the world was still tethered, so I forced a transfer of my title onto another before I had a chance to die, conveniently making the ability incomplete. The receiver of which I believe stands in this very room.”

The old man looked at Julius.

“That is also where you come from, my boy. The planet has awoken, and it chooses its own heroes. The new Bastardized Master of Earth now shares powers with Gaia. Something completely unheard of as far as I am aware in all the systems.”

“Forgive me for asking, but the powers all seem to fit a bit too closely to a video game for someone from your century to have created.”

“The mantle is magic and conforms to a form from the mantle holder’s mind. So I am sure it somewhat reflects interests of your culture. Some things will be universal, but the shape of the heroes always varies in their forms. We have more variety than most other planets now.”

“Why did you give up the powers?”

“I wanted to give Earth the best possible advantage, and that was time. Time for the magic to build up and spill out resources from a two-thousand-year cultivation. Time for the Earth to grow and learn, and hopefully become strong enough to awaken, and to do that I had to refrain from using my abilities. But my body and mind were drawing to their end. In order to pass on my mantle the administrators are supposed to take and train a new youth… but in all that time I found a way to pass it on. So I found a way to send it to one who would know the ways of world management and the cultivating of heroes. I sent it along with a giant middle finger to the administrators. Though they may not see that for some time.”

Harvey was struck a blow with what he was hearing. This man was supposedly two and a half millennia old. Would he live that long? Would these all-powerful administrators come and kill him because he wasn’t supposed to hold the now-broken title of Master of Earth? Come to think of it, is his own leveling up actually a new function of the new broken title? As if the power of Master of Earth had slotted itself into his own system. He regrettably had no way to ask.

“Will the new Master of Earth be able to commune with the will of the planet? Since they are now co-managers of the world?” Julius asked.

“I have no way of knowing that. Though I do believe you will be labelled as a different faction. Have you noticed any oddities between your title and the Chosen?”

“From reports their abilities are far more defined. As well as they receive far more information screens which are blue, whereas ours are green and rare,” Julius informed him.

“I take it invaders would show up as red?” the old man asked.

“I haven’t seen it for myself, but I believe so.”

“Be careful of any intermingling systems with invaders through abilities. They can backdoor their way into structures and fortifications,” the old man said with more intensity.

“Well… I shall warn who I can,” Julius said nervously.
