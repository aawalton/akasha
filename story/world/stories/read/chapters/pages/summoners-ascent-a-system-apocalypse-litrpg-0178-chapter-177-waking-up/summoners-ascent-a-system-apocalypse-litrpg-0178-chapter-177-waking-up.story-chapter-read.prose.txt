Ashton slowly opened his eyes, but saw completely nothing. For a moment, a bit of panic set in, until his half-asleep mind realised what was going on.

While he was asleep, he was able to see perfectly fine in his dreams, so the sudden switch was a little startling again. Ashton was sure he would get used to it, though.

Carefully, he pushed himself up from his mattress. He had a massive headache and his body was still a bit sore, but at least the overall exhaustion was gone

Now that he was awake, there were a few things that Ashton had to do first and foremost.

"Backpacker?" Ashton asked with a hoarse, dry throat. The spirit appeared without hesitation, and he could feel their relief that he had woken up. Why they were so relieved was something he would deal with later, just as he would deal with the ridiculous amount of system messages that had accumulated while he was sleeping.

For now, the spirit gave Ashton a few things that he had prepared beforehand. First, a bottle of water that he instantly chugged. And then, right after, he ate a couple of oat bars. He hadn't eaten for a few days now, and though his resilience stat should prevent him from having adverse effects from suddenly eating something, Ashton had encountered plenty of people who struggled a lot in this kind of scenario.

Ashton technically didn't need to eat in Lumia since most of the time the Summoner simply supplied him with mana, but there were periods of time when they 'experimented' on him and made sure that he would starve. And when he snuck some food during those times, he often had a pretty rough time, too. He definitely didn't want to risk anything happening here.

Of course, it wasn't like anything dangerous would happen even if Ashton ate something else, he just didn't want to hide behind some bushes while out and about and do his 'business' when he had much more important things to take care.

After getting something into his stomach, Ashton got started on something else. He quickly grabbed one of the last pre-made summoning circles for the wisp and placed a small bottle on top of it.

"Come to me, lesser wisp," Ashton briefly chanted while channelling mana into the paper, and the liquid inside of the plastic bottle disappeared. It was a potion.

While technically, food was Ashton's favourite method of supplying 'effort' to the wisp, anything that he made himself would work just as well. Ink, potions, food, or any other item worked.

And right now, this was the easiest solution, since cooking without being able to see in this context was pretty hard. The wisp appeared in front of Ashton, and he immediately smiled.

"Thanks for joining me. I hope the past couple days weren't too scary after what happened," Ashton said, apologising once more as he petted the elemental. But it simply pushed back into his hand, seemingly just happy that everything worked out fine.

And with that, he proceeded to channel the elemental immediately. The world around him quickly came back into view, and Ashton let out a slight sigh of relief. Everything seemed to be alright here. Though, he remembered there being less of certain materials here. Did Sam stash some extra stuff while Ashton was asleep?

"How nice," Ashton said, clearing his throat lightly. It was still a bit uncomfortable to speak, but he just had to push through this for half an hour and he'd be fine.

The next thing Ashton checked on was the arcane pocket watch; it was really the only way for him to gauge the time as things were right now. It was around seven in the evening right now. He was extremely grateful for the small symbol that said whether it was before or after noon.

With this, Ashton was ready to actually get up. He positioned himself in the middle of the office and did some simply stretches, before walking out into the hallway. He had a mental checklist that he was going to quickly take care of now.

Ashton's first stop was the nearby restroom. There were a few people in the hallway, though he really didn't pay much attention to them.

Once there, Ashton picked up the dyed fabrics from the sinks. The water had evaporated a bit too much for his liking, but he was extremely happy with the even pattern of mana in these, though it was a lot more intense than he expected. These dyes worked the best over the course of a few days, and since they were magical in nature, Ashton was able to ensure easily that the colour absorbed easily.

Sure, he should check with Sam later to see that the colours were as he wanted them, but he was positive these turned out great.

Ashton quickly decided to summon the undine and sylph as well, using them to not only wash out the excess dye from the fabric and dry it afterward, but also to give himself a proper wash. Of course, he also cleaned up the sinks decently.

He put on some new underwear, his trousers and his boots, and for now, put on the cloak as well so that he wasn't running around shirtless. Ashton didn't care, but others did, apparently.

With the new fabrics in hand, Ashton headed back over to the office. He didn't exactly have the time to actually use these to make the clothes he planned for, but he would get to it as soon as he had a bit of free-time.

Mentally, Ashton checked off the next box, and then asked the Backpacker for his pen, his tome, and some ink with a high concentration of mana. This one in particular was made using flower pigment and pollen, specifically meant for flower pixies. Ashton mixed it with the blue ink from the seven-coloured inkwell and started to draw.

Since this was his first time drawing a summoning circle for Poppy, he figured he might as well go all-out, and put it straight into the tome. It was actually a little exciting, since this was the first time he drew one in here.

Ashton hummed slightly, still ignoring all the messages that kept popping up around him. At this point it was just the constellations adding on to things, but he wanted at least a little bit of peace before having to deal with them. Plus, he kind of wanted to annoy at least one of those three as a little bit of revenge. The other two were just collateral.

The summoning circle was drawn fast, and Ashton quickly took out some candy, popped one into his own mouth, and used a couple of the rest as an offering.

"Come to me, Poppy," Ashton said as he poured his mana into the summoning circle, and just a few moments later, the pixie appeared right in front of him.

Without a moment's hesitation, she flew at Ashton's face and gave him a massive hug. Her voice kept ringing as she spoke to him; Ashton wasn't quite sure what she was trying to say, but from the feel of it, she was upset at how long Ashton was asleep for.

"Yes, yes, I missed you, too," Ashton smiled, letting the small fairy do whatever she wanted for a little while. As he did, he figured he should also get to drawing some summoning circles for his other contracts as well. Just for now, while he was giving himself a bit of time for his headache to go away.

He opened the window and lit up a cigarette, too. Just a simple one, rolled using the Asterian tobacco that he bought from the Salesman, mixed with a couple of herbs useful for physical recovery and mana stability.

If he had a nice cup of tea right next to him, he'd have himself a pretty solid, relaxing morning. Well, evening, technically. Poppy was seated on his shoulder and was watching happily, and the Backpacker sat beside him on their phone, while the elementals were floating around Ashton.

Frankly, this was all pretty... nice. Despite how much shit was running through his mind right now that he had to process later, he was feeling pretty good.

By the time Ashton filled the tome up with the current contracts that he had formed, he had gotten through not just the cigarette but another bottle of water, leaned back in Sam's office chair.

A long, purposefully stretched-out sigh left Ashton's mouth, as he finally paid attention to the backlog of system messages.

"Alright, you three, what'd I miss?" Ashton asked, closing the tome.

[[Daughter of the Scarlet Heart] says it is about time]

[[Executor of the End] sighs and greets you]

[[Observer in the Veil] is glad to see that you have made it back with your soul in one piece]

[[Executor of the End] adds that things were pretty close there a couple days ago]

[[Daughter of the Scarlet Heart] looks at the Observer and Executor confused]

Ashton smiled, crossing his legs as he leaned onto his hand. "Don't worry about it, Daughter. Just had a small run-in with someone while I was asleep. Actually, multiple someones."

[[Daughter of the Scarlet Heart] asks what that grin is about]

[[Executor of the End] is curious about your tone of voice]

"Now it's your turn, Executor. Don't worry about it," Ashton replied. He figured it was too early to hint at the Daughter what he now knew about her. "Anyway, did I miss anything big while I was asleep? The town's still standing, right?"

[[Observer in the Veil] nods and says that the town is safe]

"Don't patronize me. This town isn't safe. I asked if it's still standing. If the council pulled anything fucked up," Ashton sighed, and a few more messages popped up. The constellations were really just starting to chatter again, not saying any details. They couldn't even if they wanted to, anyway. It still seemed worth a try.

But with that, for now, Ashton had to look at the other messages.

[Class | Summoner - Level 11 -> Summoner - Level 12]

[Skill | Veil Mastery - Level 2 -> Veil Mastery - Level 7]

[Skill | Precision Aether Sensitivity - Level 10 -> Precision Aether Sensitivity - Level 12]

[Skill | Channel - Level 9 -> Channel - Level 10]

[The [Channel] Skill has reached Level 10 and can now be refined]

[Contract | Lesser Wisp - Level 10 -> Lesser Wisp - Level 11]

[Contract | Flower Pixie - Level 5 -> Flower Pixie - Level 6]

[Contract | Backpacker - Level 9 -> Backpacker - Level 10]

[Due to persistent growth, a contract is ready to advance]

[Through the high trust between you and the summon, you have been given the right to choose the advancement]

[[Main Quest 2] has ended! [Main Quest 3] will soon proceed!]

Ashton went through the messages one by one. Quite a lot had happened while he was asleep...

The veil mastery skill levelling up a full 5 times in one go? And his aether sensitivity going up twice as well.

"I guess astral projection is a bigger deal than I thought," Ashton whispered, surprised. If he could enter the astral plane every time he slept, then that skill was going to level up ridiculously fast. Then again, he didn't really know if that was how it worked, anyway.

For now, the most important parts were really the Channel skill and the Backpacker's contract both reaching level 10.

But just when Ashton was about to check out what options there were for each of them to develop into, the door to the office swung open.

Sam stood there in the doorway, letting out a loud, relieved sigh. "Holy fucking shit, mate, you had me scared for a second there."

Ashton raised a brow. "Oh calm down, I just walked around in my underpants for a bit. My giblets weren't visible to anyone, as you've asked."

"Huh? What– Oh, no, that's not what I'm talking about. Ash, do you know how long you've been asleep for?" Sam stepped up to the desk. He was wearing different—far better—gear than he had when Ashton went to sleep.

"The quest's over, and it's evening, so... I'm guessing a day and a half-ish?"

Sam shook his head immediately, groaning at how lightly Ashton was taking all of this. "Well that's what we thought, but fuck no, mate. You've been sleeping for more than three days."
