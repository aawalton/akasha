Don moved about in the forest with a brisk pace. The faint strays of sunlight sprayed through the canopies, enveloping everything in a bright green and yellow light. It had been years since Don had been in a forest during early morning. The dew moistened the cuffs of his pants and branches and bushes gently scraped his skin. Quite the contrast to the dark woods he had skulked around yesterday.

Don found something strange and unexpected.

He was enjoying himself.

He had been so afraid and confused yesterday. But the scary darkness and fight after fight with monsters had tempered him. The good night’s sleep had solidified it. Now he did not feel like he was scrambling about, trying to avoid monsters. Hell, he was looking for monsters. He felt at home and at ease in the forest. It felt right.

After some time, following the vague directions of Charlie, Don found a forest opening. He pushed through a copse of young trees and crouched in wait. In the middle of the opening was a white platform. There were strange etching on it, like runes carved by an ancient civilization. In the middle of the platform stood a strange white building. It was ten feet high and five feet wide. A completely white uniform block of metal. It was certainly not made by humans.

On one face of the white block was a screen. It was black for now.

This has to be the System Point dispenser. But where is the—

Then Don saw it—a hulking abomination, like a bear crossbred with nightmares and steroids, emerged from the treeline. It was massive, easily as tall as the dispenser and twice as wide. The fur was bright red. It had nothing to do with camouflage and everything to do with intimidation. Just try me, it screamed.

The face was contorted in a still visage of bulging eyes and rage. The snarl of the bear-monster revealed two rows of jagged teeth. Don immediately looked at its claws. Yup, three inches, sharp as razors, red as blood. On its back, instead of fur, was a spine-ridge of protruding spines. They seemed soft and waved as the bear moved, but Don’s instincts told him this was another defense measure.

Don used Identify on the monster

[Blazeclaw, level 15 Boss Monster]

Don didn’t need to see the level to know he could absolutely not take on this creature. But the sight filled him with excitement. He would come back and take on this juggernaut. The feeling after killing the boar had been one of the best in his life. He wanted that triumph again. Don knew defeating this beast would give him a stronger high. But he was no fool. He would need more levels and more evolutions to fight the Blazeclaw. So he did his best to press into memory the location of this dispenser and then stealthily moved away, before looking one last time at Blazeclaw’s hulking frame.

The next order of business was simple. Look for prey. Preferably Barbtail Gibbons. Don wasn’t sure if the monkeys were nocturnal only, but it wouldn’t hurt to look. He could feel that he was only one or two kills away from acquiring an evolution trait from them.

But any monster would do. Don fell into a comfortable jog through the forest, sprinting intermittently to increase his skill and just because it was so damn fun. Additionally he got a deeper understanding of how the energy system worked.

Soon his Survival Instincts flared up. Not much, but enough to put Don into a mode of alertness. He stopped and crouched next to a thick tree. There were two Barbtail Gibbons feasting on a human corpse. Don watched in disgust as their bloody hands ripped apart the body. These gibbons were large, much larger than the ones Don had encountered the previous night. They were muscular and almost human sized. Their tails were long with thick barbs at the end. He used Identify on them.

[Barbtail Gibbon Alpha, level 8]

A notch above Don’s previous prey. And there were two of them. Don grinned to himself. What a nice way to start the morning. He didn’t waste any time and started creeping closer. His fairly high level Stealth allowed him to get close enough to hear the monkeys arguing and ripping the flesh off bones. The dead body was a mangled corpse of a thirty year old man.

Don flicked his wrist and the sleek claws flashed out. He allowed himself a quick moment to look at them in the sunlight. A bit over two inches long, sharp, black and hard. Interestingly, also the musculature in his fingers was thicker when the claws were out.

Don watched the monkeys flick their thick tails around as they enjoyed their meal. He made a quick plan. Simple, as plans like this should be. Anything elaborate would be tossed out the window the moment combat started.

Sneak up to one of them, assassinate it with a quick stab at the neck. Grab the other one by the tail. Cover head.

Don knew he could withstand the damage to his body and the pain. But a severe head trauma might incapacitate him and that would be the end of that. He would love to have some defensive evolutions sometime soon.

I doubt I’ll get one from the gibbons, but I’ll start my morning with any kind of growth.

Don snuck forward, until he was close enough. Then he went into a full sprint and pulled his right hand back. The monkeys turned and screeched. The first target was too slow. Don connected all his fingers into a point and thrust the claws deep into the gibbon’s neck. The screech was cut short and replaced with a gurgle. The monkey slapped at him and struggled, but it was weakening by the second. Don wasted no time. He tried to stomp on the other monkey’s tail. It swiped the tail away and to Don’s surprise pushed the body of his dying comrade at Don, toppling them both.

The smelly monkey fell on Don and the other gibbon immediately aimed its tail like a flail at Don’s head. Don only managed to block it partially, and immediately blood spurted from his forehead and his vision went blurry.

[Health at 88%]

[Energy at 81%]

[Debuff inflicted: Mild bleeding]

He pulled the dying gibbon to cover his head. The other monkey jumped on top of the pile and started slamming and pummeling while the barbed tail whipped at Don’s head. The monkey was strong and in his dazed state, Don had trouble getting up. The stabbed monkey let out a rasping breath.

[You have slain [Barbtail Gibbon Alpha – lvl 8] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 12 System Points earned]

Using his System enhanced strength, Don managed to throw the battering and screeching monkey off him, but it attacked with ferocity again. However, it bought Don enough time to scramble up from under the dead monkey. Warm blood trickled down his face, blinding his other eye.

Don blocked a few swipes from the gibbon and kicked the monkey in its stomach. The gibbon dodged and wrapped its tail around Don’s thigh. The barbs bit in deep when the monkey squeezed, but Don only grinned.

“You fucked up.”

Don grabbed the tail and yanked. The monkey was pulled forward and Don headbutted it. The monkey reeled backward, but Don yanked the tail again and repeated the brutal strike. By this time, the tail had already loosened, but Don kept holding it. After two more headbutts the monkey’s consciousness was fading and Don jammed his clawed hand into its throat. It let out a weak growl and died.

[You have slain [Barbtail Gibbon Alpha – lvl 8] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 14 System Points earned]

Don fell on his butt and groaned. His leg was bleeding pretty badly and the head wound was making him woozy. He wasn’t in immediate danger, but the system kept yammering about hypovolemic shock again. He had eight minutes before bleeding dry, but he needed this moment.

This was his favorite feeling in this new world. The relief after struggle. The sweet victory in a life-or-death situation. He drank the feeling in. It invigorated him and Don didn’t suppress the wild grin spreading on his face. He breathed the cool morning air of the forest in and sighed contently. His thigh spasmed. He tasted blood in his mouth. His other eye was temporarily blinded from the blood.

Don had never felt so alive.

After a minute or so, he used Devour on the gibbons. He scored a total of 37 Evolution points, and of course topped up his Health and Energy. The wounds in his thigh and forehead knit up. But most importantly, he got the evolution. The system message was a joy to see.

[Evolution trait Barbtail Gibbon: Evolved!]
