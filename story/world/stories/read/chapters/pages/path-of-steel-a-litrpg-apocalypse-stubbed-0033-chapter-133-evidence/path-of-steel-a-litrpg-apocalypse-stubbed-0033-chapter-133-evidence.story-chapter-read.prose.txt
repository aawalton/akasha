The Witch Coven was nestled in the heart of a verdant forest, small buildings coexisting with the trees. Even the clan’s darker half, the satanic circle, believed in harmonizing oneself with nature. Just a different nature.

In a small office carved into the roots of a tree, the door suddenly opened. A lean man wielding off-brown robes with cyan trimmings stepped inside, his face expressionless but carrying the casual violence of a man who’d inflicted a lot of it and wasn’t afraid to do it again.

“Greetings. Is this the private lab reception?” he asked.

The woman manning the counter—the only other person in the room—looked up. She had brown hair and sapphire eyes. Commanding features carrying unmistakable beauty lit up the room, while her natural aura made even this man feel calm. Her gaze, however, quickly turned cold.

“What could a Heaven Reaching enforcer want with me?” she asked.

“My name is Brochor, a first-degree enforcer of the Heaven Reaching Clan’s Clan Floor’s Enforcement Hall. As part of an ongoing investigation, I’m here to inquire about lab rentals.”

“That’s private information.”

“I don’t want it all. Only for a specific person: Asim El’Shenawy. If he has rented any private labs recently, I’d like to know.”

The woman’s expression didn’t change. “You’ll need the permission of the Disciplinary Circle.”

Brochor raised a piece of paper. The woman inspected it, then retrieved a thick black book from under the counter and let it land on the counter. It echoed. “Asim El’Shenawy…” she said, flipping through the pages at an angle that Brochor couldn’t read.

He could, however, see that her movements were slow. She was hesitant to give him this information, but he knew how this always went. He waited. Five minutes later, she spoke.

“I do see a rental. He held a private lab for three months.”

“Is he still renting it?”

“Yes.”

“Is he currently inside?”

“No.”

“That’s fine. Let’s take a look.”

She snapped the book shut and shook her head. “You need permission for that.”

“I got permission.”

“You need another. Laboratories fall under the Alchemical Archives. You need their permission and the Disciplinary Circle’s to search a lab without the consent of its rentee.”

Brochor’s brows fell. He studied her for a moment, then left the room. The woman stood still and nonchalant, but she’d already opened her chat and asked the supervisor at the Alchemical Archives to delay the hostile man. Then, she opened a large cupboard under her desk to reveal a coop. Three chickens rested inside. One had just laid eggs. The woman smiled at the chickens, then picked one up and whispered something in its ear. The chicken nodded. It ran off, leaving the room through a special hole near the ground, and sprinted for the residence belonging to Asim El’Shenawy. With any luck, he would be there.

Asim was stirring a cauldron in his small residence. “Not like that!” a girl laughed. She was gorgeous, with purple hair and short dark robes revealing long, enticing legs under her inviting features. Unlike those of the nature circle, the witches of the satanic circle possessed a certain wild charm which made them irresistible to everyone regardless of gender.

In fact, the Coven had a special place just out front where love-stricken men could live while waiting for the target of their affections to acknowledge them. It never happened.

“You do it then, Delilah,” Asim replied with irritation, tossing her the ladle.

She laughed again as she grabbed it out of the air, then reached the caludron’s side. “You want to keep a steady tempo and brush against the walls. Look—the little whirlpool in the middle is where all the magic happens. The herbs release their essence, which mixes into the base of the Dishonesty Potion.”

“That’s exactly what I was doing,” Asim said.

“No, you were doing it wrong.”

“Fuck you. You just wanted to show off.”

Delilah laughed. Suddenly, a chicken barged in through a hole near the floor and rushed to Asim. “Asim El’Shenawy!” it cawed. “An enforcer from the Heaven Reaching clan is asking about your laboratory. Caw! He will get permission to investigate it in a few hours. Caw! Please be safe. Remira. Caw!”

With that, the chicken started poking at the ground like a regular chicken. Asim’s face turned ashen. So did Delilah’s.

“Asim…” she said.

“Don’t worry. They only asked about me,” he said.

“I just hope you’re okay… I can testify if you want.”

“Thank you, but there is no need.” He grabbed the chicken and whispered in its ear, “I appreciate the heads-up, Ramira. Next dinner’s on me.”

The chicken ran off. Asim was already putting on his overcoat. “Can you wrap this up?” he said.

“Where are you going?”

“To meet a friend. We have a plan to hatch.”

“Can’t you just message them?”

Asim froze. “I keep forgetting magic exists,” he muttered, opening his chat.

Asim: Chloe, a Heaven Reaching enforcer wants to search my lab. They’re onto us.

She replied immediately.

Chloe: They must have found you through Gary. Shit.

Asim: I can’t clean up the residue. They’ll know I was melting boss balls. I’m skipping town.

Chloe: Wait. Don’t leave yet. I’m coming over.

Asim: Why?

Chloe: If they know you’re guilty, they’ll connect you to me and Gary. We can’t endure a proper questioning. We’d all need to leave town together.

Asim: Correct. It’s not the end of the world.

Chloe: I have a better idea. It’s a bit risky, but it can save us all.

Asim: What idea, Chloe?

Chloe: We’ll leave that enforcer a little gift.

Brochor walked into the opulent villa. Thinly-clad servants bowed at him—a few smiled—but he ignored them. He entered the tea room.

“You walk triumphantly, Brochor,” Elder Sui said, enjoying a cup by himself. A second, smaller cup appeared on the table, but Brochor only bowed.

“I investigated Gary Steel as you instructed, sir, and found two people he became close with during the Floor of Trials. One Chloe Santana from the Deep Blue Academy and one Asim El’Shenawy from the Witch Coven.”

“The Coven? The same place giving the Muscle Sap recipe to students along with the equipment to produce it?”

“Precisely, sir.”

“When will they learn?” Elder Sui said, chuckling. “And?”

“Chloe Santana has been seen with the Cult of the Broken Compass, to which the fugitive dealer belongs. The extent of their relationship is unknown, and I couldn’t dig deeper. I then visited the Coven and got permission to search Asim El’Shenawy’s private lab. They delayed me unnecessarily.”

“They don’t give a shit about Imperial Law. They let students study forbidden recipes. Well, a new drafter renting an expensive private lab is suspicious in and of itself.”

“Precisely my thoughts, sir. I threw around some names to get permission, and I discovered traces of boss ball melting. Asim El’Shenawy was our alchemist. He and Chloe Santana are both gone now, someone must have tipped them off, but Gary Steel is still here. I have a scout watching him.”

“Hmm. This isn’t solid evidence, but I’m confident I can demand his re-questioning,” Elder Sui said.

Brochor gave a rare smile. “That won’t be necessary, sir.”

He threw an object, which cluttered on the table. Elder Sui didn’t care about the gesture. He inspected the item, then laughed out loud. “Who cares about the little alchemist? This is a great find, Brochor. It draws the connection we needed. With this as evidence, we have enough to drag Gary Steel in for questioning, if not execute him outright.”

“Yes, sir. I thought you might want to be present for the arrest.”

Elder Sui was unable to hide his smile. “Not yet,” he said.

“Sir?”

“We have enough evidence to capture Gary Steel and have Whitefang question him. If he’s guilty, which he surely is, the truth will come to light, and we can have him executed by nightfall. That was my plan so far, but with evidence this concrete, we can aim higher. There’s a bigger fish out there. I want Colorful.”

Brochor’s eyes brightened. “How should we go about it, sir?”

“If Gary Steel is apprehended now, he will be punished for his crimes, but Colorful will at most receive a scolding. However, if we wait for Duang to slay Gary Steel and then reveal this evidence publicly, there will be outrage. Punishment will need to be delivered, but Gary Steel will no longer be there to accept it or testify to his master’s innocence. Who, then, will take the blame?”

“Vice Head Colorful,” Brochor said with calm certainty.

“Well, hopefully,” Elder Sui spoke with joy. “I doubt we’ll get him sentenced, but a public humiliation will make my mood. Let’s see him suffer a bit, and who knows? If he says the wrong thing with Whitefang present, we really might get him killed.”

“Will he really make a mistake, sir? He’s crazy but not a fool.”

“I’ll taunt him. His whore of a daughter gave us enough material, thank God. If he resists the taunts, I win, and if he fails, he dies. We can’t lose, Brochor.”

“Then I suppose, sir, that I should keep an eye on Gary Steel. He can’t be allowed to run away.”

“Record anything illegal or suspicious he does, too. As long as Gary Steel doesn’t run away and dies to Duang, our plan can’t fail!”
