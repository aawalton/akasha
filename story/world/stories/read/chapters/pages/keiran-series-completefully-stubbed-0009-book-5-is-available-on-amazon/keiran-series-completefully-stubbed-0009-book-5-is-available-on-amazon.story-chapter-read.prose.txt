Book 5 is now available on Amazon, fully edited and with a shiny new cover. Thank you for reading the whole series. It means a lot to me that so many people enjoyed my work. Prior to launching Keiran, I was ready to give up being a writer. I was running out of savings and things just weren't working out with my previous works, so this story really was my last shot at making a career out of telling stories. I cannot properly express how amazing it is that Keiran has been so popular both here and on Amazon. Seriously, you guys. Thank you.

I'll make a new post here to let you guys know if/when something goes on sale (we have no control over Audible sales, for example. They don't even tell us they stuck something in there), and to let you know when the sequel series launches (hopefully sometime in 2026). Just to set expectations, it will be in the same world, the epilogue of book 5 should give you plenty of hints about what kind of story it will be, and Keiran will be present, though he won't be the protagonist.
