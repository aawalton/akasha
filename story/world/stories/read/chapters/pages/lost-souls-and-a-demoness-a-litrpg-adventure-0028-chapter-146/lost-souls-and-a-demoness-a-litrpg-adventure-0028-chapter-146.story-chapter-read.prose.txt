For once, Jade was one of the first to get up the next morning. Newly rested and imbued with an unusual amount of energy for the early hour, she decided to start the day off with a little fun. She slipped out of the tent without disturbing Naomi or Siora and quietly greeted Parker and Fenrin, who were finishing the last watch shift. After confirming that nothing dangerous was around and telling them where she was going Jade took to the sky, soaring into the crisp morning air and reveling in sensations of speed and freedom. This was exactly what she needed to shrug off the unease that had plagued her the previous night. It was a new day, and she had reason to celebrate: After Illysport, their next stop was Lysara.

She was finally going to get Siora the ingredients she needed to make the elixir that could help her brother.

Jade grinned, swooping over the foggy swamp they'd crossed yesterday and marveling at how the morning sun sparkled against the rippling surfaces of hundreds of pools and streams. Dense greenery carpeted the portions that weren't submerged, forming a patchwork quilt of verdant hues. A simple change of perspective was all it took to change something murky and unpleasant into a thing of beauty and life. She wondered at that, banking into a stiff breeze and spreading her wings to glide along invisible currents of air. It reflected the aspects of her new form, comprised of both parts she loved and ones she despised. The two were inseparable, as both were her nature. But that didn't mean she had to cultivate both.

She caught an updraft, relaxing as it held her aloft. Jade was practiced enough with flight now that she felt comfortable turning her attention to the many notices the system had queued for her. It was finally time to see the fruits of their labor. The first three were level-up notifications.

Level Up!

You have reached Level 25.

Reward: +2 stat points.

All taken together, Jade was raised to level 27. She allocated her stat points, dividing them primarily between agility and fortitude. Strangely, the latter was lower than it had been before the battle. Had she sustained damage so severe it had altered her stats? Regardless of the reason, increasing her defenses was of paramount importance. The battle had reminded her that it was impossible to dodge every attack, and that she needed to be tough enough to take some hits if she wanted to have any staying power in a fight. Level 26 came with a class feature as well as stat points, and she pulled up the list of available options. Of the new ones presented to her, two stood out.

Siphoning Strikes

Rating: C level talent

Cost: None

Duration: Passive

Cooldown: None

Effect: Your melee attacks are imbued with mana drain. Mana drained scales with your Intelligence and is the same regardless of damage dealt. Successful attacks have a chance to inflict Mana Poisoning, which further depletes mana and inhibits regeneration. This effect can stack.

The other one was:

Curse of Lethargy

Rating: C level spell

Cost: High mana

Duration: 1 minute

Cooldown: 30 seconds

Effect: Curse a nearby target, reducing its movement speed and attack speed. If the target attempts to cast a spell while under this effect, there is a chance for the spell to misfire. Each time the same target is afflicted with this condition, the potency and duration of the effect increases. This is a curse.

Jade considered the two options, mindful of the fact that she was a few hundred feet above the ground. While there wasn't anything to run into in the sky, flying still took a degree of focus. Both abilities appealed to her, each for a different reason. Siphoning Strikes appeared to be the levelled-up version of one of the D level talents the Labyrinth presented to her early on. Its current incarnation was quite an upgrade, providing a potential solution to the ongoing issue of Jade's mana supply. While Intelligence was her second highest stat, providing Jade with a large maximum mana pool, the 40% decrease her willpower suffered while she was in her demon form largely negated that advantage. Without a matching mana regeneration, she tended to exhaust her reserves before longer battles finished. This had hurt her in the battle against Seraphine and was only getting worse as she acquired new spells and abilities that consumed mana. Even her flight power, as wondrous as it was, consumed magical power at a significant rate if she was flying in the light of day.

On the other hand, another affliction she could apply at range would be a welcome addition to her arsenal. The versatility to contribute equally from a distance as she could up close would allow their team to employ different tactics, giving them the opportunity to surprise opponents who thought they had them figured out. It was an enticing option but, in the end, Curse of Lethargy's mana cost proved the deciding factor. Until she had a more reliable way of maintaining her reserves throughout a fight, accumulating more spells would only compound the issue. Jade selected Siphoning Strikes, making a mental note to take another look at Curse of Lethargy when she got her next class feature.

The next few notifications dealt with completing the Time Bomb quest related to the corespace meltdown, a generic Labyrinth Core quest, and a follow up quest to find and deactivate yet another Core. The amount of experience awarded for the pair of quests was massive, and explained how she had managed to jump three levels at once. The rewards were an equally impressive amount of crescent coins and another superior upgrade point. Fortunately, Jade had long since learned how to deposit quest reward earnings directly into her dimensional bag, avoiding a rain of thousands of coins that otherwise would have showered into the bog below. There were a few more windows waiting for her attention, but Jade pulled up her character sheet before getting to them. She'd been waiting for another upgrade point and knew exactly what she wanted to spend it on.

Succubus Racial Ability: Seducer's Mantle

Fortify your body with a magical array that protects you from harm and enhances your presence. Gain passive damage reduction and a bonus to charisma. The strength of these bonuses is proportional to your amount of exposed skin, ranging from very low to high. This ability's effectiveness is reduced while not in your demon form. This is an enhancement effect.

Superior Upgrade Effect: The defensive and charisma bonuses of this ability are significantly increased. Additionally, nearby creatures of your choice are distracted by your presence and may be less willing to attack you and your allies. Effected creatures are less likely to resist your charm spells. These effects are more pronounced in creatures that find you attractive but affect all creature types to some degree.

"That's more like it." Jade murmured to herself, closing her character sheet. Now her defenses were truly reinforced, with the unexpected additional benefit of her charm spells becoming more reliable. She hadn't even bothered wasting mana on trying to charm Seraphine, but if she kept stacking improvements like this, something like that might become possible. Jade wasn't about to start counting on effectively cheating her way through boss fights, she suspected the Labyrinth wouldn't allow it, but the ability to charm powerful demons could make a big difference in the days to come.

Finally, Jade turned her attention to the last set of windows. The first couple were various hidden quests related to boss fights, neither of which yielded substantial rewards. The ones that came after, however, were quite surprising.

System Warning

You are absorbing essence with an opposing nature to your physical being. If you proceed, physical and spiritual damage may occur.

Jade frowned, swiping the message away. Maybe setting most messages to auto-hide during combat had certain downsides. She resolved to fiddle around with the system options later.

The next few notifications were similar, increasingly urgent warnings about the physical damage she was inflicting upon herself. Since she was, well, not dead, Jade could only assume that the life force she absorbed from Seraphine had largely negated the bodily harm, but she could very much feel the aftereffects of the experience. Her body still felt hot and a little achy, like she was running a low fever. Fortunately, it was slowly improving. Jade's vision was finally mostly back to normal, and the last thing she needed was another lingering malediction. She scrolled to the very end of the list, wondering if there would be any indication of how long recovery would take. What she found was not nothing like she'd expected.

System Notice

You have absorbed a significant amount of incandescent essence through spell "Kiss of Night". This essence has been incorporated into your being. Your physical aspect is incompatible with incandescent essence and has rejected it. Your spiritual aspect is compatible with incandescent essence. Your spiritual aspect, base: human, has been infused with new power.

System Notice

Your body's rejection of new essence has caused it to sustain damage. You have lost four points of fortitude.

Jade grimaced. At least that explained the change to her stats. It wasn't one of her primary scores, so the loss of that much at once had a big impact. Fortunately, draining Seraphine had triggered her spell's stat drain feature, adding to her willpower. It didn't fully offset the loss, but it would let her focus more on fortitude in the coming levels. There was one final window waiting for her, and Jade pulled it up.

System Notice

Your spirit's acceptance of new essence has altered your aura. Aura manifestations now include an incandescent aspect.

"Incandescent aspect?"

Jade didn't know what that meant, but it sounded important. The morning flight was starting to seriously cut into her mana reserves, especially now that the sky was brightening with the first rays of sunlight. She descended, landing amidst a cluster of trees at the top of one of the hills bordering their campsite. With the last of the notifications out of the way, she turned her attention to her aura. Something had felt… not quite off about it, but still… there was a difference. A subtle heat crackled around her, like sparks on kindling. Subdued, but ready to ignite.

Maybe if…

Jade concentrated, carefully shaping her personal mana shroud into a small blade projecting from her fist. It wouldn't be effective as a weapon, she needed her daggers or claws to form a base for an aura construct to withstand any real force, but it was good enough for a simple experiment. The construct flickered to life, her mana concentrating until it gained physical form as a faint, crackling blade, shimmering and vibrant. Only this time, it was different. There was a golden tint to the manifestation now, a radiance that hadn't been there before. Light shone from the mana blades, bright enough to illuminate the ground around her. She felt an instinctive aversion to the incandescent energy, and something about manifesting it felt wrong. But those feelings were fleeting. There was a warmth to the power, a purity. And the more Jade studied it, the more she came to accept it.

"Well, that's new." Jade's concentration broke, the blade puffing away into glimmering embers. She turned to see Luis picking his way through the trees. He gave her a cheerful wave, and she returned it with a smile.

"Just testing a few things out. I think I, uh, absorbed a little more of that angel than I meant to.""Was that the same kind of power just now? The stuff you're weak to?"

"Incandescent. And yeah, it was. Apparently, I can conjure it up myself, now."

"So, you'll be stronger against demons. That'll come in handy."

Jade blinked once, then looked back at her hands.

"That's right…" She grinned, balling her hands into fists. Sparks crackled between her fingers, golden motes of shining light swirling around them. It was an impressive effect. At least, until they started burning her hands.

"Ouch!" Jade winced, dispersing her aura again. "It still stings like nothing else…"

It was odd. She would have thought this kind of power impossible for someone like her to channel. Seraphine's radiant attacks had ravaged her, inflicting searing pain that felt like it harmed her on a deeper level than other kinds of attacks. The system had said something about her physical and spiritual aspects, though. Her body was demonic, but the essence had latched onto her soul. Was her spirit still human, then? What did that even mean?

"Gonna have to be careful with that, chica." Luis said, wincing in sympathy. There was a sword slung across his back. He shrugged it off, drawing it from its silver sheath. "And with me, until I get the hang of this."Luis held up Seraphine's silver sword. Elegant runes ran along the length of the blade, which gleamed with a mirror-like sheen. He gave it a quick test swing, and the sigils lit up, faint threads of golden light trailing behind the blade to leave twisting patterns in the air around him.

"You can use her sword?" Jade asked, surprised. "I assumed it would have some crazy restrictions.""I thought so too, but I just meet the strength requirement." Luis said with a grin. He stepped through the web of golden energy, the threads phasing through him with no effect. "They don't affect me, but I can't make them safe for anyone else. Part of the weapon bond."

"That's awesome." Jade said, even as she took a half step back. She knew from painful experience what brushing against those threads was like. "Forgive me if I keep my distance, though. Until you know what you're doing with it."

"No worries." Luis laughed. "It'll take some getting used to, but this will be a great way to lock down some of those faster monsters.""And other demons.""That too." Luis's smile faltered, and he looked away. "It's like the Labyrinth is preparing us to fight them. Like it knows we'll face them again."

"And that's a bad thing?"

"I just wish we didn't have to. It feels like the rules of the game keep changing."

Jade didn't know what to say to that. Luis was the rock of their team, a source of eternal optimism and support. But all of them struggled under the mounting pressure, Jade knew that better than anyone else. It only grew each day, the stakes getting higher as the battles grew more difficult. She wasn't used to seeing him express it, though. Luis sighed, shoulders drooping as he planted the sword between his feet."We'll find a way through though, yeah? Just gotta keep going. One step at a time.""That's right. And remember." Jade touched his arm, careful to avoid the fading threads of light. "We aren't the only ones fighting."

That got a smile out of him.

"Nope. We're just the best."
