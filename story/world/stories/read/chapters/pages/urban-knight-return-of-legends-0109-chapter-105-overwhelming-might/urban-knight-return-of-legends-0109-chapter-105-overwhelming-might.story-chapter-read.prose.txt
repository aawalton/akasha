“Hmm…” Ascalon muttered with a thoughtful expression, scratching his chin slightly as he plunged down the chasm, his figure darting down, straight like an arrow. The wind roared past his ears, his clothes furiously flapping around—but his mind was already racing with possible solutions to his current predicament.

Unfortunately, he had been blown away quite a considerable distance from the rocky mountains, so he had no foothold whatsoever to regain his balance mid-air. But even so, he wasn’t worried—he could feel Pride’s presence plummeting down alongside him, on the opposite side of the peaks, meaning his friends were safe for now.

As he fell, he thought about possible ways to fight back against Pride’s skill. They were now, apparently, at the same level—evenly-matched so to speak—and their latest clash had made it clear. From experience, he knew these kinds of fights either ended in a flash or dragged on until one of the combatants could no longer stand… and somehow, he could wager they would end up experiencing the latter.

For a moment, old memories of similar battles flashed through his mind—confrontations where the slightest advantage, or mistake, could make all the difference. But he had a few trump cards to rely on if needed, so he remained calm and confident—even amidst his unstoppable descent.

“But still… how high up were we?” Ascalon pondered aloud, his voice barely audible over the raging wind. His frame sliced through the air, the peaks above blurring in the distance. Minutes passed, yet the abyss below seemed no closer than the heights above, his patience being tested by what seemed like an endless fall.

But since Ascalon couldn’t do anything to change his current predicament, he took the chance to free his mind of useless thoughts, sharpening his focus. With his friends far away from the battle, he could now concentrate on what he did best: rampage across the battlefield! He had to give it his very all, as his opponent was no mere bump in the road.

Eventually, the chasm below transformed from a distant shadow into a verdant expanse of trees and dense vegetation. The view should’ve brought him a sense of relief, since the ground was finally within reach, but instead, it only served to further steel his focus, as his senses zoned in on Pride’s presence.

Ascalon could sense her readiness from a distance, her aura expanding as she braced for the impact. The final stretch was but a few seconds long, yet in their minds it was seemingly endless.

And then, it happened. Ascalon’s boots struck the ground with relentless force, the immediate area erupting beneath him under the strain of a shockwave of immense power. As he bent his legs to better absorb the force of the fall, he could clearly feel a crater expanding all around him, the sheer force of his descent nothing but raw destruction.

The very earth trembled as if it were quaking, with a similar tremor echoing from the other side of the mountain—Pride had likely hit the ground as well. While debris flew furiously in all directions and curtains of dust surged upward, Ascalon wasted no time, springing into action.

Leaning forward, he burst into a dash, his right arm snapping back as he readied Exemplaris. Light descended upon him, a most radiant luminance enveloping his form, the entire surroundings bathing in pure, almost undeserving bliss. In the next moment, he pierced ahead, striking at the mountain from a distance.

A ray of white, incandescent fury erupted from Exemplaris, a force so strong it decimated anything and everything on its path, the greenery succumbing to Ascalon’s might. The attack struck the rocky walls with unyielding momentum, drilling into the mountain and piercing clean through.

The cataclysmic attack left a huge tunnel in its wake, and Ascalon wasted not a single breath before darting into the newly formed darkness. His figure blurred as his speed increased, chunks of rock and even water cascading around him—perhaps from a hidden lake or deposit, though Ascalon paid it no mind as he raced through.

In what felt like a mere second, he reached the other side, the aftermath of his attack greeting him in the form of a long, straight crater stretching far into the distance. With the surviving trees lined up neatly along its edges, it almost resembled a pathway! That aside, he lunged at Pride without hesitation, who advanced relentlessly toward him.

“You… saved us some time with that!” She exclaimed in a fierce voice, her figure jumping high as the clicks and whirs of her gauntlets started to echo around them. Though she appeared unfazed, Ascalon could feel some hesitation behind her words—his show of strength had not gone unnoticed.

Still, since she had the same levels and stats as him at the moment, she was equally capable of such feats—he had to be cautious to not give her any funny ideas! As he vanished the thought, he drew Exemplaris back, promptly striking upward and unleashing a devastating piercing strike.

At the same time, Pride plummeted from above, her red-glowing gauntlets guiding her descent with unstoppable momentum. The two attacks collided once more, the force of their impact causing the ground beneath Ascalon to crack.

Just as before, the ensuing shockwave sent them both back—Pride soared through the air, twisting her body to land gracefully a good distance away, while Ascalon skidded across the ground, his boots carving a deep trail.

They sized each other up for a moment, with both tensing up the muscles on their legs, the same idea flashing across their minds. In the next second, they pounced at each other, her fists and his lance slicing through the air as they unleashed a barrage of punches and piercing strikes, nearly mirroring each other’s movements.

Their bodies twisted and rotated as they attacked, defended, and dodged in perfect harmony, their weapons unleashing continuous shockwaves and cutting winds that roared around them. Sweat rolled down their cheeks, their teeth chattered as they clenched them tight, and whirring and clicking filled the air.

The remnants of their strikes reached far into the distance, with trees and vegetation being completely obliterated all around them, the entire area suffering under their unstoppable onslaught. It was a cataclysmic event exchange, as the very earth trembled beneath their feet, as if groaning in distress.

Ascalon and Pride remained engrossed in their fight for what seemed like an eternity, with neither of them relenting their offensive. Ascalon pierced continuously with Exemplaris, adding horizontal or diagonal slams whenever possible, while his left hand parried and blocked incoming blows.

Pride punched as if her life depended on it, her gauntlets whirring with a grating sound, glowing in crimson incandescence. She made sure to put all of her strength behind each hit, which were further powered by the magically enhanced properties of her mechanical weapon, steam exuding from the gloves as sparks crackled in the air.

They were so focused on the fight that no words came out of their lips—other than grunts or quick bursts of breath—and the stalemate was more than evident. It seemed as though the fight had no end in sight—yet the knight still had a few tricks under his sleeve, a confident smile adorning his face as he met Pride’s gaze.

Ascalon’s resolve burned fiercely in his eye, and Pride twitched, as if sensing the ultimatum he was conveying. In the next moment, Ascalon’s body began to glow bright, a yellow luminance tracing the contours of his frame. Pride opened her eyes wide at the sight, clearly confused and—perhaps—scared.

“Wha—?” Pride muttered amidst her unrelenting barrage of punches, while Ascalon’s presence grew, his light all-encompassing. As his body was bathed in blissful blessings, his might surged dramatically, the force and speed behind his attacks increasing with each passing moment.

Gradually, he began to overwhelm her. Bruises and gashes started to appear on Pride’s body—her steel-like skin being completely disregarded. Her furious offensive quickly switched to more defensive motions as her senses grew wary of the knight’s growing power.

“How!?” She blurted out in a panicked voice, her widened eyes and clenched teeth revealing her confusion and agitation.

“I—I should… be as… strong…!” She kept talking to herself, as if speaking the words would help her make more sense of the situation. Her face began to blush with a fierce crimson, a mix of embarrassment and exhaustion taking over her expression as beads of sweat dripped down her body.

"Hmph!” Ascalon exhaled sharply, stomping hard on the ground as he lunged forward, his attack carrying the weight of his whole body behind it. Exemplaris flashed bright with the light of the heavens, a wave of purity expanding around them as it cut through the air.

Pride could do nothing but raise her usual cross guard, hiding behind her steam-powered gauntlets and tensing her legs tightly, bracing for the impact. Ascalon’s holy-infused spear struck true, an explosive shockwave erupting violently and cracking Pride’s gauntlets, ultimately sending her hurling away, pitifully.

The entire area trembled as the spear’s aura surged in a barrage of light, engulfing Pride’s entire frame in a ray of divine energy. The force traveled unimpeded, devastating the landscape—trees, rocks, and the very ground exploded, all sent flying in every direction.

Ascalon sighed as he whipped his spear to the side, severing the connection between him and the radiant light, the remnants of his attack fading far into the distance. Admiring the wreckage in his wake he felt a bit embarrassed, but his attention quickly shifted to Pride, who was rising weakly from the ground.

“I—impossible… me… d—defeated?” Pride whimpered feebly, her eyes tearing up.

Steam rose from her body, scorch marks and dried blood covering much of her now bare skin, as she was as naked as the day she had been born—presumably. Ascalon let out a prideful ‘hmph’, thinking she ought to be grateful for her skill—after all, mirroring his stats had saved her from certain, righteous death!

“Damn it…! Damn it…! Fuck!!!” Pride fell to her knees, screaming her lungs out. Her battered, almost disintegrated gauntlets clung to her skin, likely melted shut. As if throwing a tantrum, she began to slam the ground with both her fists, blood splashing all over her as she continued the motion for various seconds.

“If it weren’t… for this body… for this stupid horn…!” She complained to herself, anger consuming her entire expression. She grabbed her small horn, pulling on it as she winced in distress.

Ascalon stood impassively, watching over her with his spear at the ready. As far as he recalled, ogres were a prideful, strong, and unreasonable race. Being a woman, with a smaller build and a short horn, she was likely labeled as weaker than the other ogres. Perhaps, all of that combined was the root of her frustration—or something along those lines, Ascalon thought.

Yet before Pride could take her anger out on her own body, a familiar voice echoed through the area, startling both Ascalon and her.

“I believe that’s enough, Pride.” Wrath spoke from the beyond with a firm voice, prompting Pride to lift her head, a vicious look taking over her visage.

“No!” She growled, showing her teeth in anger, her brows furrowed into a most displeased frown.

“As I said before, I can’t have you die here.” Wrath declared, and a shadowy, deep fog began to rise up around Pride, coiling and swirling around her. Ascalon readied himself, aligning Exemplaris toward Pride, prepared to stop her from fleeing—but her words made him stop for a second.

“This… this isn’t over yet… you just wait…!” She began, her words trembling with irritation, shame, and perhaps even a bit of sadness.

“Next time… I’ll don the mask… so wait for me, Lilithra!” Pride exclaimed in anguish, the shadows wrapping around her and ready to take her away.

“And you… I know how to defeat you now…!” She added with a maniacal glee in her voice, glancing over at Ascalon. At her words, he frowned and moved, but it was Wrath’s voice who stopped him this time.

“You should worry about your friends instead, radiant knight.” The man beyond the shroud warned, causing Ascalon to falter. In that brief moment of hesitation, Pride’s body was swallowed by the darkness, her presence completely vanishing from the area.

“Curses…!” Ascalon cussed at his own blunder, having let Pride escape from his grasp. But Wrath’s words had planted a seed of worry in him, as his friends could be in danger at that very moment!

Ascalon looked up at the distant peaks, regret assailing his mind as he tried to come up with a way to go back to his companions quickly. Just then, a blue window appeared before him, signaling a countdown: one minute left before the dungeon closed.

His fight had dragged on far too long, and that single minute would feel like an eternity in his current state of mind. Therefore, he decided to take a deep breath and try to calm himself down.

There was nothing he could do in such a short amount of time, so he had to be patient, and hope his friends were okay when they all reunited back in New Lumingard.

After the countdown finished, he sighed in relief, his world twisting and warping as he was pulled out of the Howling Chasm by a mysterious force—much like the dungeon escape skill he had experienced before.

As the surroundings faded, amidst the aftermath of his rampage, he caught sight of a familiar monument far in the distance… though perhaps it was just a trick of the light.
