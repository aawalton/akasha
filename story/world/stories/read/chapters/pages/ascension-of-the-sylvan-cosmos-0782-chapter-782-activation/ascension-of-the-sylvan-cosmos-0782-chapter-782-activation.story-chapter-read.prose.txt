Setting up <Eternity Of Stars> turned out to be much more complicated than she first imagined. While the theoretical and most of the practical experiments had gone well, when it was time to activate all nodes, things became complicated.

Due to the changes to Corruption Of Stars, which was initially created to take over, and was now being changed to reinforce, some small connections interfered with the activation.

Moreover, the amount of essence needed to keep it running was not low. ‘Thankfully, I gained my baby’s <Mortal-Growth Blessing>, increasing my Aether and Anima regeneration by converting my Phoer regeneration.

“But…” Liu Shu was somewhat confused. “The first effect of this blessing, how will it affect me?”

She had not used it because she was someone whose lifespan was supposed to be inexhaustible. Therefore, using the active effect, which could sacrifice part of it for a risky and last-chance burst, became a touchy thing Liu Shu didn’t dare experiment with.

‘What if I start consuming, and my lifespan changes back to not being endless? My lifespan being endless might be a delicate balance that can be disrupted with self-harm methods…’

So, Liu Shu was really reluctant to use that blessing unless absolutely necessary. ‘There are three outcomes when I use it. One, I lose my endless lifetime because of some imbalance. Second, nothing happens, or negligible effects occur due to the title being unable to consume my lifespan. Third… I can use it without the risk involved with it and gain a permanent buff.’

Shaking her head, Liu Shu decided not to bother for now and throw that effect to the back of her head.

“Anyway… Little Fatebrushing, I am counting on you, darling.”

After muttering that, Liu Shu used the active skill of <Fatebrushing Blessing> once more today, and then, she tried to activate <Eternity Of Stars> once again.

Since she had plenty of time, Liu Shu had been building nodes until they reached 999 of them. Among those 99 of them were core nodes, such as those Liu Shu destroyed during the war. The rest were just repetition nodes.

This was also the weakest form of <Eternity Of Stars>, with an effect of S- Rank Rune Array strength.

‘The blueprints I created are upgradable, so I'd rather get it going first and then expand.’

Her mind soon focused entirely on it, her essence flowing through her roots and pouring into the nodes. She was basically using herself as the starting link before leaving the rune to gather energy by itself.

To support a Solar System-wide Rune Array, Liu Shu’s essence reserves were sorely lacking. Even as a World Tree, her few dozen <Ternary Essence Stars> could not affect such a wide range for a long time.

During the war, she used her entire energy just to create one “radar” like wave. It was a single pulse to help her detect oddly moving things. Something incredibly crude when compared to what <Eternity Of Stars> could truly do.

So, what was Liu Shu’s solution? Focus the array range.

Liu Shu, instead of trying to cover everything, moved all 999 nodes near the currently most important area: The sun.

The sun in the Ceperet Star System was by no means a large star, but it was a star nonetheless. The diameter of the Sun was well over one million kilometers. An area of that magnitude surpassed Liu Shu’s largest attack range by at least two or three orders of magnitude.

Still, the nodes were not there as decorations. Their most important effect was, without a doubt, their ability to draw essence from the cosmos and become self-sustainable.

Otherwise, this type of massively large array would be impossible to maintain.

As Liu Shu felt the usual resistance, the <Fatebrushing Blessing> main effect finally activated. Liu Shu’s focus, because of a strange flicker, moved toward a section of the node rune array.

“Hm?”

Focusing on that strangely odd section, she noticed a big problem. “...No wonder. The underlying array system in this area was blocked. It is a small section, but it clogs the flow, making the self-sustainability impossible.”

A sigh of relief escaped Liu Shu. While she was a bit frustrated that such a mistake had escaped her, she was glad it was an oversight rather than a flaw in her theories.

Her roots extended to that section, space around them opening as materials poured out of the <Lignified Vault>. “...It’s quite expensive, but well. I’ve already poured enough materials to build 10 Golden Sap Cities, so a little bit more is not a problem.”
