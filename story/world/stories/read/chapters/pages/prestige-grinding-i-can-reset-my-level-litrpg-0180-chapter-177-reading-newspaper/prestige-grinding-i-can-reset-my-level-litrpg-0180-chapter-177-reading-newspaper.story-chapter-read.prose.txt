After an hour of catching up with his parents, Sora began to feel lighter. The weight on his chest had eased, replaced by the warmth of being with family.

Henri leaned back in his sofa, swirling a cup of coffee in his armchair. “Despite what you think about yourself, the world sees you in a different light now. You’re the Beacon of Hope, after all.”

Sora’s eyebrows shot up at the mention of the nickname. “Alius said the same thing to me before… What does that even mean? How would people know me? I’ve never done anything worth remembering or stood out in public.”

Kara shook her head, ready to counter his words, but Marie spoke first. “We didn’t know at first either. But shortly after you disappeared into the tower, we started getting newspapers daily, filled with all sorts of secrets and articles about players. That’s when we started seeing your name mentioned.”

Sora was surprised, 'In those players' memories, there was nothing about me... It's true they weren't the kind of players who interacted a lot with others, but why would they miss so much information about the tower?'

Sora frowned, 'And why would my parents suddenly start receiving mail with newspapers with classified information? Did the group or person who posted players in front of the house do this?'

Marie’s proud smile made Sora pause, piquing his curiosity.

Henri chimed in. “One of the papers explained that the player who performed the best inside the tower would earn a reward so valuable, it could change the fate of humanity. Something so valuable that it would benefit countless people.”

Kara looked surprised. She had discussed the tower rewards with Sora, and was surprised others had hints about them. “How would they know about that?” she asked.

Henri shrugged. “Do you remember the player who hit level 50 first and triggered the first World Update ? He's Roméo—also known as Xgamer170— and he leaked the information. He claims whoever reaches the top of the tower will be humanity’s Beacon of Hope. People latched onto it.”

“Roméo...” Sora murmured, remembering the name . “Did he ever make it to the top of the tower?”

Henri shook his head. “We couldn’t find any confirmation about that. We’ve been devouring every newspaper and article published over the last few months. But while his own achievements are vague, there’s testimony from other players about what’s happening inside the tower.”

Marie leaned forward, her smile widening. “Your name came up everywhere.” She glanced at Kara, adding with a chuckle, “And yours too! The two of you were constantly mentioned as part of the ‘monster rookies.’”

Sora’s curiosity was piqued. “I need to see these papers. Where are they?”

Marie pointed toward the bookshelf. “Go on, have a look.”

On the bookshelf, more than a hundred papers were stacked. He could notice that a few dozens were marked with colors.

Henri explained, "The ones with blue bookmarks mention you, in a positive light. The ones in purple mention you in a bad way. In yellow, they mention Kara. In red ..."

Sora understood the meaning behind her silence, "Rachel?"

Marie nodded.

He chose one that had a both yellow and blue bookmarks, and started reading it.
