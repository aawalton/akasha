A few weeks had passed since the combined military force set up shop in what had once been a sleepy little hamlet.

Mind-numbing daily strategy meetings had given way to bi-weekly meetings, which from Adon’s perspective were broken up by fun sparring sessions with Goldie.

The gathered men and women, preparing to take their homeland back, were eating the countryside bare, but it was happening slowly. Training advanced more quickly than the new recruits stripped their homeland of food. Hope had blossomed in the people’s hearts. They were growing stronger, under the Queen’s leadership and with the Parmonians’ help.

The Empire had not tried to advance much beyond the capital. Perhaps they already knew what awaited them beyond the city of Wayn if they sought to invade further. The Parmonians and Claustrians were capable of giving them what would undoubtedly be the stiffest resistance the Demon Army had yet faced since they began their incursion.

So the informal stalemate simmered quietly on, the Kingdom gathering its strength for a push to reconquer its lost territory.

If things continued on at their present trajectory, the growing sentiment among the gathering survivors was that the people of Claustria would manage to take back their homeland with their own hands.

Yes, there would be help from the Parmonians and perhaps the Dessians, but the key effort and the core of the strength to retake the country would come from within.

Such was the cloud of good feelings that Adon moved through whenever he approached the fortress town from a morning of hunting.

And the butterfly was in an optimistic frame of mind, too.

Weeks of slowly hunting and training with Goldie had not yielded the same quick and dramatic growth in strength that Adon had experienced in the garden, either in terms of shifts in his fighting style or in Evolution Points spiking dramatically.

Yet training with Goldie did uncover some of his weak points, and fighting and killing the strongest creatures in the forest had simultaneously grown his reserves of Evolution Points.

His unique fighting style as a transforming creature with magic had developed bit by bit.

His status had grown to the point where he was certain he would now be a threat against any human and probably any demon in combat.

He had ground up his base physical stats to a point that seemed unrealistic despite his basic physical form being a butterfly.

I should be able to lift Rosslyn naturally now, even into midair, without my wings breaking. I’m not sure about the physics of that, but my wings seem capable of creating gusts of wind that are stronger than should be physically possible. Maybe it’s better not to question it.

Most importantly, his mana reserve was night and day compared with what it had been.

As a butterfly, strength was frankly still his dump stat, but it had doubled from back when he had checked it in the dungeon. Everything else had made similarly meaningful strides forward.

Today, he had finally gathered enough Evolution Points to buy something he recognized was probably important.

Something that had been out of reach back in the dungeon, last time he considered it. Something that might have made all the difference when he and the others were fighting monsters—or perhaps even afterward, when he and Rosslyn emerged out into the Demon Empire’s invasion.

Maybe I could have saved Samson, Adon thought. It was almost certainly too late to save Rosslyn’s father by the time we left the dungeon, but my brother?

It was impossible to be certain, but then, it was also hard to be filled with a sense of regret. He had continually chosen Skills and Adaptations that he could actually use throughout his dungeon experience, and he had never chosen anything that had turned out to be unnecessary. The peak of his Transformation Adaptation had been absolutely necessary to escape the dungeon with Rosslyn.

Now, let’s see what this Skill actually does.

Butterfly Effect: For an instant, take in the universe of possibilities surrounding a situation within a defined area. Consider every proximate factor that affects the outcome of the event, and see the paths that can be taken to alter events using your own power and influence. Becomes less effective if the defined area is too large.

Yeah, it’s as important as I imagined, he thought a little gloomily. He refused to let himself wonder if there was a way he could have acquired it back in the dungeon, if only he had been a little more patient and accrued more Evolution Points. That way lay madness.

Although I guess the road not taken is going to be my whole universe now, since I’ll be able to figure out exactly what will and won’t help me in a given situation. The Butterfly Effect is literally a see-the-future-and-change-it Skill.

Adon chose the Skill without further hesitation. Its benefits were too obvious for him to doubt the decision.

He felt a pounding pain inside his head that lasted for literally two seconds, and then everything was fine.

I guess the way it works, I just learned how to use it, and it maybe made a tiny modification, but I already had most of the hardware inside?

That was the easiest way for Adon to make sense of how brief and relatively easy that had been. It was night and day compared with when he had acquired and upgraded Telepathy or Magic Perception.

From brain surgery with a hammer to just a little twinge of pain.

“You got something good?” Goldie asked.

They were still out in the forest, and the two had each taken a minute to review his Evolution Store options. Goldie had remained in her human form for this, while Adon reverted back. The spider seemed increasingly fond of using Transformation for prolonged periods as she grew more proficient at it.

Perhaps it signaled a deeper desire to leave the life of a spider behind.

Yes, Adon sent. The Butterfly Effect. It’s a skill that took a lot of Evolution Points to get, but it should let me… change the future.

“Explain?”

He did, as best as he could.

“Adon, this ability reminds me of all the legends people refer to about your predecessors!” Goldie said excitedly. “A butterfly changes the whole fate of these people, and they can’t easily explain how or why it was possible. But it happens over and over. So they just chalk it up to miracles and say the butterflies were sent by the Goddess, and that must explain it. It’s not just that, though. You were actually sent by the Goddess, but you still couldn’t make huge changes to the destiny of Claustria. Even though you tried. Goddess knows you tried…”

You think… you think this was the piece I was missing? Adon asked. He hadn’t thought about the new skill that way. By all means, it seemed valuable, but he had never imagined it would be the key to becoming the kind of mystic beast that legends were made of. If he thought about it that way, the weight of the skill felt immense.

“I do,” the spider replied. “But I think you need to test it out. Otherwise you’re just going to doubt yourself. It’s important to check things like this when it doesn’t matter, right?”

Adon transformed into his humanoid form and nodded.

This felt like an invitation to spar again, not even waiting for the afternoon. They had the perfect reason, after all.

Testing out a new skill.

“Let’s go.”

They squared off.

As Goldie took up her fighting stance, Adon activated the Butterfly Effect immediately. He felt his mind accelerate as if it was under the influence of mental magic.

He saw a world separate from the real one, a world with a slightly warped coloration compared with the real world. It existed beside the world where Goldie faced off with him. He saw her move in the second world, while she was standing still in the real world.

She attacked with acidic venom and transforming claws and invisible silk that he could somehow sense, all in the world that Adon’s mind had invented—in the future he predicted?

There was more than just a premonition here, though. His vision subtly showed him places of tension. That was the best way to describe how they initially felt. Places of tension, points where he could apply pressure, or weak spots.

His power was telling him how to win the fight. Multiple options unfolded in his imagination as he explored each pressure point.

Then Goldie started to move. The vision ended, a wave of mental fatigue struck—but Adon remembered the core of what he had seen, in particular the path he had chosen to adopt.

As she stepped in, arms transforming into claws, a ball of acidic venom forming inside her open mouth, Adon moved forward at the same time. Mana rushed into his left hand, and he threw a single palm strike at her collarbone.

Goldie’s own momentum carried her forward into the blow. She made a retching sound as the acidic venom burst from her mouth onto her arms and chest.

Adon stepped back as the spider staggered forward, using her claw arms like crutches to keep from falling face first onto the ground.

It took her several minutes to stop coughing and heal from the self-inflicted damage she had done with her acidic venom.

By that time, the two were sitting beside each other on the forest floor, knees folded in front of them.

“All right, that was good,” Goldie said. “Was that your Butterfly Effect, or have you just been going easy on me up until now?”

“It was the new skill,” Adon replied, snorting. “You know it was.”

“I wanted to be sure. Adon, you recognize how much more efficient that made you? How much deadlier?”

He nodded, his face conflicted.

“If I use it, that will make any sparring matches in the future meaningless,” he said after a moment.

“Forget about that! If that skill scales, you could change the outcome of a battle easily. As long as the force on our side has anything close to parity with the other side, it should be impossible to lose, right?”

“I guess so. I hope so. I… I guess we need to run more experiments.”

He sensed that he could do it again that day if he needed to, though he didn’t know what backlash there might be inside his brain.

But he didn’t know how things would change if he had to simulate the future of something as complex as a battle with thousands of people on each side. That sounded like a much more demanding task.

The mystic beasts began walking back to the fort town, still discussing how they might make productive use of Adon’s new skill, but speaking in a hushed tone as they drew closer. They both understood that this was a secret that would have national security implications.

They entered, and following their usual custom, they presented the game they had caught to the guards on duty.

Then they moved across the fort town. Another meeting was scheduled, naturally. Adon had reverted to butterfly form, anticipating that he would be in human form for hours again.

It was while he was positioned on Goldie’s shoulder that they heard a yell.

“We have new arrivals!” came the shout from the two guards nearest the entrance.

They sounded excited, and they felt excited as Adon read their emotions through Telepathy. Maybe just a little too excited?

Adon instantly reverted to butterfly form and shot across the fort back to the entrance.
