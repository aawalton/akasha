Charlemagne’s eyes snapped back to their usual position as consciousness slowly began to return to his body.  He had fallen face down, but the icy projectile sticking out of his chest had hit the ground first, causing him to flop over onto his right side.  The fall had also driven the icy spear further through his chest, causing further damage to his already punctured heart and lungs.  But that didn’t matter to the plucky young rooster.  Ignoring the spreading numbness that was threatening to rob him of all feeling, Charlemagne pushed himself upright using his right wing.

Zenrage activated.

Charlemagne focused on the advice Grimfalk had given him, burning with determination to live up to the expectations of his Patron.  Unthinkable amounts of heat surged through his body, originating from the Ember Core smoldering in the rooster’s chest.  Sensation began to return to Charlemagne’s damaged tissues as his internal temperature rose, allowing him to feel the stabbing pain that came from being impaled by a spear made of magical ice.  He used the spreading pain as a focus, pushing his anger to greater heights as he pulled in the ambient mana and made its power his own.

The Empress’s eyes widened as she sensed the rooster’s power waxing, but, instead of retreating from the inferno, she smiled.

“Oh, you’ve still got some fight in you, eh?  I was worried that I’d accidentally killed you there.  Sorry about that, but no harm, no foul, right?  Well, if you survived that, then I’ll have to improvise a little here!”

The ice within Charlemagne’s body began to twist as it sent out icy tendrils into the rooster’s circulatory system.  The rooster nearly fainted as his blood pressure dropped precipitously, but he clung to consciousness by embracing the fiery mana that still freely flowed throughout his body.  His temperature continued to rise, but the ice didn’t seem to be affected at all.  If he didn’t do something soon, he’d collapse.

His mind, already foggy from his injuries, began to slow as he desperately sought a solution.  The spear had almost entirely changed shape as it continued to invade his body, wreaking havoc on his internal organs and causing unspeakable agony.  His eyes slowly began to roll back once again as his vision faded to black, leaving nothing but his extrasensory perception active.

That was the only thing that allowed him to see the solution.

Reaching inward, Charlemagne ripped into the ice’s structure, not with his claws or beak, but with his mana.  He tore apart the mana that not only animated the icy projectile but also sustained it.  The ice-aspected mana fought against his Ember Core, lowering the conversion percentage to the low single digits, but that wasn’t his goal.  Melting the ice was.  Without mana, the magical ice was simply water in solid form.  It had no defense against the level of heat that Charlemagne was putting out.

The rooster crowed in triumph as the last of the ice melted, allowing his potent regenerative powers to close the massive hole in his chest as well as repair the extensive damage to his cells that stemmed from half of his body being frozen solid.  Hunger began to gnaw at his stomach as his digestive tract absorbed the magical metal, Endurian, somehow extracting nourishment from the process, but not enough to fully power his regeneration.  It was here that Inferno’s Embrace proved its worth again, allowing the rooster to power through despite the remaining damage to his heart and lungs.

“So, you figured out the trick to handling my ice constructs.  But I almost had you there, didn’t I?” the Empress boasted.

Charlemagne glowered at his opponent and began gathering in more and more mana, charging his ultimate attack.  He didn’t think that she would survive the blast, but he also didn’t care.  She had wounded him, and she must pay.  The blazing heat surrounding him once again swelled to new heights, melting not only the surrounding snow but also the rocks beneath his feet.  The lava felt good on his chilled claws.  His wickedly curved beak extended further as the attack began to form just in front of his wide-open mouth.

A huge ball of blue and white energy grew from a single point of searing light as Charlemagne put his all behind the blast.  The ache in his stomach grew as his body expended energy it couldn’t easily replace.  The ball formed faster than it ever had before as the rooster willed the mana construct to complete before the Empress had a chance to act.

“Wow…if you hit me with that head on, there’s no way I could survive it,” the Empress of the Forest said as a crazed smile spread across her face.  “It’s a good thing I can do this.”

A tiny but dense bit of environmental mana swirling next to the ball was suddenly ripped from Charlemagne’s control.  That seeming trifle led to a chain of events that happened far too quickly for the rooster to follow.  The mana was condensed even further, turning into a thin, sharp, needle-like shape.  It then pressed its point against the outer wall of Charlemagne’s mana ball attack, destroying itself almost instantly.  But it resisted just long enough to destabilize the ball, causing the swirling mana streams in the outer layers to snarl.

The situation went downhill from there.

A thundering detonation sent the Empress of the Forest flying backward through the air, the sheer acceleration causing microtears throughout her body and even putting stress on her Manifold Core.  Moments later, an enormous beam of energy sheared through her body, ruining her left arm and leg but missing her Core.  The damage, while problematic, was insufficient to disable the Empress.  Still being forced backward by the shockwave, she continued to tumble end over end for a while before managing to reassert control over her surroundings and righting herself, laughing and wincing as she did.

“Well, that ended with a bang,” she chuckled.  “I didn’t expect that to be so effective…he really packed a lot of mana in there.”

Phatagin and Josephine had retraced their steps back into the palace before wandering around the frozen hallways in search of an exit.  Phatagin turned to the Attendant and was about to say something when a chorus of howls shattered the near silence of the seemingly deserted structure, echoing through the icy corridors.

“Were any of those nearby?” Josephine whispered, looking slightly worried at the thought of confronting whatever beast had produced the blood-curdling cries.

“I think not,” the pangolin whispered back before adding, “I just attempted to contact Charlemagne, but my missive felt like it was somehow blocked.  What do you recommend we do?”

“Well, we can’t go very high up, remember those crazy defenses that almost shot us out of the air?  Why don’t you just break through the walls until we make it outside?  I want to see what’s going on with Ser Charlemagne,” the Attendant suggested.

The pangolin took a deep breath before nodding his assent.  His Momentum Core flared for a moment as he turned to face the nearest wall, hoping that he wasn’t about to head deeper into the palace.  Then he assumed his cannonball form and shot through the air, slamming into the icy stone with surprising velocity.  The wall collapsed, allowing Josephine to pass through after him.  Phatagin continued his best wrecking ball imitation until he finally burst through the palace’s thick outer wall with a gasp of effort before flopping to the ground, panting from the exertion.

“We…succeeded,” he gasped as he crawled to his feet.

Then an explosion rang out, and the pangolin was thrown back the way he came.  Josephine caught Phatagin, letting out a rather undignified “oof” as the impact knocked the air from her lungs.  The Attendant, realizing that the source of the explosion could only have been caused by the battle between the rooster and the Empress of the Forest, picked up the pace, racing out into the open while still holding Phatagin.

The air was hot and sticky, a testament to Charlemagne’s unbridled power.  The rooster himself was hot and sticky, a testament to the damage his own attack had done to him.  Thankfully, the majority of the energy had been directed outward at the last moment, or Charlemagne’s tenure in the Elite Systemic Games would have been at an end.

Phatagin stirred and began to pull away from Josephine, who allowed him to float under his own power.

“That’s…that’s gruesome,” she said to the pangolin.  “I am surprised he’s still alive.”

Phatagin uncurled and took a better look at the injured rooster.

“That is quite unfortunate indeed, but his powers of regeneration are formidable.  I imagine that he’ll have quite the appetite once this battle has concluded.  I doubt those rings, as magical as they may be, are sufficient sustenance.”

The pair watched as Charlemagne pulled himself off the ground and began to patch the many wounds that rent his body.  There was no visible blood, not because he wasn’t bleeding, but because the incredibly hot fluid immediately boiled away once it left his veins.  Still, the rooster was a hot mess.  His head and neck were the most affected, but great swaths of flesh had been gouged out of his torso and wings as well.  The only body parts that seemed unscathed were his scaly legs, which suddenly began moving.

Charlemagne was charging forward.

The Empress laughed as she descended toward the ground.  Snow rose to meet her, dancing around her as the frozen substance reshaped itself into a web of shrapnel.

“Isn’t she smaller than she used to be?” Josephine asked, squinting her eyes to get a good look.  “Yes, her mass has diminished by over thirty percent.”

“You have a Skill that can measure that precisely at a distance?” Phatagin asked, confused.

“How else could I be expected to know when one of my courtly rivals has gained weight?”

“Why ever would you care about such trifles?” the pangolin pressed, but Josephine shushed him as she refocused on the battle.

Charlemagne’s charge brought him within ten paces of the Empress when the ground underneath suddenly erupted.  The rooster, having sensed the flow of mana, hopped into the air and used his damaged wings to immediately descend again.  A mana shield snapped into place underneath his next footfall, blocking another earthen spear that had sought to pierce him from below.  He jumped.

The Empress of the Forest frowned as the rooster passed just out of range of the icy shards that were protecting her.  She quickly reoriented herself without losing control of a single projectile, confused by the failed attempt to get behind her.  Charlemagne, on his part, conjured another mana shield that he used to change his trajectory, this time passing the Empress on her other side.  She spun again, confused by the tactic.

The rooster continued creating more platforms in the air and using them to circle around the Empress until the area was filled with shimmering, almost completely transparent shields.  Then he began to close in earnest.

His opponent finally seemed to realize what was going on and concentrated all her defenses to the front while beginning to create another icy spear above her head.  Charlemagne never gave her a chance to finish.  The rooster bounced off one final mana shield and, engaging a Skill, rocketed forward with incredible acceleration.

“He once used that to fly to outer space,” the pangolin said to Josephine as they watched Charlemagne put on a final burst of speed.  He flew through the swarm of ice shards and, in an impressive feat of unarmed combat, drove his claw straight through her body, stopping just short of shattering her Core.  The shards circling the Empress fell to the ground as she looked at the rooster, pure shock written all over her face.

Phatagin strained his hearing to the utmost, but he was unable to hear the short conversation that followed.  Afterwards, Charlemagne released his foe and fell heavily to the ground, panting heavily as his wounds slowly healed.  The Empress of the Forest rose further into the air, and she raised her voice loud enough to be easily heard by the Squire and the Attendant.

“I have been defeated in a duel of honor, and my life is thus forfeit.  Unfortunately, my contract with the System takes preeminence over any oath I may take.  But, until such time as the System requires my continued service, I pledge my loyalty to Ser Charlemagne.”

The System, seemingly understanding that the Empress of the Forest had been defeated but not killed, acknowledged the rooster’s efforts.

“Bawk!” Charlemagne objected.
