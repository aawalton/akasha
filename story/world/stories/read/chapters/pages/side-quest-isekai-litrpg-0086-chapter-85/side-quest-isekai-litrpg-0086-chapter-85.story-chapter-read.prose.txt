Logan waited out of sight on the flat, tiled floor behind the pedestal. The ceremony was taking place beyond the pedestal and around a corner, but he could still hear the speaker’s words, amplified by some kind of magic.

Several gnomish symbols on the ground glowed with a bright, ultraviolet, almost white light. The light was only visible to Logan because he had Aetherlens activated, but even when he toggled the skill off, he still felt the warm buzz in the air.

Thanks to his chatting back and forth with Mariv and Avalyn, Logan now knew this flooring was a type of gate separating a huge source of raw, pure mana from the surface. As the master of ceremonies continued with his speech, each step in his address caused the mana to churn more, powering the tiles. If it wasn’t for the reassurances from his chat messages, he would be worried about being melted by some kind of nuclear blast.

He was more focused on repeating over and over the vow Razor had told him via Avalyn. The celebrant hadn’t yet reached the part of the ceremony that mattered for his purposes.

Mishki: Logan, you’re probably seeing more activity beneath the floor. That’s because Miriam just opened a different mana channel. There’s a map up here. Each channel looks like an empty riverbed that fills with colored water as it becomes active. It looks like three out of five channels beneath the site of power are active.

At Logan’s side, Nugget let out a bored chirp.

“Just a bit longer, buddy,” Logan said as he pulled out a non-magical dessert from his inventory.

For the moment, that was enough to satisfy Nugget.

Mariv: Avalyn, how is Razor progressing? Has she broken the barrier in ascending from level 100 to level 101?

Avalyn: Not yet. We have been running into some… challenges. She asked me not to go into detail with you.

Mariv: That’s entirely unfair. You can’t tell me that unless you plan on going into ALL the details.

Logan: Hey, they just got to the part about calling on the sages.

Mishki: And the fourth channel just activated.

The brilliant light shifted. Now all the obsidian sigils glowed, the light making the obsidian almost entirely translucent. The mana began to flow in on itself in a spiral current beneath the tiles.

Mariv: Really, I must protest. Why doesn’t she want me to know? Was there a monster she couldn’t overpower? She knows I wouldn’t judge her for a silly thing like that.

Avalyn: I don’t think strength is what she’s so embarrassed about.

Mariv: Embarrassed, you say? Hm. Yes, she wouldn’t be embarrassed about a feat of strength… I doubt she would have lost in a strength contest anyway. She only struggles with… Oh. Oh! It’s something she can’t conquer with sheer force, isn’t it? I think that makes my most recent vision make more sense.

Avalyn: I really have told you too much.

Logan: Guys? Did you see my message?

Avalyn: Yes. Razor says to wait until the channel opens and the gate becomes completely sheer. Once the light bathes you, make the oath.

Mariv: I bet it involves her HOLDING BACK on her strength, doesn’t it?

“Lastly, we call upon the power of the Cosmos itself! Let your wishes be made whole as the supplicants offer themselves as your vessels!”

Mariv: Did she tell you I had to teach her how to make a campfire that was small enough not to burn down a forest? She hated every moment of it. You should see her try to make small talk with passing shepherds. We had to make it through three different regions just to get it right. A sufficiently tame campfire, that is. The small talk still isn’t quite there.

Mishki: The fifth channel is filling up.

Mariv: Avalyn? Did you ask Razor about the campfire?

The glow of the floor swirled again, and the tiles shook lightly as they separated. Logan was standing on a circular tile at the very center, and all the other adjoining tiles parted slightly from his. A warm breeze drifted up from the tiles, and the air turned to a chromatic blend of pastels.

Aetherlens is level 32!Aetherlens is level 33!

Logan: Okay, it’s happening!

He set his palm on Nugget’s forehead and took a deep breath. He locked eyes with the golden dragon, and for the moment, they were all that existed.

“Flame to breath, soul to spark, I, Logan, pledge myself to the Balance through you. Let my will temper your fire, and your light temper my shadow. In the stillness between Chaos and Order, our bond shall burn everlasting. My life for yours, my purpose for our harmony.”

Nugget chirped, and his eyes softened. Something almost like words brushed against Logan’s awareness, split between being heard by his physical ears and inside his mind. The little dragon nuzzled his forehead firmer against Logan’s palm, and Logan pulled Nugget into an embrace.

A calming reassurance fell over them before the wind picked up, making Logan’s messy hair swirl upward like dark chocolate softserve.

Mariv: You should have seen her trying to write with a quill! Ha! Ha! Can you hear my laughing over this? Ha! She holds a pen like it’s a weapon.

Logan: We said the words! Uh, now what?

Avalyn: Razor says you should receive a prompt from the System any moment. The influx of mana might need to stabilize before your oaths settle in.

Mariv: Aha! So you ARE still in the chat. I was right, wasn’t I? It’s something too mundane for her. That’s why you won’t answer any further. Tell Razor I believe she can do it! Tell her not to give up, and to remember how good she ended up at giving advice to Liorna when the chickens weren’t laying eggs!

Logan received another message from Avalyn, but in a thread that included only her, him, and Mishki.

Avalyn: Remind me never to get caught up in sharing messages between those two.

Meanwhile, Mishki typed into the group chat.

Mishki: Oh, all the channels just mixed together. I think the big moment is coming your way, Logan!

That bump in the title had to be because of standing at the site of power, but Logan barely considered it as golden warmth filled him.

Nugget shone in the aftermath of the glowing light. The dragon flew up into a twirl around Logan, starting at his feet, doing an airborne loop above his head, and then settling on his shoulder, where the dragon nuzzled his snout into Logan’s messy hair. He could actually feel the dragon’s happiness, like a separate little chunk of emotion residing in his chest.

Logan laughed and patted Nugget’s side. “I know, bud. I’m happy too.”

According to Razor, Nugget would be able to sense Logan’s feelings directly also. This was different than Moodprint, though. They each would have a more intimate access to feelings, fears, and emotions, regardless of distance or mana. And it wasn’t limited to single emotions either. Right now, though, they were both just excited.

In the chat, Mariv was still trying to get Avalyn to share any juicy gossip of what menial tasks Razor surely needed to achieve to level up.

Logan: It worked!

Mariv: Congratulations! I am setting aside a rare fruit from Skydale, and we will juice it when you visit next. I have some more newt’s feet for Nugget as well.

Avalyn: Razor sends her love.

Mishki: Miriam just sent the order for the guards to close site of power’s gate. They should receive it in a minute, and then you will have thirty seconds to get back to the other side of the pedestal.

Logan: Got it.

Logan let out a satisfied breath, trying to bring himself back to reality. The wash of dimming light receded, starting from the center of the ornate tiled floor and fading out to the rim.

From the guards’ perspectives, Logan was no longer needed to retrieve the prism. Therefore, anybody could walk up to the pedestal to grab it. But if someone walked up there now, they would reach the high ground of the pedestal, and Logan had nowhere to hide from their line of sight. His only time advantage was that they would wait until the residual effects from the surge of raw power faded away.

Right now, Cassandra would be ready to intercept the guards, just in case Logan needed a few more seconds.

Logan, meanwhile, would use that time to switch to the Void affinity and Null Pulse his way to a ledge overlooking the site of power’s gate. Then he could scale down a narrow ledge, which would put him out into the main crowd. Senna, Alden, and Bromlin would already be at the path’s outlet onto the main road so Logan could blend right into the group.

Most of the light had faded except for a residual glint at the far side of the tiles, but it was dwindling. Logan tapped a void stone and accepted the System prompt to change affinities.

Mishki: Okay, it looks like all the mana channels have emptied. Logan, you should be able to cross back out.

Logan read the message but cocked his brow. That spot at the edge of the tiles still kept a small golden glow.

Logan: Are you sure about that? It looks like there’s still a trace amount of gold light.

Mariv: Gold? Raw mana shouldn’t be golden.

Avalyn: Razor is asking if it’s golden like Nugget.

Logan chewed his lip. It was Nugget’s exact hue. Was that going to affect their bond? He confirmed it via message.

There was a slight pause as Avalyn relayed the message, but that minor hesitation felt like it was dragging out forever because Logan also heard Cassandra’s voice. She was standing with the guard now, on the other side of the pedestal. That conversation was what would give Logan his thirty seconds to get out of here.

Avalyn: You should still be okay to leave the site of power without messing up the bond. The process is complete. That golden color should be related to yours and Nugget’s bond, not the mana channels. The barriers should be down, and you should be able to exit without dying.

There were too many shoulds in that message for Logan’s liking, but Mishki assured Logan not only were all the mana channels down, but the screen he had been tracking the gate-opening process with had just turned off also.

“Well, Nugget, according to the others, the barriers are down. That light just has to do with our bond.” He peered at the light, and then at the ledge he was supposed to Null Pulse toward.

If that light was a hint of something quest-related, what was it doing out here? They were supposed to be keeping a low profile, and Logan had already botched that up by meeting the master carpenter and the archmage. The last thing he needed was to draw attention to his presence in a restricted area.

Reluctant, Logan opened his cloak for Nugget to hop in and curl against him. They really needed to find a better way to transport Nugget. He was still small but had grown slightly, and hiding him like this probably would not work for too much longer.

But Nugget shook his head and hopped to the ground, spinning in a circle once. He ended with his snout facing the golden light. A brief wash of curiosity mixed with concern came over Logan through the bond.

Logan sighed. “Yeah, I’m wondering about that too.” Luck in this world didn’t mean that Logan could get through difficult things easily, but it did point him in directions that so far had all proven pretty important.

Logan: Mishki, can you try to stall?

Mishki: Me? I’m with Miriam.

Logan: Sorry, I gotta check this out.

Null Pulse!

Instead of navigating to the ledge path back to the main road, Logan covered the distance to the far side of the tiled circle. Cassandra would hopefully see that he hadn’t left yet. Between her and Mishki stalling for him, that might buy him and Nugget at least a few more seconds. Null Pulse was a fast way to move, and hopefully he could accept whatever quest awaited him, then make it back to the ledge, all still in time.

What Logan didn’t expect was for the light not to be on tiled platform itself.

When he reached the edge, a spike of rugged stone separated the lip of the big circle from a mountain’s slope. He hadn’t realized how high up they were.

Either way, the light was coming from on the slope.

And there, a giant prowler lay on its side, foaming at the mouth and panting heavily. Logan didn’t need Analyze to tell him it was suffering from extended exposure to the blight, but the label popped up automatically anyway: Black Prowler (Destabilizing).
