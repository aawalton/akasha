The city of Illysport was more frantic than ever. Jade navigated the crowded streets, pushing through the throngs of people clogging every road and alley. The flood of people seeking shelter in the major cities had dramatically increased in recent days. The Labyrinth's announcement had reached everybody, or at least everybody in this region, with predictable effect. Nobody wanted to be caught out alone or in their small towns when bands of rampaging monsters from myth might soon come calling. In times like these, people trusted in the defenses of the larger city-states.

Shouts came from up ahead. Jade stepped away, pressing against the wall of a street-side shop to make way for the column of armored figures marching down the road. The crowd parted before them, yielding before the grim determination of men and women marching to battle. Their weapons and armor gleamed with the shimmer of powerful enchantments, and a quick inspection told Jade that their levels ranged from the low to high twenties. Was that high enough? Maybe, if their equipment was of sufficient quality…

It really is harder for non-Wayfarers to gain levels…

She'd already known that, but this sight made it feel real. The enemies they would face were formidable. How many of them would be coming home?

We have to stop this.

The column marched on, disappearing into the crowd of refugees as Jade resumed her journey. She was alone, disguised as a human and wearing one of the newer outfits she'd picked out in Illysport. Her companions were busy making their own preparations for the journey to come, and it had fallen to Jade to gather some last-minute adventuring supplies. Of course, that meant visiting Ashar's new shop. A pang of regret flashed through her stomach as the familiar sign came into view. The young merchant had been instrumental in gathering some of the rarer materials for the restorative potion Jade had intended to give Alan. In the end, she'd been forced to use it to bring Naomi back from the brink of death. That pain had eased somewhat with the goddess's assurance that she would do what she could for her brother, but still…

Jade came to a stop just outside the shop. Light spilled through the window, just bright enough to make out in the shadow of evening. She caught a glimpse of Ashar speaking animatedly to a group of several well-equipped men and women, gesturing towards a rack of weapon upgrades. No surprises there. With everything that was going on, who wouldn't be looking to get themselves better equipped? Jade leaned against the wall beside the door to wait, pulling up her character sheet and skimming through her summary screen. The battle with the wraith demon had pushed her across the threshold to level thirty at last. A few more stat points and another class feature. It didn't feel like a reward worthy of the dire confrontation they'd just endured. Nor did it feel substantial enough to prepare them for what came next.

Jade slipped one hand into her belt pouch, fingers searching for the reassuring presence of the divine relic. It wasn't there, of course. The priceless artifact was carefully secured in her dimensional bag, a storage solution that should render it immune to divining magic of all kinds. According to Tara, it had to remain sequestered while they were in cities lest they trip magical alarms searching for dangerous relics. As secrecy was a core component of their mission, they couldn't afford the awkward confrontation that would inevitably lead to. So, as much as she might like to reaffirm the presence of divine power at their back, it would have to wait.

Mentally chiding herself for being childish, Jade forced aside her worries. She may as well go over her character sheet's recent changes while she waited for Ashar to finish with his current customers.

Name: Jade Callian

Level: 30* (34) (Mark of the Wayfarer)

Race: Succubus

Class: Shadow Witch

Advanced Class: Glamourblade

Origin: Earth

Base Stat Scores (Human)-(Succubus)

Strength: 14-17

Agility: 28-34 (+4 from armor)

Fortitude: 17-20

Intelligence: 21-25

Willpower: 19-11

As her level continued to grow, so too did the disparity between her demon form and her disguises. They hadn't been so dramatic at first, but as she continued to gain stat points, the 20% increase to her first four stats grew ever more potent. These days, Jade often found herself missing the enhanced speed and strength of her succubus body whenever she shifted. It was an odd sensation considering that her current human form was dramatically superior to her old body in every way, but the deficit still felt restrictive. Like her body was draped in small but invisible weights.

She swiped the list away, tabbing over to her talent page. There was one new entry.

Talent: Discordant Pulse

Rating: C level talent

Cost: Low mana

Duration: Instant

Cooldown: 45 seconds

Effect: Interrupt the flow of mana in a nearby target, disrupting their channeled abilities, talents, and spells. The target suffers a magical backlash, dealing damage to them proportional to the amount of mana they were using when hit by this effect. This talent is more effective when touching the target directly.

It was exactly what Jade had been waiting for. She couldn't count the number of times she'd been frustrated by her inability to counter a monster's ability or an opponent's magic. Her kit had many tricks of its own, yes, but disrupting an opponent's strategy was the easiest way to create an opening. She'd picked the talent immediately upon seeing it, and early tests with Naomi and Tara were a resounding success. With this, her ability set finally felt complete.

The sound of approaching voices broke into her concentration. Jade closed the window just as the door beside her opened, bells jingling.

"Are you sure you can give us all five of these for what we gave you, Ashar?"

An elf paused in the doorway, her slanted eyes narrowed in concern. One of her companions, a large man in heavy armor, jovially clapped her on the shoulder.

"How many times does that man have to tell you it's alright, Fen?"

"I know, but the guild marketplace charges at least twice as much for upgrade runes, and they don't even stock some of the ones we bought."

"My friends." Ashar stepped into the doorway, holding it open for the last of their group. "This isn't the time to fret over profit. We are in crisis, and you are the ones who will be protecting us. Of course I wish you to be as well-equipped as possible."

He remained on the threshold, smiling that bright grin of his. If Jade hadn't been able to sense the tight knot of worry and tension at the core of his spirit, it would have convinced her.

"The other merchants don't seem to see things that way…" Another of them grumbled.

"Then be sure to visit me again. I'll be eager to hear how my masterful enchantments helped you conquer the battlefield."

The group of adventurers departed in good cheer, earnestly talking amongst themselves. Jade stepped into view, hands on her hips. She raised one eyebrow.

"What was that about? Another ploy to draw in new regulars?"

"Jade! Welcome." Ashar straightened his jacket, greeting her with another smile. He stepped back inside, holding the door open for her. "Of course. Goodwill generates good business. I'm still new to this market, and standing out takes some effort."

Jade followed him into the warm, well-lit shop, grateful to escape the evening chill. Less than two weeks had passed since she'd last visited the establishment, but things had changed quite a bit. Previously, the shelves had been filled with numerous adventuring items, weapons, and upgrade runes. Now, there was quite a bit more empty space.

"Seems like it's working. You're nearly sold out."

"Eh." Ashar made a non-committal sound. "Everyone is gearing up, fearing for the worst. I'm selling faster than I can restock, especially now that my suppliers are fleeing the Labyrinth."

"Don't you make a lot of your wares yourself?"

"With help from my assistants, yes, but we're short on materials too. Sourcing those gets more expensive by the day."

"Then don't you need to raise prices to keep yourself afloat?"

"Well, I could…" Ashar busied himself with sorting through a pile of crystal reagents on a nearby shelf. "But people are desperate, you know? My enchantments are supposed to help people. If prices surge, then they won't get out where they're needed."

A flutter of warmth rose within Jade. She turned, making for the upgrade racks.

"You really aren't cut out for the business world." She whispered to herself, a slight smile tugging at her lips.

"Hmm? What was that?"

"Nothing."

"I'm sorry the potion didn't make it to your brother." Ashar said, an uncharacteristically gloomy expression clouding his face. "But I'm glad it was put to good use. As things are now, I don't think I'd be able to source any other reagents like the phoenix tear, though. There's just too much going on."

"It's alright." Jade said, gathering up the various upgrade runes and potions she'd just purchased. Their coin was mostly spent, but Ashar's generous prices had stretched the money beyond what they'd hoped to get for it. "I have other leads to follow up on. But thank you. Without your help, Naomi would be…"

Jade couldn't finish the sentence. Part of her wished she hadn't let the others convince her to let them accompany her to the demon world. She'd seen firsthand what a powerful demon could do to them, and now they were going to try and sneak through an army of them? How would she live with herself if she made it back, but they didn't?

But that wasn't her choice to make. Jade understood that, now. Despite her fear and uncertainty, she chose to have faith in those she held close.

"You're heading off to do something dangerous, aren't you."

Jade glanced up at Ashar. Though his tone was light, his dark eyes were filled with concern.

"Well, I am a Wayfarer. That's no different than usual."

"I suppose so. But please do one thing for me."

"What's that?""Come back safely, yeah?"

That sensation of warmth intensified, unexpected, but not unwelcome. It accompanied the slivers of emotion she could detect from his spirit. A sense of powerlessness, concern, and… was that hope? She instinctively flinched away from the emotion, anticipating the bitter sting that her succubus nature affixed to such positive feelings. Only…

It wasn't there.

Jade blinked in surprise, sending her senses inward. What was going on? Had something changed? She tried to think of the last time she'd felt such unpleasant reactions to positive emotions, only to come up short. It hadn't happened in a while, had it? But, why?

The answer came to her as she scrutinized her spirit. A subtle warmth permeated her aura now, absorbed from Seraphine's divine energy. It wasn't much, not nearly enough to substantially alter her nature, but its influence lingered, dampening the more primal urges her succubus nature forced upon her. And, apparently, removing the taint from positive emotions.

"Jade?"

"Huh? Oh, sorry."

With a start, Jade realized she'd been staring off into space for several long moments. Embarrassed, she looked away.

"Just… realized something important."

"Something good, I hope?"

She smiled. The path forward was fraught with danger, the stakes were higher than ever, and everything rested upon their shoulders. It was a comparatively small thing, but right now, it made all the difference.

She could feel again.

"Yes. Something good."

Jade stepped around the counter, pulling a surprised Ashar into a tight embrace. After a moment of surprise, he returned it, patting her gently on the back.

"Be safe out there."

Up close, the confused mixture of emotions was more intense than ever. Hope, compassion, kindness. She focused on those, breathing them in like a diver surfacing from beneath dark waters.

"I can't promise anything." She finally said, resting her forehead against his shoulder. "But I think I'm ready now."

No longer would she have to shy away from companionship, no more would the affections of others haunt her. She was still a succubus, and the demonic part of her spirit still simmered with dark desires. But now, there was something to temper it.

Buoyed by her newfound hope, Jade left to find her friends.

It was time to visit Tara's home.
