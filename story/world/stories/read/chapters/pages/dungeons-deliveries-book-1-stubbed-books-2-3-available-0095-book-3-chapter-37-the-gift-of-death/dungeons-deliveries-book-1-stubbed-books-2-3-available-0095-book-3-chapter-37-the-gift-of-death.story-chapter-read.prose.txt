“Meow!” Emilio hissed at Petunia from the Throne of Life.

“Oi! Alright! I hear ‘ye, Sun ‘n Shade,” Petunia yipped as she scooted back. “None of me special tea for our new Queen. Maybe another time, eh?”

Other gnomes waiting to meet their Queen rushed in.

Emilio glared at Petunia until she made the thimble of sludgy liquid disappear about her person. For a woman whose husband had just announced his death, she seemed quite normal to Alex. Even the remaining gnomes were acting like it was all as business as usual once the charge began. The clearing was quickly tidied, the Throne moved to float above Thumb Rock, and the gnomes went back to their responsibilities. Alex hadn’t seen a single tear shed.

Oh, Petal. Why? Alex thought as he swayed in a daze near the Throne. He didn’t remembered walking over or how long they’d been waiting to meet Boanne.

Snu pressed into his side, saying something soothing in his ear, but he couldn’t parse out her words.

A cacophony of fighting noises from the dark northern woods was all he could hear. Alex had watched in horror as the gnomes marched into the gloomy forest at a steady pace. Those who’d followed Petal into battle weren’t visible any longer, but the felling of trees, cheers, and the occasional hollered order bounded back. He’d also heard far too many death screams as gnomes lost their lives in battle.

The gnomes were his friends. They’d help save Snu and Giat as well as Nino and Nina, and worshipped his cat. Alex wanted to act now. To help any way he could. It was personal. More than personal.

Somewhere deep in the woods was his grandmother, influencing the Beast of Blightwood to who knew what end. If Nino and Nina were to be believed, she was the source of the cursed bracelet and likely had something to do with the Collector. None of it was mere coincidence.

It’s to do with me, Alex thought, feeling the wariness from the terribly long day he’d had. It’s been about me since the beginning.

He pulled away from Snu, striding to the forest with an exhausted mind. People called his name and Emilio prodded him mentally. Alex ignored them, stopping with his toes right at the threshold of the Blightwood to cast a controlled [Burrow Sense].

Initially, he’d thought the skill was for finding hidden treasures. With it activated, the world became muted and anything essence or System related superimposed itself in a bright kaleidoscope. [Shiny Snatch] and [Burrow Sense] was the combo in his mind, and even if he hadn’t found a ton of uses for it, Alex saw the appeal. Scan for hidden treasures and grab them. His core felt strange at the cast, both full with his connection to Emilio, but also like it was stinging. Still, he felt to need to act, and with a measured cast, [Burrow Sense] offered much more than just simple treasure hunting.

Alex gritted his teeth, unsatisfied with what he saw with [Burrow Sense]. He’d used too little essence. Emilio prodded him harder mentally, but Alex shoved him back, leaning harder into the skill. It took all his concentration, and when he finally got it right, he gasped at what the skill revealed.

Squirming dark essence blanketed the forest. It smothered the slow beating life of trees, with many already snuffed out and fully infused. There was virtually no variety. Bright green footprints of where the gnomes had stepped screamed out, but the wormy essence was eating it. Looking down, Alex saw the essence gnawing against a wall of subtle, relaxed green light. It was the same green of Emilio’s eyes, and his foot was half in half out of both essence types. Inspecting it closer, he was thankful that the dark didn’t appear to be making any progress, but it was reaching incessantly and even picking at his feet.

“I keep my monsters out for now.” A rumble of a voice said, startling Alex and snapping his head around.

With [Burrow Sense] still on, he came face to face with a potent presence of essence. It was spread out across the whole of his body, swirling constantly with hundreds of barely visible threads extending back towards the safety of the green light.

Alex snapped off [Burrow Sense], looking up to meet Runs with Wind’s eyes.

“It’s...It’s everywhere. And Petal and the gnomes are in that.”

Still with his foot in the Blightwood, the dark essence picked away at his foot.

Runs with Wind nodded. “And they are brave for it. They have prepared long for this night.”

Anger spiked in Alex’s heart as the essence needled in. He gestured towards the gnomes crowded around Emilio and Boanne.

“How are they acting so normal?!” Alex shouted, causing many of the gnomes to turn his way. “Petal went to die for you all!”

Alex, step out of the forest. Emilio’s voice said in his head.

“And you!” Alex pointed at Petunia, who seemed to now be holding court over the gnomes crowding Boanne. “You’re husband sacrifices himself over some BULLSHIT RITUAL, and you’re offering tea—”

A strong grip yanked him back out of the Blightwood, and the cold anger left immediately. Alex suddenly felt exhausted as Brody helped him stand upright.

“Now you see how brave the gnomes are for their charge.” Runs with Wind said. “Any of my roaming monsters fall under the Blightwood’s influence quickly. We haven’t been able to determine why they are more resilient to its force than us.”

Alex shook his head to clear it and realized that the gnomes around the Throne watched him with concern. Especially Petunia. Emilio sat at the edge of the Throne, looking into the Blightwood with a sad look, all the while pressing his warm presence against his connection into Alex.

Alex’s anger was gone, but it had been replaced with sadness and frustration.

Why aren’t you helping Petal, Emilio? Alex asked his cat mentally.

Emilio turned to him, and Alex realized the cat was full of his own frustration.

“Come, Alex, greet me.” Boanne said. “I expect we have similar concerns.”

Alex walked back over, noting the adolescent gnomes with blonde hair like Petal’s. The gnomes parted as he and Brody approached Emilio and Boanne. Snu gave him a sad smile, but kept close to Giat. The Throne of Life hovered a foot above Thumb Rock, and though there wasn’t any strong wind, the flowers and plants growing out of it swayed. Boanne barred her teeth at him in what he believed was a smile, and Emilio came to sit next to her.

“Please allow us some privacy.” Boanne said to the gnomes circling her. They quickly departed, trying to look busy, and the dragon called after Petuna. “Not you. You stay.”

Petunia froze, turning back around and sheepishly coming to the more private group.

“So, you are Alex.” Boanne said as she leaned down to sniff the air around him. “The one who first filled Emilio’s heart.”

Alex tried to shake his grumpiness, offering her a forced smile of his own. “He tell you that? It’s an honour to meet you, Boanne. This is Brody.” He gestured over his shoulder and then to Snu. “And that’s Snu and Giat. Dungeon Bosses.”

Something rumbled deep in Boanne’s chest as she turned and nodded at Snu and Giat. “Pleased to meet you, Succubi.” The sisters smiled, and Boanne turned back to Alex. “Yes, he did tell me. I believe he hides how much he truly cares for you from me, but I have picked up on it.”

“Cats, am I right?”

Aww, Emil, Alex said to Emilio, You just formally met her and you’re telling her all about—

“Wait.” He said aloud. “You can hear Emilio too?”

The rumble coming from gnome vibrated the Throne as she turned to regard Emilio. “Of course I can. Do you hide your voice from everyone? I am already learning so much.”

“I ain’t heard nothing from him.” Petunia tsked.

“Me…neither.” Brody agreed.

With all eyes on Emilio, the cat seemed to look embarrassed for the first time in his life. When he finally spoke to Alex’s mind, it was different than before, as if his voice was being broadcasted rather than spoken directly in his head.

I am only able to speak with those I have bonds with, it appears.

“How special I must be, then.” Boanne said to Emilio, rubbing her snout along his grey fur and vibrating harder. Alex realized the dragon was purring, and that Emilio had formed another bond.

“Woah, bonded already?” Alex asked. “What kind of bond?”

Both Emilio and Boanne turned towards him together as if synced.

None of your business! Emilio said in a light tone.

“It wouldn’t be proper for me to share.” Boanne said a moment later as her wing wrapped Emilio and pulled him in closer.

“You know what? I don’t really want to know.” Alex laughed genuinely. “I mean I do. But every couple needs their secrets.”

Boanne had moved a claw that somehow appeared delicate and fierce at the same time to scratching Emilio’s back. Alex wasn’t sure if he was supposed to be grossed out or find it cute that his cat seemed to be in heaven.

“And I promise to aide him however I can.” Boanne remarked.

“Quick as that, huh?” Alex asked. “It’s quick, isn’t it? Love at first sight for you too?”

“You humans.” Boanne curled her mouth to expose her fangs again, glancing at Snu for a moment. “You Bosses. Always complicating things. Doesn’t the heart recognize what it wants? What it needs? Can’t you tell from the scent of a potential partner? I knew the moment I laid eyes on him. Still, I am pleased with my partners prospects. The Throne of Life is quite a good place to start.”

Emilio chortled in both of their heads.

Alex scratched his own. “I don’t think we can…uh…figure things out by pheromones, or whatever, like you can.”

“Pity. Another thing I have learned, then.” Boanne looked at Emilio. “You are right, then. Your human really is a—what was it?—a blender of emotions? A noble being with immature tendencies? No. You called him a daring mewling who requires regular—”

“Hey!” Alex laughed, grumpiness parting in place of tiredness. “If you’re going to give me names, it’s Runner of Wonders, or Speed. No, Mr. Speed. Yeah. Or Bringer of Delight.”

Boanne boomed a snorting laugh, which got a surprised look from Emilio.

After half a minute, the dragon finally got a hold of herself. “Yes. I can see why Emilio bonded you, Alex.”

“He didn’t even know he did that, you know?” Alex said, causing Emilio to cast a warning glare at him. “Boanne, when there’s time, I’ll tell you all about him when he was younger and less…you know, powerful. Like the time he got duct tape stu—”

“Meow!” Emilio hollered, silencing him as Boanne snorted again.

“We have much to share, then. But let us understand the task at hand.” Boanne said, turning her tone more serious and regarding Petunia as well. “Petunia, you were not surprised from Alex’s reaction when he stepped into the woods there. I felt a similar irk, though not quite so explosively. Explain to me why you are not in despair at your husbands suicide mission? Ritual or not.”

Petunia seemed surprised at her question. “What? Ye’ can feel terrible about something or other and still know it’s the best thing ever, can’t ye?”

“Best thing?” Alex asked. “He’s going on a suicide mission!”

“Ah, ye’ just—ye’ can’t understand.” Petunia shook her head. “We’re not humans. We don’t think like humans, or Bosses, or cats, or dragons, or whatever thing ye’ slap yer willy on all weird like.”

Alex blinked at her explanation but didn’t interrupt.

“We’re bloody garden gnomes.” Petunia smacked her chest, continuing. “Born hollow. Even our children are. We barely got a handle on procreation, and it’s awful slow and dangerous too. How else we supposed to continue on forever, eh? Most anyone can make babies easy, or respawn like bloody Bosses. We don’t heal up though. Well, we didn’t, until Petal got that skill. But ye’ know where that came from? Eh?” She nodded towards Emilio. “From ‘im. From our mighty Sun and Shade. And aye, Petal didn’t know ye’ could do that, sir, did you?”

“Meow.” Emilio responded while lowering his head, sounding oddly like a ‘no.”

Alex glanced at Petunia, wondering if she knew that Emilio couldn’t read.

“He’s powerful, of course.” Alex cut in to save his cat the embarrassment. “But an Eternity Seed? That’s…that’s too strong even for him.”

“Oh, aye.” Petunia nodded excitedly. “But yer fergetin’ someone.”

“Who?” Boanne asked haughtily at mention of another someone.

Petunia cackled once. “Ye’ relax yerself, Queen. Ain’t no other lady dragon poking around. And I don’t care if he’s our king. Our men ain’t got ‘em, but we know plenty about a good neutering if need be. I’m talking about Gaia.”

“Meow.” Emilio said, sounding oddly like an ‘of course,’.

“Who?” Alex, Boanne, Brody, and Snu asked at the same time.

“Gaia. You know Gaia.” Petunia sniffed.

Alex looked around for an explanation. Snu shrugged her shoulders, and Brody scratched his own head.

“Is this Gaia a…monster? Another Boss? Someone like Emilio? Was there more than one GnOpal?” Alex asked.

“Oh don’t be ridiculous. GnOpal only existed ‘cuz of her anyway.” Petunia shrugged. “She’s just Gaia.”

Alex looked at Emilio, pressing him mentally with his unspoken question.

Emilio spoke to him and Boanne together, Gaia…is an interesting being. She is always there. Always was there. Part of my early climb to power was aided by her. And no…I have not met her. I cannot seem to locate her, but her scent is all encompassing.

Alex pinched the bridge of his nose. “That’s what we need, more hidden powerful people influencing stuff.”

“Oi!” Petunia grunted. “Ye’ don’t speak about Gaia like that. She just ain’t capable of bad things, get it? She helps with our Rituals, and so does Sun and Shade, alright? That’s what ye’ need to know. We love her and he loves us and she loves us too.”

“Alright, alright.” Alex held up his hands. “Gosh, everyone and their secrets.”

“It’s an honour, what Petal did.” Petunia smiled sadly. “The greatest honour we gnomes got. So, ye’, I am sad. But I’m also proud. And Petal’s not gone, remember that. We’ll get his core.”

Boanne vibrated. “How delightfully…morbid.”

“Eh, morbid depends on how ye’ look at it.” Petunia responded. “Maybe for you. But garden gnomes ain’t any normal people. Why we have to abide by everyone else’s morals?”

“We will play by our own rules, Petunia.” Boanne nodded. “I can assure you of that. And you, my lover, I have a question of you as well. What stops you from joining Petal and the gnomes in their first charge?”

“Mud and shite, I swear, can’t ye understand?” Petunia answered for Emilio. “We asked him not to.”

“What, why?” Alex asked. “He can cast lightning and levitate shit! He took down a Lich!”

“Oh, how sexy.” Boanne purred.

Alex wanted to make a comment about Emilio’s general well being, but Petunia laughed at Boanne’s remark and explained.

“We can’t rely on Sun and Shade fer’ everything, can we?” Petunia said. “We follow him. We will conquer and spread and make nice warm perches for his name. But gnomes ain’t gonna fall into the same traps humans did. Yer gods didn’t save ye’ from the end of the world, did they? All yer’ praying. Oh, Sun and Shade hollered. Complained and told us no, no, no. But we insisted and he accepted it in the end. We’ll need him soon yet, but let us form our own trail this time. You get it? You get it. It’s a gift to choose yer’ death without fear.”

Petunia finished, looking at everyone around, and pulled out her thimble full of dark sludge. Tapping a loud slurp, she smacked her lips.

“Still gonna sip this, I am. Not truth tea, is it? Nah, just something to take the edge off. Nothing dangerous.”

Maybe you need some, Alex. Emilio said to him, a moment later coming back with a grimmer tone. I also see why you joke constantly. Humor masks the pain I feel.

Yeah, you learned that one from me, buddy.

“Damn.” Alex shook his head. He understood her point, and why Emilio hadn’t charged in. It still sucked, and his mind kept jumping back on doing something to help. Surely there must be something he could do.

The Boss sisters strolled over, and Snu laid a hand on his back to comfort him.

“Thank you for sharing.” Boanne nodded, then said to Emilio. “I am elated to find that you possess honour with might.”

She nuzzled into Emilio, and though she was much larger, the cat took the snuggles without stumbling.

“What…would Petal want us to do?” Brody cut in.

Petunia smirked to herself, somehow pulling out enough porcelain thimbles for everyone from her porcelain dress. Each was full of that same sludge the gnome’s called tea.

“My Petal? He’d just want ye’ to drink to his name.”

They all took the tiny thimbles of Petunia’s blend, and Alex knocked it back.
