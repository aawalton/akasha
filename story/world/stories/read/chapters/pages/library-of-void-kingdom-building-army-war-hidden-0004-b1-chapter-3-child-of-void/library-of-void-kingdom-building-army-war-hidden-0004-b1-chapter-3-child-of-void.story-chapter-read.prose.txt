As the System's interface progressed slowly, Vector's thoughts wandered to his memories—his previous life on Earth.

The technologies. The orphaned life. Both gave him a chill. The technologies, obviously, for their destructive capabilities. And the orphaned life—a life of living alone, where no one shared happiness, no one relieved your pain. He gave himself a mental pat on the back for surviving such a dreary existence.

But the technologies used by the general populace offered him a new path forward. The internet, mobile phones, laptops. And most important of all: education. That could change the world. Developing new theories and technologies always required capable minds. And access to education was the most crucial element.

He praised Earth for being enlightened in this regard. He wasn't going to argue about the capitalist mindset. Discrepancies always existed.

Ahem... focus on the education.

He alone wouldn't be able to recreate everything. But by opening his own academy, he could make it possible. The world of Ethereum appeared to be in an 18th-century era when compared with his memories of Earth. Ethereum had 20,000 years of recorded history, yet it had only advanced to the level of 18th-century Earth, and there were records suggesting its civilization was even older. It was all because the people here practiced Martial Arts and Body Refining—disciplines that could extend their lifespans, giving less importance to technology and innovation.

But with him providing the boost, it would advance more rapidly. And he had the power, so why not? His family made it possible.

Hehee... He laughed inwardly, thinking of his future titles—like the titles scholars had earned on Earth. The Architect of the Universe. Father of Evolution. Father of Automobiles. The car, the train...

Eiit... He reflexively shuddered at the thought of the train. The memory of that impact was still painfully vivid.

Train-kun helped me reincarnate. Just like those truck-kun I saw in anime. He gave it a positive attitude. Otherwise, he really wouldn’t be here on Ethereum.

While his thoughts moved from one point to another, he sensed a disturbance in his head again. Reflexively, he thought, No... not again.

But this time, he didn't feel any pain. Information began appearing in his mind. Not memories.

An inheritance.

As the information passed through his consciousness, he learned who had provided him with this Void Library System. The force that had pulled his soul from the brink of death—from Train-kun's divine judgment—was called the "Will of the Void". It had not only granted him a second chance at life but had also profoundly transformed his soul, rendering it far stronger than any ordinary human's. This alteration explained the sharpness of his mind and the clarity of his thoughts.

The energy that had wracked his body again and again, making him suffer soul-rending pain while strengthening his physique and soul, was Chaos Energy—a double-edged force capable of both creation and destruction.

This came at the perfect time to help him increase his strength even more rapidly. Though he believed that given enough time, he would reach the pinnacle of Ethereum—a Fourth Stage expert—all because of his family. The Astrums.

The people of Ethereum practiced Body Refinement, a form of Martial Arts. Only a few could practice it, primarily due to resource scarcity or unavailability of Body Refinement manuals. Arts.

He also possessed talent—once in a millennium—which added icing on the cake. Normally, one could awaken Blood Energy at age twenty-one. But he had awakened it at twenty.

But one revelation caught his eyes: the origin of the Will of Void. It is not a god, nor a conscious being in the traditional sense. It is a self-born cosmic intelligence, emerging from the infinite emptiness after eons. Cold, impartial, and absolute, its role isn't conquest or morality—but balance. It acted to preserve the sanctity of the Void around the universes.

What confused him was the System's origin. The inheritance he'd received only explained that it was provided to Vector by the Will of Void, but not its creation. Whether it was created by the Will of Void or someone else remained a mystery. He knew that even when the System awakened, he still wouldn't get the answer. Because the inheritance had briefly explained the System's function and purpose—how it was going to help him improve his strength.

Its purpose. To unlock his potential, gradually enhancing his control over Chaos Energy, and to gather and store knowledge beyond human comprehension, including a database of technologies from his previous life on Earth. It had archives of all the knowledge that ever existed on Earth—whether technology, medicine, war, or philosophy.

But he had to unlock them. He didn't know how yet. But if it was one of those mission-giving Systems that forced compliance, then sorry—he wasn't going to ever use this System. After this type of System came, he knew he wouldn't be his own self anymore, but a person dancing to someone else's tune. And he didn't want that. It simply meant becoming someone else's pawn.

He would find out about it shortly.

The blue interface hovering before him pulsed with a faint glow, indicating that the System had awakened.

[98%... 99%... 100%]

[System Initialization Complete.]

A soft voice, devoid of any emotions, echoed in Vector's mind as the System fully activated.

[Void Library System – Activated.]

[Welcome, Host – Vector Astrum.]

A feeling of profound connection and clarity washed over Vector, a sense that something vast had just opened within him. This was no mere system; it was his path to power, knowledge, and the legacy of something far beyond this world.

The interface shimmered before Vector as the System came to life, revealing glowing panels for its status, Database, and Functions, each beckoning him to explore what lay hidden within. Then, as the interface settled before him in a structured formation of glowing panels, a calm, mechanical, yet distinctly sentient voice echoed once more in Vector’s mind.

[Introduction of the system.]

[This system is created to help the host. My objective is to assist the host in unlocking their full potential in controlling Chaos Energy. And provide the host with a myriad of knowledge.]

The voice was emotionless, yet carried a strange weight, like the whisper of an ancient force shaped into digital clarity. He understood the System’s from the inheritance he received just now which matched with the System’s explanation as the voice continued to explain while the interface also continued to change with the words.

[Designation: Void Library System.]

[Primary Function: To provide knowledge, facilitate training via Simulation Space, support martial and technological advancement, and serve as a conduit between the Host and the Will of the Void.]

[To assist the Host in controlling Chaos Energy until independent mastery is achieved.]

Vector’s eyes narrowed slightly, absorbing every word presented to him.

Huh… To serve as a connection between me and the Will?... he thought, as the system elucidated its purpose.

But this Origin Energy… he thought. Chaos energy was a form of Origin Energy. Why I haven't come in contact with someone who had started practicing Origin energy?

Previously, he'd been so engrossed in reading the inheritance that he'd completely ignored this Origin Energy. In Ethereum, he'd thought that Fourth Stage Warriors—Body Refinement experts—represented the pinnacle. But the inheritance told otherwise: there existed a system beyond Body Refining for gaining strength by cultivating Origin Energy.

But his attention was diverted again by the System's voice.

[System Notification]

[Host can now explore the Status Panel, Database and Functions of the System.]

After hearing the prompt, he commanded, System, show me the status panel.

The translucent, blue holographic interface materialized before him, displaying the Status, Database, and Function with detailed information within each.

[Void Library System – Status Window]

[Host Name: Vector Astrum]

[Physique: Void Body]

[Soul & Mind Attributes (10x Normal Human; Normal Human have Soul-1 max., Intelligence- 10 max.)]

[Storage Space], [Scanning], [Simulation]

Vector scanned the interface, its blue display hovering steadily before him as his mind absorbed the vast possibilities it offered. As his gaze settled on the status panel, he began analyzing the stats.

It showed the stats of his body transformed by Chaos Energy. It was three times stronger than a normal human, with the only exception being Vitality, which had a tenfold increase, and his soul—also ten times stronger, a direct result of Chaos Energy's strengthening. Should he become adept at wielding Chaos Energy, he would command even greater power and might.

But first, he had to find out about Origin Energy. One more task added to his bucket list, along with education.

The Strength, as usual, represents physical power.

But similarity arose between Agility and Dexterity—agility represented movement and speed, while dexterity signifies the harmony between mind and body, or more generally, coordination, and most crucially, reaction speed.

A memory surfaced: an old story about samurai cutting arrows mid-flight with their swords. He could achieve that only if he had enough speed to move the sword—the agility—and dexterity, coordination and fine motor skills, to have enough time to react and cut the arrow. He could probably even cut a bullet with a sword... Nah... not truly, but if it were within extreme far distance. Theoretically absurd, yet with his stats, terrifyingly possible.

And those Second-stage Warrior could easily do it.

Vitality was more than just health. It encompassed endurance, stamina, pain resistance, immune resilience, and recovery rate. With this absurd stat, he could now withstand a broken bone, continue fighting despite it, and even walk it off if capable of enduring the pain. This was simply inhuman. Probably, his Vitality was more than double that of a First Stage Body Refining expert. What would happen if I reached First Stage myself?

Sleep? He could stay awake three or four days without mental fatigue. If a human took a week to heal a minor cut, he would heal the same cut in a mere half-hour to an hour. A broken bone? Fully healed within a few days, maybe—which usually took one to three months for normal humans.

The last and most important attributes were Soul and Intelligence. He was shocked after reading their stats and finding their explanation in the inheritance. They were so monstrous that he couldn't imagine their true limits. They were ten times stronger than normal humans, just like Vitality.

One reason for his astonishment was that neither Body Refinement nor traditional Martial Arts could increase Soul Strength or Intelligence. They typically remained constant unless an external force was used, such as rare elixirs or herbs. This transformation had also vastly changed his mind and comprehension, allowing him to easily recall photographic memories, every detail of both his past and present lives, and any knowledge he had once encountered or was easily understandable—due to enhanced comprehension.

He could now comprehend and deduce any knowledge in hours, if not minutes, that had previously taken him months. The genesis of all these inhuman stats? Chaos Energy and his unique physique: the Void Body.

The Void Body was unique. It wasn’t merely a special physique but a foundational structure built to endure the influence of Chaos Energy. His Void Body physique’s main feature wasn't physical but the Soul and Intelligence. With enough soul strength he would be able to control Chaos Energy more efficiently when he starts cultivating Origin Energy.

His strength, agility, and dexterity were certainly enhanced, yet vitality was the true hallmark of today's transformation to endure Chaos Energy.

Only this physique could withstand the Chaos Energy coursing through him. Had he possessed weaker vitality and lacked the Void Body, he would be torn apart the moment the Chaos Energy entered. This explained his dramatic increase in Vitality. If he could further augment his Vitality, it would concurrently boost his other three stats—Strength, Agility, and Dexterity. Though it granted near-inhuman toughness and healing, this Vitality did not include regeneration. That was his sole regret. He couldn't regrow limbs or instantly recover from fatal wounds. He could heal fast, faster than any human, but not like the mystical monsters of legend. That had to wait.

After being utterly shocked by these inhuman stats, he finally focused on the System's Database.

Locked.

Hey System! How can I unlock the database?

[System Notification]

[Technology Tree Database: Conditional Access]

[The Host may unlock technology databases by progressing through their corresponding trees. Research and successful creation of initial-tier technologies will grant full access to the complete technology tree database of that field.]

He couldn't help but smile, contemplating the technologies he could introduce to this world—innovations that would not only empower him but also forge his forces that even he couldn't imagine. This made education one of his primary requirements. Simply easy.

He would make simple magnetic motors, and all the information from the Electricity branch and the Motor branch would be at his fingertips.

If he made one engine, he would gain access to all types of knowledge derived from it—all the advanced knowledge that existed on Earth. Furthermore, all research and technologies from Earth would be at his fingertips.

For now, the Will of the Void had already granted him an extraordinary advantage: it had provided him with technological and medical knowledge capable of strengthening humans beyond their inherent limits. The only obstruction was that he had to create early technologies to unlock their relevant technology trees.

This, however, was merely the beginning. The Void itself was infinite, containing knowledge from countless universes. As his connection with the Will of the Void deepened, he would gain access to even greater knowledge and secrets.

Seeing all the things. The System’s power. he couldn't help but comment.

It is not wrong to call myself 'Child of Void'.

System! Do you also give me tasks to grant me special powers? Cautiously like he was eager to have special powers.

[System Notification]

[The System has no task requirements or imposed objectives. Its purpose is purely supportive—to aid the Host in managing the vast database, guide technological development, and assist in gaining expertise over the Chaos Energy within.]

Sigh! Whether it was for relief… or not gaining cheat like abilities. Instantly making him strong. He himself was confused with this sigh of his.

It wasn't one of those predatory Systems. It had no heaven-defying function like granting him heavenly abilities. It was only like a guide that would help him manage his database and his Chaos Energy—though when he could actually use it, he didn't know.

His true cheat could be called Void Body physique. And the function of the System.

One final notification appeared, interrupting his ruminations.

[System Notification]

[A Gift from the Will of the Void is awaiting retrieval: 'Void Library Building'.]

[Condition: A territory is needed to place the structure.]

[Please specify the location where you want to place the 'Void Library Building'.]

Vector's eyes narrowed. He didn't have any knowledge of it in his inheritance. He became visibly curious about the power and function of this Void Library Building that the Will of Void had given him.

Well, let's build it on Agastya Mountain when I go to Elara, he thought.

But first, he contemplated, I need to find out the betrayer's purpose, if possible.

It had been less than fifteen minutes since he'd told them to leave the room. So, he prepared to exit. He didn't see any reason to remain here. His body was well and fine—even more powerful than before.
