Eric was extremely happy with his evolution. Even with the rushed breakthrough he had still managed to end up with a Mythic class. Pretty good for D grade and his patron shared that opinion. Falling back to Legendary rarity had been very likely. Thankfully with all the materials from integrated space he had managed to push his light cultivation even further which probably was the reason why he obtained a Mythic class in the first place.

Solarion had told him that he no longer needed to worry about Thalion because the guy had joined Solans new faction who apparently kept bragging across the entire multiverse about the achievements of his mortal. Supposedly Thalion had won the second system event as well and this time with an even larger lead over the other participants.

For Eric the second system event had been horrible. The main issue was that almost everyone who entered such an event simply never came back. After the first system event there had been many who performed poorly and wanted to somehow regain their lost time by achieving a good placement in the second one. Then there were those who had been too weak to join the first event and now tried their luck as well.

From the survivors Eric heard only horror stories. The trial had been an utter shitshow with no loot and almost no beasts to farm experience from while death waited around every corner.

He seriously wondered whether it would be smarter to outright forbid everyone from joining the third system event because after the second one he had lost over twenty percent of his citizens. He needed every helping hand possible in the war against the elves.

Eric honestly had no idea how humanity was even supposed to turn this war around. The average elf was stronger than the average human and most elves were already several hundred years old which gave them absurd amount of experience. Moving silently through forests came naturally to elves while humans sounded like bulldozers crashing through the wilderness. Their superior senses only made things worse because even a tiny branch snapping could supposedly be heard from far away.

It wasnt only the average elves either. Eric had to admit that the elven Chosen were monstrously powerful as well. Sure Eric and the other human Chosen were strong too but the elves had almost twice as many Chosen.

He also wondered what the other factions were doing. Since arriving on New Earth he had heard nothing from the vampires or the orcs.

What worried Eric the most however was the fact that there hadnt been a single attack against the fortress during the last three nights. That felt deeply unnatural for the elves. Eric only hoped it was because they were busy defending another outpost and not because they were preparing something massive to overwhelm his defenses.

So far he had absolutely no idea what they could even attempt.

His base was gigantic and heavily fortified. Nobody should realistically be able to breach its walls. The underground itself had been reinforced which meant tunneling below the fortress was impossible unless a D grade spent enormous amounts of time on it and even then the defenses should detect them long before completion.

In Erics opinion tunneling still remained the most realistic option for the elves because every other method seemed outright impossible.

The walls themselves couldnt be penetrated from the outside no matter what tools were used. The gates remained weak points but they were surrounded by towers which made reaching them without being slaughtered nearly impossible.

The dome shield also shouldnt be underestimated and was likely the strongest part of the strongholds defenses. Even if the elves somehow created some superweapon and managed to deliver it directly onto the walls the fortress still wouldnt collapse.

That eased Erics mind slightly.

The other human Chosen were currently out leveling even though advancing in D grade already felt impossible. Eric himself had gone hunting once and killed ten beasts which hadnt even granted him five percent progress to the next level.

It was probably because he was now a full grade higher and because D grade simply required absurd amounts of experience. It definitely would be a long and painful grind.

On the positive side he would remain in D grade for a very long time which meant this was the perfect stage to truly improve his rarity and foundation. The war on this planet likely would be long decided before he even needed to seriously think about reaching C grade.

His patron had also mentioned that most other Chosen would likely do the same. In the end the gods wanted their investment to succeed and letting someone recklessly rush from grade to grade without focusing on body tempering and skill upgrades made true godhood almost impossible.

Eric wondered what it actually felt like to become a god. Solarion had already guided many Chosen to godhood before so Eric believed he would manage as well.

Solarion had once told him that during all his existence he had barely found anything truly negative about being a god. Especially a powerful one.

Still the god had also warned him that everything became more difficult the higher ones level rose and once you entered godhood you would face nothing but absolute geniuses. Most possessed bloodlines and powers beyond imagination and every move had to be planned centuries ahead.

Eric shook his head and focused back on the map before him. Something needed to change soon or humanity might be utterly doomed.

Until then he would simply continue hunting. If he got lucky he might even encounter a D grade beast and finally gain another level.

But before all that he would grab another bowl of that insect soup. He had eaten it once already and honestly it had been surprisingly good.
