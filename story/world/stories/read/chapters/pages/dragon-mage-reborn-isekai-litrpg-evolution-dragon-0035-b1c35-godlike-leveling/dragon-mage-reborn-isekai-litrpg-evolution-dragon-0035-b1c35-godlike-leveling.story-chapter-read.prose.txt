People would get their lives back, entire civilizations restored, and the future of Earth and Etheryn would be secure.

I was getting tingles just thinking about it!

I never told Nolan the other reason I was doing all of this. It wasn’t just charity work. I wanted to find my mom. My sassy, loving, strong-minded and steadfast mom. I’d never gotten my closure all of those years ago. Her disappearance broke me. While I also missed dad, I was definitely closer to my mother, and the chance to reunite with her again was what had truly driven me to say yes to the biggest quest in my life.

So yeah, this cure was a serious big deal.

I was working it up for something special I had in mind, and I was one step ahead of my codex with my… dark intentions:

I felt the connection take hold, that familiar rush of dominance as the dire wasp’s consciousness submitted to mine. Its compound eyes flickered with a glowing blue-violet sheen—my signature. The wasp’s body trembled one final time before going still, and now it was an extension of my will.
