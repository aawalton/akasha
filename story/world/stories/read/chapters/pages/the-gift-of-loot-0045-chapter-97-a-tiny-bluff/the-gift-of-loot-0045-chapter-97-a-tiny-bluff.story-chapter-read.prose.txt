The first thing Thomas did was check inward to his core. Sure enough, he still had four layers, which was a bit of a shame because he felt so strong and so whole that he half-expected his power to increase to 5 layers. If that were the case, he would be eligible for a class.

Instead, all four layers blazed with power that sparkled with unicorn blood and which he knew held a darkness of corruption mana in its core.

"Tom?" Zach asked, staring at him. The whole team was. "You okay, bro?"

Thomas took a deep cleansing breath, straightened, and then nodded. "Yeah, that did the trick, I think."

"You're fixed?" Jo demanded. "Just like that?"

He grinned at her. "Just like that."

"He's the team healer, what do you expect?" Zach said with a laugh then held up his hand for a high five.

Amused, Thomas returned it.

"So you're going to do that for us, too, right?" Twitch immediately asked. "The Purge thing? Because I could use some muscles too." He flexed a very skinny arm.

"No way, I already called dibs way before you got here," Zach said with a grin. "Get in line, new guy."

Twitch leveled a look of mock disgust at him. "Full offense, but why do you think you need the beautification treatment?"

Thomas held up his hand. "Okay, first, the Purge didn't give me muscles; that was another skill. Second, Twitch, I'm pretty sure it would wipe out every single one of your tattoos, so you'd have to be okay with that. Plus, we sorta don't have time for either one of you to be sick for an hour. Does anybody know how long we still have left on our boon?"

"Probably a good few hours left at least," Jo said, "but I'm sure the System didn't give us a 12-hour countdown willy-nilly. We need to clear out this whole facility before then."

That rang right to Thomas. Also, he was certain that clearing out the facility meant getting rid of Tiny and his lackeys. That was fine by him, especially since there was a good chance they were trying to steal his loot from the inventory room.

Well, the team's loot. Same difference.

On the other hand, killing Tiny, Sue, and Holden didn't sit right with him, even if they had brought Courtney into the battle. He didn't know if it was his loot heart or being a healer or if he was just weird like that, but he was so tired of killing.

So, fuck it, maybe he wouldn't kill. He didn't have the Gift of Murder. He had the Gift of Loot, and more mana crystals than he could shake a stick at. He might as well buy his way out of his problems for once.

"Hold on, I got an idea," he said, and dug in his pockets for one of the other expandable bags he had pulled off the guards. Finding one, he dumped out the contents, then started quickly sorting through the crystals, tossing in Electric, Light, and Nature mana. It was all level three or lower, so useless to his level four core. Then, with a pang, he dropped in a couple of Wellspring mana too. The lower-grade ones.

"Is this really the time to be sorting through your treasures?" Jo asked, though she sounded fondly amused.

He sighed, feeling actual pain at the plan. It beat killing. Barely. "This is what we're going to use to buy Tiny off." Then, knowing that it was not enough, he added one of Laveris's skill shards too: Bound with Armor, which sounded interesting on the surface but had a plain enough name to be not all that special.

He thought about adding a Corruption mana, but even he wasn't that much of an asshole.

"You're serious? There are four of us and three of those fools, let's just take them down," Twitch said.

"If we have to, sure," Thomas said, "but let's just try it my way first, okay?"

Twitch stared at him for a moment then jerked his shoulder up and down in a halfway shrug.

Zach nodded slowly, though he looked doubtful, as if he thought that Thomas was being too naive about this and they'd probably have to kill no matter what. Maybe he was right, but Thomas at least had to try.

Jo looked at him with compassion. "All right," she said, "let's see what we can do. Give it over." She held out her hand for the bag. "I know him best. If we're going to try to talk him out of just leaving, I should be the one to try."

It almost physically hurt to hand over all that potential wealth, but Thomas made himself do it.

Those are all low-level mana crystals, he told himself, and tried not to mourn. I have plenty more from the dungeon plus all the other higher-level stuff I looted from the elves.

Jo looked in the bag, grunted at what she saw, then cinched the sack closed. "Okay, I know where the inventory room should be. Let's get a move on."

_____

Jo led them down a series of hallways that made no sense to Thomas at all, but they were at least empty of enemies. They heard clanging long before they reached the inventory room. It was at the end of a long, curving hallway.

Tiny and his team were already there.

The inventory room was blocked by a giant vault, the kind that would look normal in any large bank, only the silver lines spiraling around the central lock pulsed with a dark, angry red. Tiny, who had gotten a warhammer from somewhere, was currently swinging at it as if he meant to batter his way in. Unfortunately, though Tiny's muscles bulged and he was clearly putting all his strength into every swing, there wasn't even a scuff on the vault door.

Holton was standing guard facing them, and the moment they became visible, he immediately sounded the alarm. "We got company!"

Tiny swung one last time at the door with a roar. The warhammer clanged, but it did nothing. Sweat glistening off giant muscles that put Thomas's to shame, Tiny turned and looked at them. His gaze went to Thomas first.

"Thought you were dead, for sure," Tiny said as Thomas braced himself to be smacked with false emotion, but nothing of the sort happened. Instead, Tiny nodded. "I'm surprised to see you alive."

Thomas shrugged. "Well, Courtney did her best."

"Her best clearly wasn't good enough," Tiny replied wryly, then looked down at himself. "What the hell did you do to me back there? It was like you gave me instant food poisoning, but when I came out of it..." He clenched his free hand into a fist and flexed one of his huge muscles.

"You received an upgrade," Thomas said. "Now there are no more toxins messing with your cultivation path."

"I'd think that you're full of shit, but I feel pretty damn amazing," Tiny said. "Which is good for you, or else I would have killed you by now."

"You're welcome," Thomas said ironically.

Jo stepped forward. "Tiny, you have to know by now that you're not getting into that inventory room."

His face darkened. "Courtney said that she knew the way into this place. That was the only reason we brought her along."

"She's dead," Zach said, "And now you've set off an alarm trying to break your way in there."

"How do you know there's an alarm?" Sue asked sharply.

Zach casually nodded to the pulsing red lines. "That's my first hint. If the other branch families didn't know things were going wrong before, they'd figure it out now."

Jo jumped back in. "You're not getting in there, but we're willing to make a deal with you." She lifted the sack, then, to Thomas's mild alarm, threw it so it clattered at Tiny's feet.

Uh, wasn't she supposed to wait to do that after he agreed?

Jo continued, "We have a whole bunch of level three crystals in there. Most of them are Electric, Nature, and Wellspring. It's more than enough for the three of you. You should take it and walk away."

Sue and Holton stared at the sack, but Tiny was a little smarter than that, and didn't take his eyes off Thomas and the team.

"What about skill shards?" he asked. "One of those elves had something like what I can do with my Gift. Did he drop anything good?"

Thomas felt a shudder of horror at that. Ravath's aura skill shards combined with Tiny's Gift would be a potent mix, which was exactly why he would never get it.

"He didn't drop anything," Thomas said.

Tiny looked straight at him and said with perfect confidence, "You're lying."

"No, I'm not."

He tapped his forehead. "I can feel it."

Oh shit, what if he could?

Thomas didn't dare look at the others, but they hadn't looted the room and wouldn't know what Ravath had dropped. They’d be radiating innocence. So, he took a chance.

"You can't feel anything," Thomas said. "You're guessing."

Tiny stared at him, a frown growing on his face. Maybe he could read Thomas's mind, or maybe the two of them were trying to psych each other out. Either way, Tiny wasn't leaving.

Tension thickened in the air.

Zach shifted his weight. "Last chance, big guy. Take the treasure and go, and no one else has to die today."

Tiny's eyes finally left Thomas and snapped to him. "Is that a threat?"

Zach shrugged and seemed to produce one of his dark teleportation daggers out of nowhere. He casually flipped it once in his hand. "Dude, that's a promise. We're taking this facility and dealing with the elf problem on the backend. You don't need to be part of that."

There was no signal, but Sue and Holton came up to flank Tiny, making a triangle. They knew that they were outmatched, but it looked like they were still ready to fight.

"Tiny," Jo stepped up. She gave a half smile. "You've got people waiting on you back in New York, don't you? This is stupid. Take the win and go home.”

Again, he focused on her. “You’re not coming with us, Jo?”

Say what? Thomas almost stepped forward, but Twitch casually gripped his upper arm and held him back.

There was no need to get in the middle of this. Jo swayed for a moment and Thomas got the distinct impression that he was using his bullshit Gift on her, but she was aware of them now and that seemed to take a lot of the power away. “No, I'm not leaving my daughter, or my team.”

“So we’re just supposed to go and leave you the dungeon?” Now Tiny sounded petulent.

Jo smiled. “I thought you’d be tired of running that thing anyway.”

Tiny frowned, but finally nodded. “Fine,” he said shortly, “But how am I supposed to trust that you're going to send me back through that portal to the right place? Not somewhere else?”

"Portal's dead anyway," Twitch said.

That got everybody's attention.

"Yeah." Twitch flashed his braces in a grin and bounced on his toes. "I forgot to say I fried the control panel. I didn't know anyone would need it after. Oops."

Tiny's face went blank. "Why the hell did you do that? That was the only way we had to get out!"

"Nah." Twitch dug into his pocket and pulled out a ring of square car key fobs, then held them up, jingling them. "Half the dead guards in this place had keys on them. There are a whole lot of cars outside the quarry. You can drive out of here. No portal needed."

Thomas blinked. He didn't even know that Twitch had looted the guards they’d fought when they first got out of the dungeon, but he wasn't surprised. Despite himself, he felt a spike of fondness for the thief. He had the feeling Twitch had probably took their wallets too.

He did belong on the team after all.

"There's going to be backup coming," Zach said, nodding at the red lights. "It's pulsing faster, don't you see? The other branch families are going to know that this place is in trouble. You still want to be standing here when they show up?"

Finally, Tiny looked down at the sack by his feet, then at Thomas and Jo. He seemed to be weighing his options. Then he bent down, picked up the sack, looked in, blinked at what he saw—which was a minor fortune to most people, and Thomas had made sure the skill shard sat on top—then slung it over his shoulder.

"Fine," he grunted. "But if I find out you lied to me, we're going to have a problem."

Oooh, a problem, I'm so scared, Thomas barely kept himself from saying. Instead, he just nodded, expression serious.

No way Tiny thought he was telling the truth (which was fair, because he wasn't), but Thomas would allow a sop to the man's ego by letting him have the last word.

He and his team stepped back, allowing them to pass. Holten glanced at Thomas and gave a shrug before following his leader.

Thomas watched them go with a vague sense of guilt for the lackeys. He didn't know if Sue and Holton really were under Tiny's sway or if they followed out of habit. Should he have tried to break Tiny's hold on them?

As if reading his mind, Zach's hand landed on his shoulder. "Let it go."

They waited, hearing Tiny retreat, and Jo used her Gift of Hiding to follow them. She returned a few minutes later, confirming Tiny and his team had made it to the exit.

Twitch let out a long, shaky breath and bent over with his hands on his knees. "Oh my God, I was not ready for another fight. That guy might have broken me in half. Yeah, I can bounce back from it right now, but shit still hurts..."

"Fight's not over yet," Jo said, nodding to the door, which was still pulsing red. "How long do we have until backup comes, do you think?"

"Oh, I was totally lying about that," Zach said.

Everybody turned to look at him. He shrugged and smiled.

"Frederic said that he already called for help from the elves. Backup came. Branch families are totally set against one another—they're not coming to help."

Thomas barked out a laugh. He'd been ready for a fight, but like Twitch, he hadn't been looking forward to it. "Still, how do we get through the door, do you think? Any ideas?"

"I got one," Zach said. "Thanks to the boon, I'm, like, really in tune with my Teleportation mana right now."

Then, in the next second, he was gone. Thomas stared at the empty spot where he'd been standing. As one, they swung back to the door.

Behind the thick vault door came a muffled click, followed by another, then a long grinding sound as if a massive bolt were being drawn back. The red lines around the lock flickered and went dark as the door swung open.

Zach stood on the other side, framed against golden light, with a very smug expression on his face. Ever the showman, he gestured them in. "After you, broskies. Welcome to the inventory room."
