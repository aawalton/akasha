But that wasn’t the worst part of it. Not only did I lose my gun and my scrolls, but I was dragged into the heart of Zev’s operation to get my own shit back. Sprinkle that with an ungrateful spoiled pain in my side and well, I’d say the high hour of the evening is spent. Now, I’m a pretty simple man. That asshole Khunum wants me dead, because he knows what I can do. But I can’t do much of anything half-cocked. Which is the type of situation you put me in. The top floors were simple to stomach, nothing besides a few floating body parts in jars. Zev is a sick man, everyone knows that, but once I stumbled into those subfloors….” Lun gulped, the horror in his eyes making my stomach sink. “I couldn’t be next.
