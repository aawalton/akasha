Caleb saw nothing, but his other senses were still available to him. The darkness gave them a warning that trouble was coming and Caleb heard the now familiar sound of Mettius throwing up a shield.

Caleb moved to where Kelia was and threw his arms around to cover her with his own body. He pulled her toward Mettius so they could both benefit from the cover of his shield.

He urged Bog through their bond to run to the edge of the darkness field they found themselves in.,

Then the arrows came.

He sensed them through his Metal Affinity and as soon as he detected them, he put all his mental might behind pushing them back. Five bolts came in total and with each he pushed away he felt the force of the blow impact his body. Each blow was spread out evenly so it didn’t harm him, but the force behind them was such that he staggered under each. So focused was he on the task that he even repelled the bolts that would have crashed into Mettius’ shield.

“Five bolts!” Caleb called, alerting his allies to the threat.

Kelia broke from Caleb’s grasp at the words and Caleb followed, running straight in the direction he’d detected one of the arrow. Mettius broke as well running out of the darkness in a different direction.

A hawk’s shriek rang out before Caleb had regained his vision and to the side he heard the scream of a man followed by the thud of him crashing to the ground.

“In the trees!” Kelia shouted.

Caleb ran out of the darkness just in time to see the next bolt coming for him. This time he deflected it, using far less mana to send the bolt down into the ground a meter before him. He scanned the surroundings and saw that there were four men up in the trees and a large four meter dome of darkness behind him. Before he could act on the information however, the world once more became covered in darkness.

“Four in the trees! I’m blind again.” Caleb shouted as he deflected two more bolts.

“Same!” Came the shouts of Kelia and Mettius.

Through his bond Caleb sensed that Bog too was blinded. Another hawk shriek pierced the darkness, but this one wasn’t followed by a scream.

“Avis is down!” Kelia shouted.

Caleb closed stopped moving and tried to focus before deciding he already had the perfect skill for the situation.

He activated Iron Vortex, whipping the wind up all around them. The arrows he’d already deflected got picked up and started sailing around in the wind, but he willed the metal control aspect of the spell to send the arrows sailing out of the circle as was their natural inclination and not hold them within.

The ten arrows he’d already deflected flew out into the surroundings out of Caleb’s ability to perceive.

The next arrows that came were taken up by both the wind and Caleb’s Imperium without him having to even react to them.

“Get inside my vortex!” Caleb shouted, now trying to decide how to deal with the attackers now that they were free of attacks.

Then he sensed powerful movement in his wind sense as gusts manifested out of nothing in the air. Each gust was followed by a scream as another attacker fell. Four shouts came out in total.

“They are all on the ground now!” Kelia informed the group.

She used her Imperium Ventorum, Caleb realized.

Kelia hadn’t practiced with that skill as much, but she had the same level of strength with wind that he had.

Caleb let his Iron Vortex go after that and ran to the nearest fallen attacker.

Iron Vortex has increased to level 2.

Still in the darkness, he drew his hammer. Once he was within 10 meters the metal on the fallen assailant shone like a beacon in the darkness to his sight deprived senses. Buckles, arrows, a sword, daggers and studs holding together plates to leather armor outlined his enemy perfectly.

The man was standing with a sword drawn in a guard ready for him. Caleb’s course had been off by a few degrees and if he continued on, he’d pass the enemy up a few meters away. Once Caleb was aware of his exact location however, ran still on this incorrect path.

While still 5 meters away, Caleb pushed on all the metal on the man with the full force of his Imperium. He expected the man to go flying as the full force of Caleb’s run was transferred into the man, but instead he simple fell over backwards. Caleb still felt like he’d crashed into a person running at full tilt and came to a dead stop despite the less dramatic result on his foe.

What happened? He wondered.

Some force had been lost in the effect, more than could have been explained by the distance between them.

He didn’t dwell on it, as it had done the job well enough. Caleb moved over to the staggered man, and brought his hammer down on him, striking him in the chest and killing him with one Imperium aided blow

Adolescent Dark Archer (error) has been killed.

That message was followed shortly after by two more of the same as Bog and Kelia each killed an attacker.

“A-little-help-over-here!” Came the sped up voice of a hasted Mettius as he ran back and forth in the darkness raising shields to avoid blows but having no ability to find his attacker.

Caleb ran in the direction he’d heard another archer fall and had to deflect another arrow. He’d grown accustomed to the force needed to repel these and he let this one get close before pushing back on it with the full force of his Imperium. The arrow stopped and reversed direction, sailing back at its archer. Caleb sensed that the arrow’s trajectory was heading into the dirt between them however and he called upon the wind to lift the arrows flight. Caleb didn’t sense where it his, but the shout of surprise told him he'd struck. He followed the arrow with a charge, deflected another one from behind before the first injured attacker entered his perception. He couldn’t he couldn’t sense the arrow he’d fired any longer pierced as it was inside his foe.

Caleb repeated the full body push on the equipment this archer was holding, and braced for the impact he only staggered slightly and continued on moving. His enemy had seen this maneuver already, and they rolled with the impact coming up with a sword raised in the guard.

Caleb brought his hammer down spike end first and aided by an Arcane Power Attack he shattered the sword held up to block his blow. The archer’s flesh and armor was no more up to the task of defending against Caleb’s attack and another messaged appeared.

Adult Dark Archer (error) has been killed.

Caleb spun for another foe when the last messaged came.

Adolescent Dark Archer (error) has been killed.

With the last message the darkness vanished, and Caleb could see the battlefield. Kelia stood in the center with her bow drawn while Mettius stood between Caleb and her with a shield raised. Two of the attacks lay dead with familiarly fletched arrows sprouting from their chests while one had been slain by Caleb and the last by Bog. The blood on their bodies was an unnatural black, but otherwise the dead men and women around them looked no different from the dead legionnaires he’d seen. None of the corruption evident on the other error beasts evident on them.

Kelia ran over to where Avis lay in the ground and Mettius rapidly began climbing a tree to get a view of what was happening.

“We have to move!” Came the voice from above. “There are more on their way!”
