Cheers erupted. A twenty-foot-long banner unfurled, spelling Baldinator with a lit light bulb on each side, while three people wearing Baldinator t-shirts took out gongs and started hitting them in rhythm.

“Baldinator! Baldinator! Baldinator!”

“Who the fuck made shirts?” Gary asked.

“Who knows?” Asim replied. A few disciples sitting next to Gary had left during the day, so he and Chloe had snuck closer. Their regular seats were across the arena.

“There is history in this match!” Rollo cried out, drawing everyone’s attention. “The Lord of Pot was particularly heavy-handed throughout the preliminaries. As luck would have it, two of his matches were against Gary’s close friends!”

The projection showed snippets of the Lord’s previous battles. Everyone saw him hitting Alicia hard in the head, then pummeling Cog even when it was clear he was already unconscious.

“Was it illegal? No! Was it reasonable? The Baldinator definitely doesn’t think so! He’s here to take those perfect jives and wear them as a trophy, but can he do it? The Lord of Pot, though a complete underdog, breezed through the preliminaries undefeated! Some say he never even showed his full strength! Can the Baldinator draw it out? What is the Lord hiding? There is only one way to find out—a straightforward battle for vengeance! The battle of the underdogs! The battle of the unlikelies!”

“He’s sure having fun,” Cog said.

“I can’t believe they showed me,” Alicia said, downcast.

“Don’t worry, guys. I’m going to break that pot of his,” Gary said, grasping his spear.

“Don’t even try it,” Asim said, squinting at the Lord. “That’s a middle-grade alchemical furnace. I don’t know where he found it, or why he calls it a pot, but it’s made to contain explosions. You could hammer it all day and never make a scratch.”

“Then I’ll break him instead,” Gary said.

“Yeah, that works.”

He jumped, robes fluttering as he reached the arena. His exhaustion was swept away by the excitement—yet, it was still there. Gary was painfully aware this wasn’t his top condition, but it was close.

“I’ll cook you,” the Lord said.

“You’ll fart on me and call it wind,” Gary replied. The audience replied with confused laughter.

“An odd statement by the Baldinator! Hopefully, his skill with the spear is better than with words!”

Gary’s brow twitched. He stared at his opponent’s face—two heavy eyes looked right back. He clenched his spear. “Let’s do this, Goldie.”

“Yes!” Goldie exclaimed from inside the spear.

“Fight!” Rollo yelled, and Gary was surprised to find the Lord charging first. He held a spear in one hand and a pot in the other. He tossed the pot before even reaching Gary, but instead of dodging it, Gary slapped it away. That quickly turned out to be a bad idea, because the pot was much heavier than it seemed. Gary’s eyes widened. He quickly ducked to the side, barely dodging the flying pot, and was forced on the defensive by the Lord’s spear.

“What’s that guy doing? Who slaps away a pot?” someone in the audience said.

“He’s doing it to entertain us!” another said, laughing.

“Baldinator! Baldinator!” the fans cheered.

Gary met the Lord in spearplay, but to his surprise, he didn’t get an immediate advantage. His opponent was more than a brute. He thunderstepped away the moment the pot entered his aura field, letting the Lord grab it again.

“Black Bolt!” Gary exclaimed, thrusting with lightning.

“Potty Potty!” the Lord yelled, simply raising his pot. The spear thrust right into it. The lightning fizzled out—just as Asim had said, not a single scratch was left on the pot, and somehow, the Lord wasn’t even pushed back. Gary frowned. He launched a series of thundersteps, flashing around his opponent and pelting him with strikes.

“Potty Potty! Potty Potty!” the Lord yelled, spinning crazily to block all attacks. The pot took them like a champ.

He’s absorbing my power! Gary realized. The pot draws half my power. That’s why he’s not pushed back! That can’t be good.

Energy didn’t just disappear. It had to be stored inside the pot, somehow, which would inevitably backfire on Gary. He stopped thrusting wildly and focused on feints. He mixed the first song into his strikes, alternating slow and fast attacks, but it mattered little. The Lord of Pot could keep up. Gary’s power kept getting absorbed by the pot.

“One makes his opponents bald, the other uses a pot and unconventional naming sense! The battle of underdogs is turning into the battle of clowns!” Rollo shouted over the din of weapons, irritating Gary further.

He jumped back to make some distance, but the Lord simply chucked his pot. It flew at Gary as if possessed, leaving a faint green trail behind it. Even when he dodged it, it changed trajectory mid-air to pursue him. Gary found himself running around the arena to evade a pot.

“What a brave warrior you are, Gary Steel!” the Lord yelled, laughing. “Take the hair off my pot if you can!”

The audience laughed, too. The Baldinator cheers weakened. Gary gritted his teeth and turned to face the pot. “Nail Gun!” he yelled. His spear exploded. A series of rapid thrusts nailed the pot, each incapable of pushing it back by itself, but he had many. A dozen strikes later, the pot had gone still, but Gary ran into an unexpected difficulty. Some of his thrusts missed.

It sways oddly in the air! he realized. It possesses some liquid that sloshes around! Damn the Lord of Pot and his silly tactics!

Finally, after using double the number of strikes he had intended, Gary extinguished the pot’s momentum. It fell to the floor. Before he could step on it, it flew in a straight line back to the Lord’s hand.

So it can change direction but not gain speed while it’s away from the Lord. If I stop it, he has to take it back, Gary deduced. Good.

“You know, you have a stupid fucking name,” Gary said out loud. “It should have been the Lord of the Pot! Or Lord of Pots, if you have many! Lord of Pot is just misleading.”

“You know nothing about me!” the Lord exclaimed.

“I know grammar!”

The audience laughed again. The Lord gritted his teeth. “Fool!” he said, chucking the pot again. “If you want to know about my name, I’ll show you! Inside my pot—” The lid flew up as it approached Gary, spilling a torrent of boiling green liquid “—I’M BREWING POT!”

The amount of liquid coming out was much more than what the pot should have contained. It was fast, too. Gary thunderstepped away, but the boiling liquid followed him, breaking up in multiple streams to surround him. The empty pot cluttered to the ground.

“You boiled this your thrusts, Gary Steel! What are you going to do now?” the Lord yelled, laughing crazily. He was waving both hands around like a maestro conducting a symphony of weed. While Gary struggled, the audience had erupted into laughter.

“This is too much! I almost can’t comment from laughing too hard!” Rollo Frollo yelled.

Gary dodged wildly, but speed was no longer his strong suit. The streams caught up with him from all sides. His back ran into the arena wall. He was cornered.

“Shit,” he said.

“I’ll boil your skin and make you so high you can’t even stand!” the Lord said, laughing. “This tournament is mine!”

Gary grabbed his spear from the middle and span it rapidly. “Fan the Spear!” he yelled. A black-and-golden wheel appeared in front of him, shooting the burning liquid in all directions. Most of it splashed on the wall around him, painting it green and mossy. Smaller streams came from the sides, however, and Gary couldn’t defend against them. They covered his body. He screamed. The steam rising from him merged with that coming off the walls, hiding him from sight.

“Hahaha! If you thought I was heavy-handed before, try this, Gary Steel! Goodbye!” the Lord exclaimed, laughing crazily.

The audience gasped. “Stop the fight!” someone yelled. The presiding deacon stood close to the cloud of steam, squinting at it. He ignored the cries to stop the fight. The steam began to clear.

“Let’s see your sorry state!” the Lord shouted. Gradually, however, his smile dropped. His eyes widened. “What!?” he screamed.

Gary was revealed through the steam. He held his spear before him in an act of defiance. His upper robes had been burned, but his hair and eyebrows were still there. His eyes were furious and only a little red. Though his body was riddled with burns, none seemed serious.

“How is that possible!?” the Lord exclaimed.

“You’re a fucking joke,” Gary said, stepping forward. “You mentioned heavy-handed? I’ll show you heavy-handed.”

As he stepped fully out of the steam, his body was revealed, and the audience suddenly fell quiet. The gasps and intermittent chuckles disappeared. Whispers spread.

“Hey, is that…”

“He’s surprisingly handsome…”

“I thought he was just a prankster, but is he really strong?”

“That’s hot, actually…”

Pecks that stuck out. Wide shoulders. Rock-hard abs, and a powerful frame.A perfectly sculpted upper body was revealed through Gary’s missing upper robes, cutting short all the people who had been laughing before.

Many things could be gained through the System or cultivation. A well-toned body could not. It was only achievable through training and hard work, two values that anyone would respect, be it on Earth or the Tower.

Many women in the audience suddenly felt the temperature rise. So did a few men. As Gary stepped out of the steam, surprisingly well-built, with black hair fluttering in the wind and eyes holding boundless fury, he seemed like a god. Black lightning crackled around him. He raised his spear and charged.

“Impossible! How much vitality do you possess!?” the Lord of Pot exclaimed, hastily withdrawing his lidless pot, but Gary was already there.

“Thunder Thrust,” he said, thunderstepping into the Lord with his spear extended.

“Potty Potty!” the Lord exclaimed in panic, but it was no longer the same. The pot lacked its lid. It couldn’t absorb any power. The strike sent the Lord flying, even if blocked, and Gary was already there when he slid to a stop.

“Nail Gun!” he growled. His spear bit out. Three thrusts were blocked by the pot. Another seven were parried by the Lord’s spear. As Gary’s thrusts never stopped coming, however, the pot flew out of the Lord’s hand, and his spear was pushed aside. A flurry of blows rocked his body, cracking bones, tearing skin. Gary stopped them all short to avoid sending his opponent flying. The Lord of Pot’s eyes turned from panicking to empty.

Finally, Gary drew back his spear. “Thrust of Mercy!” he yelled, smashing a merciless thrust into his opponent’s chest. He stopped it short to avoid skewering him, but the full force of his aura followed through, bending the Lord at the waist. He flew back fast, trailing a thin line of blood from his mouth, and smashed into the hard arena wall like a comet.

His body bounced to the sands and stayed there. His glorious jives lay at Gary’s feet.

“That was for my friends, you piece of shit,” he said, and the audience erupted into cheers that shook the stadium.

“Baldinator! Baldinator! Baldinator!”

“Gary Steel! Gary Steel!”

“Winner, Gary “Baldinator” Steel!” Rollo Frollo announced, sounding joyful. “Well fought! That battle was quite a joke, but nobody can deny the skill it involved! Both fighters flew valiantly! We just hope everyone is okay!”

The Lord of Pot still lay at the sand. The deacon was bent over him, then finally stood. “He’ll wake up in a few hours. No permanent damage,” he announced, leading to widespread relief. When no one came to pick up the Lord, the deacon awkwardly carried him to the disciple seats and had him lie at an empty seat.

“Tell whoever occupied this seat that they shouldn’t have left before the end,” he said.

“That was amazing, Gary!” Cog exclaimed.

“Thank you…” Alicia said.

“I wonder if you can take his pot,” Asim said.

“That concludes the first day of the tournament! This was Rollo Frollo, presenting on behalf of the Heaven Reaching clan. We’ll see you tomorrow, everyone, and remember—nothing’s over till it’s over!”
