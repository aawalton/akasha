The group stood in awe at the cave’s entrance, admiring their surroundings and wondering just how cunning these wolves were. To begin with, trees surrounded the whole area, and grew denser the closer to the cave, providing shelter from the snow hails and serving as a visual barrier at the same time.

Snow mounds were piled up all around it, whether by natural means or the beasts’ doing, making it impossible to access it from any other way other than the path they had taken—and even that was hidden behind a set of thick, white bushes that blended almost perfectly with the snowy landscape.

Ascalon pondered thoughtfully, impressed by the canines’s resourcefulness. They had quite the secret ‘base’, and the constant snowing would make it hard for other dungeon dwellers to find it—unless they simply followed after the wolves, like him.

As he wondered about such matters, the wolves headed in, slowly limping into the darkness. Everyone exchanged glances once again, and Lilithra produced her phone and fiddled with it. Soon, the gadget lit up as she turned on the flashlight, illuminating the way inside.

“A–are we sure about this…?” Lyra suddenly asked, her voice shaking with both timidness and wariness. It was understandable—they were, quite literally, heading into the wolven’s den.

“I feel no hostility from them, and I cannot feel any strong aura inside—but I promise to be vigilant!” Ascalon replied with firmness, puffing out his chest in order to reassure Lyra. The felinae sighed softly and nodded shyly, causing Lilithra to pat her head as if by instinct.

“Yeah, let’s be careful.” Lilithra added, ruffling Lyra’s head gleefully. Selorien and Ascalon chuckled at the sight, while the cat girl let out a little yelp under the affection. After a few moments, everyone nodded resolutely, and they headed inside.

The interior of the cave—illuminated only by Lilithra’s gadget—felt damp and humid at first, but grew warmer the further they headed in. The walls and ceiling were full of pointy, gray stalactites, and Ascalon had to bend down to actually traverse the low space.

Surprisingly, they walked for minutes, the long tunnel stretching far underground. Eventually, the way opened up into a bigger section of the cave, where the wolves awaited with narrowed, exhausted eyes. Lilithra raised her phone high, pointing the light up in order to bathe the surroundings in brilliance.

Just then, a collective gasp escaped everyone’s mouths as they took in the breathtaking sight before them. The cave’s ceiling sparkled like blue diamonds, casting a soft, ethereal glow around them. It looked mystical, like a starry sky right out of a fairytale, made all the more enchanting by being hidden deep into the mountain.

While the others admired the view with shimmering, excited eyes, Ascalon focused on the wolves. His senses picked up on, perhaps, the real reason they were guided inside—after all, it was unlikely the beasts had brought them there just to show them their home.

The pair of wolves approached a mound made of grass and old rags, probably scavenged from the surroundings, where a small, round white ball awaited. Under the creatures’ urging gaze, Ascalon advanced toward them, drawing his friends’ silent attention.

With each step, the small, round object twitched. But as he drew closer, Ascalon noticed it wasn’t a mere object, but a trembling ball of fur—a living being! It shivered slightly as one of the wolves nudged it gently with its long nose, causing it to stir awake.

A tiny, enchanting creature slowly rose up. Small, shimmering shapes adorned its coat, as if bathed in stardust, adding to the mystical air around it. Its pristine, white fur glistened under Lilithra’s phone light, as a pair of captivating, icy-blue eyes blinked open, trying to get accustomed to the sudden brightness.

Its fluffy ears perked up, its head tilting slightly as it looked around in confusion, a small yet animated tail starting to sway behind it. It was a cub—a snow wolf pup, and irresistibly adorable at that.

“O-M-G!” Lilithra blurted out, her excitement echoing through the cave and startling everyone, pup and wolves included. The bigger ones glared at her, as if to warn her to not startle their cub, but their weary stares were more pitiful than scary.

“S–sorry…” Lilithra apologized anyway, blushing slightly, as if being scolded. Lyra giggled briefly, while Selorien sighed, glancing at Ascalon. One would think the knight would be jumping with joy at the sight, since he was quite partial to dogs, but no.

Ascalon was simply standing there, in stunned silence. The brilliance of a pup so adorable and fluffy was so overwhelming, so pure, it had struck deep. He didn’t know how to react, nor what he was supposed to do. It was clear the wolves had guided them there to show them their progeny—but why?

The answer came rather soon, as one of the two adult canines collapsed, whimpering slightly. The cub cried out and finally moved, its soft, white fur looking bouncy and most soft. Ascalon clenched his teeth, fighting against the urge to plunge and scoop the puppy in his arms! His body trembled, causing his friends to worry about him—for various reasons.

But there were more important matters at hand, the reason why Ascalon was holding back—the wolves were dying. Were they, perhaps, hoping Ascalon and his friends would take care of the cub…? The knight pondered briefly, slightly shocked at such a thought.

It was true they had fought side by side—well, kind of—but to entrust their cub to them, enemies come from another world? As he mulled over that, the small cub cried once more, the sound of a solid thud echoing against the cave’s walls.

The remaining wolf had fallen to the ground, its breathing slowly ceasing. It looked at the cub with a warm stare, and then, with its remaining willpower, forced itself to look at the group. Its eyes burned with resolution—an unvoiced, yet genuine plea. They didn’t have to cross words, as all of them realized the intentions of the wolf by its unwavering gaze.

The cub whimpered as it noticed its brethren weren’t moving anymore, tears welling up in Lilithra and Lyra’s eyes. Ascalon watched the sad scene in silence, yet he knew, most of all, that such things were normal. Death came for everyone, be it in the battlefield or not, and he had witnessed events like these countless times—with humans and monsters alike.

But somehow, this time, his heart ached so very strongly. His time in New Lumingard had surely softened his nerves, though it was not like he thought that was a bad thing. After all, he appreciated being able to maintain his humanity.

That aside, he stepped forward, causing the small pup to flinch, staring up in clear fear. Ascalon kneeled slowly, a gentle smile adorning his lips as he gradually extended his hand, offering the palm to the cub. The creature looked at him with nervousness and wariness, recoiling slightly and pressing its body against the deceased wolves.

It cried, howling sadly for multiple minutes, with the knight patiently waiting. The rest observed in silence, unsure of what to do. Lilithra in particular, clenched her fists tight against her chest, a pained expression taking over her face as she heard the small creature cry in distress.

Soon enough, the pup exhausted itself, looking at the group with a frightened stare. Ascalon kept grinning, and nodded at the cub, trying to convey his feelings toward it.

“It is okay—we will not hurt you.” Ascalon whispered in a soft, caring voice, the pup tilting its head in suspicion at the words. Yet, after a brief pause, it started to walk over to Ascalon. Had it understood his words? While he pondered the thought, the puppy gently nudged his hand.

Ascalon’s heart melted at the gesture, while Lilithra and Lyra squealed from behind. With slight hesitation, Ascalon caressed the cub’s head, and then quickly scooped him up in his arms.

“I am taking this one home—I shall not hear any complaints.” He suddenly declared, his voice authoritative and firm enough to make his friends take a step back in surprise.

Even though the last time they had told him he couldn’t take a wolf home, they weren’t about to do it twice—not because they were scared of him, he was their friend after all, but because the situation had simply resonated with them.

“B—but… what about its parents? W—won’t they ‘respawn’?” Lyra’s timid voice reached their ears, drawing everyone’s attention. Ascalon let out a thoughtful ‘hmm’, conceding she had raised a valid point.

“That is…” Ascalon began, conflicted. On one hand, he really wanted to take the adorable pup home! But he also felt like he was taking a child from their parents, somehow.

“Well… did you see, huh… Moonstone?” Lilithra chimed in, frowning as she pondered over the matter. Ascalon opened his eye wide, realizing that he had not felt that particular wolf’s presence. Moonstone, the adult canine Ascalon had meant to take from the dungeon the last time. Was it… dead? Or maybe in another place where his senses couldn’t reach?

“Well, we can’t be sure they’re the same monsters every time they, huh, respawn, I think.” Selorien suddenly joined in, caressing his chin thoughtfully.

“Irwin and the rest only said they were living beings—but are we sure they resurrect or respawn? Are they replaced with new, similar monsters?” The elf mused an intriguing point, causing the rest to mull it over in silence.

“But anyway—take it! You know they kinda pushed it onto you.” Selorien smirked as he looked at the pair of dead wolves, causing Ascalon to nod.

“I know! Let’s just come back another time and see for ourselves, duh!” Lilithra suggested, puffing out her chest. That was a great idea! Ascalon smiled gently at her words, his doubts fading as he raised the pup high in the air.

“For now, let us take Moonstone home!” He declared under the warm stares of his friends. The cub was completely confused, but didn’t seem to be as scared or wary as before, even calm enough to suddenly let out a burst of pee aimed at Ascalon’s face.

But the knight was not one to fall for such treacherous attacks, and quickly dodged the liquid while turning the pup the other way, letting it to do its peeing toward a nearby wall.

The mood shifted immediately as his friends started to laugh, taken aback by the sudden scene and the knights' deft reflexes. Ascalon sighed as he grinned in amusement, shaking the dog slightly once it was done with its business. He then turned it around once again, looking into its big, round eyes.

“Moonstone…” Ascalon repeated the name he had selfishly imposed onto the cub, and the creature barked playfully in response. Lilithra’s eyes sparkled with excitement as she grabbed Lyra’s hand and pushed forward. The Felinae was startled for a second, but didn’t complain and followed along.

“H–hey, can we hold it too?” Lilithra asked shyly, unsure if Ascalon would comply. After all, this was a side of him they had barely seen before—perhaps he would end up being too protective of the cub! But contrary to her fears, Ascalon offered the pup without complaints, a big grin plastered on his face.

Both Lilithra and Lyra squealed as they received the cub, showering it with caresses and affection. The creature complained slightly, but soon found their gestures to be quite enjoyable, whimpering with clear glee. Just then, Selorien chuckled in the distance, starting to head out for the exit.

“I’m going ahead~ I’ll wait by the entrance.” The elf spoke with a carefree tone, humming as he walked. Ascalon narrowed his eye, knowing Selorien was just acting tough. He would fawn over Moonstone sooner or later! That was a promise! And as he thought about that, he faced the two girls still engrossed in petting Moonstone.

“Let us go, then! We still need to complete the quest.” He spoke with a tender yet firm tone, causing both Lilithra and Lyra to nod in agreement. They gave Ascalon the cub back, knowing he would keep it safe.

With a new, cute companion on hand, Ascalon and his friends followed the way back, aiming to regroup with Selorien and to finally set out to find the herbs.
