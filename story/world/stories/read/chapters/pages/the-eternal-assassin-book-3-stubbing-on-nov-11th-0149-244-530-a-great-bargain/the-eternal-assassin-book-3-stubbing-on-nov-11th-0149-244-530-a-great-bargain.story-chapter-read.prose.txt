Asher Recalled back to their room in the royal palace faster than ever before. Samantha appeared to be out, most likely still off practicing her archery while flying around, but he barely even thought about that. Throwing himself down onto the couch, he focused on his interface.

Spend 100,000 shards to evolve Judgement?

“Hell yes,” he muttered, tensing in anticipation of finally finishing up the Stage of Honing. He’d even collected another three hundred and sixty eight thousand shards from his quick rampage through what remained of Dormaul’s Assassin’s Guild, which meant even with this skill evolution, he still had more shards than he’d started with.

Shards: 4,892,676

Judgement (passive) - Lvl 21

Tier 1: View the weight of a soul.

Tier 2: Gain a sense of a soul’s most used skills.

Huh… that’s an interesting evolu—

Asher’s thoughts were scattered as a burst of power erupted out from the center of his body. It felt as though his very soul had sent an electric pulse of energy out from his core, and his body jerked back into the couch as he nearly lost control of his muscles for a moment. The pulse traveled down to the tips of his fingers and toes before snapping right back and reabsorbing into his soul. He felt the faintest sense of his soul and the energy contained within growing tighter, more compact almost, as though it had grown denser. The unexpected sensation was gone nearly as fast as it had arrived, and Asher laughed as he shook his head.

Alright, that was one hell of a rush!

Letting out a content sigh, Asher pulled up his full interface.

Name: Asher Holden

Class: Assassin

Unique classes available: Demon Slayer, Awakened Beast Hunter, Assassin, Librarian, Guardian

Element: Spatial

Ability: Astral Dip - Lvl 35

Ability: Complete Resilience - Lvl 36

Ability: Eternal Mark - Lvl 28

Ability: Permanence - Lvl 24

Ability: Sever the Thread - Lvl 29

Resistance: Darkness - Tier 2, Truth - Tier 2, Scrying - Tier 2

Shards: 4,892,676

“The Stage of Bleed, huh?” he muttered, scanning over how far he’d come. Finally, after fighting who knew how many battles and consuming a truly astronomical number of shards, he’d completed both the Stage of Discovery and the Stage of Honing. Actually having all of his skills evolved into the second tier was absolutely wild to him. Especially when he could still so vividly recall his very first day in this world, having nothing more than a single element and skill to his name.

Almost just as crazy was the fact that a few of his skills were already nearing the next level cap. Astral Dip, Eternal Regeneration, and Complete Resilience were all within five levels of reaching level 40. They would each require a cool, million shards to evolve, but they would receive a third-tier effect in exchange.

Alright, we’re finally here, he thought, scanning over his already large list of skills as he sighed. I have fifteen skills already, and I’ll end up developing three more as I progress through the Stage of Bleed. That means I need eighteen million shards to evolve all of them to the third tier. Time to start hoarding.

The fact that he already had enough shards on him to evolve his three skills closest to hitting the cap as soon as he could was reassuring, but it was merely a drop in the bucket. The fresh greater demon corpse they got from assaulting Zygrul’s lair should net them another nice chunk of shards, but they’d already agreed to split the profits from that four ways, seeing as all four of them had worked together to take the greater demon down. If anything, Asher was the one getting the better end of the deal, as he’d really only shown up at the very end of the fight to deal the finishing blow.

Getting to his feet, Asher walked around the room a little bit, staring at his interface while he paced as if hoping that random energetic pulse wasn’t the only surprise waiting for him. He knew that his three blended skills would arrive in due time, but a small part of him had held out hope that at least one of them would be given to him the moment he reached the Stage of Bleed. Theorizing just what those skills would end up being was always a fun way to pass the time, and he was more excited than ever to soon have three entirely new abilities to mess around with. Evolving his already owned skills was a great way to add tools to his arsenal, but there was something to be said about gaining totally new skills that never got old.

While pacing around and trying to mentally encourage the universe to give him the first of his blended skills, Asher was surprised by a knock at the door. A quick check of his Eternal Mark told him Samantha was still out near the outskirts of the city, and she wouldn’t have bothered knocking on the door to their bedroom anyway. Curious, Asher walked over, pulling it open and blinking at the unexpected visitor.

“Richard?” Asher asked more than said, shocked to find the wizard standing nervously in his doorway. After the whole ordeal with the wild realm, Richard had stuck to his agreement and hadn’t once bothered Asher on his own accord. He’d even helped Asher try to figure out what was with the strange gemstone dust covering Zygrul’s journals, before admitting he had no idea what the technique was. While Asher certainly wouldn’t call the two of them friends, they were at least neutral acquaintances with one another at this point.

“Can I come in?” Richard asked, his tone hesitant and unsure, as though he’d never once bothered asking someone for permission before barging into their place in his entire life.

“Sure,” Asher said slowly, holding the door open and letting the wizard enter. Nodding his thanks, Richard stepped into the apartment, peering around before taking a seat at the table.

“Congratulations on hitting the Stage of Bleed,” Richard said, his eyes seeming to sparkle as he stared at Asher. “You’ve been in this world for what, two or three months? Truly remarkable how quickly you’ve progressed.”

“Thank you. It’s certainly been a journey,” Asher chuckled, now more curious than ever as he took the seat across from his surprise guest. This was the first time he’d ever heard Richard actually compliment someone, let alone him. The wizard was clearly up to something, and while he wanted to know what that was, he was also still so excited about hitting the next stage that he couldn’t help but focus on the subject. “Do you have all three of your blended skills already? Actually, what stage are you even in?”

“I’m in the Stage of Power, so yes, I have all of my blended skills,” Richard confirmed. Just like the first time Asher had laid eyes on him, Richard’s aura was little more than a lazy gray cloud, slowly drifting about the man’s form with little substance. Though now, if he focused, he could actually tell that there was a much deeper depth to that cloud than at first glance. It was almost like the cloud stretched on forever, going deeper and deeper within itself. The sensation wasn’t like anything Asher had ever experienced before, and he shook his head as he tried to make sense of it.

“Do you have a skill that… condenses your thoughts, or something?”

“In fact I do,” Richard nodded, not at all surprised by the question. “I have the Knowledge element, and one of my passive skills that I treasure most is Alternate Thought. At this point, I functionally have three separate brains all working around the clock at the same time. I most frequently have two of them working on solving advanced magical problems, while my primary one focuses on relaxing to keep me sane.”

“Wow, that sounds insanely useful for a researcher,” Asher admitted. “I didn’t realize you had the Knowledge element as well.”

“In some cultures, that would make us almost family,” Richard said, chuckling nervously as he fidgeted with his hands. “Not in the Noala Kingdom, of course, but there are countries with entire clans founded around a single element. Without one, people aren’t allowed to join, and even their children are cast out if they do not earn that origin element. They can come back down the line if they manage to find and absorb it for themselves, but it is still a rather harsh lesson in parenting if you ask me.”

“While that is actually super interesting… Why are you here, Richard?” Asher asked, deciding it was time to get to the heart of the matter. He’d never seen the wizard ramble on like this, or look so strangely unsure of himself. Normally, the man waltzed around like he owned the place, throwing his status of ‘national asset’ around like he was the High Prince of the entire kingdom instead of Donvath. Seeing this other side of him was honestly pretty jarring, and Asher had to admit he didn’t like it.

Sucking in a deep breath, Richard gave him a small, pained smile. “So… I actually had something of a favor I wanted to ask of you. It would push my magical learnings ahead by years, and I’d seriously owe you one for the future. Having the kingdom’s most powerful wizard owing you a favor is no small thing, let me tell you.”

“What’s the favor?” Asher asked, cutting through the pointless fluff as he raised an eyebrow.

“I know you’re not a wizard, so you wouldn’t understand this, but there is quite a lot that goes into learning new spells and about the universe as a whole,” Richard continued, as though he hadn’t even heard Asher’s question. “Really, the expectation as a community is that we help one another out. I know we had our slight… accident, the other day, but I like to think we’ve actually bonded over that! Two national assets against the world, you know what I mean?”

“Richard…” Asher said, beginning to grow concerned as he stared at the sweating wizard. “...What’s the favor you want?”

“Oh, nothing impossible, or anything like that,” Richard said, tapping his fingers together as he cleared his throat. “I was just wondering if… you could maybe… withdraw the greater demon from the Grand Auction and give it to me instead!”

This last line was said hurriedly in a single breath, and Richard panted, sinking down in his chair a bit as he finally got the ask out. Asher could only stare at the man in response, whose nervous smile had grown much wider as he waited.

“Just to make sure I understand what you’re asking. After throwing me into the wild realm and leaving me there for a couple of hours to fend for myself. You now want me to hand over something that you and I both know is worth somewhere around ten million shards? For a favor?”

“In my defense, we’ve already established it was your skill that prevented me from retrieving you at our agreed upon time,” Richard corrected, clearing his throat and wincing under Asher’s continued stare. “That said! This wouldn’t just benefit me, I’ll have you know. With an actual greater demon corpse to study, I should be able to figure out any hidden weaknesses they might have, or devise a skill to better counteract them beyond your current Lesser Realm Sight! Maybe even better understand that strange weight that they naturally carry!”

“Richard, I’ve already killed two of the greater demons,” Asher pointed out. “Do you really think I need to know any more of their weaknesses? You know I just hit the Stage of Bleed, I really need to start stockpiling shards more than anything else right now.”

“I have four million shards saved up I can give you!” Richard added, dabbing at his forehead with a handkerchief as he pleaded his case. “Four million shards, and a favor from a skilled wizard! I have hundreds of skills I’ve learned to cast with my magic over the years, and I am one of the most learned men in the entire kingdom! Please, as much as I hate to admit it, I’m getting on in age, and shaving years off of my research is worth more to me than anything!”

Asher could only shake his head and sigh as he leaned back in his chair. It was obvious this meant a lot to Richard, and it really wasn’t that bad of a deal. Bellod, Asher’s personal auction master who handled his items’ appraisals and took care of all the annoying stuff, had already warned him that this second greater demon corpse almost certainly wouldn’t go for as much as the first. The downside of putting two of them up for auction so close to one another was that buyers were going to suspect that more would follow. According to Bellod, they’d be lucky for this second corpse to hit seven or eight million shards. Which meant half of that and getting in good with Richard wasn’t that bad of a deal at all. The wizard already owed him one for the whole wild realm fiasco, but Asher didn’t see any harm in doubling down on that.

“Four million shards… and a no-questions-asked favor for each member of my team, not just me,” Asher countered, crossing his arms. “We were all going to split the shards anyway, so this is the only way that’s fair. I’ll also need to check with them before I actually agree to this.”

“Four million shards and four favors,” Richard said, positively beaming at the realization he might actually get his hands on the greater demon corpse. “Thank you, Asher, you have no idea how much this means to me.”

“Two national assets against the world, right?” Asher repeated, unable to stop himself from grinning as Richard hurriedly nodded. It was hard to believe that the excited man sitting before him had been one of the rudest, most abrasive people Asher had ever met only a few weeks ago.

Nothing a little unintentional kidnapping and trading of corpses couldn’t solve!
