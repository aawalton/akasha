“That was simultaneously satisfying and aggravating,” Ben grumbled, switching to another racing drone.

As two grasshoppers dove down at her like kamikaze pilots, she jumped backwards as a dust cloud exploded around them. She dove in to crush a grasshopper with her bat, but the other grasshopper immediately leapt at her from the side, forcing her to roll out of the way. Some dust got on her shoulder, and she could hear it eating at her clothes. As some of it touched her skin, she felt little stabs of pain.

She ran back toward a tree and waited for them to veer around it. As one passed by, she smashed downward, crushing its back and wings. She immediately covered her face with her arm and backed away from the dust cloud that exploded off its body.

It wasn’t exciting. It wasn’t easy. The bug she hit still wiggled its body around to jump at her. She needed to get mad. All she felt was insecure about her place in this team.

Ridley spun to see a grasshopper leaping at her face.

Pop! Pop!
