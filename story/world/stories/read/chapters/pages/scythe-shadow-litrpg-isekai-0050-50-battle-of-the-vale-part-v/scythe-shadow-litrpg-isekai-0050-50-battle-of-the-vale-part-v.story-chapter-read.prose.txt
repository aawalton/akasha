The Emerald orb flew toward me. The throw by Jericho had been good, but the balcony was too far away. It wouldn't reach.

But I could reach it.

I Aero Shifted up into the air toward the tumbling Will. The darkness of impending death crept at the edges of my vision, growing larger, swallowing me like the mouth of a giant whale. Soon I could see nothing but small pinpricks in the center of my eyes, the soft glow of the Emerald Will visible there.

Of course, none of this would matter without my scythe, laying on the floor in the tower where it'd been left behind when Pirhani pulled me away. I'd called it the moment I saw the green orb. And I could feel it approaching now, spinning through the city toward me. Getting closer.

So. Close.

The Will was within arms reach now. I exited my Aero Shift and reached out my hand—not to the orb, but to the side. My scythe thwunked into my palm with the force of a cannonball. I closed my carapaced fingers around it and swung.

The blade shattered the flying Will into hundreds of little green shards. Glowing Emerald essence burst out, but was immediately sucked into my scythe.

I began my fall back toward the arena floor. My vision was completely gone now. My body weak. I was dying. On death's doorstep. The door opening before me.

But I am Death.

Mid-flight, with the last tendril of life in my veins, I shoved the scythe through my chest and into my own cracked Will.

[Advancement to Emerald Rank commencing…]

The setting of my advancement was like the first time, when I'd used the Amethyst Will during my battle with Zeus. This time, instead of purple, the misty haze around me was green like a noxious gas. Amid the haze, brighter waves of particles flowed like a gentle tide. They seemed to follow me, changing direction as I waded through the emptiness.

Each wave came with a spark of emotion. There for an instant, then gone. A zap of grief. A jolt of love. A zing of regret.

The waves raged faster, larger, washing over me as if fueled by a hurricane. There was no substance or force behind them, aside from the feelings they carried. The bigger the wave the more intense the experience.

A surge of anger, so fierce I wanted to rip something in two. Yet so hollow. I didn't know the source, just the feeling. I grabbed my arm, ready to tear. Then another wave hit. Tears streamed down my cheeks as I collapsed to the non-existent ground.

I closed my eyes and wept.

Then it was gone.

No longer in the void among the mist.

I stood at a sink, scrubbing dishes with a soapy pink sponge. The warm water washed over my hands. I couldn't control the motions, just observe them, like last time.

And like last time, I knew how this would end.

This alternate reality—a peak at how my life could've played out differently.

Then I realized the body I inhabited had painted fingernails. And… breasts! Was I— No. No, that's not what this was. I had it all wrong.

This confirmed it. I wasn't viewing an alternate ending of my own life.

I was viewing someone else's life.

Something was going to happen here, like before with the car accident when I advanced to Amethyst. What I now understood to be a sin that I'd hopefully prevented by taking this Will.

Someone behind me said something I couldn't make out. My body—this woman's body—turned. The plate I/she had been holding slipped from my/her fingers. It shattered on the floor like the Emerald Will I'd just sliced to pieces.

A man stood there holding a large kitchen knife. He plunged it into my stomach. Flashbacks of Pirhani gouging me overwhelmed my mind. Then a different memory surfaced, unbidden. Unwanted. Long locked away in the recesses of my mind.

As the man pulled the knife out, he was no longer a man, but a young boy.

A young… me.

The knife was bloody in his hand, red liquid dripping over his fingers to his wrist, staining the sleeve of his shirt. I held my stomach, but couldn't contain the blood. My hands came away covered and sticky. It soaked through my shirt and pooled on the white carpet beneath me.

The boy-me smirked.

No. This was all wrong. This wasn't how it happened. This—

Then the memory was gone and the boy was a man again. Knife still bloody, face still smirking. I dropped to a slick tiled floor, my hands slipping in the blood as I tried to catch myself.

I lay there, breathing slowing. The man stood over me, saying words that were somehow incomprehensible to me. At one point laughing. Then he knelt beside me and watched me silently, reveling in the agony and emptiness he wrought.

It too a long time to die this way. Bleeding out onto a floor.

It'd taken a long time for my father too… My real, biological father…

When I/she finally succumbed, everything flashed black.

And I returned to Penumbra.

I woke, laying splayed on my back in the arena dirt. A dome of crystalline Emerald surrounded me. Little time must have passed because Pirhani still stood in the exact spot I remembered. Although…

Shit. Something had changed. The doors in the colosseum stands had shattered. Dozens of Unchained Sins now prowled in the stands, waiting for an order. From Amber to Emerald, even a Sapphire or two.

No one else had moved to take action. They all stared at me.

I stood and broke the bubble.

"Ah, he's back," Pirhani said. No one else daring to speak after her display of power and the looming threat in the stands.

I took a step toward her.

"Congratulations on your advancement to Emerald, Zephyr. Truly remarkable what you've accomplished in so little time. Inspirational really."

I took another step.

"Unfortunately, as dramatic as your little stunt was, you know it's all for nought. You could advance a dozen more ranks and I'd still just kill you again. You know this. Valciferous knows this. Your foolish friends know this."

I continued toward her.

"Yes. Return to me. I'll make the end quicker this time. I promise you that. Nothing you can do will change the result here."

I stopped.

She was right. Despite everything, nothing I'd done had harmed her. My Scythe. Aero Shift. Life Drain. Prismatic Bow. Even Lancelot had failed to leave a mark on the goddess. Being a rank higher wouldn't change that.

There was only one thing that might. One trick I hadn't tried. One skill I didn't have before.

It was a mystery. A Unique Skill. Effect unknown.

But it was my last gambit.

If it didn't work I would be dead. All the Unchained would be released on my friends and probably destroy them. There were just too many.

I clenched my fists, more determined than ever, feeling in my heart that it would work. That for some reason, I'd been given this skill in this moment for a purpose. For this purpose.

"We'll see," I said.

Then I activated Threads of Fate.
