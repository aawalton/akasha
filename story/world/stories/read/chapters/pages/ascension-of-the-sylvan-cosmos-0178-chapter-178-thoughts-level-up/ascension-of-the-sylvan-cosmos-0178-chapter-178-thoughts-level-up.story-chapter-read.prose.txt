The road management was a combination of Liu Shu's past absorbed knowledge and new ideas that the Explorers hired by Verdantia pushed forward. Liu Shu also listened to the people in the town about their complaints, learning and slowly changing the designs.

Liu Shu pondered while looking at the town. 'Everything looks well… What about waste management? Is everything being safely disposed of?'

Her vision went around the toilets of the town, ignoring it whenever she found a naked person inside. 'Hm~, everything seems good. The small creatures were moving around with their day to day life, the Rose Fairies being born almost daily in the garden by her side, the new life and old life. Everything developing around her canopy moved fast as if everyone was rushing everywhere. 'I don't like it.'

Liu Shu didn't like this feeling. It made her feel anxious. There was a person running in one part, another tripping on the other side. Of course, most were calm, but the rushing people were like lightbulbs in a dark forest.

She could efficiently process everything; she could see everything, but it was highly stimulating for her mind because she was trying to accelerate those processes. 'I am feeling rushed because of them.' The World Tree looked at the existences around her with indifferent eyes. 'Because I care, I am feeling part of their sensations. I am feeling empathy. Everyone is nervous about the town opening soon, so they don't stop moving around.'

Liu Shu hummed. 'I should ignore them.' Her mind looked at everything in her <World Tree Eye> range, and she muttered. 'Focusing on the town is not what I should do. What happens in the town is not my problem.'

Liu Shu detached herself and thought. 'I need to look outward. I need to ignore them.' Her mind suddenly felt like someone pinched it, and that woman's voice echoed in her mind again.

[Little one, the path ahead is difficult, but I hope you don't ignore those close and sincere to you in search for greater strength.]

'I know. I am not ignoring them.' Liu Shu humphed. 'But those people are not close to me. I just need to take care of Flor, Ignatia, Verdantia, Cinderielle, Tempestria, Clavel, Amapola… and others that are close to them… So, their people, and therefore, the town as well…'

Liu Shu looked into the ground, traveling her massive root network without aim. 'It's difficult… I like being left alone and… stretch out!' Liu Shu giggled. 'I like to grow tall! I like fruits! I like it when my roots pierce the hard rock to advance!'

Liu Shu stopped and asked softly. 'Who are you? Why did you tell me those words? It makes everything harder…' Liu Shu remembered the Willow Tree by the lake's side, and she sighed. 'Even my Mom there doesn't sound like you. However, I feel so much more closeness to you than I do to my real Mom.'

Liu Shu discovered it long ago. She had noticed that the Willow Tree by the lake's side was her biological mother, that her body grew from one of her seeds, which probably got eaten by a bird and then dropped where she was. 'I couldn't grow tall at first because I was not Leveling Up and because I didn't have enough water. Without becoming a World Tree, I would've probably dried out by now…'

Liu Shu saw and felt her roots digging through the hard rocks below her, going deeper and deeper. She asked again. 'Who are you?'

However, no answer came. Liu Shu expected it, so she didn't mind. 'Take care of those close to me~. That's something I should do. To take care of them, I need strength, not prettier fruits… Nature's Gift can wait. I should Level Up.' Liu Shu smiled to herself. 'When I can relax, I can grow fruits and stretch leisurely. Flor might praise me again when I become taller instead of always looking worried!'

The World Tree felt revitalized and returned to her senses. Then, she called her. 'Flor!'

The Rose Fairy Queen heard the always soothing voice of the World Tree and smiled. "What's wrong, Liu Shu?"

'Hehe~, I can Level Up! Do I do it now?'

Flor paused, and her eyebrows subconsciously locked together. "Level Up… Will your camouflage last during the Level Up?"

Liu Shu, who expected praise, paused and looked at Flor's worried face. After a few seconds, she commented. "It should work. No one should notice if I Level Up slower than usual, and as long as they do not look at me too deeply. However, the entire town will be enveloped in my aura. So, hiding it completely is impossible. My Level Up affects everything, after all."

Flor hummed and crossed her arms. "I see. Well, it's not like you can get stuck in Level 13. We can always take this situation as an experiment. Let me warn the people of your Level Up so that they are not startled."

Liu Shu answered. "Okay~."

Flor blinked and looked outside of the window. She felt strange for a second, so she asked. "Is everything okay, Liu Shu?"

Liu Shu's answer reached Flor after a few moments, accompanied by her usual giggle. "Yes! I'll go and start my Level Up! I will take a few days."

Flor tilted her head. 'Is it my imagination? Liu Shu sounded a bit sad for a second there, no?'

The World Tree then looked at her system and started pouring her energy into her Level.
