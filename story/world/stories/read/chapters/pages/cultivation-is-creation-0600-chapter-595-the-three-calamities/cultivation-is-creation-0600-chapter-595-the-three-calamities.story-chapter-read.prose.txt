The moment we placed our foot on step ninety, I noticed the differences immediately.

The spiritual pressure was heavier than anything we’d felt before, the silver veining in the stone beat faster, hell, even the air tasted wrong. And this was all a prelude to the materialisation of the three figures who formed in the centre of the massive step.

The first man had clearly participated in countless battles, if his battered armor was to be trusted. On his back, there was an enormous cleaver-style blade that was so large that it shouldn’t be possible to wield even with two hands.

The second man was bulky, and his presence filled space, giving the impression of a mountain that had been given human form. The texture of the skin was that of weathered stone. The stubborn expression on his face made me instinctively know that he wouldn't be moved unless he chose to.

As for the final figure, he seemed younger than the others, but with the way his stances shifted constantly, it was clear that didn’t mean he had less experience. In fact, the contrary could be true. This man seemed to be adaptation made manifest. At least that’s the vibe that I got from him.

"I am Tian Feng," the first one said in a rough, grinding stone type of voice. "Heaven's Edge."

"Di Heng." The second rumbled when he spoke, which made the air tremble. "Earth Eternal."

"Ren Sheng." The third called out, his eyes crinkling into a smile. "Human Life."

"The Three Calamities,” Song Wei's eyes narrowed.

It seemed that the title wasn’t something the group was fond of by their reactions: Tian Feng's expression darkened as his jaw tightened, Di Heng’s face remained as impassive as his body was immovable, and even though Ren Sheng kept smiling, his eyes sharpened.

"We prefer to think of ourselves as the Three Pillars," Ren Sheng laughed. "Much more positive, don't you think? Calamity sounds so... destructive."

"It's accurate," Shen Bolin said quietly.

“Well,” Ren Sheng murmured, shrugging. "I suppose we'll have to agree to disagree."

That was the only warning we got because that was when their Laws activated at the same time.

Tian Feng: "All Things Have An Edge."

Di Heng: "What Stands Cannot Fall."

Ren Sheng: "Evolution Never Stops."

Instantly, the pressure tripled, forcing my knees to buckle before I flooded spiritual energy into my channels to stabilize my body. Li Na wasn’t so lucky, the poor girl actually collapsed to the ground, which didn’t surprise me because three Peak Lucid Lawbearer Laws activating at once was more than most Oneiric Sovereigns could withstand.

"Eternal Triangle Formation."

They moved into position with Tian Feng taking the lead, his massive cleaver resting across his shoulders, Di Heng positioned himself at the rear, arms crossed like he had all the time in the world, and Ren Sheng settled into the center between them, his stance shifting every few seconds as he evaluated us.

The formation created perfect synergy between their different specializations: offense, defense, and adaptation working together as seamlessly as a body's organs.

Song Wei and Shen Bolin exchanged a glance before they attacked together.

Silver energy erupted from Song Wei's body, bending probability around Tian Feng so that when offensive cultivator's next strike should have connected perfectly, it instead missed by millimeters as reality rewrote itself.

Shen Bolin's blade manifested from dream qi, seven copies attacking from different angles.

Tian Feng's cleaver caught the real sword among the copies. The impact released a shockwave that cracked the stone beneath their feet, causing fragments of black dream-stone to fly outward, with some pieces still glowing with silver veining.

Song Wei tried to use this opportunity to attack Tian Feng from the side, but Di Heng was already there with his massive form blocking the follow-up attack. The silver energy crashed against him like waves against a cliff, but he didn't even flinch.

Ren Sheng watched from the center, not really doing anything, but something strange was going on. For a moment he'd stand like Tian Feng, weight forward and aggressive. Then he'd shift to mirror Di Heng's grounded immobility. Then he'd flow into something else.

"He is copying what he sees," Tian said quietly from his position near Xie Ling. "Learning techniques in real time."

What an interesting ability. The Law let him adapt to any combat situation instantly because he was recording everything every movement, every technique, every tactical decision, and developing counters before we could use the same approach twice.

"We need to separate them," Xie Ling said. Ice crystals formed around her hands. "Break the formation."

“We won’t be able to.” Song Wei shook his head sharply. "We’ve tried before but it doesn't work."

"Then we overwhelm them like we did with the couple," Shen Bolin said, flaring spiritual pressure outward. "If all of us attack at the same time, we have a chance."

I wasn’t so sure about that. It sounded like a terrible plan because brute force didn’t usually work against opponents who specialized in adaptation and defense, but we didn't have better options, so we attacked.

I activated my transformation, causing vines to erupt from my arm. Xie Ling created a blizzard of razor-sharp ice. Tian's sword work accelerated to speeds that blurred his movements. Li Na's formations spread across the stone beneath our feet, trying to disrupt the Eternal Triangle's perfect positioning.

Unfortunately, like I had expected, the Three Calamities adapted immediately.

Tian Feng's cleaver cut through my vines like they were grass, which made me realise that his Law made everything have an edge, this included his spiritual pressure as well. Even the air around him was sharp enough to draw blood, and being within ten meters of him was like standing in a field of invisible blades that pressed against exposed skin.

Xie Ling’s ice storm hit Di Heng’s body, but didn’t penetrate, not even an inch. His Law ensured that nothing could move him, which apparently extended to energy trying to harm him.

As for Tian’s sword work, it was easily dismantled by Ren Sheng copying our own techniques. He threw sword strikes that matched Tian's precision, manifested ice that rivaled Xie Ling's control, and even created vine constructs.

However, despite being able to accurately imitate the others’ techniques, I noticed that while the vines that Ren Sheng manifested looked exactly like my own, they weren’t empowered by the raw devouring hunger of red sun energy.

This reminded me of the shapeshifter in the Fallen Realm when it had manifested the Dao of Envy itself, but there was a big difference between them.

As for Ren Sheng, his Law, Evolution Never Stops, was limited to the energy type he cultivated. Dream qi couldn't imitate red sun energy, especially since the two suns energy was a higher tier energy than dream qi.

As for a Dao, it transcended energy types as it was a principle of reality itself so it was able to replicate the two sun energies, but even that being couldn't handle the power and ended up imploding.

So, when Ren Sheng's vines lashed toward me and I blocked with my own, the difference was immediately apparent. With ease, my thorns pierced through his constructs as the red sun energy devoured the dream qi holding them together.

"Your vines feel different from mine," Ren Sheng murmured in confusion. "There's something underneath that I can't quite replicate."

I didn’t respond because there was no way I was going to explain why to someone who was trying to kill me. But despite this small victory, we were still losing, and we were losing badly if I was being honest.

Without a doubt, the Three Calamities had been fighting together for a very long time, I knew this because they didn't need to communicate to fight as one body. Tian Feng would create an opening with overwhelming offense. Di Heng would protect any gaps in their defense. Ren Sheng would adapt to whatever we threw at them and immediately deploy counters.

Song Wei's spiritual pressure exploded outward. "Probability Collapses To Certainty."

Everything changed as his Law forced one specific outcome: the Three Calamities' formation breaking. For just a moment, they were pulled apart by reality itself rewriting their positions. Tian Feng stumbled forward, Di Heng slid backward, Ren Sheng found himself three meters from where he'd been standing.

The Eternal Triangle shattered, and Shen Bolin didn't waste the opening. "Dreams Made Real."

Seven copies of himself manifested in the space between the separated cultivators with all of them attacking from different angles.

Tian Feng tried to reform the triangle, but Song Wei's Law kept pulling them apart.

So, every time they moved together, probability made them miss each other by crucial centimeters.

The formation that had protected them for centuries was broken.

The battle escalated into chaos as techniques flew in every direction without coordination.

I threw what I could at them. More vines, enhanced with red sun energy but disguised as nightmare cultivation. The thorns pierced Di Heng's defenses for the first time, enough to draw a thin line of blood across his massive arm.

He looked down at the wound, then at me, and his eyes narrowed with respect or killing intent.

"Interesting," he rumbled, and he crossed the distance between us in three, earth-shaking steps.

As his fist came at my face with the weight of an avalanche behind it, I used Blink Step.

His fist hit empty air where I'd been standing and created a crater five meters wide as I teleported ten meters to the side.

"Fast," he said. "But speed doesn't matter because I am inevitable."

He came at me again. This time I couldn't dodge. His reach was too long. His speed had increased to match mine. The adaptation specialist must have shared data with the defensive specialist because Di Heng now moved like Ren Sheng had taught him.

His palm caught my chest, cracking something as I was sent flying backward.

I hit the ground hard, rolled, and came up bleeding from my mouth.

Di Heng was already moving. Following up on his advantage. His massive form covered the distance between us in two strides. His fist descended toward my head, and at this range, with my injuries, I couldn't dodge or teleport fast enough.

This was it. If I didn't do anything, I would die here.

Well, I guess it was time to use qi and even up the playing field.
