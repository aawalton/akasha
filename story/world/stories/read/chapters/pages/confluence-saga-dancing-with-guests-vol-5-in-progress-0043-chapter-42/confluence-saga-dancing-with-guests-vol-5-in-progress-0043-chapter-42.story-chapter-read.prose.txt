The overlap between LowPonds such as Earth and the cultures of the WidePond as a whole are fascinating. Unawakened Earth has a multitude of cultures and mythologies. Similar cultures exist throughout the WidePond, but tend to be centralized as a single culture per Pond. The mythologies often resemble aspects of Awakened society that Unawakened should have no awareness of.

This is hardly unique to Earth, most WidePonds have these spreads of cultures and similar tales. Whether the LowPonds are the root of these cultures, or Awakened form their origins is uncertain. It may even be both.
