Locksmith wasn't the type to fight seriously; he didn't need to. His doors left his enemies bewildered enough that his combat skills weren't really tested. He certainly wasn't the type to stop training, either; you never know when you'll encounter a Emblem user.

Despite this, before being a fighter, he was first and foremost a thief. Perhaps even a magician, depending on the perspective. He was as skilled with his knives as he was at sneaking, and that said a lot about his actions as a Locksmith. What Harry realized, however, was that they would be of no use whatsoever in that battle.

As soon as he made a passage and appeared before Matthews, he was startled to find his brother waiting for him, his fist already clenched and ready to make contact with Harry's jaw. He appeared to the left of the skeleton, who immediately launched a curved attack, narrowly missing Locksmith's head.

He didn't know exactly where the young thief would appear, so he launched a general attack, hoping to hit him. Harry then shifted to Harry's side, his smile now on display. This only made Matthews even angrier as he tried to grab him by the tie. What he grabbed, however, was his own neck, as his hand now passed through a door the Locksmith had generated in front of his chest.

Matthews yelled at the sky, his fists still tightly clenched. "Fight like a man!"

Taking a step back, Harry replied. "You want to talk about manly dignity!? You have two Emblems!"

The skeleton, seeing Harry a few steps ahead, as if wanting to prove a point, lunged at the thief. He used the strength of his fists to create a propulsive force and launch himself forward, like Goldman and Daniel did. Harry, however, this time, was prepared. As soon as the man made the move, the Locksmith was already falling into one of his doors, narrowly escaping his brother's clutches.

Following his winding path, Matthews fell behind the door, determined not to lose sight of him. How annoying! He muttered to himself, grasping the vacuum left by Harry. He braced himself against the edges of his doors before they disappeared, forcing the young thief to twist them at counter intuitive angles.

Having trained for many years with these skills, the change of position wasn't a problem for Harry. He had only been a little desperate with Plankton's Timon Emblem because he wasn't under control. In this situation, however, he knew that... What!? How is he following me! Harry was startled when he looked back and saw the empty yet furious stares of his brother, almost grabbing him as if he were death itself.

At that moment, Harry decided to use his surroundings. Spotting, amidst his leaps, a shell falling from the boat, he threw himself towards the piece of Meta with the purpose of using it as armor.

As soon as he emerged from a passage, he created two doors that shared a passage in front of him. Before Matthews had enough time to react, he fell into the gap in another passage, one that was higher up. His brother, with a grunt, when he realized what had happened, found himself falling inside the door, which led to the back.

"...How confusing...!" He found himself muttering once again as he searched for Harry, thinking he was about to escape. He spotted the man in mid-air, rising to grab something. No... Not yet. He made a smooth movement before leaping, as if his punches were some kind of fuel for his speed. He threw himself into the air and prepared a precise punch to Harry's stomach.

The young thief then turned at the same moment and struck him squarely in the face with his armor while dodging to the side. Damn it! I wanted to use it as a shield, but now, seeing it up close, I see he could have destroyed it...

Taking advantage of Matthews' temporarily vulnerable state, the Locksmith grabbed the long coat covering him and pushed him towards one of his doors leading to the ground floor.

Seeing that this had barely hurt him, Harry tried to keep his distance until he could think of a strategy, now that he had an aerial advantage. Not for long...

At first, he had even considered using his closed doors, one that hadn't yet been joined with another to create a passage, as a shield. He couldn't be sure if that punch would destroy the carapace, but the pressure coming from Matthews told him it would. He has the Emblem of the Fist... Harry pondered. Well, if he has two Emblems, he can certainly withstand some of his own medicine... can't he?

Determined, the Locksmith, before his brother could get up, advanced towards the skeleton. Swoosh! With a sudden movement, Matthews freed himself from his prostration and punched the air once more. Or so he thought. What he felt, however, was a severe pain from a blow directly to his jaw.

"Argh!" The skeleton felt the connection his fist made and quickly deduced where the attack had come from. "You son of a—"

"Don't finish." Harry said with a grin, but the tone of his voice betrayed that appearance.

Like thunder on a sunny day, Matthews's fury was silent, but no less dangerous. "Let's see what you're made out of."

"What do you want me to say? Skin, lips, ears... Hair?" Harry joked. "I'm just trying to find what would upset you the most. Little guy."

Even Harry knew he should not have said it. What followed was perhaps the most chaotic fight that had ever taken place in that arena. From the outside, one could only see blurs and gusts of air being deflected. From above and below, the directions shifted as much as Harry's confidence in the outcome of the fight.

He rarely fought an opponent as ferocious as Matthews. When he assaulted or engaged in combat with military members or personal guards of nobles, their doors solved the problem. If it was someone larger, a few punches from themselves were enough to knock them down. Matthews had survived both. Like a gorilla, he was as strong as he was durable; Harry could see it in his eyes — or at least where his eyes were — behind his glasses.

"You left...!" Matthews seemed to say, exasperated. Between leaps and leaps, the man didn't hold back as he seemed to grow angrier with each passing second of the fight.

His fighting style, however, was delicate, even elegant when he focused. His head was always forward while he fought, as if he were willing to listen to what the other had to say. Curious, isn't it? How one of the most brutal men the police had ever seen had always been the most empathetic.

"...Even after that!" Harry narrowly dodged his punches, almost certain that one would have knocked him unconscious the moment it touched any part of his body.

Matthew's fists followed the flow of water; patient and destructive. Still, his legs seemed locked, as if he had trapped them in the roots of a tree. Always been clumsy... Harry seemed to smile as he remembered seeing his brother crying because he tripped in the forest.

"What are you laughing at!?" His expression remained the same, but his voice screamed with discomfort.

Mannerisms that Harry had forgotten. Moments that his mind pushed away, almost as if it didn't want to remember. However, the memory was in front of him, and it couldn't be pushed away.

He could feel the bloodlust in his fists, and before he could blame the army or the outside world for his actions, for the first time, he understood what his brother was saying. This is hurting us all... he thought, melancholically.

"...What do you think about stopping, brother?" Harry said in a soft voice, which brought a confused reaction from Matthews. Before he could answer, Harry, with two passes directed in opposite directions, separated them.

"I'm sorry." The Locksmith said, in order to prevent a sudden advance from Matthews. "I... I understand now."

"Understand what!?" Matthews, still with clenched fists, replied, holding himself back from jumping on the defenseless man. "You don't understand anything. You can't just apologize and pretend that 15 years isn't a long time! You..."

"I know what I did..." Harry said, taking a step forward. "I... I thought I wasn't going to hurt you—"

"You weren't going to hurt me!?" He replied indignantly. "I was 8 fucking years old, Harry. Eight. Years. I bet you just waited for Mom to die so you could go to the capital and enjoy the pleasures the world offered, didn't you? You..."

"...Shut up." His brother pleaded, but Matthews continued.

"...you're nothing but a bully, Harry. Terrorizing ordinary people just because they, what, have money? Seriously? Is this all about envy? I should have guessed, it's always the same..."

"..."

"...No matter how hard you try, you'll never change, will you? You fucking egocentric—"

"SHUT UP!" The Locksmith shouted loud enough for half the arena to hear. A broken man, his deep, distinct voice seemed to choke in his own throat. Still, he made an effort to be heard.

Matthews, as if snapped out of a trance, inadvertently took a step back upon hearing his older brother shouting. Following his order, Matthews seemed to fall silent.

"I-I..." Harry gestured irrationally with his hands, a clue to his mental state. "I didn't know, okay? Every time I looked back, and I thought of you, I also thought of her. I remembered her. I was the one who caused her death, you know? I was the one who left the fireplace lit... I was the one who..."

The young thief held back tears as he tried to explain himself.

"...I thought you'd be alright. I didn't want to cause more trouble..." He continued. "Since you were under his care, you'd be safe—"

"It was never about safety, Harry," Matthews said, his emotions now more controlled. "It was about companionship. All that shit happens overnight, and besides losing her, I lose you too? I thought you'd abandoned me, man. That I was a fucking burden. Especially after seeing that some guy called "Locksmith" showed up in New Cavebrook..."

"It was my way of honoring her—"

"By being a thief!?" Matthews took a step forward, pointing to himself. "It was me, Harry, who had to endure years, silently, listening every day to our uncle saying that you were a pride to the family. That you were the first Emblem user. Do you have any idea about that? A fucking thief brought more pride than me..."

"I-I—"

"I had to work twenty times harder than the others, not because I wanted to, but because he forced me. He said I had to think about you. That I had to be inspired by you. I only did the first part."

"...I'm sorry, Matt." Harry said after a moment. "I... I shouldn't have done it."

"We could have done this together, man." Matthews said, his voice shaking. "We could have climbed this fucking Tower. The two of us. You know we would've made it without a hitch. That's what it should've been, shouldn't it? The two of us against the world."

Even if his face was a skull, Harry could feel it. He can't even cry. "...It's all my fault. Everything. Petrikov... mom... even Prism."

Looking around, Matthews forgot he was in the midst of a true chaotic scene. A dead boat's body part cutting the arena in half as explosions surged from one corner and frantic screams from the other. Then he stared at Harry, his facade no longer covering him. ...Fuck you, Harry.

The skeleton then, using his fists as propulsion, launched himself to Harry, who didn't bother to look up and accepted his fate. Prepared to receive a devastating punch, he closed his eyes.

In the deepness of the black, a substance covered his mourning heart. Not only that, but he felt embraced.

A hug. A hug from his brother.
