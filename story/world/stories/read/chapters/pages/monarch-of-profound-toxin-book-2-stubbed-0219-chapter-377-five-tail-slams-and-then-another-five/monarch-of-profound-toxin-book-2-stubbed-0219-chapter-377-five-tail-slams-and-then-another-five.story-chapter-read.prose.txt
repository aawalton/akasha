Chapter 377

Although the practical process of creating the grade 7 Potion of the Metal Body, as well as the spiritual treatment of the active agent, was quite a bit different than the grade 7 Potion of Mighty Strength, now that Eik had already clashed with what was essentially a quasi-conscious fragment of his own will and power, he knew what to expect of it.

In a total of six attempts, four of which were simple error during the establishment of the foundation of the potion, Eik succeeded in the creation of a grade 7 Potion of the Metal Body.

Testing a potion of this nature—one that granted his body increased physical durability as well as greater resistance against hazards such as temperature and hazardous materials like poison—was not nearly as dangerous as the Potion of Mighty Strength. However, just to be on the safe side, they still went into the remote wilderness and away from civilization.

Unfortunately, while the testing of a defensive potion was certainly safer than an offensive one, it was also much more difficult to get any kind of accurate results. With something like a grade 2 Potion of the Metal Body for an Awakened of the appropriate rank, for example, it would not be particular troublesome as there were plenty of Awakened, or even monsters, walking around who were more than powerful enough to push the strength of the effect past its absolute limits.

That was simply not the case for a grade 7 potion. Even without the effects of the Metal Body coursing through his system, Eik was generally completely invulnerable to any physical harm, and with the potion, that truth would only solidify further. So how did one go about testing the limits of it in such a situation?

Well, if you were to ask Eik Magnasen, the answer was simple.

“Fuck if I know.”

Robert threw his hands up in frustration. “What about your big snake then? Spaghetti Jones? Can’t you make him, like, whip you with his tail or something like that? That would hurt, right?” Halfway through the potion brewing process, he had come to find Meri and ended up hanging around until the end to see how it would go.

Eik rolled his eyes. “Yeah, of course it would freakin’ hurt, dude. But do you seriously think that my pet would be able to actually harm me enough to prove anything? He’s super big and super strong, but I’m still super stronger than him when it comes to concentrated force.”

“But he wouldn’t have to be able to kill you or anything like that, would he?” the man argued. “You know roughly how strong Spaghetti Jones is in comparison to you, right?”

Eik nodded. “Yeah, more or less.”

“Then wouldn’t you be able to get a pretty good idea about how effective the new potion is if you just take a few big hits from him and compare how much damage it does compared to normal? Get him to hit you five times, take the potion, and then take five more hits.”

Eik stared wordlessly as his brain worked. After a few seconds, he cleared his throat awkwardly. “I guess that could work,” he muttered quietly under his breath.

“I’m sorry, what?” Robert asked.

“I said, there’s a chance that might work.”

“I’d say there’s more than a chance,” the man said. “It makes perfect sense.”

With a huff of annoyance, Eik pulled half a dozen pastries from his bag of holding as well as two random pills of Eik’s Legendary Mystery Medicine and threw them at the pesky administrator. “Just take your damn bonus and go over there and be quiet!”

Pulling the front of his shirt up like a makeshift sack, Robert caught the reward with a beaming smile. “That’s all I wanted to hear, Boss. I’ll be over here.”

“We made these potions possible, and Bobby gets that much just for thinking a little faster than you?” Meri protested when she saw the size of the bonus.

Eik’s brow knit together. “Did I forget to tell you how much I intend to pay you?”

“You haven’t said a thing!” she said.

“Oh okay, well, my plan is to pay each of you,” he said, gesturing at the entire team of alchemists with a sweep of his arm. “about five times what I just gave Bob. With seniority and proven loyalty, he of course gets paid better for his work, you must understand. He is my direct right-hand man and carries much of the responsibility for one of the largest companies in the Unified Mass on his shoulders. It’s only right that he gets what he gets.”

After the first part of the statement both Meri and the rest of them seemed to have briefly gone offline. She shook her head to clear it. “Di-Did you just… say five times what you gave him?”

“Five times the pills, all of which it is up to you to decide whether to sell or keep, and, of course, five times the pastries. That’s more than thirty cakes, you know. Not a small number by any means.”

“No, tell me more about the pills. What kind of value are we talking about here?”

Eik pursed his lips. “I mean, I want to say they’re priceless, but if you’d rather have us sell them and just take a lump sum somewhat equal to their market value, I suppose each of you would receive something in the area of, what, two hundred million, two hundred and fifty million credits? Is that about right, Bobby?”

“What?” Robert shouted from a rock he had taken a seat on, already digging into a classic cream pastry. His ears weren’t quite good enough to hear Eik’s words.

“I said, is two hundred and fifty million credits for ten pills about right?” Eik shouted back, not bothering to project his voice with aura.

“Probably closer to three hundred million or three hundred and twenty million credits at current estimation!” Pastry crumbs flew like flower petals from his mouth as he yelled.

“Okay, well, there you have it,” Eik said. “So something like that.”

A couple of the alchemists immediately fell to their knees and bowed their heads in gratitude. The rest soon followed. Disjointedly, they voiced their gratitude loudly. As always, Eik felt mild discomfort at the emotional outpouring and didn’t quite know how to handle it.

“All right, anyway. I’m calling my snake now. Spaghetti Jones,” he spoke in a normal voice.

A second later, the cloud cover above broke apart and admitted the world serpent. Utterly silent, it hung in the air and watched its master with those titanic, slitted pupils. Eik stared back as a realization struck him.

“If we’re doing it like this, we should probably move to a different world, actually. No need to destroy this one where Gimleh is.”

***

“Okay, boy, that’s right! Just like that!” Eik shouted at deafening volume, waving his hands around in an attempt to direct the mountainous tail into position just above him so it could crush him into the ground properly. Spaghetti Jones jittered nervously at the prospect of purposefully attacking Eik.

“And you guys are good over there?” In the distance, almost an entire mountain range away, a small squad of Living Manifestations tasked with the protection of the team of alchemists signaled in the affirmative. “Perfect! Cool! Spaghetti Jones, we’re all good to go! Give it your all now, you hear? If you hold back, this whole thing will be pointless. And you don’t want it to be pointless, do you?”

The leviathan only offered a small nod in return, although with the sheer size of its head that movement alone could have leveled skyscrapers.

“Okay, let’s g—” The first slam of the massive tail cut short any more words he had been hoping to get out. “Da—” The second did the same, and the remaining three strikes followed in quick succession.

When Spaghetti Jones withdrew, all that was left was a layered crater kilometers wide, dust and debris still raining into the hole and filling it gradually. Presumably somewhere deep within, Eik was nowhere to be seen.

Stressful anxiety began to build up in the serpent’s body. It watched the crater with trepidation, afraid that something might have gone wrong. But that wasn’t possible.

Its master was all powerful. Five simple strikes from Spaghetti Jones would not be enough to harm him. It could not be.

Lowering its head slowly, Spaghetti Jones’ eyes scanned the enormous crater frantically while the alchemists watched from a distance, unable to see anything besides the snake seemingly freaking out.

“Did something go wrong or something?” Meri asked with concern. “Should we go over there?”

“Nah,” Robert told her, caressing her back as he stuffed that last of his bonus pastries into his gullet. “I’m sure, he’s fine. That’s kind of his special skill. In the end, he’s always fine.”

Back at the crater, a deep, rumbling keening rolled out from the Spaghetti Jones’ throat, filling the atmosphere with a sound that made the bones feel heavy in the body.

“Guh!” Eik coughed as he finally broke free of the soil and crawled out into the light. His clothes were tattered to rags and utterly covered in grime, but otherwise the only sign of his ordeal were a couple of light bruises that were already in the final stages of healing. “I was starting to get worried for a second there. Couldn’t figure out which way was up.”

Spaghetti Jones rubbed his snout against Eik’s head, inadvertently grinding him bodily several meters into the ground as Robert and company arrived, carried by the Living Manifestations.

“Are you okay?” the guild master asked with concern.

“Not a scratch. Well, I did get a few little itty bitty ones, but they’re all good now, as you can see,” he said and spun around with his arms out. “It didn’t feel great, though, if I have to be honest.”

“Are you up for more?”

“Naturally. If this potion does what I think it does, then it’s only going to get better from here.”

“True.”

***

In the end, it wasn’t easy to determine how powerful the effect of the potion was. There definitely was an effect. Of that, Eik was absolutely certain, and it must have been considerable, if it improved anything at all for his X-ranked physique.

Briefly, one of the alchemists aired an idea that they could invite Chop the Blade over to get a few cuts in on Eik, just to get some proper data, but Eik shut that crap down quick. Regardless of how well the potion worked, he was still going to take as much of it as he could when he eventually faced the Lord of the Moon, and he was still going to do his damndest to avoid being hit even a single time.

He could do just fine without getting sliced into high-grade sashimi by that nutjob of an old geezer. Getting tail slammed by a snake long enough to constrict the planet would simply have to do.

Skipping the creation of Kiq’s Temper for now, they jumped into the Potion of Magical Empowerment. Now that he had an even better grasp on the general process, he finished the potion in just three attempts, marking the end of the experimentation and leaving only one more thing.

Conversion to Legendary Mystery Medicine followed by what Eik liked to refer to as Crafting Hell.
