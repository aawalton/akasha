Alice bit her lip. Prim had just received quite the upgrade, and if Iggy's hunch was right, many of the people in the Protectorate Guild were receiving high levels with additional help. Why not her dragons?

And why hadn’t anyone mentioned this to her before?

"I will allow it," she said slowly, but held up a finger before Bella or Iggy could react. "But the same rules will apply to you. If something goes wrong—if you experience discomfort—then I will take you immediately to the healers."

She paused and realized that Numi did not have a stone of her own. "Numi, do you have something you would like to take, or a stone for Natura?"

"No, not yet," Numi said in a way that made Alice suspect she had been anticipating the question. "But I believe I know where I could find something useful to me, and also it would be a way for me to level up my lockpicking skills."

Alice knew what that meant. "I will not have you stealing." She paused and rubbed at her right eyebrow, feeling like she was getting a headache. "But I suppose you have something in mind?" she asked in exasperation.

"Well, yes, and yes, it is still stealing. Technically." Numi said. "But what if it's from somebody who will probably not miss it? Someone who already has a lot of magic." If she had eyebrows, she would have been wiggling them in Alice's direction.

"You're talking about Samantha," Alice said flatly.

She bobbed her head. "Or one of the other sorcerers. I'm not picky."

That was not something Alice was at all comfortable with, but... perhaps it was something she should think about. Later. She decided to put Numi's request aside for now. “I will think on it.”

Next, she turned to Bella and Iggy. "So, do you agree to my terms?" she asked again, just to make sure they were crystal clear.

"Yes, Alice." Iggy held out the pearlescent stone, which was as tiny as the end of one of his claws. Nevertheless, it seemed to radiate a power that she could feel from several feet away. This was not the first time she had seen the stone. Why had she not noticed it before?

Perhaps because Numi had drawn her attention to it. The power felt... slippery somehow.

Meanwhile, Bella had the bloodstone they had found in the forest. It was larger than the pearlescent and also seemed to radiate its own power, though of a wholly different flavor. Something dark but also full of potential.

Iggy and Bella were eyeing each other as if each wanted to go first, but neither wanted to argue in front of Alice for the privilege. Alice decided to break the stalemate.

"Iggy, are you certain that stone will be compatible with your magic? If it's not, we can always find something that will work better for you." She did not want him to make a hasty decision because he was feeling jealous of Prim.

With a shake of his head, Iggy broke eye contact with Bella. "This will suit me perfectly. I can feel it."

Well, it was now or never. "Okay then, take the stone. Bella, dear, I want you to wait until we are certain everything has gone well with Iggy."

Bella made a face but she did not argue. Meanwhile, Iggy had already tossed the pearlescent stone back into his throat and swallowed it. As it was tiny, swallowing was not nearly as traumatic as it had been with Prim. They all waited with bated breath.

Suddenly, Alice got a notification:

Aspect 3, Igcendiorum "Iggy", has consumed a source of extraordinary compatible magic.

This source has automatically merged with his Ultimate Armor skill to provide magical protection as well as upgraded physical protection.

Iggy must have received his own notification because he bellowed, "What's this? What's this!? I did not receive a choice of a new skill? No evolution? This is barely an upgrade!"

Alice, though, was pleased. "Iggy! This is a good thing. With magical resistance, you will be protected more than ever before."

Prim agreed. "Now you have additional defense against sorcerers, wizards, and mages. Think about it, Iggy—with this type of magical protection, you can more easily put yourself between Alice and any danger she faces."

Iggy perked up. "Well... perhaps it is not bad, but my stone was very tiny. I should get first choice of anything new we come across." He slid a look of pure jealousy toward Prim.

"Numi and Natura should get the next, as neither one of them has had anything at all," Alice said. "And neither have the sub-aspects."

Though Aconite and Spark did not seem interested in the stones whatsoever, Natura spoke up. "I am satisfied with my skills and will only choose a stone if it perfectly suits me, not gulp down the first bit of power I come across."

"Why not?" Iggy demanded. "Power is excellent."

He had turned to speak to her, and as he did, his scales caught the lamplight. Alice did a double take. "Iggy," she squinted, "come closer to me."

Instantly, Iggy turned back and glomped across the bed. He had grown longer and heavier over the last few weeks and practically shook the mattress with every hop. No way could he fit on her shoulders now. He was about as long as Alice was tall, though with his short legs he did not even come up to her knees, and so was much too small to ride.

"Turn to the lamplight," she directed. "Ah, there. Do any of you see a difference?"

Under the lamplight, it was clear that his red and gold scales had gained a new pearlescent sheen to them. It was as if the stone he had swallowed had been flattened and distributed all over his body. With his overall red and gold coloring and wild red mane, Iggy had always had the most dramatic look out of all of Alice's dragons. Now the pearlescent sheen added a new layer of beauty.

The others crowded close and oohed and aahed.

"Iggy," Numi said, "my assessment skill says that if you drop any of your pearlescent scales, we could likely sell it at a silver apiece."

"I like my scales where they are," Iggy said. "They are my protection."

Bella was the only one who had not come over to inspect Iggy's scales. Instead, she had waited patiently with her bloodstone clutched in her claws—at least until now. "Alice..."

Alice smiled at her. "Go ahead, dear."

Usually Bella consumed food using her burnt sacrifice, but that was not appropriate with the bloodstone. Instead, she licked it from her palm and swallowed it whole just like any other dragon would.

Her eyes flashed blood red and at the same time, Alice received a new notification.

Aspect 5, Bellanyx "Bella", has consumed a source of compatible power.

Choose where to place this power.

Upgrade - Smoke Form

Upgraded - Curse of Ash

Blood Tap: Drain life from an enemy by touching its blood, replenishing your own health or mana.

"How is that fair?" Iggy demanded once Bella bragged to the others about her choices. "I did not receive a new skill."

"Bella is young, and it seems that almost everybody gets an additional skill almost right away," Prim said. "You are a red dragon, not a green dragon. Do not be jealous."

"Hey," Numi said, offended. "I'm never jealous."

Alice stared at the skill options that Bella had received. Two were upgrades to Bella's current skills, which admittedly she rarely used. The third, Blood Tap, was new and came with an explanation, almost as if the stone were particularly suited to it. Drain life from an enemy by touching its blood, replenishing your own health or mana.

"I want the third one," Bella said immediately.

"Are you sure? Your smoke skill could be interesting if it was upgraded."

"No, I want something lethal to kill my enemies by draining them of life. Think about it. I can drain my enemies using blood tap, and then later on I can do my burnt sacrifice to feed on whatever remaining energy their corpse has. It's two for the price of one!"

"That does sound logical," Numi said.

Frankly, it sounded horrifying to Alice, but she was pleased that Bella was so happy with her stone. Plus, she trusted that her dragon would not be so assertive for no reason... right?

Had she not assigned a sub-aspect to Natura just so the dragon would be safe? This skill would ensure that Bella could better protect herself. Anybody who tried to hurt her would regret it.

She picked Blood Tap and Bella’s scales flashed darkly.

"I want to try my new skill on rats tonight," Bella announced.

Better rats than people, Alice thought.

"First, let me try my illusions," Prim spoke up, drifting to the middle of the bed to catch everybody's attention. She conjured several illusionary rats, which skittered across the bedspread. They were so lifelike they actually made the soft fabric dip under their little paws.

Alice nearly shrieked, but pressed herself against the bed's headrest instead. Bella pounced on them immediately, and they dissipated into nothing, much like the sorceress's illusions had. Then again, that was where Prim had gotten her power.

"Oh." Bella lifted up her claws to find there was nothing underneath and was visibly disappointed. "I guess my blood tap skill will not work with illusions. It needs to be something real with flesh and—"

"Blood?" Numi asked, cocking her head.

"Yes," Bella said.

"Oh, that makes sense." Numi turned. "Alice, do you wish to come with us as we search out real rats?"

"No," Alice said faintly. "You go on, dears, have fun."

Then she noticed that Prim had a particularly smug look on her face, as if Alice had missed a joke.

"Prim?"

"Yes, Alice?" her dragon said, aloud.

Prim was the only one of her dragons who had a voice of her own--part mind-voice, part illusion. Alice always figured it was because she was her first and wasn't hatched with the power to make herself as instantly understood as the rest.

Now, that had changed. In fact, it had since Prim had swallowed the stone but the change had been so smooth that Alice had not noticed.

"I can hear you." She cupped her own ear. "Really hear you!"

"My illusions are more realistic than ever," Prim said, still smug.

Alice scooped up her first dragon into a quick hug, but Prim was not the only one who needed attention.

Numi was regarding her with exception clear on her face. Oh right, Numi’s plan. Alice sighed. "I don't like Samantha, and I am certain she doesn't like me," she said, "but at the same time, I don't feel it would be right to steal from her."

At least not yet, she thought to herself.

Numi nodded but then immediately shot back with a brand-new idea. "What if we steal more upgradables from somebody who has already stolen? Like a thief?"

Again, Alice hesitated. Why did it feel like she was sliding down a slippery slope? "I suppose if the thief was stealing high-end jewelry. I do not wish for you to take from someone who is already living at the edge or has been forced to steal in order to feed their family."

"Yes, that makes sense, and it gives me an idea," Numi said. "Plus, it's a good way for Prim to practice her illusion skills..."

With that, she started to lay out her plan.
