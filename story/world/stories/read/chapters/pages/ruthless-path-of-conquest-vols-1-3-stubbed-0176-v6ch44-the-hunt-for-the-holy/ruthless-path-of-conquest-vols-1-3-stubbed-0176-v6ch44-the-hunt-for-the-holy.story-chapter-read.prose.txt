Fisher Emperor, giant alligator, and Taiwanese warrior flew through the air at breakneck pace.

James was following the signal sent by one of his subordinate Rulers. He had enough allies present—even with a few, like Luna, having been taken out—that he’d had multiple options for which way to go after defeating the Chosen One of Apophis. They were almost all near Holy Kings. Ultimately, he’d decided based on proximity.

The Great Blue Heron King was closest. This was one of the many Rulers who had surrendered to James following the Battle of Orlando. He had reported he was in the middle of combat with a Holy King who had detected the Heron King through stealth.

From the way he stopped responding a minute after James checked in with him, the Fisher Emperor guessed the fight was not going well, albeit that James could sense the Heron King was still alive.

The trio moved toward their destination almost in silence, breaking it only for a brief discussion of procedure.

Han Jianguo made the suggestion that James should come up with a signal to use when and if he wanted Skill Nullification used again.

By the time that was settled, they were almost within sight of the fight. James could feel the shifting auras and air currents from a violent midair skirmish. The Heron King was still fighting, it seemed, but he simply didn’t have the leeway in the midst of battle to chat.

“Why did we stop?” asked Samuel.

The trio were still floating in midair, as James’s Precision Gravity Control had rendered them weightless, but James had stopped using Warp Step, so they were just hanging in place.

“They’re not far,” James said. “Just out of sight. If I can take a minute, I’d like to scope out what this Holy King can actually do. Given that the great blue heron has had trouble subduing him, it’s just wiser to make sure I’m prepared for whatever kind of trouble he might cause.”

“Understood,” Han Jianguo said. “I’ll try to keep my presence subtle.”

Well, you didn’t tip off the Chosen One of Apophis to your presence, James thought. I’m a little more worried about the kaiju-sized alligator with me.

To his surprise, as he was thinking that, Samuel began shrinking. His body glowed gently and became more compact, until he was roughly the same height as James.

“I call this stealth mode,” said Samuel, grinning toothily at the surprised looks of the other two. “So that we can remain silent as well as difficult to spot, how about we work out different ways you can signal me for different tactics? Nudge me this way if you want me to attack with a frontal tackle…”

The three made their way into sight range of the Holy King in another minute, though they still weren’t close to see details clearly. The arena had been massive, and it was a testament to that fact that James hadn’t realized this Holy King had an incredibly flashy fighting style.

Identify confirmed the enemy’s identity as a Holy King. His name was Omar al-Alawi.

But it was hard to get more details visually, because as he watched, every movement from the Holy King was illuminated by near blinding white lights.

He wielded a brightly glowing white sword, he launched energy beams from it when he swung, there were dazzling flashes of light when he threw a punch, and more harsh lights when Omar used some sort of technique that accelerated his movements suddenly.

James was able to discern that al-Alawi was wearing shoes with wings on them, probably indicating that the midair battle was enabled by that set of equipment; al-Alawi’s robe, despite looking flimsy, had some sort of protective enchantment, as every strike from the Great Blue Heron King that seemed likely to slash or pierce through appeared to be deflected once it made contact with the cloth; and al-Alawi’s movement Skill was acceleration-related rather than actual teleportation, as James could track his movements when his eyes were adjusted enough.

“All right, I think I’m as ready as I’m going to be,” the Fisher Emperor whispered. The three were hiding behind a cloud.

“Are you certain?” replied Han Jianguo in a low, restrained voice. “I’m confident he has not shown his full arsenal yet, just based on rumors of the Holy Kingdom. I think that out of all the Holy Kings, this one is known to be particularly prominent in their homeland, which could mean stronger than the others. You might want to tread lightly.”

James pointed at the Great Blue Heron King. The razor-winged bird had been in over a dozen midair clashes with Omar since the trio had begun watching the fight, and the Heron King seemed to be slowing down noticeably now. Despite the relatively small target area the bird presented, a half dozen significant cuts streamed wounds from various places across his body. His brilliant blue plumage was stained red all over.

“If I don’t jump in soon, my asset is going to die,” James said bluntly. “It’s not permanent here, but it’s also not worth losing an asset, even temporarily, just to sate my curiosity.”

“That’s a good reason,” Han Jianguo said.

“What do you want me to do, James?” whispered Samuel.

“Try and hit him from behind,” James said. “Maybe do that swamp-summoning move I saw out of the corner of my eye earlier to cut off any escape route and separate the Great Blue Heron King from the action. I’ll order the Heron King to back off when we close in so he doesn’t get caught in the fighting that comes next. I think he needs the break.”

As he said that, the Heron King shot down toward King Omar once more.

James and Samuel took that as their opportunity to break from cover.

The Fisher Emperor grabbed the alligator and used Warp Step once more.

Suddenly, they were right in the midst of the action.

King Omar became aware of them entering the periphery of his fighting space as soon as they arrived. He half-turned to face them, and that left him vulnerable to a full force strike from the Great Blue Heron King.

A curtain of bladed feathers slashed open an artery in the Holy King’s arm, but he couldn’t be bothered even looking at the bird as the Heron King made a speedy retreat.

“You,” Omar said, glaring at James. “I had wondered if I would encounter you here.”

He raised a hand and placed it over his arm where the Heron King had cut him. A glowing white light emanated from his palm and healed the wound in an instant.

Does that mean he couldn’t let that continue to bleed until it healed on its own? James thought. He didn’t know all the details of the Heron King’s abilities, since the bird had only joined him recently, and with his rivalry with the Great Egret King—who had surrendered to James at the exact same time, literally pressing up beside the Heron King in the queue—the Great Blue Heron King was reluctant to say much of anything about himself, lest it become a weakness for the Egret King to take advantage of later.

The internal politics of the Fisher Empire were becoming a little more complicated with every Ruler who surrendered to James.

“I’m afraid it’s your bad luck,” James said. “Or maybe bad karma? Have you committed any terrible sins lately?”

He was just talking to distract the Holy King for a moment. Samuel swung his trident in a quick loop beside James, but Omar didn’t spare him a glance for another moment, too busy locked in his staring contest with the Fisher Emperor.

That cost him the momentum. At the moment when King Omar turned to look at Samuel, eight geysers of swamp water burst forth from the ground all around the Holy King. Two of them separated Omar from the Heron King. The other six restricted his retreat options backward and to the sides.

“Oh, you think I want to run away?” he asked, raising an eyebrow.

Only if you’re smart, James thought. I have more aura than you, and you’re outnumbered, all by yourself.

“Go,” James said, not bothering to answer Omar’s question. If he had his way, this fight would be over as quickly as it had begun.

He and Samuel darted forward on opposite sides of King Omar, forcing him to divide his focus—or at least, that was the plan.

The Holy King smirked, raised his glowing white sword, and suddenly James and Samuel were stopped in midair.

James couldn’t see what his own position looked like, but Samuel stood across from him, also frozen in place, a glowing white sword that mirrored the sword in Omar’s hands floating over his head.

“Too slow, gentleman,”  King Omar said. “The bird was too quick for me, but your attack pattern was predictable, and your movements weren’t anything special.” He chuckled. “Then again, there was always a decent chance I would catch you. Wherever the light touches is my territory, and even you can’t outrun light.”

Experimentally, the Fisher Emperor tried to move around individual body parts. He could still control his Mana, and his fingers and toes could move more or less freely, but anything beyond that seemed to be restricted.

It was annoying. Nothing he couldn’t overcome, but annoying.

Guess I should have moved at my top speed, James thought. He’d been overly cautious, trying not to rush in faster than Samuel, aware that there was strength in numbers. But apparently this was one time it would have benefited him to just Warp Step into touch range.

King Omar pointed the glowing white sword in his hands at James.

“I’ll take care of you first, Fisher Emperor,” he said.

Hit me with your best shot, James thought. Once you get within range…

Then he felt it. The force freezing him in midair released, and he dropped.

King Omar wasn’t dropping, but it was those winged shoes of his keeping him floating. The glowing white sword was still working, too.

So Han Jianguo used Skill Nullification again. Our equipment is still working, but our personal Skills aren’t. All right.

The Holy King adjusted to the new reality quickly. He grinned, and his body shifted in midair, rushing toward James with his glowing sword raised.

Roscuro was transformed into his bracelet form at that moment, for concealment, and James restrained a smile as he gave the Soul Eater his next orders.

Wait until he’s almost close enough to touch…

King Omar swooped into within range to swing his glowing sword at James’s head, and suddenly Roscuro transformed into a spear.

James jabbed once, and he heard and felt the penetration through the man’s robes. They had provided adequate protection against the Heron King’s many attempted attacks.

But not this one.

There was a horrendous scream as the Holy King, taken by surprise, shattered and disintegrated.

“And another one bites the dust,” James said, slightly amused despite his quiet awareness of how objectively horrific this was.

“Onto the next one?” came Han Jianguo’s voice.

James nodded. “Thank you again for the assist.”

The trio moved on to a Holy King that the Great Egret King had engaged. When they arrived, the Great Egret King was doing even worse than the Heron King had been. He was on the ground, bleeding out, the Holy King standing over him.

James Warp Stepped in and stabbed the Holy King in the heart from behind.

He did the same exact thing to the next Holy King, who was locked in a struggle with the Dusky Pygmy Rattlesnake King.

No muss, no fuss, no ability to avoid the attack when one was already engaged in a distracting duel to the death.

“It’s not exactly sporting, but I know you have your reasons for doing things this way,” Han Jianguo said. His tone was one of approval, James noted. Perhaps the fact that James had been targeted by a group of Pú Tiě Shān’s allies had altered the man’s attitude. It was hard to say. But the mild suspicion or hostility that the Fisher Emperor had sensed from Jianguo earlier seemed to have dissipated entirely as they completed their task.

If I asked what he was thinking, he’d just keep silent, the Fisher Emperor thought. That’s the kind of guy he is.

“We do what we must,” James said. “Hopefully we find another enemy soon.”

There was a weariness growing behind his eyes, despite the energy the Soul Eater was taking in and partially feeding to his master. It was a bitter thing, destroying all these souls.

But there was no time to rest. The more time elapsed, the greater the chance of failure.
