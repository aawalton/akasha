I was trapped in a box. Well, the Mimic part of me was trapped in a box.

Billions of years before first contact with another being. I can kind of remember it. Sort of. Like the fuzzy edges of a dream I can barely remember. Just the emotional response and the lack of interaction.

Anyhow, I'm the Incarnation of Change, I was trapped, and here's the bit where I'm wondering about metaphors and here and now reality, everything in this world was stupidly stagnant until the Keeper helped me break a single tentacle free of that box.

Where I realized immediately what had left Karen a little speechless. Yasmin, the oldest Reveler in my New Amsterdam congregation, laid out on my Altar almost like she was sleeping, Marie and Dana standing over her as if they'd been talking quietly. I let out that breath. "I thought you were dead."

She let out a sound that might have been a chuckle. "Won't be long now."

"Oh. Oh, shit. Is there anything I can do for you?"

She smiled weakly. "I thought that was what I was supposed to ask you?"

I shook my head, chuckling at the joke as I reached over, lifted her up, and pulled her to my arms. "Thought I'd made that clear. I'm here to support you guys. Not the other way around."

She kept smiling as I looked into that deep dark secret place inside her, although she whispered, "turn and turn alike, Goddess. We support each other."

In a Holy parallel to what I'd done to Diana when she was last Artemis, I disconnected a few nerves. Numbness isn't as good as bliss, but after so long in pain surcease of pain can be just as much of a balm.

Just the dreaming echo of her Soul, but still so intense I could feel it even while awake. But... Die without pain, without fear, without, and this kind of shocked me, regrets.
