At the shore north of Liu Shu’s position, a large group of people arrived in a fleet. The group had almost 600,000 people, showing the massive scale of this group’s influence.

At the lead, a woman with shifting blue and purple hues as hair and wide hips held a gorgeous flute as she squinted. “You really chose to trust that child, Harmonixia? The <Umbral Desolation Keepers> now have all our territory and will be able to develop quickly. We’ve actually created a breach in the power balance just to move here. Melody, you might’ve guided our entire race to doom.”

An elderly Sylvan with similar qualities sighed. “Octavia, when will you stop opposing your sister at every turn? Melody wouldn’t suggest such an outrageous request without having certainty.”

Melody was Harmonixia’s mother, a woman with elegant beauty and gorgeous hips. She was holding a pipa in her arms while looking around, searching for someone.

“Melody! Stop ignoring me!”

“Always screeching without any decorum.” Melody snorted as she glanced at her little sister. “No wonder your songs are always out of tune. You screaming like that has probably hurt your vocal cords.”

Octavia’s face gained a touch of red as she glared at Melody’s calm and elegant face. “Arrogant! Always with that condescending look as if you know everything and everyone around you is nothing but dust!”

An aunt at the side spoke with a sigh. “Octavia, if your big sister didn’t love you dearly, do you think you’d still be part of the family? Speaking like that to our healthiest <Fourth Energy Cycle> and not being punished is a privilege only you can enjoy.”

Octavia’s face turned beet red as she crossed her arms and snorted. “Whatever.”

Melody blinked and smiled. “Oh? She is coming in person to receive us?”

In the distance, they saw Harmonixia riding a massive, light green-colored Arboreal Winged Serpent and waving at them. “Mooom!”

Octavia made another comment. “Are you not going to comment about your daughter’s unladylike behavior?”

Melody kept her smile, but she muttered between gritted teeth. “If you continue having this attitude in front of your grandnephew, I swear that I’ll spank you until you cry for our mother.”

Octavia pressed her lips tightly as she tensed her butt cheeks. She believed that Melody would do it because it wouldn’t be the first time.

Meanwhile, the elderly Sylvan, who stood by their side, smiled widely, her eye wrinkles crinkling gently. “I really like how my children get along well with each other. I wonder when I can hold my great-grandson.”

All the other people around them looked away, not bothering to comment. Regardless of their attitude, it was the main branch of the <Songtree> family that kept their race afloat. In the past, it was Melody’s mother who reached the <Fourth Energy Cycle> and acted as guardian, and now, it was Melody. So, no one had any complaints about their ruling.

The Arboreal Winged Serpent descended, landing in front of the massive group of people slowly walking down the many ships docked along the wide beach. When she landed, her reptilian green eyes locked onto Melody, feeling an incredible sense of threat coming from the gentle woman. ‘...Powerful.’

Melody also noticed the Arboreal Winged Serpent. Although her face remained calm, her heart jumped with surprise. ‘Such a powerful <Third Energy Cycle> creature… Is it a native species of the <Wild Continent>? Or… Is it the Arboreal Winged Serpents Little Harmony talked about? If it is the latter, little Liu Shu’s summoning prowess is truly frightening.’

Harmonixia landed on the ground and jumped into Melody’s arms with a wide smile. “Mom! Thank you for trusting me!”

Holding her very tall daughter in her arms, Melody looked up with a gentle smile. “I trust that you haven’t spoken out of emotion. Still, we need to confirm that your words are truthful. Do you understand? We’ve left behind our ancestral home and came here because of that bright future you’ve promised.” Then, her face became stern. “If your words were exaggerated in any way, shape, or form, you’ll be heavily punished.”

Even if Harmonixia was her daughter, the sacrifice this time was too high to hide it. The people they carried with them were too many to take responsibility for. Honestly, Melody was slightly nervous because, if Liu Shu was not what she expected, she would’ve single-handedly ended the long tradition her family held for nothing.

Harmonixia understood Melody’s concerns. And, before the War, she would’ve never suggested that the entirety of the Main Branch actually change locations. However, now things had changed.

“Don’t worry, Mom.” Harmonixia’s face was also serious. “I wouldn’t play with the lives of over half a million people.”

Octavia spoke at this moment, her tone slightly gentler than when she spoke to Melody. “Niece, are you sure that Golden Sap City can house all of us? We are over half a million people.”

Harmonixia chuckled. “Don’t worry, Golden Sap City could house ten Songtree Clans and still feel rather empty. The current population has already reached 11 million.”

The leaders of the branch families opened their eyes wide. Cities with more than a million people were rare around the world, not to mention those with populations above 10 million. The cities that managed that needed to have a very powerful World Tree to constantly protect the city from Beast Tides and other natural disasters.

“Ah!” Harmonixia exclaimed. “I almost forgot to give all of you access to Liu Shu’s buffs!”

Octavia chuckled. “Child. We’ve seen many things in our lives. You don’t need to—”
