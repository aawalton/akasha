A tide of fury rose within Ira as she watched Lord Vastrozun's form begin to steam and glow, celestial heat pouring through ragged tears in his mantle of darkness. She knew immediately what had gone wrong. These wretches had played a pathetic trick on them, hiding a mote of Light within the relic they'd so readily agreed to trade away. More of the goddess's handiwork, no doubt. Ira cursed her former master even as she drew her blade. No magic she possessed could cleanse the demon lord of his affliction, it was something that he would have to endure himself. What she could do was take care of the pathetic lot sullying his throne room.

"You'll pay for this!" Ira snarled, her silver sword igniting with stellar flames as it came free from its scabbard. The party of Wayfarers were arrayed in a battle formation. Amusing. Did they think they could take her head-on? If so, they were about to be sorely dis-

Ira felt something around her crack. She slowed, nearly tripping down the last few steps of the dais as her steps faltered. The compulsion hit her like a ton of bricks. A distant force tugged at the back of her mind, urging her to follow its guidance. The call of the Labyrinth. The last act of disgraced gods.

"Urgh- No!"

Consumed by his own struggle, Lord Vastrozun failed to maintain the mental barrier shielding Ira from the summons that had ensnared the rest of her kin. She could resist it, of course, that's what she had been doing before he'd lent her some of his strength, but that took time and focus, two things that she wasn't likely to find on the battlefield. Sure enough, in her moment of inattention, the Wayfarers struck.

The good news: Their plan had worked! The divine relic was clearly having a dramatic effect on Vastrozun, who was clinging to his chair just to remain upright. Jade would have led her friends on an offensive that put an end to this conflict once and for all if not for the bad news: The mighty angel was charging towards them, brandishing a longsword that shone with the light of a blazing sun.

"Get ready!"

Jade shifted focus, placing herself between Ira and their support casters. The rest of the team followed suit. They didn't need instruction. They'd been fighting together for long enough that everyone knew what they had to do. The setting may be different and the stakes higher, but right now, it all came back down to defeating the enemy that stood before them. And that was something they could do.

They began moving, Jade two steps behind Luis as he rushed to halt the angel in her tracks. Then, Jade felt a ripple of unsteadiness course through Ira's aura. It was impossible to tell what caused it, but whatever it was, it was strong enough to have physical effects. The angel stumbled off the last step of the dais, unsteady and off-balance, her face the picture of surprise. Whatever the reason, it was perfect timing.

"Go!"

Luis arrived first, hammering through her weak attempt to parry with her blade and scoring a clean hit to her armored chest. Jade took a less direct approach, circling around to plant a rune trap at the angel's back while harassing her with thrown blades as often as Phantom could snap back to her hand. She was careful not to put her back to Vastrozun, who was still hunched over his throne. Sparks of incandescent energy were shooting out of him now, burning holes through the walls and floor and preventing anybody from drawing near. Jade wanted to observe him more closely, but there wasn't time. They were already confronted with a dangerous enemy who, even distracted by an unknown force, was a formidable threat.

Ira was tough. Although their initial assault put her on the back foot, she refused to go down easily. Fenrin's elemental arrows shattered against her tough armor, discharging bursts of fire and lightning while Naomi's enhancing music reached across the cathedral, infusing them with greater speed and dexterity. Tara eschewed more complex spells in favor of raw damage, drawing her pulsecaster and raining a storm of condensed mana bolts upon the angel, breaking her stance and punishing each moment of inattention. It was only due to this split focus that Luis was able to meet the powerful angel blade to blade, and even then, it was a near thing. With each clash, Ira's starfire sword bit into Luis's shield, slowly melting its way through the enchanted metal like a divine blowtorch. Luis didn't back down. If anything, he pressed the attack, ignoring the splashes of molten steel spraying his armor as he fought to keep her pinned down. His sheer tenacity left Jade in awe, but there would be time to express that later. Now, it was time to go all out.

And they did. Perhaps it was because they had so much on the line, or maybe it was a simple matter of only having one opponent to focus on, but Jade and her companions fought like they never had before. Anticipating each other's actions, covering their openings, following up attacks with perfect timing. They achieved a level of unity that they'd only brushed against in previous battles. Luis tethered Ira with a frost hook right as she tried to break away towards Naomi, rooting her long enough for Fenrin and Jade to pincer her with counterattacks. When Ira took to the air, Tara conjured a powerful, wide reaching arc of lightning that sent her reeling back to the ground. And when the angel's blade flashed bright, drawing in the surrounding light and charging up to unleash an undoubtedly devastating attack, Jade was there in a flash, disrupting it with Discordant Pulse.

All of this: Their incredible synergy, the mysterious ailment that Jade could sense distracting their opponent, and a substantial numerical advantage, were not enough to win them the battle. Such was the angel Ira's strength and skill, absorbing and deflecting countless attacks and spells while meeting out blasts of radiant energy that could only be described as smites from the divine. No, this new height of prowess and unity was merely enough to allow Jade and her friends to set foot in the arena with such an opponent. Had they been less skilled, trained together any less rigorously, or possessed of lesser strength, then Ira would have overwhelmed them in an instant, strange affliction or no.

The battle was interrupted by a mighty roar that shook the plateau itself. Vastrozun collapsed to his knees, slumping over his throne. A mixture of light and shadow played over his armored figure, twisting together in a scintillating pattern that showered the dais with umbral sparks. Jade was thrown off her feet, narrowly avoiding falling through the web of radiant energy left in the wake of Luis's celestial sword. The massive black vortex rising from the demon lord's throne surged with one final burst of power and then, an instant later, winked out.

Is that… did we really…

Jade didn't have time to complete the thought before Ira was upon her. The angel swooped in, murder blazing in her eyes. Jade rolled to the side, avoiding her first strike and then parrying the next. She fired a bolt of Clinging Darkness straight into Ira's chest, but the spell only took hold for a few moments. The celestial dispelled it with a sharp gesture and drew back her blade to strike again, only to be blasted off her feet by a deafening wave of sound.

"Jade! Above you!"

Naomi's timely intervention gave Jade an opening to scramble to her feet, but she wasn't out of danger yet. Heeding her warning, she looked up.

"Oh, great."

Cracks split the stone ceiling, rapidly spreading from one end of the massive chamber to the other. Chunks of stone tore themselves away with a sound like cracking thunder, raining down on them like medieval artillery. Jade dodged to the side, avoiding a stone slab as it crashed into the ground nearby, kicking up an enormous cloud of dust. More debris fell from above and they scrambled towards the door, bursting back out onto the edge of the plateau just as the large structure collapsed behind them. Jade only had time to complete a quick headcount to ensure everyone had made it out before--

"Hyaa!"

Ira emerged from the cloud of dust with an explosion of stone fragments and a blood curdling shriek, blasting straight through a boulder-sized fragment of the building and hurdling towards Jade. Luis moved with uncanny speed, interposing his shield between them just in time to intercept the attack. Ira's flaming blade smashed against his shield with the force of a missile, driving the large man to one knee and sending more molten shards of metal spraying in every direction. The angel kicked off him, rising into the air and evading their attempts to counter.

"You will pay for this." She said, spreading her dark wings wide. Backed by the crimson light piercing through the thick clouds, the angel's visage was truly demonic. "I will not allow my kin to suffer any longer!"

Motes of starlight flared to life in the dark void of her outstretched wings, and from them erupted a hailstorm of radiant missiles. They lanced out in a broad cone, celestial spears that exploded with bursts of holy fire on impact. Jade dove for cover behind a collapsed pillar, but not before one of the projectiles caught her in the side. It pierced her armor's protective barrier, plunging into her flesh with a crackling hiss. Pain. White-hot pain exploded through Jade's body. She bit back a scream as the radiant essence tore through her demonic form, and everything started to fade.

No!

She couldn't give in. They were so close! Jade sucked in a ragged breath as more explosions rocked the courtyard. Flecks of celestial energy showered her as she huddled behind her meager cover, burning her wings and back. Nearby, someone screamed in pain. Tara? Damnit! She could barely see anything, and she couldn't move while Ira had them pinned down! How long could she keep this up?!

More spears of light cracked the flagstones nearby. More explosions. The barrage continued unabated. And then, Jade heard another crackle. She looked up in time to see Fenrin, an electrified arrow drawn back on his bow, leap out from behind the corner of the building he was sheltering behind. He let it fly right as one of Ira's spears caught him in the leg, taking him down in a flash of light.

"Fenrin!"

The arrow flashed over Jade's head like a bolt of lightning. Ira was hovering above the courtyard, unmoving as she flung spear after spear from her outstretched wings. An easy target, if one were willing to pay the price. Fenrin's arrow flew true, slicing through the onrushing swarm to embed itself at the base of the angel's wing. Lightning exploded across her body, interrupting her attack and sending spasms through her limbs.

"Jade! Naomi!"

Luis didn't have time to say anything else, but he didn't have to. The three of them had been together from the beginning. They had an opening, and they knew what to do with it. Jade burst into motion, flying fast and low across the courtyard back towards the still-collapsing cathedral. Her body protested, but she gritted her teeth and ignored the pain. Just… a little… further!

She flashed under Ira, skidding to a stop on the ground behind her and placed her palm on the ground right as Luis leapt into the air, latching onto her with another Frost Hook. He heaved with all his might, dragging her down and slamming her into the ground right in front of Naomi.

BOOM.

The bard strummed once, and the eruption of sonic energy from her guitar caught the angel before she could recover. She was knocked back… and slid straight into Jade's rune trap. Umbral chains erupted from the ground, ensnaring the disoriented angel as she fought to regain her bearings. They didn't give her the chance. They struck together, Luis from the front, Jade from the rear. Her daggers found the gaps in Ira's armor, targeting the points her Vital Sight highlighted with practiced precision. Luis took a more forceful approach, battering the angel down with sheer strength before she could recover her stance. One strike. Two. Three! Silver blood sprayed across the dark flagstones. The angel shrieked, whether in pain or rage Jade couldn't tell. She didn't stop, pushing her body to its very limit. This was where it ended. This was where they finally--

A surge of bright light flashed from Ira's body, melting through the chains that bound her and burning Jade's flesh. She cried out, instinctively shielding her eyes as she staggered back. That moment of distraction was all it took. Ira's foot connected with Jade's chest, and she felt something crack in her body. She collapsed, groaning, each ragged breath accompanied by a stabbing pain. Ira stood over her, armor cracked and torn, her face streaked with glistening blood.

"This is over." She said. Her hands were empty, she'd lost her sword somewhere along the way. Not that she needed it. Jade was in no condition to fight. Ira flinched, one hand clutching her head as she grimaced in pain. Jade felt it again, a mental force tugging at the angel's conciousness. But whatever it was, it wasn't enough. She raised an armored boot. "You've lost."

Luis lay nearby, dazed. He tried to pull himself up but slipped, crashing back to the ground. He, like Jade, was done.

"Hyaa!"

A flash of dark hair and a streak of sunlight cut into Jade's vision. Naomi burst out of the lingering cloud of dust and smoke, the angel's own blazing sword gripped tight in both hands. Ira spun towards her, but Jade managed to choke out one, ragged word.

"Noctis!"

A bolt of darkness struck the angel, clouding her sight. Her spell could only affect such a powerful being for a moment, but that was all it took. Ira faltered, and in that instant, Naomi drove the flaming sword straight through her chest. For a long moment, everyone was frozen in shock. Naomi took a faltering step back, as if she herself couldn't believe what she'd just done. Ira stared mutely down at the blade piercing her chest, her mouth open as if to speak. No words came out, only the soft gasp of her fading breath. Then she collapsed, lying motionless on a pile of broken stone, the sword inside her still sputtering with white flames.

"Jade. Jade!"

The world slowly came back into focus. Unfortunately, so did the pain. Jade groaned and immediately regretted doing so as that made the pain in her chest spike. Calming warmth flowed through her a moment later, soothing her aching limbs.

"You'll be alright." Siora was crouched beside her, hands pressed to her chest. Green energy spiraled down the alchemist's arms, sinking into Jade's body. "Sorry, I had to take care of Fenrin first."

"Is…" Jade coughed. Speaking hurt, but she pressed on. "Is he-"

"He's alright. So is Tara. They both took some bad hits, but Naomi is helping them. Everyone will be okay."

That was a relief. Jade lay back, allowing herself to relax.

"I'm more worried about you." Siora continued, frowning down at her. The elf looked exhausted.

"Me?"

"You're covered in burns, Jade. Your armor's wards broke down completely. They aren't coming back, you're practically defenseless now."

Jade shifted to inspect what she could see of the straps that comprised her "armor". Even that small motion sent a twinge of pain up her side, but it was growing more muted as Siora continued to heal her. Sure enough, the runes etched into her leather armor were sputtering and sparking, their normal glow muted and fading.

"It did its job." She grunted. "Remind me to thank the guy who made this for me."

The energy flowing down Siora's limbs flickered and then faded. The soothing warmth in Jade did so as well, leaving her aching, but intact.

"Sorry, Jade. That's all I have.""No, you did great." Jade allowed Siora to help her up. She took a few tentative steps, testing her body. She wasn't in great shape, but it would have to do. Unfortunately, it was still too soon after drinking Siora's special elixir for her to safely drink another potion. "We couldn't have done this without you."

The rest of her companions were gathering nearby. Nobody had emerged unscathed, but everyone was accounted for. The courtyard was quiet. Ira's body lay nearby, and the demolished cathedral lay in ruins not far beyond her. Above, the sky was beginning to brighten. The vortex of dark energy had dissipated with Vastrozun's defeat. Now, with nothing holding the portals to the Labyrinth open, the goddess would be able to start closing them. That wouldn't do anything to stop the demon forces who had already escaped, and more would no doubt slip free of this world before it was sealed away again, but it would have to be enough. They'd done what they could.

"Naomi." Jade hugged her friend as soon as she reached her, holding her close. There was so much that she wanted to say, but in the end, she only managed two words. "Thank you."

"Is this it, then?" Tara asked weakly. Her white armor was blackened and soot-stained, and blood covered one side of her face. "Did we do it?"

"I… I think we did."

"We should check to make sure." Fenrin croaked, gesturing towards the collapsed cathedral. "Since we came all this way, we should make sure we did it."

"There's no way anybody could have-"

"Naomi, don't say it." Luis said, gripping her shoulder and giving her a shake. "It's bad-"

As if the Universe had been waiting for that exact moment, the ground shook. A wave of dread ran down Jade's spine. They all turned to look at what remained of the cathedral. The plateau shook again and then, like a zombie emerging from the grave, an ashen arm broke free from the pile of stone. Another emerged, and a battered, bloody figure clawed itself free from the entombing rock.

"No…" Jade didn't want to believe what she was seeing. He couldn't have lived. He'd exploded.

Lord Vastrozun looked terrible. The cloud of darkness that had shrouded his features was gone, revealing a muscular demon with ash gray skin and sundered armor. Every bit of exposed skin was covered in angry welts that still leaked a mixture of light and dark smoke that hissed and crackled as they mingled in the air around him. Dark blood spattered the ground beneath him as he staggered towards them with an unsteady stride. But his eyes were bright and filled with lethal intent.

"I will not," he coughed, sending another spray of blood to spatter the ground in front of him, "be stopped. Not by the gods, and not by you. I have bested Death before… and she still cannot have me."
