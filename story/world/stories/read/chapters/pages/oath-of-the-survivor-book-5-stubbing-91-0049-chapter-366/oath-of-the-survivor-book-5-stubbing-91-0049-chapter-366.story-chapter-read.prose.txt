Kyle realized early on that he and C.H.A.D.D. made a mistake when preparing to fight against this creature.  They’d planned to face a large, brutish monster that would be primarily reliant on its physical dominance, using its stone manipulation abilities to hunt.  As such, they’d planned to bombard the creature from a distance to wear it down, before coming in to finish it off with Parasitic Resonance.  Instead, they found an adept elementalist; one capable of manipulating its domain with power and precision.  It was comfortable engaging them at a distance, and Kyle knew it would be just as adept at close-range combat.  From what he’d seen so far, Svynar’s belief that he could handle the hodag if it appeared at the village was questionable at best.  Its stone manipulation was simply that good.

Fortunately, they had a plan.  One of C.H.A.D.D.’s greatest disappointments in Kyle had been that he’d failed to secure the drone a stinkbug essence back on Earth.  While it wasn’t a perfect substitute, his greater connection to the World Tree Seed’s archives had given them access to something the drone had been giddy to experiment with.  Pungent plants.

Several basketball-sized objects appeared in the air near Kyle, each covered in a thick, spiky rind.  Even from this distance, Kyle wrinkled his nose a bit at the smell.  However, their true payload wouldn’t be revealed until the stink bombs broke.  He pushed the fruits off to his left with Aura Force while moving himself the opposite direction to draw the hodag’s fire.

He felt a headache coming on as he tried to split his focus three ways with the skill; blocking small attacks with planes of force, keeping himself moving to evade large attacks, and also gently guiding the olfactory concoctions out of the line of fire, avoiding crushing them in the process.  His focus only wavered more as he took several sharp attacks, unable to adequately block all of the smaller shrapnel as he focused on dodging the most deadly projectiles.  Adaptive Regeneration patched up the small wounds as they accumulated, but he knew he’d be in for trouble if he didn’t act.

With a snarl, Kyle blasted the falling fruits directly at the unsuspecting hodag.  Even beneath the thick stone armor, Kyle spotted its eyes glance toward them, and it retaliated by firing several smaller stones to intercept the slower-moving projectiles.  The defense did its job admirably, the rinds being shattered by the fast-moving stones.  Exactly as planned.

The moment the fruits were ruptured, they exploded in a foul-smelling liquid and cloud of pollen that covered the entire area.  Though it was expected, Kyle had to fight to flinch from the sudden stench.  The hodag had it worse.  The cloud of pollen expanded to cover it, the pressurized gas from within the fruit having done its job when the rind was punctured.  Despite the thick armor of flowing rock, the creature still needed to see and breathe.  That was the vulnerability that Kyle was aiming for.

The drone’s devious plan paid off as the hodag let out a terrible roar, that transformed partway into an explosive sneeze.  The moment it did, the floating stones in the air wavered, just as the thick stone armor cracked and fell off in places.  It was the opening Kyle needed.  For everybody in C Grade, strong mana control was a basic requirement.  This was something Kyle experienced during his fights against the hodags and Reynolds, as Parasitic Resonance struggled to overpower them despite his superior Willpower.  However, all mana control still required at least some focus.  Dealing large amounts of damage was a tried and true method to breaking that concentration, though C.H.A.D.D. had come up with something that would work almost as well.

Damage worked because it was a dramatic shock to the system.  That being the case, C.H.A.D.D. theorized that using something sufficiently unexpected, such as powerful odors could do the trick as well.  Though nothing in Earth’s records indicated any research on the topic, it was actually a fringe field of study within the Collective.  That was where they’d hit their first snag, however.  Most mundane odors just didn’t have enough bite to get past the Willpower of a D Grade or above.  His success with the Graft skill had changed that meaningfully.

Earlier on, C.H.A.D.D. combined seeds of the durian fruit, rafflesia, and corpse flower to create a non-awakened stink fruit that it theorized could be used as a less-violent method to deal with larger numbers of unawakened, something Kyle already felt very comfortable doing.  Once they’d learned to use Graft to improve the seeds, however, C.H.A.D.D. had gotten to work raising their quality into something that was artfully disgusting.

Resting in early D Grade, the fruits had three primary functions: foul, sticky liquid to limit movements, a cloud of stinking pollen to obscure vision, and as many histamine-producing components as possible.  The goal was to create an irritant to as many senses as possible to buy Kyle a precious moment before his opponents could get their bearings again.  It was a one-time gambit, and it paid off.  The moment the cloud of pollen exploded, Kyle swapped to Model: Shrike, took aim at the monster’s tail, and let loose.

A razor-sharp blade descended from Kyle’s position, the hodag too distracted to even attempt to intercept it.  The attack sliced through the remnant stone armor and bit deeply into the tail, severing through the upper layers of flesh and carving into the bone.  If this had been the same Model: Shrike that he’d first developed, that would have been the extent of the attack.  Thankfully, Marcus had offered some recommendations to improve the prosthetic.

As the bladed arm snapped forward to release its attack, smaller vines pulled it back as a leaf on the arm’s underside was exposed.  Releasing another wave of mana, he felt the arm crack as a second, smaller crescent blade of energy followed the first.  The follow-up attack crossed the first deep slash, and finished the job.  The hodag roared again in agony as its tail was severed roughly halfway down its length.  Kyle rightly suspected that neither Model: Shrike nor Model: Cannon would have been able to take the monster out in a single shot.  His glancing blow with Model: Cannon earlier proved that it was just too durable, doubly so with the remains of the thick stone armor covering its torso.  Even if he landed a big hit, it wouldn’t be out of the fight.

The pivot to attacking the tail worked out well.  His experience fighting the hodags reinforced how deadly the tail weapons were when they could be brought to bear, and the last thing he wanted was to be on the receiving end of this variant’s club-like tail.  Neutralizing it meant that he could fight with relative safety in mid-range while creating openings for follow-up attacks.

It was a point in his favor, but the hodag still had plenty of fight left in it.  The shockwave from its roar had dispersed most of the pollen, and the stabilizing and flowing rock told Kyle it had also adapted to the stench.  Its armor began to form again, thick plates staunching the bleeding from the stump of its missing tail.  It drew in a deep breath, and Kyle felt the mana gathering, even as he flew out of the way as it released its sonic attack.

The ground shook under the pressure, and Kyle was knocked off-balance despite having avoided the direct path of the attack.

Unfortunately, he’d picked the wrong direction to dodge.  He landed on the ground for a moment, and immediately felt his feet sink into the stone as it rose to grasp him.  He used Aura force to pulverize the grasping stones, but already waves of rock were rising to bind him while spears of stone shot straight for his chest and head.  Instinctively, he activated Storm Shelter to block the layers of stone threatening to encase him, while Aura Force intercepted the spears, shattering one and knocking the other off-course enough for him to tilt his head out of the way.

A moment later, Kyle was in near-darkness, the only light visible produced by Storm Shelter and the gentle golden glow of Genesis Engine.

[YOU NEED TO MOVE.]

Though it was faint through the thick layer of stone, Kyle could feel the hodag’s aura approaching as well.  It’s trying to kill me while I’m inside the stone.  The monster certainly had the skillset to do so between its natural weapons and stone manipulation.  Even if it just encased him in a thick enough layer of stone, he’d run out of air eventually.  That is, if he just stayed put and let it do its worst.

Pushing outward on Storm Shelter, Kyle willed the shield to expand, fighting to shatter the shell of stone surrounding it.  The moment he was free, he launched himself forward and underneath the hodag, narrowly avoiding one of its massive paws as it crashed down on the space he’d left behind.  Stone rose to bind him again, but this time Kyle was prepared.  Shattering the rocks around him with Aura Force, Kyle concentrated his efforts on several of the small cracks in the stone on the hodag’s underbelly.  He compressed blasts of force, then released them all at once in the weakened parts of the armor.

The destructive power wasn’t enough to fully destroy the stone or do meaningful damage to the hodag, but it opened a network of cracks across its body, which was all he could hope for.  With a quick activation of Cultivate, seeds appeared in the air near him, which were slammed into the newly-formed cracks with a blast from Aura Force.

That was all he had time for as the hodag dropped to body slam him, walls of stone rising on all sides to limit his movements.  With no other choice, he activated Storm Shelter, empowering the skill with Heart of Creation.  Silver light blazed in the darkness as the hodag descended, Immutability, Timelessness, and other concepts he still couldn’t quite comprehend bolstering his barrier to incredible heights.  The stone above him cracked as the small mountain of a monster crashed into his barrier, and it held.

Of course, Kyle still needed to find a way out from under the hodag.  For all of Heart of Creation’s power, Kyle still hadn’t been able to fully stabilize his skills when it was active.  He suspected that it overloaded the skills with various concepts from the World Tree Seed, and that his abilities just weren’t up to the task of functioning under the strain.  At least not yet.

Making a snap decision, Kyle infused Aura Force as well, just as he applied Adaptability to Storm Shelter.  He allowed the barrier to flow and move slightly, and launched himself toward one of the stone walls as fast as he could.  At the same time, he released condensed blasts of force directly at the entrapping wall, shattering the thick stone while Storm Shelter held against the weight of the titanic creature.

The moment he was out, he released both skills, watching the hodag crash into the ground with earth-shaking force.  Steam that smelled of rotten eggs erupted from several newly-formed cracks in the earth, and the monster gave Kyle another anger-filled roar as it turned to face him.

With both Storm Shelter and Aura Force unable to be used for the moment, Kyle’s strongest defensive options were gone.  His mobility was reduced, and he was staring down his most dangerous prey to date.  And he felt alive.  Model: Shield appeared as his left arm, and the verdant armor of leaves and bark began to glow golden as he poured energy into it with Infuse.  Finally, he withdrew a dark wooden staff with brilliant crimson petals.

The hodag lowered its horns and charged, and Kyle instinctively jumped as the ground beneath his feet tried to draw him in.  Vines lashed out to one of the fallen tree trunks that Kyle had dropped on the creature, pulling him out of harm’s way in the nick of time.  The hodag turned to him, roaring again as Kyle prepared himself to dodge.  So this is what a real fight at C Grade is like.
