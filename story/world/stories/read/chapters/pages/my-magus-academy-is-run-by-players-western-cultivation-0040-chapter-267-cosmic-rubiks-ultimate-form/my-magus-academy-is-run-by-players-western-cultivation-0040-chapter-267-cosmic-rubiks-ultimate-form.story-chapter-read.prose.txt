[Mission Accomplished!]

[Congratulations on passing the mission. You have successfully installed Brightmoon House’s sole heir, Lillie arc Brightmoon, as the rightful lord and ensured that every town and village within the territory submits, while also gaining recognition from the surrounding powers.]

[The reward (15,000 academy points and a Cosmic Rubik’s piece) has been issued.]

“Ooh, nice. With this, the magic artifact will finally be complete.”

The completion of the main mission had come as a pleasant surprise, causing Victor’s grim expression to brighten. Over the past week, the players had labored relentlessly, conquering every major settlement within the Brightmoon territory under the academy’s banner. Their efforts had not only brought stability to the region but also spread the name of the Sanctum far and wide.

From his observations, the so-called recognition of the surrounding powers was less about politics and more about acknowledgment of the academy’s existence and force that it posed, which was why this didn’t take too long. After all, most of them had joined the Merlin family invasion, and their Magi were captured. Plus, the players had clearly shown themselves to be a group that wasn’t to be taken lightly.

Without wasting another moment, Victor turned and went straight to the Nexus Beacon to claim his reward. Standing before it, he raised his hand, and the final Cosmic Rubik’s piece materialized in his palm, emitting powerful mana fluctuation.

“Finally… all four pieces.”

As he said that, he brought out the combined three pieces and fitted the final one into place. The moment it clicked, a blinding light burst forth, flooding the entire basement in radiant brilliance.

Victor squinted, raising an arm to shield his eyes as waves of dense magic energy surged from the floating Cosmic Rubik. Then, without warning, the artifact plummeted into his grasp, and its surface unfolded like dark purple liquid metal before enveloping his entire body. In an instant, it reshaped itself into a futuristic suit of armor. Its surface glimmered with constellations, lines of cosmic energy coursing through veins of silver and azure. Segmented plates adjusted and interlocked with seamless precision, moving as if the armor itself were alive and aware of its new bearer.

“So, this is… the Cosmic Rubik’s true form.” His voice was filled with awe.

The Shadowlink Mark on his wrist flared to life, linking directly with the magic artifact. At the same moment, a surge of power coursed through him from within the suit. The sensation was unlike anything he had ever experienced—mana, Chrono, and Cosmo elements intertwining and resonating in perfect harmony, flowing through every fiber of his being.

It almost felt as though he had reclaimed the power that was sealed because of the curse. How exhilarating.

Cosmic Rubik (Exosuit)

A complete magic artifact forged from the union of four ancient fragments said to originate from the forgotten stars beyond the astral space. When fully assembled, it transforms into a sentient, full-body exosuit capable of channeling the purest forms of cosmic energy. Its structure harmonizes with both spatial and temporal laws, allowing its wielder to bend reality itself on a limited scale. It can evolve alongside its master through consuming enough energy and comprehension.

Rank: Intermediate-grade magic artifact (can grow with the user)

“Holy shit! No wonder it required four pieces… The value of this magic artifact is beyond even an advanced-grade one!” Victor couldn’t help but exclaim, a wide grin spreading across his face. “A full-body exosuit that can consume and convert any form of energy… This is perfect for me.”

He flexed his fingers, and the armored gauntlet responded with mechanical precision. The dark purple suit’s texture was both metallic and ethereal, like condensed dark matter shaped into armor. Even with his power sealed, Victor felt confident he could take on a weak Nexus Temporal Magus while wearing it. And at his full strength… perhaps even challenge a peak one. This magic artifact was just that good!

The heavy gloom that had clouded his heart since the curse had taken hold seemed to vanish, replaced by a faint spark of exhilaration. His expression brightened with hope. He wanted—no, needed—to test the suit’s capabilities for himself. More than that, he could feel the armor’s power slowing down the curse’s corrosive energy even further. According to his readings, the decaying process had extended—what was once a single year had stretched into three.

Quickly, Victor deactivated the suit, letting it dissolve into motes of starlight before reforming into its compact Rubik form. To his surprise, the Cosmic Rubik could be stored directly within his Shadowlink Mark, a far more practical and secure option than his interspatial ring. Satisfied, he straightened his Magus robe and made his way upstairs.

The moment he opened the door to his office, a small fairy darted in front of him, her wings beating anxiously. “Master, I sensed a disgusting energy coming from the basement…” Lizbeth said worriedly. “Did something happen?”

Seeing that it would do him no good to hide the truth, Victor sighed. “Yes. I was hit by a curse, and a rather nasty one at that.”

Lizbeth gasped, her small hands flying to her mouth. “A curse? From who? How? The academy is protected by magic barriers… for it to be able to bypass them unnoticed…”

“I’m not entirely sure yet,” he replied, walking past her toward his desk, doing his best to mask any discomfort. However, the faint weakness in his movements didn’t escape her eyes. “But judging from its nature, I suspect the Doomsday Cult. Also, it’s called the Soulrot Curse. Have you ever heard of it?”

Her wings dropped in dismay. “Soulrot Curse? I’ve never heard of that before… Are you all right, Master?”

“For the moment, yes,” Victor said simply, taking a seat and leaning back against the chair. “If I hadn’t reacted quickly, I’d already be dead. It eats away at my core and infused mana, draining both my power and vitality. I’ve managed to seal it for now using Chrono energy, but it’s only a temporary fix.”

Before he could say more, Lizbeth flew toward him and wrapped her tiny arms around his head, pressing her cheek against his hair. “Master, you’re not going to die, are you? Who’s going to feed this precious, lovely fairy of yours then? Nooo! Don’t die yet!”

Victor almost laughed at her antics, the tension in his chest easing. “You’re worried about food even now? Also, don’t kill me off just yet.”

“But—”

“Don’t worry,” Victor interrupted gently. “I don’t plan on dying anytime soon. I’ll find a way to remove this curse. That much, I promise.” He reached up and patted her head softly.

Lizbeth looked at him for a moment before nodding. “I believe in you, my dear Master.”

“Good.” He returned the nod. “Still, this curse doesn’t behave like any Magus spell. I need more information before I can risk tampering with it again.”

He paused, rubbing his temples as his thoughts churned. “For now, I’ll head to the citadel after this. Their archives might have some record of this curse. If not, I’ll contact Valeria directly. As a powerful Magus, she’s bound to have some useful information. Or perhaps even the ability to undo the curse herself.”

Lizbeth bit her lip but nodded resolutely. “Then I’ll strengthen the academy’s defenses while you’re gone. No one will dare set foot here uninvited. The academy is safe with me, Master.”

A small smile crossed Victor’s face. “Mhm. I’ll leave that to you. Oh right, how’s the update going?”

“Well…” Lizbeth gave a wry smile and answered, “Some players got a little too excited about the new R18 content and tried to abuse the system, but were penalized for it. Others got overly enthusiastic about the meditation paradise and ended up fighting over the limited slots. But aside from that, everything’s running smoothly.”

“I see.” Victor nodded; much of what she told him was within expectations. He then prepared to leave and said, “All right, I’ll be going now.”

She hovered closer, eyes gleaming faintly with concern. “Please be careful, Master. That curse… whatever it is, it doesn’t feel like it belongs to this world.”

Victor’s gaze darkened slightly. “You’re right.”

Victor stood before the bottomless ravine where the pocket dimension was. Luckily, there was a Teleportation Waypoint straight to the entrance of the Steelrose Order’s pocket dimension. Otherwise, he would have had to take the long route through the treacherous Thornwood Forest, perhaps even enlisting the help of players to clear out magical beasts if he didn’t have the Cosmic Rubik.

He then pressed his fingers against the mark left by the overseers in the past, channeling mana into it. A ripple spread through the air before him as the fabric of space folded open, revealing a swirling portal of light. Without hesitation, he stepped onto the transparent stairs leading into the pocket dimension.

Right now, no players were nearby; most were focused on exploring Brightmoon Territory, while the few who frequented this place should already be inside.

As Victor entered the portal, a sense of disorientation hit him. However, because he still had a strong constitution, it didn’t bother him. Once inside, he took out the Cosmic Rubik. At a single thought, the magic artifact responded, unfolding in his palm and wrapping his body in an instant.

Victor glanced down at his gauntleted hands and smiled. The exo-suit hummed in resonance with his heartbeat, its internal systems stabilizing his mana flow and suppressing the curse beneath his chest.

“Since I got the chance, let’s test this suit’s abilities first before going to the mechanical tower,” he mused.

Victor’s eyes gleamed behind the helmet of the Cosmic Rubik suit. First, he adjusted the energy output to a fraction of its capacity — any more would have been excessive — and lifted smoothly into the air. The meadow stretched endlessly beneath him. Even from this height, he could sense several faint magical presences lurking beyond the trees.

Moments later, a low growl appeared across the grasslands. From between the shrubs emerged a pack of shadowy wolves — magical beasts whose bodies held Umbra properties, their bodies rippling with smoke-like darkness. Each radiated mana fluctuations on par with a first-stage Initiate Attunement acolyte.

“Perfect.”

Victor raised his gauntlet, and the runic veins etched across the armor flared to life. “Let’s start with Dimensional Phase Shift.”

His form blurred, flickering between planes of reality as the wolves lunged. Claws and fangs passed harmlessly through him, leaving only distortions behind. With a flick of his wrist, Victor activated Energy Conversion, condensing his stored mana into a crackling pulse of violet light.

A shockwave erupted outward, ripping through the meadow. Three wolves disintegrated instantly, their dark bodies scattering like mist. The survivors snarled and circled warily, but before they could strike again, the Adaptive Field deployed automatically, forming a near-invisible barrier of dark purple light that bent their attacks aside like ripples on water.

Victor smirked. “It even predicts movements based on mana flow. Impressive.”

He rushed at them, each step propelling him like a bolt of lightning. Within seconds, the remaining beasts were obliterated, reduced to drifting embers of mana and dust. The armor pulsed softly, absorbing the residual mana in the air, restoring its reserves.

“Good. Everything works flawlessly,” he murmured, filled with satisfaction over the suit’s power.

He was truly impressed by what this magic artifact was capable of. The first ability, Dimensional Phase Shift, allowed partial detachment from physical reality, granting intangibility and short-range space-time displacement. It resembled his Ethereal State spell, but far surpassed it in precision and scope. With this ability, he could likely evade even the attack of a powerful Nexus Temporal Magus without taking a scratch.

As for Energy Conversion, it enabled the transmutation of external energy sources into infused mana or raw output for offense and defense alike. With this, he would never again have to fear running out of infused mana mid-battle — so long as there existed any form of energy nearby that he could absorb and redirect.

Lastly, Adaptive Field Manipulation created a semi-sentient energy field that automatically adjusted its properties in response to threats, enhancing defense, amplifying attacks, or even reshaping the environment around him. It was, in essence, a living barrier that moved and reacted faster than thought itself.

Victor crossed his arms. “An artifact that strengthens, defends, and sustains its user… This isn’t just a suit; it’s practically a second body.”

Deactivating the barrier, he turned his gaze toward the distant mechanical tower rising beyond the hills. “Now… let’s see if I can find any useful information on the curse from the archives that the Steelrose Order left behind.”
