With the name decided, the guild 'The Band' was founded with Milla as the guildmistress. Rather than spend the rest of the day celebrating and getting up to speed with what everyone had been up to during the last weeks and months, everyone decided it was high time they struck out and found something to fight. There would be plenty of time to navigate the guild interface later.

Going forward, dungeons would be tackled by a single party or perhaps two, but just this once, Milla wanted them all to go together. That would be their way of celebrating the formation of a guild that would take the city, then the country, and finally the world by storm.

Using the map-system, they all teleported to 34th Street-Hudson Yards. Luke lagged behind a little for a brief meet with Lana, who ran in to hug Milla the moment everyone else disappeared. Once they'd exchanged a few pleasantries, that was all they had time for at the moment, Luke turned to Milla. "You're not coming?"

"Nah," she said. "I've got some catching up to do with Lana. And I've decided to class change into the Guildmistress class, so I won't be doing much more in the way of fighting."

"Didn't know that was a thing."

"Same," Milla said. "Until just now."

Arriving at the teleport location, after saying some quick goodbyes, Luke found a cave-like underground subway station that looked somewhat modern and didn't smell all that bad. People were walking up a long flight of stairs, and when Luke followed, emerging into the street, he directed them to the portal they were looking for.

The teleport map-device would be able to show reported portal sightings, but that required a few upgrades first, so Luke had stepped in to feel out the location of a portal. He'd done a decent job of it, too, he had to say, since it was less than a five-minute walk from the subway station.

He chatted a little with Kenny on the way and found that he'd stayed active. As a healer, his services had been in demand, so he'd reached a respectable Level 18 in his class, which was just called 'Healer.' While the name wasn't all that inspiring, Kenny assured him it was good at keeping people alive, and what more could you ask?

Triss, with her debuffs and curses, hadn't had much luck in finding a party, so she'd followed along with crowds of Integrated and supported as best she could from a distance, whittling down the health of larger monsters or debuffing hordes. She, more than anyone, was happy to have found a place where she'd get to fight alongside the same group of people again and again.

Once inside the dungeon, with Ray entering first to set the difficulty at a reasonable level, they found themselves in a large, circular arena. The floor was sand, and rows upon rows of benches surrounded them. Rather than a roaring crowd, though, it was dead quiet.

New Quest: Assassinate the Chancellor.

Reward: 5,000 Credits.

In the distance, a thick-looking gate rose into the air, pulled open by ropes. From the darkness beyond, monsters charged out, barreling toward The Band. They were... pigs. Or boars, more like. Hundreds of them, small with gleaming tusks. As the enemies charged forward in a wave, a figure appeared on a finer chair at the top of the stands, almost directly opposite the now open gate.

"Meet your doom, heretics!" the figure bellowed.

Counselor Seerjme. Level 20.

"Is that guy... a pig?" Ray asked, coming up to stand next to Luke, while most of everyone else either flung spells and loosed arrows toward the stampede, or readied themselves to meet the boars head-on.

"Kind of looks like one," Luke said, scratching the back of his head.

The Counselor stood on two legs and had long arms and legs, like a human, but his face was that of a boar, complete with beady little eyes, a pig snout, and tusks. When the monster up there spoke, you could see saliva dribbling down its immaculate white robes, even from such a long distance.

"It's a counselor, not a chancellor," Ray continued, shaking his head. "It's not the guy."

Luke nodded as he kept a close eye on the stampede rushing toward the guild. It wouldn't do for a whole bunch of them to get injured, or even die, in their first dungeon together as a guild. "Guess not."

"Screw this. The quest is designed for me. That much is obvious, since I was the first one to enter this place. I'll head out and find this Chancellor dude and make sure we finish the quest."

With that, Ray disappeared. A moment later, he re-appeared up by the still screaming pig-man. Before the monster could react, it was leaking blood through a deep cut along its throat. Ray didn't even stay to make sure it died. It did.

Spells, arrows, and other skills thinned out the herd of rushing boars, and the more melee-oriented Integrated dealt with the remaining bunch. Luke, who thought the others had the situation well in hand, hadn't bothered to step in. Instead, he headed over to Lemira, who stood off to the side with Elijah, who had also opted to stay out of it.

"Hey! Not joining in on the fun?" Luke asked.

Lemira seemed to give the question some thought before answering. "No. My current pets are ill-suited to a field of battle such as this one, I think. Perhaps if I had something more defense-oriented in my current stable."

"I used to have a stable," Luke said, unable to stop himself from thinking of Smudge. The little bastard had up and disappeared on him. Since then, there had been no hint of his returning. Luke hadn't given up, though. If anything, the void itself should watch out, or Smudge might eat it.

"Your class has companions?" Lemira asked as the other Integrated dispatched the last few boars. No one had sustained any sort of injury.

"No," Luke said before explaining the little voidling and how he'd come to be in Luke's position.

"That's," Lemira said, looking stunned. "I have never heard of anything like that before."

Luke shrugged. "It was weird. And the situation where I lost him wasn't the most fun time one can have."

After explaining that part, it was Elijah's turn to put a question forward. "You've gone to a different system and found a way to teleport yourself back?"

A larger monster appeared out of the gate, the exact same one Luke had fought in the Tutorial Dungeon. This was turning out to be quite the nostalgic day.

"Yeah," he said, not taking his eye off the Groll Champion. "Almost killed me, though."

"You are a man of many talents, Luke," Lemira said as she, too, turned her attention to the hulking monster. Her eyes went wide, and just like that, she was off, hurrying toward the larger group of Integrated, preparing to face off against the disgusting-looking creature.

"What's she up to now, then?" Elijah asked.

Luke chuckled. "She wants another pet, would be my guess."

Elijah looked a little nervous. "Do you think I should maybe follow and make sure she isn't hurt?"

Sam and Mas, wearing maroon jumpsuits for the occasion, were in the process of pulling health straight from the monster, taking far more than what they'd been able to last time Luke saw their abilities, and Priyah held it in place. Sure, she was struggling, her whole body trembling, but she was holding what amounted to a boss monster while Nash and Savannah rushed it. They'd grown stronger, the lot of them. Good, because they would need it going forward.

"I think you're fine to stay here, Luke said. "It's more a question of whether she'll be able to hold the others back long enough for her to claim the core."

Waiting to see what would happen, Luke took the opportunity to contact someone who should've been present.

Luke: "Curtis. Where'd you go?"

Curtis: "Hannah is already on my ass about missing the meeting thing. I'll join the guild when I'm back, don't worry."

Luke: "Fair enough. So, what are you up to that's so important?"

Curtis: "Intruders. I've felt them a bunch all over the city, but the trail goes cold before I can catch up. Not this time, though. I've got my eyes on one, and this time, it isn't getting away."

Curtis: "I think they're looking for something. Either that, or preparing for something."

Luke: "Like what? And Curtis, maybe you should have someone with you. Going after them alone might not be the best idea, you know?"

Curtis: "I asked my brother, but they're in a dungeon, like every other time I've reached out. What's the point of having a little brother if you can't boss him around?”

Curtis: "You're welcome to come, but I know you're in a dungeon too."

Ray: "I've found the bastard. Looks tough."

Luke: "Ray. Can you take him yourself?"

Ray: "My dagger is looking for another soul to feed on. This'll be a piece of cake. Just give me a moment."

Luke: "If everything goes to plan here, Curtis, we should be just about ready to wrap this up. I'll come find you afterwards. Wait for me?"

Curtis: "If I can. Not losing the scent again."

In the end, Lemira failed to capture the monster core of the Groll Champion. The Band faced off against another two waves of monsters, but beat them back without issue. Only then did they get the expected update.

Quest Complete: The Chancellor has fallen.

Dungeon Closing in 60 minutes.

Hannah (Guild): "Well done, gang. Let's head back out."

Nash (Guild): "Oh, yeah!"

Nash (Guild): "Let's go party!"

Milla (Guild): "Like Hannah said, well done, everyone. Return to headquarters now. I've got a surprise waiting for you. Be careful on the way back. And Nash, please don't spam the guild chat. I would hate to mute you."

Luke: "Done here, Curtis. Heading for you now."

No answer came.
