Why has Prince Mahk remained single?

We all know how Families socialize. They announce their eligible scions, they parade them through various parties and interactions until the most recent generation finds itself paired off, one way or another.

Even the newest families, those with next to no standing, join in the routine. His sister had a Consort, a pairing that lasted a few decades before souring. She remained alone for the remainder of her life, at least officially.

Mahk on the other hand has never once been seen with anyone who was not clearly a temporary arrangement. In the past he would escort others to events, but from the moment he was appointed High Duke he has maintained his solitude.

He claims to have descendants, to be a patriarch. Clearly he must be, the Velli nose is iconic and there are no other acknowledged children in his generation.

Who is the mother? No, who are the mothers? Where are they? What happened to them? Why have none ever come forward?

Someday the truth will be revealed, someday we will know.

Seb looked across the cavern. There had been a noticeable increase in the challenge compared to their last trip to the Depths, the goblins seemed smarter, more coordinated, less willing to commit to doomed assaults unless left with no other choice. The same thing had happened in the past, an extra level pushing them into new behaviour when everything else remained the same.

Except it hadn't remained the same, at least not for this cavern. They'd run across the occasional giant rat over the years, almost always in the middle of a tunnel where there was no difficulty, occasionally one would wander into their campsite, almost always prompting Anya to tease him about the first time it had happened.

This time however there had been a pack of them, or perhaps several packs. The goblins worked with them, almost as though they had raised and trained them. Rats as mounts, rats as pressure, rats as-

Seb spun away from the sight, doubling over and gasping at the feelings filling the band. Similar sounds came from Gwen while Anya and Cara had it worse. He-

-looks like Mithrax's dinner, Cara sent.

Stop, Gwen sent. Don't look.

I.. it- Anya sent before the nausea swept across all four of them once more.

Seb clamped down on his thoughts as best he could, pulling his aura close to limit his awareness of the corpses around them. Cara was closest, Gwen was already at Anya's side and trying to steer her to the entrance, away from the battle.

We won't trip, he sent as he slipped under Cara's arm. Keep your eyes closed.

No- she began as they lurched towards the entrance. See in my thoughts. Stare at the ground.

Seb nearly felt the ground rise up to meet him as Anya dropped to the floor in the tunnel. They weren't in the meld, they weren't fully connected yet at the same time they were. The feeling started to fade, her emotions and thoughts stabilizing as she took slow breaths.

Almost there, he sent. Almost out.

He felt Gwen's approach, her footfalls sounding heavier than usual as she joined them and took Cara from him.

I've got her, Gwen sent. We're just outside.

Seb straightened and moved to join Raoul who stood watching from the entrance.

"What happened?" Raoul asked.

"The battle? It went as expected. A good test of our advancement."

"I meant the aftermath."

"I don't know," Seb admitted. "We were fine and then."

"It's my fault," Cara said, leaning against the wall next to Anya. "I thought of Mithrax's meal last night as I was looking at the rats and…"

Seb caught a hint of green spreading through Cara's hair before Gwen knelt next to her, visibly vibrating with concern.

"Meal?" Raoul asked.

"They joined us for dinner at basecamp," Seb explained. "His choice of meal was freshly-charred haunch."

"I see. Perhaps not the best choice of cavern then."

"It wasn't this bad last night," Cara said. "It was weird and definitely not what I'd want to eat, but it wasn't-"

"It was gross," Anya said. "I know Seb will eat raw steak, but this was beyond that."

"It was different," Seb said. "It was impressive how-"

Seb! Anya sent. Stop thinking about it, please.

"The remaining caverns should not have anything except goblins, and perhaps their larger cousins," Raoul said.

Anya screamed, the nearest goblins physically recoiling from the sound as she slipped out of the pack. By the time the creatures recovered Cara had taken her place, freeing her to unleash her emotions on the incoming reinforcements.

It had taken longer than she would have liked for them to recover from the mishap with the rats. She felt a flicker of unease at the memory and turned it into additional force behind her blow. She'd been fine dealing with the aftermath, she'd been fine the night before watching Mithrax tear into their meal with bites that defied logic. Whole chunks had been torn out as easily as she might bite into cake.

Anya? Seb sent. Do you..?

No, she sent. Cara's got them.

We're good, Gwen added. Feels faster this way.

Another goblin fell, blown off its feet into another pair, giving her all the space she needed to handle an incoming strike. It was faster, it was smoother, she could hold the line, trap the threat so it could be whittled down, but Cara did so better still. Seb was more mobile, but when a more durable threat appeared she was best suited to eliminate it.

No. Rats, Cara sent. We have this.

Anya felt her lips curl as she dove towards the pair of hobgoblins, at least that's what Gwen had called them. Closer to grey than green and over five feet tall, they were strong enough to actually pose a momentary threat individually. The first one had nearly surprised them, throwing a spear from beyond Seb's awareness. No one had been hurt, the creature had clearly misjudged their approach, but it had been enough to keep them on their toes.

When her first strike barely staggered the beast she felt a flicker of concern. Her next blow, thrown from a balanced position, sent it to the ground and she relaxed. They were still slower than her, still weaker, still small enough to be more dangerous in numbers than individually.

A second pair came around the corner to reinforce them, only for all four to flinch as Energy burst around Seb's blade as it flew past her. Bladesong granted her further advantages, turning the new threat into something barely more challenging than most goblins.

Thanks, she sent.

Of course, Seb answered. We're clear, except for mopping up.

The rush of interest from Gwen and Cara made it clear they intended to take on the last of the goblins. Cara bowled into every creature that she could find while Gwen bashed her way through the ones that came running at them. Seb managed to pick off a few stragglers, leaving the cavern silent.

Feel better? Seb asked.

Much, Cara sent.

I would have been fine if Cara hadn't made the comparison right when she did, Anya sent. I can think about it now without any issue.

I was fine looking at the rat until Anya reacted, Cara sent. It didn't bother me.

It was weird, Gwen sent. It wasn't bothering me, I felt fine, but I barely…

Anya knew Seb had thought of something but Raoul was approaching, they had all agreed they weren't going to face fewer caverns just because they'd been delayed after the first. Time to get going.

We reacted to each other, Seb sent. Compounded how we felt.

That isn't supposed to happen, Gwen sent. Not… Later. We need someone who knows more about bonds and bands to confirm a few things.
