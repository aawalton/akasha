Adrian quickly followed Rose as he wore his shoes in a hurry. Without tying his lace, he simply jumped down the carriage, closing the door as he did.

"Could you two perhaps come to White Tiger Guild's office the day after tomorrow?" Bella questioned, as she looked behind at the Weeble siblings from her seat.

"What for?" Rose questioned with a neutral expression.

Bella raised her eyebrow, an 'isn't it obvious?' expression spreading across her face. "To collect the bounty money, of course."

"We wouldn't need…" Rose began to speak, but before she could complete her sentence, Adrian slapped slightly at her back.

"We'd be at the guild by midday," Adrian replied with a small smile, his enthusiasm leaking out.

"What was that for?" Rose blurted out, her drowsiness vanished and frustration evident.

But ignoring him, Adrian continued talking with Bella. "It's already quite late, Miss Bella, you should get going," he said. "You also need to take Sir Alan to his home, so you shouldn't waste your time with us. Have a nice day… uh, I mean have a mean night."

Bella looked at Rose with mild amusement, who was hissing at Adrian like an angry cat. A subtle smile spread on her lips. Bella turned back and took the reins of the horse in her hand. "Have a mean night too, Adrian," she said with a chuckle.

With that, Bella drew away with the carriage while Adrian remained standing at the back, flustered.

"What the fuck did I say?" Adrian said with a shocked expression.

"Mean night," Rose repeated before laughing at Adrian as if her life depended on it.

"Hey! It wasn't that funny," Adrian retorted.

"Well maybe it isn't funny, cause you can't really see your face," Rose said as she retracted a small circular mirror from the pocket attached with her dress. She opened it and pointed it towards Adrian.

"Just shut up!" Adrian shouted, slapping the mirror to close it again as he began walking at a fast pace.

Rose stopped laughing. She rubbed a bit of tears that had come in her eyes because of it. She brisk-walked towards Adrian, catching up to him. Looking at his face, she spoke up, "Why did you stop me? We weren't in it for money, were we?" Rose questioned.

"We were not, but that's just for the 200 gold coins the head knight brought, since that was needed to bargain with the White Tiger Guild," Adrian said. "I never said I wouldn't take the bounty money awarded by the viscount. I mean, it's not like I am a money-hungry person, you know!" Adrian exclaimed.

"Is there some artifact you have your eyes on?" Rose questioned.

Adrian's eyes widened. "How do you know?" he questioned genuinely shocked.

Rose smirked. She placed her hand behind her head and crossed them, walking in a carefree manner. "I could just tell," she replied.

Adrian let out a long sigh as he began to speak. "It's an artifact called Tears of Dragon," Adrian said, looking straight ahead. Then he turned his eyes towards Rose as he continued, "They aren't related with dragons, just so you know."

"Bummer," Rose said with disappointment. "How do they work then, make you cry?" she said mockingly.

"Any mage could store their mana in it and either use that mana to recharge them, like how Miss Bella gave you some of her mana. Or they could blast it, and in its full capacity, its blast radius is like 50 meters!!!" Adrian added with excitement.

"It could recharge my mana level?"

"No, it could recharge my mana…" Adrian said, looking at Rose, but he quickly corrected himself. "I mean yeah, you could use it too, I guess," he added in a sad voice after a brief pause.

"Nah, don't worry," Rose said. "I would take one of those for me too if I ever need it. About the goblins, I believe I had killed 100+ Hobgoblins and 2 Goblin Commanders, so it's probably 30+ gold coins, perhaps 40 if I include goblins in it too," she said. "But, I need 10 gold coins for something!"

"You have finally decided to invest in armor?" Adrian questioned, his eyes flickering with mock astonishment.

"Nah, there's this cool-looking blue gem necklace I want, and since I earned a lot during this fight, I could obviously invest a bit on some stuff I like now, can't I?" Rose said.

"Sure," Adrian replied with a nod. "Talk about wasting money," he whispered.

"It's not wasting money, mister. Looking good is a part of adventuring too!" Rose added with an enthusiastic look. Her gaze turned towards the left. A familiar tavern called Esclaris was now in her vision. "Hungry?" she questioned.

To which Adrian nodded without a moment's delay.

Both Adrian and Rose,quickly walked into the tavern. The main gate of the tavern was made up of transparent glass, while the walls were simply made with golden and grey bricks. It was supposedly the owner's favourite colour combination despite it looking like a prison. The food though was extremely good and slightly costly for a normal adventurer of Alcia.

"Welcome, Sir & Ma'am," the waitress immediately said. Her bunny ears and lack of pupils were enough to know she was of Cunelin race. "Oh it's you, Rose," the girl blurted out as she placed her free hand on her mouth.

"Yup!" Rose replied enthusiastically. "What's special today?" Rose questioned, her eyes gleaming with curiosity and excitement.

"Special menu, huh…" Fiona mumbled. She placed her free hand on her chin as she thought for a second. "If it's you, I bet you'd love some Cockatrice meat!" Then Fiona looked at Adrian and narrowed her eyes as she looked at him for a bit. "For you, Adrian, we have some gobl…"

"Ha ha ha," Adrian laughed sarcastically. "You take me for a dog, Fiona?" he added, narrowing his eyes with a bit of frustration.

"Oh no no," Fiona replied, "There's no way I could think of Master adventurer Adrian as a dog. I was just messing with you," Fiona added. "Would you like Basilisk too or your usual medium-rare steak?" she questioned.

"Hmm…" Adrian mumbled. "Is Cockatrice egg already sold?" he questioned.

"Nope," Fiona replied as she shook her head.

"Then a simple omelette would do just fine, nothing too fancy," Adrian replied.

"Okie dokie," Fiona said with a playful salute.

"Fiona! Enough of playing around with the customer! Table 2 & 3 requires cleaning," the tavern owner, an old lady with brown hair and Dwarven heritage, yelled, causing a frightened expression to spread on the poor girl's face. The young maid Fiona ran towards the tavern's counter and quickly moved into the inner room to take care of the dirty utensils in her hand.

"Miss Darya is surely quite tough on Fiona," Adrian mumbled as he looked at the whole ordeal. He walked towards the empty table and took his seat.

Rose gave a small smile and an acknowledging nod to the dwarf who looked in her direction with a smile. She followed her brother and took a seat opposite to him. "Wake me up when food's here," Rose mumbled, leaving a tired yawn.

"Sure sure," Adrian mumbled sarcastically, but Rose ignored his tone and went to take a quick nap, leaning on the back of the green cushy sofa. She sure can sleep anywhere, Adrian thought to himself. He looked around the tavern, noticing the familiar face of adventurers and a few regulars. Not like anyone would care much about a sleeping wild Rose, he mentally added, leaving a sigh.
