A Maus sized, blue-black leather vest materialised.

“Look at that, Maus,” Scarlet said, beckoning her Familiar closer. “You’ll have a voice all your own.”

Maus Blinked off his pillow and onto Scarlet’s lap. Then he raised himself onto his hind legs. Scarlet carefully looped his arms through the holes and handed him the two front flaps.

“You can do it,” she said, sending him a mental image of how to fasten the closure of the garment.

His movements were careful, but the little guy had way more than enough STR and DEX to pull it off on his first try, earning him a gentle finger five.

Then Scarlet had to turn away for a moment to regain her composure. She pressed her fingers against her tightly closed lips as she processed her Familiar’s impossible adorableness.

“Boss?” He tried.

Instead of his voice sounding directly inside her mind, it echoed softly through the room.

"Boss? Am I still the most handsome?" He asked, twisting this way and that in his new vest. Scarlet turned back, her face solemn.

"You look very dapper."

"Dapper?" he repeated.

"Yeah, buddy," she said. "Very dapper.” Then she looked back at the notification. Even without the added capital, the item alone is an acceptable consolation prize for surviving the assault of a Tier 2 at Tier 0.

Instead of materialising a literal ‘token’, the System simply transformed her old cane. In front of her eyes, she watched as possibly her oldest piece of gear floated up and began to change.

It became a rich, dark wood that shimmered beneath the bunker lights. Its new handle was elegantly hooked, perfectly polished, incredibly sturdy, and made of a metal alloy Scarlet had yet to work with. The whole thing seemed to almost shimmer with restrained potential as the transformation completed.

Scarlet plucked the descending cane from the air. The artefact had excellent balance. Scarlet had an urge to learn some sort of staff combat just to compliment it.

On the side was a discreet button hidden within the grip. Curious, she pressed it.

She felt a metaphysical tug as the artefact bound itself to her. Then the hooked handle immediately shifted and reshaped beneath her fingers. The cane transformed into the hilt of a dagger as sheathed blade slid impossibly free from within its slender shaft. It was long and wide enough that it had to use some form of spatial manipulation.

[Scion's Sceptre – Tier 1: An exceptionally durable walking cane that can act as either a concealed blade or a bludgeoning weapon. The blade causes a continual bleed effect that is exceptionally resistant to healing. The cane acts as a discreet amplifier for psionic energy.This artifact grows alongside its userSoulbound]

Scarlet inspected the weapon carefully, allowing herself a satisfied nod.

Perhaps the System sensed her reluctance to part with the weapon that she’d beyond outgrown. It had become increasingly obsolete throughout the Trial, yet somewhere along the way it had stopped being merely a tool.

It was her cane.

Scarlet sighed and looked down at her familiar notebook. It contained meticulous accounting of everything that had happened since waking up to find Maus gone.

It was bittersweet to realise this might be the final time she wrote in it

It didn't take long to organise her remaining thoughts and note them down.

She wasn't yet capable of writing with the effortless elegance the Historian had displayed while drafting their contract, but she was approaching printer speed. Two pens floated beside her, writing in perfect synchronisation as page after page filled with observations, hypotheses, conjectures, theories, and carefully organised notes.

Once the last page was filled, Scarlet pulled out her Inscriber and quietly worked the final enchantments into it. Now, without the correct combination of mana signature, psionic signature, and DNA from both herself and Maus (who was somehow considered a biologically separate entity, despite being a part of her), anyone trying to open the notebook would find it had become a very temperamental explosive.

Satisfied that the notebook was secure, she placed with the growing stack of journals that had quietly become the record of her life since the System descended.

Scarlet lingered for a moment, her thoughts on the stack.

Together, they chronicled only a handful of days on Earth and several weeks within the Portal World, yet somewhere between the first page and the last, she'd transformed into a woman she wondered if Day One Scarlet would recognise.

A life completely changed, or as the Historian might have said: ‘A history created.’

Scarlet leaned back against the chaise lounge and let out a slow breath. She took a single moment to come to terms with the past weeks. Then she stood and headed to her ‘notebook’ shelf.

She pulled a particularly lovely, leatherbound, embossed one she’d been saving for a special occasion.

She grinned, the expression coming easily now that she was no longer under surveillance. She hadn’t even realised she was doing it. She heard Maus just as she resettled on the chaise.

“I like your smile, Boss. Very dapper!” His voice was warm, and entirely sincere.

Scarlet froze, then the smile slowly faded from her face. She swallowed and looked at her Familiar where he had resettled onto his little pillow.

"...Thank you, buddy," she said quietly before refocusing on her notifications.
