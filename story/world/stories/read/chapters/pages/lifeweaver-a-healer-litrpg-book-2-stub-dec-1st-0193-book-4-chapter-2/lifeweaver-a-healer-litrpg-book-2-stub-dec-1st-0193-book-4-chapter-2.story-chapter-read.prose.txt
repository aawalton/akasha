The streets of New York weren't safe anymore. Other than The Band, few Integrated braved the emptiness. All non-Integrated had left long ago, leaving a shell of a city behind. Now, with the Singers having arrived and taken over, the chances of running into someone had dwindled to almost zero. That was fine for Luke's purposes. He had decided that hiding wasn't in his best interest this time around. Since he wasn't far from their portal, patrols grew more numerous. Moving about unseen would have been almost impossible without the robe giving him an edge. Even with it, it would have been impossible. Luke raised his hand and waved when a Singer among a patrol of three spotted him even from a distance.

The trio approached with caution, holding up their hands in greeting before gesturing for Luke to pull the hood down. While the Singers had invaded Earth, they didn't kill every single Integrated they happened upon. Their ultimate goal was still unknown, but it was not the total annihilation of the original inhabitants. Showing his face would elicit one of two responses. Either they attacked him on sight, since he'd taken down quite a few Singers already, or they would 'arrest' him and take him back to Singer leadership, since he was on a list of names of people who were invited to the Singer system due to a deal struck by Relian. The only reason Luke's name was on that list, that any names other than Relian's were on it, was because The Fallen Shepherd had covered his bases and had figured he might leave Earth in any number of bodies, and a Lifeweaver's body was what he seemed to desire most.

Integrated Solutions Group had turned Dot over to the Singers the moment she rejoined them. When Luke sent her back to Earth from the 'destiny dungeon' as the Singers named them, she healed the injured members of The Band, despite many of her guild members having just died right where she stood. After that, though, she left, despite Milla explaining what was happening with the Singers. Luke shook his head as he approached the trio on patrol. He had given up trying to understand that young woman's motivations. They gestured for him to lower his hood again, and this time he did as they asked.

The flash of recognition in the group leader's eyes was instant, and the tall, oval but humanoid face turned into an expression of surprise. His grip on the pommel of the sword he carried at his hip tightened, but he didn't draw the weapon.

"Luke Quinn," the Singer said, his voice low and confident, only betrayed by the slightest shudder, "Lifeweaver. We welcome you to Singer territory. Please follow me."

They had gone with option B then. Now it was up to Luke to decide how this would go. Either he followed them, acting meek and accepting their invitation to join them, or he attacked and got it over with. If he was quick enough, he might be able to finish them off and disappear into Central Park before a second patrol showed up. Sighing, he discarded that option. Even if he killed them, they would have had time to raise the alarm. Fighting all the way to the portal would not work since he needed to focus and meditate when he got there. The only way they would let him do that would be if they were all dead, and that was beyond Luke's abilities, despite his high level and his understanding of the weave.

"Fine," Luke said. "Let's go."

At the end of the previous dungeon, he had reached Level 46, gaining three levels when Ezezal choked on death energy while being hanged by his own system. The attribute points from that, Luke had shoved into Intelligence, Focus, and Wisdom, getting the first ones up to the nice, round figure of fifty, and the latter to forty-seven. The skill points had been a little more difficult to spend, since he had gone through the list and picked the most appetizing options a few hours before leveling up, so he'd saved up the points so far, not picking anything. As he walked in the middle of the group of Singers, he considered the list of available skills again, but still wasn't sure he needed any of them, and level fifty wasn't that far off anymore. Maybe he'd get new options to pick from then.

"Are you taking me to your leader?" Luke asked.

The trio gave him a look but didn't answer. Probably not, he figured. Allowing Luke anywhere near their leadership would be too great a risk, no matter what deal they had signed. Said deal didn't stipulate anything about him having to play nice, but if he killed a general or something, he was pretty sure they'd decide to just do away with the whole thing. Either way, he was sure to get a good and close look at the portal this way. He had considered weavestepping to the Singer Lifeweaver he met and rescued in the pink dungeon, Luom, but decided against it. In doing so, he would end up in the Singers' real territory, their system, and he wasn't sure it would be anywhere close to the portal. From what she had told him, she wasn't much of a frontline fighter. Also, jumping over to a different system would put him on his ass for a while, and returning would be difficult without a whole lot of mana to draw upon, since his own would be pretty much wiped after one jump. The Mana Orbs Penny had gifted him would be a help, but they wouldn't be enough. So, having discarded that idea, Luke went with the first and best idea he had been able to conjure: walk up to them and say hello.

Mana surged through the three Singers' mana channels, and their muscles showed how tense they were. Happening upon Luke and transporting him back to their headquarters had not been on their bingo cards for their day, he figured. Even when he did his utmost not to arouse suspicion and made no move to attack, they treated him like a wild animal about to pounce on them. They had no idea. Meeting Lifeweavers in the dungeon hadn't changed Luke's feelings about the invasion, not one bit, nor had the removal of the emotional blocker disappearing. The Singers had come to Earth to take over and make the planet and system theirs. No matter how you looked at it, that was a mistake Luke would make sure they regretted until the moment their own system winked out of existence. For now, though, he would play nice.

"What are your names?" he asked them, keeping his voice calm but assertive.

They didn't answer him, didn't even let out as much as a peep, and he didn't ask again. Having walked for about ten minutes, they'd ended up on the road following along the west side of Central Park. They were getting closer now. The road itself was a mess of abandoned cars, bikes, and even baby strollers, along with all sorts of other crap. When people left, they didn't bother taking most of their stuff with them. That made the walk slow going, and the Singers moved them over to the park proper. With the electric grid down, it was getting dark fast, despite it still being summer. Had to be getting late. Luke's internal clock was all over the place now that his sleeping habits had changed. With every level he gained, it seemed his need for eating and sleeping decreased. An hour or two per night was more than enough now, a few more if he'd been in a fight resulting in a near-death experience. Since he didn't own a clock other than his phone, and the battery on that was long-dead, the passage of time was starting to turn a little fuzzy. Even so, dark meant night. Not so long ago, you wouldn't have wanted to be anywhere near the park at night during a power outage, but now it was just dark and silent except for a couple of dots of light in the distance.

As they approached, Luke saw they were other Singer patrols. The ones escorting him didn't have a light, but the others still found the group and joined in without a word. Several more groups added to their numbers, and Luke soon walked in the middle of an honor guard at least thirty strong. Weaver's Perception made him aware of each individual and their strengths. Not in a numbers way, but he could guesstimate a person's strength with relative precision just by the mana running through their channels and the strength of their Meta-Heart, as well as their muscle mass and density, lung capacity, etc. If push came to shove, Luke thought he would be fine even against so many. Few among them had any real strength to boast of, and his own power had increased a lot even since the Singers opened their portals and landed on Earth.

His chance of beating them all grew smaller as they approached a well-lit area near the northwestern part of the park. They had arrived in the area surrounding the portal. Here, some real power-houses made themselves known. A few, Luke even recognized.

"Luke Quinn!" one shouted, leaving a conversation she'd been having with some other Singers around a table out in the open. "Prepare to die!"

The Singers surrounding him scattered.

Luke straightened and got into a ready position with a sigh. "Hello, Zelaui. Long time no see. No hard feelings, right?"

"Die!" the Singer shouted. He'd fought her in the goblin dungeon where they found Smudge. Killed her, even, but it didn't stick. Several times, if he remembered correctly. By the look of things, there were some hard feelings. Quite a lot of them, in fact.
