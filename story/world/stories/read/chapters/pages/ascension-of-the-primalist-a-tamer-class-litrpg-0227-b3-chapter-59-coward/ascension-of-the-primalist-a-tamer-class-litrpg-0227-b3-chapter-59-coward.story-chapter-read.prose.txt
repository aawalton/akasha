Then, the moment the deadly flurry of piercing stones was about to skewer his chest, he vanished.

Seth reappeared beside the beast's flank, and his fist slammed into its ribs. Jagged scales exploded like brittle glass under his absurdly high Strength and the penetrating power of Armored Phantom Fists.

Even if the monstrous beast boasted an incredible Toughness, it didn't matter: the spell drove deep into its iron fur and shattered ribs beneath.

Seth unleashed a relentless barrage of blows, each punch crashing into the bear's side with enough force to buckle its large body and crack bone. The punches reverberated across the battlefield like thunder and made the beast roar in pain.

Blood spurted from its maw as its head was whipped to the side. It lashed out on instinct, snapping its fangs toward Seth. Yet the jaws closed on empty air—Seth had already slipped beneath the attack.

Seizing the opportunity, he exploded upward, and his fist crashed into  the beast's jaw.

The uppercut snapped its head, causing teeth and blood to spray out. The beast staggered, but Seth refused to give it even a second to recover or use a  spell. He surged forth, driving another savage punch into its ribs, then another into its throat.

Behind him, Jaz let out a roar filled with murderous rage. The Guardian planted his feet and raised his  warhammer toward the black sky.

And the sky answered.

A colossal pillar of white-blue lightning descended from the clouds.

It struck Seth's back with a deafening clap.

His leather mantle burst into flames though the downpour soon extinguished the blaze. Agonizing lightning arcs ripped through every muscle in his body, locking his limbs in agonizing spasms.

Seth gritted his teeth but refused to fall. Instead, he threw another punch. Then another.

Ignoring the pain, he hammered the Iron-mantle Bear with everything he had. The beast desperately cast several spells, wrapping itself in fresh layers of stone. Some imperial soldiers tried to leave the distortion's field to use Instant spell-scrolls to heal themselves while others lunged at Seth with swords and spears alongside Jaz.

None of it mattered.

Every layer of rock that formed was pulverized a second later.

And every healed wound was worsened by more blows.

Seth danced around the beast with incredible speed, never remaining in place long enough for the surrounding soldiers to pin him down.

Jaz roared and swung his warhammer to cave in Seth's skull.

Like all the others, he was too slow.

His weapon struck empty air.

The giant Guardian's confidence began to crack. Panic flashed across his face, and he cast a spell to wrap his beast with a  hazel barrier. It only delayed the inevitable.

Armored Phantom Fists was made to break barriers.

Seth's next punch tore through the layer of aether, smashing it into  fragments before continuing on its path. His gauntlet buried itself into the bear's skull, and bone burst inward.

The beast's eyes turned vacant before its large body even fell, and neither Jaz nor the surrounding imperials could do a thing to stop it. The creature then crashed into the mud, shaking the battlefield.

Jaz let out a scream of pure fury. A normal reaction for someone who just lost a contracted beast. Unlike Celess' own reaction.

Seth slipped beneath the Guardian's next hammer swing just as the teal aura of Armored Phantom Fists flickered and dissolved from his gauntlets. The spell had ended, so Jaz seized the opportunity to press on.

He slammed his hammer into the ground, causing a violent ring of lightning to erupt outward. The thunderous wave tore across the battlefield and forced Seth to dive aside as bolts of electricity ripped through the rain and scorched the  ground where he had been standing moments before.

Once he'd  rolled back to his feet, his eyes landed on Celess in the distance.

The Empress' daughter had retreated beneath the protective radius of another Stabilizer Obelisk near the edge of the imperial camp.

Seth glanced back at Jaz, who was already charging back in.

The giant Guardian had survived multiple attacks empowered by Armored Phantom Fists. Without that spell—or Dusk Scythes—bringing him down would take far too long.

Seth's gaze moved back to Celess as he knocked aside a wind-clad spear thrust from a nearby soldier.

Unlike Jaz, the noblewoman looked terrified. It was as if she had never faced any danger before in her life.

Seth changed direction and charged straight toward her. I don't need either of the spells to kill her.

Seth shifted his weight, which let the summon of flames hiss harmlessly past his shoulder.

"Stop him!" she screamed at the guards who'd begun forming a desperate ring around her.

Seth wove between the attacks of the soldiers converging on him.

Seth gritted his teeth. Pathetic.

One of the nearby imperial soldiers lunged forward, swinging a broadsword in a wild arc.

Seth parried the blade with his armored forearm, stepped inside the man's guard, and drove a crushing punch into his sternum.

The bone shattered under the blow, and the Warrior crumpled lifelessly into the mud.

Seth turned and let his eyes settle on Jaz, who had for once stopped charging and was barking orders to reorganize the men around him.

'Seth, we need some help here,' Nightmare's tense voice echoed through Link.

Seth's attention snapped toward the direwolf.

He raised an armored gauntlet and knocked aside a heavy broadsword, though his shoulder strained from the impact.

The direwolf and Colossus carved relentlessly through the imperial soldiers, both of them under the empowering effect of Beastmaster Rage, yet it wasn't enough.

Devus and Elena desperately held the collapsing defensive line alongside the battered remnants of their squad. Marine, Selena, and Henry had abandoned their concealed artillery positions and joined the others to fight shoulder-to-shoulder.

Seth's gaze found Jaz.

The giant Guardian stood among the imperial ranks as fury twisted his rugged features. His massive hands tightened around the haft of his lightning-wreathed warhammer.

Without Dusk Scythes or Armored Phantom Fists, breaking through the man's defense would take far too long. By the time he brought Jaz down, Kastal's line would already have collapsed, and his own squad would likely be dead.

Jaz wasn't following.
