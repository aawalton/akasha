I’m sure as shit not playing this thing’s games.

Don looked around. The shack had no windows and only one door. That meant he had two options. Either go through the door and get willingly ambushed by whatever was prowling outside.

Or…

Make a door.

He flicked open his claws and struck them into the floorboards. The wood cracked under the force of his blows. He yanked and pulled the floorboard off its nails. Then he did it again. The hut was suspended just a few feet in the air. Enough for Don to crouch under it.

After he had removed three floorboards he squeezed out of the hut and crouched.

Lets see whose Stealth is higher…

Don crept with careful steps, crawling on all fours under the hut. The tall grass tickled him, and he saw a pretty creepy green spider scuttle away from him.

He crawled to the edge of the hut and looked closely at the vegetation on the cavern wall. He could see the unnatural bulge more clearly now. There was something off with how everything looked. There was definitely some kind of beast hidden under the vines.

Don was maybe eight feet away from where the enemy was hiding. It didn’t move. Don had to assume it was watching the door and actually hadn’t noticed him. Even if it had, the advantage of ambush was wholly ruined. Either the beast would escape or let Don attack first.

Don had the best odds he could possibly get.

Recruiting all of his Power and Agility, he bet the house on a singular strike. He sprung from under the hut and zipped at the hidden enemy. Something twisted, but it was too late. Don’s claws bit into flesh and something green spurted.

The creature turned slightly visible. It was like slicing into thick jelly. The creature was low and lean. Some kind of lizard, but wrong in every way. Its skin was slick, and translucent with faint colors, like oil on water. Four limbs splayed out spider-like, each ending in a tri-fingered thick claw meant for rending. Its snout was long and full of sharp teeth. Eyes bulged black and glassy—no pupils. Just emotionless void.

It twisted, screeched, and spat something at Don.

Green acid sprayed all over his face and chest, and immediately started sizzling and burning.

[Health at 80%]

[Energy at 78%]

[Debuffs: Chemical Burn, Mild Poisoning]

Don was half blind, but he rushed to pin the creature against the cavern wall. It struggled and shrieked, and tried to find an angle to spit at Don again. Don pushed his claws into its neck, forcing its head sideways.

It was relatively weak and Don managed to overpower it. He kept pressing the translucent beast against the wall and twisting his claws in its body. Eventually it went limp and Don let it slide off.

[You have slain [Bilespit Ambusher – lvl 10] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 35 System Points earned.]

Camouflage and acid spit? What a bunch of bastards.

At least Don had something to look forward to from consuming these guys’ Evolution Trait. He wasn’t sure which he would prefer. An acid spit would give him a projectile weapon, which would open a whole new ways of fighting. However, the camouflage ability was immensely powerful and would augment further Don’s assassination style.

One thing was for sure. Don would definitely increase his Mental Attribute on the next level up. The potion had been a lifesaver. Without it, Don wouldn’t have had acute enough senses to pick up the clues of the ambush. It also made his mind overall… faster. Don hadn’t noticed it so clearly as his Mental boosts had been +1 so far. Sure he had noticed subtle changes, like having better reflexes, but that could be attributed to Simian Physique or just leveling up.

But no, Mental played a bigger role than he thought. It was actually the Attribute responsible for answering the Stealth skill for one. And most likely many other effects such as paralysis or illusions, if those ever came up.

Overall it just seemed to increase Don’s processing speed. As his body got faster and faster, his mind would have to be able to keep up. And besides. He needed to be able to see these lizard fuckers before they could get the jump on him.

He checked his interface, and saw that the potion still had 57 minutes of effective time left. Don would have to make the most of it and traverse the dungeon fast. Maybe if he killed enough lizards, he could get a level and increase his Mental. He would also get to level 10, which would likely give him something extra.

Don looked at the now visible lizard creature on the ground. Whereas the bears and Vulpins had definitely been genetically altered abominations, this was a bona fide monster. The skin was still translucent and the white teeth and black eyes were the only clearly visible part of the monster. It was skinny, with an almost snake-like torso.

The acid burned. There was definitely some kind of poison in it. It wasn’t overpowering as Don had only gotten a single spray of it. But he could still feel it weakening him. His other eye burned and he couldn’t open it. He checked his health. He saw it tick down from 78% to 77%.

Don used Devour on the lizard.

[40 Evolution Points acquired!]

[Evolution trait Bilespit Ambusher: Consumed]

[Health at 100%]

[Energy at 100%]

[Debuffs: N/A]

“Alright,” he muttered. “Time to move.”

Don found it was very uncomfortable to move around not knowing when he might be ambushed by a goddamn invisible lizard. He chose to go along the cavern wall. While the enemy had clearly been comfortable using it as a point of attack, it also gave Don a clear point of attack to watch out for. If he went into the tall grass in the middle of the cavern, he could be ambushed from almost any direction.

He consciously attempted to use his heightened Mental, watching for clues of abnormalities in the vegetation. He moved relatively slowly, but being careful was the key here. He didn’t know how devastating the ambush from one of these lizards might be and he sure as hell wasn’t going to find out.

Soon he found another anomaly. It was ten yards forward along the cavern wall. A strange bulge of vines. Under it some faint movement and distorted lines that was hard for the mind to understand.

Don was sure the creature could see him and was waiting for ambush. Considering the creature’s size and physique, as well as the positioning the previous one had by the shack’s door, Don figured they would attack at around five or six yards.

Good. That meant Don had the initiative.

He needed a plan here. He wasn’t sure if the lizard would attack with its acid spit or lunge at him. Either way it would be a direct frontal attack with high speed. Don considered his options. He could dodge under the attack. This was risky, but would also give him more time to counter attack from an unorthodox position. Another, more vanilla choice would be to sidestep. If the creature ambushed with its claws, that was great. He would steal the momentum of the fight. But if the creature used acid attack, Don might be put in a disadvantage where he was forced to dodge multiple acid attacks with no space to turn to tides. A third option would be to use the cavern wall as a point of attack, but it was the lizard’s element. Don wanted the fight on the ground, where he could use his superior strength.

Fuck it. I’ll duck.

So Don charged forward, flicking out his claws with a sharp shing. Don took a few paces, and as he predicted, the lizard attacked.

First Don only saw two black eyes open in between the vines and moss. Then the shimmering, translucent features of the camouflaged lizard started to draw an uncanny outline in the vegetation. This all happened so fast, only Don’s enhanced Mental stat managed to process it. Within fractions of a second, the monster lunged towards Don, talons out, spitting a glob of acid at him. Some of it struck his neck and shoulder. He winced in pain as his skin sizzled at the impact. Don ducked forward and struck upwards. His claws bit into the monster. The fight was on.
