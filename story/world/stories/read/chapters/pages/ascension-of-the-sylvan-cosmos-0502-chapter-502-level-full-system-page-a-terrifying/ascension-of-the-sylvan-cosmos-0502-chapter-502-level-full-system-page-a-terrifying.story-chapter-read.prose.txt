During the last year, Liu Shu’s transformation was ridiculous, to say the least. Previously, Liu Shu was close to being the tallest World Tree in the world, with almost 3000 meters in height. However, as the two years of her transformation occurred and changes moved at an accelerated pace, people started to notice just how tall Liu Shu was growing.

When a month had passed, sharp-eyed people noticed the difference. The difference must’ve been tremendous when people could see the changes in height of a being nearing 3,000 meters in height. After all, how could someone feel a difference when something measuring 2800 meters changed to 2900 meters?

However, they weren’t alone.

Golden Sap City was built quite far away from Liu Shu’s main body. When she first started building, Liu Shu made sure to separate the circular city into an outer and inner ring at a fixed distance from each other. This would leave most of the area for people to live in while keeping a place for Liu Shu to fight, create farms, and engage in various personal endeavors.

The dividing line between the inner and outer edges had always been <Mother Of Thousand Flower Field>, which was right at the middle distance between Liu Shu and <Lignified Living Wall>.

During the few decades people lived in Golden Sap City, they understood that the gorgeous sea of flowers spanning tens of thousands of meters was part of Liu Shu’s protective strategy. Citizens saw how the flowers rose and rained hell on their enemies, and they also saw the same flowers blast people who thought they were too clever and approached them without permission.

The power of <Mother Of Thousand Flower Field> was the best deterrent against people trespassing into the city’s inner domain. As a matter of fact, the closest they could come to Liu Shu without permission was right at the dividing line inside the <World Tree’s Endless Library>.

Nevertheless, Liu Shu didn’t just create the divide to have a personal land. The true purpose was to have personal space. After all, while living near a tree 200 meters tall was bearable, even if you were close, its giant canopy would still swallow your house almost 24/7.

Now, what if that tree grew to Liu Shu’s height? Forget the Sun, you’d live permanently in the shadow of the gargantuan World Tree. The only sunlight would fall during Dawn and Dusk!

The World Tree’s city had never stopped growing in size. The walls constantly moved outward. The places that people previously called the edge were now almost in the middle of the habitable space.

During the last two years, Liu Shu’s outer edge had reached an absurd distance of 17,400 meters! That meant that the city, from one side to the other, was now nearly 35,000 meters, or 35 kilometers (22~ miles).

One might think that the place was just too large. Nevertheless, the size of this massive city paled in comparison with the actual gargantuan being that resided within it. If the city were not this large, everyone would feel cramped because of the permanent shadow of their World Tree.

After the growth the World Tree experienced during her Level Ups and changes, her size had reached an absurd 5,670 meters in height. Moreover, this height was calculated from the base of the tree to the top of its canopy. Meaning, the underground was not counted at all.

This gargantuan being, which towered so high that clouds started to gather around its canopy, was the beacon of power and zeal for all who lived in the Wild Continent. Her body doubles could also be spotted in different parts of the continent, and while they lacked the true majesty of the World Tree’s main body, their power was truly fearsome.

Flor and Ignatia sat on the branches of one of the Lignified Town Halls, looking up in silence at the colossal monster towering above them. This being, whom they called their daughter, was a creature far beyond them. They could tell that Liu Shu had finally left her infancy phase and had become a Young World Tree.

Flor muttered. “I feel that she can destroy a mountain with just a single flick of her branches…”

Ignatia chuckled while hugging the petite fairy within her embrace. Flor’s back was pressed against Ignatia’s softness, relaxing her entire being. Ignatia spoke with a chuckle. “Regardless, remember that she is our big baby. So, don’t feel stressed. I know it is difficult to look at our Liu Shu and feel anything but awe. Nevertheless, Liu Shu’s soul, her core being, won’t change with her size.” She spoke firmly. “I believe that Liu Shu will change if we stop treating her… as if she were another being other than Liu Shu.”

Flor smiled. “I understand this, Ignatia. It’s just that I fear that people around the world will become too aggressive once they receive news of her. Now, it is harder than ever to control all the information. I know for a fact that people had already sent news outside. Although thankfully, we were able to delay it enough for Liu Shu to finish her Leveling Up process.”

Ignatia laughed. “Although I am considered powerful, I think that Liu Shu is too much of an outlier. Honestly, even if I gather my entire colony and try to take her down, I doubt I’ll be successful.”

“Really?” Flor blinked. “Hasn’t that big baby, Solaria, become a <Fourth Energy Cycle> <Ant Princess>? She has another three siblings that have also reached the <Fourth Energy Cycle>, right?”

“It doesn’t matter. Against a World Tree, numbers matter so little that it is laughable. Moreover, I am ignoring the surrounding city, <Mother Of Thousand Flower Field>, and the Guardian that is silently lurking at Liu Shu’s base.”

“Right!” Flor blinked. “Liu Shu built a guardian at the base of her tree a long time ago. Only, it had never seen action other than that time we restrained Delia.”

“It was superfluous.” Ignatia laughed.

Remembering their girl’s attitude toward the System, Flor’s lips arched. “It is just a hunch, but I feel that Liu Shu will be super annoyed at the barrage of System Messages she is about to receive.”

Ignatia’s antennae twitched as her lips arched with laughter. “Honestly… I think she will as well. We’ll have to prepare to hear her whines later.”

“It doesn’t matter.” Flor smiled softly. “She is my big baby. As her mother, listening to her whining is my privilege, even if it is tiring or annoying.”
