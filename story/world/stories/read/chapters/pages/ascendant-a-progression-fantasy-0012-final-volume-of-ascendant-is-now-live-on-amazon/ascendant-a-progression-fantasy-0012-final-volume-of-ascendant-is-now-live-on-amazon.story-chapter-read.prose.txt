I am incredibly lucky to have found so much support from this community that I was able to make enough money to keep the lights on all the way through 2023.

I am still writing of course, and you can find all my newest work here on my RR profile and my older work by searching for either my online handle EmergencyComplaints or my real name, D.E. Sherman, on Amazon or Audible.

Once again, thank you. I've wanted to be an author for over twenty years, but it wasn't until about a two years ago that I actually thought I could.
