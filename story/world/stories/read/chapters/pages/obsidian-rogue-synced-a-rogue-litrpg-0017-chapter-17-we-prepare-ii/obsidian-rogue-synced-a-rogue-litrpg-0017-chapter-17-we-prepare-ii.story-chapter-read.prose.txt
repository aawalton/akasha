Of course, that wasn’t the only thing that made him look around; his eyes were constantly scanning for threats… scanning for Ghost. While it was true that he hadn't seen the rogue since he’d escaped from Gantus, he knew that Ghost wouldn’t let him off the hook easily. There was no doubt in his mind that the rogue would be searching for him at the moment.

If he were strong or smart enough, then he could get into the E-rank within three to four months. It was a big if, but it was his best shot at still breathing. But he had to ask, would becoming an E-rank really be worth it? Ghost had managed to kill the party that had been transporting the obsidian stone all by himself. According to Ghost, the party had at least two members at the C–rank, and yet they’d not been enough to stop the rogue. So what rank did that make Ghost?

B–rank? A– Rank? S-Rank? What kind of monster could clear out a party like that, alone? Levi's brain was heating up at the type of problem he’d landed in.

Grak me.

Ghost possibly couldn’t be anything below the B-Rank, which meant that Levi was utterly Grakked any which way he looked at it. Of course, the rogue had ruled out any possibility of Ghost being an S-ranked as there were only a few of those alive in Rinesta. Reaching the peak of Ranks was no easy feat. In fact, many synced died before reaching the B–rank, and there was still the A-rank after that, followed by the S-rank. Any synced that achieved the remarkable feat automatically became untouchable. S-Ranks were afforded a lot of leeway when it came to breaking laws for their own gain. It should've been seen as unfair, but then again, what in this world wasn’t? The nobles stepped on the poor. The heirs of the nobles received resources to advance their ranks quickly, and those of the higher ranks got to do whatever they wanted to. The truth of their lives was apparent to all those who bothered to look. Rinesta belonged only to those who had power, those who had clout, those who were born into influential families. The rest of them were left to die in the dirt.

What an unfair world.

Levi glanced at the mage out of the corner of his eyes. A bit of jealousy seeped into his emotions the longer he stared at Xara. People like her had never had to suffer for anything. They’d never had to wake up hungry and still go to bed hungry. They’d never had to beg for scraps of food… They'd never had to steal to survive. No, that wasn’t right. The nobles did steal. They stole from the poor, depriving them of resources that could level the playing field. They created separate areas for themselves so that they didn’t have to see those who suffered because of their greed. Everything good was hoarded by those who were strong enough to keep it, and the weak were left to suffer. That was how it’d always been, but every once in a while someone would emerge from the dirt, the hunger of a thousand deprived souls trapped in their belly. That person would rise through the ranks and become a symbol of hope to those below.

Can I be that person? Can I shake up the upper echelon of Rinesta?

Levi scoffed lightly at the thought; his mind had led him to fantasy land, somehow forgetting that he was currently fighting for life. His current issues demanded his complete focus; he couldn’t afford to be daydreaming about how good it’d feel to change the status quo. It’d do more for his life span if he spent that time considering how exactly to stop Ghost from killing him. If he could do that, then he could consider other things. Besides, if he could survive Ghost, then he’d be stronger for it. It’s like they always said, what doesn’t kill you will make you stronger. The first step in changing things was to survive being hunted by Ghost. But for the most part, he’d think about more pressing issues… Like the system bank.
