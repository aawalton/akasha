A seam split down Malzareth's soul, a seam shining with silver as it marshaled the entirety of its capacities.

Then it brought it all down on Zane at once.

Spatial Laws—Time Laws, Fate Laws, its Bloodline—working with its Supreme—

Its finest Creation started shimmering into existence.

Zane felt it as it did. He instantly knew he was late—he knew he had to work fast.

He stepped, and space contracted before him.

In the same moment Malzareth's eyes flared crimson.

Reality hardened around Zane, the same skill that halted the Sage—

He broke free through virtue of sheer physicality, shattering that hardened reality.

Hardened essence-walls closed in on him from all sides. They could barely stall him at all, just for a fraction of a second. They didn't land; he side-stepped instantly.

And then three fleets of spears warped into him.

Three fleets all at once, in one of the single biggest reality-warps Malzareth had done all fight. It flooded the space in which Zane stood.

An excessive showing, but it spared no expense.

It was as though all it needed to do was to hold him there for that extra sliver of a second...

And then its attack expanded around him.

A universe. A true universe, in miniature—a temporary universe, but it represented the apex creation of any of those who dabbled in the Creative arts.

A Skill that represented the apex of all Malzareth's abilities.

Zane let out a silent cry and attacked—and then he vanished.

Wrapped within that depthless dark sphere... vanished.

His aura was gone.

He'd been totally sealed off in a space that would seem to him hundreds of clicks in size. In a space that'd seem as true as the Universe itself.

It was a world that could not be exited.

And it was impossible to create a more hostile environment to a man; Malzareth knew this, and it was very deliberate about this. The world itself was built by Corruption, built to nullify man's essence.

It was also the first and only attack, all fight, that managed to make Zane's efforts seem small.

You could still see Zane's struggles. They showed up as tiny ripples over the surface of that dark sphere, which seemed only a mile in radius from the outside.

Exposure to this world would be enough to kill him within an hour, even accounting for his Vitality; Malzareth had calculated this.

But it was not banking on that.

In that moment, dozens of Destruction-spears burst into being around that sphere.

Every single one of them was of the same caliber as the spear that'd almost killed the Barbarian Sage.

With a vicious cry, Malzareth rammed them in.

And then it summoned another dozen.

And it rammed them in again. And then another, and then another—again, and again, and again—

It cried out as it did so, like it was releasing some deeply held emotion in its soul. Like it was imagining hammering Zane's prone body as it did.

It drew so deeply, so recklessly from its essence pool that the scars on its soul opened up, one by one, pouring out soul-matter in the Astral Plane; it ignored it.

It just kept hitting him.

Ten seconds. Ten seconds of that relentless attack, and the surface of the sphere began to still.

Twenty seconds, and it'd gone utterly still.

In that time, Malzareth had unloaded about half its essence pool. It was essence it'd saved up all fight—saved up for this very moment, essence that it spared from not having to maintain its Summons.

It would've been an incredibly impressive feat for a True God to hold out for ten seconds under that assault. Even a True God of a Universal caliber.

It'd expected that much from Zane.

After twenty seconds, only a handful of men and women in the history of the Universe—a Universe that was orders of magnitude the size of Dragonspire.

After thirty seconds Zane was dead.

There was no other interpretation of the facts.

At some level Malzareth knew it was beating a corpse—albeit a robust one.

But it kept throwing spear, after spear, after spear—it just couldn't stop itself. It screamed as it did, like it had to; like it had to purge whatever feeling was in its soul, like it couldn't stop until that was gone.

In another circumstance it might've done this as a way to hurt mankind or to torture the Mistress.

That was not in its mind in that moment.

It simply wanted release for itself.

And then—after what felt to it like hours—silence.

Even its vast essence reserves, at that point, were exhausted. Its very soul felt wrecked. It could hardly summon an emotion anymore.

In that time, it hit Zane with enough raw damage to kill that human fifty times over.

It closed its eyes there, in silence. It stayed there for a time.

It sensed Zane's allies dropping in from several floors up and simply sealed off the floors with a flicker of will; it said nothing.

That periphery had lost all relevance.

It opened its eyes.

It could sense how many eyes were on it in that moment. Its powers of perception were sensitive enough.

It let itself smile.

Did you think he was your answer?

It almost giggled, surprising itself. I do not blame you.It regarded the Universe again. And here,it said.I was starting to think—

It stilled.

It just watched the surface of that universe.

Silence.

The slightest of ripples.

It deposited two dozen spears straight into the surface of the Universe—almost the remainder of its essence—and was rewarded with stillness once again.

Another silence—longer this time.

It almost didn't dare take its eyes off that Universe, its expression frozen, incredulous.

A seam split its surface.

A single seam, and through that seam came a single brilliant slash.

A slash that caught Malzareth right in the chest.

It'd lost all its power by the time it landed, but it still made the dragon cry out.

It slammed in another fleet of spears...

Yet another silence.

And then another, five seconds later—it was drawing nearly empty at this point—only to see a second seam light up the outside of that Universe.

Spear, after spear, after spear—at first in fleets, and then fours—twos, as it struggled to regenerate enough—

It attacked with the eyes of the Galaxy on the scene.

Reina saw it, just outside the tower.

Haxorax saw it. The rest of the top-rankers all saw it.

The Scryer's Guild captured it all, but you didn't need a military-grade scrying glass. In its current state, the Throne Room was open for all those with access to advanced scrying to see.

Which included those in the ranks of the Steelheart Conclave and those in the World Tree—they saw Malzareth try, with every last shred of essence it had, to break the will of Zane.

It included Evan and Avery, crying out as they saw a new seam, the biggest one yet, carve through the face of that Universe.

It included the top-rankers of Earth who'd all seen his rise—watching as yet another new seam showed through, and it was clear that Universe couldn't hold—

Watching as a man staggered out and collapsed to a knee...his big, once-sturdy frame was barely intact at all.

This was a man clearly in Critical Health, but whose eyes were still that shining silver, and whose heart was still intact and pumping—even if the rest of him had been utterly brutalized.

But that Supreme filled him back up by the second.

Swelling his muscles, solidifying his bones—

In a military ward, in Dragonspire's outpost K, a Sage sprung to his feet, fist pumping, lifted his eyes to the Heavens, and screamed with all his heart a certain phrase.

In that moment Malzareth made a decision.

Zane put a fist to the ground, chest heaving. He made himself get up by sheer force of will.

His hands closed around his chains.

And then he realized what it'd done.

There—just above the remnants of its throne—stood a portal in space.

And Malzareth was gone.

It fled through the spatial tunnel at top speed.

It had to get away.

He can't be killed.

It thought this again and again, feeling like its entire understanding of reality had been punctured in that moment—

He can't be killed!

It wanted to laugh, and cry, at once—

And then it was clear of the Superdungeon.

It knew what it'd said—that if the attack failed, it'd be at peace. It had been serious at the time it'd said the words; it had not planned on making use of that tunnel.

It'd changed its mind.

It burst into open space, claws filling with the little essence it'd regenerated in those brief seconds—still easily enough to slash open the void—

A flight of silver arrows carved through the air before it, and it was forced to halt, snarling—

"Going somewhere?" said an icy voice. A woman's voice.

It was Reina, the Mistress.

At her side, Noughtfire, looking unamused.

And behind them, spread out in a formation resembling a net—eight of Dragonspire's twenty Top-20 fighters. There, impossibly.

Malzareth screamed.

Their exchanges lasted twelve full seconds. Twelve seconds in which it was clear that their only goal was to hold it off.

Almost as though they were waiting for something.

Someone.

And then... a bolt out of the blue.

A line of light that split the air.

A single spear carved through Malzareth's heart.

A spear thrown at speeds so fast no one there could react in time—thrown with so much force, so much intent not even Malzareth itself had a chance of reacting in time.

And then it was reeled straight back.

That great dragon was ripped back by a force only one man in that Galaxy could generate. Thrown through its spatial portal in a fraction of a second.

It smashed through its own throne.

It'd barely struggled upright when the chains wrapped its neck. Welded its limbs to its body.

It looked up and saw a certain man.

A man who'd just taken everything it'd had to offer. A man who was very much still standing.

"Did I say we were done?" said Zane.
