At its face, the job was a simple one.  Scientists from the Pillar were working on nurturing a natural treasure for one of their up and comers, and required data from a handful of untamed planets within Pillar space to do so.  Unfortunately, the planetary environments were too dangerous for D Grades to be sent in.  C Grade phytans could likely handle it, but because their bodies were effectively small domains, there was a risk of corrupting the data between placing the measurement devices and retrieving them.

That was where Kyle came in.  His job was to pick up the probes from a lab in a Pillar-run space station, set them up one at a time, and wait until their data collection was complete.  Then he’d pick up the probe, return it, and start the process all over again.  It sounded like it would be terribly boring, but at a price of over a million credits per probe returned, Kyle wasn’t going to complain.  And, knowing Jarberry, there were layers to this job.

So, after stocking up on rations and new clothes, Kyle was off.  Even though these planets weren’t currently colonized, they were still within Collective space, and covered within his nav bracelet’s range.  That was more comforting to him than he expected as he traded messages with his friends during the long shuttle ride to the lab.  Arhades mentioned an upcoming return to the Alabaster Court heartlands, while Tillienne signed up for another planetary suppression job to help cull some upper D Grade beasts on an Archdrake Supremacy world.  Jarberry and Suierrillax were both wholeheartedly focused on preparing for the next hearing at the Tribunal, and though he was never mentioned, Kyle suspected that Marcus was involved in supporting them as well.

The messages that surprised him the most, however, came from Skippy and Duroc.  The content of Skippy’s message was largely unexpected; that the skrell had reached C Grade and wanted to spar.  However, tucked away in the latter part of the message, he also mentioned that Benjamin Corthus wanted to meet.  While there was no way that would happen with the Tribunal looming over his head, it was a meeting Kyle intended to take.  He didn’t attribute any malice to Corthian Mining for making the decision they did, particularly in light of his presumed death.  That said, if Kyle could use it to get a better understanding of what was going on in the Gray Conglomerate, he would use it.

The message from Duroc was a surprise.  The orc hadn’t kept in touch very much since his departure from Corthian Mining, and from what little Kyle had heard, he’d made his way back to his homeworld to deal with some issues that had cropped up.  With time to spare as he waited on the shuttle, Kyle opened the message again.

Kyle,

Glad to hear that you’re still alive.  Skippy told me you’d died, and I was glad to hear that he was wrong.  He and Benjamin just visited, it was nice catching up with them, and news of your survival brightened the occasion.  In any event, I’m writing to let you know that a special job may be coming through the Collective’s guild system soon, asking for you.  I didn’t request it, but some members of my clan decided to move forward after hearing about you.

You’re under no obligation to accept of course, but the context is important.  Our clan’s warchief is in failing health, and I will likely be taking over the role.  Historically, that comes with the new warchief sending Couriers to the others to issue challenges, but it’s more symbolic than anything else.  Plus, with the added dangers on the roads between villages, it’s probably more of a pain than it’s worth for the credits.  Lots more dangerous beasts appearing lately, and some are pretty dangerous to boot.  Wouldn’t want you getting eaten by a hodag, after all!

Hope to connect with you when things have settled down.  Take care of yourself.

-Duroc

Kyle didn’t know a lot about hodags outside of his extermination mission with Arhades, but to hear that another planet was having issues with the beasts raised his hackles.  He’d been concerned that something was spurring their growth during their initial hunt, and this made him all the more convinced that this wasn’t a coincidence.  To say he was curious was an understatement, though the timing was flatly bad.  After this job concluded, there would be the matter of his Tribunal to continue, followed shortly by the auction of Kertan Six.  It simply didn’t leave him a lot of time to chase mysteries.  Even if he didn’t end up having time to personally investigate, however, he still planned on sharing that nugget of information with somebody who might.  Namely, Arhades.

Sending the velgian a quick note, Kyle turned his attention back to the approaching space station, where he’d pick up the first of the probes.  The influence the Pillar had on the Hub’s creation was clear to see, with the floating arms of the space station making it look like a strand of ribbon drifting through the void.  If it was expanded to massive size and wrapped around thousands of stars, it would be a very similar sight.

[ARE YOU READY, DR. MAYHEW?] C.H.A.D.D. asked as they pulled up into the docking hangar.

“I am,” Kyle replied, walking toward the door of the viewing room to depart the shuttle.  “It’ll be nice to have something simple to focus our attention on for a little while.  And hopefully give us a chance to do some experimentation while we wait.”

As the door to the lab opened, Kyle expected that he would be invited in to the space station to pick up the probe and his orders.  Instead, a small drone entered the shuttle as soon as the door opened, probe in tow.  The moment Kyle reached out to grab it, a quick message played, with coordinates to the first location uploaded to the shuttle and Kyle’s nav bracelet.

Courier,

Planet GAM21226-Alpha is your target.  You will be dropped in the planet’s upper atmosphere due to the perpetual storms.  When the probe has finished collecting data, it will notify us, and a retrieval pod will be sent down for it and for you.

More instructions to follow.

-Roole

Kyle read the message over a couple of times, then shrugged.  He’d been through nasty storms before, both during his Practicum and for his first delivery on Pokke.  Does Jarberry really think I’m just good for sitting in bad weather? Kyle mused, pulling up the information packet on GAM21226-Alpha.  While details weren’t too specific, it looked like there were two major regions across the planet; one that contained major volcanic activity, while the other was a frigid waste.  The probe was to be placed at a location in between, where a constant storm raged due to the opposing forces.

From the initial job description and the note provided with the probe, Kyle knew that it would be a decent wait before they would be able to leave.  Though the timeline was up in the air, there was an opportunity for them to continue their projects.

“Let’s get a list together before we land,” Kyle decided.  “We don’t know how long it will take for the probe to collect its data, but I’d rather have projects we can rotate through.”

[AGREED, DR. MAYHEW.  I BELIEVE THAT FOCUSING ON THE GRAFT SKILL SHOULD BE NEAR THE TOP OF THE LIST, IF I MAY SHARE MY PIECE.]

“That’s near mine as well,” Kyle agreed, pulling up a screen.  After placing Graft near the top, he added a few other items as well.  He and C.H.A.D.D. went the rounds as the shuttle approached GAM21226-Alpha, but they finally landed on a set that they agreed upon.

While they both agreed on the importance of experimenting with the Gradft skill, Kyle and C.H.A.D.D. disagreed on most of the rest of the list.  The drone’s interests were primarily focused on the World Tree Tender class, and making the most of the myriad seeds and options at their disposal.  Kyle, by contrast, wanted to focus more on developing his Unbroken Survivor class.  He would need to reach Level 60 as an Unbroken Survivor before he’d be able to upgrade the World Tree Tender to D Grade in any event, and the boost in terms of attributes was worlds apart, particularly as it was tied to the Union Symbiote class as well.

[I AM STILL SOMEWHAT CONCERNED THAT AN INCREASE IN ATTRIBUTES WILL LEAD TO LOWER LEVELS OF CONTROL.  QUALITATIVELY, THE ENERGY YOU ARE USING WITH MANY OF THESE PLANTS IS ALREADY LEAGUES MORE POWERFUL.]

It wasn’t the first time the drone made the point, and it likely wouldn’t be the last.  “I appreciate it, C.H.A.D.D., but we’re already at a point of diminishing returns on just reinforcing fundamentals.  Graft is new enough that it needs the attention, I agree, but so much of the other work is still limited by a power threshold.  Besides, even if it’s not entirely optimal, we also need to be prepared for whoever is behind the Tribunal to take a more direct approach if it fails.  Not to mention that the Sanction on Zierlan is on a timer now.”

[A CENTURIES-LONG TIMER,] C.H.A.D.D. countered.

“A timer nevertheless,” Kyle said firmly.  “I promise not to forget about the other projects, and I’ll even portion off a healthy chunk of mana for you to work with on your end.”

[THOSE TERMS ARE ACCEPTABLE,] C.H.A.D.D. replied with a feeling of resignation.

A ping rang out across the shuttle, and Kyle looked out at the planet below.  GAM, as Kyle decided to call it, had one side facing a nearby star, and the other shrouded in perpetual shadow.

We are nearing position.  Prepare to evacuate the shuttle.  As previously mentioned, a pod will be sent down, which will need you to infuse mana before it is teleported back to the shuttle.  Good fortune, Courier.

The bay door opened as powerful gusts of wind buffeted the interior of the shuttle.  Kyle took a deep breath, then tucked the probe in under his right arm, binding it with some quickly-grown vines.  With a smile on his face, he leapt out of the shuttle, plummeting toward the planet below.  Diving through a thick cloud of steam, Synaptic Barbs and a sense of warning from C.H.A.D.D. were all the warning he got as a massive chunk of ice hurtled toward his position.  Redirecting it with Repel as C.H.A.D.D. helped move them out of the way, a gout of flame leapt up from the ground below, narrowly missing him.

With a smile spreading across his face, Kyle sharpened his focus.  This looks like it’ll be fun.
