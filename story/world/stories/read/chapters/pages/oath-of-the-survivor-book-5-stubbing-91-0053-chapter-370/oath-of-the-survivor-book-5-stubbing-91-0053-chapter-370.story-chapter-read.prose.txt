If he really beat a mid-tier C Grade after just evolving himself, my bet is that he used that skill to do it.”

More than anything else, I’m not confident that he has the tools he’d need to pin me down. If he did, I’d like my odds a lot less.”“And what do you think about my odds against him?” Duroc pressed.

From what my skill shows me, you’re more well-rounded than Mangalica, which means that he’s got you beat in terms of Strength, Vitality, and Willpower. You’re a fairly even match in Endurance, and you’re ahead in Intelligence and Perception.”

Potentially, the orc was even following the Velgian’s path of using a variant Warrior class as a second class, integrating Mage skills to create better synergy. Given that early C Grades didn’t gain much relatively in terms of attributes from their second classes, it was the best guess Kyle had, and he expressed as much.

Days blurred by as Kyle, C.H.A.D.D., and Duroc each settled into their projects. The drone was mostly focused on making continual enhancements to its garden, with an emphasis on replacing the more potent resources Kyle had burned through during his series of deliveries. Between Aspect of the Tree: Branch and their improved mastery of the Graft skill, they were able to produce better quality plants than the ones they’d expended, and do so with a fraction of the effort.

For Kyle, that meant he was able to dedicate more focus to watching Duroc work. Compared to Marcus’ mechanical and technology focus, the orc’s work was downright primitive. And it was mesmerizing. Duroc’s process of creation involved taking raw materials; crystals, metals, stones, and then forcing them into shape through compression. The materials that couldn’t stand up to the pressure Duroc exerted were ground into powder and fed into the Forge of the Ancestors, which then produced new blocks of raw material with slightly better attunement to Duroc’s energies.

As to what Duroc was working on… well, it would be easier for Kyle to list what his friend wasn’t upgrading. Layers of woven chain forged from mana-conductive crystals were being inlaid beneath slate gray plate armor, that in turn was inscribed with more durability and repair enchantments than Kyle could count. Boots and vambraces sat already completed off to the side, though Duroc still replaced small components here and there. The projects that took up most of the orc’s time were the upgrades to his spear and shield.

The shield was already a potent D Grade piece of equipment, made from a metal strong enough to clash against titanic creatures and incredibly efficient repair enchantments that allowed it to regenerate at a visible speed, even during combat. Duroc looked to be feeding the shield crystals that were slowly absorbed into the steel, each carrying with them traces of Duroc’s own mana. At the same time, Duroc was slowly shaping a wall of stone that perfectly mirrored the shield’s size and shape. Given all the compression he’d done, Kyle would have been shocked if the slab weighed less than a ton. He had no idea how the orc intended to integrate the materials, much less actually use the upgraded shield, but he trusted his friend had a plan.

The spear, on the other hand, was much simpler. The old weapon had been entirely dismantled, its components now slowly being forged around a new core made of compressed crystal that practically vibrated with Duroc’s energy. There was no doubt in Kyle’s mind how this weapon would work; it was designed with similar principles to the prosthesis the doctors on Er’Mithren made for Ghork. It would resonate with Duroc’s mana, and amplify the orc’s skills as he used them. And of course, it would be sharp. Really, really sharp.

As they worked, Kyle took mental notes of Duroc’s crafting process. While his own methods would have to look different, there was something in the cycle of creation and destruction that really spoke to him. He could already envision creating a harsh environment for his plants to attempt to grow, continuing to keep and cultivate those that could withstand it, while others were repurposed or used as fertilizer for the best prospects. A cruel method, to be sure, but one that limited waste. Duroc had the Forge of the Ancestors to recycle and refine materials, while Kyle and C.H.A.D.D. had the World Tree Seed. With luck, they might be able to develop a couple of new hidden aces as he broke into mid-tier C Grade.

The moment the final layers of metal wrapped around the spear, Duroc stopped working, drawing Kyle’s attention.
