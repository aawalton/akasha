In just a few short minutes, we had passed through more trees than I could count and finally arrived at the scene.

From our high perch among the thick branches, I spotted them below.

Five goblins were running for their lives.

They tore through the undergrowth in blind panic, stumbling over thick roots and crashing through thorny bushes, their breaths coming in ragged, desperate gasps. Branches whipped across their faces, leaving red welts as they pushed forward with everything they had left. Among them, one larger evolved goblin stood out immediately. He was carrying a much smaller female goblin on his back, her tiny arms wrapped tightly around his shoulders as she cried openly, her face buried against him in terror. The others stayed close, constantly glancing back over their shoulders, their eyes wide with raw, animalistic fear.

Afraid that Gopuran might rush straight into danger, I immediately stopped her.

“Wait! Puran! Stop!” I ordered.

Fortunately, she was not foolish enough to leap in blindly, even though panic was clearly written all over her body. Her hands were shaking way too much. She crouched low on the branch, every muscle tense, staring intently into the deeper part of the forest.

(Now what could it be?)

(What the hell is chasing these poor goblins?)

I got my answer almost immediately.

Something enormous burst into view.

A giant bear.

No.

Calling it a bear almost felt too small.

This thing was a monster!

Its body was massive, easily twice or even three times larger than the giant Horned Wolf I had killed before, covered in thick black fur. Its maw hung open, revealing rows of thick yellowed fangs slick with saliva. As it roars, strings of drool swung from its jaws. Its eyes burned red, a savage, hungry red.

Each step hit the ground like a hammer strike.

BOOM!

BOOM!

The earth trembled beneath its paws.

Small trees and saplings were crushed flat in its wake, while thicker trunks cracked and splintered as it barreled through the forest like a living avalanche.

Compared to this thing, the Giant Horned Wolves seemed more like kitties.

Then it swiped.

BOOM!

A nearby tree exploded apart. The thick trunk shattered instantly beneath its claw, sending splinters and chunks of bark flying everywhere. The goblins ducked just in time, narrowly avoiding being torn apart.

But the bear was not done.

With a furious roar, it raised one massive paw and slammed it into the ground.

BOOOOOOOM!

The sound was thunderous!

The earth cracked beneath the impact.

A visible shockwave blasted outward in a violent ring.

All five goblins were caught in it.

They were blown off their feet and sent crashing across the forest floor, rolling through mud, roots, and broken branches like rag dolls. Even from our high perch, I felt my vision shake through Gopuran’s eyes.

(Shit! What the fuck?!)

(Is this a skill?)

(It has to be a skill!)

(Damn, this bear is way too strong!)

I immediately tried using [Vassal’s Eyes] to assess its stats, but the skill failed to activate. So that confirmed it — I could only use it if the creature was inside my territory.

The situation was bad. Really bad.

(Fuck.)

(If Gopuran goes in, she will definitely die too!)

I could already tell what she was thinking. Her body shifted forward, ready to jump, but then even she hesitated and pulled back slightly.

That alone made my nonexistent heart sink.

“Puran! Think this through!” I shouted in panic. “You are no match! Leave them!”

Yes, saving those goblins would mean gaining five new citizens, which would be incredible. But the odds of actually defeating that thing, or even escaping alive, were so slim that risking Gopuran’s life for it felt insane. It was basically a suicide mission.

Theoretically, we could try to lure the giant bear back to the castle. If I dropped hundreds of units of wood on its head, maybe I could kill it.

Maybe.

But even that plan sounded far too dangerous. Puran could be caught and tore apart before she could even reach my castle.

But… the final decision was still in Gopuran's hand.

And quickly, she made it.

She reached into her ponytail and pulled out the leaf-wrapped poison mushroom she had hidden earlier. Using the blunt side of her dagger, she crushed it, and thick purple liquid splashed out. She smeared the glowing venom along the blade with one single quick swipe.

(Right! Poison! We definetely can use it!)

If what Gopuran had told me was true, if the [Purple Cap] could paralyze a Horned Wolf, then maybe, just maybe, it could work on this bear too.

“Big Boss, I…” Gopuran muttered, her voice trembling, choking from tears.

She was asking for permission.

Because she knew exactly what it meant if she jumped down there.

I hesitated for only half a second before making my choice. A difficult choice.

“Go, Puran.”

“If things go bad, you run first. Save yourself.”

Even though she was my citizen, she still had her own free will. If she was truly determined to save her friends, then who the hell was I to stop her?

“YES!”

Her voice came out firm now, sharp with resolve.

Fear was still there.

But so was determination!

******

Below, the evolved goblin put down the smaller female from his back, let out a desperate roar, and suddenly lunged at the giant bear.

He pushed off the ground and leaped forward with everything he had, dagger raised high above his head. His face was twisted with fear and fury, yet there was a fierce, desperate determination in his eyes.

“YARRRRRR!!!!”

For a split second, it almost looked heroic.

Then the blade came down. The dagger struck the bear squarely on the side of its massive skull.

And bounced right off.

The evolved goblin’s eyes widened in disbelief.

Not even a scratch.

The blade had failed to pierce the thick hide beneath that dense fur, as if he had just struck solid iron.

The bear did not even flinch. It slowly turned its monstrous head toward him.

Then it roared!

ROOOOAAARRR!!!

The sound exploded through the forest like thunder!

The shock of it hit the goblin point blank.

He froze up instantly.

His entire body locked up, ears flattened, dagger slipping from his trembling hand. The evolved goblin stood there completely helpless, his face drained of all color. Even without words, I could read the terror in his eyes.

He knew.

This was the end.

The giant bear raised one enormous claw, its curved talons glinting in the dim forest light. The goblin’s body trembled violently, but he could not move an inch.

Then, just as death was about to come crashing down…

Gopuran dropped from above.

BOOM!

She came down like a green meteor, crashing straight through the branches and slamming into the bear’s shoulder with all the force of her fall!

Leaves and splinters burst into the air as she drove the poison-coated dagger toward its neck!
