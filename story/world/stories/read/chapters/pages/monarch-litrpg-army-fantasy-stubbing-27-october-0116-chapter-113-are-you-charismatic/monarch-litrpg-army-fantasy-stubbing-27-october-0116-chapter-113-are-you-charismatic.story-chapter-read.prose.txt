Rayne didn’t know when the darkness had taken him.

One moment there had been pain, tearing through every part of his body, and the next, there had been nothing at all, and he had lost his consciousness.

But this time, there were no nightmares. His sleep had been disturbed by nightmares for a while now, and it had only worsened after the dungeon. He would keep seeing himself running after the necromancer, and losing more of his party in the process.

Sometimes, he will even see his past life, and the day he had been shot down. No matter how many times it repeated, the outcome never changed and he always found himself in a red puddle.

Every time he woke up from it, there would also be a weight sitting on him, a dull exhaustion that never really went away, no matter how long he rested, but this time, there had been nothing.

As Rayne slowly opened his eyes, he realized that he felt fresh and clear.

His mind felt lighter than it had in a long time, as if something that had been pressing against it for weeks had finally been lifted. Even his body responded differently as he shifted slightly, a faint sense of ease replacing the constant tension he had grown used to.

Rayne let out a quiet breath before pushing himself up, stretching his arms slightly as he sat.

He was still inside his tent. That much was clear with a single glance around, but someone had moved him as he laid on his cot now, a blanket pulled over him too.

Rayne’s gaze moved briefly around the tent, but there was no one else inside.

Through the slight gaps in the flap, he could see that it was already dark outside, the faint traces of firelight flickering from somewhere in the camp.

If he had to guess, he had been out for at least twelve hours. Maybe more.

For a while, he didn’t move.

He simply sat there, letting the quiet settle around him as his thoughts drifted back to his class advancement. Then, his expression grew a little more focused.

With a thought, Rayne pulled up his status screen.

Just one look was enough to tell him that things had changed a lot. His class no longer showed [Soldier], and was now [War Weaver].

His stats had shifted as well. There were two additional points in Arcane and Vitality, both higher than before, and alongside them sat something new.

Charisma.

Rayne’s gaze lingered on it for a moment. It stood at eight points. He had never seen it before, never even heard of it as a stat, and yet there it was, sitting alongside the rest.

Focusing on it, another notification appeared almost immediately.

Charisma: Affects presence, influence, and the ability to draw attention or sway others.

Only looking at the description, Rayne could already tell why the Charisma stat had appeared. It was clearly related to his class.

[War Weaver] leaned toward leadership at its core, and for such a class, Charisma stat made a lot of sense, especially if he was able to command larger numbers of soldiers more efficiently by increasing it.

Rayne’s gaze lingered on the number of points the stat had for a moment longer.

If he was right, the stat would have been six originally, and had gained two points due to him reaching Level 31 with the class advancement.

He didn't know if five was the average because he did feel like he would be above average with charisma originally. The stat would also get points differently if he recalled right with the class description.

Normal stats get points every level, but he would only get one point every three levels in Charisma. Fortunately with his otherworlder title, he would be getting double of it.

But he didn't know why he wouldn't get one point every level. Did all the hidden stats work differently? Were there even more of them?

Rayne frowned slightly as he considered that. He had never even known they existed before today.

Everything about them felt new, unexplained, and separate from the rest of the status he had grown used to. Maybe Casper would know something about it.

She was the most knowledgeable when it came to mana and the system among the people he knew. If anyone had an idea about hidden stats, it would be her.

The thought stayed in his mind for a moment before he pushed it aside. There were more immediate things to look at.

Rayne shifted his focus down, his gaze moving from his stats to the skills section that had also changed, but the first thing he noticed was the absence of something familiar.

[Sword Mastery] was gone, and in its place sat [Weapon Mastery].

Rayne stared at it for a second, his expression tightening slightly. Not only had it replaced the previous skill, but all the levels he had built in [Sword Mastery] were gone as well.

It had been reset.

A faint sense of loss rose in him at that. He had spent time building that skill, learning the Swift Wind Style and practicing till his skin bled, and now it had simply been overwritten.

Rayne exhaled quietly, pushing the feeling down as he focused on the new skill instead. If it had replaced the old one, then there had to be a reason for it.

With that thought, he opened its description, and was pleasantly surprised.

***

Weapon Mastery (Uncommon) - Level 1

Anyone can master one weapon, but those who master all of them are called weapon masters, and now you have the chance to become one.

The skill grants basic proficiency with all weapon types—Swords, spears, bows, axes and everything else you can think of.

Gives weapon proficiency, reduces inefficiency when using unfamiliar weapons and increases learning speed for all weapon-based skills.

Current Proficiencies -

Sword Mastery (Basic) - Level 3

***

Rayne read through it once, then again, making sure he hadn’t misunderstood anything. A quiet breath left him as the meaning settled in, and the tension that had built earlier eased almost instantly.

His original skill hadn’t been erased. It had simply been placed under something broader.

Instead of losing everything he had built, it had been expanded. His experience with the sword was still there, still intact, but now it was more of a sub-skill.

The main [Weapon Mastery] skill itself was strong.

Basic mastery in every weapon wasn’t something most soldiers had, but the army and the veterans always made it a rule for the soldiers to practice every weapon, and now he had a skill to do that for him.

He wouldn’t be as skilled as someone who specialized, of course. If he picked up a bow, he wouldn’t suddenly match Bran’s precision. But he wouldn’t fumble either.

He would be usable, and more importantly, he would improve faster. That alone made it almost overpowered.

Rayne hadn’t had the time or the freedom to train with different weapons before. Everything had been focused on survival and refining what he already knew. But now, with this skill, that limitation wasn’t as restricting anymore.

Still, it was something he would have to test later.

For now, he let the panel fade, his attention shifting to the other skills he had gained, and this time, he felt a faint spark of anticipation.

Unlike [Weapon Mastery], the other two new class skills were clearly mana skills.

Hence, he didn’t waste time and pulled both descriptions up at once.

***

Weaver’s Protection (Uncommon) - Level 1

Defending yourself is the most important thing for a warrior in battle, and with this skill, you can protect yourself better than anyone else.

The skill grants you the ability to create condensed mana threads and use them as barriers.

Gives the ability to create mana threaded barriers anywhere on the body or around the user, offering protection against physical and mana-based attacks.

***

War Pulse (Rare) - Level 1

A warrior needs to know what's going around them as a blade or spell could come from anywhere, and with this skill, you have the pulse of a war.

The skill allows you to be aware of all entities within range, ally or enemy.

Gives the ability to send a controlled pulse of mana within a defined area to become aware of everything inside of it, tracking of both allies and enemies and allows you to temporarily strengthen allied targets through mana.

***

Reading through both the skills, Rayne found himself thinking only one thing.

He had made the right choice.

The feeling settled in firmly, leaving no room for doubt. Whatever uncertainty he had felt before selecting the class had already begun to fade.

The first skill alone had already solved a major problem for him.

He had never had a proper defensive mana skill before. He had relied on his skill and physical shield to defend. But now, with [Weaver’s Protection], that gap was gone.

Rayne’s fingers twitched slightly as he imagined it in use, the idea of forming barriers out of mana threads sounded effective and cool, even flexible, something he could shape and adjust depending on the situation.

He wanted to try it immediately, but then he focused on [War Pulse]. It was, without a doubt, the best one he had gained.

Being aware of everything around him in a certain range, being able to track both allies and enemies, and even strengthen those fighting alongside him—it changed how battles would feel for him entirely.

Formations would become easier to manage, and he would be able to coordinate everyone. A perfect skill to better himself as a leader.

Rayne exhaled slowly, letting the thought settle.

He would still have to test both of them. He needed to understand their range, their limits, their mana cost, and how quickly he could use them in real combat.

But even without that, he already knew they were exactly what he needed. They would push his strength to another level.

Even more so when combined with the other gains he had gotten from the dungeon. With a thought, Rayne closed the skill panels, shifting his focus to something else as new ones appeared.

***

Analysis (Rare) - Level 1

Everyone wants to know information about people they see, and now you can see everything if you have enough mana.

The skill allows the user to gather basic information about a target using mana.

Gives details such as name, level, and affiliation. Increased mana input reveals deeper information, but cost rises significantly when used on stronger or higher-level targets.

***

Gravewalker

A lot of people have faced death in the face, and returned alive to tell the story, and you have done it enough times for even death to be angry with you.

Gives a 15 to 30% increase in all stats when you near death, scaling the closer you get to it.

***

Rayne studied both for a moment. He had already tested [Analysis] a few times. Mostly on soldiers.

At its base level, it gave him simple information, the most useful being the level of someone. That alone was useful, especially in unfamiliar situations. But when he had tried pushing more mana into it, the results had improved.

There were more details like class, even stats, but the cost had been high.

Even just gathering basic information from one soldier had taken around fifteen percent of his mana. That wasn’t something he could use freely in combat.

Still, it was valuable. With time, it would become even better.

As for Gravewalker, Rayne almost let out a faint breath of relief. He was glad it wasn’t something extreme.

When Nate and Kesh had joked about gaining the title turning him into something like a lich, he hadn’t found it amusing. The last thing he wanted was a skill that pushed him toward something unnatural.

But the skill was simply a boost, one that would save his life one day. Of that, he was certain. Though, it was still something he needed to test and—

The sound of footsteps outside the tent interrupted his thoughts, and he reacted immediately.

The panels vanished from his sight in an instant, leaving nothing behind. A second later, the flap shifted open, and Bran stepped inside.

His eyes moved toward Rayne almost immediately, taking him in before speaking.

“You woke up earlier than I expected,” he said. “Captain Edran said it might take a day.”

Rayne straightened slightly at that. “You know about my class advancement?” he asked.

Bran gave a small snort. “Everyone does,” he replied. “Captain Edran called for me and told me to look out for you while you went through it. When I came to check, you were already down.”

He shook his head slightly.

“You should have asked someone to guard you while advancing.”

Rayne frowned faintly at that. “I didn’t know I would pass out. The captain didn't tell me. I thought I would be safe enough here.” He paused briefly before adding, “But I guess people are still looking to put a knife in me.”

Bran’s lips curved into a faint grin.

“Some are,” he said. “There are a lot of noble sympathizers even among common soldiers. And you know your reputation.”

Rayne simply nodded, and saw Bran’s expression shifting slightly as sharp interest appeared in his eyes.

“What did you get?” he asked finally. “I won’t force you to tell me, but I’m curious.”

Rayne went silent for a moment, and considered it.

There wasn’t really any harm in telling him. The class name alone wouldn’t mean much to most people, especially when even Captain Edran hadn’t recognized it, but then another thought crossed his mind.

Rayne looked back at Bran, a smile curving up.

“Why don’t I show you?” he said.

Bran raised an eyebrow slightly. “Show me?”

Rayne gave a small nod. “Yes, call everyone,” he said. “I think it’s time for a practice session.”
