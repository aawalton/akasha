"I wouldn't say you would've died," Ais responded, tapping her chest with a confident gesture, she continued with a smirk, "I could've saved you even if you were touching the Goblin King's mouth… or something."

Alan looked at Ais’s face, noticing her utmost attempt not to laugh at her own joke. "Funny," he said as he stood up, "Could you create a diversion for me to attack?" Alan questioned.

Ais’s eyes narrowed. "Aren't you asking a lot of things today? Wait, wait, wait, are you skipping your training, Mister solo knight?" she added with a smile with her only motive being to piss Alan further, and she was quite successful in her attempt.

"At least I am fighting despite a burnout… not like a certain someone who got injured by the Goblin King's dead body!" Alan turned his gaze away from Ais’s face as he mockingly continued, "I wonder how the so-called s ranking swordmaster would have such slow reflex," he added.

"Slow reflex! Huh? Remind me who saved your ass just a few minutes ago!" Ais yelled at Alan.

Before Alan or Ais could continue, a loud sound caught both of their attention. The Goblin King was getting uneasy, he was breaking the rock pillar he had created earlier with his own hands in search of the two adventurers that had challenged him.

Ais removed her sword from the brown scabbard attached to her belt. It was a sword with a long silver blade, a golden circular crossguard, and a spherical pommel. It also had a dark bluish leather grip.

Ais Blackwell tightened her hold on her sword, her eyes focused on the stone pillar that stood right in front of her. She analyzed it for a second and turned her gaze towards Alan. "Behind me…," she instructed Alan.

Alan nodded and quickly moved behind Ais. The Goblin King's footsteps kept getting louder and louder as he aimlessly searched.

Ais turned her head to look behind herself. She looked at Alan, who was standing two steps behind her. That won't do, I need to be closer to him, she thought to herself as she took a step back.

"Wait, what!?" Alan said loudly, as he got flustered from the sudden contact.

"Just shut up and look the other way. Might make this less awkward," Ais said, her eyes fixed forward. She drew her blade to her left as Alan turned his head. The two stood back to back, ready to fight.

Ais closed her eyes as she tried to concentrate on the Goblin King's sound, picking up on his breathing, his footsteps, and every move. She filtered out every other sound, and she finally had him. Ais’s eyes opened. She knew where the Goblin King stood, he stood slightly to the right of the center of the arena he had created.

“Void Piercer” Ais said, and with her words, her blade began to illuminate with more light than ever. The rocks that were behind her melted with the sheer heat of the invisible blades projection, and with a swift motion, Ais slashed her sword in an arc.

Ais’s sword slash ripped through the stone field conjured by the Goblin King, shattering it into rubble in an instant. Every pillar in the area exploded and collapsed under the overwhelming force of her strike. The Goblin King, though positioned far away, was once again cleaved cleanly in two. But this time, the wound mended faster, black liquid oozing from the severed halves as it's body swiftly rejoined.

"It's getting faster," Ais mumbled, "Quick step!" She yelled. She vanished from her position and reappeared right behind the Goblin King. About time I checked my durability, she thought as a smirk appeared on her face.

The Goblin King instantly snapped his gaze toward Ais and struck her sword with a powerful punch. His physical reinforcement allowed him to strike Ais's blade, though there was an issue. Ais wasn't putting any strength in her sword. She backtracked her steps and let the Goblin King's full-powered punch hit the ground.

Alan appeared behind the Goblin King, who was facing Ais, and taking the benefit of Ais drawing his attention, he used his sword and the Formless Caster ability to cut the Goblin King's left hand.

Before the Goblin King could recover, Ais moved toward him in an instant and stabbed his right hand that was on the ground with her sword. "Orb of lightning!" she yelled, and instead of the sphere of light projecting in front of her hand, this time it projected right in front of her right foot.

Ais took hold of the sword with her left hand and gave a roundabout kick with the orb of lightning to the Goblin King's torso. The blast was big enough to take out half of its stomach, and it also propelled Ais backward.

Her right foot was deeply scarred by the attack she pulled, but it was less severe than the damage inflicted on the Goblin King since she enveloped her right foot with mana for protection. Ais looked at the Goblin King, his left hand didn't show any attempt to reattach to the main body. Meaning? The fight is nearly won.

"Alan, it's your chance!" Ais yelled at the top of her lungs.

I know it already, Alan thought as he heard Ais shout. His eyes narrowed, locking onto the Goblin King’s body. In the next instant, he dashed forward with his top speed.

The Goblin King stood back up again, his attention now focused on Alan. He had noticed the fact that his left hand wasn't healing. He rushed towards Alan with a growl letting his feral instinct take hold of himself, in an attempt to kill the adventurer before he could cause him more damage.

"Formless Caster, Inferno!" Alan yelled. Condensed flame surrounded his black blade, threatening to engulf the battlefield in one attack.

Alan lowered his stance, bending his knees as if a viper readying to jump. "Quick step," he mumbled, and used the propelling fire to quickly traverse the distance between himself and the Goblin King. Alan's sword Ferrust pierced the Goblin King's heart, and in the next moment the condensed Inferno spell was unleashed within the creature’s body. Dark black blood was splattered around the battlefield, and a large hole appeared on the Goblin King's chest. The beast finally fell to its death.

Alan looked at the Goblin King's motionless body, Guess he's finally down for good…, Alan thought. His vision began to blur in the next moment his body had reached its limit. he was now not only suffering from not just mana burnout but also a physical toll from all the fights he took part in. His grip on his sword loosened, and it fell on the ground.

Ais appeared next to Alan using, Quick step spell and took hold of his free-falling body before he could hit the ground. "You can sleep now," she mumbled in a caring voice as she took Alan's left hand and placed it on her shoulder.

Ais looked at Alan's face, a mischievous smirk appearing on her lips as she saw his helpless body. Should I carry him more effectively? she questioned.

"Princess carry huh, hehe, that surely would be funny, especially when he wakes up and Sir Blake or Bella teases him," Ais added. She removed his hand from her shoulder and placed his body on the ground. Ais looked to her left and bent to grab Alan's sword placing it in his scabbard.

"Well that takes care of his sword," Ais added as a villain-like smile spread on her lips. She bent down, reaching to hold him in her arms, but stopped midway. Wait, wouldn't it cause the other adventurers to make fun of him too? she thought.

Ais stopped and gave a thoughtful look to Alan before giving him a simple shoulder carry instead of her humiliating plan. "I am letting you off the hook for just today," she said with slight frustration as she walked with Alan towards the other adventurers.
