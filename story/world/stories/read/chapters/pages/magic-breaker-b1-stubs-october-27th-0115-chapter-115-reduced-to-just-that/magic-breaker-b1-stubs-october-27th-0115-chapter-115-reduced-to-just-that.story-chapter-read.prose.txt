If you’re in danger, go up. If you can’t go up, go far. If you can’t go far, dig deep.

Treat the forest with respect, because its guardians will kill you. The soil is not your friend. The plants are not your friends. Most climbers on the third don’t die from beasts - they die from having desecrated the forest, and getting hunted down by guardians.

So, go up, because the canopy is out of the reach of the forest. Run far, because distance is safety. And dig deep, because if the roots can still reach you, so can the forest.

But there is one upside to this. If you’re running from something other than the forest, then you’re being hunted. And there is nothing the forest favours more than prey on the run.

Understand this. Understand what fairness means, understand that your best friend may change on a dime, understand that if you wish to rest, be strong or hide. You cannot trust the forest, but you must rely on it, anyway. So make sure you are in good standing./
