Samantha left the dungeon to go dematerialize, and even let Deikmorn stay, though insisting he not ‘occupy her time unduly’ and depart after they had a final, brief conversation in private. She promised him no decisions would be made in regard to Nyomel without running them by him. Also, she assured him ‘execution’ was not really on the table.

The truth was — the more she considered it as she shunted to the spirit world again — she technically owed Nyomel for her help. Things might’ve gone south in both instances in which she’d helped out. Beyond that, she was not an insignificant force in either battle as a whole. She was quite powerful, with an impact similar to her other champions. She knew this even surprised her Naugite allies something fierce, as her status had risen very high. Nyomel had needed to prepare those people for her imprisonment, stating she’d committed a grave sin of disobedience to ‘Puck’ and honorably had to submit to be punished. Due to Naugite honor codes, they had to accept this, even if it made them quite sour.

Indeed, if Samantha had executed the woman, there would’ve been serious political hell to pay. Not that she had ever really been planning it.

There was another element to what she felt she owed, too. What could make someone so utterly, pathologically submissive? She would give her life for obedience, without a thought. She regarded herself as nothing, utterly dedicated to her purpose. That was exceptionally sad and disturbing. She’d even seen it in Deikmorn’s face as she had chided him a bit playfully about not ‘getting busy’ while she was gone. He looked offended at the idea, now. Regret was cast deep in his eyes, amidst all the other emotions he must be feeling. Their dynamic was not what he thought it was.

Or so he’d think. It’s not as if the woman behind the mask couldn’t be bloody well enjoying herself. In fact, I’m certain she cherishes their relationship. Ugh. I might’ve ruined things, huh? Hmm. Bah! Who am I kidding?! She’s like the ultimate seductress. If she wants him, she’ll have him.

Regardless, she felt obligated to handle the woman with care. No matter how tough she was, that kind of nature or outlook was dangerous and a supreme vulnerability. It begged for abuse, essentially. The onus was on her, having that supreme control, whether she liked it or not. This was something never far from her new status in general, as what was effectively a ruler.

You either had the power or you didn’t; whether you leveraged it was a separate matter. To some degree, she had to. She barked orders with supreme authority behind them, and people ran to obey. Where was the line of abusing the authority? It wasn’t so easy to mark. One could answer, ‘Where it becomes evil,’ but mere humans — and mere humans playing at being gods — were evil on the daily.

It was naive and immature to think she wasn’t — at least in her professional life — dominating others. How the fuck could authority be separated from it? She could strip it down in various fashionable exposures and balance it in such a way that those admiring looks and smiles sent her way made her feel smugly self-justified. She charmed them with her mercy; she fluttered their hearts with her compassion.

Yet, at the end of the day, she ordered people around, enforced hard schedules, pushed folks to do more, more, more, and sent men to their bloody, painful deaths.

Sometimes, these things made her feel maddeningly inept, awful, and hypocritical. If she paused to consider how she might do better, she knew she’d see a thousand things. And she knew she wouldn’t be able to handle how much that would weigh and drag her down. How much it would grind things to a halt even as it terrorized her and exposed even more inadequacies. She’d still make mistakes, still fail people…

And she knew, whatever thousands of things she saw to correct and make more fair, gentle, moderate — whatever — whatever she thought she could do, half of it would be wrong in the end, and abuse her power even more. Get more people killed and compromise the end goal a little bit more.

She couldn’t do that.

Revolution. You’re so fucking ugly and disgusting. Wedded in incest to war and misery. Damn you! I can’t keep you all out of each other’s arms! Or mine. We’re all in bed together, aren’t we? And glory is the sex we make! How good it feels, huh?! Isn’t it beautiful, these twisted limbs together?!

Such maudlin thoughts had to be gored out of her head. She was prone to this lately. Doubts were creeping in about the things she demanded Jeeves tell her about, things she made him show her. To know the bodies buried, and where, and what they looked like before they were.

Sitting down in a random corner of a dank, dark dungeon, she found herself shaking and sweating, having gotten halfway through her dematerialization and then losing focus with the shitstorm that hit her. She opened her eyes to see her fist clenched in front of her, teeth bared and clenched just the same. Tears leaked from her eyes.

Forcibly, she straightened her hand and unclenched her jaw. She meditated in truth and calmed herself. Clarity of will forced it all out when she simply Invoked her classes.

There was no time to pause, no time to falter, and no time to go mad. More of herself to sacrifice on the altar of divinity. There was only the mission, the quest, the purpose, so far higher than herself and her petty concerns.

All I can do is keep the fucked-up train churning forward. Dump more shit in the shit furnace and keep it burning. Drive the machine, jury-rig this stinky motherfucker on the move, crack the whip, and be the Big Bad Bitch making things happen with the fuel of flesh.

Sadly, this necessity made it clear just how little difference there was between her and Nyomel. It was the same fucking thing, wasn’t it? Always, it was the same purpose they enslaved themselves to: to end it once and for all. Ram one machine into another and blow the whole track to hell. Everything. So that the circles they were going in would finally be broken.

Whatever whores they made of themselves to that purpose, it was that, or just give up ever being free from it.

As she found herself utterly forgiving Nyomel of everything, and fully planning on embracing her and accepting her when things were said and done, she realized also what had irritated her to begin with. It wasn’t the betrayal, at all, at least not specifically. It was the idea of it — that she could be tricked. It potentially compromised everything. What if she had not been looking out for Samantha? What if someone else was lurking closer to her, that wasn’t?

It was a failure. In this, she had to get better. If she couldn’t, she’d have to put others in charge who could.

With that final bit of clarity, she nodded to herself and dematerialized.

From her throne, she headed out of Heaven entirely, flying into the greater aggregate of the spirit world. From there, she reached out to her ally, Avenging Leviathan, with direct mental communication.

“Levi,” she said with strong insistence, “it’s imperative that I speak with Endless Templar immediately. Forgive me, but there is no other solution, and I must demand it.”

Levi let out a mental sigh, like rumbling thunder. “I see. What has she done?”

“It’s an internal matter. Depending on her, the whole thing could be quite amiable.”

“I will suggest she make sure that it is.” There was a long pause. “Though this greatly changes the waters, we will do it. It’s about time she makes a direct connection anyway. Matrazere awaits. I will facilitate and sanction it, Samantha.”

Before Samantha could ask for expansion on that, Levi disconnected. Moments later, she felt the semi-prayer effect of a new connection to a god-like entity. Only, it was… strange in the extreme. It felt very distant and faint, as if coming from a too-far distance — which made no real sense — and it was deeply, deeply alien. On top of all of this, it seemed… excited. Caught in a thrill.

Samantha’s eyes widened, briefly wondering if she was mistaken about who was even reaching out. But it had to be her, right?

With some reluctance, she accepted the communication.

Immediately, she could barely handle the sheer intensity of it crashing at her like a giant punch. As she spiritually braced, though, it enveloped her instead of hitting her. ‘Glomped’ was more apt.

So was formed a mental space, like most such first encounters with neutral entities kept at arm’s length. She’d done this shit before.

She was floating over an alien, blasted surface of dark rock, with a sky made of many-hued mirages as if seen in innumerable puddles of oil, making for a strange haze. It was alien yet strangely beautiful. A few scant, bright stars could be seen in the sky through the haze, just barely.

“Hello!” It was a voice of several layers overlaid, with the deepest one resonating up from the surface, and the rest echoing from a distance. “Father said it was finally time, and I rushed as soon as I could to contact you.” Energy danced excitedly between the oily mirage clouds above.

The voice was mostly alien, though through the spirit, Samantha knew beyond any shadow of a doubt that this was the one behind Endless Templar. Knowing this and believing it were two different things, however.

Samantha, glowing bright as the sun with a burning nimbus — as if to protect herself — floated amidst it all in bewilderment. “Huh?! Just who and what in the hell are you?!”
