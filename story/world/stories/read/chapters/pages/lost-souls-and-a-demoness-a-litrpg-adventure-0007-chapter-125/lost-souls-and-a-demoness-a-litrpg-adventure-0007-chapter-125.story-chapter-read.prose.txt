Jade looked over her stat page, marveling at how far she'd come. At how far they'd all come. Luis and Naomi were both past the level 20 mark as well, breaching C rank alongside her. Neither had received advanced classes, they didn't have enough titles yet, but it was an achievement for all of them. She remembered Aylin, the mage they'd travelled with on the previous floor, telling her that it typically took four to six months for wayfarers to break through level twenty. And here they were, barely two months in.

It's all happening so fast. Jade mused, paging through her spell list. And now that we're back in the Labyrinth, it's only going to get faster.

Fast was good. The more cores they destroyed, the safer Earth would be. The sooner they uncovered the next demon ritual sites, the less likely an interdimensional invasion would be.

It sounded so easy when put like that… But Jade knew it would be anything but.

Shifting her attention back to her stats, Jade finalized her point allocation. Every attribute save fortitude had received a boost, with agility and intelligence getting the largest. With her warded armor and a potent defensive racial ability, Jade couldn't justify spending many attribute points on her vitality. Speed, stealth, and charm were her true defenses, and focusing on anything else would be a mistake.

"We're going to need better armor, now that we're C rank." Jade commented, not taking her eyes from her menu screen as they drew closer to the quadrant node.

"That's right!" Luis exclaimed, slapping his fist into his palm. "We can wear higher level gear now!"

"With all the money we've gotten from quest rewards, and the materials we grabbed from the core space, we should have no problem buying better gear when we get to the city. They'd better have something nicer than these boring leathers.""You can wear my armor if you like." Jade offered."Not quite the fashion I'm into." Naomi smirked. "But you pull it off surprisingly well."

"Surprisingly?" Jade asked, accompanying the words with a flat look.

"What? You can't tell me it's your first choice. I'll be sad to see it gone. It's very… striking.""It won't be, unfortunately." Jade muttered. "The craftsman in Lysara made it upgradeable. He told me an enchanter in any big city could elevate it to C rank once I could handle it."

"Really? That's awesome! How much stronger will it get?" Luis asked, intrigued.

"No idea, but it should be able to take more hits."

"Great! That'll give me more time to get a better shot.""A better what?" Jade asked Naomi, confused.

"Never mind, I'll show you later." Her friend gave her a wink. "When it's done."

No amount of questions were enough to get Naomi to spill the secret. Finally, Jade returned to her menus with a huff. She wanted to get this done before they transited to the third floor. Tabbing to another page, Jade viewed her remaining unspent upgrade points.

Upgrade points

Standard upgrade points: 2

Superior upgrade points: 1

The standard upgrade points could be used to enhance any one of Jade's abilities or to boost the progress of a spell or talent. The only difference between a regular upgrade point and a superior one was that the latter could boost a spell all the way from C level to B level at once while the former could not. With that in mind, Jade chose to spend her first upgrade point on the spell Charm Creature. It gained experience more slowly than most of her other spells and if she was going to be venturing onto the third and fourth floors, she wanted all of her tools to be up to the task. That, and her racial boost to charm spells would send it all the way to B rank if she cast it while in her demon form.

System Notice

Spell 'Charm Creature' has advanced from Level D (51%) to Level C (14%).

Jade's magical senses were now sensitive enough for her to detect the subtle shifting of her body's internal magical circuitry. The sensation only lasted a few moments, but she could immediately feel the difference. The pathways for the spell hadn't changed so much as they had grown more… efficient? Capable of carrying a heavier load than they had moments before. The spell would now be more effective, and when cast in her demon form, be capable of targeting multiple creatures at once.

Serrethis, who tutored her in Lysara, had explained to Jade how long it took to achieve these kinds of advancements without the Labyrinth streamlining the process. Years of training and study, all bypassed with a single mental click. Not for the first time, Jade wondered at the true purpose of this world. A Universe that constantly ravaged new planets while simultaneously gifting great power to those who entered it. And at the heart of it all, a prison that held vast armies of ravenous demons. She'd gleaned some insights into the Labyrinth's nature, but it wasn't enough. She needed to learn more.

And to do that, she'd need to be stronger.

Jade's next upgrade point went into the ability granted to her by her advanced class.

Ability: Heartbreaker

When you hit a target you have charmed with a melee attack you may choose to consume to charm to deal critical damage. Bonus damage scales with the strength of the charm effect. If this damage kills an enemy, all of your cooldowns are reduced by thirty seconds.

Upgrade effect: When you deal critical damage with this ability, one stat of your choice will be greatly enhanced for a brief period.

Another window popped up, asking Jade to confirm which stat she would like to choose. Though the choice appeared to be permanent, the decision was an easy one.

Ability: Heartbreaker

Upgrade effect: When you deal critical damage with this ability, your agility stat will be greatly enhanced for a brief period.

"These advanced class features are something else."

"Rub it in, chica.""Don't be a grump, Luis." Naomi said, coming to her defense. "She's more than earned it."Looking up, Jade saw that they were nearly at the node. A group of L-Ref scouts were positioned atop the valley ridge the crystalline orb was situated on, establishing a perimeter around the transport device. She noted that just as many of them were watching the node as were monitoring the surrounding area.

"Hold on, I'm almost done." Jade said, stopping near one of the soldiers. "Just finishing up.""I thought you'd already decided on your choices?" Naomi asked as Luis moved to speak to the guard.

"I have. Just taking one last look."

While Luis worked to get them cleared to use the transit device, Jade pulled up the selection screen for her superior upgrade point. This one was an easy decision. When it came to spells that had served her best in her time in the Labyrinth, there was a clear winner. She spent the point.

System Notice

Spell 'Allure' has advanced from Level C (2%) to Level B (0%).

System Notice

Spell 'Allure' will be unable to progress further until you reach B rank.

Another rush of power, this one more familiar. Jade had cast Allure at B rank many times already, thanks to her succubus nature. Now, she'd finally be able to cast it at A rank. At least, without relying on her Bondage Queen ability to boost her spell strength while tied up. Shaking her head to clear some especially embarassing memories, Jade finally moved on to selecting the new Shadow Witch class feature reaching level 22 granted. Unlike her previous levels, this time there were only three options to choose from.

Ability: Dark Strike

Your weapon damage increases by 25%.

Ability: Spell Penetration

Your spells are 25% harder to resist.

Ability: Stride in Shadows

You are 50% more difficult to detect.

Jade had glanced through these options before returning to Earth and she'd already put a lot of thought into her selection. Her initial instinct was to go with the damage boost. It was a tangible upgrade to her combat power that would be applicable in almost every battle, whether against Labyrinth monsters or demons. Some soul searching, combined with discussions with her friends, convinced her this wasn't the right path for her. She'd chosen the Glamourblade advanced class over Umbral Assassin for a reason. Her role in this party wasn't to be a damage dealer, at least not primarily. No, her main responsibility was to disrupt the enemy, get into their heads, and generally cause chaos.

With that in mind, the decision was clear. She selected 'Spell Penetration'. Her body pulsed with another rush of power, blue light swirling around her before fading away. Jade closed the last of her system windows as the effect subsided, taking a deep, calming breath.

"Everything okay?" Naomi asked

"Yeah. Just getting my head on right. How about you? Ready to dive back into this mess?"

"I can't believe I'm saying this but… Yeah, I think I am.""We've changed a lot in a short time, haven't we."

"Mostly thanks to you, I think."

"Huh?" Jade blinked in surprise. "What do you mean?"

"You're always in front, leading the way and driving us forward." Naomi grinned. "It gets us into trouble, but it also keeps us going. Without you, I'd probably still be hiding on the first floor. You pushed us to get better."

Jade didn't know what to say. She shuffled her feet, embarassed.

"Thanks, Jade." Naomi grabbed her hand and started walking towards the node. Luis was there, waving them over. "Now, let's go find the rest of our team."

"I hope they're alright. And that Tara hasn't gotten into too much trouble..."

"Oh, I'm sure she has. Let's get there before she decides blowing something up will fix the problem."

"What if it would fix the problem?"

Naomi gave her the side eye.

"Okay, okay… No blowing things up. At least, not until we've tried something else."

"…Good enough."
