Hitting my target was not the issue, but I realized something upon my approach. That rancid smell that lingered in the air around it was sooo much worse up close. Then, when my legs wrapped around its squishy flesh, I instantly felt the wetness of its rot flesh.

I tried to cast my mind away before I began questioning every single life decision that led me to this moment and instead focused on stabbing.

I jabbed both daggers down, they entered the flesh surprisingly easily, almost like butter. I ripped them back out at an angle and stabbed again and again as the creature began to wail.

It thrashed around, and thanks to the slickness of its rock flesh I was soon thrown off. I flipped backwards through the air and landed against a wall, one hand reaching back to grab a windowsill as I hung above the monstrosity that stomped around in circles, trying to find the source of its pain.

{Veil of Shadows}.

I began scaling horizontally across the face of the wall, its sight drifted right past me.

You know, as I mentioned before in my new puppy life, the smell of rot didn’t bother me nearly as much as I’m sure it did Lisha. If I was hungry enough I didn’t even always mind it because even old meat still had some good bits left usually.

But this was on another level. It was just awwwwful. I needed something to plug my nose with.

But no, I could deal with the smell. Honestly I already felt it fading from my mind, like the trauma to my senses had actually stopped my brain from processing it.

Then I felt my fingertips vibrating. I looked up and around myself, trying to find the source. It was like the whole world was shaking all of a sudden.

But then I finally found it—it was Luke!

Clouds of dust kicked up under his paws as he darted out into the open area. With the Pale Wailer’s attention focused high, it didn’t see him coming at all. But Luke didn’t strike. Instead he just started running literal circles around the monster, kicking up dust.

My brother was big, but he wasn’t that big. Yet I could feel his pawsteps vibrating up the stone building three stories up, and not only that, down below I saw how all the shattered bones danced and bounced along the surface of the ground.

That had to be some kind of skill! And all these little things were not the only effect, and far from the most significant.

I watched as the Pale Wailer tried to turn in a circle, but it was stumbling, taking a quick step back to right itself. It seemed confused, being able to get its eyes on Luke but not keep him in front.

I decided to make things even harder on the overgrown dinosaur maggot!

When its back was turned, I launched myself at it once again. I could see spurting grey goo from the wounds to its back I’d caused and I aimed for another spot just a little lower. I decided I wanted to try something a little different this time because I didn’t think poking this thing over and over again would ever actually take it down.

This time when I landed, I didn’t try to grip onto it. I just drove in both daggers and let myself sliiiide.

Going backwards wasn’t going so well, and as I felt it try to hike me off I flattened my body and turned, leaving one dagger in as I slid my way down the world’s grossest slip and slide, dragging a single dagger above my head then flayed the monster as I went.

“WRRAAAAAAAAAHHHHHGGGGGGGAA!!” It wailed, more piercing than any before. It rattled me completely, not just sinking into my bones but deep into my skull as well. By the time I made it to the base of its tail, I realized something felt very wrong.

I tried to kick off and launch myself towards the buildings, but my legs felt like jelly. I instead rolled off the side and went to the ground, but by the time I got there I couldn’t dart into hiding like I planned.

I felt Luke’s skill vibrating through the soles of my bare feet and up through my knees, and at first thought that was the problem. But when he realized the friendly fire, Luke stopped and retreated into an alley.

Yet, my legs still felt wobbly, but not just my legs.

Lifting my head, everything spun around me, and as that wail crashed through my skull once again I felt my vision fading.

“Ooooh n-n-nooo,” I gasped, stumbling for a building, setting my eyes on the open doorframe with a splintered old collapsed door hanging from it. But I didn’t make it far.

A massive white leg kicked backwards, practically folding my body in half as it lifted me off the ground and sent me flying.

I spun through the air, having no idea which way was up. That is, until my body flattened against a stone wall, at which point, up was definitely the wrong way.

I started falling head-first back to the street, and thankfully Cat Footed took effect even through my delirium as I managed to land in a crouch. Actually, I felt surprisingly better already. This time when I moved my legs, I was instantly able to get a full run going.

And good thing too, because the monster was charging right at me.

Its mouth gaped open and sucked in a breath, and I realized what the consequences might be if it screamed directly in my face.

Its chest expanded until its ribs slid outward, dripping rot, but I’d already turned tail and run.

I dove behind the corner of an alleyway as I heard it scream, I felt the sound sinking into my body again, but not quite the same as last time. This time it wasn’t burrowing deep into my skull, so I knew I’d be all right.

But I just continued to run, making it to the next corner and looking back to see its massive body blocking the entire sidestreet. But now wasn’t the time for a showdown, I had to back off.

I dove into another alley then started jumping. Making my way up to one building, and then another. I let out a breath of relief, knowing I was safe up here. I heard it stomping around down below, and I wondered where Luke had gone off to.

I went to the side of a building to check for him, but a shadow caught the corner of my eye as if something was jumping above me. Themiel?

No, it’s a skeleton lady!

The Night Sentinel descended on me and I barely rolled out of the way in time. When I popped back to my feet she was slashing at me with a follow up attack and I got my own daggers up just in time to deflect.

She jumped back, I feinted an attack, she backed up more, but I took an extra step and stabbed for real.

My dagger plunged between her ribs. She screamed and grabbed me so I grabbed her back. We had an awkward standing wrestling match, turning each other in circles until finally I got into an advantageous angle and twisted my body in a quick circle all at once.

I lifted her off her feet and tossed her off the roof, then followed her to the ledge to watch.

She reached for a wall but found nothing to grip, ending up bouncing off it instead and falling to the street below with her head popping off and rolling.

But she wasn’t dead, her headless body scampered after the screaming skull, but there was an even bigger shadow coming up behind her.

Thump thump thump thump thump.

By the time she slammed her head back onto her shoulders and turned to look behind her it was too late.

The Pale Wailer dove, scooping her up and crunching through her bones just as fast as the last one it had snagged, then shaking it around, sending the bones flying in all directions.

Undead Vanquished! Level 22 Skeleton Night Sentinel.

Partial Experience Gained!

Huh, partial huh? That hasn’t really happened before. Guess I haven’t really done much killing outside of it happening with my pack.

But I got full experience with Luke, or maybe just when something is completely unallied but finishes off a target that makes the difference.

Whatever the case, I also got a good look at the damage I’d done from my maggoty slip-and-slide tactic, and I saw a huge gash down the length of its body, thick grey sludge bleeding out and oozing down its body on both sides.

At least my close call produced a substantial—

ANOTHER ONE!

Another Night Sentinel came at me. Her daggers whistling through the air. I had to retreat off the side of the roof, swinging myself down and into a window. I landed in the dark room and cast {Veil of Shadows}, slinking to a wall and watching the windows. Down below, I heard the Pale Wailer let out another of its horrifying screeches but it didn’t sound like it was actively chasing anything at this point. Luke seemed wise enough not to attack on his own, but I still had to finish dealing with these stealthy skeletal nuisances so I could get back to the real fight.

I saw skeletal fingers grip the top of the window I’d just come from as the Night Sentinel flipped herself into a crouch on the window sill. Her ever-smiling skeleton face turned slowly as she scanned the dark room and slowly drew her daggers from her waist.

She stepped down, slowly, quietly, and started moving through the room, the glow inside her hollow sockets looking this way and that.

I crept along the wall, intending to move in behind her, but a clink of bones drew my attention to another window.

Damn it! A second skeleton lady!

Don’t you gals realize it is beneficial for you to allow me some peace while I finish off that maggoty monstrosity down below? I’ve seen him chomp through at least two of your friends without a second thought!

The second one stepped down from the window sill as well, and started creeping through the room just as slow and stealthily as the last.

I made my way to a third window and looked up and around the outside of the building, making sure there were no third skeleton ladies who were late to the party. But no, it seemed these two were the only ones I had to worry about.

This is fine. I’ll just assassinate one and then deal with the other, then get back to business down below, I thought.

I slid away from the ray of sunlight coming through the window, then matched Skeleton Lady 2’s steps as she followed Skeleton Lady 1. I put each of my footsteps down right at the same time she did, except I stepped a little further, stretching my legs wide so I was slowly gaining on her.

Closer, closer, until finally—

I lunged, wrapping one arm around her and yanking her back as I stabbed my other dagger at her eye. I wrestled her to the ground, clinging to her while I stabbed and stabbed and soon—

Undead Vanquished! Level 22 Skeleton Night Sentinel.

Experience gained!

Hell yeah! Take that!

I popped back up just in time to use my daggers to deflect an attack from skeleton lady 1. We pivoted around each other, her slashing, then me slashing, each backing up and barely dodging, kind of feeling each other out.

I kept the walls of the room in the back of my mind, I didn’t want to get cornered.

Control the center, I thought. I didn’t know much about boxing but I know getting your “back against the ropes” was a bad thing and obviously that would be because the loss of options mobility-wise.

I dodged an attack, pivoted forward and stabbed.

I caught her ribs, slicing open the bandage wrap a little and maybe chipping some bone, but it was a minor attack.

She came at me, I dodged, she continued to press the attack. I pivoted to the side, lifting a dagger to block a committed lunging strike then dropped low and slashed at her pelvis.

She shrieked, so I guess it hurt her but as she backed up I pressed the attack, coming up with an uppercut with my dagger right under her ribs.

This was a significant hit, I could tell simply by how she abandoned offence and instead clinched against me, wrapping her bony arms around me and trying to spin away and escape my dagger. I continued to rip and twist, trying to get my other dagger up so I could stab her face but her bony arm had it pinned to my side.

I instead decided to take this to the ground, throwing myself down with her, then as we rolled, I found myself underneath but cocked my knees back into my stomach. I pushed my back against the ground and kicked out, sending her flying off of me and into a—

No, she went right past the wall, into a dark stairwell I hadn’t noticed.

I heard her scream and clatter down the stairs, until she tumbled all the way to the bottom where I heard a shattering sound like dropped dishes.

Okay, let’s go finish her off, I thought, following her to the stairwell.

But as I came to the top of it I realized I had a different problem.

It wasn’t shattering dishes, that would be ridiculous of course.

It was shattering pots.

My mouth fell open as I saw Skeleton Lady 1 stand up from the mess of sludge that covered her, she screamed, dropping her daggers and started clawing at herself, trying to wipe the sludge off. But it was too late.

Tendrils from the puddle on the floor shot out, wrapping around her bones, pulling itself into her. As soon as she managed to fling some off of her, more attached to her, tethering itself to her, pulling her down to her knees.

“Ah—” I took a step back, looked towards the light at the end of the room from the windows we’d entered from.

She continued to wail.

“GYAAAAAAAAA!”

She was brought to her knees as the black sludge totally overtook her. Not just wrapping around her, but filling her up, sliding under her ribs, filling the hollow cavity of her gut and pelvis, and worst of all… taking shape.

Muscles began to form, wrapping around her bones and twisting into cords of black-red flesh.

“Ooooh, not good, not good,” I muttered, gripping my daggers. Should I just run? Would she follow me? I had enough to deal with right now, damn it!

More sludge crawled over her, layering over the muscles, her arms and legs thickening; her skeletal fingers disappeared beneath blobs of flesh before she extended an arm and new digits pushed out, each one ending in hooked, black claws.

The screaming stopped, and somehow that was worse.

The thing that she had become slowly lifted its head. There was no face yet, the squirming flesh was still working on that part, patches of skull still visible beneath half-formed flesh. But as its mouth opened, black sludge like saliva dripped between its jaws. Then, pinpricks of green light appeared where its eyes should have been.

“Hahahahaa—NOPE!”

I turned tail and ran, but as I made it nearly to the window, I felt a rush of wind behind me. I threw myself sideways as something went ripping past my head. A clawed hand punched straight through the wall beside me, collapsing it and spraying stone chips outside.

I scrambled back to my feet and leapt for the next window I saw, kicking off the windowsill and jumping clear across the street to the next building. I scaled it quickly, glancing below just long enough to see the massive maggoty tail of the Pale Wailer disappear down the adjacent roadway.

I scaled my way to the roof and looked back to the building I’d just come from.

The fleshy monster stood in a window sill, claws gripping the side of it, jaw open and dripping more of that black sludge. It still had the Skeleton Night Sentinel’s cloak hanging from its shoulders, flapping in the wind.

Then, it leaped towards me with an inhuman jump, rising one story, two—clearing the distance without any trouble.

I ran to give myself some room but stopped at the other end of the building, turning and taking my fighting stance just as it landed in a crouch. It wasn’t carrying daggers, it didn’t need them, but its green glowing eyes set on me and it almost seemed to smile.

Somewhere far below, the Pale Wailer let out another scream.

“WRRRRAAAAAAHAHHHHGGGAAAAAA!!!”

Seriously! Didn’t I say I had enough to deal with right now?!!!
