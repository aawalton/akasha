That will just make them more of a pain in the ass to deal with later, James thought. Maybe I should have been nicer to people when I was walking around the room… No, I made a good impression, right?

Wait, are they anticipating someone attacking them? James wondered. If they’re thinking they’re going to lose people, could they be worried about me? I mean, religious groups always expect persecution from the people who don’t believe as they do… and they’re often right. But this is a big faction. Fuck your recruitment efforts.

Looks like I saved her from being saved, James thought. Go be religious on your own, if you want to, but stay away from these wackos.

As with most of the Rulers present, he was noticeably inferior to James in power and presence.

Oh, of course it would be that. I was starting to wonder. I thought I kept a lid on it, but of course they would have some way of knowing… Damn it, I killed Cyrus to avoid something like this happening!

Fuck, they know what happened fairly specifically, then. Not just about Cyrus dying, but the whole group.

Right, I kind of forgot that Luna was branded in the aftermath of that fight. Damn. Shit. I actually don’t have a good excuse for that. At least not one they’ll accept.

Fuck you. If there’s one thing the last century or two has taught us, it’s that world leaders can kill whoever they want within their own territory, and as long as they remain sovereign, there’s nothing anyone can do about it.

Assuming that the future Sister Strange showed me is set in stone, it won’t make any difference whether I give them what they want or not. They’d still find some reason to attack my family and my country even if I was dead.

That would sound sort of reasonable if I trusted you at all. Unfortunately, I can throw any of you much further than I trust you. This sounds like a plot to enlarge your Kingdom of Heaven.

Building heaven on Earth doesn’t sound consistent with the Bible at all, James thought. Honestly, it sounds blasphemous. Your family?”
