Clementine took out her phone to look at the list that she had just written down, and quickly read it out to Ashton, so that he could cross-reference the info with what he could see.

Also, since the people in charge of checking the store out were standing right here, he could also give some advice depending on the item, because he actually figured he knew what some of the objects were.

And just like he thought, a lot of the basic items overlapped with the vending machine in the primary school. The same sorts of potions, candies, chocolates, magic stones, and similar items were offered here in this shop. Just that this place had a greater variety at a time than the vending machine.

"I've tried some of these items before; most of them are safe, and you can just sort of guess at the effect based on the product's name. Even so, still only ever try a small amount at first," Ashton started. "And from my experience so far, all the items with 'slime' in the name, even if they're called 'Soda' or 'Candy' are not edible. They're potion ingredients that need to be neutralised first. They're very acidic and will melt you from the inside."

"Huh? Wait, seriously?" Clementine looked up from her phone, surprised. "I actually bought one of those..."

"Mm-hm. I mean, they do have flavouring, and the orange one that I tried was pretty good, but it did burn my tongue and throat so much that I had to immediately follow that with a healing potion," Ashton explained. "It's relatively easy to neutralise the acidity if you know what to do, I'll give you the instructions later. I'm sure there's someone at our camp that can use the slime for something. Anyway, what about the other items?"

"Uhm... Oh, right, so this place actually sells what seems like alcohol? I thought that might be good to know for your Drunken Bear. It also sells cigarettes," Clementine whispered the last word and tried to hide it by clearing her throat, moving on before Ashton could say something. He did hear what she said, though. "But! The most interesting thing is the newspaper that is sold here. It's called 'The Local Daily', with a subtitle 'Drakewood Single' that seems important... They're really expensive so we haven't bought one yet, but they seem to talk about things that happened in town, but they're all dated a few days back."

"Local Daily, huh? Well, they did have something similar in Lumia when the system came..." Ashton whispered, crossing his arms. "How expensive are they?"

"2500 Coins each."

"That's a bit steep, isn't it?" Ashton asked, turning to the puppet manning the kiosk. But it just stood there and did nothing, as usual. "What does it say on the front pages? Anything important?"

Clementine hesitated slightly, "Well, we can just see the front covers of two of them... one of them is from the day when you rescued us... It talks about the 'Frost Zone', but there's also something about zombies on there. And the other one is from while you were asleep, and I'm not sure if you were told already, but, uhm... the building you used to live in collapsed because of the wyvern."

"Huh. No wonder I'm still at the top of the ranking, I can make the front page even in my sleep," Ashton said with a smug grin, but then noticed Clementine's confusion. "Don't worry, I'll tell you later. Anyway, let me know the rest of the prices in a second. I'll have to save up a bit anyway, though. I barely have more than a thousand coins right now."

"... Seriously, how do you have that much money all the time?" Clementine sighed.

Ashton shrugged. "I'm one of the few people that can take out monsters as easily as yawning, so that's probably why."

"You know, your cocky side has gotten a lot stronger in the time we didn't speak."

"Hah, yeah..." Ashton replied. "So, what about those cigarettes you mentioned?"

Clementine's expression dropped. "... You heard that, huh?"

"Mm-hm."

"Okay, fine... There are a couple different ones. 'Dragon's Tongue', 'Druidic Bouquet', and 'Tundra Fox'. It looks like there's some loose tobacco as well, but uh, they don't really follow that same naming pattern?"

Ashton smiled. "That's fine, magical tobacco is usually known for its region of origin. How expensive are the packs?"

"1500 Coins each."

"Hm... That annoyingly sounds actually pretty reasonable. Alright, I'll come back later," Ashton clicked his tongue and turned away, walking back through the crowd.

"Are you really going to buy some? Didn't you say that you wanted to quit?" Clementine asked, rushing after him. And for some reason, even Perry seemed to agree.

"Right, smoking really isn't good for you. Get out of that habit as fast as you can."

Ashton stopped and let out a long sigh. "I get what you're saying, but these aren't regular cigarettes, and we have literal healing magic now. I could smoke a dozen packs one after the other, and then I'll take a swig or two of a healing potion right after and I'll be fine. Plus, with some of these cigarettes, I'm literally healing myself. Others help stabilise my mana as I'm casting spells, and others boost my physical ability," Ashton explained, leaning onto his staff. "And yes, am I a little addicted to nicotine? Probably, but I really don't care. Do you know how often I've been stabbed in the past week alone? Clementine, that's way more harmful than smoking."

"Well yeah, I get that, but–"

"But what?" Ashton interrupted her. "What do you want me to do? I've been smoking for more than a hundred years, and you want me to stop now?"

"Okay, there's that 'hundred year' thing again..." Perry pointed out, even if the question was quickly ignored.

"Mind giving us some privacy? I think there are a few things we should talk about privately," Ashton said, and Perry groaned loudly, shaking his head.

"No, I want to know what you guys are talking about. If you've got some weird shit going on, then–"

"Leave before I tell everyone that you're 'Crusher' in the community. Apparently you've been trying to hide it."

Perry opened his mouth to try and reply, before grumbling to himself. "How do you even—Fine. Just whatever. Was good to see you're still kickin'," Perry turned and patted Ashton's shoulder as he did.

Clementine seemed confused. "Wait, that guy was 'Crusher'? He seemed like a pretty big deal around here but he was a little weird..."

"He is, and he will only get weirder. But don't worry about him, he's harmless. Well, not harmless. Apparently it's just a thing now for him to try and attack me at random. But never in a way that would actually hurt me, I think he just wants to get one over on me," Ashton explained. "His sponsoring constellation probably told him to do that whenever he can. Anyway... here."

Ashton turned to his friend and held out a hand. Clementine sighed and grabbed it.

He enclosed her hand with both of his, and smiled. "I promise you that I'm okay. I know what I'm doing, I promise. I'm not the mess of a guy that you and Sam had to take care of all the time anymore, alright? I'm going to survive to the end of this entire thing, no matter what. I promise. Every step I'm taking is one toward my ultimate goal in this all."

"Your... ultimate goal?" Clementine repeated, not sure what exactly he meant. Ashton's smile grew wider for a moment as he squeezed his friend's hand.

"I'll tell you later. I can't, with so many of them watching." Ashton focused on the messages from the constellations. Sure, he figured that the three that were with him were on the better side, but there was only one of them that he truly trusted.

The Observer was acting a bit too weird with Millie for Ashton to completely trust them, and there was a lot about the Executor that was just worth looking out for. If they were really an incarnation of death like Ashton figured, then they were probably fairly neutral, but beings whose existence centred around death could really go either way. It wasn't clear what sort the Executor was yet.

The only one that Ashton had a certain trust with was the Daughter. If they were really that same demon that he knew, then he felt like the bond they built during those eighty-odd years was enough to last throughout the couple thousand years between then and now. Or rather, he hoped that this was the case.

Ashton wanted to find a good time to have this conversation with her. After all, it would be extremely weird if Ashton woke up and then suddenly asked for a private chat with the Daughter. He would do it later tonight when taking care of some other stuff, it was easier to make it seem more natural to the other constellations then.

"Anyway, you've bought some things, right?" Ashton asked, letting go of Clementine's hand as he got out his pocket watch. "You should head back before it gets too dark."

She nodded and sighed. "Sure. You're headed to the primary school, right?"

"Mm-hmm. I'm two days overdue for picking up Millie. I'll be staying the night there though, and then sometime tomorrow I'll probably bring her to Calibur with me and introduce her to everyone."

"About that..." Clementine started. "Are you sure you want to do this? I did visit the school and spoke to this Rory person, so apparently you guys talked it all through, but taking care of a kid isn't anything to take lightly."

"I know," Ashton said, focusing on a particular message from one of the constellations. "I have some experience taking care of a little brat, and she was much more of a handful than Millie is."

"You do? Did you, you know... have a kid there..?"

Ashton scoffed. "No, of course not. I was the only human in that entire world, so that was never anything to be too worried about."

"But you... did? You know, that?" Clementine asked, staring at Ashton with pure curiosity in her face before realizing what she had just said. "Wait, is that weird to ask?"

"You know about all my exes from Earth anyway, and I about yours. I don't think we're at a point in our friendship where we need to draw the line there," Ashton laughed as they walked over toward one of the other gates, the one closer to the primary school.

"Right..." Clementine nodded.

"Anyway... you know, of course I did that kind of stuff. Sometimes when I was travelling... It's easy to get lonely," Ashton explained, feeling a weird sense of nostalgia well up in him. But also, memories of the people that he had lost. He cleared his throat and continued. "Oh and there were a lot of summons, and in the times that the Summoner was sleeping, sometimes for weeks at a time, we were left on our own accord. We couldn't get too far away, but we could... mingle amongst ourselves."

"Wait, so what, was it like a big orgy or something?"

Ashton immediately shook his head. "Of course not. Listen, these people were my friends. My family. They made things... survivable there. It wasn't all just a physical thing, and not even really a romantic thing either. I mean, I don't even know if fae or demons or angels experience things like romance or attraction the same way in the first place. I'm pretty sure they don't. All I know is that we all could feel fear. And we could all feel comfort and love for each other. It's the only reason I'm not a babbling mess in front of you right now."
