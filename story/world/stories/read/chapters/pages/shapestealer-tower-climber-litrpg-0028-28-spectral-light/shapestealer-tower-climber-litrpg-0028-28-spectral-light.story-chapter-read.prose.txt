Though if that were the case, she couldn't yet explain to herself why they looked so different from Sophia.

But aside from that, there wasn't much else that the Shapestealer could find out from the scattered journals and papers in the area. For now, she decided to refocus her efforts on finding both Valor and Sophia.

Faint light from sporadic torches flickered against the walls, while chains hung from the walls here and there.

Most of the cells inside were either open, empty, or both. The few cells that weren't empty contained the corpses of pale humanoid creatures that looked eerily similar of the ghouls Maya had encountered back on the 6th floor of the tower.

Maya continued to go deeper into the prison. But she suddenly stopped as she felt an ominous presence looming above her. Immediately, she instinctively hovered aside as fast as she could.
