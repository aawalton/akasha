The morning brought fresh life into the dense forest. A gentle breeze stirred the leaves, creating a soft rustling sound. In the distance, the beautiful cry of a peacock could be heard; different birds chirped everywhere.

Orange sun rays entered through the thick canopy, scattering inside the forest from above. Each beam illuminated the dark environment, revealing huge tree trunks, low shrubs, and leaves stirring in the gentle wind. The ethereal glow brought the forest to life.

The morning breeze cleared the lingering fog, revealing dew-filled cobwebs that hung between branches like sparkling nets. Dust motes danced within these luminous rays of light. The air was cool, carrying the clean scent of soil, wild fruits, decaying leaves, and the faint smell of budding wildflowers.

While the forest started becoming active in the morning, an unknown voice reverberated inside the mother black cat’s new den below the humongous tree’s roots. “Hmm, so the little lad found a family… Great luck you have, lad… Fate always works in an unprecedented way, huh…”

“Miew… miew… miew… miew.”

The voice was filled with care, astonishment, and relief. But no one was nearby, not even in the vicinity of the huge tree. Only the adopted baby and the five kittens were in the small den. As soon as the voice reverberated in the den, all of them got startled and started mewling.

“Don’t worry, kids, I’ll not harm you.” The kittens and the adopted baby started mewling even louder. All of them started frantically pushing each other.

“Miiieeew…. miiieeew… miiieeew… miiieeew.”

“I can’t even make conversation with them… Huuh…” The voice stopped and never spoke again. The startled kittens and the adopted baby kept mewling nonstop.

—Near the Edge of the Forest—

In the dense forest, a blurry creature was moving swiftly through the shadows of the trees and shrubs. It was fast and agile. It was the black cat. She was running effortlessly through the dense undergrowth. Even while running fast, her paws barely disturbed the fallen leaves and small dried branches on the ground.

She ran with a singular purpose: to reach her new den deep within the roots of the big tree. This was her destination. After eating roasted meat from the little human, she was now returning to her new den.

She was extremely happy and excited. Last night, she had successfully transferred all her kittens to a safe place. What's more, she also had a permanent source of food, the little human girl.

Her belly was full after eating all the roasted meat that the little girl had given her this morning. She understood that as long as she went back to that place in the morning and evening, she would get delicious food.

But even with a permanent source of food, she knew she needed to become stronger to survive and protect her babies. Her brutal battle with the dogs had carved a new understanding into her mind. Survival demanded more than just getting food; it demanded strength, speed, and agility. This wasn't a reasoned thought. It was just an urge to ensure her own survival.

Suddenly, while running, she noticed something and stopped immediately. A faint but familiar, musky scent was in the air—that of a rat. It was close. Her hunting instinct rose; her prey was in the vicinity. But it was time to feed her kittens and the new member. She couldn't wander around for now. Her kittens needed feeding first. She would return to this area when she was hungry again. The rat would wait.

She moved swiftly towards the inner forest. Soon, the huge tree roots of her new den emerged in her vision. She stopped running and entered the dark opening below the two tree roots.

Her five tiny kittens and the white furball that she had adopted earlier were mewling frantically.

She noticed they were not hungry but were extremely scared. Instantly, she became alert. She went to the den's opening and looked around, but no one was there. This was extremely strange. She went back to her kittens and started nudging them with her snout and licking them to calm them down. After a while, all of them settled.

The kittens had grown in the last two weeks, and the eyes of every kitten were almost open. Only the new white member had fully shut eyes; it was the youngest member after all.

As soon as she lay down near them, her own kittens immediately dragged themselves to get the best spots for milk. The kittens started pushing and nudging each other with their tiny bodies. The white creature displayed the same frantic, blind instinct. It squirmed, jostling with its new siblings, desperately rooting for a source of milk.

Her own kittens, perhaps sensing its difference or simply driven by instinct, pushed the youngest one aside. With gentle nudges of her nose and soothing licks, the mother cat settled the restless ones and fed everyone. After a while, the mewls quieted into a contented sleeping sound.

The den was now filled with the rhythmic purring of sleeping kittens, and the black cat curled around her precious litter. She felt very sleepy after running from the forest to town and then from town to the forest.

—After a few hours—

After a few hours, she woke up. It was time to eat again; she was getting hungry. She moved out of her den and started running through the forest undergrowth. Her destination was the rat hideout she had found in the early morning.

When she reached her destination, she smelled the air, which led her back to the location where she had detected the rat’s scent earlier.

It wasn't just a smell of a fleeting trail; this was the scent of a rat's burrow. A network of tunnels must be nearby. She started sniffing here and there while walking as silently as possible.

Finally, she found a possible location. She could see a fallen dead tree from afar in the dense forest. The rats must be nearby. The area was filled with the musky odor of multiple rats. The fallen tree was very old; there were no leaves, and dried, thick, broken branches were everywhere. Mosses grew on the whole tree trunk.

This tree was surrounded by dense forest and couldn't be seen unless someone got closer. This was the perfect location for rats to live in.

There were many trees and dense undergrowth between the cat and the dead tree. It provided plenty of places to hide for her hunt.

The cat could smell the rats but couldn't see them. She started moving silently at a fast pace, but she was not running. She started hiding from one tree to another, then to a nearby shrub, then to a nearby tree. Bit by bit, she got closer to the fallen tree.

The black cat stopped near a tree. Her whiskers twitched slightly, and she could sense that her position was good. She didn’t need to change her location because of the downwind.

The downwind was bringing the rats' scent toward her, meaning the rats couldn’t smell her and run away. After calculating every aspect of her hunt, she started moving again. This time, the black cat lowered her body into a hunting position.

She moved while lowering her body, her belly was almost touching the ground, and her black fur was blending perfectly with the shadows of the forest. She was moving slowly and silently.

After getting a bit closer to the fallen tree trunk, she noticed that it had multiple holes. There were also a few holes on the ground just beside the fallen tree trunk.

Finally, after getting a bit closer, she also noticed several gray rats.

They were active. They were constantly moving in and out of their burrow and the tree trunk holes. They were bringing food.

There were two large rats that were bigger than normal. One was almost half the black cat’s size, and the other was slightly shorter. Their eyes were red, and their skin was almost black. The cat could sense that they might be stronger than most rats she had hunted previously. So she decided not to attack the big rats first.

The mother cat had never hunted rats this big before. Most peculiar were their red eyes.

One big rat was closer to the burrow, and another was further away near a tree. The whole rat family was sniffing at fallen berries, taking them from nearby shrubs, and bringing them to their burrows.

This was a jackpot for the black cat.

She was a bit far, and there were a few trees between her and her prey. To avoid alerting the rats, she didn't get closer. She waited patiently in her hunting position under the nearby undergrowth.

After a few minutes of waiting, she saw a small young rat come closer to her while looking at a nearby fallen berry. It was the perfect prey for her: small, vulnerable, and far from the bigger rats.

Suddenly, the black cat launched herself and sprinted forward. A few small rats noticed the cat and immediately started running away towards their burrow. Seeing the running rats, every small rat started running away.

A pandemonium erupted among the rat cubs. The mother rat noticed this and immediately looked around to see what was happening. Then she saw one of her children, who was farther away, running toward the burrow, getting caught by the head from above by the black cat.

The black cat captured the small rat by the head with its mouth and picked it up immediately. With a squeak, its hanging body stopped moving. It had been bitten on the neck with its head inside the cat’s mouth. The young rat went limp immediately.

There was no dramatic struggle, no prolonged chase, just the swift action of a predator securing its meal. The sound of a faint, choked squeak was barely audible.

The mother rat saw everything clearly and immediately attacked with a loud, screaming squeak. The father rat, farther away to the side, also saw everything.

Both of the rats’ eyes started glowing with a bright red light. Their vision fixed directly on the black cat. Both rat parents were in a berserk state and started attacking the cat head-on.

The black cat had never seen a pair of rats this big, and their eyes were the most unusual.

Both rats charged in a straight line with rapid movement. The mother rat charged from the front, while the father rat, who was farther away on the side, charged from its position. The mother rat was near the fallen tree, so she was much closer to the cat.

It didn’t take long for her to reach the cat. The rat was extremely fast, and the cat was not prepared for this. The moment the black cat noticed a pair of teeth about to reach her neck, she jumped up, still holding the dead rat in her mouth. But the dead rat was accidentally thrown out.

The mother rat’s charge was so fast and instantaneous that the black cat got startled and jumped. This saved her life.

Because of the sudden dodge, the rat passed below her and stopped a bit further away.

As soon as the cat landed on the ground, another pair of teeth was near her face from the side. The cat could see from the corner of her eye that something was reaching towards her eyes.

It was the father rat, who had just jumped to attack the cat’s eyes. The black cat immediately bent down and smashed its jaw hard on the ground due to the unexpected sudden movement, and the father rat passed above her.

Although the sudden slamming of her jaw saved her from losing her eye, it was still painful for the black cat.

The mother rat’s movements were perceivable, but the father rat was very quick. He was farther away than the mother rat during their initial attack, but he reached the cat almost at the same time.

All this happened within a few seconds, and the cat didn’t stand there to become a target. She instantly ran toward a tree in front. The father rat followed her in a frenzied state, and the mother rat ran toward her fallen dead cub.

The chase was not long, as the forest was dense and trees were everywhere. As soon as she reached the nearest tree, she started climbing without looking back. The father rat followed her up the tree. While this was happening, the mother rat was pushing its dead cub with its snout and squeaking very loudly.

The cat climbed the tall tree, reached the closest branch, and looked below. The father rat was also climbing. She was not expecting the rat to chase her up the tree. As soon as she saw the rat closer to her branch, she prepared to attack. The rat soon reached the branch where the cat was. Before the father rat could get onto the thick branch, the cat went closer to the branch’s base and scratched directly at its eyes, and the rat fell.

As soon as the father rat hit the ground, it squeaked loudly with full-blown shrieks. It was hurt. Its eyes were also bleeding. Seeing the father rat on the ground while squeaking, the mother rat became extremely enraged. It started running toward the tree while squeaking loudly, wanting to kill the cat instantly.

Earlier, during the initial attack, the black cat was saved by its pure reflexive movement. The black cat now understood that these rats were extremely strong. Both rats were as strong as the pack leader dog she had faced a few days ago. She knew she had bitten off more than she could chew and was in danger.

The mother rat started climbing the tree. The father rat, seeing its partner climbing, started following from the opposite side of the tree trunk. The Father rat learned its lesson not to charge blindly again. It deliberately climbed from the opposite direction to the mother rat to pincer the cat from both sides.

Both rats were climbing quickly, but the father rat was especially fast. It soon passed the mother rat, which was above him. Now, for the cat, the situation was critical. To escape, she either had to make a long jump to the other tree or be cornered on the tree branch.

As soon as the father rat reached near the branch, it stopped. It remembered the earlier attack by the cat from a higher position quite well.

Now the rat waited for a few steps below for its partner to reach the opposite side of the same tree trunk. When the father rat heard its partner’s sound from the other side, it climbed together with its partner and reached the thick tree branch from two sides.

They were about to climb the branch. The black cat turned and looked at the nearest tree. Inside the dense forest, there was no shortage of trees. She could easily figure out where to jump to escape. Then the cat lowered her body and, just like a spring, jumped to the nearest tree from high above the ground.

As soon as she jumped, the rats on the previous tree saw the jumping cat in shock. It was an unexpected development. The rat didn’t want to lose the cat. It wanted to take revenge.

With her sharp claws, the cat held the tree branch after jumping. After getting a good hold and standing on the branch, she looked back at the rats. One rat was nowhere to be seen, and the other was still on the previous branch, looking at her.

The rat, which was on the branch, seeing the cat further away, started descending the tree. The cat looked around to find the other one, but still couldn't.

The black cat knew that the rats were not going to leave her anytime soon, so she climbed higher and made another jump to the nearest tree. As soon as she landed on another tree’s branch, she climbed it to reach the upper branch to look for the disappeared rat from a higher position.

The rat, which couldn't be seen earlier, suddenly appeared on the branch from where she had jumped before. Then another rat appeared there soon. The rats started looking here and there for the cat. Then both noticed the cat in another tree.

From the new tree, the cat could now see both. She immediately understood that previously, one rat was consciously descending the tree from the opposite direction so that the cat couldn’t see it.

This was a hunting process, and it was not foreign to the cat. The cat does this every morning and evening. She understands the logic behind hiding and then attacking when the prey is unprepared.

Now that both rats were in her vision, she knew what she had to do to escape.

She waited for them to climb the current tree. After a minute, both rats started climbing the cat’s current tree again. But this time, the cat deliberately waited for them to come closer.

When the rats almost reached her, the cat jumped again to the nearest tree, then she descended the tree a bit, and then jumped to the ground from a significant height. She landed without injury and ran away.

The rats were not fast enough to climb and descend again and again without tiring. Multiple high jumps after a bit of rest were not hard.

On the last tree, the rats were visibly tired, which is why the cat waited to jump to another tree and then descend to the ground. This time, the rats couldn't follow her fast enough. She then ran away for good.

The hunt was unsuccessful, but at least she saved her life. She decided never to come to this place again. The rats were too unusual for her.

The black cat had never seen an evolved animal before. It was her first time encountering such animals.

While this was happening, someone was looking at the black cat from afar. This being was very far away, most probably a few miles away. But the being could clearly see the black cat even through the thick canopy.

“Struggle, young lass, struggle... You need to struggle a lot... Struggle so much that you evolve sooner…”
