Ashton crossed his arms, satisfied, as he looked at the selection of pots in front of him. There were a dozen of different sizes, shapes, and intended uses. The largest reached up to around his hip and was so large that Ashton wouldn't be able to close his arms around it. The patterns engraved on the outside looked complex, but were actually quite simple from his point of view.

Ashton was probably one of the few people that could make use of these things properly at this point. You had to have a decent understanding of mana manipulation to be able to change the 'setting' of these. Plus, they needed some sort of mana source to be able to function properly.

Right now, that could be done by slotting in some magic stones into the side of the clay pot, but with the right spirit calling these pots its home, that would change.

And so, Ashton placed his hand over the contract circle in front of him and started the chant to actually call on it.

There were a few different options for what Ashton could form a contract with here. The best contract for something with plant-nurturing abilities was a celestial contract, but Ashton did not have what he needed to form those yet, sadly.

Celestials were very finnicky to form a contract with, not only in regard to the offering they required, but also because they were often quite stuck-up and often had very specific conditions. Elementals and spirits were far more flexible in that regard. Even demons were, really; as long as you made offerings of blood, flesh, or life, then they usually didn't quite care the form it came in.

Well... as long as you had the right contract. The Spawn of Akrazoth had the upper hand in the contract, and could make whatever ridiculous demands they wanted. At least Celestials were still better than that.

But there was a close second to that celestial gardener, especially once it was raised up beyond lowest grade.

The Root Mouse. Not only was it able to protect plants, speed up their growth, and make them grow as large and healthily as possible, even strengthening the plants' innate magical qualities... it was also ridiculously cute.

And it wasn't like Ashton's other summons were cute, either, but the lowest-grade, young Root Mouse was truly an adorable little thing barely larger than a finger, and definitely smaller than two.

Plus, it was actually quite easy to summon. The offerings it asked for were just some vegetables to eat. For now, at least. Offerings became a bit more complex at higher grades, but the important part was really just feeding it things with high amounts of mana in it.

That was why it took a while until Ashton could consider summoning this one. Well, another reason was his contract capacity, though the quests did an absolutely wonderful job increasing this to a point where Ashton could even form a few more low-grade contracts. But the main reason was that Ashton needed a root vegetable with some solid amount of mana in it, and that was pretty easy to get your hand on these days. Even already half-grown vegetables could take on loads of mana and a massive growth boost.

As Ashton advised them to, some people had gone out and gathered things like potatoes and carrots that naturally seemed to take on more mana than others. In a town like this, there were plenty of people that had their own small vegetable gardens, so it was pretty easy. So, borrowing some of those before there were planted in the garden downstairs was quite easy. And after this, the Root Mouse would be able to grow its own food.

Ashton would reserve one of the pots for that; to let the Root Mouse prepare its own food, ever-increasing in quality and mana. Some of those potatoes could also be shared with the others to act as new seedlings that would grow faster and larger than before.

[[You have formed a contract with a [Root Mouse]!]

[[Root Mouse] is being registered in the [Spirit Summoning] Category]

Ashton smiled lightly as he saw the fist-sized potato in front of him pulsate. A small mouse slowly ate its way out from the inside. It mostly just appeared like any normal mouse, just that this one was overgrown with moss on its back. Even without being able to see anything like its colour, Ashton could tell that this was a truly adorable little thing.

"Hey there, little Root Mouse," Ashton said, as the tiny creature devoured the potato multiple times its own size. Maybe even faster than Ashton would be able to eat it, even.

The mouse looked up at Ashton, tilting its head to the side slightly as Ashton quickly picked it up and placed it onto one of the pots. Ashton channelled the spirit, quickly designating all the pots as the Root Mouse's 'garden'.

Of course, it would only be able to take care of these while Ashton was in the area, but it wasn't like the Root Mouse needed to be there all the time anyway. At night, while Ashton was resting, would be more than enough time for it to do its job.

The Root Mouse jumped into one of the pots, digging into the soil that was already inside of it. It soon came back out; but not in the same pot that Ashton had placed it in originally.

It was just hopping around, magically jumping from pot to pot, like they were all interconnected. Well, they were, now, through the Root Mouse's magic. It should be able to hop into any soil within Ashton's channelling range, as long as it was part of the spirit's garden.

"Perfect," Ashton stretched a little as he stood up and walked back into the tent. He had already sorted out exactly which plants he wanted to grow. Different plants that Ashton had already been using to make potions. Most of these weren't actually native to earth, but instead had just popped up here and there across town. Especially the botanical gardens had a ton that Ashton had been using. But those plants hadn't been progressing at the speed or in the direction that he wanted.

Like this, by planting their seeds in these special pots, Ashton would be able to maximise exactly all the qualities that he wanted. Plus, with the Root Mouse, he should be able to get an amount that would truly let Ashton mass-produce some of his potions.

Some of those would be spread around the survivors... but Ashton would sell most of them to the Salesman. High-quality potions weren't exactly cheap, after all, and Ashton had a century of experience making potions using numerous powerful and rare ingredients. And while there was a lot that Ashton couldn't do on his own, simply because unique magics were required to create certain effects or changes, there were few individuals that could make potions quite as well as Ashton.

Yes, obviously, on the scale of the multiverse, Ashton was just a small blip. Huge corporations have formed that specialised in making potions more powerful than Ashton could ever hope to create no matter how long he lived. There were even constellations that ascended simply due to the power and influence of the potions they made.

But even so, the potions Ashton could make would still be worth quite a bit. Potions were luxury goods, at the end of the day. Even if the level he could make could be found elsewhere much more easily than through a Salesman.

But Ashton did figure that there were those that preferred dealing with spirits rather than the corporations connected to the Interdimensional Council, even if it came at an upcharge.

Either way, this would speed up Ashton's money-making endeavours quite a bit, once the first batch of plants was done.

Ashton planted all the things that he needed in the pots. For some, he had to gather seeds first, for others, he could just put in the whole plant.

Using the Dryad, Ashton made sure that the seeds germinated and that everything had set root in the soil. And then the small mouse spirit moved around and used its magic on all those plants.

Ashton continued using the Dryad's magic on the pots that entire time. Not to do anything in particular, but rather, to provide some nature magic to the Root Mouse to take advantage of. And Ashton could feel it sucking up some of that magic, redirecting it toward the plants in its own way. And with the mouse as a 'filter', the plants would be able to grow faster without withering away soon after. Not even close to as fast as when Ashton just used the dryad's magic normally, of course, but at this pace, if Ashton gave them another hour of this, they should start a growth cycle that led the plants to be fully grown and harvestable by tonight.

And those plants would be of a better quality than the ones that were planted. Ashton would then harvest them and re-introduce some nutrients to the soil, grab some seeds or sprouts that were needed to grow more, and then use the higher-quality plants for the next batch in the evening.

That way, though there was of course a limit, Ashton should have ingredients that would let him make better potions basically every night. As if the plants were levelling up as well. Plus, it was great experience for the dryad and root mouse in the first place, so levelling them up would be an absolute breeze.

As Ashton focused on the dozen pots in front of him, he just couldn't help but feel excited, since the future where that staff his old friends prepared was his was inching closer and closer.
