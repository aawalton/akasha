“This much power…” Pride whispered as she raised her hands, opening and closing her gauntlets as if measuring the strength on her very fingers.

“With this… I could show those assholes…! I could show them all…!” She continued, her frowning expression and flushed cheeks making her anger clear.

But who was that fury directed against? Ascalon wondered, with Pride’s overwhelming presence crashing against him like a tempestuous gale, his sole eye narrowing as he quickly inspected his network interface.

This effect cannot be blocked or resisted, and can only be lifted by Pride herself.

Can’t be used alongside - Pride’s Playground -

Just as Ascalon feared, Pride had used a new skill, though this one had a different—albeit quite related—effect.

“I see…” Ascalon muttered, his posture shifting as he crouched slightly, anchoring himself to the ground and placing his shield in front of his chest. He then raised his spear, aligning its pointy end toward Pride, bracing himself for the fight.

Pride seemed to notice the change in his demeanor and smirked, lifting her fists to her chin. “I’ll make you regret mocking me!” She exclaimed in irritation, her teeth grating against each other.

In the very next moment, she leaned forward and disappeared from view, causing the spectators in the back to gasp in surprise. Ascalon, on the other hand, tracked Pride’s uncanny speed with ease, seeing her dart toward him in a straight line—so fast it seemed reality itself struggled to keep up with her.

The ground split and cracked in her wake, while Ascalon tensed up his muscles and tightened the grip on his shield. He watched her movements carefully, noticing how her right arm pulled back for a split second, before being brought forward in a devastating straight punch.

The impact echoed with a clank as the gauntlet struck the shield. While he had blocked as usual, he immediately felt the difference between her prior and current strength—this was way beyond her previous attacks.

The force propelled him backward, dragging him across the ground. A violent shockwave expanded around them, causing the plateau to shake uncontrollably, rocks and debris flying in all directions.

Ascalon’s friends ducked low, pretty much throwing themselves to the ground, barely avoiding being flown away by the force of the clash.

Undeterred, the knight stopped his momentum with a forceful stomp, his right boot sinking slightly into the rocky terrain. In one fluid, spring-like movement, he propelled himself toward Pride. His lance gleamed under the light as it pulled back and thrust forward in a single, seamless motion—a thunderous, piercing blow unleashed directly at his opponent.

Pride seemed surprised for a moment, but quickly raised her arms in a cross guard, blocking the tip of the spear with both gauntlets. Although she managed to defend against Ascalon’s spear, the hit generated a shockwave of its own, causing Pride to be sent flying backward across the plateau.

She twisted her body in mid-air, trying to regain control. “Hmmph!” She exhaled sharply as she catapulted her right arm back, the gauntlets humming to life as the clicks and whirs started to be heard again. Then, she struck toward the direction she was flying to, aiming slightly at the ground.

The power of the strike halted her momentum cleanly, her frame quickly returning to the ground. Without catching her breath, she sprang back into action, her body becoming a reddish blur as she lunged toward Ascalon.

The whirring and clicking intensified, her gauntlets brimming with a red glow as dense clouds of steam hissed and expelled from their mechanisms. This time, however, the fumes expanded through the plateau, shrouding the battlefield and reducing the visibility for the ones watching from afar.

Yet for both Ascalon and Pride it made no difference, as they could clearly sense each other’s presence amidst the dense steam—their clash was all but inevitable.

Taking a more proactive approach, Ascalon dashed forward and matched Pride’s pace, aiming to intercept her midway. In a heartbeat, they sized each other up.

Pride pulling both arms back in preparation for a powerful attack, the mechanisms on her gauntlets roaring loudly and vibrating with a grating hum.

Ascalon, undaunted by the crimson glow of her armaments, raised his shield as he charged with unrelenting bravery, his stride quickening as eagerness fueled his resolve.

As soon as they were in each other’s range, Pride whipped both her fists forward in a ferocious two-handed slam. Her entire upper torso arched forward under the strain, the raw momentum behind her offensive causing both the ground and the air to ripple in her wake.

On the other side, Ascalon braced himself, pressing the shield tightly against his chest. With unwavering determination, he pushed forward, launching a charging bash that carried the weight of his entire existence behind it, his strength amplifying the force with every step.

The two attacks collided in a cataclysmic clash, the plateau beneath them shaking, cracking, and splintering as shockwaves expanded violently around them. The air crackled with energy as Pride’s dual punch met Ascalon’s shield, the sheer force of their encounter sending a brutal reverberation through the area.

Both of them gritted their teeth as they locked in a power struggle, pushing against each other and refusing to relent. Sweat rolled down their faces as they fought for supremacy, each feeling the strain of their respective force crashing against the other.

The rocks beneath them groaned, further cracking and shifting under the weight of their grappling—but both endured and pushed on, and on, and countless minutes passed as they struggled.

With time, Pride’s breath began to quicken, her gauntlets glowing ever brighter, while Ascalon’s grip on his shield tightened more and more, resolve burning fiercely in his sole eye.

The stalemate was broken by their very armaments, as a loud crack echoed across the mountain, emanating from Ascalon’s shield.

“Eh?” A gasp escaped his lips, his attention focusing on the defensive equipment. Right in the middle, where both of Pride’s fists had connected, a thin vertical line was gradually forming—the mighty Aegis had been overpowered.

While Ascalon himself had the strength to carry on for as long as needed, it seemed that his shield was reaching the end of its lifespan. Memories of countless battles flooded his mind for a split second.

Clashes against minotaurs’ huge axes, giants’ clubs guided by brute strength—even a certain demon’s massive, raging fireball had been thrown against his very shield. And yet, it was now beginning to falter… perhaps, Ascalon had pushed it way past his limits.

When was the first time he had wielded it, anyway? Aegis, the legendary shield of Ascalon Rendland—that’s how people would call it. Yet he knew it wasn’t as special, just a piece of equipment he had picked up somewhere, made by a nameless blacksmith.

Still, Ascalon had grown alongside both Aegis and Exemplaris, forming a bond that would make no sense to most people, as they were simply armaments meant for fighting. But under his skilled use, the shield had grown to be an impervious fortress.

“I am sorry…” Ascalon muttered, his voice carrying a saddened tune. Pride twitched at his words, her relenting pushback faltering ever so slightly—in confusion for the sudden apology and whoever it was directed at—and Ascalon didn’t let the chance slip by.

With a quick, regretful motion, he let go of his shield and sidestepped with all his might, getting out of Pride’s way. With him and his opposing force suddenly gone, Pride stumbled forward with a perplexed expression on her face. Her legs lifted slightly off the ground as the combined weight of her gauntlets and the momentum of her attack dragged her forward.

At the sight, Ascalon twisted his body, rotating to the left as his right arm pulled the lance forward, unleashing a quick, piercing attack aimed at Pride’s exposed left side. The hit struck the helpless ogre with extreme force and precision—but, to Ascalon’s surprise, a metallic, dull ‘clank’ rang out instead of the familiar sound of flesh being torn.

“Agh!” Pride groaned as her body was hurled through the air under the force of the impact, her frame rolling across the plateau for a few moments. Ascalon narrowed his eye and focused on Pride’s left side of her stomach, where her skin looked grayish instead of red!

“That was close, you asshole!” Pride screamed at him in a mix of anger and frustration, her figure slowly rising up from the floor. She patted her side, where the lance had struck, and stuck out her tongue at Ascalon.

“You thought I was wide open, huh? Dumbass!” She exclaimed in triumph, a bold smirk spreading across her face. Ascalon let out a thoughtful ‘hmm’ as a few theories popped up in his mind.

But, thankfully, a familiar voice cut through the air to aid him in his time of confusion!

“Steel skin! It’s an ogre skill, I think!” Lilithra explained from a distance, causing Ascalon to glance in her direction. She looked nervous, anxious, as if she were agitated by something—perhaps she was fighting against her ‘pride’ and a desire to join in the fight.

“I see… Thank you, Lilithra.” Ascalon replied in a gentle voice, his doubts now clearing up. So Pride had an ogre skill to increase her defenses, just like the abilities of the ogres he used to know—although this was the first time he had seen ‘steel’ rather than ‘rock’ skin.

He focused again on Pride, and was momentarily taken aback by the view. Pride was looking at Lilithra with a flushed face and a deep frown, her gauntlets emitting soft metallic sounds as her fists trembled.

“Even under my influence… you still… ugh!” Pride groaned to herself, clearly affected by Lilithra’s actions. Was she angry because Lilithra had told him about her skill? Whatever the case, Ascalon had other worries in his mind.

His left hand felt strangely lonely… his eye catching sight of an old partner. Aegis lay on the floor, broken in half, its solemn, weathered appearance contrasting greatly with its usual stout, unwavering surface.

“You have served me well, Aegis…” Ascalon whispered with a soft voice, a sad smile spreading across his cheeks.

“W—why are you… smiling…?!” Pride’s voice reached his ears, prompting Ascalon to snap back to attention. Unintentionally, he seemed to have angered her even further!

Just then, he felt an ominous shift in the air, his eye now glued to Pride’s movements. Pride lifted her right arm to the side of her head, then pulled it back, twisting her entire torso with the motion. The whirring of her gauntlets grew louder, while the wind in the area suddenly changed direction, growing violent.

Ascalon stood his ground as he felt a powerful force pulling on him—the air rushing past him and toward Pride, a visible gale beginning to fiercely spin around her right gauntlet. The wind howled around her, intensifying in loudness and size, as if a tempest were descending upon the plateau.

As her armament powered up, the crimson glow cut through the violent winds, urging Ascalon into action. He burst forth in a dash, darting in a straight line toward Pride and bending low as he grabbed Exemplaris with both hands. His charge was met with tempestuous winds, but he made sure to stomp harder with every step, gaining an unrelenting momentum.

The tip of his spear started to gradually slice through, like a projectile defying the very storm, and his march quickened until his entire frame became a streak of yellow cutting through the plateau.

The entire area shook under the weight of both his advance and Pride’s powering-up, with the few spectators simply watching in stunned silence.

Just as Ascalon lunged forward into a piercing strike, Pride twisted her body to the left, unleashing all her pent-up strength in a charged-up punch. The massive force behind both attacks created an immediate vacuum, the resulting shockwave so powerful it hurled both of them back before their strikes could even meet!

The entire mountain cracked in half as the force expanded, a crater ripping through the plateau in all directions, decimating the whole upper-end in a heartbeat. Lilithra and the rest were pushed back by the strong, explosive pressure, saving themselves from plummeting into the depths by a mere stroke of luck as the peaks collapsed!

Both Ascalon and Pride had been thrown off the mountain by the force of their own attacks, their figures now nowhere to be seen.
