The air in the forest was thick with the scent of pine, sunlight filtering in dappled patterns through the canopy. Seth crept out from behind a large oak, Nightmare invisible by his side. Colossus remained inside the teardrop necklace; his bulk wasn’t exactly suited for stealthy approaches.

Ahead, in a small clearing, a Thorn-Legged Mantis was cleaning off the blood from its last kill. The beast was an unsettling sight—roughly the size of a grown man, its slender, emerald-green body was a collection of sharp angles and chitinous plates. Its triangular head swiveled on a surprisingly flexible neck, multifaceted eyes glinting with predatory awareness.

Two powerful forelimbs, serrated like wicked saws, were held ready, while its other four legs dug into the ground each time it moved. An almost imperceptible shimmer of aether clung to it, and Nightmare’s Danger Sense tingled.

It’s at least Mid-Iron, Seth guessed.

'Alright,' he said through Link to the others. 'If it’s Mid-Iron, I’ll let you two take it alone. If it’s High-Iron, I’ll jump in.'

'Perfect. Let’s go, Small Bite!' Nightmare’s eager thought was immediate, and the direwolf blurred forward.

Simultaneously, Colossus sprang from Seth’s necklace, materializing with a thud in the center of the small clearing. The sudden appearance of the massive, three-tailed scorpion startled the mantis, its head snapping toward the new threat, while its forelimbs rose defensively. Seth, still concealed, cast Intermediate Identify.

The synergy was evident. With the mantis forced to focus on the large scorpion, Nightmare had an easier time. He danced around the enraged insect, his fangs tearing at its joints and less-armored sections while the dark smoke billowing from his maw sizzled on the mantis’ green carapace.

Colossus, for his part, absorbed the creature's furious assault. Quartz Armor was taking a beating, chips and cracks appearing with every strike, but the scorpion stood his ground, his own claws snapping out and trying to pin the insect.

Occasionally, a shadowy wolf would pounce and turn into thorny tendrils, further hindering the mantis’ movements, which gave Colossus a brief respite and Nightmare more time to dodge once Chitinous Taunt’s duration was over.

Seth watched, quite impressed. They were both working extremely well together, covering each other’s weaknesses. Colossus was the unyielding shield, Nightmare the piercing sword. Seth estimated in less than a minute they'd have the mantis down.

Suddenly, Seth’s Feral Instinct core screamed, and Nightmare’s Danger Sense erupted through Link as well.

'Small Bite look out!' the direwolf shouted at the same time, his voice filled with urgency.

But before Seth could even react or pinpoint the direction of the threat, three shimmering blades of wind sliced through the air from their right. They struck Colossus and cut through his remaining Quartz Armor in three distinct, bloody gashes along his side.

The scorpion shrieked and stumbled to the side, Desert Aegis flickering around his body.

"COLOSSUS!" Seth roared and burst from behind his tree, sprinting toward his wounded companion. Out of the corner of his vision, he caught a fleeting glimpse of something—a majestic flicker deep within the trees.

A large bird perched a dozen feet high on a thick branch overlooking the clearing—its feathers shimmered in shades of forest green and golden yellow, catching the light while its eyes, like polished amber, glinted through the leaves.

Seth threw himself between Colossus and the threat, Iron Fists manifesting around his combat gauntlets, followed by the azure shimmer of his Protecting Belt. 'Colossus, stay down!' he mentally yelled as he cast Intermediate Identify on the newcomer.
