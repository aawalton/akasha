Lilithra opened her eyes as she slowly regained consciousness. Her mind was hazy, disoriented, and it took her a couple of minutes to actually regain her senses.

“Hmm…? what… what happened…?” She asked herself, confused.

She felt like she had awoken from a long, long dream. And, for some reason, a dreadful feeling lingered in the back of her head.

She looked around, and her eyes widened. Her heart started racing as she caught sight of her surroundings.

“What the…” she exclaimed in pure disbelief.

A hellscape.

Dancing flames. Brimstone. A reddened sky. Ashes scattered in the wind.

All around her, complete and utter destruction.

Leveled buildings, burnt trees, and cracked streets—things she could recognize, in nightmarish forms.

She started coughing, her lungs burning up with the hot air.

“G—guys?!” She screamed in pain, clutching both hands to her chest, scared and panicked.

Where was everyone?

Where was she?

What the hell was going on?

A fitting word, as she could only describe the place as hell.

Her pendant stirred—rejoiced.

A journey of her own was about to begin.

No one had foreseen such an outcome—not even the one behind it all.

Everywhere else, things had begun to spin into motion.

Everyone had their own path to follow.

Athanya’el sat in a throne of gold and light, high above the world, visions of dear fellows glitching in and out of existence before her. She clutched her hands in prayer for them.

Raphael, soaring through the sky over a flooded city, his radiant wings casting a holy shine on his ruthless revolvers. Wicked creatures chanted his name from the depths, but his flight did not waver in the slightest.

Selorien, leading a girl by the hand through the dark shroud of the forest. Awful shrieks followed in their wake—abominations racing madly in pursuit. A crescent scythe glimmered close to Selorien—in the hands of an unusual, but formidable ally.

Lyra, casting her most powerful spells onto a weakened child, his breath steadying as its wounds closed. Immediately, she moved onto another wounded—she had no time to rest, even as tears rolled down her cheeks. At her side stood her brother, his new legs shaking as he struggled to get accustomed to them. But this time, he was going to protect her.

In the sealed island, the swords of light had begun to crack. But The Order remained steadfast in their mission. A winged valkyrie had descended to assist them—fear had no place in their hearts.

And Ascalon, walking through an abandoned tunnel, illuminated only by his presence. His steps were resolute, and his face was the very image of serious, unwavering resolve. Close behind, bound to him by silver chains, followed Lilithra.

Or rather, whoever it was that dwelled in her body.

“Come on, I’m so tired of walking~ let’s take a break, love!” she spoke with her usual tone of voice, but her mannerisms and even her choice of words was amiss.

Ascalon ignored her plea and kept going, his eyes set on the path ahead. ‘Lilithra’ smiled, a soft giggle escaping her lips as her sight remained glued to the holy knight.

“I won’t let her out…” She whispered.

“Not until you accept the deal~” she continued, laughing in a most charming manner.

Ascalon stopped for a moment, turning his head back to her.

‘Lilithra’ stepped ahead, trying to snuggle up to him, but Ascalon pushed her back, gently. While she was not her, it was still her body.

“Keep your forked tongue behind your teeth, demon.” Ascalon declared in a stern voice.

The girl stuck out her tongue at him, smiling playfully, clearly unbothered by his words.

Ascalon sighed as he resumed his walk. The corridor seemed endless.

The darkness was being pushed away by his holiness, but it tried its best to fight back.

The girl’s humming and his own footsteps echoed around them.

“Oh, Ascalon.” Athanya’el lamented as the vision dimmed out of existence.

She felt helpless, unable to help him. Rules had been set up again.

“Be brave, my knight.” She said as she clutched her hands tight.

Dangers, trials, new horizons—beings beyond his imagination awaited at the end of the path.

But also the key to regaining her.

The only thing that could guarantee bringing Lilithra back.

Ascalon had to remain focused, and hopeful.

His light would shine bright once he crossed the tunnel—once he stepped through the boundary.

Into another world.
