Purging while healing himself tested Thomas in every way.

He was wracked with spiritual and physical pain from having his mana forcefully converted into something his spirit wholly rejected. At the same time, his body was literally rotting from the inside out, all thanks to Corruption mana.

A good part of him just wanted to lay down and die. At least it would be over.

As corny as it was, hearing his friends' voices kept him from giving in. Jo and Zach were counting on him and would be gutted if he died. That helped remind him that his nephews were waiting for him back on Aether, too. Thomas still had to make things right between himself and Derek, and make sure his eldest brother, Pete, really got the message to get into a dungeon before it was too late. He couldn't let his parents bury one of their own sons.

Thomas wanted to live.

So he fought on, and the Adaptation mana really came in clutch.

Corruption tore through his Healing, Light, and Wellspring like fire through a tissue paper factory, but for whatever reason, his Adaptation mana was a lot harder to burn.

Luckily, Thomas had used a lot of high-level Adaptation to build his most recent layer. He'd have to send Sateryn a gift basket.

Thomas wasn't quite sure how, but he was able to use the Adaptation to harden his core against the intrusion of the Corruption. Some still got in, bringing a whole new level of pain as it dug deeper through the layers of his core—which he was starting to think were layers of himself—but the Adaptation bought him time.

When the corruption blade was unceremoniously removed, the reservoir was gone, and Thomas was finally able to fight back. He initiated the Purge, focusing on Corruption as the main toxin he wanted kicked out.

By then, his core was a ruin, and so was his body. Most of his Healing and Wellspring were gone, but most wasn't all, and he had made sure to take in Level 5 healing mana to complete his last level. His Wellspring had always been some nebulous level above that, and he'd been sipping off that crystal since the very beginning.

Both fueled his overall healing and Living Architecture skill to rebuild what had been destroyed.

He squeezed his poor, abused core for every last mote of Healing, Wellspring, and even Light to fuel the process. Adaptation helped him do the impossible and hold off the last bit of Corruption he was still trying to purge out.

Even with all that, what he had left wasn't going to be enough to put himself back together.

Again, Thomas had to battle through the urge to just give up and die already. Heavy malaise weighed him down, most of his organs weren't working right, and his own thoughts were disordered.

Somehow, he climbed back onto his feet. Through eyes blurred from literally crying out toxins, he looked around. The purge had torn him down to his absolute base self, and he still hadn't built himself back up yet.

His base self wanted loot.

He located a drop near a body. A Healing mana crystal lay beside it. Thomas snatched it up and absorbed it.

Only when the first burst of power tore through him did Thomas realize that had been Laveris, and he was way above level five.

So was the crystal.

The next few moments were akin to what he imagined riding a bucking bronco was like. The Healing mana was in control; all he could do was hang on and maybe direct some of it through his Living Architecture to rebuild as well as heal.

The last of the Corruption mana evaporated under the force of blinding power.

Oddly, the motes of high-powered healing failed to interact with his core at all. That was weird.

Thomas inhaled a clean breath of air, straightened, and looked down at himself. He was a mess, but he was alive.

Bracing himself for bad news, he looked inward to his core.

It was worse than he imagined.

If his core were a building, it would look like a depiction of a decayed ruin skyscrapers from that old show, Life After People. The top two layers were vague scaffoldings of Adaptation, with desultory motes of Healing, Wellspring, and Light clustered here and there. It was marginally better the deeper he looked into his core, except it was still riddled with holes.

The whole thing felt magically fragile, like a stiff breeze could blow him apart.

None of the new Healing motes were sticking to his core. They just hovered halfheartedly around the outside. There was no magical weight remaining to pull them in.

Then, right in the center of it all, sat a sphere of Corruption mana encased by a seamless layer of Adaptation. It was trapped like a grain of sand inside a pearl.

Only this wasn't a pearl. There was Corruption in his goddamn spirit. That couldn't be good.

On the other hand... it wasn't doing any active harm. The Adaptation had completely cut off the Corruption from the rest of his core.

Thomas certainly wasn't going to mess with it. He'd have to bring this to Akilah and see what he could do. Maybe a full core rescission to reset back to zero? Assuming that was even possible at this point.

He'd had enough navel-gazing.

Thomas looked around again. He was the only living being in this room. No surprise: Corruption mana made the purging process ten times worse, and anyone with a sense of smell had gotten out so fast they hadn't stopped to pick up the loot.

However, someone had left behind a bucket filled with water, hand soap, and towels from the facilities' supply. He imagined Zach had flash-teleported it in.

He felt a lot better after cleaning himself of the filth and started to look around for his backpack. Weirdly, it was lying against one wall along with his Mutable shield, though he had no idea how either had gotten there.

He'd packed spare clothes in his pack, but while his pants fit just fine, his shirt was a problem. It was now at least two sizes too small.

"Uh..." Thomas looked from his shirt to his arms. His shoulders and biceps were thick with a lot more muscle than he was used to seeing. Even his forearms had some new bulk.

The Purge had melted away body fat but hadn't done the same for the muscle he'd built up thanks to his Wellspring skill. He was still no Schwarzenegger in his prime, or even the Rock in a non-Disney movie, but damn this was an improvement. His stomach was washboard flat, and he had real arms and shoulders that turned his former basic T-shirt into a compression top.

Thomas started doing an inventory. Every familiar scar was gone, down to a jagged one across his knee he'd gotten skateboarding as a kid. His thighs weren't bulked out like his arms, but every muscle in his legs were as tough and firm as if he spent his weekends biking straight up hills. His face felt the same as it always had, though he had no mirror, and there was nothing reflective in this room. He'd probably gotten the same refresh as Jo, though.

It was amazing but also unsettling.

He also chucked that worry to the side, along with his shirt that no longer fit, and focused on what he'd been itching to do since he woke up: collect the loot.

He wasn't sure why the others hadn't done it already, though after a glance at the biohazardous mess he'd left behind, he figured he had his answer. After all, it wasn't like any of the loot was going anywhere.

First, he went to Frederic's body. The guy looked like he was in an advanced state of decomposition, as if he'd been dead well before Jo had stabbed him.

Corruption mana was fantastic stuff to have in one's body. He was so excited about having his own.

Anyway, Frederic had dropped level two Corruption and Light crystals as well as a skill shard.

Huh. Corruption and Light? Going by his own experience, he had no idea how the two would be able to coexist in a core. Also, he'd only been a level two.

Frederic, you could have done so much better, Thomas thought and bent to pick up the skill shard. Chances were good that was the remainder of his Gift.

The Purge had cleared out all the gunk in his System, leaving his limbs working smoother and quicker than he was used to. As a result, his grab was a little sloppy, and the back of his knuckles brushed the Corruption crystal anyway.

Thomas winced back, expecting a bad zing. Except it didn't come.

Frowning, he picked up the Corruption mana crystal. Before, he couldn't stand the touch of someone who had it in their System. Now, the mana felt completely neutral to him. Thankfully, it wasn't something that felt good, but he wasn't instantly repelled.

Was it because he now hosted a pocket of the stuff in the center of his core? Or had he been forever tainted?

Either way, he didn't want more of it. Thomas tossed it to the side, then bent to scoop up the Light mana and the skill shard to take a closer look.

Ultimate Technique: To Bend Shade To One's Will

Nothing about that immediately spoke of possession, but the word "bend" might be doing a lot of heavy lifting. Thomas had certainly become good at stretching his abilities.

Next, he stepped to Courtney's body.

His memories were fuzzy, but he was pretty sure Twitch had killed her. If so, he felt sorry it had fallen to him and even sorrier for Courtney, who just couldn't be talked out of going to the dark side. First Tad Woolworth, and now the Light Elves.

She'd dropped level three Air, Electric, and Water mana crystals, as well as two shards. One had to be her Gift, and one was a shard she'd likely already had.

Ever Elastic PhysiqueThe Earth Receives All Blows

That was... a lot.

Ever Elastic Physique was probably derived from her Gift. While titles could not entirely be trusted, Thomas was glad to see that this shard specified 'Physique'. He could easily see how a Gift of Rubber might lead to malleable opinions. Or morals. Courtney certainly had no problem switching sides back and forth.

The second was for... tanking hits? With Earth mana? Then again Water didn't compress, either. She had been injured a few times but not out for the count. At least until the end. Maybe that had been her secret.

He stood over her corpse for a moment, wondering if he should say something profound or, like, haul back and spit considering what she had put him through.

Instead, he just turned away and went to the elves.

There wasn't much left of Raveth but ashes and elf hair, which apparently didn't burn.

He had dropped Light, Corruption, Healing, and Wellspring, as well as a couple of others he didn't recognize on sight, but they glowed like they were within the same family. Just from the feel, they were all above level 5.

Oh yeah, also four skill shards.

Holy. Shit.

With a giddy sound that would have been embarrassing for anyone else to hear, Thomas snatched up the new mana types first.

His System labeled them as: Harmony and Communication.

Harmony? Did that mean harmony between his own mana types? Because Thomas felt no inner harmony being around that guy. The Communication briefly stumped him until he recalled his ability to impose his Aura on others. Was that some sort of oppressive communication?

And he, too, had Corruption along with the rest. Healing too.

Thomas frowned in thought. Actually, that may not be a given. His enhanced Gift was at least partially responsible for these drops. He seriously doubted that Raveth had any innate resonance with Healing, so that mana crystal, at least, had come from Thomas. It could be that the Corruption had as well.

Either way, he plucked all the mana crystals (except for the Corruption) and stuffed them in his pack.

Next were the skill shards, and they were a doozy.

Precedes the King Along His Path

Induce The Sheep's Instinct to Lower One's Eyes to The Lion

The Full Weight of Heaven's Gaze Falls Upon Spirits

Domain of Ultimate Suppression

He would have snorted at the starkness of the last skill if he weren't so horrified. It looked like Raveth had gone all in on what he did best: aura-stomping people until they couldn't fight back.

If he were a higher level, he surely had more skills than this, but these were the ones which dropped.

Time to see what Laveris had left him.

Thomas had already picked up a Healing mana crystal from the body when he'd been in desperate need right after the Purge. So he was surprised to see another, along with an Adaptation crystal.

Yeah, his Gift was definitely influencing these drops to some degree.

He'd also dropped Light, Wood, Metal, and Crystal mana, as well as three shards:

Standing Unbowed Against the Rising Tide

Bound with Armor

Field of Command

Poor Laveris. Other than the first one, which Thomas suspected was his Gift, his other two shards had been pretty plainly named by System standards. He wondered if that meant they were cheaper.

In any case, he quickly searched the body and didn't come up with any extra storage bags. Either one of the others grabbed it, Laveris was actually poor, or he just hadn't brought it with him on what he thought would be an easy job to scare the humans.

Thomas took one last look around to make sure he hadn't left anything unlooted, then nodded to himself. Chances were low anyone would explore this place after he left. The poor portal room was never going to smell the same after all that had come out of him. He'd really underestimated how severe the Purge was, and that said a lot because he had glimpsed it from the outside with Prinna and Jo. It was a complete rejection of all toxins.

Physically, he felt amazing. Magically, he felt eighty years old. Even though he'd just picked up some great stuff, there was no way he could use it to build on the broken foundations of his core.

Time to face the music and tell his team.

He walked out. Zach, Jo, and Twitch were waiting in the hallways a little ways down. As usual, Antonin silently stood in the background.

Jo immediately brightened. "Thom—" she started, then stopped and stared.

They all were staring.

Twitch went a little creepier and very obviously looked him up and down, probably because he knew it would make Thomas uncomfortable. Then, unexpectedly he said, "What the hell? You were half-dead. Why have you come out looking cut like Captain America?"

"He's not blonde," Zach said, like that was the point, though he sounded slightly strangled.

"Captain America's boyfriend, then. The one with the arm."

He should have expected this. Jo had come out looking like a model. At least no one had wolf-whistled. Still, they had to focus. "Yeah, okay, my shirts don't fit anymore, but I have a problem."

Zach waved a hand. "Dude, I'm sure you can get tailoring. We're just glad you're okay."

"Or just go around like that," Jo suggested, still blushing. She wasn't quite looking at him anymore.

"Yeah, why not?" Zach nodded. "If you've got the guns, flaunt them. We’re all body-positive here."

Twitch flashed a thumbs-up.

Thomas cut across the nonsense. "The Corruption mana ate holes in my core."

The atmosphere instantly changed. Zach lost his shit-eating grin, Twitch flinched, and Jo actually looked at him again.

"Wait, what?" Zach exclaimed, actually teleporting close rather than taking the time to walk. He reached out a flat hand to Thomas's middle, only to instantly pull it back as if he'd been burned. His eyes went wide. "Those aren't just holes. Shit, dude. What happened?"

"My mana was converting to Corruption, then I purged it out. It did something like that to Antonin, didn't it? Only with his mind?"

"Shit," Zach repeated in answer, then ran a hand back through his hair. With his Gift of Mana so enhanced, he must have been able to sense Thomas's ruined core. So much for telling himself it wasn't as bad as it looked.

"It's that bad?" Twitch asked as he and Jo joined them.

Nodding, Zach clearly tried to look on the bright side. "Okay, well... uh, look at it this way, dude: you're stable, so we can get you to the Dark elves. Maybe they can do something?"

"Wait, haven't you said you can throw out your core or something and start again?" Jo asked. "Healer thing, right?"

She was also trying to put a good spin on it, but the idea made his heart sink. "It's not just the mana. It would be my skills and probably my abilities too. Everything I've learned and done up until now would be gone."

"Thomas," she said, meeting his eyes, "you're more than just your mana and skills."

"He's not-not his mana and skills, either," Zach said quietly. "It's all tied to your spirit, too."

Twitch spoke up. "Well, the System owes us, right? It enhanced our Gifts. Why can't it fix Thomas, too? Let's just finish up this quest or whatever, and Thomas can make it a condition of his reward."

That was assuming a lot, starting with the idea he could demand anything of the System. As far as he had seen, it was the other way around. Still, what choice did he have?

"It's not the worst idea," Thomas said slowly. "But as a heads-up, I've got to tap out of any more fighting. I don't even know how I healed myself with my core like this. It's not even building mana anymore, so I don't think I can help anyone else."

"No more big loot drops then?" Twitch said, too innocently.

Thomas flinched before he caught himself. "I don't know what you're talking about."

It didn't help that Jo and Zach had both turned sharply to stare at Twitch. Jo, with a look like she was ready to kill. Zach with weary resignation, as if he'd half-known that Twitch had already guessed.

"Yeah, I'm not stupid." Twitch rolled his eyes. "You don't got no Gift of Senses, nothing I saw was enhanced that way, but I did see what dropped from people and all that dropped in the Grizzly dungeon." He shook his head. "Shit, man, why aren't you living large in Vegas or something?"

Well, hell. Thomas let out an exasperated sigh. "I'd like to, but life keeps getting in the way!"

"If you tell anyone..." Jo started with a growl.

Twitch fluttered a hand at her. "Yeah, yeah, yeah. Zach already threatened me before this thing began. You think, knowing what I know, I'd sell out the living loot train who can also keep me alive? I want on the team. Uh," he looked at the others, "if you'll have me."

This was probably something that required a lot more thought, but Thomas was emotionally exhausted. Besides, it was either ally with him or kill him, and Thomas didn't want the loot he'd drop. "Sure, but Vegas isn't my thing. I just want to be able to afford something good by the lake."

Twitch grinned. "Better keep diving, then. I've seen those prices."

"Conditional team membership." Jo did not sound happy. She added, "And if you betray us, we'll kill you."

"Nah, I'll just do it," Zach said easily. "Won't be hard." Then he stuck out a hand. "Welcome to the team."

Flashing his braces in a grin, Twitch shook their hands.

After that was done, Thomas looked around. "So, uh, what happened to Tiny and his lackeys?"

"My guess is they went for the inventory room," Jo said.

Thomas felt an honest-to-God pang in his heart. "Damn it, I wanted to get in there."

Zach grinned. "You still might, bro. Something like that is bound to be locked up tight. We'll probably have to get in there, anyway. I get the feeling it's part of the whole 'reclaiming the area' part of the quest. Oh, hey," he snapped his fingers, "there's bound to be natural treasures in there, too. Maybe something to help you, yeah?"

Thomas brightened. "Then what are we waiting for?" He slung on his backpack, absently heard a clink inside, then stopped.

Natural treasures?

"Wait... actually, hold on a second, guys." Kneeling, he started sorting through his pack. He'd tell his team about the goodies afterwards and he was certain Jo and Zach already knew he would never leave the room without cleaning it out, but the mention of Natural Treasures reminded him of something. Oh wait, he'd put it in his front pocket, hadn't he?

Thomas unzipped it and pulled out the vial of Unicorn Blood, holding it up to the light. Little bits of what looked like glitter caught the light among the red goo. He'd gotten this drop at the same time he "met" up with Problem Child. "Unicorn blood is supposed to be restorative, right? I'm not just remembering that from Harry Potter books?"

Twitch, Jo, and Zach looked at each other.

"Uh, I think so?" Zach said, and Jo just shrugged.

"Yo, wasn't that a real bad idea in the books, though?" Twitch asked. "You get a cursed life." He grew defensive when the others looked at him. "What? They're always in prison libraries."

More cursed than having a ball of Corruption in his core? Or having to stand back and watch his teammates go through another fight, this time without Thomas being able to help? Tiny was no pushover.

Besides, he had a feeling about this.

Before he could really think about what he was doing, Thomas ripped the stopper off with his teeth, then put the vial to his lips and tipped it back.

The blood was... meaty, like the few times he'd tried wild game from someone's hunt. It was also body-hot, like it was still fresh. The glitter, thankfully, did not have a taste, just a terrible sandy texture against his teeth.

He swore he heard a familiar neigh in the back of his mind. It sounded triumphant.

The power of the Unicorn blood immediately shot to his core where it started to plug the holes in the lower levels, and then stick to and rebuild some sort of structure around the empty scaffolding of the higher levels.

"Whoa," he heard Zach say. "That's... really weird."

Thomas put his hand to his stomach, right over his core. "You should feel it where I'm from. I think it's trying to fix--" He stopped as with a slurping sound he hoped no one else could hear, the free-flowing motes of Healing mana he'd absorbed earlier were sucked in and down. Right to his core.

Thomas's eyes went wide and he snatched for his backpack, reaching for the crystals he'd looted from the elves. They were higher leveled, but that was the opposite of a problem right now. Light, Healing, Wellspring, and Adaptation. He absorbed them one by one, uncaring how each was so strong that the power burned. He'd just been through a whole lot worse.

Besides, it didn't stay in his body for long. The motes were immediately sucked inward and stuck to the Unicorn Blood in his core like it was glue until his rebuilt core shone like a miniature sun with power. Though it still had a sullen spot of darkness in the center.

Then with a final burst of energy that vaporized the blood, but left the glitter, the core contracted down to a compact sphere of power, and stratified.

Thomas had four layers once more, and now they'd been rebuilt from the first level up with higher mana.
