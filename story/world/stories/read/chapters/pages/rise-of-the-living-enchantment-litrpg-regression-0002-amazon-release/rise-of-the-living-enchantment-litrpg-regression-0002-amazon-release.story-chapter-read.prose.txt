Rise of The Living Enchantment: The Weaver

What do you do when you and a group of people are summoned to another world to stop a rising darkness and your brother becomes the Demon King?

The answer to that question is quite diverse, but Aiden had one.

In an attempt to save his brother, Aiden is caught in a clash of spells between the Demon King and a mythical opponent, suffers recommended termination at the hands of the very system that had become a part of his life since being summoned, and wakes up back at the first day.

Armed with the knowledge from his past life, he's got another chance, and Aiden Lacheart has no intentions of allowing his brother to become the Demon King once more. There's only one problem: he doesn't think he's the only one who's come back in time.
