3.22 Update:

Obviously, my initial timeline estimate below was a bit too eager. Putting an update here instead of a new post to prevent clutter in the chapters:

Chapters will definitely be coming back; you don't need to worry about me disappearing. I'm writing the next book as we speak and am still interacting with people in the Discord daily!

I just signed on with Aethon for the next three books of Spell Weaver, so this is still my primary writing focus, and there's plenty more coming your way.

I'll also be attending LitRPG Con as a panelist this year to talk about writing, and I'll also be going to Dragon Con with Aethon in September.

So yeah, all that to say, I'm still really in the litRPG / writing scene! This particular stretch of writing has just been a bit hard for me to push through, and because of that, my brain wants to do almost anything else at the moment.

I have some fun stuff planned for this book, it's just not something I've written before, which makes it feel challenging. Also, Alex has a LOT to do and a TON of rewards to reap for the effort put into the previous three books. A lot of payoff is coming in this book, and I want to make sure it comes across well and isn't just dumped on him or the readers. Since it's different writing, and also challenging in terms of pacing and tension, I'm just working through it a little slower and trying to keep the story in line with the premise and my goals from early on.

I'm doing my best to find a proper balance between:

1. Pushing through this patch due to the responsibility of wanting to deliver something to waiting readers

2. Not wanting to rush things too quickly and output something crappy just for the sake of word count

3. Not obsessing over the whole "everything needs to be perfect" feeling.

It's tricky, but we're getting there!

Patreon is still paused to prevent people from getting charged each month. I'm posting a chapter a week there for now to keep the pressure on me low while I work through some of the plot hiccups and frustrations I've been running into. Once I push through and get back into the swing of things, I'll make a more official announcement about starting things again in full.

My mental health and love of the story are in a great place overall.

Everyone in the comments and Discord has been very supportive, which I appreciate. Just know that the story isn't going anywhere. Thank you all for being here and being understanding.

Just thought I'd take some time to give those of you here a status update since I've seen a few people asking around in the last chapter of Book 3. At the end of most chapters I say this, but seriously, if you want consistent updates, joining the Discord or Patreon are your best bet. I don't think this is the best medium to post an update.

Anyway, I am indeed alive and doing well!

My break has been really refreshing. I got to spend time with family, make some big progress at my job, played a ton of Old School RuneScape and Path of Exile, and even got to read a book or two! This break has been seriously needed, and a full three months is probably longer than I should have taken off from writing.This whole project has gotten a lot bigger than I ever thought it could, and while that's amazing, it can be a bit overwhelming as well.

I know many stories can die from even a short break, so I appreciate all of you for still being here and interested in Spell Weaver!
