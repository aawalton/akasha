I whirled into existence in the Umbral Plane, air rushing around me like a tornado, then landed right beside the lantern at The Meridian tavern. It was still only mid day—or maybe it was already the day after I'd left here. Understanding of how Penumbral days worked still eluded me. It did seem like they were a bit shorter than Earth days though, if there was any relation at all.

I headed toward the door, when a familiar little orange salamander came barreling toward me along the top of the nearby fence. He launched at my head, limbs spread eagle, and landed on my face like one of those face-sucking aliens, little claws digging into my forehead and cheeks. I plucked him off and held him in my palm.

"Good to see you too, Nuzzle."

He licked my hand, then scurried up my arm to rest on my shoulder.

Apparently, we're buddies now. I half expected never to see the little critter again, unsure if he'd stick around waiting for me. Guess, he'd gotten attached. He wasn't the only one.

Together, we entered the tavern.

Inside, Reapers and Penumbrans alike filled the tables and bars, though few intermingled. Four or five servers delivered trays of drinks and hot food.

I didn't even consider needing food here. Did I need it to stay healthy and energized on this side of reality too? Technically, my body's just sleeping right now, right?

I made my way over toward the jungle bar again. Jericho was already there, halfway through a Bloody Mary. He'd really taken to those, apparently.

I eyed a plate of food at a table of Emerald Reapers as I passed. Nuzzle sniffed at the air, his eyes growing wide. I followed his lead, taking in the familiar scent of a home-cooked meal. A notification popped up.

[Baked Fish & Roasted Vegetables

Effect: Minor Health Regeneration, Minor Agility Buff.]

Ahh, interesting.

My own health hadn't fully replenished from the battle with the rhino. And my Volition lost about a third due to the transfer back here from Vitria.

I sat beside Jericho and waved down Judith.

"Ah, Zephyr, my boy. Welcome back!"

"Jericho." I nodded to him. "Calliope told you my new name, then?"

"Indeed. And I must say, I approve."

"What would I do without your approval?"

"Gods only know."

Gods… I'd almost forgotten that was my goal here. To find the one who spoke to me during my… erm… 'surgery.' Well, this is as good a place to start as any.

"Speaking of go—"

"So you're still alive," Judith interrupted, wiping down the bar in front of me.

Nuzzle jumped onto the bar and chirped happily at her, leaving dirty footprints exactly where she'd just cleaned.

"And so is your pet. Wonderful."

He pouted and licked up his own footprints, replacing the dirt with smears of saliva.

Judith sighed. "What can I get for you today?"

"What meals do you have that can regenerate Health and Volition?"

Judith blinked at me, then at Jericho. "You gonna tell him or am I?"

Jericho put a hand on my arm. "You can't regenerate Volition. That only comes from—"

"From relinquishing a Will. Right. Got it." I turned back to Judith. "Just Health then? And whatever buffs you think would be good for a noobie Amber."

"You become rich overnight or something?" Judith asked, flipping through a notepad of recipes.

I looked to Jericho for clarification, confused. He rubbed his fingers together. "Meals are a bit pricey."

"And by the looks of you," she added, glaring at the hole in my shirt that was still there from Zeus' lightning, "you haven't got a cent."

Jericho tossed a few of those black coins on the counter with a clink. "I'll spot the boy on this one. Get him something nice."

"You're such a silkie."

"Hey, I'll have you know there are situations where being silk is fine and dandy. Why, I was once told I'd make a brilliant mentor." He winked at me.

"Hah!" Judith laughed, walking away.

I thanked Jericho, not super eager to owe him, but also not comfortable walking around with reduced health. The whole exchange brought to light something I hadn't realized though. Penumbra had an entire economy I didn't know existed.

"I really don't wanna hear the answer to this… but I'm gonna need to find a way to earn money somehow, aren't I?" I asked.

"Don't you worry about that, my boy. There are plenty of ways for a Reaper to make coin. Do jobs for Penumbrans who don't want to travel out into the Meadow. Get a job yourself, of course. Sell item drops. Even sell Wills if you get desperate." Jericho said. "You'll make money eventually."

"Wonderful," I said. "I know exactly what my first purchase will be." I held up my shirt, looking at him through the hole in the chest.

He laughed. "A wise choice, my boy!"

"Where would I go to get that?

"The market in Meadowvale, of course."

"Oh, of course. Why didn't I think of that?"

Footsteps tapped on the floor behind us, followed by Calliope's eager voice. "So we're going to Meadowvale, then?"

"Can someone just tell me what Meadowvale is?" I asked.

Calliope and Jericho looked at each other with devilish grins.

"Nah."

A few hours later I found myself sitting in Jericho's wagon again. Nuzzle perched on Jericho's shoulder at the driver's seat, tongue flapping in the wind as we followed a beaten trail through the Meadow. Calliope sat on the bench opposite me, her horse—Allium—dutifully trotting along behind us without even a lead to tether him.

It's impressive how loyal he is.

"You all didn't need to come with me, you know?" I said.

Jericho craned his neck back to look at me. "Its no trouble, my boy. I have some business in the city anyway."

"Don't you have better things to do than guide me around places?" I asked Calliope.

"Don't flatter yourself. I just go wherever the day takes me. Usually that's sticking with Grampa Jer here."

"Watch it, little lady…" Jericho grunted.

She grinned. "But I do what I want. That's the glory of Penumbra. Today, I want to go to Meadowvale, so that's where I'll go."

I shrugged, not sure I could ever feel the same, simply wandering about aimlessly. I needed a goal, something to strive toward. That's why I had to earn some coin, start progressing.

Find a god.

Calliope hopped over next to me. "Can I see your scythe? Everyone's is different, almost like a fingerprint. And it'll never change no matter how far you progress. Isn't that fascinating?"

"Uh, sure, why not?" I summoned my weapon, being careful to do it slowly in the cramped space of the wagon.

She whipped a pair of leather gloves on and snatched it before it even finished coalescing, bits of carapace trailing in the air from my arm to complete the handle. "If you touch someone else's scythe it'll burn you," she noted as I eyed the gloves. "Wow, I love the flowing design, it kinda matches your hair. And…" She paused, staring at the blade.

"What is it?"

"I… never asked. What Affinities are you?"

Calliope moved her hands closer to the blade, moving it slowly so the light reflected just right. Glimmering images appeared faintly on the metal, like a foil edition from a trading card game.

I moved closer to examine it. I'd never even noticed them before. On one side of the blade, the eight-point star—the symbol for Astral Affinity. On the other… A skull.

"You're Death, aren't you?" she whispered.

I glanced to her eyes. They were wide, almost fearful, her face pale.

What the hell?

"I mean… I'm not Death. But, yeah, I have the Death Affinity," I said, trying to sound as casual as possible. But that reaction…

She placed my scythe on the floor and removed her gloves, absorbing them back into her carapace inventory.

"Is that… bad?" I asked.

Calliope shook her head and some color returned. "No. No, it's nothing."

"What Affinities are you? Ice obviously. And?"

"Glacial, thank you very much. And Life. That's why I can bond with Allium."

"I'm not life and I bonded with Nuzzle."

"Mhmm," was all she said, then lifted her leg like she'd sat on something uncomfortable. "Oh, what are these?" she pulled several crumpled notecard-sized scraps of paper from under her butt. The handwriting on them was almost completely illegible. She examined them for a moment.

Trying to change the subject? That's odd. I—

I looked closer at the heading on one of the notes she held. 'Amber… Welcome… Instructions…' Amber Welcome Instructions? Oh!

"Hey Jericho, I think Calliope found your—"

"Eh, probably not important," she said as she tossed them out the back of the wagon to flutter away.

"What was that, you said?" Jericho called over the rush of wind from the front.

"Never mind!"

I was about to ask Calliope about her reaction to my Affinity again when Jericho pulled the wagon to an abrupt halt.

"What's wrong?" I asked, scrambling to the opening behind him to see why we'd stopped.

At the bottom of the next hill a woman in a red cloak was yelling at a little Amber boy and pointing furiously at the corpse of a Sin nearby. We were too far away to make out any details or words, but the boy just stood there as if he was being scolded.

I looked to Jericho. His cheerful demeanor was gone, his eyes blank, staring off at nothing.

"Umm… Jericho?" I patted him on the shoulder.

He shuddered and shook his head, then crawled into the back of the wagon and sat on the other bench, looking out the back.

"What's going on?" Calliope asked, as she scooted up to look out the front too. "What the Hell is a Ruby doing way out here in the Meadow Realm?"

There was a long silence, then Jericho finally spoke, soft and melancholy. "That boy is about to go white."
