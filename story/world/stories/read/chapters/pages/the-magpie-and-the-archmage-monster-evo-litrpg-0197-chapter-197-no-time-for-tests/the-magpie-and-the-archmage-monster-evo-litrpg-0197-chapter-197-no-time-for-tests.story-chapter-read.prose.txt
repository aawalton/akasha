We shall spare you the details of the planning session. It was long and messy, and many times Liora believed that the fight would begin early because of how loud the shouting got when people disagreed with the strategies.

In short, Renir wanted everything to be done his way, and everyone else disagreed. For some reason, he was eager to keep Liora—who was effectively the strongest fighter in the group—back while relying heavily on Cee's traps and his own fighting prowess.

Neither Liora nor anyone else in the group trusted Renir to have any fighting prowess on a mountain where external magic didn't work, so all his plans were shoved aside. Instead, they decided to rely on Liora's and Cee's tried-and-true hunting method.

They called it "Bait and Web".

"Hey, you, ugly pig-faced sad excuses of flying creatures!" Liora, while in her magpie form, taunted the group of bats as they fluttered about the mountain, likely in search of their next meal.

They all turned to her as one and let out a high-pitched scream. The noise felt as if somebody had struck Liora's head with a hammer. They had anticipated this. Renir had warned that many monsters relied on sound-based attacks in this mountain.

Liora had hoped that her Sound Resistance skill would nullify the effect, but it was at Novice level, so she couldn't expect a high level of protection. Nonetheless, it was enough for Liora to power through the unpleasantness and fly into one of the more narrow mountain passages.

The bats flew after her, still producing their high-pitched screams; however, they weren't able to clump together anymore with how narrow the passages were. Instead, they were forced to fly in a line, one after another. Exactly as they had predicted.

Liora folded her wings close to her body and flew through the small opening in the Cee's web that the spider had left for Liora. Yes, it was their old strategy. The same one that allowed them to level up rapidly in the Hell Marsh.

Unfortunately, Cee's elemental webs were a bust. While they were within Cee's hold and not connected to anything, they appeared to function just fine, but as soon as the webs got anchored anywhere, they lost their properties and became regular webs.

But hey, there were a lot of things one could do with regular webs too. Immobilizing a bunch of bats was great.

Or it would have been great if they had actually flown into the webs. They were almost invisible to the eye, only barely noticeable by Liora, whose vision was as good as it could get. The bats, on the other hand, weren't known for great vision.

But Liora had forgotten that they weren't like the birds they had hunted in the marshes. These guys stopped right before the web, not flying into it. They knew it was there. Not because of their eyesight, but rather the echolocation.

"I told you it wouldn't work!" Renir complained.

"It was worth a shot," Liora objected.

The bats screeched angrily, producing a different kind, far more powerful sonic attack. But there was nothing for them to fear.

Caspian, Cee, and Smokey emerged from the crevice where they had been hiding, and the merman began to sing. In a way, it didn't make sense. His voice was soft and melodious, not loud like the screeching of the bats.

Nonetheless, it overpowered the disgusting noise with ease, covering them in a blanket of pleasantness. While magpies weren't songbirds, it wasn't lost on Liora just how masterful Caspian was with his voice. The screeching of the bats was nothing more than some distant noise, not quite able to penetrate and hurt Liora.

"Commence Plan Bounce!" Liora announced, flying out between the small opening between the webs.

Cee scuttled up the web, urging Smokey to follow. The wyvern leapt up to the edge of a cliff ridge, actually managing not to catch onto any webs. Cee masterfully closed the opening right after, leaving Caspian alone, protected by a wall of webs.

The bats continued their screeching. No, it appeared that they were trying to switch up their attacks. Liora could only tell because the noises sounded somewhat different. However, thanks to Caspian's protective song, none of their attacks did anything.

Carrying him around all the time was finally paying off.

Liora transformed into her dracoryx form and activated Decoy Fade. The decoy flew up higher into the sky while part of the group chased after her. Another part of the group flew towards Smokey, while the last part went after Cee. Or tried to, at least.

Cee jumped away the moment they came close, disappearing and reappearing some distance behind them.

Liora meanwhile attacked the group chasing after her decoy. She flew from behind and, with her razor-sharp talons, slashed into the membranes of the bat wings. They were tougher than she had expected, but her talons could cut through steel, so in the end, she managed to tear through them with ease.

One after another, the bats she slashed plummeted down. Not like rocks—they could still fly—but nowhere near as well as before. The bats in front who were chasing the decoy turned around, confused by the invisible assailant. Liora was about to slash all of them.

But suddenly she felt herself get pierced from behind.

"CAW!" She screamed, and her decoy disappeared. That she hadn't expected. She thought that the decoy would disappear only if it was hit by an attack. It turned out that her getting hit also dispelled it.

But what had hit her?

Liora looked down and saw that something red had pierced her side. At first she didn't know what it was. But then she saw more of these crystallized red shards flying at her.

"What the hell?! This is cheating!" Liora pulled the strange shard out and turned into her magpie form to dodge the incoming attack.

They were coming from the injured bats who had latched on the side of the mountain and were shooting the red shards at her.

"It's blood magic!" Renir called from below. "It'll work even in these mountains because blood is part of their bodies. It's an incredibly strong magical catalyst."

Liora wanted to groan. Of course these bastards had found a workaround for the mountain's constrictions. But they weren't the only ones who had found workarounds.

Cee jumped on one of the bats and wrapped her electric web around its neck, effectively trying to choke it. According to Renir, these guys were incredibly weak to electricity. And since Cee's web wasn't connected to the mountain, it couldn't be drained of its magic.

The bat convulsed almost instantly, losing control of its wings and falling like a rock. Cee let go and jumped away from the bat. It fell straight into her sticky web below, still constricted by the electric web.

"One down!" Cee announced, jumping around to confuse the bats.

Smokey was another weapon they had. The bats that were flying for him had truly underestimated the might of a wyvern. Especially a wyvern who was born on a volcanic island. He could breathe fire after all.

Real fire. Not a magical one. Renir said that there was a special organ in his throat that produced the stuff, so it didn't matter to Smokey whether the mountain drained external magic.

When the bat swarm flew at him, he opened his mouth and let the flames out. As expected, it was quite effective against bats. They got scorched in hot flames, those at the front screeching such a ghastly scream that it rang through Liora's bones.

Even worse for the bats was the fact that flames stuck to them. To keep the explanation short, Smokey produced some kind of flammable liquid which he spewed out. It meant that his flames didn't just burn and disappear. They stuck around for as long as the liquid remained.

Kind of like the thing humans used to get their barbeque going, except even more potent because it kept burning for longer.

But there was an unfortunate side effect they hadn't accounted for. The bats burned by Smokey quickly lost control and flew like crazy. Some of them fell and got tangled in Cee's web. Which was flammable.

Caspian's song stopped as the merman screeched. Fire was spreading through the web at astounding speed, effectively locking the merman in a fiery prison.

"HELP! HELP!" Caspian screamed.

"Goddamit!" Cee screamed, spraying part of the web with her watery mist to put out the flames for long enough for her to get through.

This probably would have been easier if the whole web just burned up. But Cee's webs were far more resistant to fire now. So they burned, but didn't burn up instantly. Kind of like candle wicks, coated in a layer of wax.

The bats caught up in the webs were screaming and burning alive. With Caspian's song gone, there was nothing to protect their hearing, which meant that Liora's head once again felt like it was being hit by a dozen hammers at once.

The bats weren't stupid; they instantly jumped on the opportunity to use their sonic attacks. Cee staggered, unable to reach Caspian, who was curled up in the middle of the flaming prison, while Smokey was throwing his head around in confusion, completely disoriented by the noise.

Liora wasn't sure what to do. Their whole plan had gone completely awry.

"Liora! To me!" Renir called.

Thankfully, it appeared that he hadn't been affected by the sonic attack. He didn't strictly speaking have ears. How could he still hear?

Who knew? It wasn't the right time to think about this question. Liora flew down to him while equipping the saddle.

Renir did fumble while trying to get on top of her, several bats chasing after Liora. A few shards of the blood hit her back, which Renir grabbed and yanked out as soon as they were in the air.

"I'm going to give your attacks an electric attribute, but you must use physical attacks; otherwise, it won't work," Renir said, putting his rocky hand on her neck.

"Easier said than done!" Liora flew, trying to dodge the incoming blood shards.

These guys clearly didn't intend to let Liora close anymore. They knew that in close combat, Liora would be the winner.

Some of the bats were also trying to get to Caspian, searching for an opening in the flaming web, while Cee was desperately trying to save the merman.

What could she do? These guys were completely overwhelming them with the sonic attack. Even Liora wasn't able to think straight. She barely had enough mind power to dodge the incoming attacks, much less to come up with a plausible plan.

[Your trait <Sound Resistance> has reached [Adept] level.]

[Sound Resistance – At [Adept] level, greatly reduces the harmful effects of sound-based attacks, including disorientation, confusion, stunning, pain, and forced loss of balance. This skill also helps you remain focused when exposed to loud noises, sonic vibrations, and magically enhanced cries, songs, roars, cackles, or shrieks.]

Liora instantly felt the difference; the sonic screeching of the bats felt more like a nuisance. Like a neighbor blasting loud music in the middle of the night. Annoying as all hell, but it didn't hurt her anymore.

At that moment, her mind cleared up. She could think again. The bats were everywhere. Chasing Liora, throwing their blood magic shards, some were about to close in on Smokey, and some were about to strike Cee. They were too confused to defend themselves.

Liora needed a way to hit them all. But she couldn't use Wind Blast, because it was external magic. However, she could use her Resonance Mimicry. Her ultimate weapon.

And she had Renir.

"Renir! I'm going to sing. Do whatever you must to let electricity carry through my voice," Liora said.

"Wh-What? I don't know whether such a thing would even work. I might hurt you by accident. We need to test—"

"No time for tests! Do it," Liora commanded and opened her beak.

She started blasting the beat of the most electrifying song she knew.
