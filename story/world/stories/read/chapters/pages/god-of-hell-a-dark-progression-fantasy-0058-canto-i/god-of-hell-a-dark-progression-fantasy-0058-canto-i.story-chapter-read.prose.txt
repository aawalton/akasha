Canto I

Tayalnisyan. Limbo.

The birthplace of my first Nightmare; the birthplace of my first love.

Even now, as I scribble away on parchment—each word bringing me closer to a place that seems as distant from me now as my time before the Inferno—I remember the scent of your hair, the colour of your smile, what made you laugh, what made you frown, what made you…you.

I have changed so much since then. Done so much.

Gained friends, enemies, brothers, and sisters. And lost so many of them too.

In my dreams, I see you return; in my Nightmares, I see you return.

In the former, we are overjoyed, filled with love and awe for one another. We bask underneath the stars and live a life of infinite bliss.

In the latter, you are horrified by me and my actions. You raise your bow at a man you once knew with a shaky resolve. And you fire.

In both, my torture ends.

Selvas.

The white leopard.

Hunter of Stradale.

Saviour of Nero.

I miss you terribly.
