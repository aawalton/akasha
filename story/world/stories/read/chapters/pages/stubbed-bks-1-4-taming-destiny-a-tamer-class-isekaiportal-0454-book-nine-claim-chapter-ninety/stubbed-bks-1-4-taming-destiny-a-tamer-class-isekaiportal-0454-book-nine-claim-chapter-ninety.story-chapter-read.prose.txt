Everyone clear on the plan? I ask tensely through the Alliance Bond.

The Tier four seems to have entrenched itself thoroughly in the cave system we previously identified. Taking a deep breath, I centre myself, acknowledging that people will probably die in this battle – and deeming our reason to fight worth the risk. Flashes of faces of so many people turned feral with the parasite’s control go through my mind. I feel my resolve firm.

Valence feels the power of the order go through him as if he still had a Bond. Almost against his will, his body jerks forward into a run along with everyone around him. It smooths as he centres his mind into it and reminds himself that he wants to do this. That he’s honourbound to doing it even if he didn’t have such a personal stake in it.

A moment before the spearpoint of his retinue slides into the body of the beast army, Valence activates Ally Defence, then raises his sword into a position ready to swing, holding it with a firm, two-handed grip.

Father wasn’t wrong, it’s almost unbearable, he thinks distantly as he fights through the pain pulsing through him to swing his sword.

Meanwhile, his Physical Resistance and Magical Resistance Skills as well as the armour’s basic enchantments keep even the most desperate beast attack from leaving a mark, as the beast that leaps to bite his wrist finds out to its detriment. Valence doesn’t even need to kill that one – Mul snatches it and simply stamps on it. The Tier one doesn’t stand a chance against his strength.

A moment later, the Classer returns to fighting and Valence throws himself into the fray alongside him. Their task is to cut a swathe through the low-tier beasts to the entrance – and the Tier threes that guard it.
