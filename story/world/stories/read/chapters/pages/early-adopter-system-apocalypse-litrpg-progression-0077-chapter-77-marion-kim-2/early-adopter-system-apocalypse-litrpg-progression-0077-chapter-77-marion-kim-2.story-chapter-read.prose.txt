Whatever was happening didn't manifest as an attack. Instead, it felt more like a subtle, pacifying effect. Perhaps some kind of passive? Or maybe a buff?

For someone else, it might simply have made them calmer. For Scarlet, it slowly stripped away that invisible layer between her and the world.

Dangerous. Fascinating. Definitely concerning, but also, oddly freeing.

Was this how other people felt all the time? No passive internal filter between them and their emotions, no calculated expression, no mimicry. Just feeling something, and allowing the feeling to be?

"You're a dangerous man, Mr. Kim," she said.

"How do you know my name?"

Well, Investigate was the real answer, but she reached out a delicate hand, and gently flicked the nameplate on the desk in front of her.

It made a resonant ting, like a metal hitting a tuning fork.

Amusement and a hint of relief rippled through his aura and dissipated like everything else before it.

"Marion Kim. Feel free to call me Kim," he said, breaking the beat of silence as he inclined his head at the nameplate. Scarlet reciprocated the gesture. “What can I help you with today?” he asked. It seemed he had settled back into customer-service mode.

"Scarlet Bourne, feel free to call me Bourne," she said. "I have a proposal for you, Mr. Kim," she said, crossing one leg over the other. At the same time, she gave Maus a mental nudge.

He’d huddled himself away the moment they'd released the crude technique they’d used to bypass the dome of death.

When the little guy poked his head out of the collar of Scarlet's shirt, Marion showed no surprise. Scarlet wouldn’t have been surprised if he had something like Maus’s spiritual sense and knew her buddy was there the whole time.

'Maus, see if you can't dispel whatever it is he's doing around us.'

'Dispel?' He mulled over the word.

'Yes. Remove the effect, or make it stop affecting us.'

'Like what we did outside?’

‘Exactly like that.’

‘Okay, Boss! I can do it,' he assured her.

Whatever Kim was doing, this was a good training opportunity. Part of her mind split off, working alongside Maus to find a way to target and negate the effect.

"A proposal?" Kim asked. Her mental conversation with Maus had barely taken any time.

"Yes."

She placed the glowing orb on the desk between them.

"This is an Orb of Transformation, specifically an Umbros Orb of Transformation."

"Umbros," he said, with a hint of recognition. His eyes involuntarily flicked down to the book in his hand before darting back up to her.

"Yes. They are one of the many races in the greater System Network."

He tilted his head to the side. The curiosity was obvious in the action, but he restrained himself admirably.

“I see,” he said. Scarlet doubted he did but moved on regardless.

"This orb facilitates what is known as a racial transformation. Where one species becomes another. It is effective so long as it's done before the first Tier."

"Is it impossible to change species later?"

Scarlet shook her head, inwardly amused that his response was academic curiosity rather than surprise. She supposed she couldn't expect incredulity, considering all that had already happened since the glowing boxes had appeared.

"This orb only works before Tier 1. I have no knowledge beyond that."

"I know of the Umbros race," he said. "They are a race that is at a disadvantage when trying to progress on their own. Their progress is highly dependant on who they decide to bind themselves to, if they decide to at all. My Tome described it as a symbiosis between light and shadow."

"Yes. It is a partnership."

He looked down at Maus, then flicked his eyes back to Scarlet’s.

"It is not a subordinate bond like that of my Familiar. It is a partnership of equals, at least in terms of one's own will. As far as I understood it in the brief – very brief, mind you – discussion I had with a member of that faction, the Shadow-Bond acts as a mutually advantageous system. Both would raise the inherent potential of the other. The drawback being, and I quote: ‘The shadow can only be as deep as the light is bright.’" She smiled at the poetic wording the umbros had used.

"So, the higher a shadow's partner climbs..." Kim mused, obvious interest flashing through his aura.

"Yes. If a partner stagnates, the shadow does as well. Usually it's not an issue because, well, the Umbros are generally limited in their ability to progress individually. Don't ask me the specifics. They weren't exactly a priority in the brief time I had to speak with them. From what I understood, the benefits can be immense, especially if an umbros partners well. The downsides, however, are just as real. If an umbros binds to a bad partner, or no partner, well." Scarlet held out the hand she didn’t have on her cane.

"And you are suggesting I become one of these… Umbros."

"I am suggesting that that this path would be uniquely advantageous to someone of your talents. I am also suggesting that a partnership between us would be mutually beneficial."

The two stared at each other for a while.

"Why?"

"I need administrative assistance.” Scarlet took in a deep breath before letting it out slowly. “I require competent people, trustworthy people. However, for those closest to me, trust and a contract are not enough. Their fate must be bound to mine." She stroked Maus’s head.

The little guy was getting somewhere. That sense of ‘offness’ was beginning to recede as Scarlet regained some of her composure.

"I have been doing just fine here in my library. I do not see a need to leave to become a part of whatever it is you are trying to do."

Scarlet looked at Marion Kim. Before Initialisation, she'd had one of those feelings about him. A recognition of a kindred spirit, more than anything else. Those feelings were rarely wrong.

She loved Maus. He was, in many ways, the reason she was who she was right now.

But he was her Familiar, and as things stood now, he could never be an equal.

Not unless they met exceptional circumstances. Circumstances she would work towards, but that was a long way off.

So, she explained to Kim. Told him of the Safe Zone Quest, the Factions, the doom timer looming over Earth. Explained that she absolutely knew there was a Portal in the basement, and that she'd happily support him in clearing those Trials, gaining more rewards, and getting stronger.

Then she explained that she was no light. In truth, she was just a much better disguised shadow.

She was not looking to be some shining beacon of hope. What she sought was the very freedom that would allow him to continue sitting in his library and sipping his tea as a battle raged on outside.

By the end of her mini-pitch, she had presented him with a System contract and an offer.

A new path to power, guaranteed potential, genuine partnership, and all for the low, low price of binding his fate to hers.

"I'll admit, Ms. Bourne, you've set up a remarkably compelling case." He looked down at the contract, then at the glowing orb, then back to Scarlet. He studied her for a while as she looked back at him, both of them seeing more than the other was likely comfortable with.

Then he picked up the contract.

"Okay," he said.

"Okay," Scarlet repeated, handing him a fountain pen.

He read through the contract, asking clarifying questions here and there. But for the most part, they sat in companionable silence.

Scarlet sipped at the tea he’d offered and waited, iterating on the spiritual energy defence with Maus while she waited.

When he was done, he looked back up at her and nodded. Just once.

The two smiled at each other. Kim's a fleeting impression of emotion; Scarlet's, a deliberate act to signify a genuine feeling.

Marion Kim raised his pen and signed. Energy flowed into the contract, then zipped directly into both Scarlet and the librarian.

"Welcome to the team," she said. "I will leave the orb with you, but I highly recommend you do not use it until you are at the threshold of Level 9. Try to get your Stats as high as possible. In fact, do try to max them out. I'm not sure how many Free Stats you have, but the cap is 25."

She explained Stat Saturation, point distribution, and exercises to increase his main Stats.

"Now, about that Portal," she began, but was cut off by something she sensed.

Scarlet deliberately stretched her perception, pulling back from all other directions so she could range further and further into town.

It appeared backup had arrived, but not for the defenders.

There were now two more beasts heading over to wreak havoc on her future Safe-Zone.

That was simply unacceptable.

She turned back to the librarian and tossed him a simple metal brooch with intricate enchantments overlaid on it.

"It's fairly crude," she admitted. "However, it will allow us to keep in touch." She touched her ear where there was an innocuous earring and smiled. "It's fairly self-explanatory. Simply insert some mana, and you'll be able to contact me. Complete the dungeon when you have a chance, and remember to remain safe."

Then she pointed to the Spirus Blade, a very loud elephant in a very quiet room.

"Be careful with that."

It was obvious she knew what it was. It was obvious he knew what it was. It was obvious he knew she knew what it was.

"I will."

She once more hopped over the counter, Maus blinking to her shoulder as she made her way out.

Turning back, she looked regretfully at the tea she had to leave behind. It had been delightful.

"Scarlet," Kim's call stopped her. "Thank you," he said, sincerity rippling through his aura as he tapped the orb.

But the ripples in his aura were too strong to be solely about the artifact.

That was alright, she thought as his emotions settled into stillness once again.

"I look forward to working with you," she replied.

The rhythmic clacking of her cane followed her retreat as the automatic doors once more slid open and deposited the young woman and her Familiar back into what felt like a separate reality.

Then kinetic energy surged from her, and she disappeared, barely leaving an afterimage behind.
