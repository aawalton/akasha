Following Groln’s shocking news, Asher gathered up his teammates and quickly brought everyone together. A brief call with Mako confirmed that they had also never seen head nor tail of any greater revenants, and that the team was actually almost to the center of the quarantine zone, having managed to wipe out their three revenants far quicker than anyone else. After picking them up, Asher Recalled to Groln before letting everyone out of his rift so they could talk.

“The Hojon Empire gives specialized sending stones to its greater powers that allow us to track and even scry one another in emergencies,” Groln explained as he held up his stone. “As far as I can tell, the stones held by the greater powers in charge of maintaining the quarantine have been abandoned. We’re ordered to never let our stones out of our sight, so the fact that scrying them results in nothing more than an image of grass billowing in the wind means that the worst has happened. The greater powers are dead.”

“And Eupher potentially has two more greater revenants under him now,” Rulfar pointed out, the bloodhound still in his battle form. “What do we do? There’s no telling where he’s gone off to.”

“Unfortunately, there is,” Groln said, grimacing as he looked off into the distance. “We don’t know much about Eupher, but he is prideful, and seems to have a bone to pick with the Hojon Empire. I believe he will strike at the empire itself before attempting to flee. Claiming two of its living greater powers won’t be enough for him, he will wish to rain havoc and destruction on the empire itself. Doubly so now that we made an actual effort to destroy him rather than just keeping him contained.”

“Can we head him off?” Samantha asked, her face paling at the idea of another innocent village being razed to the ground. “Do you know where he will attack?”

“The Hojon Empire is quite vast. It would be impossible to pinpoint which settlements he is going for. However, I don’t think he’s going to go after just one regardless,” Groln admitted, gritting his teeth. “He knows we are capable and have plenty more forces to throw against him. He will need to strike hard and fast, and then run before our full force is brought to bear in response. In order to cause the most damage, I’m willing to bet he’ll split his forces. Any one greater revenant will be more than capable of wiping out an entire village or town all on its own, if not more.”

“With potentially five greater revenants and the lich himself, Eupher could destroy a few dozen towns before running,” Asher finished, his heart sinking at the news. “He’d claim tens of thousands of lives.”

“At a minimum,” Groln nodded. “I’ve already informed the Queen of what I believe is going to happen, but this is all happening too quickly. The Hojon Empire is massive, and our other greater powers are busy at the border. Teleporting from the capital this far out will be quite draining on our wizards, but we have no other option. I have already called for their assistance.”

“Should we remain in our three teams, or split up further?” Amanda asked, taking in everyone and giving the members of her Order a proud nod when she realized everyone was still among the living.

“I’m hoping that Eupher only managed to make one of the two greater powers he took down a revenant, or that they managed to kill at least one of the already existing minions during their fight. That would mean only five potential targets including him. However, we should assume the worst, which would be five greater revenants and one lich. We need six teams, so we’re going to have to split up even more,” Groln agreed. “My team, the Eight Crests, and Asher’s team make three. Falling Sun, can you split your order into three?”

“Consider it done,” Amanda nodded, turning to her Order and dividing the members into three groups. With thirty total members who had come to fight alongside her, three teams were created easily enough. Curiously, the teams were far from equal. Rather than three teams of ten, Amanda formed two teams of twelve and one of only six, which she herself moved to stand in front of. It was clear from the division that she viewed herself as being equal to six of her fellow warriors at a bare minimum, but not a single member of her Order complained. Asher hadn’t been that impressed with what he’d seen from her during her fight with the slayer, but she’d said herself that the blood storm swirling around overhead had hampered her somehow, and he didn’t get the sense she was one to make excuses.

While the teams were being made, Groln raised his voice so that everyone could hear as he continued talking.

“Normally, the capabilities of greater powers are a national secret, but seeing as there is a good chance we will be fighting them shortly, I’m going to reveal what I know, so listen carefully. The two who were in charge of the quarantine were known as The Warden and Flaming Thread. The Warden has Isolation, Iron, and Strength as his elements, and is just about the most dangerous one-on-one fighter you could ever find yourself going up against. If he manages to trap you within his skills, you’re already dead. Flaming Thread has the Silk, Fire, and Shadow elements. She’s almost impossible to see coming, and will slice you to pieces if you let her get the drop on you. I have no idea how Eupher managed to take them both out, even with the greater revenants already under his control, but be on your guard.”

“Those sound like some pretty fun people to fight against,” Moxy grinned, punching her fists together as Asher rolled his eyes.

Shortly after they all finished splitting up into new teams, there was a flash of violet light and seven people popped into existence not far from Groln’s position. All seven of them wore colorful robes with cords of varying lengths and materials draped over their shoulders, and a quick glance with Identify confirmed that all seven of them were wizards. One of them looked red in the face and was clearly doing her best not to double over and pant, but the other six stepped forward, all of whom gave Groln a small bow.

“Hellspawn. Are your teams ready?”

“Yes,” Groln nodded, stepping forward. “The six closest towns and villages to where the fallen greater powers’ sending stones lay. But first…”

Turning toward Asher, Groln stared at him, his piercing red eyes meeting Asher’s own. “Can you place a mark with each group? We don’t know which if any will end up running into the lich, and we’ll need you to put him down.”

“I can do it,” Asher admitted, wincing as he ran through a mental list of his current marks and realized he was going to have to replace some of the ones he’d had out in the world for a couple of months now. At the moment, he had Eternal Marks on Samantha, Brian, Rosh, the royal palace in the Noala Kingdom, Helpha’s store, Scritchems, equipment that he’d given Groln and Mako so he could Recall to their teams, and two empty marks he’d taken to leaving in reserve now that he’d adapted to using Recall in combat. The only marks he absolutely refused to erase for any reason were the marks on Brian and Scritchems, but he could be a little more flexible on the others. Mentally letting go of the mark on Helpha’s store in Dormaul, Asher placed a mark on each of the three teams that had been formed from Amanda’s Order. He’d swap out the mark he currently had on Rosh if he found he needed to use Recall in the midst of combat, but for now, he left it in place.

“I’ll help you get the marks you lose back into place later,” Samantha told him, squeezing his hand reassuringly as he returned to his team.

“You all know the drill!” Groln called out, turning to take in the six groups. Asher got his first real look at the greater power’s own group of royal slayers, and he had to admit, they were a rather colorful bunch.

Groln stood out the most, seeing as he looked like a living demon, but the others were far from normal. There was a woman with dark blue skin and gills of all things who seemed to have water constantly dropping off her. A man whose skin looked like it had transformed into chunks of granite, who was currently crunching away on what appeared to be various small rocks he was pulling from a pouch at his side. And last but not least, someone Asher couldn’t tell a damn thing about. Their body was covered in a dense, small layer of clouds that obscured their form completely. It seemed each member of Groln’s slayer team was well within the Stage of Power themselves, as their bodies had clearly been altered by their elements. He couldn’t help but wonder what his own team would end up looking like one day once they got to that point, but he was torn from his musings when the wizards stepped up to the different teams and began teleporting them one after another.

“Are you ready?” a man in a billowing, red robe with sashes made from some sort of vine dangling from his shoulders asked.

“Let’s go,” Asher nodded, making sure they were all together before giving the wizard a thumbs up. The moment he did so, the wizard raised a hand, and a bubble of tritessence spun into place around them. Just as quickly, it twisted along with the land around them, before popping and spitting them out on the outskirts of a small village.

One that was currently engulfed in flame and filled with the terrified screams of fleeing villagers.

“By the realms!” the wizard gasped, covering his mouth with a hand. “How could—”

Five brilliant streaks of flame shot out of a nearby shadow without warning, each one aimed for a different member of the group. Asher flicked through the astral, dodging the attack aimed at him with absolute ease. Samantha brought up her emerald wings, forming a shield and blocking the attack effortlessly. Moxy met the attack with a powerful Air Blast of her own, stopping it dead in its tracks before it could even reach her. Rulfar leaned into it with his axe, trusting his strength and powerful form to block the strike using his enchanted weapon.

And the wizard was beheaded by the flaming projectile, looking like he hadn't even spotted the attack coming, much less have time to react to it.

Asher swore when he returned to the material realm to the sight of the wizard’s body hitting the ground as blood spilled everywhere. He hadn’t exactly been planning to rely on the man in a fight, but Kroult had shown time and time again that having a wizard supporting them from the background was only ever a good thing. Before any of them could say anything, a woman emerged from the shadow that the attacks had just come from, smiling coyly at them as she did so. Her body was covered in nothing but a few flaming ribbons that barely kept her modesty as they curled around her, and her very hair was an active inferno, the locks burning just as brightly as the town behind her.

Guess that answers which of her elements is influencing her body.

“My my, would you look at that,” Flaming Thread said. Touching a finger to her lips, she gave them all a curious look. Her hair flared brighter as she tapped her lips and hummed. “Four little candles, come here to burn.”
