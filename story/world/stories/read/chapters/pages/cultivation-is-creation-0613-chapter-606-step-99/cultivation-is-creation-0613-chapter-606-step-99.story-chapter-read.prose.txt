Now that we were about to face the manifestation of the Dreamgod, we all sat down at different parts of the black dream-stone to nurse our wounds and recover whatever spiritual energy we could before the most difficult fight of this expedition began. And we definitely needed the break.

Even Li Na, whose Nine Deaths Transfer technique restored her to peak form, it still had a mental cost to use the technique seven times in rapid succession, and now she was pale-faced, staring blankly at the void above us where that number "1" floated in golden light.

As for Wu Kangming, he sat cross-legged about ten meters away from me, and despite his breathing being steady as he cycled dream qi through his meridians, the way his shoulders were slightly hunched and tensed told me that he was bearing the psychological toll that dying repeatedly had on the mind.

I was a witness that the mental fortitude required to maintain your sense of self after experiencing mortality over and over again within the span of a few hours was beyond what most cultivators would be able to stay standing after.

As usual, Xie Ling stood at the edge of the step with her back to the rest of us.

The ice princess was staring at the darkness below, and since I couldn't quite read her expression from this angle, I couldn’t help but wonder what she was thinking about as we prepared to face the final enemy standing between us and the Chamber of the Sleeper King.

What was on my mind concerning her was her agenda. I had no idea what it was. However, she clearly knew not to trust Song Wei and Shen Bolin, why else would she hide her cultivation level?

It was obvious that a Lucid Lawbearer would be wary of others at the same level, whatever plan she had, she didn’t want the expedition leaders to be alerted. And as helpful as she has been throughout, it would be prudent to keep an eye on her.

"We should discuss strategy before proceeding," Xie Ling said without turning around. "Shen Mohai's manifestation will at least be at the level of Peak Lucid Lawbearer cultivation, but likely stronger since he is the palace's creator."

"He'll know every technique used by every cultivator who died in this palace over fifty thousand years," Wu Kangming added.

"The manifestation will test more than combat ability," I said, drawing on what I'd observed from the previous historical cultivators we'd faced. "Every enemy on the stairway has challenged us in different ways. Chen Huan tested our ability to handle overwhelming numbers. The Three Calamities forced us to adapt to perfect coordination. This final manifestation will probably test why we're here and whether we deserve to go on any further."

"Philosophy won't matter if we can't survive his first attack," Xie Ling said, finally turning to face us properly. "I've never fought a Dreamgod Ascendant, but the gap between Peak Lucid Lawbearer and someone who nearly achieved true divinity through dream cultivation is insurmountable. And it doesn’t fill me with confidence even if he is only at the Lucid Lawbearer realm."

“Then if we want a chance to win, we need to coordinate better,” Wu Kangming said. “Xie Ling, you need to provide battlefield control with your ice formations. Li Na, you stay behind and support with healing arrays and strategic transference when necessary. Hou and I will engage with directly."

Despite the plan making tactical sense, I couldn’t help but feel that something felt incomplete about it. Sect Master Yuan had told me that the tournament's true purpose was to test the character and identity of the competitors. Which explained why the Dream World was chosen since it operated on similar principles. What mattered here was belief, conviction, and the strength of one's sense of self. So, the question running through my mind was how would the Dreamgod Ascendant manifestation test that?

However, since the battle wouldn’t begin until we stepped onto the next step, we used the time to review everything we knew about Shen Mohai from the documents and expedition reports. We were looking for any weaknesses or patterns that might give us an advantage, even a small one. In our situation, we’d take whatever we could.

And it was like this that an hour passed, and when we were all rested and prepared as we would ever be, we made our way to the archway that would lead to step ninety-nine.

The archway shimmered with that same silver-white energy that had marked every transition point, creating a barrier that prevented us from sensing what waited on the other side until we actually crossed the threshold.

It was finally happening. This was it. This was the final challenge before reaching the Chamber of the Sleeper King and taking the Celestial Dream Lotus. Only succeeding in that would make this nightmare stairway worth it.

"Whatever happens up there, we face it together,” Xie Ling said, turning to look at us with a soft expression that I had never expected to see on her face. “No matter what happens, we don’t leave anyone behind. Okay?"

Instead of saying anything, I just nodded. While I appreciated the words, how many cultivators said something similar before being the very one to stab you in the back? After what happened on step ninety, I was more cautious than ever before, and it would take much more than sweet words to convince me.

"I won't run," Li Na nodded, clearly trying to prove herself trustworthy after the betrayal. "I swear it."

"We’ll find out soon enough,” Wu Kangming murmured, saying out loud what I was thinking.

The sword cultivator didn’t bother sparing her a glance, he simply walked through the archway, and we quickly followed after him. When we emerged on the other side, we had expected to find a godly figure standing before us, but instead we found it to be completely empty.

Honestly, had it not been for the number 1 floating in the void above us, and the ambient spiritual pressure pressing down on our shoulders like it wanted to make us kneel down before its majesty, I would have wondered if we were even in the right place.

But the size of the step, which stretched out at least two hundred meters in every direction from where we stood, created an arena that could accommodate large-scale techniques and reality-warping abilities without running out of room, so it made me think that this was place for the grand battle.

In some ways, the waiting for the manifestation to appear was a trial in itself because as each second passed, our minds thought of all the terrible possibilities about what form the Dreamgod Ascendant and what abilities he would possess.

But fortunately, he didn’t take too long to appear, a few minutes later, a slight distortion in the air before us alerted us that the battle was beginning.

The distortion gave way to figure, that was taking shape molecule by molecule from compressed dream qi, spiritual pressure, and whatever other exotic energies a being who'd touched divinity had learned to manipulate during his ascension attempt.

This was Shen Mohai.

But I couldn’t get a good read on him because his features shifted constantly between different ages and appearances as though I was seeing every version of him that had ever existed overlaid on top of each other in the same physical space.

What really drew my attention was the Crown of Clarity.

Actually, it wasn’t a Crown of Clarity as that was something that signified the Oneiric Sovereign realm.

This crown had evolved into something the records called a Crown of Dominion, and it floated above his head in a way that looked like it was constructed from concepts that he had comprehended throughout his life.

But without a doubt, the worst part was the eyes. It was a well-known saying that the eyes were windows to the soul. As for these windows, they seemed to contain infinite dream realms, which was expected of someone of his rank, but what made my heartbeat faster was the madness swirling behind them.

This was the madness of someone that wanted to devour everything in sight.

"Welcome, disciples of Azure Peak Sect," he said, his voice coming from everywhere and nowhere simultaneously. He was ignoring Xie Ling and Li Na entirely like they weren’t important enough to deserve acknowledgment, his gaze was fixed directly on myself and Wu Kangming, which only made me warier. "You have finally arrived. I have been waiting a very long time for you."
