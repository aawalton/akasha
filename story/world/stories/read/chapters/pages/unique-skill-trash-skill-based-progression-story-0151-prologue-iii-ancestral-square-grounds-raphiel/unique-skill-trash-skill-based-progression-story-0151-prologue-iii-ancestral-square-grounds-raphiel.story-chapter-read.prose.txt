Prologue III

(Ancestral Square Grounds)

(Raphiel Necrotin)

“Chittering.”

Just walking onto the sacred grounds caused the Death Eaters to tremble slightly. A mental connection to his familiar, his own personal Death Eater rang out in his mind, speaking of its displeasure of being here.

With a simple reassuring pulse of life energy, he soothed the Death Eater. Then to help bolster its waning shell, he provided a surge of Necrotic infused energy into its shell, a staple of his bloodline. Also, a sign of just how far they had come as a bonded pair. Originally, when they first met as enemies, his bloodline flames caused damage. It wasn’t until they completed their soul pact and trained together that his familiar first gained a resistance to his bloodline ability and then began to gain power for such lethal flames. A product of his Death Eater having to eat the flesh of a creature recently killed. At the time, Raphiel had not been as skilled of a hunter and had to resort to burning prey with his necrotic flames. Flames that the familiar first avoided but slowly began to eat closer to the burn marks, until it finally consumed everything he provided, even the burnt flesh. That was the day, the day he knew they had fully bonded. And the day he finally found his purpose in life.

To correct the misgivings people had about his pet, his familiar. That was why he suggested this operation today.

The plan was simple, come, observe the crowd, and let his familiar plant the next generation of Death Eaters into the crowd. Planting the seed here, so that when these people eventually died, more children of his familiar would rise up, having a bond to his companion. A bond that would strengthen his familiar, while also ultimately empowering Raphiel to gain his vengeance against house Bloom.

Watching the procession of healers come out, Raphiel watched, his face covered in a mourner’s veil. A good thing, as the look of utter contempt he had for those coming out under the banner of the empire caused rage to rise up in him.

Yet, something was off. As watched and counted, there were four for the empire. Pausing for only a second, Raphiel thought about scanning the newcomers, but ultimately decided against such a tactic. Opting instead to remain anonymous, a good thing as someone else foolishly chose to scan the healers, causing all to turn their cloaked heads into the audience at the very spot where someone else did what he opted not to. There was a glare, and a few guards even pointed out the audience member, following the gazes of the healers and seeing the culprit easily, their eyes still glowing a deep resonating blue.

Shaking his head, Raphiel mentally insulted the person who had done the exact rookie mistake that he himself had almost performed a second ago.

With everyone’s attention drawn away, Raphiel gave a silent but unmistakable pulse of energy to his familiar, beckoning him to leave his shadow and enter the shadow of those he was next to. This was a long process, one that required a minute of contact for a full seed to be established. While partials were enough to convict anyone of being tainted, a full seed was only possible with minutes of contact and luck.

If possible, getting into the soul of a healer would be best, as they have the ability to heal and could hid many of the symptoms of prolonged seed cultivating. But this was the best he had. With such a public spectacle everyone would be present, even authority figures who might otherwise remain hidden behind walls. These were the people that the local Necrotin cabal would target, for they would cause the greatest disruption after their death, when everyone came to pay their respects months later, only to find that the body of their beloved king or queen had clawed their way out of the grave and were now running rampant in their own empire.

While Raphiel could not dispute the effect that such a conversion would have, Raphiel played the longer game. Opting to go for the servants, the cooks, those who were accepted as part of everyday life, but ultimately dismissed. These were his targets, which is why he found himself in the middle of the crowd, letting his familiar choose people at random to infect.

“Shriek,” just entering a new shadow caused his beloved familiar pain. And if anyone watched, they would see the faint traces of rippling bubbles coming from the shadows at their feet. A sign that the Death Eater itself was suffering from contact with the ground. In this case, the purified fair grounds were so saturated with holy energy, that even being in a shadow caused immense pain for his familiar. This was why Raphiel stayed close, trying to have his shadow cover the targeted victim’s shadow, so he could maintain his connection, share his life essence, and help heal the damage that was being suffered.

“It’s okay, just a few more and we can leave,” Raphiel cooed to his familiar. Everything seemed to be going smoothly, then he heard the cry that he had been expecting to happen all along, but came out far too quickly.

“NECROTIN! NECROTIN ASSASSIN!” One guard first called out, only to have the call echo out.

However, the call was from a distance, turning as everyone else in the crowd did, Raphiel looked towards the source of the noise and saw something that made his blood chill, as his familiar feeling his concern and fear through their bond jumped out of the shadow it was possessing and back into Raphiel’s shadow, ready to leave. A plea all but crying for them to leave, rang out in his mind. His familiar sensing the danger that was already revealing itself before him. For there in the middle of the square was a glowing golden-eyed demon slayer, sword raised high to strike out at a cabal member that had been too obvious with their infections.

Turning his head, Raphiel realized that other guards were moving and reacting as well. Realizing his cabal had been caught, Raphiel knew it was now time to go to plan B.
