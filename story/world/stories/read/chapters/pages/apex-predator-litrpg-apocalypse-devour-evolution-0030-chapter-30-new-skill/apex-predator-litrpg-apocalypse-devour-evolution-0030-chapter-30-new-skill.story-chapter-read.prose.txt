With Mal’asha gone, Don was alone in the dungeon again. He still had Attribute points to distribute. Three of them. He’d get to that in a minute. Don also had almost twenty Grimoid corpses to devour. He figured he should actually do it immediately, since he was fairly sure the life force’s potency faded overtime.  He also had an evolution choice for hitting level 10. Excellent. Don was eager to open that interface, but he controlled himself.

He did that just in case. Just in case the System hadn’t locked in the options yet. Just in case Don could squeeze in a new skill, maybe even get some levels on that skill before looking at his evolution options.

Alright, first things first. Gotta eat your vegetables… Or Grimoids. Let’s get Devouring.

Don went over a total of nineteen Grimoids. One by one he used Devour on them. The evolution points were a bit on the lower side, when compared to bears, but that made sense. The Grimoids were weak. Don couldn’t even imagine facing off almost twenty bears, even though the levels were the same. Still, each swamp goblin gave him an average of 22 Evolution Points, which totaled to 418 points. Massive haul.

And since he was literally brute forcing the System, it yielded him an Evolution Trait.

[Evolution trait Grimoid: Consumed]

[Evolution trait Grimoid: Consumed]

[Evolution trait Grimoid: Consumed]

…

[Evolution trait Grimoid: Evolved!]

[You have acquired an Evolution: Poison Resistance (F-grade)][Poison Resistance: Your immune system has adapted to be able to deal better with lesser toxins, venoms and pathogens.] [+2 to Constitution]

“Nice…” Don muttered.

While the evolution was clearly one of the lesser ones, like Night Vision, it was still extremely useful. The Constitution was welcome and since he would eventually go back to hunt the lizards for their evolution, he would get to test if this evolution worked on acid too. As far as Don was concerned, acid was definitely a type of toxin. If it melts your goddamn skin off, it has to count, right?

But now Don was ready for the next phase in his little operation of upgrades. He was filled with vigor. After meeting Mal’asha, he had been filled with excitement again. It was mostly the fight, but her presence didn’t hurt. She was a god. It was possible to obtain such power through the System. Don had to admit it. He wanted it.

He was sure becoming a god wasn’t exactly a picnic, but hell, he was no stranger to effort and pain. The first step was to become as strong as the Dominator and Reaper had been. Level 100. But Don knew that levels were only one part of the System’s puzzle. Which is why he was excited to get started with a new skill.

He kicked one of the drained Grimoids over and plucked a broken spear shaft from the ground. Then he found a clean place and he sat there cross-legged, back against one of the shanty huts.

Don went over the ridiculous fight he had just witnessed. It was really hard to say what had been Attributes, what had been abilities, skills, classes and whatnot. But Don had realized something. If telekinesis was indeed a skill, it could be trained. And while he was sure the Dominator had had all kinds of force multipliers when it had been throwing rocks like bullets, he could imitate that. He had been so tunnel-visioned on leveling, that he hadn’t considered something more than intermittently: deliberate practice.

Yeah, he had been using sprint sometimes just for the sake of it to level it up. But he had just been running mindlessly. He hadn’t leveraged the skill. Really focused, really gotten into the zone, where the magic of growth happens.

Don decided he would do that now. So he looked at the broken piece of wood on his lap and concentrated.

His third eye activated and his forehead tingled. The energy field was all around him. It was subtle and faint, like translucent smoke. But Don could sense it. He could communicate with it. So he reached for it and used his will to wrap around the broken spearhead on his lap.

It was like trying to push into a shoe two sizes too small. Don struggled and found that his Energy was actually ticking down surprisingly fast. For minutes Don concentrated on the spear shaft, willing it to move. He kept sending out waves of commands through the intricate energy field of psychic magic bullshit. Finally, after a good twenty minutes of sweat and expletives, the damn thing nudged.

Don groaned. The relief was real. He reveled in his success and squeezed the good feels out of it before he returned back to focus. After a few minutes of struggle, Don managed to nudge the spearhead again. That did it. The System gave him an A for effort.

[Racial Skill Acquired: Telekinesis!]

[Telekinesis Level 1]

“Fuck yes!” Don said between gritted teeth. However, he was getting into a groove, so he didn’t throw a big party. He locked in.

Now that he had confirmation the skill had registered, he focused again. The faint tingle on his forehead was still there, but instead of feeling like a passive pulse, it was now like the thrum of a live wire. The System had clearly responded. Now that he had the Skill, manipulating the psychic energy was vastly easier. He reached for the spear shaft again, not with hands, but with that third sense—gripping it with his will the way someone might try to recall a parent’s phone number. He exerted mental energy, using focus and intent.

This time, the spearhead lifted.

Just a few inches. It wobbled in the air like a drunk mosquito. But Don grinned. He forced it to stay there, fighting the sensation that it was slipping through some mental fingers he didn’t yet know how to use.

His Energy ticked down again.

[Energy at 92%]

He tried to hold the shaft steady, but it tilted left and flopped to the ground.

Okay. Fine. We’re not throwing boulders yet.

Don was extremely pleased. He was eager to keep practicing. And damn it, he had to. Right now his telekinesis was just short of a party trick. He needed it to be useful for combat. Sure, he could improve his Mental Attribute later, and he would. But practice makes perfect. How did Don know Mental was the stat governing Telekinesis? He shrugged. He just did. It seemed like the System imparted the knowledge to him. It also made sense, just like his Unarmed Fighting got better with Power and Agility.

Don took a few breaths. Then he reached again. Lift. Hold. Drop. Over and over. He cycled it until his Energy dipped under 80%, then he took a break and rested his mind. After a few minutes of deep breathing and muttering obscenities at the floating piece of firewood, he tried again.

By the time he got the damn thing to hover for a full five seconds, sweat was streaming down his face. He pushed for ten. Failed at eight. Tried again. Nine seconds. Almost there. He tried again. Ten seconds. Eleven. Then it dropped. Don had a grin so wide it hurt his cheeks.

Then came the ping.

[Racial Skill Increased: Telekinesis]

[Telekinesis Level 2]

That one felt better than it should. His control sharpened. The shaft lifted faster, held steadier. He started walking it through the air, like he was pulling a balloon on an invisible string. Forward. Back. Side to side. It still dropped sometimes, but not like before.

Alright. Now we’re cooking.

Deliberate practice made all the difference. The skill still wasn’t combat ready, but Don was definitely improving fast. The differences in levels were also very clear. Seemed like some of the skills were a lot more subtle. Stealth was still a mystery to him, but so far it mostly worked. Unarmed Fighting and Sprinting were hard to gauge, because he had an understanding how those should work at a baseline. Besides, increasing his Attributes made him more powerful physically.

But now that Don was only focusing on Telekinesis and not getting stat increases in between, the differences were stark. Don continued to focus. The plan was simple. He would exhaust his Energy until he was at 30%. That was a safe enough margin. Then he would go back to the lizards. Not that the Grimoids were a difficult foe, but if he had to face a horde of them again, it would be risky to be at low energy. The lizards hunted alone, and he might as well go get the Evolution Trait, because the dungeon was clearly getting a lot harder the further inside he went.

Don continued his practice. He switched it out for a blowpipe. But of course he made a game of it. The blowpipe was maybe three feet away from him. He reached for it with his mind. It was like trying to grab a hold of a slimy fish. But as his focus deepened, he managed to sink his proverbial claws into the blowpipe and slowly drag it towards him. Once it was at Don’s feet, he tried levitating it. He tried lifting both the blowpipe and the broken spearshaft at the same time. Immediate failure. His concentration split, and they clattered to the ground like sad toys.

Don cursed and tried again.

He pushed the spear shaft up while nudging the blowpipe an inch on the floor. That worked. Then two. It was like doing squats while balancing plates on his head. But eventually he got it. Both objects lifted. Both hovered. His eyes stung from the strain, but he held them.

The mental fatigue was mounting. His energy was still at a healthy 83%, but his actual, non System-related brain was begging for a break. Don stopped.

Fine. You get one minute. Then we get back to the grind.

Don would not get up before getting another level. And then, he would check his Evolution choices…
