I needed to relinquish this Death Will and see what that was all about. I was expecting something unique, but was trying not to get my hopes up.

But first…

I strolled toward the colosseum, wanting to get a closer look. The bazaar opened up into a wide courtyard in front of the giant arched structure. It was more decorated here, several well-kept garden beds giving off the scent of fresh flowers. A few pools of water rested along the edges of the area. People sat around them, eating or reading.

But none of that interested me at the moment. No, what drew the entirety of my attention was the giant statue standing in the center of the courtyard.

A Reaper, carved in stone with a swirling cloak and a—probably exaggerated—huge scythe, sat upon the back of… A dragon. A real fucking dragon.

Well, a statue of a real fucking dragon.

I stepped closer and found a plaque adorned to the base of the statue.

'Zenith the Great, Savior of Savarine, Skull Reaper. This statue was built to celebrate your commitment to abolishing Sin and to honor your sacrifice. The people of Penumbra are forever in your debt.'

Some ancient hero?

An elderly Penumbran man in a simple robe knelt down beside me and prostrated before the statue whispering a few words, then stood.

"Here to honor the Savior, as well?" he asked.

"I'm kind of new around here. Who was he?"

"The last Death Affinity Reaper, of course. Came this close to ending Sins forever." The man held two fingers close together in front of his eye. "Or so they say."

"What do you mean the last?"

"Ho ho, well there hasn't been one since him. Hopefully someday before my old heart gives out."

That was interesting. Maybe they were rare or something. Could explain Calliope's reaction.

"When did he die?"

"Oh, long before you or I were here. It's been generations."

Generations? Generations!?

There hadn't been a Death Affinity in Penumbra in that long? Until now…

No… Why me?

I backed away from the statue. "Uh. Th-Thanks for telling me."

"Of course. Come back any time. I come here every day. Keeper of the records down at the library, so I know a thing or two." He winked at me. "Renauld's the name."

"Sure. Yeah. I'll… Talk to you later."

I turned and hurried away, back through the bazaar. A mixed sense of dread and excitement swirled inside me. I'd thought I was just another Reaper, but this changed things. Even more… It explained things.

This was why the god spoke to me. Because I was a Death Reaper. Because he expected something of me.

I peered back at the statue. A savior. Someone who could potentially end Sin forever. That's who I was supposed to be.

It's too much.

I was supposed to just be here so I could live my normal life happily. Do what little I could to make the world better along the way.

But this… If it was true—if I was the first Death Affinity Reaper in generations—and if that meant something… It would be all-consuming. I couldn't escape that pressure even by going to Vitria.

I shook my head. My Volition was getting low. That needed to be dealt with before anything.

That Death Affinity Will still rested within my scythe. And one thing was for sure… I was even more interested to see what it would do now.

I made my way toward the nearest pillar. Even among the buildings it was easy to see the beacon of dark light piercing the sky, occasionally flashing with color as other Reapers relinquished their harvested Wills.

It took weaving through a maze of dark alleys to eventually reach the pillar, and I instantly regretted my choice. There were been plenty of other beacons within the city walls, but this had been closest to the market. It was just kind of plopped in the middle of an alley, the buildings on either side designed with curved foundations to arc around the telltale circle of frost around the pillar. Several Penumbrans who appeared homeless slept around the edge, leaning against the buildings.

A single Reaper sat among them. She wore a tight leather bodysuit adorned with a number of straps and buckles with pouches over her arms, shoulders, waist, and legs. A whip looped onto her belt completed the BDMS ensemble. I would’ve assumed she was sleeping if she wasn’t mixing something with a mortar and pestle.

I ignored the strangers and stepped over the threshold of cold air.

The process continued much as it had my first time, with the added anticipation of powering up with a Death Affinity Will. The temperature dropped drastically as I neared the pillar, frozen cobblestones crunched underfoot. I summoned my scythe and held it toward the pillar.

[Will Relinquish Commencing.

Rank: Amber.

Affinity: Death.]

I was prepared for the violent waves of emotion that accompanied the transition this time. I accepted the tingling up my spine, the knot forming in my neck, the blackness closing around my vision…

I reappeared on that galactic overlook, and endless sea of stars spreading out around me. The Amber sphere of the Will I harvested rested in my hand, same as before. Symbols of the different Affinities took shape in the stars.

I immediately found the skull—the symbol for Death.

Here goes nothing…

I held up the orb and it cracked, releasing the mist of energy contained within. It joined with the stars above, glowing brighter.

[Will Successfully Relinquished

Rank: Amber

Affinity: Death]

[Matched Affinity Identified.]

[Initializing skill augmentation…

…

No associated skill identified.

…

Initiating skill selection process.]

I felt myself moving through space on the platform, rapidly approaching the star-forged skull in the sky. It grew larger with each second until it hovered before me like a mountain before an ant. At this vantage, I could clearly see a few of the stars burned brighter than the others. Indeed, as I examined one such star closer, a new text box appeared.

[Affinity: Death

Foundational Skill: Volition Drain

Skill Type: Drain

Effect: Rank Locked

Status: Rank Locked.]

I'm learning a new skill? Now it made sense why Calliope had insisted I fight the Unchained and earn its Will. When it matches your Affinity it improves skills instead of stats. I had a feeling that would be far more valuable in the long run. Though it appeared I didn't have access to all the skills at my current rank. As awesome as Volition Drain sounded, it wasn't available to me as an Amber.

I browsed several more skills, but only two were Amber rank. I examined them closer.

[Affinity: Death

Foundational Skill: Decay

Skill Type: Necro

Effect: Debuffs target's Strength and Defense -10%

Status: Available.]

That sounded useful, especially if I ever had to fight opponents stronger than myself, which had already happened a few times, and would likely continue to happen if Unchained were truly becoming more prevalent. The next option sounded even better though.

[Affinity: Death

Foundational Skill: Life Drain

Skill Type: Drain

Effect: Steals Health from target and replenishes own by 20% of amount stolen

Status: Available.]

I selected Life Drain.

[Initializing New Skill:

Life Drain.]

Amber light from the star burst forth, striking me in the chest, filling my body with warmth.

[New Skill selection complete.]

I felt myself falling backwards, zooming away from the skull symbol in the stars and back to my position in the center. The floor was gone an instant later and I fell into my own body again.

I couldn't fucking wait to try out my new skill. I—

Something smacked me on the back of the head and I hit the cold cobblestones hard. The taste of sticky iron filled my mouth. Blood?

Leather boots crunched on frozen ground beside me.

“Welcome back,” a feminine voice purred.

A long whip slid to the ground and wiggled like a snake.

I tried to get up–to get away–but a firm hand gripped the back of my neck and shoved my face into the stone.

Fuck.

I knew this pillar seemed sketchy.

I struggled, but the woman was inhumanly strong. A cold, wet cloth covered my mouth and nose.

It smelled like sleep…

I woke to blackness.

My eyes were open, but took in so little sight that I was unsure if I’d been blinded or simply in such complete darkness that they couldn't adjust.

Am I in Vitria? My heart lurched hopefully, but dropped as I stood and felt my surroundings.

A rough stone cylinder enveloped me. A cell? But why? Was I robbed? I had practically no possessions for someone to steal.

Or did this have to do with being a Death Affinity Reaper? Did someone know? But why would they attack me?

The prison was tight, just enough room for a single person to stand. I thought about summoning my scythe for defense or comfort, but it would be too long to fit here.

Thank god I'm not claustrophobic…

Though, depending on how long I remained in here, that truth might turn.

I ran fingers along the cold, curved wall again, being more attentive this time. My hands found a rectangular seam and traced around it. It was the size and shape of a potential door, but it wouldn’t budge. No ceiling within reach above either.

Hmm… That sparked an idea.

I spread my legs, straddling them against the curved wall of my cylindrical cell. I raised my hands and pushed against the stone, propping one foot up higher on the wall. Then the other.

I made it a few feet up before I slipped back down.

Three more attempts left my hands scratched and calves sore with no further vertical progress.

I sat down on the hard floor and leaned my back against the wall.

Even returning to Vitria wasn’t an option without a visible shadow. I imagined if it were, I’d respawn at the same lantern as before, but there was no guarantee that still applied if my Umbral Plane body was incarcerated.

The ground beneath me shook briefly.

I jumped to my feet.

Metal gears clanged somewhere nearby. They ratcheted together repeatedly, rotating.

Then the floor began to rise.
