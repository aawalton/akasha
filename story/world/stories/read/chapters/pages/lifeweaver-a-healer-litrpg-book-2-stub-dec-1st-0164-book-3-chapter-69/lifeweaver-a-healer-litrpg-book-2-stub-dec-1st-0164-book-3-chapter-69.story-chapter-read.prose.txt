"Well, shit," Luke groaned. Some arrows had been deflected by his shield woven from mana, but not enough. With holes in both arms, one in his shoulder, one through the left lung, and one piercing his gut, he couldn't hold off on using healing mana anymore, or he might be done for.

Ray: "Let's switch places."

Luke: "Huh?"

Ray: "You're screwed in that matchup, and I'm not doing too hot here either. What's the point of stealth if every second monster can see through it? It's bullshit! Also, the lion is almost dead."

Ray: "Switch places with me."

Luke: "Fine."

Luke gave the archer demon the finger and then threw himself off the building. Threads of Mana with Needle of Life shaped as ice picks worked just as well on the way down, and it didn't take him long to reach Ray, who was running in the opposite direction.

Ray raised his hand over his head. "High five!"

Luke slapped it without taking his eyes off the swordsman, who was in the process of cutting off one of Lemira's lion's limbs. Another arrow flew straight for Luke's back, but Ray jumped up and intercepted it mid-air, like some judo master. Luke would never say it to Ray's face, but it looked kind of cool.

Reaching the swordsman... swordsdemon? Luke threw himself headfirst into the battle in an attempt to get between the monster and the well... monster. That didn't turn out so well. Rather than retreat, the demon heaved and cut clean through the lion's front leg as he ignored the lion's jaw, which clamped down on his shoulder. The long, curved blade didn't stop there. Continuing downward, it bit into the back of Luke's shoulder, and would have cut through him too if not for The Healer's Moment.

The blade still embedded in him, Luke reached out with several threads of mana. This demon's physique was a lot like a human's, with a Meta-Heart and mana channels. Rather than the orange-glowing sludge, the swordsman even had human organs. Was this a half-demon, like Lemira? The red skin told Luke that no, that wasn't it, but he didn't know enough about demons and half-demons to know for sure. What he did know was that having organs and a Meta-Heart was not a good thing when facing a Lifeweaver.

Drawing mana through several channels, Luke breathed an internal sigh of relief at being able to top himself off. While doing so, he pierced several vital organs, including the brain, of his opponent, using Needle of Life formed into a wider point, almost like a screwdriver. The Healer's Moment shuddered, and Luke felt the demon attempting to push him out. That was not something he was about to allow.

This moment was Luke's domain. In there, he made the decisions. In there, he shaped the future. A future without the demon standing over him. Reasserting his will on The Healer's Moment, Luke cut through the demon's main mana channel, severing the Meta-Heart.

Time resumed, and the demon pulled back with a too-human scream, sword still clutched in his hand. Luke ignored the growling lion and stepped toward the demon while a thread of mana sewed his back together, closing the long wound left by the sword.

The swordsdemon put his free hand against his chest with a look of utter bewilderment. "What did you do?" it growled.

Impressed the bastard hadn't just died, Luke pushed on, not wanting to let the demon recover. "I killed you."

"Not dead yet," the demon sneered, just as Lemira charged into him atop her llama, running him down.

"Perish, scum!" she cried, continuing on to the other end of the street before skittering and turning on her mount.

Luke was about to reach forward with threads of mana again when the demon's form blurred and went from sprawled on the ground back to standing. Weaver's Perception told Luke his opponent was healing at a rapid pace. Thin strands of mana connected the severed Meta-Heart to the rest of the mana channels. This one had some sort of self-healing skill. That meant there was no time to lose.

Again, Luke charged forward. It turned out he'd underestimated what the injured demon was still capable of. That much became apparent when Luke's arm dropped to the ground, the demon appearing behind him, blood covering his blade. Even so, it wasn't Luke's complete defeat. In that split second when the demon blurred again, Luke had used a skill for just such an occasion.

Loom's Reminder [Active - Mana Cost: Moderate]: For a brief moment, each touch produces a thread.

One thread was all he needed. The demon was quick and able to heal himself, but that didn't matter much when Luke had the ability to create a literal explosion of mana inside an opponent.

Weaver's Rupture (Rank 2) [Active - Mana Cost: Moderate]: Create a controlled blast of mana to disrupt any who would threaten you.

It had been a while since he had used the skill, and he had grown more proficient since then. This time, he split the thread in two, moving one from the metaphysical plane to the physical, creating two simultaneous blasts, one in the physical heart, obliterating it, and one in the metaphysical.

The swordsdemon died so fast his expression didn't even have time to change from the smug look he'd worn when taking off Luke's arm. Right, the arm.

"Luke!" Lemira was shouting, her face white.

Luke reached down and grabbed the demon's corpse, then the sword, putting them into his inventory. Only then did he reach for his arm. Putting it back just right proved difficult with only the other arm, so he used threads of mana to guide it into place. A good helping of healing mana did the trick once he'd connected the nerves. Weaver's Renewal would have been faster, but this way saved on mana, and the many skills he'd just used had taken a lot out of him.

Testing his hand to make sure all fingers worked the way they should, he turned to Lemira. "Don't worry. This wasn't the first time."

Her eyes were still wide, but his comment made her blink. "First time what? Losing an arm?"

"Yeah," he said, nodding. That fight had taken a lot out of him, despite how brief it had been, but this was far from over. Turning, he saw Ray still fighting against the archer, who had now dropped the bow in favor of two slim blades, since Ray was a lot better at closing in than Luke.

"Should we help him?" Lemira asked.

"I don't know," Luke said. "Maybe the others need it more."

A low rumble from above, like rocks sliding down a mountain, made him look up. He cleared his throat. "Maybe they don't need help."

"Huh?" Lemira asked, eyes still on Ray.

Luke grabbed her arm and weavestepped to the left. "Watch out!"

An enormous demon head thudded to the ground, orange sludge splashing in all directions. Luke threw himself over Lemira, shielding her from the worst of it. The rest of the massive demon's body fell to the side and away from them, crashing into a building before thumping to the ground with enough weight to make the ground shake.

Before Luke had even healed the damage from the rain of burning grossness, they heard a loud wail. It was the bow-wielding demon. Once Ray closed in, it hadn't taken long for him to get the upper hand. Now, his new dagger was embedded all the way to the hilt, the handle sticking out from the middle of her chest.

The weapon glowed bright enough for them to see even at that distance, but it was brief, lasting only until the glow went out of the demon's eyes. Once she'd collapsed, Ray pulled the weapon out, nodded to himself as he looked at it, then looted the now dead demon, even taking her corpse into his inventory.

"That was rough," Curtis said, appearing out of a patch of darkness.

Luke looked over at him. "You can teleport now, too? Like Hannah?"

"Short range only," he grunted as Hannah appeared by his side.

She was again covered in tribal-looking tattoo patterns. This time, they even reached her face, giving her a sinister look.

"Are you okay?" Luke asked, wrapping his arms around her.

After a moment of silence, she reached up and hugged him back. "Yeah."

"You two a thing now?" Curtis asked, sounding and looking unimpressed.

Hannah turned her head to face him. "Kinda?"

"Huh," he grunted.

A blur of motion resolved into Ray, who laughed. "Did you guys see that? That archer asshole didn't stand a chance!"

"Good job," Luke said, letting go of Hannah to take a look around. He'd received a ton of messages during the fight, but hadn't been able to divert the attention to read them. Just as he opened the interface, small droplets of rain fell from the now darkened sky.

Milla (Guild): "Phoenix Phalanx destroyed their demon."

Milla (Guild): "Two more demons spotted."

Milla (Guild): "The lesser demons are becoming a problem. There are too many of them. Need people with AOE to cull the horde!"

Luke watched Hannah pull out a crystal like the one he'd looted off the first greater demon he bested, and was about to send a reply to Milla when she sent another one.

Milla (Guild): "PORTAL OPENING UP IN THE NORTHERN CORNER OF CENTRAL PARK!"

Finished reading it, Luke drew in a sharp breath and met Curtis's shocked gaze.

"Intruders," Curtis said, looking west. "Lots and lots of Intruders."
