Achievement awarded: Skill Forger

Not satisfied with what you have, you are determined to change it. Yet not in the way of your ancestors, by trial and error, exploration and growth. Instead, using an awareness of your inner workings that is rare to see in those governed by the System, you go directly to the problem and fearlessly transform your inner being. Is it skill and good sense or luck that helped you avoid the catastrophe that awaits those who dabble with such things without careful direction? Congratulations, you have been marked for greatness for those who have such eyes as to see.

In recognition of your efforts you have been awarded 10% Wisdom, 5% Intelligence, and 5% Willpower, and a new Skill: Insight (Passive).

My mouth hangs open as I stare at the message and its oddly personal final sentence even as the sudden influx of Energy crashes through my internal matrix like an ocean.

For a moment, even the message hanging before my eyes disappears into a blinding gold-tinged white light as the immensely pleasurable, and equally painful force rearranges my body to suit itself.

And then that moment passes; the Energy subsides. Yet in its wake, I can feel the effect of the rewards – the increased sharpness of my processing, the noticeably increased complexity of my internal matrix, the firming of my hold on my Bonds.

Feeling almost shaky, I pull up my stats.

With that single achievement, I’ve gained three points to Intelligence, five points to Wisdom from the ten percent bonus, and eight points to Willpower – which, of course, means an extra point to Wisdom through the effects of Willpower Leverage plus an extra point from my Willpower Leverage. It’s the equivalent of almost three new levels! In a single achievement! And as percentage boosts, they will keep rewarding me – every ten points I add to Wisdom, or twenty points to Intelligence and Willpower, I’ll earn an extra point, essentially for free.

I shake my head in wonderment. Nicholas won’t believe me when I tell him about this. He told me that achievements are rare – they have specific conditions that are usually difficult or dangerous, and many are only awarded to the first to complete those. And yet I’ve earned quite a few now. Most of those were probably because I was the only one in the samurans’ world to have a Class – it may have awarded me achievements that others had already earned in this world. However, this one is clearly something that no one has achieved.

Why not, I wonder. I can understand that it might not have happened on Moriax – the ignorance there as to what makes up the foundations of Classes is so deep as to make me wonder if someone is intentionally keeping it so. Yet now I know that there are elves on this continent who ‘advance as beasts do’ as well as humans with Classes, I find it hard to believe that no one has ever done this kind of exploration.

Yet this achievement doesn’t seem to be the kind that might be awarded to multiple people – its rewards are too generous for that. Normally, the only achievements that can be earned multiple times are those that only offer a couple of points to one or two stats. This has offered percentage boosts to three stats, and not small ones at that. Moreover, it’s actually offered a whole new Skill! Nicholas didn’t mention that achievements could do that. I suddenly wonder if he knows.

That I’ve apparently been ‘marked for greatness’ is also rather ominous and once more makes me wonder exactly where the System came from at all – and whether anyone is monitoring it. I swallow, hoping that Murphy really is on a long holiday right now.

I concentrate on the Skill name, hoping that there’s a message that tells me what it does.

Would you like to see the Skill description of: Insight?

Yes, I answer as soon as the box replaces that of the achievement.

Insight (Passive)

Your insight into the very functioning of the System that underpins your Class is improved.

Not a very informative description, I note with a touch of exasperation. And ‘passive’? What does that mean?

I pull up my Skills list, finding it at the bottom of my General Skills.

Insight

The most curious thing about it is that it isn’t at Beginner rank. In fact, it isn’t at any rank. Does that mean it can’t advance? I wonder. The only other Skills I have in my list that are like that are ones that are Subskills of other Skills – they advance with the main Skill rather than separately. But Insight isn’t a Subskill – it’s a Skill in its own right.

I decide that I’ll have to just see how it goes. Maybe it will upgrade in ways I haven’t yet experienced. Or maybe I have to make changes directly to its structure, I muse, considering the way in which I earned it. Well, I won’t even consider doing that until I know a whole lot more about what it’s doing.

Pulling my thoughts away from Insight and onto the Skill that was my main focus, I see with pleasure that Bond Teleport is no longer greyed out, but is there in solid black. I also notice that it’s leaped up to the next rank – now at Initiate.

I return to my messages, opening the one that I haven’t yet viewed.

Congratulations!

You have advanced a Class Skill past Novice: Bond Teleport is now Initiate 1. You have discovered a new effect, and improved the functioning of the existing effects.

Due to your greater understanding of how Bond Teleport works through the Bond you have with your companions, the cost of teleportation decreases with the strength and depth of the Bond between you. In addition, due to the channels of Bond Teleport being more efficient, teleportation will take effect faster.

New effect: due to your removal of certain features of the Bond that limit the backlash of a failed teleportation attempt, your Bond Teleport has now become two-way. You may now teleport yourself to your Bonded. Note, you no longer have any protection from the mana backlash if the target of your teleport successfully defies your attempt to teleport them. Also note, if you teleport yourself to one of your Bonded and you do not have enough mana to pay the cost, this will be taken from your target.

I note the changes with overall satisfaction. Most of the features are nothing but improvements – now I can see why teleporting Fenrir to my side was both easier and faster. However, it’s the new effect that really wows me.

I can now teleport to my Bonded, I note with excitement. Not only does that offer me a lot more utility in battle, but out of it too. I can send someone out scouting, then, if they spot something interesting, I can teleport to them to see it. Then, when I’m done, I can teleport back to someone who’s still at the settlement, and then summon my scout. Or…

I practically salivate as I consider how this might interact with my spatial tattoo. If I could bring everyone Bound to me but my scout, I could end up bringing an army behind enemy lines without them noticing. Noir is already a thief Class – if I make sure he gets as many Skills as possible to conceal his presence, he would be a perfect companion to send.

I need to test this. It could be a gamechanger. As long as it doesn’t require me to pay the cost of everyone I’m transporting with me, of course. Though even if it does, it doesn’t mean this strategy is useless. I could always do it more piecemeal – send Noir ahead, teleport to him, and then summon my companions one by one, using potions where necessary to bolster my mana pool and regeneration rate.

But, as I feel a sudden disruption in the network, I decide that I’ll need to do that later. Because the kind of disruption I’m feeling can only mean one thing.

“Someone’s Evolving!” I exclaim, looking at Fenrir who leaps to his feet, tongue lolling out of his mouth with his own excitement.

I touch the Bonds between me and my companions, trying to work out which one the telltale pulses are coming along. When I realise which it is, my breath catches.

“Artio,” I breathe. And I know just where he is. “Come on!” I tell Fenrir and then take off running towards the farm area.

Behind me, I hear a hiss as Fenrir orders Rock Fenrir to follow, and then there’s the clatter of his claws against the stony ground, and the louder pounding of Rock Fenrir behind him.

It takes us only a minute or so to get to where Artio is engulfed with a shroud of dark forest-green sparks. He’s in the edge of the forest closest to the cultivated fields, bridging the wild and the domesticated. I can’t help but feel that that is deliberate.

We’re not the first to arrive – he’s surrounded by a group of fascinated Plant Mages and Farmers. Even a couple of non-Classers are there too, though what they can glean from the proceedings, I don’t know. Maybe they’re just there for the show.

Only a few seconds after I arrive, Lathani blinks into the shadow of a tree, then lies down, watching the sparks whirl with avarice. Storm’s Shadow appears only a moment after, and is quickly replaced by Ninja. She bites at Lathani’s ear, sending her a wave of annoyance for not waiting – Lathani bats her away without taking her eyes off Artio.

I glance back towards the settlement and see several others are on their way – Bastet is flying towards us with Ivor and Noir following her in a V-shape, a humanoid figure is surfing on streaks of ice, and several other smaller shapes are just leaving the gates now.

Looking towards the forest I see that others beat us here who I hadn’t noticed – Sirocco greets me with a chirp from her branch and I move around to scratch Artemis and Orion behind their ears as they lie beneath her.

“Did you know this was about to happen?” I ask them quietly.

Was coming, Artemis tells me with certainty.

Big progress from big plant, Orion adds. Then he muses, Storm helped too.

My hand hesitates for a moment, before resuming my caresses when he nudges it impatiently with his head. The storm. Of course.

I eye Artio with concern – I haven’t checked him since he woke up. What if his channels have been malformed? I admit to myself that I would also dearly love to see another Tier three Evolution – I missed Bastet’s due to the demands of getting our ship free of the Tier five’s influence, so the last I saw was Flower’s back in the samuran village.

“I’m going to make sure he’s fine,” I tell my companions as an excuse. Orion accepts it, but Artemis and Sirocco both give me knowing looks. I send them a flicker of playful challenge through the Bond, but neither says anything more.

Pulling away from the deris, I step into the shroud of whirling sparks, feeling their presence like static against my skin.

Placing my hand on the owlbear’s shoulder, I dive into his Core space.
