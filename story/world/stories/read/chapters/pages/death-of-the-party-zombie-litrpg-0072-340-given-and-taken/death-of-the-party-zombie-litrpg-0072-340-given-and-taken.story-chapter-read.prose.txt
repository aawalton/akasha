His path of destruction had killed thousands upon thousands of roaches, but this area was better guarded than he expected. It was rare that restraints could hold him, magical or mundane. The Radochs just happened to be well versed in technology to disable all manner of powers.

A new army stood before him, just across the bloody waters surrounding the land bridge. At least four thousand strong, made up of elite Radochs. It was an unthinkable amount of power - and that wasn't all.

War engines, massive monsters, and weapon platforms. Clutches of odd creatures being herded about. Several specialist groups that were more powerful than the elites. More than that, beyond Theo's line of sight.

He could be having so much fun tearing through all these new and novel threats, if only he hadn't stepped right into their trap.

All he had to do was wait for them to make a mistake. Then he could break free and attack. The end of all this could be one more rampage away. He bared his fangs as he continued to pull at the chains.

The trouble was, they were ignoring his efforts. He grunted. Waiting was such a drag.
