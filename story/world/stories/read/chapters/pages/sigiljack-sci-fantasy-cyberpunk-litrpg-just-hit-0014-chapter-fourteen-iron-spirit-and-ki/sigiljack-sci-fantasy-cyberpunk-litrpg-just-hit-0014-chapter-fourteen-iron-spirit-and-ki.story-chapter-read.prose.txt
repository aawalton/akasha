The sun hadn’t broken the skyline yet.

Just a pale hush on the ruins. Light brushing the skeletal silhouette of a city that didn’t know it was dead. Steel bones jutted like ribs. Melted girders. Twisted loader arms. The scrapyard stretched out like a graveyard for machines that used to dream.John had absorbed the cores from the mana-ghouls on the way over as Red drove. Turns out Kaito had delivered Red's share to the oni as well. Say what you wanted about the vampire fixer, but he was efficient and timely.

Regardless, the cores brought John's Mana attribute up to the second tier.

Red stepped over a heap of slagged alloy. Grunted. “This place is a shitpile.”

John followed. Boots crunched through rust and broken drone frames. “Yeah. It’s where I learned to fight.”

Red raised a brow. “How the hell didn’t you get tetanus?”

“My aunt paid the doc extra to keep my boosters current. I was always getting into shit I shouldn't, in places I shouldn't. Pretty sure it was that or let me become a biohazard.”

At the center of the wreckage stood the ring.

Not regulation. Not legal. Just welded car hoods bolted into a crooked square, chain-linked ropes sagging between fence posts. Forgotten by city ordinance. Remembered by the few who had grown up under the tutelage of Uncle Benjin--an old retired and poor as hell boxing champion who had trained John, and the other young ruffians, in the sweet science. Benjin had done it for free--on the condition that his pupils stayed out of the gangs and drugs.

John stepped through the chains. “Still here.”

Red followed. Tossed him a cracked pair of MMA gloves. “Barely.”

John tugged them on. “Guess I won’t be losing any teeth this time.”

Red smirked. “Wouldn’t want to ruin that pretty little smile.”

John flexed his shoulders. “Starting to think you just like hitting me.”

“Was that ever a mystery?”

John rolled his neck. “So how do we start?”

“Breathing.”

“…That’s it?”

“To use Ki,” Red said, “you need control. Skill-Energy runs in your body and blood. Mana runs in your circuits. Breath brings them into sync. It’s the bridge. Simple. Reliable. Universal. You fuck that up, you fuck up everything else.”

“Show me.”

Red nodded. Drew a slow inhale. For a moment, nothing changed.

John squinted. “Color me dazzled.”

“That was one Ki point.”

Red inhaled again—slower. And again. And again.

This time, the air around him flickered. Not light. Not aura. Just… distortion. Like heat-haze on asphalt. Candle-glow bleeding out from his skin.

“Now try to hit me.”

John eased into stance. Bounced on the balls of his feet. “Seen this before.”

Red didn’t posture like a boxer. He rooted. Thai stance. Compact. Coiled.

John jabbed. Fast and crisp. Red’s shimmer of aura caught the hit itself, slowing and smothering it, giving the oni just enough to pivot. Into a side-kick that landed high and hard on John's shoulder.

John grunted. Triggered [Body Blow Lv. 2] on reflex—drove a shot to Red’s ribs.

The punch connected.

But it didn’t land right.

Red’s Ki blurred the impact again, bled it off. His counter smashed John in the ribs with the opposite foot as before.

John staggered. Sucked air. “Two kicks. In a boxing ring? Come on man."

“You’d’ve slipped a punch.”

John rolled his shoulder. “You’ve used that slow-aura trick before. Seen it.”

“Ki-enhanced Iron Strike,” Red said. “Usually just boosts stats. With Ki? It becomes defense too.”

He stepped forward. “Now watch what happens with Death Clinch.”

Red surged. The afterimage of his movement left behind like a smear of ghostlight. And he did indeed clinch John.

John triggered [Clenching Tactics]—slid, shifted, denied leverage.

Didn’t matter. Red moved like gravity was on his side. More force in every twitch than should’ve been possible, even for him. His aura itself also proved slippery, making it hard for John to get a grip, even with his Skill designed to help him do just that.

“You’re augmenting your Skills.”

“And upgrading their effects.”

John grunted and launched a liver shot—[Body Blow] again.

Red flared Ki. Diffused it once more. And side-hooked an elbow into John’s jaw.

John reeled. Slung an uppercut to break the clinch on his way out.

Red, still protected by his aura, barely flinched. Took a breath. Punched at John as he retreated.

John activated [Hardbody] right before the impact.

Didn’t matter. Still got rocked by the ki-punch as it rushed into him and then diffused over his ribs.

John wiped his mouth. Blood again, from the elbow before. “System’s unfair. You already get more out of Body because you started bigger. Ki just turns your stat block into a cheat code.”

“Impressed?”

“Useful,” John admitted. “How much can you use it though?”

“Depends on control. You burn Skill-Energy and Mana to make Ki. How much you can hold, how fast you recover--that’s all in Ki Manipulation.”

“So how do I start?”

“Feel your skill-energy in your muscles. Feel your mana in your circuits. Breathe deep. Bridge them in your core with the air you suck in.

John inhaled.

Focused.

Mana still buzzed like foreign static.

But his body knew Skill-Energy. Knew how to call it.

He felt her in his lungs. In his diaphragm. Breathing for him—more efficiently than he ever had.

Skill-Energy flowed into his torso, bleeding out of other muscles. Mana followed, changing its flow to go where Athena willed it. For the first time… they met.

[Ki Points Gained: 1 (1/4)]“We were successful,” Athena said. “Optimizing protocol.”

John smirked.

“How long did it take you to learn Ki?” he asked Red.

“Weeks. Don’t feel bad.”

John nodded. “Once I’ve got Ki, I just… use it on Skills?”

“Any physical one. Some mental. Just takes control.”

John grinned.

Tugged off a glove.

Held up his fist, as if about to demonstrate something.

[Ki Points Remaining: 1]His skin glowed faint. A shield of energy. But that wasn’t the point. The power wasn’t around him—it was in him.

In tendon. Muscle. Bone.

He flexed. Felt like steel woven in flesh.

Red blinked. “What the hell did you just do?”

Athena materialized on the ring’s edge, arms folded. Smug. “Further advancement will require sparring experience and upgrading our Attributes.”

John raised a brow. “Guess I’ve got something you don’t.”

“You little shit,” Red laughed. “Proud of you. Now put your glove back on. Been a while since I had someone with real Ki to spar.”

John grinned. Tugged the glove back. “Nah. You just lost your edge, Kai. You’re not hitting anybody.”"You're welcome, John," Athena commented--smiling.

For the next hour, the scrapyard echoed with fists, curses, and joy. Grit and hits and ki traded under the bleeding sky. Until the morning broke free of the night in earnest.
