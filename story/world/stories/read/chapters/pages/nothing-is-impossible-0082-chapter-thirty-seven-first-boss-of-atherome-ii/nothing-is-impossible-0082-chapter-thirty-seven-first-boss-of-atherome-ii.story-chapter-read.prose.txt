A glancing blow that just about ripped its arm off.

Atlas jumped forward again, stabbing 4 times in quick succession. The Wolfman managed to phase through each of them before retaliation, knocking Atlas’s spear upwards and slashing out.

Atlas quickly changed his spear to a halberd before slashing down as he backpedaled quickly, slamming into the Wolfman's outstretched paw and cutting it down to a bleeding stump.

The Wolfman jumped backwards and healed again before dashing back into the fray. They fought for a few more moments, with Atlas dealing damage before the Wolfman just shrugged it off.

Atlas was losing stamina faster than his heightened regeneration could give him. If there was one bad thing about his Voidborn Archdragon skill, it was that it was a stamina hog, and he would already have been sucked dry if not for his Void Gloves, which gave him 5,000 stamina every time he dealt damage to the Wolfman.

Atlas tried and failed to attack the Wolfman while it was healing, but found it basically impossible to do so. It was just too good at timing its phases and avoided him every time he beat it.

Atlas decided to take a gamble, and quickly changed his halberd to a javelin, throwing with all the force he could muster at the Wolfman, sprinting right after it.

The Wolfman phased through the javelin, and it kept soaring until colliding with a nearby tree. But in its focus to dodge the projectile, it didn’t notice the human trailing after it, and Atlas’s gloved and armored fist connected solidly with its face, sending it flying straight into the tree with the javelin that was stuck in it. It slammed into the butt of the javelin and cried in pain, only for Atlas’s fist to slam into its face again, sending it straight back down into the javelin.

Atlas quickly grabbed the javelin and stabbed it, going straight through the Wolfman's eye, and directly into its brain, killing it.
