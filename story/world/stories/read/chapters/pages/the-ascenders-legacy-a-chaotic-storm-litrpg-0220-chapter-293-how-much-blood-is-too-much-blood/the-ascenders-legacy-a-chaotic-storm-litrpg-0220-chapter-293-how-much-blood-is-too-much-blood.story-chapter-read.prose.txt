They were a threat to the group whether they wanted to be or not. The handlers would never forget to utilize them. What if Serythion showed up and they attacked?

That would ruin what little trust the dragon had placed in them, destroying their entire plans and completely eliminating any chance of survival they may have had.

It wasn't the best solution, but it was better than killing them. This way at least, they could be managed.

A shriek split the air in that moment, closer now than the last time, and the tension in the air suddenly sharpened.

"I apologize for the disorientation you currently feel, but it was important that you were up to speed, and that was the fastest way in which we could do it. I cannot answer your questions—there's no time for that—but I urge you to believe them because they are true, and they could be our only hope of survival out of this place."

Another shriek split the air, even closer this time, and with a grimace, Aodhán launched himself into the air, wishing he could explain more than he had. But he couldn't. He would just have to hope they were smart enough to believe him.

Inhaling deeply, he raised both hands to the sky and activated Storm Creation. Thunder boomed as a colossal cloud formed—smaller clouds roiling and gathering, growing as huge amounts of energy and willpower were funneled into the skill.

Lightning flashed—gold and white, lighting the sky in short bursts of color and light. Winds howled, picking up dust and debris to form mini-tornadoes, and even from his height in the sky, Aodhán could hear the awed whispers of those below as they watched him conjure a storm that could potentially wipe out an entire city if left unchecked and unhindered.

It was a testament to how powerful he had become that he wasn't even using his full power, but that was intentional. He was saving his strength for something else. And that something else came an instant later when a clap of thunder—so deafening it split the sky—suddenly ripped through the air.

The storm exploded instantly, a deluge of rain pouring down to drench all those below in crystal-clear water. Winds howled violently, and lightning arced, making it completely impossible to keep his eyes open.

Fortunately for all concerned, Aodhán didn't need to see for the next step.

He simply unleashed the force of his spirit, and chaotic essence surged into him in response to his intent—so much so that he felt the Origin Plane bend slightly toward him—so close it almost breached the veil of reality. And then from within his core, willpower surged up like a tidal wave—merging seamlessly with the chaos pouring into his spirit to create a pulsing mass of black-gold quintessence brimming within his spirit like an explosive on the verge of going off.

But Aodhán simply forced it under control with a burst of even more willpower.

Ever since his evolution to the advanced class, the capacity of his spirit had increased drastically. The amount of energy that had nearly torn him apart at the Steppin' Plains now seemed like child's play, and without hesitation, Aodhán drew in more chaotic essence.

Energy clashed and warped within him, threatening to explode as the energy grew to dangerous levels. But Aodhán wasn't satisfied yet. He wanted to give this moment his all, and he wasn't nearly done yet.

Without hesitation, he harnessed the quintessence of two full seals and gasped sharply as heat exploded within him like an inferno, blazing so hotly that his skin hissed with smoke. The quintessence merged with the pulsing mass of chaos and willpower in his spirit—forming a bright gold liquid so dense it moved like sludge.

A thunderous roar split the sky in that moment, reverberating through the air in its intensity. And then another came, familiar, less intense, but no less powerful.

Varéc and Serythion were on their way.

Aodhán grunted in shuddering relief, his body shaking as he fought to keep the energy under control within his spirit. And the instant it steadied, Aodhán channeled it straight into his newest skill.

Tempest of Transformation.

And its effects were absolutely mind-blowing!

Blistering energy exploded out of him and rushed straight into the storm, the beam so bright it was blinding. Thunder cracked the sky in response. Lightning flashed. Reality warped.

And in an instant, as if someone had suddenly pressed pause, the world stilled.

He could sense the awe and anticipation of those gathered below him—he could smell their fear, and even taste their terror.

The overwhelming sense of awareness left him immediately, replaced by an aching sense of weakness as he lowered himself back to the ground, watching as the golden drops splashed against the heads of their intended targets.

Aodhán let out a shuddering breath. He had given it his all.

Now, he only had to hope it would be enough.

The spasming bodies of the others as a single droplet took their abilities and amplified them in an instant—Mirith saw it all with crystal clarity.

There were a few insights she still needed to gather. Knowledge she needed to gain.

But the world, it seemed, had a crueler sense of timing.

Mirith had no idea how it had suddenly appeared so close when it had been miles away a few seconds ago, but she didn't get the chance to question it as the phoenix's form suddenly blurred, a huge wave of blood spilling out of it in an instant.

Mirith reacted immediately, palms reaching out to unleash a blistering beam of raw telepathic essence.

But she wasn't the only one.

Even as she reached out, Aodhán, Baxter, Monica, Daruk, and Serenya also did the same—energy, quintessence, and willpower surging out of them in an all-out attack.

The phoenix shrieked as it lost control of its flight and thought patterns immediately, flailing as its fragmented mind tried to piece an action together.

Monica didn't give it a chance.

I GAIN STRENGTH TO GIVE IT.

No! He screamed in his mind, realizing that the phoenix no longer wanted to kill them itself.
