"All able-bodied guild members, come up here!"

The True Hero, Voxx demanded. A collection of thieves, assassins, and Night Stalkers rose to the occasion. A few of the guild members looked towards Voxx, confused as to why a random Foxian was behind him, though.

Voxx shrugged and waved his hand dismissively, bringing their attention back to him. There was a group of twenty-five, uninjured fighters who walked up to Voxx and the trio. The rest were tending to the lost citizens and other hurt guild members.

"I think we have the manpower to get it done, Voxx."

Kae said, eagerly.

"Are you insane, fox?"

The True Hero didn't take her confidence kindly. But Kae just shrugged.

"No, I'm just a good player, and I have a great plan."

Kae tapped her finger to her chin and thought about the past. She'd soloed a version of Raha on her overpowered main, Ray Belmont. So, she knew the rhythm of the fight like the back of her hand. But just like any Fallen Angel battle, more people made the fight manageable.

"Do you really believe Raha is the cause of this, Voxx. Could there be anyone else that we're overlooking?

Palona asked from the back.

"I do. I've dealt with Raha's magic personally. That Fallen Angel is the source of this chaos."

He nodded, no hesitation. But even so, he just didn't want to risk the lives of those they had already risked to protect. Despite that, though, the Night Stalker knew that going in circles was only going to lead to their doom.

"Then if you're confident it's Raha, we strike to take him out before it's too late for both Chalaco Village and the guild. So, we'll have to build a raid team to do it!"

Kae clenched her fist in excitement as she brought out her notes.

"Raha will likely have the same buff and blessings as Perry, [Enlightenment], so we have to be prepared for a longer, harder battle too."

Voxx closed his eyes and sighed deeper than he'd had in a long, long time. This was the moment he had been dreading, one that would put his friends and family in danger.

"Men and women of the Thieves' Guild, we've come to the conclusion that Raha, the Anubis of Death, may be controlling Perry and the darkness of the Chalaco Desert."

Their leader said, voice in a low but determined tone.

"Raha, the Anubis of Death?"

A female Night Stalker asked. She had short black hair and an eye patch over her right eye. Her clothing was tattered, likely from earlier battles. Even from a distance, the trio could tell she was a skilled, seasoned veteran of battle, too.

"I see, so we'll have to face him if we want to survive? And it's likely he'll be just as strong as that bird out there?"

Her leader closed his eyes and nodded sharply.

"That's right, Gaia. But don't worry, we won't go out there mindlessly. We'll come up with a plan, so prepare to leave for the Dungeon of Death when ordered! Especially you, Gaia, you will be our guide in enemy territory."

Voxx ordered, and with that, the group all scattered, preparing for the battle ahead. The True Hero turned to the fox.

"Alright, I'm up for suggestions, Kae Loredom? If I object to anything, though, we change things. In the end, I call all of the shots, alright?"

The Foxian happily accepted that. Voxx was the True Hero in the end. Excited, Kae stepped up to the plate.

"First off, Dareth, we're going to turn you into our meat shield. You need armor and skills that make you soak hits. If the game calculates defense from gear and VIT, we'll pump your defenses high enough that you can hold the line."

"Ah, you want me to redistribute my stats, huh? Sure, I'll do that. But I'm still at a lower level..."

"That's right, but you are also a Trade Baron. Your class line is made for tanking hits. The Thief class isn't, despite how high a level they are. So, this is where you will shine. It's the only way we can have a chance to win against higher-level monsters."

The Trade Baron didn't disagree while the fox snickered as she continued. Her excitement was overflowing. Fighting high-level mobs at low level was one of the many excitements of an RPG player. Winning by strategy alone rather than power, this was the kind of game and world she wanted Keostar to be.

"Raha's monsters are weak to fire and ice. Electricity and earth won't work here. Palona and I can exploit that."

The four elements (main) are important in this fight, and the veteran knew this.

Fire

Water

Wind/Electricity

Earth

Knowing how to exploit that is the key to fighting higher-level monsters in Keostar. Voxx crossed his arms, skeptical.

"I'm sorry to burst your bubble, fox, but you all don't strike me as strong enough to deal with a Fallen Angel of this caliber. How do I know you won't all die inside the temple the minute you get in?"

"You don't, but that's the chance I'll just have to take, Voxx."

Her candid pride was mind-boggling. She approached the Night Stalker, who was still wary of where all her confidence was coming from.

"You are one of the True Heroes of Keostar. That means you at least have the skill [Assassinate], am I right, Voxx?"

"I do..."

Assassinate is a skill based on LUK and resistance exploit. Its main skill can instant-kill a high-level mob, but not Fallen Angels. One of the best things about the Raha fight, though, is that the player isn't fighting against a Fallen Angel; they are fighting against mobs. And that is where Kae Loredom was going to exploit this fight.

[Basic]

[Higher]

[Elite]

Three classifications of monsters.

'[Assassinate] has the chance to kill [Basic] and [Higher] tier monsters. All of Raha's monsters fall under [Higher] aside from the last one he brings out."

Kae walked over to the Knight, arms crossed, in the corner.

"Palona, Knights can enchant weapons with elements. Do you have those skills yet?"

"I do, Loredom. Will that help?"

Then, it clicked for the Knight what the crazy Foxian wanted to do.

"Wait, are you going to have me enchant Voxx with the elements we need to [Assassinate] each of Raha's monsters?"

"That's the goal. To boost Voxx's chances of [Assassinate] activating, we're going to use our low-level [Fire] and [Ice] spells to lower the enemy's resistance. Voxx will be the True Hero to shine in this fight, though."

Voxx smirked at the thought of it all. His lips reached past his scarf.

"Using me as a glass cannon. Only there to strike and kill when Raha's monsters' resistance is lowered."

He sighed, but the grin on his face didn't wane. It reminded him of working with Ashley, the crazy True Hero who gave him a run for his money all those years ago.

"That might work, as long as you all survive to keep the pattern going."

Voxx praised. Kae wagged her tail as she walked back and forth. She probably wouldn't say it, but she's likely walking around for that sweet grandfathered stat eventually.

"The hardest part will be getting into the chambers..."

Kae explained, and Voxx picked it up.

"My twenty-five men will make short work of the chambers. The fight to get to Raha can be tougher than his actual battle. I'll tweak your plans a bit more, Kae, to ensure everyone makes it out alive."

Voxx took Kae's notes. The plan was coming together. Dareth was redistributing his stats, he laughed out loud.

"Ah man, this is the kind of stuff I like about these kinds of games. Fighting this Fallen Angel is going to be great!"

Even he, the guy who said he was bored with the game, was lighting up with the idea of facing a challenge too high for his level. It showed Kae that even someone like Dareth could have "fun." Despite how dangerous this will be for her and Palona.

The assassin lowered his scarf, understanding the rules Kae wanted to exploit. He was amused as he asked...

"Who are you, Fox?"

"Just a Foxian who's been in this world a little too long."

She shrugged, feeling confident in her plan. The True Hero was enjoying reading through her thorough work. He found holes in places, so it wasn't perfect, but that's what his experience was for.

So, for the time being, Kae, Palona, and Dareth went about their own way. The Knight still needed to rest, so she lay down on a rough-looking bed.

"Are you okay, Palona? Am I pushing you too much?"

Kae asked as she sat down next to her.

"I'm fine. My energy is coming back. I have to remember, I'm not in the body of a Goddess anymore."

She turned over, looking up at Kae.

"It's strange, I know of the world... but hearing you speak about different mechanisms within it... It's like I don't know what truly lies beneath this place I've watched over for years."

Kae couldn't help but smile softly.

"I just... played this game a little too much, is all. Maybe if I didn't... my life would have been a little different."

Before Palona could speak up and ask Kae what she meant, the Foxian got up and made her way out of the central chambers, back into the round table room where Voxx and the rest were fixing up her plan. She was too tired to follow and knowing that Kae made her a central part of this operation, Palona had to rest.

"Huh... that's the first time I heard a bit of sadness in her voice. Sometimes, that fox doesn't sound human. As if she was made for this world."

Exhausted, the Goddess in flesh closed her eyes and fell asleep.
