We slammed into the waves, and I felt Ali slip off my shoulders with a loud curse.  Pressure works a bit differently underwater than it does in the atmosphere, something I should have considered before depth-charging the Raft with one of Jace’s toys.

With a solid lungful of air, I could dive for a pretty long time, but I wouldn’t be able to compete with what was going on below.  Snarling tangles of lutrakin swarmed the larger forms of the merkin tribes.

Orcinis, with his stubby arms being largely decorative, was simply snatching any otters unlucky enough to go anywhere near his mouth. His monocle flashed red, and a bubble of steam shot away to fry an otter trying to organise another attack on the big target.

We were hilariously outnumbered.  For every merkin, there must have been ten otters.  Squads of the cute little monsters coordinated to distract and harass as their brothers and sisters swooped in on the flanks of our larger fighters.  Those three-pronged metal claws the lutra favoured left deep cuts and patches of pink water in their wake.

Something slammed into my chest, and I coughed a bubble of air.  Dropping my tails down low, I brought my wings together and sent a shockwave of water at the ballista I’d seen before as a pair of pale brown otters tried to reload it.

“Conjurare Barnaculum!” I gargled and smiled in satisfaction as the weapon and the orifices of its users were covered in crustacea.

I glanced up to see Ali doggy paddling back and forth, largely ignored in the underwater melee, then dove down.  How the hell was I supposed to find the bloody king in all this chaos?

My heat vision was coming into its own.  Despite the thick insulation their fur provided, the lutra were all yellow or faintly orange in the strange sense.  My allies tended to be more ambient, but the disturbances in the heat flows as they moved gave me a rough idea of where they were.

“Missa Vivica!” More bubbles escaped my mouth to vanish above me.

I’d been very careful with my focus when I cast the mass resurrection spell.  Accidentally bringing your allies screaming back through the veil to inhabit their own corpses was not an effective technique for making friends.

Without having to issue an order, my new zombie-otters spread out and flicked through the battle, not looking to fight but searching for the king.  Somehow, I knew in my bones what they were doing.  I fought on, largely serving as bait for the organised groups of lutra to attack.

The wing-clap move was very effective, and more than once, I stunned a group that was then brutally murdered by a passing walrus-bloke or simply gobbled up Orcinis.  The little claws just skittered off my scaly armour when they got close, and none of them were stupid enough to go anywhere near my snout to try and attack my eyes.

More harpoons clattered around the base of my neck.  They were focusing fire to try to break through my ARM stat.  I snorted, losing more air, and immediately regretted it.

Glorious attempts to take me from behind were not a wise move either.  There must be some kind of sub-brain for each of my tails.  The kraken theme also helped.  Any attempts to stab me in the ass were met with swats that sent the brave otters tumbling through the water.  However… they were learning.

They were ganging up on the merkin.  Small groups aimed to distract Orcinis and myself, while the rest began to slaughter the merkin.  Everscale was a flicker of bluey-green, constantly moving, lashing out with a slim dagger in each hand.

“Conjurare Barnaculum!”  More air lost, but it was worth it.  I used my crusty, trusty little summons to blind as many of the living otters as I could, and the tide began to turn back in our favour.

“Lord.”  An undead otter announced himself as he swam in front of my face, almost getting eaten for his troubles.  I closed my jaws and pulled back my snout.  My tails were swirling behind me, slapping otters as they made another attempt to distract me.  “We have him.”

He turned and dove, uncaring that half his face was missing.  I flicked my wings and began to flap them, swimming quickly into the darkness below.

Everything was cold down here.  But there were levels of coldness that still made the thermal vision useful.  A tangle of yellow shapes was duelling with ones that were very much room temperature.  At the centre, there was a yellow shape with a glowing red worm poking out of its head.

The otters were furiously engaged with their former brothers and sisters, but zombies don’t care if you stab them in the guts.  Being dead kind of inures to any worries about dying, for some reason.

I cut through them like a shark through a school of mackerel, snagging the king in a pair of tails and breaking for the surface.  Fresh air!  A spike of agony erupted in my derriere, and I wrapped another tail around the furious otter monarch.
