[Time]: 11:45 AM[Location]: Milan'thir · East District · Abyss Magma Hotpot

The air solidified for three seconds.

Rhode was whistling, intently examining the ceiling fan. Lin was focused on studying the magical patterns on her fingernails as if they held the secrets of the universe.

Only Alucard, with those dead, grey dragon eyes, stared silently at Hathaway. The heavy White City Public Order & Punishment Code in her hand looked ready to smash down at any moment.

Hathaway took a deep breath. Her rationality told her: The woman in front of you might look like an overworked corporate slave who is one spreadsheet away from cardiac arrest, but her last name is Milan'thirskaya. The floor tiles in her house are probably worth more than my entire net worth. Sympathizing with her is practically an insult to capitalism.

However.

Looking at Alucard’s dragon horns, which were drooping slightly from chronic overwork, and seeing the exhaustion so thick it couldn't be dissolved even by high-grade potions, the soul of the "Earth Corporate Slave" inside Hathaway trembled.

The pain of not wanting to fill out reimbursement forms... the agony of approval workflows... I understand. I understand it too well.

"...I'll pay."

Hathaway’s voice was trembling, and her fingers closed on the cold, hard object in her pocket: the Black Gold Magic Crystal Card. According to the fragmented memories, this month's "Family Trust Fund Dividend" had just arrived yesterday.

It was a massive sum. Enough to buy a small island in the mortal world. But in the White City? It was just enough for a young lady's monthly expenses on dresses, alchemy cosmetics, and gacha games.

And now, this fresh, warm allowance was about to be wiped out before she even spent a single coin on herself, not because she bought something nice, but because she fell from the sky and smashed a sidewalk.

The card felt like it weighed a thousand tons. Hathaway felt like she wasn't taking out a card, but surgically removing her own kidney without anesthesia.

Heartache. Physical, literal heartache.

Her soul, the one that had once reconciled a paycheck down to the cent, was screaming in agony:This is 30,000 Gold Solars! Do you know what this means? This isn't just pocket money! I could have bought a limited-edition, customized Broomstick with a high-performance mag-lev engine! I could have whaled a C6 character with R5 signature weapons in ten different gacha games!

And I'm handing it over just to save a Dragon Witch from filling out paperwork?!

But she handed the card over anyway, the movement slow and jerky, like a robot lagging in slow motion.

"H... Here..." Hathaway gritted her teeth, squeezing the words out.

A faint light finally ignited in Alucard's dim eyes. It wasn't gratitude. It was the relief of "I don't have to write a 10,000-word incident report."

Her palm, covered in fine silver scales, pinched the other end of the card. "Thank you," Alucard said concisely, preparing to pull the card away.

It didn't move.

Alucard looked up. Hathaway was still maintaining the "handing over" posture. Her face wore the serene smile of a Compassionate Saint, but her fingers, those pale, slender fingers, were clamping onto the Black Card with enough force to crush rock.

Her knuckles were white. Her fingertips were embedding themselves into the Mithril plating of the card.

"...Miss Hathaway?" Alucard tugged tentatively.

Still immovable. Hathaway's entire body even leaned forward with the pull of the card, like a fly stuck to flypaper.

"Huh? Oh... H... Here..."

Hathaway said "here" with her mouth, but her hand was applying force in the opposite direction. Forty-two thousand M-Units of body reinforcement had opinions about letting go. Somewhere below language: Let go? In your dreams! Unless you chop my fingers off!

Alucard sighed and put down her chopsticks, getting a little serious. As a Dragon Witch (albeit a corporate slave one), her physical strength was catastrophe-class.

"Miss Hathaway," Alucard reminded her politely, her voice laced with helplessness. "Please let go. If you crush the card, the replacement process takes three working days. By then, you will have been running inside the 'Municipal Mana Turbine' for seventy-two hours."

She leaned in closer, whispering the true horror. "And since the turbine drains mana to generate electricity, you won't be able to use body reinforcement. It will be seventy-two hours of raw, merciless cardio."

The word "Cardio" pierced through Hathaway's miserly defenses. It triggered the primal trauma buried deep in her memories: The Pacer Test. That relentless beep... beep... getting faster and faster until your lungs tasted like copper and your legs turned to jelly.

Hathaway shuddered. A single tear of regret, for the lost money, slid from the corner of her eye. Then, one by one, with agonizing difficulty, she pried her own fingers open.

Swish.

The card was finally in Alucard's hand, and Hathaway collapsed back into her chair, clutching her chest, feeling like her soul had been hollowed out.

Goodbye, my money. Goodbye, my happiness. But at least... goodbye, cardio.

"Swipe it." Alucard handed the card to the Octopus Witch waitress who had been waiting for ages. "Include the repair fees for the two craters, and..." she pointed to the unfinished pot of red oil, "this table's bill. Put it all on this card."

Beep.

Then the card reader, cheerful and mechanical:

[Payment Successful! Remaining Balance: 250 Gold Solars. Have a magical day!]

Hathaway read the number twice.

Two hundred. And fifty.

"Haa..."

Alucard let out a long breath, and Hathaway watched the Archon make an expression she had not made once all lunch.

It was the look of someone laying down a burden of a thousand pounds. Her dead-grey eyes actually cleared, regaining a sharp, lucid focus, and even the silver dragon tail behind her, which had been lifelessly dragging on the floor, gave a gentle, happy tap against the ground.

"No public account process, no 'Application for Financial Allocation regarding Sudden Damage to East District Streets', no need to look at the sour faces of those old hags in the Council..."

Alucard muttered to herself, the corners of her mouth curving into a very faint arc, then stood, placed the card back on the table, and slid it in front of Hathaway. Then, with great solemnity, as if bestowing a medal of honor, she patted Hathaway on the shoulder.

"Hathaway von Ludwig," Alucard said seriously. "Although you are a legally illiterate litterbug (jumping off buildings), you are a humanitarian hero. Next time you want to jump, please notify me in advance. I will mail the fine directly to you, cutting out the middleman."

With that, the Archon straightened her tie, grabbed the thick law book, and turned to leave. Her back looked at least five centimeters straighter than before. That was the power of Money (someone else's).

Hathaway weakly retrieved her card, looking at the pathetic "250" on the holographic display, her heart bleeding.

Just then.

"Wow! Generous!"

Rhode revived. She took off her sunglasses and looked at Hathaway with a face full of emotion. "Since someone is treating, we won't hold back! Boss! Ten more plates of Marbled Frost Dragon Meat! Top grade! My little cousin is rich anyway!"

Lin also elegantly raised her hand. "I'll add to the order too. I heard the Polar Ice Marrow Pudding here is excellent. Pack twenty servings for me; I want to take them home for my cat."

"..."

Hathaway looked at these two seniors who had transformed into gluttonous beasts, and her vision went dark.Why did I sympathize with that dragon? I should have sympathized with my own wallet!

Her hand went up on its own, trembling, and the words came out with it. "Bring me one too! No! Two servings! I need to eat my money's worth!!"

Wait.

Hathaway's hand stayed in the air. A cold sweat broke out on her forehead.

Ten plates of Dragon Meat... Twenty servings of Pudding... plus two for me...Can 250 Gold Solars cover this?Oh no.If I can't pay later... do I have to wash dishes here for the next ten years?!

Amidst the steam of bubbling inferno, Hathaway finally understood a fundamental truth:In this world of Witches, kindness is a luxury good.And being broke is the start of a tragedy.

[Time]: 12:30 PM[Location]: Abyss Magma Hotpot -> Street

"Total is 2,800 Gold Solars."

The Octopus Witch waitress smiled sweetly, holding out the crystal terminal. Her six tentacles were already cheerfully packing the twenty servings of takeaway pudding.

Hathaway stared at the terminal, then at the "250" on her card balance, and cold sweat went down her back.

Two thousand eight hundred. I can't even afford the tip.

"Um..." Hathaway's voice trembled. "Do you accept... washing dishes as payment? I'm very good at scrubbing pots. I used to clean up bugs in code for a living, pots should be easier..."

"Step aside, rookie."

Rhode rolled her eyes, pushing the panic-stricken Hathaway away. She didn't take out money. Instead, she grabbed the stylus and swiftly signed a flamboyant signature on the terminal's screen. The moment she lifted the pen, the signature ignited into golden flames and vanished into the crystal.

[Transaction Approved: Ludwig Main House - Monthly Settlement]

"Listen up, Hattie," Rhode patted Hathaway's shoulder earnestly, like a mentor teaching a disciple a secret art. "In the White City, taking out cash is something only tourists do. As a Ludwig, your face is your wallet. Just sign it to the family account. As long as you don't buy a warship, the accountants at the Main House won't even blink."

Beep.

"Signature Confirmed. Identity Verified. Thank you for your patronage, Lady Ludwig!"

Hathaway's raised hand was still hovering where the terminal had been.So... I didn't spend money.I just... increased my future debt?

She understood why the credit card bill in her memory was always astronomical. This spending habit is a bottomless pit!

[Five Minutes Later, Outside the Restaurant]

Three Witches walked out of the hotpot shop, Hathaway clutching her stomach, her jaw still working through the last of the dragon meat. She had eaten back as much as she could, literally stuffing herself to minimize the loss. Her mana stomach was currently processing enough energy to power a small village.

"Alright, lunch break is over."

Lin wiped the corner of her mouth, her mood visibly improved after securing twenty servings of ice pudding for her cat, and her gaze shifted from 'looking at a walking wallet' to something sharper: 'looking at a raw gemstone.'

"We have an hour before afternoon classes start." Lin checked the time on her wrist crystal. "Since you've treated us to such a lovely meal (on credit), it's time for the first lesson."

"Lesson?" Hathaway blinked, a bad premonition rising in her gut alongside the indigestion. "What lesson? Don't we just go back to the dorm and sleep?"

"Sleep?" Rhode laughed as if she had heard the funniest joke of the year. She threw an arm around Hathaway's shoulder. "My dear cousin, you have 42,000 M-Units of mana, but your control is as sloppy as a drunk goblin. Do you really think we're going to let you walk around like a ticking time bomb?"

Lin pointed towards a massive, metallic structure looming in the distance: the 'Iron Rose' Comprehensive Arena. "We're going to the VIP Training Room." Her smile was terrifyingly beautiful. "I want to see how long a rookie with S-Tier stats can last against me when I only use Rank 1 cantrips."

Hathaway's stomach, already carrying two servings of Marbled Frost Dragon Meat she could not afford, performed a slow roll. Fighting Lin. The G.O.A.T. of Dueling.

...Wait.I have 42,000 M-Units of mana. And more importantly... these eyes.

Game balance 101: a drastic cosmetic mutation in a character’s eyes is never just aesthetic. The Sharingan. The Mystic Eyes.

Earlier, when Lin reached for the pudding, Hathaway swore she had seen the muscle trajectory twitching a microsecond before the actual movement.

Hathaway glanced at her reflection in a shop window. Those deep crimson eyes, filled with rotating geometric patterns, looked like a terrifying hardware upgrade.

I have zero combat software, Hathaway reasoned internally. But if my hardware is genuinely this broken, if these eyes are a built-in perception cheat, I can exploit it. I don't need to beat a World Champion in a fair fight. I just need to use my broken stats to land one lucky, glitchy hit.

"Fine!" Hathaway straightened her back, gripping her untested hardware advantage like a lifeline. "Let's go! But since you're a World Champion, I'm setting the handicap!"

She gritted her teeth, thinking of the 2,800 Solars added to her debt. "If I manage to land even a single hit on you, just graze your clothes, I win! And you have to pay off that hotpot bill for me!"

Rhode and Lin exchanged a look. It was the kind predators give when they see a particularly plump, naive rabbit jumping into the stew pot voluntarily and asking, "Is the water warm enough?"

"Deal," Lin said softly, her blue-grey eyes crinkling in pleasant amusement. "Let's go fix your brain."
