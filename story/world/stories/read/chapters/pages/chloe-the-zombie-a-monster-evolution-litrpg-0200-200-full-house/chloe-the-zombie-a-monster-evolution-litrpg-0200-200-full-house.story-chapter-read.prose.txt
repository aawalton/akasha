Well, Administrator A had finally been totally annihilated by Z's super awesome, incredibly cool mystery sword, or whatever that terrifying thing actually was, which meant it was finally time to get back to the important stuff. They still had to rescue Raenof and Sierra, and also Greg.

But before they could do any of that, Chloe, Liz, and Z needed to meet back up with Percy and get him to tag along. Leaving him behind at the generator had only been temporary, after all, and Chloe was pretty sure they'd need everyone they could get at this point.

The good news was that Percy hadn't exactly been sitting around doing nothing while they'd been gone. Apparently, while waiting for them at the generator, he'd hunkered down and focused his attention, using his abilities to search through the facility. Somehow, he'd managed to sense the locations of Raenof, Sierra, and Greg.

And also, he'd drawn them a map, and a pretty decent one too. Well, it wasn't exactly the prettiest map Chloe had ever seen. There were crooked lines and a lot of hastily scribbled labels, but it was surprisingly detailed, and Chloe had to admit that she was impressed.

"Whoa! Percy, you drew this?" Chloe asked, leaning over the map.

Liz glanced at it as well before giving Percy a curious look. "You know, Percy wanted to be an artist when he was younger."

"Really?!" Chloe asked.

She looked back at the map, suddenly seeing it in an entirely different light. The crooked lines weren't crooked anymore. They were artistic, maybe in a modern art kind of way.

"That's actually really cute." Chloe said, looking at Percy.

Percy, meanwhile, seemed considerably less enthusiastic about having his childhood aspirations brought up. Anyway, Chloe thought it was pretty neat.

Percy stared at Chloe for a moment. Then he sighed, but it was like, the kind of sigh that somehow said a lot without actually having to say anything. Chloe wasn't entirely sure what she had done this time, but she figured Percy was probably just embarrassed about the whole secretly being artist thing.

"Anyway, we should go." Percy muttered.

"Oh! Right." Chloe straightened immediately, since she was suddenly reminded that there was some rescuing to do!

Percy folded up his makeshift map and tucked it away before turning toward one of the corridors leading deeper into the facility. The others followed behind, with Chloe kinda just bouncing along beside him. She was in a pretty good mood since Administrator A was gone, things were really on the up and up!

The halls seemed to feel less heavy now that they weren't being chased by an overwhelmingly powerful Administrator. Chloe kept glancing around as they walked, wondering just how enormous this place actually was.

According to Percy's map, they had quite a bit of ground to cover. Fortunately, the first destination wasn't particularly far. Percy eventually stopped in front of a large reinforced door.

"Are they in there?" Chloe asked.

Percy nodded. "Pretty sure your two friends are in there, probably."

"Probably? What, are you unsure?"

"I'm… mostly sure, and it's not like we have any better leads."

Chloe hadn't immediately rushed toward the door. "Raenof! Sierra!"

"Wait," Liz said, grabbing Chloe by the back of her clothes before she could throw herself at the door. "Maybe don't rush into an unknown room."

"Oh, yeah that's probably smart, but if there is trouble, can't we just fight our way in anyway?" Chloe pointed out. .

To Chloe's surprise, Z spoke up next and said words Chloe didn't really expect would come from her. "I agree with Chloe. We have already disposed of the most dangerous threat in Administrator A, I doubt there's much left that could pose a meaningful threat against us."

Liz just kinda looked at Z but then shrugged. "Eh, if you say so."

Percy moved over to the control panel beside the door and started working on the locking mechanism, pressing a few buttons until a series of clicks echoed, followed by a soft hiss as the seals disengaged. Finally, the massive door slid open, and Chloe's eyes immediately widened as she peeked inside.

The room beyond was dimly lit, but she could still make out two figures sitting toward the center of the room, one was an elf, Raenof, who was slumped against the wall looking completely exhausted, while beside him sat Sierra with her arms wrapped tightly around herself, her ears drooping sadly and her shadowy tail lying limp against the floor.

Chloe hurried across the room. "Raenof! Sierra! Are you okay?"

Sierra's ears immediately perked up at the sound of Chloe's voice. Her head lifted, and for a moment, she simply stared as though she couldn't quite believe what she was seeing.

Then her expression softened with obvious relief. "Chloe..."

"I'm here! Oh, and I also brought some new friends, you should get to know them better in a bit" Chloe announced proudly.

Z was already examining the sort of futuristic looking restraints holding both prisoners in place. The devices hummed softly as they suppressed their abilities, and after a few seconds of careful inspection, Z crouched beside Sierra and placed a hand against one of the restraints. Golden mana immediately flowed from her fingertips, making the runes flicker wildly before the entire seal shattered with a sharp crack. The mechanical clamp released at the same time, and Sierra blinked in surprise as she slowly pulled her arm free, rubbing her wrist.

"You really came for us." she murmured.

"Of course we did!" Chloe said.

She immediately threw her arms around Sierra. Sierra stiffened in surprise. Then, very slowly, her arms came up and wrapped around Chloe in return.

"Thank you," Sierra whispered.

Chloe squeezed her a little tighter. "No problem!"

Sierra's tail gave a small, hesitant wag behind her, and Chloe smiled since that made it clear that Sierra was definitely happy to see her. But then again, only the jerkiest of jerks wouldn't be happy to see her anyway.

Z moved on to Raenof's restraints, and this time the process went much faster now that she knew exactly what she was dealing with. Another surge of golden mana coursed through the seals, making the glowing runes flicker before cracking apart and collapsing in a shower of fading light. The clamps released, and Raenof immediately rolled his shoulders and rubbed at his wrists, letting out a tired sigh as he finally stretched his arms freely again.

"A, freedom. Beautiful, wonderful freedom. I was beginning to worry I'd have to spend the rest of my life decorating this cell."

Chloe giggled. "Maybe you could've drawn something on the walls!"

Raenof glanced around at the bare metal walls. "I considered it. Unfortunately, my artistic talent is roughly equivalent to that of a drunken sailor."

Chloe immediately thought of Percy and his map. "Percy's actually pretty good at drawing!"

Raenof raised an eyebrow. "Who's Percy?"

Chloe pointed to Percy, who was already moving behind Liz. "The guy hiding behind the cool looking girl."

Raenof just nodded a bit. "I… see."

Raenof tried to put up a confident face, but Chloe could see how exhausted he really was beneath the joking. His clothes were dirty, his hair was a mess, and there were dark circles beneath his eyes. Even so, he looked genuinely relieved to be out of those restraints.

Chloe stepped closer and looked him over."You okay?"

Raenof waved a hand dismissively. "I've been better. I've also been considerably worse, so I'd say we're doing pretty well."

"That's good! But how did you and Sierra end up in here anyway?"

"Maybe that's a story best for another time, but suffice to say it was quite the terrifying experience." Ranoe said."

"Yeah I bet." Chloe said before turning towards Sierra. "And you're okay too, right?"

Sierra nodded quickly. "I'm... I'm fine. Just tired."

Chloe reached over and gently patted her shoulder. "Well, you're free now."

Sierra looked at her for a moment before giving a small smile. "Thanks again, Chloe."

"You're welcome!"

Chloe then remembered something important. "Oh! Right! You haven't met everyone yet."

She turned around and waved toward the others. "Raenof, Sierra, this is Liz!"

Liz lifted a hand in greeting. "Cool, nice meeting you two."

Chloe then pointed toward Percy. "And that's her brother, Percy!"

Percy looked at them. "Hey."

Chloe blinked. "He's really friendly."

Raenof stared at Percy for a moment before looking back at Chloe. "Is he?"

"Yep! But he's just kinda quiet, so don't mind him."

Percy sighed. Sierra gave a tiny, nervous smile while Raenof chuckled under his breath. "Well, it's certainly a memorable first impression."

Chloe grinned. Everything was going great! They'd found Raenof and Sierra, they were both alive, and now everyone was finally together. Well… Almost everyone. Chloe's smile faded slightly as she remembered the last name still missing from their group. Chloe glanced toward Percy. "So, where's Greg?"

"One floor down," Percy answered.

Liz immediately stepped forward. "On it."

Before anyone could ask what she meant, she raised her hand and unleashed a burst of magic into the floor. The resulting explosion tore straight through the floor, sending chunks of masonry and dust cascading into the room below. When the smoke cleared, a jagged hole had been blasted through the floor, large enough for someone to climb through. Liz peered down through it, apparently satisfied with her handiwork, before jumping through.

Percy followed without so much as a complaint. Raenof stared at the hole for a moment, then looked toward Chloe with an expression somewhere between amusement and disbelief. "Your new friends are an interesting pair."

"They're pretty nice, though, so don't worry." Chloe said.

With that, she hopped through the opening herself. Z, Sierra, and Raenof exchanged brief looks before following after her, although Raenof took considerably more care than Liz had when climbing down through the newly created passage.

Once everyone had regrouped, Percy led them deeper into the lower levels, following the route he'd marked on his makeshift map toward wherever Greg was being held. Chloe stayed close behind, glancing around curiously as they made their way through the increasingly dark and industrial corridors.

Eventually, they stopped in front of a super fancy looking reinforced steel door that looked a whole lot tougher than anything they'd encountered so far. Percy stepped closer and examined it for a moment, but before he could figure out how to open it, Liz just sighed and then raised a hand. A concentrated blast of dark energy slammed into the door with a tremendous BOOM, blowing it inward and sending the heavy metal slab crashing to the floor. Chloe blinked at the sudden destruction before peeking past the wreckage, revealing a small holding area on the other side.

There, behind another set of reinforced bars, sat this guy in a plain jumpsuit. At first glance, Chloe wasn't entirely certain what to make of him. He definitely had the vibe of someone who had once been far more imposing than his current circumstances suggested. A pair of folded wings marked him as a fallen angel, while a long, unkempt beard framed a face that looked thoroughly accustomed to being alone and grumpy. So, this has to be Greg, right?

He was sitting comfortably with a book in his hands. The man who was probably Greg looked up from his reading and looked at the group standing outside.

His eyes settled on Liz and Percy. "Took you two long enough."

Percy gave a small shrug. "Sorry."

"Hey, at least you're free now." Liz said.

Greg closed his book and finally turned his attention toward the rest of the group. His gaze passed over Z, Sierra, and Raenof before settling on Chloe. He looked at her for a moment longer than the others. "Hey, you kind of look familiar."
