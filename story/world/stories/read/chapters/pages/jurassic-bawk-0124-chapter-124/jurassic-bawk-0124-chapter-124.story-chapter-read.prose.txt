The rooster stood his ground as a hail of projectiles streaked towards him like flechettes fired from a shotgun.  He ignored the cries of his companions and focused on defense.  His boiling blood burned hotter as Inferno’s Embrace projected a shield of searing flames around his body.  But Charlemagne didn’t trust in his enhanced physique alone to stop the assault: he had experienced the force behind the Empress’ attacks firsthand moments before.  A trio of mana shields sprang up between him and the incoming projectiles, wide enough to cover both Josephine and Phatagin as well as his own body.

The first mana shield shattered under the barrage of icy shards, but the searing heat softened the points enough that they lost much of their effectiveness.  The volley smacked into the second shield, where it was stopped short.

“Good instincts!” the Empress of the Forest cried as she rose up and put distance between them.  The ritual chamber they were in was expansive, but not truly large enough for a battle against a rooster that could move faster than sound and a being who commanded the wrath of the elements.  Still, the floating Empress was able to strategically position herself in a corner, which meant the rooster had no choice but to face her head-on.

Charlemagne was already on the move.  He had yet to experience serious combat after having fused his Skills into Inferno’s Embrace, but even his nervous system felt like it had received an upgrade.  The lag time between thought and action, one of the biggest challenges faced by human athletes before the System Integration, had shrunk to less than a millisecond, allowing the rooster to place his body exactly where he wanted it at any given time.  His eyesight had also gained another boost, allowing him to notice when his opponent was about to perform an action, although it was difficult to tell what that action might be.

The rooster’s upgraded perception stood him in good stead as he hopped to the side, his sharp senses and his Advanced Fowl Arts warning him of an impending attack.  A stony hand with icy claws emerged from the floor where he had just stepped a moment before.  Another ping from his Skill caused him to flap his left wing, the action sending him flying upward and spinning in cartwheels.

The Empress hissed in annoyance as the lightning bolt aimed at Charlemagne’s head missed by a hairsbreadth.  But, even as she did so, her next attack was already unfolding.  An enormous blob of water emerged from her chest before flying forward with startling speed.  It was both too fast and too large for the rooster to dodge.

Instead of creating another mana shield to block the water, which would have forced him to delay closing with his foe, Charlemagne decided to tank the hit straight on.  It was only water, after all.  His burning aura flared to new heights as he forced his Ember Core to circulate mana through his body at an accelerated rate, pulling in ambient mana to replace it with but a sliver of his consciousness due to Battle Meditation.  The blob struck the rooster straight on, and he closed his eyes out of reflex.

The world became light and pain.

Charlemagne’s body convulsed as a stream of lightning ravaged through it, empowered somehow by the floating sphere of water that now encased him.  Incredible heat flowed from his body and into the water, causing it to boil.  Steam rose in great gouts, obscuring Charlemagne from mundane senses for several long moments.  The rooster and water combination was still moving forward, rising through the air toward the Empress.

“Not good enough!” she yelled, flexing her will.

The bubbling water and steam began to freeze as the Empress used her left hand to pull thermal energy from the liquid, flash freezing it around the rooster’s body.  Her right unleashed another storm of lightning.  The smell of ozone began to fill the room as arcs of pure electrical power flashed over and over.

Then the sphere of ice exploded, and the Empress caught sight of a clawed foot blazing through the air just before it slammed into her midsection.  She slammed into the stone behind her, breaking through the wall and disappearing into a deep hole.  Charlemagne, who had been pushed back by the force of his own attack, hovered in the center of the room, staring at the Empress’ last known location with a penetrating glare.

His body had taken some damage from the chain of attacks, but Zenrage had yet to activate.  The lightning had been annoying, but it hadn’t done as much damage as it could have, likely due to the extreme changes caused by Inferno’s Embrace.  In fact, his wounds had already mostly healed, his incredible regenerative powers combined with the raw magic of the rings he was still digesting fixing injuries almost as fast as they accumulated.  As long as he had mana, Charlemagne was confident in his ability to tank damage.

“You’re strong, far stronger than anyone at your level has a right to be, First Gen.  Your Patron must have spent everything that they had on you, but the gamble paid off.  To be here, at the Elite Systemic Games, given your situation?  Incredible.  Centuries of planned breeding, gathering natural treasures, and stockpiling System rewards are usually needed to create a being strong enough to even get in the door, much less win.  But you’re still new to combat, that’s for sure.”

“Bawk!” the rooster answered, annoyed at the delay.

“You never should have allowed an opponent this much time to prepare an attack unpressured,” the Empress shot back as the entire ritual chamber began to rumble.  “Your companions may want to leave the room, quickly!”

Phatagin and Josephine needed no second encouragement, disappearing within second past the enormous double doors to watch the battle unfold from the safety of the hallway.  The Empress unleashed her attack as soon as they were out of harm’s way.

“Rocks Fall!”

Huge stones dislodged from the ceiling as the entire roof began to collapse.  Charlemagne, with a flex of his will, created an enormous mana shield that caught the incredible weight of the rocks above him, carving out a small zone of safety as he began to charge up his most powerful technique.  He ignored the rocks falling all around, filling the ritual chamber entirely save for the spot directly below him.

Charlemagne could sense that the Empress was above him and to his left, although he was having trouble pinpointing her location.  Her mana signature was bleeding into the ambient mana, making it easier for her to manipulate while at the same time obfuscating her exact position.  The rooster wanted her to teach him how she did that, because it seemed like a useful trick.

Charlemagne hurried to launch the cracking inferno in spherical form as soon as he could, worried that his slippery opponent would sense the energy build-up and escape.

“Buck-kawk!” he cried as the mana attack blazed forward, expanding as it flew towards the general location of the Empress, melting the very rock into slag as it passed through his mana shield and slammed into the collapsed ceiling, leaving behind a molten tunnel that extended all the way to the surface.  Not wasting any time, Charlemagne raced up the tunnel, popping out to find that the snow all around him had melted from the intense heat, leaving behind mud in its wake.

“Not bad, not bad,” the Empress’ voice came from behind.  The rooster spun around to find that she was once again rising up into the air.  Despite her nonchalant air, Charlemagne noticed that his opponent had taken at least some damage from the attack.  Not only had her body shrunk in size, but small cracks radiated through some of her skeletal structure on the right side of her body.

“Bawk,” the rooster answered, feeling a strange sensation welling up inside of him but unable to label it.

“Yes, you certainly have me outclassed in raw power.  Which is shocking, since this simulation was designed to test your leadership and problem-solving abilities rather than your combat skills.  There are several powerful allies that you could have recruited with special Skills or Special Abilities that would have served to counter my own quite effectively.”

“Bawk,” Charlemagne boasted.

“Well, as they say, ‘no being is a completely closed system’.  You don’t necessarily ‘need’ your companions, at least not at the moment, but they do make your life easier, don’t they?”

The rooster remained silent.

“Case in point,” the Empress pressed as she snapped her fingers.  All at once, howls rose from the forest around them.

“I have minions that could occupy a small portion of your attention, granting me a small advantage should I choose to employ them.  Fear not, Ser Knight, for we have agreed upon a duel.  They shall remain where they are; I only meant to use them as an object lesson.”

“Bawk,” the rooster admitted.

“Now that we are up here, I might as well show you my full power,” the Empress answered.  “I was holding back so that we wouldn’t accidentally kill your friends in the crossfire.”

With that, the Empress rose further into the sky and began to absorb an incredible amount of ice-aspected mana from the area around her.  Hidden batteries within her icy palace fed her as well, causing her Core to swell with the sheer amount of mana at her disposal.  Charlemagne watched, fascinated, as she crammed more power into her Core than he would have thought possible based on his understanding of his own body.  Another area where the Empress might be able to teach him.

“Endless Winter!” she cried, power flooding out of her and creating a sphere of absolute cold in a wide radius around her.  Charlemagne, caught in the edges of the strange aura, began to lose body heat as the kinetic energy of his very molecules was stolen by the vortex.  He squawked, suddenly chilled, and poured more energy from his Core while analyzing the spell.

But his opponent wasn’t about to let him stand there and leisurely come up with a counter to her abilities.  Once again, the Empress began to sling icy shards at the rooster, faster than before.  Charlemagne raised energy shields to protect himself, expending mana from his reserves to stave off the attacks.  Battle Meditation warred against the ambient mana, which was now almost entirely under hostile control, pulling away mere slivers to replenish the rooster’s reserves.  But even this was hampered as the ice-aspected mana was largely incompatible with Charlemagne’s Ember Core.  Without a way to replenish his Core, he would eventually run out of energy.

The Empress, on the other hand, seemed to be re-energized by the icy domain around her.  The cracks in her “bones” began to heal, while her missing mass was gradually restored as she continued to fling barrage after barrage of icy needles into Charlemagne’s defenses.  It was a stand-off, and Charlemagne was slowly losing.

That is, until the Empress yelled and unleashed a burst of power that eclipsed anything she had exuded before.

“Take this,” she shouted as she raised her hands high above her head.  An enormous lance of ice formed in the air, its tip so cold that it appeared as if light itself froze around it.  There was an incredible crack as the projectile accelerated to top speed in a fraction of a second.

Charlemagne, having spotted the surge of mana before it had even begun in earnest, prepared his defenses.  Five shields, buzzing with energy so fierce they temporarily pushed back the icy mana around them, sprang into existence.

“Not this time!” the Empress cried in triumph as the icy spear somehow phased through the first several shields before slamming into the fourth, which immediately burst under the strain.  Then the fifth shield was struck dead on.  It lasted only long enough to blunt the projectile’s velocity by a few percentage points.  Finally, the lance pierced into Charlemagne, instantly freezing the flesh, bone, and blood around it as it penetrated directly through his lungs and out his back.

There was a long pause as the rooster looked down at the lance stuck through his torso.  Then, his eyes rolled back in his head, and he fell to the ground.
