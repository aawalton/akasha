The smoking crater before him was the only tangible proof that BloodLust was actually gone. No more BloodLust. Their weapons and armor vanished, ascending into the same light. Now Marshmallow was back in jeans and a t-shirt, the same outfit he'd worn when the Survivor Game began. As he'd started, so he would finish.Suddenly, Marshmallow staggered as his legs gave out. He fell to his knees, then collapsed completely. He, who had started as the weakest among a thousand participants, had emerged victorious.
