We long to enter.

Realm of all that is good and striveful, Éden, where the Almighty walk and carve canyons with their footfalls. Éden, where plentiful harvest never wilts and silken wine-threads hang on the winds. The forever ending afterdream, Éden, we beg of you to accept us within your breast.

Let the Clenn pass under the white coral gates of Tűzmother, warmth giver, the eye who keeps its target in sight, who stands above and guides those who had kept their souls clean in the grayness. For we studied your histories, kept your orders, and found ourselves deserving as you had declared in the beginning.

Tűzmother, you're all that was.

Let the Clenn march across Hadúr's maroon warpath, chaos and decreation may follow in his fall, for whom defeat and victory are blight. Like shower to a scorched desert, like hammer to a shapeless lump, it is through your cruel hand that we become our true frames.

Hadúr, you're all that is.

Let the Clenn walk the silver-misted trail of Vadász, bringer of the whitening orb, arrow in flight that finds the target that was never. May we lose ourselves within your direction, and may those who had never shunned Blindlight's glory embrace you with closed arms.

Vadász, you're all that will be.

And we beg of you, Almighty trinity, that you forgive those you had cast below, the Scarrd. Inside the grayness, the sinful home of men, they carried your marks of punishment upon their skin, shunned, and shamed, and gentled. But their atonement complete, if paid in gold and misery, now they smile toward the heavens in hope of sharing in the fruits of Éden.

We thank you for your divine gift, the surge, sent from Éden that is a slice of your realm shining through our mortal lives. It is through diamond will and boiling sweat that we may step closer to your warmth, Under, and Over, and Arch, but only a peek through the cracks until we breathe our final sigh in the grayness.

Oh Almighty trinity, we long to enter your realm.

We remain your servants, the Emberek, and plead for the end times when we, inevitably, nest by your side.

- Thirteenth prayer of the Church of the Ember Republic, from the quill of Bernárd Regola, 694
