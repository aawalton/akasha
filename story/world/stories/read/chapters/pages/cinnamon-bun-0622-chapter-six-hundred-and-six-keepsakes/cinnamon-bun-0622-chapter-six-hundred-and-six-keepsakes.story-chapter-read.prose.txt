Chapter Six Hundred and Six - KeepsakesThe Black Avatars were destroying their own home.It was a shock the first time it happened. A Black Avatar that I only recognized in passing, one with a huge sword, raised his weapon overhead, then brought it smashing down across one of the smaller stone buildings along the edge of the keep. There was an explosion, bits of rock and masonry being flung every which way.Then the entire building and the stone outcropping below it slid off the keep and tumbled away. It gained speed, racing down to the ocean--still thankfully far below--where it crashed down with a huge splash."What are they doing?" I asked, aghast."Shedding weight," Amaryllis replied. "All that stone? It's more mass than what the Beaver could lift empty."I watched, feeling strangely ill as the Black Avatars moved elsewhere and continued to mutilate their home, carving off masses of stone bigger than my parent's house.It felt like something someone would only do if they were desperate.Tristain had left to help the Avatars take care of things. He was handing out orders and suggestions, and the Black Avatars were following through with those. They were lightening the Stormpiercer by throwing unneeded stuff overboard, then piling on even more stuff that they hauled out of the Keep.The docks had a few other little vessels and those had been powered on and were being checked over by hurried crews. It looked like a full-scale evacuation was underway."I'm going down there to help," I said."How?" Amaryllis asked. "They're moving at third-tier speeds. If we head down there, we're more liable to get in the way than anything else."It was true, but... I looked over and watched as an Avatar carried a chest over one shoulder. It was large, and when they opened the top I saw that it was filled to the brim with books. That had to weigh a ton! Then again, better to save those now rather than let them sink.I was still worrying about what to do when someone jumped up and landed on the railing a few paces away. Guinivere! She balanced with her four tails twitching behind her, then glanced over me and my friends. "Is everything well here?" she asked. "You weren't hurt?""Calamity was stuck by one of the thorns, but I think he'll be okay," I said. "Otherwise, we're fine. Ah. The ship took a little damage.""I see that," she said.Desiree strode over and bowed. "Your magical capabilities were a sight to behold, Lady Guinivere," she said."Hah! I didn't even participate much!""I got a level up as well," Amaryllis said. "I suspect a few of us did. But celebrations can wait. Lady Guinivere, did you come here for a reason?"She nodded. "I did. Guinevere finally said. Her eyes were bright. "I feel like I just woke up. We locked ourselves up in this Keep, hid from civilization, and told ourselves it was for the best. What utter dreck.""Isn't this your home?" I asked."In some sense, yes," she said. "But it's also a birdcage of our own making." She stretched out her hand; a spark of fire shining on the tip of her index finger. "None of us grew strong by cowering in a thunderstorm. We all need to fly."She gestured like firing a gun, and the spark shot out to strike a minor tower, detonating in an incandescent fireball. Debris rained down toward the sea."There goes my bedroom!" she cheered with a massive grin. "Nothing left to hold me back now."I glanced down at the other Avatars. "They seem kind of annoyed with you.""Well, I am disrupting their work." Her voice was unconcernedly breezy. "They've got things they want to save, some of which are admittedly important... that's the reason I came up here, actually. It's about the dungeon core."I raised both eyebrows."We have a few members empowering our hover spells manually. That will give us a few hours, at least, and I ought to join them soon, but we can't keep that up forever.""What will you do with the core?" I asked."I was hoping to stash it in your ship for the moment," Guinevere replied. "If that's acceptable to you? Tristain mentioned that you were going to Elkensteel.
