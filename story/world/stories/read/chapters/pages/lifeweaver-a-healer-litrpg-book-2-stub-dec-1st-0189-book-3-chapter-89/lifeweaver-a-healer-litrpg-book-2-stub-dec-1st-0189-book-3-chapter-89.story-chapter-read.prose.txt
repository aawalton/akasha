The demons were not pleased that Luke had arrived in their domain. He wasn't sure if Infernal Flux referred to the world they were in or the city far away in the distance and down the slope, but either way, those standing in his way didn’t want him reaching it.

It didn't take long before Luke recognized another demon. The damn thing towered over the others, but it was far from the hulking behemoth rising over the buildings in New York. While it was the same one, and Luke was sure of that, it was smaller here in its own world. Curtis and Hannah decapitated the greater demon, but here, standing between Luke and the city beyond, it wasn't headless, nor was it without a weapon. The damn thing carried two long, slender blades.

Panting from the extended fight, Luke held back, waiting to see what the greater demon would do. The last one fled. This one did not. It charged straight ahead, barreling past its fellow demons, knocking them aside like bowling pins. This opponent was strong, far stronger than the ones Luke had destroyed with ease up until now.

While it was fast, it didn't have the ludicrous speed he'd seen in some, the kind where they seemed to disappear then reappear right in front of you. It gave him enough time to weave a shield. When the strike came, Luke blocked one sword, but the second came at a strange angle, below and to the left of the shield. Metal scraped Luke's ribs as the weapon pierced his chest. It destroyed a lung and hit a couple of arteries, but it was nothing Luke couldn't handle. The pain of it was like a dull ache compared to the fiery agony of taking the greater demon's infernal mana and making it his own.

Threads extended from the woven shield, up the demon's blade, to grab hold of its hand before the bastard was able to pull back. Now, with stronger threads, they could act as extra limbs without being destroyed the moment someone looked at them sideways.It was unfortunate, but Weaver's Whip did not work on threads inside Luke's victims, but no matter, he had several more to choose from. Lashing out, Luke went for the neck.To his surprise, the demon stepped back. A twinge of pain told him the blade embedded in his body had been removed, but he was a little shocked to see the blocked blade and the hand holding it stay behind when the demon pulled back. It had cut its own hand off to escape the threads.

"Impressive," Luke said, wiping sweat from his face while healing his lung and insides with a rush of infernal mana. There was so much of it circulating inside him now that it was difficult to tell where the infernal mana ended, and his own began, especially since they also mixed whenever they met.

Luke narrowed his eyes as he looted the hand and the sword. Was the absorption of infernal mana getting even easier? Before, his Meta-Heart had been taking care of it all, converting to regular mana. Now, it was happening all over again, and not all of it was converting, either. Some just kind of drifted around Luke's mana channels and acted as regular mana when he needed it.

He wasn't sure if this was a development he should be happy with or not. Since it was demon mana, probably not, but he didn't have much time to consider it, since the greater demon rushed forward to attack again. The hand had regrown, and it held its single blade in both hands now, having lost the second.

"My ancestral blade is not for a human to wield!" it bellowed as it came. A spellweave formed in front of it, something to do with fire and more of that orange sludge. That much was obvious to Luke's senses.

Rather than wait, Luke weavestepped forward and lashed out, destroying the spell with Destabilize Spellweave. The demon lowered its shoulder and slammed into Luke with enough force to throw him back into a crowd of onlookers. Clawed hands and gnashing teeth reached for him, and Luke panicked for a moment, unable to get free as the greater demon advanced, rage burning in its small, orange-glowing eyes. Another spellweave formed, and this time, Luke was too far away to destroy it. Instead, he weavestepped to the side just as a roaring fireball crashed into the demons that had held him, destroying a large group of them.Far from finished, Luke grabbed a demon who'd ventured a little too far ahead of the others, drained its mana with a forceful pull, then rushed the greater demon, staff held out in front of him, like Hiroki had taught him way back at the bojutsu dojo.

The greater demon bellowed what sounded like a laugh. Luke suppressed a smile. Underestimating a Lifeweaver because he's wielding a stick is a surefire way of ending up in the ground, or wherever dead demons go.

Ignoring the pain and decay of his insides, Luke focused everything on timing the next blow right as the demon raised its sword to meet the swooping arc of Luke's staff. The Healer's Staff did not make the greater demon flinch, but it kept the staff intact, and that was all Luke needed. Threads of mana snaked out from the length of it, wrapping the sword up tight. With a yank, Luke pulled it free.

"Sorry," he said. "Was this an ancestral blade, too?"

The greater demon, now without a weapon, roared in rage and threw itself forward, opening its huge jaw as if to bite down on Luke's neck. It towered over Luke, but the size difference didn't matter much. The moment it took its eyes off the staff, Luke dropped the sword and used Weaver's Whip, wrapping four threads around the greater demon's neck.With a twist and a yank, the head dropped to the ground, its jaw still open, just before the sharp, jagged teeth could sink into Luke's flesh.

Congratulations! You have reached level 43!

Another day, another corpse to sell. This time, with two additional swords. He was getting quite a collection. Perhaps he ought to find someone who could find use for them. Elijah, maybe. Or Curtis, depending on what sort of fighting style he settled on. If they were still alive...

The thought threatened to make it all come crashing in again, but a distraction arrived just in time. This one, Luke didn't recognize, but one thing was clear: it was a greater demon. Not wanting to run around with unallocated attribute points again, he added them all to Wisdom.

While most of the demons he'd encountered since coming to Infernal Flux were stronger than the lesser demon beasts roaming the streets of New York, they still didn't pose much of a threat. The greater demons were different. Even without inspecting, there was just something about its aura that told Luke to expect a challenge, or perhaps it was the other demons making way and acting all subservient to it. Either way, it approached at a slow walk, burning gaze fixed on Luke.

This one wasn't much taller than the normal demons, who came in all shapes and sizes, and it looked out of place with its humanoid shape. It looked almost a little too human, except for the red-tinted skin and the horns atop its head.

"No weapons?" Luke asked, hefting his staff.

The greater demon never stopped its approach, but didn't speed up either. When it spoke, its voice was a little too human for comfort. "I have my fists. They will be enough."

It kept on coming, and as it got closer, Luke couldn't deny it was an impressive specimen. Rather than bulk, this one was lean and stringy in an unassuming way, but still lean-muscled. A martial artist of some kind. The other demons pulled back, enlarging the ring of empty space Luke had enjoyed while fighting the previous demon. Judging by the expressions on their faces, this was a serious opponent. They looked afraid, even.

"Well, shit," Luke said, raising the staff to bring it down on the new arrival's head.

The greater demon side-stepped the blow with ease and kept coming. Once it got within a stride of Luke, it raised its fists and stepped in with a jab. It was quick, but not so quick that Luke didn't see it coming. Bracing for impact, he reached forward with threads of mana, grabbing hold of his opponent's leg.

The punch connected, and Luke came to a second later when the back of his head struck a boulder. Cursing, he leaned forward to keep on his feet and raised his hands to get the staff in position against the still coming greater demon.

Except there was no staff. And just the one hand. Looking down, Luke found his right arm gone. Not just that. His entire shoulder was missing as well. A gaping, bleeding hole was all that remained, much of his chest missing as well. Healing mana was rushing out from his Meta-Heart to restore him, but that wouldn't be enough, not even close.

His vision dimming, Luke dropped to his knees, mumbling, "Well, shit," as he forced his brain to work through sheer willpower. Weaver's Renewal. He needed to restore himself before the greater demon got close enough for another punch.

Blood fountained out of his throat and got all over the beautiful but torn robe as Luke used the skill. His mind seemed far away, as if he were looking down at himself from above. The demon was close now. Too close.

Luke's mind snapped back into place as his shoulder and arm returned. Or rather, a new shoulder and arm took their place. Using the skill ate through most of Luke's mana, but it was the only way to survive long enough to think of something. He couldn't take another punch like that.

Casting about for a way out, Luke considered using his ace in the hole, but decided against it. If he wasted it now, there might not be a way for him to ever leave this cursed place.When the greater demon stopped a few strides away from Luke, he still hadn't found a solution. Rather than continue, the demon spoke.

"I expected more from my sister's guardian."
