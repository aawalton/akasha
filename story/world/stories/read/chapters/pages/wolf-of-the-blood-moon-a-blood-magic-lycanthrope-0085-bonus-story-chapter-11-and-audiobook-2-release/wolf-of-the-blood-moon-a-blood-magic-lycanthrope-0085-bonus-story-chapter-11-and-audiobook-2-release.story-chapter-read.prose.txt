Scarlet

Yeah, I think this’ll work.

I nod my head in satisfaction as I look at the large dimension spread out in front of me. One filled with various different biomes ranging all the way from cold tundra, to volcanoes, and even just regular forests.

And while they’re currently empty of any sort of creatures for people to hunt, the biomes themselves are quite well done.

So I’m very satisfied with my work so far.

I glance at Isaac at my side without taking my hands from my jacket pockets. But he’s just gazing at me with those fanatical eyes as always.

No surprise there.

Then I turn around from the dimensional crack in front of us to glance back at Earth from our place here in space.

It’s been a couple years since I originally returned after saving the universe and I’ve spent most of the time just working on creating this lovely little dimension. With Isaac helping me along the entire way with his bizarre fanaticism.

And I haven’t felt even the slightest bit bored throughout it.

Everyone else on Earth are doing quite well too.

Aria is working with her father to scout other planets for Earth, and Belle and Arthur are currently stuck raising kids. As for Mom, Dad, Mother, and Father? They’re all off doing their own things as well.

I think the most surprising thing of the past couple years is how little has actually changed.

Well, other than little Lucy, who is growing as quickly as a little one her age should.

My beloved little sister.

I can’t help but smile as I think of her.

Then my thoughts are interrupted when I get a call from the official tournament staff for the Warden Cup. Which is what they eventually settled on as the name for the grand universal tournament that any planet connected to Earth can participate in.

I answer the call to hear the Chairman of the Warden Cup Association answering right away, “Excuse me, Your Eminence, would it be improper of me to ask for you to attend the Warden Cup this year? We have designed a very special layout for this year’s tournament setup.”

Oh. Right.

The one thing I can’t get used to no matter how long passes.

People talking to me like I’m some goddess.

Which I guess I technically can be considered now? Sort of?

I’m not really sure how to feel about that.

“I was planning on attending it anyway,” I answer without any hesitation as I grab Isaac by his collar before instantly moving us straight down to his mansion on Earth. Where I toss the guy and head over towards my mansion. “Aria told me she will be participating, so there’s no way I’d miss it.”

“That’s wonderful news, Your Eminence!” he exclaims, sounding both extremely relieved and excited at the same time.

Hmm…

Why do I feel like they’re up to something?

“We have a special booth prepared for you,” he continues as I walk through my mansion with my hands still in my jacket pockets. Ignoring all the people bowing whenever I pass by. “We all hope you enjoy yourself when you arrive!”

“Yeah, yeah,” I respond before asking, “is there anything else you wanted to talk about?”

“No, Your Eminence,” he states right away, following which I hang up the call and continue walking through the mansion.

“Where are you going?” Tar suddenly says as he appears out of a rift near me, not surprising me in the least.

“The Warden Cup,” I answer without stopping as he flops down on my shoulder and uses me as a ride.

“Is it because of Aria?” he asks while tilting his head.

I nod my head in response as I open the door to the Artificial Reality room within my mansion. Then I go ahead and move into the pod within before entering the Artificial Reality meant for the Warden Cup.

Which is set to start within a few hours.

When I emerge within the Artificial Reality, I find myself appearing inside of a very fancy booth with a very grand looking throne overlooking a large arena.

They’re still going way too far with this opulence.

“Says the person who often makes thrones of blood,” Tar comments, but I choose to politely ignore him.

And he scoffs. Although whether he’s doing that due to my use of the word politely or because I’m ignoring him, I choose not to think about.

“What do you think this tournament will be like?” Tar asks while moving from my shoulder to my lap the moment I sit on the throne. “Wasn’t the last tournament some sort of battle royale?”

“It sounds like they made something unique for this year,” I answer as I begin to pet the Fae King on my lap. “But I don’t really care what it is as long as Aria has fun.”

And wins.

Tar chuckles at that.

I continue sitting on the throne for a few hours while chatting with Tar, just catching up with him about the stuff that’s happened in the last month or so. Since the last time I saw him.

We’ve both been busy after all.

Me with my little dimension and him with his work as the Fae King.

And after the time is all used up, fireworks begin to explode all throughout the stadium. Meanwhile the bleachers, which were previously empty, immediately start filling to the brim in seconds as people rush to get in first. All to get a spot to watch the tournament live.

Of course, I see numerous gazes turning towards me in my booth. And I can hear the cheers of all the people who see me immediately after that.

But all I do in response is give them a brief wave before focusing on the arena of the stadium in front of me.

Where the MC of this tournament appears in a flash of shadows.

The Black Knight of Humanity.

Who would’ve thought he’d become an MC.

I chuckle at that thought as the man begins speaking.
