On the other side of the Wall Forest, Marlius looked up as a massive tree burned down and wailed in pain. A chuckle escaped him, and he spoke. “That’s what you get for denying my offer, World Tree Farlun.”

From the dying World Tree, a strained voice that was holding back the screams of pain reached Marlius. It was not full of anger or resentment. Just a strangely peaceful and pitying tone. “Marlius, you are too arrogant. The cycle of Life and Death is not something anyone can control, and eventually, your time will come. If you continue as you are, this moment in time will approach faster. You are like a moth running toward the light, thinking there is salvation there, only to find that you will be burned to ashes.”

A frown appeared between his eyebrows, and his gaze toward the World Tree looked annoyed and frustrated. “Why do you all not care about yourselves in your last moments? No World Tree begs. No World Tree curses his killers.”

Farlun commented softly as her Phoer Body Cores slowly reached zero. “What’s the point? We are all beings participating in a competition, a race against life that starts the moment we are conceived. You proved that you were able to overcome my defenses and managed to defeat me… Therefore… there is nothing… to resent.”

A black message appeared in front of Marlius, unlike the usual transparent screen with white letters.

[You’ve killed <Level 61 World Tree Farlun>. The murder has been committed by a group, dividing Experience Points according to contributions.]

[Your contribution: 14%]

[Experience Points earned: 23,855.]

Marlius sneered at the black System window. It was clear that the System was despising his actions, but he didn’t care. ‘Why is killing humans okay but killing World Trees is not? Ridiculous.’

[You’ve killed more than 10 World Trees.]

‘Hm? There is more?’

[Title <World Tree Killer> evolves into <World Tree Murderer>.]

<World Tree Murderer>: Your heartless actions have been ingrained in your soul. You are unable to atone yourself other than with Death. All World Trees that see you will feel abhorrent hostility, and no aura concealer can hide it. (Note: If the title evolves one more time, the consequences will be dire.)

Then, the usual system page appeared.

[Energy Method: <Soul Sanguine Nexus Absorber> takes effect! You earn an extra 10% Experience Points.]

[+2,386 Experience Points.]

Marlius raised his eyebrow with a smile. “As expected, they are a good source of Experience Points.”

A greyish bird flew down from the sky and landed on his shoulder, carrying a letter. He looked at it and read it.

“One year has gone by since our assault started. We’ve managed to conquer around 70% of the territory from the <Verdant Sanctity Covenant>. However, the top World Trees, World Tree Garaya, World Tree Hamaya, and World Tree Baraya are still standing. We will start an all-out pincer attack on them in three months. Use this time to loot the places your group has conquered and set up temporary bases. Reinforce the southern part as well.”

Marlius tilted his head. “What about the Wall Forest? Should we ignore it?”

There were no words about it in the message, so Marlius was unsure. The assistant by his side spoke, who was a different one compared to the one from the past. “Lord Marlius, there are no real threats beyond the Wall Forest. Why are you concerned about it?”

Marlius looked at his new assistant and felt that it was a shame that the other one died in the battle against World Tree Farlun. After all, killing a Level 61 World Tree was not an easy task, even for his mighty army. He liked that person quite a lot. Still, the feeling was brief, and soon after, he answered him. “The World Trees beyond the Wall Forest are indeed too weak. However, wasn’t the <Dark Aether Swamp> cleared by one of them not long ago?”

The assistant nodded, thoughtful. “That is indeed the truth. World Tree Tianlian Liu Shu has proven to have much more strength than expected. However, that’s just in comparison to the rest of that area. If World Tree Farlun was where World Tree Tianlian Liu Shu currently is, she would’ve been able to do the same as well.”

Marlius found that reasoning sound and nodded. “Well, you are right. However, I have a nagging feeling that this World Tree might prove to be much more of a problem.”

His reasoning was beyond the news of the demise of the <Dark Aether Swamp>. When traversing the Wall Forest, he found a Life Tree in the past that forced him to retreat. When he marched with his army, he wanted to deal with it on the way. However, that Life Tree was gone. It was as if she had vanished into thin air.

Then, he received news that a Life Tree had appeared in World Tree Tianlian Liu Shu’s territory, making him suspicious. Of course, the mere thought that a World Tree was able to move a Life Tree was ridiculous. The amount of power needed to move a High-leveled Life Tree was inconceivable, even for <Fourth Energy Cycle> powerhouses.

Regardless of all his reasoning, there was a nagging feeling at the back of his head.

The assistant noticed that Marlius’ frown didn’t disappear and pondered. “Lord Marlius, if you are so worried. How about we attack World Tree Tianlian Liu Shu and get rid of her?”

Marlius blinked and rubbed his chin, interested. “The orders were to do as we pleased during the three months, right? Since we’ll spend a month at most to loot what we’ve captured, we can use another month to get rid of World Tree Tianlian Liu Shu and also reinforce ourselves. The last month, we can spend it relaxing and resting for the final assault.”

The assistant smiled. “That’s for the best. We are controlling a branch of the Nexus’ main power, with over 5,000 <Third Energy Cycles> and 100,000 <Second Energy Cycles> over Level 40.”

Marlius was surprised. “There are so many even after our fight?”

“Ah.” The assistant coughed. “Well, there are 3,800 <Third Energy Cycles> and 68,000 <Second Energy Cycles> left now. Still, I heard that a few soldiers had been enlightened and might reach Level 50. Our elite soldier numbers should be back to normal soon enough.”

Marlius smiled. “War is, after all, the fastest way to gain profit. Okay, let’s get the plan started.”

Meanwhile, on the Golden Sap City’s <Lignified Wall>, Galia, Flor, Ignatia, Eila, Verena, and Wert stood together with speechless expressions.

Flor muttered, baffled. “What is this?”

Wert agreed, equally confused. “There is no way, right…? How far are we from Liu Shu?”

Galia muttered, having never seen something like that. “Has this always happened?”

Ignatia chuckled. “It has happened since the beginning. The <Lignified Wall> grows and gets further away as long as Liu Shu grows in size. However, the movement we’ve seen in the last year is truly… terrifying.”

Eila sighed. “You still have time to laugh? Must I tell you that the Wall is almost double the distance away from where it was before? Look at the <Guardian Trees>! With their height reaching almost 400 meters, Someone might mistake them for World Trees…”

What if she gets some very cheap and ordinary fruit? She will be growing tens of thousands a day… Do we need to go out and conquer a few more fairy tribes? What about the payment? Well, the berries per se can pay and…”

Everyone else looked down at the crouching fairy with speechless looks. ‘What is this woman murmuring about?’

The first ant communicated with her antennae and pheromones while the second one used her two front agile legs to give each of the people gathered here a parchment.

Galia couldn’t help but observe the two black-colored ants. They were distinct from the others, and their eyes shone with a certain intelligence that felt like a threat. Galia couldn’t help but ask Ignatia. “Hey, have your children developed intelligence?”

I can’t even imagine how much stronger your colony has become during the last year after gaining 2,000+ Level 50 ants!”
