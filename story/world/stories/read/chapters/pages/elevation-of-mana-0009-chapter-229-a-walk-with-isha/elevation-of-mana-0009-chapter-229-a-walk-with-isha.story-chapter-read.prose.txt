“Well, it's lovely,” she said. “Even if it's a bit dark.”

“It looks the same,” she noted as she looked in, but didn't actually enter, a decision I supported.

“It does, there's no reason it should, but it seems to keep what it looks like. For a long moment we stood there, content, calm, it was something that happened too little for my liking, being that it wasn't where I found myself all day every day. For that time we just existed, just were, no responsibilities, no research, no worry, just us, just alive.
