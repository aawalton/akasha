"Okay, here's what we're going to do. Liora, dear, are you alright?" Renir said, looking at Liora nervously.

They were still inside the azure dragon's cave, and the carcass was starting to stink. They did pull some of the meat into the inventory, but the whole dragon was a bit much. The nasty smell wasn't a deterrent for Smokey, who dove into its meat as soon as he opened his eyes in the morning.

Was it even morning? Liora couldn't tell. It didn't feel like she had slept all that much. They didn't exactly have time to sleep for long.

There was also the matter of that dream. It was fading now; she couldn't even remember what it was about. It wasn't anything dramatic or important. But it did leave her with a heavy sense of loss.

"I'm… I'm okay. Just a bit tired," Liora said.

Renir walked up to her, patting her feathers. This was new, but the gesture wasn't unwelcome. His heavy hand helped her feel more grounded, which was exactly what she needed after a dream like that. It was important to feel that she was living in this moment, not in the past.

"It's going to be tough, but we need to come through. As soon as possible," Renir said, raising his voice so everyone could hear. "While I would love for us to train and level first, I'm afraid we have no choice but to proceed up the mountain. However, all of us going isn't feasible."

"Hey! What do you mean? You want us to split up?" Cee protested. "Wasn't the point that we work better together? I don't want to split up."

"Me neither," Liora narrowed her eyes at Renir, although her anger quickly faded as she noticed the shimmering of his beautiful body. Surely, being so large and so shiny was a crime. How was a magpie to resist?

"Hear me out! What is our goal here?" Renir prompted.

There was a moment of silence as everyone thought. Liora answered first.

"Kick the butt of those stupid Night Order mages!" Liora opened her wings. Granted, it wasn't all that intimidating since she was in her magpie form.

"No, the main goal is to cleanse the river, but, likely, we can't do it without dealing with the Night Order first," Renir clarified.

"So why do you suddenly want us to split up?" Caspian asked. "You seemed quite eager to work as a team."

"I really thought it would be easier as a team, especially since I have unlocked more of my magic. I could really help with the purification process. But yesterday we uncovered that the mountain amplifies sigil magic done by Cee. It means that the power balance has shifted. Cee is officially the strongest member of our team."

"Hell yeah!" Cee wiggled her front legs happily. "I learned loads of new sigils too."

"Exactly. This combined with Liora's Draconic Avarice, which helps her recover reliably on her own, means that these two would do better together rather than with us," Renir said.

"Hey! You never know what they might encounter up there. I think it is important they have me around for protection, as well as Smokey, who can take hits," Caspian protested.

Renir walked up to the merman who was cocooned in Cee's special water hammock. His voice lowered, so Liora could barely hear him. "You think I feel good about letting them go on their own? I don't. I don't want to do this either. I want to be there to protect Liora and Cee. But let's be honest, we're a burden."

"A burden?" The merman blinked incredulously.

"Yes! We are heavy. Although Smokey can fly, he's very bulky. It will be harder for him higher up the mountain where there are lots of strong winds. It won't be a problem for Liora, because she has Tempest Wings."

"No, no! You are not staying behind," Liora protested. "If those Night Order assholes are nearby, then they'll be able to sense you. What if they come after you while we are gone?"

"Not a problem. Any locator spells don't work in the mountains. Its own natural field is just too overwhelming. I'll be safe. Smokey will be able to eat the dragon. Caspian can fully rest in the water hammock. Meanwhile, Liora, together with Cee, will be able to utilize their stealth abilities and dexterity to their maximum potential."

Cee and Liora shared a look. They knew that what Renir said made sense. They were undoubtedly the strongest members in the party, especially in such difficult terrain as this mountain.

But separating again… Liora never liked separating from Renir.

"If you don't want to be apart for long, how about you finish the job quickly and return to me?" Renir teased, patting Liora's wing.

She looked at him for a moment, then finally nodded. "Very well. We'll go and destroy whatever those Night Order freaks are doing before they even figure out what we're doing."

Renir nodded. "Maximum stealth. If you can remain unseen, remain unseen."

"My Shadow Walk skill has just been upgraded to Shadow Incarnate. It lets me remain hidden for much longer, so long as there is some darkness to latch onto."

"It works very well together with Liora's Nightveil. While she is flying, she will be obscured in shadow, which I suspect Cee will be able to latch onto and hide in," Renir explained.

"Wow, really? That's quite neat," Liora said.

"Yes. This is what I meant: our team needs to synchronize more. We'll implement more of such traits and skills in the future. But for the time being… You two should go," Renir said.

Liora still wasn't fully sure about it. She didn't want to leave Renir. If only they weren't under such time constraints. Why was there always some crisis that had to be solved yesterday? Why were humans always waiting until the last moment to solve a problem?

Oh, yeah, because mages couldn't do anything without magic.

"Let's go, Cee," Liora urged, before anyone could change their minds.

Cee looked forlornly at Smokey. "Be good, Smokey. Listen to Caspian and Renir, okay?"

Smokey let out a low whimper, clearly intending to follow. But Renir blocked his path. Well, not that he could actually bodyblock the wyvern, but Cee's Pet Bond skill was high enough level that he would listen to any of her commands. Perhaps it had already been upgraded.

Cee got on Liora's back, and they flew out of the cave. Liora didn't dare to look back. She knew that if she looked back, she would lose her resolve. She had to deal with that corruption quickly.

Although the point of Liora and Cee going ahead was for them to reach the goal faster, it was still far from easy. While Cee could adhere to Liora's back like a snug backpack thanks to her much more streamlined rump and the eight legs that could clasp around Liora's body without impeding her flight, the winds were more brutal the higher they flew.

Sure, Liora had Tempest Wings, so the violent breezes didn't impede her flight. But it tired her out immensely, so much so that Draconic Avarice couldn't keep up with the stamina regeneration while she was flying. It made sense, given that both traits were still at Basic level.

So they were forced to rest often while following the traces of the corrupted river, which could only be seen thanks to Liora's Prismatic Twilight Vision. The mountain was doing a good job of isolating the river from everything else, although the higher they flew, the more alien the landscape became.

Frankly, Liora was afraid to fly anywhere near the actual mountain, and stopped to rest only when necessary. Things were shifting constantly. She couldn't tell whether the crystal trees moving about were just trees being moved by the mountain or actual monsters.

When she tried to inspect them with her Guild interface, all it showed was a bunch of question marks, meaning that it had no clue what was going on either.

There were these random bursts of magic, almost like tiny volcano eruptions except with magic. Cee apparently couldn't see them, but Liora saw magical energy suddenly coalesce in one spot and then burst out in the form of something strange.

It was different every time. One time it was a colorful rainbow geyser which ended in a rain of some weird frog creatures that could glide much like sugar gliders. Another time, tentacles shot out into the sky, grabbing anything in their path and sucking it into the mountain. Several titan bats got caught this way.

The weirdest one by far was that colorful mist that initially seemed harmless, settling over the area much like regular fog. However, soon after, the monsters caught in the mist began… dancing.

Liora and Cee had inhaled some of the fog too, although not as much, and even they felt the compulsion to dance. Thankfully, they managed to get away with nothing more than intensive head bobs.

"This mountain is insane…" Cee whispered, clutching onto Liora for dear life.

Flying was further impeded by low-pressure zones, which made Liora drop low at random times. Apparently, Tempest Wings couldn't help her mitigate these. Not yet, at least. Sometimes she would rapidly drop because of the low-pressure zone. They seemed to be common around the places where magical shenanigans were happening, which meant that Liora had to be extra careful not to get pulled into the mountain like some of the other monsters.

"Holy shit, Liora! Is that a dragon?" Cee whispered.

Liora looked about and saw the head of a dragon emerge from some cave within the mountain. It was huge. Much larger than the azure dragon they defeated, and it had green scales instead of blue.

[Emerald Dragon — Level 92]

"Hide, Cee! Hide!" Liora warned.

It was daytime and the sky was a brilliant blue. Nightveil was keeping Liora's shape rather vague, but she was a rather visible target even if one couldn't tell exactly what she was.

Cee used Shadow Incarnate and disappeared. Like completely disappeared. Liora expected to still feel the spider on her body, but instead it was as if she had never been there.

The dragon looked about curiously, so Liora dove closer to the mountain, hoping to hide herself behind some of the twisty trees. Some fungoraks were scuttling about the roots of the trees.

She had encountered these monsters before on the Blightpeak, but these were clearly more advanced versions of the same species. Their caps glittered as if encrusted with gems, and Liora could see waves of poisonous-looking purple mana emanating from them.

[Gem Fungorak — Level 75]

Liora probably would have tried to attack them. Their names were yellow, and it was likely that their weakness was similar to the smaller fungoraks—their roots.

But Liora never got a chance to attack.

Instead, the dragon, having somehow seen the mushroom-like creatures from a distance, emerged from his cave, making the mountain shake as it moved, and flew straight at them.

Liora activated Decoy Fade and flew as quickly as she could. The gusts created by the dragons' wingbeats tossed Liora left and right like a ragdoll. The giant creature likely didn't even notice her. Or if it did, it saw her as nothing more than an insect.

The dragon's focus was on the fungoraks. The mushroom creatures released a thick cloud of magical spores. Liora wasn't sure what it did, but she saw the purple glitter.

Whatever it was, the dragon wasn't bothered. It chomped down on the first fungorak it saw and ate with delight.

Liora, meanwhile, struggled to get away from the scene, her decoy having been destroyed during the dragon's attack. Cee materialized on her back soon after, screeching. "Fly faster!"

"I'm trying!"

It's been a while since Liora felt so small. So small that the monsters around wouldn't even conside Liora worth their time to pursue. Being stealthy and quick was the only real way to proceed through here.

Had they gone all together… No, at their current level it would be equal to suicide. Neither of them stood a chance. Sneaking was the only way.

As Liora righted herself and flew higher, she finally saw what was undoubtedly their goal. At first, she had wondered how those crazy mage freaks remained safe in these dangerous mountains.

But it was clear now. There was no need for them to have any crazy defense. A single mage corrupting the river would have been all they needed

The peak of the mountain was completely isolated from everything else. Long, sharp spikes protruded from it. Ridges stuck out of the side of the mountain, and it seemed that gravity itself was warped in the area as one of the fungoraks that was trying to escape the dragon suddenly got yeeted into the air, sending him away from the mountainside and into the jaws of the waiting dragon.

Beyond it, Liora could see the corner of a clearly human structure as well as a large concentration of dark mana, which clearly signaled that this was the source of the corrupted river.

"There it is…" Cee whispered. "Time to start our mission."
