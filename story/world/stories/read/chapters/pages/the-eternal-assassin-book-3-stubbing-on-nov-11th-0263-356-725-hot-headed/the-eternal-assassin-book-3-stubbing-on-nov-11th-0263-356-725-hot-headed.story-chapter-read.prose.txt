Despite the fact that Flaming Thread couldn’t have been a greater revenant for more than a couple of hours at the absolute most, the former greater power of the Hojon Empire looked like she was having the time of her life roasting alive the same people she’d spent her life protecting. Combined with the dead wizard at his feet, the sight was all the confirmation Asher needed that the woman wasn’t herself any longer.

[Duelist] - 3/3 elements

“She’s a former greater power. Don’t let your guards down,” Asher warned, holding Crescent and Nail up before him as he prepared himself for anything. The woman’s aura was that of an out-of-control wildfire, blazing around her so brightly it was almost impossible to look at while it consumed everything in its path.

“Bah. You’re supposedly one of these greater powers now, and I can kick your ass anytime I want,” Moxy said, her usual manic grin creeping onto her face as she grew excited.

“I’m going to help the villagers!” Samantha declared, her eyes locked firmly on the scene of death and destruction going on behind their opponent. “Good luck!”

Blasting off from the ground with Flight Burst, Samantha at least had the sense to give Flaming Thread a wide berth, banking around the enemy rather than trying to go right past her. That said, it wasn’t nearly enough consideration. Flaming Thread lazily gestured to the side, and streaming ribbons of flame rocketed out of her hand, blazing bright enough to sear the eyes as they shot straight for Samantha.

“Not gonna happen!” Asher shouted as he Recalled to Samantha’s side just in time, grimacing when Spatial Lock intercepted the attack. That single block ripped a good quarter of his energy reserves from him, but he succeeded in ensuring Samantha escaped the battlefield as she shot down to the village. While she began spraying water all over the burning buildings and using her speed to snatch people up and fly them to safety, Asher landed on the ground and pointed a dagger toward Flaming Thread. “We’re not going to let you hurt anyone ever again!”

“Ohh, so fiery,” the woman grinned, running a hand through her flaming locks and causing embers to fly around her as she turned to take in the three of them. “I suppose I don’t mind starting with the three of you. Though I don’t know if you can handle me.”

“Oh, realms, she’s one of these types,” Moxy drawled, her grin only growing wider as she cracked her neck. “Been a while since I beat one of her kind up.”

With that, the talking was done. Moxy went on the offensive, throwing a few powerful jabs into the air, each one of which sent powerful bullets of compacted air straight for their target. With another lazy wave of her hand, a flaming ribbon snapped up and blocked each strike, easily shifting into the position from one to the next. While she did that, Rulfar charged forward with a roar, intent on crushing the small, flaming woman before him. As he got close, another flaming ribbon shot out and went to wrap around him, but Rulfar cleaved through it with his axe before lowering his shoulder and attempting to crush her beneath his bulk. Rather than let that happen, the greater revenant blew Rulfar a kiss before leaping high into the air, floating in a pocket of shimmering air as she laughed and clapped her hands together.

Embers exploded from her hands, quickly morphing into small meteors that rocketed down toward the ground and set the dry grasses ablaze wherever they landed. Asher cursed when the marked dagger he’d hurled at the greater revenant impacted one of the meteors and was knocked right back down toward him. Rather than trying to thread a dagger through the deadly storm, he shifted his focus to defense and slipped into the astral to wait for the barrage to come to an end. Moxy was quick enough to dodge out of the way of each meteor before it struck, but Rulfar was far too large to do the same. Instead, his body began glowing red as he focused and triggered Unyielding Blood, grimacing with each flaming rock that impacted him.

“My, you three aren’t half bad,” the greater revenant giggled, tapping her lip again as the meteor shower came to an end. She was still hovering up in the air on a pocket of shimmering air, and she seemed like she was thinking hard based on her expression. “I think I’ll start with… you.”

Six flaming ribbons exploded out of the woman’s back, each one streaking through the air like a flaming snake as they all converged on Moxy’s location. Eyes sparkling, Moxy laughed as she narrowly avoided one ribbon after another, using concentrated air blasts to knock herself around like a pinball. Even as she dodged, the warrior somehow found the opportunity to punch her own attacks back at Flaming Thread, yet the greater revenant batted the strikes aside easily with another ribbon, smiling all the while as she watched Moxy struggle.

Seeing as his daggers kept getting slapped down, Asher did the only other thing he could think of. Racing toward his teammate, he shouted. “Rulfar, bear cannon!”

Rulfar’s eyes widened slightly as Asher called for the move that Rulfar and Moxy had spent plenty of time practicing on their own, but the bloodhound nodded, quickly realizing what he had to have in mind. Leaping the last of the distance between them, Asher landed in Rulfar’s lowered hands, preparing himself before Rulfar launched him into the sky with all the power his Strength element lent him. Yet again, Flaming Thread tried to slap him out of the air with her ribbons, but now, Asher was able to flicker through the strikes as he shot upward. While his plan was working, he had to be careful. He was moving fast enough that even only a split second in the astral exhausted him from the distance he traveled.

Flaming Thread was so focused on catching Moxy that she appeared legitimately surprised when Asher got close to her. Before she could strike him down or use any further tricks, he stabbed through a compressed tunnel of space with Crescent, intent on finishing this battle before anyone got hurt as he aimed to shove his blade straight through her neck.

But before his strike could land, Asher froze, his entire body coming to a sudden stop.

Confused more than anything, he tried to figure out what had stopped him, quickly noticing the hundreds of near-invisible threads that had thoroughly ensnared him. It was like he’d landed in the world’s thinnest spider web, and despite the fact that it wasn’t sticky, there were so many threads present he’d gotten tangled in an instant. Laughing at the look on his face, Flaming Thread leaned back in the air, curling a lock of fiery hair around her finger.

“Come now, you didn’t think it would be that easy, did you? Have you ever even fought a greater power before? Or am I your first?”

Asher gritted his teeth when he felt the threads start to constrict, slicing into his flesh in a hundred different places as they drew blood all across his body. His momentum had been entirely canceled out, and he wasn’t close enough to use Distortion to stab her. If it weren’t for Kroult’s spell enhancing his durability, he was pretty certain her skill would have already cut him into little pieces. He could escape it by slipping into the astral of course, but then he’d be right back to where he started.

Luckily, Asher wasn’t fighting alone, and the perfect diversion came from where he least expected it.

A glowing violet arrow burst through Flaming Thread’s chest, causing the revenant to gasp as she spun around and glared daggers at Samantha’s distant figure. The hunter was nearly a quarter of a mile away and still busy rescuing townsfolk, but she had to have noticed the trouble they were in, as she’d taken a moment to launch at least one attack. Asher could only grin at the opportunity. He couldn’t move most of his body, but that was fine.

Twirling his fingers, he flipped Crescent around as he grabbed it by the top of the blade, flinging it as hard as he could with only the strength in his wrist. The dagger only moved about ten feet before it started to fall, but that was more than enough. Recalling out of the web he was stuck in, Asher caught the dagger in midair and hurled the blade at Flaming Thread’s head with all the strength he could muster. The greater revenant reacted fast enough to yank her head out of the way, but the dagger still cut a deep groove down the side of her face, sending still-fresh blood spilling everywhere.

“How dare you!” she screamed, her flaming hair flaring so brightly that the rest of her body began smoldering as well. Her attention was fully on him, and her eyes narrowed into pinpricks that shined so brightly Asher saw spots just trying to meet her gaze. “You will—”

Moxy’s knee smashed into the woman’s nose as yet another untried variation on the original bear cannon bore fruit, knocking her off her pocket of superheated air and sending them both tumbling to the ground alongside him. Despite the fact that Flaming Thread’s skin was actively on fire, that didn’t stop Moxy from grabbing the woman by one of the flaming ribbons and repeatedly smashing a fist into her face again and again. Asher vaguely noted how Moxy had some of her pelts functioning as thick gauntlets to protect her hands, but there was no way she wasn’t in excruciating pain.

Though she certainly didn’t look like it from the wild grin on her face.

“About time you shut up!” Moxy laughed, continuing to punch the undead in the face over and over again, keeping her from getting her bearings. “What, no jokes about you being too hot to handle? Come on, I’m all ears! Rulfar, you ready?!”

“Do it!” Rulfar shouted. Asher blinked when he realized the bloodhound had taken up position beneath where they were falling, his knuckles white around his great axe as he prepared for his turn. After one last punch, Moxy pulled her feet in and kicked down into the greater revenant’s chest, sending out an explosion of air that sent her flying upward once again and the revenant screaming down to the ground like one of her own meteors she’d been attacking them with as she was sent hurtling straight toward Rulfar. Before she could recover, Rulfar swung upward with his axe, the metal gleaming as it cut the woman neatly in half, separating her torso from her legs as the two halves smashed into the ground on either side of him.

Raising his axe over his head to cut her again, Rulfar grunted when the undead pointed and sent four tendrils of flaming ribbon straight through his abdomen. Despite having been separated from her lower body, Flaming Thread lifted him off the ground seemingly without effort, holding him high up in the air and keeping him from finishing the job.

“You think this is enough to stop me?” Flaming Thread asked. The greater revenant cackled as thousands of threads began stretching out from the bottom of her torso, dragging her legs back to her upper body before beginning to sew the gaping wound shut. “I am not bound by my old mortal form anymore! I have finally evolved beyond the Stage of Power! I am no mere—”

Asher’s blade silenced her as he appeared out of nowhere and severed her head from her torso, having Recalled to the dagger he’d thrown after her while falling himself. She must not have noticed the lone dagger tumbling through the air amidst Moxy beating her face in and Rulfar cutting her in half, because she didn’t even try to stop him. He watched the woman’s corpse carefully, waiting to see if she had any other sort of trick held in store for them. Her declaration that she’d evolved to something beyond the Stage of Power had worried him slightly, but seeing as she’d died just like anyone else when she was decapitated, he figured she’d just been showboating. Whatever the case, the battle was over.

The first of the greater revenants was officially dealt with.
