Don woke up in the middle of a forest on a strange white platform. It was in the treeline, forty feet in the air. The sky was normal again, a dark blue of dusk.

His heart hammered like it was trying to burst out of his chest. His whole body was flushed with a thick, heavy sweat. Don tried to force himself to breathe through his nose, as his body was clearly working on overdrive.

“What the fuck? What is going on?”

It was too much to take in. On some intellectual level Don understood what was happening. But his mind was numb, like it was protecting him from being overwhelmed. That was good. It allowed Don to think. This situation might be a survival situation, and panicking was death. Gradually, his breathing evened and his body calmed down.

Don peered down the platform. Down below he heard the sounds of the forest, owls hooting, wolves howling, leaves rustling in the wind. On the platform was a leather belt with a satchel and water flask attached, but nothing else. Don picked it up. It seemed to be good material. He put it on. Then a disembodied voice suddenly startled him.

[Welcome to your initiation to the tutorial. We will keep the explanation succinct. Your planet has been chosen to be integrated into the Multiverse, and thus by law you are given a grace period of a tutorial of 90 days. Do you have any questions?]

Don blinked twice. Slapped himself in the face, to make sure this wasn’t a dream. “What do you think?”

[Usually subjects do have questions. Was that a rhetorical question?]

Don grumbled and ignored that. “What has happened? What is the tutorial for?”

[The System ever-expands and subsumes all life in the universe. You are given two options: Survive in it and thrive, or become energy for its growth. The tutorial is to ensure your planet gets a fair opportunity to integrate. After the 90 days of tutorial are up, your world, Earth, will be merged with Alpha Tudus, and its primary conscious race, the Atanians.]

“And you expect me to do what?”

[We appreciate that you ask these questions. Not many who are going through this explanation are able to. To answer you: we suggest you get as strong as possible as fast as possible. The Multiverse is an expression of the ultimate natural law: Competition for resources. By chance you have been granted an unique opportunity. We are pleased.]

Then suddenly the same white box that Don had seen earlier appeared in his vision. First came three pop up messages, which Don read and then swiped away. Somehow he knew how to do that.

[Race has been adjusted!]

[Human (F-rank)] -> [Evolutor (Spawn)]

[All Attribute Points Reset]

“What the hell is this? It’s like a video game? Is this magic? Or some computer shit?”

[The System is not magical any more than it is a computer program, or any science. The system just is. There are no words in your language to explain it. The best way for you to comprehend it, is to think of it as a very particular arrangement of reality. We do not wish you to focus on this, rather you should focus on how to best utilize the System.]

“I’m supposed to level up, like in a video game?”

[Precisely. We still do not understand how these oracles humans call “game developers” managed to divine the existence of the System, but it is like that. You level up your race through being true to your nature, and your class by performing class actions.]

“How do I get a class?”

[You reach level 20 in your race.]

“And how do I do that?”

[By acquiring experience points. Every class and race gets experience points by killing the monster in the tutorial, as well as other humans. In addition there are System points, which are obtained by killing beings. Killing a human will let you receive their accumulated System points. System points are used to purchase various items at their designated dispensary units scattered around the tutorial.]

“Did you just say killing other humans?” Don said. He was feeling nauseous and hoping this was just a really screwed up dream.

[The essence of the Multiverse and the System is competition. We do not expect most of you to survive. We will discuss again, but now our time is up.]

The disembodied voice disappeared, but the white box was in Don’s vision again.

Tutorial has begun.

Designation for Don Damer: Tutorial Area 30011

Tutorial Contestants: 3000/3000

Objective: Survive

Length: 89d, 23h, 59m

System Points Collected: 0

The platform lowered in an instant, and when Don hopped off, the earth swallowed it. Now he was in the middle of a forest completely alone. Humanity had been collectively put into some kind of free-for-all death game for survival. Whatever people had had before was gone. Money, connections, status. It all meant nothing. All they had was their own resourcefulness and whatever was in the belt pouch.

Next to the belt pouch was a water flask, made of well cured dark leather. Don put it on. Then Don looked at the belt pouch and inspected it. There was a foldable pocket knife. The blade was one inch, and the handle was hardwood. The quality was nice. Not much of a weapon, but as a tool it was bound to be useful. Small set of flint and tinder, which was sure to be useful. Additionally there were six vials, the size of an index finger. They appeared to be plastic, both the tube and the cork. Don used [Identify] on them.

[Health Potion (F-grade)]

Restores Health. Scales with Constitution

[Energy Potion (F-grade)]

Restores Energy. Scales with Energy

In addition there were three ration bars. Don used the skill on them also

[Dense Ration Bar (F-grade)]

Decreases hunger

Self-explanatory, but good to know. Then suddenly Don felt a strange sensation in his navel that spread throughout his body.

[General Skill increased: Identify!]

[Identify Level 2]

Sure didn’t hurt.

Don examined his feelings. He felt surprisingly calm. Truth be told, somewhere deep in the repressed recesses of his heart, he had hoped for something like this. It didn’t matter if you were a college graduate if you wanted to apply for this job. Didn’t matter if you knew the boss, or you had a lot of money and experience. None of that mattered. It was a level playing field, and Don felt like he wanted to play. Was his life in danger? Probably. Was this completely screwed up? Absolutely.

Will I make it? Can I survive in this reality?

A slow grin spread on Don’s lips. That question resonated within him. He felt that there should be fear. And there was, of sorts. But it was the fear that if he wouldn’t seize this opportunity, he would die.

Don examined what he had left behind, and wasn’t bothered. He would miss modern plumbing and take-out pizza, but at least he didn’t have to work anymore.

“Well, I do have to get food… And shelter. I suppose work does never end. But this time I’ll work for myself, and that makes a world of difference.”

Most of all, he missed Baxter. The poor guy was probably dead. But maybe that was for the best. The System would have turned him into some monster. Rest in peace buddy.

But there was a chance Baxter was fine, if the red beam had altered him. It sure had done something to Don, although he wasn’t sure what it was quite yet.

He brought up his status screen again, and wondered what in the hell was an Evolutor. He felt normal. He looked down and his body looked normal. He felt normal too. But Don hadn’t forgotten the moment the red lightning had struck him. It had felt like his whole body was changing, like his DNA was rearranging.

“Might as well, for all the crazy shit that’s happened today.”

But now was no time for idle thought. Don needed to get on the move. Food, shelter and… points?

Being unarmed and just wearing a simple t-shirt and sweatpants was not how Don wanted to start an apocalypse. But it was what he had, so he started making his way deeper into the forest. At least it wasn’t cold. It was getting dark though, and he needed to move fast.

After some time he saw a shape ahead, a black outline in the foliage. It was some four-legged beast, standing next to a boulder. It was looking in the opposite direction from where Don approached.

He crept forward, between trees. He carefully pulled the branches aside as he moved, crouching low. He kept watching his target, and the ground, so he didn’t trip or step on a dry stick. It felt very natural to move, and Don enjoyed the slow rhythmic beat of his heart. He managed to get surprisingly close to the beast.

[General Skill Acquired: Stealth!]

[Stealth Level 1]

There were two thick bushes between him and the beast. It was medium-sized, and looked like a fox, only bigger, and the fur was black.

Don decided he wouldn’t waste time hesitating. He would not get a better chance for a surprise attack. He surged through the bushes and kicked the creature. It slammed against the boulder, but instead of yelping in pain, it let out a menacing growl.

This fox, or whatever it was, had surely gotten the monster beam treatment.

The jaw was elongated and unhinged, revealing a lolling, unnatural tongue and sharp teeth in three rows like that of a shark. It did not take a single step back or wait to assess what attacked it.

It just lunged.

Don dodged and tried to kick the animal again. It danced around and the thick tongue wrapped around his leg. The fox-monster yanked and almost toppled Don. He managed to stay in balance, but when the fox yanked again and then immediately jumped on Don, he knew this was going to get nasty.

The fox-monster snapped its giant maw at Don’s face. He dodged to the side and immediately slammed his head against the creature. It hurt his neck like hell, but the monster was dazed.

It didn’t stop it from attacking. With its claws it managed to swipe two deep gashes in Don’s forearm.

[Health at 90%]

[Energy at 88%]

[Debuff inflicted: Mild bleeding]

He gasped in pain, and punched the monster on the snout. The fox-creature toppled from the strike, but immediately got up and lunged. It charged at Don and sank its nasty teeth in his shoulder. A white hot pain enveloped his whole arm. The teeth ripped flesh and Don saw stars as nausea mounted.

[Health at 74%]

[Energy at 82%]

[Debuff inflicted: Mild Bleeding -> Moderate Bleeding]

[Debuff inflicted: Mild Infection]

Adrenaline and survival instinct surged inside him, and it overrode the fear and pain. He attacked with pure primal rage.

Don toppled the snarling beast and managed to clamp his good arm’s hand on the creature’s neck, while pushing his knee against the scrambling torso.

The fox-monster screeched and scrambled, snapping its maw so rabidly, its saliva was practically raining around them. It was strong, much stronger than a beast its size had business being.

It took Don all his strength to hold the beast down. He tried to find an angle for his knee, until it latched between two ribs. Don pressed down and finally felt the bone give and crack. The beast screamed and fought, but it was now weaker. Don moved the knee and soon another rib cracked. The beast stopped fighting and finally slumped.

[General Skill Acquired: Unarmed Fighting!]

[Unarmed Fighting Level 1]

[You have slain [Vulpin – lvl 2] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 2 System Points earned]

[’DING!’ Race: [Evolutor (spawn)] has reached level 2 – Stat points allocated, +2 Free Attribute Point]

Don watched the system messages one after another appear and disappear in the corner of his vision as he fell on his back on the forest floor and breathed. He closed his eyes and reveled in his victory. He had just survived a beast attack, which was no joke. Unlike humans, beasts never held back. They fought to kill. And so had Don. But the lesson had been costly. The rush of adrenaline was keeping the pain back, but Don knew he had fucked up. The bleeding was bad. As if on cue the system notified him.

[Health at 70%]

[Energy at 78%]

[Debuffs: Moderate Bleeding, Mild Infection]

[Estimated time until incapacitation from hypovolemic shock: 25 minutes]
