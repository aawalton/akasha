Yeah. I know, but I’ll make it quick. Trust me; it’ll be worth it.

Except that’s not him in front of me, is it?

Five…

Got it. We need to get a little closer.

On the clock at nine, eleven, twelve, and one and three, it looks like.

Ah... Sorry, Aina… but thanks for the heads up.

Rachel!

“I have come much too far to walk away now, Raelynn.”

This must be irritating him, right? He can’t stomach this much longer.

What?
