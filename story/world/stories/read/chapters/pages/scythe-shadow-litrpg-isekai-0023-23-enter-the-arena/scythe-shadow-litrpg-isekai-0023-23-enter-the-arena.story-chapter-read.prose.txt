I braced my hands against the cylindrical walls as they slid past. Water trickled along them now, acting like a minor lubricant as the rising platform ground against the stone.

A circular portal opened above. White blistering light shone down upon me along with an indistinguishable echoing voice like a loudspeaker.

I squinted my eyes against the light’s brilliance after so long in the dark.

It engulfed me as I emerged up from the black prison.

My small circular platform continued to rise as my eyes adjusted behind the shield of my carapaced forearm.

The sound of cheers bombarded me from all angles.

I lowered my arm once my eyes had fully adjusted.

I stood on a pedestal on one side of an ovular arena. Another Reaper in an Amber cloak stood similarly across from me. He looked far less confused than I and far more prepared, a large two-handed hammer-like weapon ready in his grip. Not to mention the dark gray armor that covered every inch of his body.

Around us, a bowl of spectator stands looked down nearly full with cheering Reapers and Penumbrans.

“And our second combatant for this initiation brawl,” a booming male voice announced from seemingly all directions at once, “The newly awakened Amber, Zephyr!”

The crowd roared.

“It is my humble privilege to welcome you to the Umbral Plane in style. Zephyr. Kaiju. Ready yourselves for battle.”

There was no shadow at my feet, nor anywhere in the arena. Scialytic lights surrounded the entire periphery at various angles dispelling any potential shadow.

No escape to Vitria then.

I gazed up at the stands as cheers and jeers rained down around me from Penumbrans and Reapers. The cloaks in the audience were mostly Amber color, though a few others were sprinkled throughout as well.

So this was what it was about. Some sort of hazing for new Reapers? I’d wanted to get a closer look at the colosseum… Well, here was my chance.

“The rules are simple,” the announcer said, voice escalating above the din of shouts and clapping. “First to phase the other out wins. No scythes. Assembling your blade is grounds for automatic disqualification and banishment from any future arena duel. Aside from that, you may use any means necessary to claim victory. All items, equipment, and abilities are fair game.”

That seemed a little harsh. I didn’t have much else in terms of attack beside my scythe. Aero Shift was for maneuverability not offense. I did have Life Drain now, though, so that was something.

I had no equipment, but did have the ring and syringe from the Sins I'd killed. Of course, I had no idea what they did and didn't really want to take my chances. BDMS lady had ambushed me with no warning or preparation.

I was going into this battle completely blind and out of my depth. I might be better off just summoning my scythe and getting myself intentionally disqualified. I didn't need to deal with this shit."

"And the reward?" the announcer continued.

That piqued my interest.

"Gold. Fame. Volition regeneration for both the winner and loser. Oh, and a meeting with our very own God of the Meadow. Valciferous!"

'God of the Meadow?' Now that sounded promising. I tried to look into the crowd but the lights were too glaring to make out many details. Was he here? Was this the god I was looking for? Who'd summoned me to this place?

This whole setup was too much of a coincidence for it not to be.

I had to win this fight. I needed answers.

“So without further ado, let the countdown begin!”

The light around the arena changed to red as a horn sounded.

[Arena Battle Commencing.]

The lights turned yellow. Another horn.

[Good luck.]

Fucking hell…

Green.

A gong-like bell chimed three times.

I dove from my pedestal and rolled in the dirt of the arena floor.

As expected, a long-range attack blasted over my shoulder into the pedestal. My opponent—Kaiju apparently—wasted no time striking first.

A stone spike erupted from the ground behind me and split the pedestal in half, towering several feet over my head.

Something with affinity for stone then.

That was fine with me.

No fucking rock could outrun the wind.

I stepped into Aero Shift and blinked forward as another sharpened pillar burst through the arena floor where I’d been.

Kaiju remained on his pedestal as I closed the distance.

He continued his barrage of spikes. They were gradually getting smaller, though just as sharp.

Curious that he can change the size of the spikes. But why would he make them smaller? Maybe it wasn't intentional. Some sort of stamina-related atrophy? Or maybe skills were more flexible in use than I previously thought. I hadn't experimented with Aero Shift enough to know, using it only when the situation necessitated it. Usually when I was about to be killed…

I should've been playing around with it outside of battle all this time for practice.

I Shifted sideways, dodging another spike, but this time I intentionally tried to move a shorter distance. It worked! And it used far less Volition.

So that's it. Not atrophy, but conservation. That was extremely useful to know. So nice of Jericho and Calliope to tell me I could do this…

The ground in front of me tremored. I twisted to the side, but no spike appeared. Then a stone spear hurtled through the air and clipped me in the side, slicing through my Health.

I rolled to the dirt, trailing blood. The taste of soil and iron lingered on my tongue. The crowd gasped, but I barely heard it, so focused on the battle.

Another spear burst beneath me. I Aero Shifted away without a second to spare. Another chunk of Volition used up. Plenty left for now.

Kaiju and his pedestal were only a few feet away.

“C’mon, damnit!”

I Shifted forward again, dodging another strike. The stone attacks were only reaching knee height now. Kaiju's Volition must be getting low. He's wasted all his big attacks up front.

I could see the desperation in his eyes. They darted back and forth. Sweat dripped down his temple.

Now for something unexpected. I held up my hand and cast Life Drain. A dark smokey shadow formed from my palm and stretched out toward my opponent. His eyes widened behind his helm, mirroring my own shocked expression.

The crowd, all at once, silenced. I'd barely even registered the applause and cheering until it was gone.

But why did they stop? I hadn't gone deaf. They'd just… stopped.

The smoke of my Life Drain sought out Kaiju's eyes and entered his body. Then it split into more streams, finding his nose, mouth, ears, even fingertips. The blackness gradually stained red, flowing back to my hand.

My Health recovered a significant amount, nearly full again, but my Volition tanked hard. I must have taken a lot more Health than I intended. Life Drain was awesome, but it also used up way more Volition than I anticipated. I definitely didn't have enough left now to risk using it again.

Whispered murmurs escaped from the stands.

I didn't have time to think about it.

With Kaiju distracted, I grappled at his feet. He lost his balance and fell from his higher vantage, his armor clanging as he hit the arena floor, hammer tumbling several feet out of reach.

“No!” Kaiju yelled from his hands and knees. He tried to summon more stone pillars, but they appeared slower now, giving me ample time to step around. Even if they did hit, they’d barely pierce my boots at this point.

"It's over." I grinned as I sidestepped another tiny spike.

How the hell was I going to beat him though? He had all that armor. Fists? It was my only option without Life Drain.

Kaiju scrambled up, his back against the pillar.

I clenched my hands and pulled my right back to pummel him in the face.

Then he raised his own arm.

His scythe materialized, crawling the length of his forearm and solidifying.

The crowd gasped. The lights around the arena flared red, bathing the world in a blood-like filter.

“Reaper Kaiju,” the announcer commanded, “retract your scythe immediately–”

The rest of his words were lost to panic as Kaiju swung.

I barely ducked beneath the blade.

He came around again, from the other direction.

No time to think.

No time to act.

Then my body was moving forward, inside the arc of his weapon.

His eyes widened as I grabbed the shaft of his scythe.

My hands burned on contact as if the shaft were a pan fresh from the oven, but I only tightened my grip.

Steam sizzled from between my fingers.

I yanked with all my might and pulled the scythe from Kaiju’s grasp. It clattered onto the ground several feet away.

The world was still filtered in red light.

I shoved my burned hand to Kaiju’s face, the smell of charred flesh following it.

Then I Aero Shifted forward, carrying Kaiju’s head with me.

Straight into the pedestal behind him.

His skull exploded. Blood, bone, and brain splattered over the surface, some spraying onto my face, into my mouth.

The Amber cloak around his body swirled like a vortex and consumed the rest of his mutilated form, pulling it back to the Vitrial Plane.

[Arena Victory!]

[See Colosseum Registrar for reward disbursement.]

Normal light returned.

The crowd of the colosseum remained silent.

It lasted an agonizingly long time.

The announcer finally spoke. "I uh… If I'm not mistaken… I think we just witnessed the Death Affinity skill Life Drain. Which means our winner here, Zephyr, is… A Death Reaper."

Then the audience roared.

So much for keeping a low profile… My cheeks burned red.

I tried to step back and wave, but my arm tugged against me.

It was embedded up to my elbow inside the pedestal. I pulled, but it wouldn’t budge, as if the stone had been cast around it.

“What the fuck? No way…”

I was not going to walk around the rest of my life–well, Umbral Plane life–with a giant stone block instead of a hand.

Calm down… Breathe.

I Aero Shifted backward a short distance, half expecting my arm to rip in half and be left with a bloody stump.

Luckily, the pedestal released my arm. My Volition was getting dangerously low, though.

I sighed in relief and finally waved to the crowd. The lights around the arena had dimmed now, so I could see their faces.

And there, sitting in the front row wearing idiotic grins on their faces, were Jericho and Calliope.

Nuzzle bounced up and down on Calliope’s shoulder, tongue flailing.

I extended my middle finger up at them.

They only smiled wider.

Assholes. Did they plan this somehow? Did they—

I froze, barely trusting my eyes as they caught sight of something—someone—above. At the top of the arena, seated in a large balcony overlooking the entire colosseum…

Was a faceless god.

And he had some explaining to do.
