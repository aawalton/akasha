It was afternoon. The sky was slowly becoming orange. The temperature in the forest was also going down. The atmosphere was good enough to take a stroll outside. But inside the forest of Hylas, someone was struggling with the weirdest thing ever.

Dawn, the mother of five kittens and an adopted creature, was facing the most troublesome foe ever. This was the most unusual enemy she had ever faced. Even after chasing it for hours, she still didn’t know how to deal with it. This foe was formidable. It was none other than her own status screen.

In her head, she was hearing voices in an unknown language. Moreover, her vision was also getting blocked because of this. This was annoying.

This screen was doing nothing but hovering in front of her face. It was blocking her vision from the center.

When she tried to damage it, it simply moved following Dawn’s movement pattern. It even passed through walls or anything else that came in its way, including her babies. No matter how much she tried, she was not able to get close enough to attack it successfully. In the end, she ended up chasing it for several hours.

The blue sky became slightly orange. Now it was afternoon. But still, she could do nothing to even touch this thing.

Right now, she was lying in her den, breathing heavily due to exhaustion and tiredness. Moreover, she didn't even have the energy to stand up. Her whole body was aching due to excessive movement in the last couple of hours.

Suddenly, a similar voice rang again. But this time it was inside her head. [‘Don’t worry user, it is only your status screen. I’m not here to hurt your babies.’]

The Aegis system knew that if it spoke at the wrong time, its host would not understand the language and take its voice as a threat. So it waited for its host to wear herself down.

This would allow the system to show that, even if Dawn was tired and unable to protect her babies, the system would do nothing. This would allow the system to prove its harmlessness.

Hearing the screen talking in her head, Dawn was shocked. She didn't know what to do about this. She didn’t know what was about to happen to her or her babies.

She was tired and exhausted, and her previous injury made everything even worse. Although she was not bleeding from her neck anymore, it still hurt her during her chase previously.

There was no way she could stand up immediately, especially right now. Who knows what would happen to her or her babies if this formidable foe decides to attack now?

She was anticipating an attack from the hovering screen.

But even after preparing herself for the worst, nothing happened. Even when she was lying down while breathing heavily, the screen did nothing. It only stayed in front of her face, maintaining the same distance.

After some time, she gathered a bit of strength and stood up. It was already evening. She had enough time to rest and gather her strength for a slow walk. She wanted to gather all her babies. They were scattered earlier when she suddenly started attacking the system.

She started walking slowly and gathered all her babies in the small ditch one by one. Even when Dawn was doing all these, the screen moved accordingly and kept its position intact in front of Dawn’s face.

Although Dawn was sceptical about the screen’s presence, it still made her sure about one thing. As long as Dawn was awake, the screen would not do anything. The whole time, Dawn was unable to move; the screen stayed still in front of her face, even when she was moving her babies to the ditch.

Even after coming to this conclusion, she decided to observe the screen while guarding her babies. This was the best thing she could do.

So after moving all her babies, she turned her body towards the den’s opening and sat in her hunting position in front of the ditch. The screen followed her face’s movements and moved accordingly. Now, babies were behind her, and the screen was in front of her.

Then the evening turned into night. The moon was up in the sky. But Dawn was still looking at the screen. Until this time, the annoying screen did nothing but stay in front of her face.

Now her body was not aching anymore. She was also getting hungry. She wanted to go to town to get food from the friendly humans. But she couldn’t move away because of the screen. She usually takes a short nap whenever she is in her den. But today she didn’t sleep at all.

Hunger, tiredness, and sleepiness were assaulting her at the same time. Her whole body was not aching anymore, which was the only plus point for her.

When her babies started mewling in hunger, she went back into the ditch and fed her babies while staying vigilant. Then she returned to her earlier position. Thankfully, there was no major reaction from the screen apart from following Dawn’s face.

Outside the den, visibility became almost zero. Moonlight was the only source of light in the surroundings.

Then suddenly, Dawn opened her eyes. The screen was still in front of her. While looking at the screen, she dozed off unintentionally. Her exhaustion got the better of her. She didn’t know when it happened. She immediately got alerted, looked at her babies behind, and all of them were silently sleeping.

Strangely, the screen did nothing, even when she accidentally fell asleep.

Then a realization dawned upon her. Maybe the weird thing was not after her babies. Instead, it was actually after Dawn herself. She should have realised this sooner.

Dawn started growling in a low voice. She prepared to attack again while glaring at the screen. But she knew very well that attacking was useless. It would simply move away again. So, while looking at the screen, she formulated a plan to deal with it.

Then, slowly, she walked out of her den in a straight line, constantly looking at her babies and the screen again and again. She didn’t go far and only walked enough so that she could keep the ditch in her visible range, and if the screen attacks her babies, she would run straight to them.

As expected, the screen was after her, and not her babies. The screen didn't leave Dawn alone even after going out. From outside, she could easily spot her babies, so there was no issue.

Then, after waiting a while outside, she decided to go a little bit further away to look at what the screen does. But still, the result was the same. The screen was still in front of her.

Now, she was certain that the screen was after her. But for some unknown reason, it was doing nothing but covering the central part of her vision, as if waiting for something to happen.

Since the screen was clearly after her, she decided to become daring. She headed towards town vigilantly. Now she had enough energy to travel to her previous den and get food from the friendly humans.

In case the screen attacks her, she would dodge it and deal with it accordingly.

With the screen following her constantly and covering her vision, hunting food would surely become hard. So getting food from the humans was the best option given her condition. Thankfully, she didn’t need to hunt in the morning and evening. Humans would give her food during those times, so she was not that worried.

By the time Dawn reached near her old den, she was extremely late. The humans had already left. Her food was there and the container was also filled with fresh water, but there was a huge problem. She could not eat even if she wanted to.

Looking from afar, she could see two dogs. They were leisurely eating her meal. Seeing this, Dawn was extremely disappointed.

This was the last thing she wanted to see. If it were any other day, she would simply turn around and walk away. But today was different. Not only was she injured and tired, but her vision was also partially blocked by the blue colored screen.

Now she needed to hunt in this condition. This was not something she wanted to do.

[Stats update]

[Health +10]

[Stats update]

[Endurance +1]

Suddenly, the screen hovering in front of her face started acting weirdly. This has not happened until now, so she got startled.

Two small rectangular boxes, similar to the screen’s interface, popped up. Within a second, the rectangular boxes disappeared. And then, two options started blinking for a few seconds on the status screen.

For Dawn, it was not hard to miss. This was even weirder. Why was it doing this thing now?

She finally got notifications of her status update. The threshold was finally met for the stats increment. Since she was a normal wild cat, there was no way Dawn could understand this about the system. She was constantly struggling with the status screen, even after being injured during her earlier fight. This caused Dawn’s stats to increase.

At first, she was confused. She was not expecting it to do something weird again. Then she got scared. She thought maybe the screen was doing something to harm her babies.

She turned around and started sprinting towards the border of the town. She scaled the tall border without stopping and jumped from above without a hint of fear in her eyes.

After landing on all four feet, she started sprinting again. Long, extended sprinting was not her forte. But this was the only thing she could do to reach her den as soon as possible. She entered the forest and continued her sprint.

Dawn was tired, her sleep was not enough, and she was injured; moreover, she was far away from her babies. Now that the screen had started acting differently, she was sure that something was happening. But she didn’t know what.

Earlier she was sure that the screen was after her, so she was prepared for hindrance due to her partially blocked vision. But this weird movement on the screen was unprecedented. She was not expecting the screen to do something different after reaching so far away from her babies. She didn't want to take any chances.

While running through the forest, she ran through the territories of other animals. But she was not daring enough to run in a straight line and ignore other predators entirely. Her life was important to protect her babies after all.

During her sprint, she had to stop several times to catch her breath. This only increased her worries. She had never sprinted this long. She started feeling pain in her chest. Her whole body was fatigued. But still she didn’t stop. She only took a few seconds for a break and continued her sprint.

Then, suddenly, two notifications popped up again.

[Stats update]

[Stamina +1]

[Stats update]

[Speed +1]

After two pop-ups, the main stats screen blinked again for a few seconds. This only increased her worries. Now, she pushed herself even more. She picked up her pace even while continuously sprinting.

After fifteen minutes, she finally reached her den. She went straight to her babies and found them lying in the ditch. They were sleeping soundly. After observing for a few seconds, she was relieved.

Now, she was breathing heavily. Her whole body felt like it was on fire. Now, she wanted rest more than anything else. Seeing that nothing had happened, she lay down beside her babies and went to sleep. She didn’t even consider checking the screen anymore.

Now, she knew the screen was harmless. There was no more proof she needed. Even when she was vulnerable, the screen did nothing. Even when her babies were alone, the system did nothing. This was enough proof for her.

The plan Aegis system devised worked perfectly. What was even better was that Dawn's stats increased by four points in a single day. Chasing the screen nonstop for hours and then sprinting nonstop through the forest finally increased her stats.
