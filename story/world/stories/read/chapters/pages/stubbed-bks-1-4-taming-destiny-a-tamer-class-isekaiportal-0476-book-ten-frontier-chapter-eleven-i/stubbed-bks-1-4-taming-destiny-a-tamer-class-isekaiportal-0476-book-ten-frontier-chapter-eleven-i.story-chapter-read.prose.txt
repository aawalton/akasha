Kalanthia feels the release of pressure, but for a moment can hardly believe it. It’s been so long since she was free.

And as her mana stirs inside her again, an overwhelming sense of fierce satisfaction wells up inside her.

Pressing her magic into the earth feels like greeting an old friend, and she urges it to ripple under her paws just because she can. She lifts her head and roars loudly.

The sound echoes around the cavern, an expression of her sheer joy to be back.

“Glad to see you’re happy,” Markus tells her with the baring of teeth that, to humans, seems to indicate pleasure. Still, she can feel the tension in the Bond between them. Why is he still tense? Is something wrong? Her mind leaps to the obvious conclusion.

Markus Wolfe, where is my cub? she asks urgently.

Suddenly, she hears a loud roar, similar to her own, yet stronger. Kalanthia freezes in place, knowing instinctively that it is not Lathani’s – yet is familiar all the same. It can’t be….

“Lathani’s fine,” Markus informs her hurriedly and there is no suggestion in his body language or the Bond that he is lying. “But we need to get to the entrance. Now.”

With that, white wings of compressed air sprout from his back and he springs into the air, giving quick words of farewell to the three who had helped him remove the muzzle.

Kalanthia pays them little mind – can it really be who she thinks at the entrance?

Markus Wolfe, who is waiting for us? she asks him urgently even as she runs, matching his pace, though not quite as easily as she used to be capable of. He has found new speed, and her muscles have deteriorated in her captivity.

Despite the urgent tension that still rattles along the Bond, she feels a spark of mischief from the human’s direction.

A visitor, he answers mentally – presumably due to their speed that would rip the words from his mouth.

What visitor? she demands impatiently, intentionally projecting just how unimpressed she is with his decision to channel a cub at this moment.

Oh, I don’t know. Just a male nunda calling himself your mate.

The news makes Kalanthia falter just a moment, a wave of longing crashing straight through her.

Tilathis? she murmurs, not even knowing whether she projects it to Markus or not.

Apparently she did as the human responds.

He didn’t give his name. But he smelt Lathani and thought she was you, and came here to threaten us into letting you go free. The tension Markus is projecting briefly strengthens further. Actually on that note, he threatened to wipe out the colony if we didn’t bring you back by dawn, so that’s why we’re in a bit of a rush – dawn started several minutes ago.

It is him! She’d thought her conclusions too unlikely to be true. Their territory is far to the north – or it was. Had so much changed in the time she had been away?

Could Tilathis have taken a different mate? She had left him without much explanation, after all.

But no, Markus indicated he followed her scent, believing her to be in captivity – and is threatening them for her release. Those are not the actions of a male who has moved on.

Though the idea raises a new question. Kalanthia glances up at the flying human with puzzlement.

I am surprised you are worried about an Enlightened's threats, powerful though I know Tilathis to be. She knows how competent Markus and his people are. And considering they have an Enlightened beast on their side in Bastet, and at least one more from what Kalanthia can feel of the pack Bonds, her mate should not cause such concern.

Enlightened? She feels Markus’s surprise over the Bond. This one is an Immortal.

Kalanthia stumbles over her own paws, like the most clumsy of cubs. He Evolved while she was away? He had been at the peak of Enlightened, but they had still thought there was time before he would accrue enough momentum to push through.

She feels bereft and oddly betrayed, though she was the one to leave. I should have been there to see it.

Kalanthia feels an almost irresistible urge to find a dark cave and huddle down inside it, hiding from the too-observant world.

Only just a short time ago she was in that awful cage, mana frozen, movement curtailed, facing the choice between ever more pain or submission to one she despises.

Then what felt like sharp hooks had dug themselves into her flesh and were dragging her through a frightening, too-small tunnel of noise and rushing wind, until she could bear no more and finally lost consciousness.

She woke only to find that she was home – yet still bound in that hateful muzzle. She has had barely enough time to register that she is now free of it – finding out that her mate is waiting for her, and that he’s Evolved….

It is too much, far too much.

Markus is continuing to speak. Her thoughts feel sluggish as she tries to engage with his quicksilver impressions. Normally, he’s easy to understand, for the most part, but now? With her mind and emotional stability in tatters?

Still, she makes an effort to concentrate. The human pulled her out of the nightmare she’d landed herself in – and at great personal cost if the traces of Cores patterned into the stone and his own pain were anything to go by. She can at least offer him her attention.

…but now they’re only managing to keep him from entering the cavern by using some of our most powerful shield enchantments. Since some of those are one-use only, and others will require recharging after use, we’d rather not have to use more than necessary.

It takes Kalanthia far too long to put the pieces together. Her mate is so concerned with her safety that he is threatening to destroy those who stand between him and her. If she were still with her previous captors, she would have welcomed his fierce protectiveness.

As it is, she will not allow her pack to be harmed when they are the ones who saved her.

I will see what I can do, Kalanthia tells the Binder with determination. After everything that’s happened, her domain is fragile, her confidence shaken. But she can still press it forwards, doing her best to touch that of her mate even as she increases her speed as much as she can.

The wall comes into sight a short, but urgent while later and Kalanthia feels how it is resisting her mate’s domain – it’s a relief to realise that that is why they were unable to touch. She had been fearing her domain was even weaker than she’d thought.

“Open the gates!” Markus cries from above. Kalanthia continues running at full speed towards them.

At first, there is no reaction, and then the gates open ponderously.

Kalanthia has to slow down very briefly to squeeze through the still-opening barrier.

And then, just outside the gates, she slides to a stop as her domain slams up against one that is so much stronger than she remembers, but is still almost as familiar to her as her own.

A snow-white nunda stands, less than three body lengths away. He, too, is frozen. Between them stand their cub, smaller than them both, but her own presence undeniable, body language indicating just how angry she is.

There are others too, but Kalanthia doesn’t spare them more than a moment of attention – only to identify them as pack and not threats. Her golden eyes drink up the sight of her mate.

Kalanthia, the male breathes, his domain caressing hers.

Tilathis, she replies, reaching out eagerly with her own domain to return his caress.

Kalanthia cannot say whether she closes the distance between them, or he does. Perhaps it is a mixture. The obstacles in their way vanish and, after so long apart, they are finally together again.

Pressing eagerly into his body, Kalanthia inhales the sharp, slightly acrid scent of her mate’s magic, and under it, the musk that has been his since they met as mere Evolved nundas. She rubs her cheek against him and feels him respond in kind. Air cools and warms her skin in equal measures as Tilathis breathes in her scent as eagerly as she does his.

For one moment, it is perfect, well worth the strain of raising a cub by herself in a world far from her own, never mind the price she had to pay – still has to pay – to get there in the first place.

After the most recent moon, his solid strength is everything she needs, and she allows herself to sink into it.

For a moment, he leans back into her as they share a moment of peace.

And then she smells her mate’s scent change, feels his domain become agitated as it digs into her own. She slumps slightly, knowing what he has felt, wishing that she could have kept it from him while knowing that it was wishful thinking to imagine that he would miss the changes. Markus might have healed her physical wounds, but the moon she has just experienced leaves its traces nonetheless.

She also feels the moment he draws the most obvious – and incorrect – conclusion from the undeniable cracks of pain, fear, and despair in her presence and soaked in her fur.

I will slaughter them all! he snarls, his pale golden gaze glowing white as his eyes focus on Markus who has followed them out. He crouches, clearly preparing to pounce.

Kalanthia deliberately nudges him – hard. She is smaller than him – she always has been, yet the difference is even more stark now. If it came to a physical battle between herself and her mate, she knows she would lose.

She has faith that it won’t get that far.

Tilathis hesitates, his ears flicking forward in confusion as he turns his head to stare at Kalanthia instead.

I welcome your anger on my behalf, but it is misdirected, Kalanthia informs him. Markus Wolfe and his companions saved me.

Tilathis’s ears flatten on his skull.

I sense the chain between you, he tells her, a snarl beginning to rumble in his chest, his lips pulling back slightly. Perhaps he is forcing you to protect him.

Kalanthia snarls back.

Do you think so little of me? When she has spent the last moon suffering because she refuses to give in?

She knows that Tilathis knows nothing – that it is unfair of her to accuse him of belittling her. But she feels what she feels and she is not in the mood to make allowances. Not even for the mate who she has missed.

How can you ask me such a thing? Tilathis demands, puffing up bigger in physical expression of his agitation. Would I have mated with you if you were not the strongest female I know? Would I have remained faithful to you despite the cycles since you left if I did not think that you were the best mate for me? Yet you reappear at the behest of a Binder, an active, strong chain between you, defending him – and a human at that! What am I to think? It has not been so long, surely.

Kalanthia deflates, her snarl dying away. Put like that, it’s hard to deny Tilathis’ suspicions.

The history between myself and Markus Wolfe is a rich one, for all that it is not that long, she admits. I have been forced to put my trust in him several times and he has never betrayed it. The Bond was necessary to allow me to accompany Lathani who is determined to remain part of his pack. Yet I have not found it a burden at all – and have chosen to maintain it. She casts a look at Markus. His expression is tense but otherwise unreadable, the Bond revealing little of his emotional state. How much he is following their conversation, she knows not. Nonetheless, she would not say anything different were he listening in.

In fact, she admits after a moment, if I had revealed my purpose to him instead of simply leaving, I might not have suffered the last moon as I did. It had seemed the right thing to do at the time, but now she isn’t so sure. I do not protect him because he orders me to do so; I protect him because he’s earned it.

Tilathis regards her, his ears still pinned, his sharp fangs still visible past his tense lips. He is not convinced. Kalanthia doesn’t blame him – it is a significant change. Yet she believes he will come to understand in time. Markus has that effect.

Then if it was not this human, who did this? Tilathis demands, barely appeased. Stepping back, his eyes rake over, seeing every hint of her current state. If it were anyone but Tilathis, Kalanthia would hate the sense of vulnerability, the sense that someone is able to see through the core of her that wishes just to hide and lick her wounds. As it is, her mate has seen her in worse states – and seen her through them too.

Then Tilathis’ eyes fix on her back legs.

You bear traces of ropes – was it humans again? he demands, then flicks his eyes to Markus who doesn’t move. Other humans, Tilathis concedes, though still sounds unconvinced.

No, she answers shortly. Worse.

Her mate hesitates, his eyes asking a quiet question. Kalanthia looks away, her silence saying everything he needs to know.

Kalanthia…. Weren’t you the one who told me they’re dangerous? Tilathis’ protective fury rises once more – uselessly. The ones who held her are powerful enough that even an Immortal would not take them on lightly.

I had no choice! she snaps at him – clicking her teeth together in threat. Her mate knows better than to take the threat seriously, but instead of calming, he redirects his anger, turning to snap at Markus.

You! Human! Remove the ropes from my mate’s legs!

Kalanthia feels a sudden surge of amusement mixed with dread. She can feel in the Bond how close Markus is to losing his patience.

She can only hope it doesn’t come down to a fight. Because between her mate and the human who has protected and rescued both her and her daughter several times…supporting either side against the other would tear her in two.

And she’s so very tired of being torn to pieces.
