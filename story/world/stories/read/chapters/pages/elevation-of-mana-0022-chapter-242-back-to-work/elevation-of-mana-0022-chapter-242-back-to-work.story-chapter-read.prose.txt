Five days passed in a breeze. We'd found a spot near our old village, well outside of the new one that was being built there now, or the research station that I almost thought about going to check in on. They were fine, if they weren't fine, well, Chien knew how to contact us.

Magic really did make things easier. It was almost a cheat, how I could put up a house literally anywhere, build one from my mind in seconds so long as everything was available. Then my lovely Isha could make us food, comfort, all the good bits of home that I missed, for she'd gotten quite formidable while I wasn't looking herself.

Five days, five days lounging, talking, doing... what spouses do. It seemed to pass in a flash, here one moment and done the next.

“We could stay longer,” I offered.

“You'd go stir crazy,”

“A fair point.” I had indeed enjoyed my vacation, immensely, but even I knew I would miss my work before long.

Disassembling the little home we'd made was a mere act of will, a wave of the hand and it was gone. That was almost sad, but Isha had planted a small tree there, one of the ones that eventually became the massive giants still used to home people to the North of Atal. It would take generations to grow to anything, if it did, but I was willing to wait, and so was she.

Getting back home wasn't hard either, a simple teleport, finally reliable after several years of practice, and we were home before dark.

“How goes things?” I asked Chien as I came into the office, seeing him sitting at my own desk.

“Oh swimmingly,” he replied, handing e my list, basically everything was checked off.

“What?” I asked, not believing his cheek.

“I set up programs to handle those, handled the ones other than that myself. Oh, your wife helped me with a bunch of it before you two left, she's been picking away at your list too, just didn't tell you.”“Seriously? Did you plan this?”“Yes, of course. The look on your face is all the reward I need.”“That's almost all of it,” I murmured.

“Almost, there were one or two things I couldn't do...”“Fair enough, okay... redesign the golem eyes, yeah, that one's for me. Two projects I need to check and sign off on...”“I checked those boss, you need to sign off still, but they're right.” I thought about his words for a minute, tapping my finger against my chin, then I sat down at the small desk he kept in here and began drafting a new missive, on his paper, with his pen. Chien laughed in the background as I moved to sign, then place a stamp from one of my rings on it.

“What's this all then?” he asked.

“Congratulations, you're now authorized to act as my hands, your words are my words, your commands mine, your signing off, is my signing off. You are now officially recognized as such. To be fair you've unofficially be recognized as such for years, but here's the paper for it.” I laughed. “You're the boss when I'm away now.”“Oi, oi boss don't say things like that!” he yelled at me, and I got a thorough laugh.

“You wanted to finish my projects, you did even, and now you've got full authority to do so. I trust you to use it as you see fit.”“Bold choice,” Chien said.

“Earned trust my friend. Earned over many, many years. I've trusted you with my life, with my child, with my kingdom. You've never once betrayed that trust.”

He looked at it for a long, long time. “Shit,” he finally declared.

“Yes, that's right Chien, responsibility, that is your reward for being responsible!”“Perhaps I should endeavor not to be in the future,” he joked.

“Good luck, but wait until I'm home. Now, I need... an airship, and golems, and... well, to prepare for our trip! I leave the things here in your capable hands.”

“Have fun boss,” he said as I leapt from the room.

By days end there were men at work, me, primarily. I already had plans, and ample materials, and I'd wanted these before, so some of the groundwork was being run at least. Without hesitation I began work on the airships that would take my wife, myself, and our entourage to the new continent, big, safe, maybe even comfortable if I could manage, nothing like the one I'd taken to battle. It would be a good surprise for our little Adia.

This became my work for the next several weeks, only stopping to see if I was needed for anything further. One or two minor matters, people who wanted to speak to me rather than Chien. Those got told to get over it by me personally, then shown the missive I'd written to that effect. One got thrown out of my palace.

Isha got a good laugh out of me working like this too, shaking her head.

“My husband doesn't know what to do if not work,” she declared. “Even when he hands over all of his work to another he find new work to do. It's who he is.”

Yet for the first time in a long time she aided me with these things. She gave her thoughts on the dirigible we'd be using. Asked for a space for the two of us, decorated it herself. She added a few bits here and there to the design, all well within tolerances that I didn't even need to redesign anything for. I would have of course, I would have given her anything she wanted. To see her with me, working at this was a true joy. She so seldom helped me with my work that I could hardly take my eyes off her the whole time, or my hands. Old or elven or not I was still a man after all.

This of course made the work take longer, but I didn't mind, and I was fairly sure my wife didn't either.
