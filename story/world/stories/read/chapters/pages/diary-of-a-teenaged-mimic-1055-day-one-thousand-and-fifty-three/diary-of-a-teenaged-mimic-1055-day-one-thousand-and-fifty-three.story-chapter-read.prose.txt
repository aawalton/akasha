Dear Diary,

Y'know, I never thought I'd not only discover something simple and practical that I could do to help with my chronic depression, but also something that I could actually maintain, what with the completely overwhelming unmedicated ADHD. But here we are. Getting out of the house on a regular schedule to do something I know is objectively helping make the world a better place really does help keep the Black Dog from biting. Then again, it probably doesn't hurt that I'm doing not one but two different things like that, and one of those two things gets me some reasonable exercise.

Because I'm not really the duBois 'stand in the middle of the Practice Yard barking out direction, encouragement, and correction as needed' kind of phys ed teacher. I'm more the 'run alongside, doing everything they're doing, while I'm also giving them tips and encouragement and even help' kind of coach. Okay, I also jump from student to student while I'm doing it. Which, now that I think about it, might be a little intimidating for some of the Cadets, but the flip side of that is that if they're gonna be incapable of performing because they're intimidated by somebody who is presently out of their league, they might be in the wrong profession.

On the one hand I technically understand that it has been a long, long time since I faced someone who was legitimately on my level. Fuck, I don't think I've faced somebody who was on my level since I broke myself out of that chest on the bottom of the river. Thing is, I didn't realize that. I didn't know I'm a small continent that decided to make the world a better place. Okay, the me I think of as me that actually does thinking rather than just, I dunno, emoting at conscious me and dreaming isn't that big, but that's the only thinking part of me, and for quite a while I figured that small part of me was the only part I could reliably count on.

But even if I never really was in that position of 'one tiny human-shaped person against everything the assholes in this world can throw at me, up to and including the world itself', I sure as fuck felt like it. So I know the Cadets need to get to a point where they're not going to be easily intimidated.

I mean, yeah, I'd like for them to get to the point where they're just not intimidated by shit, where the knee-jerk response to someone deliberately trying to do so is to get up in that asshole's grille and make them throw down or stand down, but there's some really intimidating shit out in the wide world here. Dragons the size of small islands or towns. Liches who can fling lightning. Deities who think being a God gives them the right to lord it over people.
