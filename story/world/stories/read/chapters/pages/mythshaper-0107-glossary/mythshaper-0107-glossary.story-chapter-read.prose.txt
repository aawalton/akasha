Glossary

!Mild Spoilers Ahead!

A few readers have pointed out that the story needs a glossary, as some terms seemed to slip off their heads. So, even though this is not complete, this may help with that. More information will be added as the story progresses.

Hierarchy:

Plebeian: Commoner; can be titled or untitled. Only titled plebeians can own land.

Equites: Titled plebeians or legionnaires who have served the empire for years.

Patricians: High nobles.

Note: One can climb from lower ranks to higher, but can also fall to lower ranks. More info will be added later.

Places:

Karmel Valley (Karmel): Arilyn's home. A part of the Dimir District.

Dimir District: The region in which Karmel Valley is located. A remote, agrarian area.

Klearon: A great city connected to four districts. A large city with a Waygate, serving as a central trade and gathering center.

Vashar: Home of the Amadeus family. A mountainous region, implied to be colder and wilder than Karmel Valley. Home of Winterheart Deer and Sabertooth Tigers.

Althelon: A pilgrimage spot of myth and legends.

Nalanda, The City of Stars: Main neutral city for the Awakened. Home of Oracle Academy, United Guild Coalition, and more.

Revalas: Capital of the Ablberan Empire.

Olympus: Highest mountain

The Lonely Wanderer: Inn in Klearon.

Oracle Academy: A prestigious institution for magical study.

Spirit Realm/Aetheric realms: Source of aether.

Groups/Organizations:

The Knights of the Emberleaf: A group devoted to the Tree of Life and pledged to service.

The United Guild Coalition: A force behind the world, made up of various magical factions that prioritize their interests over the world as a whole.

Serpent's Spite: A smaller guild serving as the police in the land in Dimir district.

Gifts & Paths:

Gifts: Unique, inherent abilities. Can be essence-based or soul-based.

Paths: An Awakened's specialty or calling.

Shaper: A path that enables one to mold elements, manipulate the building blocks of creation, and control magic.

Augmenter: A path that enhances primarily physical capabilities.

Classes: Hierarchical system denoting progression within a given Path, including Common, Noble, Prestigious, Fabled, Heroic, Legendary, and Mythic...

Honors: Unique qualifications given for displaying incredible achievement.

Crafting/Smithing Terms:

Fabricator: A general term for crafted magical items.

Artifact: A high-quality fabricator with an internal faux weave, requiring a precise creation process.

Relic: An extremely old artifact with historical and cultural significance.

Apparatus: A system of tools designed for a particular task.

Engraving Pen: An instrument used by artisans and scribes to engrave runes.

Elensil: A special type of essence paper aiding in essence engraving and rune molding.
