Despite these people having done their utmost to kill him, Luke didn't want to go straight to the nuclear option and wipe them out. These sorts of attacks on The Band and himself didn't just come out of nowhere. Bored people don't go around attacking others in a city on the brink of being overrun with monsters and Singers. There was a reason somewhere in this, and he intended to get to the bottom of it.

The two guys on the couch tried to stand, but Luke brought out his staff and used the butt to push one back down, leaning on it as he reached into the other with threads of mana. Having wrapped one thread around the trachea to stem the flow of oxygen to his lungs, Luke leaned forward even more and punched the still breathing one in the face. Judging by the lack of resilience, neither of these two was a fighter. One clawed at his throat, getting just enough air to stay alive for the moment, while the others took a fist-induced nap.

That was three down. A fourth barreled into the living room where Luke appeared by weavestepping to Lee.

"You!" the man shouted.

He was bald with wide shoulders and a nose that must've been broken more than once before. With all that bulk, he reminded Luke of a bouncer at some club, more than anything else. By the sound of this man's voice, it was the one who'd berated Lee just before Luke's escape.

"Me," Luke said, stepping toward him.

A second later, he found himself crashing into the far wall, narrowly missing a window. Vision spinning from the back of his head, hitting what felt like concrete, Luke fell to his knees, his staff bouncing to the floor. Breathing hard, he pushed to meet a furious charge from the bouncer.

'Meet' was perhaps not the best way to describe it, since he had no intention of going head-to-head with this guy again, not when he'd broken three metacarpal bones and at least one phalanx bone when his fist connected with the side of his opponent's head. The bouncer was tough as a tank.

While the latest Weavestep with Manipulate Thread hadn't damaged him as much as the previous jumps, it had still taken a lot out of him. Luke didn't think he could take this guy in a stand-up fight on his best of days, and this was far from that. Good thing he had plenty of other tools at his disposal.

The bouncer closed in with surprising speed, considering how small his legs were compared to his upper body, but the hook was slow enough that Luke could slip under it and past, heading to the couch where he undid the thread keeping the first man from drawing a proper breath. A couple of seconds more, and it would've had lasting effects. Another half minute and the guy would be well on his way to permanent sleep.

Leaving him to his relieved breathing, now almost emptied of mana, Luke turned to see the bouncer having closed in again, shouting as he approached. "Time to end this!"

Luke had the same idea. First, he dodged back from a quick jab, then under another swing, right into a knee he hadn't anticipated. Head thrown back and up, Luke braced himself for another blow. This one sent him crashing into the guys on the couch.

"Ow," Luke muttered as his body knitted itself back together. This was not the time for Needle of Life, so Bountiful Recovery would have to do the work. Before his head stopped spinning, the bouncer was on him again. A slight sheen of light surrounded him with every punch, and it didn't take a genius to figure out he was using some skill to improve his movement and punching power.

"Let's see how you do that without mana," Luke growled, reaching forward with several threads of mana.

The bouncer stepped back, wary. It was the right idea, but he wasn't careful enough. With Luke so close to the couch, he'd moved one thread into the leftmost guy sitting in it, continued on to the next, then out to reach the bouncer. Rather than drain mana straight away, he wrapped it around the man's wrist as he stepped into punching range again. When another blow came, a right straight this time, Luke pulled on the thread. It put the attacker off balance and gave Luke the opening he needed.

Going in low, Luke used another thread, wrapping around his legs even as he kicked at the back of the bouncer's leg, bending the knee. Stumbling, the asshole couldn't move his feet well enough to catch himself, stumbling over Lee's still unconscious form on the floor.

This time, he was the one to crash into the couch, and Luke took the opportunity to wrap him up in more threads of mana. Each thread pulled mana from the big guy, and it turned out he didn't have a whole lot of it to spare.

Keeping an eye on the entrance to the room, Luke stepped up and pulled enough mana to force the bouncer into unconsciousness. He'd wake up with the mother of all headaches, but at least Luke hadn't killed him. Using The Healer's Moment, he could have ended the fight at any moment, but he was still wary of others. Two more people were in the apartment, but they hadn't made a sound.

Leaning in toward the one Luke had choked the fight out of, he spoke in a low voice. "What are the others doing?"

"Huh?" the guy, a twenty-something with sand-brown hair and almost no chin, asked. He looked terrified. "Please don't kill me."

Huh. He thought the other three were dead. That served Luke's purpose well enough.

"There are more people in the apartment. Tell me about them. Now."

"T-there should o-only be o-one," the gormless asshole stammered. "S-sandra is a mage."

"Why isn't she coming in here?"

"I-i don't know."

Luke straightened and raised his voice to call out into the apartment. "Sandra?"

"Yeah?"

The female voice came from across the hall, in what he thought was the kitchen.

"Are you with these guys?"

It was silent for a moment before she replied. "If I say no, will you leave me alone?"

"No."

"Well, I'm not," she said with a sigh loud enough to carry all the way to the living room. "I guess I am, but not really. One of those guys in there is my boyfriend. Was my boyfriend. It's complicated."

"The big one?"

"No. I hate that guy. Did you kill him?"

"Not yet," Luke said, growing a little irritated at the woman's uninterested inflection.

"Yacob is the one with the white shirt."

"He's alive too," Luke said. "Why don't you come in here? I won't hurt you if you tell me what I need to know."

"Can't do that."

Luke sighed. "Why not?"

Ray: "I'm back from the dungeon. Your sister said something about you almost dying. Again. What's up?"

Milla: "Luke? Are you alive? I've got people outside headquarters now, but they don't see you."

Luke: "I'm alive, Milla. I've got it under control."

Milla: "That sort of reckless behavior will not be tolerated. This is your guildmistress talking, not just your sister."

Luke: "Won't happen again."

Luke leaned back to look out the window, confirming his suspicion. The guild headquarters was right outside, twelve floors or so down.

Luke: "I'm still alive, Ray. Don't worry about me."

Ray: "Wouldn't dream of it."

"I'll get shot, probably," Sandra said. "Min-Hee is totally out there in the hallway with her stupid rifle."

"Shut up, bitch!" another voice called. It was farther away and had a slight echo to it. Min-Hee was outside the apartment, looking in, he figured.

Luke: "I could use a favor, though."

Ray: "Name it."

Luke explained the situation and gave Ray his location. Some backup wouldn't hurt.

"You're the one who shot me?" Luke asked, raising his voice even more. The bouncer stirred, so Luke siphoned some more mana from him, then did the same to Lee. With that, he was topped off with both regular mana and healing mana stored in his Meta-Heart.

"Yeah. Did you like it?" Min-Hee shouted.

Luke was losing his patience with the situation. "You shot me in the head more than once, and I'm still here! How do you think this will work out for you? Just come in here instead!"

"I've got bigger bullets this time!"

"Sandra," Luke called. "Can you close the front door?"

"Not without getting shot," Sandra called back. She was doing something out there in the kitchen now. "I'm making coffee! Want some?"

"If you show your ugly face, I'll shoot you!" Min-Hee shouted.

"See? Told you!" Sandra said.

"Coffee sounds good," Luke said, shaking his head before looking down at the one conscious guy on the couch. "You're with Ming-Hee? Is she your girlfriend?"

The guy shuddered. "Hell no. She's with him," he said, indicating the bouncer before adding. "She scares me."

"Why?" Luke asked.

Ray: "There's a chick here on the floor with something that looks like a CheyTac M2000 Intervention, except it's glowing red."

Luke: "A what?"

Ray: "It's a sniper rifle. Why would she use it up close like this?"

Ray: "Should I ask her? Maybe not."

Luke: "Can you disable her?"

Ray: "Disable?"

Luke: "Knock her out or something."

Ray: "...A little late for that."

Ray: "Unless you get here in like... Nope. Never mind."

Luke: "Is she dead?"

Ray: "Yeah. She twitched. Thought she saw me or something."

Luke took a moment, then raised his voice to shout again. "Sandra! You can come out here now. The coast is clear."
