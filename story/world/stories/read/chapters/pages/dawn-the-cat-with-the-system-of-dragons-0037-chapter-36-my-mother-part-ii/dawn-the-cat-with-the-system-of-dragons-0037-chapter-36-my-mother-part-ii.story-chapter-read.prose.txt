It was morning. The sunrays were penetrating the dense canopy, dissipating the fog, revealing a calm, beautiful environment. But within this calm forest, something dangerous was brewing inside.

The animals that had run away before the cyclone began returning to their previous territories. Many animals took this chance to claim a better area than before; many gave up their previous territories without a struggle, and many began preparing for a life-or-death battle.

And during this impending chaos, Dawn was sleeping soundly inside her den. From getting swept up in a cyclone to seeing Elenora use her large-scale explosion, every event she faced the previous day was too extreme. Her built-up exhaustion made her sleep throughout the morning.

But for her kittens, everything was as usual. As soon as the sun began rising, they woke up and latched onto her belly. This was their second meal since Dawn went to sleep. Even the infant dragon was scuffling with the kittens to satiate his hunger.

The first time he woke up, it was still midnight, and he didn't want to act like a dumb kitten, so he ignored his hunger, tried to stay sane, and when he couldn't fight his hunger anymore, he went back to sleep.

The second time he woke up, he was so starved that he threw away his sense of ‘creepy behavior’, crawled out of his new ditch, climbed above the sleeping kittens, and went straight for the source of milk. His desperate movement ended up waking up all the kittens.

Seeing him drinking milk, the kittens began pushing each other and went straight for their own drinking spots. But the infant dragon ignored their unspoken rule and began drinking milk from the best possible position, which belonged to the biggest kitten.

Seeing this, the biggest kitten crawled towards his spot and tried to push aside the dragon. But the dragon had no intention of backing away. You had your share of milk previously; I didn’t even drink any… Go away!!! He continued drinking milk and began pushing the kitten with his hindlimbs.

Because of the scuffle between the infant dragon and the biggest kitten, the two medium-sized kittens, which were just beside them, were also struggling to get into their own positions.

Only the two remaining kittens were enjoying their meal. Since they were the smallest among all the kittens and their usual drinking spot was not particularly good, no other kitten bothered them, and they were able to enjoy their drinking time as usual.

After a minute of struggling, the biggest kitten began biting the dragon's limbs. OUCH! You want to play this game… Fine… In retaliation, the infant dragon continued drinking and began kicking the kitten with all his might.

In the end, the two kittens beside them were not able to drink even a single drop. The medium-sized kittens got so annoyed that they also joined the fray, and the one-on-one scuffling turned into a four-way scuffle.

But even after so much chaos just above her belly, Dawn didn't wake up. She kept snoring in her sleep.

After a few minutes, their scuffling became so intense that two medium-sized kittens decided to turn this into a food battle. They immediately began biting and scratching the dragon and the biggest kitten. But now, the dragon was full, and he had a lot of energy to spare.

You A*******, you guys have been annoying me too much… Now see what happens; I’m not letting you have even a single drop of milk. Simultaneously, the infant dragon began pushing, biting, and scratching all three kittens.

But tackling all three kittens at once was not that easy. In the end, the food battle got so heated that, in rage, the infant dragon unintentionally activated his electric ability, suddenly shocking everyone at once.

“MEOEOEOEOEOEOEOEOEOOOOO...” In shock, Dawn jumped in her sleep, hit the ceiling hard, and positioned herself to land perfectly; then, after landing, she immediately prepared to dodge the next attack. But when she looked around rapidly, there was no enemy.

“Miew miew miew miew miew.” All the kittens were lying scattered outside their ditch, mewling in pain from being electrocuted and getting thrown by half-asleep Dawn. The infant dragon was also in the same state, but the only difference was that he was still generating electricity throughout his body.

Dawn was confused. What just happened? She looked at all her mewling babies, but when she noticed the state of the white infant dragon, she thought, Did this one just shock me???

Her anger rose so much that she unintentionally began releasing her energy with killing intent. Because of her half-asleep state, her killing intent was a lot more intense than the previous day.

Gradually, her eyes began glowing golden, her body began emitting a golden haze, and consequently, all her babies began feeling a suffocating pressure, especially the infant dragon. They were unable to move even a single inch.

Within a few seconds, all of them began mewling in distress. This brought Dawn back to reality. She looked at them and thought, They are all distressed!!!

As soon as her anger turned into a genuine concern, her killing intent stopped gushing out, and all the babies felt immediate relief. But all of them were extremely exhausted.

After a few seconds, the kittens began crawling towards Dawn, hoping to get the protective warmth of their mother. Only the infant dragon stayed where he was, observing Dawn with a scared look. He was still emitting electricity throughout his body.

Looking at them, Dawn sighed and began moving the kittens back to their ditch one by one. After finishing, it was finally time to deal with the infant dragon.

She moved towards the baby and prepared herself to be electrocuted again. But before she could grab the dragon by the nape, a system notification popped up.

[SYSTEM NOTIFICATION]

[Temporary user,

This is a notification from the Aegis System.

The primary system installer chose to make you the host of the Aegis System for several reasons:

But if you, the temporary host, direct your killing intent towards the original user, or do anything that will endanger the original user, Aegis System will intervene accordingly without any prior notice.]

In the back of her mind, Dawn knew that she was accidentally electrocuted by her adopted baby because he didn't even know how to control his own power. So initially, she let go of her anger.

But when the system notification popped in front of her face, the limit of her patience was broken. Not because she understood what was written there, but because she hated the system with a passion, and more importantly, this obstructed her vision yet again.

She closed her eyes, controlled her anger, and then opened her eyes again to look through the translucent pop-up. Then she began licking the sparking infant dragon. Just like the previous day, she pushed through despite feeling an intense electric shock through her mouth.

While she was trying to calm down the infant dragon, the fixed distance between the system pop-up and her face made the pop-up window disappear into the ground.

The infant dragon didn’t miss the movements of the system window. What the hell is that… Why do I feel like I have seen this before? Wait! Why is it following the cat’s face?

The moment he tried to remember, he started having light migraine. Then he remembered the consequences of trying to remember forcefully, so he stopped and began focusing on Dawn’s rough tongue instead. What a day… He sighed. Now I’m relieved because a huge cat is currently licking my body with its enormous tongue… wow… I should award myself…

This distracted him from his initial fear, and the uncontrolled sparks generating from his body gradually stopped.

Dawn didn’t miss this chance. She picked him up, holding his nape, and carried him to his ditch. Then she went to the kittens and began licking them roughly to settle them down.

Now that her immediate problem was resolved, she could focus on what she was dying to do. She slowly walked out of her den and began releasing her killing intent again. But this time, her intent was aimed at the system window, obstructing her vision.

She walked several meters away from the opening and suddenly began attacking it nonstop. First, she dove towards the window to reach it with her claws. But even after reaching it, her claws passed through, just like they did the first day. Then she stood on two hindlimbs and tried to do rapid fire with both pairs of claws, but again her claws passed through. Still, she had no intention to stop and continued her series of attacks.

From inside, the infant dragon and all five kittens were looking at their mother’s bewildering behavior. Sometimes she was running, and other times she was diving and attacking the system window while growling and meowing in anger.

Right, she is a cat in the end! The dragon sighed heavily and decided to ignore her. He curled up in her ditch and went back to sleep.

After half an hour, Dawn finished her venting session, and the notification window was still there. But there was nothing she could do. She had no way of removing this weird floating thing. Though she could approach Elenora, after what she did, there was no way Dawn would approach her.

In the end, Dawn accepted her fate and decided to go on as usual.

Since the sun was already up and Dawn had yet to go out hunting, instead of returning to her den, she decided to go deeper into the forest. But then she remembered something important. Yesterday, she had hunted down many wyverns, and many of their bodies were lying not too far from her den.

She happily ran towards the wyverns and thought, For the next few days, I didn’t need to hunt with obstructed vision.

She reached the closest wyvern and immediately began inspecting its corpse. From the smell of the meat, she could tell that it was very high quality. She could not wait any longer to tear off the best meat from the body of the wyvern.

What she didn’t know was that the wyvern meat was a rare delicacy, and it was especially popular among high-ranking adventurers for various reasons. And she had piles and piles of wyvern meat.

After finishing her inspection, she tore a small piece and immediately carried it to her den. But as soon as Dawn reached her destination, the infant dragon woke up at the smell of delicious meat. The kittens also began raising their heads to look at what their mother brought; they were not that interested.

But the infant dragon had another story; his stomach began growling again. He crawled up his ditch and began calling Dawn. “Eeeeeuuu eeeeuuuu”

Dawn knew it was not yet time to feed meat to her kitten, but she was not sure about her adopted son. She picked up the entire piece of meat and placed it in front of the infant dragon.

The infant dragon began eating as if he had never eaten before, and Dawn was looking at him, bewildered. Within a few minutes, the entire piece of meat was finished. Dawn was not even able to have a single bite.

The infant dragon called his mother again, hoping to get more, and Dawn heard this message loud and clear. She sighed and ran out of the den to bring even more meat.

After a while, she brought a five times bigger chunk of meat than last time. She gave one third of it to the dragon, and she took the two thirds of it, thinking, This should be more than enough for this creature.

But within a few minutes, she understood how wrong her assumption was. The dragon finished his part very fast and began asking for more. Dawn hasn't yet finished even half of it. She gave the rest of her share to the infant dragon.

The infant dragon finished even Dawn’s share and began asking for more. Now Dawn was finally annoyed. She picked up the dragon by its nape and began carrying him towards the wyvern corpse. You like meat! Fine… Have as much as you want.
