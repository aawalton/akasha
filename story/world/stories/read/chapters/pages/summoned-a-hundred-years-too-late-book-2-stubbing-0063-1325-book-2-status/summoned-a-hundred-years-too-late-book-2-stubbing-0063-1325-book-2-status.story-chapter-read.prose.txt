Class Perks:

Quest Ledger – Unlocks Event Quests and Training Quests. Event Quests are granted based on external circumstances, with a reward dependent on the quest’s difficulty. Training Quests are designed to help you improve your skills. Select a skill to generate a quest that will reward you with a skill level.

Adventuring Contract – Draft a contract to share your quests. Set the share of the reward in agreement with the quester. Daily and skill quests excluded. Contracted questers must be of the same rank or have a maximum difference of 15 levels.

Streak Enhancement – Improve the bonuses received for each streak milestone. Applies retroactively to all streak milestones that have already been achieved.

Contribution Points – Replace all non-daily quest rewards with contribution points. Contribution points can be traded for rewards of your choosing. The number of awarded contribution points is equivalent to the value of the original reward.
