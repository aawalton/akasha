Charlemagne was pleased with himself. He had baited the trap perfectly, using his top hat, still in helmet form, to surprise Drago. Had it not been for the Item’s invulnerability, it would have never held up to even a glancing blow from the incredible sword that the Boss wielded.

Incredible…and edible.

The rooster’s beak snapped shut, catching the blade just above the hilt. Drago roared and attempted to pull the sword away, but Charlemagne gamely hung on, pouring his vast reserves of mana into the crystalline lattice that formed the sword’s outer layer. The rooster’s newfound Magic Smithing Skill was still low level, but, now that he was in contact with the Item directly, he was able to sense some of the Item’s physical properties.

Whatever material Phosphor was made of did not behave like a true metal, meaning that it could perform under conditions that normal metals could not tolerate. For one thing, a metal sword would have softened under the blazing heat that emanated from the mana-dense core. And, no matter how strong a metal was, there was an upper limit on how much pressure it could resist before beginning to deform.

“Let go! Vile beast!” the draconic creature shouted as he continued to wave his sword around with one hand while pummeling Charlemagne with the other. The rooster barely noticed the powerful impacts. Like a moth to a forbidden lamp, he was drawn to the sword, entranced by it, teased by it. He had to have it. His jaw muscles groaned in protest as his beak pressed down onto the blade like a hydraulic press.

Phosphor’s exterior, composed of crystalline, covalent bonds, could not deform under the extreme heat and pressure. It was hard, harder than the purest diamond. And diamonds do not bend. Diamonds shatter.

With a squawk of effort, Charlemagne’s bite force exceeded the sheer strength of Phosphor’s blade. The resulting snap was so loud that it nearly perforated the rooster’s eardrums. But a little bit of temporary hearing loss was a small price to pay for such a prize. Suddenly freed of Charlemagne’s rather substantial weight hanging from the end of his sword, Drago lost his balance, falling onto his backside.

Without wasting a moment, the rooster swallowed Phosphor’s blade whole. His Inferno’s Embrace Skill protected him from the worst of the searing heat that threatened to fry his digestive tract from the inside out, while his regenerative powers kicked into overdrive to mend the damage that the white-hot sword was causing. Charlemagne’s stomach gurgled even harder as the wonderful aroma of cooked meat filled his nostrils.

Then the blade reached deep enough that his Special Ability, Food for the Stomach and the Stomach for Food, considered Phosphor to be “ingested”, which kicked off the real fun. The blade, despite not melting under its own incredible heat, began to dissolve in the rooster’s stomach acid.

Pain followed. The rooster’s internal body temperature soared. His flesh, having long surpassed the limits of mere biology, endured, buoyed by a rush of pure power. His Ember Core roared as superheated mana flooded in, pushing the magical organ to its limits, and beyond. Another jolt of agony radiated out from the rooster’s center as his Core nearly tore itself apart before he clamped down on the mana within, forcing it to condense further than it ever had before. A moment later, a second source of internal pressure began to manifest: the air that had accompanied the blade into Charlemagne’s stomach was expanding rapidly. At first, the sensation was almost pleasurable, but the distention didn’t stop at mild bloating. The rooster’s torso ballooned out, his ribs and sternum creaking dangerously as the pressure mounted.

Thinking back to the last time this happened, Charlemagne recalled how he had previously resolved a similar issue by getting rid of the excess energy. He opened his mouth to begin charging up a mana blast.

An enormous burp rang out as the superheated air erupted from Charlemagne’s mouth, along with gouts of flame and dense black smoke. Drago, who had just stood up, was knocked back over.

Feeling much better, Charlemagne waited quite chivalrously for his opponent to get back up again. His delay had nothing to do with the incredible focus that it took to contain the unleashed power of a magical sword forged in the fiery breath of a draconic deity. But even chivalry had its limits. After a while, the rooster’s curiosity finally got the better of him, and he managed to waddle over to where the Boss lay. Well, the lower half of the Boss, at any rate. There was nothing left of Drago’s upper body except for his armor, which had miraculously escaped unscathed.

The crowd, which had gone completely silent in the aftermath of the rooster’s enormous burp, erupted into thunderous, if somewhat confused, applause as the smoke finally cleared. Above the roar of the masses, the booming voice of the announcer cut in.

“Good creatures in the audience, the battle has been decided! Phosphor, the Unquenchable Flame, has been broken! Drago, the Master of Iron and Fire no more! Charlemagne is the victor! All hail Charlemagne, Devourer of Blades!”

The crowd continued to cheer as Charlemagne basked in the approbation of the masses. His stomach was still churning, and his Ember Core felt raw and angry, but he was happy nonetheless. Whatever the magical material was that had made up Phosphor’s blade, it had filled him up completely. He was comfortably stuffed.

“That was incredible!” Phatagin cried as he ran up. “Was that flamethrower attack the result of a new Skill? It certainly surprised the Boss!”

The pangolin laughed gleefully as Charlemagne silently accepted his Squire’s praise.

“Oh, I must tell you that I finally acquired the ‘Mana Sense’ Skill! I think it will give me better options for my next Class Evolution!”

The rooster’s sarcastic remark died on his beak as the Squiggles arrived with his well-deserved final score…and his rewards.
