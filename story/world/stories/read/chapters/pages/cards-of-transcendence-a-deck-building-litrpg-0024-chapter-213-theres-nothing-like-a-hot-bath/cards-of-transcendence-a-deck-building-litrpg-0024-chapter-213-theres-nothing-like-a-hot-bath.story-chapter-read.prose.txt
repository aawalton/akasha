When Flynn and Nyx got to the Golden Compass, they found their horses in the stables behind the inn. There was no sign of Throm, but the innkeeper informed them that the mercenary had secured three rooms for them.

“You will find a change of clothes in your rooms,” the short, stout innkeeper told them. “Leave your clothing outside your door and it will be cleaned and repaired.”

Flynn frowned, worried about leaving his Elite and Epic rank armored clothing in a hallway where anyone could just take it.

The innkeeper smiled, brushing a strand of graying-brown hair behind her ear. “Do not worry about your clothes, young master. All of your belongings are safe here at the Golden Compass. As far as washing and repairing, we use the Gilded Washboard. The owner possesses an Epic level Radiant Cleanse card. They can handle anything below Legendary rank, and most gear at that level already has both self-cleaning and self-repairing enchantments.”

“The Gilded Washboard is used by many high-level citizens of Khalreth,” Nyx assured him then turned her attention back to the innkeeper. “That takes care of our gear. What about a bath.”

“Two baths have already been prepared,” the innkeeper said. “We have a communal bath house. I assume you have no problem bathing in the same room.”

Flynn felt his face flush.

Nyx smirked. “No problem at all. He doesn’t have anything I haven’t seen before.”

Flynn tried to force a scowl onto his face but felt like his effort probably fell flat.

The innkeeper handed room keys to both of them. “Your rooms are on the second floor. The bath house is out back. There are robes in your room.”

Flynn grabbed his key and headed for the stairs. He heard Nyx tell the innkeeper something about him being shy as he climbed the stairs.

Once he reached his room, he quickly removed his dirty clothes and armor and put on the robe. He hurried to the bath house, making sure that he was fully submersed in his tub before Nyx arrived a few minutes later.

Despite her prior insinuations, Nyx acted as modestly as possible when she entered the bath house a few minutes after Flynn. She wrapped a towel around herself before removing her robe, only removing it after she was fully submersed.

Flynn got the feeling that she was hiding her card tattoos as much as her body, but once they were both submersed, he allowed himself to relax. His muscles relaxed as his eyes closed, and he succumbed to exhaustion.

When his eyes fluttered open again, his water was cool and he was alone in the bathhouse.

After returning to his room and dressing in the spare clothes left for him, Flynn went to the common room where he found Nyx sitting at a table in the corner. There was a mug of ale in her hand and another in front of the empty chair next to her. She waved him over and motioned to the empty chair and full mug. “I ordered a blackberry ale for you. It’s a local favorite.”

“Thank you,” Flynn said with a nod as he pulled out the chair and sat down. He grabbed the mug and slowly brought it toward his mouth, inhaling the aroma of the berry brew before bringing it to his lips and taking a drink.

The sweetness of the berries hit him first, followed by the natural bitterness of an ale. He nodded in approval, commenting “that’s pretty good,” before taking another, deeper drink.

Nyx flashed a smile that said, “I told you.”

Flynn sat in silence, drinking his ale as he looked around the room. The common room of the Golden Compass was similar to every other inn or tavern he’d been in, but there was a noticeable difference in the clientele. Most of the patrons were obviously powerful people who carried themselves in a manner that exuded confidence, but they didn’t force their power on others, either through boisterous comments or aggressive actions.

These men and women know they are powerful and have no need to try and convince others of that fact, Flynn realized.

He also recognized that that made them incredibly dangerous people.

After that realization, Flynn’s eyes—and Analyze card—started picking out weapons that were far more powerful than their worn appearance indicated.

It was another sign of true power, as opposed to the flashiness most nobles opted for.

“Is everyone here a powerful adventurer?” he asked Nyx softly.

“Or mercenary,” she replied.

“Do we need to be worried about that?”

“Why, do you intend to start a fight?”

“No. It’s just…”

Nyx put a hand on his arm. “Don’t worry. This place isn’t like Aramath. At least not here. The people that come to the Golden Compass possess real power. They don’t need to lord it over others to make themselves feel strong. And if they did, the Compass has means to handle it.”

Flynn relaxed, at least a little bit, but stayed vigilant.

As he searched the room for trouble, he caught sight of Throm the instant he came through the front door. The large man spotted them immediately and quickly crossed the room, only slowing to wave at the barkeep to order a drink.

“Well this city has sure turned into a powder keg,” the mercenary commented as he sat down. A serving girl put a mug of ale on the table almost immediately after.

“Yeah, we’ve already experienced that,” Nyx said.

Throm raised an eyebrow. “Let me guess, Church of Knowledge?”

“Yep,” the blue haired woman replied. She pulled her new gun from its holster and put it on the table. “I had to pull this out just to keep the peace.”

Flynn leaned forward and looked at the weapon. “I’ve been meaning to ask you. Where did you get that? I saw your old Thunder Thrower explode.”

Nyx waggled her eyebrows. “Can’t a girl have any secrets?”

Flynn frowned. “You’ve got plenty of secrets, but if you’ve got an unlimited supply of powerful weapons, I think we should know about it.”

“Hmph,” she grunted. “My supply is not unlimited. At least not yet.”

Throm also leaned forward, his eyes going from the weapon with the glowing blue crystal to Nyx. “Now you really need to tell us more.”

Nyx gave the two men a self-satisfied smile. “Well, if you insist.”

She leaned back and put both hands behind her head. She looked toward Flynn. “As you know, I created a gun that uses an enchanted crystal to shoot lightning bolts like a magic wand.”

Her focus shifted to the mercenary who had never seen the weapon used and hadn’t been there when the original Thunder Thrower was destroyed. “Unfortunately, that original weapon was destroyed.”

Nyx leaned forward and put a hand on the gun on the table. “I gave my plans to someone I know at the Storm Temple. This one is a protype for mass production.”

Throm leaned back and whistled. Flynn just stared at the weapon wide eyed for a moment before asking, “What are you going to do with all those weapons?”

The blue haired woman sat up straight and gave Flynn a serious look. “Didn’t you hear what Throm said? This place is about to blow up, and the Church of Knowledge is going to be at the center of everything. If we want to stand up against them, we’re going to need powerful weapons. A lot of them.”

Flynn was silent for a moment. He definitely didn’t like the idea of fighting an entire religion, but he couldn’t ignore the fact that he likely wouldn’t be given a choice. It was unlikely that the local members of the Church of Knowledge knew that Jerome was chasing them—at least he hoped they didn’t—but they probably would eventually. At that point, there wouldn’t be any way to avoid a fight.

He blew out a breath. “Okay. How many of those do you have.”

Nyx looked down, visibly deflating. “Just this one.”

Flynn’s head dropped too. “That’s not going to do us a lot of good.”

“I know. They are really, really expensive to make.” She looked up, determination in her eyes. “But now that we know it can be done, all we need to do is start saving up for mass production.”

“How do we do that?” Flynn asked.

“We?!” Throm said before Nyx could answer. “When I agreed to help you two, nobody mentioned you were working with the Temple of the Fallen God and would be going to war with the Church of Knowledge. That’s something a guy should know about before committing to an endeavor.”

The blue haired woman frowned and cocked her head to the side. “Are you backing out on us?”

The mercenary laughed. “Of course not. I just like to know about things that might get me killed.”

“You’re a mercenary. Everything you do might get you killed.”

Throm chuckled, but with a little less mirth. “True enough.”

He looked from Flynn to Nyx. “So, what are we going to do.”

“We’re going to wait for Khalor to reply to my message.”

“We can’t just sit around and do nothing,” Flynn said. “When the local church finds out Jerome is after us, we’re going to have problems.”

“There’s nothing we can do about that,” she replied, “but I didn’t say weren’t going to do anything. In fact, I’ve got a little excursion planned.”

Nyx looked from Flynn to Throm then announced, “I think it’s time for a dungeon dive.”

Throm slapped the table. “Now that’s a plan worth drinking to.”

The mercenary grabbed his mug and held it out over the table. Nyx grabbed her mug and clanked it into his.

Flynn sighed, grabbed his mug, and added his to the group.

The three quickly downed their mugs of ale and ordered another round. Nyx conveniently waited until Flynn had finished off three mugs before elaborating on her dungeon dive plans. By that point, the young man didn’t even care that the dungeon she was taking them to was under the Temple of the Deeper Realm.
