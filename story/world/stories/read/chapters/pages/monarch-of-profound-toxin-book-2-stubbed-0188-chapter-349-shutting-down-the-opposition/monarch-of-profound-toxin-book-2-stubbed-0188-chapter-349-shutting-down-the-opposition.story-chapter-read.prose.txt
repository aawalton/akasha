As one might expect of a serpent, Spaghetti Jones’ maw was capable of opening to absolutely physics-defying widths.

Eik had honestly never thought to see how big it could go. Turns out it was enough to basically swallow up the entirety of Gimleh. And it wasn’t a pretty sight.

At this point, not just the Gohkamorians, but everybody in attendance looked terrified. Even Eik’s friends and family, as well as the other Earthlings, who had come to observe the proceeding that might finally get Earthlings at least a little bit of rightful vengeance, didn’t appear convinced that this was such a good idea.

“Mr. Magnasen, please!” the judicial representative shouted. His voice was perfectly audible since, despite the leviathan size, Spaghetti Jones’ movements were frighteningly silent. It felt like a beast of that size should have made some kind of noise at least. “Please, do not overstep.”

Bathed in blue, Eik threw a glance back at the man. He didn’t look afraid of Eik himself as much as he was afraid that he would do something that would lead to more drastic consequences.

With a stutter, the X-ranker’s personal buffs faded in rapid succession. “Did you not hear what he just said?”

“Mr. Magnasen…”

Spaghetti Jones silently retreated halfway up until he hung in the middle of the sky, enormous eyes watching like a god beast. Eik spread his arms wide and addressed the crowd.

“Just recently, when I was appointed Elixir King, I promised to do everything I could to protect you all from the Lord of the Moon and his minions. I intend to keep that promise, now and in the future, and when we find him, I will make sure he can never return.”

Not a single person looked away as he spoke.

“And I was not going to ask for anything in return. It was going to be a no strings attached kind of thing, but now I’m not so sure that it can be like that after all.”

Breaths were held as the subject approached something grave.

“I am willing to put my life on the line for you, and so are my compatriots. This will not change. But I would like you all to remember that nobody did that for my people when we were being slaughtered. All I ask of you now is to consider how you might be able to support a people, who stood alone and powerless against a merciless omnicide, in a time when they might be able to regain some of the dignity lost. Please, speak up for what's right.”

Eik stood straight and silent, gaze firmly on the crowd, and bowed deeply to them all. For a good ten seconds he held a perfect ninety degree posture as the crowd stared dumbfounded.

Was an X-ranker, who was also a Worldbreaker, seriously bowing to them right now? Wouldn’t it make more sense for them to be forced to bow to him?

“Fools!” the Gohkamorian representative howled and whirled around to look out over those gathered behind him, his face darkening as the blood of anger rushed to his head. With his size, he would be visible even to those in the back. “Are you completely unable to see through his filthy tricks? It is obvious that he seeks to cause division among the civilizations of the Nidafjeld Alliance! This is the first step of total conquest!”

Eik remained silent. Protesting and trying to argue against the accusations would only make him seem desperate and potentially guilty. With so many people present, it was not easy to read expressions and gauge what people thought of the Gohkamorian’s theory, but it likely wouldn’t gain much traction.

Eik had proven himself to the people plenty of times at this point and although it might be more than a little unprecedented, Earth’s demand for the surrender of the Gohkamorian elite wasn’t really that unreasonable.

Abruptly, the largest man turned and dashed through the rows of seats, deftly avoiding toppling over any people or chairs. He moved with a swiftness that would be difficult to follow for anyone except Eik, and caught a rock thrown from somewhere off to the side. It would have hit one of the S-ranker’s weaker compatriots.

“Fuck you, you filthy pieces of garbage! Nobody’s going to believe the words of an ugly, dumb, murderous asshole like you!” a voice shrieked across the heads of those gathered. Eik’s senses located the sources immediately.

It was a woman. A human woman. A human woman from Earth.

Still fairly new to the Unified Mass, and the Nidafjeld Alliance even more so, Eik had more or less been left to navigate the alien society on his own when it came to arguing Earth’s case, which was one of the reasons it had been a rather bumpy ride. Half the time he had absolutely no idea what he was doing, and it was only now, when he had reached a point where he could genuinely say he had become powerful and influential, that he was even beginning to make headway.

Way back when Mikla had been kind enough to help him figure out that the civilization responsible for the monster hordes tearing through humanity had indeed been the Gohkamorians, there had been nothing at all to be done about it. Any attempt from a fresh world like Earth to seek retribution and compensation would have been met by a solid wall and pushed back into the dirt by the much older and stronger civilization.

The expression on this woman’s face alone was proof that, just because most didn’t have a voice to speak up for themselves, that didn’t mean they didn’t have anything to say. And she wasn’t the only one. Many more humans shared her expression.

“You murdered my mom! You murdered my dad! My brother! You even murdered my grandparents!” she hurled another rock, but it flew wide, cracking off the raised wooden stage where Eik stood with a hollow thud. Tears streamed down her face seemingly endlessly. “My friends and family are dead! And it’s all because of you bastards!”

The Gohkamorian representative grimaced and crushed the rock in his fist as he listened to the screaming woman. With every words spat from her lips, her face grew redder and more incensed. None of the other people gathered made any moves to stop her tirade but did take several steps back to put distance between themselves and this obviously low-ranked woman continuously throwing verbal abuse at one of the most powerful Awakened in the Nidafjeld Alliance.

“You are evil! You’re demons! I vote for the eradication of all of you!” she screamed, looking around but appearing unable to find another rock to throw. “None of you should be left alive! I wish you would all die in pain!”

Eik let her continue. If even one human could use this opportunity to release at least a fraction of a decade-long accumulation of frustration, grief, and hatred, then he wasn’t going to be the man to deny her that. She herself was powerless to oppose the hostile alien civilization, but with an X-ranked Worldbreaker kinsman at her back, she was free to speak, or scream, her mind.

“Are you simply going to let her continue besmirching our name?” the furious Gohkamorian shouted, his eyes seeking the judicial representative, Imkoh, who looked absolutely ready to just call it a day and go home. Mitigating aggression between an S-ranker and an X-ranker was probably a good few paces out of his comfort zone.

All of the other Gohkamorians were chorusing the words of the largest, so he could already tell that this was going to become a great, damned headache later.

Imkoh was dressed for a normal day in court, for crying out loud! What use were clothes with no creases supposed to be for this situation? Even the high-level conflicts and disputes his office often took care of were always handled over the course of several meetings or in a court room. If anything more drastic was required, they had in-house security forces on standby to be deployed at any time.

But this? Yeah, actually, what the hell was this crap supposed to be? Imkoh felt his eyelid twitch as stress drove a hook into his stomach. Eik Magnasen had just dropped by one day—with no prior appointment, mind you—and told them that he needed someone who could make the thing he had planned “all official-like”, as he had put it.

And what had the higher-ups done? Acquiesced. That’s right! They had done absolutely nothing to oppose the guy, just because he was strong and was in the middle of saving all of their lives.

All right, maybe that was a pretty good reason to go along with it, but still, had it really been necessary to force Imkoh specifically into spearheading it?

He was helpless to do much more than shrug as his eyes flitted to Eik Magnasen who still stood silent on the stage, watching the whole thing go down like a statue.

The large Gohkamorian shifted his gaze to Eik instead then, spittle flying once more as he roared. “You! Rein in your citizens! This is public harassment!”

“Oh, get over yourself, you fragile little thing,” Eik muttered with a mocking pout. “You’re making a huge deal out of an insignificant little issue. Isn’t that exactly the kind of argument you yourself so love to employ?”

Wordlessly, the enormous man ground his teeth audibly as he stared daggers at the X-ranker. His fingers twitched with unreleased tension, his rage-muddled brain telling him to decapitate the haughty little man standing in front of him while every other fiber of his being was telling him to put his legs on his neck and get the hell out of there.

So distracted was he, that the rock that then came sailing in—this time thrown with considerably more careful aim—actually struck him cleanly in the back of the head. Given his S-ranked physique, an object thrown by a low-ranker would not hurt him whatsoever.

But in front of all these spectators… Damn, if it didn’t sting.

“You vicious bitch!” he howled as aura rushed out of him to wash over the crowd and crush them into their seats. “I’ve had enough of you!”

The Earthling woman who had thrown the first couple of rocks held up her hands. “Hey, wait! I didn't throw that one!”

But the Gohkamorian was already in the air, clearing all seats and people between them in a mad pursuit. Closing in, he raised a fist high to strike, but a blue hand suddenly seized him by the back of the neck, eliciting a croak, abruptly arresting all momentum and leaving him struggling as he hung in the air.

The elongated arm of Profound Toxin shrunk until the Gohkamorian hung before Eik. The Supreme Divinity of Toxin gazed up at him, fingers on his chin. Any trace of the aggressive aura had been eradicated in an instant by Eik’s own.

“So… who are you anyway?”

Fingers clawing at the steel grip around his throat, the Gohkamorian grunted an answer. “Hro-Hromlung, F-First Seat of the Se—… Second Gohkamorian Armada…”

“Excellent,” Eik said with a beaming smile.

Then an azure katana wielded at otherworldly speed took the Gohkamorian's head.
