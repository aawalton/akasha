Ashton looked at the blackened sky. There were cracks forming throughout it.

"Ah, shoot, is it really already over?" the masked spirit next to Ashton complained. "We've only been chatting for a couple hours at best."

"It's fine. We were able to do a lot of catching up. I'm glad that you're all doing alright," Ashton said, but the spirit just laughed.

"Of course we are. We've had a lot of time to heal. I'm more shocked that you're still able to push through it all and come out with a smile," the spirit pushed himself up from the ground.

"After spending a couple decades as a depressed, angry mess, you just kind of get sick of that, I guess."

"Hah, yeah, I remember that phase. You were so silly back then."

"Shut up," Ashton scoffed and laughed.

The spirit turned back to Ashton. "I wish you as much good fortune as you need to make it through this all. I hope you can create some good eyes for yourself in the future."

"I'll try my best. If I find the right ingredients, it should be fine. Though eyes aren't really a focus right now, I can see fine by channelling the elementals right now," Ashton explained. "I'm more worried about getting the proper equipment. I'll be forming a core soon, so I need things that will last."

The spirit's mask excitedly lit up with numerous bright colours. "In that case, I'm sure you'll like the gift that some of us prepared for you. We gave it to the Salesman you contracted through some loopholes. It's nothing ridiculous, since you'll still need to pay for it and all, but it should be in the salesman's permanent collection waiting for you. Hopefully, it can help you through the end of your world."

"Wait, seriously?" Ashton asked surprised. "Man, I don't know how to thank you."

"Then don't. Just see it as us trying to repay you a little bit for the debt that we all feel we incurred with you," the masked spirit said. "And there is one more thing."

His wing came out from under the cloak, and some claws grabbed onto his mask as he slowly pulled it off. Ashton turned away immediately, but the spirit stopped him.

"No, look at me."

Ashton did as his friend asked, and looked at his face. Those of his clan, the 'Mask Makers', never revealed their face to anyone unless it was someone they trusted with their very soul. Spirits didn't exactly understand things like romantic love or that sort of attraction, but they could still love very deeply in ways that Ashton probably couldn't fully understand if he tried.

And so, Ashton decided to repay that trust and love from his friend, and looked straight at him.

"... For someone who hides away his face since the day they were born, you're annoyingly handsome."

"Hahaha, says you? I haven't met a single fairy that won't swoon over you when you really try," the spirit laughed loudly. "But that's not important right now. This dreamscape is collapsing, and it will be a while until I can see you again. Some of the others might come and visit you sometime, though. So, before they swoop in, I want to be the first."

Ashton raised a brow. "The first? What do you mean?"

"I'm giving you my true name."

Stunned, Ashton looked at the spirit, and shook his head furiously. "No, no you're not! Why would you do something like that?"

The spirit continued to laugh. "Calm down, will you? I'm an Elder Spirit, even if my true name is known by others, they can't just fully take advantage of me. I've put up enough safeguards to protect me like that. But there is one thing that this will allow you to do."

The spirit stretched out his wings and pulled Ashton back into a hug. "When you have the power to, call on me, okay? I had sworn off contracts, but if it's you... I will answer your call, whenever you need me to."

Ashton could feel something flowing into his mind, as the spirit said his true name out loud, maybe for the first time in his entire life. The true name of a spirit was more than just something to call them by; it was their deepest essence, and something that stood at the core of their very soul.

It revealed a lot about him. About the weight of the spirit's power, and Ashton's understanding of his importance and the Mask Maker clan's as a whole increased to a point where it felt like just a moment ago, he was talking to a complete stranger.

It wasn't a name that Ashton could speak. No, he simply didn't have the means to. But he knew how to feed this name into his own magic, and how to use it as a conduit to reach out specifically to him during a contract ritual.

Ashton slowly pulled back, and smiled. "I will never forget this. Thank you for trusting me with this."

"Of course."

The spirit pulled back, and slowly placed his mask back onto his face. The two looked at each other.

"It was great to see you again, Ashton Garling, my old friend."

The smile on Ashton's face grew brighter. "I promise it won't be the last time, Elder Mask Maker."

And as the black sky collapsed, and this world around Ashton faded away, it was like a weight was lifted from Ashton's shoulders that he didn't even know was there.

He could feel himself falling, and slowly faded back into a deep sleep.

At least... that's what Ashton thought. But instead, he woke up. He sat up on the mattress that he put down earlier.

Though... no, it wasn't the mattress. There was the knowledge of a sensation, but he couldn't actually feel it. And going back from the sight that he had gotten back in the dreamscape, it seemed like he was back in darkness for a few moments, but before he knew it, something began to shift around Ashton.

He could feel the veil flowing around him. No, he couldn't just 'feel' it, he could truly see it. Ashton experienced the veil with every single one of his senses.

And almost immediately, he understood what was happening. Ashton wasn't awake; instead of dreaming, he fell into Astral Projection.

He had no real idea why this was happening, though he had a good guess. This was probably another effect of the 'Veil Mastery' skill. That was the only explanation Ashton could see.

'Really, this is such a weird sensation... Not exactly unfamiliar, though,' Ashton thought to himself. He had tried to speak, but he couldn't.

He looked around slightly. What he was experiencing now was the imprint that the physical world left behind on the veil. It wasn't a perfect imprint of course, but for the most part, this close to the physical realm, things were mostly one to one.

However, the imprint was relatively basic here. Things were still shifting a lot. The imprint was stronger and more stable the more mana was in a place, so around mana wells it would probably be a lot more accurate.

For now, Ashton figured he should just explore. This part of the veil wasn't really all that dangerous, so there was no reason why he shouldn't. But he had to come back here to this place, where his soul was anchored to, otherwise he wouldn't be able to wake up again.

'As long as I stay on this side of the veil, I will be able to find my way back.'

Ashton walked out of the office. Things were still solid in this plane of existence, but interacting with them was inherently a different sensation. What he was using to traverse this place was basically his own soul. It was why he was feeling so refreshed, since there was no mental or physical fatigue to keep him down.

Essentially, his body was still resting and recovering, and what he was experiencing here was basically the same as a dream. He could feel thoughts passing through the back of his mind, making him process all the events of the past few days. Right now, it felt more like he was constantly daydreaming while going on a walk.

He soon reached the outside of the building. There weren't any people around, nor would he see any living being in this world. Well, plants did leave imprints, but only because they were stationary for so long. With how much people, monsters, or animals were moving around, the best they could do was shift the physical world's imprint ever so slightly.

Mostly, that just meant that in places where a lot of people were gathered, the veil shifted more rapidly.

But even if he couldn't see anything like animals or people here, that didn't mean that this area was empty.

They were young and still unstable, with their forms shifting almost constantly, but Ashton was able to see elementals. They were the main inhabitant of this part of the veil that was closest to the physical world.

The ones that Ashton had contracted were technically the same as these fellows, just that through the contract, they were able to instantly stabilise themselves and develop their own sense of identity. But these little ones weren't the same.

Tiny wisps were floating around like petals in the wind, and many small sylphs were flying through the sky. Some looked like birds or butterflies, but there was even one that looked like a paper airplane. Not that they were sticking to that for very long. Before Ashton knew it, they had all taken on seemingly random forms that kept shifting in front of his eyes. Below him in the ground there were numerous gnomes, similarly still not possessing their own form.

But there were also ones that Ashton hadn't contracted yet, though knew pretty well.

There were a few small dryads, plant elementals, born from the vines that Ashton planted to keep Calibur's walls safe and upright. Lumens, light elementals were also floating around Ashton, as if they were dancing with the many wisps, and in the places where shadows would be gathered right around now, tenebruns, darkness elementals, were slowly bubbling up from the ground.

Of course, right now, there were numerous of them. Far, far more than would actually make it to the point of a 'lesser' elemental. Right now, they were still a step before that.

The vast, overwhelming majority of them wouldn't make it, and their consciousness would fade back into the veil. And then, amongst the handful that did make it through to becoming 'lesser' elementals still had to gather power and more magic over time, and only then could they potentially appear within the physical world.

While walking around, since he got the veil mastery skill, Ashton had felt magic that felt like elementals here and there, but this was the first time that he really saw something like this. It was a quite pretty sight.

Maybe Ashton should try and see if there were any elementals that were more advanced than these guys already.
