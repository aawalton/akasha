With my newfound understanding of mana, I realize that perhaps it isn’t best to start with apprentice water magic. It was the first magic aspect I learned, but I have deduced that light comes far more naturally to me. Since my next step is to take what I have learned and apply it, light magic seems the obvious choice.

Not that something like Ice Spike wouldn’t be useful, but Lesser Heal seems far more important. I can smash things all I want with my hammer, but healing is something I cannot do easily–without potions anyways.

I open up my Storage which I can apparently still do inside my spirit. It takes me a second to sort through the lists, as I have been gathering up quite a bit of junk as of late. I can’t wait to get to a city so I can sell it all off. Alas, I still have a bit of growing to do until then, namely I want to learn at least one apprentice skill and hit level sixty if I can. That might be a bit ambitious, but it shouldn’t be too hard.

After those thoughts, I finally find the scroll and pull it out. The paper feels weird in my hands, and I instantly recall that moment that Carla handed it to me. I wonder how she is doing, probably fine, she is a genius after all. As I hold the scroll, I can feel the power within it, the understanding of Lesser Heal is contained within.

I have tried to use it before, but I have always been missing something. Some component that allows me to actually complete the spell. But, if my theory is correct, I have just discovered that missing part. The way that the mana shifts must be important. If I do a bit more theorizing, perhaps lower tier spells require less of that light quality mana. Whereas higher tiers require more of that specific type of mana. For those naturally in this world, hitting the threshold for the apprentice tier is a lot easier than for foreigners like me.

But I suspect that higher tiers are more complicated. Both in the quality of mana required and other bits of theory I am likely missing. Those are all later questions to ask though, for now I just need to take it all one tier at a time.

“<Lesser Heal>”, As I incant the words and I can feel the mana flow through my body. It gathers in the air in front of me, but it quickly fizzles out. I expected that, though. I need to get a feeling for the spell, I think. So I cast it again, and again, and again.

With each cast, I pay closer and closer attention to how the spell feels. The words are still meaningless to me, but I start to figure out what they are doing. I latch onto that feeling of the mana shifting. A few attempts later, I even manage to influence it ever so slightly. It is very strange, as I am trying to manipulate something that lacks physical presence in a way that is also odd. Dozens of more attempts pass, and I feel myself grow ever closer to grasping what I need. Yet for some reason, it keeps eluding me.

Why?

I at least somewhat understand the theory, and I can sense and even affect that part of the spell. So I should be able to do it, so why can I not seem to do it? It is like there is some component I am still missing. I think back on what Valdir said, most of it didn’t really make much sense. But he did emphasize that I must somehow feel the mana. That the system somehow prevented that in some way.

Which is definitely something I need to fix. Yet, I don’t think that's quite it. Many people are surely casting apprentice and even up to master tier spells without going that deep. I get the sense that Valdir isn’t exactly the most familiar with the earlier tiers of magic anymore, and his weird knowledge translation spell can likely only go so far.

The shifting of mana thing is definitely the secret to apprentice tier spells. I don’t know how I am sure, but I just am. System bullshittery most likely. But what if it is not that part I am lacking, but something more basic? If only I had a magic teacher that was native to this world lying around and wasn’t some immortal entity. They could surely point out exactly which part I am missing.

Instead, I make do with what I am good at. Which is taking things that don’t want to be changed, and smacking them with a hammer really hard until they relent. Though in this case it is not metals, but mana that I must smack. I also don’t need a physical hammer, but a metaphorical one, or mental one perhaps.

I try to cast Lesser Heal once more, but this time I don’t try to shift the mana. Instead, I just hit the spell with a truly ridiculous amount of it. Of course, the spell does not work, but it does it in a way that I have never seen before. Rather than that slow fizzling out like before, the mana gathered in front of me explodes with a force of light strong enough to throw me on my ass. Luckily, I’m in my spirit, so it doesn’t hurt.

Now the question is why the spell exploded. Lesser Heal has nothing to do with explosions, yet I somehow created an effect despite it not being remotely close to the correct one. It makes me start to think about mana itself again. Perhaps it isn’t just that I need a certain quality of light mana, but I need to get rid of all of the other stuff?

For instance, in my Orn-mana, there is all sorts of stuff. Water mana, for one. But probably a whole ton of other stuff. Perhaps I don’t just need to increase the amount of light mana, but get rid of some of the other stuff too? I feel that weird tingle again, and it would seem I am on to something once again.

To test it, I incant Light Ray this time. However, as I do, I focus on the mana as it flows through my body. Rather than simply let it flow right into the spell, I stop it from doing so. Even after I complete the incantation, the spell just sort of waits there for some mana to fuel it.

Ever so slowly, I let a trickle of mana through. I am very careful though, and I sort of smash and manhandle the mana a bit before I let it go. What bits I do let go, I ensure are mostly light mana. Of course, I can’t get all of the other junk, but I don’t need to.

The moment the more purified mana flows into the spell, the world in front of me explodes into light. It was a tiny amount of mana that I actually spent, barely a quarter of what I would normally give the spell. Yet it had almost double the effect. As if to prove my efforts aren’t for nothing, I hear a ding in my mind.

‘Ding’, New Skill Learned, [Fundamental Mana Manipulation (Inferior)].

‘Ding’, New Quest Chain: A Mage’s Path.

‘Ding’, New Quest: Initiate Tier Magic

I guffaw as I see those notifications. A quest chain, and a quest for what I presume is those just having unlocked magic. It would seem that I did skip a step or two, which is quite hilarious. I bet the goddess is cackling too right about now. It may be a bit late, but I open up the descriptions anyways.

[Fundamental Mana Manipulation (Inferior)]

You have gained an insight into the manipulation of mana! Not only have you cast your first fundamental spells, but you have surpassed the tier entirely. With your newfound ability to purify mana, you are now able to learn initiate tier spells far more easily.

New Quest Chain: A Mage’s Path

Estimated Difficulty: None-Quit Now

Estimated Length: Your entire life

Background: You have successfully unravelled the first secrets to magic. Thus you have set upon the path to becoming a mage. While some may view mages as scholars that sit in their studies for decades upon end, this is often far from the truth. For that would be really dull. Most mages tend to just blow stuff up every once in a while, attributing it to ‘research.’ Good luck on your path, young mage.

Associated Quests: Initiate Tier Magic

New Quest: Initiate Tier Magic

Type: Progression

Background: The initiate tier is one full of many useful spells. From stabilizing the wounded, to even some basic offensive spells. However, in the field of magic there is always greater power to seek. So go forth, young mage, and learn to your heart’s satisfaction.

Objectives: Learn 3 initiate tier spells (6/3), Uncover the barrier to apprentice tier magic (complete), successfully cast an apprentice tier spell (0/1)

Rewards: Major boon, lesser boon, lesser boon.

Instead of laughing, I just sigh. It would be nice if the system could call me less of an ignorant fool. Like, I get it, I should’ve learned all this a long time ago. But couldn’t it be a bit nicer… it calls me a young mage like I am some sort of child still. While it is nice to be called young, I do not appreciate the condescending tone it seems to have when writing it.

Putting all of that to the side, it would seem I have made a step in my magical journey. Apparently I was missing this purifying mana step, and that is why I couldn’t cast apprentice tier spells. The barrier as the system calls it to apprentice tier magic is the shifting of mana quality, which I somehow learned before the barrier to initiate tier magic. Which is odd, shouldn’t I have been unable to learn to initiate tier spells?

I sigh again. Why is it that every answer just gives me even more questions to ask. Not that I hate it of course, but it would be nice to just have a break every once in a while. Something tells me that won’t happen until I learn everything there is to know though, at which point I would be the strongest being in this world.

Not only does that sound boring, but I expect that would take far more time than I have. I do have heroic things to do, after all. So it would be best to wrap this magic stuff soon.
