The Zenith.

It wasn’t claiming that this point was the zenith of the water affinity, but it was a representation of it. Of the path that leads to the zenith. The goal and progression of finding the top.

Patterns of waves flowed along her visible arms and legs while her hair floated up as if it was underwater.

Something that she had learned from Sky. The best strategy is to find basic leather and have the Refiner attune it using essence shards.
