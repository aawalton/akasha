There is no point in blathering about all the ways people claim to have breached into the Overrealm without the Great Gods inviting them in. None of them are real. There is no truth to any of these so-called methods. No one reached the Overrealm through a sacrifice. No one broke into the Plane of the Great Gods by hitching a ride on a being ascending into the Overrealm.

What? There is a ritual? And someone has already gone in there once? Enough of your lies. If this Realmbreaker has actually achieved such a thing, then let him proclaim it himself.

Devout Recordings

Zatrus K.

Ryland wasn’t really expecting to deal with yet another Incarnation on top of everything else going on here. He was being hounded by these duplicitous Juren mages who were desperate to claim Rhel for themselves and their masters. Then there were these pestering Avatars, who were so strong that he was the only one who could reliably handle them all.

Until the Incarnation of Growth had appeared, that was. Speaking of whom, they needed to figure out a name for the fellow. Not that battlefields were great opportunities for introductions.

“Please handle matters here,” Ryland said to the Incarnation of Growth. “I’m assuming we’re on the same side at the moment, so if we are, I’d appreciate your assistance.”

The Incarnation of Growth nodded. “I like to think that we are indeed on the same side. For the time being, at least.”

That was good enough for Ryland. The Incarnation was growing more verbose the longer he remained present. Whatever miracle Xyke had performed had allowed him to regain most of his intelligence rather quickly.

Ryland nodded again, then rifted away. If there was yet another Incarnation active, then he needed to be there to ensure things didn’t devolve into outright chaos.

Good thing the pillar of blinding light made it pretty clear where he needed to go.

It also clarified who and what exactly Ryland was about to face. For he had seen that descending beam before, and the familiarity of its circumstance beckoned him with greater urgency.

The rift brought him to the location of the Oracle. Ryland was relieved to see that she was alright, so to speak.

Unfortunately, that was where his relief ended.

The Incarnation of Faith had arrived yet again, and it was possessing the Oracle’s form. There were signs of recently healed wounds, but with how much her form was overflowing with divine energy, he didn’t need to worry about her safety for the time being.

Ryland couldn’t say the same about the rest of the gathering. No doubt, the Juren mages he had left behind inside the [Void Seal] he had cast were the catalysts of the trouble he was witnessing. But they were now facing due comeuppance. A wave of blistering, liquid light was assaulting them like the relentless tide of a stormy ocean.

They were barely fending off the tide of drowning, burning power with their combined barriers, but it wasn’t going to last long. Not that Ryland was concerned about them.

His attention instead fell on how the Incarnation’s volatile magic was spreading. It would have been fine if it was just the Juren mages being attacked. Instead, the Incarnation’s fulgurous, divine power was swamping everything, having already overtaken a fallen Ophelia while quickly encroaching on Ryland’s Inscription.

“[Translocate],” he incanted again, rifting through the intervening space to reach Ophelia before rifting away with her just as quickly. “[Immortal Rejuvenation].”

Ophelia’s wounds started healing as he gently placed her back on the ground. Her injuries were severe. Ryland held back a grimace at how her chest had been carved open gorily and her shoulder was bent entirely the wrong way. He was lucky to find her still alive.

“[Eternal Bastion],” he incanted next. The spell took effect around Ryland and Ophelia just in time.

For the Incarnation of Faith had attacked.

They simply arrived at his exact location before smiting him. Hard. Despite his Incarnation form blazing to life, Ryland was still forced back a dozen or so armspans. He frowned. The Incarnation’s strength wasn’t surprising at all. What hit him hard, however, was their behaviour.

The last time he had faced off against the Incarnation of Faith, they had still backed off when they had seen Ryland healing the Oracle. There was still a modicum of intelligence that allowed the Incarnation to recognize that Ryland wasn’t an enemy.

Not so, now. Whatever the Juren mages had done, whatever interferences had occurred, left the Incarnation in a state where they saw everyone as a foe.

The first attack came in the form of a dragon. Ryland remembered it well from the time the Oracle had summoned it to assault him. A great dragon constructed purely of divine energy erupted into being over the Incarnation, rippling power and sending mighty bolts of divine lightning spearing into the atmosphere.

Uldovyx the Sun Dragon, the scion of Great Arcoryx. Its roar echoed across the surface of Rhel, making everything shiver. The very ground was cracking.

And as if that wasn’t bad enough, the powers of other Great Gods were quickly emerging from the Incarnation. The next display was one Ryland had grown unfortunately very familiar with from recent experiences.

With a bright spark, the Incarnation split off into multiple versions of itself. They rushed across the ground with halos burning around them as though they were meteors shooting through the atmosphere. Worse, every copy of the Incarnation, born from the power of Ovrega, was calling on more Great Gods’ powers simultaneously.

“[Absolute Timefort],” Ryland incanted. “[Concentric Time].”

There was no point in taking the Incarnation lightly. He didn’t particularly wish to fight back until it was absolutely necessary, and if his opponent saw that violence wasn’t making any headway, they would be more predisposed towards a calm discussion.

As it was, the Great Gods’ various attacks landed upon Ryland with apocalyptic fury. The dragon’s breath came out like a beam not dissimilar from [Dragongod’s Endbreath]. One of the Incarnation’s divine avatars was swinging in with a spear that Ryland knew for certain would disable a great number of Inscriptions he kept on himself. Another one was creating gigantic swords to slash at him.

He took them all and suffered through their impacts. The beam from the dragon’s breath pushed him back since he had taken to the air to prevent his Inscription getting damaged. His barriers weathered it, but not without suffering a mark.

Then came the other attacks. [Concentric Time] was excellent at taking multiple hits, but [Absolute Timefort] wasn’t doing well in that regard. Beams just bypassed temporal blocks by being continuous streams. The only reason [Concentric Time] worked well against it was because Ryland could endlessly create more and more layers of time to keep himself safe.

Of course, that meant his focus was too much on keeping himself safe against one attack. It left him vulnerable to other methods of assault.

The gigantic swords flew in with deadly edges. Then came the Incarnation with the magic-deleting spear. Weathering the first few blows before taking a hit had convinced Ryland that he needed to counteract them actively. Ignoring them would be foolish.

Thankfully, a simple but powerful Glyph of Repulsion was momentarily all he needed. It pushed away not just the Incarnation themselves but also everything they threw at Ryland.

“[Dimensional Lock],” Ryland incanted next.

He was hoping to trap the Incarnation and then deal with its copies. One interesting point was that the Incarnation hadn’t created copies of the Oracle that was hosting the main form. It meant Ryland could wipe them out without worrying about the fallout.

They attacked him while he kept the main one locked down, but he rifted away to dodge them without engaging any of them directly.

“[Singularity Trap],” Ryland incanted. His next rift took him to a spot below them so his following spell could blast them with no fears about impacting the Inscription. “[Dragongod’s Endbreath].”

A massive rift threw an enormous chunk of Rhel into shadow, before spewing out the maw of a colossal dragon. Its jaws yawned wide. That motion alone was enough to unleash a powerful gust. Then came the searing beam. If the one by Uldovyx was brighter than a solar flare, then what Ryland unleashed put even that to shame.

Ryland didn’t even look at the fake Incarnations. He turned back to the real one as the sky turned maroon and white, so bright that it felt as though Ryland had summoned the sun again.

The real one had remained busy, however. It was now drawing on the strength of other Great Gods. One allowed it to bloom life itself wherever it willed, a strange aura spreading farther and farther from its location. Another divinity’s power was allowing it to tamper with [Dimensional Lock]. This thing’s arsenal was seriously impressive.

It would break free soon. Then it could start causing untold problems. Already, the explosion of unchecked life was hampering his efforts.

Trees were bursting across open ground, roots were eating through the earth, and grass blanketed everything and wiped them all off the surface. And it wasn’t just living matter. The very power of life itself, through the blessing of Akverz, the Great God of Life, writhed into the land, soon overtaking any other magic in the area.

Such as the Inscription Ryland had placed down.

Loath as he was to truly harm a fellow Incarnation who wasn’t outright evil or malicious, despite their aggressive actions thus far, Ryland knew that holding back too much would just lead to disaster for his own goals. He needed to be decisive. He had to stop the Incarnation.

“[Eruption],” Ryland incanted.

A large rift opened up in the face of the encroaching vegetation. Their wild growth was immediately stopped when the peak of a volcano emerged from the huge rift. The volcano proceeded to unleash a violent eruption.

That, quite obviously, put a stop to the wild growth trying to overtake Ryland’s Inscription. A storm of ash, lava, and hellish heat exploded outwards, quickly swamping everything the seemingly trapped Incarnation had created. It took mere seconds before all of the overgrown life was wiped out.

“Ryland.”

Xyke had appeared, Viren not far behind her. Her manasoul was burning to life too, though she hadn’t fully assumed her Incarnation form yet.

“Great timing,” Ryland said. “I don’t think our adversary will be stymied for long. I will need your help again.”

“I know,” she said. “That’s why I came here.”

“As fast as we could,” Viren said, an addition that Xyke clearly didn’t appreciate going by her minor scowl.

They didn’t have time to chit chat and banter. Through the smoke and debris, through the horrific stench of burning sulphur, Ryland sensed his fellow Incarnation calling upon other Great Gods in order to use more divine energy.

The possessed Oracle broke free, shooting out of the volcanic storm and borne aloft by godly wings. A blessing from the Great God of Ascension.

“Didn’t you trap it with [Dimensional Lock]?” Xyke asked as she began soaring after their target.

“I thought I did,” Ryland said, though he wasn’t sure she heard his reply thanks to the rush of air flying past them. “[Wings of Salvation].”

He rose higher. Faster. Soon enough, he was matching the Incarnation’s pace, and they clashed across the sky as both sought to subdue the other with the pure force of their magical auras of condensed Conceptual Glyphs. The sky turned into a scattered spray of bursts, each of their collisions unleashing what felt like a small supernova across the atmosphere of Rhel.

At one crash, one of Ryland’s wings finally broke. The Incarnation’s form surged in triumph, quickly reconstructing the damage that Ryland had dealt with the earlier clashes.

The Oracle embraced within the Incarnation’s glowing form was fine, which reassured Ryland enough to continue.

“[Translocate],” he cast.

The Incarnation had turned around, blazing in triumph and ready to finish Ryland. But that was their fatal mistake. Ryland’s rift opened right next to them.

Out came Xyke, shooting past the Incarnation and leaving a single touch.

Her fist had grabbed a handful of the Conceptual Glyphs making up the Incarnation of Faith, and she disappeared into another rift. Then she reappeared right above their adversary, quickly stealing another bunch of Glyphs before disappearing into another rift, leaving a trail of chaining Glyphs in her wake.

The Incarnation was too flabbergasted to react properly. Ryland could tell they were once again seeking to summon the power of the Great Gods. But Ryland and Xyke’s plan was so thorough, so well thought-out from long ago, they didn’t even need to communicate.

Soon enough, Xyke’s endeavour finished. She had successfully trapped the very Conceptual nature of the Incarnation. Ryland could have tried imitating the Incarnation and gone toe to toe, but that wouldn’t be necessary here. He would much rather settle this with a minimum amount of fuss.

“Have you any idea how much you owe me?” Xyke asked.

Ryland had absolutely no clue about something that vague, but he could figure out a ballpark. “I’ll give you Rhel when I’m done.”

Xyke’s eyes flashed. “Deal.”

They both turned to survey the Incarnation, who was trying to pull themselves free from the chains of Conceptual Glyphs Xyke had erected around them. That magic wasn’t going to last long. Even now, a few of the Incarnation’s own Glyphs were exploding.

Ryland had noted that the Incarnation had momentarily stopped trying to call on the powers of a Great God. Now was his chance.

“[Endless Evolution],” he incanted. And then— “[Everlife Spectre].”

The only way to handle the Incarnation was by handling the very soul itself. That’s why Ryland wasted no time and charged straight at the trapped being, seeking to reduce its influence and pull the Oracle to safety.

But as soon as he was within reach, he was forced to stop.

The Oracle’s eyes had flown open, and her arm reached up to grab Ryland’s before his skeletal one could make contact with the Incarnation. Her eyes, hidden though they might have been behind a blindfold, confessed her intense awareness of the moment.

“Stop, Ryland,” she said.

“What is the meaning of this, Oracle Vertha?” Ryland asked back. “Don’t tell me you summoned the Incarnation and allowed it to go on a rampage of your own accord.”

“This is needed!”

“Needed for what?”

“To end the influence of the Great Gods. What else?”
