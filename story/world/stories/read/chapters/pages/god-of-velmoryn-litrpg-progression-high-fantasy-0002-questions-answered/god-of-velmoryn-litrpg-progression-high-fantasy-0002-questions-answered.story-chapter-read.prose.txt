3) shaunwinsor - I have a completely unrelated question. What got you into writing?

----

Always loved writing. Even as a kid (7–8), I loved to write short stories. They were mostly about Kiki and Zizi (I don’t know why those names...) having small adventures together, usually ending with a lesson - likely influenced by the stories my parents used to read to me when I was much younger xD

Then I kind of stopped... until a few months ago, when I got extremely frustrated with how The Divine Hunter ended. That frustration sparked something, and I ended up writing a short outline of how I would’ve liked the story to progress and end. It was just an outline, nothing more, but the process was so satisfying that I started writing a fanfic - Naruto: Azure Awakening. I’m not proud of how the early chapters turned out, but I believe they helped me learn how to better show the images I see when shaping a story for my readers.

So, to answer your question, there are two reasons I write: first, I love sharing my fantasies, what I imagine, what I feel... and second, I hope to write something lasting, something that might inspire others the way LOTR, LOTM, and A Soldier’s Life influenced God of Velmoryn.

The answer turned out a bit longer than I intended, but it is what it is xD

7) MilkTheShark - Is there a cap to the amount of stats you can get from essences? I think it was stated earlier that they gave you stats. If there is a cap, what is it, or is it capped based on tier ie: lesser essence can give you 15%/+15 flat of base stat but greater essence can give you 20%/+20 flat etc.

---

There are five types of essences: Faint, Lesser, Greater, Refined, and Mythic.

Each higher tier gives 500% more effect than the one below it.

* A Faint essence gives 0.02

* Lesser gives 0.1

* Greater gives 0.5

The system starts to change with Refined, and especially with Mythic. Those essences (and creatures which yield them) are on a completely different level compared to the lower tiers. The increase in stats is permanent (in terms of time) and can grow infinitely, there is no upper limit.
