I had tested it, and yes I could focus inward and shut them out and move towards the emptiness of pure meditation. But I didn’t need more introspection. I had done the inner work, and it was time to direct my attention towards the outside world— namely magic.

So I focused the full powers of my concentration towards the floating mana particles. And that made me a happy baby.

The best part?

Meditation and Mana Sight worked synergistically. I had found an exploit!

Focusing on the mana particles deepened my focus. And focusing on them made them more prominent. Easier to distinguish from another. It was easy to achieve flow with this method. So these two Skills formed a positive feedback loop. I wondered if I could apply Meditation to any Skill. For now it did require stillness. If I fidgeted or got too hungry, the concentration was interrupted. But that could change, because who knew what mysteries the System held.

Overall I was extremely happy. My focus was getting deeper, my senses were getting sharper and I was starting to see more mana particles.

I had also made a second observation.

They seemed to be mostly making little circles, as if in an orbit. But sometimes they would make erratic dashes before returning to another orbit. That was interesting. Maybe there were some heavier particles they were circling that I couldn’t see. I needed more levels to figure it out!

The problem was, I still couldn’t control the mana particles even after multiple attempts. My working theory was that this was a result of multiple things. First of all, I was a baby. Babies are loopy, and I needed to take a damn nap constantly. Higher Focus Attribute would help. Additionally, I believed my Mana Flow Attribute was too low. The description mentioned connection and communication with mana, after all.

I decided to devote myself to improving my existing Skills, and maybe I would reach a breakthrough.

After some more time passed, I finally got to see outside. My mother wrapped me in a cloth against her chest and I was blasted with bright sunshine and the salty scent of sea. To my misfortune, I couldn’t see much, being pressed against my mother. Naturally I cried about it. She fed me, which was great of course. But not what I wanted. So when she was about to wrap me against her again, I cried some more. After some instructive bawling, mother turned me around and I made happy baby noises instead. It was important to train your parents with positive reinforcement.

“Demanding aren’t you,” mother said softly and brushed my cheek. “Tell me if it's all too much, little gem.”

It was a lot.

The village wasn’t large. There were maybe fifty huts scattered between the shore and the jungle treeline. But it was alive.

Men and women were trading fish, herbs, clothes and pottery. There was laughing, drinking, eating, bartering and gossiping. Massive fish hung off bamboo racks and young men wrestled and threw spears in a sand pit.

Everyone was wearing clothes of rough fiber, but they were colored with beautiful vibrant hues of red and blue. The men were muscular and tall and wore blue paint on their skin. The women had silky black hair with flowers braided in, their builds lithe and agile.

So this was my home, I mused. And these were my people. They were all clearly happy. I couldn’t blame them. The wide blue sky above was beautiful and the sun was shining, but it wasn’t hot because of the ocean breeze.

The village was primitive though. No meaningful technology beyond mud bricks and drying racks. That wasn’t… ideal. First off, even though my memories were fading, I couldn’t forget hot showers and toilets. I really hoped I’d forget them soon, or I’d be suffering. But the second and more pertinent worry was the lack of evident use of magic. Not a magic school or wizard’s tower in sight. I was starting to feel that my journey towards magic might take the long route.

But I would not be disheartened. At least I was born in a peaceful and happy place.

My mother was swarmed by other women of her age who formed an excited circle around us. The women doted on me and cooed over me. They all exclaimed how beautiful and cute I was, which made it clear that these women had sharp eyes and good taste. I was traded around like a peace pipe and was given a kiss and a name for an introduction. I smiled and babbled at them which the women found adorable.

We continued our tour, which was basically my mother showing me off. The village was small, and there were around a hundred people out and about. So I estimated the total population must have been something around three hundred. There were a lot of children running around. Curiously enough, not so much playing. Well, it was play, but it was the same thing the older boys were doing. Wrestling and spearthrowing. Some young girls were doing crafts with older women, like weaving baskets. I had never seen such amazing baskets. Sturdy and beautiful. And the sheer speed at which the girls wove them made me sure that Skills were being used.

Now that I looked around closer, I noticed something. Yes, everything was pretty primitive. But damn, if it wasn’t well made. The houses, the ropes, the drying racks, the clay pots and firepits. Everything had been done with extreme care and craftsmanship. Especially the spears that the boys wielded.

This had to be the result of Skills. It made sense. With Skills making people this good at their work, I wondered how much pressure there was to develop machinery in the first place.

This also made me fairly sure that I was on an island. All of the material here seemed native to the jungle. If there were trade routes, they were not used frequently. There was barely any glass or metal in the village.

Mother also took me to the beach. The shore had a lot of people. Clearly there was another village nearby. A lot of people were swimming, and boy were they swimming fast.

The older boys took to water like actual fish. Olympic swimmers from Earth couldn’t hold a candle to even the slowest boys of fifteen years.

Further out on the ocean was a large raft. Normally a baby this young had absolutely no business seeing that far, but I had a pretty decent Perception Attribute. There were adult men with spears on that raft. They were all tall, muscular and covered in blue paint. They were looking out to the ocean. There were man-sized blue fish with large backfins and a sword-nose jumping into the air, trying to catch some kind of bird.

Were they… spearfishing?

“Look,” mother said. “There’s papa.”

I wasn’t sure what I was looking at, until one of them threw a spear. It flew through the air with devastating speed and struck one of the jumping fish. Immediately the man pulled on the rope attached to the spear and the fish slammed into the water. Then the man dove into the ocean. Within only a few breaths the man and the fish both burst out of the waves, as the fish made a desperate jump into the air. The man held one hand on the spear, and another on the pointy nose. The fish jumped into the air multiple times, trying to buck the man off like a bull. Each jump was a little weaker than the last. Until finally there was no jump. After a silence, a bloody spear emerged from the waves and my father let out a victory cry. All the men on the raft cheered for him, as did the people on the shore.

“Papa is very good at fishing,” mother said.

It clicked with me. The wrestling, the spearplay, the swimming.

Holy crap. This was fishing? I had thought my father just picked up a rod or a net from outside our hut every morning and sat in a boat.

Instead, my father was a super athlete!

These men were fishing enormous fish by wrestling them down in the water. That was awesome.

I wondered what levels and attributes you needed to swim that fast and to be able to wrestle down a fish in the water. Primitive or not, these people were superhuman. I just couldn’t wait to level up more. This excursion had been fun, but I was already burning to get back to meditation.

Days flew by fast. We did some more trips outside and when I wasn’t so overwhelmed by all the new experiences, I managed to apply my Mana Sight, and noticed that there were mana particles everywhere. Most people had some on them. The large fish that the fishers hunted was clearly also potent in mana. I was still only sensing a fraction of the true amount of mana in this world, but with each level, I saw a gradual uptick in my perception of the moving particles

I hadn’t had success with controlling the mana particles yet though. Since I had the time, I was mostly focusing on increasing my existing two Skills. The advancements were getting sparser. Once I thought I couldn’t get substantial Attribute or Skill increases that would help me create a good foundation, I would move on to attempt to control mana.

As rewarding as it was to try to figure this whole thing out, it was also difficult. I wish I could just ask the adults.

I had many questions, so I was also learning to speak. I could understand perfectly, but my mouth was literally not able to create the sounds. So I babbled. My father especially loved to make nonsense sounds with me while he made funny faces. At first I had felt a little weird about it, with having a mature mind and all, but as my memories of old life gradually faded, I found I started loving his weird faces more.

I was also starting to get some mobility. Nothing crazy. But I was able to turn on my stomach and even move myself an inch using the crib’s vertical rungs! Crazy, I know.

I was motivated and training hard, but I think that just made me more tired than even a regular baby. I was sleeping so much. It was gradually getting easier to stay awake, but my meditation was constantly interrupted. Not only that, but concentrating on Mana Sight was mentally draining. Despite all of these obstacles, I was making steady progress.

I sent my intent through that channel to the nearest particles. I wanted them to move. To do that jump from orbit to orbit that they sometimes did.

There was nothing there to persuade.

So what was I to do?

This was a puzzle I had to solve. I had the strongest intuition that this was the pivotal next step to learning magic.

To my absolute delight the particle stirred. It froze for a moment, before it shifted to orbit a spot close to my finger.
