The squawks of a Cloud Li Crane banged in front of Shuren’s room after he cleaned himself up. Finally spraying the small bottle of perfume on his neck, he moved to open his door. The Cloud Li Crane bellowed as it saw Shuren, spitting out the letter in its mouth, aiming directly at Shuren’s face. “This bird is so worked up… really trying to irritate me this early,” he said as he caught the letter. It refused to budge, bellowing in his face even further.

He rummaged his pockets to take out a single spirit jade stone, flicking it back at the Cloud Li Crane. With a harrumph, it took flight, making sure to smack its wings on Shuren’s face. “You-” He stretched out his palm, almost forcing his Qi around its wings to make it drop down, before rubbing his forehead, “It’s just a bird. What am I doing? I don’t have time for this idiocy.”

Shuren opened the letter with both his hands, with broad shoulders outlining his robes. The effects of the consistent routine of battle missives had long broadened his frame. It was about time to upgrade his wardrobe at this rate.

My son, a cycle of the year is upon us since you’ve joined the Seven Clouds. We’ve prepared a gift for your recent success, and planned a banquet in your honour. Of course, I made sure that your mother will also be there. It will be held in a month, so ensure that you free up your time and get clearance from the sect.

From your father.

Shuren’s mood slightly improved as an awkward smile crept up on his face. It’s been almost a few months since he last saw his family, especially his younger siblings. “Little Yuxi, Little Jing, I’m expecting the both of you to have made good progress by now. The Qidense Realm should soon be in reach for you two.”

“Almost a year has passed…”

When Shuren reached out to the Ruyi Staff hanging against the wall, he drew it closer to observe the engravings of his father’s runic symbols. Dried blood was beginning to be stuck deep within it. At the very least, there was no apparent stench, but Shuren couldn’t help clicking his tongue when he saw it.

There was a specific type of cloth, made from the fur of Purion Sheeps, Shuren would use when it came to cleaning his weapons. Especially the Ruyi Staff gifted to him by his father, he wouldn’t dare hold back on carefully maintaining it. And though expensive, Purion Sheep had fur which would self maintain itself, with just a few drops of water being enough to excrete any dirt still remaining in it. They were a favourite when it came to cultivators wishing to maintain their tools, due to them being relatively inexpensive.

“I haven’t cleaned it well enough,” he said with a slight frown. Putting the duty of cleaning it further at the back of his mind, Shuren left his room, allowing the morning hues of dawn to soak in.

“I had to take some time to rest up, and finished my report to Elder Spiritweaver. I’ll head to the Hall of Missives to get my rewards. Then, I’ll wholeheartedly focus my efforts into breaking through to Stage 11.” With a clang of the metal band on his left hand, a flying cloud manifested at Shuren’s feet, and he stepped on to head towards the fourth peak, where the Hall of Missives were.

Swerving behind the backs of the mountains, the smell of ripening flowers in spring abundant. Out of habit, he grabbed a few leaves off the Lisoran trees scattered around. Their leaves when grinded down, exuded a refreshing minty taste, perfect for a tea after a hot day. Or a day filled with sweat and blood.

As usual, the Hall of Missives was more quiet during the early hours. No one lingered around the outside, except for a lone swordsman.

Cutting off the emotions of irritations like dousing out an incense flame, Shuren approached the swordsman with a casual stride, his right hand raised out. “You look like you’re in good shape.” Their sheathed weapons on their waistbands clinked together, their hands resoundingly clasped in a hand shake. Compared to when they first joined, their hands were riddled with rough calluses and scars.

“As they say, fortune favours the finest fighters,” Ming chuckled before letting go of Shuren’s hand. His worn and colour ravaged robe inscribed with patterns of sword strikes of his family path.

But before they continued to head towards the Hall of Missives, Ming stood square in front of Shuren. “So? When are you gonna drop that facade?”

Shuren had a wry smile, before slouching his shoulders. “I suppose we’re past the point where I need to put up a mask around you. If only I wasn-”

Shuren felt the cold tap of steel thud on his forehead. “Don’t get started. You did well. That’s all there is to it, nothing more, nothing less.” With the hit of his sword across Shuren’s face, Ming lowered it back into his side once more.

“Unless you had the power to kill a Core Genesis demonic cultivator, in which case I’d be beating the shit out of you right now,” Ming added on with sarcasm.

“Nothing gets by you. You’re right. Did you hear much from her?” Shuren asked about Aerestia.

Ming shook his head. “Leave her be for the time being. I doubt she’d harbour any malice towards you anyway, so don’t waste any thoughts worrying about that.”

Shuren clenched his jaw when he thought back to the incident, but sighed in the end. “That’s my mistake for inviting her. I should’ve known better.”

Ming stared at Shuren with raised eye brows for a few breaths, but decided not to address it. Instead, Shuren felt a large thud on his back as Ming slapped it.

“Come on Shuren, when are you gonna catch up to Stage 12?” Ming goaded as he changed the topic.

“I don’t eat, defecate and fight all day long like you do Ming. Why’re you even an Inner Disciple in the sect if you barely worked on your vocation?” Shuren ribbed, elbowing Ming’s sides.

“Blame the breathing style you taught me. It’s practically begging me to be a madman, killing while absorbing Qi even as I fight. Every time I take down a Rank 1 Beasts while cycling my Qi, my body keeps absorbing and refining whatever my sword gets contact into,” Ming said as he itched his ear with a finger. “Besides, Elder Swordfall’s teaching us on charm making. I need to make my own resources if I want to travel the world.”

Shuren had a slight frown for a moment, before continuing, “Charm making. That’s the last thing I’d expect you to do. The Wu Clan is known for reinforcing the formations around Cloud City.”

Zeal Artifacts were the utmost peak of craftsmanship, only capable of Rank 2 Cultivators to wield. But Sacrosanct Artifacts were the pinnacle of defensive artifacts, capable of saving someone from a fatal attack. These were naturally ludicrously expensive, only affordable to the wealthy and powerful.

The reward Shuren had gained long ago had been used up in one of his battle missives with Ming, when a beast almost sliced his jugular vein.

It acted as a shield at the last moment, and had long been used up.

Charm making was one of the many prerequisite vocations that lead to the road of crafting the pinnacle of defensive artifacts. It was to imbue cloth; whether from skin of beasts or Rank 1 and above materials, with your own Qi.

Ming scoffed as he shrugged, “So? I do what I want. Besides, you can consider charm making a minor derivative from the Formation Path.”

Shuren fidgeted with his Ruyi Staff, tying it back on the back of his robe. “So I can say brewing tea is also from the Alchemical Path.”

“How cultured, do you also serve tea when you’re fighting your opponent.”

“Actually, I’ve seen that plenty of times back home. In fact, customs amongst alliances and cultivators that visit acquaintances dicta-”

“Let’s just go grab our rewards. It’s almost time for me to get those ridiculously expensive spatial rings.” Ming faced his back away from Shuren, leading the way to the Hall of Missives entrance.

Remembering the letter Shuren received from his father, he brought up the topic to Ming. “My father will be holding a banquet at our manor a month from now. Come by, I’ll show you how the Liu Clan feasts,” he said with a prideful smile.

“Hah, of course I’ll come. Send an invitation to the Wu Clan of Cloud City, I’ll bring my family along. Dad will get off my back too about making connections while you’re at it.” Ming waved a palm while he walked as he accepted Shuren’s proposal.

Well, it’s always good for father to make new friends. Though I wouldn’t be surprised if he already made contact with them to make the defensive formations around our manor.

Yesterday he had killed demons, today he would reap rewards, and over morrow he would celebrate with a feast.

Ming stared at Shuren’s back, thinking back to that day.

Though his sword was destroyed, he didn’t bother asking Shuren to pay him back for it. It wasn’t anything expensive.

What he was wondering more was about Shuren’s past and background.

“Shuren.”

“What is it?”

Ming thought for a moment with a serious expression.

“You want to reach Rank 2 as soon as possible. Yeah?”

Shuren scoffed, as if Ming was asking a stupid question. “Of course. What else do you think?”

“You’ll fall if you keep it up.”

Shuren stopped walking, but he didn’t turn around to face Ming.

“Your shoulders are slouching. You need me to take some weight off it?” Ming said.

Shuren chuckled, though with a hint of wistfulness. “That just means I need to train harder. A strong man can walk with his head raised up high.”

Ming could only shake his head in return, though with a smile. He walked ahead, slapping Shuren’s back. “Then reach Rank 2 and stop falling behind.”

He took the lead, walking ahead of Shuren.

And Shuren thought back to that incident.

The seed of vacillation slumbered.

It was soon when they saw that at the side of an entrance, a dull-eyed disciple with baggy eyes stood, leaning his back against the engraved walls of the Hall of Missives. With a pipe in his mouth, multi-coloured smoke rose from the end, swirling above his head.
