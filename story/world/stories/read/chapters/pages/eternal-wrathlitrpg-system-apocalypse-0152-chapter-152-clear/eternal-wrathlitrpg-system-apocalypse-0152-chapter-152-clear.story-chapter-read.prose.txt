“James, Kouki! Aim for the queen! We’ll do it at the same time! Use your ranged attacks!” Andrew yelled.

The fact that the monsters tried their best to protect the queen could only mean one thing: the queen was weak and vital to the colony. If they could defeat it, they would win. Andrew even wondered whether the queen could move at all. It still had wings, but its stomach bulged like a truck container and curled in front of the throne like a snake. He recalled how queen bees and ants behaved in real life. They almost never left their nests.

Kouki hesitated, then steeled himself and charged forward.

“Ralph! It’s now or never!” Kouki yelled. His dragon charged ahead, breathing fire onto the queen. James followed suit with his bow. The larvae wiggled, their flesh torn asunder as James’s arrows penetrated them deeply. Behind the larvae stood the cockroaches.

The treemen retaliated. They threw their spears. A rain of golden meteors swarmed toward them. Kouki readied his sword skill, but his eyes widened. There were too many. At that moment, Ralph charged in, using its body to shield its master. Two dozen spears stabbed into the white dragon. The dragon roared, breathing its last flames upon its enemies before falling into eternal slumber.

“Ralph!” Kouki screamed. His eyes darted toward the dragon before he charged in from the left flank. His sword blazed with fury as he cut down the tree monster that had hurt his dragon.

James also leaped in. Using his sword skill, Hurricane, he cut down two spearmen from the flank. He then doused the larvae protecting the queen with fire, but the fleshy wall stood firm. As more monsters were cut down, more spawned from the walls. It was an endless, relentless horde, all roused to protect their queen.

James kept fighting. He made one last desperate charge. He leaped, and with his jet thruster, rocketed toward the queen from above. His sword glowed golden as he sped like a bullet. The cockroaches flapped their wings, spitting acid. All aimed for James’s life, but they couldn’t catch him. The acid burned, but James pressed on. He believed that if he could land this one strike on the queen, he would win. And so, he persevered. This was not the first life-threatening act he had ever done.

James reached the throne, but his sword hit empty air. The queen bee had disappeared. Despite her bulging abdomen, she was still nimble. She used her legs to leap away.

The cockroaches, larvae, and treemen moved toward her, barring James from approaching further. James felt his body lose all energy at once. His body burned. He roared as dozens of spears were thrown at him. His shield blocked the ones aimed at his heart and head, but the spears bored holes into his stomach and legs. James dropped to the ground. He had failed. Despair washed over him. He thought this was it—his time to die.

No. He was still alive.

James gathered his resolve. He refused to give up. As if answering to his resolve, his new legs began to heal. He forced himself to stand, ignoring the hole in his stomach. As he prepared for a second round with the monsters, they came to an abrupt stop.

At that moment, the tree pulsed. The death throes of the queen echoed through the throne room. James blinked in confusion as he saw a spear jutting out from the queen’s chest. His eyes followed the line, and he found Andrew further back, his hand outstretched. James’s lips curved into a grin. He chuckled, his body plopping to the ground.

It is finished.

The earth seemed to shake as the treemen scattered. The massive cockroaches and larvae disfigured and melted into puddles of goo. The tree ecosystem, having lost its monarch, fell into disarray. Walls collapsed and reformed as the queen’s body lay limp on the floor.

“Damn it. You stole my kill…” James groaned.

“Really? With that wound?” Kouki asked.

“That was a joke, Kouki. Do you need a health potion, James?”

“No. I can heal with my biomass.”

“Kouki? What about you and Ralph?” Kouki only had a few scratches, but as James knew, his dragon didn’t look so good. James could repair his raptor, but Kouki’s dragon was unique.

“…He is dead,” Kouki said as he caressed his dragon’s corpse. “He protected me.”

“Ah… sorry for your loss.”

“…It’s fine. Hah… I think I’m going to retire after this. The dungeons are popping up one after another. I am sure the soldiers can handle them. We’re already on track to free the Earth from its clutches. Our job as heroes is almost done,” Kouki said.

“Yeah. We just need to clear this hard-difficulty dungeon, shut off the reactor, and our jobs as survivors are done,” Andrew nodded.

“…”

“…”

“System, are we done with our mission? When can we go back to Earth?”

[Mission complete. A gate to the next floor will spawn momentarily.]

“Fuck! We aren’t done! There are only six hours left. We’re wiped!” Andrew cursed as an orange gate rose from the ground. Seeing this gate reminded James of the gates in novice dungeons.

“It’s fine, Andrew. Sanders and Anderson are evacuating the residents, right? Once we get to the next floor, we’ll still have a few more days before they die of starvation,” James said.

“…Yes. You’re right.” Andrew nodded. James spared the corpse one last glance.

White dragon’s claw(Arm slot)

White dragon’s scale (Damage resistance +10%)(Skin slot)

White dragon’s teeth (mouth slot)

White dragon’s wings (Fall resistance)(Extra slot)]

James inspected Ralph’s corpse. He closed the screen. He wasn’t so insensitive as to plunder the dragon’s remains. This dragon was Kouki’s friend. But then, white motes of light gathered around the corpse, consuming it. When they faded, an egg was left behind.

“Is that…”

“Apparently, a dragon will leave behind an egg when they die,” Kouki said.

As the group entered the spawned gate, they were transported into an arena where the sky sprawled across the horizon. James looked beneath the clouds, wondering whether they were still on the same floor. There, a massive orb floated in the sky—a red orb. The blood moon.

“I suppose we need to grab it?” Andrew muttered. “But how? It’s floating way over there.”

“Or maybe we just need to destroy it. The system ain’t responding,” James said.

“James, you have your jetpack, right? Can’t you just go and get it?” Kouki asked.

“Maybe, but how am I going to get back? The jetpack has a cooldown,” James muttered.

“Don’t you have a rope?” Kouki asked.

“Ah… I did.” James pulled out the steel cord from his backpack. After buying a few more rolls, he tied them all together and secured them to himself.

“We’ll pull you in. Don’t worry, James,” Andrew said. Those words were exactly why James was worried. He didn’t want to entrust his life to others.

James leaped toward the moon, his hand stretching out. The moment his hand touched the glowing sphere in the air, the moon expanded. It swallowed him whole. Red light blinded his vision as he crashed into another chamber.

As he reoriented himself, he felt a tug on the cord entwined around him. Then he felt a strong pull, as if Andrew and Kouki were trying to yank him away.

“I’m fine!” James called out as he peeked from within the moon. “Come here.”

Andrew and Kouki gaped. Thanks to their stats, they climbed with James’s cord into the red moon’s embrace like a pro climber. Inside the chamber, they found a pristine red gem nestled at the center.

[New objective: Gather or destroy the dungeon core.]

“Well, look who’s late to the party,” Andrew mocked the system for its late reply.

“Well, what are we waiting for?” James approached the gem, but Andrew and Kouki drew their weapons.

“Careful, there is a chance the dungeon core will resist,” Andrew said. “If it starts doing anything funny, shoot it.”

“No need to tell me that, smartass,” James said as he approached the gem. His sword was ready. A golden glow shone from it. Kouki grabbed his sword tight, ready to unleash a sword skill the moment the gem made any funny move. Alas, James went for the gem and successfully picked it up. The red glow of the moon soon dimmed. The system remained silent, but a familiar golden ball spawned before him.

“Is this…?”

“That looks like a teleportation ball from when you clear a dungeon. But why didn’t the system announce that we cleared it?” Andrew frowned.

Kouki touched the red glowing ball, and the world turned red as they were transported into the waiting room. A loading screen was then carved at the edge of the stone slab. Once it reached 100%, the world flashed white and they returned to the nuclear complex.

No fanfare. No congratulations. No rewards. They were spat out just like that. James and Andrew looked at the surroundings in a daze, wondering what had happened.

“What the heck?” Kouki cursed. “No reward? Damn. So much for a hard dungeon.”

“Is the dungeon cleared? Is the nuclear complex safe? Hey! Are there any scientists here? Let’s go inside!” Andrew snapped himself awake and rallied the gathered people. He looked over the people who were transported here. Several people had died—Andrew knew that—but it seemed most had survived.

A general and a captain rallied their men. A team of scientists went inside the nuclear complex and pressed the first SCRAM button they saw. The humming of the nuclear reactor died down as they scrambled for the control panel.

James stepped outside, looked around the nuclear complex, and sighed. The results might not be satisfactory, but they did it. They had cleared a hard-difficulty dungeon.

The red gem rolled in his hand.
