Feeling yourself, or, well, part of yourself, being dragged along a river of power and then slammed into and through a portal was perhaps not the strangest thing Luke had experienced these past couple of months, but it was up there. When his consciousness stabilized, he found he wasn't alone. Falzyxi was in there with him. Defining it as being in the dungeon was not entirely correct. It would be more accurate to say the two of them together were the dungeon. Luke was part of the weave. No, he was the weave, and so was the adversary. Together, they were the weavers.

Not having a physical body wasn't new, but the space his consciousness occupied was so different from his usual experience that it took some getting used to. His skills were not available to him, and neither was his interface. Luke couldn't even see or hear, in a sense, because he didn't have ears or eyes, nor their metaphysical counterparts. The weave, though. That was all there. Through that, he gleaned information, information he needed. Time didn't work right, for starters. Hannah and the others hadn't stepped into the dungeon yet. Through Luke's limited understanding of the space, he surmised that a second had passed since he found himself having become part of the limited weave inside the dungeon. Everything about it was there for him to know and manipulate. Or it would have been, had not Falzyxi beaten him to it.

The void being knew what it was doing. Either it was knowledge the bastard carried with it, or it was a faster learner than Luke would have thought, because it was reforming the dungeon to its own specifications, changing the intended scenario and winning conditions. Things changed too fast for him to follow along, but since he knew it wouldn't be a good idea to let Falzyxi set all parameters, he leapt in to attack. Again, 'attack' was not a great word for it, because there was nothing to attack. Falzyxi was as much a part of the weave as Luke. In a sense, they were both one, and neither would be able to hurt the other without hurting themselves in the process.

The fight, then, as it was, could be better described as a child ruining another child's sandcastle on the beach. It didn't hurt the other kid, but damn, did it piss him off. Falzyxi had been hiding part of his source in some obscure cave system while also changing the inhabitants of the dungeon so the void could reach in and affect them. Yeah, the void. It had come with Luke and Falzyxi, or perhaps the void outside the weave of the dungeon was part of the void being somehow. That information was unknowable in Luke's current state. He was limited in so many ways, and the creature who was supposed to get beaten after being dragged to this place was thriving in ways Luke didn't even understand. It had experience with this sort of thing. There was just no other way it could know how to work its will into the dungeon, not with such ease. Falzyxi was not supposed to have followed its Source. Luke hadn't even known such a thing was possible.

Both of them were still out there in the void, fighting going on around them, but those were just shells. The real fight would be in the form of working the weave in this dungeon. Somehow, Luke would have to learn and come out on top. The question then was how?

The group stepped through the portal, their threads joining the weave with all their glorious splendor. Through them, Luke read fear and apprehension, but also an excitement and courage that made pride flow through him. This was how. Together with his friends. They would kill Falzyxi together and make sure Earth survived to fight another day. Soon, they would be allowed to step into The Greater System. That was not something they were going to miss just because some sort of hunger demon in void form decided they looked like a nice snack.

The appearance of his friends did not go unnoticed by Falzyxi, who set several threads into motion. Luke wanted to follow those events to see what was going on, but more immediate threats took priority. Falzyxi was working on closing the portal. The why of that action stumped Luke, who figured the void being would want to escape through it, but it didn't matter. Falzyxi wanted it closed, so Luke wanted it open. His friends needed a way out, if nothing else. Then again... With no portal open, there would be no risk of a dungeon break. The monsters in there would be far more dangerous than anything they had faced before. Even if he had trouble finding such monsters right at this moment, would it not make sense to close the portal? That way, no one would have to stay behind and guard the exit. The hesitation made the decision for him, and the gate closed. The adversary's hunger radiated through the weave, but there was something else there too. Amusement. That bastard was laughing! Luke would make sure to shut that asshole up one way or the other.

His friends were on the move after recovering from the closing of the gateway, and they were about to make a mistake right away. Nick seemed to be in charge, and he wanted them to split up. Didn't Curtis's brother ever watch scary movies? You never split up! This was something he could fix, Luke thought. Reaching into the weave, into himself, he pulled on threads and reformed connections between others. Once he got started, he thought he was doing a pretty good job of it. Monsters would appear. Strong enough to challenge them and make them see that they needed each other. Just a quick fight and then they'd be a group of 12 again, rather than two groups of six.

That didn't work out so well. Falzyxi corrupted Luke's workings somehow. The monsters he saved, but turned them into potential void creatures. It was a difficult concept to wrap his head around, but they now came with the possibility of turning. That cost Falzyxi, though, and the void being lost a small amount of its Source to that decision, which was interesting. Interesting indeed. New knowledge was good, but it had come at a cost. Two monsters in different locations, one for each smaller group, and both would be far more challenging than what he had planned. Perhaps too challenging.

The events that followed were disconcerting. Falzyxi's hunger translated to monsters turning into void creatures and infecting others who didn't have the will to withstand the attacks. Kenny didn't make it. Priyah lost an arm, but in reading her thread, Luke found she was stronger for it. Ray's Source blade put in some good work, and Penny was a star. Her command of the weather, combined with an already strong grasp of both will-based attacks and the ability to target Falzyxi's avatar's gathered Source made would have made her the most formidable Integrated in that group if it hadn't been for Curtis.

Curtis, Curtis, Curtis. What worried Luke the most about his friends in the dungeon was not them having to stand up to void creatures that were part of Falzyxi, but the way Curtis went about it. Guide had been pretty clear on the fact Integrated should stay away from pure Source, on account of the whole dying thing, but Curtis hadn't listened. A small sliver of Source from the thing Kenny became was enough to get a taste for it, and that taste looked like it turned into a hunger of its own almost right away. Rather than die, Curtis grabbed the power and made it his own, even without him being able to even see the weave, much less attack Source channels. He just sensed its presence and leapt at the chance of consuming it. That brought about a change in the man and the way the weave reacted to him. Luke didn't think it was a good one. One other small clue was the fact that Falzyxi more or less poured more Source into Curtis by force. That was too much. It couldn't go on. Luke would have to step in.

The question was how. Embodying the weave itself in this dungeon did not mean Luke was able to reach in and have a direct influence on what was happening. He was no god. Falzyxi managed through affecting minions, but Luke didn't want that. Tugging on a friend's thread like that might destroy them, and that was an unacceptable risk. An avatar, that was what he needed. Someone or something to influence.

A peasant hid in a storage shed next to a potter's shop, trembling with fear. Looking down on him, it was difficult to look past the exterior of a person, but Luke knew the inhabitant was no more than a mirage placed inside the dungeon to act out something that happened long ago in a now-dead system. Still, he felt apprehension when reaching down and grasping the selected target's will, molding it into his own. Falzyxi noticed what was happening, of course, and for a moment, it looked like the much stronger entity would grasp control before Luke could finish, but its will was weak and spread too thin. In redesigning the dungeon and reaching down with its infectious void, Falzyxi didn't have the power to deny Luke in this one thing, at least not for the moment.

The person in the shed stood and straightened his shoulders once Luke had reshaped him into his own image. He wanted the others to recognize him, but making an exact copy was beyond his capabilities. The Avatar of Healing was born, and Luke clad him in a robe not unlike the one he usually wore, opting to make it white to show separation from the enemy. Luke wasn't in the Avatar. It was more like steered him like a puppet, almost, and he was able to direct his own will through it. That way, he would be able to affect the dungeon in a more direct way.

Reaching the square where Falzyxi was pouring Source into Curtis, Luke hurried over. On closer inspection, it wasn't going into Curtis's meta-heart, because he didn't seem to have one. How was that possible? It was just darkness in there, except for the thin sliver of Source that was getting thicker by the moment. No mana, no health, no stamina. Just darkness and nothing else. Almost nothing else. To Luke's senses, it looked a little too much like the inside of a void creature, but there were two differences. Darkness was not the same thing as the void. The void was nothing, and darkness was still something, even in this context. Also, it was Curtis. While he was a grumpy, curt asshole sometimes, he was still a friend, one with values, personality, and wants, even if those wants were now being overwhelmed by the need for Source.

Luke reached through his Avatar and out to Falzyxi's. In that moment, their wills clashed, and he felt the adversary's hunger. It hadn't lessened at all. If anything, it was even more all-encompassing now that they were in the dungeon. The Source belonging to his opponent was still a raging, unstoppable river, but here Luke sensed a difference. Falzyxi had used a lot to bend the dungeon to its will, and the Integrated inside had taken more still and claimed it for themselves and Earth. The plan was working, at least in part. Luke's Avatar was stronger than Falzyxi's, but not by much. In chasing off this aspect of the void being, he felt himself dimming. Not wanting to lose the Avatar he had created, he reached down and plucked it out of the physical plane, at least satisfied that Curtis was out of harm's way. Before he went, he shared a few words with his friend through the mouth of the Avatar, urging him to stay away from the Source and to hurry. The others were in danger, and Luke felt a shift coming. Falzyxi was making plans within plans, and Luke wasn't sure he could keep up.

Nick was dead. He died the moment he got caught in the void of another creature. Luke wondered what that would do to Curtis once he found out, but didn't have time to dwell on the question. Hannah, courageous and willful Hannah, threw herself at that same creature to stop its rampage. Her initial attempt at fighting against a void creature in the weave hadn't ended all that well, but she seemed to have taken the right lessons from it, and was now able to go toe-to-toe against this one, despite her mind wandering and her focus being thrown off again and again. Hannah beat the weaker void creature by herself in the end, but there was something about her state of mind that worried Luke. He couldn't get a good sense of what happened between her and Falzyxi's minion in those final moments, but it left a lasting impression in her that worried Luke. He wanted nothing more than to be there next to her, but it wasn't to be. Not yet.

The weave of the dungeon began shifting around him, and it took a moment for Luke to understand what was going on. This was the natural progression of the changes Falzyxi set in the moment before. They had been slight then, but it was all crashing in now to a worrying degree. Reaching in, Luke pitted his will against Falzyxi's to stall while he tried to figure out what was going on, but found it wasn't just the adversary he needed to contend with. It was also the weave itself. The small tweaks from earlier had propagated and turned into a river of change. It was well beyond Luke's ability to stop the flow of new events as the dungeon transformed. Reading into the weave, he saw what Falzyxi was attempting, and shuddered at what it would mean for his friends in the dungeon.

Unable to stall any longer, Luke forced his will into the river and tried his hand at the craft Falzyxi had apparently already mastered. It was nothing more than small things, but perhaps those tiny corrections in the raging river would create a path out of this place for his friends, one where they walked out with their lives intact.
