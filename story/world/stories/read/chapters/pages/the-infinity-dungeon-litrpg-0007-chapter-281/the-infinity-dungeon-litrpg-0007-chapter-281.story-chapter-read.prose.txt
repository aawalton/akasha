Chapter 281

When Master Yu vanished, the Qi followed him, draining away from the room and leaving it empty, cold and dim. The pillars remained, and their characters transformed into letters. While the letters did not need Qi to convey their teachings anymore, they also lost their luster and supernatural aura. Now they were simple text, a reminder of old lessons but nothing more. A stele that Michael could come back to should he ever need to remember the teachings of the old man and his strange game of black and white stones.

Only the altar at the far end of the room still held some lingering energy. Which was slowly coalescing into something. It felt familiar.

“What do you think is going to happen?” Icarus asked as they walked at Michael’s side.

“I have a hunch already,” Michael said. “Master Yu called it a seed, but this is the dungeon, remember?”

The altar was a simple marble block without any decoration. The only thing of note was a small bead of swirling Qi hovering above its surface, very bright and surrounded by a gale of energy that radiated out from the center and slowly disappeared among the dim pillars of the huge room.

Michael extended his hand, watching as the Qi around the little thing parted around him without hurting him. When his fingers touched the bead, it was cold and smooth to the touch. It then shot toward him, much like this very same floor’s reward had shot toward his forehead when he had won his first game against the Master.

Origin System™ (OS-v.0.4) - Admin Mode | Debug Mode

Inbound dungeon connection detected.

Decoding message…

Icarus took over, waving the dialog box away. “It is as you thought,” they said, reading Michael’s mind. “We just completed a hidden challenge.”

They paused, looking up and around. In truth, they were probably interacting with a million screens and data lines in the inner space, and the slight twitches of their elf ears in the real world avatar were only a reflection of what they were really doing.

“Dungeon magic is attempting to alter the state of the Inner Space,” they said.

“You are cute when you’re all focused,” Michael teased them.

“Overriding,” Icarus said casually, ignoring the teasing. “I’m going to try to let it through and trace it back.”

Origin System™ (OS-v.0.4) - Admin Mode | Debug Mode

Attempting to piggyback dungeon connection…

Attempt failed…

“Well,” Michael said, patting Icarus on the head and making them squirm. “We tried.”

Michael retracted his hand, making Icarus pout.

“Is this your way to get back at me for trying to ragebait you yesterday?” they asked, still fighting the shivers that coursed through their body.

“I don’t know what you are talking about. Oh, the David overtaking me situation? Of course not.”

Then the playful smile turned serious, as before him, on the altar, the bead of condensed Qi vanished. For the briefest of moments, he felt a familiar energy touch him. He looked up, half expecting to see Infy behind the altar, but saw nobody there.

Icarus smirked. “Would you look at that. There is a new item in one of the storage rooms.”

***

“Would you look at that, indeed,” Michael said.

Before him, a gigantic piece of blue paper took up almost the entirety of the huge warehouse. Above and below, as well as all around, other similar warehouses were slowly being filled with random items, coming through portals that continuously opened and closed, depositing new things and taking others away.

The sprawling complex of storage rooms, some looking like warehouses and others so fortified they were more similar to miniature Vaults, had been built in a relatively empty portion of the inner space. Data lines, artillery stations and other weapons surrounded the structure like orbital rings, the lines providing a connection to the Origin System that was quicker than the general-purpose connection that existed in the rest of the inner space and in Michael’s Domains.

Its purpose was simple. These storage buildings were the heart of the Origin System’s Inventory module. As of now, not many users had unlocked it–it was expensive to unlock it through the Shop–but some had and were beginning to see its usefulness. It was a good thing that they were still testing the System internally, plus some other guinea pigs like the President, because running the Inventory was ludicrously expensive. Expensive enough, that even the recent addition to the collectors was beginning to not be enough.

“It’s a blueprint,” Icarus said, walking around it like with feline grace. “I’m going to touch it.”

As soon as they did, the blueprint started to burn and vanish. Moments later, it was gone, and Icarus phased out of the room, flying into the wider space on wings made out of fire while dragging Michael with them. The two stopped when they were hovering right above the Aura Accelerator’s main ring, where one of the original Qi coins Master Yu had given Michael worked as a focusing lens for the mana coursing through it.

With a mental command, Icarus summoned a hologram.

“Is this what the blueprint was for? You didn’t give me time to study it,” Michael said.

Icarus nodded with an excited grin on their face. Hovering a few inches above their head, a pair of fox ears bobbed up and down–also made of dancing flames like their wings.

“A complete overhaul of the whole ring…” Michael muttered, in awe at what he was seeing.

From the focusing lenses, to the connections, to the very structure of the ring, the schematic left nothing unchanged.

Icarus too was struggling to come to terms with what they were seeing. “This is a huge project, Michael.”

“But once it’s complete, if I'm understanding the design correctly, the ring will take any lower energy we have and just seamlessly transform it into Qi,” he replied.

“Yes,” Icarus said. “This seems to be the dungeon, or Infy, giving us a hint about how to proceed to Gold Tier the proper way.”

“By transforming the lower energies into higher ones.”

“Once we figure out the mechanism, I will be adding it to the Candle Light wiki and make it purchasable through the Origin System to those who reach the requirements.”

“Yeah…” Michael said distractedly. As he looked at the hologram and started deconstructing it, numbers sprung up in his vision. Either him, the System, Icarus or a mix of the three had extrapolated a few things from the blueprint already. “Gosh, look at the material costs! This puts upgrading all of the collectors to shame! What even is Trinium Alloy? Spirit Stones?”

Icarus shrugged. “Nothing new, my other half.”

“I’m pretty sure they are new.”

“The materials themselves are,” Icarus said with a chuckle. “The ludicrous material costs are not. We have been at it for weeks and we are still only three percent of the way through upgrading the collectors and batteries!”

“It’s been weeks already?” Michael asked. He checked the date and indeed, it was close to the end of October. “Man, I miss the time dilation. Say what, let’s try to upgrade something every day from now on.”

“If we can get the materials, sure.”

“And we need to expand the inner space. It’s getting cramped.”

A notebook appeared in Icarus’s hands, alongside an oversized pencil with a pink rubber tip. “Sure, adding it to the list,” they said in an exaggerated tone. As they spoke, the flames making up their wings and ears began to dance wildly, getting brighter and brighter and illuminating their and Michael’s face with orange light. “Let’s see. Expanding the inner space requires more aura, which requires collectors and batteries. So, step one brings us back to step zero. Then, once the aura pushes the void chaos clouds stuff–whatever it is–away, you will surely want to expand the shield generator. Why not also expand the aura ring as well? And we can’t forget defenses. Adding more materials to the shopping list.”

Michael laughed. “Yeah yeah, you cheeky little elf AI. Had fun already? It’s all stuff we need to do, we can’t slack off.”

“I know it well. I was just teasing you, Michael mine. What’s some more work when we already have so many unfinished things that all require our attention, am I right? I’ll begin working on, well, everything.”

Several more Icaruses appeared all around them, each wearing slightly different clothes. They all flicked their tongue at Michael, giggling playfully, before flying away to different sections of the space. The Icarus that remained behind was about to speak when they suddenly looked into the distance.

“I’m getting a signal,” they said.

Michael detected it a moment later. “Is it the MIF Garden? Finally!”

“Yes,” Icarus said. “It’s Gaia. She's asking us to prepare the defenses and be ready to open the shield! The ship is under attack!”
