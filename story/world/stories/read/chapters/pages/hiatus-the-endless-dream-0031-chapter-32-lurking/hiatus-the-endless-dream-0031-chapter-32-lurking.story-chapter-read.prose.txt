The reluctance didn't leave the man's shoulders. There was a shaky, pleading look in his one eye. I opened my mouth to reassure the man of my reasons, but closed it just as readily. I motioned towards the path and changed my words. "I'm sure these Survivors will last all by themselves."

The sentiment landed, so I pushed a little more. "You know. Jeren saw only two futures. The one where I wasn't here, these two were protecting your corpse."

He flinched at that knowledge. Eye and words were both directed towards the ground, and I couldn't quite make out the shape of either. It got him moving.

I followed closely behind.

New blots took their place, and the more I looked, the more cohesive the parts became until ovular slits formed in the dark.
