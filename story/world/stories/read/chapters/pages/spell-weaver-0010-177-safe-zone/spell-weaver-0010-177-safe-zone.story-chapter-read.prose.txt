Mage shook out both of his arms and moved his head from side to side, preparing for the worst-case scenario. He breathed out quickly a few times before holding out his hand and going still.

“What are you doing?”

“Might as well try it too, right? We have the same stats; if we both practice it, we can combine what we’re figuring out.”

“Yeah, good call. You work on the movement, I’ll work on the perception?”

Warrior took a few more steps to the side, creating extra space, and turned to the right, looking from one end of the workshop to the other.

Spinning up the first test spell, he did it with as low an amount of power as he could. This meant building the spell circle just barely large enough to fit around his body and intentionally keeping the [Spell Weaving] stacks low. Building the targeted base, followed by the control nodes, and finally the swift rune, meant he was naturally at three stacks when it was cast.

Warrior took the time to do the same, casting the spell with the same parameters before attempting to take a few steps forward. With more caution than Mage’s first attempt to run, he was able to move without falling, but it felt awkward. “I don’t think it’s bad either, though,” Mage said, his excitement growing. “What’s our biggest strength?”

“Dashing good looks?”

If speed of thought or mental agility is the only thing this spell is lacking… does that really matter? We just need to adjust our mind to move this fast. I’m certain it can keep up.”

“Hell, yeah. Where should we start?” Mage asked.

“Let’s start with base [Spell Weaving] stacks and go through a few mana pools’ worth of casts to get used to the movement. When that’s a bit better, we can move to increasing the stacks and eventually do a few tests with the layered circles.”

“Oh, yeah. That will be amazing. Holy…” he tapered off as the implications fully settled in. “How long do you think this’ll last when layered with the extended duration?”

“Extended duration? I was thinking more about the empower rune layer. If it’s already moving this fast… what happens when it’s boosted?”

They both laughed, eagerly jumping into testing the new spell's limitations.

Scout sat nervously, looking at the metal table that acted as the centerpiece of the HA holding room. He’d been worried that something like this would happen while entering the Safe Zone.

Edwin had even warned them before he left that the requirements to get in had grown stricter over the last month. But the main-Alex had assured them that it wouldn’t be an issue as long as Scout had the government-issued ID card from the shadow rift.

In the end, he knew that there wasn’t really anything they could do. The ID was legitimate, and if they called anyone with actual standing, they’d find out that the ID was from the Epic Rift they had specifically set aside for power leveling their stronger members.
