Leaving the broken-down version of Earth was like being torn into an infinite number of pieces while the entirety of existence folded in around you. It wasn't painful per se, but Luke was conscious during the whole process, and it was not a pleasant experience. Following that exit, a vast, brilliant field of stars spread out before him. Without the feeling of movement, he appeared in front of one such star. Luke's system. Earth. While leaving the other system had been an uncomfortable but ultimately painless process, entering his own was something else.

Being pieced back together from a cloud of pieces small enough to be almost non-existent wasn't like what happened when The Fallen Shepherd got his hands on someone. It was worse, like being mushed together in a ball of matter and then twisted like a Rubik’s Cube until the pieces fit together, showing solid colors on all sides. Luke felt all of it but was unable to do a damn thing about it. Healing didn't work, not even to deal with the pain. If he had a working mouth, he would have screamed until he lost his voice. Once that part was done and he'd been re-integrated into the system, the worst had to be over, didn't it? No, it didn't. Now, he got the full effect from touching the greater system thread with Manipulate Thread. Like before, his arm tore itself to pieces, followed by his chest crushing in around his meta-heart. Entering meant the restoration of his skills and abilities, meaning Luke could heal himself, restoring the broken mess that was his body again and again as he raced ever closer to his destination. Blood poured out of his eyes again, and when he screamed, more blood gushed up his throat. Even if he had managed to scream, his hearing went out again and again, even more blood oozing out. The robe, now repaired again, had formed around him, but it was soon wrapped tight around Luke's body, slick with blood. Weaver's Renewal helped again and again, but mana was a scarce resource, and it was running out at a never before seen rate. This had to be over soon, or the journey between systems would claim Luke's life.

Through it all, Luke held on to the thread, refusing to let go. He was returning to his home, and it was with a singular purpose in mind. The light grew larger and larger, so bright he was glad for blood obscuring his vision. More than half-way to the point of madness from the pain, pain that would not answer to numbing his own nerves, even, Luke bellowed, followed by his throat once again filling up with blood and bile. The light embraced him, and Luke felt air whipping around him, blessed air.

Holding on to the echo thread, Luke used Weavestep, ignoring the immense pain, his lack of mana, and the fact he was covered in blood as he stepped in. The open air disappeared, and in an instant, he appeared in an office building, again screaming at the top of his lungs. Everything was so vivid, he could even see the threads in the dress-shirt of the man sitting right where Luke appeared, and the individual pores in his skin even as he turned. The moment held and stretched for what felt like an eternity as Weaver's Perception spread out in all directions, finding life everywhere. No more silence, no more dead Earth. This was the moment he'd returned for. This was it.

Luke drove his fist forward, packed with all his frustration, rage, fear, and pain, pivoting with his hip to deliver a punch filled with everything he had to give. It crunched into the side of the guildmaster of Integrated Solutions Group's neck with enough force to break it. Alan's head snapped to the side with a crack. He flew off to crash into the wall of his office and dropped to the floor in a heap, dead in an instant. All his planning and scheming were reduced to nothing.

Laughter bubbled up Luke's throat, and he couldn't hold it in. Relief washed over him. He'd found his way back. He'd made it! Raising his fists over his head, he let out a whoop of joy. Holding that pose, face tilted up toward the ceiling, it dawned on him what'd just happened. That's when the screaming started. Or more like, that was when his ears unclogged, pieces of dried blood dropping out. Rubbing at his eyes to clear them enough to see what the hell was going on, Luke turned to face a small gathering of people standing on the other side of the side. A quick glance over at the heap on the floor made his victorious grin sour a little despite the feeling of triumph filling his chest.

"Luke!"

He blinked. "Dot?"

She was shrieking at him, standing behind two broad-shouldered police officers, her eyes wide with something. Fear? No, couldn't be! Could it?

"What have you done?!"

His hands dropped to his sides as he took in the room, ignoring the police officers pointing guns at him. A mirror on the other side of the room showed his reflection, and it was a horror-show. Blood covered him from top to bottom, staining his skin and robe red.

"Oh," he said. "Relax. It's my own blood."

"You killed Mr. Schmidt!" a young woman farther back in the room said, her hands covering her face, like she was trying to hide behind them, peeking out through her fingers.

"Well, yeah," Luke said, deflating. He hadn't expected a fanfare, clapping or anything. Truth be told, he hadn't thought about anything other than strangling the life out of Alan. Once he was gone, his mind manipulation was supposed to drop, but now Dot was looking at him like he was a monster.

"Hands in the air!" the cop on the left ordered.

"Dot," Luke said, taking a step toward her.

She shied back. Dot was scared of him.

"Don't make me shoot you right here!" the same cop shrieked.

Ray: "Oh shit, it's working again. You're back?"

Luke stopped dead in his tracks and raised his hands to buy a little time. Taking a bullet from an overzealous cop wouldn't be the most dignified way to end a day of suffering.

Luke: "What is Dot doing in Alan's office?"

Ray: "You got the guy, I take it?"

Luke: "Ray..."

Ray: "Look. It wasn't my fault, okay? I took her to the other building and dealt with that sniper chick, but she refused to stay. Your girlfriend kicked me! When I held her down, she used that slithering thing inside me and poked at my heart or something. Wasn't a good time, let me tell you. Anyway, they tracked her down and took her back after a while, a whole group of them. Couldn't get away carrying her with me, now could I? Figured you didn't want me to kill that many."

Ray: "...Sorry."

Ray: "Really didn't mean to fuck up again."

Luke: "It's fine. You did the best you could."

Luke took in the room again. They were still in New York. That much was obvious. It wasn't the same place they'd fought in before, but he thought it was the same building, at least. He swallowed hard. "Dot."

She gave him a look full of disdain that made his chest constrict. When she spoke, it was in a tone he hadn't heard before, low and full of disappointment. "I can't believe you killed Alan. You killed again." After another moment, she spat out one final word. "Murderer."

In that moment, her read of the situation and feelings around what he'd just done were just too much for Luke to handle. The shouting and general chaos of the situation didn't lend itself to explaining either, and, when he looked inside himself, he found a deep anger at her reaction. After everything he'd done, she didn't trust him? Fuck that. Rather than waste any more time, he turned from her and toward the others, speaking in a cold, calm but loud voice.

"If you hurt her in any way, I'll return here and destroy ISG and everyone in it. I'm sensing my friends far down. Below ground, if I'm not mistaken." Luke pointed at the man standing in the center of the group of suits. "You will take me to them. Now."

"Get down on the ground or I'll shoot!" the same cop shouted.

The other one added. "Hands up!"

Luke turned to glare at them both. "Do you think your bullets can hurt me? Try it."

After a moment of hesitation, both cops lowered their firearms, their faces white and eyes wide.

"Didn't think so," Luke said, turning to walk out of the room, waving for the suit to follow. "Come."

For once, someone listened, and the man followed out onto the staircase. They left the room behind. No one followed. After descending a few flights of stairs, Luke stopped to let him catch up. "The other Integrated."

"Yes?" the man's voice was warm but serious.

"Any casualties?"

The man cleared his throat and held out his hand. "I'm Peter."

Luke just looked at the hand until Peter dropped it.

"Right, casualties. Aside from Mr. Schmidt, we lost six guild members. Three lost their lives attempting to subdue the Assassin, two for the same reason but against the Reaver, and one succumbed in the initial fall."

Luke gave a slow nod. "Sorry about that. Only meant to kill Alan."

Luke: "I can sense you inside the building."

Ray: "Just hanging around to see if I can spring the two losers who got caught."

Ray: "Wasn't sure when you were coming back. Didn't expect it so soon, to be honest."

Luke: "How long was I gone?"

Ray: "An hour? Maybe hour and a half?"

Luke: "I'm coming down the stairs. You can join us."

Ray: "Be right there."

Peter kept his gaze locked on Luke's face during the messaging, waiting. "Loss of life is always unacceptable, of course, but I can understand your needing to reach for extreme measures. There are many of us who did not agree with Mr. Schmidt's methods."

"Sure," Luke said.

Peter stopped. "I'm serious, Mr. Quinn. Integrated Solutions Group will not cease operations after this. I was hoping maybe you would consider rejoining."

"No."

"Understandable," Peter said, clearing his throat. "That is understandable, but perhaps we could find a less antagonistic path forward where you, uh, don't kill any more of our members?"

Luke gave him a look. "I'm not a killer. Let's just get my friends out for now."

"Of course not," Peter was quick to say, waving his hands in the air. At first, he'd seemed stoic and serious for such a young guy, but his nerves were beginning to show. "As a gesture of good faith, we will see what we can do about the aftermath of all this. Perhaps we can find a way to mitigate the legal repercussions."

Ray appeared on the stairs and gave a small wave of his hand. "Hey. You look like shit."

"I feel it too," Luke said, gesturing to his side. "This is Peter."

"'Sup," Ray said, falling into step with them. "Let's go get Curtis. It'll be so fun rubbing his face in his getting caught!"
