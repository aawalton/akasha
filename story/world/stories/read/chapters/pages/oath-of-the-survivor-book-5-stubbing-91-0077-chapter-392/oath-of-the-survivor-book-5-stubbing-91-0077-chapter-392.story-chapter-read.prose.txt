Garth took the opportunity to take a swig of the antifungal medication Corthian prepared for them, fighting back a grimace at the taste. Even though he didn’t feel like he needed it, Garth took the extra seconds.  They were fully engaged in a war of attrition now, and every opportunity he had to rest was important.  As one of their last lines of defense, he wanted to be careful not to extend too far or take any unnecessary risks.  After all, that’s what Kyle was relying on him to do.
