After catching the cloudfin herring, it seemed Adam’s fortune had turned, because he quickly caught a second, and then a third. After that, he didn’t get anything for over half an hour, so he decided to relocate to the western edge near the market, where he caught his first cloudfin trout.

Adam managed to catch another trout before the sun fully set, and he now had more than enough to sell in order to buy the upgrades he needed to evolve. He still needed to use his challenge stone afterwards and he wanted to do more training with shaping his slime body, but he was glad that his time spent fishing had been fruitful.

Part of him had been hoping to catch something like the legendary blue vein spirit that Beck had caught, because it had given him a boost to his luck for free, but Beck had said it took three days to catch, and that was despite knowing the river it swam in. Adam had no such intel on any super rare fish swimming around in the clouds below Interim.

When he went to find Fisher by the pond near his house, she wasn’t anywhere to be seen, and he ended up wasting half an hour searching the island for her in the dark, before finding her behind the dimensional tavern, fighting with a tree that her fishing line had gotten stuck in.

Adam melted the branches with his slime hand to free her fishing rod and then sold his catches to her.

But then he drew up short.

Shit… I forgot about the twenty-five-upgrades limit between stages.

Adam clutched his head, frustrated with himself for forgetting such a simple thing. All his work had been for nothing…

Setting aside the fact that Parvillo could somehow read his thoughts, Adam felt so thankful that he tried to reach out and hug the floating eldritch nightmare cube, but Parvillo zipped out of the way with ease.

“I know you don’t really give a shit about me, but I still appreciate that. Would’ve been a real bummer if I couldn’t buy all the upgrades I needed.”

Just like last time, Adam was stumped by the choices. He went over to the fountain and sat on its edge to think. Parvillo followed after him with the three holograms floating in front of his main eye.

The Trapper immediately caught my attention, just because I can foresee it being incredibly good against other players, but also because it’ll let me farm points, which I definitely need. There’s also nothing about being required to actually kill or harm anyone hit by a trap, so perhaps I can trigger it on friendly targets too, depending on how broadly the ‘trap’ word is applied.

“When it says ‘trap’, does that mean anything that can be considered a trap, even if I make it with my bare hands?” Adam asked.

Awesome.

Two of these at my disposal would be awesome, and since they deliver player damage and all my afflictions, they'll likely be able to one-shot anything until stage seven. And depending on how they’re deployed, getting a barrage glove might make it possible to duplicate them, if they are considered a projectile mid-flight at least. If only I had the Ghost evolution, I’d be able to do a lot of shenanigans. Parvillo might even let me get away with it too.

Jubilant Crafter was a bit of a wildcard, since it required the ability to fuse relics, which meant Adam needed a conjuring stone, either a legendary one or the ‘cracked’ epic version.

“A ‘flawless’ fusion is what, exactly?”

[A Flawless Fusion means that, even if you utilise a Cracked Conjuring Stone, any Fusion Relic made as a result will not have a weakness.]

That’s pretty good.

Crafter would rely on a lot more planning and point investment, but could produce some truly busted combinations, especially if I use a legendary conjuring stone.

Obscene Hunter is similar to Leering Prankster which I picked last time, but depending on what counts as a boss and what is considered ‘contribution’, it could be a lot more powerful, though it’ll require good timing and forethought to utilise effectively.

Adam scratched the bottom of his featureless head with his shapeshifter’s claw.

This is a tough one.

I really like the idea of using relic fusions to get powerful relics, but I don’t actually need to pick Crafter to be able to do that.

I’m also wondering if it’s possible to use a relic renewal stone to make it so a legendary conjuring stone doesn’t break on use, and then recharging it with my Leering Prankster skill. If that’d work, then I’d just need to buy one and could craft as many fusion relics as I want, without having to worry about making cracked relics.

Hunter is also just a bit too complicated to pull off effectively, and trying to satisfy its requirements to reset my relics could end up making me act recklessly. Not to mention, while it’s not as powerful, Prankster’s skill basically does the same thing.

In truth, I just really want to use bear traps.
