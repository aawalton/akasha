On Fae and Names

Names matter. Everyone knows that. And you likely have more than one you go by. The one your friends call you, the one your peers use, the one that identifies you formally, the one your parents use when you are in trouble.

Keep the last in mind when dealing with the Fae. They use names all the time, with each other, even with Humans when interacting. But there is another name they use when Naming. A being's Name is their true identity, it is who they are. Using it touches on that part, influences it.

Most of Awakened Humanity is unaware of their own Name, let alone that of another. They might know the word, but not the intent behind it. Without knowing your Name a Fae has no special power, no undue influence.

Using a Name is not without cost, it is effort, it is intent, it is willpower. It forms a connection, imposes a temporary bond. It draws on comparative authority, on context, on what it is meant to do. Use the wrong Name and you may find yourself influenced, even overcome, or at the very least come under scrutiny you did not desire.

Having them joining us for morning training feels weird, Seb sent.

He did say he expected all four of us. At least Cara looks recovered.

Seb glanced over at Gwen and Cara as Lord Percivus approached. If anything Gwen looked the more out of sorts of the two. Cara's hair was back to normal, bright yet calm with no trace of the exhaustion she'd shown yesterday evening. Gwen kept shifting, not quite fidgeting but not standing still.

"Good morning," Percivus said. "I am glad you all chose to be here. Once you are all warmed up we can discuss how training will proceed."

Despite starting the now routine laps the moment warming up was mentioned Seb still found himself trailing behind both Anya and Cara. He could feel his wife's need to keep up, to push herself and not let Cara pull ahead but it seemed Cara had the same plan. Gwen kept pace with him, her eyes locked on the ground ahead of them as they gradually increased their pace.

Seb eventually caught up to Anya after she outran Cara, his longer legs giving him the advantage. It didn't matter how hard she pushed, in an outright race he would eventually take the lead, it didn't mean she didn't try, that she didn't grumble at him whenever he proved the point. He didn't slow down though, the one time he'd paced her she'd snapped at him.

As he passed Gwen and Cara for the second time he noticed them slow to a stop. Anya joined them rather than continuing. The next time around he did as well. Compared to their usual training this was barely enough to start feeling exertion, but he had said warm up not push.

"I will not be the one to share information," Percivus said. "You are all aware of what the others know and what you can practice in their presence."

"What of Bern?" Gwen asked.

"You will both need to make some decisions. His actions, while problematic, are not actually unprecedented."

"He," Cara began, her hair flashing. "That's true but he still…"

"And failed," Percivus said. "He did not succeed yet chooses to stand by his intentions. You arrived as a team, you have trained as a team, can you still work as one?"

"If we can't?" Gwen asked.

"You will still be expected to perform at a satisfactory level, both in duels and in the Depths."

"Depths?"

"Did you believe you would stop progressing? This year was meant to ensure you all had proper baselines for accelerated advancement. Master Leo's lessons become practical, and extended following the next family visit."

If there are slimes you get to handle them on your own, Anya sent.

If there are slimes we'll find another cavern.

What if they're the lesson?

Slimes aren't a lesson, they're a mess.

Seb could see both Gwen and Cara hesitating, slight gestures hinting at their talking to one another. He supposed that was how he and Anya had often looked, probably still did if Fay's teasing was anything to go by.

"Are teams sent alone?" Gwen asked.

"That will be up to Master Leo. But typically whole dorms Dive as a unit, only the Depthsborn fighting is done by team."

"Should you be telling us all this?" Seb said.

"No. However considering the circumstances I decided, letting Master Leo know as well, that it was best not to have the situation be dealt with as a crisis."

"Have you asked Bern?" Gwen said.

"He expects everything to continue as it has. He is the elder, you are part of his team and will fit into his plans."

Do you think anyone will notice if we hit him rather than make it clear we could have? Seb sent.

Seb! You're supposed to be the-

-one who reminds you that we don't need to lash out? That tries to stay calm?

Yes?

He tried to get you hurt for no other reason than because you are here, and he's acting as though he did nothing wrong.

You aren't fighting my fights for me.

He tried to hurt me too.

He felt Anya start to giggle then stop herself before her shoulders gave more than the slightest twitch. She was right about him not fighting her fights, but Bern was a problem for both of them, or rather all of them.

"Can you fight with him?" Gwen asked, turning towards Cara.

"If you say we are, I can."

"We will talk to him," Gwen said. "Not today though. I will make things clear to him. We can try to work together, but as it stands that is all we will be doing."

"Yes," Anya said as Gwen started to turn back towards them. "You can keep using the spare room as long as we don't find ourselves disagreeing."

"I-" Gwen hesitated, apparently not having been expecting to get an answer before having to ask. "Thank you."

"That may not solve your issues when Diving," Percivus said. "However there is still time before that matters. You have an hour before breakfast, I expect to see you all putting effort into your practice."

Spar? he sent.

We need to push too hard if we do to get any results.

Tether? We haven't tested that since we came here.

Only if you behave.

You first.

It took Seb a minute to find something suitable. He saw Percivus watching them in silence, Gwen and Cara going through variations on katas he thought he recognized if he accounted for the difference in weaponry. It wasn't quite the same as what they were used to using, but the point wasn't for it to feel typical, just for it to restrict their options.

Almost immediately Seb noticed the difference. Months ago he would have closed on Anya, kept forcing the flow as they danced past and over one another. He would have been happy to balance the moment so they only pulled the cord tight as they reset. Now he kept trying to leverage his range, to keep his distance and take advantage of his blade.

Anya kept closing, kept threatening to grapple or strike from inside his reach. The tether didn't let him stop her, didn't give him space to change their tempo. He found himself struggling, not to avoid being hit, but to counter, to push her onto the defensive.

Fight back, Anya sent.

He didn't bother answering, he could feel her amusement at his dilemma. He dropped the sword, the imbued effect pulsing out at a thought and giving him the slightest opening to finally go on the offensive. It didn't last, Anya adjusted before he built any momentum and soon they were settling into a new normal. He'd disrupt her for a moment, she'd recover, they'd fall into their old dance with an emphasis on him defending himself, then the next pulse would start things over.

Seb lost track of time, his focus narrowing on the fight with Anya, on her thoughts, her attention, on the moments where they clashed, where they separated. The meld stretched between them, each motion coordinated despite trying to gain advantage. When they finally stopped at Percivus' call, they found Gwen and Cara standing there watching.

"Breakfast. Off you all go," Percivus said before turning to leave.

"How do you not hurt each other?" Cara said.

"How do you not end up tangled up together?" Gwen asked at the same time.

Seb focused on untying the tether on Anya's wrist as she blushed enough for the two of them. They'd gotten tangled more than once, some of them intentionally.
