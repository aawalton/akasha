Once the Claws were dead and buried, we learned that a stampede of undead beasts was headed for the settlement, although we wouldn’t discover the true extent of the problem for a while longer.

It turned out that a scion named Corpse was behind it all, although he wouldn’t find out until the big squid was eliminated. We worked harder than ever, desperate to grow strong enough to survive and bolster our defenses. With a two-month time limit to reach the free city, Edge, Jumo, Sasha, Trapper, and Mel set out, crossing the Ravenous Wastes and Sea of Trees before arriving at the Lakelands and entering World’s End. At that point, they had three weeks to prepare for their meeting with the Coalition, which takes us to the start of volume four.

They needed to figure out how to talk their way out of joining the Coalition, or failing that, advance enough to negotiate from a position of strength and set favorable terms. After entering the hollow butte containing the free city, their first stop was the Hunters’ Association, where they hoped to hire a local guide and find a job so they could earn some creds.

After hiring her as a guide, Astil took the team to the Lost Traveler, the inn they used as their base of operations while working out of World’s End.

“Edge needed a new weapon that could handle high-stage opponents and make the most of his new skill. He went with Mel to check out the stores on the silver ring, a part of the free city that is only accessible to warriors stage two and above. They stepped into the Notched Blade, which was run by the master weaponsmith Helena—Ander’s mentor and one of the best crafters in the settlement.

“Since the damage bonus provided by Dragon’s Greed scaled with the value of the weapon it benefited, he wanted a killing tool that would maximize the synergy between them. Helena found the challenge intriguing, as well as Melenia’s need for a weapon that would make the most of her Epic skill Titan’s Axe. The craftmaster agreed to take on both commissions for a staggering sum. It was way more than they could afford, so Edge decided to take a loan from the funds Dialla had given him, figuring that he couldn’t look out for the settlement’s interests if he couldn’t grow strong enough to survive the trying days ahead.

“Meanwhile, the crew made some major purchases at the Mortium exchange, filling their empty skill slots with some awesome new powers that weren’t available in Puppet Town. They upgraded their weapons and armor too, which would help them take on the powerful beasts living in the Lakelands.

“Edge did some research in the Hunters’ Association, learning more about its societies along the way, before Trapper found a job that the crew could tackle together. The team rented a floating wagon and set out for a subterrain sub-biome called the Money Pits. Their mission involved exterminating a colony of lithovoric slugs known as Credit guzzlers that were devouring the high-grade resources the harvesters were trying to mine.

“The beasts were acid specialists, and the crew couldn’t afford to take more than incidental fire or the corrosive magic would wreak havoc on their gear, not to mention their bodies, which meant this operation required a stealthy approach. After checking in with the hunters who had posted the job, Trapper’s crew entered the pits and headed below ground. Their first goal was to wipe out as many slugs as possible before the colony caught wind of their intrusion, past which point, their task was going to get a whole lot harder.

“Sakura used Remote Viewing to scout the mine while the crew advanced in stealth mode, eliminating the guzzlers tunnel by tunnel as they approached the massive enclosure the beasts were using as a nest. As with all things in life, their run of good luck wasn’t fated to last forever. The slugs eventually spotted the hunters and bunkered up.

“The guzzlers weren’t particularly strong or agile for beasts their stage, but they made up for those weaknesses in three different ways. They were armed with powerful acidic attacks, which we will get back to in a minute. Second, the whole colony came together if they were threatened, acting as a slimy army led by a trio of stage-three elites. Finally, the beasts had used their corrosive magic to build a series of fortresses inside their nest—a perfect place to sling solvent while shielded from return fire.

“The slugs’ defense was too tough for the crew to crack, which meant they needed to pull the creatures out of position and wipe out a fair fraction of their forces before besieging the rest. Luckily, Sakura’s new skills were perfect for the job. She used a combo to create in illusory lure that fooled the beasts’ senses, a massive mound of the high-grade mineral that was the beasts’ favorite food, which was known as celestial dolomite.

“A third of the guzzlers took the bait, including one of the stage-three elites. When the beasts left their base and approached the lure, Edge and his friends hit them with an alpha strike comprised of their strongest attacks. While he shielded his friends from incoming acid, the crew hit the creatures hard, including some amazing strikes from Solar Flare and Titan’s Axe.

“The hunters wiped out the first set of slugs and proceeded into the nest, where their true test awaited. Faced with scores of acid-flinging opponents, Edge played defense while his friends started killing, using every trick at his disposal to keep the lethal magic away from his team. He figured out how to boost Foebinder with Warlord’s Mantle, and together, man and chain blocked and dodged like a pair of demons while the hunters cleared the slugs out of the first of three forts built into the caverns’ wall.

“They managed to pull it off, luring out a second squad of slugs along the way, including another elite. However, the third and most powerful stage-three was still holed up in the center bulwark, sniping with powerful acid attacks while the hunters battled the other beasts. Edge was the only one who could squeeze his way inside, so he entered the fort take out the elite while his friends wiped out the rest.

“He defeated the sniper slug, ranking up Harden along the way. Before he made it out of the beast’s base, he ran into a problem. The slugs had honeycombed the stone below his feet while searching for dolomite to dine on, and the incredible volume of acid that was fired over the course of the battle had been eating away at the rock. The floor fell out from under him, dropping Edge and countless tons of rock deeper into the Pit.

“He survived the impact thanks to Shadow Play, then battled a final group of beasts in the cave he tumbled into. That was when his run of bad luck turned right back around. With some help from Dragon’s Greed, he discovered a massive deposit of celestial dolomite, along with a metal that was more valuable still. A fortune had fallen into his lap, and with his Spatial Storage Rune, he was able to take it with him as he climbed to where the crew was waiting.

“With the motherload stored safely in his vault, the hunters returned to World’s End and got paid for their successful mission. It turned out that the resources Edge had found were perfect for upgrading implants. He held onto enough for the crew to upgrade their devices and planned to send the rest back to Puppet Town, which would make a big difference for the people back home.

“Everyone had their own priorities for advancement over the coming weeks. The rest of the crew needed to cycle up as quickly as possible and rank up their skills the old-fashioned way. Edge wanted to maximize his skill growth by Extracting some high-rank Rares, which meant he needed to take on some powerful beasts that weren’t ideal for his friends’ needs.

“He decided to join an experienced crew on a big game hunt, led by the veteran hunter Sheela, who most people call Widow. The crew wasn’t leaving until the next morning, so he stopped by the arena’s training annex, hoping to receive some expert instruction from one of the free city’s gladiators.

“That was where Edge met Beelo, a polearm enthusiast and master of the glaive. The pair hit it off right from the start. Beelo showed Edge some useful techniques for fighting with his naginata, and he promised to return for another lesson when Helena finished foraging his new weapon.

“When he left the annex, Edge had one hell of a surprise waiting for him. The crowd was frozen in place, and when he saw who was coming down the road, he shifted into shadows and watched some living legends pass by. No one had seen any sign of the Wardens since the anomaly, but that was about to change. Three captains and their lieutenants were passing through the free city, any of which could capture him in the blink of an eye or destroy him on a whim.

“Salandra the Artist could make her dreams come to life. Drag nightmares from the depths of your subconscious and order them to devour you. Sand-Fist Gin-Bei was a master of stone, a warrior capable of wiping entire settlements off the map without breaking a sweat. Karos the Walking Swarm had a body made from thousands of venomous insects arranged in a vaguely human form. They could appear anywhere at any time, and no one who roused their ire lived to tell the tale.

“Edge didn’t know much about their lieutenants, although they were perfectly deadly in their own right. For some reason, Cassius—Salandra’s right hand—stopped to stare straight at him before the procession moved past, piercing Shadow Play’s concealment effortlessly. The second they were gone, he raced back to the crew to report the unsettling development.

“The crew had no idea what six heavy hitters from the planet’s strongest faction were doing in World’s End, but given their status as scions, he and Sakura needed to get out of the settlement until they had a better grasp of what was going on. One thing was certain, if they Wardens caught wind of the duo’s Unique cores, they would hunt them relentlessly and there would be no escape.

“Fortunately, the job Edge had signed up for would take him to the far corner of the Lakelands, making it easy to lay low until he had a handle on the situation. He met up with Widow’s crew early the next morning, planning to stay in the field until it was safe to return.

“Their target was a peak stage-three rock eel that had killed some people working on the free city’s docks, which meant he was about to embark on his first hunt on open water. He boarded their ship, the Wave-Bound Warrior, and set sail for the eel’s lair a day’s voyage to the north. Along the way, they rode out a seeker storm, and he caught sight of a mysterious bank of clouds that were travelling against the wind—a magical phenomenon native to the Lakelands that people called “the Stranger.”

“They camped for the night and approached the beast’s den shortly after sunrise, a sheltered cover with only one entrance. The eel was sleeping off a heavy meal, which gave the hunters a chance to prepare the field before the battle began. The creature was a plant-manipulator that used massive strands of kelp to bind its prey. The hunters began by sealing off the cove with stone and ice, then flooded the water inside with a defoliant designed to kill the plants the eel employed as sword and shield.

“They managed to complete their preparations before the beast awoke. When it sensed their presence and realized what was happening, the eel knew its only chance was to fight to the death, at which point, a brutal battle began.

“The beast emerged from its underground lair—eager to exact its revenge on the hunters that had dared to defoliate its garden, seal its cove, and challenge right to devour whoever it pleased. While a fair fraction of its kelp was dead, the eel could still control them to a lesser extent. It sent the vines streaking for the ship while looking for an opening to draw close and sink the vessel itself.

“The Warrior was protected by a potent one-way barrier that blocked the beast’s attacks while letting the crew return fire. Apou did some serious damage with his water blades, Stellia let loose with her lightning, and the crew’s captain went to town with the gigantic harpoon launcher mounted to the deck.

“Unfortunatly, the magitech shield was an aether hog. After getting in some nice attacks, Widow had to switch it off. Edge had been firing a crossbow so he didn’t feel useless, but at that point, he switched to defense. He started chopping kelp with his naginata while Foebinder used a machete, keeping track of his allies and enemies with All-Seeing Gaze.

“They managed to hold their own and score some wounds along the way, but the stage-four beast had survived countless life-and-death battles and still had some tricks to play. In addition to controlling aquatic plants, its magic could manipulate plant-type beasts. It had a whole squadron of shore nymphs lurking within the cliffs bordering the cove. The wooden creatures leapt onto the battlefield, just as the eel brought out some fresh vines from a sheltered location inside its den—safe from the chemicals the hunters had used to kill the other plants.

“The hunters had their hands full repelling the nymphs that were boarding the vessel, which meant no one could stop the eel and its army of vines from sinking the ship… no one except Edge, that is. He had been hiding some of his skills, but at that point, it was time to show what he could do. He buffed the whole crew with Warlord’s Mantle, leapt from the deck, and went skimming along the water, firing Vacuum Blasts while letting loose with that oversized gatling boltcaster he loves so much.

“He took on the enraged beast and its army of vines, dodging like a fiend as he stayed one step ahead of the creature’s lethal magic. It bought enough time for the hunters to regain the upper hand, but just when it seemed like the battle was won, the eel flipped the script. It grabbed a half dozen hunters with its kelp and retreated to its lair, planning to kill them all and recover before returning for round two.

“Edge wasn’t about to let them die on his watch, and he needed to get up close and personal if he wanted to claim the predator’s powers for his own. He leapt into the water, used Repel to catch up, and activated Overdrive. He sliced up the vines on his way to the lair, freeing the hunters as he got ready for a ninety-second, one-on-one brawl between man and beast.

“The eel ensured their privacy by sealing the lair with some giant boulders, which was just fine with him. Now he didn’t have to worry about giving away Skill-Eater’s existence—go all out while using every power he had. It was one hell of a battle. He used the chains to ride the enraged eel like a bucking bronco, stabbing with his iceblades and getting slammed into the walls while his skills kept him together.

“In the end, he wound up conjuring multiple lightblades in an electric endurance match that stunned the eel and gave him a chance to Extract its skills, not to mention, score the final blow and earn some bonus experience for his contribution to the fight. Widow wasn’t thrilled that he’d disobeyed her orders, but she forgave him since he’d saved their asses and completed the mission to boot.

“It still wasn’t safe to return to World’s End, so he joined her crew on a second hunt. Sasha came along for the ride while the rest of Trapper’s crew pursued their own avenues of advancement. When they were certain the Wardens had left the free city, everyone returned and got paid. They proceeded with their plans to secure resources and forge alliances for Puppet Town while Edge prepared for his looming showdown with the dark passenger.

“The insatiable entity was starting to wake up, and the meeting with the Coalition was looming large on the horizon. He was running out of time, and he couldn’t tackle either problem until he figured out how to open the Tome of Skill-Eater and restored the ravenous lord’s missing memories.

“On the bright side, Helena had finished crafting their weapons. Edge received Opulence, an amazing glaive that cost a literal fortune and turned the damage bonus from Dragon’s Greed into an astoundingly lethal tool. Mel obtained the primal axe Extinction, which was designed to make the most of her Epic skill, Titan’s Axe.

“When the implant upgrade kits were ready, Edge underwent surgery to have his devices taken to the third and final tier, gaining an extra Auxiliary Skill Slot and an expanded aether well for his Organ-Guard.

“In between hunts, the crew did their best to learn more about the Wardens, the Coalition, and the situation in World’s End. Mel made friends with members of the city watch, and Sasha did the same with the Magisters’ staff. Edge and Trapper forged relationships with some prominent hunting crews, and Jumo entered Lowtown to make contact with the organized crime ring known as the Syndicate, and their leader, the Best of the Worst.

“Everyone bought some new armor, and the rest of the team upgraded their weapons. Jumo and Sasha commissioned kite shields that could blind their foes when struck and fossil spears that could suck blood and spit it out the shafts. Trapper purchased a longbow so big the long part was an understatement, and she and Sakura bought buckler shields with gravity mods that could pull projectiles into them. The scion also acquired a nifty sword that could absorb poison, which meant she didn’t have to reapply it before every battle.

“Back Puppet Town, Dialla, Ander, and Melchior managed to upgrade the core manufactory, taking her one step closer to having all four thousand residents cored before the year was through and ensuring the settlement could thrive in the new era dawning before our eyes.

“Edge and Mel attended a match in the arena, where they watched Beelo kick some serious ass. We were introduced to the ruler of World’s End, The First Among Equals, and her fixer Gill, a seriously scarry dude who could give the captains a run from their money.

“Trapper’s team purchased a ton of resources and arranged for Widow’s crew to travel to Puppet Town to deliver them, along with updates on everything that had happened thus far. On the other hand, Edge hadn’t made any progress on opening the tome. Their meeting with the Coalition was right around the corner, and everyone needed to grow stronger before sitting down at the table.

“The rest of the crew needed Mortium to upgrade their implants, and Edge needed lifecoins to steal more Rare and Epic skills. With that in mind, Trapper accepted a monster-extermination mission in a sub-biome in the far corner of the Lakelands called the Haunted Vale. They had enough time to get there, get back, and spend several days hunting monsters in between, so the team set off to do just that.

“The crew crossed the Lakelands and then camped in a sub-biome called the SunScorch Badlands, planning to enter the Vale early the next morning. A little later that night, one of the planet’s moons would pass Ord far closer than usual, making it appear bigger and redder than normal—a phenomenon people call the Devil’s Eye.

“The team planned to wake up at moonrise to enjoy it together, but a flock of gigantic bats decided to crash the party. Some were melee specialists and some were snipers, so everyone besides Edge returned fire from behind cover. It was more than a little reckless, but he had come up with a plan to drive the beasts off and test out his new weapon at the same time. After running into the open to draw the bats’ attention, he Hardened himself and let one carry him high into the sky, at which point, our hero went to town.

“He used Glide and Foebinder to travel from beast to beast, wreaking some serious carnage with Opulence along the way. Bathed in the light of the Devil’s Eye, Edge carved through one bat after the next, convincing the creatures to seek easier prey after killing their stage-three alpha. The fight took Sakura to the peak of stage two. Before she could evolve, she needed to solve a puzzle box that materialized after acquiring the final cycle—a puzzle that was in line with Lore-Weaver’s nature and might offer Edge clues about how to open the tome if Sakura managed to solve it.

“The next morning, the crew surveyed the borderlands and crossed into the Haunted Vale.

“Many of Ord’s biomes and sub-biomes produce unique magical effects formed by stage-zero plants and animals working together. The Vale’s could play tricks on your senses, luring you onto dangerous terrain or into a predator’s path. It was spooky, but the crew had come prepared with a counter, working together to stay safe while they searched for the bipedal monsters they were hunting.

“The terrain was divided into two distinct strata. Hilly highlands formed a ring around the lowlands, which were filled with a sprawling maze of crevices and ravines. They began by circling the highlands but didn’t come across any sign of their quarry, or any high-stage beasts for that matter. The hunters knew something strange was going on, but they desperately needed to grow stronger and decided to explore the lowlands in pursuit of the missing monsters.

“They spotted salt deposits on the ravine walls, which didn’t make sense, as the Vale wasn’t near any major bodies of water, and the closest ones were fresh instead of saline. Edge sensed corrupted magicytes as the day wore on—far more than he had encountered outside of a dungeon until now.

“After searching for hours, the team finally came across their first monster, but it wasn’t the species they were hunting. Instead of a bipedal predator, it was a colossal stingray that could swim through the air. It was peak stage-three, armed with a triple stinger that could deliver a lethal payload of ice, fire, and lightning mana.

“They crept close enough for Sakura to use her informational magic, and she learned that most of the monster’s skills were specialized for hunting beneath the ocean, which made its presence feel even more out of place than it already was. They couldn’t explore the lowlands with the predator in the region, and since it wasn’t well-suited for its environment, they were confident they could prevail.

“After agreeing to take it out, they unleashed a powerful alpha strike comprised of their strongest attacks. It looked like my friends were going to end the fight in a flash but wasn’t how the story played out. In addition to its elemental tail and gravity powers, the stingray was packing high-speed Regeneration, which let it heal the damage the dealt within a few seconds flat.

“It was an unfortunate development, but the team was prepared to handle that kind of magic, which meant it was time for all their new blood-loss weaponry to shine. Edge kept the monster distracted while the hunters hit it with their bloodsucking spears and crossbow bolts, draining it dry as they fought for their lives.

“The monster was in trouble, but it still had some tricks to play. It shoved the crew back with a power that simulated a tidal current, almost wiping them out before they managed to adapt. In the end, they were able to stun the big fish long enough for Edge to steal its Regeneration skill, along with a copy of Elemental Blade and the skill that was shoving them around like leaves in a whirlpool. Defeating the ray awarded a ton of Mortium, and since there weren’t any people nearby, that meant it had been killing other monsters.

“While the situation was highly unusual, the rewards were solid and the team decided to continue their hunt. While they were charting the lowlands, a flash flood appeared out of nowhere and almost washed Trapper away. That was strange all by itself, but the water was salty and chocked full of tainted magicytes, which made the phenomenon even more mysterious.

“The crew decided to call it a day, camping in the highlands before resuming their mission in the morning. When they returned to the lowlands, the crew searched for the source of the water along with the monsters the had come here to kill. They found more monsters all right… just not the ones they were looking for.

“The flooding occurred again on several occasions, bringing more salt water, corrupted magicytes, and monsters that were adapted to an oceanic environment. The twisted creatures were disoriented and poorly suited to the terrain, making them far easier to defeat than would otherwise be possible.

“The crew earned a ton of lifecoins and experience along the way. They were doing well despite their bizarre circumstances when matters took a dramatic turn for the worse. An incredible eruption of magical energy ensued from the sub-biome’s heart, followed by a second surge originating from the System.

“The dark passenger stirred when it sensed the disturbance, but it didn’t try to take Edge over this time. The hunters knew something was wrong and tried to run, but it was already too late. Before their disbelieving eyes, the lowlands were sealed in a ring of rocky spires as the System transformed the region into a dungeon with the team trapped inside.

“That was unprecedented in and of itself, but what happened next was stranger still. Edge received a quest unlike anything he’d seen before. The planetary AI had been growing stranger in the aftermath of the anomaly, but this time, it addressed him directly and referred to itself as Neo, implying it was different from the System he’d known until now. It warned him not to show any signs of the strange message or reveal its existence, but he didn’t have time to worry about that now.

“The message told him a rift to the monsters’ dimension had opened in the center of the sub-biome, which explained where all the water and aquatic monsters had come from to begin with. They were weak after passing through the portal but would recover quickly. Even more pressing, the handful that had appeared thus far were nothing compared to the thousands that were on their way.

“The rift was artificial, and the crew’s only chance of surviving was to find the portal, pass through to the other side, and destroy something called a dimensional anchor that was forming a bridge between worlds. Their lives were hanging in the balance, but everyone living in the Lakelands was in danger too, since the swarm of monsters was too great to contain within a dungeon for long.

“Edge and his friends had an ordeal on their hands unlike any that had come before. They needed to reach the rift and close it before too many monsters passed through, then wipe out the rest before they recovered and regained their full strength. The team had to race against the clock, dealing with the floodwaters and killing every monster they passed along the way.

“The silver lining was all the magic in the monsters’ realm was corrupted, which the creatures couldn’t use to cast spells. Until they had time to absorbed untainted magicytes, they couldn’t use skills or protect themselves with Disruption, making them easier to kill. The hunters cut down foe after foe, advancing faster than ever as they pushed for the portal.

“Edge reached the peak of stage two along the way. Before he could evolve, he needed to open the Tome of Skill-Eater, discover the truth behind the monster’s invasion, and pick up a third Epic skill, which wasn’t going to happen right away.

“When they drew near the rift, the corruption grew so thick that it would kill them in minutes if they tried to pass through. However, the scions’ Unique cores generated a barrier that shielded everyone inside, allowing the team to reach the portal at last.

“They were ready to head through together, but that wasn’t in the cards. The lords’ protection couldn’t shield the team on the far side of the rift. They had to stay behind with Sakura and kill every monster that came through while Edge stepped into the portal to destroy the dimensional anchor.

“After a vision of travelling from one dimension to another, he found himself standing on the surface of another planet—a place he had seen several times while touring a monster’s core but never expected to visit in the flesh. It was a scene straight of out an apocalyptic nightmare. A dying sun hung heavy in the sky, and all traces of life had been extinguished from the surface, except for the monstrous variety.

“Once he obtained his bearings, Edge realized he was standing on the bottom of what had once been an ocean, which had been reduced to a desolated wasteland with a few lakes of ultra salty water. The portal was below the waterline at first, but the pool had drained over time, and now it was accessible from the land.

“There was a legion of monsters heading for the rift, but he couldn’t afford to worry about anything other than destroying the anchor if he wanted to save his friends and everyone in World’s End. He followed the magic sustaining the gateway to the top of a nearby island as the dark passenger opened its eyes.

“When he reached the top, he discovered the dimensional anchor was powered by the life force of a stage-four monster, which was trapped on the island by a massive rope connected to an onyx pillar. Edge was ready to make his move, but before he could activate Overdrive, the Ancient dwelling inside his soul awoke and tried to take over.

“Meanwhile, Trapper’s crew was holding their own against an army of horrors. They slew monster after monster as they emerged from the rift, weakened and disoriented from passing through the portal. Sakura managed to solve the puzzle box and evolved to stage three, and Trapper did too, upgrading her core from Uncommon to Epic in the process.

“Things had been going well up to this point, but the creatures they had defeated were only the tip of the iceberg. Hundreds more would arrive within minutes, along with a stage-four blob that was far more powerful than anything they’d dealt with yet.

“Back on the monster’s homeworld, the dark passenger was making its move. Edge had planned to deal with the Ancient once he opened the Tome of Skill-Eater and restored the ravenous lord’s missing memories, but the monster had other plans. He was forced to fight the dark passenger without the book’s power. The giant bound by the chains of oblivion split into two creatures—the monster he had been during his previous vision, and a mysterious silver-eyed figure covered from head to toe in a heavy robe.

“It was a two-on-one battle, but there was something wrong with Skill-Eater. The Ancient, however, was fully alert and ready to claim his body for its own, leaving Edge and Skill-Eater trapped as prisoners within his flesh while the monster tried to devour every scrap of magic on Ord.

“He did his best to hold on, but he knew it was only a matter of time before he was defeated. His only chance was to open the book and have Skill-Eater join the fight, but no matter what he tried, he couldn’t break the seal. That was when he remembered Sakura mentioning that the key to opening the relics was tied to the lord’s true nature.

“Long before it had been infested by the Ancient, the ravenous lord had been part of a happy family, which had been shattered when Realm-Traveler disappeared and Boundless-Well returned to Ord possessed by the monster standing before him. Edge had lost his family too and was no stranger to hiding from painful memories.

“In that moment, he realized the reason the book wouldn’t open was because the lord didn’t want to remember its past. He poured his heart and soul into convincing Skill-Eater to face the tragic memories head-on and strike back at the creature that was the source of its suffering.

“In the end, the lord was moved by his plea. Skill-Eater unlocked the book, and its missing memories were restored. Edge was caught up in the ensuring magic and wound up reliving a portion of the lord’s past. The twenty-one had traveled to Ord from another world, living happily until Realm-Traveler and Boundless-Well left to explore the next dimension over.

“Boundless-Well was possessed in the process, and when he returned to Ord, the Ancient twisted his magic and changed its nature. Instead of producing unlimited mana, the lord began consuming it instead. When Skill-Eater, who was known as Skill-Thief back then, confronted its brother, it was attacked, forcing the lord to steal the power that was devouring it.

“Skill-Thief killed Boundless-Well and absorbed the Ancient’s will in the process. Over time, the lord was corrupted by the monster that had corrupted its brother, becoming the skill-devouring monster whose form the Ancient now wore. Skill-Eater’s rampage was stopped by its siblings and it was turned into a core, sleeping away the centuries until Edge opened its reliquary and forged a contract.

“When the vision was over, Edge found himself back in his inner world. Skill-Eater’s memories were back, and the lord was pissed. It took on the Ancient in an astounding exchange of magical might. The furious lord defeated the monster and bound it with the chains of oblivion, sealing it away for the time being.

“The two of them had a great deal to discuss, but now wasn’t the time. He still needed to break the anchor, save his friends, and race to the portal before it closed.

“He activated Overdrive, prepared for a showdown with the stage-four lobster, but things didn’t go according to plan. The creature used an Epic skill to split into three copies, with the original shielded in an incorporeal state. Edge was in big trouble, but he wasn’t going down without a fight. With only ninety seconds until his ultimate ran out of juice, he decided to risk it all by steal the skill that was shielding the monster and barely pulled it off in time.

“Once the lobster was vulnerable, he dredged up every scrap of his power at his command—desperate to break the anchor embedded in its shell. With some help from Foebinder, he unleashed his strongest combo ever, calling upon Elemental Blade’s newest power, Devastating Discharge. He almost broke the anchor, but it was still hanging on by a thread and he didn’t have time to finish the job.

“It would have been the end of our hero if Rue hadn’t come to the rescue. The fox teleported out of his den and onto the monster’s back. He used Warp Claws to sever the anchor, which could damage creatures far above the beast’s stage.

“Rue was sick from the tainted magic, but the beast was still alive. He teleported into his den while Edge grabbed the anchor and leapt off the island, one step ahead of the furious monster. The exhaustion from his ultimate hit him hard, but his skills had his back. Magebane dragged him along the dry sea and toward the portal, which was shrinking by the second. He leapt through in the nick of time, returning to Ord just as the rift winked out of existence.

“While he was busy, the crew was enduring a crisis of their own. The stage-four blob had passed through the portal while he was fighting the lobster. Even in a weakened state, it was too much for the team to handle, but Sakura learned the monster was weak against magic that could alter its body. Trapper hit it with the Flesh to Glass spellshot the System had given Edge, which was strong enough to kill it, but the big bastard blew itself up instead.

“The explosion crushed Mel’s chest, tore Trapper in half, and cleaved Sasha’s head from her shoulders, breaking every bone in Jumo’s body for good measure. A heartbeat before she died, the crew’s leader fired another high-rank spellshot, Healing Sanctuary. The magic put everyone back together as Edge emerged from the portal, which meant that while it had been way too close, they had all managed to survive.

“The System provided a massive reward, and Neo promised to contact him later when it was safe. Edge was about to read through his updates when he was pulled inside his core. Skill-Eater walked through the black door and removed her robe, revealing that she had been a woman all along.

“That takes us to the start of book six, where Edge and the ravenous lord are about to have a serious conversation.
