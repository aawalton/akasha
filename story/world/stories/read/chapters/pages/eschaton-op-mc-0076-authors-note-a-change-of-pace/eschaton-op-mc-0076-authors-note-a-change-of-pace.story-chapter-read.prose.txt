Okay, so I have some pretty unfortunate news. My RSI (Repetitive Stress Injury) that I've mentioned before has become worse again, and is once again not improving, despite all that I have done. My pain is pretty severe again. It's bad enough that it seriously impacts my ability to use a computer as well as just do stuff outside of that. My hands generally ache most of the time. It's really goddamn unpleasant and it's clear that what I am doing right now is no longer working.

That basically leaves me with two options. Option A is to just keep writing and doing what I'm doing now. That would mean I'd probably start popping tylenol like candy and lose use of my hands by age 35. This is a bad option.

Option B is to basically stop all use of a computer beyond absolutely critical tasks. I still don't know what exactly this would entail (I plan to discuss this in detail with my physical therapist.) or how long it would last. But I imagine it would be at least a month, if not more.

But if I want to get better—and I do want to get better!—this is it. This is the only option that isn't an expensive surgery. We've reached the nuclear option. I've been struggling for almost a year now trying to get this under control without having to resort to it, and it's very clear that's no longer possible.

So basically, I have to stop writing completely. There is a possibility I could do some dictation, but that wouldn't allow for edits, so I still wouldn't be able to produce anything that would be postable, unfortunately.

So what does this mean for Eschaton? Well, it means I'll be dropping down to just one chapter a week for the foreseeable future. I have the backlog to keep this up for up to two months and change. That does include Patreon's chapters, by the way. If I start to dig into Patreon's aheadness (that's a word now) then I probably have the rest of the year covered with a chapter a week. My health is really important and I don't think I should destroy myself for this web serial, as much as I love to write it.
