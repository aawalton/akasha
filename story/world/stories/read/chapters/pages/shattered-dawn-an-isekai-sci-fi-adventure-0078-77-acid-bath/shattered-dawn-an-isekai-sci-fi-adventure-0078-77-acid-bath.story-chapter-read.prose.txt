The barrel pinwheeled through the air, end over end as acid sprayed out of it in a spiral. A bit of it splashed back at Elion, but dripped off his greased suit without effect.

One of the warlocks shouted, tearing at his robe as it hissed and smoked. The barrel struck the leg of the arachnatronic but was already empty. It dropped to the ground with a clang.

“There!” The man riding the arachnatronic—Dave—pointed at Elion. “He’s there!”

Elion heaved the second barrel of acid, trying to fling it further than the first. One of the warlocks blasted it in the air with a bolt from his wand. The bolt blasted a hole in the side of the metal cylinder, sending it spinning and splattering droplets everywhere. Elion held up his arms, trying to protect his respirator from the acid.

The bulk of the caustic liquid splashed down onto the ground, running across the path. The warlocks were forced to scramble for safety, not wanting to submerge their feet. Scrambling backward, the arachnatronic moved out of range, though a few spots on its legs hissed and smoked.

“That’s him!" the weaselly man shouted. “It’s pointing right at him!”

Elion grabbed the last barrel and spun with it like an olympic discus thrower. He tipped the barrel, holding onto it and flinging a sheet of acid through the air, trying to reach the arachnatronic and damage the machine holding his knife.

A blast from one of the warlocks struck him in the chest, sending him staggering backwards, nearly tipping into the acid pool. As he windmilled his arms, trying to regain balance, another blast struck him in the left armpit. With no breastplate protecting him there, the strike hurt.

His hurled wave splashed across the ground, drenching one of the warlocks but falling short of the arachnatronic.

Elion cursed as the warlock screamed. His companions left him writhing on the ground, running toward Elion. They splashed through puddles of acid, boots and robes hissing and smoking as the reaction burned into the garments.

Out of prefilled barrels, Elion dashed to the pump, and activated it. The motor spun up and a stream of green liquid came pouring out of it, spilling across the ground. The sent of corroding acid and metal stung Elion’s nose.

He scowled as the arachnatronic turned, running away from the scene. The man was probably going for help, and he was taking Elion’s knife with him.

One of the warlocks stopped to rip off his dissolving cloak. He’d been splashed too much, and the acid was probably burning through to his skin.

The other blasted the pump, a flash of purple light that smashed a hole through the turbine. Acid and sparks showered the area like a deadly firework show. The machine down, though, squealing harshly as it stopped.

The warlock gestured, forming a shimmering protective bubble around himself that partially deflected the toxic rain. Elion hoped he’d coated himself well enough in grease as droplets splattered across him.

The warlock fighting with his robe screamed when the explosion sent a spray of acid into his eyes. He stumbled, falling to the ground and dipping his hands into a green puddle.

Flecks of acid had gotten onto the partially unprotected parts of Elion’s respirator. The mask hissed and smoked. He tasted the tang of acid etched plastic. He was going to have to dump the face mask soon.

He charged out to meet the final warlock, grabbing hold of the man’s long wand and clutching his shoulder.

They grappled, gripping each other, splashing in the acid. Elion tried to throw the warlock down, but he slipped away, throwing Elion off balance. Elion would have fallen, but the warlock’s hand slipped off of Elion’s greased arm.

Tugging on the wand, Elion tried to pull the warlock off balance. The man stumbled forward, but twisted his momentum and forced Elion back. He pinned Elion up against the damaged pump, and pressed his wand to Elion’s neck.

Elion pushed back against the wand, but even in his ascended state, the warlock was stronger than him.

The man snarled, his eyes tinged with violet as he glared into Elion’s face. *He’s ascended too,* Elion realized. *A warlock ascended state.*

Elion struggled to breath as the man blocked off his windpipe. He brought up a knee, slamming it into the warlock’s stomach.

His opponent gritted his teeth but continued the pressure.

Elion tried activating Save a Friend. It had worked before, after all. But the golden threads of light did not come when he called. *Maybe it doesn’t work on Ascended states.*

Twisting, struggling, trying to slide free, Elion slipped across the metallic surface of the pump, the grease on his back lubricating and letting him glide. Pressure from the warlock forced him downward and sideways, toward the acid pool.

Malevolence shone in the warlock’s eyes, and Elion realized that this man wasn’t trying to capture him alive. He was trying to kill Elion.

Elion slid to his knees, the warlock following, keeping up the pressure on his wand. *Just a little further…*

Letting go of the wand, Elion reached for the acid pool. Immense pressure crushed him against the pump as the warlock pushed harder.

Elion’s fingers skimmed the surface of the pool. He dipped his greased hand into the acid, hoping that he hadn’t rubbed off all the protective grease. Cupping his hand, he splashed a handful of acid into the warlock’s eyes.

The man grimaced, clenching his eyes closed. His pressure on the wand slackened, and Elion surged to his feet, pushing the warlock back.

A stripe of red, burning flesh sizzled on the warlocks face, his eyes screwed shut as the acid burned his eyelids away. His skin wriggled, growing back only to be burned away by the acid again.

The warlock did not give up, clutching at Elion, still trying to wrestle him to the ground.

They staggered closer to the pool. The warlock stumbled. Elion saw his chance and pushed. But the warlock caught hold of Elion’s neck as he fell, pulling them both into the pool of acid.

Elion flipped over the warlock, landing head first in the pool as the man splashed down onto his back. His momentum carried him down, fully immersing him in the water.

Elion started to breath, and realized his mistake as he felt acid fill the filters of his respirator. He held his breath, lungs already burning, and swam. His head burst above the surface of the pool, but he didn’t dare take of the mask and breath.

Nearby the warlock thrashed, billows of smoke rising from his clothes and skin as the acid burned his flesh. Elion ignored him, swimming for shore. A wet, burning sensation ran down his side. The acid must have burned through a part of his suit that had not be adequately greased.

A few strong strokes and he reached the stony rim of the pool, hauling himself out. He tore the mask off his face, gasping for breath and choking on the fumes of the dissolving warlock. Doubled over on hands and knees, he coughed. Acid fumes burned his nose and lungs and eyes, and his head spun wildly.

Pulling himself away from the pit and climbing to his feet, he staggered across the uneven ground, ripping the rest of the protected suit off.

In the area under his left armpit—where he’d been struck by a warlock’s blast earlier—the suit had completely melted away, allowing acid to run down on the inside of the suit. The suit ripped apart in his hands, tearing along areas weakened by the acid.

His armored clothing beneath the suit steamed but did not seem to be as susceptible to the corrosive power of the acid. Elion’s left side and parts of his leg burned and stung where acid had reached his skin. Ascendency fatigue threatened, as his Ascended state fought to keep his skin from melting.

The jumpsuit shriveled on the ground in front of him, a smoking pile of slag. He rushed over to the washout station and ran the pump. Fresh water, pulled from a reservoir up on the cliff, mixed with a basic solution, rushed out of a shower head. The mixture soaked him, diluting and neutralizing the acid.

He stood in the cold flow of water, shivering as it soaked through his clothes, washing away the dangerous liquid.

Two bodies lay in puddles of acid on the path, smoking as they dissolved into mush. The third warlock was nothing more than a film of scum atop the acid pool now. Elion shuddered, watching as the arachnatronic with his sword reached the warehouse at the foot of the cliff below the refinery. How long before Venya came after him?

He shut off the water and crumpled to his knees, shivering. That had almost been a disaster. No, it had been a disaster. After nearly dying, he hadn’t retrieved his knife. He hadn’t bought them any time. All he’d done is revealed their position.
