"...Go to the left!" Goldman's voice could be heard as it spread through the forest like a wildfire. With no further due, Harry and Madeline realized something was up as they headed back using Harry's Emblem.

A large man could be seen hovering in the air as he then dove downwards with great force. The sound of trees shuffling and ground rumbling could also be heard, making the thief duo increasingly more concerned for the safety of their group.

Reaching the source of the ruckus, Goldman could be seen shielding Brun and Manes, both of which seemed surprised by the attack. On the right, with a clean cut on his cheek, Daniel faced two unknown individuals.

"Locksmith!" Noticing the Harry's appearance, the viking said. "Grab Brun and Manes and get out of here!"

The young thief then simply looked at Daniel who nodded without looking back. Shit. Why can't we have a few minutes of normalcy? Vela, before Harry jumped, grabbed his arm.

"Let me come." He said. "I lost my candelabrum, so I'll only get in the way."

"Are you sure?" Harry asked, and as soon as Vela nodded, he jumped.

Leaving Madeline, Harry and Vela were now behind Goldman. Carrying Manes, and before the kid could complain, he opened a passage, Brun and Vela following him.

Maneuvering passages between the woods was difficult. The young man never had much experience traveling through this type of terrain. Trees hindered his vision, making it difficult for him to create clear passages.

A few doors later, he stopped and put the boy on the ground, who was screaming and scratching his neck. "No! Let met out! I can fucking help!"

"No, you... can't." Harry finally addressed him, a sense of fatigue now clearly catching up to him. "It's safer for you and Brun to be here."

"Why am I with you guys if I can't help?" Manes once again complained. "Remember how I beat yours and Vela's ass back on the second floor? I can do it again!"

"You did that by catching us off guard in a specific circumstance—"

"Don't patronize me." Manes said, frowning. "You'll probably need all the help you can get."

Not the first time someone's told me that... Harry added inwardly. "Patronize? You don't even know what it means. Just..."

He was interrupted by more yelling from behind, this time from Daniel. As the conflict heated each second it passed, Harry gave Brun a look, mentally asking if she could help.

"...It is not optimal for you to be there, child." Brun said, her voice warm. "Your presence would only make them hold their punches. You'll be needed when the time comes. Isn't that enough?"

Manes scowled at the answer and ventured into the forest, away from the conflict.

Brun then tried to grab him, but failed. She then turned to Harry. "...I'll see to it. We'll be fine."

Harry simply nodded as he hang from one of his doors. Still tired from before, he feared he wouldn't be too much of a help on the upcoming battle, but he needed to be there nevertheless.

"If anything happens, come to us, okay?" Harry assured Vela. "I know you're one good fucking fighter, but... you know."

"I know, Mr. Harry." Vela said with a smile as he went after Brun and Manes.

She was never this maternal with me... Harry thought to himself as he jumped around with his doors. Twisting the directions as he gained speed not to accidentally hit a branch and lose a hand again, he could only rely on his instincts. Like a new type of sharpness that flew through the air, Harry, for a moment, felt in unison with the world. That feeling went away as quickly as it came once he reached Goldman and Daniel.

Will they be okay? The young thief asked himself. Maybe this was fight was just another distraction to capture Brun and Manes; a coward's way of assuring leverage. He was grateful, though, that Vela was there. Out of all the group, he probably trusted the monk the most, aside from Madeline.

At the scene, Madeline, Daniel, and Goldman fought against two unknown individuals. One of them was broad and wore suspenders, his beard surprisingly well-groomed. The structure of his torso, however, was curious, to say the least. As if he wore iron bars in place of his navel, an unnerving heat filled his interior. The edges of his belly dripped as if his skin were melting from the heat, making his stomach almost like the door of a furnace. Even through the suspenders, it was possible to see the crisp edges of the fire and its insatiable appetite.

Fuckin' hell... Harry remembered as the man screamed uncontrollably, both from anger and excitement. The broader man then ripped off his suspenders, revealing what lay in place of his belly.

"...Is that a furnace?" Harry asked rhetorically, approaching Madeline, who was as shocked as he was. "Damn, man. Emblems are really weird."

His face, caught between a liquid and solid state at the same time, was almost completely red while he wore a menacing smile.

Behind the man, the other individual seemed to be... massaging him? Upon closer inspection, Harry became even more confused. Using his own arms as a kind of whip, he lashed the wider man's back as he grew increasingly hot. Like a hellish cart, the "driver," with his abnormally flexible limbs, whipped the furnace-like man's back.

Noticing Harry's presence, the fake cowboy, with his almost scrawny stature, remained sincere.

Daniel, realizing he had to act fast, summoned an arrow right behind the whip-man's elbow. Lacking a better view, the most he could do was delay the cowboy, interrupting his flurry of attacks on the broad man's back. In any situation, he would have let them fight each other, but he knew this wasn't a normal situation. He was somehow generating power for the furnace man.

Launching himself upwards, Daniel forcefully pulled one of his arrows from a nearby tree, now airborne. Seeing this, in a precise strike, the cowboy extended his arms and pulled Daniel down like a frog devouring a fly. With his limbs wrapped around the man, he would have been quickly swept away if Madeline hadn't struck the man's arm with an acid ink slash, releasing Daniel into the air.

Like a jellyfish, Daniel floated in the air, not because of his arrows, but because of Goldman, who prevented him from falling hard to the ground. Daniel then gestured for Goldman to let him fall and turned to the two of them. "...The heat," the wide man chuckled as lines formed around his skin, like the patterns of a brick wall. "It's intoxicating!"

At that same moment, like an unstoppable gorilla, the uncontrolled man shot toward Daniel, who dodged to the side as soon as he saw him. Before he could collide with Daniel, however, he passed through a doorway and appeared several meters above, plummeting towards the cowboy himself.

With the subtlety of a weaving spider, the elastic man leaped aside and, using his arms and legs, embraced the furnace man while one of his legs coiled around a nearby tree. As if rehearsed, he redirected the man — who was still laughing — toward Harry and Madeline. Damn it! Resembling a slingshot and a cannonball, Harry could only imagine that the pair had certainly had plenty of time to practice their moves together.

Ready to instinctively use one of his doors, a tiny part of Harry thought it wouldn't be the wisest of choices. The duo's dynamic clearly involved enraging and energizing the furnace man while the cowboy used him as a force of destruction. Redirecting the furnace man's attacks wouldn't be helpful since they could easily work around his tricks. On the contrary, by using his doors in this way, he would give even more kinetic energy and opportunities to the group.

His line of thought resumed in a sharp Fuck! as he still created a passage upwards to force the ball out of their way. Thankfully, though, Goldman was a quick thinker. As if the wind itself had done it, the furnace man was diverted to the right of Madeline, who ducked as soon as the man came to their direction.

Crashing and tumbling into a few trees, the man was steaming as he got up, a black substance rotting inside his belly. Is this charcoal? Is he really a fucking furnace? Harry thought about the nature of his emblem as he then suddenly dropped to his knees, hands on his chest.

"Harry!" Madeline came to his aid. "Did he hit you? Are you okay?"

Not feeling a sharp pain like that since his encounter with the Shepherd, he panted with wide eyes, not sure he could talk and breath at the same time. I... I need to keep going.

"My..." He got up, his legs trembling. "I'm... I'm fine. I'm just a little exhausted."

"There is no such thing as 'little exhausted'," Madeline said, helping him. "You're either exhausted or you're not. Shit... You need rest, Harry. You'll die like this."

"Job duties, right?" Harry replied with a weak laugh followed by a violent cough.

Breaking their momentum, Harry and Madeline felt a tug as they were telekinetically pulled by Goldman, barely avoiding a whip attack from the scrubby guy.

Growling and turning to him, Daniel then did the same movement as before. Pulling an arrow and hitting a tree, he propelled himself towards the cowboy. A second before the guy could react, he summoned an arrow behind a smaller tree, closer to its base. As a result, he avoided the whip attack from the man and landed a solid punch on him. Rolling on the dirt, Daniel got up before him.

As the viking rushed at him, Gale made a snap movement with his fingers right on Daniel's knees, making him fall. In sequence, his fingers bent to supernatural proportions as they each elongated to form a whip-like attack on Daniel. Between Cracks! and Snaps!, a barrage of attacks swarmed Daniel who was now on the ground, shielding himself the best he could.

Looking to quickly finish the fight, Gale turned so fast it seemed his body twisted. With that, he prepared a leg attack aimed at Daniel. Just before he could strike, his leg was grabbed by Daniel, who still kept his head down. The brutal man then tightened his grip on Gale's leg, who suddenly felt a sharp pain like never before. He hadn't broken a bone, since his leg was still in whiplash mode, but he certainly still felt pain.

What scared him most was the strength of his grip. Did he lower his guard on purpose? Is that why his punch was so weak when he fell? Gale frantically searched for a reason, but his mind burned as the pain overwhelmed his body.

Daniel then twisted once more and pulled Gale's leg like a rope, bringing the man's body forward. With a precise blow, Daniel struck him squarely in the torso, taking his breath away and instantly reverting his body to its solid form. He then fell to the ground, groaning and rolling onto his side, but quickly getting up.

Like a carved animal, Daniel's face bore numerous cuts and linear patterns that screamed for attention. He ran his hand over his cheek and felt a thick drop of blood. At that moment, he licked it and grinned at the scrubby man, who now had a horrified look on his face.

"You should've stayed with the other one," Daniel said. "Because I'm not your fucking horse."
