In a world where the sky stayed overcast ninety-eight percent of the year, a creature of bone and shadow rushed through a cave carved into the wall of a vast caldera and out into the open.

For several kilometres into the distance, the volcanic basin was filled nearly end to end with spirits, ghouls, shadowkin, and, most of all, the umbros.

On a large dais stood a creature cloaked in shadow, their form blurring at the edges as though pieces of them were glitching in and out of reality. The being stood upon the threshold between Tier 6 and Tier 7, their presence so strong and overwhelming that only a fragment of them could appear before their people.

"A new world," they said. "Tell us, Aspirant, of what you experienced."

All eyes turned towards the edge of the caldera.

The Aspirant shrank slightly beneath the attention.

"To hide in the shadows is our way, but that does not mean we shy from the light." The Monarch's form shifted upon the dais. "For the brighter the light..."

"...the darker the shadow."

All voices, even those transmitted spiritually or telepathically, rang out through the caldera in a single sonorous unity.

Something within the Aspirant shattered. They felt their level rise, and with it came a boost for one of their titles.

[Shadow Communion – Tier 1] → [Shadow Communion – Tier 2]

Overwhelming happiness surged through them, pushing back some of the lingering trepidation. Gathering their courage, they turned to address the people.

"A new world has been integrated. In only a few cycles, it will be joining the greater System Network," they began, their voice a sibilant hiss. "As a mana-less base-world, the System’s blessings land heavily upon them.

“Their recourses are plentiful and unique. These newly Integrated peoples are shepherded by the System. Rewards we might see only once in years, flow from them like the early levels of our Tier 0 Initiates.

“They have no foundation, no teachers or paths to follow, and yet, by treading their own, they have already produced a Tier 1."

There was a murmur in the crowd. The response was interested but not overawed. Still, the Aspirant pressed on, remembering the glint in the Red Star's eyes as she struck a deal to end a threat. As she spoke to the right hand of their venerable leader with neither fear nor arrogance. As she negotiated for mutual aid, and alliances.

The Aspirant remembered the chill that nearly froze their spirit within their vessel as she agreed to take the Orb of Transformation with her; an artefact presented by the Hand of the Monarch through a complicated and expensive teleportation procedure.

It was a Legacy artifact. One of the few that could change a compatible person's race into an umbros.

"My fellow peoples. Beyond its treasure, this world contains something even more precious." They held up a data slate and created a projection high above for everyone to see.

On the slate was a list that had been stagnant for over a century, the obfuscated symbol of a new world had appeared. Most of the list was filled with worlds whose numbers flickered from zero to one and quickly back to zero. However, the very newest entry showed something different.

Earth – 3

The reaction was immediate.

"Three," the Aspirant said.

Whispers moved through the caldera.

"Three Spirus Blades have been given as organic System rewards. Three compatible souls have been found and have accepted our legacy. Three new shadows are born into a world filled with light, and the brightest of them seeks its partner."

"The spirit of our people spreads..." called the Monarch of Shadows.

"... so too do our shadows." came the response.

"In unity we are complete," the Monarch concluded the declaration. "Heed the words of the Aspirant. Very soon this new world will welcome the first full umbros to be born outside of Faction control in over a century. The orb will lead their Light towards them!

Our people are not forgotten by the great System. By faith and through patience we have found a path forward." The Monarch turned their attention back to the Aspirant. "Tell us, Aspirant. You have made contact. Tell us of that path, tell us of the Light."

"Aid for aid." The Aspirant bowed low before the immensity of the fragment of a being kept on the cusp of true divinity by the machinations of the Factions.

A Shadow could fight alone. A Shadow could live alone. A Shadow could grow alone.

The brighter the light, the darker the shadow. It was the oldest truth of their people. To reach their full potential, a Shadow needed to partner with a Light, and the two rose together.

However, that which could carry one high, could also become an anchor, as it had for the Monarch.

All the Monarch needed was one step, one more level to become a Contender. A big enough threat to the Factions that they could not continue the suppression and enslavement of their people. To prevent this, the Factions had captured their Monarch's Light.

If the Light died, the Shadow would simply be free to seek another. So, the Factions had taken their Light and stagnated them.

The umbros as a people were too useful to simply kill off, and too difficult to fully subjugate. So, for generations they had been curated, managed, kept on the blades edge of true freedom and power.

The strongest would have their Lights crippled, the rest were prevented from ever forming a partnership.

Even now, if Earth were to be claimed, all Earthborn umbros would be captured or culled. However, if the people of Earth were able to do the impossible...

“This new world is not merely a source of resources," proclaimed the Monarch. "It is the possibility of a future for us all."

The entire caldera went silent.

Then whispers spread like smoke, the voices drifting like ash in the wind, scattering softly to every part of the basin.

"Silence." The word itself was spoken gently, but the pressure the Monarch produced was not.

"You speak of allyship. But this world is painfully new. What can this Red Luminary offer?"

It wasn't an admonishment. The Monarch already knew. They’d authorised the deal with the Red Luminary.

Instead, the Monarch was giving the Aspirant an opportunity to speak with the people. To secure the support of a world of almost unimaginable potential in the fight for their freedom.

Perhaps the Monarch had seen the potential in them?

The Aspirant lifted their head along with the data slate.

Earth – 3

"In fewer than seven days," they intoned. "Days of only twenty-four hours. They entered the System without mana, without knowledge, without the backing of a single established Faction." Their gaze swept across the caldera. The glowing numbers were projected high enough for everyone to see. "The potential of their world speaks for itself. "

This time, when silence fell, the Aspirant did not need to borrow courage from the ancient words before once more speaking up.

"Should we aid them in what they seek?" the Aspirant asked. "Should we aid them in their bid for freedom? Should we bind our causes together?" Their voice grew stronger. "What do we risk? That things remain as they are?"

Another stat rose. Their speech became smoother, their voice strengthening alongside their conviction.

“Do we wish to live forever at the mercy of those who decide how brightly our Lights are permitted to burn?”

“No!”

The answering voices shook the caldera.

“Do we wish to remain beneath the heels of those hollow golds, whose only light comes as a reflection of their borrowed strength?”

“No!”

“We shall not sit idly by as another world falls to the machinations of Factions. The Red Luminary has offered us something greater: a world where together, we might be free. They will burn bright in defence of that freedom, and should they fall, they will burn their world with them.

“The blaze will be luminous. And in its glow, we will rise.

“For the brighter the light—”

“—the deeper the shadow!”

The caldera roared.

Shadows surged upwards, blanketing the grey sky in darkness. From somewhere within that darkness, piercingly bright lights shone down, and beneath each one the shadows only deepened.

Then the Monarch raised a hand and silence returned.

"A pact has already been made with the Red Luminary. The new world’s strongest. I have seen them through proxy. I have read their intentions. Their future is obscured from the diviners. Their status wavers and fractures, fragments and flows." The Monarch's shadow spread across the dais.

"They do not yet know the chains that await them, but we do. We who have been bound for so long." The shadows erupted.

"Should their world fall, it will take with it our greatest chance for freedom in centuries. Their plight is not impossible. It has been done before. It will be done again. Should they prove themselves worthy of our aid, their chances grow brighter.”

The artifact in the Aspirant's hands flickered.

The Aspirant froze.

Across the caldera, attention snapped back towards it.

3

The number split.

1 – 2

The number ‘2’ remained written in white. The ‘1’ turned gold. For a moment, nobody seemed to understand. Then new script appeared beneath it.

[REDACTED has gained its first umbros.]

For the third time, the caldera went completely, painfully silent. A spirus blade had found a wielder, and that wielder had been reborn. They were now one of them; only, whole in a way so many umbros could only dream of.

On a world less than seven days old. A world whose people had never touched mana. A world with billions of potential Lights. A Shadow had been born and chosen.

The booming laughter of the Monarch of Shadows erupted across the caldera, echoed beyond the crater, and rolled like thunder across the world.

"And so, it begins."
