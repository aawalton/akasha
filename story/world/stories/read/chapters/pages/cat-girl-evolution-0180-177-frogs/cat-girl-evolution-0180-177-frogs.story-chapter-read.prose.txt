“Because the ever-gracious and beautiful Dryadil won’t let us face consequences for minor offenses committed in the heat of much-warranted passion. This very pretty place wouldn’t let us be punished for that.”

“Yona, what the fuck are you talking about?”

“I don’t understand.”

“That’s true, but we don’t need to sell frogs to get rich doing this. Zorg is dead, remember? His castle is abandoned. The ruins are empty. Everyone cleared out when the demon came out. But how long do you think it’ll stay that way? Jane’s assistant might have grabbed the Soul Weapon, but what about all the other treasure that Zorg had?”

“Shouldn’t he have had that on his person?” asks Lauren. “There’s no way a Greed monster doesn’t have a spatial storage.”

I open my mouth, then close it. That’s a great question.

Abby, if he had Inventory, what would have happened to all that stuff in it when he died?

“It should have been spit out.”

I don’t remember seeing anything…

“A few small things came out, but you’re right. Nothing big enough to be a fortune.”

I didn’t notice that.

“You were pretty distracted, and you don’t have my processing power.”

Where is all that stuff now?

“Some of it landed on the corpse, so you have it. The rest sank into the swamp where he died.”

Okay. Hmmmm…

I check his corpse, still sitting in my Stomach. Aside from the body, it’s mostly worthless. There’s his clothes, then a few daggers and knives and things. They’re pretty nice, I guess, but nothing too special. There’s also a Merchant’s Guild badge… I wonder how he managed to get that.

“Can we do anything with Zorg’s Merchant’s Guild badge?”

“If he had anything there, it will stay with the Guild for either twenty years, or until someone with a valid claim takes it,” says B. “Otherwise, it’s theirs.”

“Right… Well, Abby says he dropped some stuff back at the frog enclosure, so we can look around while we gather frogs.”

We arrive at the first frog enclosure an hour after sunset. The first thing we do is make for the area where Zorg got killed. The swamp is pretty destroyed in this area from the battle between me, Mac, and Altair, but it’s nothing compared to the aftermath of the Metzli battle, so it doesn’t take long. There, embedded in the mud at the bottom of the swamp are a few miscellaneous things. There are a few bits of flesh from when Mac obliterated his head, including a couple relatively undamaged teeth, a pouch of about a hundred gold, and a simple iron key. No spatial storages, though, and nothing extremely valuable, except maybe the key.

“I think we need to go back to the castle,” I say, turning the key over in my hand. “I think he has a vault or something.”

I show the others the key, and while Chad and Lauren just nod, A and B’s eyes widen.

We discussed the plan on the way here, and after a very brief huddle, we all split up to fulfill our roles. With no pressure of guards, we’re all able to participate, which makes things easier, but without the box, it’s also more complicated. The box made it so I could just Snatch them and throw them in. Without it, we need to kill them to store them, which is hard, because they’re Tier 4 monsters, meaning they’re pretty durable, but they’re also small, so if we do too much damage, the meat is ruined.

So, the current order of operations is for Lauren and Chad to smoke them out, locating the extradimensional burrows and scaring them, while I Snatch them, B traps them, and A kills them. I’m not sure exactly how she’s doing it, but he shoots a wave of mana that doesn’t damage most of their body while melting their brains. It looks painless from the outside, but watching it happen with Food Sense is pretty gnarly.

We spend hours doing this, starting in the first enclosure, then moving on to the other one. The number of frogs in my inventory soon numbers in the hundreds, reaching as high as 700 before we finally get our first guests. It’s another Party of Adventurers, and they had the same idea as us.

They appear above our heads, flying in on a wooden sailboat, and when they see us, they stop and stare. We stare back for a few seconds, then they fly off to the other side of the swamp to start harvesting. We redouble our efforts, but soon a third Party shows up, then a fourth, and only an hour after midnight, we’ve basically cleared out every frog in both enclosures. Our final total is 850, which is a very good number. I am annoyed that the other Adventurers showed up so we couldn’t get a full thousand, but it’s whatever. Once I figure out how to get a steady supply of essence for my Dungeon, I can have infinite frogs, and 850 should be enough to tide me over for a while, even if we end up selling some of them.

After that, we’re gonna take our money and do a shopping splurge, and then finally we’ll set out for Ocula. And somewhere in there, I’ll have to talk to Wesley, and also get vivitecture lessons from Jane and upgrade my Class.
