Don couldn’t move a muscle. The System was blaring at him. The good news was that he didn’t have seven seconds to live. He had twenty two.

The bad news was he literally couldn’t move a muscle. Don was as good as paralyzed. No matter how much willpower he mustered.

This is how it ends…?

No. Don wouldn’t let his life flash in his eyes. No quiet goodbye. He would fucking fight.

In a flash of genius, he used his mind.

Fifteen seconds.

With Telekinesis, he slid his body on the floor towards the body of the Grimoid king. He couldn’t quite levitate himself, but it was close. His arm got dragged through a splinter. It burst into bleeding like a ripe tomato. Hell, if he cared. Looked nasty as hell, but Don couldn’t feel it.

With Telekinesis, he turned his head, then slammed it close enough to the king’s massive shoulder to kiss it.

Don couldn’t bite down. But he used Devour anyway.

First nothing happened.

Five seconds.

Don used Telekinesis. His psychic energy flailed around. It wasn’t so much a plan, as it was the only thing he was able to do. Then he felt it. The Devour ability was in a way within his Psychic sense. In some strange alchemy Don combined Telekinesis with Devour.

[Hidden skill upgrade unlocked: Devour]

[Hidden Skill Upgrade: You have combined two Racial Skills: Devour and Telekinesis!]

[Ability slot unlocked!]

[Racial Ability Acquired: Life Drain!]

[Life Drain Level 1]

Don didn’t know what the hell that was, but he used it.

He felt a tug with his psychic sense when the life essence of Homass started streaming towards Don.

It was like being pulled above water just a moment before drowning.

The vitality poured in.

The relief was insane.

Don was still barely lucid. But somewhere in the haze, in the back of his mind, an image of Mal’asha licking her lips flashed. He heard a faint sultry whisper.

[Magnificent…]

Creepy…

Don ignored it for now. He just drained in the vitality from Homass.

It wasn’t a rush of energy in a one and done move. It was a steady trickle that required his psychic attention.

Don drank in greedily. The energy was potent and delicious. Don’s broken body started knitting back together. The pain was receding, but his nerves were still raw. He still couldn’t move, but that had more to do with the paralysis than the damage on his body.

Even when his Health eventually ticked back to full, he still felt like there was a massive lead blanket on him, immobilizing him.

Since Don had nothing else to do he looked around the carnage he had caused and thought for a minute.

It was a good fight. The king… He fought well.

Don recalled the moment the king had gotten up. Don may have won, but that was a lesson he would take from his fallen foe.

The king had struggled up and faced death on his feet like a warrior. Don could respect that.

He had strong resolve, he knew it. He had proven it. But he still had much to learn. This was a good lesson.

The berserk ability had been amazing. But it had awakened something primal, violent and dark inside Don. Something that was now dormant, but its shadow lingered in the recesses of Don’s mind.

He had been like a violent beast. Sure, he had indulged in a little bloodlust before, but this had been different. Dark and dangerous.

Is this who I am? Or was it just an effect of the ability?

Don wasn’t sure. He had been lucid the whole time and he had a clear memory. He couldn’t deny it, the berserk had felt amazing. But it was definitely not something he would seek out again, unless he absolutely had to.

For one, he was still completely immobilized and would be for a while still. That was a risk. If he hadn’t finished off Homass when he did, this might have gone the other way. Any low level beast could come in and kill him right now, as he was completely helpless.

Nothing I can do about that. If I die here, I die.

The fight had been intense. Without Ursine Vitality, it would have been a one-sided stomp. Don had taken a foolish risk, but somehow it had managed to work out. He would be more careful in the future. But he was very happy with his first boss kill.

He had even gotten a title. Soloing a boss. He wondered how many other people had discovered dungeons. These clearly weren’t designed to be fought alone, despite the System adjusting them. Don doubted Jacques could have taken this fight.

That meant Don was likely ahead in their little competition. But truthfully he had no idea how Jacques was doing.

Jacques slid another arrow from his quiver. He leveled the bow and loosed. The release struck him every time like a hit from a cigarette. He missed those deadly little things, but this was better.

The arrow pierced the chest of the [Warrior] charging him. He collapsed. Jacques scoffed.

Killed… By a single arrow. Such weakness.

But another one of the raiders attacked. They usually moved in groups of four or five. Their leader threw a spear at him, but Jacques dodged with disdainful ease.

“[Double Shot],” he called and the System answered.

His next arrow split into two and struck both the leader and another raider approaching him. At least the leader didn’t fall.

He snapped the arrow in his chest and drank a healing potion. But the potion was a starter version. While the arrow was pushed out, the wound did not fully close. It still bled.

Jacques could sense it.

“Good,” Jacques called. “You are not completely hopeless.”

The raider looked around and grimaced when he saw all his comrades on the ground, one or two arrows sticking from them.

Jacques shot another arrow. The raider blocked it with a shield, discarded it and jumped to pick another before slipping behind a tree.

“You the one that keeps hunting us?”

“Yes…” Jacques said. “Although only so few of you give me proper sport.”

“Why the fuck are you focusing on us? Plenty other people to harass.”

Jacques looked at the tutorial window and flicked it away as he readied another arrow.

1852/3000

Over a third had already died. It had only been a few days.

“You are convenient prey,” Jacques said lazily and loosed an arrow at the tree. The man behind it flinched. “Amoral, so my friend will not be vexed.”

“Look, I didn’t do nothing bad to anyone.”

“I do not really care,” Jacques said. “Question is, will you give me sport before you die, or are you just cattle?”

“You sick fuck,” the raider said. “Boss will kill you.”

Jacques only smiled. The raider was level 10 already. That was good. He gave more experience. Some kind of a shit class, though. [Spearmaster]. Hardly a master. But he had managed to entertain Jacques for at least a little bit.

“Would you rather die like a dog cowering behind a tree,” Jacques said as he nocked another arrow. “Or like a man?”

The man did the obvious. He ran, as the cattle did when a predator appeared. Jacques scoffed.

He loosed an arrow. It flew at the tree, but swerved in the air. Jacques used his [Blood Mark] ability. Nifty little trick he had acquired from a level up to fifteen. It allowed him to mark a target he had bloodied and his arrows would have a homing property. They could even weave between objects if Jacques focused, like he did now.

Soon he heard a satisfying cry of pain. Almost giddily he jogged and caught up with the raider, who was clutching his thigh.

“You chose to be cattle,” Jacques said and shook his head. “You chose to be cattle. You are not suited for the System. A weak coward.”

“Wait—”

Jacques didn’t wait. He never did. Listening to the pleas had been amusing at first, but it was always the same song. They would offer something they didn’t have. Make promises they couldn’t keep, ask for mercy and other nonsense. It was disgusting. These cattle… they had no dignity.

“Ah… But there is one that does have dignity. I wonder, Don Damon. What is it that you are doing currently?”

Jacques knew he was ahead in levels. The matron goddess had told him so. But she had also told him that Don was much stronger than him. How could it be if they were the same level? Was the gift of the goddess truly so strong?

Jacques had attempted to kill Blazeclaw when he had hit level 15. There had been no chance. This thing, this boss guarding the dispenser unit had been extremely strong. Not even with all of the upgraded gear he had bought from the lesser dispenser had helped, nor his [Blood Mark] ability.

It made no sense. The bear boss was level 15. Jacques had killed a lesser boss before. The one guarding the lesser dispenser. But apparently Blazeclaw was a true predator.

Jacques would try again at level 20. That could take a while. The levels were starting to slow down. But surely he would get there before Don Damon.

The matron refused to tell him where Don Damon had vanished. Jacques could not track him. But apparently he was alive, and getting stronger very fast.

“You will be ze dessert, Don Damon,” Jacques said as he licked his lips. “The grand finale of this tutorial. We shall feast upon the prowess of each other.”
