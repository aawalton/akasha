“Luma, you ready?” I ask the fox standing on the cliff next to me. It has been only a few hours since she unlocked her new combat form. But both of us were really excited to give it a try, so we immediately went out in search of a monster for live experimentation–there is no better way to figure things out, after all.

“Yip!” She affirms and I start to feed some spirit into her. Four hundred lasted about five minutes last time, so I give two thousand. That should be enough for the foe in front of us. With a popping sound, she assumes her battle form, which is still really fucking terrifying.

I jump down from the cliff and start running into the canyon towards our next target. An [Iridescent Basilisk - Level 64] that is currently curled up around some sort of nest. It’s a bit higher level than what I’d normally take on, but I figured that it would be the best way to test out what Luma can do. Plus, I do want to level up a whole bunch, and the best way to do that is to be risky.

Luma and I race across the open space with a glee that can only be found by running alongside a comrade. Once we are within about three hundred meters, one of the monster’s eyes opens up. They are iridescent, just like its scales. Something tells me they could be worth quite a lot at a market.

“<Light Ray>” I incant, shooting a beam of light right into the monster’s eyes. It thrashes about and quickly unwinds all several dozen meters of its body. The basilisk lets out a roar of challenge, and it is one that Luma and I gladly accept.

I Plane Step right next to the end of the basilisk's tail and smash it with a quick Anvil Strike. Meanwhile, Luma uses the opportunity to jump up onto its back. Her claws cut through its scales like they are paper as she climbs up near its head. Oh, and she looks really damn cool as she does it.

Her first use of her fangs is to dig them deep into its skull. So deeply that I can hear the cracking of bone even from this distance. Luma doesn’t let up though, and she just ruthlessly mauls the back of the basilisk's head.

The monster tries to shake her off and bite her, but with me keeping its tail busy, it can’t get a good angle in. It quickly gives up, realizing the futility. Then its scales start to shine all sorts of colors like a really pretty rainbow. It isn’t until about a second later that I start to feel the mana building up inside of them.

Luma, jump off! I shout through Spirit Link as I Plane Step a hundred meters away. Luma gets my message and jumps off just in time, as mere instants later, the world goes white. It is like each of the basilisk scales turned into a bomb, and pretty damn powerful ones at that. I dig Luxmore into the ground to avoid being blown back, but it isn’t enough and the shockwave sends me flying.

I stick the landing, and glance over to see Luma seems to have survived too. Yip! She says before racing back towards the basilisk. I Plane Step next to her and join in as we run towards the monster yet again.

It seems to have shrunk, now only about half the previous size. Also it lacks scales, that attack must’ve scarified all of them. Which is just perfect, fleshy bits are a lot more susceptible to claws, and hammers.

I duck under its mouth as it tries to swallow me whole. Then I carry my momentum into an upwards Anvil Strike. A bit unconventional, maybe, but the principle is just the same.

The basilisk goes flying into the air from my attack, which gives Luma the perfect shot with her Light Cannon. The beam of pure light rips through the basalisks’ skin, cooking it from the outside in. But the damn thing just doesn’t want to die for some reason.

Even when it should be dead, it still struggles to move. I get in close to finish it off, and by some unknown resolve, it manages a last second burst of speed. It is too fast for me to react, and it ends up biting down on my arm. I grind my teeth through the pain, and kick it really hard a few times until its health finally hits zero and it dies.

‘Ding’, You have slain [Iridescent Basilisk - Level 64].

‘Ding’, Magic Smith has advanced to level 56. Stat points distributed.

‘Ding’, Luma has advanced to level 53. Stat points distributed.

‘Ding’, Luma has advanced to level 54. Stat points distributed.

Three levels for a single kill? That is quite good, all things considered. The basilisk wasn’t nearly as dangerous as I thought it would be. It was like it didn’t want to move around to dodge our attacks. Almost like it was anchored to its nest and refused to stray too far.

Yip! Luma says, drawing my attention over to said nest where she is. I jog over and when I peek over the edge, my jaw drops just a bit. For sitting right in the center is an egg. A pretty damn big one too. I didn’t even know monsters could lay eggs, perhaps it laid it before it got corrupted with malice? It all makes sense now, it was trying to defend the egg, despite being a monster. Anyways, I inspect the egg.

Egg of the Iridescent Basilisk (Epic)

Within this egg dwells the potential to create an iridescent basilisk. However, it would seem that something has gone wrong in its development. Malice has corrupted the egg, and it will not hatch until it is purified. Such a purification is beyond the hope of any but a true master of beasts.

Hmm, something tells me I am not going to be classified as a true master of beasts. Nor do I ever hope to go down such a path. Imagine if I had both beasts and spirits, it would get crazy real damn fast. For now, I just put the egg into my storage, it should stay nice and safe there until I can find a good home for it.

“Yip.” Luma says from beside me, just moments ago she transformed back into her cuteness form. As for her combat power… it isn’t the best thing in the world. She is quite agile, as one would expect. Yet her damage isn’t that high unless she uses her light cannon. Then again, she is supposed to be a support class like me. So it does make sense, even if I have basically been ignoring that idea due to me being alone.

Her extra attack power is definitely going to come in quite handy. Plus if the whole calamity thing is any indication, she is only going to grow more and more powerful.

Or in other words, "Who's a good fox?” I say and pick her up into my arms. I reach behind her ears and give her so many scritches. After all, she is a really really good fox. Her healing aura has even helped to fix up my arm already. Luckily this type of basilisk had no venom, otherwise I imagine I would not be doing nearly as well.

After quite a long break involving many scritches, I get to work harvesting the basilisk. All of the scales were destroyed, but the eyes are ripe for the harvest. I quickly pocket those and also grab one of its fangs that is still in pristine condition. It is odd, I have noticed that monsters often only have a few harvestable parts, no matter how careful I am.

If I had to warrant a guess, some magic bullshittery is afoot. Perhaps all of the magical energies choose a few parts of the monster to coalesce into and make those into proper items? That is just a theory though, and it would seem my theories about this new world tend to go unanswered. Which is fine, as that means my theories are also never proven wrong. Meaning I am technically a genius with a ridiculously good ability to theorize things.

I like that, Orn The Genius Theorizer. However, this world doesn’t give a shit about theories. It wants hard stuff, actual power that can be wielded. So it is about time I unlock what is likely the greatest untapped well of said power, magic. Specifically the sort of magical understanding that Valdir was talking about. I have put it off for far too long, and this basilisk nest is probably quite safe. Monsters and beasts out here likely won’t notice its dead for quite a while, making it a great place to safely meditate.

Oh, and there is also the fact that it is really hard to find monsters to slay without Dovey’s scouting. The poor fella is still recovering, which gives me the perfect excuse to sit on my ass and think for a while.
