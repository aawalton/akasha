Back on Argand, the first thing Beth did was circle up with her team, gathering them and informing them of what had happened. With the now not-so-mysterious off world backer sorted out, the rebellion on Argand had nothing and no one. Beth’s team was way more than enough to mop up and sweep up, and her team had already done much of that before she even finished dealing with her fight with the so lamely named Ascended, Mark. The results of her infiltration made the cleanup both easier and harder, though it was all working out better than she had first hoped. It was harder because they had to deal with disentangling company assets without causing more damage, as Beth didn’t want to keep sparing with Prime Explorers or whatnot, but it was much easier in that they had the information to disassemble the mess with little challenge. Nobody that wasn’t a company asset was of much skill or strength, either which made the whole process far easier.

It was probably one of the easiest Platinum missions they had done or would ever do, all things considered. Beth had spent most of the time they were working pretending to be a repairwoman and teleporting around to various buildings to investigate the enemy. At the end of the day, she hadn’t really done much fighting, even after she came back and updated her group on the status of their mission and what she had found. They had wrapped the entire rebellion up within four days, and that was total time that included their arrival and Beth’s investigations, as well as the hour it took her to write a quick after action for Arianna over on Teolos III. Wrapping up a rebellion in less than ninety-six hours was pretty impressive, though Beth did make sure to stop by the offices of Argand’s planetary governor and lay down the law. That didn’t go over so well at first, until she ‘accidentally’ burned away a small paper that had an official notice on using her Ideal, which the governor was experienced enough to recognize for what it was, and then the conversation suddenly went so much better.

That mission wrapped up their first batch of missions in going for Platinum, and it was exactly as they had been told and expected; Platinum missions were rare and highly sought after. It would likely be a couple months before the next mission cropped up, now that they had cleared the five missions that had either been in the backlog on just been created on Arbitus. There was always a chance they could shop around and find a mission in a random Hall, but that chance was rather slim, considering Platinum missions were much higher in priority than Gold missions; they often got passed up the chain immediately. Even if Arbitus wasn’t immediately aware of a mission, there were always eager Gold and Platinum Emblems ready to take a mission, up to and including Hall Masters themselves, so Platinum missions were far more of a needle in a haystack type of situation than even Gold missions tended to be. Their network of connections with Hall Masters didn’t help much, either, considering that even Hall Masters would be interested in doing Platinum missions.

That was a perfect break for them, however, as they were basically ready to undergo their next rebirths at this point. The requirements were skewing more into combat every rebirth, though they still had rare and valuable items to find for their rebirths at this point, but those things could be sourced given time. They had all their materials, and Beth’s gate made much of the combat work trivial, at least up to this point, though Beth knew it would get a lot harder as they kept going. Still, she didn’t see any reason at this point to stop doing the True Rebirths, and the rest of the team certainly agreed. They had to take a couple weeks, objective time, to finish out the last of their requirements, but they’d managed to get the rest of the materials over the last few months, much of that sourced from the trading firms, but some of it gathered by themselves. It was another thing the members of the team not concerned with getting an Emblem any higher than Gold could do in their downtime, though sometimes the best way was to use Beth’s gate and they might not be together with the rest of the team.

They would use the gate now, but not for farming, just for undergoing the rebirths, with the whole doing it in the private residence area she could set the gate to. It was still the most convenient way they had of doing it, with the medallion creating a portal that was rather large and very obvious in terms of mana signature; her gate created a much smaller portal with a very faint mana signature, by comparison, and was good for anything they wanted to do in private. This rebirth was something private for them, not that it was embarrassing, or all that secret, but it was something they wanted to keep from certain prying eyes. That and the fact that it could cause a big disturbance in the mana and surrounding area if they weren’t in the gate was all the excuse they needed to disappear for an hour and get it done, with the rebirths going very smoothly overall. Nobody discussed their class selections ever, if they got them, which applied to Adam, Andrea, Bjorn, and Val. Kris, Neph, Blood, and Sera didn’t get classes as sapient beasts and instead had their humanization and species strength to rely on.

This, the fifteenth rebirth, put them halfway through Enlightened, approaching Ascended at, while not a record pace, an extremely rapid pace that would see them there in just a a handful of objective years. Well, a handful might be a bit of an exception, but they were going to get there much sooner rather than later, and the whole team was still in lockstep at this point, meaning they would have a full group of Ascended, plus or minus whatever Veren was up to. Still, Beth wasn’t at all worried about him; the very worst case scenario would be that he would still be in Enlightened and a Sage whenever they saw him next, which was more than enough to make him a terror in his own right. Speaking of Sages and the like, it was also a good chance for them to work on their weapon skills. If anything, that was a bit of a well-known bluff that many people participated in, with many wanting to hide their current skill or hide their path through the ranks and thus hiring instructors at certain points.
