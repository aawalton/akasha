The bath was bliss.

One moment I was standing uncertainly at its threshold, eyeing the water longingly but wary of the figure behind me.

Then Velidan pointed out more soaps, oils, and scrubbing stones than I’d ever seen in my life, handed me a towel that felt like a cloud, and disappeared.

I waited a bit, clutching the cloth to my chest and listening for any sound beyond the door. All I heard was the beating of my own heart. There’s nothing to worry about. Well. Not right now. In general, yes, but… as for right now…

I bolted the door and, for peace of mind rather than any real feeling of need, lay the witchwood knife just beside the water’s edge. I draped the towel overtop, sucked in a breath, and undressed.

Then I melted beneath a tide of peace. Every weary muscle unwound within the water. Soothing heat kissed every inch of my skin- including a hip I’d been prepared to nurse, but now bore no bruise and simply earned a long, puzzled look.

Petals floated all around, sending up the heady smells of roses, jasmine, and lavender. Silvery clouds and pale gray light from the windows made the water shimmer around me. Lush colors swayed beyond the glass as I scrubbed rough stone and fragrant suds along my skin until I felt soft and polished smooth. I wrung my hair with slick soap until it pooled through my fingers like silk. Then I simply turned on my back and floated in a sapphire ocean, fingers tracing trails through heat.

I could have stayed forever.

“Wow, you are pink!”

The room erupted in shrieks and splashes. “Slip!” I floundered to cover myself, gaping at the hunched green figure. “What are you doing?!”

The goblin just grinned. She stood near my things and seemed to be holding my pants. “Lord Vel said there’s something I’m s’posed to get from your pocket. A little silver thing.”

“H-he said what?” I looked around helplessly. “You’re… stealing my mirror?”

She looked offended. “Of course not! He said you might see some bad stuff and need something to cheer you up. Oh, here. This thing, right?” The glass shone like a little moon in her hand.

Water lapped at my shoulders. The very air seemed dense with steam and confusion. “Yes, but if I might need it later, why are you taking it?”

“I’m not.” Slip set the mirror down, then shot me a wink. “I’m taking your clothes.”

“What?!”

She vanished.

Fear held me frozen. Wide eyes turned to the door. Blood roared in my ears as instinct screamed for me to dive for the knife, to… to…

Stop. Breathe. I’m being silly. Saints, I’m panicking over… she’s probably just going to wash them.

I glided to the dagger, and a quick peek showed that it remained hidden. Another glance showed Slip had indeed taken the rest of my things and had left a pair of slippers behind, along with…

My jaw dropped. Familiar bundles of fabric from the pleasure house lay strewn about the tile. Wary uncertainty turned to stiffened shoulders and clenched teeth as I pieced a few things together; the way Slip had snickered when asking Velidan about color preferences and his own smirk as he’d said I’d need to wear ‘something’ today.

Water spattered on tile as I climbed out, wrapped myself in a thick cloud, and examined my ‘somethings.’ My heart sank in equal measures of dread, revulsion, and horror.

The first option was less an article of clothing and more an elaborate contraption formed by slick leather and spiked hooks. I tried to puzzle out how one would even put such a garment on, found no answers, and kicked it aside.

My second choice was a generous length of scarlet velvet. I drew it up with a relieved sigh that quickly became a moan. Slits split the garment from hem to hip, and its bodice was more a suggestion than anything else.

The third was, much like the first, very elaborate, though far more beautiful. Shimmering black chiffon cascaded from silver bands about the wrists and throat. A delicate chain rounded the middle, cinching swathes of fabric and letting them float unevenly below. It was breathtaking and I almost wanted to wear it, but the bands looked too much like shackles.

My fourth dress was formed of azure lace and glittering beads. Both wound in snakelike patterns, turning everything that should have been private into a sensual display. I glared at the garment.

My gaze trailed to the last-

“Your breakfast is ready.”

I nearly jumped out of my skin. Shaking fingers drew my towel tight as I looked to the closed door. “This isn’t funny.”

Silence loomed beyond the threshold. Then, in a tone clearly quavering with laughter, “You’ll have to be more specific.”

“I’m not…” I nearly choked on angry, embarrassed heat. “I’m not wearing any of this.”

“You’d rather go naked?”

I swiped my eyes. “Give me my clothes back!”

“They’re filthy.”

“I’m in a giant tub! I’m surrounded by soap! I’ll wash them!”

“They’re not suited to the occasion.”

“I’m not coming out until-”

“Then I’ll come in.” He sounded far too pleased.

My knuckles twisted and whitened around the towel. “I l-locked the door. I-”

“I’m very strong, Brin. Locks are suggestions more than anything else.” The still-quavering tone fell to silence as my heart slammed a maddening rhythm. When he spoke again, mirth melted into a measured lowness. “We have to leave soon, and you need to eat. Choose one, or I will. You have five minutes.”

I gasped out, watching the door beneath a tide of wild panic and blazing anger. I seized the anger as I seized the last dress.

It was, aside from the chiffon, the least scandalous option. A bodice of fitted ivory silk and silver thread made soft flesh swell beneath bare shoulders and a too-heavy jewel. Beneath that the garment was much looser; pale, featherlight gossamer swung in layers, caressing my skin and fluttering to mid-thigh. A thick ribbon of silk and heavy thread- so shining and pure that I wondered if it was, in fact, actual silver- cinched beneath my breasts, then dangled to sway between my legs.

But there was little else to cling to. I felt naked. I felt vulnerable.

I knotted my hands, looking uncertainly between my choice and the other options and deeming all of it unacceptable. Incensed heat carried me towards a hairbrush- though I nearly chose the knife- and I stormed to the door.

I expected Velidan to be looming just outside, but he was reclining in the foyer’s burgundy chaise with a steaming mug in one hand and a book in the other. Both were set down as I appeared in the doorway.

“This isn’t funny!”

A slow gaze trailed the length of me. He said nothing.

“You-!” I snapped my jaw and eyes shut. Rose-scented air filled my lungs and I forced even, hopefully-compelling arguments past the rage. “I agreed to cooperate. To go with you. I let you put this on.” One hand closed around the jewel. “I’m trying to help you, but that means I need to be calm and able to think and feel safe. I can’t do all that if I’m… l-like this.”

It took him far too long to answer. “And what is this?”

My jaw worked for a moment. “Humiliating.”

“That is not the word I’d use.” He took in my frozen silence and leaned back, both hands interlocking behind his head. “Mortal, if humiliating you was the goal, I would have left you that leather contraption and nothing else.” He smirked. “I gave you choices. Two of them suit our purposes, while sparing you any real embarrassment. I’d hoped you’d choose the black, but this is… acceptable.”

Heat pooled in my cheeks. “Don’t act like… saints, choices?! There were clothes everywhere last night! You could have easily told me to find something!”

“And you would have chosen something very modest and inexpensive.” Glittering eyes traced a path I didn’t want to acknowledge and his smile sharpened. “I much prefer this.”

“Humiliating me.”

“I’ll wager this is the finest garment you’ve ever worn in your life. The most flattering, as well. Perhaps you can even wear it home.” He sneered, and iron edged the next words. “I’m sure your toothless guard dog would enjoy that.”

It wasn’t just my face, now; every inch of my skin blazed. “My what-”

My knuckles turned white around the brush. Legs trembling with anger clenched together and bare toes curled against marble.

He drew closer. “Did you prefer ‘plaything?’ Or perhaps ‘pet?’”

He had a point, so I seized his sleeve instead. The seams along a button started to rip until he caught one wrist, then the other, and held them between us.

That stilled me. I sucked in air through flaring nostrils and tried not to think about my literally, and far too obviously, heaving breasts. Instead I forced every blazing scrap of fury I had straight towards his smirk.

If another High Fae tells you to do something, you will obey.” He thought for a moment. “Unless it contradicts my wishes. You may look to me if you feel uncertain.”

My eyes narrowed to slits as I attempted an acid-tongued retort.

He finally lifted his hand. “Yes?”

He seemed to find the statement, and my quick pace back to the bath, unremarkable.
