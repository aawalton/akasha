The real test of his build, however, was how it fared against a slug dragon.

Lasse had been able to use his build to make the massive boss monster explode with just a double blink.

Adam’s ultimate combo of the lucky charm, two barrage gloves, and barrage master’s coat wasn’t quite as explosively destructive, but the 375 kunai they summoned were still more than enough to reduce the dragon to a husk of ruined fat and bone fragments.

Given the way the order of relics worked, the first to take effect was the barrage master’s coat, which made 21 copies of the original kunai, at full damage. Then the two barrage gloves activated, each creating 62 copies, not counting the original kunai used by both. The normal glove had kunai that dealt a third of the original projectile’s damage, while the ghostly glove’s projectiles dealt a sixth. All of the 125 copies were then cloned by the Ghost’s effect, creating 250 phantom kunai that either dealt a sixth or a twelfth the damage of the projectile they had cloned, depending on whether they were created by the ghostly glove or the normal one.

The result was much less powerful than Lasse’s, but in no way weak. The thing Lasse’s build had going for it though, was the fact that neither the Metallic Cascade fused evolution, the barrage skill from Archer, nor the barrage master’s coat reduced the damage of the projectiles they created. Meanwhile, Adam’s damage didn’t go up by a lot since the gloves either split the projectile damage across all the projectiles or even halved the damage in the case of the ghostly glove, while the Ghost merely doubled the damage of a projectile across its two additional clones.

However, the bleed and venom inflicted by each projectile all stacked together thanks to the afflictionist’s corset, delivering two monster afflictions that would theoretically cause a massive explosion of blood many times the original damage when anything survived the initial attack and could be bled, though nothing had so far, not even the slug dragon.

Actually, I’m not sure about the math there, since it’s hard to say which copies deal a third and which deal a sixth. Anyway, let’s just say it’s 300 damage. It somehow feels throttled… Adam thought after respawning the dummy and trying again with three, four, and five projectiles.
