If Kael gave the order, these people might attack Thalion’s forces, and that was what he wanted to prevent.Convincing them to switch sides might not be too difficult. Kael’s lies hadn’t erased the stories of Thalion’s stand against the orcs. He still led the largest human faction, and he had never made any secret plans to exploit or rule over others. All he wanted was stability.Time was slipping away. Thalion considered using the Crippled Eclipsari form to ascend quickly, but that would reveal a trump card he preferred to keep hidden until the inevitable clash with Kael. The tunnels weren’t wide enough to allow sneaking past unseen, and his bloodline skill couldn’t cover the distance required.
