Heyo, YJarex here,

First off, the bad news. I am dropping this story for the foreseeable future. Rise and Rapture was fun to write, but it didn't gain the sort of popularity I was hoping for it, mostly due to a couple crippling mistakes of mine. I wanted to tell a story about a young orphan adventuring among a sea of stars and planets a la Starfinder. However much I enjoyed the first few introductory chapters, or setting up the delve into the crab dungeon and also the rest of the adventurer exam (which in hindsight I really should have rewritten a different way), it was too slow a start, and failed to create promises for me to pay off before most people decided this wasn't for them.

To everyone who enjoyed the story: Thank you so much for your support, and I'm sorry I couldn't deliver at the very least one finished book. Rest assured that I will be rewriting this story sometime in the near future, much how I did with A Lonely Spiral, which soon enough turned into Our Little Dark Age. I'm not a fast writer, but hopefully with time I will evolve into the type of writer that can knock out banger after banger for you all to enjoy. Maybe I need to molt one or two more times. Maybe I should shower.

In the meantime, I have something for you to feast your eyeballs on:

I've just launched a new story called Trigger Happy: Magical Girl Arachnid Edition. If that sounds like something you'd be interested in, you can check it out here: https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/138254/trigger-happy-magical-girl-arachnid-edition

Emotions are fuel. Happiness is ammo.

Sam just wants to be a good person. It’s why she chooses to use her ability to see the unseen world of werewolves and vampires to comfort the ghosts of the departed until they’re ready to move on. But when shapeshifting aliens kill her cat, all the hugs and pleasantries in the world won’t get her back. Becoming a magical girl will.

Nobody ever said it was going to be easy. However, even equipped with magic, guns, and extra limbs, she is woefully unprepared when an entire alien invasion descends on her hometown…
