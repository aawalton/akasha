As Noah fondly reminisced, the memory of the day that had shaped him to become who he truly was, a serene smile graced his lips, different from his usual brash and reckless self.

Despite both being from the White Room and fellow Constellations, Jupiter and Noah never really saw eye to eye. They were simply too different in both their ideals and actions for them to agree upon anything.

However, Jupiter’s death changed everything for Noah and also the rest of their brethren. It was the day he stopped simply being a strong soldier and finally took steps on the path to becoming a true hero— the savior of his world. It was a long and arduous journey, but he was able to reach the worthwhile end. Now, he had started a new journey.

“Hey, hero, you should wake up now.” A sarcastic voice echoed in his ears, prompting Noah to open his eyes, a composed look shimmering in them. Feigning sleep had been harder than he thought it would be.

In front of him was the same infuriating princess. Blonde hair and blue eyes, a face that could be called beautiful by any human from back on Earth.

Noah scrutinized this face calmly. There were no fluctuations in his expression. Even now, he could feel no threat from her. At this distance, all he needed was one clean blow, and he would be able to literally explode her skull if he so wished.

Since there was no threat in sight, he started observing his surroundings. The other passengers had already been moved away by the princess and her accomplices. Only the two of them remained on the ship at the moment.

Status.

He noticed that all his stats went up slightly. It most likely resulted from devouring those Abyssal Beasts during his fight against the Time-Devouring Beast. Sadly, the increase in his stats was almost negligible. It was not very satisfying.

I guess growing will slowly become harder, huh? He sighed inwardly but showed none of it in his expression..

“What is happening? Where is everyone?” He asked, knowing full well that some smugglers had boarded the ship after the warp, taking everyone away from this location while leaving only him alone, probably at the orders of the princess before him.

“Hahah. Not so smug now, are we?” She placed the baton she held against his chest and poked him without holding back. “Don’t even try to move. The drug you guys received is still active. It’s strong enough to paralyze even some high-ranking Abyssal Beasts.”

She sneered and dug in deeper with the baton. “I wish I could give you a lesson right now, but the boss wishes to meet you.”

Noah had been contemplating whether he should blow away her head or not, but hearing her words, he decided to wait a bit before acting. The very reason he even let himself get kidnapped was because of his desire to catch a way bigger fish than the small fry before him. If he was right, he might have just landed straight at the Pied Piper’s headquarters.

How lucky… Though, what was her name again? Eve, do you remember?

— We never learned her name.

Oh…

He mused for a split second and realized that they had never managed to hear her name. That was a pretty big oversight on his part.

“What is your name again?” His question was genuine. But they lived in a world where honesty was the last thing to be appreciated. Her expression of joy transformed into an ugly scowl.

“Bastard!” She pressed a button on the baton, and immediately a surge of electrical current entered his body. She continued holding the button for a few more seconds to make sure Noah truly faced hell.

“Learn your place! The only reason you are still here and not in the mine or the slave market at this very moment is because you still have some value. But trust me on this,” She approached him, her eyes blazing with anger. “The very moment the boss is done with you, I will make sure to make your life as miserable as possible.”

Noah could not help but be confused by this mad woman’s behavior.

Hey, did I do something to that woman and forget?

— No. This is simply what you humans call envy and jealousy.

Ah… Yeah. It has been so long that I have forgotten how ugly people filled with nothing but envy can be.

Meanwhile, the princess only frowned. The electric discharge she was using was set to non-lethal. Nonetheless, it was enough to make most people scream and writhe in pain.

Yet that bastard was feeling nothing?

“What…?”

She was about to speak when she saw Noah start trembling and twitching lightly. At the same time, his once confident expression seemed to be mixed with unease and pain.

“Haha. So you were hiding your pain? How adorable of you, lowly wretch.” She stopped electrocuting him and placed the baton back in her holster. She was clearly satisfied by his small performance, and Noah could only applaud himself. If he were on Earth, he would have definitely won an award with that level of skill.

— Let’s just kill her?

No. For now, we will stick to the plan. Get more information first. I am very interested in this mine.

“Now, follow me, vermin.” Not knowing how close to death she had been, the princess took out a pair of handcuffs and bound Noah’s hands with them. Immediately, he could feel that they were surprisingly heavy, but they did not affect him much, if barely at all.

“Stay in front of me from now on. It’s time to meet the boss.” She nudged him, and Noah complied, slowly walking out of the small transport ship.

As Noah stepped out, he was immediately hit on the face with a gust of wind, stale, thick with the smell of rust and damp stone. The facility stretched out before him, a maze of rough-hewn tunnels reinforced with steel beams and flickering industrial lights. He did not know if they were underground or in space, but it wouldn’t matter much.

People of all races and genders dressed in tattered uniforms worked in slow, mechanical motions. Some were digging at the stone with dull pickaxes while others hauled heavy carts along rusted tracks. Their faces were blank, drained of anything resembling life. Hollow, mechanical eyes devoid of emotion stared ahead, not even sparing him a glance. The only sounds were the rhythmic clangs of metal hitting against rock and the occasional barked order from the guards, making the already miserable lives of the people even more miserable.

They are surprisingly healthy.

Noah did not like what he saw, not even a bit. However, he was able to calm his hot head and stop himself from simply tearing the throats out of all the guards of this place in one go.

What he did notice was that, despite being dirty and broken, their bodies were perfectly fed and healthy. He didn’t know if this was a curse or a blessing.

Unlike the broken workers, the guards were clearly different. Alert, armed, and relaxed in a way that told him they had complete control of the situation. Their weapons hummed with energy, and their armor, though not pristine, was leagues above what the average guard wore.

The princess pushed him ahead without hesitation, clearly used to this place.

“Quite the sight, huh?” she said, her tone laced with amusement. “A little paradise for people who don’t know their place.”

Noah did not bother to reply to the haughty bitch. His gaze flicked across the cavern, taking in every detail of his surroundings. The structure, the people, the exits.

They are too organized.

He had expected a group of disorganized mobs, but what he was looking at was a well-trained small army. He was still not experienced in the detection and sensing of Dark Matter, but he was positive that the armor worn by the guards was leagues above what Omid’s pirate goons were wearing, even the elite mecha pilots of his group.

This did not come as a surprise to Noah, however. After all, the Pied Piper was clearly working on a whole other level than a mere pirate.

At the far end, a massive metal door loomed above everyone, guarded by two men in heavy armor. When they saw the princess approaching, one of them pressed a control panel without saying a word and went back to his guarding station. The door slid open with a mechanical groan, revealing an elevator leading further down.

“Get in.”

The elevator door slid shut behind them with a dull clang. A faint hum filled the small space as the platform jolted slightly before descending. The walls were smooth metal, reinforced, and clean— unlike the rest of the prison outside.

Noah leaned against the side, watching the princess as she stood with her arms crossed, looking pleased with herself. She had not spoken about it yet, but the smugness in her posture told him she was enjoying this moment.

He let the silence stretch for a bit before finally speaking.

“So, this boss of yours,” he said, tilting his head slightly, “what’s he like?”

The princess scoffed at his relaxed attitude. “You’ll see soon enough.”

Not the most informative answer, but he did not expect one from the start.

Instead, he shifted his attention to the small display above the door, which showed the floor number ticking down.

- B5 -

- B6 -

- B7 -

They were going deep, or they were climbing up for all he knew. Many parts of space and the vast array of mechanisms out here still confused the hell out of him.

— Getting a map from someone's mind after devouring them shouldn’t be too complicated.

“Those mines,” he said casually. “What are they digging for?”

The princess glanced at him, her smirk widening. “Curious, are we?”

Noah did not respond, just waited for the princess to spill out the answer.

She let out a small chuckle. “It’s not just a mine. It’s a production site. Slaves work here, sure, but some of them don’t just dig. Some get taken to other sections… the ones with real potential.”

A vague answer, but one that confirmed something for him. This place was not just about forced labor. There was something bigger happening here.

The elevator finally slowed to a stop, and with a loud ding, the doors slid open.

The room beyond was different from the cavern above. No jagged rock walls, no scaffolding… just sleek metal hallways, lined with reinforced doors and bright, sterile lighting. The air was colder here, controlled, the kind that came from heavy ventilation systems.

The princess stepped out first, turning back to gesture at him.

“Don’t have any funny ideas. It won’t end well for you.”

Noah gave an innocent smile. “I would never.”

He definitely would.

He had, in fact, many funny ideas.

One of which started with her slow and painful death. Furthermore,

— I think… I can try to enter and take control of her body a try.

Noah had to fight to stop the giggle from leaking out. For years, he had fought against parasites, and here he was about to do the very same thing they had done to people on his planet.

The knowledge made his heart feel uncertain. However, would he not do it just because he felt a bit uneasy?

No. Not in the slightest.
