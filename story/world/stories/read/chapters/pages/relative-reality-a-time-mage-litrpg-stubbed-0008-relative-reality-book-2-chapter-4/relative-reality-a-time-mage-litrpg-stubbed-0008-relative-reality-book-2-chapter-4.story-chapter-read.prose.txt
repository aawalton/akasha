Rowan stepped out of the Illusorum chamber alcove, shaking his head to get rid of the last effects of the magic. Across the room, Dagen and his team were doing the same. Vin, their team’s main healer, walked over to Elise.

“What was that? I had two different barriers and you punched straight through them!”

Elise glanced at Helja questioningly.

“We have no reason to hide what we can do from them anymore, go ahead,” Helja said.

Elise stared intently into Vin’s eyes for a moment, presumably speaking in her mind. Meanwhile, Dagen was complementing Arne on ‘being an untouchable wall’, while Soril looked mostly confused.

“Good plan, Helja,” Rowan said.

“It worked. Only because their team is half healers, but it worked.”

What Rowan could see on Helja’s face, that she didn’t want to say out loud with the other team there, is that it worked due to their opponent’s apparent lack of training. Only Soril of the four in their team had reached Tier three, while Arne, Helja and Elise all had done so. Even Rowan was near to Tier three, and there was no foot up for being lower Tier like there was for having less members in your team.

Conversation continued, much to Elise’s despair, as they returned to the observation area. There were another two matches for the first round, after which they would be left with five teams to take part in the timed test. As they arrived, the two teams split up to find their own separate areas to discuss their plans for the next part of the tournament.

“I still say we should go last. There is no elimination for the next round so there’s no point wasting energy,” Helja was saying.

“Helja, I don’t think it’s going to be a problem. Do you feel tired at all? The Illusorum Chamber doesn’t seem to make us tired at all,” Rowan countered.

“Our match was quite quick and remember that during our training sessions here during the year we did get mentally tired eventually.”

“Let’s just consider who we know will be a part of the next round, right? Hendrik and his team are great at being defensive, but they aren’t particularly fast. Same with Felir and his team. Sure, we rely on Melody to gain power over time, and Arne isn’t exactly what you would call quick, but you, Elise and I can be quick. We don’t know which other two teams will be there, but we should consider trying for first.”

“There is no knowing what is timed test. Maybe we destroy something fast?” Arne said.

Helja turned to Melody and Elise.

“What do you two think?”

The two looked at each other, then shrugged.

“Might as well wait to see what the challenge actually is,” Melody said.

Helja sighed, but let it go.

The next two fights came and went. One of the two teams that won was led by a woman called Anya. Her team was much the opposite to the one that Rowan and his team had just fought. They had no healer, nor even what one might call a support member. Instead, they worked purely by individually self sufficient powers as a group. Everyone had ways of taking care of their own health and mana pools, with a lot of those methods focused around doing damage. This team was one of the ones that Rowan had a feeling would not be beatable in any kind of time challenge. Their blitz tactics and potential for outright damage would make them a hard opponent.

The other team that won was led by Norok, and was much more of what Helja called a traditional team. A hard to hurt frontliner like Arne, a healer, and a couple damage focused team members. They would be much like Rowan and his own team, capable of speed but slow compared to Anya.

It came down to what the timed test was, then. If it was a footrace, Rowan was sure he could win against just about any single one of the other students. He had a feeling that was not the case, however. A feeling proved correct very quickly.

Once all the teams were gathered in the viewing room once more, Instructor Sky announced the next test.

“For the timed test of skill, we have programmed five different emergences into the Illusorum. Each team, starting with the team that defeated their opponent the slowest during our previous round and going in order of speed, will choose one emergence. You will then each complete your team’s chosen emergence in the Illusorum Chamber as quickly as possible. The timer for your team will be stopped once the anchor entity for the emergence is defeated. Failing to complete your emergence will result in an immediate placement in last. Should multiple teams fail to complete their emergences, we shall decide the placement between them ourselves. Additionally, the deaths of team members will result in a penalty. Hendrik, you and your team are first up to choose.”

With that, Instructor Sky led Hendrik’s team out of the viewing room.

Rowan looked at Helja, who seemed thoughtful.

“Well?”

“Well… I can see us winning this, yes. It isn’t what I was expecting for a timed test. It will need us to use all of our skills, and a truly balanced team will do best. Penalty for deaths is particularly important, as Anya’s team will struggle to blitz through an emergence as fast as possible without losing one or two people.”

“I could sneak to the end of the emergence and kill the anchor entity myself,” Elise said.

Instructor Sky and Hendrik’s team came back in, and the team led by Norok was called out.

“You could, I think that would be dangerous though. If you got caught, or couldn’t quickly kill the anchor entity, you might die and give us a penalty.”

“Well, why don’t we just go about clearing it normally? With my Song of Power we should be able to get it done faster than most of the others,” Melody asked.

“You’re right,” Helja said, “but I want to try something a little mischievous at the start to get ahead.”

The time came around for their team to choose their emergence. With both Norok and Hendrik’s teams having gone first, there was three options remaining to choose from. Unfortunately the instructors had decided that the information they had for each emergence was somewhat limited. They were given nothing on what types of enemies they might face or the types of abilities that would be seen. All they were given was an environment along with a few images.

First in line was a desert, with some images of high sandstone walls and arches. The ground was sandy, probably the reason no one had chosen it yet, with sun beating down from above. Next was a series of green forest groves surrounded by thick trees and underbrush. The final images showed an underwater complex with some kind of magic holding back a crushing ocean overhead.

Looking at all of the images, Rowan could tell why the instructors saw no reason to give any more information. The types of emergence entities and enemy powers they would see in each emergence seemed obvious enough.

“The ocean emergence? It looks like it has the most isolated rooms and that’s what we were looking for,” Rowan said.

“It does, but my flight won’t be much use in there, and I am stronger under the open sky and Melody is stronger in the direct sun,” Helja said.

Elise crossed her arms.

“Well I am weaker in the sun, and I hate the look of that desert. I much prefer the shadow of the ocean emergence.”

Rowan pointed to the forest groves.

“Looks like that’s our best choice then. Some shadow, some sun, relatively isolated rooms. I’d rather not be trudging through sand under the hot sun either.”

“And this is my favourite place,” Arne said.

“We’re decided then?” Helja asked.

Everyone in agreement, they told Instructor Sky their choice and went back to the viewing room. They now knew that Felir and Anya’s teams would get to choose between the ocean and desert emergences, not that it made much of a difference to them now they had made their own choice, but information was information.

Once they were done, all five teams were brought back into the room with the Illusorum alcoves where they were to begin their emergences all at the same time. Rowan began to feel a little anxious about the real meaning of this particular test, as more than anything else in this tournament, what they were about to walk into was the true trial of their training at the academy. Clearing emergences would be the next six months of their lives after the tournament, and potentially many hundreds more years of the same after that. Despite the competitive nature of what they were about to do, this was the first time that Rowan and his team would be truly alone in clearing an emergence, even if it was just an illusion. Each and every other time, there had been an instructor or more powerful person there to fall back on, but not today.

Helja gestured for their team to gather around her.

“Just stick to the plan, we’ve got this,” she whispered.

“Alright everyone, into your alcoves we will begin shortly,” Instructor Sky announced.

The low whispering across the room faded as everyone got into their alcoves and strapped in.

“Okay students, your time begins the moment the illusion is active. Everyone ready?”

She waited a moment, and with nobody objecting, the test began.

Rowan’s vision shifted until he stood with his team in a lush forest. They sprang into action immediately, making a dash for the gap in the trees that led to the first room of the emergence. As soon as they entered, a chorused hissing sounded from the underbrush around them, but they ignored it entirely as Rowan used Relativity on the entire team, pushing them all to extreme speeds and Elise activated her ability Elusive Repositioning.

Elusive Repositioning - Soul - Blessing - Stone of the Tactician

Tier 1 Effect:

You and target allies are rendered invisible for a short time. This is broken on any individual that makes an attack or uses an ability.

Tier 2 Effect:

The speed and dexterity of all targets is increased for the duration of this ability.

Tier 3 Effect:

The first attack made upon exiting this invisibility has increased damage

Gaining immense speed due to the overlap of both abilities, and invisibility from the emergence creatures, the team sped through room after room, their only communication Elise mentally calling out which path to take as Helja chose them. She led them using her Wind Sense innate ability, which gave her a sense of touch and smell through the air. It thankfully didn’t break Elise’s ability, being more of a passive effect.Their short burst of speed and dexterity thankfully ran out before Rowan’s mana did and they fell back into the normal time stream. Helja’s idea to gain some initial ground had been a great one, but now came the dangerous part.

Arne held his ground at the entrance to the room while Melody set up her songs and Afterimages. Rowan ate a small amount of solidified mana to help enhance his mana regeneration, and Helja prepared for the dangers of the room they were in. The creatures didn’t take long to emerge from the surrounding brush, a strange mixture of snake and monkey, hunched and gangly with scales and fangs.

Helja went all out, slicing creatures in half with her fiery blades of wind while Elise made more precise strikes into lethal spots on the larger of the creatures, her cloak absorbing attacks to regenerate her mana and keep her safe.

Arne called out as he came under attack from the entrance to the room. Three summoned forest guardians stood by his side, holding the door firm. Helja turned her attention from the creatures surrounding them and began to cast Tornado. Seeing it was his time to act and with some mana regenerated, Rowan took over her work in keeping their own clearing safe while she and Arne dealt with the entities at the entrance.

Seeing that he would need to deal with some of the entities now lest they build up before Helja was ready with Tornado, Arne used Fiery Form.

Fiery Form - Body, Mind, Soul - Avatar Form - Stone of Fire

Tier 1 Effect:

You change form, becoming fiery. This alters your abilities in different ways while active. Defensive abilities become offensive:

Tier 2 Effect:

The damage of all fire based effects created by this ability are increased.

Tier 3 Effect:

This ability becomes an active with an extreme ongoing mana cost, rather than a cooldown.

Flames licked across his summoned roots and the three forest guardians burst into fire, their solid form melting into stone and lava. The shield in his hands turned into a massive war axe coated with fire and Arne began to lay into the quickly mounting creatures with reckless abandon.

Rowan lost track of Arne’s situation then as he had to focus on his own job. While Elise did what she could to thin the numbers, she was much better at taking down singularly strong targets than she was at dealing with masses of them. Helja was safe up in the air, but Melody was central and exposed to danger.

It was down to Rowan then to defend for her and he did his best. Still Tier two, Rowan desperately fended off the fanged creatures using speed and elusiveness as his strengths. He was everywhere at once, redirecting an attack here, dodging out of another strike there. Time slowed to a crawl as he shoulder checked one of the beasts, sending it flying. Reality shifted again, resuming a relatively normal course of time as Rowan summoned Fracture to help. Three of the creatures found themselves trapped in a recursive section of reality as Fracture bent space around them, allowing Rowan to sprint to the other side of Melody to stop an attacker there.

Despite his best efforts, Rowan did not have the pure destructive force that Helja had. They were about to be overwhelmed when Helja finished casting Tornado at the same time that Melody burst an Afterimage playing the Song of Power. A burst of strength washed over the team and the various creatures at the door being held back by Arne were torn into the sky and set aflame by whipping winds. All of the sudden the pressure was off of Rowan as Arne arrived by Melody’s side. Rowan was free to focus on bursting down singular emergence entities with short but extreme uses of Relativity while Helja delt with the main masses of them.

Already playing yet another song, Melody was once more safely under the protection of Arne, an immovable fortress of vines supported by his forest guardians. The slaugher continued until the flood of creatures turned to a trickle, and the team were left refreshed by a burst of health, mana and stamina as Melody used Afterimage Consumption on yet another two of her songs.

There was no time to stop and breathe though. On a timer still, the team pushed on further into the emergence as fast as they could.
