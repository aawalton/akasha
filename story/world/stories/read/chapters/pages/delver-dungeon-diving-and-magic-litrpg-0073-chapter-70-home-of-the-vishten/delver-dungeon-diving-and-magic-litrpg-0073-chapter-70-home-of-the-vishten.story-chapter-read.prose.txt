Finally, Seer Varia of the Vishten nods her head to a cushion opposite her, and slowly lowers herself to the floor. She places her staff on the floor in front of her, then gently picks up the kettle and pours three cups of tea.

Lady Kala's shoulders relax slightly before she sits across from the Seer. She looks at the tea, but doesn't touch it.

I hesitate for a second before sitting between them. I take the cup of tea and take a sip.

It's bitter, but I like the aftertaste.

Seer Varia gives me a small smile, then takes a sip herself.

"It has been a long time since our two people shared tea." Seer Varia remarks.

Lady Kala hesitates, then reaches down and takes a sip herself. Her eyes close for a moment before she swallows. "It is quite good."

Seer Varia places her cup on the table, her expression hard. "I know why you are here. But tell me in your own words, what do you want?"

I glance over to Lady Kala, trying to read her body language. Sadly, her mask prevents me from seeing any of her thoughts. "I want my people to be safe. If it is possible, I would like to have the safety of your stronghold."

They didn't even need me for the conversation, not really. Still, it's an hour of listening to negotiations before I manage to escape.

I walk out the door, several guards, both Vishten and Dralk noticing me. The two women are still hashing out specifics, so I wave them away before entering the town proper. And it is starting to look like a proper town. It's actually surprising how advanced the buildings look, making me wonder if the Vishten have some accomplished craftsmen among them.
