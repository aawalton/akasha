Once again, Ronan felt the Temporal Obelisk had grown in size. He was certain that its… presence—for lack of a more suitable word—had grown. It occupied the empty white void in a way it hadn’t before. He was drawn to it, even knowing that there was nothing more it could offer him just yet.

It no longer promised further gains from the stage 3 reward ever since it had evolved his personal trait, but he assumed it was still absorbing the excess energy. For what purpose, he knew not, but it would likely benefit him.

Throughout the iterations he’d completed of the hard difficulty tutorial, Ronan had also amassed 16 new and new-ish uncommon skills. New-ish because some were skills he’d already possessed, but had been upgraded by receiving a better rarity of them.

There had also been 5 duplicates, which annoyed him, but he’d never invested much in luck, so he couldn’t really complain. 16 skills were plenty, anyway. He was beginning to wish he had a way to combine, or consume skills, as many of them were losing their usefulness.

All in all, most of the uncommon skills were useful to Ronan. Even those that weren’t directly beneficial for his current issues had potential future utility. Especially skills such as repair and suture wound—if he could learn to replicate them with sorcery, he would be able to improve their effects.

Self healing and item repair were beyond valuable and if he was able to replicate them without skills, and without a prerequisite class, Ronan could utilise them in every future iteration to great effect.

Next up he pulled up his status. As he looked over his base stats, he realised he’d been lacking in his eagerness to use mineral skeleton. There were plenty more materials he could try to integrate into his skeleton lying around the tutorial. Anything that improved his stats from the get-go was incredibly useful and he would be a fool not to make the most of every advantage he had available.

Ronan realised he’d also unlocked a couple of new masteries in the previous iteration. Breath and meditation were interesting ones, and he knew exactly what had caused them to unlock. He did wonder why he’d never unlocked meditation before, but perhaps there was some sort of spiritual limit he had to break through first.

Before he began the next iteration, where he was finally going to defeat the damn goblins shrouded in darkness, Ronan sat and thought about a niggling worry he’d had. Manasynthetic was supposed to absorb mana from spells used against him, but it did nothing against the darkness. Yet, his mana breathing did.

I suppose it wasn’t used directly ‘against’ me… The shaman simply cast it into the environment. I’ll have to see what happens when it uses a curse or damaging voodoo spell on me, he thought to himself.

With his strategy decided, Ronan simply had to hope he received a suitable weapon drop before stage 4. He would prefer a club or a spear, but a sword would do. If he had to fight using only his body he wasn’t sure he’d survive the first attempt, but it wouldn’t stop him from giving it his all.
