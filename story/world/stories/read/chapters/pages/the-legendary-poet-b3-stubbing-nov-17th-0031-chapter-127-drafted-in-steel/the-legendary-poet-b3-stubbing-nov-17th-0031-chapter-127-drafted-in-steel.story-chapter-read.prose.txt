The Discarded Draft fought like a sentence with the verbs torn out, and Ren read it anyway. His class wanted him to lean on the auto-parry buff stacking, and he refused it, the way he always refused it, slipping the Draft's reaching arm by exactly the width of his own forearm and no more.

Ma-ai. Distance was a language. He spoke it fluently. He hated that fluency, because fluency was the proof he was working for something a better man would have been given.
