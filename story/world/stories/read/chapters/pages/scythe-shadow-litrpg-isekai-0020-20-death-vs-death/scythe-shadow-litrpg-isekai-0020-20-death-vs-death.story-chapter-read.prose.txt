Calliope and I returned to the wagon in silence. Jericho had maneuvered it a different direction and was back in the driver's seat. Nuzzle was curled up in his lap, sleeping.

He nodded to us solemnly. We didn't say anything.

We didn't have to.

I understood now why he'd reacted the way he did. It wasn't just that there was a boy similar to his lost son. He'd known the boy would die. Like his had.

We climbed back into the wagon and continued the journey. It took a little time, but the further we got from the scene, the more everyone seemed to perk up.

A short while later I found myself staring up at a massive wall constructed from a patchwork of gray and brown stone. Several taller buildings of the same make peered over the top from the distance. Green flags with pink flowers fluttered in the breeze atop the wall. At the base a large moat surrounded it, broken only by a stone bridge leading to an open gate.

Meadowvale. It was apparently the largest settlement in the Meadow Realm, and based on the appearance, I didn't doubt it.

A pop like a small cannon sounded nearby, followed by a stream of colored smoke arcing from the top of the wall directly overhead.

"Unchained sighting," Jericho noted. "We should pick up the pace a bit."

"Where?" I asked.

Something rammed against the side of the wagon with a crack. It rocked onto two wheels then crashed back level. I fell onto the floor, rough wood splintering into my hands.

Calliope landed on top of me. "We really should've added seatbelts," she grunted as she pushed herself up.

"I found the Unchained!" Jericho said.

Gee, thanks.

The wagon lurched again. I had just gotten up and went flying. My back slammed against one of the benches. Something snapped below us and the wagon tipped even further, momentum driving it sideways. It crashed to the ground, still being dragged along by the stallions. The canopy tore with a visceral rip as dirt and pebbles flew up into my face and bruised my limbs.

It stopped after a brief moment.

"Everyone out!" Jericho shouted above the noise.

Calliope was somehow already out the back, grabbing onto Allium's reigns. She swung her leg up over the saddle and summoned her scythe in one quick motion.

I scrambled out to join her.

The wagon was a wreck, the back left wheel completely missing, the side cracked in three places. A mountain of a beast swatted down at the remains of the canopy, ripping it to shreds in a single blow. The Amber aura around it wavered like a flame.

The Sin snarled at the defeated wagon, wolf-like fangs dripping saliva. It howled into the sky, pointed ears laying flat on its far-too-human head that ended in a hairy snout. It circled the wagon, lean, muscled human body controlled by powerful lupine legs, veined in glowing Amber rivers.

Jericho looped around the other side, joining us with Nuzzle and his two undead stallions. "Well, that's unfortunate. I literally just upgraded that wagon."

"Do you think we can outrun it to the city?" I asked.

"Not a chance in hell. I'd be happy to do the honors," Jericho said, summoning his scythe. "If the wall guard had given a little more notice I'd be done already and my wagon wouldn't be a pieces!"

"Wait," Calliope said. "I think we should let the silkie give it a go."

Jericho turned to me. "Sure, why not? I haven't had a chance to see him fight yet."

Uh.. Excuse me? The wolf had finished mauling the wagon, realizing there was nothing interesting there for it.

And it turned to us.

"I've already got a Will to relinquish anyway," Calliope added, before turning to me. "And I think this one might be best for Zephyr anyway."

They both looked at me, smirking like idiots.

"Ah, fuck me…"

The blue bar below my health sat at about the halfway point. It had cost some Volition to return here, and been steadily draining since I arrived. Being the newest, my Volition was probably the lowest, even with the enhancement from the other Will I relinquished.

Technically I do need to replenish my Volition soon if I don't want to end up like that boy, but… The wolf hybrid let out a snort, devilish eyes meeting mine, promising violence. The Amber veins coursing across its skin pulsed.

"Sure, I could use a refill." And one less Sin in the world.

I stepped forward accepting the beast's challenge and examined it with my Second Sight. The symbol of a skull orbited the Sin's burning Amber Will.

[Identity: Sin

Rank: Amber

Affinity: Death]

Calliope nodded to me. So that's why she said I should fight it. This Sin was a Death Affinity like me.

I stretched out my armored arm and summoned my scythe.

But nothing happened.

I stared at my hand accusingly, panic rising in my chest. What the…?

Then I remembered. Calliope took my scythe to examine it. I hadn't even brought it with me to see what was going on with the Ruby and the boy. Idiot. But that means…

The wolf stalked toward me, his cunning eyes, studying me. Behind it, the demolished wagon lay in ruin.

…It's still somewhere in there…

"Well, shi—"

The Sin bolted forward.

I Aero Shifted out of the way, my Volition dropped almost a third of what I had left. I could only use the skill two more times or I'd be out.

And if I run out…

I sprinted to the wagon, a clear path to it, now that I'd side-stepped the wolf.

"What are you doing?" Jericho shouted.

I could hear Calliope swear beside him. "His scythe!"

I dug through the wreckage, more splinters piercing my left hand. I focused more on using my armored hand on the more dangerous shards of wood.

Where the hell is it?

Pain seared though my calf as fangs dug into it and dragged me backward. I could feel its saliva dripping into my wounds, thaw heat of its putrid breath on my leg.

It yanked me and my head clanked on a shattered axel.

As if I need another concussion…

I kicked at the monster, but he held firm, teeth digging deeper into my leg. My Health plummeted. The pain was so raw I couldn't even scream.

My mind betrayed me in that moment. Instead of a wolf Sin, I saw a hideous decaying lion. The lion. It tore at my leg, gorged on my insides. I was broken. Helpless. Dead.

No.

That had already happened. I fought the memory, forcing myself back to the present. Not a lion, a wolf.

And this time, I wasn't helpless. I can fight!

I lunged my upper body forward, shoving my armored fingers straight into the wolf's eye. It yelped, releasing it's grip enough for me to scramble free.

I got to my feet, struggling against the pain. Each step was agony as I turned back to the wagon, just in time to see Calliope jump off Allium and dive into the rubble. She ripped through the remains of the wagon like a beast herself. Seconds later she pulled my scythe from the wreckage, her face a grimace as sizzling steam leaked from her hand gripping the shaft.

She threw it toward me, twirling through the air like a boomerang. But the throw was off.

I reached out, a desperate grasp at nothing.

The scythe whirled, curving in mid air, and thunked straight into my outstretched hand.

I didn't even have time to be awestruck.

The Sin nipped at my heels.

I swung the weapon behind me, spinning to face the beast. He backed away briefly, registering the change in the battle but not retreating.

After a moment of calculation, it snapped at me. I backed away, holding the scythe out like a spear to keep him at bay.

He snapped again, teeth clanging against my blade.

I kept backing away, but this time, when the wolf opened its mouth, I lunged forward, sending my scythe past its fangs toward the back of its throat. I sliced, feeling the blade grate against muscle and bone.

The beast's jaw hung loose, half attached, dripping blood. It made a gurgling howl, but I cut it short with a swift downward strike into its skull.

I dropped to me knees, chest heaving. The mutilated Sin lay motionless.

I won. "I won."

"By Jove, you sure did!" Jericho said, jogging up beside me. "It was touch and go for a moment there, but overall fine work, I must say."

"Definitely already improved since your last Unchained encounter," Calliope said, punching my shoulder.

Nuzzle scurried up and licked my bloodied leg repeatedly.

"That's disgusting, buddy," I said, shaking him away.

He licked the bloody grass around the dead Sin instead.

Not much better, dude… But at least it wasn't my blood anymore.

I stood, keeping as much weight as possible off my left leg. Jericho and Calliope offered to help, but I brushed them away. This was my victory, I could do it on my own.

My Second Sight revealed the dormant Will in the beast's chest. I slid my scythe through its rib cage, piercing the orb. The Will within burst through the cracks and flowed into my blade, like before.

[Will successfully harvested.

Rank: Amber

Affinity: Death]

I wasn't sure what it meant to harvest a Will that matched my own Affinity, but Calliope had clearly intended for me to collect this one for that purpose. I'd likely find out when I relinquished it into one of the pillars.

I knelt down and picked the revealed item from inside the rapidly mummifying and decaying corpse.

[Syringe - 5cc

Effect: Unknown]

Sure enough, I held a plastic syringe—needle included—in my fingers. Luckily, the needle was capped. I didn't need that thing stabbing me from inside my carapace inventory.

I put the item away and turned back to Jericho and Calliope, my legs nearly crumbling beneath me. "I think I'll take that assistance now."

Another smoke signal fired off from the city wall, arching off to our right.

"Right. Let's make it quick," Jericho said.
