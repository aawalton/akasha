The room behind Zach was an absolute dragon's hoard of mana and loot.

Thomas's first thought was that Tiny had been an absolute fool to have been bought out for a single expandable sack's worth.

The inventory room was at least forty feet deep, with shelving reaching all the way up to the ceiling. Loaded on those shelves, and arranged by grade and type, were mana crystals.

Light mana was the most predominant and produced such a vibrant glow that those sections were almost painful to look at straight on. Judging by the color of the other sections of mana, Electric and Nature were heavily stored as well, all grades up to huge A grade crystals.

That was all just the mana. He saw bits of armor and vials, which must have had potions and... just holy shit.

Thomas felt a touch on his arm and turned to see Jo smiling up at him. "I think we broke Thomas."

However, Twitch looked just as stunned. "Yo, why were they all just sitting on this? It's like mana Fort Knox. They could've just turned it all into money."

Thomas answered automatically. "Because there's a good chance money isn't going to be worth the paper it's printed on in a few months. Also, a lot of the guards and higher-ups seemed to be running on a Corruption mana mix. Adding Light types to that is a little like throwing gasoline on a fire." He blinked as something occurred to him for the first time. "Actually, how's it even possible they're running a mix? Corruption mana turns everything it touches into more Corruption."

Granted, he had Corruption in his mana core now, which was being held back by a thick coating of Adaptation, but he didn't think that was everyone's solution.

To his surprise, it was Antonin who answered. The guy kept so quiet and made himself so small it was easy to forget about him.

"You accept it." He tapped over his heart with the flat of his hand, then shrugged and looked down.

"Yeah," Thomas said slowly. He supposed getting stabbed with the stuff was not the optimal way to absorb Corruption mana. "Still, I think it's telling there's no Corruption mana stored here with the rest—Hey, is that Wellspring?"

He was thrown off track as his eyes caught a familiar glow in the back of the room. It had a different quality to it, like looking at an LED versus a traditional light bulb.

Drawn to it, Thomas stepped over the threshold. As if that were the signal, the others entered too.

Thomas walked fast to the back of the room. Sure enough, there was a whole small section of shelf dedicated to Wellspring mana. Sure, it was all level three, but that didn't matter. He grabbed the other spare expandable bag out of his backpack, the one with the loot drops from the elves, and started throwing Wellspring in.

Like hell was he going to leave any of this behind after all he'd fought for, and especially taking the chance anything left behind might be funneled to the Light Elves to help them take over.

The main term of the quest was to take back the facility and the dungeon for the people of Earth. Well, Thomas was more than happy to deprive his enemy of resources.

You're welcome, humanity.

"Found the skill shards," Zach called from a spot a little closer to the entrance.

Thomas shoveled the last of the Wellspring into his bag and immediately converged over there along with the rest of the team.

His first impression was that there were fewer skill shards than he would have expected.

Then again, people had to have a general level of capability with mana in order to absorb it into their cores. Skill shards were slightly more flexible. While they burned mana as fuel, most seemed to be able to burn different types of mana to get there, or none at all. As an example, his Combat Foresight burned stamina instead of mana. So shards would be easier to sell and easier to give away as bonuses to lackeys or to gift to the higher-ranking families.

Each skill shard was arranged in its own little plastic display box like someone's valued card collection. Most had tiny one-line descriptions underneath that gave basic information on their functions.

There was a shard that used Electric mana to let the person function as a mana battery, a Nature mana type that promoted fertility, and a Light mana that 'revealed the truth about objects,' which might be an analysis skill.

Thomas was pondering the last one when Jo made an excited sound and snatched a box off the shelf.

"What do you think of this?"

A Toxin of Everything

Description: (Nature Mana) Twists attacking mana types into personal poisons.

"Uh..." Thomas's first thought was that the skill sounded a bit evil. Then again, all of Jo's skills were a little shady. That fact used to bother her, but right now she seemed excited. Her short time as a prisoner had hardened her.

Either he had been quiet a moment too long or something in his face gave his unease away, because she blinked at him. "What's wrong? I think I should be able to absorb Nature mana, and if not, I don't see why it won't work with my Poison mana."

"Try making it work with Nature, first," Thomas said, remembering his discussion with Akilah on mana types. That way Jo wouldn’t go all in on Poison mana. To his relief, she nodded and seemed to take him seriously.

"You won't be the only one trying a new mana type," Zach said, joining the conversation. He held a skill of his own, grinning. "Check this out."

There was no written description, but the title was flat out ridiculous: Supreme Movement of Transcendent Light.

Jo's eyebrows raised. "Is that what I think it is?"

“Light speed?” Thomas gasped.

"Yeah! Not quite teleportation, but movement at the speed of light is close enough, am I right?" Zach's enthusiasm dimmed slightly. "It'll definitely need Light mana, but I'm going to see if I can help it along with some Teleportation too."

Well, since the cat was out of the bag with this Gift and they were all looking into skills, Thomas decided to show off all he'd scooped up.

"Before anyone adds anything, take a look at what I got from the elves. Some of these might be higher leveled than anything that came from the dungeon."

He started laying the skills out.

Everyone seemed as uneasy as he felt about Raveth's aura and pseudo mind manipulation skills. Twitch was initially a little interested in Laveris's skill, considering how much of a tank he'd been, and overall pain in the ass to try to take down. He ended up passing, however, when Thomas explained what he knew about simple skill titles equaling lower power.

To his surprise, Zach declined Courtney's skill, Ever Elastic Physique.

"Nah, man, I'm still feeling like I'm getting a handle on my Dancer's Grace skill, and..." he shrugged, covering up some uneasiness. "I don't think the skill is corrupted because she was, but I still don't like the idea of taking a part of her into my core. She was something else."

Jo paused and looked up sharply at him. She had just picked up Frederic's skill, To Bend Shade To One's Will. "You think that's a possibility? I mean, a bit of the last person’s personality hanging onto their skill?"

He shrugged again. "It’s more like I’ll be thinking of her every time I use it, and you know my Gift runs off vibes."

Thomas very much didn't like that line of thinking, either, considering he took Grimm's Eyes of Riches into his skill set, but he forced himself to brush it off. "If you think the shadow skill will help, you should take it."

Jo hesitated again. "I'll just hold onto it for now. Not like I'll be able to take it until level four, anyway."

With a nod, Thomas turned from her and noticed Twitch looking hard at the second skill Courtney had left behind: The Earth Receives All Blows. He had a complicated look on his face, and Thomas could easily guess why. Twitch had been the one to finally end her.

Thomas stood next to him. “You okay?”

Twitch jerked his shoulders in a quick shrug. “It’s not like she was my girlfriend, but… taking her skill is a little messed up, right?”

“Maybe,” Thomas admitted. “I took Grimm’s Gift as my skill and it didn’t twist me. Like Jo said, it’s not like you have to decide right now.”

Twitch closed his eyes for a moment. Then he shook his head and his expression firmed. “Nah, I’m not going to be a pussy. My first layer’s Earth, this is perfect for me.” He snatched the skill up.

“All right.” Thomas clapped him on the shoulder then finally turned to look at the rest of the weapons and items that were stored in the room. Unfortunately, there weren't any Light weapons. He got the feeling any of those that dropped in the dungeon were immediately sold to their elf allies/overlords. The remaining ones were a variety of swords, mostly non-enchanted, some stun sticks that worked off of Electric mana, and even electric boots, which he didn't see the purpose of, but Twitch insisted he take. No one fought him for it.

The vials were potions for brilliant light, which they guessed were meant to be used like flash grenades or flares. Those would be useful.

Interestingly, there wasn't a single crystal of Healing mana to be found. Those were either kept for personal use or sold back to the elves.

Thomas stood by for support as Zach absorbed a Light mana crystal. He went with a C-grade. It was technically below his level four core, which meant he should be able to handle it if things went wrong.

Light seemed to briefly flash through him as he absorbed the crystal. He stood in place for a few seconds, flexing his hands.

"It's not too bad," Zach decided after a moment. "I think Light's like a cousin to Fire, not as close as you'd think. But..."

"What?" Thomas prompted after he trailed off.

Zach rolled his shoulders like he was still settling into himself. "No offense, Thomas, but I think I know why Light Elves are assholes. Light's like... liquid confidence, and clarity of choice. Except that has to be an illusion, you know?"

As usual, he didn't know. "Huh. I can't say I felt that," Thomas admitted. Then again, he didn't have the same connection to mana as Zach. "So you’re saying I'm going to go megalomaniac if I keep up a heavy Light mix?"

That seemed to snap Zach out of his inner musings. He flashed a grin. "I'll call out pineapple if you start monologuing and evil laughing, but I think your other mana types will help keep you humble."

Thomas nodded, though he wasn't actually worried about himself. "You're good, though?"

"Yeah." He nodded to himself. "I can definitely work with this, but I’m going to wait to absorb more until I can get the level four mana. After that, I am so going to go light speed."

Great, their team’s adrenaline junky was going to be brimming with confidence. Well, if it became a problem, he’d call it out. "Speaking of teleportation," Thomas said. "We gotta figure out how to get all this stuff out of here."

That got the attention of Jo and Twitch, who looked at him.

Thomas was surprised at their surprise. "What? Come on, we're not leaving this. Any of it." Not a single crystal, if he could help it.

"Yeah, yeah, but, what do we do logistically?" Twitch asked. "We can't keep it here. So, what, we bury it all out in the desert?"

Thomas snorted. "I was thinking more along the lines of a storage unit."

"A few storage units, bro," Zach corrected. "In different cities."

Twitch nodded. "Splitting 'em up is better, in case someone knocks off one. Those things are usually filled with junk, though, so they're not huge targets. Just don’t roll up in a flashy car and draw attention."

“We got the team bus right here,” Thomas said and pointed to Zach who gave a double thumbs up.

Jo broke in. "Lord knows I'm not saying no to the wealth, but is that everything, then?" She looked at them all and asked the sixty-four-thousand-dollar question. "How do we even know when the System's quest is completed?"
