Alan Pioneer News

“And we’re here again with more Pioneer News!!!” Allan exclaims with a wide smile as the screen behind him changes. “Of course, by now the only Pioneer anyone in our sector of the Alliance cares about is our lovely Aqua! So let’s get right into the news!”

The screen begins to show Aqua as she looks at a large crab monster from on top of its body. All while controlling the deeper water pressure her skill lets her create to wrap around parts of the massive crab and crush those parts one after another.

Gradually killing the crab by water pressure alone.

“Aqua has been hunting larger monsters one after another for weeks now, all while training her latest skill,” Allan says as Aqua sits down on the crab’s head with her hands in her pockets and begins trying to create an abyssal servant out of the creature. “And with every monster hunted, her level increases, and so does her army of abyssal servants.”

“Her increased army size doesn’t seem to help her all that much though,” Rodrick says when the two hosts see Aqua succeeding in creating an abyssal servant out of the crab.

“She only has a certain amount of mana after all,” Alan says while narrowing his eyes. “No matter how many abyssal servants she has to choose from, she can only summon a certain number of them with the amount of mana she has.”

“What do you think her current goal is?” Rodrick asks while glancing at Allan.

“Amassing as much power as she can before taking on that quasi creature the locals of Oceana are so worried about,” Alan answers without taking his eyes from the screen, where Aqua can be seen riding on the crab out of the coral reef she was in and in the direction of the next subject of her hunt. “Leveling up and gathering stronger abyssal creatures to fight against the monster as she does so.”

“In that case, how bout we make a bet?” Rodrick says as he narrows his eyes a little at Allan.

Allan blinks before grinning at him and asking, “Don’t you remember the last time we had a bet? Are you sure you want to do this?”

“Of course,” Rodrick agrees without hesitation. “Because this time I’m the one proposing the bet.”

“Then what bet are you proposing?” Allan asks with his eyes squinting a little in his suspicion.

What is he up to?

Rodrick’s face doesn’t give anything away for him, so Allan isn’t sure exactly what he’s planning.

Is he trying to get revenge for our last bet?

Technically both Allan and Rodrick lost their last bet, but since Allan worded the bet the way he did, he still came out on top. Getting exactly what he wanted in the end.

So Rodrick might be trying to do something similar this time by proposing the bet himself.

“How about we bet on whether Aqua will manage to extract an abyssal servant out of the quasi when she defeats it?” Rodrick suggests, making Allan blink in surprise for a moment.

Then Allan narrows his eyes at his friend and says, “Alright. Which side will you take?”

“I bet Aqua will get the quasi as an abyssal servant,” Rodrick declares with absolute confidence.

Allan stares at him for a few seconds before turning to look at the screen. At which point he thinks about the previous monsters she’s turned into abyssal servants.

The higher the monsters level compared to her own seems to have an effect on her success rate, so it entirely depends on when she decides to challenge the monster…

He continues staring at the screen for a while, only to turn back towards Rodrick again.

But even if she tries to fight it while above the monster’s own level, there’s still a high chance it might fail.

With that in mind, he decides to go with his gut as he asks, “What are we putting up for the bet?”

“If I win, you never bring up any incident of me sleeping outside in any way ever again and you’ll have to sleep outside for two nights,” Rodrick says with a grin. “But if you win, I’ll give you that.”

Allan’s eyes narrow as he asks, “You mean it?”

Rodrick’s grin grows even wider when he says, “I do.”

“You have a deal,” Allan says while reaching over and shaking Rodrick’s hand. “If you win, I’ll sleep outside for two nights and will ever bring that up again. But if I win, you better be ready to give it to me.”

“Of course,” Rodrick declares, his grin unchanging.
