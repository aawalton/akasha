Heya everyone.

It's been a long time since I've given any updates on this series, because I've been really busy with other stories after wrapping the first half of Exorcist back in November 2024.

As you can tell by the title, Isekai Exorcist Book 1 (chapters 1 - 46) will be stubbed on June 17th.

It will be published on July 1st by Aethon Books and you can already pre-order it here, if you're interested.

I've talked about continuing the story of Isekai Exorcist with what would be book 4 sometimes in Q2 or Q3, but I've currently got two books I'm writing on simultaneously and have been really busy as a result. However! Once I wrap up Book 3 of Madman Apocalypse sometime before September (hopefully), I was planning on starting Exorcist back up again.

The ideas for where the story goes next have been swirling around in my head for a long time, and my trip to Japan back in February really gave me a ton of inspiration. I visited Ryuuta's hometown of Kyoto and also had my first visit to Tokyo despite having been in the country several times before. Also got to experience my first Shinto welcoming ritual by the priest in my girlfriend's family, which felt really magical and put my imagination into overdrive.

I feel like no matter where I go and what I do, my imagination always keeps me company, and I feel genuinely blessed to be able to use it to craft stories that others are reading (without coercion no less!)

I really hope the publication on Amazon goes well, because I want to be able to keep writing stories for you guys. 2025 is sort of the year I figure out if that's viable for me as a career or not. I dipped my toes into the amazon market with my self-pub of Madman Apocalypse last month and it really just showed me how difficult making writing into a full-time profession really is.

I don't want to make it sound like my ability to keep writing hinges on how my books perform on Amazon, since I'll always keep writing, but when I graduate in February 2026, I have a very important decision to make and if my writing isn't able to sustain me by then I'll have to severely cut back on how much I can write every week in order to pursue full-time employment.

I'm doing my best to sign good deals for my books and trying my hardest to make all this work, which is why I signed with Aethon, because in my opinion they give LitRPG/Progression books like Isekai Exorcist the best possible chance on Amazon. Stories of course still have to stand on their own merit, but the hardest thing is always getting people to even look at you in the first place.

I really think Isekai Exorcist works better in a book format rather than webserial because of the pacing, so I have high hopes for the launch and hope y'all will give it a gander when it's out.

Even if you're not interested in re-reading it on KU or listening to the new audiobook, I hope I can count on you to tell your friends about it if you liked the story thus far. There should also be a physical version available at launch for those wanting to feature it on their shelves at home.

Anyway.

You might have seen that I changed the cover on RR, and I hope it makes you excited about what's to come.

As you'll know if you've finished reading up to Chapter 181, the journey will take Ryuuta and his Party to the distant continent of Crescent. However, there are still loose threads in Lacksmey and Arley, not to mention a disguised Exorcist they have to rescue from himself and the powerful mask he's carrying. There's also the mystery of what the unique new 'Soul Singer' Role does and how the reforged Kumi Inoue will contend with returning to a mortal life.

Lots of exciting stuff lays on the horizon for Isekai Exorcist, so I hope you will stay tuned for when I (hopefully) bring you an update around September-October this year.

If you read this far, thank you!

Isekai Exorcist was honestly my first proper 'hit' on RoyalRoad and what set me on the journey towards making writing possible as a career.

And I have all of you and your support to thank for that!
