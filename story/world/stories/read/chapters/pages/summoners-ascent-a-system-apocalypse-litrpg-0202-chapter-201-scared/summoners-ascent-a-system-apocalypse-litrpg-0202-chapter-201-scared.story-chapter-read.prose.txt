With a long, draw-out groan, Sam walked up to the tent and squatted down next to Ashton.

"When did you even tell her to say that stuff?" Sam asked, clearly embarrassed. "It's one thing when it comes from you guys, I don't even care anymore, but from a kid? That's a low blow."

Ashton shook his head. "That's not it. Millie, who's watching right now?"

"Hm... Just the four are close. The others are still far away," she replied. Her voice was quiet and she turned her head, avoiding Sam's confused looks.

"Good. Then, just in case," Ashton held his hand forward, beginning to construct the 'silence' spell.

Once the outside sounds faded away, Ashton started tapping his finger on the table next to him. "Millie can see certain things. Souls, specifically. Like that, she can see through someone's personality, feelings, thoughts, and sometimes deeper things. For example, she was able to see the contracts that I formed within my vessel, and she can even see the constellations to some degree." Ashton smiled. "I'm sorry to say, but what she saw is your true nature, or something hidden inside your soul."

Sam stared at Ashton for a few moments. "Fuck off."

"Language, there's a kid around."

Rolling his eyes at him, Sam looked at Millie. "You cuss, right?"

"Of course I do, I'm ten," Millie replied with a huff, as if it were obvious.

"See? She doesn't care. Now tell me that you're lying."

Ashton shook his head at Sam's frustration. "Nope, sorry. But listen, it's not like we know too much about this ability yet. It doesn't have to mean that you're somehow related to King Arthur, and it doesn't have to mean that you're supposed to be the King of the Britons."

Sam's head dropped into his palms. "Fuuuuck!"

"Wait, but why am I wind, then? It's not like I'm particularly, uh... windy?" Clementine complained, looking back and forth between her friends confused. "Oh! Do I have some super big talent for air magic or something?"

Ashton involuntarily scoffed and held a hand in front of his mouth to stop himself.

"Huh? Wait, what's so funny?" Clementine asked, and Ashton just cleared his throat and smiled.

"Nothing, nothing. I just thought it was funny that you can't see what the deal is. Wind is an element that's often connected with freedom. Tell me, why do you love running so much?"

Clementine hesitated to reply for a few moments. She scratched her cheek with an awkward smile. "I guess, now that you're asking like that... I like the wind in my face, and the way it makes me feel so unbothered. Like nothing else at that moment even matters," she explained, thinking about it for a little longer. "But no, it's not like I feel super 'free' or anything in any other way. I'm stressed all the time, I'm locked into a career that I actually fucking hate, my family is fucking terrible, and I can't even get over–" She stopped herself, glancing at Ashton slightly. "I just don't feel very free."

Ashton's smile didn't drop, even though he knew what Clementine almost said just there. "It's not about the reality of the world around you. It's about the truth in your heart. What you look for, what you need, the thing that fulfils you. And most importantly, the truth that may be buried beneath the surface."

Clementine slowly nodded. "I see... So... does that mean if I work toward it, that it's something I can achieve?"

Ashton smiled and turned to Millie. "Well, what do you think?"

The girl looked at Clementine again, a bit more intensely this time. "There's tons holding you back, but yeah. I think so. Probably."

"Probably? So I might not?"

Millie groaned and looked at Ashton. "What am I supposed to say? I don't know! I can't see the future! I'm not a... uh..."

"Seer. I know," Ashton replied, trying to give her an encouraging smile. "But this gift that you have is a lot more than just 'sight'. You can feel things about other people that can give you more insight than anyone else. Like... during this conversation, haven't you been glancing at the two of them the whole time? You saw them changing a bit, right?"

"Wait, we changed? But didn't you just say that this is 'our truth' or something like that?" Sam asked confused, and Ashton nodded quickly.

"Of course, but emotions seem to have a big impact on the form that you take in Millie's eyes. Right?" Ashton turned to the girl, and she nodded in agreement.

"Mm-hmm. Your armour, Mister Sam, became a bit more rusty. And the parts of the crown started shaking. And you, Miss Clementine, you became darker? Like when the sky gets dark when it rains. You were both scared, I think," Millie explained. But something about her demeanour concerned Ashton, as it had the whole time the girl interacted with Sam and Clementine. But it was okay. He would address that later.

Sam and Clementine felt nervous as well. Just having something so innate about them be seen would probably be exhausting for most. Well, Ashton already had all his inhibition stripped away over the course of a century. His body, soul, and heart had been laid bare so many times that something like this couldn't bother him anymore. Not that he didn't understand why others felt the way they did.

But something like this was also a great opportunity for them. While the system streamlined everything, whenever you were working with mana in any capacity, whether as a mage, a fighter, or a crafter, knowing who you were and what your strengths and weaknesses were was an incredible help.

Being able to have the shape of your soul laid bare in front of you could feel almost humiliating, but Ashton knew that Sam and Clementine could handle it. That they would come out the other side stronger.

"Well... what about you?" Clementine asked, clearing her throat, with her shoulders pulled in. "What do you look like to her?"

The moment the question was asked, it was like most of Millie's anxiety was stripped away. "Oh! Ashton looks really cool! He looks like a mountain! Or a giant, or something! He looks the same, but it's like I'm looking at him from so, so, so far away!"

"Oh..." Clementine replied. Ashton was focused on keeping up the silence spell, so his channelling range wasn't particularly compressed, but he could still tell the expression that both of his friends were making perfectly well. That same look of concern bordering on pity. Like they were trying to figure out a way to help Ashton.

He loved them both more than words could express, but he hated this look.

"And, and, actually, something else happened earlier! You know, when we were outside, and you were fighting, or when you called that manbat guy." Clementine pointed out excitedly, sitting up straight and leaning toward Ashton a bit, as if trying to get a better look. "Tons of writing just popped up on your skin, and it was bright and glowing."

"Oh?" Ashton raised a brow. He picked up his summoner's pen and dunked it in a pot of ink, quickly starting to write a few common sigils and runes on a scrap-piece of paper. "Did they look like this?"

Millie nodded excitedly. "Mm-hmm! Not those exactly, but similar!"

"I see. Now that's interesting."

"And when you were fighting, there were like light, or electricity in your arms and legs, it was super cool!" Millie explained next, like she was running down a list that she had formed in her head that she wanted to talk about.

Ashton smiled. He let some of his internal energy, the mana refined through his core, flow around his body, strengthening the muscles in his arms, back, and legs. Even before he had to ask, Millie's eyes widened.

"Oh, it's happening again!" She looked Ashton up and down, following the flow of the internal energy with her whole face.

"Hm, alright. So you can see things like that, too? Sam, go, use a skill," Ashton said immediately. Sam snapped out of that concerned stare, and slowly sighed. "Sure, fine."

He took a few steps away and stretched a bit, and then, threw a few punches into the air using the 'Strike' skill. The air almost seemed electrified by the mana that was released in that moment.

Ashton then looked at Millie. "Did you see anything happen there?"

Millie tilted her head slightly. "I think? Maybe? The armour is pretty and super shiny but it's kind of rusty and scratched up all over. But it was glowing just now, I think. Right in the arm. But it was gone so fast that I couldn't tell, sorry..."

"No, no, that's perfect. Good job," Ashton said with an approving, bright smile on his face. "That's actually really amazing, you know? If you can see things like that, then you can predict exactly what your opponent is going to do. And maybe even how their abilities work! Hell, you might even be able to use those abilities against them somehow..."

Ashton looked at his friends. "You two, buzz off for a second, yeah?"

"Huh?" Sam let out. "Uh, are you sure?"

"Yes, I'm sure, I need to ask her something. You guys just process the things she told you, okay? Make sure to think about what that all means, and how you can take advantage of what's hidden inside of you," he instructed them, though he clearly just gave them some homework to do so that they would leave him be for a while.

Plus, there really was something that he had to talk to Millie about. Both Sam and Clementine left, still with those looks on their faces like they were worried about the implications of the way that Millie saw Ashton.

But soon, they were far enough away, and outside of the range of the silence spell. Immediately, Millie seemed to relax.

"Are you scared of them?" Ashton asked, quite clearly. Millie flinched.

"Scared? What? I.... don't think so?" she replied, slowly looking away. "There's just something... weird about them."

"Weird? How? Is that why you called them 'Mister' and 'Miss'?"

Millie nodded, nervously. "I didn't say it when they were here, because I thought I shouldn't let them hear it, but... there was something in them? Not the same as you, where the uh... summons live in you. But there's something scary in them. Something that shouldn't be there. It's like it's growing through them, like a plant."

Ashton fell silent. He placed a hand in front of his mouth. "Did you see something like that with others?"

Millie shook her head. "No, I don't think so."

"I see. Tell me the moment you do, okay?" Ashton asked. He was concerned about this, incredibly so. And then, Millie said something that made Ashton's heart skip a beat.

"Oh! But I think they look a little similar to the cracks on your body, somehow," Millie explained.

"... I see." Ashton could feel his stomach turn upside down, and his face go pale. 'So, the scary thing that took root in my friends feels similar to the damage the Summoner did to my soul...'

He didn't like the implication of that. Not one single, tiny bit.

That was something that Ashton had to look into more. As soon as he could. But if that was somehow connected to that thing, then Ashton also had to be careful.

Trying not to let Millie notice the layer of cold sweat on his forehead, Ashton began to smile broadly. Though, she could probably tell that he was a bit shaken.

So, he decided to focus on Millie for a few moments. The magic stone in her hand, which was so bumpy and rough, and had so many clear edges all over, was already rounding out. She really did have a talent for magic, that was undeniable.

Plus, her innate trait of 'sight' gave her the ability to perceive souls, emotions, and magic in ways that almost nobody else would be able to match if it was trained enough, with the right set of skills that matched her well.

There was just one class that Ashton could think of. And if he could train her well enough, maybe Millie might be able to help Ashton remove that from his friends' souls.

"Millie. Do you want to become a Witch?"
