Dear readers, I know you've been waiting for more chapters, and I'm sure that you think I've dropped the novel. I apologize for the sporadic uploads and the two-week hiatus. However, this announcement is to inform you all that I will be discontinuing Magicapita on Patreon and RR and will be contracting with WebNovel, which is where it will be released exclusively at a rate of two thousand words every day.

This decision was not easy. I was quite committed to releasing it on RR, then Patreon, and Amazon and Audible, but unfortunately it has simply not done well. Thus, I have no choice but to shift platforms, because I do this for a living. The good news is that once it is contracted, it will be uploaded at a daily rate, necessarily. However, it will take a few months on WN for the novel to catch up to the latest chapter, chapter 384, so you will have to wait a while.

Thank you for all your support up to this point, and I'm sorry for disappointing you. Nonetheless, I hope you will enjoy the story that is to come.
