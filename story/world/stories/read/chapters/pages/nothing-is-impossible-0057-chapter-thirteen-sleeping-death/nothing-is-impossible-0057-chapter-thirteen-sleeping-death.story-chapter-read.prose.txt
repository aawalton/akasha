A little past midnight, 5 cloaked forms climbed through the third floor of the guild barracks, using a magic item to not set off any of the magical wards that were set up all along the perimeter of the building. They then activated another magic item, this one blocking all the sound in the air. It was widely known as silencing magic and blocked the casting of any spells, whether they needed to be spoken or otherwise.

After looking around and making sure that they had not been seen at all they began securing the perimeter of the room and making sure that there were no loose ends before they began their operation. They spotted their target and got into position.

Once everyone was in position, they struck.

***

Even as Atlas was sleeping, his body felt a spike of danger and reacted. He shifted to Strength before teleporting out of harm's way, lashing out with his scythe and decapitating the closest man in an explosion of blood. When the blood settled, there was nothing left of the man, Atlas had obliterated him with an effective 118,850 strength. There was absolutely nothing the man could do except die.

Atlas moved again, killing two more men before the shock of the first’s death could be fully comprehended by the attack group. The two remaining men hastily raised their defences, lashing out with magic wildly, trying to hit him and incapacitate him.

Two seconds later, they were all corpses.

The scythe went slack in Atlas’s hand, and he collapsed to the ground. He had never woken up after all.

Danger Sense (Z): Your body responds to danger that you are not aware of by taking over your body and fighting for you, not disturbing you at all in the process and leaving you in a coma-like state. This skill will warn you of the danger seconds before taking action itself, and if you don’t respond, it will carry on as previously stated. When fighting, your body will hold nothing back and completely annihilate all aggressive entities.

Atlas had slept through the entire thing.

He fell on the ground, sleeping soundly once again. He didn’t know he was sleeping in a pool of blood.

He was far away from the bed he had slept in, and he wondered what had happened.

Satisfied, Atlas began creating a new skill. He had gotten an idea after waking up this morning and finding himself in the center of all those corpses. What would happen if he combined his danger sense with multiplying debuffs? It felt like it could be a brutal combination and he hadn’t been using his debuff as of late. It seemed stupid, to have such overwhelming firepower and magic and instead use debuffs to slowly weaken the enemy.

So he decided to take a chance and combined them. Not bothering to think more for fear of rationalizing a reason to not do it.
