Thankfully, Asher was spared what he could only assume would have been one of the most awkward conversations in his entire life. Seeing as the lives of hundreds of thousands of people within the capital were currently at risk, Bordan took over the task of telling Theorn what to do. After grabbing a rock with an Eternal Mark on it, the truthseeker crouched down and leapt with explosive force, clearing half of the second ring in a single bound as he made his way toward the Frent family estate. That left Asher, Clare, and Owl all standing aimlessly at the gate, watching Bordan leap across the city.

“So… what the hell do we do?” Clare asked. Her chains raised Owl up a little higher, before shaking the shadow man like a doll. “Do we kill him? Can we kill him?”

“We have no way of knowing what Lethinal’s defenses are like surrounding the dome,” Asher said, shaking his head as he tried and failed yet again to Recall to Brian’s location. “Bordan said even with Theorn’s incredible speed, if he leaves immediately, it will probably still take him about forty-five minutes or so to reach the capital, depending on the wind conditions. If Richard’s guess is right, which is a big if, we’re barely going to have ten minutes to figure out how to take down Lethinal, stop the ritual, and save the capital. We’re going to need all the help we can get.”

“You’re seriously planning to work with this guy?” Clare asked, raising an eyebrow as she looked at Owl. The Knuckle had been silent ever since they’d agreed to help, letting them piece together a plan without interruption. “Can we trust him? He is a master assassin, after all.”

“You trust me, don’t you?” Asher asked, turning to look at Owl’s literally blank face. “Well? Are you willing to help us take down Lethinal and save everyone? I’m not just going to cut a hole in the barrier and let the Head escape with their life. This is an all-or-nothing kind of deal.”

“Then I will help you,” Owl nodded. “As much as I can. I already explained, but my tritessence reserves are limited. I am not willing to deconstruct myself, so my help may be limited.”

“We’ll take what we can get,” Asher said, gesturing toward Clare.

“Your funeral,” she muttered, releasing Owl from her chains as they faded once more. The Knuckle landed silently on his feet, nodding his thanks toward Clare for letting him go. “So what now?” Clare asked.

“Assuming Theorn is as good as Bordan claims he is, we’ve got forty-five minutes to prepare for the final battle,” Asher said, offering a hand to each of them. On the inside, he was positively freaking out at the thought of the entire capital being erased in only a couple of minutes, but he tried his best not to let it show. If he was to have any chance at saving Brian and all the other innocent lives within the capital, it was critical he keep a cool head and not fall into the throes of panic. “Now we collect the troops.”

Knowing that for however hard he was freaking out about Brian, Samantha was going to have it ten times worse, Asher started with Poltar. One Recall later, and he found himself standing on the floating city. Samantha was in the middle of waving goodbye to someone she’d just healed by the look of water dripping down her arm, and she gave him a tired smile.

“Finally finished all your bouncing around? Is it over?”

“Not quite,” he grimaced. Taking a deep breath, he quickly explained everything Owl had told them. To his surprise, Samantha took the news of Brian’s current situation surprisingly well, all things considered. Despite looking like she wanted nothing more than to flare her wings and start flying toward the capital with all the speed she could muster, she knew her limits better than anyone and no doubt understood she’d never make it in time. She let him finish with the plan, before nodding her approval.

“I know this is selfish… but once we get there, I need you to cut a hole in the barrier for me. I’m going to rescue Brian.”

“You’re going to go inside the giant sphere of annihilation?” Asher asked, before catching himself and shaking his head. “Stupid question, of course you are. I would too if I wasn’t the one required to kill Lethinal. But do you even know where he is? He’s hidden in the truthseeker academy.”

“Remember how I mentioned Donvath made sure I still got my days with Brian when you were dead for a few weeks? Without your power to Recall in and out of that room they had prepared, Donvath set up a sort of in-between room for us to meet. It’s through a secret passage in the palace. I think if I follow it, I’ll find the academy. I’m assuming all the truthseekers are currently working to try and find a way out of the sphere, which means I shouldn’t have any issues blitzing in and grabbing Brian. Though I’ll need you to cut another hole in the sphere for us to get out once I have him, assuming the sphere is capable of sealing itself shut.”

“Alright, I can do that,” Asher nodded. “Sorry. I wish I could offer to pop in, grab him, and pop out, but according to Owl, Spatial skills won’t work on the inside.”

“Don’t worry. That’s why I’m going to get him,” Samantha said, a dangerous fire burning in her eyes as her grip tightened around her bow. “Lethinal better hope you’ve already dealt with him by the time I get back, or so help me I’m going to find a way to make a lich feel pain.”

With Samantha’s help in giving him a bird's-eye view of Poltar, Asher quickly found Moxy, Sefta, and Sarah as well. Unsurprisingly, it didn’t take long at all to convince both of the battle-loving maniacs to join him in his quest to take down the leader of the Council of Death, and Asher shuddered as he pulled them into his rift one after another. He was a little scared of what finally introducing the two of them to each other would result in, but he didn’t have time to worry about that. Sarah was just as eager to meet back up with her team and happily joined their cause.

Next was Juhstain, where Asher again explained what they’d learned to everyone gathered there. The slapped-together team of Rulfar, Amanda, Groln, and Groln’s quiet, rock-based teammate who he only now learned was named Dustin all looked equally shocked at the news of what was to come.

“The entire capital?” Amanda asked, her face scrunched up like she couldn’t believe she was hearing that correctly. “Are you sure?”

“Our intel is admittedly from a slightly questionable source, but we’ll know for certain soon enough,” Asher said, focusing on the Eternal Mark Bordan had handed off to Theorn. True to Bordan’s word, the mark was currently screaming across the kingdom at speeds Asher hadn’t even known people were capable of reaching. Tossing his awareness over to the mark showed Theorn crouched down over his pegasus’ neck like a horse jockey, a visible slipstream of wind splitting around the pegasus and rider as they shot through the sky like a missile. Based on his current trajectory, he’d make it in time.

“One more lich,” Groln muttered, his blood-red eyes narrowing as he flexed his claws. “Count us in. We’ll do whatever we can to finish wiping out the Council.”

“I’m pretty spent, but I’ll do what I can as well,” Amanda said, giving him a tired smile as she raised her sword. The glow coming from her body was weaker than he’d ever seen before, but he wasn’t surprised. Amanda had gone head-to-head with two different liches, each one a serious powerhouse, and she didn’t have Complete Resilience to help counter fatigue. He was impressed she was still standing.

Unfortunately, not everyone was still in fighting shape.

“I’ll do what I can, but it’s not going to be much,” Rulfar admitted. The weary northman was currently sitting on the ground, looking like he desperately needed a cup of coffee. He’d reverted back from his battle form, and he clearly needed to go off and hibernate for a few days. His blended skill allowed him to remain in his powerful battle form for far longer than most people, but the catch was he needed serious time to recover once he let it drop.

“Go with Samantha. She could use your help finding Brian,” Asher said. Even if she knew the rough location of the truthseeker academy, having Rulfar’s nose to actually help locate Brian would make their odds of finding him far greater.

His next stop was Furora, where he told the same story a third time, this time to Cora and Jameston. The two of them had spent the last few hours working together to help with the wounded. Jameston could easily locate people trapped under chunks of ice with his Mind element, and Cora could carefully melt the ice down to retrieve them. Despite everything they’d already been through with Mako’s hopefully temporary death, they each agreed to join him immediately.

“If Lethinal’s the leader, that means he’s the one who’s responsible for all this,” Cora said. Her gaze was hard, as if thinking back to Lanra’s funeral. “Let’s tear him apart.”

“You have my support as well,” Jameston nodded, holding out a hand. “Let’s finish this, once and for all.”

With only a few minutes to go before Theorn reached the capital, Asher recalled back to Whikoga. Popping into existence besides Rosh, he blinked when he found himself not just looking at his old friend, but Draken’s main crew, and even Bordan of all people.

“Realms, took you long enough,” Thunder snorted, bouncing her electrified mace on one shoulder. “What were you doing, telling everyone your life story?”

“Thunder,” Draken said firmly, causing the elite to nod an apology and stop talking. Stepping forward, Draken fixed Asher with a hard stare.

“Bordan’s already explained everything. If the capital is truly about to be erased from existence, we are coming with you.”

“I’ll admit, I’m sort of surprised you care so much,” Asher admitted, wincing as he realized how that sounded. “Not that you wouldn’t care about so many innocent lives being lost… but I know you’re not exactly a big fan of the nobility of this kingdom.”

“I may despise most of the noble houses. But the High Prince has done right by us,” Draken explained, nodding respectfully toward Bordan. “Many rulers of other nations simply wipe out those who hold forbidden elements within their cores, regardless of if it is a naturally occurring origin element or not. My own existence puts Donvath in a tricky place. The fact that I’m allowed to live at all is a kindness, and one I have not taken lightly. Today is the day to repay that kindness.”

“And we’re coming to make sure nothing happens to the boss,” Crush said, tossing Asher a warm smile. “And because we figure you’ll get hurt again without us.”

“I’m coming because I want to see the world!” Smiles admitted, thrusting his soulsteel blade excitedly into the sky. “And because I’ll die if I don’t, which is excellent motivation!”

“Obviously I will be joining you as well,” Bordan said, as if there had never been any doubt. Despite the blood and undead viscera covering his armor, the truthseeker stood tall and strong as ever. “I need to make sure the High Prince is safe, along with all the civilians within the sphere. Not to mention I don’t trust Clare not to get up to trouble without my oversight, and she’ll just sneak away if I tell her not to go.”

Smiling so wide his face was starting to hurt, Asher turned to look at the final member of their crew. Rosh was trying unsuccessfully to keep Asher in between him and Bordan, and the logistician snorted when Asher turned toward him.

“Nine hells and counting, don’t look at me! I’m not joining you suicidal maniacs in your battle against the head of the Council of Death!”

“Thank goodness,” Asher laughed as he clapped Rosh on the back. “If you’d offered to come with us as well, I would have started to suspect I’d already fallen into some sort of mental trap of Lethinal’s. Don’t worry, I think we have more than enough firepower as it is.”

“Are you sure?” a voice rumbled from the heavens. Asher’s heart skipped a beat when a dragon smashed into the ground beside them, sending dust and bits of stone everywhere. Elnor’s sudden appearance caused Rosh to squeak and go white, and Asher feared the man was going to pass out entirely when the dragon’s head lowered toward them, flames licking from the corners of her mouth as her giant eyes stared at them.

“Room for two more?”
