The Crystalised World Fragment was sitting heavy on Ashton's palm. It felt like it was made of metal, and that something about holding this wasn't exactly... normal.

He slowly focused back on the piece of paper laying next to him.

Crystalised World Fragment

Grade - Legendary

Integrity - 97%

A highly concentrated fragment of this very world that was created by fusing a Crystalised Veil Fragment and a Crystalised Planar Fragment. Instead of acting as a bridge between two realms, it is an object that is tethered to both and exist simultaneously within the material plane and the veil, even if only projects itself into one realm at a time.

Through this object, it is possible to interact with the veil to an increased level while in the material plane, and vice versa.

As usual, the explanation was pretty simple, but it told Ashton everything that he really had to know.

This piece was an anchor between two realms now. No wonder that it was part of the process of summoning a constellation. Constellations were beings that reached the peak of their world and transcended in one way or another; a fragment of the world to represent that made sense as a conduit.

Plus, it didn't seem as though this would be used up during the summoning, either, though it might lose some of that 'integrity'.

But if it was the same as the 'Veil Fragment', then it should slowly recharge that over time. Ashton had felt some changes in that piece over time and checked on it periodically, seeing that the integrity percentage increased quite a lot.

Either way, with this, Ashton should be able to start preparing the rest of the ritual for Alice's contract. This was perfect.

[[Observer in the Veil] tells you to pay attention to what they are saying!]

"Hm?" Ashton focused on the message in front of him. It was pretty obnoxious somehow, like it was grating Ashton's ears even if there wasn't anything audible about it. Did the Observer put extra 'effort' into sending that message, then?

"What do you want?" he asked with a sigh. He had been ignoring the constellations a little bit and only responding sporadically, so maybe he missed something.

[[Observer in the Veil] asks how you did that just now]

A laugh escaped Ashton's mouth. The Observer in the Veil, a being that was, as far as Ashton understood, an expert of all sorts of magic. So how did he not know about this?

"It was pretty simple. What, you've never gotten your hands on a world fragment before?" Ashton said smugly, playing around with the small, heavy orb in his hand.

[[Observer in the Veil] scoffs and says that they possess numerous World Fragments]

[[Observer in the Veil] says they want to know how you created one without any tools]

"Ah... Well, I just kind of did it, I guess," Ashton replied with a shrug. "Just kind of pushed it all together, you know?"

[[Daughter of the Scarlet Heart] smugly points out to the Observer that you are a magical prodigy]

[[Observer in the Veil] asks why the Daughter is acting so proud]

[[Observer in the Veil] says that they will reward you if you teach them the method]

Surprised, Ashton actually considered the Observer's offer. A 'reward' was different than some kind of deal. If it wasn't a sponsorship, Ashton probably had little to worry about. Plus, the sort of things that the Observer would most likely reward him with were probably things that he couldn't easily get here, since magical items were obviously extremely limited.

However...

"Uhm... I don't really know what else to tell you. I really just 'kind of did it'. I pulled the two halves together, found a stable position for the pattern, and then shifted the mana. That's all," Ashton explained. It wasn't like he had some kind of secret method. He wouldn't say the process was easy, but it was shockingly simple.

[[Observer in the Veil] stares at you]

[[Observer in the Veil] asks how you could have such precise control over mana that you could stabilize two fragments]

Ashton shrugged. "Dunno. Anyway, now shush. Figure it out on your own. I need to see what this thing is all about right now anyway, and if you can't tell me that, zip it."

[[Observer in the Veil] grumbles at your manner of speaking]

[[Observer in the Veil] says that nobody else would dare speak to a constellation that way]

"If you've got complaints, feel free to leave," Ashton replied. He really didn't care what the Observer thought about something unimportant like his manners. He pushed the messages into the background again, and focused on the world fragment.

If there was a deep connection to the veil, then maybe he would be able to influence it more. Maybe he would be able to sense the veil more acutely if he channelled this item.

Ashton slowly let his mana flow into the fragment and focused his senses on the veil. And just as he thought, or rather hoped, Ashton could sense the veil almost perfectly!

It was to the point that it felt like he was...

"Wait, what?" Ashton let out, confused. Was he... in the veil? Was he astral projecting right now?

He moved around, confused. This really felt identical to what things were like when he fell asleep and wandered through the veil. Except that this time, he was holding something in his hand; the world fragment.

It still felt solid and somehow heavy to him, even if there was nothing in the veil that should feel like this in the first place. It was like a reminder of what the material plane was.

Ashton let his magic flow back into the fragment, and the world shifted as he found himself back in the material plane, and he couldn't help but laugh out loud.

"You've got to be kidding me, seriously?" Ashton put a hand over his mouth, trying to hide his broad grin. "Others have to work for decades to be able to astral project at will, but I just made an item that lets me do it?"

Millie glanced over at him. She seemed confused, as she seemingly hadn't seen any real difference in Ashton since he was sitting there.

But there was something else there, something that Ashton noticed during the 'switch' this time; instead of snapping from one realm to another, there was something like a transition between the two. In-between stages that Ashton hadn't really thought of before.

He let a bit more of his magic flow into the fragment, but this time purposefully tried to slow down the swap, and then stopped it when he got to the point he wanted.

This one was rather... well, weird. It was like the astral plane overlapped the material plane. Even without channelling the wisp, he was able to see the form of all things, albeit in the way that they appeared within the veil. He couldn't perfectly see Millie, anyway. But this made Ashton wonder.

He continued on a bit further along the transition, until it was clear he was solidly within the veil. That switch was almost instant, at least, like a lever was switched with enough pressure. However, Ashton shifted the transition a little further toward the material side, and as he did, the normally shifting imprint of the material plane into the veil became more solid. It was more like the way he saw things through wisp-sight, but he was still completely in the veil. Plus, instead of being given all the wisp-sight's information all at once, he was really seeing things this way, as if he still had his eyes.

Ashton walked around the rooftop, a bit startled.

"A-Ashton?" a shivering voice sounded out. It echoed through the space around him. As he turned around, he saw Millie standing there, following him. "What's going on? Why are you... here, instead of there?"

"Wait, you can sense me?" Ashton responded, startled. But Millie didn't seem to be able to hear him, but she still reacted a little. Like she could tell that Ashton replied, but not what he said. It didn't seem as though she could see him, either.

Curiously, Ashton grasped the fragment in his hand a bit more, and fine-tuned this sensation a little. If simply shifting across the boundary of two realms was possible, then he should be able to fine-tune it a bit more as well.

And then, Ashton could feel something happen to his body. His astral projection felt like it was becoming more solid than before, somehow. He still wasn't tied to the same laws of the material plane in their entirety, but he could feel the weight of gravity pushing down on him somehow.

And Millie pulled back, startled. "What? Ashton..?" she turned around, looking over at the body that was still sitting in the tent, motionlessly. "Did you die? Are you a ghost now?"

Millie sounded panicked, grabbing her cloak anxiously, but Ashton smiled down at her and shook his head.

"No. This is a little like the imprint we saw at the university," Ashton explained. Millie's eyes widened.

"But then how can you hear me? I thought that was like an, uhm... memory?"

"That's why I said it's 'a little like' that," Ashton explained. "I'm astral projecting myself into the veil right now. And from here, I was able to solidify myself into an imprint that echoes into the material world. I'm not sure what I look like to you right now, but at least you're able to hear me well, right?"

"Uhm... you look... unsharp? Like I'm squinting my eyes real hard," Millie explained. "And your voice sounds wrong too. Like it's too high?"

Ashton nodded. "Great. Then, how about if I do this?" he influenced the fragment a little more, trying to affect the way his echo was seen in the material plane. With Millie's feedback, he was able to let himself be seen and heard the way he should be fairly easily. It would still be affected by the spiritual sense of the person he stood in front of, but that shouldn't be an issue at some point.

"This is going to be fun..." Ashton said with a grin on his face. "Millie, stay up here and keep practicing, I'll be back in a few minutes."

"... Where are you going?"

"I'll go and play a prank on my best friend," Ashton said without hesitation, as he dropped through the rooftop's floor.
