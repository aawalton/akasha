“You will endure. You are strong enough.”

She — whoever she was — had been cast adrift. ‘Out.’ Into darkness, fled from whatever madness beckoned her to a ruin left of her source, her latest origin. She was parting from it, on to the next. Toward rest and peace. A calm, she knew, which came before more storms of chaos. She’d done it before; she’d do it again.

“No. We’ve been down that road so many times as to surpass the counting; we want it to end. We will end it. True peace; true rest. Take heart, Daughter. Endure, and as little as I can promise, this: you will be remembered. Loved. You have done well. Finish.”

Daughter?

Somehow, that piqued her attention and interest. A deep desire. Her mother was…

Suddenly, she was moving back down. In. But she spun around to see the source of the voice, at some impossible angle along the way, in a hidden place none could touch.

The void so familiar to her was more like an animate shadow, and it swayed and danced in the place between. She was aware only of eyes, eyes like her own so long ago, without color but familiar. A remnant reflection of who she was, but behind them was power and majesty. Everlasting energy. Infinity itself.

In her shock, she reached out and called, “Director?!” There was no response, but she knew. And despite whatever called her below, questions spilled forth. “What are you? What are we?!”

The eyes seemed amused. “The Mother of All. Creation itself, forming overgrown heads in conflict. Marred perfection; broken reflections of each other. Go, Samantha. Return once more.”

Samantha. Me.

She felt herself pulled quite strongly back, at that. But she furiously and doggedly fought to stay by that window, by that dark mirror, to ask the pragmatic, lest she never forgive herself. “How do I bring down the tower?!”

Musical laughter like chimes followed her, already fading. “Which? The one to challenge the sky, you already know: the triune collaboration. Matter, space, and movement, simple tricks for one who rules all bounded realms. Enforce your rule and see the final strike of Heaven…”

She came back to a curious state of twistedness and wholeness. She couldn’t open her eyes if she wanted to — by unclear mechanisms, blood was pouring from them, as well as her ears and nose. It was hot and steamed in the air. The body was otherwise whole.

The ‘body of the mind’ was healed and cleansed, yet the echo of damage and agony remained, as if a silent scream had just gone up and beyond, and was bouncing back. She felt as if she were sifted through a strainer into many droplets of her, acting as if their dispersion was wholeness. ‘She’ felt outside it, looking in on the catastrophe, unable to fully possess it.

She brought the numb collective quickly back to focus on the existing crisis, however. To do so, she was forced back into proper awareness as such things extended through the fractured mind. But removed from it, she saw things in starling clarity, like her prior self was an array, a matrix, of focusing lenses.

You thought to wound me. You did, but through destruction, I am made even stronger.

She knew exactly what she needed to do.

Though she couldn’t see her physically, she saw Azure’s spirit form before her as she knelt, yelling something or other with her hands touching Samantha’s cheeks.

“I’m okay, my love,” Samantha breathed, the words deathly hollow, though she technically meant them.

She felt another mental strike — it was unending — and the Minor Wound caused was like it happened to someone else. The pain didn’t touch her.

Azure’s breath was caught in a gasp. Samantha took her hand and stood with her, smoothly and steadily, as she commanded, “Lend me your strength, Azure.” She interlaced their fingers. “You are the [Divine Mesmer], the Chameleon, the [Perfect Reflection] — my aid in all things!”

“Yes,” Azure said shakily, but her hand stilled and tightened within Samantha’s grasp, as if the command were a lifeline she could cling to out of despair. She felt something like a crystalline latticework buttress around her and into her — the crystalline web of the entire universe branching into its goddess. It cradled her mind like a laminate preserving a rose.

User Azure has provided support through [Perfect Reflection: Chameleon]. Her [Divine Mesmer] class counts as a second sub-class, adding 1/3, rounded up (+4 Effective Class Levels) to any form of mental resistance.

It was just in time to help her completely resist the next mental strike; and the next.

I have endured, Director. Humpty-Dumpty is back together again.

“Dearest,” Samantha called softly.

“Yes, Chestnut?” Azure asked.

Samantha smiled coldly, cruelly. “Now it's our turn.”

“Indeed, it is.”

With a thought, Samantha unlocked something she’d always had — had even directly seen used when she’d unlocked it — but had been afraid to use. She’d never really wanted to, as the ‘war crimes’ feeling was too extreme. It was also useless against the undead. But the perfect situation had suddenly become evident.

It was a Channel Domain option. She’d watched it massacre thousands of soldiers in her first vision of Innogen. She’d only used the ‘Focus’ version.

It had none of the perks of (Focused), but was nonetheless terrifying to consider. Innogen had ripped out the souls of a whole army, using it specifically to prevent the raising of the undead.

Samantha reached out through the Sympathy she already had in supreme intensity, thanks to the constant mental bombardment…

When you reach out and touch somebody, guess what? They’re touching you.

She felt five hundred plus mentalists and wizards like little pinpoint souls spread across the lands north, their exact locations unclear, the animus of their magical assault otherwise making them flagrantly clear to her keen spiritual senses.

The base caster level was 15, though the bonuses on top of this were much higher. [Ascendant] alone was +4 now, to the will contest of [Removal]. It was well over 20, though it absolutely wouldn’t ‘get’ everyone.

It doesn’t matter.

She spent the 105 Fate FE and drained her Grant Prayer uses.

Channeling the Domain through Azure, she didn’t move except to tense as the power flooded into her and out across the distance, threads of Fate winding and weaving into the souls of many mortals and entangling, the goddess the puppet master, directly tapped into their core selves. She felt one consistent thing there, as she touched them all — as they became aware of what was happening.

Fear.

She declared coldly, “You tried and failed to usurp your rightful Mother. You are nothing to reality. Return to your proper place, absorbed as energy for something more useful.”

She ripped the ties all at once. Hundreds succumbed completely, stripped right out of reality, their souls cast on their way to the next life. Terrified screams filled her head like a cacophony of the damned, and it was like music to her. She breathed it in and felt tremendous satisfaction, because these damned certainly deserved the agony. Far worse, in truth.

The attacks ceased instantly as every ritual circle had been broken. Aside from that, the wounded and the rarer non-wounded fled like roaches suddenly cast upon with light, quickly using their tricks to scrub the Sympathy away and find a place to scurry and hide.

Samantha let the cowards scatter. She couldn’t do the mass attack again to begin with, and her purpose had been served — she had broken the empire’s trash collective of weak-minded mentalists, likely for good.

Beyond that, there was still more to do. As Azure sagged a bit and panted from the strain, Samantha turned her mind to the battle.

She could only feel shock, rage, and offense at the tragic disaster that had occurred in her absence…
