She trusts her.

Victoria had been standing at this coordinate for months, and Hathaway had finally arrived at it. Trusting Cecilia didn't mean trusting she would survive.

It meant trusting that, no matter how catastrophic the end, no matter how deep the water, she would never allow her own collapse to drag the people she loved down with her.

Hathaway folded the paper, tucked it back into her coat, and finished her coffee.
