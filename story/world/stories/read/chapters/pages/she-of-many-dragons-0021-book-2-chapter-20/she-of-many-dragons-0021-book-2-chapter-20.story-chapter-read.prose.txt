"Alice," Prim said a few days later as they were resting in the evening.

Alice had found the Protectorate library, learned to check out books, and was slowly but surely discovering a love of reading... though she had to stop frequently to look up words she did not know. Having the ability to lose herself in a novel felt luxurious, like something only nobles with free time were allowed to do. She was determined that within a few months, she would be reading as well as anyone here. She often spent her evenings in her room like this, nose close to the pages.

"Yes, dear?" she asked, turning from her book to scribble out yet another word that she would have to look up later. In truth, she was only half listening to her dragon.

Prim's next words captured her full attention. "I believe it would be best if I swallowed the illusion stone."

Alice put down the book and blinked at her dragon. She must have heard incorrectly. "What was that?"

"The stone that sorceress Samantha gave you to train the others," Prim reminded her. "I have studied the illusions it can create, and I believe the magic will incorporate very well with my own. In fact, it will likely give me a boost to my own power. I have taken my illusion powers to the limits of their capabilities. This will provide me the boost I need. " She flipped her wings along her back as if she had made all the points she needed, and the natural answer was going to be 'yes'.

Alice stared at her, speechless. "You want to swallow the stone? Can you?" The stone was as large as Alice's fist.

"Well, she could technically do it," Numi piped up. "I have assessed it as a consumable object. Of course, if no one uses it then eventually its store of mana will run out." She squinted at the stone, which lay on Alice's desk when not in use at the training ground. "I believe that it is halfway empty already."

"And once it is run dry, Alice will be forced to come to Samantha to ask for a recharge," Iggy growled.

Spark, who sat by his side as loyal as ever, chirped in agreement.

"Yes," Numi agreed. "And I doubt she'll do it for free."

Alice had considered that once or twice, but she had set it aside as a worry for later. She was still trying to wrap her mind around Prim's odd request.

Turning, she looked at her dragon, who had certainly grown over the last few weeks due to the influx of good food. But with a bit of balance, she still fit on Alice's shoulder. Prim was easily her most delicate dragon, with her thin pointed head, long S-shaped neck, and rosy golden-tipped wings.

"It cannot be safe for you to swallow something of that size," she said, still halfway wondering if this was a joke. "That stone is larger than your head, dearest."

"It is safe enough," Prim said, entirely unconcerned. "If there is any issue, a healer can surely remove it."

Bella piped up. "If you die, can I use your body as an burnt sacrifice?"

Prim turned to her, disgusted. "No! Why would you want to do something like that?"

"Because your energy would go to me. It's not like you'd be using it anymore. And of course, I would use that energy to serve and protect Alice."

Prim's mouth snapped shut and she cocked her head, obviously thinking it over.

That alarmed Alice. "Prim, you are not going to die. If, and I mean if, you do this, you will be swallowing it in the presence of a healer." She had been exploring the entirety of the Protectorate building over the last week and had located the healers' lounge. They were quite high-ranked and valuable classers and were given one of the best floors in the building.

Unfortunately, it had been a short, rather unpleasant visit, as the healers were all of noble rank and seemed to share the same distaste for Alice and her dragons as Samantha and the rest of the mage-type classers.

Well, it did not matter. She did not care what the cost was—if Prim was in trouble or in pain, she would pay anything for the surgery to get a stone out.

That was when she realized she was truly considering Prim's bizarre request.

"There is no need for a healer. She won't come to any harm. I eat stones all the time, see?" Numi reached into her mouth and pulled out the bloodstone that she had picked up in the forest.

"You retrieved that from your storage space," Alice said. "That is much different."

Upon seeing the ruby red stone, Bella cooed and crept closer, her dark eyes wide. "Where did you get that?"

"From that terrible forest, before your egg hatched." Prim seemed to realize that the conversation was getting off track. "Alice, you know that I would never do something that would harm you. And consuming something that would kill me, your first dragon, certainly would harm you. I would not take that chance. But I do believe that this will strengthen my illusionary powers even more than simple leveling up could."

"You cannot believe that all these nobles simply reach the heights that they have with their rare classes only through hard work," Iggy rumbled. "We are usually the only ones using the training rooms at all."

That stopped Alice short. He had a point, and one that she had never considered before. She had just assumed that nobles with rare classes simply spawned more nobles with rare classes. Certainly, that did help, but... it did not explain how powerful they had gotten without seeming to outwardly train.

Alice looked around at her dragons to see what they thought of this. Natura had not joined in on the conversation. She was busy pruning a tiny tree in a pot that she called a bonsai. She had been leveling well over the last few days and had settled into the city, even if her main focus was beautifying it.

Bella looked all for the idea, but that was likely because she was hoping to create another burnt sacrifice out of Prim's corpse.

Numi met her gaze steadily and seemed perfectly certain that this would be safe. She was the most logical out of all of Alice's dragons and would certainly stand up to Prim and tell her it was a bad idea, no matter if it annoyed her or not.

Iggy was the last holdout. He seemed uncertain, tapping his long claws against the top of the wooden desk.

"Iggy, what do you think?" Alice asked when he stayed silent.

"If Prim’s illusions were better, she could help train Bella as we would not need to use the training hall exclusively. But mostly, I do not like for you to have to be dependent on Samantha. She does not like you, and that tells me she does not have your best interests at heart."

Alice did not want to make an important decision based on the dislike of others. However, something inside told her that people like Samantha were the enemy.

That brought up another good point. "Then what shall I tell Samantha if she asks where this incredibly expensive illusion stone went?"

"I doubt she will ask," Prim said. "She has not checked in with you once yet, and if she ever does, you could tell her you found another way to charge it with mana. Based on her attitude, she seems like she would be happy not to have to deal with you again."

So would a lot of people here, Alice thought with a sigh, but she kept that to herself.

And she found that she had made her decision. "Okay, yes, then I agree. But Prim, dearest, if you feel the least bit of discomfort, I will take you to the healers. No argument."

"Yes, Alice," she said equitably.

Alice hesitated for a final moment, wondering if she was doing the right thing, but then grabbed up the illusion stone and held it out to her dragon.

Prim took it in her long finger claws, then, startlingly, seemed to unhinge her jaw like a snake. She swallowed the large stone down in one gulp, and Alice saw it as a lump traveling down her long, sinuous throat.

Prim shivered. "Well, that was interesting," she said as proper as any fine lady, even though she had just swallowed the thing like a snake would a large rat.

"How are you feeling?" Alice asked immediately.

Prim half-closed her eyes as if she were looking inside herself. After a long moment, she answered, "It is warm inside. I believe that my body is refilling its mana storage and trying to incorporate its magic."

Suddenly, her rosy scales flashed with an inner light. The gold highlights along her wings, down her spine, and neck seemed to glow. That glow traveled upward to her eyes.

"Oh my," Prim said.

A moment later, Alice received a notification.

Aspect 1 Primordialis  “Prim” has incorporated ‘Basic Combat Illusion Stone’.

Do you wish to merge this skill with Primordialis’s Illusion skill or create a new skill?

Alice looked to Prim. “I believe that merged skills are supposed to be more powerful.”

“I trust your judgement,” Prim said easily.

Alice just hoped that her trust wasn’t misplaced. With a mental push, she urged the stone to merge with Prim’s Concealment Skill.

Success!

Aspect 1 Primordialis  “Prim” has evolved a skill!

Illusion - Tier 2 Journeyman Rank

The skill flashed and the glow faded, leaving Prim looking extremely pleased with herself. Alice wanted to immediately ask what the new evolution of her skill meant—and especially find out if it was worth the risk of using the stone—but instinct held her back. Through her connection with Prim, she felt that the dragon was still incorporating the skill. She would be able to find out what this new skill was about soon.

Meanwhile, Bella turned to Numi. "What other stones do you have in your stomach?"

"Well, as I said, I have this bloodstone." Once again, Numi held it up, and the bloodstone glinted like a drop of blood in the lamplight.

"I want it," Bella said.

"You will not have any extra magic before I get a turn, young one." Iggy easily pushed Bella aside and gave the bloodstone a considering sniff. Instantly, he recoiled. "On second thought, you can have it."

"Is something wrong with it?" Alice asked.

"No." He shook his head ponderously. "I can just tell that it will not be compatible with my magic."

"Oh, I have others." Numi stuck her whole hand in her mouth and seemed to be shuffling around in her stomach storage space, looking for the right thing. Then she pulled out a very small opalescent stone that Alice only vaguely remembered. "What about this pearlescent stone? I feel like it will match with you."

Bella gave it a long look as if she were considering it herself instead of the bloodstone, but Iggy immediately snatched it from Numi. He sniffed and then purred. "This will work perfectly."

"Wait," Alice said. "Numi, how many magical stones have you been swallowing?"

"It's my storage space," her dragon said, which was no answer at all.

Alice was getting a bad feeling. "Have you been stealing them?"

"No, I didn't steal either of these," Numi said, still not really answering the question. "Remember that strongbox we found in the general store? The pearlescent stone came from there."

Iggy and Bella looked like they were about to gulp the blood and pearlescent stones down, greedy for more power.

"Wait," Alice said. "Before anyone swallows any more strange magical stones, perhaps we should wait a moment and see how Prim does with her own."

"I am perfectly fine," Prim said, then burped sparkles.

All of her dragons, with the exception of Natura—who still did not seem to be paying attention—turned to Alice with big, pleading eyes.
