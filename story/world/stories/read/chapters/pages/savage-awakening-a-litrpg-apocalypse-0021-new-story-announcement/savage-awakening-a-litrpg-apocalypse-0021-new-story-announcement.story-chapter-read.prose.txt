Hey folks, I’ve launched a new story! It’s called Awakening in the Altered World.

Rather than having a System Apocalypse happen immediately like in Savage, in this world it happened 25 years ago with the arrival with rifts, towers, and a few other world-altering aspects. Except this version is much better contained by the Awakened, so cities and the internet and such still exist—think Isekai-ing a Solo Leveling-like setting.

The MC, Alex, wakes up in a world where Awakened are 1% of society, he Awakens a few very high talents.

If it sounds like it could be your thing, check it out! I'm very excited about this one.
