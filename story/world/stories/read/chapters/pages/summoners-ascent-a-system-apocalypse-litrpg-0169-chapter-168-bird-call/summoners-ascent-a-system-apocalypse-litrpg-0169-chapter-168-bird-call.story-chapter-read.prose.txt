Ashton chugged two potions, one after the other. With this, he had fully used up all that he had made of both the stamina and the mental recovery potion. He had to make some more when he could after waking up in a few days.

He took a deep breath and shook the headache away, as Rory entered his channelling range.

"You alright, lad?" he asked. He must have seen that Ashton was swaying pretty hard just now. The exhaustion set in really damn fast, and he almost wasn't able to make it before the Backpacker gave him the potions.

"I'm fine," Ashton replied with a smile. "What about you, I hope those three left without causing any further trouble."

"They did. But I must admit... I'm not quite sure about what happened there."

"Hm? Oh, yeah, I just have a history with them, don't worry about it."

Rory frowned lightly. "A history that made you take away that one lad's armour?"

"I mean hey, he locked me out of my own flat while I was butt-naked once. I think that makes us even," Ashton laughed. "But really, don't worry. The armour was barely even useful to him. His skills were enough to counteract the negative parts of wearing an armour like this. That piece wasn't made for actual combat, so it's not all that sturdy. He'll be better off without it."

Rory didn't seem particularly convinced, but Ashton didn't really care. There was something else that was much more important for them to talk about.

"I'm going to leave again soon. Within the next ten or so minutes when these potions fully kick in. I'm going to take care of a few last things, and then I'm going to go back to the camp I'm staying at and will sleep for a pretty long time," Ashton started, though he could tell that Rory was a bit confused about that. "I've been forcing myself to stay awake at peak condition for the past three days, and the one moment of rest that I had was when I was briefly laying on the ground while a demon ripped my eyes out, so I should sleep for a day or two at least. And when I wake up, I'm going to come back and I will take Millie with me."

Even more confused, Rory straightened his back. "Millicent? Why'd you want to take her with you?"

Ashton scoffed. "A boy threw a rock at her earlier. If I hadn't caught it, it would have cracked her skull open. She's not safe here. I can justify her staying for a day or two longer, but not a minute more than she needs to."

"Lad, I know that people are a bit... uncomfortable around her, but that's going to pass with time! She's such a bright young lass, I know that people will–"

"Rory. She doesn't just need people that will tolerate her. She's a kid. In this world, Millie will need a 'Guardian'. Adults that will take responsibility for her," Ashton said clearly. And he knew that this was earth, and not Lumia, but... "I've seen one too many children that were left behind when it mattered the most, for more petty reasons than fear."

"And you say you will be this 'Guardian'?"

"Yes."

"Why? She was a stranger just a couple days ago, wasn't she?"

Ashton didn't quite know how to answer that. He didn't want to reveal the truth about Millie's ability to Rory, but he didn't know what else to really say.

"Because... she's special, in a way that will make other people and even constellations go after her if they ever find out. And I believe that I'm the only one that can really protect her. Plus..." A bitter smile appeared on Ashton's face. "I know what it's like to be a kid without any adults looking out for them."

Rory let out a long sigh. "When you say it like that, I can't very well say 'no', huh? Does the lass want to come with you as well?"

"I spoke about it with her earlier, yeah. For now, she's going to stay here with a class that I've already picked out with her, and work with everyone here at the school to get a feel for how to use skills and have her level up a tiny bit. And then, when I'm back next time, she'll come with me. I promise, I will look out for her."

The older man patted Ashton's back before turning away. "Well, alright then." He walked a few steps, then turned around briefly again. "I trust you, Ashton. You've done enough to prove that ye've got the best for these kids in mind. But if I hear that you can't take care of her properly, I will bring her back in."

Ashton smiled. "Wouldn't want it any other way."

And with that, Ashton had just one more thing left to do before heading out. He only had four more hours, at best; he had to hurry the hell up.

Ashton made his way over to where the three kids were running around earlier. It didn't take long for them to rush up to him again, either.

"You're really gonna go already?" Sean asked, disappointed. He probably wanted to play with the Drunken Bear a bit more.

"Sadly, I have to. But I will be back in a few days," Ashton replied, before turning to Millie. He couldn't see anymore, but he should at least keep up the illusion for Sean and Joey. "And until then, make sure to work with the others to get the class we picked out for you. I'll help you switch it when I'm back, but try to get a good feel for mana until then, okay?"

Millie happily nodded. "Yessir!"

"You three, make sure not to get into too much trouble while I'm gone. And you two..." Ashton turned to Sean and Joey. "... you better make sure that nobody messes with Millie. And while I couldn't exactly do much against that kid earlier..."

Sean and Joey looked at each other and grinned. They knew exactly where Ashton was going with that. Of course, he still wanted them to stay out of trouble, though that was mostly supposed to mean 'do not get badly hurt while I am gone'. Whether they got into a fight or two probably wouldn't matter too much. He would leave them with some potions, just in case.

"Alright. See you kids later." Ashton smiled, and Millie chuckled slightly to herself.

With this, there were really just two more things for him to do before Ashton could finally sleep and leave behind these terribly long past few days.

Ashton left the schoolgrounds, being seen off by Rory and the kids, and almost immediately had Poppy cast her speed spell on him before starting to run. This would exhaust him slightly faster, but that was fine. It was more efficient to do this, anyway.

The first stop was going to be the last Marked that Ashton had to take care of, though this one was probably a little bit annoying. Especially for Ashton the way he was right now.

There was a post in the community talking about how someone's friend had gotten 'sick'. There were some updates in the comments of the original post, explaining how that friend was slowly changing into something inhuman. Growing feathers, their toes turning into talons... until finally, the posts stopped.

Ashton had a pretty good idea of what happened. The poster's friend was a Marked that turned into some avian monster. They would probably appear and act like some sort of harpy.

But from the way that person's traits had been described, Ashton had a pretty good idea of what they had turned into. And in that case, it would actually be pretty easy to lure them over to where Ashton was once he got to the right area.

It was just twenty or so minutes away from Market Street, so it was actually a good idea to take care of this fast before the marked wandered over there.

And once he got there, he called on the sylph, and started to take control of the air right in front of Ashton's mouth.

Essentially, he was doing the same thing that he had done to trick that lizardman while infiltrating the costco; he was artificially altering his voice with magic. This was a rather crude way, but it should work. Back in Lumia, he was actually able to change his voice in various ways just by manipulating the muscles of his larynx. That was a pretty neat trick, that Ashton would hopefully learn again. It could really come in useful. There were limits to what could be done by manipulating the air.

Especially because Ashton had finally chosen the sylph's advancement earlier. They had left the choice to Ashton, just as the others had, though he could tell what the elemental really wanted.

The two main options that seemed promising went into completely different directions. One of them allowed for more precise manipulation of the air. Simply put, if used right, it would have been useful to suppress sounds and manipulate air precisely the way that he had to exactly right now.

But the other one, the one that the sylph was actually leaning toward, was the complete opposite. Essentially, it increased the ferocity of the sylph's wind. It changed its basic nature, rather than just adding an additional ability like with the undine and wisp. In exchange for simply being 'wilder' and harder to control, the wind was stronger and it was possible to manipulate much more than before.

It was useful for combat, extremely actually, so Ashton had little reason to complain, but he might have actually preferred the increased precision since it would have been a lot more versatile.

But with the right level of sensitivity, it was still possible to manipulate things with solid precision, it was just a bit harder.

Ashton took a deep breath, and then pushed a whistle through his hands to form a bird-call. But with the air magic, it was shifted just right in the way that he needed, and soon, the flutter of a particular sort sounded out.

Just a few moments later, carrion crows from the area came flying right at Ashton. They were easy to take down of course; carrion crows weren't particularly strong. Just in this case, there were many of them.

Ashton continued walking through the area, repeating the same call again and again. The carrion crows came to him, some with chunks of flesh or even full fingers hanging out of their four-way, toothed beaks, but were just as quickly dispatched.

The sylph's new magic was actually pretty useful for that. The more fervent wind could knock down flying opponents with ease, and once the crows were on the ground, they could be crushed before recovery was even an option.

He attracted other monsters too, of course. Some goblins, a few lizardmen... anything could hear these calls and would come to check out the source, so it was getting a little annoying. But before long, his target was finally approaching.

"There we go," Ashton said with a sigh, as a figure stepped into his channelling range. They had long, thin legs and talons digging into the ground. Their arms were hanging loosely off their shoulders, as if this person never learned how to use them in the first place. And on their back, in an almost angelic way, were two pitch black wings hanging off them.

It was actually quite a pretty view. If it weren't for the four-split mouth with that deep toothy maw they were immediately showing to Ashton, he might have even thought they were pretty.

Anyway, it was about time for Ashton to finally finish the Executor's quest.
