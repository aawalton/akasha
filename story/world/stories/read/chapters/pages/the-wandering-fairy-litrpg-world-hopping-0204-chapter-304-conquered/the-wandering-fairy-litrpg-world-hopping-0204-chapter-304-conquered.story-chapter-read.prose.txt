There was truth to Mordred’s claims.

Soren couldn’t help but recall everything that had transpired during their confrontation against His Living Shadow. Its actions before he broke out of the banquet hall’s time loops were certainly different from how it acted afterwards. At least, according to the memories Biblion had transferred to him.

At the time, Soren had simply guessed that the mechanisms behind His actions were likely related to Morgan. That was why he decided to declare war on the Black Thorned Knight to begin with, introducing a new variable for His actions that would go against the previous directives Morgan likely issued to Him.

Of course, this was only possible due to the knowledge [Eyes of the Fairy] granted him after their initial meeting once the time loops were ended. Within that broken and chaotic data forming His newly created Status Page, was an ability whose condition could only be described as strange:

“[The Siege] - A fortress must never fall before its enemies.”

Although these skill descriptors were always written in an annoyingly vague fashion by Biblion, the raw knowledge he had obtained through [Eyes of the Fairy] gave Soren far more context than it could ever chronicle in words.

Namely, this extra information was related to who these supposed ‘enemies’ could be… And the answer to that was rather simple: anyone and everyone who would dare to challenge the Traitorous Fortress!

Simply put, the mere declaration or desire to conquer Mordred’s Soul Weapon provides [The Siege] the conditions it needs to activate automatically. After all, a fortress must never fall before its enemies…

This ability provided Mordred with the power to bend space, enlarging His presence on the battlefield in comparison to His enemies, scaling His physical power in the process with no drawbacks in agility or dexterity. Meaning, the enlarged Mordred would be able to move as if He was exactly the same size as His original self!

However, it did come with a single flaw. One that has an impact on His psyche.

Cassia had told Soren about this once before during their brief group study period in the underground library. Augmented Class Soul Weapons like hers or Mordred’s tend to sometimes manifest skills that contain a heightened spark of the original Abstract Rune’s will. These abilities could, at times, overpower the host user and force them into performing certain actions they may not have consented to.

For Cassia, merely using [Serpent Transformation] elevates some of her more primal emotions to a heightened degree, leading to potential frenzied or animalistic actions. She would begin to view her enemies similar to how a serpent views its prey.

In Mordred’s case, however, this effect could only be described as horrifying…

[The Siege] forces Him to obliterate His enemies the moment His Soul Weapon discovers a potential challenger. There could be no option of surrender or acceptance of defeat. If anyone—even an elderly man or child—were to exhibit even an iota of ambition against the Black Thorned Knight, death was the only way to settle the difference.

Of course, the host could attempt to deny the exerted will of the Abstract Rune responsible, but that would eventually lead to instability in their Soul Chain. As for a Living Shadow who exists purely on instincts, such an option would be even more improbable.

This was why Soren devised this plan. He knew that the only way to temporarily break Mordred away from Morgan’s control would be through the introduction of another manipulative force. In this case, that force happened to be the shadowed existence of His Abstract Runes and their influence over their host. By declaring war directly, the Black Thorned Knight had no choice but to fight the two Magi without holding anything back, leaving Camilla free to deal with Morgan who was still trapped in the Throne of Glass.

I wonder how she’s doing right now…

Since they had been dragged into Mordred’s inner castle, there was no way for them to know what was occurring outside of this shadowed realm. Of course, Soren had been anticipating Mordred’s usage of [The Spire] as well. Every one of his actions in coordination with Ynixia and Albert were designed to lead Mordred’s Living Shadow into using that Soul Weapon skill.

“To think you would even use [The Spire] against me,” Mordred sighed. “Merlin was correct—a prepared Magi will always be more venomous than any deadly thorn.”

Soren smiled. “Zhuge Liang’s Empty Fort strategy… It’s a well known war tactic from Earth that originates from a book written several centuries after you and your cohort left for the promised land. The idea was fairly simple: invite your enemy into your fortress willingly, making them suspicious of a trap that may not even exist.

“Of course in our case, the situation was slightly different. [The Spire] didn’t exactly give us a choice on whether we should enter the fortress or not, after all.” The Traveler shrugged then shook his head. “So I simply decided to flip Zhuge Liang’s strategy on its head. Rather than fearing an empty fort possessing a trap, your Living Shadow began to fear us—its enemies—of being the ones who possessed one. Throughout that entire fight, it was constantly considering whether or not to utilize [The Spire] to drag us inside its limitless void.”

Mordred didn’t shift—only His lips slightly curled upwards. “It was those mirror fragments, wasn’t it?”

“Correct,” the Traveler nodded towards HIm. “Although your Living Shadow was running on pure instincts, that did not exactly make it completely unintelligent. It was still the Living Shadow of an actual Monarch, after all. It understood that my mystic eyes were able to grasp information about its abilities and flaws. There was no denying it—using [The Spire] early on would have been far too risky from its point of view.”

And that analysis wasn’t really wrong. As Mordred had guessed, those Mirror Fragments were indeed special. Each explosion from the Traitorous Fortress’ bombardments targeting his clones caused more and more of those tiny mirror shards to spread across the battlefield.

Unbeknownst to the Black Thorned Knight, this was simply another added effect of the Fractured Reflections spell that it inherited from a Ward affinity runes he had previously accumulated.

With these shards, Soren could then recast the Fractured Reflections spell at half the anima cost, rebuilding his illusive clones with the scattered pieces of the previous iteration of the spell. This added not only another layer to the illusion, but also gave Soren a way to preserve his anima during longer battles.

However, it also came with an additional, hidden benefit. One that was related to a different 3rd Circle spell he had created with the help of Biblion:

Mirror Shard Fulguration.

If he had to be honest, this one was somewhat strange—in fact, they were only able to create it by accident.

When Biblion conducted testing of the new material runes they had obtained, there was one that intrigued both of them very deeply. A simple rune which they named Voltis. The effect of this Ward affinity rune was rather simple: it allowed magi to manipulate their anima into behaving similarly to electricity.

Strangely, the anima itself doesn’t actually change into electricity. It simply mimicked its behaviors in a limited fashion—especially in relation to the properties of conductivity.

Through numerous trial and error, Soren and Biblion combined this rune with the other runes he had acquired, creating a spell unlike anything else within the Mesmerism affinity…

A spell that could conjure controllable lightning that was made entirely from an illusion!

“You probably understand how we’re able to be here, right?” Soren chuckled. “After all, it should technically be impossible for us to be alive right now. With your Living Shadow’s Mythical Incarnation active, mutations and other biological changes would quickly take shape the moment [The Spire] began to pull us closer to the Fortress.

“And, in a way, that threat didn’t exactly vanish completely.”

Soren glanced down at his palm—watching small shards of metal coagulate across his mangled skin, slowly attempting to burrow inside his lacerated flesh. He smiled then closed his fist, glancing back up at the old warrior sitting before him. “If it weren’t for my spell activating at the last second, Ynixia and I would truly have been finished.”

As the name implied, Mirror Shard Fulguration required mirror shards to fully function. The ‘electrified’ Mesmerism anima that the spell generated could only pass through such a medium—that was how its conductive property operated.

This was why the Fractured Reflection spell was important. Every clone the Traitorous Fortress destroyed only created more shards, spreading Mirror Shard Fulguration’s effective range.

Of course, Soren’s Soul Realm was still a factor in this equation too. But because of how twisted the space was within that battlefield, the colossal size Mordred eventually took due to [The Siege’s] passive effect only managed to work in Soren’s favor.

“After it absorbed all those shards alongside Ynixia and I, its fate had already been sealed. The protective outside armor of its Mythical Incarnation and Soul Weapon could no longer act as shielding for the spell’s attack. And since the ‘electrified’ anima of Mirror Shard Fulguration dealt psychological damage rather than physical, your Living Shadow quickly lost control over its Mythical Incarnation and other abilities.”

Soren couldn’t help but laugh slightly. “I’m pretty proud of myself, you know? I poured all my anima into that spell, making sure there was enough illusive electricity to cover your entire body. Over a million mirror shards were used in the process too…

“Even now, it remains in slumber—though I don’t think it will last for that long…”

Hearing all of this, Mordred remained as motionless as before. Though, there was indeed a tinge of emotion across His face that couldn’t be hidden. He eventually sighed, shaking His head slowly.

“To think I—the Black Thorned Knight who’s never lost a single war throughout my entire life… would be defeated by a pair of children.”

Ynixia’s cheeks flushed red. “U-Uncle… It was your Living Shadow…”

“So what?” His lips curled into a smile. “That is still a valiant achievement. As for you…” Soren felt His gaze directly, despite the old warrior continuing to stare at the cold lake. It was as if the surrounding darkness itself was watching him with growing hunger:

“Your wits cannot be denied—this plan of yours is truly elaborate. But it does take a level of recklessness unseen by any Magi from my time to even dare fulfill it… I don’t believe even a bastard like Merlin would attempt something similar.

“Tell me. Are you truly seeking your own demise?”

For a moment, the eerie silence of this eternal void had quickly returned. Soren stared at His statue-like figure without any changes in his expression. Though… the same couldn’t be said about his emotions which seemed to fluctuate like the wind.

Ynixia on the other hand felt somewhat embarrassed. It was as if Mordred was indirectly scolding her for even deciding to go along with a plan devised by such a lunatic. She decided to look away in shame.

Sensing the growing silence, Soren simply sighed then shook his head. “It wasn’t really that exciting of a plan. I’ve certainly had more reckless gambles in the past.” He glanced at the floating enchanted book beside him and smiled.

“Indeed, your Living Shadow was certainly aware of my prying. A clone mimicking your experiences would definitely conclude that its flaws could be in danger of being exploited—practicing precaution with [The Spire] would be a rather obvious choice for any warrior at your level. Even I would have made a similar choice if placed in its shoes.

“However…” Soren’s gaze fell once more on the jaded warrior sitting plainly at the edge of the shadowed shore, “I would guess its biggest concern was you all along. The Fragmented Inhabitant it was sheltering inside its shadowed fortress walls…”

Mordred didn’t say anything, but that practically confirmed Soren’s suspicions anyway. His Living Shadow consuming its own Fragmented Self was in a way, something the original Mordred had to have consented to. For whatever reason, this legendary knight had decided to seal Himself away here—forgetting even His own memories in the process.

And the Living Shadow was always aware of His presence. From the very beginning, its biggest fear with using [The Spire] wasn’t just the risk of Soren possessing a trap that could exploit that ability’s weaknesses. The biggest risk was the opportunity it would provide that allows them to contact the real Mordred sealed within this abyss.

In that moment, a part of him couldn’t help but sigh.

Glancing at Ynixia who still seemed to be consumed by her own thoughts, Soren simply shook his head and walked over to where the jaded old man sat, deciding to sit beside him. The two of them watched the soft waves crash slowly against the abyssal shores—neither spoke for a while.

“Since Ynixia and I were aware that your Living Shadow would likely remain hesitant in using [The Spire], we had to make sure every other contingency plan it possessed was cut off first, forcing it into making that dire choice.” The Traveler’s mystic eyes observed the ripples forming across the lake’s vibrant blue surface with a tinge of intrigue.

“Of course during that time, the thought of us dragging the fight toward such a conclusion would naturally make it suspicious of a trap. But there was also a chance it was all simply a bluff too. With the fight dragging on, such psychological pressures would make any warrior crumble, even if only by a small margin.” He turned toward Mordred’s statue-like figure—frenzied flames of madness sparkling within his irises as he smiled:

“Those small mistakes were all we needed to draw the siege to its natural conclusion. By that point, whether we had a trap prepared or not was no longer in the calculation. It had no choice but to rely on its final trump card. Unfortunately, its suspicions did turn out right in the end—the inverted Empty Fort strategy I employed was never a bluff from the start.

“This war… it ends with our triumph. Neither you nor your Soul Weapon can deny this truth—The Traitorous Fortress has once again been conquered.”
