Skippy’s grin broadened as he looked first at the dead hodags, then at the crater left by Model: Cannon.  “You’ve gotten much stronger.”

“As have you,” Kyle said, feeling the incredibly powerful aura rippling around the skrell.  From everything Kyle could tell, Skippy was a generational talent at the same order as Arhades.  A key difference was that while the velgian was raised up with a huge investment of resources and precise training, Skippy was raised up through violence and combat.  Something  for which the skrell had a unique talent.

“This seems like as good a place as any for a quick match,” Skippy continued, jumping off the stone he’d been resting on to land lightly near Kyle.  “Should we set ground rules, or anything goes?”“Whoa now,” Kyle said, raising his hand with the palm out.  “I’m not saying a spar wouldn’t be fun, but I still have to report in to Duroc.  And if you’re here, I’m guessing Benjamin is as well?  Do you really want to leave him alone?”

Skippy’s smile turned sinister.  “You’re ahead of schedule, and Benjamin is with Duroc as an invited guest.”

“You know as well as anybody that my skills aren’t well-designed for sparring,” Kyle tried, the argument falling flat even to him as he saw the glimmer in Skippy’s eyes.

“Just do your best,” the skrell said, his right hand a blur of motion as it lashed out to strike Kyle right in the chest.

Kyle was too slow to react, being knocked back nearly a dozen meters by the force of the blow.  Fortunately, the attack seemed more designed to push than do any serious damage, with the wood of the World Tree Seed holding up just fine against the force.  What it did accomplish was serving as the starting bell for their match.

With a quick sigh, Kyle activated Genesis Engine and Aura Force.  Skippy was already closing in for another attack, telltale flames swirling around his palms.  The skrell was moving too quickly for Kyle to release a precisely-targeted blast of force, so he instead created a wave of repulsion, throwing Skippy away as he created more distance for himself.

Model: Shield was drawn out of storage, as was a weapon Kyle hadn’t had a chance to use yet.  A long staff, carved of dark wood rested comfortably in Kyle’s right hand.  One side was sharpened to a spear point, while the other had a bulb of tightly-wound crimson petals.  It was a prototype; more a proof of concept for the other staff Kyle had been painstakingly working on, but a match like this was the perfect opportunity to test it out.

Skippy did a quick flip in the air, landing on his feet and launching himself at Kyle once again.  This time, Kyle used Aura Force to lift himself off the ground, while releasing a wide-area blast straight down.  Skippy was caught in the domain, and was slammed into the rocky ground below.  Hard.  Stones cracked under the pressure, and Kyle used Cultivate to release a set of stranglevine seeds directly toward the prone skrell, willing them to grow and bind his opponent.

As flames swirled around Skippy, Kyle understood that ending the match wouldn’t be that simple.  The skrell responded by using his own boosting skill, and the grasping vines were batted away with ease as the gray form shot toward him like a rocket, the flames wreathing him focused upward to counteract the downward pressure from Aura Force.  That meant, however, that Skippy was entirely unprepared for the domain to shift.  Kyle turned the force to push directly sideways, while also pivoting himself the opposite direction to create space.  In terms of sheer explosive speed, Skippy had Kyle outmatched, but Aura Force gave Kyle a clear advantage in midair maneuverability.

The Pugilist was undeterred as he landed gracefully, exploding back toward Kyle in a straight line.  Kyle projected a plane of force low in an attempt to trip the incoming gray meteor, but the moment he hit it, Skippy vanished.  Synaptic Barbs warned Kyle about the sudden appearance of a skrell-shaped objected approaching from behind him, and he ducked low to avoid a flaming palm strike.

Too slow.

The punch caught him in the C.H.A.D.D. pack, knocking Kyle toward the ground, even if it didn’t do any meaningful damage to the high-tier C Grade construction.  Kyle countered with a wide shockwave of force, which Skippy blocked with crossed arms as he pursued.  The skrell was hardly pushed back at all this time, something about the fire surrounding him disrupting the blast.  Just as he attempted to reengage, Kyle spun the staff in his hand, surrounding himself in a veil of highly potent sundew acid.

Skippy’s palm struck the veil, an audible sizzle coming through as the acid burned him, buying Kyle enough time to interpose Model: Shield between himself and the strike.  A gentle push with Aura Force kept the acidic mist away from his own body while obscuring him behind the cloud.  Using the opening, Kyle launched the deep red sundew petals after Skippy in an attempt to bind him, reducing the potency of the acid in exchange for a thick, syrupy sap.

Unfortunately for Kyle, the petals were too slow to catch Skippy.  What they did accomplish was creating space.  Condensing the mist of acid around him with Aura Force, Kyle blasted it forward while taking to the sky once again.  Skippy sidestepped the sizzling liquid, and Kyle saw him grinning maniacally as power gathered around his right hand.  A shockwave of power was released a moment later, and Kyle interposed Model: Shield, using Infuse to empower it even further as he furiously channeled the concept of Immutability.

The blast scorched the wood of the prosthetic, leaving deep cracks and gouges across it.  Marcus had recommended some changes to the shield’s shape to help deflect the worst of attacks like these, and if he hadn’t made those shifts Kyle was confident the whole arm would’ve been broken off.  He didn’t have much time to reflect, however, as Skippy appeared beneath him, his hand glowing with the telltale aura of his finishing combo.

“You’ve gotta be-“ Kyle began, being cut off as he had to block the first strike with Model: Shield, causing the prosthetic to splinter as Kyle was knocked further skyward.  Skippy leapt to pursue, and Kyle countered with a storm of vines that flew out of the spear-tip of his staff.  Many were simply burned away by Skippy’s blazing aura, but they obscured the real attack; invisible tendrils of Parasitic Resonance.

With a stern mental warning to Phaegos, Kyle connected with the tendrils, just enough to disrupt the mana flowing into the attack.  It didn’t stop him from getting punched in the chest, nor did it stop the massive explosion of mana that knocked them both backward.  But it did prevent Skippy from moving on to the third step and beyond, something he had no doubt the skrell fighter was capable of doing.

Kyle raised his palm, panting as he spoke.  “We’re calling it here.  We keep going, one or both of us are going to have some pretty nasty injuries.”

“But I didn’t even get to test myself against your upgraded shield,” Skippy pouted.

“If it makes you feel any better, the shield hasn’t been upgraded yet,” Kyle supplied.  “Maybe we can set up a rematch once it has.”

Kyle was surprised at how much he meant that.  Even this brief spar was very helpful for him, even if Skippy did get a bit carried away toward the end.  Getting real combat experience against somebody who outclassed him in speed the way the Pugilist could was invaluable.  Learning what worked and what didn’t would only set him up better for the challenges he’d invariably face as he pursued his path.

“I’m going to hold you to that, Kyle,” Skippy replied, and Kyle noted that his aura was still perfectly stable, despite using a skill that carried incredible backlash just a few short years ago.

Together, they resumed the trip to Duroc’s village.  No more hodags appeared on C.H.A.D.D.’s radar, and they made quick time.  Along the way, Skippy brought Kyle up to speed on some of his adventures since the disaster on Kertan Six.  After recovering, he took a leave of absence while Benjamin was back in Corthian territory for Malcolm’s funeral.  He used it to travel to non-Collective space to some well-known hunting grounds, where he made the push to C Grade.  He’d only returned from the trip a couple of months before Kyle’s survival had become widespread news.

Soon, the outskirts of Duroc’s village came into sight.  The architecture was odd, the entire village looking almost more like a termite hive than anything else.  Large spires shaped from red clay jutted up from the ground, with a singular mound in the center dominating the rest.  Kyle couldn’t resist using a quick Identify to better understand the properties of the mud as they approached the outer walls, and was surprised to see how mana-rich the clay was.  He expected it would provide welcome shelter from the elements, and was likely as durable as any more standard construction material.

A D Grade orc waved them in, giving a warm greeting to Skippy as they passed.

“You know everybody here?”  Kyle asked.

“I’ve spent quite a bit of time here,” Skippy said.  “Benjamin had a lot to talk about with Duroc, and the guards like watching when he and I spar.”

“I’m surprised he has time while he’s preparing to take up the mantle as War Chieftain,” Kyle said with a chuckle.

“What exactly do you think a War Chieftain does?”  Skippy countered.  “You’ll hear more from him, but the whole point of this ritual is to show the others that he’s strong enough to be respected.”

“And if he’s not?” Kyle asked, following a winding road toward the large central spire.

“Then he’ll die, and this village will be taken over by one of the more powerful chieftains,” Skippy said cooly.  As if seeing Kyle’s alarm, he quickly added.  “That won’t be a problem for Duroc, though.  He’s scary strong.”

Another guard gave them a curt nod as they stepped inside the large tower, the air immediately cooling once they were inside.  Following a winding set of hallways and stairs, they finally entered what Kyle could only describe as the war room.  Large tables covered in various maps were standing side-by-side, with stacks of notes and other documents being moved about by orcish attendants.  A neatly stacked set of scrolls was set on the edge of one table, each being individually sealed away in metal containers.  Kyle suspected those would be his deliveries.

At the center of the room poring over one of the maps was a massive orc nearly four meters tall.  Red bristles covered much of its head and muzzle, with large tusks jutting from its lower jaw.  It gave off a monstrously powerful C Grade aura, though its voice when it turned to face them was familiar.

“Kyle Mayhew, it is wonderful to see you again.  And unless my nose deceives me, you even brought snacks!”
