Dawn was currently in the air, circling around a huge cyclone. Her body was being controlled by the Aegis system. Aegis took over Dawn’s body to assist, but then, it started going in another direction.

Initially, Aegis planned to return control after the cyclone was finished. But now, Aegis was hunting a group of wyverns. The goal? To get the elemental core and pump a massive amount of elemental energy from both the core and the cyclone, to rapidly create elemental circuitry inside Dawn’s body.

Even if the elemental circuit doesn't get solidified, it would still be worth it. Dawn’s strength would increase by leaps and bounds, even with an extremely thin layer of elemental accumulation.

Until now, Aegis has hunted a total of five wyverns. Four wyverns of a group of seven wyverns, and one she had hunted previously. The rest of the three wyverns of the group were scattered far away, nowhere to be seen. This gave Aegis leeway to take out and eat the elemental core from another wyvern.

It was holding its peer previously. After being separated due to Aegis’s attack, its partner was nowhere to be seen.

After absorbing the first elemental core along with elemental energy in the cyclone, Dawn’s natural level grew a lot. Dawn’s capacity to wield energy also increased significantly.

Since the situation was not good, the Aegis system held off all the notifications and status updates.

After having the second elemental core, Aegis again started the absorption, utilization, and accumulation process. This time, the energy didn’t feel overwhelming. In fact, Dawn was feeling very energetic and refreshed because of her increased capacity.

Suddenly, Aegis stopped controlling Dawn’s body physically. Since immediate danger had decreased, Aegis decided to make its user gain battle experience with bigger foes. More big predators would soon be visible. The system was sure about it.

Dawn had yet to learn about using elemental energy and how it works. So, Aegis was still handling the energy accumulation process, full body enhancement, blue energy sheet usage, and the weaponised claw extensions.

Until now, Dawn felt like her body was being controlled forcefully, but suddenly, it stopped. Dawn didn’t know that Aegis had stopped controlling it, and she could control her body again, so Dawn did nothing and kept getting blown in the cyclone while anticipating how her body was going to be controlled now.

After a while, nothing happened. Her body was still not moving and was only getting blown in the cyclone.

Suddenly, Dawn saw a wyvern flapping its wings rapidly, trying not to get pushed by the wind current.

It was flying opposite to the wind direction. Since Dawn was gliding with the wind current, she was approaching the wyvern from the front.

As soon as the wyvern noticed Dawn, it roared from afar. “Rooooooaaaarrrr…”

Hearing the roar, Dawn got alerted. The distance was getting shorter rapidly. But still, Dawn’s body didn’t move. It kept gliding straight. She started waiting for the things to unfold automatically.

The distance was reduced even more. She started wondering, Why isn’t her body doing anything apart from gliding? Until now, it had moved on its own. Dawn tried to move her front paw just a little bit, and it moved according to her. It was shocking.

Dawn then moved her front paws in front of her face to look. As soon as she moved her front feet, air movement was disrupted, causing her to roll in the air.

Dawn was not able to control herself anymore. She started mewing loudly. “Meeeeeeeeooooooooow…”

She also started descending slowly. The upward air current also started slowing down. The cyclone’s thick body was gradually thinning. The excessive energy inside the guardian of the forest was about to finish. This affected the cyclone’s wind current and air pressure.

Upward air pressure was still there, but it was not enough to push Dawn towards the sky anymore. The approaching wyvern also noticed this. This was the wyvern leader, the first wyvern that attacked Dawn.

Noticing the change in wind, the wyvern leader prepared to attack. It finally got the chance to take revenge in the best way possible. Now Dawn was in the same condition as when Dawn hunted down the wyverns.

As soon as the wyvern leader reached near Dawn, it opened its mouth and started using its Breath attack to burn Dawn alive. It was certain that Dawn would not be able to survive. Previously, it didn’t attack with its breath because its peers were around. But now it was free to proceed however it wanted.

When using the breath attack from its mouth, the wyvern followed Dawn's movements.

After Dawn passed the wyverns from below, the wyvern leader redirected its body and started chasing, hoping to look at the condition of its foe.

But unfortunately, nothing happened to Dawn’s body. There was no burned fur. The whole body was the same as before.

Seeing Dawn still rolling in the air and trying to regain the balance even after the breath attack, the wyvern leader was shocked.

When Dawn was about to be enveloped in flames, Aegis used a lot of elemental energy to create a protective layer around Dawn’s fur. So, she was safe. But heat transfer through the protective layer was still unmanageable for Dawn. She was scared.

Dawn was not expecting to get attacked like that. She was exasperated about what was happening. Not only was she unable to control her body movement, but she was also being attacked by the wyvern’s flames. Moreover, it was chasing.

Suddenly, Aegis started controlling her body again and spread her limbs in the air. Dawn regained control immediately, and Aegis again stopped controlling Dawn’s body. Although the lifting force of the wind current had reduced, it was enough to glide.

This time, Dawn didn’t fold her front limbs. She kept her front legs straight so she could glide straight. She finally understood how to stop rolling in the air.

Dawn was gliding at a great speed, and the wyvern was chasing her from behind. They were going far from the cyclone.

Although the cyclone was still there but its extreme upward wind had toned down a lot. Cyclone was not pulling everything aggressively anymore. It was only a matter of time before the cyclone stopped.

They were moving towards the Sylvane from a very high altitude. It usually takes about twenty minutes of running to reach Dawn’s den from the Sylvane border. But within a few minutes, they were about to cross the border from high above the ground.

Outside the border, many high-level Adventurers and Rangers were stationed, looking at the forest. All their attention was towards the particular area where nothing could be seen. They were dispatched near the border to create a barrier just in case the cyclone starts expanding. This was the only thing they could do.

If it were a normal tornado, they wouldn’t be so worried. But it was a cyclone purely made up of elemental pressure. It was condensed in a wide area and covered more than a few hundred meters. It was not far away from the town, so it affected many buildings, and many people were injured.

While waiting for the orders, A few adventurers noticed the big wyvern flying towards Sylvane.

“Is… is… is that a wyvern???” One of the adventurers said, and everyone looked at the approaching beast. This beast was flying away from the disaster zone.

“Yes, seems like it.” A ranger replied from behind the shocked adventurer. Running away from such a disaster was a miracle. But what the ranger didn’t know was that although the Storm was there, inside the affected area, air flow was being managed by the forest guardian. That’s why the wyvern survived.

Then the ranger shouted loudly. “EVERYONE, RAISE YOUR WEAPON AND PREPARE TO INTERCEPT THE WYVERN,”

The ranger saw that there was something in front of the wyvern. It was glowing with a blue color. Whatever it was, it was approaching airspace high above the ground. He was not sure whether it was good to attack or not. If the blue glowing beast and the wyvern were strong enough to survive the cyclone, then they were probably strong enough to kill everyone.

Thinking this, he gave another command. “DON'T ATTACK UNLESS I GIVE YOU AUTHORIZATION… I REPEAT, DON'T ATTACK UNLESS I GIVE YOU AUTHORIZATION.”

Every adventurer behind the Ranger prepared themselves to attack the moment they got the order. Some were holding staffs, some were holding bows and arrows, some were holding spears, and some were holding swords.

If the glowing thing and the wyvern cross the town and fly to the other side, there is no need to attack and provoke them, especially in these conditions.

The situation was dire for many people. Many houses were destroyed due to the strong wind. The whole town was filled with chaos. Many people were not able to breathe properly due to the strong elemental pressure.

But thankfully, the pressure started decreasing. The cyclone inside the forest became very thin. Visibility became better.

While gliding, Dawn got attacked again from behind. The wyvern again used its breath attack. The result was the same as before. Dawn’s whole body was enveloped, but Aegis used elemental energy to cover Dawn's body.

Again, Dawn got startled and folded her front legs, and she started falling down. But this time, there was no significant air pressure to slow down her speed.

Dawn was free-falling. The wyvern kept chasing.

Dawn knew what she had to do. She opened her front legs.

But she was upside down. The plan failed. Due to her falling momentum and her upside-down body, she was not able to glide again.

She tried to turn her body immediately. But it was hard to turn towards the ground. The wind hitting the sheet kept her rolling in the air.

After a few attempts, she was finally able to turn around and open all her legs as wide as possible. But by now, the distance between the ground and her had decreased significantly. The upward air current was not there anymore.

In the end, she was not able to glide even after positioning herself properly. It was working until now, but things changed entirely after going out of the strong upward wind currents.

Then suddenly, she got unbalanced again, turned in the air. Her body became upside down, looking at the wyvern closing in with its jaws open.

Previously, the wind currents helped Dawn glide through the strong upward wind flow. But as soon as the distance between her and the town decreased, the wind current decreased significantly. Gliding using the element sheet didn’t work anymore.

Being a cat, her limbs were not long enough to create enough drag using the elemental sheet to glide properly. So, without an upward wind current, it was not possible. Aegis could help using the elemental energy, but the system had no intention to interfere, so that Dawn could gain battle experience with bigger foes.

Dawn didn’t know that the wyvern was about to catch her with its jaws. But after being unbalanced and turning around, she saw a jaw was about to reach her body.

Dawn opened her claws to scratch. Aegis, noticing this, enlarged the claw extension instantly.

As soon as the wyvern was about to bite Dawn, Dawn used the full force of her claws to attack the jaws. Dawn used all her strength to deliver the blow. Her whole body turned with the momentum.

When Dawn attacked, she subconsciously activated a huge amount of energy. Aegis only created the enlarged claws to cut the jaws, but what Dawn did was astonishing.

What happened was, the wyvern’s whole body got sliced into five long slices, instantly.

The adventurers and the ranger saw everything. They had their eyes wide open.

After Dawn completely absorbed the energy core and dense elemental energy from the cyclone, her level was increased by leaps and bounds. Not only this, Aegis also used excess energy to enhance every part of Dawn’s body.

During this attack, Dawn subconsciously created her own technique, using more than half of the elemental energy from the core she had eaten earlier. The amount of elemental energy she used was enormous.

Through her claws, she sent a sharp, invisible blade, which moved like a hunter's rope, and kept going until it sliced the wyvern’s whole body. Each of her claws on the limb created one invisible wave, just like a blade, so the body was cut into 5 long slices.

Dawn was stunned seeing this.

Suddenly, she saw tree tops from the corner of her vision. The ground was close and she was about to hit it directly.

Immediately, Dawn turned her body towards the ground to land with her limbs. Thankfully, her body was temporarily enhanced, so she landed safely even after falling from the sky.

But unfortunately, she landed not far from the adventurers.

“ATTAAAAAAAACK…”

“Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa…” Every adventurer started sending a barrage of attacks towards Dawn.
