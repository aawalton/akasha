One of the harder things to recognize about existence is how little of it is tailored to our experience. The senses of cultivators can plunge deep and wide and high, given the time and the inclination to grow in such a manner, but we are all born in specific conditions so precise that they are almost literally impossible to replicate without experiencing them first.

There are places where the world is so heavy that air itself becomes a liquid, naturally and without Qi to infuse or initiate the process. There are suns that glow other hues than our own, and around them, existence defines itself differently. I have found landscapes which possess almost the exact same conditions as what might once have been our primordial, pre-life homeland, and found that missing a single component or having even the most slightly unbalanced mix of reagents transformed those places into alien things, bereft of life or filled entirely with things far stranger than I can describe in words.

The conditions for life as we understand it are, as best as I can determine, an unbelievably specific blend of happenstance born from infinity and intention born from madness and malformed thought, beyond our comprehension to interpret.

It goes deeper, though. Even in these places I have described, certain things we hold as permanent, foundational truth are in play. If the spaces between our smallest pieces were only miniscully smaller, nothing we can comprehend or envision could exist at all. If the forces between fundamental pieces of ourselves were to vary in any direction in the slightest possible degree, all of reality would be something so unknowable and utterly inhospitable to life that the very idea of beings such as we could never come to pass, even in billions of years’ time.

Existence is a single moment, brought about only by the size of all else that is and the will of all those who have made it be. And yet, in that infinitely small slice of possibility, all the variation and beauty and wonder and complexity of being rests and grows and transforms. Imagine what worlds we have yet to find. Imagine what mysteries we have yet to experience. Imagine, for a moment, what all of this means.
