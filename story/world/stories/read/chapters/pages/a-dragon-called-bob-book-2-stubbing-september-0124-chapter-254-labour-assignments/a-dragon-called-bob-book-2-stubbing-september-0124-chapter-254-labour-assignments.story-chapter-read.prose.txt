Kat, for obvious reasons, was not happy with me.  The one eye I could see was full of anger, and the cutlass that had replaced her long-forgotten letter-opener-sized greatsword had already struck my snout seven times this morning.

“No, I just don’t want to spend half my fucking hoard on it!”

“If we have the dwarves dig out a half-mile-wide pit on Bob Island—you aren’t allowed to name anything else, ever—then line it with terraces and make the centre a raised stage, we can get a couple of hundred thousand people in here.”  She flew down on her fairy wings, which seemed incongruous on the tiny sexy pirate outfit, and stalked back and forth on the sand as she ranted at me.

“That’s a shit load of people.  And work.  The dwarves are busy with other stuff.”  I settled down onto the sand, and while it wasn’t as comfy as my treasure, the warm grains were a pleasing yellow colour, and between the latent heat of the beach and the fake sun in the sky, I relaxed as I baked in my scales.

“Well, the humans in Armand and Pratnip’s towns might disagree.  The shorties are building walls for them.”

“Not letting the world get Unravelled or whatever you call it, avoid letting Morris terrorise unaffiliated towns into paying tribute or joining my empire, go win over the elves… I’m not seeing the problem with my current goals, Kat.”

“Don’t take that tone with me.  You think this is just a joke?  A vanity project?”

“Yes.”

“Oh.  Well, it kind of is, but that’s looking at the little picture.”

I sighed and spread my wings out, pressing them down into the sand.  One great thing about the resort floor was the complete absence of sand fleas.    “So what’s the big picture?”

“You’ve got my attention.  But the tickets have to sell for that to happen.”

You’ll end up richer than before, as an executive producer.”  She watched for me to respond, and while Vanity did start flexing in front of an imaginary mirror in my mind, I was unmoved.  “Hmmph.  We’ve got a captive audience, “she continued.  “You do know that Duke Smith controls the second-largest city in the empire?”

I assumed it wasn’t for victory.  “Having the Emperor’s bestie assassinated in front of his eyes and then accepting the guy's head like you were completing a grain sale.”  There was no rude gesture that involved three fingers that I was aware of, but I got the feeling Kat meant it that way.

You united the Skuldrak, turning a fractious group of tribes who kept each other in check into a unified force that you’re going to unleash on the people of Ankmapak.  There is a reason that you’re the focal point of half a dozen prophecies relating to what might be politely referred to as ‘the end times’.”

Heaven’s Hate, he’s like… well, like a king or president is to the average person back on Earth before you monkeys invented telecommunications.  Not a person, an institution.
