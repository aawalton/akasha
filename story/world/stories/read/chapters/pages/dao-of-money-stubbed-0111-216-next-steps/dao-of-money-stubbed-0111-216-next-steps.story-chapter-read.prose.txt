Everything on every floor was trying to kill us.”Yalan snorted. “Maybe you. Nothing on the first five floors could really harm me.” She leaned back slightly. “I took the opportunity to check out the competition, see how big this place actually is, and work a bit on my dao.”Chen Ren was immediately interested in the part about checking out the competition, but he decided to ask about that later. Your tokens fluctuated in the arena. What did you buy from the merchant?”Chen Ren replied calmly, “Something useful.
