The first thing Asher did after recovering from the shock of having a sword shoved through his stomach was try to slip into the astral in order to free himself from the blade.

The second thing Asher did was look down in horror at the sword still lodged in his midsection as his skill failed to activate in the slightest, leaving him stuck in the material realm, very much still with a blade sticking out of his front.

“Hey now, no running away!” the pale man giggled, laying a reassuring hand on Asher’s shoulder as if to hold him in place. “Don’t you know that’s rather rude? Guests are so rare this deep in the Deadlands, there’s no need to rush things!”

Before the stranger could say anything else, Asher triggered Comprehend’s second-tier effect, slowing the world to a crawl. Still staring at the black blade stuck out of his gut, he tried to figure out what was going on.

Either the metal in the blade or some sort of skill that man has is preventing my own skill from activating, similar to Clare’s Binding Chains. But this is different. I can still feel my skills, it’s not like I’m locked out of them like with Clare. There’s no debuff for me to purge with Complete Resilience.

Asher focused on the blade more closely, gleaning what he could from it with Identify.

Soulsteel Short Sword - Masterwork Quality (Devourer)

Soulsteel? I’ve never heard of that material, but that doesn’t sound very good. And the fact that it’s a masterwork weapon with a name doesn’t really fill me with hope. Whoever this guy is, he’s seriously bad news.

Even if Asher didn’t know what soulsteel was, he felt pretty safe in assuming the unknown metal was the cause for the disruption of Astral Dip. Which meant more than anything, getting free from the sword lodged in his gut was priority number one. He could still use Comprehend’s second-tier effect, for whatever reason, so at least some of his skills still seemed to be functioning.

As his thoughts slowed back down and the world returned to normal speed, Asher lurched forward, relying on his Belt of Strength to break free from the young man’s grasp and free himself from the soulsteel blade.

To his shock, the man’s hand on his shoulder didn’t budge so much as an inch, his fingers digging into Asher’s flesh like a vice in order to keep him all but locked in place regardless of how hard he strained.

“Come on, I just told you no running away!” the man said, pouting at him like Asher was trying to run off with his favorite toy. With the pale stranger standing directly behind Asher, he still couldn’t get a good look at his attacker beyond his creepy face. “What brings you into the Deadlands, anyway? You don’t look like the slayer type!”

Rather than answer, Asher tried to activate Recall, focusing on Samantha’s mark in an attempt to get away. Yet even more concerning than not being able to teleport, he realized he couldn’t even feel any of his Eternal Marks, let alone the one on Samantha. Sweat began beading down Asher’s temple as he realized he might actually be in some serious trouble, and he cleared his throat as he struggled to come up with a way to escape his captor.

“I’m searching for strong opponents to kill,” he decided on, swallowing hard as he tried and failed to activate Spatial Lock. He’d hoped maybe dragging them both into the astral realm would startle his attacker enough for him to release his shoulder, but he couldn’t even summon the usual barrier around his location.

“Well that’s convenient, I’m searching for strong opponents to kill too!” the man laughed, his high-pitched voice seeming in stark contrast to the bright-red blood in his eyes. “I don’t actually have a name, but the others tend to call me Smiles, on account of how much fun I’m always having. What’s your name?”

“Rulfar,” he said without thinking, seeing no need to give the increasingly creepy Smiles his actual name. Still working on a way out of his current predicament, Asher decided the best way to get out of this was to keep Smiles talking as long as possible. All it would take was a single flick of the man’s wrist to cut Asher neatly in two, and he didn’t fancy his chances of managing to heal up this deep within the Deadlands. “How are you able to survive the Death Aura?”

“You know, I was going to ask you the same question!” Smiles said, laughing as though they were best friends just hanging out. “You see, I’m not actually alive, which means the Death Aura just makes me stronger. But you’re not one of the troops, and you’re definitely not one of the King’s Servants, which means you have to be an outsider. And your blood is bright and red, which means you’re actually alive!”

Smiles squeed, like a high-school girl catching a glimpse of her favorite boy-band before continuing. “What’s it like being alive? Is it warm? Is pain fun? Do you eat lots of stuff?”

Alright, Smiles is some sort of undead monster created by the King of Death, and I need to get out of here yesterday or I’m going to get vivisected by him, Asher thought, shuddering at the thought of the grinning man eagerly plucking different organs from his torso and asking him excitedly what each one did as Asher slowly bled to death.

“Being alive is pretty nice,” Asher said slowly, desperately racking his brain for an idea. “You don’t feel pain?”

“No, we don’t really have a need for it. I asked the King about it once, and he said pain is just a signal warning living stuff that they’re at risk of dying. Seeing as we’re already dead, there’s not really any point!”

“That makes sense,” Asher admitted, running down his list of options. No Astral Dip, no Spatial Lock, no Recall or Eternal Mark, and no Personal Rift either. Pretty much every skill I have that would let me escape is unavailable, but Comprehend’s second-tier effect still worked. Why would that be the case?

Suddenly, the answer came to him, and Asher would have smacked himself in the head if he wasn’t in such a dire situation. Just as Smiles started saying something new, Asher laid a finger on the cold metal of the soulsteel blade, focusing on the only skill that stood a chance at freeing him.

He gasped when the short sword withdrew from his stomach as Distortion’s second-tier effect shrank the blade to little more than the size of a small knife. In the same moment, he ducked and twisted out from beneath Smiles’ grip, leaping away from the monster and turning to face him head on.

Finally face-to-face with his attacker, Asher took Smiles in as the monster went against its namesake and frowned at him, before staring down at his shrunken sword as it returned to normal size. Other than the pale skin and bloodshot eyes, Smiles appeared to be a fairly normal guy. He wore simple woolen clothing with old shoes that showed their years, and other than his soulsteel short sword still dripping red with Asher’s blood, he didn’t have so much as a speck of armor or other weaponry on him. Like the liches, Smiles didn’t seem to register to his Identify or Judgement, further proving he was far from a living person.

Holding up his sword, Smiles pointed at it. “That’s not very nice to use a skill on someone’s property without asking!” The monster pouted, looking more than a little upset that Asher had freed himself from his blade. “It’s not easy sneaking up on people you know! I worked hard to get so good at it!”

“Trust me, you and me both,” Asher said, holding a hand against his bleeding stomach and letting out a sigh of relief as he felt the wound ever so slowly beginning to close. That confirmed his suspicions, and he pointed his living wood dagger at Smiles. “Your freaky blade… the metal stops tier-1 skills from working, doesn’t it?”

“What, soulsteel?” Smiles asked, tilting his head to the side in confusion. “Maybe? You’re not the first person to ask me that, but I never bothered to check. I’m normally too busy asking my own questions, and nobody ever bothers to actually talk to me! All they do is scream all the time. It’s very rude!”

Even if he doesn’t know, that has to be the case, Asher thought, running through his skills in his head. Astral Dip, Spatial Lock, Eternal Mark, and Recall were all skills that were reliant on their first-tier effect to even utilize their second tier, so it made sense none of them worked. Comprehend and Distortion, on the other hand, had second-tier effects that were separate from their first tier, and they’d worked without issue. Identify and Eternal Regeneration were interesting in that both of their second-tier effects simply strengthened the skill’s first tier, which seemed to be enough to keep them running, even through having been stabbed by the soulsteel blade.

A quick check of Samantha’s mark and realizing he could sense her again sent a wave of relief through Asher as he confirmed that his skills were back online. He was tempted to Recall away rather than risk getting stabbed by that sword a second time, but he didn’t like the thought of Smiles getting the drop on him again. The undead monster had already let slip that he specialized in stealth, which meant it was probably in Asher’s best interest to kill him here and now.

He’d also mentioned that there were others, which was more than a little concerning.

“You’re not saying anything!” Smiles whined, suddenly smiling as he pointed his blade directly at Asher’s chest. “I know! Normally I can get people to talk to me when I start cutting them open! Usually it’s little more than begging and pleading for their lives, but that’s still more than nothing!”

This thing’s got serious—

Asher swore as Smiles shot forward, moving far faster than anything he’d ever fought before. He barely managed to slip into the astral in time to dodge the slash of his soulsteel blade, clearly aimed at slicing off one of his legs. The monster was completely silent when it moved, and it wasn’t until Asher vanished that Smiles blinked, frowning once more as it looked around.

“What kind of teleportation skill is that?” Smiles asked, tilting his head to the side as he scratched his scalp. “Where did you—”

Smiles moved faster than Asher thought possible, blurring even through his Comprehend as he turned around and raised his short sword into position to block the point-blank thrust from Asher’s living wood dagger as he returned to the material behind the undead. The monster had reacted in a mere fraction of a second, and Asher paled as he finally realized Smiles’ earlier dash hadn’t been a one-off ability and that he was drastically outmatched when it came to speed.

Asher slipped back into the astral before Smiles could decide to attack again. But rather than frown, the monster grinned wider than ever before, showing off its rows of pointed teeth.

“Not teleportation… vanishing! You’re still here, I just can’t see you! Oh, it’s like a game!” Laughing, Smiles lowered his sword once more, closing his eyes and swaying back and forth as he began humming under his breath. Seeing as Smiles’ eyes were closed, Asher didn’t waste his chance.

Flicking back and forth from the astral into the material, Asher tried again and again to land a hit on the undead monster. But no matter how little time Smiles had to react, he always managed to blur his sword into position at the last possible second, halting Asher’s dagger a hairsbreadth before it could pierce his pale flesh. Asher tried to stab him in the chest, the head, the back, and even the feet. But try as he might, he just couldn’t stab faster than Smiles’ could block.

This thing is freaky as all hell, Asher thought, trying to come up with a way to kill the monster. No matter how quickly he struck after coming back from the astral, Smiles had already proven he could react fast enough to block everything Asher could throw at it. Seeing as it was already undead, Asher didn’t think his final vial of magadrine powder he’d been holding onto from Eight’s supply would do anything either, and that was his usual back-up plan for when he didn’t know how to kill something. He could try dragging the monster into the astral realm in the hopes of defeating it similar to how he’d taken down the Head’s Knuckles, but Smiles was far more dangerous than any of the Knuckles had been on their own. The moment he realized his energy was being drained, he’d probably stop messing around and just gut Asher like a fish.

All of a sudden, a thought struck him, and Asher realized he might be going about this all the wrong way. He might not be able to defeat Smiles when it came to speed…

But maybe he didn’t have to.
