Geography:

Nanon:

Nanon Kingdom is where Viscount Jack and most of the Cha hail from.

A roughly rectangular landmass, 500x500km (310x310 miles) in size, with a population of 3 million people.

Most of it is hilly area, with fertile areas surrounding rivers.

The story begins here.

Nobart:

The MMC, Jack is the viscount of the county of Nobart until the beginning of the story.

The very northwest part of Nanon, Nobart is relatively well-off, due to it’s mineral wealth and possessing Nordhaven, the fourth biggest port in the country.
