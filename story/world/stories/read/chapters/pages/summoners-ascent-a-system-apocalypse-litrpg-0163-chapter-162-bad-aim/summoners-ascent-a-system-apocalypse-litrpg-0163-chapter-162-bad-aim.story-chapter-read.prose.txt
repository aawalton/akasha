Small snowflakes floated from the ceiling, landing on the ground of the science room. The kids were grabbing at them excitedly, holding the snow that simply didn't want to melt. And it was cold, but it wasn't freezing like snow should be. This basically only looked like snow, but most of the experiences of handling it were completely wrong.

But the children didn't care about that in the slightest. They had watched as Ashton put some bottles of water onto a table, before a thick mist flowed out of them and formed clouds that stuck to the ceiling. And then, snowflakes dropped from those clouds.

It didn't matter whether this was real snow or not. They were seeing something fantastical that they never could have imagined seeing before. The first time Ashton met these kids, they seemed weirdly mature and put-together, but maybe that was just in comparison to anyone else that he saw before then. They were the only ones that weren't openly scared in this entire school. But at the end of the day, they were still just kids.

And maybe it was a little silly, but who doesn't want kids to think they're cool? This situation made Ashton just show off a little.

He had kept control of each individual snowflake, but actually manipulating them was pretty hard. So, for just that little bit of a boost, he channelled the undine's magic through the staff instead of the wisp's. And when he did, after a few moments, the staff changed shape slightly.

The wisp was released from its embrace, and instead, the wooden coil flattened and turned into a ring. The water elemental swam into the ring, and from its own body, a small disc of water disconnected. Droplets of water came flowing in and out of the disc now held in place by the ring, as the undine swam around the top of the staff.

'Now, this is new...' Ashton thought to himself, even if he tried not to let his own surprise show. He should try and see how the staff could interact with his other summons later, too.

But for now, there was something else he wanted to do. With the severe boost in the undine's magical power caused by the staff, Ashton quickly manipulated all the snowflakes.

They were pulled from the kids' hands and from the ground, coming together on the table in front of them. And there, Ashton built a small snowman, made up of three individual balls of snow. He made the snowballs jump up and down and move, leaping around different places in the room.

Honestly, this was actually pretty good practice. Controlling this all, especially without letting the cores of the snowballs just turn into a solid mass of ice, was shockingly hard.

But the look on the kids' faces was worth it. They were just looking at everything in wonder. As far as Ashton could tell. He had compressed his range a little bit, so everything was slightly blurry. Their expressions were just so bright that Ashton was pretty certain even through that blurriness.

However, sadly, even this had to come to an end. Before long, someone else stepped into the room, awkwardly knocking on the half-open door.

"Uhm... Rory?" they asked, and the old man quickly turned away.

"Yes?"

"There are some people at the gate, and they, uhm..."

Rory let out a long sigh. "Come out with it already, what's the issue? If they're injured, they can come in and stay for a bit, but only outside. If they're related to the bairns, they can stay longer."

"They said, they, uhm..." this person, seemingly a teacher when this place was still a proper school, seemed nervous. They were glancing into the room. "Apparently there are people at the university whose siblings are here? And they've come to pick them up. They have a list of names, but..."

Rory sighed, nodding. "Aye, I get the issue. They can't actually prove what they're saying, but they also refuse to leave?" The teacher nodded, so Rory scratched his scruffy beard. "I'll come out and talk to 'em. Lad, do you mind staying here with the three of them?"

Ashton shrugged. "I can stick with them, but do I have to stay here? I wanted to head to the vending machine again."

"Ah... Yeah, that's alright, just watch 'em? They've been..." Rory sighed lightly, "...very lively since ya left yesterday."

Without further ado, Rory left, and Ashton turned to the kids. They were silently staring back at him.

"What did you guys do?"

"Sean kicked a guy in the balls," Joey said almost immediately, and Sean snapped his head toward her.

"Hey! You're the one that got him all mad, so I had to calm the situation down!"

With a slight sigh, as if this was all beneath her, Joey shook her head. "It's not my fault if a grown man gets upset by being called a cun–"

Ashton interrupted her by clapping his hands. "Alright! Let's head to the vending machine, shall we?"

He quickly led them outside, and though her friends went ahead while bickering, Millie was just quietly following behind. Ashton could already guess why that was. Whoever Sean and Joey were fighting with clearly said something to, or about, her.

"You kids have a special quest right now, right? You're supposed to get yourselves an apprentice class, yeah?"

Millie looked up at Ashton, and slowly nodded. "Yeah, that's what the screen thingy says at least. Joey and Sean already have one, but I'm still thinking about it..."

Ashton raised a brow. She was still thinking about it? Sure, the deal that the Guardian of the Innocent made with the council made it so that these kids didn't get the ultimate penalty when failing these main quests, but they would still be penalised. They would essentially be cursed for every quest they failed, until they were physically so weak that they had no way of surviving. It was pretty brutal, but there were ways for them to recover as well. The situation that the kids underwent was dangerous, but a lot less so than what everyone else had to deal with. So Millie should use this opportunity to grow as much as she could.

All these quests would take years to actually wrap up, if humanity managed to survive that long. But even then, the 'Child-Safety System' would only be fully intact during Stage 1, the most chaotic, where a civilization still had to try and get used to this new world they were now living in.

After that adjustment period was over, while children under 16 still received certain benefits in regard to things like the class system and the support of their guardians, if people like that were available in the first place, the 'protection' that children had right now would fully disappear. They would have to take part in quests just as anyone else did.

And Ashton believed that they should take advantage of this current time as much as they could.

"You know, you can pick basically any class you want for now. You can switch really easily later," Ashton explained, focusing on the girl a little more. Honestly, he had noticed it before, but Millie's mana was extraordinary. Not necessarily in volume, but in clarity. The pattern of her mana was quite pretty, even without any magical ability, to the point it even rivalled Ashton's back before he was able to use magic.

He didn't want to make any promises to her, but if she learned how to manipulate mana, Millie would end up doing quite well. And since any class could be severely enhanced by learning to manually control one's own mana, she really had free reign in any way she wanted. That was Ashton's assessment.

"I think I–" Millie started to speak, but stopped herself and flinched. A rock was being thrown at her. Ashton had compressed his channelling range, so he saw it too late to call the gnome. Instead, he just caught it.

The rock was pretty sharp, and even cut into Ashton's palm. If it had hit Millie...

Ashton clutched the stone tightly and walked forward. Maybe it was just because he didn't have the silent step shoelaces anymore, but his walk felt more intense than usual.

By the time he reached them, Sean had already thrown one of the kids to the ground and was trying to pummel him in the face, but Ashton stopped him.

He had the wisp swoop down under Sean's stomach and push him up, stabilising the boy by placing a hand onto his back.

"Don't," Ashton said, squatting down in front of the kid laying on the ground. "Did you throw this rock just now?"

The boy stared up at him, scared. A bit of blood was dripping from the cut on Ashton's hand.

"An-And what if I did, huh?" he asked. It was likely that everyone here knew about Ashton at this point. They had all seen or at least heard of what he was capable of, but that didn't mean they trusted him. Rather, the kids might have been told by their parents to stay away from Ashton in the first place.

Ashton smiled, holding his hand out to the boy. "You shouldn't practice inside with your skill. Your aim is so bad, you could almost think you were aiming for Millie's face." His laugh was quiet, but was still the only sound in the hallway right now. There were some people watching, but they didn't say anything. The boy was confused, taking Ashton's hand before being pulled up. "Run along now. Practice elsewhere, okay?"

Glancing back at Sean, who was flipping the boy off thinking that Ashton couldn't see him do so, the boy silently nodded, before running off with his friend in tow.

Frankly, Ashton was extremely angry, but he couldn't scare this kid even more right now. The only reason the boy had done this in the first place was out of fear anyway, so that wouldn't help.

'Not that I have any fucking clue how to deal with kids the right way in the first place,' he thought to himself, before heading back to Millie. She was still startled, but Ashton just smiled and put a hand onto her back, guiding her forward again.

"Why did you do that? I could have just knocked him back into his right mind, with a good one-two combo!" Sean exclaimed proudly, throwing a few jabs into the air.

"And then, he would have run back to a teacher and complained that you hurt him, while he claimed he was just practicing his new skills," Ashton pointed out. It was faint, but there was definitely some mana in that stone. Likely the boy had some kind of apprentice version of a 'Thrower' class.

"So? Then I'll just say I was practicing my own skills," Sean added smugly.

Ashton smiled, as the three kids continued to follow him. This time, he made sure to keep his channelling range as wide as possible, so that he could be prepared for anything. "Well, maybe next time, then. For now, let's just go to the vending machine."
