"Oh, he's treating me like that because I'm a Copper? How long till I can be a Silver?" she asked eagerly.

She was starting to realize she might be more of a musclehead than she had thought, the changes to the world allowing her to pursue what she truly enjoyed more freely. It didn't stop her from becoming exhausted, collapsing onto a bench by the early evening.

Beth set her cheek on the top of the sitting girl's head as Sabs leaned back into her, releasing a contented sigh. I wasn't able to one-shot it like this gorilla," Sabs answered, poking Beth in the side at the end. Beth just grunted in response and kept eating.

Sabs' daggers are another ten silvers. So that's at least eight golds and some change. And that's just to get the most basic stuff, not even including basic supplies like potions or camping supplies for long dungeon runs."

"Doing it the old-fashioned way," Beth replied calmly.

"There's really not much else we can do, other than keep our eyes out. If we find anything valuable, like weird, rare plants or something, I might be able to sell them for some money at the CRA. Otherwise, we're grinding dungeons and killing beasts. Relax, it'll be fun," Beth said.

Beth immediately found Wiggles sitting in the hallway outside her door, picking up the massive cat and holding him in her arms as she walked downstairs.
