Don sat on the platform made of gilded wood in the flickering shades of torchlight. The bog was quiet, and eerily peaceful, although Don knew what lurked below.

“Mal’asha…?” Don asked nonchalantly.

No answer. Don swore silently. She had been so eager to butt in, but now nothing?

But after a few moments he got a System notification. It was a message. Not from the System, but from Mal’asha.

Mal: Yes?

Don perked up. “Do I get a chance to later choose the evolutions I pass?

Mal: It’s no fun if I have to hold your hand. Be a big boy and make a choice. Ta-ta.”

“Jerk…” Don muttered. She was right of course, but it hadn’t hurt to ask. Although, with Mal’asha who knew, maybe it did hurt to ask. But done’s done. Don had to make a decision.

If it wasn’t for his race with Jacques he would choose the Devour-evolution. But he was on a timer and he didn’t know how much sand the hourglass even had left. Jacques could be fighting Blazeclaw right now, for all he knew.

But if he never got this option again, he would regret it for the rest of his life. The upside was massive. It came with a risk, but Don needed to put things into perspective.

If he chose the psychic poison, he would augment his combat power, but ultimately his trajectory wouldn’t change. However, if he chose the Devour-option, his trajectory was altered permanently. It was such a standout. How could Don not take it.

“If I choose wrong here, I might die. But I’ve only been in this new reality for such a short time. If I snuff it now, eh it is what it is. But if I make it through this tutorial… And I have this Devour-evolution… The upside was just too massive.

Of course, the downside was him dying. That was the ultimate downside. But nothing ventured nothing gained. Don had to take calculated risks. If things got rough, he could always hunt easier prey and leverage his upgraded Devour. It was his signature move, he had to take it.

Don chose Devouring Physique, before he could change his mind.

[New Evolution Acquired: Devouring Physique (F-grade)]

[Devouring Physique (F-grade)]

Your digestive system begins processing consumed life force more efficiently. You extract more essence from enemies you Devour. +3 Constitution +1 Power [Passive]: Slight increase in Evolution Point yield per Devour. Slight increase in chance to gain evolution traits from consumed foes. Slight Increases in Health and Energy from using Devour. Scales With: Constitution, [Devour]

Don felt a lurch in his stomach. A convulsion ran up from his esophagus to the back of his throat. Something constricted, then expanded. Don breathed in. He felt stronger, more alert now. More like himself, as weird as that sounded. Maybe it was just the extra stats. Either way, Don looked at the option to upgrade the evolution. It cost a massive 400 points, just to go from F-grade to E-grade.

But Don had a fat stack of Evolution Points. The Grimoid massacre had doubled them. He had a total of 827 Evolution Points. Paying half of them to get a permanent boost to all subsequent points? Hardly worth a second thought. Don did it. He had the points to spare. And besides, gotta spend Evolution Points to make Evolution Points.

That just rolls right off the tongue…

Just for his amusement, Don checked the next upgrade to D-grade. Yup, 1000 points. That might take a bit of hunting. As far as pure Attribute gains, that might be inefficient. He would have to see in action how much of a point increase per Devour he got now that he had this evolution.

Now that Don was mostly ready to continue fighting in the dungeon, he decided to spend his free Attribute points. He looked at his stats. The physical stats looked nice and balanced. Mental was still low, but the recent evolutions had seriously bumped that up. Now it was Energy that was lagging behind. Don hadn’t needed to pump it up before. Devour had been keeping him topped up. But as the fights got harder, he would need more stamina. Especially since he was going to introduce his psychic abilities into the mix, which seemed to drain a lot of Energy. He decided to put both of his points into Energy. He figured it was a nice sort of a balance move, since he had opted to not pick a combat oriented evolution. He might need the extra stamina to run away.

Either way, Don was pleased when he looked at his Status screen. It was seriously coming along.

Don wanted to go test his improved Devour ability immediately. He had two options. Venture deeper into the dungeon. Fight more Grimoids, and hopefully find a new chamber, because he sure as hell wasn’t taking Morshoff on right now. Or he could backtrack and go find more lizards. They were annoying to fight, but relatively safe. He could practice using his Telekinesis against them and acquire an Evolution trait from them.

It made sense. Backtracking felt annoying, but Don had to be smart about this. He would likely get an acid spit or a camouflage ability from the lizards. Either of those would be a massive boon as he ventured deeper into the dungeon. He didn’t know what was waiting for him, so he would acquire every tool available before piling on more risk. He had already taken a chunky one with the Devouring Physique. Now it was time to play it safe, grind on some lizards and then venture forward.

Don picked up a few handfuls of blowpipe darts and broken spearshafts. Once he had two satchels full of them, he was ready to go kill some lizards.
