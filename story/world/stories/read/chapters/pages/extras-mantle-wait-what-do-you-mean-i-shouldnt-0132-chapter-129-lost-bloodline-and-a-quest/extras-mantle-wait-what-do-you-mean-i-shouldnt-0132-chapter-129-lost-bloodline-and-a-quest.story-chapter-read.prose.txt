Veric lit another cigarette and surveyed the room with a critical eye.

Elenor was arranging the furniture with excessive precision, moving chairs half an inch to the left and then back again like the exact positioning would somehow determine success or failure. Hans stood near the door, arms crossed, watching the corridor outside with the alert posture of someone expecting trouble.

Vera sat at one of the tables, pale blue hair pulled back in a practical braid, her fingers tracing patterns in the air as she tested the orb they'd received from Master Illiana.

And standing in the corner, grinning as they'd just pulled off the world's greatest heist, were Brocki and Flocki.

The twins had returned.

"Sir!" Brocki called out, waving enthusiastically. "We got the word out as you asked. Recruitment's happening, official-like, and people are already lining up."

"Mostly 'cause they heard about the gear," Flocki added with a shrug. "And they could stop even a spear and a fireball. But hey, motivation's motivation."

Veric took a drag from his cigarette and exhaled smoke toward the ceiling. "Good. That's exactly what we need. How many so far?"

"A couple of hundred," Brocki said. "Maybe more by the time we actually start processing them."

"We set up a basic queue system in the corridor," Flocki continued, gesturing vaguely toward the door. "First-come, first-served, but we're prioritizing anyone with combat experience or useful skills."

Veric nodded. "Excellent work."

He turned his gaze to Vera, who was still fiddling with the orb, which looked like a pathwork of crystal and metal fused together, its surface etched with delicate runes.

"Magic aptitude assessment orb, and it works," she explained, noticing Veric's attention. "Place your hand on the surface, channel essence, and the runes will light up according to your potential. More runes active means higher aptitude."

"Well, I think he's still exhausted," Vera said.

Elenor straightened, eager as always. "Three primary marks. First: Jin's criteria—magical potential verified through the aptitude orb. Second: physical fitness assessed through basic endurance and strength tests. Third: motivation to defend the city, determined through an interview."
