Three weeks after possessing the oversized squirrel on Mapel, an interesting development with Percy’s familiar drew a sliver of his attention back to Remior. Most of his mental pockets were still occupied with mindset creation and his work cleansing the residents of the small village, but the clone cohabiting his familiar’s carapace had the rare opportunity of experiencing her evolving mutation first hand.

After devouring five of the giant eel’s special glands, the exoskeleton of the Ruby Princess had stopped absorbing the magical substance. It already shone far more brightly than it had before, bathing every corner of the late Empress’ oversized chamber in its ruby-coloured light.

More importantly, something had changed within the familiar’s body as well. Her jelly gland had shifted slightly to the left side of her thorax, making space for a second, similarly shaped but differently coloured organ to mirror it on the opposite side. Numerous thin capillaries branched out of the new gland, linking it to every square centimetre of the wasp’s carapace.

Percy guessed that Nephthys could now generate and secrete the crimson powder directly through her shell like the quasi-divine beasts from Ruby Heart. However, the new organ was also connected to his familiar’s throat and mouth like its older sibling, presumably giving her the additional option of vomiting the magical substance out as with her royal jelly.

[Congratulations! Your familiar’s mutation has evolved: Ruby Carapace -> Ruby Source!]

‘Thank Phoebe this worked!’ Percy’s main body exhaled in relief.

He still didn’t know whether the upgraded mutation would fuse with the rest during the wasp’s upcoming evolution, but he was glad that they had a reliable source of ruby powder on Remior. Nephthys’ output would hopefully scale with the size of her body, eventually matching or even surpassing that of the colossal eels.

With any luck, her future heirs would automatically inherit the upgraded mutation. Percy would rather not have to butcher a quasi-divine beast every time a new Ruby Princess hatched, though he understood that that was currently out of his control.

Then again, the plan had always been for Nephthys to eventually attain divinity once more, so Remior’s supply of ruby powder should remain secure for the next three million years at a minimum. Percy was sure he’d find a more permanent solution by then, and it might also be a little premature to be worrying about what would happen when he and his friends lost control of their internal worlds and turned into Celestials.

For the time being, it might not be a bad idea for his familiar to start slowly upgrading the carapaces of her Starry Commanders at her leisure. Each Blue bug would need to consume a huge pile of ruby powder, so Nephthys would have to labour for decades to strengthen all fifty thousand of her strongest nieces, even after accounting for her upcoming promotions.

Still, the hive would be a lot stronger once she was done, and ruby powder didn’t contain much mana, so producing it in large quantities shouldn’t interfere with her jelly output much.

‘I suppose it’s time to compress my beast mana to the next grade as well?’ the Ruby Princess tentatively asked.

Percy hesitated for a moment, but eventually nodded. They’d already decided to go through with this, so there was no point in having second thoughts.

His familiar developing a perpetual dependence on her Cradle wouldn’t be without risk, but their current situation was arguably even more precarious, since the Great Houses could theoretically attack the Fungal Spire at any moment. Nephthys was still unable to manage her subjects properly, and her advancement would greatly alleviate that issue.

Over the next few minutes, Percy watched the reincarnated deity compress her mana. She’d already experienced the process when he had elevated her second core, and he could certainly trust a being as old and wise as her with this relatively simple task.

‘I keep forgetting that she’s also a trained alchemist,’ he thought.

Nephthys might have lacked Percy’s many gifts in life, but she was still a former mind deity who had lived for hundreds of thousands of years. It would have been strange if she hadn’t dabbled in the field at some point. Not to mention that it was her people that had originally invented the compression principle upon which the artificial promotions had been built, which meant that she was likely well versed in that subject as well.

Percy had every intention of leaving his clone permanently stationed in her body, regardless of whether she ever became an aspect or not, as it would be a pity to waste the powerful domain he was cultivating through her promotions. At the rate he was sharpening this branch of willpower, it wouldn’t be long before it could match the one he shared with Micky. If Nephthys were to turn into an aspect right that instant, their merged domains would probably reach a similar level right away, giving Percy twice as much willpower to funnel to his soul clones.

Despite having no plans to retrieve his wisp in the foreseeable future, it wouldn’t hurt for Nephthys to know how to handle the Cradles on her own, in case of an emergency.

While the princess focused on her core, Percy took a break from giving the Moirais’ Decree to the Green wasps with his main body to prepare his familiar’s meal. Walking to her chamber, he dug out a large pit in front of her with his domain, filling it with vast quantities of royal jelly and dead wasps that they had gathered earlier. Nephthys would need to consume a literal hill of nutrients to grow to the size of a small house, and it would be better to have everything ready in advance.

Once the final strands of Blue mana banded together inside her core, a wave of primal hunger filled the clone’s and, by extension, Percy’s mind. It was thankfully accompanied by the sweet ecstasy of a notification.

[Congratulations! Your familiar’s mana core has advanced: Green -> Blue!]

Phoebe’s Decree had yet to say anything about Nephthys’ mutations, so Percy and the Ruby Princess eagerly dove into the tasty soup, stuffing her face with the building blocks of her growing body.

Jelly and pulverized wasp remains melted the moment they entered the reincarnated deity’s mouth, flooding every cubic centimetre of her flesh with new power. To his delight, Percy felt his familiar’s organs shift and mutate once more – a sign that Nephthys’ evolution would give them exactly what they had commissioned. In fact, the changes felt more aggressive and far more widespread than those the wasp had experienced in the past few weeks, making Percy extremely curious as to their outcome.

The first mutation clearly had something to do with her new gland, though it wasn’t limited to that organ. The ruby powder gland, the jelly gland right next to it, and even the acid gland inside her abdomen all grew larger and closer to one another. Numerous thick tubes formed between the organs, linking each to the other two and to Nephthys’ circulatory system. Her petiole – the thin circular section connecting her thorax and abdomen – grew visibly wider to accommodate the changes, but not so much as to hinder the wasp’s flexibility.

Nephthys’ jelly gland had always interacted strongly with her first core due to her Royal Lineage, allowing her to easily transfer and convert beast mana between the two organs. A similar relationship had existed between her second core and acid gland ever since she had awakened the corresponding mutation. Evidently, her three glands would now share resources with one another freely, potentially allowing the Ruby Princess to convert her acid mana into royal jelly even more efficiently than she had been able to through her spells.

Percy had honestly expected those particular mutations to evolve along such lines, though he was surprised by the changes occurring in a different section of his familiar’s body.

Three new pairs of antennae sprouted atop the wasp’s head, with all eight of the protrusions rapidly growing longer, thicker, pointier, and more rigid. At the same time, the pyramid-shaped gem embedded in her forehead also began to grow, spreading and seemingly fusing with the crystallized antennae to form a dazzling tiara.

Through his human eyes, Percy watched his already-giant familiar grow even taller. Her glowing carapace towered over him like a living statue fashioned out of the world’s most polished piece of ruby, adorned with lines of liquid gold swirling wildly across her frame.

The colours of all of Nephthys’ affinities bled into the strangely-shaped jewel that was rapidly forming around her head, giving the multi-pronged protrusion a different hue depending on the angle from which Percy viewed it – an angle which kept changing as his familiar continued to grow taller.

A wave of panic surged through the connection he shared with Nephthys, as the reincarnated goddess was obviously concerned about the fate of her son and subjects. She was only able to calm down a few seconds later, upon realizing that the Amenthei were still sleeping safely within the strange organ. Whatever was happening to her had to be a good thing.

After what felt like an eternity of devouring magical goo and having their body practically turned inside out, Nephthys’ growth finally halted, giving Phoebe’s Decree a chance to announce the results of her transformation.

And there were two notifications this time – not just one.

[Congratulations! Your familiar’s mutations have merged: Royal Lineage + Ruby Source + Acidic Gland + Thin Haemolymph -> Resource Monarch!]

[Congratulations! Your familiar’s mutations have merged: Soul Predator + Spectral Ark + Charged Antennae -> Prismatic Crown!]
