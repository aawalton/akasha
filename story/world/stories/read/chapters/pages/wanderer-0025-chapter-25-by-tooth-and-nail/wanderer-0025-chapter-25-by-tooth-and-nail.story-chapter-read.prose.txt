I scramble to my feet, keeping one eye on the creature while my hands struggle to locate the sword. Fuck! Of course I dropped it.

Meanwhile, the corpse lifts her arm and looks towards it, spreading her fleshless bony fingers wide before curling them back into a fist. Another surge of energy rocks the mound and I almost stumble again. But a glimmer catches my eye.

The sword!

Somehow in my fall it scattered a few meters away, halfway between me and the creature. In the time it takes me to leap towards it, the corpse opens her fist once again and the sword flies off the ground into her waiting hand.

Well....fuck.

But I don't have time to dwell on it, as she immediately starts swinging cuts at me. I duck the first one then tuck into an evasive roll as another sends a caressing swoosh of air mere centimeters from my neck, slicing a copper curl that floats to the ground. I tumble out of the way, grasping the rusted sword in my hand that I discarded earlier. The corpse unleashes a guttural growl in frustration that shakes the walls of the mound, the feather cloak billowing behind her on a phantom wind.

Remember your training. Do not yield. This is fucking real Beatrix.

She charges at me again, but this time I meet her blow with my own. The clash of metal sings through the air, and the power behind her strike sends jolts through my arm.

How is she so strong?!!

The muscle and tendons may be hanging off her bones like ribbons, but she has a strength that would give Torsten a run for his money. She's also a good 4 inches taller than me. We stare each other down in the bind, and when I look into those glowing pools of eyes I see no sign of humanity. Only the promise of death.

Cold dread sinks into my bones, but I shake it away.

You have to win!

I manuever the strong edge of my sword to try to wrench hers down, but she resists, launching me away from the bind with the force of an ox, slamming me into the side of the boat hard enough to send wood debris everywhere.

Oof, that one fucking hurt.

Digging my heels into the rotting wood, I manage to regain my footing and we begin to circle each other. My eyes search for any weaknesses, but it's impossible to find them when she literally looks like a walking corpse and is defying the laws of physics. She has inhuman strength and telekinetic powers apparently...at least with that sword...so I'm quite at the disadvantage here.

Just as I feared, she grips her other hand into a fist, and a pile of weapons nearby start rattling. My eyes widen as three swords come flying at me from the pile. I use the blade of my sword to block and change the trajectory of two, but I'm not fast enough for the third and it grazes my right bicep, slicing through skin. I hiss at the pain, but don't give myself the luxury of examining my wound as I refuse to take my eyes off of her.

With another growl, she sweeps her sword over her head and levels it towards my face in challenge. Not giving her another chance to use those telekinetic powers, I throw a cut of my own and she meets it midway with another swoop. With a metallic clank, the top half of my rusted sword clutters to the ground.

Shit!

I clamber back, ducking another slash and try grasp a hold of anything around me. A shield! I bring it up in front of my face just in time for her sword to slice into it like butter, catching on the iron knob center with a clunk. My arms shake with the effort of keeping her at bay, but thankfully her sword is preoccupied. I throw the shield to the side to throw off her balance, and it barely gives me enough time to retreat further back into the boat, grabbing anything I can to throw at her head. A barrel of supplies. A pot. She slashes them away like they are toys.

Think Beatrix! Fucking think!

I'm starting to panic, but I can't afford to do that right now. In the midst of tossing more debris at the creature, I find another sword, unsheath it, just to find it as rusty as the last. But it's got a point and sharp edge and I can work with that. Rather than aiming for her chest and head, I duck her next slash, dropping to the side and throwing my sword out to slice the back of her left knee with everything I have. She makes no sound that acknowledges any sort of pain, but I've cut through all the rotting tendons in her knee, perhaps some bone as well, and she drops to her other knee with a beastly grunt.

Without hesitating, I leap up, plunging the sword into her back with all my body weight until I feel it pierce through her other side and into the rotten wood floor, then I twist. I scream from the effort, but when she tries to throw me off her back, she is unable to, trapped. When that doesn't work, she slashes blindly behind her with her sword, and another hiss of her blade comes too close to my face for my comfort. I jump off, searching frantically for another weapon while I have her subdued. After tossing some broken wood shards aside, my hands find a Dane axe and I lurch back towards her, dragging it with me. She's still hunched over, trying to reach behind with one arm to grasp the hilt of the blade trapping her to the floor, or...wait....

Although she's not touching it, the blade is starting to untwist from the ground.

Fuck! I got to act fast!

Hefting the Dane axe up over my head, I sweep it down in a wide arc, bringing it upon her neck with all my might. Steel meets flesh, and her head lops off and hits the ground with a thud, stilling her body. Sweating, my shift covered in dirt and blood, I slump to the floor, using the staff of the Dane axe to support me while I catch my breath.

Well done, Wanderer. Now claim the spoils you have reaped.

I lift my head, glancing at the sword still gripped in the corpse's hand. It beckons to me, glimmering in the moonlight. With a crunching sound that makes me wince and want to gag, I lift the bony fingers one by one that grip the sword until it's released. Sword in hand, I search for the scabbard and find it discarded amongst the debris. After re-sheathing the blade, I can't help remember what Freyja mentioned earlier.

The real treasure has not yet been sought.

What did she mean by that? She also mentioned spoils. Plural. As in more than one. But looking around, I locate no silver, gold, or other weapons unmarred by rot and rust. Except...I look back at the corpse. The soft, golden feathers of the cloak seem to still shift on a phantom breeze despite it's wearer being no longer reanimated, and I shiver. But...like the sword, it looks untouched by time and ruin.

I step closer to the corpse, examining the cloak further. The feathers, which I realize now are falcon's, are woven in delicate patterns that must have taken countless hours to sew into the fabric. Doing my best not to tear the fabric further, I untwist the blade with a grunt and pull the hilt from the corpse's back. The effort has me sweating again, removing the blade being twice as hard as when I put it there, but I finally manage to free it from her and I let it clatter to the floor. I see now that a golden clasp keeps it rooted at the corpse's neck, but with her head no longer there, I'm able to slide the cloak off. I try not to look to closely at the severed stump while I do so but it's hard not to throw up.

Years later, I have the cloak free. As soon as it's in my arms, a feeling of lightness comes over me, as if I could leap into the air and take flight like a bird, but that's absolutely absurd.

Come to think of it, how AM I supposed to get back? Do I call for Heimdall again....Or is the test not finished? I shudder at the thought of facing something else...but...if I have to...I will.

I look to the cloak again, trying to locate where the tear must have happened when I plunged the sword into the corpse, but I can't locate it.

That's....odd.

Like the sword, this must be a magical item.

"So....what am I supposed to do now to get out of here?" I whisper to the dark. "Heimdall?"

Moments pass, but nothing happens. No one answers.

I sigh.

"Well, I guess I'm climbing my ass out of here."

I walk back to the circle of light in the ceiling, my only exit, and eye it skeptically, a good 5 feet or so above me.

Maybe I could use the sword to climb up?

Deep within me, Freyja stirs, but with an air of annoyance. Clearly, I am missing something here, but she's not going to spoon feed it to me, obviously.

I look back down at the cloak and raise an eyebrow.

There's no way.

But, what do I have to lose?

In a dramatic sweep, I throw the cloak over my shoulders and that feeling of lightness gets even stronger. Alright, here goes nothing.

I throw my hands in the air and jump, superman style...and promptly land back on my feet in the mound, sinking into a hole in the rotten wood caused by the impact. My hands swing back down to my sides as I look down to inspect the damage. I bet Heimdall is laughing his ass off up there back in Asgard.

Time to take a different approach, I guess. I close my eyes, feeling the soft feathers brushing against my skin, imagining that they are my own.

Take it further.

I imagine my bones shifting, reforming, shrinking down within myself to become something else. My whole body starts to tingle, starting at my nose, moving it's way to my ears, and and then down to my fingertips. Suddenly, a sharp pain tears throughout my limbs, and I double over. When I cry out, the sound I make is no longer human. I no longer feel the cloak. I feel....different.

My talons grip the earth beneath me. I cock my head left and right, and adjust my wings. Finally, I open my eyes...and catch the dust particles sifting through the moonlit air, the legs of a beetle cleaning it's antennae while it rests on an exposed root hanging from the ceiling, and the refracting colors of light of a dew drop falling from that root. It's like seeing through a macro and zoom lens at the same time. But I feel....a little less like myself. I don't think I can stay like this long...my gut tells me.

I hop over to the sword that fell to the ground when I changed, grasping my talons around the leather strap of the scabbard. Then with a leap, I take flight....and run into the wall, dropping the sword.

Oof. Guess I gotta get used to this new body.

After shaking my head, I swoop down to clutch the strap and try again, this time pumping my wings harder to go straight upwards towards the ceiling. It's a bit dodgy of a maneuver, but I manage to break through the surface. I make a shrill call in triumph, and it echoes over the open field.

Climbing higher, I get a spectacular view of the sparkling fjord and mountains surrounding, and my heart soars.

I'm flying! I'm actually fucking flying! This is incred...

Rainbow luminescence surrounds me, pulling me from my victory flight. I'm plucked out of the air in one moment, then flying directly into a very startled Arne the next.

"ARRGH!!"

We collide, feathers, sword and all, knocking Arne into the tub behind him. Frantic, I flap my wings to try to escape but Arne has got a hold of me.

LET ME GO! I want to shriek, but all that comes out of my beak is a wild "KEK-KEK-KEK!!!'

"CALM DOWN BEAST! I will not hurt you! OW! Do not bite me! How in Odin's name did you get in here???"

I'm about to snap at him again, then pause. This is Arne! It's okay.....he's not...going to hurt you...I'm struggling to form thoughts.

Uh oh. I need to change back, and I need to change back fast.

But how?!

I look up at Arne in fear, eliciting a nervous "Kik-Kek-Kek!" but I don't know how to communicate what's going on.

"It's alright...there now...see...I'm not going to hurt you," Arne coos, stroking my feathers. That...that fees nice, actually.

What was I supposed to do?

My heartbeat finally starts to slow down again, and I shift my talons so that I am perched on Arne's forearm. He looks at me reverently.

"You sure are beautiful, aren't you. It's a bit late for you to be flying out like this, isn't it?"

Do not dally, Wanderer. You will need to build your stamina to keep this form.

I cock my head, to and fro. "Kek?"

NOW WANDERER!

"Do you know where my Beatrix went off to, my lovely falcon?" Arne asks.

Beatrix...

A sense of urgency hits me like a truck, but it feels far away at the same time. I think of a woman with curly copper hair. Imagine myself being her.

Take it further.

My bones start shifting, twisting, turning, and I shriek in pain.

My scream turns guttural and human, and then I am me again. Naked, with the exception of the cloak draped behind me, sword in my hands, in the tub with Arne, as he's holding me in his arms and looking at me like he's seen a fucking ghost.

Why the fuck do I have to be naked?

"Oh...Hello... I'm back," I say with a crooked grin and wave awkwardly up at him.
