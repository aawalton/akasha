A bare left uppercut, delivered with a fist wrapped in chains, lifted Cromm bodily off the ground.

Another tight left cross, a picture-perfect kinetic chain from feet to taught back-muscles to arm to fist, all lit up with essence—landed clean, and snapped its head almost all the way around.

In its Supreme state the Meat King was overrun with Pure Size, Size at nearly 100% of the Law's effect. But with the forces it was taking, you never would've known it.

Its Vitality visibly started to tank in the Astral Plane.

Stegar's response told Zane something about the Primes' understanding of how this fight was going. Arcs of steel flew out from its back, circling to get a better angle; thick beams of ion light sliced out from them, each with 800+ shards of Destruction—a vast expenditure of essence, even for a Prime.

And the entire Mirrorworld angled to accommodate it. With this much light lancing the air he knew keeping up a clean fight was impossible.

He kept diligent with his blocking, kept letting Cromm take the bulk of the hits, but he accepted a few ion beams to the back. It felt like he'd been lashed with a whip of pure energy, but it didn't halt him.

Cromm redoubled its efforts, limbs flooding with that deathly white essence, but it was clear it was two steps behind. It couldn't get its bearings, nor its balance—it seemed utterly lost in the fight.

Zane kept hitting it again, and again, and again—still simple, purely mechanical force.

Which was supposed to be Cromm's game. This pure, Skill-less, essence-less fight...

If it couldn't beat him like this, it was finished.

The Mirror Dragon threw up new crystals—focusing shards that made some of those ion beams hit so hard they actually managed to halt Zane, just briefly.

It still wasn't enough to get Cromm truly on its feet.

Its Health dipped below 40%... then the silver in its eyes flickered brighter.

And Zane saw six bones light up in its skeleton. Every one a Sacred Bone, shining with the light of its Supreme.

Its signature essence flooded out of its arm bones, flooding the muscles there, and he saw them transform in quality; saw them go up the equivalent of several treasure grades in the Astral Plane, the muscles went the color of its soul—

It didn't matter. It couldn't make use of them at all, it could barely stay on its feet.

He saw a bone light up in its skull. Its entire nervous system took that same deathly white color, and it started twitching violently, at such speeds that its entire body was shrouded in afterimages. Something akin to the Bloody Starveds' hyper-reflexes, Zane quickly realized.

Three exchanges passed where he couldn't land—his fists grazing afterimages—just enough for it to halt its backward momentum.

Then chains wrapped its arms, wrapped its legs, stilling it.

It tried wrenching free one of its newly empowered arms, only to receive such a combination Zane designed to halt this comeback in its tracks.

A left hook to the left side, hammering internal organs he'd just shattered, followed by a wound-back, full-body uppercut.

He landed, and it might've been the hardest he'd ever hit another living creature. It was an uppercut that represented the full capacity of his physicality—at least, his full capacity without his Supreme.

He saw its body go slack for a split second like he'd hit it so hard its body had shut off for that tiny moment.

The punishment resumed.

A bone lit up in its chest, flickering desperately, and it granted an insane flood of vitality—enough to replenish the Health pools of most Empyreans six or seven times over—it might've given it five seconds' more reprieve, but that was all.

It still hadn't even truly hit him.

And he knew it might have a chance to take this fight back if it did; at least, he still knew it could hurt him with the strength in its arms.

He'd simply make sure it'd never get that chance.

He'd expected more anger here. But it still seemed more disoriented than anything, and he realized it'd never felt like this—it'd never been truly hit like this before.

He was also starting to understand the Primes' plan.

He figured they'd been trying to draw out his Supreme here, and stall it out—'waste' it on this floor before he got a chance to wield it in the Throne Room. In other words—extract that concession from him, and maybe even kill him here if they were lucky.

The Mirror Dragon screeched at Stegar now, and its tone made him more certain of it.

He'd come in with that possibility in the back of his mind and had told himself he wouldn't give them that concession here. It was looking like he'd stand by that without issue.

Zane landed one more blow on Cromm, a clean, hard straight.

It was a blow like any of the dozens of blows it'd taken.

But this was the one that brought it to a knee.

Another clean fist brought it face-first to the ground.

It was a performance that quite frankly engendered no respect in Zane.

A stronger mind, with that body, could've challenged him, he felt.

He dropped his chains and willed his axes to hand. All he had on his mind was the finish; it was his sole focus.

Then the Mirror Dragon screeched once more, and Stegar nodded. Its drones instantly beamed back to its main body.

The Dragon slammed its two front limbs into the crystalline ground.

Essence surged in—and an array circle appeared underneath the crystal, out of thin air, several miles in size—something that had to have been prepared ages before.

On pure instinct, Zane re-summoned his chains.

An immense light lit up the circle.

The surface of that smooth field of crystal cracked. A shining crack that ran miles and miles—Zane just barely managed to loop a chain around Cromm's ankle.

A blink, and that single crack was ten thousand.

Another blink, and he was wrenched sideways, hard, by a sudden force—a force wrenching up his own chain.

He heard Reina cry out something, but their connection was severed mid-sentence.

And then he was tumbling, utterly unsure of up and down.

He felt Cromm just yards away—felt a burst of space and essence, the signature of Stegar hyper-jumping—felt several of its drones latching onto Cromm, trying to spirit it away.

He gritted his teeth and wrenched, and the Meat King tumbled toward him as he clenched his other fist tight—

He could've righted himself, escaped this. But he made a decision in that moment, and he committed.

Air.

A new world. A world with a single moon that took up nearly a third of the sky, and below lay endless fields of white flowers. Each field had a single spike planted in the middle, a spike that registered to Zane's mind as a 100,000+-year Sacred Bone. He saw dark, bony hands, each 5-6 feet tall, reaching out of the soil—it all seemed some kind of strange garden, or perhaps a farm.

This all came to him in a split-second as he grappled, through chains, with what he now knew to be the second-strongest creature in the Galaxy.

And now that it had a point of connection, his chains—and a moment to get its bearings, without a constant hammering of blows—and its ally, trying to wrest it free—it seemed to realize the importance of this moment.

It finally truly started putting up a fight.

He was just three miles from the ground, teeth gritted, when he glimpsed the array formation light up somewhere far below, stretching miles and miles—

Fissures of Destruction-light split that strange world.

Then the realm gave way to void.

He gave a full-body, blood-in-mouth effort as they passed the line between worlds, and that was enough to send the Meat King hurtling over to him. Twenty feet, ten—

They grappled, still falling—wondrous sights streaming past, barely seen—a floor full of jagged tears of space and tiny portals went flying by...

Pocket dimensions all over, left and right, and spatial tunnels scattered throughout—

Hreinn's Lair?

He saw only fragments. He lashed a second point of connection, a new chain, on the Meat King.

At this point Cromm surprised him by stopping its resistance.

Instead it pulled, matching his force—he blinked, and then a single giant fist slammed into his chin, and he heard Cromm's triumphant bellow.

He was right.

It could still hit very hard, if given the chance.

Still not hard enough to knock Zane out. But enough to daze him for a split second, and in that split second, he saw the next world stream by, upside down—no, he was upside down—an astonishing sight in other circumstances, a pristine bone-white tree dozens of miles high, with leaves the color of soul silver, leaves that drifted slowly down, landing on the surface of an onyx river—it all came to him through blurry eyes... Daressa's Lair, he realized dimly.

His jaw set; it all snapped back into focus.

He snapped back just quickly enough to dodge another blow, wrenched his foe closer, treated it to a punch of his own, right to the center of its face. Its head snapped back, like it'd done so many times—

Its giant hands clamped around his head.

It'd managed to keep its bearings this time.

He couldn't see. It tried digging its thumbs into his eyes.

It squeezed, trying to crack his skull like a melon.

Its deathly-white essence flooded Zane's head, his brain—a very strange, translucent energy. It somehow instantly bypassed his soul defenses, and it felt like it struck directly at his Vitality. It had an effect on his soul he couldn't quite pinpoint in the moment. One of Reina’s spells wreathed his soul like a soft shield, and the essence slowed as it met the barrier, lessening slightly—but it still passed through.

It felt like his head had been cased in a block of ice.

It managed this for all of a second before Zane pried off its hands, teeth gritted.

He took that chance to wrap its remaining arm in chains once more.

They broke through another layer of void, still locked in combat, silver lights winking out behind them—

They traded, again, and again—and Zane had felt strangely weak in those first few seconds after it'd clamped its hands on his head, but his strength was quickly returning. It'd felt as if his soul was strangely detached from his body for a few seconds, like the bulk of his strength was still there, but it'd been locked away from his mind—not unlike those dreams where you can't seem to exert force properly. An incredibly strange sensation.

In that time he'd felt slightly out of it, like all his senses were quite murky—but somehow his body still knew how to fight, and he emerged in solid position.

Out of the corner of Zane's gaze there loomed a dark continent.

He let out a war bellow.

One, two—he felt Cromm's arms go strangely limp. Three, four, five, each blow more decisive than the last—

Cromm's back struck solid ground.

It lay there, still limp.

And only as Zane knelt on top of its unmoving body, knuckles bloodied to the bone, chest heaving, did he realize what he'd just done.

The notification popped up a second later.

A Prime Endbringer has been slain.

In its Supreme, the Meat King, Cromm, had taken the attacks of half of Dragonspire's finest and lived. In its Supreme Command had considered it essentially unkillable.

He'd just beaten it to death—while it was in its Supreme—with his bare hands.

And this was his choice. He could've let it go quite early—let it go free as the worlds collapsed.

He'd made a conscious choice, pretty certain of where it'd end.

Then he looked where he'd landed, and he knew.
