Not everyone was as useless at a distance as Luke. Curtis took a page out of the demon's playbook and unfurled large wings of darkness, taking to the air. While it worked well enough, it was plain for everyone to see that the Reaver hadn't spent a whole lot of time practicing his flying skill, because he was anything but graceful in the air. Hannah took a different approach and ran to a wall to get a clear line of sight to a chandelier hanging from the roof. She sank into her own shadow and appeared far above, hanging from a chain as her shadows descended on Simean. The monster kept its gaze on Curtis and the rest of them on the ground, and didn't spot her until it was too late.

Like a blob of oil, she splattered all over Simean and lodged herself on the demon's back, holding onto its wings. Despite her best efforts, she wasn't able to bring their opponent to the ground.

More and more spells flew out, most of them variations on the first, with burning sludge hurtling in every direction. Others sent spears of flame toward the floor, or strange, writhing constructs that let out moaning sounds and came apart when they struck the ground. Luke saw more of what might be souls rising back up from those, even as he flailed with Threads of Mana to keep everyone safe. They'd gathered around him. Bunching up like that meant all enemy spells hammered against one concentrated spot, but the others weren't able to defend themselves, so that job landed in Luke's lap. Better that than healing seared flesh, at least.

Curtis made it high enough to grab hold of Simean's clawed foot, then pushed again with his wings before they fell apart, turning into another shape, that of a large two-handed blade. The Reaver swung with one hand, and it looked like it would be a beautiful blow, with the monster preoccupied with Hannah and all its spell-flinging. Rather than strike at flesh, however, it was met with the wails of souls, poured into what Luke first thought was a shield emerging from the runes on the demon's chest. But it wasn't a shield, but a sword. It dwarfed Curtis's and was crafted from the same sort of material the monster had pulled from the dead cultists. Judging by the sounds coming from it when Simean struck out and hit Curtis's blade, shrieks of pain, souls might have been trapped inside.

"They're feeling pain," Nick breathed, walking up to the rest of them. "Thank the Lord Clementine isn't here to witness this."

Luke nodded as he sliced another spell apart, unraveling the spellweave hiding within the attack. "The quest says we should be freeing souls. Are these it?"

"Maybe," Ariel said.

"I doubt this creature is The Conqueror," George said, his face twisted in concentration as he attempted to wield his skills from a distance.

"Let's kill it and find out," Luke said, looking to the others.

Nick shrugged. "How? My brother is doing the best he can up there."

It was true. Curtis had been thrown off Simean after that initial exchange of blows and was now doing his best to keep up with the more maneuverable monster in the air, his two-handed sword of darkness clashing against the enormous blade of souls the demon wielded. Hannah did her best to hinder its movements, but it didn't look like she was accomplishing much. The barbs and teeth of her shadow-tentacles were digging into the monster, Luke knew, but he wasn't sure it'd be enough.

Just as that thought passed through his mind, Curtis took an ugly blow as he failed to deflect or parry a strike, and it followed through, digging into and through his shoulder. Rather than meeting nothing more than darkness, as was usual when someone or something struck the Reaver, it bit into his actual body, digging a deep wound that saw him tumbling from the air. The dropped sword of darkness dissipated as Curtis struggled to use the darkness within the blade to heal himself, even as he dropped toward the ground.

"Shit," Ray said, but Luke wasn't listening. Instead, he charged forward. Once the swordplay had begun in earnest, the spells had stopped coming, so the others would be somewhat safe for a little while. Luke was not about to lose Curtis to the first dungeon they did together after reuniting. He wasn't going to lose his friends, not ever, for as long as he drew breath.

Nick, much quicker than Luke himself, rushed past and advanced to the spot where Curtis would land. Rather than just stand there, the Knight leapt into the air, grabbed Curtis, landed, and rolled. The two made it. Curtis was still breathing. What neither of them saw, however, was the massive demon swooping down, sword held high, coming for them with incredible speed.

Luke sped up. If he timed this right, then maybe, just maybe, he'd be able to do something before the two brothers took a sword swing straight through their chests. While flight was beyond Luke, perhaps he didn't need wings to get into position. Instead, he jumped, using his now much stronger legs to gain a decent amount of air, aided by pushing down into the ground with threads of mana, adding speed and momentum. Faster, he had to go faster. And it wasn't just his speed that was lacking, but also his altitude. Luke needed to go higher, much higher. That was when Weavestep came into play.

The new rank gave him the ability to add to his momentum, pushing himself into a leap. While he'd practiced as he ran toward New York, this was different. Not only did he need to get high enough, but he also needed to time it just right. The first use of the skill added so much speed that it took Luke by surprise, and he rose through the air, almost like he'd done a double jump in some platformer. Screaming, he angled the next thread of mana just right, pointing himself down and to the side before launching himself.

'NOW!" he shouted at himself, performing another weavestep while pulling something out of his inventory.

The idea had been a good one in theory, but the execution left a little to be desired. Luke tumbled through the air with incredible speed. Pulling out the staff, he used every bit of momentum he could muster to put weight behind the blow. He'd overshot the target a little, which meant he soared above Simean's head, but the length of the staff proved sufficient, and Luke slammed it into the raging demon's face, caving it in, much like with the Solarian. It was like hitting a stone wall. He had done everything he could, but as he soared past, he saw it wasn't enough.

Burning sludge poured out of its face, and it had slowed, but the demon kept going, hurtling toward Curtis and Nick with the sword of souls held at the ready. Luke slammed into the far wall with a thud and began to fall. As he did so, he saw Nick stand and turn. The distance was too great to make out any expression, but the Knight radiated a cold rage as he set his shoulders and lifted both sword and shield to stand against the demon.

Simean's sword streaked through the air, leaving afterimages of drifting, wailing souls. The blade slammed into Nick's raised shield, dwarfing it in sheer size. To Luke's surprise, the shield held. The stone around Nick and Curtis, still behind his younger brother, cracked in several places, and the shield buckled inward, but it held, and Nick himself seemed unhurt. A faint, light-blue glow surrounded Nick. He drew back his sword and stepped in as the demon pulled back for another swing.

A single thrust opened a gaping hole in Simean's arm, more sludge pouring out. Nick continued his assault, advancing to the legs. The blade extended with a blue, rather than white, glow mid-swipe and dug through the monster's leg in a single attack, severing it.

"DIE, DEMON!" Nick shouted as he thrust forward again.

Hurt by Luke's assault on its face, Simean couldn't see well enough to protect himself. The loss of a leg unbalanced him, and the constant leaking of its life-energy was starting to get to it. Nick's thrust speared through the monster's gut, coming out on the other side. Holding up his shield to divert the flow of sludge pouring out, the Knight danced to the side, avoiding a slow, clumsy overhand blow from the blade of souls.

Luke had landed with a thud just as Nick engaged and was running in to help, but he was still too far away when Simean batted its wings, trying to escape back into the air.

"Oh, no, you don't!" Hannah shouted. She'd been all over the monster's wings for quite a while now, and had, it seemed, managed to break through, because Simean's wings were now all but useless.

Ray appeared out of stealth by the side of Simean's head and slammed both daggers right through its skull before continuing forward, landing in a roll and getting to his feet. Dirk was satisfied using his arrows, several copies letting loose a constant barrage while Curtis got to his feet. The Reaver wanted vengeance, that much was obvious, and he was set on getting it using the two-handed sword of darkness.

This time, he took a page out of his younger brother's book. The dark blade extended as he swung, reaching Simean's raised sword arm, taking it off at the shoulder.

Even with all that, the demon refused to die. Instead, it reared back and let out a deafening roar of rage before spinning to get everyone off it, succeeding in shaking even Hannah off. Freed, it dashed away, clawing at the air to open a path for itself back to whatever hell it came from. But it must've made some mistake in its addled state, because no opening appeared. When it still threw itself through, landing on its belly on the floor, Luke knew something was off. It didn't take a genius to know who'd stepped in to close off its path of escape. George held a self-satisfied smirk where he stood, off to the side.

Nick, Curtis, Ray, and Dirk caught up in an instant and cut the monster apart until it stopped moving, living far longer than Luke would have thought possible. In the end, though, the fight left it, along with enough burning sludge to create your own volcano.
