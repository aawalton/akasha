The Way, or Cerpitals' Way as it was infamously known as, was a passageway between dungeons. No one had been able to figure out how it worked or what exactly The Way was, and even though there wasn't any confirmed theory by the synced researchers, one had gained weight over the last decade. One that most of the residents of Rinesta believed was the truth.

The Way was suspected by the majority of synced researchers to be the space in between.

Well, that was how it had been explained to him, and right now, as Levi stood on the void expanse atop a glowing translucent blue bridge, he couldn't argue with the theory.

From his perspective, he could certainly say that the bridge looked endless, and that sent a chill up his spine.

What would happen to me if I were trapped here?

Levi stared at the screen, going over the details until he was satisfied that he had everything down. The last part didn't concern him as much as it should've because he had no plans of staying beyond the time limit given to him. Any of the five dungeons offered to him would work for him, as he was only looking for a means to escape Gantus. A means that was now in his grasp, and all he had to do was choose, but he held back, his eyes transfixed to a singular dungeon — Dungeon D.

The dungeon was located to the west of the dungeon of Gantus, in the same direction the mage had told him to go if he could survive the night. The mage had told him to seek out the black baron in Plantrith, and while he had no idea who the black baron was or where Plantrith was, his answers lay there, or maybe his death did. It wasn't far-fetched for him to assume that the mage only let him absorb the stone so that he could take it to the black baron and have the stone taken from him — and by extension, his life. He shrugged his shoulders in annoyance at the sudden complexity of his situation.

"I could still go west, just not to Plantrith." He muttered, "I'll find out more about this Black Baron before making my decision."

Levi nodded his head at his idea. He wasn't in any rush to get to the black baron, and he'd have enough time to make a decision, especially since the settlement closest to the dungeon D would be Pelossi, not Plantrith. Truth be told, he didn't know either of those places, but he expected them to be different places, and that would be enough for him.

"I choose Dungeon D," Levi said.

[ Location Confirmed;

Levi Tate has chosen Dungeon D.

Instruction: Walk through the portal that appears on the bridge to be transported to your chosen location. Beware of your protection timer.

Protection timer [ 2 : 39]

]

A blue portal appeared five paces from where he stood on the glowing bridge. Needing no further prompting, the rogue hurried to the portal, a mixture of excitement and dread propelling him forward. Excitement because it was going to be his first time using the way and dread because something could go wrong and he'd be trapped here, in the way, left to battle the corrosion of The Way once Cerpitals' protection wore off.

It was a short walk, but one that he'd been extra careful making. He hadn't walked on the magical bridge since he'd appeared on it; aside from that, the bridge itself lacked any sort of barrier or railing, which made him nervous. His dread only dissipating once he'd made it to the glowing portal. The rogue didn't hesitate for a second before going through it, another wave of tingly sensation moving through his body as he exited Cerpitals' Way.

Relief flooded Levi's body as he safely made it to the other side of the portal. A wave of fresh air hit his face that he didn't know he'd missed. He took a step forward, and the portal behind him winked out of existence, causing him to look back in askance before returning his gaze to survey the room. It was bigger than the boss room of the dungeon that he was coming from. The room was well-lit by multiple glow stones that were mounted on the walls and ceiling.

The safe room was tiled and devoid of any furniture, which Levi could understand. His eyes turned to the side as a blue portal suddenly winked into existence a few feet to his right. His heart leaped to his throat at the sight of the portal, his own heartbeat echoing in his ears as he paled, his fear of Ghost chasing him to the ends of Rinesta for the stone coming to the fore. Levi pulled up his daggers from underneath his cloak, where he'd hidden them, his knuckles turning white from how tightly he gripped them.

How could he have found me so quickly? Levi thought.

He couldn't believe that the rogue could have found him that quickly, especially since he himself hadn't even known where he was going a few minutes ago. Even with the multiple hows and whys bouncing about in his head and the fear saturating his body, the rogue kept his focus on the Portal, defiance shining in his eyes. If it were Ghost, it wouldn't do him any good to leave himself exposed by trying to flee towards the saferoom exit, which was further away to the North Side of the room.

He'd have to stand and fight. Sure, his exhaustion had begun to return, but he didn't think a fully revitalized him would have a chance at holding back Ghost. If anything, this was just the confidence from his recent achievement of clearing the dungeon seeping into his brain and muddying his thoughts. The old him would've sprinted towards the exit as far as he could, even though it was a certified lost cause.

Shaking his head to clear his distraction, he focused on the blue portal, which seemed to grow brighter, and soon enough, the person on the other side crossed over. Levi was on them in a flash, the rogue registering too late that the person who'd crossed to the other side wasn't Ghost, but a girl his age with luscious locks. In his bid to catch Ghost, he clattered into her hard, unable to stop his momentum.

The collision between the two synced sent them tumbling to the ground and away from the portal, which soon winked out of existence. The pair rolled to a stop, with Levi atop her. Heat spread to his face at the situation he found himself in, and before he could utter a word, a fireball hit him square in the face.

[ You have lost 15 health ]

"Arrgghh," he screamed, as he discarded Cerpitals' message. The heat from her fireball hit him square in the face, singeing his eyebrows, baby moustache, and a bit of his front hair. The brightness of the fireball caused him to shut his eyes even as he threw himself to the side, distancing himself from the girl he'd just collided with.

When Levi opened his eyes, the girl stood over him as he lay panting on the floor. In her right hand was another fireball, and when Levi looked into her eyes, he saw righteous anger at being bundled over for no apparent reason. The girl, who was undoubtedly a mage, evidenced by her fireball and the mage robe she wore, stood poised to release her readied fireball when Levi raised a hand, causing her to stop; her fireball still hovering in her right hand.

"Sorry, I thought you were someone else. Someone bad!" Levi said hurriedly, trying to avoid getting hit by the second fireball.

"Hmmph," The girl said, her hand arcing up for another strike before she stopped abruptly. Her eyes widened momentarily as she glanced at the top of his head, which was exposed due to his hood being down. She smoothed over her features, her arced hand going down as the fireball dissipated into nothingness. A full-fledged glare returned to her face a heartbeat later, and Levi was left a bit confused at what had just happened.

"Keep your body to yourself, creep." She said. Muttering something else about 'idiots' as she walked towards the exit. Leaving him alone on the floor in the safe room. His confusion still evident.

"Women," He muttered as he pushed himself back up.

He'd never been able to understand girls back in Gantus, and it seemed that it still hadn't changed after getting synced. Try as he might, Levi couldn't just set aside what had happened; he couldn't fathom why she'd stopped upon looking closer at his face. Sure, he was handsome, at least he considered himself so, but that wouldn't warrant him getting off the hook that easily. The rogue wondered what exactly had prompted the look of surprise that had briefly crossed her features. Maybe it was just his face... or not, because her eyes had only widened once she'd looked a bit higher up, once she'd looked towards his forehead, towards the mark.

Grak

Levi cursed himself internally as he remembered that he'd been walking about with the freaking tattoo on his forehead exposed. Well, it was incomplete as of now, and he didn't think the girl had any idea about what the tattoo actually meant, but he still couldn't rule it out. She'd definitely seen something to make her stop, and his words definitely weren't what had saved him from her wrath. Shrugging his shoulders at the weird encounter, he pulled the hood over his head.

Regardless of whether the girl knew what his incomplete tattoo meant, he couldn't take any further risks, especially since his life was on the line. If anything, this was a subtle reminder that he had to be cautious of the tattoo on his forehead; he couldn't afford any more slip-ups like that. With his hood securely over his head, he waited a bit, making sure the mage he'd just attacked would have gotten far enough before he headed for the exit, eager to put this horrid day behind him.
