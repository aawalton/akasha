Arthur retraced his path through the cave using the flawless memory he’d honed back in his spying days. But halfway to the entrance, he paused. The ground ahead looked… browner. Earthier. Suspiciously suspicious.

He narrowed his eyes and scanned the area more carefully.

You have acquired a new skill: Mana Sight.

“Wait, what?” Arthur blinked as the System threw the message in his face.

He mentally willed more information, and a short description appeared:

Mana Sight – Pass mana through your eyes to perceive the flow and density of mana in your surroundings.

Arthur relayed the discovery to Drogan, who went uncharacteristically quiet.

“…You’ve gotta be kidding me,” Drogan muttered eventually. “Unfair… unbelievable… I had to train blindfolded in a gravity pit for three years to get that skill.”

Arthur pretended not to hear the sulking and pressed on. “So, how do I use it?”

Drogan grumbled. “Close your eyes. Shut out the world. Focus internally. Use your heartbeat as a guide. Search for the spark. If you’re lucky, in a year or two, you might spot your soul’s mana node. And then, if you’re extraordinarily talented, you’ll coax the mana to flow.”

Arthur followed the instructions. Then, in about three seconds, found a glowing blue nebula hovering in his chest.

“...What the hell?” he whispered.

When he willed the mana to his eyes, it flowed instantly.

He opened his eyes.

And immediately regretted everything.

The world exploded in color - mana was everywhere, radiating from walls, dripping off stone, swirling through the air like a rainbow on caffeine. The data slammed into his brain like a sledgehammer. He grabbed his head, clenched his teeth, and squeezed his eyes shut. Only his past life’s torture training kept him from screaming.

Desperate, he willed the mana toward his brain. The pain dulled. Relief washed through him like cold water after a fever. He inhaled slowly, redirected the mana back to the spark in his chest, and opened his eyes again.

Mana Sight increased by 2 levels. New Skill Acquired: Mana Heal.

Mana Heal – Pass mana to an injured area to accelerate healing. Effectiveness depends on injury severity and mana applied.

Drogan, meanwhile, was muttering curses.

“You’ve had the System for, what, ten minutes? Overpowered little brat! You sure you’re not secretly a demigod?””

Arthur ignored him and began experimenting. He pushed mana to his legs and tried a forward sprint.

He shot across the tunnel like a launched potato.

Right into a wall.

Face-first.

He crumpled to the floor with a thud, blood dripping from his nose, his lips numb, and a few teeth suspiciously… absent.

Drogan laughed so hard he wheezed.

Arthur groaned and, through the pain, focused mana to his face.

The bruises faded first. Then the cartilage in his nose snapped back into place with a wet crunch. A few moments later, the System pinged him again:

Mana Overdrain: 0/180

Pain exploded in his chest.

He stopped the flow immediately and gasped. “Drogan,” he croaked, “how do I recover mana?”

“There are two ways. First, meditate. Slowly absorb ambient mana. Safe, clean, boring.”

“And the second?”

“Mana potions. Chug one and get a refill. But they leave impurities. Long term? They’ll screw with your growth potential.”

Arthur checked his nonexistent inventory. No potions. So he sat down cross-legged.

He focused on his breathing, on the mana in the air. Most of it flowed past unnoticed, save for a single thread that trailed his breath. He caught it, willed it to the spark.

His mana ticked from 0 to 1.

A single point.

But it was enough.

Arthur exhaled and fell into rhythm. Inhale. Mana. Exhale. Focus. Inhale. Gather. Exhale. Store.

And then...

Arthur smiled.

A slow, tired, victorious grin.

And though he was still bruised, bloodied, and definitely down a couple of teeth…

He was smiling all the same.

Gap-toothed. Glowing.

Because for the first time in this world,

He was finally getting somewhere.
