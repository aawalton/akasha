The feeling of the Ghost Qi, or rather, Yin Qi, got stronger, and my cultivation technique was yearning for it.

Using this technique, I could break through into the Foundation Establishment Realm without any issues, but the cost of becoming a ghost-like being was too great.

Even with Elder Su’s technique, it was still a risky endeavor. Not to mention that it would severely limit my potential.

I had to admit, it was a little funny that a Yin cultivation technique was my first one, then got a lightning cultivation technique afterwards. Pretty ironic.

Such was fate, I supposed. It wasn’t like I wanted to keep using the ghost technique. It was too bad I couldn’t remember any other technique, or I would have already broken through and dominated these lower realms…

But the Divine Lightning Transformation Technique was pretty good in its own right.

Besides the added moveset and techniques, just being able to form a Lightning Soul was a great boon.

I wasn’t sure if I wanted to take that path but-

Shaking my head, I realised that I was letting myself get distracted by stray thoughts again. That wasn’t good.

Taking a deep breath, I flared up my Ghost technique, and from deep within the far ends of the north of the jungle, something replied.

I shot in that direction without hesitation, trees, Spirit Beasts, clouds, and everything else becoming a blur as I hurried forward, collecting points at the same time.

After flying for what felt like hours, though I couldn’t be sure, since the sun wasn’t moving, I’d arrived at a place where the concentration of Yin Qi was greatest.

Strangely enough, it was just as Elder Su had said. ‘Walk north,h and you will find it.’

In retrospect, that was rather weird, but I couldn’t deny that it was true.

And with that revelation done, I landed in the vicinity, probing for an entrance.

I couldn’t see anything besides the jungle, but I could tell that the place Elder Su was talking about was right there. It had to be.

Did I need a key to open it, or what was that all about? Maybe there was some sort of mechanism? A cry of sorts?

What was it that people said? Open, I dare you!

Ah well, whatever it was, I had to figure it out. Time was running… Low…

I was thinking of that just as a feeling entered my mind. A sort of communication from the secret realm…

There were only twelve hours left!

Oh great!

Well, that also meant all of the participants had entered.

Being last, or close to last, must have sucked. They were probably not going to profit a lot from this secret realm, while I had more points than I knew what to do with.

With that new information in mind, a new sense of urgency came over me. Damn it, I was collecting too many plants!

It was beneficial, but also, I could have stopped, couldn’t I? Ah well, I just had to get what I needed to fast and get out.

But first, well, I had to enter!

And to that end, I began trying all sorts of things, from flying around to sending out pulses of Yin energy, but nothing really worked.

What I’d found to be the best was just standing around and trying to feel for the location of the entrance like a human compass. That was one of the ways, and I managed to find the supposed entrance with a good enough accuracy, but I still couldn’t open it.

Finally, after what hopefully wasn’t a lot of time, I had the bright idea of using the Ghost Qi Cultivation Technique, following its teachings to guide the Qi through my body.

That seemed to have done the trick, as the more I rushed the Qi through my body, the more the entrance felt more real.

Vast amounts of Yin Qi gathered around, and as I sped up my circulation, so did the appearance of the gate.

Shortly, a giant gate made out of black stone and engraved with a sort of blue crystal appeared in front of me.

I circled around and behind it, not needing to circulate Qi anymore, and was surprised to see that… Well, it was just a gate, and nothing more.

I went back to the front, where the handle was, and turned it.

Yes, it had a handle; someone wanted to make it easy to enter the hidden realm within the hidden realm.

After turning it, the gate opened almost by itself, but there was nothing behind it. Well, I couldn’t see anything, but there was just a wall of blue light.

Feeling the aura of teleportation coming from it, I took a step inside and got sucked out through the other side.

The warping that time around was far better and gentler, making it easier to handle.

When the blue within my vision vanished, I found myself inside a large grotto. It reeked with the smell of old people, or maybe it was just old.

Besides that first feeling, the second feeling I’d gotten was just how dense with Yin Qi this area was. I could basically feel it within my bones, which began to shiver, well… not my bones but my body.

I had to cover myself with Yang Qi to endure the place.

Of course, if it were a normal Yin cultivator here instead of me, they would quite literally be over the moon. The density made it so cultivation was about five times easier than on the outside, since extreme Yin cultivators needed to find specific spots or use the full moon.

It was not convenient in any way, and the payoff… It was paying off… If you liked being a ghost, I suppose.

Anyway, that didn’t matter as much as the cave itself.

Giant, large, with blue plants illuminating the ceiling, walls, and floor with a cyan light.

There was a lot of vegetation around, and a river stretching from behind me toward the inside flowed, creating a calming rhythmic sound within the otherwise quiet place.

Using my analysis skill on the first few plants, I was disappointed to see that there was nothing special about them. The fruits on the ceiling were simply called Qi Fruits, which was just a generic name for fruits rich with Qi. That didn’t really help, but judging from the place I was in, it was probably a Yin type fruit.

Just to be sure, I grabbed a few… dozen…s.

My dantian had enough space for many plants, and as long as the little shit didn’t eat them, it should be fine.

Of course, nothing was guaranteed in this world. For all I knew, I would put something that it liked in there, and suddenly I would be out of rare fruits!

Caring for a child sure was hard work…

Anyway!

I made the wise decision to explore deeper in. I didn’t know how time worked in this place, but for the moment it was safe to assume that there wasn’t any difference between the inside and outside…

Or maybe it was safer to assume that time moved faster here, and that meant I had to get a move on!

Running past several medicinal plants, I grabbed what I could with my Qi, talons and wings while rushing deeper in.

The best stuff had to be at the end of the grotto, after all… The only issue was what was going to challenge me while I got there. I didn’t want to deal with any Spirit Beasts, even if they were fake.

I ran alongside the river, since the water inside was also filled to the brim with Yin energy. I was a little tempted to drink from it, but I knew better.

Instead of doing that, I opted for just following along, since it was basically acting like a path or guide for me to take.

The deeper in I went, the larger the river got, and the more Yin Qi there was. If Elder Su were here, she would have probably lost the floor by default because of this.

Too bad she wasn’t, and while I wanted to do her a favor and get that reward from the system, I also didn’t want to waste too much time on it, but I wasn’t sure how I could leave either… I hadn’t thought of that.

It was too late to regret it now, so I just kept going until I could get something good.

Or meet something to kill. Hopefully not the latter; I didn’t feel like it, but I was sure my needles would make quick work of any of them anyway.

There weren’t any interesting medicinal plants or fruits on the way, but when the river got about as big as three roads and pretty turbulent, there was a sudden change in scenery… After it became a waterfall.

The river fell into another massive area, though it was yet another forest, or jungle. Unlike the previous area, which was mostly barren, this one was a thriving environment filled with lush vegetation, most of it hidden under blue leaves.

Curious, I flew toward the ceiling to inspect the powerful light source, and as it turned out, they were Qi Fruits! But bigger, so of course, I grabbed them.

I double-checked just to make sure that the little shit didn’t eat my fruit, and he was still sleeping.

Apparently fruit of this caliber wasn’t good enough for its royal ass!

Well, better for me. The only issue would be when I got something actually valuable…

Well, I could just eat the sea serpent meat as well; it would do me good as well!

With that thought in mind, I flew down after gathering a bunch of fruit and picked a nice, empty spot. Creating a large fire, I picked up some sturdier sticks and covered them in Water Qi, then stabbed two of the organs I’d gotten and let them roast.

Could I have eaten them raw? Sure.

Did I? No.

I wanted to see what cooked organ tasted like!

Although in my experience, just eating it raw was better, considering that I was a Spirit Beast.

I would eat half cooked, and the other half raw! Good, what a smart Phoenix I was.

The fire didn’t crackle, since I didn’t use any wood, but it was still satisfying to watch. The meat didn’t sizzle either; there was no fat to speak of…

Wasn’t this just a disappointing barbecue experience then?

Whatever, food was food. I devoured the first few pieces raw, immediately freeing a lot of space in my bag of holding, and also gaining some weight thanks to the Overeater skill.

A skill that allowed me to store excess energy as resources and use it at a critical moment was really good, and it had saved my life a couple of times. It was very valuable, especially in this place where I didn’t know where true danger would come from. It was better to be safe than sorry in those types of situations.

As I was devouring the high-quality Spirit Beast meat, most of it tasting like iron to be fair, a notification appeared in front of me.

[Congratulations!]

[Skill Overeater(Expert) has become Overeater(Master!)]

Oh!

Amazing!

…

Did I just become fatter?

Letting out a sigh, I devoured the rest of my food and tasted the cooked organs… There was a slight difference, but both tasted horrible either way.

I was now about… five, no, seven times as fluffy as before, which made it a little hard to move around.

I’d activated Strengthened Constitution just to move around a little bit better, and it helped greatly.

On the way, I’d gotten thirsty, and I had no water or any other liquid on me, so of course, the only natural thing to do was to lean down and drink a little bit of the river water.

Even if it was extreme Yin Water, that didn’t mean it wasn’t good to drink, just that I had to be careful.

Leaning down and taking a few sips, I’d immediately felt refreshed.

So imagine my surprise when I was happily hydrating, and something just came and hit me in my butt without even being able to detect it!

Looking back as I fell, I didn’t see anything, and even though I tried to get away, it was as if the water was dragging me down intentionally.

At that point, there wasn’t much I could do besides protect myself and see where the river took me.
