“I said I’m sorry!” Alex shouted over the gushing air. “I’m sorry! I’m sorry! I’m sorry! Can we please figure this out after you’re safe?”

Brody avoided eye contact. He sat cross legged and armed, as if they were having a disagreement on a couch. Except their fall whipped his hair up, and the ground coming fast would do more than just support his sit.

“Damnit, Brody!” Alex shouted again, pushing again [Illusory Copy] to port into his clone’s body. It fizzled out, unable to activate. There was a problem.

Somehow, for the first time, when Alex tried to use his skill, Brody prevented it. He could feel the slap back. It wasn’t all in his control. System notification completely forgotten, Alex yelped for Brody’s life.

“I can [Phantom Step] and maybe live, but if you won’t let me use—”

“Too bad!” Brody grunted, finally glaring back. “You…used me when it is easy. Friends? That wasn’t…friends.”

Alex’s panic was temporarily replaced with guilt. He’d been an ass to Brody when he’d reached out, and all the other time when things were more normal, he forgot about what it must be like to be loosely affiliated with reality. Brody was dependent on Alex to be brought into the world. He wanted to stay within it and was always there for Alex when he needed it. Alex had treated him like a tool, not a person.

“I’m sorr—”

Brody held up his hand, interrupting and trying to contain his laughter.

He’d already forgiven Alex before being pulled through. Right away, actually, as soon as Alex had ran away at Wonderland. He’d felt Alex’s hurt. It was just fun to give him a scare for once. And what better way to do that than activating his own port through [Illusory Copy]?

“Titles…Alex. Read your Titles. Or…,” Brody winked. “Our Title.”

Seeing Alex’s shocked face for a moment got a bark of a laugh out of him. Then his best friend was gone, sent to the unspace instead of him.

Brody unfurled from his sit, stretching like a cat as the ground rushed up. By his estimation when he glanced down, he had four seconds till impact. That was just enough time to get into position and call Alex back.

Four…three…

***

Alex popped into the unspace. His eyes darted around, trying to get his bearings. While his heart was hammering and the terror Brody splatting on the ground was still there, his surroundings and their cause stunned him.

He stood on a lush mattress covered in black, silk sheets. There were bits and bobs neatly organized on tables, and even a severely underwatered houseplant in a corner. It looked like a twenty-year-old guy’s bedroom. Except, the floor was colorless flat black and perfectly square, it hung and floated over infinite nothing, and the strangeness of the unspace stretched out forever. Plus, he, Alex, was there.

Alex couldn’t wrap his head around it. How could he be in the unspace and Brody out there in the world? His clone had said something about Titles. He’d received a notification, but the fall and fear of splattering had taken precedence.

How the—

Before the thought could finish, Alex was ripped back to his reality.

***

During the milliseconds the trip took, Alex decided he did not like being thrown back and forth through the unspace and the real world. Then the sounds and sights of the city came streaming, followed by a horrifying view of the concrete rushing up.

“AHHH!” He screamed. “BROD—”

“Skill!” Brody yelled back. “I forgi—”

Time slowed to a crawl, dragging out Brody’s voice to a croak, and the whole walking square beneath the CN Tower lit up in purple. [Relative Voyeur] had activated, and the illuminated floor meant death approached. Inch by inch, the ground came closer.

Alex wasn’t able to move his body, but his eyes shifted as normal. It took him only a moment to find Brody.

Oh, you little bastard, he thought as Brody came into view.

Brody had positioned himself in a stereotypical kung-fu kick, with one leg out and the other tucked in. Alex could admit that was pretty cool. But it was his hands that almost got a chuckle. One hand was formed into a finger gun, pointed right at Alex’s stomach, and his other hand was a clean middle finger shoved into Alex’s face.

He would have laughed if he could. Or if Brody wasn’t a foot from exploding into mist. It did feel good that he’d been forgiven. At least he figured his clone had, with the obnoxious positioning and his sentence cut off from the time dilation. But he needed to focus. With next to no time left, Alex had to figure out how to get them both out of this.

Good one, buddy, but I’ll get you back.

The wombo combo wouldn’t work. They were falling too fast. Usually, they’d slow themselves down some with upward [Phantom Step]s after a port into Brody. Alex knew that wouldn’t be enough here from how his gut churned when he’d thought of using that tactic. Aiming the combo to make him end up parallel to the ground would shoot him out at catatonic speeds. There would be no way to slow down, and while he was good at dodging, it was sure to get a bystander or himself injured. There was, however, one other option.

The path out formed in Alex’s mind. It was risky. It was idiotic.

Ah, fuck it. It’ll work.

He ported over to Brody as time crawled. The risk of death was still there, and everything ticked by as he went through the tunnel to form into Brody’s place. Alex focused on the maneuver without a spare thought available to regard the other places.

Popping into Brody’s kung-fu kick, the ground was within inches as he quickly selected the angle. He pulled on everything he had, drawing from the well of essence his connection to Emilio allowed, and activated his favorite skill of [Phantom Step] at forty-five-degrees. Or as close to the angle as he could figure.

The System grinded into the next moment, determined that that Alex in that plane wasn’t going to die any longer, and turned off his [Relative Voyeur]. Time resumed as normal for him.

With the momentum from the freefall coming through Alex’s port, he was blasted away from the floor as if he could fly.

“Wooohooo!” He hollered, body still locked in the kung-fu, finger gun, middle finger position Brody had set him.

Alex soared through the city square like a rocket. The bustle and smells had felt so far away at the top of the tower, but now that he was back in the thick of it, and the fact that Brody had forgiven him, he realized he’d missed it.

“Kyeah!” Alex yelled, leaning into it, as his flight continued to over two hundred feet. He just kept going up at an angle, twisting out from the kick to go fist first.

“I’m flying! I’m kind of flying! I’m—”

Alex stopped his cheering. The interiors of apartments were becoming much clearer. One he saw directly into had a couple playing a board game with wooden figured that fought. He could make them out clear as day, and the details were getting closer.

“Oh shit.”

He was shooting straight into the building, and he wasn’t slowing down at all.

“Oh shit!” Alex yelped again, noticing that [Relative Voyeur] wasn’t activating.

Smashing through their window won’t kill me? Still going to hurt!

The girl in the apartment noticed him, and her eyes went wide. If he didn’t decide soon, Alex would break through and end up ruining their game. His looked down, saw there was just more building, then up, and spotted the top ledge. The flight path he travelled on in his [Phantom Step] launch would land him eight floors from the roof.

He quickly did the napkin math in his head. There was no way he would make it. He could do three [Phantom Step]s in a row, but that would still land him short. After that he’d fall straight down. But [Phantom Step] wasn’t the only skill useful here.

“Brody,” He grunted, the path forming in his mind. “Time to quintuple jump!”

The window was within three feet when Alex cast [Phantom Step] straight up.

He ported parallel to the building’s face, his fist punched straight up. The windows into people’s lives flashed by. There was a family of six watching an animated movie, all shoved onto a couch. Then he flew by a man doing something he’d rather not remember with an apple. His flight up began to slow, and Alex came face-to-face with an old man staring out at the night. The man looked shocked at the appearance of someone floating slowly upwards outside his window.

Alex managed a wink and a salute, then fired the furthest pullout of [Illusory Copy] he could manage.

Brody appeared five feet above him, looking around and quickly finding Alex.

“Upward wombo!” Alex shouted. “Up!”

Then he activated the port into Brody, and this time, his clone didn’t prevent it.

He took over Brody’s position and immediately fired a [Phantom Step] up. Shooting another twenty feet towards the roof, Alex pulled Brody out again.

The pair repeated the combo two more times. Brody came out, five feet higher than Alex, who’d port over and then [Phantom Step] up. They were in perfect sync, as old colleagues or friends always are, not even truly needing to think that hard about what they did.

Alex fired up out of the last combo with outreached hands. He had available essence due to his increased connection with Emilio, but his core wasn’t strong enough to keep firing the skill over and over again. That only came with levels.

Right at the peak of his last ascent, the tips of Alex’s finger grasped the edge of the apartment build.

“Hell yeah!” He shouted as he dangled.

Planting his feet against the brick, he began the precarious shuffle up, when something startled him.

“What the shit!”

A firm hand reaching over the edge grasped his wrist. Panic flitted for a moment, and he went to yank his arm out, but a smug face with perfectly coifed hair peered over.

“Don’t worry…I got you.” Brody said as he hoisted Alex up with ease.

Stunned, Alex blinked at Brody. Then a big smile broke across his face as he leapt for joy.

“Did you see that wombo combo!?” Alex shouted, grasping Brody around the waist to pick him up and spin him around. “I fucking flew!”

He put Brody down and saw his clone was equally excited as he was. The two cheered on top of the building as the hustle of Toronto’s night continued. They were two friends celebrating a victory.

“But how?” Alex asked after both calmed down. “How did you stop me when I tried to port over you? And how can you port to me yourself?”

Brody raised an eyebrow. “Aren’t you…forgetting something?”

“Shit, yeah.” Alex nodded. “Man, I’m sorry I’ve been so awful to you. You reached out when I was freaking, and I pushed you away. I’m so caught up in my own stuff I forget about all the crap you have to go through. I apologize. I can’t promise I’ll be perfect, but I swear that I’ll do be—”

Brody interrupted him with a giant bear hug.

“It’s okay...I forgive you.” Brody said, pulling back. “We just have to…have our backs.”

“Yeah, we have each other’s backs.” Alex agreed. “Now tell me. This means you can stay here like you want? You won’t get pulled back to the unspace on a timer?”

Brody grinned. “Not fully, but…it’s a start. Check your…notification. Our notification.”

Alex was giddy, and went to mentally bring up the System notification, but before he could, his phone buzzed in his pocket twice.

“Probably Snu. One sec.” He said, taking it out.

Snu: we’re waiting at leather spires. and thanks for the title.

Snu: hurry up. You still owe me a big sorry 😉. no touchy touchy till then.

Alex blinked, confused at what she was talking about.

“We?” He asked Brody, who seemed to be hiding something in his hand. “She said we. Giat too? And she also got a Title? Dude, what the—”

His phone buzzed in his hand again, and he looked down. It was from some number that he didn’t recognize.

416-111-2345: read your Title!

Alex once again turned his eyes back up at Brody, and his jaw dropped from what he saw.

Brody waggled a phone in his face, showing Alex that it was him that sent the message.

“A 416 number? Those are insanely expensive!” Alex yelped. “How the hell did you afford that?”

“Rich girlfriend.” Brody answered with raised eyebrows. “Now, Title.”

Alex wasn’t annoyed that his friends and girlfriend kept tricking him. All the hints they were dropping we’re making him excited. What kind of Title could affect both Brody and Snu? Eager to find out, he pulled up the notification.

[Title: Innate Linksmith]

“Oh SHIT!” He shouted. Flicking his eyes down, he couldn’t believe what the Title granted.

“No fucking way, eh?”
