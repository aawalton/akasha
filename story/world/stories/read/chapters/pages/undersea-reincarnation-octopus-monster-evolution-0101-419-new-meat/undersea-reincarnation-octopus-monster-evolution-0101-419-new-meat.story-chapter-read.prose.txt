Huh. I was right, he thought while water roared around him, still filling up the cave while he examined the odd restricting sensation. It was still active, but he couldn’t tell exactly how it worked, nor where it was coming from. Not on the first attempt, anyway.

This will be useful if we try to chat with the missing turtle, he mused. Gar’Something hadn’t taken as much damage as Henry would have liked, but with three Octominds on him, keeping him occupied was easy enough.
