And wreck they did. Lemira guided them to a spot not far from where Luke killed the first greater demon. At first, the group found no traces of demonic activity, but once they searched nearby buildings, the clues were difficult to ignore. A small, old, two-story house built with stone blocks crammed in between two modern apartment buildings didn't just look different on the outside. The inside was like stepping into another world. Writhing flesh covered every surface other than the floor. It was all wet with some oily substance. Blood dribbled from the ceiling, and it kept making squelching noises, as if the flesh wanted to communicate with them.

While it was different from The Fallen Shepherd's use of people's limbs and other various pieces, the 'decorations' drew Luke's mind to that particular bastard, but he pushed the impulse to go hunting him right away. One crisis at a time. First, demons. Then, Singers. After that, Relian had better watch himself.

It didn't take long for the greater demon to make itself known. Before they laid eyes on it, all of them began having hallucinations. Penny saw her friends eaten by the flesh-walls, she told them. Rather than lose it, she grew agitated and reckless, running through the house to find whoever was giving her the visions. Sharing what they saw, Luke learned that Ray saw himself cut in two, bleeding out in the corner of every room. Mateo's face was stark white, and he refused to put words to what he was seeing, which meant it had to be bad. Real bad. Lemira wasn't affected, which, according to her, was to be expected. Luke didn't see anything either, which he thought was a bit odd, but after a while, he realized the mental attacks were directed at him as well. It was just that the healing mana in his Meta-Heart dealt with it through some means he didn't understand. The others were losing health points, so perhaps that was it. The longer they stayed inside, the more affected they were.

After sweeping the house, they didn't find a damn thing. Even looking through cupboards, up at the attic, and in the different bathrooms, they came up empty. What solved the issue was Penny's agitation bubbling over. She released a sort of nova that crashed through the entire lower floor, electrocuting everything non-human in its way. An enormously fat, naked, and too-human-looking demon appeared in the corner the moment lightning struck it, and the disgusting blob of a creature writhed in pain, smoke coming out of its eyes and even its mouth. It died before they got even a second attack off.

"Mind demon," Lemira said with a shudder. "Physically weak, but they can be more dangerous than the giant you battled earlier."

"Pushover," Penny scoffed, throwing the crystal she had received over to Lemira.

"Two more," Luke said.

Once they'd left the building, Lemira directed them to their next opponent. On the way there, they ran into a small group of human Integrated pursued by Singers. Not even stopping, Luke, Mateo, and Ray threw themselves at the Singers to work through some frustration. Calling killing satisfying would be in poor taste, but Luke couldn't deny it was just that. Also, the mana he'd drained from them went straight into the orbs, and they weren't even getting full. What a marvel, and one Luke would find great use for soon, he was sure. The Integrated they'd saved stood there with their mouths open and eyes wide after having witnessed the one-sided fight, and it took Lemira several tries to get their attention and provide directions to The Band headquarters, where Milla would keep them safe until this was over.

The second-to-last greater demon was an opponent for Mateo. A tall, female demon wearing clothes straight from some old samurai movie: billowing white robes tied down with strings of leather along her arms and legs. She sat with her legs crossed on top of a broken-down bus, a long spear lying across her lap. When they approached, she looked up from under a wide-brimmed hat, her red eyes glowing with attention, all of it directed at Mateo. He, being the prideful Lancer that he was, stepped up as if to challenge the demon in one-on-one combat. Before she could stand to answer his challenge, however, Ray stabbed her in the back from stealth, severing her spine. As he danced back, he also threw several vials of poison at her. Even so, the female demon struggled to her feet, growling in frustration as the poison ate through her clothes. She disappeared in a blur of speed and appeared before Mateo, her spear having pierced his side. Mateo's own spear had gone straight through the demon's infernal core, which was enough to put her out of her misery.

"A hollow victory," Mateo sighed as Luke healed him.

Luke gave him a look. "All we need are their crystals, Mateo. We don't have time to mess around."

"Honor is not 'messing around,' it is the lifeblood of humanity, la savia vital de la humanidad."

While it was clear Mateo didn't appreciate Ray butting in, he soon got over it, and the two of them spoke like friends again before they reached the ninth and final demon. Not the last greater demon in all of New York, Lemira explained, but the last one they needed. Many more roamed the city, but she was certain their little group wouldn't need to defeat them all to get the system even to the next stage, whatever that was. As it turned out, that stage was anything but simple. Getting there, though, was.

Perhaps simple was an exaggeration. The greater demon they faced next had wings and liked to use them. Penny's exhaustion meant she wasn't fit to fight something airborne, and even if she did, her mage-like class was a poor fit against the agile warrior who favored a whip that, Luke learned, could tear the flesh right off the bone. In the end, Loom's Reminder saved them, attaching a thread to the demon when it swooped down to attack yet again. With its speed, he couldn't hope to get at its neck or infernal core, so Luke did the only thing he could think of and yanked on the thread to pull the demon down to the ground. In that moment, he learned two things. First, the demon was stronger than him. Second, the threads were now sturdy enough that one could hold him as the demon flew off, carrying Luke behind it.

What ensued was an exercise in frustration as Luke clung to the demon, holding its wings so it couldn't maneuver well enough to cast him off, while he also used Malleable Needle of Life to saw through its neck. Around the time he got through about two-thirds of its flesh, the demon opted to crash into a building, turning itself into a wet splat as Luke fell. Hitting the ground from that sort of height was not ideal, but he pieced his skull back together and healed a few crushing injuries and a persistent heart-stopping that left him full of dread. In the end, Luke pulled through and returned to the others with the ninth and final crystal.

"Nice one," Ray said, holding up his hand for a high-five.

Luke just groaned in reply, then handed the crystal to Lemira.

"Thank you," she said, accepting the item. Lightning lit up the night, and the thunder that followed set nearby windows quaking in their frames. Lemira shuddered. "Perhaps we ought to do this next step in a more accommodating location?"

Mateo nodded toward a building. "I see a grand lobby inside that one. Let's get out of the rain."

Said and done, they broke into the building. Once they'd changed into dry clothes, except for Luke, whose robe never got wet, Lemira brought out all the small crystals they had gathered.

"Now what?" Ray asked.

Lemira studied them in silence for a moment, then placed them in a heap on the floor. "Like so."

They soon began to glow. The light intensified until it hurt to watch, and when it receded a little, all nine pieces had combined into a larger crystal in the shape of a horn.

"I received a prompt," Lemira said, her eyes getting that faraway look that showed someone was perusing their interface. "It is asking me if we are ready and able to continue to the final stage."

Luke was about to reply when a message appeared in his interface.

Milla (Guild): "This is going out to everyone on my list. Scouts have spotted a blue line expanding outward from the building with the Singer countdown object. Stand by for more information."

Milla (Guild): "They report..."

Milla (Guild): "I have lost contact with all scouts."

Milla (Guild): "I have also lost contact with Phoenix Phalanx and other groups near that area."

Milla (Guild): "Oh, god. Get somewhere safe. It's coming, and it's coming fast!"

"Do it!" Luke shouted. "Select yes!"

Lemira just stood there, as if in a daze.

"Lemira!" Luke grabbed her arm and squeezed. "Wake up!"

She blinked, and her eyes focused, her gaze falling on him, then the horn on the ground. "S-sorry," she said. "I'm doing it now."

Even inside a building, they could all see the blue, shimmering line moving along the ground at incredible speed. It hit them just as the horn imploded into darkness that expanded to the size of a manhole cover. No, it wasn't just darkness, Luke realized as the world around him dimmed, his head crackled with pain, and his lungs refused to fill. Deep down inside that hole, he saw a raging inferno. Infernal Flux, the domain of demonkind.
