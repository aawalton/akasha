At any rate, the moment she said, "ours," I wound up having all that run through my head, which put a smile on my face at how much I've actually grown up and how, as noted, stupidly hilariously inappropriate my initial response was.

Okay, I guess I was a little happy for me, too, what with enjoying the momming process so much. Although I tensed up a little when she put an arm around me and stepped me over to the Altar, guiding one of my hands to the biggest of the eggs.

Yours?

I picked up her implication that time, and put her hand over mine with a tentacle. "Ours." I wouldn't know how to care for them properly. Here, on my Altar, with my High Priestess, I know they are both safe and well cared for.

Trust?

I woke to something unusual, an almost completely quiet house. Uh, where is everyone?

Mostly out harvesting fruits and vegetables, thought Saffron.

I hopped to her, then watched for a moment as I saw the clear circle of authority going on. And by that I meant that the women were looking to Saffron to make sure whatever they were doing was right, Saffron was looking to Lindsey, and Lindsey was clearly looking to the women. Okay, I saw all that going on, I'm not sure if anyone in that circle realized.

Okay, maybe Lindsey, possibly Saffron, maybe even some of the women themselves, but fuck it, there are worse things than the nominal person in charge defers to the expert who is asking them for... Yeah, some of them may have felt some kinda way about Saffron deferring to Lindsey, but after watching a little more I saw that all of them realized Saffron knew who Lindsey was looking to for confirmation.

I cleared my throat. "Anything I can do to help?"

Saffron turned to me with a smile, reaching up to caress my cheek. "Of course, love." Then she spun me around and slapped me on the ass. "You can get back in the kitchen with Marie where you belong today."

I almost bristled at that until I realized why she'd said it. In the fields, I was just another hand, and a clumsy one at that. In the Kitchen, I might not be Marie's equal, what with her having more than three centuries experience more than me, but I could still do work, and do it well.

Funny, today's work wasn't all that complicated. Other than some bread and crackers, almost everything was just cutting fruit and veg into eye catching bite sized shapes. Okay, we made some sauces, not to mention whipped cream, but that hardly counts.
