Drachma Denominations

Following his victories against Devil Kings Admiral and Dragonus, Alistair Tan faces his greatest challenge yet: defeating George Moulin, the leader of the Devil Kings. Despite his growth, this task seems almost impossible. The sixth and final Quest, [Armageddon], has arrived. The First Wave, Earth Asunder, features natural disasters far beyond Category 5 hurricanes. When reports emerge of one of those strange storms and a mysterious colorless region, Alistair must investigate to protect his already fragile freehold.

The Holy Ravine

Alistair and Oliver venture into a mysterious Mana Storm where color has been completely drained from the environment. Within this strange region, they discover a ravine between two mountains. Upon exploring it, they're trapped and stripped of almost all their power, returned to near pre-Initiation levels.

Inside the Holy Ravine, they discover a hidden community of martial artists. To leave, they must train under the Silver Comet Sect with Master Ko Pao and Pike Zenbatty. Their ultimate goal is to gain approval from Silvanio Apostolos, the region's ruler and a martial arts genius who took over the region by storm several decades ago.

During this training, Alistair undergoes brutal conditioning to obtain the Steel Body technique. He also learns that the planet the Pathfinder AI transported the Holy Ravine from, Lisorte, holds an ancient Dao History that must be unlocked. After receiving a physical key during a vision which he hands to his master and defeating Silvanio's champion, Alistair is finally able to return to Earth with newfound power and a more solid foundation in martial arts, less reliant on the Pathfinder AI's guidance.

Back on Earth, Alistair faces Oracle, the Second Devil King. With his new finishing skill—a thousand-armed bodhisattva manifestation—he defeats her. However, Oracle's hidden Karma ability activates upon her defeat, unexpectedly transporting Alistair to the moon.

On the lunar surface, Alistair discovers the Grand Dungeon, a part of the Second Wave of Armageddon, The Second Step, which promises substantial rewards. He's forced to enter alone while racing against time—and all the Devil Kings working as a team. Inside, he completes the Cosmic Blood trial, where he meets Gu Fuhao, a talented music cultivator who is also set to join the Clear Water Sect.

The world experiences the brunt of the next three waves. During the Third Wave, Kaiju Break, Alistair and George come to a truce, agreeing not to have anymore bloodshed until a final confrontation in the Sixth Wave, where both parties believe they will have the advantage. The Fourth Wave, the Mindaugust's Trial, is a test more of brains than brawn, while The Tutorial takes the majority of the planet into a lesson plan they should have received at the beginning of the initiation.

When the finale begins, both sides, comprising thirteen combatants each, are transported to a futuristic metropolis with five missions to complete. While Alistair and George face off directly, Alistair's allies must contend with a clone George created using an item reward of the Grand Dungeon. The other Devil Kings scramble to complete the missions.

After a brutal fight that leaves him gravely injured, Alistair almost defeats George, who flees to his allies. Summoning the Spirit of a True Hero, Alistair defeats Morgana the mage and tackles George from the mayor's tower. Upon impact with the ground, Alistair emerges victorious.

The good guys win, and the initiation is complete. The Devil Kings are finally defeated, but at great cost. Among the fallen are Caren Locasta (the vampiric Chronicler), Bartholomew Wood (Alfred's brother), Whimsy (Pharaoh's friend), Jesse Waterfall, Brigid Mwangi, and Blaise Blanchett.

Alistair's earlier victory over Anthony Ricci attracts attention from higher powers, with a scion from a Princely Progenitor Clan arriving on Earth alongside a Grand Imperator. Alistair defeats the Grand Imperator in a cultivation-equalized duel, proving his worth.

However, that same day, the mysterious Man in Shadows—revealed to have been secretly controlling the Devil Kings—attempts to kidnap Alistair. Though the Grand Imperator and Alistair's sect matriarch intervene, the villain's partner unleashes an ancient demonic curse. The true Pathfinder AI stops this threat, but not before the Man in Shadows kidnaps Alexandra and Silvanio Apostolos.

Devastated but determined, Alistair continues his journey of justice. Now that the initiation is complete, he has six months to reach level 100 and the Adept realm before joining the Clear Water Sect.
