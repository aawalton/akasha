He dug his fingers into the ground with his last bit of strength. Agony coursed through his entire body; with only one arm, he pulled himself closer to the pedestal, fingernails peeling off as it scraped along the dirt.

The explosion caused his eye to rupture.

He was blind; it was total darkness, but he had to continue pushing onward. Pain permeated throughout, but he couldn’t easily identify its origin. With flesh missing from his head and an arm completely gone, blood flowed, and he could only taste iron. He reached out his arm one more time, not being able to tell if he reached the pedestal, but the looming darkness forced him to stop.

“Please, anyone, please help me,” he stated, choking on his blood. Blood mixed with his tears, flowing from his shut eyes. His facial nerves had been destroyed. He looked like a demon as his wails echoed toward the sky.

I can’t give up now, I’m so close. Luke thought as he did everything he could to push on with whatever strength he had left in his arm. Loose layers of skin on his arms flapped about, showing parts of the underlying muscles beneath them.

He stopped once more and couldn’t move forward. Not because he couldn’t pull himself, but because the pedestal blocked his path.

Luke pushed his mind forward towards the pedestal, hoping that just laying his hand on the base was enough. He sensed a slight throbbing of a connection. He couldn’t tell if the pedestal panel had appeared, but he tried it anyway, willing whatever points he had to be spent towards a restore.

All at once an energy entered him, overfilling him with new, rejuvenated power. The flesh rapidly rebuilt itself, folding over each other in bands as the muscle and tissue mixed. Being covered with a fresh, beautiful layer of skin. As soft as a newborn baby’s hands, he seemed brand new.

But he noticed that not everything was there. He was missing his left arm; Luke faced the sky, falling onto his knees as all feeling in his body disappeared.

Is this really what this world has planned for me? Death is the only answer, huh? Why should I be going through all this pain? All these fights? For what? A chance at survival? I was doing perfectly fine before. I can’t take this anymore!

Standing up, clenching his jaw, rage rushed through him. Grasping the rugged pedestal with all the strength he could muster, he aggressively thrashed his head against the rough surface.

Veins boiled with anger, clenching his fists even tighter. His breathing became shallower with every thud against the pedestal. His head blistered with blood as he continued to hammer it. The impact echoed throughout the silent island, just him and the pedestal.

Blood spilled from his head, splattering against the pedestal.

“Hahaha… this is it. This is the pain I need.”

As if something clouded his mind, he stood there senseless, without reasoning. With no will behind it, he demanded the pedestal to summon another slime; this time, floor 1 appeared before him. Staring at the seamless area, waiting for the gathering of energy. Then it occurred, condensing into a glob.

Luke scanned the island and saw all the branches gone, obliterated in the explosion.

I guess I have to do this the old-fashioned way, he thought while chuckling. His chuckles sounded like wails, as if he were a dying old man on his deathbed.

His devilish smile shone brighter than ever before. He continued laughing as he took small steps in the direction of the blue slime.

Cracks scarred multiple sections of the island, and craters and crevices littered the landscape, but Luke didn’t care. He gathered strength into his legs and launched himself toward the blue slime as fast as he could, laughing wildly, his mind incapable of any thought.

He reached his dominant arm toward the slime, not even calling upon the souls that remained in the sky. Few remained, but it would still have been enough to help him. Luke tried his best, clawing at the slime, but did little, leading to his hand dissolving in its gel.

But the slime didn’t just take the hits. It suddenly transformed its right side, lunging a huge spike toward his arm. Adjusting his frame as much as possible, he sped up further, unconcerned by the potential for injury. Luckily, his increased speed slightly offset the attack, nicking him in what remained of his damaged appendage.

He tried thrusting his right arm into the slime until it started producing results. The pain of his skin melting didn’t nearly affect him in this moment.

All he cared about was unleashing his anger, pain, stress, all his emotions on this one entity.

The slime repeatedly tried to jump away to dodge, using its quick movements to strike him from different angles, but that didn’t work. Luke stretched out his arm; no matter how much damage he took, he tried to drill his entire arm through it.

His strength barely amounted to anything; it was enough to keep it from escaping his reach. He pushed through, even using his fleshy stump to create an opening.

His mind ran wild, eyes covered in red, his vision tainted and almost blind. But he pushed on. He was unsure whether he could win.

His rage grew hotter, and the souls in the sky combined in one place where his left appendage used to be. On his stump, an ethereal form of a deformed arm appeared, barely visible. All the souls disappeared, forming a very weak arm that seemed to hold little energy.

But appearance meant nothing; he understood its power. Luke’s devilish smile never left; it grew even larger, almost reaching ear to ear. He understood he had done it. Even in his near-unconscious state, he shifted his entire body weight, turning his hips, twisting his shoulder, smashing the ethereal fist into the slime.

It seemed to have no heft, as if the energy substituting the appendage on his left side had no prior existence. He threw everything he had. Nothing stopped it as it pierced through the slime. Acid ate away at the ethereal energy, causing it to flicker.

Luke opened his mouth wide enough, ready to bite the slime if need be, but the slime vanished into particles. Two stars gathered in the sky, filled with more energy than a regular green slime. He observed himself with a feeling of contentment. His mind was a mess, and he couldn’t make sense of what he had achieved, but he knew it would help. He collapsed; blood seeping from his wounds.

The slime landed a devious attack that left him lying in a pool of his own blood, his hand tattered from attacking it viciously.

The void returned to silence. Luke lay on the ground, defeated but smiling. This might have been the moment he could have died, but at least he tried his best. But he knew this wouldn't be the end. He slept on the ground as if the pain had never existed.

Faded cracks marked his body, along with his current bleeding wound. He woke up shortly after and dragged himself back to the pedestal, though it wasn’t as bad as before. His own mistakes had completely broken him. He could have killed the slime more safely, but Luke had let his mind wander and used the soul energy even when being his last resort. He observed the island once more, seeing all the damage. His mind still felt disconnected.

What an interesting battle this had been, Luke thought, chuckling to himself. A slight smirk appeared. Well… what now?

He hadn’t actually thought about what came after that battle. He had lost a lot of power since he still had no control over his soul energy. And not only that, but he couldn’t even use his left arm.

He looked back at the stump on his shoulder. He had lost an arm, but at least it wasn’t his dominant one. Slowly turning back, he flexed his right arm, which still looked a little fleshy from the acid.

He stood in front of the pedestal and pressed his hand against it. The pedestal had taken heavy damage from the explosion, left rugged with chips and dents, and with a large splatter of blood along the top.

He pulled up the panel and saw he had 2 points left from his original 15. Stopping, he stared longer than he ought to have.

“I know for a fact that I had more than this!” He furrowed his eyes, screaming.

Reviewing his status display once more. Everything was the same price as before. Then his eyes landed on the restore option. He focused on it; nothing else could make sense but this.

“You’ve got to be kidding me.” Luke said with annoyance.

Had the price of restore increased with the injury. His mouth hung open at the realization.

But why hadn’t it increased with my previous injuries? He couldn’t figure it out; it made no sense. The price was 5 before the Elite slime fight, meaning his earlier injuries cost around 15 points.

Anger flared again. About to lash out once more, he stopped himself, breathing, exhaling, checking if he had missed anything. But the adrenaline faded, and he felt the stinging pain on his forehead from smashing it. His eyes turned towards the next available floor.

Is it time for floor 2? Luke thought, already knowing it's going to be hell, but he knew how the slime attacked, what patterns they took. But he didn’t know if the next creature followed the same trend. He looked up once more at the two sparkling souls in the dark sky. As blood still trickled down his face, he readied himself for the next endeavor.
