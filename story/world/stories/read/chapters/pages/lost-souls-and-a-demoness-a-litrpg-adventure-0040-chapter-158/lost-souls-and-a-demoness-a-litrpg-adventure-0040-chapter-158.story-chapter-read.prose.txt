The warm, humid air of the jungle was surprisingly nostalgic. Jade leaned back against a tree, ruminating on the feeling as she rested in the shade. Her companions were nearby, their simple camp mostly finished already. There wasn't a real need to pitch tents, this quadrant had a Sanctuary only a half-day's hike from here, but this felt better.

"I'm glad you've returned."

Jade turned to find a familiar figure sitting beside her, dappled sunlight playing across her tan skin. Juniper smiled, although Jade could detect a hint of sadness in her eyes.

"Well, we did say we would." Jade shrugged. "Sorry it took us so long."

"Not at all. It's merely been a season to me. I hear that in the outside world, things can change a great deal in that time. But that is not the case here. For all that the tides of nature ebb and flow, the jungle remains largely unchanged. Lush, beautiful, and vibrant."

"It really is peaceful here." Jade said, gazing up at the shifting canopy. "Things have changed more than I ever would have thought possible, and it just doesn't stop. Thanks for letting us stay with you for a few days. It will be good for us."

"You helped keep my home safe. You're welcome for as long as you wish."

"Careful." Jade smiled slightly. "Siora might take you up on that."

The two of them watched the elf excitedly picking her way through a patch of lush wildflowers at the edge of camp. She'd somehow roped Luis and Tara into helping her sort through her findings and appeared to be giving them instructions. Tara looked confused, like she wasn't sure exactly how she'd ended up here, but when Luis jumped to it with enthusiasm, she quickly followed suit.

"Have things stayed peaceful since we left?" Jade asked curiously.

"They have. No new powerful monsters have manifested, and I'm capable of protecting the jungle from any of the smaller ones that get out of control."

"Good." Jade sighed. "I just hope we don't bring trouble down on you. We seem to attract it."

"Don't worry about that. I'll be watchful. Now, rest. The flow of your life force tells a story of trials and heavy burdens. Let them be at ease."

"I'll do my best."

Juniper left, striding across the camp to join Siora and the others as they studied their collection. Jade watched for a time, enjoying the warm breeze and the earthy smell of nature. Most of a week had passed since the demon incursion at the L-ReF headquarters, and the pain of those events was finally ebbing. Alan's frequent insistence that Jade had done the right thing, and his repeated assurances that he would be okay in general, had finally gotten through to her. In those interactions, Jade had learned an important lesson. Worrying for a loved one was healthy, but it could be taken too far. People adjusted to their circumstances, and bringing to bear excessive concern and anxiety for their sake could be more burdensome than helpful. She wasn't about to give up on finding a way to help her brother, but neither was she going to let an obsession over that goal warp her relationship with him.

If only the Wayfarer trial didn't limit our visits home so strictly. Jade thought to herself with a sigh. She had every intention of continuing to push deeper into the Labyrinth, but a little more time with family would do wonders for her mental health.

That train of thought made her turn her attention back to the system windows she'd minimized. Their party had returned to the Labyrinth for a few reasons, one of which was to consolidate their gains. Not being able to access the full interface on Earth meant that they weren't able to apply their level ups, and all of them had received at least one following the frantic battle. Given that their remaining allotted time for being outside of the Labyrinth had almost elapsed, they'd opted to set up somewhere comfortable and familiar while they sorted out their new features and figured out what to do next.

On the most improved front, Siora was the clear winner. The elf had obtained her Wayfarer mark less than two weeks ago, yet she'd already jumped up to level 16. It was still quite a ways below the rest of them, but the gap was closing rapidly.

If she gets strong enough, maybe she'll be able to learn a spell that can help Alan. Jade mused, tabbing through her various level-up screens. It was something to keep in mind. According to Siora and Tara, there were cleansing spells capable of curing serious, systemic diseases, they were just high level. Siora wouldn't be able to learn anything like that until B-rank, which started at level 40. At the rate they were progressing, that might not be as far off as it sounded…

But that was enough distracted musing for the moment. Their recent victories had granted Jade two level-ups, bringing her to level 29. This gave her four stat points to work with and, more importantly, her next advanced class feature. She assigned her stats first, deciding to throw a couple of improvements into some of her lower scores.

Name: Jade Callian

Level: 29* (33) (Mark of the Wayfarer)

Race: Succubus

Class: Shadow Witch

Advanced Class: Glamourblade

Origin: Earth

Base Stat Scores (Human)-(Succubus)

Strength: 14-17

Agility: 27-32 (+4 from armor)

Fortitude: 15-18

Intelligence: 20-24

Willpower: 18-11

Jade found it interesting that the system now estimated her power to be a full four levels above what her true Labyrinth system level was. Mastering her aura control and manifestation hadn't taken a huge amount of work, and yet the gains were substantial. At first, she'd wondered why so few others put time into learning the technique. After her attempts to teach it to Luis and Naomi ended in complete failure, though, she began to understand why Serrethis and Aylin had been so surprised that she was capable of the feat. Apparently, manifesting your aura wasn't something that came naturally to most people.

Now, it was time for the exciting part. Jade pulled up the next window, looking over her new advanced class options.

System Notice:

Welcome to advanced class progression! Your chosen advanced class: Glamourblade, grants you a new feature once every eight levels. Unlike standard class features and racial ability selection, any options you do not select at each advancement stage will not be available at future levels. All selections are final.

Jade frowned slightly. The ability to select options she'd previously passed up on was convenient and meant that she didn't have to stress as much as she would if every choice were final. She'd gotten used to the way the Labyrinth system worked, and nobody had told her things changed when it came to advanced classes. Jade took a few minutes to discuss the issue with Tara, but the tek'kalla simply shrugged. This was just the way things were. Supposedly, advanced class features imbued a greater magnitude of power into the souls of Wayfarers than standard abilities did. The tek'kalla people theorized that the prohibition was a safeguard against potentially volatile mixtures of power. Cast in that light, Jade understood the restriction. Nobody wanted to randomly explode one day because they'd taken two powers that changed them in incompatible ways.

She returned her attention to the prompts and blinked away the notice. This didn't change much, she just needed to think the options over carefully before deciding.

Something I'm historically great at, of course…

Fortunately, there were only two choices to mull over. Jade read the first one.

Ability (Glamourblade)

'Till Death do Us Part

Effect: Damaging a creature charmed by you has a greatly reduced chance of breaking the charm effect.

Wow. Just, wow. Jade had to resist the impulse to make the selection right then and there. The ability to attack, and potentially eliminate, creatures enthralled by her enchantments would be a complete game changer. A non-insignificant part of their team training revolved around how to best communicate which targets Jade was charming so that the team could coordinate their attacks around them. That wasn't too challenging for Luis and Naomi, who primarily dealt damage to single targets, but Fenrin and Tara had to place their area of effect spells carefully if they didn't want to disrupt any Allure or Charm Creature spells Jade deployed. And that wasn't even considering the benefit it would give Jade directly. This ability would essentially give her an easy victory in any one-on-one confrontation she found herself in, provided that she could land her spells. And, with the increasingly high potency of her casting, that was growing to be more of a certainty against all but the strongest of opponents.

And yet, Jade had some lingering reservations. The thought of mercilessly stabbing a creature over and over while they just stood there, confused and in pain, made her stomach twist in disgust. Intellectually, Jade knew that there was little difference between killing a monster while it fought back versus while it was magically prevented from doing so, but still… it didn't evoke pleasant imagery. Conflicted, she set that one aside and pulled up the next.

Ability (Glamourblade)

Everlasting Appeal

Effect: When a charm spell successfully affects a target, the spell doesn't go on cooldown.

"Holy…"

This was it. Jade didn't make her selection yet, but she knew she'd found a winner. The first ability choice was still powerful, if she combined it with her Enrapture spell, she'd likely be able to take down an entire group of enemies singlehandedly. But ultimately, it didn't change much about her overall capabilities. The latter option did. Jade currently possessed three charm spells: Allure, Charm Creature, and Enrapture. Enrapture had a relatively short cooldown, but those of the former two were several minutes. Since most fights lasted a minute or two at most, that effectively meant Jade was limited to deploying her most potent spells once or twice per battle, depending on the mixture of opponents she faced. Removing that limitation would open up so many options…

Still, Jade didn't immediately make a decision. She'd promised herself that she would talk everything over with the group, and so she did just that. Unsurprisingly, they agreed with her assessment. Both options were tempting, but the second one had much more potential. They would have to rework some of their tactics to take into account newly charmed targets appearing regularly on the battlefield, or even managing multiple charmed enemies at once, but that was a small price to pay for such a force multiplier. Satisfied with her decision, Jade finalized the selection. Immediately, Jade felt a warmth blossom within her core. New power flowed through her body, spilling into each limb and filling them with a strange buzz. The odd sensation lasted only a few moments before quickly fading, leaving her a little breathless.

Jade was about to ask Tara if that was normal when something strange happened. A new window appeared in front of her, filled with small text. It disappeared just as quickly as it had arrived, vanishing too quickly for her to read.

"What…?"

The window appeared again and then disappeared again. This time, it took her whole interface with her. The health status, map, and summary pages winked out all at once, just like they had at the shrine in Lysara. A spike of alarm shot through Jade, and she wondered if she'd somehow messed something up during her level up, but a moment later the entire system was back. Another window popped up.

System Notice

Resolving system errors. Please wait for system restoration. Cause of error: Entanglement mismatch. Estimated reboot time: 5 seconds. Please remain still.

True to its word, the system blinked out again. Approximately five seconds later, everything was finally back to normal.

System Notice

Your Labyrinth experience was impacted by an unforeseen error. Your patience is appreciated!

Reward: 1 silver compensation token.

A familiar-looking token appeared in Jade's hand a moment later. She inspected it dubiously, finding it to be identical to the token they'd received early on in their Wayfarer careers, other than that one being bronze in color.

"Did your interface just get messed up too?" Jade asked Naomi, who was building a small campfire nearby. She shook her head.

"No… did something happen to yours?"

"Yeah…" Jade flipped the coin over to her. "The Labyrinth gave me this as 'compensation'. Looks like it's all fixed now."

"Isn't one of the quadrant shops kind of near here? We should go tomorrow and see what you get for it."

"Good idea. With all the money we've looted, we should be able to afford some more upgrades, too. I wonder if Skraag has made any progress with his bomb projects…"

"You are not getting more of those things!" Naomi exclaimed, rolling her eyes. "Didn't you almost blow yourself up with the last one?"

"That was user error. There wasn't anything wrong with the bomb."

"You say that like that makes it any better."

The two of them locked eyes for a few moments. Then, they both started to laugh.

"How is this our life…" Jade said, flopping down to lie beside the crackling fire. "It all feels so unbelievable, sometimes. You know?"

"I do…" Naomi's hand slid over the patched-up gash in her armor where the wraith demon had nearly killed her. Luis and Tara had been able to get it functional again, but they should really get her a new set as soon as possible. Even enchanted armor could only take so much punishment. "Jade, I…"

"Don't." Jade held up her hand in a quieting gesture. The word must have come out harsher than she'd intended, because Naomi winced. "No, sorry…" Jade sat up, propping herself up on her elbows. "I just meant, we've already talked about it. Alan and I did, too. You heard him. He wouldn't have had this any other way."

During their short Earth-side vacation, Naomi had wanted to visit Alan. It ended up being a group visit with the whole party, which alarmed some of the hospital staff. Apparently, people on Earth weren't used to being around non-humans yet, and Tara's appearance was more than a little exotic. Alan had been thrilled, of course, and was eager to meet the rest of Jade's companions. The memory made her smile.

"I know." Naomi poked at the fire for a few moments, staring into its flickering depths. "Just know that I'm not going to stop looking for a way to help him, too. I don't care if he says that's not my responsibility. It's what I want."

"Thanks, Naomi. That means a lot."

They talked for a while longer, coming up with some ideas. There was a chance that Naomi's class would eventually give her access to high level healing spells, but that wasn't the sort of thing one could find reliable information on. If not, then they could always find a healer willing to travel to Earth with them. Once they were able to afford the fee for such a service, at any rate. For Jade, just having a path forward was enough for now. The jungle darkened as the evening stretched late, twilight descending over the quadrant. So much had happened so quickly. Last they'd heard, director Holston was still severely injured and would be out of commission for a while. And with the deputy director both dead and revealed to be a demon, L-ReF as a whole was in complete disarray.

That wasn't reassuring, but the immediate increases in security would help keep her brother safe, if nothing else. The subsequent investigations had uncovered several more demon infiltrators, and more than one other attack was thwarted before it began. That wasn't to say what happened at L-ReF HQ was an isolated incident, though. Similar attacks had taken place in various countries around the world, mostly directed at military bases and other secure facilities. The thoroughness of the demon infiltrators caught Jade by surprise. Her experience with their kind was quite limited, but she'd had the impression that they were bestial and monstrous creatures driven largely by instinct. While some of them might be just that, it was clear that there was more to their people. Demons came in many varieties, maybe some were trained for social deception rather than battle. For all that he'd slipped by undetected for so long, Sam had proven to be something of a pushover when confronted head on…

In either case, Jade was still kicking herself for not having picked up on Sam's disguise. She'd warned the director about potential spies while remaining completely oblivious herself. If she'd been more alert, maybe she would've noticed incongruencies in his emotions and behavior. For that matter, maybe she would have picked up on the astonishing number of facility staff that had been under his control. In the aftermath, Jade had been pulled in to act as a charm detector. No critical base personnel had been under the effects of any charm magic, but a shocking number of functionaries bore either active spells or lingering traces of them. That turned out to be how the weapons' transfer had occurred. It was incredible what could slip through a bureaucracy when the people at the top were fed false information from the people down in the weeds of the work. Jade wondered how many other worlds the demons had done this to. The entire operation had been far too sophisticated for it to be the work of amateurs.

As a result, she and Tara had a new joint project. The tek'kalla still had the demon detection charm Ashar had sold her back when they'd first met. Tara kept it on her at all times, now, although she needed Jade's help so she could make the necessary adjustments for the magic to ignore Jade's presence. Hopefully, they'd be able to supply L-ReF with similar equipment. Earth was just too new to magical deception, and the few safeguards in place were crude and easily subverted.

But those were worries for later. They were here to recover, both mentally and physically. A few more days, and then it was time to get back to the front line and see what progress their fellows were making with the Cores. Jade found that she was looking forward to getting back to the action. She had some frustrations to work out, and a bunch of Labyrinth monsters would be the perfect target.
