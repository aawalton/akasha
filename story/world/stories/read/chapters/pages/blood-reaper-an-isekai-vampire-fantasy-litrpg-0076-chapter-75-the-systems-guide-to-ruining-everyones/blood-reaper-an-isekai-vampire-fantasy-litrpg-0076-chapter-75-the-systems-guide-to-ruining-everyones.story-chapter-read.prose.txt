This wasn’t going well.

Ace winced as Raven roared, the sounds coming out of the man’s mouth no longer remotely human. The sentinel’s remaining eye vanished beneath the tide of shadow essence. What stood before them now was a churning vortex of pure shadow corruption shaped vaguely like a man—a hungry void consuming everything it touched.

Oh, fuck.

He was drawing energy from the Shadow Realm, and that meant he was up to something dire. The corrupted guardian had become a conduit, a doorway for something infinitely larger.

The air temperature plummeted. Frost crystallized on the blackened grass, spreading outward from where Raven stood. The corruption expanded, first engulfing his form entirely, then shooting across the ground in pulses of absolute darkness. Each wave froze the ground it touched, as thought reality itself was beginning to crack under the pressure.

Through the hivemind, Ace sensed Marcus’s psychic probes being systematically crushed by whatever lurked behind Raven’s consciousness. This wasn’t just corruption anymore—something ancient had taken notice of their battle.

Olivia abandoned her position and darted to Marcus’s side as his knees buckled. She caught him before he hit the ground, her own breath coming in short gasps.

A surge of panic flowed from her through their connection, followed by a desperate plea to retreat.

Raven—or whatever remained of him—had stopped attacking. The corrupted guardian now stood motionless at the center of the expanding darkness, his arms outstretched. Shadow essence poured from him, creating a whirlpool effect that dragged smaller rocks and debris closer.

A laugh escaped the vortex—not Raven’s voice but something deeper and older. It resonated in Ace’s bones rather than his ears.

Ace closed his eyes, drawing upon the combined blood essence now flowing through his veins. Tara’s sacrifice had given him incredible strength.

Time to test how far he could go.

His skin tingled as he activated Shadow Realm Immersion, but instead of fully phasing, he held himself at the threshold between worlds. Through half-lidded eyes, he saw reality overlaid with the monochromatic Shadow Realm. The vortex appeared different from this perspective—not expanding chaos but a structured pattern, a deliberate summoning.

The boundary between realms was thinnest here, drawn open by Raven’s desperation. But that thinness worked both ways.

Ace stretched out his senses through the hivemind, connecting not just to his teammates but to the blood essence that flowed between them. Tara’s healing energy, Marcus’s psychic patterns, Olivia’s blood magic—all created a network united by his presence at the center.

I need your strength, he transmitted. All of you.

Understanding flowed back to him. Without instruction, Tara channeled her remaining energy into the hivemind. Marcus redirected his psychic power to enhance their connection, and Olivia released a burst of blood magic.

Their combined essence flowed through Ace. He stood at the threshold between worlds, one foot in reality, one in shadow.

Through the hivemind, Ace felt their essence merging once more, creating patterns of energy that flowed between them. He closed his eyes, sensing the thin boundary between realms, the shadow that waited just beyond perception.

“Well,” he said. “Here goes nothing.”

Ace opened his eyes.

Reality split apart like torn fabric. The physical world blazed with color and texture on one side while the Shadow Realm yawned open on the other—a monochrome negative where light bent wrong and the darkness had substance.

Ace stood at the fracture point, his body flickering as it existed in both places simultaneously. Each shallow breath drew in air from dual realities like cold fire that scorched his lungs. Through eyes that burned with the strain of holding himself in the In-Between, he watched Raven’s vortex transform between worlds. In the physical realm, it twisted and churned in mindless destruction—raw shadow blistering with hate and hunger. But in the Shadow Realm, it revealed its true nature—an intricate tapestry of arcane runes rotating in perfect sync, each pattern calculated and deliberate.

But he wasn’t the only one who could use this power.

A metallic bite filled his nostrils, something he couldn’t name, and it mixed with the iron tang of spilled blood. Each breath brought glass shards to his lungs. The air vibrated against his skin as both shadow and reality remained at war.

Blood essence pulsed through the hivemind network. Tara’s crimson healing threads. Marcus’s vivid purple magic. Olivia’s blood red essence. Ace at the center. The conduit. His mental map blazed—Tara crouched thirty yards back, weak but determined; Marcus and Olivia flanking him, pulsing with battle-readiness.

He felt his hold on the In-Between falter, and for a moment, the world snapped back to reality. It flickered violently, and he gritted his teeth as he struggled to hold on to his place.

He needed more energy.

His team’s immediate response came in power, not words.

Tara channeled her remaining Soul Meter. Golden light flared around her fingertips. Healing magic unwound from her core, flowing into their network. Her face went chalk-white. Her lips, pale. Sweat beaded her forehead. Her body trembled. Still, she gave everything.

Marcus redirected his psychic energy. His form shimmered. The air around his head distorted. Thought fragments—tiny luminescent motes—orbited him like planets. Each pulsed with his breath, feeding power into the hivemind.

Olivia’s hands danced in complex patterns. Combat stances transformed into artful choreography. Each completed form sent crimson ripples through the air. Her expression remained serene, almost detached, even as sweat soaked her clothes.

All at once, the raw power of his team surged through Ace.

His skin crawled. Shadow and blood battled within his veins. Every cell vibrated. His vision tripled—physical reality, Shadow Realm, and the hivemind awareness, all overlapping. He felt stretched across dimensions, thin and malleable.

“You think you understand what you’re playing with?” Raven’s voice cut through the air like ice shards.

The corrupted guardian hung suspended in the Shadow Realm twenty feet above. His form no longer resembled anything even vaguely human, as he was little more than a swirling vortex of shadow essence. The column of darkness twisted and churned, spanning from ground to sky. Void-black tendrils lashed outward, whipping at his surroundings. Frost crystallized wherever they landed, and anything they touched imploded into dust.

Raven’s face occasionally formed and dissolved in the vortex’s center. His features contorted in rage and twisted in pain. His eyes remained constant, however—pools of absolute darkness weeping shadow tears that evaporated mid-air.

“I was going to show you mercy,” Raven said, his voice layered with the words of other people and darker beings. “I won’t make that mistake again.”

The vortex compressed into a midnight spear above them. Shadow squeezed against shadow, pressure building as it focused into a lethal point. The air went from cold to frigid. Frost bloomed across the grass in jagged patterns, crackling as it spread. The ancient pines surrounding them popped and groaned, the wood shrinking in the sudden cold snap.

The vortex pulsed.

Once.

Twice.

Again and again.

Each vibration hammered out a sound so deep it bypassed Ace’s haering entirely, shaking his teeth loose and turning bone marrow to jelly.

This was Death itself, readying its strike.

And strike, it did.

Ace was ready. Through the hivemind, he sent commands like electrical impulses—not words but pure tactical intent firing across their neural network.

The team erupted into synchronized action.

Marcus channeled Terror Cascade while Olivia’s form already blurred into multiple Blood Echo duplicates. The psychic assault manifested as violet ripples slamming into the vortex the same instant Olivia’s duplicates took position in a perfect triangle around its base. Terror energy destabilized the shadow construct from within as Olivia’s blade slashed at its foundation.

The vortex shuddered—rotation faltering, streaks of lighter shadow creating fracture lines through its structure. Its smooth hum transformed into a discordant wail. Shadow tendrils lashed out in all directions, targeting both Marcus and Olivia simultaneously.

Marcus dove across frost-covered ground as tendrils punched into the earth where he’d stood. Soil sizzled on contact. Two of Olivia’s replicas vanished on impact. Blood essence scattered upward like crimson sparks while the real Olivia slashed through shadow with a silk-tearing sound.

Steel met darkness. Shadow essence sizzled and recoiled. A gash opened in the vortex, leaking night tendrils that writhed like wounded snakes while Marcus’s fear energy widened the breach from within.

The vortex wobbled, trying to heal two different types of damage simultaneously.

Olivia’s water-fluid movements carried her beyond his retaliatory strikes while Marcus jumped behind a frost-covered boulder. Shadow tendrils shattered its top, sending crystallized chunks raining down around him.

The coordinated assault had wounded Raven’s construct, but the damage was already closing—darkness folding back on itself, sealing ruptures with angry pulses of shadow.

Ace seized the moment. Through the hivemind, he pulled at the Shadow Realm energy flowing through their connection, drawing power across dimensions like threading lightning through the eye of a needle. Black energy crackled between the four teammates, jagged bolts arcing from one to another that left burning afterimages on their retinas. Shadow essence swirled around them in protective cocoons, gradually absorbing the ambient light until they stood in pools of absolute darkness.

Their eyes shifted in unison, transforming into black pools with crimson pinpoints at their centers—vampires evolving to see beyond the veil of worlds.

With a thought, Ace called forth his Abyssal Armory. The void-black portal materialized before him, its edges rippling with shadow energy while miniature lightning crawled across its surface, sizzling in the frigid air. He reached inside, fingers closing around the familiar hilt of his Crimson Sword.

The weapon responded to his touch, vibrating with recognition. It hummed a single, pure note that cut through the vortex’s discordant wail, ringing with bizarre clarity as if originating from somewhere beyond physical space. When he withdrew the blade, it no longer glowed its namesake red but pulsed with the combined energy of his entire team—blood essence and shadow power interwoven along its length. The metal appeared partially transparent, a window into the void beyond, while ancient symbols flickered along its edge. The runes appeared and vanished too quickly to read, each leaving burning afterimages like characters in a language predating vampires themselves.

Simultaneously, he summoned every Duskblade he could afford to bring to life. Dozens materialized around him in a defensive sphere, shadow-forged daggers humming with the same hybrid energy. They orbited him in concentric rings, their paths inscribing complex geometric patterns in the air while reality itself thinned where they passed, offering brief glimpses of the Shadow Realm through tears in existence’s fabric.

“Look at you,” Raven laughed, his sound splintering into echoes. “Playing with powers beyond your comprehension. The Shadow Realm isn’t a place—it’s a predator.”

The vortex accelerated, compressing further into a needle-thin spear of absolute darkness aimed directly at Ace’s chest. It generated its own gravity now, pulling small rocks and debris into its rotation while frost spread outward in crystalline patterns, flash-freezing the soil beneath. The shadow spear shot forward with impossible speed, trailing void-black energy that scorched the air itself. Its advance produced a sound that bypassed ears entirely to resonate directly with the brain—a high, keening vibration that scraped against the inside of their skulls.

Through the hivemind, Tara’s essence pulsed with warning. Her healer’s senses detected the lethal intent in Raven’s attack—this would not wound or incapacitate.

It would annihilate.

Ace didn’t flinch. The Duskblades surrounding him responded to his will, shooting forward to meet Raven’s attack. One after another, the shadow daggers intercepted the spear, each absorbing a fraction of its energy before disintegrating.

The first blade struck the spear’s leading edge, and shadow-forged steel met condensed void essence with a bang. For a fraction of a second, the two forms of darkness recognized each other—shadow greeting shadow across the dimensional divide. Then the Duskblade shattered, its fragments dissolving into wisps of night that swirled briefly before dissipating.

The second dagger met the same fate, then the third. Each interception slowed the spear’s advance by milliseconds, bleeding away minute amounts of its power. Each destruction released a burst of negative light—darkness so intense it burned the eyes—while the air filled with the scent of char and something more alien, like reality itself burning.

The impact drove Ace backward, his boots carving deep furrows in the blackened soil. The ground beneath him compressed under the force, stone and dirt fusing into glass-like sheets. Through the hivemind, he sensed Marcus and Olivia bracing themselves, using their own abilities to reinforce his stand.

Duskblade after Duskblade sacrificed itself to the void spear. Fifteen, twenty, thirty—each one slowing the attack, each one buying precious fractions of a second. Sweat poured down Ace’s face, immediately freezing in the unnatural cold while his muscles screamed with the effort of maintaining his position. Blood trickled from his nose until he didn’t know if he could take it anymore.

When the final Duskblade shattered, the spear had slowed to half its original speed—still deadly, but now within the realm of what Ace could counter. Through the hivemind, he drew the last reserves of power from his team. Tara’s healing essence, Marcus’s psychic energy, Olivia’s blood magic—they all flowed into him in a final, desperate surge.

Ace channeled everything into the Crimson Sword. The blade drank in the shadows until it resembled a tear in reality more than a weapon. Light bent around it, unable to touch its surface, while the air ignited where it passed, creating tiny blue flames that danced along its edge. The weapon’s weight shifted in his grip, becoming simultaneously heavier and lighter—as if it existed in multiple dimensions at once.

With a shout that tore at his throat, Ace lunged forward. The Crimson Sword cut upward in a perfect arc, meeting the remnants of Raven’s attack head-on. The blade whistled through the air, leaving contrails of black smoke in its wake while each molecule of atmosphere it displaced exploded with tiny pops that combined into a deafening roar.

Metal edge met shadow essence, and for a heartbeat, nothing happened.

Then reality cracked.

A shockwave of released energy blasted outward from the point of impact. The air compressed, then expanded with explosive force. Sound deserted the world for one infinite moment, a perfect silence spreading outward from the collision point before noise returned with deafening intensity—not a bang or explosion but a deep, resonant tone that vibrated through bone and soil alike.

The ground beneath them buckled, splitting into radiating fissures. The dirt heaved upward before collapsing into newly formed chasms. The blackened grass disintegrated into ash that swirled upward in choking clouds, forming a miniature tornado that danced across the battlefield. Even the obsidian lake’s surface rippled, waves spreading outward as if a stone had disturbed its perfect stillness.

This was it.

The final blow.

With this last attack, they had either won or, worst case, sacrificed the last of their magic in a desperate final blow that had ultimately failed.

Ace held his breath—exhausted, broken, and bleeding—as the thinning darkness finally cleared.
