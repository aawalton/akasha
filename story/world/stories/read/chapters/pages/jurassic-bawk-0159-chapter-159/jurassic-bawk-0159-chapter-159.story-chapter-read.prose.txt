“Welcome, volunteers, welcome to the System’s Favor.  I am Paragon Prime, one of the System’s Champions.  But you, my comrades in arms, may refer to me as ‘Prime’.  I am so glad to see another crop of new arrivals has joined the war against the nefarious, evil, immoral anti-System forces that threaten not just our way of life, but also our families, our loved ones, and reality as we know it!  With your help, we will prevail!”

As he spoke, Paragon Prime repeatedly grinned at the camera while striking various heroic poses.  He was standing in what appeared to be outer space, high above a green and white planet that was encircled by an enormous metallic ring that appeared entirely artificial.  In the distance, Charlemagne could just make out small swarms of black dots that were emerging from the ring and slowly growing larger.  The planet itself was fascinating, but what was even more interesting to the rooster was that the screen appeared to be emitting whiffs of mana, allowing those who possessed more esoteric senses than sight and hearing to better understand the scene in front of them.

The rooster could feel the overwhelming and visceral might emanating from Paragon Prime.  Even seemingly diminished through the medium of transmission, he felt smothered by the waves of power roiling off the System’s greatest Champion.  The only other time he had felt so small, so insignificant, was when he had stood before his Patron, Grimfalk, and compared his own existence to the deity’s.  At least, Charlemagne believed that to be the case, although he had dim memories of a strange conversation with a being that far surpassed even Grimfalk.

Charlemagne’s attention was drawn back to the present by the gasps of those around him.  It appeared that he was not the only one affected by the broadcast: roughly one in every ten of the beings around him reacted in some way to the naked power on display.  Some gasped, others laughed, while a few actually appeared to lose consciousness entirely.  This caused the entire room to erupt into a frenzy of half-whispered, half-shouted conversation.  The outbursts lasted for quite some time, which annoyed the rooster; he just wanted to see what would happen next.  Unfortunately, the Orientation Officer seemed disinclined to step in and put a stop to the interruptions.

Seemingly aware of his effect on the audience, Paragon Prime waited some time before he began speaking again.  But, even so, his voice was lost in the hubbub for a moment until order was restored.

“….to witness the absolute barbarity of those who align themselves against the noble System.  For, not content to merely inhabit those universes not yet brought into the System’s warm embrace, they actively attempt to drive it out from its own territory.  Some of you may have seen the results of their work.  In fact, many of you are here today because someone you love was taken from you by these evildoers, these terrorists who destroy everything we hold dear in the name of their so-called ‘freedom’.”

Prime stopped for a moment and gestured at the planet in the distance.

“For example: this planet is called Panfauna.  It used to be so beautiful.  Pristine oceans, tall mountains, and lush vegetation as far as the eye could see.  It was a place where giant, savage beasts roamed endless forests and fields, fighting for dominance in an environment so rich, so dense with mana that even newborn creatures could fight toe-to-toe with mighty hunters.  It was once my favorite hunting grounds, a place where I sharpened my skills and trained many a young warrior hoping one day to surpass me.  But now, look at it. The anti-System forces have destroyed all live on the surface, using the primal power as fuel for forbidden technomagic.  Even from here I can feel its effects: my connection to the System is weaker, my Skills have dulled, and my Attributes have been reduced.”

While Paragon Prime was busy pontificating, the clouds of black dots grew larger until they finally resolved into individual shapes.  Thousands of what appeared to be metallic-looking insects flew silently toward the lone warrior, who had so far ignored their approach.

“Thankfully, since the System’s Favor is nearby, I can fight at almost full strength.  I’ve been itching to get back to Panfauna and kick the anti-System forces off of it.  While it will never be as beautiful as it once was, with the System’s help, it can become something glorious again!  And, of course, justice will be served.”

Prime flexed as he finally turned to face the enemy swarm, his muscles bulging with barely-restrained power as mana swirled around him, its pale blue hue easily discernible against the darkness of outer space.  With a grunt and a flex of his willpower, the magical energy suddenly rushed to his hands, condensing until it felt almost solid.  The enormous man pulled back his right arm and let forth a punch that moved so fast it seemed to outpace reality itself.  Not content with just one punch, Paragon Prime attacked again and again, his two fists blurring as he yelled a battle cry into the void of space.  Then he turned back around to face his audience.

“There, that should do it,” he said with a smirk.

Charlemagne was unsure what had just happened.  He hadn’t been able to follow the man’s fists: they had been moving far faster than his eye could track.  His magical senses, however, could tell that there was enough mana infused into each blow to rip him asunder.

He was still trying to puzzle out what the man’s plan when the explosions started.

The entire left flank of the enemy melted away as each detonation took out scores of the flying insects.  Then the center suffered the same fate.  The right flank lasted a bit longer, as the enemies began to spread out in response to the overwhelming bombardment, but their evasive maneuvers were too little, too late.  They blew up all the same.

The rooster watched with fascination, awe, and not an insignificant amount of envy.  Paragon Prime had just wiped out thousands of enemies in a flash, displaying overwhelming might and range.  Each of Prime’s punches contained far more power than Charlemagne’s strongest attack.  And that way that he manipulated mana was just…sublime.  It was like playing a kazoo while watching a maestro conducting a world-class orchestra.

“And that wraps that up, dear comrades,” Paragon Prime laughed as he gestured to the carnage behind him, where, among the corpses of the enemy swarm, secondary explosions cropped up here and there as the dregs of the anti-System forces lost the battle against oblivion.  “Now that you’ve seen a bit of what you’re up against, and who is on your side, I’d like you to once again give your full attention to my very good friend, Orientation Officer Salma Hiin.”

For some reason, the way that Paragon Prime said the words “Orientation Officer Salma Hiin” sounded off, as if someone else was attempting to imitate Prime’s voice.  But it didn’t seem to matter: the recruits stood and began to cheer as a new figure strode up to the stage.

“Thank you for that warm introduction, Paragon Prime.  Now, I’m sure you’ve got a few questions after seeing Paragon Prime.  Yes, that video was one hundred percent real.  Panfauna was one of the anti-System’s first major bases, but we wiped them out without taking a single loss that day.  Yes, Paragon Prime is the System’s strongest Champion.  No, you’re not likely to ever meet him in person, not unless you work as hard as you possibly can and get much, much stronger.  You might represent the best and the brightest of all System-integrated space but that doesn’t mean a whole lot here.  You’ve got a lot of competition: every single being here is a prodigy that has volunteered to fight until they have covered themselves in glory or died a warrior’s death.  In fact, since you’re the newest batch of recruits, you are going to have to put in some hard work in order to catch up with those already ahead of you.  As you are now, you are under-leveled, under-Skilled, and sorely lacking in combat experience.  We are going to change all that over the next few dozen cycles.

Some of you may be wondering, ‘if Paragon Prime is so powerful, why do you even need us?’  That is a good question.  Some of you that have two brain cells to rub together might have deduced that the distance one can safely travel from the System’s Favor is dependent upon the individual.  Paragon Prime, the Symbol of the System, cannot stray far from the System’s warm embrace.  This is no secret: the anti-System forces are well aware of our Champion’s limitation.  You, being much weaker than he is, tend to be capable of ranging much further into non-System space while still maintaining combat readiness.

So that is the other reason that you are here.  Your very presence here feeds power into the System’s Favor, allowing the great System’s influence to support the incredible strength of mighty Champions like Paragon Prime.  But, beyond that, you can go to places where our elites cannot.  You can be the tip of the System’s spear, seeking out new enemy installations, overrunning supply convoys, harassing enemy scouts, and a host of other duties that are important to the war effort.  The System’s Favor is only one vessel, and cannot be everywhere at once.  But you can.  So train hard, because we’ll be putting that training to good use in the very near future.

Now, on to logistics.  You will each be assigned a bunk, a mess hall, and a training station.  Those of you who have crafting Skills will also be assigned to a crafting station.  Don’t worry, you don’t need to remember much of anything: the System will take care of leading you anywhere that you are authorized to go.  All you need to do is ask and you’ll be led to the right place.”

Officer Hiin paused for a moment and appeared to read a message sent to her by the Squiggles.

“Ah, apparently we have a recruit who possesses a pocket dimension containing…his family.  Could this recruit please identify himself?”

Charlemagne, not realizing that he was being asked to speak up, sat in silence with the rest of the recruits.  There was a long pause before the Training Officer tried again.

“Okay, maybe that was too complicated.  Did anyone here request access to a small piece of land?”

The rooster finally put two and two together and called out.

“Bawk!”

“Yes, I see.  Well, thank you for speaking up.  I needed to inform you that we’ll be conducting some security tests on the nature of the connection: no one has ever arrived at the System’s Favor with their own demiplane before, so this is a first for us.  The System will let you know when you are able to access your territory.”

The rooster was annoyed by the delay, and began to puff himself up with indignation.  However, the memory of Paragon Prime and the overwhelming might he had displayed was still fresh in Charlemagne’s memory.  Surely such power was worth suffering a short delay in reuniting with his flock.  His annoyance evaporated almost immediately, replaced instead by an intense longing to be back among his kind.  He was even looking forward to speaking with the human servants who attended to the needs of his family: after all, they were much better conversationalists than most of his flock.

His mind filled with happy thoughts, Charlemagne lost himself for a few long moments as the Orientation Officer continued to ramble on about his new life in this austere but somehow benevolent organization.  The rooster almost didn’t notice when the session finally ended much later, and the other recruits began to disperse.  When he snapped back to reality, a message from the Squiggles was awaiting his attention.

A glowing yellow shape pointed toward one of the room’s many exits.
