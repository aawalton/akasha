Still, he was using the new Permaflect skill beautifully, and when he corralled them into a tight knot, Logan pounced.

Eldertread!

The pain wracked Logan, and if not for his Bleed Immunity amulet, he was certain he would have suffered the affliction. Although no blood poured out, he could see straight to the bone beneath his bicep. He couldn’t swing his gauntlet with that arm in this condition, so he pinballed himself down toward the ward. By the time he noticed a raptor between him and his goal, it was too late.

Invigorating Flame!

Bromlin’s battlecry was so loud, even Senna had to clamp her ears shut.

Invigorating Flame!

Logan kept one eye out for any sign of additional raptors, and it was only absently that he checked his own mana supply. His eyes bulged when he noticed his HUD showing it at a mere 27%.

Sure, he had used some mana bouncing from beast to beast, but not that much. The only heavy drain he had put on it was… using Invigorating Flame on Bromlin.

That was something to keep in mind. Invigorating Flame boosted others’ power proportionate to the amount of mana invested, and for as long as the caster concentrated on it. Logan’s multifocus benefit was great, but in the heat of battle, it was easy to lose track of the fact he still had a concentrated spell engaged. Bromlin had used up a great portion of Logan’s mana, even without Logan having intended it. And Bromlin had pulled hard from that draft.

Logan frowned. That couldn’t be right.

But apparently it was, and as the entire ward platform jittered and raised a collective whoa from the group, Logan racked his brain for a reason.

The ambient mana sprang to life around him. Something was different here. The layered striations were imbalanced, and Logan groaned as he realized the root cause.
