Of course, what good was there in having someone who could use your skills if you didn’t upgrade your killer move?

Activating Last Stand depleted me of all of my Qi and stamina, but I wasn’t too worried about it.

That was because I searched into my bag of holding and grabbed a handful of Qi Fruits, instantly regenerating enough Qi for a subsequent use of Flaming Rebirth later down the line.

My clone didn’t use it yet, instead trying to unleash a flurry of blows on me, all of which failed.

Phoenix Reflex at the Master rank felt so good! I could scarcely believe it. How could I have been so blind to trying to upgrade it? I could practically see attacks before they were even swung!

The old me was nothing now!

Taking advantage of the moment of confusion on the clone’s face, I gave it a fully powered kick right to the head, swinging down and forcing it to stand still. Afterwards, using the last few seconds of Last Stand, I knocked it backward and waited no more than two moments before I activated Flaming Rebirth right on top of it.

Two moments, not right as the previous skill stopped its effects. If my theory was right, then this would give me a nice, easy victory.

At that moment, it had no choice. It would either die while I generated all my resources to one hundred percent, or…

It activated Last Stand as well!

Haha, just as I’d planned. Now, while I was regenerating, it would tank the powerful blast, and it would probably use Flaming Rebirth right after.

However, considering that I had a cooldown on the skill, I would much rather not have to deal with the version of the clone that was yet again back up at one hundred percent.

Instead, I had a more devious idea.

As the explosion spread out and the thing tanked everything due to the skill that gave it a strange power not to take damage by somehow dodging a literal explosion, I wasn’t going to question that; I took advantage of the fact of its lack of experience.

Sure, it had my appearance, stats, and skills, but that didn’t mean that it could just copy my thought process. It saw me use Flaming Rebirth with a delay, so it would probably do the same thing…

And I was right!

Last Stand ended, but it didn’t activate the life-saving Flaming Rebirth right away.

Quickly closing the gap between us with Side Step and Ember Step, I gave it a very light Tail Whip right across the face.

Shwoop!

The clone stopped moving, and the place where I’d hit it separated from the rest and melted down before the entire thing followed suit, becoming nothing but a puddle of silver that came closer together like some sort of slime.

There was no notification for defeating it either, and no experience gain. That was fine, since I didn’t want to have too much experience backed up just in case it would hurt me.

As for the slime… It was… a little disgusting to look at, but in the end it stopped moving, and the mechanical voice echoed in the area.

“Congratulations! You have defeated a clone of yourself, and have earned the right to summon one at will, whenever you want! Seriously, take it away; we have way too many of these things.”

As its speech finished, the slime jumped up and rushed straight into my bag of holding, making itself at home.

O… kay…

“I hope you’re ready for the next trial!” The voice said as it faded, and a small gate appeared at the end of the room.

I lazily made my way there and took a look back at the place that allowed me to rank up some of my precious skills, and grinned.

Stepping inside the portal, I wasn’t sure what to expect next.

And it was… another arena.

I let out a sigh, but didn’t immediately see the thing I had to fight. Instead, the fat rat spirit appeared in front of me in its true form.

“That was dirty.” It said, most likely referencing what I’d done in the clone room.

“I think I used it exactly as intended.”

“Sure, but that used up a lot of time, you know? There’s only four hours left in the realm as a whole.”

I groaned. Of course there would not be a lot of time left; how annoying…

And I still hadn’t found what sort of thing Elder Su wanted.

“Do you know what a Ghost cultivator would need to break through their current bottleneck?” I asked without giving much context.

“Well, a lot of things, but what your friends would probably want is the core of a powerful Yin-Type beast. There’s nothing better to help with a bottleneck in my experience. I would know.”

“I see… I assume I would find that on the last floor, or level of this hidden realm within a hidden realm within a hidden realm?

“That…” The fat rat looked at me with a strange expression before sighing. “Sure, I guess that’s an adequate explanation of our current situation. And to answer your question, yes. Well, this is also the last trial.”

“Really?”

“What? Were you expecting more?”

“It just seemed like something that would have more challenges.”

“It was supposed to be very challenging. For a Qi Condensation cultivator.” The fat rat enunciated some words more than others, and well, sure, he couldn’t have expected that I would be a Spirit Beast… But come on, this was far too easy.

“So, why did you appear?”

“Just to tell you that you played dirty, and to give some information on the thing you got.”

“Oh? Do tell.”

“It’s a substance that can clone you, your equipment, stats, and everything else that makes you, you. Besides personality, evidently. It’s sort of a puppet you don’t control.”

“So it’s a good thing then, right?”

“Sure, depends on how you use it. It’s not better than an actual clone, but until you unlock that skill or technique, it could do.”

I was a little suspicious of the fat rat. “Why are you telling me this?”

“It’s my obligation, especially if you were planning on using it in this fight. You know, almost nobody ever defeated themselves. Most of the time, they would win if they survived.”

“What? What sort of bullshit is this?”

“Hey! Don’t blame me; I didn’t design this place. I just had to tell you, in case you were planning on doing something stupid and didn’t know it worked. That’s also one of the reasons why we have far too many of them stockpiled. The fact that you used such a dirty tactic to defeat it is commendable, though.”

“It’s not dirty, it’s efficient.”

“Oh, spare me. Anyway, now that you’ve gotten the information, good luck.”

“Huh? Why?”

“Because your next opponent will be someone very special.”

“A giant Yin-Type beast?” My eyes beamed, but the rat’s smile told me that it wasn’t so.

“It was, but since you like cheating so much, I decided to change things up a little. Enjoy…”

His figure disappeared as he said that, and the air in the arena changed a lot all of a sudden.

A freezing wind blew past, making my body shiver. I had to use my inner fire to stay warm, but it wasn’t entirely enough.

A wall of fog appeared on the other side of the arena, where there was no door, and a loud thumping, like a heartbeat or a war drum, echoed in the otherwise empty area.

I watched with widening eyes as a figure walked out of the fog.

He looked just like he did in the projection. Bulky build, short black hair, and wielding a miniature version of the Yin Despair Sword.

However, the cold, indifferent gaze in his eyes was different than what I’d seen in the projection. He was clearly not real, and only an illusion or form made with Qi, but what did that change?

When I saw him, a shiver ran down my spine. How was this a challenge for a Qi Condensation cultivator? These people were insane!

I’d just watched this man destroy thousands of plants with a single swing of the sword; how was I supposed to defeat him?

So what if he gave the aura of an early-stage Foundation Establishment Cultivator? A genius like him could kill Core Formation cultivators at this stage!

Maybe I fooled around too much…

“You damned Sword Spirit, what is the meaning of this?!” I yelled, but there was no reply, and I wasn’t expecting any to begin with.

No matter what, this thing was pure evil! EVIL!

“If I ever become the owner of this sword, I will see that you will not rest easy!”

Now that it’s cheated me of an easy win and a high-tier beast core, I wasn’t going to go easy on it.

I’d heard it mumble about the owner of the sword or whatever. What were the requirements? Damned be the heavens, I was going to meet them!

Gritting my… Well, not teeth. Figuratively gritting my teeth, I waited for it to make its first move. I didn’t have much time left in the realm, and I was planning on leaving toward the center as soon as I’d finished here. I already had an insane amount of gain; there was no need to risk myself further. If I couldn’t get what Elder Su wanted, then I could just concoct a pill for her later.

What was important at the moment… Was the opponent before me!

He didn’t make a move yet, and it didn’t look like he would move either. So whatever the case was, I had to attack first.

Walking forward, I took a deep breath.

I also did a ceremonial bow, just in case there was some awareness of the thing.

Doing it didn’t give me an advantage or whatnot, but respecting someone of such strength, even if they died, was important.

With that, I activated Strengthened Constitution, Heaven’s Protection, and also took a deep breath.

This man was more powerful than any Spirit Beast I’d faced so far, and even though the cultivation level wasn’t there yet, even Peng Long would have had a hard time dealing with him.

That damned rat…

I could physically feel the danger radiating off of him! How was that even possible?!

I didn’t even have Flaming Rebirth available, and even though Last Stand’s cooldown wasn’t as long, it would still be there.

Which meant that, for all intents and purposes, I couldn’t cheat anymore!

Damn it!

Thinking about my strongest possible combo at the moment, it definitely had to be any involving the Four Calamity Lotus Flame.

Would it really replace my Nirvanic Flames…?

Was I willing to have them replaced…?

That question tugged at my heartstrings more than it should have. Shaking my head, I left that question in the dark, to be answered another day.

Right now, what mattered was winning, and doing everything in order to do so.

With my body strengthened to its peak, I kicked off the ground and dashed toward the man. My feathers were covered in fire, as were my talons, imbued with Iron Claws and Flaming Talons, as well as my wings with Blade Wing.

My entire body was now a lethal weapon, ready to destroy anything it touched.

Side Step forward to close the gap, followed by Ember Step, leaving a trail of dark flame behind me.

Next was Elemental Wave, a few pulses hitting the man directly, and soon enough, it was time for me to attack.

Talons aimed at the stomach were deflected with the cold metal of the sword. I spun around and tried to wound his neck with my wings, but he’d stepped back, making me miss by a hair’s breadth.

Turning to face him, I took to the skies with a powerful flap of my wings and rained down on him with Pyro Breath.

A shield deflected the attack, and an illusory hand made the long way around in order to grab me.

I stopped my attack and flew up just in time to dodge, but they were relentless. I had no choice but to keep flying, only for the hands to multiply, easily becoming more than a hundred in a short amount of time.

The arena wasn’t spacious enough for me to do as I’d wished, and I would have no choice but to fight fire with fire.

Letting out a screech, I summoned four lightning dragons. Lightning was the natural bane of anything Yin, so they should be more effective than the Four Calamity Lotus Flame.

However… There were just too many hands!

Each time my dragons destroyed one, three more appeared. Just what sort of devious technique was this?

I had to be mindful of my Qi, as I couldn’t use too much. Recovering it in this place was nigh impossible, so both my stamina and Qi were a limited resource.

I had to end the fight as soon as possible, and as quickly as possible.

But that was easier said than done!

Just as I was distracted by another dozen hands trying to grab me, I’d failed to realise that the man had already appeared right beside me.

He wasn’t there a moment ago…

BAM!

His foot destroyed my shield as if it was nothing, and the kick sent me flying through the air, the world spinning around me until I finally stopped when I’d hit the wall.

Ah… MOTHERFUCKER!
