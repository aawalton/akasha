—Weird Dream? Past?—

The sky was clear, and countless stars could be seen in the night sky. Since the moon was not visible due to the lunar eclipse, the whole region was filled with darkness, with almost zero visibility. A mild wind current was there, soothing the gloomy atmosphere in the middle of the sparse forest.

A group of fourteen people were walking in a straight line silently while aiming their rifles and light machine guns. Everyone's guns were heavily modified with scopes, suppressors, and other attachments.

They were dressed in black uniforms. Each one of them wore body armor and black helmets equipped with night vision, a headset, and a radio. They were also carrying huge armory bags on their backs.

Their destination was to reach a terrorist hideout in the middle of the forest and rescue all the civilians and military personnel.

According to their intel, terrorists had occupied a standalone building in a mountain range. It was estimated that they had captured a few military personnel along with fifty to sixty civilians.

They had to complete this mission before dawn. Otherwise, the captured people would get transferred deep inside the enemy line. And it would become impossible to rescue them.

After walking for two whole hours, they finally reached the villa. They were still hiding in the darkness, observing the building. There were a few terrorists who were guarding outside the bordering wall. A few terrorists were stationed on the roof.

After checking the camera locations and the people guarding outside with binoculars, the whole team separated into four teams. Two assault teams of five personnel and two sniper teams of two agents each. The sniper's team ran separately in different directions. They had to cover the Front and the back gate of the villa from afar.

And the two assault teams were waiting in the shadows, not far from the gates. Both teams were prepared to go in from the front and the back door.

Since they were hiding in the forest, patrolling terrorists couldn’t see them

After a few minutes, one of the agents asked on his radio. “How many can you see? Over.”

The sniper replied, “I can see two on the roof and six outside the villa. Two on each side of the bordering wall. Over.”

Another sniper replied, “I can see the same, two on the roof and six outside the villa. Two on each side of the bordering wall. Over.”

“The bordering wall is guarded by two people on each side? Over.”

“Yes. Over.”

“Yes. Over.”

“Do you have a clear shot at the roof? Over.”

“Yes. Over.”

“Yes. Over.”

“Everyone, take your positions.” As soon as the leader commanded, everyone circled around the border slowly.

After finishing their positions, the leader commanded the snipers. “Take care of the guards on the roof. Over.”

As soon as the terrorists on the roof were down, every member of the assault team used their knives to attack from behind and kill all the terrorists outside the border silently.

After clearing the area, one of the team members stood close to the border wall, and another team member climbed on his shoulder to look over the wall using a mirror. He saw a few surveillance cameras in the vicinity.

If they want to infiltrate for an ambush, they have to do something to take out the surveillance. But seeing the camera positions, they determined it was not possible. All the cameras were facing the walls. As soon as they tried to enter by jumping over the wall, the alarm would ring.

“Team one, prepare for frontal assault. We’ll break through the front gate. Team two, you’ll hide outside the back gate. As soon as terrorists try to retreat from the back, you’ll shoot them down without showing yourself. Sniper team one and two, you’ll give cover fire and then retreat if necessary. Is that clear? Over.” The leader commanded over the radio.

“YES, SIR. OVER”

“Team one, attack.”

Team one started firing and took out four terrorists immediately, who were standing inside the huge gate. Then all of them started pushing and killing everyone who was trying to fire at them.

After a while, a few of the terrorists exited the back gate. They wanted to move from the back and attack Team One from behind. But Team Two killed them as soon as they reached a few meters away from the gate.

Team two intentionally didn’t kill immediately so that people inside wouldn’t see them getting killed and come running. This plan was successful.

More terrorists came and started running outside. As soon as they saw dead bodies, they were about to shout, but Team Two reacted fast and killed them immediately before they could shout.

Team one, along with their leader, had already entered the main gate and were about to reach the building's entrance.

Before entering, their leader immediately used a smoke grenade and threw it inside. His subordinates also followed and used a smoke grenade. The room inside the building’s entrance was big, and multiple smoke grenades were necessary.

As soon as the big room was filled with smoke, the terrorists started coughing. Seeing this, the whole team that entered started firing towards them. A few terrorists were killed; a few started firing with their rifles everywhere without even looking.

One of the team members got shot in the arm, and his gun fell out of his hand. He was equipped with armor, so none of the bullets penetrated his body. As soon as he fell down, the leader held his armor and dragged him towards cover.

Then the leader used his hand grenade and threw it towards the firing terrorist.

“MIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEEOOOOOOOOOOW.” And then he heard a loud, sharp, and piercing meowing sound.

Immediately, the infant dragon jolted up, opened his eyes, turned his body, and looked at the source of the loud, painful scream.

For the first time, the baby dragon was able to open them properly. Previously, his eyes only opened partially for a few moments due to undeveloped vision. Moreover, since his birth, he was never able to stay awake unless he was hungry or panicked.

And now he woke up due to sudden panic.

In front of him, a huge black cat was holding its head with its front paws and rolling on the ground in immense pain. Its head was glowing with blue light.

This black cat was Dawn. She was inside her den.

Due to her scream, all her babies woke up and started mewing in distress.

“Meeeeooow.eeeeooow.eeeooow.eeooow.” She was screaming nonstop.

The baby dragon got scared seeing the huge cat rolling violently on the ground. Then he lowered his head to the ditch and kept peeking at Dawn from the edge of the ditch.
