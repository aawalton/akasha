My baby had antlers! Huge branching horns on top of his head!

“Whaaaat?!” I knelt and turned his head from side to side. They were real, not a headband. “You grew horns in your human form?”

Eggy giggled and squirmed away from my grip to lower his head and playfully attack Mr. Bear with his new horns.

“Teacher, I’ll go ask Nani to join us,” said Kharli.

“Okay.” I turned to the others as Kharli dashed off. “Soooooo?”

Lari and Mo looked at each other. Mo shrugged, and Lari said, “We don’t know what happened. I feel like I blinked, and, suddenly, he had antlers like a deer.”

I scratched my head, baffled. “He seems fine. It’s probably nothing. Like the way Lady Hoshi’s fox tail and ears pop out now and then. But let’s wait for Nanny before we do the next task.”

Nani arrived quickly and smiled when she saw Eggy fighting invisible enemies with his new “dragon horns.” She told me it was fine and taught Eggy how to summon and unsummon the antlers as needed. In addition, she told me about the difference between deer antlers, Deer clan antlers, Azure Dragon clan horns, and other horns. Animal horns were permanent and grew continuously, while antlers were shed and regrown annually. Clan members, on the other hand, had horns and antlers that grew very fast during their childhood, which they shed and regrew like antlers. However, once they reached adulthood, they grew much more slowly and no longer shed their horns and antlers. The parts that fell off were made of a substance similar to bone and fingernails, and people ground them into a powder, which was mixed with water and drunk by the kids.

“But why does Eggy suddenly have huge antlers? They were stubs yesterday!” I said.

“He’s simply a very fast grower.”

I gestured to Eggy, who came over for a hug. “Oh, I see my balloon boy ballooned his horns.”

“Mom, you like horns?”

“Yes, I like your horns.”

Kharli and Mo tried to decorate his horns with flowers, but they kept falling off, and sticking them on with ribbons likewise failed. Instead, they made a flower crown for him and tied it around his forehead.

Now that the mystery of the horns was solved, we decided to continue the quest. Nanny was curious about it and asked to join us for the next part, which was to [Dig five spirit wells].

The System map showed the locations for each. Due North was the [Water Spirit Well]. Clockwise from there were the [Fire Spirit Well], [Earth Spirit Well], [Metal Spirit Well], [Air Spirit Well], all evenly spaced in a circle that encompassed the whole of Emberstone Farm. I had been hoping that my apprentices’ farms would also be included, but no such luck.

First, we visited the site where I had planted the [Flower Seed] and [Stone]. There was no change. I hadn’t really expected any, but it would have been nice if the seed had already sprouted. Second, we fetched Cutie since I wanted to take her for a walk. Third, we went to the Northern part of the farm.

This was the most naturally beautiful place on the farm. The groups of fruit and nut trees we had planted were in various stages of growth, some towering with thick trunks and broad canopies, others no taller than my waist, their thin stems barely supporting branches. The [Apricot] and [Peach] trees were in bloom, their branches weighed down by a sea of delicate, sugar-spun blossoms and filling the air with a crisp, sugary scent.

Walking here was pleasant thanks to the paths we had sown with the [Fragrant Evergreen Walkway Seed Mix].

[Fragrant Evergreen Walkway Seed Mix:

A mixture of seeds that include chamomile, creeping thyme, and resilient, low-growing grasses that give a small boost to soil health. Once planted, it thrives effortlessly, drawing from the earth's energies to sustain itself even with minimal rainfall and no fertilizers. It’s perfect for creating green paths that are delightful to walk on. With every step, a sweet fragrance will fill the air around you. These seeds will grow into plants that will suppress weeds by outgrowing them and will also help keep the soil from washing away.]

The grasses were soft underfoot, releasing a subtle fragrance with every step. The path was defined by the flattened growth where people had walked, but at the edges, the plants stood full and unmown.

Since trees needed a lot of pollination to bear fruits and nuts, we had also planted several [Insectary Gardens] here, which were full of spring flowers.

[Insectary Garden:

A special garden that attracts pollinators and beneficial predator bugs that eat aphids, thrips, white flies, and other pests. Tough native plants that have flowers bloom for a long time and are full of readily available nectar are the foundation of this valuable part of the farm that boosts yields and improves the quality of crops. Using the Insectary Seed Mix will ensure that flowers will be in bloom all year round.

More than one Insectary Garden can be planted on the farm.]

I spotted [Snowdrops], [Crocuses], [Daffodils], [Primroses], [Forsythia], and [Hyacinths]. I was also pleasantly surprised when Mo discovered a new type of flower growing in it.

[Heart’s Ease:

A low-growing herb distinguished by its three-colored petals of dark purple, pale yellow, and bright violet. Its small, oval leaves have notched edges and grow in dense clusters close to the ground.

In local folklore, carrying a dried bloom in one’s pocket is said to attract small, lucky coincidences, such as finding lost coins or arriving just as a rainstorm ends.

Also called Love-in-Idleness.]

“Teacher, I’ll take a few and dry them,” said Mo.

“I’ll help. Let’s take a few of the others, too. I think we can make scented sachets with them,” said Kharli.

The girls gathered flowers as we walked. When we reached the right spot, we saw that there was a large, moss-covered rock in the way. Lari scraped some of the moss away, and I wasn’t surprised at all to see that runes were carved on it. He stored it in his inventory for Scholar Wu to examine later, while I used the System’s [Build] tool to dig a hundred feet down.

A System pop-up appeared.

[Do you want to line the well with stone? Consumes 1000 Stones.]

Of course, I clicked the [Yes] button.

A magic circle appeared on the ground as usual, and flashed with brown light three times before disappearing. The opening of [Water Spirit Well] that appeared in its place was capped with a metal cover, even though the System hadn’t asked me for any metal. Lari tried to open it with help from the Nanny, but it was stuck fast.

“Don’t force it,” I said. “I guess this is to let us know we aren’t supposed to take water from this well.”

“Mom, can I use my antlers to open it?”

I pulled him away, laughing. “No, you’ll break them.”

We went to the other spots, Kharli and Mo still gathering flowers to dry, and Eggy charging at imaginary foes and savaging them with his new antlers. Cutie bounced alongside us, occasionally eating a flower or two.

Cutie was a golden slime, one of the many from the The Mines, also called the Chitinous Caverns, in the Southerlands. She was my pet, but she was far from an ordinary spirit animal or monster. Her real name was “The Resplendent Architect of Evolution with Eyes of Gold and Luminous Aura,” and she was the architect of a slime revolution. Cutie's faction had left their ancestral underground home in The Mines to build a new village out in the open sky.

She mostly bounced around on her own, but I often picked her up and petted her while we walked all around the farm.

I dug the wells on the other spots on the map, finding that they all appeared neatly covered with metal disks.

“Kids, learn from your Teacher. It’s best not to disturb a sleeping lion, or, in this case, a covered well infused with elemental energy.”

Lari sighed and said, “I was wondering what would happen if I mixed all five elements.”

My lips twitched. “Wouldn’t it explode?”

Right on cue, Cutie said, “Chu!”

I think she was agreeing with me.

The next step was to wait three days, so we went back to the house to bathe and change our clothes for the midday meal.
