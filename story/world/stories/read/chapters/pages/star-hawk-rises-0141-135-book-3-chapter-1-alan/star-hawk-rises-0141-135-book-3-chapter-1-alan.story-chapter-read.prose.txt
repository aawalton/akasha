The red light suddenly changed to green and the hatch lock spun before the door opened, and Alan led the twelve marines behind him through it and into a pristinely clean ship’s hallway. They quickly fanned out before slowly started to walk down the hallway, each time they found a door a fire team broke off and quickly cleared the room, finding no one inside.
