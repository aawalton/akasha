Chapter 61: Hammer

Ambrose was actually kind of looking forward to a fight, as stupid as he knew it was. There were no telling how many armed guards Bedrock Spire had. It had to be in the hundreds, bare minimum. Most were probably not especially strong or well-equipped, but they didn’t need to be. Unless you wielded the kind of broad, destructive, wide-sweeping power that wizards like Vanderborn did, numbers were dangerous.

One day, they’d get there. If they survived.

They’d gone down several sets of stairs, and his legs were just starting to turn a bit rubbery when they found trouble. It was two levels above the train station, if Ambrose recalled. With the layout of Bedrock Spire keeping the stairs from being in a line, they had to travel inward a bit. Others flowed past them, but the streets were largely empty at this point. The few people who were there…

They were in uniform, and two of them had their Signature Creatures out. One was this odd lizard with webbed wings between its legs. It wasn’t dragon-like at all, save for the long snout and prominent teeth. The other looked like a monkey, but the size of one of the molekin, and sturdy as hell.

The group of five or so guards turned in their direction as they got close. At first, Ambrose wanted to get the jump on them, and either say something disarming or attack, but he didn’t get a chance.

“Hey, is that them?” One younger guard with a thin mustache asked. He pointed a finger at Ambrose. “Tall, stupid beard, and wearing purple. That’s gotta be him.”

“Shit,” Raeva managed to say, before combat started.

Round 1

Cantrip: [Vampiric Kiss], Creature: [Night Terror], Spell: [Shred Mind], Signature Creature: [Hateful Imps], Spell: [Umbral Imp]

During the Pause, Ambrose took a moment to consider their opponents. Five of them, all their age or a little older, all human. They were likely offworlders, brought in by the Hermanic Empire to act as goons. Back on Amaria, it wasn’t uncommon to blood new military and mercenaries by having them enforce the state’s will on the fringes. That usually meant people who weren’t especially competent or strong in the day to day roles.

Ambrose and company may have been outnumbered, but between him and Raeva, they weren’t out-armed. He took a long, deep breath to steady himself, going over the order to cast his spells. The guards were in for a bad time.

When things resumed normally, Ambrose immediately cast his Signature Creatures. The summoning circle appeared right in front of him, while two of the guards summoned creatures of their own. Two rushed them, swords raised and creatures in front of them, while the last, the youngest of the group, took off running in the opposite direction.

The imps and dragon hatchling finished summoning just in time to attack the oncoming enemies. Neither of their Signature Creatures were fearsome looking, but they didn’t need to be. Raeva’s hatchling breathed a small spout of flames, slowing them to a stop, and then the imps each tackled one of the guards and began biting and shredding everything they could find.

Ambrose took a step back and cast his cantrip next. Free spells were great, and he could cast all but one card in his hand. It might not be necessary just yet, but judging by the screams of pain from the guards, they weren’t going to be alone for long.

The webbed lizard went for Raeva, getting a running start and leaping over the fallen forms of their guard. Before it could land, it winked out of existence as its master died. That left the rampaging monkey, scampering towards Ambrose. It howled in anger, showing disturbingly large teeth.

They didn’t coordinate it at all, it just happened. Ambrose took a step back while his arm glowed purple and white with the effort of channeling the magic. Luthor jumped in, blocking the blow, which of course blew up in the monkey’s face and sent him sprawling, where the hatchling finished the job with more flames.

That was two down, more or less, but the others had creatures now too. And unlike the previous creatures, these were bigger. One was, funny enough, a big spider, though nowhere as big as they’d seen. The other a large cat with wicked, curling fangs.

Before Ambrose could be worried, Raeva hurled her cantrip at the cat, getting its attention. Luthor strode forward, shield raised, while his [Ancestral Archer] finished summoning behind him. It took aim and loosed an arrow at the tiger.

The spider skittered forward, huge, pointed legs clanging against the metallic floor of the city. Ambrose’s imps went forward while he called up the next one, [Shred Mind]. This was a bad matchup, so he mentally directed them to try something else. When the spider got close enough to strike, the three imps juked and slipped past, moving on to the two guards, who were casting their next spells.

One spell went off, and the spider nearly doubled in size. His imps swarmed the caster, but the damage was done. The now ten foot tall spider went after them, seemingly impervious to the [Flame Lance] Raeva sent its way. The flame washed over the spider, hitting an unseen barrier before dissipating harmlessly.

“Shit,” Raeva repeated as it and the tiger closed in fast.

*****

Raeleq wasn’t afraid of much. Fear wasn’t particularly useful to hunters, warriors, or leaders. Sometimes he didn’t know which of those he was most, but he tended to not be afraid of much. Hearing the fighting and knowing that it was probably his friends and sister? That scared him.

Getting caught and killed wasn’t nearly as bad as knowing that it might happen to Raeva. They were so different, and she was the older and bolder of the two, but both had been raised with a simple but strong message: look out for each other. She tended to be better at it than him, but he refused to let anything happen to his twin.

“Faster,” he said, taking the stairs two at a time, even with the Levi-Box weighing him down. Jessica and Dexi’Lynn were both still intoxicated, and very short, but they did their best to keep up with him. His own heart and lungs were working overtime, but he’d sooner drop dead than stop.

He finally crested the stairs, and saw something that made his blood turn cold. There was a guard station there, and one guard ran screaming for help.

“Oh fucknuts,” Dexi’Lynn wheezed.

“There they are,” Jessica pointed over to the fighting.

Raeleq looked in time to see a giant spider sink mandibles into his sister’s left arm.

Before he knew what was happening, he dropped the Levi-box with a rough thunk. His feet moved seemingly of their own accord. There was a good fifty yards between them, but Raeleq was tall and his legs powerful. When he needed to, he could move. As such, he made it about halfway before things Paused.

Round 1

Cantrip: [Ironwood Club], [Spell: Feral Frenzy], Spell: [Rose Charm], Creature: [Mosquito Swarm]

He didn’t concern himself with tactics or a plan. When it came to fights, Raeleq was a blunt instrument and left the surgical strikes to others. He had a target, and he was going to hit it until it stopped moving.

As soon as he was able, he summoned his club. The cantrip took only a second and a half to finish, and then the heavy, balanced weight was reassuring in his hands. He cast [Feral Frenzy] as the distance dwindled, and he was filled with speed, strength, and the need to hit things.

Raeva screamed in pain as the spider jerked and sent her sprawling. Luthor was at her side in an instant, hands glowing gold while he cast a healing spell. His archer continued to harry the tiger while a handful of imps fought against their masters.

He didn’t know which of the two had summoned the spider and buffed it. It reeked of magic, an odd, unnatural scent that played with his senses when enough spells flew. The smart thing to do would be to take the guards out, but Raeleq had momentum, fear, and anger. He put all of those things into his swing, and snapped off one of the spider’s legs.

“Raeleq!” his sister called out in pleased surprise. “What took you so long?”

He wanted to answer, but the spider whirled around on him, ichor dripping from its stump. The leg itself twitched on the ground. Raeleq swung right as the spider attacked and broke off another leg. The creature buckled, and his rage carried him through the last few seconds of [Feral Frenzy], swinging again and again, driving the spider back and breaking pieces off.

When the spell ended, dizziness washed over him, and it made him hesitate just long enough for the spider to get an attack in. It struck with wide mandibles dripping venom, but Raeleq managed to bring the club up between them. It bit down hard enough that his club bent.

A second later, the spider disappeared, and then so did the tiger, who had been mid-leap. Raeleq looked over, panting, to see two things. One was the other two guards in the fight, now on the ground and bleeding profusely, if they were even still alive. Four imps crouched over their bodies, eating, until a sharp whistle from Ambrose brought them back.

The other thing he saw was the guard station’s doors open, and a dozen new fighters pouring out.

“Reinforcements,” Luthor called out. “Retreat the other way!”

“But Jessica,” said Raeleq, remembering his abandoned duty, and the part they badly needed to escape.

Jessica and Dexi’Lynn carried it between the two of them, running as fast as their short legs could take them. They were halfway between their friends and the new wave of enemies.

“Fuck,” said Raeva. “Okay, uh, we go all out, and then get down to the trains?”

“Sounds good to me,” said Ambrose. He slashed with his hand, and the four imps got moving.

Raeleq took a deep breath and tried to center himself, but the rush of battle was too loud. He got moving, right as the round ended.

Round 2

Cantrip: [Ironwood Club], Signature Creature: [Reggie], Creature: [Pack Raptor], Spell: [Control Overgrowth]

The last spell was useless, but the other three would do just fine. As soon as he had control again, he cast [Reggie] before meeting Jessica and Dexi’Lynn, who got behind him as soon as possible.

It would take a few seconds for his Signature Creature to appear, but that was fine. The guards nearest them paused in place to summon creatures of their own, and that was his opportunity. Even without [Feral Frenzy], Raeleq was a powerful man, capable of many feats of strength. Some people wondered why his cantrip was a weapon.

He grinned and hurled his [Ironwood Club] as hard as he could. It spun horizontally in the air, arcing just a bit before it beamed someone in the head. He dropped, and his summoning circle disappeared. The people nearest him paused, fear in their eyes.

Those precious few seconds of hesitation were all he needed. [Reggie] came into being behind him and stomped over to his side. The dinosaur drew his head back and let out a ferocious, ravenous roar, declaring his intention to break things and eat people, but maybe not in that order.

The others reached his line, and they stood united again, against an enemy they’d have trouble with.

“Well,” said Ambrose, never missing a chance to gamble, “I bet you ten crowns I can get more of them than you do before we’re killed.”

Raeleq laughed, oddly full of joy as he summoned another club.

“You’re on.”
