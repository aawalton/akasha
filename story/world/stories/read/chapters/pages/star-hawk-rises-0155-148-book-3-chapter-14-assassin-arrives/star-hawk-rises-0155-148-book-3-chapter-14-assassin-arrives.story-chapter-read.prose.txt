I had been cycling my Qi for several rotations before my eyes landed on the scroll, and I remembered this had belonged to the man who was a member of the sect and had left it to be a spy for them. A heartbeat later, it occurred to me that the core disciples or perhaps only the elders, maybe just the core elders, also had their own versions of divine cultivation techniques. It took a few seconds after this realisation for trepidation to run down my spine as I realised I was arrogantly considering waving my nascent realm in their faces like I was an immortal.

“Arrogant, arrogant, arrogant!” I chastised myself. “Humble yourself, lest you be smited by hidden power!”

I let out a sigh a few moments later as I let the cycling stop and my gaze stared at the scroll laying on the floor by me. “If I ever meet this great-grandfather of mine, I should thank him properly for leaving behind this remarkable technique. I also should probably contact my clan and smooth things over, I could even help them start cultivating, although considering we are a celestial clan perhaps they already are.” I mused to myself.

I suddenly smacked my cheeks and glared at the technique, “time to refocus and push for the immortal realm,” I declared to myself. “And I'm not coming out of my cultivation room until I have!”

I mentally slipped back into my dantian, stopping to watch the baby swimming around for a moment. With a giggle, I turned my attention to pouring Qi into it, to strengthen my soul.

It took several cycles before my eyes snapped open and I fled the cultivation room. I didn’t return until I was wearing a plain black robe and little else. I had a weird feeling that once I broke through to the next realm, numerous impurities would be purged, and I didn’t want it all over my nice robes.

I calmly sat back down on the black cushion surrounded by my potted plants that made the room smell nice, like back home in Scotland, the backdrop of space added a wonderful touch if I did say so myself. It was all calming and wonderful.

I slipped back into my dantian, returning to strengthening the soul baby, my hands resting comfortably in my lap. Pulling the Qi from the air and circling it through my meridians and into my dantian before exhaling the air out.

I sank into the cultivation soon forgetting about my surroundings, something in the back of my mind told me that this was foolish, and I had read enough xianxia novels to know better. However, at this time, my mind was consumed by the excitement of cultivating and progressing to the next realm. Like I was chasing a white rabbit down a hole with a fat, juicy steak waiting for me at the end.

Within moments, I had lost all sense of time and after what felt like hours, I was slowly discovering I was gaining no progress. It took another hour for me to realise I was meant to be aware of time and space, not just my dantian.

I let out a long sigh and slowly let my senses float out of my body, trying to observe the emptiness of space and the potted plants around me. It took hours to notice the effects of time on the planets, causing me to frown as I wondered why they were noticeably dying in real time.

I soon put the reason out of my mind, as I decided, I should focus on the effects of time on the plants. It took me hours, if not days, but I slowly began to progress again, igniting the excitement to continue cultivating.

The old man was staring at the Young Blood ship as he moved his ship towards it, trying to remain silent and invisible. He had bribed and struck deals with a fair few people to find this ship. Interestingly enough, the Mackenzie clan was in this dimension, he had approached a Clara who was the leader of the clan. While they had expressed some displeasure with the one named Kenna, they were tight-lipped and even hostile when asked if they could point him in her direction.

“She is definitely one of them” he muttered as his thoughts went over it again.

Shaking his head, he refocused on getting near the ship while keeping most of his ship turned off except for adjustment thrusters and life support. He had managed to almost match its speed before he had turned the propulsion systems off and coasted for the last ten hours. He was now within minutes of slipping into docking position with the Young Blood.

He silently cheered as the ship successfully docked with the Young Blood, and he shut down what was left, transferring control to the other ship, letting it keep his life support on and sneakily charge his ship's power banks.

He moved through his ship and exited the airlock, stepping onto the Young Blood, a smile across his face as he saw no one was there to greet him. He turned his head left to right a few times as he tried to orientate himself.

“Okay, now where would this demonic cultivator be hiding?” He mumbled to himself.

A few feet from the airlock got him next to a wall that his hand waved over to reveal a map of the ship; it wasn't detailed, just a guest map to help him find his way.

He ran his finger following a path, he had quickly figured out where the bridge should be based on what was and wasn't on the map, as well as the decades of experience he had navigating other peoples ships during the course of his work for the sect.

“Her quarters should be here, that’s normally where you would find the Commander's Quarters on a sect ship like this one.” He muttered as he memorised the path. He turned and walked away from the map, following the path.

He walked calmly and acted like he was supposed to be on this ship, even managing to convince a few outer sect disciples that he was meant to be, with his sect token hanging from his belt.

He glanced down the hallway every now and then as he approached the commander's quarters entrance, making sure no one was in the hallway when he knelt down and began to access the terminal to open the door for him.

He winced when he found the damn thing's security was a little tighter than normal, someone had tossed a formation trap on it. He managed to get past it and open the door, though, luckily without setting off any alarms… or the trap.

He eased into the room, letting out a slow breath when he found it empty, and slowly began to search. He made to walk silently by placing the outside edge of his foot down first, near his little toe. He then slowly rolled inward across the ball of his foot, before finally lowering his heel last. Using this technique to walk silently around the room.

He finally found his target when he opened the cultivation room door, and found her sitting cross-legged on a black mat. She was wearing a black robe, her hair swaying slightly in the non-existent wind, as she cultivated Qi. What made him pause was not that she had her eyes closed even though the power flowing off her should have alerted her to his presence the moment he entered her quarters, no matter how silently he moved, or tried to hide his presence. It was her fingers, crossed together in her lap, it looked like her index fingers on both hands were joined together.

“Time dilation,” he whispered in surprise.

His eyes glanced around the room, not finding any formations that could get him trapped in the dilation, before stopping back on her and realising that it was her who was creating the dilation.

However, before he could make any decision on what he should do next, her eyes had snapped open and were staring at him. ‘Crap, she heard me speak, a rookie mistake’ he thought as he chastised himself.

I stared at the man standing in my doorway slightly crouched, covered in black robes, he looked like a ninja with his face covered. However, I could see the green eyes staring at me. The wrinkles around his eyes told me he was old, most likely near the end of his lifespan.

I let out a sigh as I stopped cycling my Qi, having luckily just finished solidifying my gains from reaching the new cultivation realm. I was about to stand up and throw some Qi at him to blow him out of the room when he spoke first.

“You get two words to name your cultivation Master. Know that if you use more words I’ll cut you down, as I have been ordered by a mission from our sect.”

“Michael Jamerson” I replied, even pausing for his eyes to widen before asking a question of my own, “what is this about, and what do you mean ordered by a mission from our sect?”

I saw him blanch and even took a step back as I asked the question, only to relax slightly when I had asked it. He then let out a slow breath before stepping forward.

“I am Edgar Dirk of the punishment pavilion, assassins division. We received a report and request from an influential source with strong ties to the sect. They claimed you betrayed the sect and fled to this dimension, kidnapping all the sect members aboard your ship.”

“And you are telling me this to… what? Justify yourself, coerce an answer or confession from me? Why are you telling me this?” I snapped at him.

“Because, I did a little research into them before accepting the mission, their scion is the second disciple of Ancestor Michael Jamerson.”

I let out a sigh as realisation dawned on me without him having to spell it out, but I still felt the need to say it for him to confirm. I looked up at him, feeling a little exhausted by this experience.

“And by having me killed, their scion becomes the most likely to inherit whatever” I said as I waved my hand dismissively, before adding sadly, “and I don't have any backing for repercussions."

“That was my conclusion, after seeing you cultivating, and then finding out who your Master is.”

“What a mess… So, old man. What are you going to do now?”

“Suggest you move your ship to a new star system, then do it again a few more times, add formation defences and inform your captain of the sects' assassination mission. As for me? I'm going to find a nice room and cultivate while I wait for the mission to be cancelled. While I do that, I will also do my best to protect you. Because, I am certain your Master will wipe anyone who even glanced at this mission out if you are killed.”

I let out another sigh as I closed my eyes, trying to make peace with the fact that assassins were after me now. It wasn't easy, and when I opened my eyes, it wasn't because I had made peace with it. It was because I couldn't, and I didn’t really feel comfortable with one staring at me while I tried.

“Do you need guidance or something, or can you do it on your own?” I asked him.

A second later, he snatched his sect token from his belt and tossed it to me with a wry smile. “I need you to approve my tokens transfer to your peak, commander.” He replied.

I glanced down at his token, surprisingly no sooner had I thought I wanted to approve him, the formation on the token lit up, and the words changed to display he was a member of the Young Blood Mountain Peak.

Shrugging, I tossed the token back to him without saying a word. He grinned at me as he caught it and vanished out of my cultivation room, almost skipping. I shook my head, trying not to laugh at his antics.
