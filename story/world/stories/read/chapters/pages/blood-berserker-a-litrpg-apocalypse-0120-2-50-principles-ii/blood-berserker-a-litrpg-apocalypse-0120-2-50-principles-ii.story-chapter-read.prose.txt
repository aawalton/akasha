[Congratulations! You have gained Aura.

All Entities within 5m of you will now feel the weight of your presence.

Aura Level 1.]

The system notification mirrored exactly what the demon prince had just said. But it didn’t mean much to the berserker who was finally face-to-face with the source of all his troubles. The berserker was itching to put the demon in the ground and be done with this.

Nathan dismissed the system notification with a thought, then turned his full attention to the demon.

Both sides sized each other up, and while Nathan couldn’t read minds, he could definitely tell by the way that the demon was looking at him that it was not impressed even the slightest by him.

Its condescending gaze seemed to rekindle the berserker's lost long rage, but Nathan didn’t let the emotion consume him as he focused on his foe. Doing his best to break the cliche of berserkers being mindless, as he tried to dissect his opponent, but the demon prince's expression was a mix between nonchalant and condescending.

It was almost as though the demon was stuck between deciding if it should care or be insulted that the pesky human before it believed it had a chance at fighting him, a whole demon prince in his own stronghold.

But what the demon prince couldn’t understand was that Nathan had been laced with a touch of madness since the apocalypse had begun. The berserker had grown an appetite for fighting monsters that were far above him, and the demon was just another one of those.

Nathan didn’t mind that the demon easily dwarfed him with its massive frame, and neither did he care that the demon had almost ripped his arms apart mere moments ago.

It was all irrelevant, and nothing mattered more to him than having this condescending demon dead at his feet, as it paid for all the lives it had ruined by offering power to those gullible enough to seek it.

With his heart beating hard against his chest from the mental image of the dead demon prince, he charged straight at Kabash, Oblivion rising and blurring as it arced in a vicious swing aimed at the demon's neck, only to be stopped as a heavy aura descended upon him.

Nathan gritted his teeth as his aura exploded out of his body. It felt like he’d grown another layer of skin that encompassed his body and 5m around him.

The berserker's brain seemed to be overloaded by a wave of new information, as he received feedback from his aura.

He could feel every grain of sand within the space of his aura, and he could feel that of the demon pressing up against the edges of his own. The two auras fought for dominance.

Nathan quickly pushed the sensory overload to the back of his mind, opting to press his attack as he pushed the sword closer to the demon's neck, even as the demon's aura bore down on his own, preventing the sword from reaching its intended target.

The demon remained unperturbed, staring down at Nathan with its face still holding a look of barely disguised disgust. "You get a puny aura, and for some reason, you think you can go toe-to-toe with me. Pathetic."

The demon prince gripped Oblivion's black blade with one clawed hand and used his other forearm to backhand the berserker, ragdolling him across the clearing.

Kabash wrapped his fingers around Oblivion's hilt even as Nathan continued to bounce, until he eventually came to a stop.

[Warning: 75% health remaining]

"You pathetic inhabitants of this planet are all fools, thinking you can wield a sword forged by me in the deepest recesses of hell,” Kabash said, venom lacing each word. “You are all unworthy."

"Especially that pest, Sparrow, a nothing fool who sacrificed his own soul and the souls of many just for me to grant him a crumb of power. And still, he died, killed by someone just as unworthy and pathetic.” The demon prince’s expression morphed into one of serenity as he raised Oblivion to its eye level, staring into the abyss that was the black blade. “Like you."

Kabash seemed to have lost himself as he surveyed the blade, just as a sculptor would observe a finished piece. It was a solemn moment for the demon prince, a clear contrast to the berserker, who dismissed the system notification, twisting to the side, and coughing up blood as his body reacted to the demon's attack.

Every part of Nathan's body ached as he pushed himself off the ground, his bones creaking in ways they shouldn't be. "I'm going to kill you just like I killed him."

His word seemed to be enough to snap the demon’s attention from the sword as Kabash allowed Oblivion to drop to his side, its gaze focusing on Nathan.

The demon chuckled dismissively, an odd sound from his monstrous face. "Feel free to try. But before you do, I would like to give my thanks to you and that fool, Sparrow, for feeding my blade souls for me to feast upon once we are reunited." The demon prince raised Oblivion to his face once again, his eyes now fixed on the black blade with undisguised hunger.

Shit!

Nathan wasn’t surprised that the demon was the owner of the black blade; he put that together a while back.

What did surprise him was the fact that the demon claimed that it could feast on the souls within oblivion.

That piece of information troubled Nathan a lot.

It can’t be.

The berserker had assumed that the blade used the souls it consumed to advance itself, but the demon prince who was the creator of the blade had just broken that illusion by claiming that the sword was a sort of battery that was used to store souls that Kabash could consume.

Nathan balled his fists and, as he rushed towards the demon prince, his eyes narrowing in frustration as he saw veins rapidly spread across the demon's wrist and up through its body, the demon drawing out the souls that Oblivion had consumed.

Its muscles bulged, and its already massive frame grew slightly larger. The feasting process had been instantaneous.

"You don't know what it's like to feed directly on the souls of demons. It is degrading beyond anything you could ever imagine. But you have fed my blade with so many souls." The demon fixed a hard stare at Nathan, a chilly smile spreading across its grotesque face. "This is a feast worthy of me and—"

Nathan did not let the demon finish. He closed the distance between the pair and swung his right fist at the demon's torso. His fist stopped dead in the air as the demon stared down at him, unimpressed, his aura locking Nathan in place.

The berserker's aura pushed against that of the demon as it sought to free him of the aura that kept him in place. His aura was having as much success as a newborn cub trying to pull free of its mother's jaw.

Its failure to break him out of the demon's aura did not mean that his aura was entirely useless.

The berserker barely felt the weight of the demon prince’s aura, even though he knew it was there, and that wasn’t the only thing, as Nathan noticed that the wave of negative emotions that had tried to corrupt him the first time they had met did not assault him this time. His own aura seemed to be shielding him from the worst of the demon's pressure. Even so, every millisecond gained him only an inch—nowhere near fast enough to do real damage.

He swung his left fist in an uppercut—stretching his muscles to the max even as he ignored the pain of his battered body—but that too was held in place as both red and black auras fought for dominance—with Nathan clearly on the losing side.

The demon prince was clearly the clear favorite for the fight, but Nathan had been an underdog before.

Sure, it had been a while back, since he’d met a monster—apart from the demon prince—that had pushed him to his max and even beyond that, but Nathan wasn’t a stranger to the underdog status, and he knew that if he just had enough heart, he wouldn’t lose. And now more than ever, he couldn’t afford to.

"You could be a valuable pawn for hell.” The demon tilted its head downwards and locked eyes with Nathan. ”Already, you have done more for me than any demon under my command has managed in decades.”

“Accept your place as my underling, and I will forge you anew. I will make you stronger, faster, better, and you will be a valuable member of hell."

Is this demon... trying to recruit me? And to hell? Nathan wondered as he strained against the demon's aura.

Nathan briefly wondered what it would be like, as a human walking through hell serving demons; his mind also registered the fact that the demon had claimed it would mold him. He couldn’t help but worry that the demon prince would actually try to turn him into an actual demon.

It certainly seemed like it, and with demons, things were rarely as straightforward as they sounded.

The berserker couldn’t help but squeeze his face at the thought of his body being turned into a demon. Count me out.

"Not on your life," Nathan spat.

"Very well."

The demon prince moved so fast that Nathan didn’t register that he had been kicked in his midsection until he was ragdolled again, flying deeper into the clearing than he had the last time.

Unfortunately for Nathan, the demon seemed to have given up on trying to recruit him, as it now charged at him—rather than giving the berserker the chance to recover as it had done before.

The demon stomped forward, each footstep sending powerful shockwaves through the earth and into Nathan's body, causing the berserker to scream in pain—his broken ribs protesting the shockwave rattling his body.

Nathan's eyes widened as the demon brought its foot down on the head of one of the priests, crushing his skull without breaking stride as it continued its charge. The demon didn't even seem bothered by the brain matter coating its left leg.

The berserker steeled himself. There was nothing he could have done for that person, but there were others still barely alive that he could save. He just had to get his act together and fight.

He summoned King's Wrath into his free hand, fingers wrapping tightly around the ruby blade, and pushed off the ground as fast as he could to meet the demon prince head-on.

The demon swung Oblivion the moment Nathan came within range. Nathan brought up King's Wrath and narrowly blocked the strike aimed at his head.

It became a battle of strength—the demon pressing Oblivion down with crushing force while Nathan held on with both hands, doing everything he could not to be skewered.

The demon was using only one hand to keep Nathan fully occupied, while Nathan needed both of his just to survive. And yet he was being driven deeper into the earth with every passing second.

[Hidden Mind (I)] triggered.

The demon had attempted to mind-control him again, but the skill deflected the mental assault even as the physical pressure continued.

It was a testament to the demon's raw strength that it was holding Nathan at bay with one hand. And just when Nathan had reached his absolute limit, straining to keep Oblivion from driving him into the ground, the demon raised its other massive fist and hammered down on the blade, knocking Nathan face-first into the dirt.

The berserker tried to scramble up, but before he could even push off the ground, he saw a massive foot descending toward his face. His heart beat faster against his chest as he realized that the demon planned to crush his skull the same way it had crushed the priest's.

Left with no other choice, Nathan activated his skill. [Dissipate (I)]
