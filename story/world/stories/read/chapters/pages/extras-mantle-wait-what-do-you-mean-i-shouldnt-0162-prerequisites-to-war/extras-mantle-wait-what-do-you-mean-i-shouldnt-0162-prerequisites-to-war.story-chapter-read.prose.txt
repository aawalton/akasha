As Book II—which I’ll admit has been running on for a long time now, which is entirely my fault. Poor planning and other pressing projects took my time. More about that later; for now—and we approach the epic climax, the war.

The city of Vienna is poised on the brink of total collapse as they have finally run out of time.

#1) The war and why it is happening.

The cult is using Vienna as one of the necessary sacrifices to conduct a ritual on a very grand scale. This ritual feeds on the souls of all those sacrificed to allow the incarnation of “The Darkened One” to walk onto the world of Eldamar IX. The reason why Vienna is that of the bastion that is present under the city of Vienna.

When all five continental ritual circles align and link deep through essence conduits, they will form a single ritual circle spanning the entire globe.

The convergence reaches critical saturation, which turns the very air thick with miasma or corrupted essence, which will kill anyone not protected with an aura. As of Chapter 158, we are at T-minus 1 days to D-Day.

It’s safe to say failure in this war will lead to a total wipeout of the city and its population.

#2) THE ENEMY → The Cultist of Darkened One: Ego, Ambition, & Puppet Strings

The “Cult of the Darkened One” is massive, with various clans, factions, and powers, each interpreting the powers of the Darkened One in their own capacity. The cult faction that took over Vienna belongs to the Vasht clan, which focuses on darkness, corruption, rituals, and sorcery as their focus.

SANGVATH ANOR is a pseudonym of Malachar. This faction, while appears to be a cult—and is outlawed and declared as terrorist—isn’t actually a cult. There are only limited members in the SANGVATH ANOR and each of them bears Malachar’s power seed and is known as a Hand. Malachar is an avatar of SANGVATH ANOR, the revenant. Just like in Vienna, we see Malachar leading alongside the cult… he is also present in another form, another avatar in each of the cities where this ritual is being carried out.

#3) HERO’S FORCES → The Survivors of Vienna.

As of CH158, the surviving people of Vienna are all holed up in the Bastion, which is an ancient architecture wonder whose true purpose is lost to time, and only a few remember the purpose.

We see hints at how dire the situation becomes—even for the Z.A.C system to acknowledge—when bastion protocols fail. We get more hints at Joe’s elusive past, and the threat of bastion failing had been put off immediate fires with close to one year to find the resolution.

The Veil and the invasion started somewhere around 70-75 days prior to CH158. And it was said the barrier lasted a total of 90 days. Technically, we still have 15 days till then, but the essence density reaches a critical threshold around the total 78-80 days mark, and so Jin had to start the war keeping that in mind. Even though he wants to finish everything in one fell swoop, that is sadly not possible; hence, the war starts on day 76.

Over the course of a couple of weeks after cleaning the bastion of cultist infiltrators, Jin had managed to establish “22 Mage Companies” totaling 2,200 sorcerers all at Base ORDER III power levels.

This is the bulk of Jin’s Army. The members of mage companies could also be termed as “One trick ponies” because the companies know one or two sorceries imprinted into them by Jin.

Mage companies 1-6, being the most experienced, have access to multiple One-verse sorceries.

This may sound mediocre, but the might have one sorcery cast by 100… or 1000 sorcerers all chained together can reach the damage potential of ORDER IV combatants in sheer raw power. They may lack quality, but Jin established these companies for the sole purpose of getting the named leads into the heart of the war, the nexus area of the ritual.

#4) THE VARIABLES

Now I’m a firm believer of “No battle plan can survive the first round of a bullet,” and in a war, the advice is doubly true. There are so many variables to account for, and “anything that can go wrong will go wrong” hence, the best our heroes can do is to adapt and take immediate sound decisions which may minimize the losses.

Once such a known variable is the presence of demons. I won’t spoil a lot but in the very start of the series, Jin recalls this:

"I can quote it word for word," Jin muttered as he searched. “The simultaneous fall of Vienna, Svethaal, Rin’ Tora, Mol’Daine, and Karash marked the beginning of the end. Five jewels of civilization, each home to hundreds of thousands of souls, simply... vanished."

"When investigation teams finally reached the sites ninety-one days later, they found only craters filled with horrors that defied classification. No survivors. No answers. Only the terrible certainty that something vast and malevolent had announced its presence to the world."

"And another report mentioned monster corpses. Thousands of them scattered throughout the ruins. But here's the thing—no survivors, yet plenty of dead monsters. That means people fought back. They just weren't strong enough or smart enough to win."

"But the big revelation came in Volume Seven's appendix—demonic essence signature detected in underground tunnels. A whole fucking compound built under the Cathedral of Prime!"

To summarize, Malachar does indeed summon demons, or rather, opens a portal to thier realm in the original timeline. He does this to muddy the waters and help the crown and other leaders deflect the blame onto demons, to push the mindset of war with demons.

So, will we get demons? Jin certainly thinks Malachar will use all the advantage he can get. And as such, he has taken some countermeasures, but “anything that can go wrong will go wrong.”
