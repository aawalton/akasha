Hair care was not among them.

Which, after nine years, she practically did.

Nine years.

Nine years since Alicia had arrived in the village with nothing but the clothes on her back. Nine years since a family that had no obligation to do so had taken in a frightened little girl and simply... decided she belonged.

A surprisingly important responsibility.

Emma immediately resumed talking.

Not speaking.

Talking.

An endless stream of stories, questions, observations, and entirely unrelated thoughts.

Emma remained completely undeterred. Years of experience had taught her that silence wasn't rejection.

It was simply Alicia.

Emma wasn't holding still. She never did.

Excellent food when cooked by someone who knew what they were doing.

Paused.

Defeated.

A comfortingly familiar one.
