Back on the rooftop, accompanied by someone with the 'Medic' class, Ashton stepped up to the tent. It seemed like the watchman was stable, but he was clearly still not doing great. The bleeding apparently stopped, though.

And while the medic checked on the watchman, Ashton focused on Millie. She was just staring at Ashton.

"Did you get a good look at everything?" he asked, squatting down in front of her. Millie shrugged lightly.

"I don't know. Maybe? The monsters all look really weird to me," Millie explained. "They don't have anything that I can 'see'... but I think maybe I was able to see them use magic? Maybe."

Ashton thought for a moment, nodding along to what Millie was saying. "I see... I guess it makes sense. The monsters should all only have artificial souls, so most probably aren't complex enough to give you something to see. I'll bring you over to a monster that can use magic sometime, so that way we can confirm if you can see anything with them at all."

Millie nodded, excited at the thought. But even so, she glanced past Ashton at the harpies laying here on the rooftop. "Uhm... so everything is okay again? Is everyone safe?"

Ashton hesitated. He wasn't sure what to say. The medic next to him tensed up, clenching their jaw.

"A few people died. More got injured," Ashton replied. That was the truth. The casualty rate for this camp had been relatively low compared to the others, but of course there were still people that passed on every day because they fell victim to the monsters.

And in an attack like this, where a whole field boss dragged a bunch of minions along, it was hard to expect nobody to die. It really wasn't that many people; all things considered, this was a successful defense battle. But as Ashton walked through the inside of the building, he could still see people grieving the ones that passed.

There were also plenty of folk that were injured, and some that would probably lose function of an arm or leg, but that was sadly the norm now. And Millie knew that better than almost anyone.

"Oh... okay," Millie slowly looked down. "Is this going to keep happening?"

Ashton slowly shook his head. "No. The next quest should start sometime tomorrow. It's a little more complicated, but once the first step is done, this area will become a 'safe zone'. Most monsters won't be able to attack as long as we keep our defences up."

"Really?"

"Really. So people will be able to stay a bit more safe in here," Ashton explained. Though, that was only part of the truth. In reality, while safe zones would be created, the world outside of those safe zones would become sharply more dangerous.

The reason why the harpies attacked was probably that they were chased out from their nest by something else, stronger, that settled down there. That being the case, Ashton would need to prepare for that.

After helping the medic carry the watchman down to the sickbay, Ashton came back upstairs and started making some food for Millie, himself, and of course also the wisp to make them last through the day.

"So, Millie... show me how far you've gotten with manipulating the magic stones," Ashton said as he stood in front of the stove, preparing some eggs.

Millie excitedly walked over to her bed and brought over the small pile of magic stones that she had kept in there.

She was decently far through the list of shapes that Ashton had given her. They weren't perfect, of course, but they were pretty good. Especially for someone who was so new to this.

"Alright, these are passable. But really try to get those points a bit 'sharper'. You see how they're still pretty rounded here?" Ashton asked, briefly turning and holding up one of the magic stones. Millie quickly nodded and started at the stone.

"Okay!"

Ashton smiled. "I've got some stuff to get ready, and then we'll head over to the university later. Just keep practicing all that for now. And then, I'll give you a task to do while we're at university, alright?" Ashton suggested. Millie's eyes widened in excitement.

"A task? What kind of task?" Millie wondered, and Ashton just grinned at her.

"You'll just have to wait and see. For now, just concentrate on the magic stones, okay? You can only really do the task once you can control your own mana well enough."

Millie excitedly nodded her head. She was so eager to get started that, even when Ashton put her breakfast down next to her, she wouldn't eat until Ashton told her to.

He was glad that she was adjusting so well, though. Plus, with her being so focused, Ashton was able to pay attention to the things that he had to get done. The first step was simple.

Ashton had to prepare some inks. Well, one of them had already been 'brewing'. He was extracting pigment from a bunch of leaves. The other was one that he could only really do now that he had the right materials.

The jewellery that Jacky gave him had plenty of precious gemstones. He only needed the metal bits to summon the Helping Hand, so he could use the stones for something else.

In particular, he was waiting for the diamonds in some of these rings. They were the perfect material to use. Ashton got out his mortar and pestle and popped the diamonds into it.

Poppy was sitting on his shoulder, curiously watching him. She had no idea what he was about to do, but Poppy seemed to like shiny things. Pixies in particular were often pretty attached to precious gems like this. So when Ashton started crushing up the diamond with a few solid slams, she was shocked.

So was Millie, actually.

"Wait, what? How are you doing that, I thought diamonds are super strong?" Millie asked, confused. She leaned forward, trying to see into the mortar.

"They're strong in some ways, but diamonds are actually shockingly brittle. And by infusing mana with the right pattern, you can increase the tension in the stone and make it easier to shatter," Ashton explained, smiling as he prepared the powdered gemstones. Now he would just have to mix this with a good binder and he'd be done with the 'Lesser Gemstone Ink'.

He would wait until he was outside to actually form the contract with that elemental, though; it was useful, but mostly in some very specific circumstances. And since gemstones were hard to get your hand on, Ashton would have to be careful until it was easier to get some more. There should be some places on the way to the university where he might be able to find gemstones in the rubble.

So for now, Ashton took the rest of the jewellery and started taking it apart. He used the salamander's help to heat up some parts of the metal to fuse it all together in the right parts, so that it wouldn't come undone, and also widened the heated-up rings so that they could fit around his fingers.

It wasn't that easy without the right tools, but knowing the right enchantments really helped. Plus, he was able to just use some magic stones as a guide. In the end, Ashton ended up with something very simple. Two 'ring gloves'. Each one had a tight chain bracelet that connected to a ring on each finger. On the back of the hand, they came together and formed a sort of 'web' in the form of a simple magic circle.

there was only one small issue with this. Each of these 'ring gloves' had five rings, but Ashton was missing his right index finger. There was a solution to fix this issue for now, but it was sort of brutal, so he went outside to take care of it, telling Millie to not come out.

When Ashton bit off his finger to avoid the zombie's bite spreading through him, he went basically all the way to the base, so there wasn't anything he could even dangle the ring off of. So, he just had to make that 'anything' himself.

Ashton prepared some potions, a knife, and a small piece of metal. It was really just a small stud that would only reach about halfway to the first finger knuckle. It was small, but enough to safely attach a ring to.

Ashton had been preparing this here and there when he had free-time, just shaping it here and there. Well, Ashton would remove this again someday once he had the help of a spirit that could actually make the things that he needed. And then at some point, he would just replace his finger with a new one through a flesh seed or something of the sort.

But right now, Ashton had to make do with what he had. And so, he took a breath and placed a dish-towel in his mouth. Even he wouldn't be able to do this without making any sound at all, and he wouldn't be able to concentrate enough to keep up the 'Silence' spell.

And so... Ashton cut into his small stub. He removed the scarred skin, and dug open a small divot, so that the metal wouldn't just be sitting on top of his skin, but was actually safely in there.

Ashton heated up the metal with the salamander's help until it was red hot, and then pressed it into the open, bleeding wound. He groaned into the towel, and then slowly held his finger into a cup of water mixed with a solid amount of the Rapid Healing potion. The hot metal and his flesh basically fused together, and the potion did the rest.

Usually, the rapid healing potion would force out certain foreign entities, like all those worms that were in Ashton's body before. But well, forcing out everything came with some complications on its own, especially if someone had prosthetics. So it generally didn't actually remove non-biological matter, instead setting it safely into place so that it wouldn't wander and could be removed surgically at a later time.

So in this case, the potion really set the metal safely into Ashton's flesh. It could be removed still, but it would probably take the same amount of force as tearing off a finger usually would. So not that much, all things considered.

But with this, Ashton could place a ring safely on top of this small metal stub. And so, he put on the two ring gloves and took a deep breath.

Now, to help speed up the process for the other contracts Ashton would form after this, it was time to draw up the contract circle for the Helping Hands spirit.
