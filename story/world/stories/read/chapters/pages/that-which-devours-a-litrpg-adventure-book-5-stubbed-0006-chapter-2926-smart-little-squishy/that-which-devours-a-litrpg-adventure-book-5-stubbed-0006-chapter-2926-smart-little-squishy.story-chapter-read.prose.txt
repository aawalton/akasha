Noseen, on the Sisters…

The preparations for war were underway.

Several of my people had been notified. All who wanted to leave this place and try to step even farther up the ladder had been given that chance. None wanted leadership, but all wanted power, more than they could get here.

All that remained was the wait.

Patience was something that came naturally to me, after all these centuries. The fact that the quest I’d given was still open, however, was a bit concerning.

The tiny bonded creature should have brought the 3 Clan members to Alex, yet only two out of three had been delivered.

Now it hung there.

A buzzing hovered in the air around me, and I quickly snapped it off when I realized I was doing it.

The downside of a quest like this was that it didn’t give me much information, if any, on its status. Just that Alex had seen them. Nothing about if they were dead and eaten.

At least Alex was much less squishy. Once they ate the other gift, they’d be so close to the next step to the stars that they could deal with those three without a problem. Especially if they were smart.

I resisted interfering. Already, the quest pushed the boundaries of what I should do, according to the unspoken rules of propriety that our kind chose to adopt. Then again, if everything were to line up perfectly…

I fluttered my wings as I continued to my home. The hot air rushed over me as I traveled through the dark sunlight toward the massive tree that was mine.

All mine.

None dare trespass into my domain.

Alex would be fine.

An icy coldness crept along the touch I had that connected me to Alex. It made my wings tremble, then I sped up, landing on the building carved into the trunk of the tree. Once stable, I traced the line.

They were still on the world of the Knowledge Bearer. One I dare not cross into uninvited.

The cold didn’t stop. A shiver wracked my body, then the connection snapped. The mark under their ear was just… gone.

“Alex?” I whispered into my home, turning to look at the sun. “What did you do?”

Alex, in the white room…

The duplicate across from me smiled as I froze, wondering how to respond to the taunt about succeeding at my own trial. Before I came up with anything reasonable, the room dissolved into nothingness. For a moment, an icy wave washed over every inch of me, and sunk in deep.

The cold snuffed out the hot mana inside my chest and the rest of my body, leaving my stomach rumbling, and my hands shaking.

The nothingness shifted as I landed in the center of a very familiar place.

Lakeside Landing.

The sky turned a violent purple, with a jagged line crossing the clouds pulsing with energy. Winds ripped across the buildings, sending people flying. The storm doors to the cavern weren’t closed, and two people struggled to get one side into place.

All at once, everyone I knew appeared.

Lenna was standing tall on the top of a building, firing arrows at massive growling creatures tearing into the fence.

Abby was holding back waves of creatures crawling up the path from the lake.

Dengu and Strange worked together with Grizzle to take down two massive T-Rex like creatures that came from the east.

My father worked with Hawk to close the storm shelter, as another ripple of bright purple streaked across the sky. People ran from every direction, heading to the cavern for safety.

My stomach grumbled as Doc rushed by, and my mouth watered, smelling something smoky.

No instructions appeared as my body spasmed.

I tried to trigger my inventory to reach my stack of meat, but nothing appeared. Hunger rippled through me again, as the edges of my eyesight dimmed.

“Hold the line!” A groan rose from Abby as her shields flickered, holding back the water creatures.

[Pack of Shoal Grunner, Mana Sense, Level 130, Predator, Unknown.]

The creatures were some sort of cross between a river otter and a lizard, with powerful shoulders, a massive jaw, and a fin jutting upward. Webbed claws from one of the larger creatures tried to tear at the barriers Abby held, while a long tail knocked into smaller creatures behind them.

My father watched Abby struggle, but stayed with Hawk, yanking the first side of the shelter into place. They both rushed to the other side.

A copper scent rose from Abby as the edges of her barrier flicker. I reacted before I truly thought about it.

I launched myself at the pack of Shoal Grunners. At least 8 of them clawed at the barrier now. By the time I reached them, I’d shifted into my Shadowstalker form. My claws tore the first to shreds, and I crunched down on another, tearing off a chunk of its shoulder in my mouth. I swallowed.

A fishy flavor coated my tongue, but I kept biting and tearing. More than half of them tried to gang up on me, but I rolled away, closer to the water. The first carcass lay too far behind to dig into, so I tore into the live one trying to chomp on me.

Its teeth skittered on my armor, but my claws dug into its chest. It froze as I dug in deep, before finding a small round object and yanking it out. Nothing sounded inside my head. I tossed the prize in my mouth as another creature slammed into my back.

Energy flooded my body as I stumbled, but then straightened. One by one, I took care of the pack.

Abby retreated to the main plaza and reinforced Lenna, who still held back a force from the east.

A pulse inside my head sent me to my knees. Mana blazed from the tear in the sky, flooding all over the area. Everything it touched paused just for a second, then changed.

The first wave hit one of the T-Rexes that Strange and Dengu were harassing.. The purple light caused it to freeze, as feathers rapidly appeared then shifted into spikes.

Both Dengu and Strange backed away as it suddenly roared louder than before.

A buzzing filled the air as the shuttle appeared and fired at the now stronger T-Rex. The giant predator staggered back, but another wave of purple mana washed out of the tear. It slammed into the creature again, making it grow even larger.

A massive beam of light cut across it from the shuttle, removing a hunk of the enormous beast’s tail and shoulder. But then the shuttle flew away, leaving the rest for Dengu and Strange.

The tear.

My eyes locked onto it.

Everyone else could deal with the creatures. The tear, though, they couldn’t do anything about.

The surface from the lake rippled as something rose from the depths.

[Shoal Grunner, Mana Sense, Level 190, Predator, Fishy.]

Its powerful jaw opened, pointing at the tear in the sky. A growl escaped its lips, as it crawled onto the rocky shore. Its eyes stayed on the tear, which hovered over Lakeside landing.

People froze in terror in the plaza.

I triggered Sovereign's Call as I raced toward the doors to the cavern. Everyone who had frozen in fear turned in my direction and followed. My energy lagged a little, but it was worth it as it cleared the plaza, leaving only the fighters behind.

The Shoal Grunner growled in my direction, and lunged. Teeth snapped at me. I jumped up and landed on its enormous head, near its sharp fin. I dug in with my claws, and three massive scales went flying.

It suddenly rolled, and I barely dodged out of the way. Then its tail slammed into me, sending me across the plaza into the side of a building.

Three rapid-fire shots came from my father, who stood in the much smaller opening to the cavern. All that remained was enough opening in one door for my father to stand, his rifle to his shoulder.

“Retreat!” he yelled. His voice called out over the area, but no one moved from their spots, even as the clouds darkened farther overhead.

The distraction gave me a moment to catch my breath, before I launched myself at the side of the Shoal Grunner, who now glared at my father. I slammed into its head, claws aiming for its kinda-cute eye. It popped, making me shiver.

The Grunner roared in pain, as its tail flailed about and it tried to claw at me. I danced back as it twisted to claw in my direction again. The loss of the eye didn’t slow it down much. It was clearly still able to target me, which sucked. Especially as a massive clawed hand slammed into my side.

I skidded back as pain rippled across my hips, then launched myself up again, aiming for the area where I’d already broken through its scales on its head.

My Dad fired another three shots, which distracted the creature as I dug down behind its fin. It wiggled back and forth, but when it started to roll, Dad lunged forward. The distraction worked, and the Grunner went to grab him instead of dislodging me.

I missed my spear as I tore deeper into the creature, before it finally shuddered to a halt. I shoved a few chunks into my mouth, but didn’t stop to talk to my father.

The tear needed to be dealt with. Who knew what else would show up, drawn to the mass of mana pouring out of the sky?

I sprinted to the edge of a building and leaped, landing up on the roof near Lenna. Her arrows didn’t stop, and, with Abby helping her, she made progress on the stampede of creatures from the East. People still rushed into the cavern opening from all directions. Hawk and my Dad focused on getting the second barrier in place, but it was a struggle with the winds.

“Abby, I need a boost!” I pointed up to the tear.

Her eyes went wide, but she nodded. A small barrier appeared, horizontal, about halfway up in the air.

I leaped and landed on it with a manic grin.

The winds up here were worse, and I almost lost my footing, but then I caught myself.

The mana funneling out of the tear saturated the air up here, and it felt more like a higher level dungeon.

Then I inhaled.

Like before, I gorged on the mana itself, focusing on pulling it away from everyone else. The tear needed to close, though. Runes flickered through my mind as I tried to find one for a seal. The best I had was a lock.

The barrier under my feet shuddered and I jumped up, aiming to get as close to the tear as I could.

To my shock, I passed through it, stumbling into a higher-concentration mana area. My feet hit a darkened ground, with the tear now rippling behind me.

Another gush of mana started to spill across me, but I inhaled as deeply as possible, trying to eat as much of it as I could. The less that spilled out down below, the better.

Energy blazed inside me as I studied the edges of the tear, trying to figure out how to close it. The area around me felt like a different world. The ground stretched out as far as I could see in all directions, with spires of rocks and things shaped like trees scattered throughout.

Light blazed from an orb in the sky.

I traced the lock rune, using some of the mana from my core, on the edges of the tear. It glimmered in the air but did nothing. Only one edge caught, and the other flapped free before the rune dissolved.

Bigger. It needed to be bigger.

This time, I sketched the rune out bigger than me with the glowing tip of one of my claws. Touching from one edge to another, and funneling as much mana from my core as I could, the lock rune hovered in the air. It shimmered brightly before the edges pulled closer together, slowly sealing the tear closed.

I was on the wrong side.

I twisted and ran, trying to reach the open area before it closed, but didn’t make it as the rip sealed shut.

My radar suddenly went wild as something massive appeared in the distance.

Two large orbs hovered in the air, then vanished, as something blinked in the deep purple light. Large yellow teeth flashed as a mouth opened.

Well, fuck.
