Chapter 203 / B3-48: Shadowed Shop

Joe had no idea where to go to buy an athame, but he did have a good idea where he could start looking. He and Mojo headed down Skill Street to the Crafters Market, looking for a certain dunian merchant. Chanaquoth’s booth had shifted to a slightly different location, a better one in Joe’s opinion, since it would receive more shade from one of the few palm trees left in the open area of the bazaar.

“Joe the healer! Welcome back to Chanaquoth’s Bountiful Boutique,” the large merchant exclaimed.

“Bountiful Boutique? Is that new? I don’t remember you dropping that name last time I was here.”

“I’m trying it out. Kemsado used to run a stall called the Bountiful Boutique, but she changed it to the Serene Disguise. She let me have the name for a bottle of six-year-old Desert Puryonac. What do you think?”

“It’s pretty good,” Joe admitted, rocking his head from side to side. “Though given how diverse your magic item selection is, I might have gone with something using emporium, instead of boutique.”

“Ooh. I like that better. I was thinking I was a bit too burly for a boutique,” he replied, flexing his broad shoulders. “So? What can I do you for, Joe?”

“Do you sell athames? You know the ritual daggers?”

“Is this for some minor incantation, such as [Summon Familiar] or [Restful Slumber]? Or are we talking about rituals of a higher order?”

“Definitely higher order. I just got a skill for curse-breaking and have a doozy of a curse I’m trying to crack.”

Chanaquoth dropped his jovial smile as he ruminated on Joe’s request, before leaning forward and answering in a quiet tone. “Now, don’t get me wrong. I sell quality goods, but for something like that, you don't want just ‘good’ quality. If you’re working with complex rituals, you want the best you can get. I have a few athames that are of middling grade, which are fine for everyday invocations, but I can't in good conscience recommend them for something complex. Have you ever been to Four Rod Road?”

“Nope,” Joe answered.

The chocolate-skinned dunian pointed southwest, into the South Wall district. “About three blocks, thataway. It’s a street of mage shops. At the end of the road is the place I go to when any of my tradegoods have a curse. It’s called Ashen Offerings. The place is a bit creepy-looking on the outside, but trust me, it's where you want to go, Joe.”

“Thanks, Chanaquoth. You’re giving up a sale? Just like that?”

“Ah. You’ll be back. Or maybe you want a luck charm before you go. Anyone playing around with curses can use all the luck they can get.”

“Yeah! Good idea. Give me two.”

With a large smile, the dunian plucked a pair of necklaces with a miniature whistle hanging on each of the braided cords. Joe forked over the minor fee and identified them.

[Flukish Flute] (Item: Consumable - Uncommon) For the next 10 minutes after a melody is played on this pipe, the bearer will have an elevated {Luck} affinity. Single Use.

He looped both necklaces around his neck and then quickly tucked the tiny fifes under his shirt as Mojo tried to get his paws on them. The {Air} aligned shug-monkey was very intrigued by the small wind instruments and seemed intent on setting off the charms immediately. The pup’s pleading face was unbearably cute.

Even knowing he would almost surely regret giving in, Joe bought another enchanted whistle for the little man to play with. Chanaquoth promised the enchantment would guide the broodling towards pleasant music. Joe had his doubts, expecting a cacophony of random bleating notes.

Surprisingly, the first few sounds the changeling produced from the wooden penny-whistle were actually quite nice. Mojo’s eyes opened wide, and he ruffed excitedly. Decorum flew out the window as the goofball took off running a lap around the Crafter’s Market, tooting his heart out. To the item’s credit, even the most blaring notes were not nearly as annoying as Joe expected they would have been if Mojo had a mundane instrument.

Joe eventually caught the little lunatic and convinced him to ease up on the music a bit. They walked southwest with a consistent trill of pipes coming from the happy critter trotting along at Joe’s side, but Mo was smart enough to keep his toots soft and low, lest he lose the fife altogether. When the shug-monkey couldn’t contain himself any longer, he’d dash far enough away from Joe to not deafen his caretaker and play his heart out on his new toy. He usually managed to find kids to play for, and the little showoff was having fun hamming it up for whoever he stopped to serenade.

Joe was fairly impressed. In the matter of just the few blocks it took them to reach Four Rod Road, he could already tell that Mojo’s playing was getting better. While he knew the broodling was far more clever than his goofy appearance suggested, he was pretty sure there were also some affinities at play. {Air} for sure, but it was more than that. Joe was sure that there had to be a {Music} affinity, probably under the base affinity of {Sound}. Scooping up the pup, he tried to guess what the other half might be.

The lane in front of Joe and Mojo was a wonder. For starters, it was completely shaded by magically shaped trees that reminded Joe of rainbow eucalyptus. The bark had the same multihued patterning and thick, dark green leaves, but the foliage looked more like heavy, waxy fronds than the teardrop leaves of a eucalyptus tree. The trunks and branches had to have been manipulated by {Wood} magic. The limbs all uniformly arced out over the road and interwove themself with those of their neighbors across the street.

A countless number of faces were not carved out of the wood so much as grown and given animation. Everywhere Joe looked, a colorfully barked countenance was looking at him or the other streetgoers. These faces smiled and winked. He passed a lion face that was delighting a number of children by silently roaring at them.

The air was full of fey lights, flittering illusions, and birds unlike any Joe had ever seen before.

The first shops looked more like magic toy stores than a place for wizardry, causing Joe to wonder if he really was on the right street until he glanced down the lane and saw what had to be his destination. At the end of the cul-de-sac, just beyond all the color and glamour, lurked a dark edifice, coiled with twisting shadow and an eerie mien. Yet as Joe approached, he could see that the shop mostly looked exceedingly creepy due to the mirthful nature of its neighbors. The closer he and Mojo got, the more Joe recognized its artful illusion of the macabre. Like a haunted house at a carnival, or the touristy voodoo shops around Bourbon Street, this shop was playing at being ominous. It was not a place that people would shun; it was one that folks would sneak into to enjoy being spooked.

As Joe entered the Ashen Offerings, his guesses were immediately confirmed. A skeleton, dressed in formal attire, was standing behind the counter. It bore a curling handlebar mustache between its empty nose hole and its exposed teeth. A top hat adorned its bare skull. On its shoulder, a small imp was speaking for the voiceless cashier. He spotted two other skeletal clerks with demonling companions assisting customers in the vast store. Once again, Joe found himself in a location where the inside was bigger than the outside.

When one of the pairs finished with the patron they were helping, the duo stepped over to Joe. As it approached, his nose picked up a slight whiff of brimstone. Not enough to overwhelm his hyperactive sense of smell, but it confirmed that the little fiend riding on the skeletal clerk’s shoulder was a real imp and not an illusion. While the small wafts of the demonic made Joe a little edgy, the tiny clerks must be very minor summonings, since he could pretty easily push past it.

“Welcome to the Ashen Offerings, sir,” the imp intoned in a voice too small to pull off the ominous tone it was attempting. “How may we serve you?”

“A merchant named Chanaquoth, from the Crafter’s Market, recommended this was someplace I could get a high-quality athame.”

“Oh. Very good,” the tiny demon exclaimed, its voice dropping the act. “Follow me to the back. This is the fun side of the store. The real stuff is this way.”

He followed his bony guide deeper through the shelves and up a flight of steps. They passed through a swinging gate at the top of the stairs, and the shop took on a far more practical appearance. Bookshelves lined the outer walls on both sides of the area, with glass-enclosed display tables filling the middle of the room. The demonling and skeleton headed for the first one on the right, which displayed dozens of black-handled blades. Some were straight, others curvy, but what they all lacked were sharp edges. It was clear these ritualistic knives were not designed for cutting, or at least cutting anything physical.

Joe looked into the case, reflexively using [Identify] on the closest dagger within it.

[Moonveil Athame] (Item: Tool - Uncommon) This implement glows in moonlight. The level of lunar luminance indicates the degree to which your rituals will be enhanced. This blade was designed to enhance divination magic, granting visions with greater clarity and reducing the chance of misinterpretation. {Divination} ‡ 335 g ‡

He was surprised to note that a price had been tacked onto his identification. The ‘g’ had to mean gold. Joe scanned the case with [Witchsight] and found plenty of security wards, as he expected. Additionally, etched into the edge of the glass was an information spell that explained how a pricetag was added to his notification window.

[Bloodscribe Blade] (Item: Tool - Uncommon) This thin, crimson-stained athame drinks a small portion of the wielder’s blood during rituals. In return, it amplifies binding spells, making summoned entities far more obedient and harder to dismiss. {Summoning} ‡ 410 g ‡

[Ashen Covenant Athame] (Item: Tool - Rare) Forged with the cremated remains of Sir Deskin Rawl’s ashes fused into obsidian glass, this blade gives a Median strengthening to pact magic. Contracts made with it are magically enforced, punishing oathbreakers with curses. {Summoning} ‡ 1240 g ‡

[Verdant Rite Dagger] (Item: Tool - Uncommon) With its handle wrapped in living [Black Twiner] vines, this spell focus has Major resistance against {Disarm} effects. Nature-based rituals performed with it gain a Moderate increase in potency, and living plants used in rituals with the athame gain a Median growth acceleration for the duration of the ritual. {Summoning} ‡ 380 g ‡

[Mirrorbound Athame] (Item: Tool - Rare) Crafted using waters from the [Well of Mayz], this blade reflects not the present, but possible futures. When used in scrying rituals, it enhances one’s {Divination} affinity and reveals glimpses of alternate outcomes, though not always the most likely one. {Divination} ‡ 1800 g ‡

[Gravecaller's Edge] (Item: Tool - Uncommon) Cold to the touch, this bone-handled knife enhances necromantic rites. Ancestral spirits summoned with it are more cognizant, with a greater capacity for speech. {Summoning} ‡ 580 g ‡

Having identified just the few ritual blades nearest to him, Joe realized that his search might take longer than he had expected. Only one of the half dozen implements mentioned curses, the Ashen Covenant Athame, and even then, it was not referring to what he was looking for.

“Is there a way to sort these cases for just athames that help with casting and breaking curses? Oh, and warding too?”

“You must be thinking of selection boards. We have a different method,” the tiny imp replied. The skeleton touched the showcase in front of Joe and then reached over to lay its fleshless fingers on the next closest display case, which was also filled with even more of the black handled blades. A glow blossomed around roughly three dozen of the casting tools. When Joe focused on just the shimmering athames, he could see that each one of them was crafted to assist with curse or ward crafting.

He asked the paired clerk to limit the list a few more times. Many were combat-focused items, designed to do what [Curse Cleaver] already did for Joe, disrupt active incoming curses. He also removed some of the really vicious curse-crafting blades. He’d be happy to find an athame that boosted his lesser hexes, but he was not looking for a ritual dagger that was designed to fashion horrific maledictions.

His eyes were drawn to some of the more expensive items, but one of the good things about this shopping trip was that Joe did not have to sweat the cost of this purchase. Not only did he still have about two thousand gold from the [Bring Him Home] quest, but he had also received numerous gifts while attending the Jarlscarp ball.

All throughout the evening, well-dressed members of Fort Coral’s upper crust had come up to Joe, shaken his hand, expressed their gratitude for rescuing the legendary founder, and handed him an envelope. These envelopes had amounts written in them. Joe just had to give his consent, and that amount of gold was sent to his new dim-cuff. The lowest amount was two hundred gold, the highest was two thousand from the head of the Jarlscarp clan himself. All totaled, Joe had earned almost twice as much in gratitude coinage as he had gotten from the quest itself.

Knowing he could afford to splurge, Joe ended up buying three of the ritualistic spell instruments.

[Witchbane Thorn] (Item: Tool - Rare) This vine-etched athame was crafted within the Veil, the barrier between Illuminaria and the Feylands. It provides a Major enhancement to the disruption of curses cast by hags and witches, and a Median enhancement to those created by any other type of fey. It is especially effective with {Wood} and {Beast}-based enchantments. {Curse} ‡ 940 g ‡

[Malison Needle Athame] (Item: Tool - Rare) Thin as a stiletto, this athame is used for precision ritual work. It excels at isolating and removing malevolent constructs without disturbing adjacent beneficial enchantments. {Expertise} ‡ 1325 g ‡

[Worldroot Blade] (Item: Tool - Uncommon) A heavy, ancient athame tied to primal forces. When creating formation magic, it can draw energy from ley lines to empower the crafting. When dissipating malific formation magic, it draws the cursed energy down into the ground, dispersing it through ley lines where it loses all potency. {Root} ‡ 680 g ‡

The [Witchbane Thorn] was exactly what Joe was looking for, and thanks to it being such a narrowly focused tool, it was cheaper than one would expect given the bonuses it offered. The needle would be excellent for delicate work. The [Worldroot Blade] he planned to use more for warding than he did for curses.

Ready to get to work, Joe paid and headed for home.
