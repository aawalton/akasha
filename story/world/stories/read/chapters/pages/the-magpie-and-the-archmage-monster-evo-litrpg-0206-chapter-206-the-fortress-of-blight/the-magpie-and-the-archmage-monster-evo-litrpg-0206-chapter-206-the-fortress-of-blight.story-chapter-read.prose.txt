"Okay, but like how do we get there? I doubt it's as easy as just flying up there. Otherwise, I'm pretty sure that those dragons would have razed that place to the ground by now," Cee whispered as Liora observed the natural barricade from a safe distance.

She could see waves of mana circling the peak. It was a strange deep purple, almost black color, and unlike most magic, it didn't have that bright sparkle. Liora suspected that this was gravity magic. She did see one of the fungoraks being flung away, so it would undoubtedly affect Liora too.

Had Liora not witnessed the dragon's might with her own eyes, she likely would have flown recklessly at the place, relying on her superior flying skill and Tempest Wings to get her through. But the emerald dragon looked very mindful of the peak. It didn't fly past a certain point while chasing fungoraks, gobbling them up one after another.

So whatever protected the peak was strong enough to keep a dragon at bay.

"I have an idea of how I could get through, but then you'd be left behind," Liora said.

"Featherflash, you mean?" Cee asked.

"Yeah. But I'd rather not go there alone…" Liora muttered.

"It's okay. Renir provided me with a sigil just for such an occasion," Cee said. "Fly steady for a bit, and I'll weave it on your back."

Liora kept as steady as she could while the spider wove on her back. It wasn't easy keeping steady in the howling winds, and the dragon's presence wasn't comforting either. What if it ran out of its snacks and decided to have Liora and Cee for dessert?

[Your trait <Tempest Wings> has reached [Novice] level.]

"Okay, I'm done!" Cee announced after a few minutes. "Pull that sigil in your inventory and, once you get up the mountain, place it on the ground. Signal me, and I'll be able to get to you."

"What's it going to do? Teleport you?" Liora asked, pulling the web into her inventory.

"Exactly. Normally you'd need a ton of mana to fuel a teleportation sigil, but since the mountain is amplifying all of my sigils, it should be good."

"What if it doesn't work?" Liora asked.

"I guess… I'll wait for you down here?" Cee muttered.

That didn't sit right with Liora. Perhaps Liora could get through the mountain's natural barrier. Well, it was safer to try it without Cee at first.

She left the spider by one of the crystal tree groves, well hidden from the dragon. With that done, Liora flew up. She decided to try to get through with her own strength first.

Liora approached carefully, watching the deep purple mana like a hawk. The moment her body came into contact with it, she felt the same disorienting feeling when they fought the crystal elemental. She was being pulled sideways, away from the mountain's peak.

Flipping over, Liora tried to change her direction back to the peak, but just as soon as she tried that, the direction of gravity shifted, and she was pulled into the sky. Then it kept changing every few seconds, tossing Liora in every direction and not giving her the time to orient herself.

No wonder that the dragon couldn't get through this. It didn't matter how big and strong you were. In fact, the heavier you were, the more gravity worked against you.

Fed up, Liora activated Featherflash, turning into a mote of light, unaffected by the pesky gravity mana. She hurried to pass through the natural barrier, phasing through spikes and rocks. But her ability lasted for only ten seconds, and that wasn't enough to get through the black gravity mana.

She materialized and felt herself being pulled down.

I'm so close…

Just a bit more and she would reach the outer wall of the fortress. Liora transformed into her magpie form, which instantly lessened the burden of gravity. She was still being tossed in every direction, but being much lighter now, Liora could orient herself quickly enough to manage those last few meters to the fortress.

Once she reached the stone wall, Liora turned into her dracoryx form and latched onto it with her talons. By flapping her wings and using her talons to anchor herself to the wall, Liora pulled herself through the nasty gravity field.

What a nightmare… yeah, there was no way I could have done that without Featherflash.

Liora was about to search for a spot on the ground where to put the sigil. It had to be connected to the mountain after all. But with her vision, she saw that there was magic within the stone blocks. It was made from the material of the mountain.

It was a bit of a risk, but seeing the magic, Liora withdrew Cee's web from her inventory, willing it to appear on the wall. As expected, it stuck to it perfectly.

Seeing it was secure, Liora signalled Cee. The signal was a roar of a dragon. The emerald dragon roared in response, its voice still loud despite the distance.

A moment later, the web sigil flashed, and Cee appeared while the mana within the surrounding blocks was drained to fuel the magic.

"Whoa! We're on a wall. How was it getting up here?" Cee asked.

"Absolute nightmare. I can see how these assholes feel safe up here. I don't think I could have made it up here if I didn't have a small magpie form or Featherflash."

"That means that they won't be expecting any guests. If we remain hidden, then we should be fine. You should probably switch back to your magpie form," Cee suggested.

Liora nodded, turning small again while Cee scuttled up the wall without any issues.

"What are we looking for exactly?" Cee asked.

"The source of the corruption. The concentration of the corrupted miasma is low on this side, so we'll have to move around the wall to the western side."

Cee nodded, and they continued forward. There were watchtowers set up at certain intervals with a few people stationed in them. However, avoiding them was incredibly easy for the pair. By sticking close to the wall, right beneath these watchtowers, the two of them passed by without being noticed. Not that the guards were particularly vigilant. Half of them were asleep.

For Liora in particular, it was easy thanks to her Nightveil and Featherlight Flight, which made her silent. Cee could use Shadow Incarnate and hide in Liora's shadow.

[Your trait <Featherlight Flight> has reached [Novice] level.]

[Your trait <Magpie Disguise> has reached its maximum potential. Would you like to upgrade it for 100 glimmerstones?]

"That's interesting…" Liora muttered.

"What?" Cee asked.

"I can upgrade Magpie Disguise now."

"What would that even upgrade into? You can already shift between two forms without issue?"

"Maybe I won't get weaker while in Magpie Form?"

"That would be useful… Oh, look at that!" Cee said, pointing ahead.

One didn't need to have any magical sight to see the horrible black lake at the peak of the mountain. A plethora of corrupted monsters raged within the water, trying to get past the barriers set up by the mountain.

They threw themselves mindlessly at the spikes, not caring about the injuries or black ooze that came from their bodies. Some of the monsters that looked like plesiosaurs had their necks filled with such spikes.

Whenever a spike was broken, a new one emerged, and the monsters just kept raging against the mountain itself.

A giant dam was built within the lake, becoming part of the fortress wall. Black, corrupted water came from it, and it was clear that the source of the corruption was hidden within the fortress itself, away from all the raging corrupted monsters and away from the mountain.

"We have to get inside," Cee said.

"That's easy. We just sneak in from above," Liora chuckled, flying up as she activated her Decoy Fade.

Her decoy remained hidden beneath the watchtower while Liora flew safely up above the fortress wall. However, as soon as she peered inside the fortress, Liora realized that this might not be as simple as she initially thought.

The place was teeming with mages. They walked through the inner courtyard, doing whatever it was that evil mages did. There were many watchtowers set up within the fortress watching people within the fortress.

There was also a clear distinction between the mages in the watchtowers and those working below. The watchers had much more elaborate clothing, and Liora could sense a few valuables worth stealing on them. Some of them had some kind of animal with them, like a dog or panther. Meanwhile, the workers were poorly dressed and had no valuables on their person.

Something strange was going on here. It almost seemed… like these people were here against their will. They certainly didn't look happy, and the fact that their every move was being watched made it clear that they were on the bottom of the pecking order.

Liora returned to Cee, dispelling her decoy.

"So? See a spot for us to sneak in?" The spider asked.

"Well… I think it would be possible to sneak in, but we likely wouldn't be able to stay hidden for long. Everything is well lit, and there are mages watching everything."

"What? Why would they need to watch the inside of their own fortress?"

"It has to be because of the workers. I suspect they are not here of their own free will."

"Aww, danggit! Now what? I was ready to place a bunch of mana overcharge sigils everywhere and just blow this place up. We can't do that if there's innocent people in there."

Liora nodded in agreement. They had to get in somehow without being noticed or attracting attention. Unfortunately, being a monstrous spider and a magpie, they were far from subtle. And they couldn't rule out that some of these mages could detect them using magic. Liora had learned early that even if something couldn't see her, it didn't mean they didn't know you were there.

"Give me a moment. Maybe Magpie Disguise can be upgraded into some improved disguise trait?" Liora pondered.

"I guess so. Otherwise, we might have to resort to some unsavory tactics. I mean… We're trying to prevent a war, but I really, really don't want to hurt people if they are innocent."

Liora agreed. She didn't want to be pushed into choosing the lesser evil. Blowing this whole place up would be easy with Cee's sigils. They already sunk one evil laboratory that way.

Show me upgrades for Magpie Disguise.

[Magpie Disguise (Rank 2) upgrades available]

[Critter Disguise – Unlock additional small animal forms: sparrow; rat; squirrel. Receiving any damage will make you revert to your original form.]

Liora thought about this one. Perhaps she could get away with being caught as a rat. Rats were literally everywhere. But did she want to be a rat? Rats were absolutely disgusting, and she didn't want to be associated with one, much less become one herself.

[Magpie Spy – Your hearing, vision, and stealth ability are increased while you're in disguise, increasing with each skill level.]

Oh, this is pretty good. It means that I'll be even harder to find. But… it doesn't seem like it'll be able to hide me from those with heat vision or mana detection skills similar to my Prismatic Twilight Vision.

Damn, this would be great if I didn't have to deal with a fortress full of mages.

[Borrowed Shape – You're no longer limited to magpie form. You can take the form of any bird if you've observed it for long enough. Observation time required at [Basic] level: 1 minute]

This was once again a choice that would be great in any other scenario. Find an appropriate bird monster with appropriate skills and abilities, and copy it. She could have a whole library of useful birds: peregrine falcon, ducks, penguins, etc.

But there were no birds up on this damned mountain. Even if there were, there wasn't a single bird these guys wouldn't find suspicious.

Isn't there something better?
