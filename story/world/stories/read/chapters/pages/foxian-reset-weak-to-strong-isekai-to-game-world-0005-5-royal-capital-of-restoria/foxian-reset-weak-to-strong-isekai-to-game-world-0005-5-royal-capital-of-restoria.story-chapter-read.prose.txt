Swordsmen practiced in the courtyards, hunters darted between stalls, and banners fluttered overhead with a promise of adventure. The smells of roasted meats, fresh bread, and molten metal filled the air, and Kae’s tail twitched with a mix of nostalgia and wonder, as if she were seeing the city for the very first time.

There were players all around, chatting, trading, buying, and selling. It was a castle town with a few main guilds. Restoria had always been one of Kae’s favorite cities to relax in when she first started playing.

“Okay, I’ll go log into my merchant. I’ll be right back.”

Layla warped away, logging out of the game.

It was strange seeing it from this point of view. Actually, seeing everything from the point of view of a regular person was bizarre. There was no screen in front of Kae.

The world looked as realistic as if she were walking around in the real world. But she had known Keostar so well that it felt right to see things this way, as if Keostar had always been a real world for her to enter and explore.

“Hmmm...”

Maybe she had always been keeping her distance from the game too...

Kae looked up at the sky to see the beautiful clouds roll across the world above. Getting away from the bustle of the city for a moment, she stepped back outside, past the gates, and into the open world once again.

The grassland, trees, lakes, and flowers were so vibrant that she had to hold back the urge to roll around in them. The corner of her lips curled, and a sensation she had long thought forgotten until recently warmed her chest.

“I’ll roll around in the grass later. Yeah, I really want to roll in the grass.”

A simple promise she swore to keep. If the new fox was going to be trapped here, she might as well enjoy everything it could offer. Satisfied, she made her way back into Restoria.

Still waiting for Layla to return, she window shopped and started thinking more about this entire situation she found herself in.

“Hm, I wonder if Rexia ever played this death game with other players before. And why did she choose me?”

She pondered while walking in place, making sure to get her AGI up eventually.

This game had grandfathered stats, small things you could do to make your stats build up past the limit. Walking so much would eventually raise her AGI by one point. So, walking in place, as strange as it looked, would eventually give her a point. To keep grinding even while not grinding, she was going to do it!
