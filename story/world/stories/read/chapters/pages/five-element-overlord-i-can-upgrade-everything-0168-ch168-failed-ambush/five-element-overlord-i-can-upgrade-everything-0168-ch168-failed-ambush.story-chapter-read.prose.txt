I begin walking down the Mountain.

It would be faster to hop on my flying vessel, but after a half day of sparring, I am in a good mood, and the sight of the sky turning orange from a setting sun is nice to watch at a leisurely pace.

The path the glowing orb in the sky takes is strange, just like the moon at night.

No matter where I am, thousands of kilometers away in the sect, or here, it travels directly overhead at a steady pace.

The sun dips below the horizon, and the walkway leading through the dense forest on the front side of the mountain begins to cool off.

Fewer servants and less noise from Wang Clan descendants can be heard, and I’m left with only the sound of my own breathing.

Little Red grunts on my shoulder, sending me a pulse of thought telling me he’s done his job, and he spits out the foundational crystal in front of me. His aura returns to a state without any qi as I stow the crystal away.

I nod and open the bronze door in my mind to send him back, but he just burrows further into my robes to watch the sun set with me.

As the door opens, the sensation of Meimei waking up tugs at my soul next, but she just stretches and walks around the hidden realm for a while.

I release the hold on my meridians I’ve been maintaining for over 6 hours, and let the mixed impure 5 elemental qis flow out of my body from many different meridian release points.

A cloud of mixed colored qi flows into the air behind me like mist.

There are some impurities left behind, but not enough for me to care to do a full marrow cleansing session just yet.

Instead, I focus for a few seconds, and push fresh elemental qi back into the cycle to continue my practice.

The forest becomes completely dark as I start walking again and make my way further down the mountain.

There are lighting fixtures on the main path, but the wilderness a few hundred meters on either side becomes dark.

The glowing lights of Lingshi City’s streets come into view eventually, and I make up my mind to pull out my flying vessel soon, but a subtle shiver runs down my spine.

There is an almost imperceptible feeling of being watched that passes through me.

No other cultivators are walking up or down this path, I’m sure of it.

Last time I ignored feelings like this, foundational demons snuck up on me with no warning.

My gaze shifts around, piercing through the darkness quite well, but I don’t see anything.

Three hot stirs manifest in my illusory demon’s concealment art next, lower down the mountain, coming up my way.

I conceal both my qi aura and physique vibrations entirely and walk out of the light to hide behind a large tree’s shade.

As these three approach, I can feel two mid-stage illusory demon physiques, and one late stage physique. It puts me even more on edge.

Demons this weak wouldn't make my instincts crawl so much…

There must be a stronger illusory demon avoiding my senses.

The three warm sensations I can feel come within range of my 105 meter spiritual sense range, and I prepare for only the three system scans to hit my senses, but instead, there are four…

The first three show me the weaker demons are all at the 10th level of qi refinement.

The fourth, I cannot even sense his presence.

Two of them hold mid stage physiques and aren't even 50 years old. They look stern but nervous to be here.

[Shadow Assassin in training of the Ye Clan, does not make public appearances. He has a damaged dantian holding his qi cultivation at the 10th level, but has a half formed foundation from a failed breakthrough.]

An emerald swallow spins around my head, an azure lightning talisman crackles in my left hand, and my right is free, ready to summon multiple options from my system storage.

Left behind the tree, a 2-meter-tall black-armored warrior awaits my command with a string of my consciousness tethered to it.

My footsteps are not silent, my tier two artifacts give off dense qi pressure, and my own usage of the Illusory Demon’s Concealment Art resonates with these demons once I enter their senses. However, I do close the gap.

I easily continue running forward, dodging all of them as my emerald swallow circles behind me, shattering all three into mist.

The backlash makes every demon in my field of view slow their steps from the pain and gives me enough time to easily catch up to one.

I release the full strength of my Martial Spirit Physique around my fist and break through this demon’s back in a single concise punch, shattering his physique, dantian, and heart in a single blow.

Little Red doesn’t bat an eye, but the excitement of battle in my soul does alert my other bond.

A ripple in the air appears above my head as a white and silver fox yips loudly and looks around to see what’s happening.

The two younger illusory demons that just watched their teammate be killed in a single punch both run in opposite directions. One yells out.

“Senior, we’ve been caught! Save us-”

Meimei jumps and swings her tail to release a concentrated slash of pure white soul wisp, qi, and physique-imbued energy straight at this demon’s neck.
