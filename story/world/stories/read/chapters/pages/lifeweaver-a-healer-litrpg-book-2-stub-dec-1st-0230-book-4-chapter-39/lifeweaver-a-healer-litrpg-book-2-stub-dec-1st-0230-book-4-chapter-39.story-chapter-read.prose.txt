A weave full of purple threads expanded before Luke's senses, separate from the red ones representing dungeon portals. The efficiency gains with this new revelation were out of this world. Using Weavestep, he would now be able to jump from dungeon to dungeon without downtime as long as there were monsters to drain of mana inside. The possibilities were beyond exciting, but at that moment, he couldn't spend much time considering what the future might hold. He needed to focus on the present moment and the task at hand: bringing down the Singers.

Luke drew in a breath and reached in, grabbing a red thread. Weavestep brought him to a new location, this one inside an empty building. Closing the portal in there took no more than a few seconds, and he was soon on his way again. The second location had a couple of guards, but that was a good thing. Traversing the weave required quite a bit of mana, after all, and Luke needed a refill.

Milla: "Luke? Radio silence at this point is not appreciated."

Luke: "Noted. I'm closing portals as we speak. Did Ray report what he found?"

Milla: "He did. He also whined a lot about losing his knife."

Milla: "How are you getting around?"

He explained his discoveries and what he planned, and while Milla wasn't stoked about Luke doing it all on his own, she couldn't put up a good argument for bringing another person and endangering them, not after what happened to Ella.

Continuing in that same manner, Luke popped out next to random portals and closed them while taking some time for himself to drain any Singer he found of mana. In most cases, the portals stood in groups of three or four, meaning he could get at several with each jump. It didn't take long before he met more resistance. The Singers were quick on their feet, if nothing else, and reacted to the new thread with admirable speed. That didn't matter much when none of those sent to protect the portals could stand in Luke's way. Weaving through his opponents with threads and whips, he found it easier and easier to make their resistance crumble, and none could do more than offer up weak strength of will. Spellcasters did a little better on average, and some Singers used skills to protect themselves, but even then, it seemed Luke had unlocked something that put him above most other Integrated, at least in this regard.

As he leapt from portal to portal, closing each one, he sensed the number of threads diminishing. One by one, he would get them all. Like so many times before, overconfidence did him in. Appearing on a platform, he saw a small gathering of Integrated that sparked recognition. One of them in particular seemed so familiar, but he pushed on and landed right in the middle of them, reaching out with threads of mana to end their lives as he'd done with so many before. His threads were all either torn to shreds or rebuffed. A sword came down on Luke's arm, but he leaned back, saving it from full amputation even as a spell struck his back, sending him sprawling with a mouthful of sand and gravel.

"Lifeweaver," one said. It was the female he'd now been able to place.

Luke got up, grabbed a bottle of water from his re-stocked inventory, splashed some on his face, and took a long pull to rinse out his mouth. Only when he spat did he reply. "Intruder."

"You remember us," she said. "How nice."

All five of them were on him with terrible speed. Two hacked at his head and body with weapons, one formed a spellweave to create some sort of ice prison, and the last two brought out the machines Luke now knew would drain him of mana. How bothersome. He didn't want to waste the skill, but it would take too much time fighting them off and, if he was honest, Luke wasn't sure he'd win against all five of them working together. There was a line between confidence and foolishness after all, and he didn't feel like this was the time to step over to the other side.

The Healer's Moment froze time. Even so, he felt their combined wills battering against the skill, and it wouldn't take them long to overpower it. He'd been on the other side of that skill a few times by now and knew you didn't perceive the shift in time, but something still allowed you to fight against it. Yet another thing to research if he ever got a quiet moment that lasted longer than the effect of this skill. With little time to spare, Luke reached out and healed a small cut on the female Singer's cheek, then reached through the weave and weavestepped from one dangerous situation to one with the potential of far-reaching consequences.

"Shit!" Ray shouted when Luke popped into a chamber that looked a whole lot like the one where they'd rigged the Dot trap. A large, empty structure with plenty of room to move about, if not for the massive, cannon-shaped weapon right in the middle and the huge number of Singers surrounding it. Curtis never described what the weapon they used against New York looked like, but from his explanation, it hadn't been much bigger than a modern mortar-type launcher. From the devastation caused then, Luke wondered just how much damage this thing would be able to dish out. It was the size of a bus. Were they planning on obliterating Earth itself? Either way, they were getting ready to use it, and his appearance hadn't done anything to change that. Ray's yelp made all of them turn their way, and the woman standing next to the weapon sped up her work. Not a great sign.

Ray: "You scared the shit out of me!"

Not wanting to waste any more time, Luke rushed forward, using several threads of mana to push against the floor. Gaining speed, he leapt and used Weavestep while in the air, altering his trajectory so he wouldn't land on top of the first line of defenders. Instead, he went over them. The weapon radiated heat to an almost absurd degree, and he was sure it would burn him if he touched it. The amount of energy generated inside would have to be insane, and it was still building. Soon, it would have to be released, and that release would be directed right at the portal now opening in front of it. It almost wouldn't matter where that portal went, because the result would be devastating wherever it struck.

Now that Luke found himself right next to his weapon, the Singers were reacting. Some seemed to be funneling mana to the female Singer building the weapon and its attack, but other than the couple of them working the portal, everyone else was rushing forward. So far, none had launched ranged attacks. Instead, they came at him in melee. Luke figured they were scared they'd hit the cannon-like weapon behind him and feared he would destroy it himself. Oh, how wrong they were.

Instead, Luke rushed toward the portal, feeling the energy building inside the weapon come to a terrifying climax.
