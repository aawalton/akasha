Chapter 189 / B3-34: Expand the Pack

Joe was a bit bummed that Kenda couldn’t join him that evening, but given the insanity resulting from Margen’s return and Fort Coral’s embroilment with the fey courts, all five guild officers: Septimus, Myllo, Puqmup, Kendell, and Tezeno, were going to be in meetings with the city council deep into the night.

Instead, his guest for the evening was Yuk.  While the raggamuffin was thrilled by the invitation, Mojo was rather confounded by the discorporated youth. Joe was aghast as the monkey-pup kept eating bits of his swarmling friend.  Yuk found it funny.

‘So, when are you going to call out the hounds?  We have been dying to see them.’

“The death curse has me a bit leery.   I was thinking of waiting until we had figured out how to break the bane, but I think I’m being over cautious.  I’m almost certain that it won’t be tainted.  And, I really want to see ‘em, too.  Let’s do it.”

Joe withdrew the heavy, silver disc from his dim-bag.  Even just holding it in his hand, not a force paw, he could feel presences within the artifact; multiple points of awareness were reaching out to him. In response, Joe felt wildness flare [Pack Master]. This time, the untamed spirit did not invoke the skill as a tool of domination. In the ziggurat, Joe's wild side had used [Pack Master] like a hammer to crush the will of the undead beastlings. Now, it felt more like a shout of joy.  Mojo also perked up as the hidden psyches awoke.

The first to emerge was a lean white spectral hound. It did not appear out of the amulet; rather, it stepped into existence a few feet from Joe, appearing in the room as if it had just walked out of a fogbank.  Joe could somewhat see through its body, and its legs ended in wisps as if someone had forgotten to paint its feet. The canine spirit resembled a Scottish deerhound in size, shape, and coat, yet it possessed slightly more refined features than the rough-coated hunting hounds.  His muzzle split into a wide, toothy smile.  The spirit let loose a quick spectral howl before unleashing the most epic case of zoomies Joe had ever beheld.  He moved so fast that at times, the beast was just a blur of unfettered energy.  Clearly, someone had been pent up for far too long.

Ranu: Level 22: Spirit (Cù-Sìth): Skirmisher: Dexterity

Mojo yipped joyfully and took off after the racing ghost.  As the chase flew around Joe's small apartment, two more spectral beings strode into the room, facing Joe.  The first was far larger than Ranu.  She leveled a fierce gaze at him, clearly letting him know she was not to be trifled with.  Joe expected the wildness to slam down on this challenge.  Surprisingly, the feral force inside Joe stood firm, confident in its place in the pack.  [Pack Master] activated again, but it was not a flare of aggression, just a simple nudge.  The wildness was saying, ‘I see you,’ without any attempt to force the ghost hound into submission.

Baru: Level 24: Spirit (Cù-Sìth): Soldier: Perception

Finally Baru dipped her head, which appeared to be the signal the third hound was waiting for.  Smaller than the big girl beside her, about the same size as Ranu, this cù-sìth spirit let loose a chorus of exuberant barks at such a deafening volume that Joe had to cover his ears.

Bayu: Level 22: Spirit (Cù-Sìth): Controller: Spirit

“Bayu! Quiet!” Joe commanded.  Thankfully, she obeyed even though Joe could tell she was struggling to contain herself.  Even though the cacophony had ceased, the room was far from calm. A moment later, Mojo dove across the dining room table, chasing after the fleet-footed Ranu, sending all of Joe’s warding instruments flying.  “You two! Take it outside!”

“Holy moley, “ Joe breathed as the pair dove out the window, followed by the boisterously loud girl.  The remaining hound, Baru, sighed and looked at Joe as if to say, ‘See what I have to deal with.’

“I take it you three have accepted the bond,” Joe asked her. When she bowed her majestic head, Joe received a notification.

You are soulbound with the [Trisynun Ossuary].

“Sweet,” Joe cheered.  He would have been seriously bummed if, for some reason, he was unable to ally with the medallion.  He was excited as well for Mojo.  Having playmates would be a good thing for the little guy.

Listening, Joe heard another bark among Mojo’s ruffs, Bayu’s shouts, and Ranu’s yips, something larger than the shug monkey but not as ethereal as the ghost hounds.  Sticking his head out the window, he looked down to see the small pack dash around the building.  A fourth dog had joined them.  As was becoming his habit in this world, Joe assessed it.

Mahq Catstaff: Human: Druid / Savant 10

Joe smiled at the scene.  He was happy that the beast-shaped boy was far more animated with the ghost hounds and his companion than he was with people.  Unfortunately, the decibel level was rising.  Obviously, the hounds had been cooped up for a very, very long time and were in dire need of some playtime.

“They are going to rile up the whole neighborhood,” Joe groaned. Already, he could hear the sounds of doors opening around the Abakka House. Leaping out into the evening with Baru at his side, Joe chased after the four maniacs. Even flaring [Hunter’s Pursuit], there was no way he was going to catch the racing runners. Thankfully, the trio was just bolting circles around the vine-coated apartments.

As he came up to the front of the building, Joe turned around.

In seconds, Mojo, Mahq, Ranu, and the happily howling Bayu turned the corner, charging right toward him.

“STOP!” Joe growled. They did.

“Sit!” he added. And they did that as well.

As Bayu was about to yelp out a greeting to Joe, he interjected. “Quiet, Bayu. Clearly, everyone needs to work off some steam.  How about we all go for a run?  Mahq, go ask your grandparents if it's okay.  We’ll wait for you.  Tell her we’ll be taking a lap over to Cliffside and back, saying hi to Mercy Suku.”

Instantly, the boy was standing before Joe, transforming in the blink of an eye.  “Okay,” he uttered, not meeting Joe’s gaze.  He scooped up Mojo and jogged into the courtyard.

Joe followed the youth with his eyes, only to see that half of the residents of the Abakka House were out in the lovely atrium.  Halten was there, glowering at them under his heavy, hairy eyebrows. Runkbadok was wearing an apron and holding a long, dripping spoon. Ord and Berti were standing by their door below Joe’s apartment, looking sheepish, certain their eccentric grandson was almost surely part of the ruckus.

Neighbors from surrounding homes were also looking out of their front doors.

“Sorry, everyone,” Joe stated in a chagrined tone. “Things got a little out of hand.”

Runk gave him a friendly salute with his ladle and headed back to his kitchen.  Halten shook his head before wandering off.  There were a few grumbles from the next-door residents.  Joe wasn’t sure how much longer his beautiful, floral apartment would be able to accommodate his growing family.

‘We're going for a run.  Wanna join us?’ Joe mentally asked the hooded form looking out of the upstairs window.

‘Just running for running's sake?  Why?  No thanks.  At your place, we don’t have to worry about someone finding us … or flooding.  We plan to ransack your pantry and relax.  You have fun running for no reason, Joe.’

‘Suit yourself, bud.  Eat whatever you like.  We’ll shop on the way back.’

Moments later, they were all dashing through the streets of Rockpoint.  Joe was struggling to keep up, until he recalled the medallion had a speed buff.  He activated [Ranu’s Dash] and received a notification window.

[Ranu’s Dash] Gain a 24% bonus to your movement speed.  Additionally, increase the range increment of your skills.  Choose a specific range distance and increase it by 1 range increment. This choice can be changed each time you level. Passive

Please select the range you wish to extend.

Joe thought through his spells as the group ran past Rosline’s Ring.  Ranges went from Touch, Immediate, Close, Short, Medium, Long, Distant, Extreme, up to Sight.  There was also Self, but that was more of a condition of a skill than an actual range. Thanks to [Casting Claws], Joe could extend the range of all of his spells by Medium range.  Even though the vast majority of his ranges were Touch, Joe didn’t think making those Immediate and therefore adding just three feet to that increment was worth it.  Instead, he went with Close, doubling the distance for [Deaden Flesh] and [Purge].  Considering he was on the cusp of a new level, he could always change it in a few days if it turned out to be a bad choice.

With the 24% speed boost, the whole group sped up.  Ranu had to be out front. Everything about the spirit beast projected its love of running.  It was then that Joe noticed the similarities in the names and the personality of the cù-sìth it belonged to. Ranu was the runner.  Bayu could not contain her baying voice.  Joe even recalled Padu, realizing that ‘pad’ was a perfect descriptor for that regal lady's quiet movements.

So Baru’s root would be ‘bar’.  Joe assumed that meant ‘to prevent,’ not ‘a place to drink’ or ‘a piece of wood or metal.’ As he watched the largest of the spirit hounds, he realized that, instinctively, she was running in a shield position for the lower-level members of the pack: Mojo and Mahq.  She was the pack’s protector, the one who would ‘bar’ anything hostile from reaching her charges.

When they had to stop to let a wagon cross the road in front of them, Joe and Baru exchanged a look that told Joe she would not let the boy or changeling out of her sight.  Running a hand down her back in a reflexive gesture of thanks, he expected his limb to pass right through her body. Surprisingly, as long as Joe didn’t put much weight behind his motion, he found his fingers sliding over a semi-tangible torso.

“Ok, that’s cool,” he beamed, watching at his fingers lightly gliding over the beautiful girl’s back. “Tomorrow, I have someone else I want you to meet.  I think you’ll like him, since he can take care of himself.”

Before they resumed their run, Joe sent his awareness out across the ocean seeking a certain ahklut.  It took less time than he expected to feel Finn respond.  The big guy was already heading back toward Coral Bay.

Joe looked forward to another round of introductions and play.
