Chapter 279

President Terris woke up with a gasp. It was still the dead of night, and his room in the White House was cold and empty. He had been sleeping in his king sized bed alone for months, but today was the first time he had woken up and felt the oppressive weight of the freezing emptiness.

It had been the fourth night of nightmares after the fateful event that changed the trajectory of his life. For a moment, he wondered if what had happened yesterday had just been another nightmare, but then reality settled in. The actual nightmares were different. They were memories, made nightmarish not by sleep, but by the state his waking mind had been while he had lived those moments.

The memories from yesterday were different. While strange, confusing and weird, they were coherent. He could reconstruct the sequence of events that had unfolded during the whole day, one after the other, with clarity and precision. It was enough to know that he had actually lived through it, the demonstration of magic and the reveal of the dungeon and its terrifying power and dangers, rather than it being just a figment of a broken mind.

He slowly pulled himself up. His mind was no longer broken, and neither was his body. He could get up from the bed with much greater ease than usual, his back no longer protesting and his stomach no longer feeling like it wanted to empty itself of what little contents it held. In fact, he felt a healthy amount of hunger.

Then there was the elephant in the room. A thing that, had he not been told to expect it, would have been enough to make him doubt his own sanity to the point that, perhaps, he would have taken his own life. It was better than slowly fading away to become the shell of a person he had been for the last two years of his life.

But no. The message that stared back at him in his field of vision was very real. He had been assured of that.

Welcome to the Origin System™ (OS-v.0.4), President Arthur Terris.

His hand went to touch a device he was wearing around his left wrist. Outwardly, it looked like a Rolex–his own Rolex–but in reality it was much more than a simple watch. The confirmation of the item’s anomalous existence came after looking at it a little too long, when a new dialog box appeared in his vision.

Wearable Domain Emitter mk-2

Leased item. Property of Unity Corporation

A wearable device capable of generating a small sub-Domain field with diameter of approximately 5 feet. A micro-portal feeds it energy from the nearest full Domain to ensure continued Origin System™ operativity. Does not provide energy for the User.

He breathed. Letting out a sigh, he got out of bed and approached a mirror. In it, a face he almost did not recognize stared back at him. Younger, healthier, slimmer. The light in his eyes was alive.

He found himself grinning. The gin became a laugh, and the laugh became roars of laughter that echoed across the room. Someone heard him from the corridor and banged on his door, surely about to open it to check what the dementia-ridden president was up to this time.

“One moment!” he yelled instead, and the banging stopped.

“Everything alright, Mr President?” the woman outside asked.

“Yes. I’ll be out in a minute. You can go.”

He heard her steps grow distant and then disappear in the maze of corridors of the White House. Perhaps it was not going to feel like a maze anymore, he thought. He couldn’t wait to finally be out, to finally live his life again! So what if he had to sell his soul to a corporation to do it?

The thought seemed to trigger something. A new message box appeared, much bigger than the others.

SYSTEM NOTICE: END USER LICENSE AGREEMENT

By mentally engaging with this interface, you (hereinafter "The User") acknowledge that

the Origin System™ (OS-v.0.4) is the exclusive intellectual, biological, and metaphysical property of Unity Corporation.

The User agrees that all physical enhancements and supernatural abilities granted by the System are leased, not owned, unless stated otherwise. Unity Corporation reserves the right to terminate access or initiate remote neural shutdown at any time, for any reason, without prior notice.

The System will only work inside a Domain or a sub-Domain. Leaving a Domain will result in immediate loss of all enhancements and abilities not fully owned by the User.

The User consents to real-time monitoring of all thoughts, sensory inputs, and biological functions by the System Administrators.

There is no privacy setting.

Unity Corporation is not liable for any loss of sanity, rapid-onset cellular decay, or existential dissociation resulting from the misuse of System privileges or failure to complete assigned Quests.

Do you wish to proceed?

[ ACCEPT ]

[ DECLINE (Initiate Biological Failure) ]

His breath caught in his throat. “I accept,” he croaked, suddenly parched and struggling to speak.

The message window disappeared, and he could breathe again. He felt at the verge of panic for a moment, but he was the President, damn it.

New Quest

The Official Narrative

The world is watching the black dome over Huntington. Panic is setting in. The previous administration was weak and compromised; you are the strongman who returned from the brink. You must shape the truth before the media does. Unity Corporation requires the public to believe the situation is contained, dangerous, and requires specialized intervention only we can provide.

Objective: Deliver a televised National Address within 4 hours. Declare the state of National Emergency and designate Unity Corporation as the sole government contractor capable of handling the emergency. Do not enter into details until you talk with your contact inside Unity Corporation.

In the inner space, Travis Tyrell was leaning on the strange holographic desk full of screens and data–the Central Dashboard of the Origin System. He watched the President get ready for work, walk toward the Oval Office with purpose burning in his eyes, and shoo away anyone attempting to interrupt him. As soon as he was alone, he took out pen and paper and began jotting down some notes for a speech.

“Very good, Mr President,” Travis muttered, lips curled upward in a lopsided grin. “Be a good pet and I’ll be sure to give a lot of nice treats.”

“You’re having way too much fun,” an androgynous voice said, less than an inch away from his ear.

“Icarus!” Travis exclaimed as he almost fell from the chair. “You startled me!”

Icarus laughed. “It was on purpose. I saw you all focused, planning and scheming. I thought, why not have some fun?”

“Ah well,” he said, still wheezing. “Listen, I apologize for antagonizing you in the past. I know I called you creepy but I didn’t really mean it.”

“Pff,” Icarus said with a laugh and a shrug. “You were acting because the Don was watching, weren’t you?”

“Of course.”

“Now,” Icarus said as they leaned in. “How’s the President doing?”

“He’s preparing his speech,” Travis said.

“Did he open his status yet?”

“No.”

“Hmm,” Icarus made a face. “Boomers like him might not immediately think about opening their status… I will have to fix that. Adding it to the starter quests. Aaaand, sending it to him.”

Through the screen, they saw the President react to the sudden appearance of the new quest. He looked around, confirming that his office was empty, before looking down and muttering, “status.”

It took him a while to absorb all the information, although he was quicker than Travis thought he would be, given the magnitude of the changes. While the President mulled over his status screen, he asked Icarus about the [Presidential Authority] skill.

“It’s actually [Voice of Command] in disguise, just with another name. If he clicks on the benefits of his title, he will see that it comes from there. Ah, he just did that. He’s a quick learner, isn’t he?”

Travis nodded. “Yes, he is. Did you and Michael have something to do with that?”

Icarus shook their head. “Nope,” they said. “We are not in the business of messing with people’s heads like that.”

“Not yet,” Travis muttered.

“Not yet,” Icarus said with a nod. “Anyway, I do have the ability to read his mind. It seems like he’s finally realizing the implications of what he’s seeing.”

Through the screen, they could see the President get up and open the liquor cabinet near a mirror. He poured himself a glass and gulped it down, then another which he sipped while looking at his own reflection. His brow was furrowed, and his features tight. Then, he sighed and relaxed.

“This is the new world,” he said to the mirror. “And I am the first pioneer. System, give me more information about the main tier, please.”

“Oh!” Icarus perked up. “He said please!”

Michael stared at the entrance of the dungeon, at the heart of Site 00. The reinforced concrete building that encased the actual cave, manned by several operators who had been given considerable power through the Origin System, did not prevent his sight from penetrating through the rock until finally some mysterious force or energy blocked his perception.

“Feels like it’s been ages since I’ve last been here, doesn’t it?”

Icarus appeared beside him. “For some reason, it always ends up feeling this way.”

Their presence was enough to make Michael relax a little. He nodded, waited for the operators to open the door for him, and vanished inside. Goblins and shielded skeletons greeted him, but he did not move. Instead, Icarus stepped forward and made finger-guns with their hands, sweeping the whole room in a wide arc. Moments later, all the monsters fell to the ground, bisected in two pieces that already were slowly evaporating into motes of magic.

“I like the laser,” Icarus said while Michael bent down to gather the few copper coins the dungeon had left behind as a reward. “But it feels clunkier than using the light element to achieve the same effect.”

Michael hummed. The coins vanished, only to reappear in one of the warehouse-sized data rooms in the inner space. He had still not worked on restoring his elements, which was a thing he had to do soon so that they would be available to lease through the System.

“Oh,” he said as he suddenly perked up.

Next to him, Icarus had the same reaction. “David has just completed floor eight,” they said.

“Looks like it was yet another challenge. He’s now close to peak Silver. That was quick.”

“He’s not only overtaken you, he also just increased the power of the Origin System by 0.08%,” Icarus finished. “Not bad. The new skills he brought in are very juicy.”

“Overtaken… You know my power can’t be easily measured in Tiers,” Michael said.

“Well,” Icarus purred, getting close and using their index finger to draw circles on Michael's chest. “Your aura at full power is–not counting the System–only 44.7 Silver, while his is close to 80. Yours is mana-only, his has Qi and Intent and his strange exotic element.”

“Which we can also use,” Michael interrupted.

“It’s ours, yes, but it’s not ours-ours. Besides, David did not even use the System to power-up.”

“You know it goes against his ethos to accept outside help.”

“Yes, it surprised me that he even accepted to get the System.”

“He did it for the spiral. Anyway,” Michael said with a snort. “I won’t let you bait me like this.”

Icarus laughed. “Hmm. You did, though. Your heart rate went up by fifteen percent, your body temperature–”

“That’s enough,” Michael said softly.

“Alright,” Icarus said. There was still a teasing smile on their face. “Let’s go deeper. Ah, but first we are stopping at the Valley. You and I are going to spend a nice afternoon together doing some sweet sweet nothing.”
