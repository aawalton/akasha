Jack had always had a personality that Logan didn’t understand, but Logan didn’t know why the tiger dog would suddenly be so fascinating. Either he was ignoring Logan on purpose or something else was at work. Did transforming into one of Ernie’s undead minions alter him in a fundamental way?

“We’re still ourselves, but better, I think,” said Tasha, her eyes glowing blue. “We feel loyal to Ernie, but if it came down to it, if he did something truly unforgivable, I think we could resist his commands. We’re not just mindless drones following everything he says.”

Humans who were struggling to keep themselves connected to their human brethren. For all their physical status, they seemed more or less there. Plus, the damn bugger was looking at him with his wide, black eyes, eyes that were pleading for forgiveness.

As far as Logan was concerned, there was nothing to forgive.

“All right,” said Logan, patting Ernie on top of his helm. “I get why you did it. Still, no more humans, you hear? Tasha and Jack were different, they needed this, but I’ll have a rebellion on my hands if any of the others think the kids or the rest are in danger of turning into a zombie. Being one of your minions might even give them a boost in stats, but it won’t matter. Just the word ‘undead’ will make most run for the hills.”

“It’s nowhere as stylish as my new helm, it’s crusty and old!”

He’d need to fashion a holster using [Mimicry Armour], but for now, Logan rested the dagger against his shoulder.

She is mighty mighty, close to level 200 when we encountered her! With a minion army of at least a thousand.”

Ernie puffed with pride. “While my army is smaller than the queen serpent’s, my minions are loyal! They fight because they believe in me, while the queen’s minions do her bidding because of fear!”

Ernie shivered in eagerness, his tentacles twitching, his eyes greedy. “Does that mean we’re going to war…?” he whispered.

Logan drew his brows together, his face tightening. It was possible that the queen serpent over classed them. If she had been close to level 200 when they encountered her, what was her level now? Not to mention that her minion army was more than twice the size of Ernie’s.

And yet, Logan had a few things going for him. His stealth player status. Although in a straight up fight, he tended to judge an opponent based on their level, his encounter in the trial dungeon had made him more powerful in a level to level comparison.

On top of that, although he continued to have a vulnerability to mind attacks due to his low constitution stat, Logan didn’t remember the queen serpent deploying any mind commands against him in the underwater tunnel. She’d had tons of time to try to overpower and catch up with him in the tunnel. Sure, the serpent eventually cornered him, and it was only his escape through the portal that allowed him to survive, but if it had been Logan in her place, he didn’t see how he would have let an opponent get away.

And he had epic skills up his sleeves. Two skills. The queen serpent might out class Logan and Ernie, sure, but she didn’t have [Life Fabricator] or [Liche Devourer].

Logan nodded. “Tomorrow morning. Get your army ready.”
