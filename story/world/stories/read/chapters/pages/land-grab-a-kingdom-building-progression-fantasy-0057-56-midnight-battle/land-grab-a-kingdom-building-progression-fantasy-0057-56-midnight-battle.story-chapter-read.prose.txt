The black sky glittered with stars, occasionally obscured by smoke as Tavian and his men crept up the streets toward the Anarvian compound. Ghouls had flooded into the warehouse as they’d evacuated.

Lord Anarvia didn’t seem to realize that Tavian and his men had slipped away, because as yet, there was no hoard of snarling undead chasing them down. Maybe they were searching the warehouse grounds for him.

Braun and the Calenorian captain—his name was Vaden—jogged alongside Tavian.

Tavian’s men were slower than the Calanorians, slowed by Anarvian influence.

“What is your plan, my lord?” Vaden asked.

“We’re going to get or destroy the Witherstone,” Tavian said. “Before he realizes what’s going on. Donath and his men should begin their assault at the front gate any moment.” Tavian tapped on his shield. “We’ll be going into the back.”

The man bobbed his head, understanding.

“A key part of any tactical maneuver,” Tavian explained between breaths. “Give your enemy dilemmas, not problems. Problems have a solution. Dilemmas force a choice. Lord Anarvia is going to have to choose between reinforcing his front gate and fighting against us.”

“And if he chooses to fight us?” Vaden asked.

“Then we hold out as long as we can. Donath will break through the gates, and overrun the compound.”

A ghoul ran out of a dark alley, eyes locked on Tavian. It snarled and gnashed its teeth, scampering on all fours.

Vaden dispatched it with a few blows of his sword. “We’ll clean the streets of these foul creatures yet,” the Calenorian captain said.

Tavian felt the pull of the shield. He’d hoped to pass through this portion without having to encounter ghouls, but the shield was drawing them to him. They were attracted to its power.

A deeper power lay within the shield, calling to Tavian. It begged him to open himself up to the power, let it merge with him. The Aegis could do much more than simply knocking down walls.

⬣ UNCLASSIFIED Item: Aegis of Tavirost ⬣

⬣ Traits: Hardened Structure, Lightweight ⬣

⬣ Abilities: Shockwave, Ability Unknown: Ability Locked ⬣

He knew what he had to do to unlock that ability.

Tavian rejected it.

They moved through an open garden area, where trees grew among colonnaded courtyards, then reached the side wall of Anarvia’s compound.

Tavian waited for his forces to gather.

He listened carefully, and heard shouts from within, and the distant clash of men fighting. Donath had arrived, right on time.

There was no sign of ghouls yet. It wouldn’t take long for Anarvia to figure out that there was nobody left at the warehouse.

“Everyone ready?” Tavian asked. “As soon as I smash through this wall, they’re going to know we’re here. Remember, we’re going straight to the heart. If we can’t find the Witherstone, we’re going to be overrun.”

He knew it was risky. What if Anarvia wasn’t even here? What if he was commanding his ghouls with the witherstone from some other location?

But the gears were already turning, and time was running out. If they couldn’t stop Anarvia now, there was no hope against the Karath Amon.

Tavian placed his shield against the wall, waving everyone away from him.

The force of the blast sent man sized stones flying, smashing into trees and demolishing a gazebo. Tavian stepped through the wreckage, entering the private gardens of Lord Anarvia. His men flooded in behind him.

Tavian stood in the darkness, looking around.

“I think we came in too far to the left,” Braun said, peering through the shadows.

“We need to go that way,” Vaden said. “I’ve been here before.”

Tavian and Braun followed Vaden through the garden, their men behind. They wound around pedestals where decorative statues had once been—likely turned to Essence to fuel the Core—and came to a gate. It led through to the buildings that made up the compound.

Lord Anarvia should be in there, somewhere.

Tavian crossed over the threshold, scanning the open area for a threat.

Vaden came out behind him, pointing forward into the heart of the compound.

A shadow moved on a nearby roofline.

“Get back!” Tavian shouted, throwing his shield up, and intercepting a bolt. It smashed against his shield, exploding into fist sized chunks of stone.

The garden fence provided concealment, but not cover, and more stone lances began smashing down from the rooftops, hurled by several dark figures on the roofs.

Tavian thought he’d been betrayed by Vaden as he stumbled back against the barrage, but the man seemed just as shocked as Tavian.

Then a stone spear ripped through Vaden’s chest, and he fell lifeless to the ground in a spray of blood.

More shards of elemental stone ripped through trellises, bushes, and fences, tearing Tavian’s men apart. Someone had anticipated his movement, and set a trap for him. Tavian used the ability of his shield, blasting back another volley of stone lances. There was more to the shield, he knew it.

Tavian gave the order, and everyone fell back, taking shelter around the side of a building. They left the dead and wounded strewn across the smashed gardens.

“How did they know?” Braun asked, blood on his face, eyes wild.

The attack stopped, and someone called out from within the compound.

“Give it up, Tavian. We’ve got you cornered. You’re going to lose a lot of men trying to break out of there.”

He knew that voice.

That woman had already tried to murder him. Somehow, Ilara had predicted his movements.

Tavian threw his black dagger aside and reconnected with Lea, grimacing as Anarvia’s influence pushed against him.

“We’re under attack!”

More stone lances smashed into the corner of the wall where Tavian was sheltered, spraying him with debris.

“It looks like a stone [Lancet] spell,” Lea observed. “It’s higher tier. You’re dealing with advanced classes, probably Tier four.”

“Donath?” Tavian asked.

“We’ve no word from him in the fortress. I assume he’s still at the gate.”

“We have to get out of here,” Braun said, preparing the men to retreat through the garden. “We can’t fight that!”

Tavian ground his teeth.

“We can join with Donath and push in one united front,” Braun urged. “Claim tiles.”

Tavian called to Lea again. “What are the consequences if I let the miasma from the shield take me over?” he asked. “I need to unlock its full potential.”

“Don’t do it, Tavian. It’s not worth it.”

“That’s what you keep saying. Let me decide for myself.”

“Functionally? The more you use miasma, the more it will try to destroy you. You’re going to light up like a beacon, and attract ghouls like a flame attracts moths. And, if you let it grow too far, it will kill you. You may become a lich, like Cormath, your flesh rotting off your bones until you’re nothing but a skeleton.”

More stone lances slammed into the ground nearby, and men shifted deeper into the shadow of the building.

“Go around,” Tavian whispered to Braun. “Take some men. Either get behind them, or get to the main gate and help Donath.”

Braun nodded, slipping away into the darkness. Tavian huddled against the stone wall with a few dozen men.

Tavian grabbed his black dagger again and hefted the shield.

Attracting ghouls was a problem; his men would easily be overrun by a thousand screeching undead. They were probably already coming toward them. But if Tavian could get to the Witherstone, he could negate that threat.

He activated his Darksight Perk and scanned the compound, searching for compressions of miasma, that would give away the position of the stone.

Nothing but his shield and the black daggers caught his attention. Beyond that, the humanoid shadows of ghouls moved. They were coming for him.

Ilara had created a serious dilemma for him. If he tried to retreat, they’d be ripped to shreds. He’d lose a lot of men. In a flash of insight, Tavian saw her hand reaching much further. How much of this had been her plan? The entire coup?

Tavian reached through the miasmic mass in his side, exploring the workings of the Aegis. With his mind, he felt the trigger for Shockwave.

And an idea came to him. The wall he leaned against was buttressed by thick columns every six feet, meaning that it would be thinner here.

“Everyone back off,” he shouted. He’d survived Ilara once before, and he was about to do it again.

Pressing the shield against the wall where they sheltered, Tavian ducked his head down and out of the way. He activated the blast.

With a crack of stone and crumbling masonry, a gaping hole opened up into the building. The black shockwave of miasma reverberated through the air. With his darksight still active, Tavian watched it absorb into the ground and stones around him.

A glance over his shoulder showed him black miasmic masses, pits of blackness in the night, attracted by what he’d done. Ghouls were coming.

“Let’s go!” Tavian exclaimed. He leaped through the hole, men streaming in after him.

Tavian’s men were flooding through into the building, when a solid wall of stone solidified, plugging the hole. Men were trapped inside of it, limbs dangling from a sarcophagus of rock. A dozen men had made it through with Tavian.

He couldn’t blast back through the wall without killing his men on the other side. Tavian cried out as he heard his men dying, when he could do nothing for them.

Tavian ran forward, down a hall. This building had entrances on either side. He hoped Braun had managed to get deeper into the compound.

Tavian used a shockwave to blast a new opening in the other side of the building, diving out onto the road.

Tavian was now behind Ilara and her mages.

There were only six of them, now standing down in the street trying to get an angle on Tavian’s men.

Six against a hundred and fifty, and Tavian had been outmatched still. That was the brutal math of Kingdom Core warfare. Only his shield could even the odds now.

The blast of Tavian emerging from the wall drew their attention, and they all turned toward him. He fired a few poorly aimed elemental projectiles, focusing pushing life, water, and earth essence into them, the relative weaknesses of stone.

The mages scattered. Ilara was among them, wearing a black gown which swept across the ground as she danced out of the way. She wore a sword, buckled around her waist. In the dim light, Tavian recognized it.

The sword was his.

A white knife of fury cut through him. He saw her face, twisted and bloodied as she struggled with him in his antechamber.

He should have made sure of her death then.

Some of his men were sheltering in the gaping hole he’d just made in the building. Others ran across the street, taking up a position opposite. They weren’t going to last long against more volleys of stone lances. Tavian felt a pang; these men were running into the jaws of death for the sake of their city.

Tavian fished into his miasma tainted soul. An image of ghouls tearing into his flesh appeared in his mind. He opened himself to the power within the shield, and the mass within him grew. Icy pain gripped him, throbbing in his veins. With each pulse of darkness, he became aware of more ghouls beyond the walls. And they became aware of him, wanting nothing more than to devour his flesh and consume the miasma inside him.

A new ability slipped into his hands. He shut himself off immediately, stifling the growing storm of darkness.

⬣ New Ability Unlocked. Ability Identified ⬣

⬣ Bulwark ⬣

⬣ Shield Abilities: Shockwave, Bulwark, Ability Unknown: Ability Locked ⬣

He triggered the new ability as a hail of stone lances raced toward him.

The shield expanded to more than twenty times its regular size, leaving a shimmering barrier in the air. The enemy’s attack shattered against the projection, and the shield pushed against Tavian’s arm as though it had been struck. The miasmic shield projection crumpled as Tavian moved. He would have to hold position to maintain the shield.

Tavian ran toward the nearest of the mages and fired a Shockwave at her. He was too far to do much damage, but she was still knocked down.

He flipped his shield around to intercept another attack. He used elemental [Projectile] again, this time picking out Ilara and targeting her specifically.

It landed, ripping through her dress and clipping her shin. Ilara shouted out in pain. He caught a glimpse of darkness glittering in her eyes, and he knew she’d be coming for him. She shouted furious orders to her mages.

“Kill him!”
