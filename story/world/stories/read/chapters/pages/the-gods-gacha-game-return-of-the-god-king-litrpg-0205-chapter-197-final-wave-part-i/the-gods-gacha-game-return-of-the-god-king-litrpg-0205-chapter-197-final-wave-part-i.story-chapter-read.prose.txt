Is that enough? I quickly realized something was off—I hadn’t received a kill notification from the System. Since he had transformed into a true monster, killing him should have granted me experience points.

But at that very moment, a figure blurred forward. Marcel, the leader of New Hope, appeared behind the fiend, his weapon already poised. Without hesitation, he drove it straight into the creature’s core, piercing deep into what remained of its heart.

“…!”

“Raaa… Rhoo…”

Huh? What is he saying?

And worst of all, they had succeeded in one thing, which was planting doubt. There could be other traitors among us… and they might be closer than expected. As expected of the God of Deception… he was a much trickier opponent than that regressor’s faction.
