Mission: Half friend

Your half-friend Bojo has gone missing. You don’t know his condition. As a half-friend, you must determine his current location and condition.

Objective: Find your half friend’s whereabouts. Ensure he’s safe; help him overcome any obstacles. Save your friend and prove to yourself that you are a true friend.

Reward: Unknown

Lin stared at the mission panel, jaw clenched. “So that’s it, huh? Not even pretending this is optional anymore. Bojo goes missing, and suddenly my social life, survival, and whatever scraps of system goodwill I have—all hang by a thread. Hell of a way to motivate a guy.”

Resignation warred with grim determination. There was no “ignore” button. No loophole. He could grind all day, hoard his seedlings, even try running—but if he left this unchecked, the repercussions would haunt him for the rest of his days. Somewhere deep in the system’s code, Lin thought, it’s smirking. ‘Go play the hero now, little goat. See where apathy lands you.’

He closed the notification bar and tried to focus on his daily missions. But just as he was about to get back to work, he felt something—a prickling on his skin, a gut feeling that someone was coming.

Lin remembered how anxious Bojo had been when they arrived here. He didn't waste a single second, grabbed a few quick bites, and ran. He only stopped after a solid minute, glancing back from a safe distance.

A moment later, a goat tore into the clearing at a shocking speed, stopping right where Lin had been. What the hell is that speed? Can goats even move like that? It was like watching a car running at 100 kilometres an hour—maybe not literally, but for a goat, it was a wild sight.

Right, this is a magical world. Maybe that goat’s enhanced or something. I’m not sticking around to find out. Lin turned and kept running. If that goat came after him, he is toast.

After a few more minutes, Lin stopped, panting, and looked around. No one was behind him. A few goats grazed in the distance, but none seemed interested in him. He found a tree, flopped down in the shade, and took a breather.

After a short rest, Lin thought about the mission given by the system. Damn, where I thought I would have all the milf in the world. But instead of having milf i am running after goats. He let out a sigh.

After a little rest, he thought it was time to test his headbutt finally. He wasn’t a total newbie—just two days ago, he’d taken a few headbutts himself. He eyed his target, locked in, and even though filled with hesitation, he charged at the tree.

BAM. Lin hit a tree, head spinning. But he didn’t waste time. He got up and tried again. And again. After nearly fifteen headbutts, he couldn’t continue—his head was throbbing.

The rest of the day passed like that. The next morning, he woke to the same strange scent in the air, stronger than ever. He ignored it and focused on his training. This time, as he kept racking up steps, a notification appeared:

────────── [Hidden Achievement Unlocked!] Congratulations, user! You have completed 5,000 strength steps for three consecutive days.

Reward: 10 exp

He stared at the notification, a tired grin spreading across his face. Finally, something’s paying off. Ten experience points. The system said he’d need a hundred to level up. Not so far off, now. If he kept this up, he’d hit that milestone soon enough.

But that wasn’t the only thing on his mind. The system also mentioned upgrading skills with experience. He wasn’t sure how it worked—he hadn’t even tried. Should I try to upgrade something?

Lin didn’t hesitate. He opened his status window. Only one skill stared back at him: Blood Resilience.

Lin ignored all the information and focused on the little green arrow pointing upward. He squinted, then concentrated on the arrow.

A system notification appeared before him:

───────────────────────────── Do you want to upgrade the skill “Blood Resilience” to Level 2? Cost: 5 EXP ─────────────────────────────

Five exp? That’s not bad. He hesitated, weighing his options. Should I do it? In the end, curiosity won. Let’s level up the skill.

He confirmed. Instantly, five experiences vanished from his total. Another system prompt appeared:

───────────────────────────── Congratulations! Skill “Blood Resilience” reached Level 2. ─────────────────────────────

Blood Resilience (Level 2): Deeper Recovery

Type: Physical, PassiveSkill: Basic/Level 2

Description: Your healing accelerates further. Moderate wounds (deeper cuts, torn skin) begin to close on their own, and your blood clots even under stress.

Effect:

Side Effects :

Seeing the upgraded bonus, only one thing came to Lin's mind: Hey, that’s pretty good. Now he’d be able to recover faster. But as the initial excitement faded, a chill crept in. There was a catch, a side effect, and not a small one.

If this trend continues, what happens when I upgrade this skill to higher levels? Maybe one day he’d push it so far he’d die just from hunger. How am I supposed to find a solution for that? If an overpowered skill comes with an overpowered side effect, is it even overpowered anymore?

He shook his head, a wry smile tugging at his lips. Guess that’s the price of power in this world, a little progress, a little paranoia. I’ll have to figure it out as I go. No free lunches here, not even for goats.
