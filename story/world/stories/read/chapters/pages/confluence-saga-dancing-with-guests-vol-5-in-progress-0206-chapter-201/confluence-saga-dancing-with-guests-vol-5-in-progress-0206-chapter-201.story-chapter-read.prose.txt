Imminent Nuptials … Delayed?

The arrangement between Michelle Hammerswell and Clyde Velli, previously reported as a coup for the long-absent Velli Scion, appears to have met some unforeseen roadblocks.

First an unsubstantiated rumour that Clyde may have registered a commitment prior to his absence. There are no indications as to who the alleged partner may be or whether she was even aware of the situation.

Next a public sighting of Michelle attended by a very attentive partner, identity impossible to discern.

Then an anonymous statement that she was never consulted prior to the agreement being formalized and that her interests lie elsewhere?

Is there any future where the pair are together? Will details emerge that force a change of plans, or at least of the contracted allowances?

Spokespeople for both Families have refused to comment in any way.

Seb pretended to hide his smile behind his coffee. He could feel Anya trying not to burst out laughing at the shock coming from Gwen and Cara. The family visit had been unusual, even in comparison to the tension surrounding Lys's outburst last time.

Gwen and Cara had decided to avoid the arrivals, supposedly to avoid the risk of crossing paths with Bern or her parents, but from the looks of things it had been to let Cara perfect Gwen's hairstyle. Then everyone had been late because Sandra had been required to get George to agree she was completely healed, for the third time. Finally, to hear Fay describe things, the Doorkeeper had been sluggish and asked questions it should have known the answer to.

So instead of having a few hours to socialize and catch up before eating they'd gone straight to the dining hall. Gwen and Cara hadn't even managed to sit down before Briana had turned to Anya and asked how long they'd taken.

"How… What..?" Gwen said with a hint of panic. "We… you…"

"What gave it away?" Cara asked.

"It was obvious," Briana answered.

You told them, Cara sent. You had to have.

"What was obvious?" Fay asked.

You were saying? Seb sent.

"Fay…" Bri said. "Just look at them, they're both relaxed, both smiling. Gwen's hair is freshly styled and they're sitting closer together than they have in the past, without it looking like Cara is only there to make sure she can protect Gwen."

"Her hair is nice," Fay said. "They just look happy."

"They're a couple."

"They- Oh!"

Is she right? Gwen sent.

Well… Anya sent, letting her thoughts linger on the word until Seb frowned to avoid blushing.

Probably, he sent. It was fairly obvious the first few mornings, but then again we-

Seb!

"Right after we went back into the Depths," Anya said.

"And Fay never noticed?" Briana asked.

"We weren't trying to hide anything," Cara said.

But you also never told her, Anya sent. You hugged her, you thanked her for advice about your Gifts, you both gossiped with her, but you didn't-

I thought she realized, Cara answered.

Seb frowned, letting them all feel his disbelief. He'd seen their behaviour shifts around others. They'd been more cautious for the first few weeks around Raoul and Sigrid, they were more reserved in classes. They didn't go out of their way to hide their relationship, but they weren't nearly as visibly affectionate if there was the slightest chance someone might see them.

"We…" Gwen began then set her coffee aside and took a breath. "You have to understand there will be opinions about us. There will be criticisms and judgments."

"From who?" Fay asked. "Why?"

"My family," Gwen said.

Seb felt Anya start to tense, saw Briana frown, saw Fay give the smallest of nods.

"I'm a Fairy," Cara said. "The Raex family doesn't care what arrangements you make, who you grow to love, even if the partner is a human. Fairies however are…"

"Most Fairies are retainers or servants to the Royal Families." Gwen said as Cara hesitated. "Certain members have strong opinions."

"Your parents?" Fay asked.

Seb felt the shared surprise at Fay moving to that conclusion so quickly.

"Lady Phila," Gwen said. "She-"

"If she says anything," Anya snarled. "If she…"

"You can't face her," Gwen said. "None of us can, not yet."

"She won't," Cara said. "We won't give her the chance."

"Enough," Gwen said. "She isn't important."

"So other than your relationship that Fay completely missed," Briana said. "What else is new?"

"Nothing we didn't already tell Fay on the last visit," Anya said. "Other than finally pushing through to level 16."

"Congratulations," Briana said with a smile.

"Are you going to keep Diving past the reset?" Fay asked.

"From what bit Fay has said about Raoul and Sigrid I suspect they'll push for you to continue," Briana said.

She remembered our comments about them but didn't notice? Cara sent.

Apparently, Seb answered.
