Brown eyes glared at me, but I continued.

“She stayed true to her path, and held till dawn. It was inspiring, and should be talked about for a long time. Maybe even added to the library, so future generations remember.”

Both her parents paused as they turned to her at once.

She quickly stepped forward and grabbed their hands. “That’s not how it happened…”

Sile and my dad being in the same space worried me for some reason. Not to mention, we’d left him with Hai at the Runic Challenge. Hopefully, he broke through to the next rank.

[Sovereigns Call. Current Pledges: Dengu, Lenna, Strange, Kabi, Holls, John, Abby, Saevel, Vienna.]

Between Havi, and I, that shouldn’t be a problem.
