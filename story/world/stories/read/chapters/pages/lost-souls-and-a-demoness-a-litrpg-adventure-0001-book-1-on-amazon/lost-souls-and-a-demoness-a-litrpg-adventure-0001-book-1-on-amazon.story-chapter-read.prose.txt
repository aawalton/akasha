Release day is finally here! It's been a long journey, but book one of Lost Souls and a Demoness has now launched on Amazon and Audible! Click the link above to check out the new and improved (and professionally edited) version of book one!

When I started this book I was just writing something that I enjoyed. I thought that taking a succubus, traditionally either an antagonistic or comic relief style character, and turning her into the protagonist of an adventure story would be a new take on a progression fantasy novel. There were definitely some kinks along the way, like when the story was almost booted off the site because of the whole 'succubus protagonist' thing, but it all worked out in the end! I never imagined that so many people would enjoy reading this ridiculous story. Thanks for following Jade on her... unique... journey as a fantasy protagonist.

Book 2 is complete, and I'm about to start editing it for a proper launch as well. As for book 3, I've already started writing. It'll probably be a week or two before I'm ready to start posting chapters again, but they'll be here soon.

I've learned a ton through the process of writing my first series, and I like to think that my writing is improving as I get more experience. My goal is to make book three the best of the series. I hope you'll stick around to see the conclusion of Jade's story!
