“Get ready for the Varg experience,” he boasted. The metal gear he had strapped around his shoulder like a half chest armor began to crack and pop as electrical currents started coursing through it. That was no regular armor, it was imbued with magic….

Damn, enchanted armor? I wish that was the least of my worries, because all of a sudden, the plate on his chest began to whistle hot steam down his arm. I heard churning and clanking as the steam continued to roll down, until Varg swung his arm up, where I noticed a mech sleeve fresh out the chrome shop!

“Heheheh,” Varg chuckled as he flexed his metallic fingers. “What a perfect stage for your demise. An audience to watch your inevitable defeat. It’s time for your last showdown, scumbag.”

‘Nolan, what’s the plan?!’ I asked him privately, but Varg made a comment that mapped out our fight before Nolan could respond to me.

“Brava, Lun, you go after the dragon. I’ll take care of the bloody mercenary myself.”

Brava the bunny nodded, Lun then following her as she walked over to me with her bow already cocked and loaded.

“This should be quick,” she said, eyeing her arrow and then turning her gaze toward me. It was clear in her eyes that she thought of me as nothing more than a nuisance, a mere obstacle between herself and the bounty on Nolan’s head. Lun hissed at me, his lizard tongue flicking out from between his jagged teeth. His eyes were cold and calculating, sizing me up with a predator’s gaze that triggered me to spring into action.

But as soon as I did that, Brava fired her arrow.

Nolan was quick with the counter, shooting right through the shaft of Brava’s arrow. She yelped, not expecting Nolan to pivot his attention from Varg to save me. Varg reacted to the save, charging right at Nolan. But his shot was the distraction I needed to escape.

There was no way I could fight a level 19 and level 17 bandit on my own without strategizing first! So I dashed off as fast as I could, not realizing the lizard was right on my tail. By the time I looked over my shoulder it was too late; Lun had summoned a bolt of bright yellow energy at me.

I was tossed right into a house, with my body tearing clean through the front door. All I could hear was the sound of my belly bopping on the table as I flew, knocking plates and breakfast on the floor. Shrill cries from the three women inside filled my ears as I continued to tumble, and there was so much noise bombarding me all at once. I tried to shake it off until they started throwing crap at me, from baskets to books and everything in between.

Ticked, I gave them a glimpse of what I was made of, belching out a burst of fiery breath. The flames roared and danced across the room, but did not singe a single thing. The magic was carefully controlled, but their screams amplified as I made a show. Riddled with terror, they didn’t know where to put themselves, bumping into each other before they finally scrambled to hide behind their couch.

I killed the flames and quickly looked for an exit. If I stayed here long, Lun and Brava would find me. Unfortunately, I’d run out of time. The ground beneath me started to tremble. The vibrations intensified until it felt like an earthquake was about to split the ground open. With no time to plan a defensive strategy, I looked over to the fireplace to hide. In there, I should be immune to the flames, and to my surprise I was.

The second I ducked inside, the floorboards cracked open, and a geyser spat out! It was so strong and fast that it tore right through the ceiling, prompting the girls to escape through the open windows.

“Come out, you little runt!” Lun retorted as he stepped inside. His partner in crime came in soon after with her bow engaged, and I needed to think of a plan quickly before they cornered me.

“Where did that baby dragon go?” Brava muttered to herself as she searched.

“Hold on, let me grab my seer charm,” Lun said as he reached into his inventory, but I wouldn’t give him a chance to spot me. I triggered my [Cyclone Shield] and took one big leap out from hiding, the flames of the fireplace whirling around me like a blazing tornado. The rapid rotation of flecked flames created a barrier around me, though I didn’t plan on using it as a shield. Instead, I used my cyclone skill as a defensive play to separate the two, Lun and Brava clinging onto the wall as they struggled to keep their feet grounded.

“Lun, do something!” Brava exclaimed as she shielded her face with her arm. Finally she lost grip and her hand slipped, and I sent the bunny girl flying through the torn roof.

She wailed, Lun turning toward her to attempt a save, but I summoned my [Meteor Strike], sending large chunks of molten rock down on the lizard wizard.

“My, you’re a clever little runt, aren’t you?!” he growled, and then his grip on his scepter tightened, the orb on the crown of it beginning to glow. All of a sudden, an arctic wind blew fiercely through the remains of the house, so cold I could see my breath. The searing heat of my fire barrier met his freezing blizzard, a violent clash of temperatures that caused steam to envelop us completely.

“Don’t underestimate me!” Lun shouted, his voice echoing around the steaming room. I couldn’t see him, not until the steam began to clear.

Not giving him a moment to collect himself, I unleashed another hell burp, catching Lun off guard. I nearly nipped him, but he was fast enough to whip his scepter again, summoning a hexagon grid shield to absorb my flames.

“Wha…” he stuttered, his glassy eyes huge. “What in the great god of Alutus are you?!” He was stumped. “You look like a frost dragon, but you have wind magic and flame magic. It’s not everyday you see a hybrid dragon!”

Lun was right. My hybrid nature was uncommon. However, it did give me a huge advantage, my versatile powers unexpected and hard to counter.

“No matter. You’re still no match for me! You’re a mere child compared to a master of the arcane arts! Even so, I should probably [Inspect] you and see what I’m up against here. You might be a… w-w-wait a flippin’ minute, a level 19 Dracapod?!?”

He squawked, his eyes practically bulging out of his head. His breath hitched in surprise, the lizard boy barely able to contain himself. “But you’re just a hatchling! How is this possible?! How are you stronger than me?!”

He glared down at me.

“This doesn’t make any sense! I am the strongest mage of the Empora District! I have trained under the guidance of nobles, mastering every arcane discipline there is!”

Nobles? Well how the hell did he become a bounty hunter?

“And yet a mere child outmatches me?! This cannot be!”

While he was trying to wrap his head around the concept of me being stronger than him, I thought about an idea. If he was as strong as he said he was, maybe I could induct him into my thrall.

Making a powerful mage my servant was a no brainer, the only question was, could I subdue him?

Lun bellowed as his breakdown continued. It was obvious that he thought very highly of himself and his expertise, which made me think that his willpower and charisma might be high. Though, it might not be as high as mine to overpower my [Conquer].

There was literally no downside to trying, so as he continued to rage, I took a step forward and began the process.

“I will not be mocked by a hatchling!” he blared, before the unfiltered fury on his scaly face began to subside, the lizard mage catching onto what I was doing.

All of a sudden, his pupils dilated, and his body stiffened. His chest began heaving, with his nostrils flaring as he harshly sucked in air.

“You trying to make an underling out of me?!” he muttered under his breath coldly. “You think you can control the might of a great wizard?!” His scales began to glow, a shimmering pattern of arcane symbols flowing across them like liquid fire.

“Not today, whelp! Not ever!” His grip on the scepter tightened and the head of it blazed brilliantly with a vibrant, indigo light. I stopped trying to [Conquer] him—it was clear that he either had an item that protected him, or that he had a skill scroll that negated my mental domination.

The world around us seemed to disappear as blinding light from his scepter filled the room. I could sense a surge of arcane energy pulsating from it, scaling the room with an electrifying charge. He began to cackle like a mad man just as the first stray spark of blue electricity began to fly. They went everywhere, without direction or purpose. At this point the asshole was just flaunting his strength, making me bob and weave between his shots until all of that stopped the moment Varg bulldozed through the west wall.

Chunks of wood panels and dust filled the air, my instant reaction to move back bringing me closer to the east wall. Fortunately for me, Lun got caught in Varg’s crosshairs, the weight of the bull knocking him out and tossing his scepter across the floor. Nolan was on top of Varg wrestling him down, the concept making me do a double take. Varg was easily five times Nolan’s size, so the scene was hard to process. Until I realized what Nolan was wearing—it was the loot we’d gotten for beating the mind sever in the tier 2 dungeon:

-Armor of Invulnerability: provides resistance to non magical damage-

-Tunshra Gauntlets: when worn, provides boost in physical strength-

“You feeble human!” Varg growled in a taxing breath as he struggled to break free from Nolan. “Using an item is the only way you could even begin to challenge my natural strength!”

Nolan smirked. “Whatever gets the job done, try-hard.”

His retort earned him a loud bellow from Varg, the depth of his voice literally rocking the floor. The bull’s muscles flexed, and for a moment I thought he would break free from Nolan’s hold. But the bandit held strong, his gauntlets glowing with a soft white light.

Nolan bore his fingers into his knuckles, Varg’s cry prompting me to scurry across the other side of the room for the scepter. I snatched it with my mouth and then turned over my shoulder to witness Nolan feeding Varg round after round of hook jabs into his face.

The scene turned bloody real quick. Blood spurted from Varg’s nose, coating Nolan’s gauntlets in a dark red sheen. Each punch landed with the brutal force of a hammer, transforming the bull’s menacing features into something grotesque. A sickening crunch echoed around the room with each strike, drowning out everything else around us. Varg’s eyes became two swollen orbs barely visible through the slits that were once his eyelids, bruised violet and puffy red.

The bounty hunter’s fur was wet from warm splats of his own blood. Nolan threw one final punch to Varg’s jaw, making the bull collapse onto the floor like a sack of potatoes. The Armor of Invulnerability and Tunshra Gauntlets were definitely solid dungeon souvenirs, and this beat down was a testament to the great power they had.

Breathing heavily, Nolan loosened his grip on Varg’s beaten body, stepping back with a satisfied grin on his bloody face. His chest was rising and falling rapidly as he wiped the sweat off his brow, tossing a look my way. I could see the adrenaline still coursing through his veins as he nodded proudly. Without him saying anything, I knew he was glad that I’d handled business on my own, until the scepter I was holding suddenly started tossing sparks of electricity again!

My neck swayed back and forth as the bolts of electricity fired about the room like a rave! I swear I wasn’t doing it on purpose—this must have been a residual effect to Lun’s magic. Thankfully the show was short-lived, Nolan coming back up from his crouched position to flash me a deadpan look.

‘Heh! Sorry!’

He chuckled. “Come on. Let’s tie up these pests and collect."
