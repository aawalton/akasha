Harvey was a little surprised when Alexander walked into the portal and blipped off of his program temporarily. For a full hour he could not find Alexander's whereabouts anywhere. This was alarming as he should have been able to see some of the results of the programs he initiated. He would set up a scenario and the program would take over from there and adapt the scenario for the player. It was frustrating that he didn’t have the fine-tuned controls like he did running a campaign, but there was nothing he could do about it now. He would just have to wait and see if it happened again.

Finally, he saw Alexander come stumbling out of the portal breathing heavily. He seemed to be flustered by something, If Harvey could tell that much, then Alexander must have had a truly impactful encounter.

He was sad that he missed Alexanders first real flag event, but he didn’t dwell on it long because he saw what he had cued up next on the Wizard's list.
