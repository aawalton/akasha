Lemira's lion-monster pet thing had climbed all the way up to the demon's knee at that point, digging its claws in and biting at every opportunity. It did no damage whatsoever. As far as Luke could tell, the demon hadn't even noticed the monster scaling it, since it was busy with Curtis and Hannah far above.

"My knives aren't doing shit," Ray said without coming out of stealth. "Any ideas?"

The greater demon took another half-step toward the building, its hip grazing the facade, throwing a shower of glass, metal, and stone over them. Cowering, Luke shook his head. "I'm not sure. There's one thing I could try, I think."

"What?" Ray asked. "Drain its mana?"

Luke shook his head. "That won't work. This thing's mana is like fire, and I'd have to take it into me before using it."

"Rough."

"Yeah," Luke said, hurrying forward to reach its massive foot. Reaching out with a single thread of mana, he connected himself to the demon.

"Wait," Ray called out. A moment later, he spoke again. This time, he was farther away. "Okay, go!"

"Coward," Luke grumbled as he let his consciousness follow the thread into his opponent.

"No use in both of us getting squished!"

Draining mana from the greater demon wasn't a bad idea. At that size, it was sure to have a ton of it, but even an ocean of the precious resource would run out at some point. While Luke wasn't able to straight-up take mana from it, he did have a skill that might work.

Bleed The Weave [Active - Mana Cost: High]: Rupture a weave to make your target weep mana.

The description didn't make much sense, since it didn't touch the weave itself, just the target's mana channels, but the few times he'd used it, the skill had worked well enough. Using it now, Luke found the results a little underwhelming, and that was putting it mildly. A thin stream of mana separated from the thick channel and made its way outside and into the physical realm. The resulting bleed effect looked a lot like a tiny spring emerging from the demon's red skin. At the rate it was going, it would take years to drain it all. Good thing it didn't have a cooldown.

Since the demon hadn't reacted in the slightest on the first use, Luke used it twice more, then drank a mana potion. He was running out of them at that point, but the Integrated shop wouldn't let him buy more, since he was in a combat situation.

Luke: "Hey, Ray! Do you have any mana potions? You should start making them, or maybe you don't need healing anymore?"

Ray: "That's blackmail!"

Luke: "Just the cost of doing business."

A trade window appeared in Luke's interface, along with a small pile of Integrated Shop-bought mana potions. It wasn't much, but it was better than nothing.

Ray: "I'll see about crafting some later."

Luke: "I knew you'd see reason."

After a couple more uses of Bleed The Weave, Luke's mana was getting a little low, so he stopped to let it regenerate. Spots of darkness and shadow warred with relentless punches and even spell-casting from the demon far above.

Luke (Party): "Are you okay up there?"

Curtis (Party): "No worries."

Hannah (Party): "Curtis has almost died three times already."

Curtis (Party): "I have not... Maybe once or twice."

Ray (Party): "Why don't you kill that big bastard already?"

Hannah (Party): "Working on it."

"Luke!"

The scream came from farther up the street, and Luke recognized the voice. He turned as a blur of fur whirled past. "Lemira?"

She'd returned after moving the downed Integrated to a safer location, but now found herself in quite a situation. A male demon of average height stood right next to her, holding a sword to her neck. The demon was shirtless and looked like he spent far too much time in the gym. Even from a distance, Luke could see that this new opponent's face was too wide and filled with sharp teeth that showed through a sneer.

"Put down your weapons," the demon said in a haughty voice, its voice carrying across the distance.

"I don't have any weapons!" Luke shouted back.

Ray: "I'll deal with this."

Luke: "Don't let that asshole hurt her."

The demon leaned over to Lemira and said something Luke couldn't catch, then drew back the hand holding the sword.

"No!" Luke shouted.

Lemira fell back with a yelp, hitting the pavement with a thud as the demon's thin sword whizzed through the air and over her head. The surprise was evident in the demon's expression, but it didn't last long. A shockwave exploded outward from it, pushing everything, including Lemira, away. She rolled with a grunt but summoned her llama and mounted it with what looked like practiced ease just as her lion closed the distance and barreled into the demon.

A thick arrow came out of nowhere, and Luke, on pure reflex, slapped it to the side with a thread of mana, diverting its trajectory so it struck his arm rather than the center of his chest. The incredible force of the projectile meant it ran him straight through, leaving a hole gushing blood.

Turning to look up at a nearby building, Luke saw the source of the attack. Another demon. This one was tall and lithe, with much more of an hourglass figure. Clad in nothing more than bits of leather, this one showed even more that made Luke sure the archer-demon was female, and by that, he didn't mean its long hair. She loosed another arrow, and this one proved even faster than the last, forcing Luke to the side.

Since Ray, Lemira, and her pet lion had taken on the swordsman, that left Luke with the archer. A poor matchup on the best of days. Rather than speak, she attacked again and again. Each arrow would have struck him through the heart if he hadn't had threads of mana to protect himself. Even then, he got hit with another, this time through the leg. The thin nature of arrows was a small blessing. Since the holes they left were small, he could heal them without spending too much mana.

Charging forward, he dodged side to side, running with irregular speed and in scattered directions. Even then, the arrows flew at him with precision and care. His approach didn't seem to spook the demon at all, not even when he pushed off and leapt halfway up the three-story building using threads of mana and Weavestep to give himself a boost.

Last time he'd scaled a building, he'd been forced to use his threads to find places to hold. Now, he forced Needle of Life into something akin to ice picks and used several of those to climb up at a speed that impressed even himself.

His opponent, however, remained unimpressed. A veritable storm of arrows rained down on him as he climbed. It was a pain. From that angle, her main target was his head, and Luke really didn't want to experience what it would be like to have an arrow enter his skull and then penetrate all the way through. He wasn't sure if the entrance or exit would be the most unpleasant, but he decided he didn't need to find that out. Instead, he formed a sort of roof over his head with threads of mana, weaving two slanting planes. When an arrow hit, it ricocheted off to the side rather than penetrating. All those threads were a bitch to keep track of, but even in that dire situation, Luke was happy to see the system recognizing just how much he was pushing himself.

Boon of Potential grants you an attribute point (+1 Willpower).

Boon of Potential grants you an attribute point (+1 Focus).

When he reached the lip of the roof and hauled himself over, Luke couldn't help but groan. The demon was gone. Another arrow struck him right in the hip, and Luke cried out in pain, stumbling to the side to catch a glimpse of his opponent on top of the next building over. This one was even taller.

"Fucking fucker," he grumbled, pulling the arrow out of the hip bone using both his hands and a set of threads. The hip throbbed even after he closed the wound, but this was not the time to waste mana by converting it to healing mana or letting the stores in his Meta-Heart do their thing. This was far from over, and he guessed this wouldn't be the last injury he suffered.

"Get over here!" Luke shouted up at the archer demon, shaking his fist like an angry old man yelling at a child for trespassing in his yard.

Rather than answer, the archer just loosed another arrow his way. This one split into three, and those three split into three each, and then again. It drew the mind to Mateo's thrown spear attack.

"Well, shit," Luke muttered as he weaved a shield that would be far from enough to protect him from the avalanche of arrows coming his way.
