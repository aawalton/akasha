It was supposed to be an easy Dive. We'd been down to the 14th at least a dozen times. We were only a single level up, we weren't taking any risks. We'd added a combat healer a month earlier, we had a full squad of five. We knew the mess slimes made, we knew the rabbits were little more than pests, we'd studied all the usual green vermin formations.

So when they asked if we'd explore a new branch, if we wanted first pass at unexplored caverns, all fees waived, we didn't hesitate to say yes. Fifteen percent doesn't sound too bad, until you realize how much more you'll make keeping it and staying down there for twice as long.

The slimes we avoided as usual, the piks, the dagger wielding rat-like bipeds should have been our first warning. But they weren't any more dangerous than the rabbits so we didn't think anything of it. We couldn't find the green vermin.

That should have been our cue to leave, or at least be more careful. There are always vermin of some sort. We managed to find them eventually. They weren't green and they weren't small. They fought like the goblins, but they were as big as a grown man, as strong as one too.

We managed to run away when we felt the ground shake. We found out later there were even bigger ones, easily twice our size with clubs that might as well have been trees. We got away, the next group lost half their number and swore to never go back.
