Getting to the planet’s surface only grew more challenging, with the fire and ice intensifying each moment.  Kyle expected that he’d be traversing a space between two separate but passive domains, and his current circumstances couldn’t be different.  It was as if the competing powers desired to transform the entire planet, yet found themselves evenly matched against one another.  Why that meant hurling fire and ice at one another, he didn’t know.  That was a question that could wait for his down time.

Kyle kept Repel active all around him as they descended; slowing their speed, but rebuffing many of the weaker shards of ice and blasts of flame.  He still needed to take a more active approach against the more intense bursts, though with C.H.A.D.D.’s help they navigated the elemental battlefield with relative ease.

Still, as they drew nearer to the surface, the waves of heat and cold only grew more deadly.  Fire met flurries of frost, and for a fleeting moment Kyle was concerned that there wouldn’t be a safe place for them to settle in.  However, after a particularly intense blast of flame that required a blast of Repel to disperse, he saw the space marked on his map.  A narrow crevice was open in the ground below, the crack in the land providing a modicum of shelter against the warring elements.

[THAT WAS QUITE THE JOURNEY, DR. MAYHEW,] C.H.A.D.D. noted as they nestled inside the crevice.

“Definitely an interesting environment,” Kyle agreed, commanding the vines to unbind the probe and hold it out in front of him.  It highlighted a non-descript piece of the stone nearby, and having no other instructions, Kyle pressed the probe into the stone.  Metal prongs extended, biting into the crevice, and he could feel it drawing in gentle amounts of ambient energy.

“Is it working?” Kyle asked, pulling up his nav bracelet to check the job status.  Seeing it note him as being in process, he leaned back against the stone wall, surveying the space.  There wasn’t much room for him to settle in, but with some creative application of vines, he could make a reasonable shelter.  Of course, that could wait until after he got some much-desired training in.

“I know we put Graft pretty high on the list, but I’d like to get a better sense of what’s happening on the surface.”

[IT IS CERTAINLY AN INTERESTING ENVIRONMENT.  I AM CURIOUS ABOUT THE NATURE OF THE CLASHING HEAT AND COLD.  I AM CONFIDENT THAT THERE IS SOMETHING UNNATURAL AT PLAY HERE, THOUGH I AM NOT SURE WHAT IT IS.]

It was a sentiment Kyle agreed with wholeheartedly.  The heat and cold felt like they were more than just opposing natural forces; rather being actively antagonistic toward one another.  Activating Identify as he climbed back toward the world’s surface, he focused on the thick spears of ice flying toward him, trying to understand the concepts and intent behind them.  There was water and cold, both of which he understood, but something else underpinned them.  Something he couldn’t quite place.

Fortunately, he had time.  He pulled out an additional layer of protection made of reinforced stonebloom material designed to cover the C.H.A.D.D. pack, then willed his Ferric Augmentation to expand.  Exhaling slowly as spears of ice crumbled against his reinforced body, Kyle slowly pushed outward with Repel, gently activating Heart of Creation to empower the skill and weave in the concepts of Adaptation and Growth.  He needed Repel to become more flexible, and this was the best way he knew how.

Days turned into weeks as Kyle waited for the data collection from the probe to finish.  The bulk of his time was spent training his skills against the harsh environments, though he committed to avoiding Storm Shelter, instead forcing himself to continue using Repel or Cultivate to deflect the larger chunks of ice or particularly potent gouts of flame.

The evenings were in turn spent focusing on the Graft skill and communicating with Jarberry and Suierrillax about his next Tribunal meeting; something that was taking longer than expected to schedule.  Apparently, there was a shortage of special investigators that were either willing or interested in taking the case, something Kyle suspected Casiniax knew and was taking advantage of.

Fortunately, he had a play of his own.  Kyle, C.H.A.D.D., Jarberry, and Suierrillax were in agreement that one of the objectives of this Tribunal was to keep Kyle occupied and away from Kertan Six.  While they didn’t yet know why that was important, Suierrillax filed Kyle’s formal interest in taking part in the auction for the planet.  While he didn’t expect to actually compete for ownership, his involvement meant that the auction itself couldn’t take place until after Kyle’s Tribunal was completed.  Until then, Kertan Six was under strict Collective supervision; even having been moved to a space near the Hub for better monitoring.

That naturally let Kyle spend more time and focus on his training, and the efforts bore fruit.  The first benefit came in the form of levels.  While the zone between the opposing elemental forces was rife with conflict between the fire and ice, the environment itself was relatively temperate.  It was the perfect place for him to practice with Repel, and he still spent several hours each day pushing the skill in an effort to better control its trajectory.  Progress was slow, but by slowly rotating his body as he activated the skill, he was gaining a better grasp of the mechanics each day.

By contrast, moving deeper into either zone came with fewer of the random attacks, trading them for more extreme climes.  Kyle rotated between molten fields of magma to blizzards cold enough to cause frost to instantly spread across his body.  All the while, he channeled Adaptive Regeneration and focused on the concepts of Adaptation and Immutability.  Occasionally, he would need to activate Healing Impetus or Genesis Engine, but he wanted to lean on his body’s natural abilities as much as possible.  He was beginning to hit the point of diminishing returns, but after so long without any real strain, the growth was staggering.

The focus was simple.  Kyle wanted to forge his body to handle any of the chaos that the world could throw at him, and this gave him a chance to further lean into both of the concepts that defined his path.  As his understanding grew, so too did his body’s ability to handle the environments.  That, in turn, allowed him to push his limits even further, allowing for more growth.

Finally, during one particularly cold meditation session, Kyle felt himself break through to the level he’d been waiting for.  With a smile on his face, he focused on the image of his center, and the orange orb signifying an available skill upgrade.

KYLE MAYHEW.  UNION SYMBIOTE. UNBROKEN SURVIVOR.  LEVEL 30 (C)

SECOND CLASS: WORLD TREE TENDER.  LEVEL 46 (E)

PATH SYNERGY: 97%

DEVOURER’S ANATOMY – ENHANCED CARAPACE (FERRIC AUGMENTATION), AURIC PERCEPTION (SYNAPTIC BARBS), EXOSKELETAL STRENGTH (MUSCULAR COMPRESSION), CEASELESS VITALITY (TENACIOUS VIGOR), INSTINCTIVE INTELLIGENCE (PACK LEADER’S INSTINCT), COVERT DEXTERITY (REFLEXIVE EVASION), UNBREAKABLE WILL (HEART OF CREATION)

VITALITY: 2925

ENDURANCE: 3850

STRENGTH: 1153

DEXTERITY: 2070

WILLPOWER: 4733

INTELLIGENCE: 3643

PERCEPTION: 2878

FREE ATTRIBUTES: 50

Allocating his free attributes toward Vitality, he focused his attention on his skill upgrade.  Excited to see how his efforts bore fruit, he selected Repel, and reviewed the first option available to him.

SHEARPOINT BURST

As Kyle focused on the possible upgrade, he quickly understood that this was a fundamental upgrade to Repel in almost every way.  The amount of mana the skill could contain was significantly higher, its range was longer, and it could be focused even more narrowly, maintaining that focus for longer.  It did look like it would take slightly longer to charge, though he expected with practice he would be able to reduce that drawback.

All in all, it was a solid upgrade choice.  Yet, Kyle found himself somewhat disappointed.  It felt like none of the various alterations he’d made to the skill carried into this upgrade naturally, and if he selected it, they would need to be figured out once again.  Hoping he’d see something different, he looked to the next option.

AURA FORCE

This skill was much more interesting, and Kyle felt his heart leap as he understood what this one provided.  It was a true domain skill.  He’d found it interesting during his skirmishes with various C Grades that almost all of them had some sort of wide area ability, and Repel was the skill he’d been hoping to upgrade into his own.  From the impression he got, he would be able to project his mana into the air around him, grabbing control of the space and allowing him to use any point within his control as an origin for Repel.

The drawbacks were the massively increasing mana costs to expand out to larger ranges, as well as the inefficiency of creating blasts of force as he moved farther away from his body.  But still, it was a true domain.  The limitations could be shored up as he gained power, and the flexibility it offered was nearly unparalleled.  Anything or anybody within range would have a much harder time trying to close in on him, and the ability to project attacks from any angle opened up a wide array of new possibilities.

Kyle was tempted to grab the skill right away, but he at least wanted to see the final offering before making up his mind.

COMPRESSION BOMB

This… was not a direction Kyle expected.  Instead of the larger blasts that he’d been using, this upgrade would instead focus the force of Repel into tiny points, entering a target before exploding with massive force.  From the impression he had, the offensive power was magnified several times over, at the cost of overall flexibility.  It was an interesting option, and the best application Kyle could see would be to channel the skill through vines connected to the World Tree Seed to unleash some devastating effects.  Ultimately, however, he knew the skill wasn’t for him.

He'd wanted a domain skill to better control the space around him, and to shore up some of Storm Shelter’s shortcomings; namely its lack of mobility.  If he could use specially grown constructs in conjunction with barriers of force and his own resilience against most attacks, he could reserve Storm Shelter for truly dire situations.  Moreover, he could continue to guide that upgrade path in a direction that would capitalize on its greatest strengths.

With two skills upgraded to C Grade, he gave some thought toward his planned sequence.  Parasitic Resonance was the next clear upgrade, offering him the single greatest increase in offensive power.  Coupled with the domain from Aura Force and the significant boost from Genesis Engine, Kyle’s power as an early E Grade would be leagues ahead of most of his contemporaries.  However, moving into mid-tier C Grade offered a few different paths forward.  He was almost certain that Identify and Iron Will would wait until high-tier C Grade before they were upgraded, which left him Adaptive Regeneration, Healing Impetus, and Storm Shelter for the next step of his journey.

Both of his healing skills were already top-notch in Kyle’s opinion, and their synergy with one another made them worth more than the sum of their parts.  At the same time, an upgrade wouldn’t necessarily make a world of difference for them.  Increased potency or other secondary effects could be helpful, but the core functions of each skill were what made them so useful to Kyle.  That meant he would likely choose Storm Shelter as his Level 61 upgrade, and then choose one of the two healing abilities at Level 90.  That left the final healing skill for Level 120, with Iron Will available as soon as he crossed the threshold into high-tier at Level 121.

The only problem Kyle saw with that sequence was that his combat ability would effectively stall between Level 61 and Level 121.  There were certainly worse things in the world, but it was a problem that warranted further consideration.  Fortunately, he had time.

[DID YOUR SKILL SELECTION GO WELL, DR. MAYHEW?]  C.H.A.D.D. asked.

“It did,” Kyle confirmed.  “And now it’s time to see how it works.”
