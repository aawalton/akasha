Callie looked around the swamp, trying to find the source of the voice. A shimmer of green scales between the trees, a tip of the tail sticking from within the bushes, or a pair of amber eyes observing from afar.

Her heart was beating like crazy from both excitement and fear. Could that have been the dragon?

"What's wrong, Callie?" Callen called.

She threw another careful look around, but there was no sign of anything anywhere nearby. Everything was completely still.

"Brother, did you hear a voice?" Callie asked.

"A voice? No. It's just the two of us here," Callen said.

But Callie was certain that she had heard it. A deep, low voice that seemed to carry wisdom.

Do not trust the still waters.

It felt like a warning. Were the still waters supposed to be the water of the swamp? Did the dragon or the arcane beast that told her the warning want her to leave?

Callie looked at the mushrooms she had collected. Would this be enough for her brother? It didn't seem like enough. But ignoring the warning felt foolish. Certainly, nothing good would happen if she chose to proceed deeper into the swamp.

"Brother, let's go home," Callie said as she slowly made her way back to Callen.

"What? Why all of a sudden?" Callen asked.

"I heard a voice," Callie said, watching her step carefully, all too aware how easy it would be to fall into the mire.

"A voice?" Callen narrowed his eyes. "Like that of Wiggles?"

"Yes. It said not to trust still waters. I… I think we should leave."

Callie finally reached her brother, who outstretched his arms and pulled her to safety.

"How many mushrooms did you find?" He asked.

She opened the satchel, showing him her modest findings. He frowned.

"I don't think that's going to be enough."

"I know… But I feel that if I don't listen to the voice, something bad is going to happen. What if it's the dragon warning me?"

Callen scratched his head, looking torn, but after a while, he nodded.

"Okay, if that's how you feel, then let's go back. We did get some mushrooms at the very least."

Callie sighed in relief. She didn't know what she would do if her brother objected. Frankly, she felt bad. What would happen now? This surely wouldn't be enough for Callen to get his new coat. Would Mom and Dad insist on collecting frostmoth cocoons?

Is there something else I can do to get money for the family? Or perhaps there's a safer place to search?

As they slowly made their way out of the swamp, Callie kept her eyes peeled to see if there were other places where the mushrooms could grow. She thought carefully about the hole where she found the mushrooms. Why had they grown exactly inside of it, instead of outside?

It had muddy soil, saturated with swamp waters. These mushrooms undoubtedly needed incredibly moist places to grow. But moisture was everywhere in the swamp, yet these mushrooms only grew in crevices. Perhaps, they required complete darkness, too.

Moisture and darkness…

"Brother, could we do something else before we go home?" Callie asked, an idea popping into her mind.

Callen looked at her skeptically. "Did you hear another voice?"

"No, I didn't! I think we should ask for help first before giving up," Callie suggested.

"Callie, Mom said that you shouldn't get close to any arcane beasts." Callen stopped and crossed his arms over his chest, showing that he wasn't supporting her idea.

"It's Snowglow! She already gave me a petal. It should be safe to approach her," Callie argued.

"Snowglow… It was a glimmerhare, wasn't it? Are you sure we can trust it?" Callen arched an eyebrow.

"She! Snowglow is a girl! And yes, we can trust her. Although…" Callie looked her brother up and down. He was large, tall, and in Dad's coat looked a bit scary. "Maybe it's better if you wait here."

"I'm not letting you go alone!"

"But you're going to scare Snowglow with your… largeness. Just stay out of sight, okay? Maybe we can still get more mushrooms," Callie said.

"Callie, what if it, I mean, she leads you somewhere dangerous?" Callen asked.

"Snowglow would never do that! What purpose would that even serve?" Callie narrowed her eyes at her brother.

"I don't know… Maybe to eat us?" Callen suggested, but then laughed at his own words. "She eats vegetables, doesn't she?"

"Snowglow loves carrots! Now, let's see if I can find her," Callie said, walking ahead.

Callen did follow her, but he stayed some distance behind her as Callie called for Snowglow.

"Snowglow! Snowglow!" Callie called while she tried to focus on finding magic.

It was difficult to reach for magic at first. It seemed evasive and didn't want to do what Callie wanted. But she did her best to remain calm while also focusing on Snowglow. What feeling did she need to reach the glimmerhare? What did she feel when she grew the dandilynx flower? It was happiness, was it not?

She tried to latch onto the feeling and guide it to find Snowglow. Her magic reacted, albeit it was far from stable. Sporadically, she would get a strong sense of which direction to go to find Snowglow. But most of the time, her magic remained unruly, just roiling inside her, unsure of where to go.

Thankfully, it was enough to guide her in the right direction. Frankly, she didn't even need magic, because she found the glimmerhare not too far from the dragon's cave.

Callie?

Callie looked around but didn't see the glimmerhare anywhere. She must be hiding because Callen was nearby.

"Can you come out, Snowglow? I have something I want to ask you. The man with me is my brother. He won't harm you, I promise," Callie said.

For a moment, there was no response, so Callie worried that Snowglow was too scared to come out. But then she felt something press against her leg. Looking down, she saw it was Snowglow.

Since the forest was covered in a layer of snow, Snowglow's white coat blended in perfectly. Not to mention that the fuzz on her antlers had also become lighter. Not quite white, but rather a light wood color. If she were to sit still next to a tree, then people would undoubtedly mistake her antlers for small branches.

"Hi, Snowglow. You're so well hidden, I didn't even see you," Callie said and crouched down to pat the glimmerhare. Her fur seemed much fluffier and thicker than before. It was a bit thinner on her previously injured leg, but even that was almost back to normal.

Hide well!

Snowglow raised her head proudly. Callie wondered whether her brother had even noticed that the glimmerhare had appeared by her side.

"Can I ask you something?" Callie said, although she kept petting Snowglow. She was too soft for Callie to resist.

Yes.

She reluctantly withdrew her hand and reached for her satchel to show the mushrooms she had collected. "I'm looking for these kinds of mushrooms. I found some in the swamp, but it's too dangerous there. Do you perhaps know some other places where they grow?"

Snowglow looked at the mushroom, then stuffed her little nose into the satchel, sniffing.

See these somewhere.

"Really? Where?"

In cave. Underground.

Callie deflated. How was she supposed to get into small, narrow underground caves, which likely were too small for Callie to get through?

"Can you lead me there?" Callie asked, hoping that perhaps there was a chance for her.

Not remember where. Must ask others.

Callie felt her heart flutter with joy. "Others? You mean other glimmerhares?"

Yes.

"Can I meet them?"

Snowglow looked at Callie, then she looked in the direction where Callen was.

Big man stay. He scary.

Callie glanced at Callen, who was standing dutifully and waiting for Callie to finish.

"Brother, can you wait there?" Callie asked.

"What? Where are you going? No way!" Callen said, taking a step towards them.

Snowglow jumped back a few steps, clearly frightened.

"Brother, no! You will frighten them. Glimmerhares know where to find the mushrooms,"  Callie said.

"I can't leave you alone with them. What if they lead you astray?" Callen said, taking another step closer.

Snowglow looked like she would bolt away at any moment. They had only one chance to gather the mushrooms. Dad made it abundantly clear that there would be no others. Callie had to try her best.

"Brother, please! I'm trying to help you. Please, trust me," Callie said.

"I trust you, but I don't trust them!"

"But I do trust Snowglow, and the other glimmerhares. They are kind creatures. Just because we don't understand them, it doesn't mean we have to be afraid of them," Callie reasoned.

At these words, Snowglow seemed to calm a little, and she hopped closer to Callie.

Callen looked at Callie, his expression pained. He rubbed at his chin and then pulled on his hair.

"Brother, please. I am safer together with Snowglow rather than in some swamp. I will be alright. Just give me a little time," Callie urged.

Callen grit his teeth and finally sighed. "Fine, fine! I'll trust you. You have half an hour. After that, I'm going after you!"

"I'll be sure to return quickly!" Callie cheered and turned to Snowglow. "Lead the way."

Snowglow assessed Callie, looking her up and down, then hopped towards the direction of the dragon cave.

Come. Callie should fit.

Callie was a bit surprised that they were going to the dragon's cave. No, they were actually going inside the cave. It was strange, since she didn't remember seeing any mushrooms there before, especially not glowing ones. She felt that something like that would have stayed in her memory.

But once they got deeper into the cave, Snowglow led Callie to a narrow opening. She hadn't even noticed it before, but then again, it was rather well concealed in the dark murk of the cave. It was the glow from Snowglow's antlers that made it easier to spot.

Follow, follow!

Snowglow urged, and Callie crouched down to squeeze through the opening. It was a bit difficult, especially with the coat, but Callie was just small enough to manage it.

The glimmerhare led the way, her glowing horns illuminating the passage. Good thing they did, otherwise Callie never would have crawled into such a dark, narrow space. Before long, the narrow passage led into a slightly larger cavern.

Brought friend. Everyone come. Callie nice. Callie save me.

Who was Snowglow talking to? The only thing Callie could see was darkness. But then a light appeared not too far from Callie. And then another. And another. Soon, Callie was surrounded by many lights.

Before her stood dozens of glimmerhares, all with their antlers glowing. Some of them were smaller, and their antlers were nothing more than small nubs on their head. Some were larger with huge antlers, the glow of which seemed to fill the whole cavern.

The cavern was filled with various openings, just large enough for the glimmerhares to get through. It was like a whole underground city just for them.

"Wow…" Callie exclaimed, marveling at the beauty.

The smaller ones got frightened and darted into the tunnels.

"Oh, no… I'm sorry, I didn't mean to scare you," Callie said as softly as she could and crouched low to the ground, so as not to scare the glimmerhares. "I just wanted to ask for your help."

Help? Human? Why?

One of the larger glimmerhares hopped forward, holding his antlered head high. His voice was deeper than Snowglow's and held an authority to it. Just like Dad.

Callie friend. Callie help.

Snowglow tried to appease the larger glimmerhare.

"I only need to find more of these mushrooms," Callie said, showing the mushrooms to the leader glimmerhare.

He cautiously hopped closer, examining the mushrooms.

Bitter glow mushroom. It grows lots underground.

"Really? Could you show me where?" Callie asked.

No.

"Why not?"

Human too large. Not fit.

Callie looked at the surrounding tunnels and realized that even she wouldn't be able to fit into any of them. They were just too small. But what was she supposed to do? Without that coat, her brother would be cold the whole winter.

Snowglow hopped closer to Callie.

I bring mushroom.

"Really?" Callie looked at Snowglow hopefully.

Elder, please show where!

The large glimmerhare looked at Snowglow, then at Callie. Although she couldn't understand glimmerhare expressions too well, she could tell that he was reluctant to help Callie.

Okay, but human must help us first. Human bring the orange root to the colony.

Callie looked around the cavern. There were dozens of glimmerhares here and more peeking occasionally through the tunnels. They would need a lot of carrots. Could their family spare that many? After all, they would need every bit of food they could get during winter.

Besides, she couldn't just head home and grab the carrots as she pleased. This was something she would have to discuss with her parents.

"I'll bring as many as I can," Callie promised and headed to the exit. She had to try to convince her parents to feed a colony of glimmerhares. That would not be easy.
