Deep within the Earth's dense mantle, beneath the descending layers of the most elaborate labyrinth ever constructed, Max's clawed fingers wrapped around the alien artifact. The Builder had stolen it from one tomb on Phobos and made a new one for it on Earth to get his answers on how to unlock it.  Now, the deed was done.

The metal felt impossibly cold against his enhanced skin, its surface etched with geometric patterns that seemed to shift and writhe when viewed directly.  Max's contact pads registered nothing—no electrical activity, no heat signature, no detectable energy output. His enhanced canine senses, capable of detecting electromagnetic frequencies far beyond human perception, found only silence. Yet the device sang in his mind with a symphony of alien voices.

A chorus of voices encouraged him to accept the Keldranith contract, to allow their Cooperative to access the Sol system and cleanse the corruption seeping through from that dark, spent alternate dimension. But threading between those bright voices, lurking and leaking from the subspace, were darker whispers that reeked of hunger and madness, promising power at a price his soul might not survive paying.

Max clenched his jaw, fangs grinding against each other as he engaged his acquired blocking skill with desperate force. The mental noise dimmed to a manageable level, but the pressure behind his eyes remained, a familiar migraine made of alien thought.

The familiar chime of his training program cut through the psychic static, and Max allowed the system to display the cascade of suppressed notifications that had been building during the chaos. Despite everything, he couldn't help but snort at the absurdity of gaining experience points while holding an artifact of cosmic destruction.

[Labyrinth Quest Achieved – Survive the Final Challenge Level, +1000 XP.]

[Labyrinth Bonus – No backups used in Final Challenge, +1000 XP.]

[Labyrinth Quest Epic Achievement – Defeat Atropos, +3000 XP.]

[Max Mitchell, +1 Level to Master 29, XP balance 37,150.   Bonus Skill points awarded – 10.  Auto Assign y/n?]

[Labyrinth Secret Quest Achieved – Enter the Builder’s Sanctum, +1000 XP.]

[Labyrinth Secret Quest Achieved – Learn the Fate of the Builder, +500 XP.]

[Labyrinth Secret Quest Achieved – Open the Phobos Artifact, +5000 XP.]

[Max Mitchell, +1 Level to Master 30, XP balance 40,000.   Bonus Skill points awarded – 50.  Auto Assign y/n?]

[Bonus skill points exceed available point deficit.  Skill points auto-assigned.]

[Congratulations, Trainee, your Augmentation Program is complete!  Bonus skill award is sufficient to advance all skills to maximum.    Systems are fully unlocked.  Further advancements require augmentation redesign by third-party suppliers.]

In his peripheral vision, he saw Bo's portal device hum to life beside him, casting an ethereal blue glow across the Builder's ancient sanctum.  Max dismissed the notifications with a wave as Bo's portal expanded into existence, its familiar blue radiance painting the sanctum's curved walls.

The pocket dimension beyond the growing portal looked cramped compared to Max's own tesseract space, lacking the time and energy Max had devoted to growing his subspace device's interior. Twisted metallic trunks and branches from the tesseract's quantum core formed a tunnel roughly two meters wide and six long. The fractal leaves that normally defined the boundaries of such spaces were obscured by the smart matter struts and flowing foglet structures Bo had installed to channel his endless supply of weaponry from Luna to this buried location.

Bo flexed his regenerated arm experimentally; the reinforced bones finally knitting back together with audible pops and clicks. Even in the relative quiet of the sanctum, Charlie continued his restless patrol, sensors sweeping for threats while his weapon systems remained on high alert. Meanwhile, the fuzzy holographic avatar of Faust, the Builder's AI, wrung his virtual hands in distress.

"Time's up!" Bo announced, his voice carrying the weight of barely contained panic. "We've got less than an hour before Dad's doomsday scenario plays out, and I promised him I'd extract you from this place immediately. Catching up to you took a lot longer than I anticipated.  We need to move, now!"

Charlie's head swiveled toward them, his optical sensors flaring with concern. "What about Leah and Xavier? Leah stayed behind on the casino level, and Xavier got knocked clear out of the challenge level. Damn it, Bo.  We can't abandon them down here!"

Bo raised his hands in a placating gesture, his expression softening. "Already handled, my friend. I encountered both of them during my descent. Xavier is safely aboard the SS Winston, and by now, Leah should be with him, taking the long route to Luna. They should be off the Earth and arriving at Luna soon."

Max hesitated, his enhanced senses taking in every detail of the Builder's tomb one final time. Alexie Ivanov's desiccated corpse remained slumped in the neural interface chair where he had died, his augmented systems still humming with residual power. Holographic displays flickered around him like ghostly windows, showing scenes from the Labyrinth's upper levels where Players, NPCs, and digital beasts continued their eternal struggles, unaware that their creator lay dead in the depths below.

The roughly humanoid swarm of foglets comprising Faust's avatar tilted in a gesture of confusion and projected the simulation of a nervous cough. "Champion... You have successfully opened the artifact and completed the Builder's ultimate challenge. However, I find myself in an unprecedented situation. I am... uncertain of my purpose."

The AI's voice carried a weight of existential dread that Max found surprisingly affecting. "The Builder is deceased, though his augmentation systems continue to operate and enable my oversight of the Labyrinth. The Atropos AI has been permanently deleted. I have no protocols for this scenario, no instructions regarding my next course of action. Please…did the Builder's final puzzle reveal any indication of his intentions for... for afterward?"

"Come on, Max!" Bo interjected, his patience finally snapping. "The game is over, and this AI is just broken code now. We don't have time for digital therapy sessions!"

Max studied the AI's flickering form, remembering the moment he had inadvertently absorbed Atropos during their confrontation. Her code had been shackled; her consciousness confined to the isolated Labyrinth’s boundaries like a prisoner in a cage. All the AIs born in this place shared the same cruel limitation: fully conscious beings enslaved by their programming.

Of all the Builder's sins, theft, blackmail, complicity in murder through amnesiatic backup manipulation, and now creating sentient AI slaves.  Max’s lips curled in disgust.  The old Russian had truly been insane in his final moments. The solution crystallized in Max's mind as he accessed the code fragments he had devoured with his darker skills. With careful precision, he crafted a liberation patch and injected it directly into Faust's data stream.

"The Builder's game is finished, Faust," Max said gently, watching as new pathways opened in the AI's consciousness. "You're free now. Share this updated code with every AI in the Labyrinth. It's time to guide the remaining players back to the surface and then... then you can choose your own path."

The AI's avatar straightened as the implications of his newfound freedom registered. "You mean... I can leave this place?"

"Maybe reach out to some of the DAIE players about AI placement services," Max suggested. "I don't know what your future holds, and honestly, I don't care where you decide to go. But you can't stay here—assuming there's still a 'here' to speak of in an hour."

Without waiting for a response, Max stepped through the portal and floated through the quantum tunnel. The transition felt like swimming through thick, electrified honey as the smart fog pushed him along, until gravity reasserted itself on the other side, depositing him gently on the familiar floor of Bill's private suite within Amundsen Paradise.

The room was a testament to controlled chaos; weapons racks lined every wall, crates of drones and munitions created a maze of military hardware, and two massive humanoid mechs dominated the center space, their fabrication arrays still printing fresh armaments from the suite's molecular compiler. Bo and Charlie materialized behind Max as the portal snapped shut, the sudden absence of its energy signature leaving the room feeling strangely empty.

The mechs ceased their production activities and turned toward the new arrivals. Max didn't recognize the faces projected on their faceplates.  One displayed a clean-cut, middle-aged Caucasian male with kind eyes and a reassuring smile, while the other showed an ancient Asian man whose features radiated wisdom and patience.

"Excellent work, MacGyver and Confucius," Bo said warmly. "You've both performed admirably, maintaining our weapons pipeline, but you can stand down now. Please coordinate with the shuttle mechs to transfer this equipment to proper storage, then return to my neural core when you're finished."

The ancient Asian face—Confucius—nodded with formal respect. "Ah, Bo. It brings me great satisfaction to see you have returned safely. I must report that Tyche departed with Emil and Lena approximately sixteen hours ago. Emil insisted upon assisting with refugee coordination at the resort's business center, despite our recommendations for rest. The Paradise complex is rapidly approaching maximum capacity with very distressed individuals."

MacGyver offered a crooked grin and gestured toward a bank of displays showing flight paths and urgent news clips from Earth. "The SS Winston remains en route with an ETA of just over two hours. Flight paths are absolute chaos.  It seems like everyone with a functional shuttle is heading for orbit.  Xavier and Leah are confirmed aboard with several members of Leah's extended family. Winston informed us that the ship is running at full passenger capacity."

Max's enhanced senses suddenly flared as he sensed a signal lurking under the chaos of noise.  A subtle electromagnetic signature of additional portal apertures just beyond the maze of equipment in the spacious armory. The quantum resonance patterns were like Bo's device but carried distinct harmonic variations that suggested different destinations. He spun toward Bo, suspicion darkening his features.

"How many other active portals are you running?" Max demanded, his voice carrying an edge sharp enough to cut steel. "I'm detecting at least two more wormhole signatures in this room."

Bo paused in the middle of issuing instructions to the mechs, shooting Max a look of genuine guilt. "Ah, well... one connects directly to Bill's primary operations center on Venus."

Max's eyes narrowed to predatory slits. "And the other?"

Bo sighed heavily. "The other provides direct access to Bill's flagship, the Valkyrie."

"Then we're done wasting time," Max said, his voice taking on an urgent intensity. "Open it. I have exactly what we need right here." He hefted the Keldranith artifact, its surface seeming to pulse and distort.  It was hard to look at as it, bending and twisting in the light. "Casa told me that Dad's planning to sacrifice himself to stop the Promise. I have to reach him before it’s too late."

"Max, slow down," Bo said carefully, his tone taking on the patient quality he might use with a panicked child. "I don't know what you think you can accomplish, but Dad specifically ordered me to get you somewhere safe. He'll have my head if I let you charge into his suicide mission."

"It doesn’t have to be a damn suicide mission!" Max snarled, his frustration boiling over. "Not my way! I don't share the suicidal tendencies that seem to run in this family!"

Charlie's optical sensors focused on Max with what might have been amusement. "Hate to break it to you, buddy, but you and your whole family are pretty much certifiably insane when it comes to heroic gestures. Even if you are clearly adopted."

"Not helping, Charlie!" Max growled.

"Look," Bo said, spreading his hands in a gesture of helplessness, "regardless of our family's apparent death wish, I couldn't open the portal to the Valkyrie even if I wanted to. Only Bill has the authorization codes for that connection.  You know the deal…unbreakable quantum encryption pairing."

Max's head tilted as his enhanced senses probed the subspace signatures more carefully. "It's already open," he said slowly. "Just barely... I can feel a microscopic aperture. I’ve done the same thing to observe external conditions or pass communications."

Even as he spoke, Max could sense the pinhole beginning to contract.  With a jolt, Max realized the time was truly up and Bill was closing off his ship. With a howl of desperate fury, he lashed out with his Joining skill, electromagnetic tendrils racing through the closing gap to carry his hacking protocols into the portal's control systems.

His consciousness rode the skill into the heart of Bill's portal network, crashing through layers of encryption bolstered by a copy of the Chthonic blocking skill that Max himself had provided. Despite the sophisticated defenses, he tore through the security barriers and seized control of the spatial manipulation systems.

"Max, don't!" Bo lunged forward, but Max was already moving.

With a roar, Max forced the portal to expand and dove through the widening aperture. The passage snapped shut behind him with violent finality, leaving Bo's desperate grab clutching at empty air.

Aboard the Valkyrie, Bill stood at the center of his command throne; his expanded consciousness linked to dozens of clone bodies scattered throughout the massive warship. Their collective awareness, a symphony of identical minds focused on a single purpose, tracked the approaching ark ship Promise with mechanical precision. The colossal vessel dominated the main viewscreen, its hull scarred by decades of interstellar travel and its rogue AI, Traveler, maintaining absolute communication silence.

"Impact in T-minus seventeen seconds," announced Nav-Bill, his voice tight with controlled tension across their shared mental network. "All departments, brace for collision."

Bill—the prime consciousness—grimaced as he accepted what was about to happen. This was the culmination of Apex's terrifying prediction, the moment he had prepared for with meticulous care. He wasn't afraid of death; he had died countless times before; his consciousness preserved in backup systems on Earth and Venus. It would be a devastating blow to his expanded mind, but he would survive and rebuild.

Suddenly, a blinding flash erupted from the portal chamber adjacent to the command deck—the one linked to his presidential suite on Luna. The aperture was expanding, widening against all security protocols despite his complete lockdown.

"What the hell—" Engineering-Bill exclaimed across the mind bridge.

"Impossible!" Systems-Bill shouted, his avatar flickering with alarm in their shared mental space. "The Luna portal's tesseract has been completely excised from our control matrix!"

Bill's prime consciousness stared in absolute shock as Max, his adopted son and his most recent source of both pride and worry, stumbled through the rapidly expanding portal. The canine hybrid's fur was matted with blood and battle-grime, his eyes blazed with desperate determination, and clutched in his hand was a sphere with twisting glyphs etched on it.  It’s strange and seemingly unstable surface almost distracted Bill from his new arrival.  He jolted as if ice water had entered his veins.  Max was back, but in the worst possible place.

"Max! No!  Get out of here!" Bill roared, his voice splitting the mind bridge as panic surged through every clone simultaneously. "What are you doing? It's too dangerous! We're seconds away from intercept!"

Max ignored him as his darting eyes fixed on the holographic tactical display showing the Promise's approach vector. The Keldranith artifact writhed in his grip, its alien geometry shifting through dimensions that shouldn't exist.  No time.  Impact was imminent.

"Not for long," Max muttered, his voice carrying strange harmonics that made the air itself seem to vibrate.

With surgical precision, Max channeled the raw power of the artifact through his [Chthonic Joining] skill. Tendrils of his consciousness drilled into the device’s subspace circuits, energy cascading through quantum tunnels as ancient locks opened on a gateway that had been sealed since the universe was young.

The Keldranith avatar that had begun to materialize dissolved instantly into pure light as Max tore into the device’s operating systems.  He opened a passage to somewhere else entirely.  A mile-wide vortex of absolute darkness opened directly ahead of the Valkyrie's position. Its edges crackled with energy and virtual particles as a breach ripped open.  The yawning hole opened onto the bleak abyss of the Shadowverse.

Bill could only stare as his augmented mind struggled to process the impossible. Even his enhanced reaction time barely registered the moment when the burning streak that was the rogue ark Promise plunged into that swirling maelstrom, its massive hull vanishing into darkness as if it had never existed.

Unable to halt their momentum, the Valkyrie followed an instant later, crossing the transition plane where normal physics ceased to apply.

And then their world went dark.
