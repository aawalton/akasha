To the shock of no one, a feast was announced within seconds of Velistraine’s arrival. As soon as Zerathstra let her go, with an arm still gripping her shoulder, he shouted up the tree, where almost immediately, heads began peeking over the edges of the settlement and its many stairs and bridges, followed by celebratory whistles and shouts. The ape grinned at the blushing, but still smiling Velistraine. But only for a brief instant.

One moment she’d been basking in the attention, and the next there was a sharp thud that had her grimacing in pain as Zerathstra smacked her upside the head.

“What were you thinking?” he hissed at her, and what followed was a very understandable, but still uncomfortable dress-down of a somewhat unreasonable parent to a reckless child. Clearly, both of them knew that when that tear had opened up, there weren’t that many other options to seal it back, but Zerathstra wasn’t letting logic get in the way of his reprimand, and Velistraine didn’t try. So Henry and Maurice drifted back and let the two take the lead as they floated closer to the stairs leading up to the city.

A claw nudged Henry, who looked down just in time for Maurice’s voice to ring in his head. “[Should we say something?]”

Henry chuckled inwardly, then wiggled an arm.“[Nope,]” he simply said. “[This is between them. But don’t worry. It’s not too serious.]”

Looking up at the approaching canopy, Henry felt some of the stress from the last few weeks slide off of his back. It had only been a couple of hours since they left the void of space, and while Henry wasn’t exactly feeling tired, there was still some restlessness to his thoughts. Like he had a couple of coffee cups too many. But nothing he couldn’t handle. Still, he didn’t need to pass down his bad habits to Maurice.

“[We should make the most out of this break,]” he added after a moment. “[Things might get rough for a while.]”

***

Velistraine’s return home was like the procession of a parade, with everyone dropping whatever they had to do to follow up the stairs toward the usual celebratory space. Henry and Maurice watched from a short distance back, though after a bit, the crab went to join the front of the parade, both to say hi to his young friends—including Ash and Fabian, the former who looked sharp—and to have a little fun. As for Henry, he put on his human suit and just trailed after them, walking leisurely up the wide stairways while people, young and old, rushed around him. With a cocktail in hand, he nodded and smiled to those that greeted him, while in the background, he continued orchestrating the activities of his Avatar.

Henry knew that he should be taking his own advice and resting, but he had too much energy for that, and there was still so much to do and to prepare for. He’d take a break soon. But not right away. Still, Henry couldn’t help but consider the scene ahead of him. This was Velistraine’s home. To a certain degree, it seemed like somewhat of a home to Maurice as well, as the latter had integrated pretty well with the locals. Then again, Maurice could make every place he visited a home. He had that… easy going and friendly predisposition to him that made most people warm up to him.

But was Thalis Henry’s home? Was this world his home?

Henry’s brows furrowed, making the waiter’s smile wink out before the kraken caught himself and corrected the misunderstanding before his thoughts drifted back to the mental path he’d been wandering. When he was partially stranded outside of the System’s bubble with Velistraine and Maurice, he had sometimes thought of this place as a home. But now that he was down here, walking its paths and roads, he didn’t feel like he could call it home. It was a safe harbor, absolutely. A nice spot to relax and rest. But could he stay here for years at a time and become part of it?

Would he ever feel like he belonged here?

Henry would not mind putting it to the test, but something in the back of his head told him that he wouldn’t fit. He could try forcing the incompatible pieces of the puzzle together, and they might hold in place for a bit. But they wouldn’t click like it was clicking for Velistraine. Like he wanted it to click. This didn’t mean his old world would feel like home either. He might have a spot for it, as he had lived on Earth all of his life. He had friends there. He had memories there. But he wasn’t the same person he used to be, anymore. The hardship of the early days, fighting and killing to survive had changed him. He was a kraken, and he could tell he’d grow just as restless back there. In fact, his restlessness might turn problematic if Henry were to settle there for any length of time. As far as he knew, there was nothing supernatural on Earth. If he were to show up, he’d be like a living god walking among them.

Fading in the background, Henry turned himself invisible, then floated up in the air as he took in the festive grounds ahead of him, putting his musings aside for a moment. He was in a strange mood, all of a sudden. This didn’t feel like something he’d be able to smooth over with the snap of a finger and well, he could consider all of that when an army of insectile monsters weren’t on the lookout for them, waiting for an opening to rip this world asunder.

That can’t be a healthy outlook, he thought to himself as a grim smile tugged on his lips. But speaking of insectile monsters… what should he do with the corpses he was still lugging around?

This question being much more comfortable to contemplate, Henry turned his full attention to it. In the heat of the moment, when he had stored the first elite corpse, Henry had mostly done it to remove his tracks, and then, he started doing it to potentially get a bite in and study them. But throughout their foray in space, Henry had had plenty of opportunities to ingest some samples from the elites. For some reason, Magic Mimic struggled a bit to get anything working out of them. Maybe it was something to do with their nature as an insectile, nearly identical, and potentially lab-grown being—or whatever the equivalent was. Maybe it was because of their nature as invaders. But eventually, Henry’s skill had managed to synthesize two skills, which he had condensed into a one-time ability before storing them.

There were two more, but one was basically Shapeshift, and he had the superior version of that, and the second had something to do with getting more nutrients from eating his own kind, or something of that vein. That one Henry had extracted then chucked out in the void of space. As for the two skills he had kept, they were Psychic Screech and Suppressing Touch. But Henry hadn’t wanted to spread himself too thin back then, especially with how weak the skills had felt. They had been, at best, at C-rank. And what was a C-rank to do against powerful A-ranks?

Maybe once I get everything I need to A-rank, I’ll start working on them, he thought as the celebration continued below. Velistraine was now surrounded by her crew, who had all stayed in Thalis, and Henry’s thoughts drifted back to the corpses in his storage. At least the bodies had served as proof, and they could still keep doing that, he decided as he zoned in on one particular Avatar he’d had on standby for a bit. It was the one hovering near the entrance to Wolf’s Den. With a swift flex of will, Henry cast Shapeshift and fashioned his human form on that Lifeblood Avatar, which, by now, was merely the work of a couple of seconds. Then he hesitated.

Why was he pushing himself so much? And why was he in such a strange mood?

Henry sat with the uneasiness for a moment while the music and the sound of merriment picked up around his real body. His freshly shapeshifted Avatar was still hidden, so Henry kept it there while he listened to this… urge he had to stay busy.

Why am I doing all of this? Henry asked himself, and an answer came by easily enough. Because this world, and all the life it carried was in danger.

Why him and nobody else?

Because he was well equipped to contribute and coordinate their efforts. Arguably he was the best one equipped for such a task.

Henry frowned. That question led him away from what he should be looking into. Saving the world was a valid reason to be restless, but that wasn’t all of it. There was something else.

Henry dug deeper. Why was he finding it so difficult to sit down and rest? He should be feeling good. He was finally A-rank, and he was finally not as vulnerable to the whims of the powerful beings of this world. He wasn’t at the top yet, but he should have been able to take a breather. But he couldn’t. There was a swarm coming.

Then it dawned on Henry; or at least, part of the issue became clearer.

Once this… invasion-situation was dealt with, if they all made it out alive. What was to happen to him? What would he do?

Suddenly, he felt the urge to keep busy come back tenfold as yet another truth peeked at him from around the corner. Henry had been so busy surviving and going after power to keep himself and Maurice safe, that he had no clue what to do with himself when he’d gotten that power.
