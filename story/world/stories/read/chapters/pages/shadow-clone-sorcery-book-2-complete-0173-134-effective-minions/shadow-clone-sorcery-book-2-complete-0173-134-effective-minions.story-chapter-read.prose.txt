The following morning, Penelope, Lukas, and Mira left the Gray unmolested. The woman kept glancing at him, expecting direction or Shade’s Mantle. Instead, they walked tall, past early morning work crews and exited through a gate near the former Frozen Phoenix guild house. The Aquila guild now used it as one of their training bases. In the meantime, Morph Prime and his little squad of sub-Morphs led pursuers on a wild goose chase. Two of them adopted Penelope’s appearance, and another pair disguised themselves as Mira. They went around the city and then out through opposite exits, each accompanied by an undisguised clone. The final Morph visited the Aquila guild as himself and took some time off to rest and recover, citing stress from working under the taskmaster that was the Cold Fire Sorcerer. He then retired to one of the city’s premier bathhouses, where he was seen relaxing for hours on end.

Lukas knew straight away when the clones in the Gray dispelled themselves. Only the Super Clone in the bathhouse remained and was to behave as Morph usually would unless needed. The city council and all watching parties needed to believe that Penelope’s usual party and Lukas weren’t heading toward any known Jotun encampments. Ogden and the Shadow Seekers guild had assisted in the deception, generating false quests and assigning them to Lukas, making everything appear legitimate.

Two hours after leaving the city limits, Lukas and company rendezvoused with a trio of clones and Bass. They had departed the day before, wearing disguises and carrying supplies. Lukas wasn’t yet confident about carrying food in the journal’s storage. Things no longer decomposed or went moldy within, but things tasted off. He didn’t trust it as sustenance.

Lukas had grown used to the prospect of the Shadowsteel Keep over the past few days. It didn’t matter to him that the confluence ability wouldn’t come with immediate combat uses. He adored the idea of somewhere to escape to. He could imagine summoning parts of the structure into the material world to act as his visible home or shopfront. Lukas couldn’t help but imagine an ever-expanding keep.

Railguns, cannons, and hot oil on the outside. Maze of terrors on the inside. Shadow clones and infinite castle.

“What’s so funny?” Mira asked, as they trudged up hill. She snaked an arm around his, sticking close to him. The uneven uphill terrain didn’t bother them. His Body Pillar was now at tier three, and she wasn’t too far behind.

“I was just thinking how jealous the old me would be of everything I have now,” Lukas answered. He wasn’t just talking about the old him from Earth, but also the version who spent six decades on the Realm of Greater Beings.

“You mean because of me, right?” She grinned.

“Of course,” Lukas lied. Mira was only a part of it.

Bass grew to the size of a horse as they walked, acting as Penelope’s mount. They stayed in the front of the party with a Bruiser equipped with Alter Metal Mass. Bowie, having finished his work in the Anglar plains, walked just ahead of Lukas and Mira, quietly playing his lute. Joybringer allowed him to control the music such that only his allies could hear him. A lone Stalker and Ace brought up the rear.

Zwei, the newly titled Arcane Chronicler, stayed in the workshop with Eins, keeping up appearances. They needed to keep up appearances alongside Morph and were only to be summoned if things got chaotic.
