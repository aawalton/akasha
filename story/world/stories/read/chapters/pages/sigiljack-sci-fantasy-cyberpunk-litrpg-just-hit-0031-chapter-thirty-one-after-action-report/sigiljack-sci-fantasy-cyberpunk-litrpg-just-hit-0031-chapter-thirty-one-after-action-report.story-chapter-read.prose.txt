The threadlink call buzzed in his HUD.

He accepted. “You get the data?”

“Yes,” Kaito answered. Calm as ever. “My associate above should already be working through it. That is the reason for my call, Ranson‑san. You have completed your task well. I am pleased.”

[Threadnet Transfer Incoming: 4,000 Credits.]

He accepted and watched his credit balance tick upward.

“Had to kill some of the residents to do it,” John said. “And I saw our old friend from the last job.”

“That is… regrettable. Would you accept this for your trouble?”

[Threadnet Transfer Incoming: 1,000 Credits.][Accept: y/n?]

“I don’t want blood money.” He paused. “But I’ll take it.”

He hesitantly accepted the second transfer. His account climbed again.

“The Grin agent from your job with Ghaz‑san,” Kaito said, “he was present at the complex as well?”

“Only in cyberspace. I stabbed his avatar through the chest and he shut down the private netspace he'd pulled us into," Job explained, voice growing darker as he continued. "But before that he threatened my family, Kaito. And that wasn’t part of the gig.”

He was starting to really hate the Grin.

“I see. I will have Nabe‑san coordinate protection around your home,” Kaito said, “provided you are willing to continue working with us?"

That surprised him. His first instinct was to refuse. He did not like the idea of Kaito’s people near Claire, but the vampire already knew where he lived. And he liked the idea of the Grin near his family even less. If Nabe was running the detail, he could maybe live with it. She did not strike him as someone who took harming teenage girls or defenseless women lightly.

"You still worried about the Grin virus getting your people? They didn't even try to use it on me tonight."

"That is likely because they knew it would not work. But I also reward reliability in those whom I employ with more employment."

The lift jerked to a stop. Doors opened. The vault door waited right in front, still closed.

“You said your guy is working the data, right?” John asked, eye on the heavy door. “He's going to take a break to let me back in, right? I’m standing in his elevator now.”

“Apologies. He is dedicated to his craft. Allow me to notify him.”

“Thanks.”

Silence for a breath. Then the door's heavy internals groaned. The vault door swung open.

The hooded, masked vampire from before stood behind it. Same deformed build. Same watchful, inteligent and almost kind eyes. His gaze flicked to the droid and the hovering drone behind John.

“Kaito says hi,” John told the man in front of him, hoping the Japanese vampire's name would keep him from being read the riot act about the droid and drone for a second time.

"You're alive. That's good for you," the gravel-voice of the masked vampire said.

"Sure am. And I got you some nice server-logs to sift through."

The vampire squinted at him. "I don't like being patronized."

The threadlink crackled with Kaito's voice. “I would also appreciate it if you did not use my name so lightly... There is a car waiting for you outside.”

“Thanks for that,” John said aloud to Kaito, then to the masked vampire, “Door?”

The hooded figure grunted and walked ahead, unlocking the door to the server room’s exit. He held it, apparently just as eager for John to leave.

John passed him and hit the stairwell. The droid clanked after him. The drone hummed along overhead.

“What job did you have in mind next?” John asked Kaito as he climbed.

“That will depend on what is found within the data you acquired,” Kaito replied in his ears. “Likely we will strike at the Grin’s infrastructure in a targeted manner. If we are fortunate, we will strike at the power behind them.”

“Then have your guy down there look for anything tagged 'Angel',” John said. He pushed the door at the top of the stairs and stepped behind the front counter of the net parlor. Empty. The woman from before was gone. He kept moving. “I saw a feed of it in their datafortress. Pretty sure it's behind the mana virus.”

“The Angel,” Kaito echoed. “And you believe this is an important entity?”

“Yeah. Probably some kind of fucked-up program or AI. Looks like they trapped it in a server somewhere. It tried to talk to me. For what it's worth, it said the Grin are keeping it somewhere outside the city and it wants to be free. Not sure if that's a good idea though."

Outside the city, in the mana-mutated hellscape known as the badlands.

Athena's voice cut in, just as he was having that thought:

"John are you sure this is what we should be doing?"

"We always need the money and we need to find out what's going on with you."

Kaito made a thoughtful sound. John shoved through the parlor's front door into the night.

Sector air hit his face. Wet from the earlier rain. Chemical and polluted. The street was quiet in that way that meant everyone smart had already gone home.

Across the road, a black sedan idled, red accent light along its sills. He was surprised it wasn't just another taxi. It was Nabe’s usual ride. He did not walk to it yet.

“It would have to be a very advanced artificial intelligence to produce their virus, would it not?” Kaito asked.

"Probably."

Or and AGI... like the one I have in my head, John thought.

“Do you know exactly where it is located?”

“I only know what I told you.” John stared at the nearby car and its tinted glass. “Listen. I’ll help you find it if the pay’s right. But the kind of shit the Grin have does not get to end up with you. I’ll make sure they don’t have it anymore either, if that's what you want too. But that's as far as I go.”

He needed to find the Angel anyway for Athena’s sake. If a payday could be wrapped around that, all the better. But he was not handing a vampire access to whatever the Angel was.

Kaito was quiet for a long few seconds. “I believe I find your terms acceptable. Rest. I will be in touch.”

The call cut.

The driver’s door of Kaito's car opened. Nabe stepped out.

“Are you waiting for me to open your door for you?” she asked.

John smirked. “Only if you’re feeling extra nice.”

Her eyes went to the droid and the drone hovering at his shoulder. “Are they clean? They do not look clean.”

“Sorta.” He shrugged. “I’ll tell them not to touch anything.”

“It is difficult to avoid touching the seats,” she said. "You do not look clean either."

John remembered that he had spent a good bit of the night in an abandoned undercity sewer system.

He gave her a look that said 'what do you want me to do?'. “Only me and the big guy need to sit. He can hold the drone.”

The military droid in question looked between Nabe and John and then gave a placating thumbs up to the Japanese woman.

Nabe considered, raising an eyebrow at the expressive droid. Then nodded once.

“Very well. Would you sit next to me this time, John?" she asked.

John was pleasantly surprised by her request, last time having sat in the back of the fancy car. "I'd love to."

He approached the passenger side of the sedan as Nabe slid back into the driver's seat.
