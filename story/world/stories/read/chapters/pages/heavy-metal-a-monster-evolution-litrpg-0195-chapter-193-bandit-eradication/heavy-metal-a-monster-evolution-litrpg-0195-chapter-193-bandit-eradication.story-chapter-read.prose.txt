“Who are you? Show yourself!”

“I won’t let some monsters destroy what I’ve built!”

“...”

This monster had taken on a new form, wider and nearly as tall as the bandit leader himself. As it moved, the ground sank beneath its heavy metal boots. It was strange, since only moments ago it had been living armor, yet now it carried the weight of an iron golem.

The ant monster, instead of attacking him directly, had vanished and turned its attention toward his unconscious men and the few who had somehow managed to stand back up. He realized he was not alone, yet he could not tell where his true opponent, the monster tamer or summoner, was hiding.

“Come out and face me!”

With a single kick, he sent the monster crashing into one of the guard towers, which collapsed instantly. He raised his axes in a victorious pose, believing the battle was over. Yet the monster did not fall. Even though its body was riddled with cuts and gashes from his weapons, the wounds closed almost immediately. This was not an ordinary creature. He had never heard of a being like this, one capable of healing while not being made of flesh. Its kind already possessed strong armor and great endurance, so how could it also be able to regenerate inorganic parts?

“Do I need to find the one controlling this thing? But where are they?”

The bandit was certain this was not his true enemy, but he could not see, smell, or sense any other powerful presence nearby. It made no sense. There were limits to how far a monster could move from its summoner, which meant the mage responsible had to be close. Tamers usually worked with living creatures made of flesh and blood, and this one was clearly formed through magic. He was sure a mage was behind it, but he could not find the culprit.

“Then I’ll just kill this thing and find the one responsible!”

He charged at the steel monster again, but just as he was about to strike, a strange haze of energy surrounded it. The creature suddenly became faster and stronger, and its attacks carried magical power. Its halberd clashed with his axes, and this time an electric surge shot through his body, leaving him paralyzed for a brief moment. The weapon struck again, piercing one of his legs and sending another shock through his body.

“Arghhh!”

He screamed as the monster continued to attack with relentless force. Despite its heavy frame, it moved with unexpected agility, striking with precision and going for critical body parts as if it were a practiced warrior. One of his axes was knocked into the air, and before he could pull it back, the monster’s weapon sliced clean through his wrist, severing his hand.

The bandit leader roared in pain, clutching the bleeding stump where his hand had been. His eyes burned with rage as he bared his teeth. Blood streamed down his arm and mixed with the dirt beneath his feet. Still, he refused to fall.

“You think this is enough to stop me?”

Even with the injury, he did not stop. He lunged at the monster, catching it by surprise. His teeth sank into the metal hide, but aside from a slight bend, it did little damage. His remaining hand swung the axe again and again in a desperate attempt to wound the creature, yet his strength was fading fast. His body was covered in new wounds, and his breath grew heavy and uneven.

“Damn it… What do you want? I’ll give you everything. You want the gold? The women? Anything!”

With a single kick, the monster sent him crashing into the corpses of his fallen men. When he realized he could not win, he began shouting into the open air, hoping to draw out the hidden mage. He knew that monsters could not be bargained with, but perhaps the mage would spare him or reveal himself in exchange for treasure.

“Stop! Without me, you’ll never find the hidden chest. Only I know where it’s buri-”

Before he could finish, the monster hurled its weapon at him. The halberd shone like lightning as it struck his bare chest. He looked down in shock, his vision fading as he drew his final breath. His life flashed before his eyes, and then he fell to the ground like a puppet whose strings had been cut.
