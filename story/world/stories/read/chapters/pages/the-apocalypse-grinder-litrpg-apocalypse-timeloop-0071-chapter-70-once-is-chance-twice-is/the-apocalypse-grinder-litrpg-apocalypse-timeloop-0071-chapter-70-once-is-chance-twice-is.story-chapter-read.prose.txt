His plan worked as he’d suspected. Even though there was no direct boon to energy cultivation from his skill, the serene state it granted to his mind and body allowed him an unparalleled degree of focus.

Using that, he did his best to twist and mold the energy of the shard between his desires and its own urge to ravage his body. He had felt that his heart was the source of mana, so that was where he focused his efforts.

Halfway through, there was a worrying moment where he felt a sharp pain in his heart. In that instant, Ronan had feared heart failure—or worse. It passed a breath later and he had continued with far more caution.

In the end, his efforts were a success and the chime of the system rewarded him once more.

Ronan grit his teeth in preparation for the pain. However, he felt himself becoming weightless. The pain he had expected never came.

The world shifted, the starlit sky around him vanishing as an empty, obsidian blue void devoured it and took its place. Ronan floated in the expanse, an instant of utter nothingness passing. An eerie whining noise cut through the empty silence.

A wave of… fuzziness, for lack of a better word, radiated outwards from his navel, ending in the tips of his toes and the crown of his head. When it passed, he was back under the stars of Planet 2.48e11E.

If this was possible the entire time, why the hell did I have to go through that damned infusion the first time!? Ronan inwardly cursed. After a few moments he thought it through, deciding it was possibly due to the second infusion being an evolution. Perhaps going from classless to having a class is more… intense?

It wasn’t the most scientifically sound theory, but it was a start. Through the iterations, he would eventually solve all the system’s intricacies. Until he could punch it in the damned face.

With the brief, painless evolution complete, Ronan pulled up his two new class skills to check out their descriptions. The first wasn’t that surprising, while the second left him feeling bittersweet.

Adaptive meditation wasn’t much more than a standard evolution of the original martial meditation skill. Despite the fancy language, it had basically just increased the percentage boosts and allowed him to meditate in situations other than sitting still.

That wasn’t to knock the new skill—it was fantastic. Even the possibility of cultivating while on the move, no matter how inefficient, was incredible. If Ronan continued to evolve this skill it could become something exceptionally potent.

More than that, he would attempt to study the underlying mechanics. When he’d first sensed mana, he had worked hard to manipulate it outside the bounds of his skills and the system. He had seen moderate success.

If the same manipulation was possible with his skills, then keeping the benefits of class skills in iterations where he hadn’t chosen the same class was a distant possibility. He couldn’t wait.

As for the second skill, learn through suffering made him bittersweet because it sounded incredibly overpowered, yet he would lose it at the end of this iteration. He vowed to dedicate himself to understanding his skills on a deeper level, to prevent such losses in future. Maybe the Chronos Clan has a way to stop me from losing class skills? A future enhancement, perhaps? he mused.
