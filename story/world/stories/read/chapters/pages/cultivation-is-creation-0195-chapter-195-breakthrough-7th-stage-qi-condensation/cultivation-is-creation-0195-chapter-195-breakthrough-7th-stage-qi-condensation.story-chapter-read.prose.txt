Ke Jun's hands closed on empty air where my face had been a moment before.

I had already used Blink Step to appear next to Liu Chang, who Yan Li was helping back to his feet, and the stone cultivator's body was in bad shape with cracked armor and blood everywhere but his eyes were still burning which made him an effective leader through everything that had happened today.

Ke Jun's red eyes found me and widened slightly as he felt the changes that had come from my breakthrough.

"A demonic technique?" he said, studying the energy fluctuations coming off me. Then he shook his head and that smile of his got wider. "No, not quite demonic, but the energy signature is unlike anything I have encountered."

I didn’t say anything back to him. There was no point in hiding what I was doing anymore because I had already been forced to drop the Shroud rune, and against someone like Ke Jun, I needed every advantage I could put together regardless of what it revealed about me.

"Master," Azure said, with concern in his voice. "Are you certain about this?"

"Not even slightly," I muttered. "But I’ve run out of better ideas."

I activated the Overclock rune.

The red sun in my inner world swelled immediately, its surface rippling with deeper crimson and brilliant orange, and my physical essence jumped from 33 to 45 units all at once and the surge of it felt raw and untamed moving through me. The red sun's increased capacity meant more power available for every other rune I had access to, but I wasn’t done yet because I also channeled the blue sun's energy through my meridians to help stabilize the chaotic output of the Overclock. The combination of the two together produced something that felt new, and the aura coming off me should help conceal the blue sun's presence from Ke Jun because the last thing I needed was to give him more reasons to be interested in my cultivation.

"Your energy signature is quite fascinating," Ke Jun said, watching me the way someone watches an experiment they are running. "The World Tree Sutra was always known for producing unique variations, but the problem with the World Tree Su—"

He did not get to finish because Liu Chang came forward, but not the stone armored fighter I had been watching all day, his qi was moving in a different pattern now forming what looked like ghostly armor around his body instead.

"Ten Thousand Ghost Prison!" The echo in Liu Chang's voice was something I hadn’t heard from him before, and spectral chains erupted from his body trailing cold mist that crystallized the air around them.

At the same time Yan Li's hands were moving through complex patterns. "Imperial Heavens Gate!"

A massive circular gate appeared behind him radiating authority so pure that my own knees were trying to buckle even though I wasn’t the target of it.

Ke Jun's eyes actually widened a small amount. "The Imprisonment Scripture and the Gate of Imperial Authority, how unexpected." His hands came together in a complex seal. "But if you wish to discuss ancient techniques..."

The air got thick and heavy and took on a reddish color, and instead of the division lines from before, globules of blood red energy began forming throughout the entire area, each one pulsing hungrily.

"Blood Essence Devouring Palm!"

The technique worked by consuming everything around it, the blood red spheres drawing in air and stone and spiritual energy alike and converting whatever they touched into pure essence that flowed directly into Ke Jun's spectral form.

Liu Chang's chains shattered the moment they made contact with the spheres, the ghostly metal consumed instantly, but he wasn’t finished. "Rising Ghost Tide!"

The temperature dropped hard and fast as thousands of spectral forms that looked like Liu Chang rose up from the ground and formed a wall around our group, and each ghost absorbed some of the blood spheres and was destroyed in the process, but the technique disrupted what Ke Jun was doing.

"Blood Sea Manifestation!"

The stone platform beneath our feet turned liquid, becoming a rolling sea of crimson energy that pulled at everything standing on it, each wave carrying something parasitic in it that tried to get into our qi and turn it against us. This wasn’t a pseudo domain, but it was close enough to one that the distinction barely mattered.

"Nine Heavens Waterfall!" Su Yue's voice came from somewhere in the chaos and instead of fire, she had somehow converted her flame essence into pure spiritual water that fell from above and created a protective dome of purifying energy around the group.

It seemed like everyone had been holding something back for exactly this moment.

Shen Xuanyu went next. "Thunder Dragon Ascension!"

The lightning that moved through him was pure white instead of his usual jade colored variety and his body actually began taking on draconic characteristics as he launched himself directly at Ke Jun, becoming essentially a living weapon made of electrical energy.

"Blood Dragon Counterstrike!"

The crimson sea surged upward and formed a dragon of pure blood essence that met Shen Xuanyu head on, white lightning and blood red energy fighting against each other with neither one winning, but Ke Jun's attention wasn’t entirely on Shen Xuanyu because his left hand was already moving.

"Blood Essence Binding!"

Threads of blood red energy shot from his fingers toward each of us and where they made contact, they started draining spiritual energy directly out of our meridians.

Zhang Wei responded immediately. "Nine Transformations of the Earthly Dragon!"

His body shifted through characteristics of different beasts, dragon scales and tiger claws and phoenix eyes, each transformation letting him break free of the blood threads while hitting back with devastating physical force.

I used the opening all of this created without wasting a second of it. I attacked from every angle I could, the scorpion tail going for vital points while Explosive Seeds detonated around him, vines erupting from the blood sea itself trying to entangle his spectral form, and the Shockwave rune firing as my palm connected with his back and actually drew blood from his mouth. For a moment it looked like it was working, Ke Jun's form stuttering and his outline becoming unclear as the number of angles he was being forced to defend from exceeded what he could handle cleanly.

Then his eyes blazed. "Blood Sun Apocalypse!"

I used Blink Step the instant I felt it starting.

A miniature sun of pure blood essence formed above the entire battlefield and began radiating waves of consuming force that broke down everything they touched at a fundamental level, the blood sea rising in massive waves that could erase whatever they hit, and this wasn’t pseudo anything, this was a real domain, and we were inside it.

"Imperial Mandate, Heavens Shield!" Yan Li's golden light formed a dome above us that barely held the waves back.

"Blood Essence, Ancestral Memory!"

The blood red energy began resonating with something inside Yan Li's golden light itself, ancient patterns spreading through the barrier and weakening it from the inside out.

"The imperial qi in your veins," Ke Jun said, and his voice was almost gentle about it, "it remembers when it was simply mortal blood, when your ancestors were governors working on basic cultivation..."

The golden barrier broke apart.

What came after that was brutal and fast.

Shen Xuanyu's thunder dragon form failed first, his electrical energy overwhelmed by the blood essence, and the draconic features fell away as he collapsed, unconscious but breathing, his meridians damaged but not destroyed.

Zhang Wei managed one last leap backward before going to one knee with blood at his mouth and his beast forms flickering out. Su Yue held on longer than she had any right to before her legs stopped working and she went down with her spiritual energy nearly empty but her core still intact.

Liu Chang fought the way he had done everything today, which was without any thought of stopping, his Ghost Prison technique building a fortress of spectral chains and spirits around himself and Yan Li.

This bought time for Yan Li to attempt one final technique. "Heavens Final Mandate!"

The golden light that came out of Yan Li carried ten thousand years of authority behind it and for a moment it actually pushed the blood waves back and created a space of order inside the chaos of Ke Jun's domain.

It wasn’t enough and couldn’t have been enough.

The blood waves overwhelmed it and the backlash threw both Liu Chang and Yan Li back into what remained of the shrine walls and they went down, severely wounded but with their spiritual cores still faintly pulsing.

Which left me.

The Overclock rune was reaching the end of how long I could sustain it, the red sun's energy was becoming increasingly unstable, and the blue sun was doing everything it could to keep me standing, but there was a limit to what it could manage.

"And then there was one," Ke Jun said, spreading his arms wide. "Come now my child, enough of this, your body is mine by right and has been for centuries, why continue fighting something that can’t be avoided?"

I looked at the battlefield, which was now a rolling sea of blood essence in every direction. I had one option remaining.

One thing I had been holding back because using it had a genuine possibility of killing me. The best-case outcome was falling unconscious for somewhere between a few days and a month, and the worst-case outcome was something I wouldn’t be around to have opinions about.

I reached into my inner world, past the shrinking red sun and past the blue sun still trying to keep my body from coming apart, to where a tiny star pulsed with power that had no business existing inside someone at my cultivation level.

"Master," Azure said urgently, "channeling Stellar Realm energy in your current state could…"

"Kill me?" I said. "Yes, but not doing it also kills me, so I don’t have much of a choice."
