Blood remained uninterested, but Sera answered, "We should really try to get to Expert, though that'll take a long time. I know we have some free time right now, but we do have some pressing things slowly losing time in the background."

"That's true," Beth said with a frown.

"What about your box," a lazy, naked Blood growled from the couch.

"That relic thing that your hero or whatever is in," Blood grunted.

"My what…" Beth said, then realized. "Oh, you mean the reliquary? What about it?"

"Aren't there thirteen really strong people in there? Didn't they say they'd train you?" Blood asked.
