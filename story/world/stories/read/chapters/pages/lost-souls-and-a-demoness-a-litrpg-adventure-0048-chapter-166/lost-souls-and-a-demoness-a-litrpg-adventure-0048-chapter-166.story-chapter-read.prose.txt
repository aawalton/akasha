The wait was getting tedious. Ves had used her blood magic to home in on the renegade succubus's location, following the trail to a forest Sanctuary on one of the lower floors. She led Vylith there, and the two found a concealed spot amidst the undergrowth where they could lie in wait until their quarry emerged. There wasn't much else to do, any group that entered a Sanctuary was given their own instance of it to occupy, so it wasn't like they could pursue them inside. The vampire waited impatiently, itching to exact vengeance on the Wayfarer who had humiliated her.

The Labyrinth shook again, interrupting her thoughts. Ves's finely tuned magical senses detected a ripple of dimensional energy pass through the ground. She glanced at Vylith, but the wraith demon's expression remained impassive. She'd barely uttered a word since they'd departed together, and not even evidence of her people's imminent escape from their prison world was enough to break her focus. The demon was all business, which was tiresome. Hopefully, their time together would soon be over.

Finally, two hours after they'd arrived, the Sanctuary door opened. The succubus emerged, accompanied by a group of companions. Ves tensed, readying her magic. Even with the advantage of surprise, the fight wouldn't be trivial. A full party of Wayfarers working together was a formidable threat. Fortunately, she'd planned for this. The vampire withdrew a sealed vial of dark powder from her belt patch and opened it, sprinkling expensive summoning agents atop the exposed soil around her. Dark essence, nearly invisible under the starlit forest canopy, began to swirl around her, slowly taking shape. The specters would take a few moments to form and wouldn't last especially long once summoned, but the incorporeal monsters were the perfect backup for a fight like this.

"Alright." Ves whispered to Vylith, extending her claws and rising to a half-crouch. "Almost ready-"

Vylith leapt forward in a blur of motion, phasing straight through a series of trees and aiming directly for Jade. Ves swore under her breath, cursing the demon's lack of patience. Would it have killed her to wait a few more seconds?

I hate working with demons…

Ves charged after her with a snarl, closing the distance with supernatural speed. Her eyes fell upon the closest of the group, a large man wearing thick armor. He wasn't the succubus, but he'd do for a start. Blood simmering with barely contained hunger, Ves led her newly summoned host of spirits into battle.

Tara's expertise in artifice saved them. Alerted by her demon detection pendant, Jade spotted the attack just in time. She parried the demon's lightning fast thrust with Phantom and Fang, deflecting the attack to the side and leaping out of her path. Vylith surged through their midst, scattering the group like a bowling ball as everyone scrambled to react to the unexpected threat. She didn't get a good look at her assailant, but the feel of her aura was all she needed. It was her, the wraith demon who had nearly eviscerated Naomi back on Earth. Righteous fury roiled within Jade's spirit, and she took off in pursuit, only to detect more creatures rapidly approaching from the same direction. To her surprise, she recognized another one of the auras. It was dark and sickly sweet, like the scent of a poisonous berry.

"There's more!" Fenrin shouted, beating Jade to the punch. "The vampire!"

"Our enemies are working together now." Luis grunted, hefting his shield and turning to face the new threat. "That's just awesome."

Jade slipped between them, weaving through darkened trees in pursuit of the demon.

"Just saves us the trouble of hunting them down." She said. Her heart thundered in her chest, but by now they were all accustomed to sudden ambushes and could resist the urge to panic. The other monsters began to come into view. A host of purple spirits, easily spotted against the backdrop of the nighttime forest, flowed towards them, led by a familiar figure in black. The vampire, Ves. The wraith demon's momentum was still carrying her away from the group, so Jade risked a quick glance at the monsters.

Specter

Level: 21

Creature Type: Undead

Origin: Summoned

She checked another, confirming they were the same level. Good. There was a half dozen of them, but they were weak enough to handle.

"Tara, with me!" She shouted, resuming her pursuit of the wraith demon. "The rest of you, focus on the undead!"

They moved with the practiced efficiency of a team, splitting into two groups to confront the unexpected enemies. Jade was proud to note that everyone had responded quickly to the surprise attack, even Siora. The elf joined Naomi behind Luis and Fenrin, and a moment later the pounding chords of Naomi's fleetfoot spell began blasting through the forest.

"Spotlight." Tara said, dropping in beside Jade.

"Right."

After their first encounter with the wraith demon, they'd spent some time discussing how to face other powerful, elusive foes that they couldn't just unload damage into until they dropped. It was time to put theory into practice, and Jade couldn't think of a better target to unleash her pent-up frustration on. The rest of her companions clashed with the undead behind them just as Vylith rushed at the two of them from the front, her spectral form unhindered by trees or undergrowth. They exchanged a flurry of blows and Jade, despite her resolve, was immediately put on the defensive. Even with Naomi's speed boost, she wasn't faster than the demon, and her strength was well and truly eclipsed. While her enchanted blades and manifested aura could both block the demon's incorporeal weapon, they didn't grant her body the power to meet her blows head on.

Fortunately, she had backup.

Tara fought by her side, slinging beam after beam of energy at the elusive demon. These weren't her standard blasts of searing flames, but rather concentrated cones of blinding light. While the spell did little direct damage, the broad attacks were hard to avoid. Even better, Vylith's incorporeal form didn't protect her from being dazzled by repeated blasts of intense luminous energy. The first few staggered her, disrupting the rhythm of her relentless attacks and allowing Jade to score a counter or two. But the wraith demon was a fast learner, and she was prepared to face Jade's celestially infused strikes. Each successive clash yielded less impressive results as she began to successfully ward off Tara's spells, fighting with a fluid grace that denied Jade the opportunity to land a decisive blow.

Frustrated as another one of her attacks met nothing but empty air, Jade changed tactics. She dropped rune traps, fired sprays of daggers with hail blade, and conjured clouds of darkness to disrupt her opponent. These tricks did much to even the odds, but both her and Tara's defenses had already sustained heavy damage from Vylith's initial onslaught. Jade gritted her teeth, launching herself away from the demon's lightning-fast blade with a powerful flap of her wings. Her armor's barrier cracked, a portion of it shattering as it absorbed the edge of the attack's force. She cursed under her breath. This wasn't working.

It was time to try something else.

Sheathing both daggers, Jade wiped away a line of blood dripping into her eye and pulled a coiled whip from the hook on her belt. Vylith paused her assault, eyeing the weapon with a spark of interest. She stopped a dozen paces from them, still in a ready crouch.

"That is no Labyrinth weapon you carry."

"I took it from one of your kind." Jade growled. She rejoined Tara, trying to hide the fact that she was panting. Vylith, on the other hand, looked like she'd barely broken a sweat. "Malice. We took her down, and we're going to do the same to you."

"And all the others that crawl out of the hole the gods put you in." Tara added. Her nostrils flared. "Your people have caused enough pain."

"Pain, suffering." The wraith demon shook her head. "Joy, happiness… none of it matters. Our ancestors were promised the stars." Vylith's eyes narrowed, and her aura erupted, slamming into them with enough force to send them staggering. "And we will have them!"

The battle resumed, fiercer than ever. Jade was dimly aware of Luis, Fenrin, and her other companions fighting off a group of ghastly spirits in the near distance, interspersed with clashes against the vampire. But soon, that all faded. Jade entered a trance-like state, her focus absolute. Vylith was an overwhelming opponent, moving with the speed and grace of a practiced predator. Without Tara backing her up, Jade wouldn't have lasted a minute. As it was, it took the two of them everything they had just to keep her at bay.

Jade's whip cracked, barely grazing Vylith as she ducked in close, her dagger flashing silver in a shaft of moonlight. It bit into Jade's arm, spraying blood in a wide arc and sending a flash of pain lancing through her shoulder. Another of Tara's spells blinded her assailant before she could follow up, giving Jade enough time to slip out of range and resume her attacks. Getting in close with Vylith was suicide. She was equally fast and far stronger, and now she knew to be wary of Jade's radiant aura. She didn't rely on her incorporeal nature to resist attacks, instead deflecting and dodging them with a skill that told Jade she wasn't the first of Vylith's opponents to possess abilities that posed a true threat to her. This demon was no one-trick-pony, nor was she arrogant enough to think herself invincible. Great. This was exactly what they needed. Their enemies were getting smarter.

Jade growled in frustration, lashing out with a series of slashes. She wasn't as practiced with the whip as she was with her daggers, but neither had she neglected it in her training. Its enhanced reach allowed her to land hits from beyond Vylith's striking range, and it was quick enough to catch her. Unfortunately, Jade's aura didn't extend far enough to coat the whip's lash, and the blows she did land appeared to inflict no more than minor wounds. Barely suppressed anger pulled at Jade's mind, threatening to drag her into a state of reckless fury. This was the monster that had almost killed her friend. This creature was the reason Alan still suffered. A red haze began to cloud her vision. The wraith demon was all she could see. A ghostly form, long and limber, flitting through the forest as a streak of crimson. She would-

Vylith surged forward, her dagger slashing in a silver arc. Jade blinked away with Mirage Step and then shot into the air, preparing to dive at the demon from the air.

But she hadn't been aiming for Jade.

Tara screamed in pain as the demon's dagger and claws tore through the chest piece of her summoned armor, shattering it and digging deep into her torso. A large crack accompanied the fountain of blood and Tara dropped to the ground, moaning. The sight shocked Jade out of her trance, and awareness returned to her in a cold rush. Tara was out of the fight. Luis, Naomi, and the others were still fighting off the vampire and her minions. It was just Jade and Vylith. The wraith demon stood over Tara's fallen, weakly stirring form, her blade already raised to deliver a killing blow.

"Like hell you are!"

Jade dove. The sudden assault must've caught Vylith by surprise, because Jade didn't pass through her. Instead, she slammed into the larger woman, sending them both crashing into a thick cluster of bushes. Jade clung to her, clawing and tearing at her flesh like a deranged beast. She flared her aura, feeling it burn against the wraith demon's form as it enveloped them both. Vylith shrieked, wildly slashing over her shoulder with blade and claw as Jade continued to cling to her back. She had to keep her off-balance, not give her a chance to recover. She-

WHAM

A sudden impact launched Jade back into the air. Her reflexes kicked in before her mind caught up, sending her twisting into something resembling a controlled landing even as something in her chest made a painful crunch. She rose to her feet, whip still held in a trembling hand. Her chest felt tight, and each inhalation sent sharp spasms through her body. The straps of Jade's succubus armor sparked and flickered, the defensive enchantments taxed to the limit. Tara lay beside her, still stirring feebly.

They couldn't lose now. Not after the road to victory had finally revealed itself to them.

I'm not letting that happen.

Resolve broke into her mind like water through a collapsing dam, washing away the pain and fear. She wasn't out of this, not by a long shot.

"Pathetic." The word dripped with disdain. Vylith stalked out of the bushes, her face a mask of scorn. "You fight like a child."

The demon moved more slowly now, and blood flowed down her side. She was wounded, but not enough.

One last chance…

Jade slipped a hand into her belt pouch. She hadn't let the anger overwhelm her. She had a plan. It wasn't a smart plan, but it was a plan.

"Yeah? Well, this child took your arm off last time." Another step closer. She just had to keep her talking. "Or don't you remember?"

Anger flashed in Vylith's eyes. Jade felt it in her mind, a ripple that disturbed the disciplined detachment of a seasoned killer. She kept approaching.

Almost there…

Jade resisted the urge to tense up, fearing it would give her away. She slumped back against a tree, one hand pressed to her side to stem the flow of blood.

"Anyone can get lucky once." Vylith said coolly. "But it won't happen twi-"

"Stop."

Jade met the demon's gaze with one of blazing fire. Strength fled her body as nearly all of her remaining mana soaked into that one word, the A-rank Allure spell siphoning everything it could from her reserves. Strengthened by the eye contact and Jade's spell penetration ability, the spell slammed into Vylith's mind like a psychic sledgehammer. But the elite wraith demon's defenses were formidable, and even that might not have been enough… if not for Jade's final advantage.

Subjugation

Rarity: Rare

Requirements: Can only be wielded by a succubus.

This enchanted whip strikes with unerring precision, easily slipping past defenses and penetrating armor and spell barriers. Wielding this whip enhances the 'Allure' spell, granting it greater duration and potency. When you successfully strike a target with Subjugation you may apply the 'Will Breaker' debuff.

Will Breaker: Applies a temporary penalty to the creature's willpower. This debuff stacks.

Jade felt the moment her spell found the cracks in Vylith's force of will. A psychic connection flared to life between them. It was weak and tenuous, already breaking down as Vylith's mind raged against its prison, but, for a single breath, she stopped.

And that was all Jade needed.

She wrenched the splinter of Destruction free from her belt, pointing it directly at Vylith's chest. Jade only had a split second to act, and she wasn't going to waste it with an attack that might not finish the job.

"Go big…" She whispered, sending a tendril of aura into the divine artifact as Elaria had instructed. "or go-"

The artifact triggered with a flash of violet light and a deafening crack of thunder. And just like that, everything in front of Jade was wiped away.
