The Samanthian side had little choice but to divide their attention after the ‘eye rings’ were summoned — on the ground, the elites protecting Glae were fully ready and waiting to respond to the challenge, striking as soon as the rings were.

The dragon-form of Athos let out a gout of seething fire, burning up perhaps half a dozen tentacles in one go, even as it carried through and scorched the ring and the eyes themselves. Bob cast a destructive divine light spell that began firing out lasers to fry the tentacled weapons that got close. Aodhan has shapeshifted into a massive treant with six branching arms, grabbing and wrestling away many attacks at once. Gelmak tossed two axes, chopping through more extended limbs and the ring viscera itself.

And most effectively of all, due to the sheer mass of attacks needing to be countered, Gabriel leapt right next to the cage and cried out, “Evil shall not prevail against my shield!” This activated Absolute Defense from the Heaven Wall Shield.

The weaponized limbs striking for people, and namely, Glae himself, focused on the ritual inside his protective Antimage cage... all accomplished nothing, unable to penetrate the protective energy field that stopped all harm from being dealt to the wielder’s allies.

Even as she winced from the awful line, Samantha felt a sense of profound relief, because that had quite possibly just succeeded in being a hard counter. Indeed, multiple ‘pointy’ type tentacles were rebuffed even as they stabbed inside the cage and hit Glae.

Samantha noted the detail and commanded in the gestalt, “Focus on the stabby tentacles! Don’t go for things that can’t fit through the cage gaps until the more relevant weapons are severed.”

The rings proved tough as nails against ensuing attacks, though the absolute elites in the mix, like Gelmak, could slay the eyes, at which point whole clusters of tentacles were disabled. But the eldritch thing also quickly regenerated after any damage.

AoE attacks pivoted from light-based to fire, acid, and pneuma. Fire was actually the least effective, making the more exotic stuff better for the situation, particularly pneuma. Normally, high-intensity magic of that nature was an arcane specialty, but the cleromages of Samantha had little trouble switching to rarer types. It was one of their things, just as it was hers.

In any case, Samantha spared no more time in thought or observation of the ring defense, trusting her compatriots to deal with it effectively under Gabriel’s protection.

Samantha began a front against the colossal demon, designed to confuse and punish it with high-intensity attacks. She knew from her initial movement that the demon was watching her with no less than three eyes, and was definitely aiming to reserve most of its defensive focus for her, now that she’d proven herself the current largest threat.

She made two copies of herself via [Conjure], one made to flow independently and one copying her motions as she phased in and out of it, making it hard to determine which was the greater threat. Furthermore, her mimic was easy to use without much action economy, as it was trivial for her to ‘spend time’ on something both primarily mental and simple enough not to need much tactical precision. The latter ‘sapient computation’ was the main limit of taking actions in addition to the physical restraints. If the control was very simplistic, like ‘copy what I’m doing,’ it was a pretty minuscule effort.

Another plus was that a [Conjure] copy wasn't an illusion and could emulate physical effects. It wasn’t strong enough against this thing, but it was something.

Working in coordination with others, Samantha first slung out a combination pneumanic plasma cone and a new engagement with her staff, though not going for a Charged Strike. Unsurprisingly, walls blocked her, but she at least didn’t waste an SP on blowing up a wall. In fact, it wasted two walls against ‘useless’ Conjure attacks, so she’d occupied the majority of its defenses already.

The walls were cracked, but not destroyed, and hung there after blocking, like a new branch extending from the demon, as connected by viscera. But interestingly, it wouldn’t ‘move’ walls to block. Instead, after a moment or two, the viscera retracted, and the wall began to fall and degenerate into fuming ash.

It creates faster than it moves, it seems.

Her occupation of its defenses was not without purpose, as her stronger allies swooped down in response, timed in coordination.

Two more walls blocked angles directed at its head and two of its hands. However, it couldn’t block access entirely, and here was where two dragons, Dart and Porthos, moved in, grappling and chomping down with intent not only to maim, but at the very least to occupy and gum up the demon’s castings. To this end, both dragons, once biting on the hand, breathed destructive fire on it, and then moved their claws in, wrapping around like snakes. Their size was such that each was the length of an arm, presenting quite an issue to the demon, who looked a bit consternated by the incident.

“Problematic reptiles,” the demon commented.

“Grrrr! Mrrg rhargh rrrgh!” came Dart’s grunted replies as he bit and chewed and clawed with fury.

“Good boy!” Samantha called. “Get that nasty demon!”

The demon’s one free hand had a swooping Deathsting wrap around it, biting, clawing with hind legs, and stinging with his tail as he called out, “This should teach you of your betters, you great fleshy sack of worthless, never-belongs-here!” Though significantly smaller, Deathsting was actually stronger than the dragons, and his attacks ripped up the fingers of a hand, exposing meat and causing blood to ooze from multiple lacerating wounds, almost certainly making it impossible to form signs with that hand.

Meanwhile, Azure tossed a potion at its face. To this, it turned its head away and got splashed on the side and back of its face with a corrosive alchemical substance.

Samantha had her additional haste action stacking on top of it all, for which the demon had clearly saved its ‘seventh’ wall block for, despite everything else.

Instead of attacking, Samantha, via [Mirroring Duplicates], summoned Ozra.

‘Against an unknown king, bring an unknown jester.’

Ozra materialized in the air in an appearance clearly showing she’d read the intent: she was dressed in a full harlequin outfit, complete with curling hat points and a checkered dress in elaborate checker patterns of red, black, and white.

With her arms and legs splayed out as a bright aura glowed around her, she cried, “Ta-da! Now this is how you stack the deck, baby!” Grinning, she pointed and winked at the demon. “Suck on this, scum!”

Ozra snapped her fingers, and a gigantic, curling mass of crystalline hard light materialized, the checkered patterns and colors of her outfit all represented. It shot for the demon’s face like a giant skewer.

“Already tried that, Ozzy,” Samantha muttered.

Ozra smiled brightly with her eyes closed. “Just watch, girlie!”

Predictably, a wall formed to block the spear. The hard light growth didn’t shatter against it, instead bowling over and around it and continuing on as if nothing had interrupted it. The crystal grew all over the demon’s head in multiple layers, blinding it.

“Ha!” Samantha exclaimed. “Good idea, yeah.”

“Thanks!” Orza replied, giving a thumbs-up. “This is a lot harder without spells…”

“Tell me about it. But I think we played a good hand.”

“We’re playing cards?” the demon asked. “In that case, it’s fair to say we need to up the ante.”

The hardlight around the demon’s head buckled and then shattered in multiple places, as six eyes burst through and went down, falling, even seeming to dive with quickly-building momentum, falling like meteors, their trails not of fire, but of gross viscera.

Samantha, as only she could, took quick stock of their trajectory and called in the gestalt, “They’re going for the stones, and those motherfuckers are going to hit hard, I think! We have to block them!”

The force and speed involved convinced Samantha quickly that the six falling eyes were too much for her to stop at once. She was fast enough in the time allowed to perhaps block one. She could cram more time in through the use of a defense — which she hadn’t needed due to the demon’s intelligent priority of targeting — and [Light Speed: Precisive Action].

Simple and stupid was necessary under the circumstances. Through [Matter, As Spirit], she slammed one with her full, raw telekinetic force as hard as she could. The eye was rammed out of the air and knocked off course. She did the same thing with a second eye, hitting it with the force of a hurled train, punting it away from the circle stones and sending it spinning.

A rarity of others were prepared to defend against the sudden, speedy attack. The high-level Naugite war priest, Revemort, had previously been calling forth a giant divine-energy gauntlet fist to attack the flesh rings — he just managed to divert his attention to an eye and grab it in time, pulling it to tumble out in the open dirt of the space free of the standing circles.

Several other attacks from Servitors failed to move the eyes enough or stop them.

Three eyes continued down, nearing impact. Samantha wasn’t sure the ritual could handle all that disruption of energy…
