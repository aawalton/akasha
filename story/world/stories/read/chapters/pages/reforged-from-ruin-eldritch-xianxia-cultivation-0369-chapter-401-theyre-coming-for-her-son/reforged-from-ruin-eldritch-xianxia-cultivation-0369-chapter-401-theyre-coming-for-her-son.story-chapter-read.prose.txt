A Blessed Mortal is an interesting religious conundrum, at least on its surface. To the uninitiated, a veneration of life in a Church of Death might well seem a madness, or at least an oxymoron. However, any deeper analysis reveals the truth- there is no Death without Life. There is no Life without Death. They are two sides of the same coin, as real and as concrete as the beliefs of the savages and their “yin and yang”, as real and concrete as nothing-and-something. Without Life, Death would not exist.

Thus, while the nature of conversion demands the mantling of Death, and the more powerful and precise that Death, the deeper its truth. A life, well lived and properly refined, inevitably leads to a fuller Death, and thus, the cycle of refinement continues, multiplies, enhances itself.

The power of Death is an inversion. It is the mantling and empowering of an Echo, made all the vaster by the size of what came before, made all the vaster by the void it leaves behind in the moment of transition.

What, then, does it mean to be a Life that is already loved by Death? What would it mean, for an inversion to already possess what is being Inverted? For an Echo to have more Echoes within it?

A Blessed Mortal is only an oxymoron to the uninitiated. To those who see, who understand, it is the refinement and evolution of an already potent truth, made all the more real.
