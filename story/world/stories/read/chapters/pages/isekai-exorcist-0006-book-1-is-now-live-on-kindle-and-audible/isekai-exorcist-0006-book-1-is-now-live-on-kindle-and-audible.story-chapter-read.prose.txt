Book 1 of Isekai Exorcist just published on Amazon today, and I hope you will check it out!

It is available on Kindle Unlimited and Audible, as well as in paperback for anyone who'd like a physical copy.

Kindle: https://www.royalroad.com/amazon/B0DZ8N4MYGAudible: https://www.audible.com/pd/Isekai-Exorcist-Audiobook/B0F84Y971P

I self-pubbed my story Madman Apocalypse in May and that was quite a stressful ride, so this time I'm happy to kind of sit back and let Aethon take the reins, since they know what they're doing.

There have been a lot of minor changes to book 1 of the story, since I had a lot of stuff I wanted to tweak, and because I knew that many of the opening chapters were quite rough around the edges. If you have Kindle Unlimited, I hope you'll check it out and see these changes for yourself, but I'm personally very happy with the final result.

I think the nature of Isekai Exorcist meant that it didn't get as big on RR as other isekai stories tend to, but I am confident that it will find more readers like those of you who have enjoyed it on here now that it's on Amazon.

I'm finally done with my exams and have the next two months to write to my heart's content. As soon as I wrap up book 3 of Madman Apocalypse, I will immediately begin on book 4 of Isekai Exorcist, so I hope y'all will look forward to that.

After writing quite LitRPG-heavy stories like Loopshard and Madman, I am looking forward to returning to this series. Honestly, of all the books I've written, I think this series has my favourite writing and pacing. I also just really love how Ryuuta grew as a character while I wrote the first three books.
