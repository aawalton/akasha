Callie and Callen shared a troubled glance.

"Please, tell me that it was an owl…" Callen looked at Callie imploringly.

"Brother… That was a bit too loud for an owl, don't you think?" Callie said, her heart beating like crazy.

Why had the glauxis come to the village now? It must have been prowling near the village for weeks, keeping a cautious distance. Yet now it chose to announce its presence to everyone? Why?

But then a realization struck her, and her whole body broke into cold sweat.

"This is my fault…" Callie muttered.

"What? How is this your fault?" Callen looked at her in surprise.

"The egg. I moved it. The glauxis must have sensed it somehow, and now, when he's felt it, he must be trying desperately to find it," Callie explained.

"Y-You don't know if that's true. This might be a coincidence…" Callen said, but he didn't sound very sure.

Another loud hoot resounded outside, and Callie could hear Dad's troubled voice beyond the door.

"NO!" he screamed.

Callie and Callen hurriedly came out of the room, only to find Mom already half-dressed in her armor with a sword clutched in one hand.

"Maeve, you're not even fully healed yet!" Dad was holding Mom firmly by her arm, preventing her from darting out of the house.

"I'm well enough to fight! Let go, Rowan," Mom hissed, trying to get free of Dad's grip.

"Think of the children!" Dad snarled.

"I am thinking exactly of them! There's no way I'm going to let that monster harm them. Let me go, Rowan!" Mom yelled.

Callie watched the scene in horror. Mom was about to fight the glauxis. No, no, no… This couldn't be happening. If they fought again… Then one of them would die for certain. Perhaps even both. All because Callie had found the egg and not returned it to the glauxis as soon as she had. All because she had been selfish and worried about getting punished.

"Mom, you can't go!" Callen said, joining Dad in trying to hold Mom back.

"Don't get involved in this, Callen! You are to remain at home and guard your sister. That's an order!" Mom said firmly, making Callen flinch and take a step back.

"Maeve, you must leave this to other hunters!" Dad said desperately.

"This isn't a monster one hunter can handle. Rowan, if you don't let me go this instant, I'm going to get seriously mad!" Mom warned.

Callie's heart raced. She couldn't let Mom fight the glauxis. She didn't want either of them to get hurt. Nor did she want anyone from the village to get hurt. She had made a complete mess of things, and the people she loved might get hurt because of it.

She couldn't let that happen. She wouldn't let that happen. There was only one thing she could do. This time, Callie would take any punishment, so long as the matter itself could be solved peacefully.

"I'm going to go!" Callie announced as loudly as her shaky voice allowed her.

The argument stopped instantly as everyone looked at her in shock. Callen looked most terrified of all, because he actually understood what Callie wanted to do.

"Callie, no!" He said, shaking his head.

"What do you mean by that, child?" Mom looked at Callie as if she had gone crazy.

"Calluna, now is not the time for you to play that monsters are nice. Head to your room this instant," Dad demanded, pointing angrily at the door of her room.

"No! You don't understand anything. The glauxis is angry because his egg was taken away. I have found the egg, so I will return it to the glauxis," Callie said, although her voice came out sounding far more shaky than she would have liked. It wasn't easy speaking up against her parents when they both looked like they were fuming with rage.

"What kind of nonsense are you spouting? Calluna, now is not the time!" Mom said dismissively, prying her arm free from Dad's grip. "I don't have time for this. Rowan, watch the kids! I'm going."

"NO!" Callie ran towards Mom, grabbing her hand desperately. "Please, trust me! If we return the egg to the glauxis, it will leave without hurting anyone! Please!"

Mom looked angrier than Callie had ever seen her. She could feel her muscles tighten underneath her grip. She opened her mouth to say something, but Callie hurried to speak before her.

"I met the glauxis before. I talked to him. He told me his egg was taken. Please, trust me."

"What?!" Mom exclaimed in shock.

"Callie, enough for your childish fantasies," Dad snapped, reaching for Callie.

But Callen grabbed his hand firmly. "It's true. We met the glauxis before!"

Now, both of their parents stared at Callen in disbelief.

"You… met the glauxis?" Mom spoke slowly, clearly not believing a word they were saying.

"What are you kids talking about? Speaking to a monster? Missing egg? Callen, if you're enabling Callie's fantasies…" Dad said in a dark tone.

"I'm not! Callie, she has…" Callen looked at Callie with a pained expression. He clearly didn't dare to expose her secret without her consent.

Callie appreciated his thoughtfulness, even in this grim situation. But it didn't look like Mom and Dad would believe her otherwise, so she rolled up her sleeve, revealing the mark with two petals on her forearm.

"I… I can use a bit of magic. It lets me understand arcane beasts…" she admitted.

Dad glanced at the mark, his expression twisting with anger. "Callie, this isn't funny! While this scar is concerning, claiming that it's magic is… Maeve?"

Mom was examining her mark carefully, tracing her fingers over it. Callie could feel a slight tingle where her warm fingers traced her mark, as if the magic was answering her touch. Callie leaned into the feeling. She tried to push away the fear and panic she was feeling, for just a moment. Instead, she focused on Mom's warm touch and a strong wish to show her the truth.

In response, her mark glowed. It was a weak glow, nothing like when she touched Wiggles or Snowglow. But nobody could deny that it was, in fact, glowing.

"Holy…" Dad's jaw dropped.

"That is magic…" Mom said in disbelief, then looked at Callie with wonder. "But how?"

"I… I'll explain later. But could you trust me this one time? Let me return the glauxis egg!" Callie begged, grabbing onto Mom's hand.

"Please trust Callie!" Callen grabbed Mom's other hand, also begging her.

"This is too much… Returning glauxis's egg… Would that really work?" Mom looked unsure, but Callie felt that she could be persuaded.

"Wouldn't it be better if the glauxis left without needing to fight it? Without anyone getting hurt? Isn't it worth trying at the very least?"

Mom appeared deep in thought, while Dad seemed out of it completely, muttering "magic, magic, magic" as he paced around the room with his hands pressed against his temples.

"I… I frankly still can't believe any of this. My daughter has magic. You two met the glauxis and walked away without so much as a scratch. That alone is worth something," Mom said, then let out a heavy sigh. "I can't believe I am saying this, but okay. Let's try returning this egg. Where is it?"

Callie couldn't help but break into a smile and throw her arms around Mom. She had little hope that a hunter would actually be willing to not fight an arcane beast. The fact that Mom wanted to solve this peacefully made her happier than anything.

"Thank you, thank you!" Callie said, latching onto her arm.

"Maeve, you can't seriously be entertaining this!" Dad said, coming out of his stupor.

"Rowan, that is a glauxis. It is not only powerful, but also incredibly agile and can fly. We, hunters, barely stand any chance against it. If there's but the slightest chance that it will leave peacefully and never return, I will take it!" Mom said, then looked at Callie. "Lead me to the egg."

"I'm coming too," Callen said.

"Absolutely not!" Mom said firmly. "If it's just Callie, then I can easily get her out of danger if necessary. I cannot protect you both. Rowan, make sure this boy stays inside."

"But… I can't just stay here if Callie is going," Callen pleaded.

"You're only going to get in the way!" Dad said firmly. "You're not yet ready."

Callen looked like he had been slapped and stepped back dejectedly. Callie felt a little bad for her big brother, but there was no time to console him. The glauxis let out another loud hoot, and bustle could be heard outside as the villagers no doubt discovered the threat.

"Maeve…" Dad looked at Mom, and it looked like he wanted to stop her. But in the end, he clenched his fist. "Please, keep our little girl safe!"

"You know that I will," Mom said and placed a quick kiss on Dad's lips.

Callie looked away. Was now really the time for kissing?

As soon as they were done, Callie grabbed Mom's hand and pulled her to the back entrance of the house. "The egg is in the forest!"

Dusk had descended into the forest, making it difficult to see, but even so Callie had no trouble finding Tristan's secret base again. It was almost too easy, she realized. There weren't actually that many spruces growing close to their home, so his secret base actually wasn't that secret at all.

Callie's heart beat like crazy. She feared that the thief might have found the egg in the brief moment she had been left alone. But to her relief, when Callie crawled into the secret base, it was still there. Some of the ferns had slipped off the egg, though, exposing it completely.

Perhaps it was a good thing that she had to return it now. Who knew if it would have remained here until morning? The egg stood out against the green foliage. It wouldn't be difficult to imagine that somebody could see it through the holes in the branches. Especially if they were looking for it.

"I got the egg!" Callie announced as she got out of the base, passing the egg to Mom.

Mom took it gingerly, staring at the egg in disbelief. "There really is an egg… It's so big."

"We have to take it glauxis!"

"We? No, kiddo. You're going to be heading back home," Mom said firmly, clutching the egg.

"What?"

"You heard me. I'm not going to expose you to the glauxis and risk you getting hurt or worse. There's no guarantee that the monster will calm down just because we returned its egg," Mom said.

Callie opened her mouth to argue, but the expression on Mom's face said that she wouldn't budge this time. Time spent arguing was time wasted when people and the glauxis could be fighting. It was best to take the egg to him as quickly as possible.

"I understand. I'm going to go home. But you have to give this to the glauxis as soon as possible!" Callie insisted.

"You must head straight home!" Mom said firmly, staring daggers at Callie.

"Yes, I'm going. Just please hurry!" Callie said, turning and running towards home.

Mom watched her run for a moment, then rushed into the village at once, clutching the egg in her grasp. Callie sighed uneasily, looking over her shoulder at where Mom ran. It was difficult to convince herself to head home. She wanted to be there when the glauxis got his egg back. She wanted to be sure that everyone was safe.

But she was just a kid. Mom could handle this better than she could. Callie would trust her Mom. She would wait at home along with…

A loud hoot resounded through the village.

HUNTER! RETURN MY CHILD!

Despite the distance Callie could clearly hear the blind rage of the glauxis. It was then that Callie realized that the glauxis and Mom had already met before, and then they were intent on killing each other.

To the glauxis, Mom wasn't returning the egg. She was the hunter that had tried to kill him. It wasn't difficult to imagine his reaction to seeing this supposed killer with his egg in her arms.

Callie couldn't let her Mom get hurt. She had to save her! She was the only one who could talk to the glauxis.
