Standing, almost drained of mana, a round ripped through his arm, then another slammed into his shoulder, putting him on his ass again. Pain had caught up at that point, and there was plenty to go around. A few Singers were foolish enough to get within reach, and Luke leapt at them through weavestep like a rabid vampire, sucking them dry of mana to feed his own regeneration. The fighters weren't enough to get him back up to more than the halfway point mana-wise, but it would have to do, because Luke had more to worry about than just himself. Ella.

They'd both come to a sudden stop when she ran at full speed into a see-through wall. Bullets and blasts of energy rained down on what was left of her. If not for Luke's Guardian Weaves, there wouldn't be much to find other than the splatter of red running down the wall. Other than those streaks, there was nothing to give away the trap they'd erected to put a very successful stop to Ella's rampage. A weavestep and a lunge brought him to what was essentially a brain, a spine, and a set of organs giving off the faintest of light blue glows. Connecting himself to the brain through a thread, he cast Weaver's Mercy, then reached out to the weave in desperation and clutched the first echo thread he found.

They left chaos behind but found it at their destination as well, where those from The Band and Phoenix Phalanx destroyed every Singer in their way.

"Mana!" Luke shouted.

Penny descended on a gust of wind, lightning crackling through her hair as her eyes glowed a bright yellow.

"What is that?" she asked. "It's not a... is it?"

"It is!"

"Take my mana," she said as she brought out the Mana Orb she kept. Luke needed no other invitation. No more than a few seconds had elapsed since their accident at that point, and his senses told him Ella was still alive, in a way, if the meat in front of him could still be called Ella. There were no guarantees that this would work.

"Why is Luke naked?" he heard Nick call out from over where they were fighting a group of Singers standing between them and the turrets on this platform. Curtis was there as well, fighting alongside Hannah, who threw a worried glance Luke's way, and the two of them were crushing any resistance standing in their way.

Weaver's Renewal drained almost all of Penny's considerable stores of mana, and by then, he'd reached out with threads of mana, jumping through allies until he reached Singers, draining them as well. Little by little, Ella returned. It was like watching a 3D printer creating a person from the inside out, with a skeleton forming in no more than five seconds, followed by nerves, organs, and muscles. Only when the skin returned, and her hair regrew in its natural, undyed color, did she open her eyes and draw breath. All the air from that first breath turned into screams, then gasps and more screams. She kicked and flailed, then turned around and crawled into the fetal position, weeping and screaming.

Luke purchased a large robe from the Integrated Shop and put it over her, then got some clothes for himself. Rather than let the Singers go, he extended his reach and poured mana into himself and Penny, maxing their mana pools. Only when his meta-heart was once again at capacity with healing mana did he stop. At that point, there were no more Singers to fight.

Hannah ran up to them. "What happened?"

"We almost died," Luke said. He tried to put a hand on Ella's shoulder, but that only made it worse.

Penny shuddered. "Maybe it's best if we give her a little time."

"There's nothing wrong with her, except a couple of missing tattoos," Luke said. "I think it's shock."

"That poor girl was reduced to a brain," Penny said. "I'd say trauma is a valid response to that."

Hannah walked up to stand next to him. "How are you doing?"

"I'm fine." He put his arm around her shoulders. "You?"

They settled in and waited until Ella's screams turned into whimpers.

"We need to push," Curtis said, walking up to the group. "And there are more Singers coming through the other portals." A group of Singers walked through at that precise moment, but Curtis's arm extended as it turned black, and it engulfed the new arrivals in darkness before they got a single attack off. When that darkness turned back into an arm, there were no signs of Singers ever having stood in that spot. The limb was much larger at first, but returned to normal size, as if compressing itself. Luke reached out and closed all portals except one, leaving a way for the group to retreat.

"I can't go anywhere without Ella, but you should keep going. I'm sure there's a plan B. Also, Ray said the Singers are getting ready to blast Earth into rubble, so we should probably look into that."

"Probably," Hannah agreed.

He pulled her into a hug.

"Oh, the romance!" Penny said with a fake giggle, pretending to swoon.

"Let's just go," Nick sighed. "I'm relaying all this to Milla so she can coordinate. You guys are a bunch of dumbasses." He raised his voice so that it reached Curtis, who was already halfway down the ramp. "That goes for you, too, brother dearest! Dumbass!"

"Shut up!" Curtis shouted back without looking as he morphed into a wave of darkness and descended on the next wave of Singers.

"Don't be a stranger!" Penny shouted as she let a gust of magic wind carry her into the air.

Hannah just squeezed his hand before following along with the rest. That left Luke alone with Ella. Except she was gone.

"Shit," he muttered, and reached out to the weave.

Luke landed in freezing salt water with no land in sight anywhere. Keeping above water was a struggle despite his enhanced physical strength, and he sputtered as he weavestepped again, appearing on dry land next to a sign that read Ballyhamon. Another jump put him next to a lighthouse.

Luke: "Ella! Stop!"

Ella: "Don't you dare follow me!"

Ella: "I'm alive, so thank you, I think, but I'm not going back there!"

Luke: "Just wanted to make sure you're OK."

Ella: "I'm not OK!"

Ella: "I was dead! Please leave me alone."

Luke dismissed the weave and wiped the blood coming out of his nose with the sleeve of his shirt. People were chattering around him, and he spotted a group of Singers in the distance. They hadn't seen him yet, so he sat down on a bench to stare out at the sea.

"Går det bra med deg, unge mann?" an old lady asked him.

He turned to look at her. "Sorry, what?"

That startled her, but she powered through it and spoke in broken English. "Duh. Yuh. Need. Uuuh. Hjelp?"

"No," he said, forcing a smile.

That seemed to satisfy her, and she nodded as she left to rejoin her group of old people boarding a bus. The Singers were coming down the road and would get to Luke's position in a moment, but Luke just needed a moment to rest. He'd come awfully close to drawing his last breath. Perhaps he should react more like Ella, but he didn't have it in him. This wasn't his first brush with death, after all, and it wouldn't be his last. Weary didn't begin to describe what he was feeling, though, and he wasn't sure what to do now that he didn't have Ella to hitch a ride with. Closing his eyes, he just enjoyed the breeze. That's when Ray's words struck home. In the chaos following the crash, he'd forgotten all about them.

Luke: "They mentioned Relian?"

Ray: "About time you checked in."

Luke: "I was in the middle of a crisis."

Ray: "When are you not? Anyway, yeah, they didn't say much, but they mentioned him in the same, uh, talk about integration with The Greater System. They've got him somewhere in Singer territory, I think. Kind of creepy if you ask me. It's like I'm expecting to stumble into him whenever I round a corner."

Luke: "Can you find out more? But also, be careful. You know what'll happen if you're not."

Ray: "Trust me, I know. But no, I don't think so."

Luke: "Why not?"

Ray: "Those guys are kind of dead."

Ray: "I killed them."

Ray: "They were about to send out the order for those big attacks. Figured if they're dead, they can't order the destruction of, you know, Earth."

Luke: "No, I get it."

Ray: "Now there are all kinds of alarms going off here, and people are running around and shouting. And by people, I mean Singers."

Luke: "How inclusive of you."

Luke: "Hold on, I've got company."

Ray: "Got to go, anyway. Talk to you in a bit. And hey, be ready to come and get me out of here. Don't want to end up in another one of those trap scenarios."

Ray: "That reminds me. Can you teleport to that other Lifeweaver guy? I'm not giving up my dagger."

Ray: "That's my dagger, Luke."

Ray: "I wants it."

Luke: "I'll see what I can do."

Luke: "In return, find me those big weapons."

Ray: "I'll think about it."

The group of Singers wasn't anything special. If he had to guess, he'd put them below level twenty-five. Three out of the four carried spears, with the fourth wielding a wand that looked a lot like a random stick he'd found and put a glass bead on top. Judging by the civilian human reaction, seeing Singers wasn't out of the ordinary, and the Singers themselves didn't hassle the humans. Their showing up seemed to be more about making sure the people, even out in this isolated corner of what Luke guessed was Norway, didn't forget they were under occupation.

Rather than kill them, Luke ignored the group, and they ignored him, unable to even sense he was Integrated. If he killed them, more would come, and they might mess with the civilians. Better to take the safe route for once.

Milla: "Yeah, buddy. I heard what happened. You OK? Great, we've got a replacement for you."

Milla: "Get back to headquarters ASAP. You can rest when you're dead."

Milla: "P.S. Don't you dare die. D.S."

Luke: "Be right there."

The moment he sent that message, the Singers, now near what looked like a touristy restaurant, whipped around and pointed at him.

"What the hell?" Luke muttered as they charged him.
