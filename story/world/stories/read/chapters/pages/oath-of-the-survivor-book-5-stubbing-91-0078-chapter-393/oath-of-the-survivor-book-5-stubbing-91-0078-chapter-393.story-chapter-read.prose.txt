The cavern shook as the C Grades got serious, four auras clashing in the heart of the moon.  Dozens of whip-like tendrils turned into hundreds as DeRosa unleashed his full might, slowing their advance toward the heart of the chamber.

Despite his domain, Kyle found himself pushed back by the sheer volume and force of attacks.  Even with Genesis Engine active, he was just too slow to react to every strike.  His overlapping domains helped him avoid taking any major damage, but his advance was stopped in its tracks.  The same could not be said of his companions.

The very space around Skippy shimmered and warped as the skrell gleefully danced through the air toward the heart of the chamber.  His fiery aura only intensified as he continued to deflect blow after blow, dodging the attacks he couldn’t block by a hair’s breadth.

No, Kyle realized.  It wasn’t that Skippy was dodging, it was more accurate to say that the pinpoint attacks were missing the small target.  The subtle warping of space around Skippy shifted the trajectory of the whips just enough to make them miss, allowing him to keep his forward momentum.

Tillienne’s approach was much more direct.  The edge of her silver battleaxe and the tip of her lance radiated crimson power as the spectral wings appeared on her back.  With her shield raised she pressed forward, overcoming the attacks through sheer force.  The axe flashed out to cut any ensnaring tendrils, while her lance pierced through anything obstructing her path.  Kyle saw cracks open on the silver armor, only to watch as they mended moments later, the armor itself seeming almost alive to Kyle.

[DR. MAYHEW, YOU ARE FALLING BEHIND.]

“I noticed, C.H.A.D.D.,” Kyle growled, making a decision.

His left arm was drawn back into the World Tree Seed, replaced with Model: Cannon.  He aimed the weapon toward the cavern floor, then fired.  He allowed the recoil to knock him back, then withdrew a second Model: Cannon, firing toward the fungal flesh on the wall to his left, then pivoted again as he withdrew a third and fired toward the right.  A trio of explosions rocked the cavern, though to Kyle’s annoyance the final projectile was intercepted before it could reach its intended target.

At first, there was little other reaction.  And then the entire cavern began to tremble and shriek as a secondary set of explosions rang out.  A sickening, sweet scent began to waft through the air as the acidic payload dissolved the fungal constructs.

So far, he’d been thinking of this battle the wrong way.  They’d been treating it as though they had to fight through an army of constructs to get to DeRosa, when the reality was that every bit of fungal mass they destroyed directly harmed him.  They were performing an extermination as much as they were hunting a singular target.  All of the biomass within the cavern had been saved and stockpiled to fight them, forming the core of his power.

As Kyle discussed with Tillienne and Skippy, it was much more efficient to manipulate existing material than to grow something new.  He had the luxury of storing his creations within the World Tree Seed; and advantage he doubted DeRosa shared.  Victor’s pre-grown tools were all out in the open.  Vulnerable.

Kyle dropped toward the cavern floor where the smoke from the explosions was still obscuring him, flying low as he pushed outward with Devourer’s Domain and Wither.  Even damaged, the fungal material here was orders of magnitude more robust than the tendrils.  Mana rallied through it, connected as it was to the central pillar.  Kyle still found some purchase, leaving a trench of desiccated fungal flesh behind him as he approached the pillar from below.

Above him, DeRosa’s assault on Skippy and Tillienne intensified.  Beams of sickly violet light lanced toward them as the fungal whips were joined by piercing spears and massive hammers, all shaped to try and push them away.  “Try” being the operative word, as his companions’ advance kept a steady pace, slicing and piercing through the defenses.  They were nearly halfway across the chamber by now, and Victor finally changed tactics.

A warbling cry echoed across the walls of the cavern, followed by a green and violet haze.  Kyle knew what they were facing before C.H.A.D.D. could even flash a warning.  Toxic spores.

It was a strategy they’d prepared countermeasures for, as any encounter with awakened fungal beings tended to involve them.  Already, the flames around Skippy intensified, burning away the spores around him as Tillienne’s armor flowed into the shape of a mask around her face.  Vines rose from the C.H.A.D.D. pack, and Kyle felt a high-quality medical mask get secured around his own face, providing an extra layer of protection in case the spores could somehow harm his artificial organs.

You truly think your pitiful efforts will be enough to stop me?  Fools.   You should have left when you had the chance.

“Could you get more generic?” Skippy shouted back.  “You’re not only boring to fight, you’re even boring to listen to.  At least try to find something original.”

A pulse of mana moved through the chamber, and a moment later the spores all around Skippy exploded to life, deep purple mycelium erupting toward him with incredible force.  The space around the skrell warped as he used his movement skill, but soon the attacks began to activate all around both Skippy and Tillienne.  Tillienne was the first to get overwhelmed by the seemingly-endless tide of attacks, with Skippy getting clipped and bogged down a moment later.

Kyle swore as he changed course, moving toward his friends with Devourer’s Domain on full display.

Though the range was still severely hampered within DeRosa’s space, it was still an incredible counter to the madman’s new tactics.  The spores nearby were completely destroyed, while the constructs themselves were weakened by Devourer’s Domain and Wither before being blown apart by focused efforts with Aura Force.  With the other cavern walls damaged, DeRosa didn’t seem to be able to manipulate the existing biomass as easily, and his allies had done a number on the other, sturdier constructs DeRosa had been bringing to bear.

Soon, Kyle was at Tillienne’s side, destroying layers of the ensnaring fungus while she fought to free herself from inside.

“Thanks for the assist,” she grunted, swinging her axe to finally break free from the fungal cage.

Feeling a hint of instability in her aura, Kyle raised an eyebrow.  “How are you holding up?”

“Fine,” she said, rolling her shoulders.  “Damn mushrooms are mana-drainers.  Be careful of them.”

Kyle snapped his head toward the spiking power surrounding Skippy, who was barely visible within the warping space and heat mirage from his attacks.  “So that means,” he began.

“We need to up the tempo,” Tillienne finished.

A moment later, a blazing comet broke free from the constraints, the space between Skippy and the central pillar seeming to fold in on itself as a column of multicolored flames burned into it.  Kyle swore as he pushed himself faster, trying to catch up to the skrell.  Skippy bought them some precious time to catch up, but Kyle also recognized the beginning of his friend’s finishing assault.  If it wasn’t enough to put DeRosa down on its own, the task would fall to Kyle and Tillienne.

The key word being “if.”  The warping space surrounding the central pillar spread as Skippy continued his attack, with entire chunks of the cavern wall being twisted and crushed, crumbling to the cavern floor with resounding crashes.  Kyle had to deflect several of these falling pieces with Aura Force, making it abundantly clear that the cavern wouldn’t be able to last under the pressure of Skippy’s assault.  Huge swathes of fungal flesh had been burned, warped, or pulverized by the continuous combination, the central pillar being riddled with holes and still burning in some places.  When it went down, Kyle knew the cavern would come down with it.

Finally, Skippy’s assault came to its conclusion.  The small gray form still wreathed in fire appeared at the base of the pillar, and released a shockwave of power directly above him.  The upper part of the pillar was entirely sheared through by the attack, as a hole was opened in the cavern’s ceiling, revealing the sky far above.  An inhuman shriek shook the space as the pillar thrashed, violet light expanding out from it with terrible pressure.  An orb of the energy condensed around Skippy, then exploded, leaving the skrell singed but standing.

Kyle swore, pushing his speed even faster as he deflected another falling stone.  Almost there.  Skippy managed to sidestep another explosion, when a third caught him on his right leg.  Weakened as he was after his attacks, he simply wasn’t able to avoid the now chain of explosions DeRosa was focusing on him.

“Give me a platform!” Tillienne shouted, gathering power as she raised her lance and shield.

Kyle didn’t question her, layering planes of force behind her, angled toward DeRosa.  She stood for a moment longer, then exploded forward, the recoil shattering the makeshift launchpad as she rushed past him, coated in crimson energy.  She drilled straight into the central pillar, disappearing within it for a moment.  Then the entire thing was bisected, as a blade of crimson light ripped outward, revealing her now holding her battleaxe with her upper two arms.

The top half of the pillar fell to the ground, still wriggling, as the bottom half that was still connected to the cavern thrashed about wildly.  Already fungal tendrils were reaching up to connect to the fallen piece, but Kyle didn’t have time to watch it further as another explosion of purple energy rocked the cavern.  Skippy was barely standing, the aura around him flickering as he swayed.  Worse, Kyle could see creeping veins of darkness expanding across Skippy’s exposed skin where the explosions struck him, courtesy of the toxic environment.

Another blast of energy was compressing around Skippy, but Kyle was finally close enough.  Vines lashed out and pulled the skrell away as a wooden barrier appeared around them, protecting them from the explosion’s other effects.  The shrieks from the cavern only grew in fervor, but Kyle’s attention was wholly focused on his friend.

“Still so much room for improvement,” Skippy coughed, looking at his right hand.  It was badly burned, and even now remnant spatial distortions flickered around it.  “Space is a hard concept to control, you know.”

“I believe it,” Kyle said, already running Healing Impetus across Skippy’s body.  His goal was to get Skippy stabilized, then join the fight, but he frowned as the veins of darkness didn’t recede as the skrell’s other injuries began to mend.  Activating Identify, his breath caught.  The fungal spores had already started trying to take root in Skippy’s body, and were drawing the vital energy from Healing Impetus into themselves, fueling their growth.  Worse still, the tendrils were all creeping toward the skrell’s vital organs.

It didn’t look like this was a full-blown infestation; rather an intentional feature to mitigate the effects of healing.  In fact, traditional healing practices could likely result in more harm than good, particularly if the spores were deeper in the body.  Using Devourer’s Domain, Kyle focused his attention on his friend; carefully draining energy from the growing fungal roots, while trying to leave the rest of Skippy’s mana network unharmed.  It was slow, agonizing work.  The battle raging on outside didn’t help his focus, but Kyle couldn’t leave these injuries untreated.  Hang on just a bit longer, Tillienne, Kyle thought, slowly carving away more of the corruption.  Just hang on.
