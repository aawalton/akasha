Part of Ezra couldn’t help but evaluate the Duke's grip as he tried to understand the assignment he had just been given. Ezra’s hands had never been soft, but after over half a year of daily weapon practice, the skin on his palms had become rough and thick, the calluses forming tough barriers everywhere that held his spear, sword, or primarily axe, these days. The Duke’s hands, as he looked into the Adept's eyes and requested Ezra to be the Duke’s son’s bodyguard, were soft. The grip was firm and not particularly weak, but the noble who stood before him was a man who ruled not necessarily with strength of fist, but through politics. And possibly magic, considering the fact that he’d just cast a spell.

Only a few seconds passed, but Ezra could already see that the three people around him were, if not concerned, at least curious as to what his answer was going to be. Chloe had made no secret about what options were actually available to him, and Ezra gave as genuine a smile as he could as he nodded.

“Yes, I’m willing to serve as one of your son’s guards. That said, I’m not sure of what exactly that will require for me to do, as I’m sure I will remain a member of the potential Knight squad.”

“You will continue to train with your peers,” the Duke nodded, looking over his shoulder at Dominick. The old Knight stepped forward, his straight back and firm step seeming to be completely at odds with his wizened face and liver-spotted hands, but he nodded in agreement with the Duke.

“Your days will remain much the same as they have been,” Dominick explained as he stepped forward. “However, instead of spending much time patrolling, that time will be dedicated to one-on-one training with me three times a week and with Michel or another Pseudo-Knight of his choosing three times a week. The last day will be undergoing etiquette training.”

“That doesn’t really sound like my days remaining much the same,” Ezra narrowed his eyes. “It sounds like I’ll be away from the squad most of the day.”

“You won’t. And don’t interrupt me,” Dominick replied. Going off of the smile on the Duke’s face and the scowl on Dominick’s, the Knight wasn’t nearly so lenient as his master. “All told, patrols take about three hours in one day. Those are three hours that you would be separated from most of your squad anyways, and instead of wandering around the city, you’ll be undergoing additional training. The main difference will be the time after dinner, which will be when you eat in the garrison and then come through the same passageway that you did tonight to report here for your shift. Your evenings will be spent with your charge, ensuring that his own trainings go well and remains safe as he trains with the sword. Then, you will spend the evening in a room adjacent to his own to ensure that you can protect him in the event of any assassination attempt. In the morning, you will be relieved and will then return to the garrison for your breakfast. Do you have any questions?”

Ezra listened to the proposed schedule, and a question occurred to him.

“Does that mean I don’t have to participate in the daily conditioning? That happens before breakfast every day, after all.”

Behind him, Chloe laughed, and the way that Dominick’s eyes narrowed let Ezra know that he’d probably just asked a stupid question. The old man’s face twisted into what might have been called a smile by somebody more generous. Ezra thought of it as an evil grin.

“I will ensure that you do not miss out on becoming truly battle fit.”

Ezra didn’t have anything else to say to that, so he forced a smile onto his face and looked back at the Duke. The bearded man smiled and nodded at Ezra.

“From tomorrow onward, that will be your schedule. For today, go back and rest in the room that has been yours while you have been here. We will move most of your things over to a room in the manor, but the room will remain yours in the garrison. I’ll introduce you to Adonis tomorrow, once you’ve gotten a better introduction to what your responsibilities will be.”

“Yes, sir.”

As he said it, Ezra bowed his head and looked at Dominick. The Knight looked at Ezra and nodded once.

“Starting tomorrow, we’ll also be instructing you on all of the security protocols for things around here. Don’t come in thinking that you’re going to be in charge of anything. You’re capable, so you’ll be protecting somebody important, but you’re not gonna be making any commands anytime soon and don’t go pissing people off because you suddenly think you’re a big fish. Got it?”

“Yes, sir.”

At that, Dominick seemed satisfied with Ezra and nodded his dismissal. Despite the severity with which the old man carried himself and the obvious seriousness with which he took his charge to protect the Duke, there was something in Dominick’s body language that seemed to be almost playful as he gave his goodbye to Ezra. Despite his rising curiosity, Ezra simply followed Chloe’s lead as she bowed her head and led the way out of the audience chamber.

“We won’t be talking about this until we get to the tunnel,” Chloe said quietly, her voice barely above a whisper.

Ezra didn’t argue with that and simply did as he was told. With polite smiles and quiet steps, they walked through a path that Ezra figured would become familiar to him before too long. After walking through winding halls and unassuming doors, the two found their way back down to the security post there. The same guards as before waited for them.

This time, instead of walking right past the guards, Chloe turned to the one of the four that had a red right sleeve. He, meeting her gaze, narrowed his eyes and snapped a quick salute.

“Captain Chloe. What can I do for you?”

As he spoke, his fellow guards snapped to attention as well, no longer considering this to be a routine passage.

“This is Ezra Soldier,” she said without any needless fanfare. “Starting tomorrow, he will be passing through here twice a day, returning to the Garrison in the morning and coming back to the manor in the evenings. I’m sure you all will receive orders about this tomorrow, and if you don’t, consider my words to be entirely forgotten. But, as he will be here frequently starting tomorrow, I wanted to introduce you so that you'll know who he is when he comes back.”

“Yes, ma’am.” The leader of the squad nodded. Then, he looked at Ezra.

With nothing else to do, Ezra stepped forward and stretched out a hand.

“Like she said, Ezra,” he introduced himself.

“Elijah Tanner,” the leader of the squad replied. “I’ll probably be in charge here most nights, with Mariusz here in the morning. Even so, despite the fact that I know you, the first week or so is going to be slow with getting you through this choke point, especially in the evenings when you’re coming in.”

Ezra smiled as he nodded. “I’m not surprised to hear it. You’re doing your job, and I won’t get in the way of that.”

“If that’s the case, then welcome. Looking forward to seeing you.”

Elijah didn’t suddenly become a ray of sunshine, but he did loosen up ever so slightly as Ezra and Chloe made their way out of the checkpoint and into the long corridor. The heavy, reinforced door boomed closed behind them, and Ezra and Chloe walked a good twenty meters before the Pseudo-Knight turned and looked up at Ezra, a wide smile on her face.

“You didn’t expect that, did you?”

“Of course I didn’t expect that,” Ezra grunted, reaching out and pushing her shoulder. He could feel the moment she started to draw on her inner world, as she went from moving easily with his shove to becoming as steady as a wall and ended up just making him push himself off course. “Is this why you’ve had me training in etiquette? Did you plan for me to end up here?”

“More or less,” she nodded. “I wasn’t sure if it was going to be Adonis or Hera, but I was fairly certain that, if you remained as capable as you had been, you’d end up here before long.”

“I just…” Ezra trailed off, shaking his head. Then, he changed what he was going to say. “I know you probably couldn’t have told me all of this before now, but I wish you had. Why does Adonis need a new bodyguard? How old is he?”

“Easy answer is fourteen, just a few years younger than you. As for the reason for the new bodyguard, it’s largely because Adonis himself has insisted on it. Well,” Chloe amended, “Adonis and Dominick. It mostly came about because Adonis doesn’t trust Mikhail and neither does Dominick, while the Duke is much more patient with people who disagree with him.”

“What do you mean?” Ezra demanded, stopping where he stood to look at Chloe.

“What I mean is that Mikhail has made it no secret that he retains interests in the anti-Duke faction. He’s not entirely anti- the man himself, but he wants there to be a diversification of rule and for money to go a further way than it already does.” Chloe shrugged helplessly as she explained it. “The Duke doesn’t entirely disagree with that stance, not because he thinks that people should be able to buy power, but because he doesn’t think that aristocracy is the greatest way to rule a country. Because of that, we’ve ended up in a somewhat uncomfortable standstill, where the Duke somewhat agrees with the people who dislike him, so the people who support the Duke can’t fully hate or throw themselves against the people who stand against them, even as they continuously make jackasses of themselves.”

Ezra remained stopped in the middle of the corridor, and Chloe shrugged helplessly as he looked at her.

“Honestly, I wish I was kidding,” she said.

“Sounds like complicated political bullshit.”

“That’s because it is. And, though you seem not to understand, that’s going to be your life moving forward as well.”

Ezra went to squint at her, but as soon as she said it, he realized what she was saying. His eyes widened, and he didn’t try to hold back a groan.

“I’m always going to be with Adonis when there’s some sort of politics going on, aren’t I?”

“Yup.”

“And this is gonna be some sort of status thing? That being able to have a Pseudo-Knight as a guard was a demonstration of the Duke’s strength? And now that I’m only an Adept, everybody’s going to be pushing me to become a Pseudo-Knight as fast as possible?”

“Right again!”

Though Chloe’s words weren’t unkind, and her tone excited, Ezra was familiar with the way that her smile wasn’t reaching her eyes. She was having way too much fun with this, and he decidedly wasn’t.

“Is Dominick going to be a difficult trainer?” Ezra asked, already knowing the answer.

“He’s the strictest old man I’ve ever met. Probably the toughest out of any men I’ve ever even heard of,” Chloe said by way of answer. “He was always fair, but fair doesn’t mean kind. He never demanded anything impossible, though he certainly has high expectations. He’s a good teacher, and I’m proud to call him the man who helped me become a Pseudo-Knight.”

Ezra supposed he had to accept that, since, just like Chloe said, he agreed to do it. Now, he just had to live with the consequences of the choices that he had made.

As they walked down the corridor, Ezra had half a dozen questions he asked, before finally, he couldn’t help but laugh as a thought occurred to him.

“What?” Chloe demanded.

“I’m just thinking of how I wanted nothing more than to get away from being anything other than somebody training to be a Knight. Now, I got exactly that while also getting stuck with being a noble’s bodyguard. Got one thing I wanted and stuck with one thing I didn’t.”

“Life has a funny way of doing that,” Chloe shrugged. “Now, it’s time for you to tell all your friends about this change.”

In response to that, Ezra could only smile, looking forward to seeing Telemachus’s response to this new assignment.
