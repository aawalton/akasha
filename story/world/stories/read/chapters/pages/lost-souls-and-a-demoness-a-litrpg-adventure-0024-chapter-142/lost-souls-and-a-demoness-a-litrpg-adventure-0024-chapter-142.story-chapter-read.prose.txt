The second stage of the battle was, somehow, more frantic than the first. Seraphine's silver sword struck with explosive force, golden energy bursting from its length with every swing. Even Luis struggled to block the relentless attacks, staggering away from their power and speed. Jade didn't bother trying, instead focusing on conservative attacks and quick retreats. She redoubled her efforts on applying debuffs, flinging her Clinging Darkness spell and targeting vulnerable points every chance she had.

Unfortunately, the boss seemed to possess a newfound ability to cleanse afflictions. Bleeding wounds closed, the darkness over her eyes burned away, and her slow effects were removed with alarming swiftness. They still bought the team moments of reprieve, however, and Jade fought to offer them as many chances as she could.

If all Seraphine did was swing her sword, they would have seized a decisive victory despite the advantages of her extreme speed and strength. The angel, of course, did no such thing. Whether it was a property of the weapon or a power channeled through it Jade did not know, but each stroke of the blade left thin, golden threads in its wake. Seraphine could step through them with impunity but for the rest of them, the crackling energy stung and burned to the touch. She danced between Luis, Parker, and sometimes Urgraan and Jade, weaving a complex web of potent energy in her wake. Jade was nimble enough to fight around the impediment, but it was impossible to avoid entirely if she wanted to remain effective. Each time she did come in contact with the threads they seared her skin with piercing pain, exploiting her demonic vulnerability.

Her companions weren't as agile. Luis, Parker, and Urgraan quickly began racking up wounds, putting further strain on Naomi and Siora. Jade entered a state of absolute focus, darting in and out of the fight, helping maneuver the angel into rune traps, and then launching a coordinated strike when she became ensnared. The traps never held her for long, with Seraphine cleansing them as easily as she did Jade's other debuffs, but every hit that fell upon her before she freed herself was bolstered by Jade's Bondage Queen ability. Every instance of damage counted, and more and more wounds were marring the angel's pure visage. Slowly, they were wearing her down.

Jade landed another hit with Fang, striking a vulnerability and siphoning some much-needed life force back into her tired, battered body. There was only so much further they could push themselves, but she couldn't stop. Seraphine was moving slower as well, not retaliating as quickly to her attacks. The succubus nimbly evaded her next counter, flying up and over the web of burning light to escape. The angel didn't follow, already preoccupied with Urgraan, who was throwing rapid-fire punches into her armored back.

Hope began to rise in the back of Jade's mind. Naomi's mana reserves were running low, but she was keeping the rest of the group refreshed. They had the advantage. They were starting to slow down, exhaustion taking in, but Seraphine had it worse. Their ability to damage her far outpaced her ability to recover, and it was beginning to show. Naomi must have sensed it too, as she called for one of their more aggressive team moves. Jade ran into position and then stopped as a large crash sounded behind them.

On the other side of the large chamber, beyond the buzzing, sparking Core gem, a trio of armored Celestial Spirits finally burst through the stone barriers Aki had erected. In the brief instant Jade devoted to glancing towards the commotion she caught sight of a veritable crowd of monsters pressing against the double doors, forcing their way inside as the Eita and Aki slowed them down with a fighting retreat into the cathedral. It was only then that she realized the whining drone of Tara's pulsecaster had increased from a sporadic burst to a near continuous zap-zap-zap at the other end of the building. Her earpiece crackled with static, but nothing intelligible came through. The Core's energy was interfering with their radios. Jade turned her attention back to the battle, desperate to finish off the boss before they were overwhelmed.

Blinking forward, Jade abandoned the idea of defensive fighting for a more direct approach. She renewed her attack, daggers sliding off the angel's impossibly hard armor as she unleashed a barrage.

We're so close!

Parker, Luis, and Fenrin joined her, pouring a whirlwind of thrusts, slashes, and piercing arrows at their wounded foe. In her desperation, Jade forgot one of the basic tenets of combat. A lesson Serrethis had been quick to instill in her during their weeks of training. Calm, deliberate technique would always triumph against blind ferocity.

"When you allow emotion to dictate your tactics, you lay bare your intentions. An opponent that can anticipate your every move has already won."

Her mentor's words flashed through her mind as she continued to chain attack to attack, aura blades crackling to life as she tried to hammer her way through what remained of the angel's armor with sheer force. It was too late. Seraphine pivoted, allowing Jade's blades to hit her square on. They deflected harmlessly off an undamaged section of her armor and Jade, expecting more resistance, was caught flat-footed. A single kick sent her stumbling into Luis, interrupting his attack as the boss expertly parried Parker's follow-ups. One of Fenrin's electrified arrows found a gap in her armor, discharging its energy into her flesh, but it wasn't enough. Seraphine was faltering, her movements no longer graceful and fluid, but she was not so weak that a single lucky hit could bring her down.

Jade gasped as she and Luis fell through a pair of burning strings of light, falling to her hands and knees. Luis kept his feet, standing over her protectively as she quickly downed a potion and hauled herself upright. Dropping the empty vial, Jade retrieved her weapons and…

And they were out of time.

Despite their valiant efforts, Aki and Eita's running battle against the encroaching knights and spirits was only delaying the inevitable. The broad chamber was too large to hold back so many enemies, and they were already spilling into the room. A single glance at Tara's side of the cathedral was enough to confirm that they were moments away from a similar disaster occurring there as well. Jade's first instinct was to dive back into the battle, seizing victory from the jaws of defeat as she had time and time again. Ever since entering the Labyrinth her gambles kept paying off. If anything, this world rewarded taking such risks. But her experience in the last corespace stuck with her. How many more times could she roll the dice before they came up short? And, more importantly, who would pay the price for her recklessness?

Everyone was still fighting, desperate to finish the battle before they were overrun by the constantly spawning minions. They were far too powerful to take on with the boss and, as wounded as Seraphine looked, they had no way of knowing how much more punishment she could truly take. In the end, the choice was simple.

"Fall back!" Jade shouted over the din of battle. "Back to the portal!"

"What?" Luis glanced at her, surprised.

"We'll be overwhelmed if we stay!" She said, still projecting her voice so that the rest of the team could hear her. "It's over, we're getting out!"

Giving up went against Jade's nature, both her old one and her newer, demonic instincts. But it wasn't just her here. The group had decided that she would oversee the corespace expedition, and that made the responsibility of keeping her friends safe her own.

No one argued, and the group retreated towards Tara's side of the large room. Seraphine pursued, still fighting with her unnervingly blank, emotionless face. Luis and Jade stayed at the rear of the group, holding off the boss as best they could until Aki and Eita passed them at a run, flinging earth and air spells back at the monsters tailing them.

"Go!" Jade shouted, flinging herself into the air before the mob reached them. She engaged the eight-foot-tall angel from above, switching to her whip and lashing her from a distance. "I'll be right behind you!"

"But-"

"I'll be fine! Tara needs help!"

Luis cursed, then sprinted after the rest of the group, moving with the inhuman swiftness of one of his movement talents. If they couldn't clear the area in front of the cathedral's doors, none of them were getting out of here. That single moment of distraction almost cost Jade her life. Seraphine's blade, imbued with shining incandescent energy, flashed like a bolt of lightning. It hit Jade square in the side, imparting enough force to shatter her defensive barrier and sending her spinning out of control.

Jade barely recovered before falling into the crowd of armored minions stampeding across the cathedral, twisting in the air and kicking off a knight's head to launch herself back at the angel. She wove through a deadly forest of naked steel and lances of light, crashing into the boss before she could pursue her friends. Jade clung to the larger woman, eschewing the whip to rake demonic claws across her armor. Through more luck than intent the blow caught a rent in Seraphine's armor, slicing through a glowing vital point and eliciting a pained grunt and a spray of golden blood. Some of it spattered across Jade's face, where it seared her skin like hot oil. She ignored it, grappling with the boss, holding the two of them so close that the angel couldn't wield her sword effectively. The close quarters favored Jade, and she continued to claw at the woman, carried higher into the open air of the large chamber by their momentum.

"Graaah!"

It was the first time Seraphine had made such a noise. Throughout their fight, the angel had remained stoic and silent. Now, anger and frustration twisted her fair features, the angel's beauty marred by naked fury. A knee caught Jade in the stomach, blasting the air from her lungs and launching her another ten feet closer to the swiftly approaching ceiling. Pain throbbed in her chest, and Jade instinctively knew that only her enhanced fortitude had saved her from multiple broken ribs. As it was, she barely recovered in time to dodge Seraphine's next attack, flitting between two thick wooden rafters to evade a series of golden arcs that tracked her movements. Snaps and cracks rang out as the attacks slammed into the rafters, sending shards of flaming splinters spraying into her body.

The forced separation gave Jade a chance, however, and she seized it. Her friends had fought their way clear of the cathedral, and she could hear Luis calling for her to join them at the portal. Pouring on speed, Jade touched down on one of the rafters and sprinted along the narrow beam, dashing for the nearest wall. Towards the ceiling, the walls were lined with large, stained-glass windows. They were closed, but Jade didn't care. She'd smash straight through and fly to the portal to escape with the team. Forty feet. Thirty. Twen-

Seraphine appeared between Jade and the window in a cyclone of whirling embers, wings spread wide, feet firmly planted on the wooden beam. Jade jumped on reflex, leveraging her wings and enhanced strength to leap over the boss, arcing in a course that would send her straight through the window and out into the relative safety of the open sky.

She almost made it.

A vice-like grip closed around her ankle and suddenly, Jade's world spun. The angel smashed her back down onto the beam in front of her hard enough to crack it. Jade grunted, stars bursting in her vision even as she scrambled away, flipping backwards to land on her feet in a maneuver made possible by her high agility. She swayed slightly, vision swimming as Seraphine stalked closer, her sword raised high.

"No." She growled, piercing Jade with an icy glare. "You don't get to run."In stark contrast to her earlier tone, the angel's voice was so thick with pent up emotion that it bordered on unhinged. That sense only grew as she continued, stalking Jade as she backed away.

"This is all your fault. The endless battles, the constant bloodshed. My purpose has been twisted and tarnished by your kind, demon!"

"What are you talking about?" Jade demanded, trying to keep Seraphine talking as she hurriedly switched her whip for Phantom and Fang. "I didn't do anything to you! I'm not even a real demon!"

The once-beautiful angel was now a torn, bloody mess, golden ichor dripping from dozens of wounds. If she was weakening, though, she didn't show it in her face.

"I don't care." Seraphine said softly, as if the fire of her anger had been snuffed out like a candle. The boss's voice shook with pent-up emotion that Jade could sense was completely genuine. Caught off-guard, all she could do was listen, watching warily for a sudden attack. "I didn't ask for this. This isn't what I imagined, nor what I want. I… I was so sure. You're the first demon to come before me in over a millennium. I don't care where you came from. You didn't ask for this, but neither did I. And if I am to be denied the peace of rest, then I will at least take this small, meaningless vengeance."

There was no escape. Seraphine had backed Jade into the middle of the room. Far below, the corrupted Templars now clashed with Celestial Spirits, the floor around the Core a congested melee of flashing gold and black. The angel could match her speed, and turning to run would risk a quick, violent end.

That left only one option.

I hope the others made it out…

She spared a thought for the safety of her friends before zeroing in on the boss looming over her, matching her icy glare with one of fierce fire. The demon inside her, forever kept sealed away, stirred. Jade hesitated for only a moment. Then, reluctantly, she gave into it, bolstering her flagging reserves and fraying nerves with the strength of fury. It was a dangerous choice, but a cornered animal had no safe plays left to make.

"Fine." She growled, eyes flashing crimson. "We'll do it your way."
