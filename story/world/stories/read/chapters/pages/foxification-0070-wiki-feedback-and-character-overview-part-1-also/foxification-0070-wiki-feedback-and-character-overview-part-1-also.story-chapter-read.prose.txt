Kiomi

Kiomi is the main protagonist of Foxification. She was a young girl named Prim sacrificed in a blood magic ritual as an attempt to exert control over a Dungeon Core. Although rescued by Cilia's intervention, Prim, now Kiomi, was still bound to the Dungeon Core but instead became the Avatar of the Dungeon Core.

Read more: https://foxification.wiki/Kiomi

Cilia

Cilia is the Goddess of Foxes, Light, and Life, associated with the Foxkin race. She serves as the adoptive mother of the main character, Kiomi, and is one of the deuteragonists of the novel. Originally starting as nothing more than an abandoned fox kit, she was found, pampered back to health, and adopted by Zaleria.

Read more: https://foxification.wiki/Cilia

Myra

Myra is the first floor boss of Kiomi's dungeon. She is a former Foxkin who died in the War of the Great Forest, was stored in the Domain of Cilia, and reincarnated as an upgraded Flash Fox, but mostly retains her original form. Currently, she is rekindling her love for smithing and may or may not be hiding several orichalcum rods under her pillow so she can dream about them.

Read more: https://foxification.wiki/Myra

Marisa

Marisa is a former priestess of Cilia, the Light of Life. She saved Kiomi from being sacrificed and enslaved, but had to make her escape when the dungeon collapsed. Weeks later, she made her way back to the dungeon, accompanied by her childhood friend Alfred. Instead of ruins, she found the Dungeon of Foxes and a statue dedicated to her goddess. Her prayer was answered by Cilia, who chose her as her champion, her sole wish being that she would act as Kiomi's worldly mother when she could not.

Read more: https://foxification.wiki/Marisa

Aspen

Aspen Lowee is the leader of the paladins' order in Abervale. He is probably the strongest man in the town, and he is the son of Richard 'the Dragon'. He was, like his family for generations, dedicated to Veritas, the Goddess of Order, Oath, and Promises. Following the desecration of Cilia's statue and the ambush on his men, he swore a new oath to Cilia in chapter 58.

Read more: https://foxification.wiki/Aspen

Sarah

Sarah is one of the very few Ink mages in existence. Due to the influence of her magic, she lost her normal sight at 13 years old and gained the ability to store other types of mana inside her body. As an artist, Sarah has found a method to use the different colors of magic inside her body to vividly recreate scenes that only her eyes can see. Her artwork has the unique ability to cause the viewer to experience various emotions, ranging from anger to lust. After her death in the dungeon, Cilia gifted her the ability to see once again, returning one of her eyes to its original form while keeping her magic intact.

Read more: https://foxification.wiki/Sarah

Diona

Diona is the Goddess of Cats, Hunt, Individuality, and Exploration. She is also responsible for overseeing Adventurers and Dungeons, with her associated race being Catkin. Just like Cilia before her, Diona was once a pet of Zaleria, a mere stray cat that caught her attention and became a welcome outlet for her motherly love. Zaleria never managed to tame her adventurous spirit.

Read more: https://foxification.wiki/Diona
