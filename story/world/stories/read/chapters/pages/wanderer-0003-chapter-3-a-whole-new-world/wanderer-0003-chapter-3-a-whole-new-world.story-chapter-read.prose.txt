After my episode of hysterical laughter ends, there's an awkward silence as I realize Froðe is still waiting for my answer, that eyebrow rising higher the longer it extends.

"Have you forgotten?" the smirk turns into a grin. More laughter erupts around us.

My eyes narrow. Riiiight. My name. "My name is Beatrix."

"Ahh....Bea....trix," Froðe sounds my name out, like it's an odd taste in his mouth. "Interesting. Well, Bea..trix" his hand shoots out faster than I can block and grasps my chin strongly, but not enough to hurt. I catch a faint scent of thyme and mint in the air as he moves closer to me. "You are now mine. It will be in your best interest to cooperate and come easily without a fight, although I'm sure my men would enjoy it more if you didn't." His grin turns absolutely feral and my stomach drops. The men around us snicker and cheer in agreement as if to prove his point.

"Come on Froðe, you must share," a man with a partially shaved head says, his hair coming to braids in the back of his head. One of the ones who was leering at me earlier. "She is sweet on the eyes. And she speaks our language! She'll understand what I want to do to her," he adds sinisterly. Unable to look at the man directly with my chin in Froðe's hand, I glare at Froðe instead. His eyes glitter in even more amusement in response.

My gut tells me to try to fight and flee, but I try to force my heartbeat to slow so I can think clearly. This man appears to be their leader and is implying that if I cooperate, I'm less likely to end up in a worse situation. For now. I have no weapons, food or proper clothing for the elements. Even if I was able to fight my way out of this miraculously, I have no idea where I could go for help afterwards and would likely be wounded. Froðe seems like he wants to keep me alive for the time being, and I have some form of protection if I do what he says. Logically, it makes sense to cooperate, and these people might even feed me and provide water in order to keep me alive. When I can get my bearings and find the opportunity to do so, I will escape and figure out next steps.

Somehow, I've been given another chance at life. I'm not going to waste it.

"I won't fight," I say to Froðe.

"Hmmm. Good." He lets go of my chin and starts walking back to his mare. "Torsten, bind her."

"Damn, I really was a' hoping she'd fight. She look'd like she had so' in her!" Torsten dismounts, taking a loose bundle of rope from a pack on his horse and approaches me. "C'mon lass, give me yer hands."

I silently raise them together for him as he begins to knot the rope, which looks to be made from hemp, around them.

"Why is she dressed so strangely?" I hear another man ask.

"Maybe she's a selkie, and cannot get the clothing right" another offers.

"She has hair like yours Torsten, do you reckon she's from your lands?"

"Ach, I dinnae reckon so," Torsten says. "The lass speaks too diff'ren."

Hands bound, Torsten leads me to Froðe's mare. "Here's yer kærasta, Jarl," he says to Froðe jokingly.

Kærasta, (darling), my newfound language knowledge tells me, and Jarl, which is chief or leader, confirming my assessment of Froðe earlier.

Froðe smirks again. "Thank you, Torsten."

"Up you go, woman!" Torsten unceremoniously grabs my hips and starts hauling me up in front of Froðe. I frantically try to grasp the pommel with my bound hands and miss, about to fly over the other side of the horse. Thankfully, Froðe quickly grabs under my elbow to assist, keeping me upright with one arm easily and allowing me to swing one leg over in front of him. My pencil skirt rides up, revealing my upper thighs way more than I would like, but there's nothing I can do about it. As soon as I'm in the saddle, Froðe wraps a hand around my waist and pulls me into his hard, muscular body. Despite the awkwardness of the proximity to this strange man, he is warm, and the cold ache in my bones starts to ease a bit. It takes me everything not to scoot in closer to the warmth and I internally scold myself for it.

Instead, I ask, "Where are we going?" I need as much information as possible to figure out my next steps.

"We ride for Tír Tairngire." Froðe makes a clicking noise and nudges his mare forward with his heels. We begin moving toward the front of the group, Torsten not far behind. Torsten must be his right hand man, then. That's interesting, because he seems like an outsider with his accent.

"What's in Tír Tairngire?"

I feel Froðe lean his head down until his breath brushes my right ear. "Inquisitive thing, aren't you?" I try an fail to suppress a shudder at the intimate gesture, all at odds with the question and the warning in his voice. Moments pass and I start to think he won't reply when he finally says, "depending on what we find, perhaps my next settlement. The locals tell us it's a lively fishing town rich in trade, with a monastery filled with gold."

"And what happened to those locals?" I ask. If this is an expedition, I can only imagine what the raids have been like for the locals in this region based on what I know about viking history from some of the films I've seen.

Froðe chuckles darkly. "What do you think, fiery one?" Ok, so I'm not wrong.

"Stop calling me that," I say before I can stop myself. What is wrong with me?! I'm not usually this confrontational, and I need to not piss off the only person that is showing any interest in keeping me somewhat safe, despite whatever his real intentions are. Maybe death has made me bolder, somehow. Maybe this man brings it out of me. Or maybe I'm just losing my mind from shock. Probably the last one. Definitely the last one.

"Why not? Your hair is like fire," he says as he sweeps a hand through my copper curls, sending a tingling sensation through my scalp. "And...you seem to have some fire within as well."

I snort. If only he knew past me, I think to myself. I could have used some fire to deal with that horrible boss of mine. At least I will never have to see him again, nor hear any more of his disappointment about my reports. A thought suddenly strikes me. In fact, he's never going to get that finished report. This time, a feral grin appears on my own face. Maybe you'll just have to write it yourself now, Asshole.

Froðe speaks again, breaking me from my sinister thoughts. "One thing you should know fiery one, is that it's a good thing to be useful or valuable," he says as he returns his hand to my waist. "That is how you survive in this world."

This world. If I really have been reincarnated in a new life, perhaps this one is similar to that of the viking age of my own, but it also feels different. A parallel world, maybe? There's a strange energy in the air that I can't quite place. It ebbs and flows like water, shifting in the breeze and occasionally causing my skin to tingle. There are regions and towns I have never heard of, though this place feels similar to Scotland. And then there were the strange light orbs I saw earlier when I arrived here. What were those?

I remember the pain I felt right before being able to speak and understand Froðe and his clan. It must be part of the reincarnation somehow. My Isekai power? In order for me to start a new life, in a new world, I need to be able to speak at least one of the languages here so I can communicate with people and have some chance of survival in this next life. Thank GOD I was reincarnated with clothes on. It could have been worse. Although, it would have been nice to start with clothes and gear appropriate for the climate and region. Oh well, beggars can't be choosers.

We continue riding for a few more minutes before Froðe says, "So tell me, how did you come to be in Lothlend?"

Uh oh. Uhhh....

"I can tell you are not from here, based on your appearance."

I hesitate, not knowing how to answer. Fuck! Come up with a story.

"Did you run away from home?" He guesses.

Thinking quickly, I force my body to react and go rigid, like he guessed the mark. Honestly in a way, it's not really a lie. Except I died.....I think.

But Froðe does not press further. "Well fiery one, I will find out sooner or later."

"She loo's wealthy," Torsten offers. I didn't realize he started riding astride us to speak to Froðe more easily. "Maybe some'one is lookin fer her. She'd fetch a good ransom."

"I'll decide after we reach Tír Tairngire. Until then, make sure that none of the men touch her."

"As yeh command, Jarl," Torsten says and lets his horse fall back once again. Well, safe at least until Tír Tairngire.

We continue riding for several hours, through endless grassy highlands with the occasional copse of trees, until we stop once at a bubbling creek to water the horses. The creek cuts through a copse of silver birch trees, with scattered shrubs dotted with plump yellow berries that look a lot like raspberries.

Froðe dismounts behind me, then surprisingly, turns to me and lifts me off his mare effortlessly instead of letting me struggle off myself. He does however take his sweet, sweet time lowering me, brushing me against his chiseled body and maintaining eye contact. You'd think it would be super awkward, but those eyes continue to draw me in like some mysterious power of his and goosebumps pebble my skin. Traitorous body. Years later, my feet finally touch ground.

"Stay close fiery one. If you try to run, you will not get far before I find you, and you will not be happy with what happens when I do." His hands still grip my waist from lowering me, and the menace in his eyes is enough warning for me, even though his voice is calm and almost casual. This man is terrifying. I simply nod, then he lets go and walks over to Torsten who approaches on foot, already dismounted from his horse.

"Based on what the villager told us, Tír Tairngire is a few more hours ride from here," Torsten says as they meet. He takes a swig from a water skin. I eye it, wondering the last time I ate or drank something. In another lifetime, I realize. Froðe notices me eyeing the skin, then reaches to his hip where one is strapped. He unhooks it and throws it to me, which I actually manage to catch somehow with my bound hands. Unstopping the cork proves more difficult, but I eventually get it open and drink. Water has never tasted so good in my life. Lives. Huh, that's going to take a while to get used to saying.

Suddenly, a screech pierces the air and a massive black bird swoops in towards Froðe. I duck on instinct and nearly drop the water skin as it barely misses my head, but Froðe recognizes the bird and extends his arm. As I get a closer look, I recognize it as a Raven. "Huginn," Froðe says and strokes the bird with a a surprising gentleness I didn't think he was capable of. The bird makes a series of small croaks and nibbles his hand affectionately.

Froðe looks at the raven quietly for a moment, and that strange energy I felt earlier crackles, what is that? Then he turns to Torsten. "Huginn tells me the raid in Torlundy was a success. We acquired gold from the monastery and new thralls from the village. The rest of our party should join us in Tír Tairngire in a day's time."

Did he just...communicate with the bird telepathically?

"And the Seeress? Ha' she made an'y more prophetic claims?" Torsten asks.

Froðe looks over at me, then lifts his arm while Huginn takes flight into the sky. "When Skuld reaches Tír Tairngire, she will meet the woman."
