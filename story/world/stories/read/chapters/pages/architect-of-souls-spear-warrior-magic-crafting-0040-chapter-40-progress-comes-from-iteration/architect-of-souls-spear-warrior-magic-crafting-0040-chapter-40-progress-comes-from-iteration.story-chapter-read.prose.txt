Back in their room, Ferris and Jade laid down to cultivate. Ferris was getting more used to meditating several times a day, the practice from entering and exiting for each spirit gem paying off. It still took at least fifteen minutes, but that was much faster than it had been.

Ferris was excited to make a new, hopefully better crossbow. Unfortunately, he needed to gather qi before he could begin. He quickly made a new lightstone and got to work.

The Air Passage was still suffering from theft by the cloaked demons, giving Ferris only about half of what it could. They encountered two of the wretched thieves, but the things ran away each time.

In the slimespace, about one-sixth of the clams had produced a new qi chunk. He looked forward to the next day, hoping to get all of them enough food to make qi. Mother Lily had been feeding Jade at least triple portions for each meal, which implied Jade would need to eat enough for eighteen people to fill all the clams. Ferris had ordered much more than that from the second branch’s food provisionary, but he didn’t know how efficient the animal feed would be.

The process of fishing up the qi chunks went smoothly. Ferris had to make one new chain, but overall gained four times the cost of qi to create it.

After dumping the last of the qi chunks into his pool, Ferris started to make his new crossbow. The overall design was the same as before, as he didn’t understand everything referenced in the second blueprint of the library’s jade slip. Still, he wanted to improve what he could with demon components and intent forming.

The stock was once again the first component he built, as it took the most qi and had to interconnect with the most other parts. The simple qi construct had worked for the first version, but it had been too fragile. It had crumpled a bit when Ferris needed to smash a demon with it, so Ferris wanted to make it as hard as possible with demon parts.

For hard demon material, Ferris had chains and bones. The chains were metal—and thus harder—but he wasn’t sure if the nature of the chain would work against him. Could he focus on the metal aspect and ignore the flexible chain part? Could his ‘intent’ change which properties of the base materials mattered?

In the end, Ferris decided to risk it and filled the cooking pot half full of qi and half full of chains. He would need to hunt more chain demons soon to replenish. Jade would be happy.

After the mixture was cooked and cooled, Ferris placed a hand inside and concentrated. Beyond the form and function, Ferris attempted to remember how the weapon felt in his arms. What it was like to carry it around, reload under pressure, and then turn to pull off a perfect shot. Ferris had nowhere close to the years of practice and exposure like he did with the spear, but the experience he did have was in actual battle.

Pulling the new crossbow stock out of the liquid, it had the same dark silver color as the demon’s chains. It looked much sturdier than his first one, but compared to the bone spear, it felt… lacking. Making the bone spear yesterday had felt like the culmination of years of effort, while this crossbow stock just didn’t have the same spark.

A bit disappointed, Ferris put it aside and started on the next part. For the flexible limb, he wanted to try a bit of an experiment. So far, he’d focused on physical properties of his materials.

But he wondered, could a monster’s fighting style also be used to change the qi forming? The frog demons used their back legs to leap into powerful charge attacks. Could their corpses be used to make a bow limb that sprung with more force?

An idea like this would be silly in the real world, but the demons were mental constructs. If intent could affect the outcome, why not this?

He had two of the blue frog demon corpses. They were much smaller than the chain demons, but he figured he could get enough for two bow limbs from each corpse. He tried to use more of the frog’s muscles, particularly the back legs, but it was difficult to separate the pieces with just his axe. And unpleasant. And gruesome.

Happy to be finished, Ferris couldn’t wait to have something like Acid Dismantle to make this easier. As he placed his hand in the cooking pot after it had mixed and cooled, Ferris pictured his fights against the frog demons. The way they waited, coiled back slightly, ready for the slightest opening. Then, with an explosion of force, they would leap forward. He then connected that idea to pulling the crossbow taunt, aiming, and finally releasing a bolt forward.

The result was a solid success. Ferris had tempered his expectations compared to the bone spear yesterday, as nothing was as amazing by comparison, but he was happy with the bow limb. The color was a similar blue to the frog demons. Flexing the limb, it felt stronger than the first version and sprung back to its original shape with a satisfying spring. It would likely require a stronger draw, but that would help propel the bolts. Hopefully, it wouldn’t crack as easily as the last version, as well.

The new bow limb’s material was an improvement, but the shape didn’t fit at all into the crossbow’s stock. Thankfully, Ferris had enough material to keep trying, and the next one slid in and fit. It hurt to waste qi on the failed limb. He really wanted to learn how to create properly sized qi constructs, but didn’t have any ideas other than practice.

The trigger was the most complicated part, as it had to both fit on the stock and slide up through a hole to release the string when compressed. Ferris used demon corpses to make it stronger, but couldn’t think of anything else he could improve. At least this trigger only took two tries instead of four like before, showing that Ferris was getting better at forming them.

For the string, Ferris tried the same technique as he had with the bow limb, using parts of a blue frog demon’s corpse. The results were disappointing. He wasn’t sure if the concept didn’t work, if he used the wrong parts of the corpse, or something else. The string wasn’t usable at all, and he decided to make one made with just plain qi.

After making eight new bolts, mixing in the bones of the chain demon, Ferris once again lamented an almost empty Qi Pool. It was a bit more than the bare handful he had before, but not by much. He stopped making bolts at eight. That will have to be enough, he thought, because I can’t afford any more.

Worse, he would have no qi to water either the Core Tree or the two abilities tonight. It was regrettable, but clearing out the chain demons’ fort took priority.

Practicing with the new crossbow, Ferris immediately felt a difference compared to his first. The other one had felt almost like a toy he had to be careful not to break. If he’d pulled the string back too hard or too fast, the bow limp would have folded in on itself. Put too much pressure on the stock, and it would’ve snapped in half. Comparatively, the new crossbow felt solid.

He had to adjust his aim, as the bolts flew out with more power. Overall, there was less spread from shot to shot, and Ferris’ accuracy quickly improved.

Ferris and Jade set out for the chain demons’ fort, this time with a vengeance. They soon ran into a group of three chain demons, but they were simple to defeat with the new crossbow. Ferris ran out of bolts before killing the last one, but was able to finish it off with the bone spear.

After the fight, Jade was sitting in front of the corpses. Ferris had given the slime the go-ahead to consume them after he had retrieved his bolts, but instead Jade just swirled in confusion.

“Ferris, I need help!” Jade called him over.

Ferris looked up, confused. Jade had never hesitated to eat before.

“Yes, Jade?” He asked a bit tentatively.

“I know I need to… practice something, but can’t remember what,” Jade said plaintively, rocking back and forth.

It’s the game I made up yesterday, he thought, hoping to get Adaptability to provide Acid Dismantle. Jade had been fired up to keep playing it, but Ferris had decided to go to bed. Now, the slime had forgotten everything, except that Ferris told them they needed to practice.

“Yes,” Ferris said with a sad smile, sat down, and pulled Jade into his lap, “I can remember for you.”

Ferris explained the game again, and Jade struggled just like the night before—three out of ten—even with three corpses to try on. Ferris silently watched the slime, who was now happy as can be, eat through the demon corpses. He resolved once more to do whatever he could to solve Jade’s memory problems.

Afterwards, Ferris had Jade eat the small amount of leftover chains. He would have loved to keep them, but they would only slow him down as they moved closer to the fort.

Two more demon groups appeared along the way, but neither proved a challenge. Arriving within sight of the fort, Ferris looked to see if anything had changed.

More groups of demons roamed around the hill the chain fort rested on, but everything appeared the same from the outside. Ferris and Jade started the slow process of isolating and culling the demons. They had the advantage against any one group, but the demons would charge into any fight they could see or hear.

With the new crossbow, Ferris slowly improved his accuracy enough to kill three of the chain demons with his eight bolts. Jade also got a lot of practice attempting to eat the demon corpses while leaving the tightly wrapped chains behind, eventually earning the coveted ‘four out of ten.’ Jade did a little jiggle after hearing it, rippling in joy.

There was a tricky fight with three of the blue frog demons, but the bone spear showed its superiority. Before, Ferris could only properly fight one of the frog demons at a time. Now, he was able to keep two at bay while Jade consumed a stunned one, the bone spear gracefully moving perfectly to deflect their coordinated leaping charges.

Once Jade had finished one off, Ferris was able to knock another into the slime. Now, both in one-on-one fights, Ferris and Jade were able to quickly win.

After the fight, Ferris considered his difficulty with the blue frog demons. The bone spear had made Ferris stronger overall, allowing Ferris and Jade to confidently fight three at a time… but it wasn’t the tactical advantage the crossbow gave against the chain demons.

Ferris would likely see many more frog demons in the future, and they would only get stronger. He liked the idea of being adaptable, taking advantage of an enemy’s weaknesses. What would best counter the frog’s leap attack?

Most of the frog’s danger came from their ability to quickly get past his spear, slamming into him with the horns on their head. Their claws were much less dangerous. If he could stop their change without getting skewered, they would be easier to deal with. Ferris remembered the technical manual on crafting spiritual armor constructs at the library. Some protection would likely stop their claws entirely, allowing him to grapple and take away the advantage of the charge. That wouldn’t work against a group, though…

Looking at Jade, Ferris considered the slime’s chance against a blue demon frog. In the desperate fight against four of them, Jade had been able to injure one of the frogs. If the demon had been able to charge the slime, it could easily knock out the core. Since the demon didn’t get a leaping start, Jade was able to injure it. The frog had escaped, but had been hurt enough that it was out of the fight.

If Jade used Assimilate Object on the chain cage, it would be a different story. The slime could likely catch the frog’s charge, stopping them cold by putting the chain’s length in front of the slime’s core. There was no guarantee Assimilate Object could hold the chain strong enough to stop the leaping frog’s momentum, but if it could… Jade could easily defeat a dozen of the frog demons. Neither the claws nor leap would pose any threat; it would just be a matter of time before Jade ate them all.

Ferris smiled, remembering Brix holding Jade while Cyrus tossed food at her. Why not just hold the slime up and let Jade catch demon frogs? Then Ferris stopped smiling… that could actually work?

Jade finished disposing of the corpses, and they moved on to the next group. Soon, they had cleared out all of the demons out in the open.

All that was left was to enter the fort.
