Alex, with the others…

We couldn’t stay here. Even now, the massive dog circled in the darkness, sniffing in this direction. While I might be able to take it, there really wasn’t a point. I wasn’t here to take out puppies. I was here to rescue my dad, and destroy the Forgers once and for all.

Plus, the pupper only wanted to play. He didn’t realize that his play was dangerous to ones smaller than him. Now wasn’t the time to teach him what he needed to know. Nessetra might be able to help him, though. Once I got back to Lakeside, I’d speak with her on his behalf.

The scraping of the chain dragging across the ground hummed in the background of my mind. Here it was easy to listen for. It kicked up even more dust into the dark air as it slowly cut a massive furrow in the dirt.

Havi wouldn’t meet my eyes, while Manee looked like a cat that had gotten the cream. She practically bounced on her toes. Sile just gave me a sharp nod when I handed Racket off to him.

Racket hadn’t liked flying through the air suddenly. But at least he hadn’t tried to hit me or anything. Just whimpered. It was such a stark juxtaposition to the guy who had survived in the downed drop ship with Valeria.

He couldn’t keep going forward. I hoped it was the pupper that broke his mind, not my armored form.

Whispers came from that direction, as Valeria tried with Sile to calm him down.

Before we did anything, I needed an answer from my Dad and Hawk.

“Back to the others, or are you going up?” I asked. I wouldn’t make the decision for them. But, honestly, I couldn’t escort them all the way back. It’d take too long. Instead, I’d set them on the path to the Shanty Town, and they could stay there until we dealt with the larger Forger problem.

“We will follow your lead,” said Havi, giving a quick look to Manee. She nodded.

I felt the ding as they were added to my allies list. Just great.

Sile led Valeria and Racket to us. “He cannot go on.”

I nodded, thinking about the best way to get him over to the path.

“I’ll go with him,” said Valeria. She shook her head lightly, then she gave me a sharp nod and hugged her weapon to her chest. “This isn’t a battle for me. Not yet, anyway.”

“I can get you guys on a safe path. It will involve some flying,” I added. I gave my Dad a glance, as he turned to Hawk. “Dad, I’m not saying you aren’t strong enough, but you can’t face a Rank 3 creature right now. Same goes for you, Hawk.”

“Do we have to fly?” Hawk finally asked.

My dad broke a smile.

“Where are you taking them?” asked Sile.

“There’s a small safe haven not far from here. I’m gonna set them on the path leading to it. They should be fine there.”

It took forever to fly each of them to the path. Poor Racket said nothing, and closed his eyes the entire time. Valeria tried to look at everything but me. Hawk just kept watch after giving me an awkward hug right before I left.

Then it was Dad’s turn. “I’m staying,” he said.

“Excuse me?” I asked, getting ready to leap into the air again.

“Hawk wouldn’t go if he thought I was staying. I didn’t want him to stay just because I did, but I want to see this through.” He stared at me, and I got nothing from his face.

Havi and Manee glanced at one another. Sile stepped closer to him with a frown. “My friend, we underestimated this world. That's on us. Please don’t give your life for our bad choices.”

My father opened his mouth, but Sile suddenly blew something in his face.

I moved before I could think about it, and caught him.

“I’m sorry, Alex, but we couldn’t keep them safe,” he said, tucking a small pouch away inside his inventory. He wouldn’t meet my eyes.

“You should have waited for me. I would have come.” I let out a sigh. “But they all lived.”

Within seconds, I’d leaped into the air and flown back yet again to the trail that led to the Shanty Town.

Hawk frowned as I landed, catching sight of my dad, out cold. “He was gonna stay, wasn’t he?”

“Yep. Sile took care of it.” I set him down on the ground as carefully as I could. For once, he looked peaceful. “I don’t expect him to be out long.”

“Happy hunting,” he said, moving closer to my father. “I’ll get them back safe.” Valeria and Racket watched me from a distance, but they kept careful watch on the area around them, as well.

“Stay safe,” I said, then off into the air I went. The others had already started toward the chain. Not the platform at the Forgers camp, but around it. The goal was to meet the chain before it met the platform.

The elevator-like object moved up and down the chain constantly, and would touch near the bottom once more before it reached the platform.

Our goal was to get on at that point, and ride it up.

It should be unexpected.

The platform in the camp appeared to make it easier to get on, but the chain did move rather slowly across the landscape.

The cool air rushed over me, and in the far distance a flash of green pulled my attention. The second of green light revealed the dog had fled the plains, and half the rolling hills in the distance were gone. Massive trees stretching farther up then I thought possible had taken their place.

I shivered and flew faster, following the dots on my map.

The Forgers’ camp glowed from the campfire, and several black dots appeared on my map. I resisted chasing them down right now, even though I wanted to figure out what the Wisps, and a Whisper, did. Now wasn’t the time.

Havi, Manee, and Sile waited for me. Not to mention the others, who were all stuck in various ways waiting for us to finish our business.

Three black dots hiked into the plains, very clearly headed in the direction the others had taken.

This would be fun.

I corrected my flight, aiming for the last black dot in the cluster. My spear appeared in my hands as I buzzed closer to the ground. They caught sight of Havi and Manee before I caught up with them, and took off chasing the three Azurafolk.

Too bad they didn’t look up.

Well, too bad for them, at least, I thought.

[An OFFENDER has been detected in your area, with an active bounty. You have been given a temporary boost to stats and abilities.]

[Rinden, Slave Hunter, Level 240, Predator, Unknown, OFFENDER.]

[Ranrc, Sleeper, Level 225, Prey, Unknown, OFFENDER.]

[Nacer, Momento, Level 235, Prey, Unknown, OFFENDER.]

Havi and Manee turned to face them just as my spear cut through the back of Momento. Everything shuddered for a second, like time tried to stop, but then the skill failed as I jerked his body up on my spear. Then there was a crunch as his core broke that made my stomach growl. I tossed the carcass to the ground.

Sile took that moment to attack Ranrc, while Rinden shot off closer to Havi. Runes glowed under his feet, freezing him in place, almost causing him to stumble.

I darted in that direction but Havi and Manee tag-teamed him. I pivoted back to Ranrc, but Sile had already taken him down.

Rolling my eyes, I landed and headed to the carcass of Nacer, and searched for the core. It had broken in half, but I still ate it. Apples this time.

[You have gained experience from combat.]

[You have collected a bounty: Gained a Wisp.]

[You have collected a bounty: Gained a Wisp.]

[You have collected a bounty: Gained a Wisp.]

[You have devoured a level 235 Nacer and gained knowledge about time.]

For once, nothing came to me from the knowledge gained. No images. No memories. Nothing. It made me pause before shaking my head. The notifications about getting bounties for all three of them, even though I only took down one, made me smile.

[Do you want to create a Whisper?]

The notification popped up slower than the others, and didn’t have a sound attached to it.

Of course I wanted to create a whisper, even though I didn’t know what it would do.

A small, pitch-black orb appeared in my inventory, but that was it. My stomach growled as soon as it appeared in my hand. I tried to stop myself, but I crunched down on it before I could help myself.

As soon as it broke apart in my mind, voices echoed all around me. Then they solidified into one person. Grey space took over everything else.

“To see, or to cut?” The question rang out in my mind, and the answer was taken from me before I could voice it.

My eyes burned for a moment, making tears run down my face.

“Walk your path.” Then, the voice was gone.

“Do you need to eat more?” asked Sile in a soft voice. Then he jerked back. “What just…” Instead of finishing, he shook his head.

“I’m okay, I think…” I replied, while my mind raced. I let the tears dry, unsure of what had just happened. The system didn’t give me any answers, either.

I felt fine, actually pretty good, all things considered. “Let’s just keep moving.”

We left the remains behind as we took off on foot, heading to the chain. The elevator lowered itself down the chain. The huge distance made it seem to move slowly when it was far up, but now that it was closer to the ground I could see it was moving very quickly.

We all picked up speed, racing toward where it would stop near the ground. None of us wanted to miss it.

Nothing else followed us across the plains, though the dog did head in the direction of the camp.

Havi held up a hand and stopped, near a massive trench dug in the ground. The chain moved along the path, heading in our direction. “We need to jump onto the platform as it approaches. Alex can help any who fall.”

I nodded, not having a problem being the back up plan.

Then a howl broke out behind us.

The dog had found the remains of our fight.

That didn’t take long.

I triggered second sight to check on the chains, and every link glowed with power. Whoever created it had used more mana then I thought possible. All four chains glowed like that, coming down from the floating mountain.

How was that even possible?

“Ready…” whispered Havi, and we all heard it even over the noise coming from the metal grinding in the channel in the dirt. Manee lowered herself in a crouch, along with Havi. Both put away their weapons.

Sile hung back, along with me.

The platform didn’t glow with the same energy. It didn’t operate with the same sort of vibe. I doubted it was created by the being who had built the chains.

Gears ran along the links, using them to move up and down, while other cables hanging from above kept it level. Panels stuck into the wooden platform created low walls around the square, but that was all.

Manee leaped before it had even reached the bottom, and she landed gracefully on the platform, like a dancer. Havi followed, though his feet made more noise.

Sile jumped, but I reached out and yanked him back down as something passed by overhead.

Four large wings and a massive mouth, about the size of a beach ball, were my first impression of the thing. It cut through the space Sile would have been in.

He stumbled, then nodded his head.

“Go, I got this,” I said, leaping up. As soon as my wings appeared, the thing dove for me.

Sile made it to the platform, and away they went as I darted through the air.

Several of the creatures followed the chain, but they blended in with the darkness. They seemed to use the light to attract prey. Well, it had worked, but no matter what they thought, I wasn’t prey.

All of them turned in my direction.

[Flock of Wings & Teeth, Pack Hunting, Unknown, Unknown, Unknown.]

Oh, fuck.
