A tiny mirror fired a burning beam at the shadowy lich, which hit his torso and seemed to energetically explode upon contact, ripping away a sizable amount of the shadow armor in a blink. The second laser fired as the lich tried to flinch away from the shot this time, and it hit his free arm, detonating again and making the arm nearly visible beneath the shadow as he was partially transforming back yet again.

As a secondary thing, Samantha moved closer to Azure and Gabriel. Not normally something she would do, but the circumstances begged it at the moment. She also moved her wire mass near her, still inclined to use it as a last-ditch shield.

All the while, Azure had been conjuring her own array, and her version was more potent and efficient. The conjured daggers that appeared were not her typical darker element, but glowed faintly white and ghostly. One flashed across the distance and hit the lich square, detonating and ripping away the shadow stuff supremely, causing it to dissipate and reveal the deteriorated undead substance beneath.

Her second shot was smashed out of the air by the swipe of the staff, detonating and not doing much despite damaging AoE.

The third shot was not avoided, but it once again hit the off-arm, detonating and completely exposing it as the shadow-stuff was stripped away.

The Samanthian corner wasn't done yet, though. Samantha followed up her mirror beams with her extra hasted action, casting her hand out and dropping another [Spellfyr: Unleash]. If she could spam it, she would, but it had a 5-second cooldown.

The blast enveloped the lich despite its efforts to skirt away. From the neck down, annihilating energy swallowed him. Samantha held hope to see nothing when the bar’s white energetic consumption cleared. She attempted to stop the length of the effect enough only to make an angled pit in the floor, as anything further was irrelevant and might give access to another chamber — or who knew what else.

When the flash ended, the lich was still there, screaming, bubbling, and smoking as he’d been scoured tremendously by the blast. In addition, his off-arm had been completely incinerated, probably due to being more exposed than the rest of him.

Finally! True progress.

And then a Contingency triggered.

Nooooo!

It was one effect, and had to be his last. A simple floating shield appeared in front of him, large and angular and obviously a magical construct.

At least his wounds had not been repaired. He was undead, so despite the grievous damage dealt to him, he was not impaired, and his shrill scream was one of offense and rage rather than pain.

Naturally, the staff restored the shadow over him again, hiding his form behind the armor, or transmuting him back… she honestly wasn’t entirely sure, despite that damaging it revealed the lich underneath. Regardless, he would have to be battered through again. He had a shield to get past, now, but he was also one-armed.

The lich didn’t trip into the angled hole behind him this time, wisely darting away from it. He lifted his staff once more, and the pages fluttered. One page, this time, but the pattern continued: it was still going deeper, with only a few pages left. Whatever that meant.

A different effect occurred. Basketball-sized black spheres, oily-looking and liquid-like, appeared throughout the room except near the lich. This included near Samantha and company-

“Fear not, for I shall protect thee!” came the corny line of Gabriel, as she leapt dramatically on the wings of an angel and landed between Samantha and Azure, thrusting up her huge metal shield. Pulsing energy flared from the shield, stretching seven meters in every direction as she made use of Absolute Defense, which would protect allies within the field completely from physical harm for ten seconds. It wasn’t like a sanctuary, either — they could still attack. Other than Gabriel, who needed to essentially emit the field the whole time. But if anyone left the field, that person lost the protection.

Temporary impunity, in other words. Ten seconds. Hopefully, more than enough.

The orbs in their vicinity exploded, slinging black sludge that splashed all over them. Samantha kinda twitched from that, but the sludge couldn’t touch them. It sloughed off as if they all wore slippery forcefields immediately past their flesh and clothing. When the sludge touched the floor in great clumps, it began laboriously eating through the stone and creating foul-smelling vapor and likely harmful fumes.

How many damn times do I have to deal with black sludge?!

The sludge spheres immediately on the edge of their ‘territory’ came zipping toward them, too, in what she guessed was from motion detection triggers. They exploded once near, and made more of a sludgy, steaming mess everywhere. Meanwhile, the spheres further away in the chamber stayed there. Many dozens of them.

Hmm. A potential problem for mobility.

The two giant undead got sprayed with the sludge. It stuck and began destroying them… slowly. They were probably resistant. Meanwhile, they’d be slinging it everywhere as they attacked.

Deathsting cleverly lured the already diced-up, sludge-covered ape-giant into the field, letting it get a strike in that did nothing and was utterly arrested. Deathsting took advantage of the glaring opening a combatant got when they soaked a hit without any defense committed, and dealt a vicious sequential counterattack, ripping his enemy’s face off, goring its neck, and severing its leg at the knee, dropping it down into even more sludge.

The other giant, intent on Gabriel, lunged and tried to stab her in the back with his poleaxe. The lizard undead seemed quite confused and perturbed when the point of his weapon broke off and went spinning into the floor, and he visibly vibrated from the force of the reverberation sent back down the shaft.

Azure said in the team gestalt, “I can help with the spheres if we want to trigger them.” She casually flicked a hand, and out of a dimensional pocket, small mirrors spawned in a huge mass. A few began moving, controlled by Azure in the back of her mind as she focused on her ensuing offense.

Samantha was grateful. With her own prodigious multitasking, she easily took dozens of them at once and launched them around the room, expending very little focus to do so. She somewhat regretted she hadn’t first set up her [Conjure] effects when spawning in the room, as she could easily manipulate things in a large radius when [Conjure: Independence] was already on tap. But it needed an initial action to get going for any hardcore effectiveness, which she’d been hard-pressed to produce. Perhaps she shouldn’t have initially shot those lasers at the zombie?

In any case, [Conjure] was very versatile defensively and could surprise on offense, but it wasn’t in the same league of potency as her more specialized offense, and she had needed to be the offense.

The mirrors thankfully triggered the explosions as they passed close to spheres, detonating more of them, and hopefully giving them decent space. On a whim, Samantha also tried to lure one toward the lich, but it didn’t play ball — it did move closer, but once within a certain short radius of the lich, it gently slid to a stop and did nothing.

Damn.

Samantha and Azure endeavored to repeat their previous round because, despite the new defensive addition of the shield, their array-effects were stronger in ensuing rounds. Samantha could fire three beams for an action without any mana expenditure, and Azure could do four.

Samantha shot two beams with her prodigious speed, and Azure shot a ghostly dagger. A beam got blocked by the interposing shield without much damage dealt, but her second beam hit home, blowing away more shadow-stuff on the lich in a small dissipating cloud.

Azure’s first dart was blocked, causing a huge explosion that chipped pieces off the shield as if it were made of brittle stone. Azure’s second dagger… was blocked again.

Damn it! Perhaps brute force was a mistake when this thing appeared.

The shield had lost significant material and gained cracks at its bottom edge, the previously angular perfection now jagged.

Samantha moved herself to the furthest edge of the protected zone that she could, to distance herself from Azure, and Azure coordinated with this, doing the same on her side.

The mirror beam fired right before Azure threw her dart. The shield moved to block the beam, and try as it might, it couldn’t then move to block the dagger in time. It slipped past and hit the lich, detonating on his center mass and ripping away shadow-stuff.

The next dart was held with slight hesitation, as the two lovers coordinated once more. They didn’t want the shield mitigating Samantha’s Spellfyr effect. So they’d strike one after the other.

Azure fired her dagger out of her array, and Samantha gestured with a palm outstretched, creating the great cylinder of annihilation once more…

The shield didn’t even move to block Azure’s dagger. The lich tried to hurl himself away from it, but got blasted in the leg, blowing away all the shadow and exposing it.

The bar of destruction touched the shield, and in a flash, it entirely collapsed into the object as if absorbed. It promptly exploded from the inside from blinding bright energy, sending shrapnel in every direction, pelting the immune within Gabriel’s field, the giants, the sludge spheres, and even the lich, with the shards. Pieces even ripped into and through his exposed leg, which he desperately tried to shield by turning his body and taking the majority on his ‘shadow-protected’ side.

He was obviously not brought down. The staff drained itself yet again, getting lower in its remaining output, and the lich’s shadowy protection relayered.
