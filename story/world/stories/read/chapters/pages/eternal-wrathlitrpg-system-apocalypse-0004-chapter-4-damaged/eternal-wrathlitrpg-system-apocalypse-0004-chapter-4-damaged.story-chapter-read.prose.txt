“Hahaha… I won…” His voice was hoarse, his voice tinged with awe. He had fought with everything he had. He thought he wouldn’t make it, but he did. His leather armor was bitten and scratched everywhere, some parts were ripped off. His arms and legs were worse as blood kept pouring out from knives that were stuck to them. However, he triumphed.

He said that James didn’t work hard enough, he didn’t take any risks, and he always played it safe. However, James knew that if he worked hard, all the rewards would go to his boss, not him. That’s why he didn’t even try.

“Shit. This world still runs on blood. I am going to die.” James cursed. He felt himself weakening.
