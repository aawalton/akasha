Don woke up feeling like a million bucks. The morning sun was breaking out. Don figured he had slept four, maybe five hours. Best sleep he ever had. The soreness and pain was gone. When Don removed the bandages the camp healer had put on, he noticed his wounds were healed. Only thing bothering him was hunger. He was absolutely ravenous.

Don took a look at the camp. It was well organized. There was a firepit in the middle and the perimeter was secured between a few large boulders and a stake wall built around them. Next to Don were snoring two other people, who looked tired to death even in their sleep. About were maybe fifteen or twenty humans, carrying filled water flasks and giving them to people. There were three people at the firepit skinning and processing the giant boar he had killed.

Don got up and noticed that his leather satchels were empty. He had gone to sleep with them on, and now all his potions were gone.

“I saw Vance eyeing the potions before… Goddamn it, I should have known. So that’s how it is around here? Is this a power move? I don’t have the time or patience for games. If I don’t show him I’m serious now, he’ll always think I’m a pushover.

So the first thing Don did was walk up to Vance. He was talking with some shirtless dude with a sharpened stick for a spear. When Vance saw him approach, he waved the spearman away.

“Don, was it?” Vance said and smiled. He talked loudly, so that everyone around could hear him. “How did you sleep?”

"Quite well," Don said. "I'm grateful for having a safe place to sleep. You're doing good stuff here, Vance."

“Yes, about that,” Vance said calmly. “We have a policy here. Everyone contributes. And then me and my men distribute as fairly as we can.”

Don gestured at the boar being butchered. “I killed that thing to save Charlie, one of your own. How’s that for contribution?

“Oh, that was your kill?” Vance said, scratching his beard. “Why, I didn’t know.”

“Of course you didn’t.”

“How about this,” Vance said and tried on a smile. “I’ll give you back half of the potions, because you helped us out with hunting.”

Don closed his eyes and bit on his lips, trying to stay patient. When he finally spoke he did so in a very measured manner. “I could have traded you for those potions. Hell, I could have even given you some, if you asked nicely. But you will not fucking steal from me.”

“What are you going to do then?” Vance asked.

“You’ll give me all of the potions back,” Don said quietly. “Or you are going to have your hands full putting back your spilling intestines.”

“What did you just say to me?” Vance said and pulled a knife from his belt.

A short blonde girl in a tattered school uniform stomped up to them. “Hey what the fuck!”

“Vance here thought it was a good idea to steal my potions from me while I slept,” Don said in a loud voice. There was a crowd gathering around them.

“It was not stealing,” Vance said firmly.

“Well just give them back,” Charlie said. “What’s the big deal?”

“Yeah,” Don said. “Just give them back.”

“It doesn’t work like that,” Vance said. “The camp needs to distribute them fairly.”

“And you think it fair to just take mine?”

Vance grimaced and then addressed the crowd. “He just threatened to kill me.”

Don watched as Vance’s men started gathering around them. This could get ugly.

“I’m only going to repeat myself once,” Don said quietly. “Give me back what is mine, or you will die.”

“He’s level 7, boss,” one of the spearmen said. “I used the skill. Didn’t see his class though. Pretty weird.”

Don’s eyes darted to the spearman. The spearman seemed uninterested in him. Whatever the Identify had shown, it didn’t reveal that Don was no longer a human. Suddenly he felt much more at ease. Now he just needed to deal with this big bully. He looked back at Vance.

“Really?” Vance said to the spearman dubiously. Then he turned to look down at Don. “What kind of class do you have?”

Don scoffed. “A deadly one.”

“That so?” Vance said, raising an eyebrow. “And you’re going to, what? Kill me and all these four other combat classed men?”

Vance chuckled and the men who were sliding closer joined in the laugh.

Don shrugged. “Probably not. I doubt I can beat you all in a five versus one. But one thing is for sure…”

“Hm? What?” Vance said and crossed his arms.

“If you don’t give me back my potions, this is going to get violent. I might die. Eh, probably will. But you will absolutely certainly die. I’ll make sure you do.”

Vance was taken aback and furrowed his brow. “Why throw your life away just to kill me?”

“I don’t want to die over a few potions, but if that’s how it’s going to be, so be it,” Don growled. “Whatever it takes to make sure you understand, that you are not to fuck with me.”

“You’re bluffing,” Vance said and motioned for his fighting men to get closer. They were all holding sharpened sticks for spears and knives.

Don looked at the fighters unimpressed. “Try me. What do you think I have to lose?”

“Just give him his stupid potions, you idiot,” Charlie snarled. “Don’t you understand he’s more useful to your camp as an ally and protector. He saved my life twice? That Hunter guy is still out there.”

Don shot a grateful glance at Charlie who smiled at him. Vance looked at Don with a hard stare. Don looked back, completely unimpressed. He was so done with playing power games. If the next words out of this bully’s mouth were not to his liking, that would be it. No more warnings or negotiating. Don had no reason to give this guy an inch. He wanted what was his, and that was that.

Vance’s mouth was a thin line as he measured Don with his gaze. Don dropped the gaze. He had nothing more to say.

He readied himself, flexing his leg muscles. It could get ugly, but Don was fairly sure he could kill Vance quickly and then escape. If he sprinted through the camp gates, he doubted these spearmen could catch him. He used Identify on the five men around him and saw they were all ranging from levels 4-5. But they had classes! Rogue, Warrior. Vance was a level 5 Paladin, whatever the hell that was. He wasn’t sure how powerful this group was. Maybe he would actually die, if Vance was stupid enough.

Finally, Vance came to a decision and nodded to himself.

“Fine,” the tall man said and took a step back. “How many did you have?

“Seven of both Healing and Energy potions.”

“You’ll take six of each,” Vance said. “One of each is payment for a safe sleep.”

“I’ll take seven,” Don said flatly. “The sleep was bought with the boar and saving Charlie here.”

Vance waved his hands. “Fine! Now get out of my sight. I don’t want you in my camp.”

“What?” Charlie said loudly. “Why the hell not?”

“He is too stubborn,” Vance said. “It’s my call.”

“The hell it is,” Charlie said. “Sure you’re the boss, but we all want to survive. We all help. We have say in this.”

The crowd around muttered in agreement.

Vance looked around and his shoulders sagged ever so slightly. “Fine. But you have to earn your keep.”

Don scoffed. “Here is how it’s going to go. I’ll bring you food and protect you from beast attacks. As ‘payment’, I get to sleep here. And the next time anyone touches my stuff, I’ll run you through without asking a single question. Deal?”

Vance grimaced and looked at Charlie who was staring Vance down, hands on her hips. There were a lot of people who seemed to want Don to stay. Or maybe it was that they didn’t want Vance dictating everything. Either way, as far as Don understood, Vance had to give.

“Deal,” Vance said and offered a hand.

Don squeezed it. He could feel Vance wanting to crush his hand, but his Attributes were way too low for that. Don gave him a look. Vance let go.

“I’ll get my potions and go hunt,” Don said and left.
