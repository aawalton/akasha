“That kid… Here’s no mere orphan or some fresh Inner Disciple!” Su Kang gazed at the actions of Tez with caution. “A seed planted by the Suzaku Empire to vie for the Unhallowed Monolith? Or is a hidden master using him as a disciple to build his reputation? The amount of people who manage to tap into the aspects of the Path without a weapon as a conduit, by Stage 9 Qidense Realm has very rarely been recorded…”

He brought out a Jade Transmission Slip from his spatial ring, sending a message to his comrades. Look into the disciple of the Seven Clouds, an eastern outlander called Tez. I’ll send a description over.

“If I take out a Memoralias Inscriptor to get a picture, the elders will notice easily. Still, there’s too many growing variables.” Su Kang held a palm over the railing, observing from above. With dissipating expectations he watched. “Why do you keep holding back? Or have you designed this to also prod for the nature of Tez?”

Grand Elder Wanshang observed the spectacle. But a stern expression was all he had when he gazed at the body of Tez. He had begun to realise the abnormalities of this person from long ago, but with what he just saw now, it only compounded to become a completely new matter.

“That’s not an ability from his Lost Enigma. That ability to seamlessly copy, or rather, learn his opponent's abilities without even a single glance… Is he really part of the factions trying to stop us from claiming the Unhallowed Monolith, or an unknown variable? But this entire matter with the duel is far too constructed, and the rising tensions within Cloud City is also worsening at an alarming rate,” Elder Wanshang ruminated out loud, but no one would hear his words.

Because he didn’t allow these words to travel throughout the air, of course.

Within a breath, he had considered dozens of speculations and outcomes. The calculating ability of thoughts which were expanded the further you got closer to ascension. And he was only a step before it, at Nascent Soul, a Rank 5 cultivator.

“So that’s how it was. They got us good,” he said with a realisation as his dark brown eyes gleamed. “They used the one weakness that our sect has for being only a Rank 5 force, with no Immortals amongst us.”

He once more looked at Tez, making his new plans. “Best to make him a loyal seed of our sect, after confirming my suspicions.”

It was like a dozen needles carved its way around his insides.

A burst of Wind Qi traversed into his abdomen through his body, ripping through his blood vessels and tissues in his abdomen, and even his internal organs.

Shuren stared at Tez with widened eyes, when the secret of his abilities came to light. “You dare use my own technique against me?!”

He’s still injured, there’s no way he has no repercussions from using an aspect of the Paths before Rank 2! Even I could barely reach it at Stage 11, and my mastery of Qi has been honed for over a decade!

Shuren backed off immediately with a hand to his abdomen, ensuring at least a few metres between them.

He saw as Tez pulled back his palm, parts of his skin ripped off until muscle could be seen. Blood continued to pour through his hand, but Tez still raised it towards Shuren while shaking.

Wind Qi reformed around his palm once more, ready to hit Shuren.

He was still relaxed.

Shuren wouldn’t be hit by such a move again.

Bearing with the sharp pain in his abdomen, Shuren waited patiently until Tez’s attack came out.

He would just dodge at the last moment.

Tez put his right palm outward again, this time stabilising it with his shaking left hand. Wind Burst once more came out, and even more shreds of skin fell off his palm from its usage.

As the Wind Burst, an almost invisible bullet of air, sped twice as fast as before, reaching at Shuren’s face within an instant, he loosened the tension on every muscle on his body, except for the ball of his feet.

And he weaved. Dodging it with ease.

But Shuren’s muscles spasmed uncontrollably, and his breath began to come in ragged and raspy breaths, as if his lungs were being squeezed by an invisible hand. The pain of the internal injury was like a relentless beast, gnawing at his insides with a ravenous hunger.

My Qi Reserves are still being sapped throughout the whole battle. Even if I’m easily dealing with Tez, in a battle of attrition, I might be on the losing end.

Shuren was now annoyed that he had to seal his cultivation level to Stage 7. His only weakness was limiting the amount of Qi he could use. And Tez’s strange attack only took further advantage of that.

If Shuren was Stage 11, he would’ve easily dealt with Tez. If he was even Stage 9, Tez would probably be lying on the ground right now.

But if he didn’t seal himself, the true extent of this no name orphan outlander would’ve also stayed hidden. And he finally began connecting the pieces.

Of the nature of the tensions surrounding Cloud City like a dark storm on the horizon. And even this duel had been played up to the masses by some force, probably by whoever was backing up Tez.

Such as that strange System.

And in the worst-case scenario… If he’s backed by a powerful cultivator, potentially even an Immortal, then everything would make sense.

There’s a method of dealing with my Spirit Meridians going into chaos… Fight fire with fire… I’ll be revealing my cards early to the Liu Clan, but…

Tez also slumped over, regaining his bearings. The searing pain of skin being melted and dropping off from his palms only added to his multitude of other injuries. But the only thing on his mind was numbers. Numbers that were counting down slowly.

And it had to reach zero.

“You have a good sword. To think you found such a powerful weapon as your crutch in this fight. But, that won’t be enough,” Shuren proclaimed with confidence, entering a stance, with his two fists raised above his waist.

Within a single breath, Shuren changed the composition of the Qi that flowed in his body.

The Sword Saint Style, and the first stance. I used his breathing style to break through to Stage 11, so with my comprehension, I should finally be able to use it with ease, even without a weapon.

Sword Forge Stance!

To burn one’s body until it's reforged as the strongest sword!

If his Qi was going to be chewed and manipulated, then he had to burn it all, until all it became was just fuel for his technique.

Shuren once more felt his insides burning up, and both sweat and blood evaporated off his body and robes. Though a shadow of its former glory by using it at his weakened state, and with the limited capacity of his meridians at Stage 7, he only had a short while to use it. A thin veil of silver aura, barely noticeable, covered his entire body.

And the shackles of that invisible blade had melted off. If there was a hole in his Spirit Meridians, then he simply burnt and grafted that hole with Qi.

Every vestige of his Qi burned up by the second. Maybe three minutes at most, until he would collapse.

But that just meant he needed to deal with Tez in 3 minutes.

“Easy.”

Grand Elder Wanshang’s eyes widened when he saw that stance, for once forgetting to fidget with his beard. “He actually has the Sword Saint’s inheritance? Why in Heaven's Will is he even in our sect?! The situation at the Liu Clan must be more complicated than I thought. This must be related to the downfall of their clan, when both the Sword Saint and his second son, the patriarch of that time, died.”

While Elder Spiritweaver laughed at the performance of Shuren, Elder Swordfall observed in silence, focusing on the aura itself.

One he had not seen for a very long time.

And he realised how Shuren had ended up in such a sorry state when encountering Lixi.

Shuren ran like a madman, his entire body at a forward angle as he dashed. The dirt off the floor broke apart with each step, a trail of debris in his path. Tez would have no option but to dodge, but he still maintained on using another ability.

“Too slow!”

Within a step of Tez’s range, Shuren saw his palm turn into a slice, as wind formed into a blade.

“Wind Blade!”

Seeing Tez use that technique, Shuren retained his calmness. There was no way a swordsman would ever get the jump on him, let alone a crude technique such as that. His right arm clawed and gripped on the wrist of Tez’s sword arm, delivering a jab straight to his liver with the left.

He felt like punching into iron. Tez’s defence tightened up once again.

He’s getting better at shifting his Qi aura the longer we fight. Basically like he has a second brain, just for defence. He truly has an absurd talent.

Perfectly countering by reinforcing his liver with Qi, Tez swallowed the rising spit, standing without flinching. He tried to use Wind Burst again by facing his locked palm at Shuren’s face.

Shuren easily spun his entire body to get behind Tez, kicking his calves to make him fall, but at best Tez only staggered a bit.

Tez brought his arms together firmly, protecting his head. Turtle Shell! I’m counting on you!

Shuren inhaled a long breath, and both his arms were completely tensed as Tez turned around to face him. And the moment he exhaled out mists of hot air…

A large flurry of blows was all Tez saw.

Each punch felt like the slash of a sword.

Each jab the stab of a dagger.

Each hook the slam of an axe.

Each haymaker the cleave of a glaive.

And the only thing in Tez’s mind, as he took the brunt of each hit, was that this wasn’t a barrage of punches.

It was a storm of blades.

This entire man’s body was honed to the utmost.

To be a human sword.

The blood on Shuren’s fist continued to evaporate, while Tez pathetically attempted to defend each blow. But not even with his massive advantage over Liu Shuren, of having more Qi Reserves to defend himself, could keep up.

Ah… Now I understand… Why the quest said to survive blows… Rather than beat him…

His right thumb was torn, the bone sticking out. Multiple fractured ribs. A broken wrist. Fractured right arm. A busted lip, swollen, bruised face. Strained joints in his legs, torn muscles everywhere. But the most strange part was the amount of small cuts all over his body from the barrage of punches.

Tez shivered on the spot, blood and bruises battered all over his body.

“How did it feel, Tez?” Shuren asked as he adjusted his robes. “When you beat down my cousin like this.” The skin of his knuckles had burst open from the sheer amount of punches he landed on Tez’s sturdy body. Even when even moving his fingers had stung him, he grabbed Tez’s collar to bring him to his height.

“Did you enjoy it when you destroyed his future as a cultivator?! Answer me!”

Tez opened his mouth, but no words came out of it.

A glob of spit landed on Shuren’s face. And Tez stared with a maddening jeer.

Shuren’s anger disappeared, replaced with utter contempt. “I see.”

He placed his fist squarely on the ribs of Tez, only a single inch before it. “I will admit. You are strong, Tez. But your path ends here.”

Shuren softly pressed on Tez’s chest with his fist. Only in the Sword Forge Stance, where his own body had become a weapon, could he use such a weak force to enact this technique.

The enclosed fist is the sheathe. The open palm is the blade.

“Stellar Strike.”
