Hey guys, I’m afraid the day has come… I am henceforth going to be releasing one chapter a week.

I hoped that two chapters a week would be enough for me, but I still find I’m doing frantic last minute edits before posting each chapter, and I haven’t managed to build out my backlog at all. Plus a bunch of other reasons - I need to step up at work, I need to edit the story to put on Amazon, I want to work on some other stories, I’m still limited by tendonosis in how much I can write, etc. (thank you all for the advice, by the way! I do now have a very ergonomic setup, and I think I’m doing everything I feasibly can to help heal and reduce flare-ups)

I am sorry to do this, and I hope you won’t be too disappointed, but I do feel this is the only way forward. Mask of Humanity is planned as a looooong story, telling it will take a long time - I foresee myself still writing this years from now. Tbh the rate at which I’ve been writing these past two years has been quite unsustainable, I sacrificed a lot of time in other areas to keep it up and as soon as I ran out of backlog it became gruelling, in the months leading up to now I started getting really burned out.

But, since I made the decision to go down to one, I feel like there’s a weight off my shoulders, and I believe that this is a rate I’ll be able to comfortably sustain in the long run. One thing I really don’t want to do is end up abandoning the story, cuz I’ve had the experience too many times of getting really into a story only for it to end without a word, the author seemingly dropped off the face of the earth. So I want to assure I’ve no intentions of that, I am very invested in MOH and have a ton of awesome moments and crazy shit planned for the future, both near and far, and I want to make sure I’m able to actually keep writing and get to them. I’m not sure what changes the years may bring, and I may even end up going down to an even slower rate, but I’m determined to keep going and reach the end.

I’ll also add that I’ve made some changes. If any of you have seen the more recent chapters on patreon, you’ll have seen that they’re averaging a bit longer. This is because I’ve given myself the challenge of trying to really improve my pacing and ensure that each chapter is actually advancing the story. For each chapter, I set myself a goal of what I want to accomplish in it, then execute on that, then post it regardless of how long it ends up. In the past, if a chapter ended up going longer than anticipated (4000+ words), I would typically cut it in half to pad the backlog. This is the reason for the occasional chapters that feel very light and lacking. Since I’m going to 1 a week I don’t feel a need to do that, so you can expect chapters will be a bit longer, and (hopefully) be better paced with more content. I figure this will be a better fit for once-a-week release cycle. That said I’m not promising that every chapter will end up really long, I still aim for about 3,000 words, it’s just that I set myself goals of getting a decent amount of stuff happening but I always tend to use more words than necessary to describe that stuff.

The other benefit of this is that I’ll have the time to try and handle the more major issue - while fighting to just keep going at 3 chapters a week I put too little time into plotting and too much into writing in quite a directionless way. It’s challenging because I’ve already bloated the story quite a bit, but I am dragging the narrative around with the goal of finally answering some of the big questions and major plot shit during this section, and working out some kind of reasonable roadmap to get Nicolai to the Lizard within 200ish chapters, otherwise it will just be endless tournaments and scamming people…

To sum up, ultimately I believe that though the story will be releasing slower in wordcount, with more time to actually think about what I’m writing instead of just feeling like I have to write something, I feel that I have a much better chance of ensuring the story is actually going where it needs to go.
