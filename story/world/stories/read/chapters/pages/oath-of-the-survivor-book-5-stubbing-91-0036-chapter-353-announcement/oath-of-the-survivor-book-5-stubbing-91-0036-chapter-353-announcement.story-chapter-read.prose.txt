Kyle took a moment to gather his thoughts, eventually landing on the simple and honest answer.  “I had a moment of inspiration, and took advantage of it.”

“For a couple of hours?”  Tillienne asked with a raised eyebrow.

“Wait, it was a couple hours?”  Kyle asked.

[TWO HOURS AND SEVEN MINUTES, DR. MAYHEW.]

Kyle blinked.  “I didn’t realize it was that long, I’m sorry.”

“Don’t apologize, just tell me what you learned.  And do it while we’re moving, you cost us some precious travel time,” Tillienne said as she watched the progression of aquatic chaddlings waddle up to Kyle as they were absorbed into the World Tree Seed one by one.

“It was really just a moment of inspiration for a skill that I recently unlocked,” Kyle began, trying to find a way to put the inspiration into words, and finding them wanting.  “Have you ever had a moment where things just made sense?  Where something you were struggling with just started to work?”

“All the time,” Tillienne said with a nod as spectral butterfly wings grew out of her back.  “Learning how to fly, for starters.”

Kyle couldn’t help but crack a smile as he thought back to his early training with Repel, and the constant crash landings.  “Believe me, I can appreciate that.”

“What, you can fly now?” Tillienne asked with exasperation.  “Just what the hell kind of second class did you get, Kyle?”

“A great one,” he said, growing an assortment of flowers with Cultivate, then handing them to Tillienne.  “But the flying comes from a skill upgrade in my main class.”

“Well then, let’s see it,” Tillienne said, taking a whiff of the flowers before tucking them away in her satchel, separating out a pale blue one which was tucked behind her ear.

Kyle activated Aura Force, launching himself skyward with a powerful blast, then suspending himself above the treetops with steady pressure on all sides.

Tillienne caught up a moment later, giving him an appraising look.  “That’s an interesting application of your mana blasts,” she finally said.  “I take it you didn’t move the skill upgrade into one purely for mobility?”

“Definitely not,” Kyle said, launching himself in the direction of the nearest collapsed settlement.  “It’s a handy side effect though.”

Tillienne pursued, the fae woman maintaining a steady pace while Kyle moved in jerkier spurts and bursts.  Need to work on more precisely controlling the pace, he thought, dozens of small corrections being made every moment as he kept himself aloft.

“Your main class is a bundle of mysteries too,” Tillienne muttered as she flew alongside him, not even pretending to hide the use of her identification skill.  “It might have the most eclectic set of skills I’ve heard of, and forgive the comment, but it seems to have virtually nothing to do with your second class.  And yet, you seem to be progressing just fine.  How does it feel, knowing you’re actively spitting in the face of all commonly accepted practices for C Grade advancement?”

“You do realize that advancing to C Grade doesn’t come with an instruction manual, right?”  Kyle shot back.

“It does if you have the right heritage.  Which I just so happen to possess.  So long as I possess the talent and work ethic, I can likely follow this path to high-tier C Grade or beyond.  Something that most people would say is impressive in its own right.  Something you’ve never seemed to care about.  It’s as if you never doubted your own potential to grow powerful enough to reach the peak.”

“I haven’t,” Kyle replied honestly.  “I figured I’ll either get strong enough to accomplish my goals, or I’ll hit a wall along the way.  My whole path is driven by overcoming adversity.  Isn’t it natural that I’d keep progressing after all the chaos that I’ve gotten wrapped up in?”

“I suppose it does,” Tillienne said, a faraway look in her eye.

Kyle let her ponder in silence for a moment as they approached the nearest of the fallen settlements.  Twisted and destroyed rebar and scorched plates of metal were strewn across the impact crater from its swift descent.  Is this the one?  Kyle asked C.H.A.D.D. through Pack Leader’s Instinct.  The drone was silent for a moment, then sent over a tentative affirmation.  That difficult to tell?  Kyle asked.

[YES, DR. MAYHEW.  NOT HAVING SEEN THE FULL EXTENT OF THE EXPLOSIONS ACROSS THE PLANET’S SURFACE, IT IS DIFFICULT TO CALCULATE WHERE THE VARIOUS SETTLEMENTS COULD HAVE CRASHED.  IN ANY EVENT, THIS PLACE IS AS GOOD A STARTING SPOT AS ANY.]

Tillienne’s quizzical look made Kyle sigh.  “Sorry, was trying to keep things quiet so you could think.”

[SOME CONCEPTS ARE BETTER EXPRESSED VERBALLY THAN WITH IMPRESSIONS, DR. MAYHEW.  THERE IS NOTHING WRONG WITH CLEAR COMMUNICATION.]

“I’m not saying there is, C.H.A.D.D.,” Kyle reassured the drone as they began their descent.

“Another quirk of your primary class?  Or is this one part of the second?”  Tillienne asked.

“Neither, and both.  If it wasn’t for C.H.A.D.D. helping to save my life, I wouldn’t be here.  The process involved improving our ability to communicate without words.”

“I’m getting too curious for my own good,” Tillienne said with a chuckle, her gentle laugh the sound of bells on a winter morning.  “I envy you, you know.  I’ve studied C Grades extensively.  For myself, I know what each skill selection should be, what each upgrade should look like.  And yet I feel like I’m only scratching the surface of what you’re capable of.”

“Tell me about your heritage,” Kyle said, surveying the ruins.  “At least, what you’re willing to share.”

“You realize you’re asking for state secrets?”  Tillienne teased.  “The queen would be most upset if she knew that you were asking for that information.”

“Then it’s a good thing she isn’t here to punish me,” Kyle joked back.  “No pressure to share, but we’re going to be sorting through a lot of metal.  It’ll help us pass the time.”

“Do you honestly think we’ll be able to get through all of this in two days?” the fae woman asked, amused.

“With the right help, absolutely.”

As Kyle spoke, he began to summon chaddlings.  Tillienne watched with interest as the area was soon filled with over a hundred of the acorn-like soldiers.  C.H.A.D.D. divided the fallen ruins into a nine part grid, and split the labor force into three parts to begin sorting and sifting through the rubble.

“Come on,” Kyle said, lifting himself into the air with Aura Force as the chaddlings got to work.  “We’ll start on the far side, and move toward the group.”

Tillienne floated alongside him as they soared above the wrecked settlement toward their destination.  “And C.H.A.D.D., these creations stem from your abilities?”

At a mental nudge toward honesty from Kyle, the drone spoke.  [THEY DO, MS. TILLIENNE.  THOUGH THEY CERTAINLY HAVE ROOM FOR FURTHER REFINEMENT AS DR. MAYHEW CONTINUES TO DEVELOP HIS ABILITIES FURTHER.]

“It’s truly remarkable.  You should be proud of yourself, C.H.A.D.D.  And Kyle, you should count yourself lucky to have such a wonderful partner.”

“Believe me, I do,” Kyle said, feeling the drone beaming internally.

Tillienne touched one of the charms on her bracelet, drawing out a massive silver lance.  “We have craftsmen back home who specialize in making creations out of these liquid metals, and I’ve even seen some in C Grade that deviate with the techniques to create automatons and the like.  A visit might provide you with some inspiration, if you plan on making refinements.  I would be happy to facilitate some introductions.”

[THAT IS A GENEROUS OFFER, MS. TILLIENNE.  WHEN THE BUSINESS OF THIS TRIBUNAL HAS CONCLUDED, WE WOULD GLADLY ACCEPT.]

There was a surprising amount of conviction coming through Pack Leader’s Instinct from the drone, but Kyle wasn’t going to protest.  After all, he still owed the fae woman a date.

“Then we’ll make it happen,” Tillienne said with another gentle laugh as they started descending toward their first section.  “Now, I believe you were asking for some state secrets earlier?”

“I was,” Kyle confirmed, activating Cultivate as vines lashed out from the World Tree Seed, wrapping around and lifting nearby metal plates.

Tillienne just shook her head as she began systematically moving the debris into piles.  “When I reach C Grade, my second class selection is supposed to be Ranger.  I should unlock a variant class that’s designed to be focused around a danger sense ability, which will be the first I choose.  From there, I need a long-range vision ability, a stealth ability, and a movement skill while the class is still in E Grade.”

Kyle nodded along, the synergies with Tillienne’s existing combat skills clear to see.  The fae woman was a bit of  a glass cannon, her Endurance and Vitality both relatively lower than her other attributes.

Tillienne continued, reciting the next pieces as though she’d spoken them to herself a hundred times.  “The D Grade evolution of the class should offer an early upgrade to the danger sense, and be aligned more with a mobile fighter theme.  That’s where I’ll start picking up offensive skills, two focused on long-range, with a mid-range melee attack and short-range counterattack skill.

“My main class will already be evolved into a mobile fighter archetype, which should give me the synergy to align both classes in high-tier C Grade.  That’s also when I’ll be eligible to join the Royal Guards back home, if I want to.  I’m not going to share too many more details, but it’s a heritage that stays powerful every step along the path.”

“It’s impressive,” Kyle agreed with a nod, C.H.A.D.D. highlighting another large chunk of metal that needed to be moved.  “Feel free not to answer, but I don’t get the sense that you’re super excited about it.”

Tillienne sighed.  “I’ve been wrestling with it for a long time, ever since our Practicum, really.  I see people like you and Arhades, forging your own paths, and I wonder if I’m doing the right thing.  I have my own ideas, but I don’t know if it’s worth the risk to pursue them.”

“That’s a hard question to answer,” Kyle said as he grew some mana-infused bamboo beneath a particularly sizeable plate to gain some leverage.  “What have you felt more drawn toward?”

Tillienne gave him a smile with a twinkle in her dark eyes.  “I’ve actually been considering choosing Healer as my second class.  Tell me, what was your experience like?”

The two spoke for hours about Kyle’s medical training and experience as a doctor back on Earth, while Tillienne asked questions about the different levels of study and resources available through the Collective.  Though she had no desire to become a Collective-licensed doctor, she’d already put in a surprising amount of research, much to Kyle and C.H.A.D.D.’s delight.  It was good to talk about their shared interest and passion as the hours wore on, until finally they received a ping from one of the chaddlings.

[THEY FOUND THE TELEPORTATION HUB, DR. MAYHEW.]

Sure enough, in the fourth section of C.H.A.D.D.’s grid, debris had been moved away to reveal the door to what had been the settlement’s main teleportation hub.  Though the runic formation was damaged and inactive, they quickly found the command console still mostly intact.

Taking a deep breath, Kyle held out his nav bracelet, downloading the information about the teleportation history from the ruins, as well as communication logs with the Gray Conglomerate.  Everything looked as expected, until Kyle got to Valentine’s teleportation.  He could see the coordinates set differed from all the others from the evacuation, which made sense.  However, two communications packets were sent in after.  One was to the Gray Conglomerate’s hostage negotiators, confirming the teleport.  The other was to an anonymous recipient.

The location, as requested.  Rethink your decision, this man is dangerous.  He won’t be an easy asset to control.

Kyle clenched his fist, taking a deep breath to calm himself.  One piece of the puzzle had finally been uncovered.  Somebody saw an opportunity with Gregory Valentine, taking in a mass murderer to make use of his power.  Likely the same faceless people who were trying to keep Kyle from coming back to Kertan Six in the first place.  People Kyle was excited to expose.  And once he did, he looked forward to having a rematch with the former Liberation leader to finish the fight they’d started back on Earth.
