Fay barely blinked as her EDN feeds activated. She'd barely stepped through the doorway and it had recognized her presence. A couple weeks ago she had to sit down before that version realized she was there. Not that it did much more than react to her presence, she was still in the testing phase.

Yshe had finally confirmed that she'd be able to run her tests without any strain on the Holding systems. How the woman was able to determine the expected load of the Constructed Techniques, more specifically the load of an intentionally non-integrated version, without the details of what it would be doing was beyond her, but the answer was she could test.

Sitting down at her desk she ran through her notes one last time. Yshe should be arriving in about an hour so that they could finally create a version that lasted past a handful of days. She'd probably be able to remove and recreate them going forward without assistance, the steps were apparently quite straightforward, but there was the slight risk of critical failure or disastrous overloads. At least according to Yshe.

She dismissed the feeds, only for them to pop back up. A second unsuccessful attempt left her sighing as she ran through the technique she had used on this iteration. It wasn't supposed to keep the feeds open if she closed them, it wasn't supposed to do anything but recognize her arrival. If she wanted the feeds closed they should be- Now it closed them?

"Coffee," she muttered as she stood. "I need more coffee if I'm going to deal with this nonsense."

"Talking to yourself? I know you're not talking to that thing."

"Bri!"

"I thought you could use some coffee," Briana said, holding out a steaming mug. "You barely ate breakfast and raced down here. And now I hear you talking about coffee to your desk?"

"It kept re-opening the feeds when I wanted them closed, and then finally closed them when I was ready to give up and let it stay open."

"So you offered it coffee?"

"No, I said I need more coffee if I'm going to have to think this through."

Bri stepped in to her office and handed her the mug. The smell of the coffee put a smile on her face. It wasn't kaf but it was closer than anything she'd had outside of Clan restaurants since she'd left home. Bri had managed to improve the brew she was able to make after having tried it herself.

"Thank you. I needed this."

"I know," Bri said before picking up a carafe from out of sight in the hallway. "I think this will last you through Yshe's visit. Make sure you tell Sandra that this is yours, there's a small plate of bacon in the kitchen for her next to a pot of regular coffee."

"I'm not sure she'll hear anything after bacon."

"Why do you think I took the time to cook some."

She smiled and set the carafe down on her desk.

"Hopefully shouldn't be too long, it's supposed to be easy, as long as you know what you're doing."

"I'll let you know when it's time to get ready for supper, we have a reservation."

"Where?" she asked.

"I'll tell you while you get ready." Bri said with a smile. "Don't want to distract you with thoughts of food."

"But…"

Bri was gone before she could point out that not being told meant she would be distracted. She'd be wondering what the plans were and what she'd need to wear all day. How was she supposed to focus on this project if she was busy thinking about dinner with her fiance? It was the middle of the week, they didn't go out in the middle of the week, at least not to anywhere fancy, but that still left plenty of options.

Fay sat back down at her desk, the feed popping back up the moment she did.

"I didn't… Well maybe I did arrive. I did sit back down which could be interpreted as arrival. I didn't leave the room though so it shouldn't have seen it as my leaving…"

"You'll drive yourself crazy," Yshe said. "I don't know the details of how the fancy ones work, but I've been told the simplest ones have no ability to reason, only act on the task set to them."

"The task is to open up my feed when I arrive. It worked. Then it wouldn't let me dismiss my feed, and now it opened my feed up because I sat back down despite never leaving the room. I would have to leave to be able to arrive again."

"What is it supposed to consider arriving?"

"My presence coming into proximity. Which used to be the desk. This version can detect me at the doorway."

"How long until it stops working?" Yshe asked.

"Should be just about done, already six hours past the last version."

"And Energy allows it to detect your presence? Sounds like it needs a better power source."

"Where are the others?" she asked.

"Briana passed us in the hall and mentioned bacon and coffee. Sam promised to save me a piece and bring me a mug. You'd think those two hadn't had any in weeks to see them run off together."

"Other than wait so they can see the Engineer side of things, is there anything we need to do to prepare?"

"No, we just need them to hurry up and bring me coffee."

"Fay!" Sandra said as she followed Sam into the office. "Did Briana tell you she left bacon? Did you eat yours already? There isn't any left, just the piece that Sam said was for Yshe but I think he planned to eat it himself because Yshe always says she doesn't need any and…"

"Sandra," Yshe said sharply. "What have I told you?"

"Engineers focus on the work that needs to be done. Conversations about other things can wait until the job is done."

"Exactly, now if the two of you will put your coffees down and focus we can… Why is this coffee so good?"

"Briana made it," Sandra said. "It's better still at Kaf-Fay."

"I know how good it is there," Yshe said. "But this was from the kitchen, wasn't it?"

"She's spent hours making sure the machine is as good as it can be," Fay said. "And she's made sure Anya knows not to touch it."

"Why..? No. We have work to do. You know the Technique you plan to use?"
