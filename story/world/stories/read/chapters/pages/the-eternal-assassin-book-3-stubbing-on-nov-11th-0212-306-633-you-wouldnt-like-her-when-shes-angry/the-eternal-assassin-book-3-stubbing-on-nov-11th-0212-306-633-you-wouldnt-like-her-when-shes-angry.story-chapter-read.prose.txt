Thanks in no small part to the many sessions he’d had with Mornamol, as well as his frequent sparring with his team and the constant battles to the death he seemed to get himself into, Asher knew he was a pretty decent fighter. He might not have been the most skilled dagger user, but with his skills factored into the question, he was a nightmare to fight against for most and, in his opinion, he’d truly earned his nickname of The Reaper. With all that said, if anyone had come up to their team and demanded they all share the name of who they thought was not necessarily the best at killing their opponent, but the best fighter in the entire group, Asher was pretty certain they’d all have the same answer.

Moxy.

The woman who had spent most of her life crushing her opponents in the underground fighting rings where just about anything went shot forward like a violet comet, not even bothering with her usual long-range Air Blasts as she twisted in the air and threw a devastating haymaker straight at The Lost Lady’s torso.

Having already overcome her surprise at having her hairs severed, the lich didn’t even move a muscle as her long hair shot out to meet Moxy and stab through her. Yet at the last second, Moxy dodged to the side, her momentum somehow shifting in an instant as she whipped around the hair in midair and continued forward. The flames burning within The Lost Lady’s eyes burned bright in shock before Moxy’s glowing foot smashed into the side of her head, sending the lich flying to the side with a powerful crack.

“What the hell?!” Asher shouted, unable to keep his jaw from dropping as he stared at Moxy in shock. After spending so many months training and fighting against the battle-manic warrior, to suddenly discover she’d been keeping a skill like this under wraps was mind blowing.

However, the skill didn’t come without cost.

Just from how Moxy landed, Asher started as he realized the crack he’d heard hadn’t been The Lost Lady’s skull splitting open like he’d hoped. Moxy’s kick had been so powerful that it snapped her leg, breaking cleanly through the bone just from the force of the strike. That said, as her body continued glowing and radiating off that violet steam that warped the air around her, Moxy’s leg all but snapped back into place as her Life element healed her back up faster than ever before.

“I said take out the minions!” Moxy shouted at him before she blasted off toward The Lost Lady again, who had already gotten to her feet and was scowling at Moxy. Her white hair was spreading out around her like an octopus with various weapons for limbs, but Moxy didn’t seem concerned in the slightest. “And then get ready to finish this one off once I’m done with her! We need Sever the Thread for the killing blow!”

“Right! Sorry!” Asher said, forcing himself to turn back to his own fight. The minions were just about upon him again, and he was officially scraping the bottom of the barrel when it came to tritessence. He was low enough that he feared pulling out his void dagger again would make him lose consciousness, which meant there was really only one move available to him.

Asher reached into his Personal Rift and yanked Rulfar back out.

“Asher?!” Rulfar shouted, cursing as he turned and blocked a spinning kick aimed at his midsection by one of the blank-faced minions. The fact that the kick was powerful enough to knock even Rulfar back a step while the bloodhound was in his battle form was a testament to just how hard the lich’s minions hit, and Rulfar frowned at him as he unsheathed his battle axe and forced the second minion to leap back by trying to cleave him in half. “What’s going on?! What about the hairs?!”

“Moxy has the lich on the backfoot, I think she’s too distracted to do that anymore!” Asher answered, sparing another quick glance over at the center of the cavern. The Lost Lady was desperately trying to punch pointed tips of hair into Moxy’s flesh, but she was having difficulty. The thin hairs capable of taking her over again couldn’t get past the practical wall of air radiating off of Moxy’s body, and the larger clumps of hair that had been formed into weapons were either being dodged or deflected as Moxy continued wailing on the lich.

To Asher’s shock, he discovered there was another surprising advantage Moxy had against the lich specifically. With her body constantly emitting concentrated Life energy, every single blow she landed against the lich seemed to hurt twofold. The physical damage was evident and unfortunately healed up in an instant from the lich’s Flesh element, but there seemed to be some sort of underlying purification that The Lost Lady was having a harder time dealing with. Regardless of whatever fortifications she’d built into her body, the fact of the matter was that she was an undead. So long as she was radiating Life energy from every orifice in her body…

Moxy was The Lost Lady’s kryptonite.

“Why is Moxy glowing like that?!” Rulfar shouted as he tanked a devastating punch to his stomach with little more than a grunt before slashing out and scoring a deep cut of his own against the offending minion.

“Blended skill!” Asher said, laughing as he ran a bit farther back and dragging a blinking Samantha out of his rift. “I think it’s her skill tied to the Life and Air elements! It seems really powerful, but it has to have a serious drawback if she’s never showed it to us before now! Samantha, help Rulfar!”

“Right!” she said, whipping out her bow and immediately providing ranged support as she sniped past Rulfar’s large body. Asher didn’t miss how the hunter remained close to him rather than take off with her wings, but he couldn’t blame her. He figured losing control of one’s body like that had to be a horrifying experience.

“Do we have a plan?” Rulfar demanded as he knocked one of the minions into the air, allowing Samantha to lend a clean shot that blasted straight through its head and put the man down for good.

“I think the plan is let Moxy beat the hell out of the lich and then move in to finish the job,” Asher admitted, wincing as Moxy was forced to block a chunk of hair that crashed into the side of her with the force of a runaway truck. It looked like Moxy managed to start moving in the same direction as the hair at the last second to cushion the blow somewhat, but the crunch that echoed across the cavern was plain as day. Even so, Moxy barely slowed down, spinning with the force of the strike to slam her heel into the lich’s head yet again, the two of them both going flying in different directions before catching themselves. The Lost Lady halted her momentum by slamming spikes of hair into the ground, while Moxy seemed to be pushing small blasts of air out of herself from all directions, almost like a spaceship maneuvering in a vacuum. She wasn’t flying, but using those minute blasts of air, she could move around far quicker than normal.

Of course, all good things had to come to an end.

Moxy hadn’t even been fighting The Lost Lady for a full minute yet, and already her brilliant violet glow and the air wafting off of her was beginning to fade. Of course, the battle-manic warrior didn’t let that slow her down, and she blasted herself forward once more, desperate to inflict as much damage as physically possible with whatever time she had left. But based on the rapidly dimming light and how hard she was breathing, Asher had a good feeling she was nearing her limit.

“Rulfar, keep the last minion busy! Samantha, try and shift support to Moxy!” Asher ordered, steeling his resolve as he pulled out Crescent and Nail and began running toward the lich and Moxy as they exchanged blows powerful enough to crack bone and snap muscle. He didn’t have enough tritessence left for much more than a few slips into the astral or one or two uses of Distortion, but it would have to do. If Moxy’s weird blended skill wore off and he wasn’t in a position to finish the job…

He didn’t want to think about what would happen to all of them.

Thankfully, Samantha had the brilliant idea to fly over and attack The Lost Lady from a different angle, keeping the lich’s attention away from him as she began firing enchanted arrows at her. The Lost Lady hissed as one of them punched straight through her arm, and even though she was able to heal the wound instantly, it still gave Moxy an opening to land another devastating roundhouse kick to the side of her leg, snapping the lich’s knee and forcing her to rely on her hair to move her out of the way of Moxy’s follow-up attack. The more the lich used her hair for mobility, the less of it she had available for her own attacks.

The fires in the lich’s eyes flared even brighter as if the white flames were threatening to leap out of their sockets and engulf everyone, and The Lost Lady screamed. As her wail echoed across the cavern, Asher stumbled and nearly fell to the ground, not expecting the wave of pain and disorientation that came with it. Looking up, he laughed as, rather than falter from the unexpected pain like the rest of them, Moxy’s fist smashed into the lich’s face, silencing the woman’s skill and rocking her back as her face twisted in confusion.

“You think I haven’t fought a Pain element user before?!” Moxy laughed, her manic grin filled with more than a little fury as she continued laying blow after blow into the lich, rocking her back with each flash of dying violet light. “Three words! Evolved! Pain! Resistance!”

Yet despite Moxy’s big talk, it was clear to Asher she was starting to panic. From having sparred with her so many times, he spotted the moment she shifted from her more calculating fighting style to a wild barrage of strikes that left herself wide open as she rushed to inflict as much damage as physically possible. The lich certainly didn’t let the opportunity go to waste either, as she willingly took all the punishment Moxy was so hungry to dish out, retaliating in kind by spinning all of her hair up into a single point.

And then driving the gigantic spear through Moxy’s dwindling violet blasts of air, ramming it straight through the warrior’s chest and out the other side as she lifted Moxy’s limp body into the air.

“Moxy!” Samantha shouted, her eyes wide in horror as the warrior’s skill finally came to an end and her violet light winked out. But despite having a spear of hair harder than steel and the size of Rulfar’s biceps punched through her chest, Moxy merely grinned even as blood poured from her mouth.

“Big… mistake…” she taunted, coughing up a mouthful of blood as she wrapped her arms around all of the hair devoted to stopping her and held on for dear life. “Tag out… Runt?”

“Tag in,” Asher said as he returned to the material realm and lunged through a compressed tunnel of space. Covering the last twenty feet between him and The Lost Lady in an instant, he slashed his elongated mithral dagger straight through the back of the lich’s neck. The shock and confusion on her face was plain as day as her head tumbled to the ground, and Asher fought to keep from falling to his knees as the last of his tritessence was used up.
