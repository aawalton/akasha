Lately, a few people who had come to hunt in the Golden Sap Forest, the forest where Liu Shu resided, noticed the strange movement of creatures. This pushed people to investigate, and they saw groups of creatures of one type moving around.

In this world, <Monster Waves> existed, which were usually created when a significant shift in the ecosystem forced the majority of beings in one place to move away.

As beasts, plant creatures, and other types of beings moved, they didn't attack each other because their focus was on migrating. This lack of aggression could create large masses of creatures of all types that could overrun cities.

The name <Monster Waves> was given to this phenomenon in honor of the monstrous destruction they caused. It could get so bad enough that even when the highest levels of the creatures inside the mass were in their 10s, Level 20, Level 25, and more could fall prey to them.

Plus, the real danger was that, as it advanced and swallowed more creatures, Experience Points were diverted across the stampede, increasing their strength over time.

With this latent danger in mind, people would always check out what was happening when a large number of beasts moved at once.

Spotting them beforehand could prevent many tragedies.

So, Fortuna and other surrounding cities received news of this, making the powers stationed there give missions to their disciples to go and check what was happening.

If reports said that the chance was high, then the powers would send their higher-level people. When it was confirmed that a <Monster Wave> would occur, then the real powerful people would go and clean up a bit.
