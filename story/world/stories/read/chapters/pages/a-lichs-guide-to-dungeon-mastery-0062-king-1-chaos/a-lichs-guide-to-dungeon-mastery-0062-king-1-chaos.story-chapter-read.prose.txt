The day began with screams.

All through the halls, there was chaos, and as King Yalten unfolded his mighty perception, he was made aware that everyone was woken in the night with the same sound.

The source was quickly spotted.

A monster, with rippling muscles, black fur, giant antlers, and the stench of death stormed through the halls. Guards tried to stop it, but it was useless. Small nicks appeared across the creature’s flesh– no, its armor– as hits were landed, but for each small slice removed, a guard was knocked down or thrown through the walls.

The king quickly gathered his armor and sword– each piece expensive enough to bankrupt a barony, and potent enough to allow a commoner to conquer one too– and quickly donned it.

His consort was woken by his efforts, and quickly hid in the corner, but he ignored her, rushing out to defend his palace.

The monster noticed him immediately and roared its displeasure, shoving his guards aside with even greater vigor.

King Yalten pushed forward and raised his sword, bringing it down on the beast as the space before him cleared.

A burst of power released from the King's blade, and with a flash, the beast was cut in twain.

With a proud smile, the King turned to his guards, but before he could speak a whistled tune interrupted his thoughts.

Further down the hallway was a… wolfman? A wolfman and a dozen oddly-furred wolves accompanying him. These, too, reeked of death, and a slight suspicion entered the back of the King’s head. He pushed it away, though, and readied his blade.

The wolfman spoke, then, in a slightly accented voice that the King didn't recognize. “I've been told to offer you a chance to submit. Do you surrender?”

“Yalten never surrenders, especially not to the likes of the dead. You'll have to kill me first!”

A wicked grin stretched across the wolfman’s face. “Much obliged.”

Spells erupted all across the room, steel flashed, inscriptions roared, and space was torn in the battle that followed.

Yalten’s armor defended him from the worst of the attacks, and the strange wolves' spatial attacks were fully negated, but the wolfman was tricky. Every time the King thought he had the upper hand, a spell would somehow erupt from beneath him, using vines, space, rock, or even walls of blades to catch him off balance and force him to go on the defensive. It was only due to the magic runes inscribed all across the halls of his palace that he was kept in tiptop shape and the greatest of the attacks were mitigated.

The wolfman was slowing down, however, but the King knew better at this point than to assume victory just yet. Only once he was sure that the strange wolfman had nothing else up his sleeves did he go in for the kill.

He only made it halfway before a diamond club met him halfway, crumpling his armor and reducing his body to paste.

Healing spells and defenses poured in from inscriptions on the walls, but they were too slow.

The King of Yalten had fallen in a single blow.

And so had his kingdom.
