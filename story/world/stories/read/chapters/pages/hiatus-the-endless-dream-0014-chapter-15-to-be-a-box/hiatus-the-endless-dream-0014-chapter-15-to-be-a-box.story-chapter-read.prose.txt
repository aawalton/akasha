I turned Peeler over in my hands. The metal was cold, and its surface looked almost polished in the light -- It wasn't actually, but its matte finish was devoid of bumps or imperfections, which gave the unnatural illusion of smoothness. Longing welled up at the corners of my vision. I wish I had done things differently. I wish I could go back in time and fix it all.

But what's done is done. The only path was forward. I allowed myself one small solace as I stared at the static in the corner of my vision and focused on the item in my hands.

[Peeler - "Happy Birthday"]

[Note. Due to the power of this item, you will not start an instance with this item. Instead, it may be found somewhere relevant.]

What all of this came down to is that I needed 2,000 Presence. After that, I could directly learn more about what happened to Rowan and not have to tiptoe.

525, over a fourth of the way there. I checked my stats and did some quick mental math.

Assimilation: 13

It'd leave me with 17 Identity, which was a lot less than what it was currently, but I'd gladly sell all of it if it meant I got answers.

I'd do it the second I got out of this Instance. I wanted to do it now, but I needed every advantage. I could be patient.

I blinked away the static and gave my situation some thought. I came up with blanks.

The issue was that I didn't have enough information. That always seemed to be the damned problem. Could the plant monsters mimic other people, and if they could, did they replace Nate and Maggie? For all I knew, the two of them could be part of this Instance and just be pretending to be Visitors. I had no way to tell.

I wanted to trust them, but before I did, I needed to confirm that I could trust them.

I thought of some plans. What could I do?

The thought of one of the creatures having made it into the Greenhouse unnoticed caused my adrenaline to spike as heat burst through my body. I forced the scream down as I saw the air ripple and shimmer. It resounded like a heat haze off asphalt in the space between the crowbar and nothing.

The description of Peeler said to slam it into something, and ostensibly… The air was something.

I glanced to both sides to make sure I was alone and started pulling. The movement was slow and heavy, like I was trying to open a flooded door from the waterlogged side. The Instance fought against the discontinuity Peeler had made in its fabric and then shunted the crowbar out with a destructive force that sent the weapon hurtling right over my head and through the wall of the Greenhouse.

The wave of force wasn't content with just sending the crowbar flying. It rippled out across the room.

I was thrown a few feet backward and skidded to a stop on my side.

The cots were upended, boxes turned over, and packs of supplies scattered every which way in a cacophony of noise.

The thought made me chuckle. What a bad joke.

Shit. Peeler.

I heard a commotion from inside and what sounded like Nate saying something, but I didn't hear it. I was too busy sprinting out the back entrance of the Greenhouse. I pivoted abruptly on my feet, darting in the direction I had seen the crowbar fly off.

"DORIAN. SPIKES." Nate's voice sounded, and I realized it a second too late as one of the sharpened stakes punctured through the sole of my shoe and embedded itself in my foot. I was moving too fast. I couldn't stop my momentum. My knee began to buckle from the sudden pain.

In a moment of clarity-filled desperation, I drove my foot deeper into the stake. The skin gave way, then the muscle, until it broke against bone. I let out a cry but kicked down, all in an attempt to propel myself as far as possible. I managed, just barely, to make it over at the cost of a bleeding foot, torn jeans, and scraped leg.

I hissed in pain but limped on one leg. There was a series of corn stalks with their tops torn off, so I followed the trail of destruction until I found Peeler's vibrant red resting on the ground.

I heard footsteps chasing after me, and I whipped around, crowbar leveled.

It was Nate. I kept the crowbar up.

"What the hell was that?" It looked like he had replaced his Hawaiian shirt.

I thought I could get out of this situation if I pretended to be just as confused, but that wouldn't work. Obfuscate, misdirect, seem like I'm lying, but about something else. It was one of the main things Rowan had lectured me on when I was younger.

I could keep using his weird reaction to Presence against him.

I sighed and lowered the crowbar. "My reward. From the tutorial." I did my best to look bitter about it, as if I might be consumed if I lost this Instance.

Peering inside, I saw the base of the flagpole, a myriad of different flags of sizes, shapes, and colors neatly organized on a table.
