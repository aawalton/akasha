Bella…

Bella was finishing her morning meal when a particularly strong emotion came from her Bonded.

Cass is deeply unhappy about somethin’.

It wasn’t an unusual emotion from her mercurial partner. His feelings tended to bounce up and down throughout every day to the point that she had become slightly numb to the experience.

It didn’t mean she didn’t care. But in the time since the contract had been enacted, she’d learned to place his feelings in the back of her mind.

However, between the sadness he felt yesterday when the silver man died, and the pain he’d experienced fighting in that big blue bubble, there’d definitely been more bad than good of late. So she decided to do something about that.

Today.

I need to make Cass happy…what would do it…

I could learn more of that old language! He loves it when I talk like that.

Yes, that’s perf-no. No no. That won’t do it. Maybe I could…no. He said yesterday that I’m getting better, but I’m not perfect yet.

Hrmm…Adya is satisfying him on the mately side of life, so another one isn’t necessary. He still thinks it bothers me when they’re getting frisky.

My Bonded is far too easy to fool. He’ll need to work on that.

So what should it be? Food? No, that would make me happy. He doesn’t care much about that. He’d eat slop if it was in front of him.

This was proving more difficult for Bella than expected.

Cass likes those shiny bits, though not as much as the money wench. He likes Gary, but so does everybody. Gary’s just a nice guy for a two-legger. Oh, oh, he likes helping people!

So that’s what I’ll do! I’ll go out and help people like he would! If Cass is having a terrible, no-good day, then it looks like it's on me to fill in his role.

Like that could hold me.

That’ll do.

Hooray!

That was quite easy. I see why Cass likes doing this.

Those look quite heavy. I bet he’d appreciate it if they were in his cart when he gets back.

Growing only her head, Bella swiftly bit the items and placed them in the cart for the man. Spitting out small chunks of wood that got stuck in her mouth, she nodded at the group of people who’d stopped and stared with wide eyes, then moved along to the next location.

This is fun!

Bella…

Somehow, Bella had wandered from the Fifth ring to the Fourth. In that time, she’d helped several more people.

Including a woman who couldn’t open her door, discovering it suddenly very open from a well-placed kick. A runaway chicken learning that a horse’s teeth have quite the grip strength. And a fragrant, but sleeping man just outside of an even more fragrant building being awoken by a powerful neigh from a tiny horse.

Just for fun and practice, Bella had been altering her size over and over again. Right now, she was no more than two feet tall when a familiar sort of speech caught her attention.

Bella stopped.

More of the old language!

Using her noggin, Bella shoved her way through a crowd in her haste to get closer to the sounds she was hearing, her mission totally forgotten. A group of two-leggers was acting out a play on a wooden stage no more than fifty feet away. Bella needed to hear every word.

More shoving and bodies falling behind her occurred until the tiny horse became just a little tinier. She squeezed herself under the platform the actors were using so she could hear every word said.

The visuals were for other, less enlightened minds, but the sounds came through beautifully. Like she was on the stage with them.

“Why do they run away,” an actor said. “This is a knavery of them to make me afeard.”

Ohhh, knavery.

Foolish.

Perceive.

That’s pretty. Cass would like that one. Maybe I should change my old talking. Make it prettier.

I’ll have to plan out my words more. Hmm.

Lying beneath the stage, she had a rare view of the world. Notably, she caught sight through the gaps in the stage’s wood of a man reaching into the spectator’s clothing while they were distracted by the play.

Forsooth! A thief in the midst of this wonder! Knavery, I say!

Not knowing if what she silently yelled was accurate or not, Bella charged out from under the stage. The moment she cleared the roof, her size grew to just a little taller than the man she was charging.

The crowd parted with squeals of alarm and interpreted delight as Bella’s fast-moving form burst onto the scene. The thief barely knew what hit him as her head crashed into his chest, knocking him clear into a group of people who were so kind as to hold onto him.

Take that, you foul fiend!

Sadly, her majestic appearance seemed to have stopped the words from coming. She looked back at the stage to see every actor staring at her, mouths agape. One of them wore a hat that made him look like her mortal enemy… a donkey.

Ugh! That’s worse than the fiend!

Glaring at the donkey-headed man, she stomped the ground once, shaking the immediate area. Trembling, the actor lifted his hands, removing the offending visage from his head.
