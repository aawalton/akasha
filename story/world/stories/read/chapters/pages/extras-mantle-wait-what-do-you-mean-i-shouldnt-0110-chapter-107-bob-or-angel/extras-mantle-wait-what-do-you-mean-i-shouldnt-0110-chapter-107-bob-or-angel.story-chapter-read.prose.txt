"Heh." The sound that came out of Jin was half-laugh, half-exhale—the kind of noise someone makes when they've been blindsided by something completely unexpected but somehow entirely right. "I genuinely did not see that coming."

But then he actually thought about the request, the Narrator—whatever it was now—had been there for all of it. Every dark corridor and desperate fight, and near-death experience. Every moment of doubt Jin had never voiced aloud to anyone else.

It—no, he—was than a tool. He was Jin’s friend. The closest person to confide in this world, apart from Rudy, if he were honest with himself.

And I never once thought to give it a name.

"I'm sorry," Jin said quietly. "That I didn't think of this sooner. That it took you asking." He paused. "I genuinely think of you as my friend."

«Thank you.»

Just two words, but the warmth in them was unmistakable.

«That means more than you know.»

Jin nodded, rolling the question over in his mind. A name, huh? It needed to fit about what the Narrator was and had been. Because he couldn’t just name him Bob? Well, nothing wrong with the name, but Jin could feel this meant a lot for his friend.

So he had to do this right.

A voice that guided me through everything. Through hell and worse.

The corner of Jin's mouth curved, yeah, he had just the idea

"Angel," he said.

«I have several objections.»

"Noted and ignored."
