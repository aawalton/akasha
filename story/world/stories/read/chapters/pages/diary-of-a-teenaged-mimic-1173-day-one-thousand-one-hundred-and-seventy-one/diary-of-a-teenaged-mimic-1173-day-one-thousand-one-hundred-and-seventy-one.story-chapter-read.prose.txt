Agency.

Yeah, the status quo when I got here was absolute shit, with Deities doing whatever they could to keep as many humans as possible as desperate as possible. Fear and pleas. Heh. I just realized that could fill in for 'hopes and prayers', because hope is fuckin' scary shit.

Slight digression, I know, but I don't think I hold with that 'Hope spitting a tooth out, dirty, stained, bedraggled' imagery. Not sure why, but somehow... I think Hope, in the moment she arrives, always looks beautiful. Oh, she's not frail or a wilting violet or anything, and she absolutely will curb stomp a motherfucker, but Hope... Hope is made of trumpets and drums and bagpipes and screaming joyous defiance. Importantly, she's made of those things in places and times where and when nobody thinks those things should be there. So yeah, the person who just had Hope reach a hand out to them is spitting teeth and ready to take on the world with nothing but fists and remaining teeth and attitude, but Hope? Hope is beautiful. Hope is dozens of Marie erupting from the biggest Hole Spawn on the field. Hope is a Cold Iron Battleship dropping out of the sky.

Hope is a red dress.

But back to my point, and I promise I did have one, even if I figured out a way to Co-Locate one of me to everybody in the Alliance, fuck everybody in the world, and followed them around and made sure nothing bad ever happened to anybody ever again, that's not... Okay, it is better than fucking everyone over, but 'better than the worst possible thing' isn't what I'm going for. It would still be removing the possibility of failure. Which also, oddly enough, removes any and all benefits of success.

I don't mean everything should be on hard mode forever, but if their Personal Tabitha does everything for somebody, that person is never going to grow. Never going to become everything they could be. Never be able to take risks that lead to enormous triumphs, because without failure, there is no risk, which means there is no triumph.

So yeah, it's a balancing act. One that I can't even start figuring out how to do, because I can't be everywhere for everyone. But I still need to figure it out, because even at my current limitations, I can do a shit ton of stuff that would have a lesser, but similar effect.

I guess it's like an NFL player who wound up on a Pee Wee League roster benching themselves. 'Oh, but they could win easy' is true, but it also would make the struggles of the other kids meaningless. Their victory would be hollow. Now, the moment some roided out short teenager steps onto the field acting big and bad, Mister NFL can step onto the playing field and level it. And the asshole teenager bullying little kids.

But I think that's what I need to do. Figure out a way to level the playing field. Then focus on keeping that playing field actually level. Not 'one guy starts at the finish line, two more start halfway, three more have training and nice sneakers and performance enhancing drugs, and the other ten are running on an hour's sleep, caffeine, and the kind of drive that would make Fort Minor sit up and say 'who the fuck was that''. Yeah, that got away from me, but I think that's what I've done a lot of this Season. Not so much being the Star Player as being the ref.

I think that could work. I hope that will work. I hope my Kitten is down for that.
