White is all I see when I open my eyes. The air smells of magic bullshit too, or I imagine it does. I seem to lack the ability to smell.

“Smelling is a biological function.” A far too familiar voice says from in front of me. Sitting up, I burst out into a fit of laughter. It's a duck, or a goose? What is the difference? I am just going to go with a goose. Though this is no ordinary goose, for it is wearing a tuxedo and bowtie, for some reason.

I sigh and lay my head back down. Perhaps if I just refuse to acknowledge this it will all go away? It is definitely just a dream, surely if I just ignore the goose in the room, it will go away and I wake up in that super cozy bed again.

“Hero, you are dead.” The goose declares, and I shoot onto my feet. “Just kidding, but damn that got you up quickly.”

Hmm, I would recognize that sense of humor anywhere. “Goddess?”

“Woah, you are a sharp one, eh? Guess that makes sense, with the sort of company you attract.”

“Am I supposed to know what that means?”

“Nope, but isn’t discovery fun?” The goddess-goose takes a step towards me. “Anyways, I need you to do something for me. Two somethings, actually.”

“Couldn’t you have issued a quest? Wait, how are you even talking to me like this? If you didn't have that same shit sense of humor, I wouldn’t even believe you are the goddess. What is with the goose in a business suit form? Ah you know what, nevermind. Ima wake up now, cya.” I try as hard as I can to close my eyes and pretend none of this is happening. I am no expert in divinity, but I am really damn certain I do not want to deal with whatever shit this divine goose is going to ask me to do.

“I mean, I won’t force ya, but don’t you at least wanna hear what it is? Oh, and for some reasons that you'll learn eventually, the fabric of the world or whatever is thinner in Valdir’s domain, and certain other places. Hence why I can sometimes talk to you directly.”

“Oh fine,” I say, letting my curiosity get the best of me, “you want me to slay a dragon or something again?”

“Nah, only punch one.”

I blink as I stare at the ridiculous goose that just asked me to punch a dragon. Why did I even agree to hear her out? This goddess is clearly deranged, and in the worst possible way. I am all for a bit of insanity here and there, but not this much. “Not a chance.”

“Please? The dragon is Valdir, if that helps make it easier. It's a long story, and I’ll save you the time of listening. But it would mean a whole lot to the guy.”

“And what do I do when he asks why he shouldn’t make me cease to exist?”

“Just tell him an old friend asked you to do it. He will understand.”

I can’t believe I'm actually considering doing this. But like, I do kinda owe the guy, and it seems like this is sort of important. After all, why would the goddess ask me personally like this? “So what’s in it for me?”

“Nothing.”

“How enticing.”

“Do you want a sticker or something?”

“What’s a sticker?”

The goose lets loose a laugh, “Oh right, uh, you might figure it out someday. Anyways, I can’t make you do it, or even offer you anything in return. So it's up to your own curiosity to motivate you. Something tells me that curiosity is going to get the best of you though, mortals are like that.”

“No promises.” I declare, already resigned to doing it. “So what is the second thing?”

The goddess does the closest she can to a smile in her goose form. “When you reach the pinnacle, make sure to bring some of Ari’s cookies. I really want to taste them, to see if they truly are that divine.”

—

I open my eyes again, but this time I am in a far more normal place. Well, if a bed in the lair of a primordial can be considered normal. What does normal even mean?

“Yip!” Luma notices I'm awake and spares me who knows how long of considering the implications of things irrelevant to me. Naturally, I pay her back by spending the time I would’ve spent giving her many headscritches.

With those out of the way, I stand up and stretch in a way I haven't for a long time. The bed was truly divine, though that might just be because I haven’t slept in one for a while. Beds aren’t too common this far from a city. Pretty sure I should run if I see one, as it is almost certainly some sort of changeling or whatever other horrors dwell in this world.

Hmm, not much has changed as of late. Just a couple levels, really. Which isn’t to say I haven’t grown as of late. A lot has happened in the past few days, Valdir is only a part of that. While the numbers may not have gone up much, I have definitely grown stronger.

Me too, master! Dovey chips in, he has grown a lot too. Apparently most of the time I was chatting with Valdir he was off practicing with his new skills. Since apparently Valdir can control his aura, to a point where he could even walk into a town should he want. Though people would still notice the sheer amount of energy within him. It's a primordial perk, or so he said.

Speaking of Valdir, I leave the cozy bedroom and head out into the main cavern. I end up having to use Plane Step a dozen or so times to get across the distance, and end up in the doorway to Valdir’s kitchen.

Before I can even see Valdir, an incantation rings out through the cavern, “<Fires of Eternal Destruction>.” I am forced to avert my eyes as the entire kitchen is engulfed in flames that definitely live up to the spell name. Luckily I was standing outside of the kitchen, or I would’ve been cooked alive. Part of me is curious what tier that spell was, and how it works. But I am pretty sure I don’t actually want to know. Some part of me fears that I will have nightmares if I knew at this point.

“Oh Orn, you are awake!” Valdir exclaims the moment the fires subside. In his hands is a pretty large piece of meat, charred a black so deep that it must have lost all flavor. “Would you like a piece? Its prime greater wyvern, a bit tricky to cook, given the heat resistance, but the inside is nice and juicy afterwards!”

“Nah, I already ate. Thanks for the offer though.”

“Your loss, I suppose.” Valdir says, and then in a process that I would rather not think further about, he swallows the meat in one bite.

“Shouldn’t you like, savor your food?”

He laughs, “Ah, but you see, I simply warped time so that I was able to spend several years savoring it. Isn’t time magic wonderful?”

Ah, immortals. “Anyways, I don’t know how else to do this, so uh.” I step towards Valdir, and then punch him in the shoulder. I don’t bother worrying about holding back or whatever silly nonsense. I doubt I will ever be able to harm him really.

To no one's shock, Valdir doesn't even budge after my ‘attack.’ In fact, he doesn't even seem to consider it a hostile action. Guess I am just that weak. “What was that for?”

I shrug, “Someone told me to punch you, said I should tell you it was about an old friend or something?”

Valdir falls to his knees, and starts sobbing. Like, actual tears pouring out of his eyes sobbing. It is quite an uncomfortable thing to be watching. But I can’t seem to avert my eyes.

“Are you, uh, okay?” I ask after about a minute of staring at him.

It takes a few seconds, but he manages to find some composure. “Yah. Uh, sorry. Just recalled some shit.” He goes silent for a few more seconds. “Guess I should thank you for carrying that message. Hold on a second.” He seems to glance off somewhere, the system interface perhaps? I have never seen someone look at it for so long though. Eventually he returns, and a small black sphere appears in his hand. “Take this, it might not seem useful, but I am sure it will come in handy for you some day.”

A bit baffled by the whole ordeal, I take the thing and try to identify it.

[Small Black Sphere (Mundane)]

“Huh, it's uh- I mean, thank you?”

He chuckles a bit, “It may seem strange, but know that even things that seem worthless can come in handy. Storages are functionally infinite, so if something catches your eye, don’t hesitate to grab it.”

“If you say so.” I take the sphere and put it away in my storage. It seems pretty well… mundane. But if Valdir says it will be useful someday, I’ll believe him. Oh, and it would seem he is a fellow hoarder, what I wouldn’t give to see his stash. Unfortunately, I must now depart, get back on track and whatnot. I do have a banquet to attend and if I wish to fulfill the goddesses' other request, a strange girl to find.

“Well uh, I guess this is the part where I say goodbye.”

Valdir guffaws. “Do not think of it as a goodbye, but merely as a see ya later. The world may be vast, but it can be oddly small at times.”

“As you say, o wise spirit dragon.” I say and turn around as if to leave, only to realize I have no clue how to do that. “So uh, where's the door?”

“I’ll teleport you back out. Though I can only take you to the edge of my domain. Good luck, hero, and remember to always have fun.” With those final words, I accept the teleportation magic that wraps around me.
