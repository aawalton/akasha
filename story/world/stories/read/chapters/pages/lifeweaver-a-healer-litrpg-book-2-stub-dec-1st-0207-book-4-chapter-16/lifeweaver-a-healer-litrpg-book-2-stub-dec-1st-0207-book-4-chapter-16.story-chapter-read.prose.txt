In that moment, a long system message appeared, and not just as text, but read aloud in a voice Luke didn't recognize. It sure as hell wasn't Guide's.

The next step of system Integration will soon commence. Inhabitants of Earth, you have done well to survive thus far. Other systems have shared your journey, and some have done better than others. Your current status as a subjugated system, conquered by the Singers, will mean lower status upon joining, resulting in reduced access to worthwhile tasks in The Greater System.

Time until joining: 4230 hours.

A single word stood out. Subjugated. It stoked the anger in Luke's chest, turning it to fury as he leaned in toward the three Singers who had just stabbed him. "Subjugated?" he spat. He'd show them subjugated.

The poison was child's play to heal, and rather than preserving their Meta-Hearts for Smudge, Luke speared through them with Needle of Life, killing all three even as he drained their mana. More mana, he always needed more mana. Even as the Singers died, others struck out against Nash, adding to his many injuries. Lemira's male lion roared and leapt to her defense, protecting her with his own body. Martin fought on with valiant effort, and Savannah did her best, though she didn't have any more to give.

It was an untenable situation. Even with Luke there, the others were in danger. The Singers circumvented him, most of them understanding there was little they could do to inflict permanent damage, and instead turned their attention to the group of lower-level Integrated.

"Subjugated?" Luke repeated, triggering two mana-intensive skills at the same time.

Cascading Threads [Active]: The threads weave from one to another, and to another.

Explosive Reach [Active]: The Lifeweaver may extend their reach for a moment in time.

With his reach almost doubled, he pushed his will outward as an incredible number of threads reached from within his mana channels and out into the Singer ranks, as well as into the group of humans. Healing and harming at the same time required a tremendous amount of concentration, but Luke's focus was up to the task. Sam had recovered somewhat, but her health and mana draining, which she took from Singers and poured it into herself and Nash, wasn't enough. Luke added healing mana across the threads, turning his attention to the enemy. His threads cascaded from one to three more, then three again, turning into a weave of Luke's making, and this one would depict the scene of a massacre.

Some fought against the threads invading their mana channels and Meta-Hearts, but Luke brushed even their combined wills aside, rage still burning hot within him as he pushed, pulled, cut, and took. Mana flowed from them into himself, and he used that mana to hurt them, to cut through their spellweaves, to sever their limbs, and scramble their brains. The Singers died in wide swathes, falling to the Lifeweaver's ire.

"Subjugated!" Luke screamed, feeling the spittle bursting from his mouth. "You call this subjugated!?"

A few still stood, threads of mana connecting Luke to them cut in one way or another, but there were always more threads coming. As long as there was mana to be had, Luke's threads were endless. He was one with the weave, and the weave required justice. Justice for Mas and everyone else who had died following the Singer invasion. They would pay. They would all pay. Once Luke was done with them, there would be no more Singer system. He killed and killed without mercy and without remorse, and didn't stop until something struck his senses. An attack launched from a distance, one full of killing intent, but the intended target wasn't Luke. What he felt was part of his network of threads going dark, one that had just a moment before been shining with a brilliant, glowing light.

"Nash!" Luke shouted, turning around.

The Singers still standing turned and ran, fleeing from Luke into the undulating darkness up the street. Luke let them go. They would find their doom in the hands of someone even more unforgiving than himself as soon as they reached the edge of that darkness. Nash was dead.

"Not you too," Luke said, his voice low. Even if he was still bursting with mana, it was as if all energy had drained from him. Exhausted, he took a few trembling steps towards where Nash had stood. There was nothing left of him. Nothing to heal. Whatever spell that had been, the Singer who cast it must have been powerful. Turning to look over at Smudge, who was happily munching on the Meta-Hearts of the Singer healers he'd destroyed, Luke couldn't fathom where the spell must have come from.

"There," Martin said, pointing off into the distance. "Movement. I'll deal with it."

The Phase Blade made as if to run off toward the target, but Luke put a hand on his shoulder. "No. You take the others and get out of here."

Martin glanced over at Savannah, who was on the ground panting from exertion, no longer able to hold her constructs. Sam was staring at what was left of Nash and her brother, as if unable to comprehend what had just happened.

"There's more than one of them," Martin said before turning to gesture toward the rest of the large group of Singers. "And then there's them."

Luke put on his best comforting smile, which couldn't have been very comforting at that moment, considering all that had happened. "You're strong, Martin, but the others are done. They can't go on. I need you to help them get to safety."

Martin hesitated for a moment, and in that moment, another spell crashed through the open landscape, hurtling toward them at a ridiculous speed. It would've hit if not for Lemira's lion, which jumped forward to intercept. Blood splattered in every direction, but there was no other trace of the pet monster. Lemira drew in a sharp breath, then let out a long wail.

"Go," Luke told Martin, turning to the threat in the distance.

The first attack, he hadn't been able to tell what it had been, but this time, he was able to do at least that much. It hadn't been a spell, like he thought. The opposite was closer to the truth. An arrow. Yes, it was made out of a strange energy, but that didn't mean it wasn't an arrow. Lemira gave him a look before following Martin, who pulled Sam and Savannah with him. Two out of five had died. What a terrible day, and it wasn't over yet.

Another arrow soared across the landscape. By its path, the attack wasn't meant for Luke. No, it streaked toward the fleeing guild members. The anger returned to Luke, but this time it was cold rather than hot. Having seen the attack twice now, he'd grown a little more used to its speed. As he ran toward its origin with Smudge at his side, Luke jumped and pushed off with threads of mana, gaining enough altitude to reach upward with threads of mana and give the arrow the gentlest of nudges. Blocking it would have been impossible, but he could do this much. The altered angle saw the arrow crash into the hillside, well out of range of the others.

"Smudge," Luke said, and his friend understood.

This time, he grew even larger than before and took on a shape that made Luke grin. He jumped on top of Smudge's back and felt the wind pull at his hair. As they took to the air, Smudge beating a pair of large wings to get off the ground, Luke raised the hood of his robe, obscuring his face. From afar, they would look a lot like one single organism of darkness. Their flight speed was something to behold. Still rising higher, Smudge banked right to avoid another arrow, then left, returning to his original path.

"Will those arrows harm you?" Luke shouted, but his words were carried off by the wind without Smudge having caught them. Judging by the way they kept dodging, he figured that must be the case, but it didn't take long for them to find the one responsible for Nash's death. A singer, of course. What else?

This one was a little taller than the average Singer, which was saying a lot, and the woman held a crossbow in her long, slender arms. Rather than jump down from above and attack her, not wanting to risk getting an arrow between his legs, Luke allowed Smudge free rein of their approach. The voidling dove, beating his wings to make jarring movements to the left, right, and even up and down, avoiding a barrage of shots.

Luke and Smudge approached at top speed, but the lone Singer didn't even look worried. Instead, she just stood there, firing her crossbow again and again, missing each time. When Smudge swooped down and opened his jaws to chomp down on the Singer, it became apparent why she seemed to think herself invincible. To most, she probably was. Like with Martin, they phased right through her. Surprised, Smudge didn't catch himself in time, and they went down in a heap. Rolling, Luke saw arrows overhead, a great many arrows. Weavestep brought him out of their area of effect, but Smudge wasn't so quick to react. One struck his side, and the voidling made a sound Luke hadn't heard before, a sort of shrieking whimper that tore at Luke's heart.

"Lifeweaver," the Singer began, raising her face to look down on him. "I'm..."

That's as far as she got before Luke reached her and slammed threads of mana into her Meta-Heart, detonating it with Weaver's Rupture. Her weakness had been the same as Martin's. While he and she were able to phase themselves out of the physical realm, their metaphysical selves remained, and Luke was an expert in doing harm to someone's Meta-Heart and mana channels. Rather than let her go on, he figured it was best to deal with the threat with one decisive blow.

Turning to Smudge, Luke found that the voidling had settled down a little, and the arrow was almost dealt with. At least that's what Luke thought his friend was doing, since parts of its void rose around it, smothering the arrow in pure nothingness. How the archer had been able to hurt his pet was still a mystery, but it was not one he would be able to solve right then and there, especially not when his friend was still fighting whatever remained of the more than a hundred-strong Singer force.

"Let's go, Smudge," Luke said. "It's time we joined Curtis and ended this. But first, let's see if we can't find that lightsaber. I think I know someone who would be able to put that to good use."
