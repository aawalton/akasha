✨ AMAZON LAUNCH: Low-Fantasy Occultist: An Isekai LitRPG Adventure Book 2✨

by Persimmon

The Green Ocean is hungry, and Floria is on the menu..

Nick Crowley is no longer as helpless as he first arrived in this world. He has survived a monster stampede, developed unique sensory magic, and even stared down a demon. But as the mysterious dungeon in the deep forest expands, a new and far more beautiful threat emerges: The Summer Court..

When an expedition led by his father and the legendary adventurer Arthur sets out to destroy the dungeon’s core, Nick is conscripted as their primary scout. His "Wind God’s Third Eye" can see through the thickest foliage, but it can’t see through the deadly glamours of the fae.

As reality begins to warp and allies turn on one another under the weight of ancient illusions, Nick must lean on dangerous rituals just to keep the expedition alive.

With a powerful Grand Exorcist, Moss Oni, and vengeful Fae clashing in a battle of natural disasters, Nick is caught in the crossfire of forces that treat mortals like ants.

Can a young Occultist tip the scales when titans walk the land?

Book 2 of a new reincarnation isekai LitRPG adventure where being an Occultist is the ultimate anomaly and magic isn’t a gift, it’s a System exploit waiting to be found.

What To Expect:

- Progression fantasy with unique occultist magic

- Reincarnation with past-life Occult knowledge

- System mechanics and class advancement

- Dark fantasy elements with high stakes
