What easier way is there to level up than having an easily accessible hunting ground? Breakfast in the morning, experience by the afternoon.”

He already out’d me. What am I going to do? Scream at the ceiling, “No! Not my super special upgraded ability that only I can use!” This cat’s out of the bag; may as well show it.

Not that it much matters.” Looking back at the group, he yawned then scratched the back of his leg, “You’ve got the goods, so the deal is on the table. Will can even do that down here when he’s still up above. Definitely a higher tier.
