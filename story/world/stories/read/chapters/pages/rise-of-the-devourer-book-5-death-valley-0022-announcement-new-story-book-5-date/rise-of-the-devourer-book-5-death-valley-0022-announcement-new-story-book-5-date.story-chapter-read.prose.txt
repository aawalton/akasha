When the Apocalypse began, we expected to see the best of Humanity. Feats, Classes, Titles. People with something special in them who would rise above the rest. But alongside them, an odd name appeared.

A Stoneheaded madman who when given a wall to climb would decide to use his head to smash through it instead. A Wanton Bandit who steals skills, traits, entire bloodlines and runs off with ancient legacies. But worst of all…he's a pest. An Undying Cockroach.

No matter what he keeps rising back up. Even when the world gives up, he doesn’t. And it gets me thinking... perhaps even a pest can climb the high heavens, if they are stubborn enough, to the point that the world has no choice but to give in before they do.

Wouldn't you all like you find out? — SysAdmin 007, on a rare, slightly less hateful rant about the contestant named ‘Zayn King’ otherwise known as...The Helldiver.
