Alex, inside the orb…

Light filled every space of my being, and it hurt. The liquid flooded into my lungs, then the creature in the orb with me moved. Darkness surrounded my hand, then trailed up my body like ooze.

A white room suddenly surrounded me, and a shadowy creature stood right in front of me, where the system avatar normally appeared during rank-up.

“What are you?” it asked in a deep voice, then paused as it leaned closer. The creature had two legs and two arms, but a long tail behind it. It had no facial features, no face that I could see at all. The form was created from darkness itself. “You aren’t the Creator.”

Seconds ticked by as I tried to understand what could possibly have happened. My lips moved, but nothing came out when I tried to answer the question.

Something buzzed at the back of my mind. The unseen objects in my inventory vibrated, like they were asking to be let out, but I couldn’t even touch them. They suddenly vibrated again, hard.

“Creator?” I asked, my voice suddenly working, trying to figure out how we’d appeared like this. Pressure surrounded me from all sides. I had earned a reward from a Creator for freeing the dog, and another for the Drake. “Who is the Creator?”

It reached out, and fingers stretched out from its arm like tentacles.

I suddenly couldn’t move again as it touched my face.

Memories of sneaking through the mountain, then of the high council, flickered through my mind. It felt a lot like when Noseen searched through my memories.

At the thought of him, different memories took shape: of first meeting him, and then of our discussions about devourers. My stat sheet flickered into being, but not as it had become. Instead, the sheet was how it was in the beginning. No class, nothing else, until the crash.

An impact rushed through me as the shuttle crashed again in my memories.

The golden box hung there in the air for a second, and then I selected it.

The vibrating heaviness in my inventory vanished. Darkness pulsed along the tentacles flowing from me to the thing made of shadows.

“One of my own. Balance keeper, from the Creator,” whispered the creature, pulling away, like it had read an answer from something I couldn’t see. Two dark orbs of energy were cradled in its tentacles, then vanished. A jagged grin filled with bright white teeth flashed as the being shuddered.

Dark light pulsed inside it, and for a second I saw two heads, and the shadows of wings.

“Child of the Hungry Shadows. Heir to Blood Throne. First of a New People.” The words hung in the air. They were titles I didn’t know what to do with, but each touched something deep inside me.

“But, where did you come from?” The being of shadow turned away from me, and my lungs spasmed.

Time rewound inside my head, from before the shuttle crash. The colony, trying to survive, searching for survivors before the colony, then on the colony ship as the alarms went off.

It went back even farther. Me climbing into the tube, training sessions with people judging me. Whispers of folks trying to reach the ship before it launched from orbit.

Then a single image of Earth, and my hand pressed against the cold window. Before we’d left the docking ring around the moon.

“I will show them,” my voice hung in the air, as I pulled away and my fingers tightened into fists. I didn’t remember this at all before now, but time froze again, with the planet outside the window. Thoughts of that moment came back to me.

We had needed to launch early. The environmental collapse had sped up faster than predictions had said, and faster than the government could cover it up. We were the last ship to leave.

Something tingling from the base of my spine, moving up inch by inch, brought me back to the present.

The eyeless creature stared at me. “From beyond the system. Movement across space.”

“What are you?” I asked when I could move again. The edges of the room turned dark.

“I will wake soon. When I do, this place will fall…” It said, instead of answering my question. “The Creator will wake.”

“Will the mountain fall?” Images of the green river and the creatures below came to mind, along with the other side of the planet. “But what about the above?”

“They do not matter. Balance will be restored to this place.” The figure stared at me, making everything inside me want to scream. “Do not fear the Creator. You must survive. Promises must be kept.”

“Who is the Creator?” I asked again, as everything splintered around me. My chest hurt, and the white room started to break apart.

The creature reached out and touched my forehead. “The Most Great One…”

The words were the last thing I heard before gravity took a hold of me. I slammed hard into something under me. Things inside me crunched on impact. Dust floated through the air as I tried to open my eyes. Something itchy coated my skin.

I clawed at the thick layer of slime.

Then I started coughing. It set off a chain reaction of pain all over my body. At that moment, my shadow sprang forth, coating my entire body. The slime and dust vanished, leaving behind my clothing, and nothing else.

Energy surged through me three times as the titles the creature of shadow had spoken flashed through my mind and appeared as notifications.

[Child of the Hungry Shadows.]

[Heir to the Blood Throne.]

[First of a New People.]

There wasn’t any sign of Ourus, which worried me. The orb was gone, along with a good chunk of the room. I had landed in a bit of a crater, filled with dust that stretched to a different doorway I hadn’t noticed before.

[You have completed a quest: Mana Lens.]

[You have gained a skill: Absolute Penetration. Absolute Penetration: You use Aether to bypass armor, constructions, spatial and physical shields as if they don’t exist. This skill remains latent during normal combat but wakes when your vitality drops to critical levels or when facing an undeniable fatal threat.]

[You have completed a hidden quest: Free the First.]

[You have gained the notice of the First Devourer. You have come far, little one. How high can you climb?]

A cold trickle dripped down my spine, reminding me of the notification when I had first chosen the devourer class. The System messages had felt darker at the beginning. This had that same tone.

The First Devourer?

That Creator-thing was the first devourer? Parts of my mind froze. Maybe… or maybe not. But either way, that had been a tremendously powerful being.

The ground trembled underneath me, snapping me back to the present, and I climbed to my feet. Dust drifted from above as everything shook. The doors that led outside had been damaged, with half of one just gone.

I headed in that direction as everything shook again. I picked up speed, as rocks fell from the ceiling and a rush of mana washed over everything. Mushrooms popped out of the ground as I raced away. The stone shifted color and glowed, then I burst out to the fresh air.

The massive crack in the sky had shifted.

[System Warning: Mana is becoming unstable in this area. Sudden changes may occur.]

[SYSTEM WARNING: EVERY SENTIENT BEING NEEDS TO FLEE THE PLANET.]

[YOU WILL BE DIRECTED TO THE CLOSEST PORTAL.]

The second notification almost knocked me off my feet. A star appeared on my map, along with a deep dread. The closest way off the planet was the same way I had entered. The portal that led to the megacity.

I couldn’t make it that far.

The floating island trembled under me yet again. Another burst of mana rippled out from the darkness behind me. I stumbled forward three steps, then took off into the air as my wings sprouted from my back.

I almost turned back when I realized I didn’t have my spear, but didn’t dare.

While leaving the weapon I’d come so far with behind hurt, my father’s voice telling me survival was the most important thing echoed inside my head. I had promised them, all of them, that I would survive. Nothing would make me break that promise.

After a few moments, it became clear I didn’t have a shot of getting out of here via the portal. Even reaching the edge of the landmass blocking me from flying back to the Above was too far. The pressure surrounding me ticked up, and I knew even giving it everything I had, there was just no way to get there in time.

A countdown started in the corner of my vision, giving me less than twenty minutes to get off this planet.

None of the beasts below were even trying to leave. Hounds dashed around the shifting ground, some even twisting into new beasts as mana washed over them. The green river itself reared up, and part of it became a massive snake, that then grew massive wings.

Its head twisted in my direction, and it roared.

Insight didn’t work on it.

Its wings started flapping and it lifted off from the ground. One of the massive hounds slammed into the side of it, its teeth tearing into a wing.

There was really only one choice. I didn’t want to make it, but necessity drove me. I turned away from the star on my map.

The green crack in the sky loomed, closer than the closest portal.

Yanking on every bit of speed I had, I flew directly to the massive rift in the sky, racing against the ticking clock. My wings buzzed behind me, even as the crack started to heal, bit-by-bit.

I had to make it.

Noseen, destroying the floating island…

A mana signature floated through the air as mana washed over the rock I hunted inside, causing me to pause. Blood balls shot out from me and coated the back of Ourus, as he tried to get away from me.

I had already destroyed the cloning facility hidden in this floating mountain. There wasn’t much left to uncover. And I had not found any remains of Alex.

I had failed her. Somehow, I had failed yet again. Another little one slaughtered, by one she had no business fighting.

My anger cracked something deep inside my soul. The sound that tore free caused everything around me to freeze. A red tint covered every inch of the room.

The blood coating the back of Ourus burned through his body. Everything it touched turned to dust. Then, he was just gone.

Energy surged through me.

[You have destroyed an Abomination.]

[You have gained a level.]

The level was worthless. I didn’t care about the level.

Where was she?

Something itched at the back of my mind, as the entire mountain shook. This time, the shaking had not been caused by me. Energy surged back in the direction from where I’d arrived, and a presence swept along the area.

It was Him.

Father. The First. The Lost.

I moved faster than thought. Rock disintegrated as I moved in a direct line.

I burst out of the floor, coming back to the room I had originally appeared in.

It lingered. He’d been here. Something glittered on the edge of the room. Something I hadn’t noticed before.

A spear.

I clutched it and paused, even as everything shook around me. Her scent lingered under his. Faint, but there. More than before.

She lived.

[SYSTEM WARNING: EVERY SENTIENT BEING NEEDS TO FLEE THE PLANET.]

The notification made me pause. I’d never seen this warning before. Unusual. Mana swept through the area, though it didn’t touch me. The walls twisted as the mana touched them. Stone transformed into other.

As if the world was being created.

As soon as the thought crossed my mind, I flew, following her scent.

No wonder this world existed outside the system. It was pre-system. The First was here to claim it for the System. Now, it needed to be terraformed.

The spear in my hands suddenly grew heavier, as gravity shifted. The mountain moved around me, but I kept on my path. Stone turned to dust, which then became something else as soon as it moved outside of my sphere of influence.

The spear went into my inventory. She couldn’t survive a terraforming. Not without another rank or two. I had to make sure she got out. I would not fail her. I would not.
