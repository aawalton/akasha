I decided to make the difficult decision of recalling my flame dragons. It was obvious that they weren’t going to do much anymore.

However, seeing the snake form in real time gave me another idea, though whether or not it would work would be left to the future me to see.

The dragons gathered in front of me before dispersing. The Four Lotus Calamity Flame was a good offensive flame, but it had too much Yin energy to be effective.

However, the one flame that had an incredible amount of Yang, and the flame that followed me through countless realms, would be.

Taking a deep breath, I pushed Nirvanic Flame, Dragon Flame, and Fire Control to their limit, copying the former owner of the sword and summoning a giant dragon made out of Nirvanic Flames.

I'd finished forming it at the same time as the snake, and the two let out a horrifying screech at the same time. The two giant figures filled the entire room, their bodies almost breaking through the ceiling, if that was even possible.

Making the sensible choice, I’d shrunk my flame creature, and the former owner of the sword did the same for his own beast.

Now that they were both manageable, they immediately went for each other’s throats. My flaming dragon’s fangs bit into the snake’s thick neck, while the Yin creature did the same in turn.

My dragon had the advantage of having limbs, so while it bit into the creature, it could also scratch away at its scales.

The extreme Yang claws ripped through the ethereal scales of the creature; its howls of pain were muffled by the fact that it was biting into my dragon.

I’d noticed that its fangs were injecting a sort of Yin venom into my beast, which was both surprising and strange. However, I did my best to expel it while also flaring up my own invasive Qi inside the man’s body. It wasn’t long after that the former owner of the sword attacked as well. He’d come up behind me, but I managed to dodge using Phoenix Reflex, though I failed to counterattack in time. It wasn’t the best experience, but the fact that I managed to dodge to begin with was impressive.

Just as I’d hoped, he couldn't use his dominant hand anymore. Although his movements weren't entirely clumsy, it was still much better to fight against this man when he couldn't slash me up into pieces with a single swing of his blade.

I knew that it wouldn't be possible to disable his second arm anymore, but I still had the advantage of being able to split my attention into two sides, so I could fight him while controlling the dragon.

As for him, I didn’t know if the same thing applied, but a normal Foundation Establishment cultivator shouldn't have such powers.

Then again, he was anything but normal.

The snake did seem a little sluggish when he was focused on attacking me. I was glad to see that this clone or illusion, or whatever he was, didn't have the same abilities as the real deal.

If he did, I would not have survived for so long. This was a being that had reached one of the highest ranks of cultivation after all.

Still, that didn't mean they were unbeatable.

After dodging another one of his strikes, I turned around and scratched him with a Blade Wing. That did little to help me, as his powers of regeneration far exceeded my damage output.

But I was counting on the inner fire to rip him apart eventually. The more I exhausted his energy, the better it would be. Once he was out, then it was only a matter of time until he couldn't fight it anymore.

My dragon and snake were somewhat evenly matched. The extreme Yin and Yang canceled each other, forming a perfect harmony. The only thing that would determine the winner was technique and quality.

I would like to believe I didn't fall far behind in that regard, considering the source of the Nirvanic Flames and the use of Split Mind to do more than he could.

Still, the amount of Qi the former owner of the sword could output was simply insane. If I didn't figure out a way to deal a decisive blow, then I would be the one losing. I didn't know what the consequences would be, but I would rather have the rewards even if death wasn't a punishment.

They were bound to be great, after all.

I had to find a particularly good moment to deliver a killing blow, but in a fight like this, it would be difficult.

The key would probably be to strike at the same time as the destruction of the Serpent.

But doing that was much more difficult than it was to be said

Tentatively, I tried summoning the needless, but the moment I tried to do so, a head-splitting ache spread through my Skull.

As expected. I would need to join my dragon in fighting the serpent, but I doubted the bastard would let me.

There was no other way; I had to pull out the big guns.

The thing that was the true bane of all Yon cultivation. I wasn't sure about using my own element because of the lack of affinity.

But the thing I was about to pull out had no such weakness.

With one flap of my wings, I flew as high as I could and summoned the sword from within my dantian.

As soon as I did that, the room had changed colors, turning darker. Clouds gathered on top of the sword, lightning flashing dangerously as it began to let out its aura.

Even I gulped upon seeing that damned sword, and I could immediately tell that the cultivator's face fell upon seeing it.

As much as it was an unfeeling machine, even it was terrified of the intense amount of Yang energy coming from the sword.

Lightning was the natural bane of the Yin, and anyone struck by it would find themselves dead before even knowing what had struck them.

Even so, this wasn't the average Ghost cultivator.

He immediately raised a barrier around himself, trying to defend against the stray bolts of lightning hitting everything on the arena floor.

Unfortunately for him, the Serpent didn't have the same luck, as it was too big. Most of the lightning bolts had focused on it, making it let out painful shrieks.

Within seconds of the sword appearing, the tides had turned. With the snake suppressed, my dragon was able to make a somewhat quick work of it. Biting deep into its neck and also fed off of the intense Yang energy in the air. With one quick struggle, the snake's head got ripped apart with one final howl.

As the creature dispersed, the former owner of the sword coughed up a mouthful of blood.

My dragon didn't stand on ceremony and went in for the attack at the same time as I did.

Using Aerial Dive, I charged straight at him with lightning-infused Iron Claws.

There was a chance he could have dodged it, but maybe the shock from losing his spiritual link to the serpent made it so he couldn't react in time.

The moment my talons touched his barrier, it collapsed as the sword attacked it at the same time.

It also wanted to attack me, but thankfully I knew better and flew up again, dodging the hit.

When I dove down again, he was unprotected, and there was very little he could do without being able to use his dominant hand.

For a second, it looked like the fight was over.

Unexpectedly, though, the former owner of the sword raised his hand and a giant, spectral image of the blade manifested in the palm.

He cleaved down almost as soon as it appeared, and took both the sword and me by surprise.

Heaven’s Protection activated and shattered as soon as it appeared. I did my best to cover my body with lightning, but without a skill similar to Proficient Fire Control? Then I had very little chance to do anything worthwhile using my lightning.

The sword wasn’t affected by the attack, but neither did it care about me.

As a last resort, I’d tried using the techniques from the Lightning Transformation Technique, dodging and weaving, but the sword was relentless.

Side Step and Ember Step did me little good, but I wasn’t out of options yet.

Right as I landed after my last skill usage, the Yang dragon had appeared and snatched me up, shielding me from the Yin sword.

But even so, despite its incredibly high Yang affinity, it only blocked half the blow of the sword.

The other half landed on my back, and I shot forward straight into the wall as the intense Yin energy invaded my body.

“Ah… You’re a bastard…” I muttered as I slid down the wall, coughing up blood.

The invading Yin energy immediately started freezing my blood vessels and meridians, but I couldn’t help but smile to myself despite the agonizing pain.

Not even moments after the blow landed, I began using the Ghost Qi Technique to cleanse and absorb the intense Yin energy.

However, the other problem occurred when I didn’t know what to do with it.

There was nowhere for it to enter my dantian, and the quantity was far too high for me to feasibly do anything with it but absorb it.

I tried redirecting it outside after it had been refined, but that didn’t work either.

For a solid second, I’d gotten worried that I would have no choice but to break through using this technique, but… I couldn’t allow that.

Ah…. Fuck it.

I didn’t know how, but I had the impression that the sword definitely wanted to do the same thing I was thinking of.

In fact, I didn’t even need to ask it.

Immediately as I’d thought about that, the sword redirected one third of its attacks toward me.

At the very least, it was a little merciful.

My body jumped up in shock, but at the very least, the surging and refined Yin energy within me was being eaten away by the powerful Yang.

Did I enjoy the process? Hell no!

But it was effective. The yet-to-be-tamed Yin energy didn't yield as easily, but the faster I processed it, the better.

As for the former owner of the sword, the remaining two-thirds of the sword were still attacking it alongside my dragon.

I couldn’t let out a breath yet, though, as the lightning still fucking hurt.

The cultivator remained calm while being attacked from all fronts, but it didn't seem like he was going to do anything.

I wasn’t sure what his plan was, but anxiety grew within me as a deep sense of foreboding rose up within me.

His expression changed, and a mouthful of blood was spat out as his inner defenses finally gave.

I felt my flames spread through his body, doing to him what he was wanting to do to me. Unfortunately, he didn't have a fuck you sword as I did.

Thankfully, there didn't seem to be any other trump card left in his arsenal.

With great difficulty, he raised his right arm and called the sword. It flew right into his palm and, in an unexpected move, he stabbed himself with his own sword!

He stumbled for a few steps, then fell backwards with an insane smile on his face.

I took a breath and looked up at that damned sword. The fucker dared to redirect all of its lightning at me!

Oh, fuck you too…

I was just about ready to do something drastic to try and seal it back in my dantian, as not doing so would either result in me losing it, or worse, it killing me!

The sword… settled down completely! Its lightning that used to rain down everywhere, especially on me, had settled down, and it let out a feeling of obedience.
