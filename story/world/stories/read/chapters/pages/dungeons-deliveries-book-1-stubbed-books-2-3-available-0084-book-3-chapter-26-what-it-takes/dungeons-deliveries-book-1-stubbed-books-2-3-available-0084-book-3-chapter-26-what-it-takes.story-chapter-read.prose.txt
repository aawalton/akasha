“But you don’t feel it anymore? The chills and hunger? The gnawing?” Snu accepted the burnt hotdog and leaned in to whisper. “The…Taking?”

Loopy chuckled like a twisted Santa and crouched down in a mechanical whirr to her level. Looking around his Boss lair, he made sure none of the others listened in.

They weren’t. They argued instead. Alex led the charge, currently jamming a finger in Nino’s flame-lit face as the others tried to calm him down. Petal Gravewhistle shoved himself between the pair, Nina and Mary nipped at each other, and Mr. Mystical kept cutting Brody off. Emilio sat and watched with the drones from the top of the hotdog stand, as far away from Tank as possible, who insisted on sticking close to the drones, much to his displeasure. That, and the distant happy screams of Wonderland’s thrilled occupants, blanketed in their discussion.

“Oh, it whispers,” Loopy answered. “But once I found what filled my Taking, it’s farther away. Much, much farther away. I have to keep feeding it, you know. For me, for Nibbles, and for GrumbleSnout too.” The massive Boss’s eyes glazed over with grease, and he wiped away his tear to smile sadly at Snu. “It’s still there. Don’t think it ever does go away forever. Those that say theirs did? I’m not convinced. It’s part of us.”

Snu deflated. “Cursed, then. We’re cursed forever with only one way out.”

“System, no, Snu. No.” Loopy laid a massive hand on her small shoulder. “Death isn’t the way out. Please, trust me when I tell you, it is worth finding. I’m stronger, able to construct wonderful things that bring people terror and joy. That bottomless hunger is full. My monsters are content and growing too.” He pulled back and bit to gaze into the distance. “I’m…I’m happy. Everyone who comes to be thrilled leaves thinking me a friend.”

“Boss,” A high voice interrupted from behind Snu. “Two couples going up the TerrorJump. You think strawberry or chocolate?”

Snu gawked as she saw Loopy’s Dungeon monster. He looked drycleaned and she could feel the strength of his essence. Unable to help herself, Snu flexed her massive core and used one of her Boss skills, [Monster Management – Assess].

“Wonderful news, GrumbeSnout.” Loopy smiled as he stole a glance at Snu, feeling her use of the skill, and allowing the full spread of information to flow to her. “The riders are eager, even after the Hunt. Our infamy grows! Now, chocolate or strawberry topping for their funnel cake? What do you think?”

“Maybe one of each so they can share with each other? They seem close.” GrumbleSnout answered after a moment.

“Excellent choice. Just please make sure that Nibbles doesn’t insist on the spiking. He means well but forgets some might still find him only terrifying.”

“Yup. No insisting on psychedelics in the funnel cakes. Work on being less scary.” GrumbleSnout nodded and looked over to everyone arguing. “Hmm. That looks intense.”

Nino’s bare, tattooed body was engulfed in flames as he barked at Alex. The lich’s aura seeped out, tinging the lair with a dangerous presence. Petal was grabbed firmly onto Nino’s calf to keep him in place. Alex shouted back as Brody tried to tug him away. Mary and Nina yelled over one another as Mr. Mystical tried to butt in, clattering the mocha pot. Tank stood on all fours, excited at the escalating argument as the Paladromes waited patiently and swung their feet. Most surprising, Emilio had stood as well. And over it all were the screams of delight that wove through calming funhouse tunes as passengers were flung along Loopy’s coasters, thrill filling their hearts.

“No matter,” Loopy said as his breath caught, the thrill in his Dungeon filling him up and soothing the Taking. “At least they care. Up and down, this way and that way, but they’re here because Alex called. They will all work it out.” He turned back to GrumbleSnout. “And no dental work discounts.”

“No discounts. Gotcha, Boss.” The monster nodded and scurried off, grumbling to himself. “The discounts work, though. I can’t help it; I’m the smile enthusiast.”

The level the argument was reaching concerned Snu, and she knew she ought to step in and mediate. She’d practically beamed when Alex had called in a panic, half demanding and half begging her to come to Wonderland now please. This is fucked and I need you. Of course she’d bustled there, thinking through all the possibilities, and that constant, bottomless itch of the Taking had subsided on her drive over.

But now it was back and demanding something she couldn’t figure out. It made her queasy and despise the fact that she itched for adventurers to enter her Dungeon. Their deaths would sate it for a bit. Then, it would be back.

Again, and again, and again, she thought.

Snu clenched her jaw, envious and disappointed in herself that she had no clue what would hold her Taking at bay.

She sighed. “It’s…It’s…”

“Amazing?” Loopy nodded toward GrumbleSnout’s departing form. “I can only tell you what filling the Taking has done for me. But you saw his stats and info. It helps them become more too. Look again with whatever monster management skill you have.”

It felt almost rude to assess another Boss’s monster. Like opening the wrong door accidentally and happening on a neighbour’s private matters. Dungeon monsters were personal. But as GrumbleSnout rounded the corner, she flexed her skill again, Loopy let the information through, and she gawked once again.

[GrumbleSnout – Smile Enthusiast]

[Monster level: 28]

[Skills: Dental Demise*, Inflict Terror*, Malleable Mechanics, Unwavering Bondage*, Cooking]

[Titles: Smile Enthusiast*, Horrifying Medic, Thrilling Track Shaper]

[State: Content]

[Modify monster? Unavailable]

[Recycle monster? Unavailable]

“Content,” Snu said. “I’ve never seen content on any of mine. They’re all either hungry, tired, horny, or when the Taking gets bad, angry. That’s when they fight the best. They’re angry because I’m hurting and they want to help.”

“I only saw content on any of them when I finally realized it was giving thrill for me.” Loopy explained. “And they’re changing. Their skills are morphing. GrumbleSnout’s [Dental Demise] is turning into something to help, not just hurt. All of them are quite excited about it.”

Snu turned back to him, her mind whirling. She’d been so distracted by the monster’s state she hadn’t thought much of the strange asterisk on the skills. If she figured it out, what filled her Taking, her monsters would change? The glimp’s [Excruciating Overstimulation] would morph? The nun’s [Spanking of Repent]? What on earth would her hentai monster’s [Sliming Penetration] become?

“But the people coming in? They’re all here to enjoy and have fun? No one is trying to farm you and your monsters?” She asked.

“Oh, of course adventurers come to kill me and my monsters, but word is getting around, and more and more people are coming for thrills. It doesn’t stop the others. We are still cycling wells of essence and relics. No, they’re just that much easier to deal with. I’m stronger, my monsters are much stronger. It won’t be perfect and the Taking is still there. It’s just…there are no words to explain it, but there is no going back.”

Snu deflated in on herself.

“You can figure it out,” Loopy forced her chin up. “If anyone can, it’s you. You’ve helped the gashapon, you’re healing monsters that need it with your Safety Spires, you’ve been a friend to me when I needed it. Please don’t give up.”

“I can’t figure it out,” Snu spat. “I thought it was being brave. Then I thought it was helping others. Then I thought it was helping myself. Even love doesn’t fill it.”

She cut herself off, terrified he’d heard. Leaning over to look around Loopy’s monstrous form, her anxiety quickly relaxed. Alex hadn’t heard her, thankfully. He was too busy crying into Nino’s shoulder. The lich lovingly rubbed his back as he cried himself. Petal joined the pair, bawling as he hugged Alex’s leg. At least it appeared the two had made up for now. Nina and Mary glared at each other as they watched the men sob it out. Mr. Mystical appeared to be apologizing to Brody, at least from the way his mocha pot was tilting in the air. The drones were now ranting at Emilio while the cat sneered and tried to keep his cool about how close Tank kept trying to get to him.

“Love?” Loopy asked. “You told him?”

“No, no, he doesn’t know. God, I’m terrified to tell him. He’s so sweet that if I told him about how love might fill my Taking, he’d yell it back instantly. But that just feels so…so…wrong. Like I’m forcing him. That’s not what love is at all. And then that leads me to think that my Taking is something horrible. Something I would never do to Alex. Even if my skills would let me mess with his mind. I won’t force him with my skills. I won’t ever do that.”

“Maybe it is love and maybe it isn’t. From all the other Bosses I’ve spoken to about their Taking, it’s never something bad. I don’t think it works like that.”

Someone said something they shouldn’t in the group, and suddenly Brody was yelling in his guttural way into Nina’s face. Whatever it was, the argument was back on. Everyone screamed at each other with newfound fury, and Mr. Mystical had flown over to squawk at Emilio.

That’s not going to end well, Snu thought, feeling ridiculous and rising to her feet.

“This can wait,” she said. “Thanks for the talk, Loopy, but they’re going to keep going around in circles yelling. God, my boyfriend has Toronto’s Tax Guild Stone in his hand, he’s yelling at a ridiculously powerful lich, and I’m over here wallowing. He said he needed me.”

Loopy chuckled and stepped out of her path. “You’ll figure it out. It’s quite interesting, you know? Now that I can think more clearly, I’ve realized I got in my own way before. My own hurt made me hurt myself more and hurt everyone else worse.”

Snu didn’t hear him. She was already stomping over to everyone.

“You…liar!” Brody shouted into Nina’s face. “You did…this! Greedy! All…you are is greed!”

WOOF! Tank barked, rushing over to stand next to his angry master.

“Cease your arguments, I beg of you all,” Petal cried out, trying to get a handle of the situation. “Do not let your frustration infect your words!”

“Oh, that’s rich,” Mr. Mystical mocked. “The gnome who worships a kitty cat is giving life advice now.”

Snu saw Emilio’s brow furrow and heard the rumbling in his gut. But her walking over pulled the cat’s gaze towards her, and while she couldn’t speak cat, but the look Emilio gave her were words enough.

Can you believe this guy?

Or maybe it was:

Can step in already?

She forced down a laugh and flicked a spot of essence through her core. Just enough to put some pep in her voice with her skill, [Dominion of Desire].

“That’s enough!” She shouted, her voice forcing everyone’s eyes to her. “That’s enough. Now that you’ve all gotten it out of your system, we actually talk this though. No more yelling. You all understand me?”

She released her skill and they all shook their heads in confusion.

With all the attention on her, from Alex grinning at her, to Tank coming up and licking her hand, to Nina looking surprised and a bit shell shocked, to Nino sucking down three cigarettes at once to calm down, to Mr. Mystical and Mary and Brody settling down, she didn’t notice something important.

The gnawing from the Taking was gone.

“Good. Now talk.” She ordered them all, with no skill backing her voice.

“Best get some funnel cakes ready. Or maybe my new special tenders? Hmm.” Loopy grumbled to himself as he wondered what thrilling food he should prepare.
