Howdy,As of today, Build fight survive is on hiatus, I will be spending the summer planning and plotting book 3, and hope to start releasing chapters again on August 1st. In the mean time, I will be releasing a new story over the summer called Lazy Loops: an Idle litrpg.I have been working on this story for almost a year at this point. It's just one of those ideas that I couldn't get out of my head. I love fantasy. It's my favorite genre, but one thing that I never get to see enough of is the quality of life magic that every character takes for granted. It got me thinking about how I could tell a story about a mage who made it his mission to focus on that kind of magic. It had to be a lazy mage, why else focus on it? I had also just finished binging the excellent Stubborn Skill grinder stuck in a time loop and an idea clicked into place.  Lazy Loops. The laziest mage ever stuck in a time loop.

I know its a sideways genre (factory to Academy Scifi to fantasy) but I think this is my best work yet. and hope yall will give this a read.  Plus if this takes off and I make real money from my writing, I can more easily justify time spent writing. Because as fun as this hobby is, it takes up a lot of my free time.
