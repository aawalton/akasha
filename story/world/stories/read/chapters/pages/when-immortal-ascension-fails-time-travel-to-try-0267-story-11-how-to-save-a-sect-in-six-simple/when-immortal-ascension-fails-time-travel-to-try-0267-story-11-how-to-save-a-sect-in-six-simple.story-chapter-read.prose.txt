After gathering my stuff from the alchemy room, my forge, and all the other places I left things, I teleported into my bedroom.

“Holy shit!”

Aside from my window, which had a beautiful view of Fairy Lake from several floors up, everything had become much nicer. Lacquered chairs and a table had been placed next to the wall. There was even a vase that held a single lotus from the lake. It was as if the dragon had gotten tired of my boring decorating skills and wanted my room to have a super nice aesthetic.

The bed I barely slept in anymore changed from its utilitarian look to something that wouldn’t be out of place in an ancient palace. It had white cotton sheets and a blue silk comforter.

Fuck it. I used my spiritual energy to collect everything I owned, including the damn bed.

”Sister Lin! Check this out!” Little Spring’s muffled voice called to me.

I took a step and appeared next to him in the larger hallway.

He had opened the door to the time-stopped pantry. It had quadrupled in size. The barrels of dangerous vines that had been a thorn in our side had vanished, leaving the room looking barren.

The body of Demon Lord Xu wasn’t visible, but after a quick scan, I found it. A small portion of a wall had long drawers like you’d see in a morgue from my past-past life. He’d been stored in one of those.

The gold dragon appeared above Little Spring’s head and began to circle around him like a halo while looking too pleased with itself. “As you can see. The space can now absorb the dangerous substances placed in here and use them as energy to power itself. It’s much larger and has one more function! Go ahead and set a food item on this shelf.”

He gestured to a thick chunk of polished wood with a live edge that had been embedded in the wall. The characters for ‘Treasure of the Immortal Mirror Chef’ had been carved into its underside.

Little Spring took out a cup of cooked spiritual rice from his ring and placed it on the glossy surface.

“Let’s see, for simple spiritual rice, place a low-tier spirit stone next to it,” the dragon directed.

As soon as the stone touched the shelf, it sank into it. A second rice bowl, exactly the same as the original, appeared beside the plate.

The dragon excitedly gestured to the two bowls.

I narrowed my eyes.

”What?” Its tail drooped. “You can see that it will copy any meal, right? As long as you use enough spirit stones.”

Did that practically plagiarizing author seriously give that fucker Bloodsword a limited version of Meng Hao’s mirror? One that only duplicated immortal cuisine?!

Those motherfucking reprehensible assholes!

Just thinking about what I went through! Goddamn it!

There was a time I had to live with a painful, lingering injury. I needed a meal from a Grand Flaming Lizard’s Tail to counter the ice energy left in my body. The only place where those lizards lived was in a secret realm that opened every century. It had closed twenty years earlier, so I couldn’t find any on the market.

One night, I noticed that Fairy Garlic made Bloodsword and his wives a massive dinner that included that lizard tail as a dish.

I flew over because I thought the asshole had finally done something good for his martial sister, but when I arrived at the outdoor feast and asked if I could have a portion, the fucker and his wives refused. They threw me out with so much force that I landed back in my immortal cave.

When I promised to pay triple what the dish was worth, they yelled at me for being selfish! How the fuck had I been selfish? They were selfish!

After that, I took several decades to study the teachings of the Sage Physicians I’d bribed. Then I spent five years developing a treatment for those injuries!

And all that work could have been avoided with a handful of high tier spirit stones and a less fucked-in-the-head main character.

”Sister Lin?”

”No… that’s great. Really, very cool... and exciting.” Maybe if I became a powerful immortal, I could visit the previous timeline and murder that tail-eating, selfish scum bucket?

He had this shit the whole time and could have easily given me a portion to heal me.

Little Spring beamed. “This means we just need to find one set of the ingredients for the Golden Core body cultivation dishes, then earn enough spirit stones to pay for multiple duplicates!”

I nodded. Without this mirror shelf, even if we had the money, there weren’t that many ingredients on the market. Not when we needed 60 portions.

I smoothed down a loose strand of his soft hair. “Let’s keep our eyes out for the ingredients we need. Maybe we’ll get lucky, and some of them will be easier to find on this continent.”

”Oh! I use this to make two years of meals for you!” He looked sheepish. “Since we’re leaving soon, I won’t be able to bring them to you right away. I’ll have to send them through that merchant we met, if I can find her. The one who told me about Immortal Island. She owes me.”

”You don’t have to do that.” While I now enjoyed the food he made me, it wasn’t necessary. As a cultivator, I’d long since gotten used to fasting.

He frowned. ”If I don’t, you’ll just go back to eating grain liberation pills or nothing at all. We’re both still developing. We need to eat healthily if we don’t want to stunt our growth.”

Why was this kid so obsessed with food? He was a cultivator, damn it.

I crossed my arms. ”You’d better pull out all the stops, because I’ll only finish them if they taste amazing. Be prepared. I’ll message my critique to you when we have our weekly discussion at the Alchemists’ Guild.”

He grinned. “You’re taking the wall?”

”It’s my wall. They’re not getting it back.” I only promised to return it when they sent me home, which they didn’t do. “Anyway, I’ve finished packing. Let’s meet up with our teams, board our ships, and get out of this sect for good.”

With a simple flick, it flew over to him. He clasped his hands around it and bowed. “Thank you, elder!”
