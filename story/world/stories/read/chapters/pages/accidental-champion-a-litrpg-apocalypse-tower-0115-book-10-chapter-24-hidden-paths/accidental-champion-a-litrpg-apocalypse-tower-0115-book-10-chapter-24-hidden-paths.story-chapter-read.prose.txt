Xavier halted all movement.

He’d been walking, very slowly, down the tunnel, stretching every one of his senses to their limits, falling into a strong meditative state and sinking into his spiritual sense, relying on his instincts to show him what he needed to do.

The Personal System had told him his spiritual sense was stronger than he knew. That he hadn’t realised its full potential. Xavier had been at a loss as to how to realise that potential, but whatever state of mind he’d managed to slip into seemed to be working. He could sense something out there, something stronger than the first five Void Dungeon-created beasts he’d faced already.

Something powerful.

Though perhaps “sense” wasn’t the right word, because whatever was out there wasn’t something he could fully detect with any one of his senses—not even his spiritual sense—he just somehow knew it was there. Even before the System, most people had an instinct that told them when they were being watched. An itch on their back. Perhaps it was something deep in a human’s biology, something ancient from a time when they were both predator and prey. Perhaps it was the spiritual sense, weak though it would have been without much, if any, ambient Celestial Energy on Earth.

Xavier wasn’t human anymore. Not strictly speaking. But every creature that survived this long had a similar instinct, and Xavier’s was currently setting alarm bells ringing inside his skull.

Can you tell me what other beasts are in this dungeon? Xavier asked the personal System.

Negative. That is not information I was provided with.

So you know how many floors it is, but not that?

You are correct.

Very helpful.

You’re welcome.

Xavier shut his eyes. He’d meant that sarcastically. When Derrida had said she’d created the Personal System, he’d thought it would have some of her personality. It certainly seemed to have personality when it had told him to grab a coffee, take a load off, slay your enemies.

That personality wasn’t shining through right now.

Xavier opened his eyes. Unmoving, he focused on what he could sense, wondering how he knew something was down there even though he shouldn’t be able to know. Was he imagining things? Was he just working himself up, thinking something was there that wasn’t? It wasn’t as though he was immune to irrational fears, despite everything he’d gone through.

But no. He didn’t believe he was imagining this feeling.

There was definitely something up ahead, something watching him.

Xavier could simply keep going. He’d encounter the beast in a moment. But he wasn’t in this dungeon simply to fight beasts. It wasn’t just about slaying his enemies. This whole experience was about learning—learning things he couldn’t learn back in his home universe.

Even Jhanku, one of the top ten most powerful beings in his universe, had been someone Xavier could sense. He’d been able to pierce the man’s veil—and he would have been able to do that even without the help of the other Xaviers.

Xavier knew how to conceal his energies. He knew how to become invisible. But he didn’t think he could do it on the level of the beasts here. He certainly wouldn’t be able to hide from a Wanderer.

Those first five Gorgotlin—he hadn’t been able to sense their energies at all. Not until they’d cast a spell. When Derrida had come to his door, he’d only been able to see her with the naked eye. Somehow, she was immune to any other means of detection, the Void Dungeon-created beasts seemed to have that same kind of immunity, though he imagined theirs would be weaker than hers.

When he’d unlocked the Immortal Soul lens, he’d thought he’d come to possess the most powerful form of spiritual sense. His soul could detect things his normal, inferior spiritual sense simply couldn’t. Even Roln had been impressed with this discovery.

Xavier was starting to wonder about that, however. About all the things Roln had been impressed with. About all the things Roln had told him no one else had ever managed to do. Something told Xavier much of that wasn’t true. He didn’t know if Roln had been lying, or if Derrida had withheld the information from him.

Hell, it could be that Roln had never known about things like the Immortal Soul lens…

Xavier wanted to believe the mental construct wouldn’t lie to him, considering how much he’d grown to trust the dead Wanderer. But he would have known Xavier’s Immortal Soul lens wouldn’t be powerful enough to detect these simple Void Dungeon-created beasts without them casting a spell, wouldn’t he? Why hadn’t he ever mentioned it? Because it wasn’t relevant?

I’m thinking too much. Far too much.

Not for the first time, he reined in his wandering mind and refocused all his attention on the task in front of him. It was in that moment he realised he was ignoring an incredibly valuable asset.

The Personal System was supposed to use his own ability to detect things and enhance it, to help him describe the world around him. If Xavier could sense something out there, even faintly, it followed that the Personal System would be able to as well.

He prompted the Personal System about this.

There is a presence. A faint outline of power. Very faint. It’s on the very limits of your detection ability.

Is there any way I can detect it better?

There was a pause. Longer than usual—usually there wasn’t one at all.

Yes. You must strengthen your spiritual sense.

Another pause.

Do you wish me to activate a Training Quest?

Xavier blinked. A Training Quest? What’s that?

I have knowledge of how to enhance almost all of your abilities. I can help guide you down the right paths to accumulate more power in ways that benefit you. A Training Quest is one of the ways I can facilitate this.

Xavier gritted his teeth. He couldn’t help himself. Why didn’t you tell me about this earlier?

You didn’t ask.

What other things can you do? Xavier thought, then waved a hand. Not right now. Let’s focus first on this Training Quest. Yes, I would like you to activate the Training Quest!

Wonderful! It’s good to see you be proactive about enhancing your powers! You’re going to need to be, if you want to survive the Void Dungeon.

A lot of people die here.

Creating Training Quest…

Training Quest created.

Activating Training Quest…

Training Quest activated.

Training Quest: Get Some Sense

Goal: Enhance your spiritual sense, Immortal Soul lens, or both, to better sense cloaked enemies so they don’t sneak up on you and stab you in the back when you’re sitting there, picking your nose.

Xavier rolled his eyes. He was wondering if the Personal System had been listening to his thoughts when he’d said it had no personality, and now it was overcompensating.

The notification continued.

Method 1: Strengthen your Immortal Soul

Method 2: Strengthen your base spiritual sense

Method 3: Strengthen your ability to utilise the senses you already possess to their fullest potential.

Focusing on all three methods is optimal, but focusing on any one of these will help you step forward in the Get Some Sense Training Quest.

Which method would you like to focus on first?

Xavier drilled deeper into the first method.

He’d been strengthening his soul for a long time. Soul Harden was one of the ways he did that. But it was his Living Soul he’d been strengthening, not necessarily his Immortal Soul. The Living Soul was where his Soul Energy was kept. When he manipulated energy outside himself, whether to manifest a spell pattern or to dispel energy on the subatomic level to disable an enemy’s spell, he used the Immortal Soul to see, and the Living Soul to touch.

He didn’t know there was a particular method to empower an Immortal Soul. He knew that Immortal Souls were what was left over of a being when they died. That an Immortal Soul was the spirit. Void Beings were Immortal Souls, as were otherworldly spirits. Ghosts. All of them. The souls he reaped were Immortal Souls as well, meaning Xavier could cause an enemy to have a true death—no after life for them.

Xavier’s Ancestral Soul, whom he hadn’t managed to contact for some time, was also an Immortal Soul. Actually, it was many Immortal Souls.

Xavier didn’t understand all that much about the Ancestral Soul, but the fact it was guiding his life, helping him head in the right direction… it had brought some comfort to him, since he’d discovered that.

Once he’d gotten over the strangeness of it.

The Personal System outlined several methods for increasing the strength of the Immortal Soul. Some of them sounded familiar, even if he hadn’t realised they could strengthen the Immortal Soul.

Others… didn’t sound all that familiar.

The strongest method of strengthening an Immortal Soul was Astral Projection. Xavier had heard of Astral Projection, of course. Hell, he’d heard of it well before he’d been integrated into the System. There were different forms of Astral Projection, but the most powerful form was one that allowed a being to have a true out of body experience—to separate their Immortal Soul from their body.

Xavier had encountered enemies who didn’t possess souls before. Sovereign Rewke Fouran, when he’d faced Xavier and the arena, had removed his soul before the fight, as he’d been afraid of having it reaped. He’d also encountered people like Arrack who’d had their souls essentially “ejected” at the time of their death, so they could be reborn in a similar way to how Xavier had done for Volkarin.

But the form of Astral Projection the Personal System spoke of was nothing like that. It was also nothing like the others forms he’d encountered, where one’s image might appear far, far away, at varying levels of solidity. This was a method where the user’s Immortal Soul left its body to fight other spirits in a different phase—a different realm.

Xavier knew of different phases. There were plenty of them. He’d used spells that allowed him to phase into them—Otherworld Phase being quite a prominent ability. But none of those abilities had allowed him to be conscious in that other phase.

Astral Projection would.

It worked like this: Xavier would cast this spell, and his Immortal Soul would then drift out of his body and into the spirit realm, a realm that shared the same terrain as the mortal realm. This wasn’t the spirit realm as Xavier knew it—it wasn’t the Otherworld or other similar afterlifes out there in the multiverse.

It was all around him, all the time.

Except, it wasn’t here in the Void Dungeon. The void didn’t have different realms and phases. The void simply was. But that didn’t mean there weren’t spirits to face, for all spirits eventually made their way into the void if they weren’t tethered to a universe strongly enough.

Xavier worried about how effective using this method would be here in the Void Dungeon, considering none of the enemies he faced had souls. And the void was a big, big place. Should he simply have his Immortal Soul fight Void Beings?

When he’d asked the Personal System, it had told him in no uncertain terms that going up against a Void Being as his Immortal Soul would be akin to suicide.

But that didn’t mean Astral Projection wasn’t a method he could use. Indeed, according to the Personal System, there were spirits all around him. Xavier figured he would be able to identify these spirits, considering how powerful his spiritual sense was, but apparently that simply wasn’t the case.

Why didn’t Roln ever tell me about this? he finally asked the Personal System.

Roln did not have this information. His spiritual sense was powerful, far more powerful than your own, but this was a path of power the man never walked down, even as a Wanderer. There are many paths to power, Xavier, and many of those paths are hidden, even from other Wanderers.

This is one such Hidden Path.

There is a reason Wanderers like Derrida can conceal themselves more strongly. There is a reason they can sense something a trillion universes away. And there is a reason they keep these abilities to themselves.

Even many who know of this path do not choose to walk it—Gathering Spirits is much the same as reaping them. You are giving these beings a true death.

Xavier tilted his head to one side, narrowing his eyes.

Hidden Paths.

He shook his head.

He didn’t like the idea of Hidden Paths. What if Derrida had shared these powers with her faction? With an entire universe? Maybe they would have become strong enough to take on the Interdimensional Dragon… though Xavier had his suspicions that wouldn’t be the case.

When he’d agreed to come out here, into the multiverse, he’d expected to be introduced to new paths of power. New knowledge. New paths of advancement. But he hadn’t expected there to be so many secrets, even out here.

Perhaps that was incredibly naïve, but he simply thought that someone like Roln—not to mention all the other Wanderers who’d been travelling the multiverse for trillions upon trillions of years—would know… almost everything there was to know.

I guess I was wrong about that. I wonder what else I’ve been wrong about?]
