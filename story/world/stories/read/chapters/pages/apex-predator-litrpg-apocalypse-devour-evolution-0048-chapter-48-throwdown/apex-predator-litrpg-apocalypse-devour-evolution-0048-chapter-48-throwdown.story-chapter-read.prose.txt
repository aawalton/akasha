Don was winded. He took a knee and tried to steady his breath. Snarlak just watched him, haughty arrogance in its eyes, as it stood proud and tall in its new form.

It was waiting for Don.

Don chugged down his last health potion. That brought him back to 70% Health. The fractured bones debuff persisted. He would just have to work through it. A few cracked ribs wouldn’t slow him down.

Don just breathed and watched his Energy tick up. He would need it for the psychics. He had a feeling he would need every single advantage he could muster.

The fight had been rough, and the previous damage Don had done to Snarlak seemed to have been recuperated. Great. So Don was at a disadvantage on that front. The new exoskeleton the bastard was wearing looked durable. The boss certainly was heavier now. Telekinesis would be harder to use.

Life Drain could be pivotal here. If he could dance around the boss and keep the drain on, he could get back to a safe Health.

But Don just had a feeling it wouldn’t be that easy.

Still, better a shitty plan than no plan at all.

Snarlak blasted him with a sonic boom. Don had enough presence of mind to block it with a psychic vacuum. The message was clear though. Get over here.

Don grunted and got up. It was time to fight. The icy bridge crunched under his feet. He took slow steps, still taking his time to steady his breath.

As he ascended the bridge, Don got a better look at the platform. Assessing the environment was important. There were advantages to be found. Stalactites in the ceiling. They were pretty high up, and when Don tested if his Telekinesis could reach it, the answer was no.

There were stalagmites on the floor though. They were thick and interspersed randomly every five to seven feet. The floor was gleaming crystal clear glass ice. Don sighed. Their battle arena wouldn’t just be slippery. It would be a goddamn skating rink. However, Don was starting to formulate an idea.

Snarlak took some paces back and it moved smoothly on the ice, its talons crunching into the ice. When it took steps towards the middle of the arena, the cracked ice repaired itself, making the ice crystalline clear again.

What the…

When Don was fully on the platform, Snarlak grinned. It was an uncannily human expression. Then to Don’s shock it spoke.

“Welcome human,” Snarlak said in a guttural voice. “This is your death.”

“You can talk?” Don asked. “Look. Let’s slow down for a moment and—”

Snarlak let out a sharp and short screech.

“No slow. We fight, you die. Prepare.”

Then its throat gurgled and it looked like it was about to vomit. Soon the black sludge started pouring out of its mouth. Don’s Survival Instincts absolutely screamed for him to dodge. He jumped down on the ice, face first.

Three globs of black goop flew over his head like missiles. They struck somewhere in the cavernous spire’s wall, but Don was focused on the few drops that had fallen on the ice. The drops hissed as they burned through the ice. However, soon the ice repaired itself.

Don was sliding, and when Snarlak jumped in the air and shot out another volley of goop, Don used Telekinesis to pull himself towards one of the stalagmites, completely changing his trajectory. In a controlled slam, Don hit the stalagmite and immediately kicked off, sliding around the room on his stomach, zipping from stalagmite to another, while he dodged the black bile.

Don had never gone ice sledding on his ribs before, but here he was, shooting across the rink on his chest like a pissed-off human hockey puck.

Snarlak wasn’t just vomiting bile randomly either. It was tracking. Every angle, every bounce. The bastard was smart now. Don kicked off another stalagmite and slid into a low crouch behind one. Just in time. A bile glob hit the side and hissed, chewing through the stone like acid. The stench alone was enough to make Don gag.

So. Sonic blasts. Armor. Flight. Acid bile. Human speech.

Just great.

Don pushed off the stalagmite, circled wide, and skated in low. He was now crouching low as he slid on the ice. Better to have his feet on the ground. With his superior physique he could keep himself balanced.

Snarlak watched. Didn’t fire again. It was waiting. Learning. Its long arms were spread like it was ready to hug a truck. The claws twitched. The four wings beat once and it hovered just off the ground.

Don launched a spearhead with his mind. Just to probe.

Snarlak deflected it. Swiped it out of the air like a mosquito. Then it moved. A blur. It closed the distance in a second.

Don kicked back, slipped on the ice, but caught himself in a backpedal. Just in time to catch a claw swipe with his own. The blow verberated down from the black metal of his claws. A stalagmite exploded behind Don.

Don pulled the locked claws downwards and towards himself and headbutted Snarlak.

Snarlak shrieked out a sonic blast. Don flinched but held the vacuum. Unfortunately that black spittle flew out and it burned Don’s face and shoulders. The boss followed up with a backhand, but Don slid under it on one knee. As he passed under the thing’s body, he raked his claws across its gut. They cut into the exoskeleton but drew no blood. However, Snarlak was absolutely shocked that Don had managed to penetrate its armor.

It jumped backwards several yards immediately and held a hand over its abdomen.

This guy is definitely sapient. Talks. Fears death.

Snarlak snapped out of it. It screeched again—not sonic, just rage—and lunged. In a blur it shot towards Don like a cannonball, both taloned hands raised up in the air

Don dodged left, but slipped. The black claws came down like guillotines. He caught one with his claw, but the other changed trajectory at the last second and tore across his side.

[Health at 44%][Debuff Inflicted: Moderate bleeding]

“Shit—!”

Don pushed off with Telekinesis and skated away, clutching his side. He needed space. Snarlak followed relentlessly. It was enjoying this now.

Don would not be prey.

He sped up and started skating around, using his Telekinesis to drift off with tremendous speed across the platform. As fast as Snarlak was, it couldn’t keep up with Don’s acceleration. So it started shooting bile at him again.

That was what Don had been waiting for.

He ducked under a volley and yanked a stalagmite from the ice. His claws sank in easily and the ice cracked under his strength. Then he took a hold of it with his mind and threw it at Snarlak like a javelin.

Snarlak caught it with one claw and shattered it.

Don grinned.

While the boss was distracted, he pulled himself forward and leapt, aiming right for the damaged crack in the abdomen.

He struck with both of his claws. Then he clamped his legs around one of Snarlak’s and pulled it down, using Telekinesis as leverage.

The boss toppled and its head hit the ice with a satisfying crack.

But that wasn’t nearly enough.

Snarlak howled and thrashed. It flailed and Don clung on like a parasite. He kept grinding his claws into the exoskeleton. Finally when he twisted a wrist, he drew blood. Black oily sludge started pouring out.

The boss was having none of it. It vomited the black goop all over Don. His back hissed and felt like it was literally burning

[Debuff inflicted: Moderate burns, Moderate poisoning]

Don bit his teeth together and kept slicing into the enemy’s guts.

Then Snarlak launched into the air.

Don’s stomach dropped. He was being carried. Ten, twenty, thirty feet up. The boss soared and began to spin. Fast. Like a goddamn blender.

“Oh come on—”

Don was flung off. Spiraling. He hit a wall, bounced, and landed on the ice at the edge of the platform.

[Health at 37%]

[Debuff inflicted: Fractured bones]

Snarlak descended near Don. It was grinning again, with a sadistic glee in its eyes. Its body seemed to be shaking, like it was laughing.

It lit something up in Don. He found he was thrilled. This was where he was meant to be. This was what was best in life. In the heart of the battle. In the intensity of deathmatches, Don found himself, and he liked what he found.

In this crucible of two souls struggling for supremacy, Don was starting to awaken. He was done playing footsie. It was time to go all out.

Despite the broken bones, the acid burns on his back and the blood in his mouth, Don laughed too. He got up and pushed the pain down. He laughed and the whole goddamn spire echoed with his voice.

Snarlak didn’t like that.

“Madman!” it screeched.

Don got up, spat out some blood and gave Snarlak a bloody grin.

“You play with your food too much. Last mistake you ever made.”
