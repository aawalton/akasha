But those were all, in Niala's mind, lesser options.

On the couch nearby, Isaac reviewed the day's lessons with Annabelle to her very obvious displeasure. The little girl's exasperated tone as she answered each of her brother's questions like they were the stupidest, most obvious things in the world made Angeline chuckle despite herself.

Too early to know for certain, but evidence was mounting that the kingdom was being meddled with by two different powers: the surprising involvement of the Pakag clans, and the somewhat expected, yet brow-raising, Sonnel Empire.

The former's recruitment of mercenaries throughout Amberfall was all but confirmed at this point, yet the pieces did not fit at all with the money laundering circle that her sister's actions had uncovered.

What's more, that bit of unpleasantness was turning out to be only the tip of the iceberg.

She sighed and lowered her hand; she'd re-read the report four times already. At this point, she was just using it as a distraction.

Isaac glanced her way with a concerned but supportive look; the man seemed hyper-alert to every one of her moves or moods, always ready to offer his support.

She rewarded him with a genuine, loving smile, because she loved the attention.

Because it was his attention. Her beautiful, dedicated, dutiful, charmingly submissive husband that she adored to bits. Those heady, swirling feelings still caught her off-guard, and she hoped that would never change.

He beamed back at her before returning his attention to his sister, while she let her thoughts flow back to the content of the report.

Scheming nobles were normal for a large kingdom like Amberfall. A certain amount of it was even, in a perverse way, healthy.

However, the attempted interference of the Tavergunn principality had brought up suspicion that something larger was at play; the country was too small, by itself, to risk the ire of Amberfall, the main trading partner of the mostly land-locked nation.

They had to have had reassurances that any negative impact would be worth their while.

From Karline's report, that reassurance had been a few boatloads of Sonnel Francs.

All from verifiable sources, of course; donations, special taxes, one-time merchandise trades, wedding gifts. Very normal, except for the amount and frequency in the weeks following the Sangliore fortress siege.

It was classic Sonnel playbook; use their large and far-reaching trade network to move vast sums of money in many smaller transactions. Hard to keep track of and notice.

Unless you had a Karline in your back pocket. That woman was incredible at data collection. Angeline didn't know how, yet, but she had to pry her away from the Scourer's camp.

Thoughts for later. For now, they had to figure out just how deep Sonnel's involvement with the current crop of plotting nobles went.

She'd turn the tables without the interlopers realizing, and then they'd be the ones paying for everything.

Yes.
