Luna watched as the man with the flaming crown who had attempted to blast her Emperor descended from above the clouds.

He thinks he is beyond us all, she thought. I can tell just by looking at him. He believes he is worthy of challenging the Emperor directly. Such arrogance must be punished.

There were two others who descended alongside him, a winged man with crossbows mounted on each arm and an armored figure who wore his hair in dreadlocks.

The wolf kept two sets of eyes on the humans floating down from the sky while one head scoped out James’s situation. He was still inside the barrier created by the other Rulers, but he didn’t appear to need any help. It looked as if they were running from him while he warped around, trying to catch them.

Then my job is to make certain that no one rescues those two from you, my Emperor, she told herself. It should be simple enough.

“I’ll take these two,” the fiery crown man said, pointing at Samuel and Luna. “You two fight the other two. Unless there’s some disagreement?”

He looked back and forth from his allies to the monsters arrayed before him, as if daring anyone to argue.

He believes he’s the strongest out of both his group and ours. It was hard to be certain he was wrong. He certainly had an intense aura. Being under his gaze was like withering in the heat of a nearby sun. It was difficult for Luna to look directly at him.

The Panther Queen moved first, leaping through the air toward the man with dreadlocks.

Mageddon and the winged man moved off to the side in the opposite direction, leaving the flame-crowned man, Samuel, and Luna, facing off in a little triangle.

“Your Emperor underestimated us,” the man with the fiery crown said. “He has more vassals than just you lot. I’ve seen your full crew at the Summit, or at least more of them than you four. He should have summoned all of them. He’ll regret that decision when he has to watch all of you fall.”

“He summoned all those he needed,” said Samuel. “He even added a margin for safety. We outnumber you, despite also being stronger than you are. My comrade here won’t even need to lend a paw.” He winked at Luna, and she understood his signal implicitly.

Hold off on fully engaging until I see an opening. Let him take the lead. Fine, but you’d better know what you’re doing, Samuel.

“Big talk.”

The man with the fiery crown fired a blast of concentrated flames straight at Samuel, but the giant alligator did not back down. It struck him full on in the chest, and although Samuel winced with the impact, he remained standing. He began to twirl the trident he carried in his front limbs, and Luna saw Earth Mana and Water Mana swirling together around it.

“Tougher than I thought,” the fiery crown wearer commented as the fiery blast dissipated.

The front of Samuel’s body was smoking where the flames had struck, but Luna could see his back. There was no exit wound. The attack had failed to penetrate.

On the other hand, the flame-crowned man wasn’t breathing heavily, which suggested he had depths of power yet to be plumbed.

“Magic resistant flesh,” Samuel replied with a cocky, toothy grin. “My turn.”

He aimed his trident at the man, and a torrent of swampwater materialized and rushed across the air.

“Far too slow,” the flame crown wearer said. He disappeared in a blinding flash and reappeared further away, off to the side from where the attack had been launched.

But he avoided it. Is water a weakness? Or earth?

The fiery crown bearer fired a blast of heat from his palm, aiming for Samuel’s head this time. The attack did exactly as little this time as the previous one, but Luna managed to get a better look at what was happening.

It’s as though parts of him vanish when he strikes. This attack was slower than the last. Why?

Samuel emerged from behind the attack, grinning, and the flaming man disappeared in an instant and reappeared just under Samuel’s head. He struck with an uppercut without losing any of the velocity he had gained with his sudden and impossible to follow movement.

The giant alligator let out a surprised grunt, his head jerked backward, and he stumbled and landed on his tail.

The flame crown wearer grinned, disappeared, and reappeared in a crater in Samuel’s chest. He had struck at the same place where his initial attack hit, and where the first attack had gouged a relatively small, black hole in Samuel’s surface, this one widened it to the size of a man.

Samuel let out a pained grunt, and Luna saw blood begin to flow from the hole in his chest.

The giant alligator swung two massive limbs at the human whose body was partly embedded in his chest, and Luna saw the trick as the man vanished this time.

I think… he turned into light.

“Far, far too slow,” the Ruler with the flaming crown gloated. “You must have grown soft living in that man’s shadow. He pointed his chin in the direction of James, who was still inside the barrier box, pursuing the other two Rulers, who looked as if they were beginning to weaken.

He’s too fast for the eye to track, Luna thought anxiously. If he can truly turn into light, how would any of us fight him? I’m sure that the Emperor would figure something out. I have a move I could use, but I need him to hold still…

“You’re just too much of a coward to take hits from one who is stronger than you,” the wolf proclaimed loudly from her central head. “Your speed is your only true advantage.”

The fiery crown wearer bared his teeth at her.

“So what if it is? It’s not something the likes of you could ever overcome. Only he is capable of offering me a decent challenge in this place. That’s the real reason I was willing to join this pitiful mob. Once James and I have our fated confrontation, I don’t care about whether he succeeds in pursuing the Holy Kings or not. Each of them is too weak as an individual to be worthy of my attention. They’re only even a problem for the Emperor because they band together to hide their weakness. But as you and the lizard there show—” he grinned with menace—“a group of weaklings together is just pathetic squared.”

Samuel thumped his trident against the ground, and mud geysers sprang forth in a circle all around the combatants.

No, not mud. It’s swamp water…

“We’ll see what this group of so-called weaklings ends up accomplishing,” Samuel said.

“I’ve wasted too much time with the two of you already,” the flame crown wearer said. He raised a hand, and Luna felt the sun above them glow more brightly, the heat beating down on them as if they were inside an oven. The swamp geysers began to thin out somewhat, as if evaporating before their eyes.

“You’re just showing me I had the right approach!” Samuel exclaimed.

He thumped his trident once more, and more swamp geysers burst forth from beneath the soil, joining the existing ones and crisscrossing overhead to form an almost complete dome, blocking nearly every route to get away from the circle where the trio was fighting—or, more accurately, where Samuel and the fiery Ruler had been tussling while Luna tried to figure out what she could do to help.

“That…” The flaming crown wearer darted his eyes from side to side, trying to look around subtly. But it was obvious to Luna’s sharp eyes.

So, the water is an actual weakness for him.

By the time he looked around, the water had cut off nearly all possible escape for the flame Ruler. Perhaps, after some minutes, he would figure out how to get away, or perhaps he would manage to evaporate the swamp water with the intense sun that still baked overhead. The water was full of Samuel’s Mana, so it might not be that easy, but it was at least a viable possibility to explore.

The alligator wasn’t going to give him that long, though.

As Luna observed the fiery crown wearer, she also saw that the curtains of water that had risen on all sides were beginning to close in. It happened slowly at first, but it accelerated so that it became unmistakable.

“Not a bad move,” the fiery Ruler admitted.

Then Samuel hurled his trident. Luna began running at the same moment. She knew where she needed to be.

The flame Ruler had responded to Samuel’s attack one way thus far.

Dodge, then counter attack.

It was his strategy. Use openings to attack quickly and get out fast, before anyone could do any damage in return. One of the best types of openings was when the opponent attacked you. They were rarely defending themselves properly at the same time they attacked. Samuel was full of openings, but he’d done that deliberately, trusting in Luna.

As the trident shot through the air toward him, the fiery crown wearer made a mock shocked expression, smiled, dodged fairly comfortably, and then disappeared again.

He reappeared—with Samuel’s left claw around one of his feet.

“You’re predictable, human,” the alligator breathed.

He swung the flaming Ruler at the ground, and the man struck headfirst and left a big dent. Samuel pulled him back and raised him to swing again, but the fiery crown shot a burst of flames that struck Samuel right in the eyes, blinding him.

“Maybe I’ve got more surprises than you think,” the human said through bared teeth. His hands went down to where Samuel still had hold of his ankle, beginning to try and pry the claws loose.

“Maybe not,” replied Luna from just behind him.

Three sets of wolf jaws latched onto his arm, lower back, and the nape of his neck.

“Ah, fuck!” the Ruler screamed.

Bite of Sealing.

In addition to the pain of a giant three-headed wolf’s fangs locked onto his body across three sections, the enemy would now be suffering from the inability to use his Mana properly.

It was Luna’s latest Skill, one which she had only acquired during the melee itself. Hunting down full-fledged Rulers, even in a setting where death was not permanent, had turned out to be quite fruitful for the wolf.

“You…”

She felt him try. He reached for his magic, attempted to burn her or dematerialize or something, but he found the place where it ought to be completely blocked from his reach.

Normally, this would be where Luna would ragdoll the man, tearing him to pieces as best she could with her three sets of jaws. At least, that was how she’d seen this going when she gained the Skill.

But she had someone else with her in this fight.

Samuel blinked his eyes open. Luna wasn’t certain if they had been safely protected behind a thick, fast-moving reptilian eyelid, or if they had simply regenerated that quickly, but based on the scorch marks still visible around his eyes, she guessed the former.

“Farewell, human,” Samuel said. He reached out with one massive paw and began to slowly crush the flame-crowned man’s throat with simple brute force.

The man’s body jerked and flailed as he lost his ability to breathe. He used his hands and feet to punch and kick at Samuel, but the giant alligator was implacable, his grip unbreakable now that he had no flames or impossibly swift movements to contend with. In a contest of sheer physical strength, there were few who could best Samuel.

The flame-wielding Ruler’s body jerked and spasmed, his face beginning to turn purple as Samuel choked the life out of him. His Fortitude was such that his throat was not crushed easily, and his neck had not snapped. But this just meant his death would be slower, more painful, than if he was weaker.

That was what Luna thought.

A shadow appeared, and she only had a split-second to warn Samuel.

“Behind you!” Luna barked, releasing her grip on the Ruler’s lower back with one of her jaws.

Samuel’s body pivoted just as the enemy struck.
