Luke lay heaving heavily, eyes hazy and barely open, while he embraced the ground below him. As the pain still affected him, the effort he had to give throughout those long days had finally led him to create a blade of energy.

“Why do I have to go through all this?” he barely mumbled while holding back tears.

Even though he had to overcome the backlash from such a convergence of energy, it was still rewarding to kill a slime like that so quickly. But the pain that he had to go through locked him in place.

Luke could barely move his body, or more like he didn’t want to set off another backlash of energy.

Overcoming one obstacle after another barely left him okay, only pushing through with whatever willpower he had left.

I need to at least be able to reach Mom. This thought was the only thing that coursed through his mind.

He tried spreading his fingers apart as they locked themselves in a bent position. Even though he couldn’t spread them out completely, he could still clench a fist, and that’s all he needed to do. Letting it fall back towards the ground, he grunted, trying to lift his upper body to at least sit upright.

Waiting to see if anything would happen, he patiently waited as the time ticked away. His world flooded with questions.

Why did I go through all that? Am I in such a rush that I would destroy my body like this? Is this the only way I could reach mother? Just how much more do I have to go through?

Chaotic thoughts mingled with whatever his desire could be. But he shoved them all to the back of his mind. But they still echoed out, trying to devour his emotions. Luke could barely contain himself.

He looked toward his soul, trying to comprehend how much damage had truly occurred. The quick moments of pain don’t account for the possibility of permanent damage to his soul. Peering inside to see his entire white soul had turned slightly grey, with one-fifth being covered in darkness.

It hadn’t gotten better, with even the blood-red veins remaining without the current use of soul energy.

He could only hope it was possible to find a solution to his current issues or any information that might not make the current situation any worse than it should be at this very moment.

Luke, at the end of the day, still needed rest after preparing himself by fighting various silver slimes in multiple ways. Trying to figure out what stuck to him the most. He barely left time for himself, and it clearly shows.

Luke scratched away at his bald head until his scalp started to bleed.

He peered down toward his bloodied hand; his breathing grew ragged. His heart quickened, his throat clenched up. He found it hard to breathe, reaching toward the pedestal. But he was out of reach; his body twitched.

Taking shallow breaths as the world closed in around him. He shut his eyes, trying to take in as much air as possible. Chills shot through his body as his chest heaved lower until it returned to its normal pattern.

Luke's eyes jolted open, looking toward his still bloodied hand. His eyes quivered. Losing focus, he could only close his eyes again. His body shook occasionally.

“Mom…I miss…you,” Luke said through his quivering lips.

Still limited to only one entire arm and a barely functional hand, he just left it all to punching for now.

Luke slowly succumbed to his own tiredness as a wave of exhaustion washed over him, leaving him there believing he wouldn’t die in the next fight.

Luke began hacking away at slimes for his morning routine, finding basic exercises a waste of time instead of actually accumulating points. But he could barely get his thoughts the moment he woke up. Hoping it's way to the front of his mind.

Am I really doing this all for my mother, or is it for myself? This one thought stood out clearly above the rest.

Without even batting an eye, he punched out towards a green slime, enduring its acidity with his bare skin. It boiled over, skin reddened as it became prickly, with the layers slowly shedding away from his fist to the point he could just peel it off. Luke didn’t try risking it anymore, exerting the strength he could muster into his arm lifting the slime up, slamming it back into the ground to release his hand from its grasp.

Only to die from the shock which echoed out through its body, destroying its core. The goop lay there oozing, bubbling the ground before it all disappeared into particles.

Luke watched with pity; every slime he killed would eventually empower him someday. Without thinking much, he continued his daily routine and ended up accumulating a massive amount of points just fighting the silver slimes, without the use of soul energy, with the state of his soul currently.

Then something clicked in his head.

Why has all I’ve been doing is killing slimes? How long must I continue this life of torturous pain? Until I clear what this tutorial is? Until I become strong enough?

What…even were these slimes…every one of them left behind a soul…weren’t they living things…where had they even come from…I’ve killed hundreds.

Then it all came back in a rush of emotions, everything he had locked within himself. All the wrath he had for this place, all the sadness associated with the slime lives he had taken, all the cruel pain he had to endure.

His mind blanked in and out; he wavered side to side, stumbling as he walked toward the pedestal. The arm that he had lost, the restore that provided him everything he needed but not the taste of food, not the feeling of being able to quench his thirst for water himself.

He couldn't remember the last time he actually felt true happiness, the last time he had fun, the last time he felt peaceful ever since he had stepped into this place. His thoughts were never true to himself; his decisions were never right. He just continued on and on without giving himself a break. All he knew was the assumptions he had.

He wanted to wipe out every fire slime that dared turn him into this state but hated the idea of taking a life.

His body felt like it wasn’t his anymore; the world twisted around him while he tried to find meaning within himself.

“I was just a normal high school student... why am I sent here…why must I continue to live this way?” Luke screamed while clawing at his own body. His stump reminded him of his missing arm. His bald head reminded him of the pain of dealing with the fire slime. His chest, which harbored the souls within him.

Then silence prevailed in an instant. His mind returned to a tranquillity that had never happened before. Luke stared down at his bleeding fingertips. Something heavy was stuck in his chest and wouldn’t come out.

Tears flowed down his eyes.

“What..have I become…what..am…I doing to myself?" He could barely let the words out as the pain in his body reminded him of his actions.

Luke rushed towards the pedestal, quickly purchasing a restore.

Even with the vitality flowing through him, his mind still harbored the intent of revenge. He still wanted to kill the fire slime that had done most of this to him. But this anger and ill intent weren’t completely his. Deep down, he just wanted a break, but something kept making that anger rise.

Luke clenched his teeth while inserting his fingers into his stump. Trying to calm himself down, but his rage only increased; he felt a burning passion that quickly replaced the deep sadness in his chest.

I..I...can’t lose…myself just yet. Luke thought, but he could barely comprehend what was happening before his vision became red; his grey soul pulsed as the color gradually grew darker.

His gaze settled toward the sky. Taking a long breath, he reached out towards the sky, drawing out its power as 4 souls appeared in his chamber.

Luke placed his hand on the pedestal, quickly restarting floor 4.

His legs stood straight; he held his head high; he didn’t flinch as the atmosphere changed around him; the heat sped up, becoming a scorching summer day.

The energies shifted, condensing into a single point. The energy revolved around it, appearing from within a mass of pure fire. The fire slime had been summoned.

Luke locked eyes with it, his eyes held firm. The distortions of heat didn’t make him stop his approach as he supercharged his soul energy.

The souls spun faster than they ever had before, with each rotation draining the energy of the souls as disbursed throughout his body. Every cell felt rejuvenated, dancing with power. Luke planted his feet into the ground. Holding his signature stance, feet shoulder-width apart, keeping legs slightly bent. Non-dominant left leg in front, right leg slightly behind. With the stance, he approached like a bullet. Shifting the gathered energy towards his legs, immediately closed the distance as his body strained to keep up with the sudden change.

A flame wall manifested itself in front of him. Pivoting his foot, he launched himself into the air, landing over the fire, appearing face to face with the fire slime. The energy coursed through his body, shifting it all from his legs to his upper body. Supplementing himself as the energy toward his fist created a layer of manifested soul energy.

That was all, as his fist barely missed the slime but continued onward to destroy one of the flame bolts.

The slime backed away, sending 2 conjured flame bolts towards his left. Luke swiftly jumped up while dodging. Landing swiftly back on the ground, he found his balance, immediately rushing back, sprinting towards the slime, closing the distance. Throwing out his fist, which grazed the slime, heat erupted in his face, charring his skin. Screams escaped Luke’s mouth as his body trembled.

Blinded, Luke tried his best to move backward, wary, trying his best to hear anything through the blistering flames.

Finding a chance to look through all the distortion, he laid his eyes on a smaller slime that was once there. Without further ado. Luke approached while staying low, tensing himself up while slowing the speed of the soul revolution when they weren’t in use. He tried his best to make way through all the flames that still lingered on the surrounding ground. The island was covered in blistering flames from the minor eruption.

Luke could only watch while scratching his stump. Standing straight up, he glared at the slime with the hatred of a thousand suns. It dared to destroy his home. Clenching his fist almost to the point of bleeding, he didn’t care anymore, darting towards the slime. Moving with unforeseen speed, his soul thumping in his core, the energy poured out like a waterfall; he was draining the soul of its very essence.

Before the slime could run away, it threw out 3 blazing arrows, barely missing on the way towards Luke.

He wasn’t worried about any injuries, only the slime in his view, trying to bring divine judgement on the slime for destroying his home. Not knowing how to restore his island, he could only protect it with his life, as it was his new home.

“DIE!” Luke said while his fist shot toward the slime, this time covered in a thicker membrane of soul energy, as if like a glove.

Luke’s fury burned while his soul energy pulsed outwards, its power outdid everything in the few days of training he went through. The energy released in an outburst of silver; his teeth clamped down on his lip, trying to keep himself conscious. His eyes rained tears of blood as the energy flooded through his body; in that one instant, the glove extended outwards into a blade that pierced the flaming slime before his very eyes. He watched with bulging eyes as the blade slid smoothly through the core, destroying it before the slime had a chance to even denotate itself. Just like that, the energy floated out into the atmosphere as it merged with the air. He could barely comprehend what was happening before his very eyes. As the flames died down, he felt a stab in his heart as if it had been pierced by a sword.

Holding his chest, he coughed repeatedly, finding it hard to breathe with whistling noises coming from every breath he took. He dropped to the ground, head held towards the ground as blood poured from his mouth. Before it could get worse, Luke rushed towards the pedestal, but it wasn’t with the speed he had before, crawling with his hand impeded into the ground. Even using the same teeth to try biting into the ground to aid him. Thankfully, with his increased strength from all those stat points he poured in, he barely had the energy to help him move to the very pedestal that kept him bound in this place. Punching towards the pedestal, he cast a heal, not even looking at the price, worried more about his life.

Laughing hysterically as his body recovered, he kept his eyes on the pedestal and slowly backed up away from it. His eyes moved around rapidly; he itched his thumb, which began to bleed. His ferocious scratching played in his ear. He exhaled, darting his eyes back toward the pedestal, taking in his full reflection. Walking back toward it, he stretched his arm towards the next floor, ready to restart floor 3 again without even waiting for his brain to register it. The energy around the stage condensed again, forming a slime that looked even less ferocious than before. The slime looked almost minuscule in his eyes, as the world around him changed color between vibrant red, orange, and yellow. His eyes went from blood red to black. He wobbled himself towards the slime, marching in a disfigured way, like his legs were broken.
