The dark smoke of my Life Drain slithered over the face of the nearest Reaper. A combination of panic and anger stretched across his features, but were soon lost in the fog. His companions wasted no time though. All three charged at me.

I Aero Shifted away from several attacks that converged on my previous location. Now behind the original Reaper who ambushed me and was now victim to my Drain, I slammed his face against the pillar. His skin sizzled against the scalding heat of the mysterious stone and he screamed like a pig being roasted alive.

I released the Life Drain and sent it after the next closest enemy, who was now coming at me with a stone suit of armor like a discount version of Jericho. His eyes and mouth were left uncovered though and my tendrils found them, sucking Health away.

A flash of light from nearby blinded me. A stabbing pain pierced my thigh and I dropped to a knee. The scythe from another Reaper stuck straight through the back of my leg and out the front.

Life Drain continued to replenish my Health, even as the Reapers chipped away at it. The pain remained. Each little cut or bruise hacked away at my morale. My tolerance. I Aero Shifted in short bursts, always on the move.

The first Reaper, who I'd burned against the pillar, was out of commission—not phased out, but laying unconscious on the frozen ground. The Stone Affinity Reaper had given up on the armor and was launching boulders at me. They moved slow enough that I could dodge, but the Light Reaper was working with him, obscuring the boulders with glares and shrouds. I took one to the shoulder after misjudging the trajectory, Shifting to the wrong spot.

The Light Reaper wasn't doing any damage, but she was the most dangerous, helping the other two remaining foes connect on their strikes and keeping me distracted.

I needed to get rid of her. And I had just the thing.

My Volition was still near half. I silently thanked the Reapers who'd defended the city in my absence and left all those Wills to be claimed. Without the boosts I'd gained, I may already be dead.

I ducked beneath an arc of flame—the last Reaper finally joining the fight—and Aero Shifted farther away from the melee. Several feet behind the Light Reaper. I summoned my new Prismatic Bow and took aim.

The arrow flew straight into the Reaper's back. I fired two more before she could even turn around. The arrows were more like fragile spikes that metal shafts or beams of light. They shattered into thousands of geometric shards when they hit, which then embedded themselves around the point of impact dealing additional damage.

She fell forward, but her cloak swirled around her, sending her back to Vitria before she even hit the ground.

Two remaining.

The Flame Reaper, who'd been on the outskirts most of the fight looked at the spot his companion had been, then to the first Reaper still unconscious by the pillar. He took out an item. I braced myself, but instead of attacking, he vanished in a puff of purple smoke.

Okay… One remaining then.

The Stone Reaper was back in his armor. It must have been a huge Volition sink, because he only used it intermittently. It was effective now though, deflecting my prismatic arrows for minimal damage as I fired a volley in quick succession. He charged and I switched tactics, returning to Life Drain.

My Volition was getting low now though. Summoning the bow had cost nothing, but each subsequent arrow unleashed took a small chunk away. More than I would've liked. I should've paid more attention and not shot so many once he put up the armor. Stupid.

Suddenly, my Life Drain smoke trail dissipated. I checked my Volition again. Still enough. Then what—

The Stone Reaper let out a muffled roar as he barreled at me. He'd closed the stone around his face, running blindly. So that's why Life Drain ended. He'd blocked any hole it could get to.

I side-stepped the charge, not wasting any Volition to Shift away from a blind attack. But then I was tumbling through the air backwards. I rolled on the cobblestone, me chest throbbing. I got to a knee, clutching my ribcage. Definitely a few broken bones in there.

How the hell had—

He charged at me again, still full enclosed in his armor. This time I Aero Shifted, wincing at the pain as my body wrenched through the air and away from my enemy. When I touched down again, somehow the Reaper knew exactly where I was and turned toward me. He must have had some skill that allowed him to detect vibrations in the ground to locate me.

Well if he was gonna try to blind bandit me, I'd have to pull a twinkletoes on him.

As he approached with another charge, I braced myself. Then I launched straight up, Aero Shifting as high as I dared. At the apex, I turned to face down, watching the Stone Reaper, praying this plan would work. He had to be getting low on Volition too.

I was nearly back down to the ground when he finally lowered the stone helmet around his head. He didn't even think to look up. I came down directly on him, scythe blade spearing the top of his head, down through the hole for his neck, and into his chest.

My scythe pulsed.

Pulling something in…

No…

It pulsed again, seething in power.

I hit his Will. I felt my scythe trying to draw the Will out. Panicked, I ripped my blade from his body and threw it across the road. With Second Sight activated, I stared into his chest. The Will was cracked, a stream leaking out, still seeking my blade. But then the Reaper's cloak spun around him and he vanished.

I collapsed to the cobblestones, lungs heaving.

Fuck. That was too close.

I didn't know what would happen if my scythe fully consumed his Will. Well, no. I did know. I'd seen the Reapers working for Pirhani kill and harvest Wills without consequence. In all likelihood a perk of their oath to the goddess. I wouldn't be so fortunate. Marked for death, hunted like a Sin.

Maybe Valciferous could pull some sort of string to rescue me, but he was a little preoccupied at the moment and I couldn't afford to risk it.

I stood up, still somewhat in shock that I was alive. Defeating four opponents—well, three-and-a-half (I wouldn't give myself full credit for the coward who ran away)— on my own was no small feat.

Of course, after spending all that time harvesting Wills and replenishing my Volition, it was already low again. Unfortunately, relinquishing multiple Wills had no added benefit to my Volition capacity. One filled it completely and any excess was wasted. If I was smart, I would've relinquished a few and saved some for later knowing I had more battles to come. I didn't think I could withhold them once they were in my scythe and I activated the pillar, but I could've at least hidden a few in convenient places to harvest later. Curse you and your severe lack of forethought, past Zephyr…

Luckily, it didn't take long wandering the streets I hadn't travelled yet to find two more dormant Amber Wills. I hid one and brought the other back to the pillar where I relinquished it as fast as humanly possible to avoid getting blindsided by another ambush.

That accomplished, I turned back to the lone standing tower, now more confident than ever that Pirhani was somewhere within. The base was a church-like structure, but the towering steeple rose above, a thin needle in the sky, probably wide enough for a single staircase. I thought about where I'd hole up to observe my conquest if I was a god… The answer was obvious.

At the top.

There'd probably be a lot of Reapers and Unchained between here and there. And apparently they'd bring me right to her if I simply asked.

But I had no intention of arriving on her terms. Or dealing with her minions.

The highest window of the tower beckoned.

I'd never Aero Shifted that far. Didn't even know if I could. How accurate did I have to be? And it'd take a decent chunk of Volition, but far less than the alternative of repeating the battle I just barely survived countless times over.

"Ah, fuck it." There wasn't really much of a choice.

Once I snuck close enough that I felt more comfortable with the jump, but would still avoid notice, I readied myself, drawing my prismatic bow. Notched an arrow. Took aim at the window.

Then I Aero Shifted.
