He had fortitude though, his pain and his guilt had steeled his determination, to the point of being suicidal even. And so, he stabilized his state, his tension dissipated, and he recited the chant of the rite and prepared his first step towards the Whispered path. Soon the air hummed around him, matching the timbre of his chants. The more layers he painted, the more he chanted, the louder the buzz got, and the four types of Anima Ewan could sense surged in his premise.
