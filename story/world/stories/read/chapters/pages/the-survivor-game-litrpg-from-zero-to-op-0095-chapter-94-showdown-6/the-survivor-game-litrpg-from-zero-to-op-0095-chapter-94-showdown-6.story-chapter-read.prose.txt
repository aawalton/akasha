Marshmallow watched in horror as the Dragon Goat collapsed under BloodLust's relentless assault. His companion, who had stuck by his side through thick and thin in the Survivor Game, now lay motionless on the shattered white marble ground, scales dull and stained with blood. Marshmallow wanted to rush over and check on him, but before he could even cover half the distance, BloodLust was on him like he'd teleported. A cruel smirk twisted his face as he prepared to strike."Too slow, Marshmallow," BloodLust hissed. The impact was so violent that Marshmallow felt the air leave his lungs. He coughed up blood, a metallic taste filling his mouth.Before he could even think about defending himself, a devastating kick to his side sent him flying. Marshmallow's body bounced across the ground several times before rolling to a stop. When he finally came to a halt, every fiber of his being was screaming in agony."Is that all you've got?" BloodLust taunted. "I thought you were tougher than this. Maybe I was wrong about you. Maybe you're not the opponent I hoped for."Marshmallow desperately tried to get back up, but his body seemed to struggle to respond to his commands. The pain was blinding, and he felt like every bone in his body had been shattered and then put back together wrong. Despite wearing the Primordial Dragon Armor, he felt more vulnerable than ever.The reason for this apparent weakness soon became clear. The armor, once indestructible, now showed thin cracks spreading like spiderwebs across its surface. BloodLust had found a way to corrupt the very essence of the armor. Each blow not only damaged Marshmallow's body but also the protection he had relied on."Wondering why your precious armor isn't protecting you, huh?" BloodLust showed no sign of pain."Water against fire," BloodLust said with a sadistic smile. "A classic, don't you think?"BloodLust then lunged at Parabellum. "It can't... it can't end like this!"But it was already too late. In a matter of seconds, Parabellum's fiery body returned to its original form. A stomp straight to the chest sent him flying next to the others, probably fracturing a good part of his rib cage. Lynx, connected as she was to the Tree, felt every vibration, every crack forming in the vital wood.
