Gwyn’s Secret looked more like a shop now than it did the last time Alric visited, although last time it didn’t even have a sign.

The new sign had gone up; it said Gwyn’s Secret, although Alric’s mind kept warping it. There were drawings of flowers and scissors. Alric shrugged, thinking it must mean something to someone. All the shutters had now been barred and painted black.

“What do you all think?” Alric asked his guards, pointing to it. They glanced around and shrugged.

“Looks expensive, maybe?” one of the guards said, pointing to the shutters.

“Because the shutters are closed?” Alric asked with a raised brow.

The guard shook his head. “Cause they're black, and you can’t see in.” The guard said, pointing.

Alric stood and made a “Huh” noise. When no one else added anything, he shrugged and reached for the door. The guards took up positions outside.

Knowing the rules of this place, he closed the door immediately; a new curtain had been added to enter the shop proper. He thought that was smart and entered properly.

A plain blue dress on a wooden frame was the first thing he saw; its pop of color grabbed his attention. Turning, he saw that a lot of work had been done. One wall was a cloth display with pigeonholes filled with folded cloth of different colors. Bolts lay next to that, leaning against it, but mostly plain colors.

Gwyn was at the large worktable at the back of the store. She held something and approached Alric. Molly walked next to her as well, the other woman Brannick had hired as an arbalist. Both wore long cloaks fastened at the neck, although neither had a sword belt or anything else that Alric would normally associate with guards.

Gwyn smiled. “Master Alric, good to see you. Is Vale not with you?”

Alric nodded, smiling as he looked around. “No, Vale is doing brewery collections, but uh… Good to see you too, but uhm?” Alric said, finishing by pointing at Molly and the other guard. That was the second time she had asked after Vale, he noted.

“Oh, they're both wearing the new underwear,” Gwyn said with a smile. She turned to the pair, “Do you guys want—”

“No, no, no, no.” Alric said quickly, his hands stretched out in front of him and shaking his head, “I saw far more of my guards than I ever wanted at the bathhouse, thanks.”

Both guards reached up to the ties at their necks but stopped at his reaction.

“No? But then you can’t see how it fits?” Gwyn asked, tilting her head at Alric.

“No. I leave this one to you to figure out, Gwyn.” Alric said, now folding his arms.

“Mister Alric, don’t be a prude. We need—” Gwyn began, a frown forming.

“Absolutely not, Gwyn. I will probably work with those two every day for years. It’s not something we're going to do.” Alric shook his head again. Gwyn pouted. When she didn’t say anything more, Alric continued. “I helped you figure out the idea. You need to make it work. You are the expert now.”

Gwyn’s pout intensified.

“Don’t pout at me, you are the expert. This is your shop after all.” Alric said with a small grin.

“I wasn’t pouting. But fine.” Gwyn said, her head tilting upward and taking a deep breath.

Molly cleared her throat. Alric turned towards her. Her expression seemed serious, but Alric could sense something to it.

“Is it really so bad, Mister Alric?” Molly asked, tilting her head to the side. “You have free rein to look at women in underwear, and you are complaining.”

Alric stared at her, but his expression went very flat. Molly lowered her head, but her shoulders were shaking; the other guard developed a very sudden interest in the other cloth.

“Thank you, Molly.” Alric said after a considerable pause, “I can be uncomfortable with something, you know. Cloaks stay on.”

“Your loss, Mister Alric,” Molly said, still grinning at the floor.

Gwyn let out a deep sigh. “Then I can describe it?”

Alric nodded. “Sure.”

“The new ones are much tighter on the chest and sides,” Gwyn said, passing him a sheet of paper. “It works a lot better.”

Alric studied the paper; it was a much clearer version of the pattern they made before. It did have many charcoal marks on it, though.

“They can run in them comfortably and spar,” Gwyn said, gesturing to the guards. Alric looked up at them.

Molly nodded at Gwyn and added, “Jumping too. Brannick had us running up stairs. I may have considered shooting him.”

“Sounds like Brannick.” Alric nodded along.

“The difference is… It’s hard to find the words. It’s tighter, yes, but I can breathe, twist, and move.”

“That was the plan from the start.” Alric said, looking back at the pattern, “I don’t imagine adventurers want much bouncing.”

Molly made a small sound. Alric looked up slowly, his mouth to the side. Molly held a fist to her mouth and bit her finger.

“Do you want to add something to that, Molly?” Alric asked, tilting his head. She shook her head vigorously. “Great.”

Even Gwyn was struggling to hold back a grin now. She then showed Alric how she had adjusted the shoulders and made other parts thicker. Alric nodded along; the different parts all made sense.

“See, I wouldn’t have come up with this. You don’t need me for this.” Alric said, handing over the pattern. “I’m guessing this is taking a long time, though?”

Gwyn’s smile faded, her shoulders dropped slightly, and she looked downward, her mouth pulled to the side. “Yes. Each time we change something, there is another fitting, and then they need to train again.”

“Ahh, so these two are why it looks empty?” Alric asked, raising both brows.

Molly opened her mouth incredulously. “We are helping!” She said, which made Alric chuckle. When Gwyn saw him chuckle, she straightened a bit more.

“You aren’t opening next week, though?” Alric asked.

“I still want to,” Gwyn said. She glanced at the blue dress. “I can make ordinary pieces when the shop is open. I wanted to finish this first, though.”

Alric frowned. While the guards were probably the best bet for testing movement, most customers weren't going to spend several hours running around with crossbows to see how their underwear performed.

The shop and displays are too plain, though. Nothing here shows a point of difference.

He let out a sigh, glancing at the blue dress and trying to figure out how to deliver the bad news.

Turning to Gwyn, he gave her a sympathetic look; her shoulders dropped slightly at his expression. “You have a problem,” Alric said, tilting his head a bit.

Gwyn frowned and glanced at the floor for a moment.

“Here’s the issue. You are competing with people who have been tailoring for decades, right?” Alric gestured to her; she nodded. “You are making dresses that are nearly the same as everyone else’s, but you don’t know wealthy merchants, or nobles, or who buys and sells what.”

“But that all takes time?” Gwyn asked, frowning.

“It does, but if I went outside with that dress style, how many would I see like it?” Alric pointed to it.

Gwyn glanced at it and back at him. “But it’s about the craftsmanship? And the stitching?”

“Maybe, but none of it shows you. You. The underwear patterns are going to get out. We know this, but then you have little to fall back on.” Alric explained, trying to keep his tone gentle.

Gwyn drooped further. Alric felt for her; he really did. “I think you stay closed for about three more weeks while we do something fresh,” Alric said with a smile.

That perked her up. “Fresh?”

Nearby, Molly and the other guard snorted; Alric grinned too. “You should be designing women's clothes that work around your underwear.”

Gwyn seemed to rock to and fro while she thought about it. “Why would anyone want clothing that looks… weird?”

Alric grinned. “Good question. So you do something that is fresh and new but still stylish.” He paused, gauging their reaction, before he continued, “You can’t make the same dress as the other tailors because people go to the tailor they already know. Also, your shop is a women’s-only shop, so you are already strange, so just… lean into it.”

Gwyn rocked a bit more; Alric realized it would be easier to just show her.

“Come on, let's do some drawing,” Alric said, gesturing to the workbench at the back.

Gwyn followed along. Molly was hot on her heels.

Alric felt a small twinge of guilt; Earth spent far too much time and money making fantasy stuff look more fantasy than this actual fantasy world, and he was about to claim credit for their work.

People here wore tunics, gowns, and practical clothing. If the world was going to be disappointing about the matter, he could at least steal from people who had imagined better versions.

He started by thinking about cosplay. Any cosplay elf outfit had to have some kind of cute skirt, maybe cut at an angle. It would absolutely have shoulder straps and then off-the-shoulder sleeves. Using a little thread on the inside could shorten the sleeves and make them a bit poofy; he was sure that was a professional term, so he didn’t say it out loud. He drew that, but he lowered the neckline.

He passed that over to Gwyn, whose eyes widened; she immediately began redrawing it. So, the drawing became workable.

Alric had just started drawing a polo neck sweater when he heard Molly gasp, “That’s scandalous.”

Alric glanced over at her with a flat stare again. “This is coming from ‘look at me in my underwear’? The idea is to design clothing that works with the new underwear.” Alric concluded.

Molly, however, blushed properly, all the way to her ears. The other guard had to walk away, biting her fist. Molly’s shoulders were shaking again.

Gwyn, however, was glaring at the drawing. “So if the straps go here, I don’t have to cover the shoulders?” She said to no one in particular.

“That’s right. Design around the underwear.” Alric said, turning to draw the polo-neck.

Eventually, they had several designs. A pleated skirt that was angled and had layered cloth. The polo-neck, a blouse, the top he considered Elven, they incorporated a thick cloth belt that could go with any of the designs and come in different colors.

“I don’t understand this one. It looks like noble clothing.” Gwyn said, pointing to the layered skirt, “It uses so much cloth.”

Alric just shrugged. “It has one extra layer. It doesn’t have to be cheap, it can also just be a display to show what you can do.” Alric explained.

Nearby, the two guards were inspecting the drawings; however, “Oh, this is so cute.” The other guard said to Molly. Molly was talking about the polo neck. Gwyn glanced at the pair, slightly annoyed.

“I think I understand now,” Gwyn said, crossing her arms. Her gaze flicked between the drawings and Alric.

“Well, I leave it to you then. You should explore this more, though.” Alric said, gesturing to her. Gwyn took a deep breath, but Alric could feel the resolve; he grinned. “I guess that’s all from me.”

“No, wait. You must know others?” Gwyn said, looking at him in alarm.

“Maybe, but they should be yours. This is plenty for now.” Alric said, gesturing to the drawings.

Alric took his leave shortly after. The three girls let out a deep breath. The other guard turned to Molly. “I can’t believe you tried with the boss.” And immediately started chuckling.

Molly grinned and held out her hands in a helpless gesture. “You miss every shot you don’t take.”

Gwyn grinned. “He said no very fast, though.”

Molly opened her mouth at Gwyn and then closed it before she grinned again. “I don’t think I’ve been rejected that quickly, but enough about that. What’s this about the clerk?” Molly said with a smile.

Gwyn’s eyes widened, and she blushed. That prompted more chuckles all round.
