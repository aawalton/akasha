Ashton focused on the crowd that was starting to storm the cathedral. They came in here like they knew what was going on, with an aggression like their only purpose was to try and defend Jonah.

They almost seemed like a crowd of mindless zombies. Not just with the way they didn't seem to have much light behind their eyes, but also because their bodies were practically in decay. Some were clearly limping, and the smell of both old and fresh blood filled the room. It wasn't Jonah's; that man did not bleed anymore. His body simply crumbled away into nothingness.

That blood came from all those people. He hadn't realised how bad a state they were clearly in when they when Ashton had checked this place out during the service. It looked like they fell victim to some monsters out on the street and barely made it out.

"What the fuck?" Ashton scoffed out in disbelief. Was this Jonah's idea of what it meant to show faith? Of what it meant to be a filial believer of a greater being? "You're way too much like them, after all."

As the cultists came closer, Ashton's first response was to jump. He boosted himself with the sylph's air magic, and created a place to hold onto with his hand in one of the pillars. He wasn't exactly 'safe' up here, but he could have a moment to figure out exactly what was going on.

Ashton tried his best to focus on them, when Jonah started to speak.

"My children! My dear, beloved flock! Come to my side, for there is a devil in our midst!" Jonah yelled, his voice booming. With every word, the people that heard him seemed to flinch, as if they were minutely adjusting their next action.

It was like they only existed to do what Jonah wanted them to do, and barely had a mind on their own anymore.

What kind of item was that tome? It was charged up through Jonah's pain, and then that power was used to control others? It must be related to the type of 'faith' that Jonah was constructing here. If his 'religion' was built around sacrificial pain, then that slab-like tome was gathering that pain in its entirety and using it to brainwash the believers even more.

Though it was much more than that. If Ashton hadn't resisted against the tome's magic, he would have probably begun to feel some sort of attachment to Jonah as well. Magic that could affect one's mind was powerful like that, and could take even the most minute ounce of sympathy and turn it into true loyalty, as long as the spell was powerful enough.

So that was how Jonah managed to turn these people into his cultists so immediately. Ashton heard the stories of people trying to interact with those from the Cathedral camp, and they were always clearly fanatical interactions where the cultists tried to convert those around them, like that was their only goal.

But if that tome was the culprit, and these people weren't acting out of their own free will, then he couldn't just go and fight them all.

"Get down here!"

"You sinner!"

"Freak!"

"Face your punishment, coward!"

Ashton listened to the crowd. Well... maybe breaking a couple more bones or giving a few extra bruises wouldn't be that big a deal.

The Backpacker had come to him, sitting on Ashton's shoulders. It was an awkward position, but also the only place for the spirit to safely appear. They gave Ashton a branch, and then disappeared again. He held the branch in his hand while one of the Helping Hands held his own tome for him, and quickly summoned the lesser dryad. The plant elemental inhabited the branch and slithered around Ashton's arm, finding a cosy spot in the gaps of his body.

The people were starting to throw things at Ashton. Stones, bibles, their shoes; some were trying to throw weapons at him. But none of them seemed to have throwing skills, so they were easy enough to just deflect with the sylph's gust.

And then, Ashton returned the favour, and threw something back at them. A few seeds. Using the dryad's magic, Ashton made the seeds sprout before they even landed on the ground.

The dryad coiled around his outstretched arm, as Ashton constructed a spell circle in the air. There was a spell here that should be useful. And if he could channel the dryad's magic through the spell circle, then it should be boosted even further.

The spell was simple. Technically it was an altered enchantment, which was used to boost the growth speed of plants. It wasn't a particularly useful enchantment on its own in most scenarios, since it was almost impossible to control the growth. So, it was often just a small part of a larger array. In this case, though, Ashton took the structure from the enchantment and turned it into a spell, as he had done with the frost enchantment before.

The dryad's magic was strengthened considerably, and the seeds that had just barely sprouted a moment ago immediately took root. Said roots took hold of everyone's clothes and dug through the fabrics, wrapping themselves around their legs. The roots were tied together and knotted themselves up, continuing to grow and tangle up everyone that stood below the pillar.

A few people seemed to have taken notice and had stepped away, barely avoiding being trapped by the wild roots that still would not stop growing no matter what they tried.

Whether they attempted to rip the roots off of them, or jump away, once the roots had taken a hold they didn't let go.

Right now, the dryad's level was too low to make this happen naturally, so the help of that spell was needed to capture this many people at once. But when it reached past level 10, it should be able to get at least pretty damn close.

The only annoying part was that the plants would wither almost immediately when the magic was released, so Ashton would have to use quite a bit of mana on 'upkeep' of these plants as he continued to fight Jonah.

Ashton leapt down from the side of the pillar and used the sylph to push himself further through the air. He was aiming straight for that thing using a man's name, and was going to strike down at him, but... someone stood in the way.

Clementine blocked Jonah. Or rather, she was trying to block Ashton from getting to Jonah. She was affected by that tome's magic. Usually, Ashton may have stopped and tried to get her to break free from the control, but he didn't really think he could do that. He didn't have a lot of time to spare, and he didn't even know how that magic worked and if he could stop it from affecting his friend.

But most importantly, there was no going back. Jonah had to die, and Ashton wasn't going to let him survive past tonight. If he made it to the next quest, he would receive some protections as the leader of this camp. They weren't impossible to circumvent, but Ashton would rather not have to deal with that. Plus, it would probably doom all the people here, who were already just Jonah's victims anyway. Ashton wanted to avoid hurting them even more, when everyone had to work together to struggle through this new world.

And so, Ashton twisted the stone staff in his hand and used the sylph to readjust his position. Instead of striking at Jonah, he pushed the staff around Clementine's back, trying to do so as gently as he could, and shoved her out of the way.

"I'm sorry," Ashton whispered, as he struck down at Jonah again. He hit the man's head and caused him to stumble backward, which Ashton just added onto with a gust from the sylph.

As Jonah still struggled to stand upright, especially now that he was unable to properly balance himself with his arm just loosely swinging beside him, Ashton struck at that man again and again. He was able to pull some of the staffs that the gnome had prepared beforehand toward him when the one he was using broke, but at some point, some of the remaining cultists started grabbing at them. Others tried to run at Ashton, and that included even Clementine, but he was keeping them away using fire. He combined the salamander's flames with the sylph's gusts, creating a wall of fire that they were instinctively stopped from approaching.

The wisp shot around Ashton and Jonah, hitting the god-kin Marked in tune with Ashton's attacks. His body continued breaking apart, and the stone covering that massive body was really crumbling away. It was thicker, more like liquid gold rather than real blood.

"You-You're making a mistake!" Jonah yelled out. "I'm trying to save people! Just because you disagree with my methods, does that mean that I am a villain?! Does that mean that I–" Jonah groaned loudly as Ashton's staff hit his jaw.

"Save it for the gods that will come and collect your soul," Ashton sighed. He struck and struck at Jonah, and as the Marked's defences were slowly reaching their end, Ashton pushed back against him one more time. He pushed him down to the ground, his staff against Jonah's throat. Clearly he didn't seem to be in pain, but he was panicking. He was scared. As he should be. "This is your last chance, Jonah. Say it. Say something, anything, that will make this all easier for your child. The child that you were supposed to love, and cherish, and care for to the fullest degree. The child that you raised your hand against time and time again."

Jonah laughed, blood gathering in his mouth. He spat some of it up, hitting Ashton's face. It was hot, enough to cause burns on a normal, 'level 0' human.

"What do you know... about raising a child?"

Ashton scoffed. "More than you. I've raised a child, and cared for many others on and off. I'm taking care of another right now."

Clicking his tongue, Jonah shook his head. "You don't understand anything about life! Being stuck here, having to work under those nonsensical, worthless heretics, having to watch the people love them! Do you not understand the power... the power that a man like that holds?" Jonah asked, glaring up at Ashton. "I–I can share it with you! You can rule my flock beside me, you can make us even stronger! I promise, you can do whatever you want with them! You can have any women you want! Or–or men, even! You're one of those queers, right?"

Letting out a laugh, Ashton glared at a man that he knew he despised with every fibre of his being. "Go fuck yourself, mate. Just tell me honestly. Do you love your daughter?"

Jonah stared up at Ashton. "Her? Is this what this is about? Hey! You can keep her! I promise, I won't touch her ever again, she's all yours! You saw that my sermon worked on her, right? I can tell her–tell her to worship you, and you alone! She will kiss your feet! Just please don't–"

Ashton clicked his tongue as he struck down again. Underneath the screaming that filled the space, a dull thud sounded out.

[You have killed a [Marked Human]. For your efforts, you are awarded 53 Coins]

Ashton reached out to the side, and was handed a piece of cloth by the Backpacker, throwing it onto Jonah's face, or what was left on it.

He dissolved the wall of flames that surrounded him, and the moment he did, the wailing of dozens of people the church.

And most prominent voice amongst them was Clementine.
