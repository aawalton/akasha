“Indeed. I can.”

This withered tree, as if injected with the essence of life itself, recovered quickly and began growing in size.

From the messages they heard, the first monster wave would arrive a week after Aionthra noticed their presence. They didn’t know how strong the Monster Wave was, or if it became stronger with time. Even then, it was not something they could lightly ignore with a Level 50 World Tree to protect.

Aurek began giving orders. “Spread out and plant Rune and Array traps. Also, set up a simple defensive array around the Willow World Tree if possible, but do not prioritize it. After all, we have the external barrier Lady Willow World Tree is maintaining.”

It would be a bit of a pity to consume the barrier’s limited time. However, they couldn’t consume all the resources they’ve carried with them before even seeing a shadow of the BOSS Monster, could they?

Even when space bags with larger storage capacity were available, the storage space inside was not that extensive. After all, the technology to cram mini dimensions in items that could be closed was not simple.

Thanks to the new advancements in Rune and Array fields due to Liu Shu’s evolved blessings, they managed to create something like a space bag that amplified inner space by around 10 times.

In short, the bags each of them carried had much greater storage capacity than they appeared to have on the outside.

So, everyone got moving for a few hours and then took a nap to recover some energy. They were quite exhausted after fighting the Aionthra’s Echo.

Meanwhile, Aionthra glanced at the large World Tree that she needed to defeat and smiled faintly. “Alright, my children… Go tear them apart.”

Deep within the forest, several low and menacing roars burst forth, shaking the sky.

Those on guard duty moved quickly, waking everyone. The Willow World Tree spoke as well. “Soldiers, wake up. A new challenge faces us.”

Aurek and the rest noticed a large group of 100 fully armored humanoids appearing. Due to the thick armor, whether they were female or male couldn’t really be told at a glance.

These 100 soldiers split into four groups, each led by someone with golden armor. Then, the four groups stopped at the four entrances of the city.

Aurek and the others, seeing their helpers, didn’t feel a single moment of relief. If the <Dungeon> was giving them such powerful helpers, the reason was more than clear. The monster waves were that powerful.

The Willow World Tree’s city was not too large, and between the walls and the forest, there was around one kilometer of nothingness. The barrier was exactly behind the walls of the city, so to damage the barrier, the monster wave would need to destroy the walls first.

This knowledge relieved Aurek. ‘If we couldn’t even use the walls as defense, it would be stupid.’

While he was thinking, a scout shouted. “The monsters are here!”

Everyone glanced over, and they noticed around 200 monsters rushing out of the clearing. These 200 monsters were led by two Mini BOSS monsters, one of which was the <Echo Crystal Tiger> they defeated before, and the other was a <Echo Crystal Bear>.

The tiger was already large, but the bear was at least twice as large and bulky. Of course, in terms of overall power, both were very similar.

Aurek pulled his bow’s string and pointed it up. “<Sky Arrow Rain>.”

BANG!

The arrow shook its surroundings after being released and reached the clouds above the 200 monsters. Then, from the sky, a torrent of arrows descended like shooting stars.

Explosive sounds covered the charging beast army, making them roar in pain. In Aurek’s eyes, messages of defeating beasts appeared three times. He frowned. ‘Only three deaths?’

He was, after all, a bona fide Level 200 powerhouse. These Level 199 beasts should’ve easily fallen to his attacks.

Their speed was swift, so even when Aurek blocked their way, they had already covered half the distance between the forest and the city walls. Other than Aurek, the rest of his group was not idle. Those at the other gates rushed over, while the 25 knights at this door unsheathed their wooden swords.

Of course, the wood they were made out of was not normal. Woodcrafting ensured that these swords could cut through iron by their weight alone. Other enhancements were applied to them as well, showing that neither the armor nor the weapons were normal.

They were good enough that some people in their group felt tempted to get them.

A second later, a barrage of Aethermancy and Soulmancy burst toward the charging beast. The bear at the front roared lowly, a mass of purple crystal manifesting around its body.

Then, with a powerful leap, the bear took all the damaging effects and pushed forward.

The golden armored soldier spoke, his voice low and solemn. “For Lady Willow World Tree!”

“For Lady Willow World Tree!”

The soldiers repeated, and all of them pushed forward together with Kaelen and the other melee fighters present.

BANG! BANG! BANG!

Collisions occurred everywhere as the knights and beasts entangled in a head-on battle. Aurek quickly analyzed while releasing another arrow. ‘The knights are really strong. Even without us, defeating these 200 beasts should have been possible with only a few casualties.’

So, with their added power, the battle was basically effortless.

‘This is not right…’

Aurek’s eyes widened and quickly turned. “Healers, follow me! Kaelen, go to the north gate! Riven, guide a group ot the South gate!”
