Chapter V.XXIX (5.29) - A Historical Confrontation

The curtain swung closed as Kizu dashed away from the window. He needed to get out of this tower yesterday. As it was, he was trapped in a complete dead end and cornered if any professor tracked him down. And, judging by that nasty smile, it seemed Arclight had her sights on him as her next victim.

He sprinted down the astronomy tower’s stairs, taking three steps at a time.

Something slammed into the tower, shaking the building’s foundation for the third time since this challenge’s start. This time Kizu barely stumbled as he caught himself on the wall and his monster leg took a chunk out of the stone step.

“Kaga Kizu!” Arclight’s voice boomed from above. “I know I saw you up here! Don’t tell me you’re hiding from me! Come out! Let us battle!”

Kizu continued to run.

He felt each of the combat professor’s heavy footsteps as she propelled herself down the stairs in pursuit.

Nearly at the bottom of the tower, Kizu quickly checked in with Mort.

Mort was clung to the roots of a plant in an underground tunnel. A little ways away, Weston was buried waist-deep in the dirt with a blue hand print on his face.

Kizu immediately feared that he was currently pincered between Arclight and her husband, Oasaji. The elemental professor would be a nightmare to face off against. Perhaps even more terrifying than his wife.

When Kizu reached the bottom of the stairs. He had two choices. Either follow Weston’s footsteps down through the hatch that led into the tunnels beneath the academy, or out the door that led into the open air of the academy, leaving himself completely exposed.

One step towards the door. Then Kizu blinked and saw the visage of a man through Mort’s eyes.

A man wearing a tweed jacket and reading a book.

Professor Krimpit. Not Professor Oasaji.

Kizu’s mind flashed back to the one time Krimpit had showcased his magic, back during Necro’s attack on the academy. During that fight, the professor had used earth elemental spells and mentioned their utility for excavating historical sites.

It made perfect sense how Weston might fall to Krimpit’s skills and end up half buried in the dirt.

In a split second decision, Kizu threw open the tower’s door leading into the courtyard, but then dove into the hatch leading to the tunnels below. With luck, Arclight would think he’d gone through the open door. She might even trust Krimpit to handle those under the academy. She’d have plenty of other students still to hunt.

Maybe he was putting himself into an even worse situation than before, but it was a gamble he was willing to take at the moment.

He created layers of thin spatial barriers to crash through and soften his impact to the tunnel ground.

His initial plan was to run off in the opposite direction of Krimpit and use Mort to track the professor’s movements. But the moment he touched the tunnel ground, Mort saw Krimpit snap his book closed and frown in Kizu’s direction.

Kizu slightly cursed himself and immediately created a spatial barrier under his feet to keep him from contact with the dirt ground. Krimpit specialized in excavations, of course he noticed a disturbance in the earth when Kizu made contact.

Kizu crouched and let his barrier carry him forward, over the ground of the tunnel. It used a significant amount of blood to carry his weight while moving and, after half a minute of travel, Kizu gave up. Krimpit was moving in his direction. The damage was done. And he’d move faster by foot.

In pursuit of Krimpit, Mort swooped and leaped between the overhanging roots. If the professor noticed the familiar, he showed no sign of it as he strolled in Kizu’s direction.

Dashing through the tunnels, his monster leg leaving craters in the dirt with every powerful step, Kizu nearly reached the crossroads. All he’d need to do was round the corner and stay ahead of the professor.

Sadly, it simply wasn’t meant to be. Ten meters away, Krimpit stepped into the center of the intersection. The drably dressed man pushed his spectacles up with a wrist and the ground under Kizu’s feet gave way, as if transformed into loose sand.

But Kizu had been expecting as much. He raised a hand and caught himself on a barrier, then pulled himself up. The dirt grappled him and attempted to seal him in place, but Kizu let his boot slip off.

The roiling earth underfoot battered at the barrier but Kizu was safe. At least for the moment.

“Kaga Kizu.” The name dripped with disdain from Professor Krimpit’s lips. “You trample through my territory with the grace of a stampede of beasts.”

“I figured you were the safer alternative than facing Arclight,” Kizu said. If he could get Krimpit to go off on a rant, then that would be seconds or even minutes where he continued to be an active player in this challenge.

“You are one of the few students who has borne witness to my capabilities,” Krimpit said. “Am I as powerful as the combat professor? No. Not in an arena. However, historically, most battles have not been fought in arenas.” Krimpit raised two fingers. Walls of earth slammed up, blocking off all four passageways. “You foolishly decided to face me in a place surrounded by that material that empowers me.”

“What makes you think I decided to face you?” Kizu asked. He was eyeing the walls of stone. With his jumping locked down for the moment, he had precious few means in which to break through. “I’m just fleeing from Arclight.” “Your monkey familiar has been following me ever since I dispatched the Tainted student. Now, enough of this prattle, two more students just stepped into my domain on the south end of campus.”

The ceiling collapsed overhead and Kizu barely had time to create a barrier over himself. Then the tunnel’s rocky walls burst apart and slammed into newly formed barriers that Kizu managed to form just in time. In less than two seconds, Kizu was boxed in with barriers. The only side not being crushed by earth was in the direction of Professor Krimpit.

The professor raised one hand and a spear of earth materialized over his open palm. Longer than his arm and thin as a needle. Only at that moment did Kizu recognize the attack. Krimpit had used these spears against Necro… while behind Kizu’s barrier.

Zip! The spear shot in Kizu’s direction.

Kizu slammed himself to the side of his barrier box and the needle of earth pierced through and ripped a gash in his uniform pants. It exited the other side of the box and disappeared into the crushing weight of earth, leaving Kizu with a throbbing calf. It had been his human side, which wasn’t nearly as resilient as the monster leg.

“You could have killed me with that!” Kizu shouted at the professor.

“Hardly. At worst, I remove a kneecap. Professor Taroe is more than capable enough to repair such damage.” To punctuate his sentence, Krimpit released another spear of earth.

This time, Kizu cast an elemental spell in return. A gust of wind slammed down in front of him, catching the length of the spear and interrupting its flight. It narrowly missed Kizu’s toes as it pieced through the barriers.

Krimpit was already forming a third spear with the clear intent to keep hurling the piercing attack at him. But, at the same time, the professor was looking over his shoulder and frowning in the direction of one of his sealed off paths. The south side. Where he’d mentioned two more students had accessed the tunnels. He was distracted.

An owl monkey fell on his head from above. The professor swore in an archaic tongue and his third spear attack crumbled as he ripped the monkey off his face and tossed Mort aside.

The earth swallowed Mort up to his neck. Kizu’s familiar squirmed but was completely pinned by the attack.

Krimpit turned his head back to address Kizu, but frowned at the empty space before him.

In the seconds Mort had spent blinding the professor, Kizu had dashed over to the side on a newly formed barrier and surrounded himself in an illusion. He was suspended on a barrier, not touching the ground, and shrouded from visual detection. Kizu’s heart beat rapidly and there was an irrational part of him that was certain the pounding of his chest would alert the professor.

“Frustrating,” Krimpit commented as he slowly scanned his surroundings. He’d now constructed a spatial barrier of his own around himself, likely thinking to protect himself from any attack from Kizu. “I could simply collapse the earth down on this chamber. But that would risk a mortal blow.”

Kizu licked his lips while he crouched in a small orb of rocky illusions. Krimpit wouldn’t be able to continue wasting time on Kizu. While the professor could undoubtedly outlast Kizu in a battle of attrition, as Kizu was currently maintaining a barrier to stand on, an illusion to conceal himself, and a divination spell to keep the illusion from detection, there were other students that demanded Krimpit’s attention. Kizu bet the cantankerous professor would need to retreat for other spoils.

But, instead of retreating, Krimpit extended a hand and then twisted it into a fist.

Kizu’s eyes widened as the entire room swirled.

“Perhaps I lack your combat professor’s strength and vigor; however, I am quite adept at peering through untruths,” Krimpit stated. “You won’t be able to hold up an effective camouflage illusion with an ever-changing backdrop. And I have faith my own barrier will protect against any pitiful attack you send my direction.”

Krimpit was right, in a moment he’d locate Kizu. So, instead of holding up his current illusion, Kizu dropped it at the same time he created two brand new illusions.

Two brilliant balls of light blazed into existence on either side of Krimpit and caused the professor to reel back in surprise and shield his eyes with an arm.

Kizu threw out an auditory illusion for good measure, the pattering of feet a few meters away.

While Krimpit took the bait and the earth surged around the noise, Kizu slammed a potion vial into the nearest barricade of earth.

The acid within rapidly ate through unenchanted dirt. It only had two seconds before the oxygen in the air nullified its corrosion, but that was enough for a meter high hole to form.

Kizu’s barrier shot through the opening, with him crouched on it.

Behind him, Krimpit shouted something and another wall of dirt started to form in front of Kizu.

Kizu blasted it back with a wave of antimagic. While the dirt itself wasn’t magical, it momentarily disconnected Krimpit’s control, allowing Kizu’s barrier to zip over it.

Behind him, he saw through Mort’s senses as the earthen barricades around Krimpit crumbled, returning the intersection to its previous state. Dirt swelled up beneath the history professor and carried him off at a rapid speed. To the south. Away from Kizu.

Krimpit might be stubborn, but apparently even he knew when to cut his losses.

Half a minute later, Kizu leaped onto a stone staircase and bounded up the steps and emerged in the academy hallways.
