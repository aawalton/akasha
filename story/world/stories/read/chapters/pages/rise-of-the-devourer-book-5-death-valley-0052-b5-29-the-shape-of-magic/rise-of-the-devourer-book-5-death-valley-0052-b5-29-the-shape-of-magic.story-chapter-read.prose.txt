Every time he started to wrap his head around one section, the one beside it was different again, but the more he looked he gradually began to understand the flow of it despite how many different shapes it evoked. Coral, roots, petals, mushrooms…any of those descriptions could be applied to some part of it, but the whole escaped categorization.
