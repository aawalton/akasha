Greenie’s idea raised a chicken and egg scenario. A player might resist the demon with Heavenly Favor active. They might cast it before donning the relic. Relic bearers only needed to keep it refreshed while wearing it and remove it before bed, opening a loophole for the cursed item. It meant I could wear the relic.
