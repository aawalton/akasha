The battle consisted of perhaps thirty Singers holding back half again as many Earh Integrated, with the invaders having suffered little in the way of losses thus far. That couldn't be said for the attackers. Many humans were on the ground with wounds of varying seriousness. With no time to lose and having gained some approximate information about the Singers' capabilities from Weaver's Perception, Luke threw himself into combat.

In a situation such as this one, with a lot of people on either side, it made little to no sense for him to jump in and start swinging. Instead, he landed right in the middle and reached out with threads of mana in all directions. Careful not to lose focus, Luke jumped from Earth Integrated to Earth Integrated, disregarding their shocked and feeble attempts at keeping him out until he reached the injured. Healing through a daisy-chain of others proved no more difficult than doing it through a direct link, but doing so while also crashing through the Singer lines with still more threads was a challenge, but one he took on without reservation.

Blades of all shapes and sizes came for him as he crouched down there in the middle of their ranks, but Luke had the presence of mind to deal with them. Some he pushed to the side, a couple he blocked with the new Strengthen Thread skill, and a few fell short since he had cut the tendons in his attacker's arms. An arrow slammed into his side, but it was nothing more than a nuisance, since he, at that point, had begun draining the Singers' of mana. Spells kept forming. More and more of them were directed at him. No matter the target, he sliced through all spellweaves before making sure the mages would never cast another spell again. Threads emerged everywhere without him even needing to use Cascading Threads, and for a moment, he got an inkling about that particular skill, something to consider later. Once a thread left its Singer body, activating Weaver's Whip worked just fine. Luke kept his eyes closed, but still felt shock ripple through the Singers as whips lashed out again and again, killing with almost every single attack.

The fight lasted less than two minutes. A few Singers stood against Luke's attacks for a time, but once their ranks thinned out, he pushed harder. That crumbled the defenses of those remaining, and they too fell.

Luke rose to his feet and looked around. Three humans were dead. Two of them had died before he arrived, and he'd missed a third one who'd fallen to a spear thrust at some point in the chaos. With the large number of dead Singers littering the ground, it was an acceptable result, but the loss of life on Earth's side irked Luke. It wasn't his fault, but maybe he should have shifted focus more toward protecting the others. Then again, killing the Singers meant they didn't have time to attack again.

Sighing, Luke stepped over the closest Singers, turning to walk back to the portal.

"Hey! Ggwe ani?" one of the Integrated called out. "Who are you?"

Luke looked over his shoulder and saw all of them staring at him. Awkward... He raised his hand in a brief wave and shouted back. "Luke! Uh, I've got to go!"

"Smooth," Ella said when he returned to the platform. "Impressive fighting, though."

"Thanks," he said, walking on without stopping. Glancing back again at the still stunned-looking people he'd helped, Luke stepped through the portal.

It was still pretty quiet on the other side. The turrets were twitching, and smoke rose from electrical panels on their sides. He gave Ella a look, and she shrugged. "Needed to do something while you were off saving the locals. Can you just," she waved her finger at the portals," do your thing now so we can go?"

"Did you try closing them yourself?" Luke asked, reaching out to the weave.

"No," she said with another shrug. "Saving my energy."

All four portals winked out. He figured it was best not to leave one open, since he didn't have much faith in the force back there on Earth invading the Singers. He didn't even know if they were in on the plan or not.

"Where to next?" he asked.

Ella reached out her hand and spoke with a terrible German accent. "Come with me if you want to live."

Racing through Singer-land in some sort of speedster bubble, Luke didn't get to take in a whole lot of the environment. It was blurriness followed by a platform somewhere outside of a city. Once he'd closed the portals, they left for the next almost identical platform. A few held Integrated from Earth fighting Singers. When the duo happened upon one of those, they made a brief stop to help before moving on. After a while, more and more Singers waited on the platforms, but their attempts to stop or hinder Luke didn't do much good since he was getting the hang of it, and they soon didn't need to stop for more than a couple of seconds each time.

Ray: "Hey, Luke. I've got news."

Ray: "Let's just say they've noticed what you're doing."

Ray: "I'm sort of sitting in on a couple of meetings. They're about to bring out the big guns."

Ray: "The New York destroying kind. Except bigger, I think."

Luke: "What about us closing the gates?"

Ray: "Not a word on that."

Ray: "Either they're not worried, or there are other guys working on that."

Luke: "Can you find out where they'll bring the guns?"

Ray: "That's the thing... I might be missing some local color in their language, but it sounds to me like they're not bringing weapons through."

Luke: "What do you mean?"

They stopped at another platform, and Luke kept an eye on Ella as he lashed out at the threads binding the portals to their destination.

"I think I can do it," she said, her eyes glittering.

Ray: "Maybe they're looking to fire through a portal?"

Luke: "Can you find out?"

Ella grabbed his hand, and they were off before the Singers caught wind of them.

Ray: "I'll try."

The blur of motion around them lasted a little longer this time, and when they stopped, the platform held no more than two portals, the least they'd seen so far.

"You closed one?" he asked, before weavestepping forward to reach a mage Singer flanked by two tank-types holding large shields. They fell before she got a single spell off.

Ella held up her left hand and made a peace sign, laughing. "Two."

"Down!" Luke shouted, and she turned into a blur and disappeared just as a spear hurtled out from behind a couple of blue, plastic-looking water barrels.

Ella: "I'm fine!"

The offending Singer looked like he was scared out of his mind, where he huddled behind those barrels, and the moment Luke approached, he turned and ran screaming down the ramp, to where a large contingent of its brethren was making its way toward the platform. While Luke dealt with the spear thrower, Ella had closed the two remaining portals.

"Let's go!" Luke shouted, and before he knew it, he was whisked away.

Again, the blur lasted much longer.

Ella: "I did it!"

Ella: "I don't need your slow ass anymore!"

Luke: "Maybe give it a couple more minutes before you discard me in a ditch, yeah?"

Ella: "Meh. We've closed down another platform, and you didn't even notice, did you?"

Luke: "I didn't. Good job, Ella. Just don't get carried away."

While they rushed through Singer-land, Luke received reports from Milla about their progress. It sounded like things were going well on their end, but then he got a message from Hannah.

Hannah: "Curtis is getting kind of scary."

Hannah: "And that's saying something coming from me."

Hannah: "He's kind of... Eating Singers."

Luke: "We shouldn't judge those different from us."

Hannah: "Har-de-har. It's just that he's different."

Luke: "We can try talking to him again, I guess. But I'm not sure what to say. It's his class."

Hannah: "Yeah... I know. Still."

Luke: "Yeah."

Hannah: "More Singers. Got to go!"

The next message was like someone had dipped Luke in a cold spring.

Ray: "Holy shit! Luke, they just mentioned The Fallen Shepherd!"

Luke was about to reply when something tickled his awareness, and a terrible sense of foreboding hit him.

Luke: "Ella! Stop!"

It was too late. The two of them slammed into something solid, and everything came to a squelching halt. Luke's body exploded into what felt like a million pieces. Blood splattered everywhere as flesh ripped itself apart. There was no pain, not even any loss of consciousness, which was strange, seeing as the few parts of him that made it through unscathed were those wrapped in Guardian Weave. All senses winked away but returned in a flash of awareness. A second after that, he drew in a gasping breath and slammed Weaver's Renewal into his own meta-heart, draining far too much mana out of it. That had been close. Far too close.
