Next, let's do a bit of recap, shall we? Just a paragraph for each book. OF COURSE! SPOILERS AHEAD! YOU'VE BEEN WARNED!

Book 1: The Visitor

Frein discovers the existence of other worlds through his girlfriend, Katherine, who came from Brymeia. He essentially coerces her to bring him back to her world, not knowing this invoked a prophecy that left Katherine no choice. Within 1 year, Frein will die, unable to return to Earth. The man accepts his destiny, not knowing there's more to it than just dying. In Brymeia, he finds friends, learns Meiyal Arts, stops a war, and discover mysteries that essentially led him on a path of sacrifice.

Book 2: Unveiled Destiny

While enjoying his time on Brymeia, Frein and his friends discover ways to increase their strengths. He, in particular, to prepare himself better in fulfilling his destiny, whatever it is. He finds out the tangibility of Destiny, how gods manipulated it for their own agendas, and how he's somehow supposed to peer into it. On Atlas Sid, a literal flying city, he finds out more about Katherine, the love of his life. And as they travel, they're faced with a challenge from the Nightmare itself. Not to worry, because their real problems lie in the Nightmare Incursion.

Book 3: Nightmare Incursion

When the shadows began to move, Frein finds himself separated from Katherine. Locked deep within the dungeons of Befall, the world begins to speak to him. The truth behind his purpose, and how his enemies had taken it from him. Now, he doesn't have to wait a year, because he's about to die. But he's been given a chance to correct his Destiny. For he has so much more to live for. As soon as he breaks out, he takes the life of the one who took his purpose. Rather than correct, however, he was given a chance. A path to break free from his Destiny, and take full control of it. Become a god himself.

Book 4: Talendrym Isle

If Frein wants to survive, he has to become a god.

After training for a year with Zerax'thum, Frein has become Brymeia's first Worldborn in what has been thousands of years. And now, he's attracted not only everyone's attention, but also those beyond the cosmos. Entities from away infiltrate the world, attempting to take something dear to Brymeia.

But before Frein can protect the world, he must first help his other self, Elizzel, find the truth. For the path to godhood is hidden there. In Talendrym Isle.
