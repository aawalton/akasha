"Luke!" Ray shouted as he woke up with a gasp. Hands scrabbling, he lifted his shirt and stared. Whole. He was whole. What the hell had just happened?

Had it all been a bad dream? No, that wasn't it, since he was lying in an office building on top of a carpet full of dried blood that Ray was pretty sure was his own. Swallowing hard, he sat up. He'd died, but now he wasn't dead. Ray remembered a hospital room. Luke had been there. Unsure what the hell was going on, Ray got to his feet and walked over to the open window where he'd caught that archer Singer, who turned out to be pretty good.

It was raining. A lot. Something itched in the back of his mind, something he was supposed to remember, but he couldn't recall.

Ray: "Hey, Curtis. Where did you run off to?"

Ray stood there by the window for a good long while, just enjoying the occasional splash of water on his face when the wind blew rain toward him. He was alive. Goddammit, but wasn't that just beautiful? Another chance. Exactly how it came about, he wasn't sure, but damn. Goddamn!

Curtis: "Ray?"

Ray: "Yeah. Where you at?"

Curtis: "You died. You were dead."

Ray: "And now I'm alive. Keep up, newbie."

Curtis: "How?"

Ray: "I'm not sure. Decided to just roll with it. Where are you?"

Curtis: "...I'm near the portal. The Intruder's."

Curtis sent the address, as if Ray would know the specific streets in a city he'd visited once. Also, it was dark as hell out. Even so, Ray went out looking and eventually found the grumpy bastard on top of the roof of a building overlooking the park where the Singer portal opened up. Easy peasy. All Ray had to do was look for a piece of the darkness that was even darker than everything else. Great times were had getting soaked to the bone while avoiding demons and Singers, and sometimes demons fighting Singers, or Singers fighting humans, or all three fighting each other.

It was a mess out there.

"Couldn't you have found a place inside the building? Lurking works just as well inside, you know?"

Curtis looked strange in a kind of expressionless way. Eerie as fuck. Then he smiled. So that was something, at least.

"You are alive, then."

Ray wrung some water out of his shirt, shaking his head. "What? You thought you were communicating with a ghost?"

"I saw you die."

"We've been over this," Ray said, walking up to stand next to Curtis. Frowning, he looked the Reaver up and down. "What's up with you?"

Curtis kept looking down at the Singers. "Nothing. My class changed."

"Really?" Ray asked, raising an eyebrow. "To what? Grump?"

"Hollow."

"Sounds like a riot. Got a new skill? Let me guess! It's called 'rainbow party,' and it makes you shit out a full set of a kid's princess and rainbow themed birthday party?"

The corner of Curtis's mouth turned a little, his equivalent of a full-on belly laugh. Satisfied with the reaction, Ray turned to look down at the Singers. He leaned forward, frowning. "Is that?"

"What?" Curtis asked.

"That's a person down there talking with that Singer. A human, I mean."

"You can see details that far away?" Curtis asked.

"Assassins got to see who they're killing," Ray said, trailing off. That's when it came back to him: Luke's message. "Oh, shit."

"What?" Curtis asked.

"ISG. They're working with the Singers."

"...What?" Curtis asked, his expression turning grim.

"Yeah..." Ray said. "I think I'd better send a few messages to Luke's dear sister. I'm sure she'll be happy to hear from me."

Above, thunder rumbled. Somewhere in the far distance, the screech of a demon echoed between the buildings.
