The sound of Nathan's black blade biting into the flesh of the Wyvern was accompanied by a loud hiss as the large beast recoiled from Oblivion's blow. It was a reaction that Nathan understood all too well. After all, he himself had tasted the blade of Oblivion, and he could testify to its effectiveness when it came to throwing whoever had been struck by it off balance, psychologically, that is.

Former recipient of the blade's foul power or not, Nathan had no sympathy for the Wyvern, which was why he quickly pounced on the advantage Oblivion had provided him.

Now to keep it permanently grounded, Nathan thought as he sprinted toward the recoiling Wyvern, the berserker rapidly eating up the space the beast was trying to put between them.

As far as Nathan was concerned, the beast was already as good as dead. Of course it still required a bit of work on his part, but Nathan considered it a small price to pay for the life of the Wyvern, which had believed him to be the prey in this encounter.

Nathan immediately snapped out of his thinking as the Wyvern seemed to realize that he wasn't going to give it a chance to reorient itself. It had changed tactics from trying to flee to now standing its ground, which had Nathan a bit on edge, his instincts flaring up, and he might’ve continued to eat up the distance, but no, it was not just his instincts that flared up. There was something more to it...

It's trying to warn me of something.

His connection to the wind, which was his Dao, was still in its infancy, and Nathan had yet to grasp the fundamentals of what a Dao itself truly was. Yet he hesitated on whether he was to trust his bloodlust to help him overcome whatever it was the Wyvern aimed to throw at him or if he was to trust his instincts and the wind, which urged him to stop in his tracks and observe what was to happen.

Fuck me, Nathan cursed as he stopped in his tracks, a move that went against the basis of his archetype.

Not stopping was what Nathan preferred, if he was being honest and true to himself, but he couldn't deny the tugging sensation at his very soul as the wind tried to communicate with him. But even with his bloodlust disagreeing with the soul tug, he had stopped. And now the two creatures' eyes met. His human ones locking onto the Wyvern's serpentine ones. The creature no doubt had something sinister in mind, just as surely as Nathan, yet he found himself unable to break free from what troubled him.

Pausing in his tracks as he had, Nathan stared at the beast. He could observe every little thing about it: the way its body heaved, the cut on the side of its neck still streaming with red blood from where Oblivion had sliced at it. The black blade had pierced through its scales as though they weren't even there, which now, come to think of it, stood out to Nathan as an odd detail.

It seemed the sword was made a lot more effective for more than just demons, which meant he might potentially have one of the most dangerous weapons on earth in his possession, but it wasn't his soul blade.

Come on, why did you make me stop? Nathan thought, his eyes scanning for whatever threat had caused the wind to warn him.

Yet he found nothing… nothing visible to the naked eye, that is. But there could be something, judging by the way the Wyvern remained still as if taunting him to come after it. The scene had Nathan's hair on edge, his grip on Oblivion as tight as ever, as he considered the scene and what the possibilities entailed.

Nathan didn't know which of the three details he picked out saved him, whether it was the shifty head of the Wyvern, its twitching barbed tail, or its focused slit eyes. Whatever it was, it was enough to move the blood berserker to act as he instinctively dodged to the side, catching a whiff of ozone as lightning struck the exact spot Nathan had been standing at mere seconds ago.

The scent was so faint that he could’ve missed it. Although the warning from the wind had been early. The thought caused Nathan to quickly scramble to his feet, his teeth gritted. Well, this certainly makes things interesting.

The Wyvern seemed to be an elemental, or at least it had control of fucking lightning, which was just fan-fucking-tastic. It was a tricky situation for sure, but Nathan believed he could win, yet he found himself wondering exactly how he was planning to handle it.

It had been a close one, as the Lightning had been so fast, deadly, and precise. Nathan had nothing but the wind, his instincts, and his paranoia to thank for moving out of the way.

Body hits, claws raking through his chest, its massive barbed tail and its sharp teeth biting into his skin he could tank, but fucking lightning, Nathan wasn't sure about that, and he wasn't quite eager to try it.

A loud hiss dragged him out of his thoughts as the Wyvern seemed to be preparing for another attack. Nathan couldn't figure out whether it was more of the physical kind or the elemental type. Whichever the case may be, Nathan couldn't deny the fact that the Wyvern now had his entire attention locked in on it. His hair still stood on end from the charged atmosphere, and his mind had already begun cobbling together the beginning of a plan, as far as plans related to berserkers.

“I'm going to have to wing it,” Nathan muttered under his breath as he summoned King's Wrath into his free hand.

This wasn't going to be a clean and easy fight in any stretch of the imagination, but Nathan trusted in his abilities to be able to handle the Wyvern.

At least that was the one thing he could always count on, and this time wouldn’t be any different. As far as Nathan was concerned, fighting the Wyvern was as good an exercise as he could get currently, so he was going to fight it, and he was going to have fun fighting it as well.

Lightning, huh? Nathan thought as he charged toward the Wyvern. His entire being shook with excitement at the freedom to unleash his berserker side, which he had had to restrain a few moments ago. It was no secret to Nathan that, to become the best berserker there ever was in this cursed world and system that had been thrust onto Earth, he had to at the very least be able to use his brain, unlike other people who had gotten the berserker class and decided the best course of action was running into every fight like a headless chicken.

If Nathan behaved like them, he would undoubtedly meet the same end as them, dying to the stupid whims of his battle rage. But Nathan was smarter than that.

At least, I hope I am.

All thoughts of that left Nathan's mind as he clashed with the Wyvern. Barbed tail meeting King's Wrath, a solid thud echoed in the forest as both weapon and tail bounced off. The force of the attack caused the muscle under Nathan's left arm to ripple.

But he did not pull back. The berserker was starting to get into the flow state, so he put his trust in the wind to alert him of the next lightning strike. His eyes tracked the Wyvern's movements, spotting its attempt to clamp down on his shoulder with its sharp rows of teeth. But Nathan couldn’t let that happen.

He dodged. A single step to the side was enough to give him the space that he needed. And then he used Oblivion to slash at the side of the beast, warm red blood splattering across Nathan's chest from the cuts that he had just opened. The sensation made him feel alive.

The creature might have been huge, with what most would consider significant power, but to Nathan, it was just a dumb beast that needed to be put down, compared to the demon prince and the mercenary he had just fought. Plus, there wasn't a small voice niggling at the back of his mind telling him to watch out for cleverness. Not at all. This was just a beast, and he'd approach it as such.

What else was there to it?

This was his new life. This was what he had come for, and this was the fire and the forge he would have to go through if he intended to evolve into something much better. This was what Nathan would have to do to break through his limits and become something in this world.

"No, this is the bare minimum," Nathan muttered under his breath as he barreled into the creature.

He charged, knocking the creature back, but Nathan knew that there was much more he needed to do. He wasn't even, at the very least, content with just pushing the Wyvern backward.

Nathan needed to dominate. He needed to kill it in such a way that it knew, even in its dying breath, that they were never on the same terms, that there was no hope, no need to even try.

But now the Wyvern didn't have that feeling, and Nathan knew it. The Wyvern believed that it had a chance against the Crimson King.

It believed it could battle him and survive. Hell, it believed it could win this fight, and Nathan wouldn't stand for that.

The berserker in him seemed to be growing with each fight he survived, and now caution had been thrown to the wind. Blood was the only thing he wanted, and Nathan couldn't tell if it was because of King's Wrath in his left hand, baying for blood, the Ruby blade reminding him that he needed to carve the creature, or was it Oblivion in his right hand whispering with a little voice at the back of his head, telling him to feed it the creature's soul.

And now the decision was in his own hands, the choice of which blade deserved the life of the Wyvern, and that was just not it at all. Even his bare fist would do enough damage to the creature.

But more than that, Nathan was fixed on the unjustified rage that was starting to build up at the base of his skull, his heart thumping as he rushed toward the creature once more. Loud hisses echoed in their little part of the forest as the Wyvern sought to beat its wings, probably to use its aerial advantage, but Nathan had no intention of allowing the creature that chance.

Oh no you don't, Nathan thought as he dug his heels in and exploded toward the Wyvern, his mind already moving toward the next course of action as his hands moved. Oblivion and King's Wrath both slashed at the creature's left wing.

Even if the creature had gathered enough momentum to launch itself into the air, he would make sure the wings were porous enough that it could only hover. That was if it could even get off the ground when he was finished with it.

Nathan's plan seemed to have worked, as the creature's wings were pierced by Oblivion and King's Wrath, red blood seeping down both blades. But that didn't even slow the Blood Berserker, who had already moved on to the next target. Its hind legs. The creature hissed, this time its pain evident in the sound.

The creature eventually managed to take off, but not fast enough, as Nathan quickly withdrew King's Wrath into his soul space. He wouldn't give the Wyvern even a hint of hope. He would capitalize, and that was what Nathan did.

His left hand wrapped around the Wyvern's leg. His hand wasn't quite long enough to go fully around it, but Nathan managed. He held the Wyvern back even as it beat its wings furiously, trying to properly pull off into the air even with its bleeding wing. But Nathan had no intention of giving it that opportunity.

His entire being rebelled at the fact that the creature even sought to escape. He planned to dominate.

So far, he had shared the battleground with so many creatures, most of the time relying on luck, sometimes surviving just by the teeth of his skin. But now he wanted something different.

He wanted dominance in fights. He wanted it so badly that he ignored the claws of the Wyvern's hind leg digging into his torso, trying to force him to free it. Nathan continued to ignore the feeling as he stabbed into its left thigh with Oblivion, eliciting another hiss from the creature. But that wasn't enough.

Nathan needed more than this. And he could feel it, his body and mind getting in tune with his berserker side.

He needed this. Nathan needed to push beyond his limits. He needed to feel the thrill of the battle. He needed to dance to the hum of blood. He needed the pounding in his head to intensify. He needed caution thrown to the wind, and he needed the wind at his side.

What else could he wish for? What else should be accomplished? Nothing mattered more to Nathan than this moment. Oblivion in his grip, his sword twisting into the thigh of the Wyvern, causing its hiss to intensify.

All of a sudden, Nathan let it go, ripping Oblivion free from the wound. No doubt the sword had already begun to suck the soul out of the creature, but Nathan wasn't bothered. Neither for the black blade's effect on the Wyvern nor the possibility of it winning him, because he knew deep inside him this fight was his to lose.

To be honest, Nathan no longer thought of his dance with the Wyvern as a true contest. If anything, he considered it a test of how fast he could impose his will, his presence, and his aura on the field.

Like clockwork, Nathan released his aura. Even now, he could feel how far it stretched in the different directions surrounding him. So far, it hadn't even grown in area, but his aura expanded into the air, making it denser.

Nathan wasn't sure if it was the torn wings of the Wyvern, or its bleeding thigh which had tasted the wickedness of Oblivion, or the aura he had just released into the air mixed with his bloodlust. But one thing was clear, the Wyvern could no longer maintain its aerial flight, and the little hovering it had been enjoying just moments ago had come to an end as it was forced to drop to the floor ungracefully.

Its wings bent into stubs that it used to hold its weight up against Nathan's aura and bloodlust. Or maybe that wasn't it. Maybe the creature now truly knew what it faced off with.

Nathan caught a whiff of ozone. But the berserker stood still, no longer hesitant. Lightning was a formidable element, but Nathan put his trust in himself and his abilities. Let the Wyvern release whatever it could, he would rise to the challenge and survive.

It was no surprise when he got the newest system notification.

[You are trapped within a [lightning cage (IV)] for the next five minutes.Timer: 4:59]

No sooner had he received the system notification than the atmosphere around him became charged, and all around him, white, blazing lightning crackled and crisscrossed in a pattern that resembled a steel cage surrounding him on all sides, effectively trapping him and the Wyvern in the elemental cage.

Nathan couldn't help the bloodthirsty grin that split his face.
