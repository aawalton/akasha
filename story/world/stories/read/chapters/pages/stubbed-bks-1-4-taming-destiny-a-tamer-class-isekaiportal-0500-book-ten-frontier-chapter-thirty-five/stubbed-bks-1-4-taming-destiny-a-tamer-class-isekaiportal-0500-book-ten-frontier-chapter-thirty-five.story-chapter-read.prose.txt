As soon as Artio’s forest-green internal matrix comes into view, I immediately feel an intense pressure threaten to push me out of the space entirely. It’s like I’ve suddenly teleported to the bottom of an ocean, with the weight of kilometres of water above me. It’s been a while since I saw a Tier three Evolution from the inside, but this feels far more intense than the last one.

Artio is doing fine from what I can see. I sense his concentration, but an equal amount of confidence that it is under control. To be fair to him, there is none of the precariousness that I sensed within Flower’s Core space when I intervened there. That’s not to say that Artio is having no difficulty – Evolution is challenging at the best of times, and each Evolution becomes ever more so – but I have full confidence that he will succeed. As long as he isn’t too greedy, that is.

Since I don’t seem to be needed, I just linger at the edge of his Core space as an observer, trying to be as quiet and unobtrusive as possible so as not to distract him. I feel his presence recognise me – and then a fraction of a second later dismiss me as unimportant. I take no offence – that he hasn’t tried to expel me is enough.

Artio’s Core was bright when I arrived, but continues brightening with every moment. His internal matrix is pulsing with Energy, though the pulses are moving towards his Core. It’s like instead of the Energy for his Evolution coming from him – which is the normal process, as far as I know – it’s being pulled in from the outside, straining his channels to the limit. That would definitely be something new.

The pulsing grows stronger and stronger, the Core burning as brightly as a star, somehow hurting my eyes even in this space where they don’t technically exist. Equally, the pressure grows unbearable, even for me.

As I’m wondering whether I should leave – whether this is actually causing me damage, or only feels like it is – I sense everything reach a tipping point.

Artio’s focus shifts from containing and increasing the pressure to directing it.

A moment later, his Core detonates.

Since I’ve seen this before in Flower’s Core space, I don’t fear that this is a sign of something going wrong. Instead, I know that it’s just entered its next phase.

Pulses of Energy explode through Artio’s system with a strength that threatens to burst his channels, then rebound against his Core space, forcing it to expand with every impact. This is definitely the most violent Evolution I’ve seen. Which is interesting – both Flower and Artio had similar abilities at Tier two, yet, due to their very different personalities, their Evolutions are like chalk and cheese.

Even more fascinating is that I’m watching Artio’s internal matrix rewrite itself before my very eyes. It’s not just expanding; whole sections are being cannibalised and transformed into different shapes.

Curiosity flows through me as I see the matrix take shape. Where before it was clearly an evolution of what I’ve learned is a standard beast matrix baseline – channels reaching out into all limbs of the creature, as well as its head – it becomes something quite different. And, as the Cores start taking their place, like a seed beginning as a small dot and then swelling in size. I realise that I recognise it.

A tree, I breathe. Not a mighty oak – or not yet, at least – the internal matrix’s trunk looks almost unfinished. Or, more likely, still growing. It has a slim trunk and only a few small, feathery branches reaching out sideways. Yet its rootstock is solid, most of the Energy channels concentrated there.

One of the Cores is nestled in the heart of the roots too, with a second placed two thirds up the trunk – at the first node where the other branches start appearing – and a third at the highest point of its leading shoot. Three Cores seems to be a common number – Flower had them, and I’m pretty sure that Kalanthia has three too – however I don’t think that it implies any lesser ability on the part of the beast in comparison to someone like Bastet who has five.

What’s also interesting here is that the Cores are all very different sizes. The one nestled in the rootstock is the biggest by far – about three times the size of the one in the trunk. That in turn is much larger than the one at the tip of the top of the main leader, which has barely swelled larger than the pinprick it started as.

I’m very curious to see whether the small Cores grow over time – if I get the chance, that is. Because with Artio Evolving, it’s high time we had a very particular kind of discussion.

The flow of Energy through Artio’s system hasn’t stopped, but with the appearance of the Cores, it’s shifted into another phase. Energy pulls in steadily from the outside, but this time, I was expecting it to – no doubt the cloud of sparks around the owlbear’s figure is reducing little by little as he uses every iota of Energy that he can.

It pulls towards his biggest core, though I notice the other two Cores sparkling as they process the Energy that’s pulled in from the tips of the branches. Interestingly, even though it’s already been through one or even two of his Cores, it is still pulled down to the Core in his rootstock.

From there, it is fed into the luminous blackness. Pulling back my awareness from his Core space, I instead focus on his physical body. And there, I sense that things are changing considerably – but I can’t quite put my finger on how. It’s like the very nature of Artio’s body is shifting. In fact, it’s becoming rather more difficult to send my consciousness through his system – if he weren’t Bonded to me with Dominate, I suspect that I wouldn’t be able to do so at all.

And that mystifies me. It can’t just be due to the amount of Energy saturating him – I didn’t have nearly this amount of difficulty when healing Kalanthia, and no one can say that she’s a weak example of a Tier three. Moreover, Artio is only newly Evolved. And it also can’t be something to do with the Evolution itself or I would have experienced it before.

Resolving to get answers as soon as I can, I return to full awareness of my physical surroundings.

The cloud of sparks around Artio and me has thinned considerably – I can now see the crowd surrounding us. Everyone appears to have come. That is, all of those willingly Bonded to me bar Maxen, though Loran appears to have managed to convince Sarran to allow him to come. Or perhaps Sarran just wasn’t willing to be a hypocrite – he’s here too. As are the other lords – Layton nods as he meets my gaze, as does Valence. Evidently a Tier three Evolution is interesting enough even for non-Tamers to attend.

Romeo, the larnatis who’s slowly making progress in convincing Bastet to accept him as her mate is here too, as, surprisingly, is Tilathis, though he’s standing further back from the group than most. Kalanthia is by his side, keeping an eye on Artio, but her gaze drifting every so often to look at Loran. She was highly surprised to hear that Loran had successfully Evolved, and pelted him with all sorts of questions, most of which he didn’t really know how to answer. Proof, if I needed it, that Kalanthia doesn’t know everything.

When I sense the presence of unaffiliated Tier threes nearby, I tense, but the lack of hostility in the air prevents me from immediately ordering my companions to make a line of defence – I think they’re just here to sightsee.

Catching the gaze of Bastet, I realise that she’s fully aware of the interlopers.

Let them see, she tells me with a sense of satisfaction in her voice. Let them know of our increased power.

My eyes snap back to fix on Artio as he shifts under my hand. I take a step back, noticing that the final spark has disappeared. His Evolution has finished. And, unsurprisingly, it’s a complete success.

My companions greet him with howls, roars, chirps, snaps – in short, a cacophony of congratulations. Orion is the first to step forward to celebrate his success by rubbing himself against the owlbear who doesn’t seem to have changed much to outward appearances, but he isn’t the last.

I quickly step away to avoid being accidentally knocked over by someone with just a bit too much exuberance. I’m far too curious about how things have changed, so pull up the Bound panel on my System interface and focus on finding his entry.

My eyebrows rise as I see the direction Artio has taken. As a yelna who Evolved into the mage version of their species, his health and stamina were never as high as I know some Tier two yelnas can have. However, his species meant that he was still far more robust than many of my companions. Yet here, his health hasn’t even doubled, and his stamina has actually gone down. He seems to have instead gone all in on mana, more than quadrupling the three thousand or so points he had before.

As for his abilities, that he kept Plant-Shaping is little surprise – after watching him rewrite his internal matrix to resemble a tree, I’d have been far more shocked if he’d earned abilities that weren’t to do with plants. Fecundity is something I’ve seen before – Flower had it too. It’s a support ability, allowing them to feed more mana into plants and make them grow far faster than normal, but without any of the normal detrimental effects that can sometimes happen when Plant Mages force-grow plants.

Plant-Meld and Mould, however, are two abilities I haven’t seen before – and though I can guess at the first, I’m hoping the second doesn’t have anything to do with fungus.

“An interesting sight,” Layton comments. He’s drifted around to my side of the circle.

I send him a hint of a smile.

“Your first time seeing an Evolution?”

He shakes his head.

“While fighting by the front lines, I saw a few of the beasts controlled by the tamers Evolve. However, that was always from Tier one to Tier two. This was far more impactful in comparison. A common sight for you, perhaps?”

I laugh quietly.

“Hardly. But not my first time either.” Seeing that the throng around Artio has thinned a little, I excuse myself from my conversation with Layton and step forward toward Artio.

Perhaps sensing my seriousness through the Bond, my companions glance at me, then step away, making a path through to the owlbear. I pause in front of him, taking him in properly for the first time since his Evolution.

I had thought he wasn’t much changed, and in some ways he isn’t. His size is much the same, the look in his eyes similar. But I can feel his presence far more strongly, and he feels different. There’s also an odd kind of texture to his feathers – where before they were fluffy, now they appear rough. As he lifts his head, they move differently too – more stiffly. Curious. They didn’t feel any different when I was touching them.

I push the observation away – there is a more important question to ask now.

“Artio, congratulations on your Evolution,” I tell him, making an effort to keep my voice relaxed and the Bond between us calm. “I can tell you’ve been wholly successful – it’s impressive and worthy of celebration.”

Thank you, Binder, Artio replies, though I sense a touch of wariness in his tone. He suspects that there is a catch to my words. And there is – kind of.

“When we first met, we were enemies. You agreed to the Bond in order to avoid being killed – and with the agreement that I seek out the one who had forced you and others of your kind to obey him.”

Except you lost him. Artio replies with a hint of old anger, though it disappears soon after.

“That’s true,” I admit. I doubt that that fight is over – it’s quite likely that Torrent will want to have his revenge and perhaps he will give us an advantage in doing so. But I’m not going to promise something I can’t deliver – again. “However, you’ve now had long enough to experience everything the pack has to offer, as well as what is expected of one who is part of it.

What is your point? Artio asks suspiciously.

“That it’s high time I gave you the choice of whether you wish to stay with us – or wish to make your own way alone in the world. You’re Tier three now, and there is a whole continent to explore. If you wish to go, you will do so with our blessing, though not support. But, you don’t have to. If you wish it, you can stay with us, a part of the pack – like Bastet, like Kalanthia.” I give him a moment to think. “The choice is yours, and you need not even make it now. As long as you don’t choose to attack anyone under my protection, you will always be welcome to return. For now, I suggest you brace yourself – this will hurt a bit.”

With that, I break the Dominate Bond between us. Even braced, it hurts with a soul-deep pang. Artio churrs in discomfort in front of me, far less used to the sharpness of a Bond being severed – or the throbbing that continues even after.

Swallowing, I gaze at Artio. He’s frozen, his eyes wide and unfocused.

Then he moves.

He turns his head around slowly to look at those surrounding him on all sides. His gaze lingers longest on the two deris whose body language is a picture of impatience.

When he moves again, I see his round eyes touch on Kalanthia with Tilathis next to her; on Bastet; on, of all people, Elarion.

And then he turns his head back to me, his gaze determined.
