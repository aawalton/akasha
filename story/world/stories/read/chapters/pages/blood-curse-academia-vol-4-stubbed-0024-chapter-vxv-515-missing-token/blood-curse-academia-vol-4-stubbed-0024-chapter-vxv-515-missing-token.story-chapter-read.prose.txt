Chapter V.XV (5.15) - Missing Token

Kizu’s mind retraced his steps for the last few hours and attempted to recall every person he’d encountered since he last remembered the token being in his possession. Hundreds of other students had passed him in the hallway. Not to mention all of his Summoning F peers. He distinctly recalled touching his token during a conversation with Sene, so it couldn’t have been before that.

Without missing a beat, Kizu scrambled over to the foot of his old bed and pushed aside a pile of Basil’s failed sewing projects to create a tunnel into the deeper recesses under his bed. A minute later, he emerged from the dark tunnel with a box with divination supplies. He blew the dust off it in a cloud.

Meanwhile, Basil stood off to the side, trying to placate Kizu and encouraging him to forget about it and focus on his clothing.

Kizu ignored his friend and shoved aside a mound of clothing, making way for a clear patch of wooden flooring. Then he got to work scribbling with a stick of chalk. He knew the base structure for the divination ritual. But what he really needed was Anna’s divination book that he’d used to track down Anata in the first semester. But that was all the way down at Owl’s Respite.

As Kizu thought of the ship, another view came into his sight. Sleepy eyelids opened, revealing the sight of the bookshelf portal he’d created. Mort slept in their room, groggily awakening as a result of Kizu’s panicked emotional state.

Kizu begged his familiar to activate the portal and retrieve the book from onboard the ship. Once, Mort wouldn’t have been able to understand such complex instructions through their bond alone, but today full comprehension passed through his familiar. Mort certainly didn’t bother to understand the nuances of Kizu’s distress, but he understood the basics of what Kizu wanted.

As Kizu’s familiar, Mort could act as a conduit of Kizu’s spells. It was weaker the further they were apart, but activating the portal thankfully required very little effort.

His attention split, Kizu accidentally crossed the chalk’s basic formation incorrectly. He cursed and removed the error with a wave of earth elemental magic.

“Wait! Did you just toss chalk dust on my shoe collection?” Basil asked, aghast.

Continuing to ignore his roommate, Kizu felt a deep sense of relief as Mort reached the book on a shelf above the hammocks. With the thick tome now in Mort’s grasp, Kizu pulled Mort. Acting as an anchor for the monkey to jump to his location.

There was a pop, and both book and monkey appeared in the dorm room. Kizu sent a silent thanks to Mort through their bond then snatched up the divination tome. He thumbed through the pages until he found the one he needed. Locating a lost item.

He’d learned a variation of this spell a couple months ago. This one though would give him a distinct impression of where the object was, rather than pulling in the object from nearby.

Glancing at the ritual formation for reference, Kizu continued his work. Every small mistake was eliminated until he completed the complex pattern.

“Good, that should do it,” he pronounced, scrutinizing his work.

“You’re done then?” Basil asked. “Then perhaps you’d consider putting on the clothing I painstakingly—”

Basil’s voice cut out as Kizu slammed his hand down on the ritual formation and channeled his magic into it. Everything blurred. It would be easier if he’d held the wooden token for longer than a few days, but it did also have his face on it, so that helped deepen his connection to it.

After continuing to push his magic forward through the blockades, Kizu’s vision cleared and focused on a red scaled Tainted boy grinning at someone out of sight. He raised his hands and gestured, as if telling some sort of story.

Kizu gasped as his senses returned to the dorm room in a rush. The pungent scents of perfume slammed into his noise and nearly made him puke.

“The…red scale Tainted,” Kizu said, wheezing. “Who is he?”

“In the dormitory?” Basil asked “That’s Charles. Son of a duke or count or something over in Edgeland. Decently popular with the girls. I believe he’s on his seventh girlfriend since coming to the academy. All lookers too. Though, if I remember right, he tried asking Aoi out last year and got knocked down a few pegs.” Basil added that last bit with a triumphant smirk.

“He has my token. I need to get it back.”

“Does he really?” Basil’s eyebrows rose. “Any particular strategy in mind?”

Kizu debated. Then he looked to Mort. Everyone in the academy knew Kizu had a familiar, but ever since Anata had been recovered from the World Dungeon, Mort had been seen less and less by the other students. Kizu couldn’t even recall the last time someone had called him ‘monkey boy’ or a similar insult. Now the basic insults he occasionally overheard usually involved his hair color or witch up-bringing.

A plan began to take shape in Kizu’s mind.

“Basil, which room is Charles’?”

“Second to last on the left. But you won’t be able to break in easily. It has security measures.”

Kizu frowned at his friend. “You broke into Aoi’s room with me in the first semester without any issue.”

“Well, I’d been in that specific room before and knew exactly what security measures were in place. Plus, I am not fond of the idea of assisting a break-in of a room down the hall from where I sleep.”

“Fine. I don’t need you to break in. Just point at the door for me.” Kizu turned his attention to his familiar. “Mort. Nobody saw you enter the academy. They won’t see you coming.”

Mort hummed and cocked his head at Kizu.

“I’m going to overlay you with an illusion. Mort, I know this goes against your very nature, but I need you to stay perfectly still in the hallway and wait for Charles to enter his room.”

“And then Mort will slip inside, steal back the token, and jump back to you,” Basil said, immediately catching on to Kizu’s plan.

“Exactly.” Kizu made a face. “But I’ll be juggling this with dinner with Ione’s mother as well. You’re certain you aren’t willing to help out, Basil?”

“I can help you with your clothes!” Basil suggested merrily. “Now, take off those bland academy issued pants and try on mine.”
