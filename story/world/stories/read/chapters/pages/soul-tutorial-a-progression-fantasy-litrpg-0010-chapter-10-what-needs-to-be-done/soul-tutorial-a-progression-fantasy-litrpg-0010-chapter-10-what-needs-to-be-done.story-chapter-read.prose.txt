The silver slime body caved in at the point of impact, originating from the dent, sending a shockwave through its body and destroying its core. Multiple ribs cracked, piercing through Luke’s chest, rupturing his lungs, and destroying his arm all the way to his shoulder.

Once again overwhelmed with pain, Luke quickly tried to stop the bleeding, only for the pain to intensify.

His right leg was mashed into a paste. With no way of getting up without a struggle, he could only focus his efforts on breathing correctly. Choking on his blood that began trickling out of his mouth.

“Why does this keep happening to me?” he screamed through clenched teeth, trying to drag himself to the pedestal with his unusable arm and stump. The world twisted around him, making it difficult to even follow the right direction.

I don’t think I can make it through, echoed through his head. While he barely moved even an inch from where he was. He had taken much more damage than he had thought he would. Trying to stretch his arm, which was bent in 3 positions, he realized how useless everything was.

Luke couldn’t recall how he got here in the first place, blacking out at the point of impact. With burns surrounding his upper body.

The presence of the slime was gone, besides a pit it had created when it last slammed against the ground.

Luke had all his attention on making it to the pedestal alive without bleeding to death. Maybe the points spent on constitution had stopped him from dying from his injuries; not only that, he didn’t know how long he had been out for; it could have been minutes or even hours.

Still not being able to tell the time bothered him, but not as much as his wounds or the fact that he was leaning very close to death. Luke had to do something; the boy forced himself through the pain as he tried to lean on his stump.

Thank God I hadn’t gotten both legs crushed by that slime. But that was beside the point; blood continued gushing out, not only from his leg but also from the mangled arm. Clenching his teeth as tightly as possible, Luke used what was left of the stump and his broken left arm to try to stand. He cried out in pain, barely containing the throbbing pain that seemed to eat him alive. The pedestal was his only way to survive. His only friend on his forsaken island. Even if the pedestal wasn’t alive, that was the only thing he could rely on here besides his strength.

But his strength had never been enough. Looking back at it, he was just lucky. If he kept climbing floors at this pace, death would come sooner rather than later. The boy had barely survived an encounter with a floor 2 slime.

It had been just a slime after all. What could have been so hard? After some time, Luke had lifted himself onto his left leg, wobbling occasionally since his balance had become a total mess.

If he fell over, that would be it for him; it had taken everything out of him not to black out again. The pain had been making him insane as he covered the distance to the pedestal with small hops.

An unbearable stench of blood wafted around him as it kept dripping from his wounds. The taste of iron lingered in his mouth. Luke kept pushing with the thoughts of his weak mother appearing in his head.

Luke was relieved to find he wasn’t as far from the pedestal as he’d feared. But all that hope quickly disappeared. Luke tumbled toward the ground, tripping on his right leg that hung in front of him.

He had forgotten how messed up the island had gotten from the previous fights. He landed headfirst on the ground.

Not like this, not right here. I can’t have given up.

Even if the pain had hindered him. The fear of death had driven him to try everything he could, barely able to curl his fingers.

He had dug the remnants of his arm into the earth. The exposed bone allowed him to grip the dirt, assisting his forward movement. With each passing second, he drew closer to the pedestal, being mere arm’s length away.

Compared to his prior event, this didn’t seem as terrible. Luke was experiencing more pain; the Elite slime had removed most of his pain receptors, although he should have died there. Luke’s willpower had pushed him onward.

His mind was clouded, but his constitution kept Luke from bleeding out, and he was currently gripping what felt like the pedestal. Calling forth the veil and just as quickly spending his points on healing. He lay on the ground as he felt itching pain completely overwhelm him to the point of losing consciousness again.

Luke sat there once again, just staring at the sky, but this time he had pulled himself upright enough that his back lay along the pedestal.

The rough exterior left him feeling a little prickly, but he ignored it for the moment.

The sky’s darkness seemed exceptionally lovely that day, likely because of the souls that obscured only a minor portion. But there was something different that he noticed when he woke up. Two souls stood out distinctly from the rest.

He attempted to tell the slime souls apart, but he wasn’t able to fully succeed. Luke understood that each soul possessed a unique color, yet no other ideas surfaced in his mind. But the boy decided he would do that at a different time.

Having checked his body for any wounds from the restore. He noted the stump had recovered spectacularly since he had used it to smash the silver slime. Blowing up a bit into mush, just like his right leg. Oh, that quickly reminded him of his right leg, which had been nonfunctional. Luke marveled at the pedestal, wondering at what point it had ever changed, but he pushed that thought aside and could only be thankful for whatever the pedestal was.

Because without the pedestal, he would have been dead, bleeding out at that very moment. The near-death experience still crowded and overwhelmed Luke’s mind, and because he could do nothing but risk his life, every single time he fought something.

He was too worried about the elite slime spawning instead of farming points to get as strong as he could. The cost of his injuries had used up 8 points, which left him with only 5.

Looks like the grey slime gave 3 points. Luke chuckled; at least he had some points left over. But that chuckling quickly stopped at the increased price of his restoration.

The cost of an injury increases along with how serious that injury is. He thought while rubbing his stump. All he knew was that if floor 2 was like that, he wasn’t making it past floor 3 unless he gained a huge spike in his attributes.

However, Luke also observed that he had not earned any title from the elite slime. He couldn’t comprehend how anyone could get strong enough to fly through the floors. And it seemed like it wasn’t only one guy but probably thousands who were ahead of him. The result utterly confused Luke.

I really needed to stop comparing myself to others, Luke thought.

But he needed to continue improving his strength. Power was the only thing he needed to overcome these battles. Luke looked back up at the sky, only to watch the souls shine in the dark; it was oddly satisfying to him. They brought joy to this lifeless place, and he felt deeply connected to them.

The blood and sweat he had poured into killing those slimes had paid off, becoming his power. He finally decided he wanted to catch up to the others and survive. He would need to rely on this power; he had already gotten a title that had to do with controlling energy, and in his case, that seemed like souls.

If souls were his energy, then so be it; he would use these as a stepping stone to become stronger, and his first goal would be to become familiar with controlling it. So with his plan now in full swing, Luke abruptly got up and, without wasting another second before he regretted it, he spent 3 points on soul resonance.

Now, with level 3 soul manipulation, he was hoping he would have an easier time controlling or gaining access to the souls. Luke wanted to test if he could use soul energy once again. This oppressively warm feeling started to wrap and fuzz up in his stomach. Trying to dig deeper and see where this energy could come from. He felt it moving up from his stomach swiftly into his chest. If he hadn’t been in contact with soul energy, he would have freaked out.

He felt a sense of familiarity as he curled his hand. Luke felt excited, and a bright smile completely covered his face. He could use soul energy again, or at least that is what he thought. So, taking it upon himself, Luke stared back into the sky, which seemed to hold around 30 souls. He had to recount a few times; it was actually a bit more difficult than he had thought it would be to estimate. But it seemed low in value. The boy thought he had killed a lot more slimes than that. With Luke constantly having to battle them, he must have been deeply mistaken.

Surprisingly, it felt quite different from when he had first used it. His memory of his previous actions was fuzzy, relying on pure instinct. So when Luke had finally concentrated on that warm feeling in his chest, all that stood within him was a bright blue orb with white lines intermixed throughout it.
