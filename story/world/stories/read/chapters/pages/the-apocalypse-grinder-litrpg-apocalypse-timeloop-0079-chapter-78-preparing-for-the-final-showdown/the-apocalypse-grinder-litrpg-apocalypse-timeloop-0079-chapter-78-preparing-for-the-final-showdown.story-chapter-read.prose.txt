Once he’d allowed the system to drone on about the integration, the first thing Ronan did was read through his status—the detailed version. After that brief bout of duplicate skills, he’d started to earn unique ones again. Although four of the tomes were more duplicates.

That alone made him ponder how skills were distributed. It couldn’t be entirely random. Perhaps there are limits depending on how far into the integration a challenger is? Ronan guessed.

There were quite a few new additions to his status, most of which weren’t immediately useful but would slowly grow in value over time. Especially once he had more freedom to experiment with new classes.

From what it had been when the system first descended upon his world and granted its gift—or curse, depending on one’s perspective—Ronan’s status had ballooned in size and complexity. It was practically an essay now, but that was a reflection of his effort.

He was sure his heritage was working to make it even smoother to understand at a glance, too. For example, the real percentage buffs of cultivation after deep cultivation was factored in were displayed alongside the base percentages.

Speaking of his deep cultivation, he pulled it up for good measure. He wondered what the real increase had been from purchasing tier III. The diminishing stacking effects were annoying.

Deep cultivation III: As a clan which transcends the limits of time and space, the act of cultivation holds a far more significant meaning to those of the Chronos heritage. Maintain 10% more of your progress towards the next cultivation realm for every tier of this enhancement. Cultivation benefits are 25% more effective for every tier of this enhancement.

[Current effects]

Maintain 23% more of your progress towards the next cultivation realm on iteration

Cultivation benefits are 42% more effective

The maths lined up, but that didn’t make him less frustrated. Still, all improvements were worth acquiring. Even the skills that barely helped him in his current situation. He was sure to find a use for them eventually.

First, he needed to beat the tutorial once more. It was barely a challenge now, making Ronan actually excited to see what the next step up in difficulty would hold. Not long now, he reminded himself before he made a rash decision.

Ronan found himself cultivating under the stars once again. He noticed that the shards which went on sale in the marketplace were almost identical each iteration.

That wasn’t necessarily a bad thing. It meant he could continue to push his cultivation forwards, even without obtaining higher leveled shards. However, it did mean that he was yet to obtain even a single soul aspected shard.

His soul cultivation was the only one of the four paths which hadn’t reached the first stage. No use worrying about what I can’t control. I’m sure I’ll find a source of them as I keep pushing onwards.

What he was attempting right now was painful, but rewarding. Before his final battle against Lord Rockmore and his first against Vulparis the Trickster—which he hoped would also end in his victory—Ronan wanted to push for the second stage of tempered body cultivation.

His body currently felt as though it was submerged in a vat of molten metal while being pounded by a hydraulic press. However, a pained glance at his status told him he was nearing his goal.

Half an hour later, the pain subsided and Ronan collapsed in a sweaty, aching puddle on the roof. A satisfied grin was plastered on his face as he stared at the notification that had appeared when he collapsed.

Congratulations! Your hard work and effort have allowed you to reach the first realm of Tempered Body Cultivation!

Tempered Body Cultivation has advanced from [Stone ★] to [Stone ★★]!

Physical damage reduction +6%

Efficacy of vitality, strength, agility, and resistance +10%

He pulled out a bottle of water and downed the entire thing in a few gulps. Then he unwrapped a stale protein bar and finished that in two bites. It wasn’t real sustenance, but it did the job well enough.

He checked his status and saw the slight increase to his health points. A few light jabs and some brief stretches helped him get used to the increase in agility and strength. Perfect, he thought to himself.

With a final look at the beautiful starry sky that watched over the broken city, Ronan clenched his fists and headed towards the stairs. It was time to kill Lord Rockmore.
