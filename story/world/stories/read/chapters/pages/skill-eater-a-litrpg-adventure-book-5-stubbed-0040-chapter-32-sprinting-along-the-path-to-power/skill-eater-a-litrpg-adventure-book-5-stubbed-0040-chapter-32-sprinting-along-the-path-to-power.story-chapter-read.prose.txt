When those words left his lips, Edge’s soul was baptized by flame.

His reactor roared to life like he’d swallowed a dragon, belching a river of incandescent might until his mind knew nothing but apotheosis and blaze. It was far more intense than his last evolution, and the cataclysmic cascade coursing inside him was just the opening act.

When the searing surge reached a crescendo, a second stream of energy joined its infernal twin. Instead of magma flowing through his veins, it felt like he’d mainlined a lightning bolt, crackling along his nerves in an electric stampede.

Ord’s magic saturated every cell in his body, filling him to the brim with no sign of stopping. While a portion was busy reconstructing every fiber of his being, the lion’s share was gathering below his heart, growing denser and denser as it refined his core like a fist squeezing coal into diamond.

He hadn’t been able to process what he was experiencing when Skill-Eater advanced to stage one and stage two, but Edge had come a long way since then. He had grown more aware of his inner world and learned to deal with sensory overload without being overwhelmed. His will had been tempered by his battles with the dark passenger, and he’d taught himself to function despite excruciating pain.

All those hard-won lessons came together in that moment, letting him perceive the mind-boggling torrent of magic raging behind his ribs without losing himself along the way. He sensed mana and Mortium thrumming through the mix, although the bulk was pure potentia—multiple orders of magnitude stronger than when his core cycled up and generated attribute points.

It was too bright to see anything, but he could feel the transformative energy permeating every inch of his inner world, suffusing the components housed in his central chamber, his skill village, and the realm of mist that was home to his Disruption diffusor.

Every aspect of Skill-Eater was absorbing the rich bounty, tension winding tighter and tighter until the process hit critical mass, catalyzing his core’s transformation to the next stage. By now, the fire and lightning were fading, and he could sense his central chamber expanding at a rapid rate. Meanwhile, his reactor and reservoir were changing, growing more complex, powerful, and efficient, all at the same time.

Now that Edge was cognizant of the changes taking place, he was blown away by how amazing every aspect of the device known as a power core truly was—awestricken by the magic that had fused with every iota of his existence. He felt ecstasy and jubilation flowing through his skill bonds as his avatars drank from the excess energy flowing through his core.

He’d lost all sense of time while his metamorphosis was underway, but he had no choice other than to buckle up and enjoy the ride, trusting Mel to protect his body while his mind was away.

Eventually, the pyrotechnics came to an end, leaving Edge standing in a central chamber that had undergone a remarkable renovation. He couldn’t wait to take a tour and examine every change at length, but he had an exhibition match to win, so satisfying his curiosity would have to wait just a little while longer.

He was eager to leave his inner world and leap into the fray, but first, he needed to read the fine print. Hands trembling with excitement, he opened his updates with a mental command and examined Skill-Eater’s gains.

Edge’s jaw hit the floor as he read through the upgrades, which were even better than he’d imagined. He had suspected unlocking the tome would make a difference, given the artifact’s interaction with Overdrive while it was sealed, but he hadn’t expected anything like this. He needed to wrap his head around the details, so he took another minute to process the changes, which would only equal a few seconds in the outside world.

The number of cycles had doubled instead of tripled like before, following a pattern he didn’t even pretend to understand. Regardless of the reason, this was fantastic news. While it would be a long and arduous journey to reach the peak of stage three, it would only require two-thirds of the experience he’d anticipated.

The extra attributes per cycle would certainly come in handy. Seven was a little better than most stage-three cores, which would provide a considerable advantage as time wore on. He already knew how many skill slots he would obtain, but he’d picked up an additional implant point and one more trait slot than he’d guesstimated, the latter of which he planned to take advantage of here and now.

Before equipping his traits, Edge considered the upgrades to his ultimate abilities, which had been turbocharged by unsealing one of the ravenous lord’s relics. It made him greedy to recover the rest, but entertaining such pleasant speculations would have to wait.

He licked his lips as he reread the changes to Extraction, anticipating all the delicious meals to come. Being able to steal skills every fifteen hours instead of twenty was a major upgrade that would let him advance his powers faster than ever, but he was even more pleased that Extracting Rare skills no longer cost Mortium, and the price for Epics had been cut in half. Faster digestion kicks ass too.

It was exactly what he needed to hunt high-stage beasts and streamline his advancement. He could rank up his powers at an accelerated rate and would be less reliant on lifecoins going forward. It made him wonder how many Mortium he would have to sacrifice to steal one of the Legendary or Mythic skills he’d seen in his visions.

Moving on to Overdrive, Edge was astonished that his ultimate had improved across the board. He would gain a staggering sixty Amplification instead of the thirty he’d been expecting, and cutting the cooldown in half was flat-out amazing. The bonus from the tome was great too, extending the extra thirty seconds it provided while sealed to five full minutes of unlimited mana. That would make a world of difference when fighting powerful foes, particularly in large-scale conflicts where he was boosting his allies with auras.

The improvements to Skill Bond were utterly badass. Gaining an across-the-board mana efficiency bonus was a timely improvement. Going all out had grown quite costly as he added more high-rarity skills to his collection and unleashed far more combos than before. On that note, his combos growing more powerful as his avatars’ relationships improved was insanely overpowered, and he couldn’t wait to find out what they could do.

His new ultimate was awesome too. Even if it didn’t benefit him directly, Loan would be an amazing asset when fighting with his friends, enabling tactical advantages he couldn’t even begin to process right now. The first thing that came to mind was Loaning Harden to Rue, which would let the beast start hunting with the crew ahead of schedule.

Last but not least, Foebinder and Magebane growing stronger independently of ranking up Manifest Chain was invaluable in countless ways. The twin chains of oblivion would have an easier time extracting problematic avatars going forward and be even more useful in battle.

Edge was blown away by everything he’d witnessed, and he hadn’t even examined the upgrades to his core’s components, which were certain to be potent too. He forced himself to focus with an effort of will. That can wait until the Clash is over.

When he returned to his body, he raised his gaze to the stands. Sakura started a count when she sensed his evolution was underway. From her raised fingers, he knew eleven seconds had passed while he was away.

It was clear that some of the contenders had realized something strange was going on. Mel’s Wall of Force was covered in cracks, and more projectiles were striking the surface with every beat of his heart.

“I’m back. Hold on for a few more seconds, and I’ll be good to go.”

She grunted her agreement without turning her head—laser-focused on weathering the barrage. Her muscular body was poised to deflect strikes with Extinction when her barrier shattered. She hadn’t told him there was a problem, so he ignored the attacks and trusted his partner to get the job done.

Edge pulled up his menus and equipped both inactive traits at the same time—the one he’d earned from closing the portal to the monsters’ dimension and the one he had created by using the Trait-Mutation Token on Fit and Toned.

While he waited for the System to jumpstart his newest powers, he reviewed their descriptions from memory.

We’ve already established that it wasn’t a natural event, which heavily implies that it was unnatural instead. Monster Slayer wasn’t going to help him win this match unless the Ancient was secretly participating, but Not a Moron was another story. Wait. Fuck that. I’m going to call the trait Brawny, which is the name the System gave everyone else.

Unlike potentia, which permanently changed his body and core, traits granted by the planetary AI could be unequipped, although doing so wasn’t a simple process. That meant they were more like auras in how they functioned—power granted by an outside agent instead of triggering a structural transformation.

Edge felt the System’s magic enshroud him like a cloak before sinking into his skin, adding two new entries to the list of powers at his command. He couldn’t sense any obvious changes from Monster Slayer, but the massive boost to his physical attributes wasn’t exactly subtle.

Strength infused his limbs as his stats shot up, granting him Power, Endurance, Durability, Control, Reflex, and Speed beyond anything he’d known before. His Perception had been enhanced too, which was bound to come in handy during the mayhem he intended to deploy imminently.

“All set,” he called out. Mel’s reply was cut off when a lance of golden energy pierced her force dome, shattering it like glass. Some warriors would have blocked the strike head-on. Others would have tilted their weapon at an angle, deflecting its trajectory to evade the blow.

Mel did neither. Discerning the nature of the attack in a fraction of a second, the guardswoman swung her massive weapon in a flashing arc, severing the spell after judging its mana had assumed physical form. He whistled in appreciation while dodging arrows, then they took off in a dead sprint, following the perimeter wall before the next volley arrived.

It bought Edge the final seconds he needed. His skills had shut off while his evolution was underway, so he reactivated his passives and aura while priming his domain. He had considered using Tidal Stream, but with all the mana filling the air, he decided to go with Maelstrom instead. He had more practice using it—it was resistant to being countered, and the wind would shield them from ranged attacks while knocking his foes off balance.

The audience hadn’t stopped screaming since he evolved—likely because of Vio’s commentary, which was magically muted so the gladiators couldn’t use it to obtain a competitive advantage. That being said, at least a dozen warriors were staring straight at him. They had either realized what was happening or sensed the incredible discharge of mana produced by the process. Either way, the duo was on the radar of some dangerous individuals. If he lowered his guard or made a single mistake, he wouldn’t be walking away.

In a bit of misdirection, he pulled a handful of skill gems out of his belt pouch and stored them in his vault, pretending to fill his new slots. The rules categorized gems as implants instead of consumable items due to their semi-permanent nature. His sleight of hand would explain why he could use a ton of powers no one had seen before without people asking questions he desperately wanted to avoid.

At the same time, he activated Manifest Chain and called upon his stalwart allies, who were ready and eager to fight by his side. His storage rune counted as an implant too, which meant he was free to pull out any weapons he pleased. As Edge summoned the upgraded chains of oblivion, he contemplated how he wanted to use them for the next few minutes.

During the initial stage of the exhibition, countless attacks would be coming in from all sides. With that in mind, he decided to give Foebinder the scapula shield and Magebane the ivory scythe, enhancing their capacity to repel physical attacks and energetic powers, respectively.

The living links wrapped around his biceps and looped behind his head before curving back around. The slack sections near his body limited how much their motion affected his arms, although Foebinder was much better at the technique than its temperamental twin. When his allies were ready, he handed the gear over, effectively adding two more members to the team.

Edge could tell the left chain wanted to try out its new polearm, which Pip and Coal had finished in time for the big fight. Don’t worry, it’s coming as soon as the weaker contenders are eliminated and we’re ready to deal with the true threats.

By now, almost every eye in the crowd was pointed in his direction. He grinned when he heard them chanting his name. Just wait. You haven’t seen anything yet. Before long, you’ll be screaming ‘Mel’ too.

It was one of the most amazing moments of his life, but he didn’t let it distract him. He turned his gaze from the crowd above to the warriors prowling below, almost ready to make his first move. For this fight, he was ready to reveal Dragon’s Greed’s true strength. Since it was expensive, he activated it last, causing a potent layer of mana to coat the mastercrafted glaive.

With that, he was as ready as he could be. “All set!” he called to his partner. “Thanks for buying me time. What’s our play?” While his attention was divided, Melenia had chosen their first victims while watching for incoming threats.

When the guardswoman pointed to a pair of spear-wielding gladiators, Edge matched her pace, and the couple went in for the kill.
