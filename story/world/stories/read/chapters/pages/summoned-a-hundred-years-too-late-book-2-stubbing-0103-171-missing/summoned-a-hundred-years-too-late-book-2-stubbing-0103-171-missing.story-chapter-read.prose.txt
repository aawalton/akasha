Councillor Torak’s residence was located high on the cliff. Carved right into the highest rock face, it could be seen from across the city. The stairs leading up to it were wide and strangely pleasant to climb. Each step was spaced perfectly so that it barely felt like they were going up.

With Cem leading the way, they passed without resistance through multiple guarded doorways hiding lush gardens and sprawling fountains.

“How is Torak so rich?” Alexandra asked.

“This is none of your business,” Cem answered as they walked up another flight of stairs.

“Come on. I’m sure everyone in town knows.” She paused. “Unless it’s something illegal.”

“Commerce and high rank.” Cem scoffed. “Mind your words.”

They strolled into the troglodyte building, crossing a few underground rooms before stopping in front of a door. Cem knocked. He didn’t wait for an answer to push it open.

Torak was pacing around the room. As soon as they came in, he turned his head toward Alexandra.

“Where is my cousin?” he asked, moving toward her.

Alexandra blinked. “Who?”

“My cousin.” The councillor grabbed her shoulders. “Where is she?”

Cem coughed. “Councillor, I believe she is not aware of your relationship with your cousin.”

Alexandra shook him off. “Yeah, that. Also, don’t touch me.”

“I apologize,” Torak said, gathering himself. “Yes, I’m sorry. I let my emotions get the better of me.”

“Aww. You’re making me blush,” Alexandra teased, waving her gloved hand in front of his face. “What do you think?”

Torak’s face reddened, and he took a step back. “That…”

Cem coughed again. “Sir.”

“Right. My cousin. She was last seen leaving Balrum with you. It’s been more than three weeks. She should be back by now.”

Alexandra raised an eyebrow. “You still haven’t told me who your cousin was.” She paused. “Wait. Zeynep?”

There it is.
