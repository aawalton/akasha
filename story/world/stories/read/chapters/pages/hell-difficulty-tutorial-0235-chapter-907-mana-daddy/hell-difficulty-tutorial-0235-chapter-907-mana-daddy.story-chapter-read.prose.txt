Instead of flying back, I decide I’ll just walk through the canyons. Sometimes I boost myself up when I see something that looks like a sidewalk, and stroll along it to get a nice view of the valley carved into the stone around me.

The rivers, greenery, unusual trees, or rock formations. This is the first time I’ve had to take in the sights of the 4th floor of Beyond at a reasonable pace, usually, I just fly by it all.

The fight didn't even take that long. A mere few minutes after weeks of preparation and so much more effort leading up to all this. It all happened so fast that taking it slow now feels refreshing.

I wonder if I'm being observed. I'm well aware of the way the Rulers like to stalk us, along with some of the Handlers, so I sometimes randomly give a middle finger toward the sky. In other cases, I give a middle finger toward a cute fox-like animal that appears nearby, just in case they are observing through its eyes.

I also show a middle finger to the fish in the nearby river.

Of course, I can feel it. My mood keeps improving as I feel it tugging on the corners of my lips. Not even in the slightest do I care that they might have seen me in my weakened state after the fight. Well, maybe a little bit. It would be weird if it didn't annoy me for someone to have seen me in a moment of weakness.

Honestly, does it really matter at all?

At one point, I'm not even using body reinforcement and slip on a rock and fall off the edge of the sidewalk I've been walking on. I don't even try to catch myself or slow my fall, I just absorb the impacts and slightly change my defensive layer.

I let myself bounce off the rocks like a ragdoll, sliding across the dirt, freefalling, breaking a few branches on the way down, and landing on the ground.

Then I stand up, dusting the debris from my manatex clothes, and continue to walk.

From down here, the canyons look so huge, awe-inspiring even. Whenever I allow myself a moment to react in a way my pre-tutorial self would, I just can't get enough of them. Never in my life did I think I would get to see anything like this, and I don't even mean the System and all that. More like places on Earth. Sure, I dreamed as anyone does, but the doubt was always somewhere in the back of my mind?

The thought that I would grow up, find some shitty job to keep myself alive, possibly find someone, barely get by in some shitty apartment, maybe adopt a dog. Day after day, that would slowly devolve into a parade of the same old things.

And yet, here I am.

I'm being careful about saying I wouldn't trade it for anything else, as there might yet be things I can’t yet imagine that I would love to do. But yeah, I cannot currently imagine anything else I’d want in life.

Maybe a bit more of this, less of that, and other such grumblings. But in the end, it's just a trade-off. You get some things and lose others. Such is life.

Damn, I'm really in a weird mood right now.

With a soft chuckle, I boost myself through the air, reaching the warehouse where the head technician is currently sitting just outside the huge door on a simple chair, reading some sort of blueprint.

Noticing me coming closer, he scrambles to his feet and stares at me with wide eyes. Inside the hangar, multiple technicians react too.

So I show him my right forearm and the keys inscribed on it.

“T-THREE!?” he shouts, and then coughs up and repeats, “Three?” He still shakes his head, and his voice turns to a high pitch again, “Three motherfucking KEYS!?”

I give him a shrug, and after giving a wave, I teleport inside to my room in the hangar where I left one of my marks.

I have grown as a person. My mind sharpened, and I have become smarter. So, of course, I have a replacement set of clothes already waiting, as well as a shower I had built.

That need and tug to check my notifications is constantly there, but I'm enjoying it as I move into the shower, using some fancy soap and shampoo. I probably take a bit longer than usual, but I think at this point I deserve it.

Just in case, I point a middle finger toward the ceiling a few times.

Once I dry myself, I watch my figure in front of the mirror while carefully touching one of the permanent burns on my chest and the scars. My last touch is to my forehead, where that laser bored through.

With a shrug, I put a clean set of clothes on myself, not minding that they are a bit more formal than my usual set of casual Earth clothes.

I go outside by teleporting once again, and by this time, all the technicians are gathered around, and I acknowledge each and every one of them by giving a thanks and a nod. I remember them from the work we did on Regalia, their ideas, blueprints, and excitement. Even if some of them just came here to design the cranes, I appreciate it.

It feels a bit off, but I accept their deep bows in turn, and then launch myself off toward the Megacity.

Instead of aiming for something like a helicopter landing pad for people capable of flight, I land near the main gate, where I'm let in without delay.

Just a few seconds after I begin to walk through the city, a demon wearing a suit and glasses joins me, walking by my side.

“I saw what happened, and I just want to say I admire your guts, human.” That's all he says, and a few steps later, he’s gone.

I barely pass a few stores when another presence pops up on my left. “You won me a shitload of shards. The fight was fun!” I manage to catch just a smile on a fur-covered lynthari face before the presence is gone.

“I still think you were just lucky, but fuck it, most of us are, aren't we?” Only a second later, another voice says from my right, “I hope you make it, you beautiful lunatic.”

Thoroughly weirded out by all of this, I teleport away, but a new presence finds me anyway. A man in a suit with a bear face. “I lost a bet,” he says, completely unbothered. “Part of the payout is that I have to call you mana daddy. So here we are. Good job, mana daddy.”

Then he’s gone just as quickly as he appeared.

I almost want to throw an attack at the next presence that approaches, but this one hangs onto my arm and pulls me in closer to her. Just a glance tells me it’s Ruby, masked in her fake Handler form.

“They can't listen now, so you can speak freely. And yes, more of them wanted to barge in and annoy you, so you owe me,” she says simply, pulling me through Megacity toward the headquarters of the Primordial Knights.

“I would call you nice and all that if you hadn’t broken my arm just now,” I say.

“Some time ago I overheard your group talking about your Constitution. Naturally, I went to Earth to research what a hummingbird actually is. As it turns out, it’s a tiny, delicate creature with an embarrassingly frail constitution. Did you know their hearts beat up to 1,260 times per minute in flight? They live in a state of perpetual panic where failing to eat every ten to fifteen minutes results in immediate death.”

“I inherited my mana deficiency from them.”

“Sure you did. You are lucky I'm so good at controlling my powers, otherwise, you and everyone else on this entire floor would’ve been dead in the presence of my true body.”

“Lissandra would dig herself out of her grave just to insult you if you couldn’t manage that basic level of control. And even the Rulers would die in your true body's presence?”

“Yes, even that bitch Lust, if her pets didn't keep setting up barriers for her.”

“I didn't-”

“So you,” she shakes me a little, “should be thankful. And I'm sorry for breaking your arm.” She sighs. “You did really well out there.”

“As always.”

She gives a smile. “Almost as always. You and your sister still don't realize how troublesome Starless Sky's revival is.”

“I think with all that’s been happening to us, it's a bit of a dick move to expect us not to care for ourselves first over any trouble we might cause for others.”

Looking at me for a moment, she nods. “Fair enough.”

We take a step, and instead of covering normal distance, I find us standing in front of the entrance to the Primordial Knights headquarters.

“No one will annoy you here, so take your time. I’ll keep an eye on you and interfere if I must. And select carefully. I won't give you any advice, I know you’d only reject it anyways.” She waves her hand, and as she takes a step, the air around her wavers just the tiniest bit before she’s gone.

Giving my third shrug of the day, I use my mana signature to pass through the defenses. I think out of all the top guilds in the Beyond, the Primordial Knights have the weakest defenses around their buildings.

It's like they're just hoping someone will attack.

Using Wraith Dance a few times, I pass and weave between people, opening a few doors along the way until I reach the garden that’s still restricted to anyone the guildmaster hasn’t invited. It is something Nyssa set up previously, and I haven't changed it.

The garden isn’t large, it’s framed on three sides by buildings. In the middle of it stands a single tree, its trunk surrounded by a circular patch of grass and a lone bench. Said tree is pale white, its thick stump scarred by damage that has clearly split it in two. One half remains dead and hollow, while the other half grows into a new trunk, living and strong. The tree has a scent to it. Like freshly cut grass with a faint hint of citrus.

I sit on that lone bench and take in the view, which reveals Megacity below and the valley framed by the mountains. The square opening looks almost like a portal, a window cutting through the surrounding buildings.

Then I go through my notifications.
