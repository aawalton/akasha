Holy shhhh...Bad brain! stop making me picture vulgar stuff! It is a spoon and nothing else! That is a lot of grip strength for such slender fingers, though.

I didn't really plan on becoming a cultivator, but I will have a lot of time to kill on the road, so maybe it's worth getting a pointer or two, since both Arctic and I recently completed the initial stage as Majusculus tasked us.

The pair had yet to learn any method or technique that would allow them to utilize their reserves. Emi found herself able to keep partying full throttle night after night with nary a consequence, and Arctic put the newfound increase in his endurance to good use, as he started taking extra shifts to earn some pocket money that he wouldn't have to add to the travel fund.
