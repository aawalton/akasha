To say that the abyss stared back into him would have been too much of a cliché, perhaps, but something akin to that did happen. Not at first. No, it took a while of metaphorical staring into the vast nothingness of the void to even glimpse even a hint of... something. Focusing on a hint of movement, Luke stared until he made sense of it. A wisp of a thread. Was that it? Yes, he thought so. It didn't come closer, exactly, but the more he willed his mind to perceive the object, the more it became apparent that it was indeed a thread. Something about it was wrong, very wrong. With no color or light, but more importantly, without concepts connected to it, the only way he could describe it was that it was dead. A fragment of a thread drifting through the vast void. It tugged at Luke's heartstrings to see it out there alone. He knew it didn't have feelings or any sort of sentience, but he still couldn't help but feel a strange emptiness when watching it.

It didn't take long before he saw more of them, but something changed before he had the time to consider what it meant that they floated around out there. A consciousness approached, one he knew well. The void being had found him. Since Luke wasn't in the void itself, but rather looked out at it from within The Greater System Weave, he was safe from direct contact. At least he thought so. As long as he didn't cross over, that thing couldn't get him. Even telling himself that, it was difficult to stay put. Size was a strange concept out there on the rim of reality. The void being wasn't big, per se, but the vastness of its sense of self still made Luke feel like an ant in comparison. Pitching his own will against it would be like a fly attacking an open flame. The mere idea of it almost made him burst out laughing.

The void being seemed to pick up on it. Mirth came off it in waves. Its interest in Luke was plain to see, but this time, it didn't attempt to make him cross over. Instead, it turned to focus on the depths of the void. In the far, far reaches of his mind, a different entity stirred. One of joy and playfulness. This one, he knew well.

Smiling, he called out. "Smudge!"

Smudge approached and passed the void being with a huff, almost as if it wasn't even worth consideration. To Luke's surprise, it did nothing to stop the little voidling from passing, and he moved all the way to the edge of the void, where it touched The Greater System Weave. The critter let out a whimper as he charged up and down the length of the weave. Wherever he came close to it, threads weakened and frayed. That wasn't good. Not good at all.

"You can't pass over," Luke said. Said was a strange word to describe the sending of intention and emotion through the weave and beyond, but in the context at hand, he didn't have a better one. Smudge was stuck out there, unable to get back in himself. That's why the void being hadn't made a move to stop Luke's companion. He couldn't let that stand. The decay of the weave near Smudge was a problem that needed solving, but to do that, he needed his friend in the weave, not outside of it.

About to reach outside the weave, Luke stopped himself. This wasn't why he was here. Saving Smudge from eternity in the void was important, yes, but the reason he'd come was to find Hannah. It wasn't fair to Smudge, who was doing everything in his power to escape the void, despite not seeing anything that could be described as progress, but Luke needed to make an attempt. Perhaps the two could even go hand-in-hand.

"Hey, Smudge! Have you seen Hannah?"

The answer didn't come in spoken words, of course, but it was immediate. Smudge tilted his head to the side, very much like the image of a dog considering something. A moment later, Luke got the sense of an answer, and that answer was 'no'. Even so, Luke expanded his senses into the void, past even the void being. Something glinted out there. A thread! And not just any thread, but an echo thread. It was her, it was really Hannah! Or, well, a path to her, at least. In a stroke of terrible judgment, Luke reached out, pushing through the crumbling barrier between the weave and the void, so he could push a thread of mana through the nothingness.

Not hesitating for even the briefest of moments, the void being lashed out with its mind. Luke was quick to reach the echo thread, and the moment he did so, he got a clear sense of Hannah and even her will.

Not yet.

That's what she sent him. Not yet. What the hell was that supposed to mean? Did she want to be in the void? Did the presence of the echo thread even mean she was in the void itself, or was the dungeon somewhere in there? Was that even possible? That was about as far as he got before the void being cut Luke off from The Greater Weave.

From one moment to the next, all the wonderful emotions, connections, and people represented in the weave fell away into a nothingness so deep Luke couldn't understand how he was still even there. He hadn't moved from his safe position, but the extended thread was enough. The void being used it to bring Luke over and into its grasp. A single thread moved behind him, floating in the void, cut off from his home, The Greater System. Mana drained from every inch of Luke's body, and there was nothing he could do to stop it. If this carried on for much longer, it would be like bleeding out, or perhaps like he was at the bottom of the ocean without even a speck of air to draw into his lungs.

His first instinct was to withdraw and return to his own body, but he found that he couldn't. The void being's hold kept him from moving even on the metaphysical plane. This time, Guide hadn't stepped in, and even in his frantic state, Luke could see why. Last time, if he'd gone through, it would have meant the void coming into the system. Now, it was the opposite. Luke, fool as he was, had gone over the edge himself. What was one life compared to the lives of everyone in the system? Nothing. It was as if Guide, by staying away, told him in no uncertain terms that he wasn't special.

The void being reached out with its mind, buffeting Luke's consciousness. It was like being struck over the head with a war hammer over and over again, the pressure building until it felt like he was about to pop. Even as he pushed back, he knew that he wouldn't withstand the attack for long, and it even felt like his very being was unraveling just being in the void, in the presence of that being. Now, it was picking him apart like a child pulling the legs off an ant out of curiosity more than any real sense of evil. If he was going to get away, it would have to be soon, before he lost every ounce of strength and power. With a roar, Luke did just that and found just how insignificant his own power was in the presence of this thing. It was like throwing pebbles at a god, but still, Luke pushed. And pushed, forcing his will to burrow into the void being as the thread dangling from his back withered and disintegrated little by little.

Not giving up, Luke pushed outward with several threads of mana to wrap them around... something. His opponent didn't have a body in the traditional sense, but neither did Luke in this space. Back when he thought he'd destroyed The Fallen Shepherd, this creature of the void had shown itself as a large, inhuman head floating in the darkness of space, but that wasn't real, just a projection of sorts. Even so, Luke found that his threads connected and found what he could only describe as more of the void. For something to exist in this realm, of course, they would have to assimilate themselves, or maybe they had been born in it. Either way, the being was full of it. The void coursed through it like mana through Luke, and the moment he touched it, that void rushed into him. A mistake. What the hell had he thought doing that would accomplish? Luke pulled back, but it was too late. Even with his mind floating out in all directions, as if it was about to scatter, he could follow that small piece of the void enter his mana channels and rush inward, heading to the core of his being, the Meta-Heart.

A powerful pull yanked Luke from the void being's grasp, and Luke found himself surging back to The Greater System Weave. His thread had been cut, and he slammed into it like a wall, as if he was no longer part of it. Like Smudge, it would not let him enter. The voidling was by his side.

"Did you save me, boy?" Luke asked as he prepared himself. Now out of that thing's grasp, his mind stabilized, and with it his will. How Smudge managed it was a question for another time. Now, they needed to solve the problem in front of them. They needed to find a way back into the system.

For Luke, brute force had always been a good first option. What he was about to attempt would not please Guide, but he didn't care. Weaving his threads into a spear in front of his hands, Luke was about to thrust it into the wall that kept him out, but he thought better of it and instead went with Needle of Life. If you wanted to go back in and alter a weave, you needed precision and care, not brute force.

The void being hadn't pursued them. Instead, it held back, waiting like it expected Luke to do something funny. Maybe he was. Steadying himself, he used Needle of Life to push a thread into the wall. At first, getting through felt impossible. Refusing to give up, Luke dove into the task with all his will and focus, pushing with every ounce of his being.

"Give way!" he sent. "I am returning!"

The wall gave. Luke wasn't surprised. Surprise would mean he hadn't expected it to work, and if he didn't expect it to work, it never would have. So, safe in the knowledge that the needle would make it through, he pushed, readying himself for what would come next. The tiniest of openings was all he needed.

"Get in!" he sent to Smudge, but found that he wasn't there. Instead, he'd placed himself between Luke and the void being, which was now approaching like a hungry beast having smelled a feast. After all, it might see that opening as an invitation.

Having just found Smudge again after so long, Luke refused to let his companion sacrifice himself. Not again. Reaching out with a thread of mana, Luke went through the opening and rejoined The Greater System, pulling Smudge along. Heaving, he got his companion through and then set to mending what he'd destroyed. The tear in the weave would be enough for the void to seep in. Even now, the fraying grew much more intensive, and the nothingness crawled in, destroying everything and anything it touched.

Cursing, Luke set to sewing. Four threads of mana weren't enough, so he added four more, then four again. It was more than he'd ever attempted without a near infinite source of mana, but he didn't care. Stitching with Needle of Life, mending what he'd done, Luke fought with his mind to restore the weave before the being forced its way in. Smudge whimpered, but Luke ignored him. Luke could do it. He knew he could. There was no doubt in his mind. He used his own threads of mana, but also used Manipulate Thread to make use of those around him. Only the threads of The Greater System itself would be enough to keep the void being out.

In the end, it worked. Luke closed the wound in The Greater System, healing what he himself had done to it before more of the void, or the void being itself, could make it through. Even after all he'd suffered, he thought that it had been almost too easy. The void being hadn't been serious about breaking through. That was Luke's suspicion, but he wasn't sure. What he was sure about was that Smudge was back. Now the problem to fix was the fact that Smudge's mere presence seemed to deteriorate the threads around him.

"C'mere, boy," Luke said, waving Smudge closer. "Let's see if we can do something about that before you end up being the one to destroy The Greater System Weave."
