The second Aspect Henry upgraded was Lifeweaving of the Trickster, and this time, it had been a fascinating process to witness.

Lifeshaper’s Form—the cornerstone of the Aspect—was a pretty fragmented skill. Or at least, it looked like it was when one read its description. But underneath all of the passives and skills, as he sensed the part of his soul that was responsible for it, it only actually had two distinct and pretty even parts: one was for shapeshifting and camouflaging, and one was for healing. So when Henry had started the process, he wasn’t sure which one of the many actives and passives was going to be affected and empowered. He didn’t know if only one of them would change, or if all would be upgraded. But when the process was done, the results turned out to be a little obvious, in retrospect. After all, the skill was centered around healing and shapeshifting, so it was those two abilities that had taken the bulk of the upgrade, propelling themselves upward and if Henry was right… shapeshifting might have taken a bigger portion of the pie.

Lifeweaving of the Trickster (B) - Level 1

Lifeshaper’s Forms

…

Shapeshift: Alter and reshape your physical form, molding your limbs, organs, and body into complex forms and structures. Well-practiced forms can be accessed swiftly. You may gradually adjust your overall size, mass, and proportions. The drawbacks of smaller forms are significantly reduced. Maximum Mass: 820,000 kg

Life Surge: Expend mana and Lifeblood to release a regenerative surge that restores health, purges ailments and foreign concepts, and accelerates soul recovery. It can be channeled to others with touch, and for a greater mana cost, the effect is greatly empowered.

…

Henry quickly scanned through the changes as he munched on some eels from his perch on top of a nearby reef. This one was the telekinetic kind, so it offered him nothing.

Lesser Shapeshift became simply Shapeshift, he noted Which means I might be able to actually take a human form instead of creating a shell through Mimicking Shell.

Henry had to stop chewing for a second as he considered that. Did he want to take back his human form? He’d gotten used to being a kraken and frankly, the many arms were pretty damn practical. On the other hand, a human form made through Shapeshift would bypass the weaknesses of the shells he’d been creating. With a full shapeshift, he wouldn’t need to create his own mana channels or worry about the durability of the shell. It’d still be his body. Which would be mightily useful if they found themselves in human lands on their search for the metallic orbs. After all, a powerful enough spell could shatter his shell, and then he’d be exposed to the world. Changing his Identify tag with the skill and with mana signature manipulation would not be enough to hide his identity if his eight.

I guess I’ll think about it. And anyway, the skill might be difficult to use, he thought as he eyed his new maximum mass and his eyes glimmered. Could he make myself into an eighty-two thousand tons human?

That’d be terrifying, he chuckled. He’d be a walking, talking giant. That could be interesting to see. Tough that reminded him of another point. Henry should consider working on the wings, soon. If he had time for it. With him being able to move himself with Telekinesis, the need for a flying solution had dropped in priority, though he’d keep some seagull corpses just in case.

Oh well, we’ll see, he thought as he turned his attention to the changes of Life Surge next. While Shapeshift had gotten the most of the upgrade, Life Surge had not slacked. Not even close. Considering his recent experience with strains and the A-rank seagull, the new inclusion of soul healing and the ability of purging foreign essence were god-sends. Henry was pretty damn happy with how the upgrade turned out.

Glancing up, Henry took a moment to check on Maurice before he got started on his next Aspect upgrade. The crab was still deep in meditation. Henry considered moving them up to the surface, but with the swarm being scattered, he didn’t mind continuing down here.

There was just something about deep waters that appealed to him, nowadays.

Turning his attention back to his soul, Henry funneled the next Aspect the fuel it needed. Now that he had some practice, coaxing the energies of the tokens was easier, and within the hour, he was done.

Dancing Shadows of the Trickster (B) - Level 1

Trickster’s Shadow

Create a clone formed of ink and lifeblood that follows your commands and can be controlled remotely via yourself or through your Octominds. You may perceive through its senses, use skills through it, and every clone can be used as a relay to extend your range of influence. A connection to a clone can be weakened, but it cannot be fully severed so long as both parties are within the same plane of existence. You can teleport with any Trickster’s Shadow you can perceive, taking its place while it takes yours. The further the clone is, the longer and the costlier the teleportation will be.

B-rank: The distance before the connection between clones starts deteriorating is dramatically increased. The number of clones that can be maintained all at once is greatly increased.

Henry eyed the description, hummed in thought, then started casting. The limit of this new kind of clones had been around seventy Trickster’s Shadows before the upgrade to B-rank. It was a lot more than the older blood clones, which he could only sustain a dozen, but lesser than the two-hundred ink clones he’d used for scouting. But considering the Shadows were a mix of both, seventy had been a fair number.

That was the benchmark. Seventy Trickster’s Shadows. So Henry kept casting and counting. He soon got to seventy, but he could tell he still had more in him. He just needed to recover some mana and for that, he popped a Lifeblood Orb in his beak than continued, until he reached his limits at around a hundred and fifty lifeblood clones.

Well, that’s pretty good, I guess, he thought, looking around from atop of his perch as, spread out across the sea, a hundred and fifty inky, flitting Shadows hovered ominously, like a swarm of underwater demons.

Henry was sure now that the ability was at B-rank, the number would swell fast. For now, he spread the clones about. He already had a couple still up on the trees, and he could tell that the change had already cascaded to them. But later, he’d think of how to use these new clones. He had to test the range between them first, and if it was wide enough, he might do something interesting with it.

At first, the upgrade had seemed a little underwhelming to Henry. But now that he was considering it, he could see how useful it could become and clearly, his Octominds seemed to agree. The ghostly octopuses swam around the clones, examining and poking them, and Henry… found his gaze swiveling from the clones to the Octominds.

What if all of those clones were as powerful as the Octominds’ projection? Right now, the new clones were around fifty levels lower than him when he identified them. And while the Octominds didn’t show anything when Identified, he’d put them somewhere near the top of C-rank. If not B-rank in strength. Henry wondered if one day he’d field a whole army just as strong as he was. Something like the Fractalkin evolution he’d been offered.

Oh well, he thought. That’s a project for another day. For now, it was taking most of his mental effort and the hidden support of nearly half of his Octominds to control all of his clones. It was challenging, but once they were in position and had their roles, they could act as sentries and it should become more manageable. Plus, it was bound to get easier as both Aspects evolved. Henry was pretty sure the Octominds still had room to grow, and he was about to upgrade their Aspects.

That should give him a decent cognitive boost.

Still, Henry was a little awestruck. The range he’d be able to cover with them was becoming gigantic. And considering he could switch places with them on a whim…

I’m about to have my own teleportation network, Henry realized, rubbing his arms together with glee. Eventually, I’d be able to have them all around the world, and just hop from one to the next.

The possibilities were endless. But he’d come back to it later. For now, he stationed them all over the place, then hid them. Maurice was still out of it, so Henry might as well continue his work.

This one barely took half an hour. And immediately, Henry could sense the relief as the strain from managing all of his clones suddenly lightened.

Thaumaturgy of the Many-Faced Trickster (B) - Level 1

Council of the Outer Minds

Passive:

Your consciousness unfolds into a network of sentient projections—your evolved Octominds—each a sliver of self orbiting you like sentinels. Their presence sharpens your awareness, deepens your insight, and augments your cognitive speed and casting prowess.

The Octominds can act on preset triggers or tactical intuition: subtly guiding your body to evade, countering hostile magic, or casting assigned spells and skills. You may bind abilities to each, allowing specialization—to heal, to navigate, to act as a decoy or to defend, and much more. They act near-independently, yet in perfect, seamless harmony with your core self: one mind thinking, many executing.

B-rank: The Octominds’ projections are empowered further, significantly increasing their durability and the speed at which they recover when destroyed.

Around him, the forms of the Octominds shuddered and slowly, they grew denser. They hovered about him, watching their surroundings.

Henry wondered how durable they were now. He hadn’t even had the time to test their limits. He’d only gotten them hours ago, and already, they stepped into the next rank, thanks to their Aspects upgrading.

I guess it’s fair, considering the System had a hand in the evolution, Henry mused. Glancing about, Henry wondered what he should do. With no eels or predators in sight, he could take a nap or rest. But he was full of energy still. He’d just evolved. And now, he had a swarm’s worth of cores waiting to be processed.

Processed, huh, Henry thought, rubbing the back of his head. I guess I might as well transform some of them into levels.

His Aspects were all finally in B-rank, and there was really no reason for him to stick to the first level of B-rank. So Henry just got started. He began consuming them for levels while waiting for Maurice to finish up. He consumed dozens, then hundreds. The raw power he could feel flood through him and through his limbs was like liquid bliss. At this point, it was mostly stats, but it wasn’t a negligible amount of them. Still, he did consume a lot of cores. Henry went through a whole hundred of them for a couple of levels and at some point, he just decided to stop. Specifically when it took nearly three-hundred cores for a single level. It became clear that he needed high B-ranks and maybe even low A-ranks to progress with any speed, so he saved the rest.

They should be worth a few A-Rank tokens when converted, and eventually, Henry will find a use for them.

A-ranks, huh, Henry thought. He wondered if he was ready for those. At least he’d have the spar with Velistraine soon, and that should give him a clue. He could also check on that blue lobster near the ruins.

Pulling up the level-up notification, Henry felt good. Things had gone well. All that work with Arisia was bearing fruits, and Henry hoped Maurice would get the B+ evolution as well. While he didn’t think that it was that special in and of itself as they would likely all be equalized at A-rank anyway, he was curious what the change would look like in his friend. Plus, as beings halfway between B and A-rank, they’d be twice as safe while traveling.

...

Veilwatch Kraken (B) - Level 37 achieved!

7x Stat points awarded.

All stats have been empowered by 3 points each

Wait… what?

Henry blinked as he read through the notifications. While he was still earning a free point every five levels, now whenever he gained ten levels, he was gaining a point in every stat.

So Perception and Dexterity are also getting a boost, he summarized. Nice… this along with the evolution will make sure every stat keeps growing evenly. Actually, can I buy some stat points? Can I make them myself? It seems like the menu for that is gone as well. But I bet I could figure it out.

A nice change, all in all, as Henry tested the speed and reflexes of his arms by casually sparring with two of his Octominds, he sensed a sharp claw poke at his inner arm, Henry opened up his grip and watched as the fiery drive sputter out in the eyes of the level hundred crab. Maurice stared around, arms hanging limply at his side as he took in the sea of shredded flesh being nibbled on by weaker fish before he looked up at him.

“[Where’d the eels go? I left you alone for barely an hour!]”
