Aira, Lila, and Al sit around a small fire in an unfamiliar landscape. The stars above are different, the air carries scents they've never encountered before. Shardforger rests across Aira's lap, all four Shard slots now filled with glowing crystals. The journey has visibly changed them all—there's a confidence in Lila's posture that wasn't there before, a serenity in Aira's eyes. Al sits nearby, his silhouette somehow different in the firelight, though he remains silent.

Lila: sorting through a stack of leather-bound journals "By the fox's tail, looking back at these old logs makes me feel like we've lived three lifetimes already."

Aira: smiling "It does feel like another world ago, doesn't it? Me not being able to speak to anyone, finding you, losing you, bringing you back, all the stuff that happened later..."

Lila: "Cold as the cliffs, I still can't believe we made it out of that tree prison in Ziemrot! And now here we are—entire realms away from all that."

Al makes a gesture that might be agreement, his movements more deliberate than they once were

Aira: running her fingers along Shardforger's handle "I'm glad we kept these logs through it all. Even when we were racing against time."

Lila: "Got more cracks than a cliffside, these early entries. Memory isn't always perfect, you know? Some details just wash away with time."

Aira: nodding thoughtfully "That's why I think it's worth taking time now to revisit the first part of our journey, clean up those logs while we have this moment of peace."

Lila: "Solid as oak, that idea. Besides, I keep finding notes we scribbled about things that happened much later. Talk about getting ahead of ourselves!"

Al nods emphatically, pointing at one of the journals with what seems like particular interest

Aira: laughing "See? Al thinks it's a good idea too. It's settled then."

Lila: looks toward the horizon "Our tale's been growing like wild bramble. Might need some pruning before we share the rest, wouldn't you say?"

Aira: "Indeed. We have all the drafts for what came after Lamcada, but it would be good to build up a proper edited backlog before continuing the story."

Lila: stretching her arms above her head "Sharp as thorns, you are! Plus, gives us time to figure out how much to tell about what happened in Veskai. And with Ainorrh..." her voice trails off meaningfully

Al makes an expressive gesture that somehow perfectly conveys dramatic intensity

Lila: laughs "Exactly, Al. Exactly."

Aira: hefting Shardforger with a knowing smile "Some stories are better told when you're far, far away from where they happened."
