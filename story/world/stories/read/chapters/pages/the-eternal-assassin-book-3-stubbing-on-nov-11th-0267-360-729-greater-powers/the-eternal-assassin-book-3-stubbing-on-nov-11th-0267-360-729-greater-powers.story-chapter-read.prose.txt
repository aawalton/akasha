Rather than stick around and lose their lives like a handful of their comrades had, the wizards took off immediately after depositing their fighting force between the lich and the coastline. There was no sign of Eupher or his two greater revenants just yet, but Asher had no doubt he’d show up soon enough.

This close to the ocean, the tall, dry grasses and flat land that had been everywhere in the quarantine zone had been replaced with small, rolling hills and scattered trees filled with tiny fruits that reminded him of crab apples. Samantha looked more than a little excited by the addition, though she was torn from her examination of a nearby one when Groln clapped his hands together.

“We don’t have much time, so we’re keeping this simple! Stick with your team, and stay alive. Asher, you and your team go after the lich, seeing as you’re the only one who can put him down. The rest of us will destroy the greater revenants and get you a shot at him.”

“Got it,” Asher said, taking his team and bringing them aside while the others started planning their own strategies. “Everyone feeling okay? Moxy, how are your hands?”

“I hate to say it, but my gut was right earlier,” she admitted. With a grimace, she held up her blackened fingers. Her hands were like dark claws, and she flapped them about pointlessly as she growled. “I’m healing as fast as I can, but it’s just not fast enough. I can’t even make a fist right now, let alone throw an Air Blast. I’m pretty certain there was something unnatural about that woman’s flames beyond their intense heat, but it’s not like we can ask her.”

“I’m patched up from the fight and ready to go,” Rulfar said as he unsheathed his great axe and stood tall, dwarfing the rest of them.

“And I didn’t take any injuries, so I’m more than ready,” Samantha added, her gaze hard as she stared off in the direction the lich should be arriving from any minute.

“Kroult retains his stance that he doesn’t want to come face-to-face with any of the other liches, which means it’ll just be the three of us,” Asher said, shaking his head. “Though I admit I’m not a huge fan of the idea…”

“Hey, Asher,” Mako called out, walking over to them with Cora and Jameston at his side. “Mind if we join you? If you’re going up against a lich with a Mind element, you’re going to want a Mind element of your own playing defense. Even with those fancy rings Groln lent you, there’s no harm in doubling down. The lich has had a thousand or so years to perfect its abilities. Better safe than sorry.”

“That’s a good point,” Asher said, smiling at Mako as he motioned for the Eight Crests’ team to join them. “And with Moxy out of commission, we could use the added hands.”

“I can’t really fight, but I’ll do what I can to support from the sideline,” Jameston projected into all of their minds, causing Samantha and Rulfar to jump. “And don’t worry about protecting me, I have the Steel and Life elements. I’m rather hard to kill.”

“We’ll still take whatever help you can give,” Samantha said, giving Jameston a warm smile. “With the artifacts Groln gave us and your own skills, we’ll just have to hope it’s enough.”

Despite their dual layers of defense, Asher mentally switched his Complete Resilience’s immunity to the Mind element. Unlike the others he actually had three layers of defense thanks to Complete Resilience’s first-tier effect, but he wasn’t taking any chances with a lich capable of invading the minds of others. Even if Eupher couldn’t force them to turn around and start killing one another, the last thing they needed was to be hit with a blinding migraine out of nowhere in the middle of battle.

While Mako and Cora gave the rest of the team a quick run down on what they were both capable of, Asher took a quick glance at his interface, noting all the improvements he’d gained from the last few fights.

The fact that all fifteen of his primary skills were out of the twenties filled him with no end of excitement, and he dismissed his interface after confirming Astral Dip and Sever the Thread as his next two skills closest to their third evolutions. Going to turn his focus back on Mako, Asher blinked when he felt the slightest of tremors through the ground beneath his feet. He thought he’d imagined it at first, but when the sensation continued and hundreds of birds took to the air from the surrounding trees, cawing a warning to one another in panic, his heart sank as he realized it wasn’t just his imagination.

Something was coming. Something big.

What didn’t make sense was the fact that they couldn’t see anything. Anything capable of shaking the very earth with such force had to be massive, but beyond the screeching birds, Asher couldn’t make out anything above the treeline. His brow furrowing, he turned toward Mako.

“One of the greater revenants still standing is The Warden, right? Isolation, Iron, and Strength, based on what Groln told us? Do you think an Isolation element could give someone a skill to hide themselves and their allies from sight?”

“Can’t say I have any experience with the element, but even if it can’t, they have a powerful wizard on their side,” Mako pointed out, moving a few feet away from the group and sucking in a deep breath. Aiming toward the area the lich was supposed to be coming from, he let loose a powerful scream that tore up the ground in front of him and knocked down a few of the smaller trees before expanding and stretching off into the distance. Rather than simply fade away, however, something unusual happened.

The shockwave of noise hit something that wasn’t there.

Asher’s eyes widened when a colossal being standing what had to be nearly ten stories tall shimmered and came into view as Mako’s sonic attack washed over it. It was a transparent human made of pale blue energy, mimicking the undead nestled safely within its chest as it strode forward one step at a time, each one carrying it what looked like an entire city block. On each of its shoulders stood an additional undead. One of whom wore a crisp uniform covered in blood, his face still filled with a bit of color as if he’d only been turned into an undead within the last few hours. And on The Giant’s other shoulder…

Stood the lich.

Wrapped in a white robe that was strangely clean, Eupher pointed with a staff toward the distant ocean, twin flames burning a brilliant gold within his eye sockets as he ordered his minion forward. He didn’t seem concerned at the fact that their camouflage had been disrupted. Not only that, he didn’t seem concerned about the coalition’s presence in the slightest, barely even sparing them a passing glance down at the ground before turning his focus back toward the horizon. As insulting as it was, Asher couldn’t really blame him.

The Giant was about to crush all of them underneath one massive, glowing foot in just a few seconds.

Before Asher could so much as shout a warning to his team, Groln let loose a roar almost as loud as Mako’s sonic attack before leaping up into the air with so much force the ground erupted beneath his feet. Groln rocketed toward The Giant’s torso, the very fabric of reality seeming to twist and harden around his body as that strange, unexplainable weight that all greater demons seemed to have manifested. Like a living cannonball, he crashed into the dead center of The Giant’s glowing chest, not only stopping the thing in its tracks, but somehow knocking it backwards and off its feet.

Asher stared in utter shock, at a complete loss for words as the ten-story-tall glowing construct of energy crashed to the ground hard enough to nearly knock the rest of them off their feet. He’d known Groln was strong and a greater power in his own right, but this was the first time he’d actually seen the man and his Demon element in action.

Before he could grow too excited, however, things took a turn for the worse. Aiming himself down and looking like he was going to finish the job all on his own, Groln suddenly froze in place, his entire body turning into a rigid statue as if bound by invisible chains. The greater revenant that had been riding on the giant construct’s shoulder landed onto Groln’s frozen form, letting out a wild laugh as the two of them stood there, suspended in the air.

“I always wondered which of the two of us was stronger!” The Warden shouted loud enough for the rest of them to hear. “I guess I have my answer!”

“Let’s go!” Groln’s teammate who looked like he was a sentient statue made from granite shouted before causing a pillar of rock to burst out of the ground that sent him flying up toward his captain. The final teammate, the woman with the blue skin and gills, summoned a geyser underneath her feet, launching herself right after him. Asher was beyond curious as to how exactly the two of them were going to rescue Groln while he was suspended in the air like that, but unfortunately, his attention was drawn elsewhere.

Mainly by The Giant slowly pushing himself back onto his feet.

“We’ll keep that thing pinned down, you deal with the lich!” Amanda ordered, not waiting for his response as her body began glowing brighter than ever before. Sucking in a deep breath, Amanda’s body all but vanished as a streak of light shot up into the sky, quickly disappearing among the clouds. Within seconds, a second, blazing sun appeared in the sky, and Asher’s face paled as he finally learned just where Amanda had gotten her moniker from. It seemed he wasn’t the only one who was concerned either, as rather than place the lich back upon his shoulder, The Giant scooped Eupher up and hurled the lich away from the battle, sending the undead flying through the sky and closer to the ocean. As their surroundings grew brighter and the temperature started to rise from the falling calamity, Asher turned and shouted at his friends.

“Into the rift, we’re going after him!”

He didn’t get any arguments. The moment everyone was secure, Asher leapt onto Samantha’s back and held on for dear life as the hunter’s wings snapped out and she rocketed away from the clashing of titans behind them. Asher had understood on a logical level that greater powers who specialized in combat were capable of devastating feats of strength unlike anything regular people could ever hope to achieve.

But seeing them fight was something else.

He was forced to shield his eyes when the tiny sun Amanda had become crashed down into The Giant’s glowing form, erupting into a blinding flash of white and blue light as their energies mixed and The Giant was sent flying backward a second time. The shockwave emitted swept over them hard enough that it nearly knocked Samantha out of the sky, and she cursed as she righted herself and threw a glance back over her shoulder.

“That was Amanda?!”

“That was Falling Sun,” Asher corrected. Shaking his head, he tried to put the incredible display of power behind them and focus on the task at hand. The Giant had thrown the lich hard enough that they were barely going to reach him before he hit the shoreline, and he gritted his teeth when he realized they weren’t going to have much time to stop him. He didn’t even know why the lich reaching the ocean was so bad, but Amanda and Groln had made the results of such an event occurring quite clear.

If they failed to stop him in time, millions of innocent lives would be lost.
