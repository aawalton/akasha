Analysis of Holding Syl - Restricted to Venzani Elders

As some of you know, we were recently made aware of this newly minted Holding due to the presence of one of our own.

Preliminary analysis indicates that they are growing at an accelerated rate, the Holders focusing almost entirely on their own growth while competent retainers handle almost all facets of the operation. No indications of intentional outreach to any Clan connections, nor use of Clan advantages to ensure progress.

Their local pond has interesting, and rarely seen, goods that are of significant interest to many Clans, the current trade outreach shows promise and will likely need a more established hand in the coming years.

Investigations do indicate a potential connection to markets that have been considered unavailable since the signing of Treaties. Should this be true then we may need to enact the standing protocols and re-confirm the ancient agreements.

Seb caught his blade just in time to brace it against the incoming blow. The goblin staggered back from the last second impact before letting out a shriek as a pulse of Energy swept through it and its allies.

Without hesitation Seb sent the sword flying once more, this time towards the tunnel leading deeper into the cavern and the reinforcements rounding the corner. One goblin fell before he disengaged, leaving the remainder to flail in his wake. Anya would be among them before they recovered, her Technique already capturing their attention.

Clear, Cara sent.

We've got them, Gwen added a moment later as the pair charged into the newest wave of Depthsborn.

Seb came to a halt, his awareness sweeping the cavern. For the first time in nearly an hour there were no new targets to tag, no new threats to flag, no creatures unaccounted for. Spinning on his heel he crashed into the backs of the goblins he had just escaped.

No longer worried about fresh foes he pulled his aura tight, darts of Energy striking creature after creature. None were strong enough to cause true damage yet each created an opening to be exploited. Half were for him, the others for his wife.

They fought as one, two bodies guided by shared intent. There weren't enough goblins in the pack to have been a threat to either of them, let alone to them both.

As the last fell he relaxed his hold on his aura, confirming what he already knew. Gwen and Cara's emotions across the band were filled with satisfaction, their coordination almost uncanny as they took out the last few enemies.

Done? Anya sent.

Last- Gwen answered.

-one is- Cara continued.

-stubborn.

Seb felt Anya's thoughts become distinct, their meld dissolving now that the threat was handled.

Think they noticed? she sent.

That they're answering as one? he asked. That this last cavern had them-

Yes, Gwen sent. We noticed. At least I noticed.

It isn't the way you've described, Cara sent. I knew where she was, but couldn't have anticipated her next movement. I just knew she'd be there when I needed her to be.

Seb felt Anya's thoughts flash back to memories of previous battles, to how they had learned to fight together. It wasn't the same, at least not exactly, but that didn't mean anything. Bonds and coordination went hand in hand, but the improvements even compared to yesterday were surprising.

"That makes six," Raoul said, stepping into the cavern. "Trying for a seventh would almost certainly have us returning to base camp quite late. Based on yesterday I would have expected you to get to this point noticeably later if you did at all."

"I was quite hopeful, but there was always the possibility, especially with the significant increase in difficulty."

Even Raoul noticed, Anya sent.

"So we passed the test?" Seb asked.

"Yes. You won't be fighting tomorrow, we'll spend a good portion of the day traveling then set up an initial camp."

Seb tried not to grin as Raoul visibly tensed at the question. They'd all agreed that they suspected Sigrid would be the second guide, she was the only guide they knew anything about, in particular how she'd felt about working with Bern.

"Why would you…" Raoul began before sighing. "Yes. Only a handful of the guides have Master Leo's approval to work independently. Of the others she is the one I have the best working relationship with. It only makes sense for her to be the second guide."

"It isn't gambling when you know you're going to win."
