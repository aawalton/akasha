Dear Diary,

Y'know, I know I've mentioned that I hate waiting, but here's the weird thing; it's absolutely worse when I don't know when the fuck whatever I'm waiting for is gonna happen.

Yeah, I've got a certain amount of that neurodivergent need to keep my schedule clear for an inordinate amount of time before some scheduled event, but that's where my whole ADHD thing comes in handy. As the memelords have decreed, the call is coming from inside the house. I can distract the fuck out of myself for a ridiculous amount of time with nothing but my own brain. Yeah, I used to do that shit with my phone, but that was just a convenient excuse, really. I didn't need jack or shit to distract myself from the passing of time. If I even noticed said time passing, because that's another one of those things that is awful in most circumstances but really fuckin' useful when you're waiting for something.

Except for me it only works if I know when whatever the fuck I'm waiting for is gonna happen. I'm not one of those clockwork engineer types, I don't need a precise number of seconds, minutes, hours, or even days. Okay, not knowing how many days might suck, although again if I know it's 'on the Solstice' or 'on my birthday' or some other fixed point in time, I can totally vibe with that. It's like there's one of those computer progress bar jobbers somewhere in my head, and it's steadily filling up, and when it's done the thing I'm waiting for happens.

I mean, okay, I've had problems now and then when that whole 'lack of sense of the passage of time' bites me in the ass because I didn't set an alarm or anything and I miss whatever I was waiting for entirely. ADHD is not a superpower, it's just a different way of processing brain information. I think the term is 'neural architecture', but I haven't been able to Scry up anybody surfing for that precise shit lately, so I'm relying on my admittedly shitty pre-isekai memory. So that might not be the right term, but it gets the point across. Neurodiverse folks don't not think, even those of us who are epic dumbasses, we just think different.

Sometimes that thinking different is useful, like I read where folks like me would be great at standing watch at night while the rest of the tribe slept, because I just have to stay awake until dawn. Which, Deific shenanigans notwithstanding, happens at a reliable time every day. Yes, I am aware of orbital mechanics and how they gradually change that time day by day, but it's not changing so fast that it feels like it's changing, and if I cared enough to do the math I could figure it out.

Like, dawn tomorrow is gonna be a few minutes after five AM.

I am now trying not to lose my shit over the fact that I just did that shit in my head, and the only reason that I can't be more precise is that we don't really have precision clocks here. I still wanna get one of those, by the way.
