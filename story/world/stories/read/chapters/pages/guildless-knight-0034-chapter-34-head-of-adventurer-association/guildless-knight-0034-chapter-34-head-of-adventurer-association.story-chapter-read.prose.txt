It’s as busy as ever, Alan mentally noted, as he walked through the main market road of Alcia. The association hall was roughly two blocks away from it.

“Young man! Want some fried fish? They’re fresh, tasty—and cheap!” an old man called out, trying to catch his attention.

“Hey, know any good place to buy potions from?” a female adventurer questioned the man next to her, nudging him with her elbow.

“Leave that to me,” the man replied, tapping his chest with a confident expression.

Hmm, I should probably stack up some potions myself, Alan thought. Not like I would have a healer next time I fight, he added, giving a slightly awkward smile.

Alan reached the alleyway, the one where he had met that adventurer—what was his name again, he wondered. “Blemp? Right…” he muttered, walking into the damp, cold alleyway. I suppose that’s not it, he mentally added.

Dark affinity, “Quick step,” he mumbled. A dark orb of magic released from his feet, which burst with a [Baaam]—nothing too big to cause any actual damage, but just enough for him to move outside of the alleyway in an instant.

I really need to start using dark affinity more, i am rather sloppy with it, Alan thought to himself. He turned his gaze to the left and began walking towards the entrance of the association hall. With a small exhale, he pushed the door and walked in.

As soon as Alan walked inside, he felt a lot of eyes on himself—it wasn’t anything unusual for him, though, considering how unusual it was for him to come to the association hall and how he was an A-ranker. Even so, the feeling he got was different.

“It’s solo knight,” a female adventurer whispered to her teammate, her eyes glaring daggers, as if she was judging him.

Do I have something on my face? Alan wondered. Walking towards the guild desk, he noticed a pair of familiar faces in the far corner, the Weeble siblings were sitting together, eating their breakfast.

He noticed the expression on Rose’s face. It looked different. She wasn’t looking at him with anger. Rather, her face had a tense expression with relaxed eyes, almost as if she was looking at him with pity.

“Did he really kill all of them?” someone spoke up.

Oh right, they all heard about the battle? Alan thought. He stopped, standing right in front of the head receptionist of the adventurers’ association.

“Miss Vanessa, morning,” Alan greeted with a subtle smile.

Vanessa looked at Alan. “Are you doing well now, kiddo?” she asked.

“I would say I am,” he replied with a nod. “Just woke up like half an hour ago, and Alisa told me I shouldn’t let Master Gilbert wait, about how giving my report to him is way more important than having my breakfast!” Alan added, his tone slightly sarcastic.

Vanessa chuckled. “There’s no way she could say that,” she replied, then turning her head back at the paperwork, she added, “There are some rumors about you.”

Rumors, yeah, the one about my attack, right? Alan internally thought. “They are mostly true—”

“About you killing several hostages and laughing at their death, and you being a battle maniac who used an advanced spell for your sadistic enjoyment,” Vanessa added, her voice low, barely above a whisper.

“They are not true!” Alan yelped, shaking his head. “And Miss Vanessa, you know me, I’m not stupid. If I had to laugh, I would’ve done it in private rather than on a literal battlefield. And Ais would’ve killed me too if I did that,” Alan added, slightly defensive.

“I know, I know,” Vanessa replied, as she stood from her chair. “That’s why they are called rumors, right?”

As Vanessa walked toward the stairs, she added, “Don’t let their words bother you; just ignore everyone for now.”

Alan followed just a step behind Vanessa. While climbing the wooden stairs, his mind went back to the rumors. Why would anyone even believe this stupid rumor? he questioned, frustrated.

Vanessa stopped at the first room on the second floor, in front of a wooden door with an association head metallic tag attached.

[Knock, knock] Vanessa knocked at the door and waited for a moment.

“Come in,” a voice came from within.

Vanessa turned her gaze to Alan. “You may go inside,” she said in a calm tone, as she took a step behind.

Alan nodded. Leaving a sigh, he grabbed the metallic doorknob and opened the door. Gilbert sat on a leather-cushioned armchair. He looked up from the table as the door opened.

“It’s you?” Gilbert said, giving a quick look to Alan, turning his head back to the paperwork.

Alan walked inside. The door closed from behind, with the sound of Vanessa walking away coming into his ears. “I just woke up,” Alan replied, giving a small smile. He walked up to the table and took his seat. “I was told that you wanted to see me.”

“I did. There are a lot of rumors going around… about you,” Gilbert replied calmly, turning his gaze up. Looking at Alan, he questioned, “Any idea about what it is?”

“It’s pure bullshit, I’ll tell you that!” Alan blurted out. “Sorry,” he added immediately after. Don’t raise your voice in front of him, idiot! He internally berated himself.

Gilbert blinked. “Right, rumors… they are the worst, aren’t they,” he mumbled. The problem is, it’s not exactly rumors we are dealing with—it’s false allegations.”

Alan’s eyebrow raised at Gilbert’s words. “What are you getting at?” he questioned, looking directly into Gilbert’s red eyes.

“The hostage who survived… well, let’s just say they have a different recollection of the event.”

Alan’s eyes narrowed. He knew well enough where the conversation was going. Obviously, they are the ones that accused me, he thought, unimpressed. “They say that I fired the spell without any real danger, right?”

Gilbert nodded. “But it’s not like I believe them,” he shrugged. “I mean, how could I believe their word over you? I mean, hey, we adventurers gotta trust each other, right?”

“Right,” Alan replied in a dry tone.

“Good, good. Now that we’re on the same page, take care of the dungeon boss of floor 13th for me,” Gilbert added, a smug smirk spreading on his lips.

Asshole! Alan internally yelled. “I understand,” he replied with a small nod. A smile spread on his lips as he continued, “I’ll take the same payment you offer Black Wolf guild, though.”

“Tch!!”

Did the moustache bastard just tch me? Alan internally grumbled, his eyebrows twitching. “You said something, Sir Gilbert?” he asked calmly.

“I did not,” Gilbert said, grabbing a yellowish paper. He stamped on it, passing it to Alan. “I’ll take care of the hostage,” he paused, thinking for a moment. “I suppose… They are patients now.”

Alan nodded. He isn’t planning to silence them, is he? No, no, no—Gilbert is anything but a murderer… wait a second, knowing his philosophy, he might just do it!

“Try not to be too harsh on them,” Alan asked, looking at Gilbert with a pleading look.

Gilbert tapped his chest. “Don’t you worry. I have my ways to not harm them anymore physically… I mean, wouldn’t want to increase the Healer Guild problems, right? Hahahahaha.”

“True,” Alan mumbled. “Hahahaha,” he laughed as well. He’s gonna psychologically torture the ones who don’t take their statement back, he thought. But I mean, it’s better, right? He added, standing up from the seat.

“I’ll be going,” Alan said, clutching the mission scroll in his right hand.

Gilbert nodded, turning his gaze back at the colossal mess of paperwork in front of him.
