The attack came with no warning. Jade had just stepped off the rear ramp of the still-invisible airship when she caught a flash of violet light out of the corner of her eye. After months spent in near-constant combat, she knew better than to waste time looking to see what it was. Jade threw herself to the side, rolling on the hard-packed sand and leaping to her feet with weapons already drawn as a globule of something bright and hot whipped over her shoulder. Heat washed over her but was immediately dampened by the magical barrier that sprang up around her as Luis hastily cast a defensive spell. Jade spun on her heels and focused her senses, scanning for the source of the spell. There! Phantom ripped from her hand like a bullet, slicing through the air towards a faint distortion that shimmered a short distance away.

Clang!

The dagger deflected to the side, clattering to the ground before warping back to Jade's waiting hand. The distortion flashed, fading to reveal two demons standing naught but thirty feet from them. The sheer shock of their presence made Jade hesitate, and she wasn't the only one. They'd checked the area just moments ago, and she hadn't sensed anything. How… how had they gotten so close?

"What have we here?" The taller of the two spoke in a smooth, deep voice. He had a muscular frame and midnight black skin mottled with faint violet patterns. His dark armor glistened with an oil-like sheen that evoked an uneasy feeling. And his eyes… something about them drew Jade in. They were twin pools of ice, within which lay a fathomless depth of suffocating darkness.

"Who are you?"

Jade challenged him, doing her best to sound less surprised than she was. She knew they should attack before he could summon help, but the creature's imposing aura, which she could still only barely sense, gave her pause.

"I am Zertrolk." The demon crossed his arms, apparently unconcerned with the party of Wayfarers arrayed before him. The shorter of the two demons, a slight, red-skinned woman clad in torn and singed robes, shuffled back a few steps, distancing herself from the standoff. "Commander of the vanguard that opened the path to our people’s liberation."

He swept his gaze over their group before settling it back on Jade. She tensed, tightening her grip on her daggers.

"And you, I take it, are the group that gave Malice so much trouble. Yes, a group of Wayfarers with a succubus. I suppose this means that Vylith failed to take care of you." He shook his head in apparent wonder. "To think I would find you here in the forbidden lands, so close to the emperor. But how…? No, it matters not."

Whatever technique the demon used to hide his true strength was formidable. Despite looking straight at him, Jade could only catch faint hints of his aura. What little she could sense was not reassuring. This was a powerful foe, on par with the wraith demon assassin they'd clashed with.

Damnit. Jade thought savagely. Why now? Why here?

The timing of this encounter couldn't be any worse. They had to resolve this quickly. The demon lord was, presumably, in the palace at the top of the plateau. If he noticed them and summoned reinforcements, they might never make it to him, let alone catch him off-guard. They couldn't allow that to happen.

"You're the one who unleashed the demons into the Labyrinth?" Tara asked, and Jade was impressed by the challenge in her tone. The tek'kalla was resolute and fearless, even in the face of such an intimidating opponent. "You're responsible for all the death they wrought?"

"Yes." Zertrolk replied simply. There was no remorse in the words, nor did his face betray a hint of triumph or gloating. It was simply a fact. "This is but the first step of reclaiming our birthright. You might have witnessed the next, if you were not so foolish as to come here."

Finally, an expression crossed his face. A slight smile.

"Lady Ira and Lord Vastrozun will be quite pleased to hear I've eliminated another thorn in their side."

The battle erupted with stunning speed. Elyx dove for cover as a giant fireball, courtesy of the tek'kalla mage, detonated on top of Zertrolk. Chunks of flaming sand and rock battered the demon ritualist as she crashed to the ground, breaking through her meager wards to pelt her back. She groaned in pain, crawling behind a rocky outcropping that afforded some small protection from the explosive conflict. She caught sight of the commander charging through the flames and summoning something in his hand, shrugging off more spells and arrows with apparent ease. Elyx had never seen the pain demon wield the infamous weapon, but just the sight of the whip of violet flames clutched in his clawed hands made her shudder and begin casting defensive spells with frantic speed.

Why, oh why had she neglected to conjure her usual wards on this trip? She knew the answer even as she mentally berated herself. What was the point of maintaining them when she was already a dead woman walking? But she hadn't counted on this. Another explosion sent a second wave of sand and smoke washing over her, making her cough and lose focus on the spell. Damn it, she was a ritualist, not a combat mage! She wasn't used to casting under pressure. But even accepting that she would soon be dead, there was no way she would risk being hit by Zertrolk's soul fire. Tales of a pain demon's unique power were infamous among her kind, and she had no wish to check the veracity of the stories herself.

There was another loud WHUMP, followed by a third blast of hot flame. The air was thick with spell mana as the combatants clashed again, projectiles of fire and ice crisscrossing through a cloud of smoke that partially obscured the conflict. Elyx could make out the form of the rogue succubus exchanging a series of rapid strikes with Zertrolk, who simultaneously fended off the thrusts of a larger, armored human while dodging attacks coming from those further afield. Fast, high-energy music filled the air, weaving an unfamiliar enchantment throughout the space. The commander struck back with his conjured whip, slashing it in a complex pattern that snaked through his opponents' defenses to batter the barriers that protected them. With his free hand he conjured balls of violet flames, sending them arcing out towards the Wayfarers' back line and forcing them to scatter and dodge.

As the battle raged on, Elyx grew increasingly surprised at how long the mysterious group of newcomers was lasting. She'd expected Zertrolk to cut them down swiftly and with little effort, but that wasn't the case. The greater demon clearly had the upper hand in the battle, but his opponents weren't without tricks of their own. She watched as the large, armored man redoubled his offensive, silver blade flashing with golden light as he struck again and again. Zertrolk ducked, dodged, and parried with his bracers, but the attacks left threads of celestial energy hanging in the air, and he was forced to step through them to avoid being boxed in. The succubus took advantage of the distraction, darting in from behind with empowered weapons of her own. Was that… was that celestial essence infused in her aura? But how? That shouldn't have been possible. Reality, it seemed, didn't much care about what Elyx thought, and she watched as the succubus scored a solid hit on the commander's back with her radiant blades. He snarled, kicking the larger warrior away and spraying a blast of fire towards her. The succubus leapt into the air, launching herself skyward with a single, powerful flap of her wings and leaping over the arc of violet flames. She flipped in the air, throwing a cloud of daggers that multiplied fivefold in flight. For a moment, Elyx truly thought they had him. Spells and projectiles hammered the commander's defenses while he grappled with two opponents wielding celestially empowered weapons. The hits were adding up, forcing him back one step, then two.

And then Zertrolk roared.

"URAAAAAHG!"

A sphere of dark energy erupted from the pain demon, passing first through the two opponents he was locked in combat with, then the backliners, and finally through Elyx herself. She gasped, her eyes going wide as an icy fist clutched her spirit. Something cracked, and each and every one of her defensive enchantments wilted away beneath its unyielding grip. The backlash of her layered wards failing in an unexpected cascade left Elyx dizzy and she fell to all fours, head throbbing in sudden pain.

Have… have to get up…

This was her chance. The Wayfarers would be dead soon, and then Zertrolk would lead her to that very same fate shortly thereafter. But for the moment, his attention was fully occupied. If she could make it back to her mount and slip away into the wasteland before the battle concluded…

A scream arrested her focus. It was a sound unlike any she'd heard before. A sound of pure, unadulterated pain. She looked up in time to see Zertrolk's soul fire whip wrap itself around the rogue succubus's ankle. He pulled hard, jerking her out of the sky and into the hard ground with enough force to send up another cloud and dust. Zertrolk launched another wave of violet fire towards the remainder of her group. They scattered again, struggling to evade the grasping tendrils that erupted from the roiling flames like the tentacles of a hungry sea creature.

I should… I have to…

Elyx couldn't look away. The battle continued, but Zertrolk had regained the initiative by tearing down his opponents' defenses. Now, it was only a matter of time.

The pain was like nothing Jade had ever felt. It was as if her blood had been replaced with scorching fire, pumped by her traitorous heart into every corner of her body. She didn't remember cutting herself free from the demon's whip, nor did she know how she managed to pull herself back to her feet. Her barriers were gone, as was the dark fire that had scoured her leg. It left no burns, no mark of any kind, and yet it had pierced her more deeply than any blade.

Focus…

Her thoughts were sluggish, fighting through the remnant quagmire of agony that had washed away all else. Someone else screamed, Jade couldn't tell who, and she shook her head sharply.

Focus!

The battle was turning against them. She regained awareness just in time to roll away from another burst of fire, dodging the grasping tentacle that tried to pull her back into its sinister embrace. Luis was on his knees nearby, face screwed up in pain as he struggled back to his feet. Zertrolk was already gone. The pain demon was charging towards Fenrin and the others, more of those violet flames curling around his arms and gathering in his palms.

"No you don't." Jade coughed, taking off in pursuit. She stumbled on leaden legs, ducking and dodging around pockets of smoldering sand where clumps of his arcane flames still sputtered, sheathing one of her daggers and reaching into her pouch. "Let's see how you like your own trick."

Jade's fist closed around a small orb. She withdrew it, flicked a switch on its metal surface, and then hurled it straight at the demon's back with all her strength. It was a good throw, and Skaarg's "bomb of dispelling" nailed Zertrolk right between where a human's shoulder blades would be. There was a loud crack, a flash of blue light, and all the fire filling the area winked out of existence.

"Now!"

Her friends needed little encouragement. Luis charged past her, slamming a force blade into the demon's back and sending him staggering forward before he could recover. The dispelling bomb must have stripped away some kind of arcane defense on the creature's armor because that single blow cracked it, achieving what none of the others had managed so far. And that was just the start. Tara and Naomi joined in, each sending streams of fire slamming into his side while Fenrin loosed arrow after arrow straight into his chest. Siora stayed in the rear, distributing healing spells as quickly as she could chant the incantations. Jade followed it all up with Clinging Darkness and rune traps, trying to keep the powerful demon on the back foot long enough to claim victory.

They weren't quite fast enough.

Zertrolk cursed in a menacing growl, slamming a fist into Luis's chest and knocking him to the ground. He was on Jade a moment later, deftly avoiding the traps she'd set and laying her out with a similar strike. Dark blood dripped from numerous gashes in his sinister armor, and one side of Zertrolk's face was charred and burned. That didn’t stop him from smiling savagely down at Jade.

"You're a pathetic mockery of the might of our people." He growled, his smile twisting into a leer. Another arrow struck his shoulder, but it burned away in a burst of renewed violet flames. They curled around his body, framing his form in a sinister aura. "That it has taken us this long to remove you is a disgrace I will not abide."

He sent another wave of fire in the direction of Fenrin's arrow and Tara's spells. Jade couldn't see them. She was lying on her back, gasping for breath through the weight of the elephant sitting on her chest. She tried to sit up, but he kicked her back to the ground.

"A quick death is more than the likes of you deserve, but I will deign to have mercy on you. If only because you were kind enough to remove Malice for me." He raised one hand and a spear of flame formed within it, the point aimed directly at her throat.

"Glacente!"

An unfamiliar, feminine voice shouted from directly behind Zertrolk, and a lance of ice as thick as Jade's arm punched straight through his gut. Black blood sprayed across her as the demon roared, whirling to face his attacker with an expression of shock and fury. The spear in his hand wavered but didn't fade. In front of him stood the slender, shorter demon, who was already slowly backing away from the enraged monster looming over her.

"W-wow…" she stuttered. "You're tougher than you look, commander. I really thought that would--"

Her hands came up again mid-sentence, throwing more shards of ice into Zertrolk at point blank range. Some deflected off his armor while others pierced through, but still, he didn't fall. The pain demon lurched towards her, catching the other demon in the side with his flaming spear. She collapsed with an ear-piercing shriek, her limbs spasming as he wrenched the weapon free. Jade's searching fingers found what they were looking for, and they closed around Phantom's hilt. With a herculean effort she pulled herself to her knees. The world spun, but she hung on, desperately trying to manifest her aura around the blade. But she couldn't focus, the mana slipped through her fingers like water. Zertrolk was already turning back towards her, murderous rage burning in his eyes. She met his gaze and tried to speak, but nothing came out. Her breaths were too ragged, and the charm fizzled on her tongue. All Jade could do was glare back at him, head held high in defiance as he raised his spear once again.

Only for a blast of concentrated mana to blow through his shoulder from behind. This time, Zertrolk fell without a sound, revealing a gasping tek'kalla in a scorched and burned white combat suit, a smoking pulsecaster held in both hands. Tara stomped a foot on the demon's chest and levelled her weapon.

BAM! BAM! BAM!

She fired another half-dozen blasts straight into his head and torso, the focused magic ripping through his armor like paper. When the smoke finally cleared, Jade's tear-blurred vision could only make out a smoking stump where his neck had been.

"Just to be safe." Tara gasped to Jade, falling to one knee and clutching her side. "You… can never be… too sure, you know?"

Then she collapsed in an unconcious heap beside her.
