Chapter 130

The Long Way

(Anastasia Bloom)

(Twenty Minutes Ago)

Seeing the future is not always a surefire deal. Sometimes you think what should be more than enough time to resolve an issue, is in fact too much time. For it is what that extra time allows, that causes deviations in patterns.

Take for instance this afternoon, Anastasia had all of the variables. For she knew all of the elements already at play. She knew about the records being sent to Unity University about the Self-Created Teams Doctrine, a contract that was already listed as 100% completed, for Anastasia Bloom herself was a faculty member and alumni still in good standing with Unity University. This meant that aside from trying to personally come and disqualify her Apprentice firsthand, that the odds of Matriarch Florence Livanathan, the head of the Healing curriculum to come herself was at most 55%, or roughly a coin flip’s chance to go either way.

Anastasia also had the detail of Sal being tried in her city, which she and her Apprentice would be more than enough to verify personally, making the need for outsiders to come and help regulate were at 20%. No one liked attending a trial of the condemned, and no one liked leaving from a place of luxury, like the grounds of Unity University, to travel to the edge of nowhere, all to try apparent strangers.

Even when combined, the two factors alone should drop the overall effect of both sets of variables to be lower. Rather than adding the two variables together, and getting a 75% chance, still less than a hundred percent chance of occurring; adding the two variables together should have lowered the overall percentage. Taking what was originally a 55% chance, and lowering it to at most 35% chance, or lower.

Anastasia had even factored in the current state of political pressures going on at Unity University at this time. As it was obvious that there would be backstabbing and infighting between the divisions, making it so a career driven woman like Matriarch Florence Livanathan would want to stay to take care of political maneuverings that were occurring in her own backyard. Again, this should have been a factor that if known would make someone as power hungry as the great Matriarch Livanathan to wish to stay put at Unity University. While this factor alone had been enough for Anastasia to recuse herself from her role at Unity University some time ago, allowing Matriarch Livanathan the chance to take her spot, this set of variables once again should have put the overall chance of coming at 10%, or dropping all other contingencies down to 10% as an absolute must. A fact that would only occur if Matriarch Livanathan herself was ordered to go by the Dean. Yet, no dean would do such a thing for what good is running a premier university dedicated to fighting and training the next generation of fighters, if the school of healers will not back you.

However, the one thing that Anastasia had not factored at all. The fact that didn’t even come across her mental tableau of factors to consider was the curiosity that Matriarch Livanathan might have in relation to the Apprentice Anastacia chose to pass on her legacy. Especially because said Apprentice was not part of her bloodline at all, but a foster child from the local DiVoratore household. Had Anastasia realized that this might be a factor of Matriarch Livanathan’s then she would have changed her rubric entirely, knowing that it would be a 100% certainty that not only would the Matriarch come here, and proceed over the trial, but she would come early to take in the Apprentice firsthand.

This was why, Anastasia was caught off guard for the first time in quite some time, when she got home from taking care of issues in Bloom Park, to find that a foreign delegation was awaiting her. Not only was a delegation awaiting her, but it was clear that they were curious about what they described as sounds of combat happening from the back of the estate.

“Are you having a competitive tournament here, now that the arena is shut down?” Were the first words that Matriarch Livanathan spoke. The first words that the two who had been closest friends at one point, now exchanged after ten years were not cordial greetings, or exchanges of pleasantries, but an indictment on Anastasia’s character herself.

“To what do I owe the unexpected visit?” Anastasia replied, not answering the undignified comment, and pretending that the first words that Florence had spoken were typical greetings.

“We have come to evaluate your Apprentice and see if she meets the morality requirements needed as a healer assigned to fulfill a Self-Created Teams contract,” Florence spoke. Hearing the words and the condescending attitude coming from her contemptuous former friend, it was clear that Florence was here to do anything but accept her.

As Florence spoke, Anastasia began going through all data that might have been transmitted to the University, particularly as it relates to her apprentice and involves the others. For a moment she wanted to dismiss the claims of knowledge, suspecting that she might be fishing for information that could be used against her or her apprentice later. Especially since Florence never appreciated the arena for what it was truly designed for, as a way for people to practice and hone their martial combats and techniques in new and adaptive ways. Knowing Florence’s character, they had many clashes about this very subject, and seeing her reactions right now, it was clear that she still felt this way. However, Anastasia took too long in her evaluations, giving away the chance to redirect or take advantage of the silence given, as Florence continued her questioning.

“I ask again, are you hosting a competitive tournament here?” Florence Livanathan asked, and just as Anastasia was about to open her mouth to refute such a claim, her instincts took over. Opening her mouth, she felt it, the wisp of instant regret blew through her with a biting chill, the way only a true seer could feel. She exhaled, having her breath forced from her lungs, making any reply impossible, which was a good thing. For that was the exact moment that a burst of green healing energy erupted from the backyard of the estate.

Judging by the direction and burst, Anastasia figured that the burst had come from the dueling arena, meaning that the event that Anastasia had made a 90% probability of happening, aka a forced encounter where Catarine, her apprentice witnessed members of house Bloom antagonizing Luna and Lucia was happening, just as she had predicted. What she had not predicted was the sheer scale and volume of healing energy being emitted by her apprentice, who clearly took the duel with the older Bloom members far more seriously than she had any right to.  Then again, this was also factored into the equation as it was clear that Catarine could heal any damage she caused, as could most of the others. What Anastasia had failed to factor in were the new residual mana amplifiers that Anastasia herself had added to the arena area. This was originally meant to identify both when two family members dueled for honor or some such status within the Bloom estate, while also being able to identify new and promising talent within the Bloom estate. Sadly, the latter never happened. At least not until now, not until these extra variables that had not been anticipated were suddenly here and throwing off all of her calculations and proving that sometimes probability is improbable to quantify correctly.

“It would appear so, though if you will follow me, I will show you that this is just a matter of Bloom estate members practicing their craft. As you can see,” Anastasia commented showing that a rather bright green flare was now being created, showing that intense healing Skills were being used. Seeing the flare, and intensity, it was clear that these could be made by none other than her Apprentice, for no one else in the Bloom estate could rise to such level of power or ability. Adding to the intensity of the colors being shown is the fact that at least three healing related Skills were being used in unison, further brightening the overall energy signature being emitted.

When Anastasia had first broached the idea with Pierre, she had recommended the back arena for this very event, as she knew what would come next would be most important in ensuring Catarine joined on with Luna and Lucia in her place. Namely, the moments after the battle were where they would finally have a moment to accurately express their feelings and teenage hormones effectively. Wanting to give as much time as possible, Anastasia chose to go the long way, to give as much time as possible. This would be the path that would lead past Pierre and the other house servants’ chambers. An area that was completely off the main track, and no self-respecting member of the Bloom household would dare go there, if only because going there would be seen as lesser. However, what Anastasia failed to consider was the fact that Catarine did not have the same stigmas as the other members of the Bloom household. People who thought that lacking healing potential was somehow contagious. Meaning that by the time they had wandered down the back hallway, they came across her Apprentice doing the worst thing possible. She was actively doing the impossible, healing away necrotized skin with her standard set of Skills.

For a moment, Anastasia wanted to call out to them, to at least warn them or Lucia that they were present. However, a quick raise of her hand and implementation of a sound dampening Skill later, allowed it so Florence could watch on and stare at the odd methods being used by Anastasia’s apprentice. With the holding up of a fist to stop movement, Florence took over the moment and began observing, as her position allowed and dictated in this setting. At least as far as the potential evaluation of a healing candidate for Unity University.

“Is this the eradication method that was noted in the report? The one used to heal Howard “Lefty” Lowitski?” Florence asked, using full volume to speak, her belief and reliance on her sound dampening Skill that high, that she was absolutely certain that nothing would breach the barrier of her Skill. Meaning anything they said, or any noises they made in these hallways were silent and hidden from Catarine and the others.

That is how they managed to watch Catarine clear up not only facial damage, but also managed to heal both the external damage, and internal wear caused by such a technique.

“What is she doing?” Florence asked, trying to see if there was anything she was missing as, it was clear she was doing what should theoretically be impossible. At least, researchers all agreed that no current cure for removing necrotized skin existed during Anastasia’s time at the university. Yet, here was her apprentice showing just how impressive she truly was. Seeing her Apprentice at work, and seeing the increasingly flustered look filling Florence’s face, the person who pushed Anastasia out of her academic role from Unity University had a poetic form of irony to it. Anastasia had been kicked out for a multitude of reasons, but one of the most egregious cases was the fact that she was both adamant that she would not take on an apprentice, and second that there were cures for the diseases plaguing the world, they just hadn’t refined the technique. Ironically, it was only now that she was in her final years, that she found one person worthy of achieving both distinctions.

“She’s doing the improbable,” Anastasia replied, a note of satisfaction born by years of vindication finally being unleashed back at its source filling her voice. “I’d say get used to it, she does this quite often.”

“Heal the injured?” Florence asked, leaning forward as she tried to understand exactly what her Apprentice was doing to remove the calcified layer of dead skin that the necrotic spores create for protection.

“I was referring to her doing the improbable, but yes most of her improbable events circle around her ability to heal.” Anastasia admitted.

They all watched in silence, as even the delegation that Florence brought with her, likely as an intimidation tactic all looked on with muted looks of horror, confusion, and wonder. Having already seen this process before at the arena, Anastasia felt confident enough to look around at Catarine’s perceived competition, which is why she missed the moment that she had tried to set up all night. A moment that was now witnessed by twenty foreign delegates who had no business knowing about this personal relationship that Anastasia might have secretly been rooting for herself.

“Oh, an amorous relationship?” Florence commented, raising one black gloved hand to cover her face, as she mocked on in faux horror. Hearing that, Anastasia felt her chest tighten as she looked forward and saw that the moment she had been rooting for had happened, her Apprentice had finally found a deeper connection to this world, and it had all been witnessed by the very people who should not know about this relationship at all.

Stepping forward, Anastasia pressed her way through the wind barrier created by Flornce, during which time she coughed loudly, trying to draw everyone’s attention to her.

“Ah-ha-hem,” the cough worked almost instantly, for Catarine and the others all jumped back, giving space to their otherwise previously intimate position. Not good, but there was nothing Anastasia could do about that currently. Seeing that there was nothing they could do about the past, she pressed forward trying to warn everyone present about the gravity of what they would do and say next.

“Apprentice, this is Matriarch Florence Livanathan, of Unity University. She is here to evaluate Lucia and Luna’s candidacy for Unity University—” and like that, the game of political chess involving her Apprentice and the next generation of healers had begun. Whether her apprentice realized this moment in time, or not.
