Liora stared at the demon—who might or might not be good looking if you discounted how horrifying he was—while the demon stared bad. What the hell was she supposed to do?

Obviously, letting the demon fly free was NOT a good idea. Liora didn't have to be told that. But the gate to the underworld was rapidly shrinking, and the guild system couldn't even identify this guy's level. The label was completely black.

[Demon]

I have to at least try to shove that guy back into hell! Maybe if I'm fast enough…

Liora was about to activate Decoy Fade and take her chances, when she heard Renir yell.

"Liora, don't!"

She looked back and saw the bright light coming from Renir's body was growing dimmer. Just as the gate to the underworld closed, Renir stopped shining, revealing his new body.

It was strange.

He was larger than before, almost as large as Liora in her magpie form. He had a proper crystal head on a crystalline body with crystal arms and legs. He even had tiny little crystal fingers. Basically, he looked like a toddler, if said toddler was a little monster made up entirely of amber crystals.

Liora was so entranced in admiring Renir's new form that she had completely forgotten about the demon right behind her. When he spoke in what sounded like the same gibberish Liora was forced to chant for the incantation, she flinched from fright.

Everyone stared at the demon. Was he going to attack them? He regarded them all with a curious expression, then cleared his throat.

"Do you understand me now?" He said in a perfectly suave voice.

Nobody dared to answer.

"That's odd. I was sure that this was the language mortals in this realm used."

Liora looked down at where Renir sat. The gate to the underworld was gone. What the hell were they supposed to do now? Open another one and try to get this guy inside. What if something even worse came out?

Maybe they could somehow convince this guy to leave? Or at the very least talking to him might buy them some time before they came up with a proper strategy.

"We understand you," Liora said.

"Wow! I did not expect for the bird to reply," the demon said and suddenly flew closer to Liora.

She panicked, flapping backwards and wondering whether Purifying Lunge would do any damage to this guy. Maybe if she tore at his wings.

But instead of attacking, the demon stopped about a meter away from Liora, just observing her. No, not just observing. It felt like those red eyes were very piercing right into her soul. Tasting it.

The demon suddenly licked his lips. "My, oh my, you sure are an interesting one! A human stuck body of a monster. But look at all that delicious potential!"

"Back away from her!" Renir yelled from below, trying to take a step forward. But it appeared he wasn't used to his body yet, so he fell face first into sand.

The demons laughed, a laugh that sounded like it came from depths of hell. Deep, rumbling, the kind that made your whole being shake. Corrupted monsters had nothing on this guy.

"Don't you worry. This little one still needs to grow. I merely wanted to thank you for letting me out of that hellhole," the demon said and gracefully bowed in the air.

"That was not my intention at all," Liora said, backing away from the demon a little.

"Intentional or not, it is a result most desirable. I owe you a favor. Feel free to call for my aid, should you require it. Just call my name and I'll appear right next to you."

"No, thanks. I don't even know your name."

"My apologies. How rude! I haven't even introduced myself. My name is Azathiel. And what is your name?"

Liora didn't want to answer this guy. He was seriously creeping her out. But she needed to stall him somehow. Could she somehow signal Cee to make another one of those ritual circles? It had taken her quite a while the first time.

"I'm Liora…" She said reluctantly.

"Liora. A stunning name. I'll remember that. But I'm afraid that we're going to have to part our ways here. I hope to meet you again someday. Once you are much stronger," he said, bowed again and flew away.

He flew so fast, that Liora barely managed to blink when he was already nothing more than a black speck on the horizon.

Cee was the first one to come out of the daze. "Holy shit! Holy fuck! An actual fucking demon. Christ, isn't this like really, really bad!"

"Renir…"  Liora looked helplessly at the gem, who had managed to get up to his feet at some point.

"Hmm… yeah… That just happened…" he muttered nervously.

Liora flew down and landed next to him. "Don't just say that. What do we do?"

"There's nothing we can do. It's demon. A high-ranking one, by the looks of it."

"Is it a threat to our colony?" El'akari stepped closer to Renir, while the other antkin clicked their mandibles nervously.

"At the moment, I don't think so. But a demon roaming around this world… It can't be good. That guy most definitely needs to be sent back to the Underworld."

"His name was black!" Cee shrieked. "How are we supposed to do anything against him?"

"In our current state, we're not. The best we can do for now, is keep growing in strength. We're going to have to deal with that demon later. If we're lucky then for the time being, it'll just… explore around."

"Isn't that demon equivalent to murdering puppies and stealing souls from virgins?" Liora asked.

"Uh.. yeah, might be something along those lines. But there's seriously nothing we can do about it. At your current speed, you couldn't even catch up to that demon."

Liora's feathers puffed out in annoyance. That guy was flying so fast. Much faster than her even though he didn't have an avian body suited for flying. He just had some bat wings stuck to his back. How disgraceful!

One day she would fly much faster than that demon. Much, much faster!

"So what do we do now?" Cee asked Renir.

"The same thing we were planning to do before. We're heading to meet my brother and break another seal. Go, grab Caspian. There isn't a moment to lose," Renir said, trying to step forward, but slipped on sand and fell down again.

"Are you okay?" Liora asked, picking him up carefully with her talons. He was heavier too. Carrying him in magpie form would be impossible now.

"Yeah… I would say that this will take some getting used, but if things go as planned, we'll be breaking another seal very soon."

"When do they ever go as planned? We just accidentally unleashed a high-ranking demon onto this world," Liora grumbled.

"Yeah… Let's try not to do that again…"

By the time it was evening, Liora and the group had already made their way to the capital of Azhar sultanate.

Liora was proud to see how good of a flier Smokey had become. He was nowhere as fast as her, of course, but his stamina had vastly improved. He could easily fly all the way to the capital while carrying Cee on his back.

"I'm so glad to have a flier buddy," Liora said.

Smokey let out an approving growl, his tongue lolling out of his mouth.

"Do you see where my brother is?" Renir asked. Cee had secured him around Liora's neck, but it wasn't very comfortable for her. He was too heavy. They would have to figure out a better way to carry him.

Caspian had gotten heavier too. The antkin must have fed him well.

Liora scanned the surroundings and noticed a tent set up some distance away from the capital. It had a little sparkling jewel set atop.

"Found it!" Liora announced and flew towards the location. She wanted to snatch the jewel, but realized that she couldn't while carrying Caspian.

"Why is it that a girl has to carry two fully grown men? This is ridiculous! You two should learn to fly!" Liora grumbled.

"If we break a few more of my seals, then flying might be a possibility," Renir said.

"Yeah, no, that's not happening. Merfolk don't fly," Caspian said dryly. He's been a bit cranky ever since they woke him up.

Never disturb a merman's beauty sleep.

"You learned how to walk. Why stop there?" Liora teased, as she gingerly set him down into the sand.

Aenir emerged from the tent, his eyes widening in surprise as he looked at everyone in turn. His eyes lingered on Renir.

"I see you've managed to break another part of your seal. You're like a little rock golem, except made out of gemstone instead. So is this your team?" Aenir said, looking at everyone.

"Hello, I'm Cee! I'm Renir's apprentice," Cee said, while waving enthusiastically.

Aenir took a step back. "The spider talks too…"

"How rude! Of course I talk," Cee announced, appearing mildly annoyed. "Renir, do you look similar to your brother?"

"Not even a little!" They both said at the same time.

Liora looked at Aenir, then at the crystalline form of Renir. "What do you look like then?"

"Break my seal, and you'll find out!" Renir said cheekily.

"Oh, please! She'll only be disappointed. Obviously, I'm the better looking one," Aenir boasted.

"You? You don't even look like a mage. You look like a brawler with all the intelligence of a fly."

"Okay! Let's not start a fight!" Cee interrupted. "I believe it crucial that you meet us all. The grouchy guy in the back is Caspian. He's a merman, but he might be a bit grouchy at the moment. We woke him from his beauty sleep, and he doesn't exactly like flying either."

"I am not grouchy!" Caspian grumbled.

"A merman? Walking in the desert? That's certainly not something you see every day. And what's the story with the wyvern?" Aenir asked.

"He's my pet!" Cee announced proudly.

Aenir blinked. "Your pet?"

"Yup! Isn't he the goodest boy?" Cee said, scratching Smokey's chin. He wagged his tail happily, sending sand everywhere.

"Stop it!" Caspian growled, positioning himself behind Liora to avoid the sand.

"You know… It's fine. It's not important. We're here on business. Renir, are you ready to break another one of your seals?" Aenir asked.

"How do you two even know that Aenir can break it? Up until now it has been incredibly difficult to break them," Liora said, then muttered. "I mean, we just had the unfortunate side-effect of summoning a demon."

"WHAT?!" Aenir exclaimed, looking incredulously at Renir.

"The ritual required opening the gate of the underworld for like a minute or two. A high-ranking demon might have slipped out," Renir said.

"For fuck's sake! A corrupted river is the least of our problems if there's a high-ranking demon on the loose!"

"Hey, he didn't murder us all on the spot. I think this might be one of the more reasonable ones. He'll like stay under the radar for a while so the divine don't find him," Renir shrugged.

"If he didn't murder you right away that only means that he's plotting something even worse!" Aenir rubbed at his temples.

"Well, yeah… but there's not much we can do about it now," Renir said.

Aenir groaned. "Just come in here. Let's take a look at your seal!"

He opened the tent and ushered Renir to go inside. Liora was about to follow the clumsy gem, but Aenir raised his hand to stop her.

"You all better stay outside."

"I need to concentrate, and you all… " Aenir threw another glance at them all. "You all are quite distracting!"

"It's okay. If something happens, I'll call for you."

Liora nodded, and watched him head inside the tent. All they could do now was wait.
