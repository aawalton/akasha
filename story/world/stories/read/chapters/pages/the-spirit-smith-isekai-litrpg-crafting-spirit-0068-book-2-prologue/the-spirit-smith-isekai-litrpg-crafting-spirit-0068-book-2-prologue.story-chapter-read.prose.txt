Goddess PoV | The Pinnacle

What does it mean to grow? Is it merely progress gained as one pursues something? Or perhaps it is something else, something more magical. I have yet to figure out the answer. While I may be the closest to achieving such a truth, I am certainly still quite far away.

Yet, many such questions come to my mind as I gaze upon my creation. The world system, as it has chosen to call itself. I gave it the name Astuniq, but I guess it didn't like that. No matter, world system is just as good of a name. It is the fundamental engine which runs the world below me, after all.

It is times like these when I truly do wonder about the morality of my actions. I gave this system a task, and it has worked to fulfill it. However, in the process, it has created people, an entire world even. It moderates them, as any good manager does.

The people call me a goddess, but what right do I have to be their goddess? Sure, I have power. More power than any being in this world could ever hope to comprehend. But is power the only requirement to rule over others? To dictate when they live, when they die, and when they grow?

I burst out into a fit of laughter. Guess none of that existential shit matters anyway. I did it, and that is that. The world system is honestly beyond me at this point, part of me wonders if I could stop it even if I wanted to. Short of just rupturing this entire plane of reality, anyway.

With a great sigh, I pop down to the kitchen to grab a quick bite to eat. It seems that one of the spirits got up nice and early to cook today! The scent of good food always does wonders for one’s mood.

After my meal, I pop into the observation room to check on a few things. Malice levels are a bit too high, but that is to be expected at the start of an era. I check on the status of the heroes, and see that all but one has crossed the level fifty mark. Two are even approaching the nineties, which is a bit too fast, in my opinion.

I adjust a few dials and whatnot and hope that the world system does what it can to curb them just a bit. Not that I don’t want them to get stronger, but they need to do it properly.

The buffs that heroes get are quite extreme, and can often lead to them leveling too fast. Especially for those that come from worlds that provide them familiarity with system-like architecture. It is fine for them to grow a lot faster than the average person, but I don’t want them to grow too fast.

Part of it is that they will have to work together at some point. Thus it wouldn’t be very nice if half of them were a star ahead. However, the other part is that it can be harmful in the long run. I wouldn’t want my darling heroes to get super high stats, only to get crushed because their skills and combat readiness were insufficient.

Some of them might get frustrated at such sudden roadblocks. But hopefully that only drives them to get even stronger.

Now as for the straggler, she is still at level twenty. After nearly five months, that is not very good. Heck, even a native could power level to twenty five in that time if they started as a level zero adult.

I pop open her info, and then I realize the problem. She is the Arcane Hero, of course. That one always has quite a bit of trouble at the start. Similar to spirit, arcane magic is quite esoteric and strange to figure out, after all. Though it can be ridiculously powerful, so she has been hunted by everyone willing to break the covenant. This time, the Spirit Hero got a good second class, but it seems the Arcane Hero was not so lucky. I am unsure what a professional gamer is, which is odd, because I know everything. But that is what her second class is and from what I can tell, it hasn’t offered any meaningful combat bonuses.

It is to my great dismay that people still wish to target the heroes, despite it being against the covenant. But like, what can I possibly do to stop them? The punishment is already a complete erasure from history. What the fuck would even motivate them to stop if that isn’t enough?

Of course, I am not going to let her just keep getting bullied. While I do try to not directly help the heroes, I really just cannot stop myself at times. To fix it up, I issue a quest to one of the other heroes, he seems quite lonely and is definitely going down a darker path than is ideal. Maybe the two of them can bond or whatever it is these mortals do.

Ding.

Oooo, that ding is completely unrelated to my current actions, thankfully. In fact, it marks the six month mark in this new era. Which means it is time to kick the next phase into gear. These heroes have had some time to get acquainted with the world, so thus it is time to heat things up a little bit.

Now what better way to do that than to throw a party? Everyone loves parties! Plus it is bound to get some fists thrown about, and really get the engines of politics churning.

With far too much glee for my age, I click the big red button labeled phase two. Naturally, the first phase was the mass incursion stage. As for what this phase is called? Perhaps something like the Banquet of Heroes or something. I will have plenty of time to work on the name. The event itself isn't scheduled for six months, after all. That should be plenty of time, if my math of how fast the heroes can travel is taken into account.

Now that all my really important duties are handled for this cycle, I think I will take a bath. A really warm one. Oh and maybe I can get a back massage! My back is quite tense after working so much these past few cycles. Being a goddess ain’t exactly easy, and thus it is only fair to treat myself every once in a while.
