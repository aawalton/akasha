The elite that showed up was not the Singer. That much was obvious before Elise could even see it. Even without being able to sense its aether signature properly, it was obvious. The elite that approached them was much bigger, and as it drew near, she could hear the sound of metal scraping across stone.

It rounded the corner, showing its face—or rather lack thereof. Standing over eight feet tall, larger than any of the other draugr they had met thus far, it was completely encased in black metal armor. The sound of dragging metal had been the end of its flail, a misshapen hunk of spiked metal half-covered in a spiderweb of black membrane and what might have been dried blood.

It almost could have passed for a humanoid. With the armor covering its body, it was almost impossible to tell what lay underneath. The only thing that gave it away was a single tentacle end sticking out of the breathing hole in the helmet’s mask. And its System tag.

[ Abyssal Draugr Knight, lvl ??? ]

It came leading a small horde, and when it spotted Elise and Charlotte, it raised the steel handle of the flail, pointing and letting out a guttural roar. The twenty or so draugr behind it echoed it, and came flying forward. A few shot out some ineffective aether attacks from their eyes, but Elise and Charlotte were well beyond the point where such strikes could affect them.

They had discussed what to do in the case of an elite—Singer or otherwise—appearing, and had created a somewhat standard plan that they immediately fell into. Charlotte stopped playing around with her new Skills, instead doing what she did best and tearing the charging draugr to pieces. Meanwhile, Elise flew up and deliberately drew the attention of the elite, flying upward and away from Charlotte while hitting it with a few aether pulses to anger it.

Even before Elise did that, though, it was already focused on her, though. She still did the aether pulses anyways, but she could feel its gaze on her the entire time even without them. Something under its helmet glinted briefly, and the next moment, a soul attack crashed against her defenses. She withstood it without issue, but its power was on par with the later pulses of the soul storm.

She immediately fired back with an aether spike of her own, using perhaps half of the power she could muster. It might have been smarter to go all out and make sure it was dead from the start, but she frankly didn’t feel too threatened by this one. Generally, something physically focused wouldn’t be too powerful in other aspects, and even down here where everything was abnormally powerful, that basic rule still held. The things strong in body were only strong enough in soul to survive the soul storms, and were generally weaker in mana, or their mana existed purely to enhance their physicality. And those were the types of opponents she excelled most at facing, so she had no qualms about victory here.

The draugr knight was mid-step running toward her when the aether spike hit. It faltered for half a step, then continued onward, raising its flail and letting out another roar. Elise grimaced, flying higher and out of its reach. The attack had worked, but the knight had much stronger soul defenses than she had been expecting.

Unwilling to take too many risks, she connected to the System, using {Suggest} to misdirect it as she flew around to the side. She was glad she did, because if not, she would have been severely injured. It swung with its flail, and while she should have been out of reach, the chain extended as it swung, and it passed right through the spot where it had thought she was. If she had actually gone that direction, it would have taken out her legs or worse if she didn’t react in time.

She hit it with a full power aether spike, and while it screamed in pain, it didn’t actually do much more than her first one. She could feel its aether was disturbed, but it was nowhere near as much as she would have hoped for. She would either need a couple dozen more aether spikes, or she would need to find out a way to up her power even further.

Just more training for the Singer, she thought with a sigh.

Rapid firing aether spikes was something she was somewhat capable of, but before she went all out to kill it, she had one more experiment she wanted to try. As it turned to face her, raising the flail once more, she fired a {Power Word Kill} at it. Just as it had with the Singer, it was completely ineffective, completely nullified by something within the draugr’s soul. It did seem to anger it, though, and as it swung and missed again, it let out a howl, louder than any of its shouts from before. A second later, as Elise hit it with another aether spike, there was a response in the distance. It was a familiar screech, and it was followed immediately by the sight of a winged black shadow rising from the ruins.

The Singer.

“Charlotte!” Elise shouted as she dodged the flail again. “Plan 2!”

They had discussed this as well. Charlotte taking care of the small fries while Elise took on the big one was mostly to give Elise time to work, but it was definitely not the most efficient use of their abilities. Plan 2 was the most efficient use, and was their option for an emergency, such as the impending arrival of the singer with a potential full horde of draugr behind her.

There were still a half dozen smaller draugr left alive that Charlotte was picking off, but she immediately switched focus, ignoring them and charging toward the Knight. Elise, meanwhile turned away from the Knight, zipping over to the weaker draugr to finish them off before they could pounce on Charlotte from behind. A set of aether spikes took care of them in an instant, and she turned around just in time to see the flail flying at her once more.

What–?

She dodged as quickly as she could but wasn’t quite fast enough. The flail clipped her side, tearing a chunk out of her flesh and clothes, and sending her spinning out of control. The pain was blinding, and she barely managed to right herself in time to avoid crashing into the ground. She was not unfamiliar with pain, but had been a long time since she felt physical pain like this, and it left her momentarily disoriented.

Charlotte shouted something, but Elise didn’t hear it, and the next moment, she felt a powerful gust of wind beside her and heard a loud crash as something slammed into the ground just a foot or two away. Still not fully aware of what was happening, she was at least lucid enough to realize she couldn’t stay still like that, so she flew upward, and started using the dust from her wings to recover.

Her swimming vision cleared, and she saw the Knight wind up to swing again. It was still looking at her, for some reason, and she realized she needed to move. She was still too disoriented to use {Suggest} to misdirect it, but she could at least fly, and Charlotte was right there on the ground. As it swung, Charlotte rammed it with her shoulder, knocking its flail slightly off-course, letting Elise dodge it.

Why is it still aiming at me? Elise wondered.

She glanced down at the wound in her side. It was a bit gruesome, and was bleeding profusely, but it didn’t actually seem too deep. She could feel that a couple ribs were broken, and that she likely had some organ damage, but nothing was missing. Nothing but a big chunk of skin and other exterior flesh, at least. And she didn’t think the damage was anything immediately life-threatening. It was something she could easily recover from with the help of time and {Fairy Dust}.

Not that she had much of either right now, though. The Singer was still on its way, and if she put all her effort into healing, she wouldn’t have enough aether left to fight. She instead focused the dust more on stopping the bleeding and reducing the pain and clearing her head while turning her attention back to the draugr.

Charlotte had her hands buried in its armor, and was actively melting the flesh beneath, but the Knight’s gaze was still inexplicably focused on Elise. It swung again, but Elise had the wherewithal to redirect it this time as she flew near. Whatever the reason for its obsession with her, it ultimately worked out in their favor now that she was clearheaded again. Charlotte would have it dead shortly. Maybe not before the Singer arrived, but it wouldn’t be long.

They needed to leave, though. The Knight needed to die faster so they could retreat. She was injured, and they couldn’t afford to stay out long. Technically, they could just leave right then with the help of {Royal Presence}, but she wanted to have at least a little bit of closure here.

“Let’s kill it, then leave,” she said, swooping in over Charlotte’s head and firing another aether spike into the Knight.

“Okay!” replied Charlotte.

Elise felt Charlotte’s mana output increase as she pumped more acid into the Knight’s body. The Knight screamed in pain, but still, he did not even look at Charlotte, swinging again at Elise. Elise redirected the attack, firing two more aether spikes.

With each aether spike, she made slight modifications. It was something she had briefly discussed with Erun and Rayna, about how certain types of attacks worked better against certain types of defenses. The aether spike was the simplest of attacks, being more or less a battering ram of aether, but it could still be slightly altered to suit the target.

What Elise sensed from the knight’s defenses matched its outward appearance. It was like a hard steel suit of armor surrounding its soul, blocking and deflecting damage entirely. But Elise knew firsthand the weaknesses of such a defense. She shaped her aether spikes to be “sharper,” and more concentrated. The raw power didn’t increase, but the way it impacted the Knight’s soul changed, and it reacted more strongly with each one.

As Elise refined her attacks, Charlotte’s acid was doing a lot of damage as well. The Knight’s whole body was leaning to the side, and the holes in its armor where Charlotte had shoved her arms in were oozing foul sludge. The damage to its body was weakening its soul too, and it screamed in pain and rage, but the whole time, it never took its gaze off Elise, and all of its attacks were aimed at her.

It raised its arm for what would likely be its last swing, and at that moment, Elise finally succeeded. Her aether spike punched through its soul defenses, and the soul beneath was much more fragile. Its arm twitched, and then a second spike into the same hole in its soul finished it. Its body went limp, and it slumped forward, nearly falling onto Charlotte.

Dang it! Elise thought as she realized she had forgotten to disconnect from the System.

Growing stronger felt good, but that was a big chunk of experience hitting all at once. Between that, and the condensed soul fog she had stored in her soul over the past year, she wouldn’t have been surprised if she could hit the evolution threshold right then and there if she really wanted to.

“Okay, let’s go,” she said.

Charlotte’s wings appeared on her back, and the two rocketed off toward the tunnel they had come from, skirting along the edge of the cavern. The Singer had been nearly upon them, but had to change course to try and intercept them as they flew. For a little, Elise hoped they could outrun it, but it soon became clear that would not be happening.

Ignoring the throbbing pain in her side, she called out to Charlotte and slowed her pace, turning to face the approaching draugr. For now, the Singer was alone, but Elise could hear the rumbling of hundreds of footsteps behind it, and she could sense another powerful presence on the way. A third elite. Definitely more than they could handle as they were.

She waited until the Singer was within range, then activated {Royal Presence}. Just like before, it stopped in its tracks, staring at her blankly in midair. She felt the other presence closing in quickly as well, so she waited for it to get near as well so she could command both of them at once.

The new one looked more like a standard draugr than either of the other elites. The only real feature that made it stand out was its right arm, which was a long mass of entwined tentacles that narrowed and hardened into a spear-like point. Its face made it look like it had once been a younger man with long hair and a bushy beard, but the beard was now made of tentacles, and the hair was greasy and uneven and matted to his head. His clothes were fairly nondescript too, not making Elise think of anything in particular.

[ Abyssal Draugr Revolutionary, lvl ??? ]

She didn’t think much of its name at first, especially when it too stopped and stared at her when it entered the range of her presence. She only notice the problem when she tried to give it a command with {Royal Decree}.

“Leave, and command the other draugr to do the same.”

The Singer turned around instantly, but the Revolutionary stayed where it was. Elise only had a moment to be confused before it opened its tentacle-obscured mouth and spoke.

“No.”

Elise felt her hold on the Singer snap, and the other elite stopped midair, turning back around.
