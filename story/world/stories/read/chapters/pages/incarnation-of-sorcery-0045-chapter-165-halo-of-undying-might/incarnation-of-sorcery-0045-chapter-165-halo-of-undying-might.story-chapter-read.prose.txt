It didn’t take long for Ryland to fully realize that he was now facing perhaps the strongest foe in several decades, if not a century at least. Mavar really was Transcendent-ranked, as was infamously known across the Realms. A power that could rival and even overcome that of most Calamities. A power that was unmatched in the ranks of the Grand Frame.

The peak of what mortal mages could attain in a given lifetime. A rank he had been at for years if not decades, far longer than the level Pious had merely reached when Ryland had fought the Order mage.

That was what Mavar Val Arona, the self-proclaimed Demiurge of Rhel, was capable of.

“I know you, Ryland,” Mavar said. His eyes bled starlight. His knees, elbows, and shoulders had turned into vents ejecting compressed energy. His entire form pulsed like the core of a star, convulsing out magic in waves so potent, he could feel the void reality of the cosmos changing its very nature in the presence of a Transcendent. “And I know you still think you’ll win.”

“Wrong,” Ryland said. “I don’t think any such thing. I am certain of it.”

Mavar growled. “Your arrogance will be the end of you, as I’ve always said. And I will be the one to deliver that end! [Suncatcher].”

His fists sparked with immense power. If he had been glowing like a star, both his hands now turned into the compressed light of entire galaxies. Energy shot off them in every direction, scorching into the cosmos, spikes so potent with power that Ryland had started sensing the birth of an atmosphere around Mavar without even the need for any spells.

Ryland was just about to cast his own spells when he felt something. He was being tugged downwards.

Mavar grinned at his surprise. Then punched him.

The flash forward was impressively fast. Oh, yes. Ryland was correct. The Demiurge of Rhel was an opponent he couldn’t hold back against. He had been right to unleash his Incarnation form the instant he had arrived, for it was all that stopped Mavar.

Well, his Incarnation form and the barrier he had already wrought around his body with [Eternal Bastion].

Ryland didn’t feel hurt. Even besides his barrier, there were defensive and strengthening Inscriptions upon him that further decreased any impact he suffered.

But all of that only stopped most direct damage. The moment the punch from that fist emblazoned with Mavar’s [Suncatcher] spell connected with Ryland with a blast that could rival a supernova, Ryland felt himself shifting position. Normally, his barrier spell would have kept him stable spatiotemporally as well, but Mavar’s strength shattered that assumption.

Ryland was suddenly faced with a grinning Mavar, the light from his shining fist granting his expression an extra layer of ferocity.

“I told you,” Mavar crowed, voice audible despite the rush of air tearing past them, despite the explosive energy blasting off their continued impact. “Your arrogance will be your undoing!”

In answer, Ryland just countered him. “[Endless Evolution].” Mavar was clearly casting more spells, but Ryland went on with his own magic regardless. “[Hierophant’s Resurrection]. [Everlife Spectre].”

Whatever spell Mavar had cast had granted him a cloak made of shifting Glyphs. Ryland noted it for later, then punched Mavar back with the arm with which he wasn’t blocking the glowing fist.

The impact was just as explosive as the Demiurge’s hit on him, the rippling arm of mana manifested through the Ancient Hierophant’s miasmic magic unleashing a tremendous detonation that rocked the upper layers of Rhel’s atmosphere as they continued descending.

But it didn’t dislodge Mavar.

If anything, it only reinforced his grip. At some point after that initial punch, he had held onto the now skeletal-spectral arm that Ryland had blocked his blow with.

“That was nothing!” Mavar cried. Indeed, his barrier hadn’t even taken a scratch. “See what I mean!”

He punched back. Ryland tanked it, his own barrier rippling even after he cast [Animus Aegis], before he retaliated with another blow. Impact after impact continued detonating, lightning up the sky of Rhel with constant, sky-hueing flashes like a plummeting meteor wreathed in a lightning-firing storm cloud.

At one point, Ryland managed to slip his arm free from Mavar’s grip, stabbing the spiritual skeleton into Mavar’s body. It normally wouldn’t have worked. He had already seen just how powerful Mavar’s barriers could be on that moon.

But Ryland had once again managed to create a modified rift with [Translocate] that allowed him to bypass the Demiurge’s defences.

“I have you now,” Ryland said, uncaring of the punch landing on his skull. It left its mark, fracturing even the Incarnation-empowered barrier from [Animus Aegis], but it was nothing to be worried about. “I have your soul.”

Mavar glanced almost contemptuously at him. Then he proceeded to tear himself away from Ryland. Quite literally.

A good third of his body remained behind in Ryland’s grip as blood and viscera exploded everywhere, the other two-thirds taking up a position farther away. He showed no pain, no inkling that he had just performed what was perhaps one of the most horrific acts of magic one could enact.

Not that it was truly horrific to experience for Mavar, clearly. In mere instants, the two-thirds became whole once more as powerful healing Inscriptions returned him to normal.

“You were saying?” Mavar said mockingly.

“[Alchemic Reconstruction],” Ryland incanted, channelling his magic into every organic matter in the vicinity besides himself.

Mavar’s eyes widened at the response.

Normally, the spell used Ryland’s Soul Glyph of Energy to reform matter by manipulating the links of energy between the particles that made it up. It was one of the most effective ways of reconstructing things Ryland had ever come across.

But the same principles could be applied to living matter as well as non-living matter. He just had to add his Soul Glyph of Genesis as an important Subglyph in the spell circle.

Right then, the one-third of Mavar the Demiurge had left behind now transformed, reshaping with gruesome effectiveness and bloody precision until it had turned into a scaleless, flesh-and-bones wyvern. It immediately shot at Mavar with its maw flaming.

“[World’s End],” Mavar incanted.

With a construct taking up the Demiurge’s attention, Ryland had no problem reading the opposing spell circle. In simple terms, it was just dialling up the air pressure to an unfathomable degree, much like how one of the Guardians had attempted in order to slow Ryland down. In this case, Mavar was taking up a notch to directly crush the gory wyvern.

Which had no effect whatsoever because Ryland had already imbued his creation with the same protection he himself enjoyed. Rippling coats of mana cracked across the man-sized wyvern’s body, keeping it entirely unharmed as it almost reached Mavar with its ferocious intent entirely intact.

Mavar physically slammed it away with one arm, recasting his earlier spell, but modified to hold the wyvern in place with unstoppable pressure. Then he attempted to punch in with [Suncatcher] again.

Ryland’s counter was a lot harsher this time. “[Reality Scar].”

Mavar dipped backwards just as fast as he had attempted to attack Ryland. He scowled as reality started tearing apart, scars of nothingness fracturing across the Rhellian sky.

“Now, you’re the one wasting time,” the Demiurge said. “What a nuisance. You truly think the whelps will be able to free themselves?”

“Of course,” Ryland said with easy confidence. “I have no doubt they’ll perform admirably. So long as you don’t interfere, that is.”

Mavar was clearly having a great deal of trouble stopping himself from glowering. “I’ve had enough of this. You seem to doubt the sheer extent of my powers and capabilities. So, I will happily elucidate you.”

Magic poured out of Mavar like a faucet turned on to its maximum. His Soul Glyph manifested into reality. The Soul Glyph of Unity.

Ryland frowned. The Glyph wasn’t just manifesting next to Mavar. It was appearing everywhere. The pockets of air around Ryland, the ground far, far beneath, the clouds floating not too far off. Even the sky was daubed by it, like twinkling stars blanketing the night. Mavar had Inscribed his entire Realm with his Soul Glyph.

“Unity, Ryland,” he said, voice rippling over itself in multiple echoes. “We are all one. And we stand united. Against you.”

As evidence of his words, the entire planet of Rhel attacked Ryland.

“[Awakened Avatar],” Ryland managed to get off.

His spell fired just in time. Air compressed upon him with hammering blows almost as potent as Mavar’s [Suncatcher]. Light became searing beams seeking to sizzle right through to his very soul. Space itself attempted to crush him, closing down the scars of unreality that Ryland had cast upon the world.

Impressive. Mavar Val Arona was crushing reality itself around Ryland with his Soul Glyph. The boundlessness of his power almost tempted Ryland to consider its applications.

An impulse quickly curtailed by how Rhel itself sought to end him then and there.

“[Halo of Undying Might]!” Ryland incanted when it started to get a bit much.

Finally, it was all repelled. The weight of the air, the drag of the gravity that had brought the surface of Rhel much, much closer, the beams of light and occasional blasts of lightning. They were all now striking ineffectually against a sphere of compressed mana about ten armspans wide around Ryland.

For a brief moment, he was once again impressed by the sheer level of catastrophe Mavar had cast against Ryland. It wasn’t just the blinding storm of his world’s atmosphere or the searing energy of light and radiation.

Ruptured pieces of the ground were now flying up like reverse meteors to crash against Ryland’s barrier. Mavar was nearly out of stops to pull.

Ryland raised a hand. “I’ve had enough as well. [Continental Step].”

Mavar cursed. Probably because he recognized the spell.

A crackling golden fracture ripped apart in the sky before a country-wide cliff came crashing down. Its lower end glowed molten hot, overheated air forming a scorching corona as it crashed down upon Mavar. He squawked in a very satisfyingly undignified manner.

It had also smashed in upon Ryland himself, destroying all the spells Mavar had been flinging in his direction. His expanded barrier from the combination of [Awakened Avatar] and [Halo of Undying Might] ensured he was perfectly safe. It was just Mavar being smashed down.

Sadly, it didn’t last long. The footstep of the behemoth Ryland had called upon had stopped as sorcery empowered Mavar to an incredibly mighty degree. Magic crackled and sparked along his length as he began shoving the titanic foot back into the rift high above them.

“This is nothing,” Mavar ground out. “[Terrestrial Abhorrence].”

Rhel broke even further as Mavar counterattacked. Mountains ripped off the earth, glowing with heat and sparking with lightning. Water ascended up in great, twisting spouts. Wind whipped into cyclones. They all slammed at Ryland yet again.

As before, they collided against his barrier without leaving any mark against him directly. But he couldn’t call them ineffectual. Not any longer.

Even as mountains shattered against Ryland, as lightning and energy sparked off him, they regathered. It was almost like Mavar had cast his own version of [Alchemic Reconstruction].

Ryland didn’t give him any room. “[Star Brigade].”

Rifts cracked open. Bolts of energy straight from the sun itself crashed into whatever constructs Mavar was trying to build, eradicating them in no time. The skies of Rhel lit up, not just at the frightful magic its ruler was using to rip the planet itself apart, but at the sheer overwhelming power that Ryland could call forth as well.

“I have the strength of an entire Realm behind me, Ryland,” Mavar shouted through the din of their magic, his voice thundering over everything. “Do you truly think the centuries of piecemeal power you’ve stolen can stand up to the faith reality itself bestows upon me?”

“You’re projecting, Mavar. [Extinction Event].”

“How dare—”

Mavar’s outraged cry was cut off when Ryland’s next spell took effect. Since the sun itself was dealing with everything Mavar was casting, he was free to switch from defence to complete and utter offence. The spherical barrier around him cracked and condensed into energy so dense, he started sparking.

Then he shot at Mavar in the same manner he had attacked the glaive wielding Guardian who had attempted to stop his meteor. Ryland crushed through reality itself with uncompromising power, flying with the ferocious force of a species-ending comet.

Mavar cried out, tried to stop him with a variety of spells, even attempted to turn tail and flee, but it was all for naught.

Ryland crashed in with an impact that blasted everything so far away, only a vacuum was left behind at their exact location. Mavar’s barrier was broken, his body suffering tremendous burns, his chest mangled entirely as his breaths failed him.

Yet he was conscious. As the rest of the world rushed in with the subsequent implosion to crush them both, Mavar’s powerful healing Inscriptions were already reconstructing his shattered body. Even when Ryland stabbed into him with [Everlife Spectre] once more, he didn’t seem defeated in the slightest. If anything, his ferocity redoubled.

“My world endures,” Mavar said, not even bothering to tear himself away from Ryland. “As do I.”

He did. He was right. Ryland found himself blinking rapidly as everything around him took a twisted turn. The skeletal, spectral arm of his that had sunk into Mavar just kept going and going. It felt like he was reaching into a fathomless lake, a soul so enormous that it defied belief.

Ryland’s eyes widened as the realization hit him, as the universe itself seemed to throb around him. Colours distorted, light stretched and twisted, space no longer made sense. If Ryland hadn’t had centuries of rifting experience, his senses might have rebelled. “This…”

Mavar had the gall to laugh like a maniac. “It’s like reaching into the soul of the world itself, isn’t it? You see, now? [Existence Aggression].”

Ryland’s arm immediately began burning. The pain was a degree so severe, it bypassed all his blocks against it and he instinctively removed his arm from within Mavar. All he retained was a smoking stump.

Mavar spat at him. “You’ve never felt true wrath in your entire life. I’m going to rectify that.”

When he punched back, it was like the power of [Suncatcher] multiplied by ten. Even the barrier of [Awakened Avatar] rippled all over Ryland as he was sent flying back by almost a dozen armspans. And Mavar still wasn’t done. Another blow landed, enwreathed by a storm lighting up with lightning all at once. Then came another, covered with molten earth.

“[Reality Scar],” Ryland incanted, trying to carve open reality to create some space between the two of them.

It didn’t work for long. With the way Mavar was manipulating space itself with his Soul Glyph, repairing reality to stop unreality, he bridged the gap to resume his relentless attack. The aggression of existence indeed.

But the little bit of time Ryland got before that had been enough.

“[Corona of Eradicating Wrath].”

Dozens of orbs of blistering magic ripped into being around Ryland. They were accompanied by more rifts cracking open nearby, a hundred demonic arms ripping out with ravenous intent and thousands of baleful glares. Scarlet eyes of pure, unbridled malice injected fear into the world itself.

Now their clash was a lot more even. Ryland was perhaps treating Mavar like any other opponent, allowing the Demiurge to live up to his self-proclaimed moniker and send forth magic that set him apart from nearly any other spellcaster in the entirety of the Accords.

But that didn’t mean Ryland had no answer to his might. That didn’t mean Ryland couldn’t overcome it.

Rhel itself tore apart at their next collision despite the fact that they were still a few thousand armspans high in the air. Fractures snaked across its surface as Mavar pulled out the blood of his own world to assault his enemy. Rivers and seas emptied as water broke apart into explosions that could have wiped out entire cities with no trouble.

Even the atmosphere itself was cracking away, breaking apart, leaking into the cosmos as Mavar didn’t discriminate from whence he pulled his power.

But the dozens of demonic arms Ryland had called upon struck back with their ripping fury. With their endlessly devouring gluttony. After all, everything Mavar was enacting was a result of mana, and the demonic arms Ryland had summoned ate magic itself.

The earth, the water, the air, all forms of energy that flew abound, they were all consumed summarily. Much of it escaped the demonic clutches, but that was where those revolving orbs of condensed destruction came in. Ryland remembered how that scimitar wielding Guardian had turned into a drill, and now, Ryland was creating the same effect.

And yet, Mavar matched that destruction by empowering himself even further.

Rhel’s surface now fell away entirely, its very core emerging out with a sea of molten magma flooding the world and turning the sky black with smoke and lightning. Ryland really, really hoped everyone was off the Realm already. The Rhellian Institute was on the other side of the planet, but at this rate, everything would truly die.

With volcanic fury, the power of the core rose in a geyser, magic studding it with spikes of furious energy. It covered Mavar, obscuring his form entirely, before slamming towards Ryland with the true wrath of an entire Realm.

Another detonation went off that was the envy of stars themselves. Destruction rained upon the already-mutilated Realm of Rhel, debris falling in parabolas spanning its atmosphere. Several cannonballs of broken sorcery even shot into the cosmos, pinging off the barrier around the moon itself.

Through it all, both Ryland and Mavar survived.

Ryland’s Incarnation form blazed as ever, pieces of him breaking off, but he himself remained unharmed as ever.

Meanwhile, Mavar looked like his own magic had dealt greater damage to him. His body was burning in truth, a few bits of molten magma eating through his skin and flesh, setting parts of his hair aflame. Yet, he hadn’t lost his smile. There was a fire in his eyes too, and that hadn’t dimmed. Not yet.

“Are you still holding back, Ryland?” Mavar asked. “Even now?”

Ryland took a breath to steady himself. Then he stretched out a hand again. “[Overrealm Rift].”

Mavar froze. Even the flames licking his body petrified. “What? No.”

Magic emerged from Ryland’s incandescent manasoul like strands of finest sunlight, a thousand threads latching onto reality and separating it into dozens of planes. The rift that began forming was carving open a hole that excavated in a wholly different direction.

In a divine direction.

“Is this how you intend to hold nothing back?” Mavar growled. “Is this your answer to your impending defeat? Relying on the Great Gods?” He scoffed. “The ones who hate you?”

Ryland didn’t answer yet again. His spell would do the talking for him.

“Fine, then,” Mavar spat. His eyes glowed with malevolent magic. “I will show that even with the Great Gods’ stolen power, you won’t—”

A crack that nearly shattered Ryland’s eardrums interrupted both his and his opponent’s magic. Another flash lit up the sky, turning the red and black of a destroyed realm into a blanket of radiant white, all emanating from one point.

The moon had broken, its surface cracking apart like a shattered egg.
