Callie would have loved to begin the search for the egg immediately. Granny Ethel had given her some good clues—she needed to find a place that was warm, dark, and possibly moist. Barns, coops, and sheds seem like very likely places where an egg could be kept. Some villagers had special workhouses where they would do all the cooking on the brick stove. Those should also be checked.

But, unfortunately, after her apprenticeship, Callie once again had to head to Mister Blackthorn's lessons. She was barely able to pay attention as her mind was filled with places where she had to search. Should she make a list so she wouldn't lose track of which places she'd already checked?

"Calluna!" Mister Blackthorn addressed her sternly.

It was only then that Callie noticed that the man was standing in front of her. The proximity allowed her to see the large scar on his cheek up close. She nearly flinched back. Even if the scar was old and white, it ran deep. It must have really hurt.

"Y-Yes?" Callie squeaked out.

"What are we learning about today?" He demanded, leaning in closer. His face was even scarier up close.

"Um…" She faltered for a moment, racking her brain for an answer. "Building shelter?"

The man straightened and nodded. "That's right. Pay attention, Calluna. You too will have to learn how to construct your own shelter."

Callie nodded, although inwardly she wasn't happy. That sounded like it would take a while. She didn't have the time to try to build a stick house. Finding the egg was far more important. But obviously, she couldn't announce that, so she was stuck watching Mister Blackthorn's instructions along with the other girls.

Well, it wasn't actually instructions. He just gave a few tips, like not building a shelter anywhere near water, and then brought them into the forest, not too far from the flower meadow. Then they had to build a shelter as well as they could.

Aurelia and other girls had no issues with this task, having done it before, but Callie didn't even know where to begin. What would Mister Blackthorn be happy to call a shelter?

They weren't given much to work with either, just a pocket knife for cutting branches and some twine. Callie wasn't in the mood to think hard, so she cut some thin branches to make a roof and tied them together with twine as best as she could.

The resulting shelter was very small, but Callie could fit inside it, and that was all that mattered. Right?

"This won't do at all," Mister Blackthorn announced. "Redo it!"

"What?!" Callie exclaimed.

"Come, take a look at what other girls have built," he motioned, and Callie begrudgingly followed him.

Their shelters were undoubtedly better than hers. They were bigger, made of larger, sturdier branches, covered with a thick layer of leaves on top, and lined with moss and ferns on the inside.

"Your most critical mistake was building it low in between the roots. Should it rain, water would gather there, and you would be soaking wet."

Callie grit her teeth. She wanted to proclaim that she had no time for this. Searching for the egg was far more important. But she didn't dare. Instead, she skulked away to prepare another shelter.

"Good luck, carrothead!" Aurelia sneered as she left the lesson, her head held high. Her shelter had been proclaimed as the best one.

Frustration filled Callie, and she could feel her magic bubbling within her in response. But she held it firmly in place. Burning the forest down wouldn't solve anything.

Wait… could I perhaps grow my own shelter?

She had managed to grow the flowers and the roots around Callen's and the glauxis's legs. Perhaps, she could guide the roots to grow in the form of a shelter?

Callie shook her head. No, growing a whole shelter or tree would certainly not be possible, not to mention it would likely look suspicious. But perhaps she could at least grow enough greenery at the bottom to save herself from the trouble of gathering it.

This time, she chose to gather thicker sticks for her shelter, using the already broken branches on the forest floor. After that, she tied leafy branches and large ferns to the larger ones, using the twine. A thought crossed her mind as she built the shelter.

Could something like this be used for hiding the egg? If it were small enough and lined with plenty of moss, it would be hard to notice. The inside would be dark and warm, perfect for an egg. Adding a bit of moisture wasn't an issue either.

This was an unpleasant thought. If the thief had used a shelter in the forest to hide the egg, then finding it would be so much harder. All the more reason she couldn't waste any more time here.

With the outside complete, Callie crawled inside the shelter, making sure that Mister Blackthorn couldn't see her.

Let's try this!

Callie's heart was beating with excitement as she reached for the energy within her.

Grow moss, grow moss, grow moss!

She imagined the moss growing on the bottom of her shelter, but for some reason, the energy resisted her. Why was it resisting her? She tried to push and order the magic to move, but it refused. Was she too angry again?

Callie took a deep breath to calm herself and imagined the moss growing again. The magic seemed to—

"Calluna, what are you doing?" Mister Blackthorn called, his head peeking into her shelter.

Callie yelped and jumped back with such force that her flimsy shelter collapsed right on top of her. Had he seen her trying to use magic? Was her mark glowing?

I was too impatient! I shouldn't use magic while anyone is nearby.

Mister Blackthorn lifted the destroyed shelter from her, looking down at her with a disapproving look.

"I have no clue what you were trying to accomplish, but I suggest you take these lessons seriously. A flimsy shelter like this, made from old, dry branches, wouldn't protect you. Not to mention the lack of insulation. Nights can easily get very cold, even during summer. Should you not make a proper shelter, you could even suffer from hypothermia."

It appeared that he hadn't noticed her magic. Callie sighed with relief.

"Calluna!" Mister Blackthorn growled, likely having misinterpreted her sigh as annoyance.

"No, I-I didn't—"

"You shall come to my lessons every evening until you can make a proper shelter," he said firmly, his face wrinkling into an angry, scary grimace.

Callie wanted to protest; she really did. But the scary expression on the older man made her hands tremble. There was no way she could object and not get into even bigger trouble.

"Yes, sir…" she replied meekly.

This wouldn't work. She needed to find a way to search for the egg while the chores and lessons kept her busy. But how?

***

Late that evening, after Mom and Dad were asleep, Callie sneaked out of the house, heading to the chicken coop.

"Wiggles, Wiggles!" She called in a whisper, afraid someone might hear her.

The chickens inside the coop let out some disgruntled noises in their sleep, but settled down quickly. There was no sign of the mireling. Callie headed behind it and called again. No response.

"Wiggles must be sleeping too… I guess I'll have to ask him tomorrow morning," Callie said to herself dejectedly.

As she turned to return home, she noticed a faint glow coming from the forest. The light bobbed up and down, and for a second, Callie was startled. Was that a ghost?!

But as the light came closer and took the distinct shape of small antlers, Callie realized who it actually was. Snowglow.

"Snowglow!" Callie called cheerfully and ran towards the glimmerhare.

Callie!

Once Snowglow had hopped out of the forest, her antlers stopped glowing. She approached Callie cautiously, looking around nervously.

"How are you doing, Snowglow? How's your leg?" Callie asked.

Cloth uncomfortable.

Callie nodded. It certainly would feel uncomfortable to keep it on all the time.

"Let's take a look. Behind the coop so nobody sees."

Snowglow and Callie went behind the coop, where the glimmerhare lit her horns again, allowing Callie to see the makeshift bandage. It was covered in dirt again; however, after untying it, she saw that the injury underneath had healed significantly, with a fresh layer of pinkish skin on top.

"This looks much better!" Callie said cheerfully.

Feel better.

"Would you like something to eat?"

Yes.

Callie grabbed a carrot from the garden and, after washing it, presented it to the glimmerhare.

Tasty, tasty!

Callie watched with delight as Snowglow ate, her little black nose and whiskers moving adorably the whole while. Her antlers glowed steadily the whole time. Callie leaned in closer, trying to understand how exactly they were glowing. It seemed that it wasn't actually the antlers that were glowing but rather the thin fuzz that covered them.

Snowglow's white coat looked soft and pleasant to the touch, so after a while, Callie tentatively reached out her hand. "Do you mind if I touch you?"

Snowglow froze, her nose twitching nervously.

Callie pulled her hand back."Sorry, sorry! I won't do it if you don't want me to."

Snowglow looked at Callie for a moment, then resumed eating.

I do not mind.

Callie could barely contain her excitement. Slowly, carefully, she reached her hand towards Snowglow and touched her white fur. It was incredibly soft. Probably the softest thing Callie had ever touched. She couldn't help but keep stroking the glimmerhare while she continued to eat contentedly.

Feels nice.

Callie spent a good while patting Snowglow, all of her worries completely washed away. Frankly, she would be content spending the rest of her life patting Snowglow. But looking at the white fur, she was reminded of the glauxis and his missing egg.

"Hey, Snowglow, do you know a creature named glauxis?" Callie asked.

Snowglow cocked her head.

Glo-kys?

"I met one recently. He was huge with the head of an owl and body of a lynx and large white wings."

Snowglow trembled at the description.

P-Predator!

"It's okay! It's not here now," Callie hurried to reassure the glimmerhare. It made sense that it would be afraid of the glauxis. Callie was also afraid. But that was all the more reason to find his egg quickly and return it.

Snowglow relaxed, albeit not fully, as Callie could feel how her muscles were still wound tight underneath her touch.

"The problem is… It is somewhere nearby, searching for his egg," Callie continued.

Why egg here?

"Somebody stole it. I want to find it and give it back to the glauxis so he would leave," Callie explained.

Thief… stealing eggs horrible.

Callie nodded in agreement. "You haven't seen anyone hiding or carrying an egg, have you?"

No.

It was to be expected. Obviously, finding the egg wouldn't be easy.

But it must be the horrible human.

"Horrible human?"

One putting traps in the forest. I saw him.

"What?! Really? What did he look like? Wait, it's a he? So it was a man?"

The barrage of questions startled Snowglow, who looked at Callie with a trembling nose.

"Sorry… Could you tell me more?"

Cannot tell humans apart. But I think it man. Much taller than Callie.

"What about his hair and eyes? Clothes?"

Too dark. Couldn't tell.

Callie thought it over. It wouldn't be surprising that the man who had laid out cruel traps in the forest would also be the one stealing eggs. But why? Was it really all for money?

If it were for money, then the person must be a really greedy one. Although some of the villagers could be quite mean, she couldn't think of one who would go that far just to get coins. Things were often traded around the village without even using money, and it was mostly needed to get stuff from the merchants coming into Greyfen.

"Wait… It could have been one of the merchants," Callie concluded. Merchants were always eager to get more and more money, and they weren't from the village. The merchant also wouldn't care if glauxis attacked so long as he could run away with the egg.

Callie didn't want to believe that anyone from the village could be so evil as to make traps for animals and steal eggs from arcane beasts. Only an outsider could do something like that. That meant that Callie had to search for a place where an outsider would feel safe hiding the egg.
