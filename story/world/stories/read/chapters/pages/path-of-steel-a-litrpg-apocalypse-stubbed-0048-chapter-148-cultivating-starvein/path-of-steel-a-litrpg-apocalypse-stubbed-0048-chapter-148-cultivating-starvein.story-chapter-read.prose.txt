Even if I could do it, it’s not like they’ll line themselves up for me. Everyone else is trying to get merit, too. The crucial factor will be how many enemies I can find, not how many I can kill.

It’s a shame I can’t be everywhere at once…

“You get their merit points. If they die, they die. You just got to protect them,” the champion replied.

Gary was about to feel disgruntled when he noticed the reactions of everyone around him. Even the Core Formation disciples looked at him with envy.

Can they not afford mercenaries? Gary wondered. Oh shit, I’m rich! I should definitely get some. It feels like a scaling thing, but I don’t want to stake all my money on this… Maybe they’ll just die immediately.

System, give me three mercenaries.

Beef Boy, Level 22 (Mercenary of Gary Steel)

Hey! was all Gary had time to think.

If this is the power of the average level 22, killing fifty might be doable…

The Starvein Immortal Body Manual.

The point was, the manual’s introduction promised endless power and nigh-indestructibility at the later stages. Gary liked the sound of that. Finally free of the stress of having to memorize the book, he settled into reading.

The scripture contained five stages.
