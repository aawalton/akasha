With so many other Singers present, Luke figured the charging Zelaui would stop and not continue her mad charge, but soon realized the rage shown in her expression trumped any sort of deal to leave him untouched and unharmed. Those annoying claw-like weapons appeared in her hands as she lunged at him from several strides away. The Singer roared her anger and slashed at the air, but Luke was no longer there.

In a physical battle, he wouldn't stand much of a chance against her, he figured. Trying to punch a fighter-focused Lifeweaver back in the pink dungeon had been instructive. When the bones in his hand shattered, he learned the lesson that he should keep his hands to himself in a battle against fighter types, and he had learned that lesson well. Instead, he had weavestepped to the side and hunched down just as Zelaui flew past, striking out with threads of mana. Mana would not be her main source of power, not as a fighter, but it would at least slow her enough to see reason and prevent Luke from having to kill her. A few neat slices at precise points of her mana channels cut off the flow to her arms and legs.

As predicted, she landed in a heap. What he hadn't anticipated was that she got back up, angrier than ever.

"Calm down," Luke said. "The list? Remember?"

"The list can chew faar-leaves for all I care!" Zelaui spat.

With that, her skin glowed a dull brown as she activated some skill he hadn't seen before. Another lunge followed, this one too fast for Luke to follow. Both sets of claws pierced his upper body, tearing through muscle and organs alike. No claw had come close to his heart or lungs, which were protected at all times, but it had been a close thing.

Luke did his best to hide the dull pain. While it didn't hurt as much as it would have before he became Integrated, it was still quite a few blades embedded in his gut. He raised his eyebrow. "Done?"

That made her angrier. This time, it wasn't a surprise. More Singers were gathering, and a familiar voice shouted orders at Zelaui, trying and failing to get her to listen. When she didn't, and pulled one hand free to slash at Luke's throat, he took action again. Reaching out, he used Weaver's Whip with Strengthen Thread, focusing on maximizing impact. While he didn't want to reveal everything he was capable of, a taste wouldn't hurt. That way, they'd know he wasn't a pushover.

When Zelaui's wrist reached Luke's neck, her hand was no longer on it. He had wrapped it in a thread and sheared clean through with a yank and even pocketed it to increase her confusion. It seemed to work as she stared at the bleeding stump that was her forearm. Her face twisted as she tried to make sense of what she was seeing, then came another scream of rage as she yanked her other hand free. Was she going to try the same thing again? Really? Yep.

Before she got the chance, the owner of that familiar voice reached them. The massive hand of Perwen, the paladin-type Singer who had been able to resurrect the others, caught hold of Zelaui's remaining limb.

"That's enough," he said, his voice too quiet for anyone but her and Luke to hear.

Her face was white, but her expression didn't soften. "My honor demands!"

"Your honor is like the wind," Perwen said, his voice stern but not angry. "The Lifeweaver is not to be harmed. Not under any circumstances. You know this."

"B-but," she said, now looking like she was about to cry.

Perwen shook his head and released her arm. "Return to your duties, melody of my heart."

What Luke figured was a term of endearment just made things worse. Zelaui stiffened, gave Perwen a glare, and shook her head before turning to leave. "We will have words of this in private."

Once she had left and Luke had healed himself, he turned to the tall, wide-set Singer. "Is she your wife or something?"

Perwen gave him a long, searching look, then sighed. "She is indeed. Singers do not apologize for the behavior of others. If she wishes to make amends, she is free to do so on her own."

"Uh, okay."

"May I have her hand so that it can be re-attached?" Perwen asked, holding out a massive palm in front of Luke.

Luke shrugged and handed it over while also throwing out a question he'd been wondering about. "So, how do you resurrect people?"

"Thank you," Perwen said, ignoring the question as he directed Luke to keep going with the original trio who'd found him on the street.

"Fine then," Luke told Perwen. "Keep your secrets."

The following hour consisted of a lot of standing around and waiting for different Singers to move him back and forth between a multitude of temporary buildings that looked like a cross between a tent and a trailer. It suited Luke just fine, since he could spend that time delving into the weave with his mind to examine the large portal in the near distance. The reach thing had been a bummer because it felt just like with the Threads of Mana skill. Extending his influence past a short area was difficult, but possible with enough work, at least to an extent. If he had an echo thread to work with, that limitation didn't matter, but in this case, he didn't. That meant needing a massive amount of concentration to get anything done. The Singers standing guard near him kept glancing in his direction, but Luke ignored them, pushing himself deeper into the meditative state necessary to even see the system weave. That first step took the better part of that hour of waiting. Finding the portal wasn't difficult, since it was so different from everything else. Even then, it didn't appear as a portal. Not in the way Luke's had in the dungeon. It was a thread like any other, and the only reason he found it was because so many other threads converged on or around it.

A Singer thread was different from a human's in ways that were difficult to describe. Saying it had a different hue would be wrong, but also right. In the end, they were nothing more than manifestations in Luke's mind. The threads he saw were all there, part of the weave, but also, they weren't. Trying to explain the weave even to himself was an exercise in futility, and he'd long since learned to just go with it. Now, the portal. Since it was a thread originating from a separate system weave from Earth's, Luke found himself able to identify it with ease. Since it was a thread, all he needed to do was cut it. Separated from the Singer system, the portal would wither and die. Perhaps the Singers would go with it, but he doubted that.

Standing there in a half-open tent on wheels with his eyes closed, he could pick out Singers coming and going through the portal and throughout the camp. Getting an exact number of them was impossible, but the rough estimate put them at several hundred. Far more than Luke had thought going into this. Coming to this place had been a quick decision that he had based on gut feeling, but now he was unsure how to go about things. Identifying the portal in the weave was not the same as being able to cut it from a distance, so he would need to get closer. That was a risk, though, since the only way they'd allow him to get that close was if he traveled through the portal with them. Safe passage to the Singer world was what Relian had bargained for, but what then? Weavestep and Manipulate Thread should be enough to escape most binds, but he couldn't go in and take the skills for granted. Heading through would be like entering Infernal Flux again, and he doubted the Singers would be arrogant enough to allow him free rein of the place.
