After they had finally settled the question of whether everyone was willing to continue, Z suggested that they take a little time to figure out an actual plan for reaching the lowest floor of Tartarus, which, according to her, wasn't actually all that far away in the grand scheme of the facility, although Chloe suspected that not that far was probably a very relative term.

Getting there wasn't going to be quite as simple as Chloe had hoped, because Z apparently had a rather important problem to explain, and judging by the way she manifested her laptop and then opened it in front of everyone, Chloe had a feeling they were about to receive some kind of extremely serious presentation. Sure enough, Z connected the laptop to a small portable projector which came from… somewhere? Chloe wasn't really looking since she was busy thinking about what she'd do when she met Mom again, anyway, there was now a large image that popped up on the wall.

Chloe leaned forward slightly. "You made a slideshow?"

Z nodded. "Correct, there are several important things everyone needs to understand before we proceed, and I believe this will be the most efficient way to explain them."

Everyone gradually found places to sit or lean against the surrounding walls, with Chloe dropping down onto the floor beside Sierra while Raenof took a more comfortable position against the wall, Greg stood nearby with his arms folded, and Liz and Percy settled in where they could see whatever Z was about to show them.

Chloe wasn't entirely sure what to expect from the presentation, especially since the whole situation still felt a little strange to her; they had gone from rescuing everyone and arguing about whether they should continue, to apparently having a proper planning session, which made this feel less like an adventure and more like one of those meetings Mom used to have whenever something really important was happening. Then again, this was pretty important overall.

Z, meanwhile, seemed completely unfazed by the change in atmosphere as she clicked a bunch of stuff and began her presentation. "Although I am no longer an Administrator of Divinity Inc., I retained access to a number of my old files, including several documents I had saved locally before my resignation, and one of those files may be particularly useful to us."

The projector flickered, and a new image appeared across the wall. Chloe leaned forward immediately. It looked like some kind of enormous set of architectural plans, except these were far more complicated than any ordinary building blueprint she'd ever seen, with multiple levels drawn in detail, a lot of different symbols scattered throughout the layout, and dozens of small notes written beside various areas. There were even different markings indicating what appeared to be entrances, checkpoints, restricted areas, and other things Chloe couldn't immediately make sense of.

"Whoa... what are those?" she asked, staring at the projected image.

"These are the initial draft plans for Divinity Inc.'s... special operation into Tartarus." Z explained.

Chloe stared at the plans for another second before her expression slowly changed. "Oh, you mean, invasion, right?"

"Yes, the invasion." Z continued, "These are only the initial drafts, so we should not assume that everything shown here remains accurate. Tartarus is an enormous and constantly changing environment, and considering how much time has passed since these plans were created, it is almost certain that certain routes and other aspects of the plan have changed."

She tapped a section of the projected plans, causing one of the areas to become highlighted. "However, even if portions of this information are outdated, these documents should still provide us with a general idea of what we are likely to encounter on our way to the lowest floor, as well as the obstacles Divinity Inc. originally anticipated having to overcome in order to reach that location."

Z shifted the display slightly, bringing another section of the blueprint into focus. "The first and most immediate threat will be the waves of automated security units and Divinity Inc. personnel stationed throughout the facility. Based on these records, the deeper we go, the more frequently we'll encounter them, and they appear to have been deployed specifically to slow down or eliminate anyone attempting to reach the lower levels."

Chloe tilted her head, looking at the complicated diagrams on the wall. "So... robots and a bunch of Divinity Inc. employees?"

"Correct."

"Well, that doesn't sound too bad."

Z stared at Chloe for a moment before calmly clicking to the next section of the presentation. "Well, if that is all you are concerned about, then I suppose you will be pleased to hear that the security forces are not the greatest obstacle we are likely to encounter."

Chloe's smile faded slightly. "They're not?"

"No. The next major challenge will be dealing with the other Administrators."

Chloe's expression immediately changed. "Oh."

Liz glanced toward the projected plans before looking back at Z. "Wait, how many Administrators are there?"

"There are several former Administrators who have resigned from Divinity Inc. over the years, and not all of the ones who remained were involved in the original plans to take control of Tartarus. Based on what I know, however, we should expect to encounter at least two of them on our way down." Z said. "Normally, that number would be three, but Administrator A is no longer a concern."

Raenof raised a hand briefly. "And how powerful are these Administrators, exactly?"

Z's expression became slightly more cautious. "Hopefully, neither of them will be as strong as Administrator A. However, I wouldn't recommend underestimating them. Even if they are weaker than A, they are still Administrators, which means either one could present a significant challenge on their own. I would likely have considerable difficulty defeating either of them in a direct confrontation."

Chloe's eyes widened slightly. She understood why Administrator A would be a problem, but she wondered if even Z would really have trouble fighting the other administrators?

But hey, there was no use being too down in the dumps. Chloe glanced around at everyone before giving a small grin. "Well, that's why we're all going together, right? We can just beat them up as a team."

Z stared at her for a moment before letting out a quiet sigh. "That is the hope, yes."

Chloe nodded, feeling a little better now that she had put it that way. If one Administrator was too much for any of them to handle alone, then they simply wouldn't fight them alone.

"There is, however, one problem," Z continued.

Chloe's smile faded slightly. "What's that?"

"I have no idea who these two Administrators are."

"You don't? But Z, I thought you knew everybody in the company?"

"Unfortunately not. There has been considerable restructuring within Divinity Inc. over the past several years, and even while I was still employed there, personnel were being reassigned, promoted, dismissed, or replaced with surprising frequency. The organization I left was already considerably different from the one I originally joined, so I cannot say with certainty which Administrators are currently stationed here."

"Uuuh, could you shorten that."

"Chloe, I don't know everybody who works there. Most, but not all."

Greg, who had been listening with his arms folded, shrugged. "So we don't know exactly who we're dealing with, but we know there are probably two Administrators waiting somewhere between us and the bottom."

"That is the situation, yes." Z said.

"Then maybe we should just get on with it and find out what we're dealing with firsthand." Greg said.

Chloe nodded. "Yeah! We shouldn't worry too much about all the little details. We'll figure things out when we get there."

"I'm not attempting to worry everyone, Chloe. I'm simply trying to make sure that everyone understands what we're walking into so that there are no unpleasant surprises later." Z said.

Chloe smiled sheepishly. "Well, I understand."

Liz, who had remained quiet throughout most of the discussion, finally decided now was a good time to make her opinion known. "I think we're all on the same page, Z. We're going down."

***

Before everyone could finally decide that it was time to get moving, Z insisted on going over one last rather important detail: exactly how they were supposed to get from their current location all the way down to the lowest floor of Tartarus. Chloe had been hoping the answer would be something simple, like pressing a button or taking an elevator, but judging by the way Z was studying the plans, things probably weren't going to be quite that convenient.

"So," Z began, pointing toward the projected blueprint, "we need to determine which route will actually allow us to reach the lower levels without unnecessarily exposing ourselves to every security force between here and our destination."

Greg scratched his chin as he looked over the blueprint. "A place this big has to have emergency stairs or something, right? Why don't we just find one of those and use it?"

Chloe turned toward him, looking skeptical. "Emergency stairs?"

"Yeah."

"At a Divinity Inc. facility?"

Greg gave her a confused look. "What's wrong with that?"

Chloe gestured vaguely toward the plans. "I mean, this is Divinity Inc. We're talking about the same company that's currently invading Tartarus and sending waves of robots after us. I'm not exactly convinced they'd spend extra money making sure every emergency staircase actually works."

"Nah, there's no way they wouldn't have emergency stairs. They're standard in all of Divinity Inc.'s major facilities."

"And you're really sure about this?"

"Yes, I'm sure. I've worked for the company, remember? Whatever else you want to say about Divinity Inc., they don't mess around when it comes to building regulations. They have emergency exits, evacuation routes, fire suppression systems, backup power, all that boring stuff."

Chloe frowned at the plans, still somewhat unconvinced. "So you're telling me they actually planned ahead?"

"Of course they planned ahead."

"Even though they're evil?"

Greg sighed. "Chloe, being evil doesn't mean you forget to install stairs."

Chloe opened her mouth, then paused. "Eh… I guess."

"Greg is correct. Divinity Inc. is a lot of things, but they do place considerable importance on structure and building plans. Every major facility is designed with clearly defined emergency routes, and those routes are generally maintained regardless of how the building is being used." Z said.

Chloe looked from Greg to Z and then back at the projected plans. "So there really are emergency stairs."

"Most likely," Z replied. "And if the original plans are still accurate, I believe I know where we should start looking."

Chloe still wasn't entirely convinced that the super evil corporation would bother installing emergency stairs in a place like this. But then again, both Greg and Z did sound really confident about this whole "stairs" thing. So, in the end, Chloe figured she'd just go with the flow for now.
