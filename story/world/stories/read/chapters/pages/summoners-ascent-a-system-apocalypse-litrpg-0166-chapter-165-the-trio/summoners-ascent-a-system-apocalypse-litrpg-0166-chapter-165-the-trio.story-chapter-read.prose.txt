Chris was absolutely frozen in place. This was probably the last thing that he expected to happen.

Mickey, Bart, and him had grown into a pretty strong trio over the last week, so they had been put in charge of quite a lot of important work after taking shelter at the university. Some of it was pretty annoying, but at least the students were making it worth their time.

When they asked Chris to babysit and escort a bunch of kids, he was pissed off but figured that it was going to be easy enough.

But why, why did this guy have to be here?

Ashton was just standing in front of him with that big, stupid grin on his face, and for some reason Chris couldn't even flinch, let alone cuss out this insane bastard that left them to die a week ago.

"Why... why are you here?"

There! Chris was able to force it out! It was just a couple words, but somehow it seemed like a massive achievement right now.

But Ashton just came closer and pushed down on Chris' shoulder with a ridiculous level of strength that a skinny guy like him shouldn't have.

"Hey, hey, why are you talking like that, Chris? I thought you'd be happy to see me, bud."

Bud? What the hell was he even talking about?

After a few moments of staring, pushing through the pain in Chris' shoulder, a surprised expression appeared on Ashton's face.

"Oops, sorry about that. I didn't mean to get that mad," he laughed, finally lifting his hand again. "I guess I just couldn't help myself after hearing your smug fucking voices."

In an instant, all that pressure that Chris hadn't even registered was there until now, was lifted from him. Mickey and Bart were the same. And while Chris was overcome with a wave of relief, Mickey seemed to have another reaction.

"Fucking psycho! What are you doing, hanging around a bunch of kids?!" Mickey yelled out with a knife in his hand, looking like he was ready to throw it.

Ashton's smile disappeared and was instead replaced by utter confusion. "Wait, what? Dude, I don't know what you're implying but I just helped this place out a couple times."

"Hah, really? With that sort of getup? We read about some guy acting like a cult leader in the community, was that you? Are you trying to indoctrinate kids into your stupid 'summoner' bullshit?"

Ashton sighed. He would have rolled his eyes if he could have. "Yeah, yeah, just keep squeaking, I'm done with that. Rory, what's going on?"

"Uh..." Rory looked at Ashton confused. "I, uh... do you know these fellows?"

"Unfortunately. It sounds like they don't want to leave?"

"Not without the children they came here for." Rory shook his head, and Ashton laughed.

"Hah..." Ashton rubbed the bridge of his nose. "Unfortunately, I can vouch that they're really from the university. The armour he's wearing was displayed there. And they're dickheads, but... they're not those kinds of dickheads, so don't worry about the kids. They're probably telling the truth."

One of the teachers immediately spoke up, still protesting. "So what, are we supposed to just send the kids along with them?"

"Huh? What? Fuck no, don't let them leave. Even with what happened here yesterday, this is still the safest place in town at the moment. Don't send out any kids."

Immediately relieved, the teachers sighed breaths of relief, and Rory also seemed visibly calmer.

But that confused the dickhead trio even more.

"What? Why does he get the last word?" Chris asked, confused. "We're not leaving without those kids, end of discussion."

"Well, he's risked his life for us and the bairns multiple times, so of course we'll appreciate his input," Rory explained. The teachers and him seemed a lot more confident now that Ashton was standing here with them.

"Hah, he risked his life? Probably just conned you guys. It's all over the community, those fake little stories about what 'Ashton the Summoner' has done." With a loud scoff, Mickey stepped in front of his friends, staring up at Ashton.

And the man in question just... smiled happily. "Oh, really? Sorry I haven't been keeping up with it, but it kind of makes me happy so many people are talking about me like that."

Mickey looked back and forth between Ashton and Chris, confused. "What the fuck is happening, why is he acting like that?"

"Don't ask me..." Chris shook his head. "Either way, clearly everyone here is well indoctrinated into his little–"

"Oh Jesus Christ, will you guys shut up about that already?" Ashton clicked his fingers, and the Backpacker appeared right by his side. At the same time, a mole of dirt jumped up from the ground, and the wisp disconnected from Ashton's staff and began to float around him. "I'm actually a summoner, okay? Here, I can do loads of cool stuff."

Briefly channelling the gnome, Ashton pulled on the stone bricks the trio were standing on. Mickey barely caught himself, but Bart and Chris both fell onto their asses.

The three were just staring at Ashton in disbelief, as he walked up to Chris and held out his hand.

Chris frowned annoyed but reached toward Ashton's hand, though he simply scoffed. "I'm not trying to help you up, man. Give me the list of kids you're supposed to bring. Idiot." Grumbling, Chris gave him the note, and Ashton quickly gave it to Rory. "Can you read it out for me?"

Rory of course took the note and quickly looked it over, but before he could start reading, Mickey nervously laughed.

"What, did using magic make you go blind or something? That why you're wearing that stupid mask?"

Ashton turned to him and pulled the mask up onto his forehead. "It wasn't the magic, but as a matter of fact... yes. That's why I'm wearing the mask."

"Oh... shit, I..." Mickey stumbled over his words immediately. He wasn't the only one shocked to see that. Chris, Bart, and the teachers were equally stunned.

Ashton pulled the mask back over his eyes. "Listen, I hate you guys. And clearly, you hate me as well. But can we just stop this whole thing and act in a way befitting the situation? You know... the fucking apocalypse? Where we should all be trying to help each other out somehow? Yes? Good, thanks. Now, Rory, please."

"Right," Rory replied, finally given the time and quiet to read the note. Essentially, it was an introduction from one of the university students, explaining the situation. Due to the current situation, they wanted the kids to be with their family, believing the university to be safer than the primary school.

There were five kids on the list. Rory explained later that they were all kids without any family that had come here, whether to protect them or to pick them up. Joey was one of them. Apparently her sister was a student and wanted her to come.

"So? Why aren't their siblings here with you three?" Ashton asked, patiently waiting a response. Finally, Chris spoke up.

"... Three of them are injured, real bad. They'll live, but they're still recovering. The other two don't have fighting classes, so they're worried about going out with all the monsters."

Ashton shrugged. "I can understand that the injured ones aren't here, but why couldn't the other two come along? You could have just protected them on the way here."

Chris scoffed, shaking his head. "Do you know how hard it is to fight while protecting others? It's fucking hard. It was easier to just go alone."

"Are you... are you pretending, or are you actually this stupid?"

Confused, and a little startled, Chris stared back at Ashton. "What was that for?"

"Chris. You would have had to fight while protecting people anyway. How did you expect to get the kids to the university? Teleportation?"

As if the thought had only occurred to Chris for the first time just now, he stood there in silence for a moment. He turned to his friends. "Did you guys realize this?"

Bart closed his eyes for a moment and took a couple deep breaths, while Mickey nodded. "Of course we realised this, that was never the reason why they didn't come along. Those two are really important to keep protecting the university, even if they can't fight. So they couldn't be spared."

"There we go. It's a shit reason, but at least it's an actual reason," Ashton groaned. "Listen, I'm sure you've already been told, but the only way for kids to be taken out of this place is by having a guardian pick them up. And the kid has to go willingly. So either bring those five, or the kids are staying here."

"Or, you know... we can just take the kids?" Bart pointed out. This was the first thing he said this entire time.

Ashton was starting to get exhausted with these guys. "Okay, maybe you are those kinds of dickheads. Yeah, no, you're not going to kidnap a bunch of kids."

"It's not kidnapping, we're bringing them to their families," Chris spat out, but Ashton shook his head.

"Not happening."

"Really? And how are you going to stop us?"

"You're still on your ass."

"Well, have you tried to get up in a full suit of armour?"

Ashton was really fed up with these guys. He just wanted them to leave already. "Listen, there's no way you guys can beat me. Just go before I change my mind."

"Before you change your mind? You just caught us off-guard! Do you really think you can beat the three of us?" Chris asked, but Ashton just sighed.

"Yes, I can. Very easily, too," he replied, as a grin formed on his face. "Want to bet?"
