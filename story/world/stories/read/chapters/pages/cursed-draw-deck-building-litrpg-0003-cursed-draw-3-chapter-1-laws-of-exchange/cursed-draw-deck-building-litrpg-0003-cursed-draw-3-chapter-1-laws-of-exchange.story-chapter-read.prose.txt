Part 3: First Frontier

Chapter 1: Laws of Exchange

Alabaster

Alley blinked a few times and looked around at the burning lobby. He was on the bottom floor of the huge inn that the pair of princes had put him, Darius, and Kyera up in. What Alley couldn't explain was how he and his companions had gotten here, or why, or even why the large room was slowly filling with smoke and flame.

Judging from the looks of bewildered shock on his friends' faces, they weren’t so clear about these subjects either. Except they weren’t looking around in confusion like he was, no every set of eyes in the large room was firmly locked onto Alley.

“What?” He tried to ask before bursting into a coughing fit. Alley couldn't remember a time that his throat had been so painfully dry. It felt like he had been drinking goblets of sand or salt, and if that wasn't enough his attempt to speak seemed to wake up every other ache and pain his body had been keeping from until it felt he was alert enough to endure them.

That time had apparently come, and all of a sudden every inch of the young man was reporting intense pain.

Alley wasn't sure if there was a Card out there that could replace all the blood in your veins and the Life Essence in your Resonance channels with acid, but if such a Spell existed he had to imagine this is what it felt like to have it cast on him.

With pain however, came clarity. Not quite as much as Alley would have preferred, but enough that he remembered why he was down here.

‘Mercenaries.’

The building had been invaded by mercenaries, who had gone out of their way to burn everything and everyone they could in between the princes on the top floor and the exits down here. There was more, the attackers had deployed a field of crimson energy that had enveloped the outside of the giant inn.

Alley's blood ran cold when he considered the innocent guests whose only crime was hiring a room in Skalean's fanciest establishment, unfortunately that cold blood was just an expression and did nothing to sooth how burned his internals felt.

Wiping his mouth as his coughing fit ended Alley took a deep breath and managed to croak out that someone needed to run and get help.

He would have been more than happy to seek out the Skalean militia and whatever passed for an anti-fire brigade in the mostly wooden city if it wasn't for the fact that his limbs felt like they were made of pudding.

“Oh thank the endless void.” Said Denderra as the white haired ghost floated down beside him.

“Its Alley, everyone its Alley again.” It was clear from the way that everyone watched her that Denderra was making herself visible to everyone, but that didn't explain the weird thing she said. Of course he was Alley. When had he ever not been Alley? Still, there was an audible release of tension when she said it, and everyone seemed to calm down a little.

Kyera who was the closest to the lobby's exit gave a little nod, then her and the youngest prince tore out into the night presumably to seek aid for the burning building, and those injured within it.

Alley would have expected Malchik or the pair's bodyguard to have joined Kaj rather than Kyera, instead Alley's eyes widened as he realized the hired guardsman was barely hanging onto life in the center of the lobby; his face and much of his armored body covered in some of the most horrific burns Alley had ever seen.

The dark-haired boy's expression of surprise didn't change as he felt something shift inside his Resonance system, something huge. Undeath Resonance, he was all but certain it was an enormous ball of Undeath Resonance. Alley’s Resonance channels felt raw and overly sensitive, yet even if they hadn’t been he would have recognized the way Undeath Resonance twisted around itself as it moved through his body.

Eyes going wider still Alley realized that his body was utterly rejecting the mass of Resonance, even he who was being slowly moulded by The Crown Of The Cursed King couldn’t hold or process this degree of power. What that meant for the amount of Doom inside him Alley wasn’t sure. In that moment however he was less worried about himself, not when he was all but certain he knew what was about to happen once he expelled the swirling mass of Undeath from his body.

“Dari.” Alley fought his way through a second coughing fit and a severe bout of nausea. “Darius.” He successfully said on his second attempt. “I think you are going to get hit with a big wave of Undeath.”

“Whats this now?” The hunter said, striding over to Alley. “ What did you say?”

Squeezing his eyes shut with effort he managed to repeat himself with only a second to spare before the purple colored energy burst from his eyes and mouth. Just as he had felt it back at the swamp mansion when Lady Astley had tried to overwhelm him, Alley felt a gate that didn’t belong to him swing open and begin to drain some of the Undeath Resonance.

It wasn’t enough to completely get rid of the mass his body was rejecting, but it did sufficiently shrink it that Alley could absorb and expel the remainder without his Resonance channels and then his veins splitting open at the passage of the overwhelming chunk of Undeath.Alley couldn’t see the lobby around him through the streams of purple energy that poured from Alley’s face and dissipated into the air. After another seven minutes where his vision was obscured and his body furiously fought to convert the Resonance into Life Essence, Alley regained enough clarity to see and interact with the world once more.

Alley was sore on a level so deep it transcended the physical, but he was just happy to be alive and not permanently blinded. Alley knew there was no time to celebrate right now, not when he had once more poured nigh on toxic Resonance into Darius.

The first thing Alley saw when his vision returned was a trio of armed militiamen surging into the building whilst another two directed another three Deck wielders who were playing Water Resonance spells, and a single water elemental of some sort. That was good, there was even some sort of militia healer trying to help Piotyr. Though, judging from the amount of steam rising from the man’s wounds as his body tried to pull on Life Essence that wasn’t there to heal itself.

Even less encouraging was the second thing that Alley saw. The light brown haired Prince Malchik was behind Darius restraining the redheaded hunter. Considering the man was a rank eight True-Human he should be easily strong enough to hold Alley’s friend back without difficulty.

That wasn’t what he saw though, the elder prince was struggling to keep Darius held as the hunter thrashed around like a wild beast with its leg caught in a trap. He was screaming too; mostly illegible wordless rage, but there was the occasional word or phrase in Frostlander or one of the other languages Darius spoke. The few chunks of Southern that Alley could understand were worrying in the extreme.

“SLAUGHTER! I WILL SLAUGHTER EVERYTHING! THE BLACK SUN! ALSHAMS ALSAWDA!” As he screamed Alley could make out flashes of purple inside Darius’s mouth, and tattered wings of ethereal darkness that appeared behind him in equally brief flickers.

“Oh shit.” Alley said with a  sigh. He had hoped that the degree of control Darius had demonstrated a few days earlier would be enough to hold back this sort of thing, but the Undeath Resonance the  hunter had drawn from Alley to heal himself was the trickle of a tilted jug compared to the river of Resonance that flowed between the two men now.

Based on what Alley knew from previous transformations and what he had read in Clataire’s journals about Undeath Darius should need to be exposed to a ‘black sun’ Card either from Alley’s collection or a strange transformation of their own that seemed to take hold of Darius’s Cards. The hunter had never been so desperate to do so before though, it had simply been an option.

In this case it was more like the flood of Resonance had overwhelmed Darius and was forcing him to use the transformation as a way to cleanse the alien Resonance perhaps? Alley wasn’t sure, but he knew the stream of helical Resonance that was invisibly flowing between himself and Darius had all but dried up. There was no way he was going to let Darius loose to look down at his Hand and potentially play a ‘Black Sun’ Card, that would be a disaster if they hadn’t found a way to lock him down before then. So for now all Alley could really do was help Malchik restrain Darius, and hope his friend could manage to process the energy that had been pumped into him. Alley wasn’t expert enough to say with anything resembling certainty, but to him it seemed like there was a possibility that he not only wouldn’t be able to deal with the Resonance, but that it would forcibly trigger the shadowy gargoyle form of Darius’s without any Card needing to be played.
