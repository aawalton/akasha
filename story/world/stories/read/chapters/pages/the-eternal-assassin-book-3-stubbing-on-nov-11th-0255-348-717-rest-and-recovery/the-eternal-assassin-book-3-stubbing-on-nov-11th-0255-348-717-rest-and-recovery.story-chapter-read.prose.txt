It felt a bit strange to lounge around and do nothing when the potential collapse of the Noala Kingdom was quite possibly only a couple of days away, but those were the healer’s orders. The coalition wouldn’t be going anywhere fast anytime soon with Lanra out of commission and Richard focused on her recovery, and while Asher was feeling better with each passing day, he knew it was just as vital that he get his soul back into pristine shape before throwing himself into a life-or-death battle that would require him to stress all his skills to the maximum. Donvath sent a pegasus courier with a rock carrying an Eternal Mark back toward the Hojon Empire so that Asher could handle transportation once his skills were back online, but until then, he had nothing but free time.

So it was that Asher found himself on functionally a mini vacation all of a sudden, despite the end of the kingdom dangling just past the horizon. He could still spar with his team and do standard physical conditioning, but his active skills were largely off limits in order to speed up his recovery. With that in mind, he did his best to focus on what training he could do, as well as take care of some of the more mundane tasks that were on his to-do list.

First, he took the time to organize his Personal Rift again. He’d been putting off doing so for ages now, but ever since Watcher had forced her way into his personal pocket dimension, the place had been an absolute mess. At this point, the skill had managed to reach level 37, and the space that had once been little more than the size of a small backpack was now roughly that of a small warehouse. He’d picked up so much random junk during his adventures that it took him almost a full day to catalogue each and every item that was worth keeping and what had been destroyed beyond repair, and he also took advantage of his winter-cleaning to make a pile of things to sell at the Grand Auction along with his latest haul.

Because that was the second item on Asher’s to-do list that he took care of, this one quite eagerly. Bellod had been handed a small treasure trove of gemstones and some advanced golem pieces, courtesy of their mission to take down Flunx. On top of which, Asher also gave him the chunk of that weird, almost indestructible black stone that he’d taken from the bunker the last lich had been hiding out in. Those two finds, along with much of the other random junk he’d been holding onto over the last couple of months, brought Bellod no end of excitement, and the auction ended up doing even better than he could have hoped.

Asher had spent a significant amount of his fortune evolving his identification resistance, but this latest windfall helped restore those lost shards and then some. The ten million shards earned from taking down Flunx and the roughly sixteen million they ended up getting from the mountain of gemstones, golem parts, and other miscellaneous things in his rift, went pretty far toward accomplishing that. Even after splitting that bounty four ways, that was still six and a half million shards for Asher personally. Tack onto that the two million he received in exchange for Recalling over to take care of the last lich, and Asher was once again sitting pretty with a truly ridiculous amount of wealth.

Shards: 16,518,762

What was most shocking about his current fortune was that, seeing as he’d already evolved Distortion, Eternal Regeneration, and Complete Resilience to the third tier, at the moment he only had fifteen other skills that needed to be evolved to that extent, if he included his third blended skill he hadn’t even unlocked yet. Doing so would cost an incredible fifteen million shards. A number that would have sounded absolutely impossible to acquire only a few months ago.

A number he not only had already reached, but had surpassed by over a million shards.

Asher had been so focused on amassing enough wealth to chip away at that eighteen-million-shard mountain he knew stood between him and the eventual Stage of Power that suddenly realizing he’d already succeeded in doing so was more than a little astonishing. Still, that didn’t mean he was planning on taking it easy regarding collecting more wealth for the future. Artifacts weren’t exactly cheap, and Samantha still had a long way to go before she had the required shards in order to get all of her skills up to the third tier.

Technically there was always adding yet another resistance to his growing list, but with the exponential increase following each purchase and evolution, picking up another resistance would cost a ridiculous one-hundred-million shards. Unless Asher suddenly found himself in possession of the largest windfall in all of history, most likely, his resistances were officially complete.

Beyond basic training, cleaning up his Personal Rift, and stockpiling shards like a hungry dragon, Asher took advantage of this time to work on a few of his harder to train skills, such as Judgement.

Judgement was simple enough, as training it was as easy as people watching and making a point to focus on the most-used skills of everyone who walked past him. He had figured out ages ago that the skill leveled more frequently depending on the power of the person he was judging, but there was still something to be said for sheer quantity over quality when one was devoting their entire time toward training. Spending a significant portion of each day wandering the busy streets of the capital and examining everyone who strode past was excellent training, and Asher managed to push Judgement up to level 33. This felt particularly nice as, beyond his blended skills, Judgement had been the last of his skills still stuck firm in the 20s for level.

And speaking of blended skills…

While Eternal Knowledge was an active skill that he’d been instructed to hold off on using, that didn’t mean he couldn’t prepare himself for when his soul was fully repaired. During his days spent wandering the capital and markets training Judgement, he kept a careful eye out for any objects of importance that he’d be able to use to train his skill. He ended up picking up a solid number of odd pieces here and there while exploring some of the seedier markets off the beaten path that he was pretty certain contained plenty of stolen items. While people weren’t willing to sell objects of personal value, thieves and pickpockets certainly didn’t mind selling such objects for them.

Asher figured he could try and take out two birds with one stone, using these items to train his new blended skill, while maybe even managing to reunite said objects with their rightful owners based on what he saw once he could train the skill again.

Lastly, at the same time he did everything else, he worked on Remote Control.

Remote Control was a weird skill in that, similar to Permanence’s second-tier effect, after the initial activation cost of gaining control of his severed body part the skill was then purely passive. Seeing as Eternal Regeneration was completely passive, that meant so long as he only did one body part at a time and strained his soul a tiny bit, he was free to chop off as many parts of his body as he wanted and practice trying to control them while they were in another room or halfway across the city before pressing them back to the stump he’d created and letting them heal.

While roaming the city using Judgement on everything that moved and picking up treasured objects for Eternal Knowledge, Asher kept his left arm thoroughly concealed under his cloak, making sure to hide the fact that he was down a hand. That on its own wasn’t all that strange of course, as there were any number of former warriors or unfortunate workers who were missing limbs here and there, but Asher’s wound was just a tiny bit different.

In that rather than a stump of healed-over flesh, his wound looked as if it had only just happened moments ago.

As he went about his other tasks, part of his mind was split, controlling his hand and using his fingers to run around on the ground like some sort of horrific spider. He couldn’t see too much of the surroundings with the skill when operated in this way, but he could see plenty enough for the purposes of running laps through a small obstacle course he’d set up in his and Samantha’s bedroom. He’d debated trying to navigate the palace itself, but scrapped that idea almost as soon as he had it. Donvath may have grown used to his strange skills by now, but there was no need to give any of the palace workers who were so kind to him any lasting trauma by turning a corner and seeing a severed hand crawling toward them on its fingers.

After a few days of running these obstacle courses as his soul healed, which Samantha even took the time to try making her own to help keep things fresh and unpredictable, he eventually reached the first benchmark he’d been aiming for.

Remote Control - lvl 20

Spend 100,000 shards to evolve Remote Control?

Oh yeah, Asher grinned, taking a seat on a bench a few miles away in the capital so he wouldn’t get bowled over in the street while he was distracted. Lets.

Shards: 16,418,762

Remote Control (active/passive) - lvl 21

Tier 1: Command every part of your body regardless of distance, realm, or condition.

Tier 2: Isolated portions of your body no longer feel pain and will not allow harmful skills to connect back to you.

Asher couldn’t help but let out a relieved sigh as he took in the skill’s second-tier evolution. It wasn’t a flashy one by any means, but it solved the exact problem he was most worried about.

While he could think of quite a number of unique ways to try and use Remote Control, he’d been more than a little hesitant about most of them simply because of the fact that there were countless skills out there people had access to that could do some serious damage if they got their hands on a chunk of another person’s body. The Lost Lady had been one such example of this, as she would have potentially taken him down for good had he not unlocked Remote Control during his fight with the lich and used it to snatch his severed leg from her. While not always the case, both the origination of blended skills and that last little push skills needed to reach level 40 and achieve their third-tier effects usually came from moments of extreme need or duress, often in life or death battles. A fact that he was well aware of by now.

The first tier of Remote Control skill was useful, but he knew better than anyone that there were methods of blocking spatial skills. If an angry wizard with the right knowledge or someone like Clare with her Binding Chains got their hands on his severed hand while it was crawling around, Asher wasn’t going to be able to recover the missing body part with Recall or run away through Astral Dip. This latest evolution meant that was no longer a concern, which was a huge load off his mind.

Eventually, Asher’s muted training came to an end when he was given the all-clear from one of the palace healers capable of viewing the soul. It had taken four entire days for Eternal Regeneration to bring him back to the peak of health, which just went to show that even if he could now regenerate from having his physical body utterly disintegrated, he was far from immortal. Soul damage was now his number-one concern when it came to things he needed to avoid if he wanted to truly live forever, and he made a mental note to hit the library when he next had some time to spare to do some research on any and all potential sources of soul damage. However, doing so would have to wait.

Shortly after Asher received the all-clear from the palace healer, Richard surprised them with some long-awaited news. According to the wizard it had been touch and go for a while there, but Lanra had finally woke up.
