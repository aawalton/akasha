She was still groggy, but she managed not to do something too silly during her time with Tanya. ‘Let’s… go bother other people!’

Liu Shu extended her senses and locked onto Taro’s place. The man had gone from a young and elegant man to a middle-aged and classy one. He was in his city hall’s office, looking through a few of the documents there. “Tarooo.”

“Hmph!” Liu Shu snorted. “Why are you so serious? Loosen up!”

Taro smiled faintly. “Liu Shu, have you done something like drinking?” As he asked, a person knocked on his door. “Hm? Come in.”

The person who entered carried a letter with them and placed it on the table before going away. Meanwhile, Liu Shu answered his question. “Drinking? I am always drinking! The lake’s water flows north, and the few rivers flow south. The underwater caves~. Everywhere I am, I drink water!”

“No!” Liu Shu answered, and her tone became mildly annoyed. “Why are you so focused on that? I came here for… for…” The World Tree stopped speaking. “... I forgot. Ugh, whatever. Bye-bye, Taro!”

Leaning back on his comfortable and reclining leather chair, Taro read the letter and amusedly smiled. “Tianlian Liu Shu is acting strange, so in case you communicate with her, try to see if she has been negatively influenced…” He stopped reading there and closed his eyes. “What could cause that?”

This surge of energy is still occurring as well… From past events, I can confidently guess that Liu Shu is either Leveling Up or Tiering Up. However, instead of being asleep like she usually is, she has appeared at different locations acting abnormally. Then, you take Tianlian Liu Shu’s gentle attitude and worrying nature into account…”

After listing the situation and preconditions, Taro found the answer quite easy to deduce. “Has Liu Shu tried to remain awake during her Leveling Up or Tiering Up process, but only half-succeeded, leaving her in an impeded mental state?”

The Flower Fairy froze, and her mind began creating thousands of scenarios of Liu Shu doing damage everywhere out of drunken fits. The Ant Queen happily obliged and used her hands to caress the fairy’s body and transform her into a fairy blob.

Tempestria was flying by Liu Shu’s head, looking at the gargantuan countenance of her World Tree lover. “Love, what are you doing lately?”

The enormous head of the World Tree slowly turned sideways, making leaves, twigs, and even some flying animals fall from her hair. “Tempestria?” Liu Shu’s tame and tired voice reached the Wind Sylvan. “When did you become so small?”

Tempestria nodded. “So… Why have you captured me, love? You are now pulling me into your hair as well.”

Liu Shu dragged Tempestria and pressed her against her hair. The soft surface made Tempestria feel as if she were sinking into a soft grass bed. The Wind Sylvan blinked, confused, and then saw a few of the hair vines raise around her, some pointing at her.
