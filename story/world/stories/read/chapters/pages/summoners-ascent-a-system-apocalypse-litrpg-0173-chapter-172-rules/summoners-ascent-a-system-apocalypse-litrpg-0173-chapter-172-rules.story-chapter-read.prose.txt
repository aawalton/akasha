As his body kept on dragging and starting to feel heavy, Ashton finally knocked on the doors to Calibur.

Though, rather than knock, he really just slammed his fist against the metal door. The way here was... annoying. There were so many monsters out for him that it felt like they were doing it on purpose to tire him out. And hell, it worked. He was exhausted beyond belief.

The doors opened up, and wordlessly, Ashton pushed through.

"Oh, Ashton, Sam said that you should go to him as soon as you come back. He's up in his office," the 'gatekeeper' told him. He turned and nodded, still not in the mood to talk.

As Ashton walked inside, he had multiple people greeting him almost immediately, though he ignored most of them. Some fighters that were about to head out for a hunt to level up, some crafters that were working on turning this place into a proper base, and of course, members of his direct circle.

"Yo, Ashy Bashy," Sabrina said with a wave, continuously flipping a coin with her other hand.

"... Ashy Bashy?"

"What, you don't like it? You didn't seem like the boring kind."

Ashton sighed loudly, walking past her. "Listen, I don't care, I just need to sleep."

"Oh, come ooon! I wanted to show you something," Sabrina said with a broad grin on her face, quickly rushing in front of him. With a proud smile, she pulled a deck of cards out of her pocket. "Take a look at these, cool, right? The game guys made them for me. I figured out that I can use my skill through things like this too."

Ashton nodded slightly. "Mm-hm. There are limits to it though." He yawned. "For that skill specifically, when you're trying to replace your coin, you need to limit the variables as much as you can. As you level it up more, you can increase the different types of cards in your 'deck' or whatever."

"Wait, you knew about this?" Sabrina asked, staring down at the cards. "But why didn't you tell me?"

"Sabrina, there are thousands of different applications for skills. You can use literally anything you want with that skill, okay? How many leaves will fall from a tree in the next hour, what the next number drawn at bingo is, or hell, you could even use how long I'm going to sleeping for that skill. You just need to define the outcomes and their consequences properly. Just... test out different stuff."

Sabrina looked up at Ashton for a few moments. "Dude, seriously? Then what is the difference between the 'Betting' skill and the 'Gambling' skill? Can't I just do everything through the Gambling skill?"

Ashton shook his head. "Nope. 'Betting' is basically a skill that ensures that you get your earnings when you make a bet with someone else. 'Gambling' lets you use the causality created by the outcome of your flip to use predetermined magic. Sure, if you're strong enough, the effect can be 'make this person give me the stuff they promised', but why do something that can fail if you can just as well use the sure-fire 'Betting' skill?"

The gambler looked down at her cards again, nodding slightly. "Hm, alright. I guess I really do need to play around with my skills more."

"Mm-hmm." Ashton nodded. "Anyway. Will sleep now."

"Well, alright. Sleep tight, Ashy Bashy," Sabrina pointed finger-guns at Ashton, and he just waved as he continued upstairs.

He was barely able to still keep the wisp up, he was so tired. He was almost falling asleep here on the stairway.

But Ashton just pushed through, soon getting to Sam's office while avoiding any more useless conversations.

The moment he stepped inside, he saw Sam and Clementine, sitting on opposite sides of the desk. Clementine was wearing the shoes that Ashton prepared.

"You're back!" Sam said with a clear air of relief. "Thank fucking god, man."

"Mm-hmm." Ashton continued walking, pushing some things aside. He called on the Backpacker and had them pick up all of the important things he was most worried about getting damaged or stolen, really stuffing the bag full. While he was at it, Ashton also prepared some item chests that were filled with important things in case of different kinds of emergencies, which he had the Backpacker give to Sam.

"That one has a ton of magic stones, that one has a stash of weapons taken from monsters, that one has a lot of potions and first aid things, and that one is filled with food rations," Ashton said as he had the Backpacker pull out something that he picked up on the way here from a store.

It was taking up quite a lot of space, but it was worth it; it was a mattress, a blanket, and some pillows.

"Wait, are you fully just going to sleep right now?" Clementine asked, walking over to Ashton. Seeing that he was stumbling a bit, she quickly helped him set everything up and move some stuff aside.

"And in my office?" Sam wondered, though nobody really reacted to that.

"Listen, I'm reaching my limits, okay? I just... need to sleep. There are a few things that I need to tell you about, though... urgh..." Ashton took off his cloak with a loud groan and gave it to the Backpacker, and quickly took off his shoes and trousers as well.

Clementine turned her head away the moment he did so, but Ashton really didn't care.

"You should've seen him at the beginning of all this. He was walking around completely in the nude."

"Huh? Seriously?" Clementine asked startled.

Ashton shrugged. "I still don't see the issue." He sat down on the mattress and leaned against the wall, already covering himself with a blanket. Ashton was completely ready to sleep, with everything important stashed away. The only summons that weren't dismissed completely were the wisp, the Pen of Insight, and the Backpacker. The pen was a construct that couldn't be fully dismissed, and Ashton had spoken to the spirit that they would just pop out sometimes to make sure that nothing bad was going on around him. That way they could get help from Sam or Clementine with some games, too.

"Okay, you guys, just... listen for now. I really don't know when I'll just doze off..." Ashton started, as Sam also walked around the table and came closer.

"What happened?" Sam wondered, but Clementine was already holding her breath, knowing what Ashton was going to be talking about.

He let out a long breath. "I went to the church. Clementine... your parents are alive."

Instantly, she let out a long sigh, though her expression didn't exactly scream complete relief.

"I'm not sure it's a good thing. Over the next few days, until I'm awake, you need to make a decision. Clementine, your father became a Marked. I don't know what sort, but he's got his faculties at least. But he's still violent. He's still beating your mother. And he's now the leader of a cult using the church as a base of operations."

If possible, Ashton wanted to tell her all of this in a slightly less blunt way, but there was no other choice at the moment. Ashton had to be blunt, or he wouldn't be able to say everything he had to.

"Wait, he's a Marked? What do you mean?" Clementine asked with an awkward laugh. She was filled in on everything important, so she had to know, even if she was acting confused.

"I don't know exactly what he did, but the most likely option at this point in time is that he killed someone during the first quest. I'm sorry, but this is just the truth." Ashton furrowed his brow, trying to fend off the headache he was feeling right behind where his eyes should be. "I decided to not take him out right there because he's your father, but in any other situation, I would have. I mean, he even tried to attack me because I spoke back to him."

"I..." Clementine looked down at the ground. Ashton understood why she was stunned, but he didn't have the capacity to really take care of her right now. He 'glanced' at Sam, though it was really just him positioning his face toward his friend, trying to signal him to take care of her while Ashton was asleep. Sam immediately nodded.

"I'm sorry, I wanted to talk about this more, but I really can't... There's more," Ashton said, feeling himself already drifting off. "I went back to the primary school. The dickhead trio was there. They work with the university camp now. It's complicated, but I think Rory made a post to talk about it and make sure that the rules relating to the primary school were clear again, so just check that out later. Please keep an eye on the community. If anything happens, send people over, okay?"

"Of course, we'll make sure that everything runs smoothly while you're asleep."

Ashton really didn't need to worry about that in the first place. Sam was on top of these kinds of things.

"Thanks. Oh, and... this will be a bit confusing, but I promise I will go into more detail when I wake up... I will go back to the school and bring one of the kids with me. She has a special power that will make her a target for constellations in the future, and also gives her a ridiculous magical potential. She didn't even have a family before all of this, so I will become her guardian and guide her."

"Huh?" Sam and Clementine made the same sound almost immediately. Of course, since this part came completely out of nowhere.

Sam took a step forward and leaned down. "Did you just say you're adopting a kid?"

Ashton groaned, burying his head between his legs while pressing his hands onto his ears. "Don't yell, you're being too loud right now." He groaned loudly as he lightly lifted his head again. "No, I'm not adopting her, I'm making her my apprentice."

"Are you serious about this? You're not going to treat her like one of your summons, are you?" Clementine asked instantly, but Ashton quickly shook his head.

"Nah, don't worry. I thought about it for a moment, but the thought left a bad taste in my mouth. To be honest, I don't even know the summoning patterns for humans, so I don't think I could do it if I wanted to."

Clementine's eyes widened. "That's not what I meant! You actually thought about forming a contract like that with her? Isn't that kind of.. immoral?"

"What's so immoral about it? I'd basically just be employing her. No, the reason it left a bad taste in my mouth was because the thought of summoning a human made me throw up a little and I started shaking because it felt like my own Summoner was breathing down my neck." Ashton ran his hand through his hair. "Listen, again, I'll explain more later. But for now, the last thing I want you to know." He yawned and laid down, pulling the blanket onto his chest. "I explained what a fairy sanctuary is, right?"

"Yes? Did you find one?"

"Mm-hmm. In the garden of my ruined childhood home. Apparently the nymph that created the sanctuary and saved tons of fae was already there when I was a kid, and saved me from drowning. I think she's kind of attached to me," Ashton explained, though he wasn't really sure which part here was important and what wasn't. Hell, he could barely comprehend what he himself was saying at this point. "In an absolute emergency where you don't have anywhere else to go, head there and say that you are my friends. But only you two, nobody else. I'm sure the nymph will open the sanctuary to you."

"Okay..." Sam nodded. By now, he had started taking notes on his phone to make sure he got it all. "And what then?"

"I don't know. But it's safe, even from constellations... just take some time to figure things out, and then leave again. But you know, follow the rules," Ashton said, groaning a little because he didn't want to keep talking anymore. "The fae that are there aren't those sorts of tricksters, but still be careful. There's the pixies that you know, a brownie, and some shadowy sprites. I'm going to tell you some rules, follow them as well as you can. And seriously, definitely write these down." Ashton instructed his friend.

"First. Don't accept any gifts, and don't eat any food that you didn't bring yourself. Two. Stash away any iron and plastic. Iron is harmful to them, but plastic is so fucking toxic, you don't even know. Three. Be careful with the phrase 'thank you'. For some fae, that implies that there was an inequal exchange, so you will owe them something now. There are many sort that don't care, but all types of fae are different, so really, just don't say things like 'thank you', and never fucking say 'sorry' unless you completely mean it." Ashton tried to recount the most important ones. These were the sort of rules that people interested in fae were probably already aware of.

A lot of fae were good-natured, but definitely not all. And even more than that were those that simply didn't think the same way that people did. That could be said especially to those that originally came from the Feywilds. Ones born in a physical world like this were usually more mellow on such rules.

"And lastly, if there is some fae that can speak in words you understand, just shut your mouth if you're not sure what to do. Take everything they say completely literally. 'May I have your name' means that they are asking to have your name. If you give them your real name, they will own it, and with that they will own you. It's really not very pleasant," Ashton groaned. "Convincing them to give your name back to you is genuinely a pain, trust me."

Both Clementine and Sam were listening closely. But by now, this had all gone on far too long. Ashton was pretty sure that he said all that he wanted to, but even if there was something he forgot, by now he wouldn't care.

"Alright. Thanks, man, we'll keep this all in mind. Though, let's hope that we don't even have to go there," Sam said with a nervous smile. He looked down at Ashton, whose breathing was had changed ever so slightly. "... You still awake?"

But Ashton didn't respond. The moment that he stopped talking, he had already fallen asleep.
