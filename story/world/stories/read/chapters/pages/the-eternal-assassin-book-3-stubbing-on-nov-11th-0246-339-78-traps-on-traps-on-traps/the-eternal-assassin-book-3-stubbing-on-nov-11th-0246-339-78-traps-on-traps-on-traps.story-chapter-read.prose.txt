“Realms above, that was a nasty trap,” Rulfar panted. Even the man’s high endurance had clearly taken a hit after going up against two unkillable opponents for so long. “Erasing the air in the hallway and then sending those things after us? Without Moxy, we would have been done for.”

“I’m just mad I couldn’t physically kill mine,” Moxy said, frowning as she kicked at the golem’s fallen torso. “If there’s one thing I’m learning from all these lich hunts it's that I need to come up with a stronger attack.”

“Please do. Fighting with the void dagger for that long is exhausting,” Asher admitted, wiping the sweat from his brow as he shook out his hand. Even with his Eternal Regeneration, his hand always tingled for a little while after utilizing the void dagger. It was like acting as a conduit for his tritessence being drained had some sort of underlying effect that even his regeneration struggled to combat. “For starters, did anyone other than myself get injured?”

“I took a few punches while fending off two of them at the same time, but they didn’t leave more than some nasty bruises,” Rulfar admitted as Moxy laid a glowing hand on Asher’s leg and let her Life element get to work. The bones had been so thoroughly shattered that not even his Lesser Restoration potion had managed to fully patch him up. “I think I was too big for them to really try to grab.”

“How many more traps like that do you think we have to go before we reach wherever Flunx is hiding?” Samantha asked as she started spraying a gentle stream of water over Rulfar’s bruises. Her healing wasn’t as powerful as Moxy’s, but Soothing Spring was more than enough for superficial damage.

“The castle isn’t that big, and we’ve already been moving for a while,” Asher said, trying to picture the path they’d taken through the castle thus far. Most of the rooms were completely empty or filled with nothing more than some potted plants, which made remembering how many they’d passed a bit tricky. “I can’t imagine we have that much farther to go.”

As if waiting for that moment, Jameston’s voice sounded in Asher’s head, causing him to jump.

“Asher, we’ve got movement out here! Flunx has begun attacking Lanra’s working directly, and a small army of golems are trying to do us in! Mako’s Sound element is a good match up for constructs, but there’s almost no end to these things! Flunx is making her move to escape, you need to hurry!”

“Got it!” Asher said, unsure if Jameston could actually hear his own thoughts in return. Seeing his friends give him odd looks, he hurried to relay the message. “Flunx is finally going after the others, she’s trying to make a break for it! I guess seeing us take down her golems was the last straw. Rather than risk us making it to her, she’s attempting to flee!”

“Then what are we waiting for, let’s go!” Moxy shouted, turning and going to run down the hallway before Rulfar grabbed her by the collar.

“We’ll pick up the pace, but we can’t just go sprinting headfirst into another series of traps,” Rulfar stated, looking at Moxy like she was an idiot. “We have to trust in the other team that they can hold out long enough for us to make it to the lich. Asher, they’re strong, aren’t they?”

“I’ve seen them in action, they’ll be able to hold out for a while,” Asher nodded. “The real concern is Flunx going after Lanra’s spell. It doesn’t matter how skilled they are, the moment Lanra runs out of tritessence, Flunx is gone. We need to find her before that happens.”

“Then we move quicker, but not recklessly,” Rulfar declared, dropping Moxy with a grimace as one of the pelts on her shoulder turned and sank its teeth into his beefy fingers. “Come on, let’s not waste time!”

Moving faster in a trap-filled castle was just about the worst thing they could do, but they didn’t have any other choice. If Flunx managed to get away, there was no telling where she’d try to hole up. Finding her again might not even be possible, which meant they couldn’t let that happen. The team forced their way through trap after trap, narrowly avoiding death time and time again as they worked together to offset whatever Flunx had in store for them.

A corridor flooded with water before shark-like constructs came for them, and Samantha managed to save the day with her Water element, swimming literal circles around the constructs with the incredible speed and maneuverability of her Fins skill to keep them distracted while Asher finished them off. Molten metal fell in a dense sheet from the ceiling, which the team managed to dodge from Asher pulling them all into the astral realm until the metal cooled off enough that they could keep going. The corridor walls themselves tried to smash together and crush the team at one point, held off solely from Rulfar’s impressive Strength as the bloodhound relied on Unyielding Blood to keep himself from snapping apart as he bore their full weight.

They didn’t face off against many more constructs, but from Jameston’s occasional updates, Asher figured that was because Flunx was throwing every last one of them at the team outside in a desperate attempt to take them out on multiple fronts.

“Things are getting bad, Mako’s almost out of juice!” Jameston warned him as they kept moving. “We’ve had to dip into our tritessence crystals to keep him fighting, but that means Lanra’s going to run out of energy even faster now. I don’t know how much longer you have, Asher, but we’re down to the wire here!”

“We’re moving as fast as we can!” Asher grumbled, slipping into the astral to dodge a wave of freezing temperature so cold that a few inches of frost coated the walls almost instantly. “We’re picking up the pace again, everyone. Let me know if you need a rest in my rift!”

As they started moving even faster, it wasn’t long before their speed came back to bite them. Samantha’s second use of Winged Savior was tripped when a spray of acid swept over them, and the hunter resigned herself to the rift now that she was liable to be killed for real and down her main defensive skill. Rulfar nearly lost his legs when an explosion detonated beneath his feet, sending him smashing into the ceiling hard enough to lodge him there for a moment before he came crashing down. A potion and Moxy’s healing were enough to get him moving again, but every second wasted was another second closer to Flunx getting away, so the two of them took care of the first aid from within Asher’s rift as he kept moving on his own.

Without Moxy’s sensitivity to her surroundings or Rulfar’s impressive nose, Asher ended up suffering extensive damage from the varied traps he kept encountering, but Eternal Regeneration and Complete Resilience proved more than enough to keep him on his feet and moving forward. Eventually, after having pulled Moxy and Rulfar back out to join him, the three of them reached a massive door that was enchanted so thoroughly it physically hurt to look at. The door took up the entire end of the corridor, and Identify didn’t even seem capable of understanding what he was looking at.

“That’s got to be her,” Asher stated the obvious, staring cautiously at the door before them as he moved to pull out his void dagger. “I’ll get to work.”

But even as he approached the door, yet another trap revealed itself, as a force unlike anything Asher had ever felt before crashed down on him from above. His body smashed into the ground hard enough to crack the stone, and Asher groaned as he tried and failed to push himself up onto his arms. He couldn’t even turn his head to look at his friends, but based on their struggling sounds, he didn’t think they were faring much better. It was like he was being dragged down into the depths of hell itself, and even with his Belt of Strength he could barely move.

Which of course was when the walls opened and more of those blank-faced constructs walked out, seeming entirely unaffected by the crushing force the rest of them were suffering from.

Asher’s eyes widened as he realized they were in trouble, but something about seeing those constructs walking perfectly upright while he and his team were struggling so badly helped him realize what was happening. Before the constructs could take more than a single step, he made his gamble.

Immunity to… Gravity!

Instantly, the crushing effect vanished, and Asher gasped as he took in a full breath of air again. Leaping to his feet, he was surprised when he wasn’t the first person to manage to land an attack on one of the four golems.

Two of the golems were blasted away from a ball of compressed air, and Asher turned in shock to find Moxy had managed to force her way up onto one knee, every muscle in her body shaking simultaneously as she battled the enhanced gravity with everything she had. Based on the violet glow and the shimmering air that was coming from her entire body, it was obvious she’d triggered Overheal, her deadliest blended skill and one that she hated using above all others. So long as it was active, life energy coursed through her body, giving her far greater strength and allowing her to channel her air blasts with clinical precision. However, the skill didn’t even last for a full minute, and the moment it ended, she wouldn’t be able to move for hours. Moxy hated feeling helpless above all else, so this was only the second time he'd ever seen her use the skill.

“Don’t just stand there gawking! Kill the bastards!” Moxy shouted at him, forcing herself up onto her feet with a few micro-blasts of focused air before throwing an Air Blast to knock back a golem running straight for her.

“Right!” Asher said, sparing a quick glance down at Rulfar to ensure the bloodhound was alright. Despite his Strength element, it seemed for once Rulfar’s giant battle form was distinctly working against him here. The ten-foot-tall northman who had to weigh close to half a ton under regular gravity was half-embedded in the stone floor, struggling to raise his arms off the ground. As one of the golems raced toward him and went to grab his head between its marble fingers, Asher darted forward with Distortion as he unsheathed his void dagger, cleaving through the golem’s wrists and sending its hands tumbling to the ground before it could trigger whatever skill it had in store for Rulfar.

Slicing through the rest of the golem’s limbs, Asher spared a moment to drag Rulfar back into his rift before turning to help Moxy. The battle-manic warrior never failed to impress, and Asher spared a single second to watch in awe as Moxy managed to fend off three of the golems at the same time while barely being able to move. Air Nova combined with pinpoint strikes of Air Blast kept the golems at bay, and Moxy looked more demonic than ever as the powerful gravity dragged at her face, morphing her deranged grin into something truly horrifying. Shaking his head, Asher joined the fray, leaping onto one of the unbalanced golems as he slashed through its legs.

The two of them managed to finish off the golems just before Moxy’s Overheal skill wore off, and Asher winced as she smashed back into the floor for a second time, this time breaking at least a bone or two from the impact by the sounds of things. Knowing Samantha would get right to healing her, he dragged Moxy into his rift before turning to face the final obstacle in his path. With the golems gone and the gravity unable to do a thing to stop him, all he needed to do was get through that giant, dense, heavily enchanted door and strike down Flunx hiding on the other side.

The question was if he still had enough tritessence left in order to do so.
