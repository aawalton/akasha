Seraphine held herself aloft with her white plumed wings, attacking from all sides as Jade danced along the wooden beam, not a single step misplaced. Her parries transitioned to momentum that gave the succubus windows with which to counter, striking back with flashing steel. Even with the enhanced mana regeneration of the corespace the brutal pace of the preceding battles had drained much of Jade's mana supply. She guarded those reserves with the jealous grip of the lustful demon she was, unable to afford the cost of sustained flight or aura blades. Instead, she relied on her swiftness, martial talents, and the long held back simmering fury that boiled within her veins.

Jade took another hit, blood spraying from her shoulder. Incandescent energy tore at her flesh, searing through her body. Even a glancing hit from Seraphine's sword dealt substantial damage, especially now that her armor's shield was depleted. The defensive boost from Seducer's Mantle only went so far in protecting her from that kind of energy. But Jade hardly minded, that just made it all the more exciting. Her blood pulsed with the rhythm of war as they met blade to blade again and again, reenacting a battle as old as time. Angel against demon. Judgement against wrath. She drank it all in with a manic laugh, sacrificing minor wounds to get in attacks of her own.

For the first time, Jade reached a state of oneness with her new body. A unity of mind, body, and spirit that transcended words. Her being was aligned like a phalanx of spears; all aimed at one goal: Extinguishing the light that burned before her. She moved like a shadow, dancing between Seraphine's thrusts and slipping through the graceful tendrils of radiance it left in its wake, striking back with a precision that belied the primal savagery of her attacks. To be a demon was not to be claimed by rage, but to claim it as a honed weapon.

As a succubus, Jade could taste the purity of Seraphine's angelic nature, the unblemished light that was her soul. Or at least, it had once been that way. Now, that spark had diminished, and a hint of something dark tainted that which had once been clean. She could feel it, a deep anguish that permeated her spirit. A desire to bring pain to others, so that they might know her own. But Jade did know it, and that knowledge came with power. For accompanying that dark desire was disgust. Disgust at herself, for becoming the very thing she despised. It was clear, now, in a way that it hadn't been before. Now that Seraphine's emotions had finally broken free of the hold she had on them, Jade could taste the beauty of them all.

"So brutal." She laughed, leaping free of the beam she was on and flapping her wings once, propelling herself to the next. A sudden strike forced her to parry awkwardly and she slid back, barely managing to keep her balance. "Such hatred. It's not just because I'm a demon, is it. It's because of what I represent."

"Silence!" Seraphine shouted, slamming her blade down in a massive overhead chop that sliced through the space Jade had occupied a moment before and smashed straight through the beam, cleaving it cleanly in two. Jade was forced to take to the sky again, taking another glancing blow to her thigh but scoring two clean slices of her own before darting away, elusive as a leaf on the wind. Her mind was no longer fixated on escaping. As soon as she'd realized that was impossible, the idea was swept from her mind. Now, there was only one thing she desired.

"You hate this." Jade purred, landing on another beam away from where Seraphine's radiant web glittered in the air. "You've been fighting for so long, it's become all you know. You thought it had purpose. Honor."

The angel shot a trio of radiant bolts at Jade, firing them with a mighty flap of her wings. Jade turned invisible, dashing along the length of the rafter to evade.

"Maybe it did, once. But now, war is all you know."Jade launched herself from the beam, flying up towards the vaulted ceiling of the cathedral.

"And now you've learned the truth. There is no honor in battle, only blood and pain."

Seraphine gave chase, following the sound of her voice and rising with her. The succubus reached the apex of the room and twisted, still invisible, planting her feet on the ceiling. Channeling a burst of mana through her legs she blinked down, emerging from Mirage Step directly behind the boss and plunging both Fang and Phantom into the small of her back. Phantom glanced away, deflected by her armor, but Fang found a crack, gouging deep into her flesh.

Seraphine screamed, whether in pain or rage Jade could not tell, and kicked back at her. The blow was too fast for even Jade to dodge. It caught her in the wing, crunching through something that sent fire rippling through her wing and back. Her dagger's effect saved her, draining just enough life force to restore the wound before she dropped too far.

"And the worst part is." Jade continued, gritting her teeth through the pain of her numerous open wounds. "You like it anyway. You want to share that pain, to make others feel how you do. You savor it."

Jade had no idea how much of what she said was true and how much was embellishment. Her senses could only pick up the broad strokes of Seraphine's mental state, not specific details. Whether it was true or not, the delicious scents of the angel's anguish and wrath indicated her words were having the desired effect. Her foe was getting sloppier, leaving larger openings and making rash decisions. On the flip side, her attacks were only growing stronger with her mounting anger. Jade's barrier hadn't restored itself yet, and all it would take to put her down was one clean hit. She was playing with fire, and she knew it. But when you were all alone and at the mercy of the elements, you used the tools you had.

Or maybe she was the one being controlled by her emotions. Letting her demonic instincts for pain and torment drive her to ruin. She casually pushed the thought aside, flitting past Seraphine to land another invisible strike to her wing. The blow staggered her, and she took longer to recover than with previous hits. Like Jade, all it would take was one decisive blow. It was thrilling. Jade's heart thundered in her chest. Never had she felt so alive. Here, in the sky above a cathedral filled with warring spirits, she danced with death to the dark melody of battle.

Seraphine raised a glowing hand again and Jade felt the magic of her invisibility spell ripped away, revealing her to be standing below and to the right of the angel. She dodged a pair of attacks, the boss putting her on the defensive once again with astounding swiftness. Jade darted further away, summoning an orb of darkness and slamming it into the space between them. A sphere of shadow enveloped them, Clinging Darkness plunging a large area in all-consuming gloom.

The darkness of her own spell didn't impede Jade and she reversed course, running back into reach while Seraphine was blind. A sheath of crackling energy formed around Phantom as Jade plumbed the depths of her depleted mana to manifest an aura blade. She lunged, swift and silent, going for the kill.

WHAM

Jade was blasted away by an eruption of golden energy. It radiated out from Seraphine in a sphere, burning away the darkness and searing Jade's skin, flinging her off the beam with tremendous force. She fell, disoriented, senses scrambled. Darkness threatened to take her, encroaching on the corners of her vision. Her strength was spent. Depleted. Her body bled from a dozen wounds, and the portion of her skin not covered in blood was burned an angry red. She tried to right herself, to spread her wings and catch the air, but she couldn't. Even the fierce rage that had fueled her attack wasn't enough anymore. Jade glared up at the angel's receding form, channeling every ounce of fury and spite into the look. One small, final act of defiance.

She was done.

With all of them here fighting beside the exit portal it wasn't an overwhelming force, but who could say how long that would last.

The dark knight's blade shattered Luis's defensive barrier, catching him in the shoulder and sending a spray of blood across where Siora and Naomi hunkered down behind him, furious casting healing and cleansing spells.

She can't be trying to do this herself again… Can she?
