In the celestial realm atop a majestic mountain, a beautiful island could be seen, floating in the clouds. Ethereal lights shrouded a palace made from pristine marble.

“The Cycle performs better than the last,” A humongous figure, shrouded in silver and golden sparks, revealed with a rare grin. His voice was shrouded in a thick layer of Divine Power.

A second towering figure, a faceless woman, appeared next to the humongous figure.

“According to our calculations, the Anchors will blossom faster this time. The World Bridges will connect us to Earth earlier. Our domains will descend soon, Your Excellency!”

The largest of the towering figures stared at a shimmering pool of water, his expression filled with satisfaction.

“This time, nobody will be able to take the Root of Origin!”

The Gods of the Primordial Pantheon joined the Godfather. They praised him endlessly.

Only a few Gods remained silent, and only one of them smiled vibrantly in the darkness of the pristine palace of the Primordial Pantheon.

The Old Gods had long since gotten drunk in her opinion. Their greed and arrogance intoxicated them.

The Old Gods of the Primordial Pantheon couldn’t even see what had changed compared to the last Cycle…or who…

The Goddess in the shadows continued to smile, her eyes lingering on the shimmering pool of water that showed a youthful mortal plundering a seemingly ordinary library. He swept everything into his spatial ring, without leaving a single book behind.

‘You stirred the Wheels of Change, little Godkiller. All that’s left is to keep moving forward and to keep struggling.’
