Bean and Chloe share another long look.

"Um… Minerva, don't you… I mean, your parents sort of also… you know," Chloe tries.

"Just because they didn't want me doesn't mean I wouldn't save them if their lives were in danger!" I insist. "I'm sure they probably loved me in their own way, and anyway that's not important. What matters is that the artifact and Luna are connected somehow. Probably. I still think she's being blackmailed or threatened or something!"

"I can hear you, you know," I say. "I have superpowers and stuff."

"Well, if nothing else I definitely agree that Luna is being coerced," Bean continues, ignoring me. "But we kind of already knew that. There isn't really any other explanation for what's going on."

"Oh," I say. "Right. Well… shoot."

I was so excited about my theory, too! Why would she lie about her parents if they weren't important? It seems like she was lying about a lot of stuff regarding who she was and where she came from. Why did she even need to do that in the first place? And why would she invite Bean over if she was trying to hide it all?

"Well," I say, "I still think there's some kind of connection between Luna and—"

Click. The door opens, and Luna walks inside. She's as overwhelmingly miserable as before, if not even more so. Oh, crap. Well, here she is. No time like the present.

Are you sure this is a good idea?

Look, Fulgora, we're not very smart. We're not clever. We'll never find some great way to fix everything. But what we are good at is direct blunt force. And honestly? I don't think Luna is ever gonna tell us the truth if we don't force the issue somehow or another. Plus, we have so much evidence right now. Maybe not like, the best evidence, but we have a lot of it at least!

I guess. And, if nothing else, I'm tired of doing nothing.

"Hey, Luna," I greet her. "Where've you been?"

I feel her fear and despair rise, but she ignores me, navigating over to YouTube, of all things. Is she planning to just ignore me until I go away? Well, I'm not going to be deterred quite so easily!

"We can protect you!" I insist. "Whatever they're threatening you with, we can stop it. I'm way stronger than I was before, and I know for a fact Castalia will want to help too."

"Hey! I know you're upset, but don't ignore me! You'd better not think you can be more stubborn than I can!"

Luna reaches up with one hand and points behind me, staring me down as she does. Hesitantly, I turn around, and… wait. That's Nanaya. Limping like she's hurt. And all around her, the black mists of the Dark World writhe and swirl.

I'm not really sure how to respond to that at first, but Bean steps over and sits down on the couch next to Luna, and Chloe sits down on the other side, giving Luna a hesitant hug, so ultimately I plop down on the other side of Chloe and watch the video. She's finally talking to us, at least. It's something.

I haven't gone on a lot of expeditions in the Dark World. For obvious reasons, it's something that the Preservers want us to do only when absolutely necessary. And honestly, the video doesn't have all that much visibility. It's just mist and more mist, Nanaya limping across gravel for a few minutes before finding a metal hatch in the ground. Zooming in on some sections, focusing on the little microscopic tragedies that flood the room. It's harrowing. The kind of stuff you might find in old war documentaries. Which… I guess is exactly what this is.

"I don't like the Dark Rebellion," Luna's phone suddenly chirps. "They are, frankly, completely fucking insane. But the Preservers? The Preservers are worse. They don't think of us as anything other than fuel."

"So?" she asks. "You don't know any Preservers other than Uma'tama."

She gets up, walks into her room, and locks the door behind her, leaving the video playing. Chloe, Bean, and I all stay seated, and we watch.
