Fire spirits were largely outlawed because they always destroyed more than they saved. Wildfires raged out of control; cities razed to the ground. Azure Blaze was different. Such was the power of S-rank rifters—and their weaknesses.

That’s where they are… Kab thought. I know it… He flew toward it, but stopped when he felt a destructive power coming from within. What the hell is that? he silently exclaimed.

Nothing good—he could tell that at a glance.

There was a coran pattern coming from the abyss. It was slightly reminiscent of a double helix, two sets of springs moving clockwise and counterclockwise.

How’s that even possible? he wondered. That’d make a core explode…

Kab felt a sinking feeling in his gut. He didn’t want to go down that well. Whatever was down there—he didn’t want any part of it.
