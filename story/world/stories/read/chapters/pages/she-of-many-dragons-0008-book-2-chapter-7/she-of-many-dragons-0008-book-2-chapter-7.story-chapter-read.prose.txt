Spark got in on the game and retrieved mud from the riverbank—only taking a little extra time to zap the occasional eel that tried to lunge for him.

Prim began weaving everything into a nest. She hadn't done this for the other eggs, but Alice trusted she knew what she was doing. Perhaps she needed more preparation to ensure this would be a good match for Natura.

Meanwhile, Natura sat off to the side, looking slightly poleaxed.

"Is there something wrong?" Alice asked, concerned. Had she erred? Did Natura not want a sub-aspect, or did she object to the thought of a protector?

But when Natura looked at her, her eyes were very big, and she seemed very young. "You would do this...for me? Waste one of your sub-aspects?"

"Why in the world would you think it's a waste?" Alice asked, puzzled. She placed her hand on Natura's head. "My greatest wish is to keep you safe."

"You're a part of our family, and I wasn't angry with you then," Alice said. Inwardly, she was horrified that Natura still saw herself as such an outsider. But to be fair, Alice could feel that she was not quite in lockstep with the rest of her dragons.

Not to say that Numi, Iggy, and Prim weren't their own personalities, but they had been created from Alice's class, from something that she had needed at that moment. Natura had not, though she was quickly becoming one of the team. Had she picked up any unease from Alice? She promised herself that she would do better.

"You are just as precious to me as Prim, Numi, and Iggy," she said truthfully. "And Natura, you're so young and so powerful already. You've done amazing things. Dragons could not cry, but the way that Natura stared at her, still wide-eyed, made it easy to imagine that tears were welling up.

"I... I will accept this gift," she said softly, then went over to see what else Prim might need.

Prim did not take much time to make the nest. She seemed to know instinctively how to weave everything together, and when she was satisfied, she settled down, then soon moved away to show off the new egg.

Then his tail snapped forward and struck so fast, Alice only began to react after the moment was past.
