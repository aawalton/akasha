And even if I decided to go into it, then it would be my decision." His gaze locked with the others as he continued. I respect you far more than that. I ask those questions because I cannot afford to have my ticket out of this cursed realm die on me just because he made a stupid decision. That was the reason why I asked if you went into the chamber—because I thought you'd been reckless."

Poison.

Void runes.

He'd found the dragon's chamber.

He should go back.
