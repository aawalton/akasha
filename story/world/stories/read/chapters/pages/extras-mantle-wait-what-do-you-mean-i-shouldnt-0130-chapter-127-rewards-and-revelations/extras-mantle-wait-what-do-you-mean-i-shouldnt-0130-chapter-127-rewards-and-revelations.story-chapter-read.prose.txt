Immediately.

Five hours.

Five goddamn hours.

It had only been six hours since the fight ended. He probably needed rest more than she needed answers.

Probably.

Finding out that Trish was a monster. Becoming friends with her anyway. Learning that Jin had rampaged through the bastion like a force of nature, killing cultists by the dozen. And then—impossibly—taking down an Underlord.

An Underlord.

Monster, she thought, but the word lacked heat. Jin was a monster, yes, but he was their monster. And in a world that was trying very hard to kill them all, having a monster on their side was probably the only thing keeping them alive.
