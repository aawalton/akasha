Alex, outside the cave…

One second I stood near Noseen, trying to figure out how they were here and why the shield would fall. The next I flew through the air, as Strange clutched onto my boot. My form shifted immediately, taking on the additional armor I’d stolen from Grent.

Mana blasted out from the cavern around a tier three level. Uneven pockets of nearby mana were erased. The light filled the sky, then vanished.

The battle paused just for a second. Long enough for me to figure out it was down to three. Three massive beings. Two of which were ganging up on another.

All three noticed me at once. All three were closer to level 300 than I was.

The one getting ganged up on used that moment to claw at the eyes of another.

I dropped to the ground and took off down the mountain.

Mentally, I plotted out different places in the swamp that I could practically vanish, using the shadows to my advantage. Both from my journey here, and from the knowledge I’d gained.

I needed levels, and I needed to devour. I didn’t need to get into a fight. I couldn't win against multiple creatures stronger than me.

Stange raced down the mountain side with me. I couldn’t stop even if I had wanted to, as gravity helped me along. My surefoot skills and my dexterity were the only things that kept me upright.

I crossed the frost line in record time, but the heat and mugginess didn’t hit me as hard. It wasn’t until I almost made it to the swamp that the others even reappeared on my map, way behind me. Red dots swarmed all over in the swamp.

The first enemy I literally ran into. I smacked hard into the back of a monkey-shaped creature fighting with three of those scaly birds. They were the biggest birds I’d seen so far. Each one was at least the size of a soccer ball, with sharp beaks, talons, and scaly wings.

My claws sunk into the monkey’s shoulder and I ripped down. It screamed as I took the limb. The birds dove at me for stealing their kill, but scattered as the two creatures chasing me down the mountain arrived, crashing into the bushes behind me.

Strange rejoined my shadow with but a whisper, fear flooding our bond. This was not a place or time for him to be out in the world. He was much safer where he was.

Notifications pinged, and I chucked the whole monkey-thing carcass into my inventory. All I needed was a place to vanish. I dove behind a massive tree and merged into the shadows, before leaping onto a rock, also covered in darkness.

One creature raced by, followed by the other, who shot something at its back.

Neither of them paused for long enough to notice I wasn’t still running.

My heart pounded as I tore into the meat I’d just obtained, chewing before swallowing. I checked the notifications as I dug through the carcass for the core, eating as I went.

[You have survived a battle with a level 225 Madid.]

[You have gained a level.]

[You have gained a level.]

That was easy. Almost too easy.

Stumbling into the battle and taking the final kill felt almost like cheating. It wasn’t the nicest thing to do, but food would help, and I needed all the help I could get.

Small red dots started to fill the trees, sending a shiver down my spine. They formed a circle on the map, with me somehow in the center. Once I found the core, I tossed it into my mouth with a crunch.

[You have devoured a level 225 Madid and gained bonus experience.]

[You have gained a level.]

Energy rippled through me, but not like before. It satisfied my stomach, but little else. I allocated some free stats, but didn’t know what else to do. Noseen told me to devour, but the creatures out there weren’t ones I wanted to fight one on one.

A hum started to fill the trees, coming from the birds, and I decided to get the heck out of there. I left the remains of the carcass behind and dove into the water. Swimming beneath the murky surface, I headed to the area that I knew from my memories.

[Your standing has increased. Current Standing: 2]

What the heck?

All I could come up with was that the first place creature had taken out the second. That left me to take the next place. I didn’t really want to run a planet. I had other goals, like growing and helping my family try to keep up with me.

A question whispered in the back of my mind, wondering if that was all I really wanted.

Near the carcass I’d left, a larger dot appeared on my radar. All of the smaller dots fled with screams loud enough to hear even underwater. The red wave of dots rippled through the area.

The massive tree I’d skirted around on my way here, the one hiding something dangerous, came to mind as the giant red dot followed me, jumping into the water. I sped up, swimming in that direction before aiming closer to the surface.

Vines appeared floating on top of the water, and I quickly dove back to the bottom. This time, I waited, trying to stay as still as possible.

A shadow swam in my direction.

[Peddles, Sniffer, Swamp Prowler, Level 276, Predator, Unknown.]

The cat-like creature had fins running along its spine, and webbing between its claws. It used its long tail like a rudder as it moved through the water. It reminded me of the massive alpha cat back in the Sanctuary, who had almost kicked my ass. Except this was a much higher level gap.

Not good.

My eyes flickered up to the vines on the surface, along with the massive red dot that just touched this area. Even without my insight picking up anything, I knew those vines were bad news.

Peddles continued in my direction, and its eyes locked with mine, even though I hid within the shadows.

Really not good.

I waited as it rushed through the water in my direction.

Steady… steady…

Then, just before it struck, my spear appeared in my hands, with the butt braced on the rock behind me. It pierced right through the thing’s chest and out its back, its paws stretched out to reach me.

I heaved up.

The tip of my spear touched the vines on the surface.

Everything moved, all at once.

Vines shot down into the water, grasping my spear and the cat. Before I lost the spear, it vanished into my inventory. I dove deeper into the water as the cat jolted forward, with the spear no longer stopping it. The wriggling vines took it.

Vines yanked it above the water as I rushed through the depths back the way we’d come.

[You have gained bonus experience from combat for surviving against level 276, Peddles.]

[You have gained a level x 5.]

[You have gained territory.]

Energy pulsed in my center as the new stats took effect, yet the boost barely registered. The last notification caused me to slow down a little.

Why didn’t the tree thing take the territory?

Something smashed into the water behind me, and I couldn’t help but look. The carcass of the prowler sank to the bottom, now headless.

My stomach grumbled, and everything inside me strained to go eat it. Before I stopped to think, I found myself next to it, digging my claws next to the wound my spear had caused. The surface of the water rippled as another red dot, much smaller than the massive one, appeared.

As soon as my claws touched Peddles’ core, I grabbed it and shot back out of there. I waited until I left the red dots behind before shoving it into my mouth. Time to get back to the Dread’s area. Its old lair would be a good place to plan from.

[You have devoured a level 276, Peddles and gained the manifestation: Underwater Adaptation.]

I triggered it as soon as the words appeared. My claws shifted as pain rippled over my body. Webbing appeared between my claws, and something happened to my feet. I didn’t dare look, as something cold rose from my spine and stretched out behind me.

I really didn’t want a tail.

Yet, the results surprised even me as I shot through the water faster than I had been jumping through the jungle. Even my eyelids changed, and I could now see everything on the surface.

Again, my thoughts pushed away the pleasure of the new manifestation. The thing that finished off the prowler only took the head, leaving the core behind. It also didn’t claim the territory it had won.

Why?

In the far distance, a whisper caused me to twitch, and another cold thought raced down my spine.

Lenna.

Immediately, I went from swimming toward the Dread’s area to swimming as deeply and quickly as possible back to the Clan village. Well, old village. The portal to return to where Lenna was. Otherwise, I didn’t know how to find her.

If all of this was going on, was she okay?

Concern from Strange didn’t help. I swam faster.

The village was on fire.

Light flickered in the night sky, but nothing moved. Rather, no clan member moved.

Ignoring it all I stayed under the water, trying to not think about the fact I hadn’t needed to breathe. I didn’t let it worry me, and ignored that I probably had gills or something now.

By the time I made it to the island with the portal and slowly climbed out of the water, nothing registered nearby. The webbing on my hands vanished, along with the tail I was still trying to ignore.

Freaking tails.

Heavy smoke clung to the area, making my nose itch. The portal wasn’t active, but it shouldn’t be too hard to figure out. All it needed was mana.

Strange crept out of my shadow, but he stuck right next to my foot. He sniffed at the air several times and shook his head, almost sneezing.

Using my second sight, I studied the portal, trying to figure out how to activate it.

Currents of mana hung around, twisting in a looping pattern, but they were weak. The tendrils were practically see-through, and almost completely gone, but touching the stone archway.

“I must be lucky,” whispered a deep voice from off to my left side. “Second place comes right to me to fight.”

I didn’t move, just continued to look at the portal using my second sight. Mana flickered around the sharp creature. It had claws longer than mine, spiky fur, and six limbs. Plus, a spiky clubbed tail.

“I’m not here to fight you,” I said quietly. I slowly motioned to the portal. “I need to turn this on and leave.”

Its head cocked to one side. “Don't fight?”

“No, I’m not on this world for this stuff,” I said, shrugging. “I finished my task, and now it's time for us to leave.”

It hesitated, then inched closer. “What task did the great ones give you?”

Strange vanished into my shadow, but all his focus remained on the creature.

The creature's head snapped to the empty space, but then moved back to my face.

“The great ones didn’t give me any task. Apochas did,” I answered, seeing no reason to lie.

The creature jerked back with a growl. “Apochas failed to stop them! They caused all of this!”

I snorted. “If you believe that, you are welcome to do a better job. Though, the Clan is gone, from what I can see.”

“Eaten, the clan is eaten!” It growled again, baring its teeth in my direction. “I will rule this world, and beasts will rise!”

“Sounds like a solid plan,” I said, not really caring. I stepped closer to the mana pattern. From what I could tell, I only needed to fill the holes with mana, and it would trigger the portal. Pretty easy, since I could see it.

“That lady of light will be eaten along with the rest of those creatures!” He tore into the wooden platform as he approached, his feet and shredding the wood. “Even the great ones wouldn’t stop me!”

“Lady of light?” I asked carefully, as an image of Lenna came to mind. Her abilities glowed, sometimes like footprints in the dark.

“She sent images to me of a different way, one of creatures and beings of every shape. Full villages, with civilized beings and beasts working side by side.” He shook his head, spittle flying everywhere. “Horrible, all horrible!”

I pressed my lips together. She still lived, since it hadn’t eaten her yet. It needed to remain that way.

This was not my battle. But, I would not allow Lenna to be eaten.

Glowing yellow eyes locked on me. “You are with her aren’t you, helping her protect those who deserve to die!”

I let out a sigh, wishing I had some insight into what this even was.

“Give her to me, and you can leave. Stop the white light around her, and you can go.”

“Not gonna happen.” My voice came out soft, but clear, and I turned to look at the creature full on. This had just become my fight.
