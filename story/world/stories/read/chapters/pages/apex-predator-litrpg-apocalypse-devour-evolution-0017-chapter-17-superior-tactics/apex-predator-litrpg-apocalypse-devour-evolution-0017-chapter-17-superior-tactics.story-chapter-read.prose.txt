Jacques slumped up against a tree after felling the bear. Even with help from the Hungerer, this had been a tough kill. He preferred hunting humans. There was something very different in a man versus man death fight. It was exciting. Or it should be if everyone here wasn’t such a damn weak fool.

Fighting beasts was different. It was… the expected state of things. Of course humans should dominate beasts. But when it was human against human, Jacques wanted to see who was the dominant one.

One thing was for sure. He had underestimated the tall man, which the Hungerer called Don. Don… Chosen of the Hungerer. Not without reason it seemed. He could not believe that Don had managed to kill one of these monsters alone.

Jacques had of course seen the Raiders take down these bears with their sacrificial pawn tactics. Disgraceful and cowardly. Jacques enjoyed deeply killing the Raiders.

But most of all, he wanted to fight Don. The Hungerer whispered that time was nigh. He could soon challenge Don.

Who is this man that the Hungerer wants to cultivate? Is he better than me? I barely survived this bear. If it wasn’t for the Hungerer’s gift, I would be dead… And Don does not have the gift, I know this.

But one thing was for a certainty. Don was no ordinary man. And Jacques could not WAIT to see who would be the hunter and who the hunted once they finally had the chance to dance.

Jacques sighed and savored the iron taste of blood in his mouth. It made him feel alive.

“Don…” Jacques whispered to himself as he drank a health potion. He rolled the name off his tongue, testing it. “Don, Don…Are you ready for me?”

*

Ultimately, Don decided the safest thing to do was to backtrack and find a few Gibbons. After killing three of them, his Health and Energy were topped up again. It felt great to run with a whole body again. Don kept sprinting on and off, pushing off large rocks and bulging roots for speed. Just in case he would level up. This time he finally did.

[General Skill increased: Sprinting!]

[Sprinting Level 5]

As Don headed back towards the area with the bears, he tested his sprinting ability. He figured he was maybe… ten? Fifteen percent faster? It was really hard to gauge, because his Agility and Power certainly affected his running speed. But just based off what kind of max speed he had had directly before the Skill level up, Don figured his top speed was now around ten percent faster.

That was not nothing. In fact, that ten percent might be just the edge that was required to survive a tough fight. And since Don was heading back towards the bears, he had tough fights coming up. Some unhinged part of him was excited by the prospect. Don chuckled and shook his head. He wasn’t sure why he seemed to fit so well into this new world, but he was glad he did. He had run past enough corpses to know not everyone was so fortunate.

He headed back to the area with the bears, passing a grassy hill that separated the area between the gibbons and the bears. For now he figured he’d try to kill one and go back to the gibbons to top up his Health and Energy and then back to bears. It might not be the most optimized amount of experience, but it was his best plan. Another would be to wander around and look for a suitable monster. That could take hours. And if he kept doing the bear-gibbon loop for hours, the bears would become more and more manageable.

But as Don approached the bear area, he was formulating a new plan. The sheer mental exhaustion, and his limited amount of potions and rations made fights like the previous one unsustainable.

He needed an upgrade. Fortunately the fight with the thugs had netted him a lot of Evolution Points. Don brought up the interface, and saw that he had 273 Points.

Ever since obtaining the Simian Physique evolution, Don had relied on it a lot. It enabled a level of physicality that was absolutely superhuman. Unfortunately the System recognized how valuable it was, and the upgrade to E-rank would cost 350 points. However, the upgrade of the Claws, would cost 200. He would have enough.

Don had to bite the bullet and take the upgrade. The bears were tough and he needed every advantage he could get. Hell, he would have gotten this upgrade before his first fight, if he knew how powerful the bears were.

[200 Evolution Points spent]

[Evolution Upgraded!]

[Retractable Claws (E-grade)] -> [Retractable Claws (D-grade)]

Don immediately inspected his upgraded ability.

[Retractable Claws (D-grade): You can shift your fingernails into metal-like claws at will. The claws are 2.5 inches long, half an inch thick and have the qualities of steel. +3 to Agility, +6 to Power]

Don felt a surge of delicious power as the stats were added to him. He flicked open his claws. They were slightly longer, still black and gleaming. The keyword iron had been changed to steel. That made the claws significantly stronger. Don couldn’t wait to try them out.

He heard a growl somewhere ahead and crouched into a stealthier stance. He felt that his improved physique allowed him to move quietly very fast. The System was clearly helping him out whenever he stepped on dry leaves or brushed through bushes. That made moving so much easier. Don would have to figure out the limits of his abilities, but right now it seemed his Stealth skill was overleveled for these threats.

Don found a bear pushing a bloody snout into a human body between two trees. Level 9, so slightly weaker than his first one. Still large and menacing, with a black fur somewhat covering bulging muscles. The giant round head was moving rhythmically as it chewed. It was eating the dead woman with a singular focus. That allowed Don to sneak up close and perform an ambush. He had a plan now, he would just need to see if it worked.

Don got really close this time. In a moment that felt right, he lunged and sliced at the back leg with both claws. They bit into muscle and sinew and cut the flesh into ribbons. The claw upgrade had done significant work. The tendons were cleaved much more easily and the blades of his claws bit deeper into the muscle.

The bear turned and attacked immediately, swiping a paw down. Don dodged to the side and slashed at the paw. The wound wasn’t thick, but it would slow the bear down.

Then he sprinted away.

The bear followed, enraged by Don’s ambush. It was fast, but when Don glanced behind him, he could see the back leg was limping.

Don darted between trees, low and fast. This was not a desperate escape like last time he had fought a bear. Don had a strategy. If he had managed to keep ahead of a bear while half-dead, he would surely be faster now. He kept his distance just far enough to keep the bear committed. Let it rage. Let it bleed.

Every few meters, Don glanced back, gauging the limp. The beast was strong, but it was already dragging its leg. It left a trickle of blood in its wake as it rushed towards Don.

He swerved behind a thick spruce and ducked low.

The bear came crashing through half a second later.

Don exploded out from behind the trunk and raked his claws across the bear’s flank.

Blood sprayed, dark and hot.

The bear bellowed and swiped wide. Don had already slipped behind another tree. He could hear it stumbling, trying to reorient. It was still rabid and dangerous. With terrifying force it charged at Don, trying to dash around the tree. Don kept darting in and slicing the bear’s limbs with his claws.

Don was already running. The bear followed, still fueled by rage, but it was clearly limping. Both hind and front legs of its right side were bleeding. Don darted behind another tree. He watched the bear approach.

In a surge of fury, the bear roared and rammed the tree. Bark flew. Don reappeared and carved two quick cuts into its right shoulder. It twisted and snapped—teeth grazing his forearm.

[Health at 89%]

Don backed off. Fast. The bite hadn’t landed properly.

It’s losing coordination.

He sprinted again. Another feint. Another flank. Another cut.

This time he hit the back again—same leg. He pushed his claws into the back of the bear’s thigh.

The bear shrieked and collapsed onto three legs.

Don started circling around the beast. It watched him and kept turning as Don moved. All the while as it bled out.

On some primal level it knew it was short on time. So it went for a hail mary.

The bear thrashed wildly and tried to grab Don into a bear hug. But Don danced away easily. The bear was just too slow.

The bear showed a moment of weakness and frustration. There was a resigned slump of the shoulders. Don didn’t even think. He saw the opening and attacked immediately.

Don lunged forward and dug both claws deep into the beast’s throat. The resistance was thick and meaty.

He twisted and tore.

The bear let out a final, shuddering moan and collapsed in a heap.

Don stepped back. Breathing hard. Body slick with sweat. His claws dripped red.

This has to be it… right?

He waited.

Just in case. It never hurt to be careful when battling a six hundred pound giant bear.

[You have slain [Blackmaw Grizzly – lvl 9] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 70 System Points earned]

Don immediately fell on his ass and started laughing. The relief, the sweet victory. It rushed into him and mixed with the adrenaline. He had figured out a way to kill the bears efficiently.

His predator’s body was already expecting the reward. Don was actually surprised at how insistent his need to use Devour was.

Is this going to be a thing..?

It didn’t seem anything too crazy right now. Don shrugged and claimed his rewards.

[40 Evolution Points acquired!]

[Evolution trait Blackmaw Grizzly: Consumed]

Just as Don lifted his head up from the bear’s neck, he heard a shuffle in the nearby bushes. He turned to look and saw a cloaked thin man emerge.

The man’s eyes bulged as he looked at Don’s bloody mouth and the drained, dried up bear corpse.

He spoke in a faint  french accent. “So you are special, Don Damon.”
