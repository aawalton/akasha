The force with which Goldman was thrown had been so great that Harry could barely process it. Launched like a cannonball, he pierced the ceiling, flying out of the church. Pieces of cobblestone of all sizes rained upon Harry's group as they turned to see Conrad, cracking his metal knuckles; the man was a living armor.

Even though he seemed like no match for the Shepherd, Daniel stood in front of the group as he commanded everyone to get out. At the same moment, the church's doors opened, and a dozen of cultists appeared, wearing their red adorned robes. Behind them, Vela could be seen. Strapped down in a piece of wood like witches from old times, the former monk appeared unconscious. Don't die, buddy. Harry thought to himself, unsure why felt this rage inside of him.

"Go!" Daniel yelled as he stared at the living armor. "Get him! I can hold him off." He definitely couldn't.

The command seemed to snap the group out of shock. Madeline, tightly holding her brush, painted a few slashes on the air, which, in succession, broke through the barrage of cultists as they all screamed. The acid attack wasn't lethal, but it sure could slow them down. She then painted colorful stairs that remained in stasis in the air. Following Madeline through the yellowish inky stairs, Brun looked back, only to see her father stand up against Conrad. Don't die, not again.

Shit... Torn between two options, Harry chose to remain in the church, helping Daniel fight the colossus. Yes, it was the same man who picked a fight with him because he wanted to test the man's honor or integrity or some shit like that. Although, what kind of man would Harry be if he left an ancient viking to be viciously destroyed by a living armor? That's the kind of story you don't bail out of.

"Now we duel to the death! I—" Daniel paused as he noticed Harry to his side. With an expression of someone who just sniffed a bowl of molded bread, he added. "Who are you!? And what are you doing here?"

"What the fuck, man? We met like a few hours ago. I'm here to stop you from killing yourself." Harry replied. "What about you?"

"I can definitely, um, stop myself from killing myself!" The viking replied, his tone divided between confidence and confusion. "Now get out!"

"May the Tower bless you in your next life." Conrad dragged his head from one side to another, as if drunk. Then, with a sweeping motion, he conjured a diagonal log from one of the pews, which directly hit Daniel in his stomach.

The viking, then to stop himself from hitting the rigid walls of the church, conjured one of his arrows behind a pilar. The arrow was strong enough not to bounce off of it, promptly canceling the force that was carrying Daniel.

The viking discovered soon enough that if he wore to pull his arrows to himself while they were stuck, he would be the one to be pushed, making for very convenient movement. It was rather exhausting, though, pushing yourself like that. Either way, it worked.

By the gods... Daniel panted as he finally recognized the strength in Conrad, as if throwing Goldman like a pebble wasn't enough. At least his ignorance didn't cost him a liver.

The tree trunk that hit Daniel the seemed to disappear, just like Harry's doors after a few moments. That's his Emblem... So what is up with that armor? The Locksmith added inwardly.

From a place of rage, the viking bellowed once again and used his Emblem to do a majestic leap towards Conrad. He also conjured a few arrows that all tried to pierce the Shepherd's armor, only to bounce off. The young thief, imagining the colossus next move, created a passage with his doors to displace Daniel from the logs attack.

"WHAT...!?" He looked around, then gave Harry a stare that could boil an entire lake. "YOU. Are you trying to kill me?"

...Are you serious? Harry thought to himself as he saw the man switching his targets. "I'm on your side, you imbecile! He was going to hit you again. Let's just—" And Harry was nearly hit by an arrow. He touched his left cheek, only to see his bloodied fingertips. "You know what? Fuck you, Daniel. Do what you want; just don't mess with me."

The Locksmith could've been more diplomatic, sure. But he could've also let Daniel be struck once again, with a higher possibility of real damage. If that was how Daniel treated him, then why should he care? But a part of Harry did. He knew Daniel didn't mean to do it, the action was simply a product of his uncompressed rage. Either way, he wasn't eager to find out if his rage could get worse.

Harry then lunged at Conrad with a dagger in his hand. Swiftly avoiding a hit from the Shepherd and hitting it in the joints; normally, armors had blind spots in there. But, even the supposed fragile spaces were also covered in metal. What the hell!? It wasn't your average chain mail, but an extension of the armor itself, as if it were his own skin.

"Oh, you're here!" The figure's head then snapped at Harry, narrowly missing him.

Instinctively, Harry fell through a door as he searched for a safer spot. Behind one of the pews, the man stared at his dagger. If the old object were a person, it could be described as a lethargic and blind beggar. Cracks surfaced through the dagger as the weapon's tip was now blunt. Unusable.

Looking around while swinging his arms, Conrad's fighting stance was... unnerving, to say the least. Each hit stronger than the last, the furious attacks resembled Harry more of an uncontrolled ape than a serious knight.

Daniel then jumped at him, once again. The curious thing, though, was that Conrad only noticed it, even as the viking leaped in front of him, once he yelled. Daniel summoned a few arrows and landed a clean punch on Conrad's armor, only for his fist to repel against it. Swallowing his pain, which left his throat a bitter taste, Daniel then used his arrows as propulsion once again, pulling himself back as Conrad failed to hit once again.

Harry jumped to where Daniel was and whispered. "Don't do that. I think he doesn't—"

"WHAT ARE YOU SAYING!?" The viking said loudly, once again attracting unwanted attention from the Shepherd. "Why don't you always talk like that? I like it; it matches with your strength."

Harry then brought them both to the other side as Conrad barraged through the pews. "Shut the fuck up and listen to me. He's blind. I don't know if it has something to do with his Emblem, but he's blind. If we can just coordinate our attacks and be silent, we can—"

"He has the Log thing, it can't be a part of his powers." Daniel cut him off. "Your strategy and logic are feeble and should be burnt down with fire. I am ashamed of your proposition and politely decline it."

"That's you being polite!?" Harry was indignant; how could this man be so stubborn? Fuck. he had to deal with this faster so that he could help Vela and Goldman, wherever he was.

Well, I can always try this approach. Harry left the spot and captured Conrad's attention. The man then proceeded to throw a fist at him, only for a door exactly its size swallow it. The result was a powerful hit from the colossus against himself from a passage in his left. The man stumbled a few seconds until he regained his feet.

"...Join and separate." He whispered to himself as his stance changed from a drunk to a praying mantis. Using a log from one of the walls, he hit himself with it and leaped towards Harry, who simply fell through a door. However, Conrad still managed to grab one of his legs.

Oh shit.

"Hey!" Daniel yelled from behind him as he conjured dozens of arrows to, once again, bounce off of the armor. Conrad, like grabbing a rooster in the neck, maintained him in the air as he secured the two perpetrators who were disturbing his sacred space. He focused, thankfully to Harry, more on Daniel. The Locksmith could've been upset that it meant Daniel was more noteworthy than him, but it wasn't time for foolish thoughts. He slid through the man's grasp and fell on a broken pew.

With little time to react, he jumped through another door, connecting him to the roof. He then fell through another door which led him, horizontally, towards Conrad. With a stored momentum, he aimed his bump into his knees, which promptly made him fall, and therefore, loosening the grasp on Daniel, who now panted on the cold floor.

Although it was a fair use of his Emblem, Harry wasn't fond of it. Yeah, it caused damage; just not only to his opponent, but also to himself. The pain he felt on his back was absurd as he wondered if he'd broken anything. The whole church was still a blur as he tried to get up, only to be met with a death grip from the titan himself.

Thump! Thump! His heartbeat was getting exponentially faster, and Harry wondered for how long he could push himself. From passing out for several hours, to getting jumped on by a viking and then what looked like God's right hand? The Locksmith was panicking. But he swallowed it once again. If he could afford being scared, he could afford reacting; at this point, panic was a luxury meant for outsiders.

What Harry didn't expect, though, was how fast Conrad wanted him off of the fight. It was almost humiliating, really. As if the Locksmith was spoiling the titan's duel. Creating momentum from a single movement, the Shepherd spun him around and threw him exactly through the hole created by Goldman.

The Locksmith wasn't dead because, for some reason, Conrad still wanted them to help him reach the top of the Logos Tower, again.

It was an exaggeration to say that Harry's life was flashing before his eyes, but that's how it felt, just in a marginally smaller portion of said life. Why am I not dead? He thought to himself, as the fight became clear in his mind. The way the Shepherd moved and his odd phrasing made it obvious that Harry was right; Conrad was blind. Apparently, he had the Log Emblem, which allowed him to summon... logs? Okay, it isn't fair to judge a man's Emblem based on his Symbol, since Harry could do a lot with only doors. Either way, that wasn't the problem. He has two Emblems. The Log and the... Armor. He has two Emblems. Two. Fucking. Emblems. How were they going to defeat him?

Harry could see the appeal in the fire, though. Majestic and yet terrifying, the mother flame induced that sensual danger.

Layers of a shy substance clashed with Harry's attire. It felt like the wind, but heavier. It was like clouds, but less imposing. Am I dreaming... or is this a dream? The young thief pondered over the floor's reality as he felt his senses fading away. At that moment, however, he realized that he wasn't outside. The wind and the church were as real as the Logos Tower wanted it to be. Maybe that's what made this prison worse than death; it trapped you in an illusion, and it made sure you knew its nature.

Out of a sudden, he felt a tug. No. A repulsion, coming from behind him. As if an invisible force were pushing him against the force of the throw, he gradually came to a stop in mid-air.

"Are you alright, little one?" A reassuring voice came from behind Harry.

"...Goldman?!" Harry said. "What... How are you..."

"It's my Emblem, lad," He replied, "I can induce magnetic fields in things around me and, with that, add repulsion and tugs depending on the magnetic north or south I determine. It works best with metals, however..."

Like telepathy, he kept his palm pointed at Harry as he came from below. The only strange thing was the metal plate embedded in his palm, but after his explanation, that made sense. It was also possible to notice the metallic sole on his boot as he floated.

"I have to be closer to the ground to make this work. The flight, I mean," Goldman continued, "If I go too high, the field of my boot with the ground becomes too large and the repulsion loses its effect. Believe me when I say things can go south pretty fast... Literally!"

And he chuckled for a brief moment. You like to talk, don't you? Harry was infected with a smile as he shook his head and explained what had happened to him, the two of them still in the air.

"Understood." He nodded as they both descended, "And you say Daniel is in there, fighting, right?"

"I don't think you understand how insane it is to have two Emblems, Goldman." Harry added. "He's basically impenetrable with that armor. Besides that, he can create and manipulate, um, logs... It doesn't seem that menacing when I say it out loud, but he can pack a punch."

"I..." The gentle giant, with his blond hair, sighed deeply. "I am aware."

In the Emblem community, or the closest thing they had to a community, the full effects of having a second Emblem weren't all that known. What they knew, however, was that most ended up crazy in one way or another. Some scholars suggested it was simply the way the human mind worked when it had this much power. Others assumed it was a property inert to the Emblems. In Conrad's case, it was both.

Harry then saw Vela on the distance, almost fading away to the fire that surrounded him as Madeline fought dozens of cultist, each with an even weird Emblem. The monk had — and that was a high estimate — maybe 10 minutes left. The Locksmith could even see parts of his skin being devoured by the fire, leaving only lifeless blotches. Is that how he would've liked to die?

"But we keep on going, don't we?" Goldman's belated response woke him from his regretful thoughts.

"...We do." Harry smiled back at the man, who left him on the floor and levitated towards the church in a straight line.

Vela wasn't going to die, for Harry still wanted to talk more to the former monk. And it wasn't the Logos Tower who was going to stop that.

...And his Emblem is very useful. Harry added inwardly.

Like that mattered, huh?
