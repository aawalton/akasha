Alice stared down into the pit with one hand covering her mouth. Below her lay a gruesome sight. The child's skeleton was finely preserved, with the flesh and clothing all gone, leaving its bones curled around an egg.

The egg was as big as two of her fists put together. Tan was the base color, with black striations running down the shell. For a moment, she thought those lines were cracks, but no. They were only vivid markings. She knew down to her core that this was a dragon’s egg.

"I don't understand," she murmured after a moment. "There is no dirt on the shell. Has it been freshly laid?"

"No," Natura said.

All of her dragons were currently with her, also staring down into the grave. Numi had been so loud and excited that the news had spread quickly. Everyone had rushed to see.

"How can you tell that?" Prim asked Natura, though there was no accusation in her voice. She seemed merely curious.

"The soil has not been disturbed, other than Numi's haphazard digging."

Far from taking offense, Numi just blinked her big yellow eyes at the nature dragon. "You can tell that?”

“Soil is alive, after all."

"It's what?!"

Ignoring them, Alice knelt at the edge of the grave. The poor child. And the poor egg, buried so deep in a bed of cold soil with no hope of rescue.

Chicken eggs died when the hens left their nests and allowed them to grow cold for too long. There was no reason at all why this egg should still live. Yet, as she hovered a flat hand over it, she felt life beating sluggishly within the shell.

Without thinking, she plucked out the egg and brought it close to herself. "Oh... the shell is deathly cold. Iggy, please kindle a fire so we can warm it."

"I'll gather dry wood!" Natura exclaimed and went with Iggy to start a fire.

Prim, meanwhile, stayed with Alice in her customary place on her shoulder. She looked at the egg with open curiosity. "You wish to hatch it?"

"I..." Alice had truly not thought that far. "Could I? No." She answered herself with a shake of her head. "I could right now if I wanted this egg to hatch as a sub-aspect. But I think... not." She looked down at the egg cradled in her hands. "This poor creature has been waiting for someone for ages. I feel bad, but it must wait a bit more until I can achieve my next level."

"And for you to decide what you need it to be," Prim added.

A chill slithered up Alice's spine. "Yes, I suppose that too."

What did she need a dragon laid in the grave of a child to be?

It did not help her mood when Numi piped up, "Well now that I know the scent of the bones, I can tell that there are people buried all in this forest. I shall see if there are other eggs within the skeletons, or more treasures."

With that, she scuttled off into the underbrush.

Alice and Prim exchanged a look.

"Perhaps we should not camp here for the night," Prim said.

"No, let's not," Alice agreed.
