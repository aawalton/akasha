The girl was meddling with things she should not. She wanted her assistance with a project Valene had approved. Of course Valene approved it, Yshe couldn't remember a time where Valene had been truly cautious. Why should he, it always worked out in the end. At least for most people.

She wasn't an Architect, she wasn't allowed to make the changes that would be required. Then again those few who would be allowed were unlikely to be willing to work with such a small Holding, to create such a thing.

The Doorkeepers were not something you meddled with. They were dangerous, and eminently capable of acting if they decided Fay was a threat. Not that the threat would be believed, techniques were only as dangerous as their wielders, Constructs were passive things meant to improve the life of Awakened. They didn't meddle, they didn't interfere, they were things. Because no one knew about Doorkeepers.

She'd need their help. They'd be able to get into the necessary areas, make the necessary changes. Most of them that was, she'd have to perform the most critical work, the acts that needed finer control of Energy and sufficient strength to handle the flow.

"Where's Sandra?"

"I'm here. I'd have been here sooner if Sam had held the door for me, or if he'd bothered to help me get out from under that table."

"You managed fine, you were only a few steps behind me."

"Sam," Yshe said.

"She was fine."

"You two have to learn to work together, not against each other," Yshe said. "There's work to be done. If you can't work together you'll probably break something. I'd rather work with only one of you than that happen, and that will take much longer."

Yshe saw them exchange glances. She wasn't sure why they kept acting this way. They could get so much done when they got along, but they seemed to prefer bickering. They'd learn in time.

"Something for Syl?" Sandra asked.

"Yes. Something only certain Architects are permitted to implement."

"But Auntie, you're not…"

"Will you teach us?" Sandra said.

"Some. Enough."

"When do we start?"

Yshe waved her hand across her work table, sweeping it clean before calling up plans she'd thought never to need, knowledge she'd kept despite the rules. If they were to do this they'd do it properly. No one would fault her work or find it lacking.
