By the time the Old Kingdom’s army was within reach of the Forlorn Castle’s walls, the sun was high in the sky at its zenith. Given that the soldiers of the Old Kingdom could reach the walls intact, it was safe to say the others had done a good job laying out the welcome mat.

Still, they were immediately set upon by reanimated people and animals as soon as they entered the city, and they lost easily forty soldiers within the opening attack.

Because they had several priests with them under the leadership of Father Adam, all of the dead were blessed after falling, preventing Adam from targeting them with his magic and keeping their bodies out of the Shadow’s reach. The same went for the enemies they fought, since they didn’t want to be stabbed in the back by the fallen rising again once they’d moved deeper into the castle town.

Seeing as the army was competent enough to keep itself safe, Adam linked back up with the others and moved towards the castle entrance. Within was the melody of one of the twin knights, as well as something stronger and denser behind him, which had to belong to the Forlorn King.

“Are you ready?” Adam asked, handing each of them a torch.

Adam and Seo-Yoon both wore their aspirant’s cowls, since it should allow them to see the dark mana used by the invisible twin.

Out through one of the large viewing portals in the top of the tower, the castle roof could be seen. It was coated in unnatural darkness despite the time of day, and Adam was certain this was what had tried to swat them out of the air yesterday.
