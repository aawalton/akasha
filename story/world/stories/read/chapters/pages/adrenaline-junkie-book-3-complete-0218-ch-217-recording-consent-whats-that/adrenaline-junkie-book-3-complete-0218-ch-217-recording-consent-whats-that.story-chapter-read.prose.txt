Archie’s gaze lingered there onto the smooth blue skin where he saw her cybernetic enhancements used to be, which he assumed signified her as some sort of mage, which suited her Sparks of Vitality.

The silence between them stretched uncomfortably long as Archie was doing his best to figure out how she was here in the Second Tutorial Event and why she was following and recording him.

They stared at each other, unblinking, for several long seconds until the girl let out a sharp, whining groan and flopped her head back dramatically.

“Ughhh… busted.” She pouted for a breath before jabbing her camera toward him. “Do you realize how long I worked and how perfectly silent I made myself to tail you? But even after all my work and effort…” she tilted the lens toward herself, angling it so he could see the faint smears where three flecks of blood clung to the glass. “…three drops. Three. Drops. Of. Blood… I knew I should have bought that splash guard camera modification when I had the chance…”

Gaining the faintest twitches of a smile on his confused and curious face, Archie continued to listen to her energetic rant.

“Honestly,” she went on, blowing air out of her nose in frustration, “while I get that your Profession is some type of smith, your Perception stat is truly ridiculous to spot three drops of blood far away from you and up above on a tree. To think months of success only to lose your trail, then find you again here, the System–”

“Thanks for the roundabout compliment,” Archie cut in flatly with a steady tone, eyes never leaving hers as he attempted to discern her fighting ability and how high of a threat she was to him, “but why are you following me?” His brow furrowed, and then another thought crossed him, the words slipping sharper. “…Wait. Have you been following me this whole time?... Since that cave-in at the border wall?”

The blue-skinned woman didn’t answer. She just lowered her camera slightly and gave him a thin, sly smile that spread wider than it had any right to.

Archie’s frown deepened. “When Aoife and I got ambushed by the Hounds?”

She gave him the exact same silent response.

His jaw tensed as he pressed again. “When I was in the city of Louther?”

She shook her head and produced a smirk as she calmly pulled out a small tube of cloth from her belt pouch and began cleaning the smudged lens of her camera.

Even further? He thought to himself in disbelief. And not once did I see her?

Achie’s disbelief finally cracked through as he quietly and weakly said, “… Since the Temple of the Handlers?”

At that, her smirk broke into a full-blown smile that quite literally reflected light off of it. The smirk broke wider, teeth flashing as she leaned forward, balancing effortlessly on the branch despite her camera in one hand and a rag in the other. Both arms went up in the air in mock celebration.

“Ding! Ding! Ding!” she said in a sing-song voice without a single note of mocking, the words echoing faintly across the upturned forest around them.

“How?” Archie asked in disbelief, his tone carrying a mix of frustration and genuine curiosity. His eyes narrowed on her as she finally put away the rag and tucked the camera against her hip. I had Vital Sight active the whole time I was in there and tried to escape, and didn’t see her vital outline nor her Sparks of Vitality anywhere in or out of the temple.

“Boo!” she shot back while childishly sticking out her tongue. “No, if you don’t figure it out yourself, then you’ll never know.”

… Is she being for real? Archie thought to himself in confusion. She just openly admitted to recording me without my awareness.

Archie blinked once, slowly, the muscles in his jaw tensing as he ground out, “I feel like I should know, considering how you’ve apparently been recording me without my permission. You realize on Earth that’s illegal in most places, right?”

“Bleh.” The sound popped off her lips as she stood gracefully on the branch, spreading her arms wide for balance like a street performer working a tightrope. “But we aren’t even on Earth. We aren’t even on my home planet anymore,” she added with a lilt of mock cheer, twisting her heel and walking backwards along the wood. “Universe 72 was destroyed, remember?”

With a sudden kick of her leg and a push from her toes, she cartwheeled into the air, front-flipping neatly onto another branch across from the first. She landed with hardly a sound, straightening up to give Archie a questioning tilt of her head as if his concern was the strange part in all this.

Archie went quiet, the weight of her words settling over him; Ah, right...

And with that, most of the tension in his body bled out to the point where a low sigh escaped from his lips. “Then… can you just stop recording me and following me around?” he asked as the edge he’d been holding in his tone began to soften. As his previously raised and confronting posture eased up, he said, “I don’t consent to being recorded, and I’d hate to break your camera because…”

He trailed off, but only for a breath before continuing with blunt honesty. “Judging by your whole camera persona, I’m assuming your Profession revolves around that thing. And if it’s System-enhanced, unique rarity…”

Archie’s brow furrowed as he Identified it, and instead of being greeted with a detailed System Panel, he was given only a few lines with a couple of keywords in them: Unique rarity, Growth & System Enhanced.

Nothing else.

“...then I’d be crippling you and your Profession if I destroyed it, and considering you also helped me back when I was trapped, I don’t think I could genuinely bring myself to do that.”

“Nope,” the blue-skinned girl replied without even a flicker of fear or a blimp in her sing-song-like tone as she hopped down from the branch she had been perched on.

She landed just a couple feet away from Archie while pinning down the hem of her tunic by placing her hands on her hips. “I’ve known you and seen you for long enough to the point where I know how you’d act. Archie isn’t the type of person who’d attack their benefactor, especially one who’s helped him many times.”

“Many times,” he echoed back with furrowed brows. “I only remember you helping me out once, at the tunnel cave-in at the border wall.”

She giggled in response, lifting both her index fingers and wagging them at him in a slow “no-no” motion. “I also helped you, Aoife, and Tim out when you were in the Salvoris Silver Mines. While I didn’t help you with the miners there, the golems, and those other creatures there, I helped you with the other ones; you know… the people who were hunting you both down? The Hounds of the Duke of Dreadmire.”

Archie’s eyes narrowed further. “…How?” he asked, the question carrying both suspicion and genuine disbelief. “I only saw–”

“Six people above,” she finished for him without missing a beat, her grin curling smug at the corners. “I got them all distracted and wandering around trying to find their way to you.

Archie’s frown deepened, his mind racing back. That’s right. I saw six people who were above Aoife, Tim, and I, but they were all D-Grades given by their Sparks of Vitality. His jaw tensed as the realization turned over in his head. Was she also a D-grade back then? How?

“But then how did you escape?” he pressed, his voice sharpening. “The whole mountain blew up.”

“Oh, that?” the girl chuckled, her long, droopy elf-like ears suddenly flicking, fanning up and down like restless pendulums. “I hopped in with you, Aoife, and Tim in the elevator express!” She clapped her hands once, eyes lighting up. “It was super fun! We should do it again!”

She actually jumped in place and made a few noises akin to those of a monkey on a sugar high as she said, “It was such a good ending to the first book, especially when you– ” Her voice faltered mid-sentence as her brain finally caught up to the words that left her throat.

Her eyes went wide as her lips then pursed themselves tightly together as if she’d just caught herself letting something slip.

Archie’s eyes narrowed instantly as he caught onto her words. “Did you just say Book? First book,” he repeated, low and deliberate. His tone had shifted from being utterly confused to now being incredibly suspicious. “What do you mean by book? Why would I be mentioned in a book? And why did you specifically say first book?”

“Uh…” Her bright eyes darted side to side as she realized that she had accidentally put herself in a horrible position. “Um…”

Then, seemingly coming up with an idea, she let out an exaggerated gasp and shot a finger toward his left, pointing at something behind him. “Wow! Look at that!” she exclaimed with forced wonder.

Archie didn’t react to her sudden reaction; instead, he kept his gaze locked on the blue-skinned woman’s comical pointing pose and crossed his arms over his chest to show that he was not going to react to her tricks.

“I’m not falling for the same trick I use,” he said plainly as she awkwardly shifted out of her comical pose. “So, now that that failed. Can you please explain what you meant by a book, and what the ending of the Salvoris Silver Mine Quest has to do with the ending of your book?”

“Uh…” The blue-skinned woman said with a nervous edge creeping into her tone. Her eyes flicked to the side as she began to take a slow step backward and cupped one of her long and droopy ears. “Er… do you hear that? I think another one of those Cragspew Ravagers is coming around back; you might want to do something about them. How about you deal with that while I, uh, clean my camera’s lens.”

Archie didn’t budge, nor did he move a single muscle away from keeping his attention on her.

Seeing that her attempts to divert Archie’s attention were not working, she snapped her right hand’s middle finger and thumb together, creating a faint, yet visible ripple around them.

In the moments after she snapped her fingers, Archie saw a sudden surge of mana bloom from her sternum, spreading rapidly through her body. His instincts screamed at him – whatever she was doing, it involved her trying to escape.

Archie lunged forward, covering the distance between them in less than a second and using nothing but his physical stats to do so, as using any of his other skills would potentially harm her.

As Archie’s right hand closed over her left wrist, the mana that was emanating from her sternum now practically covered her entire body.

And in that moment, his furrowed eyes locked onto hers, and confusion and annoyance met surprise and guilt.

But then, as quickly as their eyes locked onto each other’s, the blue-skinned woman vanished, leaving Archie’s right hand to clutch at nothing but empty air.

Even with Vital Sight still active, he could no longer see her nearby; even after tentatively running his hands through the place where she previously stood.

Even after turning Vital Sight to its maximum, the only D-Grade being he could detect nearby him was a non-humanoid one.

[Bi-Beak Dodo Lv 106]

Archie’s jaw tightened as he watched the dodo bird three kilometers away from him, rouse itself from its stationary position, and lunge after a high E-Grade snake that tried to sneak past it.

He muttered under his breath, almost to himself, “I didn’t even get to ask her for her name.”
