After their initial surprise attack, with Asher deleting its stinger and Rulfar chopping off one of its six legs, the battle grew far more intense. As soon as the monster recovered from the unexpected pain, it changed up its entire strategy. Asher didn’t know just how deep that glint of intelligence in its eyes ran, but the moment it realized he had some form of healing factor, the monster turned away from him. Instead, it went straight for Rulfar, raising its massive bulk up onto its hind legs before bringing its entire body down onto the bloodhound, far too quickly for him to dodge out of the way.

“Rulfar!” Asher shouted, looking on in horror as he waited for the last of his bones to finish mending.

“He’s fine!” Moxy laughed, taking advantage of the monster’s diverted attention to continue blasting small craters out of its flesh. “Focus on the monster!”

Sure enough, Asher blinked when the dust cleared and the monster pulled back slightly. Rather than a flattened and broken bloodhound, Rulfar stood there unmarred, the fur covering his body having gone rigid and glowing a dull red as he focused on his Unyielding Blood blended skill. Not only was Rulfar completely fine, there was a large indent in the monster’s chest where it had tried to crush him into paste only to discover Rulfar was all but immovable with his skill active.

Even so, the skill wasn’t all-powerful.

Buckets of sweat dripped off Rulfar’s muscular frame as his body trembled and the reddish glow surrounding him flickered out. They hadn’t talked about his blended skill in too much depth, but Asher understood that the greater the force he repelled with his skill, the more energy it took from his body. After having the equivalent of a large building dropped on Rulfar’s head, Asher wasn’t surprised in the least that he may have been drained dry surviving that lone attack.

“Rulfar needs time to recover, draw its attention!” Asher shouted, cancelling his levitation and sprinting toward the monster with the aid of Distortion.

“On it!” Samantha shouted, flying right past the monster’s face as she fired a glowing arrow straight into one of its eyes. Flinching back, the monster roared in pain before raising a leg and trying to knock her out of the sky. Clearly having been waiting for this, Samantha banked hard and twisted, changing her flight path in an instant and narrowly dodging out of the way of the powerful strike. At the same time, the edge of one of her wings sliced deep into the monster’s arm, carving a good dozen feet along the length of the limb as she shot down toward the main body before breaking away again.

While the monster roared and tried swatting at her with another hand, Asher reached its rear, elongating Crescent and stabbing deep into one of its hind legs. His weapon was definitely more impressive than Rulfar’s enchanted battle axe, but he didn’t have nearly the same level of strength as Rulfar did. Rather than slice the leg clean off, Asher had to settle for cutting through dense cords of muscle and arteries before using Distortion to leap upwards and dodge the reflexive tail swipe that nearly crushed him against the side of the monster.

“I’ve got a wound on it!” he shouted, trusting his team to understand what that meant. As of now, the battle had transformed into one of attrition. None of them needed to risk their lives trying to do anything flashy, all they had to do was keep it distracted and let it bleed out as Sever the Thread did the heavy lifting. Already, Asher could see that the gaping wound from the leg Rulfar had cleaved off was beginning to seal itself shut, showing that this monster wasn’t just insanely large, but that it had a powerful regeneration factor as well. If they turned and ran, the semi-intelligent monster would probably rip off its hind leg to rid itself of Asher’s wound and simply grow a new one.

“I’ve got a few dozen!” Moxy laughed, the warrior still running around behind the monster and punching an unending barrage of Air Blasts into its flank. The monster was large enough to shrug off most of her attacks, but it seemed constantly having flesh ripped from its body over and over again was starting to grate on it. Giving up on the nimble flier who kept peppering it with arrows, the monster spun around, snapping its neck out as it tried to bite Moxy in half.

“Woah!” Moxy laughed, grinning as she narrowly dodged the monster’s jaws with a blast of air at the last second. “I think I made it mad!”

“See if you can make it madder!” Asher shouted, landing on top of the monster with Distortion. Trusting in his assassin class to keep his balance, he ran along the monster’s body as he raced up toward its head, trailing an elongated Crescent beside him as he carved one long wound up the monster’s entire back. Its neck was far too large and dense for him to behead in a single strike, but he could definitely do some damage if he tried.

The monster lunged forward again and again, nearly throwing Asher off each time as it continued trying to close its jaws around Moxy’s form. But every time it lunged forward, Moxy seemed to blast herself out of the way at the exact same moment, as if knowing precisely where the thing was going to snap its teeth shut. The warrior continued laughing all the while as she danced with death, somehow even still finding the time to fire devastating Air Blasts directly into its face to keep the monster’s focus on her.

Deciding worrying about Moxy was a pointless endeavor, Asher turned his attention back to the task at hand as he raced toward the monster’s head. Waiting for it to try and fail to bite Moxy in half again, Asher didn’t waste a second as he leapt off the monster, twisting about in midair as he soared down past its neck. With flawless timing, he turned and swept an elongated Crescent to the side as he fell, slicing straight into the monster’s jugular and creating a wound that began violently spraying blood everywhere.

“Got it!” Asher shouted. Focusing on his Eternal Mark on Samantha, he Recalled to safety right before a powerful swipe from the monster’s hand could crush him into the dirt. While he’d escaped being turned into paste, suddenly finding himself fifty feet off the ground was a little jarring.

Thankfully, they’d trained for this exact scenario. He hadn’t called for Samantha to stop what she was doing and catch him, so she kept flying, continuing to try to snipe the monster in its second eye despite its repeated efforts to dodge. As he fell, Asher imbued a sliver of Eternal Mark into Nail before hurling his dagger straight into the back of the monster. The moment the blade hit, he triggered Recall again, saving himself from his fall as he warped onto the monster’s back once more and yanked Nail out of its flesh along with a spray of blood. Using Eternal Mark and Recall to move around an active battlefield wasn’t easy, but it was something he’d been practicing during training for some time now.

“Rulfar’s back!” Samantha shouted, acting as their eyes in the sky as she soared down and scored a deep gash across the back of the monster’s neck with her wings. “He’s on the tail!”

“Just keep it busy!” Asher shouted to everyone as he found his footing and started cutting up the monster’s back with reckless abandon. “We just need to outpace the monster’s own regeneration!”

Despite the fact that the monster was undoubtedly losing gallons of blood every second, the thing was absolutely massive. Asher didn’t exactly have the time to do the math, but it had to have an Olympic swimming pool’s worth of blood coursing around inside its body if not more. Not to mention the fresh blood it was creating due to its powerful regeneration. Even if Asher could keep it from healing, he couldn’t exactly stop the monster from creating new blood. They had to get to the point where its wounds were spilling the blood out faster than it could be created, and that was no easy feat when it came to something of this size.

Though that didn’t mean they wouldn’t try.

Minutes passed one after another as Asher and the team continued whittling down the monster one little bit at a time. Moxy acted as the primary distraction, keeping most of the monster’s focus on her as she barely managed to avoid being bitten in half or crushed to death time and time again. Her ability to change direction in an instant with a well-timed blast of air, and her Life element keeping her minor wounds healed and stamina topped off, made her perfect for the role.

Samantha scored countless gashes along the monster’s face and neck all the while, her Razor Wings carving deep into its flesh as she continued her call outs and kept everyone informed as to what was happening. Her impeccable accuracy meant the monster had to keep a sliver of its focus on her at all times to prevent its remaining eye from being shot out, making it harder to actually land a hit on the rest of them.

Rulfar remained at the back of the monster, his focus on tackling the tail itself and keeping it from swinging out and crushing anyone to death. Despite the missing stinger, the sheer mass of the tail and the speed of which it shot out meant it was still an incredibly deadly weapon. Rulfar managed to carve into the base of the tail deep enough that most of the strength in the tail was lost, and it did little more than fling blood everywhere when it tried to lash out.

And all the while, Asher ran up and down the monster’s back, impossible to dislodge thanks to his assassin class and ability to Recall to the dagger he’d marked and embedded in the thing’s back. Everywhere Asher went, he carved into the monster, slicing out massive chunks of flesh and cutting through bone and sinew alike. He stabbed into shoulder joints, carved into the back of its head, and distributed overall carnage with reckless abandon. The monster was simply too large for any one blow to take it down, but that was fine by him.

Death by a thousand cuts would have to suffice.

It took almost a full ten minutes of them working together flawlessly, which felt like an eternity when it came to a life or death battle, but eventually, it happened. The monster tried to rear back and swipe Samantha out of the sky again, and when it missed and came crashing back down, it struck the earth with enough force to send a quake across the battlefield as its own legs gave out. The quake was so powerful it managed to knock Moxy off her feet, sending her sprawling into the blood-soaked ground that seemed to spread out in all directions for a few hundred feet.

Not willing to miss this chance, Asher Recalled to Samantha once more to save himself the hassle of sprinting along the entire length of the monster before hurling a marked dagger down into the ground in front of its face. Recalling directly before it, Asher ran to its neck and shoved an elongated Crescent as deep into the neck as he could.

With a strained grunt, he slowly forced his blade up and around, using the craters Moxy had blasted all over it to climb up the monster’s powerful neck and drag his sword in a large circle around the entire length of its neck. The fact that the monster was so large that such a move still wasn’t enough to actually behead the thing was wild, but it was still more than enough to finish it off. Blood gushed out of the thousands of wounds covering the monster, and the deadly, intelligent light of intelligence quickly faded from its eyes as its head tilted to the side and its body relaxed.
