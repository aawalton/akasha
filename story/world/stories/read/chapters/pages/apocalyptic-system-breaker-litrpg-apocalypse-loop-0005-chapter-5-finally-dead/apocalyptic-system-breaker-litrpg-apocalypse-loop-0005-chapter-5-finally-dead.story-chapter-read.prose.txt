William opened his eyes slowly. He felt his face pressed onto the puddle underneath him, though it wasn’t enough to get into his mouth or nostril to suffocate him. Waking up again and again in a cold puddle was not something he had ever expected to be comforting. It represented another opportunity. A chance at becoming more than he could have ever been before falling down the stairwell. All he had to do was get up and make another attempt at killing the Corpse Guardsman–

He stopped himself from getting up.

I already killed it.

Long moments passed as he allowed his body to relax. It felt weak and sore, but like any time before that he could remember, there was no alarm to wake him. William could lay there even if he already felt fully rested. It was the first time he had gotten a full night's rest, probably more, without the need to scramble and get up for work at an ungodly time of the day. Worse was when he was still recovering after hard, grueling activities the night prior.

William groaned as he pushed himself up off the ground. His shirt was soaked. He yawned and stretched, ignoring the fact that he was completely drenched with water. There was nothing that could stop how great he felt. He took a moment to look around himself, listen to the dripping of the water, and watched the torch flames flicker back and forth in a hypnotic pattern. Then he called out his second pair of clothes within his inventory. The potato sacks they called clothing were as harsh as ever, but they were dry and would prevent him from getting sick. He took off the wet, dirty set and hung them on the horizontal bars of the cell.

His eyes then drifted back to his inventory, finding everything that he had been promised after defeating the Corrupted Guardsman. William had three [Earthly Realm Rune Shard]s sitting there, waiting to be used to level him up, the medallion that was still an unknown to him, and finally, the (F+) ranked [Skill Shard]. Not to mention the set of armor and ripped up clothing that were of much higher quality than his potato sack rags.

There was the potential possibility that the clothes that had been in direct contact with the Corrupted Guardsman could be tainted with some foul disease considering it was literally a corpse. Then again, the system would have self prompted with a danger sign as soon as he pulled them out. Yet, he was still going to be extra sure. William walked up to a puddle of water on the side of the room and dropped the shirt and pants into it. He would be keeping an eye out for them and if they system would give him a notification.

A detailed explanation would be optimal, though he needed to make sure what he could rely on.

Whether it was the [Skill Shard], [Earthly Realm Rune Shard]s, or the medallion, it tended to give him self prompted analysis.

Then again, he was too happy to really care at the moment. “I actually did it. I actually killed that thing.” William felt a laugh bubbled out of his chest. Satisfaction, greater than anything he had ever felt before, filled him from head to toe. He felt like he could fight a thousand Corrupted Guardsman right now and come out on top.

William received a system notification.

And I almost walked away from all of this?” William said as he stared in wonder. He gained twelve stat points and a boost to healing just by putting the items on. There was no way he was going to leave this cell until he was fully fitted in his new loot, but that meant waiting on the now soaked basic clothing set since he doubted having potato sacks rubbing back and forth on his nipples and chaffing his skin was a good idea.

Yet, the most important question remained. How many runes had he gotten from dying over twenty some odd times?

William closed his eyes and pulled out his status page. It showed up in his vision even in the darkness of his shut eyelids.

Runes Held - 2512

That was more than he was expecting. William had that nagging worry at the back of his mind during the entire fight and die stage until he finally killed his foe. The fear the system would screw him one way or another after it forced him to fight something that was almost impossible for him to beat, only to give him less than a full level. While all the loot was nice, seeing his level go up was far more satisfactory.

He pulled up his status page and stared at his pathetic [Class]. William wondered whether he should level it up as far as the runes could take him, or not at all, he couldn’t decide whether it was worth the time and effort at least until he placed the new (F+) [Skill Shard] into a slot. Maybe it didn’t work and he was stuck with this [Class]. He shook his head as he moved to sit down on the driest part of the cell with his back on the wall. There was much he needed to figure out before he continued.

The fact that the system malfunctioned and could potentially give him multiple classes could be a sore subject for others. He had no clue how anyone else would take it. The danger of people reverting back to their burning witches on the stake ways simply due to the catastrophe that was system initialization sent shivers down his back. Anyone different, odd or weird tended to be dealt with in very aggressive manners. Especially when safety was such a distant thing when Corrupted Guardsman could kill a dozen men alone without issue.

William sighed.

Before even that, he had to figure out if his system provided attribute points per level. If it did, then it opened many avenues for him to take advantage of. If it did not, then he was simply stuck to finding the most powerful type of class he could find. It would be purposeless to level something so low. Even an (F+) class seemed to be a waste of time, though he had no choice at the moment other than to take this path until he figured something out.

“Let’s do it.” William said to himself as he put just enough runes to level up his [Pathetic Tavern Brawler] class to level one.

William had already seen what a single stat to strength could do for him in a battle of life and death. It was not something he planned on underestimating. The tiniest advantage, even if it made no sense to him, may have the largest impact toward his victory.

Simply put, he would continue to build upon what he had and then figure the rest out at a later date. Slow, incremental boosts that would feed off of each other.

More importantly, he had been shown two different paths from what he’d seen so far that he could abuse. William could level up a dozen [Class]es for the lowest level amount needed for runes and gain first level skill unlocks as well. Moving from level zero to level one was far easier than attempting to do the same thing from level nine to level ten. Or worse, the much higher levels like the forties. That meant he basically had free stat points per class he could unlock.

Not that he would push it too far. William needed to be calculative when dealing with his classes. Maybe he was only given two, or three, or maybe even a dozen, but he had no solid evidence to prove any of them. He would keep to his specialized [Class] distribution plan to increase his survivability and combined power. It didn’t seem like his stats changed when he switched from a [Class].

Though he still wanted to see if he could get another, stronger class.

He couldn’t help but wonder what something stronger would be like. How bright would it have to be to keep this type of obvious change? Or maybe they were different colors entirely?

He picked up the [Shield and Sword Mastery]shard and put it into the first slot and waited with bated breath. He feared that the system would either go haywire or reject the shard completely, forcing him to never have a chance at a better [Class]. It took a few seconds before the slot began to swirl as he saw the first time, whooshing and accepting the [Skill Shard].

He stared at the ones available to him; [Pathetic Endurance Boost], [Pathetic Dexterity  Boost], [Pathetic Hardiness  Boost], and [Pathetic Strength Boost]. William had to put one to the side and use the rest, except each one felt just as necessary as the other.

Yet, if his theory proved to be right, he should have access to more than just two classes as long as the system didn’t go haywire, not that he was planning to waste anymore potential skill slots on anything in the (F) ranks. He would save his next one for a proper, real powerful [Class] unless he was given another solid investment [Skill Shard]. One that would solve a potential issue down the line. If two classes were available to him, why not three, four, and five? All different and with specialized purposes to tackle different enemies he would have no hope against with his basic classes.

[Pathetic Tavern Brawler] could remain as his base identity to hide his strength from those around him.

Everyone was liable to underestimate a person with a (F-) [Class], even if they looked or seemed strong. He would take advantage of that once he returned back to society.

Especially if that was a common mob he would deal with.
