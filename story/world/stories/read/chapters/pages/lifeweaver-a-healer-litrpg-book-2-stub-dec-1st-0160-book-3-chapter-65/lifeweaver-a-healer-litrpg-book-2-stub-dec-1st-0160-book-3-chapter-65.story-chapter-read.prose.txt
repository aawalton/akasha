Threads of Mana was Luke's primary weapon. Considering that, it was almost criminal how underutilized the skill was. Other than holding an opponent, diverting a blow, or just gaining access to their body, both physical and metaphysical, he hadn't considered what he might use the skill for. That was a mistake Luke decided to correct.

Despite being blind and bleeding from hundreds of wounds on his body, Arazek wasn't done. The half-demon still kicked and flailed as it bellowed in equal parts pain and rage. Some attacks came closer than others, but none of them hit anything other than the smaller demons. It did, however, give Luke an idea. With a staff, he could block and even use The Healer's Staff. Without that option, like in this instance, vulnerability was a problem, but perhaps there was a way to mitigate that.

Low on mana as Luke was, he wasn't able to bring the idea to life the way he wanted, but when his shield woven from interlocking threads of mana absorbed almost all force from one of Arazek’s blind, desperate blows, he knew he'd found another tool for his arsenal, and this was one he vowed he would learn to wield with expertise. The blow Luke took on the shield threatened to burst the entire thing apart, but with an effort of will, Luke kept it intact. How it would fare against a sharp blade, or fangs for that matter, remained to be seen, but this was a start.

Satisfied with the little experiment, Luke figured it was time to end this fight before the half-demon managed to heal itself. Getting in close, he pulled tight on the threads wrapped around Arazek's legs and added a third for good measure, locking them down just as his opponent punched the empty air again. This way, Arazek overextended himself, and being unable to move his legs, fell to the ground, crushing several lesser demons to death.

Fending off a few more of the smaller bastards, Luke leapt onto Arazek's back and breathed in to steady himself. Ignoring the pain from standing in burning sludge, Luke reached down with threads of mana. Needle of Life was thinner and smaller than a scalpel, but when you wielded several, cutting through even large chunks of flesh wasn't outside the realm of possibility, and for the first time in days, Luke felt like anything was possible.

Slicing again and again, Luke cut through the half-demon's neck. To Arazek's credit, the damn bastard lived until only a shred of skin kept the head on its body, though not for long.

Congratulations! You have reached Level 40!

"About fucking time," Luke grunted as he glared at the remaining lesser demons. They just stood there, as if they didn't understand what had just happened.

Re-forming his shield, Luke took a step toward them. The demons fled.

Head spinning from a lack of mana, Luke dissolved the threads keeping the shield going and drew in another deep breath. He nodded to himself. "Better, but still not enough."

"Could use another hand over here once you're done gloating, asshole," Ray groaned, appearing right where Luke had left him. In the distance, voices called out. Reinforcements. Luke hadn't needed them, not this time. Something had slotted into place during that last fight, and he felt stronger for it, but knew he'd just begun his renewed trek toward power.

Milla came running as Luke healed Ray, restoring a couple of cracked ribs and a ruptured spleen, of all things. "Shouldn't you have potions for this stuff?" he asked the grumbling Assassin.

Ray looked up at him with a grin. "Been focusing on poisons and things that go boom. Why waste time with a health potion when I've got my own personal pocket healer?"

"Pocket healer," Luke scoffed, getting back up to his feet as his sister approached.

"Luke!" Milla shouted.

Luke raised a hand in greeting. "I'm fine, Milla."

She stopped and frowned up at him. "Of course you're fine. Lots of people need help. Stop slacking off!"

When he gave her a dark look, she just chuckled and punched his arm, hitting the same spot she always did. "Just messing with you! Not about needing help, though! Can you get to Emma?"

He rubbed his arm with a scowl. "Was she the one on top of a burning building?"

"Still is," Milla confirmed.

"I can't fly. How am I supposed to get her down from there?"

She stopped and tilted her head for a moment, as if listening to something. "No, wait, never mind. She's fine. Someone got to her."

"Good, I could use a rest."

Lemira: "Please tell me you're still with us."

Luke: "Would you miss me if I wasn't here?"

Lemira: "Yes. Also, I need help."

"Lemira needs help," Luke told Milla, who nodded.

She turned and pointed down the street. "She's coming here. Lots of monsters following her."

"Demons?" he asked.

"What else?"

Ray grew bored and walked off, sat down, and started fiddling with what looked like the medieval version of the beakers and burners they'd used in high school. Alchemical supplies, Luke figured.

"So, what do we do?" Luke asked.

Milla looked at him in the way only she could, showing just how much of an imbecile she thought her little brother was for asking that question. "We're going to kill them, little brother. There are no portals, and the demons give excellent experience. Kill them all and feed that sweet XP to me."

"Huh?"

"I gain experience when you gain experience. Not much, but with enough members, it's like being power-leveled. Pretty nice, actually."

"That's cheating!"

"It's called 'being a guildmistress'!"

"They're coming!" Nash shouted, having just joined, along with the rest of his party, minus the ones who were still stuck inside that dungeon. Luke hoped they would find a way to bring them back out once this was done.

Nash was right. In the distance, Luke saw movement. Lots of movement. "Is it the same lesser demons all over the place?" he asked Milla.

"The dog-sized ones? Yeah. They're not that strong, but there are shitloads of them all over the place. We've got people scouting on buildings, and they're saying they roam the streets in waves."

"We need area of effect skills against them!" Ray shouted from over by his strange contraptions. "I'm experimenting to see if I can make stronger explosion potions!"

"Any stronger demons spotted?"

Milla's face fell a little, but she soon got her mask of confidence back on. "A few. Phoenix Phalanx is dealing with one, but said it will take them a while. Three more have been spotted so far, not including that one." She pointed at the corpse of Arazek. "Good job with that, by the way."

"Thanks," Luke said, keeping his eye on a dot that was a little big larger than the ones following it, still far in the distance. "Reached Level 40."

"Cool," Milla said, hiding what must be incredible pride over her brother reaching that milestone a little too well for Luke's liking. "You should probably check what skills you can get now."

"Yeah. I will. I'm not good against the lesser demons, so I'm thinking I should find the baddies."

"I'll come with you," Hannah's disembodied voice said as she rose out of Milla's shadow.

Milla shuddered. "That's creepy as hell, Hannah!"

Hannah winked at her as she moved closer to Luke. "It's how I roll."

"I'll come too," Curtis said, climbing out of the now dormant rift in the ground.

"I told you not to go inside the portal!" Hannah said, raising her voice.

Curtis mimicked Hannah's wink. "It's how I roll."

"Children," she muttered, turning to what they could now see was Lemira riding her pet at top speed, racing to stay ahead of the incredible number of lesser demons chasing her.

"Get ready!" their guildmistress shouted, before turning her back on the entire scene and jogging to the plaque, shouting over her shoulder. "I'm not much of a fighter! You deal with that noise and then report back to me!"

Ray put his kit away and joined them. "Told you we should have stolen a few more nukes. Too late now."

"You'd want to nuke New York?" Hannah asked.

Ray shrugged. "Maybe."

"Maybe we should keep that as Plan B," Curtis suggested.

"Told you," Ray grunted. "We're not carrying any more weapons of mass destruction."

"Right," Luke said, clearing his throat.

Curtis stepped out to the front of the group and raised his hands to his sides. Darkness streamed from his body and formed small specks that all began to rotate in the air over Curtis's head.

"You got it to work?" Luke asked, bracing himself for impact as Lemira thundered up the street.

"Still needs some tweaking," Curtis said with a grunt, just as Hannah stepped up next to him, her shadow growing until it covered almost the entire street.

"Shadow and darkness," Ray snorted, hefting a large explosion potion. "They're the same thing, aren't they?"

The two of them just glared at him, then shared a suffering look, shaking their heads.

"Here we go!" Nash shouted. "Time to rock and roll!"
