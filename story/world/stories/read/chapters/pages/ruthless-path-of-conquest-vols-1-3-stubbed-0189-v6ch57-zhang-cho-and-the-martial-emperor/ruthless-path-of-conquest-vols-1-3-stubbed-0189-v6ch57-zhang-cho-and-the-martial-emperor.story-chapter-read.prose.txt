James spent just a fraction of a second assessing his own physical condition before he launched what would be his final attack.

His body was a bit fatigued, but still recovering quickly surrounded by the swamp suffused with his aura. His chest wound had not stopped bleeding and did not appear to be healing properly at all. Even by a normal human’s standards pre-System, it was bleeding a great deal. He could control and manipulate the blood, of course, but it was worrying that he couldn’t forcefully heal the wound.

He could sense that it was as if the sword wound had disrupted his body’s functioning in that region.

So that’s how it works. Neat trick.

But James still had tricks left of his own. Even as the blood streamed from his body, he could still control it. He had a vast quantity of Mana building up around him, they were surrounded in swamp water that contained huge amounts of his aura, and all he likely needed to do to win was disarm his opponent.

James manipulated the blood to surround Zhang Chāo with it, and he pulled more from out of himself. His body could easily replace it if he focused his healing on simply producing more blood. His blood was more “dark water” like the swamp itself, but it contained far more carbon than swamp water.

Using the Mana James had gathered around him, invisible because of Hidden Magic, James prepared to combine the blood with a construct.

Zhang Chāo was still surrounded with more and more freezing water, slowing his movements, freezing his joints, keeping his smashed wing from straightening out fully. James accelerated the freezing process, pouring more Mana into manipulating the water.

At just that moment, the staff struck the hard bottom of the swamp.

The two men locked eyes as both felt the impact.

Then Zhang Chāo was paralyzed, as James used Soul-Freezing Gaze again at the same moment that he darted forward. His eyes bleeding and the backlash inside his head were just as bad as before, and Chāo started moving again at almost the same time that James lunged. But some damage had been done. The Mandate of Heaven that sprouted from the staff missed James’s chest this time, and as Zhang Chāo tried to sprout more branches from the weapon and direct them at James, ice encased the King’s head. At the same time, Mana from around James’s body turned into silk around Zhang Chāo’s body, encasing him in a binding made of James’s silk mixed with James’s blood, all cast in the form of dense, bladed chains.

They wrapped themselves around the King’s limbs and torso and began sawing through his exposed skin, gouging everywhere that could be cut.

James’s Soul Eater blade slashed through the water, and James poured Mana into surrounding the weapon, wreathing it in swirling blades hardened water and earth to give it additional cutting power. He watched the point of impact in tense anticipation as the weapon slashed through Zhang Chāo’s weapon-wielding arm, and he felt it when the bone cracked and split under his blade.

Yes!

Arm and staff separated from the body and began drifting downward in the water.

The Emperor used his control over the swamp to grab both and pull them awayo—or he tried to.

The Mandate of Heaven transformed back into its usual sword form, and it fully resisted the pull of the swamp water, cutting a trajectory through the muck and back to the Heavenly King.

How do I stop this thing?

James erected a stone box around it, but the blade cut right through as if it was nothing but water.

He turned and began hacking at Chāo’s remaining arm, but the blade landed in Zhang Chāo’s hand before James could do much damage.

The King’s body glowed brightly, and then it began to change. Scales sprouted from his skin, the ice exploded from around his head, his lower half stretched out like a snake’s tail, and his four limbs turned into claws—including regenerating the arm that had been severed as a dragon’s claw.

James felt that Chāo’s aura was not becoming much more powerful, but it was still growing stronger and denser.

You kept something back. You son of a bitch. You were holding out on me!

Unlike the western-style dragon James had fought in the melee, James now faced a Chinese dragon.

The dragon’s head reminded him of a lion’s, if lions were reptilian.

Only one problem. I can feel it. You’re not much stronger!

James darted in quickly with the Soul Eater longsword, and the silk and blood combo that had loosened as Zhang Chāo’s body stretched out tightened around the dragon again, restraining his upper limbs for a moment. James slashed up through the middle of his ribs, beginning to fillet Chāo like a fish, and the Emperor was gratified to see that his weapon could cut through Chāo’s scales.

Not much stronger than you were before, confirmed.

A surge of electricity suddenly swelled up from within the dragon and blasted James back, but the swamp water redirected most of it in other directions to protect its master.

You could do that already. Show me something I haven’t seen before.

James charged in again, and the Mandate of Heaven swung at him. But it was slightly slower than before. Chāo wasn’t used to this form, clearly, and perhaps more importantly, though his actual speed was probably the same as what it had been pre-transformation, it was harder to move quickly with a longer, more ungainly body.

The Emperor used the swamp water to jerk Chāo to the side so that the sword meant to parry James’s attack was yanked out of line, and he severed the limb that he had chopped through before once again.

The next few minutes were just hack and slash by both fighters.

James stabbed and slashed at Chāo’s transformed body over and over again, stabbing him in the torso, chopping off both of his legs, hacking into his shoulder. Chāo didn’t land as many clean hits now as he had been able to earlier, still slowed by the difficulty of properly using his transformed state. He managed to sever one of James’s legs, but James used swamp sludge to instantly make himself a new prosthetic leg that would function for the duration of the fight. He plugged up the wound on his chest the same way.

Diamond-like armor grew around some parts of Chāo’s body as the fight wore on, but James just cut around them, and eventually, the King seemed to get worn down.

James began to outpace him more drastically, darting around behind Chāo and slashing his back or stabbing him in the side before Chāo could swing his own sword around to counter. It was all the Heavenly King could do to keep his sword-wielding limb from being severed in order to keep fighting.

When the dragon opened its mouth, both men were streaming blood, James from a near-severed arm and Chāo from dozens of different wounds.

But the King had decided he’d had enough.

“I give up.”

Somehow the words carried through water, so that James, and presumably the System and others, could hear it.

The Emperor blinked and found himself in the waiting area again.

The timer appeared instantly in the corner of his vision.

[The next round will begin in two minutes. Prepare yourselves accordingly!]

[00:01:59]

At the same time, blood gushed out from every place he had patched up with swamp sludge, the substance suddenly gone.

Shit!

James tried to circulate Mana to heal the injuries, but neither his body’s natural healing abilities nor Laying on Hands seemed to do anything. It was impossible for him to pay attention to anything around him as he bled from what felt like everywhere across his body.

Chāo’s not doing anything anymore. It must have been a property of the sword itself. Fuck! What a waste. How can I win the next round?

James finally resorted to biting off the ends of a couple of wounded places, and that seemed to alleviate whatever curse the Mandate of Heaven had placed on the limbs. His injuries still healed far more slowly than they ought to have, but they at least began repairing themselves.

But by the time he appeared in the ring, James was still standing on one foot.

He stood across from a man he remembered from the Summit, one wearing a plain white martial arts gi tied with a black belt. He was well-muscled but only of medium height and build relative to other Rulers. Still, James’s heart sank as he recognized the figure.

It was one of the only other human Emperors: the Martial Emperor, Aleksandr Abaturov.

He was a man in middle age, or perhaps late middle age, but his appearance didn’t show it. His unlined face gave no hint at his lifelong experience with fighting. James had only briefly spoken to him at the Summit. He knew who the man was mainly because Abaturov had been a martial arts champion of global fame before the System ever appeared.

“You seem to have bad luck, Fisher Emperor,” the older man said. “Zhang Chāo has already defeated you for me.”

“We’ll see,” James said. He knew he could not afford to hesitate, and he crossed the distance between himself and the Martial Emperor in an instant. Roscuro was already transformed into a long sword once again.

Abaturov raised a palm, open, in front of himself, to block. James avoided it and slashed at the arm just below it. He had seen a bit of one of Abaturov’s fights during one of the break periods before, and he thought he understood how the Martial Emperor’s ability worked. When he was striking or blocking, Abaturov surrounded his hands and feet with an invisible energy that made them hard and strong enough to stop blades and other weapons and penetrate the human body.

At least, that was how James thought it worked.

The blade slashed through Abaturov’s sleeve and bit into his wrist.

Or it should have.

James heard a sound like metal striking against metal, and the Soul Eater’s trajectory stopped.

“Like I said, unlucky.”

Abaturov’s fist came down and slammed into James like a sledgehammer. He felt the ribs closest to the blow shatter like eggshells.

Fuck. He gritted his teeth and staggered backward. Even if he didn’t feel pain anymore, he could still sense the weakness rippling through his whole frame as bones in the middle of his body broke.

The Martial Emperor didn’t pursue him. He simply took up a stance, both hands raised, palms open, feet spaced apart, and waited.

Okay, so his immunity to cuts and slashes extends to more than just hands and feet. New plan.

Lightning Strike!

James lunged in, blade in one hand, the other extended in a fist, and he attacked from both sides. Abaturov brushed the blade off with one palm and caught James’s fist with the other. His facial expression didn’t even change as he received the attacks. The lightning seemed to have no effect.

“You should know, I am immune to magic,” Abaturov said quietly. “You are wasting your energy.”

I don’t believe anyone could be completely immune to magic.

James pulled back, and again, Abaturov let him go. The Martial Emperor’s expression wasn’t pitying as he looked at James, but it was almost as bad. Abaturov was visibly disappointed.

The Fisher Emperor tried to come up with another plan, one that would not be reliant on magic. His leg was regrowing, so at least he would be able to put more force behind his attacks soon.

“I saw you use better tricks on Zhang Chāo,” Abaturov said. “But I guess you spent all your fire on him. It looked like the two of you gave everything against each other.” He pointed at the crowd in the stands. “He’s in even worse shape than you, I think.”

James decided that given the Martial Emperor’s advantages, this was not a trick to make him look away from the fight. He turned his head and glanced back into the audience where Abaturov had been pointing. He saw Zhang Chāo, looking pale and weak, like he could barely sit upright, beside Han Jianguo. A few feet away sat Mina and the rest of James’s party, as well as Yulia, Junior, Abhi, and the other children.

I guess everyone got to attend this part. I just didn’t see them, because I wasn’t looking in the stands, and they never made it to the break room.

James gave them a little wave, Mina smiled at him, and Yulia used Junior’s arm to wave back. Abhi and his siblings waved, and then Junior waved on his own.

He’s certainly learning quickly. I need to make sure I spend more time with him.

There was a flicker of movement in the corner of James’s vision, and he saw it was Han Jianguo, who was pointing impatiently. He mouthed the word, “Fight!”

James turned reluctantly back to face the Martial Emperor.

“Do you really want them all to see what I’m about to do to you?” Abaturov asked, sighing.
