Jeela’s face steeled when Hayden told her that he was announcing the auction. “For the coran flight technology?” she asked.

“Correct,” he confirmed.

“Why now? Isn’t the rift about to open?”

“That’s precisely why I’m doing it. Once the Vast Four realize how valuable I am, they will investigate, dismantle, and destroy Belia Alicibria and the Crimson Sickles if they touch me.”

Jeela’s expression conveyed that the true gravity of the auction was dawning on her for the first time. It wasn’t a simple event. He was going to boil Belia Alicibria alive and play the most powerful people in the universe to get what he wanted. If causality planned to destroy him, he would fight back with celestial resources—and crush anyone or anything in his path.

Jeela swallowed. “You said you wanted resources. What do you want?”

“These.” He lifted a folded square of paper and then slid it across the table. “Within the week. I don’t care how much they cost or what it’ll take to obtain them. If you want me to survive and hold this auction, you will spare no expense.”

Jeela unfolded the note with trepidation, but relaxed when she saw it. “This is it? Even if the Sickles can’t touch you, you’re entering the grand rift. I’m not comfortable giving you so little.”

“That’s the right amount. More would be problematic. So focus on quality over quantity.”

Jeela reached for her wine glass nervously. “And if you die? Is it an absolute loss?”

“It is. So make sure you prepare accordingly.”

Jeela lifted the glass and spun it. “I want a contract.”

“And shoulder any legal troubles that happen outside or within the rift?” he asked.

Jeela clenched her jaw, nervousness boiling into fury. She fell silent for twenty seconds, contemplating the deal seriously. Then, she said, “I’ll help you. In exchange, I want that auction—and your participation. But I’ll warn you: if your technology isn’t real—if you can’t follow through—I will clasp hands with Belia and tear you to shreds." She made eye contact with a grotesque expression. “Today’s enemies are tomorrow’s executioners when the subject’s betrayal. Remember that.”

Hayden passed her another document. “The details on the auction.”

Jeela snatched it and stormed out of the room, leaving Hayden slightly satisfied. Wise enough to know her position; strong enough to stand up to him. He determined then that Jeela Rian would make a perfect subordinate in his future government.

Her link rang in her mind. She glanced and saw she had a message from Hayden, with a link to Golden Hawk—the top streaming site in the Golden Belt.
