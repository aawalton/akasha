Making a decision wasn’t as easy as he’d expected it to be. Looking past the semi-transparent screen and into the eyes of the injured mage, he couldn't help but internally curse at the situation he’d found himself in. The healer class would undoubtedly save her life, but that was at a massive risk to his own.

He still had a lot of people chasing after him, and while a healer class could technically help, it wouldn’t do much for his offensive capabilities like most of the other subclasses would. In fact, he could confirm that the healing subclass was not as appealing as half the other subclasses. A fact that made his dilemma absurd.

I suppose I could keep my mouth shut about what I’ve seen. There's no need to alert her to the fact that I might actually have a way to save her.

If he could keep his mouth shut until she died, then maybe he could bring her back with the necromancer subclass. Of course, it wouldn’t be her, but it’d be on record that he made an effort.

Sure, necromancy was frowned upon in Rinesta, but Levi had never been shy to go against the grain. However, he’d have to admit that the possibility of commanding dead people to do his bidding did, in fact, bother him. Then, again, necromancy was a smart choice; whether it was during dungeon runs or regular fights, there always seemed to be an abundance of dead bodies to make use of.

It was a reasonable option, but he shelved it for later.

Necromancy might’ve helped him during his dungeon run, but it certainly wouldn’t do much for him when it came to fighting someone like Ghost. The rogue who was after the stone was another reason Levi was hesitant about choosing the healer subclass. It’d save Xara’s life, but it’d do him little to no favors if he encountered syncs like Ghost.

The healer subclass would undoubtedly be a game-changer for any normal synced, but he wasn’t just any other synced. He was the obsidian rogue, and so he needed something that would let him keep his life. He needed something that would offer offensive and defensive options, and he needed something that would mesh well with his rogue class.

That leaves me with Portalist, Summoner, and Necromancer. A subclass like sharpshooter would give me range, but it wouldn't do much against Ghost.

Levi knew better than to make his decision without factoring in the rogue that was chasing him down. It wasn’t something that he was proud to do—making Ghost the centerpiece of his decision-making process—but he had to acknowledge that the rogue was heads and shoulders past him, and he’d have to adequately prepare himself for their next clash.

There was no doubt in his mind that they’d cross blades again, and when that time arrived, he’d have to be as ready as ever. So it made sense for him to discard the other subclasses—including the sharpshooter class—as he focused on the four other subclasses that could make or break him.

Necromancer, Summoner, Healer, and Portalist.

Necromancer and Summoner seemed like the same thing to the rogue. The two subclasses were a way for him to attack or defend using other beings, and the main difference between them was the quality of minions under his control.

Necromancy obviously dealt with corpses, a limitation that meant his minions would be bereft of any creativity in the way they attacked or defended for him. Summoning, on the other hand, would bind him with a sentient creature that he could control—a much-welcomed option but one that he wasn’t sure of due to hidden limitations.

At least with necromancy, all he needed was a corpse, but for summoning, he’d probably need to find a creature to bind, or if he were lucky, the system would provide him with one, but he highly doubted that he’d be able to bind with anything of a high enough level to keep Ghost from killing him within seconds. The same went for any undead he’d be able to raise at the early levels. His assessment of the two subclasses in relation to Ghost made him shelve them just as he'd done with the healer class.

That leaves me with Portalist.

He’d saved the most likely option for last, just to be able to dissect it better. The portalist subclass was one that meshed well with his class. It was also the subclass that offered him the highest chance of surviving his next confrontation with Ghost. And by surviving, he meant running away.

None of the other subclasses provided him with a better chance of escape if things turned sour than the portalist subclass. Besides that, the attacking avenues that the portalist would offer him were beyond enticing.

The only problem now was the dying mage; he could choose the healer subclass and save her, or he could choose the portalist subclass and save himself in the future. There was no in between. No ifs or buts—he needed to choose between him and her. But before then, he had a question or two he needed to ask the mage.

“You know the surrounding area, right?”Levi asked, receiving a slight nod from the mage. “Then do you know any place I could take you around here that could maybe help to treat your injuries?”

It was a bit of a reach, but he hoped that the mage would have something tangible to tell him, especially since she was a noble and their type loved to play around with maps and such.

“I-I don’t—” She stammered, then stopped abruptly, blinking rapidly as she clenched her bloodstained teeth in anguish before letting out a pained sigh. “T-The blood loss… It’s messing with my head. I-I…There should be a village of unsynced just to the east of here. We should be able to walk there within three hours. Hopefully, I won’t slow you down much.”

Blood dripped from the corner of her lips with every word, and Levi felt moved enough to almost choose the healer subclass on the spot—almost. “Don’t worry about slowing me down. Just give me a minute to get myself together.”

I’ll choose the Portalist subclass.

He just hoped that he hadn’t traded the mage’s life for more power.

Speaking about the mage, he’d also been thinking about her when he chose the portalist subclass. While portalists wouldn't help her immediately, it would surely offer aid if his hunch on what the subclass could do was correct.

I guess it's time to see if my hunch is correct. I accept my rogue class and portalist subclass synergy.

What if this isn’t what I expected?
