Liu Shu was quite angry that someone came during her Level Up process, but she didn't lash out at everyone around her. What if Flor and the rest got hurt because of that? She needed to find them first.

Seeing nobody above ground, her vision sunk into the underground, almost failing to notice the change in size from everything around her. 'Why is everything so small…? It doesn't matter; first, find Flor and the others.'

While Liu Shu searched, the awakening of the dormant World Tree came as a surprise for everyone in the town. Her aura swallowed everything like a voracious beast, and since the World Tree was scanning everything consciously, each time her gaze fell upon anyone in the town, they would completely freeze in fear from the terrifying pressure.

During her Level Up, Liu Shu had grown to 60 meters in height. She was now a truly giant tree, with a canopy that covered as much as she was tall. Moreover, Liu Shu's connection to weeping willows endowed her with a majestic figure and an enchanting air of beauty and serenity, accompanied by a faint touch of nostalgia.

If you added her current stats to her overall aura, Liu Shu felt like an absolute monster. Not many would be able to match her strength or presence.

Power surged from Liu Shu's roots, and her mind sharpened to incredible levels. Her Willpower and Anima stats helped with mental strength, and with her new comprehension and <Energy Cycle>, the information her mind could process skyrocketed.
