Get ready for a wild ride, because Volume 4 is just around the corner—and it's bringing some major upgrades for Jin Shu.

Jin Shu unlocks a brand-new, improved cultivation technique crafted by none other than Nano. This time, it might not even get him called an idiot by his mother… maybe.

The new technique harnesses the power of Metal, and Tattoos, boosting both his weapons and his techniques—past, present, and future.

Abandoned by both his mothers, his daughter, and even his lovers (how rude), Jin Shu sets off on a solo journey. But fear not—he’s not truly alone. His multiple souls and tiny robo-companion are still with him. Mostly because they literally can’t leave.

To reunite with his missing loved ones (who, okay, technically just left ahead of him), Jin Shu must cross the Demon Mountains, traverse the entire southern region, and venture into the great beyond.

What awaits him? A lot of danger. Some opportunity. (But mostly danger.)

Along the way, Jin Shu meets a very interesting new companion—one with a mysterious link to a past life. Intrigued? You should be.

Also, they own a mobile inn. Think Howl’s Moving Castle. Except… not even remotely like that. But still cool.

If you’ve seen this teaser, you’ll be glad to know—yes, that scene is 100% happening in this volume. So hold onto your spiritual cores, because—
