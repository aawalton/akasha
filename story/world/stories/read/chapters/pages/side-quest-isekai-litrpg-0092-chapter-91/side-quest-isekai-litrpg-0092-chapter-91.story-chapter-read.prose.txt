After digging the object out of the dirt, Logan brushed it off, revealing a chromatic cube that didn’t look like it had any seams. Based on its description, though, he was hesitant to handle it too much.

Name: Ultra-Grade Catastrophe ContainerType: ItemLore: While sealed, any disasters contained within are rendered harmless. The last seal has been validated by a gnomish divining wizard in Gnashridge Heights. This item bears an enchantment.

Logan: Mariv, I just found an ultra-grade catastrophe container lying on the ground in Caerwyn. It mentions a gnomish divining wizard from Gnashridge Heights, so I’m guessing that’s you. Any idea what this is?

Mariv: How peculiar. I gave that to Eurelius at the same time I gave him my perk divining rod. It is something that unleashes untold Chaos. But all my augury tests showed it would never open within the Fold. I don’t understand how it ended up in Caerwyn, but as long as it’s sealed, you have nothing to worry about. If it’s open, well, then it would be very, very bad. One face should have a symbol inscribed on it. Does it show an image of a skull-headed snake?

Logan’s fingertips prickled with nervous energy as he turned the cube over to find the symbol in question. The small object barely weighed anything, but he didn’t like the idea of holding a sinister curse. When he saw the patterned butterfly, he let out a relieved sigh.

Logan: Fortunately not. It just has an image of a butterfly. Remember what I was saying about Alden’s teleportation theory and how entire sections of land might have been teleported with the caster? I think that might be how this arrived here in Caerwyn.

Really, Logan couldn’t see how it could be any other way. The blight blast had triggered nearby Eurelius’s cottage, and if Amatra had survived and teleported their way here, the container might have been on the ground near Amatra at the time and got carried along too.

He was relieved to have a logical explanation for it. All those talks of prophecies with Brother Timmons had made him feel uneasy. He actually wanted to ask Mariv about those prophecies too.

Mariv, though, still hadn’t responded.

Logan waited a few seconds, which turned into a couple of minutes, before pinging him again.

Mariv: Yes. I’m here. And you’re sure it’s not the skull-headed snake eating itself?

Logan: Uh oh. Are you saying the scary-sounding symbol is a sign the curse is actually still sealed? And the pretty butterfly—which would make a whole lot more sense to symbolize something good—means it’s free?

Mariv: I’m afraid so. The lore goes back to the Devouring Coil, but in symbolism only. The concept is basically that as long as the curse is endlessly looping on itself, it is contained. But when it has reached its maturity and is unlocked, it will emerge from its casing like a butterfly from a cocoon. Give me a moment, I am double-checking my notes.

Logan scanned the area for signs of a terrible curse plaguing the area. Everything seemed normal immediately nearby. There was, of course, the growing concern of the spreading blight outside Caerwyn, but that had been happening steadily for some time now. Other than the warped mana signatures that led Logan to find the container, everything else here seemed fine. Even the mana warping didn’t seem like it was bad enough to summon a monster.

Mariv: I’m back. To answer your earlier question, yes, I do remember Alden’s teleportation theory. And I believe you’re correct. For all my tests on the curse’s container, and the assurances that it would never in all my days, or those of anyone after me, open and spill into the Fold, I suppose nothing assured me it couldn’t be unleashed elsewhere, such as the Ruined Wilds or Kural.

Instinctively, Logan checked on his quest to help the gardener in the Kural. It was still active and it didn’t appear to have had any changes. He hoped that meant Kural was safe.

Logan: Is this related to what you think the reavers are planning here in Caerwyn?

Mariv: It is definitely a possibility, but the reavers are still in a preparation phase. I feel as though I would have heard them talking about curses if this was part of their plan. Tonight will have a clear sky, and I will consult with my astrolabes. I should be able to tell if any drastic abnormalities with mana scarring have occurred.

Logan didn’t love the idea of the curse being unleashed, but at least it hadn’t popped out in Caerwyn itself. For now, it was contained behind the Boundary. He still didn’t like holding the empty container, though, so he stashed it in his inventory. But that wasn’t the only topic he wanted to discuss with Mariv.

Logan: Okay, I’ll stand by to hear your findings. By the way, remember the Casket Box of the Shrine of the Living Path I found sealed away behind the massive wall display in the Temple of the Five? Well, long story short, it’s in my inventory. And it’s not empty.

The sweet sound of coins clinked from Logan’s pocket as he left Lambert’s workshop. He could have put the coins in his inventory, but there was something about the weight filling his pockets after having gone so long with barely any money to his name that made him happy.

From his chat with Mariv, they had decided he should read the skill book concealed within the supposedly empty casket box. The safest place to do that was at the inn. After wrapping up the chat, Logan’s first stop had been to deliver the charged manaweight calipers to Lambert and to receive the coin.

When Logan asked Lambert what he should do with the faulty lumber, the carpenter said it didn’t matter and he could keep it or throw it away.

Logan’s jaw dropped at that. The lumber might have been damaged in some places, but there was still plenty to salvage. From the price tags on similar wood, Logan could guess what profit he could make on it. Even at a fraction of Lambert’s standard costs, it was a number with more zeroes than Logan probably needed.

While he could sell it, he had already decided to give it to the innkeeper at Gnashridge Heights. She had given him a quest for lumber, after all, and this saved him from having to chop down a bunch of trees.

Once he reached his room at the inn, he opened his cloak and let Nugget out. It had been nice to have Nugget along with him, even if it was increasingly difficult hiding the dragon within his cloak.

Logan rubbed the dragon’s forehead. “I promise I’ll get you outside the city soon so you can stretch your wings more.”

Nugget’s throat rumbled something like a purr as he pressed into Logan’s palm. While he had flown around inside the temple, that hadn’t been true exercise.

“Okay, Nugget. Ready to see what this skill book does?”

He pulled the casket box out of his inventory. It shone in the daylight streaming through the window. The clasp was cool to the touch as he hooked it with a thumb and opened the box.

The book inside was bound in simple brown leather. Its pages were yellow and worn, and it looked like it might fall apart if Logan handled it too roughly. With extreme care, he lifted it and held it in front of his face.

Logan: Mariv, I’m in my room and I have the skill book out. Should I wait for any of the others to get here? Will I have to worry about unexpected side effects?

Mariv: This is a utility skill book, not a monster core, so you don’t have to worry about side effects. The only real requirement is that you have the Life affinity when you open it.

Logan was glad to hear that. He still had the Life affinity active, and he didn’t have to worry about any 24-hour cooldowns before switching back to the affinity. He wanted to practice proper caution, but he was also eager to try this out.

Without further waiting, he opened the book. Strange symbols covered the pages, and they twisted and contorted as they lifted off the paper and swirled into his forehead. The notification that popped up was the most technical-sounding one Logan had received yet.

Congratulations! You have learned a utility skill!Shrine of the Living Path (non-leveled)Some pocket dimensions store things. This can store you.

You may access the shrine mentally or physically. You can do so from temporary portals or qualified fixed locations.

To open a temporary physical portal to your shrine, you must be at a safe haven. Safe havens require the following conditions:

No enemies are nearby

No danger is nearby

You are alone, or else with trusted, appointed individuals.

Approved individuals and related permissions can be managed in your interface. Your Willpower governs how many individuals may be approved. You may only have one temporary physical portal at a time.

Qualifying fixed locations can be assigned by nodal points. Nodal points may be increased by progressing along the Way of the Living Path. Nodal points must meet the safe haven requirements and be places that you have visited before.

When you exit the shrine, you may journey to a nodal point of your choosing. If you enter through a temporary portal but leave through a fixed location, the temporary portal will disappear. It cannot be saved for future use.

While within the shrine, if any one of your exit routes does not meet the safe haven requirements, it will become inactive. To stay within the shrine, you must have at least one exit available at all times. If you would be stuck in the shrine due to all exits becoming unavialable, you will be automatically be ejected at the last remaining moment before such an occasion would occur. The same rules apply to anyone with you.

Synergy detected: Draconic BondYou are bonded with a dragon, and therefore have automatically granted one slot on your approved list to your companion. Your companion cannot be removed from your approved list.

Synergy detected: Interdimensional Storage RippleSkill upgrade in progress…

Congratulations! You may now access the contents of your Interdimensional Storage Ripple from within your shrine!

Logan read it slowly and consulted with Mariv on the details, but ultimately, it sounded like all his worries about keeping Nugget safe and hidden without having to be separated were solved. Or at least, almost solved.

He tapped deeper into the skill’s details and saw a dashboard, which included a list of current approved individuals. It was just Nugget for now.

A green light showed he currently met the conditions of a safe haven, and he had 1 nodal point available, but he didn’t have an option to assign the point to anywhere. That meant he would only ever have a temporary portal for Nugget to enter through, and it would close whenever Logan exited it, which would therefore also kick Nugget out of the space.

He didn’t care, though. This was a step in the right direction. He grabbed Nugget into an embrace, squishing the dragon’s face to his cheeks as he spun. “No more hiding out at the inn, Nugget.”

Nugget nuzzled Logan, and joy came over the bond.

Wait. Would it be more cramped there than here?

There was only way to find out.

Shrine of the Living Path!

Logan’s storage ripple shimmered at the corner of his vision. It became somewhat opaque, like translucent quicksilver, until it enlarged and appeared in front of him.

Despite his excitement, he now wondered how safe this actually was.

Before he could weigh the risks of entering this portal, Nugget chirped and jumped in. The dragon’s head, chest, and wings seemed to warp as if a black hole were swallowing him.

“Wait!” Logan reached for the dragon, but caught only his tail. With his extra-grippy Vicelock grip, he tried to tug Nugget back, but he lurched forward. As Nugget’s tail elongated too, so did Logan’s arms. He felt a little loopy, as if he had just been given some laughing gas, and then everything turned blindingly bright.

The next moment, Logan somersaulted into a wide-open green pasture. Ambient light made everything appear bright as day.

“Whoa.”

Nugget trilled in agreement, then sprinted in circles around Logan.

Logan laughed and reached down to pluck a flower from the grass. The moment he did, it vaporized and re-materialized on the ground.

Mariv: Did you read the skill book yet?

Logan startled at the message. He hadn’t actually considered whether his chat system would work here.

Logan: I did. I’m in it now. How long has it been? Does this distort time like traveling through the Ruined Wilds does?

Mariv: Oh goodness, no. The Ruined Wilds is a place completely untamed by the System. It is governed by all kinds of clashing rules. This is a skill under direct governance of the System. I’ve been able to learn more about its properties since you first mentioned seeing the casket box at the temple. Much like your storage ripple, this is an interdimensional utility skill. The appearance of the interior can be upgraded over time, but that’s a matter for another day.

Logan: I just tried picking up a flower, but it doesn’t look like I can bring stuff from inside here with me when I leave. It’s all just an illusion or something.

Mariv: When it comes to the shrine and anything that is part of it, you are correct when you say you cannot bring it with you when you leave. However, it is not an illusion. These places truly exist, but not in our dimension. That is how interdimensional storage places work.

That opened a ton of questions for Logan. Were there infinite storage spaces? Was there a real-life counterpart to his Void ripple somewhere in the world? Did any of these spaces somehow connect back to Earth? He had a brief idea of some floating food items from Feast’s banquet table drifting past the Hubble space telescope.

Mariv: Try to summon something from your inventory to you and you will see that it comes when you command. Your utility skills ought to work together.

Logan tried, and it worked just as Mariv said it would. He even envisioned his quest board, and it popped into view right in front of him. It was vaguely stylized like the physical quest boards outside the Adventurer’s Guild.

As he skimmed over the contents pinned to the corkboard, he let out a low whistle. Nugget flapped his wings to hover in front of it and let out a small peep. “Yeah,” Logan agreed. “That’s a lot of quests.”

In the Adventurer’s Guild quest section, one popped up right as Logan stared at it. Logan squinted as he skimmed the details.

He and Nugget shared a glance.

“Are you thinking what I’m thinking?”

Nugget trilled and flew in a circle around Logan’s head.

Quest accepted!
