“Are you alright?” Samantha asked him, rushing over and going to grab his hand. Her eyes went wide as she realized his entire right arm was missing from the shoulder down, and she gasped.

“I’d say he’s looking all-left,” Moxy laughed before he could say anything. Ignoring Samantha's glare, Moxy crouched down and began poking at the greater demon’s skin. “Damn, this thing could shrug off my air blasts like it was nothing. Never fought something quite like it before.”

“I told you greater demons were nasty,” Asher said, dismissing the notification that Sever the Thread had jumped up yet another level, all the way up to level 29 now. Giving Samantha a reassuring smile, he placed Crescent back into his rift so he could pull her into a quick, one-armed hug without accidentally stabbing her. “And I’m fine. The greater demon dragged me over to the lich itself, where it was actually meeting with another member of The Council of Death. It’s a long story, but… I killed one of them.”

His three teammates all froze in what they were doing, their jaws dropping as they stared at him in shock. Rulfar nearly slipped on a puddle of demon-blood, barely catching himself as he began slowly returning to his regular human form. Based on the sounds of bones popping and shifting, Asher could only assume doing so at least partially healed his caved-in ribcage on its own.

“You actually killed one?” Rulfar asked, the large man looking fatigued for perhaps the first time Asher had ever seen him. “What happened?”

“I’ll tell you guys everything in a bit, but we’re on a time crunch,” Asher explained, glancing up at the ceiling as he half expected the entire base to start shaking like Loratta’s compound did when it was collapsing. “Zygrul ran off to another realm, and it said it was going to pull this entire base along with it! We only have a few minutes!”

“Zygrul?” Moxy asked, raising an eyebrow as she stopped prodding at the greater demon. “You’re on a first-name basis with the liches now, huh?”

“Explanation later, looting now!” Asher snapped. “We have to find a lead on where we can find another member from the Council before this place is taken from us!”

“Okay… Should we split up?” Samantha asked, not looking at all like she was a fan of the idea despite being the one to propose it.

“There might still be demons about… And we still haven’t seen heads or tails of the second of the two greater demons that were summoned either, so we need to be careful. Rulfar and Moxy, you guys take that side of the hallway, me and Samantha will take the other,” Asher ordered, making a quick decision as he gestured where he wanted them to go. “Meet back here in two minutes, don’t be late!”

With that, the four of them ran into action, rushing out of the slaughter room and hunting for anything that might be of use for them to continue their hunt. After shoving the greater demon corpse into his Personal Rift, Asher briefly contemplated trying to run around and shove as many of the lesser demon corpses and imps into his rift as he physically could as well, but they just didn’t have the time. Not to mention the vast majority of the corpses were in horrible condition at this point, his three teammates not exactly having gone for ‘clean kills’ on any of the demons. Limbs had been lopped off, imps had exploded, and there was blood absolutely everywhere. Deciding they’d just have to be satisfied with their one massive payday from the greater demon, Asher left the rest of the carnage behind them, leading Samantha straight to the room that Zygrul had recommended he check out.

Despite seeming like it was loosely on their side, Asher didn’t trust the lich one bit, and for obvious reasons. Zygrul had actually admitted to being the one responsible for the destruction of Horntho Village and the other border villages along the Lavash Forest. That said, there was no arguing the fact that Hylm had only been taken out so easily thanks to Zygrul setting him up. Zygrul had distracted Hylm, drew her attention away from Asher, and even broke him out of whatever freaky Love enchantment the lich had placed on him. Zygrul clearly wasn’t aligned with The Council of Death any longer, and for that reason, Asher was willing to trust the lich at least this much.

Throwing open the door for the indicated room, Asher paused at the doorway, blinking at what was waiting for him within.

The room was completely empty, other than a large desk situated against the far wall. There were thirteen hand-made journals of varying sizes neatly arranged atop the desk. Some appeared to be no thicker than a couple of pages, while others were nearly the size of a small book. Asher sucked in an excited breath as he stepped into the room, his eyes scanning over the journals, praying Zygrul had left them what he thought the lich had.

Sure enough, printed directly on the top of the seventh journal in large, easy to read lettering, was a name he recognized. Hylm.

“It’s information on all the members of the Council,” he whispered, barely even able to believe their luck. “Zygrul must have been planning this betrayal for some time!”

“The lich that summoned you to this world and burned down Horntho is helping us now?” Samantha asked, looking equal parts confused and angry. “Why?!”

“I don’t know, but we’re up against nearly a dozen ancient foes with who knows how much power. We need to take whatever help we can get,” Asher said, grabbing the first of the journals. He went to Incorporate it by reflex, before pausing at the last moment. According to Identify, there was nothing magical about the journal whatsoever. However, Secret Seeker drew his eye to the strange, shimmering nature of the cover, and Asher frowned.

“I think there’s gemstone dust or something pressed into this,” he muttered, turning the journal this way and that, watching how the light reflected off it. “Can people enchant stuff like that?”

“You’d know more than I would,” Samantha shrugged. “Maybe just toss it in your rift for now? If it’s cursed or trapped in some way, you probably shouldn’t pull the information into your head with Incorporate.”

“That’s a good point,” he said, doing just that. The last thing he needed was to stumble directly into a trap left for him by Zygrul. Despite seeming as though it focused its studies on the realms and summoning, there was no guarantee Zygrul didn’t dabble in mind-magic as well.

Once all thirteen journals were safely stored within his rift, Asher turned around, a shiver going up his spine at the fact that everything still appeared to be completely normal. The ceiling wasn’t shaking, the floors weren’t flashing with lights, and only the sounds of their own movements and breathing could be heard. There wasn’t the slightest indication that what Zygrul said about taking its base with it would come to pass, and that terrified Asher more than anything.

“Let’s meet back up with the others,” he decided, the memory of Zygrul’s black, flaming eyes burning in the front of his mind as he recalled the sensation of his energy being erased right out of him. “I don’t want to push our luck, and I have a really bad feeling about where Zygrul is planning to take this base.”

With a nod, Samantha joined him as they raced back to the start of the hallway. With every door they passed, Asher cursed their lack of time, curious beyond belief as to what secrets this massive mountain base held. Still, he wasn’t willing to risk getting snatched up by the lich and brought to its true domain, so he shoved the regrets down and focused solely on the plan. Thankfully, Moxy and Rulfar were waiting for them right where he’d told them to, meaning he wouldn’t have to choose between trying to hunt them down or escaping with his and Samantha’s own lives. Even more exciting was that while Rulfar appeared to be holding one of the random suits of armor that had been lining the hallways closer to the entrance, Moxy gingerly held what appeared to be a darker-than-black dagger that reminded Asher of the flames burning within Zygrul’s skull.

“I don’t like this thing one bit!” Moxy said, looking more nervous than Asher had ever seen before as she held the dagger out and away from her body by the handle like she was afraid it would bite her. “It feels… wrong!”

“Let me see,” Asher said, snatching the dagger from her and staring at it. To his shock, Identify didn’t detect the dagger as an actual object, despite the fact that he was holding it in his very hands. He’d had a few instances now of Identify failing to register people, such as with Draken or the liches, but it had never failed on an object before now. Even the liches’ phylacteries had returned a handful of question marks rather than evade the skill entirely.

The hilt of the dagger felt almost cold, in a way, but that wasn’t exactly correct. It wasn’t cold so much as it was absorbing the surrounding heat and even light. Asher could practically feel the metal hilt of the weapon pulling the heat from his fingers, and he nearly dropped the dagger entirely when he finally realized heat wasn’t the only thing it was pulling from him.

The dagger was eating away at his very own tritessence.

“Alright, that’s definitely a good find. I think,” Asher said, shoving the dagger into his rift before he could change his mind. He wasn’t a huge fan of storing the mysterious weapon inside his skill, but he couldn’t exactly keep it on his person if it was constantly draining his energy. Thankfully, storing the dagger in his Personal Rift appeared to negate the drain for whatever reason, and Asher shook out his hand as he tried to get feeling back into it. He tossed Rulfar's newfound armor into his rift as well, figuring it might be worth something despite Identify informing him it was nothing but an old set of corroded, iron armor. He cast another look of regret around at the silent base that was still largely untouched, but there just wasn't any time. If it were only his own life on the line he might be willing to risk it, but not when he had his full team with him. “Everyone into the rift!”

With three nods, each of his teammates allowed themselves to be pulled into his rift one after another. Samantha saved herself for last, giving him a quick kiss and narrowing her eyes at him. “No sticking around, okay? You need to heal!”

“I won’t, I promise,” Asher said, chuckling at how well she knew him. In fact, even as he was pulling his teammates into his rift he was debating exactly how long it would take him to run back to one of the rooms they’d passed and just poke his head in.

With his promise secured, Samantha entered his rift as well, and Asher gave the surrounding base one more quick scan, just in case Secret Seeker had missed anything. He didn’t think Zygrul was bluffing, seeing as fleeing to another realm would probably be the only actual way to escape from the other members of the Council. But he was at least a little suspicious about Zygrul’s claim that it was going somewhere Asher wouldn’t be able to follow. He’d managed to survive for a good couple of hours within the wild realm all on his own, and his team had managed to work together and take down a creature from the abyss without sustaining any serious injuries.

Crouching down, Asher laid a hand on the cool marble floor of the secret base, imbuing a sliver of himself into it as he placed his last open Eternal Mark down. Now that Recall had evolved to the second tier, he was capable of following his marks even to entirely different realms. With that addition to the base, wherever Zygrul was planning to run off to, Asher would be able to find it.

“This isn’t over,” he muttered, standing back up and throwing one final look back toward the study he’d spoken to Zygrul in. Even now, his missing fingers tingled as they recalled the sensation of gripping his dagger as he cut the head off of one of the liches. He didn’t have any sort of concrete proof of what he’d accomplished beyond Sever the Thread gaining three levels instantly and Zygrul's own admittance, but at least he knew the deed was done. Focusing on his mark back in the royal palace, Asher felt the world shift around him as he activated Recall, calling out one final challenge to the absent Zygrul.

“One down… thirteen to go.”
