After Hannah's proclamation that the void was on Earth's doorstep, Luke reacted as if on instinct. Ever since seeing her, he'd felt as if something was wrong, but it wasn't until her words of warning that he realized that sensation hadn't been entirely centered on her. So, with her head in his lap, Luke closed his eyes and focused outward and inward, drawing himself into a deep state of meditation. The weave presented itself to him in stark clarity, but he ignored it and went through, or perhaps beyond, reaching into The Greater System Weave. If the void was on the attack, it was here he would find it. Earth's system was part of the greater one, yes, but it also acted as a sort of shield or buffer in the way that it wrapped itself around the newly integrated system. Coming to a metaphysical stop with his senses at the edge of everything, Luke found himself surprised that Hannah had been right.

A part of him, the main part, to be frank, had dismissed her proclamation as a sort of waking nightmare borne of trauma from whatever horrors she must have experienced in the destiny dungeon, as the Singers named them. That wasn't it. It wasn't the first time the void being wasn't there, but never before had there been something else coming out of the void. Excitement, hunger, fear, and confusion radiated from Smudge in his secure spot in the Stable as Luke looked out across the edge of The Greater System and saw three creatures attempting to push through, gnawing at the end seam, fraying the threads, and in some spots, to his horror, succeeding.

"No!" Luke shouted, willing himself forward.

The beasts took no notice of him, and they didn't let up their relentless assault for even a second. Looking at them, Luke couldn't help but be reminded of the creature they had escaped so long ago, that Hannah had made part of herself. The Deep Dweller. These were more solid than Smudge, not just shapes of nothing, but they were still dark as night, monstrous apparitions of gnashing teeth in far too many mouths, tentacles with razor barbs, bodies in twisting shapes that defied logic and made him dizzy, and eyes. So many eyes. In all the colors of the rainbow. They all stared at the weave in hunger. No, hunger was too weak a word. Need. Desire. The overwhelming imperative to destroy and exterminate. No wonder they didn't care about Luke, not when they had a chance to feed on The Greater System itself.

This had to be the spot where Hannah re-entered. Had she been fleeing from these as she made her way back? Her path through the void must have lured these things out, or perhaps even summoned them in some way. Left unattended, they would in time consume everything. As Luke approached, the creatures - calling them monsters wouldn't have been accurate, not in the sense of what he was used to - a presence appeared from within The Greater System Weave itself, a blur of intent and dizzying power. Everything lurched. Threads snapped and rearranged themselves as the weave turned on itself, moving and twisting. It all happened in the span of a second or less, but when it finished, the presence was gone, and things had turned even more dire. The things coming out of the void were no longer gnawing on The Greater System Weave. Things had shifted around them, and their sharp teeth now tore into Earth's weave as they worked their way into Luke's own system.

"What?" he blurted out, despite not having a mouth.

From one moment to the next, the incursion had gone from horror-inducing to, well, much worse. Whoever or whatever that presence was, it had shifted The Greater System Weave itself away from the void in this precise spot, and moved Earth's system weave to take its place. No longer snug and protected, Luke now found his home bordering the void itself, and his home was under attack. Whether this was a permanent change or not wouldn't matter in a couple of minutes, because Earth would no longer exist, not with the rate these things were tearing into it. While the end seam of The Greater System Weave withstood their attention to some extent, Earth's sure as hell didn't, and the void creatures slobbered all over the weave as they made their way through it. Each thread represented a person, a relationship, or even an idea. Who could tell the extent of the damage these things were doing? It needed to stop, and it needed to stop right this second. Since no one else was coming, Luke would have to be the one to do it.

As he approached, he realized something else. This was the exact spot he crossed through when he escaped the void being's attention after rescuing Smudge. Luke's repair of the weave was still there, except now it was broken by these beings. The problem with that realization was that when he fixed the hole, it had been in The Greater System Weave, not his own. That, too, had shifted. There were undercurrents here, actions taken and things rearranged, Luke couldn't grasp, and that annoyed him. Carrying that annoyance forward, he attacked the void creatures with everything he could muster, focusing on the one who had almost made its way into the weave proper.

Not having a corporeal form was nothing new. Fighting in that state, that of a drifting consciousness, was. While using threads and other skills proved very much possible, everything had a floating quality to it, like when trying to run in a dream. The void creatures were different. Their forms held physical weight, as did their attacks. Telling himself that it didn't matter, Luke lashed out with a thread to probe at the closest one. The moment it connected, he sensed vast quantities of void mana, and in that moment, the creature noticed him. Hundreds of eyes turned to stare at him, and the gnashing ceased, the corners of all those mouths turning up into a too-intelligent grin. Void mana exploded outward from the creature, pushing through the thread as the thing lashed out with tentacles.

Cutting the thread to avoid being pumped full of void mana, Luke reached out again, this time with eight threads all burning with infernal mana. With Weaver's Whip activated, he struck at the ever-reaching tendrils with the intent to cut them off before going in to blind all those staring, eager eyes. The threads sliced through. No, they didn't. They went right through, not hurting the void creature at all. All those mouths smiled wider, then wider still as they opened to consume him. The other two creatures had noticed too and were pushing in like eager puppies wanting a taste. Luke used Weavestep to create some distance between himself and those three, but soon wished he hadn't. Now that they were honed in on him, the creatures squeezed into the weave proper, leaving the void behind. The word catastrophe ran through Luke's head, but his first instinct was still to flee. They had come into his home, like burglars come to take everything from him.

Conventional attacks and conventional ideas hold no power here. Focus, young Lifeweaver.

It was that presence again, appearing and then disappearing. Or had it been there all along, obscuring itself as it watched Luke fumble and fail? Gritting his teeth, Luke took the message to heart. If someone comes into your house, you don't hide under the bed. You grab a bat and meet them on the stairs, ready to start swinging. Focus, the presence had said. That was something he knew well. Turning to face the three horrific opponents, Luke did the mind equivalent of raising a hand, then leaned forward in the metaphysical realm of the weave, focusing for all he was worth. But focus would not be enough here. No, he needed more. Luke needed his will, and there was plenty of that to go around. To do this, he needed to understand these creatures, and for that, he needed information. Since the weave itself was now the field of battle, system rules should apply, shouldn't they?

Hunger Schrlyatx. Level ?

Hunger Tjiensxi. Level ?

Hunger Ooeinyula. Level ?

Indeed, they did. The rules applied, and within the weave itself, the Lifeweaver is in his element. Directing the full force of his willpower, Luke focused on the three advancing lifeforms, if they could be said to contain life, and sent a single idea through the weave.

No.

They stopped, eyes narrowing.

You are not welcome here.

Two of them shuddered, but the one who had almost consumed him stirred and advanced again. Luke, not about to give up and surrender the weave, pushed back against the onslaught of will coming from the thing. It was a wild thing, its willpower, undirected and scattered, but oh so powerful. If all of it had been directed at Luke, he wasn't sure he would've been able to withstand it. Actually, he was sure he wouldn't. Now, though, it hungered for the weave, and that hunger mingled with an unbridled hate of itself, its kin, and even the concept of life. All those ideas made for a powerful opponent, but not one with the focus necessary to insist upon itself. Luke, on the other hand, wanted nothing more than for these three to get the hell out of his system. Nothing else mattered. Nothing else existed. Mana coursed through his channels and meta-heart, almost as if it was begging to be used, but he ignored it all. This fight, as the presence informed him, would not be won through the use of mana, but the mind and the will.

Leave.

He sent again, and this time it was like a raging, unstoppable torrent. It slammed into the three, and rather than move on their own volition, it was as if they were pushed back by the wave of sheer will and intent coming from Luke. It didn't hurt them - he wasn't sure anything could hurt them - but it didn't need to. All he wanted was for them to be gone, and his will made it so, pushing all three out of the weave.

But the fight wasn't over yet. The void creatures turned back toward Luke, their eyes blazing with hate. Again, they charged.
