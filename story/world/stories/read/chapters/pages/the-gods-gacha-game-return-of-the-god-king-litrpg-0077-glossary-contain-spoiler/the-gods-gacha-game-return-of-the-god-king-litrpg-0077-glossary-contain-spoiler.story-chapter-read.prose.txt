Divine Will

The game of the gods created by the God-King.

It is a divine game where gods competed for supremacy. Gods summon divine warriors to be their soldiers and battle other divine warriors under different gods. They do this to raise through ranks among gods as well as for entertainment.

Fantasia

The divine city of Imagination inside Istellia’s divine kingdom.

Fantasia is divided into several districts based on rank, ranging from District A to District F. Each district offers resources, training grounds, and places of respite, but the quality and variety of features improve significantly as you move toward District A.

Scenario

A scenario is a divine trial generated by the Divine Will, designed as both entertainment for the gods and a proving ground for the divine warriors chosen by them. Each scenario is a self-contained world or event with its own objectives, environment, and threats.

While it appears like a “mission” or “dungeon” to the players, in truth, it’s a fragment of reality woven by divine energy—sometimes recreations of past worlds, sometimes artificial realms built to test faith, strength, and imagination.

Scenarios are ranked by difficulty (E to SSS+) and completing them grants soul coins, skills, stats, and items as rewards. They often contain extra conditions, granting additional rewards for uncovering secrets, aiding hidden targets, or influencing the outcome beyond mere survival.

Signature Skill

A signature skill is a unique ability that manifests when a divine warrior’s class, experience, and willpower reach a certain threshold. Unlike normal skills, it reflects the user’s individual nature and growth, often evolving from their existing abilities. It’s their personalized trump card—a skill no one else can replicate, tied to their soul and identity.
