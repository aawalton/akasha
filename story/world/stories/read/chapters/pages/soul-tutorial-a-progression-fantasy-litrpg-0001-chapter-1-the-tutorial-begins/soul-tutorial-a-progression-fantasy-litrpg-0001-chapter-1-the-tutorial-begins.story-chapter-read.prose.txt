Waves of heat brushed past Luke’s face. Bruises and cuts covered him. With the smoke present in the air, he had a hard time catching his breath. Everything was happening too fast for him to process.

He raised an arm to guard his face from the heat. I need to find Mom.

He watched as people vanished, flying off somewhere as they became light. Heat continued to crash into him as cars smashed into each other. His ears rang, vision turned white.

He ran into a damaged building with his chest heaving. Luke slowly brought himself over to a chair and slouched on it, gripping his heart with his hands.

“What the hell is happening?” Luke slurred.

It’s only been a few hours…How many people have disappeared for all of this to happen!?

Tears welled up in his eyes, and he tried to keep himself from sobbing. He wiped his tears with his fingers. I don’t want to die.

I don’t want to be left alone. He tightened his fists. Curled up on the chair, he sat in silence. He listened to the cacophony of screams, explosions, and the blazing flames of the outside world.

Every explosion made Luke twitch in fright; every scream Luke heard, he tried to comfort himself.

I hope Mom is okay. Why did I have to go to the park with my friends? Tears flowed down his cheeks. He slammed his fist on the table.

“It’s going to be okay. I’ll be alright,” Luke muttered through clenched teeth.

A sudden white glow steadily consumed his body. Luke looked at himself in fright.

“I don’t want to die!” Luke shouted while jumping out of his seat. He sprinted toward the door. Before he could open it and make his way outside, he felt his world spin as the white glow around his body brightened. Feeling as if his body was being broken down, compressing itself into a single needle that pierced through nothingness.

The moment Luke opened his eyes, he looked down at himself. The light is gone.

Feeling quite lightheaded, Luke stumbled to his feet. Hurrying to survey his surroundings, he froze at what he saw. It wasn’t any place he had seen before, but a floating island in the middle of nowhere, the surroundings shrouded in darkness. Even though it looked like only darkness was present, he could see everything so clearly.

Luke cautiously took a few steps forward on the barren ground. His eyes scanned the island, landing on a pedestal.

He made his way to the pedestal. Its black metallic look intrigued him. The pillar reached slightly above his waist, with a flat top tilted at an angle.

Luke observed the pillar as the whole situation had him bewildered. He knew that not dying instantly when he arrived here meant that this place was at least somewhat safe. The quiet island also seemed better than the situation he was in back on Earth. He hoped.

With wonder, Luke stretched his arm out to touch the surface of the pillar, stopping just before his fingers grazed the surface.

He knew that something could happen if he touched it. Luke waited longer before proceeding with it and seized the opportunity to check out the place he was in.

Luke rummaged around to find a few shabby, thick branches that he could grip in his hands and that also had a slight weight to them. A lot of them came in various shapes; there were also small pebbles and rocks.A few dead bushes clung to the outskirts of the island, with no other signs of life. The ground was tinted orange, feeling like a cool autumn season to him.

Surprisingly, it smelled quite fresh, with the scent of lavender in the air. Luke walked around the island a bit more; it wasn’t too large nor too small. He couldn’t exactly measure the island, but estimated it to be around 60-65 meters across.

Luke started walking back to the pillar, standing directly in front with slight hesitation. He extended his non-dominant left arm, just in case, resting it upon the pillar. It glued his left hand to the surface. Luke panicked as he desperately tried to remove his hand.

“Why are bad things always happening to me?” Luke yelled.

He shoved his feet into the ground and tried to pull his hand off with all his might. Seconds passed by, then minutes. Luke felt his doom approaching. Slowly, tears streamed down his cheeks.

Luke tried one more time to pull his hand off and tumbled backward. His entire arm prickled with numbness. He clutched his arm to his chest, noticing its intensifying redness.

Suddenly, a white dot in the air no bigger than a baseball appeared, shimmering with intricate trimmings of gold lines that seemed like cracks as it mysteriously circulated throughout the object. Slowly, as if alive, thumping occasionally, it grew larger to become a thin veil with fragmented corners, the gold lines moving to become barriers of the veil. Black text filled the veil.

[SYSTEM INTEGRATION COMPLETE] [SOUL CONNECTION ESTABLISHED]

Luke gawked at the floating text. His fingers twitched as he instinctively reached out, only for his hand to pass right through.

More words appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Name: Luke Holloway

Points: 0

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Attributes]

Physique: 0

Constitution: 0

Agility: 0

Intellect: 0

Sensory: 0

Soul Resonance: 0

His mind spun. Attribute? Like in a game? What did Soul Resonance even mean? Luke rubbed his chin in thought.

Then another veil materialized.

[FLOOR 0]

[FLOOR 1 — LOCKED]

[SUMMON]

So I have to fight. Luke turned deathly pale. Shaking his head, Luke went back to scanning the island.

Luke remembered seeing quite a few branches as thick as his arm before interacting with the pedestal. He started making his way back to the branches, carefully selecting a sturdy one. Staring back at the pedestal, Luke's body shuddered, overwhelmed by an intense itching sensation.

Is this really the best course of action? Is this the only way I can proceed? He quickly decided to just test things out for now. Standing once more in front of the pedestal, his gut told him he shouldn’t proceed; he had to at least try to make some progress if he was stuck there for the moment.

Placing his hand back on the pedestal, hoping it didn’t get stuck again, he flinched as the veil materialized again. Floor 0. He mentally accepted it. Then everything went silent.

He saw small particles being condensed a few meters in front of the pedestal. Something green grew as the energy around the surrounding area appeared to fluctuate.

Luke felt as if he were suffocating, grasping for air, and recognized the lightheadedness. Fear rose to eat away at him. He grew pale; sweat slowly dripped down around his forehead.

Then it all stopped at once. A green blob, short in stature and narrow in width, stood resting on the ground. The first monster was born on his island.

Awe and fear washed over Luke. His legs trembled and his breath caught in his throat as he wondered what the blob could be. His heart pounded a little faster. In the empty void it rang out like drums. His mind couldn’t make sense of what happened. He was thinking of anything he had learned in school or from books, but it made little sense.

Luke was stiff, and his shoulders wouldn’t budge. Could it be slime or a gooey substance? He tried to scout out the unknown being by hiding behind the pedestal. He noticed a core the size of a marble within the slime.

Nothing seemed to have happened after a short period. Luke sneaked closer to the substance, but he had no cover to hide behind. Luke, being about a couple of meters from the substance, jolted. The slime had jumped, which frightened Luke.

Luke watched as the slime approached him, his chest heaving constantly, his body burning, arms stiff, finding it hard to catch his breath. It launched itself towards his body, closing the distance fast.

Luke’s movement was quite sluggish in response to the unexpected attack. Luke dodged to the left, throwing his entire body to get away from that thing. Unfortunately, he also dropped the branch—it ended up a meter away from him.

Luke crashed knee-first into the ground beside him. The slime landed on the ground in the spot Luke had been in before. It was about to launch itself at him again as Luke tried his best to escape.

He prepared his legs as he pushed with all his force. He used his hands to drag himself, almost in a crawling position, jumping to a random location from his unstable stance.

That time, he went headfirst into the dirt. He hit his nose, a swift signal of pain making him stop whatever he was going to do next. This opening left the slime with enough time to attack him in his defenseless position, while the dirt on his face blocked his vision. He attempted to wipe the dirt from his eyes, but startled, he lacked the speed to do so.

The slime landed on Luke’s back. It was way heavier than he had thought. The air left his lungs; a sudden pain enveloped Luke’s back. Screams escaped his mouth. Tears ran down his face. His back sizzled from the acidic substance. Luke never knew the slime would ooze out acid. His heart rate quickened; Luke’s thoughts were a mess. The scorching pain on his back caused him to feel weak. But there was one thing Luke thought throughout this whole thing. “I don’t want to die yet”. Those words slipped from Luke’s mouth along with his screams.

Luke gasped for air as he dug his hands into the dirt in front of him. He tried to drag his body away from the slime on top of him, but it was all meaningless.

Luke finally turned his body, with the slime on top of him. The slime would corrode him from the outside and burn his organs the longer he took to escape. He knew if that happened, he’d be as good as dead. Finally, having the strength to do so. Luke flipped the slime off himself. The slime landed, already setting up to pounce back on him again. But surprisingly, the slime didn’t stick to him.

With his vision blocked, Luke took as many steps as possible to escape from the slime. His body was already hard to control as he was going through unbearable pain. Blood dripped from his back. The only smell that entered Luke’s nose was the smell of his burnt flesh.

Finally, getting some distance away, he almost fell to the ground as the adrenaline slowly drained from his body. The battle wasn’t over yet. However, he bruised his knee when jumping to the left.

Tears fell from his face. How is this any better than what’s going on back on Earth? Why do I have to go through this entire ordeal?

He gasped for air. He held his arms up. The slime was only a few meters away from him, and Luke was only a few meters from the edge of the island. Luke stared into the void as the pain in his back didn’t help him calm down.

After steadying his breath, he looked around the island and saw some branches on the ground. Luke moved towards one of them. Each step Luke took cast a burden on his body. With hurried steps, as the slime was closing in, he grabbed the branch and swung it a few times. It was quite sturdy, but it wasn’t very long.

Luke’s new weapon sent the slime back—it bounced its way over as it stood a few steps away from Luke. It shrank even further, preparing for its pounce. But the second time around, Luke was ready, crouching with a burned back and swollen eyes. The slime lunged, but Luke sidestepped, realizing it wasn’t as fast as before. Luke swung his branch down like a sword, his form terrible, his right foot ahead of his left. He almost fell, but he still landed a hit. It bounced off, but he used that to his advantage as he swung again, grazing its membrane.

Small amounts of a green substance oozed out of the impact. Joy stretched across Luke’s face until some of the ooze sprayed out and landed on Luke’s chest. He tried to hold his scream in and used the anger he currently had to swing harder and harder at the slime. Time passed as Luke was still beating away at the slime.

His wounds worsened. Some parts of his body didn’t even listen to him anymore. The tiredness and pain settled into his body. Landing one more hit on the slime nearly ended its life, but in its dying effort, it tried to get on Luke, which partly worked. With Luke’s last bit of strength, he swung down with his arm with the nearly destroyed branch, ending the slimy life; the core cracked. It dissolved into the ground.

Luke stood, his body a horrible mess. Parts of his arms and legs had dissolved down to the muscle. Some areas of his body were deformed. His branch was absolutely obliterated. Luke collapsed to the ground unconscious, succumbing to his injuries.
