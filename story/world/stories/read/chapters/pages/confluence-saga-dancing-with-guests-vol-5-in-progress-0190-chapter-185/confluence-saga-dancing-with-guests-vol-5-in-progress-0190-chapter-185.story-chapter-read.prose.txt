Jean,

Did you have to start rambling in front of my former protege? You could have sent me a letter, a message, reached out one of several ways. Instead you let students see you out of sorts, you asked a visitor to bring me your words.

I understand your worries about how it will seem, about what people will think of you. But that doesn't change what you did. Doesn't change what you said.

And to blame me? To bring up our past? How long has it been since you took a break? Since you let yourself be the Jean I knew? How long has it been since I did. We're both turning into cranky old men. Especially me.

You're going to laugh about this someday, we both know you will. Don't take too long before you do.

Cara stared at the door after Anya and Seb left. She wanted to look at Gwen, to see her reaction to their parting words. She didn't want to look at Gwen, she was afraid of what she might see.

"Cara?" Gwen whispered. "Did they both just…"

Cara turned to look at her out of the corner of her eye. Gwen's face nearly as red as her hair, her eyes wide enough their amethyst glow was nearly lost.

"I think so? It sounded like they expect we are going to-"

"Cara!"

Cara could actually feel her hair reacting to their unsaid words. Normally she merely felt the emotions and was aware of what that implied for her hair. She could keep focused and minimize the manifestation of her feelings. At this moment she couldn't, her scalp felt warmer than her cheeks, she knew it had to be as crimson as Gwen's.

"We- We said we would wait until the geas was no longer in the way. That we would…"

"Is that what you want?" Gwen asked.

"I want to know, I want to be happy, I want us both to be happy."

"But what does that mean?"

Cara felt her thoughts racing. She wouldn't pretend she'd never thought about what this might mean, about what might happen. But it had always been a thought for the future, a way to let her thoughts wander. She'd had similar thoughts about others from time to time, she had no doubt Gwen had had such musings as well.

"Cara?" Gwen said. "What does that mean?"

"I…" she began before a memory came to her and she held out her hand. "Come?"

Gwen took her hand, her grip nearly tight enough to make Cara wince.

"Sorry," Gwen said before her fingers relaxed.

Cara led her to their bedroom in silence, not quite needing to pull Gwen along.

"Cara..?"

"I have an idea," she said as she looked through their things.

She turned back towards Gwen and held out a hairbrush. She'd been sure she'd find it, sure there was one buried somewhere in Gwen's clothes.

"Cara?"

"Do you remember how I used to comb your hair?"

She saw Gwen's hand move to her head, to the hair she kept in a loose ponytail so it would not get in the way.

"Yes?"

"I miss how it looked, how you looked. I miss…"

She trailed off as she saw Gwen's appearance flicker. She didn't change, her Husk was a perfect representation of her after all, but her clothing did. Cara set the brush down on the bed and followed suit.

"We hadn't fully Awakened," Gwen said. "We weren't seen as adults. We had time, we didn't have to train every hour of every day."

Cara sat on the edge of the bed as Gwen came to sit at her feet. Her hands moved without conscious thought, following a routine from a simpler time. Gwen tilted her head this way and that as Cara ran her fingers through her hair. She always started with her fingers, loosening any tangles and snarls, before moving on to brushing it out.

"You never said you enjoyed this," Gwen said. "I even remember you complaining about it more than once."

"I was young," Cara said as she switched to the brush. "You were having me brush it out every single day. We were spending hours on our own, hours we could have spent with others our age, talking to them, looking at them."

"Hours with Bern and Morgan? Hours with the various-"

"I know. I realize that now," Cara said.

"So you enjoyed it."

"I didn't dislike it," Cara said as the brush caught in a knot.

"Ow!"

"Sorry," she said as she worked the knot out. "I felt like it was a chore."

"But you missed it?"

"I did. After a month I realized we wouldn't have time. After a year I stopped thinking about it. It became part of the past."

"You never said anything," Gwen said.

"What was I supposed to say? That I missed brushing your hair? That I wanted to spend that time? We were training, and then we were Diving. Was I supposed to comb your Husk's hair while in the Depths? Ask you to shift to your True Self while we weren't safe?"

"I'd forgotten too. We had to be serious."

Cara smiled as she set the brush aside. There was one thing Gwen had never had to ask about, one thing that had always been up to her. Her fingers took over, parting and braiding Gwen's hair to her liking.

"Will you do this for my Husk?" Gwen asked.

Cara paused, her fingers hesitating as she considered the question.

"If you want? It might get tangled, get in the way when we train and spar."

"You could fix it? We could do this again?"

Cara felt her scalp start to heat again as she looked down at Gwen. She could do this again, they could spend time this way. They could be themselves and spend time together.

"I think I'd like that," Cara said.
