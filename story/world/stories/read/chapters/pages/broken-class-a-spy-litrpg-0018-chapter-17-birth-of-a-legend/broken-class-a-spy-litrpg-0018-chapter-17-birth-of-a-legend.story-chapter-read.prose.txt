Arthur had snatched up every monster within a 25-mile radius of the Narak mountain. The monsters he couldn’t capture, like the Stonejaw Hound with its overly ambitious nose had fled. Which was fair. He was terrifying now.

He’d also drained every bit of progress from grinding Grubtusks. It was time to test his skills on something new.

He sifted through the monsters stored in his ring and picked one at random. A bipedal beast with a goat-like head. Once it materialized on the ground, Arthur didn’t waste time.

He skipped the whole “stab-it-in-the-eye” routine. This time, he went straight for the throat.

The beast jerked awake with a gurgling snort, just in time to catch a second tusk to the neck. Blood sprayed. Its legs kicked out in reflex before going limp.

You’ve leveled up to 15.

Arthur grinned, harvested the core, and dumped the body back into the ring. One down.

What followed was a methodical, bloody science experiment. He summoned monster after monster from the ring. Some died fast, others took a few tries. Most he could kill with one clean move. Some, like the Stonevein Boa, required multiple attempts and a lot of swearing. Luckily, if a fight went sideways, Arthur either ran or relied on his glorious stench to repel them.

He was nearly finished with his daily monster-murder routine when a notification blinked into view:

---

[System Alert]

You have been invited to participate in the Great Hunt.

Leaderboard standings will be tracked for 30 days. Rewards will be distributed based on placement.

Accept / Decline

---

Arthur stared at it. "What the hell is the Great Hunt?"

He asked Drogan. “Master, got a minute? Should I accept this?”

“Are you a coward?” Drogan bellowed. “Of course you accept it! You win it!”

“That’s... not an explanation.”

Drogan groaned like a disappointed gym coach. “The System hosts events like this to encourage murder - uh, I mean, cultivation. Different names, different planets, same deal. It’s usually age-locked, location-locked, or Grade-limited. Kill monsters. Climb the leaderboard. Win absurdly rare loot you can’t buy anywhere. Like real artifacts, not the knockoffs peasants sell.”

Arthur opened his mouth to ask another question.

“Nope,” Drogan cut in. “I was a warrior, not a scholar. Accept the damn invite and stop talking.”

Arthur decided not to press Drogan for more answers, at least not yet. So, with a silent shrug, he accepted the invite.

---

[System Notification]

You are now a participant in the Great Hunt.

You may access your leaderboard status at any time via mental command. Good luck.

---

Arthur blinked, and a glowing leaderboard overlay appeared in his vision. It was empty. A timer counted down from 30 days.

He dismissed it and turned to Drogan. “Now what?”

A wicked grin filled his mind. “Now? We kill all the monsters in your ring.”

---

Meanwhile, far from the Narak mountains, the capital city was buzzing with ceremony.

It was the first full moon of the local year, the day the Hunt traditionally began. Noble heirs and their handpicked elite parties had gathered for the opening ritual. Most of them lounged in ornate clothes, sipping wine, waiting for the king’s speech to start.

They weren’t in a rush. The Great Hunt wasn’t a sprint. It was a month-long grind, and no one wanted to burn out on day one.

After a few minutes, the invitations arrived via System notification. They accepted with practiced ease.

Someone had already started.

Four kills.

Solo.

That alone shattered expectations. In most years, it took elite, well-coordinated parties half an hour to claim their first. A lone hunter logging four confirmed kills before the nobles had even finished their appetizers?

Impossible. Unthinkable. Unprecedented.

Whispers spread like wildfire through the banquet hall. Nobles leaned over golden goblets, guardians paused mid-toast, and faction leaders frowned at their glowing notifications.

One name sat at the top of the leaderboard: Arthur.

No house. No known affiliation. Just Arthur, who apparently didn’t get the memo that this was supposed to be a slow, strategic hunt.

Who was he? A rogue noble? A hidden prodigy? A glitch in the system?

None of the scions had ever heard the name, but now they couldn’t stop staring at it.

Four monsters. No backup.

Whoever Arthur was, he had just kicked off the Great Hunt with a war cry no one heard but everyone felt.

And thus began the legend...

Arthur the Monster Slayer.

The Ghost at the Top.

The One-Man Massacre.
