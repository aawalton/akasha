-Amelia the Enthralled-

Amelia Langton sat in the box seats of the Royal Opera House in Covent Garden. Ordinarily she would have been delighted to enjoy an evening listening to Tchaikovsky’s Eugene Onegin in such prime seating. The fact that she herself was no more than a puppet under the complete control of some type of demon practitioner made the occasion far less enjoyable, to say the least.

After the initial shock and terror of her circumstances as a glorified automaton wore off, she regrettably still had to deal with her nattering possessor. The entity had the disturbing tendency to speak to her as if she was simultaneously a close dear friend and a bug to be squashed at will.

“The arts of your land are both much improved and boorish. I find that the tales of tragedy your singers perform are far from a personal experience. Don’t you agree, Ames?”

Amelia gave her mental assent but informed him that the first performance is oftentimes a warmup for the true talent.

“Well, that is something to look forward to, I suppose. Other than the recent goblins and the occasional terrorist attack, it seems this land is infected with perpetual peace and a severe lack of personal suffering. Like you are all happy fatted calves unaware of your pending slaughter.”

Amelia complimented the pedagogy of her infinitely superior captor, mentally inflecting the sarcasm.

“Ha! At least you amuse me, Ames. I must say, possessing you has been pleasant. Normally I just get weeks of unending screams. Mental anguish with long bouts of denial.”

Amelia mostly had trouble dealing with her own voice mocking her.

A shadow approached Amelia from behind her seat before it whispered in her ear.

“A taken has found the abandoned apartment and retrieved the appendage left there. It will be personally transported back to you.”

“Excellent. I hadn’t held out hope of it ever being found.”

“One of the taken that were sent to the state known as Oklahoma has gone silent. We expected a check-in hours ago.”

The demon smiled as he thought for a second.

“Send two more.” A smile crept onto Amelia’s face. “No point in escalating too quickly.”

Amelia had a general idea of the demon’s proclivities now. It was vastly powerful but often chose to do things in the most difficult—or to him, entertaining—way. Amelia was aware that the demon willingly ignored the pedestal in the palace. Who knows what authority that would have granted them? Instead, he went to a party and slowly, through the night, ensorcelled the minds of every classed person in the room.

They now were the taken and answered exclusively to Amelia.

A new lilting voice came from the stage, hauntingly beautiful but powerful in its delivery. Amelia suddenly sat up straight and looked at the stage.

“Now what is this creature?” the demon asked Amelia with rapt attention.

She of course could not answer specifics directly, but he had firm control of her memories, so he knew of the rumors of a new talent in London that came from Russia.

Amelia’s hands finally bothered to flip through the program.

“Anastasia Sokolova,” she whispered.

They listened to a stunning performance as Anastasia crafted a prime performance. The demon didn’t shift or talk for over ten minutes. As the movement closed, she sat back in her chair as the demon contemplated.

“I will meet with this Anastasia,” she said as her arm lifted and twisted her hand in the air.

The body of Amelia was covered in darkness before resolving into red skin and a dark draping robe.

“But I shall do so as myself,” he smiled.

The demon walked to the back of the theatre to the green room and waited out the performance. As people saw him, he waved a hand and their eyes glazed over, ignoring his presence. He walked to the edge of the curtain and stood transfixed as the tale of a young woman rejected by an aristocrat unfolded. She married someone else, only to have that aristocrat finally fall in love with her and pursue the now-married woman. He stood still for over an hour waiting for the end of the performance.

“Ah, a universal story of love missed and ending in the despair of a foolish man. Wonderful.”

When the play ended, the performers lined up and bowed to a roaring crowd. The demon walked back to the staging rooms and found Anastasia’s name on one of them. He went inside and sat down to wait, striking a dramatic pose.

When the door finally opened to reveal the lovely Anastasia, she closed the door and moved to start changing. When she finally noticed the demon haunting her couch, she gasped.

“Forgive me for wanting to meet you, Anastasia, but a talent such as yours is appreciated across many worlds. I would be remiss if I didn’t get a chance to speak with you.” Anastasia’s eyes were wide, but she didn’t freak out as Amelia would have expected.

“You are one of the invaders, yes?” she managed to say with some heavy breathing.

“I prefer to call myself a wanderer. Maybe a mischief maker. I seek knowledge across the worlds, but have been stalled in my studies for some time. I cannot even recall the last play I attended.”

“I am honored you enjoyed our performance then,” she relaxed only slightly.

“Tell me, Anastasia. Have you been chosen? Your performance was one in which I felt a distinct interest. You weave your voice with such finesse.”

“I… I have some ability. Though I doubt I would pose any threat to you.”

“No, you pose no threat to me, but I find I may like a companion to keep me company while I search this world for its secrets. You might be able to assist me. I would of course reward you greatly. Would you mind if I made you an offer?”

Anastasia contemplated. “My people have been known to make deals with the devil,” she smiled shyly.

“Please, you may refer to me as Mephistus.”
