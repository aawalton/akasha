Pride’s declaration reverberated across the plateau, her unceasing aura surging in all directions like a violent gale. Ascalon stood firm and ready for the fight, but his friends struggled to stand their ground. It was more than clear—this Ravanna person was leagues above Ian, her mere presence a massive hurdle they had to overcome.

The tension in the air grew heavier as both parties sized each other up. Pride started to sway in place, her feet rhythmically skipping up and down. Ascalon quickly caught onto her movements, silently admiring the deft, polished footwork of his opponent.

But before he could analyze it further, Pride lowered her stance slightly, and then lunged forward in a burst of speed that sent ripples across the rocky ground. A sonic boom lagged behind her, immediately followed by another solid, resounding thud that echoed all around.

Ascalon had raised his shield by instinct, blocking a most powerful straight punch from Pride, his sole eye opening wide as he commended his enemy’s strength. The clash itself generated a shockwave that sent the rest of the group backwards—except for Lilithra, that is. In an act of quick-witted defiance, she spun her halberd and drove its blade into the ground, anchoring herself in order to avoid being blown away.

Lilithra’s knuckles turned white as she held tight onto her armament, her eyes narrowed due to the wind pressure. She gritted her teeth as she fought the relentless shockwave, trying in vain to take a step forward.

She was no stranger to Ascalon’s fights—battles that seemed to take place in a realm far beyond her own, despite being right before her eyes. But even so, she soldiered on. Pride’s oppressive presence caused a reckless resolve to spur from deep within her.

Even when her instincts screamed at her to retreat, she was urged to push forward and challenge the impossible odds!

As Lilithra grappled with her conflicting feelings, Ascalon drew his right arm back, the mighty Exemplaris aligning to Pride’s torso with deliberate, methodic precision. In a single, thunderous motion, Ascalon struck forward with all his might, his great lance piercing through the air with unstoppable power.

But even with the little distance between them, his weapon missed its mark. Pride sidestepped with fluid, snappy movements, her legs bending low and her whole upper body hanging nearly parallel to the ground. A blinding flash erupted from her fists, now raised defensively to her chin.

As the light faded, two massive, machine-like gloves materialized over her hands—heavy, red gauntlets adorned with a mix of glowing blue runes and circuits that pulsed with an ethereal, almost otherworldly energy. The knuckles were especially big, with added spike-like protrusions likely meant to further increase the damage of the weapon.

Ascalon glanced at them with keen interest, admiring the masterful craftsmanship! But then, the backplate of the gauntlets started to shift and separate, with sharp, mechanical clicks and whirs emanating from the curious armament.

In the next moment, Pride snapped her body upward, her right fist rocketing toward the sky as the whirs and clicks increased in rhythm. The glowing blue runes and circuits shifted to crimson, accompanied by a release of what seemed to be piping-hot steam.

Ascalon recognized the signs and shifting colors as an unmistakable warning, and quickly raised his shield toward his upper torso, his vast experience allowing him to predict the attack’s trajectory to an extent.

“It’s no use…!” Pride’s voice slid through her clenched fists, a confident smirk spreading across her face.

In the same breath, Pride’s fist soared upward into a devastating uppercut, the many mechanisms and magical enchantments within her gauntlets activating at once. As the strike connected with Ascalon’s shield, a monumental force erupted in all directions, the whole plateau shaking to its very core as shockwaves flew through the air.

Lilithra did her very best to not be flown away, her eyes glued to the fight with a mix of awe and powerlessness.

Meanwhile, Ascalon’s body reverberated under the force of the impact, a mighty shockwave penetrating his shield and rippling through his broad torso. The pain spread across his chest, taking him momentarily by surprise—but he wasn’t surprised just by the sheer power behind the attack, but also by his feet lifting from the ground, as the devastating punch propelled his entire frame into the air!

Despite the painful hit and the dangerous situation, his face showed an excited smile as he thought he had finally found a worthy opponent—even if she had ended up being a vile member of a cult!

Still, something felt slightly off.

As he wondered that, the distinct whirs and clicks began to intensify again, causing him to focus on her once more. His eye darted to Pride’s left fist, which was glowing red and vibrating ever slightly. Then, without wasting a moment, she launched herself after him, soaring into the air and unleashing yet another uppercut with the gauntlet that had been powering up.

Ascalon managed to position his shield on the trajectory once again, blocking the blow with ease but, at the same time, being sent ever higher by the attack. The impact echoed loudly, the strength behind her punch making Ascalon flinch. A painful shockwave raced through his torso, as though the Aegis’ defenses had been disregarded for the most part.

He reckoned each of her strikes could fell a giant with little to no effort—such raw strength should not be taken lightly! The fact her attacks could penetrate his shield in such a way was also quite impressive, as not many opponents had been able to pull feats like these in the past.

“Hm…?” Ascalon muttered suddenly, his senses sharpening as the distinct whirring and clicking reached his ears once again. He smiled in disbelief, a mix of anticipation and boldness flaring from within.

Pride was preparing for yet another attack, even as they both ascended through the air—she was relentless! This time, however, both her gauntlets began to glow and tremble, the whirring intensifying into a high-pitched vibration that grated on Ascalon’s ears. Steam and magical sparks erupted from her armament, her face lighting up with an intense, wide smile.

“Block this, asshole!” Pride exclaimed in an excited tone, her fists emitting a powerful blast of steam, allowing her to close the distance in a flash.

Ascalon raised his guard, the bold grin on his face unwavering as he prepared for the upcoming onslaught. Pride took his calm demeanor as a challenge, and promptly unleashed her next attack: a barrage of precise punches powered by a mix of magic, innate strength, and mechanical prowess.

Each hit echoed like thunder as it struck the shield, with Ascalon managing to deftly block all the blows. Around them, the very sky seemed to tremble with every clash, just as pain ran across his torso with every shockwave that was unleashed.

But it was a bit strange, though. Pride was indeed strong, but Ascalon was beginning to realize he felt… off—it was as though he were in bad shape, so to speak. But that made no sense at all, as he had barely strained himself in the dungeon, so he should’ve been full of energy. Still, it felt somewhat nostalgic… somehow.

Ascalon’s confusion seemed more than evident on his face, as Pride, amidst her relentless barrage, began to laugh loudly.

“Haha! You finally noticed, eh?” She giggled with glee, her consecutive attacks showing no sign of stopping.

At her words, Ascalon pondered for a moment, and a thought flashed across his mind. Still paying close attention to his opponent, he focused on summoning the network interface in his mind, and surely enough, a blue window was waiting there for him.

This effect cannot be blocked or resisted, and can only be lifted by Pride herself.

Can’t be used alongside - Egotistical Rejection -

Ascalon let out a thoughtful ‘hmm’ as he read up the skill, with Pride seemingly realizing he had noticed her powers.

“Hehe, you must be feeling exhilarated—to have this much power bestowed upon you! But you’re still no match, haha!” She spoke as she punched harder and harder, her intricate gauntlets cleaving through the air. But Ascalon was utterly baffled by her words, yet he had finally realized why he felt so strange.

“Ah, I see… this is why I feel so weak!” Ascalon chirped as he blocked the attacks, thinking the skill was actually quite honorable! But his words struck Pride’s, well, pride, as her attacks ceased and her already red skin flared with a crimson blush.

“Say what?!” She growled with a mix of confusion and anger in her voice. With her magic-infused, steam-powered attacks stopping, there was nothing protecting her from gravity’s pull anymore, so her body plummeted down.

The knight fell after her, since he was only mid-air because of her relentless barrage, and both quickly reached the ground with a deafening, massive crash. The plateau shook violently, and the earth cracked under their combined weight. Some of the jagged edges crumbled into the chasm below, the crown-like shape of the peaks now looking like a bunch of random broken stones.

Ascalon’s friends observed the aftermath from a distance, having recovered enough after Pride had ascended into the sky with Ascalon—mostly thanks to her presence weakening as they soared higher. With that, Lilithra had been able to break free from Pride’s influence, and had rejoined her companions with an embarrassed expression on her face.

But now that the two fighters had returned to the ground, the tension among the group grew thick. They had witnessed Ascalon on the backpedal, unable to strike back against Pride’s relentless assault—and such a thing was unthinkable! The only other time Ascalon had been unable to overwhelm his opponent—albeit because of his own reasons—was against Greed, which only deepened their worries.

These ‘sins’ were incredibly strong, and that was undeniable… though they didn’t really know what was actually happening, anyway!

Ascalon gazed upon Pride with a confused look on his face, his head slightly tilted to the side. Pride’s chattering teeth and trembling shoulders made for a bizarre scene, especially after all the boasting and decisiveness she had shown in combat.

“W—what do you mean, weak?!” Pride blurted out, raising her face to meet Ascalon’s stare head-on. She was still blushing fiercely, a large vein throbbing on her forehead as if about to explode.

“Oh. Well, I was feeling rather sluggish. I understand now that you lowered my ‘stats’.” Ascalon said in a thoughtful voice, smiling with satisfaction at his own words.

“L—lowered…?” Pride repeated in a low voice, her eyes widening as she gazed upon Ascalon’s face. It was not the face of a mocker or a liar—he was completely serious.

“It… it can’t be…” She muttered with a bit of agitation in her tone, her brows furrowing into an angered frown.

“L—lowered… to my level?” Pride kept muttering in disbelief, with everyone staring at her in curiosity. Ascalon was unsure of what was going on, but decided to raise his spear and shield anyway, ready for the moment she decided to strike.

Now that he knew his stats were equal to her, he felt rather enthusiastic—but he knew he had to keep his guard up. After all, she was quite quick on her feet, and she had shown she could burst into an attack from one moment to the other! But no offensive came for a few minutes, as she stood there, giggling to herself.

“I see… It's humiliating! But… but…! I’ll have to use it!” Pride spoke in a regretful voice, her stare drooping to the ground. In the next moment, she looked up, her eyes now burning with an intense, surging power behind them.

Ascalon noticed the shift immediately, as his body felt ‘normal’ again. Had she lifted her skill? But why? As he mulled over that, his senses flared, his entire body on high alert as Pride’s presence encompassed the whole plateau.

Lilithra and Selorien froze, flinching as the overwhelming presence expanded, while Lyra, now clutching Moonstone in her arms, whimpered in distress. Two of them had briefly felt a similar thing in the past—the day they had first met Ascalon as an unknown, powerful entity.

The knight too was taken aback, all of his instincts screaming at him to act. Before him stood the same Pride, yet her entire aura had dramatically changed.

It was as if Ascalon were facing off against a reflection of himself.
