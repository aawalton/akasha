Only Vaymoz was that far gone.

“What are you waiting for? Follow me.”

None took three.

But Vaymoz couldn’t, despite knowing he could die if he continued forward. Despite knowing he might never make it in time.

He couldn’t. Because his daughter was somewhere ahead.

And for the first time in his life, defiance wasn't about standing against cultivators. It was about refusing to let fear stop him from reaching her.

Without another word, he turned and ran, leaving his frozen soldiers to stare after him in silence.
