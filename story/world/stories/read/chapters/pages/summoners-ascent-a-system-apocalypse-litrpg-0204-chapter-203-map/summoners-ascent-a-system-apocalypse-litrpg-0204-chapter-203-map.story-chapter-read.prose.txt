As Jacky, Oliver, and the rest told Ashton as many details that they could remember about the town and the different monsters, Ashton was just leaning his head onto his hand and listened intently.

Usually, or at least back then in Lumia, there were some regional preferences for the monsters, meaning that one specific type of monster dominated the area. After encountering mostly goblins and orcs right at the start of this, he thought that those guys were going to be the main type here. Sure, it wasn't like he expected them to be the only kind here, but the level of variety was kind of insane.

Did the council really have to throw so many different threats at this town?

Not only were there that early hobgoblin in Market Street, but the spiders' broodmother whose children , the zombies—even if those were technically caused by a Marked—, and an insanely powerful draconid that were threats that could have taken over the town on their own if they hadn't been taken care of somehow.

And now? There were those fleshy monsters connected to that 'growth' that were actively trying to take over a part of the town, the ancient constructs that were clearly protecting something, and then apparently the forest seemed to be creeping into the town more and more.

For that last part, of course there was the expected level of extreme growth that came through the presence of mana in the world, but apparently, people said that there was an area that was growing way faster than everything else, so Ashton was worried about some weird monsters that were the cause of that, too.

But of course, none of these threats even compared to the biggest danger to everyone in this town; something that no force at this time could face off against.

The Crimson Wyvern.

Apparently, it went on a rampage right after Ashton woke it up from within the veil, turning the area around Ashton's old flat into nothing but rubble, just to go to sleep again.

It wouldn't take long until the duration it could be awake for would increase more and more, until it started going on a complete rampage here in town.

Ashton was just glad that he was able to stop the draconid's ritual. From what he gathered, they were planning to strengthen the wyvern, which would have basically allowed it to wipe this whole town out with ease.

"Hm... alright, so from my understanding..." Ashton held his hand out and the Backpacker appeared, sitting down next to him on the bench. They brought out a piece of paper and some ink for him. The Pen of Insight came out from a pocket in his bag, and Ashton quickly had the magical scribe draw down a map of the city.

It was actually pretty detailed; the pen's crystal nib was changing shape slightly according to what exactly was needed from it in that moment, so the drawing was practically perfect and even tiny details could be added without issue. And the pen could truly draw everything that was in Ashton's head without fault, which was pretty damn convenient.

Hell, it even had street names drawn onto it.

'To think I couldn't even remember where Market Street was when this all started...' Ashton thought to himself. The influence of mana really seemed to have woken up his mind again, and it was amazing.

"Whoa... that's so cool," Nicole said with wide open eyes. "There's really everything on here..."

"Mm-hmm," Ashton said, smiling smugly. He had made sure that all notable points of interest in the town that Ashton knew about were jotted down on this map as well, and marked down the areas that he was told those new monsters' zones were in. "Does that cover it all? Or is there anything else important?"

"Uh, no, I think that's about it..." Jacky replied, looking over the map a few times. "But wait, what's this thing over here?"

She pointed at an area on the outskirts of the map.

"Ah... Okay, I should be more careful with this ability," Ashton replied before letting out a light sigh. "That slipped out along with the rest, I guess."

"Uh, so? What is it?" Oliver asked, and the others were curious as well. Even Millie seemed quite interested.

"It's the house I grew up in," he said, smiling awkwardly. "But I'd appreciate it if you guys stayed away from there unless it's an emergency. For a number of reasons."

"An emergency? Is it some kind of safehouse?" Oliver questioned, but Ashton shook his head. He didn't know how much to tell them.

"No... it's just that–"

"He doesn't want you lot rummaging around in his old stuff," Sam interrupted his friend with a long sigh as he put two plates down on the table, one in front of Ashton and one in front of Millie. "Now shut up and go get yourselves something to eat."

Oliver grumbled as he got up, "We're just curious, you know? He's always so mysterious about everything."

John pushed his friend along and nodded apologetically to Ashton, but he didn't really care. It was fine for them to want to know more about him. It was only natural at this point.

"Sorry... but still, what do you mean with 'an emergency'? That part felt pretty important," Jacky asked, clearly a bit uncomfortable to ask again. But rather than just being curious like Oliver, she clearly seemed concerned and just wanted to have more options for her and her friends.

"If it comes down to it and you have nowhere else to go, and especially if you're injured or scared, go to this place. Especially the garden. Ask for help, and be genuine. Then think three times about what you say or do next. Preferably, say nothing at all and leave as fast as you can," Ashton said, before looking down. The little pixie that had been sleeping in one of his pockets slowly showed herself. "And if you can, look for Poppy."

Jacky seemed to take mental notes of everything that Ashton was saying. She thanked Ashton, and recounted everything he had said a few times over, so that she wouldn't forget, before going to get something to eat with the others.

Sam sat down on one of the now empty seats, and watched as Ashton cut up the meat on Millie's plate. Apparently some people had hunted some kind of oversized boar today and brought back a bunch of meat from it, so the chefs decided to use it before it went bad.

"What?" Ashton asked, noticing Sam's stare.

"Nothing. Just... surprised, that's all."

Ashton sighed inwardly, and started eating his own food once Millie was good to go. "Sure. Where's Clem?"

"Since she brought back a bunch of seasonings for the chefs today, she got her food first. So I think right now she's on a 'digestion run', or whatever she called it."

"Hm, alright. When she's back, the three of us are heading out," Ashton replied, popping one of the chewy bits of meat into his mouth.

Millie snapped her head toward Ashton. "Wait, the three of you? What about me?"

Ashton smiled. "It's okay, it will be just for a little while. You can stay upstairs in the tent if you want. Or spend some time with Jacky and the others. They seemed fun, right?"

Millie grumbled and nodded. "Fine... okay..."

"You know, I taught Oliver the same practice method that I showed you. Don't you want to see which of you two is better at it?" Ashton asked, and Millie widened her eyes.

"Oliver was that weird-looking guy that wouldn't stop talking, right?"

Ashton scoffed out a laugh and slowly nodded. "He looks weird to you? Alright, well, that's probably him though. He's the only other person with a magic class that I know of right now."

Millie dropped her fork, "Wait, seriously? He is? Is he a wizard?"

"No, no, he's an 'Occultist'." Ashton expected Millie to be disappointed with that, but she actually seemed even more excited.

"An occultist?! Whoa, that's so cool!"

"Do you even know what an occultist is?"

"It's someone that likes really creepy stuff and does human sacrifices, right?" Millie asked, and somehow, she seemed most excited about that last bit. Though, it shouldn't surprise Ashton. Millie had been a weird kid right from the start.

"I'd hope that he's not doing any of those. But hey, just ask him about it later. I'm sure he'll be very happy to tell you," Ashton suggested, and Millie happily nodded and continued to eat.

It didn't take long for the others to get back to the table and sit down, and Millie immediately started to bombard Oliver with questions about occultism. At first he was a bit surprised, but the moment he started talking, he was more than excited to talk her ear off.

And it wasn't just him, either. The rest of Jacky's group, as well as Jacky herself, were more than happy to talk to Millie. They really did seem like good guys. Plus, Millie seemed relaxed around them, so he figured that trusting them with her wasn't a bad idea.

They agreed shockingly fast as well when Ashton told them he had something to take care of with Sam and Clementine. He even told them where in the tent the stash of snacks, candy, and drinks was so that they could have a small party and make Millie feel at home later.

Ashton wanted Millie to learn to trust other adults beside just him as fast as possible, considering the horrible experiences she's had especially recently.

And it didn't take long for Clementine to get back from her run. Though, she seemed to be breathing quite hard.

"Everything okay?" Ashton got up from his seat and approached her, trying to get a closer look at her through the wisp. She didn't seem particularly injured anywhere. But Ashton could also hear that there was some kind of commotion going on near the tent. There was snarling and barking outside as well.

"Sorry, uh... I ended up getting chased by some dogs..." Clementine sighed, taking a deep breath. "I can usually avoid monsters pretty well, but these guys are like... uh, bloodhounds, I guess? Literally, I mean."

"Huh... should've figured," Ashton sighed. "Goblins already worked with things like wolves often, so no wonder they'd take in some hunting dogs. Don't worry, I'll take care of it. But get ready to leave again in a second."

Ashton turned and walked up to the Backpacker, who gave him his 'staff', and then looked at Millie.

"Stay good until I'm back, alright? Ask the tent to close itself if you want to be alone."

Millie quickly nodded. "Okay... but you'll be back later, right?"

"Of course," Ashton promised. "I'll be back soon. And don't bully the others too much, okay?"

"Hmm, fine," Millie said with a broad grin. "Just come back fast!"

"Yeah, yeah." Ashton ruffled up Millie's hair a bit, and then turned around. He could tell that people were gathered around the gate, trying to figure out how to deal with the red wolves, so he figured he should just go another way.

Ashton stretched a little, and then dropped down into a squat. He catapulted himself right back up, and his strengthened legs made him shoot right over the tall wall.

Just a moment later, the dogs began to bark even louder, though those barks quickly turned into yelps. Not even thirty seconds passed before there was nothing but silence beyond the wall.

Someone opened up the gate from the inside, and Ashton stepped back through, facing the area where his friends were standing.

"Guys, come on, let's go."
