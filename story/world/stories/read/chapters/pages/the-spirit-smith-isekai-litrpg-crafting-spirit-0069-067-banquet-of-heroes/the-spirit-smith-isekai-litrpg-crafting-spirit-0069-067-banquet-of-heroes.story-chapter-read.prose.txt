My life has changed so quickly. To think that just six short months ago I was but a humble smith. I was sitting in my forge, enjoying my semi-retirement. Then an elven mage and her prince showed up, took me to their queen, and I was summarily sacrificed in a ritual.

A ritual that brought me to this strange world. Well, now that I have been here for a while, it isn't really that strange. Sure, there is magic and a system and all that bullshit. But in the end, it is just a world.

As I gaze out the window of my hotel, I can just see the gentle rise of some hills in the distance. At first I called them mountains, but apparently they are nothing compared to true mountains.

So okay, maybe this world is a bit strange. But still, there are mountains and rivers and cities and all that normal stuff. It is all just a lot bigger. Guess the goddess thought that bigger was truly better. Which is fine with me, just means there is more world for me to explore.

Though couldn’t she have placed things just a bit closer together? Ari and I have spent a few days since we exited the dungeon in Carnia. You know, gathering supplies, repairing equipment, the usual. Part of that was figuring out our next destination.

Our end goal is to reach the capital, of course. But that is way too far away to make in a single journey. Ari says it will likely take three or four months, assuming we don’t get too distracted. Apparently we not only have to cross the Snarq Mountains, but also the Archipelago of Despair. Which both sound like absolutely lovely places to travel through. But the distances that we have to cover are quite vast. So naturally, we need a more reasonable target to aim for in the short-term.

Which is why we settled on a minor city on the border between Carnia and the Snarq Mountains. Its name is Itolas, and apparently it is quite a nice place. It is nestled at the foot of the mountains, and even has hot springs. Despite it being only a minor city, it is quite massive. Mostly due to it being a common stopping point for people going over the mountains.

Thus it only makes sense for us to stop there too. As a side bonus, we can even stop at the hot springs. I don’t get the whole appeal, but Ari seems downright obsessed with hot springs. She does seem a bit more experienced with traveling, and who am I to argue if she says they will be awesome?

“Alright Orn, you ready to head out?” Ari says, walking up behind me.

“Yeah, this place is starting to get old” I reply, “Not that Carnia is bad or anything. But my feet are itching to get moving again.”

“Mhm, too many things have happened in this city. Luckily, we can just go to another.” Ari says and then without any hesitation, she climbs up on top of the balcony’s railing.

“Do we really have to do this?” I ask, climbing up next to her.

“You have a really fucking powerful spirit bird. Why wouldn’t we do it at least once?”

“Master, are you afraid of heights?” Dovey asks from Ari’s shoulder. He has taken quite a liking to her, which I am not sure is a good thing. She inspires bad behaviors in my already bad behaving bird.

“Of course not.” I say, legs shaking just a bit as I stare down at the ground below us. We are several hundred meters up in the air, but I am definitely not afraid of heights. At least, in an irrational sense. It is perfectly rational to be afraid of falling such a large distance.

“Well, see ya down there, buddy!” Ari says and dramatically falls off the balcony.

Buddy? Is that what we are now? Guess it seems fine. I watch Ari drop for a moment before finally working up the courage to step off myself. Funnily enough, I am quite experienced with falling nowadays. Ever since I came to this world, it has become far too common.

I only scream for a moment before Dovey’s magic wraps around me. It slows my fall to a much gentler pace, and I actually sorta enjoy watching everything rush up beside me. Before I know it, I am brought to a stop as my feet touch the road.

Ari is doubled over in laughter, probably at my screaming. I try really hard not to notice the fact that everyone is staring at us. Crazy stuff happens in cities all the time, but it isn't like people jumping from the side of a building is common.

“That was soooooo much fun! Let’s do it again.” She says, reaching out to grab my hand. She starts to drag me back towards the hotel until I stop her. By the goddess, I think she is actually serious.

“Hmm, you can go without me. I’ll just watch from down here.” I say, sliding my hand out of hers. “I’m still getting used to having this domain inside me, you know.” I ended up using my transport skill earlier to grab the domain I left in the room. I was just going to dissolve it, but Ari said that doing so to an initiate tier domain would be too wasteful. So I now have it contained within me, somewhere. It feels a bit weird, but I am quickly getting used to it.

“Geez, you are no fun.”

“I would just rather get travelling. You said we have a long way to go, and I can tell you are the type to get distracted by literally everything.”

She steps back, seemingly offended. “What makes you think that? I am most certainly nothing like that.”

I sigh, “How are you such a bad liar even with that armor on? Can’t you invent some magic to make it harder to detect.”

“Meanie. I am totally not one to get distracted. Just watch!” She declares.

—

Literally five minutes later.

“Orn, look at this shell, isn't it pretty?” Ari says, having dragged me down a random alley into some sort of jewelry market. So much for not getting distracted. Not that I mind, the shell is quite pretty after all.

“Hmm, it is pretty. Bet it would make a great necklace.”

Ari holds the shell up to her neck. “So you think it would look nice on me… I see.”

Pretty sure that isn’t what I said, but I don’t correct her. After that short distraction, we actually manage to make it out of the city. However, a few minutes after we exit the gates, a ding sounds in my mind. I glance over it, and then open it right up.

‘Ding’, You have been issued an invitation to the Banquet of Heroes. Expand for further information.

Orn Spiritus, you have been invited to partake in a legendary opportunity. One that will certainly go down in history. For it is a banquet, held by none other than myself (the goddess)!

The festivities shall commence in six months exactly. In order to participate, you must be located somewhere within the capital city of The Realm of Humanity. Trust me, you will not want to miss this.

It is not only heroes like yourself that will be invited. Various persons of power from around the realm, and even a few guests from beyond, will be invited. This will be a great opportunity to get to know more about the world, and all sorts of political stuff I am sure.

Oh, and of course I will be there! With drinks and tons of good food. Honestly, I don’t see why anyone would not show up. It is sure to be awesome. Don’t miss it. Seriously. Do not even try to skip out.

With Love,

The Super Duper Awesome Goddess.

I can’t help it, and a laugh escapes me as I read the message. A banquet, thrown by the goddess herself. Full of all sorts of people too. This is big. Like, really big. Which means shit is about to get real, I expect.

“Hey Orn, did you uh, get that message too?” Ari asks.

“Sure did, assuming you mean the one about the Banquet of Heroes?”

“Yep, that’s the one. Guess we must both be important people, then. I can’t imagine the goddess just handed out invites to just anyone.” She says, still unaware that I am one of the heroes.

I have considered telling her, of course. But somehow it has just never felt right. Plus we did meet only a month ago, and while I do trust her, I don’t know if I trust her that much. Her first actions upon meeting me were not the nicest, after all.

“It would seem that it will be occurring in the capital. Good thing we were already planning to head there.” I say.

“Yeah, it's perfect. Though we will definitely have to make good time, and not get too distracted.”

I roll my eyes at her. “I am sure we can manage. Now, wanna race to that hill over there?” I say, gesturing to one of the hills in the distance.

Ari practically jumps with excitement. “Oh, you are so on!” Before I can even count us down or whatever, she has already leapt forward. Damn her, I take off running after her, and end up having to chain some Plane Steps to catch up.

But the instant I catch up, she starts running even faster. I sigh, and just go even faster myself. The journey ahead may be long, but something tells me it won’t be too bad. Any journey is made better by having a good traveling companion, after all. By some miracle, it would seem I have stumbled onto a pretty damn good one, too.
