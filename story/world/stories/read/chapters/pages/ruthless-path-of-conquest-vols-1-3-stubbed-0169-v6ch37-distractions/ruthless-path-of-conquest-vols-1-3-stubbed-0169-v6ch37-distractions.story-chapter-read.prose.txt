I’m joining the hunt, James sent telepathically to every one of his monstrous allies. We need every hand pulling at full force to accomplish this mission, and it’s been foolish to have kept me back for so long. Also, if you see my mother, she can use Soul Magic too. If you tell her what you need, she should be able to help.

Are you sure this is wise, Your Majesty? asked the Panther Queen.

You are putting yourself in physical danger, Chosen, added Mageddon the giant spider Ruler. For no certain gain. If you are killed here, our mission will be a failure, will it not?

I am not so easily killed, James replied, trying to keep a vein of ice from his tone. Ask any of the other Rulers who serve me alongside you. They are almost all former opponents who I defeated in single combat.

This is not a single combat arena, though, the spider replied without missing a beat. Here you can be attacked by multiple opponents at once.

I have to go, James sent.

As the giant spider’s message hit, three shadows appeared floating in the sky between James and the sun the System had positioned in this place.

“Hello, Fisher Emperor,” said an accented voice. James squinted and saw it was a bald Asian man. The other two were an African Ruler and another Asian, a man with more of a pretty boy look like Zhang Chāo.

They were all members of Pú Tiě Shān’s faction, weren’t they? James thought.

“I’m a bit busy,” James said. “I don’t really have time to talk international politics right now.”

But something told him they weren’t here to talk.

“We got the impression that the melee is a rather important stage for you,” said Baldy. “You have been rather ostentatious in what you’ve done to some of your opponents.”

“True,” James said. “Isn’t that a reason for the three of you to avoid fucking with me? Sorry to be so blunt, but I’m really busy. I don’t want to have to kill you guys right now.”

“The arrogance you showed on that stage really wasn’t a show, hm?” said the pretty boy.

“We have every reason to make things difficult for you as you embark on your mission of religious warfare, Emperor,” said the African Ruler.

“I wasn’t aware that Pú Tiě Shān was allied with the monotheists,” James said, raising an eyebrow.

At least, I never saw the two groups talking at the Summit. Each one had its own constituents. Pú Tiě Shān’s people were more secular or religiously diverse. I figured many of them had blessings from gods great and small, like me.

“The three of us figured that we could make inroads with them if we helped deal with you,” Baldy said. “We already share the common value of not wanting to share the planet with monsters, and the People of the Book are at least capable of understanding the value of strong authorities.”

“Oh, I see,” James replied.

So, Pú Tiě Shān himself probably didn’t even authorize this little game. If they were smarter, they wouldn’t have announced their presence. They’d have jumped me. No point in drawing this conversation out any longer.

He used Warp Step, reappeared next to the bald one, and slashed with Roscuro—and a fiery heat ripped through his right shoulder, almost severing it from his body.

An unseen attacker.

“Oh, did you think it was just us three?” Baldy asked, recovering from the very clear surprise of James appearing beside him.

The Fisher Emperor allowed gravity to pull him down and tilted his head up slightly to see both the trio who had confronted him directly and whatever unseen attacker had struck him.

There was a parted cloud, he saw now, with a steaming hole right in the middle. That was where the fiery beam had originated from. A laser, perhaps.

James saw the figure looking down at him from behind the cloud now. He wore a flaming crown.

The Sun King. I wouldn’t have figured he’d joined Pú Tiě Shān’s faction.

James had briefly encountered the Sun King at the Summit, along with scores or hundreds of other leaders. He hadn’t thought much of it at the time, but the man had looked at him with an air of sizing him up.

Perhaps what he’s actually after isn’t helping Pú Tiě Shān but disadvantaging me. Maybe what he was really using the Summit for was assessing his opponents for the Tournament. He looked at my aura and my physique and figured I’d be one of his primary opponents in the competition. Whatever his reasons, this is annoying. With an attack that moves as fast as that laser—I think it was a laser—I can’t just run away. As soon as I turned my back, I’d be an easy target. That thing moved too fast for my senses to warn me in advance. My only defense against it right now is that if he’s within my field of view, I think I’ll be able to see him preparing it.

James looked a bit harder. He thought he saw another two figures up above the cloud beside the Sun King.

He swallowed. The idea of taking on six Rulers alone was daunting, even for him.

“Pú Tiě Shān already had me as an opponent, but with this bullshit, he’s making me an actual enemy,” James said loudly. “Make sure you let him know, he won’t be able to lay his head down peacefully anywhere on Earth after today.”

There was an obvious hesitation on the faces of two of the men who had confronted James initially, the African and the pretty boy. They clearly understood both that he meant it and that the threat, coming from him, had to be taken seriously.

“Neither can you,” Baldy retorted. “We know your situation with the monotheists must be desperate for you to have gone as far as you have. Don’t pretend you’ll have the free time to pursue our leader while he’s in his place of greatest strength and security.”

What a waste of my damned time, James thought.

His feet struck the ground, and he felt the strange sensation of his arm smacking against the hollow space in his shoulder, the wound from the Sun King’s attack that was still regenerating—rapidly, but perhaps not rapidly enough for what James needed.

He ignored it as best he could and began a telepathic message to his creatures.

Calling all my allies and subjects, he sent. Which of you are engaged against Holy Kings or are near Holy Kings that you could ambush, and which of you are completely unoccupied?

James, none of the Holy Kings have been sighted anywhere near the lake I’ve been dominating, Samuel replied immediately.

We’re engaged against a Holy King right now! sent a frog Ruler who had joined James after the Battle of Orlando.

I had not found the next target yet, Luna sent.

Likewise, chimed the Panther Queen.

Please call upon me if you are in any danger, added Mageddon, clearly sensing from James’s tone why he was asking if they were busy.

James’s other allies either responded that they were near Holy Kings or that they were actually locked in combat. He focused his mind on those that could make themselves available.

Summon Divine Helper.

A vast amount of Mana surged up around James, and the others he was facing reacted instantly. The African Ruler raised his hands, and a thick Mana barrier appeared between the three men and James. The Sun King began charging another beam of light in his crown. Whatever the name of that Skill was, it reminded James of a more intense version of his own Solar Ray.

I don’t want to get hit by it again if I can avoid it.

The huge Mana spike dissipated in an instant, and James felt a slight sensation of weakness as his Mana dropped by almost half.

Around him, a few of his allies appeared.

Samuel held a trident James had never seen the former Alligator Monarch wield before.

The Panther Queen had her long, saber-like claws fully extended already.

Mageddon had a large section of web held up in front of her head like a shield.

Luna was her usual fierce self.

“What the fuck?! We have to get out of here!” exclaimed one of the men from up behind the cloud.

“Remain calm!” shouted Baldy. “If he was confident in beating the rest of us, he wouldn’t have summoned help. We can still win, we just have to—”

Warp Step. Lightning Strike.

James appeared beside Baldy in mid-sentence, and this time, his instincts alerted him that he was about to be hit.

James’s body, enhanced by Lightning Strike, moved faster than it ever had before, and he managed to grab Baldy and thrust him into the attack’s path in the microseconds afforded to him by the Sun King’s distance and attack speed.

“Fuck!” Baldy screamed as the attack tore through his midsection.

“Quick! With me!” the African Ruler shouted. He clapped his hands, and the barrier James had warped past suddenly acquired walls and a roof, leaving James and the bald man on the outside, while the African and the pretty boy were protected.

Not exactly honorable to try and escape the fight while your comrade is over here bleeding out, James thought.

“Roscuro,” he whispered.

The Soul Eater turned into the form of a hatchet, and James planted it in the back of Baldy’s skull.

There was a horrendous scream as the Soul Eater claimed another victim, body and soul.

Then James turned to the next targets.

Warp Step!

He appeared within the prism the African Ruler had constructed with his barrier powers.

“That was a waste of Mana, don’t you think?” James said, smirking.

“That’s what you think,” the African Ruler said, slightly sweaty but still smiling right back at the Fisher Emperor. “You made a rash decision using your spatial manipulation Skill to get in here. I control this space, and I have set parameters that prevent anyone from leaving unless I allow it. While you are trapped in here with the two of us, your allies will be picked apart on the outside.”

“I don’t think that’s the advantage you think it is,” James said with a raised eyebrow.

The Sun King and the two with him were outnumbered by Samuel and the others, at least, even if they would not necessarily be overpowered by them. And James himself was one of the strongest in the world.

Best not to take any chances, though.

Would you like to use my new Skill, master? Roscuro asked.

Go for it.

The words moved through his mind as Roscuro tugged at his hand until he pointed the Soul Eater at the enemies: Soul Vortex.

As soon as James had raised the weapon and spoken the words inside his head with the intent to use the Skill, he felt something begin to happen.

An intangible wind took shape within the small confines of the barrier box, and an invisible force began pulling the bodies of the two enemy Rulers toward James—or, more accurately, toward the eye of the storm, Roscuro.

“What sorcery is this?” asked the pretty boy.

“I told you I control this space,” the African blustered.

He clapped his hands, and suddenly, the distance between James and the others multiplied. They were all still inside the barrier, but it had suddenly gone from the size of a small room to that of a large bowling alley, with the two other men at the opposite end from James.

He still felt the invisible wind pulling, but they were too far away now, he recognized, to be moved by it.

The pretty boy looked relieved, but the African didn’t. He knew exactly the problem they faced, and James could see the wheels turning inside his head as he tried to come up with a solution that didn’t exist.

Warp Step.

The deadliest possible game of tag had just begun.
