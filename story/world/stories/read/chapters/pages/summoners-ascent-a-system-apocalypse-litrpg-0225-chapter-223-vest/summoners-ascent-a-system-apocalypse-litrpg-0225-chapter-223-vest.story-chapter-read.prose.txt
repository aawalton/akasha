Ashton sat on a pillow in front of the table at the Living Abode's centre, working more on his clothes. So far, he had only made the base of his trousers and shirt; they weren't even enchanted yet at this point in time.

For now, Ashton's focus was to make the actual clothes, so that the enchantments he placed on the clothes later could be linked together better. At the moment, his focus was on finishing the outfit's vest. All of the clothes he had were in shades of white to blue, similar to the cloak.

The shirt and trousers were a couple shades lighter than royal blue, while his button-up shirt, though said buttons were still missing and were just tied shut with some thread, was such a pale icy blue that it almost appeared white.

Beyond this, he would need a tie, and then he could get started on the enchanting. The vest in particular was going to be enchanted with loads of protective enchantments, while the trousers and shirt would get some enchantments to help with the flow and stability of internal energy.

Then there was the tie; this one would be turned into a magical focus. Essentially, an item meant to help stabilise one's mana for spellcasting. He knew about those beforehand already, but he wasn't allowed to study them or learn about their structure all too much. Though, thinking back, some of the items he enchanted were probably used by the Summoner to create magic focuses like that in the past as well.

However, in one of the books that Ashton read, spellcasting focuses were obviously described in quite a lot of detail. It was apparently a common practice in the place the book was written in for novice spellcasters to create their own focuses early as part of their studies, so it was quite a simple process to get started.

Technically, Ashton's summoner-staff already acted as a spellcasting focus, but since it wasn't something that he could just carry around with him openly all the time, having another focus on him was a good idea.

The other accessories would also be made just to improve the flow of his mana. The buttons, cuff-links; even the chain he was going to connect to the Arcane Pocket Watch. Every part of this outfit would be made to improve Ashton's magic.

In terms of the system, he was specialising in boosting his Spirit stat while ignoring everything else. Well, everything else could basically be made up for using the Spirit stat anyway. That didn't mean that Ashton could just ignore the other stats, but they would rise on their own to a degree that he was fine with for now.

However, as for the accessories that Ashton needed... he sadly couldn't make them on his own. There was another summon that he wanted to call on to make those for him, but that required quite some setup. A lot of spirits asked to be given things that they would then use as part of their contract. The Backpacker was a prime example. And a lot of crafting-related spirits asked for workstations, as did this metal-work spirit that he wanted to call on. For which he first needed a furnace and an anvil at the very least. At the very least, that spirit had their own hammers. Those were actually their absolute pride and joy.

He was actually torn between using the Settlement Box to call that spirit and calling the Root Mouse, but getting a good supply of potion-ingredients was more important than getting a spirit that could make weapons.

Due to the quests, everyone had somewhat decent equipment for the time being. Plus, there would be other people with crafting abilities, and they would have been given whatever they needed to actually do their job.

So, if Ashton remembered correctly—and he did—then there was something like a blacksmith in town. Well, only really because there were somehow tons of people that kept horses in town. While this wasn't the biggest town, there were a lot of that type of annoying rich folk in Drakewood. So if he went there, Ashton should be able to find all the things that he needed to summon a great addition to his team.

Plus, it would help Ashton look a bit more fancy, which wasn't exactly a bad thing for when he went to the university later.

Almost everyone else was struggling immensely to keep their clothes clean, or even whole, and Ashton showing up in those sort of clothes would immediately help him show off a bit. If the folk at university were trying to set up some kind of system of 'ownership' over different parts of town, Ashton had to put a stop to that. And looking more 'legitimate' would help.
