Oka spun a staff with their right hand, as Ashton watched closely and intensely.

"Do like... this. Roll over, uhm..." Oka started to speak in the local language, though Ashton just smiled at them.

"Hand," he finished for them.

"Yes! Roll over hand, like this." With a bright smile on their face, they nodded, looking over at Ashton.

"You don't have to force yourself to speak Sakan, you know?" Ashton pointed out, leaning onto his own staff with a smirk. Different to Oka, Ashton wasn't speaking Sakan, the language most commonly spoken on this particular continent. He was speaking Oka's language, and they were quite miffed about it.

In their own native tongue, they soon responded. "Hey, stop it, I'm trying my best here, okay? Not everyone can be some kind of super genius that can learn a new language in a month."

Ashton shrugged, grinning lightly. "Just say it, you're jealous."

With a roll of their eyes, holding themself back from smirking, Oka crossed their arms. "Oh, please, as if. I figure I'm picking Sakan up pretty well. Plus, where you were blessed with a mind of great memory, you were robbed of physical control."

Ashton's smile dropped. "I can do it, I'm just a bit clumsy. That's all."

He had basically just been doing whatever came to his mind, which wasn't usually particularly effective. The main reason why Ashton was able to fight the way he could was because of his raw physical ability that came from the Summoner's magic.

"Really? Do it, then. Do what I just showed you," Oka said with a knowing smile, placing their staff onto their shoulder.

"Yeah, I'm going to, don't worry," Ashton replied, slowly starting to try and spin his staff around. It went pretty well for a few moments, until Ashton tried to roll the staff over the back of his hand. Instead of just grabbing it again, the staff slipped out of his fingers and soon hit the ground, bouncing away a few steps until Ashton just stood there with his eyes closed, biting his lip. "Weird, that wasn't supposed to happen."

Oka sighed, patting Ashton on the back. He looked up at them through his brow, noticing that annoying stare.

"Oh fine, just do it already." Ashton took a deep breath, preparing himself already, as Oka started to let out a loud, almost hysterical laugh.

"How can you be so incredibly strong, but so useless with your technique?" They wiped a tear from their eye as their laugh echoed through the valley. Some animals reacted in the distance, and Ashton sighed.

"What, you're not embarrassed that you keep losing to someone like that?" Ashton asked, quickly picking up the staff again.

"It's not like I'm some great warrior. I'm a jargo-maras. I can handle myself in a fight, but if you're made of flesh and bone instead of crystal and stone, then you're not exactly what I'm used to."

By the time Ashton turned back around to them, Oka was spinning the staff around, passing it behind their back and mostly controlling it with their individual fingers.

"Braggard," Ashton grumbled. "I bet I'll be able to do it soon enough."

"Oh? You want to make a bet?" Oka asked, curious about Ashton's choice of words.

He turned toward them, confused. "Hm?"

"You said you bet you'll be able to do it soon. Do you want to bet?"

Ashton stared at Oka for a few moments, trying to figure out if it was worth it to make that bet.

"Hm... Well," Ashton scratched his beard. "It's morning now... if I can practice all day, then I'm sure I'll be able to do it by nightfall. What would you want if I can't?"

He was feeling competitive. He never really put too much effort into learning technique, either, but if he really put the effort into it, he was sure that he could do it perfectly fine!

"If you lose, you get rid of that beard," Oka said, far faster than Ashton expected. "I have a blade, I can shave it for you, but you'll get rid of it."

Ashton raised his brows. "What? Seriously? You hate it that much?"

"In the tribe I grew up in, only those that were acknowledged as elders wore a beard," Oka explained, and looked Ashton up and down a few times. "But... yes, that beard is very unkempt."

Ashton sighed. "Well, whatever. I'll be able to do it anyway. And if I win, you have to... hm, you have to teach me how to sew as well. It's getting annoying to buy new clothes when they get messed up in a fight."

Oka raised their brow. "You know, you could have just asked me to teach you. I do owe a lot to you."

"Whatever. No you don't," Ashton waved Oka away, holding his staff with a grin. "Just don't worry about that, you'll be teaching it to me soon, either way."

Ashton stared down at the pile of hair on the ground in front of him, before silently glancing over at Oka, who was cleaning their sharp knife.

"... You could have at least given me a bit more time to enjoy it," Ashton grumbled, running his hand over his smooth cheek.

Oka turned around, beaming. "I wanted to collect my winnings as fast as possible."

Ashton stood up and sighed. "You know, you're shockingly annoying sometimes."

"And you're surprisingly handsome without that scruff," Oka replied.

"Hah, sure, whatever," Ashton rolled his eyes.

But Oka looked over at him, and smiled lightly. They reached out toward Ashton's face, and grabbed him by the chin, making him face them directly. "It's not a joke, you know?"

"Hm?" Ashton stared at the face of the person in front of him. Sure, he had dated people before being dragged here, so it wasn't the first time he was complimented, but never like... this. Never in a way that he knew for a fact made his face turn beet red, and not while the other person's face flushed the exact same way.

Oka's face turned a bright blue instead, sure, but somehow seeing that for the first time made Ashton even more self-conscious about what was happening to him.

He laughed awkwardly and pushed Oka's hand away. "That's, uhm... very nice of you to say. Thank you."

Ashton turned away, putting his arm in front of his face. This was another terrible part about not having his beard anymore. His cheeks were fully revealed.

"Uhm, I'll be grabbing some more firewood before it gets too dark out..." Ashton said, without turning toward Oka, and they just let out an approving grunt.

As Ashton walked a little deeper into the woods, picking up some of the thick, dropped branches on the ground.

'Why the hell did they just say that?' he thought to himself. There wasn't any hint of them coming onto him so far, and that's how they started?

Ashton fully hadn't thought about that at all. This body didn't have those sorts of urges. Yeah sure, just like anything else, if he wanted to, he probably could let them bubble up, but there have just been no situations like that so far.

Yeah, he could still acknowledge beauty. There were a fair few humanoid summons that he had seen here and there that he couldn't deny were pretty or handsome or ethereal, or whatever other words like that existed in the dictionary.

But it didn't really matter. Oka was a very touchy person anyway, so maybe this was just a part of their culture that they didn't show yet? Maybe they just called people handsome in totally platonic ways. Or maybe Ashton just didn't understand the translation of that word correctly after all.

"Right! That has to be it! I just don't understand the word quite right!" Ashton said to himself, letting out a sigh of relief. "I guess I'll have to study up a bit more after all."

As Ashton stood there, growing a bit more relieved, he heard some snarling nearby. A wild beast had approached him through the darkness. It was one of those 'dark beasts' that had been roaming around the area recently. This was actually one of the things Ashton was supposed to investigate.

Though, first, he dropped the firewood. He was stupid enough to forget his staff, but it didn't even really matter.

Ashton took one of the most appropriate-seeming sticks that he had found, and waited for the six-legged beast to run at him. To Ashton, it looked like a mixture between a dog and a boar, though it came closer to the size of a grizzly.

It was long and though most of its body was covered in fur, it had a scaled tale and snout.

But Ashton just stood there, letting it run right at him without even worrying about it. He let his internal energy flow into the stick, and then swung it to the side just once when he figured he could hit the monster.

What was formerly its head was now splattered across the forest floor and some of the nearby trees. Its body slumped to the ground and Ashton dodged just lightly out of the way as the carcass slid over the dry leaves.

"Annoying. Can't even eat these," Ashton clicked his tongue and grabbed the rest of the firewood that he collected, that they really didn't need at all.

He turned and brought it back to the camp, where the fire was already burning away. Oka was sitting on a rock right next to it, stoking the fire a bit here and there.

"Welcome back," Oka said with a smile. Ashton wasn't sure because of the fire, but he could swear that their face was just as blue as it was earlier. Then again, he doubted he was any less red.

"Thanks," Ashton replied, dropping the wood down on the ground with the rest before sitting down at the fire as well. Not totally opposite to Oka, but also not right next to them. Just barely close enough that they could look at each other.

"So, uhm..." they started after a few moments of awkward silence. "Even if you lost the bet, would you like to learn how to sew anyway?"

Ashton raised his brows. "Are you sure?"

Oka nodded. "Gladly. We can start right away, here."

They stood up, holding one of Ashton's ripped-up shirts. It was really old and not particularly comfortable, but it held out longer than the rest. Oka had been asking for some fabric here and there to help them alter clothes for hapi into ones that fit them properly, and Ashton gave them this shirt after a beast cut up his chest.

Oka sat down right next to Ashton, kneeling on the ground. They were staring at the shirt, practically forcing themself not to look away ever, but Ashton couldn't help himself but look up at them.

They started to explain what Ashton had to focus on when trying to fix clothes like this, but he still couldn't look away for some reason.

"Oka?"

"Hm?" they looked toward him, and they locked eyes again. "You're beautiful."
