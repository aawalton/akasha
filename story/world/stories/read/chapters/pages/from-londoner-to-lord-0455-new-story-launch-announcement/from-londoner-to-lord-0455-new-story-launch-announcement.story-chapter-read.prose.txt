TEREN MARR, AGAIN

The world ends in 2 years, 11 months, and 19 days. Before Teren can do anything about it, he has to survive his first day in the city.

Teren awakens in a world of monsters, dungeons, and magic, trapped in a time loop where every wrong choice shows him one more way the city of Karthane can kill him. Each death points to the same truth: Teren Marr von Corvadryn is not supposed to exist.

For over a thousand years, the Corvadryn dynasty ruled a continent-spanning empire through their bond with dragons and their legendary purple mana. But a century ago, their bloodline came to an end.

Or so the world believes.

Teren’s blood, however, carries the same legendary power, marking him as heir to a dead imperial dynasty and a target for those determined to keep it buried.

Survival requires him to hide who he is, master the power of his bloodline, and grow strong enough to prevent the disaster no one else sees coming.

The end of the world is still years away. The city may not give him a single day.
