Tony knew a lot of different kinds of fear. Dread, helplessness, terror, horror, existential despair… all had been part of his long and empty existence at one time or another. The panic of running from a larger voidbeast that wanted to consume him, the frustration of being too slow to reach the front of the press, all of it was familiar.

Since meeting Noah, all that fear had become distant. Tony had instead felt excitement, joy, eagerness, and even hope, a myriad of wondrous new emotions as he followed his master, his new partner, into one event full of thrilling chaos after another.

That chaos was suddenly a whole lot less thrilling and a lot more terrifying.

Noah’s body had been obliterated so fast even Tony’s advanced perception didn’t see it coming, leaving only his frantically beating heart amid the destruction.

Noah gave Tony an image and a brief burst of jumbled instructions, then shoved the last of their mana into teleporting them as far as possible toward that destination before passing out.

Tony had recreated Noah before. Before his master had completed his first evolution, Tony learned from a grafting master how to improve every bone, muscle, and cell in Noah’s entire body. That process had involved breaking Noah apart piece by piece and putting them back together.

Since then, the two of them had fused closer and closer into perfect unity. Tony’s metamorphosis could extend across Noah’s body and amplify everything he was, and Noah’s lifeblood sustained and strengthened Tony far beyond anything the abyssal creature had ever imagined possible.

But though Tony was an expert in reshaping and improving Noah’s body, he found himself at a loss when faced with the task of recreating it from nothing. Anything he was given, he could improve, but he had nothing.

Only Noah’s shadow-streaked storm-sparking heart, quiescent without a body to sustain, resting in Tony’s claws.

Noah’s life spark remained, Tony could feel it in every pulse of energy exchanged between them, every beat of his wings proof that his master still lived.

Even as he flew, the heart grew heavier as it started to extend out, new veins pushing their way free as the indestructible heart began the process of creating a new body for Noah.

Not yet. Tony isn’t ready. Master isn’t safe. Tony silently urged the heart. Tony cannot carry much more.

The blood of dragons was not patient and paid no heed to a mere familiar’s plea.

Tony is NOT a familiar. Tony is Tony.

The heart of dragons accepted no equal. Each flicker of lightning that pulsed through the heart continued its regrowth.

On the one wing, Tony wanted to keep Noah alive, and so did Noah’s draconic nature.

On the other wing, they were all supposed to be on the same team. There was no point engaging in petty rivalry over specifics.

But with Noah unconscious, the dragon blood was unthinking, incapable of negotiation. While conscious, Noah would cede any part of his body to Tony without thought, without hesitation, but with neither a body nor active consciousness to act as mediator, Tony was left facing pure biology.

This is wrong.

Tony wasn’t supposed to be separated from Noah, was never meant to be an onlooker to their life or death.

Tony was best Tony. Master’s closest, eternal companion. The one thing Noah could rely on even when he lost everything and everyone else.

So when it refused to stop, Tony bit the heart.

He’d intended it to be a tiny nip, a slap to put the draconic thing in its place, but as soon as his teeth made contact everything suddenly became perfectly clear.

There was no reason for Tony to be carrying Noah’s heart. The fault was Tony’s, not the dragonblood’s. Despite all his grand feelings of unification and perfection, despite Noah ceding everything he was to Tony… Tony had never reciprocated.

Tony acted in perfect sync with Noah’s wishes, obeyed his every instruction, yes. Tony took command of the pieces Noah didn’t want to worry about. Tony augmented Noah’s existing abilities and shifted his master’s body far beyond its natural limits to the very edge of possibility.

But Tony had only ever subordinated himself to Noah. He’d accepted the offered unity and taken it as his due, his duty. He’d stretched his own limits again and again to please Noah and keep him alive.

Yet in all that deepening unity and increased power and control, Tony had never let go of what was Tony’s. Always an amplification, an addition.

The absurdity of it hit him in that one instant, Tony’s abyssal nature and Noah’s draconic nature fighting over who got to keep him alive.

That only happened because Tony insisted on remaining Tony. Even the rabbit evolved, changed its nature. Noah, too, changed over and over again. Noah was always ready to sacrifice who he is for who he could become.

Human, Modified Human, Astral Humanoid, Dragonoid Human. Each step made Noah more who he was becoming and less who he’d begun as.

Even thinking about becoming any less himself made Tony’s wings quiver, flapping harder to cover the tremor, but his conclusion was undeniable. Of everyone and everything belonging to Noah, only Tony had refused to change. He’d grown, adapted, but never changed. Never evolved.

Tony was always Tony. Nothing less, and nothing more.

If Noah were awake, were anything more than a conduit of sustaining life at the moment, Tony would have begged him for advice, for guidance.

If he’d been able to see this earlier, he could have done this already. Instead, Tony was alone, the weight of responsibility firmly on his own two wings, with no one and nothing to save him.

Tony closed his eye and strained mentally, dragging the Astral Script into being.

Name: Tony

Level: 54

Rank: Elite

Race: Void Summon (Fusion Symbiont)

Binding: Let your hunger be mine, and my blood be yours.

Life Command: Don’t eat everything, don’t destroy everything, don’t be bad. Listen to Noah, Noah is priority. Accept every command.

Abilities:

Life Binding

Metamorphosis

Void Predator

No. That wasn’t good enough.

Zax was a dragon.

Noah was a dragon.

Aurelia was a dragon.

Even the damn rabbit was a dragon.

Tony would not be left behind.

The life that flowed between him and Noah was never a physical thing. Unlike the rabbit, who required blood to act as a transfer medium, Tony was already infused throughout Noah’s body on a microscopic level.

Tony didn’t consume Noah’s blood because he lived inside it. Whenever they fused, Tony flowed through Noah’s body and remade it in beautiful, powerful ways.

Let my blood be yours. The offer Noah made, the call that he sent out to the void. Until now, Tony had made it his home.

Not this time.

All these thoughts burst through Tony’s mind in the instant he nipped at the stubborn Dragon’s Heart trying to fix Noah in its own way, and before he had time to second guess what he was doing, Tony opened his entire body and swallowed Noah’s heart whole.

Let your blood be MINE.

Tony’s awareness faltered as the bloodline bit back. A million fiery blades shredded him from the inside and it was all Tony could do to keep his body in one piece.

When he was first born, Tony had thought pain was a bad thing, but Noah had quickly taught him otherwise. Tony had been weak and frantic and alone, associating pain only with being attacked by others of his kind. But here in Erandir, where he had a body and not just a soul, things were different.

Pain was the only way to truly get stronger. Tony had felt Noah’s pain, helped it, augmented it, but had never truly experienced it himself.

Now he felt it, and this was a thousand times worse than he’d ever imagined.

How did Noah do it? Tony wanted to scream and flee, he wanted to curl up in a tiny huddling blob and hide until it was over.

What was he even doing? Burning himself alive on a reckless impulse he was too stubborn to let go of. Why? This wasn’t part of the plan. This wasn’t part of his nature.

But through endless seconds of torturous uncertainty, he still held on to his purpose.

The old Tony, the nameless creature he had been when all he did was roam the abyss and consume his kin, he would have retreated to find a less unpleasant meal. He’d have followed Noah’s instructions to the letter, enjoyed all the endless luxury of tasting life, but this?

Every instinct in Tony’s body screamed at him to submit. To subsume himself into the heart, reshape it from within, become a part of it as he guided its rebirth.

Noah is priority, not Tony. What is Tony doing?

Every moment that he resisted was agony on multiple levels. His body was burning and melting and struggling to exist at all, and at the same time, his mind was tearing itself apart trying to justify the impulse he’d committed to before he could think it through properly.

Tony’s very nature rebelled at the idea of change. Tony changed other things. Other things didn’t change Tony.

Tony was hunger. Noah was life. Tony did as Noah required and was rewarded with continued existence on this beautiful, abundant, joyous realm.

Lightning seared through Tony’s eye, through his wings, making each movement strain his muscles until he thought they’d burst. The Dragon’s Heart inside him refused to give any ground.

Tony kept flying, shaking, faltering, barely able to keep himself from falling to the ground, but there was no time for weakness.

Let your blood be mine. Let your blood be mine.

They had a deal. Noah’s blood had changed, but that didn’t mean it had any right to supercede Tony.

Tony was here first!

And yet, Tony still clung to his own nature. When the draconic essence of Noah’s blood tried to flow into Tony’s own veins, he panicked and shoved it away. Nevermind that he’d been the one to invite it in the first place. His desires and his instincts were at war and right now neither could gain any advantage.

He could only fly onward as the pain both inside his body and inside his soul grew stronger and stronger and stronger.

Until, finally, they reached their destination.

The crystal cavern Zax had shown Noah what felt like months ago was just as he remembered. The massive twisting crystal growth filled almost the entire cave, leaving only a few small spaces to crawl into. Far too small for a full-sized human, but plenty large for a Tony.

Tony, bulging from having swallowed Noah’s heart, was still small enough to flutter and squeeze his way inside the maze of crystalized mana.

Here, finally, he could relax. The power around them was too strong to be used, but it should also hide him from the one chasing them. Here, Tony could deal with Noah’s rebirth in peace.

Tony flopped down on the ground, still twitching uncontrollably from the stormy heart that continued to demand release or surrender.

It would be so easy to give up. Let the pain win. Subsume himself back into the subordinate role, let the bloodline claim precedence.

If they’d been fighting over anything else, Tony would have given in. Nothing was worth enduring this much pain and uncertainty. Nothing was worth sacrificing his very nature.

Nothing except Noah.

If Tony went through with this, then when his summoning finally ended and he was thrown back into the abyss, he would no longer be able to survive. His nature would be incompatible with Erandir and incompatible with the Void. He would be lost between worlds, insatiable, doomed to slowly starve no matter where he went.

Unlike the other Void Summons who came and went between this master and that, Tony would be bound to Noah for life. His own life, not his summoner’s, and a tiny piece of Tony rebelled at that sacrifice.

Tony could live forever, do his best within the standard limitations of a summoning contract, and spread his name far and wide. He would be a desired and feared ally, whole cults raised in his name, thousands brought to sacrifice before him…

Tony does not want thousands to be sacrificed. Tony wants NOAH.

With one final silent scream, Tony threw away the last remnants of the creature he had once been.

Tony would no longer be a creature of the Void. He would be, like everyone he loved, a dragon.

Bloodline integration complete. Race: Void Summon → Fusion Voidwyrm

Defying the nature of your birth, find yourself an inheritor of the powers of cosmic dragonkin. May your growth be as infinite as your hunger.

Tony turned his attention to the angry Dragon’s Heart still trying to burn its way out of him. For the briefest of instants, his hunger surged up in a whole new way. Tony had never experienced pride before, not the arrogance of a creature who knows all others to be beneath him, for Tony had always been a vulnerable thing. But for one moment, Tony understood that he’d fundamentally changed what he was.

He didn’t have to let Noah out. Tony could protect him forever, keep the endless fount of life as a part of him, without ever having to deal with all the stress or uncertainty of life and death struggles that happened on a daily basis.

In discarding his old nature, he’d also discarded the bindings that forced him into compliance.

In that instant, Tony saw everything he and Noah had done in a new light. Not as a default subordinate, but as an impartial observer. Every casual dismissal, every moment of neglect or mistreatment revealed…

And he found nothing but respect, companionship, trust, and… love. Tony had seen enough of the world through Noah’s eyes to know how summons were supposed to be treated. Chained and controlled, failsafes layered on failsafes to prevent any chance of escape or autonomy.

Noah trusted Tony from the start. Memory after memory, he treated Tony as a friend, not a slave. Trusted him with his life and heart and ambitions. What kind of monster could turn his back on that?

Tony’s eye split open again, plopping the heart out onto the crystal beside him. Tony’s whole tiny body was quivering with anxious excitement, fear and hope impossible to detangle within him.

His long, sinuous tail stretched out behind him and he had six wings now, an eyebat no longer but an eyewyrm.

Subordinate no longer, but he would not seek mastery either. Tony would be what Tony had always been meant to be: a partner. Symbiotic companion.

If Noah had ever bothered to look back into their summoning contract, Tony knew, he’d have dissolved the thing himself long ago. He probably didn’t even remember there was any such binding in place.

And Tony saw no need to bring it up. There was no need to bother Noah with trivialities.

Tony curled up around the sparking heart protectively, humming contentedly at the thought before he dissolved himself back into the heart that was all that remained of Noah. His brief delay and inner war had only cost them a few seconds, and their pursuer was still nowhere close.

Okay, new body, round three.

He pushed the extending blood vessels back into the heart for now as he mentally reconfigured the entirety of Noah’s body.

Tony could think of quite a few things he wanted to change this time around. He’d done his very best the first time, but he’d learned a lot since then. Perfection at E-Rank was not the same as perfection at B-Rank. Or in preparation for A-Rank.

Noah will be the greatest dragonoid ever.

Tony giggled to himself happily and got to work.
