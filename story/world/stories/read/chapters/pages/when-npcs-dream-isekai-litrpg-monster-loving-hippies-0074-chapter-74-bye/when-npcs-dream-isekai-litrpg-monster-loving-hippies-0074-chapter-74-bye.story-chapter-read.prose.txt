The already failing Hearthrock finally gave in. But I knew this wasn’t a big deal anymore. We had altitude and forward momentum. The balloons would slowly lose height, but like a glider catching the wind, we could drift the remaining distance. We were close enough.

We made it. But as most of our Hearthrocks broke, and those that didn’t were probably at the end of their life too, it was clear we were stuck here. We couldn’t possibly fly to another continent. Or hope to get to our old continent back. And since this was supposed to be a playerless continent, we couldn’t buy any magic equipment.

But that was trouble for another day.

For now, we made it. We got ourselves a new home.
