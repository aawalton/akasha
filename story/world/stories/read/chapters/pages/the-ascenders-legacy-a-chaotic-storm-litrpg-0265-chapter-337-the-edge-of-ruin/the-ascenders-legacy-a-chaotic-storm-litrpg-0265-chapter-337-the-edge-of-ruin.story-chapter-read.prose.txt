To approve a candidate for the position of High Elder within the Storm Sect, the path of such an individual must be imbued with the quintessence of every reigning High Elder. This imbuement is not ceremonial but absolute—an acknowledgment of alignment and shared purpose, ensuring that no High Elder may arise whose will diverges from the Storm's collective will. In cases of immaturity, instability, or uncertainty, the candidate may be imprinted by no more than half the reigning elders, granting provisional authority without full succession rights…

From The Codex of Thunder,

Authored by the First Seat Argen Vaelor, Founder of the Storm Sect

The next day arrived in a blur of grim purpose and gathering thunder as the sect prepared for war. Every stratum from elder to outer sect was visibly busy, voices rising as runic architects and engineers carried and positioned war machines—monstrous constructs of solidified storm essence that could shoot massive beams of lightning over great distances.

In the air, more crafters worked, constructing a lightning barrier so massive it painted the entire mountain range in an array of shimmering runes and rippling electricity. Communications and teleportation arrays were set up next, their runes pulsing in rhythm with the storm above.

All over the sect, crafters and merchants halved the prices of their goods, and the stench of bubbling herbs filled the air as alchemists pumped out potion after potion. The treasuries were thrown open, and every job paid double, providing disciples with the points they needed to strengthen themselves in preparation for the war.

And prepare they did.

The pubs stood empty. The bars were closed. The air itself was thick with anticipation and nervous excitement as disciples either rushed about their duties or ran around buying last-minute potions and treasures, after which they locked themselves in their apartments to train and meditate.

One such group of disciples included Rhetta, Yerxa, and Zarion.

After refusing Aodhán's offer to become his underlings, they had thrown themselves fully into their advancement, pushing themselves beyond their limits with every delve. Rhetta, in particular, was unwavering. Every time the others thought to rest, she refused, constantly meditating, training, and pushing herself to improve.

It had barely been a week since then, but even in that little time, the upswing in strength was undeniable. She had only gained a tier in that period, but the rush of power it provided seemed like a combination of the previous ten.

She was by no means an elite, not in such a short period of time, but she knew that if she kept at it, she had a decent chance of making it into the core sect, even if just by the skin of her teeth.

She sat within her seal chamber that morning, staring at the piles of potions and treasures she'd bought for herself in preparation. The treasures were only rare-ranked, but there were four of them, and for the outer sect, that was practically a fortune.

Inhaling deeply, she pulled the treasures closer and closed her eyes, beginning to meditate, determined to make one breakthrough or another before the day of reckoning came.

Similar scenes were occurring in the inner and core sects, with so many disciples advancing in preparation for the war. Seals were gained, new skills were created, ideals were fermented, and unique abilities were unlocked, but most importantly, bottlenecks were broken.

Despite the efforts of many to avoid gaining any new seals—as doing so meant missing the first day of the war—only a few people managed it, and before long, the screams of raging individuals filled the air as the twisted effect took their minds.

Aodhán, however, wasn't worried about gaining a seal and simply spent the day refining his Aspect and taming his willpower. After teaching the High-Elders his Miracle Rain skill the day before—a skill they'd all managed to replicate within minutes—Aodhán had gone to the library to check on Crixis and the others, a sting of betrayal eating at him.

He'd found over half of them already under the influence of the twisted effect, while the others sat serenely within their chambers, their advancement still far from finished.

Darien, Sophran, and Crixis sat firmly in the latter category, their bodies rippling as energy radiated from them in waves. Sophran and Darien had gained two seals so far, and the fact that they still hadn't succumbed to the twisted effect suggested they weren't done gaining seals yet.

Crixis, however, was in a league of his own.

He had gained three seals so far, and from the undulating energy radiating from him, Aodhán doubted he was done taking from the Origin Plane. His bloodline manifestation had turned out nearly as severe as Aodhán's, with his hair and skin taking on a dark amethyst hue.

Unlike Aodhán, though, he had no arcing lines across his body. Rather, his forehead was covered in shimmering hexagonal patterns that disappeared into his hairline, gleaming beneath amethyst strands mesmerizingly.

Aodhán watched him for a long moment before turning away, pleased with Crixis's progress. He had no idea how his path had changed Crixis's, but he knew it had, and he couldn't wait to find out what Crixis had chosen.

He'd left the library a few minutes after that and had proceeded to Elder Gumi's workshop to offer his assistance with the new batch of pendants. The moment he arrived, Elder Kobi had led him to a massive block of orichalcum ore, so tall it was nearly thrice his height.

Eagerly, he'd placed his hands on the ore to imbue it, the process of aura channeling easier now that he was familiar with it. He chatted with Elder Kobi and Sedgwick as he worked, describing all that had happened in the elder hall.

Unfortunately, neither had any answers or explanations to give him, seemingly just as confused by the High-Elders' actions as he was. Aodhán finished imbuing the orichalcum nearly an hour later. However, the moment he moved to begin unraveling the chaos treasures, Elder Gumi stopped him and ordered him out of her workshop, stating that she no longer needed his help for the project.

"But I was promised this opportunity." Aodhán had argued, pulling out the pass Elder Luseph had given him. "This is my reward for sharing the blueprints of the interface."

"And I'm not denying that." Elder Gumi stated firmly. "But you need to meditate and prepare yourself for the war. Unraveling chaos cores will do you little good. Go home, Aodhán. Train yourself."

And so he had spent the rest of the day doing just that, familiarizing himself with his old and new skills. Meticulously, he had tested each skill against Varéc, who'd returned energized after several hours of flying, trying to find ways to improve the power and effect of each skill.

He'd done that deep into the night, until he'd been too tired to keep his eyes open.

This morning, however, he had something very different in mind.

Settling down on the cultivation mat at the center of his training room, Aodhán decided to work on his essence threads technique as well as the manipulation of chaos.

The essence threads were as complex as ever, having grown even more numerous since his evolution to the advanced class. But with his control so improved by his new path, Aodhán found himself making quick progress through his torso and knees.

Essence threads that were already expanded received further refinement as a result of his improved control, while newer threads became easier to expand.

Three hours passed in that manner as Aodhán buried himself in the process, eyes closed and mind's eye focused on his spirit, until a notification ping pulled him out of his meditation.

Congratulations! Energy Enhancement Technique (Rough) >>> Energy Enhancement Technique (Good).

He let out a wide smile as the text crawled across his vision. It was about damn time. He'd been working on his technique for months now and had begun thinking it useless with how slowly it improved.

Still smiling, Aodhán decided to test out the effect of his improved technique. Energy hummed within him as it rushed through his pathways and into his essence threads, all of which lit blue as he reared his arms back and slammed his fist against the nearest wall.

At the forty-second tier, he was capable of causing significant damage to the rune-covered wall of his training room, as indicated by the small dent on the opposite wall where he'd tested his strength four hours ago.

This time, however, he wasn't just throwing a punch. He was throwing an energy-enhanced punch. And the moment his fist slammed against the wall, Aodhán felt the whole building reverberate in response. Runes flared, absorbing the energy of the punch like a swarm of hungry leeches, but they couldn't absorb it all, and when Aodhán pulled his arm back, a wide grin broke across his face.

Right at the point of impact, the wall had cracked, caving in so deeply Aodhán could see the layers of concrete within.

Varéc growled in approval, his tail swishing as he complimented Aodhán. Aodhán smiled. "Without the runes, I bet this whole wall would have caved in."

Varéc nodded, his massive head peering closer as he growled again, urging Aodhán to throw another punch.

Aodhán did. First, he threw a normal punch, then an energy-enhanced one, a wide smile on his face as the difference became clearer with each attack. With each use, he grew more familiar with his essence threads, and half an hour later, after dozens of tests, Aodhán was confident in using the technique in battle.

Smiling at his achievement, he decided to move on to the second part of his training schedule: chaos manipulation.

Aodhán was no stranger to the manipulation of chaos within his spirit. It was pulling it directly from the Origin Plane and into reality; that was the issue. The two times he'd ever actually done it had been by accident—first with the blood Phoenix, and second, against the Super Gnasher.

He wanted to learn to do it at will and perhaps even incorporate it into his fighting style.

Fortunately, the force of his spirit made it easy. He no longer needed to open his spirit to the Origin Plane or close his eyes in concentration. He could already feel the Origin Plane pressing against the fabric of reality at his thoughts, chaos bubbling in preparation to rip its way into reality at the slightest glimmer of intention.

Inhaling deeply, Aodhán let the feeling engulf him, the insights he had gained from unraveling chaos swirling in his mind. For nearly an hour, he simply sat there, acclimating himself to the feel of chaos and its eagerness as it pressed against the fabric of reality.

And then he pulled—no, thought of pulling—and chaos burst through, smoke-like essence rushing out to drench the atmosphere in the primordial essence of creation and destruction. He shut the breach before the chaos became too much to disperse, then pulled again and shut it.

He practiced that for a few more minutes, familiarizing himself with pulling and shaping chaos, a skill that proved itself as instinctive as it was dangerous. He shaped weapons and constructs, marveling at the speed at which they formed. However, unlike normal constructs, the chaos constructs were unstable, barely holding together for more than a minute before unraveling, twisting chaotically into something else as if they had minds of their own.

It was unreliable, and Aodhán realized why he'd never seen any elders floating or wielding constructs of pure chaos.

His time with Elder Gumi had taught him, however, that chaos could hold a shape. It could be solid. He just had to stabilize it, and as he scanned his status screen for a skill that could possibly do so, his gaze fell on his shared title and skill.

{Chaos Clad Butcher}: This is a title given to a familiar and its bond after a point of large-scale, chaos-infused destruction.

{Synchronized Carnage}: When you and your familiar fight in synchrony, your skills gain a coating of chaotic energy that deals greater damage to your enemies.

Both title and skill depended on him and Varéc fighting in synchrony, but Aodhán suspected the effect of the skill could be manually engineered. Every skill the system offered could be manually replicated, and for this particular skill, Aodhán suspected all he needed to do was a little layering.

Inhaling deeply, he gathered the motes of chaos still floating in the air and began layering them, not over any weapon, but over his left arm, watching as it coated his fingers and wrists like sludge. When it reached his elbow, he stopped and waited, observing to see if it would lose shape.

It did not.

His smile widening, Aodhán drew the arm back, turned his gaze to the opposite wall, and swung a punch, expecting a boom of thunder as the chaos amplified his power. Instead, the chaos dissipated uselessly in the air, seeming to even hamper his strength rather than enhance it.

Frowning in confusion, Aodhán tried again, only to receive the same outcome.

Varéc growled, suggesting an alternate strategy, and Aodhán nodded. Perhaps layering it over a construct would work.

Chaos ripped its way into reality at a thought, but this time it wasn't coating his skin. He summoned a bolt of crimson electricity, shaped it into a blade, and began layering it with chaos, inky black essence coating the blade until it was as black as midnight.

Then he swung it, anticipation brimming within him as the blade ripped through the air with insane speed, and—nothing.

Aodhán frowned in disappointment.

The blade had neither been enhanced nor hampered. It was completely ineffective.

"Why isn't it working?" he asked himself in frustration. What was he missing?

Varéc came closer, his scales just as dark as the blade, and a memory suddenly flashed through Aodhán's mind. It was a memory of his first minute within Abyssos, that moment when he'd summoned Varéc out of his spirit after several days of being cooped up.

He remembered it clearly—how Varéc had burst out of his spirit with chaos radiating off him in waves, his sinuous form so dark it almost seemed as if he'd been dipped in ink. A being born of chaos coated in more chaos.

That was the day they'd gained the Chaos Clad Butcher title and skill.

Aodhán's eyes widened as understanding dawned. He'd been approaching this all wrong. Layering chaos over an existing construct was like painting over a finished sculpture—it added nothing to the foundation. For the layered chaos to really take effect, it had to be woven in from the beginning. He had to create the construct with chaos.

Fortunately, that was easy to do.

Dismissing the crimson blade, Aodhán opened his spirit to the origin plane, breath hitching in pleasure as chaos rushed in to fill him to the brim. Energy rushed out of his core, merging seamlessly with the chaos in his spirit, and with the energy formed, Aodhán activated {Create Construct—Lightning}.

The crimson blade reappeared a moment later, only this time it was larger. Sharper. More solid.

Chaos layered it an instant later, rushing along its surface like ink until the whole blade was engulfed in darkness, with only small arcs of crimson gleaming through.

Exhaling softly, Aodhán tightened his fist along the hilt of the blade and swung it—and this time, the world answered.

There was no boom. No thunderclap. No dramatic explosion of force.

Instead, the space in front of him peeled.

The air split open along the blade's path, not torn apart by force but unmade, reality recoiling as if it had been cut at the conceptual level. A crescent of inky darkness surged outward, silent and absolute, devouring sound, light, and resistance alike.

Runes blazed in defiance, flaring so brightly they burned afterimages into his vision. But there was too much chaos. Far too much.

And an instant later, the wall didn't just crack—it shattered, concrete and reinforced stone exploding outward in a spray of pulverized debris. The protective runes held for a heartbeat, two, three—and then several gave out with sounds like breaking glass, their intricate patterns fracturing as they overloaded.

Aodhán slammed into the far wall hard enough to drive the air from his lungs, his vision swimming as dust and smoke filled the room. Varéc roared, his voice barely audible over the ringing in Aodhán's ears as rubble continued to rain down around them.

When the dust finally began to settle, Aodhán pushed himself up on trembling arms, his eyes wide with shock as he stared at what he'd done.

The entire opposite wall was gone. Damaged beyond what he'd thought possible from a single attack. He couldn't believe it. He couldn't believe a single skill had done this without quintessence or willpower.

Just energy and chaos.

Shocked, Aodhán glanced down at the rippling sword still in his hands—black as midnight—and slowly a smile began to form on his lips. If he could do this without his willpower or quintessence, then how much destruction could he wreak with them? How could he apply this to all his skills?

Excitement brimming within him as his imagination ran wild, Aodhán pushed himself to his feet, a wide grin on his face. The war was in a day, and if he could perfect this ability before then, he would truly become the Chaos-Clad Butcher.

Almost imperceptibly, his path hummed in satisfaction.

What was Ruinous Authority without ruin?
