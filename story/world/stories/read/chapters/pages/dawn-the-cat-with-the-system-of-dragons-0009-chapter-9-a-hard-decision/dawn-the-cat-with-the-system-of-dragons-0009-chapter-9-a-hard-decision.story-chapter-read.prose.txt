The day was bright. A beautiful red sun was visible on the horizon. The clear orange sky created a panoramic view around Sylvane. Birds were chirping everywhere. The cool weather made it a perfect time to start the day.

Elenora and Cinthia had just returned from the outer town after feeding the black cat. Elenora didn't waste time and started executing her plan.

“Cin darling, let's go to the lawn. Today, I’ll teach you about evolved animals.”

“Umm… let’s gooo… Then I’ll raise the kitty and help her evolve.” Cinthia didn't like studying, but this time, she was excited. She wanted to have the black cat so badly that she was motivated to study animals and their evolution process.

Elenora was finally able to coax her daughter to study more. This would let her send her daughter to school for the time being. “Hmm, that’s a good motivation.”

They soon reached their home. After getting freshened up, they both went to their lawn. Cinthia brought her small magic staff too. She thought her mother would teach her about magic and didn’t expect Elenora to start with the cat’s evolution theories so soon. Cinthia was excited.

“Now, let’s begin.”

“Umm…”

Cinthia only practiced attack magic all this time, had little to no knowledge about animals and evolved beings. “Cin, what do you know about animals and their evolution?”

“Animals live in the forest, then they become strong, and then they evolve.” Elenora got the answer she was expecting and sighed. Her daughter didn’t know anything.

Before getting started properly, she needed to know the extent of her daughter's knowledge, and only then she could start teaching her daughter.

“But how do they become strong? Can you explain?”

“Umm… Animals eat other animals and become strong.” Elenora finally understood that her daughter knew nothing and wondered if hiring a teacher was a good idea.

“Partially correct. Now tell me, do you know about Nexus?” She decided to start from the beginning and asked about the most basic building blocks of this world.

“Nexuses are small particles which combine with billions of other nexuses to become very, very, very big and form everything in this world.” Cinthia tried to answer in her own words.

“Very good. You learned that from your teacher, right?”

“Umm.”

“But do you know what it means?”

“Umm… It means very, very tiny balls combine and create everything. Like I use my elemental energy to create many tiny, tiny nexus balls and combine them to create a stone. And then I push them by creating pressure with elemental energy.”

“That's right... Cin, can you tell me what happens when you use up all your elemental energy?”

“After I use up my elemental energy, it gets filled again after some time.”

“But why does it get filled, and where does it come from? Can you tell me?”

“I don't know… the energy just gets filled after some time.”

(Hmm, so Hesper has not told her about this yet… Maybe he is going to teach her this later.) Elenora thought and decided to explain the theory properly so that Cinthia would know what she needed to do to get stronger.

Elenora decided to start by filling the gap in Cinthia’s knowledge and started asking the most basic questions. “Cin, do you know elemental energy is everywhere?”

“Oooo. Is it everywhere? Why is it everywhere?”

“Look, everyone, including you, me, Papa, the kitty, and your teacher, constantly emits elemental energy from the nexuses in our bodies. Then this energy stays in the environment.”

“Oooo…”

“When we use magic, we are simply manipulating the energy we emit from our body.”

“But Mama, what happens when we run out of our own energy?”

“We simply start absorbing a very, very high amount of energy from the environment.”

“Oooo, so when I use magic, I use my elemental energy, but after I finish my energy, I get it from the surroundings? But why absorb a very high amount?”

“To compensate for the rapid energy consumption during magic formation, our body needs more energy. You feel pain in your whole body after practicing magic, right?”

“Umm. It hurts after practice.”

“It hurts because during magic practice, your body becomes exhausted by spending and absorbing too much energy in a short time.”

“But does it mean that after we stop creating a magic formation, our body stops absorbing energy?”

“Yes, but our body does not stop instantly. Energy absorption only stops when our elemental energy is replenished and starts to overflow. That’s when our body starts emitting energy again.”

“So it hurts because I rapidly consume and spend elemental energy. And it stops when our body consumes overflowing energy.”

“Yes, Cin, that's correct… You’re indeed an intelligent girl.”

“Hehe,” Cinthia giggled.

“Now let's go back to my previous question. How do animals get stronger? Animals get stronger by constantly using their elemental energy and constantly absorbing elemental energy.”

“Ohhh… But Mama, you said earlier that everyone uses up their energy and gets energy from the air. So why do animals become strong, without practicing magic, and I have to practice every day? Can’t I simply use it and get energy like animals?”

“Great question, Cin. I’ll reward you with chocolate cake today.”

“Yaaay!”

“Now, now, focus here, Cin… You have to practice separately because you’re not in danger, and you don't need to constantly use all your elemental energy to stay safe. But animals are always in danger. They constantly use their energy to stay safe. This causes extra energy expenditure, and as a result, they keep absorbing energy from the environment. It’s like you are constantly doing magic practice all day without stopping, even when you start feeling pain. Can you understand the difference?”

“Oh, so if I constantly use my energy and get it back from the environment, I’ll be stronger?”

“Yes.”

“But why do I become stronger when I get the same elemental energy that I am spending?”

“Do you remember you said, everything is created with tiny, tiny nexuses?”

“Umm. Sir Hesper said everything is created with tiny, tiny balls called Nexus.”

“Well, when you constantly use your energy, the energy needs to be replenished properly. It starts overflowing for a short period before we start emitting energy again, right?”

“Umm…”

“What happens is that when the energy overflows for a short period, it gets accumulated in our body and forms new nexuses filled with excess energy. It happens all over our body. After accumulating for a long time, the nexuses start enhancing our inner muscles, bones, and organs. And then our ability to use energy increases. And because extra nexuses get created and enhance our body, we become stronger.”

“Mama, it is confusing. I don't understand.”

“Don’t worry, dear. The only thing there is to understand is that when you use magic, you spend elemental energy. Then your body starts replenishing energy from outside. After finishing the process, energy starts accumulating for a short time and forms extra nexuses that enhance our body. Your body becomes stronger when you repeat this process for many years.”

“Ooo, I understand now, but then how do animals evolve? Do animals use magic like us and get new nexuses?”

“Cin darling, elemental energy is necessary for everything, not just for magic. Magic is only one of the applications of elemental energy. Whatever we do, we spend our elemental energy, and it gets replenished from the environment. Even when you’re playing with Papa, you’re using a small amount of elemental energy, and it gets replenished later. And animals spend a loooot of elemental energy by overexerting themselves in the wild with physical activities like hunting, traveling long distances, etc.”

“Mama, does it mean they become strong without evolution?”

“Cin dear. First, tell me, what is evolution?”

“Evolution is when their bodies change and become bigger.”

“Correct, but their body don’t get bigger in one day. It gets bigger over a looong period of time. And change happens very slowly over many years. This change is called evolution.”

“Ooo, does it mean evolution simply means our body growing bigger?"

“Fuuh… haha… yes yes,” Elenora laughed after hearing a simple explanation.

“Mama, can I also evolve?”

“Yes, but you need to live like animals to evolve like them. Humans don’t need to evolve to become strong. We simply need to learn magic, and we’ll become strong. Although some people do live like that to become strong, most people don’t go through this process. It is not worth it. Life is too harsh, and many people die because of it.”

“Mama, does it mean that if I stay just like animals and also use magic, I can become the most powerful being in the country?”

“Of course, you can. And that is how the strongest people become the strongest. But can you stay with animals and live like animals while using magic?”

“Mmm… will I be able to eat pastries?”

“No, you’ll not be able to eat pastries if you live like animals.”

“Then I don’t want to become the most powerful. I want to eat pastries.”

“Hahaha… Don’t worry, darling, if you go to school, you’ll become the most powerful if you work diligently, even if you don’t live like animals.” Elenora chuckled.

“But then I’ll not be able to meet the kitty. Mama, I don’t want to go to school.”

“Don’t worry, darling, I’ll take care of the kitty in your place. And when you come home, you can play with her. Simple. That way, you’ll become strong enough to protect the kitty, and the kitty won't need to stay in a dangerous place. Isn’t that a good idea…”

“Umm.” Cinthia didn’t ask back this time; she only nodded. She wanted to protect the kitty. For that, she needs to become stronger.

Suddenly, Theodoric walked onto the lawn.

“El, are you finished?”

“Theo… you’re early… oh, yes, I'm finished.”

After Theodoric returned, he went straight to the lawn to practice his sword art, so he was able to listen a bit to what Elenora was teaching Cinthia. “Oh, but isn't teaching such things too early?”

“About that… I need to talk to you about it.”

“Okay, what do you want to talk about?”

“Cinthia, can you go inside? I need to talk to Papa.”

“Okaay,” Cinthia said, and she ran inside the house. Her studies were over for the day.

“So what do you want to talk about?” Theodoric asked with anticipation.

“Don't mind me being paranoid, but I don't want Cinthia to be involved with the cat, especially with such a dangerous being involved. So, I have decided to send her to the academy soon.”

“What will you do if Cinthia gets involved after coming back from the academy?”

“As long as I get assured that, that being, whatever it is, will not harm Cinthia directly or indirectly, I'll let her be involved with it. But before that, I will not involve Cinthia until I confirm this myself that, that being is not intending to harm anyone anyway.”

“But what does that have to do with sending Cinthia to the academy early?”

“Earlier this morning, when I went with Cinthia to give food, the cat was becoming closer to Cinthia, and it was being kind of… chummy with her. I could feel that if that being had bad intentions, it would be easy to harm Cinthia by manipulating beasts and evolved animals. Moreover, why did that thing show itself now? The way the tree was using space-manipulating magic and how untouched the ground was, I could tell that it had been living there for a long time, and who knows how long it has been there. It was probably born there and stayed there hidden. But now of all times, it showed itself. It's too fishy. It doesn't make any sense, no matter how much I think about it. Something is going on. So I'll be personally involved with the cat and that thing and send Cinthia away for the time being.”

“I guess you have a point there. Now that you mention it, it doesn't make sense why it would show itself now, when it could have shown itself a long time ago. And taking care of a cat is too convenient for a reason, for a being as strong as that… It must have met other animals, too, so why a cat and why now… Was it lonely? Hmm… But, sending Cinthia to Twin Moon Academy is okay? I mean, isn't sending her to the Royal Academy much better? It's far away and a safe place.”

“At first, I wanted Cinthia to go to the Royal Academy directly within the next two years, but she is not ready yet. Having only one attack magic is not enough to get her into the Royal Academy. Plus, if we need to send her to the Royal Academy, it's better to have experience with people, which she has none.”

“When are you going to send her?”

“After a month, the entrance exam will start. I'm planning to send her then. Until then, I'm not leaving Cinthia alone with that cat.”

While Elenora and Theodoric were discussing on their lawn, a being was observing them from afar and listening to everything.

(Those fools really think that I can't observe them from here… huuuuh… I have never been so offended in my life… I never wished to be involved with your town, but for the first time, you have made me want to decimate everything… huuuh… had a human not given me water, I would not have let your conversation even finish…)
