Just to address some valid questions...

The reason I keep my schedule "Daily Monday to Friday" is personal accountability. I am aware I miss that deadline at times, but that's the evil reality of working a full-time job.

I wake up around 7pm, I work from 8pm to 4:30am, get home around 5am, and then I write until about 10am-12pm, sleep, and do it all over again.

I usually need 8 hours of sleep *minimum* to function, so I try to be in bed by 10am at the latest, or even earlier if I'm truly exhausted. This means that, if I wasn't able to write at work (which is where I try to buffer myself), I fall behind on chapters.

It is what it is. If I changed my routine to "sporadic" or "updates as possible", it would cease to keep me pressured and accountable, and that's when I tend to taper off into low output.

So tl;dr, I genuinely appreciate the feedback here and in my DMs, but that's why I keep it as Mon to Fri.

Hope that gives you all some understanding!
