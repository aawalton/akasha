Yu Chun stood at the front of the box. She was not able to walk on the air like Wang Ren and I could, but I would ensure that her voice reached every single person present, no matter how loud she herself spoke. This was an important announcement.

The inner disciple who rose from the open competition would perhaps not hold as much sway as the first two, but even a healing-focused sect could not thrive with healing arts and combat alone. All professions were needed.

“There were many talented and impressive displays on the first day of the intra-sect competition. Those disciples who are not healers or fighters showed off their unique skills and talents, many of them perhaps deserving the spot of inner disciple. However, there can only be one.” The start of her speech was delivered methodically, with a tinge of trembling passion. The crowd’s attention was captured from the very first word.

Her beauty and the emotive expressions of her face were no doubt a large factor in that, but only a fool didn’t capitalise on their natural talents. Once the crowd was hinging on her every word, Yu Chun turned to address the disciples gathered on the stage below.

“Of all the disciples who participated, there were three who proved themselves to be more talented than the rest. Disciple An, Disciple Hu, and Disciple Shi, please step forwards. The rest of you congratulate your peers and leave the stage.” The three disciples did as she asked, each with comically differing reactions.

Shi An, who had played the zither for us, had a satisfied expression, but wasn’t overly surprised. It meant she had been confident in her skills.

Hu Deng, the youth who’d forged a Mortal grade sword for his display, seemed genuinely surprised to make the top three, but quickly suppressed his joy and maintained a stoic, masculine visage.

I chuckled at that—he was quite similar to Wang Ren. Many of the disciples were… Perhaps I needed to spend more time amongst them so that they didn’t take up all of my Senior Brother’s bad traits. Although I would be pleased if they emulated his good ones.

As for the third disciple—An Wei—he physically recoiled when his name was announced. He seemed shocked to be named amongst the top three, as though he’d had that little faith in his own display. Or perhaps it was simply that he’d witnessed the other disciples’ displays and felt lacking.

In any case, he celebrated with the other disciples as they streamed off the stage before joining the other two. It was a good thing he was only in third place—I felt that he might faint from shock if he received the position of inner disciple.

“Unlike the other competitions, where there was a clear winner, the open round was based on our decision as the judges. All three of you displayed talents that would benefit the sect and qualify you for a position as inner disciple, but only one can obtain the position. However, we have decided to reward all three of you in accordance with your performance and what you will bring to the sect in future,” Yu Chun explained both for the benefit of the crowd and the three disciples.

“An Wei, you displayed your knowledge of alchemy through pill refining. The pills you created, while not perhaps on the level that the Sect Leader could refine, were of decent quality and showed your potential in the field. You achieved the third position in the open competition. Your reward will be two year’s worth of resources in order to improve your alchemy skills to the next level.”

The boy bowed to his waist, eyes going wide at the mention of the investment. Two years of resources was a lot of money for an alchemist, especially a junior one such as An Wei who likely had little way of earning his own money besides selling his work to other disciples—most of whom were equally broke. “This disciple thanks the Elders and the Sect Leader for their generosity and trust. I will not let you down.”

Yu Chun simply nodded as the boy stepped back into line. She paused for a moment, letting the crowd cheer for him until their applause died a natural death. Then she turned to the last two. By specifying that An Wei had been in third place, she’d naturally created tension between them, knowing that they had been ranked.

“Deciding between Disciple Shi and Disciple Hu was extremely difficult for us. Both of the talents you’ve displayed will benefit the sect as you continue to grow, but in the end it was the Sect Leader who made the final choice. Disciple Hu, you achieved second place in the open competition for your weapon refining skills, please step forward.”

The boy winced a little, but quickly returned to his stoic demeanour. He then smiled widely and began to wave at the crowd as their cheers and applause grew in intensity. Finally, he stopped just in front of the other two and bowed to his waist towards the three of us. “This disciple thanks the Sect Leader for his consideration, and also the two elders. I hope to continue to grow under the nurturing canopy of the Grasping Life Sect and provide what I can with my meagre talents,” he said, loud enough for all to hear.

It seemed as though many of my disciples were not just talented, but also humble. It was a warm feeling. I felt that a part of my values were trickling down to them, even if it had taken a while to show. That he’d been frustrated at not winning outright was also a positive sign—it meant he truly cared about his artifact refining and had great pride in it.

“Your reward will be similar to Disciple An’s. Five years of resources invested in your refining talents, along with the opportunity to join the Sect Leader on an expedition to acquire rare resources for your profession,” Yu Chun declared. That made the disciples watching go wild, the cheers of the few hundred drowning out those of the crowd.

All of them were envious of such an opportunity. The disciple actually dropped to his knees and kowtowed once. “This one is not worthy of the Sect Leader’s favour. However, I accept it with gratitude.”

I was a little uncomfortable at the display, but I supposed I needed to get used to such things. Even for the most talented disciples in the sect, I was still a lofty existence that they couldn’t hope to match—not for a century or two, anyway.

Once he had stood up and returned to the line of three disciples, it was time to announce the winner. It was already obvious at this point, given that the third and second place had been announced, but there was a different weight to actually saying it than simply knowing it. Besides, given the extra rewards offered to the other two, many were likely wondering what Shi An would receive in addition to the position of inner disciple.

“Disciple Shi, step forwards,” Yu Chun intoned. The girl did so, her expression mostly schooled into neutrality, but the slight upward curl of her lips and the glint in her eyes betrayed her inner joy. “Your performance with the zither was not only moving for the soul and an incredible demonstration of your talents with the instrument, but a reminder of the beauty that cultivation can bring to the world when it is not purely focused on the destruction of others or advancement at any cost.

Yu Chun paused for effect, letting her words resonate with the disciples and the gathered crowds. The violence of cultivators was something mortals understood well.

“The reason you were selected as the winner was because not only did you display outstanding talent, but your contribution was one that benefited the main goals of the sect—the healing of the world and its people. The utility of your music is an area the Sect Leader wishes to research and he hopes that you will find new and innovative ways to apply it as you proceed along your path. Bow towards the Sect Leader, and when you raise your head, you will do so as Inner Disciple Shi of the Grasping Life Sect.”

The girl did as instructed, bowing to her waist and even clasping her hands in a martial salute, before raising her head and staring right at me. Her eyes brimmed with fervour and gratitude, making me satisfied with my decision.

She raised her head, looking towards Yu Chun. “In addition to the position of inner disciple, there is one more reward that Disciple Shi will receive for her victory in the open competition. In order to help progress her art further, the Sect Leader will have a new zither commissioned for her. A King grade instrument!” The Administrative Elder basked in the shock of the disciples and those in the crowd who could appreciate the value of such an artifact.

Most mortals would never even see a ‘Mortal grade’ artifact in their lives, let alone a Martial grade one. The vast majority of the crowd likely didn’t even understand what a King grade artifact represented—a tool that even a Foundation Building Realm cultivator would kill for.

“I will ensure that I live up to the expectations of Sect Leader Zhao and the two Elders. The zither was my life, and now it shall belong to the sect,” the girl replied. The response was becoming somewhat expected, but I could see she truly meant it. A girl after my own heart.

Much of what Yu Chun had said was for the benefit of the crowds, in order to put on a show. However, I was genuinely interested in the potential applications of music for healing. Even back on Earth, music therapy was a field that showed improvements for patients.

Here, I wondered just how far the art could be taken. The Celestial Jade Emperor had healed a crowd of dozens of thousands of people with the wave of a hand. When taken to the zenith, would a sufficiently powerful musical performance be able to achieve a similar, or adjacent, effect?

Until now, Shi An’s had used her musical talents to soothe the suffering patients and make their time in the treatment pavilion easier, bringing some joy in their lowest moments. However, I wondered if through working with her and providing her with a suitable instrument, could we develop a healing art that worked through a musical application?

It was a truly intriguing line of thought, another research task added to the seemingly endless list I already had. But, I would ensure I devoted sufficient time and effort to it in the coming years.

“Disciples An and Hu, please return to the waiting area. Inner Disciple Shi, remain on the stage for the closing ceremony of the competition,” Yu Chun ordered the three disciples, before stepping back and returning to her seat.

As the two disciples exited, they were passed by Xiao Cui and Pei Kexin, who were walking onto the stage to join their newly minted sister of the inner sect for the closing ceremony.

Yu Chun melted into her chair and wiped her brow. “Never make me do that again. I hate public speaking. I felt as though the gazes of the crowd were drilling into me. Don’t get me started on the disciples! It’s as though we’ve nurtured a cult. Their fervor and belief in you and by extension, the two of us, is absurd.” She glanced at the three disciples on stage once she finished venting. “Well, at least you managed to pick three excellent disciples to lead them. I have faith in what the future holds.”

“As do I. Let’s finish this competition. I want to get back to my research and cultivation, although it has been great to see the disciples’ growth,” I said, standing up from my seat and replacing Yu Chun as the speaker.

The three inner disciples stood on the stone stage beneath me, basking in the admiration of the crowds and their juniors. They had achieved something great over the past few days and proven their talents, but now they would be taking on much greater responsibility towards the sect. I wasn’t sure how they would handle it, but only time would tell.

“I would like to start by thanking all of the people of these lands for making the journey to our Million Flowers Celestial Peak to witness the competition. I put out a public invitation thinking that perhaps only a few from the local towns and cities would turn up to watch, but the outpouring of support has truly proven to me that what I want this sect to achieve resonates within the hearts and minds of every single person in this empire, from the weakest mortal to the strongest sage.” The crowd’s roars made the entire arena shake, as though caught in a sudden earthquake.

“Our disciples have shown their prowess in the healing arts, their dedication to martial excellence, even displaying their superiority to other local sects, and their talent in the various other professions that exist on the path to the pinnacle. From this competition, three young leaders of our future generations have emerged, forming the foundations of the Inner Sect. Disciple Xiao, Disciple Pei, and Disciple Shi, you are now people who your juniors will look up to for advice, an example of how to live, and even as an obstacle to surpass. I hope you live up to my expectations and those of the sect as you embrace your new positions. I expect great things in the future.” The three inner disciples bowed towards me, before standing tall in front of the crowd and the rest of the disciples.

“Each inner disciple will be granted a new residence further up the mountain than the regular disciples, along with an increased monthly stipend and some greater privileges. Remember that these are a reward for your efforts and dedication, and not something to abuse or take for granted. I trust in the strength of your characters. Return to your juniors and celebrate, for you have accomplished something great!” With a final bow, the three inner disciples departed the stage and returned to the stands to join the rest.

There was one final thing I needed to announce, something that had a high chance of further antagonising both the Soaring Sword Sect and Cloudy Falls Sect, but had to be done. In any case, the latter was distracted with their internal problems, while the former would be deliberating on my message sent back with their mutilated disciples.

“During the course of the competition, just a mere four days, an enormous makeshift settlement has sprung up around this arena. At first it was simply crowds gathering to watch and setting up temporary homes, and even some enterprising merchants setting up businesses. At first we had planned to take down the arena after the competition, but we have decided to not just leave the arena up, but also to formally establish the surrounding settlement as Blossoming Life City! It will come under the administration of the sect, but an official city lord will be appointed. It is my hope that talented artisans of all kinds will flock here to improve their skills and live in harmony with people of all kinds. There will be much work to be done in order to get the city in order, but rest assured that there is a place for anyone willing to contribute! Thank you for joining us for the first Grasping Life Sect intra-sect competition, and may your lives be long, plentiful, and filled with excitement!”

“One final note. In five years time, we will hold another intra-sect competition in a similar format, allowing at least three more disciples the opportunity to rise to the inner sect. I hope you will join us once more!”

With my speech concluded, I left the arena to the roars of the crowd. I stopped briefly in the box to let Wang Ren and Yu Chun know what they needed to do in the coming months—Yu Chun especially would be busy with running and setting up Blossoming Life City—before heading back to my house.

I had only just finished refining the first layer of my Fivefold Medicine Forge Physique and needed to acclimate to it, as well as learning what I could do now that it was finished. Not to mention the nearly endless list of research tasks to work on, among other things. Being a Sect Leader was a busy job, but it was a core part of my life now. A part of my path.

I would become the greatest healer in this world, and perhaps one of the disciples who followed after me would become even greater. Nothing would make me more proud.
