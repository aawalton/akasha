It was strange the manifestation was talking instead of trying to immediately kill us. I decided against making the first move when I saw that Wu Kangming didn’t draw his blade and Xie Ling’s spiritual pressure didn’t show any signs of attacking.

So, I thought about what the Dao of Beginning actually meant, but that was difficult when I didn't have a good framework for understanding any dao. It had been around a year since I woke up in a Cultivation World and began my cultivation journey, yet I still didn’t have a clear idea of which dao I was following. The World Tree Sutra was a cultivation method, it gave me a cultivation path to walk, but it didn’t decide which dao was right for me, it was on me to build my own philosophical understanding of the fundamental cosmic principles.

The Dao of Beginning though. What would that even look like as a cultivation path?

The most obvious interpretation was that it related to origins and creation. The beginning of things. The moment when something that didn't exist suddenly did exist. That first instant when reality shifted from one state to another. However that answer felt too simple, like I was only scratching the surface because cultivation daos were more than the literal meaning of words. It was the understanding of the fundamental truths that governed how the universe operated that made it so difficult to comprehend.

I thought back to when Sect Master Yuan had explained to me that daos could be weak and strong. Weak daos were external goals that could be achieved and then had nowhere else to go like revenge, power, or fame. Strong daos were internal principles that could grow eternally because they weren't tied to specific outcomes.

So, if the Dao of Beginning was a strong dao rather than a weak one, it had to be something deeper than starting things, in one way or another it had to connect to the fundamental aspects of existence and be able to be explored endlessly without ever reaching a final conclusion.

The thing that came to mind was potential. I’m talking about the moment before action when all possibilities existed at the same time. That instant of pure potentiality before choice collapsed the infinite into the specific.

On the other hand, it could be about cycles. The beginning that led to an ending that led to another beginning. The eternal return of all things through repetition and renewal. And I thought of that because Yuan Shi had been a member of the Sect of Eternal Cycles.

"The Dao of Beginning is about firsts." It was Xie Ling who broke the silence. "It could be the first breath, or the first anything, it doesn’t matter because everything has a beginning. If you can understand that moment of initiation, you gain power over what follows."

"That's a very basic understanding," Yuan Shi said, his smile turning sad. “You can see the surface meaning but haven’t been able to touch the deeper truth that is waiting for you behind it.”

With that, he turned to Li Na, who fidgeted under his gaze.

Realising that the ancient cultivator wasn’t going to look away, she began speaking.

"The Dao of Beginning is about creation, bringing new things into existence, like how my bloodline technique lets me start fresh by transferring my injuries to someone else."

"That is better," Yuan Shi said, nodding, which brought a pleased look on the girl’s face. "You've moved beyond simple identification and touched on the active aspect of beginning, but you're still thinking too small.”

The smile was wiped off Li Na’s face.

“The Dao of Beginning isn't about individual moments of creation or fresh starts, and it’s not a tool for resetting circumstances or avoiding consequences." Yuan Shi turned his attention to Wu Kangming next. “What about you? What do you think?”

"The Dao of Beginning is about the moment before manifestation," the sword cultivator began. "The space between non-existence and existence where potential hasn't collapsed into actuality yet. If we can understand that moment, we could understand how reality emerges from nothing, how form arises from void, and how the many become one and the one becomes many."

"This…this is better." The smile returned to Yuan Shi’s face. “You've touched on something fundamental about the nature of reality and transition, but…”

It was the ‘but’ that made us frown.

“You're still not quite there because you’re describing the mechanism without grasping the principle,” he continued. “You’re distracted by the what and can’t see the why."

Honestly, all this dao talk was difficult to follow, especially since I’d never been talented in philosophy, which was coming to bite me in the behind now that I was living in a cultivation world.

“Why are we wasting our time talking?” Xie Ling’s eyes narrowed as frost began spreading around her. "We could end this fight in seconds with the girl’s bloodline."

I could understand why she was frustrated but sometimes the trial itself was an opportunity to learn, and honestly, I didn’t get many chances to understand a dao, so I couldn’t just pass this one. Also, if we could finish this step without fighting, we might as well give it a try.  There were more ways to win a battle other than brute force, especially in a cultivation trial.

"We only have seven uses left," I replied with the reason she would understand. "The other steps will be more difficult than this one. We should save the technique for when we actually need it rather than using it out of impatience."

"Shen Bolin said that technique won't work on Shen Mohai anyway," Li Na added. "He's at step ninety-nine and he's way beyond what the Nine Deaths Transfer can handle. So, I think we should start using the technique to clear stairs ninety-two through ninety-eight. That's seven steps."

Fortunately, we were able to get through to Xie Ling, she allowed the frost to recede.

"The others have said their interpretations," Yuan Shi said, giving me an expectant look. "What are your thoughts on the Dao of Beginning?"

The others had provided reasonable explanations based on their understanding and Yuan Shi had found all of them lacking, so what could I say that would be any better?

“Azure,” I thought internally, “what do you think the Dao of Beginning means?”

There was a brief pause before Azure replied.

"Master, if you think about it logically," he said, "the Dao of Beginning relates to the initial conditions that determine what comes after it. In mathematics it would be the starting values in a differential equation that constrain how the system evolves over time. In cultivation it might be the foundational choices that shape everything that follows.”

I nodded, but for some reason I didn’t think this was the answer the ancient cultivator was looking for.

“Master,” Azure continued, “my comprehension of dao principles can't be compared with humans because true understanding needs something I don't have."

That made sense since Azure was like an artificial intelligence in how he could process information, identify patterns, and provide useful insights. But comprehension of fundamental cosmic principles required intuitive grasp that went beyond calculation. This was why it was mainly humans that walked the dao path, especially at a high level.

I tried to think about my own experiences. As a transmigrator, I'd died in one world and been reborn in another. I'd lived multiple lives across different realities with completely different rules and physics and spiritual systems. That experience of moving between worlds gave me insight into something fundamental about beginnings that someone who'd only ever existed in one place couldn't fully grasp.

"The Dao of Beginning is about the moment when nothing becomes something, but not how Li Na described it," I said. "I think it is about the fundamental transition from potential to actual, from abstract to concrete, and from idea to manifestation."

Yuan Shi’s expression told me that I had his full interest now.

"When I dream walk, there’s always a moment of transition," I continued, "a space between realities where I exist but also don't exist. It’s where I’m in the middle of leaving one set of rules behind but haven’t yet adopted the new ones. That moment is the purest expression of beginning I've ever experienced. Beginning is about the instant when reality shifts from one state to another and when the possible becomes the real."

Wu Kangming nodded since it was similar to what he had been trying to say since he'd experienced something similar with his multiple identities. The transition between being Tian and being Wu Kangming. That space where he was both and neither simultaneously.

"The Dao of Beginning is about the nature of beginning itself. The fundamental principle that allows anything to start existing in the first place. Understanding that principle means understanding how reality emerges from void. How consciousness arises from nothing. How the universe itself came to be."

"No dao has a single correct answer," Yuan Shi smiled. "Different people will understand the same principle in different ways based on their experiences and perspectives. But there are some answers that are more powerful than others. Some interpretations that touch on deeper truths and open paths to greater understanding."

He looked at each of us in turn before continuing.

"You've both touched something real about the nature of transition and emergence," he said to me and Wu Kangming, “that's worth acknowledging, but I have a different understanding."

"What Freezes Cannot Escape," Xie Ling declared as her patience broke completely.

Her spiritual pressure exploded outwardly causing ice formations to erupt across the entire step in geometric patterns that suggested she had activated her law. A massive lance of compressed ice shot forward like a cannon.

Yuan Shi just stood there with that same pleasant smile on his face as the lance hit him directly in the chest and erupted from his back in a spray of blood and spiritual energy before continuing another twenty meters and finally shattering against the black stone of the step.

He looked down at the massive hole in his chest, an injury that would have killed any normal cultivator, and laughed. "Let me show you my interpretation of the Dao of Beginning."

The wound in his chest began closing as if time flowed backward for his body specifically while everything else continued forward normally.

"In the beginning, there is nothing," Yuan Shi whispered. "Then there is something, and then there is everything. The Dao of Beginning encompasses all three states simultaneously. It is the void before creation, it is the spark of creation itself, and it is the infinite expansion after creation begins."

The blood that had spilled out of the wound flowed back into his chest and the torn flesh knitted itself together after it. Hell, even the spiritual energy that had been scattered by Xie Ling's attack entered his body and merged back with his cultivation base.

"Every moment is a beginning. Every breath starts fresh. Every heartbeat is the first heartbeat of the rest of your existence. To truly comprehend the Dao of Beginning means understanding that nothing is ever truly over because everything is constantly beginning again."

As if to make it easier for us to understand, his body flickered and multiple states of existence overlapped in the same place at the same time. There was a young Yuan Shi, an old Yuan Shi, a damaged one, and a whole one, and phases I couldn’t understand at my limited level.

"I have died hundreds of thousands of times since becoming a manifestation in this palace,” he continued as his form stabilised on the whole version. “Each death was an ending while each resurrection was a beginning. Fifty thousand years of experiencing death and rebirth is a good method of learning that endings are merely beginnings from a different perspective."

This guy…

Even though he had a soft smile and spoke about philosophy, with the ability to regenerate casually from a Law-level attack, it meant that he was a serious threat that we couldn’t underestimate. All of our eyes narrowed because we understood what time it was. We'd tried talking, we’d tried understanding, and now we would fight.
