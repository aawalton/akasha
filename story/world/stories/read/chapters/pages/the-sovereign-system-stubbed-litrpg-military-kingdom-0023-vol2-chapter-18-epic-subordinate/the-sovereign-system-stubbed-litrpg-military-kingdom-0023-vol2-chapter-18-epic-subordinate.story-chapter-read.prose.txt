Luke’s heart pounded as he gazed at Victoria. Her genuine smile, framed by limpid brown eyes and a tear-streaked face, painted an ethereal picture—one that would be etched into his memory forever.

He was so captivated by the sight that he didn’t notice the flashing system prompt hovering before him until several seconds had passed.

“Turn around, Young Master. I’ll wash your back,” Victoria said softly, brushing away a lingering tear from the corner of her eye.

“You can call me Luke when we’re alone,” he replied, a small smile tugging at the corner of his lips.

“Mmm, I’ll do that,” she said, her voice light with affection. “Now hurry, before the water gets cold.”

Luke turned away, obeying her gentle command. As he faced the wall, he fought to steady his breathing. Standing this close to the real Victoria—no masks, no personas—was far more disarming than he’d expected.

Just as he began to collect himself, he felt delicate yet purposeful hands glide across his back. He flinched at the contact. Then came the soothing scent of floral soaps, wrapping around him like a calming breeze and quieting the storm within.

While Victoria focused on washing his back, Luke silently accepted the system prompt, unwilling to lose this chance.

Sovereign’s Mask (Passive): Easily hide one’s emotions and intent. Projects an idealized version of oneself based on the viewer’s subconscious desires or fears.

He didn’t need long to decide.

Nothing beats Sovereign’s Mask, he mused.

The other two had merit, but Luke was navigating a civil war simmering beneath the surface of the kingdom. At every turn, traitors lurked. Trust was a luxury he couldn’t afford.

Sovereign’s Mask would allow him to mask his true intentions and manipulate how others perceived him—perhaps even make allies out of enemies. When survival was on the line, Luke wouldn’t hesitate.

Even if it meant passing up other powerful abilities, he’d do so without regret. Besides, any skill with “Sovereign” in the name had to be valuable.

Without fanfare, he made his choice.

Four more went into Strength, and the final point into Endurance. It made more sense to enhance what she was already good at, rather than trying to create a one-size-fits-all soldier.

“I’m finished… Luke,” Victoria purred behind him, her voice velvety.

His breath caught at the sound of his name spoken so tenderly. He closed the system window and turned around slowly—only to be greeted by the sight of Victoria’s bare back, glistening with moisture under the warm light.

This was the first time in either of his lives that he’d ever washed a woman’s back. A sudden wave of nervousness swept over him, and he gave a quick shake of his head to snap out of it.

I’ve stormed Xiu Fortress with a handful of men… faced down a thirty-thousand-man siege… survived countless brushes with death—so why am I getting nervous now? he grumbled inwardly.

Grabbing a bar of soap, he gently pressed it to her skin. Dried blood lingered near her shoulder blades—residue from earlier bloodshed that had soaked through her robe. He began scrubbing, though a bit awkwardly.

“Slow down a little,” she murmured. “Let me enjoy the moment.”

Luke froze, realizing how clumsy he’d been—like a soldier fumbling through his first dance. Compared to the soothing touch she’d given him, his efforts were practically brutish.

He let out a breath, steadying his hands and calming his thoughts. There was no need to rush.

Soon, the awkwardness began to fade. His motions became more natural, his grip more measured. As the silence settled between them, he decided it was time to share something important—something she needed to know.

After all, she was now his subordinate. If Victoria entered a fight unaware of her enhanced capabilities, she could misjudge her own strength or speed—possibly fatally.

“I have something to confess,” Luke said softly.

“Mmm?” she replied, her tone light.

“I have a blessing,” he explained. “It allows me to empower the people close to me… grant them abilities that are almost superhuman.”

Victoria let out a soft, amused chuckle. “I know.”

Luke blinked. “You do?”

She nodded ever so slightly. “Gathering information is my specialty.”

He scratched his cheek awkwardly. “Right. Well both your strength and speed have now been enhanced by me. It might take a little while to get used to.”

There was a brief pause.

“Thank you,” she said.

Luke’s eyes widened, then softened. A quiet laugh escaped his lips.

“You’re welcome.”

After dinner, Kayson and Sebastian joined Luke and Victoria in his room to discuss the day’s events. By now, the former two were brimming with questions, having held them in until now.

“The Black Tiger Gang?” Kayson echoed, raising an eyebrow.

Once Luke explained what he had learned from the assassins, Sebastian fell into deep thought, rubbing his chin between his thumb and forefinger.

“I’ve heard of them before,” he said. “They run the brothel in the city.”

Kayson’s nose wrinkled in disgust. “Why do you know about the brothel, old man?”

Sebastian shot him an exasperated look. “Hilda is working there at the moment.”

This time, both Luke and Kayson shared a look, their expressions equally disturbed. The mental image of the burly woman serving customers in such a place was almost too much to bear.

“She’s employed as security, you idiots,” Sebastian snapped. “It’s not uncommon for mercenaries to take on that kind of work when passing through unfamiliar cities.”

Luke exhaled in relief. “Can we trust her to gather intel on the Black Tiger Gang? I want to know who’s pulling their strings—or at least who they’re working with.”

Sebastian’s face twisted into a grimace. “That might be difficult. Even if she tried to be discreet, she’s… not exactly inconspicuous.”

You can say that again… Luke thought wryly.

“I will do it, Young Master,” Victoria said, breaking her silence.

Luke turned to her sharply, a pang striking his chest. Earlier that day, he might have been willing to ask her to infiltrate the Jade Silk Pavilion—but not anymore. Not after learning about her past.

Now that she had trusted him enough to open up, the idea of sending her into that kind of environment made his stomach turn.

“I’m against it,” he said firmly.

Victoria offered a small smile, lips parting as if to protest, but Luke raised a hand and cut her off.

“There are other ways to get information—ways that don’t involve anyone joining the brothel,” he said, shifting his gaze to Kayson.

The young man frowned. “Why are you looking at me like that? Do I look like someone who needs to go to a brothel?”

Luke’s eye twitched. The guy wasn’t wrong. Though Kayson didn’t hail from a particularly powerful house, his ridiculous good looks alone had made him popular wherever he went.

“There’s no one else,” Luke said with a shrug. “I’d go myself, but I’m the one they’re trying to kill.”

Kayson let out a frustrated sigh. “So what? You want me to just waltz in and start asking about the Black Tiger Gang? That’ll make me look really suspicious.”

“Not at first,” Luke replied. “Just visit casually. Have a few drinks, get a feel for the place, and see what you can pick up. Who knows, maybe one of the courtesans will take a liking to you?”

He gave Kayson a mischievous smirk.

Kayson looked anything but amused. In fact, he seemed downright uncomfortable. He hesitated, then sighed in defeat, shoulders slumping before he begrudgingly agreed.

“Where are we supposed to get the money to fund this ‘noble infiltration’ of yours?” Kayson asked dryly. “And if you even think about telling me to settle for the cheapest woman in there, I swear I’ll shove my leather boot so far up your ass you’ll be chewing on laces for a week.”

Ah, shit… I didn’t think about that, Luke cursed inwardly.

He turned to Sebastian. “Do you have any funds?”

“Young Master,” Sebastian replied, raising an eyebrow, “didn’t the king grant you a stipend when he conferred upon you the title of Viscount?”

“A stipend…”

Luke frowned, casting his mind back to that day in the throne room. “He mentioned that I’d be given land upon graduating the Royal Academy. But as for actual money? There wasn’t a single coin involved.”

“So until you graduate,” Kayson said with a smirk, “you’re essentially just a broke noble.”

Luke scowled. “You’ve been a noble far longer than me—shouldn’t you have some wealth saved up? Don’t you feel any shame?”

Kayson held up his hands. “My father emptied the treasury funding supplies for the Xiu Fortress campaign. The rest went toward paying the troops who defended Clayton City.”

Luke scratched the back of his head in frustration. “I was a deputy general. Why didn’t I see even a sliver of that money?”

“There, there.” Kayson gave his shoulder a consoling pat. “Just think of all the lives you saved. Isn’t that reward enough?”

While the two bickered, Victoria quietly walked to a nearby chest and retrieved a pouch. Without a word, she returned and handed it to Luke.

“I’ve got around thirty gold coins here,” she said matter-of-factly. “That should be enough to fund Kayson’s brothel visits for at least a month.”

Both Luke and Kayson snapped their gazes toward the pouch, eyes drawn to the satisfying clink of weighty coins.

“Where did you get this money?” Luke asked, stunned. He hadn’t expected the woman who’d been pretending to be a servant to be wealthier than all three of them combined.

“You don’t need to worry about that,” Victoria replied, her tone calm and unbothered. “It’s enough to start the investigation. But we’ll need more funds soon.”

Luke nodded and took the pouch from her. “We’ll use half for Kayson’s operation,” he said. “The rest, I’ll invest in another venture.”

His words drew a few curious glances, but no one asked for details.

“Anyway, I need rest. It’s been a long day. Tomorrow, I’ll likely be staying at the palace.”Immediately, both Sebastian and Kayson began to protest.

“It’s too dange—”

“I’ll be taking Victoria with me,” Luke said, cutting them off. “She’s been blessed by my ability now. Honestly, I doubt either of you could take her in a fight.”

Faced with that reality, the two could only grumble and reluctantly nod before making their exit—leaving Luke and Victoria alone once more.

“Goodnight, Young Master,” Victoria said with a soft smile.

To Luke’s surprise, she walked calmly to her own bed, slid under the covers, and turned her back to him without another word.

He let out a small sigh of relief—only to feel an unexpected pang of disappointment.

Am I a masochist? he wondered.
