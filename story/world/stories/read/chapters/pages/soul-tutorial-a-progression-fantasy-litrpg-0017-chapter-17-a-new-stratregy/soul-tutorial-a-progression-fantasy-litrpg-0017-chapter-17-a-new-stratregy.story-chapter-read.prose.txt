Some time had passed as he focused his attention on a pressing matter. The current development of his soul, while the white color has flourished a bit more. He had to recover as much strength as possible for a fighting chance against the opponent on floor 3.

But it all depended on whether Luke could handle the pain of using soul energy afterwards. The pure color of his soul still held strong, which lessened his worry only a little. But he wasn’t the one who got rid of the darkness trying to claim his soul; it disappeared on its own.

Shaking his head in frustration.

Too much was happening at once.

My problems are all over the place; what should I even focus on first? He thought.

Luke needed strength, which brought him back to the points he had gained earlier.

Thoughts filled his mind, and he saw this as an opportunity to gain some insight. While trying to discover a workable plan, he furrowed his brow, but it proved futile as he had many tasks to complete right away.

He needed to figure out the most optimal way to invest his stats and what his secondary option should be if his soul energy was unexpectedly unavailable. Recalling that all this strength related to souls wasn’t fully his.

What would happen if he escaped this place but couldn’t access the souls anymore?

Annoyed, Luke kicked at the barren ground, completely forgetting his current goal of fixing his soul, before he would challenge floor 3. Assuming floor 0 was his initial stepping stone, floor 1 presented him with a challenge requiring him to adapt; then floor 3 could show the significance of attributes.

He was under even more pressure than he first imagined, and that’s without even accounting for the potential appearance of an elite slime.

These floors are all attempting to train him, but what about those who couldn’t or wouldn’t survive this place? Did their fate truly conclude with their demise in a barren place such as this?

Wondering what challenge people on Earth had to endure, maybe nothing at all. Nevertheless, he couldn’t really do much to change Earth's fate or the others'; he couldn’t even handle his own problems.

So, wasting no more time, he went back to check out his core and summoned the souls to come towards him. To see if that would make any difference, but actually it put a pressure on his soul he didn’t quite see before.

So the souls had some effect on him if it only just nestled itself inside his soul, but what else could he even do. Shifting them around in some random way did little, just causing him to feel more discomfort.

It felt like rusted gears trying to turn each other, and it bulged as it tried to gain some speed. But he didn’t push it as he would in a battle, but a slight motion that would allow him to see the process.

His soul didn’t get darker, thankfully, but his golden streaks had turned almost crimson red again, gaining a bright luster.

But it wasn’t bad compared to the pain he was in, so he tried his best to handle the slight discomfort, sending shivers down his spine.

Then he finally got a glimpse, the golden lines that were beautiful to look at now had a red luster and started to shine, growing larger, struggling to branch out into his own soul, causing darkness to slowly seep in from the blood-colored lines. As the darkness fought against the dark, it wasn't destroying his soul but taking root, changing it slowly before his very eyes.

Wondering how the golden line turned red, many ideas developed in his head.

Could the souls themselves provide power but also corrupt his very being? Or was it the properties of the slime that affected him?

But it’s not like Luke can just stop using soul energy, as it was currently his only source of power.

So now it all came down to finding some alternate path to this whole soul thing, he thought while scratching his chin.

At least he figured out the problem, stopping the souls in his chest. The energy settled in his body as he disregarded the power coursing through him.

He would not be held back, despite this. This was merely the beginning, and this minor setback was something he would overcome.

Feeling fortunate after finding out about this disturbance, wondered why this hadn’t happened at some earlier point in his journey so far.

He felt he used a lot more energy with the elite slime than he did previously, but maybe he didn’t. He hadn’t rotated the energy in a way which made him use it properly; just forcing the energy into the fist might have just let most of the energy disappear through the surroundings.

Maybe that’s why the heal had cost so much back then. He thought it was just normal wounds or that parts of his body were missing from the explosion. It was the internal wounds that had taken place because of the misuse of soul energy.

But there was more to it than that; the exhaustion he still felt after the restore could only relate to the soul exhaustion he had experienced, but on a much smaller scale. There were early signs of his misusing soul energy, but he just never noticed.

Everything was clear now. His own actions have led to disadvantages, as seen in the elite slime situation and the ongoing fallout from using such energy in a fight.

Perhaps he exerted a bit too much energy in dispatching it swiftly. However, he knew that keeping some souls within him still provided him passive power, so what if that were the case?

Wouldn’t the passive empowerment be the best way around this?

Only needing to rotate it under special conditions that call for it, such as elite slime or him nearing his death. It would be a lifesaver, while it might bring back some pain. Surviving was still a better cost than completely dying, so he saw it as a worthy deal, especially since he needed to be alive to find his mother.

He could tackle this piece by piece, focusing on getting through the present moment. So while waiting for his soul to recover once again, especially after his brief experiment, he went to the pedestal to differentiate between his points.

Currently, constitution was the second-biggest contributor to his strength. This enables him to face confrontations more effectively and eases the concern of needing to trade a hit from the slime for a lethal strike.

Constitution was the best way to move forward besides soul resonance at the moment; maybe that would even help his soul recover faster, if thinking of his body as a vessel for all that power.

Especially the fact that he had also gotten a title that affected that very attribute made him think it was comparable to soul resonance.

So with no regrets, he leveled up Constitution twice.

An itching sensation filled his being, and his bones felt heavier and stronger. His skin felt rougher to the touch, like everything about him felt more refined, while still having the same mobility he had before.

He clenched his fist, feeling a difference immediately. As if his body wasn't his own.

But Luke disregarded it as the effect of the increased constitution. Hoping to notice the full effect when he would go up against the green slime acid.
