Trix was right. We couldn't fight this without help outside the Meadow. The Ambers and few higher ranked Reapers here wouldn't be enough to defeat this threat. And the higher ranks who had a chance to aid, like Jethro, probably weren't anywhere near Meadowvale—maybe not even in Penumbra at the current hour.

Someone joined me atop of the colosseum. I wasn't sure how she made it up, but Trix placed a hand on my shoulder. Her Sapphire cloak billowed in the breeze. "I can try to make it out. Our best bet is the Meridian. It's close by and there will be the most high ranked Reapers from outside the Meadow there."

I shook my head. "You're probably the highest rank Reaper in the city right now. We can't afford to have you gone. Hold them back as best you can. Rally and support the people here. I'll get to the Meridian."

"There's only one way out."

I stared out at the destruction happening at the lone gate to the city. "I know…"

Nuzzle let out a low, sad whistle.

Trix nodded, pulling out her whip. "Alright then. I can at least give you a distraction."

I darted between buildings, hiding in shadows as best I could, pulling my horse along behind. I don't know what Trix did, but something drew the attention of the Unchained and several Reapers away from the gate. Most sounds of battle came from my left or right.

Pirhani remained at the gate though. She'd been joined by the Iron-cloaked Reaper I saw with Silas in the cave. The puppeteer.

Defeating him would probably release the Unchained from whatever hold he had on them, but at this point they were already in the city. The damage had been done and the rampage would continue even if he was out of the picture.

And who could defeat him? Lancelot was a Diamond rank, but even he was below this bastard. It'd take a coordinated, high rank army.

Either way, I didn't have a chance in hell of getting through the gate past them. I'd hoped I could just ride out, but that wouldn't be happening now.

There was a clear path to the wall to my left. I tied the horse to a fence and maneuvered to it, finally stopping at a point far enough from the god that I wouldn't be noticed—hopefully.

I hadn't really given the wall a second thought the other times I'd entered or exited the city, but now that my task was to scale the damn thing, its towering height truly dawned on me.

The stone blocks were tightly packed and mortared leaving little in the way of hand or foot holds. I did still have a decent amount of Volition left…

Can I Aero Shift all the way up there? I'd never gone that far before, but I'd never tried either. Only one way to find out.

I bent my knees, preparing to jump as high as I possibly could. I wouldn't overshoot the top. Right..? Aero Shift activated and I shot through the air. I could feel the wall whooshing past me as I rose in the blink of an eye. Then it stopped.

The top of the wall was right there, a hand's breadth away. I reached out to it.

And I was falling.

I held in a scream, scrambling at the stone facade with my hands and feet to no avail. Shit shitshitshit.

I used Aero Shift again, picturing the top of the wall in my mind. This time luck was on my side. I exited the Shift right at the edge and grabbed it, pulling myself up and rolling to safety. My breath heaved in my chest—more from fear than exhaustion. But I'd done it.

I peered over the edge toward the gate. It thankfully didn't appear Pirhani or the Iron Reaper had noticed me.

Escape was in my grasp.

Except it wasn't.

A shimmering in the air split the top of the wall in half, imperceptible from down below. I could see now, it travelled the entire length of the wall and rose up into a dome. My hand stopped firm when I touched it, as if a solid barrier barred my passing. I tried Aero Shifting through it, but was again stopped.

Well, fuck… What now?

I didn't really have any other options. There was only one way in or out of the city. The gate waited below, the god of the Jungle and her Iron-rank Reaper standing before it.

I took a deep breath and dropped from the wall, sliding against the stone as best I could, then kicked into a horizontal Aero Shift away from the structure and landed on a nearby roof. The climb down from there was a little precarious, but I accomplished it without incident, trying to reserve as much Volition as I could after wasting so much to ascend the wall.

Slowly, I inched my way closer to the gate, horse in tow again. Trix's distraction couldn't possibly last much longer. If I didn't get out we were royally fucked.

In hindsight, we probably should've found someone in the colosseum who could phase out and return at the Meridian. Or who had some sort of teleportation item. In the chaos of the attack, it hadn't even crossed my mind.

Hopefully, if this didn't work Trix would find another solution.

From my hiding spot, I could see the Iron in some sort of argument with his god, though she appeared stoic against his verbal barrage.

I tied the horse again and patted Nuzzle on the head. "Can you stay here and guard our ride, buddy?"

He sat on the horse's rear and crouched into a ready pose, eyes squinted as he scanned the surroundings.

"Good boy."

I entered a building left partially standing from the initial assault, fairly close to the gate. A table was set in the dining area, half eaten food and drink left behind, chairs fallen as if the residents departed in a hurry. Understandably so.

I tiptoed through the home. A soft cry and a hushed whisper came from behind a door.

I approached it. "Hello, my name is Zephyr and I'm a friend," I said just loud enough to be heard through the door. "It's not safe here."

The door opened a crack. A family of four Penumbrans were huddled in the small closet. A baby swaddled in cloth whimpered in the mother's arms.

Their little boy looked up at me from within his father's embrace. "You're the Death Reaper," he said. "I heard my friends talking about you. Are you here to rescue us?"

Guilt stabbed at my heart. No… I thought, I only found you because I was sneaking through your house… Instead, I said, "I'm going to stop the bad people." I knelt down before the family. "But I'm going to need your help. Everyone is gathering at the colosseum. Can you go there and tell them help is on the way for me?"

"I can do it!" the boy said in an excited whisper.

"Is it safe to leave?" the father asked.

"Keep to the alleys between the houses and go straight there. You'll have to move quick though. I'm not sure how long our distraction will hold."

The father nodded and gathered his family, urging them toward the door I'd entered. He gave me a silent thank you, then disappeared into the streets.

I moved the opposite direction, finding a window facing the god and her Iron Reaper. I could hear them speaking through the shattered glass.

"I say again, I did my part," the Iron Reaper said. "Our agreement is satisfied, now hold up your end of the bargain."

"And I say again, after we have the city," Pirhani said.

"That wasn't the deal."

"I can't have Unchained running amok in the city, uncontrolled and untethered. You know this."

"I won't release them."

"And I won't release you until it is done."

"I don't trust you to honor that. I deserve what's mine."

"You're trust is unnecessary to my aspirations. But you have little choice regardless. However, as a show of faith, I'll allow you to leave. I don't need you here. Our deal will be honored when the city is mine."

The Iron Reaper grunted in disapproval. "Fucking gods."

No more words were spoken after that. I risked a glance through the window. The Reaper was gone and Pirhani had shifted her attention away toward the direction of Trix's distraction. I wouldn't get a better opportunity than this.

The floorboards creaked far louder than they had any right to as I twisted around to leave the window.

I froze.

Three seconds.

Five.

Ten.

Nothing.

I allowed myself to breath, then glanced back out the window.

Pirhani was gone. This was my chance. I risked running through the remainder of the house. Speed was imperative now, more than stealth.

I burst through the door.

And there she was. Gone from the gate because she'd come directly to me… I now stood face-to-face with the goddess of the Jungle. She grinned with wickedly sharp teeth.

"So you're Valciferous' new toy," said Pirhani.
