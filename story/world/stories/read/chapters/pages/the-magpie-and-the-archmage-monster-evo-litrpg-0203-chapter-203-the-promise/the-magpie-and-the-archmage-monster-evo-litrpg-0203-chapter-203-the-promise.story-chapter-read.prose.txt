Renir was fearing that this day would come. He was honestly expecting it. It had been quite obvious ever since he had learned that Liora was deliberately suppressing her memories by clinging to her new magpie self. She had fully convinced herself that she was a bird, and was happy to live that way.

In a way, it was his fault.

If Liora had never gotten involved with him, then she might have lived out her life happily as a magpie. But there was no going back anymore.

Okay, this outburst in particular was actually Lucien's fault. While digging through Liora's mind, he had removed the barrier Liora had placed there, unlocking her memories.

Renir had been extremely nervous about that. He had watched her very carefully after that incident. She could recall her past life far more easily than before, and it didn't even cause her any headaches.

Yet… for a while everything seemed fine. She was still acting like Liora, so the more time passed, the more convinced he was that everything would be alright.

He had grown too complacent.

It appeared that all that was required was one bad incident to trigger Liora's most painful memories. Memories so painful that she couldn't even recognize what was real anymore.

As Liora tried to tear at Renir's body with her talons, something she had always treated as precious, Renir realized that the pain Liora experienced in her past life was far worse than he had imagined.

She didn't even realize who he was, just tearing at him.

Of course, it didn't hurt him. His gem body was incredibly sturdy. Even Liora's talons couldn't tear through it. But should she get to the others, then it could end in tragedy. He had to bring her out of this state as soon as possible.

"Liora, you need to calm down…" Renir said in a soft voice, not resisting her vicious attacks.

She screamed in pain and continued her onslaught. Her voice was so tormented that it felt like Renir's own heart was being torn apart. What had happened to her to fill her with such incredible anguish?

"Liora, it's alright now. We are safe. You are safe."

"NOOO! They killed him! He died protecting me! HE'S DEAD! DEAD!" Liora screamed.

Renir had no clue who she was referring to, but it appeared that the loss of this person was more painful to her than anything else. Just what happened? He wanted to know, but Renir also understood that making Liora linger in the memory was bad.

It appeared she didn't even realize who she was anymore. She was living in the past, blind to everything else.

"Liora, you are here. You're with me. You are Liora. A magpie who loves treasure."

Her attacks seemed to calm, so Renir pressed forward.

"I'm Renir. A mage trapped in a rock. Aren't I stupid? But you love polishing me because I'm so very pretty, aren't I? Your most precious treasure. Liora, you remember, don't you?"

Liora's frenzied attacks finally stopped, and her eyes seemed to focus. She looked horrified, staring at her Renir. She looked at her bloodied feathers and the mangled corpse of the dragon. He could see part of her crack.

"Wh-What have I done?"

Renir hurriedly got to his feet. Oh no, he wasn't going to let her endure any more pain. He pulled her into a hug. Well, as best as he could with his still rather small body. His arms were wrapped around her, and he patted her wing, not caring about the blood and gore.

"It's okay. You just lost control for a moment. Look, you defeated the dragon. Good job, Liora," Renir continued stroking her feathers, which had begun to tremble.

Suddenly, Liora turned into her magpie form and buried herself in Renir's arms. He was slightly larger than her, so he could properly wrap her into an embrace. Her whole body was trembling, and something akin to sobs came from her beak.

He didn't doubt that if she were able to cry like a human, then Liora would be bawling right now.

This filled Renir's heart with incredible sadness but also rage towards Lucien. Why the hell did that bastard have to go digging through Liora's head? As if she didn't have enough shit on her plate. She didn't deserve any of this.

In fact, Renir made a promise to herself just as he patted her feather, calming down the magpie. Once he regained his body, he would do anything within his power to provide her with an easy life. All the treasure and food she desired would be hers.

Never again would she have to fight or face the ghosts of her past. So long as Renir was alive, Liora would live like a queen but without the burdens of one.

Liora didn't know how long she remained in Renir's embrace, feeling like a broken puppet but unable actually to cry. She felt the strong urge to pull on her feathers. They were marred with blood.

So dirty. So disgusting.

This was her magpie instinct Liora recognized. But Renir's heavy and comforting strokes helped her calm down. She clung to his very presence like a drowning man to a lifeline.

Because Renir was Liora's lifeline.

Renir is my family now. I am not alone. I have Renir.

This was the only mantra that helped her not to think about that horrifying memory.

After a while, Cee, Caspian, and Smokey walked up to her, and without saying a word, the spider wrapped her limbs around Liora.

"It's okay," the spider whispered.

Caspian was more hesitant, but he too soon draped his arms around Liora. Smokey didn't hesitate in the slightest, his tail going left and right as he licked Liora with his large tongue, effectively cleaning off much of the blood covering her.

"Smokey! Ewww!" Liora protested, wondering what was worse—blood or wyvern slobber.

The wyvern responded by licking her even more enthusiastically, covering her completely in wyvern drool.

"Stop it!" Liora screeched, fluttering her feathers.

"At least you're not bloody anymore," Cee said, a nervous tick evident in her voice. "A-Are you okay? What even happened back there?"

Liora paused, unsure how to respond without thinking about that… day.

Thankfully, Renir hurried to reply in her stead. "It was just a bad memory. A side effect of Lucien's tempering and the fact that Liora was reborn from another world."

"Do you need healing, Liora?" Caspian asked, but started healing her before she even answered, placing his warm hand on her feathers.

A moment later, he pulled away, wiping his hand as he tried to get the wyvern drool off.

"Yeah… I think I'm good now… It was triggered by the heat and fire…" Liora muttered, trying hard not to think about the sensation she felt like Smokey had been protecting her from the flames.

Thankfully, Renir was still stroking her wet feathers. The feeling was admittedly kind of gross, but it was calming.

"No more fire in Liora's presence!" Cee announced.

Smokey growled sadly.

Liora wanted to open her mouth to say that it wasn't so bad, but she couldn't bring herself to say it. The idea of being near fire again terrified her.

"It's okay. We'll keep fire in reserve for the time being," Renir said soothingly. "But we have uncovered something fascinating about Cee's webs."

"Yeah, what the hell happened? I sent the same amount of mana into the discharge as I always do, but it blew the dragon's leg off. I thought these guys were supposed to be crazy tough," Cee said.

"Oh, yes, which just proves how strong that attack was. I suspect that somehow the mountain actually amplified your attack, because by design Mana Overcharge takes all the mana in the immediate area and detonates it like an explosive. I thought it might mean that the Mana Overcharge is weaker than normal, but it appeared the opposite is true," Renir explained.

Liora looked at the horribly mangled corpse of the dragon, feeling her own stomach lurch. Holy shit… She hadn't even realized just how badly she had injured it in her rampage.

This was supposed to be a fearsome enemy, much stronger than themselves. But because of Liora's quick attack that gouged out its eye, and Cee's horrible Mana Overcharge followed by brutal mauling from Liora and her Resonance Mimicry, it was defeated without even having a real chance to fight back.

Thinking about it, Liora had completely missed the notification while she was… zoned out. Just how many levels did she get from this thing?

"Guys… I got four levels from defeating this thing," Liora exclaimed after having looked at her profile.

"Yeah, me too, although it was just an assist on my part. Smokey got two levels as well, although he didn't even fight," Cee said.

"I got three levels as well," Caspian said. "It's scary how effective it was."

"I said it would be so!" Renir said, appearing proud of himself, although his posture quickly changed. "But… I don't think we should be fighting any more dragons."

Liora was about to object that they shouldn't avoid such a goldmine of experience for her sake, but Caspian and Cee spoke up before her.

"Agreed!" They said in unison.

"But experience…" Liora muttered, although she felt intense relief.

"We'll figure out another way," Renir said. "Besides, you have to collect a lot of glimmerstones and treasure before we go anywhere."

The intense buzzing of Treasure Sense, which had been dulled until then, resumed, and Liora felt herself looking around the place. The dragon's treasure was scattered through the cave, some of it blown to complete smithereens. Glimmerstones were scattered all about the place, and she could feel something incredibly valuable within the dragon's core.

While Liora lamented the fate of destroyed treasure, there was still so much here. Before she could realize what she was doing, she was already picking up and gathering all the glimmerstones and treasure in sight. It was incredibly calming and helped her recover from the horrible memory she experienced a moment ago.

The notification that appeared before her eyes also filled her with joy, helping her forget.

[Your trait <Glimmerlust> has reached its maximum potential. Would you like to upgrade it for 100 glimmerstones?]

"I can finally upgrade Glimmerlust!" Liora announced happily. "You all non-believers will tremble beneath my might!"

Others cheered at her achievement, especially Renir, who even said that he was wrong about the skill. Liora felt like she should be happy. After all, she had wanted to prove to them that hoarding treasure was important.

She suspected that under other circumstances she would have been elated. But despite everyone acting like nothing at all wrong had happened, Liora felt it. No, they all felt it.

The heavy festering unease beneath the surface. Liora knew she had lost control because of the painful memory. The memory of losing the one person she cared about the most. His sacrifices for Liora's sake.

It wasn't something Liora could brush aside, because if she did that, then that person would have died for nothing. She couldn't invalidate his sacrifice like that.

Even as the system option for the upgrade appeared, Liora couldn't help but wonder whether they would be able to go the same way as before. She had spent a lifetime running from these memories, but could she keep running now?

No, there's no use thinking about it.

Once again, she pushed the unpleasant thoughts aside and approached the dragon's carcass to dig out the glimmercore from its body.
