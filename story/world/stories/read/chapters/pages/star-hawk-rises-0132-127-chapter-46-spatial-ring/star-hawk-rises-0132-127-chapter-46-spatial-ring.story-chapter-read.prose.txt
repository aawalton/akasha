The heat hit me like a physical wall as I stepped through the curtains, I had to blink a few times as I adjusted. I quickly spotted the forges and anvils, each station neatly prepared with tools that hummed faintly with spiritual energy. Crates of raw materials sat stacked to one side. I didn’t recognise any of the strange metals shaped into ingots. My eyes moved onto the shards of crystal that seemed to breathe with inner light. My eyes widened slightly when I saw the chunks of stone, I recognised some of them as jade.

Do not waste this opportunity, you may not get another.” She declared before vanishing out of the curtains quickly.

The others quickly surged forward to claim their stations, I lingered back watching them run for a moment before my eyes shifted to sweep over the materials. In my minds eye I could see blades gleaming with promise, armour shimmering faintly as if daring me to test its strength, but my gaze landed on the unassuming lump of metal set apart from the rest, and I forgot about the swords and armours, it was dark, almost plain, yet I could feel something vast folded within it, like a shadow stretched thin across eternity.

I stepped forward and picked it up slowly, turning it in my hand until my eyes landed on the jade and a smile slowly crept onto my face. If I was going to attempt forging, I might as well attempt something amazing that could carry my alchemy and formation resources.

I carried the metal and jade to an empty station, the heat from the forge already biting at my skin. I set the metal down on the anvil and drew in a slow breath, steadying my hands. As my eyes moved over the instructions for forging a ring that was secured to a stand next to the anvil, memorising every word on it.

“Ready or not,” I whispered before firming my resolve, “I will forge this ring!”

The lump looked ordinary, but as I fed a strand of my Qi into it, it shivered, swallowing the Qi hungrily before settling again, heavier somehow. I quickly grabbed the tongs and slid the metal into the forge. The flames leapt greedily, wrapping around the lump until it glowed a deep, angry red. Sparks dropped to the floor as I pulled it free and laid it on the anvil again.

The first strike of the hammer echoed like thunder in my arm. The metal resisted, hard and unwillingly, but with each swing, I poured more of my will into it, forcing the shape to bend under both hammer and Qi. I continued striking again and again until the stubborn lump stretched thinner, rounder, until the first glimmer of a ring began to take shape.

When the base was formed, I almost dropped the hammer down as I felt my fingers shake from the hard work, but I squeezed it closed before shaking the nerves out, then reached for the jade. The cool stone pulsed faintly against my skin, almost as if it knew what I wanted from it. I chiselled a shard free and placed it on the bench next to the anvil, I glanced up at the instructions again before picking up the stylus and began to draw the formation into the jade making sure to go slowly and get it right, I found I rather enjoyed this part because I was literally creating a formation for the ring to work and marvelled at how many professions required a formation to work.

Once I was done, I took the tongs and placed the metal ring into the forge again and let it heat back up before returning it to the anvil and grabbed the jade from the table and the hammer. I pressed the jade into the soft inner band of the ring, letting it sink slowly as I channelled Qi to guide it. Light spilled through the cracks, faint at first, then brighter, weaving veins of emperor green jade through the dark metal.

I suddenly felt something in the air shift, like space itself tugging at the edges of the forming ring. I narrowed my focus, biting down on my lip, as I pressed my intent into the final strikes, sealing the jade and metal into one. The moment the last echo of the hammer faded, the band glowed with a soft light, I felt a small breeze spiralling out from it, carrying the faint scent of dust and stone. It was almost like an unseen door had just been cracked open.

I slowly placed the hammer on the anvil as I stared down at the ring in amazement, unable to believe what I had just done. I slowly took the ring into my fingers and held it up, my chest heaving and sweat pouring from my body, as I stared at the ring.

“I can’t believe it, you really forged a spatial ring” a soft voice said from just behind me.
