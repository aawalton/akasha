Once the jeering calmed, Loricks began the festival.

The tournament had become a farce.

“Do you think he has a chance?” Lina asked softly.

Even at the first evolution, Goren’s Hurricane Fist could blast an area as wide as the stage with razor-sharp, three-hundred-mile winds. Even if Goren neutered the skill, it was impossible to dodge.
