Madeleine stared at Ashton's face. He could practically feel her eyes digging into his skin. Her fingers were twitching and her mouth was closed shut.

"Uhm... I don't know what you..." she responded to Ashton, laughing nervously. But that was a lie, and they both knew it. Ashton wasn't exactly subtle about his insinuation. Before he was summoned to Lumia, Ashton never would have been able to speak to others like this.

But well, now that he barely had any anxiety and shame left in his being, there was nothing stopping him. Plus, sadly, he also had plenty of experience with what people were like at the end of the world.

The people in Lumia were generally quite similar to those of earth, with the main difference that they were simply less empathetic and indulged in their personal desires far more easily. But obviously, humans were also often quite selfish and hoped to do things for their own personal and selfish enjoyment extremely often. And while hapis were simply far faster to follow through on their urges, it wasn't as though humans couldn't be 'encouraged' into doing so, too.

In this world, there were very little things that you could do to 'destress'. You couldn't play games, or watch television or movies, you were stuck in a small area with loads of people and barely any privacy, and most people beside Ashton couldn't even indulge in alcohol or cigarettes anymore.

Every tenth adult in England was a smoker, and three-quarters of adults drank alcohol, albeit to varying degrees, so there should be plenty of folk that were noticing this now. The only thing left at this point was social interaction, which seemed to be stifled even more here specifically. Everyone was just busily working.

So... while it was maybe a little mean, Ashton wanted to take advantage of that. Plus, it had been a while for him as well. Not that he could engage in that kind of thing with Millie here, but it wasn't like this would be the last time he would stop by this university.

"You know exactly what I'm saying," Ashton said, lowering his voice as he leaned forward. "But for now, how about I show you this?"

Ashton held his hand out to the side, and the Backpacker gave him a bottle of a regular healing potion. The sort that didn't need any particularly special equipment to make. If Madeleine was an alchemist, she should be able to use the methods that Ashton would give her to make these herself.

He put the bottle down on the table behind her, and pulled out the Pen of Insight, having it write down the ingredients and step-by-step instructions of how to create this particular type of potion, and even had it add in some simple tips and tricks to avoid common pitfalls when you started creating potions.

"This bottle should contain around three litres. If I were you, I would move it over into another container to keep it, and the bottle is going to disappear once you've emptied it out," Ashton explained, taking a step back. "Also be aware that potions do expire after a while, so if you notice any rancidity I'd avoid using them."

He walked over to the bag that Madeleine had carried here, grabbing a can out of it. "And be extremely careful with this, it's not a potion. It's a soda slime, so a sort of monster, basically."

Madeleine flinched. "Huh? Wait, what do you mean, a monster?"

She had been dazed and confused by what Ashton was saying until now, since there was a lot of new information here. But the mention of a monster suddenly pulled her out of that. And it made sense; ever since she cleared the first quest, she's probably been just in here and didn't really have to deal with monsters at all.

"It's fairly harmless, don't worry. Slimes are useful as potion ingredients since they have a solid amount of mana in them, and you can manipulate the structure of their mana fairly easily. I'll jot down the method of neutralising the acidity of a slime as well," Ashton explained, putting the can back down. "There are a lot more things that I could tell you than just this. How about it, is that a good start to try and convince you?"

Madeleine glanced over at the paper where the Pen of Insight had written down various useful things already, and then glanced back at Ashton.

"But... you can't tell anyone about this. Neither of you," Madeleine said, for the first time acknowledging Millie.

"Of course not. We're just doing this out of curiosity anyway, so what reason would we have to just spread this around?" Ashton asked. "Rather, we might be able to help figure this all out. Isn't that right, Millie?"

The girl turned to Ashton and excitedly nodded at him. "Exactly! We're the Ghost Crushers!"

"Ghost Busters."

"Hm?"

"It's called Ghost Busters, not Ghost Crushers," Ashton explained, and Millie just tilted her head to the side, showing him an utterly puzzled expression

"What's that?"

"You know, the movie, Ghost Busters. That's what you were saying, right?"

Millie shook her head. "Is that an old person thing?"

"... I'm 23, you brat." Well, technically at least. He was born 23 years ago, and he figured counting his time in Lumia was a bit unnecessary for this.

"Yeah, and that's really old."

Ashton sighed, and turned back to Madeleine. "So? Do you think you could help these Ghost... Crushers?"

Madeleine laughed a little, and slowly nodded. She still seemed a bit nervous to talk about this all, but was clearly less tense overall.

She guided Ashton and Millie out of the lab, and down the hallway. In a hushed voice, she started to explain. "We don't really know what it is... and not everyone can even really see it. It appears every single day, once a day, and does the same thing every time."

"And what is that 'same thing'?"

"We don't really know..." Madeleine responded. "It... looks like it's talking to something at some point, or looking for something maybe. We're not quite sure..."

As Madeleine walked through the halls, Ashton kept growing more anxious. His gut-feeling was growing stronger the further they went. After all, he remembered these halls perfectly well, and knew where these particular twists and turns led to.

"It's usually around here, in this hallway..." Madeleine started. It was likely quite dark, since there weren't any windows right here, though that of course didn't matter to Ashton. He continued to walk forward, calling on the salamander for light again, so that Madeleine could continue guiding them.

"Is it specifically here?" Ashton asked, standing in front of a particular door. Madeleine slowly nodded.

"Yeah... how did you know?"

Ashton pushed open the door. Of course it was this place; as if Ashton wasn't wondering about his parents plenty already, but now there was a ghost coming out of his dad's old office?

As he stepped inside, Ashton first found himself encountering a cloud of dust. Clearly, this place hadn't been used in a while. It hadn't been vacant since his dad quit or anything, other people probably took over here at some point, but over the past few years at least it seemed to have turned into something of a storage closet.

"So from here... and then it moves where?" Ashton asked, and Madeleine slowly pointed down the hallway.

"It starts on that end and speaks to something, goes into that room, comes back out, and walks down the other end of the hallway, but disappears when it tries to turn the corner..." Madeleine explained, and Ashton slowly nodded.

He positioned himself in the middle of that area as much as he could, so that he could capture as much of what was going on as possible. Though, he figured that what really caused the imprint was happening in the office, so that was his focus.

Ashton pulled out the Arcane Pocket Watch and quickly activated it. A wave of mana flowed through the area.

"At what time does it appear?"

"... Around 1 in the morning? I think?"

Ashton nodded. "Sounds about right. He did always like to work late into the night."

He turned the dial backward, and watched as a number of figures made of light suddenly walked through the hallway. Madeleine flinched and stepped back.

"Wh-What's going on? Why are there so many..? Did you summon... did you summon more ghosts?" she asked, but Ashton just sighed.

"Of course not. This is a magic item that allows me to see the events of the past 24 hours. From my understanding, it basically strengthens even the weakest imprints of the veil, so an actual imprint should show up as well."

"Imprint? Veil?" Madeleine seemed just that much more confused by the explanation, and Millie huffed smugly.

"The veil is the plane of magic that's everywhere at all times! So since it's everywhere, and it's magic, it remembers what happened," she explained, pushing her hand onto her hip. Madeleine glanced down at Millie, then stared at Ashton again.

"What are you looking at me for? She's right," he pointed out with clear pride in his voice. Considering how tired Millie seemed, Ashton wasn't completely sure that she really remembered everything he tried to explain to her yesterday. But clearly he never had to worry about that in the first place.

Ashton moved all the way back toward 1 in the morning, and slowed down as he approached that time. Soon, something stepped into the view of the pocket watch. It looked different to the normal figures that were shown through the watch. This one was a lot more blurry even for Ashton, like this imprint had decayed over time, or like it was never a perfect imprint in the first place.

But there was enough for Ashton to be sure of one thing; this was the person he thought it was going to be.

The 'ghost' was Ashton's father.
