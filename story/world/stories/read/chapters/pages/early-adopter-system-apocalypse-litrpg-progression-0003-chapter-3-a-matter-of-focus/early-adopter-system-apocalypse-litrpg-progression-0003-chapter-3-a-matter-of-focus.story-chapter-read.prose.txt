Magic. It seemed so impossible, but then again so did the system message – notification? – that everyone in the world saw, simultaneously. What she believed didn’t matter, she planned to be ready for even the impossible.

The guy was completely frozen, and his body went somehow even more rigid as she patted the wand twice. Two light, deliberate taps.
