Ronan didn’t immediately read the notifications in front of him. He floated in the endless white void, contemplating what he had just learned from his most recent death.

Mana was an all-encompassing energy. The mana that originated in his heart was, for lack of a better word, unattuned. No, pure makes more sense. It’s not that it lacks an attunement, but that it has one of its own, unblemished by other elemental or esoteric influences.

There were still many mysteries regarding mana that were unsolved, but he was satisfied with what he had accomplished. Ronan had failed the nightmare tutorial yet again, but in the process he had learnt much, not just about mana, but also how to engage socially, with both friends and foes. It was a much-needed lesson. A lesson that he didn’t want to repeat in the iterations that followed.

He believed that with his new knowledge about mana he would be able to master some of his ‘elemental’ spells like firestorm and fissure, as well as potentially create new ones from the ground up. Spells that he hadn’t previously possessed as skills. That would have to wait for the next iteration, though.

Ronan finally turned his attention to the twin notifications that cried for his attention, curious to see what his more… literal gains had been.

The resistances alone would make the rather brutal death worth every moment. Seeing his own nose fall off had been gross at the time, but a triple dose of new resistances made the loss of his extremities feel like a bargain price to pay. But they weren’t the

pièce de résistance of that death.

He had gained a skill from Learn through Suffering. Something he’d never encountered before, but was more than happy to see. His decision to use that first Brand of the Temporal Artificer on the Skillbound Conduit Ring was validated once more.

Ronan took the skill and the three resistance increases without further delay. Even before the analysis of the iteration began, he was already satisfied with what his time in the underground bunkers had granted him.

Ronan marveled at how quickly his multipliers were shooting back up. His decision to go through with the concentration had been confident but laced with some doubt, but this reaffirmed that it had been the correct decision. Iteration 56 would see him earning 20 times as much experience as iteration 55. It had only been a few iterations since he underwent the baptism in the Dark Sea and he was approaching his old numbers, with the exception of the credit multiplier and the cultivation multiplier.

The latter was mostly due to the lack of shards he’d encountered, but the former was simply a slower grind than the rest. Given enough time, they would all increase to staggering heights.

Having earned a few more chronos merits, Ronan was going to browse the Temporal Obelisk to see if anything was worth purchasing, but he realised there was still one more notification blinking in his vision, awaiting attention. Curious to know what it was, he let it expand and gave it a read.

Ronan felt a great headache approach when he read through the two evolutions he could choose from. Neither of them were ideal or fit the path he wanted his sorcery to take. However, having one of his two sub-classes languish at uncommon rarity was not an acceptable outcome, either.

Purely by the stats and skills, Sorceric Bastion of Purity seemed to be the superior evolution. Jumping two rarity tiers in one go would mean far more stats and superior class benefits. The class had some pretty punitive drawbacks, though.

Not being able to use attuned mana of any kind was extremely limiting. Ronan would immediately be forced to stop using firestorm and fissure as well as any other elementally attuned spells. It would also limit his future options for learning and combat. That was something he felt was unacceptable. Especially given that he had essentially infinite time to learn and grow. Putting such a harsh cap on his future sorcery wasn’t something he believed he could stomach.

On the other hand, while Frozen Sorcerer didn’t necessarily limit his ability to cast any sort of spell, it certainly favoured the cold affinity over any other. There was an equal possibility that it might lock him out of other evolutions that were more generalist and only providing benefits to a single attunement. When he had the benefit of infinite lives, why would he settle for anything less than the best?

Unfortunately, the best choice wasn’t on the table right now. Which meant he had to decide between the two options the system had bestowed upon him. It seemed like an easy choice, but there was more than meets the eye to both class evolutions.
