Max could barely contain his excitement. He had been buzzing that entire day ever since he unlocked the Mana Manipulation Skill that morning.

It didn't matter what exercise they did or what the sadistic mind of the Optio made up.

Sprints felt shorter.

The squat-pushup-jumping combination? A breeze.

Hill runs were manageable.

And even the pullups felt unbelievably easy.

All of it was based on the single notion of expectation that Max had set up and could not for the life of him wait for. His body went through all the motions without his mind following through. Said mind was back in the barracks waiting for nighttime when everyone was asleep, already staring at the Ledger and the Skill Shard's map of light only he could see.

Max could only wonder what four percent looked like compared to three.

The day could not end fast enough.

If he wasn't so worried about the potential of hurting himself in his haste or the realistic threat of the Legion figuring out he had stolen a Skill Shard with the Class Shard, he would have already done it right then and there when he first unlocked the Mana Manipulation Skill and after the talk they had with Arnie.

Alas, he had garnered too much attention somehow. Senior Casius and the Optio were discussing something about him; their drifting eyes and staring were dead giveaways.

Max had to convince himself it was too much risk.

All he could do was continue to grind through the final hours of training, the Chow Hall meal and seconds, the evening hill runs forward and backward, the well visit for water, another jog around the camp for good measure, and then the final long and arduous march back to the barracks once the Optio was one hundred percent positive that they were past the point of exhaustion and would fall face first into their beds asleep.

That was exactly what happened. None of them had any energy to keep their heads up, much less cause any issues.

Max acted the same.

He did his best to fake it as much as possible with his face in the pillow and arms limp. But he was slightly better off than everyone else. The Recovery of the Air Gladiator Skill Shard gave him a slight edge over everyone else that provided a constant small recovery and boost to his health. It chipped away at the fatigue and exhaustion just enough that he was better off and not dead tired and unconscious the second his face touched the bed.

In reality, he kept his eyes shut as the legionaries turned off the lanterns and the barracks settled into a dead silence.

Max waited even longer to make sure no one was awake. He was positive that a few of the elites, Caleb included, were waiting to sleep last or close to it. So he didn't move or shift for an entire hour. He waited until the fifth pass of the guards' patrol under his window, which he had calculated to be about sixty minutes, give or take a few, based on how fast or slow the patrol's pace was this time around.

Some legionaries were more nimble compared to others.

By then, his body had already begun screaming for some rest. He was not immune to exhaustion, even with the boosts.

Max eventually opened his eyes in the pitch dark. He shifted his attention to the ledger.

Alright. It's finally time!

He pulled up the ledger from his status page and thumbed the Skill Shard within it.

The map appeared in all its bright glory.

Most of it was dim, except for a single line and three orbs of light the size of his pinkie nail.

He guessed that the three orbs represented the three percent. It was an easy guess, all things considered. Max counted how many there were on the map. Some were clustered together, closer than even the first few, while others sat isolated and at the far end of long, winding pathways that touched the edge of the ledger's map. A few were cracked and looked to need repairs.

Max ended his count at ninety-nine.

Three percent efficacy for three orbs lit. Ninety-nine total. So each ball is one percent? That wouldn't make a hundred percent if I lit them all, because there's only ninety-nine. Unless the Shard itself counts as the hundredth. Or the starting point is zero and the scale is different. Or I'm overthinking this...

Max refocused on the map, studying it better than before.

If the orbs were the percentage point, then what did the nodes, at least four times larger than the orbs and far fewer, represent? They sat at intersections of dozens of pathways; some looked like thick knots, while others looked like smooth and clean glass balls.

What did they do?

Max shook his head. There was no point dwelling on something he had no answer to.

Asking questions he couldn't answer was a luxury he didn't have time for.

Move mana through the channels. That's step one and the simplest task on the list.

Max took a deep breath and touched upon his Mana Manipulation Skill for the first time. The difference was immediately apparent to him.

He stared in awe at how slow he felt before unlocking it.

This was only level 1 and a low-level skill as well for mana practitioners, yet it was clear how vast the gap was between someone who had it and someone who did not. For the longest, all he could do was pull one wisp at a time, one lonely wisp that trembled and bobbed through his mana channels lazily. It took real effort just to coax it to move.

Now? Just pressing onto his core made the entire group of ninety-nine wisps shake.

All of them.

The whole mass lurched forward at the slightest tug of his intent with more power than he was used to. His breath caught as all of them surged forward, rushing out of the void and into his mana channels, toward an exit that did not exist.

Max grunted as he clenched his fists and closed his eyes shut to stop the surge.

He caught it after a precarious moment of worry.

He could feel his hands trembling and sweat streak down his forehead. He held the mana tight and forced it to sit still. That was harder said than done. Any slight shift in his intent made the mana rush in that direction. Eventually, he gained enough control over them.

They sat there obediently, unaware of the struggle he was going through.

Max took another deep breath.

Okay... That was intense. I need to have better control... I can already see how important it is to level this up.

Max wiped his forehead with the back of his hand.

Then he began to take deep breaths in preparation, using Senior Casius's method to calm himself. Only when he was positive of his recovery did he start on the next step. He separated a tiny stream from the massive surge and pushed the rest back into his pseudo-core.

Max kept a connection with a thin line in case he needed it, which he was positive would be the situation soon enough.

Max guided the single stream through his mana channels until it reached his palm.

The warmth spread through his entire arm, originating from the tips of his fingers and up to his shoulder.

Max exhaled.

Now what? What do I do to put mana into the Ledger?

Max wondered if he should pull the mana outside of his body, but that seemed far more dangerous. He doubted he would go unnoticed by all the legionaries, the Optio, the captains, and Senior Casius if a sudden burst of mana made itself present in an otherwise silent night.

The Ledger seemed to be the exception.

Max frowned as he pulled up his status page and the Ledger's notifications for any hints.

He read them over a few times. At no point did it ever indicate that he needed to use mana externally. If anything, it hinted at using it within him somehow. There wasn't any other detail about it no matter how hard he looked. He was stuck without an answer unless he figured this out.

Wait. What if it's in the void?

It was the only place that seemed to have endless amounts of space. He pulled back his stream of mana and let it spread out in a wave through the void.

There! What's that?

He'd been so focused on the black void and the single point of light that was the pseudo-core that he missed multiple hidden clouds of fog with orbs within them. Max studied each one by one, numbering as many as his current skills. He didn't quite know how to explain it. Each one sat there in the darkness surrounded by nebulous fogs of a myriad of colors and unique to themselves.

Max instinctively knew which was which without having to check.

They're all there.

He looked at a single one that was darker than the rest, but far larger and surrounded by dark purple clouds. A single pathway was open into the dark orb that had no fog, clear. In the center of the orb was a single point of light that bounced off the orb's edges and changed direction in a consistent but slow pattern.

That must be the Skill Shard.

Max mentally prodded the Ledger and the map appeared before his mind's eye while within the void. It was a visual representation of the nebulous cloud, the orb, the path, and the point of light that bounced around within the orb. He thanked whoever made it with all his heart. Max knew that it was not needed, but the sheer amount of convenience was worthy of its existence and more.

Max looked back at the other skills within him. A thought crossed his mind, wondering if he could manipulate them like he did the Ledger. His gaze focused on his Battle Focus skill. It was the least obvious if he manipulated it. Max reached for it with a bit of mana, only to stop dead.

Why would I do this?

There was no good reason to experiment on the unknown. He had just skipped that very proposition a few minutes ago.

Max shook his head and turned his attention back toward the Ledger. He pushed the mana stream toward it, fighting off the irrational urge to touch the skill fogs. The mana moved carefully through the pathway. He felt no pain or suffering for his attempt, though he did make sure the mana did not touch the fog.

For all he knew, it would result in torturous agony, something he had no intention of experiencing.

The mana kept advancing until it touched the dark orb. The point of light snapped into the very center of the orb and pulsed. The mana sank in, and Max felt a sudden connection with it. He smiled as he pulled more mana from the thin line connection he carried and fed it into the orb.

The map before him pulsed once and stayed bright, showing exactly where his mana was.

Max smiled.

He moved the mana through the pathways. The mana flooded the channels, taking a few seconds longer to fill the bright nodes. Until, finally, it reached the first blocked path directly after the third bright orb of light. Max felt resistance from the channels themselves immediately.

The energy struggled to move forward because it faced friction from how damaged, twisted, wrinkled, and crunched the pathway to the next orb of light on the map was. But Max knew the assignment. All he had to do was move mana through the pathway, even if it cost him wisps to do so. All he could hope was that he had enough mana to reach the fourth point of light.

Max got to work without wasting any more time.

Step by step, the mana pushed through, costing him a wisp for every wrinkle unwrinkled, twist untwisted, crunched pathway uncrunched. Little by little, he advanced toward his goal, and little by little, his scant amount of mana dwindled before his eyes at an alarming pace.

He came to a realization.

This was not going to be a quick few nights to clear the entire map. This would take time and heavy dedication.

Max frowned as the final few wisps of mana within his core were brought out. He was more than two-thirds of the way to the orb, but he didn't need to reach all the way. All he needed to do was clear that final portion of the damaged pathway, and the connection from the third and fourth ball of light would be complete and clear.

He kept pushing until, finally, he felt the breakthrough click in his system.

A wave of cold energy washed over him, snapping him out of the void that he had been in and back to reality.

Max opened his eyes with a gasp. It felt like someone poured frigid, cold water on him.

He looked around at the sleeping figures around him; no one reacted.

A sigh escaped his lips.

It didn't help that he was covered in sweat and there was a light breeze streaming in from the slightly cracked windows.

Max pulled up the map. A massive smile spread across his face. That fourth orb came to life before his eyes as the stream of light extended from the third orb, through the channels, and into it.

I did it! I actually did it!

He pulled up his status page and thumbed his Skill Shard. The smile on his face only grew wider.

The Ledger [1/10] - Level 1

Recovery of the Air Gladiator (E-Tier) - 4% Efficacy

Skill attained by the mighty gladiators of the Grand Colosseum of the Floating Island City States. Encourages Magic, Oxygen, and red blood cells to help recovery, regeneration, healing, stamina, and weight cut relief during battle months.

recovery boosts - 2% -> 3%

regeneration boosts - 1% -> 1.9%

healing boosts - 1.3% -> 2.6%

stamina boosts - 6% -> 7%

The boost was real. All four of them jumped up from their previous numbers.

Yes, the difference was marginal, but in the long run, it was massive.

Max's face hurt from how large his smile was.

He dismissed the status page and fell back onto his pillow. He fell asleep as soon as he did so.
