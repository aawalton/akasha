Archie leaped over the torso of the corpse of the Five-Eyed Serpent, dodging another spray of undeath flames from the reborn serpent.

I’ve heard snakes could shed their skin, Archie mused as he watched the serpent slither through the giant burnt hole it made through the Five-Eyed Serpent’s corpse. But not an entire fucking body!

The veins that previously surrounded its eyes and appeared when it spewed undeath flames were now absent. Rather, the only indication Archie got when it came would be its black eye flickering for a moment before they spewed from its mouth.

“Better than nothing, I guess,” Archie conceded, charging towards the serpent whose black eye dimmed and flames stopped.

Archie crossed the distance between the two in seconds, blasting its 25-meter frame backwards by a meter before following up with a flurry of blows, this time Leeching Strikes of Fury actually stealing the vital energy from the serpent.

Previously, when he tried to reach the Five-Eyed Serpent’s core by ripping through its body, not a single drop of vital energy was transferred to him. Which made sense as it was all condensed into its core, not leaving behind any in the rest of its body. Also explaining how he was able to tear through its scales and flesh with far more ease compared to when he initially struck it.

Archie ignored the hissing that came from the serpent before it arced its upper body downwards, its mouth wide open to swallow him whole. Instead of moving out of the way, he raised his right forearm above his head with Material Carapace active; both his arms gaining makeshift shields atop them, along with the rest of his body getting covered with Sentinel Platiniteshell plates.

The serpent’s maw engulfed his right arm alongside the makeshift shield atop it, just past his elbow when Archie’s index finger on his left hand pressed the trigger on his Mana Revolver, aimed at the roof of the serpent’s mouth.

The Nature-Attuned manabolt tore through a couple of centimeters of the roof of the serpent’s mouth, coincidentally ripping a hole through one of the poison sacs connected to its left upper fang.

The sudden pain caused the serpent to clamp down onto Archie’s right arm instead of swallowing him whole.

But as it clamped down on his arm, Archie’s makeshift shield pressed against the jaws of the serpent, holding it open as Archie sent his Mana Revolver back into his spatial storage and continued to release a barrage of blows with his free left hand. All the while ignoring the toxins that splashed across his back and corroded his armor.

His Vital Metabolism was strong enough to push back against it, and with the amount of health he was leeching from the serpent with each strike, it effectively did no damage to him.

Hearing his makeshift shield crack under the pressure of the serpent’s jaw strength, Archie quickly activated Runic Amplification on both his Sentinel Platiniteshell Bracers and Chestpiece. Amplifying both the Greater Physical Resistance runic script and Self-Repair rune on both his bracers and chestpiece and the Physical Reinforcement runic script on his bracers.

The mana pathways Archie etched himself atop the respective runic scripts and runes pulsed a faint red color before the runic bases themselves glowed red, dying the mana pathways connected to them the same color.

The cracked makeshift shield pushed back against the jaws of the Triarch Serpent with renewed strength, its cracked appearance repairing alongside the holes the toxins both corroded and seeped through.

The serpent was in disbelief; even after its rebirth, it was unable to kill the weak creature in front of it. It kept flexing its jaws in an attempt to crush whatever the human shoved in its jaws.

While it understood it had lost the majority of the strength it once had and some of the abilities it had, its current body was far more stable to handle its newly gained skills. It was an acceptable sacrifice in its eyes; no longer were its flesh and scales weakened by its influx of power gained from its new eyes.

Truly, it was something it planned to resolve when the Tutorial ended, and when it would arrive on the new planet, it would be staying on. But due to the urgency of its current situation, some things needed to be snipped, regardless of future gain. If anything holds you back, you cut it off, even if it’s your own tail; words it followed to its core, which it inherited from the great snake above.

His only question: Why am I still struggling to devour this human?...Why am I getting hurt by an uneducated human that can’t even speak the USL?

Angered by this realization, the serpent activated its purple eye, unseen by Archie, who was ripping through its body with his left fist, building up the kinetic energy in his left gauntlet with each blow he dealt.

The purple eye glowed a bright purple before its vertical eyelids closed it, unlike what happened in its previous body with its red eye; the purple eye simply went dormant.

And appearing behind Archie was another serpent, looking just like the Triarch Serpent; in fact, there was no difference between the two. Both had three eyes, in the same format, with the purple one closed and the rest dulled, and its size was the exact same, even in the same position it was currently in.

The only difference was that it had no will of its own, fully commanded by the original as per its own design, and was weaker. After all, it would do no good for there to be two kings of the Emernigan Isle, especially when it would eventually take over its new planet when the Tutorial would end.

Archie, unaware of the second serpent behind him, kept up his barrage of blows into the serpent’s torso, jumping over and ducking under the tail swipes it sent at him while doing so.

So, it wasn’t a surprise for anyone other than himself when he was suddenly smacked in his armored side, creating spiderweb like cracks across his armor from the blow that quickly repaired due to Runic Amplification and almost launching him back into the jungle if it weren’t for his right arm still lodged in between the jaws of the Triarch Serpent.

Archie felt his shoulder snap and almost ripped off from the blow, gritting his teeth, he created a mana platform to kick off of. Twisting around his arm that was still lodged in the serpent’s mouth, Archie landed on the upper back of the Triarch Serpent in a crouch, looking at the new serpent that looked just like the serpent he was atop of.

Seeing that he needed to get out of the way of the upcoming blast, Archie deactivated Material Carapace and quickly wrenched out his arm before the Triarch Serpent slammed its jaws shut.

Leaping to his left and breaking his fall with a roll before breaking into a sprint, Archie dodged the torrent of undeath flames that engulfed the upper body of the Triarch Serpent.

Arcing towards the marionette, Archie felt his shoulder pop back into place, and the damage inflicted on it from both the blow and his mid-air twist repaired itself. Archie felt the unfortunately familiar pain of undeath flames covering his form, melting through his runic amplified armor, and slowly melting his gauntlets. He felt his Vital Metabolism work on overdrive, repairing as much muscle, flesh, and bone as it could with as much he’d consumed prior.
