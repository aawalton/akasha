Family comes first, Clan comes second, other Clans Third.

Everyone else is Out-Clan. They may be friends, they may be important acquaintances, but unless they become family they come last.

We all know this, we are all raised this way. Blood before Clan, Clan before Allies, Allies before strangers.

We do not turn them away, we trade with them in good faith, as long as it does not violate the Law. We allow Out-Clan to taste our goods, to buy our wares. But Clan still comes first.

She was Clan. I knew she was when she walked in but said nothing. I might have been mistaken, she might not have been raised Clan. She had not contacted us, this was not her dinner, surely she would not have come at another's behest.

She ordered, not as they do, but as one who knew the dishes that were available rather than those that were on display. I knew then I was not mistaken. Everyone looked, everyone had to see for themselves.

None spoke to her, none intruded. We knew how she carried herself even if not why.

Family came first.

Anya could barely believe how quickly the next few months flew by. They Dove, they trained, they spent time with their family. The number of Affiliated members kept growing and by the time George had been on staff at Dive Administration for six month it was projecting to be booked solid within the next six. Apparently she had a reputation with Divers, as well as acquaintances she was able to convince to follow her to the new, mostly untapped, location.

Visits to the Holding were rarely about anything other than catching up. Fay and Alex were handling everything, they only needed approvals when something unexpected came up. Everyone seemed happy, even Briana despite having to hire even more employees and consider expanding the cafe beyond the single location she had been certain would be more than enough.

Seb's 30th birthday came and went. Anya had come up with thirty possible sparring ideas as a surprise. Somehow it took them a day and a half to cover six of them and they agreed that if anyone asked her gift to him had been a massage. The rest they decided to save for other special occasions.

No one did ask what she got him, nor did they ask what he got her a couple months later when it was her birthday. It wasn't as though she wanted to tell them how great the massage he'd given her was, or how they'd- Nope, that wasn't anything they needed to hear about.

Their ongoing testing of the meld led them further and further into the Depths. They just kept pushing, kept finding ways to improve on their combined efficiencies. Their paired equipment reflected the changes, not by gaining any further abilities but by how clearly it reflected their closeness.

The blade of his epee all but glowed green when they fought, the pommel stone nearly blinding when it flared emerald with power. Even when simply held at his side the glints of green and silver were unmistakable. The faint patterns in her robe had spread, no longer limited to the trim they spread throughout the weave, a hazy green flame that danced when she did.

She'd caught sight of them more than once in the mirror of their new training hall, both while running through katas and when sparring. She'd nearly frozen at the sight, enjoying the combined effects, only to find herself scrambling when Seb forgot to be distracted along with her.

That wasn't to say he never got caught up watching her, that he never forgot why he was looking at her. She could feel when he did, she knew when and where his eyes lingered, how easy it would be to remind him to stay focused, she simply never wanted to when they were safe.

~ * ~

I think we're ready, Anya sent.

I know we were close, you think yesterday did it?

If I'm wrong I'll go squish goblins and splatter slimes on my own to make up the difference.

She could feel Seb's silent laughter, not just through the bond but through her cheek on his chest. She'd woken up before him for once, unable to keep from comparing how she felt to how much she knew they had collected yesterday. They'd be able to hit level 14 today, she knew it.

The glint of her ring caught her eye. How long had it been since he slipped it onto her finger, since they'd shown Awakened Earth who they were, since they'd found out about the deadline that she would not let stop her. She felt Seb's attention pull away from their meld, his focus turning inward despite their proximity.

Three years love, he sent. Well it will be in six hours, eleven minutes and-

Seb! she sent. You did not just calculate how many seconds.

Six hours and ten minutes now. Almost to the second, he answered before she felt his fingers tickle her sides, light enough to be a distraction and not a suggestion.

Then we check, we make sure I'm right-

You are.

-and then in five hours and thirty-five minutes we-

-push through to level 14 and know we're only one more level away.

She smiled as she pushed herself up and settled into her usual meditation pose. A moment later she felt his back press against hers. Half the time they ended up face to face, the last thing and first thing they'd see being the other's eyes, the rest of the time they chose to press back to back so they stayed in as much contact as possible.

It didn't take long for them to focus and make use of their gathered Energy crystals. Seb couldn't quite pick up and move them using techniques within his aura but they'd managed to set up a dispensing method that let him flick them in as needed. It wasn't foolproof, it was still a slight distraction that she became aware of every time Seb got the timing wrong, but it was better than the alternatives.

By the time they had both confirmed that she had been right Anya could feel her stomach growling at the lack of breakfast. The lack of coffee was almost enough to send her rushing out to get it started but she knew Seb would tease her if she didn't put more than a robe on her True Self.

Hey! she sent as he rushed out the moment she turned towards her clothes.

I'll get the coffee going, he answered.

Since when do you rush off all Wow in the Depths?

Since I know the love of my life will be out right behind me to protect me if need be? I was planning breakfast in bed.

She froze midway through the tent flap. She could hear the teasing in his words, the challenge implicit in his trust of her ability to keep him safe if need be. He wasn't wrong, she would keep him safe if needed, not that any roaming Depthsborn would be a true threat on its own, not when they would be going down another level on their next trip.

We're heading up tomorrow? she sent as she started to tidy up their campsite.

Breakfast in bed meant a lazy day, especially if they planned to try and level up exactly three years after he'd given her the ring. Had it really been three years since that day? She didn't doubt Seb's calculations, she knew he was right, but still, time had flown by.

It'll be a day early.

We won't get anything done in one day if we have to unpack then pack right back up the same day.

Extra day with family? he asked.

Fay will complain about the work she doesn't get done.

Smiling the whole time.

~ * ~

Anya fell into their melded state even as she focused her attention inward. She could feel Seb doing the same, knew his efforts were matching hers. It wasn't a case of her leveling up beside him, of him advancing alone despite her keeping pace. For the first time they were doing so while as fully aware as they possibly could be at this time.

She felt his hands rest across both their knees, her fingers twining themselves through his as their breaths and heartbeats synchronized. They were calm, they were relaxed, they were focused. They had spent the day together, putting no more than the absolute minimum effort into everything.

She knew how long they had until the moment of the anniversary. She knew how long it would likely take to do what they had to do so they could open their eyes as their echoed timekeeping hit zero. There was no rush, no pressure. If they failed in their attempt they would still be together. Perfect timing would be poetic, but it wouldn't change the outcome.

Seb's awareness swirled, pulling her attention along as they started to compress. The censer flared, fresh Energy spilling into the air as the compression revealed the slightest gaps they could still fill.

I love you, she sent.

I love you too.

Five minutes.

They waited, their minds and bodies focused and tense, yet calm and relaxed at the same time. By now they'd leveled often enough to recognize the tipping point, to know when they'd done all they needed. Sometimes Seb got there first, others she had waited over an hour for him. Today they would find the moment between one breath and the next.

The paired pop, the chaotic swirl of lingering Energy as they advanced felt intense enough to rattle the tent. She didn't move, didn't breathe, didn't let the moment end even as they finished counting.

Two.. one.

She opened their eyes, nearly slamming them shut again at the glow between them. They'd lifted their hands without realizing it, his ring level with her nose, her ring with his. Both glowed brighter than ever, both pulsed in time with their emotions.

The glow faded, the emotions remained. She saw the edges of his smile past their hands, the slightest creases in the corners of his eyes as he saw the same look on her face. She knew she was smiling, she knew he was overjoyed.

They tipped onto their side, legs straightening until they lay face to face, eye to eye. They didn't need to move further, didn't need anything more than each other, not right now.

Three years, she said.

Seven years and three months, plus a few days. But who's counting.

You are, but what?

Our room.

She laughed, the moment shattering in all the right ways. Of course he knew how long, probably to the hour. She refused to believe he knew to the minute, let alone to the second, not for this.

Thirteen Days, four hours, twen-

Seb?

Yes? he answered, his tone innocently playful.
