They needed qi to stay in motion, and none of them had endless reserves to burn. That was especially true for the twins. They were not at the peak of the foundation establishment realm, and their foundations were not as stable or deep as the others’, which meant they tired faster and had to be more careful.Because of that, they could only keep the swords going for about three hours at a time before needing to stop and recover. Sometimes, even lesser, especially when other beasts noticed them and decided to test their luck. And there were plenty of them on this floor. The dragonlings had only been the beginning.If anything, they were the first warning that the beasts here had likely not tasted human flesh in a very long time and were more than willing to correct that. Even when they took the effort to set up a defensive formation around themselves during rest, they were still attacked twice before the day was over. Land beasts were easier to handle, mostly because they could see them coming and choose how to deal with them, but the real problem was the sky. This floor had too many flying creatures, and with the mountains and rocky ridges rising everywhere, it was often impossible to keep a clear route. More than once, they got attacked because they were forced to circle around some natural obstacle and ended up entering the territory of something hungry enough to test them.That costs them time, sometimes blood too.Most of those fights never became full battles. If he found it, then the rest could be decided. If he did not, then all of this would only become more complicated.He had already gathered more than enough resources for his sect by this point. The closer they flew, the larger it seemed to become, until Chen Ren had the strange feeling that it was still growing simply because his mind had not yet caught up to its true scale.At the very least, he saw no monsters around it. No guards either.That didn’t mean there were none. Xiangrui had only told them that the library contained knowledge vast enough to answer almost anything, but when Shrey asked for something from it, he had made it very clear that such knowledge would not be freely given. Chen Ren understood why. If the place truly held so much information and anyone could simply walk in and take it, then past climbers would have stripped it bare long ago. Cultivators were not known for preserving things for the sake of strangers.So Chen Ren still expected trouble.From a distance, there was nothing to fight. Once they got close enough, another problem appeared. The library was so massive that simply finding the entrance took time. They had to circle around part of the structure, tracing its outer edge while the silver walls stretched on and on beside them, and even that nearly took an hour.In the end, though, they found them.Two giant gates, set into the structure and made of reddish metal to stand apart from the silver around them.There were runes set above the gates, and for a moment Chen Ren found himself wondering whether they would need to do something specific to open them. But when Princess Yanyue simply guided the flying sword toward the doors and pressed a hand against one side, both gates swung inward at once.She glanced back at him. “That was easier than I expected.”Chen Ren nodded, then stepped off the sword and landed on the ground. Fast as the flying swords were, he was more than happy to be off them for a while. Standing on one for hours was tolerable when necessary, but it was hardly pleasant when someone else was controlling every turn and drop. Wang Jun was still there at the edge of his awareness, but between the siege, the climb, and everything that had happened before it, the two of them had hardly exchanged more than a few words in a long while.Chen Ren had almost forgotten about Wang Jun with everything that had been happening, and he knew the head was probably going to be annoyed about that. There was only height, distance, and an endless ceiling lost somewhere far beyond his sight.He knew the whole structure was massive already, but a part of him still wondered whether spatial arrays had been used to make it even larger on the inside. The scale of it certainly felt wrong enough for that. The lift kept rising and rising, yet the space above them never seemed to come any closer, as if height inside this place did not quite obey the same rules as the outside world.Like that, more than five minutes passed in uneasy peace.Eventually, everyone settled down a little. Even the twins sat, and after a while Li Qingfeng reached into his spatial ring and pulled out a few buns, passing them around without much ceremony. Chen Ren took one immediately. He could not even remember when he had last eaten properly. Pills were enough to keep a cultivator going, yes, but surviving and eating were never the same thing. Food was one of the better parts of being alive.As he chewed, another thought came to him. And Chen Ren reached into his spatial ring and brought out four tickets.That immediately caught everyone’s attention. Before any of them could start asking what they were, he decided to explain. “These are exit tickets. I got them from Shrey. If you tear one apart, it’ll take you out of the pagoda immediately. Unlike the return tickets between floors, you don’t need to be on a lift to use them.”He passed them around one by one.“I bought more of them recently,” he continued, “in case something happened and we needed a way out.”Li Qingfeng looked down at the one in his hands and looked back up. “So this is a true escape route?”The others studied theirs just as carefully.Chen Ren nodded. “Yes. Chen Ren pushed qi into his ears on instinct, just to keep them from bleeding. Then the first book hit the barrier, and Chen Ren’s eyes widened.He had been the one to give the twins those defensive artifacts. He knew how durable they were.
