Chapter V.XXXVIII (5.38) - Meeting

For once, it was Kizu’s turn to watch Emilia from across the classroom in Illusions A. Likewise, she played his usual role of pretending not to notice his stares as she chatted animatedly with her friends.

Kizu silently seethed. He couldn’t believe she could just so easily play it off as if she’d done nothing wrong. He watched those friends carefully. Not the same as the ones who’d been participating in the origami production, but he viewed anyone who spoke to Emilia as complicate. By this point, nearly every soul in the academy knew about the origami golems. They’d somehow escaped into every single common room immediately after he’d stumbled on her. Apparently, those girls had somehow left no evidence of wrong-doing.

“Careful,” Yon said. “My mother used to say that too strong of a grudge will keep your soul tied to the mortal world.”

“What?” Kizu turned his attention to the older student. “Why do you say that?”

Yon scratched at his scraggily goatee. “You look like you’re attempting to will your ex into spontaneous combustion.”

“She—”

“Oh, I know what she did. I’m not saying she doesn’t deserve your ire. Just be careful the sort of energy you put out into the world.”

Kizu blinked. “You know? What do you mean?”

Yon pulled a piece of paper from his inside jacket pocket and flipped it between two fingers. While a bit creased, it depicted the image of a griffin with a description of the creature and estimated blood cost. “I’m not certain where you saw the origami golems, but it wasn’t the only instance. There were copies spread throughout every dorm on campus. After yesterday, everyone in the academy knows you have a right to a bit of anger.”

With a heavy heart, Kizu took the card and examined it. It was undoubtedly one of Ione’s bestiary cards. However, this was only a copy, not the original.

“Ah, mind giving that back?” Yon asked sheepishly. “As far as anyone can tell, the griffin card is the second most rare seen.”

“It was made through magic,” Kizu said, handing the card back to him. “It’ll disappear in like two weeks.”

“Regardless… it’s a limited collectible. And the paper griffin was by far the hardest golem to catch.”

“Collectable?” Kizu repeated. “Do people… want to collect Ione’s cards?”

“Ha. Are you joking? Kizu, look around.”

Kizu’s frown returned as he resumed his glare down at Emilia. The sight of her increased his heartbeat, and not at all in the same way it had back in the first semester. The Tainted girl was currently showing a friend some sort of illusion that slightly altered skin tones. Not the type of illusion they were supposed to be practicing, but Ignis had stepped out of the class and left them to study on their own for a few minutes. Nearly no one in the classroom was practicing scent illusions like they were supposed to be studying. It was turning into an unpopular study since it created a reek around the spellcaster. Emilia, of course, was not about to do something unpopular.

“At anyone other than your ex, I mean,” Yon clarified, sounding exasperated.

Kizu tore his gaze from Emilia and forced himself to examine his other classmates. At first, he just saw his peers practicing illusions and chatting with one another. Then he met a few glances in his direction. They all sheepishly turned away immediately after catching his eye. That darkened his mood further. Until he noticed what several of the people glancing at him had in common. They were clutching little cards of paper.

“Damn,” Kizu whispered. “Are those….”

“Yeah,” Yon confirmed with a nod. “Might just be a quick trend, but these things are extremely fun to collect. The last time I spotted an origami golem, three students nearly fought a truel over it. Professor Grove had to step in and confiscate the card before anyone got hurt?”

“Truel?”

“Three-person duel,” Yon clarified.

“Yeah, I picked that up through context,” Kizu muttered. He just wasn’t sure if that was a real word.

“Anyway.” Yon leaned back in his seat and drummed his fingers on his armrest. He bit his lip, as if trying to work up the courage to ask for something outlandish. “I… don’t suppose your girlfriend could hook me up with any exclusive creature cards?”

“I cannot believe you believe this to be acceptable behavior from a fellow academic peer!” Sene paced back and forth in front of the Student Council room. “You do realize this is my visage they are borrowing in the creation of these foul paper golems? Me! Their Student Council President! This goes far beyond disrespect. I have half a mind to write a formal complaint to the headmaster requesting the culprit’s expulsion!”

Most Student Council meetings Kizu would have welcomed a reprieve from Sene’s constant fixation on the campus water fountains. Unfortunately, today’s meeting subject wasn’t much better. Sene had sent out a message on their scrying orbs. She demanded an emergency meeting where they could they discuss Emilia’s social attack on Ione.

This meeting was following one that had taken place between himself, Ione, Basil, Aoi, and Stina earlier that day on Owl’s Respite. They’d come up with a plan of retribution, but Sene would doubtlessly not approve of those measures, so they agreed to keep it from Ione’s twin.

“I doubt the headmaster will do more than give a slap on the wrist,” Faible said. “The student who spearheaded it is Tainted, so if he gives her the boot, she’ll have nowhere else to go.”

“Maybe she should have considered that before she slandered me!”

“There’s not much precedent for expulsion from something like this,” Kizu regretfully agreed. “In my first month at the academy I did quite a bit of research on past expelled students. It takes a lot. They need to be both a danger to the student body and a massive threat to the academy’s image.”

“I don’t know why you care,” Ione said to he sister. For once, she was awake and alert in the meeting. She stood by the window, staring down at the campus below. “It was me they were mocking, not you.”

“We are identical!” Sene slammed her hands down on the table. “People are talking. Even down in town I saw adults swapping those cards. Everyone knows. And even if we didn’t share a face you’re still my sister. It’s an affront to our family name!”

“Thanks for caring, sis,” Ione said. Her tone was quiet and genuine, without a trace of the usual irony.

“A bit slow on the uptake though,” Faible commented. “This has been brewing for over a month now. You and Kizu are probably two of the most oblivious students at the academy. How did you not see the posters set up in your dorm room?”

“She has an apartment down in town,” Ione said, speaking up in an obvious effort to cut off Sene’s incoming verbal lashing. “The dorm was too noisy and difficult for her to focus in.”

“Still, I’m not in a dorm either and I still got wind of them,” Faible muttered.

Kizu frowned. “We’re they posted in other dormitories? I didn’t see any posters in my dorm whenever I visited.”

“Basil tore them down,” Ione explained. “He’s been going around and ripping them off the walls in a bunch of dorms. Obviously, his own wouldn’t have any up for long.”

Kizu forced his face to stay neutral as he kept his emotions in check. “I don’t get why you didn’t say anything to me.”  They’d had this conversation once already during their meeting on Owl’s Respite, but with no real resolution so the bitterness bubbled out.

“Or me,” Sene said.

“I was handling it myself,” Ione said defensively. “I’m not going to run off and beg for help for every little thing.”

“This isn’t a ‘little thing,’” Kizu replied. “Apparently nearly everyone in the academy knew except for me. Ione, I know I’m a bit clueless sometimes. Honestly, I’m mostly upset with myself. I failed. But please, you need to talk to me. I want to help.”

Ione let out a massive sigh, then finally turned away from the window. “I went out of my way to try to keep it from you. It’s embarrassing and I didn’t want you or Sene involved.”  Ione’s fist clenched. “You do realize, it’s not just insulting me. You are also caught in the crossfire. Sene too, for that matter.”

“Yes!” Sene said, belligerent. “I am! Which is why I plan to write that letter expressing my disappoint—”

“Stop, Sene.” Ione raised a hand. Then she stepped forward and slapped her own open palm on the table, mirroring Sene’s gesture from the start of the meeting. “I have a plan. Listen, Emilia’s latest attempt by spreading the paper golems throughout the academy was calculated. In the last month she realized she needed to up her game to get under my skin. The origami wasn’t clever because I care about the insinuation of me humping magical creatures, that is pretty basic as far as insults and will be the thing most people focus on. However, Emilia is clever and made it an attack with multiple prongs. First, she made the origami dolls all identical copies, playing off my insecurity as Sene’s twin. That, far more than the humping, angered me. The second thing she attempted backfired.”

“The cards?” Kizu guessed.

“Yes, exactly.” Ione smirked. “She thought they were stupid and believed sharing them around would embarrass me. That backfired because my cards are badass and everyone else can recognize that fact.”

“You broke down her thought process, what does that have to do with your plan?” Faible asked.

“Faible, my dear gnome friend. There is a great deal you can tell about a person from how they attack you. Namely, they often strike in the way they would take the most damage.”

Kizu leaned back and examined his girlfriend. Palms down on the Student Council table, head down but her eyes up, her smirk opening to show teeth.

“You’re going to hit her own insecurities,” Kizu stated.

The evil smile grew. “That girl has a lot to be insecure about.”
