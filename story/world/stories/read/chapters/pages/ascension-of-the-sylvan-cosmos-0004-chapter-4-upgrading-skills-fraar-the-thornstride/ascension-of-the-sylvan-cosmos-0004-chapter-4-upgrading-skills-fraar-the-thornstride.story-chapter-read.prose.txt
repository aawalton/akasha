Days were calm for Liu Shu. Her every day consisted of leisurely looking at her surroundings while growing fruits and sometimes using her other skill called <Enchanted Growth>.

Still, Enchanted Growth was a skill that consumed too much energy, so after she used it the first day and got dizzy after just a few seconds of using it, she never used it again.

The consumption of the E+ ranked skill was still too much for a level one being, even if this being was a World Tree.

While her days passed, a system hint would arrive from time to time, clarifying a few doubts our little Liu Shu might've had. For example, she learned that the power level difference was increasingly vaster as levels increased. Another example was that skills grew in rank whenever they reached level five and capped in proficiency.

For example, <Charm of the Seedling> was level four not long ago, but earlier that day, the skill had grown to Rank F- Level Five with 498/500 proficiency.

While looking at that number, Liu Shi was expectant of seeing it grow to another level.

Our little tree saw a beetle with a beautiful yellow shell flying and landing on one of her branches with a <Delicious Berry>, and the notification was triggered.

[<Charm of the Seedling> has attracted a [Level 3 Earth Beetle]. Proficiency increased by 3. (501/500)]

[<Charm of the Seedling> has reached the maximum level. Spend 15 Experience to increase its tier.]

When Liu Shu heard the second message, her mind was filled with surprise. 'I need to spend experience?'

[System Hint: When a skill reaches level 5, it can evolve and increase in Tier. The level will be reset to one, but the effects will go under a qualitative change. Experience needed depends on the tier of the skill. Difficulty in gaining proficiency for the skill will increase.]

Liu Shu took her time reading the message and understanding it. After one hour, she finally understood everything. Thoughtful, her branches swayed as. 'Should I do it?'

Her focus changed to the accumulated experience.

[Accumulated Experience: 91]

During the last few days, a few minor bugs tried to eat her berries before they were mature, so she swatted them with her branches, giving her about two experience points per kill.

Then, Liu Shu's eyes went to her level.

[Level 1 (0/150)]

Our little tree's mind slowly moved. 'Should I save for the level-up?'

If she had brows, they would be scrunched to the point of touching. This was the second most arduous decision of her life! For those curious, the most difficult was when she chose not to level up in the past to keep her title. An outstanding achievement for our little tree!

As she thought about what to do, the Sun in the sky was about to hide on the horizon. She was a tree, after all, so she took her time.

Finally, after much consideration, she chose to use the experience to tier up the talent. Her reasoning was nothing but thinking that since she liked being surrounded by other beings, she might as well upgrade the thing that allowed her to meet all the little animals flocking around her.

Our Liu Shu had nothing but time on her hands, so she wasn't hasty to level up. With that decided, after she gave the daily energy to the flowers growing <Delicious Berries> on her branches, she looked at the little friends that played around her and used the experience.

[15 Experience Points consumed. <Charm of the seedling> has gone from F- rank to F rank.]

When the skill improved, a gentle ripple of energy spread from Liu Shu to about 20 meters around her. The plants, animals, and insects in the surroundings stopped for a second and looked at the two-meter-tall tree, their eyes, even when they didn't have intelligence, gaining a gleam of gentleness.

However, while friendly creatures were attracted, the tree's aura also drew other types of creatures.

A few moments before upgrading the talent, a quadrupedal green beast with a tree-bark-like skin, about one and a half meters long, was leisurely walking in the forest. Because it was getting late, the beast was returning to his dwellings.

This beast was a [Level 7 Thornstride].

This kind of creature fed on plants, small animals, and trees. It was a predator that evolved from plants, with an appearance similar to a lizard, with a muscular body and sharp claws that it used to slice trees and a mouth full of razor-sharp teeth to pierce into the bark and feed on it.

In a World where creatures that evolved from plant life appeared, predators specialized in eating trees were not uncommon.

As it walked, the Thornstride suddenly felt a fluctuation to its right. The pupils of the creature shrunk as the scent tempted its stomach.

As a level 7 being, it was like a tiny overlord in the nearby zone. Its strength as a predator was higher than most creatures in a small range.

Its body shifted directions, and it began walking toward where the tempting aura originated from.

Meanwhile, Liu Shu, whose roots were already spread in an area of 15 meters, saw the beast approaching from the foliage.

When Liu Shu saw the beast, she focused on it to see its stats. It was something she did often after her encounter with the beaver.

Her aura leaked, showing her strength as a World Tree, which made the Thornstride stop for a second and become wary. Although Liu Shu was just a level one, that didn't mean she was weak.

Her physique alone was 13 points, four more than the creature, not to mention her other attributes were also far superior. The only reason her vitality was low was her low level.

Moreover, after killing bugs and such that bothered her, <Willow Strike> had leveled up, becoming stronger than before.

After measuring our Liu Shu for some moments, Fraar leaped forward with incredible agility.

While a tree's thought process was usually slow, it only applied outside of combat. When a tree fought, it concentrated every single fiber of its body into the fight, making the thought process equal and, sometimes, superior to that of common creatures.

When Fraar was 5 meters away from Liu Shu, Aether Mana flowed from her trunk, enveloping one of her dropping branches, and it lashed like a whip at astonishing speed.
