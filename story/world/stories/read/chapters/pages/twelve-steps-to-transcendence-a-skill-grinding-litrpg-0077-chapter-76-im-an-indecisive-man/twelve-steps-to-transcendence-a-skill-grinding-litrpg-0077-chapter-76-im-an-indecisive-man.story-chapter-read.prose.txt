He had already read through the first two options, which were the author signed [Mana Shell] construct blueprint and a memory shard from the Tier 3 Guardian. So he only had the last two unknowns left to consider.

And he hoped they were good. So when he read the third line, he felt his eyebrows creep up his forehead.

- [Guardian Core] (Crafting Material)

A core?

He frowned, trying to figure out what he was supposed to do with that. He didn't know the first thing about crafting, and building gear sounded like a massive undertaking for him right now... But it did seem like something interesting he could try later on, so maybe in the future?

Right now, all he could do was, maybe, get someone to make something of it. Still, it was a boss drop, so some rich guild would probably pay a small fortune to take it off his hands, which he'd be super glad to do.

Money would help him a lot on his path, after all.

The final choice gave him such a surprise though that he forgot about selling things.

- [Hidden Rift Key: Tier 2] (Single Use)

He stared at it, reading the words a few times just to make sure his brain wasn't playing tricks on him. A hidden rift key? And a Tier 2 one too… That had to be stupidly valuable, right? He didn't know what a hidden rift was but from the name alone it sounded like something he should be taking.

Nice to have a key to pop into a rift whenever he wanted.

And being hidden meant he didn't have to answer to anybody either, so he could just do his thing without a care in the world.

Unfortunately, there was a flashing warning right below the options...

[Please select one reward.]

He sighed and fell onto his pillow. The system loved to twist the knife. It was just obnoxious to give him four great options and then make him burn three of them.

Let's just go over the options.

He looked at the [Mana Shell] blueprint at the top. It was a very tempting thing, mostly because he'd prefer to have a trick up his sleeve to not die. For the time being, his only real strategy for surviving a hit was to just hope he lived long enough to heal after he was out of healing bombs.

So having a real defense sounded pretty amazing. He didn't know what the author-signed tag really was, but he suspected it was much like [Learning Model] in that someone left helpful notes.

His eyes followed the options down to the memory shard. It was from the strongest thing he had ever fought, so the memory shard might give him something big, maybe a skill of some sort, or an important memory of the guardian that could help him be better at fighting or mana manipulation or using any of his skills.

But that was the issue: it might. It was way too big of a gamble to bet on. After all, he could also just as easily end up with nothing at all.

So I don't think it's a good choice...

He moved on to the guardian core. What did the crafting material tag mean, anyway? Since it was an option alongside all the others, it had to be very valuable, but he didn't know the first thing about making gear or crafting, so if he picked this over the others, it would just end up as a really expensive rock to him.

Which meant it would just sit in his bag of holding doing nothing unless he found someone who could make use of it and either craft something or just sell it for a good price.

Last but not the least was the key.

He didn't know rifts could reward keys to other rifts, but now he knew. It wasn't technically a reward to be honest. It could supposedly open the door to a rift he knew nothing about. But that could also mean a ridiculous amount of opportunities and rewards.

He looked up and down the list of options, went round in circles a few times, wondering which one to pick, and he had to admit that he got nowhere.

Honestly, all the options were good in their own way, and that was kind of the problem. He rubbed his temples, irritated. He really hated having to throw away three of them.

He looked blankly at the ceiling.

What can I do... I'm an indecisive man.
