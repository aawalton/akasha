"A kingdom, Froðe? Really?" I ask incredulously.

We are back in our room, retiring after the evening meal and a long day of The Thing. I'm pacing back and forth, processing this development, while Froðe leans back against the wall near our beds watching me amusingly. His arms are crossed and he has one leg propped up against the wall.

"Yes, the locals call it...Northumbria."

Northumbria. Right...because England wasn't a country until the 10th century or so.

"And you really think that we can take on a Kingdom's army? Do you even know anything about them?"

"We?" Froðe asks for the second time this evening, only instead of a frown towards his brother, he's slipping me crooked grin. The one that makes my heart do somersaults against my own volition. Dammit! Stop doing that!

I bristle, pausing in my pacing to face him.

"I was speaking figuratively. Obviously I'm not joining you on this raid."

"Hmmm..." Froðe pushes off the wall and prowls to me in two paces alone, looping an arm around my waist to pull me towards him. The other sweeps my copper curls back and then cups my face. The gesture makes my eyelids flutter, and he tilts my face up to his. "Are you worried about me, Beatrix?" Ice blue eyes glitter at me with amusement, but also something much deeper. I blink before I can get lost in them.

"I just...I don't get why you are risking everything you have built here to potentially lose it all when you go against a king's army," I finally say. Also I don't get why Odin would be this involved if he were just testing mortals. Something just doesn't add up to me. Could I be wrong about Froðe's real identity? Doubt pulls at my once certainty from the other night, and Froðe mistakes my frown for concern, massaging my cheek with his thumb.

"I will not lose it fiery one...and with this raid the risk will be worth the award. A king's castle holds more gold than any monastery."

My eyes narrow. "You're still not addressing the whole army part of my question."

Froðe presses his lips along my jawline, eliciting a moan from me. He is trying to distract me...

"Do not worry...I have a plan," he says against my skin, the heat of his breath setting my skin aflame.

...and it's fucking working.

I groan in frustration and turn my head away, not only because of Froðe's elusiveness to my questions but also because I am starting to feel it again. All wound up with no release in sight. The past few days have been such sweet, sweet torture but I'm about to lose my fucking mind. Froðe, well aware of what he is doing and the affect it has, chuckles darkly into my neck as he burrows into it.

"I love seeing this side of you," he whispers, bringing me closer.

"The annoyed side?" I shoot back.

A smile forms against my skin.

"The fiery side."

I snort, but find myself wrapping my own arms around him, tracing my fingers through his now unbound hair. Froðe groans in content, the sound almost reminding me like a cat's purr.

"When you decide you are ready to join us on the raids, I will include you in our planning. For now...I do not want you to worry, especially since you are still recovering," Froðe says. I continue stroking his hair, considering this.

"You said 'when.' What makes you so certain I will?" I ask, genuinely curious, and surprisingly not offended given my past exposure to raids. What does that mean for me?

"Mmmm....I have an inkling there is much more fire in you yet," Froðe answers, placing kisses along the crook of my neck. "You have the makings of a warrior. I can feel it," he adds, in a husky voice, dragging his lips and heat of his breath along my skin. A deep ache unfurls from within my core and my breath hitches.

I'm desperate for him to keep touching me like this, but I also know it will just continue to set me on fire until I completely lose myself. Yet...Skuld's orders were very clear, and much to my utter dismay, Froðe has been patiently following them to the "T" in order for me to recover swiftly.

So...unbelievably...patient. But not without plenty of teasing like this.

The man is going to make me lose my fucking mind.

"Come to bed," Froðe whispers into my neck. "We should get some rest."

Riiiight....Like I can just fall asleep after that.

But I oblige, and soon we find ourselves entangled once again in our makeshift pushed together double bed. Froðe is wrapped around me, my back nestled into his chest. Our fingers are entwined with his arm wrapped around me, and sleep inevitably comes to take me, Froðe's breathing already slowed deep in slumber.

***

Black smoke and the smell of sulfur. The shrilling screech of metal grinding upon metal, and the feeling of free falling through the skies.

Where....where am I?

But I don't have time to think about that, because I'm falling through the clouds. My arms and legs flail about frantically, my copper curls violently whipping around my face. The rush of wind is so loud it's all I can hear, yet my heart is pounding so hard it's about to rip itself out of my chest. Time slows, and my mouth is open in a silent scream while tears fly out from my eyes towards the heavens.

"BEATRIX!!!!" A voice screams not far away. A voice I do not know...yet...my soul is torn in half at the sound of that voice's sheer panic. It elicits a pull from me that I cannot fathom to explain.

Something massive soars above me, reflecting the light of the sun so brightly that it sears my vision for a heartbeat. It's so blinding that I have to close my eyes for a moment, and when I reopen them, it's already gone.

I continue to fall...fall...fall...but now I'm through the smoke. Soot and ash is replaced with the vibrant pinks, yellows and oranges of a spectacular sunset over thick clouds. I'm clearly falling to my demise, but it's the most beautiful sight I've ever seen. Besides, death is nothing new to me these days...

Don't be so dramatic. I am coming.

The words ring into my mind like a bell, but they are not my own. They are...masculine and ancient. A voice that has lived through centuries of war and gives zero fucks.

What? Who...who's there?

A pregnant pause, despite me still plummeting to my death. How high am I up anyways?!

....Are you well?

My eyes widen in shock, as if it wasn't shocking enough to suddenly be falling through the skies there is also someone else in my head. Is this really a dream? And who the hell is in my head?!

Be ready! He comes for you.

He?? Who.....

Before I can think anything further, I'm plucked out of the sky by a giant claw that tears and sinks into my flesh, then proceeds to rip me apart limb from limb.

***

I jolt out of bed, screaming bloody murder and holding my gut where I swear I can still feel that claw protruding from my stomach.

It felt...so...real....

"BEATRIX!!" Froðe shouts, cupping my face in his hands and forcing my gaze to his. "BEATRIX!"

I'm still screaming, but I force my eyes to his. Ice blue centers me, grounds me back to the present. I choke on my own breath but manage to finally stop screaming.

His brow furrows in worry, and his voice softens.

"It was just a dream, fiery one. You are alright now...you are safe with me."

Froðe gently sweeps my hair out of my face with one hand, and wipes my tears away with the other. His troubled eyes make him look like he wishes he could leap into that dream himself and slay whatever caused me such pain. If he could find a way he probably would.

He holds me like that, continuing to stroke my hair, until my breathing finally calms and I shudder with a deep sigh.

"Well that fucking sucked," I lament.

"Tell me about it. What did you see?" Froðe says against my forehead.

I hesitate. What did I see? It was such a strange dream...almost like I was in a completely different place. It didn't feel like any of the other realms I've encountered in this world.

"Remember...your dreams hold more power here. They may speak of what is to come."

"I'm not really sure what to make of it," I say, honestly, but Froðe waits patiently for me to continue. "I was falling through the sky, and I heard a voice in my head. The voice of something ancient and dark...before I was torn to pieces by an unknown enemy."

Froðe physically winces at that last part, enough for me to feel it.

"It was not who spoke to you?" he asks.

"No...if anything...he was trying to warn me."

"Hmmmm...perhaps one of the Gods spoke to you directly, and is trying to warn you of a danger that is coming," Froðe muses aloud. "Wasn't that the case of your last dream?"

"It would seem that way," I say, but something still seems off. It doesn't quite line up, but I don't have enough information to consider what else it could mean.

Froðe opens his mouth to say something, but there's a knock on our door. He looks to me, brow still furrowed in worry but I give him a nod.

We'll come back to this later, I need to stew on it a bit more.

"Enter."

Torsten walks in while Froðe gets up to pour me a tankard of water. I take the tankard gratefully and gulp down half of it.

"Jarl, It's o' time."

My mind races before I remember. The execution. I completely forgot. Both Hiida and Ragnar will lose their lives today by public execution. I look to Froðe, but his calm, cool mask is back in place and he gives Torsten a grim look.

"Have them brought before the tavern. I will be there in ten minutes. Make sure everyone is gathered to witness."

"Aye, Jarl," Torsten nods, then leaves.

"You do not have to witness it if you do not wish to..." Froðe begins, his voice a softer tone that he seems to only have reserved for me.

"I'll be there," I say, surprising myself, but I'm fiddling with a lose thread on the hem of my shift. "This is the way of your people, is it not?" I look up to Froðe, to find him studying me as always.

"Yes. The whole clan will witness it."

"Then I am no exception, Froðe." If I am going to be part of this clan, to really have a life among these people, I need to start living by their ways as well. I will lose any shred of respect I have gained from them if I am not there for the execution. But at the same time, my heart plummets into my stomach at the thought of watching a public execution, let alone witnessing Froðe perform it.

Something resembling a glimmer of pride shines in Froðe's gaze as he acknowledges my wish, along with something much deeper, but it's gone as he begins to dress into his finer tunic. I take that as a sign that I must also dress for the occasion, and slide out of bed to do the same. My stomach is in knots, and my hands shake as I slip into my finer clothes. If Froðe notices, he gives me the courtesy of not saying anything, and we continue to get ready quietly.

Minutes later, we stand in front of the tavern with the clan assembled before us. Torsten brings Ragnar and Hilda in tow, but they are unbound. Ragnar's head is held high, but his eyes are deeply sunken into his face, haunted. Hilda walks by his side, staring at him like she's memorizing every feature of his face for the last time.

She choose to die with him. To forfeit entry into Valhalla.

While I'm disgusted that she betrayed her husband Leif for an affair, the other part of me can't deny how profound her love must be for Ragnar to follow in him in death like this, despite how dark and twisted it may be. She condoned the murder of her husband to be with her lover, and tried to protect him during the trial.

Love can make someone lose themselves completely, I suppose.

I shudder at the thought, and Froðe notes the movement, glancing at me. His brow is slightly raised in question, as if checking in to see if I'm still up for this. His hair is in his typical half braid, and the golden chains on his tunic glint in the morning light. I give him a reassuring look, before facing back forward to our charges.

"Ragnar, son of Dagny, and Hilda, daughter of Rune, you have been charged with crimes against our people and have chosen your fate. Let your execution be a warning to others to continue to abide by our laws or face the consequences."

Torsten steps forward, handing Froðe his giant battle axe. Froðe takes it, while Torsten forces Ragnar and then Hilda to their knees. Not a word of protest, but Ragnar finally looks at Hilda, and she at him, and they share a look of deep longing that will haunt me.

But I don't look away.

Not even when Froðe lifts his massive arms above his head, axe in hand, before bringing it down upon Ragnar's neck. Not even when blood splatters through the crisp, morning air and Ragnar's head is no longer attached to his body, rolling about the ground. Not even when Hilda, shrieking in grief, reaches for Ragnar's head and clutches it to her chest, deep crimson blooming upon her apron dress. Not even when Froðe grunts from exertion, bringing that blood stained axe upon her next.

I watch it all, even though something cracks and shatters deep within me, and I feel myself moving along a path that will irrevocably change me forever.
