Landing atop of Mugtopia, Archie straightened up and gave a glance over the edge of the rooftop. The view wasn’t pretty.

The street he’d just cleared out was starting to fill again with bodies, some cautious, others far too curious for their own good, and in the distance, shouting, clanging steel, and loud explosions could be heard in the nearby streets.

What the hell is going on? Archie asked himself as he could visibly see spells shoot over rooftop buildings off in the distance and exploding midair. Did I just spawn into some city in the middle of a civil war or something?

Suddenly, Sixth Sense began to rumble towards his far left. Snapping his head in that direction, he could see a massive castle off in the distance, but mere seconds after he took notice of the castle, the mana he could visibly feel building up within the castle was now reaching-.

He would need to leave asap, as it didn’t take a genius to figure out this city was going to boil over soon.

“Yeah… staying here’s a death wish,” he muttered. “That castle is about to blow any second now.”

Without much thought and following the direction Sixth Sense was guiding him to go in to escape the bast, Archie jumped again, boots thumping across rooftops as he moved. One, five, fourteen buildings over until he found a flat stretch of rooftop that was far enough from a blast as large as the one the castle was building up.

The roof he landed on was wide and made out of burnt orange brick, and mostly empty except for some rusted chimneys and a few long-dead pigeons.

Eh- it’ll do for now.

He casually swung the guy he was carrying by the leg, who was still out cold, and tossed him onto the roof like a sack of wet laundry. The guy’s body hit the surface with a meaty thud – his head visibly bouncing twice – yet he didn’t make a sound.

Archie walked over, squatted beside the guy’s head, and just… stared. One eyebrow slowly rising.

“Alright, any time now.”

Nothing.

A couple of seconds passed.

Still nothing.

Archie rolled his jaw and sighed, digging into his Spatial Storage. From it, he pulled out his Water Tin and tipped it over to splash its cold contents over the face of the guy whom he assumed to be the leader of the group that had attacked him after exiting Mugtopia.

However, even after splashing at least 10 liters worth of water atop the guy’s face, he supposedly did not wake up.

“Okay, don’t tell me I actually knocked you out that bad, c’mon, I didn’t even use a full-strength punch.”

Still, no reaction.

With a growing sense of both concern and curiosity, Archie nudged the guy’s cheek with the tip of his boot, then once more with a little twist of his foot and digging it a bit deeper into his face.

Just as he was about to dig his foot deeper into the guy’s face, a massive explosion erupted from far behind him, followed by the hundreds of screams and shouts erupting from all around him. What the hell was that?

The rooftop he was atop of trembled, and as the building he was on was shaking, he could hear the muffled crying and screaming of those below him.

Archie slowly turned his head from the castle and back to the guy who was still supposedly unconscious after all of that. “Okay...”

No change – except for the slightest flutter at the base of the guy’s throat. Archie narrowed his eyes, catching the subtle bob of his Adam’s apple as he swallowed hard.

Deciding to give the guy a bit of existential dread, he turned back around to stare at the flying and aflame chunks of the castle and watched as they struck the numerous buildings around it and began to set them alight.

Archie rolls his eyes and lets out a huff, as he sees the guy still not moving or opening his eyes, “For fu… Why do you gotta make this harder for yourself?”

From his Spatial Storage, he fishes out a small, black-stoppered vial – one of the two vials he’d found inside Mugtopia before he’d left it. He pops the top, then crouches back down beside the still-"unconscious" man.

“Alright, buddy. You left me no choice.”

He brings the vial close to the guy’s nose, wanting the man to get an understanding of what was about to happen to him, watching for any twitch – but the man gave no response. So, with an uncaring sigh, he tipped the contents over and let the oil splash right onto the guy’s crotch.

The guy doesn’t move.

Archie clicks his tongue, then says loud enough for him to hear, “I’m gonna set that on fire.”

Still nothing.

Archie shrugs. “Alright.”

With a soft fwoom, a thin stream of orange and gold flame dances to life above his open palm as he activates Forgesmith’s Flame. While the flame was utterly harmless to anything alive and at most would just make you feel a bit warm – the guy who was supposedly still unconscious didn’t know that.

He lowers the flame slowly. “Three… two…”

The fire licks the oil.

The second the flame touches the leather, the oil erupts into a flash of light and warm heat – flaring bright and dramatic, setting the guy’s crotch alight.

“AAAAAHHHHHHHH!” the man bolted upright with a blood-curdling scream, slapping and smacking at his leather leggings that were on fire. “Make it stop! – What the hell! – It’s gone! I can’t feel it anymore! AAAHHH!”

Archie leans back on his heels, arms crossed, waiting for the guy to stop screaming and feel that the flames were utterly harmless

The man blinks wildly, still flailing as the flame clings unnaturally to the soaked leather, bright and terrifying, but coming to the realization that the flames weren’t what he thought. “I– I can’t feel…! It’s… not hot?”

“Yup,” Archie replies, gesturing lazily. “It won’t even give you a tan.”

The guy freezes mid-swat, eyes going wide as he glances down at himself, then up at Archie, then back at his own very much intact crotch before falling on his ass.

“…You psycho!”

“That’s fair,” Archie says, rising to his feet and brushing tufts of his hair back inside his headdress. “Now that you’re feeling wide awake and chatty, mind telling me why you and your gang decided to dogpile me five minutes after I stepped out of Mugtopia?”

The guy groans, flopping backward again like a puppet with its strings cut, warm steam still rising gently from his pants. “We… we thought you were with them.”

Archie frowns. “Them… who?”

The man doesn’t answer right away, instead, he just stares up at the cloudy, ash-stained sky.

“The ones inside the castle.”

Archie stiffens slightly, his eyes narrowing. “You're gonna need to be a hell of a lot more specific than that. ‘Cause you aren’t making any sense right now.”

The guy shifted uncomfortably, though not because his leather leggings were still faintly steaming. “The nobles,” he muttered at last, voice laced with bitterness.

Archie tilts his head, filing that away. So, the city’s noble class had that kind of rep, huh? Not surprising, most historical fantasy books and nonfiction historical books he’d read were the same in that regard.

“Well, I ain’t no noble,” Archie answered, and while technically, yeah, he was a noble now, it’d probably be best the guy didn’t need to know that little tidbit.

“Alright. First question. Why the hell did you attack me?” Archie asks as he leans against the stone fence that went along the edge of the rooftop, doing his best to ignore the screaming and shouting below.

“You busted through the old tavern door like a lunatic in our territory,” the guy snapped. “Not to mention that you go out looking like a noble and put the streets into chaos.”

Archie blinked. “The street was already in chaos.”

Not to mention that he said I looked like a noble even though I’m practically wearing a knight’s armor minus the Savage Ursine Headdress, so with that logic, I guess nobles are knights, Archie thought to himself, filing away that tidbit of information.

“Yeah, well–” The guy coughs, rubbing his face with a wet sleeve. “That street’s always like that, it’s the Blue Wolf’s territory, but you just made it worse.”

Archie paused. “The what now?”

“The Blue Wolf gang,” the guy says, puffing his chest like he expected that name to carry weight. “Today we were under orders to stir up as much of a ruckus as we could within our territory to get the guards curious and come looking.”

Archie held back a snort. "Blue Wolf," he muttered under his breath. Well, now I know that the Blue Wolf gang took part in blowing up that castle.

He trailed off, eyes flicking toward the burning castle now belching thick black smoke into the sky. His face slackened in shock. “...Shit. That wasn’t part of the plan… Mother Almighty.”

“Which city am I in right now?” Archie asked, wanting to get away from why he’d been attacked and get more information on his surroundings.

The guy turned and gave him a blank look. “What? Are you high?”

Archie’s eyebrow twitched. “Answer the question or I’m actually gonna set you on fire.”

The man flinched hard. “Alright! Alright! You’re in the slums of Greeber, alright? Happy?”

Archie gave a noncommittal grunt, rubbing his chin as he looked out over the skyline. Greeber… What a shitty name to give to a city.

He glanced down over the edge of the rooftop again, watching as columns of smoke danced lazily into the air and the general populace began to calm down, now that the explosions and shaking had finally stopped. I really should get to moving away from here.

“Okay, last one for now at least,” Archie said, turning back toward the still oil slick man who was not standing on both legs. “What the hell’s going on in this city? Like… overall. You said the Blue Wolf gang’s causing a ruckus, but why now? Why the castle?”

The guy blinked slowly, clearly debating whether giving too much info to Archie, but given the whole flaming crotch situation just minutes prior and the beating both he and his team had not 5 minutes ago… he decided that trying to fuck with Archie would be bad.

After a second, he shrugged with a kind of tired resignation. “A lot of us don’t really know. Orders came from higher up to stir things up and keep the guards distracted. Word on the street was that something big was going down in the castle, some kinda… nobles’ summit or ritual or whatever. My group was to just focus on the distractions and keep this section of our territory as intended.”

“A ritual?” Archie’s brows rose. “Like a sacrifice ritual?”

“I think so…” The guy nodded uncommittedly, sitting up slowly and groaning. “All we knew was that it had something to do with the current noble families and some inheritance dispute, and every gang in the city was involved.”

Archie tilted his head, mind working double-time now. “Right when I show up, too,” he muttered. “Whelp, this is one hell of a start to this event.”

The guy gave him a side-eye but said nothing.

“Alright,” Archie said, pushing off the wall and dusting himself down. “I’m done with questions for now, so just sit still till I’ve got some more.”

The guy squinted up at him suspiciously, using Identify on Archie.

The guy blinked.

Then blinked again.

His eyes went wide.

And he paled.

“What…” he croaked, voice suddenly dry. “What the hell... you’re… You’re not an E-Grade.”

Archie didn’t say anything, instead, he gave him a flat look.

“You’re–” The guy’s mouth moved, but no sound came out. Then he tried again, quieter. “You’re a D-Grade…”

Archie offered him the faintest of smirks. “No shit,” he said. “What, did you now decide to Identify me?”
