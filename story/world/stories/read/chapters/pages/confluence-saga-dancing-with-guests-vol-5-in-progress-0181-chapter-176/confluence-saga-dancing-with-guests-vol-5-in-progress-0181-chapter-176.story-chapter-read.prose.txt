Breaking News

Against all odds, against nearly every prediction, the first Velli Scion in the most recent batch to be paired off turns out to be Clyde Velli.

For someone believed to have been dead until quite recently, someone who has absolutely no presence within Society at large, Clyde quickly became one of the biggest catches of the decade.

The agreement was formalized between Clyde and Michelle Hammerswell late last week according to sources. Michelle is currently Heir to the principal cadet branch of the Hammerswells, known for their social and economic skills unlike most of their kin.

No details as to which family's name either will be using going forward, nor when any formal assumption of roles within the Holding will occur.

Cara slipped her arm around Gwen without thinking as they stood at the entrance to the cavern. She felt Gwen's thoughts shift as she did, felt her move to settle more comfortably against her side. The cavern was nearly cleared, they'd be moving on to their third of the day and yet they'd barely done anything.

"This is most unusual," Raoul said from beside them.

"We're sorry," Gwen said. "We aren't trying to make things more complicated."

"Don't worry about that. Injured team members can be present as observers if they need more time to recover but the team as a whole wishes to keep going. Healers can wait at the entrance to avoid risking themselves if that is their strategy. Controllers stand wherever makes most sense for their style of overwatch."

"But we aren't injured, we aren't controllers, and Cara isn't a healer."

"My point was that nothing says the entire team must fight. Only that it must be present."

"What's unusual then?" Cara asked.

"You were not on their team yesterday. Elite teams almost never change once they've been together a decade, not unless some of them retire."

Cara nodded but said nothing. Having Gwen tucked against her felt right, felt natural. Standing here watching Anya and Seb make short work of the stragglers on the other hand felt truly bizarre. She wanted to join in, wanted to fight side by side with Gwen, with them.

We could try again? she sent.

I doubt it will be less intense, less intrusive. I doubt they want us peeking that way.

You'd rather watch?

They are impressive, Gwen sent. And I have the best company.

Cara smiled. They were impressive. They'd sparred together, dueled against each other, but watching them against the goblins was something else. Seb's awareness was on full display if you knew what to look for, nothing snuck up on them, nothing flanked them. An opening behind Anya vanished when she pivoted at the perfect moment, a group preparing to strike got hit by a pulse of Energy as Seb's blade flew by, disrupting their formation.

Anya flowed between her enemies, keeping them focused on her, whittling them down quickly enough to never be overwhelmed. Seb seemed to be everywhere, moving back and forth to manage the flow.

Cara could see where she could slot in, where her presence would shift everything and relieve the growing pressure. It wouldn't speed anything up, just simplify matters. Gwen would be safe, between her and Anya there were plenty of options for her to engage without risk of surprise. If only they could make the band work for them all.

They could hold it in the tunnels or at camp. The technique was simple. They even managed to get through the first cavern working as a group. Not long into the second cavern everything had nearly gone wrong. Anya and Seb had seemed to vanish, to become a single entity in the band, they were still clearly two sets of thoughts, but there was no way to disentangle them. No way to ignore the emotions that wrapped them together. Gwen had dropped out first, Cara following a heartbeat behind as they both fled the intimacy.

Seb had come to a stop next to them, made sure they weren't hurt, then charged back into the fray. He hadn't asked what was wrong, hadn't clarified the situation. There were goblins to kill, to keep from overwhelming Anya.

"We should have made the change weeks ago," Gwen said. "I was wrong to delay things."

"If you say so," Raoul answered. "I don't know the story, and I'm not asking."

I'm sorry I didn't act sooner, Gwen said. That it hung between us.

I'm sorry for how I acted about it. It's done now.

Hard not to act when you're told you need to..

By someone other than your sworn who knows you best? Cara sent teasingly.

Hush.

Cara had been slightly surprised at how Gwen had reacted this morning when Anya told her they were done working with Bern. It hadn't been a suggestion, even if it still sounded like one. Gwen had visibly relaxed at the words, then acted in a way Cara rarely saw but wished was more common.

Gwen hadn't hesitated. She'd gone to find Bern and Morgan. She'd told them, calmly, that she would not be Diving with Bern, not today, likely not ever. That she'd made an honest effort and nothing had changed. She was done.

No hedging about her intent, no pausing to let someone try to convince her otherwise. She made her position and opinion clear, even if not sharing all the details, and then left with a smile on her face. Cara had seen Morgan's nod of approval, seen Bern's visible frustration. Her Gwen hadn't felt the need to, it wasn't going to change anything.

Your Gwen? Back to that again? Gwen sent, somehow interpreting Cara's unfiltered emotions.

When you show everyone how strong and competent you are? Yes.

What do you think happened? she sent.

Not sure. They insisted they were fine. They're waiting with-

Raoul. I know. You did tag all three of them so we'd be able to keep track.

Time to find out, he sent. Unless you like standing next to me surrounded by goblins.

I seem to remember you proposing in a situation almost exactly like this one, she answered, her thoughts teasing. Did we miss any today?

You know we didn't.

He slipped his hand into hers as they walked back towards the others. They didn't do more than enjoy the contact, they didn't need to, not right now.

He saw Gwen and Cara both straighten as they neared, visibly taking a half step away from one another as though they hadn't been supporting one another. He wasn't sure whether they were trying to pretend it hadn't happened, to imply they were paying attention the whole time, or something else.

They'd better not be planning to treat me like their superior, Anya grumbled. I don't need them to greet me.

Is that what they're doing?

Gwen seemed to stand even straighter as they came to a stop, thankfully not quite saluting.

"Gwen," Anya said. "What are you doing?"

"I apologize that we had to drop the band."

"Don't worry about it," Seb said. "No one was hurt, there's nothing to apologize for."

Seb caught her eyes flick towards him before focusing back on Anya.

"Anya," she said. "I'm sure if we have the chance we'll do better next time."

"Gwen," Anya said sharply. "What do you think you're doing? You're not in trouble. You're not going to be in trouble."

"But we pulled out of the band mid fight, we distracted you."

"I know, and we want to know why," Anya said and held up her hand when Gwen took a breath. "I'm not planning to start ordering you around or treating you like you serve me. I thought I made that clear last night. You're family, both of you."

Seb took a step forward as Cara's eyes widened, offering her his arm to steady herself. Her grip nearly made him wince even though it only lasted for an instant.

"Sorry," she whispered.

"Better than anyone falling."

"Cara?" Anya said.

"You called me family, not just Gwen."

"Did you think we were going to just abandon you?"

"No. But I'm her Sworn, I go where she goes, I shadow her."

Seb took a deep breath, mirroring Anya as their band echoed with frustration.

"Raoul," Anya said. "Might we have a moment? I'm assuming we're headed further down the tunnel next?"

"Yes. I think I can trust you all not to try to go into another cavern if I'm out of ear shot."

Seb nodded and watched as the man jogged off in silence.

"First," he said. "You aren't just a Sworn, Gwen's or otherwise. You were our Guest too. And now you're family."

"Second," Anya said. "I don't know what following as extended family means to you, what you think it changes. I'm not going to start ordering you around any more than I had been ordering you around as our Guest. I'm not going to expect you to treat me with any more deference or honors than the rest of our family, and you've seen how my sisters act."

"But they're your sisters," Gwen said.

"Fine, then how Briana treats me, she's only my sister's girlfriend."

Technically.

You're not going to win that bet.

"So you will give me orders."

"Fine," Gwen said as she began to laugh. "If you insist on making it an order I suppose I have no choice but to obey."
