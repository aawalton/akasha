I sat down in my chair, making sure I was comfy as I observed the mostly empty field. Disciples were moving across the grass slowly, I watched them approach the cabins before raising their hands and flick a strand of Qi towards the doors.

A moment later the doors opened, people began filing out of the cabins, forming neat little semicircles around the disciples as they looked expectantly and even hopeful at them.

My mind wandered off while I waited for them to finish moving around. I noticed the disciples begin to lead their groups towards the middle of the field where they were lined up, facing towards the raised platform. I glanced at it before remembering that was where the ceremony's host sat.

I glanced over my shoulder as I considered working on my sword and foot techniques again, but before I could fully form that thought, I heard a soft voice from my left.

“Don't you dare think about abandoning that seat!” Itshu Biyu softly hissed.

I turned my head back to the screens. I was about to start looking for Yue when I heard a booming voice reach me from across the field, it sounded like it was next to me and on the other side of the stadium at the same time.

“Congratulations to all the applicants that made it through this week's recruitment ceremony.”

An image of a middle-aged man, wearing the Void Walking Sect robes, appeared above the hopefuls as they turned their heads trying to see him. He beamed down at them happily, A few moments later he continued talking.

“Shall we get the unfortunate news out the way first?” He asked everyone, then waited a couple of heartbeats, for everyone to register what he said before he began rattling off names like he was a news reporter that had more important things to do and was rushing through his list. Once he stopped, he waited again for people to register that their name had been said before he spoke again.

“If you heard your name, I am sorry to say you did not meet the requirements to enter the sect, and must ask you to please leave,” a door on the far side of the field suddenly opened to allow them to leave, as they walked across the field looking sad and crushed the host continued to talk.

“Worry not, this ceremony's surprise is all of your accrued debt has been distributed amongst those that did pass, you will be leaving today debt free, and you shall receive a bonus from the sect as a thank you for your time.”

The man paused there, and we all watched them cross the field and leave through the doors before they slowly began to close, once they had everyone’s attention returned to the hopefuls that had passed and were waiting to hear what was going to happen next.

“Congratulations, you have all met the minimum requirements to enter the Void Walking Sect. To speed things up we will group names into those that have been accepted by the same deacons or commanders, once your name has been called, and you know where you are headed, please leave the field and find your way to the designated meeting point, by following the signs.”

The opposite side of the field, from the door that let the previous group out, slowly started creaking as it opened. Everyone seemed to watch it until it was fully open and waiting like an open maw for the new disciples to walk through.

The host started rattling off names just as fast as he had last time, I watched as they were directed by the nearby disciples to step out of the line and head over to an open area. Once the host had finished saying the names, he waited for the last person to move into the new group of people before he spoke again.

“Thank you for joining our Sect, unfortunately you did not meet the requirements to reach the outer sect disciple list and now have a choice, you may walk through the gates and follow the wash-out signs or the Civilian signs, wash out means you decline the offer and wish to try again next year or give up on becoming a cultivator. Those that wish to accept you may follow the civilian signs, this will lead you to the Void Walking Servants Academy welcoming terminal, where you will be trained to work in the sect as a servant, this can range from sweeping floors to construction, maintenance to engineering. A full list of your job options can be found at the academy." He rolled his shoulders as he motioned for them to make their decision, not really wanting to list all the jobs that are done to keep the sect running.

They headed towards the doors as a group, but when they reached them, the group split, some heading towards the left door and most of the others heading towards the right. I had no idea which group was accepting to be Sect Servants and which was declining. The doors slowly closed behind the group when the last walked past them.

I turned to look back at the image of the host before hearing a third double door gate somewhere near me suddenly start creaking as it opened. I glanced down trying to spot it, but I did not manage to find it before the host began talking again.

“Congratulations to the remaining candidates, you have all managed to make it into the Void Walking Sect as outer sect disciples.” What followed was a shorter list than the previous two, and this time he was moving slower, making sure to give each name the respect to be heard by all instead of the previous rapid pace.

When he finished reading the names and the last person had moved away from the group, he spoke again, but the image above had turned to directly look at them.

“Congratulations, on making it into the outer sect of the Void Walking Sect. However, it is my sad duty to inform you that, you were not selected by any Deacons or Commanders, therefore you will head through the south side doors and from there you will be sorted into placements around the sect, where you can thrive and show you belong, the harder you work to reach the goals the more people will take notice, it is possible for you to one day stand amongst the crowd, looking for young disciples of your own!” He roared the last part as the crowd erupted into applause.

The group turned and started eagerly jogging across the field towards the gates with excitement. I watched them run under me and through where I presumed the gate was before returning my attention to the host and what looked like a tiny group of those that still remained.

Once they had left and the doors had creaked closed, I saw doors on the north side start opening as she watched the host turn to the group that remained with more joy on his face.

After a few moments he began to rattle off names before pausing, he then pointed towards a section of the crowd where a deacon stood watching the group of disciples that had just been named, the host, of course, named the Deacon. I watched the candidates start moving, after being directed to by the nearby disciples, through the north gate as the deacon left.

The group in the middle of the field thinned as more were named and pointed towards their deacon that had chosen to take them. By lunchtime, there were only two hundred candidates left waiting.

“Congratulations to those that remain, you have received a request to join more than one deacon.” He started calling individual names, then listing two or more deacons before allowing the candidate to decide which deacon they wished to follow.

I quickly turned to ask Itshu Biyu about this, but before I could say anything, she spoke first. “Yes, candidates are, of course, allowed to decide their fate, we do not force anyone into following a certain deacon, all those that were chosen and left the field get to choose to follow the deacon that selected them or request entry into another deacon's jurisdiction. If they fail to be accepted, then they follow the candidates that simply were not selected and are processed into the Sect as if they had not been selected. I have already declined over 200 requests, by the way.” I raised an eyebrow before I nodded my head and turned back to watching the field.

I did consider asking if she had accepted any but when my head turned toward her, she shook her head and I quickly turned back knowing she hadn’t accepted anyone.

By the time dinner arrived in front of me, there were fifty candidates left standing on the field, surrounded by the disciples that had been slowly moving the candidates closer as they circled them.

“Congratulations are once again given to you candidates that have received offers from inner sect Deacons, as well as outer sect deacons.” The host quickly went right back to naming candidates then offering the choice between the deacons, he even informed them of which deacons were from the inner sect and were inviting them to become inner sect disciples.

By the time I had finished eating my dinner, there were less than twenty-five candidates remaining, and they were quickly whittled down to five after being told that only inner sect deacons had made offers to them.

I saw Yue and Akemi standing in the middle of the disciple ring that was staring at them, they looked nervous and were glancing between the disciples that I soon realised had also been whittled down as there were only ten of them still on the field. The other three candidates were offered positions as core disciples of a sect elder and left.

*******

Yue watched the last three candidates leaving with six of the robed disciples and turned her attention back to the last four of them staring at her. The disciple that had been with her the entire week was beaming like the proudest parent she had ever seen as she stared at the two of them.

”Congratulations, Akemi and Yue,” the host said from above them, making her look up. “You two have received exceptional offers and have the chance to become Mountain Peak Disciples!” He roared the last part like it was a huge deal, and based on Akemi’s squeal of delight, I guessed it was a big deal.

”Lady Akemi Yusiffo. You have received fifteen offers from Inner Sect Deacons, five offers from Core Sect Deacons. Two offers from a Core Sect Elder and one offer from a Mountain Peak Commander!” He roared loudly with excitement in his voice. Before asking, “would you like to be an Inner sect disciple?”

Akemi quickly shook her head and replied, “no, thank you esteemed Deacons, I would like to respectfully decline the offer to be an inner sect disciple.” She also was asked and declined the core sect deacons before the host gave a knowing look around the field and then turned and asked me if I would like to be an inner sect or core sect disciple.

I politely declined using the same words that Akemi had before the host nodded his head excitedly and turned back to Akemi. “Lady Akemi Yusiffo you have three offers remaining, would you like to accept one of those three? The first one comes from the Sect Alchemy Pavilions, Elder Wesley Thomas!” He paused to look around with a big smile on his face before continuing. “The second offer comes from the Sect Law Pavilions Elder Lexi Verdunct!” He smiled and bowed in a direction to the north-east, clearly showing more respect to her than the Alchemy Pavilions Elder.

I saw Akemi shift nervously next to me as she shook her head at both before speaking her polite refusal of them and waited to hear the last offer. I also held my breath, wondering who it was.

“Very well, your last chance to be accepted above a random Outer Sect Disciple is the offer you have received from a Mountain Peak, Commander Kenna Mackenzie” he said calmly, making me look up at him and wonder if he didn’t respect her at all.
