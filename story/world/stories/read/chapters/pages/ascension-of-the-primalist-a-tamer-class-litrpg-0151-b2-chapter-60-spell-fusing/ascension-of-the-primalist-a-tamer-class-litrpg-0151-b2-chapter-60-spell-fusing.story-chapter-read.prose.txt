Seth once again hammered the giant creature with his fists, each blow landing with a thud against the stone. On the other side of its body, Nightmare tore at the elemental as the smoke hissed while corroding the rock. Colossus did his best to hold the One-eyed Giant Elemental's attention and absorb its brutal blows.

Seth felt the rising impatience from both his companions through Link; they wanted to use their Toughness-reducing spells. At the moment they were like ants battering a mountain. Their efforts felt futile against the defense of their foe, yet Seth held them back—despite his own core thrumming with a surge of energy he refused to unleash.

'We need to see more,' he said through their bond.

Both the scorpion and the direwolf grunted but complied.

For another few minutes, they wove through the probing attacks; without warning, the boulder-like creature visibly tired of their insignificant harassment and cast two spells in quick succession.

First came the familiar sight of Quartz Armor, the defensive ability rippling over the creature's body as earth, rock, and fractured crystal fused into a carapace that hardened its already fearsome defense. Then the Elemental's two massive arms twisted and reformed—stone flowed like molten clay until the limbs became giant maces coated with spikes.

The moment the transformation was done, the creature turned into the offensive. The newly formed weapons whooshed through the air, pulverizing everything in reach. Seth and Nightmare darted aside, barely staying ahead of the blows, while Colossus had no choice but to summon his own Quartz Armor to meet the onslaught.

Just as Seth finally felt he had a full grasp of the creature's abilities and the rhythm of its attacks, the titan unveiled yet another spell. A shimmering aura ignited around its mace-like arms and flickered.

Its subsequent attacks landed with even greater force, each impact rattling the cavern and sending out shockwaves. Those that connected on Colossus broke his Quartz Armor with ease, the hardened crystal fracturing like brittle glass.

'We have to go all out now!' Nightmare exclaimed. 'Small Bite won't hold much longer without Desert Aegis!'

Colossus flushed. ‘No, no, I’m—’

Seth shook his head before the scorpion could finish. 'No, Nightmare's right. Let's kill that thing.'

'Finally!' Without waiting even a second, Nightmare cast Predator Hunt; the howl rippled through the air before momentarily startling the titan. The next instant, he lunged forward and his fangs sank into the creature's Quartz Armor, which let the black smoke in his maw eat away at the crystalline shell.

Seth's attack was delivered shortly after, Dusk Scythes materializing along his forearms as he hacked at the creature's armor, each slash sending chunks of enchanted stone clattering to the cavern floor. Under their combined assault, the titan's defense crumbled in no time. Its blazing eye turned away from Colossus and locked on the pair.

Then the titan stilled.

If it had a mouth, Seth could've sworn it was smirking. The next instant, its body began to shimmer, and the solid rock blazed with a glow before hardening into a layer of radiant golden armor. Seth's next strike glanced harmlessly off the surface, not even leaving a scratch. Nightmare's fangs also scraped against the shining shell as the creature stood there, immobilized by his own spell.

"Seriously?" Seth grunted, the frustration thick in his throat.

This damned thing had turned invulnerable and was now making them waste precious time of Dusk Scythes and Predator's Hunt. After about a dozen agonizing seconds, the glow faded, and the creature returned to its standard form.

Seth and Nightmare dove back in, though they landed only a few more attacks before Dusk Scythes dissipated and left the direwolf as their primary source of damage.

Another calculating gleam—or what seemed like one—then arose in the large creature's single eye. Quartz Armor flowed back over its body, followed immediately by the unstable energy of the variant of Exploding Barrier.

Shit! It used that invulnerability to bait out our strongest spells, Seth cursed inwardly before realizing Colossus' Sandstorm was also still on cooldown. I'll have to use Phantom Punch to break the barrier before it explode—but that's an eighth of my Well just to stop one damn spell.

With no other option, Seth gritted his teeth and unleashed the Undead spell. The skeletal fist of teal aether coalesced around his arm as he drove his gauntlet into the creature's glowing spell. The impact reverberated through the cavern as the spell tore into the brown layer of aether and devoured a large portion of it—but failed to break it completely.

On the other side, Nightmare seized the opportunity and lunged. The direwolf tore at the weakened barrier with the black smoke of his still-evolving Shadow Bite, also trying to shred through it as fast as possible. Seth followed with regular punches as the keeper swung down its stone arms to force them to dodge and lose precious seconds. Before they could shatter the shield, the barrier began to quiver again. They weren't fast enough, even with all of that.

"Back off!" Seth shouted.

Sensing the incoming explosion, Colossus cast Desert Aegis. The hazel aether expanded outward, enveloping all three of them in its protective embrace just as the detonation hit.

For a second time, the cave became a maelstrom of heat, sound, and light. The explosion slammed into the scorpion's spell, which thankfully absorbed some of it before blasting Seth and Nightmare backward and sending them tumbling once again.

Seth's skin burned beneath his leather armor, and the searing pain left him gasping, though Nightmare's yelp cut through the ringing in his ears. Colossus had slid back several feet but remained standing. However, through Link, Seth could feel the scorpion's rage: the young beast felt he hadn't fully shielded them—he hadn't fulfilled his role.

Seth and Nightmare got back to their feet and charged the keeper once again, desperate to bring it down before it could cast the spell again. But before they'd even broken through half of the Quartz Armor, the creature's eye flared with blinding light that scorched the air. It then turned its burning gaze toward Nightmare, recognizing the greater threat.

The direwolf darted over to the right and used Shadow Step to blink away, the spell barely saving him from the beam that singed through stone and air alike. Panicked, he vanished with Illusionary Emptiness, yet the guardian's eye still somehow tracked his aetheric signature.

Don't you dare.

Seth and Colossus immediately tried to grab their foe's attention.

The scorpion clicked his mandibles and let out Chitinous Taunt's piercing screech. The compulsion struck the massive Elemental—but only for a heartbeat. Ethereal chains appeared around its body… then snapped apart at their centers and dissolved into the air. Freed from the compulsion, the titan resumed locking its blazing eye on the fleeing direwolf.

Damn it, it's resisting everything!

With a booming roar, Seth unleashed Beastmaster Rage to boost Nightmare's Agility, then leapt forward, striking the creature while throwing himself between the guardian and the direwolf in an attempt to draw its fire and intercept the beam. But the creature ignored him this time, its focus absolute.

The spell hit.

A strangled yelp tore through the cavern as the scorching light engulfed Nightmare. Bellowing, Seth pounded uselessly the guardian's side with his fist, all his offensive spells on cooldown. Colossus surged toward Nightmare, trying to shield him from the continued attack with his own body. But by the time the scorpion reached the direwolf, the spell's duration ended and the beam faded away into motes of dying light.

Nightmare collapsed behind the scorpion, smoke rising from his scorched fur, his body trembling. Seth and Colossus felt the waves of agonizing pain washing over the direwolf through their bond.

Furious, and united in their need for revenge, Seth and the scorpion charged at the elemental with everything they had.

Their blows rained down on its stone form, chips flying, but the damage was merely superficial. The titan ignored their assault and began lumbering toward Nightmare while its arms morphed once more into spiked maces.

Colossus slammed into it, yet the explosive collision barely made it stagger. The scorpion then whipped his tails to strike the creature with Deathcoil Venom and sap away some of its defense and Strength.

Seth began channeling Phantom Punch once again, aether filling its grooves, when suddenly, the titan froze. Its stone body glinted, then transformed into a statue of unblemished gold for a second time.

"Shit!"

For a fleeting moment, Seth considered cutting the Undead spell short—dissipating the gathered aether before it surged—but then another pulse of pain tore through Link.

Nightmare.

Seth's gaze snapped toward the direwolf.

Raw flesh was visible under his charred fur. Through their bond, Seth felt the agony seizing the direwolf's body as he tried to push himself up.

No.

The thought came with such force that everything else vanished. Seth couldn't let him be killed. Not Nightmare. Not the beast who had been beside him since the Wicked Forest. Not the stubborn bastard who had fought beside him when they had nothing, who had bled with him, grown with him, argued with him, and saved Seth more times than he could count.

He was his first companion, his best friend, and among all his brother.

Another spike of pain from Nightmare surged through Link, and something inside Seth broke.

His core exploded so violently that Seth could barely breathe. Every strike and every spell this creature had been shrugged aside as if they were children throwing stones at a mountain, and now Nightmare was going to die because Seth wasn't strong enough to stop it.

The thought was unbearable.

He had spent years watching his mother being slowly killed by her old nation without being able to do anything. He would not stand here and lose Nightmare too.

You won't get to take him from me.

Seth's jaw ached from how hard he clenched it. Instead of allowing the gathered aether of Phantom Punch to dissipate, he seized it with everything he had and forced it somewhere else, somewhere it could become something that would last: Iron Fists' grooves.

The spell's channel within his arms twisted violently, and Seth felt his core rip them apart as the teal and brown aether collided and intertwined with chaotic force. He didn't know what he was creating—what abomination his instinct and fury were birthing—but he didn't resist. He yielded to it, letting aether surge and flow.

A searing pain flared inside his arms, and Seth screamed. Agony and rage were equally present in the guttural sound that ripped from his throat. The world narrowed to that scorching sensation in his chest, the tearing of his spells' grooves as they bent under both Seth's and his core's will.

Teal and brown aether kept crashing together inside channels never meant to contain both, burning through him like molten metal poured into his veins. His fingers spasmed inside his gauntlets, and blood began trickling down his wrists. His knees nearly gave out as another groove tore apart, spilling aether through his arm.

Seth clenched his teeth until he tasted blood and forced even more aether through the ruined pathways. Every instinct screamed at him to stop, but he didn't.

Nightmare was not going to die.

As the golden glow around the One-eyed Giant Elemental faded, the creature returned to its normal form and prepared its Exploding Barrier.

Colossus rushed in front of Nightmare, who was still trying to stand up. Yet as the scorpion's eyes landed on Seth, he hesitated, unsure whether or not to use Sandstorm Dome.

A storm of energy exploded around Seth's body, teal and brown aether spiraling in a wild harmony. After a brief instant, the power gathered around his arms with agonizing pain and turned into two skeletal limbs that mirrored Phantom Punch, yet radiated something entirely new.

Rune-etched plates fused along the exterior of the ethereal bones, as if to protect them. The spell pulsed with a dark green hue that contained both the Undead and Earth affinity. Seth looked down at the spell, then charged blood trickling out of his mouth.

Seemingly sensing something amiss, the giant creature swung one of its mace-arms in a horizontal arc. Seth ducked beneath it, wind of its passing whipping his hair, then drove his fist into the creature's glowing barrier.

The blow sent a shockwave through the defensive barrier—yet this time the spectral, armored fist didn't vanish. Instead, it remained attached to Seth's arms, ready for more.

Without hesitation, Seth roared and hammered the creature. Each punch sent painful jolts up his arms, but he could feel the barrier faltering, the aether trembling under the assault.

The energy from his new spell pierced the protective layer, plunging in and ripping it apart from the inside with each blow. Its eye widening, the giant Elemental tried to swat him away, but Colossus surged forward and head-butted the titan's leg, buying precious seconds.

Seth's fists blurred, leaving trails of aether like meteors, and his roars echoed through the cavern. Break! Just break, damn it!

The punches came faster, heavier, and more desperate than the last. The barrier spluttered like a dying flame and thinned—then, just as it was about to detonate, it dissolved into wisps.

With Colossus' Deathcoil Venom still active in the foe, Seth continued his onslaught. His gauntlets smashed the weakening rock again and again until large chunks began to fall from the creature's side.

The titan let out a grinding hiss, yet before it could retaliate, Colossus used Chitinous Taunt to draw its attention, and this time it wasn't resisted. Desert Aegis, still pulsing around him, absorbed part of the brunt of the keeper's furious blows.

To Seth's surprise, a shadowy wolf suddenly raced past him and pounced on the towering creature. It latched onto the titan's leg as tendrils of black energy coiled around it, sapping part of its Agility and slowing its movements.

Seth's gaze snapped toward Nightmare.

The direwolf had managed to push himself back onto his feet, but that alone seemed to take everything he had. His legs trembled beneath him, blood dripping from his burned body as he struggled to remain upright.

Seth seized the opening and redoubled his efforts, stone and dust flying with every brutal punch. The Elemental countered with thunderous blows, its mace-like arms crashing down on Colossus, each strike shaking the cavern. Through it all, the scorpion refused to yield—his shell cracked and blood seeped out, yet didn't break.

After a moment, the creature's single eye flared to life once more, blinding light swelling within it as it locked onto Seth this time. The air began to vibrate, searing energy condensing to a point.

Just as Seth crossed his arms to shield himself, he felt Nightmare's anger flare through Link.

Night—

The direwolf lurched forward before Seth could react.

There was none of his usual terrifying speed. Nightmare barely managed to cross a few steps, each one uneven and labored, before his legs threatened to give out beneath him. Then darkness of Shadow Step swallowed him and he reappeared beside the elemental to pounce.

The leap lacked strength but it carried him high enough.

Nightmare's jaws opened as a cloud of darkness erupted around his fangs. It wasn't large, but it was intensely black, like an abyss devouring every particle of light around it. There was something different about this Shadow Bite. Seth could feel it through Link—a deep hunger and purpose—a will to pierce and obliterate anything that stood in its path.

The direwolf's fangs sank into stone, and a concentrated stream of corrosive darkness plunged through the elemental. Cracks exploded outward as the spell burrowed deeper, and the enormous Elemental released a roar that shook the cavern.

The light gathering within its eye dissipated.

Nightmare released his bite and dropped, hitting the ground heavily, his legs failing beneath him.

'Nightmare!' Seth charged forward.

But before he could reach him, Colossus surged past and put himself in front of the fallen direwolf. 'I've got him!'

Seth gritted his teeth and lunged toward the giant Elemental, whose body was already shuddering. His fists hammered into its crumbling form, widening the cracks with every strike while Colossus also pressed on from the other side. His pincers crushed everything they could seize and the scorpion cast Deathcoil Venom as soon as its cooldown ended while also protecting Nightmare.

Stone shattered beneath Seth's gauntlets, chunks tumbling to the ground as the Elemental desperately tried to crush him.

But just as the tide finally seemed to turn in their favor, the giant creature threw its head back—or rather, tilted its eye skyward. Aether began to surge through its entire body in waves, each pulse brighter and faster, like a giant bomb.

Seth and Colossus exchanged a frantic glance.

Almost simultaneously, Seth used Shadow Step to appear beside Colossus as the scorpion threw up a second Sandstorm Dome. The giant Elemental let out a guttural roar, and a shockwave exploded outward to slam into the scorpion's swirling barrier.

Colossus poured more aether into the spell, planting himself firmly between the blast and Nightmare behind him. His legs dug trenches into the stone as the dome buckled inward from the pressure.

But the scorpion didn't allow it to break. Nightmare couldn't fight anymore. He couldn't dodge. He couldn't protect himself.

So Colossus would.

With a defiant screech, he poured everything he had left into the Sandstorm Dome. For one heart-stopping moment, the two forces clashed in a deafening outcry that shook the cavern to its foundations before finally annihilating each other in a burst of energy.

The instant the shockwave died out, Seth charged back in. The dark green aether had faded from around his arms, but Dusk Scythes, now off cooldown, flared back to life.

Rocks fell with each blow as Seth's blades slashed in sweeping arcs. Fueled by rage, he then pounced to bury his shadow-forged weapons deep into the creature's chest before wrenching them sideways with every ounce of strength he had left. The Elemental's single eye flared wide and its glow flickered erratically, as if fighting the inevitable.

But then its body shuddered, and fractures spiderwebbed across it. A deafening crack split the air, and a second later its towering form broke apart into a mountain of lifeless rock.

Seth stood there, his chest heaving. His gaze landed first on Nightmare, who was still trembling from the injuries on the ground, then on Colossus, whose shell was now marred by deep fissures and blood.

A breath escaped Seth's lips—half a weary sigh, half a laugh.

They'd won.
