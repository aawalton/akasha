Edge squeezed Mel’s hand as they waited for the gate to rise.

They were standing in one of the ready rooms ringing the fighting floor, listening to the murmur of the crowd conducting through the thick slab of steel. It would open sometime within the next few minutes, marking the start of the Duos’ Clash. His pulse was pounding, and he could see his excitement mirrored in Melenia’s eyes—Extinction’s handle cradled in her arms with the massive blade resting on the stone floor below.

“We cut it close, but we managed to qualify in time.” She leaned in to kiss his lips, then stepped back and started stretching out. “I can’t wait to see how your little surprise plays out. The poor bastards won’t know what hit them.”

“No matter what happens,” he replied. “I’m glad we got to do this together. It was nice spending some quality time with you, and it’s fun to fight without our lives hanging in the balance for a change.”

He was confident they would make a good showing, but claiming the grand prize and qualifying for Carnage Cup was another matter. There were plenty of skilled gladiators gathered here today, many of whom had considerably more experience fighting other people and working as a two-warrior team.

However, Edge and Mel had some aces hidden up their sleeves that might even the odds. She had taken her implants to tier three and had quite a few tricks she’d been saving for today. He had a ton of powers he’d never revealed before, including several of his favorite combos, the true strength of Dragon’s Greed, and Rejuvenation’s aura mode.

He grinned, popping his knuckles as adrenaline surged through his veins. Part of it was the thrill of anticipation that always preceded a difficult fight, and part was jitters from participating in his first major match in front of thousands of screaming fans. However, the lion’s share of Edge’s enthusiasm stemmed from his trump card—Skill-Eater’s impending evolution.

Although he had cleared the requirements the day before, he was waiting to pull the trigger until the coin hit the ground and the match officially began. After investigating the regulations, he had learned that he would be disqualified from the event if he hit stage three before the fight started. However, there was no rule prohibiting him from evolving during the battle itself.

While it was a rare occurrence, he was far from the only person who had shot up a stage mid-match to obtain a potent advantage. Most of the benefits would take time to accrue, but evolving would immediately upgrade his ultimate abilities and let him equip more traits. He hoped to acquire a new ultimate too, and boosting Overdrive would make a big difference if he decided to use it today, although he wanted to save it for his meeting with King in case shit hit the fan.

The duo spent the final seconds at their disposal reviewing their strategy and double-checking their gear. For this match, implants and weapons were the only magitech devices allowed. No consumable items could be used, including potions, poisons, and weapon oils, limiting the extent to which Credits could determine the outcome.

While he waited, Edge went through his list of limited-use powers, which included his healing aura, each flavor of Elemental Blade’s Devastating Discharge, Tri-State Sentinel, and Overdrive. He also had his Organ-Guard, which would protect him from being taken out by a heavy hit until its aether well ran dry. The arena’s magic was sophisticated enough to determine when a blow was truly lethal and when the strike could be survived via other means, which meant he could take full advantage of Rejuvenation. He could even fight with his throat cut if he regenerated the damage before he passed out.

Mel’s defensive implants were potent after being upgraded twice in a row, and he could heal her if she took a critical wound or got too banged up after her devices ran out of juice—a nasty surprise for anyone who assumed she’d been taken out of the match. She had her Swapping stones charged and ready to go and knew exactly how often she could use her skills in a range of configurations, including the cost of comboing Titan’s Axe with Force Cleave.

His train of thought was derailed when the heavy barrier creaked open. The roar of the crowd struck him in a tsunami of sound as the duo approached their staring mark. Their low-rung brawls had been incredible experiences, but it was nothing compared to the energy thrumming through the stands during one of the season’s major exhibition matches.

Edge hadn’t seen so many people gathered in one place since his new life began… or too many times before it, for that matter, since he had spent most of his hours alone in his bedroom, glued to the Prison World feeds. Mel was grinning like a maniac, and he clasped her shoulder while waiting for the announcer to warm up the crowd.

Krix’s boss was facilitating the event, an elderly woman named Vio with sunny yellow hair and bright purple eyes. She let the audience’s anticipation swell for a few more seconds, then spoke into a magitech microphone that projected her voice across the colosseum.

“Welcome, one and all, to today’s main event, the Stage-Two Duos’ Clash! We have a special treat in store for you today. Twenty-five teams are arranged around the ring, but only one will emerge triumphant. Our gladiators are some of the strongest stage-two warriors World’s End has to offer. You’ve seen many of them before, but several only joined us a short while ago.

“With such a diverse collection of weapons, skills, and martial techniques, it’s anyone’s guess who will come out on top. The only thing that’s certain is watching them go at it will be a hell of a lot of fun. On that note, if you haven’t had a chance to place your bets, speak to one of the bookies wearing red jackets that are making the rounds, and you can be a winner too!

“On the subject of winners, the top five pairs will go home with some fabulous prizes, courtesy of the Hunters’ Association, while the champions of the Clash will be awarded an Epic skill gem each—one of the best prizes we’ve offered in years. Please turn your attention to the screens our illusion specialists are generating as we showcase each duo.”

With that, the statistics of each team were announced, including the skills that were common knowledge. Vio spent a few seconds introducing each team, lingering a bit longer for the crowd’s favorites. While she spoke, Edge inspected his opponents in turn, deciding who to watch out for once the fighting began.

Some of the gladiators were incredibly nervous. He assumed it was because they hadn’t taken part in a major event before, since anyone who was afraid of fighting in front of a crowd would never have qualified for the match. Some wore poker faces, giving nothing away, while others looked like they were having the time of their lives. Regardless of whether they guarded their expressions or wore their emotions on their sleeves, every warrior standing in the ring seemed like they could handle themselves in a brawl.

He spotted Bison on the far side of the fighting floor, the only other person from his evaluation group that had qualified for the clash. They met each other’s gaze and nodded before he turned his attention to the next pair. While everyone looked formidable, Edge’s instincts were certain that three teams were going to be trouble.

One was a towering man with an oversized shield who was paired with an archer with a penetrating gaze. The next was the gladiator who was responsible for Edge’s only loss, who was working with a spear-wielding woman. The last deadly duo included a man carrying a shield whose rim was bladed like a chakram and another who was wielding a dark-green whip in each hand.

He didn’t have the right kind of combat experience to tell they were dangerous from the way they moved or the weapons they carried, although Greed assured him their equipment was top-notch. However, his growing ability to sense skills told him their powers were Rare and decently ranked, and he was fairly certain that several gladiators had Epics of their own.

Before long, Vio’s introduction reached Edge and Mel. They got more cheers than he’d expected. Apparently, they had made quite an impression during the qualifying brawls. When he heard some familiar voices fighting to be heard over the din, he raised his gaze to the stands high above.

The whole crew had turned out to watch today’s fight, along with Astil, Stellia, Lanna, Helena, and Gerg from the Girthy Greave. Everyone but Gerg had been informed of Edge’s ploy. They had bet heavily on his team, including a ton of creds he’d asked them to add to the pot.

He waved to his friends, then turned his gaze back to Vio, who had finished her introductions and was about to flip the coin to signal the start of the match. The illusory screens faded away as everyone sat on the edge of their seats, eager to catch the first exchange of blows. When Vio gave the signal, the warriors activated their auras and passive skills.

Edge switched on Mantle, Aegis, and all his passive powers while Mel fired up Second Wind and Assassin’s Boon. He sensed the 30% damage bonus from his merged trait kick in after detecting more than four opponents, which it had inherited from Raid Wiper. It would give his offense a considerable boost, especially when combined with Dragon’s Greed, and that was only one of the surprises he had in store. The bonus from Crusader will come in handy too if any of the gladiators conjure constructs during the fight.

All such thoughts were driven from his mind when Vio flipped the metal disc high into the air. Fifty seasoned warriors tensed like coiled springs—poised to burst into action the instant the match began. Many were hoping to wipe out some of the competition in a flash. He had other plans and was looking for the best place to hole up while he evolved to stage three.

When the coin struck the paving stones, Melenia and Edge backpaddled until their shoulders were braced against the arena’s perimeter, limiting the direction of approaching attacks. She formed a half-dome with Wall of Force, distributed her Swapping stones, and assumed a defensive stance with Extinction at the ready.

With an effort of will, he stepped into his central chamber. Edge moved quickly. Time trickled by in this strange space compared to the outside world, but in the arena, every microsecond counted, and he didn’t intend to waste a single one.

He warned his avatars to stay away from his reactor, since it had erupted like a volcano last time he’d evolved. Rue was already safe inside his den, which was a separate space that wasn’t part of his inner world. At the same time, he called up his Guide, grinning as the mote of golden light detached from his chest and flattened to form a floating screen.

After countless trials overcome and battles won, Edge was ready to take a giant leap forward down the long path to power. At long last, it was time to upgrade his ultimate abilities, find out how his inner world would change, and discover what his relationship with Skill-Eater would be like going forward.

An electric tide of adrenaline-fueled anticipation broke over him when he read the words hovering in front of his face. When he pulled the trigger, he would transcend his limits and voyage further into the realm of the superhuman.

Congratulations. You have cleared the conditions to evolve to Stage 3.

Would you like to evolve now?

He took a deep breath to steady his nerves and voiced his intentions to the System. “I’m ready. Stage three, here I come.”
