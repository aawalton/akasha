For immediate distribution to all Imperial subjects of noble rank within the Black Pike sector.

On behalf of the Royal Family, your Princess-in-Waiting hails you and wishes you a pleasant and restful Festival of Joyous Labors. May your responsibilities rest lightly on your shoulders and may you recognize in one another the tireless contributions that keep our Empire strong.

In the name of my Liege and yours, Sykora of the Black Pike, I bring news of great consequence issued from the Prelate system.

The world Maekyon, homeland of the Prince of the Black Pike, is receiving special dispensation of self-sovereignty. Standard annexation efforts are to pause.

This weighty decision was reached after three drawn conclusions, which in trust of your loyalty, Her Majesty is prepared to disclose.

Firstly—Maekyon’s people are a firmament-wide anomaly. Maekyonites are a spermatogenic species whose males are immune to Taiikari compulsion. His Majesty Grantyde, Prince of the Black Pike, wishes to make it clear that although he cannot be compelled, he remains steadfastly loyal to Princess Margrave Sykora. His obedience and loyal companionship to Her Majesty act as ready substitutes for the biological abnormality of his people.

Secondly—criminal noncitizen elements are responsible for a subversive attempt to sabotage the Maekyon operation. These individuals will be brought swiftly to justice. While security enforcement is ongoing, a key strategic consideration is ensuring their plans are foiled. By varying the approach from standard Imperial doctrine we intend to baffle any pre-existing scheme awaiting Her High Majesty’s servants.

Finally—The Empress in her wisdom has ordained that Maekyon be a co-operative venture between the Black Pike and Glory Banner sectors. Princess Margrave Sykora celebrates the unprecedented cooperation between herself and her sister, Void Princess Narika of the Glory Banner, and marks this occasion as a historical turning point—not merely in the history of the sector but that of the Empire.

In recognition of these singular challenges and opportunities, this operation requires a novel approach. Your Princess has chosen hers. Glory to the Black Pike and the Empress.

Princess-in-Waiting Vorakaia of the Black Pike

Glad tidings to Egon of the United Nations from Sykora of the Black Pike. I bring you word of an important Imperial decision.

In the spirit of full disclosure, there has been spirited debate within the Empire on the question of your Annexation. Please understand that exception to Imperial doctrine is rare to the point of nonexistence for the past several centuries. But it pleases me to announce that we are prepared to recognize the sovereignty of Earth’s combined governments over its homeworld, as you entreated in your previous correspondence.

Maekyon, as our Imperial language designates it, will remain for the Maekyonites—the humans, to use the English term. We will not interfere with your governance of Earth, its moon, or your solar system.

Further, we offer citizenship to the Taiikari Empire to every human. If, by their own free will, a human desires to enter covenant with ourselves and our Empress, they may declare themselves self-annexed. We will provide them with integrational services and access to our Empire’s technology and territory.

To supervise efforts, and to bind tighter the compact between our people, we intend to create an embassy city on Earth, to be governed, garrisoned, and maintained by our Empire. We will be grateful for your assistance in securing a location of approximately 500 square kilometers of unoccupied land, which we might purchase at a rate to be negotiated with its steward. Inhospitability is no object; we will be as pleased to build in the polar arctic as in the driest desert.

On a more sobering note, we bring a warning and offer for aid. A dangerous criminal of our kind has concealed herself among you. She is named Yniai; by alias we know her as the Penitent. We have reason to believe she has been in contact with anglophonic leadership on Earth, and has provided Taiikari technology to your people. You must be aware that it is her intention to sabotage the safety and partnership between our species. She must not be trusted. Any assistance you may provide in locating her will be greatly appreciated, and any assistance we may provide in bringing her into custody, we will tender unhesitantly.

Twenty-seven hours from now, we will make global, public contact with your species. The ruling family of the Black Pike and the Empress we serve are eager to make the acquaintances of your people.

With anticipation for that glad hour,

Princess Margrave Sykora nai Kei’na Terokai
