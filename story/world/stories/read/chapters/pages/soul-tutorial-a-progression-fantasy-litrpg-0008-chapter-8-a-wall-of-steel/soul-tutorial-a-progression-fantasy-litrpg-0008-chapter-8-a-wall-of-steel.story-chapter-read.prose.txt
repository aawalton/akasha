Luke stood there, breathing deeply, trying his best to catch his breath. Minor bruises lay scattered throughout his body, along his arms and chest.

He made his way to the pedestal, staring at the veil that was revealed before his eyes. All the points he had just fought for stood there in that one spot. A whopping 20 points, but Luke sneered at the thought of having to purchase another restore with the points he shed blood for, but he needed to do so.

Instantly lowering to 15, while his pale skin slowly became more fleshed out, more plump and a little more lavish.

Feeling very rejuvenated, he looked around to see where he should distribute the rest of his points. There were only 5 ways to distribute this, and he was stuck between 2 options, physique and agility.

He wanted both desperately, and actually, he needed them. Without them, he might actually die on the next floor, and he wasn’t ready for the next floor, especially in the state he was in at that moment. Luke was afraid to keep murdering more green slimes; it was exhausting, and if another elite did spawn, it would literally be the end of him.

He had no current control over souls, and even if he did, it would overpower him and kill him if he abused it. Luke paced around the pedestal, deciding best to split his points as evenly as possible.

The veil appeared before him once more, giving 10 points into physique first, then waited to see the results. Until he wasn't able to upgrade the stat any further.

He had not yet figured out the number of points the attribute cost would increase by per level. As the points dwindled, he wanted to see any changes.

Points: 15 → 6

Physique: 1 → 4

His attributes shifted before his eyes, with soul resonance remaining at 2. Surprisingly, he still had 6 points left, with the next cost of his physique upgrade being 5 points. Using the remaining points, he shoved it all into agility, which brought it up to level 3.

When Luke attempted to measure his strength, he couldn’t determine if he felt significantly faster or stronger. He had hoped the points would achieve something.

This is so frustrating! He could do nothing but trust that something might have changed. He thought while walking around in circles, scratching his chin.

It would have been best just to see what a blue slime could do against him, Luke contemplated. Choosing to walk around in circles had only delayed the inevitable; he would still have to fight after all.

Luke laid his hand on the pedestal to restart floor 1 again. He watched as the shift in atmosphere changed with the blue slime appearing.

Questioning if studying that shift could help him figure out where he was or how to get out of this place, Luke pushed that thought to the back of his mind as he refocused on the stationary slime.

He hadn’t really fought one since his time spent rampaging; he chuckled at the thought of that. But Luke was happy that the wound on his forehead was no longer there. He had completely forgotten about it. A smile appeared on his face as he crept up toward the slime like a predator watching its prey. He kept his body tight as he quickly rushed through the battlefield.

The moment the slime had noticed him, he was already within arm’s reach, throwing a quick thrust at the slime. Just trying to pierce it in one attack, but right before contact, the slime just as quickly shaped its body into a spike, trying to penetrate straight through his hand. Luke quickly recalled his arm as fast as he could, which left the slime hitting air and caused it to be dazed at his movements, taking this chance to jerk his body, slamming his leg into the slime promptly sending it quite a distance away, which left it encumbered, struggling to move towards Luke.

Blue acid sizzled out. Luke didn’t let up; he might have been chubby in some places, but he had the power to back it up. Swiftly running towards the slime, not trying to trip with all the craters along the way. He kept the slime restricted to the best of his ability. Blow after blow landed, with his legs doing most of the work.

Using his whole body weight against it, he saw the slime look even more diminished than it should have been at that point in the fight. He quickly realized that his body clearly carried more power than he had originally. Each kick drove the slime deeper into a pit from which it couldn’t get out.

All it took was one more burst of leg strength and his quickened movements to dodge the ongoing spikes coming at him, ultimately cracking the slime core. He watched in silence as it disappeared into particles.

Luke studied his arm in surprise at how much power he could generate. He couldn’t really tell how much his strength had actually increased by, but the ease with which he killed that blue slime really stunned him.

Am I finally strong?

Luke didn’t really have another person with whom he could compare himself. But all he really knew was that killing more slimes could quickly get him more powerful if he didn’t constantly have to get healed. Although it looked like he killed that slime swiftly, he still took more damage, with an attack almost piercing through his thigh.

But he didn’t care about how much damage he took. He cared about how much his strength had improved, but the boy might have made a slight blunder.

How could I be so dumb? He thought.

Luke realized his mistake, as he could have upgraded Constitution at least by a single level before putting everything else into agility, deciding to put off the heal for now, making his way back to the pedestal to place his hand on it.

The 2 points lay on top of the veil before him. He inputted as many points as he could into the constitution like he did with his other stats. As he watched it dwindle away, 2 points had disappeared, leaving him with nothing, but constitution had gone up to level 2.

Not knowing if it was his own imagination, he felt a tad heavier than he had before. But he quickly brushed that off because he was already chubby.

That would be funny if I had gotten fatter from inputting stats. He chuckled as he removed his hand from the pedestal, trying to see if there was any difference or change in his body, as he had tried previously. But much to his disdain, Luke couldn’t really tell. He didn’t know if his strength had increased or endurance at all.

In most of his games, he played back with his friends; constitution was related to how many hits you could take. Still quite confused, he just did what he did with all his previous thoughts: push it all the way to the back of his mind and focus only on the present.

Luke checked out floor 1 a few more times just to heal his leg injury and save up points for any future injuries he got. He wasn’t dumb enough to fight a new enemy without having backup points to heal himself. But just as he had done before, he cleanly killed a couple of blue slimes. Which made him quite happy, finally finding blue slimes manageable even with the loss of an arm. Ending up way more exhausted, even to the point of feeling like he overexerted himself.

But that’s why he had the restore function for a reason, even though it kind of seemed to cheat death itself. He couldn’t use it in the middle of a fight, at the very least. That would have truly felt like a cheat, but something that could really help him survive in the long run.

Imagine if I could restore myself against the Elite Green Slime, I wouldn’t have lost arm left arm, he thought while trying to wiggle his left stump, which seemed to look better than it was before.

The previous scar had healed well, but showed no improvement of his arm ever coming back. As Luke reminisced about his left arm, he was already standing back in front of the pedestal. As the veil appeared before him, he had returned to 15 points, which quickly went down to 10 because he immediately spent it on himself getting healed.

With a joyful smile, Luke flexed his right leg as he got used to it not being as restricted as it had been a couple of fights ago. Feeling quite prepared, he stared at the floor options. Immediately gunning towards floor 2 before he started having second thoughts.

This was the only way for him to continue onwards at this point; energy started gathering, but Luke felt it had a different feel than he had felt at the previous levels. Slowly the energy started giving birth to what seemed like another slime. Which Luke felt was a big life saver, he already got used to fighting slimes and clearly didn’t want to create a new strategy for a totally different creature. But what made the slime different was what scared Luke a little. It wasn’t green or blue, but this time silver, metallic slime, and also quite larger than the ones he fought before, besides the Elite Slime. It stood there like a fortress, struggling to calculate the size of that thing; it looked to be only wider than all the ones before it. But still a lot shorter than the Elite. That absolutely dwarfed him in height.

Being around 5 meters away, he couldn’t really tell, but Luke thought the slime was around his waist in height. But without a second thought, since he had leveled up his stats a bit more, feeling somewhat confident. He rushed his way towards the grey slime; Luke really wanted nothing more but to lose some weight though. Because it still took more time to cover that distance, even with the increase in agility.

But when he got there, only being a few steps away from the slime. It just stood completely still as if not alive. Luke saw nothing wrong. He powerfully swung his arm forward, having already pulled it back as far as he could. Only to be met with pain the second he collided with the grey slime.

He brought his arm back, noticing it was much redder than before. He felt as though he might have broken his entire hand. Clearly, what he had just punched wasn’t just gelatin but straight steel. That slime was not gooey or bouncy. Just a solid surface he couldn’t break through. He had to take a few steps back and try to think of ways to deal with the inconvenience. Luckily, the slime didn’t seem like it would give chase, almost as if it couldn’t, but Luke couldn’t be too sure about that. But one thing he did notice was that there seemed to be a small dent in it. It was unclear to him if that originated from his attack. Maybe he had a chance after all.
