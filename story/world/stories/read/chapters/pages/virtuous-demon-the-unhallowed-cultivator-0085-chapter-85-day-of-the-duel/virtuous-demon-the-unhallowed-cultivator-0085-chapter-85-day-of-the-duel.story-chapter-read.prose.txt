“You’re being a fool, Shuren. What point is there to play to the whims of some nobody?” Ming scolded his close friend, leaning on the wall with arms crossed. “Not only that, tasteless rumours have begun spreading around you. The Silvents and Vulfoans think you’re purporting their mistreatment, using your surname.”

Shuren was also perplexed. His duel had become the Seven Clouds’ hottest topic, almost becoming a rivalry between the Outer and Inner Disciples. Even if rumours had begun to spread, it should’ve been about Tez’s character instead. He picked up the Ruyi staff, tying it to his waistband.

“The runt’s going at my throat through slander, how pathetic. But it doesn’t matter. The duel must be upheld. Once I beat him into submission, the truth will be apparent from his own words. I even requested the Hall of Punishments to wait until after the duel.”

Wei was still unconscious, teetering on the brink of his meridians collapsing. Once that happened, his hopes for cultivation would be long gone. He would become a true cripple, an outcast that wouldn’t even receive the support of the Liu clan.

It was far worse than being an unwanted bastard.

When Shuren thought back to him, his voice grew cold with a silent fury.

“Besides, the Seven Clouds has rules, especially to those like me who come from outside. Your lineage or status isn’t conferred upon when gaining admission, so how could the Liu Clan use their status to garner influence?” It wouldn’t make sense. If someone dared to do that, the Elders would easily use that not only as a reason to expel a disciple, but also gain remunerations from their backing as well.

The consequences from damaging their reputation would be far too much for them to just ignore. Everyone was well aware of how the Seven Clouds operated. Whether it was because of their Sect Leader, they were the most stern when it came to these matters.

Shuren might have some leeway as an Inner Disciple, but he once rejected the offer of staying as a delegate for a year. His surname would still be considered whenever anything occurred involved with him.

These were the chains used to handle those with influence and power, by others as well. This was the weapon of face and reputation.

The Seven Clouds Sect was one thing. They were just a Rank 5 force. But the problem was The Great Nine being heavily involved with them. They were a step above the Liu Clan.

The Seven Clouds dominion was not the playground for traditional families to play their politics or their children to run amok. Even the powerhouses of the entire world, whose backing would make any of these families, sects and clans shiver in fear, would be respectful.

That was all the work of the Sect Leader of the Seven Clouds Sect. Though he was at Rank 5, a Nascent Soul cultivator, he had singlehandedly increased the chances of Qidense cultivators becoming Path Foundation cultivators by an absurd amount.

Anywhere else, you would be lucky to get even one out of a hundred Rank 1 cultivators to reach Rank 2.

The door to the room outside the Duelling Colosseum slammed open. Aerestia stormed in, until she was face to face with Shuren. A pair of twin cubs trailed behind her, hovering near her legs. He hadn’t seen her for a while, and it was the first time he saw that expression on her. Disgust.

“Shuren! What have you been doing!” Snapped Aerestia, her brows furrowing. “Have you known all this time what your cousin has done?” Both her fists were tightly clenched up, shivering.

Only now she came, on the day of the duel to spout this nonsense, Shuren thought. He stepped forward toward her, a head taller. “I personally investigated on the day of the incident when I found my cousin collapsed and dying at my door,” Shuren began to frown, disappointed at her actions.

But the disappointment turned to anger. “You even ignored the letter I and Ming sent to talk about our last mission. Now you come? After weeks when anyone can embellish words, slandering my character?!”

Shuren stared down at his old comrade, gnashing his teeth as he narrowed his eyes. He ended up massaging the ridge of his nose with a sigh, turning his back on her.

“Stop wasting your and my time on baseless rumours Aerestia. Now if you’ll excuse me.” He brushed her aside and began to leave the room.

The twin ashen cubs snarled at Shuren as he walked past Aerestia. Even though he was the one who saved them alongside her. Then again, perhaps they had the intuition that he buried their mother. Shuren snorted at the sight.

Ming gave Aerestia a look, shaking his head as if to say that she should ease it, but she continued. “You’re wrong Shuren. I-”

“How about you look to yourself for once, when I’ve been the one to clean up after you every time,” Shuren muttered with a low voice as he stood by the door. Amongst the silent room, only the creak of the door closing with a thud was heard as Shuren left.

Ming stopped leaning against the wall, facing towards her. “Not a good time Aerestia. What were you expecting to accomplish?”

She held a frown, her eyes darting around the room as hesitation filled her mind. “I have a few childhood friends in the Evening Clouds, and the things they told me… I don’t think they were lies. That Liu Wei has been using his family name to abuse Outer Disciples.”

Ming had a long and deep sigh. “Fine, explain yourself. There’s no point in me seeing Shuren beat the shit out of some orphan, but clearly there’s something up.”

Aerestia bit her lip as she expressed regret. “Sorry, I should’ve talked to you guys earlier.”

“Leave it. Live and learn, right?” With a wave of his palm, Ming dismissed her worries. “But I’m going to sniff around, so work with me. Shuren’s too muddle-headed right now from what happened to his cousin, so I’ll have to look into things myself.”

At the height of noon, the clouds dispersed above the Duelling Colosseum. An unfettered blue sky lay high, with every corner of the battle stage alight for all to see. All were barred from the use of flying nimbus in the vicinity of the Duelling Colosseum, so everyone that came to watch had to sit down along the hard seats as mere spectators. But the spectators were not just the inner disciples of the sect. Even the outer disciples were allowed to traverse up the mountains, at the cost of their spirit stones. Another small token of motivation to push them, to work towards the status of becoming an inner disciple.

Murmurs drummed up around the forming crowd, when a young man adorned with a dark crimson robe walked towards the stage, far earlier than the allotted time for the duel. It only served to get the blood and excitement of all the spectators boiling. Naturally, all bets had been placed on him.

Those murmurs and whispers soon became chatter, but none were about the matter of the duel itself.

“Is it true? The young master of the Liu Clan dared to use his influence to bully even Outer Disciples?” a young man asked his friend, both inner disciples.

“A year ago, he was the number one fighter of the new sect examinations, winning over a thousand other contestants. Haha, maybe he enjoys beating up weaker opponents!” Another joked, pushing the narrative with the others.

“I heard his cousin was rather sleazy, and both the Liu members were pushing to make a faction in the sect. My friend from the Evening Clouds told me of the rumours, in fact,” The sentiments were spread with some of the young women.

“Tez did nothing wrong! He saw us getting bullied and was the only one to step out! Anyone who dares to say otherwise is clearly sucking up to the Liu Clan, even though the Cloud City has nothing to do with the Granxian Continent!” A large rotund man yelled out, with such a piercing voice that some of the others got annoyed.

“Shut up, Fatty Chen! Is all you do suck up to whoever gives you food?!” One of the outer disciples retorted, recognising the annoying voice.

“Screw your entire nine generations! And if you dare come up, I’ll jump off to land on you! I’ll bet my entire savings Tez will win! None of you know the type of man he is!”

“You!” The Outer Disciple almost climbed out of his seat, but it took his friends on the side to calm him down. “It’s just Fatty Chen, you really falling for it? It’ll be more fun to see his reaction when Tez gets destroyed like the trash he is.”

The attention of everyone was soon grabbed when a sudden blast of wind came from the centre of the Dueling Colosseum.

And a Core Disciple of Elder Harmony, the red-haired Wudan in the crowd watched, sensing the power from Shuren.

“He’s reached Stage 11? Is this even a duel or is it just going to be a one-sided beatdown?!”
