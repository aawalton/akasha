Cale only grinned and went away.

The ritual carried on for a while. The recruits and Fiara sat in silent meditation, only occasionally exchanging a few words. Cale laid down against the wall and closed his eyes and sighed. Every bit of ache, pain and fatigue made themselves acutely present now that the adrenaline was waning. His shoulder was messed up, flaring with an inflamed hot pain that pulsed periodically.

I think I fractured a few ribs.

But despite all the pain, the overall fatigue was mounting into something insurmountable. They had been going for a few days straight since they left the camp. While Cale was a cultivator with an extraordinary body, all the training, fighting and endless walking in the harsh winds of the Blacksteel Citadel were starting to take their toll.

“Wake me up after fifteen minutes…” Cale said and started drifting away.

Carefully, he plucked a Nevani mana crystal from his pouch. He had six and a half left. That should push him over the edge, if he attuned the mana with care.

I’m so groggy… I wish I had coffee.

But Cale pushed through. He sucked in some mana and went to work.

Despite the exhaustion-tier fatigue, Cale found working the mana easy, even relaxing. He had gone through one hell of a ringer. Just sitting on your ass, doing a little visualization and breathwork?Child’s play.

It did get harder as the mana condensed. Turning it from a gas to liquid form was almost becoming second nature. But when it came time to turn the liquid into a thick sludge, cultivation did become a strain. He still couldn’t quite get past the sludge stage into turning the mana something solid, let alone crystalline. But the liquid was thick. It was resembling more like wet clay, than the previous watery porridge. He was moving forward. But this was as far as he could go for now. Cale exhaled and let the mana spread from his solar plexus all over the body, strengthening his muscles, joints, bones, organs, skin.

The rush of power was satisfying. Like a drink of fresh lemon juice after a trek in the desert. The good feeling spread to his extremities and some of his fatigue was pushed away.

Cale knew it would only last for a moment, and so he sucked in another crystal. He resolved to finish them all or reach the peak of Middle Body Tempering before he left the room.

While Cale worked through the crystals he processed what had happened. The initial stages before condensing the mana into sludge no longer required absolute focus, which was an amazing boon unto itself.

During those moments, Cale’s eyes look at the carnage he had caused. Three dead, mutilated bodies lay on the floor, their blood pooling and coagulating. DR-12’s head had rolled a few feet off his body and his shocked eyes were fixed on Cale, as if an accusation beyond the grave.

Cale found that he did not care. He did not mind the dead stare of a man he had murdered. He met the stare to see what he would feel.

Guilt? Hell no.Satisfaction.

In fact, Cale found the notion that he had murdered DR-12 ridiculous. It had been right.

I’m becoming a cold customer.

Cale looked at his comrades. They didn’t mind. The blood from the burly woman was flowing towards Artan. It was pooling around his butt. The placid man did not even shuffle in discomfort. It was as if sitting in the blood of his enemies was nothing but a minor nuisance.

Cale found he understood his comrades and their callousness a lot better now.

Am I becoming something ugly?

Cale wasn’t sure. Cale wasn’t sure if that was even the right question to ask. Another question popped in his mind.

Am I becoming more powerful? More able? More capable?

Yes. The answer was a definitive yes. And that felt amazing. Yes, Cale had crossed another line, the Cale Bishop from his life before would have never imagined. Did it make him a bad person? Not so far. He hadn’t been any more cruel than what was deserved. He had disposed of bad people. As long as he could look himself in the mirror and smile back, he knew he hadn’t crossed a wrong kind of line.

Some lines lead to growth, some to degeneration.

Despite his victory over his enemies and over himself, Cale promised to continue being vigilant, to make sure he kept crossing the right kinds of lines, lest he turn into something he no longer smiled at.

There was definitely something different about him. His skin seemed to slightly glow, just like Ravia Wren’s. But mostly it was his presence.
