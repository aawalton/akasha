While all three creatures clung to their intent to destroy Luke, one was on a whole other level. It hated him with everything, down to the core of its being, for the simple reason that it had been close to devouring him.

Hunger Tjiensxi. Level ?

That one, he saw as their leader. The trio charged, and while doing so, combined their wills somehow, joining them together. Combined was perhaps too peaceful a word. It was more as if Tjiensxi grabbed its allies by force and pulled them to its cause, but the two others went willingly and without complaint. Even combined, their will was just as scattered as in the previous moment, too animalistic and base in nature, unable to hone itself to provide the narrow intent and focus needed for this sort of thing.

Once Luke had attempted to put his will onto the battlefield, it hadn't taken him long to understand the basic concept of how to use it. Even to his own senses, it was a wild, burning thing that defied being contained and directed to the full extent of what he knew it was capable of. Feelings and emotions were getting in the way, but even so, it was enough to hold the void creatures back. Enough to hold them back, not enough to destroy them or even make them disappear into the void. This posed a problem.

What Luke was doing was by no means simple or easy, and it required his full attention to keep the creatures from charging. In the end, it was a stalemate. That wasn't good enough. With the creatures outside of the weave and held there, he knew what he needed to do, but the sheer focus it would require made his head spin. To win, Luke had to weave his Lifeweaver tools and repair the weave while keeping the void creatures at bay. Not only that, his repairs would have to be strong enough to dissuade the creatures from keeping up their attacks until they grew bored and withdrew. To that end, Luke fumbled through his interface and picked two new skills, using his last available points.

Weaver’s Boon [Active]: Mana not only restores, but empowers.

Threads of Life [Passive]: The mere presence of the Lifeweaver's tools is enough to bring health to those without.

This would not be the first skills he had picked to add effects after healing, and make no mistake: what he was about to do was not just repairs, but healing the weave itself. Approaching the torn weave was almost like being in physical pain. His senses flickered, and his mana kept jumping around while his health drained at an alarming rate. The system was not whole in this spot, and the effects made themselves known even as Luke readied himself to make it right. Before, when he attempted to fight the creatures of the void, he hadn't noticed it, since he got a little too close to being consumed, but the proximity to where the barrier between the weave and the void was broken made for a terrible time. Even so, he kept his gaze fixed on the void creatures. If he slipped, even for a moment, they would jump at his throat, and this time, Weavestep wouldn't save him.

The mere act of extending a few threads of mana was like dragging barbed wire through his veins while having to keep up juggling with twelve balls and reciting the many side effects of SSRIs. A pain, in short. Still, losing wasn't an option. A loss here meant the end of Earth, and Luke was damned if he was going to be the one to ruin it for everyone. So, not even flinching, he grabbed hold of several frayed ends of broken threads by using Manipulate Thread. The first time he touched a system thread, it had almost killed him. While the effects had lessened as he improved and grew used to them, it was by no means a pleasant experience, and he didn't doubt he'd feel the effects when he returned to himself. Because he would return to himself. There was no 'if' in that statement.

Needle of Life was a requirement for mending the threads, like he'd done when repairing the hole the first time, but that skill wouldn't be enough this time around. Adding Weaver's Boon while keeping an eye on his mana level, Luke felt Threads of Life skill working its magic. Even before he was done, the damage inflicted by the void creatures was being undone. One by one, he repaired the threads, keeping his focus in an iron grip. Slipping was not an option, not with that trio waiting there, pushing against his mind. The damage was not widespread but focused, meaning Luke worked in a single spot, repairing the very thing that made up existence itself for the Integrated. Even as he worked, he realized it wouldn't be enough. The creatures weren't discouraged, so he added another skill.

Strengthen Thread [Active]: Use mana to strengthen the weave.

With Strengthen Thread, he intended to show these bastards that their teeth wouldn't work, not against his system. The added mana cost made his consciousness spin, and for a moment, he teetered on the brink of collapse, almost losing control in the fight. Clawing himself back to safe footing, Luke re-focused his efforts. If he even considered how close he had come to losing it all just then, all his work would be undone. Repairing the weave while holding the void back from the destroyed parts was the main goal. Pursuing that with a singular mind, Luke pushed himself to keep going despite the ache in the very core of his being. Strengthen Thread had given the creatures pause, but it was still not enough to send them back into the nothing. Something more. He needed something more.

Guardian Weave [Active]: Shield structures within yourself or another.

That was it. The skill had to work. Going by nothing other than the description, it shouldn't be possible to use it on something that was not a person, but were these threads not the representation of people? It would work. In Luke's mind, that assertion rang true, and so it was. Finishing up the repair effort, he added Guardian Weave to the end seam, the outer thread of the weave and its first defense against the void. That did it.

The Hunger trio eased off, with two of them turning to float off and disappear into the void. One remained, and Luke didn't need system inspection to be able to tell which one of them it was. It glared at him, full of hate, before it too gave up and disappeared. They would be back, he knew, especially the last one, but for the moment, Earth was safe. Easing off from the contest of wills was a lot like straightening out your back in bed after a full day of hauling frozen burgers around in the freezer. Instead of muscles screaming, it was his mind crying out in exhaustion. Despite remaining in the metaphysical realm, Luke knew he was coming back to a physical body that would be in bad shape after the contest he had just endured. What he hadn't considered when relaxing after winning the fight, or at least squeaking out a draw, was how his tired will would affect the speck of void mana inside his meta-heart.

It hurt. A lot. So much in fact that he couldn't even remain in the weave. Before everything crashed around him, Luke made sure to remove the Guardian Weave. The void creatures were gone, and he would need every point of mana for the days to come. Then he allowed himself to scream.

Coming to, he was already hoarse from wailing up into the sky, his head tilted back, blood pooling in his eye sockets and spilling down the sides of his mouth. Luke's brain was on fire, and it felt like someone was squeezing his meta-heart, now empty of healing mana, between their thighs, and not in a good way. All that damage, and the void mana accounted for none of it. That was all the result of his battle against the void. More than a little shaken, he only then realized how close he'd come to total collapse.

Despite all that noise, Hannah was sleeping with her head in his lap. As he struggled to contain the void mana again, he couldn't help but notice how serene she looked with her eyes closed, stable breathing, and pale skin without a hint of shadow tattoos. Even the shadows in her hair were gone, but they would return, he knew. Struggling, he managed to control the void mana and keep it from spreading, but forming it into its compressed spherical shape would not be possible in the state he was in. Guardian Weave would have to do the heavy lifting until he'd recovered.

When everything was in place and the pain had stopped, Luke fell back onto the moss-covered grass and closed his eyes. Sleep. Everything else would have to wait. For now, he felt like he'd earned a little sleep.

"I win this round, void," he muttered as he drifted off into sweet oblivion.
