'The weakness of the Rose Fairies is that they are quite fragile. Their small bodies allow them to move fast and dodge. However, if they are ever hit, they will receive massive injuries.'

These were much simpler for Liu Shu because they were individual crafting pieces. She just needed to focus on them and pour all she knew about their owners into the craft, trying to make it as fitting as possible.

The skills of an item appeared depending on a combination of intent, materials, rank, and crafter. All of them influenced the final result, giving it better or worse abilities.

The items that Liu Shu decided to create were wooden armor. While it sounded clunky, building them with several joints and ways for the body to move easily was not a problem. Then, adding a few extra processes to the wood gave it a light, durable, but flexible integrity, making it almost as comfortable as everyday clothes.

This process was not a fast one. Focusing on it helped Liu Shu with the crafting speed, but she still took four days to create all five E- Rank armors. However, Liu Shu felt that it was worth it.

The finishing products were not only powerful but also very stylish.
