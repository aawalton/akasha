Ashton sat inside the tent, on top of the simple mattress that was his bed. Millie was laying in the tent's actual bed, snoring away. Right now, although his exhaustion was catching up to him as well, Ashton was making a mental plan for what had to happen next.

At least, that's what he was trying to do. There were so many distractions running through his mind. The biggest one was, of course, the crown that he was holding in his hands right now.

Though the joy to have this back was overwhelming, he was conflicted. The fact that he had this back meant that the interdimensional council was watching him. They were actively interfering with him and this town. They put the ancient constructs here on purpose, so who knew what else they placed here for him to find?

It really did seem like they weren't able to mess with him directly, though. Otherwise, they would have done so already. Ashton expected that they were going to hound the crimson wyvern on him to get rid of him as soon as possible, since he was sort of breaking the balance of this area quite a bit, but that hadn't happened. Not yet, at least.

And then there was that message. 'I hope this will let you see us a little more favourably'.

So what, instead of trying to kill him, they were going to try and get him on their side? What exactly could they even want from him that they were doing that?

Ashton sighed and dropped down onto his back. Whatever it was, he didn't have the slightest way of figuring out what exactly the matter was. It wasn't like he had the power to fight back against constellations right now anyway. So it was better to just focus on getting stronger.

And the best way to do that, at least right now, was to go to bed. After all, if he was right, then he was able to spend the night in a particularly special place.

With the crown safely in his hand, Ashton slowly let himself doze off.

But instead of losing himself to dreams, his conscious simply split off from his body. Exactly the same as before, Ashton felt freed from physical constraints as he entered the veil.

He pushed through the wall of the tent and stepped outside. There were a few things that he wanted to check out while he had the chance this time around. For one, he wanted to see if he could find the location of places where dungeons were forming. Ashton could see the many mana wells all around town here within the veil, so a dungeon, which usually formed around a mana well at its centre, should be easy to find as well.

It wasn't like every mana well turned into a dungeon, though, it was just that a dungeon required a ton of mana to be formed.

But it should be easy enough to do that. In this space, Ashton wasn't restricted to just being able to see within his channelling range, so he should be able to spot them all fairly easily.

Especially now that he was starting to figure out magic a bit more. Two of the books that Ashton bought, specifically A Comprehensive Guide to Spellcasting and Advanced Magic Circle Structure, had a lot of information that would help Ashton out.

There was a lot about general mana manipulation in there, which Ashton was already basically an expert at. But now that he was sorting through all the information he gathered by reading, he was learning a lot more things about specifically spellcasting, like he needed.

Just as he figured, if Ashton wanted to cast more powerful or complicated spells, then he would need to form a permanent magic circle in his body. The spells that he was able to cast right now followed the same basic pattern as the techniques that he could use at this point. The only difference was that techniques couldn't be safely used without a core, maybe because those were directly used within your body.

But basically, what Ashton could do right now was simple alteration. He could strengthen his muscles or increase the force of an impact with techniques, and using magic he could create very simple effects. Things like 'frost' or 'silence'; slowing down or blocking vibrations. It was all generally simple in principle, but more complex processes would simply need much more power behind it.

Even so, there were some things that Ashton could do with this sort of manipulation already. For one, a basic 'visual amplification' spell. Basically a magical telescope.

While it wasn't possible for Ashton to use a spell that could interfere with the material plane right now, he was able to use ones that just had an effect here in the veil. His soul was still connected to his body, after all, so even if his mana was being produced there, he could still access it wherever he wanted.

So, Ashton held forward his hand. Manipulating magic felt very weird in here, but somehow more natural.

The veil bent toward his will, as Ashton shifted the world's mana into the form of a spell circle. The structure was quite simple, though it didn't quite look like other spell circles.

It basically had a large circular 'empty' area in the middle, which, once Ashton finished constructing it, was the area that was affected by the spell. The spell-circle frame around it rotated to Ashton's will, letting him even 'zoom in', so to say, as the centre of the circle displayed the world at a distance.

And then, there was another spell that Ashton wanted to test. It was essentially a 'search' spell, which helped you isolate certain stronger mana signals in the vicinity, while blocking out weaker ones under a certain threshold. Ashton was actually considering placing this spell into the wisp to potentially boost the strength of his 'wisp sight'.

Around the frame of the visual amplification spell, Ashton placed a slightly altered version of the search spell. It was easy enough to do; spell circles didn't work too differently to enchanting- or summoning-circles. Or at the very least, they were similar enough that Ashton had some pretty good instincts for isolating the important parts, letting him alter the form to some degree.

However, it wasn't like Ashton had suddenly just created a new, single spell. He was using two spells at once, and it wasn't like these were particularly simple ones. But even considering that, this was shockingly easy for him to keep up.

Ashton was able to quite easily shift the spell around, and through the magical lens, he could easily zoom through Drakewood's section of the veil, isolating specifically just the most concentrated spots.

"So easy~" Ashton said to himself. Magic truly made life so much more convenient if you knew the right spells. Not that it was easy to use magic, though. Trying to learn how to use spells in this way for years, developing yourself as a mage, just to be able to 'zoom in' for a bit wasn't exactly the most efficient use of time. Getting a spyglass was a lot easier.

Ashton took mental note of all the different high-mana locations in town. He would have the Pen of Insight jot them down on a map when he woke up in the morning, but for now, there were some other things that he had to try and work out.

Searching the veil was good and all, but it was better to use this time for something that he could basically only really do here. Or well, he could technically do it outside of here as well, but it was far easier to do it from within the veil directly.

And so, Ashton moved around the rooftop. As he did, with his fingers gliding across the fabric of this world, he shaped a quite large spell circle. Though, it wasn't a 'normal' spell; it was specifically a summoner's spell.

All of the ones that were noted down in the book he read were something like a mixture between classic spell and summoning circles. There were actually a few examples of spells that could use a summon as a conduit more directly, which Ashton was hoping to try out soon. But it also seemed like it could be a little dangerous for the summons, so he would wait until he had formed a magic circle and could cast more complex spells.

These were all things that were completely new to him, since the Summoner had forcefully made him 'block out' any information related to spells, but once he learned about them, he should be able to boost his power a lot more again. He had gone the path of the magician, so he should lean into it as much as he could.

With that in mind, Ashton soon finished the large spell circle. Many spells had components that one had to offer up, similar to when you were summoning things.

The same was the case here. Usually, Ashton would need to make certain offerings, but since this was literally one of the most basic spells for a summoner to use, those could all be replaced with mana. And, being in the veil while casting this, made it all seemingly a lot more efficient.

So, Ashton placed large bundles of his mana into the right spots of the spell circle, and then once it was all ready, activated it.

He watched the spell circle fall apart into numerous threads that reached out into the sky, shifting away and moving elsewhere across the veil. But there was still one thread left, like it was the spell's anchor.

It took a little while, maybe a few minutes during which Ashton had to actively keep up the spell, but before long, something came flowing down the thread. Lumps of mana came moving down the thread, gathering in one place. The mana was compressing and compressing, gathering together, until finally, the thread snapped and the compressed mana was left there on its own.

And now, it was time to unravel this whole thing.

What was floating there in front of Ashton right now was raw information saved in the form of mana. If he had cast this in the material plane, Ashton could have compressed it down into something like a small magic stone to slowly inspect over time, but since he was in the veil, it was more efficient to just parse it right away.

Ashton sat down on the ground and pulled the compressed information over, slowly tugging away at it like he was trying to unravel a ball of yarn.

The information was displayed in demonic runes; the native language of the target that he had searched for just now. But that was fine, Ashton was fluent, anyway.

So now, it was time for him to get all the information he needed to form a contract with Alice.
