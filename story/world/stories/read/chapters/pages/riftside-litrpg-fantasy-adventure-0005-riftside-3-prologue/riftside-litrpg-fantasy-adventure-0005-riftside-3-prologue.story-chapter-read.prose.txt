Vannash, the last king of Foraxia, strained against the weight of failure even as his two-handed warhammer, 'World-Breaker,' connected with the carapace of another chitinous pest.

Each strike was a kill as he stood at the top of the stairs to his palace, holding the enemy back single-handedly. The air stank of the alien monster spawn and his own brood’s blood. His body trembled with fury, and arms ached.

Endless. They are endless.

A tide of fangs and claws poured from the green rifts scarring his world. He, the last of the Sky-Kings, was reduced to fighting the invaders in hand-to-hand ground combat with the pests.

The crystalline barriers, built by his ancestors and maintained by his lineage, had shattered hours ago with the death of his younger brother and elder sister.

“Their sacrifice will never be forgotten!” he roared, a guttural sound, more beast than king, tore from his throat. With it came fire. A torrent of flames erupted from his core, engulfing the monsters trying to get to him at the top of the stairs. They blackened, shrieked, and collapsed into piles of smoking ash and bubbling goo, the stench of their immolation a fleeting, bitter victory.

But for every dead insect-like monster, two more surged forward, a relentless, unthinking sea of hunger and death. The howls and shrieks of the swarm were triumphant.

With the barrier’s fall, Vannash had failed his people and himself.

The thought was like cold water in his fiery gut, and pained heavier than any clawstrike.

This new Hive Mind, unlike those they had faced at their strongest, employed no cunning stratagems, no feints or ambushes.

Does it not know or doesn’t it care?

It didn’t matter.

It had an unbeatable weapon. Attrition. A suffocating wave of unrelenting bodies that didn’t feel pain or fear.

What remained of his kin, once rulers of their entire world, were now a flickering ember.

With a mighty swipe, Vannash’ bought a moment’s respite and hopped back, glancing over his shoulders to those hiding behind him.

A few dozen battle-scarred warriors, wounded, tattered, and grim.

With them stood the future. A mere hundred breeders, their precious eggs clutched tight, and the terrified, whimpering broodlings.

It was all that remained, but they would have to be enough. Vannash looked back to see a monster twice his height, its hide like obsidian rock, pushing up the wide stairs the tide. Eight eyes held no mercy or care as it bellowed a challenge and charged.

Vannash rushed down the stairs to meet its charge, using his bulk to squash smaller monsters, drawing upon the last reserves of his mana. A pulse of concussive force slammed into the back of the brute’s head, stunning it mid-stride and sending it stumbling. As he had done a thousand times before, Vannash sank his claws into the side of his challenger’s neck. With a wrench he tore muscles and sinew, and its grotesque head lolled to the side.

Black blood sprayed over his face and chestSome even touched his tongue.

He stomped back up to the top of the stairs, gagging on the metallic tang of forced servitude, the cloying sweetness of stagnation, and the bitter decay of a soul denied its true form.

Corruption. The specialty of this new Hive Mind.

His gaze flicked back to Lorixa, his mate, named from their world, her golden scales retaining their shine despite the slaughter.

She had fought with honor and desperation, helping him safeguard their last hope  at the gaping maw of the earth-vaults.

The deep, ancestral vaults, carved into the planet's heart, was Vannash’ final play. There they could endure the assault of the insatiable Hive Mind until it exhausted its interest in their dying world. And once it turned its gaze elsewhere, once it overextended its monstrous reach, Vannash’ would strike back.

A sliver of hope, sharp and painful, ran through him.

In the vaults he could mold the body and spirit of the broodlings into the finest warriors the newly discovered worlds would ever see. They would be a generation bred for battle. The single best force of fliers, with the best gear Vannash’ could forge.

When his mate had asked him of survival, he had scoffed.

That dream had turned to ash months ago. Their world, their vibrant, windswept home, was now an empty husk, a withered corpse of a grand civilization.

He gazed back at Lorixa once more.

She understood and a silent vow passed between them.

Only one purpose remained, burning hotter than any stomach.

Revenge.

Vannash squashed a two-headed monster with World-Breaker, and bit the head of another. He spat it into the face of a third before squashing a fourth beneath his foot.

With their new force, they would carve a path to a rift, tear through the Hive Minds’ conquered worlds, and hunt them to the darkest corners of existence.

They would become the scourge of the devourers, a living testament to a fallen kingdom.

The Hive Minds would regret the day they had set foot on Foraxia, the day they had—

An explosion ripped through the air and the ground heaved beneath him.

Where Lorixa and the others had stood a moment ago, there was only a searing white light and a mushrooming cloud of dust and debris.

Then, the entire palace collapsed.

His family.

Their future.

All vaporized and buried in an instant.

The world seemed to stop.

All that existed in him at that moment was an agonizing scream.

Soul-shattering pain clawed through his chest, paralyzed him, freezing him in a moment of unbearable loss.

Not for long.

A king, even a fallen one, did not break easily.

But long enough.

That moment was all the enemy needed.

They swarmed him, a tide of clicking mandibles and slashing claws, their sheer weight dragging him down, their numbers a suffocating blanket.

He fought, kicking, biting, his roars of defiance choked by the press of bodies, but they were too many.

His warhammer was torn from his grasp.

When he inhaled to wreathe them in fire, there was no air for him to intake.

In the end, they hauled his broken, battered, barely living form before the pulsating mass that served as the Hive Mind’s anchor on his world.

One of the true enemies.

Yet he couldn’t kill it.

He couldn’t even move a finger.

A chorus of a thousand voices, cold and devoid of emotion, echoed in his skull.

“Congratulations, Vannash. King of nothing. Strongest,most resilient, and worthiest of your kind. We grant you the honor of joining the collective.”

Honor?

He tried to spit blood and defiance.

A last burst of fire.

Nothing came.

He was spent.

Defeated.

Tendrils, like questing roots of black, diseased wood, snaked from the central mass, latching onto his skull and burrowing into his mind.

A violation more profound than any physical wound. Even worse than death.

His memories fractured, his sense of self dissolved.

Over the coming time, he was unmade, remade, and twisted into something new. Something… so much less but more and worse at the same time.

Through the agony, and through the erasure, he held onto an ember of his old self.

One the Hive Mind fanned, thinking it could use for its own purpose.

Rage.

Pure, undiluted rage.

A bloodlust that transcended memory.

A burning, all-consuming yearning to slay.

The need to kill.

That, they could not take from him.
