Asher simply stood there, staring at where Smiles’ severed head had been seconds before. The moment he’d turned his back, Watcher had warped in and snatched the undead out right from under him. After all this time, he couldn’t believe it.

Watcher had just delivered herself to him on a silver platter.

Laughing, Asher focused on the Eternal Mark he still had on Smiles, pulling his daggers out once more as he prepared to Recall over to the chatty head and stab the much freakier undead in a few of her many eyes. But the moment he felt Smiles’ new location, he froze. Currently, the two undead were zipping across the Deadlands, moving far faster than Asher could ever hope to on foot. But that wasn’t the more important part.

They were moving directly into the center of the Deadlands.

At this point, Asher only had another three or four miles to go before he reached the center. Already, he thought he could make out some sort of building-shaped smudge off in the distance, which he could only assume was the King of Death’s base of operations. He’d been planning to do his best to stealthily approach, but seeing as the Deadlands were almost completely flat and he had nothing to hide behind other than roving bands of zombies, he hadn’t really come up with a good idea for exactly how he was going to slip into the King of Death’s base without being seen. But now…

Watcher might very well be solving that problem for him.

Watcher doesn’t know that it’s Smiles himself I’m teleporting to, he realized, the smile on his face growing wider as his mark on Smiles continued shooting across the Deadlands. Damn, first Grunt letting herself get beheaded and now Watcher giving me a way into the workshop… Sure is nice when it’s the other guy making mistakes because they don’t know what your skills are.

On the one hand, Watcher showing up with Smiles’ severed head would give away what he’d been doing all this time. Though on the other hand, he figured the King of Death had to already know what was up by now. He’d taken out four of the six Servants, and Watcher had been, well, watching him from almost the very beginning. If the King of Death knew he was coming, better to let him focus his defenses outside the workshop and simply jump straight past them all.

Making his decision, Asher stored his daggers again, shifting his focus from Smiles to Samantha. Recalling back to the edge of the Deadlands, he popped into existence beside the hunter.

“Welcome back,” Samantha grinned, putting her nocked arrow away as she gave him a hug. “Can you see the lich’s base yet?”

“Not really, but I can do you one better. I think I have a way of getting inside completely undetected.”

Seeing as there were no zombies currently in sight, the team gathered round as Asher explained what had just happened. While he spoke, Samantha and Moxy worked together to help heal any lasting damages the Death Aura and his battle with Grunt had left him with.

“So yeah, I’ve officially got a marked man on the inside,” Asher finished with. “Granted, Smiles will absolutely sell out anything he learned about me, but I don’t think I revealed anything to him that Watcher hadn’t already seen for herself.”

“That’s one hell of an opportunity,” Rulfar nodded, scratching at his stubble. “But what if this is some sort of trap?”

“They don’t know I’m teleporting to Smiles’ head specifically,” Asher said. “I think this is a genuine attempt to rescue one of the Servants. Seeing as I completely killed three of the six, it makes sense they’d want to save Smiles. I don’t think it was a coincidence Watcher waited until I mentioned I was finally going to finish Smiles off. She was probably hoping he could get more information out of me, or maybe she was using him as some sort of beacon to not lose my position whenever she ran off to find the other Servants. Either way, I can’t pass up on the opportunity to completely bypass whatever defenses or undead monsters the King of Death has set up guarding his evil workshop. It’s too good a chance.”

“Yeah it is,” Moxy grinned, punching her fists together. “You sure you can’t bring us with you? Come on, what if I’m actively healing myself? I could probably last a minute or two against the Death Aura!”

“Not a chance,” Asher said. “As much as I wish you guys could help with this one, I still had a few miles to go and the Death Aura was already all but smothering, so it has to be even worse at the center than we thought. I’ll probably have to Recall within five minutes at most just to recover. You three would drop dead instantly, as if it were a directed attack rather than just a massive, blanket aura.”

“Just be careful. We have no idea what else the lich is capable of,” Samantha reminded him.

“I’m hoping that because he seems to specialize in creating powerful minions, he himself is more of a crafter type and not powerful on his own,” Asher admitted. “It would be a nice change of pace for once!”

Closing his eyes, Asher threw his mind over to Smiles, blinking as he suddenly found himself looking down at the severed head lying atop a slab of stone. There were dozens of miscellaneous tools neatly organized on the edge of the slab, and a bowl filled with glittering gemstone dust off to the side. Smiles, for his part, was merely lying there, once again humming one of the songs from the Lord of the Rings to himself.

Coming back to his body, Asher looked at his waiting team. “It really looks like he’s being prepped for surgery, or however the hell someone restores a damaged undead body. I think my best bet is to wait for whoever is doing the surgery to show up and strike before Smiles can give anything away. If I’m lucky, it’ll be the King of Death himself.”

“He doesn’t have any sort of Spatial defenses or anything?” Rulfar asked. “You really think you can just warp in there? It seems like a massive oversight.”

“I think the whole ‘constant, overpowering death aura’ is about all the defenses he probably thinks he needs,” Asher admitted. “I’m probably one of the most resistant people to such an ability in the entire world, and even I will succumb to it pretty quickly. All the Servants and undead are just added force, maybe even designed just to buy time for the Death Aura to do its job.”

“Your ability to warp in and out of the Death Aura to heal up and catch your breath is pretty unique, so that makes sense,” Moxy added. “Screw it, I say go for it. Fighting zombies has been fun, but I’m pretty done with this place. They’re not nearly engaging enough opponents for my tastes.”

“Alright. I’m going to watch Smiles and wait for someone to show up,” Asher said, making sure he had his living wood and mithral daggers in hand and ready to go. “Best-case scenario, this all ends with a single stab to the undead heart when the King of Death shows up. Worst case, it is some sort of trap, and I die instantly. Guess we’ll find out!”

Laughing at Samantha’s eye roll, Asher closed his eyes and threw his mind over to Smiles once more. The undead was still humming along to himself, patiently waiting for someone to come in and fix him up or talk to him.

Asher had no idea how one ‘fixed’ not having a body from the neck down, but he assumed it would be a rather complicated procedure. Smiles’ body had clearly been carefully designed to allow him his ridiculous speed, and that had to take time to make. Based on the bowl of gemstone dust and complex carving tools, Asher suspected it involved enchanting the very bones or muscles within the undead body somehow, though how any part of that process worked was of course far beyond him.

Normally, enchanting something was a complex group effort between an enchanter, a gem cutter, an artisan, and someone with the actual skill needed for the artifact. But if anyone would be able to handle all the steps on their own, it would probably be a millenia-old lich.

Asher waited with bated breath for someone to walk into the small field of vision he had surrounding Smiles’ head. He was slightly crouched and raring to go, his fingers clutched around his daggers as he envisioned taking down another member of the Council of Death before the lich even knew what hit him. He was poised. He was prepared.

He had a lot longer of a wait than he originally expected.

The hours ticked by, and Asher continued watching Smiles hum to himself on the stone slab. The severed head seemed completely content to lie there and wait, going from one song to the next in order to keep himself entertained. The longer Asher waited, the more he wanted to simply Recall over to Smiles and start searching through the workshop himself, but he knew that was a foolish idea. His plan to surprise whoever was coming to work on the severed head was a good one, something he continued repeating in his head as Smiles began humming the same song for what had to be the hundredth time by now.

Maybe I can just Recall into the workshop, stab Smiles, and Recall back. That would mean some actual peace and quiet for a change, and I’d still have my Eternal Mark safely nestled within the workshop.

The thought was more than a little tempting, but Asher stayed his hand. Even if he wasn’t prevented from Recalling into the workshop, there wasn’t any way of knowing if he’d trip some sort of alarm that would keep him from doing the same thing a second time.

Eventually, just as Asher was beginning to wonder if he could maybe stab his own ears, Smiles stopped humming.

“Oh hey!” Smiles said, grinning as he looked off into the void that was outside of Asher’s scrying range. “Haven’t seen you in a while!”

“You have not had need for repairs in over eighty-four years,” a second voice spoke from the darkness, its tone cold and calculating. “Even as our new Servants rise and fall, you continue to prove to be one of the King’s most impressive creations. Which raises the question…”

A tall, pale man dressed almost like a butler walked into view, his hands clasped behind his back as he stared down at Smiles with a stone-faced expression. The buttons dotting his suit were clearly carved from bone, and he had a tail forked into five separate, prehensile limbs, each tipped with what looked like a thin needle. While the rest of the man was still, the separate portions of his tail were anything but, constantly moving around and angling their points this way and that, as if looking for targets to stab. Unfortunately, while the tail was weird, there was a distinct lack of flames burning within the man’s eyesockets, which meant he wasn’t the King of Death. Curious who had shown up, Asher decided to listen in for a bit before he sprung his trap.

“...What happened to you?” the stranger finished, two parts of his tail drifting over and slowly moving around Smiles head.

“It’s a long story,” Smiles chuckled, not at all phased by the needle-tipped tails aimed at him. “I got stabbed.”

The room was silent for about ten seconds as the stranger waited for more information, but Smiles merely sat there as a severed head, grinning up at him. Eventually, the stranger opened his mouth.

“That is not—”

“I guess it was more slashed, technically,” Smiles corrected himself. “Yeah, definitely slashed. Slashed my head right off.”

“How did someone manage to harm you? You are the fastest and most deadly of all the King’s creations. It is why you were given the steelsoul blade in the first place. You predate even myself. It does not make any sense.”

“Oh, he has this cool skill that lets him share memories! He showed me a squirrel!”

“The ground-based rodent?” the stranger asked, his cold expression turning to one of confusion for a moment. “How did that allow him to stab you?”

“No, the squirrel came after. He took off my head when I got a bit distracted by a bunch of really powerful sensations. Pain, excitement, warmth, stuff like that. You should try them sometime!”

“We have no need for frivolous sensations. The King told you that himself.”

“Maybe the King is wrong?” Smiles offered, causing the stranger to recoil as though he’d been stabbed.

“You have been damaged further than I thought. Luckily for you, Watcher managed to find and retrieve your body. It should not take me long to repair you. Though there is one thing I don’t understand…”

Two of the stranger’s tails reached out into the darkness, and when they returned, they held Smiles’ body, placing it carefully down on the slab beside the severed head.

“How is it that you are no longer able to control your body? So long as you exist, the lack of direct connection shouldn’t be a problem.”

“Oh, that’s one of his weird skills!” Smiles said, looking happily at his own body. “He said something about being able to cut tritessence. No idea what that means. You’d have to experience it for yourself.”

“He can what?!” the stranger asked, his dark, beady eyes going wide.

Guess that’s my cue. Before the stranger could do anything with that information, Asher triggered Recall, shifting the space around him and appearing directly between Smiles and this new undead. Before the stranger could do so much as gasp, Asher struck.

Removing his head from his body in a single slash.
