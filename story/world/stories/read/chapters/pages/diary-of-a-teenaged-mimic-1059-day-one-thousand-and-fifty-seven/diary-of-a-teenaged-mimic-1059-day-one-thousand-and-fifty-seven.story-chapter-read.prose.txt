But that's the thing, just because I've picked off the scab, rinsed out the wound, and put a bandage on it doesn't mean I've had a medical professional Assess it, and it sure as shit doesn't mean it's Healed. I think I need to get into the habit of thinking about what I've talked about here for the past week then talk about it there, with whichever of my parents and ladies attend that week listening. Which, to be clear, was still a prison, still a cave somewhere under the mountains in Scandinavia. At least for the moment. But for our kids, it wasn't any of that. It was Grandma and Grampa's place. Like playing pieces to be moved around for some kind of advantage. Like assets.

Like things.

As a wise woman once said, all evil starts with treating people as things.

I shrugged. "Yeah, the first thing that comes to mind is the whole situation with Mads, but I know that's just me wanting control over something I shouldn't have control over."

When it comes to personal stuff, I absolutely want to know everything, even if I'm gonna forget it half the time until you remind me, but this..." I sighed. "This isn't personal."

She shook her head. "No, love, this is deeply personal."

I shrugged. "Yes and no? I think that's something that's been bothering me."

She opened her mouth to reply, then paused, obviously changing what she was gonna say. "What in particular?"

"Nothing really 'in particular'. But the general, I dunno, blurry lines. Back where I'm from there's all kinds of rules about, really against nepotism, against conflict of interest, basically shit that's saying that if you're in a position of power, like a government official, you shouldn't be promoting family members or spouses into positions of authority."

"Yeah. My point is that back where I'm from, way too many people put 'trust' over 'competence'.
