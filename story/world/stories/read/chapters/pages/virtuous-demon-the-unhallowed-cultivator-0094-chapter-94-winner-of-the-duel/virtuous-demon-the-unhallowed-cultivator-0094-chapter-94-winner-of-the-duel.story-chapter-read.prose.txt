Grand Elder Wanshang held a complex expression on his face.

For right when he was about to declare Liu Shuren as the winner, something strange had happened.

The conditions of the duel were either for Tez to lose consciousness and be defeated, or for Liu Shuren to fall to his knees.

The moment Shuren had kicked Tez away, he should have won.

But right after, Shuren had immediately fallen to his knees. In fact, he almost fell over, but used a hand to prop himself off the ground. His eyes were shaking, and his body trembled, as blood poured from his mouth.

And though Tez had fallen on the ground, his bright golden eyes were wide open, clearly conscious. Though he had a strange expression.

It wasn’t a look of victory, but of shock.

“A technique aiming for the soul. Such a thing shouldn’t be possible for a Rank 1.”

Grand Elder Wanshang had severely miscalculated the extent of this duel. Liu Shuren’s performance was understandable, as someone from the Liu Clan.

No, Liu Shuren was definitely a step above the rest, purely from his martial prowess alone. He had trained his body to the utmost limit, a method very few scions of powerful factions committed to.

But Tez was an extreme anomaly. At the end, he had used a move that aimed for the soul. Such a thing for a Qidense Realm cultivator was impossible.

Why?

Because cultivators could only understand and detect their souls once they became Rank 2; Path Foundation. And they could only develop it, evolving their Talisouls, at Rank 3; Core Genesis. Those at Rank 1 could not put up defences for their souls.

In the blistering heat of midday, Grand Elder Wanshang raised his palm, and with a voice that traversed throughout the Duelling Colosseum, he announced his verdict.

The elders to the side didn’t say anything, allowing Elder Wanshang to make the final judgement.

Shuren had now fallen unconscious on the ground.

Tez was also lying on the ground, incapable of fighting, though his consciousness was wavering.

But, this battle was not like a race. If both parties were incapacitated at the exact same time, then the answer was simple.

“The duel is over! As per the conditions of the duel, the result is… A draw!”

Rather than the rambunctious and playful atmosphere that initially began with the duel, there were nothing but murmurs droning around the colosseum.

Especially the final moment, with Tez’s declarations.

Many of the weaker outer disciples resonated with him for once.

The slanders and rumours of Shuren continued to grow, and many were also quite jealous of Shuren’s status, just for being a member of the Liu Clan.

So even if it was a draw, seeing the weak orphan fend off against a young master of the Liu Clan who, even with his cultivation level sealed, was far stronger than him…

They were going to choose the one with the more interesting backstory.

For people loved the underdog winning the battle.

“A draw… But what about my bets?” Xiaobo, one of the spectators, grumbled.

He received a slap on his neck by his friend, “Are you serious? Weren’t you the one that got to cash out on Tez surviving a hundred hits?!”

Fatty Chen left his seat, running down to the bottom to see Tez’s state. Alongside him, Wuden left to assist his master, Elder Harmony, with the coming work.

“What a disappointment. Your arrogance with defying your entire clan has led to you falling like this. You should’ve listened to my advice back then.” Su Kang left the room. For now, he had to focus on the task at hand.

And all the dull-eyed receptionist could do was sigh. “It seems I have my work cut out for me. These matters will start to become busy,” He stood up, leaving the place.

When Elder Harmony went to check Tez’s state, she scrunched her forehead with bewilderment.

I thought he was dead. For a second, I couldn’t even sense his soul. A backlash of his abilities?

She ordered her disciples on standby to take care of Shuren and Tez, letting them use the Duelling Colosseum as an opportunity to gain experience and learn. One of her Core Disciples, the red-haired Wuden, also made his way, taking a look at Shuren.

“Wuden, give me an analysis of Shuren’s injuries,” Elder Harmony instructed, testing Wuden’s medical skills on the spot.

He nodded while moving towards Shuren, now laying on a cloud bed, used primarily for transporting unconscious patients. At a glance, his eyes glowed slightly with the flow of Qi, examining Shuren’s body.

“The shallow cuts and lacerations around his body are of no major issue. There is… discordance in his Spirit Meridians… As if it’s been utterly interrupted, akin to a boulder blocking a river. This can be healed over time, albeit slower than external injuries. He has an Iron Grade Talisbody, which will help even the healing of internal injuries. But…” Wuden stepped closer, observing the liver of Shuren, bruised a dark purple hue. “No, that bruise isn’t the injury, it’s somehow seeped through his soul? What?” With realisation, Wuden finally widened his eyes with shock.

It was unheard of for Rank 1 cultivators to be able to use techniques that targeted the soul. So how could someone like Tez do such a thing?

Elder Harmony nodded with agreement, accepting the verdict of Wuden. “You are correct. Though even if it is an absurd matter, there isn’t any severe damage to his soul. At most, it’s like a small hole has formed in it.” Elder Harmony gazed at Tez once more, deciding to give her Core Disciple more responsibilities to bear. “Wuden, you will lead Shuren’s extensive care.”

He bowed with grace, hurriedly moving with his colleagues to take Shuren to the emergency hall in the Fourth Peak.

“Now then…” Elder Harmony stood from afar, watching Tez’s body shiver. No doubt a backlash from utterly emptying out his Spirit Meridians. She pointed an index finger, and a wisp of cloud emerged from it, floating towards the abdomen of Tez before digging deep inside of it. Holding her Qi, it served to stabilise his dried meridians so as to have some Qi reserves added.

She clicked her fingers, and the cloud bed that formed beneath Tez followed Elder Harmony as she headed to the emergency hall. I’ll need to investigate the kid more thoroughly. He’s no normal disciple.

On the eve of the colosseum, watching the two fighters be taken away, the stories and rumours from the Outer Disciples began to spread like wildfire to the Inner Disciples. Even as the audience had begun to disperse, the topic of the incident with Wei and Tez began to circulate amongst the Seven Clouds.

The emergency hall on the Fourth Peak was crystal clear and as blindingly white as the clouds. A suggestion made by the Sect Leader himself, in order to improve sanitation and cleanliness. While Tez was recovering, Elder Core Harmony let him be looked after by the other disciple healers in the infirmary. His condition wasn’t terrible, merely an over exertion of using all his qi reservoirs. He would recover gradually with a bit of rest and Qi rehabilitation. Qi rehabilitation was done through adding pure Qi with no properties and forcing a cycle in the recovering person’s body, improving the body’s natural healing.

When he took off Shuren's shirt, he was slightly shocked at what he saw. But he quickly moved on to focus on healing him.

Wuden had brought Shuren to one of the operation rooms. His condition on the other hand was far too strange. Seeing the wound on his abdomen, Wuden grimaced. He grimaced not at how it looked, as he had seen plenty of sights as a healer. What shocked him was how ruthlessly this seemingly external injury had injured the depths of Shuren’s soul.

“He’s no Rank 2, so he has zero defences on his soul. For now, I need to isolate the after effects by…” After administering anaesthetic properties of qi dormancy on Shurens body, he had started to clean up his body. And then, he would have to cut open his abdomen to investigate even further.

Before Grand Elder Wanshang left the premises to fulfill Tez’s request, he was in the middle of a conversation by Elder Spiritweaver and Elder Swordfall.

Wouldn’t you agree, Wanshang?” Elder Spiritweaver said as he observed the fallen pair of fighters leave the premises.

“It would be true if it were any other sect. But do you think we also work under the same rules? If the youth are willing to break out the norms set by the old and ancient, that only means that the signs of a new era is coming. If just that outburst was enough to rile the other disciples, then it just means his message resonated with them.” Wanshang fidgeted with the letter in his hand, thinking back to the request. “Old Spirit, Swordfall, you can tell his words were backed by conviction, and not of senseless drivel.”

Swordfall scoffed with a derisive smirk.

Just that action was enough to bring Wanshang and Spiritweaver’s attention.

Seeing him smiling, even out of contempt, was incredibly rare.

“If you think this was enough, then the Fourth Grand Era should have long begun. Yet we’ve stagnated even after all this time. What some kids think in one sect isn’t enough to change the world. It is simply the passion of youth.” Swordfall stood, facing Wanshang. I’ve wasted enough time.”

“Wanshang, I’m going to use my favour now. Set up an appointment with me and the Sect Leader.”

Both Wanshang and Spiritweaver were quiet for a moment.

After all, Swordfall was the only elder to have never used his single favour from the Sect Leader ever since joining. Everyone else had long done so. Whether it be for resources or connections, he had provided them all.

Just exactly what did Swordfall want now after holding on to it all this time?

“Alright. You’ll hear from the Sect Leader soon. I’ll be heading off now.”

And Grand Elder Wanshang left, heading up to the flying nimbus he had set up high.

Observing the ground where two men fought.

In this colosseum, emotions were ablaze.

The elder with the dark hair and amber eyes was different. The silver radiance of a blade, resting on a flower. At the end, even that radiance dulled to a cold metal, finally making a move.

The field of spectators were filled with a blazing two-coloured flame, vivid red of excitement, with a blazing yellow of joy.

Those flames flickered at times. Sometimes it dulled with a hue of bright yellow, shocked at the duel’s development. Sometimes it dulled with a small and cold blue flame, listening attentively to the stories of both Shuren and Tez.

Even the easterner from afar, Tez, had a strange form. A deep purple covered him like a cage, and his heart was the nest from which the blue bird rose from.

Emotions were dynamic.

Slight changes always existed.

It was a biological function of all sapient life.

“In the end, I never understand your nature.”

From the strongest Immortals to the Rising Paragons of the current generation.

All of them had immaculate strength, and vivid colours that bloomed in all sorts of shapes, sizes, and hues.

Even amongst the miserable lot of the world, like the homeless beggars on the street and starving orphans, they all held a flickering flame amidst their dark and stormy colours, to persevere and survive the next day.

And when she thought back to her youth, when she saw her fellow test subjects being used as props to understand the mandate of the heavens, even those resigned to their fate had an assortment of emotions.

Especially the small flame of hope, that ray of light that peaked in the abyss. For those that resigned themselves to death, they still held on to that light.

So that they may one day be led out of that darkness.
