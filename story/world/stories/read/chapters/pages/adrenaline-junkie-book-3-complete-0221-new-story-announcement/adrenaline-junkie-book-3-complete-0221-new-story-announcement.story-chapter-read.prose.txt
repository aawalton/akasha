In an age where superpowers have emerged across Earth, the era of Superheroes and Supervillains erupted in full force. In a world where Power, Money, and Status mean everything, what can a poor, failure of a mage working part-time at a cemetery like Cain Cole do?

With a poorly spoken wish to the universe, Cain’s world flips upside down — literally. Waking up beside a grave on a battlefield blanketed in corpses, he’s nearly killed within minutes in a world just as grim as his own.

To make matters worse, he discovers he was never fully human, and the father he thought was dead had actually abandoned him.

Now, with creatures straight out of every fantasy and mythology book hunting him, Cain must carve his path through both Earth and the dangerous realm of Wisteria — with one clear goal: find his father and make him pay years of owed child support.
