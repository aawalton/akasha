"There!" Su Yue shouted, and I could tell she had noticed the same thing I had. "He is weakening!"

She launched a fire technique that formed itself into the shape of a phoenix with its wings spread wide to cut off any route of escape while the beak drove directly toward Ke Jun's chest.

What Ke Jun did in response wasn’t anything any of us expected.

He didn’t try to move out of the way or block it at all.

His chest simply opened, splitting apart like something blooming but in reverse, creating a space that the phoenix passed straight through without touching anything, the attack continuing past him and slamming into one of the few sections of the shrine wall that was still standing and melting the stone into slag.

"A decent attempt," Ke Jun said as his body finished pulling itself back together. "But futile, this form can’t be harmed by conventional—"

"Heavens Jade Burial!"

Zhang Wei's attack caught everyone off guard including Ke Jun, because the ground beneath the ancient spirit suddenly crystallized into solid jade, the transformation spreading outward faster than even the blood red lines could divide it, Ke Jun was trapped with his legs encased in jade up to the knees and the look of surprise on his face shifted into approval.

"Now!" Liu Chang shouted.

Everyone attacked at the same time.

Liu Changs stone covered fist came in carrying the weight of something much larger than one person, Yan Li's golden light took the shape of an enormous sword, Shen Xuanyu turned the air itself into a conductor for his lightning, Su Yue's flames reached temperatures that were turning stone into vapor, Zhang Wei's jade techniques reached out to crystallize anything they touched, and I contributed what I could with Explosive Seeds and vines trying to take advantage of whatever openings the others were creating.

The combined attack was coordinated perfectly and carried an enormous amount of raw power behind it and the timing was as good as we could have managed under the circumstances.

But even then, it accomplished nothing.

Ke Jun's body came apart again, not divided by the blood red lines this time but dissolving into crimson mist that simply moved around all of it and then reformed behind us, pulling back into his humanoid shape, but the reformation took longer than it had before and his edges stayed blurry for almost a full second before they solidified again.

It was beginning to feel like each time his form was destroyed he was losing something he couldn’t get back, but the question of how many times that could happen before it became permanent was one I had no answer to.

"A commendable effort," he said. "Your coordination is excellent..."

He stopped speaking because golden light had suddenly filled the entire area, and Yan Li was standing with both hands raised and imperial qi moving around him in patterns I had never seen from him before.

"Heavens Mandate, Divine Authority!"

The technique went beyond what I had seen Yan Li do before, the golden light seemed to rewrite the actual rules of the space around us, every particle of matter and qi suddenly subject to imperial authority pressed down on everything.

This was clearly something that went beyond the Qi Condensation Realm.

Ke Jun's form wavered under it, struggling against what Yan Li was pushing out, and for a moment it looked like it might actually work, like divine authority might actually be enough to bind him in place.

Then Ke Jun laughed.

"Divine authority?" His form stabilized against the golden light that should have been holding him. "You believe heavens mandate can constrain someone who has touched eternity? Let me show you what true authority actually is."

The air became blood, the stone became blades, even qi turned against the ones generating it, and two of the Heavenly Jade disciples simply ceased to exist between one moment and the next, their techniques shattering as their bodies converted into pure qi. But Shen Xuanyu and Zhang Wei responded immediately.

Shen Xuanyu's jade colored lightning turned inward, transforming his entire body into pure electrical energy, which meant that Ke Jun's technique couldn’t find anything solid to act on.

Zhang Wei erupted jade crystals from his own body that created a pocket of isolated space that Ke Jun's influence couldn’t reach into.

Even Liu Chang and Yan Li were driven back, their techniques barely holding against what Ke Jun was putting out.

I survived because of luck and because I had been paranoid enough to already be moving the moment I saw that technique begin to form, activating every defensive rune I had while using Blink Step to put as much space between myself and Ke Jun as I possibly could in the time available.

The Aegis Mark's barrier shattered almost immediately but it bought me the fraction of a second I needed to get clear, and the Shroud rune and the World Tree Sutra's stabilizing influence did the rest.

When the technique ended and I could take stock of who was still standing, the number was much smaller than it had been.

Liu Chang was at the center of the platform with his stone skin cracked but holding, which told me a lot about the Immutable Titan Scripture because what he had just survived shouldn’t have been survivable.

Yan Li was still on the opposite side with golden light still moving around him though noticeably weaker than it had been, his robes torn and blood on them, but his eyes still focused and determined.

Su Yue had turned herself into living flame at the critical moment and that was the only reason she was still there, though the cost of it was obvious because she could barely keep herself upright.

Yan Ziheng was far enough from the center of the fighting that he had avoided the worst of it, though he looked shaken and pale, and one other Yan clan member was standing near him.

Shen Xuanyu had blood coming from his eyes and ears after reforming from his electrical state, and Zhang Wei had cracks appearing across his skin as he stepped out of his jade prison, but they were both alive.

I was at the very edge of the platform with my body making its displeasure known in every possible way from the strain of multiple emergency Blink Steps, the red sun in my inner world reduced to barely anything while the blue sun worked as hard as it could to keep my essence stable.

"Impressive," Ke Jun said, looking around at what remained of us. "Most impressive. But ultimately futile."

His form wavered again and took even longer to stabilize than it had the time before, so the reality warping technique had cost him something real, but whether it was enough, whether we could actually wear him down before he finished killing us all, was a question I didn’t have an answer to yet.

"Master," Azure said urgently in my mind. "Your cultivation is reaching a critical point."

He was right and I had already been feeling it, the stress of the entire fight and the constant desperate channeling of qi had pushed me to the edge of a breakthrough, and it was no longer something I could hold back, it was like a wall of glass with too much pressure behind it and it was going to break regardless of what I wanted.

Under normal circumstances this would be something to celebrate because advancing to the 7th stage of Qi Condensation would meaningfully increase what I was capable of and with my understanding of the World Tree Sutra and my runes I might be able to close some of the gap between myself and the Stage 9 cultivators still standing around me.

But breaking through would leave me exposed for a full second while my body adjusted to the new level of power and against Ke Jun, a second was a very long time.

"No choice," I muttered, watching him begin walking toward me with those blood red lines forming complex patterns in the air around his hands. "We are already dead if nothing changes."

I caught Liu Changs eye because he was the closest to me and gave him the formation shorthand for needing time and needing a distraction, and his eyes widened slightly when he understood what I was telling him I was about to do, but he gave me a small nod.

I took a breath and stopped resisting.

The power flooded through my meridians like a dam giving way all at once and the accumulated insight and energy that had been building throughout the entire fight poured into my cultivation base and expanded it.

"Now then," Ke Jun said, raising one hand as he closed the remaining distance. "I believe it is time to finish this. Your bodies will be useful as cultivation resources once I have divided them properly. But first, let me take what belongs to me."

His form blurred and he crossed the distance between us faster than I could track and those crimson hands came toward my face.

But Liu Chang was already there.

Stone covered and solid and immovable, he put himself directly between us and looked at Ke Jun without flinching. "You will have to go through me first, ancestor or not."

Ke Jun's smile had no warmth in it at all. "As you wish."

The clash between them cracked what was left of the shrine platform, their forces meeting in an explosion that sent shockwaves rippling outward and brought down the remaining pillars in fragments, and for a moment they actually appeared evenly matched before Ke Jun's expression shifted into something colder.

"Blood Reflection Art."

The technique took Liu Changs own power and sent it back at him inverted and multiplied in a way that violated everything normal about qi circulation.

The stone armor across Liu Changs chest exploded inward and blood came from his mouth as he was thrown backward, his body cutting through solid stone before stopping near the edge of the platform.

But he had done it. He had bought me the second I needed.

The breakthrough finished and power moved through my meridians all at once, my essence reserves refilling completely as my cultivation base pushed firmly into the 7th stage of Qi Condensation, and when I checked on the red sun, it was full again and had grown larger than it had been before.

Ke Jun sensed the change immediately, his red eyes gleamed and his smile grew wider as he spread his arms open like he was welcoming something. "Ahhh, perfect timing my child, you have made my vessel even stronger than it was, I must thank you for this gift, shall we begin the transference properly now?"

Then he was gone from where he had been standing.

My heart stopped because he was directly in front of me with those red eyes looking straight into mine and his pale hands reaching for my face.

"You are mine, boy!"
