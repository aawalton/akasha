The party began their descent in stealth mode, staring at the myriad monsters that called the lower strata home.

There were thousands of them down there. While Nick was confident his team could hold their own, that assessment was predicated on the assumption that they didn’t engage the creatures on open ground and get surrounded by the carnivorous swarm. The largest monsters couldn’t reach them inside the hall, and the building’s natural bottlenecks would mitigate the threat they faced from the rest.

While the spiders were insanely vicious, they were living creatures with self-preservation instincts. Nick was hoping if his party killed enough, the monsters would get the message and leave them the hell alone. One way or another, the closer they came without attracting attention, the better their odds of pulling this off.

Both men had activated Blend into the Background. Bandit had switched on his Necklace of Invisibility on top of his Shadow Flame Mantle. Lurk deployed the natural stealth she’d shown since way back on the Searing Isle. Considering that it worked while floating in midair, it must have been a technique all along. Everyone was using mana stealth, which his friends had practiced while he was battling inside the blade world, and they’d reigned in their killing intent, making them hard to spot via psychic powers.

Kenji had learned a new ability, Sense Ally, which let him know exactly where his friends were standing, no matter what form of stealth they employed. He shared that information with Tactical Command, and the team had no trouble tracking each other’s positions.

Bu now, the party had crossed the halfway point between the platform containing the rift and the massive block holding the Hall of Heritages. Nick was hoping they would reach the hall before the monsters even knew they were there, but matters weren’t fated to be resolved so simply.

As it happened, the arachnids laired on the bottom level were considerably more advanced than their cousins prowling the city’s upper stratas. They weren’t just bigger and stronger, although that was certainly the case. They had also developed a range Concepts, including perception specialists that were tasked to defend the colony from threats approaching from the air.

Nick wouldn’t learn the details until he returned to Aurora and conferred with the research team, but dozens of spiders had pooled their efforts to create a territorial domain—a bubble that encapsulated the lowest platform and everything within five hundred feet. The field of mana was so thin that he didn’t notice it until they were right on top of it, and by then it was already too late.

When the party sank into the monsters’ domain, the mana undulated like raindrops striking a still pond, rippling out until it reached the spot where its creators were standing. At that point, the spiders started screaming and all hell broke loose.

Monsters came boiling out of the ruined buildings, chittering as they fell into formation while raising their gazes to the party above. “Our cover is blown!” Kenji yelled. “Prepare for battle and get us on the ground as fast as you can.”

Nick set his Arcane Shields to their maximum speed. His stomach lurched as he assumed a combat stance and got ready for the biggest brawl of his life. He switched on Force Acceleration and Arctic Clarity, world slowing as he overclocked his gray matter. He had two passive slots now, so he added Enhance Senses to the mix, revealing the swarm in crisp, clear detail.

At the same time, he activated his new-and-improved Force Armor. Magic flowed out from his core, forming a protective shell of mana for every member of his team. He fired a mixed barrage of Mana Darts and Piercing Rays, kicking the fight off with a bang as his friends let loose. The party had picked up quite a few tricks since the last time they’d battled together and Lurk had upgraded several abilities during her duel with Zora’s Nightmare.

Bandit’s Shadow Mantle had gained an aura mode. A cloak of living darkness enshrouded Nick’s form, roiling like balefire though he couldn’t feel any heat. It would spread to anything that came close enough to claw or bite, covering his enemies in magic that stabbed and seared at the same time.

The dinosaur had picked up an aura too. Primal Storm formed a nimbus around their bodies—a weave of lightning and force that would impede any attacks headed their way while granting their weapons a high-voltage strike.

Not to be outdone, Kenji had upgraded both of his auras and turned Enhance Armor into one too. Tactical Command now conveyed information from the strategist’s skills, relay useful observations through its heads-up display. Rejuvenation was significantly stronger than before, providing a constant stream of stamina and regeneration that could heal minor wounds within seconds and prevent major ones from becoming life-threatening.

Unlike Force Armor, which created a durable shell of mana that sat atop its recipients’ gear, Enhance Armor reinforced the equipment itself, rendering the materials harder to damage and resistant to a wide range of elemental attacks. It worked on flesh and fur too, making the ability just as useful for the party’s beasts as it was for Kenji and Nick.

The battle’s prelude took place within a handful of heartbeats, but a great deal had changed within that short span of time. The spiders had organized themselves into combat formations. The melee specialists were waiting for the platforms to land, and monsters with ranged attacks had opened fire, pelting the invaders with everything from globs of acid to fireballs to bolts formed from silken strands.

There were three arachnids perched atop the Hall of Heritages that looked different from the rest. Judging by the dense flows of mana emanating from their cores, they were deep into Tier 2. Size Up informed him they were the creatures responsible for the horde’s strategic response.

“Shit.” Bandit let loose a furious barrage of Shadow Flame. “These spiders are way more organized than any monsters we’ve faced before.”

“At least the ones on the other platforms haven’t joined in,” Nick replied. “They must not be willing to enter the alpha pack’s territory.”

Kenji didn’t say anything, but Tactical Command had designated the spider generals as high-priority targets. Nick was planning to take them out next, but at that moment, the ranged monsters made their move. They had ceased fire long enough to coordinate their attack, firing over a hundred projectiles at the same time.

Nick aborted his spells and cast shields instead, simultaneously moving the barriers they were riding out of harm’s way. It took every scrap of his superhuman Mind to dodge and block them all, curtailing his counterattack.

That all changed when Kenji cast Bulwark and a sphere of force and darkness shimmered into being. The strategist had gained an Epic class during their visit to Yarren’s world, ascending to Tier 2 along the way. The trait from Frontline General strengthened any skill or spell with a defensive component, which was further reinforced by his Mobile Fortification Concept.

Combined with his upgrades to Bulwark, the protective magic formed one of the strongest forcefields Nick had ever seen. It deflected fifty projectiles in less than ten seconds, and that was only the beginning of what the spell could do. The sphere appeared pitch-black from the outside, obscuring the team’s positions. It was only lightly tinted from their perspective. Better still, they could return fire without disturbing the barrier, and it remained centered on Kenji’s position as they made their final decent.

Freed from the need to micromanage his platforms, Nick set his sights on the ranged arachnids and the tacticians beyond, drawing upon his dramatically boosted Magic and expanded energetic core to unleash a fusillade that would have done a gunboat proud.

Piercing Rays bored through the creatures’ carapaces, splitting into smaller drills once they hit soft tissue to wreak havoc upon muscle and organ alike. Mana Darts sank into spiders’ bodies, turning a portion of their vital energy into an invading army. The unfortunate arachnids stricken by his spells spasmed like they’d touched the third rail on a subway.

Nick didn’t want to attract any more attention than they already were, so he kept Sonic Slice in reserve for the time being. Instead, he infused Blightbane with pure mana and Devour to unleash a Severing Slice in the generals’ direction. The elite spiders scrambled out of the way, but that wasn’t a problem. The Concept-saturated wedge severed the weaves of magic sustaining their supportive magic, sapping strength from the swarm.

Beside him, Bandit released a torrent of flame, quite literally in this case. The lemur was manifesting a downpour of inky droplets onto the spiders’ ranged forces. They only dealt a small amount of damage, but dodging them was impossible and as the strikes accumulated, the inky magic burned and sliced with abandon, decimating sensory organs while raising body temperatures to lethal levels.

As impressive as that was, it was only part of the beast’s strategy. Bandit was also casting spells that looked like fireballs but served another purpose. When they struck the web-strewn stonework, the malleable pools of mana took on feature and form, revealing themselves to be Shadow Clones capable of independent action.

The constructs were perfectly capable of slinging spells of their own, firing searing lances and flamethrowers that sewed chaos amongst the arachnids’ ranks. Whenever they ran low on mana or were in danger of being destroyed, Bandit detonated his clones, burying anything nearby beneath a tsunami of Shadow Flame.

Lurk’s only ranged attack was launching boulders with her tail, and Nick had filled his backpack with rocks the size she liked. While he kept up his barrage of offensive magic, he reached into the bag and started pulling stones out. He cast Arcane Shield to create miniature barriers, setting the boulders in the perfect position to launch with extreme prejudice.

The dino bolstered her powerful appendage with stamina and force mana, causing the stones to streak forth like they’d been fired from a cannon. Lurk targeted the most troublesome spiders in the swarm, preventing them from bringing their magic to bear. Every monster she struck took considerable damage—chitin cracking and limbs breaking as the rocks landed like meteors plummeting from the heavens.

Kenji’s new class had come with a ranged attack, shoring up one of his prior limitations by letting him fight from a distance. Phantom Javelin was a forced-based spell that launched ghostly projectiles from the end of his spear. It wasn’t flashy or overwhelming, but he made up for it with precision, striking at just the right places to hobble the monsters’ momentum.

Less than sixty seconds after the party entered the spiders’ domain, Nick’s Arcane Shields landed on the platform beside the hall. The monsters had suffered serious losses during the exchange. While they were aggressive predators defending their territory, they weren’t willing to throw their lives away. After being hit by Predatory Domain and Paralyzing Roar, the surviving generals issued a shrill shriek and the swarm darted for the neighboring platform, overwhelming an unfortunate pack of voidbears as they waited for the intruders to leave.

That, however, was the extent of the good news. Even without announcing his presence via a sonic boom, the battle had attracted the attention of the city’s apex hunter. The platform shook and the outer web trembled as something massive ascended from the node’s depths. An immense pair of legs crested the rim, followed by a pair of fangs longer than Nick’s outstretched arms.

“Oh shit,” Bandit screamed. “It’s time to go!”

As the world bounced and heaved like a boat upon a storm-wracked sea, the team turned toward the hall and sprinted like mad.

They cut through the webbing blocking the doorway as a titanic monster stepped onto the platform and began its inexorable advance. The door was cracked open—enough room for a lemur or man, but not nearly wide enough for Lurk to pass through.

Nick reached out and pulled on the handle… but the portal refuse to budge. “Fuck me, it’s stuck!”

“The hinges are rusted shut.” Kenji pointed. “The door opens inward; we just need to hit it hard.”

Bandit conjured a trio of clones to slow the spider down while Kenji, Lurk, and Nick dealt with the door. The strategist stood with his shield at the ready, prepared to Shield Bash as the dino brought her tail whipping around as hard as she could. At the same time, Nick blew through the charges of his Wand of Force while free casting as much force mana as he could.

The timing was tight, but the men unleashed their might in the same instant the beast’s body slammed into the doorway. With the groan of tortured metal, the heavy slab of stone slid open and the party darted inside—mere seconds before the Tier-3 arachnid arrived.

They scrambled back as the tip of a gigantic forelimb wedged itself through the gap, fleeing deeper inside the building as the monster’s frustrated scream vibrated every cell in Nick’s body.
