As Kyle slowly made his way to the leatherworker’s area, he focused on his connection to the World Tree Seed.  He was more in tune with it than ever before, and felt like the shift to mid-tier C Grade brought with it some interesting changes.  Without needing any conscious effort, he could feel the internal garden within the seed.  He instinctively knew the status of each chaddling and plant within, and could feel the flow of mana from the Between that helped to nourish and support them.

Thinking about the chaddling legionnaires, he got the sense that he could maintain fourteen or fifteen without hitting his limit, a meaningful increase from before.  He also felt that the move to mid-tier C Grade might allow him to handle one or two chaddlings at a level beyond the legionnaires.  He didn’t have any schematics or designs to build on at this point, but it was still exciting to think about.

“When we get some time to settle in on Kertan Six, you and I will have to start working on new chaddling designs,” Kyle said.

[I COULD FEEL SOME OF THE SHIFT IN YOUR ENERGY DURING YOUR MEDITATION, AND HAVE ALREADY BEGUN TAKING STOCK OF OUR RESOURCES TO BUILD ON THE LEGIONNAIRE’S SUCCESS, DR. MAYHEW.]

Kyle grinned, turning the last corner to exit the central complex.  “I can always count on you to be thinking a step ahead.”

[IMAGINE A CHADDLING WITH RAFFLESIA FLOWERS ALL OVER IT.  AFTER OUR SUCCESS WITH THE MODIFIED DURIAN FRUITS, I BELIEVE THIS MAY BE THE MOST POWERFUL DEVELOPMENT YET.]

“Not exactly the direction I had in mind,” Kyle replied with a chuckle.  He wouldn’t know for certain until they started experimenting, but Kyle’s impression was that the next level of chaddlings may even be at C Grade levels of power.  If that was the case, they’d want to be very thoughtful about just what kind of creature they’d want to develop.  Initial thoughts split between a powerful ranged attacker, a front-line defender, or a speedy assassin.  Of course, there was plenty of time to make that decision.  The creation process wouldn’t be a simple one in any case, and they would both want to get it right.

Kyle did, however, use Cultivate to grow more of the modified durian fruits.  To C.H.A.D.D.’s credit, they’d come in handy multiple times during his recent fights.  As expected, Cultivate worked more effectively than ever before.  Before he knew it, he had dozens more durians fully formed and waiting within the World Tree Seed’s space.  He frowned as he realized that the spatial distortion within the seed even shifted slightly when he used the skill, almost as if it had a will of its own to create a better space for the fruits.  Something to keep an eye on.

The atmosphere at the leatherworkers was electric as Kyle and C.H.A.D.D. approached.  Workers in thick aprons were bustling this way and that, excitedly chatting with each other.  As soon as they saw Kyle, they came to a halt, with an old orc walking up and giving him a shallow bow.  All the excitement turned to breathless anticipation as the other workers looked on with eager eyes.

“Mr. Mayhew, thank you for responding to us.  We’ve made quite the odd find, and wanted to make sure it was brought to your attention sooner rather than later.”

“Of course,” Kyle said, shooting a quick glance toward the others.  “Thank you for your hard work, and for reaching out to me.”

“Naturally!” the older orc said with a beaming smile, gesturing for Kyle to follow him into the tannery.  “Now, as we begin, I want to make sure that you understand the body you brought us was not in great condition.  Not that I’m criticizing your work or the War Chieftain’s!  Just to ensure we are on the same page.  The materials are still wonderful, but they generally lack a certain… spirituality about them.  Certainly still C Grade material, but somewhat hollow in their overall power.”

Kyle nodded along as they walked through the main portion of the tannery, weaving their way toward the back where he knew the hodag was being processed.  The old orc opened the door for him to proceed as he continued his explanation.

“The materials will still fetch a decent price, undoubtedly, but it is unlikely that they will be able to be transformed into effective C Grade equipment.  They will serve best as materials for auxiliary items, or perhaps as components for peak D Grade armor or weapons.  All except one piece.”The orc clearly enjoyed his theatrics, which seemed to be for the benefit of the silent crowd of leatherworkers that followed them to the back.  The old tanner grabbed an inscribed wooden box, and proffered it to Kyle, who took it in his right hand and opened the latch with a small vine he extended from the World Tree Seed.  As he did, the seal on the box deactivated, and Kyle’s eyes went wide as a faint but familiar aura leaked from the box.

“As I said, everything else was deteriorating.  When we first extracted this hardened ball of scales, we assumed it to be a waste product of some sort that had accumulated near the organs.  It didn’t seem to serve much purpose, so it was set aside to be discarded.  However, after some time, we realized that it was actually drawing in mana.  Truly remarkable.  After some observation, we also noted that it was beginning to give off a strange aura, so we sealed it away until we could hand it to you.”  The old orc gave him an appraising look.  “What do you make of it?”

Kyle stared at the small, egg-shaped object within the box.  It was covered in a layer of green scales, and otherwise gave off the exact same impression as the “egg” he’d transported back during his Practicum.  Back then, he’d only been able to discern that wild animals were instinctively drawn to it, and he’d thought it may have been an egg with a creature inside.  Now, he saw it more for what it truly was.  A catalyst for growth; an organic natural treasure brimming with the concept of Adaptation.

Its mana signature was not an exact match for the hodag’s.  He suspected that this item had been used either as part of a ritual or directly by Mangalica’s unorthodox skills to empower the hodag as it evolved to C Grade.  Put differently, the hodag only became what it was thanks to the power of the treasure it absorbed.  That also explained why, when Mangalica drained the dying creature, he didn’t inherit all of the mana-resistant properties.  They were never the hodag’s to begin with, and he’d have needed to try and assimilate this object more directly.

Finally realizing that everybody in the tannery was staring at him in anticipation, Kyle cleared his throat.  “From what I can tell, it’s a unique treasure, likely something the hodag consumed or integrated prior to its death.  It’s a rare find.  A lesser craftsman would have overlooked it, thank you for your diligence.  Is the rest of the processing complete?”

Kyle asked the last question, even though a quick glance around told him there was still quite a bit of work to do.  Between making leather, processing the rest of the scales, and getting the rest of the bones in condition for transport, they had plenty ahead of them.

“We should have everything finished in the next couple of days.  Would you like us to send for you when it’s completed?”

Kyle nodded.  “That would be excellent, thank you.  And I’ll hold onto this piece, and ensure that the War Chieftain hears of your exceptional work and attention to detail in processing the hodag.”

That earned another small clamor of excitement among the craftsmen, and Kyle bid them farewell as he made his way back toward the central complex; box and treasure in tow.

[THAT IS QUITE AN INTERESTING SPECIMEN, DR. MAYHEW,] C.H.A.D.D. said once they’d gotten back to the relative privacy of their chambers.

“No doubt about it,” Kyle agreed.  “It also makes sense why the Archdrake Supremacy was willing to have an item like this transported during the Practicum.  If we take Ger’Sinh and his backers out of the equation, if the mission failed, the dragons could just send out a team to retrieve it with very little risk.  It’s not like the crocodiles on that planet were going to make a set of formations to absorb it quickly, and any other benefits would take a long time to bear fruit.”

[IT MAY BE WORTH CONSIDERING USING SOME OF YOUR GOODWILL WITH THE ARCHDRAKE SUPREMACY TO LEARN MORE ABOUT THESE CATALYSTS.  IF THEY ARE A CLOSELY-GUARDED SECRET, THEN IT ALSO STANDS TO REASON THAT SOMEBODY CONNECTED TO MANGALICA WAS ALSO A TRAITOR FROM THE SUPREMACY.]

Kyle nodded, following the logic.  “We know that Ger’Sinh’s group has at least one B Grade dragon backing him, and was pursuing items like these in the past.  It’s not outlandish to think that there might be at least some connection.”

Kyle sent a note to Suierrillax with their findings, trusting her to present the information to the Archdrake Supremacy in the best way she could.  Dealing with bureaucracy was already enough of a pain, and layering in the religious nuances made it even further beyond his personal abilities.  For what felt like the millionth time, he was grateful for all that Suierrillax did for him.  She’s definitely getting a slice of the bounty if Duroc finds anything worth turning in, he thought.

It took a little over a week for Duroc to return, during which time Kyle mostly worked on mana control exercises.  Jarberry encouraged him to keep working on developing strong foundations, advice that Kyle intended to follow.  The materials from the hodag had been cleaned and sorted, all bundled and resting in a corner of his room, with the exception of the small box that Kyle kept resting inside the C.H.A.D.D. pack.  From time to time he would take a look at the small item, but he still had no means to put it to use.  At least, not yet.

By the time he’d been alerted to Duroc’s victorious return to the village, the War Chieftain was already heading directly for Kyle’s chambers.  The orc looked more battle-weary than before, with some fresh scars added to the ones left by Mangalica.  Nevertheless, he looked to be in good spirits as he dropped a large sack to the ground with a resounding thump.

“I take it you found something worth turning in?” Kyle asked, eyeing the bag.

“I can finally afford that chef,” Duroc confirmed with a smile.  “So long as you’re strong enough to carry all this back to the Collective, of course.”

Kyle tapped the heap with his boot, feeling the solid material underneath.  “What exactly am I taking back?”“Notes, mostly,” Duroc said.  “The office also had a few jeweled sculptures and formation formulae that we put in here.  A handful of journals, some rocks, a couple of ritual daggers, all that kind of stuff.  But mostly piles and piles of notes.  I guess it makes sense that he wouldn’t want to keep the unorthodox plans saved digitally where it could get tracked down.”

“Yeah, leaving piles of paper lying around clearly worked out much better,” Kyle joked.

Duroc snorted.  “He definitely wasn’t planning on losing.  His people were shocked that we showed up and not him.  Arrogant to the end, for all the good it did him.”

“Are there injured that need treatment?”  Kyle asked, thinking about a field test of Devourer’s Domain.

“None that you need to bother with.  We took some casualties taking the village, but the guards surrendered pretty quickly once we broke the perimeter.”

“I’m glad it wasn’t bloodier,” Kyle said.  “There’s been enough death caused by that monster.”

“You’re telling me.  Now, not to kick you out too quickly, but any chance you’d be able to take this back to the Collective soon?  I feel like I’ve been holding onto a bomb waiting to go off.  I’ve had recording crystals going nonstop since we entered his office to make sure there were no questions about whether or not we read the material, and I’m still worried it’ll bite us somehow.”

“Then it sounds like I’d best be off,” Kyle said, hoisting the sack over his right shoulder as a network of vines tied the bundles from the leatherworkers over his left.  “It was good to see you, Duroc.”

“Likewise,” the orc replied with a smile, which turned into a grimace as he clutched his injured side.  “Next time, I hope it will be under less stressful circumstances.”

“I hope so, too.  And maybe with a few less hodags.”

With that, Kyle set his nav bracelet to record, activated Aura Force, and took to the skies in a straight shot toward the Guancious teleporter.  It was time to turn in his findings, collect his payment, and go home.
