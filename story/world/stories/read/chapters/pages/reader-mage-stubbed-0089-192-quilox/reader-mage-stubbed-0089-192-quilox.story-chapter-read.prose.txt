In Quilox nightclub was a special VVIP room. Merlin intended to sneak into it.

The only problem was the fact that a hand was pressed onto his chest, preventing him from further advancing into even so much as the general lounge, talk less of the secret area he was hoping to infiltrate.

“How old are you?” asked the bouncer. He was a huge man, several inches taller than Merlin, dressed in black button-ups and a security vest, with broad muscles, a bald head and beards, and eyes as hard as stone. Around his waist was a Grimoire strap, and from it Merlin could glimpse a dark-brown Grimoire peeking from its edges.

A C-Class Mage… he mumbled to himself.

The bouncer, despite being of a not-so-terrifying class of Mages, was practically menacing. Most bouncers in Lagos were, but this guy took the cake so far. Perhaps it was the fact that he towered over Merlin like a mountain to a piece of rock.

Regardless, Merlin gazed at the man through the pair of glasses he wore, trying his best to remain calm, and making certain that none of the pent up anger and frustration he had held back for the past few months bled into his expression.

“Twenty-two,” Merlin replied, distaste in his voice as he lowered his phone which displayed his table’s reservation.

The man frowned, disbelieving. He let his eyes wander about Merlin’s body, how he was dressed in a mere cardigan and black, wide pants, and the fact that he had a hoodie over his head.

“ID,” said the bouncer; and before Merlin could respond, grumbles came from behind.

“Throw him away already!” a person badgered. “Let the real customers who can afford drinks in. Right, ladies!” He laughed after his words, and giggles from women Merlin didn’t have to look back to know what kind they were echoed through the exterior of the club.

The bouncer looked up at the man and frowned, displeased. Without saying a word, he returned his attention to Merlin.

“Move,” he said, shoving Merlin to the side and making way for those he, despite his apparent distaste, considered genuine customers.

Merlin sighed in exasperation.

After figuring out that Quilox nightclub was the center of the Blackguards’ business in Nigeria, he’d made use of the whole of last week to research into the club and made sure everything was perfect for today.

Quilox nightclub was one of the biggest clubs in Nigeria. Scratch that—in Africa. And as such, they had very specific requirements they were not willing to overlook. One was the age limit being twenty-one. Merlin had turned eighteen just this morning, but that wasn’t good enough. Lying was his only option. They also required a proof of identity, which he had obviously prepared as well—a fake one—but it seemed he wouldn’t even be getting to use it. And, lastly, a reservation. That last bit had been covered by the pocket money he received monthly from Ms. Haneul.

However, none of his preparations looked to be enough. He had not considered the possibility of the whims of the bouncer on duty.

Confused as to what to do now that there was a barrier preventing him from making his way into the club, Merlin pinched his lips and watched genuine customers walk into the club without so much as any obstruction.

After a few seconds of staring, his brows pulled in.

So far, about ten men had walked into the club, and even those who were not much taller than him had not been asked their age. What he did notice that they did differently from him was that they looked far more presentable—at least for the occasion of clubbing—and they had a lady or two, or more—greedy—by their sides.

Was that it? Was that what he had missed?

Merlin retreated stealthily from the club’s entrance and towards the parking lot. There, he clicked the ear piece he had on and said, “K, you there?”

There was a loud rumble from the other end, constituting the fall of several items like someone in a groggy state had stumbled upon a trash can.

Merlin’s expression tightened as he heard the crash, and an image appeared in his head of a twenty-seven year old woman with blonde frizzly hair and the rare occurrence of brown eyes bumbling around in a singlet and sweatpants.

“Wh-What?” Kyu-Ri replied after a few seconds.

Merlin took a breath, holding back his frustration from having to share an accommodation with her.

Until three months ago he had no idea who Kyu-Ri was. It was Ms. Haneul who had introduced them to one another. She had made the claim that heading to a new country in a new continent, despite how exciting it might sound in hindsight regardless of the circumstances pertaining to it, wasn’t exactly the best of initial experiences. And that was amplified by a feeling of loneliness. So Kyu-Ri was to be my anchor as I was to be hers.

Merlin, after spending months with her, disagreed with Ms. Haneul. If anything, Kyu-Ri messed with his mind.

Moving from staying with neat-freaks like Nora and Chima and Hakyun to living with Kyu-Ri was driving him insane.

“I was not let in,” he said, dragging his attention back to the matter at hand.

A crunching sound of chips being grinded loudly by teeth came into his ears next.

“Told you,” Kyu-Ri said while still chewing. “D’ya think they wouldn’t know a twenty-one year old bloke when they see one? Pfft…” She scoffed.

Merlin played oblivious, choosing not to touch on that matter. They had argued on it all through the week, focusing mostly on how a good number of people looked older or younger than their age, and that he was one of them, considering his significant muscle mass in comparison to kids his age. Regardless, he was not ready to continue on that path. At least not at this very moment.

“I need your help,” he said. And before Kyu-Ri could argue against his incoming request, he quickly spoke over her, “Get a gown—a sexy one, if you can—and meet me at the club. I’ll like a suit too, or something a bit classy. You get it, don’t you? You’ll be my plus-one. You have an hour.”

Kyu-Ri was silent for a short second, then she barked, “I’m nine years older than you, kid! Show some respect.” Then she took a moment to draw in a deep breath. “And what are you talking about? A sexy dress? Do you want to get me arrested?”

Merlin clicked his tongue. “Regardless of the fact that what goes on in clubs stays in clubs, I’ll lie about my age. And even if we’re found out, I’m eighteen. You won’t be arrested. If it’s any consolation, it’s not like we have anything going on. Hurry up. We don’t have the time.”

Kyu-Ri ground her teeth in annoyance.

“Show me one of the ladies going into the club,” she said soon after.

Merlin nodded and walked back to the entrance of the club. Nudging at his glasses, he directed the camera hidden on its frame towards the line and a few of the ladies standing in it.

“Okay,” Kyu-Ri said. “Give me an hour.”

It took more than an hour, obviously, but Kyu-Ri finally appeared. She was dressed in a black leather jacket over a black gown that revealed an hourglass shape Merlin had no idea she possessed. From where the gown stopped just above her upper thigh, revealed a black fishnet that covered the rest of her exposed skin. Completing the outfit were leather high boots and thin black glasses with a simple silver chain and a handbag.

She would have been perfect if only she had taken the time to address her frizzly hair to a more reasonable degree. But Merlin didn’t mind at this point.

It would work regardless.

He took the clothes she had brought for him, snuck into a corner and changed. When he came out he was no longer in a hoodie but a wine loose shirt and baggy black pants, accentuated with little, silver accessories.

“All right, kid. What’s the plan?” she asked, chewing gum listlessly.

There was not much to explain.

Kyu-Ri was a master hacker. In the last few months, she had spent her time tracking the traces of the business involving the Blackguards and the ‘mana pills’ they delivered across the world, leading her to figure out that the Quilox club was one of the lead bases of the operation in Nigeria.

Back at the academy, Merlin hadn’t realized that the Blackguards had dug their fingernails into every country in the world, but now he was not so naive. And because he was told to lay low and out of sight did not mean that he would just sit back and let more mages like Nikolai suffer at the hands of the Blackguards.

Which was why his plan was simple.

With Kyu-Ri serving as his plus-one, he would be let into the club. After that, he would figure out which exact VIP room was to be his focus with the help of his Perception. Then he would storm the place and draw the attention of the law enforcement to what was happening under their noses.

Honestly, for that last part he had no idea if that would be the best decision to take. For all he knew, the Blackguards could have people within the organization too. There was no way people who could sink their teeth into the now-number one academy in the world would not be able to dig their claws into government bodies. In fact, a business being run between continents definitely had the government involved.

But Merlin couldn’t think of any other alternative.

Perhaps he would destroy most of the drugs himself just to get something out of this operation in case things were screwed up eventually, and leave a few for the law enforcement to try to carry out their duty.

Kyu-Ri popped her gum’s bubble as Merlin concluded his explanation. She didn’t seem that convinced that it would all work out, but she didn’t say anything on the matter.

Merlin appreciated that. He’d always done well in the spur of things. He’ll figure the rest out when the time comes.

With his hand placed weirdly and stiffly on Kyu-Ri’s waist, they both strode to the entrance of the club—Kyu-Ri for the first time, Merlin for the second time.

The bouncer’s domineering gaze fell upon both of them, and lingered on Merlin for far longer than the eighteen year old boy would have liked. Merlin almost began to wonder if the man remembered his face. But he had been wearing a hoodie!

Then the bouncer cleared his throat and shifted aside.

“Welcome to Quilox,” he said, and with a smile, Merlin nodded and walked in with Kyu-Ri, exhaling indistinctly in relief.

Past the metal detector and checks, the booming of music got excessively louder as Merlin and Kyu-Ri came upon a hostess waiting just by the side of the club’s interior.

“Welcome,” she said. “Do you have a reservation?”

Merlin nodded and revealed his reservation on his phone. It was for a bronze table, and the hostess smiled in response, brought out a wrist-tag, and gave one each to Merlin and Kyu-Ri.

“Follow me,” she said as she led them into a dimly lit space with flashing lights of purple, pink, and other varying colors, expensive perfume mixing with the smell of cigarette smoke burrowed into the air, and pounding music that seemed to bypass Merlin’s ears and head straight for his brain.

It was disorienting, and he almost stumbled. It was thanks to his Agility that he managed to keep himself standing without even as much as a stagger.

This was his first time in a club. The last time he’d approached one, he’d only gone as far as looking at it from across the street.

Honestly, if anyone had told Merlin on the day of his evaluation that he would be a mage with a System, who was on the run from his fellow mages as well as villains, while seeking to constantly be a thorn in the ass of a drug syndicate, he would have called them a bigger geek than he was. But that was his life now, and, goodness, was it exasperating.

The hostess brought Merlin and Kyu-Ri to their reserved table. It was just past the dance floor and DJ booth amongst the outer seating area. They took their seats, watching waiters carrying bottles and sparklers weave through the crowd for about a minute, until a server finally approached their table, courtesy of the hostess who had led them to their designated spot, bearing tidings of an apparent premium bottle of alcohol Merlin had no clue about, and what he called mixers and chasers, all with the package Merlin had paid for.

Kyu-Ri was doing most of the talking to the server, Merlin just listened.

After a few back and forth by the server, their table had a couple more drinks, and Merlin’s brows twitched.

He shifted closer to Kyu-Ri and whispered in her ear as loudly as he could to bypass the loud music, “Who’s going to pay for all this?!” He paused as another question sprung up in his head. “And who’s going to drink everything? I’ll have you know that I am not an alcoholic!”

Kyu-Ri shifted her face away from his, then used her elbow to nudge him away. Before he fully departed from her, she replied, “Do your job, will ya? I’ll pay for the darn things myself.”

“And drink?” Merlin inquired.

“That too,” she said.

Merlin hesitated for a second to say what he had in mind, then did anyway, “You drunkard! Don’t try that. Once I arrive at the location, I’m sending an SOS to you to call the cops. Do you understand what I’m hinting at?”

“I have no idea.”

Merlin raised a brow. “I know about your fling with that police dude. Call him when I send the SOS.”

Kyu-Ri glanced at him, sneered, and then shook her head. She muttered something Merlin couldn’t hear this time, but he had no intention to drag out the matter.

He turned his attention towards what was more important.

His eyes wandered from corner to corner, studying the layout of the club, and where every CCTV was placed. When he figured that out, he began to take note of how the hierarchy in the club was displayed. Those who made reservations and those who didn’t, as well as those who were in the VIP sections.

What Merlin noticed was that those in the VVIP sections didn’t go through the same door he had passed through, because he hadn’t seen anything to displace that fact. And from what he recalled during his research, the VVIPs had their own private sections, personal security, and special entrances.

In other words, he would have to dig deeper to find that private space.

Merlin leaned back in his seat, closed his eyes, and took a deep breath as he fell into a shallow trance. Without opening his eyes, the world became clear to him; physical  bodies were replaced with the subtle feel and intensity of mana shaped in the form of people. Those who weren’t mages appeared as gaps in this sequence of events, but Merlin wasn’t bothered with them. Those he sought out were definitely mages, and it made it even perfect that most of those in Quilox nightclub were regular humans. His job became a lot easier.

His senses flew out and into the building as he remained casually in his seat, blending into the atmosphere so that he did not come across as suspicious. It only took about twenty seconds for his searches to bear fruit. The VVIP sections were located directly above the general dancefloor, and in all the rooms present there, only one had the highest concentration and fluctuation of mana. A room to the far end of the hallway up above.

Merlin took a deep breath and snapped out of his trance. To his chagrin, in just the few seconds he’d been mentally away, Kyu-Ri had taken no less than five shots of a combination of all the alcohol on their table.

Unlike him, she wasn’t a mage, so he couldn’t use the fluctuations of her mana to determine if she was drunk or not, but he could see that she was teetering on the edge through the slight shimmer in her brown eyes.

She had almost the same alcohol tolerance as Janeth. Maybe she only edged the blonde-haired lady slightly. But, still, that was enough reason for her to not drink herself away. He needed her to be active for what was to come.

Merlin leaned into Kyu-Ri as though he was about to kiss her neck and whispered into her ear, “If you get yourself drunk and lose sight of our goal here, I’ll tell Ms. Haneul all about your excessive expenses, including your reckless attempts at trying to date a Nigerian guy. I can see you checking out a few now too.”

Kyu-Ri flinched and instantly became sober. With a hiccup she turned to Merlin, seething.

“You little…”

“We’re supposed to lay low,” he told her. “And we’ve both gone against Ms. Haneul’s wishes. Cooperate with me and I’ll let you have a full day to do whatever you want. I’d like the peace of mind too anyway.”

Kyu-Ri’s nose twitched. “Goodness. Why the fuck am I stuck with you?”

“Tough luck,” Merlin replied coldly. “Now, pull out your phone and remain on standby,” he added as he cleared his throat, scanned his surroundings for one last time, and then, as Kyu-Ri made an attempt to mutter something, he blurred and vanished from her presence.

Kyu-Ri was left hanging as she turned to her side. Merlin was no longer there.

With a sigh, she shook her head and took out her phone, bringing up the number of her first ever affair experience in a new country.

It pissed her off beyond belief that she would have no choice but to call the dude. But what did that matter? Lives were on the line here. And this was fun.

Kyu-Ri smiled in anticipation.
