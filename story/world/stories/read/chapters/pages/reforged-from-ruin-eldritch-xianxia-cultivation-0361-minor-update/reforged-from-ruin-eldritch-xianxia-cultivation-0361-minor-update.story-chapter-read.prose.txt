So!

As it turns out, working while traveling and experiencing a wedding and going through the trials and tribulations of the modern geopolitical landscape being on fire while being in my head is harder than it looks. That means a lot of different things, unfortunately, so I'll get to listing, even though it's not exactly anyone's fault or responsibility, and some stuff obviously will remain private. For those who want to skip ahead- TLDR: Been bad, working on being better, but big scary things abound.

In summary, some personal stuff has come up relating to travel and a big event that I've gone to and participated in, which really skewed my timing, and my energy and willpower have both been at a real low because hey, sometimes real life comes about and is beautiful and important and yes, a bit tiring! But this exhaustion is partially due to the direct travel and more partially due to having some pre-existing stuff alongside that was already messing with me. For a story of RfR's size not to have almost any books published is... relatively uncommon? The release of the first book was great, and a huge boon, but as you all know, the pace since then has been more turtle than hare, and I was already pretty far behind on that sort of thing relative to the followers and size of the work on here.

So... yeah. I've stagnated a bit, and that has consequences. There's lots that I can do that's in my direct control, such as resuming shoutout-swaps, publishing ads again, and upping production numbers, but burn out and financial stress have been a bit overwhelming, making them all a hurdle in their own ways, and they've created something of a feedback loop. Less ads, shoutouts and production mean less visibility, which means less new followers, which means less engagement, which means less money and less feedback, which means more stress, which means less ads and shoutouts and production, which means... etc.

I enjoy writing this story. I enjoy writing VISCERAE. But frankly, this is a luxury market! Not everyone has the extra scratch to be signing onto a patreon or buying a book during an economic crisis, after all, and that's not even getting into the drain the modern day has been having on people. Add to that my own issues and how I've been not-very-good at being my own agent or following through on marketability / visibility work, and that does mean that the patreon has dwindled month-to-month for almost six months now, which has affected me pretty intensely, as it's my main source of income. Book one's publication changed things, but until I get actual momentum and some second books and such out there, it still takes me about three good months on kindle to make up one bad month on Patreon, and until that equation changes, woof, that's scary as fuck.

So yeah. Between burnout and far more literal physical exhaustion, things have been scary, and knowing that there are things I can do means a lot, but it doesn't magic away the worries or the problems. Now, there is more than just whining here. This was an entirely unplanned hiatus brought about by this trip that I really underestimated, and as you saw with yesterday's chapter, there is still some movement on writing. Additionally, I'm looking to drastically change my medication in the next few months, and have a few appointments lined up to assist with that, and, as mentioned, there's a Lot I have that's still in my power to do. Real life has a lot of scary things going on, both in my head, around me, and in the wider world, but I am neither giving up nor facing a hopeless cause. Frankly, I think my writing is pretty good, and I want to keep going, to do more. I'm saddened that the Kindle release was only the first step, but I'm glad that the step happened, and there's far more possibility of success than there is certainty of failure right now.

But I am poor, I am scared, and I am tired. My work on all three of these things and more is ongoing, and, coupled with this trip, has knocked me a bit flat these last few days.

To those worried about me- don't be just yet. I have a good support system, both emotionally and otherwise, and I'm not giving up. As much as things hurt and feel unsteady now, things do change, and I am going to keep working on changing them. I apologize for the delay and for the heavy news, but I felt it unfair not to explain some of what's happening, even if I'm still keeping vague the details.

To those who want to help- word of mouth is king, queen, and country when it comes to indie projects and creative works. Reviews, both here and on kindle, mean more than you might expect, especially after momentum takes a hit like it has, and talking about it with your friends can do a hell of a lot. Patreon is available, and shit, there is a Lot of stuff on there between the smut, the art, the edits, and 'just' the direct chapters ahead, and buying a copy of First Embers on kindle is a hell of a thing, but just talking about it already has an impact. Otherwise, there's not a lot to be done on your end, and that's ok! I care about everyone who has read or engaged with my work, and it means the world to me to even be able to write as I do or make this post in the first place.

To those who want practical info- there should be another chapter out within four-five days of yesterdays, and by next week your regular updates should have fully resumed. Fingers mcfucking crossed, y'all.
