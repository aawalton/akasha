Alley didn’t end up sleeping anywhere near as long as he thought he would. After his ascension to rank ten, Alley had felt sore and exhausted, though he had managed to push through enough to finish Carding the remaining fiery Undead and evolving the Pyrrhic Pyre Zombie. Unlike the first monster he evolved, this one did nothing to his own Resonance system. Growing from rank eleven to rank ten might be all about evolving a Card featuring his main type of Resonance, but the same couldn’t be said about rank ten to rank nine.

Alley would have to check the exact number,but he did know this rank would require him to consume a collection of creature Cards that had at least twenty six hundred Power.

How one actually ‘consumed’ a Card was a bit of a mystery. Did he have to eat them? That didn't seem right, but he actually didn't know for sure.

What he did know was that after being led by the militia to the far more reasonably sized inn a few streets away, was that he wanted to sleep more than just about anything he had ever wanted in his life.

So when he awoke, perhaps eight hours later, feeling refreshed, he was confused by both the clearness of his head and the fact that he had no recollection of how he got there.

Stretching and yawning as he came awake in a warm, but barely lit room with a cozy fur blanket and a single bed that was around a third the size of the one he had been sleeping in, Alley didn’t feel any sense of danger, which was a nice change. The room around him was made of what seemed to be dark wood logs, and the small amount of light he could see by was coming from a fireplace that was virtually down to embers.

“Dendi.” He asked warily. “ Where am I? How did I get here? And uhh is everyone else okay?” He recalled fire, smoke, and death with a great deal of detail, then ranking up due to a whole day spent capturing Cards and combining them. After that, it was just a blur, which wasn’t ideal as he already had a chunk of memory that was foreign territory.

“I’m not your servant, you know.” The white-haired ghost appeared beside him, seemingly dressed in what Alley could only describe as a nightgown. Her tone was the standard snark Denderra seemed to bring out whenever Alley had a request for her. The pale girl’s eyes and face lent lie to the tone, though, Alley could see she was worried about him.

“I know that, but you are my friend. Now, are you going to tell me or not?” Alley had to admit he was proud of himself for looking at neither the ghost’s cleavage nor her bare legs, both of which were on display in the revealing sleepwear. He already knew she slept, but Alley was a bit confused by her ability to change clothes. Was there a stash of ghostly clothes stored in his Dealer, or in the dark palace between worlds that Denderra used, or could she will the garments she wore into existence as part of a ghostly manipulation of her form?

“You walked here after you were done making fire zombie Cards. The princes are fine,but staying somewhere else, Darius and Kyera are downstairs nursing drinks and pretending they aren’t flirting, and yes, everyone is fine. Nothing but a few scrapes, bruises, and a tiny amount of  toxic Resonance build-up. “

Alley opened his mouth to speak, but Denderra cut him off by adding.

“Oh, and about twenty hours till your next tournament match.”

Cleaning some sleep sand from his eyes, Alley couldn’t help but smile. “Are you sure you aren’t my servant? Cause I have to tell you that was some excellent service.”

“If you are going to do something, do it well, but call me that again and I swear by all the dead gods I will make you pay for it.”

Alley’s grin widened before he froze mid-head-tilt.

“Wait, you said Darius and Kyera were pretending they weren’t flirting.” His grin changed to an expression of pretend scandal, though his eyes shone with mischief.

“Are you telling me they are both flirting?”

The ghost’s eyes sparkled in a mirror of Alley’s.

“They are doing a very clumsy job of it, but the two of them are definitely flirting.”  The Oblivion Maiden replied with barely contained amusement.

“So I'm safe, weirdly well rested, and with plenty of time till I have to be anywhere?” Alley mused aloud.

“The well rested is because you ranked up, your body doesn’t need as much sleep because it uses that sleep more efficiently now.”

Alley nodded but waved that away like it wasn’t important.

“Great, great, but what I really need to know is if you can go invisibly spy on the two of them?”

“Weeeelll,” Said Denderra in a tone that was far less snarky and far more conspiratorial. “ Darius can sometimes see me because of his link with you, but if I make sure to stay behind them, I could probably manage it.”

“Well then, if you aren’t too busy sleeping, I think you know what I'm going to request.”

“Sleep? Huh, oh, the robe? Don’t worry about that, I'm rested and ready to spy.”

The former fisherman and the former princess shared another conspiratorial look before Denderra sank through the wooden floor, and Alley went to relieve himself and find out if there was a bath he could use to get the layer of ash and dried blood he had apparently allowed himself to sleep in off of himself. A lot of inns had some degree of a laundry service too, so he would have to avail himself of that come the morning. Alley might be more than a little discombobulated, but he could tell from the fact that his friends were drinking downstairs and the darkness outside a window in the hallway beyond his room that he’d slept well into the night.

By the time Denderra returned, Alley had given himself a once-over with a cloth he'd found next to the communal tub, which was sadly no longer warm. It was at least fairly clean-looking water, or had been before Alley began using it. The bath itself was located in a windowless bathing room intended for use by any of the inn's guests and was a large chunk of circular wood ringed by metal bands.

When he returned to his room, Alley stoked the fireplace back to a semblance of life and lit an enclosed oil lantern that he found hanging over a small table between the fireplace and the bed.

“I’m going to need new sheets.”  He mused, noticing the ash, mud, and blood he’d left all over the bed. It didn’t really matter; he wasn’t going back to sleep any time soon anyway.

Sitting down at the rustic wooden table, Alley laid his collection out in front of himself and began sorting through his Cards. He didn’t need to summon the physical copies of his Cards to go through them, but he found sorting them into piles based on various criteria helped him come up with uses he hadn’t considered.

He quickly realized that he wasn't going to be able to use his two newly acquired Cards in the upcoming rounds of the Frostwing Trials. That was annoying, given how good the pair of them were, and the fact that Alley had been left with three additional copies of non-evolved zombies that he could replace Cards he had no ability to play with.

It wasn’t long after he had placed his entire collection on the table that Alley noticed that his clothes stank enough to defeat the purpose of his bathing efforts, and he went into his pack, which had been placed near the door by either one of his friends or himself while he was half asleep enough not to remember doing it. Either way, he was in search of clean clothes and did eventually find a second set of basically the same shirt and pants he always wore. The Cloak of the Bloodless One was also hanging from the back of the door, but Alley concluded it could stay there till he went out. As powerful and useful as the artifact was, he still didn’t quite trust it.

Alley was in the midst of pulling on his cleaner pants when Denderra reappeared through his floor.

“Whoa!” The ghostly woman said and made the fakest effort Alley had ever seen to cover her eyes with a hand.

“Uh, give me a second?!” Alley said as his face grew hot with embarrassment.

“You know, back in my day, slaves were basically always naked.”

“I’m not a slave.” He said quickly, pulling up and tying the front of his pants.

“Your ancestors were.”

Mastering his embarrassment, Alley leveled his shit-eating grin on Denderra. “ So you want to watch me naked? I thought you preferred blondes.” The white-haired spectre’s mouth moved like a fish’s for a few seconds.

“I…I might be dead, but I’m not blind, okay!” She snapped her face, growing just as if not more pink than his own. “I’ve said before that I think you are kind of cute, so just drop it, okay!”

Alley gestured with his head at the collection of Cards on the table .

“I’m pretty sure you already told me what Pyrric Pyre Zombie evolved does, but I was barely awake. Could you  run me through it again, and then tell me everything you heard downstairs?”

“That, that’s a really good idea."
