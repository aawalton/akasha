Ch53: the pot throw

Or See below

December night at Vaughan’s manor, seen from above by Aziz on the third floor. Three half-round balconies. On the far one, Grein stands lit from behind by amber light, facing Val, still as a statue. On the near one, Eppie spins like a hammer thrower; the terracotta Christmas-tree pot arcs through the cold, shedding soil, rainwater and pine. Her Givenchy is shredding. Paint the pot in flight.

2

Ch53: Kiss With a Fist

The corridor afterwards. Val, in a scarlet McQueen dress with terracotta shards on it and mascara running, is on her butt, on the floor, looking stunned. Eppie is rigid with horror: Givenchy torn from hip to hem, palms bleeding, soil in her hair. Behind them, John Singer Sargent’s Madame X looks down with disapproval. Aziz’s flash is the only light. An assault caught on camera.

3

Ch56: Sukiyaki

Christmas Eve in a steam-heated service tunnel under Central Park. Eppie sings Ue o Muite Arukō to Kiritani among homeless men, with sleeping cats stirring in the dark. Bao will have to draw six cats.

Ch69: Smile

The 50th Grammys red carpet. Eppie in Vittoria Conti’s burnt-orange Miu Miu, drawing in the camera flashes and throwing them back. Zara beside her, looking terrified. Red Carpet scene.

Ch77: Father and Son

Ch90: The Killing Moon

Ch90: the roof
