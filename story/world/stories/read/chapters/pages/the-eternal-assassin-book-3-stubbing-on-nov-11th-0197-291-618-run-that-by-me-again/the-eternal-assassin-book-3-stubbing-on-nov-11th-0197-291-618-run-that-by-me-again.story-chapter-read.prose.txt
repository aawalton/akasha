The moment the words left his mouth, Asher felt his entire body relax as he partially sank back into the couch. It was as if he’d been lugging around a vest made of dense stone weighing him down these past few weeks, ever since Wilna had revealed to him that he was an immortal. While he was still absolutely terrified of Samantha’s answer, it was officially out of his hands now. He’d forced himself to ask the question, now it was up to her. Only…

Samantha didn’t react in quite the way he’d expected.

“Forever?” Samantha repeated, her eyes going wide as dinner plates as she gasped and held a hand up in front of her mouth. Her eyes began to water and her hand trembled in his own as she stared at him with an entirely new look filled with love and fear he didn’t quite understand. “Asher… I doubt this is the answer you wanted to hear… but are you sure? I expected something like this eventually, but now? After only a few months? This just… I want you to be sure it’s what you want.”

“What I want doesn’t matter,” Asher said, forcing himself to sit up as he took Samantha’s other hand in his own and stared deep into her dark brown eyes that always reminded him of the forest she loved so much. “This isn’t a decision you should make for anyone but yourself. Obviously, I love you, and I want to spend the rest of my very long life with you. You’re the best thing that’s ever happened to me, on this or any world. But at the same time, I don’t want you to be unhappy. This is a big decision to make, and I know you have a lot of things to think about. There’s Brian, or even potential children of your own one day that I know you want to have… There are just so many things to consider.”

At the mention of potential children, Samantha’s face went red, but her smile grew only wider as she leaned forward and surprised him with a tender kiss. After a few seconds, she pulled back, letting go of one of his hands as she wiped at the tears in her eyes even as she continued beaming at him.

“I know I’ve told you this before, but it means the world to me how much time you spend worrying about Brian,” she admitted, laughing quietly as she sniffed. “I mean, this is arguably the biggest decision in either of our lives, and here you are wondering how my little brother is going to take it. If anything, that just makes me even more certain of my answer.”

Taking both his hands in hers again, Samantha cleared her throat, and Asher’s heart skipped a beat as her gaze intensified. She’d been looking at him with a mixture of love and fear all this time, but now, there was nothing but deep, burning desire in her eyes as they bored into his own. Taking a shuddering breath, she nodded, giggling as she sniffed.

“Sorry to draw my answer out and torture you like this, but I had to be sure. Our lives are pretty insane right now, and there’s a lot going on… but if you think now is a good time, I’m not about to argue when you clearly went through so much of a struggle just to ask me. My answer… is yes.

“Yes, I’ll marry you!”

Time seemed to slow down as Asher’s rapidly building excitement and relief at her agreeing to live forever with him came to a grinding halt as she added that last sentence. Gasping and trying to swallow at the same time, Asher began violently coughing, his mind an utter mess as he doubled over and tried to figure out what the hell was going on.

“Asher? Are you—”

Despite knowing it wasn’t fair to her, Asher couldn’t help but trigger Comprehend’s second-tier ability, speeding up his brain and freezing the world around him as he bought himself a few seconds to think. Quickly replaying the conversation in his head, Asher mentally groaned as he realized what had happened. Asking her if she wanted to live forever with him when she didn’t actually know he was immortal? Of course she’d taken that as him popping the question! He’d been so worried about the whole thing he hadn’t taken the time to properly explain what he was asking!

Oh God, what do I do, what do I do?! Maybe I could make it to Donvath fast enough to convince him to use his Timeline skill and make it so this whole conversation never happened. Though I don’t have any idea where the High Prince is, and that wouldn’t really be fair to Samantha. I’d never be able to look at her the same way again having lived through half of this conversation without her.

Unfortunately, Comprehend only bought him about ten seconds, and Asher felt the world snap back into motion long before he’d figure out how to move forward. Suddenly finding himself choking on his own spit once more, which was rather embarrassing for someone who was supposed to be a master assassin, he continued coughing.

“—okay? Need any water?”

“I’m… alright,” he managed. Giving her a weak smile, he collected himself and took a few deep breaths, coughing one last time as he sat up. His blood froze as he realized not only did he now have to deal with this new misunderstanding, that meant he also still needed to explain to Samantha about the whole immortality issue. He wasn’t sure if it was just the sheer amount of panic and fear of the unknown reaching a critical breaking point or if he was just too worn out from the last few minutes, but Asher found that oddly enough, he didn’t feel quite the same block of fear sitting in his gut that he had the past few weeks. “Sorry. Samantha, you have no idea how happy that makes me to hear you say that… but seeing as we promised each other we’d always tell the other the truth, I have a confession to make. I wasn’t actually proposing to you.”

“You weren’t?” she said, looking more confused than anything as she stared at him. “What in the realms were you asking me then? How else am I supposed to take you asking if I wanted to live forever with you?”

“Samantha,” he said, sighing as he shook his head and just let it all come out. “I only discovered this recently… but I’m immortal. I don’t age. I’m going to be twenty-three forever. And I can do the same for you if you want.”

“Oh.” Samantha continued staring at him, her face a blank mask of emotion that he couldn’t quite read as her mind no doubt raced a mile a minute. Asher started shifting nervously as she kept staring at him, but he was determined to give her the time she needed to process just what he was telling her. After almost twenty seconds of dead silence, slowly, she shook her head.

“So… for starters… you’re an idiot.”

“Yeah, I figured that one out myself pretty early on in life,” he grinned weakly.

“Good. That will save us a lot of time down the road once we’re married,” she said, rolling her eyes at the confused look on his face.

“But I didn’t—”

“Asher, please don’t make me repeat what I just said. I love you, and even as a joke, I don’t like being mean like that too much,” she admitted, smiling as she leaned forward and gave him another kiss. “You’re cute when you’re confused. You might not have realized what you were asking me, but whether you were intending for that to be a proposal or not, there’s really nothing else it could be. Do you seriously think I would become immortal, to make the choice to live forever, and spend the rest of my days alongside anyone other than my husband?”

“Well when you put it that way…” Asher said, still trying to wrap his head around this new curveball Samantha had thrown him. Here he’d been trying to backtrack from his proposal out of honesty, and Samantha had all but rejected that idea up front, declaring that her agreeing to joining him in immortality and her wanting to marry him was actually a package deal. They’d been together long enough that he knew the look on her face meant this was a nonnegotiable. Ever so slowly, Asher nodded, running his thumb over her knuckles as he thought. “…Okay. Sorry. I have to admit, I never expected I’d be surprised by my own proposal. You don’t think this is moving too fast? I mean, obviously we’ve been through almost literal hell with one another after everything we’ve been through, but we’ve still only known each other for a few months. Is moving this quickly more normal on your world?”

“It’s still a little fast… but in the words of my own father talking about meeting my mother, ‘when you know, you know,’” she quoted, smiling lovingly at him. “I not only know a future with you is what I want, I also know you well enough that I’m betting rather than let yourself grow excited, the next words out of your mouth are going to be asking me about Brian.”

“Not to try and talk you out of making me the happiest man in the world… but yeah, those were going to be the next words out of my mouth,” Asher admitted, chuckling to himself. “I mean, immortality… You realize what that will mean one day, don’t you?”

“I think you’re getting just a little bit ahead of yourself here, mister,” Samantha said, surprising him as she giggled. He’d expected her to be a bit more morose, or at least concerned regarding potentially watching her brother die of old age. Instead, she simply shook her head as she smiled. “Asher, how long ago did you figure out you were immortal?”

“A bit over a week,” he admitted sheepishly, wincing as her smile faded slightly. Thankfully, it wasn’t anger that replaced it.

“You were struggling with all this on your own for that long?” she muttered, laying a gentle hand on his face as she sighed. “You should have told me sooner.”

“I was afraid. I wanted to see if I couldn’t come up with a way to share my immortality and at least offer you the option of joining me before I came out with it,” he explained.

“And that’s pretty much my point,” she said, rolling her eyes at his confusion. “Asher, you just admitted it took you all of a week to figure out how to make someone other than yourself immortal. If I hadn’t seen you do so many impossible things up until now, I’d be more hung up on that fact, but it’s what I’ve come to expect from you at this point. You really think I’m going to worry about watching Brian die from old age when he’s got decades of life ahead of him for you to figure out a solution? Realms, he might not even want to live forever, and that would be his decision to make. It would destroy me for a while, but still, all I want is for Brian to be happy. I’m not going to waste time worrying about potential futures when what I want right now is plain as day to me. I wanted to be with you when I thought we’d only have one lifetime together, and now that I know we might have countless more than that, I want to be with you all the more. That’s not so hard to understand, is it?”

“No. It’s what I want more than anything, so no, that’s not hard to understand at all,” he said, pulling her onto his lap as he kissed her. He didn’t know if it was simply the sheer weight of what he’d been struggling with over the past week falling from his shoulders or the fact that he was kissing his new fiancé, but either way, Asher lost himself in the kiss as he felt happiness unlike anything he’d ever experienced wash over him. Wilna had been very clear that with so many different dangers scattered throughout the world, it was all but guaranteed he wouldn’t live forever, immortality or not. He didn’t know how many hundreds or potentially even thousands of years he’d live for, but one thing was certain.

He wouldn’t have to live them alone.
