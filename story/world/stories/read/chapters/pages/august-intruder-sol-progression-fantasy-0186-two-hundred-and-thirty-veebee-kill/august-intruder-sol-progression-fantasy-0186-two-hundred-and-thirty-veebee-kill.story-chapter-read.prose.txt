There was also the part where it would look as if he didn’t know how to handle his problems, which he completely knew he could handle.

I guess I was quite dumb in my own way.

Perhaps Sanders was also manipulating the minds of their classmates, making it seem like reporting would be too much of a hassle, making it seem like they would not get any positive response from the teachers.
