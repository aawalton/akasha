Don struck a claw into the translucent abdomen of the creature. Green goop spurted out and the lizard shrieked in pain and anger. This lizard was clearly of the same breed as the previous one, but larger and more aggressive. Now that it got the jump, it behaved very differently. It was absolutely rabid. It clawed, spat and bit in absolutely wild abandon. Again it fought like a fish on hook, thrashing into every wild direction a whirlwind of sharp angles. Even having its abdomen pierced didn’t seem to slow it down. It darted at Don and clawed at his torso. Two gashed bit into his skin and whatever was left of his tattered shirt was being ripped to even finer shreds.

[Health at 87%]

[Energy at 88%]

[Debuff inflicted: Mild bleeding, Mild poisoning]

However, with Don’s higher Constitution and Ursine Vitality, the damage was clearly mitigated. The lizards were scary, fast and extremely aggressive, but Don found he could overpower them if he just avoided the initial ambush.

Soon he had the lizard pinned down, pressing a knee on its neck. It trashed and cut another gash into Don’s rib. Don gritted his teeth and slammed a claw through the creature’s head. Their bodies were squishy, and Don could feel his claw sink into the jelly-like flesh with relative ease. The lizard let out a high pitched deathrattle before it coughed up that green goop and fell limp.

[You have slain [Bilespit Ambusher – lvl 11] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 42 System Points earned.]

Don sat down panting and held his side. His heart was hammering and his breath was shallow. These goddamn lizards sure knew how to give him a workout. Despite their size they cranked up the intensity of these fights to sixth gear. Don spat out a glob of blood and tried to even his breath. However, no such respite was given.

It was subtle. Just a strange shuffle in the tall grass near him. Don’s Survival Instincts flared up to full alert and he rolled out of the way.

But just a bit late.

Two rows of sharp teeth sank into his leg, and Don buckled and fell down.

[Health at 75%]

[Energy at 70%]

[Debuff inflicted: Moderate bleeding, Mild poisoning]

Before Don could react, the creature attacking him ripped its teeth off Don’s leg, taking out a chunk. Don almost vomited in his mouth from the pain.

[Health at 40%]

[Energy at 55%]

[Debuff inflicted: Severe bleeding, Mild poisoning]

[Estimated time until incapacitation from hypovolemic shock: 2 minutes]

Don hobbled on the leg, but it couldn’t hold his weight, and he tumbled. The lizard struck immediately in all of its translucent fury.

It darted in and bit at him, nipping his arms and legs, and Don’s poisoning condition went to moderate. His leg was bleeding profusely and his mind was getting foggy and dizzy. He needed to act now, if he didn’t want to become lizard food.

So he extended his good leg, to bait out the lizard. Stupid beast as it was, it took the bait and sank its teeth into the leg. Don pulled forward and jammed his claw into its snout. The creature struggled rabidly, but Don shimmied his body on the lizard, while pressing his claw through the lizard’s snout and pinning it on the ground. Once Don got most of his body and weight on the lizard, he felt a sense of relief. The thrashing continued, but it was weakened. Don was in control. However, his leg was bleeding something real bad, and his vision was tunneling. The bastard must have struck an artery or something. Don figured he had seconds before losing consciousness.

So he used Devour.

The creature shrieked with absolute terror and agony, as Don sank his teeth into the squishy and wet neck. A rush of life force unprecedented flowed into Don. It was almost blissful. Don drank in the vitality of the creature and it weakened with every second. Its thrashing slowed down and its kicks fell feeble. Don could feel his leg wound close, but it was hard to focus on anything except the pure thrill and sweetness of the life force. Don lost sense of time and space, and only quit when the shriveled up corpse had nothing left to give.

[Racial Skill increased: Devour!]

[Devour Level 7]

[62 Evolution Points acquired!]

[Evolution trait Bilespit Ambusher: Consumed]

[Health at 62%]

[Energy at 44%]

[Debuffs: Moderate Bleeding]

[Hidden skill upgrade unlocked: Devour]

[Hidden Skill Upgrade: When using Devour on a living creature, life force consumed increased by 50%]

The previously squishy and slick lizard had turned into a shriveled up dry husk. The translucent feature was gone.

Don gasped for air. His mind was in a pleasant haze. The lifeforce he had consumed had been strong. And now he felt fantastic. He still had some damage. The wound in his leg wasn’t completely healed, and he could feel there was still some poison in his system. But damn, did using Devour on a living creature make a difference. Seemed like the System agreed.

Don was conflicted on how to feel about that. The System again encouraged violence. But at the end of the day, Don figured it didn’t really matter if he cut something’s throat or drained their life force. Dead was dead, and it would only take moments.

This will also clearly allow me to get more Evolution Points. If I can use this on targets while they’re alive, I can accrue a resource advantage. I would love to upgrade my Simian Physique.

Don used Devour on the other lizard he had killed earlier and topped his Health and Energy off.

Not wanting to waste the potion giving him extra stats, he ventured forwards.

Twice more he had to fight a lizard. One of them he saw coming, the other he didn’t. The difference between those fights was massive. He tried to use his Devour on the lizards when they were alive, but it proved difficult.

When Don passed the creek that cut the cavern in half, he carefully tasted the water. It seemed clean and the System didn’t give him any notifications about poisons or infections. So he filled his waterskin, and drank it in one go, keeping an eye on the environment. He filled it again and drank the damn thing empty in just a few gulps. He hadn’t realized how damn thirsty he had been. It seemed like Devour and pure life force could keep him going without food for a while. Even the need for sleep and water could be pushed away, but not permanently.

After filling up his flask, Don continued to the tunnel at the other end of the cavern, leading deeper into the darkness of the dungeon.

The luminous flowers still dotted the tunnel as Don entered it, but the light got very dim. He was very glad for his Night Vision and actually realized that the Evolution would give him Attribute points in Mental stat.

Huh, I should really upgrade it. At this point one lizard kill gives me more points anyway. Who knows what’s lurking in the shadows.

Before Don ventured any further, he opened up his Evolution interface and spent 60 Evolution Points to upgrade his Night Vision.

[60 Evolution Points spent]

[Evolution Upgraded!]

[Night Vision (F-grade)] -> [Night Vision (E-grade)]

[Night Vision (E-grade)]: You can see in low light environments due to acquiring light reflection tissue within your eyes.] [+2 to Mental]

Now that Don was paying attention to the effects of his Mental attribute, he could clearly see how his vision got sharper and he could take in more details of the dark environment. His vision was now mostly hues of gray in the dark tunnel and he could see approximately twenty yards ahead, before things started fading into the darkness.

“Alright, good,” Don muttered to himself. He was feeling a bit more confident now that he could see more clearly. Turned out he was still human enough to rely mostly on his vision. Maybe one of these days he’d get a crazy canine sense of smell or echo location. Who knew. But until then, he would have to rely on his eyeballs. He ventured deeper into the tunnel, in hopes of finding more lizards. They might not give him echo location, but he was definitely looking forward to whatever evolution trait they would yield.
