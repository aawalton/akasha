Now, she desperately needed to prove that she had her power back.  The scars on Kyle’s stomach twinged with phantom pain.  It was a feeling he knew well.

She’s still younger than I was when the Progenitor was attacked over Earth, Kyle thought.  If he still bore some of the scars from that, how much more was she going through?
