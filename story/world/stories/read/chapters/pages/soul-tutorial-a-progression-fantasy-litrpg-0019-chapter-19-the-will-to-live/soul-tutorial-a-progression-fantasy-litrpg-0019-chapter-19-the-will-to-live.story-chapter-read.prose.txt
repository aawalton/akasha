Luke tried his best to use the slime’s own flames to hide some of his presence, and to his relief, it worked. The flame wall acted as an obstruction not only to him but also to itself. Making it easier for Luke to maneuver around the slime or other attacks.

But he had miscalculated something as the energy shifted.

He felt that something was wrong. Luke tried to dash away with the supplied energy in his legs. Barely grasping the situation, three masses of flames shot towards him through the wall.

Grazing him in the shoulder, Luke winced as his shoulder had become charred black, but it wasn’t something that would completely stop his advances, deciding to move in faster.

He couldn’t run through the wall of fire, afraid of injuring himself more than he already had.

But Luke noticed the slime had stopped throwing out flames, so he braced himself, advancing within striking distance of the slime. While seeing if it would try anything else.

The heat travelled all the way into the ground, causing blisters on his feet. Barely maintaining balance, he closed the distance just in time for the slime to spew out just another 2 masses of energy.

The flames flew directly over his head, while the surrounding heat lessened, giving Luke enough time to circulate the soul energy towards his fist, which put an immense strain on his body. He didn’t know if he was overusing his soul currently, but his focus was all on the slime in front of him. Aiming for the core hovering within its body.

Not knowing if the core would move away, he tried grabbing it with his arm filled with soul energy. His fingers blistered the second they came into contact with its inflamed body, leaving his fingers quite incinerated the longer he kept it inside.

He lost feeling in his hand, but he continued onwards, knowing a restore would help him recover his arm anyway.

His fingers wrapped around the core, while his soul energy tried its best to resist the heat. As he maneuvered his fingertips into the slime, he held it within his grasp and poured all his strength into crushing the core. The slime resisted as the heat went up a notch.

Luke’s body was changing color, and slowly his skin oozed pus as the heat just ripped apart his skin, slowly cooking him alive.

Feeling weary from all the heat, he rotated the souls even faster, pushing the energy towards his hand. His grip tightened, and the core finally cracked. The slime's movement stopped for a split second. As it was slowly disappearing, it stopped and became whole again.

Something had gone wrong; while the core did crack, the intensity of the fire didn’t die down at all. It increased further,

"The core was cracked; that should have been the end of it!" Luke screamed while clawing at what was left of the core.

The flames surged forward, burning parts of Luke's face, while consuming its own body.

Luke tried his best to retreat, while pulling his arm away; however, it was stuck. The slime realized its impending doom and tightened itself around Luke's arm. While the heat climbed higher and higher, like a heartbeat, the flames pulsed once, then again.

I can’t lose another arm, He thought.

As if by instinct, the energy that was supplying his hand intensified, and his soul quickly darkened. The golden lines turned blood red, feeding him with power. As the energy surrounded his whole body, he felt invincible in that instant and could finally release himself from the slime in front of him.

But it was too late; the slime-disappearing body enlarged once more with the intensified flames, releasing fumes that darkened the ground around it. The slime's body began collapsing inward, folding in on itself, into an orb the size of a marble, becoming a tiny sphere of condensed energy. Which released a slower, but stronger burst of heat that charred the entirety of Luke's arm. In only a few seconds, Luke had been burned before he could even take a few steps away.

Heat expanded outwards from the sphere.

Everything went silent; Luke could barely register what had happened in that short time.

No flames, no heat, no wind, no souls, before the world erupted like a furnace, for a brief second, he experienced peace. Before the heat slammed against the back of his head, along with the increasing intensity, which created a vacuum that pulled Luke directly into the middle of the mess, blasting each part of his body, piece by piece.

The heat infiltrated his wounds, searing apart his very being and causing irreversible damage. As his blood boiled, and his eyes turned blood red. The heat reduced his bones to molten material and then refined them back into a solid as the temperature drastically changed.

He had barely taken a glimpse of the pedestal; soon, it turned into a wasteland where everything had turned to ash, along with the ground, which had become charred, and everything was distorted by the heat that originated from the sphere, with only the pedestal standing tall.

The blast rendered Luke completely frail and scorched his body. The explosion violently displaced the air, transforming his solid body into a mass of heated flesh and blood as the orb’s energy expanded and then vanished. But the heat still continued on even when the flames had disappeared.

Luke was left in a pit, his body emitting steam, as the heat persisted, lingering within him.

He still felt like he was being cooked alive. His soul energy was all that remained for his defense; applying it in whatever way he could to help him get through this. Had this been an elite instead, the island would have been destroyed, making any return impossible. The inferno continued to sear his insides, leaving him on the brink of death. With his lungs nearly caved in and his heart pulsing alarmingly, a sense of dread would surely overcome anyone witnessing it.

I can get through this, he thought.

Luke attempted to speak, but no words would emerge despite his efforts. He lost all sensation in his body, and his throat felt as if it had vanished. His back absorbed most of the heat. His spine was burnt in a way he never thought possible.

As his consciousness slowly faded away. His left eye burned shut, leaving only his right eye available. He recognized the pedestal would be his only hope for safety.

Even with an unusable arm, he still willed his right side to respond to him, digging into what he thought was his right arm into the ground, trying to bring himself to the pedestal before his consciousness faded. The flame burned off all, if not most, of his nerves, not even knowing if he moved closer.

Adrenaline rushed through Luke’s body, not wanting to give up even if he was burnt beyond recognition. Even with the awful state his soul was in, he willed the remaining soul energy, or what was left of it, to make his joints move. Pushing his arm outwards to drag his body out of the hole.

It curved upwards with a mildly steep edge, barely moving even an inch; he wouldn’t stop. Blood continued to pour out of his body through cracks in his skin. His heart beat awkwardly, as reaching over that edge seemed like an impossible task.

His muscles in his right arm creaked and ripped slowly as his strength weakened.

I can’t die here, not now, at least not yet.

Tears ran down his face before they turned into vapor as they left his cheeks.

He finally made it to the ledge, grabbing it with whatever strength he had left, barely able to even shift his own neck and head to see where the pedestal was left standing, only a few meters away from him.

He gripped what was left of the floor, his soul energy rapidly diminishing as he left a blood trail behind him.

He couldn’t feel his lower half at all, the pain not even coming through; he thought he was fine. Suddenly, blood started flowing out of his mouth; with every cough, his throat became blocked. Making it hard to breathe, he choked on his own blood.

His chest and arms were the only places soul energy gathered now, leaving the minor wounds alone. Providing the energy to keep his heart stable and his arm to continue.

Knowing he had little time left, Luke made a gamble. With the 3 diminished souls inside his body rotating, he tried his best to grab another soul from the sky.

His world flipped apart as the sky seemed to fall toward him.

Thinking it couldn’t get any worse, his soul rebounded as his previously white soul suddenly spewed out dark energy that tried to envelop his body.

Luke could only watch as the darkness tried to blind him, slowly making the pedestal disappear before his very eyes.
