In a faraway landI was now an infant and opened my eyes. Yesterday had been an unusual day, painful, confusing, and beyond it all, unusual. Trapped in a weird, strange room, almost an artificial one, then the plant, then the pain, then born. I was pretty sure I'd been born, it had been a painful and alarming experience, interesting though. Yet my consciousness from his previous life remained, still here, still with my memories.

My name was Aaron as I looked up at the thatched roof of the building I was in. Building was a really generous word actually, hut might be a bit more than this deserved in fact. Thatched... shelter, yes shelter.

At least as far as I could see, and man that wasn't much. Being a baby was weird, my head was so huge, and all my everything was weak. There were people around, some gentle, some who had no idea how to hold a baby, but I was managing. There were also the odd bubbles coming off my skin, no idea what those were, how odd.

My mother held me close, crooning a tune. She was at least mostly clean, for sitting beside a fire in a very crude shelter. I could smell it, dirt and smoke and unwashed human scent coming off of her in waves.

There were dogs, I think they were dogs, with us in here, living with this family or group or whatever under the shelter. It was so hard to tell, I could just barely make out their outlines, though the smell was that of unwashed hound, and that was a mess, then one of them barked and my mind was settled.

Was there a way home? What was I to do? Perhaps I should help these people with getting to modern technology.Hahaha, no, I didn't know how to make anything like that. Sure I knew a lot of things, and maybe some of them I could even implement. But I'd never done any metallurgy, no carpentry beyond a class in high school where we used power tools. Sure maybe I could give over some basic algebra and geometry, but what in the world would I know about that. I'd been a cop of all things the day I'd... died, yeah, I'd probably be considered dead.

That put a bit of a damper on the laughing. Died, I'd died, probably, or something like dying. Never really considered what that might have been like, never really thought about how I would. Sure, I assumed it would be in a bed surrounded by family, not that I'd had any, but I guess... I'd been alone, so alone.

But this was another chance, maybe all these people had been like me, maybe this was the afterlife. If it was it was a pretty shitty afterlife but far better than some of the options I'd been told existed. Though I suspected not. If all these people had been from Earth, we'd have a very different society, unless they just got here, but I didn't think that likely.

Man I was tired, exhausted from just doing nothing at all, just a nap.

I awoke with a start, my... diaper was a generous description, was dirty. That wasn't what woke me up though. What woke me was in the sky, everyone seemed to be looking, pointing maybe, and talking. Whatever it was, and my eyes were still so, so very bad, was huge, and round, or... oblong, man my eyes were poor.

My mothers, at least I was fairly certain she was my mother, held me close, turning me so she could look, which of course meant my view was pretty well blocked for most of it. She seemed concerned, and she hadn't done anything untoward to me yet, just a woman holding her child, being only a couple days old there was little else I could have asked for.

The thing, whatever it was, passed us, and some of the group went to chase after it. How odd that was, that these people saw something that surprised them so and still chased it into the horizon. Maybe it was an animal of some kind, a sky whale or something. Something like that would surely feed a lot of people for a long time. Maybe it was something else, I really couldn't tell.

My mother looked down and said something to me.

“Goo?” I responded, not really having any control over my mouth.

Something about that response delighted her, and I heard her voice rise in that way a woman's does when she sees a baby doing something adorable. She was shortly joined by a few others who could have been younger based on their voices, before an older one finally joined them. Her I clocked well, because she hunched and had gray hair.

Sadly I couldn't stay awake for more than a couple of hours at a time yet, so the attention from the women soon peetered off, mostly because I was not terribly interested, and there were only so many times I got peek-a-boo'ed at before I got tired of acting for them.

I didn't have anything against these people, but they clearly didn't know that I wasn't really a child in my mind, or at least weren't acknowledging it. These were things I would find out whenever I understood their language, or got old enough that it was obvious.

My days continued like this for awhile, wake up, be a baby for several hours, fall asleep again. I tried not to cause problems for anyone, that would be wrong. Would I tell them when I got older? I didn't know honestly, it would be the honest thing to do, but doing it would also make things weird. How would anyone respond to a child telling them they'd always been an adult in a child's body.

How would I even explain the situation to them? This place barely had things that stood upright, much less buildings, much less the kind of criminals that I'd been used to dealing with. Chasing a thief down an alley and finding that you were stuck in some kind of weird pseudo-world with a pool and vines wouldn't make sense to anyone from my world, much less this one.

Of course there were also the bubbles, small bubbles came off my skin now and then. I had no clue what that meant, a sign? A message? No idea, but there were things I was pretty sure of. Nobody around me could see them. That much seemed obvious because nobody touched, or pointed or anything else at them so far as I could tell.

Nobody else here had them either, so I was weird. That was not a good thing, weird in a group this size in a society that was.... I was guessing stone age, that was bad. They might think that I was possessed or an evil spirit or something. This would almost certainly lead to my death as I was still an infant.

It was days later that something happened. Getting an exact time on my age was hard, for all that I tried waking up and going to sleep like that was very, very disorienting, and I simply lost count, confused by how I was coming to and from again and again. However I did know it had been somewhere around a week since the big blob had appeared in the sky.

Several people showed up, and my mother made a noise like she knew them. However some of them were different, because unlike us, they had on clothes, definitely clothes, they were colored, blues and greens, not the colors of furs like our people wore.

Who were these people? What did they want?
