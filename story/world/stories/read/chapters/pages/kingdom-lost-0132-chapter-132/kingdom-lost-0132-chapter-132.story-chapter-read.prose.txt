Landryn stood at the perimeter of the forge as its stack puffed out a billow of steam. Metal-on-metal clanging rang through its walls. Nikola walked up counting backwards, "5, 4, 3, 2, 1," and before he reached zero a long metal tube appeared with a thud at the base of the forge. The dust it kicked up hung in the air before slowly settling to the ground.

The dull surface did not reflect the day's light as Landryn stared down the length of it. One end was open with the wall of the tube measuring the thickness of a man's thumb. The other end was forged solid with rounded edges. The open end had sharper edges.

Without a word, Landryn approached the object and leaned down. He touched it for less than a second, confirming that it had cooled. With one hand he grabbed the open end, lifting with his legs. The weight of it pulled down hard on his shoulder while the muscles in his lower back strained against it. The tube didn't budge, and he half smiled to himself. He stood up and looked at the indentations in his fingers left by the sharp edge. He rubbed his hands together and continued to study it.

After a moment, he leaned down and ran his hand along the length of it, pausing at the solid end. He rubbed his thumb over the rounded edge. He lowered his head, resting his ear on it, then with a closed fist knocked twice on the surface, half an arm's length from his face, and waited. A dull hum came from the open end and slowly faded to silence. He could feel the vibration travel through his jawbone that was pushed against the metal. Then he straightened up and returned to the open end.

He leaned in and reached inside, running his open palm over the metal while he worked his way deeper. He could feel the warmth of the metal still cooling from its fabrication. His lips squeezed together as he slowly moved further until he was shoulder deep. The sharp edge of the open end pressed into his cheek. His hand circled the smooth surface clockwise, inching to a complete stop as his eyes narrowed. Then he quickly pulled his arm out and stood up.

After looking at Nikola for a long moment, he walked to the tube's side and narrowed his eyes. "Ummmm, yes."

He then squatted down to the full bend of his knees, and the left one cracked loudly, with one hand on his chin and his elbow resting on his knee. "Huh, hummm, maybe…"

Nikola looked on, leaning forward, and his eyes moved from Landryn to the tube, then back to him.

Standing suddenly, Landryn moved to the closed end of it, reached down and pressed his palm firmly on the metal. He studied the surface. "I just don't…"

Nikola watched him work from one end of the tube to the other. Landryn's breathing slowed while he listened to his knocking. His hands turned white at the fingertips as he shifted his weight and explored the outer surface of it. His breath became almost nonexistent when he was up to his shoulder, extending his hand. Nikola watched closely for any sign of what his conclusion was and came up with nothing. Except that Landryn had paused at the closed end for a moment longer than he had at the open end.

"I wonder what goes inside, if anything at all?" Nikola said, just under his breath. "Never mind."

Instead, he opened the HUD and scanned the description of the object, looking for any clue of what it could be used for.

The listing read: Tube, material – metal, modification – closed at one end.

Nikola quickly closed the HUD and looked back at the tube, then scanned Landryn's face for anything to add to this puzzle.

Landryn stared back at Nikola and blinked. Nikola returned the blink, neither of the two breaking eye contact.

"Well?" Nikola finally said.

"Well, what?" Landryn replied.

Nikola's eyes narrowed. "What is it?"

Landryn looked back at the metal tube, lying on the ground in complete silence, then turned back and looked at Nikola. "I have no idea."

Nikola's jaw tightened, and his lips folded into his mouth. "No idea at all?"

"Well, I can tell you what it isn't."

Landryn's shoulders rose slightly as he stared back, with a blink.

Nikola turned away from Landryn and pinched the bridge of his nose. Start at the beginning, he thought.

Riley had introduced a number of items and projects that were outside the system, like the lodging house and a system building in the mine where it was thought none could be built. Then there was wild fire, using pitch and sulfur with charcoal. A weapon. The dry powder cakes she had prepared in the kiln and called quicklime. Another weapon. The biggest surprise was the black powder she had put into pots. Another weapon. All of these came from components that were the furthest thing from the idea of a weapon.

He looked back at the tube, narrowed his eyes, then looked at Landryn. "Could this be a weapon?"

Landryn looked down at the tube, then back at Nikola. "A weapon?"

Landryn turned and started walking to the far side of the tube. "If it was a weapon, how would it work? It is closed at one end, so nothing can pass through it," he said while moving his pointed finger from one end of the tube to the other.

Then he took a step back. "What about the weight? It's too heavy to move without a lot of help. Even if you were to launch it, what kind of device could hurl it?"

Landryn lifted both his hands into the air. "I guess if you could get it on the wall, one could drop it on the enemy below?"

And with that, his hands dropped to his sides. He looked back at Nikola with his eyes wide and eyebrows raised, then blinked. Nikola looked at Landryn, then back at the tube, lying defiantly on the ground.

Both men turned away from each other. Nikola stood with his hands folded behind him, looking up at the sky. Landryn walked to the far side of the tube near the closed end and squatted down. His eyes narrowed with his finger extended, rubbing against the surface, almost picking at something sharp. He stopped and looked up at Nikola, who was muttering to himself. His mouth opened, but nothing came out.

"We really don't have time for this," Nikola half-shouted over his shoulder in Landryn's direction.

A soldier in the distance pushed a wheelbarrow full of ore towards the forge.

"We have several projects on the go and the countless daily community requirements. Not to mention the ongoing resource complication and all with less than a tenth of the soldiers."

Landryn cleared his throat.

Nikola said, "I really don't see how all of this can continue."

The soldier neared the forge, scanned the situation and made a hard left, avoiding the forge completely, trying not to make eye contact with either of the men.

Landryn cleared his throat louder.

"What if we are attacked? The walls are a serious upgrade, and they will hold, but we have them barely manned," Nikola said, half looking over his shoulder.

Landryn cleared his throat and waved his hand.

"I say again, do we have time to stand here all day guessing at what this is supposed to be?" Nikola said.

"What about the small hole on the side near the closed end?" Landryn yelled.

"HOLE!?" Nikola yelled as he dropped his hands and turned suddenly.

Landryn slowly stood up and looked at Nikola. The forge's stack exhaled a puff of steam. In the distance, a horse snorted.

Landryn tilted his head. "So what do you think it is?"

Nikola looked at the tube, then back at Landryn.

"I have no idea."
