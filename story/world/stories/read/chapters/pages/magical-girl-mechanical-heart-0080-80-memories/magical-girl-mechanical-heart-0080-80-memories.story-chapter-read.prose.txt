"I honestly doubt it," I say. "She's not going to hurt Thea, but if Thea wasn't enough of a reason for her to change her ways before, she's not going to be enough of a reason now. I can't imagine a scenario where we could actually get her to back down and change her mind, and believe me, I've thought about it. You're throwing your effort away, Castalia. She's a murderer and a slaver. Even if she wanted it, she doesn't deserve redemption."

"I do not believe that is the case for anyone," Castalia says.

With the help of my skinsuit, I manage a deep sigh.

"…I figured you'd think that way," I say. "I think you're wrong, and I think that even if you are right, it won't be worth the effort."

"But you have agreed to help me anyway," Castalia points out.

"Of course I have," I say. "I love you."

There's a moment of relative quiet, as even the TV doesn't make a lot of noise when everyone is signing instead of speaking.

"…I love you, too," Castalia answers softly.

Oh. I… why is it that even though I could already feel it, even though I already knew it, those words mean more to me than anything?

"…Thank you," I whisper. It seems like a silly thing to say in response to her confession, but she just nods.

She understands.
