Despite Ray's lamenting about his dagger, he was far from harmless without it. The first Singer fell without Luke being able to tell what had even happened. Same with the second. By the third death, he caught something about the air converging around the Singer's face. Then Ray was among them. He used his hand in a similar way as Martin, but rather than phase it in and out of the physical realm, like the Phaseblade, Ray used it as a spear, piercing a Singer's throat without even letting go of Luke with the other hand. Luke couldn't help but be impressed despite the gruesomeness of the scene in the small cell.

As Ray killed his way through the small group, Luke focused and shifted over to using untinted mana. The infernal mana he'd regenerated still lingered in his system, but the pain lessened, and he felt his eyes change back to their original color. It took some mana to restore his body after the number done by the infernal mana coursing through it, but not as much as he might've feared. When healing himself, dealing with the damage in his arteries, veins, and even his heart, they weren't restored to their original form and shape. Infernal mana surging through them had altered them in what appeared to be a permanent manner. Luke wasn't sure what to think of that, but it didn't seem to hurt him in any way, and more Singers were coming.

"I'm good," Luke said, his voice low. "You can let go."

Ray stole a shirt from one of the mage Singers and threw it at Luke. "Put that on and let's go find our stuff."

The shirt was made for someone taller and with longer arms. It made him feel like a kid in an oversized nightshirt, but it was better than running around like some shirtless action hero. Luke missed his robe. Retrieving it wasn't the most important thing in the world, but Ray was right about the portals. If they left now, returning later would be a lot more difficult. They knew about Luke's ability to travel to those he'd healed, meaning they would have Dot under constant surveillance.

Popping out of Ray's shared stealth, Luke stretched his neck left and right, then back and forward, before rolling his shoulders. Mana was rushing out of his meta-heart now, meditation having kicked in. Having weaved guardian weaves around vital organs, Luke hopped in place a little, limbering up.

"You look like you're about to jump into a boxing ring," Ray said as he leaned over and grabbed a small dagger off one of the Singers.

Luke grinned and jabbed a couple of times. "Maybe I am!" Stopping when he sensed more Singers coming, Luke settled into a state of calm determination. "They're coming again."

The alarms had stopped blaring when Luke left the cell, but they started up again, and the light in the walls flickered on and off again and again, brightening each time. A loud ringing started up in his ears and the hiss of gas appeared again. They were serious about stopping them this time, but Luke wasn't inclined to go back into another set of restraints.

"Let's go," he said, heading out into the corridor. A giant of a Singer rounded the corner, and at first Luke thought Perwen had come back to life, but it wasn't him.

"Where to?" Ray asked, looking over Luke's shoulder.

More Singers appeared behind them, and more still behind the giant, who was now in the process of casting a spell. He was too far away for Luke to reach and tear it apart.

"Down, I think," Luke said and reached down, once again calling on his infernal mana, needing its destructive properties. Using Weaver's Whip, he sliced a percept circle into the floor, and the middle part fell down just as the Singer in front released his spells, a barrier of some kind, and Luke jumped in to avoid it, pulling Ray in after by a thread of mana around his waist. Ray let out a yelp but landed on his feet right next to Luke, who looked around. The relative quiet of this floor was a relief after being subjected to the seizure-inducing bullshit of the floor above, but the two of them didn't rest for more than a second before charging forward and through what appeared to be a cell intended for more than one person. It was empty now, and the door stood open. Beyond it, they found another corridor. They followed it to its end, then turned right, then left, and left again.

"This place is a goddamn maze," Ray said. "Where do we go?"

Luke pointed at a male Singer coming the opposite direction, searching for them. "Let's just follow where they're coming through. There has to be an exit here somewhere."

Without a better plan, the going was slow. Stopping to kill every Singer in their way meant that more and more of them joined the fray, and they soon found themselves harried from every side. It didn't take long for the enemy to start bringing more advanced weapons, either, like sonic rifles and mana-draining nets. All in all, they were a bitch to deal with, but it wasn't enough to stop Luke and Ray, who fought on. About three floors down, Smudge's insistent whining became too much of an irritation to bear, so, unsure if it was the right call, Luke let the voidling out.

"You've grown again," Luke told his companion, whose back was now at the height of Luke's shoulders. That size seemed to impress the Singers as well, because they let out cries of fear when Smudge first appeared, and those cries grew in both number and volume as the voidling joined the fight, tearing the Singers limb from limb before consuming their meta-hearts like some mana vampiric creature of doom, which, to be fair, he sort of was.

With a third joining their side, the going got a little easier, and it didn't take too long to reach the bottom floor, where Luke saw the outside for the first time. The sky was blue and dotted with the occasional cloud, and their sun looked to be about the same distance from their planet as Earth's. Even the trees were similar, he realized, when the Singers outside scattered and fled. The problem was, there was a whole damn lot of them.

"Why are we in a forest?" Luke asked, turning to Ray, who shrugged.

"Don't ask me."

Looking up at the building they'd just exited, it was much smaller than he had anticipated. Four floors and sort of thin-looking. Running in that maze-like, windowless place must've played tricks on them. Other than the still shouting Singers disappearing in the distance, the outside of that building was now as tranquil as the surrounding forest.

"I wasn't in a forest before. They must've taken me here."

Luke looked around. "I wonder why."

"To lure you here and keep you away from civilization."

"You think?" Luke asked.

"Yup."

"Why?"

Ray pointed to one of the clouds. "Because of that."

A dark spot against the blue of the sky was growing larger. No, not growing, moving. It was moving toward them.

"Aw, shit," Luke said. "Dot isn't here anymore. Should have realized something was up when they moved her."

"Does that mean there's a portal leading away from this place?" Ray asked, his voice taking on a tinge of worry. "I'm not very durable, and that thing looks like it'll make a big boom."

"I'll carry us away from here," Luke said, waving for Ray to come closer. Smudge hopped back into the Stable like a good boy.

"What about my dagger?" Ray asked, turning to look back up at the building.

"I bet it isn't here. That Cage guy probably took it with him. My robe and Mana Orb, too. Shit, my staff! Forgot about that. That's an asshole move if ever there was one!"

"Your staff?" Ray asked. "You've got light tentacles of death! I need my dagger! Do you know how many souls I trapped in there? A bunch!"

"Maybe you shouldn't go around trapping souls."

"Maybe you shouldn't go around trapping souls," Ray mimicked, using a baby voice. Following that, he glanced up at the sky again. The dot was now a lot larger, and you could even make out some details. If he was going by nothing more than appearance, that thing had to be a..."

"A nuke! What the fuck?!" Ray shouted. "How do they have one of our nukes?!"

Luke proffered his hand again. "Let's just get the hell out of here."

"Not back to Earth," Ray said.

"I told you, Dot will just be another trap."

"I'll put us in stealth before we go," Ray argued. "That way, they can't catch us. We spring the trap and kill them all, and I'll get my dagger."

"Weren't you in stealth before when you were caught?"

Ray glared at him. "Maybe."

The missile was coming now and would hit in seconds, but Luke had prepared for the jump while they talked. All he needed now was to select which echo thread to take. Grabbing Ray's hand by force and wrapping a thread of mana around his friend's wrist, he readied them for the weavestep.

"Go to Dot! Don't be a chicken!" Ray shouted.

Luke scoffed. "You think calling me a chicken will work?"

It did. The nuke hit the building, and Luke used Manipulate Thread and Weavestep in tandem, propelling them both through the Singer system weave. Rather than heading back to Earth, Luke directed them through Dot's thread. It was a trap, of course it was, but Luke refused to give up his staff and robe. Also, they had a job to do. There were still portals that needed closing. Bracing for the moment of arrival, Luke readied himself. This was about to get ugly, and he wasn’t just talking about the nightshirt he still wore.
