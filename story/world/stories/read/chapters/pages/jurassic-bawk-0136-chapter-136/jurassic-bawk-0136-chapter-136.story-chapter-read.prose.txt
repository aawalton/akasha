Charlemagne immediately took off after what looked like the larger group of survivors from the skirmish, noting that one of the mounted troops in the lead held a wooden pole with a red and yellow flag. For some reason, the flag made him hangry. Properly utilizing his Ignition Core for the first time, the rooster turned his focus inward, relaxing the built-in limiter that prevented it from running at full power all the time. In just a few seconds, he had caught up to the front of the formation. The wind just from his passing bowled over some of the cavalry, causing human and mount to scream in agony as bodies were crushed against the unforgiving ground.

As the rooster landed, his very presence caused more of the mounts to panic. Some reared up and threw their riders, while others wheeled around and began to sprint in a random direction. Charlemagne let them escape, choosing instead to fight those few who managed to master their mounts and themselves. He used his beak to flick the tip of a spear aside, using the momentum to spin. The edges of his feathers smoldered like lit coals as they passed through the ribcage of a horse, sending the beast tumbling end over end. Its rider did not rise.

More skirmishers attacked with a combination of weapons. He deflected an arrow with a quick mana shield before dodging the strike of a cavalry sword, responding with a peck of his own that was lengthened by a beak of pure flame. It melted through armor and flesh equally well. Leaving the riderless mount alone, the rooster turned and found his next target. A few strides brought him within range of the archer, who, not expecting to be targeted so quickly, yelled and attempted to veer away. But it was too late.

Charlemagne spun around, searching for his next victim, but the remnants of the group had completely scattered, and he did not wish to chase them down one by one. He let them flee, for there were juicier targets not far away.

Taking to the air once again, he ignored a potshot taken by one of the braver soldiers that had survived his assault and spiraled high into the air. The ground receded at a dizzying speed as his powerful wings took him high above the trampled plain. But it wasn’t the ruined earth that Charlemagne was interested in, it was what lay on either side. With a flick of his wings, the rooster shot forward, instantly surpassing the sound barrier as he made a beeline toward the camp that sported red and yellow flags that matched the one carried by the soldiers.

As he flew, the rooster reached out his magical senses, searching for strong opponents in the camp that might be worth hunting down and eating. Unfortunately, there were none. The strongest beings seemed to be clustered in one spot near the center of the camp, but each of them was only a little bit stronger than Drago. And Charlemagne was twice as strong as he had been during that fight.

Smug thoughts of superiority filled the rooster’s head as he swooped down towards the camp, pulling fiery mana from his Core and circulating it through his limbs in preparation for a rapid descent. Flames began to dance from the ends of his feathers as his internal temperature soared, causing him to glow like a shooting star. But that didn’t matter to the rooster as the lookouts had already spotted him. There was a bleating of horns and faint cries as soldiers stopped what they were doing and looked to the sky before screaming and pointing.

“D-dragon!” one especially loud soldier cried as the living fireball fell from the sky.

“Shields! Shields now!” a commanding voice boomed out over the general din. The rooster felt surges of mana from spots along the camp’s outer wall, each one tied to a figure wearing blue robes and carrying identical black and silver staffs. A moment later, a spherical mana shield coalesced around the entire camp, blocking off approach from any angle. And it was quite powerful.

Charlemagne happily accepted the shield’s challenge, as he was interested in learning the full extent of his new powers. He immediately shot forward, using Afterburner to greatly increase his diving speed. A wedge of mana and fire formed in front of his face as he gathered his strength, intending to drive his way through the shield with one blow.

The rooster’s flames flared up, burning so hot that his entire body was coated in white flames for an instant. Then Charlemagne slammed into the mana shield with a sickening crack. Down below, hundreds of soldiers were flung to the ground by the sheer force of the shockwave that bled through. A few were caught in the gout of flames that managed to push through the shield, but none of them appeared seriously harmed.

Charlemagne, however, was rather injured by the impact. He stood on the shield and looked down as his healing powers pulled his body back into shape, the incredible heat he was generating allowing flesh and bone to reshape themselves with a speed that was disconcerting to watch. His Ignition Core roared within him, straining against its limits, but the rooster did not allow it free rein. The massacre would come later, after he managed to take the shield down.

The shield’s mana switched frequencies too quickly for the rooster to match it, meaning that he couldn’t pass through by fooling the shield into thinking he was part of it. It was also both stronger and better made than the shield that had protected the asteroid, since there were no imperfections that he could find and exploit. Even using the Monocle of Refinement didn’t help him spot a weak point, which was a first for him. The rooster needed one of two things: either overwhelming power, or a new trick.

Charlemagne knew that, given enough time, he could break through the shield. But he was in a hurry to finish this Floor. The reward from completing the First Floor had been quite good, and he wanted to see what else he would get if he achieved a high score. That meant that he didn’t have hours, or even days, to wear down the shield. He would need to figure out another way through the shield.

Bawk, he sent through Party Chat to Phatagin.

I am not certain, the pangolin responded. You haven’t told us anything about your new Class.

Charlemagne, realizing that Phatagin had a point, checked the details of his Class again. Right, there was something that he could do. The rooster allowed his Ignition Core to fully awaken. His pulse quickened as liquid heat flowed through both his veins and mana channels, causing his internal body temperature to soar. His blood roared with power as he activated Molten Rage, sending his combat power surging. The last of his wounds healed in an instant, pulling energy from his fat reserves and stored cellular energy to restore his body to perfect condition.

He was ready.

In order to test what he was about to do, the rooster opened his mouth and breathed. Yellow and white flames spewed out. Most were blocked by the shield, but a few tongues managed to slip through before they dissipated. The rooster was satisfied that his plan would work.

He activated Fire Flash, focusing on a spot not far past the barrier.

For a moment, the world turned into a smear that looked and smelled somewhat like burnt bread. The rooster’s thoughts were equally blurred, only catching up a few moments after the effect ended. He looked around eagerly and found that he had done it. He was through. He crowed in victory, unleashing the energy that had been surging through his body in an uncontrolled burst of pyromantic prowess. A sheet of pure flame roiled through the air in all directions, only partially reflected by the mana shield that covered the camp.

The shouting and screaming, which Charlemagne had tuned out, became too loud to ignore. At least for a moment. Then silence reigned throughout the camp. The Squiggles arrived shortly after, signaling that the battle was already over.

The rooster, despite having just eaten, was interested in what sort of comestibles he could find in the camp. He might never have heard of Napoleon Bonaparte, but Charlemagne would have certainly agreed that “an army fights on its stomach”. He didn’t see anything promising from the air, so he swooped down lower and landed just in front of the charred tent that until recently housed the army’s commander. Upon landing and examining the situation, he realized his grave mistake.

He had burned all the food.

Not even the toughest warriors had enough flesh remaining to be worthwhile, and Charlemagne had neither the desire nor the teeth required to gnaw on bones. He was incredibly disappointed in the turn of events until he realized something. There was another camp. He immediately took to the skies.

Despite his detour, Charlemagne almost managed to beat the second camp’s cavalry back to their home base. As he approached, he saw the last stragglers passing through a gate that was quickly shut behind the returning soldiers. The camp proved to be almost the mirror image of the other, save for the green and orange flags flying here and there on top of the walls and larger tents.

Even the energy shield was the same.

To the rooster’s disappointment, no one confused him for a dragon as he streaked from the sky like a bullet. It didn’t really matter, but Charlemagne felt that the symmetry would have been ideal. He repeated his previous actions, slamming into the mana shield with all his might. He noticed this time, however, that the level up in Inferno’s Embrace had increased his body’s toughness, leading to fewer injuries this time around. It wasn’t a huge difference, but it was always nice to grow stronger. He used Fire Flash again, targeting a very similar spot as the first time. The same feeling of vertigo assailed him, and then he was whole again.

But his subsequent actions diverged from his previous encounter, as Charlemagne neither crowed nor released the heat surging within him in a single blow. He was determined to savor the experience, and get a nice meal for his troubles. So he only incinerated the outer portions of the camp, leaving the center largely untouched.

The crackling of flames and the screams of the burnt surrounded him as he descended toward the largest remaining tent, which he felt sure held the camp’s commander. He reinforced his legs with mana as he slammed into the packed dirt and took a look around him. Men and women in various states of dress ran all around, many without any armor or weapons at all. A few still held large wooden cups in their hands, their contents having been spilled in the initial panic. Still, the rooster could smell the dregs of whatever substance had been inside the mugs. It was foul.

The rustling of tent flaps pulled the rooster’s attention back to the tent. Two armored figures emerged, both wielding poleaxes. They froze for a moment as they saw Charlemagne, no doubt surprised. Then they charged as one, using the same Dash-type Skill to accelerate. The heads of their pole arms glowed with a fierce blue light, a mana-based enhancement that would make their weapons cut through defenses more easily. Before the pair entered into melee range, both weapons elongated to impossible lengths, then came crashing down with a speed that beggared belief. The weapons slammed into Charlemagne. At least, that’s what the soldiers believed, for the few moments they had left. Then their helmeted heads fell to the ground, followed by their bodies. The rooster, who had felt the surge of mana and used his wings to press forward at the last second, was both completely unharmed by the attack and within convenient melee range of his opponents.

“What’s all this shouting and noise, then!” a voice from within the command tent yelled out as a woman’s face poked out of the tent and frowned at the rooster before realizing what was going on. A smile lit up her face.

“Oh, another Knight!” she cried. “Have you come to fight with us against those annoying Mooks?”
