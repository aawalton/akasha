“I think that might have very well been the second-worst battle I’ve ever been a part of,” Rulfar admitted after Moxy finished healing his ruptured ears. “The demons over in Zygrul’s mountain base come in at a close third.”

“Second worst? You can’t just drop that tidbit and not expect us to ask about the worst one,” Moxy said.

“Maybe one day…” Rulfar said, staring off into the distance for a moment before shaking his head. “Asher, how goes the removal?”

“I really shouldn’t be talking right now!” Asher said, sweating as he ever so carefully used the tip of his void dagger to slice through the ends of the corpse’s fingers and remove the gem from its chest. It was like carving with a laser, and the knowledge that even the slightest twitch of his wrist could nick the gem and destroy all their hard work meant he didn’t want to let himself be distracted.

“Do you think it would be in bad taste to bring the corpse back and try to sell it at the Grand Auction?” Samantha asked, poking at the hand he’d cut off his wrist before shifting his attention to the gem. “I mean, whatever this thing is, it seems completely immune to all our weapons. Even mithral. If you hadn’t had the void dagger, we wouldn’t have been able to even scratch this thing.”

“That’s the wild realm for you,” Asher muttered, slowly slicing through the final finger still clutching the gem. “You never know… what you’re going… to get!”

With a laugh, he severed the last part of the corpse still connected to the gem, grinning as the gem fell forward and landed on the ground. The moment it did so, however, the entire corpse dissolved into that strange, crimson mist that had been flowing out of it during the battle, vanishing before their very eyes. Even the hand Samantha had been holding dispersed, leaving her blinking as she stared at her open palm.

“Huh,” Asher said, staring at the space the corpse had inhabited just a second ago. “Well, I guess that answers the Grand Auction question. No.”

“At least we still have the super evil-looking gem,” Moxy grinned, crouching down to better look at the gem as it continued to throb. The gem itself was almost jet black, and despite the fact that there were no changes in color or texture, Asher swore it looked like it had a heartbeat.

“So… how exactly are we going to transport this thing?” Rulfar asked, grabbing Moxy by the collar and yanking her back as she tried to poke at the freaky gem. “Asher would be the obvious choice, what with his blanket resistance to everything. But again, he’s our ride home. If he turns into some sort of replacement for that corpse and starts throwing up mist, the rest of us are dead.”

“I could hold it,” Kroult offered, his flaming eyes flickering as he stepped forward. “I am already dead, so I doubt it will have an effect on me. Not to mention my soul is not actually contained within my body. I am nearly immune to effects that target the soul, unless they are powerful enough to travel down the connection, such as Asher’s Sever the Thread skill.”

“I knew there was a good reason for bringing him with us!” Moxy said, punching Rulfar none too gently in the side in order to free herself. “So what now? We head back?”

“You didn’t get your fill fighting against an unending army of freaky mist ghosts?” Asher drawled, watching as Kroult scooped up the gem without concern. He half expected the lich to start screaming or spitting up red mist as well, but like Kroult suspected, he seemed completely fine.

“It was an interesting fight, I’ll give it that. But it didn’t last very long,” Moxy complained.

“I’m sure Christian will be more than happy to spar with you once we get back to the palace,” Samantha offered, spraying Rulfar in the side with Fountain and getting a grunt of thanks as she no doubt helped heal his bruised kidney.

With that, Asher triggered Spatial Lock, encompassing all of them within a protective barrier before focusing on his and Samantha’s room back in the palace. As they shot through the realms, he took a look at the notifications he’d received after the fight, and the act of using Recall to bring his entire team to and from the wild realm.

Distortion - Lvl 38

Secret Seeker - Lvl 30

Complete Resilience - Lvl 42

Permanence - Lvl 28

Recall - Lvl 28

As exciting as receiving five separate skill increases was at this point for him, Asher sighed as the one skill he was excited for above all others refused to tip over into the next level. Eternal Regeneration was still sitting pretty right at level 39, just out of reach of being evolved to the third tier. He’d been waiting for it to hit level 40 ever since his battle against the King of Death, and he could practically feel the skill teetering on the edge of increase. It just needed one more little push…

Before he could think about just what kind of push might do it, they arrived back at the royal palace.

“You know what? I think I will go check up on Christian. See if he’s off duty and down to spar,” Moxy said, stretching as she headed out of their room. “Let me know if I’m needed!”

“I’m going to rest up and do a little more research into the region The Lost Lady is supposed to be found in,” Rulfar said, yawning as he shook his head. “I didn’t remain in my battle form long enough to need to hibernate, but it’s still tiring. Like Moxy said, give me a call if you need me.”

Just like he had with Asher and Samantha, Donvath had gone ahead and given Moxy and Rulfar each their own sending stone so that his team could keep in touch with one another when needed. However, unlike with the two of them, the High Prince had distinctly not given either of them his personal rune. Asher thought that was probably a good idea, as he had no doubt Moxy would abuse having a direct line to the High Prince for something stupid.

“I suppose I am with the two of you. At least until we hand off this gem,” Kroult said, holding up the gem in question.

“Kroult… where are you staying in the palace?” Asher asked, realizing that would probably be a good thing to know. “Donvath didn’t exactly tell us anything beyond that your story checked out.”

“I have a room for myself, strangely enough,” Kroult admitted, his flaming eyes flickering softly. “Prior to… being bound, I had never really had my own room. And now I am staying within the royal palace of the Noala Kingdom. This has all been quite the change of pace.”

“You’ll get used to it,” Samantha said, offering him a small smile. Laying her bow against the table, she sighed as she collapsed into one of their comfy seats. “Asher, are you two good to figure out Smiles on your own? As much as I’d like to say hello to Crush and Draken again, I’m a little too tired to deal with Smiles right about now.”

“No need to explain, I completely get it,” Asher chuckled, leaning down to give her a quick kiss. “Rest up, we can get dinner once I’m back.”

With that, Asher pulled Kroult into his rift so as to not give Rosh a heart attack by teleporting a lich into his bedroom, before Recalling over to Whikoga. This time he managed to pop in behind Rosh and slip away without the man even noticing, and he arrived at Draken’s only a few minutes later.

“Asher,” Crush smiled, the large brute greeting him with a firm handshake at the door to Draken’s manor. The fact that he was willing to make such firm contact with Asher at all showed how trusting the large man was of him by now, and Asher couldn’t help but return the smile in kind. “It is good to see you again.”

“You too! How are things? Ever end up getting that third element?”

“I promised Thunder I would take one after she allowed us to work together to trap that ink-based assassin,” Crush grunted. “But no, not yet. Now that I’m on board with the idea, Draken’s giving me some time to think it over. Part of me still wants to hold out hope for that Griffin element, but a promise is a promise. You here to take that head off our hands?”

“The opposite actually,” Asher said, laughing at Crush’s pained look. “I promise he grows on you. Sort of. At the very least he’ll be a powerful ally!”

“I’m not sure if it’s worth it,” Crush muttered, turning and leading them into the manor regardless.

“Asher,” Draken sighed as Crush led him into the gang leader’s study. The man’s skin was even more pale than usual, and the bags under his eyes were no joke. “As much as I respect our working relationship, I can’t do this for much longer.”

“Hey, Asher!” Smiles called out, and Asher blinked as he realized a blanket had been draped over the head like one would a bird’s cage in the hopes of getting it to go to sleep. “I can’t actually see you, but Draken said your name! Or are we playing some sort of new game and pretending like people we know are here? That sounds much more fun than the quiet game!”

“The quiet game managed to last almost an hour before he figured it out,” Crush murmured. “What a nice hour it was.”

“I’ll admit I don’t have a solution to Smiles’ tendency to chatter,” Asher said, reaching into his rift and pulling Kroult out. “But I can at least make it so you don’t need to keep dumping your Death Aura into him every few hours.”

It was only after Crush shouted and his skin turned to metal, and Draken leapt back and knocked his chair over as his eyes went wide that Asher realized dragging a lich into existence in the center of Draken’s study without warning anyone might not have been the brightest idea.

“It’s alright, he’s good!” Asher hurried to reassure them, grateful at the very least that Draken couldn’t kill Kroult with but a thought. As deadly as his Death Aura was, he’d have better luck shouting the lich to death. “This is Kroult, and he’s on our side. Donvath already checked him out with his truthseekers, he’s okay.”

“What in the nine hells, Asher!” Draken shouted, his knuckles white as he gripped the edge of his desk and stared at Kroult. “You can’t just bring a lich in here like that! What if—”

There was a blast of electricity that blew the door open and caused Crush to stumble forward as Thunder appeared with her mace at the ready, already scanning the room. She didn’t even seem to notice Asher, the moment her gaze landed on the lich her eyes widened and she lunged forward, her mace crackling dangerously as she swung to remove his head from his body.

Asher quickly threw up a Spatial Lock just around himself and Kroult, sighing as Thunder smashed into the side of it and bounced back, pausing in her attack as she finally spotted him standing there as well.

“Kroult is not a threat!” Asher explained. “I’m sorry for not warning you guys, that was really stupid on my part. We’ve had a bit of a long day running around in the wild realm and I’m tired, but I wanted to get the Smiles situation resolved as quickly as possible. I’m not going to get into his life’s story, but Kroult here wasn’t even supposed to become a lich. It was an accident when he tried to stop his master, and he spent the last six hundred years bound by The Council of Death as they utilized his phylactery. Now… if I drop my barrier, can we all take a breath and prevent ourselves from trying to kill him? You know, kill him more than he already is?”

“Because he’s dead! Hah!” Smiles called out, causing everyone to pause and turn toward the covered head sitting in the corner of the room. “...Are we still fighting? I can’t see anything! If we are, could someone put a dagger in my mouth and throw me at an enemy? I promise I’ll do what I can!”
