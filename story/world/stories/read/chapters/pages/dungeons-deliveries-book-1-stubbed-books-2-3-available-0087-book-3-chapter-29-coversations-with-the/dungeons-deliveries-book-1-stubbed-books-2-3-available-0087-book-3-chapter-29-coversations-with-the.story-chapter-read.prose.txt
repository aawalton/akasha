Humans made things too complicated. That was a wonder, considering how they moved like oafs and how slow their brains were. It was much better to be a cat. Eat, hunt, nap, clean, play, eat, snooze, repeat. A routine of perfection. A routine he missed. His own life had grown far too complex as of late, but such was the price to pay to reach higher perches and expand his power. And he would. There wasn’t any doubt about that.

Except in his ascension, he had grown in ways that made things…

“—complicated. I messed up with Brody.” Alex’s words brought Emilio’s attention back. “It’s not his fault. None of this is his fault. He’s only been my friend, and I pushed him away.”

Emilio grunted, leaning into his chosen Companion harder to comfort him. Alex sighed, hugging back and scratching that delightful spot between his ear and skull.

The tears were finished. Now came the hard part. How did he comfort and then make Alex understand what he was truly capable of without words? Sitting with him on the edge of the tall perch overlooking his vast city had calmed the mewling. It calmed Emilio too. Alex swung his legs over the edge, snuggling in, which was a start.

“Meow.” Emilio said in frustration. It was an annoyance of his. Not that everyone couldn’t not make out the articulate inflection of his speech. He just wanted his chosen people to understand him.

Everyone else could bugger off. That’s what he told himself. But that wasn’t exactly true anymore, was it?

“Oh?” Alex looked over to him. “Yeah, I wish you could speak, Emil. Remember the cardboard boxes we slept on? Man. It’s gotten too crazy. Do you remember bringing me rats to eat? Or the tape incident?”

Alex laughed genuinely, which pleased Emilio, despite the shiver that ran down his spine. Tape. His one true weakness. A dastardly invention from the deepest puddle.

“I just don’t know how to help Brody.” He continued. “Aide the Other in his want? He wants to stay here. How the hell am I supposed to do that? [Illusory Copy] does what it does.”

A pang shuddered through Emilio. He knew what would work. Not that he knew how he knew. Perhaps it was his Demigod status. The new feeling conflicted with his innate right to sit above it all. Only, with his ascension, his already sky-high intellect had climbed even higher, which had introduced this new horrible sensation.

“I just feel so guilty about it.” Alex sighed. “And Snu’s Taking? We dance around that all the time. I try and bring it up and she gets…scared? I don’t know. Maybe I’m scared too. It’s like I ask but I’m not really asking. You know? Yeah. You know what I mean, big guy.”

Alex pulled him in tighter, which made the feeling hurt more in Emilio’s chest.

“Meow.” He offered with a fake inflection to appease his Companion.

That brought yet another new emotion. This one was most confusing of all. It made him want to put on clothes. Ridiculous. His form was perfect. Yet, he pondered. Another thing new and unfamiliar and annoyingly apt at sparking these new internal sensations he had no name for.

Emilio understood Alex was his Companion. That had been the case for quite a while. If he was honest with himself, he also understood he hadn’t purposefully brought about the connection or fully articulated the implications. It was quite some time ago, when his mind was less adept. He had chosen Alex, a young mewling who needed and deserved aide. It had felt right and it was useful. He could find his person at anytime. He could guide him in subtle ways. That was all he wanted. Why would he use the other parts of the bond?

The other parts of the bond with Alex may open the true depths of his heart. What a terrifying thought.

The pang ran through him again. How he hated these pesky sensations.

Alex pulled out the Tax Guild Stone to hold it up.

A hunger deep within Emilio rumbled, causing his stomach to growl. He wanted it. Not only this special stone the mysterious words that overlaid his vision told him about, but the one he sniffed north. Both would be enough to fulfill his belly. Perhaps enough to bring him to the next perch of power.

“You’re the one who gave me the heroic hawk core. And now I have these stupid things to fulfill. Who the hell can be trusted with this Tax Guild Stone?”

Thankfully, Alex pocketed the rock, causing the tremble in Emilio’s body to settle some.

“I feel like I’m holding back on you, Emil.” Alex continued, staring out over the bustling city below. “Supposed to help you secure your power. I don’t even know when I became your Companion. Whatever, it doesn’t matter. And I’m pretty sure you understand me. At least a little bit. I know you’d never hurt me.”

Alex moved to pick Emilio up by his armpits. If it had been anyone else, he would surely smite them. Perhaps rake their eyes before smiting them. Maybe feed them their eyes, burn them slowly, and get the gnomes to flick water at them for an hour for a good torture method before smiting them. But this was Alex. So, Emilio allowed himself to be picked up and placed on Alex’s lap.

“Can’t believe it, Emil.” Alex snorted while rubbing his ears. Absolutely delightful despite the pangs coursing through him. “I can’t believe Nina and Nino are family. The whole time. I have all these things to do and everyone to help and I’m just trying to make sure I’m okay too. They’ve helped me and used me, but I also used them. I don’t know. It’s complicated. I don’t even know what it’s like to have a family. Right and wrong get weird there. You’re supposed to tell them the truth. But did I? Not really. They didn’t, either. I just thought you were the only family I actually had.”

Alex sighed deeply, comforting himself by resting his hands in Emilio’s fur and watching the city.

Emilio felt about ready to burst. It was challenging to pretend to comfortably loaf. Alex’s laugh was plenty appropriate to sit on. There was just one massive itch he was finding impossible to ignore. Several massive itches he knew were unreachable by his profound girth and power alone.

“Hey.” Alex squeezed him slightly. “You okay, buddy? You got really still there and stopped breathing.”

Panic flitted through Emilio momentarily and he forced the breath. He’d been caught. Since he’d reached high enough levels across his skills and had a strong enough core, he hadn’t needed to breathe at all.

“Maybe you do need to go on a diet.” Alex laughed. “I don’t know what I’d do without you, Emil. Love you too much.”

The words reached Emilio’s ears, snuggled into his brain, worked toward his heart, and finally shattered his will to hold back.

Enough was enough. He would not bear it any longer. Alex was his Companion. He’d chosen him all those years ago, solidifying him as family, and most importantly, his human. Cats only select one. And if Emilio was special, then so was he.

He looked up directly into Alex’s eyes and found his Companion staring back with only trust and love.

“Meeeeeeeeeeow.” Emilio said in exasperation and leapt off his lap.

That was the problematic itch. He understood exactly what Alex was saying.

“Woah.” Alex said. “What’s up, man?”

Emilio paced back and forth on the roof while Alex continuously asked what was wrong. At first, he beat himself up. The way he had been acting toward his chosen human was unacceptable. He was better than this. It all came rushing through his heart and mind as he realized the error of his ways.

But he was still a cat. A cat never stays angry at themself. A true feline learns and quickly adapts. Forgiveness was the only path forward. He was the [Hidden Demigod of Felines], called to climb even higher. He would do his human and kind justice. If he was to evolve, then his human would come along too.

Emilio stopped and sat, facing a worried Alex. Perfect. The mewling was far away enough from the edge to ensure his safety for what was about to come.

“Did I do something?” Alex asked. “I swear I didn’t mean to touch your belly. What’s wro—”

“Mreow.” Emilio interrupted and allowed the full connection of the Companion bond to open.

A bottomless bed of comfort and self-assured might washed over Alex, causing his eyes to widen. Pride prickled Emilio when he saw Alex did not fall to his knees. He’d been worried about that. It was an apt metaphor for the type of connection it was. Unlike the putrid scent of a Familiar bond, a true Companion bond was established by one side, but by all measures, the two parties were meant to be equals.

A gush of essence flowed from Emilio’s bottomless core and filled Alex’s to the brim.

“WOAH!” Alex yelped, feeling the surge and stability of the source of power.

Emilio wanted Alex to hear his laughter of happiness. That required him to push his heart out through the bond. Before, it terrified him to do so, and now he couldn’t believe he’d been so daft.

He showed Alex the measure of his soul through the connection, brimming in delight after hiding for so long.

Emilio showed Alex his innate belief in himself. Then he showed him his desire for power. It wasn’t malevolent or full of malice. He could hardly help it. It was just that he was a feline, and they all believed themselves deserving of the most and the best.

Alex barked a laugh, Emilio feeling his joy, and pushed more.

He then showed Alex the choppy memories of his youth. How he’d been born in a wet basement, one of twelve children. The runt. The struggles he had to claim milk, then to leap on the metal box and out the window to learn to hunt.

How he’d struggled to catch food at first. But he’d adapted and become the best. Then he’d become the biggest of his small pack.

How he’d lost his mother and family too. It had happened slowly. Sickness, accidents, monsters they could not kill. Until he’d been alone.

How he hated being alone. Cats were social creatures by nature, despite what was commonly believed.

Then, one day, he saw a skinny boy crouching behind a dumpster waiting to leap on a passing rat. How funny the sight had been. How determined to survive and thrive the boy had been. That night, he sniffed the sleeping boy, shuddering in the cold, and determined he’d found his human. The spark he felt in his chest. How Emilio now understood it to be love.

Alex laughed, and Emilio pushed forward. His Companion did not need to relive the days on the street, with only him and a cat to rely on.

Emilio showed him the desire for power again. How the annoying messages pulled him forward to act. He’d get an impulse and do a thing and suddenly he was more powerful. It just kept working, and now he was here. How he did not understand the words, and they were gibberish. He could not read. But he did seem to understand where he was being taken.

Emilio showed Alex his new emotions.

How he believed he knew the answer to helping Brody. That it may interfere with Emilio’s own quest for rule. How defeating the Beast of Blightwood may be the key, and no, he didn’t know why, only that the annoying messages pulled him to grab the weird smelling heavy blonde person and bring him to Alex. That the Beast of Blightwood was actually a problem, and regardless of whatever Alex decided to do with the Tax Guild Stone, all of Toronto was in danger.

He let Alex in on how he felt responsible for the region. It was terrible to feel the weight of leadership.

He showed Alex how he was ashamed and afraid. Afraid of what he was becoming, and afraid that he had shame at all. Cats shouldn’t have shame.

“Why are you ashamed, buddy?” Alex asked, walking now towards Emilio. “You have no reason to be. These are just emotions.”

Alex knelt before Emilio to get to his level.

“You can tell me.” Alex said. “I’m with you Emilio. From the start, we’ve been there for each other. Show me.”

Emilio pushed away the fear with a mighty mental claw and looked up into Alex’s face.

Then, he finally let Alex in and allowed himself out.

I feel shame that I am thinking like a human. Emilio finally spoke to Alex’s mind and placed his paw on his hand.

And I have much to learn to be a good person.
