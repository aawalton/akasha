Risha sat up, furs falling from her, and clutched her head. The dreams were already beginning to fade, but her heart still pumped from the emotions that had filled her.

She closed her eyes, and took a slow breath.

Risha knew the pain of sleep.

Risha glanced to the sleeping spiderling on her pillow. Kliks slept deeper with the winter, even without hibernating. Thankfully, Risha hadn’t woken her.

Risha swung out of bed and made her way to the shutters of her room. They opened easily, revealing the sunlight of early morning. Those who preferred the night were going to sleep, while the creatures of the day were just waking. As more monsters joined her empire, Orctown turned into a city that never slept.

Risha enjoyed the fresh air as it blew into her room. A spirit of air slowly floated to the window, watching her, as the spirits tied to the physical world tended to do. Risha gave a small smile to the creature, and it gave her a wide smile in return before floating away.

Risha turned from the window, happy to let the cold autumn air into her room as she changed into comfortable clothing. A simple gray spider-silk cloak went over her shoulders, and she looked at Crimson and Scarlet, her chain-wrapped swords, for a long moment before leaving them and instead grabbing an older companion, the sword she’d taken from the overseer she’d first killed.

The sheath fit snug to her waist, and a small dagger was tied to her thigh a moment later.

Risha pushed her door open, and greeted the Kobold and Undergoblin who stood guard. They greeted her with small bows, only looking up again once she was past them and making her way to the other parts of the stone building that was her home.

Once she was out of sight, a familiar coyote-like kobold stepped out of the shadows, walking next to her.

“I thought you wanted to meet later, mother.” Mist-Prowler grinned.

Risha hopped up and down on her toes as the kobold shook her head at the Grand Mother’s antics. “Risha was excited. It’s been too long. And don’t call me that now.”

“Mhm. Well, you’re lucky I’m better prepared this time.” Mist Prowler pulled out a fox mask with very recognizable colors.

Risha looked at the kobold, who was trying to hold back her laughter, then slowly took the mask and put it over her eyes. Risha smiled wide, revealing her sharp teeth, “Do I look like her?”

”Not a bit. Come on.”

Risha drooped, then pulled up her hood to follow Mist-Prowler through her home to a back exit.

Two kobolds saluted, members of the Coyote’s pack who wouldn’t speak a word of what was happening. As they walked through the door, Mist-Prowler shifted, her fur disappearing and her skin turning purple. Two long batlike ears extended from the side of her head as her muzzle disappeared. She finished the look with a small piercing in her lip that held a small blue stone.

Risha looked at the High Goblin who walked beside her. “What do I call you now?”

Mist-Prowler smiled, seemingly enjoying her row of sharp teeth different from the two canines she was used to. “Just Misty.”

Risha tilted her head, “Unusual goblin name.”

Mist-Prowler laughed, “I’m an unusual goblin.”

“You are!” Risha couldn’t stop the smile that bloomed across her face as she walked into the city. The district they entered was swarming with warriors, and hunters, selling their hunts, buying equipment from shops and drinking heavily at the many taverns that catered to them. Despite the civilization that Risha’s cities created, much of the forest and mountains held very real danger to them, and more than one hunting party made a living off of selling the more magical bones and pelts of the dangerous forest.

An orc brushed past the two goblins, a large axe glowing ominously on her back. Next to her, a Brown Vulpine Kobold looked at them for a moment before following the orc.

Risha watched the two, happy to see the two creatures working together.

“What do I call you?”

Risha faced the kobold-turned-goblin and put her hands on her hips with a smile. “It’s time for the return of Arish!”

Misty choked, then laughed. “Misty and Arish! Where do you want to go first?”

Risha looked around, “I want to see the Shaman building that Elder Mo is constructing, but let’s just… look around first?”

Misty nodded, then motioned for Risha to lead the way.

Risha continued down the street, watching the various parties make their way through. Laughter and music spilled out of a nearby building, making her turn to the sound as a song was sung in chorus.

“I wonder what he’s doing here.”

Risha followed her gaze, and found an elf wearing a familiar cloak with his hood up. He walked through the street with a grace that spoke of experience in large cities, but also to his readiness with the sword that rested on his waist.

Risha hadn’t actually spent much time talking to the representatives, wanting to leave that to Sun-Chaser as much as possible. They’d been introduced at the council meeting and that was it.

Risha followed the elf, curious what he would look for in the city. He seemed to be enjoying the sights just as much as she was. Eventually he reached a small tavern and stepped inside.

Risha paused outside of the tavern, listening to the soft singing of a High Goblin woman. Misty stepped past her, and opened the door for the both of them.

It was a popular tavern, with a variety of creatures lounging about. In the corner, the Elf sat next to a human man Risha recognized from the human delegation.

Risha and Misty found seats close to the singing goblin, and Misty left to the bar to order them some food and drink.

Risha leaned back, recognizing the tune the goblin sang. It was all too similar to the songs her people would sing together, even before they could speak.

Risha joined in as she knew how, softly vocalizing the music of her people. Other High Goblins in the tavern did the same, creating a chorus that they all knew. Her eyes closed as she followed the familiar tune.

Misty sat down with food, and Risha reopened her eyes. Her companion looked around with awe at the music, but didn’t join, not actually knowing the old song.

The song ended, and the crowd erupted into cheers, howls and hollering. The Goblin Singer smiled wide, then stepped off the stage to take a break.

Excited conversation spilled the room, and Risha finally saw the food that had been set in front of her

It was some rather delicious looking fish, that had been seared, then topped with herbs. Risha happily chowed down, enjoying the food with relish.

Misty motioned towards the two men, and Risha saw them getting up to go somewhere together. For a second, Risha considered staying to listen to another song, but her curiosity got the better of her and she stood up to follow.

Two others wearing cloaks stood up at the same time, something about them catching Risha’s eye. The two parties stared at each other, then as one, they glanced at the disappearing elf and human.

Risha smiled, then sat back down. Misty glanced at her, then followed suit, letting the two other strangers leave first.

[Tribal Hunter]

Both of the strangers highlighted to her gaze as they left the building. They hovered outside the door for a moment before continuing after the elf.

”Did you know them?” Mist-Prowler voice was quiet, barely audible over the next song of the Goblin Singer.

Risha shook her head. “No. But we will soon.”

She stood up, then made her way to the door. Mist-Prowler followed close behind as Risha left the building.

The streets were crowded, but she could still smell the scent of the strange creatures that she tracked. They smelled strange. Something she didn’t recognize.

Risha stepped through the crowd, shifting into [Pack Alpha: Gray]. Her steps were sure as she picked up speed behind them. Clouds had covered the sky, turning the world a matching gray under the sun. Shadows were long, but the power of Nyx, Risha’s bonded spirit was still lessened by the day.

They turned and made their way through the city until the crowds started to thin.

The sound of steel on steel made Risha break into a run. She turned the corner to find the human on the ground, and the two strangers in cloaks clashing against the elf.

Vines rose from the ground, blocking a strike from one of the cloaked, only for the sword to glow with a strange sheen that sliced through the vines.

The elf glowed with vitality, his mana turning his eyes green as his sword deflected another strike from the second cloaked figure.

Strings of mana extended from the cloaked figure, slicing through the air to cut down the elf.

Risha stepped through the nearest shadow, appearing closer to the fight as she drew her sword.

Igni!

The spirit of Fire answered her call, and shot forward to engulf the the strings of mana before they could hit.

Her sword met the weapon of the first cloaked, that strange glow surrounding his sword and pushing against her spirit.

She switched to [Pack Alpha: Black], and threw his sword back before stabbing him through his chest.

She kicked him off of her sword, and ducked under a sharp string of magic aimed for her.

[Pack Hunter: Gray]

She slipped past another string of magic, then twisted around another.

The strings stopped, then dispersed as the second man fell to the ground. Mist-Prowler’s knife having pierced his heart.

Risha turned to the elf, who had fallen to the ground. A wound on his side was slowly healing, but [Mother’s Care] wrapped around him, helping the process. The human couldn’t be touched by her care anymore, already having left this world.

Risha trusted Mist-Prowler to take care of the elf as she walked to one of the dead cloaked figures and pulled back his hood. Silver orbs stared out of a human-looking face. Two goat horns curled around his head, almost like a demon, but different.

It took her a second to realize that she did recognize their scent, ever so slightly. It was the same scent as the tiny Grand Mother who had sat next to her at the council of Grand Mother’s.

”Fae.”

Risha looked to the elf, who was watching her with curious eyes.

”Fae Assassins of the Fall court to be exact.”

Risha looked at the dead creature again, wondering if she’d done something to anger the other Grand Mother. She’d liked the tiny creature when she’d seen her. “Why were they after you?”

“I… I don’t know.” The Elf pushed himself off the ground, the green mana that he wielded still held close. “Who are you?”

Risha paused, then nodded her head. “I’m Arish, High Goblin Swordsman”

He glanced towards Mist-Prowler.

”Misty.” Was her short answer. She had a frown on her face as she looked over the two dead creatures.

With a sigh, the elf let his mana go. “I apologize. I am Alalio. I meant no disrespect, but…” he looked at the dead human. “It is always angering to see a life cut so short.”

Risha stepped over the dead fairy to look at the dead human. She recognized him vaguely from the large delegation the humans had sent across the mountains.

One of Kulos’s spies would take care of the bodies. Risha was sure that Mist-Prowler had already informed him somehow, if the goblin wearing one of their shifting cloaks on top of the house nearby was any indication.

The Elf looked down at the dead. “Should we inform someone? It doesn’t feel right to leave him here.”

”Someone will come soon.” Mist-Prowler spoke up. “It’s better that you’re not here when they do.”

He sighed, then looked back up at the two goblins. “Would you join me for a drink? It is the least I can do as a thank you.”

Risha and Mist-Prowler shared a look.

Risha turned to the elf, then nodded excitedly. “Arish loves drink!”
