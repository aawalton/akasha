After conducting a few additional experiments to gauge the limits of his new spell, Percy revised how he classified his ethereal cords.

Beyond the default connection that he shared with his soul clones, he had the empowered and two-way cords that he created using Soul Resonance and Soul Harmony. By enriching his cords with a few droplets of dream mana per day, he could elevate them to Dream Resonance and Dream Harmony respectively – though Phoebe’s Decree had only bothered to register the most potent of the two variants.

Interestingly, while neither of the improved connections was quite as powerful as the one he shared with Micky, both were significantly stronger than the variants which didn’t use dream mana.

Based on his nauseating experience whenever he activated the spell, Percy believed that it should be possible to connect himself to his soul clones in the same way that he had linked himself to his mind clones.

The mindset necessary to accomplish that wouldn’t even be that different from Parallel Minds. The only thing he had to change was the material out of which he forged the new constellations inside his mindscape. Percy decided to fashion those structures out of dream mana, so that they would interface better with his altered cords.

Thankfully, he already had dozens of pink droplets stored inside his head, and could potentially generate an extra handful per day if necessary. He hoped that the mana infused into the mental structures would only incur a one-time cost, after which he could get by with just the three droplets per day required to maintain each connection.

As simple as the modifications were, Percy’s limited reserves ran dry a few hours into the project, forcing him to accumulate droplets for another week before he could finish.

Once he was done, a new constellation appeared inside his mindscape, floating some distance away from the small army of the same-sized humanoids fashioned out of orange stars. This one obviously shone with the faint pink light of his dream mana and was shaped like an alien lizard.

A fifteenth mental pocket formed inside Percy’s head, though it possessed far less processing power than his mind clones. As a soul clone, it only benefited from the passive component of Supercharged Cognition, but not the much greater boost that came from the mind mana that was actively fed into the mindset.

That wasn’t the only difference.

Along with the modest cognitive boost, Percy’s new mental pocket came with its own set of memories. They rapidly fused into his own like they did when a soul clone returned to Remior, except that this one hadn’t.

Percy found himself in control of an additional body. The muddy ground beneath his host’s webbed digits felt as real as the hard minerals under his human legs, and the film of warm moisture clinging to the lizard’s scales felt as comfortable as the cool – though somewhat stale – air of his cavern.

There was no notification this time, because the mindset was more or less identical to its previous version. Both the name “Parallel Minds” and its description fit its new functionality just fine, though Percy knew that this silent upgrade would fundamentally change the way he used his bloodline.

‘Hey… didn’t you create this clone before modifying Enhanced Cognition?’ Micky asked. ‘I’m pretty sure that the new mental pocket has access to the upgraded mindset, even though you registered it in its absence.’

It was true. This clone had spent the past few months spying on the natives of a lesser spring. He hadn’t found anything interesting, and Percy had been too busy with other matters to properly manage his adventures, but that was beside the point.

The fact that his clone had automatically received the benefits of Supercharged Cognition suggested that Dream Resonance could be used to share not only mindsets, but also spells, alchemic principles, and maybe even spectral traits with his counterparts. Things like blessings, cores, mutations, or bloodlines were unlikely to carry over since they were typically contained within a person’s flesh, but he was still excited about the doors that his new ability was about to open.

‘What about willpower?’ he wondered.

Controlling his host – it felt no different from sprouting a few extra limbs and organs on his real body – he walked away from the town he’d been spying on. Finding a pair of small hills standing next to one another, he hid between them and turned to face the one farther away from the sapients.

Lifting his host’s right foreleg, he tried to push his domain through the cord and into his borrowed limb as he swiped it at his target. He watched in horror and savage glee as three ink-coloured arcs shot out of his claws and carved just as many hundred-metre-long gashes into the hill, sending tremors up the lizard’s remaining legs and forever changing the local landscape with a deafening rumble.

It was all there… no losses whatsoever – a domain almost as powerful as a demigod’s, all within the tiny claws of a random Orange-cored critter in the middle of nowhere!

‘I suppose that the zelesian beads will be staying with our real bodies,’ Micky said with a chuckle.

Percy nodded absentmindedly, still not daring to believe this.

He’d known for over a decade that it was technically possible to transfer his domain through the cords, though willpower was a lot more volatile than mana, causing over ninety-nine percent to be lost as it changed hands. His connection to Micky had been the only one strong enough to let them share their domain freely, but the seamless connection he had established between himself and the soul clone appeared to work just as well.

‘Holy fuck…’ was all he could say.

Using their potent willpower, they should even be able to reinforce their hosts’ fragile bodies so that they could endure the transference of Micky’s spiritual blood more easily. They could deploy their entire army of sentries anywhere in the universe, giving even the weakest creatures the ability to scan entire countries in mere minutes, as well as enough power to hold their own against almost any mortal below the Clear grade!

The only caveat was that Percy and Micky wouldn’t be able to share the full power of their domain with more than a single clone at any given time. Even if they had enough dream mana to link themselves to the rest, their willpower was finite. Then again, they might also be able to borrow a domain if they possessed a powerful host!

‘What about the spatial seal? Don’t tell me we can retrieve that too?’ the Huehuan asked again, clearly as eager as Percy to exploit their new trick.

Percy pushed the clone’s seal through the cord, causing three bottom jaws to hang loose when the magical device reached his main body a second later. ‘If we can even share resources without recalling the clones, doesn’t that make them more or less disposable?’

Percy had never been a fan of sending his counterparts to their deaths, as they were essentially their own people. Things were different if they were fully synchronized. There was only a single person in control of them both, so discarding the clone would feel no different from losing a hand.

Regenerating his soul might take a little longer than his flesh, and Percy still wasn’t one hundred percent certain that he would recover the lost cord afterwards, since he’d never had a chance to test that. However, he was confident that he would, because he’d seen souls recover from much more serious injuries than his own severed wisps. The only thing keeping a cord from reappearing was his soul’s limited capacity, so the complete destruction of a clone should eventually free up the corresponding slot.

Pondering over the issue for a few minutes, Percy realized that there was another thing he could do to shield his main body from the consequences of his adventures.

Before trying anything dangerous, he activated Kerfyl’s Decree to place his clone under a simple contract. He swore that he wouldn’t take a single step forward and kept the penalty as light as possible. Next, he deliberately violated the oath to test whether the repercussions would still be limited to the soul clone. To his delight, only the fragment of his soul that resided within the lizard suffered some minor soul damage.

Performing a few additional tests, he confirmed that he could easily control whether a contract would apply to his main body, the clone, or both.

Armed with this knowledge, he decided to improve his Oath of Self-Destruction to make it even more effective. There was nothing he could do to protect himself from karmic tracking, but he could definitely do a better job protecting himself from just about everything else. It was no longer necessary to place his clones under complicated oaths and rely on their ability to enforce them under duress.

‘While I remain separated from my main body, I vow to be instantly and completely destroyed if he wills me to,’ he made his new mental pocket say.

He felt Kerfyl’s Decree tighten around the wisp like a coiled serpent as his Status spoke.

[Congratulations! Your spell has evolved: Oath of Self-Destruction – Crude -> Hybrid Art: Head of the Hydra – Refined!]

The only other issue that required some consideration was Ludwick’s Compass.

As things stood, Percy could only memorize a route on the way back to Remior. That wouldn’t be very convenient if he needed a clone to self-destruct without losing access to their location, or if he wished to send additional clones without recalling the first one.

It might be possible to tweak the mindset slightly, so that a mark got placed before leaving Remior, and a second mark was added to seal the route once he reached his destination. The information would ripple back to his main body immediately, and he could always discard the marks he didn’t wish to keep.

‘I’ll discuss this with Nephthys later,’ he decided.

Leaving the practical considerations aside, Percy’s mind was overflowing with possibilities.

Could he station a soul clone near the Spectral Broodmother, lend him the main body’s brainpower to master Advanced Soul Control several times faster, and instantly spread the spell out to his other clones without even retrieving the first one?

Could he grant Nephthys and the clone inside her body the Command trait without losing access to the Empowered domain the clone had produced during her most recent advancement?

In fact, could Percy borrow the inspiration granted to the clone from the Divine Spark mutation and combine it with his main body’s cognitive prowess and the external influence of an Elemental Source?

Percy’s lips curled up. The next couple of days promised to be quite exciting.
