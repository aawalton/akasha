New Quest unlocked: “Demonic Waves, Ritual Matters” — Endure the onslaught of the Gate-borne demonic hordes and protect the ritual from interference.

● Rewards: 3-8 exp in [Goddess], 6-24 exp distributed in sub-classes, based on utilization during the battle; over 75% reward grade: upgrade most relevant +0 or +1 ability used (thus, it cannot affect a +2 or higher ability).

The eldritch horde was full of varying twisted, dark demonic things of many sizes, some of them smaller and more plentiful, others significantly larger. Many ran on two legs, some on more, others moved like rampaging apes — their appearances were always crude semblances that weren’t quite this or that animal, but there were many almost-likenesses in the mix. Most weren’t fun to see… faces reminiscent of birds, seals, hippos, insects, crustaceans, sharks, on and on.

One of them even looked like Shia LaBeouf.

Best not mess with that one.

The more ‘mainstream’ demonic looks in the bunch, like the imps or the classic winged, severe-featured humanoid, were almost comforting, comparatively.

All had weaponized appendages of some sort, generally gaping, toothy maws and talons. A few wielded organic-looking weapons, such as bone spears or broken horns. Most were melee, but some shot spines, or vomited forth corrosive liquids and gases. Some gases didn’t come out of the mouth, either.

Don’t like that.

Samantha started off throwing radial bursts of light through [Light Manipulation] at the hordes, breaking up the lines, while somewhat guiltily focusing on eliminating the grosser of the enemies. This same strategy was repeated all down the line from her Angels and cleromages, creating a magnificent front of exploding energy and fireworks of great intensity.

It couldn’t kill everything, though. Some avoided the AoE in gaps, and some just tanked it and kept on charging, sizzling and steaming over their super-tough hide.

Front fighters in the vanguard were ready for them, though, enduring their onslaught with blocks and parries and counterattacking, putting down some and locking into engagement with others, needing support from their fellows to deal with the bigger and tougher foes.

A few broke through even the vanguard, sadly, fending them off or bowling them over with great strength, carrying on through and threatening the perimeter of the ritual stones.

Aerially, it wasn’t quite as bad, with only a handful of winged demons clearing past the AoE and vanguard, to face engagement from the likes of Azure, Michael, and the fearsome, golden dragon-form of Porthos. The latter contemptuously swatted a demon out of the air with his massive tail, sending it into the ground with a crash.

With a laugh, the dragon splayed his wings, blew steam from his nostrils, and boomed, “Do better, foul demons! I like a good fight.”

“Samantha,” Michael interjected in the gestalt, “Focus on the elites for this battle. We’ve got the AoE covered.”

“Roger!” Samantha replied, even as she adjusted and flew down to respond to the line breaches.

She interrupted a twelve-foot-tall, muscled beast, something like a centaur, with oily blue-black skin and ‘hair’ like a medusa, if the medusa’s snake-like tendrils were more worm-like, slimy, and dead. It had one dreadful, cyclopean eye and a gaping mouth full of too many sharp teeth. It had four top appendages that were nothing more than long, spiked tentacles.

It was moving at enough speed and force to possibly ram one of the ancient ritual stones of the outer circle. Samantha full-on hit it with the telekinetic effect of [Matter, As Spirit] to block and slow it down. It was a bit experimental, as she’d not had to use the power much in this fashion.

I’m not touching that thing, though.

It felt a bit like trying to hold on to a freight train, the power ‘shredded’ through, though the beastie certainly felt the hiccup and jerked, almost stumbling, and straining a bit in its charge, toothy maw contorting in annoyance.

Hmm. Not enough. Buuuut…

Samantha suddenly shifted all the force to one front leg, basically punching with it, just before the leg was planted in its stride.

The centauroid tripped and slammed into the ground immediately, then tumbled sideways in a cloud of dust and shredding earth, screeching with a sound like nails on a chalkboard. It came slightly short of hitting one of the big stones.

Ick!

Like lightning, Samantha followed up on the tumbling, disoriented creature, bringing her staff, Foolmaker, to bear, thankfully a divine item not at all inhibited by the arcane ban in effect.

She got in closer, but not too close, watching for an opening. With her sense of time, the chaotic tumble was seen as though frame-by-frame, and she darted the staff in for her target and struck.

It came down on the beast’s noggin completely unaware and clonked it with a nasty, resounding crack, as Samantha utilized the Knockout enhancement. Even if she wanted to do lethal damage, the artifact had its rules, and it was all converted to stunning or unconsciousness effects.

The beast stopped screeching immediately and flopped on the ass-end of its tumble, its long, spiked tongue lolling out and its cyclopean eye rolled back. The beast little more than twitched in the dirt, one tentacle touching the stone nearby.

It was unconscious, and likely for quite a while.

The bigger they are, the harder they fall, eh?

She spent no more time on it, scanning for further threats, though she noted the beastie to Jeeves to arrange for someone to finish it off when the chance arose.

Two minotaurs were locked in deadly combat with a twitchy, quick-moving hybrid beast, clearly a tough cookie. The primary form was of a brown-red, exoskeleton-coated insect with six legs, but with a humanoid form growing out of one side, topped by an armored head with glowing eyes and little more that was relatable to anything — it was just ugly and grotesque. It had two elongated arms, one ending in something club-like, the other a retracting spike that it was constantly whipping and ‘firing’ at the minotaurs, who had actually taken some wounds from it.

Scary, in other words.

With a bit of distance to cover, Samantha first fired a double shot of light lasers at its eyes. It was twitchy and fast, and moved its head away, getting only surface wounds on its armored head, which seared it and smoked but didn’t seem to do much.

This distracted the beast and interrupted its offensive, though, allowing the minotaurs to dart in and chop at its legs, not cutting or breaking but tripping the beast up and disorienting it. It tried to attack, swinging wildly and not connecting, still jerking its head around in wariness, likely seeking to protect its eyes from Samantha’s lasers.

With a little more time, she could probably predict its movements and score a hit, but efficiency was king in the current battle. Instead of trying to ‘out-precision’ a dodgy creature, Samantha took the opportunity the time allowed to get a little closer range, where she could bring the full power of [Conjure] to bear.

Accuracy could still fail, so Samantha synchronized the use with [Matter, As Spirit]. She grabbed the beast with the telekinetic hold as hard as she could, at which point she saw it immediately begin to buck and struggle against her with its prodigious strength — if not quite so strong as her last opponent. Regardless, it was heavily inhibited already, and she followed up with [Conjure], now likely with massive circumstantial boosts due to the hold.

Dozens of stone arms grew from up out of the ground to grapple the beast’s every limb, and arms wrapped around its insectoid body. It failed to avoid these, as it was already inhibited by the partial telekinetic hold. Samantha quickly switched again, handing off the stone hands to [Independence] to refocus on the hold.

With full control and a dominant pin on the creature, Samantha splayed the beast out, dropped it to the ground, and pinned its head down to the ground sideways, holding it as hard as she could, with the stone hands supporting her.

The minotaurs acted quickly, moving in and coordinating, one shoving the pick-like side of one weapon to its temple, the other gearing up for a sledgehammer blow to drive the pick in with perhaps one of the most devastating driving effects you could engineer.

The insectoid beast screeched to high heaven right up until the hammer came down with a thunderous impact and drove the pick into its noggin with a squishing sound. The coup de grace was so powerful that it even shattered the thing’s skull, and it was copiously spewing ichor out of the cracks, as well as from the mouth and ears.

The minotaur seemed a bit surprised by the effectiveness.

Samantha suddenly realized what had happened, though, and it caused her to have a little surprised gasp.

Breaker of the Unbreakable! This was an effort to break something, and I greatly contributed to it! Nice. Collaborative efforts count because it’s governed by the ‘spirit’ of it, even if I didn’t swing the hammer.

Scanning for the next thing to help with, Samantha saw another melee involving multiple fighters and demons. She rocketed over, sending a request to Michael and the gestalt as a whole, to bring the two free minotaurs over, as well — if that was tactically sound.

She moved it and did similar support tricks in layers, utilizing [Matter, As Spirit] and [Conjure] to grapple and pin demons, to make them easy pickings for others. She used [Conjure: Ranged Emission] in the zone to give her a second, free layer to hand off independently, this time making the ‘helping hands’ come from a squid-thing.

Can’t forget Sammyverse Rule #14: Always keep a reserve squid-thing!

A dance of demonic death quickly ensued, and the ‘cluster of trouble’ was cleared completely.

In truth, at least for a few moments, she’d actually had fun with it… probably because she hadn’t needed to touch anything icky.
