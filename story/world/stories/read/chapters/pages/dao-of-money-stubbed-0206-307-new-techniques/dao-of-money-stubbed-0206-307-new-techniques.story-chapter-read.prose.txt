A sect was not just elders, disciples, resources, and a few rules written on stone tablets. A large sect was almost like a small city. People fought, trained, ate, lived, and made messes every day. If no one handled those small things, they would eventually become big problems.Yanyue continued explaining more departments after that, and each one gave Chen Ren a clearer picture of what he might need in the future. Mortals could handle many of those jobs well enough, and from what he knew, most of them would probably be happy to work in a sect if it gave them safety, steady pay, and a better life.Aside from sect management, Princess Yanyue also had a great deal of insight into politics.She spoke about how things might change for Chen Ren if they ever left the pagoda. With the Guardian sects already interested in him, and with everything he had done inside this place, his name would likely spread far across the empire. That kind of attention would bring opportunities, but also danger.She warned him that people would approach him. If he wanted to survive that kind of attention, he would need backers of his own.It was overwhelming to think about, but it was the least of his worries at the moment. And if his businesses spread, he would become even richer.When he said that out loud, Yanyue looked at him with open surprise.Chen Ren did not understand why. He asked about sects, noble families, imperial customs, taxes, trade routes, regional powers, and anything else that might be useful later. With his spatial ring, it had worked well enough. He carried talismans, pills, treasures, and all kinds of strange things that could give him different effects when needed. If he lacked a technique for something, he usually tried to find an item that could do the same job.It was not a bad way to fight. In fact, it had saved his life more than once.But while learning the telekinesis technique, Chen Ren understood that he was adding something entirely different to his abilities. It was a new way to fight, one that could grow with him and make every weapon or object around him part of his attack.Funnily enough, Wang Jun was not screaming at him while he learned it.At least, not with this technique. That was because the severed head was trying to learn it himself.As it turned out, Wang Jun had no intention of sitting around forever and only reading books. A technique that allowed him to manipulate objects with his mind was one of the few powers he could actually use in his current state, and he had no intention of letting that opportunity pass.Chen Ren understood why.The old head had been powerless for a long time. For someone who had once reached the domain manifestation realm, being reduced to a talking head that needed to be carried around must have been unbearable, even if he tried to hide it behind insults and arrogance.So, every few hours, Chen Ren found himself gaining a fellow disciple.
