A big thank you to everyone for reading!

I've been working on a new story for a while, and I'm super excited to share it with all of you. It's called The Seeds of Amadrasil. This is a slightly different genre than Dungeon of Knowledge, but I think those of you who enjoyed it will find a lot to like. It is a modern portal fantasy set in a world similar to earth and being invaded by portals that bring mana and monsters.

I hope you'll give it a try!

-------

Kaldon Wolfe dreams of testing his nationals-level martial arts skills against portal monsters. Instead, he struggles to support himself and his sister, Mira. How can he even hope to afford the ruinous price of a Mana Seed?

On his next job, Kal lands in a dark, demon-infested world bathed in the awful silvery light of their exit portal peeling away from reality. When the monster hunters decide to leave Nymeri, a new harvester, behind, Kal rejects their decision, going back to find her, armed with nothing more than a harvesting dagger and a worthless relic—his father’s pistol.

After a brutal fight, Kal wakes impaled on the Hemorath’s corpse to find Nymeri bleeding out beside him and a Mana Seed no human should take hovering by his fingertips. Kal chooses to bind himself irrevocably to the blood demon’s Seed, sacrificing his dream for the power to save Nymeri. The Hemorath’s miraculous regeneration saves him, but it begins reforging him, one wound at a time.

Refusing to surrender, Kal must gather power, unearth ancient secrets, and train to grow his skills. Can he find the insight to conquer the demon’s legacy, reforging his Seed and making its power truly his own?

Drawn unexpectedly to the ancient ruins of a lost civilization, Kal has one burning question: why is the golden tree from his dreams carved upon their ruined marble walls?

Release Schedule: Monday, Thursday, Saturday.Accelerated chapter releases initially.

What to expect: Modern portal fantasy with LitRPG elements. Male MC. I use multiple points of view to tell stories. Romance plot elements are light and remain in the background (i.e. no harem).
