Daniel, not ready to cancel his momentum, advanced towards Gale with an arrow in his right hand. Manipulating its properties, he threw it with supernatural force and advanced to the left, dodging a whip from the cowboy.

Going further and further back, Gale easily dodged Daniel's attacks. He danced with his body and eyes, searching for an opening in the man with the arrows. Doesn't this guy ever get tired? He thought as he constantly twisted his body from the arrows coming from behind and from the sides. He jumped and delivered light counterattacks against Daniel, who seemed not to mind.

What Gale realized, however, was that the man wasted precious moments every time he summoned an arrow. An Emblem was like a muscle; if not trained, it atrophied. The advantage of having connected an Emblem to his own body, using the Quadrangular form, was not having to worry about that. If he had used it long enough, it would be like an instinct... He conjectured. This led him to think that Daniel hadn't been an Emblem user for very long, or if he was, he didn't use it very often. That's the problem with Emblems strictly focused on attack...

Precise as a viper, Gale seized upon these brief moments and struck directly at his left eye, causing the man to scream as he clutched his face. He followed with a barrage of attacks, each sharper than the last.

Having the Whip Emblem in its Quadrangular form, his entire body could now transform into a whip. A kinetic force coursing through his body was then released at the tip, a cracking sound echoing through the forest. He let his nails grow long and then attacked with the tips of his hands, making his strikes even more sharp.

Defending himself, Daniel continued to retreat, lacking the time and position to strategically summon his arrows. Not that he was a tactical man, but in a moment of combat, he knew that a misstep could change the course of the fight. For his arrows to inflict real damage, he needed to gain speed, therefore he couldn't simply summon one directly behind Gale; because of his Emblem, his skin was more resistant.

Since he couldn't summon arrows where he couldn't look, he decided, before the damage became more severe, to transport to a better position. He pulled himself up to a tree and then looked up, narrowing his eyes. With an idea in mind, he continued jumping from tree to tree while Gale tried to hit him from the center, not bothering or willing to follow him physically and fall into one of his traps.

The cowboy, suspicious of Daniel's actions, who looked like a monkey jumping from branch to branch, was on the lookout for arrows around him. Gale was now laughing at Daniel's desperate attempt to stop him.

Shit! Gale felt a sharp pain in one of his legs and then in his arm. Although they didn't pierce any vital organs, they certainly hampered his flexible movement. Almost out of range of the attacks, he then saw a bear leaping from one of the trees. Except it wasn't a bear, but Daniel. Although, well... the two aren't so different after all.
