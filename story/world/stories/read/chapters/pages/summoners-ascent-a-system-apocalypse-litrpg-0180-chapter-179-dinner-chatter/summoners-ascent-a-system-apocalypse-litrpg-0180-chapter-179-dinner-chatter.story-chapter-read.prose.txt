Ashton shovelled some fried rice into his mouth. It had been a while since he ate something this good. Genuinely, the only food he had really been eating recently was stuff that he threw together himself. And while he figured he was a decent cook, this was really on a whole other level.

Back in Lumia, Ashton didn't really have to eat most of the time, so that was just never a thing that he had to bother learning. He still did eat sometimes just for fun, and in those situations he had to make the food himself for obvious reasons, but it wasn't often enough to really make this an ingrained skill.

"Ash, slow down. Nobody's trying to steal the food from you," Sam pointed out with a slight laugh.

"Ha-Ha. What, you steal that line from your mum?"

"She did use to say that a lot, didn't she?" Sam asked, and Ash shrugged.

"I guess so," Ashton replied with a mouth full of food. "If you give me a little bit I can probably tell you how often. I do know that it was at least once or twice a week."

Sam narrowed his eyes. "Dude, are you actually trying to brag about your memory right now?"

"Hey, I had a mental breakdown about it the other day, I'm allowed to brag a little bit, alright?" Ashton swallowed the last bit of food. "You think I can get another portion? I don't have that much money right now, but I can p–"

"Just go, you'll earn your keep more than enough here anyway," Sam sighed, and Ashton happily skipped along to the large campfire where the food was being prepared in a massive cookpot.

Jacky, Oliver, and Paul had joined the two of them at their table earlier, though they didn't have the chance to talk a lot to Ashton yet. He had just started eating instantly and didn't pay them any mind at all.

"So, uh..." Jacky started, trying to find a way to start a conversation. "So he'd eat with you a lot?"

Sam nodded. "He lived with us for five years."

The three of them looked at each other and then Sam, confused. "Why that?" Oliver wondered. "Not a good home situation?"

Jacky hit her friend's side. "For once, can you have a little bit of tact?"

"What, it's a valid question. Plus it doesn't seem like Ashton really gives a shit about this stuff anyway," Oliver said bluntly, and Jacky sighed.

"Still, Oliver, just..."

"No, no, he's right," Sam interjected with a smile. "Ash never hid that kind of stuff. His parents are both dead, so when he was thirteen he lived in a children's home for a while and then my parents decided to foster him so then he moved in with us. He'd already spend almost all his time hanging out at my place anyway, so it just made sense."

The three of them were stunned into silence. This wasn't exactly the direction they thought this conversation was going.

"Uhm, is it really okay for you to tell us that?" Jacky asked, a slight stutter in her voice.

"Is it okay for you to tell them what?" Ashton came back with another tall heap of rice in his bowl, and sat down at the table.

"About your parents," Sam said as he scraped up the last bits of rice from his portion.

Ashton shrugged and took another bite, continuing to speak with a full mouth. "Oh, yeah, I don't care. What do you want to know?"

"I guess..." Oliver started, finding it too awkward to simply not talk about it now that Ashton offered, "... how did they die?"

"Old age."

"Hm?" Paul let out, confused. "What do you mean?"

"Well, my mom died when I was six of lung cancer, she was in her late 60's, and my dad died of a heart-attack in his late 70's when I was thirteen," Ashton explained, holding on for a couple moments before continuing. "I tell you, that was in the top 3 of bad birthdays for me."

Sam coughed out a laugh as he was mid-drink, turning into a coughing-fit. Once he calmed down slightly, he stared at his friend. "You dick, I told you to stop saying that."

"What do you want me to do? It was my birthday."

"Yeah but you've made so many fucked up jokes about it over the years that I can't not laugh anymore and I look like an asshole," Sam groaned. "And don't act like you didn't specifically wait for me to drink something before you said that."

"Touchy."

"It's touché, you ass."

"Huh? You want to touch my ass?" Ashton grinned. He was specifically keeping his channelling range small so that he could see Sam's expression twist into that annoyed grimace. Sam stood up and grabbed his bowl.

"Dickhead."

"Love you, too~!" As Sam walked out of his channelling range, Ashton waved at him and smiled broadly, before turning back to the other three, who were just listening without knowing what to say or where to interject.

"Uh... Wait, so... your parents were in their sixties when they had you?" Oliver asked, breaking the brief silence, before a slight smack sounded out as Jacky hit him with her elbow. "What? I just wanted to make sure I got it right!"

"I know, but the way you asked is annoying," Jacky said with a grimace.

Ashton smiled lightly, continuing to eat. "It's all good. Yeah, they were pretty old. It's not like, impossible, but it's super rare without, like, fertility treatments as far as I know. My parents knew they probably wouldn't be there to see me through big parts of my life, so they showered me with as much love as they can. It sucks, but..." his smile became even wider. "Even with my memory, I can't remember a moment that made me doubt how much they loved me. So I'm alright."

The three seemed relieved to hear that Ashton had such a positive attitude about such a terrible situation. They could see the melancholy in his face as he said it, but the joy in his voice overshadowed that many times over. Though, then Ashton opened his mouth again. "My siblings on the other hand made my life a living hell after my parents died, so that sucked. My brother stole my portion of the inheritance and my sister took my cat away."

"Huh?"

Ashton ignored Oliver's surprise and finished the food. Frankly, he was still pretty hungry, but he figured he shouldn't overdo it. "Anyway, with that, I'll be heading out now."

"Wait, what? You're just going to leave after dropping that on us?" Oliver protested, but Ashton just shrugged.

"Yep." He was about to get up and bring away his bowl, but he could just feel the stares of those three. Clearly, they still had something they wanted to ask about. With a sigh, he turned back toward them. "What?"

"It's just..." surprisingly, it was Jacky that started speaking. "Isn't it kind of weird?"

"What? That my siblings are assholes? I guess so."

"No, not that part!" Jacky shook her head quickly. "But come on, you don't see a connection at all? You were born under unusual circumstances, you have memory that would put a library to shame, and you can use magic. I don't know how you learned it, and you don't have to tell us, but come on... That's not all a coincidence, right?"

Ashton was about to laugh at what Jacky said, but he stopped himself. "Actually... you might have a point," Ashton replied. "I mean, apparently I grew up with the favour of a nymph that lived in my backyard, and that was when this world wasn't even supposed to have any magic. It does all feel like a pretty big coincidence, I guess."

It was a thought that had occurred to Ashton a couple of times. Not in any particularly deep way, but the question 'Why me?' had echoed through his mind for almost the entire time that he was in Lumia.

Why, of the eight billion humans in this world, why was it Ashton? He wasn't particularly clever at the time, and he had suppressed his memories greatly. He didn't take care of himself, he was antisocial, and had absolutely abysmal talent for fighting. The only thing he had going for him was the potential of becoming skilled with magical matters.

And sure, Ashton was at the level of a prodigy all things considered, but it wasn't to the point where nobody else could surpass his magical potential. But since he thought that earth was completely non-magical, he never considered the possibility that there was more here that he just didn't know about.

Then again... "Eh, doesn't really matter right now. Got more important shit to worry about."

Oliver sighed loudly. "Can't you just take a little break? We get that you've been asleep for three days, but come on, half an hour longer doesn't hurt, right? There's some more stuff that Sam probably didn't tell you yet that happened in town."

Ashton smiled. "Fine. You got one shot, if you tell me something interesting I'll stick around a little bit."

Oliver quickly looked over at Jacky and Paul, huddling slightly over their food so that they could whisper to each other, discussing what to tell Ashton. In the end, they pulled back again, and Jacky smiled smugly.

"Well, you know, a couple days ago, a buildings in the northern part of town just crumbled down randomly. There was nobody nearby, so we didn't know what caused it, but we scouted the area a bit and we have a good guess..." Jacky crossed her arms. "We think that–"

"Oh, that was me, sorry."

"Huh?"

"Yeah, sorry. Well, technically it was a shared effort with the crimson wyvern that made that place its nest. I was astral projecting and found out that the wyvern was able to see me somehow, and it started chasing me. So to get away from it, I made it break down the building." Ashton smirked lightly. He could tell that these three were so proud that they could tell Ashton something that might make him curious, but he just showed them that they really knew nothing at all about it until now. It must be a little frustrating, but Ashton didn't have time to worry about their feelings. He had to go, and quickly stood up. "Oh, and by the way, that building is where I used to live until all this started, if you want to add that to the list of coincidences. I'm off."
