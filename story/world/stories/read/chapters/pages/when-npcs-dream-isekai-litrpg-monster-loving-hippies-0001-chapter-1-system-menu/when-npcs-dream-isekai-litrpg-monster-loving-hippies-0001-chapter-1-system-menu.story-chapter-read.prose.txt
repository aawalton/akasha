“Menu! Open Menu! Status! System Menu!” I shouted.

Nothing.

And yes, I was absolutely expecting something, because I’m pretty sure I just got isekai’d.

Should I be panicking? Oh, definitely.I had just gotten sick of losing in Fortnite, turned off my computer, intended to go to bed, and poof—now I’m here. Outside. On some freaking hill with tall grass. I’m grabbing at the grass hysterically like I think I’ll fall into the sky. What the actual fuck is happening?

Okay. Shut up. Shut up. Stop thinking. Deep breaths.

Am I calm?

Sort of. Kind of calm. That’ll have to do.

Now. Just think for a moment.

Getting isekai’d is not actually the most logical explanation for suddenly finding myself in the middle of nowhere.For starters, the fact that I remember being in my room, and now I’m here, doesn’t necessarily mean I was magically teleported. It could just mean I don’t remember the in-between part.

Option 1: I went to some insanely awesome drug-fueled party, had the time of my life, and lost my memory because I got too high.

Is that likely?

Well, no. I’m a nerd. I never get invited to parties, especially not cool ones. The closest thing to a party I’ve been to is a D&D sleepover at Joe’s place, and his parents don’t even let us have beer.

So… Option 1 is not likely.

Option 2: This is all a dream.

I pinch myself (is that supposed to work?). I look at my hands, trying to see miniature details…

Yeah… it doesn’t feel like a dream. Could be an illusion. Maybe I’m in a coma? Or maybe I’ve gone crazy and I’m hallucinating?

Option 2 is possible, but it has no action item. Meaning, if it’s true, there’s no real meaning to anything I decide to do. Only if I assume it’s a dream when it’s not could I make bad choices. Better to assume this is real.

Option 3: I got in an accident, probably a car crash, hit my head, lost my short-term memory and I’ve just wandered off, maybe even escaped from a hospital.

It’s like that movie 50 First Dates, where the girl can’t form new memories since the day of the accident. she wakes up every day having forgotten everything from the day before. And it's somehow supposed to be cute that Adam Sandler keeps trying to hit on a girl with brain damage.

Maybe I’ve been doing this for years. Maybe I’m old.

I look at my hands… They look normal.

Wait—I got scratched up pretty badly yesterday fighting my cat. If the scratches are still there, that means the memory loss, if any, happened after that. I check my arm… yep, the scratches are still there.

So if I have brain damage, it must have happened recently. But I don’t feel injured, and my head doesn’t hurt. Could be something internal… But my body seems whole enough.

Option 3 is possible, but doesn’t seem very likely either.

Option 4: I got isekai’d.

My dream come true! I’m going to become a mega OP famous hero with tons of cute elf girlfriends!

This is by far the best option for me, but also obviously very unlikely for a ton of reasons.

Summoning a game interface would be the simplest way to prove it.

But…“Open Menu! Show Stats! Inventory! Map! System!”Nothing. That doesn’t seem to work.

More options? Umm… I don’t really have any other ideas for now. I guess I could walk and hope to find some clues. If I see dragons and elves, that’s a clue. If I see hospitals and nurses chasing me with a straitjacket, also a clue.

Also… it’s getting dark. And cold.

I keep walking to the top of the hill, and sure enough, I see a small town not far away. It’s hard to make out in the fading light, but as I get closer, I start to see the signs: no electrical lights, no cars, houses made of—yes! Fricking wattle and daub.

Hell yeah, Option 4 it is!

Okay, sure, it could be some historically accurate, preserved medieval park or something. But come on, that’s dumb.

Option 2, the “I’m losing my mind” theory, is still on the table. But again, there’s nothing I can really do about that. So I’m going to assume I got isekai’d and I’m about to have the time of my life.

Goodbye, stupid, boring, depressing old life.

It’s just that… well… I’m cold and tired. And to be honest, also scared and want my bed.
