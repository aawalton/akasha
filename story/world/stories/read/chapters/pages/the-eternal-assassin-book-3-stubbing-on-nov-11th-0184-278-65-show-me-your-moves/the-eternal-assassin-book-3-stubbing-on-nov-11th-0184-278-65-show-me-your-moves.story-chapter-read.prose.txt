The three mystery assailants had set the mood with their strange pyramid, and Asher was more than fine with that. He’d been so preoccupied with all the different things keeping him up that a fight to the death free from any pointless banter was exactly what the doctor ordered. The moment the walls snapped into place, Asher took the initiative, turning toward the smallest of the three assailants and hurling one of his daggers in their direction. Each one stood roughly forty feet away from him, which meant after compressing a twenty foot tunnel of space for his dagger to fly through in an instant, the dark-clad attacker only had a second to react to Asher’s attack.

Unfortunately, these guys seemed to be just as good as their preparations suggested.

The attacker’s hand turned metallic, allowing them to bat the dagger away easily enough even as they began sprinting forward along the rooftop toward Asher’s position. As that one ran, the larger one pressed their fingers to their temple, and the air before them shimmered as a near-invisible wave of some sort shot toward Asher.

Assuming that attacker had a Mind element and wanting nothing to do with whatever that attack would do to him, Asher made a quick decision. Attempting to slip into the astral, Asher frowned as he realized something about the barrier his assailants had set up was making his skill far more difficult to activate than normal. While he still managed to dodge the mental blast, he was forced to return to the material realm after only a few seconds, already breathing hard from the effort triggering Astral Dip had cost him.

Before he could worry too much about that, however, the third assailant made their move, dropping into the shadows at their own feet and leaping out of Asher’s own, their spear aimed straight for his chest. Thankfully, Asher had more than a little experience fighting against people who could move in similar ways, and he’d already been moving to block their strike the moment he saw them falling into their own shadow. Rather than run him through, the assailant’s eyes widened as their shadow spear was deflected, and Asher even managed to land a small slash on their arm before they leapt back.

By that point, the attacker with the metal element had leapt between rooftops and reached him, and they threw a powerful haymaker straight for his head. From the sheer force of wind that came off their fist when Asher dodged, he felt pretty safe in assuming this assailant had a Strength element to go along with whatever metal one they carried in their soul. If even a single one of their punches landed, he’d be dead in an instant.

Asher tried to take advantage of the opening the metal one had left, but he was forced to focus on defense instead and slip into the astral as another mental blast shot straight through where his head had been moments before. The moment he returned, he twisted out of the way of another shadow spear thrust, letting the weapon scrape along his daggers rather than cut through his side. Yet when the shadows touched his daggers, electricity coursed through the metal blades, flowing up and into his body and causing his muscles to seize up. Before Asher could react, the metal one tried to smash in his head again, silently throwing a deadly punch directly at his face.

Gritting his teeth, Asher focused on Distortion, expanding the space between the attacker’s fist and his head and causing the blow to land short. While the metal attacker stumbled and tried to regain their balance, Asher shook off the electricity, lashing out with a foot and forcing the spear holder to back off. At the same time, a third mental blast shot straight at him, and Asher decided enough was enough. Their teamwork of letting metal hands get in close, spear holder to keep some distance, and Mind guy to attack from afar was pretty clever.

But it wouldn’t work against him.

Focusing on his one and only tier-3 skill, Asher mentally switched his Complete Resilience's specified energy immunity to Mind, chuckling as the once-terrifying mental blast struck him in the face and dissipated without doing a damn thing. The bulky assailant’s eyes went wide, and Asher grinned as he turned to focus on the other two attackers.

Complete Resilience’s third-tier effect was incredibly powerful, but it wasn’t quite as ridiculous as he’d first assumed. For one, Asher couldn’t keep switching out what energy he was immune to on the fly. It seemed he needed at least a couple of minutes in between choices for whatever reason, which meant he had to be careful which choice he went with for any one fight. On top of which, he couldn’t just make himself immune to the Shadow element and then shrug off a shadow spear aimed for his throat. The immunity seemed to only care about energy-type skills. He could shrug off a devastating Death Aura like it was nothing, but Samantha’s Fountain skill and Moxy’s Air Blasts would damage him just like always, even if he tried making himself immune to Water or Air. His leading theory was that it had something to do with how ‘solid’ an attack was, but he’d only had the evolved ability for a few hours now and still had plenty of testing he needed to do.

Without their support from the rear, the two assailants up in his face quickly fell onto the back foot as Asher went on the offensive. Even without being able to rely on Astral Dip as heavily as he normally would, Asher knew from his teammates that he was a terrifying fighter to go up against from Distortion alone. The assailant with an electrified shadow spear would try and stab him through his own shadow, only to have their spear blocked by his living wood dagger, keeping the electricity at bay as Asher lashed out with his other hand to stab at them with a dagger from six feet away through compressed space. The powerful attacker who fought with their fists would try to land a punch, only to again realize the few feet of distance between their fist and Asher’s chest was suddenly twice that of what it seemed, causing them to stumble and barely manage to block his retaliation slash with an elongated dagger.

Distortion - Lvl 37

Throughout all of this, the bulky attacker continued trying their best to hit Asher with mental blasts, but each and every one dissipated harmlessly the moment it reached him. They may as well have been throwing wadded up pieces of paper at him for all that Asher felt the attacks, and he continued to ignore them as he focused on the other two.

Eventually, after almost a solid minute of the three of them desperately trying to kill one another, one of them slipped up. All this time, the attacker with metal hands had been careful about deflecting Asher’s daggers by striking the flat parts of the blade and redirecting their momentum. But fighting along a literal razor’s edge was no easy feat, and they made their first mistake of the night, failing to slap at the flat of the dagger and instead hitting the edge of Crescent’s blade.

Causing the masterwork mithral blade to slice straight through one of the attacker’s fingers and halfway into another.

Whatever metal element the attacker had was impressive indeed, as Asher had expected Crescent to cleave straight through their entire hand. Even so, cutting off a finger and a half was exactly the opening Asher needed, and his grin widened as the attacker hopped back for a second, grabbing some black cloth from their side and wrapping it around the stump on their hand as blood spurted everywhere. They probably assumed they could stop the bleeding and get right back into the thick of things.

They would be wrong.

Knowing that Sever the Thread would ensure that at least that one bled to death before long, Asher turned his focus to the Shadow user, causing them to dart back a few feet as he lashed out with his living wood dagger. The two of them traded blow after blow, the attacker still trying to stab him from impossible angles as their spear leapt out of all the shadows surrounding him and Asher compressing space between him and them to try and stab them from too far away.

Realizing they were quickly getting pushed back to the wall of their own barrier, the assailant tried something new. Raising their spear, they then hurled the shadow weapon at Asher’s feet rather than his body. As their spear struck his own shadow, Asher froze, startled to discover his body felt like it was trapped within a block of ice that wouldn’t budge. The assailant wasted no time, darting forward as they formed a smaller blade from shadows and struck straight for his neck.

Triggering Complete Resiliance’s second-tier effect, Asher purged the paralysis skill completely, freeing himself at the last second as he met the attacker’s strike with one of his own. As their head fell to the rooftop, he turned to find the metal one still struggling to stop the blood pouring freely from their severed fingers.

Ironically, from his time as a mortician’s apprentice and his first-aid studies, Asher knew that most people wouldn’t bleed out from losing a finger due to the speed of which blood clotted. However, Sever the Thread didn’t care about a body’s natural healing. His attacks were eternal, which meant no amount of healing, natural or otherwise, would remove them. If his attacker knew about his skills they could potentially lop off their entire hand and then try to stop that wound from bleeding, but whoever had come to kill him clearly didn’t know about Sever the Thread.

Ignoring the dead attacker who simply didn’t know it yet, Asher turned to the last of the three assailants. The largest of the three who was here presumably for ranged support had terror in their eyes and their back pressed against the barrier. Asher could only assume the barrier was on some sort of automated timer to prevent whoever was trapped inside from destroying it and escaping, but that meant the attackers were trapped inside here with him as much as he was. With a grin, Asher used Distortion to hop over to the larger one’s rooftop, pointing a bloody dagger at them as he approached.

“You know, this really was just what I needed to clear my head. I don’t know who sent you or why they want to kill me, but honestly, I should thank them for the surprise gift. Do you have any idea how long it’s been since I had a fight where I didn’t even end up getting stabbed? I suppose it’s not very fair of me to say this as I assume you’re not used to your mental attacks being completely pointless, but the three of you sort of failed spectacularly.”

As if proving his point, a soft thud from behind him signaled the metal attacker finally losing consciousness from blood loss, and Asher shrugged.

“See what I mean? For once, I don’t even have a scratch on me. And trust me, I’m just as surprised as you are. Now, with all that said…”

Asher reached through compressed space, causing the trembling attacker to freeze as he pressed the edge of Crescent against their throat from nearly ten feet away. The pale moonlight shone off the brilliant blue blade, reflecting off the attacker’s terrified eyes.

“...Who sent you to try and kill me?” Asher asked, losing his smile as he stared hard at the last of his assailants. While the fight had been just what he’d personally needed, he wasn’t dumb enough to miss how much danger he’d been in. With the barrier in place, Recall and Personal Rift had been unavailable to him, and Astral Dip had barely worked. If whoever had ordered this hit had sent more people after him, or even just a different series of elements that worked better together, Asher very well might have been in trouble.

The bulky assailant remained frozen for a few more seconds, before letting out a sigh of defeat. Their entire body seemed to shrink down slightly as if they’d accepted their defeat and likely death, and Asher waited patiently for an answer.

What he got instead was the sound of a crunch, and for the last of his attackers to start foaming at the mouth as their eyes rolled up into their head and they dropped to the ground lifelessly.

“What the hell…” Asher muttered, staring in shock at the bulky assailant as they twitched and spasmed on the ground before their body went still. “Some sort of poison? Are you serious? Damn it, I’m going to have to get in touch with Bordan and Clare and have her use her freaky corpse-memory skill, aren’t—”

But before he could finish that thought, he discovered the assailants had one more surprise in store for him, even after death.

In the form of a powerful explosion contained entirely within the glowing pyramid barrier he was still trapped within.
