On the further-away part of the veil, concepts were raw and chaotic and undefined. Only when they were given a body and were truly removed from the greater consciousness of the veil did their own personal minds take a form. Sometimes a being like this was just born out of coincidence, usually in old objects or sometimes even places. Construct summoning took that phenomenon and controlled it, creating a tunnel for a specific aspect of the veil's conscious to be placed into its new body.

That meant that they weren't picky, since it was like the consciousness was tailor-made for the object it was now inhabiting.
