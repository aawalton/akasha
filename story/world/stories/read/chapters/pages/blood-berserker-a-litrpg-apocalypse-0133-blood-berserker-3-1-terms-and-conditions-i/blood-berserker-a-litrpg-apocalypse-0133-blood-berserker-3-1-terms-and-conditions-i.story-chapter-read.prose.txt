He’d handled so many threats that this invasion of a thing felt more like an inconvenience than anything else. In fact, Nathan believed that the apocalypse was nothing more than his playground at this point.

There really isn't any rest for the wicked. Nathan sighed, the annoyance that had filled him before the influx of system messages now gone as he now faced the squad with a calm mind.

“Are you guys okay?” Nathan asked. The trio of Ciara, Ruben, and Tasha looked totally spent and battered. In fact, Tasha and Ciara looked like they were ready to drop dead at the slightest touch. Ruben, for the most part, seemed to be in better shape than the girls, but he obviously suffered the most, judging by the bruises, cuts, and blood that covered the chatty teenager.

“Uh, yeah. We tried our best to fight, but we couldn't win… against them.” Ruben said, his eyes downcast. “Maybe if—”

“Listen. I am not meant to be the only strong one on the island because, like it or not, things like this are going to keep happening, and you guys have to learn to deal with it by yourselves.”

“Maybe it's unfair of me to expect much from you guys, but this is a fucking apocalypse, and while you all have been helping to mine Tora for blood rock, which I am grateful for. I am also disappointed that none of you have managed to break past the level thirty, if not the forty mark.”

“We—” Ciara started, but stopped once Nathan flashed her a look.

The annoyance that had subsided had begun to rise again, and it took everything out of Nathan not to snap at them.

His frustration at the trio went beyond the fact that they’d been unable to stop the ogres. It was the fact that the trio didn’t even stand a chance if they’d tried. The situation was alarming for the berserker. The trio were weak, and aside from that, he feared more for their lives than the fact that he could've lost everything he earned since the apocalypse had begun.

“Even if you could not have handled their leader, at least the rest of them should have been dead. And yes, I understand that keeping you here to work in the mine as well as your own advancement simultaneously is a lot to ask, but I believed that you guys were up to it; turns out I was wrong.” Nathan paused and made eye contact with all three. “And it’s time I take responsibility for roping you guys into staying back without telling you what is to be expected of you.”

“Each of you needs to step up your game because this is below what I expected from you all. “Even Marshmallow,” Nathan said, his eyes pointedly going to the fluffy bunny covered in blood, “has advanced much further than you guys. Ruben is the strongest here, and he's just Level 23.”

The berserker cleared his throat and tightened his grip on his swords.

“I'll be clear, this is the last time I'm coming to save you guys, and if you guys can’t handle the threats to my mine and my stronghold on your own, I suggest you leave. All of you.”

Nathan caught the way the girls squeezed their eyes shut and the way that Ruben sucked in a deep breath, but he pushed on because they needed to hear this. “We have a beamer now, so I can send you to the mainland and release you from your fealty to me. I think maybe that way you guys will understand the reality of what the apocalypse is.”

“I need you all to decide now. Are you leaving? Or are you ready to do what is necessary to stay here?”

To Nathan, this conversation needed to happen.

It was even more important to him than the system messages about the demon king coming to earth, the invasion that was going to happen, and, most of all, that this conversation happened so they could figure out where they all stood. Because if they didn't, problems would likely pop up in the future

Ruben was the first to look up at him, and for the first time since Nathan had met the teen, there was a defiant look in his eyes. “I don't know about the others, but I'm staying. And I'm getting better.”

“When I left the tutorial, I was one of the weakest in the squad. But because of the opportunity you have given me here, to grow at Blood Rock, on this island of yours, by protecting us as well as helping us acclimatize to the apocalypse,I've grown much stronger. And I know I can do better.”

“So I will do better. No excuses.” Ruben locked eyes with Nathan. “I won't let you down.”

Ciara seemed to take her time, her left hand absentmindedly going to the right side of her head, matted with blood. “I agree with Ruben.”

They all stayed quiet, with determined looks in their eyes. Nathan could almost smell the shame radiating off them, but the determination was stronger, and that was enough for him… for now.

He had made it clear that if any of them were to die from here on out, it would not be his fault; their lives were in their hands.

Nathan himself was just a person. He had his own goals and things to do, just like each of them. He couldn't be everywhere at the same time—he couldn't be everyone's shield.

And more importantly, running Blood Rock and the mine at the same time, as well as furthering his own advancement, was going to take a lot out of him. He needed to know whether the trio who remained from the initial squad were committed to him and Blood Rock, so he could properly plan how to handle the invasion.

But now he knew that he could put his trust in them.

”Alright, that's enough, it’s time we returned to Blood Rock.” Nathan said. “There's no healing here, neither is there any dungeon, and I don't have healing potions on me, so you guys are going to have to manage till we get to Blood Rock, where you can rest for a bit.”
