Flynn almost expected to see Nyx and Throm battling the Orc Bulwark when he emerged from the portal. Instead, he found only his companions. They were in the small chamber where they’d started the dungeon dive. The wall with the tunnel that led to the goblin city was now just a flat, stone wall. That left two more tunnel openings.

“Which one now?” he asked, more to himself than anything. From what Nyx had said about having to defeat the monsters in all three places, it really didn’t matter which one they chose next.

“Both,” the woman unexpectedly replied.

Flynn raised an eyebrow.

“You saw how easy the goblins were to defeat,” she said. “All three levels before the boss will be just like that. You should be able to handle something like that by yourself. I will go with Throm to make sure he doesn’t run into any problems since he’s only got one Elite level card.”

Flynn pursed his lips as he considered her suggestion. It had been surprisingly easy to defeat the goblins and bring their entire city down. Even the Elite level Goblin Hex-Mage had gone down fairly easily.

The Orc Bulwark had been a different story, though, and Flynn wasn’t sure how they would have defeated the creature if it hadn’t simply quit fighting after the Hobgoblin Warboss had been killed.

As long as there wasn’t another Orc Bulwark, he’d probably be just fine…

“Okay,” he said. “I’ll take the tunnel on the right.”

Before he could change his mind, Flynn strode toward the tunnel and whatever monsters awaited.

Just like the first dungeon, he felt the transition into the tunnel that cut him off from the outer chamber. A chill ran down his spine, knowing there was no turning back. Flynn paused for only a second then resolutely marched down the tunnel.

After about a hundred yards, the tunnel opened up into a large forest that defied logic. Trees that should not be able to grow underground rose from the ground throughout the massive cavern. He could hear the natural sounds of a forest including bird calls, leaves rustling in the wind, and running water from a river or stream somewhere.

As he stood there, staring in bewilderment, an unusual creature emerged from the woods. It had legs like a goat, human-like arms, and a face that looked somewhat like a cat. Course brown hair covered its entire body and horns curled around goat-like ears that protruded from the side of its head. The creature wore no clothing, but there was no obvious indication whether it was male or female.

Name: Beastkin

Level: Advanced

Loot: NA

Beastkin are commonly encountered in forests. There is a near infinite variety of these monsters, but most bear some resemblance to common woodland creatures. Oftentimes, individuals share similar features to multiple animals. Beastkin are usually docile creatures but are protective of their homes and can become violent when their territory is invaded by outsiders. Given the extreme diversity of these creatures, there is no single best way to deal with them.

The hairy creature lifted its head and sniffed the air, turning to look and smell in all directions. After a moment the Beastkin focused on Flynn then let out a loud roar that echoed off the chamber walls.

The underground forest suddenly went silent, and the only sound he could hear was the unseen river or stream.

The previously docile-appearing creature bared its teeth and growled, then the Beastkin charged toward Flynn.

The hairy monster bounded forward in long loping strides that were a mix between running and jumping. As the Beastkin ran, it threw its arms out the side and long claws emerged.

When the creature was twenty feet away, it leaped into the air and flew toward Flynn with teeth bared and long claws extended to skewer the young man.

He casually stepped and let the Beastkin sail past, lashing out with his short sword as it flew by, lopping off the claws on one hand. The monster howled in pain and anger as it skidded to a stop then spun around toward Flynn. The hand that still had claws slashed at the Elite rank young man.

Flynn’s Hammer Shield special ability activated and easily blocked the attack, sending the clawed arm out to the side.

That left the Beastkin’s chest completely unprotected. Flynn stabbed forward with his short sword and swiftly ended the fight.

A chorus of mournful howls filed the air and Flynn turned back toward the forest to see dozens of Beastkin, each one unique in their features, howling at the roof of the cavern. The sorrowful tone turned angry after a moment, and all of the monsters turned their focus on the young man who had just killed one of their own.

The sound of dozens of deep growls filled the air, and Flynn could feel the sound reverberate in his chest.

Then the horde of hair monsters charged. Some ran in the loping, jumping strides like the first Beastkin. Others ran on all fours like a gorilla bounding forward using legs and arms. Ironically, the one creature that looked like a gorilla charged forward on two legs similar to the way a human ran.

Flynn studied the oncoming monsters for a moment, knowing none of the Advanced rank creatures posed any real threat individually, but he knew that even lower ranked monsters could cause damage. And if they overwhelmed him…

The thought of claws and teeth ripping into his flesh sent a shiver down his spine.

Flynn dashed forward and met a creature that had been bounding forward in mid-leap. His sword lashed out and sliced the creature from shoulder to hip while it was in the air, unable to alter its course or defend itself.

He didn’t wait to see if the attack was fatal and rushed toward the next closest monster—the creature with a muscular, gorilla type upper body. The Beastkin dove forward and reached out with both arms, trying to tackle him.

Flynn slid under the diving attack and jabbed up with his short sword. The blade dug into the monster’s torso and was drug down its body as it flew overhead leaving a massive wound.

A Beastkin with a snarling wolf head dove toward him before he could get back to his feet, forcing him to roll to the side to avoid the creature’s fangs and claws.

When he jumped to his feet, another monster was already leaping at him, and Flynn swatted it away with his battle hammer.

He glanced around and saw that all the monsters had now reached him and were circling him. Flynn knew he would be in trouble if they all pounced at once, and visions of gnashing teeth and ripping claws flashed through his mind again.

With that unpleasant image in his mind, he used his Dash special ability, slipped through a small opening in the circle of monsters, and bolted toward the forest.

Howls and screeches of indignation followed him, but Flynn didn’t stop until he reached the edge of the forest. He considered fleeing into the forest to fight a hit and run battle, but the forest was the Beastkins’ home, and they undoubtedly knew the terrain better than he did. Instead, he decided to make his stand at the edge of the forest where he had trees he could use for cover as well as to prevent them from attacking him all at once.

Flynn’s short weapons were perfect for close quarter combat, and his Elite rank combat skills were more than a match for the Advanced level Beastkin who fought with claws, fists, and teeth.

He waited for the first monster to reach him and dispatched it with a quick one-two attack with his battle hammer and short sword, then ducked behind a nearby tree to avoid the next attack. Flynn emerged on the other side of the tree already swinging his sword toward another monster. The blade sliced through the creature’s chest like a knife through butter, and he immediately reversed direction and smashed a Beastkin in the chest.

The monsters were starting to surround him again, so Flynn used his Bull Rush special ability to speed past the closest monsters and delivered a devastating attack on a Beastkin that looked like a beaver with long arms and legs. The beaver-creature was sent flying and slammed into a tree with a loud crunch of bones and cracking of wood.

The extra power of the Bull Rush attack had been completely unnecessary against the lower-level monster, but Flynn’s Dash ability was still on cooldown, so it was the best way for him to move quickly through the forest to avoid getting surrounded.

His short sword and battle hammer immediately went back to work, blocking attacks and delivering devasting wounds. He stepped behind trees when needed to avoid attacks and used Dash to create space again once the cooldown was over.

The original group of monsters was quickly reduced to only a few, but more monsters were coming toward Flynn. Despite their nature as forest creatures, the approaching monsters made no effort to conceal their approach, and he was easily able to keep track of where they were and avoid being trapped.

Flynn spent the next several hours moving through the forest battling Beastkin. When he was close to being surrounded, he used his special abilities to create space then used the cover of trees to avoid attacks and stay on the offensive.

The level difference made the battles themselves incredibly easy, usually only lasting a single dodge or parry followed by a one or two-weapon counterattack, but the constant press of enemies forced Flynn to stay vigilant. His mind was continuously working to locate threats, identify usable cover, and find avenues of escape when the monsters started to grow too numerous.

He missed fighting with Thunderbough, but this type of battle was ill suited for the Eldersloth. His familiar had grown faster, but he was still very much a stand and smash type fighter.

It’s probably a good thing summoned familiars weren’t allowed here, Flynn concluded, wondering if that restriction had been put in place to help or hinder dungeon divers.

When Flynn finally emerged from the forest on the far side of the underground cavern, he changed his mind on the lack of his familiar. Because he immediately knew he was going to have a problem.

The thick forest opened up into a semi-circular clearing right against the back wall of the cavern. A huge beast with a bull’s head sat on a large wooden throne next to the vertical rock wall.

Name: Minotaur King

Level: Elite

Loot: NA

Minotaurs are large, bull-headed monsters known for their strength and battle prowess. These beasts are typically found in labyrinths and dungeons and are frequently the last monster an inexperienced adventurer ever sees. To reach the level of King, they must be extraordinarily powerful and deadly. That gives them the right to sit back and watch others do their bidding. Minotaur Kings are usually protected by powerful bodyguards and only get involved in the fight if things are going poorly for their minions.

The Minotaur King had a small crown of gold leaves on its brow and casually held the handle of a large double-headed axe in one hand. The monsters powerful physique made it obvious it could easily wield the heavy weapon to deliver devastating attacks.

Flynn idly wondered how the creature had gotten the crown past its horns in order to circle its forehead.

He didn’t spend any time thinking about that mystery, though as his attention was drawn to the more pressing threat.

“You’ve got to be freaking kidding me!” Flynn said with disgust.

In the middle of the clearing stood a huge, tusked monster with a large shield.

Name: Orc Bulwark

Level: Elite

Loot: NA

Orcs are notoriously greedy creatures and will sell their services to the highest bidder. They are loyal to money and will fight to the death… or until they get a better offer. Bulwarks are defensive specialists that can block almost any attack up to one rank above their level. With all their focus on defensive skills and abilities, they frequently have little to no ability to attack.

The monster grinned at Flynn over the shield then jerked its arms to the side, splitting the shield in two. The orc bent its legs, holding the shields in each arm comfortably, despite their large size. Flynn knew from prior experience the creature could move them with lightning speed to block any attack.

Flynn groaned and let his shoulders sag.

Then he activated Bull Rush and dashed forward, trying to catch the defense-focused orc by surprise. At the last instant, he activated Double Double, as well, and attacked with both weapons.

The orc’s eyes widened in surprise then both shields moved with incredible speed, each shield blocking one of the attacks.

The attacks and speed at which Flynn was moving with two special abilities active couldn’t be completely stopped, though, and the large shield-bearing monster was sent skidding backward.

For about a foot.

The Bulwark raised its head toward the ceiling of the cavern and let out a deep, guttural laugh. When the creature looked back at Flynn, there was a grin on its face.

It took all the restraint Flynn could muster to not attack again immediately. Instead, he studied his enemy, then looked past the orc toward the Minotaur King lounging on the wooden throne. Just like the previous encounter, the monster on the throne was the real goal. The Orc was just an obstacle.

In the previous dungeon level, the Hobgoblin Warboss was protected by stairs and the only way to him was to get through the Orc Bulwark. This time, there was an entire open field in front of Minotaur.

Flynn didn’t need to defeat the Orc, he just needed to get around the monster so he could get to the real target.

It was going to be a tricky proposition because the Bulwark would undoubtedly chase him. He’d have to get around the Orc and finish off the Minotaur quickly. He knew it wasn’t going to be easy, but the alternative was to defeat the unbeatable monster with two shields that was grinning at him.

Well, I better be quick about it, Flynn decided and used his Dash special ability.

He sped toward the Orc with both weapons raised. Just before Flynn reached the monster, two shields came up to black the attack, but he spun around the creature instead of attacking.

Flynn charged the Minotaur King. The creature sat up straighter in its wooden throne but didn’t do anything to defend itself. Flynn raised his battle hammer over his head, wishing he could use his Double Double special ability.

It didn’t matter, he had Elite ranked combat cards and an Advanced rank Strength of Arms card. He also had the Crushing Blow special ability and he used it. It wasn’t as powerful as Double Double, which was still on cooldown, but it would give the attack 50% more power.

A shield slammed into Flynn and sent him flying. He crashed into the rock wall and fell to the ground, dazed.

He shook his head a few times to clear the confusion form his mind then pushed himself back to his feet. As his vision cleared, Flynn’s eyes focused on the Orc Bulwark standing between himself and the Minotaur King.

Flynn shook his head one more time then strode toward orc.

Most of his special abilities were still on cooldown, but he had one left that just might help him.

Special Ability: Melee Mirage

Delivers a rapid barrage of attacks that leaves illusory afterimages that confuse enemies. Actual attacks will have a moderate reduction in power.

Duration: 15 seconds

Cooldown: 5 minutes

Flynn unleashed a torrent of attacks on the Orc Bulwark, his sword and hammer moving faster than the eye could track and leaving a trail of illusions in their wake.

The Orcs shields moved just as fast and blocked each attack, deflecting some blows wide while absorbing others. As the duration of the special ability expired, one of Flynn’s hammer strikes deflected off the shield and smashed into the wall of the cavern. Rocks showered down on Flynn forcing him to jump backwards.

The Bulwark used the opening to push forward jabbing shields at the young man forcing him to retreat further.

By the time Flynn got his feet back under him and stopped the push back, he was halfway across the clearing and any ground he’d gained was lost.

He frowned at the Orc then looked toward the Minotaur King. His eyes moved to where a small pile of rocks on the ground marked the spot where he’d been sent flying into the cavern wall.

I was close, Flynn thought. Maybe if I use Dash, Bull Rush, and Double Double at the same time I can get to the throne.

All three abilities were on cooldown, but the Orc Bulwark didn’t seem to be interested in pushing an attack now that the immediate area around Minotaur King was clear any danger.

Flynn wasn’t going to let the annoying monster stand there doing nothing, though, and stepped forward and started pounding on the shields with both of his weapons.

Who knows, maybe one of these attacks will get through, Flynn thought idly while his mind worked on the plan for the next attack on the Minotaur.

As his sword and hammer strikes pushed the Orc back just a little bit, a plan firmed in Flynn’s mind. As soon as the cooldowns expired, he leaped into action.

A powerful Double Double special attack knocked the Bulwark back and slightly off balance.

Dash sent Flynn racing around the Orc and toward the Minotaur.

Bull Rush gave him more speed and further empowered his next attack.

He added in Crushing Blow to make sure his attacks would deliver a mortal wound just before he reached the Minotaur King.

Flynn was suddenly flying through the air. Again.

His speed had been used against him and his path redirected by the Orc’s shield. Flynn idly noticed that he shield had been put back together into a single large shield as he flew through the air.

Then he crashed into a tree at the edge of the clearing. The wind was knocked from his lungs and he heard wood crack.

He slid the ground and sat there for a moment. The Orc Bulwark took up position halfway between himself and the Minotaur while Flynn gathered his wits again.

Flynn heard the tree creek and looked up, afraid the tree was going to topple. It seemed solid, if damaged, but he didn’t want to take any chances, so he pushed himself to his feet and started walking around the edge forest toward the middle of the clearing.

The Orc watched him closely but made no move to attack.

When he made his way back to the point where he’d originally entered the clearing, Flynn turned to face the Bulwark again. The Minotaur King still sat in his throne, barely interested in the battle taking place. Or at least uninterested in joining the battle

Flynn studied his surroundings as he tried to figure out how to defeat the Orc or somehow kill the Minotaur from a distance without a ranged weapon. Towering trees surrounded most of the clearing, and a sheer rock wall covered the back. Climbing the rock wall was out of the question because it was a sheer vertical cliff with no obvious hand and foot holds. Climbing a tree didn’t do him any good since he didn’t have a ranged weapon.

He wondered if the Orc would follow him if he retreated into the woods. Maybe he could catch the creature by surprise. Or lead the monster away and circle back to attack the Minotaur one-on-one.

I need to reset my cooldowns, either way, so let’s try it! Flynn decided and ran into the forest.

After minute or running, he stopped and listened for the sounds of pursuit. There were none.

Flynn frowned, but honestly hadn’t expected the Orc to chase him. That would have been too easy.

Maybe I can surprise him, though, Flynn thought and started back toward the clearing. Instead of approaching form the center, he worked his way toward the wall of the cavern then followed it to the clearing

With any luck, he’d catch the Bulwark unawares, allowing him to charge the Minotaur King before its bodyguard knew what was happening.

Flynn approached the clearing as silently as he could, ready to activate his special abilities and make his attack.

The Orc Bulwark was waiting for him with its head cocked and a frown on its face. It was a look that asked, Do you think I’m stupid?

Flynn growled in frustration because that was exactly what he had hoped. The inability to battle the actual boss was beyond frustrating. He just wanted to scream. And throw something.

Throw something?!

He looked down at his battle hammer and remembered a special ability he’d been pretty excited about when he’d chosen it but hadn’t found much practical use for it.

Special Ability: Recall

Recall a bonded weapon to your hand at any time. Weapon will cause significant damage to any target struck during recall. One weapon can be bonded as part of this special ability.

Flynn stepped forward and threw his battle hammer at the Minotaur King.

The Orc Bulwark moved swiftly and threw his shield in front of the weapon. The hammer ricochetted off the shield and slammed into the cavern wall. A small shower of rocks rained down at the creature’s feet. The hammer landed fell there too.

Flynn reached out and summoned the weapon before the monster could steal it.

As soon as the battle hammer was back in his hand, Flynn threw it again, this time with more arc.

It didn’t batter. The Orc blocked the attack just as easily as the first and battle hammer bounced off the rock wall and fell harmlessly to the ground near the Minotaur.

Flynn groaned. Then he had a thought. He reached out his hand then took two steps to the left so the Bulwark was exactly between his weapon and his outstretched arm.

He summoned the hammer and it flew toward him, right at the back of the monster’s head.

At the last instant, the Orc ducked and the weapon sailed harmlessly over its head.

Flynn blew out a frustrated breath, then threw the battle hammer again, not knowing what else to do.

This time, it was deflected and sailed all the way across the clearing and hit the same tree Flynn had slammed into. There was a loud crack and the tree swayed precariously.

This isn’t doing any good, Flynn thought dejectedly. Time to figure out Plan C.

As soon as his hammer returned, he stepped back into the forest and worked his way around to the center of the clearing.

He didn’t make any effort to hide where he was going, he just needed a minute to think. The Orc Bulwark easily tracked his movement and was standing in the middle of the clearing when Flynn emerged again a few moments later. This time, he had a plan to end the battle and finish this stage of the dungeon.

It was time to throw everything he had into the battle. Literally.

Flynn threw his battle hammer at the Orc then used another special ability that he rarely used.

Special Ability: Wind Kick

Wind Kick is a special attack that creates a blade of wind that causes damage commensurate with the rank of the associated card. At Elite rank, the wind blade causes significant damage to any target Elite level or below.

Cooldown: 1 hour

The battle hammer hit the Orc’s shield an instant before the blade of wind. The hammer didn’t do any apparent damage, but the invisible air attack immediately after knocked the creature backward.

Flynn used both Dash and Bull Rush and sped forward. He recalled his battle hammer as he ran, then activated Double Double and Crushing Blow.

He veered to the left as the Orc Bulwark regained its footing and ran straight toward the damaged tree on the edge of the clearing.

Flynn crossed the open space in an instant and delivered the most devastating attack he was capable of. His sword cut halfway through the trunk on the side of the tree facing the clearing and his hammer smashed all the way through an instant later..

He bounced off the tree and staggered back as it creaked and started to fall.

Flynn watched the tree start to topple, waiting to see where it was going to fall before making his next move. He hoped it would fall between the Minotaur King and its bodyguard, or even better on the Orc Bulwark itself. Either way would allow him to attack his ultimate target.

He smiled as the tree started to slowly lean toward the middle of the clearing. Then there was a loud snap as something broke free and the tree picked up speed.

And changed direction.

Instead of falling toward the Ork Bulwark, the giant tree fell right toward the throne.

The Minotaur King looked up as the tree sped toward it then jumped up, raising its large axe over its head in protection as the falling tree picked up speed.

The large tree smashed down on the Minotaur and throne. There was a bleating grunt of pain and the sound of breaking wood. Then everything went silent.

You have defeated the Minotaur King and completed this stage of the Dungeon of the Savage Frontier. All rewards will be awarded upon final completion of the dungeon.

Flynn looked from the downed tree to the Orc Bulwark who had turned to look at the fallen tree. The creature shrugged then calmly walked toward the tree. A portal arch appeared halfway there, and the Orc walked through it and disappeared.

He shook his head and chuckled then headed for the portal.
