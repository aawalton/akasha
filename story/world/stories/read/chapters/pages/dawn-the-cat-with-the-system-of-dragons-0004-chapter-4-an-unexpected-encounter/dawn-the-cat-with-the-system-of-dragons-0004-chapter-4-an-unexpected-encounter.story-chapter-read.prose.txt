The sun was about to set on the horizon. The sky was filled with a clear orange hue. The atmosphere inside the forest gradually darkened with the fading light. The sounds of insects, rodents, and other nocturnal beings became prominent. Owls and other nocturnal hunters became active at nightfall.

The temperature dropped quite a bit in the forest. Adventurers who entered the forest for hunting animals and monsters started coming back to the town.

A blurry shadow moved swiftly across the ground, navigating through the bushes as if it thrives in this atmosphere. It was the mother black cat. She seemed to be searching for something. She kept running deeper into the forest. Her golden eyes glowed so brightly that anyone who saw them in the darkness would surely be scared.

Sometimes she simply ran through the bushes, sometimes she sniffed here and there, and sometimes she lay low to observe. Her sharp senses were enough to navigate the depths of the dense forest.

She sometimes entered the territories of foxes, other felines, or the hunting areas of owls. It was just nightfall, and nocturnal animals were becoming active. It wasn't yet so dangerous that she had to stop searching for a new territory for her kittens.

After searching for some time, she suddenly stopped and directed her ears to listen for a faint but familiar sound. It was the mewling of a feline baby, identical to the high-pitched cries of her babies.

The baby seemed to be calling for its parents in extreme hunger and distress. This sound was very similar to her own babies. But when she sniffed here and there, there was no territory marking.

This caught her attention. The presence of a newborn baby with no territory marking in the nearby area was very strange.

She started sniffing to find any lingering scent, but no territory marking was there. In the end, she decided to follow the source of the sound.

After a while, she noticed a huge tree from afar. This tree was massive compared to the others in the forest. Its trunk was especially thick near the root. It was covered in green moss.

The huge tree had an opening on one side between the roots. A dark gap formed there, and nothing was visible inside. The distinct mewling sound was coming from there.

What was even stranger was that there were no predators nearby. The mother cat felt a calming atmosphere around the tree. The calming sensation felt as if the huge tree was hidden from the surrounding forest.

The black cat could sense something odd, but whatever it was, it wasn't threatening or dangerous.

She decided not to get too close. First, she decided to inspect for some time, and then she would enter.

A calming atmosphere, a baby cat’s mewling, no territory marking, and no recent tracks of other felines made the black cat pause her advance. Her instincts, honed over many seasons, urged her to proceed with caution. So instead of entering the gap between the roots, she hid under a nearby bush and the hanging branches of a nearby tree.

Seconds turned into minutes, minutes into a quarter of an hour. Even after half an hour, no feline visited the area, and the baby was still mewling with hunger and distress. Still, no other being appeared.

The mewling drew the black cat closer to the baby. Her motherly instinct made it hard to ignore the baby’s painful cries. Finally, after waiting and observing, she decided to go inside the dark space below the tree.

The gap between the roots was small, but not small for her. It seemed to be a perfect area for any cat's litter.

As soon as she put her head inside the dark space, she noticed a damp smell, different from a usual cat’s offspring. The smell belonged to something entirely different. It was surely from a being she had never encountered before. Her danger sense was still not active, so it seemed to be okay.

Now she could hear the mewlings very clearly. They were coming from inside, but she still couldn’t see the baby. There was a small ditch at the other end. The baby was hidden there. So the black cat squeezed through the gap and moved forward towards the small ditch. The den formed between the roots had a vast space inside, big enough for multiple cats to live together without trouble.

After reaching the small ditch, she noticed a tiny baby, similar to her kittens. This was the baby that kept mewling desperately. It had almost white skin with white fur here and there, its eyes had not opened yet, its nose was different, and its tail was a bit thicker and longer than a usual kitten. It almost looked like a kitten, but it was clearly not a kitten.

The sound of this vulnerable baby reactivated the black cat’s motherly instinct. The helpless mewling, filled with hunger and desperation, made the cat want to protect the baby. She got closer to the baby, then nudged it very lightly with her nose. The baby, sensing the touch, instinctively turned a bit, then pushed itself forward towards the black cat's snout and latched on.

The black cat stiffened with the sudden change and growled in a low, warning tone, but the baby didn't let go of her snout. The mother cat didn't have enough cruelty to push the baby back. She couldn't bring herself to hurt it. The helpless mewls and the small body pressed and it silenced the cat’s low growl. The black cat had a helpless expression. The baby melted away her initial resistance. Motherly instincts overrode her wild habits. This creature was utterly helpless. It needed milk. She had milk. The logic was simple.

She decided. She would feed it this once. Her maternal instincts, heightened with her own kittens, overwhelmed her judgment. She licked this baby to calm it down, then she gently lowered herself and let the tiny creature find its way to milk.

After the tiny creature had its fill, its small body finally settled and began sleeping soundly. Only then did the mother cat carefully withdraw for the night; her own kittens, equally vulnerable, needed her attention. She went back.

— Next day —

The next day, at dawn, after feeding her own kittens and making them sleep, she ventured back into the forest. Sun rays weren't yet visible through the leaves; the forest was still dark, and the blue environment of dawn created a little bit of light in the shadowy surroundings. Peacocks started singing, and bird chirps could be heard everywhere.

Again, she was searching for a new safe place to transfer her litter from the previous den. Dogs had been making relentless efforts to get in, although the little girl chased them away whenever she was around. But still, it wasn't enough.

During the previous fight, she was heavily injured. But miraculously, she got cured and had no pain. She didn’t know how? But still, she didn't engage the dogs directly anymore. She was attacking from the small opening in her hideout every time the dogs tried to enter.

After searching everywhere, the cat didn't find a suitable spot. In the end, it was time to go back. But while going back, she decided to take a detour. She decided to visit the baby again. If its parents returned, she would not proceed.

She returned to the tree where she'd found the other baby. Before entering, she stayed a bit far, observing the surroundings intently for a few minutes. The baby's parents weren't there yesterday; they might be nearby today.

The creature's mewls were loud and consistent, but still, there were no signs of its parents, no territorial markings, no scent of other creatures, no disturbed earth around the gnarled roots. After she was convinced that there was no danger, the mother cat decided to go inside.

She entered the gap between the tree roots and reached the baby, touching it with her snout again. The tiny creature latched onto the black cat’s face immediately, just like the night before. She licked it a few times to settle down the creature. Then the mother cat sat down and started feeding it.

She was now certain that if the creature's parents hadn't returned even by tonight, they never would. There was no other explanation for such a prolonged absence.

After the baby fell asleep against her belly, she made a firm decision. She would check one last time in the evening. If the situation remained the same, she would move her own babies to this location. Despite having only a single abandoned baby, this den offered greater safety than her previous compromised den.

She went out of the den, and after a while, she started feeling the pangs of hunger. She searched for rodents in the forest, but her hunt was unsuccessful. She couldn't find a single rodent, and she needed to return as soon as possible, so she went back without hunting anything.

Near her hideout, the little human approached again. The black cat spotted the little girl as soon as the mother cat reached her den. The girl was patiently waiting with food clutched in her small hands. The black cat vigilantly watched her, hoping the girl would leave something to eat. But the cat didn't approach immediately; she simply waited in front of the little human, observing every movement.

Seeing this familiar display of caution, the girl placed the roasted meat down on the ground, then moved a few meters away. Delighted by the food, the cat slowly and vigilantly walked towards it. Her hunger was immense. She devoured the meat quickly, savoring every bite. After eating, she slipped back inside her hideout, her stomach now full.

After the girl went back, the black cat fed her restless kittens, who were squirming against their mother's belly. Once they were fed and quiet, the black cat finally slept.

— Same Day, Evening —

In the evening, as dusk painted the sky in deep blue, the black cat went straight to the forest, returning to the spot where she'd found the baby creature. She investigated, but there were still no signs of another being. Moreover, the baby was mewling as usual.

There were still no signs of another animal, no territorial markings, no distant calls, only the persistent mewls echoing from within the tree's roots. A tiny creature was consistently calling for someone in hunger and desperation.

Seeing this time, the mother cat was absolutely certain it was safe, and the baby creature was undeniably abandoned, or its parents had perished. The long hours of silence, the lack of any warm presence, and the lack of a food source made it certain that the baby creature would not survive if it weren't adopted by some other animal.

She went under the tree, went closer to the baby creature, and nudged it with her snout, and the baby latched onto her snout just like before. She sat beside the baby creature and fed it. When the baby started sleeping, the black cat returned to her hideout with a determined look.

During nightfall, she began transferring her litter. She picked up one of her kittens by the nape of its neck and began carrying it into the dim moonlight. But there was a huge problem. A tall border, constructed with huge tree logs, protected the town. It made the black cat’s task very hard. Carrying her kittens and climbing and descending the tall wooden border was impossible. She could do it alone without any problem, but with her kittens, it was impossible.

There was the town’s gate nearby, but the mother cat wasn't sure whether she should use it. People were always around. Yet, she remembered that the girl gave her food every morning and evening, and the girl wasn't dangerous, though other humans might be.

She went back to her hideout, put down her kitten, and went out again. This time, she headed towards the huge town gate, walking slowly. She decided to check if she would get attacked by humans. Since the town’s gate was big, she would have enough room to escape if she was suddenly attacked.

She started walking, and when she reached the gate, she saw a few people outside. Two guards stood on either side of the gate, blocking the path inside, and another guard was talking to several people with their carriage.

“Sorry, we can’t let you enter at this time. You don’t have a permit to enter.”

“Sir, can’t you make an exception?”

“You’re not even a local resident. How can I make any exception? Even if you were a local resident, without proper explanation, I would have asked you to stay outside or stay in detention.”

“Detention!!! Are you crazy??? It is evening. Where are we supposed to go now?”

“You traveled for many days; surely you can camp out tonight. No?”

While they were having a heated argument, the guards moved forward to push away people from outside. This gave enough room for our mother cat to sneak out behind them, as they were a few steps away from the gate.

People were engaged in talking and negotiating with each other outside the town. To prevent unauthorized people from entering, guards had to move outside and keep an eye on outsiders. During the night, they were always shorthanded.

She slowly walked through the gate towards the outside. The armored guards saw the black cat but didn’t do anything. Then she came back again after a few minutes, but still, no one bothered her.

“Look there, a black cat is walking inside.” One guy saw the mother cat. He was standing outside with the group that had just gotten stuck due to a town guards.

“Come on… Do you have time to look at a cat… are you crazy…”

“Here we can’t go out. Now I have to see a black cat entering… this is so ominous… huh.”

They were too busy to be bothered with the cat. After determining that there was no danger in bringing her kittens through the gate, she started transferring them one by one, holding each kitten by the scruff. She walked fast when she was carrying her kittens.

By the time she returned with her first kitten, the heated argument was over, and the outsiders started creating a camp near the gate. One guard was keeping an eye on them, and the other was staying on one side of the gate.

Seeing that one side of the gate was free, she started using that side to bring all her kittens.

After going back and forth, two kittens were successfully transferred safely to the unexpected foster sibling in the root hollow. Now three babies remained. When she returned for the third kitten, she saw the girl again, patiently waiting, holding a substantial amount of food near her hideout.

The mother cat growled a bit.

“Please, don’t be angry, kitty. I got late because of my teacher. Take the food, okay.” Cinthia thought the cat was angry because she was extremely late. She gently put the food on the ground and filled the container with water.

Even after giving food and water, the girl saw the cat kept growling in a low tone. She was visibly confused, her brow furrowing with concern. After that, she moved a bit farther away, but remained within the cat’s visible range, not too far.

Every day, she had given food twice, each time stepping a bit closer to the cat's hideout, and the cat hadn't seemed to mind her advances. Even that very morning, when she'd offered food, there hadn't been a growl, only a cautious acceptance. She thought (Was getting late soooo wrong?)

The girl simply couldn't understand, a pang of disappointment settling in her chest.

After some time, when the cat still hadn't touched the food, the girl murmured, her voice soft with uncertainty, "Don't you want to eat? I have more if you want."

Seeing the girl standing a few meters away and she was not moving, the cat ignored all the roasted meat. It slipped back into her hideout, disappearing into the dark crevice. Moments later, she reappeared, a tiny kitten clutched gently in her mouth.

The black cat trusted the girl for food, but her trust was not enough to transfer her kitten while remaining within the girl’s close attack range.

She headed towards the gate, her destination clear. Her third kitten was now being carried to a new den, far from the prying eyes of dogs and curious humans.

The roasted meat, for all its promise, meant nothing compared to her kitten’s safety. Yet, even as she disappeared farther away towards the town’s gate, she cast a determined glance back at the delicious meat once, deciding to have it for dinner after transferring all her kittens. The journey was long and filled with potential dangers. While changing location with her kittens, she was a vulnerable target. The distance was considerable, so it took her a lot of time to bring each tiny kitten to the new location. This made her hungry, but her kittens were the absolute priority for now. She would surely return later for the delicious food.

After observing the black cat going away with the third kitten, the girl sighed heavily, a wave of understanding washing over her, replacing her confusion with sadness. "So that's why the kitty was so angry," she murmured while tears started flowing down her cheeks. "She's moving her babies… huuuh… and I got in her way… She's leaving forever."

She had really wanted to help raise all the kittens with their mother cat, to watch them grow healthy and strong under her care, but now she knew it wasn't possible. The cat had made her choice, deciding to move her family somewhere else.

She followed the kitten from afar. She noticed that the cat went outside the gate while carrying its kitten. Going out of the city was an extreme no-no, a line she absolutely could not cross. A heavy lump formed in her throat as she imagined the tiny furballs disappearing into the unknown place. She then walked slowly back home, the excitement of the past few days replaced by a deep ache.

"Papa, Mama," Cinthia sobbed later that night, throwing herself into her father's and mother’s arms, hugging them tightly. "I want the kitty! I wanted to help them!" That night, she cried a lot, tears soaking her father's and mother’s clothes. Only the girl's parents truly understood the depth of her longing to have the cat and her babies.

Seeing his daughter's tear-filled face, Cinthia's father gently stroked her hair, his heart heavy with her sorrow. "Now, now, sweetheart," he murmured, his voice soft and comforting, pulling her closer, "don't cry. It's okay. How about we check something in the morning? If the food you left tonight is gone, it means our kitty came back. And she'll keep coming back as long as you continue to provide her with food at the right time and in the right place." He pressed a kiss to her forehead, hoping to soothe her restless spirit.

— The Next Day, Dawn —

The next day at dawn, with hopeful anticipation and lingering sadness, Cinthia rushed back to the spot at dawn, with Theodoric and Elenora walking steadily behind her. When she reached the place, her breath hitched. There was nothing on the ground, not a single scrap of food remained where she had left it. A sense of relief washed over her. Her father was right; the cat had indeed come back.

Her father knelt, carefully observing a few small paw prints near where she'd left the food and the water-filled container. He traced one with his finger. "Look, these are clearly the kitty’s footprints," the father said gently to his daughter, pointing to the distinct marks in the soft earth. "The kitty was definitely here."

“Hmm.” With a wide smile spreading across her face, Cinthia nodded enthusiastically. Her spirit lifted, and she immediately decided to go back to the market, dragging her father and mother along, determined to purchase a substantial amount of fresh food for the cat.

The cat returned in the morning, after descending the town’s border. She once again saw the little girl near her old hideout, though this time Cinthia's father and mother stood a respectful distance away, observing silently.

This time, the cat didn't growl; she simply approached, her movements more deliberate now, picked up the roasted meat, and began to eat, her hunger still a driving force. Her kittens were far away, safe and sound in their new, secluded den. There was no need for constant vigilance, allowing her to focus on feeding herself.

The girl, seeing this significant shift in behavior, was delighted. She didn't approach the cat further; she only waited and observed, her heart filled with joy. After finishing the roasted meat and drinking deeply from the water, the mother cat, for the first time, walked closer to the girl. She began walking back and forth near her legs, rubbing her body gently against them, a soft purr rumbling in her chest—an unprecedented display of affection and acceptance.

Seeing this incredible gesture, the girl gently touched the cat's body. She then decided to pick it up and hug it. As soon as she attempted to hold the black cat from both sides, the cat let out a sharp, immediate hiss—a reflexive warning, a boundary she was not yet ready to cross. Seeing this, the girl immediately retracted her hands. The cat then bolted suddenly, ran towards the town’s border and climbed it, and disappeared to the other side.

"I'll come again, kitty!" the girl called out, her voice echoing slightly through the quiet morning.
