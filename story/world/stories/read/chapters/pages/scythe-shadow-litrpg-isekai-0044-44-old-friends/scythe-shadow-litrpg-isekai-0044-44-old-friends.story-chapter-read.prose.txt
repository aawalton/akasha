The three enemy Reapers and the Emerald Unchained waited for me as I charged forward atop the undead stallion. Nuzzle, on my shoulder, curled his tail around my neck for support as if he knew what I planned.

The tavern door was right behind them. I just needed to get there. Timing would be everything, but I'd seen at least one of the Reaper's skills and they hadn't seen mine. That gave me an advantage.

The horse's hooves thundered beneath me as I dug in my heels, praying for speed. I had to wait for the precise moment…

There!

The Amethyst Reaper lifted his hands to summon the giant shadow gloves and snatch me. But I wouldn't be there.

I gripped the reigns tighter, squeezed my knees against the stallion's torso trying to make sure we remained one unit. The reddish black shadow hands rush toward me in a blur.

Not yet…

The fingers opened.

Not yet…

The palm bloomed before me, taking up nearly my entire field of vision, the fingers began to close.

Now!

I Aero Shifted forward, putting everything I had into it. The world zipped past me at incomprehensible speed as I burst through the shadow as if it were a harmless cloud. I didn't stop there, though. I continued in my Shift through their line and appeared on the other side.

The door to the Meridian was mere feet away. My Volition dropped by a third, but I'd done it. Nuzzle was still with me, the horse too. I yanked the reigns, bringing it to a stop and dove off.

My feet never hit the ground.

Liquidy chains wrapped around my torso and shoulders, suspending me in the air. I hung there, face down to the ground. I tried to Aero Shift out, but it wouldn't work. The chains only tightened.

Nuzzle squealed in pain, as he, too, withstood the constriction.

Behind, the Emerald Reaper laughed. "Thought you were pretty clever, huh? You Astral affinity Reapers are always so surprised when your Aero Shift doesn't work. My Ripple Chains prevent anything living from passing through. A mistake you're going to pay dearly for."

I braced myself to be pulled back and thrown before the Unchained like the other two Reapers who'd been offered up and skewered. But before that happened, the tavern door opened.

Two feet in yellow thong sandals stepped out. With rainbow painted toenails.

I snapped my head up.

Jericho stood in the doorway.

Our eyes met and he frowned. Confusion. Then some level of understanding as he studied my opponents.

I felt my body flung away.

"Take care of that one!" The Emerald yelled to his companions as I was propelled through the air, still bound in the chains.

"But… He's a Sapphire," one called back. "What the fuck's a Sapphire doing out here?"

I didn't hear the reply, landing in a heap before the Emerald Unchained. It's porcupine spines prickled in anticipation of the kill. Nuzzle rolled to a stop somewhere nearby and crawled back up to my shoulder.

Between the Sin's surprisingly limber legs, I could see Jericho standing alone as all three Reapers formed a triangle around him. It seemed they decided to leave me to the Unchained alone. I looked up at the hybrid beast, part human, part porcupine, frog-like limbs and face. It grinned at me, toothless, a long tongue slurping along its lips.

The tongue shot out like a harpoon.

I Aero Shifted sideways, taking Nuzzle with me, but rolling out of the motion, still in a confused jumble of limbs in the dirt after being dropped there. The tongue remained skewered where I'd been second before.

Near the tavern, Jericho remained in a stagnant face-off with the Reapers. They circled him cautiously, clearly hesitant to strike against a higher ranked Reaper. He remained stationary, head on a swivel, but growing impatient.

I realized then, that I'd never actually seen the old coot fight.

He winked at me, then rose his armored arm into the air. The carapace glimmered into a watery substance, but retained its shape. The liquid crawled over his shoulder, across his back and neck, flowing over the remainder of his body like a wave. It swam across his head and face, leaving a small visor opening like a suit of armor. Then it hardened into the same metallic blue of his carapace, spikes and spines jutting out at joints and the crest of his helm.

The Amethyst Reaper charged at him from the back, hoping to catch him off guard during the transformation. Jericho didn't flinch. An extra razor sharp appendage grew out of his back and cleaved the man in half, sending him back to Vitria in one swipe.

A spine flew past my face, bringing me back to my own fight. Another one injected itself into my calf, forcing me to Aero Shift again.

Focus on your own battle, idiot.

The initial hit only took a small portion Health, but it continued to drop. My legs suddenly felt sluggish. I ripped the spike from my leg, screaming as it tore free. Beads of green goo mixed with the blood on the end of the massive splinter.

Poison? Fucking great.

Another volley of spikes came at me. I dodged them, using up more Volition. Still enough left for my signature move though. Tendrils of smoke drifted through the air from my fingers as I cast Life Drain.

The Unchained Sin tried to bat the smoke away, but it just reformed, finding his mouth, eyes, ears. In a panic, the beast shot quills in all directions. I heard a scream from the direction of Jericho's fight. One caught me in the abdomen and launched me backwards off my feet, breaking my Life Drain.

My Health drained faster, a second dose of poison coursing through my veins. I checked around frantically to make sure Nuzzle was alright. He was so small, one skewer from those spines could end him. But I couldn't find him anywhere.

I looked back to the Unchained.

The little orange lizard was latched onto the monster's face—the one part of its body not covered in spikes. The Sin's tongue was wrapped around Nuzzle's stomach, squeezing and pulling, but Nuzzle held firm, one claw latched onto an eye socket—now empty and dripping blood, eyeball hanging below by the topic nerve—and another onto the tongue. More quills spewed out as the Sin howled.

Then Nuzzle did something I didn't expect.

He opened his mouth and breathed fire into the Sin's empty eye socket.

Smoke poured out of the Sin's orifices, but it continued squeezing Nuzzle with the tongue. Harder and harder. The lizard coughed out one last burst of flame, then went limp.

I screamed, sprinting at the Unchained. Vision black at the edges. One focus. One purpose. I used my Second Sight, finding its Will, then opened my carapace hand, like a claw aiming for the glowing orb at its heart.

I Aero Shifted into the beast.

I left the shift the moment my hand gripped its Will. The beast moaned. It looked down at me. At my arm through its chest. Confused.

Several more quills had pierced me, but I ignored the pain. Ignored my plummeting Health.

Then I ripped.

The Will tore from the Unchained Sin's body like a bloody volcano that'd been waiting centuries to erupt, leaving a trail of bones, muscle, and guts in its wake.

I fell back to the ground, clutching the Emerald Will.

The Unchained wavered for a second, then collapsed beside me.

The Will solidified in my hand, going dormant.

I let my head fall back into the dirt. Nuzzle dragged his battered and bruised body up beside me and rested his forehead against mine.

"Hey there, buddy…" I wheezed. "Thanks for the assist."

My Health continued to lower, the red bar creeping toward empty. At least six quills protruded up from various parts of my body. Most in my abdomen.

Heavy footsteps approached. An armored behemoth looked down upon me, upside down from my angle. For some reason that made me laugh as my vision started to fade.

The helm of the armor melted away, revealing Jericho's grinning face, his long gray beard tumbling out. A cup of microwavable noodles twirled into existence into his hand. I closed my eyes, unable to hold them open any longer.

A snack? Is this really the time, Jericho?

Then I felt the warm broth going down my throat, allowed myself to swallow the noodles. The sluggish feeling overwhelming my body slowly dissipated. My breathing evened out, heart rate slowing back to normal.

After a few moments I was able to open my eyes again, sit up. Nuzzle curled into my lap.

I brushed back my hair, dirt falling from it like rain. My Health had a minuscule few percentage points remaining.

"I owe you one," I said to Jericho.

His armor had disappeared and he sat cross-legged beside me. "One?" he chuckled. "How about twenty?"

I nodded, unable take the joke any further. "Twenty it is."

"I suppose there's some sort of reasonable explanation why you're out here picking on poor little Unchained Sins and disgruntled Reapers?"

I choked through a laugh and explained everything. The situation with the Iron puppet master controlling the Unchained. Pirhani leading an attack on Meadowvale with oath-bound Reapers. Some unknown conflict between her and Valciferous that I somehow got myself tied into. While I explained, I harvested the Emerald Will I'd been clutching. My scythe glowed a dim green with its presence.

"I came here for help, hoping to find some higher ranking Reapers in the Meridian to come aid the defense. Though at this point, I'm not sure what will be left to defend. They'd already breached the gate when I left."

I picked up some sort of journal item from the remains of the Sin and stashed it in my inventory.

"I can get us back to the city," Jericho said. "I've been saving up a few items and might have one that should do the trick. I imagine you'll want more help than just little old me, though."

I hesitated, Jericho watching me, knowing the question I wanted to ask, waiting to see if I had the heart to ask it. "Have you seen Calliope?" It came out as a pathetic croak, a weak whimper.

"Not since Allium," he said, eyes downcast.

"She found him then… I'm sorry. I… They just came out of nowhere. Unchained. I—"

"Save your apology. I'm not the one it's for."

I nodded, standing up, cradling the resting Nuzzle in my arms.

"I need more Reapers to help at Meadowvale." I stepped toward the tavern door. "Am I gonna find good news in there? Lancelot?"

Jericho shrugged. "I suppose that depends on your skills in persuasion."

So he is here. A weight left my shoulders, lifted by a small bubble of hope.

I opened the door and entered the Meridian.

Time to recruit the Diamond Reaper.
