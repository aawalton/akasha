Samantha took a deep breath, feeling a bit touched by the young soldier’s words. Did he experience such little kindness that what little they did was worth an obviously heartfelt oath, or was it her aura at work? “Are you a career soldier, my child?”

“No, milady. I’m just a farmer’s son. A levyman. They trained us with spears and shields. I was of age, in training for two months, supposed to be three, but the war happened, so they swept through and recruited. We did drills in Matrazere.”

“I think you can serve as a guard in Rivermount and be close to your Lana. At least until you can return to your farm after we secure the territory. If you want.”

Hugh nodded. “Thank you, milady.” He seemed to hesitate. He looked away. “I feel I should warn you, ah… Matrazere, milady? Th-the fortress, it… It can’t be taken. Never was, and they say it never will be.”

Samantha smiled patiently. “Let us worry about that. But if we do fail, you’ve got plenty of territory to flee south.”

His slight nod was not convincing as his eyes darted around. When his eyes fell on the bandaged man in the other bed, he gestured briefly with a finger. “That one, you should recruit, for sure. Begging your pardon, milady, but for fightin’. He’s a right terror.”

“Oh?” Samantha raised an eyebrow and glanced over at the bandaged mummy, who still hadn’t so much as twitched. But she was rather confident — and had been since entering the room — that the man was awake. Keen senses, intuition, and an enhanced sense of time made her hard to fool. His breathing had been perfectly that of a man sleeping, but it was too perfect, and she felt control in it.

He’s been listening in.

Hugh nodded and continued in a conspiratorial whisper, his voice gradually getting heavier and rougher with his accent, “He killed half-a-dozen men like they were standing still! Moved like a dancin’ ghost, carvin’ em up with ease. And he even killed a wizard, while shrugging off spells like rain bouncin’ off a waxed coat. For a long while, anyway. He’s a fightin’ knight, alright. Merc captain. He-” Hugh swallowed and nodded over at the figure in the other bed, lip quivering briefly. “He saved our lives. Spells did start to wear him and his armor down over time.”

Samantha’s brow climbed higher and higher. “That is most impressive, indeed. Did you get his name, or more on his story?”

Hugh shook his head, but caught himself halfway through and hesitated. “Not his name, and not much, but… But he did say something awful about wh-... what they were gonna do. The sages, milady. To us.” He paused, swallowing hard, and met her eyes uncertainly.

“Mmn? Go on, my child.”

“Undead, milady,” he said, his voice such a faint whisper that he could barely be heard. His eyes were full of shock, horror, and something like hurt. Betrayal, perhaps. “They were gonna turn us all into undead. Zombies, I reckon.”

Samantha’s eyes widened. “Oh, shit!” At Hugh’s sudden, odd look at her, she cleared her throat and said, “I mean to say, that’s truly treating valuable and honest men like dung, like cattle. A travesty and tantamount to mass murder. Entirely different than facing your enemy and unleashing destruction, when it's the sacrifice of your own people.”

Hugh looked down and nodded. “I just can’t believe they’d go that far…”

“They’d do anything to preserve their power. Anything to maintain their status quo and rid themselves of the plague we’ve become to them. I wish bloodshed weren’t needed at all, but this matter proves why it is, awful though the reality may be. They’ve been cultivating this abomination of a spell for quite possibly thousands of years.”

Hugh sighed wearily, appearing rather numb with it all.

Samantha glided all the way to his bedside and put a hand on his shoulder, to which he looked up at her, awestruck again. She smiled gently. “You should rest. And I see you are very stressed and wired, still, from your ordeal. Would you consent to me helping you? A little magic. I understand if you are reluctant, of course. I can instead send fo-”

“No, milady! I-I mean, apologies…” He blanched. “That is, please, go ahead, ah… y-you’re a healer?” His wide eyes and his tone seemed to suggest he thought she was a lot more.

Maintaining her smile, Samantha nodded. “Among other things, yes. But very well. Lay down, now.” She began to cast a simple sleep-aid cantrip on him.

As he slid down to drop his head on the pillow, eyelids already drooping, he murmured, “If I could… your name… milady…”

And he’s out!

Samantha pulled the covers up over him and his still dead-asleep, softly snoring lover, then walked over to the other bed. She paused off to the side, not quite at his bedside, and stared. Stared and stared and stared, waiting.

The man never reacted. He continued acting like he was passed out.

Samantha crossed her arms and tapped her foot, staaaaaaring as if to burn holes in his closed eyelids — the only thing visible amidst the mummified, half blood-stained assemblage of white cloth wrappings.

Still nothing.

Finally, scoffing and throwing up her hands, she called in a strained whisper, “Hey! You! I know you’re awake! Just admit it! Come on, open those eyes, soldier. Enough play-acting.” Finally, she made one sudden, swift movement, only moving centimeters, but with telekinetic control of herself, and using the full power of her speed. She made sure to create a little gust of wind propagating outward at him.

This finally caused a damning blip in his breathing, as he tensed as if to move and held his breath, just for a split moment.

Samantha flung out a finger, eyes wide with accusation. “Ah-ha! I saw that! Gotcha! You’re fucked now, buddy. Just give it up, already.”

The held breath turned into a deep one, practically a sigh. His eyes fluttered open slightly, though he didn’t meet her eyes. Very, very slowly, he pulled out his bandaged hands, showing them flat in surrender. He then made a hand sign for ‘drawing/writing’ as his eyes got closer to meeting hers.

“Hmmph.” Samantha activated [Conjure] and made a blank journal appear in his lap, along with a charcoal-style pencil.

He tensed again, his eyes focused intently on the items. She couldn’t see his brows, but she was sure they’d just furrowed under the bandages.

“Yes, soldier-boy,” Samantha said with a hint of tease in her voice, “what I just did has you completely in my power.” It was either true or possibly not, depending on his levels, particularly if he was a [Knight], but she still said it with full confidence. “It’s also quite handy, as you can see.”

Once more, his eyes flashed close to meeting hers, but not quite. He sat up, picked up the journal and pencil, and wrote in it, then turned it around. It read, ‘I APOLOGIZE FOR DECEIVING YOU. I WANTED TO SEE YOUR INTERACTIONS. I’M IMPRESSED. ARE YOU THE GODDESS?’

Samantha’s eyebrows rose. “Yes. How did you know?”

The man took a measured breath as he fully closed his eyes again, briefly, obviously struck by the magnitude of her answer. But he quickly opened them, turned a page, wrote hastily, and finally turned it to her. ‘YOUR ASCENDANCY? IS THAT THE CORRECT TITLE? MANY SMALL THINGS ADDING UP. GUESSWORK. I DID NOT KNOW. I SHOULD START BY TELLING YOU I STARTED THE REVOLT. I DISCOVERED WHAT THEY WERE PLANNING AND TRIED TO STOP IT. I FAILED. I WILL TELL YOU ALL AND HELP WHATEVER WAY I CAN IN THE HOPE YOU COULD SUCCEED. PLEASE USE ME BEFORE EXECUTING ME FOR MY CRIMES. THIS IS ALL I ASK.’

Samantha’s eyebrows could not climb any higher. “You? You’re the cause of all this? What are the bloody odds?!” Thinking about it, she quickly said, “Well, you’re obviously a badass, so I guess the odds are much higher than anyone else getting here.” She glanced at the other bed and back. “So, why them? And it’s ‘Your Majesty,’ by the way.”

The man sagged a bit and wrote quickly. ‘I THOUGHT IF I SAVED ONE ENTIRELY INNOCENT PERSON, I COULD BE SURE OF SOME POINT TO MY GREAT FAILURE AND THE MISERY I JUST CAUSED. PERHAPS IT WAS FOOLISH TO RISK WHAT I KNOW NOT GETTING TO YOU, BUT I HAD TO, YOUR MAJESTY. I SIMPLY HAD TO.’

“That’s understandable,” Samantha said softly. She crossed her arms and took a deep breath, regarding the bandaged man soberly. “I’d be careful just what you confess to, if I were you, but it's your choice. In any case, I will not execute you out of hand if you’re going to provide intel, soldier. For starters, it’s time you told me who you are. And why this writing crap? Have you taken a vow of silence? Can you not speak?”

The man was a bit slower in writing, and hesitated one click before finally revealing, his eyes nearly meeting hers as he held up the text. He used two pages. ‘YOU WILL KNOW MY CRIMES INSTANTLY. THERE IS LITTLE POINT IN DENYING. I WAS YOUR MORTAL ENEMY, YOUR MAJESTY. FIRST, WHY I AM DOING THIS IS A FACTOR: ALL FORMS OF RECOGNITION ARE DANGEROUS, AND THE LESS GIVEN TO REALIZATION THE BETTER, ESPECIALLY WHILE I AM WEAK AND VULNERABLE. THIS TENT ISN’T SECURE, AND I’M BEING GROSSLY UNDERESTIMATED.’

Somewhat ominously, his body language one of poise, he turned to the second page and displayed it. ‘MY NAME SHOULD NOT BE SPOKEN, WRITTEN, OR LIKEWISE RIGHT NOW. HIDE ME. THIS I BEG YOU, YOUR MAJESTY, SO THE POTENCY CAN BE LEVERAGED AGAINST THOSE I’VE TURNED AGAINST FOREVER, FOR THEIR BETRAYAL. IF YOU ARE WILLING, OF COURSE. THIS ALL BEING SAID, I WAS YOUR ENEMY’S ARGUABLY GREATEST CHAMPION. I AM THE MAN WHO YOUR REPUTED LOVER, AZURE, FOUGHT IN THE SKY. THE ONE SHE DISARMED. DEFEATED.’

Shock and alarm stabbed Samantha deep in the gut.

Pal-fucking-thennius.
