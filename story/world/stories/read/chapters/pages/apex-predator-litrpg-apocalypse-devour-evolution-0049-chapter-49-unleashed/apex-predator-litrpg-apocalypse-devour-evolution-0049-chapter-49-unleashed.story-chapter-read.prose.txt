Snarlak screeched something in that gutter-language again, like it was casting a curse. Don didn’t care. He was already moving.

The broken bones in his ribs screamed. The burn on his back was still oozing. Blood leaked from one ear. But none of it mattered.

All that mattered was that Don felt alive.

He sprinted across the ice, arms wide, claws gleaming, and laughed. Not a polite chuckle. Not a smirk. A full-bellied, throat-burning laugh that echoed off the cavern walls and made the ice shake.

Snarlak reared back, maybe confused. Maybe scared. Good.

Don leapt onto a stalagmite, kicked off, and launched himself straight at the bastard like a human missile.

“COME ON THEN!” he bellowed.

Snarlak met him midair.

Claws clashed. Stalagmites broke, exploding in every direction. The moment Don hit Snarlak, he activated Drain Life. They crashed into each other in the air and hit the ground together like a meteor strike, shattering the crystal clear ice. Don rolled with the impact and came up grinning, carving into Snarlak’s shoulder with a tight hook. His claws cut into the exoskeleton and Don pulled. Black blood sprayed across the rink.

Snarlak screamed and fired a sonic burst point blank.

Don was faster.

Psychic vacuum. No sound reached his ears. But the force still slammed him backward and into a stalagmite. The ice cracked around his back, and his vision pulsed with red.

[Health at 37%]

[Debuff: Internal bleeding, Fractured bones, Moderate Poisoning]

Don was still laughing.

“Nice one,” Don spat blood. “You hit like my diabetic aunt.”

Snarlak noticed the red tether siphoning life force back to Don. It tried to take a swipe at it. Then it used bile, psychic scream and everything it had in its arsenal. Nothing could break the tether. The boss locked eyes with Don again.

Don raised a finger and wagged it slowly. “Tick, tock.”

Since the boss had only one way to break the psychic tether, it opted for it: and it charged Don again.

Snarlak lunged again. Faster this time. Fluid. The exoskeletal bastard threw a spinning slash, followed by a rising kick that nearly took Don’s head off.

He blocked high, ducked the kick, slid under the next swing and countered with a slash across Snarlak’s thigh. Black blood spurted, and in a moment of inspiration, Don used Life Drain again. He already had the steady stream going. That required constant concentration. But this second addition only lasted for a short burst. But it was intense and it nourished Don.

[Health at 44%]

He could feel the vitality flood his limbs. Still burning, still hurt, but now he was burning forward. Every time Snarlak tagged him, he just stole that life right back.

Snarlak clocked him across the jaw with the back of its knuckles. His head snapped to the side, cracking the neck. Don’s vision blurred. He stumbled, blood dripping from his nose.

[Health at 29%]

He laughed again, teeth red.

“Oh, you’re fun. You are fun.”

Don pressed in again.

He didn’t bother circling. He went head-on.

One claw swipe. Two. A feint. A psychic-boosted lunge that turned into a spinning elbow, slamming into Snarlak’s solar plexus. The exoskeleton cracked under the blow. Snarlak tried to vomit acid, but Don was already sliding sideways on the ice. He snapped a few stalagmites and threw them at Snarlak.

One hit the boss in the neck. The other skidded off the shoulder.

Snarlak grabbed its throat, snarling, gurgling.

Don used the Life Drain again and felt a second tether attach.This time he could hold the burst longer, but it was a massive drain on his Energy. It went down to 28%.

But the burst of life force was great. It kept his bones intact as the fight continued.

Snarlak fired a sonic pulse into the ground to throw him off.

Didn’t work.

Don jumped, flipped mid-air, and came down with both claws pointed straight for the bastard’s head.

Snarlak raised an arm to block, but Don wasn’t aiming for the skull.

He drove both claws straight into the same spot he’d gouged earlier in the gut. Deep. Past exoskeleton. Past sinew. All the way to the heart.

Snarlak screamed, for real this time. Not an attack. Not a tactic.

Just pain.

Just fear.

Don snarled into its face. “Last words?”

Snarlak coughed black sludge. It spoke in its gravely voice.

“What are you?!”

Then Don yanked upward with both hands and tore.

The claws ripped open the monster’s chest in a savage arc. Blood exploded in a steaming spray. Snarlak thrashed once. Twice. Then collapsed on the ice with a wet crunch, wings twitching like dying bugs.

Don rolled off and hit the ground hard.

[Health at 13%]

He lay there, gasping, blood-soaked, bones cracked, ears ringing.

But he was alive.

That sweet, satisfying System message filled his vision.

[You have slain Dungeon Boss: Snarlak, The Black Scream – Level 15]

[You have gained experience for defeating a boss enemy. 150 System Points earned.]

[‘DING!’ Race: [Evolutor (Dominator)] has reached Level 14 – Stat points allocated, +3 Free Attribute Points.]

Don stared at the body. One of Snarlak’s wings twitched once more, then fell still.

He tried to sit up. Failed. He wanted to get up. He had to take a good look at Snarlak.

The damn thing had almost pushed him to his limits. Even though he hadn’t needed the berserk mode from his Ursine Vitality, this had been a hell of a fight.

And boy, did that fight take a toll.

After a second attempt, he managed to roll over and crawl.

He looked down at the torn-open chest, the acid-drenched blood, the shattered exoskeleton. Snarlak had been a magnificent beast. He had no idea what the hell was that evolution thing it had done, but it had made it formidable.

Was this guy an Evolutor too? Or something else?

Maybe it had been an Evolutor, Don figured. But the dungeon seemed to be a homage to real events with real creatures, just like it had been with the Grimoids. Maybe this cave had been some place somewhere, and Snarlak had been the alpha.

But Don beat it. He was stronger.

Don took a moment to examine himself. He had become mad with bloodlust. Don didn’t recoil at it. It wasn’t revolting. Sure it was a new discovery. The thrill of the fight and how it changed him. This new reality was starting to fundamentally change him.

Whether it was a good or a bad thing should be left for the philosophers. Don figured the change was necessary. If he wanted to survive and thrive in this world, he couldn’t be the same Don he had been on earth.

He would have to become so much more. A ruthless predator? If that was what it took.

Don enjoyed himself a lot more in this new reality. He was breathing heavy, eyes closed, mind reeling. The pain was pretty goddamn gravy. But it was earned pain. He had come looking for it and found it. And now he owned it, just like he owned this cave.

The feeling of fighting to absolute death with something as strong as Snarlak. To imagine he would enjoy such a thing.

Somehow I understand Mal’asha a little better now. She’s still a crazy fucker, but at least I’m kind of starting to get it.

Don opened his eyes and looked at Snarlak.

He had shared this experience with the boss. On some strange level, it was an almost intimate feeling.

He didn’t say anything. Didn’t laugh this time.

He just nodded.

Respect.

Then he coughed blood, flopped onto his back, and stared up into the dark. The system told him he would be incapacitated from blood loss in two minutes. That was plenty time. Besides, he still had the Life Drain tether on. He would Devour the beast soon. But now he just wanted a goddamn minute.

He rolled on his back and laughed at the ceiling.

“Best… goddamn fight… I ever had.”
