[New Evolution Acquired: Simian Physique (F-grade)]

Your muscle fibers have adapted to mimic the explosive density and elasticity of simian predators. Though your build remains lean, your raw strength exceeds normal human limits.

+4 Power +2 Agility [Passive]: Increased grip strength, explosive movement potential, and blunt force resilience. Scales With: Power, Agility [Unarmed Fighting].

Don could feel the evolution in his body immediately. There was a slight bubbling and movement under his skin. It looked wild as his muscles spasmed from fingers to toes. There was a tightening. Don actually saw his arms visibly shrink down and become wirier. Like his muscles had wrapped tighter around the bone. Sure, his arms were now skinnier. But they were somehow denser. Stronger for sure. There was a tree nearby. Don just had to.

He sprinted and ran up the tree. He kicked off the trunk and grabbed onto a branch, nine feet off the ground. He hung there one-handed, weightless. Then, with an effortless pull, he swung up and perched like it was nothing. Grinning, he dropped to the ground in a perfect landing.

“Okay,” he said, flexing a fist. “That’s a hell of an upgrade.”

Filled with a new vigor, Don was back on the move. He ran through the forest with effortless grace, and noticed that his Energy expendity was slightly lower too. The Simian Physique had made him more efficient, more graceful.

As he ran, he found that his muscle memory was trying to fight with its old bad habits. Years of wearing shoes and walking in a way that said “I’m civilized, you can co-operate me,” instead of moving in a way that was truly efficient. Hunched forward, loose, confident. Don ran and enjoyed himself.

Don encountered two more Gibbons as he ran. They screeched and charged at him. Don didn’t even slow down. He moved deftly, dodged at the right time and slice through them with his claws one by one. He didn’t even slow down.

[You have slain [Barbtail Gibbon – lvl 6] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 9 System Points earned]

[You have slain [Barbtail Gibbon – lvl 5] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 7 System Points earned]

While the experience felt great Don was unsatisfied with those kills. There wasn’t enough thrill. Not enough challenge. He needed bigger prey. Maybe one of those large bears or another Greytusk Boar.

Don moved through the forest as the sun rose in the sky, and soon he heard human voices. Carefully he climbed a thick-trunked tree and perched on a thick branch. It was so effortless with his Simian Physique, that it barely taxed his Energy.

There were five men, cornering a Blackmaw Grizzly with wooden spears and rock hatchets. The bear roared on its hindlegs and took swipes at the men. The smallest of them stood in the front and blocked a massive swing of a paw with a wooden shield. He was just a young boy and he trembled as the bear attacked. To Don’s surprise the shield glowed faintly when struck and didn’t shatter under the force of the massive bear’s strike.

“Help!” the shield-bearer shouted as he pushed the bear back.

Reluctantly the spearmen approached and poked the bear and darted back. Don watched the struggle. These men were weak. Probably level four or five at best. Brave of them to take on a Grizzly who was level eight. But stupid. Someone was bound to get hurt.

Don considered his options. He could help and steal a part of the experience. But that could invite more trouble. He didn’t know what manner of men these were. So he watched.

The bear roared and struck a terrible overhead blow at the shieldbearer. The shield glowed again, but this time it shattered. The man crumpled like paper under the bear’s attack.

“Idiot,” one of the spearmen muttered, and threw his spear. Its bladed tip set ablaze in the air and penetrated the bear’s throat. It growled and the spear stuck to it. It tried to paw it away, but the men were already striking the bear down.

After the bear was killed, the surviving four men checked the man in an air of nonchalance. The looted him and didn’t so much as look at him.

“Reckless,” one of them muttered.

“Good thing we didn’t need to share the experience with him.”

“Sure, but now we are down a tank. What are we going to tell the boss?

“Nothing if he don’t ask.”

“He will.”

“Shut up. Figure something out then.”

“Boss is hunting. Just return to camp and get another of the captives and train him as a meat shield.”

“Good thinking.”

Don nodded to himself. Good thing he hadn’t revealed himself. These were certainly not nice men. In a fashion they had adapted to the System’s, but they didn’t understand. It was not their job to cull the weak. It was the System’s. They were discarding resources. Seemed like they were capturing people and using them as monster bait.

Don wanted nothing to do with that. He was no hero who would go in and save the captives. But neither would he want to trade or co-operate with these thugs. What he wanted was to find more monsters and level up, so could fight Blazeclaw. If these guys were about to leave the area, Don might find more bears.

After the men left, Don went and used Devour on the bear. He felt a little guilty draining the bear since it wasn’t his kill, but he’d be damned if he let his pride and honor and whatnot get in the way of acquiring resources. The difference between life and death in this world could be a hair’s breadth, and he’d rather be alive than noble.

His evolution points were racking up. Don checked his evolution point interface and noted that upgrading Simian Physique would cost 100 points. He had enough for it. It was just a question of whether it was worth it or not. He could also upgrade his claws, which were his primary weapons. That upgrade would cost 200, which he could reach today, if he found prey.

But if I am going to up the ante and hunt for bigger game, I’m better off getting an upgrade. The Simian Physique is a good trait.

Don decided to be decisive and he bought the upgrade.

He gasped as his muscles got even leaner and wirier, wrapping around his bone really tightly. He felt a surge of power as he flexed his hand. His body felt light, agile, powerful. He was now at a completely superhuman level of physical ability. Don grinned. He couldn’t wait to test his body out in a tough fight.

Don’s Survival Instincts flashed a warning that grounded him back into reality. It wasn’t a terrible flaring blare like it had been when the Hunter had chased him and the kids. It was more of a ‘be alert’. To his surprise Don found that the four men had returned to the forest opening.

“Who’s you?” the largest of them said and stepped forward, spear leveled at Don. The other three started circling to the sides in crouched stances.

Don shrugged. “Just a guy. I don’t want any trouble.”

“The fuck you did with the bear?”

Don glanced at the bear. After he Devoured it, the bear’s corpse had shrunk down and the dark coarse fur of the beast had slightly whitened.

“Look,” Don said. “You guys are busy. I will just be on my way. The forest is big enough for all of us to hunt. Goodbye.”

“I don’t think so,” the spearman said. “There’s three of us and one of you, skinny boy.”

Don shook his head. He gazed down at the thug. He was a scruffy man with a thick red beard and bald head. The look in his eyes told Don that this man was ready for violence. Hell, as far as Don could tell, this man enjoyed violence. But Don saw nothing but a small puppy yipping at him.

“I am giving you one chance, and one chance only,” Don said quietly and deliberately as he stared the bearded man down. “Leave me alone.”

“Skinny boy like you with no weapons, no friends,” the bearded man said, licking his lips as he approached. “Shouldn’t make demands. Tell you what. Come with us quiet, and we let you live. You’ll be our prisoner, but we treat those well who serve.”

Don snorted.

The bearded thug frowned. “Something funny, boy?”

Don just smiled. The thought of being a servant to low scum like this was absurd.

“It’s nothing.”

Don crouched down. Through his Survival Instincts he was vaguely aware of the positions of the other two thugs. His mind was still, his breath was steady. If they chose to be stupid, he was ready.

“If you attack me,” Don said, still quietly and slowly. “I will kill you all.”

The red-bearded man only grinned. Immediately Don’s instincts flared up.
