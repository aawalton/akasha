“The Wall Forest has plenty of creatures that try to invade this way. Beast tides are not that rare, and while before we used monster-repellent due to our lack of strength, now we are allowing them to come, and we clean them up. It’s a good way of earning Experience Points.”

They have thoroughly cleaned up the few parasitic bothers I had around my roots. I feel super refreshed without that constant itch on my roots.”

Ignatia smiled happily. It was her utmost joy to hear praise for her children, even better than when someone praised her.

While the ants prepared, Flor and the others stood on the 30-meter-tall walls surrounding Fortuna City. Fortuna City looked much more conservative and tame than the super city that Golden Sap City represented. The buildings were not as tall, and they were made out of a mix of wooden and rock-like materials. It was clearly constructed according to the usual standard across all continents, a mix of civilization and nature. The streets were also covered with rock slabs, and a complex sewage system flowed under it, passing through a few purifiers before releasing the water back to nearby rivers a few kilometers away from the city.

It was one of the most commonly built World Tree cities across the world. Of course, with a Level 50 World Tree at the center, the attraction that the city had was undeniable. Having the ability to own property was something that Golden Sap City residents lacked. Hence, some preferred living in the cities on the periphery instead of the grand urban center near Liu Shu. Moreover, the major buffs that Liu Shu shared with her population reached here as well, so the reasons to move to the central city were both opportunity and ambition.

For those content, Fortuna and the other two cities were more attractive.

Speaking of Fortuna City also couldn’t be done without mentioning Cinderielle’s parents! The couple had been living here for the last three decades, and they also knew a few things about their child. They were aware that somehow, their cute little Cinder managed to enter into a relationship with the World Tree that had made so much noise in the last decades.

“... Cinder, are you sure you are not being bullied?”

After all, in her father’s eyes, she was not someone so remarkable that a World Tree would set their eyes on her. In his mind, only those heirs of noble families had the privilege of even speaking to World Trees, not to mention entering a romantic relationship with them.

“Dad, I told you that I am not young already, right? I’ve reached Level 50 more than a year ago! Don’t worry, okay. I am not being bullied…” Cinderielle then thought to herself. ‘More than being bullied, I am the one doing the bullying when we are together.’ Her eyes flashed with a hint of amusement. ‘Seeing Tiantian squirm in my arms and call my name with her sweet and sticky tone is truly stimulating~. Sometimes, I barely have the ability to hold on to my mind…’

Cinderielle was quite surprised with herself in that regard. After all, who knew that she could hold back from going all the way for such a long time when having the attractive World Tree in her arms? Of course, while her lust for Liu Shu was overflowing, her love was just leagues stronger, allowing her to control her evil thoughts rather easily.

Mama Cinder sat on the side with a soft smile. Unlike Cinderielle’s father, who had signs of aging, her mother still looked as young as 20 years ago. This quite clearly showed the difference in aging between species.

“Cinder, when will I be able to hold my grandchildren?”

Cinderielle almost choked to death. She had come to her parents' house to warn them about the war and suggest that they move to Golden Sap City for a few days. However, to her surprise, both of them shook their heads calmly and changed the subject to her relationship with Liu Shu as naturally as breathing.
