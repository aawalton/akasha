I’m gonna kill that severed head, Asher thought, suddenly finding himself completely entombed within the earth. Whatever monster had grabbed him after he’d finished off the last one, its touch had somehow made him intangible, almost like how he could walk through solid objects within the astral. However, rather than simply erasing his body when he was made solid again underground, it seemed this power worked the other way around. The dense dirt his body had shared a space with had vanished instead, leaving him alive and in one piece, but completely trapped.

Of course, that wasn’t really a big deal for Asher. Even if he was surrounded by too much dirt to phase through with Astral Dip, he had an even easier method of getting back to the surface. Focusing on his Eternal Mark, he Recalled all of a dozen feet, appearing beside Smiles once more.

“Oh, you’re back! It’s an—”

“An ambush, I got it,” Asher snapped, keeping his daggers at the ready as he watched his surroundings. Now that he knew what to expect, he was more than prepared to stab straight through a set of ghostly hands should they pop out of the ground and try to grab his ankles again. “You know you are really annoying.”

“Well that’s just rude. And here I was going to tell you about Fade!”

“Fade? So he’s the one that tried to drag me to my death?”

“Yep! Marrow was the bone construct you destroyed, but Fade is pretty different, even for us Servants. Mainly just drags his opponents down into the ground and lets them suffocate. Kinda boring, if you ask me, but pretty efficient. He’s incorporeal, so unless your weapon has a specific enchantment on it, you can’t even hurt him.”

“If you knew all that, why didn’t you tell me earlier when I asked?”

“Dunno. Didn’t feel like it, I guess.”

“You are just the… You know what, never mind, let me wrap this up really quick before we get into it.” Seeing as Fade wasn’t coming out of the ground, Asher could only imagine he was somewhere beneath him, waiting for him to drop his guard so he could try pulling him under again. The move was more annoying than anything, but Asher didn’t want to keep wasting energy Recalling out of the ground over and over again, so killing Fade would be the smart thing to do. With that in mind, he activated Spatial Lock, creating a twenty-foot-wide bubble centered around his current position. Ensuring it formed a perfect sphere, extending even into the ground beneath him, he mentally altered the rules for what could pass through the bubble very carefully.

Dirt, stone, and whatever random minerals one might find in a ground devoid of all life, was free to pass through the barrier. Everything else?

It was coming along for the ride.

Raising himself up off the ground with his boots of Levitation, Asher focused and began gradually decreasing the size of his Spatial Lock. While he could adjust the size of his barrier after forming it, doing so took a lot of concentration and it was pretty slow going. Still, he had nothing but time, and he didn’t know how else to fish the strange, ghostly monster out of the ground.

It took him a couple of minutes to shrink the larger bubble down enough to finally catch a glimpse of what he was fishing for, but eventually, his plan paid off. When there was only a few feet of dirt still left within his barrier, an ethereal man shot out of the ground, flying up toward Asher with anger on his face and an unearthly moan coming from his hanging mouth. With a grin, Asher expanded his living wood dagger and cleaved straight through Fade from head to toe, cutting the ghost in half with practically no effort. As Fade appropriately faded away with one last moan, the two halves shimmering and vanishing before him, Asher nodded and dropped his barrier before lowering himself back to the ground.
