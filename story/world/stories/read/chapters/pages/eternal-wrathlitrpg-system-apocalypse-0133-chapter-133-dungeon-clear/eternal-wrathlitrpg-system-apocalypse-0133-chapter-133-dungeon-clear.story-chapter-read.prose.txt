James slashed and slashed, no longer caring about things such as sword skill or axe skill. Sometimes they activated; sometimes he just plunged his blade deep into their chests. He fought like a barbarian, roaring as he cleaved other dogheads and jackals with his sword.

After three hours of gruesome fighting, the last of the enemy fell to a golden arrow from an archer.

They had won. It was unbelievable, but they had won. Andrew glanced at the remaining survivors— not a flawless victory for sure, but they had won this war. Andrew said his prayer, relishing the hard-earned victory. The system messaged them the final report of this battle.

[You have cleared the last wave. Casualties: 30 Knights and 15 Survivors. Time elapsed: 124 / 120 hours. Congratulations, you have triumphed. You will be awarded 2000 coins and 10 merit. Further prizes will be given by the count.]

“Shit. I lost half of my men. This was horrible,” Andrew spat. As expected from a medium dungeon, the difficulty was high. Andrew prided himself on clearing the novice dungeon without casualties, but once he got into the medium dungeon, he got party-wiped, and only on the second run did he succeed in clearing it. It was no different now. He still suffered enormous casualties.

Count Bartolomew approached the team on top of his horse and inspected the battlefield.

“Thank you, survivors. Thanks to your valiant effort, this city has survived.” Count Bartolomew bowed. The remaining knights, seemingly unable to feel fatigue, knelt before him.

“I didn’t think you would make it. I miscalculated — the bulk of their army was here. Alas, you have succeeded. You have my thanks. It must have been an arduous task defending this wall. Moreover, you have saved half of my knights. For this, I thank you. The citizens of my county will forever remember your names, brave survivors.” The count bowed again.

“So, what’s the reward? Get on with it. Stop wasting our time. I want to go home already,” Xiaoming scoffed. He and Bernard had lost most of their men. James rallied his volunteers to him. He too had lost his men. This battle left a bitter taste in Andrew’s mouth.

“Of course. I shall not bore you with long-winded pleasantries. Here are treasures from my personal vault. May they prove useful in your future endeavors.” Four citizens brought a treasure chest before Count Bartolomew, and the chest soon disappeared into motes of light.

[Congratulations, you have received an M16 gun with an under-barrel grenade launcher. A 40×46 mm low-velocity grenade has been unlocked in the shop.]

“Andrew, what did you receive?” James asked as he slung the new gun he had received.

“Steyr IWS 2000. Basically an anti-materiel sniper,” he answered. James sighed at the incredulous reward the system gave them. Here, the men fought with swords and bows while the count gave them guns as rewards. Moreover, these were all modern guns, unlike what he had received from the system before.

“You’ve got an automatic rifle there,” Andrew said, looking at the gun James had received. An M16 could hold 30 bullets in its magazine, and its firing mode could be manually adjusted for semi-automatic or automatic.

“Yeah, this might be powerful, but I would go bankrupt if I used this. A bullet costs 10 coins per round. That means 300 coins just to fire a single magazine,” James answered. Tough enemies would need a few bullets to be taken down.

“If we have this, there would be lesser casualties.” Andrew frowned. The prize always came late.

“Farewell, brave heroes. May your journey be full of vigor. We will forever remember your heroic deeds. Let it be known that Count Bartolomew had owed you a favor.”

A portal rose up beside the count. The count and the knights then returned to his keep, and villagers poured out from it, working hard to fix the damaged castle.

“There are still more floors? How many floors are there in this dungeon!” Xiaoming complained to the system, and it dutifully answered.

[There are two floors in this dungeon.]

“Oh. Is the last one the dungeon core room?”

[Information classified.]

“Pft!” James chuckled as the system went silent on him.

“Let’s go, everyone. Time to clear this dungeon,” Andrew said as they went through the portal. The world turned white. After the loading bar was full, they were teleported into an empty room, a spherical dungeon core floating in the middle. Streams of white light wound around it.

Andrew walked to the core, his steps light as if he were skipping. With this, the dungeon was over. But as his hand reached for it, a white barrier flashed from the dungeon core.

“AARGH!!” Andrew screamed in pain as his hand was vaporized by the energy barrier. Hou Yi and Sanders rushed toward him, dragging him back away from the core.

James was quick with his hands. He drew his bow, and a golden light flashed from his arrow. It drew a straight line toward the dungeon core, but a purple barrier repelled it.

Then purple lines, reminiscent of a magic circle, were carved onto the ground. There, a creature came forth, bearing the head of a jackal and the head of a bull, with the body of a cow and the legs of a dog. It growled toward the group. James’ mind raced back to the dungeon. What did he miss? Why was the dungeon getting violent?

“Everyone, arm yourselves. This is going to be a boss fight!” James rallied. Andrew stood up, groaning, his mind reeling at the scene before him. Why was the dungeon getting violent? He had read the reports, but he thought James had done something wrong in his exploration—that’s why the core spawned a boss monster on him. He never thought it would happen to his team.

“Fuck! Everyone! Let’s beat this thing once and for all! Pay attention to the dungeon core, we need it!” Andrew commanded.

“Forget it, Andrew. According to my experience, this boss is not something you can beat while holding back. Find the core and destroy it,” James said.

“Don’t! All of our work so far will be in vain!” Andrew said.

The dungeon core fused itself into the chimera, nesting inside its heart. Then, from its dog’s mouth, a fiery flame burst out, enveloping James and the survivors behind him.

“Arghh!! Help!” Screams and chaos enveloped the room. In such a cramped space, there was no escape. The fire engulfed the room; only James, who had some resistance against fire, was safe.

James created several ice walls around himself and lunged toward the chimera. The survivors rushed toward the wall, groaning as the pain from the burns stung their skin. Hou Yi and Andrew began healing the wounded. Using his unique skill, Andrew tried to salvage the team by moving the wound into another, plundering their bodies.

After the fire baptism, the bull head opened its mouth and a green cloud of gas came out of it. James didn’t stop; his sword had already gone for the jackal’s head, severing it.

[Corrosion damage level 2.]

It was only for a brief moment, but James could feel his body melting from the acid cloud spewed by the bull’s mouth. It was only thanks to his ignited firefur that the damage didn’t spread further.

“Move! The acid cloud is coming this way! Anyone with ice magic, create a dome! Hurry! Sanders, give me a spear! Move with me—you will attack it from range. Delila, shoot it with my rifle!” Andrew shouted. He then held out his gem bearing the mark of Mystis and launched his spear at the creature. The spear jabbed it in the gut, skewering it, but it showed no sign of slowing down.

The creature raised its body; its back legs transformed into those of a minotaur. It towered over Andrew by over 12 feet. It was a giant. Its front legs grew longer, with a lavish curved blade as its claw. It roared, then lunged at Andrew, its claw sweeping down on him like a sickle reaping grass on a field.

Andrew activated his spear skill and lunged toward the boss. He might not be as strong as James, but he was no beginner inside a dungeon. Even when the boss monster threatened to rip him apart, even when his friends died beside him, he would not freeze. He came here with a purpose.

His spear pierced the ox’s head through its eye, and the beast stood frozen before its body fell on the metallic floor. James had never seen Andrew fight as a frontliner, so he was surprised to see Andrew manage a kill in a frontal assault.

Alas, the boss was not done with them. Not yet. Even with a spear lodged into its head, the core in its chest kept it alive. The beast stood up like a zombie. Its arms flailed, the claws threatening to slice anything in its path. James fired his mind magic at it, but it had no effect. The dungeon core had prevented it from feeling pain, and with all of its heads gone, it couldn’t see any illusion either.

“Shit! How is it still alive?” Andrew cursed.

“Andrew! Here!” Sanders threw a spear at him as he prepared another fire spell to roast the bull. But before he could fire his spell, James lunged, his sword glowing gold.

Andrew also took another plunge, this time toward the dungeon core at the center. The golden glow drew an arc across the beast’s arm, chopping it off as it tried to pierce Andrew’s flank.

Andrew’s weapon skill hit home, and his spear lodged itself below the core. With a bit of leverage, the core was pried off from the boss’s body. It was perfect teamwork. Alas, the core came loose. Andrew tried to reach for it, but it slipped out from his finger and fell to the ground, shattering.

Without the dungeon core, the creature lost its energy. Soon, it melted into a puddle of unrecognizable goo. James sighed at the melted body before him. The dungeon had done its best to prevent James from plundering the boss’s body.

[You have completed Dungeon AU 52. Congratulations. You have leveled up to level 61. Your Attacker class has leveled to level 45. You have received 1500 coins. You and 15 other survivors have triumphed. You have received the Wing of Icarus. Please touch the golden teleportation point to return to the real world.]

[Wing of Icarus:A wing made by the son of a legendary blacksmith. Once used, the wing will bond with the user, unable to be removed. It allows the user to fly in the sky; nevertheless, the wing has a major flaw besides needing mana to operate: it can only function for 5 seconds.]

James glanced at the shattered core with a frown. Andrew knelt beside it, crestfallen, his hand clenched at the life he had lost trying to obtain it. All of that sacrifice—and it was for nothing. The core was shattered. Alas, even with a pyrrhic victory, the dungeon had been cleared. James was satisfied with the result, as he had obtained a new gear piece to augment his body.
