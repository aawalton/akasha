She had promised to work out a solution before walking away and leaving me to enjoy my trip across the station to a huge building with ‘The Lost Mackenzie’ over the doorway. A big smile appeared on my face as I looked through the doors, almost able to see my baby waiting for me.

“I’m home” I whispered, before taking a step and finding I had covered the entire distance and vanished through the doors into a reception hall. I heard someone speak behind me in shock.

“Where did she go? Is teleportation a thing now?”

“She went inside, and no, we can’t teleport” Itshu Biyu responded calmly.

I ignored the rest and walked through the reception area into the museum after having to pay a donation fee. The moment I walked into the museum exhibits, I felt like I was walking through my childhood.

My excitement quickly dulled as I continued following the path through my life. I giggled softly when I saw a ‘fundamentals of aerodynamics,’ that was right next to a ‘flight theory and aerodynamics’ textbook.

I let out a sigh when I spotted a few ‘aerodynamics for engineering students’ books nearby. And began to cover half my face in embarrassment when I turned the corner, and found myself walking through a library of every book I had ever read, from fiction to educational textbooks.

“Someone has gone a bit far with this exhibit on ‘my younger years.’” I muttered, feeling embarrassed.

I quickly hurried through it and into an open area where they had the first plane I had flown sitting in the middle of the room. I circled it a couple of times, and a small smile appeared on my face when I saw the tail number match.

“Did they rebuild this thing?” I asked myself, knowing that the plane had been left in a desert storage yard, before parts of it had been sold or recycled.

I circled it again before slowly walking away from it, heading for the next exhibit. As I left, I could hear muffled talking coming from the library section and felt a bit of embarrassment creep up again.

“Hopefully, no one knew about those books that I read.” I said as I glanced back nervously. I decided not to go back in case it drew attention to them or someone suspected there may be something missing.

The next room had posters and television screens showing small clips on what the posters were showing. I quickly covered my face again as I walked through an exhibit on all the shows, movies, anime, and cartoons I had ever watched.

“How the hell do they know what I watched and didn’t watch?” I groaned before I spotted a small sign on the wall near the exit. ‘All exhibits have been fact-checked and cross-referenced through streaming services and purchases, ensuring the accuracy of the exhibit.’ “Figures” I groaned, as I thought about how death negates all rights to privacy.

A moment later, I had exited that exhibit, and found myself staring at a model size display of the university I had gone to. I frowned at it before moving onto one of my dormitories. It was complete with a small figure of Marvin, running across the grass towards a parking lot, that had a small model of Alison and I, hiding behind a car.

I winced when I saw the next one showing only the parking lot and ambulance was parked nearby, a small figure of myself with red pain all over the side of a nearby car while Alison held me. “That looks like an over exaggeration.” I mumbled.

Deciding I didn’t want to see how the rest of the models went, I walked to the exit and headed into the next exhibit. My face lit up a moment later when I spotted my baby sitting in the middle of the room. I let out a squeal of delight and rushed forwards, only for a security guard to step away from a corner where I had not noticed him.

“Stop, no one may touch the exhibit, please step away,” He told me.

I turned to look at him, as I spotted the two that had not come out of the corner, they had playing cards in their hands, and were watching me, before returning my gaze to the one that spoke.

“What if the owner came by?” I asked curiously.

“The Museum is the owner,” he replied calmly before waving me to continue walking, but I didn’t.

“The museum is a thief then” I replied as I narrowed my eyes.

I saw him roll his eyes before letting out a sigh. I instantly thought that others must have made the same comment before. His hand was waving at a sign that sat next to the car.

I walked over to it and read it out loud. “This vehicle is the property of The Lost Mackenzie Museum, when Kenna Mackenzie went missing it was stored aboard the station, waiting for her return. However, as the years rolled by, and she did not come back after a thousand years. Her father and our current Clan Mistress decided to sell the vehicle legally to the museum so that it may be maintained and kept in perfect condition as a tribute to Kenna's great sacrifice.”

I let out a pained groan as I turned to stare longingly at my car. I considered launching myself at it and trying to take it anyway. But the doors were too small to drive it out. Plus, I knew if I stored it in my ring, then I would never make it out on foot, before the entire station came down on me.

I turned away slowly as I vowed to find a way to drive my car again. I exited the room, glaring at the security guards as they watched me go before returning to their game of cards.

“Bastards, how dare they legally keep my baby from me,” I growled. I turned, a moment later, to see the skiff I had floated around the solar system in, sitting in the middle of the room staring at me.

I sat there staring at the skiff for who knows how long, before I realised what it represented. I was about to walk towards it when I noticed another group of security guards staring at me holding cards in their hands. I closed my eyes for a moment and thought about how much I was coming to hate this place.

I turned on my heel when I pulled myself back together and marched through the rest of the museum, ignoring the exhibits, even the holographic one that had embellished my order to evacuate the ship before launching myself out in a life pod. And marched out of the museum to see everyone waiting outside. They hadn't entered and had waited as if this was something they had decided I needed to do on my own.

“Itshu Biyu, how many ships can I call to aid me in a crusade?” I asked as I approached the group. The station group looked mortified the moment I asked, like I was planning to raid their homes. Itshu Biyu on the other hand frowned before rolling her shoulders slowly.

“I am not sure, Commander. I would need to touch base with the relays and connect to the sect and see what has changed in our absence before I could find out who would be willing to fly their ships into a dead dimension.”

“Do it, I have been insulted by the corrupt cultivators in this dimension too much, it is time they learnt the heavy hand of law.” I growled before turning to look at the museum, feeling a pain at having to leave my baby behind again.

It was a second later that something I had seen in the museum clicked in my mind and I use my movement technique, vanishing from the spot I had stood. I rushed back into the museum and through it to the exhibit that had shown a scroll that I had registered but dismissed as I rushed towards the exit.

For a minute, I stood there staring through the glass at a scroll that I had seen in my father house when I was young, and just assumed it was some sort of old scroll that my had been passed down through the generations. I had never even been able to read what it said, but now I could.

“Time and Space Becoming a God Technique”

My hand moved before I realised what it had done. It smashed through the glass and held the scroll to my forehead, allowing me to absorb all the knowledge before the alarm had even had the chance to go off from the glass being broken.

My eyes opened wide as I quickly hid one of the most valuable scrolls in the dimension in my ring. The technique wasn't just a bit of information about ascending, it was everything from Nascent through Immortal to Godhood. It was a guide on becoming a God!

I used my movement technique to escape the museum before the guards got there and arrived a moment later in front of Itshu Biyu. Grabbing her arm as I continued to march back towards where the taxi had dropped us off.

”We need to leave, right now!” I said, a moment before the museum's alarm started blaring behind me. The cultivators moved to the taxi before the mortals had a chance to have their attention drawn to the museum.

We got into the taxi and I smiled softly to see our driver had waited for us. I asked him to take us back to our hangar. A moment later, as the mortals turned away from the museum to look at the taxi, the driver had pulled out into the traffic and begun to drive away.

”Kenna, what is going on?” Itshu Biyu asked as she looked out the back window for a moment before turning to look at me.

“I found something that I can’t speak of until we are back on the ship, that place has no right to claim it, and clearly can’t protect it.” I said as I scanned the surrounding area for hostiles.

The Law Department Disciples instantly started scanning the surroundings, and we patiently waited for the taxi to drive across the station until it pulled over exactly where it had picked us up in the hangar district.

I was a little surprised but hid it when we all came to an unspoken agreement to use our movement techniques where possible to rush back through the gates to our ship.

We managed to make it through, however as I crossed the hangar towards my ship I saw a few of the guards' heads turned slightly like they were reading something before all their heads snapped towards us and began running across the deck towards me.

“Okay, really time to go” I shouted as I activated my movement technique.

The moment we were all through the airlock, I quickly activated the ship's lockdown to keep the mortals out of the ship. Before I turned my attention to the ring on my finger, making sure the scroll was indeed still where I had placed it.

A second later, I was racing down the hallway to the bridge. I knew we had to get off this station as fast as possible and retreat into hiding before deciding what to do next. I was halfway to the bridge when I noticed Itshu Biyu keeping up with me. But her eyes were drilling a hole into me, like she was demanding answers.

“What would you do if you saw a scroll with the word God on it?” I decided to ask instead of telling her what I found.

“If the person who had it couldn't defend it, I would kill them and everyone that knew about it, possibly the entire planet if it's weak, before taking it and going into hiding. If I were unable to use it, then I would use the sect’s secure auction house to sell it, why… Wait, you didn't… Did you?”
