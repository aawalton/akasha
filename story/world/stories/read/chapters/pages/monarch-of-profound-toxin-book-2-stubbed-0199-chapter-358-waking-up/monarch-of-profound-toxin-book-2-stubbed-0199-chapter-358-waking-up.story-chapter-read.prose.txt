Chapter 358

It was dark.

“Ihasu?” Eik whispered out loud, his voice as hoarse as two handfuls of pebbles rubbed against each other. “What time is it? I can’t… I can’t see anything.” Considering how good his eyes had gotten at the higher ranks of power, not to mention X-rank, usually it would hardly make a difference for his ability to see whether it was the deepest of black nights or a cloudless lunchtime picnic.

So why the hell couldn’t he see anything now?

Groggily, he reached out with a hand to search for her next to him when she didn’t offer an answer. But instead of the softness of her duvet, the smoothness of her hair, or the suppleness of her skin, his palm touched cold, hard stone.

What?

He sat up, but when he did, a terrible pain shot through his stomach and sent the muscles into a spasm. He groaned and touched the skin. There was a hole in his stomach.

A hole? Like a lightning strike, memory suddenly flushed into his head as if a valve had been turned from closed to fully open in one, quick pull.

Instantly, Eik’s aura erupted outward at full force, colliding palpably with the darkness. So, as expected, this was not just any natural darkness, but something created. It was thick and viscous. Not so much an absence of light as the presence of a deep black that seemed to flow through the world and cast it in nothingness.

Eik felt the source of the shadows pouring power directly into the substance, powering it further to resist the dismantling effect of the human’s aura.

The Lord of the Moon. He was here.

Forcefully contained as it was, the potency of Eik’s aura kept growing more and more intense as it filled the small space densely. Although his injury remained critical, however long the period of unconsciousness had been, it had done a lot of good for his natural regeneration and now allowed him to unleash a continuous burst of energy that would have spread for kilometer after kilometer if it had been allowed to expand.

Within a few seconds, the darkness around him began to seriously vibrate as it threatened to shatter apart. Eik didn’t let up for even a moment.

Another two seconds and the Lord of the Moon decided to drop the ability rather than waste any more power on its maintenance when Eik would have broken it moments later.

His keen eyes suddenly able to see again, Eik blinked against an admittedly dim light. It would seem that they were in a cave, curving rock wall and ceiling creating a sense of claustrophobia. The ceiling was barely high enough to allow the Lord to stand up straight inside, and one of the spawn accompanying him had to bend over at the hip in order to fit, its gangly, skeletal frame measuring nearly six meters in height.

“Hello… a-again,” the Lord of the Moon spoke hoarsely. “I wa—”

“Shut the fuck up, you fugly piece of shit!” Eik spat, refusing to let the bastard finish the sentence.

Seemingly unaffected by the rudeness, the Lord simply stared silently, although the spawn did not take it quite as well, visibly tensing at the hostile tone. Eik stared them down defiantly. Sure, they were incredibly strong. For S-rankers.

Eik could kill them without getting up from the slab. Not that he couldn’t get up. No shackles in the world could have held him and the Lord of the Moon had probably thought it meaningless to even try. Instead, that black, squiggly, parasitic mass was securely attached to the flesh of his hands and up his arms. It wasn’t actually eating away at him just yet, but he could feel its hooks in him.

Crescent head laid on his shoulder, it was with a strange look of wonder that the Lord of the Moon twirled his fingers in a silent order to the parasitic mass on Eik’s arms. In an instant reaction, pain flared hotly.

Now familiar with the way to get rid of it, Eik practically roasted it off, but the Lord of the Moon had more where it came from, pouring swarms of it over Eik from what he feared was a practically endless supply. There was simply no way to get rid of the carnivorous substance quickly enough to keep up with the reapplication.

It was a losing game.

Eik hated the thought, but there was no way around it. Currently, he was weaker than the Worldbreaker enemy. Realistically, he knew that this six-armed abomination wasn’t capable of showing anywhere near the power he must have been able to wield in the heyday of the Worldbreakers, but he didn’t really need to.

While technically a Worldbreaker himself, Eik had only just come into his own with the power. He didn’t doubt that there were aspects of the identity that still lay dark to him. Heights he could not even fathom.

Damnation. He was missing one of his greatest sources of power. One which had helped him surpass all others and ascend to peaks of might thought to be reserved for myths and fiction.

He needed his alchemy back.

From the first moment he had stood face to face with the Lord of the Moon in the flesh, Profound Toxin had been throwing itself violently against the inner walls of his soul, fighting desperately to get out and go at him. Eik had thought they were past that kind of behavior, but the direct encounter with an ancient enemy like this appeared to have robbed his friend of any sense of reason and self-control.

Profound Toxin was in a frenzy, and Eik was beginning to feel his own mind slip into madness along with it. One would think that Noble Mentality, which allowed him to shrug off nearly any mental attack directed at him with little difficulty, would do something against that as well.

Unfortunately, it would seem that Profound Toxin’s mental influence was considered neither an attack nor as foreign since it was happening entirely within his spirit by an entity that more or less shared a soul with him.

“So, what?” Eik began, trying to distract his own mind from the aggressive passenger. “You leave your servant to fight me while you flee with your tail between your legs, only to return when I’m weakened to abduct me?”

A deep rumble bubbled up from the Lord of the Moon’s throat, but there didn’t seem to be any anger in it.

“Are you that afraid of a fair fight, you coward? Do you only dare to face me when I’m hurt?” Eik mocked. “What do you want?”

As if he couldn’t quite get the air to come out in the form of words, the Lord of the Moon gasped repeatedly before finally managing to speak. “I-I… I want to… break you, and then consume your-your power.”

How honest...

At those words, the true purpose of the parasitic mass became clear. It was not just draining him of vitality, but delivering that vitality straight to the Lord of the Moon. Leaving just became a whole lot more urgent.

“Fuck you!” Eik snarled and flipped the Worldbreaker the hardest bird of his life. “This finger means fuck you!”

The moment Eik’s energy began to churn in response to his activation of a skill, the Lord of the Moon shifted and exploded forward. There was no more than a few meters between him and Eik but it was still too late by the time he reacted.

Eik vanished.

Without wasting a single beat, the Lord looked inward, seeking the natural tracking capability of his parasites. But it came up with nothing. He checked again. Still nothing.

For a few seconds he just stared at the spot from which Eik had disappeared, leaving no trace. So long as the parasites had a hold of a victim, he should have been able to tell where they were no matter where in the Unified Mass they went.

For a third time he checked. The spawn around the small cave watched him warily as he stared silently at nothing, eyes far away. Abruptly, he howled, dark aura flooding the rocky room, forcing his minions to their knees. Lashing out in sheer frustration, he took the heads off the three spawn on his right, and the energy bursts released by the swing of his arm sent the side of the mountain that hosted the cave erupting into the sky at an angle like a sudden volcanic eruption.

His aura-boosted roar of anger echoed throughout the world like a doomsday siren, blaring across plains, forests, mountains, and seas. It heralded the end of all.

***

Like a frame change in a movie, Eik’s vision blinked from the image of a small, barely lit cave, the ugly face of the Lord of the Moon staring down at him, to blue-tinted, churning clouds far above. They almost seemed like a safe, protective blanket.

The parasitic infestation still gnawed away at his body vigorously. The Lord of the Moon couldn’t track him through that, could he? Well, better not take the chance.

Mercilessly, he eradicated the squirming substance with Profound Toxin, thousands of tiny Accelerant explosions assisting the hasty obliteration.

Eik’s head lulled slightly as he looked around the spot where he had landed. It appeared to be pretty close to the little meadow where Profound Toxin had built his twin of Eik’s house and garden. If he could just… make it there, then he could take a little break before going home.

Yeah, that… that was perfect.

The first step felt pretty heavy, but he was basically halfway there already.

Just a couple of more… ste—

Before he could realize what was happening, everything darkened again as the ground suddenly approached at speed and opened up like a maw to swallow him whole.

***

His own groan was the first thing Eik became aware of.

Next to enter his consciousness was scent. Something smelled good.

Then came speech, he supposed, because he spoke aloud before he could even consider his words. “Are you… eating without Daddy?” He was surprised at how grainy his own voice sounded. It was like he was under water.

“Eik!” Ihasu’s voice sounded in the distance. He tried to open his eyes but it was like they were locked tight with superglue. “Eik!” This time she was shouting. He tried to reach out toward her. To touch her. But he couldn’t tell if anything was happening.

“Daddy!” It was Bin. And a second later, it was repeated by Goo. His kids were there? But how terrible must he have looked.

Shocking even himself, his eyes suddenly shot open as his body kicked into gear all at once. He practically flew out of bed, slamming his head into one of the ceiling beams above and taking out a huge chunk of wood as easily as if it had been whipped cream.

He would have to fix that later, was all he could think about as he landed flat on his stomach on the floor. Multiple hands grabbed him by the arms and shoulders as he was hauled to his feet.

“I’m okay, I’m okay!” he insisted as he tried to walk forward only to literally step through his own couch by accident, cracking it cleanly in half. It took him a moment to realize what had happened. “I need to sit down,” he concluded finally.

Helped into a sitting position, he looked up at the gathered people.

Ihasu, the kids, his dad, Oli, Robert, and Michael were all there, looking down on his with faces painted in the colors of concern. “How did you get here?” he asked.

Rasmus put a hand on his forehead to check for a fever, deep grooves in his brow as he studied his son. “We’ve been here from the beginning, Eik,” he said. “You’re the one who came back here.”

“I’m home? Since when?”

“Since a little over a day ago,” his dad revealed.

“A day? I’ve been out for a whole day?”

“You should have seen yourself!” Rasmus said with a mix of worry and frustration. “You were half dead when you suddenly flopped onto the dinner table without warning! It’s a wonder you were only out for a day!”

No, Eik was X-rank. A day of unconsciousness was a big deal. There was no time to waste.

No, actually, before that… “What did you see?” he asked his two children.

Goo looked briefly up at Bin before she answered. “All of it?”

Eik let his head fall into his palms with a groan. Great. Exactly what he had wanted to avoid. Way to go…

Shaking it off, his eyes found Robert. “I need you to find me Meri and Guild Master Ganyak hir Gawak,” he commanded. “Tell them to come right now.”

Recognizing the urgency in his tone, Robert didn’t ask questions and simply turned to leave. “Actually, wait a second,” Eik added. “the guild master is one of my minions now, so…” With a thought, the old man plopped into the cushion on one side of the shattered couch.

After a single, sharp inhalation of shock, he was gasping in genuine fright at the sudden teleportation to his master’s side by way of Eik’s ability to summon any member of his Court.

“Find Meri,” he instructed Robert. Having seen what he was capable of and his potential for growth, the fiend would be the one to come for him soon enough. “I need a recipe for the grade 7 potions for X-rank. And I need it now.”
