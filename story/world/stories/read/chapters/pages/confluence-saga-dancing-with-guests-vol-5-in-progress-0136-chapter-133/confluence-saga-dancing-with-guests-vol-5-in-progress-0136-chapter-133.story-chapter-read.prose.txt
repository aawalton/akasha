Anya sat up yawning at the sound of Seb grumbling in the next room. She was pretty sure they'd gotten at least a few hours sleep, less than they'd wanted but enough to get through a day, no matter how busy. They hadn't taken the time to talk too much about what Gwen and Cara had said, they'd both felt overwhelmed by the information and had found a welcome distraction.

Seb?

No coffee, he sent back. No tea, no food, nothing. What's the point of a kitchen area, of a coffee pot, if there's no coffee!

I'm sure they'll have some at breakfast.

Not the point.

She slipped out of bed and joined him, turning him away from the bare countertop and forcing him to look in her eyes.

I know you slept, I know you were able to relax and settle. This isn't you.

She felt the tension bleed out of him, his eyes softening as his thoughts stilled momentarily. It was still there, still part of him, but he was managing it, he was back to his usual self.

We need more answers, he sent. And we need them sooner than six months from now.

She tugged on his neck, pulling his forehead down into range of her lips for a quick kiss before she danced away to dress for the day. She knew he was on her heels, close enough to seem as though he was chasing her.

Figure it out. There's information, there's people. I bet we can get more answers from Gwen.

Not the same, he answered. We didn't even get all the answers I'd hoped. The entire conversation got derailed.

By my being royalty? she teased.

Of course not Princess, he sent back, his tone as light as hers. Well actually yes. But you knew that.

She nodded absently before striking a pose at the edge of his sight line. Seeing him look up and smile was all she'd wanted as her thoughts turned to teasing him about how slow he was.

So what did you figure out? she sent.

Keldren's ancestors were bred for battle, Cara's a Fairy, her hair does sort of sparkle too…

What?

Fairies in those stories have nearly invisible wings and tend to sparkle.

Seb…

Right. They're twins and Morgan is a distant cousin…

You're just repeating what they told us.

The rule of Three might be real, he sent, continuing as she glared at him and her emotions flared. Something done or said three times is binding, is a promise that cannot be broken.

Her promise to free Cara?

There was a bit of that strange Energy, not what we felt at dinner but similar.

And my father was. Is? Part of the main royal line, which is why Gwen and Bern's looks are so similar to mine.

Names are important too… he sent and she felt a touch of anxiety fill the bond before he took a deep breath and calmed. I told her she could use my full name, she did and you reacted. She called Bern something else at dinner when there was that Energy flare. Obernyn.

And she somehow knew you were the one who came up with Anya, with the name I use.

Names and Rule of Three… Do you remember what she said the first time we felt something?

She hesitated in the doorway, stopping so abruptly he ended up wrapping her up in an embrace rather than bumping into her. She did remember, not in the sense that she could remember the conversation, the frustration at their talking around and over her, but the words themselves that had mattered.

She said they were all Guests.

We need answers, he sent.

I know, we said that.

She felt him focus, felt the intensity that normally came when they pushed each other sparring.

Too much of this matches up to those tales, to those myths. It isn't coincidence.

Guest Rights and Hospitality are another of those bits. I don't remember all the details.

179 days?

We don't know what time or what day exactly, so maybe not.

They started down the stairs, barely slowing as they heard the sound of others below. They were going to go get breakfast, and hopefully get some answers. She wasn't sure what either of them would do if there wasn't any coffee though.

Seb frowned at his coffee mug. It was good. If he was honest it was some of the best coffee he could remember having. He didn't enjoy it though. It wasn't the perfect temperature, it didn't have that slight aftertaste, it wasn't-

Briana's is better, Anya sent. Not as hot though.

Is that why you're blowing on it and burned your tongue?

Yes…

He didn't bother saying anything. He'd nearly burnt himself on his mug, only hesitating at the last moment when he felt her surprise at the heat. They both knew better than to drink coffee too fast unless Briana made it, but somehow their mugs had seemed close enough to catch them off guard.

He glanced around the room, happy to see his guess had been right. The room wasn't meant to be laid out in five long tables, it was broken up into settings for anywhere from four to ten and spread out so that no one would have to bump into anyone else.

Food had been brought to them, the only question being if they'd wanted coffee. He did wonder what would happen if they didn't like their meals, but that would have to wait for another day, the servants had vanished the moment their plates were on the table.

They just arrived, Anya sent.

A moment later he recognized them as they passed through his awareness. They both noticed Gwen hesitate as she saw them, Cara shifting from following on her heels to standing between them almost automatically as she did.

No answers? he sent.

No- she began before he spun in his chair to follow her attention.

The group had turned towards them, Bern clearly leading the way despite Keldren walking in front of him.

"You," Bern said as he paused at their table.

"Me?" Seb asked, feigning innocence.

"Despite what your appearance might indicate, despite what my sister might believe, you are not family."

Seb caught the quick shake of Gwen's head as he glanced at the group. Cara's face was unreadable, her hair a near solid tone. Morgan on the other hand was watching them, watching him specifically.

I don't think he likes you, he sent.

He doesn't have to.

"You may believe what you will," Anya replied calmly. "You are not the one who decides who is family, unless you speak for the family as a whole?"

"He does not," Morgan said. "None of us do."

"Morgan!" Bern hissed.

"She is family brother," Gwen said. "I named her such, Cara's response showed her to be such, our own eyes-"

"Do not finish that sentence," Bern snapped. "If she is to be recognized it must be by one allowed to do so."

"I'm Seb," he said with a clearly false smile. "You may not call me Sebastian. You've met my wife Anya yet only two of you have bothered to introduce yourselves despite talking at her every time we've crossed paths."

He felt Anya tense slightly before smiling as well. He saw Cara's eyes widen and hair flicker before settling and caught Gwen nodding.

"I am Bern, eldest son of Bern. And this is my sworn guardian and battle companion Keldren."

"Morgan. Distaff branch many times removed."

"Pleased to meet you both," Seb said. "We were enjoying our breakfast, I'm certain you are looking forward to yours as well. At a table able to accommodate you all?"

Seb…

"I am," Gwen answered quickly. "I suspect we all are. I haven't had a Human breakfast before."

Being polite, he sent back.

"It can be quite tasty, I hope you all enjoy it," Anya said cheerfully.

"Once you have finished your meals proceed to the grounds outside," Sarna called out from the entryway. "You will receive your schedules and further instruction at that time. Your first lessons begin in 45 minutes, tardiness will not be tolerated."

"Be careful," Gwen said.

I hope he burns himself on his coffee, Anya sent.
