Woosh.

Really? The instant I appear, and something is already trying to kill me with a jet of fire. I shake off the disorientation and search for the asshole that couldn’t wait a few minutes.

Look up, master. Dovey helpfully informs me, and I do just that. Only to balk at what seems to be a wyvern flying high in the air, eyeing me up for breakfast no doubt. Well, at least it isn't a dragon. Wyverns are a distant cousin though, and still nothing to take lightly. So I prepare to fight it like any sane person would.

I turn in the direction of Itolas and start running. Muscular Boost comes in quite handy, as I push more and more stamina into my legs. Each step I take is equivalent to several meters now, and I practically fly over the sandy terrain that makes up the Snarq Mountains.

My speed is further enhanced as I start chaining in Plane Steps. Using the skill is almost like breathing at this point. It is odd to think that I struggled to use it at first. Now with every step I cross dozens of meters, and for so little spirit that practically every step is a Plane Step.

The wyvern is several kilometers behind me at this point. I can see that it is still pursuing me though, so I don’t stop running. Though I do tone down the muscular boost a bit, it is quite energy intensive, and was a bit excessive.

I can’t really do much while running, it isn't hard, but it also isn’t effortless. Especially with how rough and maze-like the terrain is, I am either reliant on my map or Dovey up in the sky to guide me. It is quite calming though, just me, myself, my spirits, and the wilderness. No people trying to kidnap me, or rifts that break the fabric of this world or any shit like that.

Oh, and thanks to Dovey’s new ability, I am able to avoid monsters for a lot longer than normal. Though not forever, naturally, as the world has deemed fit to remind me at this moment.

Crack. A bolt of lightning splits the terrain right in front of me, and I am thrown back by the shockwave. Before I hit the ground, I have already summoned Stelan. I wasn’t wearing it while running, as it does make me a fair bit slower.

I crash into a wall and bounce off it, only to roll back onto my feet. Just in time to dodge a smaller bolt of lightning. My Energy Sense goes to work, and I am able to trace the residual energy towards the top of the canyon I have found myself in.

A figure stands there, a humanoid one. Now that I think about it, I haven’t encountered any bandits or such in this new world. I figured the wilds were simply too large and dangerous for them to be a thing. Guess that assumption was wrong. I cast out an identify, but all I get back is that they are human, and near my level. Their second class must be some sort of stealth oriented one.

Without any hesitation, I Plane Step to the top of the wall. My attacker seems to have been expecting that though, and has some lightning waiting for me. The bolt hits me directly, and I can feel my internal bits get a bit scorched. Damn, why did it have to be lightning? It is so hard to dodge, and can stun quite easily.

Unless they aren’t a bandit, but a hunter that wants to take me alive? But who would attempt that, let alone have the knowledge I was in the area? It couldn’t have been that guy, as he has certainly been punished by now. Does someone else know that I am a hero?

My thoughts are interrupted as I barely manage to dodge the next attack. I stop standing around thinking like an idiot and charge towards my foe. Luxmore appears in my hand, and I instantly activate the buffing aura. I can feel Dovey’s presence in the sky, but I tell him to stay back. He may be fast, but lightning is faster. The only way I can dodge it at all is my Energy Sense.

Now that I am close enough, I Plane Step next to the person and before they can react, I slam Luxmore into their side. A sickening crack fills the air as their flimsy cloak does nothing to stop my boosted attack.

The moment they land on the ground, I Step beside them again, and give them another whack. Oh, and a third for good measure. But as I go for my fourth, suddenly my entire body locks up. Lightning arcs around me, not an attack, but some sort of stunning spell.

It lasts only a half second, but that is enough for them to jump up and throw a dagger at me. With a mere instant to spare, I am freed from the stun and turn away from the dagger. Yet the moment it gets within a meter of me, it explodes and my entire world turns white.

My eyes were blinded, but I use Spirit Sense to still be able to see them. They think I am blinded, so they charge in with another dagger. But I am waiting for them, and the moment they fully commit, I push almost my entire stamina reserve into my muscles, and drag Luxmore down into one massive Anvil Strike.

Crack.

The sound of their body being crushed is not one I ever wish to hear. Nor do I want to hear the notification informing me of their death. But alas, I am forced to hear both.

‘Ding’, You have defeated Lixus Astrea (Lightning Acolyte Level - 57 / Blood Hunter - Level 52).

‘Ding’, Magic Smith has advanced to level 52. Stat points distributed.

Woah, I got basically a whole level from that? They were a bit higher level, and I suppose a cored being would give a lot more experience than a monster… not that I plan to make use of that information. I am no murderer, unlike this guy clearly was. He isn't the first person I've killed, but he is hopefully the last. Not to say I won’t defend myself, if someone is stupid enough to attack me, then I will kill them if necessary.

“Master, are you okay?” Dovey asks as he lands nearby, hopping over to look up at me.

“I’ll be fine.” I glance at my arm and see a good chunk of it missing from the explosion. But my health is still pretty high, so I just pump some mana in to repair my armor and let my natural healing take care of my arm. No use in using a potion for every little injury, it isn't like I have an unlimited supply.

“That person was very not nice.” Dovey declares, and he couldn’t be more right.

“No, they were not. They were definitely trying to capture me alive, too. Which means they were likely not working alone.” I look back at the notification and it contains a very important clue.

Astrea.

Normally, I would just discount it as a random name. But Ari mentioned a certain Lady Astrea quite often. A super duper not nice lady, from what I can tell, and a powerful one too. Not to go accusing people of doing things, but the situation is far too coincidental to be a coincidence. Thus I can be certain that she knows of my existence somehow, and is hunting me.

Which is just perfect, really. There really is nothing I would love more than to be chased around by a bunch of people that wanna enslave me or whatever. Damn, if only I knew how to get in touch with the inquisitors or whatever, they would certainly find this all very interesting. But as it stands, I am just a random guy. A hero, perhaps, but a relatively poor one–at least, for my level–with no real connections. Well, unless you count a secluded primordial or an elemental that is who knows where.

I sigh. The ‘later problems’ drawer was looking a bit thin, given how many questions Valdir answered.

“Master, I think you should check this out.” Dovey calls out, dragging me out of my thoughts once again. I find the small dove standing next to the corpse of the guy I just killed. The sight is not pretty, but above the corpse floats a shiny blue orb.

I walk over next to Dovey. “What is it?”

“I think it is some of the guy’s storage.” Dovey replies.

“Oh, well that does make sense. All of that stuff wouldn’t just disappear.” I reach out and touch the orb, and receive a whole ton of notifications. Mostly about materials and gear and stuff acquired, all of which I have very little interest in. It is all for the guy’s rouge and mage classes, after all.

I do feel kinda bad for thinking it, but I suppose it all will be worth a decent bit of gold at the market. I just shove it off into a corner of my storage, creating yet another problem for later me.

“Should we uh, do something about the corpse?” I ask.

“I do not understand the customs of you cored beings. But he was our enemy, right? Why should we do such a thing, leave it for a random monster to enjoy.” Dovey replies with a shock of logic that I really needed.

With my morals mostly settled and my stomach only a bit queasy, we leave the corpse behind and get back on our path. We are still several hundred kilometers away from Itolas, thanks to my detour. But that should only be a few days of solid running, presuming nothing too crazy happens.

As I start to run again, I put the whole thing behind me. The guy attacked me, I defended myself. It's that simple. Nothing else to it, really. Now it is just me, Dovey, Luma and a long stretch of very hostile terrain.
