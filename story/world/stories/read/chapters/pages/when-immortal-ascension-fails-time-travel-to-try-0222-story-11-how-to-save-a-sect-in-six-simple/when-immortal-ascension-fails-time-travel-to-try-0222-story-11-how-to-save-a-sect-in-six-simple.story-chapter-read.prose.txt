Huh. He shouldn’t have one of those. The kid was young.

Once I’d developed a treatment method for Foundation Establishment cultivators, I thoroughly tested it before allowing Little Spring to begin it. When I did, I took into account that his cultivation method differed from the unorthodox cultivators and adjusted his prescription accordingly. Unfortunately, even though he was only a couple of weeks away from finishing the full treatment, his white hair and extra ears stubbornly remained.
