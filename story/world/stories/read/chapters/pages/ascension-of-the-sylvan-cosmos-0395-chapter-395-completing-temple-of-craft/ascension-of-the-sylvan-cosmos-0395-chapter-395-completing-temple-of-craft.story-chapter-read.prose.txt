Seeing the piles of materials in her <Lignified Vault>, Liu Shu was quite satisfied. ‘I guess one of the advantages of having so many people is that my requests for materials have become rather easy. I was somewhat afraid of how to find the three crystals. However, it seems that I worried for nothing.’

Liu Shu’s roots extended from the walls of the Vault and coiled around the materials one by one, dragging them underground. Once she grabbed them, Liu Shu moved the materials to the location of the new <Temple>. The place Liu Shu chose was the same ring where the library and the other two <Temples> were. This circle surrounding her was where <Mother Of Thousand Flower Field> was located, gently covering some part of these important structures with its drooping flowers.

Speaking of <Mother Of Thousand Flower Field>, it had now transformed into a true ultra-long-distance defensive structure. After reaching Level 50, it gained new flowers as well. Before, she had non-attacking flowers, normal offensive flowers, and elite flowers. After the last Energy Cycle, a new type of Queen Flower grew all around the massive field that stretched for kilometers. The numbers of normal to elite were 1 to 1000, while the number of Queen Flowers was 100 elites per 1 Queen-type flower. Even then, there were several hundred Queen-Type flowers.

These Queen-type flowers had enough power to pierce the most formidable defense and such accuracy that could only be described as future predicting. Within 15 kilometers of Liu Shu’s body, no flying or land creature could escape their bombardment. The Queen-type flower’s height reached around 10 meters, while the elites reached around 3, and the normal ones lingered at the 1-meter landmark.

Moreover, the 15-kilometer distance was the location where shots were almost 100% accurate. The maximum range of attack for <Mother Of Thousand Flower Field> was 20 kilometers! Beyond that, the power behind the attacks would start to weaken, and the accuracy would also be quite atrocious. Even then, the attack range was so large that when firing, Liu Shu or the building itself needed to consider the planet's curvature.

After she stacked all the necessary materials, Liu Shu began creating the skeleton that the <Temple> would need to follow. During the process, Liu Shu used a super adhesive substance that hardened later to leave a robust frame. The process for the outer section of the walls flowed seamlessly.

Meanwhile, all the nature-related materials slowly fused with Liu Shu’s ternary essences. This step was essential, as the Temple would harmonize with her body and expand its influence using not only its own powers but Liu Shu’s as well.

Above all of that, at the center, the cornerstone materials like the Forgecrystal began fusing with one of Liu Shu’s seeds to create the <Temple Seed>. The seed would be used as the recognizing factor that could perfectly link Liu Shu’s will and the <Temple> itself.

Due to the complicated and delicate process, it took quite a lot of time to complete all these steps. However, thanks to her increased Energy Cycle and the fact that she was building a relatively low-tier Temple, she needed no more than 40 days.

For Liu Shu, these forty days went by in a flash, and before she even began feeling the passage of time, the temple was completed. “Aannd~, done!”
