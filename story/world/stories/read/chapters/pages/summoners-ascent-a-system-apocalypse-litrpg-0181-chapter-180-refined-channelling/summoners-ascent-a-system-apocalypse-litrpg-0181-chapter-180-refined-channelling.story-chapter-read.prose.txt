"And you're really leaving right now?" Sam looked at his friend with concern. There wasn't a lot of reasons for him to really be worried, though. All that Ashton was doing was go to Market Street and then to the primary school. He would probably spend the night in that area, and then come back tomorrow with Millie in tow.

That was the plan, at least. A lot of things that were messing with Ashton's plans had been happening recently, to the point where he had started trying to figure out contingency plans. Though the things that happened were usually outside of his realm of expectation anyway.

"Yup." Ashton already had his hand on the metal door that acted as the gate to the outside. Calibur's camp had been expanded slightly into the nearby buildings, but this was still the only exit and entrance for the time being.

"Just try to keep us updated somehow, okay?"

"Yeah, yeah, I'll ask the backpacker to reply to some of your posts or something," Ashton said with a wave as he finally left the camp again. Sam wasn't the only one standing there to see him off, but Jacky, Oliver and Paul were standing there, too.

It honestly seemed like they had been trying to everything they could to keep him here just now, just continuing to ask question after question about stuff that Ashton didn't really care to discuss extensively. He had a pretty good idea why that was happening, though.

Sam was concerned. He had probably asked the others to keep him occupied for as long as possible, so that Ashton didn't leave right away like he was doing at the moment. They did seem genuinely curious, but they seemed a bit too stressed when asking questions for it to just be coming from themselves.

But Ashton was a bit annoyed that Sam was being so protective over him. Didn't he know how strong Ashton was? Sure, in the past, it was always Sam doing the protecting. Hell, apparently one of the reasons why Sam was doing so many martial arts was because he wanted to not just build confidence in himself, but also the sort of body that could protect Ashton against bullies.

Ashton did use to get beaten up super badly on the regular, though that stopped once Sam found out and started stepping in. It had been exactly like that all the time, ever since.

Sure, at some point, Ashton wasn't one of Sam's reasons to work out anymore, but it had just become a habit and a way for him to relieve stress, but that didn't mean that Sam didn't constantly look out for Ashton.

Whenever there was a time where things were tough and Ashton didn't talk to anyone, Sam would show up at his door with beer, biryani and samosas, and when he was feeling particularly bad for Ashton, a pack of cigarettes.

Sam was his brother in every way that mattered, and exactly that seemed to show in these times still.

But Ashton wasn't the same person that he was back then. Though from Sam's point of view, the last time Ashton had one of those depressive phases was literally just a month ago. It must take a while for the image that Sam had of him to change.

"I promise, I'll be okay. I can handle myself." Ashton tried to reassure him and calm Sam down, but he just let out a long sigh.

"That's not... Just... come back with all your parts this time, alright?"

"Well, that's the plan, at least." Ashton stepped out of doors and waved as they closed down again.

With that, it was really time to get moving. First stop, Market Street. He had Poppy use her speed-boosting spell on him, and then started moving. And since he wasn't going to have that much to do while he was running, beside fighting the monsters that were popping up all around him.

There were a few new types; stronger-looking versions of the monsters that were here before, as well as simply completely new species. One that was particularly grotesque were these things that came in various different shapes. Some were humanoid, some looked like animals. It was like they were moulded from a mass of raw flesh, and tendrils and fleshy tentacles came from their body that they used to attack.

It seemed like they barely knew how to move their bodies properly, but they were still pretty strong just due to their raw strength. These luckily didn't seem particularly common—Ashton only encountered one group of them—but they were definitely freaky. Apparently they were called 'Growth Sentry'. Ashton didn't love the implications of that name, but he had already tagged them to learn more about them.

These weren't a thing during the invasion of Lumia, so he should study them carefully when he had the time.

Yet, for now, there was something else that he was more interested in. Ashton had a skill to refine, and a contract to advance.

First, he wanted to look at the 'Channel' skill.

[Skill Refinement | Channel]

[Long-Range Channelling]

[Detached Channelling]

[Manifestation Channelling]

[Omniscient Channelling]

Ashton read through the descriptions of the refinement directions, and it seemed like the routes that Ashton could take were quite evenly split between changing the 'zone' that surrounded Ashton at all times, and the way that he actively interacted and channelled the summons' power.

He was a little surprised to see this, but this did confirm one of the major purposes of the 'Channel' skill. It basically controlled and defined the 'field of omnipotence' around Ashton. This specific term and concept was one that he had heard of after travelling, from different mages he had met in Lumia, but it essentially prescribed the area that a magic user was the most powerful in.

It was an extension of their consciousness, their body, and their very soul. While not impossible, it was most effective to use magic within this range, and it was often said that stepping into an enemy mage's zone was an immediate death sentence. That was how powerful and important these were.

So in this case, the Channel skill was in charge of this extension of Ashton's self as well, the place where he was able to deploy the summons' channelled power the most effectively. It made sense, really.

And really, the two refinement paths that he could choose from for his 'zone' were really useful, too.

The Long-Range Channelling considerably increased the effective size of the zone. Though, from Ashton's understanding, it really just thinned out the mana that was used to establish this area, so his precision and control would go down in exchange.

Detached Channelling was the more surprising and interesting one out of these two options. While Ashton was already able to manipulate this zone to the point relatively well, compressing it and even shifting where he was in relation to the centre-point, he still always had to be inside of this area. But this refinement would change that. Though it probably wasn't for a long time, and he wouldn't be able to move his zone miles away, Ashton would be able to detach his channelling area from himself.

While, especially now that he was blind, it felt a little risky to do something like that, Ashton knew that there were definitely scenarios where this would come in extremely useful. For example, he could look at things at a distance with much more detail. He didn't expect that he would have the exact same precision when the zone was detached, but it would still be better than the extremely blurry view of the world that he had at his fully extended channelling range right now.

Plus, increasing the range at which the summons could act was extremely useful as well. At least right now, this was the front-runner in regard to his options.

But then there were the options that actually changed the way he interacted with the summons' powers.

Basically, they each strengthened a different part of the channelling process, while weakening the other.

'Omniscient Channelling' cranked up the sensitivity of that initial phase of omniscience that one felt when channelling a summon. That exact phase off omniscience was what Ashton used to actually 'see' through the elementals. If he chose this, he would probably get another huge boost to his 'sight', to the point where he could maybe say goodbye to the constantly blurry fuzziness all around him. It was an extremely intriguing option, but the downside was that it would weaken the manifestation phase. The part where the powers of the summons were actually used. This would affect a lot of things.

Not only would the elementals' magic simply get weaker, but the Pen of Insight's inspections would get less precise, or the Drunken Bear would be physically weaker than they were now.

And on the other side to that stood 'Manifestation Channelling', which did the exact opposite. It would make Ashton's channelled omniscience weaker, but it would increase the effective strength of the summons' powers in turn.

And sadly, no matter how much it would help him right now, out of these two, Ashton would have to forego 'Omniscient Channelling'.

What he was doing right now was deciding on the path that this skill was going to take in the future. He had to be sure that this would help him long-term, especially since this was such an important skill. And since Ashton knew that he was going to make himself new eyes sooner or later—even if later was more likely—and that he would probably get other abilities that would help him sense the world, there was no way that he could choose that option.

That left him to choose between the Detached Channelling and the Manifestation Channelling. And though it was an option he was less curious about, Ashton ended up choosing the latter.

[You have selected to refine the power of manifestation you can achieve through your Channelling]

Immediately, the world around Ashton became a little more blurry, though he could get himself back to the point where he was at before by just using some more mental strength. It was annoying, but this was the choice that was best for him long-term.

At the end of the day, getting the system to boost his powers in any way was the most effective thing. Rather, 'Detached Channelling' seemed like a waste. After all, all the system did was provide fast-track paths to power. He could technically do everything that the system was letting him do through his own power, it would just take a much longer time.

Even before the system came to Lumia, though it was a rare phenomenon, detaching your 'zone' from your body was something that some extremely skilled mages were known to be able to do. As long as Ashton kept working at it and learning more about magic, he would be able to do the same, simply without the system's support.
