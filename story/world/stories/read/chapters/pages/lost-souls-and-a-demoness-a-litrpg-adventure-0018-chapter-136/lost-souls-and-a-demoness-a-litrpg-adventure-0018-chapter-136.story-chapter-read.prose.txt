Jade's daggers were at the ready before the first of the knights took more than a step towards them, and she was the first to get a spell off.

"Noctis!"

A bolt of darkness slammed into the lead monster, forming a dark cloud that obscured its visor. It slowed its advance, letting its companions move forward as the two groups clashed. The Earth Warden Aki rushed to join Luis just as the knights reached him, raising a heavy shield and intercepting attacks along with the other tank. Powers went off all around Jade, her allies flinging spells and activating talents. Without the intensive training they'd recently completed, there likely would have been some friendly fire. Fortunately, the hard work paid off. The expanded team targeted their powers, if not completely synergistically, than at least without getting in the way of each other.

Jade slipped to the side, getting out of the line of fire as Luis and Aki continued to take the brunt of the knight's attacks. Deciding that casting invisibility would be a terrible idea with this many spells flying around, Jade instead focused on harrying the enemy at range. Phantom flashed from her hand, striking the nearest of the knights in the upper chest before blinking back to her hand, just to be thrown again, this time imbued with a hail blade. The first few strikes weren't very effective, only scoring grooves into hardened armor, but her third throw caught the knight under the arm.

The creature grunted at the impact of her blade sinking into a vital point, turning its glowing head to face her. Jade kept its attention, luring it away from where Luis and Aki were getting hammered by the rest of the enemies. She backed away, sending another hail blade flashing towards it, only for the attack to be swept away by a swift slash of its radiant weapon.

Faster than it look-

Jade barely had time to finish the thought before the knight was on her, charging across the distance with shocking speed and an oddly melodious battle cry. She acted on instinct, blinking behind the monster with mirage step and leaving an illusory duplicate for its blade to slice through. The illusion exploded a moment later, flashing into motes of blinding light that sent the monster staggering back with a gauntleted arm held over its face. Jade spun around to face it, plunging both her daggers into the same gap beneath its arm. Her blades bit deep, tearing a gash in its side and releasing more of its inner glow. She darted away as the monster lashed out, sweeping its radiant blade in a wide arc that just nicked the tip of her wing. The barrier around her armor crackled, absorbing most of the hit, but a small amount of energy pierced the protection to graze flesh.

"Ahh!"

Jade grunted as a burning, searing pain flashed through her wing, completely disproportionate to scratch she'd taken. She gritted her teeth and danced further away from the knight, her speed enhanced by Naomi's booming melody. The intense pain faded quickly, and a notification appeared.

Combat Log

You have taken damage of a type you are vulnerable to. Attacks of this nature deal 50% increased damage.

Vulnerable?

Jade blinked the notice away, grateful that the combat log only ever appeared in the corner of her vision. Having a message unexpectedly pop up in her face could easily get her killed. She quickly pulled up her status page, maintaining a safe distance from the knight as it began to chase her. It may be surprisingly fast for its size, but she was faster.

Racial Traits

Shapechange

Resistance to charm effects.

Resistance to fear effects.

Resistance to fire damage.

Resistance to poison.

Vulnerability to incandescent damage.

"Well, shit…" Jade grunted, closing the window. She flung Phantom again, the ghostly blade slamming into the knight's chest before blinking back. There was still no blood, only radiant light spilling from the wound.

The last thing she needed was for a high-level enemy to deal increased damage to her. Jade leapt into the air, soaring out of reach and continuing to harass the monster. Her strikes weren't dealing a lot of damage, but a single glance told her the rest of the group had the other three well in hand. If she could just keep it distracted a little longer-

Again, the knight surprised her. It made a quick slash with its glowing blade, conjuring an arc of light that flashed towards her like a missile. Jade just managed to twist out of the way but lost focus on her flight, dropping to the ground nearby and landing hard.

Thump thump thump!

It was rushing towards her again, sword held high and ready to strike. Four steps away. Three. Two!

"Enough." Jade growled. She pulled herself upright, glaring straight at the oncoming enemy. "Stop."

Thump-thump!

The armored knight halted before her, weapon poised to strike. Slowly, as if confused, it lowered the blade to its side.

"Better. Now, stay here." Jade said, sensing the tenuous nature of her hold over the creature's mind. As long as she didn't order it to do anything else, her control would remain firm. The Allure spell worked best on living, intelligent creatures, not spirits, but she'd learned to push its limits. Now that she could easily cast it at A-rank, the spell was more formidable than ever. Unfortunately, so was its enhanced mana cost.

"Ow…" Jade grunted, rubbing her forehead as the effects of low mana washed over her. It hadn't fully regenerated from all the flying she'd done earlier. "Have to be careful with that…"

A short distance away, the main group had already felled one of the three knights. Not wanting to miss out on any experience, Jade rushed back to help clean up.

"Something strange is going on here." Aylin said, slowly circling the corespace aperture. It had stabilized and now looked indistinguishable from the first one Jade encountered. "Very strange."

"That's all we've been hearing since day one." Luis said. "Think it has something to do with what the demons are doing?"

The fighting was done, at least for the moment. Everyone was grouped near the portal, staying ready in case more monsters emerged. Naomi and Siora were seeing to a few minor wounds while everyone else recovered from the brief but fierce engagement.

"That's my guess, although I'm not sure why that would cause the monsters generated in the corespace to be such a high level."

"She's right." Tara agreed. "They shouldn't be in the 30s, not on the third floor."

Jade paged through her recent notifications, only half listening. Her Clinging Darkness spell had finally risen to C rank during the fight. That left only two of her abilities at D rank: Hail Blade, and Chains of Shadebloom.

I need to focus on those. She thought, closing her character sheet. They're falling behind in power.

"I don't think we're going to find any answers out here." Jade said, pulling herself to her feet. She turned to face the portal. "We still have to go in there, and we won't know what we're up against until we do."

"If all the monsters inside are as strong as the ones that came out, we could be in trouble." Parker said.

"If it's too much, we can retreat. But we have to try."

She looked at her companions, and then at Parker's team. Everyone nodded, albeit with various degrees of enthusiasm.

"We handled this lot fine." Luis said forcefully. "Whatever's in there, we can take it. We just need to fight smart."

"Jade, you're going to have to be more careful than usual." Aylin advised her. "If the monsters in there deal the same damage types, a good hit could be crippling."

"I'll play this one safe." She promised. "I will!" Jade insisted, noticing Naomi's skeptical look.

"I hope so. We still only have one of you."

"I'll try and stick close to you." Siora added, laying a hand on Jade's shoulder. "Just in case."

"Thanks, Siora."

After the fight, Jade had taken the time to share her race's weakness to incandescent damage. Part of her wanted to keep it a secret, in case it was ever used against her, but the last thing they needed was for the team to get blindsided if she went down to a hit they expected her to shrug off. Then she learned from Tara and Aylin that many demons shared that same weakness and realized that hiding it wouldn't have done any good.

Jade drew in a deep breath to clear her mind and then clapped her hands, getting everyone's attention. "Alright! Everyone ready to head out? I'm going in first. As long as I don't run right back out, you can follow after a few seconds. Once we're inside, we'll assess the situation and make a plan. Got it?"

There was a chorus of agreements, and the two parties formed up in front of the portal. Jade stepped to the head of the group and paused in front of it. The glowing sheet of amethyst energy glistened and sparkled, swirling in myriad patterns.

"Three, two, one…"

Jade stepped into the portal. She felt the now-familiar rushing sensation, and a heartbeat later, she was somewhere else.

System Notice

Now entering Lost Cathedral corespace, Earth Core #7

Her feet landed on soft, grass-covered ground. It was dark, her surroundings lit only by the glow of the portal and soft shafts of silver moonlight. Above was an open expanse of star-filled sky, with a full moon hanging almost directly overhead. Jade took in her immediate surroundings first, expanding her senses and ensuring she was alone. She was. No monsters, demons, or anything else registered in the immediate vicinity.

Breathing a sigh of relief, Jade stepped out of the way as the rest of the team began filing through the portal behind her. While they recovered from the disorientation of dimensional travel Jade started investigating their surroundings more thoroughly. The area seemed to be enclosed by four large walls, which formed a rectangle that they were on the inside of. The enclosed space was massive, at least a few square miles. At its center stood a massive, imposing cathedral, complete with tall spires and flying buttresses. Its dark stone exterior was broken only by large, stained-glass windows set far above the ground, which were letting out a bright, warm light from within.

What Jade now thought of as the cathedral grounds were quite varied. As best as she could tell in the outdoor gloom there were wooded areas, gardens, and even a small lake. It was beautiful, and yet somehow gave her a sense of unease.

"What are those?" Eita asked, pointing up at the wall behind them.

Jade turned to look, seeing each corner of the exterior wall was home to a large tower. They had emerged near one, the large structure stretching nearly as high as the cathedral itself. A bright yellow light shone from the top of the tower, directed like a focused spotlight. The beam of radiance stretched across the sky from the tower and through one of the cathedral's high windows, disappearing inside. Similar beams were projected from other towers, forming four lances of light that converged at the central building. At least, Jade assumed there were four. The cathedral itself blocked her view of the opposite corner of the space.

No monsters approached, nor did any other danger manifest as they gathered themselves, everyone looking warily around.

"First thing's first." Jade said quietly, once it became apparent that nothing was about to approach them. "Let's find the Core."

She had only taken two steps when multiple system messages appeared in front of her.

Increased magical saturation has increased your mana regeneration rate.
