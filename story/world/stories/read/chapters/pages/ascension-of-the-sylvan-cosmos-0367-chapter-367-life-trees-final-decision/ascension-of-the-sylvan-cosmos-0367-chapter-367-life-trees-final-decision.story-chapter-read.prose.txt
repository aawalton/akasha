Even then, you were hurt somewhat, right? What if fifty people with that being’s strength attack you together? Are you confident in healing their damage before your healing makes them faint?”

The Life Tree couldn’t help but feel upset; she was not too bright, but she wasn’t stupid. It’s just that she had trouble reasoning deep plans and thoughts. However, something as simple as Harmonixia’s question was not hard to imagine nor deduce the outcome.

Harmonixia didn’t stop and continued her speech. “You are a strong Life Tree. However, do you believe that if Liu Shu wanted to fight you, she would be unable to?”

Life Tree exclaimed. “Of course! It is another big forest like the Wall Forest, right? I’ve heard tales from travelers.” The Life Tree spoke somewhat fearfully. “That place hasn’t given birth to a Life Tree for centuries already, and I heard that it was trying to expand recently…”

The Life Tree is subconsciously aware that if Liu Shu had wanted to invade her, it wouldn’t have been much of a problem. Therefore, there is gratitude and trust in her heart.’

Verdantia sighed. ‘Let’s hope we don’t need to resort to that. She is a very gentle Life Tree, after all.’

The strings held it in place in a perfect position for her to play. The music did not have any essence yet.

“Life Tree. Liu Shu has fought the <Dark Aether Swamp>.”

“The <Dark Aether Swamp> was governed by a Level 66 World Tree. The World Tree was corrupted several centuries ago, and the forest it governed became a dark swamp. Liu Shu, being the closest World Tree and unwilling to see something happen to those she was protecting in her domain, decided to face it alone.”

The Life Tree’s body felt uncomfortable when she imagined the owner of the gentle and soothing voice becoming Corrupted. It felt as if her leaves couldn’t breathe properly, and her roots were clogged. Harmonixia’s sad music, even if it had no energy behind it, also affected her mood slightly.

Moreover, unlike the Sylvans, the Life Tree’s external roots were surrounded by Liu Shu’s roots. When checking closely, she could feel the nasty sensation inside of the previous pure and soothing World Tree roots.

You are the only one that I can think of that can do that. I promise that you will never be mistreated. On the contrary, you will become a revered figure, and no one will dare bully you.”

The Life Tree wanted to agree with Harmonixia's words. But the second she was about to say yes, a strong fear came from the depths of her heart. When she was a young Life Tree, she had fallen in the hands of a Nexus Sect, and everyday, she was forced to kill creatures to Level Up quickly while developing her healing skills.

Every hour it was either healing or killing. During those days, her mind almost broke several times. Moreover, when the place became unsuitable, the Nexus people would cut off her roots and transport her. Eventually, they placed her here and mounted a small area to send their injured people.

During all this time, if she dared refuse, the Nexus would torture her by pouring damaging substances to the earth around her. Just the touch made her roots burn with pain that made her mind dizzy.

Thankfully, after a few years, someone appeared and slaughtered the sect. That person said to her a single sentence before leaving. “Life Tree, trust no one. While you’ve been tortured, the proficiency of your skills has reached incredible levels. You'll live for a long time as long as you live here and don’t get involved with anyone. World Trees are also very dangerous, so be careful, or they will eat you whole.’

The words of her savior resonated with her experiences. She got captured in a terrifying contract because a group of people approached with gentle and warm smiles while holding a knife behind her back to backstab her.

Now, the situation was similar to what had happened back then. A group of people approached with a sob-worthy story about a being that the Life Tree has learned to like. They were asking her to tie herself to a being that’s known for killing all major plant life as they expanded, a World Tree.
