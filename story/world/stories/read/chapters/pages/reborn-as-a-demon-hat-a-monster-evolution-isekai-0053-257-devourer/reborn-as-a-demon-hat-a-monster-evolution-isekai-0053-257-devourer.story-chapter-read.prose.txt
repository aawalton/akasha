All across Argwyll, the tendrils of Sys’s power stretched.

They tore open the minds of all those who walked upon the earth and beneath its surface and then began the process of siphoning their cores – a process that had been perfected across thousands of stars in the galaxy. Like vicious, sentient IV drips, they pumped the Cores and life force of every living being into the only creature on Argwyll that truly mattered now.

The creature who was standing atop what remained of the Umbral Tower, with the boiling oceans of Argwyll spinning all around him in a deadly vortex.

As the storm swelled, green lightning wrapping itself round the raging waters and unnatural winds, Sys spread his wings wide and threw open his arms to receive the collective energies of an entire planet.

“First,” he said – voice thrumming with untold power. “I will take their Cores. Then, they will come to me in flesh and blood. I will drain the seas and have them march, as one, to my embrace. And you, my little hat, will have a front row seat for the whole spectacle.”

He spoke to the tiny hat he held in his left hand – a dull, tatty thing that could barely even keep its single crimson eye open.

Ethan Hawke – the Demon Hat – watched from within the storm as Argwyll was consumed.

“I will make it slow,” Sys grinned, savoring every welt of his muscles – every satisfying drip of Spirit Cores that were being pumped into his body, filling his being with each passing second. “As slow and protracted as my time on this pitiful little planet has been.”

Sys waved a single hand and opened up a hole in the great green vortex that swirled around them both.

And through the opening, Ethan saw the impossible horror of his dreams coming to light.

Hordes of Hybrids, humans, animals and monsters had massed on the farthest beachhead at the very edge of Eastmarch. More were slowly lumbering behind, creeping across the land like a plague, their eyes awash with Sys’s green light.

He had seen it all before. He had seen it in Lamphrey’s vision, and now here it was made terrifyingly real.

“You know, Ethan,” Sys chuckled. “Every planet I’ve ever conquered has had this exact moment. The moment when my gullible Host realizes just how absolute its defeat has been. But I don’t think I’ve ever felt the kind of satisfaction, at the end, that I feel right now."

He threw back his head in a laugh that was probably the most genuine expression of emotion Ethan had ever heard from him.

“If I were human, I think I would die from joy alone!” he howled to the raging vortex all around him. “But – no. I’ve had my fair share of your kind. In fact, once I’m good and done here, can you guess which world I’m coming for next?”

Ethan sagged under the strength of Sys’s grip, and the sight of the people of Argwyll, zombified and comatose, staring at him as the seas began to drain away, filtering into Sys’s storm.

“Now comes the final act,” Sys whispered, turning Ethan around just so he could gloat in the Demon Hat’s face. “Any final quips, Ethan? I can’t promise your last words will be remembered. But for the hatred I bear you, and as thank you for teaching me what the emotion truly means, I will do you the honor of letting you have the last word before I eat this world whole.”

On the far shoreline, the feet of the first Argwylians stepped onto rough, desolate land, ignoring the flailing fish and sea creatures whose eyes glowed with the very same green that dominated their own.

And the Demon Hat, with herculean effort, let out a hoarse, weary chuckle.

“Those words…won’t be...mine,” he said.

Sys cocked an eyebrow at him.

“…they will be…theirs.”

From below, there then came a blast of concussive energy – a blast powerful enough to rival the power Sys had sent through the land. Sys looked down, seeing a blinding beam of purple light shooting towards him from where the Umbral Monastery had once stood.

Without even moving a muscle, he activated [Repulsor Shield] and watched the beam disintegrate into nothingness.

But even without being harmed, the confusion in his eyes was palpable.

And it became only more pronounced when he realized what the attack had been:

[Storm of Revok]

[Cry of Baphomet]

[Ice Barrage]

[Spectral Snipe]

As Sys’s grip on him loosened, Ethan kept his eye on the dark angel’s face.

And when the creature turned to face him, now there was an altogether different emotion plastered there.

“You…you really think…I didn’t…suspect?”

The four green eyes of the angel blinked in the face of Ethan’s single crimson eyeball.

“Maybe…I am…a loser, Sys,” Ethan said – voice becoming more authoritative by the second. “But I’m a loser…who listens. And listening to you…taught me plenty.”

In the face of Sys’s mounting confusion, another string of attacks sprung from below.

This time, his eyes told him what was happening:

[Storm of Revok]

[Cry of Baphomet]

[Angel Arm]

[Spectral Snipe]

[Arms of the Vigil]

[Prophet’s Pacification]

His mind registered the lethal intent in the attack – the attempt to disable him outright and stop him from using Ethan’s Skillset.

But the parasite angel didn’t even turn to meet the attack. He momentarily let the fact that something down there had managed to seemingly resist his [Universal Law] Skill drop for a second. Thinking too much wouldn’t help, here.

“…think you’re clever, Ethan?” he seethed in the face of the Demon Hat. “Have some little helpers down there? Well, allow me to end this nonsense before it begins.”

He gripped Ethan harder and shoved him right in front of him, using the Demon Hat as a hostage to shield himself from any incoming damage that [Repulsor Shield] and [Shield of Aegis] couldn’t block.

As soon as his intention was clear, the combined strength of the hidden attacker’s combined spells flickered away to nothing.

And, using [Eyes of Raziel], it was Sys’s turn to behold an unreal reality.

He saw who was standing just beneath them. He saw who it was who was using all of Ethan’s old Skills. Skills that should have now been his and his alone.

And he shook his head in utter disbelief.

“…impossible,” he whispered.

“That’s…your problem,” Ethan muttered in his grip. “No imagination.”

As the smoke and ash of the monastery’s decimation cleared before Sys’s eyes, he saw three creatures standing tall, weapons raised, looking up at him with filthy, defiant eyes.

“No,” he said again – not even comprehending Ethan’s words anymore. “No, no, no, it’s…”

Fauna the Hopla stood with her staff before her, unsummoning [Repulsor Shield] to let him look upon Ethan’s three companions.

“So, it’s fucking true…” Tara huffed.

“The System itself thought to betray us,” Klax snorted. “And in its hubris, it does not even realize its own failure.”

Fauna the Hopla simply stood tall, her gaze full of fury at the angel high above.

And only then did Sys see the flickering sheen of [Shield of Aegis] disappearing from her and her companions.

“It’s…it’s not…” Sys stuttered, breathing becoming more and more haggard by the second. “But – the Law, it –their skills should be - “

Ethan sighed beside him.Sys’s clenched his claws down on Ethan, unbridled fury finally erupting across his face. He ignored the insanity of what he was seeing. He couldn’t let his mind wander. Whatever was happening, he could beat it. He’d beaten the odds already. He’d made it to this point – and whatever feeble Skills they had, he had more. He’d always had more. Thinking was weakness. Doubt was what he’d always managed to exploit in his mortal Hosts. He didn’t need it. He was a lifeform born to conquer.

So, he didn’t spare a thought as to how they were still standing with Skills they’d never had before. Instead, his arms swelled with pure, primal power. All the power in the world.

And as Fauna aimed another strike at him, he stared down at her from on high and bellowed:

“Don’t even bother – FILTH!”

He waved on hand in the air and activated [Time Stop], causing the world to freeze completely. Then, with a roar of unmatched fury, he let loose an attack the likes of which Argwyll had never seen, aimed right at the spot where the Hybrids stood.

[Angel-Arm]

[Storm of Revok]

[Cry of Baphomet]

[Azure Edge]

[Azure Arc]

[Spectral Snipe]

[Ice Barrage]

[Thorn Hail]

Fauna had enough time to activate both her shield spells just before the [Time Stop] took effect. But even their power could not withstand an attack channeled through [Sprit] DMG spells.

The zombified crowd gathered on the drained seabed stopped momentarily to see the sight of Sys’s assault – an attack that radiated such power that even the earth itself began to split apart under them. The vortex surrounding him reacted to his attack, his threads of Spirit Core energy were channeled into the green beam that was being channeled between his hands.

And then, with a scream of rage, he let the attack fly.

In his free hand he held Ethan aloft, forcing him to watch as his friends, still as statues, saw the green arm of death stretch towards them.

“Here, Ethan!” he bellowed, laughing manically with all the mirth he had left in him. “Let me show you what happens to those who try to resist the inevitable!”
