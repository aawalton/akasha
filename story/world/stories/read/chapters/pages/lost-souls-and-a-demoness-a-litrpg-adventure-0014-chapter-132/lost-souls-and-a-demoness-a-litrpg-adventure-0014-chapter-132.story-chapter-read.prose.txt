Succubus 24th level feature

Ability: Wings of Night

Enhance your wings to allow flight. Flying consumes moderate mana over time. This cost is reduced while in shadow or darkness.

Jade's eyes were locked on the first of the two options as she cleaned and sheathed her daggers. She read it over and over again, a rising thrill washing away the weariness from a day of hard training and leaving her giddy with excitement. It had finally happened. Ever since her transformation, ever since she'd accepted this new body as being hers forever, she had longed for the freedom of flight. The curse of her new form's needs and the dark impulses that accompanied them were a constant struggle, even if she was slowly getting better at managing them. More than once, Jade had told herself that the ability to take to the sky would make it all worth it. Now, she finally could.

Out of diligence, and because she would be able to select the ability at a future level, Jade finally moved down and read the alternative the Labyrinth presented to her. For once, it wasn't a ridiculous climax power. But as much as those annoyed Jade, she immediately found herself wishing she'd been presented with one after all.

Ability: Bewitching Aura

Passively charm nearby creatures over time, with targets who find you attractive effected more quickly. This effect strengthens the longer a creature remains in proximity to you and slowly fades while you are separated. This charm is highly resistant to detection and dispelling, and more easily penetrates magical defenses than similar magic. The aura is always in effect and cannot be deactivated.

Jade swallowed hard, a feeling of disgust slowly displacing the joyous elation of finally reaching this long-awaited moment. This 'Bewitching Aura' was perhaps the single most powerful ability she'd been presented with to date. Nothing in her previous level-ups, nor anything Naomi, Luis, or anyone else had told her about, came close to the sheer strength of a permanent charm effect that strengthened over time. Jade could imagine a dozen applications just off the top of her head. The synergy with her 'Heartbreaker' ability alone was staggering. She had no doubt that it would be an exponential leap forward in power.

And it would make her a monster.

"Jade?" Naomi asked, suddenly concerned. "Everything okay?"

"Yeah. Fine. Just another weird power."

The words sounded unconvincing even to her. Naomi and Fenrin exchanged glances but stayed silent, waiting for her to say more. Jade gestured for them to wait a moment, rereading the ability. A small, power-hungry corner of her soul urged her to take it. Gaining the strength to defend her world, destroy the Cores, and defeat the demons was the whole point, wasn't it? With this, she could potentially turn her enemies into allies, or maybe even convince leaders of Labyrinth cities to fully commit to helping them end this crisis.

But the cost was too high. Jade shook her head, tearing her gaze away from the prompt. If she chose that ability, it would be a betrayal of everyone she was close to. Everyone she loved. That decision would single-handedly validate every instance of distrust and suspicion she'd encountered in the Labyrinth. Compromising the judgement of her friends and everyone around her in pursuit of the greater good would be so easy to justify to herself. But even if she never took advantage of it, and Jade couldn't say with certainty that she wouldn't, it wasn't right. And if she instead confided in her friends about how her powers were affecting them, no one would ever trust that they weren't being manipulated by her.

Now, more than ever, Jade understood why succubi were so feared. She was only level twenty-four. What kind of powers awaited her at higher levels?

Jade took a deep breath and then exhaled slowly. She carefully selected 'Wings of Night' and confirmed her decision, closing the window and banishing the thread of temptation to the depths of her psyche. At long last, she finally had the freedom she yearned for. Jade wasn't going to let an unpleasant ability option ruin the moment.

The change was immediate. Jade's wings, which she'd long since learned to control like any other part of her, surged with newfound strength. She felt a tingle as her mana shifted, flowing from the rest of her body to newborn pathways that now filled her wings. Her body felt lighter, swifter, as if she'd just cut free a weighted vest.

"Woah…"

Another smile stretched across her face as the joy returned. Naomi and Fenrin relaxed, and the bard clapped her hands.

"What're you waiting for? Try it out!"

Jade didn't need to be told twice. She flexed her wings and felt the new power respond, coiling like a spring ready to be unleashed. Unleash it she did, launching herself into the air with a burst of wind that sent ripples through the surrounding wetland pools. Jade shook free her earthly bonds, embracing the freedom of open sky. The rushing wind swept away the last of her worries and Jade laughed with joy, throwing out her arms and swooping past her companions before settling into a stable glide, wings outstretched.

There were more than a few wobbles in those first few minutes, and even one or two unintentional splash downs in the many shallow pools, but Jade quickly got the hang of her new power. One thought repeated itself, over and over. She was free. In that moment, some of the resentment Jade held for the Labyrinth slipped away. The transformation forced upon her by this world had never stopped weighing on her, even as she grew used to her new form. Now, tolerance bled to acceptance. She would take the good with the bad, come what may.

Finally, head pounding from a low mana headache, Jade alighted on the ground before Fenrin and Naomi. Not even her throbbing temples could pull the stupid grin from her face as she shifted back into her human form, releasing the penalty to her mana regeneration.

"From your expression, I take it that was as fun as it looked." Naomi said, punching her lightly in the shoulder.

"You have no idea…" Jade shook her head in wonder. "It was worth the wait."

"No more climbing up way-too-slender trees to get to nodes?"

Jade laughed, remembering the first node they'd encountered in Juniper's quadrant.

"No. I guess not."

Naomi's genuine excitement wafted off her like a pleasant scent, but there was also a hint of melancholy. Jade was getting better at discerning individual emotions from groups and homed in on it. It was coming from Fenrin, who, on the surface, looked just as happy for her as Naomi.

"What is it?"

The half-elf's expression slipped, and he gave her a sad smile.

"I was just thinking about how much Mirella would have enjoyed joining you in the sky."

Jade's gaze found Fenrin's shoulder, where his companion had so often perched.

"I would have liked that." She said quietly. "I'm only here to do it because of her. Don't think I'll forget that."

"None of us will." Naomi agreed, touching Fenrin on the arm. It was his new arm, recently regrown. Jade knew how hard he was working to regain his former dexterity. As amazing as healing magic was, it had its limits.

"Come on." Fenrin said, breaking the somber silence. "Let's go tell the others! They'll be excited. This opens up a whole new chapter in our tactical playbook."

"You sound like Tara." Jade laughed. "I'm all for hard work but sometimes, you need to take time to enjoy the little things. My brother taught me that."

"I don't think the ability to fly counts as little." Naomi pointed out.

"Well, I already had the wings. It's just that I can use them properly now."

The rest of the team was equally happy for Jade, and the next few days of training passed uneventfully. What had begun as a disparate group of individuals fighting alongside each other was slowly transforming into a cohesive unit, with each person able to anticipate the actions and needs of everyone else. They worked in new powers, testing their limits in various scenarios, until they were all comfortable with their role. Siora's presence was an enormous boon to the team, with her restorative powers and stamina potions allowing them to push themselves harder and longer than would otherwise be possible.

Part of Jade's training included growing accustomed to her new whip, Subjugation. The basic proficiency imprinted upon her by the Shadow Witch class allowed her to wield it with some amount of skill, but she quickly discovered that, as with her daggers, it would take many hours of practice before she would feel truly at ease with the weapon. As for reaching Malice's level of deft expertise? Jade didn't even want to think about the intensive training that would take. Part of her had been hoping that skillfully wielding a whip was a capability innately possessed by all succubi, but unfortunately, that dream was quickly quashed.

Group exercises with Parker's newly expanded team continued as well. The man was a confident leader, a good teammate, and had a keen mind for tactics. That didn't surprise Jade, considering his military background, but his class did.

"Why do you fight with two swords?" She asked after another battle with a river wyrm, wiping sweat and dirt from her brow as their teams recovered on a thin strip of land. "Your class is 'lancer'."

"My abilities are all about armor penetration and knocking enemies off-balance." Parker explained. "The Voice gave me a lance to start with, but I ditched it pretty quickly. It was awkward as hell to walk around with."

"He's not lying." Ellie added in a rare contribution to the break time conversations. "He nearly put my eye out with that thing on more than one occaision."

"Where'd you get the swords?" Luis asked, holding up his own curved blade for comparison. "I've been thinking about upgrading."

"At the Labyrinth store. Took most of my earnings from the second floor, but they're worth it."

"Is your shopkeeper a kobold too?" Naomi asked. "There are supposed to be different ones.""That's right. Short guy, big toolbelt, lots of teeth."

"Sounds like ours." Jade nodded. "Is his name Skraag?"

"Nope." Parker scratched the back of his head as he thought. "Said his name was Skrug, right?"

Ellie nodded and Urgraan made a sort of layered croaking sound that Jade interpreted as agreement.

"Maybe they're related." Luis suggested. "Does Skrug make bombs?"

"Bombs?" Parker and Ellie exchanged a glance. "No… I don't think so."

"Lucky you." Naomi groaned. "Skraag keeps trying to pawn his homemade boom balls onto us."

"They're bomba bombs." Jade corrected. "And one was very helpful in the corespace, I'll have you know."

"And how would that be, Jade?" Naomi asked, turning a deceptively sweet smile to her. "We didn't buy any. Right?"

"Ah, I think the others are done!" Jade said quickly, hopping to her feet and jogging towards where Tara, Siora, and Aylin were finishing off a group of small Water Elementals. "Time to mix up the teams again!"

Later that evening, a newly cleaned up and still very sore Jade pulled open the door to Tales and Treasures, Ashar's new store. She stepped inside, wiping wet hair from her face and scuffing her boots on the welcome mat. No one had mentioned the almost constant rain in Illysport, and after barely a week she was already growing tired of it.

"Good to see you again, Jade!" Ashar said cheerfully, stepping out from the back room. He was holding an unfamiliar tool and wore a pair of thick goggles, which he pushed up to his forehead. Jade found the look cute, in a dorky way. "What brings you in today? More upgrades? A new potion? Ah, no, you have Siora for that." He snapped his fingers. "Consulting for romantic pursuits?"

Jade looked at him from under raised eyebrows.

"Romantic pursuits?"

"Of course. I give famously good relationship advice. It isn't inexpensive but really, is anything more valuable than insight into matters of the heart?"

He flashed her an impish smile and Jade shook her head, hiding one of her own.

"Even assuming that's true." She replied, walking over to the counter and pulling a few items from her dimensional pouch. "Why would I go to the man who asked me on a date for that advice? You're more than a little biased."

"Why, because I'm a man of honor and integrity. I would never let my biases influence my counsel."

"Very noble of you." Jade said, continuing to retrieve materials from her belt and piling them onto the table between them. "Not that I'm in any position to doubt you, after what you're doing to help my brother."

"Ah, I have an update on that, actually." He said more seriously, flipping through a ledger. "I should have the ingredient in question in a couple of days."

"Really? So soon?" Jade asked, surprised.

"We got a little lucky, it turns out. Some wayfarers just got back from an expedition to the fourth floor where they defeated a desert phoenix. They sold the loot to one of my business partners, and he's sending it over."

"That's wonderful." Jade said, relieved. "I have a line on the last ingredient Siora needs to make the elixir, too."

"I'd be happy to hear more about this potion. Your elven companion is quite the alchemist."

"No kidding, she's been singlehandedly keeping us on our feet this week. Did you know there's an algae in the wetland marshes that can be brewed into a potent stamina potion?"

"I didn't. I take it they're effective?"

"Very! I just wish they didn't taste so much like the pond slime they look like. Speaking of." Jade tapped the desk, now piled high with the random mix of items they'd looted throughout the week. "We have a lot to sell after a week of training, and I thought I'd bring the business to you."

"Always appreciated." Ashar said, already identifying his way through the haul. "If you'll give me a minute…"

Jade browsed through the shop's selection of daggers while she waited, occaisionally stealing a glance at the earnest artificer while he worked, noticing the cute way his brow furrowed as he took notes. Then he looked up, catching her gaze and flashing her a grin before getting back to work. Jade turned away, feeling her cheeks heat up with embarrassment. What was she, a schoolgirl? Calming herself, she waited for Ashar to finish before eventually returning to retrieve a slip of parchment from him. She waited for the Labyrinth to translate the numbers on it before giving an internal wince. It was a far cry from replenishing everything they'd spent on level C upgrades.

"You wouldn't be able to move a little bit on some of these, would you?" Jade asked, giving the merchant her best charming smile and offering him back the list. "For me?"

Ashar glanced it over again briefly before setting it down on the desk and deliberately sliding it back to her side. He shrugged in a gesture of helplessness, though it was somewhat undermined by the twinkle of amusement in his dark eyes.

"Honor and integrity, my dear. Honor and integrity."
