Meanwhile, outside the city, a controlled chaos was sweeping the entire world. The energy Liu Shu was using and absorbing as she fused with Ceperet and evolved into the new Universal Tree variant was high enough to destabilize the world's balance.

Thankfully, even when Liu Shu was completely unconscious while evolving, Wisdom was able to manage Liu Shu’s subconscious thoughts and body, redirecting all the powerful aftershocks deep underground or high into the sky.

This created a torrential effect above Liu Shu’s gargantuan body, manifesting a storm of Ternary Essences in the form of a vortex.

Flor and the others looked up past Liu Shu’s canopy, their hearts turbulent.

“Is this the usual reaction of a World Tree reaching Level 100?”

No one answered Verdantia’s question, as everyone was unaware of what normal meant for the breakthrough of Level 100 World Trees. Amongst all of them, only Ignatia had otherworldly knowledge coming from her inheritance.

The Ant Queen looked at Liu Shu and sighed softly. “I am sure that this is not the case. I have some lingering sensation from the visions I usually have, and I’ve seen other Level 100 World Trees there.”

Even if she didn’t say anything, her tone and expression made it clear that they were probably much weaker compared to Liu Shu.

Cinderielle looked at her system and sighed. “Just this buff is proof that her breakthrough is not normal.”

[You are under the effects of <Ternary Essence Density>. Your recovery is increased by 50%.]

Gathering so much Ternary Essence created a buff on all creatures near her. It wasn’t a buff that spread throughout the entire world, but around 200 kilometers around Liu Shu.

Flor spoke, her eyes also looking at that buff. “From the reports of those on the other side of the world, it seems that they have gained a debuff similar to this one over there.”

There was tiredness and frustration in her voice. She gently traced her trusty teacup’s rim with her finger and sighed. “Really, this girl only makes me worry. Just imagining her gains after ending her Level Up is making my stomach twist. Ugh.”

Her mind was already imagining powerful auras that would sweep the entire world and how she would need to administrate everything. Everyone was slightly sympathetic; after all, those gathered here were both family and government workers.

After Flor took a sip, she smiled and nodded. “Yep. It’s time for me to step down.”

Everyone sitting at the park table around Flor spluttered, the tea spraying out of their mouths and staining the wooden table.

The one who complained first was not the Sylvans, but Melody. “F-Flor, my friend. You can’t step down! You are the cornerstone that is keeping everything going smoothly. Even if you slack and do nothing, you should never step down!”

Hearing Melody’s nervous reply, Flor sighed. “... So tiring.” As she lamented, her petite body was picked up by her Ant Queen wife. Then, under her stupefied expression, Ignatia pulled her dress forward and stuffed Flor between her soft mountains.

Being around 60 centimeters tall, Ignatia could only cover Flor’s body in between. Even then, it was plenty of a surprise reaction for everyone involved. “Y-You…” Flor looked up with a furiously blushing face. “What are you doing!?”

Ignatia’s compound black eyes gained a comfortable glint as her seductive lips arched. “I am calming down my girl. Don’t you love to sleep between them? You’ve even slipped into other—”

“AHH!” Flor’s face gained such a deep red color that it was barely distinguishable from her hair. Thankfully, before Ignatia spoke more, her hands flew up and tightly held Ignatia’s plump lips together. Her eyes burning like torches, Flor growled. “I-If you dare say a single extra word, I will send you to sleep alone until Liu Shu wakes up!”

The Ant Queen stiffened and instantly behaved. How could she bear the many years it would take without Flor’s love!? That was unacceptable!

Of course, even if she stopped, linking the words and imagination was rather easy. So, when Flor turned around, she noticed everyone looking at her with subtle expressions. At that instant, Flor dearly wanted to slap her horny self for accepting Ignatia’s weird requests.

“Ahem.” Melody cleared her throat and looked toward her daughter. “Little Harmony, what…” Strangely, when she looked over, she saw Harmonixia raising her head and looking at a particular place of the World Tree with a blushing expression.

“...Should I go cave diving?”

Melody almost slapped the back of her daughter’s head.

“How much do you think Liu Shu’s stats will grow?”

At that moment, when everyone was thinking strange things, Eila quickly came to the rescue.

Today’s event brought together Liu Shu’s influential family members and their friends. So, people like Delia, Taro, Eila, Mariane, Alba, Edharen, Thalgryn, and others gathered together in a picnic location at Tree’s and Aurelise’s park with Verena, Melody, Amareal, Branthis, and, of course, the Sylvans, and even some of the children.

This lake, which was previously very far away from Liu Shu, was now rather close to her in comparison. Above them, Liu Shu’s canopy extended widely, covering most of the sky. If it weren’t for their exceptional strength and level, looking at the vortex of energy above Liu Shu would be impossible with such a gargantuan canopy in the middle.

Thalgryn answered Eila’s question, her muscular arms crossed in thought. “She will probably reach 100,000 cores in reserves, right? Perhaps her raw stats reach 300,000 or something like that.”

Ignatia spoke, her hands trying to placate her ashamed lover with gentle pats. “It will be close, probably. Not many Level 100 people can reach 100,000 Cores as reserves even at the A Tier…”

At this moment, Mariane finally spoke up. She had been slurping some delicious honey tea for a little while. Not because she was shocked, but because she really liked it and was reluctant to stop. “You are all overestimating Level 100’s.”

“Huh?”

Mariane, the guardian of the Endless Library, had ridiculous amounts of knowledge. The main reason was that Liu Shu was constantly printing books about the information she had absorbed after raiding the cities of the Abyssal Void Nexus and those who opposed her.

With the many buffs Mariane developed towards the reader and learning for becoming the Endless Library guardian, she could absorb knowledge at a speed that only Liu Shu could put to shame. The distance between Mariane and Liu Shu was basically the same as the distance between normal people and Mariane.
