Xavier progressed through the last two senses, smell and taste, quite rapidly.

Smelling energy was easier than he’d imagined it would be. He’d wondered if perhaps he wouldn’t know what he was smelling when he finally encountered it, but something in his brain seemed to know exactly what it was.

Energy was as familiar to him now as anything.

Taste was a different story. It was… a very strange sense to focus on. When he singled out the sense of taste, he brought out many things from his Storage Ring to help him. Well, mostly he brought out coffee and different types of food.

It wasn’t such a bad excuse to have a moment of indulgence.

He managed to single out the different tastes faster than he’d expected. The sense of taste and the sense of smell were highly related, and that helped him a great deal. It wasn’t long before he could parcel out all the different tastes in the drinks and food he was consuming. It reminded him of a wine taster, though he’d always thought they were just as likely to get something right through guessing.

That wasn’t the case for him. His sense of taste was incredibly accurate, not to mention enhanced. That first sip of coffee when his sense of taste was all he had was… mind blowing. He’d always known he had a weak spot for coffee, but he’d never known it could taste so damned good.

It was a shame he couldn’t smell it. That would have only enhanced the experience.

All of the beverages he drank and the foods he ate were both naturally and artificially enhanced by energy. This wasn’t strictly necessary, and energy wasn’t used in the food because it helped the Denizen become stronger—though it could—it was used to bring out the flavours even more to enhance the experience.

When Xavier started to taste the energy that had been infused in his coffee, it took him by surprise. It was evident he was tasting something different, but it didn’t taste like something new.

Energy is in everything…

It tasted similar to how it smelled—sharp, strong… beyond that it was difficult to describe. It didn’t taste like electricity or music or gas. The other senses interpreted it rather different to taste and smell. He tilted his head to one side as he kept drinking the coffee, swirling the liquid about his mouth, savouring it at the back of his throat before finally swallowing it.

Xavier still wasn’t sure when tasting energy would come in handy, but he was beginning to realise the value of what he was doing. He had moved so fast through all his advancements, moved so fast to gain so much power, that there was still so much he didn’t really know about the multiverse. There were things about himself that he didn’t know. Hidden potentials, even just in his senses.

It made him wonder what other things he was missing.

When he’d finished working on his sense of taste, the sense that made him feel the most vulnerable, he took a deep breath through his mouth—the breath tasted sour, there was something off about the air in this place, something off about the Void Dungeon itself—and he readied himself to bring the other senses back into the fold.

Xavier decided he shouldn’t do this all at once. If he brought it all back too quickly, it would be nothing but overwhelming. The shock wouldn’t do any permanent damage, but that didn’t matter—he didn’t need to experience overstimulation on that level.

He brought back smell next, and took another deep breath, this time through his nose. His nostrils flared. The sense wasn’t as overwhelming as he’d expected it to be. The two senses mingled, creating a strength he hadn’t experienced before, and he was tempted to bring another mug of coffee out of his Storage Ring. Just to test it…

But instead, he continued doing what he’d planned, bringing his senses of touch, hearing, and sight back online.

When he was no longer blind or death to the world, he just sat there for a long time, taking everything in. After so long, it was strange not just having his senses back, but having them all at the same time, intermingling. He blinked and gazed about. His brain adapted to the situation quickly—this configuration was what it was most used to, after all, but his emotions were a little slower.

He stood up, and it felt good feeling his body. His coordination was perfect, and he didn’t fall over like one of the last times he’d made it to his feet.

And somehow that feels like a win… well, how far I’ve come. He chuckled.

The first thing he did when he’d found himself comfortable in his senses once more was walk to the Void Dungeon’s stone wall. He ran his hand along the surface of the stone. This time, he didn’t merely hear or feel the strange runes that he now knew were there, he was also starting to see them.

Even smell them.

When he’d been sitting there working on his sense of smell, he’d thought it was the Void Dungeon itself he’d been smelling. He’d thought that was what was… festering. But that rotten smell had been coming from the runes on the wall.

There was an incredibly faint outline in the wall, almost impossible to see. And it would have been impossible if not for all the training he did. Hell, when he’d only had his sight, he hadn’t seen it at all. Or, perhaps, he hadn’t recognised it. It was only because he knew something was there, only because he’d expanded his other senses, that he noticed it at all.

Xavier tweaked the mental image he had of all the different runes on the wall now that he had more senses to work with, making their models more complete.

Once he’d finished doing that, he bit his lip. He didn’t know what this was. The Personal System might very well know, but something was stopping him from asking it. That same impulse he’d had for a very long time—that impulse to figure it out himself first. He knew the impulse wasn’t strictly necessary, but he was feeling it very strongly in this instance.

He wondered why that was—wondered if, perhaps, the Ancestral Soul was influencing him in that direction. He didn’t think about the Ancestral Soul enough, but he knew it was influencing him, knew that when he dug deep and followed his instincts those instincts were probably the Ancestral Soul’s wishes…

Well, it’s gotten me this far.

Xavier didn’t ask any questions of the Personal System. Instead, he activated his spiritual sense and his Immortal Soul lens for the first time in what felt like forever. The energy inside him lit up like lightning. He could feel the energy inside him now, through his sense of touch, like electricity running through his channels, just as he could feel so much else within his body, as his interoception had been incredibly powerful even before this little adventure in sense strengthening.

Somehow, after all the work he’d done with his individual senses, his spiritual sense had grown stronger. All six of his senses were now working together, allowing him to sense energy in a way that had never been possible before. He cast a spell, a very basic one—Heavy Telekinesis—and pushed at the stone wall, something he knew would accomplish nothing.

All he wanted to see was the energy coalesce in the air, to hear the song of how it moved, to feel the electricity as he brushed his hands against that energy, to smell that sharp, strong aroma as it flowed out in front of him.

It’s absolutely beautiful.

And there were things he could discern about the spell now that he couldn’t before, even though he’d been reading energy in the air for a long time, just as he had been reading runes. For a long time now he’d been able to discern what a spell was simply by the way energy flowed in the air, and he’d gotten incredibly accurate at that.

But this was different. He didn’t merely sense the spell’s energy, he could understand the spell, sort of how he could understand the language of runes and manifest newly created spell patterns on the fly when he needed something specific to happen. Yet this was different. Somehow more primal, based more on instinct.

The energy spoke to him and touched him. The gaseous-looking energy swam through the air differently to how the energy moved to his spiritual sense.

But all together, it wasn’t just beautiful, it told him something of how the spell worked.

His understanding of the spell wasn’t something he knew how to articulate, but he found himself weaving another spell pattern, almost without even realising it. The spell pattern was similar to Heavy Telekinesis, but there were a few differences—or rather, improvements.

Then, when it came time to cast the spell pattern, Xavier didn’t merely initiate it. There was a way the energy wanted to move through the spell pattern. Usually, it would simply light up. Energy would be infused into the spell pattern and he would cast it. Now, as the spell pattern became active—ready to be initiated—he focused on it and pushed the energy this way instead of that way, cycling it through the spell pattern in a, well, pattern. He didn’t know how he knew to do this, there was just something right about it.

When he was done—and all of this took but a fraction of a fraction of a second—he initiated the spell pattern. The energy coalesced in the air. When he saw that energy move through the air, he knew how much more efficiently the spell was working. Again, he couldn’t articulate how he knew, but he most certainly did.

The spell slammed into the stone wall of the Void Dungeon with almost twice as much force as it had before.

Still, it did nothing, and honestly, he hadn’t expected it too. After the energy dissipated into the air, Xavier released a long breath and smiled.

There are a lot of things I’ve been missing…

Inside his mind, he looked at the improved spell pattern, then he replayed the memory of how he’d moved the energy through that spell pattern. It was something he hadn’t done before. Hell, it was something he hadn’t even known he could do. This wasn’t how spell patterns had ever worked for him in the past, and yet this was how they worked for him now.

Though I suppose they’ve always worked this way and I’ve simply never known…

He tapped his foot on the ground, and he couldn’t help but stare at the soundwaves as they rippled outward into the Dark Tunnel. Xavier had had breakthroughs in the past. A vast number of different breakthroughs that, in his universe and many others, made him an incredibly unique individual, and yet every time he had a new breakthrough he wondered how it was possible there was more that he could learn.

This is why I came out here, this is why I let Derrida take me to this place and insert this Personal System in my head, so I could learn.

And he was beginning to realise just how much he still had to learn.

Xavier forced his attention back to the wall, to the runes that were etched into it, the runes that felt wrong, that smelled fetid and rotten. He placed the full might of his Immortal Soul lens-enhanced spiritual sense upon a single rune, along with all the other senses he’d just expanded, brushing a hand along the stone.

Despite his ability to sense these runes through his other senses, his spiritual sense wasn’t detecting them at all. There was something about them that was making them invisible to that sense… He’d thought that, when he reignited his spiritual sense, these runes would light up instantly in his mind, and yet nothing about them had changed.

Curious. Very curious.

He shut his eyes and stepped back from the wall, then he sat back down in the centre of the tunnel, just as he’d done so many times now.

There was one more thing he needed to do, even though the Personal System hadn’t actually recommended it. One thing he needed to try.

He switched off all five of his natural sense, leaving only his Immortal Soul lens and spiritual sense active.
