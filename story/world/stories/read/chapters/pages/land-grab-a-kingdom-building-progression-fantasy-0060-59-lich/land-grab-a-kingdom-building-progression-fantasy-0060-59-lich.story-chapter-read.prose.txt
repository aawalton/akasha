Tavian dropped into Water stance, a flowing form that might help him slip around Anarvia and get to the Witherstone. As long as the miasmic Core was immobilizing all life, Tavian could do nothing without it. With it, he could control the ghouls, preventing them from killing his helpless men.

Then he could figure out what to do about the Core.

Purple tapestries bearing the white anchor of Anarvia burned overhead. Lord Anarvia roared, and charged toward Tavian.

Tavian blasted the man with Shockwave, but the attack bounced off the man like a slap.

Raising his shield, Tavian intercepted the first blow of the black sword. The shield rang like a gong, vibrating Tavian’s arm.

He struck back, but his dagger was too short. The sword fighting forms could be adapted to dagger and shield, but they were designed for a traditional hand and a half blade.

Lord Anarvia was well trained. He noted that Tavian had fallen into Water form, and stepped through the movements of Air; Water’s direct weakness. The flurry of blows, driven by preternatural strength, knocked Tavian backwards, forcing him to pivot into Life stance.

This provided him a little more defense against the onslaught, but Lord Anarvia adapted quickly. His enemy moved to Flame stance, slashing and stabbing as he tried to get inside Tavian’s shield.

Lord Anarvia probably wasn’t the better swordsman. Right now, though, that didn’t matter; this wasn’t a fair fight. The man had been seriously buffed by the miasma coursing through his veins. And Tavian needed a longer sword.

Tavian pushed into Anarvia with his shield, then disengaged. He returned the black dagger carefully to its sheath on his hip; if he lost it he’d lose his immunity to the crushing weight of the miasmic anti-life influence.

He hooked his toe beneath a sword that had fallen to the ground, and kicked it up into his hand. Lord Anarvia pressed the attack, and Tavian danced away, avoiding smoldering corpses on the ground.

Tavian felt the balance of the blade in his hand. It wasn’t the best sword he’d ever used, and he felt a pang of sorrow for his own blade. He glanced to where Ilara lay, still wearing his sword at her hip.

He had to get to the Witherstone.

He dropped back into Water stance, an uneven matchup against Lord Anarvia’s Flame stance, which would greatly favor the more talented fighter. Tavian was betting that, with a proper sword, he would prevail.

For a moment, that appeared to be true. Tavian’s sword whipped out, catching Anarvia on the forearm, then on the shoulder. His shield turned away the man’s attacks, and he scored another nick on the man’s thigh.

Then Lord Anarvia’s sword tip dipped, and Tavian struck, stabbing for his chest. Anarvia brought his blade up, and the black, miasmically hardened steel sheered straight through Tavian’s sword.

Tavian cursed, throwing the fragmented blade aside. He ducked behind his shield and ran; maybe he could still get to the Witherstone.

The fiery barriers across the entrances to the room were guttering now, and rabid ghouls, whipped into a fervor by the miasmic influence of the Core, were starting to break through. The bodies of Anarvia’s slain men-at-arms began rising as well. Nobody was controlling them now, and they longed to feed on the dark energy Tavian and Lord Anarvia wielded.

Lord Anarvia kicked Tavian’s shield, knocking him backwards across the room. Tavian tripped over a body and rolled, springing back up to his feet in time to catch Anarvia’s next attack with the Aegis.

Drawing his dagger again, Tavian slashed at the Lord’s ribs, but once more his blade passed through the air.

Anarvia gripped the top of Tavian’s shield, trying to rip it away from him, and Tavian stabbed, this time scoring a hit. His blade punctured flesh.

Anarvia smacked Tavian in the face with the hilt of his sword, tugging down on the shield and winding up for a decapitating blow. Tavian deflected the blow with his dagger, and received a knee in the side.

He could hear the voice of Cormath, telling him to remember his forms.

“The forms, Tavian!”

He fired off Shockwave and Anarvia grunted, but did not release his grip on the shield.

“Don’t forget the forms!”

It was like his swordmaster was there with him.

Tavian twisted his hips, settling into a modified Stone stance, the strongest base for resisting a strong enemy. He struck again with his dagger, but Lord Anarvia knocked it aside. Tavian twisted the attack into an Earth stance strike, landing a blow with his elbow on the bridge of Anarvia’s nose.

Cartilage crunched, and Lord Anarvia grunted in pain, blood trickling down his face. Tavian heaved upwards with his shield, trying to bash Lord Anarvia’s jaw.

At the same time, he slipped his dagger up toward Anarvia’s stomach.

The man used his incredible strength, lifting the shield and nearly breaking Tavian’s arm. Tavian’s blade slammed home in Anarvia’s gut, but the man kept lifting. He flung Tavian across the room, and the dagger slipped from Tavian’s grasp, remaining buried in Anarvia’s torso.

Instantly the overwhelming power of the miasmic Core dragged Tavian to the ground. Without his dagger, Tavian had no defense against the powers of the Core. The air weighed on him like an anvil. It was difficult to even breath.

He struggled to roll, his muscles straining, tendons taut. A man could break his own bones trying to move like this.

Without the black dagger blocking his connection, Lea spoke in his mind.

“Tavian, you have to do something!”

“I know,” he sent back. “I’m trying!”

What could he do with his few remaining moments, before Anarvia dispatched him like he’d done with the Scarabs?

Lord Anarvia was a black silhouette against the burning fires. He laughed, softly, as he pulled Tavian’s dagger from his belly and cast it to the ground.

Tavian braced himself for the end.

“Tavian you fool!” Cormath shouted. “What did I tell you about the forms?”

Cormath was here. The man knelt beside Tavian. Not the lively, ruddy man who’d taught Tavian to fight with the sword, but the ghastly, corpse of a man who’d replaced him.

Cormath was immune to the anti-life miasmic influence of the Core. He was already dead.

He pressed his sword into Tavian’s hand, and the power of influence evaporated. Tavian felt like he was floating as he sprang to his feet.

⬣ New Item Acquired ⬣

⬣ Phylactery of the Swordmaster Lich ⬣

⬣ Traits: Hardened Structure, Keen Edge, Sovereign Movement ⬣

⬣ Special Abilities: Soul Cleave ⬣

Tavian was familiar with the traits, as he’d had them on his dagger, but he didn’t know what Soul Cleave would do.

“You take this,” Cormath said. “Let’s get out of here.”

“No,” Tavian gasped. “We have to stop him. We have to get the Witherstone.”

Anarvia was on them, and Tavian caught the blow on his shield. Anarvia was laughing manically now.

Cormath picked up another fallen weapon. “I’ll manage the ghouls.” He paused for a split second. “Be careful with my soul, boy.”

“Get to the Witherstone,” Tavian ordered as Anarvia pressed his attack, but Cormath was already engaged with a half dozen ghouls who’d picked up weapons of their own.

Blades flashed, and cut through Cormath’s dead flesh. Each blow was another wound that could never heal.

Tavian spun on Lord Anarvia and activated Soul Cleave. Cormath’s blade glowed with a white light, and passed straight through Anarvia’s sword. It bit into the man’s bicep, and, though the flesh did not seem damaged, Anarvia bellowed in pain.

Tavian deactivated the ability, and used the blade to deflect a withering combination of attacks from the enraged Lord.

With a proper sword, Tavian was better able to move through the forms. His whole body was aching and exhausted, though, and every super-strength blow from his opponent rattled his whole body.

Lord Anarvia stabbed his sword into the ground and grabbed Tavian’s shield again. Using his inhuman strength, he twisted. Tavian felt his arm wrenching, pushed toward snapping. He struck out at Anarvia’s legs, but the man ignored the blows.

Anarvia twisted harder, and Tavian cried out in pain, joints popping. In desperation, he brought his sword up to the leather straps and cut through them, freeing his arm.

Tavian rolled away, free, and Anarvia staggered to the side. The man tossed the Aegis aside and plucked his sword back up.

Tavian took advantage of the moment, and lunged past Lord Anarvia, toward the Witherstone.

He lashed out at the man as he passed.

Soul Cleave.

His blade passed through Anarvia’s deflection, and cut cleanly through the wrist of the man’s free hand. The hand drooped, limp and dead.

Tavian took two steps past Anarvia before searing pain exploded in Tavian’s side. He’d left himself exposed, and Anarvia had taken advantage of it.

Diving forward and sliding across the ground, Tavian reached for the Witherstone. Anarvia moved faster, kicking Tavian aside.

The man stooped, fumbling for the crystal orb with his dead fingers. Cursing, Anarvia held his sword beneath his arm and picked up the ball with his good hand.

“Come to me!” he declared. “Come end this for me, once and for all!”

The ghouls had orders now. They began pouring into the hall.

Lord Anarvia laughed. “Surrender now!” he demanded. “And I’ll kill you fast!”

Tavian leaned on Cormath’s sword, dragging himself back to his feet. Blood spattered on the ground, from the gash in his side. If he couldn’t kill the ruler, maybe he could still destroy the Kingdom Core.

“Lea! Does a Kingdom Core have a soul?”

“In a way, but why? Tavian…” she trailed off, as she understood why he was asking. “Don’t try it, Tavian! Get out of there before it’s too late!”

Tavian ran toward the Kingdom Core. Could he do this with Cormath’s blade? With his soul?

Was there even still a chance to save his kingdom?

Tavian reached the Core.

Soul Cleave.

“Come and take me!” he screamed.

The sword passed cleanly through the center of the Core, as through air. Tavian slashed again, and again.

Black lines formed, tracing the path of the sword through the Core. It seemed to be inhaling, sucking miasma from the air, trying to power and heal itself. Lord Anarvia screamed in pain, charging across the room to stop Tavian.

Tavian lifted Cormath’s blade over his head with both hands and brought it down, slicing through the Core from the top.

The sword vibrated, flashing in brilliant afterimages of rainbow light; Cormath’s soul, shining through.

The sword stuck in the center of the Core. Tavian pushed with all his force, and something gave.

A noise like booming thunder and grinding stones reverberated through Tavian’s skull.

The sword cracked.

A thin line of liquid white light shone through the metal of the phylactery, a spiderweb of damage.

Tavian’s stomach dropped as the crack spread.

Cormath clutched his chest, staggering to the side as a pair of ghouls hacked at him with naked blades.

An unearthly scream of pain and anguish rose from the lich’s lungs. Flashes of light crackled around him like lightning, his soul fragmenting.

“No!” Tavian screamed. “Cormath!”

He dropped the cracked sword, his body trembling as miasmic influence buffeted him on all sides.

The Core shuddered. Tavian watched in horror, as it seemed to be repairing itself. Then, with a gasp, all the air was sucked from the room and the Core collapsed in on itself.

The Core exploded outwards. The detonation caught Tavian in the chest and flung him into the air.

Anarvia was also flung into the air. Tavian watched as the Witherstone slipped from his grasp, buffeted by a thousand miasmic shockwaves. It slammed into the stone wall, exploding into a supernova of billions of twinkling shards.

Tavian struck a wall, head cracking stone.
