For one long, chilling moment, Asher stood there, staring up at the ceiling in horror.

Then he slipped into the astral just in time to avoid a few hundred thin strands of hair shooting straight through the space he’d been occupying a moment ago.

Oh God… he thought, watching the strands as they seemed to pause in space for a moment before slowly retracting up toward the ceiling. If The Lost Lady can control all of those as easily as she could her own hair…

“Asher!” Samantha cried, and Asher’s heart skipped a beat as he turned to take in his worst nightmare. Samantha had crashed down onto the ground, the hunter trembling as she shakily got to her feet. Thanks to Secret Seeker, Asher didn’t miss the thousands of strands of hair that were now connected and covering her body, nor how Samantha’s muscles tensed as she clearly fought against whatever The Lost Lady was doing to her. Even her brilliant, emerald wings of pure tritessence had somehow been imbedded with hair.

The scariest part of all was that Asher would have expected her to move jerkily, like some sort of deranged puppet as The Lost Lady turned her to face the spot he’d been in moments ago. Instead, Samantha stood and moved almost completely normally. Beyond the slight shaking in her body, Asher never would have known what was going on if it weren’t for the look of terror on her face or the hairs he could barely make out.

“What is going on?” Moxy shouted, and Asher’s panic went up a notch as he turned to find their most bloodthirsty teammate turning to face him as well. Moxy stood as tall and proud as ever, but her face was fixed in a large scowl as she clearly struggled to break whatever was happening to her as well. “I can’t move!”

Expecting to find the last of his team in the same predicament, Asher received at least one small blessing. While Moxy, Samantha, and Kroult appeared to have been completely ensnared by The Lost Lady’s hairs, Rulfar was a different story. The bloodhound was sitting on the ground in his massive battle form, his eyes closed and a look of concentration on his face as he focused. Asher could see the thousands of strands of hair creeping over his form and desperately working to ensnare him as well, but the fur covering his body seemed rigid and stiff. There was a weak, reddish glow that reminded him of the color of blood emanating from his entire body.

“Don’t worry about me, Asher, I’m okay,” Rulfar said, cracking open an eye and smiling vaguely in his direction before he went back to focusing. “Unyielding Blood, one of my blended skills. I’m almost invulnerable so long as I’m focusing, but I can’t move while I’m using it. Her hairs won’t be able to penetrate my skin.”

Well, that’s more than nothing, Asher thought, turning his focus back toward Samantha and Moxy. Samantha had already nocked an arrow and was aiming it at the spot he’d slipped into the astral from, and Moxy was calmly walking forward, shouting expletives and curses every step of the way. For whatever reason, Kroult remained frozen over by the cave entrance, so Asher put him out of mind for the moment. The lich would have to handle himself for a minute. He had more important matters to attend to.

Like the fact he was going to have to figure out a way of saving his friends without accidentally hurting them.

Not wasting any more time, Asher darted forward, returning to the material realm as he ran toward Moxy. Knowing what was coming his way, he twisted to the side preemptively, narrowly avoiding Samantha’s arrow as it screamed straight through where his head had been a moment ago. To his shock, the arrow had been enchanted, which didn’t bode well for his near future. If The Lost Lady could somehow force them to activate their own enchantments…

His fears were confirmed when Moxy brought her hands up before her like she had hundreds of times in the past against him, punching out and firing a concentrated Air Blast directly at his head. Asher jerked out of its path with Distortion, before hurling one of his steel daggers towards Moxy’s shoulder. She at the very least had her Life element and could heal up just about anything other than actual death, and he knew she’d suffered far worse pain than a single dagger here or there. But rather than hitting his mark, a sphere of air blasted out of Moxy as she triggered Air Nova, sending his dagger flying.

“She’s using my skills, damn it!” Moxy shouted, her face turning a dark red as she seemed to strain with every fiber of her being. “Runt, get these things off me!”

“I’m trying!” Asher answered, forced to flicker through the astral again to dodge another violet arrow that shot through him.

Asher did his best to get close to either one of them, but The Lost Lady had other ideas. Samantha leapt up and took to the air as she hovered around the roof of the cavern and rained death down upon him, while Moxy danced nimbly around the outskirts, pummeling him with Air Blasts. Rulfar, thankfully, seemed to be able to keep up his blended skill for quite a while, as the bloodhound remained where he was, sitting calmly near the center of the cavern as thousands of thin threads continued pushing and prodding him all over.

Asher’s one saving grace to all this was that he seemed pretty much immune to whatever it was The Lost Lady was doing to his team. Her hairs were thin enough that he was actually able to erase them when he returned from the astral realm back to the material. As he constantly flickered between realms, he continued erasing her hair bit by bit until she eventually got the memo and pulled back, no longer attempting to turn him into one of her bizarre puppets. Still, just because he wasn’t at risk of getting taken over, that didn’t help him with Samantha and Moxy.

Or with Kroult, who had vanished at some point during his battle.

Damn it! Asher hissed, sensing Kroult moving into some unexplored tunnel through the Eternal Mark he’d placed on the lich earlier. Alright, no time to play things safe. Time to get risky.

Having sparred with her countless times and watched Samantha fight against everything from assassins to demons, Asher knew better than anyone but the hunter herself just how deadly her wings were. With a pair of wings able to function as a perfect shield, yet sharp enough to slice a body in half like it was nothing, Samantha was a terror to fight up close against, despite the fact that she preferred to rely on her archery and staying out of the thick of things. It had been that reason he’d avoided doing what he was about to do until now, but they were out of options. Flickering through a few more Air Blasts, Asher gripped his daggers as he triggered Recall and the world twisted around him, placing him directly behind the flying hunter.

Who instantly flared out her wings and began spinning like a ball of bladed death.

Asher narrowly managed to block the first strike from her wings on Crescent, the force of which knocked him back slightly but saved him from getting sliced apart by her defensive technique. He still received a few nasty gashes across his body, but nothing he couldn’t ignore for now. As he fell through the air, he stared carefully at Samantha, watching the ever-so-slight gleam of the thousands of hair strands twist and move with her as she spun around. Not even Samantha’s Razor Wings were managing to cut through the strands, which meant they were made of some seriously tough stuff.

Thankfully, Asher had a weapon that cared little for whatever The Lost Lady had done to reinforce her threads.

Recalling back up to Samantha once again, Asher ripped his void dagger from its sheath, ignoring the bite of his own tritessence being drained as he moved to elongate the dagger and slice through a large chunk of strands connected to his fiancé. Unfortunately, the dagger seemed to eat his Distortion skill, and Asher hissed at the larger chunk of energy being ripped from his own body as it did so. But despite remaining its normal size, the dagger proved to be hungry as always, instantly severing a few hundred threads embedded into Samantha and returning to her the use of her left arm and wing.

Though Asher didn’t manage to pull off the strike without cost.

Landing his own attack meant he had to remain in the material realm for a split second, and The Lost Lady took full advantage of that opening. Even as a chunk of her was freed, Samantha’s other wing scythed through the air and cleaved straight through his leg, cutting it off just below the knee. Razor Wing proved yet again just how powerful it was as the pure, emerald tritessence cut through bone and muscle like it wasn’t even there.

“Asher, I’m sorry!” Samantha cried, her eyes going wide as he began falling.

“Don’t worry, I didn’t even like that leg too much!” Asher shouted back up with a smile before taking a deep breath and triggering Recall a third time.

Again and again Asher popped into existence beside Samantha, lashing out with his void dagger and cutting her free from the hairs controlling her one batch at a time. He started with the clump by her other wing, freeing that one as well and ensuring he wouldn’t have any more limbs lopped off while he worked. Because he was doing nothing more than repeatedly falling through the air over and over again, his missing leg didn’t hamper him in the slightest. He wanted nothing more than to reach out and take Samantha’s outstretched hand and drag her into his Personal Rift, but he had a hunch The Lost Lady wouldn’t let that happen until she was completely free. The fact that she could force her puppets to utilize their skills meant there was a lot more going on under the hood than just swinging them around with her hairs, and Asher didn’t have time to figure out the details.

Not to mention Moxy was continuing to fire Air Blasts up at him from the ground, making his job all the more difficult as he was forced to continue dodging her attacks as well.

Eventually, he cut through the last of the hairs, and Samantha threw her arms around him, sobbing into his chest as they fell. Very careful not to stab her with his void dagger, Asher returned the hug, whispering to her as they slowly fell, her wings acting as gliders.

“It’s alright, you’re safe. I promise everything will be okay, but it’s not safe out here right now. I need you to go into the rift.”

“Okay,” Samantha sniffed, giving him a quick, desperate kiss before letting him pull her into his Personal Rift. Asher was admittedly quite surprised he didn’t take an Air Blast to the back during the tender moment, but he would take the pleasant surprises when the world offered them to him. With the first of his teammates secured, he finally landed on the ground once more, doing his best to balance on one leg as he turned toward Moxy.

Only to find the warrior had vanished during the last few seconds.

“Moxy?” Asher called out, his void dagger raised before him even as it continued eating away at his tritessence with every second. After using Recall so many times in quick succession, combined with the use of his dagger, Asher’s energy stores were running dangerously low, and he didn’t have an emergency crystal to shatter for a quick pick me up this time around.

“She ran off,” Rulfar said, grimacing as he glanced up at the hairs still creeping over him like some silent predator waiting for the perfect moment to strike. As far as Asher could tell, the bloodhound couldn’t move anything more than his mouth or face so long as his skill was active. “Same direction as Kroult. Wherever they went, I have a bad feeling The Lost Lady has something even more horrifying than this in store for them.”
