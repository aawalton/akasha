Thomas looked through the portal to the bleak landscape beyond and saw his life stretched out before him.

He'd be turned into a weapon against his own people, forced to use Purge to empower the Light Ones, to heal them too, if being able to heal higher levels was all that rare. They'd hold the safety of his team over his head so he couldn't even meaningfully fight back or ever recise his own core and reject his healing mana. All the while, he'd be empowering those who were subjugating Earth.

That was the best-case scenario. It would get so much worse if the Light Ones found out about his true Gift. Then he would not only be healing them and making them stronger but enriching them too. They'd probably force him to kill people in dungeons in hopes of looting their Gifts as skill shards. Very likely, other human beings.

He'd be nothing but a cancer to his people, fueling their enemies.

Thomas couldn't allow that. He would not allow that. He would die before letting himself be dragged through that portal.

He would burn his own soul first.

With that decision made, calm settled within him. He looked toward his team for the last time. Jo and Twitch stared back at him with varying degrees of horror or despair. Zach, though... he gazed back at Thomas steadily, in a mirror of Thomas's own resolve.

From the hint Raveth had dropped, he was looking at a fate like Antonin's. The fire banked in his eyes told Thomas he also planned to go down fighting rather than let that happen.

Raveth kept Zach's skills and Gift suppressed, but if he regained access to his Teleportation, he could make the most difference. Zach just needed a moment. Thomas could give it to him.

Raveth's hand tightened on his shoulder, and he pushed Thomas forward toward the portal.

Thomas grabbed him back.

He didn't reach for his Purge ability. In his heart of hearts, he knew it as a tool to get rid of toxins. Like hell was he going to make Raveth stronger, long term. Besides, Raveth's high level meant he might have the fortitude to keep his Suppression Gift active while Purging. Thomas couldn't afford that chance.

No, he fully intended to use his Living Architecture to disconnect every one of Raveth's internal organs he could reach from his blood supply. Thomas didn't know elfin anatomy, but that should do the trick.

Doing that would split Thomas's own soul in two, but then again so would becoming a slave.

Except when he mentally reached out to Raveth, he found he could connect to him not only through his Living Architecture but his newest skill: Wellspring Which Waters Its Own Roots.

Through his Wellspring skill, he felt like to like. Raveth had Wellspring mana as a foundation of his core. Light mana to a lesser degree as well.

Thomas's skill was meant as a self-buff and maybe eventually a way to reinforce and buff others, right? Akilah had even warned him against it. Now, through this connection, he felt he could manipulate Raveth's Wellspring mana.

What if Akilah had actually handed him a weapon? One he knew would work very well against Light elves?

The shock of that revelation caused Thomas to hesitate.

Raveth quickly brushed Thomas's grip away. "What are you doing?"

Too late. Thomas had gotten a feel for Raveth's Wellspring mana now, and it turned out he didn't even need a physical connection to access it.

Raveth was higher leveled for sure and finely organized. His core ran like a smooth clockwork with trickles of mana shunting in precise measurements to a multitude of skills. There were a lot of skills. Most seemed to run in background operations, but unless Raveth was level 50, he'd figured out a way to shove more skills in per level. Thomas had no idea what they all did, except he saw they'd been optimized to a T.

So Thomas reached for the elf's mana through his Wellspring skill shard and yanked as hard as he could. His own personal "fuck you" from him to the guy wanting to sell him into slavery.

Every Wellspring mote in Raveth's body and core was suddenly shunted slightly out of place and energized by Thomas's own flavor of mana, which had echoes of Healing, Light, and Adaptation to it.

None of that was dangerous, but it had to be a hell of a shock to the elf's cute little setup there.

He felt Raveth's Wellspring mana go wild in a reaction that cascaded to his other mana mix. His skills, which had been finely tuned by years of careful mana cultivation, shut down.

Thomas had basically hooked up a dirty car battery to kickstart a delicate computer system. No, Raveth's core did not like it at all.

Raveth let out a gasp like he was suddenly choking on air. But just because his mana was rioting didn't mean his physical self was affected. Raveth's retaliation was instant, flinging out a sloppy punch that struck Thomas right in the chest.

He'd definitely been holding back before. The sheer strength behind the impact sent Thomas hurtling to the side where he smacked up hard against the far wall. He hit with a bright burst of pain he somehow saw behind his open eyes.

However, that got him out of the way of the FOOSH of white-hot fire that crashed against Raveth. Looking up blearily, he saw that Zach had teleported in front of the reeling elf. Either that or Thomas had lost a few seconds after hitting the wall.

Living fire crackled off Zach's hands and arms, and seemed to empower his speed as he struck the reeling elf over and over.

A crash and the snap of electricity drew Thomas's attention to the other side of the room where he saw Twitch pairing off against the heavier, armored Laveris. The elf was striking back with what looked like a metal billy club, but Twitch could take whatever was dished out to him and immediately snap back into shape again, thanks to his enhanced Gift.

Jo was nowhere to be seen but was probably setting up an attack like a sneaky assassin. No, wait, one of Zach's daggers just erupted out of Laveris's shoulder. He must have passed them to her on the sly, and now they leaked poison mana.

That was all the attention Thomas had for his team because he was kind of in a lot of agony.

None of it came from his spirit. Apparently, disrupting someone's mana in pure self-defense wasn't enough to trigger backlash. However, several ribs, his right hip, and possibly his shoulder had been broken by being launched across the room like a Loony Tunes character.

Ooh, he hoped that hurt Raveth just as much. Dick. Come hell or high water, Thomas was going to find a way to turn these two elves into loot.

Ruthlessly, Thomas clamped down on his core and demanded all the healing it would give him. His level-four core responded beautifully. Well, it already had been, but now he was actively directing the healing, and the fog in his brain started to clear. Where were his weapons? His backpack was... somewhere. He'd lost track of it in the nonsense with the elves. He would have to work with what he got.

His shoulder literally snapped back into place with a crunch. Levering himself up to his feet, he saw that while Raveth was visibly burned in several places with a dagger matching Laveris's leaking poison mana sticking out of him, his level meant he wasn't down for the count. He was steadily backing up toward the portal.

If he got through, he would be able to call for help. Then they'd be hooped.

It had only been a few moments and Thomas wasn't healed yet. He could only take shallow sips of air through cracked ribs, and something in his hip kept catching with every step. None of it mattered as he limped toward the control panel at top staggering speed. He needed to shut that down.

It seemed Raveth would get to the portal first, though. Worse, Laveris seemed to be getting the upper hand in his fight with Twitch. With a brutal downward chop, he knocked the tank down hard, then turned to help Raveth.

Thomas wasn't going to be fast enough to make it to the control panel in time.

And that was the moment when Tiny, who now looked like every muscle had been outlined in sinew, barreled straight into Laveris. Sue used her Gift to disarm him, and Holton did something that made Laveris bellow out in fury.

A wave of artificial rage overtook Thomas, thanks to Tiny, and he used it to buoy him and pick up his pace. His healing mana came in clutch at that moment, and he was able to take a normal step, then another, then a half-run, sprinting the last few steps towards the control panel.

He still wasn't fast enough. Raveth was about to step through.

Well, it worked the first time. Thomas reached out and yanked hard on his mana again. It didn't seem to affect as much Wellspring as before, but Raveth let out a strange, high-pitched cry, like an eagle. He misstepped to the side a bare stride from the portal.

Thomas pressed the X button, and the portal shut off, closing Raveth's escape route.

Zach rubbed salt in the wound by launching a ball of white-hot energy tinged with black at him. Looked like he'd been charging that up. Now Raveth was the one who went flying, and this time his robes were definitely smoking.

Movement out of the corner of his eye caught his attention: Frederic was charging straight at Thomas.

Actually, 'charging' was a vast overstatement. He was still badly injured and staggering. But he wasn't holding any weapons. Neither was Thomas, actually, and he was in much better shape, so he wasn't intimidated.

Then a stone-faced Courtney joined him, and she held her dagger which practically radiated Corruption mana.

"Move aside!" Frederic barked at him, like Thomas was a servant.

Thomas wasn't going to roll his eyes and give them the opening to attack, but he really wanted to.

Courtney gave him a sorrowful look. "Look, you've won, okay? We just want to go home."

"So you can go back and report to your family what happened? Yeah, no." He drew himself up. His shield had been flung off somewhere in this room, but that was okay. Thomas could heal whatever these two threw at him. "You're going to have to get through me to access this control panel, then through my team to get to the portal. Give it up, Courtney. You know we'll treat you fairly." He made no promises for Frederic.

"Enough!" Raveth suddenly bellowed, and his yell came with a burst of aura pressure that everyone seemed to feel.

Thomas risked a glance and saw that Zach had managed several good burns on the elf. He was going to have to regrow his white hair. He was also reaccessing his skills again.

Well, that would never do. Thomas reached out with his Wellspring and yanked hard, a third time, giving it everything that he had. Just as before, he felt a lesser amount of the mana freak out... except a strange gap opened also in the clockwork of his magical matrix. Like the failures were cascading beyond Raveth's control.

Apparently, that gap was enough for the resident mana master. In the next second, the elf literally exploded in fire so hot that Thomas felt it across the room.

Before he could parse what was going on, Zach seemed to step out of that fire, looking grim and triumphant.

Had he just... teleported into Raveth?

Thomas knew he shouldn't have taken his eyes off Frederic and Courtney for even that long, though everyone else had watched Raveth's death too. Thomas started to flick his attention back, but then he saw glittering shards fall from what was left of the elf's body, as well as Wellspring mana crystals. That was the high-level stuff. Finally! He could use—

"Frederic, don't!" Courtney yelled.

Thomas turned back just in time to see Frederic had closed the distance and was reaching for him, teeth bared with blood and green Corruption mana showing in his teeth.

No, he was reaching for the control panel behind Thomas.

No... he was reaching for Thomas's shadow which fell over that control panel.

Thomas brought up his hands to shove him away. He held his breath, ready to establish the Support Bridge and stop this guy cold.

At that moment, Frederic grabbed his shadow.

That was a sensation he'd never thought he'd experience, or had even been possible. Under Frederic's Gift, his shadow was not only something physical, but intrinsically part of him. A part he very much did not like having an enemy handling.

Thomas activated the Bridge just as Frederic activated his Gift, presumably to possess him as he had with Antonin. Healing mana met Corruption mana, and it was a lot like running face-first into a wall of acid. A wall that also had a repelling force and made Thomas feel soiled down to his core. Even the Brighten Wizard's Dark mana hadn't felt like this.

"Eugh!" He yanked his hands away.

Frederic did the same, shaking out his wrists like Thomas was the gross one here.

He opened his mouth to say exactly that when Courtney said from behind him, "Sorry about this."

Then she stabbed her Corruption dagger right through Thomas's left kidney, twisted the dagger in one movement, and broke the blade right off inside him.

He'd been wrong before. Getting thrown against the wall and breaking a few bones wasn't agony. This was agony.
