Jorn liked to practice the bow. Not because he needed one, but because it was calming, and helped him feel closer to his father.

Maybe their lives were vastly different, maybe they would never see each other again…but it helped bridge that gap.

Currently he was practicing his aim on distant rock.

Normally shooting a rock would be a gross misuse of arrows, but the materials of his arrows practically required him to shoot rocks or else they would bury themselves so damned deep that he wouldn’t even be able to see the fletching.

The bone arrow quivered against the string, eager to leap up and do its own thing, and only begrudgingly holding still while Jorn shot it like a normal arrow for the sake of practice.

Jorn had a lot of free time as the Burned Stronghold’s monster-culling ranger. His summons did the vast majority of the work clearing the land, and June was pretty easy to get along with as his direct superior.

All he had to do was give her a brief head’s-up on where the good hunting grounds were every couple days, and she was fine.

Heath was an asshole, but the man spent most of his time dealing with other assholes.

So Jorn spent most of his days practicing his scrimshaw, his archery, or hanging out with Lily.

Jorn let the arrow fly. It hissed forward and pierced the target a few feet left of where Jorn had intended to hit.

Hmm.

With a mental nudge, the bone arrow leapt up and flew back to the bowstring, waiting for his next attempt.

“JORN!” an inhuman voice shouted into his ear.

“GAH!” Jorn flinched as he loosed, causing the bone arrow to vanish into the distance.

An instant later, the arrow came back, spinning around him defensively, looking for the source of the voice as Jorn spun around, scanning the surroundings.

“It’s me, The Devourer!” The voice spoke.

Jorn frowned, narrowing his eyes.

He couldn’t see anything with his eyes, but his Class had remarkably high Focus. He could feel a faint smudge in front of him, as though a tiny portion of the land had been darkened by a cloud.

“Devourer of…Knowledge? Didn’t you stay with Loth?” Jorn asked.

“No, I stayed with the library.” Devourer replied. “But that’s not important! There’s weird stuff happening in Will’s tower, and Steve is to blame!”

“Steve the brewer?”

“No!”

“Steve the snake?”

“Yes! There was this hole in reality and Steve investigated it when I asked him not to, and he wound up catching some of it on his tail and spreading it across the floor! It’s probably mostly fixed itself, but I can take you to where it started. Several things fell through the hole, so there’s plenty of proof of things missing. And when we have proof we can punish Steve!

I’m thinking we should sinter him into a decorative statue.” If The Devourer had fingers, Jorn was convinced she would be steepling them.

“Hold up.” Jorn said, holding up a hand. “What hole in reality?”

“There was a tear in reality in the tower. The barrier between this Floor and the next appeared to be worn and frayed.”

“Did Steve make the hole originally?” Jorn asked.

“…No.” Devourer reluctantly admitted. “But he made it worse.”

“Did he intend to make it worse?”

“Who knows with that creature.” Devourer replied. “He could be plotting our demise this very moment. How would we know?”

“Listen, Devourer. You don’t punish a goat for eating your daffodils. You fix the fence. Have you told anyone else about the hole?”

“You’re the only person who knows I exist, and therefore the quickest person to reach an understanding with.” Devourer replied.

So I’ve gotta be the one to tell everyone. Jorn thought with a groan, massaging his neck.

I knew I shouldn’t have appreciated how much free time I’ve had recently. The gods heard me and decided to punish me with more responsibility.

Well, whatever. Jorn’s job was to handle external trouble for the Burned Stronghold anyway. The world falling apart…sort of fell in that category?

“Let’s go talk to Badur and the others,” Jorn said, summoning a thunder goat and hopping on, his Leech Ability securing him to the beast in place of a saddle or reigns.

He felt a faint coldness as Devourer latched onto him, and a moment later they were on their way back to town.

RIIIIP!

Huh? Jorn felt a strange tearing sensation that seemed to travel through his body.

He reflexively checked himself for wounds, but didn’t find any.

“What was that?”

“STOP!” Devourer yelled, the faint sensation of tugging on his earlobe.

“What, what is it?” Jorn asked, bringing his flying goat to a halt.

“Look at the Burned Stronghold!”

Jorn paused and narrowed his eyes, really looking at the stronghold below them.

“I don’t see anything wrong with it.”

“It’s frozen.” Devourer said, causing Jorn to take a second glance.

There was a man loading crates onto a wagon who hadn’t moved in several seconds with a heavy crate fully outstretched, ready to be set down.

The pedestrians on the street were stopped mid-stride their front feet hanging in the air.

What the Abyss? Jorn blinked and everyone started moving again.

“What was that!?” Jorn demanded.

“I think…that was spacetime coming untethered.” Devourer said.

It can do that!? Jorn thought. “…Is it safe to go in there?”

“I’unno.” He felt Devourer shrug. “It might not matter one way or the other. would you be able to tell if your time was stopped? It might’ve happened to you already and you wouldn’t know it.”

“So it’s safe?”

“…as far as you’ll ever know.”

Jorn took a deep, steadying breath before urging his mount into action.

No sense hesitating. Lily is down there.
