After returning from Volstarn, Oswald reported the quest’s completion to the Astral Wing Adventurer’s Guild. The process went relatively smoothly, as Doctor Cecilia’s letter had already arrived ahead of him, sparing him the trouble of explaining everything in detail. Still, the sight of her handwriting stirred up memories he would’ve rather left behind for good.

Luckily, Selene had pulled a few strings behind the scenes and arranged for a team of discreet physicians to be sent to Volstarn under the guise of a routine health inspection. Her own cough also seemed to have disappeared for good.

As for the town itself, Volstarn had been left deeply unsettled. Whispers clung to its narrow streets like morning mist, carrying rumors and tales of what befell Doctor Cecilia, her nurses, and the once-proud estate.

Among the many stories, one rose above the rest: that the doctor had delved into forbidden rites, invoking powers not meant for mortal hands. The gods, angered by her trespass, cast judgment upon her home. And on that fateful night, as the estate burned and bodies vanished, a thick, unnatural fog blanketed the town, escorting the dead into the unknown.

Selene didn't seem to care about those stories though. Her focus soon returned to training Oswald again.

After finishing some paperwork at the guild, he was again training with Selene in her forest. Specifically, they were standing in a large clearing. Vivi was taking a nap under one of the trees nearby. It was more or less what was expected of the cat.

Selene stood a few paces away from Oswald, her white cloak fluttering slightly with the wind. “Before we begin, you should check your system.”

Oswald gave a slight nod and pulled his focus inward. Let's see what we have here…

Souls: 3970

Resonance: 2

Invocations:

I - Malevolent Devouring Pyre: When activated, this ability enables the user to generate an explosion of black flames at a point of their gaze. Excessive use may result in visual impairment, including potential blindness.

Days until Tribute: 30

Skills:

Slaughterborn (Special): Awakens a primal fervor for battle, enhancing reflexes, agility, and martial prowess as combat continues. These effects are amplified when facing stronger foes or overwhelming numbers of opponents.

True Sight (Tier 1): Grants the ability to see through darkness and illusion magic.

Gaze of Doom (Tier 1): When activated, drains the life force of any target that makes direct eye contact.

Ascended Physique (Tier 1): Grants enhanced physical capabilities, surpassing ordinary human limits.

Mana Sense (Tier 1): Grants the ability to perceive and detect mana cores within nearby living beings.

Instinctive Mana Control (Tier 2): Enables the activation and utilization of the mana core automatically, without conscious effort.

Soul Binding Chains (Tier 3): These chains draw upon willpower to bind both flesh and spirit, anchoring the chosen target in body and soul.

Pact Weapon - Zareth, the Unmaker (Tier 3): A relic of the Old War, this weapon was designed to counter foes with advanced regenerative abilities. Its true power, however, may only be wielded by those chosen by the Emperor.

Oswald blinked and let the data settle in. “I’ve got way more souls now than before. Also… I got a new invocation, that one is new."

“What is it?” Selene asked.

“It lets me look at something, and then I can make an explosion of black fire. There’s a catch though. If I use it too much, the system says I might go blind, permanently.”

“That does sound quite powerful, but I suppose that’s only to be expected of an invocation.”

“So… should I test it out now? mean, not tryna destroy the forest or nothin’, but I could find a patch of dirt or—”

“No, as much as it would be prudent to test its range and effect, I am not about to let you risk your eyesight for the sake of curiosity.”

“Alright, alright. Was just askin’.”

Oswald kicked at a tuft of moss near his boot and let the thought trail off. Guess it’s better not to get cocky about it… But man, blowin’ stuff up with just a look? That’s wild.

“For now, speak with your Animus Spirit. Spend the souls you’ve accumulated. Once you return, we will resume training." Selene said.

Oswald nodded. “Sure. That’s fine by me. What about you, though? You know it usually takes me a couple hours. What’re you gonna do in the meantime?”

Instead of replying immediately, Selene stepped toward the edge of the clearing. She stopped beneath a gnarled tree, one with broad roots that rose and curled like the backs of sleeping beasts, then lowered herself to the ground.

“I will speak with my own Animus Spirit,” she said, folding her legs beneath her.

Huh. Didn’t think she needed to do that much anymore, Oswald thought, watching her settle down..

He gave a small shrug and walked a few paces to the opposite side of the clearing. There, a low-hanging tree offered just enough shade and comfort without completely blocking the sun.

Oswald dropped down with a grunt, legs crossed. His back pressed against the tree trunk as he tilted his head back and exhaled slowly. Time to get this over with.

He closed his eyes and reached inward, past flesh and blood, into that strange core of himself that pulsed with something colder, older, and vastly more dangerous. It was like diving into a lake with no bottom.

The clearing faded. And in its place… blood.

When Oswald opened his eyes, the Animus Realm greeted him with the same dreadful familiarity. Tiny flecks of crimson drifted downward like ash, vanishing before they reached the ground.

The sky above churned slowly, painted in shades of red too deep to be natural. Below, the rivers of blood flowed sluggishly, winding through the terrain like veins across a corpse.

Just ahead, perched atop a jagged cliff carved from bone and glistening black stone, was Zvarah. She hadn’t moved. She hadn’t spoken.

But Oswald knew she was watching. Looks like I don't need to look for her.

Oswald approached the demoness and lifted a hand. “Hey."

Zvarah's smile came slowly, curling at the edges with that familiar knowing, like she had already read his thoughts before he spoke. “Welcome back, you’re earlier than I expected.”

She tilted her head slightly, letting her gaze drift over him. “You are here to spend some of those souls you’ve hoarded, yes?”

Oswald gave a short nod. “Yeah, I’m here for that.”

“Excellent. However… before we continue, I wish to revisit our little agreement.”

“What agreement?”

“The one regarding my tribute. The thirty souls you owe me every thirty days. I believe it’s time we renegotiate, yes?"

“Why're you bringing this up now?”

“Simple, because as your power increases, so too does my hunger. I must consume more if I am to sustain my presence in this realm."

“Fine, what’s the new rate?”

“Ten souls per day, or in other words, three hundred souls every thirty days.”

“Three hundred? What, are you trying to rob me blind now?”

“Calm down, you have grown far stronger since our last exchange. A higher tribute reflects the worth of your evolution, this is fair.”

“C’mon, can’t we work something out and lower the price a bit?"

“If I wished to bleed you dry, boy, I would ask for several times higher than this amount and watch you crawl to meet it. You should know generosity when you see it." Zvarah said.

“So what, you just gonna raise the tribute again next time I blink?”

“I will raise the tribute only when our resonance deepens. That is fair, yes?"

He stared at her for a long moment, then exhaled slowly, dragging a hand through his hair. She ain’t wrong. I have gotten stronger.

“Fine, as you keep to that… I’ll take the deal.”

“Good. Then we should not delay, yes?”

The air shifted. A faint whisper swept through the space as an unseen force etched itself into his being.

[Pact Terms Updated!]

[The Tribute is now 10 souls per day, to be paid at the end of every 30 days]

[In other words, the updated Tribute amounts to 300 souls per month]

Something shifted deep within Oswald as the pressure melted away, like a knot inside him had finally loosened. The heaviness in the air faded, and with it, a faint ringing in his ears.

He clenched and flexed his fingers once, grounding himself. “Right then, show me the skill shop.”

Zvarah's wings unfurled just enough to stretch into the crimson sky. She lifted both hands in a sweeping gesture. “With pleasure.”

[Displaying Skill Shop]

1 New Pactbound Skill Available [Cost: 1000 Souls]

Note: Pactbound Skills always start at a minimum of Tier 3!

Oswald’s gaze snapped to the glowing text. “That’s… steep.”

Zvarah turned toward him, her red eye gleaming with quiet amusement. “You possess nearly four thousand souls, boy. One thousand is hardly a dent.”

"Yeah, and I don’t like being fleeced either.”

“Fleeced? Do you believe it takes no effort to bestow you with a Pactbound Skill? These are not minor tricks or party favors, boy. They draw from my very essence. I merely ask to be compensated fairly for the strength I give you.”

Oswald watched her for a moment, then clicked his tongue. “But if it’s a thousand for this one, what’s the next one gonna cost me?”

Zvarah sighed, brushing a lock of red hair over her shoulder. “I will hold the price at two thousand souls for the next. In return, it shall be a minimum Tier 4 skill."

Oswald crossed his arms, thinking. So I can buy one now, and another right after, and still have about nine hundred or so souls leftover.

"That’s nice and all, but how do I know you ain’t trying to scam me?”

“You do recall that Selene possesses an Animus as well, no? You are free to ask her whether my prices are fair. In truth, Oswald, you are receiving a fair rate. Not every Animus Spirit is so… generous.”

He sighed and shook his head. “I’ll buy the skill. But if I find out you’ve been bleeding me dry, we’re gonna have a long talk.”

Zvarah’s grin widened. “Oh, I do look forward to it.”

[Deducting 1000 Souls]

[Payment Successful!]

Oswald’s eye twitched as a sharp, tugging pressure surged behind the patch over his right eye, the one that always burned when something deep within him stirred. Glowing red particles, fine as ash and hot as coals, drifted from his right eye and rose into the air.

The particles arced toward Zvarah in a stream, drawn to her like embers to a flame. She stood still, head tilted back, arms slightly open. Her lips parted, not in a smile, but in reverence.

She drank all of the souls in. The red motes sank into her chest, vanished into her skin like ink sinking into parchment. Her body shuddered once, a low sigh escaping her throat. “Ahhh… such richness, you’ve been feasting well.”

Oswald crossed his arms and raised an eyebrow. “You done?”

Zvarah chuckled softly, licking a fang as though savoring the taste. “Almost. But fret not, I haven’t forgotten what you deserve.”

[New Pactbound Skill Acquired: Infernal Constitution (Tier 3)]

Infernal Constitution (Tier 3): The host’s body is infused with demonic resilience, enabling rapid regeneration of wounds.

A grin crept across Oswald’s face. Now that’s what I’m talkin’ about. Gonna be a damn lifesaver once things go south again.

He looked up at her, his voice lighter now. “You ready to hand over that second skill too? Don’t think I forgot.”

Zvarah raised a brow, her expression unreadable for a moment before softening into a sly smile. “Of course.”

[Displaying Skill Shop]

1 New Pactbound Skill [Cost: 2000 Souls]

Note: Pactbound Skills now start at a minimum of Tier 4!

“Okay, I want the skill,” Oswald said flatly, not bothering to hide the tension in his voice.

Zvarah’s smile deepened. “Very well.”

[Confirmed: 2000 Souls Spent!]

More pressure surged behind his right eye, harder this time. Crimson energy, hotter and heavier than before, erupted in threads from beneath it. The particles lashed outward in sudden bursts, drawn to Zvarah like moths to a roaring flame.

She inhaled slowly as the motes hit her chest and dissolved into her skin. A shudder ran through her frame, and she closed her eye, lips parting in a quiet sigh of pleasure. Her wings fluttered once, the tips dragging faint trails of ember through the air. "Yes... exquisite. This offering bears the weight of true strength. You've weathered fiercer battles, may your path lead you through many more."

“This skill better be worth the price.”

“Oh, it is. I believe you will enjoy this one. But first, extend your hands, Oswald.”

Oswald paused only a moment before he lifted his hands, palms up. The faintest tremble edged his fingertips, but he held them steady.

"Then what?" he asked.

Zvarah stepped closer, her presence pressing against him like a rising tide. Her hands slipped beneath his, smooth and cold to the touch. “I'm warning you, boy, this will hurt."

Oswald met her gaze. “Got it."

All of a sudden, black fire bloomed in her palms. It ignited in utter silence, no roar, no crackle, living presence of flame.

It slithered up his arms like molten tar, wrapping around his wrists and fingers with terrible purpose. Heat surged through his skin, biting into flesh like red-hot blades.

The pain was instant: pure, raw, and unrelenting. It didn’t just burn, it dug in.

Pain shot up his nerves, so much so that he felt that his spine was on fire. His knees buckled slightly as his boots sank deeper into the viscous, blood-slicked ground beneath him. He grit his teeth so hard his jaw ached, trembling as the infernal flame wormed deeper, past muscle, past memory, past soul. Fuuucking shit!

The pain reached a peak… and then broke. Like a wave crashing and pulling back, the fire sank fully into him. The black flames vanished without a trace. Smoke no longer rose from his skin. Burn marks faded, leaving his hands unmarred, but they felt different.

[New Pactbound Skill Acquired: Balefire Surge (Tier 4)]

Zvarah stepped back, releasing him. Her wings folded neatly behind her once more, and her expression returned to something calm.

Oswald drew in a shaky breath. The pain had passed, but its memory still clung to his nerves. He clenched his fingers experimentally. There was no visible damage, but he could still feel the heat.

He slowly straightened up. “Mind telling me what that skill was all about?"

Zvarah tilted her head, lips curled in that half-smile that always danced on the edge of sinister and satisfied. “As before, I will show you.”
