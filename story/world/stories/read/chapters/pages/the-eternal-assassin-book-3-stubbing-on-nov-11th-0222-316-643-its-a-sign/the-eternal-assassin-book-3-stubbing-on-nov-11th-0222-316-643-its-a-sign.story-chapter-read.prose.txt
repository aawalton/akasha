But even so, they were still pushing deeper and deeper into hostile territory, and Asher didn’t doubt that just like with the Deadlands, the farther into the forest they ventured, the more dangerous their foes would become.

Though if they ran into a second version of Smiles, he was going to throw a fit.

“Swarm on the right!” Asher called out, Shard Detection giving him ample warning as he picked up the cluster of shards rapidly approaching their location. “Get ready!”

The moment the first of the monsters burst out from the trees, revealing itself to be some form of lemur with far too many arms and teeth, an arrow slammed into its head, sending it tumbling to the ground. Of course, that was only the first of many, and the rest of the swarm raced over their fallen comrade, grunting and screeching as they attacked.

Moxy’s Air Blasts and Samantha’s arrows took care of almost half their number, but six of them managed to get through the onslaught. Two threw themselves at Rulfar’s face, no doubt intending to crack through his helmet and eat it right off of him. Blocking one of their bites with a dagger, he twisted the hilt and slashed through the upper half of the monster’s skull, dropping it in an instant. The next fell almost as quickly, receiving a dagger thrust to the heart when it tried to wrap its arms around him. The third attempted to leap past him and go for Samantha, but Asher stopped it dead in its tracks with Spatial Lock, chuckling as the monkey smacked its head into the invisible barrier and began screeching in confusion before he finished it off.
