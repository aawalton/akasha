James awakened to fingers running over his hair.

“Mm, that’s nice,” he muttered quietly.

“I knew you’d be all right,” Mina said, her voice calming and gentle in his ears.

“Fisher Emperor!” Another voice cut through the soothing feeling James was getting from Mina and stabbed into his brain like a power drill.

“Ugh, who’s speaking?” James groaned. “Kill them for me, please.”

“He doesn’t mean it,” Mina said gently to quell the noticeable silence that followed.

James finally blinked his eyes open and saw that he was now in the stands alongside Mina, Zora, Yulia, Junior, Abhi, Zhang Chāo, Han Jianguo, and a gaggle of James’s other supporters.

He immediately sat up. “The fighting isn’t over yet?”

“You weren’t out for that long, skapi,” Mina said. “You just needed a little bit of healing from Yulia.” She raised her voice, obviously speaking for the benefit of everyone else. “It’s a shame you didn’t have that before the match, or you would be the one in the finals now.”

“That Martial Emperor is something else,” James said, “but I do think I might have had a chance if I knew what I was getting into beforehand, or if I’d had more time to heal and recover.”

“If you knew what you were getting into?” Zhang Chāo asked curiously.

“Yeah, I guess his secret’s out now,” the Emperor replied.

“No one knows what you’re talking about except you,” Mina said quietly before James could add anything else. “He didn’t show off anything except basic martial arts against anyone but you. No one else has performed as well as you did against him. There was a moment when he used some magic in the fight with you, clearly. But we have no idea what his powers really are. Also, he’s in the final bout now.”

He nodded. Thanks for that, Sunshine.

“Then I’ll keep his secret, after all,” James said slowly. “Unless one of us needs to fight him, it’s not as if it’s incredibly relevant information for us to discuss. He might even be offended.”

Just a little piece of information that only I possess, about probably the strongest man in the world.

James turned his head to face the arena. There, he saw the Martial Emperor squaring off against a man with fur and fangs.

Not my Werewolf, but a Werewolf, James thought. Damien was sitting a few places away from him, literally licking at some wounds on his bicep. What a farce. The human section of this Tournament is ending with a secret Vampire versus a Werewolf. Those of us who are still even arguably human are just out of our league, eh?

He and the others watched as Abaturov dismantled the wolf-man before him, literally ripping the Werewolf’s limbs off and beating him with them until eventually, his opponent’s regeneration was overtaxed by the sheer blunt force trauma and blood loss from being hit with the same moves over and over again. And the Martial Emperor did it all with an air as if he was bored.

On the bright side, James thought he also would have beaten the Werewolf, though perhaps not so handily that he could have shown the other Emperor’s sheer disdain at the challenge.

James could feel Abaturov looking at him as the Werewolf crumpled to the ground before the Martial Emperor. The Vampire had to be wondering if James would share his secret with others, or perhaps thinking that he’d had a more interesting fight with the Fisher Emperor.

Or maybe he was thinking about killing James to silence the Fisher Emperor forever. But that was probably not where Abaturov’s head was at.

Then the Vampire and Werewolf vanished from the arena.

James was startled to realize, all of a sudden, that the Tournament was actually over. The System announcer’s voice filled his ear.

[Congratulations, all our winners, runners up, and other people who technically participated! Remember, if you were in the Tournament and lost, you are one of the strongest beings in the world. We still have great hope for all of your futures, and you will have another opportunity to prove yourselves one year from today.]

Wow, getting a pep talk from the System… Why do I feel more like shit after that?

[Your prizes for your performance in the Tournament, personalized based on our assessment of your needs and scaled to your respective achievements, will now be delivered into your Small Bags of Deceptive Dimensions. Please enjoy!]

“Oh. That’s a good way of keeping it secret.”

[In five minutes, you will be returned to your pre-Tournament, pre-Summit locations.]

[00:04:59]

“It was a pleasure meeting you, Fisher Emperor,” Zhang Chāo said.

James turned to look at him, and he saw the Heavenly King wore a small but sincere-looking smile.

“I don’t think I’ve ever gotten to know someone so quickly before,” James said, thinking of their bout in the arena.

“Next time, I’ll win,” Chāo replied, an edge to his smile now.

“I’ll be stronger next time.”

James, Chāo, Han Jianguo, and the others made light conversation for the next few minutes. Rotter had already exchanged some information with them, and James also gave Zhang Chāo a parting gift: two small spiders made from James’s hair.

“To help us keep in touch,” the Emperor said. “It’s good to have redundancies.”

Before they knew it, James and the others had returned home. He blinked, and he found himself back in the Fisher Empire. He let out a little sigh, not sure if he was relieved it was all finished or wishing he could do it all over again.

If he could fight Abaturov with the information he had now, he was almost certain he could win. But the information might not be enough, given that he’d had a grueling battle with Zhang Chāo beforehand.

Just one man after another, two fellow Rulers could bring me down. They didn’t even have to team up on me. There was the proof, if he needed any, that he wasn’t strong enough yet. He shook his head. I’ll conquer the rest of Florida and kill or subdue all the other Rulers. That will kill two birds with one stone: training and acquiring more buffer space to prevent surprise attacks.

He turned to the others, who had been transported back alongside him. He looked at Mina, then past her to Zora.

“Get anything good?” James asked. She was already looking at her item, a cloak.

His mother smiled mysteriously. “The System knows how to give exactly what we need.” She flourished the cloak and then draped it over Mina. “In my case, I needed something to help my son protect my daughter-in-law.”

James sensed ethereal magical energy running off of the cloak like water. He had no idea what it did, but he knew instinctively that the artifact was powerful. Perhaps an Ego item of some sort? It couldn’t be a cloak of invisibility like the one James had owned—until he transferred the Invisibility Skill into his own Status. That would be too basic, frankly. Normal Invisibility still left the user detectable by the senses of smell, hearing, touch, and even potentially Mana sense. Far too many weaknesses. But perhaps the cloth was nigh-indestructible or made the bearer completely impossible to perceive for normal mortals, both of which would still be valuable gifts at this point.

“Thank you,” Mina said quietly, running her fingers over the fabric, which looked luxurious as well as magical. And James just nodded. His wife had taken the words right out of his mouth.

“Did you guys get anything interesting?” James asked, turning to look at his various subordinates who had participated in the Tournament, whether in the human or nonhuman division.

“I got this Moon Stone,” Damien said, holding up what looked like an ordinary gray rock. He swallowed, looking pleased and nervous at the same time. “I think it’s supposed to help me take my Race Evolution to the next level.”

“Let me see,” said Samuel. He dug through a bag of his own, which looked ridiculously small in the hands of the massive alligator. Then he produced a crystalline shield. “Hm. I’d guess it’s meant to complement my trident…”

“My Emperor, I received a Moon Stone as well,” Luna said, looking at Damien dubiously with one of her heads that was not speaking.

“How about you?” Damien asked, looking back to their Emperor.

“Honestly, I hadn’t checked yet,” James said. “Didn’t want anyone to see and know what sort of edge I’m going to have over the competition at next year’s Tournament!”

Damien chuckled at that.

But James was with his own people now. The Fisher Emperor opened up his magic satchel to look at his own prize. A round, apparently crystal, orb gleamed in his hand.

Well, it wasn’t a weapon… Is this going to help me evolve?

He focused on it and used Identify, then read the description.

[Crystal of Knowledge: A crystal ball that allows the user to observe any person or place that the user can focus on, within 10,000 miles of the user. The crystal’s watchful eye is undetectable by any entity below the divine tier. Costs a small amount of Mana.]

10,000 miles? That might mean I can spy on pretty much any location on Earth, right? Because the distance between the poles is, um, less than that? I think? So, if that’s right, the opposite side of the world should also be less than 10,000 miles away, which means anywhere should be.

He smiled. This item was clearly one of the most powerful he possessed. It wouldn’t help him win in a fight right now, but preparation was the mother of opportunity. It was a fitting reward for someone who had only lost to the Tournament’s actual champion.

Then he frowned.

Is the System trying to tell me something here? What I was lacking was knowledge? Is there something specific I’ve been missing that I need to know? Or is this just the universe’s way of telling me that now that I’m in an active war with the Holy Kings, I’ll have to pay more attention to events outside my own borders?

There was no real way of knowing.

I need to commit now, no matter what, I’m going to obtain the power I need.

He knew just how he wanted to start out. James used his powers to broadcast a message throughout his territory.

[All former Rulers, report to my location as quickly as you can. All officers in the Imperial Fisher Army, do likewise.]

“Skapi?” Mina said, raising an eyebrow.

“What’s up?” asked Zora.

“I’ll repeat it when everyone’s here, but I want us to invade the rest of Florida,” James said evenly. “It’s time to get serious about conquering. We have a whole squad of former Rulers on our side now. It’s time to bring order to the rest of the state.”

“You don’t want to wait at all, let things settle down a little?” asked Jeremiah Rotter from a few feet away. “We haven’t yet fully integrated all of these new Races of nonhumans—”

“The survivors will be integrated well enough after they’ve fought my wars for me,” James said. “And with my new powers, I’ll be able to teleport around my territory and join them wherever it’s necessary. If they see me on the battlefield where the fighting is thickest, crushing our opponents, it will inspire loyalty to our young country more than any longer term integration would. Remember, these creatures were either born into this world where might makes right, or they were transformed into monsters from animals that also had to fight for their lives every day. The way you win their loyalty is not the way you win a human’s loyalty.”

Plus, I need to take care of Vidarr’s demand regarding Fjalar. It’s a distraction, but I don’t want to lose the power that god granted me by neglecting what he requested.

“Of course, sire,” said Rotter, bowing his head.

As James saw the first group of allies approach, he turned back to his wife.

“Once I give them their orders, I would also like to spend some time in the Dungeon,” he added, smiling. “Would my Empress care to join me?”

Mina smiled. “I would be honored, of course.”

James had discovered he was lacking. Until he drew closer to omnipotent, he would need to strengthen those around him to make up for it. And Mina was the most important one.
