A howl of agony tore through the quiet forest even as the third watchman swung his sword in a desperate, wild arc. The blade whistled through the air, missing Nightmare's flank by mere inches while the direwolf shook his prey with savage force to use him as a human shield.

Colossus charged into the center of the campsite the moment the other seven soldiers scrambled from their bedrolls. The scorpion moved with ruthlessness. Deathcoil Venom dripped in translucent beads from the tip of his stingers while the three tails lashed out again and again to pierce shoulders, arms, and thighs to inject the weakening toxin into every target within reach.

Seth dashed alongside the scorpion. He swept his arm in wide arcs, his shadow blades slicing through armors and tearing into the flesh of the disoriented soldiers.

His strikes met no shimmering barriers or hastily formed casts; Captain Michaelson's poison had already hollowed out the Wells of all the soldiers, leaving them weak and vulnerable. Only a desperate few managed to trigger the reserves of their Protecting Belts.

Seth followed through with another sternum-crushing punch that sent the soldier hurling backward to slam into a large rock.

Throughout the following day, Seth maintained relentless pressure, harassing and intimidating every contingent of the Bridan Empire that tried to push through the forest. He never stayed in one place for long. A sudden Ravenous Arrow from the trees, the distant howl of Nightmare, the glimpse of movement between trunks—each hint of his presence was enough to slow their march and keep fear gnawing at their nerves.

His persistence yielded a second successful trap. Using another vial of poison, he crippled a dozen soldiers before launching a brutal ambush from the shadows.

Seth finished the last soldier with a horizontal sweep, his Dark Scythes cutting through the man's neck in a spray of blood. Without wasting a second, he recalled Nightmare and Colossus into the teardrop necklace and began moving through the corpses to loot them. He tore the Endless Pouches from their waists and tied them in a heavy cluster he could carry in one hand.

Reinforcements would converge on the location within minutes; he had no time to strip the Protecting Belts or pry loose individual armor pieces.
