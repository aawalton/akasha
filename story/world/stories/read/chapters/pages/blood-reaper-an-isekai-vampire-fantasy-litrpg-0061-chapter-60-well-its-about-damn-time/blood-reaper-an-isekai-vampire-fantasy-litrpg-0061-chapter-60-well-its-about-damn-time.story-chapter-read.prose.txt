The forest floor, a checkerboard of rocks and patchy grass, was littered with pools of acidic goop that unleashed spirals of sizzling smoke into the air. The aftermath of the carnage lay sprawled throughout the woodland, and the distant screech of another dying slug echoed through the trees.

The acidic stench burned Ace's nose, mingling with the copper tang of his own blood. What had begun as a simple grinding expedition had descended into a desperate fight for survival against a swarm of slugs that dissolved everything they touched.

Fun.

Nearby, Olivia and Marcus hunted the alpha’s surviving minions. Without a weapon of his own, Marcus simply drove a sharpened stick through the last corrosive slug’s brain, and its gelatinous body imploded as its internal organs ruptured.

"And stay dead, you oversized twat," he muttered, wiping slime from his makeshift weapon onto a nearby rock.

Across the clearing from them, in a rare spot of clear dirt amongst the corpses, Ace sat propped against a boulder beside the massive corpse of the alpha slug, his Blood-Bonded Thornhide armor in tatters. Patches of skin across his chest and arms still bubbled where slug acid had eaten through his defenses. His vision blurred at the edges, the world tilting whenever he moved his head too quickly.

Tara knelt beside him, her hands glowing with crimson energy as she channeled the last of her healing magic into his wounds. The last of the searing pain gradually subsided as his skin knit back together, leaving behind raw pink patches where the worst burns had been.

As she worked—and to his surprise—the acid-eaten holes in the leather slowly closed alongside his wounds. The material knit itself back together, thread by thread. The edges of the burns curled inward, and blackened leather crumbled away as fresh material emerged beneath it. Each hole cracked lightly as it shrank, like paper being crumpled in slow motion. The regenerated leather carried a faint iridescent sheen that caught the dim light, revealing intricate patterns that hadn't been there before—whorls and spirals reminiscent of the alpha slug's internal structures. As the final holes sealed themselves, a strange warmth spread through the armor, as if it were pleased with itself.

He pressed his palms against the chestplate, checking for any lingering damage, but there was none.

Along with his body, his armor had completely regenerated.

"Thanks," he muttered with a glance down at his armor. "I didn't know your magic worked on equipment, too."

Tara followed his gaze, her brow furrowing. "As far as I can tell, my magic only works on living things." She reached out, brushing her fingers across the regenerating leather. "That's not me."

Huh.

Handy.

Ace's head swam with pain and exhaustion. His vision blurred yet again as he stared at the alpha’s corpse. Blue sparks fizzled across what was left of the creature’s body, and he reached out reflexively to gather his loot.

“Ace!” Tara snapped. “What the hell are you—”

His fingers hovered over the bubbling goop, coming close enough to activate the loot screen without touching it directly. He peered over his shoulder at Tara and raised one eyebrow, as if to ask what the hell she was on about.

“Oh,” she said with an awkward chuckle. “Right.”

Sparks of ethereal light erupted from the creature's remains, coalescing into a translucent screen that hovered before his eyes.

———

LOOT ACQUIRED

Acidic Residue (Common) x5

Corrosive Membrane (Uncommon) x1

Iron Chits x32

———

“Are you kidding me?” Ace scoffed in disgust. “I nearly die, and all I get is some goop?”

“A stick?!” Marcus’s voice carried through the forest, shrill and furious. “My loot is a fucking stick?!”

A giggle rang out through the trees. The System popped into existence above them, one arm wrapped around her torso and the other covering her face as she laughed. Her eyes squeezed tighter as the giggling worsened, until she finally snorted from the sheer humor of it all.

“Funny,” Ace said dryly.

“Oh my,” the System said, wiping away a tear. “It really is. It really, truly is. Enjoy your stick, Marcus, dear.”

“I hate you,” the man said with a glare.

“Little old me?” the System asked with a quick bat of her eyelashes. “Whatever for?”

Marcus shook his head as he scrolled through the rest of his loot list. "'Glowing Pebble (Common): Emits soft light equivalent to a candle. No other properties.'" He tossed the small stone from hand to hand. "All that work for a glorified nightlight."

Ace tilted his head and caught Tara’s eye. “Want my goop?”

She laughed. “Pass.”

A persistent pulsing light flickered at the edge of Ace's vision. He'd been ignoring it since the battle began. Now, however, the blinking notification throbbed with increasing urgency, like a quickening heartbeat. Each pulse sent a tiny spasm of pain behind his eyes.

He blinked twice and focused on the notification, allowing his consciousness to expand toward it. The action felt like reaching out with a phantom limb—strange, but increasingly natural the more he did it.

A flurry of notifications flashed across his vision—successful kill notices, EXP gains, HP and SM warnings.

One, however, caught his attention above the rest: a single notification that exploded into a waterfall of transparent blue text, washing across his field of vision and temporarily obscuring the forest beyond.

———

[LEVEL UP] Congratulations! You have reached Level 25!

+25 to all base stats

Evolution Stage: Nightseed Vampire

New Class Skill Available!

———

A surge of power slammed into him without warning.

It began at the base of Ace's spine—a spark that ignited into a wildfire racing up his back, igniting nerve endings as it went. His back arched as the pain overtook him, his tendons straining against bone as his body fought the violent intrusion of power.

“What's happening to—”

Before he could finish the thought, his jaw locked shut. His teeth ground together with enough force to crack the enamel.

The second wave rippled outward from his core. Blood vessels beneath his skin dilated to accommodate the surge. Each breath sent fresh agony through the expanding network, and his veins glowed faintly with dark energy that illuminated him from within.

“It’s okay,” Tara said gently. She reached for his shoulders, and her voice anchored him. "Breathe through it. We all went through this when we hit 25. It'll pass."

The ground beneath him fractured from the sheer vibratory force of whatever was happening, and the cracks spread outward in a spiderweb of fissures in the stone. Small pebbles vibrated and rose into the air, levitating briefly before clattering back down around him. Dirt particles hung suspended in the sunbeams, orbiting his convulsing body like miniature planets around a dying star.

A third wave of agony crashed through him, and this one targeted his senses. His vision fractured, splitting the world into overlapping layers of color. The physical world remained in the foreground, but behind it lurked shadowy imprints of everything—energy signatures pulsing with information his scrambled brain struggled to process. The trees bled memories. The slugs' corpses whispered secrets. The very air held patterns his enhanced perception could suddenly decode.

The forest erupted into a cacophony of previously imperceptible noises—the crystallization of acid as it cooled, the microscopic scuttle of insects beneath the soil, the subtle differences in his teammates' breathing. His own breaths thundered in his ears, each powerful enough to shake his vision.

Every cell in his body screamed in protest as it was forcibly rewritten. Muscle fibers tore and regenerated, denser and more efficient this time. Bones creaked as their structure rearranged for greater strength. His skin tightened across his frame, the pores contracting and expanding in waves as they adapted to process the corrosive residue absorbed from the slugs.

"Make it stop," he tried to say, but what emerged was a guttural growl that vibrated at a frequency low enough to disturb the pools of acid nearby. They rippled in concentric circles, responding to sound waves human ears couldn't detect.

The shadow elements of his vampire nature awakened fully, reaching tendrils of darkness from his core. They wrapped around his organs, infiltrated his bloodstream, merged with his DNA. For a moment, his shadow on the ground disconnected from his physical form, writhing independently before snapping back into alignment with a disorienting jolt.

Then came the tide of information—system updates cascading through his consciousness too rapidly to process. Achievements, status changes, ability enhancements, physical modifications, all competing for his attention as his mind struggled to categorize the deluge.

Just as suddenly as it began, the storm of power crested and began to recede. The waves of energy stabilized, integrating with his system rather than fighting against it. His rigid muscles released their death grip, his fingers uncurling from fists that had drawn blood from his palms. His jaw unlocked. His vision realigned, though the shadow impressions remained as ghostly overlays.

Ace collapsed against the rocky ground, sweat pouring from his body in rivulets that steamed where they hit the stone beneath him. Every breath tasted sharper now, richer with information his enhanced perception could now catalog and analyze. The forest around him had shifted, revealing new shades of vibrant color previously invisible to him. Sounds carried nuances that transformed the forest from background noise into a symphony of tactical information.

The world had transformed—or rather, his perception of it had.

"Level 25," he gasped, his voice deeper than before. "Fuck me."

Marcus crouched beside him, nodding with recognition. "Wild, right? Not a fan of what that felt like when it happened to me, I have to admit. Not. A. Fan.”

Tara's hands glowed faintly as she monitored Ace’s vitals. "Your body temperature is normalizing." She smiled wryly. "Welcome to the quarter-mark club."

The System appeared overhead, clapping her hands with childish glee. "Oh, you should’ve seen your face! The way your eyes went all black and leaky with shadow stuff? Classic first-timer reaction!"

Everyone groaned.

What an annoying little twit.

Ignoring her, Ace flexed his fingers experimentally. They responded with enhanced speed and coordination, the neural pathways between brain and limbs now operating at accelerated efficiency. Everything felt different yet somehow familiar, as if he'd finally slipped into a suit tailored perfectly for him after wearing ill-fitting clothes his entire life.

For the first time since arriving in this nightmare world, he felt fully present in his vampiric body. The lingering disconnect between his human mind and monstrous form had vanished, replaced by an unsettling harmony.

He wasn't just fighting with the monster anymore—he had mastered it.

Tara's expression shifted from concern to professional interest as she ran her hands above his body, not quite touching him. "Your vital signs are... different. Stronger. More stable. You've changed on a fundamental level."

"Level 25 is special," the System announced, appearing directly above Ace. She floated lazily on her stomach, her chin propped on her hands as she stared down at him with undisguised interest. "A semi-evolution point. Not quite the big 5-0, but significant nonetheless."

"Semi-evolution?" Olivia asked. Her eyes fixed on Ace with an intensity that made him uneasy.

"Think of it as a preview," the System said, rolling onto her back and kicking her feet in the air like a child at a slumber party. "A taste of what's to come when you reach the next evolution stage. New skills unlock. Powers grow."

Right

A new Skill.

Ace studied the system notification in front of him, and this time, he tapped the screen to continue to the selection options.

———

New Class Skill (Select 1):

———

“A legendary, a mythical, and a rare Skill!” The System clapped happily. “Oh, my darling, didn’t you do a good job? Only the best for my favorite!”

Marcus whistled softly in admiration. “Three Skills that good? Damn. You're a fucking monster, Ace."

"Thanks," Ace replied dryly. "Just what every guy wants to hear."

"What do they do?" Olivia asked, edging even closer.

“Let’s find out,” Ace said as he tapped the first Skill in the list.

"Oooh, Royal Tribute," the System explained, her voice taking on an instructor's tone. "One of my favorites! You can temporarily access the muscle memory, combat knowledge, and techniques of those whose blood you've consumed. Higher Intelligence means longer duration and clearer memory. Think of it as... borrowing your dinner's talents."

"That's disgusting," Tara muttered.

"That's fucking brilliant," Marcus countered.

Ace glanced at the others, momentarily hesitant to share his options with them, but he quickly dismissed the thought. They had already shared all of their Skills and powers, and this one was no different.

Onward, then.

"Muscle memory, combat knowledge, techniques..." he muttered, reviewing the description again. The ability to access the Skills of those whose blood he consumed was powerful and potentially game-changing.

But something about it unsettled him.

He closed his eyes, remembering the rush when he'd fed on Tara to restore his Soul Meter during the Behemoth fight. How her memories had washed through him—fragments of her life as a paramedic, the night shift calls, her favorite gaming chair in her apartment. The intimacy of it had been overwhelming.

This Skill would take that intimacy further. Not just flashes of memory, but actual knowledge. Combat techniques. Abilities honed over lifetimes.

The wind shifted, carrying the scent of the forest with it. Sweet with decay.

"It's efficient," he reasoned to himself. "Pure utility. Every opponent becomes a potential asset."

He'd been a soldier long enough to know that using every advantage wasn't just smart, it was necessary for survival. The fact that his Intelligence stat was high meant he could maintain multiple techniques simultaneously, potentially combining fighting styles that had never been paired before.

But there was the cost - both in Soul Meter and something less tangible. The skill description mentioned "emotional imprints." He remembered the darkness he'd felt after killing Victor, how empty he'd been in the aftermath. What would happen if he began regularly absorbing not just blood but the very essence of what made his opponents who they were?

The System description mocked the "royal" aspect of the name, and maybe she was right. There was something arrogant about consuming others' lifework and calling it a tribute. As if he had some divine right to their skills.

Still, he couldn't deny its potential. Ancient blood could contain centuries of knowledge. Monster blood could grant limited racial abilities. In a world where adaptation meant survival, this skill represented the ultimate adaptation.

“Alright,” he said under his breath as he tapped the screen again to bring up another option. “Next one—Vitality Weave.”

(Unlocks at Level 25)

RARE

Effect:

Special Property: "Blood Debt"

———

Interesting—a skill that connected allies with blood-essence threads, allowing them to share healing. In theory, perfect for keeping his makeshift team alive.

But nothing in this world came without a price.

"Blood Debt," he muttered, the words bitter on his tongue. Each time healing transferred between allies, the caster accumulated Blood Debt. At five stacks, he'd either need to feed or suffer damage himself.

The strategic benefits were undeniable. Each connected ally would increase his healing output by 10%. Connected allies would share 20% of healing received. Most crucially, when an ally took critical damage, other connected allies would grant them 5% of their current HP. In the midst of combat, that could be the difference between life and death.

But the Blood Debt requirement bothered him. The System's design was deliberate—the Skill required blood sacrifice. It wasn't enough to forge bonds between allies. He would need to feed on something or someone to maintain those bonds.

He closed his eyes, considering the implications. With each healing shared, he'd sink deeper into debt. The very act of protecting his team would drive him toward the predatory nature he'd been fighting to control.

The final line gave him pause: "Successfully feeding clears Blood Debt and grants 20% increased healing for 8 seconds."

The System had built in a reward mechanism. Not just survival, but enhanced effectiveness. She wanted him to feed. To view it not as a necessary evil, but as a strategic advantage.

But he was their melee fighter. To take this on felt kind of stupid, given Tara’s ever-growing healing power.

“Next,” he said under his breath.

———

Description: Finally learning to turn your prey's weaknesses into weapons? How deliciously vindictive! Nothing quite like turning someone's vulnerabilities into their executioner!

"Oh the third one’s a little disappointing, isn’t it, Sergeant?” the System asked. “Weakness Cascade channels stored analytical data into a devastating energy blast. Damage scales based on target analysis. The more you know about what you're killing, the harder it hits them. Perfect for our little Blood Analyst over there." She peered around, as though looking for Rachel. “Oh! I forgot that the little dear is dead. Oopsie!"

Ace's jaw tightened, but he didn't rise to the bait.

Instead, he stared at the notification hovering before him – Weakness Cascade. A Rare skill that unlocked at level 25. The rest of the Fang Gang had bedded down for the night, giving him space to consider his options. Wind whispered through the cave opening, carrying the scent of blood and decay from the forest below.

He rubbed his jaw, weighing the skill's potential. An 80 SM cost wasn’t too bad, but the cooldown of forty-five seconds meant he'd need to time it carefully in combat. It dealt impressive damage, but there was one key thing he was missing to make this Skill most effective.

Hunter's Resonance—Rachel’s ability to analyze targets and identify their weaknesses.

This was the Level 25 Skill Rachel should’ve gotten. Not him.

"Take some time to decide," the System said, rolling onto her stomach and propping her chin on her hands again. "It's a big choice. One that might determine who lives and who dies the next time you encounter something, shall we say, challenging."

“Will you go away?!” Marcus asked.

She smiled innocently. “Nope.”

Ace stared at the options hovering before him. Weakness Cascade was out, and that left Royal Tribute or Vitality Weave. A legendary skill versus a rare one. Both powerful in their own right, but serving vastly different purposes.

Vitality Weave—a powerful healing Skill that would connect his teammates, allowing them to share healing, increase his healing output, and even transfer HP to critically wounded allies. On paper, it aligned perfectly with his desire to keep everyone alive.

But Ace wasn't their healer. That was Tara's role.

Royal Tribute spoke more directly to what he was—and what he needed to be. The front-line fighter. The shield. The sword. The one who engaged the most dangerous threats directly so the others didn't have to.

Being able to heal his team was valuable. Being able to defeat whatever threatened them before they needed healing, however, was invaluable.

His fingertip hovered over Royal Tribute. The blood of his enemies would become his weapon, their strength his strength. Their life's training, his borrowed knowledge.

It wasn't just about power. It was about versatility. Unpredictability. The ability to change fighting styles mid-combat, to wield a hunter's bowmanship one moment and a brawler's technique the next.

Ace accepted Royal Tribute.

In this world, sometimes the best protection was ensuring nothing survived long enough to harm what was his.
