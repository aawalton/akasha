I couldn't hold myself back from asking the only question on my mind. "How did you get trapped then? Did Continuity not help you? If you're this strong how did it happen, and how did the scientist even turn you into a plant!" I couldn't help it. I was angry.

"I trust Quinn to do her part."

I didn't know how to respond to that, so I just let out a shallow laugh instead. Denying that at this point would be useless. I didn't even believe I was behaving normally. It was one horror after another, and until the horrors stopped for more than one day, I had to keep moving forward. I could process everything afterward.

Hell. I was in hell, but I'd deal with it later.
