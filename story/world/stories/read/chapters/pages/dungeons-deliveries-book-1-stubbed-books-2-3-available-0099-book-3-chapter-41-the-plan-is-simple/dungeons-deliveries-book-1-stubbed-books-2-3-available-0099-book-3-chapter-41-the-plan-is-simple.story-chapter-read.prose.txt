“So, none of this? Absolutely nothing?” Alex asked as he pulled his pinch from the bridge of his nose to gesture at the table full of relics. “You’re kidding right? And no food buffs either?”

The old lich man poked him in the chest as Francesca cheered him on. At least he had the courtesy to look uncertain.

“Ridiculous.” Snu cut in. “We’re not sending Alex to deliver pizza to Rick the Rhino. We don’t need that asshole.”

Plus you two?” Giat looked at Nino and Nina, then pointed at Mr. Mystical’s espresso pot. “And him? And Emilio and the gnomes? Come on. It’s just not necessary. There’s no need to win over Rick the Rhino too.”

Round they went in the same argument. There was no disagreement as to what the goal was. Take down the Beast of Blightwood for twisting animals and Dungeon monsters was the start. Only, there was a little problem with that. Fatima, his grandmother, was there too, and she needed to be dealt with.

The phone from the front of house rang again. It sounded different than all other calls. For some reason, the noise was warbly instead of bright, and it sent a sinking feeling straight to Alex’s bowels. Nina didn’t bustle over to answer it, either, instead giving his wife a look a concern.

Alex chewed over the thoughts of his grandmother. He’d never met the woman, but he’d seen the dark essence blanketing the Blightwood. There was something very wrong with it. Essence didn’t eat other essence.

Nino and Nina hadn’t told him the truth about their original intentions, yes, but he understood why. The System confirmed they were family, and this mystery woman pulling strings from the get-go. People weren’t perfect, least of all him.

Besides, anytime he thought about Fatima, his stomach twisted and knotted, but not in the feeling to run away. His gut was telling him to run and confront her.

He spared a look at Nina as everyone argued around him. The old lich woman had barely spoken a word. She didn’t punch dough into shape or insist upon a second sandwich. Alex had never seen her so scared.

It’d made for an awkward makeup, as he’d recognized her fright immediately. After the lich couple’s apology, Nino had hung his head and lightly sobbed as Alex returned his hug. When it was Nina’s turn, the woman had shaken like a leaf in his arms. Fear made people do funny things, like hide the truth from those they cared about. That he understood.

System, she flinches every time Fatima’s name is mentioned or the phone rings that way.

Brody bumped him from the side, nodding at the table piled with relics.

“We…don’t need relics.” His clone said softly as everyone else kept arguing about his life.

“I mean, it would be better to use this stuff.” Alex responded.

“Yeah…” Brody chuckled, picking up a card with a turtle face on it. “You even think…they’d come? The…turtle kids? If we…called?”

Alex sighed, recognizing when someone was trying to lift his spirits.

Some of the best relics Jemin had to offer, and those Alex hadn’t sold yet, were strewn about on the table. Void grenades, a bit of twine that would apparently wrap rushing monsters up in a present, caltrops that always placed themselves under feet. There was even a snoring shrunken head that Jemin swore would scream a beam of pure energy if you tickled its ear to wake it up. Perfect relics hand selected for someone with his skill set. All meant for someone who was good on their feet and could attack from a safe distance.

“Don’t…stress.” Brody chuckle gurgled. “Remember your skill…upgrades. You’re welcome.”

Alex nodded at that. It wasn’t a surprise he’d grown. With the day he’d had, he’d expected it, finnicky as the System might be. It was how he’d grown that was interesting. If totally unexpected and a bit concerning could be considered interesting.

Just to confirm he wasn’t losing his marbles, Alex pressed against the System to pull up his previous upgrade notifications.

[One of your Bonded has copied a skill with Innate Linksmith. To ensure your essence core remains intact from the accumulated stressors necessary to enable their ability, you receive +1 level in the associated skill.]

[Illusory Copy – Level 7 -> 8]

[One of your Bonded has copied a skill with Innate Linksmith. To ensure your essence core remains intact from the accumulated stressors necessary to enable their ability, you receive +1 level in the associated skill.]

[Burrow Sense – Level 2 -> 3]

[Running – Level 8 -> 9]

When he’d opened the notification at the Safety Spires, Alex didn’t believe the words. Just because of a Title, not only did Brody and Snu gain a variant on his own skill, but he received massive benefits too. Such a thing shouldn’t be possible. Neuronium had practically panicked at the scientific implications. The squad at the Safety Spires had given him some great pointers with his skillset, but all that was easier said than done in the heat of the moment.

Plus, they’d kept coming back to his skill upgrades. No one had ever heard of gaining levels just because someone else had, bonded or not.

“Something about it feels funny.” Alex said to Brody. “It’s amazing, don’t get me wrong. A cheat. With the Title, I could bond with tons of different people and level and level and level. But…”

“It’s…unsettling.” Brody nodded. “My version isn’t…the same at all.”

Alex shook his head. “Right? You can hop back and forth between the unspace. But you can also take my spot and send me there.”

“—said NO!”

The skill behind the last word knocked Alex and Brody’s attention back to the argument.

Snu had half-activated her Boss form and loomed over Nino and Francesca in sneaker heels. To his credit, the man didn’t look scared at all. He did look sheepish, though, as if he’d poked the bear one too many times.

The succubus Boss realized what had happened and quickly shrunk herself back down to her less potent physical form.

“Sorry, sorry.” She held her hands up. “Didn’t mean for that to happen. But my point stands. Alex isn’t going.”

“I’m not?”

Their eyes turned on him. He’d said it with a little bite in his tone.

“Well—I—no!” Snu stumbled for a second when he responded but came back quickly to certainty. “No you’re not. I don’t care what I have to do, but you’re not doing this.”

Instead of rising to anger, Alex walked through the kitchen to look her in the eye.

“I know you don’t want me to.” He said. “I know it’s stupid. I know it’s risky. And dangerous. And we can sit here and come up with a million reasons to not do this. But I’m tired.”

Snu blinked up at him. “Tired? What?”

Alex released her and looked over everyone in the kitchen. These were the people who cared about him. Brody gave him a little grin, knowing exactly where he was going and always having his back. Mr. Mystical poked his head out and urged him on with his eyes. Snu looked scared of what might happen to him. Giat stood cross armed with her chin up. Even Francesca didn’t pipe up.

Nino waited to let him speak. Never once had the old lich man wanted Alex to be anything but himself.

And then there was Nina, picking at her nails and looking down.

“I’m tired of running away from things.” Alex said to them all, but his words were meant for her. “I’m good at it, don’t get me wrong. But it’s like I’ve built myself into this person that is good at getting away. From monsters, from danger, from my past. I’m done with it.”

Snu tried to budge in. “But—”

“No buts.” Alex cut her off as he walked over to Nina. Kneeling by her, he reached out his hand to lay it on her shoulder.

“She makes you want to run?”

Nina nodded sharply, not meeting his eyes.

“You’re scared of her?”

She nodded again.

“And you’re the strongest person I know. Besides Emilio that is.”

A little quirk peaked from the corner of her mouth. He didn’t want to ask her his next question, but he needed to know.

“What makes you scared of her? What is it about my gran—Fatima, that makes you scared?”

Nina chewed on the inside of her cheek for a second before finally looking back up at him.

“I’ve tell you about malocchio.” She whispered. “Or you call Italian voodoo. Is old. Is very old.”

Nina seemed to choose her words carefully.

“There are many side to the malocchio.” Nina went on. “The woman of my line always try to use for good. Sometimes revenge. Sometime to solve problem. We no say we perfect.”

“No one is.” Alex agreed.

“But there is thing we never do. Thing so bad that if one of us do…well…even least favorite cousins and women called in the help fix.”

Nina swallowed a dry mouth then.

“Like what, Nina?” Alex asked, trying not to sound aggressive from the churns his stomach was putting him through.

Nina opened her small, clenched fist, revealing a tiny budding flower. It hung just above her palm and twirled gently in the air.

“Malocchio can be use to give life. Give thing.” She explained, and Alex paled as the flower began to wilt quickly. “But it can also be use to take it for yourself.”

Before his eyes, the small flower withered dry, then rotted and crackled back into a speck of dust.

“And my sister is best ever at taking and using life from other.”

She seemed to deflate at her reveal, and Alex leaned in to pull her in for a hug. He didn’t say anything to soothe her fears. Sometimes people just needed a tight squeeze.

“I scare, Alex.” She whispered to him. “I scare for you. I scare for me.  For Nino and Barty. I scare if I no stop her, she spread her malocchio until it cover everything.”

That reminded him of the smothering essence of the Blightwood.

“I’m scared too.” Alex said back to her. “But we have each other, and everyone around us to help.”

“I no think is enough.”

“And you think getting Rick the Rhino on our side will help?”

Nina pulled back from the hug to make sure he clearly understood her.

“We need every help we can get.”

Alex believed her. She’d lied to him before, or at least hid the truth, which was the same thing in his mind. But he believed her now. The way she held herself told him that.

Besides, even if she is bullshitting me, she’s my family now. She was my family before…being my family.

He almost laughed at the strange thought that passed through his mind, and Brody snorted in the background from having heard it in his own head.

“Is there anything you can do on the malocchio side? If we’re going to do this? If she’s not playing by the rules, then you shouldn’t either.”

Nina’s face turned from fear to surprise at his suggestion. Alex saw from her expression the thoughts running through her brain, then quickly solidify as a devilish grin.

“Smart smart boy.” She reached up to place a cold hand on his cheek. “Yes. It is very risk, but I will do. With Francesca. Need another woman of the family.”

The tall Italian young woman looked shocked, then eagerly nodded her head.

“And I’m going to get a buff for the Beast of Blightwood Dungeon?” Alex asked.

Nina erupted a laugh at that, seeming to throw the cast off the gloom she’d been carrying like a cloud. She shot up to a float and rubbed her hands together as the fridge full goodies and kitchen utensils started vibrating.

“Oh, I make something good.” She said. “Nino has a lot of favorite. But this is his favorite.”

Nino lurched forward. “Not the—”

“Ah!” Nina cut him off. “No say nothing.”

It was what Alex was looking for. Through the planning at Safety Spires, of the monsters and Neuronium and Mary and Jemin giving him tips. Once he’d picked up on the fact that it sounded like it was going down, he’d felt an urge. Not to run, hide, duck, dodge, slide, or teleport out of danger. Nino was right, he didn’t need a buff. He wanted revenge on someone who, for whatever reason, had it out for him.

This time, Alex wanted to run towards his problem and take it head on.

He walked over to Snu, and she threw herself to hug him tight.

“You can’t die.” She mumbled with her face buried in his chest.

He hugged her back. “You think you can get rid of me that easy? Didn’t you hear? I can do anything.”

“Don’t be dumb right now.” She murmured and squeezed harder.

“Yeah, okay. Fair point.” Alex said before speaking to everyone.  “I’ll do it. We’re gonna run Rick the Rhino’s Dungeon, get him to join up, and then we’re going right to the Blightwood Dungeon. Damnit, I’m in.”

Brody clapped him on the shoulder and grunted. “Me…to.”

The phone rang again, but thankfully it wasn’t warbly and strange. It was the normal bright ring of a regular delivery…maybe to a Dungeon Boss named Rick.

Nino stomped off to the front.

“What’s his deal, anyways?” Alex asked as Snu peeled herself off. “Why’s he want me to run his Dungeon just to help out?”

Giat groaned. “Oh, Ricks the worst. Strongest prick in Toronto, and knows it too. Said he’d only help if we’re worth helping. Got this whole weird thing about honour and heroics. I mean…to be fair, he doesn’t use Dungeon monsters. Everything in his Dungeon is just a previous zoo animal. Never once spawned a monster.”

“I know a pretty cool animal myself.” Alex added. “Not bad with honour either. Stuck to his word for Petal. But he’d not opposed to bending a few rules.”

Are you…? Did you just—Ew dude! How long ago? You’re not supposed to answer right after! Alex sent.

Nevermind! Alex sent. You ready to rumble tonight? You and the gnomes?

Thanks, buddy. He sent to Emilio. You know what we’re getting into?
