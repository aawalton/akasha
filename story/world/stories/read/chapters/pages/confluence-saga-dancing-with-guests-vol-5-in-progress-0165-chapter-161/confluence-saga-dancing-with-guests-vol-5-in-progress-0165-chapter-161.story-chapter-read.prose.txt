The impact of long duration Diving on Combat Pathers

I must preface this analysis with the caveats that all necessities are considered met. Water creators require Level 5 Energy to function and basic Food Creators, those providing tasteless but complete nutrition, require Level 15. This also ignores the social and emotional impact it may have on those who are awaiting their return.

Practical effort and challenge are seen as the primary methods of improving both Path Advancement and the more modest gains at every level, while also accelerating growth. Higher intensity is viewed as more beneficial, with only pure Energy absorption through meditation being faster, while also providing no growth beyond reported level.

Extended Dives, particularly without any sort of safety net, creates a consistent intensity, particularly when paired with facing threats that are normally considered foolhardy. Evidence of this can be found in the accounts of almost every Elite Diving team, time spent against Depthsborn multiple Levels stronger than them promotes progress far beyond what simple numbers should account for.

Multi-month dives are also known to produce epiphanies and leaps in progress. Whether this is due to continued focus on a goal, lack of distraction or simply an additional benefit is unclear. As such Divers facing the Wall are often recommended to spend as much time as possible in the Depths with only the briefest of resupplies.

Seb picked up the last piece of bacon on his plate and held it out towards Sandra with a smile.

"Would you like my last piece?"

He watched her look at it then down at her plate, still covered in bacon and he thought he heard her whine ever so faintly.

"No, I have enough bacon."

Seb chuckled, the rest of the table joining in. After a moment even Sandra was chuckling at her admission.

Sandra had pretended to try and snag a piece from Anya's plate as everyone first sat down. She'd failed, but not before some of the wait staff had noticed and asked if she wanted bacon. When Anya clarified that her sister loved bacon and would take it from any plate she could reach the staff had come back with a veritable mound of bacon.

Seb had to admit Sandra had done a good job working her way through the pile. She'd eaten more than half of it and another third had vanished as they'd taken turns leaning in to see if she needed help. She'd said no, but no one had listened. He'd seen Cara grab more than a few pieces and judging from the fact that Gwen hadn't run out she must have gotten a few as well.

"I think we figured out how to prevent bacon theft," Seb said. "Make so much that no one can eat it all."

"No!" Sandra shouted before hunching down when everyone spun to stare at her. "I mean. I don't need this much bacon and…"

Did you hear the rest of that? Seb sent.

Yes.

"What was that?" Anya asked sweetly. "You were mumbling at the end."

"And it tastes better when I steal it."

"Really?" Fay asked. "It's the same bacon, it shouldn't taste any different."

"It tastes better because it's more fun."

Seb watched as Sandra went to take a piece, only to have it vanish from under her fingers. The next did as well, and so did the next two even when she changed sides of her plate.

"Hey!"

Seb took one of the stolen pieces from Anya as Briana gave the other to Fay.

"You're right," Briana said. "It does taste better."

Seb caught a glimpse of Cara's hair flickering as the piece she was about to bite broke off at her fingers. The only sound at the table was of Gwen giggling around the piece as Cara sat frozen.

"You know I would have given you the piece if you wanted it," she said after a moment.

"I know, but I wanted to see if they were right about the taste," Gwen answered as she picked up her last piece and took a tiny bite.

"Are they?"

"I think- Hey!"

Did Cara just? Anya sent.

"That was my bacon Cara."

"Yes, and it tastes better than mine did."

"Good morning," Lord Percivus said.

Once again Seb hadn't noticed his approach, hadn't noticed his presence anywhere in the dining hall. Turning to look up at his mentor he was startled to see him holding a piece of bacon. A glance back at his plate confirmed that his last piece was gone.

"Lord Percivus," he said. "Is that my piece of bacon?"

"It was. I heard the discussion and decided to make my own determination," Lord Percivus answered before taking a bite. "The flavor is identical, the enjoyment of it however is different."

"So it tastes better?" Sandra asked.

"That is one interpretation."

"Did you come here just to try bacon?" Anya asked.

"No. I have considered Fay's request and am of the opinion that there is both precedent and a valid reason for those of importance within your Holding and close family to be aware of your Heritage."

"Briana is my girlfriend, she is not a retainer of the Holding nor is she close family to Anya," Fay said.

"She is here, party to your reunion, is she not?"

"Yes."

"Should the Holding have ongoing interactions, will she continue to be exposed?"

"Yes," Fay said. "She is important to me, so she is important to the Holding."

"As you say," he said before holding up the last of the piece of bacon. "I would suggest somewhere less public for any discussions however, Sandra's voice carries and she seems excitable."

"I am not-" Sandra said, her voice growing louder with each word until she blushed. "Sorry."

"I will see those of you who are my students once the visit is over. I hope the rest of you enjoy your time here."

Seb was looking at Percivus when he vanished. One moment he was there, speaking, the next he was gone, his words lingering in his absence before seeming to fade away. There was something there, something almost familiar, like a song he'd heard years ago and couldn't quite remember.

He'd figure it out, he didn't doubt there was a lesson to be found. Otherwise why would he be bothering to appear and disappear when he could simply approach normally. He couldn't think of any way Percivus would benefit from sneaking up on them.

"If we're all done eating," Anya said. "I believe Sandra wanted a tour?"

"Yes, but I want to know what he was talking about too."

"The training hall is almost certainly empty," Cara said.

"That would work," Seb said. "Shall we?"

Anya paused outside the training hall and waited for everyone to catch up. They'd spread out slightly during the short walk over, pairing off in quiet conversation while Seb trailed behind to make sure no one, Sandra that is, wandered off and had to be pointed back in the right direction.

Wait, Seb sent, worry filling the band. We can't take Sandra in there, the Energy density.

Anya frowned, she hadn't thought of that, none of them had. For all that Sarna had talked about dueling at Deep Low density and the differences that would entail, they had spent the entire year practicing in level 15. If they'd gone in without thinking Sandra would be lucky to walk out, she hadn't even reached the Wall.

"We'll have to find somewhere else," she said once everyone was there. "Wouldn't be safe for Sandra if we went in."

"I'll be fine," Sandra grumbled. "I won't touch any weapons or training equipment."

"That isn't the problem," Seb said. "Energy Density on the fields is level 15."

"How?"

"They're using Adjustable Density Constructs?" Fay asked at almost the same time.

"Is that what they're called?" Seb said.

"The cost to keep them running is more than most Holdings would want to spend, even Families with dozens of cadet branches rarely set up more than a chamber large enough for one-on-one sparring."

"The density shifts the moment you're in the building, we got sent in directly after our initial evaluation with the Dean and Seer," Anya said, unable to keep from frowning at the mention of Marten. "Pretty sure the Doorkeeper could have sent us there directly when we went into the school house."

"I want to see the Construct," Sandra said. "I know it's not safe, I know I can't go in there. But when else will I get the chance to see something built to set the density to anything other than Deep Low? By the time I'm able to survive in there you'll have graduated."

Anya got Seb's warning right before Mistress Sarna came out of the building and managed to not react at the voice behind her. What was with the instructors here and startling them during family reunions. It was almost as though they liked to catch them by surprise.

"The field is powered down. We turn it off during visits specifically because there's no way to predict where family members might wander off to, or whether it would be safe. We shut down the Staging Points too, not that you'd be going anywhere near them."

"Hadn't even been told there were any," Seb said. "Let alone where to find them."

"That hasn't stopped some students from finding their way to them without approval," Sarna said before stretching one arm out and pointing at Sandra. "Some people are too curious and find what they shouldn't."

"I wouldn't, not without… Is it in an interesting Construct?"

"Probably," Sarna said with a shrug. "I don't pay attention to that side of things. The training hall is safe, at least as far as Energy density."

"So we can go see?" Sandra asked the moment Sarna had walked away.

"Yes Sandra, we can go look at the interesting Construct," Anya said, trying not to sigh. "I doubt you'll see much, you can't get past the surface."

"It's still something, and I'll be able to tell Yshe about what I was shown. I wonder if she's heard of Density Constructs."

Anya caught Fay nodding slightly as if to say that Yshe would know and felt Seb's recognition of the gesture as well. Yshe knew about a lot of things, including what a Seed was at first glance. Did Fay know something about Yshe's past?

Valene did say Yshe was an old friend, Seb sent.

Anya turned to lead the way into the hall, putting her arm out to stop Sandra from racing past her. Regardless of what Mistress Sarna had said, she suspected Sandra would find her way into something that wasn't safe given the chance to wander on her own.

"I know Sandra wants to see the Construct," Briana said. "But can someone tell me what the secret is that my girlfriend was forced to keep from me despite the fact it made her feel horrible?"

Briana knew Fay was keeping a secret? Seb sent.

Apparently.

Anya could hear Cara starting to giggle behind her. She didn't turn to look, but still couldn't help but grin at what she heard.

"They're going to call you a Fairy again."

"No they won't," Gwen said.

"What's a fairy?" Briana asked.
