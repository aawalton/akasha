Sometimes, World Trees would play-fight over resources and such, but it was clearly not something they did to kill each other.

Those who didn’t like World Trees would absolutely hate a true coalition between World Trees.

Those who formed part of the World Tree civilizations would be reluctant and against becoming subordinates of a weaker civilization.

So, in a way, Liu Shu was the link that most in power never wanted to see succeed, but she was who most normal people desired to appear.
