Lara and the girls were still asleep as Logan made his way downstairs. He had to creep past the cots and blankets they’d erected in the living room. Once everyone had learned that an array protected the cabin, it became jam-packed, so packed that Lara had to put an occupancy limit on the property. Even though half the people had spread out to the neighbour’s, he could see shelter becoming a problem as the community continued to grow.

Outside, the air had started to become crisp, a gentle morning heat that rose like a roasting oven as the day progressed. Close to the shore, Logan could make out Ernie leading his minion army through drills with a voracious pace, his tentacles flying and dancing over the water as the Rattuspede’s tail crashed against the surface like a whale. It sent a wave gushing towards the shore that crashed against the dock and splashed the tiger guard dog’s bed. It yowled and clawed the wood of the dock in dissatisfaction before it crept towards the edge, staring at Ernie and letting out a yip of yearning.

Logan was on a strict time limit. He wanted to leave early in the morning so they could make good time on their way towards the queen serpent’s lair. If he wanted to, he could use [Threshold Shift] to transport Ernie’s minions all at once, but he didn’t imagine that transporting 500 minions, one the size of the Rattuspede, would stay under the queen serpent’s radar. His preference was a surprise attack, which meant they needed to approach slowly.

So, no more than an hour of preparation and then they would need to be on their way. With the Sabáktes dagger slung over his khaki covered shoulder, Logan made his way to the Tree Fridge. He already had an idea of what he wanted to buy, but it wouldn’t hurt to review his options first.

The limbs of the willow tree rustled in the breeze, sap falling in a fine mist. He could have sworn that the protruding roots looked more immense than yesterday. Each time he visited the damn thing, it grew. Would it eventually grow so large that it would take over the property?

Logan set down the Sabáktes dagger and then clambered over the roots. The lodestone felt cold to the touch as he pressed his palm against it.

Ding!

[Welcome, Idiot! Entering the Karma Lodestone Menu.]

[Would you like to view F grade or E grade options?]

“Show me the E grade options.”

[…. Conditions met.]

[E GRADE FOOD MENU:

1 bunch of herbs: 100,000 KarmaCoin

1 can of cat food: 200,000 KarmaCoin

1 rack of ribs (of indeterminate source): 1,000,000 KarmaCoin

1 can of beer: 2,000,000 KarmaCoin

E GRADE WEAPON MENU:

1 curse: 2,000,000 KarmaCoin

E GRADE CLOTHING MENU:

1 enhanced pair of self-cleaning swim trunks: 100,000 KarmaCoin

1 form fitting suit: 1,000,000 KarmaCoin

1 waterproof wetsuit: 2,000,000 KarmaCoin

*unlock further options at higher grades]

He rolled his eyes at the food options and then narrowed in on the weapons and clothing. With close to 4 million KarmaCoin, he could clear out the entire E grade menu, but 2 million was a hell of a lot for a wetsuit that he might not need if it did the same as [Deepwater Explorer].

The same with the suit; if it were anything like what he suspected, it would form armour. Logan didn’t need armour. That’s not to say that he couldn’t buy it for Lara or one of the others, but Logan needed items that would help him. At this point, he wasn’t so flush with cash that he could afford to be that generous.

As far as the curse… hell no. For all he knew, based on his luck with the System, it could be a world-ending item. He wasn’t quite that desperate. At least not now.

That did it.

There was only one item that made sense.

[Please confirm your purchase of [1 pair of enhanced self-cleaning swim trunks] at a cost of 100,000 KarmaCoin plus a 10% processing fee?]

[Yes.]

[No.]

Logan mentally confirmed his selection.

[….Confirmed. Enjoy, Idiot.]

With a thud, the swim trunks landed in front of his feet. His heart thrumming with excitement, Logan crouched, examining them. Just as garish as the ones he was wearing, orange, with the same damn kitten pattern. At first glance, they didn’t look ‘enhanced,’ or any different than his existing pair.

Logan examined them with [Idiot’s Inspect]:

[Enhanced Orange Kitten-Patterned Swim Trunks. B grade. Self-cleaning. These swim trunks cannot be torn, pierced, or lit on fire. The drawstring can turn into a weapon on its own. When equipped, receive a 3% boost to all attributes.]

Logan blinked, his stomach sinking. That was all? After the success of the regular swim trunks, he’d assumed this had to be good shit, but instead, there was only an additional one percent boost? Logan felt cheated! False advertising!

Growling, he stood up and then almost kicked the damn things, but at the last second, restrained himself. Blowing out a sigh, Logan ran a hand through his hair. In a way, he shouldn’t have gotten his hopes up. The Tree Fridge was like a mystery where you never knew what you could get. Coal or gold. Or coal disguised as gold. You could buy an item, and what appeared to be a godsend like the green beans could turn into a world-ender.

At least there was a small difference:

[The drawstring can turn into a weapon on its own.]

Logan snorted. What, like a lasso? It could be anything, but Logan wasn’t sure if it were worth it to find out. He’d be wearing these damn things underneath his clothes and his armour, so what was the point?

With a frown, he picked them up—

Ding!

[Item convergence found! The item, Orange Kitten-Patterned Swim Trunks, has been merged with the item, Enhanced Orange Kitten-Patterned Swim Trunks, to form the new item, Idiot’s Swim Trunks! B grade. Self-cleaning. These swim trunks cannot be torn, pierced, lit on fire or altered by any being known to the System. The drawstring can turn into a weapon on its own. When equipped, receive a 5% boost to all attributes.]

Logan had only half a second to comprehend the message before the swim trunks in his hands disappeared with a pop and he had—the mother of all wedgies! Logan jumped, peering at his ass, his eyes wide, his mouth parted in shock…

Oh shit! Oh shit!

The world opened up. His perception exploded, making everything around him ramp up to eleven. This was unlike [Life Fabricator] where he could sense the auras around him. No, this time, his hearing picked up the flap of an eagle’s wings as it soared through the air halfway across the lake; he could see the moisture beading on the foliage of the willow tree.

His strength exploded, making him grit his teeth as his muscles continued to fine tune. He took a step back, just barely stopping himself from jumping in place as his agility and dexterity surged. This morning, he’d already felt refreshed, but an upgrade to his constitution attribute meant he felt like he’d had the best sleep of his life.

Not to mention the upgrade to his wisdom and intelligence attributes, which made him feel as if he could perceive things at an accelerated rate, think quicker, come up with solutions in a fraction of the time.

Gasping, Logan lifted the band of his khaki pants and stared. Yep, that was a pair of swim trunks all right. Although he was excited about the upgrade, his skin grew clammy with distaste. If ever there was a time to confirm that the System was a creepy fucker, it was now. It had magicked his swim trunks! Replaced them while he was wearing them!

Still, the upgrade washed away his outrage, making him feel so excited he could burst. Logan could rail all he wanted at the System, but he knew he’d never get anywhere. An upgrade though, an upgrade he’d take any day of the week.

Logan pulled up his stat sheet and studied the changes.

Name: Logan Hart [Hidden Name: Idiot]

Karma: 4,032/4,032

Intelligence: 658 --> 672

Constitution: 172 --> 177

Strength: 372 --> 382

Agility: 388 --> 399

Dexterity: 308 --> 316

Endurance: 692 --> 705

Perception: 144 --> 168

Wisdom: 596 --> 609

KarmaCoin: 3,822,000

His mouth grew dry, his fingers twitching. Hell yes! He’d wear a frigging orange skin tight suit and look like a madman if it gave good shit like this. Logan hadn’t known that System sourced items could upgrade just like skills, but hell, he’d take it. On the morning before battle, he’d take anything.

Best of all, each intelligence point translated into six Karma points, which meant that he’d upgraded his pool to over 4,000 Karma. That would make deploying [Life Fabricator] and manipulating olivine a heck of a lot easier. The way it stood, he’d needed to use [Liche Devourer] on the insects to source enough Karma to force the olivine to absorb carbon. He didn’t want to speculate on how much Karma he’d need for his Save Humanity Quest, but it would be a heck of a lot.

Logan studied his KarmaCoin. The System hadn’t charged too much for the enhanced swim trunks, which meant that he had a ton to work with. And if he was going to war, which meant he’d be unable to generate carbon sucking trees to earn KarmaCoin…

Well, it made sense to make headway on building up his wealth.

Go broke or go home. Deploying [Empire Building], Logan toggled through to a System market and then pulled up the weapons menu.

[WEAPON MENU:]

[Regular Metal Pole – F Grade. Cost: 500 KarmaCoin.]

[Utility sword – F Grade. Cost: 2,000 KarmaCoin.]

[Leather Whip – F Grade. Cost: 2,000 KarmaCoin.]

[Large Axe – F Grade. Cost: 2,500 Karma Coin.]

Logan ran a hand through his hair as he did the mental math and mulled over his options. It was time like these that he missed his phone, if only to help him do calculations. Still, he thought he had a solution.

[Please confirm your purchase of [400 Utility Swords], [400 Large Axes], [2,000 Regular Metal Poles], and [200 leather whips] at a cost of 3,200,000 KarmaCoin plus a 10% processing fee?]

[Yes.]

[No.]

He hesitated. That was a massive cost. Accounting for the 10% processing fee, he’d be out over 3.5 million KarmaCoin, leaving a pittance. And yet… in a pinch, how hard was it to grow a monster carbon tree and generate another 400,000 KarmaCoin?

Logan mentally confirmed his choice.

[….Confirmed. Enjoy, Idiot.]

There was a massive crash, a clang of metal against metal, poles flying, swords clattering. As if a damn storage facility had just dumped every single item stuffed inside its building from up on high. Logan took a step back and surveyed the pile with a grin. It was so high it was as tall as the willow tree. Wizard shit, hell yes.

Wasting no time, Logan threw the entire pile inside of his spatial storage collar and then activated [Empire Building], putting everything up for sale for twice the price. If all went well, he’d make a profit of close to 3 million KarmaCoin in one day. He’d need a ton of people to buy items, but if word of mouth had spread? He’d be golden.

Step one and two of preparing for war—shoring up his attributes and creating passive income—complete.

Now for shoring up his defences.

Logan walked away from the Tree Fridge and over to the empty land between his property and Jack’s. The community members tended to hang around Jack’s cabin and the large neighbourhood property, so Logan had the chance to experiment on his own.

With a blink, he willed out one of the items he’d bought from the Tree Fridge.

The pouch was hefty, weighty, the fabric black velvet. Logan balanced it on the palm of his hand, letting it flex, and then peered inside.

[Renewable Flexible Crafting Metal. B grade. This metal flexes, warps, and obeys the user’s will. It will never run out.]

The System said it was crafting metal, but to Logan, it looked like fine grains of black sand. Grains so fine they were like powder. Odd. The System didn’t state what kind of metal this was, but it seemed to shimmer the longer he stared. It reminded him of the System sourced chains, yet somehow more impactful.

As far as he was concerned, it was the perfect option for his exoskeleton. Until now, he’d used sand because it was the material most readily available. And yet, in battle, it was easy to lose sand before he had a chance to throw it back in his collar.

Sand was easy to work with, a material that he could craft into sandstone, but it had drawbacks. In the fight against the Rattuspede, his armour had cracked down the back. By now, it had gotten to the point where Logan had outgrown the sandstone material. He needed something stronger, something that could withstand a mountain’s worth of pressure.

If the System stated that this metal would never run out, he wouldn’t have to worry about not having enough. Although the pouch was hefty, it didn’t contain enough material to cover his whole body unless something hinky was at work.

Opening the pouch and widening the opening, Logan set it down on the ground and then took a step back.

Here goes nothing.
