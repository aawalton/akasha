THE STORY SO FAR …

Remmi Lee, a 24-year-old engineer from early 21st-Century America, suddenly found herself summoned to Toleste, a world of Asian fantasy governed by the mysterious but all-powerful Essence System, as one of five Sacred Heroes that were intended to fight back an unexplained Darkness threatening to engulf the Furinshao Empire.

Bewilderingly being reverted to a fifteen-year-old form, she was given the unique Gunslinger class, and her personal pistol, an M1911 clone, accompanied her. Though the unprecedented event of something material accompanying a summoned Hero initially caused issues, they were soon resolved with the help of Empress Xuhitana and her mysterious elven advisor, Sacred Yorin.

In her need for training to catch up to the other four Heroes, Remmi was sent off to the distant, but relatively safe Dabun Village in the Serazin Province. There, she met and teamed up with the fashionable and cosmopolitan elven archer, Ayre, and the two became fast friends despite the foreign girl’s oddities.

Together, they delved into the region’s sole dungeon, the Forest Cavern, and encountered, even in this quiet backwater, the reach of the Darkness. Fighting through the undead that now filled the caves that were previously rich with life, the two adventurers were able to purify the dungeon’s core and free it from Corruption.

In doing so, they discovered that purifying dungeons actually created a repelling effect against the Darkness, and were soon dispatched by Imperial order to use this newfound knowledge to construct a barrier all the way up the border with the Western Demesne, the fallen land that seemed to be the source of Corruption.

On their travels to do so, they experienced many new places, like the resort-like city of Kaiwoku, and its mysterious female-only dungeon, Desert Cove. By far, however, their greatest challenges would be found in Giri Village. Its mayor, Lord Oshu, had cut off access to the local Ogre’s Grotto dungeon in an effort to choke the native Huohi oni tribe out of mining rights to their mountains, making it a matter Remmi must see resolved before she could continue her own mission.

Ogre’s Grotto, however, proved a still greater obstacle as the team’s first Silver-rank dungeon. Even with the additional aid of the young oni mage Korrigan and the Swordmaster Hero Leuke Flamvel, the dungeon nearly overwhelmed them. Though they fought their way through to the dungeon’s boss, however, they were headed off by a new manifestation of the Darkness, the Phantom of Corruption, who turned out to be entirely too powerful, only driven off through Remmi’s quick thinking.

This new enemy raises the threat level of the danger posed to the realm, but it will hardly be the sole matter demanding the Gunslinger Hero’s attention, as new mysteries begin to stir in quiet Dabun.
