Rayne froze the moment he heard that he was going to become a squad leader.

At the back of his mind, he had already known there was a good chance it would happen. Axel had retired. Several squad leaders and veterans had died during the campaign. There had already been talks about new soldiers joining the warband and some degree of restructuring taking place.

That sort of thing wasn't unusual.

Warbands that survived major campaigns often changed afterward. New soldiers arrived. Veterans retired. Officers moved up in rank.

Even knowing all that, however, hearing it directly from Captain Edran felt different.

The reality of it truly settled in only after the captain handed him the official letter of promotion.

For several seconds, Rayne simply stared at it.

Then, somewhere at the back of his mind, a familiar notification chime echoed.

His eyes widened slightly.

Immediately, he suspected it had something to do with the quest related to becoming a monarch. Becoming a squad leader was the first task he had needed to complete for it after all, and a part of him desperately wanted to pull up the notification right away and see what rewards the gods had decided to give him.

Unfortunately, Captain Edran wasn't finished.

As it turned out, the promotion itself was only the beginning.

Since the entire warband would be moving toward the Godless Ruins, Captain Edran wanted every squad performing at the highest level possible before they arrived there. Hence, he immediately handed Rayne several documents containing information about the soldiers that would be assigned to him.

Some names were already familiar. Jason would be shifting under him. So would Kesh, Nate, Heins, and Dean.

The rest were less familiar.

Several were new recruits fresh out of training while others were forsaken being transferred after getting their sentences. Rayne quickly realized that nearly half of the squad consisted of people he had never even heard the name of before.

Captain Edran's expectations were also quite clear.

He wanted these soldiers trained, disciplined, and battle ready before they entered the ruins.

Rayne pointed out that accomplishing that might not be easy if some of them were completely inexperienced.

The captain simply shrugged.

According to him, not everyone coming into the squad was a fresh recruit. Some already had battle experience. What they lacked wasn't skill.

It was discipline.

Still, Rayne knew he wouldn't fully understand what he was working with until he had a chance to read through every file and meet the soldiers personally.

The discussion continued for quite some time after that.

Captain Edran spoke about several other appointments taking place throughout the warband. One of the newly promoted squad leaders turned out to be Hobbs, which didn't surprise Rayne in the slightest.

The man had spent years in the army and had earned the position.

Apparently, a larger meeting involving all the squad leaders would take place within the next week. Until then, Rayne was expected to familiarize himself with his new responsibilities and review the information regarding his squad.

The new soldiers would also begin arriving in Valers soon, giving him an opportunity to meet them before the march toward the ruins.

Eventually, the meeting finally came to an end.

Yet just as Rayne and Bran were preparing to leave, Captain Edran stopped him one final time.

The captain remained silent for several seconds before speaking.

“Be careful around Prince Caelan when you see him tomorrow.”

Rayne waited for him to continue.

Captain Edran glanced at his desk, then back at him before shaking his head.

“I don't think he's dangerous. At least not yet,” he said. “In fact, he's probably the least dangerous prince in the kingdom when it comes to support and politics. But men like him are often harder to understand than those who openly seek power, and in the end, he's still a prince.”

That only left Rayne with even more questions than before.

During their meeting, Captain Edran had also handed him a small slip of paper with an address written on it. Apparently, that was where Prince Caelan wanted to meet him tomorrow, though Rayne still had no idea why the prince was interested in seeing him personally.

The entire situation felt strange.

Even the fact that Caelan would be travelling to the Godless Ruins felt strange. Everything Rayne had read about the ruins suggested they were one of the most dangerous places in the kingdom.

Sending soldiers there was understandable. Sending him there was believable. Sending a prince there felt like an unnecessary risk, especially one who wasn't known for military achievements.

Then again, Rayne was hardly an expert when it came to royal affairs. If Prince Caelan was willing to travel there and their warband was being attached to his force, then there was probably far more happening behind the scenes than he understood.

The prince had shown interest in him before, and if their warband was going to accompany him into the ruins, then perhaps that interest was mutual in some way.

Whatever the reason, there was little point wasting time thinking about it now. He would have his answers tomorrow. For today, he already had enough things occupying his thoughts.

Once they stepped out of Captain Edran's chamber, Bran glanced at him and asked, “Do you want to tell the others about your promotion now, or are you going to wait until dinner?”

“Dinner,” Rayne answered immediately. “I want to go through the files first.”

Bran nodded at that. “Fair enough. I need to cancel our participation in the tournament anyway and spend some time looking through my class options.”

That immediately caught Rayne's attention. The discussion with Captain Edran had distracted him enough that he had almost forgotten about it.

“What options did you get?”

A faint smile appeared on Bran's face.

“Only two.” Rayne raised an eyebrow. “Blood Ranger and Druidic Hunter.”

Both names sounded far better to be common classes. They sounded Rare-grade even.

“I'll probably consult Casper before making a decision,” Bran continued as they walked through the hallway. “Both look useful and I don't see a reason to rush the choice.”

Rayne nodded, curiosity immediately rising. He wanted to know what skills each class offered and what made them different, but before he could ask anything else, the familiar notification chime echoed once more at the back of his mind.

“See you later,” he said immediately. “Good luck with your class selection.”

Bran waved him off before heading down the corridor, while Rayne turned toward his room.

With every step he took, his curiosity grew stronger.

Yet Rayne didn't immediately pull up the notification.

The temptation was certainly there. Ever since Captain Edran had handed him the letter of promotion, the notification had been sitting at the back of his mind, demanding attention every few minutes.

Still, he forced himself to wait.

Only after returning to his room and closing the door behind him did he finally sit down on the edge of his bed and pull up the notification.

Immediately, a long wall of text appeared before his eyes.

***

You have completed the first prerequisite to become a monarch by becoming a squad leader.

Congratulations!

Not all soldiers could get anywhere up in the ranks. Most die after taking an arrow to their knee or worse, to their chest. Only some rise above such fate, show leadership qualities and are chosen to lead the others and you are one of them!

***

The first part congratulated him on taking the first step toward becoming a monarch and kept going. Rayne skimmed through most of it rather quickly. After dealing with the system for months, he had learned to focus on the important parts.

Namely, the rewards.

His eyes widened slightly as he finally reached them.

***

You have been granted rewards for taking the first step to complete your Lineage Quest - Monarch (Legendary).

You have been granted two skills.

You have gained the skill Commander’s Aura (Rare). The skill has been registered as a new skill.

You have gained the skill Mark of Henrexa (Unique). The skill has been registered as a new skill.

***

Seeing the two skill names, Rayne felt excitement fill his heart and he immediately opened up their description.

***

Commanders Aura (Rare) – Level 1.

A lot of people command but not everyone has the aura to truly demand respect and attention.

The skill allows the user to manually amplify an aura of dominance and oppressiveness on a group to make them hear your every word and pay attention to each of your actions.

Gives a dominating presence in front of a group of men with the range of the aura increasing with every level up.

***

Mark of Henrexa (Unique) - No level.

Everyone worships the goddess, but not everyone can get a mark of her own.

The skill gives the user a chance to bring himself or an ally back from near death by healing and regenerating every single injury or affliction.

Gives absolute healing when used, but could only be used once and after that, the skill will disappear from the status.

***

Just reading each line gave Rayne a lot of questions in his head but before he could focus on them, another notification buzzed at the back of his mind and he brought it up.

***

You have completed your first step. As a result, the quest has been updated.

Lineage Quest - Monarch (Legendary)

Men aren't born special. A select few make themselves special and lay the foundation for their descendants to be the same. More than mere mortals. More than their own person.

They set themselves to be Monarch— The ruler of all.

Prerequisites for completing it (2/10)

Become a Knight.

You have risen from the bottom of the army and have made something of yourself, but this is just the start. You are nowhere near having your own empire and just stepping into the dark ocean of politics. Rise through it and gain a title that no one will be able to shrug off.

Become a knight to gain both a standing in the army and the society.

Rewards - ???

***

Rayne froze reading through it. For several seconds, he simply stared at the updated description.

Become a Knight.

Compared to becoming a squad leader, that objective sounded far more difficult. There were countless soldiers in the kingdom. There were far fewer knights.

Still, his attention eventually shifted back toward the rewards. Honestly, he hadn't expected any skills at all.

When the quest first appeared, he had assumed the rewards would probably be stats, titles, or perhaps some form of reputation bonus. Receiving two completely unique skills had never crossed his mind.

And both looked impressive.

The first one practically sounded tailor-made for leadership. The second sounded even more absurd.

A complete heal. Rayne didn't know if there was actually a healer in the kingdom that could do that, and now he could, even if it was only once.

What truly caught his attention, however, was the name of the skill itself. It was basically a divine mark bestowed by Goddess Henrexa herself.

The wording lingered in his mind.

Rather than a normal skill, it seemed far more important, and as that thought crossed his mind, a sudden burning sensation appeared on his left shoulder.

Rayne immediately frowned.

His hand moved toward the spot instinctively, but the sensation remained. It wasn't painful. If anything, it felt warm.

Slowly, he pulled back his sleeve.

The moment he saw the skin underneath, his eyes widened.

A mark had appeared there.

It looked almost like a tattoo burned into his skin. The image depicted a white wolf unlike any animal he had ever seen before, and the design was detailed enough that he could almost imagine it moving.

For several seconds, Rayne simply stared at it, and it didn't take more than a guess to know that this was the Mark of Henrexa.

And when he focused on it more carefully, he could actually feel mana moving through the symbol.

The sensation was faint, but unmistakable.

Rayne slowly let out a breath before scratching the back of his head. The more he thought about the mark, the more questions appeared.

Was Henrexa involved with the lineage quest somehow? Had she always been involved? Or was he just thinking too much about it?

Right now, he honestly had no idea.

Still, after everything he had gone through to become a squad leader, after the campaign, the battles, the trial, and finally Atticus, he couldn't really complain.

These rewards seemed worth all that trouble.

A part of him already wanted to test out [Commander's Aura], but then something else clicked in his mind.

The files.

Captain Edran hadn't handed them over for decoration.

Rayne glanced toward the stack resting on the table. He had placed them there almost immediately after entering the room and completely forgotten about them once the quest rewards appeared.

Now, however, he realized that if these people were going to be under his command, then he needed to know exactly who he was dealing with. Either way, he couldn't use any of his skills right now.

Sighing softly, he pushed the notifications aside and reached for the first file.

At first, he expected little more than names and past records. Instead, every file seemed to bring a new problem.

As one file turned into another, the excitement from completing his quest slowly began to disappear.

By the time he reached the end of the pile, he simply leaned back in his chair and stared at the ceiling for several seconds.

Then he let out a long sigh.

“I'm never going to be able to rest easy in this life.”
