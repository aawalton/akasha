"I-Is that thing corrupted?" Cee asked nervously, eyeing the monster that had emerged from within the mountain.

The creature let out a loud angry bellow, and all of a sudden spikes erupted from the ground below them. Liora just barely managed to fly up in time before she and Caspian got turned into pincushions. The poor merman had painfully clutched onto her stomach feathers.

But Smokey wasn't as fast as Liora. The spikes impaled one of his wings and his hind leg, making the wyvern scream out in pain.

"Smokey!" Cee exclaimed, then jumped straight at the Crystal Elemental. "You leave him alone!"

Cee tried to—well, Liora wasn't sure what she was trying to do—but before the spider could do anything, Liora felt the world shift once again. Instead of being pulled towards the mountain, she was being pulled up in the sky.

The poor spider flew—or fell?—towards the sky, her legs flailing, trying to find something to grip. After a moment she managed to compose herself, and shot a thread of web out of her butt straight at the mountain. It connected, and Cee quickly pulled herself to safety.

Liora meanwhile flailed in the air for a moment, while she tried to right herself. Oddly enough, the crystal elemental seemed completely unaffected by all this gravity shenanigans, standing firmly on the ground.

"Renir, what do we do?! It's using some kind of gravity magic. What's its weakness?" Liora asked, as she tried to steady herself.

"Uh… Its only weakness is its core. It has to be shattered in one hit, or it'll recover," Renir said, clutching onto the saddle.

Liora looked at the monster. She could see its core thanks to her Prismatic Twilight Vision. It was embedded deep in its chest, glowing with a faint red light. It appeared that the corruption that had turned the monster's head black was slowly spreading towards its core.

Maybe if Liora purified the head, the monster would be grateful and leave them be. Although she would have to get to it first.

Using Holy Light or Winds of Purification would be impossible in the Titanspike Mountain. The only way to do it was to imbue one of her physical skills with holy magic.

But first she had to get rid of her passengers.

"Heal Smokey!" Liora said to the merman, depositing him by the wyvern's feet. Poor thing was still impaled, although this had given him time to hook his claws into the mountainside so he wouldn't get pulled up into the air due to switched gravity.

"Wait, wait! Don't leave me!" Caspian panicked, grabbing onto the injured wyvern who yelped out in pain.

Thankfully, his healer's instinct must have kicked in, because a gentle green glow came from his palms, which he had pressed against Smokey. Liora had to wonder whether this was something only she could see thanks to her enhanced vision.

"Can you anchor yourself somehow?" Liora asked Renir.

"Don't worry. I can do that much. Purify the monster before its core goes corrupt," Renir said.

Liora flew all the way up to the ground—geez, this was so disorienting. Renir grabbed onto the ground and pulled himself up, hanging there like a bat. Liora watched him for a moment, to make sure he wouldn't fall. Once it was clear that he wouldn't, she turned her attention back to the corrupted rock elemental.

It stomped towards Smokey menacingly, and suddenly the gravity shifted again, pulling Liora towards the cliffside. She turned into her magpie form, which instantly lessened the pull on her body and gave her time to steady herself in the air.

Then she activated Decoy Fade, and had her decoy fly straight at the elemental. She wanted to sneak up from behind, but the elemental created its jagged spikes, which destroyed the decoy in a few seconds.

Gravity shifted again, this time pulling Liora up into the sky. All everyone else could do was hang on for their lives, unable to adjust to the rapid gravity changes.

Liora was the only one who could do something, but she had to get close to the elemental without getting impaled. She activated Featherflash, turning into a mote of light.

The gravity shifted again, this time to the correct direction, but while Liora was a mote, it didn't matter. Gravity didn't affect her. She could get close to the monster.

Once she materialized, Liora didn't falter. She used Purifying Raptor Lunge on the elemental's head. The feeling was very unpleasant. Her talons slid off its crystalline surface, unable to pierce it.

But the magic worked. Light spread through the blight, so Liora continued attacking the elemental's head. The gravity shifted multiple times, and the monster was desperately trying to hit Liora. She switched between her magpie and dracoryx form to avoid getting hit.

Whenever the monster was about to grab her dracoryx form, she would switch to her magpie form, which allowed her to escape and then continue assaulting the guardian with Purifying Raptor Lunge.

After what felt like an eternity, the gravity shifted back to normal, and the crystal elemental looked blue all through. The corruption had been cleansed.

Liora didn't relax, hovering in the air and eyeing its core. How could she get to it if it continued its assault? Without powerful magic or really massive blunt force, Liora was helpless. She wasn't well equipped to fight heavily armored enemies like this.

They had discussed a strategy against such opponents as a group, but it required Cee and Smokey to be in fighting condition, not stuck and unable to even move because of constant gravity shift.

The lumbering monster looked around in confusion. It regarded Liora and everyone in their group curiously. Liora worried it might attack after all. But instead the monster yawned and lay down. As if it were part of the mountain itself, the elemental seemed to meld into rock, becoming part of the scenery, almost indistinguishable if it weren't for the glowing core in its center.

They didn't linger. Liora hurriedly picked up Renir and Caspian, who had managed to remove the spikes and heal Smokey. Cee hurried to her pet, giving him a quick checkup to make sure there weren't any more injuries.

Once they were sure everyone was fine, they hurried up the mountain, settling into a shallow cave which didn't seem to exude as much magic. Although compared to their previous resting spot, this was still glowing with magic. The distinct heartbeat of the mountain was so clear, Liora could almost feel it with her feet.

"Holy shit! That was scary…" Cee said, scuttling close to Smokey and patting the poor wyvern. He was trembling. Caspian had also pressed himself close to the wyvern.

"Tell me about it! You at least can jump and secure yourself with thread. I was holding onto Smokey for dear life!" The merman shivered.

"Renir… Don't tell me these kinds of monsters are common up higher?" Liora asked.

"Yes," he said, his voice rumbling. "Since external magic doesn't work, monsters here have developed other ways to fight. Blood magic, sound magic, and gravity magic are common here, although anywhere outside these mountains they would be considered rare and difficult to master."

"Yes, no shit. That elemental was just flipping a switch on gravity. What the hell? How are we supposed to fight that?" Cee complained.

"Liora was the only one who could do anything. And the best she could do was purify that thing. I suspect it was merely too tired to fight us afterwards. If it continued battling, we'd be screwed. We wouldn't even be able to escape," Caspian said.

The discussion continued, but Liora thought about the situation. Wouldn't it be better for her to go alone? She could move completely silently and without being seen. Sure, there were other ways of detecting her, but she could move unimpeded while she was alone.

While having to carry Caspian and Renir, she always had to be mindful of their location. She couldn't move freely. Not to mention that this wasn't the sort of problem that could wait. Each moment they didn't move up the mountain was a step closer to war between humans and antkin.

"Liora, no!" Renir said, and she felt his heavy hand on her wing.

"What? I didn't say anything!" Liora protested.

"You were thinking of going alone, weren't you?" He asked.

"I w-wasn't!" Liora shook her head.

"You totally were!" Cee said, and jumped next to Liora, putting her leg on Liora's other wing. "We are not splitting apart. We are working as a team. We'll come up with a way to deal with that gravity magic. Right, Renir?"

"We need a way to counter any enemy in the mountains. These elementals rely heavily on their gravity magic. If we got Smokey close enough, he could attack with his flames. If Cee could get close and rapidly cool him with ice webs and water, then structural cracks might appear, giving us access to the core," Renir said.

They continued various strategies, while Liora listened. She felt relief flood her. While she had been ready to do things alone, she didn't want to separate from everyone again.

Yes, it was a bit of pain to constantly carry Renir and Caspian. But if it meant not being alone, it was worth it.

"I think it's clear what we need to do before we head to the peak," Liora said, interrupting the ongoing strategy discussion.

"What is it?" Cee asked, although it felt like the spider already knew what Liora was talking about.

"We evolve. All of us who can. Cee, what level is Smokey?" Liora asked.

"He's Level 12, and he needs to be Level 30 to evolve. I'm Level 32. It might take a while," Cee said.

"I'm Level 39, so I'm not so far off. That said, we can't spend a week just grinding for levels. Renir, what should we do?" Liora asked.

"The best way to gain a great amount of experience quickly is to take down high-level targets. Just like you had with the desert shark matriarch. While everything in these mountains is more powerful than you, I can imagine a few targets that could make leveling easy," Renir said, then added. "If we come up with a strong plan and stick to it."

"What are we waiting for? Tell us about these targets and how to find them!" Cee said.

"T-They are dragons…" Renir said.

Everyone stared at him. After a moment, Caspian began to laugh.

"Dragons? Us fighting dragons? Yeah, no. Let's just head down the mountain. It's really quite a shame about the whole human-antkin war, but you know what… I'd like to live," Caspian said.

Liora, on the other hand, felt her heart beat with excitement. "Are these the dragons that have thousand glimmerstones within them?"

"No, it's not quite that bad. But yes, these dragons likely possess more glimmerstones than any other monster we've fought," Renir said, then added. "They likely have a hoard of treasures hidden in their nest."

Liora stood up. "What are we waiting for? We must train our levels!"

"Calm down, Liora! These are dragons we are talking about. They must be incredibly powerful. Right?" Cee asked, looking at Renir.

"Yes, but dragons have one glaring weakness. And it is their vanity. A dragon would never think that it could be defeated by a group such as ours. And that is our main advantage," Renir said.

"Can I sit this one out?" Caspian asked, turning green, the tone of his skin nearly matching his hair color.

"Do not worry! I have faced these dragons before and defeated them on my own while I wasn't even the Pillar of Lost Magic yet. I believe that by working together, we'll be able to defeat several of these dragons and reap the benefits we require," Renir said.
