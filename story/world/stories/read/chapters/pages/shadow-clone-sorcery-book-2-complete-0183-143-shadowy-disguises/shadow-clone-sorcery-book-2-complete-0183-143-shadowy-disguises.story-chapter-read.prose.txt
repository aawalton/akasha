Unfortunately for the Elder Wyrmkin mage, Stormdragon’s Heart granted Lukas and the Stalker incredible resistance to lightning magic. The Dracosteel Scales, granting their disguise texture, further increased the attack’s potency.

Lukas had expected his clone to whip together a grand illusion and weave it with sound magic to make it extra convincing and distracting. Instead, it seemed they had driven local fauna into the camp. The ongoing rumble and bestial calls suggested it was an entire horde.

He identified several runes and functions, but the Elder Wyrmkin’s spell formations appeared significantly different from those used by humankind. If anything, it reminded him of the spells Lady Silverspine used to cast personally. The Great Silver Dragon kept them private and fuelled them using her own breath.
