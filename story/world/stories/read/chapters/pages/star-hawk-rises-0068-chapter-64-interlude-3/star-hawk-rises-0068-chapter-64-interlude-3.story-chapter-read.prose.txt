Mr. Washington was sitting on the edge of his bed, the evil clock on his bedside table kept telling him it was 3 am each time he glanced at it. He turned his head and looked out the window at the city. He certainly seemed to be having trouble sleeping. Earlier that day he had received reports that Russia was chattering about something big, there was also a decrease in Russian tourists entering the country.

“Something big is about to happen” he mumbled to himself as he slid from his bed and headed out of his bedroom. He was having trouble piecing together what the Russians were planning. He was sure they wouldn't invade America, Mackenzie was from Scotland, everyone knew that.

He groaned tiredly as he stumbled into his office and dropped into his chair, turning to his computer, he quickly booted it up and logged in. rubbing his eyes he opened his favourite game that he played when he needed to relax. It was roughly an hour later as he was using his aim bot to shoot at other players in his game that it clicked in his head. He immediately abandoned his game, not caring about it anymore as he rolled his chair across the table to his laptop. He opened it and quickly started pulling up reports and files, double-checking the possibility of his theory.

“They are closing their borders… is it… They wouldn't… wait a minute, China is doing it too…”

He fled from his office as he ran for his work clothing. He had to get to his office at work so he could start giving orders and prepare for what was coming.

It was almost sunrise by the time he made it into his office and was booting up his computer. He proceeded to spend the rest of the day giving orders and filing reports to get the borders to China and Russia closed. Anyone coming in from there should be scrutinised to make sure they were not bringing anything illegal or harmful to the American citizens into the country.

“You fuckers won't get away with it, I won't let you release any chemicals or bombs into my territory!” he hissed at his computer.

He switched to preparing orders for the navy to move their ships into defensive positions. He knew he was going to have to brief the president before he actually issued the orders, but he figured if he had them ready, he could just slap them all out the second the green light was lit. He glanced at the clock and pushed a button on his office phone and asked his secretary to get him in with the President within the hour. It was then that a global announcement arrived, informing him that Kenna Mackenzie had risen to the rank of Solar Baron.

“You red-haired bitch, how dare you steal what is rightfully mine!” he screamed.

He squished a stress ball as he growled for a few moments longer before glancing at his computer and the orders he had finished filling out, and a grin slowly spread across his face.

“Well, while I'm suspecting countries of acts of aggression and war towards the greatest country on earth, perhaps I should also consider people of committing acts of terrorism!”
