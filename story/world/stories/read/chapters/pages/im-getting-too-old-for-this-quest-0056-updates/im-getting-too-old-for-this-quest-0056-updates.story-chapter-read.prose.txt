Well, it's been a minute.

First off, I owe everyone an apology. When I announced that I was taking a couple of months off to deal with some health issues, I certainly didn't anticipate that a couple of months would turn into a couple of years. Life has a funny way of making an idiot out of you.

The good news is that I'm still here, still writing, and doing well now. I haven't forgotten about I'm Getting Too Old For This Quest, either. I actually have several more chapters written, and I've been slowly working my way back through them. I don't want to make promises about a return to regular updates until I'm confident I can deliver, but I haven't closed the book on it.

In the meantime, I've been working on something new, and I wanted to share it with all of you first.

It's called Of These Infernals (link below) and it's a progression fantasy about a boy growing up in a world where magical abilities determine much of a person's place in life. There's a good bit of mystery, a large cast of characters, and a magic system I've had a lot of fun putting together.

I've just launched it here on Royal Road, with ten chapters already available and plans to release a new chapter every day for at least the next month.

If you enjoyed my writing in Quest, I'd love for you to give this one a shot. It's a different sort of story, but I'm having a blast writing it, and I hope some of you will come along for the ride.

More than anything, thank you. I still see how many of you have stuck around despite the absence, and it means a great deal to me. I hope you've all been doing well.
