Ais moved through the destroyed landscape extra carefully, making sure Alan doesn't get hurt from the sharp rocks. As soon as she arrived outside of the rocky terrain, her eyes went on a set of familiar faces. Weeble siblings and Bella were sprinting in her direction.

Well, they sure arrived at the right time. Bella could possibly help him with her spell's, Ais thought to herself as she glanced over at Alan.

"What happened to him, Ais!?" Bella questioned, her blue eyes widened with concern as she stopped right in front of both of them.

Is he dead? Rose mentally questioned, her eyes darting at Alan's chest. She looked as it raised up and went down again, meaning he is still alright.

Rose and Adrian didn't say anything; they both stood quietly next to Bella as they looked at Ais.

"He got a mana burnout," Ais mumbled. She looked at Bella, "Any chance you've got some healing magic you could use to cure it?"

"I will see what I can do…" Bella replied with a nod as she walked towards Ais and Alan.

Ais swiftly placed Alan's body down gently on the floor, then stepped back, entrusting him to the care of Bella.

"Miss Ais," Adrian spoke up, catching her attention.

"Yes?"

"What exactly were you two fighting? Like what kind of monster was it?"

While Ais thought for a moment on how to describe the goblin king they fought, Bella used her staff to envelop Alan in a layer of ice and light magic. It was a condensed version of the area heal spell that she mostly used. Bella then turned her gaze towards Ais, curious about her answer herself.

"It was a unique monster, it looked like a goblin, it possessed ability to reinforce his strength with mana too, though it wasn't anything normal."

Adrian's eyebrows raised with doubt, “That scream came from a goblin?”

“It sure did, though it wasn't just a goblin. It was as if we were fighting a corpse, an immortal, emotionless puppet that refused to die. That goblin king sure possessed some incredible healing abilities and he was also able to use earth element too.”

"Earth element, huh? It might have been because of an artifact. Is its body still intact?" Bella asked.

“Other than one of his hand and the huge hole, it's pretty alright, I would say…” Ais replied with a neutral look.

“If you were able to kill him by bursting a hole in his chest, how exactly could he be called an immortal soldier?” Adrian questioned.

“Ahh, I wasn't the one who delivered the final blow. It was Alan, and about why I called it an immortal soldier, well, let's just say it managed to survive being cut in half. It also healed after being blasted by my Orb of Destruction spell at point-blank range.”

Bella's eyes widened at Ais' revelation. "It was able to survive being cut in half? If you are joking, it's the time you come clean, Ais."

“Why'd I lie about this?” Ais replied with a serious look.

“You aren't?”

“I am not lying, it really did survive being cut in half. You know my sword's passive ability, don't you? Bella.”

“Miss Ais,” Rose spoke up. “Was it Alan's sword that bypassed that monster's healing?”

“Yeah, Ferrust was able to bypass his healing. If I didn't have him fighting with me… the fight sure would've become a hell lot harder for me.” Ais admitted with a unsure smile.

"What would have happened if it didn't work on him, though?" Adrian questioned, with a doubtful look.

Ais glanced in Adrian's direction, looking at his curious green eyes before continuing, "We would have had to search for another way then. If we can't kill it, we could seal it. If we can't seal it, we would have to exhaust it. If it has an inexhaustible source of mana, well then we could capture it and harness energy from it." Ais stopped for a second and continued in a calm and confident tone, "As an adventurer, I always believe in the saying, Everything has a weakness. We as adventurers just have to find and exploit it.”

"That was rather insightful, Miss Ais," Adrian mumbled, his eyes brimming with admiration. "Though the saying sounds a bit tacky, it was good too!"

"Thanks for the compliment, though I don't believe I am that knowledgeable," Ais said with a smile. Though, in her mind, she seemed to be questioning reality. Tacky? Wait, really? I thought it was cool… A slightly disappointed expression spread over Ais' face as she looked at Alan's sleeping face. Good thing I didn't use these words in front of that idiot. He would have got something to tease me about! she mentally added.

Bella removed her staff from Alan's chest, the blue healing area disappeared. "I think he should be fine now," Bella mumbled. She let out a tired sigh.

"That's good," Ais mumbled under her breath. She looked towards Rose and Adrian's direction. "Why don't you two take a break? You must be tired," Ais said. She pointed at Alan's unconscious body. "Just take this body… no, no, I mean Alan and throw… I mean dump him in the White Tiger Guild building."

"Wouldn't we need to help in collecting mana stone and goblin corpses?" Adrian questioned, but before he could continue speaking any further, a hand grasped his mouth.

"We'd do just that, Miss Ais," Rose replied to Ais with a completely serious look. I really went overboard with the fight today, Rose thought. Her eyes glanced at Alan. He certainly has a hand in it, he was the one who provoked me afterall, she thought to herself as she continued to muffle out any attempt of speech from Adrian.

Ais blinked quickly at the scene in front, a small smile spread on her lips as she saw Adrian struggling to get some words out?? Or maybe breath. She wasn't sure about the reason, but it was nonetheless amusing to her.

"Ooobbh of dee…." Adrian muffled.

Bella clapped her hands, catching everyone's attention. "Rose, you better remove your hand from your brother, and Adrian, don't you dare use Orb of Destruction on your sister for such a petty reason!" Bella yelled.

Rose quickly unhanded Adrian on Bella's command. "Ahh, I didn't realise I was holding you…" Rose replied, as she turned her gaze away from Adrian, slowly whistling.

Adrian cleared his throat. He looked at Rose, his eyes narrowed with frustration. "I should've just casted a simpler spell," he remarked.

Rose looked at Adrian. She brought her right hand in front of her mouth. "Wait, you were about to hurt me? Your sweet sister?" she said in a teasing tone, her green eyes widened with fake surprise and a small smile hidden beneath her hand.

"Sisters aren't supposed to hurt their brothers too, just so you know," Adrian replied with a skeptical look. He turned his gaze towards Ais and Bella, he looked at their expressions. Bella looked like she was disappointed in them somehow, while Ais, well, she was barely holding her laughter. Ahh, I should really try to get some restraint. I have become a joke due to my idiotic sis!… Adrian thought, mentally berating Rose.
