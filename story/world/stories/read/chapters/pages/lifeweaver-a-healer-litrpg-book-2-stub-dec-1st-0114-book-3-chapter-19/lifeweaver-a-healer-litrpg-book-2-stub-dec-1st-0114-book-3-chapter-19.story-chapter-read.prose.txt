As the demon arced downward toward Clementine, it twisted in the air and guided two of its now four legs so its heels would smash right into the nun's fragile body. Rather than fear, however, the expressions Luke saw on her face as he rushed in to help were serenity and anger. A strange combination to be sure, but she managed it just fine as she grabbed a wooden cross in one hand and lifted the other so the palm faced the oncoming monster.

"NO CREATURE FROM HELL SHALL TOUCH THE SERVANTS OF THE LORD!"

Simean finished its attack, landing on top of Clementine. Rather than crush her, a white barrier sprang up in the air. It didn't budge, but thin cracks appeared at the center of the strike, snaking outward when the monster slammed into it with a roar. The stone ground beneath Clementine's feet broke apart, kicking up dust, but when Luke got closer, he saw her standing on a circular patch of stone that looked just as undamaged as she did.

Now that the demon was no longer covered in its own burning blood, it was safe to touch, but the idea of doing so filled Luke with disgust. The monster looked like someone had flayed it, and the exposed muscle and sinews looked wet and slick, as if covered by a layer of oil. Disgusting, to say the least.

Wielding Threads of Mana, Luke did not have to touch it. Small blessings. Quick on his feet this time, he was the first to make it to Clementine's side, and he reached inside the monster's mana channels as it collapsed to the ground. Falling short of breaking the nun's barrier had stunned it for a moment, giving him a chance to do some serious damage. Luke got the feeling this beast was a clever one, and that meant it would be able to rebuff the threads if he dragged his feet. So, rather than head for its monster core, he forced three threads up its left foreleg, slicing through flesh and arteries full of burning blood with two and focusing on the metaphysical side, breaking down mana channels with the third.

Just as he'd suspected, Simean didn't give him much time to operate. It took the monster no more than a second to react and let out a shout, loud enough to hurt Luke's ears. "Raaaa! The folly of humans knows no bounds!"

Just like that, Luke's threads disintegrated, but the damage was already done. Simean attempted to kick at Luke as the monster rose, but found the leg too damaged. It gave it a difficult time with balance, and it overextended, slowing the retaliation just enough for Luke to duck away and create some distance between them.

Nick took advantage of the window and dashed forward with enough speed that he left a blur as he streaked forward to thrust his extended blade into Simean's side to inflict a deep wound. Unfortunately for the Knight, burning blood pumped out of the hole in the demon's side, getting all over his arm, searing through the flesh. Forced to pull back or lose the limb, Nick screamed in pain, his wide eyes dimming at the sight of the searing and smoking remains of his sword arm.

Curtis and Hannah were there in that moment, engaging the monster to pull attention from Nick and Luke, covering their opponent in shadow and darkness. Luke, grateful for the breathing room, ignored the white glow surrounding Nick and reached into his mana channels with threads of mana. This was not the time to wait for the slow effect of Clementine's healing. This was the time for Weaver's Renewal. Burns like these were not something he thought it prudent to heal one stitch at a time. Better to spend a little more mana and get Nick back into fighting shape right away.

Nick stopped screaming and stared at his now once-again functioning arm, then gave Luke a look, followed by a nod of appreciation. "My sword," he grunted, turning back to Simean, who'd just turned and slammed the back of his fist into Curtis, who answered by using his ball of darkness, disappearing inside its bounds.

"Your sword has probably melted by now," Luke said, turning his attention back to the demon, who side-stepped to get out of Curtis's attack, only to find two of its only three functioning legs stuck in a roiling mass of shadows.

Nick nodded and steeled himself, ready for another attack. A blade appeared in his hand, and he grinned at Luke. "Good thing I have more."

The Knight charged in, avoiding a swipe of Simean's hand by ducking. He followed up by thrusting his blade up and into the attacking arm. This time, he withdrew right away and moved back, avoiding the gushing blood.

"Quick study!" Luke shouted as he followed, closing in. It'd taken a lot of mana to restore Nick's arm, and Luke had no intention of fighting while low on that precious resource when a battery stood right next to them in the form of a monster. It was time to drain some and see just how much damage he could do, now that no one was in need of his healing.

Dirk appeared again, this time on the damn monster's back. He raised his hand over his head, but rather than slam the blade down right away, another Dirk appeared, then another. In the span of a few seconds, he'd copied himself many more times and only stopped when there was no more room. Only then did he launch his attack, pushing his weapon down and through what had to be the monster's spine.

Tough as Simean was, the demon felt that one. It roared in pain and anger, turning to swipe at the Mirror Rogue. All copies disappeared, leaving the monster's back free with no sign of Dirk anywhere.

Rather than turn and attack Luke after that, who was the likeliest target, the demon swatted in the air, as if trying to get rid of a buzzing mosquito. Unsure what to think of that, Luke was happy for the opportunity to get at some of that precious mana. It wasn't right for the demon to hog all of it, after all. Hannah's shadows now covered much of its legs, but Curtis's ball of darkness had timed out, and Luke's reach was enough now that he could extend a thread of mana right up into the monster's abdomen. He just hoped it wouldn't collapse on top of him.

Entering its mana channels proved a simple enough challenge, but when Luke attempted to pull mana from Simean, pain radiated outward from his Meta-Heart, like a burning sensation. Luke's health dropped at a staggering rate, so he pulled away, intending to release the monster. Instead, the threads wouldn't free themselves, and more demon mana pumped into Luke, who grunted and gritted his teeth, feeling like his chest was about to explode. The threads wouldn't listen. The damn monster had taken control.

"BUUUUUUUURN!" it hissed.

Luke didn't want to burn, so he sliced through his own threads with new ones, freeing himself. In that moment of being connected to the demon, he'd learned something useful. Well, two things. First: taking mana from a demon was a bad idea. Second: they didn't have monster cores. It wasn't the first time he'd come across an opponent who didn't. What was different about Simean was the fact that it didn't have a Meta-Heart either. No, this was something else. Something Luke hadn't seen before. It had just been a brief flash, but it was long enough for him to know he wanted whatever riches Simean's chest held, wanted it for study, and to see what the system made of it.

The demon swatted something invisible from the air again, and Luke was worried for a second that it was Ray doing something up there, and that his friend would be squished like a bug any moment, but no. Ray was up with those cultists, slicing into them while Ariel did... something.

"BEGONE, PESTS!" Simean shouted, bringing both hands up to the sides of its head, covering its ears. "SILENCE!"

"What is the bastard shouting about?" Hannah asked as she lashed out with another shadow, wicked mouths full of razor-sharp teeth opening along its length. The attack found its mark in Simean's side and stuck to it. Even from Luke's position, he could hear the many mouths gnawing and gnashing at the monster's flesh. It didn't take long for burning blood to rebuff the tentacle, but another took its place, undulating, unrelenting darkness.

Dot: "Luke, can you talk? I think we should talk."

The timing was impeccable, and it felt as if a hand had reached out to crush Luke's chest. Even in the middle of battle, with the demon flagging, Luke couldn't hold back from answering right away.

Luke: "I agree. In a dungeon right now. Meet up after?"

Dot: "Oh, I'm sorry for disturbing you. We'll talk later, but I'd prefer it if we could do it over messages. Not quite ready to meet you again. Not just yet."

Luke: "Um, yeah. OK. Text with you later, then."

Taking his attention off Simean had been a mistake. Luke had known that right from the start, of course, but didn't feel the full consequences of that mistake until after he sent that last message to Dot. Pain exploded in his chest, and he found himself pushed up and off his feet, the floor far below him as the demon followed through with a vicious upper-cut.

If Luke had been piloting some mecha-suit, all lights inside would have been flashing red. Blood leaked out of a crushing wound in his hip, and Luke's arm was all but crushed, along with several organs, and that wasn't even the worst part. Simean was pivoting on its legs, far stronger and quicker than it should have been, pulling back to deliver a second punch while Luke was still in the air.

A glowing-white barrier appeared in front of him, and he muttered, "Thanks, Clementine," even as his body stitched itself back together, pulling from the vast store of healing mana within his Meta-Heart. That barrier would not be enough, not by a long shot, and the demon was ignoring the many attacks from the others, focusing everything on following through on its attack on Luke.

Air rushed away from Simean's fist as the demon drove it forward in an attempt to end Luke with a swift follow-up punch. Pushing a thread of mana to the side, Luke weavestepped out of the way just in time to avoid the oncoming doom, then weavestepped again right after, this time to close in on Simean's outstretched arm.
