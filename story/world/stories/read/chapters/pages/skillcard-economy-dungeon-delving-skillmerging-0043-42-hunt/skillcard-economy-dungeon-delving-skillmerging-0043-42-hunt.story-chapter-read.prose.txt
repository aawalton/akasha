I went for the thug who was alone first.

He noticed I had vanished for the alley and turned around to alert his friends, but failed. I hopped out of his shadow, behind him and grabbed his mouth. He tried screaming, but it amounted to nothing more than muffles, which no one heard.

I shot him right at the head. Cerise never told me that the sleeping shots worked faster when shot at the head. He fell asleep five seconds later.

I looked down from the railing and took aim at the huge guy. No chances. I missed. I missed the biggest target one could possibly ask for. It hit the glass bottle, it tumbled down the table, crashing into the ground gathering everyone’s attention.

I ducked and hid behind the solid piece of metal which worked as a railing.

“It’s just BigBelly.”

“Fucker, sleeping at job. Oi, wake up, Oi.”

“Let him be, let’s focus on our job.”

“Shouldn’t there have been sounds of fights by now. Why is it so silent?”

“Ken, what is going on out there? …Oi, Ken! Did this fucker go to sleep too?”

“Something’s wrong.”

Why can’t anything go the way I want it to? I cried inside.

“Hey, go check.”

I sensed two people climbing, one clearly had a revolver and other held a knife. I crawled towards the stairs.

Letting out a deep breath and I counted. Five steps, Four, Three, Two, One. I shot up, headbutting the guy with revolver in the chin. He fell back into the other. I snatched the revolver out of his hand and kicked him. Both of them rolled down the stairs.

“Shoot him,”

I vanished, and a gunshot rang through the warehouse.

And that will alert everyone.

“Where did he go?”

“Her—khaa”

I punched the one who was looking for ambushes in the gut. He too went unconscious.

Either these guys are just weak or the buff from shadowstride is strong enough knock a person unconscious.

I rushed towards the second guy, he had his knife out. I looked at his face. He was scared. He was scared of me? No one has ever been scared of me. I did not know if I liked this feeling or not.

I dodged a sloppy slash and with a punch he too went unconscious.

Five down, six left. The doors smashed open as ten men who were looking for me outside came in after hearing the gunshot.

“How did he get inside?”

“How the fuck would I know?”

“Just shoot him.”

Vanishing once again I emerged between the group.

“Hey there” I greeted the one in front of me.

“What the fuck, A tele—”

I punched him in the face. I heard his nose cracking. He fell on his back.

“Sheesh bloody,” I commented and ducked below a sword swing. Emerging from his shadow behind him, I grabbed him by the waist and performed a suplex. I should not be able to pick a grown man like this. These buffs were too powerful.

I dipped into the shadow before the incoming spear could have pierced my leg.

“Be alert, it’s a teleportation skillcard.”

“Lets catch him and get that out of him.”

“At least silver, will fetch a high price.”

I shot all three of them and did not miss a single one. Should I switch from sword to guns? I am really good at this.

I dodged a flying kunai. We have ninjas here? The kunai changed its path in the air, instinctively I raised my hand. The kunai got stuck in my gun. No more sleeping shots I guess.

“Stay alert, he likes attacking from behind.”

Two more kunai came flying, but I knew they were just a distraction. I took a step back and a blade emerged out of the floor. The kunai changed their path again. I was ready this time. I grabbed them in the air and threw them down. They didn’t hit anyone.

The ninja sat on the railing. He had a katana, his face hidden behind a mask and kunai on his waist belt.

“My first time seeing a ninja,” I told him.

He answered by throwing his kunai’s and dashing behind them. I vanished and emerged from his shadow. He swung his sword in reverse. It grazed my shirt as I took a step back.

“It’s the shadows,” the ninja shouted. “He teleports in and out of the shadows.”

“Oh man, figured me out already” I complained with a smile. “Too bad it won’t help.”

The ninja rushed at me. He slashed downward, I dodged to the side. He gracefully changed the swords path and went for my legs, I jumped and landed a step back.

The ninja too took a step back. Another ninja climbed up, they stood side by side.

“Don’t just stand there and pose, come at me.”

They moved as if they were one. Their arms moved so much in sync that one might think they were part of a hive mind. They were fast, but I was faster. Their swords moved at a turtle’s pace, a simple flick of my hand sent them done a dozen steps back. As long as I was using shadowstride I was stronger than everyone here and with how many times I had emptied my magicka pool in Life’s Last hope, it had also grown considerably allowing me to maintain this pace for another hour.

First time in my life, I was not the weakest walker in the room.

I caught one of the ninja’s wrist, used his sword to deflect the other’s sword. Swung and threw him at the other one. They stopped just at the edge. They were breathing heavily.

I rushed towards them and kicked them. The railing broke and both of them fell down. I emerged out of the shadow the two falling men were casting on the ground. I kicked both of them on the side’s, and they went flying to the far wall.

They were not going to get up now.
