Noya moved inside the carriage, effortlessly picking up Alan in her hand. With him secure in her arms, she turned around and hopped down the carriage. Her eyes turned to Bella, who had already begun walking inside the mansion’s gate, and looking at her go. Noya increased her walking speed, catching up to Bella in a moment.

"Don’t run when you have somebody in your arms," Bella scolded Noya.

"Ah… sorry," Noya replied apologetically. She then began to walk slowly, matching pace with Bella. "If he has burnout, I just need to take care of his water requirement and let him rest until he wakes up, am I right?" She questioned.

Bella nodded. She raised her right hand and gently patted Noya’s head, brushing her soft brown hair. "You've certainly gotten better at dealing with burnouts, perhaps even more than me," Bella said with a gentle smile. She withdrew her hand as both of them approached the gate of the mansion. Bella reached for the huge wooden gate and swung it open.

The interior of the mansion was as extravagant as the exterior. Several portraits of Blaive, Marcielle, and Ais were placed on the wall directly in front of the gate. The wall with the door and the one with all the portraits and scenery paintings were painted in white, while the ceiling and the other two walls were painted with a light shade of white and golden.

Bella's attention shifted to the massive flower-like glass chandelier that illuminated the whole main hall with a gentle yellow light. Other than the main chandelier, small but numerous white bulbs were also spread out in the main hall.

"They certainly are quite bright," Bella muttered under her breath.

"You said something, Bella?" Noya questioned.

Bella turned around and walked a few steps backward. "I didn't say anything," she mumbled playfully, shaking her head.

Noya blinked a few times in a quick manner. "Are you forgetting my kind is famous for our ears?" she said.

"You little shit, where did you learn to lie like this?" Bella questioned, narrowing her eyes.

"From the books you let me borrow," Noya replied, completely honest. Her gaze then shifted to the right side of the mansion. "Since Master Alan's room isn't clean, should I take him in my room?" Noya questioned.

Bella looked at Noya's face, processing what the young girl had said for a moment, hoping she had heard her wrong. "Absolutely not!" Bella exclaimed, "You don’t take an unconscious man into your arms and then take him into your room!! That’s bad, like really bad," she continued. Yeah, the books really had some bad effect on this poor girl's mind, she thought to herself.

"You seem to be mistaken, Bella," Noya replied with a calm voice. "What I mean is I could take Master Alan in my room, and I could sleep in the guest room instead," she added.

Am I the impure one instead? Bella questioned herself internally, questioning reality. "The guest room can't be that bad. Show me the way!" Bella commanded, to which Noya nodded and began walking to the right side of the hall.

Noya walked to the first room at the side. "I believe this is the cleanest guest room in the mansion," Noya remarked. She then glanced at Bella’s face.

"Is it already unlocked?" Bella questioned as she reached for the doorknob, to which Noya responded, "Yes."

Bella turned the metallic doorknob to the left, unlocking the door. She walked in ahead of Noya and looked around. The spotless bed with white bedsheet and a grey blanket, a spotless glass chandelier at the center of the room, and the rather clean floor were the things Bella saw. She turned back to look at Noya. "This room was supposed to be dirty, wasn’t it?" she questioned, with a slight furrow on her brow as her eyes scanned the room.

"It is. I haven’t cleaned it for 5 days," Noya replied calmly.

"You haven’t cleaned it for 5 days… oh my, obviously this room is dirty. We should fetch ourselves a mask or something," Bella said in an exaggerated sarcastic manner.

"But according to Miss Alisa, I should clean all the rooms in our use every 3 days," Noya replied.

"Yeah, that’s true. Cleaning the house regularly is a good thing and all, but the person you are holding in your hand probably doesn’t clean anything other than his kitchen counter and bed, like you can forget mopping the floor," Bella replied.

"He doesn’t? How can he live like that? Doesn’t he ever feel like he’s drowning?" Noya asked, her voice filled with worry for Alan.

"Probably not," Bella murmured. Her gaze drifted to Noya’s arms, noticing how she was still clutching onto Alan. He’d most certainly die of embarrassment if he ever found out, Bella mused. "You know what," Bella spoke up with a smile, "you could just lay him in bed, pull a blanket over him, and call it a day."

"Ahh…," Noya mumbled, slightly flustered. She quickly walked towards the double bed placed at the centre of the room, and took the hold of the blanket, and swiftly pulled it over Alan, leaving only his head out.

Bella turned around and looked at a small candle holder placed on the wall just adjacent to the door, it held a tiny bluish crystal that reminded Bella of the past. "Ice dragon huh," Bella muttered, as a small smile tugged on her lips. Taking the hold of the crystal, Bella poured her mana into it. And with it the crystal began to glow gently.

This should be enough to keep him cool, Bella thought to herself as she placed the crystal back on the holder. Bella then turned her gaze towards Noya, who was standing next to the bed. "I should be on my way then," Bella said. She looked at Alan and continued. "Make sure he doesn't dehydrate and if anything happens, reach out to the healer's association immediately."

"I will do just that," Noya replied with a determined look. To which Bella lightly chuckled. "Hey! I was being serious."

"I know Noya," Bella replied with a smile, "and that’s what makes it sweet. Anyway, take care, Noya and… uh, eat healthy I guess."

"Just go now!" Noya yelled, clearly frustrated by Bella's teasing.
