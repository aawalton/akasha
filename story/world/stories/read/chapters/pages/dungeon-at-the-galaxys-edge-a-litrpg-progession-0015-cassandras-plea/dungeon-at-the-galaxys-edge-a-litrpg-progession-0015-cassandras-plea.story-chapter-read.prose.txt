To: Noah Reyes (Vice-Overseer)

Dear Noah Reyes,

It has been a long time. We haven’t spoken since. You know. I take my part in it. I am sorry, Noah. I loved your son- my husband. He told me to write you if things ever got dire. But I’m not writing for me.

My mom, when I was little told me something I still think about. This place is all I’ve ever known, I was born here after all, but you. You still remember Earth. I’m not trying to hash old wounds, but I just wanted to know what happened? My mom told me of when we first orbited this God-Forsaken place. When it was first found. That everything was about mutual cooperation. Helping each other. Everyone worked to stay alive. But then, people found everything they needed in the Dungeon. Stuff was no longer a problem. They just needed to go and get it.

And that’s how your son was sold on it. He wanted to help everyone. So he did his part. Got rich from it. Helped you get to your position. Then we stopped helping each other. It became even worse than how it was on Earth. So when Daniel moved with me to B-Block, you knew what it meant. That he would see just how this divide happened when we are all supposed to be working together.

Like I said, Noah. This letter isn’t for me. It’s for you. Your grandson is entering the Dungeon, alone. This disparity that happened was in no small part because you’ve had little to worry about. You don’t have water and energy budgets. But we do. Stefano is going into the dungeon because he wants to help, just like his Dad did. So please, lets bring that time of cooperation. That hope that we can find a new world and not stay here forever. We are the last gasps of our species and the disparity that has occurred, shouldn’t happen here. I pray for you. For Stefano. And for all of us. But you can do more than pray. You can help. So help everyone.

With Love, Cassandra Reyes
