Chapter 385

“It’s a simple case of delegation of responsibility,” Eik said as he exploded after the Lord of the Moon, catching up to the beast in a moment.

The moment he turned to confront the human, Eik slapped his palm and activated his skill Admittance to the Realm.

Immediately, the mountains, forests, and the view of Gimleh only an insignificant distance away warped and vanished. The only forms that stayed intact and consistent throughout the whole process were those of Eik Magnasen and the Lord of the Moon.

And then, in the next instant, their feet landed on the soft grass of a dense forest.

The Lord was momentarily frozen in place as his brain seemed to digest what had just happened. Forceful teleportation was not exactly an everyday occurrence. Not even for an X-ranker. Especially not for an X-ranker.

Eik didn’t hesitate and capitalized on that moment of confusion.

“I’ve been hoping that we could have some alone time. How nice,” Eik growled as a sudden eruption of crushing energy poured out of him, washing over the Lord and yanking him out of his stupor.

Just as quickly as it then sunk in that something had changed, the beast moved to react. But by then, it was already too late.

In a vicious downward slam, Eik struck the Lord squarely on the forehead with the same enormous war hammer he had designed for his test of the grade 7 Potion of Mighty Strength like a farmer hammering in fence posts around a field. Pulverized completely, the earth opened up for the fiend to be sent hundreds of meters downward.

The hammer melted into liquid and seeped into the ground in pursuit as Eik dove after the enemy. The physical alterations to his frame and the feather-like structures that manifested along with the activation of Omnicide were already more or less complete.

Dramatically boosting both the potency and control of his Profound Toxin as well as general physical strength and agility, Eik had quickly come to appreciate the ability as his most powerful personal buff, despite his initial suspicion. It well and truly was a more powerful version of True Toxic Harmony.

And he was well aware that, without it, he would not have been able to put up a proper fight once the battle grew more serious, even with the many, many grade 7 Legendary Mystery Medicines he had taken.

Until now, they had mostly been sizing each other up, but that phase was probably over now that Eik had escalated with Admittance to the Realm.

Recovering much more quickly than expected, the Lord of the Moon rocketed back up through the debris at volcanic speed and slammed into Eik like a bullet. Something cracked in his shoulder but it didn’t faze him enough for the Lord to be able to avoid his hand as a spike of crystal formed around it.

The crater that was already filling up with dirt and debris was reopened even deeper by the destructive power of his attack. Eik felt it as the Lord of the Moon gasped in shock and pain, a hole not unlike what he himself had inflicted upon the scion of Profound Toxin torn into his abdomen.

In the span of a few seconds, the two fighters exchanged hundreds of crackling blows that would have vaporized any S-ranker like a water balloon against a hand grenade. At the speed at which they were moving, attacks blurred together with blocks and deflections, and evasive maneuvers turned into attacks of opportunity, all blending seamlessly into a fluid, shared function.

To call it an art or even a dance would probably require an audience capable of watching and following it. No, this was nothing more than a savage death struggle between two monsters.

Eik didn’t have to look with his eyes when the all too familiar sensation of a glowing script trickled down the inside of his forearm.

Now that he was able to actually unfold a bit against the hostile X-ranker, the levels were coming in steadily, even in the skills that already had very high levels. And the Apocalypse Canvas that he had left behind in Gimleh before forcefully teleporting away with the Lord in tow was still harvesting levels from both healing of deadly wounds inflicted upon the Awakened defenders of the city and from massacring the spawn swarming their territory.

It struck Eik only then that the Lord could well have sent forces to other hubs of civilization under the Alliance. But with the leader himself stuck here and Chop the Blade wreaking havoc out there, he was confident that Mn’Toakh and the other generals had prepared for nearly any possible strategy from the enemy Worldbreaker. And Eik had already given her a short but thorough lesson in how to cruelly manipulate the old, overpowered hermit.

Threaten to cut off access to sweets and threaten to cut off access to the X-ranked potions he had been yearning for for hundreds of years. Now that he had actually had a taste of that heavenly power, it would be that much harder for him to go back. And at this point, Eik’s grip on the market was so overwhelmingly tight that it would be utterly impossible to acquire even a single vial of grade 7 potion through illicit means.

Yes, he would just have to trust that they were going to be all right on the other side. Only then could he deal with this fucker in peace.

But for now, Eik was managing to do damage, and with Profound Toxin, all he needed to reach the end was a start.

Finally.

Finally, Eik’s fingers closed around this vermin’s throat.

“Spaghetti Jones!” he called.

In an instant, reacting to his will before his words, the azure world serpent descended from the endless floating ocean of toxin above and through the churning clouds. With a howl that quaked the air, the beast crashed into the crater, widening it for a third time.

Eik’s eyes were wild as he drove the spike further into the Worldbreaker’s body, crystalline tendrils growing outward in all directions from the main shaft to hold the bastard in place.

A shell on his shoulder took some of the power of a counter from the Lord, but not enough to prevent a pitch black rod from going straight through the flesh of his shoulder. Using their proximity, the four-armed fiend also hammered two fists into Eik’s stomach.

Blood seeped out from the corner of the human’s lips as he grinned, the ferocity in his eyes remaining just as intense. “I can’t think of anything cool to say, but eat this, bitch,” he giggled before his face melted in front the Lord’s eyes.

His suddenly liquid body was blasted apart and absorbed into the mass of the leviathan serpent, the tip of whose snout abruptly tore through the ground from above and rammed into the Lord with mountain-shattering speed and force.

“Give me an update on the triple VIPs! Have they been brought to safety?” Mn’Toakh barked, her voice carrying well across the smoking ruin of a former trade district of Gimleh. As a practiced commander, the sound amplification she was capable of doing with her aura wasn’t the only thing that made her words near impossible to ignore.

“Every person on the list of triple VIPs is safe and accounted for! We received reports that they have reached the secure location approximately three minutes and twenty seconds ago.”

“Good work,” Mn’Toakh said with a nod of approval but continued. “And squad seven, eight, and twelve remain assigned to their protection?”

The captain who had been reporting to her frowned tensely at that but nodded. “They are, but… General, if I may speak freely…” He paused for her approval, and then took her silence as such. She suspected she already knew what he was going to say.

“They are among our most powerful units, all A-rankers and each led by S-rankers. It is my opinion that we would be better served with them stationed where their strength could actually be of use to us. Guarding a frankly tiny group of individuals, which is unlikely to come under attack in the first place, is a waste. I implore you to reassign them to one of the many hot spots where people are losing their lives every second!”

“Quiet!” she shouted with sudden, uncharacteristic intensity. To the captain’s surprise, she seemed to have trouble meeting his eyes. That was… incredibly unlike the usually practical and stoic General Mn’Toakh. Always fair and straightforward, she was one of the most well-liked high officials in the entire Alliance. “You think I don’t know that, huh?” she hissed. The eyes that finally found his were narrow and stressed.

“I— No, General Mn’Toakh, I would never pres—” he tried but was quickly cut off.

“Have you given it even a moment’s thought why I might have decided to sacrifice three elite squads consisting of more high-rankers than there are people in the group they are tasked with protecting? Do you think it’s because I can’t think of anywhere else they could be of use? Do you think I enjoy the thought of dying children who might have been saved if even one of the members of those squads had been there to save them?” Mn’Toakh was fuming.

“N-No, of course n—”

“Do you even realize who the hell those people are? Do you?” she went on, not allowing him to finish even a single sentence.

“I—”

“They are the closest friends and family of Eik Magnasen! Eik Magnasen, the most powerful Awakened in the Alliance, the most influential business man and alchemist in the Unified Mass! And do you know what he loves more than anything else in this world? His friends, his father, his wife, his kids, and his fucking cat! If we let even one of them die while he’s putting his life on the line to save trillions, just because we couldn’t spare a few elite squads, what do you think will happen after he wins?”

At this point, the captain had given up trying to get a word in and simply remained silent, waiting for his commander’s frustration to run its course. It was a rare sight, so she must surely have needed to vent.

“You don’t know this guy like I do. He’s kind. Unimaginably so, in fact. Do you know that he has almost no money to his name, because he gives it all to the communities around him? His whole house is probably smaller than your living room, Captain! But if, for example, his daughter were to lose her life because we didn’t protect her well enough, while he fought the Lord of the Moon all alone… That would consume him,” she said, visible shiver running down her spine.

“And I don’t know if it’s because of the Worldbreaker he shares a soul with, or if it's just how he has always been, but sometimes, if I look a little too deeply, I see the glowing eyes of a monster inside that man.”
