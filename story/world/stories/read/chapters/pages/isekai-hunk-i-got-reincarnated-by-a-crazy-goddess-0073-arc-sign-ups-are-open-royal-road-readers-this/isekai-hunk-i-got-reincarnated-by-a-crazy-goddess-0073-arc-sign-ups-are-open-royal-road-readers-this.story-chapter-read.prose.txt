ARC Sign-Ups Are Open for A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea—Isekai Hunk Readers Are Invited!

Hello, Isekai Hunk readers!

I have some news from another corner of my writing multiverse.

A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea, Book One of the Of Sand and Silver series, releases on November 10, 2026.

The story first appeared on Royal Road as A Lullaby of Sand and Silver. After more than a year of editing, rewriting, renaming, and expanding its world, I am preparing the revised edition for publication—and ARC sign-ups have opened.

To avoid confusion: this ARC campaign is for A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea, not an advance copy of Isekai Hunk.

However, I also have an important update about the future of Joyce, Sigurd, and their unhinged divine problem.

I am preparing a new version of Isekai Hunk.

The story will remain a project created with my coauthor, C. L. Dionev. The central premise, Joyce’s reincarnation as Sigurd, the crazy goddess, the action, the satire, and the story’s chaotic spirit will remain at its heart.

The rewrite will give us the chance to strengthen its structure, pacing, continuity, character arcs, and worldbuilding. It will also help bring its gods, magic, reincarnation mechanics, and cosmology into closer alignment with the larger canon connecting all my stories.

I do not have a relaunch date to share yet. I will announce more information once the new version reaches the right stage.

At first glance, my stories may look as if they have nothing to do with one another:

Isekai Hunk mixes LitRPG progression, satire, reincarnation, political intrigue, dragon slaying, and a goddess with no respect for personal boundaries.

The Saga of Keres explores steampunk fantasy, global conflict, kaiju-scale threats, and the struggle between magic and science.

Killing Walkers follows an S-rank adventurer through a world shaped and destroyed by colossal beings regarded as fallen gods.

Of Sand and Silver follows a young assassin and an old wizard through a cozy-but-dangerous fantasy road adventure.

Despite those differences, all four stories belong to the same larger multiverse: the Codex of Ages.

They share the same underlying cosmology, outer gods, and metaphysical forces. This includes Aether and Keres, which serve as sources of magical fuel throughout the multiverse.

Different worlds, cultures, and civilizations understand and use these forces in different ways. Their magic may have different names, rules, systems, rituals, or technologies, but the same foundations lie beneath them.

Each story can stand on its own. You do not need to read them in a set order or understand every connection. However, readers who explore more than one series may recognize:

Shared metaphysical laws

Different expressions of Aether and Keres

Related gods and cosmic entities

Parallel myths and historical patterns

Recurring symbols, artifacts, and creatures

Clues connecting worlds that appear unrelated

The goddess, reincarnation, magical systems, and world mechanics in Isekai Hunk do not exist outside this canon. The rewrite will help reveal where they fit without sacrificing the story’s satire or chaos.

The two stories offer different reading experiences.

Isekai Hunk moves through battles, jokes, LitRPG progression, political trouble, and Joyce’s attempt to adapt to life as an absurdly muscular fantasy knight.

A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea follows a quieter road journey with a cozier heart. However, danger, monsters, magical disasters, and people making questionable decisions still fill the road.

Both stories feature:

Protagonists forced into lives they never expected

Action, monsters, and fantasy adventure

Characters trying to build new lives after losing their old ones

Softness gets you killed. Azhra learned that before the contract that cost her an eye.

Azhra, a young Azqára assassin, survives by striking first and trusting no one. But when a job goes wrong, she flees into the wilderness wounded, half-blind, and clinging to the sole thing she has left: her mother’s lullaby.

The wizard Ithráin finds her and offers the one thing Azhra fears more than violence: kindness.

Under his care, she becomes his apprentice and begins building a life she never expected to have. Then someone breaches the Vault in the Skyborne Archive. A thief steals something ancient—and something far more dangerous binds itself to Azhra.

Azhra has spent her entire life trusting the instincts that kept her alive.

Now she must decide whether those instincts are worth dying for.

A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea is Book One in the Of Sand and Silver saga, a cozy-but-dangerous fantasy adventure about found family, cursed artifacts, and a feral girl discovering that survival and living are not the same thing.
