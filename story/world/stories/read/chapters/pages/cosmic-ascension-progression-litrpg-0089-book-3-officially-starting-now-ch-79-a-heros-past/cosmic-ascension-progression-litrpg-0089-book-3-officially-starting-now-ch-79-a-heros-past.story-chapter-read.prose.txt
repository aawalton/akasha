As Noah went through the warp drive while faking unconsciousness, a memory he had long since buried started to surface in his mind, taking the form of a lucid dream.

It had been a while since he had experienced something like this in recent times. Looking at his surroundings, however, he realized that a more accurate term for this would be a nightmare.

When was it again, exactly?

He thought for an instant before finally remembering the exact time and scenario. It was an event pertaining to a period when he was both immature and inexperienced. This was also before he officially became the leader of the Constellations.

His eyes moved, observing all the old Mecha of his time on Earth, until his eyes settled on one machine far in the distance.

Titan-09

Unlike his red and black-streaked Ouroboros, this particular mecha had a beautiful and lustrous blue and golden design, exuding a regal and majestic beauty worthy of royalty. It was as tall as Noah’s mecha of the time and the pilot was one of the most skilled people he had ever had the chance and luck of knowing in his long life.

The fight that happened at the time transpired on Planet Mars— the government of Earth decided that reclaiming Mars and chasing away the Parasites was of great importance for their survival. The result sadly was anything that they had or could have expected.

They lost. Miserably, devastatingly. The majority of the army was wiped out and the squad Noah was put under was the last remaining bastion standing against the heinous aliens.

The battlefield was hell.

Metal and fire rained from the sky as the cosmic beasts swarmed like an endless tide toward them.

The planet was scorched, the remnants of fallen mechas sprawled like broken giants— their pilots reduced to nothing but whispers in the comms. The Valkyrion Fortress, their last stronghold, was barely standing. Its fate was already sealed in stone.

The enemy was unrelenting, a sea of monstrous abominations tearing through the final defensive line. And at the heart of it all, towering over the battlefield, was the Apex Parasite, a behemoth of writhing black grotesqueness that could barely be called flesh— its eyes gleaming with malevolent hunger and glee of having cornered its prey. It stood between them and victory, between them and survival.

And they had no time left.

In those times, not even Noah had his unbending optimism running for him. He was the strongest on the planet but his power alone was too meager to change the situation.

He had long since realized that they had lost and that they would all die here, buried with their machines, to be forgotten on this battlefield of death. In the cockpit of his Mech, he was already caressing the small pistol hoisted on the side. Dying was one thing but he certainly did not want to let his body become a host of those abominations.

Just then, something changed— everything changed, in fact.

｢This is it.｣

Jupiter's voice came out of the speakers, steady and unshaken.

｢You already know how this ends. We don’t get to live past this moment. We don’t get to see another sunrise.｣

Silence, save for the distant cries of the wounded and the crackling of flames that seemed to echo inside their very souls. Noah felt like he could hear their breathing, could feel their fear. Perhaps, just perhaps, he was feeling his own fear as he faced imminent death.

Noah could not help but chuckle. Jupiter, the 11th Constellation— Aquarius, and in a way, the big brother of all the constellations. He was always the most mature of them all and because of this Noah always had the hardest time to befriend him or even be on the same wavelength.

｢We have just one job. One final duty. We hold this line. We make sure that when the Serpent moves, he has a clear path. We make sure that when he strikes, nothing – absolutely nothing – stands in his way.｣

Noah's smile vanished. He wanted to shout at the top of his lungs, wanted to ask him what the hell was this bastard spouting right now.

The soldiers did not seem discouraged at Jupiter’s words.

｢I guess this is the end.｣

｢There is nothing we can do.｣

｢Damnit. I knew I should have married her before coming here.｣

｢At least you have a fiance. I am going to die a virgin.｣

A lighthearted atmosphere spread in the comm channel. With just a few words of his, the feeling of despair vanished and the only one who felt lost was none other than Noah himself. How could they smile now when only death awaited them?

Jupiter’s mecha, Titan-09, whirred as he gripped the controls. His voice roared through the comms like a war cry.

“We are already dead! But that does not mean we die for nothing!”

The Parasites shrieked, surging forward in a monstrous tide.

“If we run, we die! If we stand here, we die! But if we charge, if we fight, if we hold this enemy long enough— the Serpent will win! And if he wins, humanity survives one more day!”

The weight of those words was so heavy that he nearly buckled under them. His hand started trembling from the burden. He realized that they were being serious and more importantly, he realized that the entire plan would fail if he did not succeed. Their sacrifice would be utterly in vain.

｢Noah. You always said that you were the strongest, right?｣

A gentle voice, far different from the stern man he knew, reached him and in this moment, Noah understood.

｢I know it’s nothing but false bravado. Even Venus always teases you for that. But right now, I need you to believe in yourself more than anyone. You, Noah, are the strongest among us and right here, right now! It’s time for you to take responsibility for that goddamned title you just can’t seem to flaunt enough!｣

“Wait! Jupiter! Why fight to our death now!? We might still be able to flee. Why do we have to respect the orders of the shitty government scumbags?”

Noah screamed. He could not accept this and he was even ready for rebuttal. But what Jupiter said stunned him.

｢Yes, you are absolutely right.｣

“Huh?”

｢Everything that you thought had meaning. Every hope, dream, and moment of happiness. None of it matters as you lie down bleeding on the battlefield.｣

“Then!”

｢But does that mean our lives are meaningless? Would you say that to our slain comrades? What about their lives? Were they meaningless?｣

Noah could say nothing. He had no more words to relay after that.

｢THEY WERE NOT!!｣

｢Their memory serves as an example to us all. Their lives have meaning because we, the living, refuse to forget them and as we fly to certain death, we trust our successors to do the same for us!｣

Jupiter gave Noah no chance to retort, no opportunity to even speak, and immediately shouted.

｢So I ask you now, my soldiers! WILL YOU RISE WITH ME?｣

A chorus of voices erupted in response, raw and defiant:

｢｢｢YES, SIR!｣｣｣

Titan-09’s thrusters roared to life.

“Then listen well!” He took a breath, the weight of it all settling onto his shoulders. The weight of leading them to their deaths.

｢My soldiers… SCREAM!｣

The Mechas charged, weapons igniting, plasma blades drawn. The air was filled with battle cries, voices rising above the chaos.

｢My soldiers… RAGE!｣

The first impact was thunderous. Blades clashed. Guns roared. Mechas slammed into Parasites with earth-shattering force.

｢MY SOLDIERS… FIGHT!｣

The battlefield came to life anew. Titan-09 led the charge, spearing through the writhing mass of horrors, the goliath’s saber made of pure lightning and plasma carving a path through the chitinous flesh of the abominable hive. The Parasites screamed as the mechas cut into them, their glowing maws snapping at steel bodies, their tendrils lashing against armored plating. It was chaos. It was carnage— it was war. The ugly reality of their existence.

Jupiter fought with everything he had, carving through the enemy with reckless fury. One after another, his soldiers fell. One by one, their signals blinked out on his display.

Yet they did not falter. They did not break. They held on. All for their one and only hope. For the future of humanity.

And so they fought, carving a path for Noah but then, the Apex Parasite was moving and at that moment, Noah understood.

* * *

It knew.

It had seen through the plan.

It was coming for him. The plan was going to fail.

Yet, Jupiter did not let that happen.

With a final push, he slammed his controls forward, forcing Titan-09 to its limits and even beyond. The mecha’s core glowed red-hot, its systems screaming in protest.

One last attack. One final stand.

｢NOAH—GO!｣

Jupiter roared through the comms.

｢DON’T LOOK BACK! GO! NOW!｣

Noah hesitated. His mecha, Ouroboros, stood at the ready— waiting, unwilling to leave him behind.

Jupiter bared his teeth.

｢DAMN IT, NOAH! WE DIDN’T THROW AWAY OUR LIVES FOR YOU TO STAY BEHIND!｣

Titan-09’s core reached critical mass. The cockpit was burning hot, alarms flashing red.

Jupiter allowed himself a small smile.

｢Tell Juno that I am sorry.｣

Then, with the last of his strength—

Titan-09 charged.

Straight into the Apex Parasite.

And as the battlefield was swallowed in light—

Jupiter’s voice echoed one last time.

｢MOVE— NOWWWW!!!｣

* * *

There was silence. Then—

While silent tears streamed down Noah’s face, Ouroboros’s thrusters ignited and he vanished in a blur of blistering speed, racing toward the deadly wounded Apex Parasite.

<<Deep Dive 3>>

He was still too weak to reach the Full Dive. Even the 3rd Level was proving to be too difficult for him.

Nonetheless, he could not give up. He would not give up. Not now, not ever. All of them had sacrificed their lives for him for this very moment. To deliver this final strike.

His spear would reach its target no matter what. No matter the cost.

<<Gungnir!!>>

On that day? a hero was born but for Noah, hundreds of true heroes died just so that he could deal that final finishing blow.

On that day, he understood. Being the strongest meant shouldering the hope of all of mankind on his shoulders and having the duty of bringing victory no matter how many soldiers had to die for his sake.

On that day…… Noah decided to wear the mantle of Jupiter and become a man who could laugh and bring hope even in the face of the greatest despair.
