Chen Ren has found a footing in this new world, but now he has to build a sect.Having been victorious in the Cloud Mist City tournament, and after finding more about his Dao of Money, Chen Ren earned a stretch of land where he wants to build his sect.But he's quite young to be a sect leader, and no honest cultivator would ever want to be a part of such a fledgling sect. Moreover, he also had to expand his business to the rest of the Kalian Empire to keep his cultivation rising and gold flowing.To achieve both, he not only creates a new sect system, but also travels to different parts of the empire, creating new businesses and gaining both allies and enemies. All while also discovering that one of his new sect members, Anji, might have secrets of her own.Problems are in dozens, but Chen Ren isn't one to back down, especially with his trusted cat companion Yalan on his side.Book 2 in this unique take on Cultivation Progression Fantasy, featuring a highly competent and rational MC.
