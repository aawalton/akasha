Levi stared at the ranger in disbelief. There was no way he’d just been called a nobody by someone he’d never met in his life before. He wasn’t one to get easily riled up, but the way the blonde-haired man had spoken to him made the rogue consider pulling down his hood just so the ranger could get a good look at who he was calling a nobody. His head steamed from the absolute certainty in the way the man had addressed him, but he refrained from doing anything rash.

Besides, it’s not like he’s wrong. I am a nobody. The mark on my forehead wouldn't do much for me now, especially since he’s seen my level. I think it’d be better to check him out and find out what exactly we’re dealing with.

[ Name: Gretos Lrae

Class: Ranger

Rank: E

Level: 32 ]

You’ve got to be kidding me.

Levi’s disbelief only deepened at what he’d seen. The damned ranger was an E-ranked synced, and it’d be nothing short of impossible for him to take on the ranger alone. Luckily for him, he wasn't alone; the mage was at the other side, still suffering from whatever blow that the ranger had dealt to her. Truth be told, he wasn't sure that there was anything the pair could do to the ranger, especially since they hadn’t had the time to work on coordinating attacks, plus there was the issue of the arrow aimed at him.

He tightened his grip on his daggers and rolled his tongue in his mouth. His eyes drifted off to the side, where he caught a glimpse of the mage slumped against a tree, clutching her side. She was down, and this could be his chance to leave her.

She’d be safe; the ranger wouldn’t kill her, especially when the family eventually sent the money needed for the exchange.

At least, that was the possibility that Levi suspected would be the outcome of this whole thing. Besides, he had the new cloak, daggers, and boots that the mage had bought him. If he decided to trust the ranger, then it’d make sense for him to run away with those. The mage would eventually be returned to her family later on.

Sure, she’d be safe, but would she be happy? What kind of person will I become if I decide to leave her?

She’d given up nobility to travel with him, even at the threat of death from those who sought his head, and here he was considering if it was better to leave her in the hands of their attacker, in hopes that her house would be willing to barter for a spare.

“I won't be leaving,” Levi said defiantly, as he quickly moved to close the distance between him and their attacker.

The ranger loosed the arrow as soon as Levi took his first step, but the rogue had been prepared for that action; he side-stepped the attack with some difficulty, barely managing to keep his footing as he continued his charge towards the ranger, launching an attack of his own as soon as he caught a glimpse of what he was searching for.

[You have successfully cast [Shadow hold(I)] ]

Mana poured out of Levi as his skill activated, chaining the blonde-haired man to his shadow. To the physical eyes, nothing had happened, but both men knew the reality of their situation. The rogue’s skill would make it impossible for the ranger to move from his current location for five seconds. It wasn’t a lot of time, but it’d have to do.

The rogue nearly skidded to a halt as a wicked smile appeared on the ranger's face. He’d not expected that reaction from the ranger. Had the ranger broken out of his skill? He didn't think so, as the system hadn’t alerted him to that, or was this a tactic by the blonde man to throw him off his attack?

[You have entered an area of effect spell Quicksand(II) ]

As soon as the message popped up at the corner of his eyes, Levi tried to jump back and out of the area, only for his right foot to suddenly sink into the dirt rapidly.

With as much control as he could, he contorted his left leg mid-air so that he landed on his knee, sparing it from the same fate.

[You have failed to resist Quicksand(II). For the next ten seconds, any part of your body in contact with the sand that is not stable will continue to sink in the quicksand.]

Levi cursed at the system update. How in the hell had he managed to get caught off guard? He should've known to keep his distance when that mischievous smile had popped up on the ranger's face; then again, there’d been no way to tell if the ranger had just been putting on a front.

Gritting his teeth in annoyance, he considered his situation.

He was only a few feet from the ranger, and they were both stuck due to the effects of their different skills. The ticker indicated that the duration of his skill was half that of the ranger’s, meaning he’d still be trapped when the ranger regained the ability to move in a few seconds.

The blonde-haired man was already pulling out another arrow for a new attack, and both men knew that the chances of the rogue evading any arrow fired at him from such close range were slim to none.

His knuckles turned white from how hard he gripped his daggers. For a moment, he considered throwing them at the ranger, but held back. If he missed, then he’d be left without any avenue of attack.

“Remember that you could’ve walked away from this unharmed, but you chose to play hero and now you have no one but yourself to blame for what is about to happen now.” The ranger sneered, pulling his bowstring taut as he sucked in a deep breath and released the arrow.

Levi instinctively knew that he could not evade the attack, so he did the next best thing. He used his arm as a shield to stop the incoming attack that was aimed at his head. The rogue bit down on his tongue hard, drawing blood and doing his best to suppress the urge to scream as the silver head of the arrow pierced through the soft flesh of his arm.

A heartwrenching scream echoed in the forest, but it hadn't emanated from Levi—the rogue had gnashed his teeth and somehow managed to keep his mouth shut throughout his own ordeal.

The sound sickening had been from the lips of the ranger, who frantically clawed at his own face, which had now been set ablaze by an item that had hit him on the head.

[ Time limit reached, Shadow hold (I) dispelled]

He dismissed the notification with a thought, staring in horror at the face of the ranger which was covered in white flames, the flames had caught onto the bow strings and the quiver of the ranger turning them into ash instantly, even the blonde man’s cloak was not spared, the white flames ate up every material thing within seconds but it seemed to dwell on his skin a lot longer, burning brightly as the ranger screamed out his lungs.

What in the hell is that flame made of?

Freed from Levi’s skill, the ranger stumbled around in the forest, wailing as the fire consumed him. The flames seemed to consume pieces of his skin and hair at a much slower rate than they had consumed his clothes and equipment, but the rogue did not doubt for one second that the ranger was feeling the full wrath of the unnatural flames.

Looking beyond the burning man, he saw Xara. They locked eyes, and she flashed him a weak grin. The mage had moved from the last position he’d seen her; she was now sprawled on the dirt, and her skin looked paler than before. It didn’t take the rogue much time to spot the reason why the mage was down on the dirt.

An arrow was sticking out from her left shoulder, and blood pooled around the area of her robe that surrounded the injury. Her head was droopy, and in her eyes, he saw tiredness. She’d been fighting with an injured shoulder all this time, while he’d been running around seeking to catch the ranger off guard.

The same ranger who seemed to have changed his path and was now headed straight for the mage, the man screaming in defiance as he stumbled towards Xara, intent on taking her down with him.

Levi stared at the stumbling man in horror. The rogue desperately pushed against the quicksand, but that worsened his situation as he sank deeper with every movement. Frustrated by his current predicament, he threw one of his blades at the ranger, and it landed with an audible squelch, sinking hilt deep into the back of the man who was on fire. Levi fully expected the blade to stop the ranger, but it didn't.

The ranger’s sole focus seemed to be on getting to the mage, and so he was left to watch helplessly as the man continued to stumble towards the mage.

“Come on, Come on, Come on!” Levi screamed as he struggled against the quicksand. The ranger was only a few steps away from the mage, and he could tell that it wouldn't end well for her if the ranger caught up.

He held up his remaining dagger, aiming at the neck of the ranger, fully prepared to throw, even though he knew that the erratic stumbling of the man would make it almost impossible for him to land the attack accurately. Sucking in a deep breath, he readied himself for the throw, but paused once he got a system notification.

[ Time limit reached, QuickSand(II) dispelled ]

With the urgency of a madman, Levi pulled his right leg free of the quicksand, which had now turned into mud. He dismissed the system notification and charged at the ranger. He couldn’t afford to waste a second; Xaras’ life was at risk. Even at full speed, he’d almost been too slow.

The ranger had enjoyed a good head start, but Levi had managed to catch up to the man. His dagger went up in a wicked arc as he slashed at the exposed neck of the ranger, careful to avoid making contact with the white flames.

To his surprise, the slash closed up within a breath even though his blade had gone deep. The white-hot flames had cauterized the wounds in a split second, and Levi was left with no other option but to attack again.

“Die, you piece of shit!” Levi screamed as he viciously plunged his dagger into the ranger's heart.

Even though he’d stabbed from behind, he suspected that his attack had pierced their attacker's heart. His suspicion was proven to be true when the ranger dropped to his knees, desperately trying and failing to pull the weapon from his back. Within a few heartbeats, the ranger fell flat on his face, just a few steps away from the mage.

Levi pulled out his blade as fast as he could, collapsing to the floor as the searing heat of the flames and smell of burning flesh finally registered in his brain. He would’ve puked, but his stomach was empty, so he could do nothing but stare as the flames greedily consumed the ranger's corpse, burning brightly until nothing was left, and spoon enough all that remained at the spot was ashes.

The area around the ashes appeared to have been unaffected. The white flames had been controlled, only targeting the ranger and what he had on him, and luckily for Levi, he’d pulled his blade fast enough to avoid it sharing the same fate as the other one. It had burned to ashes just as the rest of the rangers' equipment.

He desperately wanted to close his eyes for a second, even if to cleanse his mind from the horror that he’d just witnessed, but before he could, he heard the mage mutter in a weak voice. “Help me.”
