“No, it stays in a fixed radius. Whoever’s in there raising it, they seem to get stronger when the wall is up.”

So, it wasn’t just a barrier to keep people out—or in. It probably also provided some buff to the caster at its center.

“Nugget, can you fly above the wall and deliver this to Alden? Stay with him and Cassandra until you know they’re safe, okay?”

The dragon chirped and gripped the vial of Snapwake Salts in one talon, then flew up high. Nervous, Logan watched and only relaxed when the dragon crossed the threshold, above the wall’s edge. It did a small flip, which Logan suspected was to ease Logan’s worry.

He tapped his finger against his chin. Three hours was an awfully long time to wait for the wall to come down. He had already lost an entire day just by crossing through the Ruined Wilds. Sure, Mariv and Razor were strong, and clearly they still lived because his quests to restore the gnomish homeland of the Waterwoods and save dragonkind were both still active. But they had to be exhausted from such a long fight.
