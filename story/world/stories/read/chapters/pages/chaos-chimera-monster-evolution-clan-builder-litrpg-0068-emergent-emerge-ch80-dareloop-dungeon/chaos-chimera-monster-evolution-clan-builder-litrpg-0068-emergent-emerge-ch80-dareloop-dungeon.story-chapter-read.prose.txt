KiAera adjusted her stance, her ears twitching at the slightest noise from the cave's depths. "This is it?"

Nex folded his wings casually, a cocky smirk tugging at the corners of his mouth. "Yep. The Dareloop Cave. Beyond here lies the dungeon you're after—if you've got the guts to venture in."

Rox gave an exaggerated shiver, her pink-streaked fur catching the faint light of KiAera's spectral flame. "Creepy, isn't it? Gets worse the deeper you go. Good thing we're here to babysit."

Viz, standing a little too close to Scarbol for comfort, nervously nodded. "Y-yeah, it's… uh, not so bad once you get used to it. Kinda. Maybe."

Scarbol snorted, unimpressed. "We're not here to play around. Let's get moving before the sun catches us." His tone was gruff, but there was an edge of unease in his stance.

KiAera turned to Nex. "You said you know this place better than anyone. What makes you so sure we won't get lost in there?"

Nex spread his wings slightly, casting a shadow that made him seem larger than life. "Because we're Nexperbax. This is our playground."

He tapped one of his four glowing eyes with a claw. "Echolocation. We see with sound. The Dareloop's twists and turns? The pitch-black abyss of the dungeon? It's nothing to us. Every bounce of sound maps the world like a picture in our heads."

Rox chimed in with a grin playful. "And stealth, don't forget that. No one sees a Nexperbax unless we want them to."

Tailza tilted her head as her hands clasped together nervously. "That's… amazing. But can you really keep all of us hidden?"

Nex rolled his eyes. "Not all of you, no. But if you follow my lead and don't do anything stupid, you'll make it out in one piece."

KiAera raised an eyebrow, her patience was already wearing thin. "That's… comforting. I hope your bravado isn't just for show, Nex. Otherwise, you'll regret dragging me into this."

Nex smirked. "Feisty as ever. I like that."

Rox clapped her claws together. "Alright, enough flirting. Time's wasting, and if you want those crystals before Mina and Boggorc come knocking, we better move."

KiAera shot her a glare as her ears twitched. "Flirting? Hardly. Let's go."

Viz let out a quiet, nervous laugh as the group began to descend into the cave. The walls narrowed quickly, the air growing colder with every step. Tailza hovered close to KiAera, the kobolbo girl holding KiAera's ear hand tightly. She gazed back, but turned her gaze away with a nod.

"We'll be fine," KiAera whispered.

As they moved deeper, the natural light faded entirely, replaced by the faint bioluminescence of glowing fungi that dotted the cave walls like tiny, eerie stars. Nex's wings brushed against KiAera's shoulder as he leaned in.

"Stay close," he whispered. "You'll want to be quiet from here on out. The Dareloop Dungeon doesn't like noisy visitors."

KiAera nodded, her spectral flame dimming to a faint glow. Whatever lay ahead, she wasn't about to let Nex or her companions come to harm.

Saying that, her ears flicked back and forth, scanning for sounds.

Beside her, Scarbol led at the fore with his shoulders squared and axe glinting in the faint fungal glow. Behind them, Nex and Rox moved, their wingbeats near-silent. Viz lagged slightly, muttering nervously under his breath as his claws clicked against the stone.

She noted that the oppressive darkness competed against her flame's luminance as they descended further into the Dareloop Dungeon. Its silence pressed against KiAera's ears, broken only by the soft rustle of their movements and the faint drip of moisture from stalactites high above.
