Since it has been a while, I'll add a summary as it would be helpful.

After fifteen years of unawakened frustration, Johan risks his life to save a child during a "Classless Rift". While unconscious, he undergoes a trial in a dreamscape called Phantasia, where he completes the quest "Save the Tree" by grafting a healthy branch onto a decaying stump. He is granted the Unique Class: Tower Farmer.

Johan soon discovers that he can use the [Translocation] skill to return to Phantasia, which is abundant with rare resources like Golden Pears. Using his [General Knowledge (Lv. MAX)] and [Basic Brewing] skills, he creates a Basic Healing Potion with 40% HP/Magik recovery—far surpassing the quality of the elite Mage Tower.

To establish his market, Johan gives a sample potion to a B-rank Hunter, Cara Rosewood. Its incredible performance during a rift raid leads to viral fame on Hunter Net. This catches the attention of Lucas White, the S-rank head of the Agni Clan.

Johan strategically bargains with Lucas, providing Ice Resistance Potions that allow the Agni Clan to clear the SS-rank rift Mahapadma, a feat previously deemed impossible. During this period, Johan also begins his "Tamer" arc by befriending Eunally, a Gluttonous Rabbit who helps automate his farming. The arc concludes with the reporter Shadowman leaking Johan’s identity to the public.

The publicity brings Merilyn Faust, the Hunter Association President, to Johan's mart. She offers him protection in exchange for a "miracle": brewing a Detoxifying Potion to save the legendary blacksmith Master Adelan from a petrifying poison.

Johan faces significant agricultural challenges, as the Night Bloom seeds require a poisonous, sunless environment. After multiple failures—including an exploding pot—he realizes the plants need a specific metallic "fresh air" environment. He succeeds in brewing a C-rank potion that saves Adelan. Merilyn rewards him by using her unique class to "pull the darkness" of exhaustion from his body.

Johan discovers that Phantasia is actually a floor within the Black Tower, making him the only farmer with direct access to "Tower-grade" soil. He tames the three-headed Guardian Beast Cerberious and a pollinator butterfly named Parakeet. He also cultivates "Pro" variants of vegetables that can burn 5g of body fat per consumption, creating a massive potential market among elite social circles.

His childhood friend, Yuna Lee, returns after clearing the Tower's 48th floor. To protect her and himself, Johan rejects exclusive contracts and instead forms a strategic alliance with Lucas, Merilyn, and Yuna. He agrees to supply them with raw Tower materials, turning himself into a resource "pillar" that the world's strongest powers cannot afford to lose.
