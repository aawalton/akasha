Seraphina Marquess, a name synonymous with tyranny, hailed from Veridara, a world scarred by endless wars. She was the fourth princess of the Marquess family, Harsh rulers who had emerged victorious from a century of bloodshed. Their dominion was absolute, enforced by unparalleled mastery over water magic. This power allowed them to summon storms, control rivers, and even manipulate the very essence of life itself.

But for Seraphina, power in her home world was not enough. A relentless ambition burned within her, a hunger for conquest that could never be satisfied. Veridara had become stale, a gilded cage stifling her ambitions. Then came the system.

Materializing on the third stage of the tutorial, the ground trembled as she appeared, bathed in an ethereal glow. Before her lay a breathtaking world of towering, bioluminescent flora in hues of deep purple and electric blue. A crystalline river snaked through the landscape; its waters so clear they seemed unreal. This untouched paradise was a new stage for her ambition.

Her companions, however, were a far cry from the loyal soldiers she had commanded on Veridara. She found herself accompanied by three ordinary citizens from her former world: Elias, a kind-hearted farmer; Anya, a spirited merchant; and old Ben, a grizzled veteran. Their fear was evident, but Seraphina cared little for their emotions. They were tools, disposable assets in her grand design. A cruel smile spread across her lips as she assessed them. This world, with its untapped potential, was a blank canvas for her to paint her reign.

Seraphina’s new class, Tidewalker, granted her unparalleled control over water. She felt its power surge through her as she connected with the nearby river. Water swirled at her command, forming a protective barrier around her and her companions. With an air of false calm, she addressed the terrified group.

"Fear not," she said, her voice dripping with faux kindness. "I am your protector in this strange world."

Elias trembled first. "Please, we just want to go home. I have a wife and children... they must be worried."

Anya, her eyes brimming with tears, echoed his plea. "We’ve done nothing to deserve this. Please, let us return to our families."

Old Ben stood tall, despite his age. "We won’t be a burden, Your Highness. I know how to survive in hostile lands."

Seraphina’s smile didn’t falter. "Your usefulness will be determined in time. For now, obey me."

Internally, she reveled in the thrill of this new world. Untouched and defenseless, it was hers for the taking. With a wave of her hand, she summoned a torrent of water that washed away their footprints, erasing all trace of their arrival. This was a place where her ambition could run wild, and she would shape it to her liking whatever the cost.

"A new game begins," she whispered to herself, her eyes gleaming with anticipation. "And I will win."
