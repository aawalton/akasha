"Why did you stop all of a sudden?" Tristan grumbled, looking around the forest. Obviously, he didn't notice Snowglow.

Callie couldn't see the glimmerhare either. She was nowhere to be seen, although Callie was certain that she was somewhere right next to them. Right?

She looked around the scenery, trying to spot the glimmerhare or hear her voice again, but everything was silent. Did she imagine that?

Help!

Snowglow called again, and this time Callie managed to see the glimmerhare. She was hiding right beneath the spruce, not too far away from them.

"What?" Tristan asked, sounding irritated.

What to do? Snowglow clearly was afraid of Tristan, and he certainly could be a bit of a bully at times. But if you discounted his first encounter with Wiggles, he had been very understanding of arcane beasts. Besides, in the worst-case scenario, Callie couldn't go anywhere alone anyway.

"There's an arcane beast around here that needs help," Callie explained in a quiet voice.

"What? Where?" The boy looked around again. "I don't see anything."

"She's hiding. Snowglow is very shy and doesn't like to reveal herself to the people. But she sounds to be in trouble. Would you mind waiting here while I talk to her?"

"You know you can't go anywhere alone?" Tristan crossed his arms over his chest.

"I'm not going anywhere. She's right here. I just don't want her to get scared," Callie said.

Tristan didn't look too happy about this, but eventually nodded with the most dramatic huff Callie had heard from him. "Fiiine! Talk to your beast friend or whatever, but I'm not leaving!"

Callie carefully made her way to Snowglow, who huddled deeper underneath the spruce while keeping a fearful glance at Tristan.

"He's harmless, I promise," Callie said, although she noticed that the boy was trying to stand on his tiptoes to see exactly who Callie was talking to. He likely still hadn't noticed Snowglow among the white snow.

Snowglow's nose twitched nervously, but it appeared that whatever problem she had was more urgent than Tristan's presence.

Must help! Big trouble.

"What happened? Is something wrong with your family?" Callie asked, thinking of the glimmerhare colony hidden in the dragon's cave.

No. Family fine. But the guardian is not. Much pain. Much blood.

Callie's breath hitched. She wasn't quite sure who Snowglow was talking about, but any arcane beast being in pain and bleeding was horrible.

You must help!

Callie nodded, getting to her feet. "Show me where they are!"

"Hey, where are you going?" Tristan questioned.

"An arcane beast is hurt!"

"And you're just going to rush to it without a second thought?" The boy narrowed his eyes and walked to Callie, flicking her forehead. "Have you not learned anything?"

Callie rubbed at her forehead. "But… I have to help. They are bleeding."

"Okay, so stop and think about what you need to do? I'm all for getting in trouble, but at least be smart about it. Sheesh!"

If Tristan was saying this, then Callie definitely needed to pause. But her heart was beating like crazy from worry. One couldn't just sit around and consider their option if there was bleeding involved.

However, Tristan was right that she needed to think a little. If the creature was bleeding, she needed bandages and millefolia leaves. That meant that she had to visit Granny Ethel first. The elderly woman would no doubt understand Callie's need to help, but could she run off without warning anyone else?

No, ideally, Callie should have an adult with her, not just Tristan. She didn't even know what kind of creature this guardian was. It was unlikely to be a predator, judging by how worried Snowglow was, but it was likely an arcane beast she had never met before.

"Who is this guardian?" Callie asked Snowglow, who was hiding underneath the spruce.

The guardian protects us. Protects the swamp. But now in trouble. Must help!

A creature that protected the swamp. Could that be the famed swamp king? No, this definitely wasn't something Callie should face alone.

"I-I'll go get some medicine and tell my Mom!" Callie said to both Snowglow and Tristan.

"Let me get the medicine. It'll be faster if we split up. We'll meet up at your house," Tristan suggested.

Callie nodded, and they both ran towards the direction of the village, splitting up at Callie's house. Callen and Dad were nowhere to be seen, likely somewhere out in the village working. But Mom was at home, looking at gasping Callie with surprise.

"What's wrong, dear? You look as if you've seen a ghost," Mom noted, as she worked on patching the hole in Callie's sweater.

"An arcane beast is injured. We have to help!" Callie said.

"Injured? What arcane beasts? Didn't we say that you wouldn't approach unknown arcane beasts?" Mom furrowed her brows.

"I didn't. Snowglow told me. She said that the guardian is hurt and bleeding."

"The guardian?" Mom blinked in confusion.

"I don't know exactly what it is, but I have a feeling it's the swamp king. Snowglow said it guards the swamp and everyone there."

Mom jumped up to her feet, her face growing incredibly pale. "Th-The swamp king?"

Callie wasn't sure what to think of this reaction. Did this mean that Mom wouldn't let her go help?

"I-I don't know if it is the swamp king. I just know that it is in pain and bleeding. Mom, whoever it is, we have to go help! It might die!" Callie insisted.

Mom shook her head numbly. "W-We can't save every creature in the forest… Nature must be allowed to act according to its own laws."

Was Mom afraid? Her hands seemed to be trembling. Callie had never seen such a strong reaction from her. After all, she had even gone to bravely face the glauxis, even if she didn't think she could beat it. Was the swamp king truly such a scary arcane beast?

"But Snowglow came asking for help! Surely, if he's seeking help, then something not natural has happened. This winter has been very tough on everyone. If we hadn't helped Snowglow, then the glimmerhares would have starved to death. I highly doubt nature intended for them to just die!” Callie said stubbornly.

Mom looked at Callie for a long moment, then sighed. "Y-Yes, I suppose you're right in that this winter has been extremely harsh. Very well, we shall go, but… if I say that the creature is too dangerous to approach, you will not approach it. Is that clear?"

"Yes, Mom!" Callie agreed.

"Good, now don't you need some supplies?"

"Tristan went to get those."

Not even a minute later, there was a knock on the door. Tristan had returned in record time, carrying a satchel filled to the brim with medical supplies.

"I explained everything to Granny Ethel, and she gave one of the first aid satchels," he said, inclining his head politely towards Mom.

She sighed. "You kids are far too laid-back about this sort of thing. When we approach the beast, you remain behind me. Both of you!"

"Got it!" Callie and Tristan replied in unison.

Mom grabbed her sword, put on her leather chest piece, and they were off, heading to the same place where Snowglow had found them.

"Snowglow! I've brought help. Will you lead us to the injured arcane beast?"

Snowglow peeked nervously from beneath the spruce. Her nose was trembling from fear, but it appeared that she was willing to endure it, so long as the help was promised. This creature must be incredibly important for Snowglow to accept such a risk.

Follow! Quick!

The glimmerhare jumped forward through the snow as quick as lightning. Callie barely managed to keep track of Snowglow; that's how quick she was. But Mom was able to keep up with the hare, running after her through the snow with practised ease. As expected of a hunter.

This obviously made Snowglow nervous as she would occasionally just disappear, making them wonder where she had gone. When Callie called out, Snowglow would reappear, and they would hurry ahead.

"We're getting quite deep in the forest…" Mom noted nervously. Her pace had slowed down.

Callie and Tristan were barely keeping up, since they had been trudging through the rather deep snow for a while now.

"Are we still far, Snowglow?" Callie asked, through gasps.

Almost there.

"We're almost there," Callie repeated to Mom and Tristan.

"Finally!" The boy grumbled.

However, as they followed Snowglow, Callie realized that she hadn't been in this particular part of the forest. The ground was oddly smooth underneath their feet, as if they were walking in a meadow.

Mom's expression grew harsh. "Kids, stop!"

"What?" Callie and Tristan both peered at her.

"This is the edge of the Deep Forest," Mom announced.

Callie and Tristan gasped, both looking around nervously. This was the forbidden Deep Forest. It didn't seem that much different from the rest of the forest. In fact, it looked more sparse.

Trees didn't grow together as densely as everywhere else. They seemed to grow in small, bunched-up clusters. As if there wouldn't be enough space everywhere else, although there was clearly plenty of space around.

"How can you tell?" Tristan asked nervously.

"We are currently standing on marsh ice. That's why trees are so sparse," Mom explained.

"That explains it…" Callie muttered.

Please, help!

Snowglow peered at Callie with large, pleading eyes. Callie turned to Mom.

"We have to keep going! The Deep Forest is dangerous because of the swamp, but if it's all frozen, isn't it safe?"

Mom looked torn. "It's true that the swamp itself is harmless while frozen, but there are still plenty of dangerous creatures here."

"But we can't let it bleed out. We've come so far already. Snowglow says we're close," Callie pleaded.

"I mean… Are we really in the Deep Forest if there's no swamp? I say that Deep Forest doesn't exist during winter," Tristan said cheekily.

Mom glared at him, but eventually relented. "We're only going a little deeper. If it's too far, we're turning back."

Callie and Tristan nodded and hurried after Snowglow.

The glimmerhare led them deeper, but it soon became clear that they really weren't far. Droplets of blood could be seen on the snow.

They all shared a nervous glance. They were close to the mysterious creature. Callie's heart thundered. What was this creature? How badly hurt was it? Could she actually help?

Slowly, taking careful steps, they followed the blood drops. They quickly grew into a trail of blood, until they finally came upon the most horrifying scene in Callie's life.

A large, majestic moose with antlers that seemed to grow moss on them as well as plants that hung down like willow branches. Its coat looked dark green, as if swamp soil was growing on its back. But the horrifying part was that it was caught in a trap.

One of its legs was clamped in a large bear trap, blood trickling down its legs onto the white snow. There were also snare wires that had wrapped themselves around the deer's other legs and body, digging painfully into the flesh. Blood was dripping down in places.

When the creature's gaze met with Callie's, she saw incredible pain in them as well as rage. Its eyes had gone red, and it bellowed so loudly that the whole forest shook.

HUMANS! YOU WILL PAY WITH WHAT YOU'VE DONE WITH YOUR LIVES!
