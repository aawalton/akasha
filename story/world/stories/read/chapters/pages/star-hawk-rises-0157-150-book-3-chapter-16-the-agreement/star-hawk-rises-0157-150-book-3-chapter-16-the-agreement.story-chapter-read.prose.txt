I sat back up and glanced around the room before remembering I had used my divine sense last time and closed my eyes. A heartbeat later, I was circling my cultivation technique as I searched the ship for Itshu Biyu.

Found her asleep in her quarters and gently pressed my divine sense against her body, watching her jolt upright and draw her sword from a spatial ring, and swung it around a few times as she tried to identify the threat.

“Relax, it's just me, I have something important I need you to carry out for me.” I said to her after my divine sense brushed up against her mind again.

I watched her lower her sword slowly, her eyes still darting around the room for a few more heartbeats before the sword vanished back into her spatial ring. She then cupped her fist and gave a half bow.

“What important task do you wish this one to carry out?” She asked the empty room.

“Assemble everyone, I wish to speak with them and allow them to make a decision. However, before that, I think we should talk, come to my cultivation room.” I told her before retracting my Divine sense.

I caught her bow again before she moved to leave the room, before my divine sense retracted far enough away. I continued to circle my cultivation technique, drawing in Qi as I waited for Itshu Biyu to arrive.

I didn’t have to wait long, before the door opened, and she stepped into the room, giving a half bow to me. Once she had stood up again, she raised an eyebrow as if to ask me what the problem was and to hurry up and spit it out.

“I am not sure how I did it, but using my cultivation technique, I opened a door through time and space to my Cultivation Master.” I started.

I had expected some sort of reaction, but I got none. She was a stone wall of blank expressions, this caused me to raise an eyebrow, slightly curious about her indifference.

“What? It is not unheard of for a cultivator with strong roots in space and time to be able to do such a thing. I will grant you that it is rare, but it is still not unheard of.” She replied as she waved her hand for me to get to my point.

“Right,” I responded slowly as I took in that bit of information. “Right, well, apparently the space the doors exist in is filled with divine qi, and Michael sat down cultivated using that divine Qi…” I trailed off as I searched her face for anything, “he broke through to the immortal realm.” I finished.

That one had gotten a reaction out of her, her eyes widened slightly, and she sucked in an almost inaudible gasp. A heartbeat later it was gone and her stone blank expression was back in place.

“He then gave me a warning, which has led me to an idea that I played around with before it slowly became less ridiculous and more the best course of action, I would like you to know what you think before I pursue it.”

“What was the warning?” She asked, ignoring the rest of what I had said.

“There is corruption in the sect, it’s declined significantly since you left, do not come back” I repeated his words.

I saw her fists tighten, her eyes widen, and her jaw clench. She seemed to be having an internal battle with her thoughts before she slowly let out a long slow breath and her body relaxed.

“What is your ridiculous idea, that somehow seems less crazy to you?” She asked through clenched teeth. “Keep in mind, you just informed me that this entire mountain has been officially exiled from the sect.”

“Uh…” I hesitated, not having realised that Michael had exiled us, “does that mean we are all out of the sect, making us sectless?” I asked.

“The term you're looking for is Rogue Cultivators,” she spat. “And yes, we are rogue cultivators now.”

“Okay, well, my idea was we could start our own sect.” I said cheerfully, trying to put an upbeat spin on all this.

I saw her freeze, her eyes darting across my face like she was searching for something. Before they shifted to the side, I could almost see the gears in her mind start turning as she considered it and assessed what resources we had onboard, like she wanted to know if it was even possible.

“I want to say no, I want to curse you, I want to take command of this ship from you and return to sect space…” she trailed off as I saw the pain cross her face. Before a realisation dawned on it. “Oh, so that’s why they demanded you reach the immortal realm before returning” she whispered mostly to herself.

I, on the other hand, was completely confused and had no idea how she had jumped from damning me, to why someone had made a demand of me. The look of confusion must have been evident on my face.

“The sect is not an immortal sect, so an immortal can not remain in it, it would take at least three immortals to defend the sect and elevate it to an immortal sect. However, there is no guarantee that three cultivators would advance to the immortal realm before someone came to destroy everything, claiming some bullshit that boiled down to crushing the weak before it got too strong.” She waved her hand as if this were obvious.

“So what happens to…” I trailed off as she interrupted me, continuing her speech.

“That is why your Master is no longer part of the sect either… he would have known he was about to be kicked out and in order to protect you, and by extension, the rest of this mountain, he exiled all of us. We are protected from the corruption that is ravaging the sect.”

“Okay, but…” I winced as she cut me off for a second time.

“I bet, since he had taken you as a disciple, some of the more powerful clans backing the sect would have forced their own scions on him. The demand you reach the immortal realm before returning would give them an excuse to keep you out here where they could send assassins. If that failed, then by returning as an immortal, you would be exiled for realming out of the sect. That would also prevent you from claiming your Cultivation Master as your Master, all benefits and resources would then go exclusively to their clans. You would get nothing.”

We sat in silence for a few heartbeats processing that information, before she burst out laughing, making me jump. I sat there watching her laugh, even bending over to grasp her knees.

“And… and… “She wheezed out between her laughter. “And then he went…. And he… realmed out himself.”

I did not understand why that was so funny, and was about to voice my confusion when she recovered from her laughter enough to open her eyes. She got one look at the confusion on my face and burst into another fit of laughter.

“Don’t you see?” She asked a few moments later. “When a cultivator reaches the immortal realm, all sect property is reclaimed by the sect and distributed evenly and fairly among all departments. The immortal is then sent either out into the world to find their own way, or to a parent immortal sect, with all their belongings. There’s nothing to inherit!” She screamed the last four words before bursting into a fit of giggles that caused her to sink to the floor.

“The clans get nothing, and all the effort that is put into trying to get his position or inheritance is now wasted.” I said slowly.

Itshu Biyu started tapping her nose as she continued her laughter. A heartbeat later, I started to giggle, which quickly turned into laughter. It didn’t take us nearly as long as I thought it would to recover, but we were both grinning at the other once we had.

“Alright, Kenna, I will start a sect with you. But first, we both need to enter closed door cultivation and reach the immortal realm before we start one. Then we need to find Michael, who has undoubtedly informed you he was no longer your cultivation master the moment he reached the immortal realm. Then we need to invite him to be our third immortal, this will allow us to start an immortal sect. He cultivates time and space as well, so perhaps it would be a good idea to share the mortal and immortal section of your cultivation technique with him, once he agrees to join.”

I nodded my head as I considered this, then nodded my head more firmly as I came to the conclusion this was a good plan, and it would be best to begin from a firm footing, instead of from a mortals, after all my cultivation technique would carry me into the divine realm, so there was no point limiting myself to a mortal sect.

“What do you tell the rest of the ship?” I suddenly asked, remembering there were a fair number of cultivators with us.

“Nothing,” she replied quickly before wincing, “at least not until we reach the immortal realm, then we can inform them what has happened and offer a solution to the problem.”

I winced, not really wanting to hide this information from everyone, but I did see the wisdom in it. If we told them now, and didn’t have a solution, panic and chaos would rain. People would abandon the ship, most likely fleeing back to the sect, selling any information they had for a way back in. However, if we waited until we did have a solution, one that was stronger than the alternative, then they would be more likely to happily join up, to what they would perceive as a better choice.

I let out a long sigh before slowly nodding my head at her. My gaze darted around a moment before remembering I was already in my cultivation room and could simply instruct the door to be locked so I could start my closed door cultivation.

“Well, that is settled then. I shall lock you in, and inform the crew we are going radio silent, while the commander and I enter closed door cultivation.” She gave me a half bow and silently left.

I closed my eyes and focused on my cultivation again. I was currently in the seventh realm, Void Refinement, and had three more to push through, Integration, Tribulation Transcendence, and finally immortal ascension. Once I had reached the peak of immortal ascension, I would be able to step into the immortal realm.

I tried not to start giggling like a mad scientist, but it was close. I managed to focus on my cultivation technique before I started giggling. My Qi slowly circulated, as I expanded my divine sense out into space instead of around me and the ship.

I silently watched the surrounding plants, at first nothing happened, but they soon flowered and dropped seeds into their pots before withering and dying only for the pot to sprout a new plant that grew up and flowered. It happened as if someone had hit fast-forward, and the entire time I was perceiving the vastness of space.

I'm not sure when it happened exactly, but at some point something clicked and I felt my realm advance. I suddenly became more aware that time was passing at a rapid speed, a lot faster than it should be. However, I could also sense that it was localised to this room. I could also feel something at the edge of my consciousness, something like a door waiting for me to push it open.

I turned my attention toward it and the moment I brushed against it, I felt the divine Qi beyond. I almost lost my cultivation rhythm, when I realised this was how I could open the passage to that space with all those doors.
