Based on the recent poll and my preferences, I will be changing the release schedule.

What will be changing?

1. Earlier chapters will receive a few tweaks and changes.

This will not be a complete rewrite of the story or a plot change, but an expansion of some areas and polishing of the earlier chapters.

Some of these changes may include.

- More emotional progression

- Additional internal thoughts and slower transitions during important moments

- Small continuity fixes and dialogue improvements

- Certain mechanics of the novel will get a better explanation.

These edits will happen gradually over time, so please bear with me while I work through them
