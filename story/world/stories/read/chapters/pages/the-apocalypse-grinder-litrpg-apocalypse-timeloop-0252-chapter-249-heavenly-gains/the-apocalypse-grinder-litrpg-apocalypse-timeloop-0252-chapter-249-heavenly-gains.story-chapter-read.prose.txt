Ronan was pleasantly surprised by the analysis of his last iteration. Despite it ending much earlier than he had planned, he had accomplished most of what he’d set out to do.

Originally he had needed just 8 more chronos merits. That would get him the 13 he required to purchase the next level of a class of one’s own, and thus unlock his second sub-class slot. He had earned 16 merits in the most recent iteration, double what he needed.

That meant he would have a few extra merits to spend after acquiring his next sub-class. It was a good outcome, and it really stoked the fire of challenge within him. He felt as though the harder difficulty tutorial was no longer that—he needed something that truly pushed his limits and gave him the thrill he sought.

Interestingly, he had reached a cap on one of his multipliers. The credit gain multiplier had reached 100,000 percent and would not go up higher. Doing the maths in his head, he realised that without the imposed limit, his credit gain multiplier would be at almost seven million percent. That was absurd. He’d be earning 7 iron credits in place of 1 bronze credit. The first tutorial goblin would give him more than the entire first step of the Path of the Gladiator had given him the first time he completed it.

Unfortunately that wasn’t the case. He had been stopped out at a hundred thousand, which was still a ridiculous multiplier, but nowhere near what it could be. This was also the first point in favour of doing the concentration that he had held off on the last time he was in the clan’s ancestral grounds. Frankly he still wasn’t sure he wanted to do it, but it seemed he was reaching the upper bounds of what he could gain through mindless repetition.
