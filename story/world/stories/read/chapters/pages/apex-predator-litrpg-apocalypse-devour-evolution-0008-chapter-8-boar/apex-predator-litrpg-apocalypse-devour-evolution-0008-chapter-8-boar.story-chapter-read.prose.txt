Rushing through the foliage, Don approached the screams with deft movements, dashing forward in a full sprint. He arrived in a small opening in the forest and saw the situation immediately: A massive boar was snorting angrily and butting a tree. There was already a dead young man on the ground, the blood still pooling out of the his gored guts.

In the tree there was a young girl, maybe sixteen or seventeen. She had short blonde hair and a tattered school uniform. She screamed and kicked at the boar as it propped itself against the tree with its front legs.

The boar was massive. It was the size of a small horse, solid seven hundred pounds of violence. It snorted and chuffed aggressively and scraped the bark of the tree with its tusks.

This would be a hard fight, Don knew it. He was inclined to escape and leave the girl. But the boar was clearly high level, and he had the jump on it. Don would certainly level if he killed this monster. If he could strike true with his claws, and run up a tree, he could bleed the pig out. That was the plan anyway… Don had noticed plans didn’t always work.

He crept closer in a crouch. Don even stepped on some dry leaves, but the boar was too engrossed in trying to skewer the girl.

The most dangerous part of the boar was its tusks, and of course its legs, since it could trample a fallen enemy. That made the move obvious.

When Don was sure the pig wouldn’t have time to react, he launched into a sprint and jumped on the boar’s back.

With one hand he grabbed the hair that ran over the boar’s spine and the other he raised when he used his claw ability. The nails elongated into two inch blades and he stabbed his hand in the boar’s thick neck. Don managed to puncture the thick hide and get the blood flowing, but the wound was shallow. He kept stabbing and managed to get some damage done. The boar bucked and kicked him off with absolutely tremendous force. Don flew in an arc and fell on his back. The boar rushed him immediately.

Don rolled and managed to take a swipe at the boar’s legs. He couldn’t generate much force on his back, but his claws were sharp as knives and the boar provided the required force with its charge.

The boar turned, bleeted angrily and charged again. Don was already up. He dodged sideways and swiped at the boar’s face. He felt something squishy give and the boar’s eye exploded. It squealed in anger.

But the beast also managed to turn its head and slice at Don with its tusk. It punctured his side, but Don managed to get out of the way. If he hadn’t, the tusk would have gored him, and he doubted any kind of healing potion could have saved him.

[Health at 77%]

[Energy at 68%]

[Debuff inflicted: Moderate bleeding]

The wound on his side slowed him down, but the boar was shaking its head in pain as well. It didn’t recover, but it kicked about and snorted wildly in berserk before it charged Don again. Don ran towards a tree, and the boar followed. He ran up and when the boar smashed into it, he backflipped on its back. Thank you system for the amazing Attributes!

Immediately Don flipped his claws out and shoved them into the boar’s ears. The monster let out a raw scream and bucked, but Don squeezed his legs around the boar’s thick neck and kept pushing the claws further into the beast’s brains. In a wild deathrattle it managed to throw Don off its back and was about to stomp on him, but it was too weakened, so it fell on the forest ground.

[You have slain [Graytusk Boar – lvl 8] – You have gained 97% of the experience for defeating an enemy. 16 System Points earned]

[’DING!’ Race: [Evolutor (spawn)] has reached level 7 – Stat points allocated, +2 Free Attribute Point]

Don pushed away the notifications. They were absolutely amazing and he was looking forward to get into them, but first things first.

While the girl was still in the tree, Don needed to act. He sliced into the boar’s neck with his claws and drank in the essence. The vitality filled him and he felt great again.

[Racial Skill increased: Devour!]

[Devour Level 5]

[32 Evolution Points acquired!]

[Evolution trait Graytusk Boar: Consumed]

[Health at 100%]

[Energy at 100%]

[Debuffs: N/A]

32 Evolution points? That was massive. He was now at 124 points. He needed to find more of these boars. They were terrifyingly strong, and Don wondered what would have happened if it hadn’t been already preoccupied. But the experience and evolution points were definitely worth the danger. Just by killing one boar, he could immediately bring an Evolution from F-rank to E-rank. Well, at least a weaker one, like Night Vision.

Don spent the Free Attribute Points on Power and Agility. Without very high stats in those two, this fight would have gone the other way. It was only through raw speed and grace from Agility that he could dodge or do these ridiculous maneuvers like running up a tree and backflipping. And it was only through Power that he had the ability to get through the boar’s thick hide.

That and the precision from his Unarmed Fighting, enhanced by the Meta-Awareness Attribute.

Don realized that the fight had been very close. If he had been just a little bit slower or weaker, the boar might have managed to gore him with that tusk charge, or simply stampede him into a mess. Fighting against nimble and medium sized enemies, like the gibbons or Vulpin was easy. His claws had great destructive power. But a big tanky boar like this? Not his ideal foe.

But sometimes you don’t get ideal. I still did it. I surpassed myself.

Don felt a wide grin spread on his face. Even with a clear disadvantage he had managed to eke out a win. That filled him with pride. He had much more trust in himself after this fight. He had limit tested and come through the gauntlet. The hidden attribute which the System didn’t govern was growing. Confidence.

The surge in power from increasing the Attributes was noticeable, although not quite as pronounced as it had been the first time. The difference between 0 and 1 had been the greatest.

Don walked up to the tree where the girl was. “You okay in there?”

Don could only hear a sniffle and a hiccup. He sighed.

“It’s fine. The boar is dead.”

“Y-you sure?”

Don suppressed an eyeroll. The girl was in shock after all.

“Pretty sure…” he said. “Do you want to come down?”

“Can you help me?” the girl said weakly. “I’m hurt.”

Don noticed a small patch of dark on the grass under the branch the girl was sitting in. Her leg was bleeding.

Don extended his hands. “Just jump down. I’ll catch.”

The girl nodded and gathered some courage, before falling in Don’s arms. She was light as a feather and Don’s knees barely buckled when he caught her. She was blonde and petite and her white school shirt was crumpled and wet.

“Um.. hi?” the girl said and blushed.

“Hey,” Don said and put her down. She hissed and leaned against Don to take the weight off her hurt leg.

“I got twenty minutes…” the girl said. “Before I die of hyp—hypo…”

“Yeah,” Don said. “Hypovolemic shock. It means blood loss. You got a healing potion?”

She shook her head. “Already used it. That’s why the wound is small.”

Don nodded and helped the girl ease her weight against the tree. Then he walked up to the dead guy and checked his belt satchel. Nice! Two healing potions. He didn’t have to spend his own.

“Found some,” Don said cheerily. The girl looked at the dead guy and her lower lip started quivering.

Don patted her awkwardly on the shoulder. “Sorry. Someone you knew?”

The girl swallowed and wiped her nose as she hugged herself. “Matthew. He was in my math class.”

The girl was shaking and just staring at the ground. Don gently grabbed the girl by the shoulders. “Hey. Look at me. We have to move. Do you have any more friends around?”

“Yeah…” the girl said weakly and accepted the healing potion. She chugged it down and wiped her lips. “Had another classmate. Jimmy. He ran away from the boar.”

The wound on the girl’s leg knitted close. She looked at it, and nodded to herself and got up.

“Points for guts,” Don said. “I’m Don.”

“I’m Charlie,” the girl said and brushed her hair behind her ear. “And, um… thanks. For saving me.”

“We’re not out of the woods yet,” Don said. “What are you going to do?”

“There’s a camp,” Charlie said. “You should come. You’re strong. You can protect us.”

“A camp? How many people?”

“Twenty I guess. It’s been a few hours since I left it. Have you been in the woods all night?”

“Yeah,” Don said.

Charlie looked at Don’s absolutely messed up t-shirt. Torn and covered in blood. She swallowed. “You look messed up.”

“I’m fine,” Don said. “Most of it’s not mine.”

“O-okay,” Charlie said. She was silent for a while before she spoke. “You’ll take me to the camp, right? You’ll protect me?”

“I will,” Don said. “I would love to get a few hours of sleep at your camp.”

“I’ll make sure you’ll be accepted,” Charlie said, a bit more fiercely now. The shock must have been waning. “You earned that much.”

“How far is the camp?”

The girl looked around. “I’m not sure. A mile or two in that direction, I think.”

Don nodded. “Let’s get going then.”
