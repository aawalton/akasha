Tavian burst through the outer wall of Lord Anarvia’s audience chambers, and stumbled into a side room, choking on dust. His knee popped as he stumbled on a chunk of stone. He gritted his teeth and ran through an open door and across an empty hall.

He wondered what had happened to Braun. Had he made it to the gate, to reinforce Donath?

Ghouls pursued him through the hole. A plan was forming in Tavian’s mind, power surging through him as he wielded the Aegis. The Witherstone was important, but maybe there was another target that he’d overlooked. Maybe he didn’t need reinforcements. He didn’t need to ask anyone else to die for him.

With this shield, he could obliterate the Core all by himself. Then they could worry about the Witherstone and the Karath Amon.

He used the shield again and smashed through the next wall, his arm buzzing with pins and needles from the impact. He dove through the cloud of grit, leaping the pile of rubble, and landed in the audience chamber, breathing hard. The Kingdom Core rose like a nail from the floor in the center of the audience chamber.

Tavian recalled the damage of the explosive bolts that Lord Anarvia had fired at Tavian’s Kingdom Core, how they’d partially drained it of Essence. How he’d felt the blows like someone was striking him.

The chambers fell totally silent for a split second as the dust cleared.

Lord Anarvia was red in the face; he’d clearly been shouting. Men-at-arms froze in the act of running from the room, likely going to obey orders just issued.

Mages who’d been about to extract Essence from valuables ran for cover.

Lord Anarvia shouted. “Get him!”

Hoping he wasn’t committing suicide, Tavian ran toward the Kingdom Core.

Soldiers began pouring in through the entrances of the hall as ghouls boiled out of the hole Tavian had made. Wielding his black dagger and furious aegis, Tavian hacked his way through men-at-arms toward the Kingdom Core. It pulsated gently in the dimly lit hall.

“Where is Halin?” Anarvia bellowed. “I need that stone!”

Lord Anarvia did not have the Witherstone. He was not controlling the ghouls. His men, not realizing the single minded determination of the undead to feed on Tavian’s miasmic flesh, threw themselves against the creatures. They tried to stem the ghoulish tide.

“Stop him! Bring me his head!” Lord Anarvia’s voice boomed over the fray.

Tavian cut through several ranks of soldiers, using Shockwave and the black dagger. Bodies flew through the air. His black blade cut through swords and punctured armor.

He reached the Kingdom Core before Anarvia understood what he was doing.

Tavian blasted men away from the Core Shockwave, then pressed the Aegis against the Core.

Just like smashing through a wall.

Shockwave.

The thunderclap of miasma that erupted from the shield sent men flying back from the Core.

But the Core itself simply flared brightly. It seemed unimpacted by the attack.

Tavian attacked again.

Shockwave.

The shockwave washed around the Kingdom Core, like the tide flowing around a boulder.

If he couldn’t destroy this Kingdom Core, he was going to die. Everyone was going to die. His heart pounded in his chest, sweat dripping from his hands.

He aimed the shield downwards.

Paving stones cracked, and tiles were smashed to dust. Shards flew into the air, but the Kingdom Core remained in place, anchored to the world hexgrid. The Core was as indifferent to the miasma as the mountains to a flea.

Tavian cursed and attacked with his dagger, trying to cut into the gleaming metal. The sharp blade of the miasmic dagger cut into the core, like a soft wood.

A rush of Essence boiled out of the core, pushing his blade away and smoothing over the damage. Lord Anarvia bellowed in pain.

But it was too slow. At this rate, it was going to take him forever to destroy it.

Fighting raged around Tavian as Anarvian soldiers fought through ghouls to form up and defend the Kingdom Core.

Men surged around him, and someone caught his wrist in their fingers. A blade pierced his leg, and a fist caught him in the jaw.

Tavian fell, and bodies piled atop him. Shockwave cleared them away, and Tavian struggled to rise.

Heat and light exploded in the hall. A wave of fire forced Tavian to lie back down among the bodies, hiding beneath the Aegis. He body ached; bruised and bleeding from multiple wounds. Lying among the dead and dying, he fit right in.

Three hulking figures entered the chamber and took the dais. They raised their hands into the air.

Fighting ghouls burst into flames in a wave. They screamed and collapsed to the ground, burnt husks of glowing embers. One of them crumbled across Tavian. He protected his face from the fire with his shield, but his legs were singed by hot flecks of molten bone.

Shimmering flame barriers sealed every entrance to the room. Tavian lay among the bodies, unnoticed. Ghouls outside threw themselves against the burning walls, and fell away scorched and wailing. In the chaos, Tavian pulled a smoldering body over him, hiding as he tried to figure out what to do next.

He needed one more trick.

Ilara strode up onto the dais. In her hands she held two objects; a glittering crystal ball, which Tavian’s Darksight perk told him was likely the Witherstone. In her other hand she held a human head.

Zael. Tavian’s stomach twisted. He’d sent the man to his death.

Ilara dropped Zael’s head on the ground, then held the Witherstone gleefully in the air with both hands. This was the first time he’d seen her face since they fought in his chambers. An angry red line cut through swollen flesh across half her face. Her eye was milky white and sightless.

Muscles spasmed in Tavian’s legs, instinct screaming at him to run.

“Lose this, father?” she asked, holding out the Witherstone. She made a gesture, and ghouls stopped trying to break through the flames.

Behind her, three massive men dragged a beaten and bloodied Halin; Calli’s coresmith master.

The muscular men were bare chested, wearing nothing but a black leather loincloth, and straps across their chests. Golden horned helmets adorned their heads and obscured their faces. Tavian knew them only by reputation.

They were Scarabs, the high tier class holders of the Karath Amon. Though Tavian didn’t know what Tier the kingdom of Karath Amon was, it had to be at least Tier VI. Their fire affinity Kingdom Core lent them powerful flame based magic; Tavian sweated in the heat of the room, and the smell of burning flesh filled his nose, sickening him.

“Thank Ishtoran!” Lord Anarvia exclaimed.

“Better to thank me,” Ilara snapped with a scowl.

Anarvia drew his sword; blade black as the night sky, a twin to Tavian’s own miasmic dagger. He moved toward Halin, swinging the blade as though preparing an execution.

“This is the last time you steal from me,” Lord Anarvia pronounced, raising his sword.

The lead Scarab reached out with an empty hand and grabbed Lord Anarvia’s wrist, twisting it until the man dropped his sword.

“Ilara, what is this?” Anarvia gasped, furious. “Tell him to stop.”

“I’m sorry, father,” Ilara said. “We simply don’t have any use for you.”

Tavian saw the sudden fear that gripped Lord Anarvia’s face. The bitter look of betrayal in his eyes. It was the same haunted look that Tavian had seen in his own father, before he died.

The scarab raised a long whip, flames burning at its tips. Lord Anarvia cringed away. “Let me have my sword! I’ll face any one of you if you give me a fair fight.”

“Leave it off, Father,” Ilara said. “You’ve lost. This is my kingdom now.”

A second scarab dropped the unconscious Halin’s arm and grabbed Lord Anarvia’s other shoulder. “Unhand me,” Anarvia snarled at the Karath Amon.

“You’ll be released,” Ilara said. “As soon as you abdicate. I’m the new ruler here.”

Her eyes glittered in the firelight, one black, one white.

Tavian felt an icy fear grip him. With Ilara in control of the Witherstone, her father’s Kingdom Core, and a force of Karath Amon mercenaries, there was no hope for Tar Corinath.

He shifted his weight, freeing his dagger. Its black blade shone orange and red with reflected flames.

There was still one ability that he hadn’t used yet.

Miasmic infusion would inject miasma into anything he stabbed with hit.

The miasma imbedded in his side reveled at the idea. Tavian’s stomach clenched.

If he did this, he would be condemning many of his and Donath’s men to death. Tavian hesitated. What was a kingdom without subjects?

Tainting the core wouldn’t stop the ghouls; but it should incapacitate Ilara and the Scarabs. It would also incapacitate his whole army.

If Tavian didn’t die outright, perhaps he could collect the Witherstone and take control of the undead before they could slaughter too many of his men.

But still he hesitated. Braun was still out there somewhere.

He rolled onto his hands and knees, leaving the Aegis lying on the ground.

Ilara noticed him moving toward the Kingdom Core.

“Tavian!” she shouted, pointing, eyes wild, face a mask of fury. “Scarabs, kill him!”

The Scarabs moved as one, dropping their prisoners and brandishing their flaming weapons: a flail, a sword, and a staff.

Tavian lunged forward, arm outstretched. The flail cracked, and searing, burning pain ripped across his back and side. The knife punched into the skin of the Kingdom Core. Pure Essence began spraying out as the core tried to defend itself.

Miasmic Infusion.

Miasma burned in Tavian’s veins. A thunderstorm of power surged through him, and flowed into the knife. Black Essence trickled into the Core, absorbed into its heart.

Pure, elemental Essence touched by miasma was instantly transformed. The core changed so quickly it sent a shockwave through the chamber, knocking everyone back. The core’s silvery exterior turned black as obsidian.

For a long, glorious moment, Tavian felt a rush of exultation. He felt the raw miasmic power washing over him, and he longed to give himself over to it. Avi—the miasmically overwhelmed mother of the dungeon—had been right. He really could have power over death.

Then every living person collapsed to the ground. Miasma powering the core did not heed fealty. Within the bounds of Anarvia’s kingdom, everything alive came under direct attack. Plants wilted. Trees collapsed. Flowers died. It was only a matter of time before every living thing was dead.

Ilara crumpled, the Witherstone dropping from her hands and rolling away toward the edge of the dais.

Tavian surged to his feet, wiping blood and sweat from his face. The Ignore Influence ability of the dagger in his hand was the only thing keeping him alive right now.

His system interface told him that the miasma powered core was destroying all life with a nearly twenty influence equivalent. His system pinged with a new message he hadn’t seen before.

⬣ Warning: Mass Casualty Event ⬣

His men were being dragged down to the ground, helpless against the fury of the unaffected ghouls. They were dying. He had to get the Witherstone to stop the massacre.

The miasmic artifact rested on one of the steps up to the dais, reflecting firelight in its crystalline structure.

“Please protect Braun,” Tavian breathed.

The Scarabs groaned on the ground nearby. Before Tavian could move, Lord Anarvia was on them, black sword whistling through the air. With three slashes, he cut through three necks. These Karath Amon would not rise again.

Lord Anarvia’s black blade wove through the air like a snake, and Tavian remembered that he, too, possessed a miasmic sword. It allowed the traitor to ignore the miasmic Influence on his body.

“Well played, Tavian,” Lord Anarvia said, meeting Tavian’s gaze. His body was shifting strangely, pulsating and swelling, as though something moved beneath the surface of his skin. Veins bulged in his neck, coursing with a blackness that moved like an infection across his body.

Tavian saw the ecstasy of power in Lord Anarvia’s face. The man was growing taller and more muscular as Tavian watched. Lord Anarvia’s eyes bulged, and blood trickled from one ear.

“I never thought of powering my core with Miasma,” Lord Anarvia said, rolling his shoulders as his tunic burst at the seams. “I suppose that was just another of the things that Halin kept hidden from me.”

He laughed, a little too giddy to be sane.

Tavian had to get to the Witherstone.

He picked up the infused Aegis from where he’d dropped it near the core, and held his miasmic dagger, pointing it at the traitor.
