Chapter 3

“You-you could have killed me!” Marcus declared, whispering to her through muted breaths as Mr. Haylick began putting the class through calisthenics.

“You would have killed yourself, I would have just been there to witness your ineptitude in action,” Ava replied, making sure to correct Marcus’s words before anyone within earshot heard and over reacted to the comment. It was clear that Marcus was about to complain some more, when Mr. Haylick cut in.

“It’s a good thing your retainer didn’t help you in any way, shape, or form to get to this floating island, for if she did, that would have constituted cheating and forced both of you to get expelled from my class,” Mr. Haylick began. Then he continued, “while we might not lose much with your dismissal from class, having a purger of retainer Silver’s capabilities would be a blow that would matter to me. So, if you are done sulking, please continue to workout while I set up the next project that will hopefully get you all motivated tofor taketaking this training seriously.”

After that comment, Mr. Haylick left the front of the formation, while he went off to procure materials for the rest of class. While he was away, there was the implied notice that so long as the students could remain relatively quiet, they could talk, but Ava knew better. For Mr. Haylick was a mage, and a very powerful onemage if thehis number of tabs awarded to him meant anything. That is why Ava remained quiet, though she did turn her face to see Marcus’s reaction to being shut down mid-complaint. Seeing that he was both annoyed and forcefully biting his lip to remain quiet filled Ava with an oddly perverse sense of joy. Sadly, this was the life of a purger, where she realized she had to take the joy in the smaller moments of life. Ones that were not given freely, but could be gained, if you knew how and where to look at just the right moment.

While Ava kept her mouth shut, the other students who witnessed the entire ordeal were not so generous.

“Oh, man, sad that the teacher’s pet is your pet, eh Aurillius?” Joren Mathison, declared. He was another mage who had a much weaker potential and overall efficiency to Marcus, but he was seen as greater due to his family’s higher standing within the city.  Seeing his antics, it was clear that he was just saying things to get under Marcus’s skin. Unfortunately, it was working as Marcus chose that moment to lash out. Angered by both the walk to class, and then the near-death situation caused by his suddenly forgetting how to fly, then panicking as he fell, Marcus became unhinged.

“She is not my pet,” Marcus hissed.

“That’s right, if she was anyone’s pet, she’d clearly be mine,” Phoebe chimed in, making her position clear. At Phoebe’s comments, Marcus could only look on in shock as it seemed like he lost his favorite puppy. No, it looked like the girl he always wanted, stole his favorite puppy right from under his nose and he didn’t know what to say. Fortunately, his silence was a good thing, for Mr. Haylick chose that exact moment to return with a sack of clinking metallic objects that filled Ava’s mind with an image of a jolly old fat man, delivering metal gifts in unseasonably cold conditions.

“All right pipe down,” Mr. Halick began, before dropping the bulging sack right at his feet.

“Today, I will be demonstrating live mage bombs, this is just a taste of what you can expect to see in the field if you are chosen for a competing team,” Mr. Haylick said. As he spoke, he reached into the bag at his feet and pulled out a faintly glowing disk that seemed to pulse with power.

“Again, this is a watered-down version of what you should feel, and most importantly what you should all be able to field during battle. This device’s sole purpose is to project foreign mana sources directly into your mana core, temporarily causing disruptions with casting, and other issues for you,” Haylick continued, but then paused when he saw Ava’s still floating form, “well most of you anyways.”

With that, Haylick hit the disk at its center activating it. Then with a giant heave, he threw the disk right into the center of the group of clustered students, right at Ava. For her part, Ava’s first instinct was to cover Marcus. That was why she was at the center of the blast radius when it went off, her honed instincts telling her first that she wasn’t in danger, and second that for whatever reason she needed to protect Marcus, and in so doing, also placed her body in a path that shielded Phoebe from the blast.

A rippling wave of force burst out from the disk like an erupting water balloon. Except rather than water, what erupted forth was attuned mana. What Ava expected was a wave of pain, flinching tightly she even braced herself for something to hurt. Yet, what she felt was not the sting of pain entering her body, but rather a tingling sensation that coursed into and through her. She felt it, felt the blast hit her, bounce around her core, and instead of causing lingering pain, it stimulated her in ways she never thought possible.

“Ahhh-ahh!” Mages cried out and collapsed into pain fueled heaps on the ground.

Meanwhile, Ava felt the tingling sensation rise in her to such a degree that she felt the inexplicable need to fan her face with her hands.

“Oh wow, that is hot,” Ava declared.

“Yes, it does feel like a bit of burning magma is running loose within my torso, right now,” one of the male students concurred from beside them. Pausing for a moment, both Phoebe, who had huddled behind Marcus, and Ava were fine. While Marcus seemed a bit dazed,clutching his chest in pain, but was otherwise fine, a remarkable feat given that he was near ground zero of the impact.

“No, it feels amazing,” Ava commented more energetically as she fanned herself and tried to get the jitters that were now coursing through her body gone. Being as she was still the only one flying, and that most of the mages near her had already dropped to the ground, or taken a knee, made Ava’s antics even more visible to Marcus who could only feel embarrassed after watching her display. Then going for the kill, Ava went with the line that she knew would cause Marcus to panic, “I mean my girl parts are—”

“Can you please calm down, you’re embarrassing me,” Marcus interrupted, hissing out the last part between his teeth as if that didn’t draw more attention to his complaint.

“Look, I can count on my hand the number of times I’ve felt this amazing, and one of those times was when I tricked Phoebe into kissing me,” Ava replied pointing her finger at Phoebe who was not two feet away at that point.

“Oh, I wasn’t tricked,” Phoebe replied coquettishly at the offhand comment. Seeing that Phoebe was playing along with the tease, Ava gave a wink and continued.

“Correction, the only time that I have felt better was when Phoebe kissed me,” Ava clarified.

There was a pause as now everyone was listening to the banter, the momentary pain of the mana burst in their cores over, as even the rising surge of endorphins being released in Ava began to dissipate, letting her face go back to its normal porcelain hue.

“Wait, you two kissed?” Was Marcus’s only response to their duo’s banter.

“That is all you heard in that statement,” Ava protested, knowing that she had kissed Marcus once too, but he didn’t even ask where his ranking fell. Only seeming to note that Phoebe’s rank was higher.

“I mean, I heard a lot, especially about your girl—” Marcus began but then cut himself off as he waved a hand in Ava’s general direction. Before anything else could be said, Mr. Haylick came back and proceeded to add some order to the chaos of the classroom.

“Again, that was a mage bomb. Many of you will likely feel pain for a few hours, if the pain continues into tomorrow, realize that you are not strengthening your cores at night like you’re supposed to. If you had a fully developed core, this wouldn’t bother you,” Mr. Haylick began speaking, as he did he would make sure the students all fell into line. Almost every mage that prided themselves on flying, had to stay grounded for the moment, that was when he saw that Ava was the only flyer still in the air, and that she seemed to positively beam with energy.

“Also, I guess this is a good time to point out the fact that having a purger on your team is great for this exact reason. Not only are they not bothered by foreign mana thrust into their centers, it is often described as a euphoric sensation to them, as you can tell,” Haylick continued while gesturing towards Ava.

“Now then, you can expect these mage bombs every third day of class,” Mr. Haylick said. As he did an audible groan was let out by the mages who all realized they would have to put in a lot of work if they wanted to succeed.

At that point, Ava raised her hand with a question.

“Yes, Silvers?”

“Can we do it daily?”

That’s when the class really started to groan in discontent, to which Haylick responded with, “if only we could. Unfortunately, regulations limit how much we can do.”

“Stupid regulations,” Ava hissed out as she snapped into position to begin the next phase of her training.

“Amen,” Mr. Haylick declared as he took off his cap and made a religious symbol while touching his forehead in a prayer. Seeing the reaction, it was hard to tell if the wiry teacher was poking fun at the comment or playing it up for laughs. Unfortunately, no one could tell, which is why there was an odd silence that settled over the group, before Mr. Haylick began with his next set of instructions.

“Okay, so since this is your first day of having real condensed mana flowing through you, thanks to yours truly,” Mr. Haylick said as he pointed to himself, “that means that your ambient energy ratings will be strong enough to finally register with our charts, letting you all enter our training fields, where you can get your first evaluation,”

“Then once you get your evaluation, you will be taken to our training center, where you will get a one-time chance to prove yourself to the great system,” My Haylick concluded, while holding up his hands and gesturing for the kids to keep their volumes down, despite no one making any noise or cheering at all. “Keep it down, don’t worry. This is nothing special. In fact, it is just a standard process that everyone gets when they first go into these evaluations.”

“Um, Mr. Haylick, what exactly is the great system?” Frida Koika asked, her hand shooting up at the same time she began asking her question.

“Good question, the honest answer is no one knows. Some people will say it is how the gods grant us power. Others will say some profound rhetoric that ultimately equates to they don’t know,” Mr. Haylick began, then with a semi-defeated sigh, he added, “honestly, I wish I had more for you. The textbook definition that I am supposed to tell you is that it is a calling used to purge the strong from the weak, and then the brave from the stupid, then to pit the stupid against each other in a form of intergalactic competitions that we can use to gain resources and improve humanity’s lot in life.”

“Wait, what?” Joren asked, not even daring to hold up his hand and ask.

“Hey, look, I could tell you a lot. Like your entire life’s trajectory could be solved by doing well on these tests. That allegedly, you can unlock Gradings, which will make you unfathomably powerful,” Mr. Haylick began.

“Gradings?” Poe Langston asked, the second boy in a row to not even raise their hands while blurting out a question.

“Can no one raise their hands? Fine, since everyone has forgotten that they have hands, let’s do this the democratic way. Everyone drop into the front leaning rest position,” Mr. Haylick demanded, then as one everyone dropped to the ground, their arms shoulder width apart as they got into a pushup position. “From here on out, I will answer any and all questions you have. And don’t worry, I won’t get mad if you don’t raise your hands. So begin pushing, and I will tell you a story of how ancient powers one day appeared, along with foreign structures that were of course explored, and that exploration ultimately led us to new civilizations that we then were forced to compete against for resources.”

As he spoke, the class began doing pushups, while many had already been close to exhaustion before this, they didn’t argue and instead listened with unenthusiastic grimaces as Mr. Haylick decided to turn this first question into a minor history lesson, before ultimately answering the question that started this whole process.

“Yet, there are grace periods that each person of each society is given. From all tests we can tell, you have to be at least eighteen years old to enter the trials,” Mr. Haylick continued his lecture while waving his hand around at all the kids who were still struggling to do pushups. Well most, as a few of the brighter students were using their elements to help give them strength, or using the second stage of Telekinesis as Ava was doing to make their bodies lighter as they went up and down on the ground. Mr. Haylick was okay with the use of powers as that was only going to help them in the long run. “Then once you enter, a timer appears allowing you to compete in these trials over and over again until you get a perfect run, or until your time runs out.”

“What is a perfect run?”  Frida asked, her words coming at the cost of a chorus of groans.

“Glad you asked. See, to understand what a perfect run is, we must first tell you the sad truth that after all these hundreds of years of humans entering these trials and competing, no one knows what actually constitutes a perfect run. We can give you guesses, as allegedly there is a dragon on the final stage, and if you beat it within the year, you get a Grading, in addition to your Attributes.”

“But what are Gradings? You never said,” Ava asked, looking up, her eyes bright with excitement as this was finally getting interesting. Again, most of the other students continued to groan at yet another question that would continue this pain, but Ava didn’t care, with her Telekinesis at work, raising and lowering her body was easy.

“As far as we can tell Gradings are multipliers for your base Attributes. Attributes have numbers that go up, while Gradings have letter designations that go down, starting with F and going down to A, and allegedly S, then double S, and finally triple S. But again most of this is lore that has been recycled back to us. All we do know is that if you have a champion with a Grading on your combat team, you are more likely to do well in the universal games that give out prizes and help set the hierarchy of the universe as we know it,” Mr. Haylick replied, giving a long-winded answer. “Now are there any other questions?”

“Oh—,” Ava began, but was quickly cut off by the violent hisses coming from the other students.

“Come on, and let us just get evaluated,” Joren hissed, his whole body was trembling, despite using his Air affinity to hold his body in place. A quick glance over told Ava that Joren was already burning through his optimal range and beginning to get into the realm of corruption build up. Not too bad, and something that would either need to be purged, or left alone to get excreted naturally by his mage system. The only problem was, he knew that having a clean core was likely needed to pass these trials. At least that was the given assumption, which was why he wanted to rest now before the real tests began.

“Fine, no more questions,” Ava replied, but then hissed out to Joren “you two started it, by not raising your hands.”

While her comment was accurate, it only seemed to infuriate Joren more, despite her being correct.

“All right, recover,” Mr. Haylick commented. At that statement Ava jumped straight up, as if the entire exercise had been relatively easy. Considering she cheated the whole way by using her abilities to assist her, it was no wonder, but Mr. Haylick knew that this was likely better as getting that level of control over her power was exceptionally important in any contestant’s development, which is why he turned a blind eye to such uses of power. If they used their arms, or their minds, it didn’t matter, as they were ultimately improving themselves, and that was all Mr. Haylick wanted from each student at the end of the day.

“Now I will tell you the most important thing before entering,” Mr. Haylick began while gesturing for each student to line up before entering the glowing rift that Mr. Haylick was powering up with his own mana that was fed into tubes and relays that ultimately sparked to life ancient runes and alien tech that split the fabric of reality apart by allowing space to fold between two fixed locations. Seeing the act in process, everyone let out a collective awe, as they could feel the immense power being used. Power that showed what humanity was capable of. Well capable of, when they used alien technology. “Remember, that when you enter this realm, you will first enter a spiritual realm where your body and mind will be tested and split. Then your consciousness will be pushed to the trial realm where you can compete against the challenges before you. Know that when you enter the trial realms, despite how real it is, you cannot actually die in that realm, despite how painful the process is, you will recover and you will be able to try again.”

“For a year?” Ava asked, raising her hand from the back of the line, as that was her place as a second-caste citizen of the human alliance.

“That is correct, for a year. Once you die, you must take a twenty-four-hour break between your next competition. Then you can try again, meaning you can at most try 365 times, theoretically. That said, try your hardest, and realize that this is your first step to being recognized individually by the universe,” with that Mr. Haylick lowered his hand and let the students enter the rift in space.

One by one the students entered, only to disappear on the other side. To make the process seem even more disturbing was the fact that once you left the platform, the students just disappeared. Their bodies not appearing on the other side, as the other side still seemed like it was an unconquered land of monsters, volcanos, and flying monsters in the background. Just seeing the land, Ava felt a thrill of excitement and wonder fill her as she got closer and closer. No student wanted to leave, as they didn’t want to look weak in front of their peers. Even the normally reluctant ones looked around, fear evident in their eyes, before they entered the rift, not saying a word.

Marcus for his part stayed next to Ava. If pressed, he would say it would only make sense for him to be near Ava, but part of him just needed to see Ava to feel confident. With Marcus clearly feeling the pressure, Ava only smiled wider, trying to help put Marcus at ease.

“You’ve got this, besides you can’t die,” Ava stated, trying to cheer Marcus up.

“I know,” Marcus replied, and he knew it too, but that still didn’t help him work through his anxious jitters that were beginning to express themselves. The way his fingers twitched unnaturally, the way he would tighten his feet, causing a bulge to appear in his boots. Everything showed that he was becoming nervous. Fortunately, the line was moving too quickly for him to be caught by anyone else entering the rift, as each student entered and then disappeared from in front of the portal almost instantaneously. Phoebe who had also gone to the back of the line was the first of the trio to go, then Marcus who Ava was prepared to push through the rift if he hesitated for even a second. Fortunately for Marcus, he went on his own. Then it was just Ava and Mr. Haylick.

“Good luck,” Mr. Haylick said as Ava prepared to go.

“Luck is for the weak,” Ava declared, before stepping through the rift in space. Instantly, Ava felt her body being stretched and pulled apart as she could feel the individual strands and molecules of her entire being get manipulated by powers that were beyond her comprehension. Suddenly she felt that maybe dissing the notion of luck was not her best move of the day. A fact that was proven rather quickly by what happened next.
