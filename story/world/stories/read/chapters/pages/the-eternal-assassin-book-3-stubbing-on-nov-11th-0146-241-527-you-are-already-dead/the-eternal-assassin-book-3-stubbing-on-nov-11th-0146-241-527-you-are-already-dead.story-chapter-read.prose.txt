Asher was blasted backward from the unexpected detonation as his front was impacted by what felt like a few hundred pounds of solid rock, and his back crashed against the surprisingly solid wall of the Guild cell. Coughing out blood, he managed to slow the world to a crawl as he activated Comprehend’s second-tier effect.

First off… Ow! Who the hell had a self-destruct style skill?! He couldn’t shake his head while his mind was overclocked like this, but he was able to see the scattered pieces of Rubble’s body already beginning to come back together. A defensive skill that functions like Moxy’s Air Nova, but with a hell of a more powerful punch… No wonder he didn’t seem all that worried about me getting the drop on him.

Asher was pretty certain it was only thanks to his newly enhanced strength that he hadn’t sustained even worse injuries than he had, but that didn’t mean he’d gotten away without a scratch. He definitely had a solid number of broken ribs, and from the blood he’d just coughed up, he was all but certain at least one of which had punctured his lungs. His head felt fuzzy, which was a tell-tale sign of a concussion, and he was pretty certain he was missing a tooth or two. Though with his tongue frozen like everything else, he couldn’t confirm that just yet. Luckily, Complete Resilience would do wonders for fighting back against even a severe concussion, but it had been a while since a regular senior assassin had dealt him so much damage all at once.

Exactly why I wanted this practice again, he thought as the world sped back up to normal and he finished sliding down the wall he’d crashed into. Keeps me from getting overconfident.

Confirming he’d actually had three of his teeth knocked out from getting a face-full of rock, Asher laughed as he watched Rubble finish reforming in only a few seconds. The man’s chipped and crumbly body snapped back into place one rock chunk at a time, and Asher had to admit it was a pretty impressive sight.

“Still standing, huh?” Rubble asked as soon as he had a head again, tilting it as he pointed his large maul at Asher’s chest. “This would be a lot less painful if you just gave up and went down.”

“Where would be the fun in that?” Asher grinned, proud of himself for not having dropped either of his daggers even in the surprise explosion. “Would you believe you’re the first person I’ve fought against capable of blowing themselves up? What kind of crazy skill even is that?”

“One that brought me no shortage of young assassins begging me to be their mentor,” Rubble said, frowning as he gestured around the room at the various corpses. His skill had somehow managed to avoid hitting a single one of them, which showed he actually had quite the impressive control over it. “You’ll pay for what you’ve done here. I’m going to make this slow.”

“Fine by me! I need the practice,” Asher laughed, his mind whirling with the best way to take Rubble down.

With that, Rubble attacked again, going straight for offense as he tried to crush Asher’s chest inward with a smash from his giant maul. Asher forced the attack to fall short with Distortion, experimenting as he used Spatial Lock to trap the two of them together. Though rather than assign the barrier any rules, he blocked nothing more than sound to start.

Slipping into the astral, Asher dodged another powerful blow, darting through Rubble and over toward the side of his Spatial Lock. Returning to the material realm, he expanded Crescent into the length of a longsword and swung with all his might at Rubble’s neck once more.

To his surprise, Rubble somehow seemed to actually understand what he was trying to do. Rather than letting the attack almost land and activating self-destruct once again, the senior assassin actually dodged the attack by collapsing his legs into a pile of rubble, dropping under Asher’s swing as his height was halved. In that same moment, the rubble that had made up his legs began swirling around him like a few dozen asteroids orbiting a planet, forcing Asher to frown and slip back into the astral.

Well damn, this guy actually has half a brain. Asher had wanted to try and get him to explode again and then change up his Spatial Lock to prevent rock from reentering the space after he was only half formed. But it seemed Rubble really was a talented assassin, as he’d figured out Asher’s intentions almost instantly. As Asher stood there within the astral, watching the chunks of rubble soar through his body again and again as they orbited his opponent, he contemplated.

Sure wish I had that Void dagger right about now… I bet that would eat through solid rock like nobody’s business. Although, maybe daggers just aren’t the answer here.

As both an avid dagger collector and enthusiast, even just the thought pained Asher a bit. But the fact of the matter was that a blade really wasn’t the best choice when fighting a being made of crumbled rock. Resigning himself to what had to be done, Asher stepped further from his opponent, letting down Spatial Lock and returning to the material realm.

“You’re making me do something I really don’t want to do,” Asher admitted, putting his daggers away and pulling out his old pickaxe from when he, Samantha, and Brian had gone mining in this very city. The pickaxe had only cost him a few shards, and it was really barely more than a bent chunk of iron slapped on the end of a thick stick. But that was all he needed it to be.

“You brought this upon yourself,” Rubble said, narrowing his eyes as the rocks orbiting him spread out farther, quickly filling up the whole room. The larger chunks that probably would have knocked out another tooth or cracked his skull upon impact broke apart as well, filling the room with hundreds of pieces of rubble, all orbiting the man at speed. Each one didn’t have enough force to be particularly deadly, but that wasn’t the problem.

Asher couldn’t utilize Astral Dip very well when he might erase a few vital chunks of his body reappearing in the gravel storm.

Still, this wasn’t the first opponent he’d fought with similar tactics, and he grinned as he snapped Spatial Lock into place once more. The moment the bubble formed, he focused with Astral Dip’s second-tier effect, yanking most of the room directly into the astral realm. Doing so cost a sizable chunk of his energy, but that was nothing compared to what it would cost to keep so many gravel shards flying about at once.

Just as he’d thought, Rubble flinched back at the sudden change in the world outside their bubble, before cancelling his skill. The flying rocks shot back into his body, reforming his legs and pushing him back up to his full height.

“Your skills are as weird as they say,” Rubble said, breathing heavily from the energy expenditure. “Still, you are only a man. And no man will ever match the power of simple stone.”

“You sure?” Asher asked, raising the pickaxe above his head and running straight at his opponent, making his intentions clear. Even if he couldn’t utilize Distortion while within the astral, that didn’t mean he couldn’t get in close to his target the old-fashioned way.

Rubble raised his maul to block, which was exactly what Asher wanted. Bringing his pickaxe down, Asher released the handle at the last moment, instead summoning Samantha’s old pickaxe directly into his hands once they’d moved past the man’s heavy maul. Rubble blinked as the first pickaxe was effortlessly blocked without any actual weight behind it, before the second pickaxe that popped into existence past his guard smashed into his head, blasting a good third of the rubble away from his skull. The assassin did manage to retaliate with a stomp that shattered the bones of Asher’s foot, but the damage was done.

Dropping Spatial Lock and bringing them back to the material realm, Asher laughed as he hobbled backwards with Distortion, grinning despite the pain flaring in his demolished foot. Pulling his opponents into the astral with him was always a massive risk, as both his two methods of dodging attacks were effectively nullified. But it was worth it to counter large-scale area attacks like the gravel storm.

Rubble finally seemed to notice something was wrong as the rocks making up a third of his head failed to zip back up and reform. Lowering his maul, he pressed a hand against the side of his missing face, the man’s one remaining eye going wide as he realized what that meant.

“Oh yeah, that’s not going to fix itself,” Asher smiled, spitting out another, much smaller mouthful of blood than before. His Eternal Regeneration was working overtime, already fixing not only his damaged lungs, but his broken teeth as well. “How long can you keep that rock-body skill going for? Another minute? Two? I imagine it’s got to be a weird feeling knowing that as soon as you drop that skill, you’ll return to a body of flesh and blood, with a third of your head missing.”

Asher had actually been aiming to hit the assassin’s head dead-on with his pickaxe, but summoning a weapon directly into his hands mid-attack was no easy feat. In fact, it was a small miracle he’d managed to land a glancing blow at all. It wasn’t a technique he’d really practiced, something he resolved to try and work on in the future with his team.

“You’re a monster,” Rubble hissed. Asher could clearly hear the hint of fear in the man’s voice as he charged forward once again, roaring with his maul over his head. Seeing as Asher currently had a shattered foot and wasn’t really able to dodge, he did the most logical thing for a man in his current situation.

He waved, only to slip into the astral at the very last moment and allow the attack to phase straight through him.

Rubble cursed and shouted, turning and waiting for him to reappear. The assassin’s maul was raised high over what remained of his head as he looked for something to strike at. But as the seconds passed and nothing happened, Rubble gradually realized what was going to happen.

Asher had absolutely no intention of coming back to the material realm anytime soon. And the moment Rubble’s transformation skill wore off…

Breathing heavily despite his lack of lungs, Rubble dropped to his knees, shakily scrounging around for fallen chunks of rubble and scooping them up into a pile. Desperately, he grabbed handfuls of rock, doing his best to shove them against the side of his head, using every ounce of his impressive strength to press them together. But try as he might, the moment he took his hand away, the added chunks simply fell away, leaving him with a third of his head missing every time. Asher watched this entire display silently from within the astral, counting down the seconds as he waited for the man’s skill to wear off. The final blow had already been dealt.

The man’s body simply didn’t know it yet.

Rubble spent another few seconds attempting to patch himself up, before he eventually ran out of energy. To the older assassin’s credit, he didn’t beg, or cry out, or even scream at Asher to come out from wherever he was hiding. It seemed after a lifetime of claiming innocent lives, something came over him at the last moment. As though he understood that it was now his time to join all those who had fallen at his hand. Gravel began morphing back into human flesh, and Rubble took one long, final breath. Taking in air by magical rock, and letting it out via human lungs. Then his transformation skill wore off completely, and he fell over as blood began spilling out of his head in earnest.

Returning to the material realm now that the assassin had succumbed to his wounds, Asher nodded down at his fallen opponent out of respect for his last few seconds. For someone who didn’t even have all three elements, Rubble had dealt him a rather impressive series of blows, reminding him that going up against ‘weaker’ opponents was never a bad idea when it came to keeping himself sharp.

Grabbing the man’s shards, Asher plopped himself down on the ground, careful not to sit in the steadily growing pool of blood. He had a good handful more assassins on his list to take care of today, but first he needed to let Eternal Regeneration do its thing.

He wanted to be at his best when he took down yet another one of the Head’s Fingers.
