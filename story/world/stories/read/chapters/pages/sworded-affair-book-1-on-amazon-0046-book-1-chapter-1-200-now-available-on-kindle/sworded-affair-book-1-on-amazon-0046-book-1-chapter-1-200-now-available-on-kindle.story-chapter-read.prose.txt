Blurb:

Cleaning antiques was supposed to be a punishment, but when monsters arrived, it saved Emma’s life.

Trapped inside an ancient suit of armor, Emma Knight awakens bound to a relic of death that answers only to her will. The Earth is dying, dungeons spread like infection, and what’s left of humanity is turning on itself.

To survive, Emma must fight through the ruins, master her newfound power, and uncover the truth behind the apocalypse, all while navigating the breakdown of society.
