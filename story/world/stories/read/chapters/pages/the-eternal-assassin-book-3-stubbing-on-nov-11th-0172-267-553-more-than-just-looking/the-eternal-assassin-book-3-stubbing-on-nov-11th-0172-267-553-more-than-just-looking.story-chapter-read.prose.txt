Asher leapt out of his chair, searching with wild eyes for the intruder that dared to break into his personal dimension. He’d never experienced something forcing its way into his rift, but there was no mistaking the unpleasant sensation. It was like someone reaching into his back pocket to pull out his wallet, putting something inside, and then closing it and slipping it back into his pocket. Even if he hadn’t seen what had slipped inside, it was his skill, and he always knew what was contained within his Personal Rift.

Until now.

“Where is it?!” he shouted, turning every which way in a futile attempt to find the invader. Whatever it was, it was masking itself somehow. All Asher could sense was that some sort of large, oval of blank space had entered his rift, but he couldn’t get a read on its exact location. His rift was laid out like a large, rectangular warehouse so long as he was in it, so there really shouldn’t have been anywhere to hide. But try as he might, he couldn’t find them.

“Oh hey, it’s Watcher!” Smiles grinned, looking over his shoulder. “I didn’t know she could do that!”

Asher spun around, staring at the blank wall only a few dozen feet away. Other than the random boxes of outfits and tools he’d taken from the Assassin’s Guild safehouse in Whikoga a while back, there was nothing to be seen.

“Wow, she moves fast,” Smiles laughed. “Hey, are those things import—”

There was a loud shattering of glass, and Asher spun back around again, staring at the remains of the potions he’d purchased from Polly. All of his own drinkable potions and the potions he’d been holding on for his team were now mixing together in a puddle of glass shards, the shatter-proof glass not quite living up to its name. The more fragile splash potions had been left untouched, as they’d been stored in a different container, but it was probably only a matter of time before they were next.

“Get out of my rift!” Asher shouted, growing increasingly frustrated that he couldn’t find Watcher. “Smiles, how are you seeing her?!”

“It’s not like she’s invisible, she’s just always behind you,” he explained. “It’s honestly pretty cool!”

Growling, Asher triggered Comprehend’s second-tier effect, buying himself time to think. Did she spatially lock herself to the back of my head?! I already know that’s something she can do, seeing as no matter how much I ran she always remained the same distance away. But if that’s the case, how the hell do I fight her? How do you stab something that’s always behind you and out of reach?

If she got close enough, he could throw a Spatial Lock around her, and then stabbing her would be the easiest thing in the world. Barring that, his only other thought was the original idea Samantha had come up with, which involved blinding the eye-covered undead with a splash potion of Flare. He either needed to grab it for himself and chuck it blindly over his shoulder, or just wait for the undead to get to the container of splash potions and destroy it. She’d blind herself without him having to do a damn thing, and that would be his chance.

Time sped back up and Asher winced at the sound of crunching rock. Another spin around, and he discovered she’d busted the glowing gemstones inlaid in the mining helmets he, Samantha, and Brian had worn when mining in Dormaul’s fifth level.

“Man, she’s really trashing the place,” Smiles said, having no trouble in the slightest following the undead’s movements. Other than when she appeared behind him, seeing as he had no neck or body to turn. “Wow, never seen her pick up a sword before!”

That was all the warning Asher got before something hard and cold stabbed him in the back. Thanks to Smiles, he’d managed to twist around at the last second, turning what would have been a stab through the heart into a mildly less deadly stab through the lung. Coughing up blood, Asher looked down, blinking at the realization she’d stabbed him with Smiles’ own soulsteel blade he’d taken from the undead earlier.

Anything I have contained within my rift is a potential weapon for her to use…

Having been stabbed directly into his back, Asher couldn’t really reach around for the handle and yank the blade out of him. Instead, he did exactly what he’d done the first time this sword had stabbed him in the back, using Distortion’s second-tier effect to shrink the blade and let it clatter to the ground. Before he could throw up a Spatial Lock to protect himself now that he had access to his tier-one skills again, there was another shatter of glass directly behind him, and his eyes widened at the cloud of glowing blue particles that shot past his head and covered his back.

“You bastard!” Asher shouted, immediately using Complete Resilience’s purge effect to cleanse his final vial of magadrine powder before it claimed his life. Not only was Watcher making him waste precious energy he needed to take down the King of Death, that was the last of the vials he’d taken from Eight’s lab. He’d been saving it in the event it was ever needed to take down a particularly durable enemy, but now, it was gone.

Complete Resilience - Lvl 38

Finally getting up his Spatial Lock, Asher winced as something shot into the back of it, claiming another sliver of energy from his core. The strikes weren’t all that powerful, but he couldn’t exactly stand there and tank them forever.

“Smiles, how is she attacking me?!”

“It’s really weird to watch, she’s not actually touching anything!” the severed head called out. “Your stuff is floating up all by itself, before being thrown at you! She never gets close!”

Some sort of telekinesis with her Spatial powers, Asher growled, flinching at another impact against his Spatial Lock. Turning, he spotted his prized heavy boar tusk now lying on the ground beside his bubble. For whatever reason, that made him even angrier than her destroying his last vial of magadrine powder. That single tusk had saved his life on two separate occasions! How dare she touch it?!

Realizing there was one thing he could try, Asher waited for another impact against his Spatial Lock before sprinting to the nearest edge of his Personal Rift. Throwing himself at it, he turned and pressed his back flat against the strange wall, his eyes searching for the undead that had dared to intrude into his own pocket dimension.

Sure enough, unable to actually exist behind him anymore, Watcher was quickly spotted standing over by some of the camping and hunting gear Samantha had picked up for their travels across the kingdom, back before she’d obtained her Wing element. Asher watched as, without moving a muscle, Watcher levitated one of Samantha’s nice skinning knives, before hurling it across the room at him.

Curiously, the projectile wasn’t actually all that fast, and Asher easily managed to dodge it just by hopping to the left. Weaver then picked up another knife, throwing that one just as slowly. It wasn’t until she levitated an arrow from one of his many barrels and shot it at him with far less force than a regular bow would have before he confirmed her weakness.

“She can move stuff around, but she doesn’t have a ton of force!” Asher called out to Smiles, still trying to come up with a plan. He was safe from further attacks like this, but he couldn’t exactly strike back against the undead at the other end of his rift.

“That makes sense! I told you, she’s not a fighter!” Smiles said. “Why not just leave your skill in the first place? Wouldn’t she be trapped in here?”

“If she can get in on her own, I’m sure she can get out as well,” Asher said, dodging the rusty suit of armor that Rulfar had picked up from Zygrul’s base back in the mountains as it clattered to the ground. “And even if the King of Death knows I’m here, he shouldn’t know I’m getting help from you, which is the only advantage I have right now. If she gets out and runs, she’ll probably tell him! Not to mention I don’t want to give this thing free rein inside my skill! What if she figures out a way to damage it for good?!”

“You really think she could do that?” Smiles asked, whistling at the deconstructed tent hurled Asher’s way.

“I would have said no a few minutes ago, but I would have said the same thing about someone else entering my rift without my permission!”

At this point, the interior of Asher’s Personal Rift was a mess. It had already been cluttered as he didn’t exactly organize the stuff he placed within it, but at least it had been neatly cluttered. Now, weapons were lying scattered around, glass and gemstone shards were all over the place, and Watcher showed no sign of stopping her one-woman rampage through everything he owned. He desperately needed her to smash the container holding their splash potion of Flare, but she seemed far more interested in hurling random weapons at him. Though now that he thought about it…

Could it really be that easy?

“Smiles!” Asher shouted, doing his best to look concerned as he dodged a hurled weight that he kept in here for Moxy during their long flights. “I know you’re just a head, but can you move at all? While I keep her distracted, I need you to get to that small box over there and guard it with your undead life! It’s the best shot we have of actually taking down the King of Death! Bite her, spit on her, I don’t care, just don’t let her destroy it!”

“I don’t know how to break this to you, but at the moment, I have all the mobility of a severed head,” Smiles said, following his pointing finger with his eyes. “Ah, and she’s already gotten it anyway!”

Immediately after shouting at Smiles for help, Watcher took the bait. The box containing two different splash potions of Flare, one for him and one for Samantha, rose into the air like it was part of a magic trick. Watcher must have been concerned he’d try and catch it if she hurled it at him, as rather than fly across the room, the box rose up to about chest height.

Before being hurled at the ground between them and shattering.

Knowing what was about to happen, Asher was the only one to cover his eyes when the box broke open and two utterly blinding explosions of light blasted out. Even with his eyes squeezed shut and pressed into his arm, he still saw some shreds of light slip through, followed immediately after by two different shouts of surprise. With a grin, Asher opened his eyes, not hesitating for a moment as he sprinted toward Watcher.

“Why were you keeping the sun in that tiny box?!” Smiles shouted, frowning as he kept his eyes squeezed shut. “I imagine that would have been incredibly painful if I were alive! Even so, I can’t see anything!”

“That’s kinda the point!” Asher said as he ran past, never taking his eyes off the intruding undead. Unlike Smiles, Watcher looked to be in something resembling actual pain. The dozens of eyes covering her exposed arms were all blinking rapidly, looking every which way as if trying to figure out what was going on as her body recoiled and flailed about blindly. He had no idea how long such an attack would work against an undead, and he wasn’t planning to find out. Before she could recover even a little, he reached her, slashing out with an elongated dagger as he lopped her head clean off. As it fell through the air, he cut it in half, and then slashed at it again, not taking any chances with the undead capable of Spatial movement.

It wasn’t until the four pieces of Watcher’s head splattered across the floor of his Personal Rift that he let himself relax, grinning at Smiles continued complaints as he looked down at the eye-covered undead. At this point, he’d taken out all of the Servants that Smiles expected to come after him, Weaver, and now even the seemingly untouchable Watcher.

All that was left was the King of Death.
