The Greater System Weave was not a fan of Smudge. It was as if the companion had a dark aura that ate into the threads surrounding it, and the weave pulled its threads away from the voidling. It reminded Luke of a plant reaching for sunlight, but in reverse. Unsure how to deal with the issue, his first instinct was to retreat back to the physical realm, but after a moment of consideration, he ruled that out. While the effect wouldn't be as bad, he guessed, it wouldn't stop either. The different realms were one and the same, just different representations. Even if Smudge perhaps wouldn't cause entropy, like buildings crumbling around him, the threads of said building would deteriorate. Given enough time, the effect would be the same. So, the problem needed to be solved right away.

"But how are we supposed to do that?" Luke asked the little voidling.

Exhausted as he was, Luke still managed to extend a thread of mana toward Smudge and reach close enough to feel the effects firsthand. The thread wavered and withered once it got close enough, but he realized that it held out far better than the threads of The Greater System Weave. Interesting.

Continuing down that avenue of testing, Luke brought forth more threads, extending them to wrap around his companion. The threads bore the brunt of the void damage, but shielded the weave, leaving it intact. That was it! Using his own mana, he could shield his friend and keep it from destroying everything, perhaps even the weave itself. With that, the next problem reared its ugly head. How the hell was he supposed to keep Smudge wrapped up in threads all day, every day? Doing so would mean having to stay right next to the voidling at all times, and it would require that he use almost all his mana for that one task. Unsustainable was putting it lightly. No, he needed a different answer. Pondering the question at hand, Luke perused his skills.

Heartthread [Active]: Bind your weave to another.

Once, when Smudge had just hatched, Luke had attempted to use a thread of mana to peer into the voidling. It was an attempt to better learn what he was dealing with at the time, but the attempt had been like staring into the void itself. Even if he'd glimpsed some of Smudge's insides, for lack of a better word, it had been too foreign for his limited understanding at the time. The thread had also collapsed in a matter of seconds, unable to hold itself together in that inhospitable environment. That was what Heartthread would help with, he hoped. Binding himself to Smudge would ease that connection. That was the idea, at least. Combining that, the insight into Smudge, one more skill might just be the ticket out of this bind.

With the plan firm in his mind, Luke got to work. First, he used the new skill. It slotted into place in an instant with no issue whatsoever, at least no apparent one in that first moment. From what he could tell at first glance, the skill added a thread between the two of them. It was thicker than a normal thread, and there was something about the energy that made him think that it wasn't just permanent, but also that severing it would come with some serious consequences. Delving into the heart thread was quicker and easier than anything Luke had experienced in quite a while, almost as if the connection yearned for threads of mana to flow through it. The thing was a thread of mana on its own, so 'through' wasn't quite the right word, but it would have to do for the moment. At the halfway point, Luke hesitated. There was that alien, unfamiliar nothingness of the void again. It was part of Smudge, and Smudge was part of it, and there was no separating the two. How Relian had accomplished the creation of the egg that birthed Smudge was a mystery, but it was not one that needed solving. Instead, Luke continued down the heart thread. It didn't just connect their bodies. No, that would have been too simple. The heart thread ran from Luke's Meta-Heart into Smudge's... whatever you wanted to call that.

A monster core differed from a human's Meta-Heart in enough ways that there was no mistaking one for the other. The core worked like a battery, powering the monster, while the Meta-Heart could be described more as part of the human. An infernal core leaned more toward what a monster carried around in its chest, and whatever Lemira had was like a mix of the two: A Meta-Heart brimming with infernal mana. Smudge's was nothing like any of those Luke had experienced so far. The mere act of extending his thread toward the inside of Smudge felt like a dangerous one. As he'd hoped, the heart thread allowed him to do so without the thread being destroyed, but what he saw was... disquieting. Inside Smudge was another void, or the same void, but separate. Wrapping his head around all these strange concepts was tiring. Getting back to the physical realm after this would be a relief, but first he had to try his idea, even if he thought the chances of it working weren't good, not after he had seen the nothingness that made up the little voidling. The core of Smudge was like a concentrated little ball of immense lack of everything, like a miniature representation of the void itself. How the heart thread even connected to it was a question without an answer, but Luke realized it gave him something to build on.

That was it. Maybe this could work. Focusing on the void core was dizzying, as if he was staring down from a great height, feeling as if the ground itself would drop away from under him, or like dreaming you were falling. Trying to keep focus as best he could, Luke got to work. The core itself emanated what Luke decided to call void energy. That was the problem. That energy ran through Smudge like mana through Luke, although the channels were not as defined or tangible, but the glow was almost as if the core itself bled void from Smudge's body, affecting everything around him.

"This isn't going to hurt," Luke whispered to his friend, hoping it was true. Smudge accepted the sending of emotions through the weave with a metaphysical waving of his tail, like a good boy.

Guardian Weave [Active]: Shield structures within yourself or another.

Guardian Weave. That was the solution he had found, the one thing to try. Using it to shore up the bleeding from the void core was the one thing he could think of. It acted as a shield, protecting the metaphysical organ within it. The idea was that it might also protect whatever was outside, that the barrier worked both ways. Luke’s instinct that something inside the voidling was responsible for the effect on the weave had been correct. So far, so good.

Without the heart thread, Luke wouldn't have been able to reach inside Smudge, and, he'd now found out, even if he did, he wouldn't have been able to build the guardian weave, since there was nothing to anchor it to. With the heart thread, they had surmounted that problem. Now to see if it would work. Gathering up all the willpower that remained within him, stoking it with pure focus, so much of it that he would be almost comatose after the attempt, Luke began weaving threads around Smudge's void core. Careful not to cut off the void channels, he looped several layers of thread in a loose pattern around the almost perfect sphere. His throat was dry, even if he didn't have one, and sweat ran down his face, despite sweat not existing in the metaphysical realm. Directing the threads, Luke's fingers trembled, fingers he didn't have. It was all very confusing. Once he was satisfied, having layered the core in three guardian weaves, Luke heaved, tightening the weaves in an instant. Smudge let out a low whimper, but seemed unhurt and unaffected, so he finished up by anchoring the guardian weaves to the heart thread just before his concentration wavered and the weave started spinning around him.

Luke felt warm and dizzy, and like he was bleeding all over. The Greater System Weave blurred and darkened. First, a thought full of horror struck him. What if he'd failed? What if it was Smudge's void core that was now destroying the weave itself? But no, he realized. It was just his consciousness fading into nothing. Not the nothingness of the void, just a pleasant, much-needed sleep. Before Luke let the last bits of willpower and concentration leave him, he saw system messages rolling down his interface.
