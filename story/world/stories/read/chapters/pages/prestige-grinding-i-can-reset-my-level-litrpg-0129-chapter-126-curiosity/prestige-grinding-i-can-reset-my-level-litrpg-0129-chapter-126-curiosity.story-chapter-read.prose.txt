'This is… insane,' he thought, feeling the rush of power. 'The more enemies I consume, the further my reach grows, reaching even more monsters.'

The more energy [Devouring Will] absorbed, the wider his [Psychic Hold] range became, pressing down on even more monsters and draining their mental power in turn.

‘This is a perfect combo,’ he realized, surprised. ‘There's no army I can't annihilate with this.’
