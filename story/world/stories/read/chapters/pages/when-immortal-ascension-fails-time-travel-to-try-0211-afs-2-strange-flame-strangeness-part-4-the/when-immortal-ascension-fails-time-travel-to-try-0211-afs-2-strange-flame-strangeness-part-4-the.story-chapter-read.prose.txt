He ran up the volcano. The steep slope made our speed decrease significantly, but there were at least fewer trees to interrupt our path. If anyone decided to look at the volcano, they’d easily see our bright white mech with its neon pink lights in the dead of night.

“Just hurry into the crater before we get recorded. I don’t want to have to hire a team of hackers to delete evidence.”

Our mech jerked as Spring miscalculated and stepped poorly. We began sliding down the slope toward the glowing surface. Error messages and warnings popped up on the mech’s viewing screen. I opened my light brain and quickly worked to correct them from here. Unfortunately, it looked like we pulled an artificial muscle in the mech’s ankle. The heat from the unnatural lava here was also a bit too hot for the shitty exterior armor this thing had. The ground melted the rubber parts of the mech’s feet.

This mech was not rated for extreme environments like our personalized mechs were.

We finally brought the mech to a stop a mere four meters before we reached the burning lake.

“Back her up. There is a semi-flat ledge ten meters to the left.”

Spring limped us over to the ledge. I quickly unstrapped myself and moved past him to open the cockpit.

“This mech isn’t rated for extreme heat environments. But don’t worry. I plan to go alone from here.”

The lava surrounded me. Even with the shield spell, the blistering heat hit my skin. My lips, mouth, and eyes seemed dried out already. This was a very powerful flame I was about to face.

“Goddamn it!” I stood and checked my mech pilot uniform to make sure it hadn’t been burned. Fortunately, it was made of sturdy SS-rank material, even if it was the cheap school provided one.

I walked down the pink tunnel until I reached a cavern where I saw a glitch in the universe.

On the ground was a small strange flame that looked like yellow fire, intersected with yellow and blue lines.
