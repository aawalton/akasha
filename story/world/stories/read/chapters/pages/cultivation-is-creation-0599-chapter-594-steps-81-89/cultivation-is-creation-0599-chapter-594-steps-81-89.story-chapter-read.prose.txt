“We move on,” Song Wei declared, checking his jade compass. “We have step eighty-one next.”

This was a real cultivation world. There was no time to mourn. In fact, there was no one left to mourn. The palace had absorbed Xu Feng completely, leaving nothing behind except those smooth black surfaces that had silver veins pulsing like a heartbeat.

As we climbed, Tian fell into step beside me. Even though his face showed nothing of what he was feeling, I knew him well enough by now to recognise the slight tension in his shoulders that told me what he was feeling.

“Hey,” I whispered. “You okay?”

“I've seen death before.” His voice was flat. “This is what cultivation means.”

That really wasn’t an answer, but I didn’t push it because right now we had bigger problems to deal with.

***

Three manifestations spawned on step eighty-one. They all wore ancient armours that were marked with crests of sects that I didn’t recognise, but it was their spiritual pressure that impressed me. The combined aura hit us hard, actually forcing Li Na to take a step back before the girl could gather her composure.

They were all Mid-Lucid Lawbearers.

But that didn’t mean much to our leaders.

Without saying a word, Song Wei and Shen Bolin made their move. While the former’s silver energy bent probability around the leftmost enemy, making its sword strikes miss by tiny margins. Shen Bolin's blade carved through the center manifestation's defense like it was made of paper.

As for me, I stayed at the back while Xie Ling created ice walls to block the third enemy's advance, Li Na maintained support formations that helped stabilize everyone's spiritual energy, and Tian positioned himself to intercept if anything broke through.

The manifestations didn’t stand a chance against two Peak Lucid Lawbearers working together, so the fight didn’t last too long, maybe a minute or two, before the number in the sky changed from 19 to 18.

***

This same pattern continued until step eighty-five.

The two Lucid Lawbearers did the real fighting while the rest of us supported and handled stragglers.

At step eighty-three, I had to use my transformed arm to punch a hole through a chest when an enemy broke through Xie Ling's ice barrier and came at Li Na.

“That was close,” Li Na breathed heavily, staring at me. “Thank you.”

"It’s no problem." I let the transformation fade. "We’re not alone, we’ve got each other's backs."

She nodded slowly, still a little shaken up by the close call.

“We don’t have time to waste,” Song Wei called out, glancing back at us with a frown. "Keep moving.”

***

Step eighty-six was different. There was only a single enemy. This set off alarm bells in my mind.

Sure, the figure was an elderly, frail woman with white hair that looked like she wouldn’t hurt anyone, but I wasn’t fooled, her spiritual pressure was massive. This was a Peak Lucid Lawbearer without a doubt.

"Students Must Learn Through Suffering," she declared, activating a law.

Within a second, every technique we used suddenly became more difficult. It felt like my dream qi had developed a will of its own. Xie Ling's ice formations cracked before fully forming. Tian's sword work lost some of its fluid precision. Even Song Wei and Shen Bolin struggled. Their overwhelming power advantage meant nothing when their own techniques worked against them. Fortunately for me, my red sun energy seemed mostly unaffected, but I decided not to reveal that.

"Her Law targets learning and adaptation," Shen Bolin murmured through gritted teeth. "The more we try, the more difficult it becomes."

"Then we stop trying to learn." Song Wei's silver energy flared. "We use only what we've already mastered."

That sort of worked. By limiting themselves to their most basic, most perfected techniques, they could bypass some of the Law's effects, but it still took them twenty minutes to wear down the old woman's defenses, which was an eternity when it came to cultivation battles at this high level.

"Better,” the lady smiled as she dissolved. “You've learned something after all. That’s all I ever wanted."

The number dropped to 13.

***

Steps eighty-seven and eighty-eight were brutal because exhaustion was setting in. Unlike what lower-level cultivators might believe, Peak Lucid Lawbearers did have limits, and I saw it with my own eyes. Song Wei's movements became slightly slower while Shen Bolin's sword work lost some of its effortless perfection.

At step eighty-eight, Xie Ling took a serious hit. A manifestation's technique caught her off-guard and sent her flying into the stone wall. She left a crater in the black surface while blood ran from her nose and mouth as she struggled back to her feet.

"I'm fine," she said before anyone could ask.

She wasn't, but we didn't have time to argue.

The battle continued, we won, and the number changed to 11.

This meant that we had eleven fights left, but I was down to about sixty percent of my red sun reserves, and eighty percent for dream qi. As for blue sun energy and regular qi, they were mostly untouched because I still didn't trust the two Lucid Lawbearers enough to reveal my full capabilities. That lack of trust might kill us all, but showing everything might do the same thing.

***

When we arrived at step eighty-nine, we met twin cultivators. A man and a woman who moved in perfect synchronisation, that fighting them felt like battling a single entity with two bodies. It was similar to fighting Chen Huan, but they were both at the Late Lucid Lawbearer level, which made their combined power more than a Peak Lucid Lawbearer.

This wasn’t something that even the party leaders could deal with easily.

If we wanted to defeat them, we needed to split them up, divide and conquer.

But before we attempt that, they called out: "Separation Is Death."

The spiritual pressure shifted as the Law took effect, bounding the twins together.

Dammit, now if anyone tried to separate them by more than ten meters, they would suffer massive spiritual backlash. The effect would build slowly, then accelerate until the person attempting separation either gave up or died. It was perfect defensive synchronization that had ruined our only hope of defeating them before we could even try it.

Song Wei cursed under his breath. "They've perfected the binding."

“We tried to separate them on our first expedition together,” Shen Bolin explained, his expression darkening. "And it cost us three people. Keep your eyes open if you don’t want to join them.”

The twins attacked as one unit. The man used flame techniques while the woman wielded ice. Fire and frost working in harmony, creating steam barriers and temperature differentials that made it nearly impossible to track their actual positions. They moved within five meters of each other constantly, never giving Song Wei or Shen Bolin a clear opening.

"Formation support," Shen Bolin called out. "Keep them pressured from range."

We spread out around the massive step: Xie Ling launched ice projectiles from one angle, Tian sent sword qi from another, and I used my vine constructs to attack from a third direction, but the twins handled it easily.

The man would counter ice with fire, melting Xie Ling's ice before it reached them. The woman created frost barriers that stopped Tian's sword techniques cold. My vines got within two meters before they were burned and frozen, the opposing temperatures shattering them into useless fragments.

Song Wei tried his probability manipulation on the female twin to make her next movement place her outside the Law's range. The technique should have forced her to stumble, to step too far from her partner.

Instead, the male twin moved to compensate. He closed the distance she was being forced to create, so the Law remained stable. The punishment never triggered because they adapted faster than Song Wei could manipulate probability.

"They've learned from our previous encounters," Song Wei cursed.

From his breathing, I could tell that using his Law continuously taking a toll on him.

Shen Bolin attacked from a different angle. His dream manifestation techniques created seven copies of himself, all attacking the twins from different directions simultaneously. The copies were solid enough to block techniques and deliver real damage.

The twins shifted into a back-to-back stance. The man faced four copies while the woman handled three. Their techniques flowed together seamlessly. Fire spiraled around ice in a double helix pattern. The opposing temperatures created a vortex that pulled in all seven copies and tore them apart before they could land a single strike.

"Centuries of synchronized training," the woman said, sounding bored. "We learned from watching Lucid Lawbearers fail."

"Every tactic has been attempted," the man continued. "Every strategy has been countered. You cannot separate what has chosen to be inseparable."

With that, they merged their opposing elements into something new – Plasma.

It crashed towards Song Wei and Shen Bolin like a tidal wave.

Both Lucid Lawbearers defended. Song Wei bent probability to make the attack less likely to hi while Shen Bolin manifested shields from his dreams. The plasma struck their defenses and stopped, but barely and not without cost.

When the technique finally dissipated, both were bleeding from their ears and noses from trying to hold their ground against the attack.

"We need to overwhelm them," Shen Bolin said. "Hit them with everything simultaneously."

"They're expecting that," Song Wei replied. "They've already trained a counter."

"But we do not have a better option, do we?"

Song Wei checked his compass and frowned. "No. I guess not."

So, they launched their combined assault. Probability manipulation and dream manifestation working together. Silver energy and constructed reality crashing against the twins from multiple angles. It was overwhelming force. The kind of power that should have ended the battle in seconds.

The twins responded with their own ultimate technique.

"Twin Soul Resonance."

Their spiritual foundations merged, and for a moment, they became one consciousness operating two bodies. The synchronization was now absolute. They moved with single-minded purpose, countering every attack with techniques that required no communication because they shared the same thoughts.

The battle escalated into something almost beyond my ability to follow clearly. Techniques flew too fast. The spiritual pressure became painful just to be near. I maintained my distance and kept sending vine attacks whenever I saw an opening, but mostly I watched two Lucid Lawbearers struggle against an enemy that had been specifically designed to counter higher cultivation power.

Li Na's formations provided constant support. Healing energy flowed to Song Wei and Shen Bolin whenever they sustained injuries. Spiritual reinforcement helped them maintain their techniques longer. Her contribution was subtle but crucial.

Xie Ling and Tian kept up the ranged pressure. Forcing the twins to split their attention even slightly. Creating small openings that Song Wei and Shen Bolin could exploit.

The battle lasted thirty-seven minutes according to Song Wei's compass. By the end, all of us were exhausted. Song Wei's silver energy had dimmed noticeably. Shen Bolin's manifestations were becoming translucent, less solid.

The twins weren't doing much better. Their plasma techniques had decreased in power. Their movements had lost some of their perfect synchronization. The Twin Soul Resonance had ended, leaving them as two separate people again instead of one merged consciousness.

Song Wei found the opening he'd been waiting for. A moment when the male twin was slightly out of position, pulled forward by attacking Shen Bolin's latest manifestation. Just half a meter too far from his partner.

Silver energy exploded outward. Song Wei's Law forced a specific outcome: the male twin would step one more meter forward. Probability collapsed and reality rewrote itself to match. The male twin's foot moved forward and his body followed. For one instant, he was eleven meters from his partner instead of ten.

The Law activated. "Separation Is Death" punished the person who had caused the separation. Song Wei.

Silver energy erupted from his body as the backlash hit. He screamed and fell to one knee. Blood poured from his nose, his ears, his eyes. The spiritual damage was catastrophic. but the male twin was out of position, which meant his synchronized defense was broken.

Shen Bolin appeared behind him, and the blade pierced through the male twin's spiritual core, causing him to dissolve into energy and be absorbed by the palace, leaving the woman alone to face us.

For the first time, she hesitated, and a hint of fear appeared in her eyes. Her Law was still active, but her partner was gone.

"Please," she said. "Don't make me—"

Without the synchronization, she was easy pickings, and Shen Bolin's strike destroyed her before letting her finish her sentence, causing the number in the sky to change from 11 to 10.

The battle was over, but Song Wei was still on his knees. The spiritual backlash from deliberately triggering the twins' Law had nearly killed him. Li Na rushed to his side, her healing formations working overtime to stabilize his spiritual foundation.

“You…you’re insane as I remember,” Shen Bolin said, but I could hear the respect in his voice. “That could have crippled you, or even kill you.”

"It nearly did." Song Wei admitted, coughing up dark blood onto the ground. "But it worked and that’s what matters."

“Hmm,” Shen Bolin murmured. “Well, after that, we need at least ten minutes to recover before we ascend to step ninety.”

No one objected to that. Instead, we all sat on step eighty-nine, focusing on recovering while praying that whatever waited for us on step ninety didn't appear until we were ready. Otherwise, it would probably be game over.

"We have nine steps left before meet Shen Mohai," Song Wei said with a tired voice after the break. "But the Three Calamities are next. It won’t be easy. We've both failed there before in earlier expeditions."

“All three are Peak Lucid Lawbearers,” Shen Bolin sighed.

"Can we beat them?" Xie Ling asked.

"Maybe." Song Wei checked his compass. "If we're smart and lucky. That’s the only way we have been able to in the past.”

Great. Two things we hadn't been particularly good at so far.
