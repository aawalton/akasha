Master, stop bullying her. Dovey demands of me.

Yes you are. You are just a big bully, you know. Plus fighting that golem was super fun.

Everything is really vibrant and magical and the entire environment has shifted. There are also brand new monsters spawning, ones that are far more dangerous than before.”

Huh, I guess that is the effects of ridiculous amounts of core leakage. Claire wasn’t lying about the entire region being destroyed. Seems like it is a completely different place than before. Maybe one day when I am stronger, I can go back and visit. That would be nice, I think.

Hmm, I could see how that could come across as a jerk.

Oh really now? Dovey adds with far too much sarcasm for my liking. He has been getting too bold as of late. Perhaps I should remind him of who won our first battle. Mercy, please, o great master that is definitely not a jerk.

After a while, I can no longer see her as there are too many hills and whatnot in the way. Dovey can still see her from the air though, but he agrees that there is no way I will catch up, seeing as she isn’t even slowing down.

I stop moving and simply collapse onto my back, staring up at the now night sky. The moons are quite beautiful, and sort of calming. There is just something nice about them being up there, hanging in the sky. Never moving, never changing. I wonder what they even are. I think some philosophers in my old world were spouting that our moon was like a physical place, and that one might even be able to go there.

But that all just sounds absurd. How can something so big in the sky be a real place? Let alone having four of them in this new world.

Of course, what is more baffling is Ari. Like, I knew she was hiding something inside. Some deep stuff, which I should definitely not be allowed anywhere near. Guess I just proved that right. In the end, our traveling together simply wasn’t meant to be.

“Yip, yip.” Luma says in reassurance as she curls up on my chest. At least I still have her. Oh, and Dovey too. It isn’t like I am alone. After all, I still have plenty of spirit friends to make, I bet.

Plus I doubt this is the last time I see Ari. We are both heading in the same direction, after all. Chances are we will run into each other in Itolas, if not there, then somewhere along the way. At the very least, we are bound to run into each other at the banquet.

This world may be incomprehensibly big, but it is also small, in a way. What way? I’ve got no clue. But that sounds vaguely philosophically bullshity. Which is exactly the sort of sentiment I need right about now.
