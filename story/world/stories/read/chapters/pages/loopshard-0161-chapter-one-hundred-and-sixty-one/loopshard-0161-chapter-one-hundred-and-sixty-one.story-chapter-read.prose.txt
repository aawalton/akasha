Luo fired off several bolts aiming for the boss’ neck, while Kateřina went to town on his legs, stabbing them repeatedly to try and damage muscles and tendons. Then she punched her right arm upwards and activated the Purple Slime Glove she wore on her hand, sending an acidic spike up through the bloodfiend’s torso, clearly piercing a lung and the heart in the process.

The Patriarch immediately stepped forward, his severed leg pulling itself back in place with tendrils of blood while he back-handed Sofia with enough force to send her bouncing across the church floor until she struck the altar and came to a sudden stop.

Adam fired his Heart Lance, swiping it up through the Patriarch’s body, but as the spell struck him, it didn’t seem very effective, and at the same time, the damage that Luo had done to his head healed itself back into place. However, the burn-mark remained from where the statue had struck.

Kateřina parried a lazy swipe from a clawed hand, creating another weak spot on the bloodfiend’s body, but when Luo and Adam both sent their attacks directly for it, the Patriarch’s massive body turned hazy and the church suddenly filled with orange-coloured Mana to Adam’s sight.

Adam turned and shot out his Spidersilk Needle, but it just passed through the large vampire.

Adam pulled the blood to himself, turning it into three separate spinning needles in the air and launching them out, while simultaneously absorbing the orange Mana permeating the church by activating his cowl, making the ambient Mana dissipate and interrupting the illusion-based attack. His needles lanced into the Patriarch’s back, stopping the boss from swiping his claws through Kateřina.

Adam immediately sunk the needles into the Patriarch’s body and spread the blood throughout his veins and flesh. Then he triggered Blood Crystal and the boss wailed in pain.

Kateřina swung the statue she’d picked up from the floor, driving it into the side of the Patriarch’s right knee and eliciting another loud scream.

A bolt struck the Patriarch in the head, and Adam turned to see Luo holding his crossbow despite his left arm clearly being broken.

The exsanguinated corpse of Father Mathias still remained on the floor, and as Kateřina struck the boss with the statue again, getting another loud scream out of him, Adam went over to the corpse and filled it with his Mana to take control of the flesh. It was not as powerfully blessed as the priest’s blood had been, but he figured it would still carry some of the First Light’s power within.

Adam quickly sculpted the flesh into a massive clawed limb, and as Kateřina struck the boss a third time, sending him to his knees while Luo added more bolts to his head, Adam swung the limb forward, driving its talons up through the torso of the Patriarch and digging it in deep. He shaped the flesh as it entered the boss, pushing it in through the wound he’d made, twisting and expanding the limb to leave a ruinous channel all the way through the bloodfiend to try and make it impossible to quickly heal.

Kateřina saw the opportunity for what it was and shoved the statue all the way into the Patriarch’s torso, which turned the wails from pained to desperate.

“You fucking thought you could kill us, you piece of shit!?” she screamed into his burn-marked and monstrous face while repeatedly stabbing her daggers into his neck, little by little carving all the way through in an attempt to decapitate him.

“It’s not over yet!” Adam warned as he ran back to Sofia, trying to get her to wake up.

As she started to blink and come to, Adam went over to Aeran’s dead body and harvested its blood and flesh, hoping he could bring it with him.

Then pink Mana flooded the church and their surroundings changed as they were forcefully pulled into a dream version of Moonport, the same that Kateřina must’ve witnessed in her dream.

Colours inverted and the walls of the church turned pure white, while the candles and their light became black like soot.

The Patriarch too had turned black, becoming like a shadow.

Adam tried to absorb the Mana with his cowl to break them out of the second phase of the fight, but nothing happened.

Luo jumped behind a white-glowing pew and Kateřina parried, as the boss sent out four rapid spikes of shadow aimed at them both. But even though they’d gone to a dream-like world, they were grounded in the real Moonport, and the boss still had a holy artifact lodged inside his body, killing him from within. He couldn’t touch the object without hurting himself, and all the burn-marks on his body glowed white like their surroundings, making it clear his time was numbered.

“Just avoid his attacks!” Adam told them.

Luo activated his grappling hook to pull himself to the altar alongside Adam and Sofia, who was still groggy from being knocked unconscious.

The blessed blood that Adam had collected from Aeran also glowed white and he shaped it into a flowing blade, which he swung at the boss before he could attack Kateřina who was the only one still fighting up close.

As Adam’s magic tore into the boss, she ran back towards the altar as well.

Every slash and cut of the blessed blood left bright-glowing marks on the Patriarch’s shadowy body.

The wails he produced were powerful enough to shake the floor and walls around him, and they made Adam’s chest hurt, as though it interfered with his breathing and heartbeat.

Then the Patriarch turned and tried to claw his way out of the church’s opening, which was too small for his big frame. Despite possessing the power of shadow, it apparently didn’t allow him to traverse through them.

Adam quickly used the blessed blood to paint out a spell pattern on the Patriarch’s back.

The moment the linework was complete, white-glowing thorny vines wrapped around the boss, binding him in place. At the same time, the holy relic Kateřina had planted inside his torn-open body was starting to do enough damage that glowing holes appeared all over his shadowy skin.

Adam took the last of the blessed blood from Aeran’s corpse and shoved it inside the bloodfiend, trying to spread it out as much as possible.

And then it was like a switch had been flicked, as the dream magic cut off and the world around them was returned to its real colours.

Jammed into the doorway lay the Patriarch’s large body. It was covered in black burn-marks, and hardly any of his pale skin was visible anymore.

Adam and Kateřina approached it, but before they could get all the way up close, the Patriarch turned to ash, leaving behind a few objects, but nothing else.

The holy statue dropped to the floor with a clunk but it was immediately clear to Adam that its power was spent and no more anti-vampire juice could be squeezed from it. Still, he didn’t want to test his luck by getting too close to it.
