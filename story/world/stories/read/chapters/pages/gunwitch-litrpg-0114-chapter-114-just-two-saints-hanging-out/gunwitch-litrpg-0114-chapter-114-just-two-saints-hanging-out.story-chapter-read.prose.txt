Hearing Kasia say that warmed my heart. She thought I was charming and funny! All this time I was scared they either had no sense of humor here, or even worse, that I wasn’t funny. Now I knew the truth: I was funny, but Silesians were just very reserved with their compliments.

My thoughts drifted back to what class I should pretend to have. Something unassuming. [Carpenter]? No, I wouldn’t want people to think I was some kind of degenerate. And I’d probably have to change into someone with a goofy-looking face to sell the whole thing. Kuba didn’t have a goofy face, of course, but I assumed he was the exception. No, [Carpenter] wouldn’t work. It would have to be something that would allow me to still be beautiful, but at the same time not give people any expectations of me. I didn’t want to have to do anything. If I told people I was a [Carpenter], they’d probably start pestering me with all sorts of unreasonable requests.

What if someone came up to me with a handful of sawdust and demanded I tell them which tree it was from? What if someone asked me to smell their house to check if the floor was still structurally sound? I didn’t know anything about structurality or sounds! To me sound was something you heard with your ears, for Goddess sake! And I didn’t know how to play an instrument either. I was getting confused just thinking about it.

What if someone asked me to fix a chair? That would be simple, right? Kuba once mentioned that making beds was super easy. And since chairs are basically small, vertical beds, those would probably be even easier to make. Maybe they are not, but they still kind of are. You’d be able to lie down on a very big chair as if it were a bed. They really are like small beds! They even have a headboard!

Kasia was looking at me expectantly, and suddenly I got an idea. A big grin split my face, like a [Carpenter] splitting a tree. This was going to be so good, and it would give me everything that I’d need. Honestly, I was pretty proud of myself for being so smart. A lesser mind would have struggled to come up with something as genius as this. Me? Only took me a second.
