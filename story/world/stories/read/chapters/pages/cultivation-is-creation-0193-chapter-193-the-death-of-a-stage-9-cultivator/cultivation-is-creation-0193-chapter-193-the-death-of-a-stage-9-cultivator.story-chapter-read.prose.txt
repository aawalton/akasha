The red mist pulled itself back together the way liquid mercury does when you split it apart, and Ke Jun reformed in front of us with a slight tightness around his eyes and a downward pull at the corners of his mouth that hadn’t been there before.

"How frustrating," he said quietly, looking down at his own semi-transparent hands. "This spiritual form appears to be incompatible with most of my usual techniques." His red eyes moved to Yan Li and stayed there for a moment. "But that imperial qi of yours is intriguing, golden variant was the sign of the Liu Clan back in my time, the Yan Clan has come further than I would have expected, I remember when your ancestors were working on the basic bronze manifestation and hadn’t progressed beyond that."

Liu Chang's expression darkened at that but before he could say anything Ke Jun's form blurred and he was somewhere else entirely, his fingers moving through a series of hand signs I didn’t recognize while blood red light traced complicated patterns through the air around him.

"Even without my primary techniques there are always other options available."

What came next was the air itself seeming to turn red, a tide of crimson energy surging toward Yan Li with savage force that made my stomach feel wrong, because this was a technique trying to destroy us all completely.

But Yan Li was ready for it.

"Titans Palm!"

Another enormous golden hand appeared, this one facing forward like a wall, and when the red tide crashed into it, the two forces met in an explosion of crimson and gold that filled the whole area with light and pressure.

Ke Jun watched his attack come apart against Yan Li's barrier and his frown got deeper. "Blood techniques were never where my real strengths lay," he admitted, sounding irritated with himself. "In my prime I would have simply…" and then Bing Lan was behind him.

"Azure Moons Final Quarter!"

Her sword technique came from every direction at once, each strike carrying enough force to split stone, and there was no conventional defensive position that could cover all of the angles she was attacking from simultaneously.

What Ke Jun did in response wasn’t conventional either.

His body simply moved around the attacks the way water moves around rocks, his form distorting and then pulling itself back together, letting every single sword strike pass through the spaces where his body was not anymore.

"The famous Dancing Blade," he said, and he actually sounded impressed. "Its reputation is deserved, that technique would have caused me serious problems if I had a proper body to worry about." His form solidified again. "Unfortunately..."

He closed the distance to Bing Lan faster than I could actually follow with my eyes and his hand barely touched her sword arm as he passed her, the lightest possible contact, and then crimson lines were spreading across her skin like cracks spreading through glass and she stumbled backward with her face drained of color, her sword falling from her hand.

"Senior Sister!" one of the younger disciples started moving toward her but Liu Chang stopped them immediately.

"Hold position! Su Yue, Shen Xuanyu, ranged assault pattern three!"

Su Yue's flames and Shen Xuanyu's jade lightning came together into a single combined attack that melted the stone platform beneath our feet from the heat it was generating, and at the same time Zhang Wei and the other Heavenly Jade disciples added their power to Yan Li's next attack, golden light and jade energy forming together into a massive sword shape that came down from above.

Ke Jun actually laughed as he pulled back from Bing Lan, and he raised both hands with red light building around them in complex layered patterns. "Better, much better! Let me show you something I used more often in the old days."

What he did next happened almost too fast to properly see.

The red light exploded outward in countless thin lines in every direction, like a web made entirely of energy, and everywhere those lines made contact with the incoming attacks they divided them, splitting them into smaller and smaller pieces until there was nothing left of them and the energy that had been in them was gone.

"The Bloodline Division Art," Ke Jun said, in the tone of someone explaining something to students. "A technique I developed after studying how cells divide at the fundamental level, quite effective against energy-based attacks as you can see."

He sent one of those red lines through Su Yue's steam barrier to demonstrate further and the barrier split perfectly down the center, both halves dissolving into nothing. Su Yue barely got herself out of the space she had been standing in before more lines cut through it.

"You are all talented," Ke Jun continued, walking forward at a pace that was almost casual while our entire group was being pushed backward. "Given a few centuries some of you might even approach the level I was at originally." He looked at Yan Li when he said that last part. "But right now you are children using profound techniques that you don’t yet fully understand."

He demonstrated exactly what he meant immediately after saying it, disappearing from where he was standing and reappearing somewhere else with no visible transition between the two positions.

"Master," Azure said, his voice sharp and urgent, "his qi signature is destabilizing further, the pattern suggests…" He didn’t get to finish because the next attack had already started.

The blood red lines multiplied until they formed a web that filled all of the air around our group, and where those lines touched stone the stone divided itself into smaller and smaller pieces until it was dust.

Several of the weaker disciples didn’t react quickly enough, the lines passed through their defensive techniques as if those techniques weren’t there at all and then through their bodies just as easily, and they didn’t even have time to make a sound before they came apart and dissolved into qi that flowed directly into Ke Jun.

"Scatter!" Liu Chang's voice cut through everything. "Don’t group together, he can’t divide everywhere at the same time!"

Everyone spread out and tried to keep enough distance between themselves and Ke Jun to have time to react to whatever he did next, and I ended up near the edge of the platform, which suited me perfectly well because the further I was from him the better I felt.

I activated Hawk's Eye knowing I had about thirty seconds before the strain on my mind became too much to sustain, but thirty seconds of being able to actually see what was happening at that level of detail could easily be the difference between surviving this and not surviving it.

Everything slowed down and the details of what was happening became very clear.

I could see the small fluctuations in Ke Jun's form, the moments where his body rippled slightly, and more importantly I could start to see the patterns in how his attacks connected to each other and where they came from.

"The lines have a maximum range of about forty feet!" I called out. "And once they are formed, they can’t change direction!"

Ke Jun's eyes came straight to me the moment I said that and I immediately knew that drawing his attention had been a mistake. "Interesting," he said. "You have developed quite a strong analytical ability, but can you analyze this?"

His form blurred and three separate lines of blood red energy came at me from three different angles, and I activated Blink Step, they cut deep grooves into the stone platform where I had been standing a moment before.

But I hadn’t actually been what he was aiming at.

"Bing Lan, behind you!"

She was already turning when I said it, but it wasn’t fast enough, his hand passed through her sword technique without slowing down at all. His fingers touched her chest with the same light contact as before and the crimson lines spread across her entire body this time, spreading along every surface, and her voice when she spoke was barely above a whisper.

"Not like..." she started, and didn’t finish.

She came apart along the lines and what was left dissolved into qi that Ke Jun absorbed, and she was gone.

What that did to Liu Chang was visible, stone spreading across his skin faster than I had ever seen it move, his qi signature climbing to levels that felt wrong for someone at the Qi Condensation realm.

"Immutable Titan Scripture, Mountain Crushes Heaven!"

The attack was extraordinary, his stone covered fist carrying enough force to distort the air around it as it moved. Even my Hawk's Eye enhanced perception was struggling to fully process the principles of mass and momentum that were built into the technique.

Ke Jun raised his hand to use the division lines again, but Liu Chang’s attack was already past the point where the lines could form in time to stop it, and the impact hit the ancient spirit directly in the chest and his physical form shattered.

It was still not enough.

"Not bad," Ke Jun's voice came from no particular direction while his form was still pulling itself back together. "The Immutable Titan Scripture, it has been a long time since I have seen that technique used, but..."

His reformed body moved through Liu Chang's guard and one pale hand touched the stone like surface of his skin, leaving traces of crimson energy that began spreading through it like cracks.

Liu Chang stumbled backward with his technique flickering as the foreign energy worked against his qi circulation, but before Ke Jun could follow that up, a massive golden fist was already forming above him.

"Heavenly Titans Judgment!"

The red lines and the golden technique met in the air between them and neither one overwhelmed the other. I used the moment that created to do something, not a direct attack because I wasn’t going to get within reach of those division lines voluntarily, but I had other things available to me.

Explosive Seeds left my fingers in precise sequence positioned to force Ke Jun into specific directions of movement, and at the same time, vines came up from the ground around him to reduce the options he had for where to go.

"Jade Thunder Empire!"

Shen Xuanyu's lightning came from the opposite direction at the right moment, and Ke Jun had to choose between the lightning or staying still and dealing with the vines closing in around him. He chose a third option instead.

The blood red lines expanded outward from him in every direction simultaneously, my vines divided, Shen Xuanyu's lightning divided, the stone platform beneath our feet divided into smaller and smaller pieces, and all of it ceased to be a threat.

This was a very bad situation, and I was fully aware of that.

Even with his power apparently reduced from what it had once been, he was taking apart everything we could do with no real effort, and the way his body moved around anything physical while those touches of his drained the life out of whatever he made contact with made the entire fight feel like trying to stop water from moving.

"Master," Azure said, cutting through my thoughts, "I have been analyzing his energy signature and there is a consistent pattern to the fluctuations."

I got behind a column that had been partially destroyed as another wave of lines cut through the space I had just been standing in. "Tell me that is actually useful information."

"Each time his form is forced to reconstruct itself, it becomes slightly less stable than it was before, the process appears to cost him a significant amount of energy each time it happens."

That was good news.

It meant that wearing him down was actually possible, it would just take a very long time and cost a great deal more than we had already paid.
