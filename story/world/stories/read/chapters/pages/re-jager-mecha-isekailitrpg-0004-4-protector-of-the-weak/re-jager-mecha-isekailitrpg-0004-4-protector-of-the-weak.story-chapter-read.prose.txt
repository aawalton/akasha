Retake Albion! Their last Empress shouted.

Retake Sicilia! Their last President shouted.

Retake Lotharingia! And the rest of the continent! Their new Emperor shouted.

Now, they dragged the entire continent, her nation included into this massive war.

No Flandrian artillery. No Sicilian tanks either, nor Lotharingian mechs, nor Albionese aircraft in the skies. Just him and this…girl that he saved from those beasts.

She was sitting on his lap since the Wanderfalke’s cockpit was only designed for one person, but thankfully, it had enough space between him and the control sticks and displays in front of him. Ah…crap. He reminded himself of something. It’s already past nine, and I haven’t eaten yet. Or fed her. Of course, just like any soldier in a survival situation, eating was far behind Hans’ priorities. Unfortunately, he stupidly extended that philosophy to her. He wanted to slap himself for that. She needed to eat.

This is why no one should trust me with kids. He began driving his Wanderfalke into the side of the road, continuing into a decently well-covered part of the forest. His mech stopped, and Hans, carefully, tried to shift Alizée into his seat as he popped his hatch open.

As usual, he’d have to clear the area, maybe gather a bit of wood and crap to start a fire, then grab his MREs to do…well, whatever food he could make. On that note, I only have a week’s worth of rations. He scratched the back of his head. I’d definitely need more to stretch that as best as I can.
