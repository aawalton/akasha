“Hello Cassandra,” Mettius said, his voice taking on a haughtier tone than Caleb and Kelia were accustomed to.

They all turned toward the fire to see a women dressed in black leather armor accented with scarlet leading two men and a woman. The woman wore robes that sounded like the wizard’s robes Mettius had describe when they’d learned that the aspects of her clothing were interfering with Kelia’s mana. The man on the left wore full plate armor and held a large two handed sword while the second man was wreathed in flames, making his clothing difficult to spot.

“I thought I’d be seeing you here,” Mettius said.

But while Mettius had said that, Caleb and Kelia also heard Mettius say something else at the same time, the words super imposed on the louder ones.

“No one can hear this but you two. Give me a moment to talk so my skills activate and then kill the wizard when I say.”

Caleb and Kelia looked at each at the same time, serving as confirmation to each that they had indeed heard what Mettius had just said.

“I’ll admit I am surprised to see you’ve thrown your lot in with the rebels.”

“Why would it be a surprise dear half brother? The Benefactor and I both love killing our father’s children. It only made sense for me to join with him.”

“And who is this Benefactor?”

“Why, the true heir,” Cassandra said as if it were hilarious that anyone couldn’t know that.

“Don’t tell me you’re buying into that fanatical nonsense as well.”

“It’s not nonsense. The First Emperor forged his throne and crown. Our ancestor had no true claim to it, but he took it anyway proving he deserved it,” Cassandra’s tone took on to fanatical glee as she spoke. She may have believed the words she spoke, but she was not enraptured by them. “But us? Our siblings? We’re children squabbling over our father’s leavings. They’re all pathetic. All except you—or so I thought. I’ll say I respected your choice in class. It was a stupid choice, but a respectable one. The Benefactor will need diplomats—people to smooth over the transition of his eventual rule. Join us.”

“Why is he deserving?” Mettius asked.

“He’s mastered the system! He’s reforging it. What more proof of a true heir can there be?”

“He must be powerful if he convinced you to worship him over your own ego.” Mettius said while at the same time shouting “Now!” to Caleb and Kelia.

The enemy had lowered their guard slightly as the two half siblings entered the verbal spar so when Kelia and Mettius fired their bolts at the wizard, she was caught off guard. She threw up a shield, but it appeared too lay to block Mettius’ force bolt which struck her on the shoulder at the same time Kelia’s frost bolt hit her shield.

The shoulder impact broke her concentration no her spell, and Caleb’s hammer that came flying in at the end struck her straight in the chest, taking her out of the fight before it even began.

“You can’t seem to ever win without your tricks,” Cassandra said without even a moments care for her deceased wizard.

Cassandra vanished, falling into the ground into her own shadow. Caleb moved in to get his hammer within range of his Imperium, but the armored warrior beside the wizard snatched it out of her caved in chest and threw it over the wall and out towards the forest and then ran to meet

The pyromancer’s flames began to spin around him in a spiraling inferno and he send a firebolt at Kelia and Mettius.

Mettius blocked it with a shield of his own, moving faster under the enhancement of his Haste.

Before the full brunt of the attack could by stymied however, Mettius was pulled into the ground into his own shadow. The flames wreathed over Kelia, and acting on instinct she emptied her ring of its cold mana to expand her mana pool. The cold mana swelled out from her and counter the fire at the perimeter of her pool.

The armored warrior charged Caleb, and he didn’t have time to draw his sword to defend against the larger man’s attack. Instead, Caleb dove to the side, pushing against the sword swing to send it wide. Caleb felt resistance as he pushed once more. The push still propelled him away using the sword as an anchor but more the sword’s path wasn’t altered as much as Caleb expected.

This time Caleb had his mana perception active, and he saw the reason for the change. As with all Imperium effects, Caleb’s mana lashed out of his pool to do his bidding. When that mana came in contact with the mana pool of others, their mana pushed back pitting their willpower against his own, absorbing some of the attack. It was only because Caleb’s willpower was so high already that he was even able to affect the metal inside the pool of another.

Caleb rolled on hitting the ground and managed to regain his feet, standing with his spatha drawn. Compared to the giant claymore of his enemy, it was a twig.

The warrior came on at Caleb with a telegraphed overhand swing, which Caleb had to block. He was a little faster than Caleb and much stronger, but with the aid of Metallic Guardian he could pit his willpower against his enemy’s strength.

It wasn’t enough. The skill absorbed most of the attack, but Caleb felt the power of the blow transfer into his arm. The man’s strength was greater than Caleb’s willpower, or he had a skill superior to his own.

The strength of the blow took Caleb by surprise, and he almost died then and there to another attack before a flying shape swooped down between them.

Avis flew out of nowhere and began raking at the holes in the helm of Caleb’s opponent. The warrior ripped the bird away with ease and threw its limp body across the field before resuming his attack on Caleb, but the actions of the bird had saved Caleb’s life.

Stupid bird! Caleb thought, not sure how Kelia would handle the loss.

Caleb deflected the next few attacks before finally landing a counterattack. He kept Metallic Guardian active at all time now, as the blows rained down, some even getting through his guard and striking his lorica. With the aid of his skills Caleb was able to hold his own, but the other warrior had the Path of the Sword, and his stamina reinforced and honed the edge of his blade to one that rivaled the strength of even Caleb’s magically hardened blade.

When Caleb landed blows, his sword was unharmed but so too was his enemy. The armor plates overlapped such that what little openings Caleb could see were covered with chainmail. A sword—even his journeyman piece—was no weapon for this fight.

Caleb longed for his hammer, but since he didn’t have it, he looked for a solution.

With each impact of his blade to the enemy’ armor he got a glimpse of the armor in his perception. His method of applying mental pressure to distant metal to map it didn’t work through mana pools, but with each hit, he caught a glimpse of the metal around the impact. Once he’d discovered this, he landed blows wherever and whenever he could, no longer looking for openings in the metal until finally he found a flaw. A welded patch in the steel that hadn’t been properly forged, just beneath the ribs.

Caleb began aiming for this spot, and in between his defenses he was able to land an Arcane Power Attack empowered strike, but all that resulted in was his blade point skipping off to the side of the plate.

He gave up quickly on that as that attempt almost saw him loosing his head. He ran through his options but had nothing that would help against such an armored foe. He used his Imperium Ventorum in addition to his other skills to stay alive but the wind would not help him pierce the armored shell in front of him. And if he tried to brute force control the metal armor, he’d be waging a war of his willpower against his opponents combined willpower and strength. All his skills had been focused on strengthening metal, not weakening it

But why can’t I?

Caleb went on the defense, drawing a nail from his pouch with his free hand. He knew how metal failed, even if he’d never tried to force it to happen before. As he fought, he probed his nail for a flaw and quickly found one. He worked against it with his mind, forcing the poorly fused metal to break apart. It gave with more mana than he expected but it worked and the nail fell into two pieces in his hand.

As Caleb fought the pyromancer. The fire mage kept trying to cast some long spell, but each time he did, Kelia disrupted the casting—until the one time she didn’t. Flames erupted from the ground coalescing into a human shaped figure with elongated arms. Caleb wanted to go help, but he didn’t know what he could do against such a creature. He didn’t have to worry though, for he sensed a familiar presence and turned to see Bog running through the front gate.

Caleb sent urgency through the bond and focused on the fire elemental and the dragon headed right for it, tackling the semi corporeal being to the ground. Bog bit at the elementals neck, but the neck only reformed just outside of his grasp. The elemental blasted the dragon with flames, but the dragon seemed unharmed entirely. The two battled ineffectually, neither able to harm the other, but neither was either willing to let the other go and harm their ally’s. It was only when Bog swiped with his mage steel right claw that the elemental reacted, rearing back in pain. Bog stopped all attempts at eating his foe and began instead to rake him with his foreclaw.

Now Caleb had a means, he just needed an opportunity. He went off the defensive, trying one more to make contact anywhere he could until he had an epiphany. With each impact on the breastplate he sent mana into the poor weld, degrading the seem. But the work was slow, and Caleb was tiring faster than his opponent.

Its so hard to hit him and the energy of my blows is just dissipated!

Caleb thought in frustration as he had to use an Arcane Power Attack to land a blow.

That’s it!

He swung again following up his ineffectual strike with another but this time he focused on the microscopic ripples his impact made in the armor with each hit. He focused on the wave and pushed it all towards the weld and he felt it crack deep within.

The warrior stepped back to get Caleb in optimal range and Caleb following him. The claymore came around at Caleb, and Caleb thrust his spatha at the weak point he’d created int the plate. He activated Arcane Power Attack and used his concentration to weather the claymore blow to his ribs.

His spatha struck the plate and punched through, deep into the man’s belly just before the claymore struck him. His magic kept the blow from breaking his ribs, but it still sent him sailing across the battlefield, ripping his sword from his grasp.
