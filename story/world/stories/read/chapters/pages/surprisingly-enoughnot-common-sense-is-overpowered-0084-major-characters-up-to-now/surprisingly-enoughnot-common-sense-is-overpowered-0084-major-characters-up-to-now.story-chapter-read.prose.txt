Ji NingA transmigrator thrown into a cultivation world that seems suspiciously similar to a cliché xianxia novel.It does not take him long to realize that this world has young masters, lucky protagonists, sudden treasures, and dramatic face-slapping incidents. Since he has no intention of dying because of someone else's plot armor, Ning decides to survive with common sense.His supernatural ability, Know Thyself, allows him to see his real-time attributes, cultivation progress, physical condition, technique mastery, and other details about himself with complete accuracy. Ning uses this to improve himself step by step. He checks his weaknesses, fixes mistakes, and builds his foundation carefully.Ning prefers preparation over reckless courage. He avoids trouble when he can, but if trouble reaches him anyway, he deals with it as efficiently as possible. He likes watching Xiao Fan run into protagonist clichés from a safe distance, though he will also use those clichés for his own benefit when the chance appears.His biggest problem is that no one in this world understands his references.Xiao FanThe protagonist of this xianxia-like world, whether he realizes it or not.He was born with a low-grade spiritual root in a cultivator family, so most people expected very little from him. Xiao Fan refused to accept that. He trained anyway, fought anyway, and kept moving forward even when everyone around him looked down on him.Xiao Fan is reckless and stubborn. He helps people even when it puts him in danger, which is why trouble follows him everywhere. Sometimes he finds enemies. Sometimes he finds treasures. Usually, he finds both.His mysterious technique allows him to refine the bloodline abilities of spiritual beasts. Because of that, his path is dangerous and unusual, but it also gives him a way to grow stronger despite his weak spiritual root.To Ning, Xiao Fan is a useful ally, a decent person, and someone he should never travel with unless absolutely necessary.Ling XinLing Xin is a peak master, an alchemist, and Ning's master. She is brilliant, but also dangerous in the way powerful alchemists often are. Her experiments can explode, and she treats that like a normal part of research. Ling Xin accepts Ning as her disciple after seeing his preparation and way of thinking. Under her, Ning finally begins learning real alchemy. She gives Ning a chance to step into a much bigger world, though whether that world is safe is another matter.Ling YinranLing Yinran is Ling Xin's niece and Ning's senior sister. She is talented and more mature than her aunt. Her first meeting with Ning goes badly because she mistakes him for an intruder and attacks before fully understanding the situation. She means well, but she can get flustered when things go wrong, especially around Ling Xin.
