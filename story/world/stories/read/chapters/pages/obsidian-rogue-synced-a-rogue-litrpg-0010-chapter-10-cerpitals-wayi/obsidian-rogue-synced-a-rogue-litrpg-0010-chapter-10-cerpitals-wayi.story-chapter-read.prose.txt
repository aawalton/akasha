"Arggh," Levi groaned as he lay on the dirt floor staring at the blue box hovering in the air that contained Cerpitals' messages.

Healing?

A wave of warmth coursed through his body at the thought; the areas around his injuries growing uncomfortably hot as they were fixed. His dislocated shoulder seemed to have been yanked into place by an invisible force. A silent scream escaped his lips at the fixing of his shoulder. The cut underneath his eyes and the one to his side were also uncomfortably hot. It felt like little invisible hot needles were being thrust into his skin over and over again, and the rogue couldn't help the sigh of relief that left his lips as the burning sensation subsided. The pain of his injuries that he'd pushed to the back of his mind suddenly vanished alongside the uncomfortable sensation.

"Some healing, right?" Levi said to the corpse of the Krul chief gremlin to his side.

He chuckled at the absurdity of his situation; he'd somehow managed to survive soloing a dungeon on his first attempt. It'd been difficult, sure, but he'd found a way to survive, and now he was talking to the corpse of the dungeon boss. Laughing quietly at the craziness of the whole thing, he pushed up from the floor with both hands. His right arm felt tingly from whatever Cerpital had done to heal it, but he didn't mind. A finger to the area where the gremlin had cut him on his face, revealed a lack of scarring.

A hand to his previously injured side revealed the lack of a scar as well, which Levi was grateful for. Well, considering his current predicament, he didn't really care about a scar if he received healing, but Cerpital had seen fit to heal him without leaving a scar. Although his injuries were gone, the bone-deep exhaustion remained. It seemed like he would have to deal with that on his own. With a reluctant sigh, he turned to survey his handiwork, his gaze skipping over the two blue portals that had appeared, choosing to focus on those he'd slain tonight.

The bodies of dead gremlins lay strewn about the small room, with the rogue responsible for their deaths, directly or indirectly. His eyes drifted off to the gremlin, which had its head at an awkward angle. Its neck had been snapped from the strike of the chief gremlin's cudgel, which had been aimed at him. A tight smile appeared on his face as he considered all the different ways his plan could've gone wrong.

Too many.

There had been too many ways his plan could've gone south, but his luck had shown up again. A second for the night and one that was much appreciated. With his eyes still on the gremlin with a snapped neck, Levi thought back to his mad plan. On the surface level, it was straightforward. All he had to do was evade the underling's attacks long enough to frustrate it into attacking wildly; then, if he was lucky, the Krul chief gremlin would step in to finish the drawn-out fight.

From there it was simply a matter of timing himself properly, and in the underling's bid to kill him by any means necessary, it'd become oblivious to all else around it, and that was when the chief gremlin came in. Levi had guessed that the chief gremlin wouldn't pause its attack on him even if it's underling was at risk of being caught in the attack. It was a guess that had turned out to be bang on the mark. The chief gremlin had ended up killing its underling, and that silver of a second between its cudgel hitting its underling's neck gave Levi the opening he'd used to kill the Krul chief gremlin.

It seemed like a mad plan, but it had worked, and Levi wouldn't downplay the effects of his turn around in luck. If the underling had managed to evade the Krul chief's attack just as he had, then he'd have lost his life, but it hadn't, and so the rogue's gamble had paid off. The rogue peeled his eyes from the corpse of the dead underling as he returned his gaze to the dead krul Chief gremlin. His dagger was sticking out of the corpse's chest, and the chief gremlin's cudgel was on the floor beside it.

Dispelling his rampant thoughts, he focused on the present, the rogue forcing himself to move towards the chief gremlin's corpse. In the absence of the adrenaline rush he'd had earlier, his exhaustion hit him like an invisible cudgel. His movements were now sluggish; his legs felt like lead, and his shoulders sagged as well. But regardless of how tired he felt, Levi knew that the night wasn't over. As he reached the gremlin's corpse, he pulled his dagger free from its chest and stood up.

Despite his body's protests, the rogue carried on with his next task: finding his other dagger, which had fallen to the floor when the chief gremlin's cudgel had struck his right shoulder. It didn't take long for him to retrieve the second dagger, and he was just about ready to leave. However, before he did that, he still had a couple of things to attend to.

Dungeon timer?

A part of him wondered what would happen to him if he were still inside the dungeon when it finally reset. With a halfhearted shrug, he pushed that thought to the side as he focused on what was truly important.
