The sun was directly overhead, and the fresh air of the morning had been replaced by a warm, humid atmosphere. The entire forest was filled with a constant, high-pitched buzz of insects. Birds were somewhat quiet, though continuous chirping could still be heard here and there.

Deep in the forest, many animals and evolved animals were hunting for food. The roars and cries of animals could sometimes be heard echoing from far away in the vast canopy.

Intense sunlight entered the dense forest from above, highlighting the huge tree trunks and thick undergrowth. Bright sunrays illuminated the forest floor, making everything properly visible in the region.

The air carried a strong scent of warm soil and decaying leaves. Now, the last of the morning's dew was gone, leaving cobwebs dry with the spiders clinging to them. Dust motes flew everywhere in the rays of light.

The dense forest had a serene view, but with dangers lurking everywhere.

In the forest, a black cat slowly walked through the undergrowth. Her ears twitched, constantly scanning for sound, and her nose tested the air.

She was breathing heavily, desperately wanting water, but there was no water source nearby. The lack of water was a burning need. She needed to visit her previous hideout to drink.

A little girl gave her food and water every morning and evening, and had even left a container for her to drink from.

Her previous hideout was far away. It would take about half an hour of running to reach the town from her new den. But she was even farther away now.

She had been chased by a pair of strong rats. The rats, being territorial, were a formidable threat, especially in numbers.

Forget constantly running for half an hour; she couldn’t even walk properly due to her exhaustion.

To get away from the rats, she had to run in the opposite direction from her hideout so they couldn't follow her easily. Even after successfully escaping, her instincts told her to stay away from the rats' territory, and moreover, she was too tired to circle back.

In the end, she decided to climb a tree to rest on the highest branch for a while. The highest branch, due to elevation, would keep her safe from most ground predators.

Even the climb seemed like hard work for the cat. She waited below a tree for a few minutes to catch her breath, then, with a quick movement, used her sharp claws to dig into the bark and scaled the trunk to the highest branch.

She was breathing heavily again, her whole body exhausted. After a while, she fell asleep. She was too tired to stay awake right now. The highest branch of the tree seemed to be the safest place for now. A quick nap would suffice; then she could visit her den to feed her kittens and the odd little creature.

After a short nap, the black cat woke up. Her ears swiveled, listening intently, and her muscles tensed. She stretched her body, then started to descend, backing down the trunk with her claws extended. The descent was not as swift as climbing the tree and took a bit of time.

After climbing down, she started circling the rats' territory and went to her den. She kept low to the ground, her belly nearly touching the soil, to avoid detection.

While she was leisurely running to her hideout, a rat was sniffing here and there, its whiskers twitching and constantly testing the air and the ground for scent. One of its eyes was scratched. Its sight was not impaired, but the wound around its eye looked painful. It was a new wound.

Thankfully, it was not deep, and the pupils were not scratched. The rat's vision was intact. Fortunately, only the skin around its eye had three parallel scratches.

It was the wound the black cat had created when the large rat initially started climbing the tree to hunt her down. Its partner was not with it. The partner was probably taking care of its babies while this one followed the scent to take revenge.

When the black cat attacked, they did everything they could to save their little one, but the cat killed its youngest baby instantly. There was no time to save it.

More than that, getting injured and being toyed with by that fearful cat didn't sit well with the evolved rat. After the rats evolved, they gained significant strength and intelligence, which also came with a little bit of human-level emotion. This fueled its desire to pay back the cat tenfold.

During its search, the large rat lost the scent many times. It even climbed a tree once but immediately got even angrier, thinking that even while searching, it was being fooled. This was where the black cat was sleeping earlier.

By sundown, it had walked several miles searching for the cat.

Since predators of rodents become more active in the evening, and its baby needed it more, it decided to search a bit more until dusk and then head back if it didn't find its nemesis by then.

—Inside the new den—

The black cat reached her den in the afternoon. Her babies were extremely hungry, their tiny meows sounding like frantic, desperate pleas. She hadn't intended to stay out this long, but the rats had forced her to.

After reaching the den, she fed her kittens and the new baby creature, then went to sleep. The previous short nap was enough to get her home, but it wasn't enough to remove her exhaustion entirely.

She needed sleep more than anything. She could get food from the little girl in the evening, so without worry, she curled up to sleep, her body still aching.

A while later, she perked up her ears, one swiveling forward while the other stayed back, a natural predator's instinct. Someone was moving closer to her den. She stood and looked out of the opening. The rustling sound of the forest undergrowth increased from the front. Someone was definitely coming to her den.

Then a rat appeared. Its eyes were red, its pupils dilated with a mix of rage and focus. One side of its face was swollen, and one of its eyes had three parallel scratches. Though its vision was not hindered, it was clear that it was in a lot of pain.

It was the male rat which the black cat had faced just a few hours ago. The black cat understood it had come here to hunt her. She stood in her den, glaring from just inside the opening, her tail lashing back and forth, ready to launch herself forward if the rat attacked directly.

After facing the pair of rats, the cat knew that this one had only one proper attack pattern. And that is to charge at full speed and bite upon contact.

Behind the black cat, her kittens were sleeping, and in front of her, the rat was preparing to charge, its body low to the ground and its tail straight and stiff. It was impossible to dodge. If she dodged, the rat would come straight in and hit all her babies. The black cat didn't know what to do except to take a direct hit and engage in close-quarter combat. She was prepared, her muscles coiled like springs.

The rat charged straight, and the black cat expected it. She also launched herself forward to intercept. Just after she exited her den, she collided with the rat, and it immediately bit the black cat’s neck. The force of the charge was so much that the cat was instantly thrown back into her den, hitting the opposite wall and falling upon her babies, who were sleeping in the ditch.

The rat also entered the den with its charging momentum and stood there with a satisfied look, its nose twitching to locate its prey. The black cat's chest bones were cracked, and her spine was hurting from where she hit the wall. The babies started mewling in pain as the cat fell on them.

She opened her eyes, and the first thing she saw was the rat about to launch another attack while she heard her babies crying. She immediately stood up, pushing her legs outside the ditch. Another attack would surely kill her and all her babies. Instinctively, she ran forward before the rat could attack, a blur of black fur.

The rat was startled by the sudden turn of events. Not only was the cat bleeding from her neck, but she had also been hit hard. The rat was sure it was a killing blow; it couldn't understand how the cat was still attacking.

The rat tried to dodge to the right by jumping, but it was not as agile as the cat. The cat instantly changed her direction, bit the rat's snout, and tore it off its face. Blood gushed out like a fountain. The rat started thrashing in the center of the den, its screams of pain a high-pitched squeal. Then the black cat attacked the thrashing rat from above and scratched deeply into both pupils.

Now the rat was thrashing even more, moving and rolling here and there, disoriented and in agony. It accidentally jumped onto the kittens and thrashed upon them. The cat's eyes widened in horror. Again, she immediately launched herself and went to the side to push her head against the thrashing rat to push it aside. It was successful.

The cat looked at her limp babies and thought about what to do. She had no idea how to cure them. The mewls of their pain had filled the den. Then she suddenly remembered that when she was bitten by a dog and lost consciousness, she was miraculously cured and woke up to see humans.

Back then, she didn't think much of it, but now she was considering that maybe, just maybe, it was they who had cured her condition.

She decided to take a chance, as she had nothing else she could think of at the moment. But how could she take the babies? It was too far away, and they might not make it if she took them one by one. And there was still the thrashing rat in her den.

It couldn't be helped; she needed to eliminate the immediate danger. She was in unbearable pain, but she resolved to start with the rat.

She jumped on the rat and bit its neck from behind. It started thrashing even harder and jumped up hard. Both hit the ceiling of the den and fell to the ground. The cat then attacked from the side, bit the rat's neck, and started tearing apart its flesh, slowly finishing it off. The rat started running while dragging the cat, who kept tearing at its flesh and swallowing without a second thought.

The rat, blinded and in pain, hit the wall with its head. Then the rat started running in another direction and hit the wall again. It tried running in another direction but hit the wall again. The rat became permanently blind because the cat had intentionally aimed at its pupils. After getting hit three times, the rat fell down. It had lost too much blood, but it was still not dead yet. The rat was evolved, and the elemental energy it possessed was keeping it alive even when it was limp.

After biting and swallowing a lot of flesh, the cat reached its chest from the neck. The cat was so engrossed in her process that she didn't even know where she was tearing off flesh from. After swallowing a lot of flesh, she felt like swallowing a small bone piece; then the rat finally died.

Immediately after swallowing something like a bone, the cat started feeling as if her injuries were healing, and she felt like she had gained energy to reach the town. She stood up but suddenly realized the dead body of the rat, with its strong scent of blood, would surely bring predators to her den. This was dangerous for her kittens.

The cat held the rat with her mouth and started dragging it outside, her jaw aching from the effort. After walking for a few minutes, she left the dead rat in the forest and went back to her den. She looked at her babies.

The biggest one seemed the most injured and wasn't even mewling in pain. She picked up the biggest one, which was not even making any noise anymore, and started running toward the town.

It was far away, but the other kittens were at least mewling in pain. They might hold out long enough until she could bring the human back to her hideout.

The black cat started running toward her old den, her focus singular, her instincts overriding all caution. She ran directly through the borders of other predators' territories, the scents of a fox, a wolf, and other evolved creatures all around her. She wasn't crossing through the center of their territories. Doing this would surely make them attack her. It was the last thing she wanted right now.

She was running fast, and moreover, she ran through the border of other predators’ territories. After about a quarter of an hour, the black cat reached the border of the town. The gate was open, and people were constantly going in and out. There was a huge line to enter the town.

The black cat started running toward the gate without looking anywhere, her mind solely focused on the task at hand. This was her mistake.

Suddenly, she was hit so hard that she was thrown far away, and her baby fell from her mouth and was also thrown aside.

"Now it feels better... Dumb cats and dogs always poop in front of my door," a man said, looking at the black cat he had just kicked far away.

"What are you doing, man? Get in line," a person who was standing in the line said to the man who had suddenly stepped out.

"Come on... Don't you hate dumb cats and dogs? Always creating trouble here and there." The man outside the line said while going back to his position.

"HEY, get in line, or I’ll arrest you for making a scene at the gate." This time, a town guard said with an annoyed expression. He always had to deal with such people at the gate. He wanted to arrest the man after what he did, but because they were short-staffed, he was too busy to take action.

The guard had seen the black cat carrying its kitten last time, so he had left her alone. But now, seeing the same cat get kicked—a cat he himself hadn't bothered—pissed him off. (Next time he does that in front of me, I'll surely make him have a good time in prison.)

"Cooome oon… whyyyyy" saying this, the man then went silent and backed away to the line. He didn't even know he was spared.
