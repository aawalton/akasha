A blast of orange fire tore across the sanctuary.

Hevestra slipped aside before the spell could strike, its body twisting into black smoke for a single breath before reforming several meters away. The flames passed through the empty mist and crashed into one of the remaining pillars near the edge of the platform. Stone exploded outward, scattering chunks across the uprooted ground of the sanctuary.

Before all the debris landed, Hevestra raised one clawed hand.

The shadows beneath Veronica’s feet sharpened.

Her eyes narrowed as she leapt back as black spikes erupted from the ground, each one thin, jagged, and curved like hooked talons. They tore through the spot where she had been standing, then bent toward her midair.

She erected a mana barrier immediately, stopping the shadow strikes before they could gain momentum. They rang against her shield like iron nails on glass. Her shield shuddered, but held. Veronica channeled her mana to counterattack, but Hevestra had already moved.

Her instincts screamed at her. There was no sight nor sound she could sense. However, the instant she felt the air shift, she reacted using Flow State. Her actions coincided with her reactions, both moving in parallel.

Veronica turned, swiping forward with her hand and summoning a bolt of lightning from the heavens to strike down in front of her. She saw the black speck that was Hevestra, right before the lightning struck and detonated. Dust billowed upward as arcs crackled from the explosion’s center.

She leapt back. Her eyes swept the area in front of her.

Through the smoke, she noticed a blue light. Large, solid, and completely spherical. She recognized it immediately.

“Seriously… why is something like this here…”

Hevestra appeared through the smoke, protected by a large barrier of mana.

“Surprised, aren’t you?” Hevestra said. Its voice grated deep within its throat as it spoke. “Although inferior, it certainly has its uses.”

“Who are you, exactly?” Veronica asked.

She kept her stance alert. At any time, the demon in front of her could do something unexpected. Regardless of its intelligence, it was still a demon. Cunning and greedy. She would never underestimate what foul things they were capable of.

“Hahah. Haven’t I already—”

It blinked forward as a trail of smoke, closing the distance between them within heartbeats.

Veronica, however, was fully focused. Flow State activated, allowing her to react immediately without wait.

Just as Hevestra’s long claws swiped at her, Veronica reinforced her barrier.

The impact slammed into her shield, knocking her backward. She hit the ground, boots scraping across the uneven stone as she forced herself to stop. Her barrier flickered, a portion of it cracked, but it was already being repaired.

“Oh?” it hummed. “You survived that.”

Veronica clicked her tongue, staring back at it.

Hevestra rubbed beneath its chin with one clawed finger. “You are still emitting so much of that crude mana… how interesting. I wonder what your—”

Veronica didn’t let it continue speaking.

She formed two separate Tier-3 spells: Flare Lance and Vortex.

Thrusting her right hand forward, a spiral of flames whirled out in front of her, twisting and raging straight toward Hevestra. Without skipping a beat, she layered her left hand behind her right, casting another spell.

[Mana: 634/900 MU]

As the flames writhed and twisted toward Hevestra, Veronica’s second spell whipped up a swiftly forming cyclone.

[Mana: 369/900 MU]

Both spells coalesced as the massive torrent of fire was dragged into the eye of the storm, combining and billowing upward as a flaming cyclone.

She freed her hands, pumping mana into her palms again. This time, both hands cast the same spell.

“Everybody! Cover your ears and brace yourselves!” Veronica shouted.

Far removed from the fight, at the edge of the sanctuary, were Dusk, Gwen, and Nasha. Upon hearing her command, they huddled together. Gwen knelt in front of Nasha, temporarily stopping her healing to plug her ears. Dusk concentrated his spiritual energy, forming something akin to a mana barrier. It wasn’t a physical substance that could repel things, but it held enough pressure to simulate one.

[Mana: 270/900 MU]

As soon as Veronica thrust her hands forward, the eye of the storm flashed white beneath the red and orange gales. In that instant, a violent explosion shook the entire sanctuary, rumbling through the ground. The sound erupted, nearly bursting everyone’s eardrums as the echo tore through the air.

Veronica maintained her barrier as a burst of steam rushed past her shield, blinding the world in mist.

Once the explosion stopped, Veronica tried focusing through the steam, looking ahead.

The spells she had just used were simple Tier-3 Path of Ruin spells. However, their output had multiplied together, creating a far more powerful explosive reaction.

A superheated tornado of fire, and a concentrated, conjured block of ice set directly in the eye of the storm. As the outer layer melted, the center turned to water and made contact with the flames. That caused the water to flash boil and expand many times over, creating a large steam explosion.

Through the haze, she spotted the black silhouette of Hevestra.

It was still alive.

However, she also noticed a spot along its body. Slightly discolored. Darker than the rest of its skin. She had managed to create a light wound near what should have been its ribs.

“What destructive power. It almost reminds me of a certain someone,” it rasped.

Veronica narrowed her eyes. She wasn’t expecting that combination to take it down, but at least it confirmed she could injure it.

But that also wasn’t the only thing she had used it for.

Hevestra looked left and right, until it realized where Veronica was. It phased through the smoke, reverting to its shadowy form. It lunged to the side, aiming to flank her. Just as it approached, it reached its claws out to strike.

However, Veronica turned to face the demon almost immediately, reacting despite its enhanced speed and hidden shadow form.

“That won’t work anymore,” Veronica said, voice rough.

She lifted her hand and used another spell. Light converged around Hevestra’s body, growing brighter and brighter before detonating in another explosion.

Hevestra flew backward, shaking its head, stunned and confused by what had happened. As it looked through the dense mist, seeing Veronica’s form approach, a sudden realization came to it.

“I see…” it said slowly. “You formed this mist to slow me down. Not only that, but you can clearly see my shadows as they move through the white steam surrounding the sanctuary.”

Veronica stepped forward. Her body trembled with power as she accelerated her mana rings further, re-accumulating the mana she had just spent.

[Mana: 256/900 MU]

“I’ve fought tricky demons like you before. Ones who love phasing in and out of existence,” Veronica stated. “I admit you’re strong, but I’ll kill you eventually.”

Hevestra cackled. “Arrogance truly befitting your species. What was that saying you humans use?” It rose higher into the air. “Like ants to a lion? Whatever a lion is…”

The demonic miasma around the edge of the sanctuary surged upward with it, coiling around its body like a mantle of black smoke. Its distorted face stretched into a grin, but the amusement in its eyes had sharpened.

It raised one clawed hand.

The black mist surrounding the sanctuary suddenly rushed inward.

Veronica’s eyes narrowed.

The miasma did not simply spread through the steam. It shredded and devoured it. The white haze twisted into the shadows, pulled apart and swallowed by the darkness as if Hevestra had opened a thousand invisible mouths in the air.

The black mist tore through the air, coalescing into a condensed sphere before it. The moment Hevestra clenched its fingers, the miasma collapsed.

A violent pressure burst across the sanctuary. Veronica’s barrier took the brunt of it, but the impact drove her sliding backward across the stone.

On the other side of the sanctuary, Dusk’s spiritual aura trembled as it tried resisting the dark mist flooding toward them. Gwen’s mana barrier from her cloak also activated, helping block as much of it as it could.

“Dusk!” Gwen yelled. Her face turned pale, eyes widening as she noticed the clear field around them shrink.

Dusk’s spiritual aura had grown weaker.

“I’m… okay,” he whispered. His voice was strained, small hands clenched into fists before him. He would last, but several more attacks like that and it would be over for them.

Gwen whipped her head toward Nasha. “Are you okay? Can you move?”

Nasha sat up, breathing heavily, but she was much better than before. Gwen’s restorative magic was taking effect, slowly rebuilding her stamina. However, things would get dangerous once the effect ran out. The more energy supplied, the heavier the crash she’d experience later.

Hevestra and Veronica resumed clashing, explosive spell after explosive spell being thrown against its demonic attacks. Their battle grew wild, forcing Veronica to step back in front of the rest of them. She had to provide her mana barrier to assist, lest the stray blows from their clashes strike their already weakening defenses.

Gwen continued working diligently on Nasha, until she heard a whisper from beside her.

“Energy…”

She turned toward him. “What?”

Dusk’s lips parted slightly, but no sound came out at first. His gaze remained glued to Hevestra. More specifically, to the place where its wound had closed.

“That energy,” he said again. “It’s not his.”

Hevestra’s eyes shifted. For the first time, its smile cracked.

Then it grew even wider.

Veronica felt the change immediately. “Dusk, what do you mean?”

Dusk took a single step forward, and the aura around Gwen and Nasha flickered.

Gwen grabbed his shoulder. “Hey, don’t move!”

Dusk didn’t seem to hear her. His face had gone pale.

“That was Dawn’s.”

For a moment, everything fell silent.

“Are you sure?” Veronica asked. The aura of her exalted form was still crackling around her, the only sound left echoing through the sanctuary. “Are you sure it isn’t just yours? It’s been siphoning from us all this time.”

Dusk trembled. “No… it’s hers.”

Then Hevestra laughed.

It was not the same loud, theatrical cackle as before. This one came out much lower, slower. It was pleased.

“I’m surprised you can still recognize what’s left,” Hevestra said.

It flew higher, raising its claws and ripping a hole through the space before it. A rift opened like a teardrop, spreading wide. Darkness spilled out, but inside was something else. Something dim.

“No…” Dusk whispered.

Veronica narrowed her eyes the moment she realized who it was. The same girl she had seen inside Dusk’s memory. However…

“What did you do to Dawn?!” Dusk shouted.

At first, Veronica had thought it was a corpse. But no. That wasn’t completely accurate.

Dawn’s body rested inside the void, a wound stretched across reality. She was suspended there, arms, legs, and throat all bound by shadows, nearly wrapping around her entire body.

If… you could call it a body.

Her form was half-ethereal, similar to Veronica’s body when she had dove into Dusk’s consciousness. Her lower half was completely withered like a ghost, while the upper half of her body was still barely solid. She was like a mana crystal, half-drained of power.

Hevestra’s smile returned. “Dawn. Yes. That was her name, wasn’t it?” it said.

It turned to face Dawn, dragging one claw down the front of the rift. “It’s been so long that I had forgotten her name.” Then it turned once more to face them. “Funny, isn’t it? Even after destroying this valley, her body was just lying there. Unbothered and unfettered. After expending all of that power, she still had enough left to feed me for centuries.”

Dusk’s aura shrank another step.

The miasma rushed inward.

Gwen gritted her teeth and rushed over to Dusk, holding him back. Her cloak activated its mana barrier one more time to help block the intruding miasma. “Dusk, breathe,” she said sharply. “Look at me. Don’t let it fool you.”

But Dusk couldn’t look away. For the past thousand years, he had been searching. And his most precious treasure was right in front of him.

Dying.

Veronica narrowed her eyes. She couldn’t move away from them too easily. Hevestra’s goal was to absorb all of their power. If she gave it an opening, it would take it.

“Are you the one who destroyed the sanctuary back then?” Veronica asked.

“Destroyed?” Hevestra repeated. It chuckled. “Please. I don’t have the power to do such a thing.”

Its clawed fingers reached toward Dawn’s cheek. “It was this little spirit. She managed to detonate the entire valley. All. By. Herself.” Hevestra faced them again. “I merely came in later. It was the opportunity of a lifetime for a demon like me. I had found a bountiful feast.”

The atmosphere around the sanctuary began to change. “But all good meals end someday…” it said solemnly. Its cold eyes stared directly toward Dusk. “Little spirit. How about a deal?”

“A deal?” Dusk repeated.

“Don’t listen to him,” Veronica said. “It’s trying to trick you.”

“Yeah! Don’t listen to it! It’s trying to kill all of us, Dusk,” Gwen chimed.

“Kill?” Hevestra tilted its head. “What a wasteful word. I have no desire to kill him.”

The demon’s gaze slid toward Dusk, lingering on him with a hunger far worse than simple bloodlust.

“Not immediately, at least.”

Dusk’s hands trembled at his sides.

Hevestra’s clawed fingers tapped against the edge of the rift. Every tap made the shadows binding Dawn pulse, and every pulse made her half-ethereal form flicker weaker than before. “She’s grown less useful to me after all these years. A half-dead spirit like this has lost its value.”

Hevestra stared straight into Dusk’s eyes. “How about you take her place? Your life. For hers.”
