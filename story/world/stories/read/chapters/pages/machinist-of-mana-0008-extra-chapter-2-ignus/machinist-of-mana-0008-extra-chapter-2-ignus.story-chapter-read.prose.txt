Ignus

Today was, well, another day. I leaned back in my chair, rubbing my head.

“I'm getting too old for this,” I told my assistant, a wonderful young woman who looked like she might cut someone if they got too close.

“Frankly sir, I think you've got a few years left of it, though you could retire couldn't you?”“True... but you're not quite ready to handle this yet. Tell me, what do you make of this,” I asked her, pushing a file across my desk.

“A rifle that, fires multiple rounds in sequence? We have those?” she asked.

“Not like this, look at the rate of fire, look at the loading.”

“Hundreds of rounds per minute!? The design looks simple enough...”“Our engineers are losing their minds, anyone could build this! You could build it with some tools and a bit of experience, and I know you have never worked in a machine shop.”“Should I be insulted?” she asked, narrowing her eyes.

“No, not at all Carla. Anyone could, you'd succeed faster, because you're smart and dedicated enough. But anyone could, any person, no matter how foolish could follow the directions and have a weapon better than what we issue to our finest soldiers!”

“My apologies sir, I'm not terribly well versed in firearms beyond the standard issue ones. Nor in battle lines, what will this do?”“I don't know, and that's the worst part. It WILL change things, but how? Not even sure our current military knows. Damn it all, today was looking to be a good day and now this. “Probably why they are so thorough sir,” Carla deadpanned.

With another huff I went down to the actual loading dock to get eyes on one of these things. She followed along, and we soon found ourselves at the box Percival had sent. No longer a child the young man had developed a knack for sending odd things through, and we'd long suspected he had some odd things. There were remnants from his old home, some of which didn't match anything we had, but as I removed the first of the new weapons from the box it pinged on some of the memories.

The files we had gave basic instructions and with a few practiced moves I loaded the rifle, aiming it down at the target, and very careful not to harm myself. Surely our benefactor had tested these, he always tested everything.
