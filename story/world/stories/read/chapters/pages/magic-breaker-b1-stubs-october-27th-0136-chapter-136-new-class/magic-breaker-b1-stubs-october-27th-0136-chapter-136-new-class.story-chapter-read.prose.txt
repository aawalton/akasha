Excluding some is easy. I don’t have much interest in moonlit dervish, or trapper. Healer feels too underwhelming, and sorcerer too ambiguous. Deathseer feels too nice, I don’t really need premonitions, and darktamer is out because I want Kuro to be themselves. No taming needed.

Dreadhunter is the one I drop next. It’s been a running theme across these past floors, hunting and being hunted. But I don’t want to make that my class. I will, if I must. And from there, finally, the choices get a lot harder.

Modifier is interesting, because I think it encourages me to experiment with skills. Deadzone sounds like the natural evolution of my current nullmage. Copycat probably consumed analyst, keeping the theme of understanding, and perhaps stealing, other people’s skills. And, of course, there’s vesselsmith. More mana.

The one I drop first is copycat. Other people’s skills are interesting. They’re excellent references for developing my own. But copying them exactly? That seems wasteful. No, I need skills that express me. Whose meaning I can interpret and decide for myself.

Modifier drops out of the race next, because despite how much I enjoy changing my skills around, it is something that I can, in part, do with my mantle. I already am an architect, so it feels a little redundant. Perhaps I’m missing out on synergy, but that’s fine.

Which leaves me with deadzone and vesselsmith.

The former is almost certainly a higher tier class. Stronger in most ways. More focused, more powerful, giving me more stats. It sounds strong. Really dang strong.

Which is why I don’t pick it.

Vesselsmith sounds like a class that knows what it’s about. It’s a foundational class. An enabler-type class. Anything I pick in the future will be all the stronger for it, because I can build myself up. I can make my vessel stronger. If I have more mana, stronger mana, when I pick up deadzone on a future class change… then it’ll be all the more powerful.

So, I choose vesselsmith. I choose to lay the foundations for myself, to start building more momentum as I climb.

Still, there is one more thing my new class owes me…

[Skill bestowed: Vessel Construction lv. 0]

That’s the final piece. Essence from the sponsorship still flows into me, whispering about the skill - so I can make sure not to kill myself the first time I use it. I tilt my head a little at the Vesselsmith, but then shake it. Yes, the Eye did help me, but it also just wants to see me experiment. See if I can find out something new - then it’ll probably try to get that information out of me.

Still. At least it’ll help me survive. I probably wouldn’t have died, even without the extra essence.

… Maybe.

Surely.

Regardless, with my class selection finished up, I have seen all my notifications. I can finally lean back, breathe and- Oh who am I kidding. Without hesitation, I activate my new skill, feeding it mana and lifeblood.

[Vessel Construction 0 > 1]

Instantly, my insides burn. My mana turns on itself like a parasite. My vessel feeds on me, devouring bits of flesh like a hungry monster. Pain arcs through me at my horrible use of the skill, even when guided by essence… but it’s just pain. It was just a tiny bit of mana.

I feel things shift inside me. My vessel growing more robust even as the rest of me decays. Shifting, changing. The skill isn’t quite accurate in its naming, I suppose. It really is more like forging.

When I trigger the skill, my own blood burns to “heat up” my vessel. After that, it becomes malleable. My mana can press on it from the inside, and expand it. Physically, it takes up the same space, but metaphysically? I can expand it.

But that’s not all. That’d stretch the walls thin. No, I also need to mend any cracks that come from this, weaving new material by blending mana with my blood. It’s more draining, too, drawing on some kind of stamina I know I have, even if there’s no nice list for it.

Then, once the cracks are mended, I need to go into tempering, make sure it cools down universally, preparing it for another round of treatment in the future…

It’s fucking complicated. What a horrendously botched skill. So many layers in that single ability, and in exchange, the price. I cannot use it on anyone outside of me. The mastery required is far too high. It’s purely internal, purely for myself. It’s brutally complex, with enough steps to make my eyes bleed just trying to envision it properly.

In other words: I love it. Couldn’t be happier. For one final time, I call up my status.
