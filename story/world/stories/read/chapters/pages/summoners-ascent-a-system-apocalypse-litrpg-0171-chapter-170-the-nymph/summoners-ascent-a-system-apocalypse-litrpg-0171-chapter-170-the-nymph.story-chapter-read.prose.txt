Ashton smiled as he looked at the nymph in front of him. She was a powerful fairy, so it probably wasn't a bad idea to treat her with as much respect as he could.

"Thank you for opening the sanctuary. I've been looking for a safe place for this treasury pixie."

The nymph briefly looked Ashton's way, though she seemed to want to dismiss him immediately as well. Ashton figured that was fine, but there was someone else that simply wouldn't have it.

Poppy quickly fluttered in front of Ashton, pushing her hands onto her hips. She was saying something to the nymph, who simply laughed in response. The sound of ringing bells was suppressed by that of rainfall on water.

Poppy seemed even more annoyed by that, and flew closer. She showed her dress to the nymph, and pointed at Ashton. At this point, the nymph started to seem a bit more responsive, as Poppy continued talking and talking. Ashton felt a little awkward just standing here while the pixie was standing up for him, but he figured he shouldn't interrupt whatever was happening.

And then, a few moments later, Poppy flew over to Ashton and started pulling on the string that was used to tie together the top of his cloak. Getting the clue, he sighed and pulled the cloak off.

Poppy continued talking, and then fluttered up to the top of his head, pulling the hair away from his face.

The nymph leaned forward, trying to get a closer look at Ashton. But it seemed like she noticed something, and was trying to motion something to him. She was asking him to take his mask off.

He was still a little confused, but soon pulled the mask onto his hair, keeping his eyes shut so that the nymph didn't have to see what was really going on there.

And the nymph was stunned.

"Uhm... what's going on?" Ashton asked, and Poppy quickly flew up to him, seemingly understanding why the nymph wasn't saying anything anymore.

"You want me to introduce myself?"

Poppy immediately nodded, and the nymph's back straightened as she eagerly waited.

"My name is Ashton Garling. I... used to live in this house, but after my parents passed, I wasn't allowed to stay here anymore," he explained, but was barely able to finish speaking when he started feeling the push of numerous pixies on his back.

They weren't exactly strong, but he figured it wouldn't hurt to play along a bit. He walked forward into the pond. It was damn cold, and he could feel the algae sticking to his trousers already, but it was a somehow nice sensation. Ashton pushed aside the lily pads in front of him, and soon was submerged up to his belly-button. He could easily dry his clothes later with the help of the undine, so this was alright. He might not quite get the pond smell out, though.

By now, Ashton stood in front of the nymph, his eyes still closed shut. The fae stretched out her hands to him, and slowly placed her hands onto his cheeks. She moved his head around like she was trying to get a real close look, and even pulled Ashton's jaw down to see inside his mouth.

But in the end, she just ran her hand through Ashton's hair, smiling as she pulled him closer. He could feel himself being pushed out of the water from below, and soon was dragged onto the large lily pad that the nymph was sitting on. It was a weirdly familiar feeling.

As he knelt in front of her, the fairy pulled him close to her chest, placing her hands onto his back.

She was hugging Ashton, and it was one filled with numerous emotions. Too many for him to understand like this.

"What's... what's happening?" Ashton asked, seriously confused. And the nymph pulled him around with a sort of physical strength he couldn't resist, until he was sitting on her lap with her arms still wrapped around him like a harness keeping him safe.

Compared to before, the nymph's voice sounded a lot clearer. Instead of the sound of rain falling on a pond, it was more like a single drop splashing onto a still, pure surface of water to create that high-pitched 'plop' sound. It repeated, with more and more drops trickling in, as the pond's water started to shift, pushing together some of the lily pads.

Quickly, a few sprites stepped onto them, while the rest of the fae gathered around the pond's edge, watching excitedly.

These living shadows quickly took the form of two people. They were the size of pixies, if not smaller, but it was still easy enough to tell their form when Ashton shifted his channelling range just right.

Three pixies came flying in, carrying an old cardboard box before placing it down on the lily pads, the flaps creating a roof as they leaned against each other.

One of the sprites began to hold the other as they looked at their new home. Soon, another sprite came along, first starting out as a small blob and then growing slowly larger and larger. And a second joined in as well.

The four sprites lived together for a while, until the 'son' sprite decided to leave without even turning back to his parents. A little while later, the 'daughter' sprite left as well, though she at least looked back and waved at the two remaining sprites as they grew older and weaker.

Until finally, the two sprites hugged, and as they pulled away from each other, a new tiny blob appeared. Ashton smiled lightly, watching as the sprites replayed the first few years of his life. It was in much more detail than the rest so far.

His first steps, the first time he played football with his father, and then, sooner or later, when his mother passed. The sprite simply faded away, leaving Ashton and his father alone.

The weak father sprite tried his best to take proper care of Ashton, and continued to show him as much love as his old body still had left within it. But his father never had to worry at all; Ashton never felt anything but a bottomless love from his parents. And he would know. He remembered practically every moment he had with them.

But then, as Ashton grew a little more, the sprite representing his father hugged him one last time and then disappeared, leaving Ashton alone in front of this old house.

At least for a moment, until the 'son' sprite from earlier returned, placing a hand onto Ashton's shoulder... before the cardboard box collapsed in on itself as Ashton was dragged away by his brother.

He sat there for a few moments, just staring at the sprites that were now gathering back on top of the lily pads.

"That was... good. I could tell exactly what you were all going for," Ashton smiled lightly, though it was horrendously forced. He knew that he shouldn't lie in this moment, so he tried to tell the truth as much as he could, but that didn't mean that he had to feel happy about it.

The nymph squeezed Ashton a little more tightly. The expression on her own face was melancholic, to say the least.

"You directed them, right? Does that mean you've been here ever since then?" Ashton asked. He could sense the nymph's expression shift in a complicated manner. "Not that simple, huh?"

The nymph shook her head.

"You know, I met this little girl, and she has the ability to see things that others can't. She said that she saw something sleeping back here once. Were you able to peek out sometimes? Were you able to just tell the memories of this place? I know some fae have that sort of ability."

The nymph slowly let go of Ashton, but seemed to slowly guide him to turn around. He kneeled in front of her again, and through the wisp, watched as the nymph lowered her head toward Ashton's.

Their foreheads touched, just ever so slightly, and Ashton could feel something from her. It was like she was trying to make him remember something. And she did.

It was a memory that he didn't even realize he didn't have until now. With how much had been flooding his head recently, it was hard to keep track.

Ashton had met this nymph before. Though, she probably wasn't the same back then as she was now.

Once, when Ashton was still extremely young, maybe four of five, he was playing out here in the garden, at the edge of the pond. Usually, his parents would watch him eagerly, like every single second was the most precious gift in the universe to them.

But still, they weren't the youngest, both in their mid-to-late sixties at the time. So, they took a small break this one hot summer, going inside to grab something to drink.

And almost the moment they were gone inside, though everything was ready to be taken from the kitchen already and they could have only been gone for at most a minute. But Ashton fell into the pond.

Or maybe he tried to swim in it. Frankly, his thought process didn't really make much sense, even in retrospect. Maybe there wasn't any thought behind it at all. Not that it really mattered, because Ashton couldn't swim yet. He had always been slightly behind some of the other kids when it came to that kind of thing.

And though Ashton sank to the bottom of the pond, he wasn't flailing or trying to get out in any way. He was just sinking, looking up at the light shining through the duckweed. Ashton could remember how beautiful that was back then.

But then, he was saved by something. By someone. Ashton was pushed back up out of the water, like two arms scooped him up from below, bringing him safely back to the edge of the pond.

Ashton could still remember the expression of his father as he ran straight at him, only halfway to the pond by now. His mother was just a few steps behind his father. But both of them weren't close enough to have saved Ashton.

He focused on the nymph, smiling lightly.

"That was you?" Ashton asked. "You pushed me out of the water back then?"

The nymph nodded with a happy smile on her face. That expression felt affectionate in a way that Ashton didn't see often from a fae to a non-fae, at least not from ones of this stature.

Sure, once Ashton got his appearance under control, plenty of fae would swoon over him, and they were generally quite friendly, but this was more than that. This nymph was looking at Ashton the way a parent looked at their child.

"I don't know why you decided to save my life, or how that was even possible, but thank you," Ashton smiled, bowing his head ever so slightly, but the nymph just pulled him back in.

She scooped up some water with her hand, and poured it over his head.

"Uhm..." Ashton tried to protest, but the nymph just happily kept going. She was cleaning Ashton up. He was covered in blood and dirt and grime, and hadn't had the time to clean himself since yesterday at the costco. He really must be a mess right now.

Figuring that there was almost no hope to 'escape' from this gesture, Ashton decided to just sit there for a little while and let the nymph do as she wanted.

He could still spare a few minutes before he had to leave.
