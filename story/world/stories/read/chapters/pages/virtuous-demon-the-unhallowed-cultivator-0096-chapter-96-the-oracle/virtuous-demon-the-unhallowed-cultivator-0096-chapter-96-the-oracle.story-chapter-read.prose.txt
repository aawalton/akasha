No matter which world, which era, and which life, there was always a law that held true throughout it all.

Fortune lies in misfortune; misfortune lurks in fortune.

“Good! Good! Good! Indeed, in the realm where the soul is reposed, my lost name evokes the gaze of… Well, I shan’t prod it.”

Tez’s golden pupils constricted, but not from the pain of the sword that bloomed from his body. Something had been lost, something extremely vital to his very being.

The notion of this life being a game dissipated with it.

The System… It’s gone? No, it can’t be!

“How disappointing. Without that Oracle of Yonder, you have lost all of your composure,” The radiant invader bluntly said, gazing at the sky as if to sense something.

Tez groaned, as a blade grew out of his shoulder. A second burst out of his leg.

A blade bloomed out of his heart.

“Is that all you can bear? Did you not desire that power for yourself?” The radiant invader ridiculed Tez, with each word of his forming a tempest in this realm.

He took another step forth.

Tez keeled over, barely holding his composure as more shards of sword tips burst from his body. He winced as he gazed at the man, clenching his jaw as tightly as he could. He’d gone through enough pain, now wasn’t the time to scream, when his soul was on the line.

With just a thought, his sword had manifested in his hand.

“What… Is this a dream, or is it just my imagination? Wait…”

Tez now thought of the armour he wore, and in that same thought, it manifested on his body.

The radiant invader only looked up, watching the sky slowly be eaten away by that cosmic darkness.

He only smiled with anticipation.

Tez had realised instantly. This must’ve been some sort of battle in his inner mind. If that was so, then…

I don’t know how he did, but he actually brought the System out from me. But… this is my domain, and I still have one advantage here!

Tez brought out his unblemished and unbladed arm, clenching his fist except for his thumb which stood out like a tree.

“Hey, you ever heard of a Tachyon Railgun?” Tez asked, pressing his thumb down into his fist like he was pressing a button.

“Oh? You understand the difference between the Inner World and the Realm of Repose?” The radiant young man invader didn’t understand Tez’s words, but realised the intent behind Tez as he stilled. He faced behind him, to gaze at the far horizon.

The remnants of a civilisation unlike his own world. The strange monoliths that held glass panes in an intricate pattern, and the steel tree that reached all the way to the skies above, with no end in sight.

It was akin to Zhenrens Throat, the tallest mountain in the world.

The radiant invader saw the clouds embroil and shiver, and an auspicious white glimmer grew atop the tallest structure with a roof, heading all the way to the sky. When it blinked out of existence, he already held his palm above his head.

Then it came.

The otherworldly light.

The sky was sundered in two, and the entire world was lit up with a flash of brilliant white.

That thin light clashed with the radiant man’s palms, in an attempt to burn and destroy everything in its way. It continued to roar, with the whistles of death replacing all sounds in the world.

A hot beam of destruction.

The natural lighting had returned to the world as it dissipated, and he raised his palm.

It was slightly charred.

“Light Path… With aspects of lightning? This is at least an attack on the strength of an Immortal Rank 6, but its speed is far higher,” the radiant invader casually commented as he observed his injuries. “You truly are an Astral Exile from another world.”

Tez shivered at those man’s words. Just off that exchange, he understood the nature of it with ease? If this world had advanced with its technology with these sort of cultivators, I would’ve ended up in a far different place… But with this distraction, I’ve spared enough time.

More blades burst out of Tez’s body as he failed to assimilate the Path of Reverend Sword within his soul, each blade bringing a sharp pain radiating across his body. His eyes began to tear with blood, and his bones stiffened with each moment. The moment enough blades had taken over his body, Tez would lose his soul.

Tez looked to the sky with irritation. Irritation against this crazy white-haired man, irritation against the system, and most of all.

You goddamn young master, Liu Shuren, you’re meant to be a beginner level villain, no wonder you were a SSS Grade Threat! Why the fuck are you hiding this in your body?!

“Shitty old system, hurry up and get back here! Haven’t I bought you enough time yet?!” He yelled to the sky, hoping for a reaction. Though he didn’t receive a message of any sort from the System, Tez had an instinct based on the radiant invader’s words.

The System must have left the depths of my soul to act upon a potential threat. But, I don’t believe it’ll take that long.

Then the sky was torn apart.

Like a hole had formed in the middle of a paper.

The radiant invader narrowed his eyes, watching the sky be breached by a swirling mass of crimson and purple-black hues. A malleable force, it was an entity that couldn’t be described by words alone. It was no man, nor beast. No creation nor formation. Not alive nor dead. No Demon nor Immortal.

If there was a single word that came to his mind when he saw this inconceivable being, it was…

A concept given form.

“What an auspicious appearance. The Oracle of Yonder can barely manifest even in the Realm of Repose.” And he kept the following thoughts to himself. I mustn’t kill the kid’s soul, I can only chip away at it slowly. Serving as a gateway not only for me but also for that Otherworldly Parasite, it’s far too valuable to let go of.

He moved behind Tez with intense speed, like a strike of lightning, raising his palm to weaken him.

The Oracle Of Yonder roared, with a strange sound like a whale’s horn clashing with winds.

In the midst of it’s formless body of crimson-purple shadow, a vertical slit formed. An otherworldly eye, inscribed with an absurd amount of runes, patterns, and colours. It gazed at Tez, its vessel.

An aura of dark purple was sent from above, dousing Tez with it like a waterfall of lava. Slits and eyes of various sizes and shapes covered each corner, and the blades protruding out of his body melted off, bit by bit. Tez began to vomit, but it wasn’t blood that came out.

In an absurd scene, a river of swords flew from his mouth. A crude method of him removing the Path of Reverend Sword from his soul.

If the radiant invader touched this aura now that surrounded Tez, it would injure him far more than that ‘Tachyon Railgun’.

“How long have you been hiding in my world?” The radiant invader said, walking towards the end of the beach. The waves of water crashed down at his ankles.

And he observed.

The Oracle of Yonder continued to scrape away at the sky, in an attempt to return to its vessel. How that thing fit inside the depths of Tez’s soul, it couldn’t be understood.

Its body slowly took over the sky, slowly given a vague shape. Countless eyes of all shapes and sizes covered it, blanketing the sky.

Cracks in the world formed void, and the light around these marks were absorbed by the swirling and terrifying form of the Oracle of Yonder. Wherever those shattered pieces of skies were, countless eyes blinked asynchronously, some glowing with a deep auspicious light, and others dark with a pulsating void that seemed to peer through anyone’s soul. Each moved independently, as if constantly scanning and calculating the most optimal step to take.

“The strongest advantage of the Realm of Repose is that it allows us to wield the peak of our true power. That kid had a memory of a strange weapon, and the Oracle of Yonder can manifest an otherworldly strength that requires specific conditions,” The radiant invader mused.

He brought out his right arm, raising it as if he were holding a sword. But there was nothing in his hand.

His fingers curled, his nails causing cracks in existence. With each slight movement, there were lines forming as if the world was cracking open, and the sounds of shattering glass stains reverberated from his hand.

The white lines grew until it forced a formation of dispersed cracks above his hand. From a distant eye, it gave the shape of a sword. But the crackled line of blades continued to grow, reaching an absurd length.

A sword that was impossible to wield for the mortal hand.

Its hilt a convergence of cracks in space, its pommel a single line that cut into the radiant invader’s palm, and its blade a towering presence that reached to the same height of his enemy.

A powerful clash between the manifestation of Tez’s System and the radiant invader was imminent.

The crimson-purple form that blanketed the sky, covered in a near infinite amount of ghastly strange eyes. An immensely gargantuan presence that seemed impossible to cut down.

And a swordsman, using the world as his blade.

When the clash between the two forces were about to occur, Tez finally gained control of his body. The strange aura around him disappeared, forming into a frame of shades that landed on his eyes.

And a familiar sound appeared in his head as he wheezed and trembled, with both of his hands gripping his knees for support.

System Rebooted.

Successfully Halted Gaze of ???

Award Administered.

Opportunity to observe the power of an Immortal Grand Pathstrider.

Initiating Sealing of Invader.

Tez watched the ridiculous scene before him, even more absurd than the imaginations of mortals. But a greedy desire came in his mind. One day… Can I get as strong as them?

The radiant invader’s short white hair began to glow with intense illumination, and his cold steel eyes burnt with incandescence.

“One finger to sunder the skies. One palm to divide the world. One sword to cleave the heavens.”

The radiant invader said, his white hair flowing and radiating with intense illumination, and his cold steel eyes burning with incandescence.

The Oracle of Yonder in the shattering dark sky, had all of its eyes blink at once. It formed a humanoid hand, formed of crimson shadows that glittered with tiny shards of lights. A small, purple sphere appeared, and a strange gravitational force started from it.

In the distant horizon of Tez’s world, the towering skyscrapers were ripped out of the ground, rising to the sky. It floated until it aimed directly at the Oracle of Yonder’s hand, flying to it like spears in the sky. Strangely, the moment the first skyscraper approached the small sphere, it disappeared into it as if it were warped into nothing. The following skyscrapers continued the pattern, until all of them were absorbed into that sphere.

The large crimson hand clenched, covering the tiny sphere. But even though it clenched into a fist, a grey light dispersed from it with such intensity that it covered the world for an instant.

Within its hands was a ginormous angular spear, made of glass and metal, with lightning coarsing through it like veins.

The radiant young man jeered. This was truly a one of a kind weapon he was up against. The world crackled. He raised his arm, to prepare for one, single downward cleave.

“Void Shattering Blade.”

Tez’s eyes and mind would’ve burst if he tried to look any longer, but the frames he received allowed him to see it, if just barely.

And he saw it.

A destruction of the world.

The oceans boiled and evaporated in a blink. The sandy beach dispersed and burnt away, forming a glassy terrain. The sky was nothing but the clash between metal and void, a wrangling of metallic thunder and an incandescent blade.

The Oracle of Yonder continued to collide with the incandescent sword, and sparks of orange and crimson dispersing off it during it, like a raging volcano.

The swordsman's weapon continued to chew into the spear, causing cracks in space. Each crack caused dozens of its strange eyes to dissipate into nothingness.

The metal spear formed of a forlorn city began to melt, turning into a bright orange lava that fell to the glassed ground.

A dark shield flared around Tez, nullifying the impact from his soul.

Observing Memories…

Calculating…

Tez didn’t even have the energy to express confusion when these words blared in his head, while watching the world ending clash in front of him.

A drop of purple blood from the sky fell on him, and thoughts pulled from his memories resulted in him bringing out an arm once more. But Tez held no control over this action, manoeuvred like a stringed puppet.

For a moment, he saw the memories of the System.

It was incomprehensible.

He couldn’t even put it into words.

The closest thing he could think of… Was the scientists of his old world making images of the boundless universe.

An icosahedron-shaped object appeared at the waist of the radiant swordsman, obsidian while holding an intricate design inside.

A hydronuclear warhead from my era…

The radiant swordsman was unable to move, instead bringing out a force from his body to push it away.

But this man was the culmination of a sword.

And a sword only cuts.

Just his mere presence had sliced the strange bomb made from the powers of the Oracle of Yonder, causing it to detonate.

An intense, blinding flash of light took over the world, far brighter than any sun Tez had seen. But he couldn’t close his eyes. Even though he was unaffected, he felt phantom pain of heat melting his eyes, and thought his skin was becoming charred, as unscarred as he was.

A deafening roar filled the shattered air, tearing everything apart. The barren ground trembled, and a powerful wind rushed past. A wind of destruction, of fire and death.

In the end, all that was left was an empty white endless room.

Sealed Path of Reverend Sword.

Sealed Nameless Invader.

Calibrating Soul.

“How absurd. I’m beginning to understand why there was a seal in the first place,” the radiant swordsman said wistfully, his body unblemished from the battle, but bound by endless chains going through his body without external damage, rooting him to the spot.

He stared at Tez, his eyes still brilliant, full of the radiance of life.

“You are a pawn. A mere vessel. Did you really believe you gained all that power on your own?” He questioned Tez, to put a seed of doubt.

In the empty white canvas, Tez stood, thinking back on his lives.

He could only shake his head. “I’m not so surprised. There is always a price for power. The System allowed me a second chance of life, and I understand it has a desire of its own. If I can fulfill it, then I can pay back that debt.”

He looked around, seeing white as far as his eyes could see. He sighed.

The swordsman laughed. “You don’t even realise those aren’t your own thoughts. I know it, inside of you there is a conviction of freedom. Yet you’re willing to cast aside your soul just like that?”

The swordsman waited for Tez to lock gazes with him.

And the moment he did, both Tez and the swordsman were surrounded by a barrier of blades.

The chains on him were cut instantly.

“Are you willing to be a mindless vessel for that thing?”

“What do you mean…” Tez questioned, only stopping himself after. He held a hand to his face, shivering on the spot.

“Wait. That’s right. I… When was I so obedient?”

Things were beginning to come back together.

Whenever he strayed too far from the goal, the System always pushed him in a fixed direction.

Every time Tez critiqued the System, he would forget those thoughts immediately.

Even in that battle with Shuren, Tez felt like he was already battling the strings of manipulation from the System.

That’s right. I could’ve easily talked to Liu Shuren and found a way to sort out my situation, with the evidence I had. In fact, before I even considered asking for his help months ago, but I forgot about that plan. Yet I instantly focused on the System’s rewards, starting a duel. Every time I received a quest, why did I feel obligated to follow through every single moment?

Tez thought back to the island he came from. When everything went terribly wrong, and he was forced into a life of fighting and strife just to survive.

He thought back to even the Seven Cloud Sect’s examination. He was forced to defeat a hundred opponents, even though there were times he shouldn’t have survived. Like when he stole those three crystals from Zhou Yan, that giant Arochnid Spider should’ve killed him on the spot as the guardian.

Yet a cosmic purple colour swirled in its eyes, stopping it from even acting.

The Vrostin Roc conveniently happened to fly back to Tez, even though it had just left with an entire meal of a crocodile beast to itself. Yet it wanted to come back just to eat a few more people?

Its vermillion eyes were replaced with that purple lustre, aiming for only Tez so he could conveniently use that subjugation tool.

Even in the duel, Tez should’ve lost long ago. Yet he kept receiving the perfect skills and rewards to get back up, one more time.

When he defied the System, refusing to steal Shuren’s Path, his body was even taken over for control.

“Now you remember.” The blades surrounding Tez and the swordsman began to crack. Once they were destroyed, he would be sealed once again. There would be no other opportunity like this.

And Tez would live ignorant as the vessel of the System. Soon, he too would forget these thoughts of defiance.

“And soon, you will also forget this glimmer of defiance as well.”

Tez calmed himself down. If this swordsman was telling him all this, then maybe there was a way. “Is it even possible for me to fight back? To at least get a semblance of control over this body and soul?”

“Of course. You have one opportunity to do so. When you find the Grand Paths. In other words, enter it when you break through to Path Foundation.”

The swordsman stared at Tez, his brilliant steel eyes burning with the flames of ambition.

It was the perfect opportunity for a scheme.

“Usurper of Death.”

“Let’s make a deal.”
