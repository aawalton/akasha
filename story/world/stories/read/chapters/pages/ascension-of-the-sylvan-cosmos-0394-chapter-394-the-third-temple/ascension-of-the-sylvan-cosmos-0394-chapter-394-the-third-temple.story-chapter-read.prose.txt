Liu Shu’s glance lingered in the Temple of Life. Recently, she had lost many people due to the relatively short and finite lifespan of living creatures. As a World Tree, she had a different way of seeing things. Therefore, such losses didn’t affect her as much as unnatural deaths. However, that didn’t mean that she was emotionless. ‘But… Is it okay to choose a temple based on those factors? Even if they don’t die in two years, they might die in four…’ As she thought about it, the urge slowly disappeared, and she thought deeply about it. ‘I am just delaying the inevitable. Once it happens, I will feel much more the loss because I was invested in preventing it.’

Thinking until there, Liu Shu sighed slowly and thought rationally. ‘War Blessing is definitely a favorite. After all, we’ll go to war soon with the entire continent. Giving them that extra edge with the War Blessing might be the thing that tips the balance in our favor. Life Blessing is also powerful for wartime. After all, recovery will become easier. In a drawn-out fight, it will have the most effects.’

Then, her eyes turned toward the other two. ‘Death Blessing is not too influential. I want to prevent deaths, not embrace them. Finally, the Crafting Blessing…’

Liu Shu couldn’t help but feel that she was underestimating this blessing. ‘We are a developing nation. Crafters are currently working at 110% to create and cope with the increasingly larger army. Even if I use War Blessing and increase our soldiers, can we arm them all? Are there enough weapons of enough quality for everyone?’

Honestly, she could see that Flor and the others were starting to struggle. Even when we looted the corpses of the armies that attacked us, the usable equipment was not much. If armies collided 2 or 3 times in a short time, Liu Shu could see a situation where her people would not have anything proper to fight with.

“Honestly, the crafters being much stronger will allow us to use large Arrays, Runic Fields, and develop everything. Adding my other titles to the Crafting Blessing, the value of each of our workers will be more than double that of other nations.” After the <Dungeon> opened, materials from inside it were also pouring out en masse. So, Level 50+ raw materials were not too scarce. Liu Shu just needed hands to transform all of that into usable gear.

Once her entire army was clad in high-level armor, adding to her existing buffs, Liu Shu was sure that it would be quite terrifying. Plus, she had tens of thousands of <Sylvanium Ore> and the other materials at her disposal. Moreover, she was going to get <Sylvanium Alloy>, a level above <Sylvanium Ingot>.

Warriors were needed, but even if she had a C Tier warrior, what could it do facing D tiers with C Tier armors? She would be wasting her talents against ordinary soldiers. Liu Shu remembered something else and looked inside the massive ant colony.

In a place near her main body, a strange industrial area had been constructed and was ready to go the second the ants managed to get the ability to craft items. According to Ignatia, she needed a small push, but she didn’t know what.
