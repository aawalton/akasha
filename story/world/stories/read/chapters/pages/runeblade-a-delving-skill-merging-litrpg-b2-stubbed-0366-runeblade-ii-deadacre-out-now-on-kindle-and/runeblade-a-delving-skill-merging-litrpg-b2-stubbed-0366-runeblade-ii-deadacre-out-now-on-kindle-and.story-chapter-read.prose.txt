BLURB:

Delving for hidden truths has consequences. The Apocalypse has arrived. Again.

Kaius has done the impossible: escaped the Great Depths alive, forged an unbreakable brotherhood with Porkchop, and claimed a Heroic Class. But his growing strength, the forbidden secrets behind his Legacy Skills, and never-before earned Honours have awakened ancient System protocols.

Porkchop and Kaius emerge into a world overrun by newly born beasts and teetering on the edge of catastrophe. With his father’s fate unknown, enemies hunting his secrets, and reality itself beginning to shift, Kaius must master his new runic magic, decide which allies he can trust, and find a place he can grow strong enough to stand on his own.

His path brings him to the remote city of Deadacre, an overlooked backwater that is home to the Delver’s Guild. Kaius knows strength alone won't be enough. Not all problems can be solved with steel or spell, and he has a lot to learn if he is to have any hope of reaching the Second Tier.

When the threats are evolving faster than you are, survival means becoming something the world has never seen.
