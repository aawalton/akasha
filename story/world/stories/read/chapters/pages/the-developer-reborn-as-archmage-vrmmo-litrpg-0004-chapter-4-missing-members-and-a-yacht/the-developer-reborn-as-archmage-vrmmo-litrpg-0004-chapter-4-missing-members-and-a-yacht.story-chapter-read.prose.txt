That's a good start, though. Maybe I should lean into it.

Dovarism was built around the Lightbearer. She couldn't remember the exact details now, but she knew she wasn't a fan. It was loosely inspired by the Abrahamic religions that she was familiar with in real life, and she didn't need that in her escapist fantasy.

She was more into the edgy red and black color palette of the Eternal Demoness in her Archdemon solo run.

So many cool banners and leather outfits and armor kits!

Karossa was a dry, arid island, if she remembered right. It all made sense now. He had become a palace guard to the princess despite being an atheist clansman. This meant his way of life had no problems coexisting with the Dovarists.

A large-scale porting spell? makes sense for an Archmage supreme. Doesn't make sense as a tactical decision mid-fight.

Sarka had destroyed her first because she was the most powerful one in the party. The sphere had swallowed her afterwards, and she had died. Warping away after defeating her seemed wrong even at a glance.

Why did he leave? Was it his idea, or did I do something to him during my fight?

They came up on a lagoon at the end of the beach. The forest beyond the beach grew thicker as they went. There were mangroves the size of apartments and coconut trees bent like giant bows.

Things were stylistically exaggerated in the VRMMO version. She hadn't expected them to be fully preserved in the RLP version, but she was glad to see that those things weren't totally removed either. The coconuts hanging from these giant trees looked as big as her torso.

They passed a small campfire where Seris and Fominel had been roasting a fish. The actual campsite was deeper into the lagoon. They kept walking along the beach through the jagged edge of the lagoon until the whole arc of the shoreline was in sight.

She didn't need anyone to point out where the campsite was. It wasn't even important compared to what immediately caught the entirety of her attention.

There was a small ship anchored in the relative shallows.

Her heart tingled when she realized why it was there. Until now, it hadn't even occurred to her that traveling around in a tropical island archipelago required a ship.

She had gone through a pirate ship phase when she was younger. She was more than fascinated with sailing, but the closest she ever got was a horrible carnival cruise when she was thirteen.

The ship before her looked as if it were pulled from a dream. It was a dark brown caravel with cream lines framing decorative golden floral trims. Folded sails were gathered on two masts, and a large black metallic chimney stood between them at about half the height.

Vainfall was set in that middle century between sails and steam. This ship was the perfect poster for the overall level of conventional technology.

The ship looked expensive and luxurious despite its size. It was somewhat weathered and beaten, but it had once been well polished and carefully maintained. Just looking at it infused the sense of adventure directly into her veins.

I can be a pirate princess with a magic staff, a pretty elf, and a katana warlock. Maybe things aren't that bad after all.

Darya immediately thought every penny was worth it. As a lead developer on Earth, she only had enough savings to buy a fishing boat. This small ship seemed like Vainfall's equivalent of a yacht.

"You sound excited. Unfortunately, we should wait for better weather, princess," Fominel said. "It's going to take all evening for us to prepare, and it's going to storm tonight. We can leave in the morning once it passes."

Darya's eyebrows twitched. On one hand, she didn't like people enough to bathe with them, and she didn't quite enjoy physical touch. On the other hand, it was the world's most beautiful elf girl making the offer. Nothing like this had happened during the thirty years she spent on Earth.

It's my second life. Why the heck shouldn't I?

They reached the campsite with three black canvas tents with floral decorations. The middle tent was dining and supplies, the other two were sleeping quarters.

Darya grabbed the second paddle, and they rowed the boat over the coral reefs in the shallows towards the Desert Rose. She was copying what Seris was doing without any experience with boats, but she quickly found her new body strong enough to contribute.

She had a respectable strength attribute of 467, which would've been enough for absurd strength checks in-game, like busting down doors with a kick. In RLP, it seemed to work much differently. She was strong, but she was only strong for her weight.

It still didn't make sense for attributes to climb to hundreds in that case. It could've easily been capped, placing each individual somewhere between zero to hundred, or ten, for example.

Based on the fact that it was allowed to be as high as four hundred for an Archmage, and ignoring the possibility that attributes were capped at a thousand, this had to be deliberate. Maybe the usage of full strength is limited? Is it what Skills do now? I'll have to check this later.
