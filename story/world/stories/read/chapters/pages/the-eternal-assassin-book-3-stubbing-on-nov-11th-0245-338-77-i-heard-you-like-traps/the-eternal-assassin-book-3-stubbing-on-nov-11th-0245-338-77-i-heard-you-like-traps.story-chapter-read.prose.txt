Despite knowing what they were getting into when it came to raiding the castle of a grandmaster of enchanting, Asher couldn’t help but be blown away with the sheer quantity of traps that Flunx had prepared for anyone who would be so foolish as to dare enter her domain. The hallway-spanning inferno was only the beginning.

Invisible, poisonous gas that slowly rose up from the floors, which they only managed to sense at all thanks to Moxy’s ability to feel the slightest of fluctuations in the air. Dense rays of death energy that thankfully struck Asher before anyone else, seeing as his Complete Resilience immunity was currently set back on the Death element. Powerful waves of electricity that locked up their muscles and tried to stop their hearts in their tracks, stopped only by Secret Seeker giving Asher a split second of notice something was coming and allowing him to get a Spatial Lock up in time to block the current.

Not all of the traps were so straightforward, either. One of the deadliest ones they encountered quite literally tried to freeze their blood solid within their own bodies, in the hopes of killing them from the inside out. Luckily, most of the team was resistant or outright immune to anything blood-related by now. Rulfar had his element, Kroult was undead, Moxy had an evolved resistance, and Asher had Complete Resilience. Samantha was the only one the trap affected, but that was still one person too many.

“Damn it, that was too close for comfort,” Asher grunted, clutching Samantha by the shoulders as the hunter took a few shaky breaths and gave him a strained smile. She’d collapsed to the ground almost instantly, and if it hadn’t been for Winged Savior triggering and buying her a moment for Rulfar to get to her, she would have actually perished.

Curiously, Winged Savior now only destroyed one of her wings when it was triggered. Evolving the skill seemed to give it a second use, as Samantha now only had a single wing sticking out of her back. She wouldn’t be able to fly for a while, but at the very least, she could still rely on the one wing for combat.

“Flunx really did her best to prepare for just about any sort of intruder,” Rulfar said, stepping back now that Samantha was okay. It had only been due to his quick thinking with Alter Flow that he’d managed to keep Samantha’s blood normal and moving throughout her body until the trap ended. “The effects are so varied you’d need an impossibly diverse group of people to be able to counter or negate all of them.”

“Good thing we’re the ones tackling this lich then,” Samantha said, flapping her one wing and grimacing as she tried to get used to the lack of her other appendage. “Our spread of elements and skills run the gauntlet, and we have Asher’s Spatial Lock to act as a catch-all for anything else. It’ll take more than some creative traps to put us down.”

Of course, Flunx quickly proved that she did in fact have plenty more than just creative traps at her disposal.

The next hallway the team entered turned out to be a multi-pronged ambush. First, a series of runes lining the walls revealed themselves, flaring bright as twin stone walls sealed off the front and rear portions of the hallway, trapping them within the small corridor. The moment they were sealed inside, the runes flared again, and Asher blinked as he went to take a breath, only to discover that all of the air had vanished right out from under them. Before he could so much as take a step toward Moxy, the third portion of the trap activated, as portions of the walls sank into the floor and a half-dozen golems stepped out to greet them.

The golems were humanoid constructs about six feet in height, created from some form of gleaming-black marble. Based on how their sphere-like heads all turned toward them the moment they stepped out of the walls, Asher didn’t think the lack of discernable eyes or facial features was going to hinder them when it came to combat.

“Moxy!” Asher shouted with the air he still had within his lungs as the golems lunged toward them.

Way ahead of him, Moxy grinned as she slammed her fists together, unleashing a powerful Air Nova that knocked everyone back a few feet as it flooded the room with entirely new air. Having evolved the skill to the third tier with her portion of the bounty pool, Moxy had finally gained the ability to create new air for herself. Something Asher had a feeling she’d desperately wanted ever since he’d managed to trick her into suffocating herself within a vacuum.

Now that they could breathe again, they were able to focus their full attention on the strange golems trying to kill them.

Before doing anything else, Asher grabbed Kroult and shoved the lich into his Personal Rift, figuring that his limited combat spells wouldn’t have any sort of effect on the golems. Once he was safe, Asher lunged toward the two golems at the front of the hallway, trusting in his friends to be able to handle themselves without him. Gripping Crescent in hand, Asher darted past the golems with a tunnel of compressed space before turning and slashing his masterwork mithral blade against the first golem’s neck. Crescent scraped along the black marble but failed to find purchase, and he cursed as the second golem’s head spun a hundred and eighty degrees before lunging out to grab him.

Flickering through the astral, Asher dodged the golem’s attempt to grapple him, not wanting to wait around and find out exactly what it had planned on doing if it hadn’t missed. After seeing the wide variety of traps Flunx had filled her castle with, there was no telling what sort of enchantments the golems had to have covering their bodies.

As the golem stumbled through him, he returned to the material realm, attempting to drive Crescent point first into the golem to see if that would have any effect. While his dagger did manage to catch and make a small scratch on the golem, it wasn’t nearly enough. With a sigh, Asher realized they were going to have to rely on their trump card already.

Compressing space with Distortion, Asher stepped to the side, moving ten feet in a single motion as he evaded the fist that the first golem threw toward his head. That gave him the time needed to put Crescent away and pull out his void dagger, and he almost involuntarily began counting down in his head as he felt his tritessence start draining into the hungry blade. Anytime he was forced to use the void dagger he was put on a timer, and Asher didn’t want to run himself dry of tritessence before even making it to Flunx.

Which meant he’d have to be quick.

Darting right back through compressed space, Asher lashed out with the dagger, blinking as the golem dodged backward and managed to avoid decapitation by the thinnest of margins. The void blade did brush partially through its round head, carving a narrow groove in it as though the impossibly hard marble were made of clay. If the golem was concerned about Asher’s ability to harm it, it didn’t show, as it immediately went back on the offensive, throwing another powerful punch toward Asher’s head as the second golem lunged low to try and grapple his legs.

Triggering his Boots of Levitation, Asher shot up and above the lunging golem. At the same time, he twisted to avoid the other’s punch and sliced through its arm with his dagger. The black-marble limb fell lifelessly to the ground, and before the golem could recover he dropped down again as he stabbed forward, cutting clean through the golem’s neck and sending its head tumbling.

Which did absolutely nothing to stop the headless construct from striking forward with its remaining arm as it tried to cave in his ribcage.

Thankfully, Asher had learned his lesson from his battle with the King of Death and the undead puppet the lich had commanded. Despite having beheaded his enemy, he hadn’t grown lax, and he shifted into the astral realm to dodge the surprise attack at the last moment. Turning to ensure the golem’s arm was no longer within his chest, he returned the material realm and sliced through that arm as well, sending it falling to the ground.

The move cost him, however, as the second golem finally managed to grab onto one of his legs, squeezing its hands together and shattering the bones in his leg with startling ease. Asher didn’t waste any time slipping into the astral to free himself, and he grimaced as he glanced down at the state his limb was in. The golem had to have had some sort of bone enchantment inscribed in it, because it felt like all of the bones in his leg had been broken from where it had grabbed down to his very toes. Even with Complete Resilience it hurt like absolute hell, and he swore as he yanked a potion of Lesser Restoration from his rift and downed it.

Not even waiting for the potion to finish, Asher returned to the material, swiping low and removing both legs off the first golem before it could run away. If he couldn’t kill these things, cutting all their limbs off was the only other option he had available to him. With one of his two attackers gone, finishing off the second was a much easier task, even with his damaged leg. Careful uses of Distortion were all Asher needed to aid his lack of mobility, as the ability to transform a step of a few inches into that of a few feet meant he could still dance around his attacker easily enough. As soon as he reduced the second golem to nothing more than a torso as well, he turned to see how the rest of the team was faring.

Samantha was tackling one of the golems that had come from the back, easily blocking its strikes with a combination of her one remaining wing and powerful blasts of water from her evolved Fountain skill. It seemed the sharpened edges of her wings made from pure tritessence were capable of damaging the golem, as while she hadn’t put hers down just yet, its black-marble body was covered in jagged gashes, and one of its arms had been fully severed already.

Moxy was battling her golem with her usual manic grin on her face, blasting the poor thing around the hallway and smashing it into walls, ceilings, and every other surface that got in her way. Despite the fact that her Air Blasts weren’t powerful enough to damage the construct, the golem was being hurled around like a ragdoll, unable to even get close to her as she toyed with it.

Rulfar was having a bit more trouble, but that was probably because he’d also taken on two golems at the same time. Relying on his massive size and large weapon, he was alternating between the two attackers, knocking them away one after another as he presumably waited for someone to come help who could actually damage the constructs. Seeing as golems didn’t have any blood, they were a bad match up for him, but he was at least keeping them in a stalemate.

Samantha and Moxy were easily holding their own, which meant the question of who to help first was an easy one. Using Distortion to compress space, Asher stepped the length of the hallway in a single motion, cleaving his void dagger straight through the legs of one of the two golem’s going after Rulfar. As the construct fell to the ground, he swept his dagger through its head and arms next, bringing their total number of attackers down to three. After that, finishing off the others was simple enough. With Rulfar’s strength, Samantha’s skill, Moxy’s ruthlessness, and Asher’s precision, they brought the battle to an end as the last golem fell.

He just wished he hadn’t had to burn through so much tritessence to do it.
