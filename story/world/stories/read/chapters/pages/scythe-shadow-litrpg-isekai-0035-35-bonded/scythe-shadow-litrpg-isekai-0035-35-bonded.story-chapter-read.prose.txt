I stood alone on the beach.

My lungs breathed in and out steadily. Not even a bit exhausted from the final moments of the battle after my advancement. I clenched my fists, feeling the enhanced power of my muscles. The stat augmentations from advancing were far greater than I could’ve hoped. Even my Volition—which had received a double boost—was still 75% full despite using two Aero Shifts and a Life Drain to finish off Zeus.

I let out a sigh. That had been… something else. I was stacking up close calls far faster than I liked.

And what was with that vision?

It felt so odd, so disconcerting, inhabiting another body like that. No control over emotions or actions, my current feelings warping against those of my host. Even if it was a different version myself.

Unless I had it all wrong. What if the vision wasn't related to me at all? What if it was related to the Sin?

That would explain why it felt so foreign and disconnected from my own life. This new theory was starting to make far more sense than my previous one. I allowed the ideas to percolate through my mind.

And if this were the case, then what I saw wouldn't be a future or alternate reality from my life. But what then? Someone else? Someone random? But why?

Then it hit me like a punch to the gut.

Like a car to the chest.

That's how the vision had ended. With a blaring horn. Headlights glaring. I—he—whoever—had been hit by a car.

A consequence of someone else's actions. Some else's… sin? I hadn't seen anything of the driver in the vision, but were they speeding through the crosswalk, too greedy with their own time to notice the people in the crosswalk? Were they drunk?

The pieces fell into place like a puzzle making itself.

The vision wasn't showing me something random. It was showing me what this sin—this Sin—had caused, or would cause in Vitria if allowed to fester. I was receiving direct feedback on the impact my actions as a Reaper were having.

I'd… prevented that from happening. Saved one, maybe two lives from ending. That's what I'd tell myself, anyway. It was better to think that I'd killed the Sin before it could cause this to pass.

That would make sense from the perspective of the Reapers as well. Inspire them to end more Sins, to keep advancing, directly knowing the good we were causing with each swing of our scythes, each Will harvested.

But I also understood how it could be overwhelming. Calliope said that Jericho stopped because he couldn't handle the advancement process anymore. I didn't know if that was true or if it had something to do with his son, but I could definitely see it either way now that I knew.

I wouldn't let it stop me though. This would fuel me.

I took a deep breath, my resolve solidifying.

A breeze blew by, ruffling my newly colored Amethyst cloak. I admired the metallic purple sheen of the fabric. It wasn’t normally my color, but it served me pretty darn well so far here.

I looked around for Nuzzle. Usually, he would've come running to me by now and had his tongue halfway up his orifice of choice for the day.

I called out to him, but heard no little chirps or scuttling feet in reply. This isn't good… My heart began pounding harder in my chest. I'd lost track of him during the battle after Zeus kicked him away. If he's hurt at all… I swear, I'll fucking kill you for real next time we meet, Zeus. I'll—

I checked my reaction, pausing, letting deep breaths calm me.

I just had to find that damn lizard.

There was only one possibility remaining. Nuzzle often ran off to hide during fights. A single large gray rock rested near the dunes, submerged partway in the sand. The last place I hadn't checked.

As I walked around the boulder, an unexpected sight greeted me.

A second crystalline dome, like the one that had protected me during my advancement, shimmered with a mystic sparkle. It was smaller than mine, but clearly made of the same material and tinted purple.

The crystal was transparent enough to see that Nuzzle lay within, curled into a ball, but the interior was too distorted and hazy to make out any details. I rested a hand on the dome and it gradually disintegrated into purple dust, tickling my hand as the particles floated into the air.

Nuzzle stirred. Then yawned. A row of tiny sharp teeth lined his mouth. Those weren't there before. As he uncurled and stretched, I realized he'd also grown significantly in bulk—now more the size of a house cat than a salamander. He was still orange, with those adorable yellow glowing eyes, but had a little frill traveling from the top of his head down his spine.

He blinked a few times and rubbed sleep from his eyes with a foot. Once he noticed me he let out a growling chirp, then dove onto my face.

I fell backwards, startled but laughing as Nuzzle licked my face, his tongue finding its way into my left ear.

"Okay okay!" I chuckled, holding him at arm's length. "I like the makeover, buddy."

He cooed happily and snuggled into my chest. Still the same Nuzzle.

"So you advanced because I did, huh?" I stood and he clawed his way up onto my shoulder, nails digging into me like two dozen bee stings and creating small holes in my brand new shirt—which, frankly, was already near ruin thanks to Zeus again anyway.

Nuzzle barely fit up on my shoulder now that he'd grown. He curled his tail around my neck to steady himself "You better not get any bigger or soon I'll be riding you," I joked.

We ventured further south as I contemplated what to do next.

I'd been lucky so far, all things considered. As an Amber I was vastly inferior to Zeus, but after advancing my Amethyst stats were enough to help me defeat him, even though he had been boosting his stats for a while. But as far as typical Amethyst Reapers went, I was probably on the lower end of power because I'd only relinquished two Wills before this. In all likelihood, I could never have defeated an Amethyst Sin on my own at that level. Finding the Will of that Unchained had been helpful and necessary to survive, but it put me at a significant disadvantage now against other Reapers and Sins my new rank.

I had to acquire more Wills, actually focus on boosting my stats and skills before advancing to Emerald.

Nuzzle hopped from my shoulder to chase a bug, testing out his new body. That trail of black mist followed him as he hopped around. A few of those cottony wisp balls came and hovered over the shallows as if they were watching him.

They really do seem sentient in some way.

After a few seconds they zoomed off over the dunes.

I hadn't been in Penumbra long, but I'd never noticed the wisps further inland. Only here by the coast.

I'll ask Jericho or Calliope about that when I—

I stopped and let my head fall back, staring up at the gray cloudless sunless sky. A yellow eagle soared above, dark trail following it like a comet tail.

No. I won't be asking Jericho or Calliope anything.

I'd killed Allium. Her horse. Her bonded friend. I hadn't struck the final blow, but the blood remained on my hands nonetheless.

I watched Nuzzle chasing a spirit-like red crab now. It scuttled into the water and he followed, sticking his head in, like an ostrich poking its head underground.

I'd been so worried and terrified when I couldn't find him, even saying I'd kill Zeus. I'd murder another human for him. That revelation sent a tingle down my back.

I only just met Nuzzle a few days ago and felt that way. How was Calliope feeling, having known Allium for years?

Fuck me…

'She's gonna kill me' was seeming less and less like an exaggeration the more I thought about it. I just had to hope she was a better person than me or that I'd never see her again.

I didn't want to think about it. The guilt was too strong.

Death just seemed to follow the Death Reaper around. Or maybe… it just followed me around. Both in Vitria and Penumbra.

The sooner I could leave the Meadow, the better.
