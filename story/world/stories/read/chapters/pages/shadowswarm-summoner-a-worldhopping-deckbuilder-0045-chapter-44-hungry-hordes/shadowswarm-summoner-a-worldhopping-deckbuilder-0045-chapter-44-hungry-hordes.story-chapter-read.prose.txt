Chapter 44: Hungry Hordes

Before looking at his cards for the round, Ambrose cursed his own growing weakness and stupidity. Letting the kid die would’ve been the smart, practical move. The kind of thing he would have written down to do on one of their many tests. No hesitation, no guilt, no doubt. In the moment, it felt like he failed himself somehow.

But when in doubt, double down.

Round 1

Cantrip: [Vampiric Kiss], Spell: [Tap Thoughts], Creature: [Black Hound], Spell: [Shred Mind], Creature: [Festering Wretch]

It was as good a hand as any, and the most important thing was just throwing things out. He had no clue if he could recover his [Hateful Imps] if they’d been killed out of combat, but it might be worth a try. On the other hand, they’d been ripped apart by the horde. He needed to buy time. Which left two options first.

Ambrose cast [Festering Wretch] as far from him as he could. The summoning circle formed thirty feet away, and by the time the imp came into view, a ghoul collided with it. It exploded into corrosive ichor, splashing several others. They shrieked, and some paused, but there were too many of them to ignore.

“Run, you idiot,” he shouted, grabbing Jack by the jacket as he went. He cast his Cantrip afterwards. Maybe it would get in one good hit and heal his blisters before it too turned inside out.

The rest of the group summoned creatures of their own. Although, maybe only [Reggie] could last longer than a few seconds against the ravenous pack. Every moment counted, and Ambrose looked past them to the networks of tunnels on the far end of the cavern. They needed to lose them, or funnel them into a bottleneck where their numbers would work against them.

Raeva sent out twin phoenixes, and she had a look of grim excitement, like part of her relished the chance to let off some steam. The explosion sent a wave of heat at his heels, but Ambrose didn’t look behind him to check the damage. It sounded pretty good though.

As always, Luthor picked the practical choice. The [Ancestral Archer] stood aside, sending arrow after arrow into their pursuers, while he also brought his Signature Creature out. This time, the [Spirit Warrior] came with a massive shield and ornamental death mask.

Even Jessica brought out her large [Whirlpool Cephalopod], which was just perfect. It undulated past her at an uneven but swift gait. Its lack of a solid body would make it last a little longer, maybe slow them down more. Maybe it would drown some of the horrifying things.

Their other prisoner, Dexi’Lynn squawked and nearly tripped, but Raeleq scooped her up into his arms and powered ahead, slipping past a group of giant beetles. It turns out they were territorial but also stupid. Once the wizards were past, an especially large one with a great curved horn turned and swept its head from side to side.

This time, Ambrose did look back. A pack of ghouls screeched in pain and went flying. A gang of them swarmed the beetle, overrunning it until it dropped. Ambrose gulped and cast [Black Hound], this time further away from the fight. The circle formed near one of the tunnels.

“Where do we go?” Jessica cried as they got closer.

“That one,” Luthor yelled, pointing to one hardly big enough to fit Raeleq without him stooping. “Everyone else first, Raeleq brings up the rear.”

Jessica didn’t argue, and leaped ahead of everyone. She ducked inside first, followed by Jack, Ambrose, and then Raeva. She didn’t seem too happy to retreat, and she threw a parting [Flame Lance] through a ghoul, who promptly exploded.

“C’mon,” he said, tugging her arm. She looked like she wanted to fight him, but cursed and followed him deeper. Her flame appeared above her again to light the way.

This tunnel was a long, windy one, and barely wide enough to fit two of them side by side. It would be terrible for fighting, and Ambrose’s heart swelled with hope.

Round 2

Cantrip: [Vampiric Kiss], Creature: [Night Terror], [Spell: Assassin’s Blade], Enchantment: [Mark of Service], Spell: [Umbral Imp]

Although he was a bit disappointed that his newest card wasn’t viable at the moment, one spell stood out among the rest. One spell was all he needed to stop the ghouls from following.

“Everyone get ahead of me,” Ambrose yelled, flattening himself against a mossy, damp wall.

Luthor trusted him, but Raeleq paused to look over his shoulder. The hungry shrieks and calls for blood echoed nightmarishly.

“Do it!” Ambrose snapped, and started casting. Raeleq stirred and ran past him while he poured all of his mana into the [Assassin’s Strike].

The summoning circle almost didn’t fit. He stayed just long enough to watch. His monstrous, seven foot tall, five feet wide muscle-imp came into view, clogging up the tunnel. It wouldn’t be able to magically kill something once a round, but was still the most powerful creature he had for now.

He joined the others in continuing down. The thought struck him that it could lead to a dead end, or get too narrow to continue. They’d not only be trapped, but he would be the first one to get eaten. The black humor part of him thought it was almost funny.

But no, Jessica yelled, “it opens up here, into a large-ish cave. I can’t see how far it goes, but anything’s better, right?”

They piled out of the tunnel, one by one, and by unspoken agreement stopped just past the exit. Ambrose burst out of it and nearly tripped. His heart raced with fear and exertion, and each rapid pulse made his tortured feet ache anew.

If it weren’t for Luthor and Raeva’s light spells, this cave would have been pitch black. Unlike the earthy, rocky exteriors, it looked to be covered in soft, shimmery white silk. Strands dangled from the ceiling, like cobwebs…Oh.

Shrieks of anger and pain came from the tunnel, but the massive imp held for the moment. Ambrose felt its pain and anger in his mind, but also determination and even joy at the chance to snuff out so many little bugs.

“How long can that thing last?” Raeva demanded. “Are we ready to put up a fight here, maybe clog up the passage with their bodies?”

“I don’t know, and maybe, but we have bigger problems,” said Ambrose, looking around.

Raeleq summoned a [Pack Raptor] and sent it down to help defend. “You mean the spiders? I love giant spiders!”

“Spiders?” Jessica squeaked, backing up.

Raeva groaned and kicked a rock. “Normally, I’d just burn the webs and be done with them, but we don’t want to die of smoke inhalation.”

“Speak for yourself,” Dexi’Lynn said. Sometime since entering the cave, she’d gotten her pipe out and puffed away at it.

“Put that out,” Luthor snapped, visibly annoyed for once. He summoned his [Spirit Hound] and sent it into the tunnel as well. Its howl echoed through the cave, sending a chill down Ambrose’s spine.

It was just in time. With one last enraged slam of its enormous fists, the Assassin Imp, as Ambrose began to think of it, fell, and the horde surged in after them.

*****

Obviously the situation wasn’t ideal, but Raeva had to admit, being able to relentlessly hurl spell after spell at a bunch of nearly faceless monsters was great stress relief.

Round 4

Cantrip: [Singe], Enchantment: [Tempo Tantrum], Signature Creature: [Fiery Hatchling], Spell: [Copy Spell]

Next round would be another shuffle, with a free [Flame Lance] coming her way. But with the first of the ghouls peeking around the corner of the tunnel and about to overrun them all, that round couldn’t come fast enough. Her friends had all taken positions a few feet apart, ready to cover each other.

No one wanted to retreat into the spider silk behind them out of fear of what it might bring, but Raeva had a feeling their fighting and screaming would attract attention no matter what. She’d keep an eye and ear out, but for now?

Now, it was time to show these things why wizards were bad prey.

When the Pause ended, Raeva cast her enchantment.

[Tempo Tantrum: Rare Enchantment. Each time a spell or creature of yours damages another target, reduce the amount of time left on the Timer by 2 seconds. 2 Mana, 1 ongoing]

She didn’t always have the time or opportunity to use the enchantment, and had even been thinking about culling it in favor of something more broadly effective. This, however, was the perfect chance to get some use out of it.

The pounding of drums echoed in the small space, and that was all Raeva needed. Her brother and Luthor stepped forward to meet the ghouls head-on. Ambrose and Jessica cast spells to either side of her, but she set herself up for the long haul.

[Copy Spell] came first, finishing in just a second and a half, and then she followed it up with her [Fiery Hatchling]. Ambrose wasn’t the only one who could double up on his creature, and the two baby dragons formed simultaneously, filling in the gaps.

Raeleq let out a cry of pain as a ghoul latched onto his arm. Her gut clenched, and she ordered her newly summoned dragons forward.

They jumped, pumping their wings for height before landing just above the entrance to the tunnel. Clinging to the rock there, they craned serpentine necks down, opened V shaped jaws, and belched flames on the creatures below. The Timer dropped by ten seconds, giving her just one chance to use her cantrip.

Luthor ducked back, keeping behind his shield and occasionally thrusting his sword into tender flesh whenever he saw an opening. Raeleq dropped his club and slammed his fist into the ghoul’s eyeless head several times until it released him.

Another leaped forward, claws and teeth bared and ready to sink into the younger Ransa’s chest, but slowed mid-air. There!

Raeva cast [Singe] and the quick, nearly instant spell flew from her hand to the floating ghoul, right into its open maw. Even if it didn’t do much damage on the first hit, it flashed inside and it dropped dead a second later. The Timer ticked, and with it a new round.

Round 5

Her deck shuffled, and a second card deactivated. Now she had to fight without [Combust] and [Obsidian Spear]. The latter wasn’t right for this fight anyway, and the former could hurt her friends if she wasn’t careful.

Cantrip: [Singe], Spell: [Flame Lance], Creature: [Kindling Phoenix], Spell: [Hotfoot], Free Spell: [Flame Lance]

Raeva had a few seconds to mentally catch her breath before things resumed. She looked at the entrance of the tunnel, the pile of dead ghouls outside and in it, and the fresh faces climbing up over their fallen packmates. From where she stood she had a perfect angle to turn things around.

Everyone else was far enough away to not get hit by the blasts. Probably. That included the annoying kid and the weird kobold they now had to worry about. They were behind them, readying a betrayal for all they knew.

No, not worth thinking about.

She took a deep breath, and the second she could move again, she cast her first [Flame Lance]. The bolt of superhot, compressed flames formed in her hand, close enough to almost burn her, but the magic in the cards protected her. The flare of heat had become a comforting thing, a reminder of the power she could unleash at a moment’s notice.

The next ghoul climbed over a body, just in time for her to hurl the spell. The fire pierced through it, and then detonated a second later, blowing it apart and sending rocks and dirt scattering everywhere. She didn’t wait to see if it worked. The second it hit, she cast the other one and threw it as well, gleeful of the timer ticking down as it exploded more ghouls.

The tunnel rumbled ominously. They had enough time to anticipate the crash before it collapsed entirely. She covered her ears to block out the horrible grinding of rocks and earth as the rest of the tunnel leading out broke down as well.

Raeleq and Luthor backed the hell up away from the entrance as dust and smoke kicked up and washed over them. Raeva coughed and heard Jessica and Luthor doing the same. They retreated, just a few steps back, until her foot touched something soft, spongy, and a bit sticky: webbing.

The dust settled, and a pressure left her mind as combat ended. She huffed for air, heart racing with excitement. Everyone turned to look at her as if she was crazy.

“What?” she demanded. “Problem solved.”

“Yeah,” said Ambrose, looking just as worn out. “And you got us locked in a terrifying spider-cave.

There was that. “We don’t know if there are any actual spiders here, right?” Jessica asked nervously.

“Oh yes, I can hear them,” Raeleq said, cradling his wounded arm to his chest. It bled freely, huge gouges ripped out of it. He and Luthor had been the only ones to take any real damage, and not for the first time, Raeva felt a surge of gratitude towards her strong, protective brother.

“Great,” said Dexi’Lynn, still smoking her pipe. Jack hid behind her, looking absolutely terrified. “Well, we might as well enjoy ourselves before we get eaten. Anyone got a flask on them?”
