“It’s time for the breaking news everyone has been hearing about!” Allan exclaims while leaning forwards with his fingers linked and his arms resting on the counter in front of him. “The very first battle broadcast live from a Pioneer inside of a monster’s body while in a dungeon!”

“Up until now, there has never been an aquatic dungeon before,” Rodrick says, expanding on the host’s words. “Not once have we seen a battle against a monster of this scale in a dungeon. Because, as we all know, the largest monsters in the universe tend to be aquatic ones.”

“No one knows the exact reason why the largest monsters tend to be aquatic ones,” Allan says as the screen behind them changes to show Aqua’s broadcast from a few minutes ago. Back when she was outside of the monster. All to show the enormous monster’s true size to the audience. “Many researchers believe it’s due to the environment of the deep waters allowing the aquatic monsters the capability to move freely regardless of their actual weight underwater. And others believe it’s thanks to their traits. But no one knows the real reason.”

“But now we get to watch a battle from inside of a monster!” Rodrick exclaims with a grin on his face. “So let’s table that discussion for later and get started!”

The screen changes to show Aqua in her current broadcast. As she digs her fangs into the monster’s exoskeleton from within and tries hard to rip an opening in the creature’s armor.

To dig deeper inside of its body.

“It’s beginning to crack!” Allan shouts while leaning forwards. “Hey, Rodrick, how do you feel about her use of her jaws as of late?”

“I’ve heard a lot of people are finding it rather unique,” Rodrick answers with a nod. “It feels like a branch off from her usual cute and beautiful image. Seeing her turning her head into that of an Abyssal Orca and tearing into monsters with her jaws.”

“I know what you mean,” Allan agrees while grinning. “What was it called again? A moe gap or something?”

“I think that’s the term,” Rodrick says without taking his gaze away from the screen as cracking sounds fill the monster’s eye socket.

“Well, it’s something like that,” Allan continues, deciding it doesn’t matter what the exact term is. “Still. This battle is going to be a difficult one! It’s a race against the clock! To see if she can outlast the poison to kill the boss or not!”

Rodrick nods his head while staring at the screen. At which point both host and co-host go quiet.

Just watching the screen as Aqua finally manages to tear off part of the monster’s exoskeleton. Revealing a more fleshy inside to the monster.

They continue watching Aqua begin ripping her way inside of the monster, tearing flesh apart with ease all while her veins begin to slowly turn black across her face. And no doubt across the rest of her body as well, including the parts covered by her armor.

But then, when she gets deeper into the monster’s body, something unexpected happens that shocks both her and the people watching.

Some of the black liquid in the monster’s body begins to take the shape of fish that rushes to attack her.

Aqua

What the hell are these things?

I quickly begin tearing apart the blood monsters inside of the massive boss monster. But no matter how many of them I kill, they continue coming.

One after another. Delaying my progress tearing into the monster.

When I realized the monster beyond its exoskeleton was just normal flesh, albeit blackened and slightly rotten like an undead, I thought it would be smooth sailing from here. Since it’s not hard to tear through this flesh unlike its exoskeleton and scales.

But now?

With these monsters attacking me?

It’s making it far harder to focus on digging through the monster’s body.

And to make matters worse, these blood monsters are somehow seeping straight through my armor. Injuring me directly while ignoring my armor in the process.

All while their attacks that hit me cause the poison in me to spread further.

I grit my teeth at the pain from the poison. Which makes me feel weaker and weaker while a stabbing, burning pain spreads all throughout my body.

And while my regeneration is fighting against it, slowing down the poison’s spread, it is still spreading. Bit by bit.

The pain continues to grow worse as I kill the blood monsters and continue digging deeper into the boss’s body. But I don’t just sit back and let this continue.

I begin summoning abyssal servants inside of the boss, leaving them to deal with the blood monsters as I focus a bit more on digging.

And digging.

And digging some more.

All the way until I find some sort of organ. But it’s not in the shape of anything I’ve seen before. So I have no idea what organ this is.

Despite that, I use my claw to dig right into the organ. Making whatever venom I have enter the thing.

Up until now I don’t think my venom has been spreading much. After all, I haven’t been attacking any major arteries or veins or any organs.

But with this, an organ that seems to have a bunch of arteries and veins attached to it?

I immediately see an effect as my pitch black venom begins to spread through the creature’s body at a visible pace.

And as it does, I realize there’s something strange about the boss.

Not only is it roaring on the outside in pain, but its movements look rather constricted.

But more importantly, its flesh begins to slowly turn into the same shade as my abyssal servants. And I feel a tiny spark of a bond beginning to form.

Just like with my abyssal servants.

My eyes narrow.

Does my venom… turn other beings into abyssal servants?

A grin slowly begins to spread across my face at this realization.

Is this any different from using my Abyssal Ruler skill? Maybe there’s some special benefit to using my venom?

Regardless of what the venom is doing, I continue to dig through the monster’s body from the inside. Completely destroying the organ after the venom has already begun spreading.

And when I do, the monster lets out a loud roaring screech of pain. All while more blood monsters made of its pitch black blood appear and begin attacking.

But I don’t stop and begin summoning more abyssal servants to fight against the boss’s own blood creatures.

The blood creatures don’t have any identify result, nor do they have a level. So they’re probably just a part of the boss’s skills.

Some sort of defensive skill to deal with any monster or thing it eats that doesn’t die in its mouth.

Which makes sense. Monsters of this size need to have something like that to protect themselves from parasites or things trying to enter their body and kill them from the inside.

And that can’t just be its poisonous blood. Which I still don’t know what is doing to me.

All I know is that its poison hurts.

Badly.

But not as badly as losing a limb or my heart. So it’s fine to continue pushing through.

I can’t imagine what the poison would do to me if I didn’t have my regeneration.

With that thought in mind, I continue to dig my way through the monster’s body. And when I have enough space at last, I begin to summon some of my larger abyssal servants and let them dig through its body with me.

In different directions, to be specific.

After all, I have no idea if this boss has a brain or a heart or something like that. And I have no idea where its critical organs are.

Or if it even has any. Seeing as it’s an undead by the looks of it.

I’ll find out eventually though.

As I continue digging deeper and deeper into its body. Fighting off its blood creatures all along the way.
