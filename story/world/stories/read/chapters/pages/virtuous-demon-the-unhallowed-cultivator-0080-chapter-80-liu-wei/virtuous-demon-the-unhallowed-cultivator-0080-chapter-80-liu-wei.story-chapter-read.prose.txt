Six years ago, in the Liu Clan’s Grassheart Pavilion, a twelve year old Shuren, clad in silver robes, was led around the courtyard by members of the clan.

A verdant prairie expanded into the horizon where golden oaken trees glistened with the whistling of bladed leaves, with sounds of sharp steel slicing resounding along with the wind.

One of the clan members at his side adorned with azure robes, Liu Guanli, had a face of pride as he explained to Shuren, “Young master, this is our main resource point for Willow Leafblades, A Rank 6 Immortal material used to make blade Zeal Artifacts. The oak is also used for-”

“Enough.” The young Shuren cut off Guanli. Do you think I’m unaware of our Liu Clan’s territory? Originally, these Willow Leafblades capable of cutting apart steel with ease were simple adornments for grandfather’s tea. Unfortunately, it will be a long time before I’m capable of appreciating his creation.”

Guanli’s gaze flickered to the silver robe that Shuren wore before clearing his throat. “My apologies young master. I thought it was worth showing you around since you just left your training with the Sword Saint.”

When the cries of a child clashed with the whistling of the wind, Guanli sent a sharp glare at the second clan member on Shuren’s side, signaling him to detour Shuren.

With a wave of his palm, Shuren ceased those notions. “No need, let’s go take a look together.”

Guanli forced his expression to remain neutral as he continued, “Very well young master. Let us go.” He could only hope the situation wouldn’t escalate.

It wouldn’t take long for them to see a brown robed child, the same age as Shuren, being beaten up by a Vulfoan adorned with a servant’s attire, laced with cyan and brown. Noticing the presence near her, she ended the punishment abruptly.

“Bastard, next time Lady Seon commands you of an errand, you are to listen without questioning.” With a tap of her shoes on the ground, the servant bowed towards Shuren. “This servant of Lady Seon pays her respect to young master Shuren.”

Shuren ignored her, instead facing Guanli before pointing to the beaten child. “Brown robes. What does that signify?”

Guanli clicked his tongue when he faced towards the servant, irritated at the disruption. “It’s for those with the blood of the clan but birthed from an unofficial marriage, and from a mortal. Just a bastard, but he still has the name of Liu.”

Shuren couldn’t help but look with curiosity.

Blue robes were for clan members that have achieved merits for the clan or direct descendants of elders, gaining a higher status. Silver robes were only reserved for the authority of the clan, alongside the Grand Disciple.

There would be only one such disciple in each clan or sect, who would receive immense tutorage and investment of resources. Often trained and groomed as the most capable of the current generation, they were the hope to succeed the leader or attain new heights, securing new alliances and gaining sponsorships from forces around the world.

Guanli continued, “Though they aren’t exactly rare, this one’s more known for failing to kill a wolf in the initial test for all Liu clan members.”

The silent expression on Shuren’s face soon shifted to one of curiosity. “He failed such an easy test? How is he still alive?”

“Supposedly, he refused to shed blood, refusing to complete the test. Naturally, many do not take heart to his blatant disrespect to the rules set by our ancestors.”

Shuren stared at the open palm of his right hand.

Guanli faced towards the regal sky ship that loomed in the distance, donning the banner of a golden crown laced with purple gems, and then clapped his hands. “Either way, it’s of no matter. The Unchained Crown Sect is about to land, so let us make haste. You don’t need to ‘take a walk for some fresh air’ right now, young master.”

Shuren walked with an open palm towards the servant, still bowing towards him, her neck laid bare. “Since when could a servant touch a Liu clan member?”

The servant of brown hair’s ears twitched as she felt goosebumps on her neck, but she refused to move. “Young master Shuren, these were under the commands of Lady Seon. As you know, she is the granddaughter of one of the Grand Elders, Supreme Sword Heifan. She is-”

With a squelch, one of her fuzzy ears fell to the grass, dying it red.

“The Path of the Sword does not care for your lineage or blood. What matters is the retention of our scriptures, reverence for the sword and resolution in your heart.” His icy cold eyes gazed upon the servant, with no resemblance of compassion, until he turned his back away from her.

“The next time Seon asks you of such a ludicrous command, say you couldn’t hear her,” Shuren proclaimed with nonchalance as his open palm returned by his side, absent of the blood.

The servant’s shoulders shuddered, but she didn’t dare renege on her bow. Sweat dropped off her forehead, scrunched up as she forced no sound to come out. The status of the child in front of her was even above her master’s grandfather.

Guanli’s silver eyes shuddered at the sight. “He truly has a Heavenly Blessing. Without even entering Qidense Realm, his martial prowess with the sword has reached the attainment of the legendary Blade Testament, with the capacity to use even a finger as a blade. The rumours of him being born with a mythical Talispirit and a Talisoul beyond Gold Grade is true!”

His adam's apple moved as he swallowed his spit, shock soon overcome with wonder. “If he grows uninhibited, he will truly become the successor of the Sword Saint, leading the Liu Clan to new heights. I may even see our clan become one of the Great Nine of the Grand Immortals Conclave in my lifetime!”

Guanli looked over to his junior, commanding him to act. “Loran, take the beast girl back to Lady Seon and make sure she doesn’t make a fuss. Use your master Azure Lotus’s name to keep her in place. There’s no need for the Supreme Sword to take notice of this trivial matter.”

Loran nodded flitted across the grass, still keeping some distance to wait for the young master Shuren to leave. Thoughts encroached on his mind to get even closer to Shuren. His influence would only continue to grow within the clan, and even more so outside.

As for the servant? Getting rid of her would insult Lady Seon, so he would diligently return her servant back.

Shuren’s cyan eyes filled with the embers of curiosity gazed at the battered and bruised body of the bastard. He knelt down at his side to extend a hand. “Why are you pretending to be unconscious? Get up.”

The dark haired boy regained vigour, taking Shuren’s hand to stand up. Even with his state, his expression held no reverence, nor pain. As if he was long used to the misery inflicted on him.

“Why do you hold back?” Shuren asked.

The boy looked down at his feet before answering. “I… I don’t like seeing blood.”

Shuren didn’t say a word as he thought with confusion. It felt contradictory, seeing the bruised boy saying such a thing. Fighting was a natural part of life, let alone in a sword clan, where cutting and bleeding was the norm. It was when he thought back to a saying from his grandfather.

Under the skies, the shine in each person’s blade will always reflect a unique tale.

So what was the reflection of a bastard in a sword clan who hated to draw blood?

“What’s your name?” Shuren asked.

The boy stilled for a moment, looking into his face. When he saw the eyes of Shuren, there was only one word that came to his mind.

Sharp.

“Young master, my name is Wei.”

Now, his younger cousin had come to his doorstep half crippled. Shuren forced his fury to slumber, immediately focusing on healing him. “Wei, it’ll be fine.” Shuren grabbed one of his personal healing pills, its ability to give the properties of healing to a person's Qi. “Ingest this and cycle your Qi throughout all your injuries.”

Shuren held his cousin’s body in his left arm, cradling his body. Yet, Wei spluttered out a few more words.

“Cousin, I can’t.”

“What?”

“I can’t cycle my Qi.” He began to tear up at those words.

“What! Where did this happen? Who did this to you?” Shuren urged Wei to quickly reply. If he still retained these injuries, his life as a swordsman would end on the spot. The largest disgrace in one’s honour as a Liu clan member, and combied with Wei’s status it wouldn’t go well.

He could feel Wei’s conscious slipping.

“Ev- Evening Clouds…T-Te…”

Shuren gritted his teeth, summoning his flying nimbus, expanding its shape to include a soft bed. He carefully lay his cousin’s body on it, moving towards Sensority Mountain. Shuren crushed that pill into his own mouth, hoping that he could use his own imbued Qi to slow the severity of Wei’s injuries.

Though it would’ve had a minor effect, like wrapping a blood wound with thin paper that would be drenched quickly, it was better than nothing.

He put both his hands on the lower stomach of Wei, gently cycling his Qi into Wei’s body. Blood continued to spurt out of his body, rejecting the notion of healing, as if it refused all sources of Qi.

Wei moaned in shock and his eyes rolled back, finally losing consciousness. as if he had only been holding onto that strand to find his cousin. Who he knew would look after him.

“Too far, whoever did this has gone too far.” Shuren’s body shivered as he almost went almost dizzy in anger. The ambience and chatter of disciples as he landed on Sensority Mountain was drowned out by constant ringing. He didn’t even bother reigning in his emotions.

He had just come back from a deadly mission where he had to face off demonic cultivators in defence of Cloud City and the Seven Clouds Sect.

Only to come back to his own cousin being beaten to an inch of his life in the sect iself!

When the disciples sighted the sorry state of Wei in Shuren’s arms, they flocked towards him.

Before Shuren even had a chance to yell out for help, A brown haired girl ran up to him. She held her hands firmly on Wei’s stomach to identify the core issues in his body, a yellow wisp of aura shifting around her eyes. “Fellow disciple, quickly head to the emergency hall up the mountain. It’s impossible for anyone here to give him quick treatment. ”

Shuren nodded in thanks, swiftly heading up the mountain to the core facility of Sensority Mountain. A few of the senior disciples followed along to give a hand. It was a normal sight to see injured disciples with broken limbs or the like, but the only way they’d come here is if it was due to a duel or infighting. If anyone arriving from a missive needed healing, they would just head straight to Evening Clouds that lay at the bottom of the Seven Mountains, which had healers designated to accommodate them.

If it was a duel, this was the worst state they’d ever seen someone come in with. A public duel would always be stopped before things got too far. This was the Seven Clouds Sect, and the Sect Leader didn’t run it like the generic orthodox sects that let their disciples maim and disable each other.

Shuren had finally entered the Sensority Mountain’s emergency hall. Within one breath of stepping foot inside, a couple of healers, not wearing robes, but rather sheer white clothing with masks and gloves, had already gathered Wei in a hammock and led him to a room. Shuren stood there in a trance, still trying to calm himself down. His raging emotions of fury and confusion were twirled around like tangled snakes.

A few of the senior disciples had also headed inside to either give a hand or understand what the issue was. All of them were confused when trying to pinpoint the main issue with Wei’s body, refusing to even accept Qi.

The worst case scenario was imminent.

Shuren stood near the entrance for over an hour, without moving a step. His forehead creased, and his eyes darkened, staring at the floor. What exactly led up to this situation? I heard Wei had only recently become an Inner Disciple, and he had ended up in this state after? Everyone knows he is my cousin, yet they would dare do this… Even if it was the Core Disciple of some elder, I won’t take this insult to my face, the desecration to my honour and clan lying down!

A young man with strands of crimson hair peeking out of his head gown walked towards Shuren. Before he could even say a word, Shuren had clasped his fists together, bowing his head down in respect.

“My sincerest gratitudes for helping my cousin. Please, can you tell me of his situation?”

“You are… Liu Shuren, correct? You can raise your head, I’m just the assistant here to inform you of my senior’s message,” with a hand raised up, the assistant healer waited for Shuren to stand face to face with him. Only then did he continue.

“His Spirit Meridians have collapsed. Because of the shock, Wei has gone into a coma. Right now, his prospects as a cultivator are… Let’s just say that his body and the immune system that’s connected to his Qi has to slowly regenerate all of his Spirit Meridians. We’re getting another senior to assist us.”

Shuren stayed still for a moment, and even his facial expressions barely moved. But the tics in his face, the slightest trembling of his blue eyes, the clenching of his bruised right hand, and the half twitch in his left eye, was apparent to the red-haired healer. So he made a suggestion, in hopes of calming Shuren down.

“Go to the Administrative Hall, and use my name, Wuden, as witness to begin an investigation. I believe even the Seven Clouds Sect wouldn’t ignore the fact that their own disciples have almost caused someone to be crippled,” Wuden said in an attempt to ease Shuren’s mind. And though his face was covered by a simple healer’s mask, the expression in his black eyes softened as he consoled Shuren.

“Don’t worry too much. This isn’t the first time we’ve dealt with this, and plenty of people have managed to regenerate collapsed Spirit Meridians. I'll send a message so our experienced seniors can come and assist.”

Shuren clasped his fists once more, this time even bowing down in respect. “I am in your debt, Wuden. In the future, if you ever need help, I implore you to use my name and ask for my assistance. Please, help my cousin.”

With a nod, Wuden left back with haste, and Shuren left the cold building.

And when met with the warm air of midday, the metal bang on Shuren’s left wrist vibrated. With flying nimbus on his feet, he left the Sensority Mountain with haste.

When gazing towards the direction of the Evening Clouds, Shuren crossed his arms as he ruminated out loud. “Even if someone had grievances with Wei and held a duel, it shouldn’t have gotten to this extent. The Seven Clouds, unlike other sects, have always been strict on this matter.”

He thought back to how the Liu Clan functioned up the north, and his father’s own experiences in other sects. Over there, these scenarios happened often, but Shuren had long signed off the notion that such a thing would happen to someone related to him. Having taken for granted how peaceful and stable Seven Clouds Sect was, his respect and reverence for the Sect Leader grew even further.

With a resolute face, Shuren headed down the mountains.

“If I find this person…”

“I’ll make sure to punish him with even worse injuries than the one he dared to give to my cousin!”

“This, I swear on my soul!”
