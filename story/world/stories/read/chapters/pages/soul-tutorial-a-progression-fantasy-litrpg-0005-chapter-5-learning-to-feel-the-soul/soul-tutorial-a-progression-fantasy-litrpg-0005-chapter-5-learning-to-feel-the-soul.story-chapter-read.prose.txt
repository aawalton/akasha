He felt a restoration fall upon him; his fatigue, hunger, and thirst vanished. Ideally, it was as if a whole new world had opened up before him. He felt rejuvenated, as if he had just been reborn. He couldn’t get used to this feeling, though. Something still felt off, as if a part of him remained empty.

‘Hmm... don’t tell me I can’t absorb soul energy,’ Luke thought, placing his hand on the pedestal before deciding to continue farming floor 1 creatures. He had considered farming Blue Slimes instead, as it would increase his growth a lot faster, they gave more points and maybe more soul energy, but he wasn’t ready for that just yet.

The island’s energy concentrated on one spot when another Green Slime appeared. Luke rushed over and used his usual strategy to attack. He blasted through them with full force, hoping they wouldn’t find a chance to retaliate.

Thanks to his newfound restoration, Luke fought with almost no mistakes. No longer slowed down, he didn’t feel the constant pain from his past wounds. His physical strength could finally be used at 100%.

He exploded forward; the ground shook beneath his feet. Just like in the previous battles, he killed and killed. Points racked up, souls rose, enveloping the sky with pure dots of light. Luke wondered if there was a limit to how many souls this place could hold.

Luke consistently used restore whenever he suffered serious injuries, then jumped right back into farming. Before needing to restore, he would kill around 5 - 7 slimes. But he never stopped being conscious of the fact that death lingered around every corner. He always kept himself in top shape.

The only thing that could stop his ongoing massacre was if something unusual happened or if he grew too mentally exhausted to continue the slaughter.

Then it happened.

A glowing red panel appeared in front of him:The requirement has been met.

A timer suddenly appeared above the area where slimes usually spawned, counting down from 10 minutes.

[Elite Green Slime]

Why does this world hate me? He watched as the minutes ticked by; his body locked in place. Every passing second, the tension in the air grew. Sweat trickled down his face, hearing it splattering against the ground.

Nervousness flooded his system as he tried to take in what this could mean.

“If this could mean the difference between life and death, I need to do something!” Luke said as he quickly rushed towards the pedestal and went to find one branch he had prepared. They were dwindling in number, as there were only a few left from his original branch.

Luke braced himself. He lowered his stance, crouched low to the ground, and slowly turned toward the timer, ever coming closer to zero. His heart pounded wildly in his chest. He felt of despair in his gut that he couldn’t get rid of. He didn’t know how this slime would attack. It could mimic the basic green slimes or fight completely differently with stronger, deadlier strikes.

‘If this isolated space were a game, I’d be a level 5 character. And that thing... It’s at least level 10,’ he thought.

Fighting something this much stronger than him was a unique experience. This could be the end of an adventure he hadn’t even begun.

The energy fluctuated wildly. Luke held his breath as he watched it form, a mass of green, acidic gel. Luke remained motionless, legs quivering from the aura of the Elite Slime. It was massive; he had to look up to take in its full size. Was defeating this thing even possible at his current strength?

The slime stood at a massive 2 meters tall. ‘This slime has to be slow or something.’ He thought.

“I hope I gain something from this,” Luke muttered hopelessly. He watched cautiously, slowly moving around the island as the Elite Slime just stood there.

‘Maybe there was a range until they take notice of me.’

When he was finally behind the slime on the opposite side of the pedestal. He charged like a bull, hoping to surprise the slime. His heels dug into the dirt and launched him forward. Deep grooves tore through the ground.

The slime reacted, gazing toward Luke. As he drew nearer, Luke leapt, but the Elite was faster. The air compressed on Luke’s chest as the massive slime slammed into him, knocking him back near the edge of the island. He barely had time to react before a second attack slammed into his side. He heard the sickening crack of his ribs shattering.

His body was flung across the island. His weapon flew from his grasp, landing on the other side of him.

Luke had no actual combat training, especially not for this situation. He had no quick movements, no practiced techniques, only instinct. He had only ever fought slimes. This was his first true life-or-death battle. He was utterly unprepared. But at least, he thought, he was stronger than many who had trained their bodies under brutal conditions. The stat points he had assigned had made a noticeable difference in his physical abilities.

Luke knew he had to change his approach. He could barely grasp the strength of this slime. He shifted himself back into a fighting stance, right leg forward, body tilted, ready to redirect the slime’s weight. If he couldn’t overpower it, he at least had to perform well enough to survive. He also couldn’t ignore the danger of the slime’s acid.

Adrenaline rushed through him; the pain in his side suddenly vanished.

He waited.

The slime rushed forward like a cannonball, shaking the ground beneath it. Luke barely dodged to the right; the creature now slid behind him. He ran towards a branch by the pedestal.

I need to have at least this to have a chance of winning. Every step felt heavy, but he pushed through, almost falling. He found his balance and picked up a branch. The island trembled again as the slime was closing in behind him.

Luke instantly turned around with a firm grip on the branch. He found the slime only a few meters away from him. His eyes grew wide as he saw the slime leap to pounce on him. He threw his whole body away, hoping to dodge.

As he had hoped, the Slime missed, as he heard something huge flying past him. He quickly picked himself back up on his feet. Ready to start the first move. Quickly rushed in, swinging his branch toward it with everything he had, grazing the top. The slime barely flinched. Luke brought his arm back, hoping to land another hit while he still had the chance.

However, it was dangerously careless.

He was too slow. The slime struck him like a bullet, slamming into his chest. His body went limp, bouncing across the dirt before collapsing near the edge of the island. He had come close to death again.

Luke realized physical attacks wouldn’t be enough.

He had to use the souls.

His trauma from nearly dying before while channeling energy resurfaced. ‘Could this really be the end?’ But hadn’t he already danced with death so many times?

His determination flared.

As the slime darted toward him again, Luke hurled his body to the side. The slimes couldn’t fall off the island. He had tested that before with regular ones. Using that knowledge, he sprinted toward the pedestal, gathering his focus. Slowly, he drew souls from the sky. He didn’t want to repeat the near-death experience from overexertion. He fought to stay mentally grounded, dividing his attention between the Elite Slime and the drifting souls above.

His heartbeat thudded loudly in his ears.

He felt exhausted. Stressed. Desperate for this to be over. ‘Why did an Elite Slime have to spawn now?’ Was his luck really this bad?

The slime prepared to launch itself again. The ground trembled beneath it as the slime compressed itself slowly, preparing for a powerful attack. Small amounts of ethereal energy were released from beneath the slime.

Luke didn’t notice what was unfolding.

One soul vanished, then another.

He knew if he gathered any more, he’d risk death. But the energy he had absorbed was still too little. His ribs burned less. Cuts and bruises slightly faded. Luke didn’t seem to take notice of this.

Slowly channeling the soul energy into his hand, he was careful not to damage his insides. He didn’t fully understand the power he was wielding or what it could become. He had also noticed that brighter souls appeared after killing stronger monsters.

Now, all the energy was in his right hand, but it was unstable, leaking from his palm. His hand felt alien, no longer his own. Slowly stretching his arm. But he had to push through. He had to weaponise it. He had to win.

Luke’s face tightened with resolve.

This was the moment.

The slime was prepared.

The ground near it stopped shaking. A godly green glow pulsed from its body, illuminating the island.

It was only an elite monster on the first floor. How much stronger could others be?

The slime appeared instantly before Luke without him realizing it. The slime made no sound. But Luke did everything he could.

He punched forward, forcing the energy to wrap around his fist, his arm extended in front of his face. The slime loomed over him. The force of the attack destroyed everything. Branches vanished. Grass and dirt lifted from the earth. Pebbles whipped through the air like bullets.

Luke felt his hand shatter. Nails, skin, muscle, bone. Crimson blood sprayed across the ground.

The slime shrank, with a gaping hole through it, its whole body dissolving. Acid disperses across the island, boiling Luke’s blood away and carving into the island.

And then.

At the point of impact.

The energy imploded, enveloping Luke and the rest of the slime.

That was the end.
