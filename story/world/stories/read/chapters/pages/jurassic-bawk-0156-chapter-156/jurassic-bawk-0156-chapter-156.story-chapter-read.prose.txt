Liang Xing relaxed on her throne as she examined the pangolin sitting across from her. Well, that wasn't entirely accurate: it was only a projection of her greatest Champion, Phatagin, that she had summoned. Nevertheless, her deific senses extended through the connection, allowing her to see for herself just how much her Champion had grown during the months that he had been away. She had almost finished her debrief of her Follower's time with the crazy chicken, Charlemagne, but there were still a few points left to cover. 
 

"Well, I must say that I am more than pleased with your performance, Phatagin. You have not only provided me with an enormous amount of Divinity, but the evolutionary changes that you have undergone will allow us to strengthen our collective gene pool, since your offspring will be smarter, stronger, tougher, and more Skilled in general. Let the other species play their little terraforming games and tinker with external aids. The future will belong to those whose genetics are able to keep up with the rapidly rising environmental mana rates."

 

Phatagin smiled upon hearing his deity's praise and puffed out his chest in pride. The movement sent a twinge of pain through his chest, an unwelcome reminder that his Momentum Core had yet to finish healing.

 

"You are quite welcome, great one. It pleases me to no end that I was able to be of such service to you, and I appreciate the rewards that you have bestowed upon me, which are far more than I deserve," he responded with great deference.

 

"Nonsense, if anything, I was not able to reward you enough. At the very least, I wish that I could give you something to help repair the last of the Core damage that you suffered, but the System appears to have limited the types of items that we can purchase from it. Your 'Shop Points' seem to be able to bypass that limit," the deity answered.

 

"That's correct," Phatagin confirmed. "I was able to purchase an elixir that has largely healed my Core, and the remaining damage should fade away on its own, given time. I believe that I will be combat-ready within a week, although fighting at full strength would not be wise."

 

"Are there any compatible genetic enhancements that you can still purchase from the System's Shop?"

 

"If you will allow me, I will check now," the pangolin's projection promised. Liang Xing nodded in affirmation. There was a long moment of silence as Phatagin perused his options.

 

"There are a number of enhancements that the System indicates are compatible with my genetic makeup, but most require more Points than I possess," Phatagin lamented.

 

"Interesting," the deity mused. "And there is no way for you to currently earn more Shop Points? You only earned them through these 'Systemic Games'?"

 

"That is correct, although we earned them through the Elite Systemic Games as well. These were the games that were interrupted just before I was sent back."

 

"Right, it was fortunate for us that the System chose you to accompany Charlemagne into the Elite Games. If my guess is correct, the System appears to have a...I wouldn't call it a bias per se, but some sort of affinity for those with Core Skills, at least based on what you have told me," Liang Xing surmised.

 

"I am afraid I do not follow. I believe that I was chosen because my Skills were better suited to aiding Charlemagne than those possessed by the others."

 

"That very well may be part of it," the deity agreed, "but I believe that the larger reason was that many of the unique and powerful individuals you encountered possessed Cores of their own. Certainly, the two sisters did, and both of them performed extraordinary feats. I would not be surprised if possessing a Core Skill affected an individual's growth in more ways than one."

 

"I believe I see your point now, yes. As the only other Party member with a Core, it is possible the System rated my ability to adapt higher than Sirius' or even Ndiogou's. Possessing a Core gives me more flexibility in battle as well, allowing me to contribute in a number of ways. Perhaps the System recognizes that Core Skills make for good entertainment?"

 

"Perhaps, and I would love to discuss this further with you, but maintaining this conversation is taxing and there is one more matter I need to bring up. Are you aware that Charlemagne gave Sirius and Ndiogou a task before they were returned to Earth?"

 

"Not at all," the pangolin answered with a shake of his head. "Charlemagne was...is not a great conversationalist. His communication with me largely involved him demanding things or ordering everyone around him to take care of any details he found boring. Why, this one time..."

 

"I will take your word for it," Liang Xing broke in quickly, the barest hint of annoyance creeping into her tone. "So you were not aware that the rooster is in possession of a large farm in the United States?"

 

"I was not aware, no, but such news does not surprise me. I take it that the farm is being monitored?"

 

"Yes," the pangolin deity confirmed. "Given its importance, the farm itself, and the area around it, has become a bit of a neutral zone. After the incident with the meteor, most deities understand the rooster's ridiculous power level, and no one wants to be responsible for any damage, however accidental, to Charlemagne's flock. There are also a number of humans who live on or around the farm, taking advantage of the peaceful surroundings to export eggs and grow crops. They've even created a Settlement that guarantees the safety of all living beings, which is quite a feat. There's just one small problem."

 

"Hmm...could it be that not everyone is happy with peaceful coexistence?" the pangolin speculated.

 

"Exactly," the deity confirmed. "And while the tension between Formua Integralis and the alliance of the great mammals neither benefits nor harms me, President Brett has made me an offer that will yield long-term benefits for us. He's even set up a special breeding area in the Settlement and stocked it."

 

Phatagin blushed.

 

"If I am not mistaken, you are implying that..."

 

"Yes, Phatagin, your DNA is now suitable for propagating, and the guides on selective breeding I purchased from the System indicated that, given the relatively low ambient mana levels on Earth, both genetic donors should be below level 100 for best results. As you are currently level 88, there is no point in waiting any longer. Your mates have already been selected and transported to the Settlement, a feat that cost me no small number of favors."

 

"My deity and Patron, I am forever in your debt," Phatagin responded with a bow. "How can I ever repay your generosity?"

 

"As I said earlier, you misapprehend the direction of indebtedness between us. To be honest, pangolins are on the brink: the System integration was more difficult than I expected. So, if you must repay me, you can do it in two ways. First, stay alive, and, second, have lots of babies."

 

Phatagin's blush deepened as he cleared his throat.

 

"I do recall you saying that President Brett had made you an offer. Might I know the nature of the deal?" he asked.

 

Liang Xing smiled, catching how quick the bashful pangolin was to change the subject. 

 

"Ah, yes, that is the third thing you can do for me. Keep an eye on the farm, please. If anything happens to it, there's nowhere on Earth that will be safe."

 

 

"Whelp, the farm has completely disappeared," Brett informed Grimfalk as soon as the theropod deity had finished seating himself across from the President of GOD. 

 

"What do you mean it's disappeared!?" Grimfalk rumbled. "We had a deal that you would ensure its safety!"

 

Brett looked up sharply and held Grimfalk's gaze until the theropod looked away.

 

"I know what I agreed to," he responded with more charity than he felt. "And, if I could have prevented whatever just happened, I would have. Right now, I have a lot of angry deities messaging me for answers, and I have none to give them."

 

"Maybe we should beat the answer out of Formua Integralis," the bulky reptile growled. "I'm certain that she was behind it. Maybe she undermined the whole region and collapsed the farm that way!"

 

Brett smiled slightly at the theropod deity's use of the term "we". While it was undeniable that Grimfalk had grown stronger over the past few months, he was still nowhere near ready to take on one of the world's strongest deities. Even Brett wasn't keen on a one-on-one battle with the deity of ants and termites, although he knew that he had enough allies to make that proposition unlikely.

 

"That actually would have been quite the clever maneuver, Grimfalk. You're getting better at thinking up schemes."

 

"For millions of years, I subsisted on nothing but schemes," the theropod complained. "Now that I've got followers again, I've got a duty to take care of them."

 

The human deity thought back to when Raul Sanchez destroyed Charlemagne's first farm and said nothing. The pair sat in silence for a moment before Grimfalk continued.

 

"So, what's this I hear about you having trouble with a sunfish?"

 

Brett sighed.

 

"Everyone's been asking me about that. Yes, I'm having trouble with a mola mola. No, I don't need help. It's just very hard to fight something that shoots enormous energy beams from deep underwater. Very few of my humans can even dive down far enough to reach it. And, even if they could reach it, they would then need to kill it. But humans aren't really made for underwater combat."

 

"Well, theropods aren't really built for that either, and apparently roosters are afraid of water. Who knew?" Grimfalk said with a laugh. "Wasn't Canius working on some sort of aquatic canine? I think he called them 'merpups'?"

 

President Brett shook his head.

 

"That's just a vanity project. I've seen them and they are not nearly as durable as Canius thinks they are. I bet that those things will never actually survive underwater."

 

"Well, then how are you going to handle the sunfish?" Grimfalk insisted.

 

"I'll probably have to poison it, or figure out how to lure it close enough to the surface to take it out. Or, I'll just outsource the problem to a few of my local Champions and let them handle it....I don't have the time or the energy to micromanage humanity the way that you can do with your followers."

 

"Well I have lots of time per Champion, you know!" the theropod shot back. "And, uh, my Champions tend to be less self-sufficient than yours."

 

Brett snorted.

 

"That's because they are all literally bird-brained. Even Charlemagne, who is almost completely unkillable at this point, is probably going to manage to off himself doing something incredibly stupid someday. He's the type that would run straight into the heart of a star if you can get him mad enough. Wait, that's it! Grimfalk, you're a genius!"

 

"I am?! I mean, I am, yes, definitely. But, uh, could you please let me know exactly why I am a genius. You know, in this particular moment."

 

But before Brett could respond, Grimfalk received a very important System message about the very rooster he and the other deity were discussing.

 

"Oh wait, no need!" he continued. "Not only am I a genius, but the System just sent me some information that neatly solves our little farm problem. Would you like to hear it?"

 

"Yes, of course!" the human deity practically yelled, equally excited at the news and annoyed by the question.

 

"It told me that it was Charlemagne himself that requested that the farm be moved. It's now in what the System referred to as 'Interstitial Storage'. Apparently he did something really good and the System allowed him to request a reward in return."

 

"He gets stronger the more chicks he sires, right?" Brett inquired.

 

"Yup," Grimfalk confirmed with a grin.

 

"Just how ridiculously strong is that stupid rooster going to get?" Brett groaned.
