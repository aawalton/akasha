I tore into the first Forger before I even glanced at them. The glowing red shield surrounding them shattered. My claws sunk through their back, and they crumbled. The carcass vanished before I even thought about it. The two others turned in my direction, but just so slowly.

An arm went flying, followed by the one trying to stab at me with a sword. Blood went everywhere as I sliced and diced. The glowing black lines extending from my claws cut through almost everything. They passed through the bodies, and even into the walls of the stone tunnels.

By the time nothing was left moving, I’d gained enough control to take a deep breath.

My father wasn’t here.

Energy pulsed through me, making it hard to think.

Another burst of gunfire echoed down the tunnel, and I took off.

The Whisper in my inventory stabbed mentally at me, caused me to stumble.

[Tracking or Tasting?]

There was only one answer right now: Tracking.

The blood red notification didn’t bother me right at the moment.

A star appeared on my map, along with a blue dot that represented Strange. He moved quicker toward the star than I did. But, so did the black dots. I shot off down the tunnel again, moving much faster than I’d have imagined only days ago.

Three more lower-leveled Forgers got taken care of, and I didn’t even pause. I just literally cut through them on my way by.

The star moved slower on the map.

More and more gunfire echoed in my direction. These Forgers carried everything, from swords to gun-like weapons, but the only thing holding my attention was the star. Nothing else mattered.

A closed door blocked the tunnel, and beyond that, Strange stood near the star. Black glowing lines cut through the air and tore into the door just before I hit it.

A massive Forger swung a sword at my Father.

His eyes met mine as he parried the swing, and dodged the strength of the blow.

A second black blade-shape came up from the Forger’s other hand, aiming right for my father’s side.

Strange chirpped, and the sound crashed into the Forger.

That second let my father roll away.

[Lord General Varick, The Dual Edge, Council’s Guard, Level 312, Predator, Unknown.]

Matte-black armor covered the Forger, but pitted scars covered the entire thing. My claws sunk a few inches into the back as I slammed into the Lord General.

The Forger stumbled forward a few steps, but then recovered and stabbed at me with the long blade. My armor tanked the edge that just touched me, but underneath I felt it.

I yanked my claws out as I pushed off Varick and jumped away.

The darkness in the room was broken by a small, flickering light.

The Forger turned, and there was nothing. That death-black armor covered it entirely, with a helm only broken by a narrow slit with orange light coming from it.

He stepped toward me, or tried to. The shadows hid my position, and I darted at him, slashing at the side of his right knee.

The dark slices cut into the armor at least a few inches, but I knew I didn’t get a hit on his actual body. His sword came out of nowhere and stabbed where I’d been only a second before.

Strange tried to lead my father out of the room and back the way he'd, come but he moved much slower than he should have.

Get him out of here, I sent to Strange.

This wasn’t a fight he could win.

The lights in the room flickered again, this time brighter than before. I ignored it. Wrapping mana all around me, along with the shadows that danced in the room, I vanished again.

The Lord General’s glowing orange eyes flicked in the direction of the light, though. Then, toward the doors I’d come through.

I darted in again, once more targeting Varick’s knee. Just before the cuts hit, I spoke in a deep growl. “Standing here while the council is dying…”

He flinched at my voice, but dodged half the strike. Still, a chunk of the black armor cracked, revealing his body underneath. He jerked away and kept turning in a circle, trying to keep his eyes on me.

He swung before I could move. His blades snapped out, sending a flicker of mana through the room. Before it touched me, I moved, leaping up over the flat gust. Again I swiped at him, but this time, at his helm.

He tried to dodge, but it didn’t come naturally to him at all.

The cut sliced into his helm.

Gunfire cut into the room, surprising both of us. The bright red bullets slammed into the Lord General’s shoulder, melting areas on impact.

A red shield snapped into place, easily tanking the second round of fire.

My father stood in the doorway, with a gun pointed right at Varick.

The Forger twisted in that direction and pointed with his shorter sword.

Strange suddenly chirped from the far left, across the room from me. The Forger twisted at the last minute, and a blast shot in Strange’s direction.

I dove, cutting at that right knee a third time.

Blood sprayed as I cut through the joint.

The Forger crumbled, but swung his longer sword, catching the edge of my shoulder.

The blast he’d shot hit the side of the room, exploding. Stone shards went everywhere, filling the room with dust.

Strange!

Still, I didn’t move in that direction. Instead, I twisted back to the downed form of the Forger. I kicked at the shorter blade, which went flying across the room. Then, I swiped at his head where I’d hit him already.

A hand snapped out and grabbed my wrist, slamming me down to the floor. My face smashed into the stone, but I triggered my shield a moment after. It shattered, but slowed down the sword that I knew was coming. Spikes grew all around my wrist where he touched me. The claws on my left hand twisted, as I stabbed them into the narrow line of orange on his helm.

The super thin razor-sharp needles pierced the area, and mana flooded into my body, right before the sword made contact.

The Forger screamed, but it came out all muffled.

He dropped both my arm and the sword, trying to remove my needles from his eyes.

The cuts in my armor healed as another gush of mana flowed in from my hand. My other hand stabbed into his chest, right after my claws thinned into the needles.

I shook as energy surged through me, then he stopped moving. The notification caused me to yank away, stumbling back.

[You have gained bonus experience from combat for surviving against a level 312, General Varick.]

[You have gained a level] x 5

[System Warning: Mana is becoming unstable in this area. Sudden changes may occur.]

Fear flickered along the bond with Strange, and I turned in that direction. My father dug at the rocks, trying to free him. He could barely push at one.

He flinched when I appeared at his side and lifted it off the small dinosaur.

Strange climbed to his feet and shook like a wet dog. Yet, miraculously, he was okay.

“You’re fine?” I asked, trying to wrap my mind around it. That hit had been massive.

Strange tilted his head. “Quest active! Shield Active!”

A cough came from my father. He clutched at his chest, struggling to catch his breath. “Too tight.”

“The mana is increasing,” I said in a low voice. My claws shifted from needles back into hands. Strange merged with my shadow.

“I know the way!” he said in my head.

A dotted line appeared on my map, and without thinking about it I scoped my father up in my arms like a princess and took off. The energy inside me needed to be used.

And I moved.

The tunnel I darted down suddenly glowed, but I didn’t dare stop.

Moss grew, coating the surface, and my wings burst from my shoulder blades, letting me take to the air.

I almost missed a tight turn, and had to kick off the wall as a surge of energy rose from behind us. Strange trembled with fear, and I didn’t dare look. I flew through two more turns, then a set of stairs that went up.

Vines that hadn’t been there before dangled from the ceiling of the cavern. Two of them snapped at me like snakes. I ducked, holding tight onto my father as we burst into the light.

The ground beneath us trembled, as the stone twisted and turned into a pool of water that slowly expanded. I flew us a little higher in the air, not wanting to get caught in that.

My father pointed at something in the distance.

It took a few seconds for me to realize it was the shuttle.

A blast of mana surged behind us, and I triggered my shield.

It shattered on contact, and the mana wave tore into my wings. I wrapped my arms around my father as we started to fall, just missing the edge of the floating mountain.

I might survive a fall like this, but he wouldn’t.

“Use… the shoot…” he said, trying to catch his breath. He still had surprises after all.

I smiled and pulled back from him.

He fumbled with the straps on the backpack, trying to take them off.

“Use it,” I said, giving him a sharp nod. “I’ll see you below.”

“Alex!”

I shoved away from him, then spread my arms and legs out to catch as much air as I could. My tail formed without any thought from me, steering me away from my father and toward the thick wall of dirt and stone.

He screamed something at me, but then his shoot opened, and he slowed down massively.

Squeaks alerted me to a problem.

Those damn birds.

My aura flared even as they rushed closer to me. The damn birds fled. Reaching out, I dug into the side of the mountain with my claws, almost tearing them off. But it slowed me down, a little.

I aimed for the massive chain still dangling from the floating mountain. My wings slowed me down even more, but pain lanced down my shoulder blades.

Fuck it.

I latched onto the chain as it came within reach.

Something in my hand snapped and jerked away, but I thudded to a halt, wrapping my legs around the inside of the link.

Once my heart caught up with my brain, I glanced down, then up.

The fucking platform was slowly climbing down the damn chain, far below me.

Taking a deep breath, I stepped down using the chain like a ladder. Yet, my right hand remained mangled.

It needed to heal first.

A buzzing sound caused me to look back, and I found one of the hoverbikes floating nearby.

“John…” I said.

It moved closer.

Carefully, I transferred over to the bike, and whimpered as I clutched my hand to my chest. Or at least, what remained of it.

Food.

I needed food.

Or at least energy.

Using my good hand, I directed the bike back up toward the mountain.

“Alex, what are you doing?” asked John through the bike.

“Need a moment,” I replied, even as my voice cracked.

I flew up closer to the floating mountain, and I crossed some sort of invisible line. The Mana drastically increased.

I took a deep breath, sucking down as much as I could, directing it to my hand. Bones cracked and snapped into place, causing tears to fall. The energy pulsed inside me, and I directed everything to my hand.

I needed it to be fixed.

Over the bones, the muscles healed, then eventually my skin.

My head pounded, along with my heart. I took one more deep breath in and directed the energy to the rest of me, then, using my left hand, I directed the hovercart to return to the shuttle.

I still couldn’t move any of my fingers, but at least it looked like a hand.

Leaning forward, the shield from the bike closed over my head, cutting off the wind.

I had survived, but at what cost?
