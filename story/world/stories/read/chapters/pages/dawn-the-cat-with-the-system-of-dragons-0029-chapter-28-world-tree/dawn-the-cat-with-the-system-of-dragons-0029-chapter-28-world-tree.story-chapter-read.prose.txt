In a distant land, it was pre-dawn hours. Birds started chirping everywhere. Soon, the daylight would brighten the whole region. Everyone started heading for their work. For elves, it was just like every other day.

Elves always follow natural laws, so it was time to start the day even though it was still dark. But there is always a bad apple, and one of them was living among elves.

In a sparse forest, where endless grass covered the ground, an elven man was sleeping just beside a tree trunk, facing upwards. Both his hands were behind his head. He was using them to support his head on the ground.

He started his day by ditching his official work and went far away from his workplace, just to sleep again after waking up.

Suddenly, his peaceful sleep got disrupted by a sudden wind pressure on his face, and then he felt something on his chest. He opened his eyes and saw a beautiful white pigeon carrying a small letter on its leg.

“Come on, at least let me sleep without disturbance. Uuuaaaaaaaaaaaahhhhh.” He yawned loudly and held the delivery pigeon with both hands, and put the pigeon on the ground.

He stretched his body for a few seconds and sat up. He didn’t want to go anywhere, but there was nothing he could do. He took the letter that the pigeon was carrying on its leg and started reading.

The letter had only a few details. “You have fifteen minutes. Lord Faelon wishes to meet you urgently. . . That’s all!!! At least write why the lord has summoned me. Huuuh…”

He stood up and started running as if he was being chased by wild beasts.

—Faelon’s office—

KNOCK KNOCK

“Lord, your vassal is here as you summoned.”

“Sillumael, you’re here. Come in.”

“Yes, my lord.” Sillumael entered the office and saw his lord’s grim expression.

Lord Faelon was sitting in his office. The office room was fairly big. It had one huge window, near which the lord’s table and chair were placed. In the middle of the room, there were two sofas along with a tea table where Lord Faelon was sitting while reading a document.

Every side of the room had a huge bookshelf. The whole room was filled with books and documents. Though it was a typical personal office, it was very elegant, showing how important Lord Faelon was.

When Sillumael entered, the lord simply commanded, “Sit here.”

“Lord!”

“Just sit.”

“Yes.” Sillumael didn’t say anything else and just sat on the opposite side.

“Aelindra, serve tea and then wait outside.” Faelon commanded the maid standing behind him and started waiting for her to serve tea.

“Yes, my lord.” She replied and went out the door.

Sillumael sensed something terrible must be happening. His lord hardly ever told him to sit. And whenever that happened, it was either to reprimand him or discuss urgent issues. The atmosphere was gloomy. He kept waiting.

After a while, Aelindra came back with a tea set and served them tea and cookies. Then she went out without saying anything.

“Sillumael, do you believe in prophecy and future sight?”

This came out of the blue. It was just so random that he never expected this, and simply stared at his lord in bewilderment. (Lord wants me to see the future. How am I supposed to answ… Wait. Is this about her?) “My Lord, are you talking about Lady Erilas’s prophecy?”

“Yes. Now answer my question.”

“Yes, I do believe. At least that’s what I was taught to believe.” (Who cares about this anyway?)

“Your expression is saying otherwise... Anyway, if you have an idea, it’ll be easy to explain the situation then... Last night, Lady Erilas gave another prophecy. It has been around six hundred years since her last prophecy, I believe.”

Sillumael didn’t interrupt and kept listening. What Faelon said piqued his interest. Though Sillumael acted aloof most of the time, when he was young, he used to be one of the greatest believers in Elven prophecy. After joining the army, he lost his interest in it. But now Faelon piqued his curiosity again.

Faelon then picked a paper from the table and started reading.

“Tomorrow, visit where the sun was hiding,There, you shall find what you’re seeking,Observe the sapling that is fuming,Hylas may not listen to your pleading,Prove your worth, if you're daring,Touch his companion and forget what you’re wishing.”

After reading, Faelon put the paper down and started looking at his subordinate. “What do you think about this?”

“Please don’t mind me saying this, sir, but it doesn't make sense. It seems like low class literature for children.” He didn’t know how else to answer his lord.

Faelon put his fingers on his nose bridge. “Huuuuh. You’re not the only one thinking like this... Anyway, I can only understand the meaning of the first two lines. The rest are very ambiguous and hard to decipher.”

“Ummm... I understand, sir. But, what is the issue with this? It doesn’t seem that urgent.”

“Do you have any prior engagement?”

“No, sir.”

“Then it’s fine.”

“. . .”

“It was not supposed to be urgent, but the prophecy mentioned tomorrow, and it was yesterday night when Lady said all these. Moreover, we have received urgent news which seems to be related to yesterday’s prophecy. You have to take action now and try to finish the task in a day.”

“Interesting . . . Sir, what do I have to do?”

“In short, you have to go to another continent today with your battalion, investigate the cause of the cyclone that’s happening there, and conquer a beast-filled forest. I’ll fill in the details after you command your battalion to prepare.”

“We are going to invade another country, investigate the cause of the disaster, and clean up their forest!”

“No, that’s a neutral region.”

“Then it is not that dangerous. Should I start preparing now?”

“Command your troops to be ready within an hour, and you need to be here within ten minutes.”

“Sir, with due respect, how am I supposed to prepare everything in ten minutes?”

“Ask your subordinate. Can’t you handle this much?”

“Of course, sir, I’ll be back in ten minutes.”

Sillumael went out of the room and went straight to his dorm. He started preparing a list of what he might need for this expedition. After finishing, he changed his clothes, picked up his sword and knife, and went out of the room.

Then he went straight to the training grounds to inform his battalion about the sudden expedition.

After twenty minutes, he was finally able to finish everything.

“Sir, I’m here. May I come in?”

“Yes, come in... So, why did you take so much time? I only gave you ten minutes.”

“Sorry, sir, I had to prepare many things, and it took a lot of time.”

“You only had to inform your subordinates. Your subordinate can’t even manage that much? Do you even teach them anything? . . . Huuuh, leave it. Sit here. I have something important to discuss.”

“Thank you, sir.”

“So, what do you think about the prophecy?”

“Sir, the prophecy doesn't make sense no matter how you look at it.”

“But our higher-ups think differently, and moreover, they think they already have a clue what it is about.”

“Sir, if you don’t mind, could you please elaborate?”

“Sure. Why not?” Faelon picked up the paper and started reading and elaborating.

“Tomorrow, visit where the sun was hiding. It should mean the western region because that’s where the sun sets. Right? And we are supposed to visit it in the future. At first, no one got why ‘SUN WAS HIDING’ was specifically mentioned. But now, with the new information we have received, everything makes sense. I’ll get back to it in a bit.…There, you shall find what you’re seeking. It is simple enough. All the elven countries are searching for the World Tree. There is nothing else needed to explain.…Observe the sapling that is fuming. It probably means the World Tree is a small sapling. At first, no one understood the second half of this sentence. But just now we have got information, and its meaning has also become quite apparent.…Hylas may not listen to your pleading. It probably means the Forest of Hylas. It is supposed to be the most dangerous forest among all the Western continents. So it probably means we need to go there, and we are going to face a lot of problems. Coincidentally, the recent news also came from there.…Prove your worth, if you're daring. It is also simple. We have to conquer the whole forest. It is dangerous, and it is supposed to be the neutral zone. So I don’t think any major power will create issues. Prove your worth is definitely pointing in that direction.…Touch its companion and forget what you’re wishing. The World Tree is a plant, so we are not supposed to damage the forest. I guess. But it is the most crucial information. It means we can’t use large-scale or huge attacks, otherwise the forest will be damaged.”

“That makes sense. But what information did we get just now? And how is it connected to the prophecy?”

“Currently, a disastrous cyclone is destroying everything in the Forest of Hylas. Moreover, the cyclone is not natural. It is made with pure elemental energy. As if someone is releasing so much energy that the weather has changed temporarily.”

“So what does it have to do with us?”

“Everyone, including me, is guessing that the prophecy is about today. ‘Tomorrow, visit where the sun was hiding’ means visit the western region. And it also means morning because it was said in the past tense, which means the sun is not hiding anymore. This only happens in the morning. Initially, everyone thought that by ‘tomorrow’ the prophecy was describing the future, but after getting this information, everyone’s view changed.”

“This makes sense.”

“And ‘Observe the sapling that is fuming’ probably refers to the elemental cyclone that is currently happening there. It must have been created by the World Tree's sapling. I think if we don’t take this chance, we’ll never find our guardian. Because if someone picks the sapling and changes the location, it will be disastrous for elves.”

“Hmmmm.”

“First of all, you’ll be leading this expedition and conquering the forest.”

We are supposed to finish it today. Otherwise, we may never find our guardian. Keep this in mind.”
