A mission that was meant to be nothing more than a routine hunt for fugitives in the ruined city had taken a shocking turn. Who could have guessed it would transform into a massive defensive battle against a horde of monsters pouring out of a Collapsed Maze?

Most of the mission’s members were killed. Tragically predictable, since many of them were still stuck at level 25, having not yet completed their class advancement quests.

This was a level 25 Raid Maze. Sure, the monsters weren’t that much higher than them in level, but the sheer number of them was staggering, overwhelming to the point of disaster. The outcome was nothing short of catastrophic.

And yet, in the midst of all this, Ethan managed to achieve his new Advanced Class during the mission. He also broke through to level 30. With that came another Rare-grade undead, raising his total to three Rare-grade thralls under his command. Even more remarkable, Franky had evolved into an Elite-grade undead, his power multiplying several times over while still occupying the same Animus Slot.

Incredible!

A new type of undead had also been discovered: the Hollow Undead. Ethan had encountered it for the very first time when he successfully raised a human from death. What made this type special was that they gained a new undead class of their own, becoming Hollow Sentinel.

It stood to reason that different humans might produce different Hollow classes, depending on their strength and traits in life.

The Undying Party had also gained two new members. One was a proper, official living member, Catalina. The other… well, how should one even put it? An undead member, perhaps?

Undead Leon, though unable to use his hunter device as before, could still activate it by supplying his mana. That meant he remained registered with the Hunter Association. And so, he officially became part of The Undying Party as well.

After returning to the human settlement, Ethan finally set out to find a new place to serve as the basecamp for their newly reformed party. As it happened, since Lina and the others already had a residence, and with Theo having decided to leave, Ethan chose to take over that place instead.

It was a four-story house near the city center, already equipped with bedrooms, a training room, and other facilities. Though for now, Ethan could hardly make use of them. At the moment, it was simply a place to stay.

Still, this arrangement gave him much-needed convenience. Here, he could freely manage anything related to his undead thralls without worrying about prying eyes. He no longer had to constantly conceal his identity. He could just dress like an ordinary person. Shorts and a T-shirt, like a proper living man, even if his skin was nothing more than a façade, crafted from his Bloodbound Resin.

Vexxa now had her own room on the second floor, and Lina had settled back into the one she used before. Ethan, on the other hand, had no need for sleep, so he didn’t bother with choosing a bedroom. Instead, he preferred spending most of his time on the fourth floor, where a wide training room was located.

The space held bows, training swords, and various gym equipment. There was also a lounge area, complete with sofas and entertainment devices, positioned so one could look out into the night.

Two of the walls were made entirely of glass, clear from the inside, giving the room a sense of openness. At night especially, the view made the place feel even more expansive.

**

It was the third day since Ethan had moved into his new basecamp. He was in the training room, dressed simply in shorts and a plain white T-shirt, when his vision drifted toward the night view outside the tall glass walls. At that very moment, a notification appeared on his Hunter Device.

He opened it without much thought and began to read through the details. It contained the full report of the sales from everything they had gathered during the previous mission. Materials stripped from monster carcasses, cores harvested from the defeated beasts. All of it had been tallied, processed, and sold through the proper channels. Along with that came the formal distribution of the mission rewards.

Ethan’s eyes stopped cold as the numbers appeared before him.

“1 Million UGD?!”

For a moment, his mind went blank. That figure was no small amount, it was an outrageous sum of money, far beyond what he had expected.

Technically, this was the official reward for The Undying Party, granted because they had been the only group to endure until the end of the mission. It was meant to be divided among the surviving members of the party, each entitled to their fair share. However, the accompanying note explained the situation clearly: Theo had personally declined to accept his portion. That decision alone had caused Ethan’s total reward to swell to such an incredible value.

And that was not all. The mission itself had also carried its own direct payout, amounting to another 1 Million UGD. Normally, this sum would have been divided among every hunter who had taken part in the raid. But given that every other group had been annihilated, with only Ethan and his party remaining alive, the full amount had been awarded entirely to him.

However, Theo had refused to take his portion of the reward.

Ethan stood still for a moment, his mind turning over the thought. Sooner or later, he would need to meet with Theo again, to uncover more about the man’s aspirations and what truly drove him. Someone like Theo had too much value to be left wandering on his own, especially given the bond he already shared with Lina.

Ethan slowly walked toward the glass wall, stopping beside the square pillar in the center of the room. His reflection stared back faintly in the transparent surface, and once again his size struck him.

Even dressed in the largest clothes available from the shops, they still looked too small on him.

He rubbed the back of his head, a heavy sigh escaping him as the truth settled in. His skeletal form was not only about “growing stronger.” It was making him taller as well.

By his own estimate, his height had already reached 2.2 meters (6'11"). And that was no ordinary figure. How many people in the world could possibly stand at such a height?

But then again, ever since humans began awakening, gaining access to these powers and upgrading their Basic Stats. Their bodies naturally went through changes as well.

For example, most people who leveled up often ended up with sharper looks and healthier skin compared to the average person. Tankers especially tended to gain bulkier, more muscular builds, sometimes even with an increase in height.

So… perhaps this wasn’t all that unusual. The only difference was that Ethan’s growth rate seemed far too fast. What if, in the future, his height continued to increase until he reached 3.5 meters (11'6")?

A height like that? Sure, it would look intimidating, impressive even. Just imagine him towering in golden armor, wielding his battle axe.

No… at that size, his battle axe would look more like a hatchet in his hands. Hopefully, his growth would not continue endlessly, because he had no idea how he would adapt if it came to that.

It would be far better if, as a Skeleton, he gained the ability to reconstruct his own bones. Right? To make adjustments. Whether increasing density and durability while keeping the same size, or altering his frame to suit the situation. That would be a far more useful evolution.

With that thought lingering in the back of his mind, Ethan shifted his focus to what had occupied him over the past three days in this new place: experimenting with his Undead Thralls. More precisely, he had been testing different methods to upgrade them.

He started with the Normal-grade undead, using the five Wretched Undead he had collected during his very first Maze expedition. The results had been an overwhelming success: all five had advanced to Uncommon grade. Not only that, but each of them now possessed more distinct features and traits.

Ethan had even gone so far as to shape them deliberately into something resembling a proper party, each with their own separate role to fulfill.

Now, he prepared to summon them again, one by one, for a fresh round of analysis.

He decided to begin with his personal favorite, the Wretched Veil, who had since evolved into an undead specialized for scouting and assassination.

Ethan turned his body toward the open space, raising his hand as he summoned the creature to appear before him.
