Getting stabbed in the brain was an odd feeling. While still somewhat recovering from the wail of the Stalker, Henry felt it when the buzzing blade kissed the spot between his eyes and reached deep into his brain and past it. There wasn’t much pain, not more than what he had been dealing with from the magic-empowered screech, but he felt the change.

It was, in a way, as if his consciousness decided to shift seats, moving away from the brain that was just injured and just… resettled elsewhere. The shift itself was barely noticeable, like a flicker of his own consciousness, but he could still feel everything in his main body just fine. He assumed he’d somehow taken over his Octominds’ nervous centers. Or something else. In any case, he’d figure that out later.

It had merely been half a second since he’d gotten stabbed, but his arm was already moving. Henry didn’t need that many arms to grab the thing in front of him. He was nowhere near his maximum size at the moment, but he still towered over the creature.

“That was fucking rude,” he hissed at it as his limb snapped around its body. With it being so close, Henry sensed it when its mana spiked, and he immediately let out the Dispelling Pulse he’d been prepping, just in time to see Velistraine, eyes and nose bleeding rush up toward him. The creature screeched in his face, but this wasn’t the magical kind, just good old savagery that smelled sickly sweet and acidic at the same time.

“[Ugh, that sucked]” Maurice sent while the creature shuddered as Henry’s magic ripped its skill to shreds before it could cast it, just as its bladed arm cut deep into his arm. Fortunately, Henry hadn’t given it too much space to move and already, he was pumping it full of toxins, through his suckers. He had to activate Bite to get through the surprisingly hard chitin, but within seconds, Henry was pretty sure there was more of his venom in its bloodstream than its own blood.

Not that the damn thing cared about it.

Mandibles gnashing and antennas flailing, holding on to the creature was like trying to stop a chainsaw with his bare flesh. It had been merely a handful of seconds since he’d caught it, but Henry was already losing all feeling of his arm as it was shredded, and to his alarm, his grip was loosening already from the violent squirming and the deep cuts the Stalker was inflicting on him. Henry knew that it wasn’t just blood loss that was affecting him, as an odd, cold sort of burning sensation was crawling up his arm.

The flailing didn’t stop, even when Velistraine lopped off the Stalker’s head, her eyes blazing with power, and her teeth bared.

Henry thought that look fit her a little better.

For nearly thirty more seconds, the damn thing continued to fight to get free, only for Henry to wrap more arms around it. He kept his senses peeled for any other attempt at escaping, but none came. Maybe it was because it was missing its head, and just like a headless cockroach, it could sustain itself for a while longer, so Henry kept pumping it full of venom while continuously taking chunks of it until finally, Henry sensed the core settle into his soul. The creature was dead. Wordlessly, he chucked it in storage along with its head, then wrapping an arm around Velistraine, he teleported them both to the edge of his network while keeping an eye on the battle site. Already, two more pillbug ships had shown up, one of which quickly landed and let out a swarm of Void Lords and another Stalker to inspect the remains of the one they’d just wrecked. Henry’s two Avatars had been enough to dispatch the rest of the creatures, and he’d already moved them out of the way by the time the newcomers had arrived.

It was a good thing that he did. Because Henry watched as the Stalker stepped calmly between the cut and bloodied bodies, as if following a thread. It looked up in the direction his Avatars had used to leave, then it made its way up to the site where the Stalker had just died. If they’d stayed to watch, this thing would have definitely found them. Likely through their scent, though Henry didn’t like how the creature kept glancing around, as if sensing that it was being watched.

Just in case, Henry pulled his Avatars a little further away.

Henry relayed everything to his companions, and Velistraine wasn’t surprised when he mentioned that by now, six more ships had shown up, one of which was nearly six times the size of the one they’d just cleared and just like it, it was clearly organic.

That reminded him. Through the Avatars that had cleared the rest of the ship, Henry had gotten a look at the interior of the pillbug ship and—well, suffice it to say, he was looking forward to forgetting it.

It was the stuff of nightmares. An unholy fusion of organic matter, with the pulsing walls of the ship only being separated from the people within by panels of chitin and glowing, purple gems that were attached to everything else with similarly glowing veins.

Henry would have examined the space further if he hadn’t run out of time. Between the time it took them to clear out the Stalker and the remaining Void Lords within the ship, he’d had less than a minute to get his Avatars away before the arrival of the first ship.

Now that his main body was far from them, though, he had another idea. Especially considering that the ship that had parked near their hiding spot had joined the rest, and Maurice must have sensed something, because immediately, he tapped him on the arm.

“[What are you up to? It feels fun!]”

Velistraine glanced up at him, looking away from the slowly closing wound down Henry’s arm. By now, the stab he’d gotten between the eyes was already healed and purged, with Lifeweaving having done short work of the familiar wound. It was pretty similar to what Velistraine had been afflicted with. But within his own body, Henry had a lot more control, which made the healing process trivial. As for the toxin that the Stalker had tried to slip into his bloodstream, it wasn’t much. A simple pulse of Life Surge cleared it out easily enough.

Right now though, he was having some fun with the creatures. Henry was using an Avatar between the arrayed ships and the doors, one that was just out of the range of detection, and he’d just shot his first empowered Toxic Ray, using Veilwatch’s Mark to tag as many of the creatures as he could as he flew away. Naturally, the swarm immediately broke out after him, leaving their ships behind as they flitted and buzzed after him, some flying, while some skittered along the road. As for Henry, he kept his eyes on the Stalkers, of which there were at least three now, and with the mana being funneled into him by the dozens of creatures he’d tagged with his toxin, Henry pushed at the Toxic Shroud surrounding him, using the most potent toxin he had at his disposal. The numbing, necrotic and mana-burn inducing agent he’d gotten from the Stinglance Tyrant trailed after him, and from the point of view of his other Avatars that were quickly flitting past him to clear the way, it looked like he was leaving contrails that were only interrupted when he was forced to Blink out of the way of a stab. In the meantime, he kept peppering his screeching pursuers with Toxic Ray, ensuring this clone would have plenty of mana to go around, and with the invaders now busy following his Avatars, Henry popped Velistraine and Maurice inside one of his camouflaged Henry-balloons and just projected what he was seeing through that specific Avatar to them with a simple illusion.

Seeing it from this angle, it certainly looked disturbing.

“[It’s like a swarm of them]” Maurice sent to both of them, and Velistraine’s sword arm twitched at the sight. Effectively, it was like a living wall was coming at Henry, as high and wide as the road allowed, squirming and screeching as it called for more of its kind to join. Even when some of them flew out of bounds, they somehow managed to continue their chase, which Henry was sure was due to some telepathic nonsense. Because even in the chaos, there seemed to be a pattern guiding the hundreds, if not thousands of the creatures. And more were coming. Henry saw them, the ships disgorging their content out into space so they could give chase. He also noted more kinds of these invaders. Some were short and fast, opting to shoot at him with purple blasts of what felt like condensed madness, while others were hulking things that seemed content to trail after the rest. He couldn’t afford to Identify them just yet as he didn’t want to reveal all of the Avatars he had around, and he wanted to make sure he got a view of the gateway, so Henry kept flying as slowly but surely, the weaker of the invaders died one after the other from what he assumed to be a mix of his Toxic Shroud and from getting trampled by the rest of their own kind.

He would see where his range and his Avatars would take him. In the meantime, he’d fill his reserves with cores and ready himself for the upgrade of Unfettered Echoes. Because right now, he knew he had enough to push it to the next rank.
