CHAPTER FOURTY FOUR

“Oh, quit being such a baby,” his body double chided.

Hand still planted on his chest, his superego did… something, and suddenly, the screaming stopped. The pain stopped. Actually, a lot of sensations just below his neck stopped sensation-ing entirely. Well, more like all of them really, if he was being completely honest.

“D-did you just do what I think you just did?”

“Paralyze you from the neck down? Yes. Why?”

“Rude!”

His double raised an eyebrow.

“Would you have rathered I left you to scream in agony?”

Richard grimaced.

“Well… no. But you could’ve asked!”

“I’ll keep that under advisement for next time.”

“Yeah, I’m sure you will,” Richard grumbled. “And there’s not going to be a next time with thatkind of attitude.”

“Promise?” the man sounded genuinely hopeful.

Richard made a sour face, but didn’t answer. Now it was his doubles turn to frown. The moment passed. Seconds went by in silence, while the personification of his subconscious ran his hands all along Richards body. Leaving ripples and knots in his wake, which he then smoothed over in the next pass. Eventually the silent treatment, coupled with the mounting tension, and the occasional tremor that rocked his library down to its very foundations, was too much for Richard to bear.

“Mind clueing me in on what exactly you’re doing?”

“Y-you don’t know!?” his double was so shocked he paused in his work briefly. “Might I remind you that this was your idea? In fact, it could be argued that you’re the one currently making these changes. I’m just a convenient standin. A figment of your imagination to help mask the true extent of your temporary mania.”

“Well, yeah but- I- you-! Can’t a guy just want to make conversation!”

“With yourself? That’s what crazy people do. You do know that’s what crazy people do, don’t you?”

“Just answer the question!”

“Yes, yes. Fine. There’s no need to shout. There are four main areas I’m hoping to address with this procedure,” as he spoke, medical journals floated up to obscure his visage—flipping through it’s contents rapidly before being replaced by another.

“Your neural, muscular, metabolic, and recovery systems are in desperate need of improvement. It is my opinion that these four should aid you the most, and in a number of areas. From faster synaptic efficiency, to a higher count of fast-twitch muscle fibers, to greatly improved nutritional intake, to, well, faster overall recovery.”

“I see. I see,” Richard paused. “And that means…?”

His double sighed.

“That you’ll react faster, hit harder, grow muscle more easily, and recover incredibly quickly.”

“Oh! So basically just an all around improvement. Why didn’t you just say that?”

“You-!” his doubles eye twitched. “You do know that I could do terrible things to you right now? Terrible, permanent things?”

Richard opened his mouth to reply, then shut it just as suddenly.

“Uh, right. Shutting up. You won’t hear a peep from me, no sir. Just go ahead and do your thing. I’ll just be… here. Yeah.”

And, not even deigning to dignify his answer with a response, his super ego did exactly that. The procedure continued, Richard unable to move a muscle and unwilling to interrupt himself in the middle of what was effectively surgery. Well, anymore than he already had. Which ultimately left him with little to do but gaze up at the roiling black cloud of fog cutting him off from the vast majority of his knowledge repository, and watch his soul palace slowly deteriorate.

Tremor after tremor came and abated, dust and stone chips raining down with each quake. Though more concerning were the hairline fractures which raced along the ceiling. A spider web of failing foundations which boded anything but well. And yet, despite the world literally falling down around them, his superego never faltered in his ministrations.

Hands slowly roaming up and down. Up and down.

Occasionally his palms would rest above his head, or cover his eyes. At which point Richard’s vision generally failed him, and, the next thing he knew, he was waking up to find that an unknown amount of time had passed. Considering his trouble double was effectively playing with the meat stuff of his brain, he couldn’t help but feel grateful for that lapse in attentiveness. He wasn’t particularly keen on the idea of receiving brain surgery without the aid of anesthesia.

Eventually though—after what could’ve been hours, but was more likely a few minutes—the violent tremors proved too much for even his double to ignore. The shaking having gotten so bad that parts of the balcony they were on had begun to tilt and shift precariously. Wiping the sheen of sweat from his brow, his superego gave a frustrated huff.

“Well, that’s good enough for now I suppose,” more dust rained down from above, coating his hair and shoulders in a layer of white. “Not my best work by any means, and I wish I had more time, but it should serve. More or less.”

“Oh! Good. That’s a relie- wait! More or less?!”

“Don’t worry. It should be fine. Probably.”

“Probably-?!”

“And really, do try to be considerate next time and sleep on it first, before you go and try anything foolish. I don’t think my heart can take another scare like this one.”

“Wait just one second-!”

Not giving him the chance to get a word in edgewise, however, the smiling fiend reached forward and tapped Richard on the brow. When he awoke from his soul palace, he was wracked with incredible pain. Having known what to expect, however, de-clawifying his right arm proved far easier than it’d been the first time.

With a sharp tug, Richard yanked the internal ability cube from his chest, and promptly slumped over, limp as a wet noodle.

Uurgh… that- that’s never not going to suck is it…?

Richard had to blink back tears staring at those last two announcements. It was like it knew…had watched his struggles, spectated his pain. All those trials and tribulations he’d faced on his way here, and how he’d soldiered on through regardless. Never backing down. Never surrendering. Watched as his plans and gambles came to fruition. Richard abruptly feeling truly seen and genuinely grateful for the first time in-

Oh who am I kidding! This isn’t enough! It was never about enough! More! I need more! All the goodies will be mine!

And so saying, Richard promptly plopped all seventy five free points into mana capacity, bringing it up to a cool one hundred and one.

Pain exploded just behind his sternum. The blooming pressure nearly taking his breath away, as his mana core was made to expand several times its original size—several years worth of natural growth—in mere fractions of a second. Thankfully, the intense, burning pain only lasted for so long, leaving a stabby sort of throbbing in its wake. His mana core feeling hot and tender, like he’d swallowed a stone and now it was just sitting there—the area around it extremely enflamed.

In hindsight, it might have been better to drip feed his free points over several days to avoid this very thing. But then, if he did that he risked not receiving as good a title as he’d earned. And the better titles he got, the better off humanity would be.

If you think about it like that, it would’ve been selfish for me not to go for broke here, right? Richard sighed. It isn’t easy, being this selfless, but it’s the cross I’ve decided to bear…

Three… HUNDRED free points?! Opon…? Was this your doing? Did you hamstring the competition so that I might feast upon this glorious bounty…?

And that said nothing of the titles themselves, the leaderboard, the metric crap ton of blank lottery tickets he received. What basically amounted to free loot, if you didn’t mind gambling.

Now, with everything he’d gained he was more than ready to evolve to F Grade. And from there? Plans, oh he had so so many plans. This world wouldn’t even know what hit it! And of course his very first step towards world dominat- ahem, towards securing world peace—yes that’s much better—was obviously… obvious… ly…

Huh?

Richard rose unsteadily to his feet.

She’d shown herself to be perfectly capable of handling herself. Pragmatism dictated he just leave her to it. She said she’d protect them, and he had absolute confidence in her abilities.

Richard began to stagger forward, awkwardly at first, but growing more confident with every stride.

And the kids? Well, they had their titles, did they not?

His slow trundle became a slow trot.

If worse comes to worse, they could just huddle up in their little bubbles and wait out the danger. It would be fine. It was doubtful anyone would even get hurt.

His trot became a hitching jog, the medicines slow in patching up the many injuries he’d so selfishly inflicted upon himself.

There was no use in worrying. In getting hung up on every little thing. Wasn’t it in the best interest of humanity he get as strong as was humanly possible, in as short a time frame as possible? To better prepare for the enemies they would face? What were the lives of a few insignificant brats compared to the whole of humanity?

He was running now, feet kicking up chunks of dry earth as he rapidly outpaced the clearing.

He had points to spend, and lottery tickets to cash, and he’d never even gotten around to finishing his soul seal. What about his F Grade evolution? What about his class, for Opon’s sakes?! Wasn’t this everything he’d been working towards? And he’d still yet to find that infernal named elite. Wasn’t his personal progression far more important than some blabber mouthed-

-little kids. …his kids…!

Richard roared. Letting out all the pent up rage and frustration he’d kept bottled up for nearly a decade in one long go. Birds startled from their nests. Critters scurried into their burrows. Predators grew very very still, lest they become prey. Richard leapt high into the air, rocketing upwards to blast through the leafy canopy above.

The earth shuddered and quaked in his wake.

Wait for me! I’m coming! Please, just hold out for a little bit longer!
