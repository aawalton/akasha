The attack thundered towards the spot where Fauna, Tara, and Klax stood, and within an instant coated their forms and obliterated what was left of the Umbral Order’s monastery. Mistborne Isle was cooked – its barren trees, fauna, and history vaporized in a microsecond by the deluge of pure, raw power that rained from Sys’s position.

And laughter spilled from the throat of the newly dominated dark angel as more and more lines of Spirit Cores entered his already bulging body.

“You see, Ethan?” he cackled, still letting the energy from his attack fly from between his gnarled fingers. “There is no escape. Not even Kaedmon could hold out against me forever. No one can.”

But the little Demon Hat Sys held in his free hand let out a hoarse, witty chuckle, much to the chagrin of the ultimate being.

When Sys finally let the attack diminish, his eyes beheld the source of all Ethan’s merriment.

“…no.”

Fauna, Tara, and Klax were still standing there – their feet planted firmly above the boiling sea-bed.

Time Stop Duration: 40 seconds!

Sys barely even registered the notification. His eyes were glued to the three creatures beneath him, his shoulders vibrating, his eyes twitching, and his hand closing around what remained of Ethan tighter and tighter.

“No,” he said again – the word becoming more like a mantra against the impossible with each passing second.

Then before he knew it, he had flown into a berserk frenzy.

“NO, NO, NO, NO, NO!”

The word flew from his lips like a suicidal battlecry. Completely abandoning his Spirit Siphoning, he activated [Dive] and crashed right into the three of them.

The ocean swirled. Waves flew to the heavens as his form hit the ground, and without even looking at the damage he’d caused he grafted [Petrification Coating] and [Poison Coating] to both his blades and slashed with wild abandon, activating [Skitter] so he could increase the speed of his attacks to levels hitherto unseen in Argwyll’s history. His dance of blades sliced through the three Hybrids with ease – tearing apart their limbs and then dicing them, cutting them up into bloody little chunks of meat which stayed suspended in midair as his [Time Stop] ticked on. With every second, more parts of Ethan’s allies were cut, but at this point Sys had abandoned Ethan entirely. The Demon Hat had been thrown to the side to watch dispassionately as Sys chopped away, screaming all the while.

After he was done, and his [Time Stop] lingered at around 15 seconds, he suddenly heard Ethan’s voice chuckle from within his mind. The shared connection between them was the one thing he couldn’t truly sever.

And Ethan had known it.

A tad much, don’t you think?

Sys whirred, looking around him and seeing nothing but the raging cyclone he’d created where Mistborne once was. Pieces of rubble, dead sea creatures, and eviscerated scraps of fauna were all that decorated this arena.

The Demon Hat was nowhere to be seen.

You’ve lost it, haven’t you? Sys sneered back. You’ve watched your friends get hurt so many times and now, at the end, your mind finally can’t take it. Maybe now you can understand a portion of my pain, Ethan.

Yawn. Sys, don’t play the victim card. It’s boring.

Sys’s four green eyes flared lambent white as he activated [Eyes of Raziel], scanning the small arena for his little blue friend.

You know this is over, Ethan, he whispered, throwing his arms wide and kicking back some dust at the mutilated bodies of the Hybrids. How you kept those pitiful friends of yours alive, I don’t know. But don’t prolong this anymore. For both of our sakes, accept that this is the fate you were always destined to have.

From somewhere, Ethan gave another jovial chuckle.

And here I was thinking you didn’t believe in fate, Sys.

Sys’s eyes went wide.

From outside the cyclone, three shadows suddenly formed.

Then again, you can’t see past something when your eyes are shut off to the reality in front of you. And you’ve been walking blind for a while, haven’t you?

Sys heard Ethan’s voice, but it was the sight of fluffy ears, tails, and righteous smirks appearing from outside the walls of the cyclone that overcame his mind. His arms actually started to tremble. His muscles began to loosen. And for the first time he could remember, he felt the heartbeat within his Host’s chest.

The three Hybrids emerged from the maelstrom of Sys’s making, shielded by Ethan’s very own [Repulsor Shield].

Tara gave the bemused angel a little wink. Klax bared his fangs. And Fauna, in the face of the angel’s mounting confusion, couldn’t help but add a little theatrical flair: she dusted off her shoulders, squared her arms, and gave Sys her most shit-eating smirk.

In her hands, she held Ethan.

And before Sys’ could take a single step toward them, a notification buzzed in his brain:

Time Stop: Ended!

Recharge Time: 1 hour.

Sys’s mind went blank for the first time in his living memory.

“…how?”

And when Ethan spoke again, this time, he heard:

“Sys, all you had to do was use [Appraisal] literally once.”

From behind, the meaty chunks of torn Hybrid bodies vanished suddenly as Ethan’s [Summon Illusion] Skill ended.

And Sys’s four eyes met the single crimson eyeball of the Demon Hat with more hatred than any mortal was capable of harboring.

You always hated that Skill, didn’t you? Ethan said. ‘Pretty much useless’, I think you said. And that just confirms it: You think you understand the stories I’ve read all my life. But even a newbie would know that a basic identification skill can save so much time and effort in the long run.

The infuriating little chuckle that then emanated from within Sys’s mind – Ethan’s chuckle – caused two of the parasite angel’s four eyes to twitch in ultimate vexation.

I honestly can’t believe you didn’t think of that, Ethan said. How can you have so many options available to you and not choose the most optimal one?

His Hybrid companions shared a collective giggle. And that was what finally drove Sys over the edge.

“BE SILENT!”

He activated a combined [Skitter] and [Wing Buffet] that carried him across the sandy arena in a second, one flaming blade aimed directly at Ethan’s eyeball.

He cursed himself for not killing the cretin earlier. He cursed himself for allowing himself a moment of hubris. But now he’d end it, finally. Now everything would be worth it. Now he’d finally never have to hear that nasally, pathetic, irritating little voice aga-

Oh, you mean this one?

Sys’s body ceased all motion as the tip of his blade came a few centimeters from Ethan’s iris.

His wings would not respond.

His Skills – dead.

The only thing that screamed at him right now, commanding movement, was his own consciousness.

And Ethan was right in there with him.

Sys’s burning hatred gave way to sudden panic. Confusion was becoming second-nature. It was becoming the newest mortal emotion he was beginning to truly understand.

Because what he’d just seen made no sense at all.

He’d just been [Time Stopped]. That much was clear.

But not by Ethan. In the second he’d pounced, the Demon Hat hadn’t moved a single thread on its hem. Its eye had glowed with no tell-tale flare of Skill-Activation.

No. Instead, it was the silvery eyes of Fauna the Hopla that had suddenly burst with vibrant, flaming white, and stopped the limbs of the dark angel in an instant.

And before Sys could even ask the question, Ethan was already busy explaining his downfall to him.

You didn’t even check your Skill List, did you, Sys?

The angel’s eyes glared into the crimson of the Demon Hat’s.

And for the first time since he’d inherited this new body, Sys did it – he manually went through the Skills he’d gained, frantically scanning for some new permutation. Something he hadn’t seen…

And that’s when he saw it:

[Hidden Skill]

Skill Graft (Grade S)

A creation of the Architect, bestowed to Archon Ethan as his part of their bargain.

Effect: All Skills Duplicated and transferred to [One] other User on touch.

Skill…transfer…

The black lines of text were like the etchings on a gravestone.

But – where -?

Sys didn’t need him to answer, this time. His memory flashed back to Ethan’s meeting with this creature – this Architect – way back in the Southern Mountains…when he’d been put out of commission by Garm’s black arrow…

You…you asked him to give you a Hidden Skill…

Something you thought only you had access to, right? Ethan said, his thready brim curling up in Fauna’s hands. But there’s creatures who have been on this planet long before you or even Keadmon. And they had their own powers, Sys. If you’d ever been bothered to actually learn about them, maybe you would have reconsidered the limits of what you believed to be possible.

Sys’s mind railed against the notion. It still made no sense. Why would such a creature lend aid to the Archon? Especially one as pathetic as Ethan. Why would Ethan have kept the Skill from him as he did?

Unless…

Unless I doubted you, Ethan finished. Which, incidentally, is something I got from you. Remember, Sys? You’re a part of me, too. The part of me that always doubts.

Sys’s mind raged. The [Time Stop] countdown was ticking. He had one chance, and he knew it. If he could keep Ethan talking…

And the rabbit? he asked, keeping his voice as calm as he could.

Fauna smiled down at him.

“Tell him, Ethan.”

Power is a funny thing, Sys. A creature like you probably thinks only the Skills you bestow give us strength. But even the simplest actions can have power. Even something as simple as a kiss.

Sys’s eyes met Fauna’s. Her victorious stare was something he’d enjoy wiping off her face the second he came loose…

Because he remembered. Just before Ethan entered the Cradle, Fauna’s kiss had meant more than just a last display of affection. It had been the exact thing Ethan had wished for when he’d asked Fauna to trust him – to take his hand. But applying this [Skill Graft] to her through a kiss…something so completely innocuous, something so utterly meaningless to a being like Sys…even the suspended dark angel had to admit he’d never seen it coming.

But that was because it still didn’t make any sense…

You’re really no good at this when you’re on your own, huh?

Sys’s eyes flew to Ethan’s eyeball again, one half of his mind fixated on the timer ticking down.

Sys, that’s your weakness, Ethan said. I realized it a long, long time ago: you have no conscience. Did it ever occur to you, at any point during all your lifetimes, that any sentient mortal being might voluntary bestow all its powers onto another? That one soul might give another the exact power needed to slay it?

The timer kept on ticking. Sys feigned panic. He let the performance go on.

It didn’t, did it? I knew it wouldn’t. Your lack of conscience limits your imagination. You can’t conceive of anybody being any different than what you are. And you’re a greedy, hungry little parasite. Desperate to have it all. All the power in the world that you think will sustain you. But power comes from the self, Sys. It comes from the self, or it doesn’t come at all.

With the seas of Argwyll suspended around them, the entire world looking on, Ethan then delivered his final verdict:

It’s your greed that fuels you, Sys, he said. And it’s your greed that’s gonna bury you.
