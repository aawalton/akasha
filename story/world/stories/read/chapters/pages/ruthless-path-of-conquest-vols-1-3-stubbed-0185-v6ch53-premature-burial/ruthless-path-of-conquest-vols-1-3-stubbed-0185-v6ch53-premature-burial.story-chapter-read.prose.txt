James’s aura flooded throughout the ring and pulsed outward, filling the space around the fighting space until it hit the stands—where a magical force field that the System must have generated itself repelled his energy.

Other fighting rings also stopped his power from crossing the borders into their space and interfering with their combatants, but that just meant that where Dominion did hold sway, in the ring and in the out-of-bounds places, his power was all the denser and stronger. It was such a small area relative to the space Dominion usually expanded to fill that it was as if James had been using the Skill over and over again for days.

He could sense every speck of movement from his monsters or Han Jianguo in the affected space. The creatures covering Jianguo’s body were all unnaturally still as James surveyed his surroundings. Jianguo seemed to have used some sort of Skill that paralyzed them.

More than that, in the moment where James had taken his focus off of the opponent to use Dominion, Jianguo had gotten Doppelganger into a headlock. It looked like the monster was about to get its neck broken. James acted instinctively to prevent it.

Hypnotic Shriek!

A burst of piercing sound erupted from James’s mouth and struck Jianguo a fraction of a second later, just as his arm was twisting Doppelganger’s neck. The Emperor saw one of Jianguo’s ears begin to bleed, and his body stilled for a moment before he shook off the hypnotic effect, blinked, and slowly continued struggling.

In that moment of stiffness, though, the paralysis Skill’s effect ceased, and Doppelganger managed to slip out of Jianguo’s grip.

The monsters dogpiled Jianguo again as they had been, Doppelganger aiming a vicious kick at the man’s head.

As Jianguo struggled to get a hold over the closest monster and also push himself up off his back, James used Proficient Silk Fabrication and conjured silk all around his body in a cocoon shape.

The Emperor felt the power of Skill Nullification wash over him as Jianguo activated the power again, and he couldn’t help but smile.

Deactivating that was such a terrible mistake. So much worse for you than for me. He imagined that I wouldn’t be ready to take advantage of the opening.

Now James’s aura permeated the area. It would be so much easier for him to use any Mana-based attack than it would have before, even without having access to the requisite Skills. He reached out with his power and manipulated the air that surrounded Jianguo, tossing the Taiwanese out of the ring and onto the ground.

‘You lose if you fall on the ground outside of the tiled fighting area and are unable to get back up for one Earth minute,’ James recalled the System saying. Let’s see if I can fulfill the easy version of this win condition.

Despite being encased in a cocoon of silk, Jianguo was far from having given up. James could sense it as his opponent strained against the silk, tensing and flexing muscles that seemed to be even stronger than James’s own. He felt the micro-tears that multiplied throughout the silken structure at every moment.

The monsters dogpiling and beating on Jianguo hardly slowed him down at all.

James’s energy had suffused the area around the ring, so he began manipulating the ground—aside from the tile of the fighting rings themselves, it was all just plain dirt—and parted the soil around Jianguo.

Under the Emperor’s guidance, his opponent was slowly sucked underground.

Jianguo cursed loudly and creatively in words that made James smile as he heard them.

“I think my mother would take that last one personally!” the Emperor taunted, trying not to laugh. “I don’t know what she did to you. Your fight is with me!”

The stream of more exotic curses—including some James would never have come up with—continued as Jianguo’s voice faded. As the ground closed over his face. The Emperor didn’t stop there. He could sense that his opponent was still moving and vital underground, and only the silk cocoon that he had not fully broken free from had kept him from breaking free from the soil that encased him already. It was made of the strongest silk James could generate. Despite being underground, breathing didn’t seem to be an issue for Jianguo. Because of course it wasn’t. At their shared level of power, James wouldn’t be that surprised if the other man just deactivated Skill Nullification and then teleported back to the surface.

Damn, you’re a tough nut to crack, James thought. I definitely don’t want to continue the face to face fight if I can win the easy way, but can I keep him down?

James erected barrier after barrier over Jianguo, from solidifying the ground into a slab of sedimentary rock in one place to creating a web of dense silk in another. He never let up on the soil, either, continuing to pull Jianguo deeper and deeper under the ground as the man tried to break free from James’s silk cocoon.

But he still felt a little nervous. His ribs remembered the impression of Jianguo’s fists they had received earlier. James hadn’t buried his monsters with Jianguo, but now he ordered the Blood Slime to descend down into the ground to keep the opponent company.

Sure enough, James sensed it when Jianguo burst free from the silk cocoon, his physical might finally tearing the material to pieces. He started swimming up through the soil, his fingers parting it like water.

Jianguo reached the first of the layers that James had raised to keep him underground. He broke through the sedimentary rock as if it was ordinary dirt and kept rising without even slowing down.

I was just wasting my energy making that, James thought.

It couldn’t have been more than a few seconds later when Jianguo reached the web of silk that James had created as another barrier. The man clearly recognized as soon as his fingertips made contact with the silk that this material would be more difficult to penetrate than the slab of rock had been. To his credit, instead of just trying to chop through it with his bare hands, he drew a short blade from his side and started hacking at it with the weapon.

He also used his spare hand to feel around, looking for the edge of the silk layer, so he could perhaps go around rather than needing to destroy it.

But James had made the silk blanket ten feet long by ten feet wide, so Jianguo was never going to find the way around it unless he started devoting more energy to that.

Instead, he opted to cut through the steel-like material with the blade he had.

How much longer do I have to keep him from getting to the surface? This does count as him falling out of the ring and being unable to get up, right? It feels like it’s been longer than ‘one Earth minute.’

James’s Blood Slime drew closer to Jianguo even as he drew closer to the surface. James ordered the monster to target any of Jianguo’s remaining open wounds for attack and to increase the amount of salt in its body by pulling it from the surrounding soil. Even if the slime didn’t manage to do lasting damage to Jianguo, at least it would be a very unpleasant distraction as he tried to get back topside.

I have a bad feeling that if Jianguo and I clash again, that meathead is going to make me actually kill him. And that would fuck up the relationship that Zhang Chāo and I have been building. Not to mention give him a reason to try and kill me right back when we fight in the next round. Truly, the cycle of violence is insidious, when it poses any risk either to me personally or to my family.

There was another barrier James had quickly managed to make between Jianguo and the surface, though it was a last minute addition and he had doubted his own judgment at the time that he bothered wasting Mana to generate it.

But he felt better about the decision as Jianguo struck it.

Fingers burst through soil and flailed for a moment, as the mind behind them was surprised to feel them moving relatively freely.

The fingers weren’t moving in the open air, though. They wiggled in the midst of a layer of swamp water. Jianguo lost a few seconds just feeling around, trying to understand where he had emerged.

Then he pulled himself up into the swamp layer and began actually swimming.

James’s power reached into the soil, and he willed the water to grow colder and colder, heavier and denser around Jianguo’s body.

James felt the jolt of surprise in his opponent as the swamp began to freeze all around him. Jianguo kept swimming, but the movements were slower by the nature of the space around him changing. It shifted from being the speed of a superhuman swimming through dense water, to that of a man swimming through pudding, and then Jianguo’s body began to have trouble performing strokes at all as the water around his joints froze and had to be broken repeatedly, again and again, just so that he could make some feeble upward motion.

For a split-second, James felt Skill Nullification deactivate again, and then there was a deafening sound of an explosion underground.

Then Jianguo had reactivated Skill Nullification, before James even had time to do anything.

He’s getting better at timing that. I’ll have to be quicker, like I was last time, if I want to take advantage of it the next time he turns it off. If there is a next time.

Though Jianguo seemed to have vaporized the liquid and ice directly near him, he was still buried at least twenty feet underground. It wasn’t the sixty feet or so James had initially pulled him under, but it should be enough. The time had to be ticking down, rapidly nearing the moment when Jianguo would be disqualified by ring-out.

With the rapid deactivation and reactivation of Skill Nullification at the time that Jianguo had employed his explosive Skill to get himself out of the underground freezing swamp layer, James could sense that Jianguo hadn’t been as careful of his own body as he might normally have been using such a powerful Skill. He was bleeding into the soil from a few places, having injured himself. The ground lapped up the red, hot liquid, drinking the Mana that Jianguo leaked along with his bodily fluids and converting the energy into James’s own power.

And the Blood Slime drew closer to its target.

James sensed it as the monster and Jianguo entered the same elevation.

The slime struck hard and fast, jellyfish-like tentacles probing into long cuts and scrapes across his neck, sides, and shins. Jianguo’s armor was already in tatters, and James could feel it rather than hearing it as his opponent screamed and writhed at the monster invading his wounds with its salty tendrils.

He’s probably cursing me out again, James thought, resisting the impulse to smile. Even if it’s only in his head.

The Fisher Emperor blinked and found himself in the waiting area again. He sensed that the monsters that had split from his body during the duel with Jianguo had all been forcefully merged back into him. That was just fine with James, since he had apparently won.

He turned his head from side to side and found that there were a bunch of people all around him. They were looking at a screen, and James recognized that it displayed the space where he and Jianguo had just been fighting. Then the screen went blank.

“Took you long enough,” said Zhang Chāo’s voice from just behind him. “All the rest of us were watching you.”

James turned slowly and met the Heavenly King’s gaze with a smile. At the same time, the Emperor opened up all his senses and recognized that the entire waiting room was standing next to him. That is, the seemingly large group of people who surrounded him was everyone that was left. Thirty-two people, including James and Chāo. James and Jianguo had been the last to finish their fight, so literally the entire rest of the group had been watching.

“Did you defeat Han Jianguo more quickly when the two of you fought?” James asked.

“It was quick but not easy,” Chāo replied. “I guess it’s my turn to try you next.”

I hope your fight at least drained some of your resources, James thought. Mine was a pain in the ass.

But Zhang Chāo looked like he was in perfect condition.
