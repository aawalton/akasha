Don let out a quiet whistle. “Oh, hell yeah.”

Suddenly Don’s vision had improved dramatically. He could see at least thirty feet forward, before it started dimming and fading back to black at further distance. Don estimated he could clearly see movement up to fifty feet ahead. That was massive. That could be the difference between life and death.

Don immediately checked his Evolution interface. Indeed, he could upgrade his Night vision to E-rank with 60 Evolution points.

Don was looking forward to that, but first he would need to find monsters. And how much easier it would be now that he could see! This changed everything. Don no longer needed to creep forward at a snail’s pace, so he wouldn’t run into something dangerous. He could move freely.

To make up for the lost time, Don rolled into a comfortable jog. He watched his Energy, and it seemed to be draining fairly slowly. He tried changing the paces, and alternating speeds definitely increased his Energy expenditure. He even sprinted a little to see if his skill leveled. It didn’t, and Don decided he would conserve energy and rolled back into the comfortable jog. But now he knew a little bit better how the System worked.

With his improved vision, it was much easier to move fast and soon enough Don found another Vulpin. With proper vision, his improved stats and combat experience, the fight was swift and brutal. He didn’t manage to sneak up on the monster, but Don employed a very clear strategy. Let the beast sink its teeth into his leg or arm, and squeeze it to death. The pain made him feel sick, but this was the safest way to deal with the monsters. Just straight up chaotically duking it out with them might lead to a chomp at his neck or groin. Don managed to pin the Vulpin against a rock and crushed the beast against it.

[You have slain [Vulpin – lvl 3] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 3 System Points earned]

[’DING!’ Race: [Evolutor (spawn)] has reached level 3 – Stat points allocated, +2 Free Attribute Point]

Don didn’t waste time and used Devour immediately on the Vulpin. The taste was sweet as always, and over too soon. It was enough to heal his wounds from the fight.

[10 Evolution Points acquired!]

Don was inclined to spend his points on Power and Agility again. It just seemed like a good bet. Constitution was definitely a good stat, as it seemed to govern his endurance in both physical activity and damage. But because Devour allowed him to regenerate his Health and Energy, he figured it was not a good investment until stronger enemies showed up. Mental was interesting. Having received a single point in it did make a difference. Don was just that much sharper. More alert, for sure. His senses seemed to be more acute as well. And he wasn’t sure, but he even seemed to think faster, although that could be chalked up to the environment forcing him to stay so locked in.

“I think for now Power and Agility are my best choices. They’ll ensure I can stay on the offensive and execute. Fast kills means more kills. If I bank on bringing my offense up to snuff first, I can worry about the other attributes later. Of course this is absolutely crazy, but because of my ability to heal damage, I think it’s a manageable risk.”

And with that, Don put the points in Agility and Power. He took a look at his status screen and was pleased with the results:

Don felt the surge of power from his new attributes. The difference was sizable. He jumped in the air from a standstill and he reached at least six feet in height. He landed smoothly like a cat, one hand bracing him against the ground. He did a cartwheel into a backflip. Just because he could.

Don wanted to hoot at the joy of such ease of movement, but he had to focus. It wasn’t safe here, and he needed to continue moving forward.

The Night Vision was an absolute lifesaver. Don walked around in the forest and saw a strange looking shape ahead. Stealthily, he moved closer and saw it was some kind of beast. It was huge, and Don was pretty sure his three Power and Agility wouldn’t be enough. He used Identify on the beast.

[Blackmaw Grizzly, level 8]

“Yeah… That’s a nope.”

Don made sure to keep a low profile and move as quietly as he could away from the bear’s sight, ears and nose. He was relieved to leave the hulking figure behind.

[General Skill increased: Stealth!]

[Stealth Level 3]

Don still wasn’t sure what was the difference between level 2 and 3 stealth. Higher was better of course, but how. Did the act of stealthing make him less visible the higher his level was? His mind kept returning to that twig he had snapped when approaching the Vulpin. It hadn’t noticed. Animals should have acute enough senses to pick up on that. Did his stealth have something to do with that?

Don’s musings were interrupted by a blaze of white in his gray vision. Something was creating light. He approached it and soon his suspicions were confirmed. Ahead was a man carrying a torch. There was a restless energy in the way the man moved. Don couldn’t blame him. The situation was kind of fucked up. Still, he resolved to approach carefully. Humans generally did better together in dire times.

“Hey,” Don said as he came up to the man from behind a tree.

“Who goes there!” the man said and pointed the torch at him like a weapon. “Are you a man or a beast?”

“Just a man,” Don said. “You hurt?”

“I am as well as anyone can be!” the man snapped.

Don came closer and saw the outline of a robe. The man was clutching a large metal cross with his free hand. This was a priest.

“Stay where you are!” the man demanded. “There are demons about these woods!”

“I think they’re just beasts…”

“The Devil wears many names and skins, son. I think the day has finally come…”

“The day?” Don asked carefully. “What day?”

“Judgement day! Finally our sins have been weighed and judged too heavy. This is our punishment. The word of God has come.” The man babbled.

“God?” Don said. “I don’t think the System is God.”

"It came," the priest whispered. His voice wasn’t afraid. It was flat and hollow. "The Judgment. The great cleansing. It came."

Don didn’t move. That tone put him on edge.

"Uh… yeah. Something happened, alright," Don said carefully. "I don’t know about Judgment, but—"

"They walk among us," the man interrupted, his voice distant. “Their eyes glowing…”

Don frowned. "Who does?"

"The demons." the priest said in a raspy whisper and scrambled closer, still clutching his cross.

The hair on Don’s arms rose. He took a step back.

"Right," he muttered. "Okay, buddy. They’re just beasts that have been transformed. Look have you seen anyb—”

The priest twitched suddenly and Don felt a flash of pain. He looked down and saw a shaking hand pushing a tutorial knife to the hilt up his abdomen. The priest twisted it and sliced sideways as he pulled it out. Don thought idly that this wasn’t the first time the priest had used a knife.

A sharp, burning stab tore into Don’s gut. He gasped, stumbling back as burning hot pain exploded in his side. Warm blood spilled down his stomach.

[Health at 26%]

[Energy at 50%]

[Debuff inflicted: Severe bleeding, Intestinal damage]

[Estimated time until incapacitation from hypovolemic and neurogenic shock: under 2 minutes]

“You cannot fool me, demon!” the priest hissed. “Your eyes glow yellow in the dark. You are a creature of darkness!”

The priest stabbed him again. This time Don knew what to do.

Don grabbed the man’s knife hand and squeezed the wrist sideways. It cracked and the priest howled and fell on his knees. Don pulled the chain on the priest’s neck and pressed him to the ground. He didn’t have the time or strength to strangle the man. He had to act now, before the pain and blood loss overwhelmed him. It was wrong. On some level it made him exactly what the priest was accusing him to be. A demon. But the priest had left him no choice. Don used Devour.

The priest screamed, but it didn’t last long. Don bit down on his neck and soon the scream waned into a whimper that died out. Don bit down and felt the meat and cartilage crunch under his teeth. The sweet invigorating essence flooded into him, and Don felt the wound in his stomach start to knit. Once the priest was drained, his skin had turned to paper and he seemed to have shrunk down, like Don had bled him dry. But it hadn’t been his blood that had been drained. It was still flowing from the bite wound Don made. But Don had taken something, maybe life force itself.

Don was confused. So many levels. And a full 18 Evolution points? He could already improve his Night Vision if he chose to. And the amount of System Points was high too.

“The goddamn system is encouraging me to kill other humans.”

Don examined what he thought of that. He looked at the dried up crazy priest on the ground. He had just killed a human. Not only that. He had devoured one.

Don looked at his shaking hands, covered in blood. What was he becoming? Could he live with this version of Don Damon?

Don sighed. He would have to.

It wasn’t killing the priest that made him feel ill. It was the devouring. It had been so sweet, almost intoxicating. And the rewards system gave for it were tremendous when compared to killing and feasting on beasts. Don did not like that the system was clearly encouraging him to hunt men.

As for the priest itself, lying on the ground? Don felt no remorse. This had been self defense. But would he kill again?

“I will probably have to. But I won’t do it wantonly. I still believe humans should stick together and figure things out. It’s what our whole race is based on. Not that I’m a human anymore apparently… But I still won’t go around murdering everyone. They’re in this same situation as me. The best thing to do is help each other and figure things out.”

But one thing was for sure… Don would not let himself be fooled again. Just because he thought humans should work together, didn’t mean everyone shared the sentiment. Humans could be just as dangerous as beasts. Don resolved to make a rule for himself. He would not actively hunt innocent humans, but he would not hesitate to defend himself or others.

Then Don looked down and saw that he was still bleeding from his stomach. It had clearly healed from the deadly mess it had been after the priest’s knife, but it was still a nasty cut.[Health at 75%]

[Energy at 89%]

[Debuffs: Mild Bleeding]

Don took out one of the Healing Potions from the priest’s pouch and chugged it down. It tasted sweet, like apple juice. Don felt the wound knit close and when he checked the system, his health was full again. So the healing potions were capable of at least healing that much, maybe a bit more. That was important to know.

This experience also showed that despite Devour being an amazing ability, it did not make him invulnerable. Taking premeditated and careful risks was a good way to get ahead. But not knowing his limits would get Don killed.

Don nodded at the priest’s corpse. He had been a good teacher. He would have to be more careful from now on, in many ways.

Before he left, he dealt with the priest’s loot. First he emptied the waterskin. It was half-empty, but Don managed to squeeze four good swallows. The water was stale and tasted leathery, but it was invigorating. Clearly Devour did not shield him from dehydration.

He strapped on the priest’s belt over his shoulder. That gave him three pouches. Filled with various items. Don was mostly interested in the potions. Now that it was clear severe damage couldn’t just easily be healed with Devour, every potion was valuable. He could still carry one more pouch without it becoming awkward. He just hoped he would find one off a corpse, rather than have to fight another human. The forest was dangerous enough without assholes killing each other.

Don crested a hill that rose above the treelines. He stayed low, so he wouldn’t mark a target on his back so easily.

It was nice to stand on top of a rocky hill. The wind was cold, but it only invigorated Don. He breathed in the serenity. Then he looked around. The moon was massive. Completely uncanny in size. There was something unsettling about it, beautiful as it was. It was a clear indicator that he was no longer on Earth or that Earth had changed.

Around him the forest spread into every direction almost endlessly. He had no idea where he was, but there were no forests this massive near his home town, that was for sure.

Don closed his eyes and just breathed for a while. It was relaxing and his mind needed to unwind. He thought of Baxter. He hoped the good boy was okay. Then he thought of his family and colleagues and wondered if they were going through the same experience. Were they in this forest? There were clearly thousands of tutorial areas and Don had no way of knowing by what basis they had been sorted. Age? Location? Some hidden aptitude? Impossible to say. The priest had been a man in his mid forties, and of decent physical condition. But a single data point wasn’t much to go on.

Don checked the tutorial screen, mostly focusing on the amount of survivors left.

2899/3000

Over a hundred people had died already. The forest was dangerous and it culled the weak. Don decided it was time to get back down in the forest. He would not let himself become a statistic. He would have to step up his game.

Before he decided on which direction to go, he spent his Free Attribute Points.This time he needed to rethink his approach, because clearly he wasn’t invulnerable. At least one point he put in Constitution. It would give him a better chance at surviving even more dangerous foes. The last point was a tossup. Agility or Power? Power would give him better sprinting and higher damage. But the fights were always scrappy. Since all Don had was a small knife as a weapon, he had to wrestle with the enemies. Agility would give him a more nimble body, giving him better control of fights. He decided to put his last point in Agility. He enjoyed the surge of power within him. The sense of Constitution increasing was a strange one. He felt like he was more… solid. He would have to see how it affected his combat abilities. But now Don needed to go back into the forest.
