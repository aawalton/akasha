In a hidden place between the provinces, deep inside a misty mountain valley, there were several people with ominous auras gathered in a room. The man presiding over the room, who wore black robes with golden details, spoke up at this moment. “If she wants to go, she has the right to. We won’t stray from our path because a small tree has appeared in our way.” The man stood up and turned around, leaving one sentence. “And if the Tree doesn’t move out of our way, we’ll just uproot it so that it doesn’t bother us anymore. Marlius, you are in charge of the main advancement unit. I’ll keep Thalgryn in check.”

Marlius smirked. “Sure, my <Voidblossom Consummators> will make sure to carry out this project.”
