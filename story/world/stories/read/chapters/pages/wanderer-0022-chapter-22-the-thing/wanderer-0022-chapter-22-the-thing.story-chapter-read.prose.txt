Possessive cocky bastard.

After breaking our fast each day, we walked through the village to check on the progress of multiple structures being built, including a massive long house and these thrones in the tavern, as well as monitoring the state of the crops. I didn't realize just how busy the clan has been since the raid, and they've made impressive progress. Tir Tairngire is quickly transforming into a viking village, becoming only a ghost of the place it was before.

Our clan population is also growing, the ship returned yesterday filled with more clans members, women and even children from Scandinavia, venturing to Tir Tairngire to start their new life now that a settlement was being established. Froðe explained to me that there was a shortage of land available in his homeland, causing many disputes and arguments amongst his people on who were entitled to what land. Typically the clan also raided Eastern lands for spoils, though there were rumors circulating of untouched riches in the West and even possible land to settle in, which is why they decided to come here.

Currently we are holding The Thing, which is an assembly of the clan that functions as a court or forum for making political decisions, upholding laws, and settling disputes. The disputes in question have been....interesting. The last speaker just finished calling out another clans member for damaging his reputation with repeated insults on his um...nether regions, which is apparently a grave offense. The offending man, wood whittling Gunnar of all people, was punished by having a portion of his spoils in the last raid confiscated, as well as his most recent figurine he finished whittling; a rather impressive wolf. Gunnar is now sadly drinking his mead in a corner of the room, clearly not happy with the result of Froðe's ruling, but dares not challenge it.

A clansman who I have not seen before steps before us, clearly upset. Dark circles rim his eyes and his beard is unkempt in comparison to the others I've seen sported by the men here.

"Speak." Froðe's gravelly voice is all command next to me, but the backs of his fingers trace a caress along my arm, sending little tingles along the path he traces. Though I am still recovering from my concussion, he has wasted zero time in learning every inch of my body with these soft touches, slowly torturing me and building anticipation for something that I think not even the two of us are ready for. As if he can read my thoughts, the ghost of a smirk tugs at the corner of his beautiful mouth.

Fuck. I'm in so much trouble.

Here I am, gawking at him again. Focus! You are trying to learn their ways Beatrix.

"Jarl Froðe...my brother...Leif, was murdered yesterday."

"Continue. Why do you believe your brother was murdered, Olaf?"

Sigrid, who is sitting nearby, spits on the tavern floor in disgust. The rest clang their tankards on the tables and more shouts fill the room.

Meanwhile...I'm at a loss. Didn't they raid this exact village at night? And kill defenseless people? How is that any different? Not to mention the several bouts of fighting I've seen amongst our own clan members with my time here. Just the other day I saw two clansmen almost kill each other at the evening meal over a disagreement.

Laws apply to members of this community only, I remind myself. And the viking people believe that you should be able to defend yourself, and the greatest honor is to die in battle and thus be granted entry to Valhalla. If Leif was killed in his sleep...without any ability to defend himself, does that mean he does not get granted entry to Valhalla?

That would explain why this would be such a severe offense.

"Do you have any idea of who might have done it?"

Olaf's eyes glimmer in rage, and he nods, looking over his shoulder to a group of seated clansmen.

"Yes...I have an idea Jarl."

Curious, I look where Olaf has indicated, searching the faces for any sign of guilt, but everyone is looking at us, eager for the rest of the story to unfold. Wait...there, I double back to a man I originally glanced over. He sits in the back, his eyes downcast, fidgeting with the handle of his tankard. Hmmm...Olaf might be on to something. But is there a motive?

Froðe rises an eyebrow at me, but nods to Olaf, indicating for him to answer the question.

"Yes, I believe he had a reason, Frú," Olaf says, and I try not to wince at the title before he continues. "He's been eyeing Leif's woman, Hilda, for some time. Never sat well with him that she choose to lay with Leif instead."

I watch the man closely as Olaf says this, and sure enough, he grimaces, gripping the handle of his tankard enough that his hand turns red.

"Name him, then," Froðe commands, but he is studying me.

"Ragnar."

Everyone's heads in the tavern turn to the man in the back. He stands suddenly, roughly abandoning the tankard on the table so that it topples over, spilling mead everywhere. It flows over the table in an amber wave and begins dripping to the floor.

"I AM NO COWARD," Ragnar roars, his face red with anger. "I would never steep to such a level. He is lying! He likely did it himself, jealous of his brother for having found a woman, and is using me to blame since he knew I was always soft for Hilda!"

Denial and deflection by shifting blame, topped with a sprinkle of gaslighting. Now that he's stood up into the firelight of our center hearth, I can see that two beads of sweat also line his brow.

"Me?! Jealous! Ha!" Olaf laughs, but there is no light in his eyes to match the expression. "I loved my brother Jarl, I would never harm him," he says turning back to Froðe.

"Hmmm..." Froðe considers this, then surprisingly, looks to me with a raised eyebrow, like he wants me to take the lead again.

"Where is Hilda now? Is she here to share her side of the story?" I ask.

Ragnar's face falters, but then he quickly schools it back into anger. Unfortunately for him, it was not fast enough for my scrutiny and further affirms that I am on the right track. Keep watching his body language, gather evidence, and make an assessment. My analyst brain kicks into full gear, considering my next questions and continuing to study Ragnar, noting the fidgeting of his hands and the sweat beads that are now dripping fully down the side of his brow. He's clearly uncomfortable.

"Hilda. Come forward," Froðe commands.

Hands are fully visible and open and she maintains eye contact. She's not lying. I might not be an expert in the art of body language, but my obsession with true crime documentaries is definitely paying off right now.

She's protecting him. But why?

An affair, maybe.

Fuck. It's definitely an affair.
