Mana crystals and objects, both glittering and mundane, tumbled out of the bags.

Then they just kept tumbling.

Oh my god, it's like Mary Poppins's purse, Thomas thought with glee as he shook out a spear that was longer than he stood tall. It fell with a clatter to the floor, along with a seedling in a pot that sparked with electric energy. He caught a glimpse of at least two Wellspring mana crystals in the absolute waterfall of mana, too. Finally, with the clink of a B-grade sized Light mana crystal, the bags were empty.

There was stunned silence for a few seconds until Jo demanded, "Where did you get all that?"

Thomas couldn't keep the grin off his face. "These were the bags the guards were holding. I guess they had been really skimming off the top."

"Oh hey, there's a skill shard," Zach said, deceptively casual, and pointed. Jo saw it at the same time and bent to snatch it up. She held it between two fingers, examining it, before her shoulders slumped. "It's called Preserve Food."

"That's all it says?" Thomas asked, then at her nod added, "Damn. Akilah once told me that the flashier the name, the more potential it has to grow with your levels. I guess this one just gets the job done."

Jo shrugged then placed the shard in her pants pocket. "You never know, right?"

"Yeah, yeah, that's cool and all, but what about this?" Twitch had picked up the long spear and looked it over with reverence. Thomas didn't know why: It looked pretty mundane. The head of this one was plain iron with no flashy designs, and several places along the shaft were wrapped in leather. He supposed that was where people were supposed to grip the weapon. He wasn't a big spear guy.

Twitch squinted at it, obviously querying his System to see its title, before he burst out laughing. "Check this out: It's a 'Growing Shaft' spear." He grinned and, in pure twelve-year-old boy humor, he positioned it downward suggestively.

Jo rolled her eyes and bent to sort through the pile of loot.

Thomas couldn't help himself. "So it gets longer?"

"Yeah, I guess. It feels like it really likes Nature mana—" Twitch broke off as the spearhead suddenly shot outward violently a few feet as the wooden haft of the spear lengthened. It stayed that way for a second then retracted to its normal length.

"I'm keeping it," Twitch said immediately, and no one argued. Between the spear, the electric stunning skill shard, and the vest, he was getting kitted out.

"You're going to poke someone's eye out with that thing," Jo said flatly.

"If all goes right, I'm going to poke someone's face out," Twitch corrected.

Thomas left him to it and started sorting through the pile with Jo and Zach. There were some other interesting things that weren't weapons, like a screwdriver that pulsed with electric mana and a couple of vials glowing with bright liquid that had the feeling of Light in them. None of those seemed to interact with his Healing mana, the System didn't give a description, and he wasn't going to test them out to see what they did. Still, Thomas took those for himself. (Not that he planned to leave any of this behind.)

He took the Wellspring mana crystals as well. Too bad they were level 3.

Brushing a Nature mana crystal away from the pile, he found a pair of brass knuckles underneath. Like the spear, they were unassuming for loot drops. There was no engraving or glow or anything else that would set them apart from weapons sold in a store.

Frowning, Thomas slipped one on and felt the motes of mana in his bloodstream instantly react and flow to the brass knuckles. Weirdly, it took all four of his mana types: Healing, Wellspring, Adaptation, and Light, without seeming to worry about being compatible with any of it. After a few moments, the flow stopped, and Thomas had the impression the brass knuckles were full.

"Huh," he said out loud, and Zach glanced up from where he'd been examining the seedling.

"What'd you find, Mana Man?"

"Brass knuckles or something. I'm not sure what they do yet, other than the obvious." He twisted his wrist to look at both sides of the device, then decided what the hell. He wasn't going to test this on a person, so he stood up and walked to the stone wall and squared up in front of it. Then Thomas punched. Because he didn't feel like breaking his hand at that moment, he didn't throw his all into it, which made the crack that ran up the wall all the more satisfying. A chunk of stone broke loose and hit the floor.

"Whoa there, tiger, don't bring this place around our ears," Jo called with a nervous laugh.

Thomas looked at the knuckles. They had definitely helped, but then he also remembered how he had matched Tiny for strength. Even now, he felt a trickle of Wellspring mana traveling from his core to reinforce the muscles around his shoulders and right arm. As a new level four, his core was generating more mana for itself than ever, and the shard was putting it to good use.

"These things are pretty mana hungry," Thomas said, feeling it out. "They basically need to be refilled with mana every time I use them."

"Bro, they pack a hell of a punch," Zach said. "What mana were you running? Light shouldn't be able to do that."

"It's taking my whole mix."

"Huh, I guess it's universally compatible. That's kind of rare."

"Yup. I'm keeping these," Thomas decided.

"Finally getting some muscle to go with all that support magic. You gonna get a leather jacket too?" Zach grinned when Thomas playfully flipped him the bird, then held up the seedling he'd plucked from the pile. "I'm taking this little guy home with me."

Thomas eyed the plant doubtfully. His mother, or maybe his scientist brother, Pete, would know what it was by sight. All Thomas could say for sure was that it was green and had four leaves. Oh, and it was sparking with arcs of blue current. "Looks like it takes electric mana?"

"Yeah, but I think..." Zach's eyes went distant as he seemed to look inward. "I dunno, I just have a feeling. You know?" Coming back to himself, he tucked the seedling in his pack as carefully as he would Kevin.

Jo, meanwhile, had picked up a mundane-looking dagger for herself and started sweeping the remaining mana crystals back into the bags. "Well, as fun as this is, Tiny's not going to be purging himself forever."

The reminder effectively killed the playful mood in the room, and everybody put their game face back on.

"Oh wait, one more thing." Thomas could have smacked his own forehead, barely believing he'd forgotten this last bit. Between the shock of the scrolls, the problem of Courtney, and the other loot bags, the final dungeon reward chest had kinda gotten overlooked.

Antonin was currently sitting quietly on top of the reward chest, but he hastily moved at Thomas's polite gesture.

Thomas opened the lid, frankly not expecting much. Reward chests were kind of a fun bonus but didn't usually stack up compared to what his Gift provided.

That was why he was so surprised to see a tiny sword inside the chest.

"Whoa, what's that, a sword for ants?" Twitch asked, coming over.

Thomas shut his mouth and gave Twitch an annoyed look because he was going to make that joke.

He didn't know much about swords but picked up a few things in the Flying Sword Dungeon. To his eye, it looked to be a standard long sword with a T-bar for the handle, just miniaturized to a couple inches in length.

"Hey Jo, got a fun stabbing needle for you," he said, then picked it up. The sword grew warm in his hand, and the moment it left the chest it grew. Twitch hurriedly stepped aside before he got accidentally impaled. A moment later, Thomas was holding a regular-sized sword.

"Another growing sword?" Zach asked, coming over. "Huh. What are the odds."

Thomas's brow knit. "Maybe it was just like that for storage." He handed it over to Jo, handle first. "What do you think?" It had to beat the rusty sword that the guards had given her.

Brow furrowed, she took it and gave a few experimental swings. Of course, her arm guard training aid was gone, but she seemed to have learned a few things from it because her movements were slow, controlled, and looked professional. Beads of blackish-purple started to seep from the blade. Jo looked at it and grinned. "Got my Poison back."

"Poison mana?" Twitch hurriedly stepped away, which was an unusually smart move.

Thomas swept the room with his eyes to make absolutely sure that they weren't leaving a speck of loot behind. Sure, a lot of the crystals in the bags were useless to him other than short-term fuel for the enchantments on his halberd, but he could always sell them for higher-quality loot. "All right, I think that's everything. Let's get Courtney and get the hell out of here and get to this quest already."

Unfortunately... The boss room was empty. Courtney, it seemed, had not been swayed by the promise of a mana shard and was nowhere to be found. She hadn't even left the shoelace bindings behind.

Thomas would never admit it aloud, but a tiny part of him was relieved because Bring Light into the Darkness was a cool-sounding skill. Now, he could keep it.

The rest of him was understandably horrified.

Zach started to swear rather creatively and sulfurously. Jo turned away disgusted, and Twitch... Twitch just stood there for a moment with his jaw flexing before he inhaled a breath and nodded once to himself.

"Don't suppose a monster or something took her?" Twitch asked.

Thomas shook his head. He was already checking out the dungeon through his Health Sight and quickly identified the human-shaped life signs which were moving through the dungeon. "Nope, she's running for the entrance. Looks like Tiny's done with his Purge too... or at least, he's well enough to move. Actually," Thomas unconsciously cocked his head, focusing on what the information was feeding him. "I think they're dealing with a small swarm of wisps we didn't get to clear out, and... Yeah, Courtney and Tiny's group are going to run into each other soon. That'll be fun."

"It may slow them down," Jo added.

Zach shook his head. "Not enough. We need to get going."

It was a grim team that went back into the exit room and activated the gems that would make them once again look like Light elves.

Stepping to Antonin, Jo smiled kindly and threaded her arm through his. "Stick with me, hun. I'll make sure no one can see you. Okay? Just keep quiet."

"Quiet," Antonin agreed and put a finger up to his lips in a shushing gesture.

"That's right."

Thomas took a breath and grabbed up the Mutable shield. Game time. "All right, fake British accents on."

He opened the exit door.

Considering how their luck usually ran, and the fact that the System told them they'd be at a disadvantage, he half expected an ambush.

A handful of armed guards were waiting for them, as well as Federick. Everybody was tense, but no one was actively pointing swords their way. Taking that as a good sign, Thomas put on his haughtiest expression and strode out.

The moment he stepped a foot out into the real world, the System granted its boon to enhance his Gift.

Thomas felt the distinctly odd sensation of something within his chest expanding as if someone were blowing up a balloon near his heart. It smoothly expanded, not painful or uncomfortable, just... weird. Between one footfall and the next, the invisible balloon sensation expanded past his torso and outward in every direction, filling him up to his fingers, the top of his head, and the tips of his toes. The worst of it faded, yet Thomas felt distinctly... more. As if he were both grounded and stronger than he'd ever been before. Like his spirit itself could shape the world.

He didn't dare glance at the others to see if they had noticed the same thing. How could they not?

The guards in front of him weren't reacting in shock or anything, so he assumed Zach wasn't actively on fire.

"Two hours, thirty minutes," Federick said. "That's about an average run for this dungeon." He waited a moment, glancing back towards the door. "Where are the others?"

Now Thomas did risk a look behind him. Jo and Antonin were nowhere in sight. She had to be hiding him using her gift.

Putting on his most snooty air, Thomas said, "They were weak and failed to make it. The dungeon reaped their lives." Oh shit, was that too poetic? Well, whatever.

Federick blinked, and a couple of guards shifted around uneasily. A few shot him aggravated looks, but no one seemed broken up by the news. Poor Courtney and those two other guards. Thomas just got the impression the rest were all hoping they wouldn't be next on the dungeon's menu.

"I fully expect your people to replace the personnel that were lost," Federick said. "That is only fair."

Thomas felt like correcting him that prisoners were not "personnel," but fortunately, he didn't have to bother as a sword weeping beads of purple-black poison mana exploded out of the front of Frederick's chest.

Jo had stabbed him from behind. There was no indication she was there at all. Her Gift of hiding kept her completely out of view, the sneaky assassin.

Frederick gasped out a sound that Thomas would have rather not heard and sank to his knees. Jo must have yanked the sword free, because it was gone a moment later, leaving only toxic green Corruption mana starting to bubble out of the wound. The sight of Corruption worked as a healer repellent, overriding any twinge from Thomas's Burden.

Not that it mattered, because he had his hands full as the guards rightfully blamed the three "Light elves" and went on the immediate attack. Thomas raised his shield just in time to block a hammer's blow. It bonged right off the surface. Then, as the man staggered back with a confused look on his face, Thomas turned and punched out with his free hand, catching another guard who was coming up to the side to try to stab Twitch, who was dealing with two others. He felt the man's arm shatter under his enhanced brass-knuckle punch. Thomas kicked his leg out from under him, and the man crashed down, howling.

Okay, he felt a little bad about that. The guy would live, though.

The guard who had rushed up to Thomas with axe-blade in hand, trying to chop him in the side, wasn't so lucky. He burst immediately into a raging fire so hot that Thomas reeled back to keep from getting burned by the air around him. He hadn't even been hit by Zach's normal ribbon of fire; he just went up so fast and so sudden that his skin started to charcoalize instantly.

Two Corruption mana crystals and a skill shard fell from the body, right before it crumpled into ash.
