Kal hadn’t really been serious before that, but now he was, and Beth could feel just how strong he was. He was clearly much more powerful than anyone else in the room, even her, and the weight of his power was highly oppressive. It also helped that Kal was touching on his Ideal; Beth could feel it in his mana signature and the power he radiated. He hadn’t completed it yet, it was still only partially finished, but he had definitely made much more progress than many Ascended had towards that point. Beth estimated that, if he could get enough time, he would step into the ranks of Exalted, which was not always true. Many Ascended could be given a million years, or even a billion years, and they would still never even touch upon the core of their Ideal, let alone partially finish it.

Mark stood and left the room, Arianna using some kind of skill or spell before he left, likely something to help her track him to really make sure he didn’t run. After that, Thomas and Kal made a bit of polite small talk before leaving themselves, Kal explaining he had a lot of things to do in overseeing the planet. Then, you were fighting against an Ascended, which is a greater level of destruction.”

“What’s the big deal with that, anyway?” Beth asked. “I had always thought that Ascended was so big just because it was such a big range. Is there something else to it?”

“It’s not widely discussed, but there’s two critical factors,” Arianna said. “For you, in Enlightened, one of the biggest benefits is that your Mana Physique is fully integrated with your body. You will be getting a bit stronger each rebirth, and your total mana capacity will increase a decent bit, sometimes quite a lot, depending on several factors.

“In Ascended, your mana capacity still increases a bit each rebirth, but there are two other factors at play, like I said. The first is that you start to increase the purity of your mana. Essentially, for a basic explanation, it’s like your mana is mist or vapor right now. When you get to Ascended, that mist starts to condense, with it fully condensing at rebirth fifty into pure liquid. It’s the other reason why Exalted can wipe the floor with everyone; their mana density and purity is much higher than anyone below Exalted. Even between rebirth forty-nine and rebirth fifty, there’s a big difference, not to mention someone at rebirth fifty now having their Ideal.”

“Right, that’s pretty easy to understand,” Beth said. “Though, I will say, it didn’t really feel all that different, duking it out with Mark versus others I’ve fought before.”

“Yes, that’s where the second part comes in,” Arianna said. “Not only do Ascended have purer mana, but they also can express more of that mana in skills. When you use a skill, you use, let’s just say, a hundred mana to use a skill. It’s not that precise, but this is just for an example.”

“Right, I gotcha,” Beth nodded.

“So, you use a hundred mana, and you cast a skill. When you become an Ascended, you still use a hundred mana and cast that skill, but now your mana is denser, so it’s more like you used a hundred and twenty, or even a hundred and fifty mana to cast the skill. So, you have the bigger oomph for the same price,” Arianna explained. “That’s the easier part to understand. What many don’t know is that you can take the same skill, at the same rank, and use up to twice the mana to get double or more the effect. It’s not just twice what you’d normally use, it’s up to twice what the maximum is, you understand?”

“Oh, wow, that’s a lot of extra power,” said Beth.

“Right, though the extra mana part is both hard to do and really, really straining. Even people with more than forty rebirths can’t just fire off a thousand skill uses in a row while overloading the skill. It’s too much strain on that person’s mind and soul to do it. If someone does it too much in rapid succession, they start to become rapidly exhausted, suffer from nausea and headaches, start to have their consciousness fade, and will pass out, sometimes with their mana interrupted, meaning even secondary skills that apply protection or buffing will fail. It’s a very risky thing to do overall so I doubt Mark made use of it, but you need to be careful when you’re fighting Ascended. The one-two combo of denser mana and overloading skills is why their far more respected than if they were just an Enlightened with a couple more rebirths,” Arianna said.
