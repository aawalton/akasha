EDN terminals are the primary interface method with the network. Communication can be audiovisual or written, with written allowing for delayed delivery. Accessed content is projected as a Feed, a hovering expanse of Energy that can be interacted with and manipulated while it creates visual data.

Terminals need to be connected into a Construct that has EDN access to be functional and specialized functions can be configured when associated with other Construct elements. A Terminal next to a Portal or Doorway typically serves as notification of incoming Awakened and defaults to displaying access requests, while one next to a Vault would typically default to displaying an inventory list to simplify collaboration.
