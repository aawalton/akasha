Chapter 132

The Anticipation

After Madame Bloom left, I couldn’t sleep. My mind was filled with an odd tribulation of thoughts. I had the adrenaline from the fight with my adoptive aunts, the knowledge that I had healed Luna’s face and made her truly beautiful. How I was to perform in some ritual for the Matriarch. But above all, I still could feel traces of that kiss lingering on my lips.

There were times when I would get close to being able to sleep. Times where I could feel my mind almost relaxing, forcing it to let go of all the different thoughts streaming in my head. Then just when I was about to finally give in, I would feel a tug, a pull, or better still, hear Luna’s words in my ear, “you fool.”

I even wrestled with the idea of having a relationship with Luna. She was legally old enough to make decisions on her own. Old enough for this lifetime, and even for my last. With both lifetimes’ worth of memories combined, I was now only at thirty-six years old. Even then, most of the memories from my past life were hollow and faded, as if only providing a loose framework to build upon. At first, I wrestled with the idea that she was only half my combined age, thinking that I should be ashamed of myself. Only to realize that I did not press for her, but rather it was she who made the first move, and what a move at that.

Touching my still tingling lips, I smiled, and then finally concluded there would be no harm if she pursued me first. That I could respond at her pace and time and make it less awkward for everyone. Only then, once I had finally come to terms with myself that I wouldn’t be embarrassed by this relationship that was developing platonically. I also came to terms with the fact that I would appreciate this relationship for what it was, my first one that I could remember from either lifetime, and that I would cherish it afterwards. Only then did my mind truly relax enough for me to finally be able to close my eyes and drift off to sleep.

***

(What felt like two minutes later)

There was an intense pounding at my door, as if I was being robbed. My mind that had been blank and peacefully resting a moment ago was suddenly awake and ready for action. My body sprang up and began preparing for defensive maneuvers almost instantly. Only to realize that I was bleary eyed, blinking and staring at a room that my mind was just coming to recognize as my own room. Well, my new room from the Bloom estate.

“Lady Catarine, it is time to prepare for the ceremony,” Pierre’s voice called out from the hallway, a note of calm understanding in his words as he finally provided my mind the anchoring point it needed to fully connect with where I was today. His words letting me know that I had apparently somehow fallen asleep at some point last night, only to wake up in what must be the worse state. That state where your mind is still fuzzy and you feel far less rested than when you first went to bed last night.

“Coming,” I reply, getting up, getting on my clothes and making sure to practice my control with my Trash Skill to remove any impurities from my body. From there, I was brought out to see Pierre ready to take me breakfast, where I had a large meal and plate set out before me. Seeing the spread, that was clearly sectioned off for me alone, I paused. Especially after I realized that was a lot more food than I was used to eating in general.

“Madame Bloom has indicated that you might not be eating enough. That along with the rigors of what you will undergo today were enough that she expressed you take in a larger amount of iron, protein, and salts, thus the platter that you see before you.”

Looking at the food, it was a lot, apple slices, eggs, sausages, and buttered toast. I almost wanted to protest, but then realized that Pierre looked like he was willing to argue with me on Madame Bloom’s behalf. Seeing the posture, I went back to my efficient ways of eating from my past life, making a breakfast sandwich out of everything, putting eggs, and sausages between two slices of toast before eating. The moment my lips tasted the food, I felt a surge of joy in my mouth that spread down. It was clear that this body was much more of a foodie than my past life. That or the memories I had from enjoying food from my past life had faded until I didn’t recall anything. I couldn’t even tell why. I Just knew that each time I tasted food in this life, it was amazing, and I could almost taste individual flavors and spices that were being used and where. What had begun as a begrudging breakfast due to how tired I was, quickly dissolved as my mind awoke at the taste and enjoyment of the food before me. Only once the first such sandwich was gone, did I realize that my now awake stomach was not only awake, but hungry. Then I created a second sandwich, trying to take my time and enjoy it, the way the golden yolks exploded on the toast just right, filling the nooks in the bread just enough to add more flavors to be enjoyed. I tried to enjoy it, but that second sandwich was finished just as quickly as the first one. Pausing between sandwiches, I used Trash to clean my fingers, and remove any mess I might have made. Then switched to the apple slices, trying to calm my stomach down. But even after the apple slices, I could tell that I would need to make at least one more sandwich. I was confused as this seemed excessive. Fortunately, Pierre was there to provide commentary, no doubt to drown out the sound of my chewing.

“As a healer, you will note that there are days when your body will need more iron and proteins specifically. Generally a week after you start really using your Healing Skills. This delay is both a good thing and a bad thing, as your body can often regulate itself to replenish most energy lost daily. However, it has been noted that every once in a while, it is suggested to take a moment and have a proper meal made available. Like so,” Pierre commented.

“Once a week?” I manage to get out between bites of food, as I come up for air.

“Yes, at least once a week. Though if you do intend to join Lucia and Luna at Unity University, then you will need to be more considerate of your diet,” Pierre begins, and I can sense a shift in his demeanor before he continues, “while you might have been able to get by with custards before your Class awakening. You will find that your body will need more food that is richer in proteins and iron than you are used to.”

I nod, taking in his words as I continued to eat. My body coming to life with each bite, and explosion of flavor in my mouth. Thinking about it, I can see that this makes sense, as my body is using mana, which logically has to come from somewhere. While a smaller amount might be easily replaced over time, larger quantities, like I have been using over the past week will start to add up if I am not careful. This is good to know. Then something in Pierre’s words hits me as if I know it was a baited hook approach, but I can’t help but grab onto the hook anyways.

“Wait, is there a way for me to join Luna at Uni?” I ask. At my words, I can see a faint smile form on Pierre’s lips. A smile that seems to host a myriad of emotions, pride, acceptance, and multiple others that are hard to put words to.

“There is,” Pierre begins, and shifts his tone, “know that Madame Bloom has put together the chance for you to earn a spot to go with them. All you need to do is show yourself well today, and the rest will take care of itself.”

Again, it is cryptic, as I understand we are going to apparently do a few rights and something to deal with my bloodline sword? But I really have no clue. Most of what was said last night is now gone, lost to my spinning thoughts from last night and was now completely lost of what to actually expect from today.

“Can you help me understand exactly what is supposed to happen today? I am still a bit confused on this whole process?”

Heairng my question, Pierre nods his head as if he was expecting this.

“While I cannot tell you exactly what to expect, know that today should consist of three parts. First, you will leave here in your healer’s garb, along with Madame Bloom, and go to the Ancestral Cathedral, where you will prove your bloodline to the other healers present. While I cannot go further into this, as I do not know. Know that this is a big thing where all seven major healing bloodlines, and twenty-one minor healing bloodlines will be acknowledged and can be proven to each other. While you are all at the cathedral, a trial for those who have been confirmed to have harbored Death Eaters at one time will be proven to the audience. Those that are found guilty, will be condemned to death. That is when you healers come out and bear witness to those who have been condemned. The youngest generation will be granted a chance to go up and save those who are deemed by the others to be irredeemable.”

Hearing his words, I nod as I think I got it all, but make sure to paraphrase it down to individual steps that will make sense to me.

“So there are three events total. The first is us going to the Ancestral Cathedral, where our healing bloodlines will be acknowledges. While that is happening, a trial will take place in the market square just outside the cathedral, where people will be tried and convicted. Then we will come out and try to heal those that have been convicted?” I ask, wondering if I am missing something.

“Essentially yes,” Pierre replies nodding back on his own.

I pause trying to take everything in, “and I assume that my having a good showing would be if I could heal one of the condemned people?”

As I think about this, I remember how I saved Johny from that glowing ember inside of him. If it was something like that, then I should be fine, in fact I even felt that I could do better than last time, now that I had more time to think about the issue. Also thinking back to that time, it was clear that the ROW member had given me a sword in preparation to kill Johny, not heal him.

“Yes, I’d dare say that being able to heal one of the condemned would be an amazing showing,” Pierre replied. I could tell that he knew more, as I almost felt the weight of expectation behind his response.

Still, I wondered, if these people were being tried and convicted, then wouldn’t it be wrong for me to overturn their conviction and try to save them?  That’s when I asked that exact question.

“My question is, if these people have been tried and convicted by the people, then who am I to overturn the judgement that is being passed? I mean, should I even try to heal those that have been convicted in such a way?” I ask, truly trying to understand this world’s justice system.

Hearing my words, I can see Pierre shift somewhat uncomfortably in his stance, as he seems to weigh and balance out his response.

“I think you are looking at it all wrong. The audience, the jurors in this case are convicting these people, not because they are a menace to society still, but that their very existence is a continued danger to society. That is why those that are convicted, will be cut, bled, decapitated, and burned to ash, in that order. Not because we take satisfaction in their pain, but that we all know what it would mean if one of these plague bearers survives and turns into a future Death Eater once they pass away. Which is what happens when a Death Eater’s spark remains within one who carried a Death Eater. That lingering spark remains dormant, until either awakened, or until the host dies. Then the spark will use all available nutrients from the host body, until it can convert to an undead state, and continue the cycle of death. That is why we must all work to stop it. That is why such trials are made public, so that no one has any doubt about the safety of themselves, their families, and the country they are in. This might seem barbaric, but it is the only way to show that we are safe.” Pierre states passionately, his words showing more sorrow and sadness than he likely meant to convey. It was clear that he had likely seen at least one of these so called trials before. As I am taking in the full weight of his words, he continues.

“That is why, I will implore you. If you can save just one of these condemned from their fated executions, then you must. You need to do everything in your power to insure at least one life survives, if at all possible.”

“But why?” I ask, my mind reeling from all the information and the sudden influx of nutrients to my system.

“Because, you aren’t saving them. You are saving those that came to watch. You are showing that there is hope. That there is a way forward, and that we can have a brighter tomorrow. That is why, above all else, you need to have a good showing. If not for yourself. If not for your chance to join Lucia and Luna at University. If not for the people that you save that will be forever scorned and subjected to scrutiny for the rest of their lives. Then do it for the fact that you can bring hope to people who desperately need something to pull them through this.”

And with that, I was inspired to truly showcase everything I could do, not for myself. But as Pierre said, this was bigger than me. This was about showing that we could bring hope, where there was none before. I nodded, then gave my two-word reply.

“I’m in.”
