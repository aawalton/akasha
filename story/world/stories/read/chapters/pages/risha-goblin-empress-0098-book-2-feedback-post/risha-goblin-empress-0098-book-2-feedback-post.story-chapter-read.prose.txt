In summary (with spoilers if you're not done reading), Book 2 starts with Risha visiting the many new factions of her Empire, evolving Klaz'zks into one of her Chosen, and then evolving the drake riders and exploring life as Empress. After meeting Hwahwei, she travels into the depths and frees the Under Goblins from the Demons, and meets her first Spark, Koliekan The Chained.

After bringing the Under Goblins under the umbrella of her empire, she meets Mother Cala-Ra and begins trade with several of the creatures of the depths. Unfortunately, on her borders, war is brewing as the Scaled attack her lands. Risha is forced to make an uneasy alliance with a new threat of the Saplings, giving them access to the Lin child she protects in the form of a teacher.

Risha rides out, fighting many battles, and slowly discovering that the Scaled are being controlled through ancient Clan bonds that have been taken over by something powerful. She continues deeper, struggling with the realities of war.

During the war, she begins to take lessons from a mysterious ancient Goblin Shaman, learning the secrets of Spirit and the spirit realm. She contracts two powerful spirits to fight by her side. Anyxala (Nyx) the void-night spirit, and Igni, a powerful spirit of fire.

Eventually, Risha wins her war, and is left with a defeated people, where she once again has to find ways to integrate them into her empire. She chooses a powerful leader, a young Dragon by the name of Elderoa, to help lead them as one of her people.

With matters settled with the Scaled, Risha travels to the home of the Kobolds to claim her spot as their Alpha. She faces challenges of both wisdom and strength.

With her battles won, she looks to the future, where ancient gods begin to rise across the oceans, and a young demoness looks to inherit the power of her long dead Mother.
