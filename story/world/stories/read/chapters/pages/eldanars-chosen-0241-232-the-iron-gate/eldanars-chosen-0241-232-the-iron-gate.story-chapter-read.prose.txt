The pulverised bone and thick, black smoke kicked up by the destruction of the siege engines slowly began to drift away on the freezing winter wind.

Josh and Bhel stood alone in the churned, ruined mud of the battlefield. They slowly backed up, their heavy boots stepping over shattered skeletons, ruined iron plating, and deep craters, until their backplates pressed firmly against the sealed iron portcullis of Ashenfall. The towering, iron-banded gates offered absolutely no avenue of escape, but the solid metal meant they could not be surrounded from behind.

It was a terrible defensive position. The gateway was nearly thirty feet wide, entirely too broad to serve as a proper, restrictive bottleneck for two men. However, it forced the approaching ocean of monsters into a direct, frontal assault.

"Keep your footing tight," Josh commanded, his voice a low, exhausted rumble behind his steel visor. "Do not step out into the mud. We make them come to us."

Bhel simply grunted in agreement, spitting a thick wad of dark blood onto the cobblestones. The dwarven fighter spun his twin iron axes, settling into a low, braced stance. His breathing was heavy, the pristine white bandages on his chest already completely soaked with a fresh, bright crimson stain.

The brief, stunned silence that had followed the fall of the Bone Goliaths shattered violently. The mindless protocol of the lesser horde reasserted itself, and the chaotic, writhing mass of rotting flesh and bone surged forward to fill the void left by their fallen siege engines.

The first monster to reach the gates was not a shambling zombie, but an ancient skeletal warrior. It wore the rusted, interlocking chainmail of a bygone era and carried a heavy, jagged broadsword. It charged with terrifying, unnatural speed, leaping over the smoking crater of Josh's landing to bring its weapon down in a brutal, sweeping arc.

Josh did not flinch. He simply raised his shield to take the blow. The rusted broadsword crashed against the dark metal with a deafening clang. The silver runes inlaid along the rim of the Bulwark of the Fallen flared brightly, instantly absorbing the worst of the kinetic shock. Josh barely felt the impact. He pivoted smoothly, sweeping his heavy steel longsword in a devastating horizontal counter-strike.

The blade sheared cleanly through the skeleton's rusted chainmail and shattered its brittle ribcage. The ancient warrior collapsed into a pile of loose bones, its glowing green eyes winking out instantly.

They were significantly tougher and faster than the rotting villagers, but their ancient bones were brittle compared to master-crafted steel. Josh kicked the heavy skull away, resetting his stance.

But there was no time to breathe. The single skeleton was merely the tip of the spear.

A massive, shrieking flood of monsters poured into the clearing. It was a horrifying, chaotic mixture of sizes and races. There were corrupted forest trolls dragging heavy chains, skeletal hounds snapping their fleshless jaws, and dozens of rotting ghouls surging forward in a mindless frenzy.

Bhel went to work with terrifying, mechanical efficiency. He ducked beneath a thrusting spear, driving his left axe deep into the kneecap of a towering orc zombie, before bringing his right axe around to cleanly sever its thick neck. A heavy spray of dark, coagulated blood washed over the dwarf's face, but he did not even blink, immediately spinning to deflect the rusted claws of a leaping ghoul.

As the massive orc corpse fell, turning to golden dust, a tiny, erratic figure lunged out from beneath the collapsing bulk, scrambling through the mud directly for Bhel's exposed legs. The creature was small, barely reaching the dwarf's chest. Its face was a horrifying mask of bruised, peeling flesh and exposed bone, but the round, unusually smooth structure of its jaw and the oversized, slightly pointed ears were completely undeniable.

Bhel raised his iron-plated boot to violently kick the creature away, but he froze for a microscopic fraction of a second.

A sharp, physical pang of recognition pierced straight through the dwarf's chest, hitting him harder than any physical blow. Dangling from the creature's ruined, mud-caked neck was a tarnished silver sunburst medallion.

It was Torrel. The young gnome healer from Bhel’s very first adventuring party. Bhel clearly remembered the boy’s clean-shaven, baby-faced smile, his endless, chatty optimism in the dark tunnels, and the way he always compulsively polished that exact silver sunburst. Torrel had been lost when the undead dungeon first broke. The dark magic of the plains had not just raised the recent dead; it had dredged up the ancient, buried tragedies of the entire province.

The gnome's glowing, milky eyes held absolutely no recognition. The tiny monster clawed savagely at Bhel's iron-plated shin, its ruined mouth snapping uselessly in a silent, horrific frenzy.

Bhel swallowed a sudden, thick knot of profound, suffocating grief. He forced his dark eyes to harden, completely locking away his emotions behind a wall of cold survival instinct. He did not hesitate again. He brought his heavy iron boot down with crushing, uncompromising force, shattering the young healer's skull and permanently ending his nightmare.

"They are just meat now, Bhel!" Josh roared over the din of the clash, seeing the dwarf's momentary hesitation as he brutally battered a skeletal hound away with his shield. "Do not look at their faces!"

"I know!" Bhel bellowed back, his voice thick with a raw, battle-crazed fury born of pure sorrow. He swung his axes with renewed, devastating violence, cleaving through rusted chainmail and rotting flesh alike. "Let them come! I have enough iron for all of them!"

They settled into the brutal, grinding rhythm of the fight. The sheer volume of enemies pressing into the gateway was staggering, forcing them to fight tightly shoulder to shoulder. The physical pressure of the horde was immense, a literal wall of bodies pushing against their raised steel.

Above them, the defenders on the timber walls were raining absolute hell down upon the plains in a desperate bid to stem the flow of reinforcements. The sky was completely filled with the dark streaks of falling arrows and the blinding flashes of elemental magic.

The magical crossfire was terrifyingly erratic.

A massive, conjured spear of jagged blue ice plummeted from the ramparts, missing Josh by mere inches. It struck a charging Crypt Ghoul directly through the chest, pinning the screaming monster to the cobblestones before violently detonating. The explosive shower of razor-sharp ice shrapnel scoured Josh’s steel plate armour, leaving deep, bright scratches across the dark metal.

"Watch the friendly fire!" Bhel roared, ducking low as a massive sphere of roaring orange flame arced awkwardly over the parapet.

The fireball landed twenty feet in front of them, completely engulfing a tight cluster of skeletal warriors. The intense thermal shock vaporised the freezing mud instantly, sending a blinding, scalding wave of foul-smelling steam and boiling sludge washing over the two heavy fighters. Josh was forced to raise his shield to protect his visor from the blistering heat, blindly swinging his sword to keep the snapping jaws of a burning, half-melted dire wolf at bay.

The deafening roar of the magical bombardment shook the iron portcullis behind them, sending constant showers of loose mortar and thick dust raining down upon their helmets. They were fighting in a claustrophobic, apocalyptic nightmare of fire, ice, and rotting meat.

Josh called upon the deep, humming reservoir of his mana core, feeling his stamina rapidly depleting. He triggered Stormforged Adamantite, pouring raw power into his evolved body.

Blinding blue lightning erupted from his heavy plate armour, arcing wildly through the freezing morning air. The high-voltage static violently scrambled the dark magic animating the dungeon beasts, causing skeletons to collapse into golden dust the moment they stepped within his electrified reach. The sharp, acidic tang of ozone pierced entirely through the suffocating stench of the burning rot.

He swung his crackling longsword, cleaving a wide, glowing path through the centre of the press.

To his right, Bhel was fighting a desperate battle against a towering, heavily armoured skeletal knight. The dwarf deflected a crushing downward strike with his crossed axes, but the sheer physical weight of the monster forced him to his knees on the slippery cobblestones.

As Bhel struggled to push the knight's heavy halberd away, a second skeleton, wielding a rusted heavy mace, stepped blindly out of the thick smoke. It raised its weapon high, preparing for a lethal, executing blow against the kneeling dwarf's unprotected back.

Bhel saw the shadow fall over him. He braced his shoulders, gritting his teeth and fully expecting the crushing agony of shattered ribs.

The heavy iron mace crashed down, but it never touched the dwarf.

The weapon struck a shimmering, translucent silver dome that had rapidly expanded outward from Josh's breastplate. The Guardian aura completely encapsulated Bhel in a fraction of a heartbeat. The terrifying kinetic force of the mace strike was instantly and violently transferred through the magical tether, bypassing the dwarf entirely.

Josh let out a sharp, ragged grunt as the concussive shockwave hit his own body. He staggered slightly, his boots sliding an inch against the cobblestones. The impact felt like a physical hammer blow against his spine, rattling his teeth and stealing the breath from his burning lungs. He tasted fresh, hot blood pooling in his mouth, but he ground his jaw shut and held the line. He kept the protective aura flared, taking the brutal punishment so his friend could survive.

Bhel realised instantly what had happened. He did not waste the borrowed time. He surged upward from his knees, driving both axes upward to completely obliterate the skeletal knight above him, before spinning to shatter the legs of the creature that had tried to strike his back.

"I owe you a pint for that one, lad!" Bhel roared, wiping black sludge and bone dust from his eyes.

"Just keep swinging!" Josh coughed, his left arm burning with sheer agony as he deflected another charging ghoul.

The horde was pushing harder, the sheer bodily mass threatening to crush them flat against the iron gates. They were running entirely on adrenaline and stubborn refusal, their muscles screaming in protest.

Suddenly, a hysterical, straining scream cut cleanly through the chaotic noise of the battle, soaring above the explosions and the clashing steel.

Josh instantly recognised the voice echoing from the upper wooden parapet. It was Brett, and the elemental mage sounded entirely pushed beyond his physical and magical limits.

"Get down!" Josh bellowed, grabbing the thick iron collar of Bhel's armour and physically hauling the dwarf to the cobblestones, dropping to one knee and raising his heavy dark shield over both of their heads.

A second later, the dark, smoky sky directly above the gateway seemed to tear completely open.

A massive, roaring pillar of pure, liquid white plasma descended from the heavens. The thermal energy was so intense it instantly evaporated the freezing fog, turning the air into a suffocating vacuum of pure heat. The plasma washed over the densely packed ranks of the undead swarming just beyond the cratered clearing.

The destruction was absolute and terrifying to behold. Hundreds of zombies, ghouls, and skeletal hounds were completely incinerated in a blinding flash of elemental fury. The intense heat superheated the cobblestones and baked the mud solid. The roaring flames created a massive, burning chasm in the front lines, temporarily severing the immediate pressure on the two isolated fighters.

Josh slowly pushed himself up from the heated cobblestones, his heavy steel armour radiating an uncomfortable warmth. He breathed a ragged sigh of relief, silently thanking the exhausted scholar on the wall above for the miraculous intervention.

However, as the blinding white light of the plasma faded and the thick, grey smoke of the incinerated monsters began to slowly clear on the freezing wind, the relief vanished entirely.

The lesser infantry had been completely cleared away, but Brett's apocalyptic fire had merely stripped the expendable meat shield from the true threat.

Stepping silently and perfectly out of the swirling, burning smoke, completely untouched by the magical inferno, came the elite guard.

There were dozens of them. The Wights moved in a terrifying, unified phalanx. Their pristine, blackened full plate armour reflected the burning fires around them, yet they radiated an aura of freezing, spectral cold that instantly chilled the sweat on Josh's face, actively fighting back the residual heat of the plasma strike.

They stepped smoothly over the glowing white ash of their fallen comrades, their heavy greatswords lowered and ready. The piercing blue soul-fire burning within their dark visors locked entirely onto the heavily breathing human and the bleeding dwarf standing alone against the gates.

Bhel slowly pushed himself up from the ground, gripping his heavy iron axes tightly. He looked at the flawless, impenetrable wall of dark steel advancing slowly towards them, and then looked at Josh.

"Mountain's tears," Bhel cursed, his gravelly voice dropping into a harsh, entirely exhausted whisper. "That is just completely unfair."
