A tall wall towered over Ashton. While it was still obviously made from salvaged materials, at this point, it seemed so sturdy that even Ashton wasn't quite sure how he would break in. There was even protection here to stop monsters, or people, from just climbing up and over it. Well, Ashton was pretty sure he could find a way over it if he tried, but he figured it was best not to be rude and approached the actual entrance.

With his fist, he knocked on the door and smiled. A small window was pulled open a few steps above and someone peered down through it.

"Hey there! I'm Ashton, kind of a regular here. Don't think we've met!" he exclaimed with a slight wave, and the gate was soon opened up.

"Right, we've heard about you," the gatekeeper explained with a smile as Ashton was led inside. "You can probably find your own way, right?"

"Yup yup, thanks." Ashton walked inside, and it sounded like there were just a ton of people busily walking around. There were plenty in his channelling range that he could see already, but there were probably far more than he had seen when he was here last time.

The gate was also in a different location, further forward, so it seemed like this area expanded again.

There were some kids running around, wearing some basic starter equipment that they got through their own quests. In general, from the items people were carrying around, there really seemed to be a huge variety in classes, though most of them seemed a little more 'basic'. Still, this place seemed to be doing pretty good.

But as Ashton kept walking around, he soon felt something. It was a small sensation, but it was definitely directed exclusively at him. A particular kind of bloodlust, which was soon followed by a man rushing at him from behind. His one-edged, curved swords had sharp spikes protruding from it that were made to rip people apart, but he attacked with the blunt side. Because despite the bloodlust, his goal wasn't actually to kill, or even seriously hurt Ashton.

Even so, he figured he should play along, just like last time this happened. Without turning toward the man, Ashton had the wisp detach from his summoning staff and split into two. Taking control of each wisp with right middle and ring finger, Ashton quickly directed the first to push itself under the 'attacker's boot as they were running, while the other took advantage of the momentary stumble to hit him in the face.

Though, to Ashton's surprise, the man was able to dodge the attack to the face and actually recovered from the stumble pretty fast, too. Instead, he used the blunt sides of his sword to hook one of the wisps and push it away in a quite skilled and precise manner.

"Oh?" Ashton let out with a slight grin as the man finally reached him. Ashton's entire body was hidden under his cloak, as he dodged the swings of the sword with a laugh. "You've gotten pretty good. Did you reach level 10 yet?"

"Urgh, what's it look like to you?"

"That you're still weaker than me?"

"Fuck off!"

"Come on, Percy, that's nothing to be ashamed of. Everyone here's weaker than me," Ashton said, still dodging his opponent with ease. He was directing the wisp to counter-attack sometimes though, even if it was mostly in annoying or embarrassing ways.

Making him stumble, making him miss just barely, that sort of thing.

"My name... is Perry!" the constable yelled out, and Ashton made a puzzled expression.

"Huh? Wait, are you sure? I could swear it was Percy, and I'm never wrong about that kind of thing."

Perry stared at Ashton perplexed, actually stopping his attacks. "Wha- Yes, I'm sure you little– Argh!" He lowered his swords and groaned loudly. "You fucking ruined this for me, screw you."

"Heh," Ashton laughed, walking past the man. "Well, I'm glad you're as lively as ever."

With a click of his tongue, Perry hung his swords by his hips and dragged his feet along. He was slouching and had his hands in his pockets, and with the... 'clothes' that he was wearing right now, he really looked pretty unique. The 'Knight of Blood and Bone' probably had some influence there, telling him what to wear and what sort of equipment to use.

[[Knight of Blood and Bone] exclaims they are impressed by your overwhelming power]

Ashton raised a brow. "Huh? Overwhelming power, what's that supposed to mean? I just dodged out of the way a few times."

"Hm? Oh, urgh... you're talking to them, right? That freak," Perry shivered slightly. "Though I've got to say, I really thought I had you this time. It took me a moment to recognise you, especially with that weird mask. What's that about anyway?"

Thinking about whether he should just reveal it or not, Ashton stopped momentarily. Though, this might actually turn out a little fun. And so, he quickly pulled up his mask and opened his eyes with a broad smile on his face while facing the man beside him.

Perry flinched and pulled back, almost falling down as he did. "Wh-What's that? Some kind of... uhm, illusion magic?"

"Hahaha, nope. I made a deal with a demon to heal my lethal injuries in exchange for my eyes, but I can still 'see' somewhat through my summons," Ashton explained quickly, and immediately, he could see Perry turn toward something in the air, as messages appeared in front of Ashton as well.

[[Knight of Blood and Bone] is utterly amazed by your passion]

[[Knight of Blood and Bone] truly wishes you were one of theirs]

[[Knight of Blood and Bone] asks you to reconsider]

[[Daughter of the Scarlet Heart] yells at the Knight and tells them to scram]

"Over my dead fucking body, creep," Ashton laughed, continuing to walk, though he slowed slightly while pulling his mask back down. He turned to Perry, although he obviously couldn't actually look at him. "And for the record, that wasn't an invitation."

With a slow nod, Perry replied. "Right..."

The two continued walking, as Perry kept staring off into the air, whispering things that he figured nobody would be able to hear, though Ashton's hearing had gotten pretty good by now. It sounded like the Knight of Blood and Bone wanted Perry to show 'passion' like that as well. His fault for choosing a constellation with a name like that. Idiot.

But as the two of them kept walking, Ashton soon noticed another presence that was entirely focused on him, approaching quite rapidly. This time, it wasn't bloodlust, more... an intense focus. They were coming from the front, and figuring he knew who this was, Ashton stopped and smiled.

Before he even knew it, the woman rushed into his range and soon hugged Ashton.

"Oh thank god, you're okay! I was so worried when you just didn't wake up!" Clementine said, letting out a sigh of relief against Ashton's chest.

"Yeah, well, my sleep's been a little weird since all of this started, to say the least," Ashton explained. "Sorry for worrying you, but everything's okay. I figured I'd stop by here on the way."

Clementine nodded, smiling broadly. "Right! I'm glad you did! Though I was actually about to run back."

"Awesome, how're the shoes that I made you?"

"Oh, they're perfect, it literally feels like I'm flying over the ground when I wear them! I tried out the ones that I got when the quest ended, but the ones you made me are way better." Clementine looked happily down at her feet, before pulling the cloak she was wearing to the side to show the messenger bag hanging off her. "I also got this bag and what I call an 'all-weather cloak'. Totally water-proof, and I'm never too cold or too hot in this."

Ashton raised his brows, reaching out to the cloak to feel the material. "Oh? That's a pretty neat function. It doesn't look like an enchantment, so is it the material itself? It's some kind of woollen fabric, at least..."

Clementine scoffed. "Since when did you know so much about fabric?"

"Since I spent a hundred years making my own clothes," Ashton said bluntly, squatting down to get closer to the bag. "What about this, does it have anything special about it?"

"H-Hm? Oh, no, it's just really... really sturdy," Clementine replied, feeling a bit awkward after Ashton just said what he said. "Uhm... anyway, you're probably here for the shop, right?"

"Amongst other things, yup."

Perry stared at the two. "Alright, so are we just going to ignore what he just said?"

"Yup," Ashton said without even turning to him. Grumbling, Perry just followed along with the two.

"So what kind of store is it?" Ashton wondered. "Like, what form did it take?"

"Oh, it's a kiosk. It's neat, but honestly kind of creepy, too."

"What's creepy about it?"

"You know, the actual 'person' attending the kiosk," Clementine pointed out. "You'll see what I mean in a second."

It didn't take long for them to get to a place that was surrounded by quite a few people, though they were mostly there trying to catch a glance. There were a few 'official' acting people in the front, trying to discuss what was going on in there.

But the wisp-sight did give Ashton a small advantage; he didn't need to look over others to be able to see what was within the kiosk.

He did quickly understand why Clementine called the 'attendant' creepy, though. It was a Puppet, a magical automaton that looked like a wooden mannequin. They were completely tied to the shop, but they would stop functioning if it was destroyed or broken into. Though, there were some people that managed to 'repair' the magic within them. They weren't particularly strong, but they could repeat simple orders and were useful in niche scenarios. But of course, if a shop was destroyed, it would never be restocked.

Considering that the stocked items would only get stronger as time and quests went on, it was generally a huge waste to simply steal from a place like this, something that Ashton already made sure Sam would post over in the community. That was why apparently, the shops that had been found in Drakewood were being protected pretty well already.

Ashton pushed through the crowd and made his way to the front. The ones in charge of checking the store weren't people that he had interacted with a lot, but they knew him at least, and once Perry told them it was fine, Ashton got to take a look at the shop.

Though, of course, he could only make vague guesses. There were a lot of items that seemed really similar to the ones from the vending machine, but he couldn't be perfectly sure.

So, he just had to have Clementine give him a quick rundown.
