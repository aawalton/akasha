“I’d apologize, but I think trying to kill a lich the moment you see one is a pretty normal reaction,” Thunder said as she continued staring at the lich in question. At the very least she was no longer poised to try and knock Kroult’s head off of his shoulders like a professional baseball player, even going so far as to rest her still-crackling mace against her shoulder as she examined him. Narrowing her eyes, she grunted. “He’s serious? You’re actually good?”

“I like to think I am,” Kroult said, causing Thunder to blink at his somber tone as the green fires dancing within his sockets dimmed and he lowered his head. “I do not blame you either. I know I am a monster. I would try to kill me as well.”

“Did… did I hurt his feelings?” Thunder asked, looking taken aback. “Your feelings? Damn, I’m sorry. I feel sort of bad now.”

“Asher, could you please do whatever it is you came here to do and then leave?” Draken asked, righting his chair and collapsing back into it. “I am tired, and I think you’ve done enough for one day.”

“Yeah. Sorry,” Asher said again, deciding that was probably for the best. Pulling Smiles’ headless corpse out of his rift, he laid that on the ground first. “This is Smiles’ body. I’ll admit I don’t really know how you would be able to go about reconnecting his head to it, but even if you can’t, he should be able to move around again once we solve the energy issue. Sever the Thread seems to work based on my intent, so despite the fact that I’m the one that beheaded him, now that I want him to be able to heal, I think he’ll be fine.”

“I can confirm that I can feel my body again!” Smiles called out, and Asher blinked as Smiles’ fingers twitched into a shaky thumbs up. “...Though I don’t have the energy to do much with it.”

“That is what I am here for,” Kroult said, crouching down and peering at Smiles’ chest. “Asher, could I borrow a knife?”

With a shrug, Asher handed him one of his steel daggers, curious how Kroult was going to go about connecting Smiles to the gem. After a few moments of examination, Kroult leaned forward and cut a gash open where Smiles’ heart should have been before shoving the gem inside. The moment he did so, Smiles’ entire body jerked and began thrashing as dark crimson smoke began drifting out of the hole where his head should have been. Asher could sense the death energy radiating out of the corpse intermingled with the smoke, but it felt… different than it had before. Almost more chaotic and unstable. Thinking fast, he quickly threw up a Spatial Lock to trap the smoke inside with him and Kroult. He was still immune and Kroult was already dead, so they would be fine.

“Asher!” Crush warned, moving himself between the thrashing corpse and Draken.

“I’m not sure what’s happening!” he admitted. “It’s like the death energy is different than it was before, like it’s unstable! If it doesn’t stop, I’ll drag it back into my rift!”

But before he could even get the chance, just as quickly as the smoke started, it stopped. As the smoke began to dissipate and Asher dropped his Spatial Lock in confusion, Smiles laughed as his corpse rolled over and got to its feet. Holding its hands out before it like it was blind, Smiles continued laughing as it stumbled around.

“I can move again! It worked! Though something happened when you shoved whatever that is into my chest. It’s definitely got death energy, but it’s… different. Not as compatible. It burned through a lot of the enchantment channels that the King of Death put in my body.”

“What does that mean?” Asher asked, watching as the headless corpse stumbled over to the blanket and yanked it off, revealing Smiles’ grinning face.

“No idea!” Smiles said, picking up his own head and trying to carefully balance it on top of his neck. “...Does anyone have any nails or anything? Maybe some glue?”

While Smiles tried and failed to keep his head attached to his shoulders, Asher sighed, giving Draken an apologetic smile. “Sorry about the back-to-back scares… I suppose you’re going to ask me to take Smiles off your hands now, huh?” Even as he said it, Asher started trying to figure out how he was going to manage to convince Samantha that was okay. She’d made it very clear she didn’t want him bringing Smiles back with them, but he felt largely responsible for the strange undead.

Thankfully, he didn’t have to worry for very long.

“No… No, that’s no longer an option,” Draken admitted, causing everyone other than Smiles to look at him in confusion. “When that gem integrated with the corpse and started running wild, I felt a strange emptiness from it unlike anything I’ve ever felt before. Before I even knew what I was doing I reached out with my Death Aura and took control of it, almost like one would an artifact. As much as I hate to say it… I think I just inadvertently bound the gem to me. And therefore Smiles with it.”

Crush’s jaw fell open as he stared at Draken, and Thunder sputtered before pointing at Smiles with her electrified mace.

“Bound to you?! What does that mean?”

“If he moves too far away from me the gem will go wild again as it briefly did just now. Which will either reduce Smiles into a thrashing mess on the ground as he just was, or potentially turn him into a mindless killing machine as the death energy overpowers his own thoughts. Like it or not, he is stuck with us for the foreseeable future.”

“I won’t let you down, boss!” Smiles grinned, finally giving up on trying to keep his head balanced upon his shoulders as he picked it up and saluted with it across his chest.

Shaking his head as if he regretted every action in his life that led him to this point, Draken gave Asher a world-weary look. It didn’t take a genius to tell what the gang leader wanted, and Asher cleared his throat, offering him one last apologetic smile.

“I suppose you’d probably like some time to get acquainted with your newest enforcer. Kroult and I will just get out of your hair. We’ll stop by and see how things are going sooner or later!”

Not giving Draken a chance to order Thunder to zap them both silly, Asher grabbed Kroult and triggered Recall, warping them halfway across the kingdom back to the royal palace. There was no sign of Samantha in their bedroom, and Kroult sighed as he looked up at the ceiling in thought.

“I feel a little bad about shoving Smiles off onto that man in that way,” the lich said, his flaming eyes flickering uncertainly. “Do you think he will be alright?”

“Who, Smiles? Or Draken?” Asher snorted, shaking his head. “They’ll both be fine. I’m pretty certain I could have tossed Smiles into a field of pigs and he would have been just as ecstatic about his new friends. And Draken will realize soon enough just how useful Smiles is. Even if he’s not as fast as he once was, he’s still an incredibly skilled swordsman, and is practically unkillable. While Smiles might not be able to leave the manor, Draken always keeps one of his enforcers close. If that becomes Smiles’ permanent job, that frees up a lot of impressive manpower Draken can use elsewhere in the city. It’s not easy running the largest gang in all of Whikoga.”

“…That man runs a gang?”

Deciding that was a discussion for another day, Asher told Kroult to go get settled in his own bedroom. Despite all they’d already been through together fighting for their lives in another realm of existence, this was still technically their first official day with the lich, and he was sure Kroult would need some time alone to get used to simply being a person again after all these centuries. With Kroult out of the way, Rulfar conducting research on their next mission, and Moxy beating the hell out of her future husband, Asher’s heart sank as he realized he had the perfect opportunity to do something he’d been putting off for as long as possible.

It was time to have the immortality discussion with Samantha.

Asher took a deep breath, and then another as he steeled his nerves, clenching his fists together as he tried to convince himself it was finally time. The problem with immortality was that time was already beginning to feel like such a minor concern to him. What did putting off this discussion for a few hours, days, or even weeks really matter in the long run? It wasn’t as if there was much of a difference in someone being twenty-four years and thirty weeks old versus twenty-four years and thirty-one weeks old. Honestly, there really wasn’t any sort of rush. Except…

That was a dangerous line of thinking.

Asher sighed as he took a seat on their couch, putting his face in his hands as his intelligence warred with his emotions. While a few weeks here and there wouldn’t really matter, it wouldn’t just be a few weeks. Asher was absolutely terrified of hearing Samantha’s answer, and he knew that if he didn’t force himself to just get this over with he would continue delaying the conversation by a few weeks again and again. Eventually, those weeks would become months and then those months would turn into years, until Samantha realized one way or another that she was growing older at a regular rate while Asher remained frozen in time at twenty-three.

He wasn’t sure exactly how long he sat there on the couch like that, arguing with himself about what had to be done, before the decision was taken from his hands for him. The door to their bedroom swung open, and Samantha walked in, smiling warmly at him as she spotted him sitting there.

“Welcome back!” she said, leaning her bow and quiver against the wall as she walked over. Giving him a quick kiss on the cheek, she sat beside him and rested her head on his shoulder. “Did it all work out with Smiles?”

“I thought you didn’t like Smiles,” Asher said numbly, his heart hammering away inside his chest harder than during any of his past battles. If Samantha accepted his offer of immortality, his life would be perfect. If she turned him down…

Asher didn’t even let himself imagine the alternative.

“Just because I’m not Smiles’ biggest fan doesn’t mean I want to see him killed,” Samantha said, sitting up straight and giving him a concerned look as she took his hand. “Asher, are you alright? You look pale.”

“I… I’ve been better,” Asher admitted, forcing himself to smile as he looked at the greatest thing that had ever happened to him. Swallowing hard, he gripped her hand in his own, careful not to squeeze too hard with his Belt of Strength still on him.

“Wanna talk about it?”

“Yes… No… I mean, I have to talk about it. But I don’t want to. I’m… I’m terrified of how you’ll react. Of what it will mean for… for us.”

“Asher, we’ve had this conversation before,” Samantha said softly, giving him a small smile of encouragement. “So long as you haven’t gone around killing innocent people, there’s nothing you could do that you need to worry about. Obviously this is weighing on you, you should just come out and say it. Whatever it is, we’ll deal with it together just like we have everything else. We’re going up against a group of the deadliest people to have ever set foot on our continent. I’m sure whatever this is isn’t nearly so dire.”

“It’s a different kind of worry,” Asher muttered, his mind still warring with the fear in his heart before he looked up and saw the love in Samantha’s eyes. The woman sitting across from him, who he’d shared everything with since almost his first day on this world and who he hoped to do so for who knew how many millennia, waited patiently for him to get his act together. It was obvious that she was worried about him and wanted to help him get whatever was troubling him off his chest, and Asher felt a stab of warmth in his gut as he realized it was now or never. Taking one last shuddering breath, he let the words tumble from his mouth, not even letting himself think as he forced the big question out there.

“Do you want to live forever with me?”
