Interlude III - Retaliation

Part II - Ichika

Ichika detected the first hint that something was off when she entered the Gardening Club’s rooftop garden. Normally, everyone welcomed her. She was an essential part of their group and had been since her first year at the academy. Ever since her joining, the club had found a magnitude of success, the likes of which had never before been recorded. Plants that stood no chance at surviving their climate, somehow flourished under her care. In fact, it was often quietly joked that Ichika had a bit of druid in her bloodline. Very close to the truth, though she’d never admit it publicly.

So, when nobody even acknowledged her stepping onto the academy rooftop garden, it was odd to say the least.

“What’s that?” she asked the club head, a boy named Jeremy. He winced at her question. Rather than answer verbally, he set the clay flowerpot down on the stone table and stepped aside. He never even looked in her direction as he hurried over to a different aisle of plants.

Scowling, Ichika examined the abandoned pot. It had soil in it, but her sixth sense recoiled at her examination. It was equivalent to stuffing her nose inside a chamber pot. Visibly gagging, Ichika jerked her head away and stumbled backward.

She whirled around, preparing to lash out at Jeremy for not bothering to warn her. Only then did she realize that every single club member stood with their back to her. Not a word was spoken.

“What are you doing?” she asked, incensed. “Hey! Look at me!”

Nothing.

Both baffled and peeved by this odd behavior, Ichika pulled away from examining her peers and turned her attention to the garden. Only then did she notice the flowerbed that normally flourished at the center of the club’s main garden. The normally brilliantly vibrant flower petals were all withered shades of gray.

Ichika scrambled up to one of the flowers and picked it, its dry stem snapping like a twig. Her senses once again recoiled in disgust, but she forced her body to stay resolute.

“What happened here?” she asked.

Nobody answered her. Their backs remained turned to her.

Her eye twitched. “What are you all doing? You’re acting like I did this!”

She wanted to lash out at them, strike them with a spell. She kept a tight lid on the familiar simmering anger that boiled in her chest. More than simply a competent mage, Ichika was a politician. She knew how to control her emotions. That was a type of magic far more difficult than any simple fireball.

Instead of targeting her peers, Ichika decided to further investigate. The corrupted plants disgusted her on a visceral level and she despised their very existence. Touching them left a gritty residue on her fingertips. Thankfully, it took no more than a few minutes until she found a message carved into a blank patch of soil.

-Ichika-

There exists within your midst a venomous snake.

Beyond waify wildflowers, all will whither.

Avert this course, turn your back and forsake.

T’is only the first act of reprisal, do not dither.

Ichika’s mouth went dry. She read the words once, twice, a dozen times over. There was no mistaking this for anything else. She’d been cursed by a witch. A powerful witch too, if the current state of the garden was anything to go off of. Obviously, her club associates assumed the message to be for them, about her, but Ichika knew far better.

A witch cursing her, speaking of reprisal, so soon after she just so happened to help Emilia target the witch boy’s girlfriend? That was no coincidence.

This wasn’t about her, it was a message to her.

Her hands shook, the anger leaking out. This was a bad situation to be in. So far, Emilia had managed to somehow escape the notice and reprimand of the academy faculty. Ichika honestly didn’t know how her friend pulled that much off. However, if the one she was attacking had the ability to retaliate with this much force… then Ichika wasn’t certain she wished to be involved. The lazy Kajima girl was meant to be extraordinarily incompetent and barely passed the threshold into sapience. Emilia had assured her that their target wasn’t influential and would simply fold under pressure. According to Emilia, even the girl’s twin sister hated her and went out of her way to avoid the girl. She was meant to be equal parts benign and friendless. Ichika was starting to believe that her friend’s info was either outdated or flat out incorrect.

“Turn your back and forsake,” Ichika muttered the written words. Emilia had been with her for years now. She obviously liked her friend, but the Tainted girl had lost nearly everything these last few months. Her family’s trade empire was all but utterly collapsed, and, with it, her family’s wealth and influence. The original reason Ichika had for seeking her out had disappeared. How much pressure needed to be applied before Ichika let the rope connecting them snap? How far did she have to get dragged down?

The problem was, there was no end in sight for Emilia’s troubles. If she couldn’t even handle a friendless girl with no friends… then that wasn’t the same Emilia that Ichika had befriended three years ago.

Ichika stood and examined the garden once again. Even to her normal eyes, it was a sorry state. It shared more colors in common with a patch of stone than a field of flowers.

All of her work this semester, now not even worth the risk of composting.

“It’s time to prune the diseased leaf before it infects the full plant.”
