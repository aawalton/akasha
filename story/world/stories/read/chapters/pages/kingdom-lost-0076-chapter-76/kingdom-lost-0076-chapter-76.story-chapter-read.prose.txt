The alarm bell rang out with vigor. The sound of yelling voices from within the compound grew with each moment. Smoke poured from the burning watchtower, thick and black, carrying the sharp stink of tar and scorched wood.

It did not take long for the raiders to mount the wall further down from the flaming guard tower. Several archers appeared through tiny doors in the upper part of the wall. They nocked arrows and drew their bows. Riley’s own archers answered first. A volley of shafts hissed through the air. The raiders ducked back inside as arrows slammed into the wood.

Moments later the raiders returned fire. Arrows whistled out from inside the compound beyond the wall. Riley screamed a warning and soldiers scrambled for cover behind the trees, while others lifted their large metal shields to absorb the assault. The impacts rang against steel and thudded into wood.

Riley crouched low behind a thick oak, heart hammering. She gripped her hatchet tighter and scanned the wall through the drifting smoke. Soldiers in the testudo formation had already begun its slow advance toward the gate, shields locked overhead like the shell of a great turtle. Thorne moved among the infantry, shouting orders in a steady voice that cut through the raging chaos.

“Hold the line!” Riley called. “Archers, keep them pinned!”

Another flight of raider arrows streaked overhead. Behind her, the bound raider prisoners lay on the ground, gagged and wide-eyed, watching their former home come under assault.

The fire in the tower crackled louder. A section of the roof collapsed inward with a crash, sending fresh sparks swirling into the sky. Shouts inside the compound turned frantic. Boots pounded along the wall walks. Riley drew a slow breath, tasting smoke and almost gagging. The real fight was only beginning and the smell of the tar fires were only going to get worse.

The next volley of arrows launched from her lines as the ram team and their escort surged forward under the cover of the shields. Once the raiders returned fire, the enemy archers took cover behind the wall. Riley yelled, "Turtle forward!"

The group in the testudo formation advanced in tight lockstep. Their modified shields were larger than the standard ones and did a fantastic job of covering the soldiers in front, above, and on either side almost completely. The formation resembled a moving fortress of metal, each man braced against the next so that no gap appeared. Arrows clattered and thudded against the shields without finding flesh. The soldiers inside grunted with effort but kept their pace lockstep toward the main gate.

The raiders' guard tower burned more fully now. Flames licked high into the sky and thick smoke billowed outward. The walled raider archers simply moved a little further down the wall, keeping their focus on the threat in front of them. They leaned out from new positions and loosed arrows that sparked off the testudo's shell or buried themselves harmlessly in the overlapping shields.

Riley watched from her position behind the tree line, her grip tight on the haft of her hatchet. She could hear the heavy breathing of the men inside the formation and the occasional curse when an arrow found a joint in the armor. Thorne stood nearby, his sword drawn, eyes fixed on the advancing turtle. The first real clash was only moments away.

"Steady!" she called out. "Hold your shields true!"

The testudo pressed on, inching closer to the wooden gate that now stood as the final barrier between her force and the raiders inside.

The sounds from inside the compound grew frantic. Raiders scrambled and yelled for buckets of water to deal with the evolving situation. The splash of water against burning wood and stone echoed over the wall, but the flames only hissed and spat without dying down.

Riley’s group shot another volley of arrows as the turtle reached the gate. Shafts whistled through the air forcing the raider archers to duck back once more.

The turtle adjusted formation slightly. The forward shields shifted and the soldiers moved within inches of the gate itself. Then one soldier started to work as he poured the tar mixture onto the wood of the gate. The group side-stepped so he could cover the gate from one side to the other, starting at the ground to about chest high. The tar oozed slightly but stuck to the wood, glistening in the sunlight as the soldiers in the turtle formation slid the shields back completely in front and backed away, returning to the waiting battering ram.

Riley yelled, “Hold!” The archers waited for the signal, bows drawn and arrows burning.

The gate now stood dark and slick with the thick mixture. Riley kept her eyes fixed on the gate, her breath steady as she watched the ram team ready themselves. The moment to strike had nearly arrived. Thorne stood beside her, his shield raised and his expression grim with focus. The soldiers around her shifted their weight, eager yet disciplined, waiting for her command to unleash the next phase of the assault.

The raiders were about to launch arrows when Riley yelled, “FIRE!”

A handful of flaming arrows screamed through the air and landed in different parts of the shellacked gate. The tar mixture ignited with a sudden whoosh like a fireball. Flames roared across the wooden surface in a bright sheet of orange and yellow.

Black smoke started to billowed up from the burning gate as the awful rotten-egg smell filled the air. The stench rolled across the open ground and stung the eyes of everyone nearby.

The raiders on the wall started to cough and yell obscenities in response to the smoke and smell, their voices cracked with anger and surprise.

More yelling for water came from inside the compound to deal with the gate on fire. Boots pounded along the wall walks as men rushed to respond.

Moments later the archers disappeared off the wall as more yelling started. One distant voice called out that the fire would not go out.

Again, more yelling rose as the sound of water being splashed echoed from within, as if bucket after bucket was being thrown on the burning gate. The water hissed and steamed against the flames, but the fire only seemed to cling tighter to the tar-coated wood. Thick black clouds continued to pour upward, drifting over the wall and into the compound.

Riley stood motionless behind the tree line, her eyes narrowed against the acrid smoke. She watched the gate burn with grim satisfaction. The ram team waited just behind the main line, the heavy log resting on the ground. The soldiers around her remained silent and ready, shields raised and weapons gripped tight. The fire had done its work. Now the gate would weaken, and the real breach would begin.

Riley gave the order to move in. The infantry soldiers with very large shields provided cover to the soldiers lifting up the tree trunk they had cut down. The archers trained their arrows on anything that might peek over the wall, but it sounded like the raiders were mostly invested in putting the fire out on the gate. Shouts and the constant splash of water echoed from inside the compound.

“NOW!” Riley screamed.

The battering ram was thrust into the gate. The end of the ram caught on fire as the soldiers backed up for another ram. The gate was weakened but still stood. Wood groaned under the impact and small cracks appeared along the tar-blackened surface.

“THEY ARE BREACHING THE GATE!” a voice yelled from within the raiders’ compound.

A second ram struck the gate. This time the gate showed more signs of weakening. The wood splintered loudly and the entire structure shuddered. Flames licked higher along the edges where the tar still burned.

A raider archer shot an arrow and caught one of the soldiers on the ram in the neck. He went down with a scream, clutching at the shaft as blood spilled between his fingers. The ram tilted dangerously for a moment before the other bearers steadied it. Two men rushed forward to drag the wounded soldier back behind the shield wall while the rest of the team reset their grip.

Riley’s jaw tightened. She kept her voice steady as she called out, “Keep the pressure on! Archers, suppress that wall!”

The ram team adjusted their hold on the heavy trunk, their faces grim as smoke continued to pour from the gate and the burning tower. Inside the compound the raiders’ shouts grew more desperate. The next strike would decide whether the gate held or gave way completely. Thorne moved closer to the line, sword ready, his eyes fixed on the weakening barrier. The soldiers around Riley waited in tense silence, shields raised and spears leveled, ready for the moment the way opened.

Riley's archers responded at once. Four arrows flew true and buried themselves in the lone raider archer. The man fell to the ground on the outside of the compound with a thud and his body lay still against the base of the wall.

BOOM!

The ram smashed into the now weakening gate. The soldiers with the battering ram cried out in rhythm. “Heave! Ho!”

Thorne stood in his full suit of armor, chomping at the bit and ready to barrel through the gate once it had opened. His sword rested against his shoulder as he shifted his weight from foot to foot.

Four soldiers dragged the wounded man back behind Riley. She looked down at him and within moments the soldier was dead. His eyes stared blankly at the sky and his chest no longer rose.

Riley turned and shouted, “AGAIN!”

“Heave! Ho!” The ram crashed into the gate and this time it moved even more. The flames had moved up above the black line of tar and started to char the uncovered wood. Sparks showered down onto the soldiers below.

Riley shouted again, “AGAIN!”

The soldiers backed up an extra step. In unison they roared and ran into the flaming gate. It exploded open with a loud crack of splintering wood. The burning panels flew inward and crashed to the ground inside the compound. Flames and smoke poured through the new opening as the way into the raider stronghold finally lay clear.

Riley raised her hatchet high. “Forward!” she called. “Take the compound!”

Thorne roared and barreled through the shattered gateway with his sword raised. The rest of the infantry poured in after him, shields high and weapons hungry for blood.
