Amy collapsed into the plush sofa of her new dormitory: a luxurious apartment with big ass windows, a big ass bed, a big ass sofa, a big ass kitchen, and a big ass bathroom, all looking extremely expensive.

The amount of things that had happened today had left her completely exhausted. She could not believe that she was lying here right now; back when she touched the Veritas Mirror and nothing happened, she genuinely thought she was done for.

"We really did manage to clutch that, huh… I can't believe it," Amy muttered. "I also can't believe that the Goddess actually set up a background for me."

The Goddess had indeed sent the book here with a bunch of papers and a citizenship certificate, and she had also created a record of Amy's existence in a village somewhere far away from here.

"That woman might be petty, but she sure isn't a slacker."

[Who do you think the Goddess is? She's quite a generous deity, you know.]

Amy snorted, not even deigning to reply to the very stupid comment and instead shifting the conversation to the topic at hand.

"But really, it was way too close."

Amy brought her palm close to her face and then clenched it. She felt some sort of tension, or rather power, passing through her.

Every single human out there has probably dreamed at least once of getting some kind of magic powers. To think she would actually get them…

"This is insane… this whole day is insane," she whispered.

[If you want to have a mental breakdown over your inability to return to your home world, right now is the best moment.]

Amy let out a dry laugh. "I should be freaking out more, right? But I think I'm too tired for that."

She exhaled slowly, staring out the window at the Academy grounds. It was only now that she realized that if she had to save this world, she would spend years of her life here.

"I wanna go home," she admitted, voice barely above a whisper.

The book tilted in the air as if considering her words. [Tell me, Amy, what was your life like on Earth?]

Amy scoffed. "Why do you want to know?"

[Because I only know about Earthlings through what I've accessed from the internet. And to be honest, I find it hard to believe that all of you are much different from the people of this world.]

She snorted at that. "Well, you would be right. Life is very boring compared to whatever videos you have seen."

[Really…?]

Amy noticed the book's voice sounding slightly robotic as it asked that question, which made one of her eyebrows rise.

"Yeah, really," she continued, shrugging off whatever she had noticed. "Although, maybe I'm being unfair since mine was pretty shit. I had basically lost all my friends, and my mother was probably praying for me to turn eighteen as soon as possible so she could get away from me."

[Well now I'm curious. Why do you want to go back to such a world then?]

Amy opened her mouth to respond, but then closed it. She thought for a second in silence, then shook her head.

"Weren't we going to look at the manga chapter and the readers' reactions that just got released?" she asked, changing the topic. "You said you could show it to me, didn't you?"

The book didn't respond for a second, making Amy slightly worried, something which proved unfounded as, without any sort of warning, it began shining in a golden light.

Amy covered her eyes with her hand, and once she lowered it, she saw the book, which was supposed to be just red with a yellow triangle in the center, had transformed into a manga tome.

"Damn," Amy, who was unaccustomed to magic, couldn't help but exclaim at this.

She leaned forward, took the book into her hands, and observed the cover. There she saw the protagonist group consisting of the edgy protagonist, Crow, with black hair and eyes; the timid but cold-looking noble lady, Lain, with white hair and pale eyes; the funny, very tall, and very muscled guy of the group, Ash, with cropped, tinted yellow hair and a smirk; and finally Lyra, a girl with an animated personality, auburn curled hair and eyes.

They were all standing in front of the Academy's gate while looking at the viewer. Behind them, nine other students whom Amy recognized some from Class S second year- were walking inside; she could see both Iris and Zayd there.

But that wasn't what mattered; the truly important part was in the corner of the cover, leaning against one of the Academy walls. Amy saw herself there, covered in shadows.

"Christ, I'm actually there." She couldn't help but leak a bit of excitement as she said this. "Though they've tucked me away in the corner…"

[Would you prefer to be front and center?]

"No…but like, c'mon, did I really have to be in the corner? And under the shadows too. It makes me seem a bit evil…" She frowned. "I look like I'm plotting something devious."

[That tracks.]

Amy rolled her eyes before continuing to inspect her portrait. "Still, they really drew me well, huh... My golden hair and eyes feel like they shine. Also, my figure is really something… they could have made my tits bigger, though…"

[Are other earthling women usually as vulgar as you?]

"Worse."

Without wasting any more time, Amy flipped open the manga to the first page, then immediately skipped it since it was about Crow visiting the orphanage where he grew up.

"Boring~" she muttered as she continued flipping pages, and only stopped when Crow finally entered the teahouse where they met.

In the panel, Crow sat, thinking deeply, as shown by a thought box.

"If this key truly is what I think it is, I might finally get a lead on my father—"

Behind him, Amy stood up from her chair and walked towards him, something that he immediately noted.

After being called, he finally turned, and what he saw was something which Amy could only describe as the most beautiful being to walk the universe.

"Damn, maybe this manga wasn't as bad as I thought..."

[I have no idea what the artist was thinking. That clearly is not you.]

"Whatever, dude."

The following panels recounted their conversation exactly as Amy had lived it, with Crow thinking something along the lines of: this might be a trap.

And I thought I was being smooth.

The manga continued with Crow deliberating whether to help her or not, and finally deciding to after a panel of him as a kid, accompanied by a figure cloaked in shadows.

"...I suppose I know what it's like to be lost," his thoughts read.

"That flashback felt kind of forced, but okay…"

[It was fine, you are just hating.]

As they exited the teahouse and continued the conversation Amy had just lived through a few hours earlier, Crow's thoughts were revealed. The entire time, he was pondering whether she was a spy or a member of the Tribe of Onyx and if she needed to be killed, something that Amy found utterly insane.

[Completely understandable reaction, if you ask me.]

"Well, no one asked you."

The scene continued with Crow asking Amy if she was waiting for him and her responding with nothing but a smile, something which Amy just realized was drawn in such a way as to show the two locking eyes and being very close, almost as if this was a scene from a…shoujo…?

What!?

Amy flushed. "I wasn't flirting; the fuck is up with this vibe!?"

[The artist certainly seems to be setting up something between you two.]

"Ew, fuck no. I refuse to be part of some cliché romance subplot."

[A pity that what you want doesn't really matter, isn't it?]

"..." Amy pursed her lips and decided to keep reading instead of listening to the book's provocations.

The manga continued with Crow looking at the book next to Amy and thinking it wasn't an ordinary artifact, but one very similar to Bloodedge in the way it seemed sentient and communicated.

He debated whether to ask her or not, and in the end he did, because he was tired of thinking and thought the best way was to be direct, since the other party wasn't exactly discreet about their attempt to get close to him.

The conversation about the book was rendered in tense panels: close-ups of Crow's narrowing eyes, Amy's barely concealed surprise, the book hovering ominously between them. A text bubble captured his words:

"That book of yours, it communicates with you; I can feel faint, intermittent signals from it, and it reminds me of something similar that happened to me not too long ago."

Manga Amy hesitated before giving a positive answer and then not elaborating further.

Below these panels was Crow's internal reaction:

She admitted it so easily. Either she trusts me for whatever reason, or she wants me to know. But why?

"Huh, so he was overthinking everything I said," Amy mused. "That's actually hilarious."

The conversation died down as the two of them reached the academy. Throughout their last goodbye and Amy's revelation of the key detail, Crow had been on edge.

How...? Nobody other than Ash, Lyra, and Lain should know that, he thought.

"That was genuinely scary," Amy muttered, studying the panel. "Like I almost shit my pants."

[What did you expect? For all he knows, that one thing might be connected to his missing father.]

His thought boxes revealed that he was even thinking of reporting her to Professor Vanheim, the main teacher of Class S and one of the protagonist party's confidants, something that made Amy frown deeply.

[We were joking before, but at this rate, you might seriously become a villain.]

"Damn, you might actually be right…"

Once Amy and Crow's goodbye ended, Crow finally moved before realizing his companions—Lain, Lyra, and Ash—had been staring at him for what looked like a while.

Upon seeing them, Crow immediately forced the tension in his shoulders to go away and put on his characteristic neutral expression, which, honestly, was such a bad idea because a misunderstanding rapidly formed.

"Blonde, pretty face… Isn't that your type?" said Ash, obviously trying to tease him.

"I don't have a type."

Lyra pouted, grabbed Crow's arm, and asked, slightly panicked, who Amy was, while Lain looked completely devoid of any reaction, making one guess what was going through her mind.

The final page of the manga ended with Crow trying to ignore his friends' questions as they entered the academy.

Amy stared at the final page, taking it all in.

"I fucking hate harems…" she finally commented.

[You made quite the impression. That was the goal, wasn't it? Aren't you happy?]

"...fuck making an impression. If I have to become Crow's ninth girlfriend to achieve my goals, I would rather die..."

[The readers would probably enjoy that, though.]

"I'm sure they would," Amy muttered. "Speaking of which, let me see their comments; I want to know their thoughts."

[I was hoping you would ask that.]

The book suddenly started shining. Amy once again protected her eyes from the light until it ended; this time, instead of a tome, she was met with the book's normal appearance, but within its pages a website was printed.

Without any sort of preamble, Amy got to reading.
