For the first time in a long time, a blue screen popped up in Nathan's vision without him requesting it, and it was the content of the screen that had him in shock. His brain tried to put the pieces together, but no matter how hard he tried, he couldn't put into place what exactly the system was saying, which made things all the more confusing.

He tried not to think about it too much, but his entire body was thrumming with energy, some sort of weird energy that kept him from being able to focus on the screen. It seemed like something had been brewing in his belly for a long time and was now looking for a way to explode. A way to get out. But for now, Nathan forced his mind to concentrate on the words on the system screen in front of him.

Nathan raked through his brain trying to figure out which one was the best option for him. The rankings seemed to be straightforward to him, with the [Gale] Technique ranked as (E) while the others were of a lower rank.

On the surface, going for the E-ranked skill Gale would seem like the most obvious option in normal circumstances, but the situation the Berserker was in was anything but normal. For one, he was locked in Sparrow's hell hole of a dungeon, suspended by runed chains. In summary, [Gale] wasn't going to help him in this scenario unless he was planning on sending bursts of air at Sparrow when he came to execute him later on.

With that in mind, it made sense for him to scrap getting the [Gale] technique. The Dash Technique seemed nice, but speed wouldn't get him out of the chains that kept him suspended in the cell. Although there might be a situation where the technique would help him escape Sparrow's stronghold after being uncuffed for execution, a part of him reasoned that Sparrow would probably have a way to deal with such a technique. Off the top of his head, he could see the mages who had played a crucial role in having him captured being a problem.

Which meant that the Dash Technique wasn't the one to be picked. The realization caused him to grunt, disappointment seeping through his features as the dream of dominating opponents with his strength and enhanced speed faded away. [Dash] and [Gale] aside, there was still a technique that he had yet to consider, [Dissipate].

Of all three, it seemed like the technique that would get him out of his current predicament.

If Nathan was reading this right, then this technique would be a way for him to get out of the runed chains holding him captive in the dank cell. With that in mind, he relaxed his mind a little. Even if he was wrong and Dissipate couldn't free him from the chains, he might as well take a moment to savor the feeling of hope.

"Hope. Such a fickle emotion," Nathan whispered to himself.

"I choose [Dissipate]."

An alien warmth circulated his system as he confirmed his technique - [Dissipate]. The feeling started from the tips of his fingers, toes, and the top of his head. The feeling converged into a single point in his stomach before exploding throughout his entire body, causing Nathan to grunt in pain.

"Frickin' system," he cursed.
