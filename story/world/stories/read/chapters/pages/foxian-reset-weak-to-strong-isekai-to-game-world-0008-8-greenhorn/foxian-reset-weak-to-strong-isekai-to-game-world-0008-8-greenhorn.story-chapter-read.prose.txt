After farming some beetles for a certain item needed in the future, and selling off some junk loot, Kae was ready to relax a bit. It was strange that she didn’t need to eat, sleep, rest, or do anything she would if she were human outside this game.

“This is nice, not having to worry about those things.”

She was never a big eater, occasionally getting food delivered to her door. So, not having to worry about that was another stress gone. And as a Foxian, she had a fluffy tail she could hold onto as she walked about the city. With her comb in hand, she brushed her soft tail tenderly.

“Oh?”

DEX +1

Kae Loredom

DEX: 14

It took a while, but Kae’s DEX stat went up. She had to keep that tail fluffy if she wanted it to keep rising, though. Grandfathered stats took a long time, but were worth it when they actually rose since it was a permanent increase. Thinking more about it, her original grandfathered stats had been off the charts.

“Hm, if I want to beat the Goddess of this game, I’ll have to make sure to get stronger than I was before.”

She thought, closing her eyes and imagining a stronger version of herself. The idea was exciting, the many ways she could reach the top. With a new burst of motivation, the veteran stepped into the armor shop, Chain Days, and talked to the NPC Caroline.

“Welcome to Chain Days!”

Caroline had bright purple hair and elf ears. Another NPC the community went crazy about. Her quest line was actually noteworthy, and Kae thought about accepting it for the minimal rewards she could obtain.

“Wait, maybe Layla would want to do the quest with me if she hasn’t done it yet? That could be something we could do together instead.”

Quests were more fun with friends, a simple fact she was slowly remembering. So, for the time being, Kae sold off the rest of her garbage loot she’d gotten from the catacombs instead. She had every intention to go back to the beetles and farm them sometime, but first things first, working on her skill tree.

15,208,800G.

“Hm, I am curious though...”

She wasn’t hungry, but she could still taste the food of this world. So, she got herself some spaghetti from “The Gathering Meal” from across the street and sat at a table.

“This always looked delicious while sitting at my PC. Time to give it a try.”

The zesty sauce and beef meatballs melted in her mouth. Garlic touched her nose as the explosion of tomato sauce lit up her world. Her tail bounced around.

“Wow, this is probably the best food I’ve ever had!”

It took everything in her body to stop her tail from whacking the back of her chair. But nothing could beat how incredible this spaghetti was.

“Okay, so for the time being, I need to focus on building a set that is ranged and powerful. As a Foxian, I’ll have low stats across the board...”

She murmured as her mind activated. The years of experience reshaped her mind. This was Kae’s safe place in the world of builds and play styles.

45 Skill Points.

Original skills…

[Tail Spin Lv.1]

[Fireball Lv.3]

[Mini Fox Lv.2]

After a bit of messing around, Kae came up with a solid build for the time being. Since she was over level 12, she upgraded [Fireball] to Lv. 9 to unlock [Blazeball Lv. 1]. She upgraded [Mini Fox] to Lv. 4 and [Circle Tail Bash] to Lv. 4 to unlock [Fox Litter Lv. 1], and she pushed [Tail Spin] to Lv. 9.

With these newly upgraded skills, the MP cost went up, but the damage did too. But that was just the price of getting stronger.

“Hmm, I’ll need to buy [Strawberry Juice] too.”

A decent MP-healing item. With the boost in levels comes the cost in skills. If she was going to start her grind again, she needed enough juice to go for hours at a time.

She stood up from her seat. The spaghetti tasted great, but she wasn’t full; she was just satisfied. It made her happy that she wouldn’t lose or gain weight anymore, though. Only good things had come since the Goddess locked her into this life-or-death game.

“Are we going to Yale Forest to get [Yale Roots]?”

A message box on the side caught her attention. There looked to be a few people gathered around a lower-level player. The character in question was only level 9, a novice acolyte priest.

“Hm?”

Curious, the fox watched from afar as they chatted.

Kae had always liked the acolyte outfit. With the white-and-purple dress and golden cross on the chest, it was a nice, easy outfit on the eyes. The acolyte hovered around the two stronger players, occasionally scratching her white hair bashfully.

The other two players were in their 50s and looked... irritated. One was an elf Knight and the other a Trade Baron.

“Here, I’ll trade you the roots for the quest, Silvia.”

The level 50 merchant promised as he approached the little bright-eyed acolyte. He was a Trade Baron, the second ascension of the merchant class. With dark hair, lazy hazel eyes, and a cunning smirk, the carefree baron stretched.

He pulled his black coat over his red shirt and put both hands on his hips. Kae didn’t like his demeanor at all. Something shady was going on, for sure.

“I don’t like this setup. I know what he’s doing.”

It all clicked like a light coming on in a dark room. Kae jumped up, but before she could approach and stop him, he used [Money Bash] on the acolyte and killed the unsuspecting greenhorn.

“Wow, that was so mean, Dareth...”

The green-haired elf Knight chided as she crossed her arms. With a sigh, the knight rolled her eyes and walked away, leaving the scene altogether.

The acolyte disappeared, likely to respawn and get away from what had just happened. Kae approached the lone Baron, fist balled.

“That was uncalled for.”

Chin up, glaring at the merchant who was taller than her. Even so, the fox didn’t back down as she called him out.

There was a very mean trick players could do in Keostar Online. The ask-to-trade menu and PVP menu were relatively the same size. Asking to trade and asking to duel had the same “yes” icon too.

“You asked to trade, then canceled and brought up a dual menu. Any novice who doesn’t know that trick will accept yes.”

Kae dressed his dirty trick down. Both hands on her hips, she berated the bully.

“What of it? She was super annoying and wouldn’t stop asking questions that you can just look up online!

The trade baron snapped.

Kae sighed in frustration.

“It’s when people do things like this that newer players grow bitter of the game and leave. Why not just help her out or give her a link to whatever she needed? We all want to have fun, but bullying isn’t fun.”

“I’ll remember your name, Dareth Darkmere. In my new world, others will help each other because this game won’t be easy!”

With that odd declaration, Kae rushed off, hoping she wasn’t too late to the center of Restoria, the respawn point.

“What a freak!”

Dareth yelled after her, hands behind the back of his head.

People like him wouldn’t have a part in Kae’s new world once she killed Rexia. It only added to her drive to get stronger and change things. The foxian’s tail fell into her arms. With that magic comb, she brushed her fluffy tail all the faster as she jogged to the Restoria respawn point.

Kae ran up to the acolyte, who was standing idle. Her character held a wooden rod and wore the novice eggshell hat, too. It was clear she had just become an acolyte.

[“Hello Silvia, I saw what happened to you and wanted to help you out.”]

Kae sent in a whisper.

Silvia didn’t reply, and Kae felt she was AFK. So, to not waste the chance, Kae bounced in place, simulating walking so she could get that grand VIT or AGI eventually.

Her fluffy tail kept her busy, too. Eventually, she wanted to get an item for her STR, INT, AGI, and LUK as well, but she needed to do a bit more farming for those.

[“Forget this freaking game…”]

“Oh, you’re back.”

Kae perked up as she approached. Silvia turned her way. Kae could see tears welling up in the acolyte’s eyes, another interesting part about being a piece of this world, she started to notice.

“That guy is a jerk, but don’t let him make you quit.”

Kae offered as she reached out her hand, a signal of friendship in this game if players got close enough.

“I’m too weak to be your friend, though. You’re level 19...”

Silvia moaned, slumping her shoulders.

“You may be low level, but that’s part of the fun and journey of the game. If you stick with me, we’ll kick Dareth Darkmere’s backside and make this world a better place.”

With both hands on her hips, Kae smiled and turned into a wonderful smug smirk.

“I don’t know. I might just leave. I’m not in the mood to play this game anymore...”

Silvia admitted, rubbing the back of her ash hair and sighing deeply.

The foxes' shoulders deflated.

“Sorry that you had a bad experience. But I promise you, there’s more fun to be had in this game. Don’t give up quite yet,”

Again, she reached her hand out to the acolyte. Silvia paused for a moment before reaching her hand out too.

Silvia accepted Kae’s friend request.

“Sorry, I don’t want to make everything about me. Thanks for being nice. I’m just having a very bad day, Kae.”

She kicked her feet bashfully like a puppy in trouble for doing something wrong. The veteran picked up on this. So, to motivate her...

“It’s fine. You needed to go to Yale Forest for the [Yale Root], right? It’s for your acolyte quest to get better gear, right?”

She changed the subject.

If Kae remembered correctly, the acolyte quest required a journey through Yale Forest to collect 40 Yale Roots. Becoming an acolyte was simple: a few items from the Onyx forest. But the gear quest was a bit harder...

The developers expected players to form a group, since acolytes had little to no attack magic skills and low HP.

“Yeah, but a lot of people don’t want to do it... and Dareth goaded me into following him.”

Silvia pouted, cheeks puffing up as she made a strangling motion with her innocent hands.

“Ooohhh, I hate people like him.”

She murmured as she choked him out in her head.

“Well, I plan to get each badge from every class quest. I’ll need to do that quest eventually for the [Heroes of Light Amulet]. Let’s do it together, Silvia.”

“Are you sure, Kae? You don’t have to if you’re busy.”

The acolyte put both hands behind her back, clearly embarrassed by it all.

But Kae gave her a smile, trying to warm up her day if only a little.

“I am. It’s part of my grand plan to kill Rexia and become the new God of Keostar. You just happened to fall into it, is all.”

Kae lifted her nose to the sky, face as smug and confident as could be.

“Huh?”

“I’ll explain later. Could you join my party so we can get started?”

Kae sent over the party request. Silvia looked at the party name and gave Kae a strange and confused expression. One eyebrow raised slightly higher than the other.

“Rexia Killers? Isn’t that, by the lore, Goddess of the game, Rexia?”

“Yes. My goal is to brutally murder her and become God in her place.”

Kae stated bluntly, as if she were ordering a coffee off The Gathering Meal’s menu.

Silvia hesitated to accept the party invitation. Kae started to worry that Silvia might log out of the game and give up entirely on all this. If she did, Kae would have to make it her grand goal to punish Dareth by beating his backside.

“I met a strange person, Dad…”

Silvia said softly, as if to herself.

Silvia joined the party [Rexia Killers].

“Let’s head to the church so I can pick up that quest.”

With a wave, Kae guided her back to the church, located to the south of the Restoria streets.
