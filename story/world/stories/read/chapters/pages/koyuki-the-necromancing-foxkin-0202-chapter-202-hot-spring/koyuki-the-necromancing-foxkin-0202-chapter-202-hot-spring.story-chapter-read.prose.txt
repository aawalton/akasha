“It’s an interesting sensation. Having your head out in the cold and your body so pleasantly warm.” Cassie mused.

Cassie, spotting her girlfriend's problem, grabbed Skadi and pulled her onto her lap. Both blushed slightly but Skadi quickly relaxed and leaned on Cassie. They were really cute together.

“How does it feel?” Helena inquired.
