That's right! Fates Parallel Volume 1 is now forever free from KU jail and has been restored to this platform. I was going to make it free on Amazon as well, but apparently they don't actually let you do that so I had to just settle for $1. The first book is quite a bit shorter than the others, coming in at just around 100k words, but I'm glad I could finally make it available to everybody. I know how controversial KU is, and to be frank I don't like it any more than you do. I'd like nothing more than to be completely independent of Amazon so that my stories can be free for anybody who wants to read them, and this is one small step towards that dream. Thank you all so much for the love and support you've given me over the years. You're a wonderful community, and I'm honored to be a part of it.
