"Don’t let that discourage you,” he said. “You’ve presented some fine items here yourself. With any luck, you’ll make quite a bit. If you have money on-hand already, you’ll have a good shot at the deed.”

Above those are the private boxes, which are much nicer, and private, of course,” he explained.

After a day of rest she was back at it, though the eight days hadn’t even been a full day on the outside.
