Sanctuary faltered.

Edward worked his jaw as his fingertips dug into the wall. Hell was filling fast. Not only with the unfortunate adventurers caught up in this war beyond their capacity, but some of the world’s best fighters were now recovering down here.

Unsurprisingly, nobody seemed keen to engage in the revelry of the faux holiday event that brought them here after death.

A projection of Chuck flickered in at his side.

“Good news, I hope,” the demon requested, dryly.

“The clock ticks closer to midnight,” the faint wireframe responded. “Not all is lost, but our approach must change. The aliens use a bait and switch tactic, but this is within our expectations.”

Edward sighed. “That’s more ‘buts’ than I hoped. I suppose I should expect more visitors down here, then?”

“Hell could contain every living soul on Sanctuary.” With that vague and ominous statement, the projection faded away.

The demon checked the latest statistics. Despite the defending side killing several orders of magnitude more Radochs, the trend wasn’t on their side. Resources were dwindling. Only the most powerful were holding their own, and even some of them were struggling.

Looking out at the large crowd of second-chancers, it would be easy to consider the sacrifice of their immortality as a misplay. But now the Radoch force was syphoning off the System directly. Their plan was to die up ground forces with uncountable numbers of chaff, while those sitting far from danger stole immortality.

Now they were tightening their grip on the powerful STAR+, but it lacked what they truly sought. Chuck had planned for this.

A humorless smile crossed Edward’s face. After all, he of all people knew how easily a well was to poison.
