I come out of the maze and into a wide open space at the foot of one of the minor mountains. Pulling out the compass, I confirm that this is the place I need to be. There is a strange feeling in the air though, some sort of magic? That tingle from when I fought the basilisk is here too. Maybe it's some sort of illusion? To keep unwanted treasure hunters out, I'd bet.

Do the effects of my title really stretch that far though? I get that it is cool and it is a unique rarity. There must be some limit, though. Right?

Yipee, yet more things to add to my ‘figure it out later’ drawer. So putting that aside, I can’t see any monsters nearby, so this is a good time to check out a bunch of notifications I have been unable to. With a gasp of relief, I sit down on the ground. The fighting has been quite constant since that basilisk, never more than one monster at once though. Just enough to keep me challenged and leveling up. I may not technically get tired, but there is just something nice about relaxing every once in a while. Especially when one has a bunch of notifications to read.

‘Ding’, You have slain [Leastest Sandworm - Level 50].

‘Ding’, You have slain [Dust Mote - Level 56].

‘Ding’, Magic Smith has advanced to level 51. Stat points distributed.

‘Ding’, Spirit Mage has advanced to level 51. Stat points distributed.

None of the monsters were particularly difficult, but it is nice to see some levels gained. That sandworm was a bit scary though, the thing was massive, easily fifty meters long. To think it was classified as a leastest. I have never seen that before, but that implies they get a lot bigger. I sure hope I never run into a normal one, let alone a greater or whatever the highest tier is.

But wait, Dovey got an ability? It seems he got it after that dust mote fight. Dovey, why didn’t you tell me you got an ability? I ask the dove.

Sorry, master. I have been trying to get the hang of it, I was going to surprise you with my newly found awesomeness. He replies, and then I feel him flying towards me.

I glance up into the sky, and manage to spot him. Or rather, all of him? I think I need to get my eyes checked, because I am currently seeing four Doveys. All four of him come to sit down around me. “I can create copies of myself! Isn’t this great? Now the world can have more of me! Well, it's the same amount of me, just split across different places. It is quite strange to experience, hence why I wanted to wait to show you.”

“Great,” I sigh, “just what the world needed. More Doveys.”

“Meanie.”

“I’m joking of course. That will come in handy for scouting, I bet. You can quite literally be in multiple places at once. Can you attack at the same time?”

“Kinda, but each copy shares my power. So the attacks would be too weak to do much against enemies of our own level. It could be useful for dealing with swarms of weak monsters though, like in an incursion.” Dovey explains.

“Okay then, you have just been promoted from scout to scout and crowd control.”

“Yipeeeee!” Dovey’s copies say and then fly up into the air in celebration. After he gets it out of his system, they merge back together and become one Dovey again. I read the ability description myself, but it seems Dovey covered all of it. Though it is interesting to note that there doesn't seem to be any upkeep or anything, he can stay split like that as long as he likes. But the amount of copies are limited by his mental strength, which is based on stats, which increase as he levels.

This was quite a shocker to me, as I didn’t know spirits had stats. “Silly master, of course we have stats. Every living entity has stats, even if they don't have a corestone to manage and make the most use of them. For those without corestones, they just increase naturally with levels, depending on the type of creature. Same with all the other system stuff, we still get abilities and stuff, we just don’t have as fine of control as cored beings do.”

“Hmm.” I say as I ponder this new information. There is definitely something I can do with this information. But for now, I shall simply shove it off to the side. After all, I have some treasure to find.

Yip yip! Luma says from inside my phylactery. I think it is an encouragement, which is really nice. It is a shame that she has to rest in the phylactery while we travel, but she doesn't seem to mind that much. Plus it is nice to get the occasional encouraging yip delivered right into my mind.

As I walk towards the mountain, another notification appears in my mind.

You have now entered The Sanctuary of The Forgotten Primordial | Danger: Certain Death (Like seriously, run the fuck away and never come back)

The moment after the message appears, it hits me. I fall to the ground, choking on nothing. No, not nothing, an aura. It is too dense, I can’t breathe, and my entire body feels fuzzy. Like I am starting to fade away.

Yip! Luma shouts in my mind, asking to be let out. I do so, trusting the little fox.

She pops out of the phylactery and I watch as she starts to glow. The sensation of being erased slowly eases, until it is all gone. I suck in a deep breath of nice air that has a hint of light mana in it. Naturally, I give Luma many scritches. It must be her great spirits aura thing, it is somehow resisting the aura permeating this area.

What is more interesting is that the aura was able to harm me at all. I would’ve figured my title would take care of that. But it must only work for actual effects and spells and whatnot. In this case, the sheer power of the aura itself was what was harming me. In other words, I do have limits.

So, ignoring those limits, I march onwards. Somewhere within the mountain in front of me lies a powerful being. Some sort of primordial. One that the system classifies as so strong that it told me to run the fuck away. I do not know what this being is, but it sounds strong. It is definitely one that is sure to have lots of knowledge and loot and stuff for me to gain. I just gotta hope they are lonely and would like someone to share a cup of tea with.

The path towards the mountain is quite long. I walk for nearly an hour, yet I feel like I barely make progress. I even ended up pulling Dovey into my phylactery, as he wasn’t making any progress whatsoever. Some sort of spatial warping shenanigans are going on. To test out a theory, I start Plane Stepping more frequently. Surely enough, it seems to be a lot faster.

I have been getting this feeling that Plane Step is not a normal teleportation skill. Mostly from the feeling of actually doing it. It feels nothing like when Claire warped me, or when I was using the dungeon’s teleportation circles. Those felt more like the power was wrapping around me and whisking me elsewhere.

But when I Plane Step, there is none of that. I simply appear wherever I intend to be, with what is now barely any disorientation. My theory about it being strange has just been confirmed further. I am no expert in spatial magic, but I know that I am somehow bypassing whatever spell is cast on this area.

Any normal methods of transportation or even teleportation would be rendered useless by it. But Plane Step seems to be the sole exception. Which can’t be a coincidence. A being powerful enough to create this place would know about Plane Step and whatever weird form of teleportation it uses.

Which means I was meant to enter this place. Or more generally, some sort of Spirit Mage was. I pull out the compass again, and it nearly burns my hand even through my glove. The thing is hot, and it is humming loud enough to make my ears hurt a bit.

Without wasting any more time debating philosophy, I Plane Step through the rest of the spatial weirdness. Now I am about a kilometer away from the mountain that must be where the compass wants me to go. However, the area is starting to feel different. More powerful. Like I am treading somewhere I really am not meant to go.

Oh well, I’ve done that more times than I can count. What is one more?

I stride up to the mountain, before finally coming to a stop at a sharp ledge that drops into a large canyon that circles the foot of the mountain. Luma stands beside me, and I even let Dovey out to scout around now that we aren't in that spatial weirdness anymore.

Just as I am debating stepping to the bottom of the canyon, the world trembles. I jump back, worried that the ledge is going to collapse. But I quickly realize the trembling is at a much larger scale.

Looking up at the top of the mountain, I see it. A giant red tail, slowly materializing into the air. It grows and grows and grows, wrapping around the entirety of the gigantic mountain. Eventually, it reaches the top and the world shudders as a head pops into existence. A head that is very clearly that of a dragon. My identify pings off it instantly.

[Primordial Valdir - Level 2500]

I am no expert, but I am really fucking certian that I cannot fight a level two thousand five hundred. I didn't even know the levels go that high. Maybe that is the limit? All such thoughts are thrown out of my mind as one of the Primordial’s eyes open. I can feel it staring right at me, appraising me or whatever.

A few moments later, a voice forces its way into my mind. My my, what an interesting pair of beings. A Hero and a young Great One. Perhaps this intrusion will actually be worth my time.
