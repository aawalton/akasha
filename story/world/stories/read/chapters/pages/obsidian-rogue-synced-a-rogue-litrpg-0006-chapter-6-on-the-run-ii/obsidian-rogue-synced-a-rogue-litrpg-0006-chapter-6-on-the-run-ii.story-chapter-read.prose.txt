The gremlins were fast, but he was faster, much faster. His body moved on its own accord, rolling backwards as he sought to move away from the attacks of the gremlins. His simple maneuver put a sufficient amount of space between him and the blood thirsty gremlins.

Shriekkkk!

An ungodly sound erupted from their throats once they landed in the empty area that he'd once occupied. Their frustration at their prey escaping their combined assault was evident. Levi pulled himself back up as fast as possible, his cloak ruffling with his hood dropping from the quick movements, but he didn't care. The monsters could look at his face all they wanted, because their numerical advantage didn't reveal the truth; they were the prey, not him. And if they were the only thing between him and a successful escape from the Ghost, then this would be their last night.

"Bad luck, try again," he taunted, knees bent and daggers angled for a quick kill.

His eyes watered from the combined odor wafting up from the multiple unwashed monsters heading for him. He was forced to rapidly blink away the tears; he wasn't sure, but he wouldn't deny any claim that the odor was part of their offensive arsenal.

It definitely has to top the list as the foulest thing I've ever inhaled.

He really did believe that, his eyes shifting to his multiple foes, all five gremlins had changed tactics; rather than take him out in one swift joint attack as they'd no doubt initially planned to do. They now seemed to favor a much more reasonable attack, the pressing kind. They were advancing towards him at a slower but still effective pace, their clawed hands to their sides, their eerie, black, beady eyes focused on his human ones.

Both sides, itching to attack but still holding back. With Levi reluctantly giving up ground as he moved backwards, adding to the space the gremlins were slowly trying to eat up. He convinced himself that he was only trying to get them closer to the glowstones, and even though that was working, he knew he was inadvertently boxing himself in, with the walled-up entrance that was a few feet behind him.

Shriekkkk

Shriekkkk

The gremlins must've come to the same conclusion, as they added a bit of speed to their movements; their claws were now bared towards his direction. The dim light of the red glowstones illuminates their features. Their faces were, without a doubt, hideous, their ears elongated to a sharp point at the tip. The tallest of the five barely reached his chest; by all accounts, these gremlins were either a cruel creation or a joke of Cerpital.

"Come on, anytime now," he whispered, daggers bared in defiance.

Whether the gremlins could hear and understand him, he couldn't tell because they themselves didn't react to his words. They simply kept shrieking at him as they advanced, and the rogue kept moving back in response. Neither side launched an attack until Levi's back finally hit the wall that had been used to cover up the entrance to the dungeon.

The maniacal grin that split his face at the feeling of the wall pressed against his back was made much more nightmarish with the red glow the red glow stones cast across his face. He'd been lucky enough that his plan to lure them right up to this point had gone through without a hitch; now they were about to pay with their lives for such a dire decision.

Levi attacked, his dagger arcing out for two targets. One of the two gremlins he'd picked for his opening move was lucky enough to survive his attack, a clawed hand going up just in time to block the attack aimed for its neck. Blood was still drawn as his left dagger bit into the clawed hand; the momentum behind the attack was not enough to push the blade through the bone of the hand, and so he pulled back.

His second target did not have any such luck. Levi's right dagger slashed through its neck, blood erupting like a fountain, spraying both onto him and the other gremlins unlucky enough to be close to it. It tried to shriek, but the sound died in its throat, just like its ability to breathe. Its clawed hands reached for its torn neck to try and stop the bleeding, but it was already too late for it, just like it was for the rest of the gremlins in the narrow hallway.

Levi charged; the panic that had erupted among the four gremlins had left them disorganized, open, and vulnerable to attacks. His footwork had always been his pride, his ability to cover as much ground as needed to get to where he needed to go unseen as a thief was invaluable, and so was his ability to make quick and precise movements with his body.

The gremlins didn't know what hit them. Their disorganization had brought him the opening he'd sought, and now Levi intended to capitalize on his advantage. A heavy knee to the gremlin to the left that had survived his earlier attack sent it tumbling, the gremlin behind it falling as well. With those two temporarily out of the clash, he turned his focus to the remaining two on his right.

After the death of their fellow gremlin, the pair directly behind it had sought to put enough space between them and the blood fountain that was the fallen gremlin. Unfortunately for them, their terrible decision had come back to haunt them. Levi descended on them like lightning, his daggers flashing upwards even as the gremlins scrambled for some semblance of defense.

Too little, too late.

His left dagger reached the first of the two gremlins to his right, tearing through the skin of its left arm, nothing but a flesh wound, but it was enough to confuse the gremlin as to the rogue's intentions. It used its clawed hands to swat away his left dagger, recognition flashing through his features too late. A wet squelch accompanied by a popping sound echoed in the narrow hallway as Levi's right dagger pierced the gremlin's right beady black eye, going all the way to its brain and killing it immediately.

Levi didn't spare a glance at the gremlin he'd just killed, his attention already turning to the other one beside it. To the credit of the other gremlin, it put up more of a fight, its clawed hands swiping for Levi's face, causing the rogue to jump back, temporarily ending his scathing attack. The adrenaline rush he was currently experiencing was more than enough to keep him on his toes and away from danger.

Levi landed on the balls of his feet, a habit he'd picked up from his thieving days. It'd allow him stability while simultaneously affording him the option of a quick burst in any direction. An option that he promptly exercised, his legs moving in their own accord as he dashed towards the same gremlin.

Shriekkkk!

It shrieked in terror as Levi's frame sprinted towards him, the rogue boots pressing upon the body of the fallen gremlins as he struck at the terrified gremlin. It raised a clawed hand, hoping to deter the rogue for a second time, but in its panic, it had swiped wildly, completely mis-timing the attack and leaving itself open to Levi's deadly strike.

Gotcha! Levi sneered. Both daggers found their target unerringly, burying themselves hilt deep in the gremlin’s chest. A pained shriek left its lips as it clawed wildly at the rogue, only managing to tear at his cloak. Within two heartbeats, its swing's lost momentum, before coming to a stop entirely as the gremlin suddenly went limp on his blades.

A quick kick to the corpse was all it took to send the body sliding off his daggers and tumbling to the dirt floor. The red glow reflecting off its lifeless beady eyes, it had died terrified to its bones. The look of absolute terror was permanently etched onto its grotesque features, but Levi wasn't moved one bit; if anything, the rogue had already moved on from the kill.

His gaze fell back on the pair he'd sent tumbling down the hallway a few moments ago. The pair hurriedly scrambled to their feet as his eyes met theirs, the silent promise of death in his eyes clearly communicated between them; words were not needed for the gremlins to know that the roles had been reversed. No longer were they predators and he the prey. Now they were the hunted, and he … he was the hunter.

Shriekkkk!

The shrieks that filled the air this time weren't directed at him. The gremlins seemed to be arguing between themselves; the high-pitched shrieks were now laced with terror, their alien-like movements frantic and panicky in the face of the rogue with bloody daggers headed straight for them. Their beady eyes widened, and they turned around, ready to flee.

"And who said either of you could leave?" He asked, sprinting at the pair of gremlins.

Their short, stocky legs gave them a massive disadvantage when it came to running as compared to Levi's longer ones. They'd only managed to make it five steps before he caught up with them. Their shrieks rebounded off the walls in an uncomfortable pitch, but Levi was far from deterred by the unholy noise; if anything, he was infuriated by their shrieks, and ending the irritating noise was just another reason for him to kill them.

The gremlins seemed to conclude that they couldn't outrun the rogue, who was fast on their heels. With their plans to flee thwarted, the pair stood their ground. A defiant, albeit useless, change in tactics, they couldn't hope to fight him and live, just as they couldn't outrun him. Their fate was sealed, and it was only a matter of time before Levi freed their souls from the bonds of their bodies.

Levi couldn't stop himself in time; their decision to change tactics had happened abruptly, but it wasn't anything he couldn't handle. He was a thief —at least he'd been one before getting synced — and the thing that all good thieves were competent at doing was improvising on the spot, and so he did just that.

He barrelled into both gremlins at full sprint, the momentum and force behind the collision displacing all three. The two gremlins hit opposite walls of the hallway with an audible thud, while Levi himself tucked and rolled to a stop a few feet ahead of them. All three quickly scrambled to their feet, with Levi the first to do so. The gremlins hadn't been the only ones to suffer from their brief coming together; Levi had as well.

A claw had managed to get him on his face, just beneath his left eye; the stinging pain around the area told him everything he needed to know about the injury: one, it wasn't too deep, and two, he'd live. The claw had narrowly missed his eye, and for that he thanked Cerpitals' kindness.

"You're going to pay for that," He spat.

Not waiting for a response, he charged at the gremlin to his right, his dagger bypassing the feeble defenses of the creature. With efficiency valued over brutality, Levi went for a fatal strike aimed at the gremlin's throat, the blades of his daggers sinking in with very little resistance; a wet squelch was the only sound that exited the gremlin.

Its counterpart, which was on the other side, slashed at the Rogue's exposed backside, expecting to catch him off guard as he was preoccupied, but Levi had already anticipated the move and reacted accordingly. He angled his body to the side, causing the gremlin to miss his exposed back narrowly. It'd been a narrow miss, but narrow was all he needed.

His counterattack was as quick as it was brutal, his left dagger stabbing at the gremlin's crotch area, a heart-wrenching shriek left the gremlin's lips at the rogue's actions, but the shriek didn't last long before turning into a gurgle, the gremlin dropping to the floor as it clutched at its bleeding throat, a pointless action. Levi twirled his right dagger with glee; the blade had been responsible for slitting the gremlin's throat.

With a chuckle, he stood to his full height, staring at the dead bodies that decorated the hall, their grotesque features barely illuminated by the red glowstones. They'd all fallen for his plan by chasing him down to the walled entrance and had died to his blades. Now, he'd have to figure out a way to kill the rest hiding beyond the reach of the red glowstones.
