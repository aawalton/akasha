Liora scrambled away from Elion, climbing to her feet. She began trying to use a Zelian ability.

Elion, in his powered up Aurelian state, brushed aside her attacks.

“You’re one of them!” Liora shouted, “He was right! You betrayed our family!”

A weak Zelian dart smashed against Elion’s breastplate, bursting away into purple mist.

“Liora, stop!” Elion said, grabbing her wrists.

“Let go of me!” she screamed. “I have to get back to the throne! You’re going to destroy everything!”

“The throne almost killed you,” Elion countered. “You aren’t strong enough.”

“Dorian said that…”

“Dorian killed our parents,” Elion snapped. “He’s the reason mom fled to earth. He ruined everything!”

Liora weakened slightly, seeming uncertain.

“I don’t believe it!” she insisted. “That letter was fake. It’s lies. You’re all working against me and you always have been!”

“What letter?” Elion asked.

“The one about Dorian,” Liora said. “Killing our grandmother and trying to usurp the throne. It’s a lie.”

“No,” Elion said. “It’s true!”

“Dorian said I was born to be a princess!” Liora insisted.

“You were,” Elion said softly, gazing into his sister’s eyes.

“He gave me powers!” she said again. “You’re going to take them away from me!”

“Don’t be blinded by his offers,” Elion insisted. “He’s lying to you. Giving you what you want to make you believe him!”

“Oh yeah? And you just believe everything Zev tells you because you’re so good at telling the truth? Why are you glowing then? Huh? Because you have powers that Zev gave you?”

“No!” Elion said, “I… That’s not why!”

“Yeah right. You always thought you were better than me.”

“We don’t have time for this,” Elion said.

A wave of Tephalian suppression rolled through the throne room, then faded. Here, beside the throne, the distortion field felt significantly weakened.

“There are more Tephalians coming and they’re going to destroy this planet,” Elion pleaded. “We need to stop them!”

Liora scoffed. “Tephalians are a lie,” she said. “Dorian told me everything. They were invented as a myth to keep people obedient and paying taxes, never living up to their full power.”

“Elion!” Isalyn called from near the throne. “We don’t have much time!”

“Listen,” Elion said, gritting his teeth and pinning Liora down.

“Get off me,” she screamed, struggling beneath him.

“This whole planet is going to be destroyed if I don’t do something,” he said. “I need to get on that throne and save us. You need to abdicate to me or else…”

He trailed off.

Liora stopped struggling.

“Or else what?” she asked, her voice deadly. “Or else you’ll kill me?”

Elion clenched his jaw.

“That’s what you’re accusing Dorian of doing!”

“I’m not going to kill you,” Elion said. “But someone else will. And it will probably be Dorian! Do you know the line of inheritance? It goes you, then me, then Dorian. The only reason that you’ve stayed alive is because Dorian didn’t know where to find me.”

Liora tried to say something but Elion pushed on.

“He couldn’t kill you and make me the heir! Until he found me, your life was safe, since he thought he could manipulate you into using the throne for him!”

“Shut up!” Liora said, kicking him in the groin. Elion flinched, giving her enough space to wriggle out of his grasp. “All my life you’ve been Mr. Goody two shoes, always telling me I’m making bad choices and choosing the wrong things. You’ve never supported me and now you’re telling me that I’m not good enough and you need to do everything your way!”

She blasted him in the face with a Zelian attack. She had some raw power behind it, but she didn’t have much skill. The attack bounced off of Elion. He knew that he was a higher level than her.

He slapped her in the face.

“You shut up! Listen to me! You never think about anyone but yourself. You’re always going out and doing whatever you want to do, and you never care about how I might be worried about you, or how I might be staying up late to make sure you get home safely. Remember that time I had to go pick you up from the county jail on my bike?”

Liora climbed to her feet, facing Elion as she jabbed a finger into his chest. “Oh you think I never do anything for you huh? Why do you have the best room in the house then? Who do you think deals with the taxes and the bills and the creditors and the government? It isn’t Zev! So sue me for wanting to live better than that!”

She continued forward, and Elion backed up until they stood in front of the throne. “I like living in a palace. I like having a maid named Savela who cleans up for me, and draws warm baths. I like having a four poster bed, and never having to worry about where my next meal comes from. I like getting the respect I deserve. Don’t you realize that this place is supposed to be our home? We were meant to grow up here. Like this!”

“I get it!” Elion said. “That’s what I’m trying to tell you! Dorian isn’t your friend. He’s going to kill us if he gets the chance, we need to get on that throne and stop him, right now!”

Liora wiped tears from her eyes.

“I’m an idiot.” she said. “I thought I could do it. I thought I could be a queen.”

“You can help. Abdicate. I’m stronger than you. I’ll stop all of this. We’ll fix everything.”

Liora’s eyes danced around the room. Her feet turned toward the exit.

“Don’t try to run,” Elion said. “I won’t be able to stop them from killing you if you do. A very good friend of ours died trying to help you, and they’re not happy about it.”

Liora didn’t move.

“Please Liora,” Elion said.

The doors at the far end of the hall blew off their hinges and Dorian stormed in.

The air around Dorian cracked with power. He laughed as he spotted Elion and Liora standing in front of the throne.

“You’ve caught him for me!” he exclaimed. “Well done, princess!”

“She’s not working for you anymore,” Elion shouted back, though he could see the turmoil on his sister’s face.

“She’s seen though your lies and joined us. It’s over, Dorian!”

Elion moved to sit on the throne, hoping to bluff his uncle into betraying the truth.

His actions worked too well.

“A shame,” Dorian said. “Then it’s plan b.”

He summoned up a massive blast of Zelian power, a terrible beam of light that blazed through the hall.

Elion threw himself in front of Liora, as she stood petrified.

The attack struck Elion full in the chest, but with the help of the defenses of the throne, Elion deflected the attack.

He staggered backward, and his sister caught him.

Her face was pale, her jaw tight.

She heaved a great sigh.

“I abdicate my right to the throne,” she said, and mist of multicolored light evaporated from her skin.

Elion felt the mantle settle on him like the weight of a massive millstone.

“No!” Dorian shouted. He charged forward.

Zev burst through the doors in pursuit of his brother.

Liora screamed, summoning up a frail Zelian shield.

Keyla, Isalyn, Tuft, and Selna dove for cover.

Dorian and Zev met in a collision of power, which blew outwards in all direction, obliterating columns and pulverizing the gems in the ceiling.

Elion fell into the throne.

It activated beneath him.

His mind expanded, and he became aware of the whole planet, all at once. Time slowed.

He tried to cast his mind back to the the narrow, tiny throne room where his family drama played out.

The throne hungered for his power. It drained him. He used his Empower ability. It drained that as well.

He tried to stop the detonation, but something blocked him. Something restrained his capacity to act.

He watched power and energy rip through Dorian and Zev, atomizing their bodies. Liora was vaporized. The columns that Tuft and Isalyn hid behind evaporated.

The power shook the throne, but Elion continued to feed it. The air burned around him.

Elion flexed the power of the throne, pushing against its restraints. He felt a familiar flavor to the weakness, and realized that the throne was under attack by Tephalian powers.

He turned his attention to the enemy, and began crushing them like bugs.

There were too many. He wasted time searching out individuals. he needed to find a way to stop…

There, approaching the planet. A ship, beaming power down to its warriors, preparing to launch its hoards down to destroy Kylios.

Elion swatted it from the sky like a bug.

He felt his power expanding. He needed to protect the planet. Cut it off from external threat.

The sphere.

The broken shards of the celestial sphere still orbited the planet. Like a gigantic puzzle, Elion saw how they fit together. He began placing them, carefully, one after another, into their positions.

Back where they belonged, one by one, with meticulous precision, fusing them together.

He labored until the sphere was once more a solid, complete shield.

Then he collapsed.
