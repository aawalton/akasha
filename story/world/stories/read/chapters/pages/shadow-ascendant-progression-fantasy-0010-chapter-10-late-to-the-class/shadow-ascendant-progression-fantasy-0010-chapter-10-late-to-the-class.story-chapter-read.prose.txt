I finally stepped out, this time making sure I had everything, or at least I hoped I did.

The hallway greeted me with its usual gloom, the bulbs dead and dusty, with only a little bit of light seeping through the far-off exits, one to the left and one to the right.

The left one being brighter of course, since it was the one leading to the main gate.

Though I turned to the right and began walking towards the mess hall. Not that I was hungry. They don’t really serve food this early anyway.

No, it was the shortcut—through the mess hall’s back exit straight onto the road to Cellera.

My shoes thudded softly against the marble floor, echoing through the silence. I wasn’t late often, it was the first time.

But being late terrified me, mostly because I dreaded what people would think if I, a mere commoner, showed up behind schedule.

Maybe they’d think I am ungrateful to the institution, to them? Maybe to our instructors?

And these were the things I didn’t want anyone to think about me… then again, I kinda fucked up by reading that book.

"Ahhhh," I sighed, passing by the mess hall that was currently closed.

Exiting the dorm, I was met with bright sunlight. My eyes squinted as the light tore through my flesh! "Damn that sensitivity!" I dramatically muttered, blinking.

I turned my eyes toward the ground, looking at the inclined grass field that was supposed to be my shortcut. Good thing Headmaster Azembor established Cellera here, I thought.

I began walking; it turned into running real fast. The slope was increasing my speed drastically. Maybe I was even teleporting, maybe not? The fast air that splashed to my face probably did wonders to my hair… I hope it did!

In a minute I reached the stone pavement, connecting the main gates of all the dormitories—Ares, Cryz, and Wiz to the three gates of Celera.

The pavement I walked on was sturdy and made of large bricks that were probably longer than my two full steps. Yes, I just counted them.

I looked up. My eyes were now accustomed to the sunlight. There were a few students moving in the campus, meaning? I wasn’t late after all.

I could probably have slept two-three minutes more. I smiled.

Anyway, so the door closed.

"....."

For a moment I kept standing there, probably smiling like an idiot. The smile vanished real quick.

And I turned around, my eyes closed as I considered my next plan of action. Maybe I shouldn’t go to class today, not like anyone saw me, hmmm, let’s say I had a fever.

Yeah, right, that would work, I nodded.

"Ohhh… looks like we are late."

I looked up in horror at the source of the sound. I recognised who it belonged to. It was Noelle, Mabel’s roommate, and one of the few noble-kids who didn’t ignore me.

She was wearing her uniform—the black shirt, dark blue skirt, and a matching bow-tie. Her black hair was short, barely reaching her neck. She simply wore a green headband, letting her hair free.

Noelle blinked. "We sure are late, haha." She laughed.

There’s no wee! I mentally yelled.

"Ahaha, sure looks like that." I mumbled, biting my lip.

"Hey Arnos, doesn’t the intertwining tower look real cool from here?" she pointed above. "See! It looks like it is invading the sun!"

Following her black eyes, I looked up. I closed my eyes. "Oh, my! It sure looks good," I lied, turning my head back down.

"Isn’t that right?" she smiled. "Well, we should get going now, or we will be late."

We are already—

Noelle walked past me. "We would be even later!" She exclaimed, looking straight ahead.

Reaching the tall, imposing metal gate, I stopped and turned towards Noelle, she was gesturing for me to knock on the door. Doing some weird hand signs, how did I even manage to decipher it?

I shook my head.

"You do that yourself," I murmured.

"I am a frequent late-comer, it won’t be as effective. They know my knocking pattern too, they won’t budge!" she murmured back.

I looked at the gate, its black paint looking scary, then at Noelle. She was giving me a thumbs up for a morale boost, probably?

Finally, I steeled myself and turned back, about to leave. I mean, I could always say Noelle saw a ghost right? And why would she even tell everyone I went back, not like I am her enemy.

"Ahhh! It is you again."

I looked back in horror. Someone else saw me too… ahhh!

The female instructor with glasses turned to me. "And you?" she added, her brows raised.

Maybe she was confused? "Sorry, ma’am, I had forgotten my book and had to go back to pick it up, and because of that I arrived late."

I apologised. I looked up; she seemed to believe my excuse. Her gaze turned to Noelle. "And you?"

"I overslept…" Noelle said, rubbing her eyes nonchalantly, as if it was the most important thing in the world.

"You overslept?" The instructor gave her a chance to change her answer, perhaps.

"Yes, I overslept."

And she decided to double down. Later, we received a brutal scolding. The female teacher went over things as if it were her practiced speech. It started with discipline and its important and ended with, it happens sometimes; just do not make a habit of it.

"This should not happen again, am I clear?"

We nodded together, and only then were we allowed into our classroom.

"It went smoothly, didn’t it? She didn’t scold me much today, maybe because you were with me. Haha, you are lucky for me," Noelle said, as we moved a few steps away from the teacher.

"Ahh right, you are frequently late, aren’t you?" I asked, slightly curious.

Noelle said nothing. Looking at her, it felt like she was thinking about something. We walked onto the school’s campus. The sunlight stopped trailing behind, and then she spoke up.

"I think you could say that. I don’t enjoy wasting my time waiting in the classroom, and so I don’t start getting ready for school until… like one hour is left."

One hour, not half an hour. What does she even do that takes an hour? I questioned, re-looking at her appearance carefully once again.

"Yesterday she lectured me for fifteen minutes, can you believe that!" Noelle exclaimed as we turned to the right.

I didn’t say anything; fifteen minutes of lecturing felt fair to me.

"Do you value your time over others, like you wasted her fifteen minutes too, didn't you?"

"I don’t know," she paused, "can’t she stop lecturi—, no, that would make everyone irresponsible." Noelle gave a tired sigh. "I won’t throw pillows at her from now on!" She exclaimed, clenching her fist.

What? Where did the pillow come from?

"What do you mean?" I asked.

"Mabel, she used to be my personal alarm clock since I don’t use one… she’d wake me up by saying weird things like—"

"No explanation needed." I raised my hand.

"Yeah, so I threw a pillow at her face to stop her from doing it. Sleep is pretty important, right?"

I nodded. This was one thing both I and Noelle agreed on.

"Wouldn’t it be good if all the teachers were, I don’t know, humans, and we could keep them as pets and, you know, get them to spat out knowledge whenever we wake up!" she added, her black eyes sparkling.

Now there were two things we agreed on.

"That won’t be good, haha," I laughed. Stopping as we finally reached our classroom.

I glanced at Noelle, who had a smug smile plastered on her face paired with a thumbs up. Again!

"You go first, Arnos," she whispered, putting her hand down.

This creature had just decided to make me face Instructor Blackwell’s wrath? I can’t let this happen to me. I must muster the strength to say something.

"Arnos, how many times have I been late this month? Already four times in just two weeks, if I knock first, we both go down. Think about it… Instructor Serena would look at you with pity, but me? Haah, she’d probably jump at me like a wild beast."

"Yes, yes, that’s what she’d do," she nodded, her arms crossed, eyes closed.

I swallowed, and then, knocked on the door. I think my first two knocks were too quiet.

"tch," Noelle clicked her tongue.

[Knock Knock]

"Come in," Instructor Serena’s voice came out. I opened the black wooden door. The class hadn’t started yet. The list on which all the students used to do their signatures for attendance was being passed around.

Instructor Serena looked in our direction, her violet eyes showing no sign of anger… then there was anger? Wait, did I piss her off or something!

"Arnos, go take your seat," she commanded.

And without wasting a moment, I obeyed. Turning to the seating area of the classroom, the bench I was assigned was the second one to the far corner.

About to reach my seat, I heard our instructor’s loud voice. "Where do you think you are going?!" she asked.

I glanced back to see Instructor Serena glaring daggers at Noelle before turning and taking her seat without a word. Noelle was probably doomed, there was no way she could get out of the situation unharmed.

Well, that is, unless she could reverse time—which she probably couldn’t. She was an air mage, after all. Ah, the poor girl, I murmured inwardly, genuinely sad, as I set my leather bag on the wooden bench and turned my full attention to the scene about to unfold.

I was, of course, totally not entertained by it.
