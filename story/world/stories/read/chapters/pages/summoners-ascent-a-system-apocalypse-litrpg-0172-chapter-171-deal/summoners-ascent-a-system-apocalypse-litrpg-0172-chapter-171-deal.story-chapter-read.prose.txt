Ashton stepped back out of the pond, and quickly called on the undine to pull the water from his trousers and boots.

Compared to before, he was looking much cleaner, and much more well-kept. It looked like the cleaning that the nymph did was a bit special in a lot of ways. The fresh scars littering his body that had been aching recently had calmed down, and though he couldn't see them, it did feel like they might have faded a little more than before.

And of course, just all the grime and blood that had accumulated since Ashton hadn't cleaned himself all too well since yesterday was now gone. He felt fully refreshed.

But now that he was here, and now that he knew that there was this connection to this nymph that had lived here for a long time, despite the lack of any proper mana in the area, there was something that he figured he should ask.

"I think there are a few more things to tell you." Ashton smiled as he put his cloak back on. "I'm a summoner. I have formed contracts with, amongst others, these two elementals, as well as Poppy, the flower pixie who you had such a heated conversation with just earlier."

The nymph looked at Ashton curiously. He figured that she was already aware of what he was going to ask, and since she didn't lose the smile on her face, he figured he could ask.

"Once I have the capacity to safely form another low-grade contract, and once I have collected the right materials for an ink that matches your magic, would you allow me the honour of forming a contract with you?"

For a few moments, the nymph was thinking, looking around the area. She was clearly nervous about this.

"Oh, don't worry at all. This isn't the sort of situation where I would actually call you to me. You will stay here within the sanctuary to protect all these children, and I will come to you when I need you, or carry around a proxy to allow me access to certain abilities," Ashton explained immediately. "There are a lot of benefits to summoning magic. For one, all your abilities will be used without exerting yourself. I will give you an offering, and that is what your abilities will be used through. I'm sure that you're exerting yourself just to keep up a sanctuary this powerful."

The more he thought about it, the more incredible this place actually was. Somewhere safe from the intervention of the constellations. Maybe Ashton should come here more often. It wasn't like his brother was here to kick him out again if he were to make this house his base of operations. Plus, if he could make this place a 'base', then he could protect it from the changes to this world that would happen in the second stage.

As Ashton was thinking about this and that, the nymph also seemed to be considering things. At least, Poppy had quickly started talking to her again, probably trying to convince her.

"One of your powers is that of enhancement, right?" Ashton asked, and the nymph looked at him surprised. "That's a power that I'm looking for the most at the moment. You can bestow the 'essence of existence' onto particular objects. I will bring you the right materials, and then you will enhance it. I've worked with a fairy with that power before, so I know roughly how it works. Plus, and this might not be that important to you, through the interference of a summoner, you can grow much more powerful and learn new abilities a lot faster. It will be beneficial to both of us."

The smile on the nymph's face became more confident, and she opened her mouth. Sounds that Ashton wasn't able to interpret came from her, but Poppy seemed to understand well enough and did so for him.

"In that case, I will come back soon with an adequate offering and anything else we need for a contract," Ashton said with a happy smile. This had turned out way better than he expected it would, to be honest.

But now, there was something else that he had to do, and it might be a bit tough to manage.

Poppy was following behind Ashton as he turned toward the edge of the sanctuary. However... "I think it would be better if you stayed here for a bit."

The pixie was stunned, and Ashton had to catch her before she dropped down onto the ground. She looked at Ashton confused, before rapid bell-ringing sounded out.

"Hey, hey, calm down, okay?" Ashton smiled. "I'm just saying that I think you should stay here while I'm asleep. It will be a while, and I don't want you stuck alone in some musty office or in my vessel. As soon as I can when I wake up, I will summon you, yeah?"

Poppy had a sad expression on her face. She wiped some tears from her eyes and nodded.

"I promise, you will have a great time here, and you will be safe. And I'll leave you with tons of candy, too."

She perked up a little at the mention of candy, so Ashton quickly gave her the small pouch that was in one of the cloak's pockets at the moment. She was barely able to carry it as it was.

"I'll use some of these to summon you, okay?" Ashton said, and Poppy agreed without hesitation. For flower pixies, candies or flowers were usually the best offerings. It was even better when candies were homemade, too. The same 'effort' that was used to summon the wisp tended to help out in any sort of offerings, so given the chance, Ashton should make as much as he could himself.

Actually, that was why he should probably ask for another favour right now. As Poppy found a good spot to stash her candy, Ashton went over to the edge of the pond again.

"Before I go... would you mind if I took a photograph of you?" Ashton asked the nymph, having the Backpacker quickly give him the instant-print camera. The nymph agreed with a smile, and Ashton quickly held it forward. He held it in a position where he figured he should get a good shot, and took the photo without any filters on it, so that it would essentially come out as a mundane picture.

He had also tagged the nymph through the Pen of Insight already, and would ask for specific types of information when the pen wrote it down later.

The best offering for a nymph was a new dress, especially since she seemed impressed by the one that Ashton made for Poppy, so this wasn't a bad idea. He could just show the picture to someone later and ask them what colours the nymph actually was, so that he could pick out the right materials.

And now, with that taken care of, Ashton left, and on the way spotted the wingless treasure pixie looking up at him, hiding behind a rock. He waved at them, before he continued to walk.

The veil shifted around him, as Ashton once more appeared back in his garden, right where he had entered the sanctuary earlier.

Ashton raised a brow. "Observer, you're back?"

Ashton focused on the messages. They said that they weren't able to see anything, but he wasn't totally sure that he could trust that. It was very possible of course, especially since he couldn't sense those same annoying presences the whole time, but maybe things were just limited.

'Seeing' wasn't the only way to learn what happened in a place. It was possible that even if they weren't able to see Ashton directly, they could still somehow extract the information in some other way. He knew that there were entities that it was impossible to hide from in the first place, so he wouldn't be surprised if there were constellations that could peruse the truth of this place or Ashton's actions and figure it out that way. Especially once constellations were able to interfere more with this world.

For the time being, though, it seemed like this place was one where Ashton could have some real, safe conversations if necessary. Plus, if he managed to convince her and gave her a good enough offering, the sanctuary could also be a place where he could hide certain things or even people. Depending on how Millie's powers developed, this might not be the worst place for her to be until she got everything under control, where constellations couldn't touch her.

But this was something that he would figure out if it ever came to that. For now, he just decided to leave. He just had two hours left, if everything went by the schedule Ashton set himself for all this.

And so, Ashton left back the way he came. He walked through the dilapidated house, and once he was out front again, Ashton started to run. It felt a little quiet without Poppy's chattering, but he would live.

With this, Ashton had done everything that he had to rush to get done, and could finally rest for a while. He knew he would feel absolutely terrible when he woke up in a few days, but at least he would be rested.

Ashton briefly focused on his hand. He was shaking pretty hard.

This wasn't out of nervosity or pain or even the cold; it was because of the side-effect of chugging as many powerful potions as he had over the past couple of days.

Potions weren't something to be taken lightly. They were extremely powerful medicines that could potentially alter the structure of your body. There was no way things like that came without any side-effects.

Back in Lumia, when his body was strengthened by the Summoner, he didn't have to worry quite as much. By the time he was allowed to drink a potion, his body—and with that the resistance that came with it—was many, many times stronger than it was right now. Ashton had experienced it a handful of times, though it was usually when overusing a 'last ditch effort' potion, which would essentially kill the user after the effect was over.

But something this weak was never anything that Ashton had to deal with, and it was... annoying.

Ashton was dealing with potion addiction. His body was reacting to the overuse of ingested potions in a deeply unpleasant way. It didn't exactly work the same as physical addictions did, though. There wasn't a physical need for the potions, but rather, one's mana destabilised and caused adverse effects. It could lead to physical addiction symptoms, but it came from an inherently different place.

It also meant that, unless it was really bad, there weren't any physical side-effects. But a mage with a severe case of potion addiction wouldn't be able to cast any spells, and even the usage of skills seemed to be much more inefficient compared to before.

But if you were skilled enough, it was possible to take control of your unstable mana to push down these effects, and hopefully, over the next few days, Ashton's mana would naturally start to stabilise again as he rested.

For that, though, he first had to get back to Calibur.
