The sun was lower now, casting long shadows between hundreds of thousands of cracked buildings and heaps of rubble within the city. The air still stank of burnt wood and scorched cloth, with a hint of cooked meat.

And the farther he traveled through the city of Greeber, the more he began to truly grasp the scale of the destruction that came from whatever the hell the Blue Wolves took a part of to overthrow the royals of Greeber.

At first, he’d assumed it was just a minor earthquake that had damaged the buildings in the slums. But he’d been wrong – very wrong.

Entire neighborhoods, ones that looked like they had once been clean and orderly, lay in ruins. Hundreds of thousands of residential buildings had collapsed into rubble. Fires were devouring laundry lines that still flapped with forgotten clothes, from the cracks beneath the rubble, and along the streets – there wasn’t a street without a live fire on it.

People of all ages and races swarmed the streets, some looting stores, both abandoned and still guarded, while others sobbed openly, clutching each other amid the chaos – unsure of what to do.

It was far worse than anything he’d imagined.

Archie tried not to think too hard about it as he and the guy, Tallen, as he finally learned during the half-hour slog through shattered alleys and barricaded streets, closed in on the southern exit of Greeber.

The gate itself loomed ahead like a crooked jawbone, half of it caved in from a collapsed tower, the other side jammed open at a weird angle, enough to let people through but just barely. Guards were thin, disorganized. Probably more concerned with not dying than enforcing exit protocol.

Which worked just fine for Archie, since he didn’t have any ID on him that he thought would be valid here – who knew if this world even accepted Omniversal Solution’s IDs, or if he’d need to be registered on some kind of local traveler list the guards kept just to be allowed out of the city.

Tallen kept glancing over his shoulder every few steps, muttering under his breath about how fucked he was that he wasn’t doing his job.

“… You want to create more chaos?” Archie said dryly, leaping over a broken cartwheel and adjusting the strap across his chest. “The city has plenty of it right now.”

Tallen flinched. “I’m just trying not to die, okay? If Boss Frel finds out that I’ve been helping someone else, I’m utterly fucked… Not to mention this exploding thingy you placed on me.”

“It’s not a death rune; if you have enough Vitality, Endurance, and or Willpower, you should be able to survive it,” Archie said absently, eyes scanning the buildings ahead. “It’s a Detonation Runic Script, like a bomb that explodes under certain conditions, such as needing to touch my mana or getting hit. If it were a death rune, then… I dunno, you’d probably be dead by having it on you… That would be a cool rune to study...”

“Not comforting,” Tallen muttered, glancing down at his chest in panic, and mumbling out a prayer.

Archie tuned him out.

His mind was elsewhere, already chewing on the scraps of information he’d picked up during their journey toward the gate, both from the people below and from Tallen.

Why get information on this almost destroyed city?

Greeber wasn't just a random hellhole on the map. It was his spawn point for the System Event, meaning that it most probably had something to do with his character’s background. Which meant there was something here. A thread. A clue. Maybe even the spark that kicked off whatever massive, world-shaking quest was tied to his origin.

And after a bit of casual questioning, overhearing panicked people down below, he’d started piecing together a bigger picture of what was going on within GReeber.

Greeber was a powder keg, and it had four main fuses.

The two major gangs – the Blue Wolves and the Gilded Grin – and the two other factions, the royals and nobles, each controlled different parts of the city, every one of them clinging to their own twisted ideology and grip on power.

Out of the four of them, the Blue Wolves were the loudest of the bunch.

They were not just some ragtag gang like he’d first assumed, according to Tallen, they were an organized crime syndicate that supposedly most of the populace preferred over every other faction.

And they wanted to erase the old system. And judging by the wreckage, they were well on their way.

But judging by the sheer destruction engulfing the city, and the constant screaming and shouting all around them, Archie seriously doubted that was the case.

Apparently, they started as a slum militia that got sick of being starved and stomped on by the other three factions. Somewhere along the way, they slowly upped their numbers and steadily rose their levels to where their inner group supposedly had a couple of mid D-Grades.

The Gilden Grin didn’t hold territories in the same way the Blue Wolves did, but they had eyes and ears everywhere. And piecing together Tallen’s explanation of them, they were a Thieves Guild in essence – dealing with information of all kinds, smuggling, assassination, and the like.

The royals and the nobles weren’t much different from each other, in which they taxed the hell out of the people of Greeber. 68% of their income was to be taxed every year, a cent or so less, and they would either get executed, conscripted, or turned into slaves.

Along with a bunch of other generally corrupt stuff, eerily similar to the way ultra-rich people operated back on Earth, they were also big on rituals. Word on the street, and according to Tallen, was that they regularly used human sacrifice to boost their levels, strength, and a myriad of other things.

Focusing back on the present after hearing Vul from within his Soulspace telling him to focus, Archie’s boots pounded down on cracked rooftops battered with soot and ash.

Tallen was just behind him, gasping for breath, his feet slipping on every jump between buildings, using his Power Boost skill to its utmost limit in order to keep up with Archie.

The closer they got to the city’s massive wall, the more the buildings around them began to look like they were damaged by something similar to massive cannonballs. Which, not a couple of seconds later, after such a thought entered his mind, a ballista bolt fired from the walls tore through the air and punctured through an apartment building a couple of rooftops away from them.

Archie skidded to a halt on the roof’s edge, glancing up at the wall that towered above them, fifty meters higher than where they were. Snapping his gaze to the upper right of the wall, he could see a watchtower that had a ballista bow attached to it suddenly explode, and from it, he could hear screaming.

Ignoring the scent of old spices and charred bodies from the barely holding together merchant’s guild building beneath them, Archie grabbed Tallen by the chest like a sack of potatoes, leather tunic bunched in his fist, and launched them straight into the sky.

Tallen screamed all the way up, eyes shut as he refused to look down at the ground that seemed to shrink away.

Then, just before they could start to fall, Archie gathered a pulse of mana into his heel and created a glowing blue mana platform into existence midair. Upon landing atop it, he immediately launched himself further upward – doing his best to keep his mana usage on the downlow to keep and prevent the guards or whoever was manning the walls from detecting him.

Tallen let out something between a sob and a choking yelp as Archie’s second jump caused him to lose focus and cancel his Power Boost skill.

They soared like bullets, arcing high above the fires and rooftops below until they landed atop the southern wall, slipping between two collapsed crenellations as the wall guards were fighting against a better-armed militia group.

Archie exhaled through his nose and flicked his fingers out, creating yet another mana platform.

The stairs continued to build as they descended, each one forming just in time beneath their feet as they ran down into open air.

Tallen, still being held up by Archie’s right arm, clung onto it like a lifeline and continued to shout the entire way down, much to Archie’s displeasure.

It was only when they touched ground a good sixty meters out from the wall, landing in a dirt stretch thick with weeds, did Archie drop Tallen to the floor and who moved to rub his sore ears.

Tallen immediately dropped to his knees, coughing and holding his chest like he was going to vomit. He looked up at Archie, eyes bloodshot and wild.

“Alright – alright! I did it, man! I did what you asked! You said help you out, and I did! I got you to the gate, I didn’t run, I didn’t call for help – fuck, I didn’t even tell you how screwed I was for doing this!”

Archie tilted his head in confusion as he took out his Mana Bike from his Spatial Storage and set it on the ground beside him.

Tallen kept going, panic rising in his throat. “Just– please, man, I don’t want to blow up! I don’t care if you’re a Grade up me or whatever the hell, just get this rune off me!”

The bike roared to life in a low rumble as Archie swung one leg over the saddle and settled atop it, just before taking out his Compass of Desire and fixing it in place on the mana bike.

Tallen, still panting in the dirt with his knees sunk halfway into a patch of overgrown weeds, looked up with wide, exhausted eyes. “Please, my family would be killed if the boss learns that I left my post to help you... Please.”

Archie adjusted the throttle, glanced back over his shoulder to get a look at Tallen’s face, and grinned. “There was no explosive rune,” he said, voice smooth but laced with just enough smugness to make Tallen's jaw drop in disbelief. “I placed a heating rune on your armor. Just think about wanting to be warmer, and it'll do its thing. You haven’t really done anything to me that would deserve you turning into a living bomb.”

There was a pause.

“You– what?” Tallen blinked. “Are you serious right now?”

But Archie was already twisting the throttle. “Yup,” he smirked.

The mana bike shot forward with a sharp crack, leaving behind a faint trail of green light in the dirt behind it as it carved through the weeds and shot southward. Enjoying as the wind slammed against his face and the world blurred past, scorched grass and distant trees melting into streaks of green and black.

Behind him, faint but not faint enough, he heard Tallen’s voice rise up in a ragged shout. “You bastard! I’ll kill you when I get strong enough! I swear it!”

Archie chuckled under his breath. “What type of anime-esque bullshit was that declaration?”

Two tiny heads popped out from the chestplate of his armor, their small goggled faces peering forward with childish glee. Volos, his sarcastic, gremlin-like spirit beast, nudged Arsenic, his equally gremlin-like spirit beast.

“Let’s just try and stay in place, okay?” Archie said, eyes still scanning the horizon. “Any attempts at trying to leap out will result in being grounded in my Soulspace. Am I clear?”

The two glenwyn centipedes whooped and braced themselves against the wind, their tiny feet gripping the armor plating as the bike continued its climb over a rolling hill – uncaring of the potential danger they were in if they were to attempt to pull themselves out from where they were poking out of.

As the last rays of sunlight bled over the horizon, Archie leaned forward, the wind biting against his armor as he whispered to himself: “Next stop, Creavenboor.”

And the mana bike roared as he activated both Runic Amplification and Runic Mending, causing the Mana Bike to almost double in speed.
