Alice Varro had not looked at any screen since they entered the room.

The Mistfall Hive Lord stood at the center of the space, her liquid-teal eyes completely unfocused, staring straight through the holographic displays. The air pressure within a two-meter radius of her position abruptly plummeted, leaving a taste of heavy, metallic static on the back of Hathaway's tongue.

She chose this spoon. She chose this angle. She made sure the signet ring was in frame. She isn't hiding who she is. She is actively presenting a curated sequence of visual assets.

So what is she showing? And what is she hiding behind it?

An impossible angle. An invisible ceiling. A severed Z-axis.

The natives worship her as a savior. They empty their global treasuries to pay her for 'disaster-averting rituals'."
