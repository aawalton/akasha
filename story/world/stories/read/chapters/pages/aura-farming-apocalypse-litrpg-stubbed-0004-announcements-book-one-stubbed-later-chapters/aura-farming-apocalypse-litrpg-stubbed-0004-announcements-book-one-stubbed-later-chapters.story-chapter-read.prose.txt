Hey guys, just letting you all know book one of Aura Farming is now available on Amazon and Audible.

Amazon link: https://www.royalroad.com/amazon/B0G8FMXLCR

Audible link: https://www.audible.com/pd/Aura-Farming-1-Audiobook/B0HDQBFR4Y

With that out of the way... You may notice that I haven't posted in here in quite a long time, and the chapters beyond just book one are also gone. I can only apologise for that. The reasons for both of those things are the same: I've spent much of my writing time trying to refine them both over the last few months, almost completely re-writing large chunks in places, only to end up discarding large parts of those rewrites as it kept cascading into more and more continuity errors that would have to be fixed.

At one point, it felt I'd written more for rewrites than the original effort gone into the books themselves. It was maddening, and it burned me out and left me a bit depressed. I... kind of gave up, I can't lie. Writing as a full time job has been a dream since forever, and I felt like I was messing it up by spending so long keeping my publisher waiting, which paradoxically left me demotivated to keep trying.

The embarrassing end result is a version of books 2 and 3 that aren't so dissimilar than what many of you will have read here... but different enough that I felt compelled to remove what is currently available on Royal Road. I'm a bit embarrassed by the current state of the story here, to be honest.

As for the future of this story, I find myself unsure. As I said earlier, I would absolutely love for writing to be a full time career, and, to be blunt, whether I devote the majority of my attention to this story depends on how much it makes in the coming months. I plan to self-publish my other stories, Late to the Party and A Glitch in the System, and the same will be true of them. Ya boy has to eat, and I simply don't have the time for passion projects any more in this cost of living crisis. It sucks and I hate that it has to be this way, but it's the truth.

So if you enjoyed this story and want to help me out/increase the chances of it continuing, I'd greatly appreciate it if you'd hop over to Amazon/Audible and leave a review. Amazon's algorithms are witchcraft to me, but apparently that would help out a tonne.

Thanks, guys, and I'm sorry if any of you are disappointed in my decision here. I'd understand if you want to unfollow, but if you're still interested in updates, stick around; I'll be posting here in future to let you know how things are going.
