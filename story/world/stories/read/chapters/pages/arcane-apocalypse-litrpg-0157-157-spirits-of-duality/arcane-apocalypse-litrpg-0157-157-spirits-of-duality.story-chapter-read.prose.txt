Keep calm, keep the mana flowing, and don’t resist the likely incredibly uncomfortable process.

Dark energy tinged with crimson, reminiscent of blood, flowed from the Slaughter crystal, spreading through the lines connected to the bowl, overpowering the natural bluish hue of the mana originating from the twelve crystals. Her reaction was almost visceral, a deep need to rid herself of this alien thing defiling her spirit, surging forth. Her mana buzzed despite her vice-like grip on it, willing, eager to take on the Aspect of Chaos and tear through these invaders.

It was the only thing she could do to stop herself from focusing on the way something started to grow on the disgusting parasite clinging to her soul.

Her Spirit had to realise that the pseudo-spirits weren’t harmful with time, right? Right. Surely. Mia just had to internalise her logic … or something.

SLEEP! You idiot!
