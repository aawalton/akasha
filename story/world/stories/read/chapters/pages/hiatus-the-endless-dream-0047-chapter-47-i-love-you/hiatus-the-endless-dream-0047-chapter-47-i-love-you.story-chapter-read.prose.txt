A smile touched my lips as the front door of the liminal space was flung open. I touched up my makeup as I passed by one of the mirrors. Fluffed up my curled hair a little too. Giving the mirror a twirl, I let out a little shout of joy before clearing my throat and stepping through the barrier.

Right away, I felt the burning light of the moon on my skin, and my previous joy vanished as I entered the smudged hellscape which had once been this Instance. My eyes squinted as I did another turn to see where the hell I was. The large stone spire stood proud behind me as if a finger grabbing for the moon.

That was the least interesting thing.

I reached out, grabbing the metal tool and feeling its weight, "Does our dear System want him to lose here too? How callous." They wouldn't answer, never would inside of an Instance. Not that I doubted they could, but they were principled with their arbitrary rules if nothing else, except when it came to my Dorian. They treated him like a project. Disgusting. It's why holding Peeler in my hands filled me with loathing.

I would've preferred happening upon it myself instead of being spawned in with it in my waiting arms.

I let out a huff of air. My heightened Continuity would let me stay in the miasmatic light longer, but not forever. I glanced further up the spire and saw a cavern entrance. That would do nicely for now. With three strides, I crossed the broken space, hunkered down, and waited for my Dorian to arrive at the spire. It was the only place he would go.

I waited.

And waited.

And waited.

Three minutes passed, and I had not seen any signs of him from my vantage point. It was agony. Torturous having to wait longer, even though I was already here. I thought about using my copy of [Open the Path] to teleport to him. It would make quite the first impression.

The thought brought me some amount of glee as I thought about opening the portal … nothing. A growl bounced around in my throat as I threw my arms up. "Oh come on. It's related to making him lose. The faster he gets here the faster I can kill him. That's my sincere belief. Come on!"

No response.

I waited longer.

Another minute.

I stood and started pacing.

Another minute.

I slammed Peeler into the cavern wall, and large chips of stone broke away.

I kept pacing.

Another minute.

Two.

My foot impacted one of the stone shards, sending it tumbling down the spire. I turned back to the wall and glared at it like it had tried to attack me. It gave me an idea. Taking Peeler, I pressed it against the wall and began slowly chipping away at portions of the wall. It was more scoring it than anything else, but it was enough for rudimentary drawings.

It held my attention for another three minutes. It was just enough for me to spot a void in space approaching the spire. I stepped out into the mouth of the cave and stared at him. I swung Peeler over my shoulder. The ball of Void stilled, and I knew that he saw it. I waved at him, heart thumping as I stepped back into the cave.

Damn. I was so cool for that.

Waiting this time was bearable because I knew there was no way my invitation would be denied, not as long as I held Peeler. Sure enough, less than a minute later, the panting form of Dorian stepped into the cave with me. "Who the hell are you?" he rasped. I studied his face, and my heart broke a little bit. He looked so tired. I needed him to lose. It was my condition for being his Stalker, but that didn't mean I needed to make things harder on him.

"I suppose you weren't expecting a human stalker? Nice to meet you Dorian. You can call me Ruby." I saw him flinch back, disoriented by my cordiality, before narrowing his eyes in distrust. Naturally, I also had his [Untrustworthy] Restriction much to my displeasure. I should …

"You really ought to get rid of [Untrustworthy] after this Instance. I'm stuck with your kit too, you know?"

In turn, he didn't say anything, his eyes lasered in on the crowbar he loved so much. I sighed. "I'll give it back if you play a game with me. Simple coin game, heads or tails." I moved Peeler behind my back, forcing him to pay attention to me. "We'll do as many rounds as both parties please. If I win I get to touch you for a moment. If you win you can ask me any question or bank it. I'll return Peeler for three questions."

"Alright. I'll answer three questions for the crowbar." He said.

I rolled my eyes, "Clearly not what I meant, Dorian."

"Do you accept then?" he challenged.

"You know I've been watching you this entire time right? I know everything already."

"I don't think you've been reading my mind."

Who he would no doubt become, certainly, but not as he was. "Can I bank them for later?" I asked, "We both have to answer honestly by the way. Defeats the purpose if we lie."
