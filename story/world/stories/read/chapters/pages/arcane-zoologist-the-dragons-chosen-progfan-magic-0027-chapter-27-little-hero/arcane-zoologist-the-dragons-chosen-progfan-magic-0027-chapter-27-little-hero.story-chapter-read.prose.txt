Callen couldn't remain still. He kept pacing around the kitchen, peeking out the front window once he went past it, and then peeking out the back window as he paced past it. There was a lot of shouting going on in the village, and occasionally he would hear the hoot of the glauxis.

Each hoot felt like a piece of metal in his heart, because it meant that it hadn't left yet. Had Mom and Callie just not gotten to the egg yet? Were they on their way to the glauxis now? Or had the monster become enraged at seeing its egg in human hands and attacked?

He had no clue what was going on, and it was killing him. His mom and his precious little sister were out there, in mortal danger, yet he was pacing inside the safety of his home, unable to do anything.

"Dad!" Callen whined, looking at his sullen father.

He was sitting at the kitchen table, his chin resting on his hands. A picture of calm, if it weren't for his leg, which he was tapping so quickly and so nervously that even the floorboards were squeaking.

"You have to wait!" Dad said sternly without looking at Callen. It was almost as if he was trying to convince himself rather than Callen.

Callen resumed pacing around the room. This was agony. This was not how this evening was supposed to go. He should have been preparing his travel bag to go to town with Silas. He should have been telling this news to Callie and trying to convince her not to search for the egg while he was gone.

Yet glauxis was here, and Callie had somehow managed to find the egg together with that nasty brat Tristan. How he hated that boy! It was clear to him that he liked Callie, yet he had spent the longest time trying to get her attention by bullying her.

Now that the brat realized that Callie didn't like that, he's trying to actually help her. But Callen didn't want that brat anywhere near his precious sister. No, no, no! She deserved only the best. Frankly, Callen didn't believe any of the brats in the village would be a good match for Callie when they grew up.

Another hoot, longer this time, and followed by nervous shouts of the villagers. Among them, Callen also heard a female voice. Was that Mom?

"DAD!" Callen looked at his father.

Dad stood up abruptly, knocking the wooden chair down on the ground. "We're going!"

Callen nodded. It seems like Dad had been agonizing over it just as much as Callen, if not more. He may not be a trained fighter, but even so, Dad was by no means a slouch. Working in the forge day in and day out was tough work, requiring a lot of strength. Not to mention that Dad knew the intricacies of weapons better than anyone, having built them himself.

While Callen wasn't as strong as Dad, he had trained a lot with Teacher Blackthorn. He could at the very least protect Callie.

Dad grabbed his axe, a weapon he usually kept hidden deep in the storage, while Callen grabbed his training sword. It wasn't a real weapon per se, but it was better than nothing. A strike with it would still hurt.

Together, both men rushed out of the house, heading towards the direction of the hoots. Soon, they saw the hunters and other able-bodied fighters. Archers were firing arrows at the monster, which was flying in the sky. But it was far too agile—their arrows didn't even come close.

Callen quickly realized just how useless he was against this flying opponent. The glauxis would occasionally swoop down, emitting one of his loud hoots and forcing the fighters to retreat behind their shield or nearby buildings for safety.

Then Mom appeared. She was clutching a large, white egg in her hands.

The glauxis went into rage the moment it saw Mom, letting out its loudest scream yet, and then dove straight at Mom, its claws outstretched. She tried to reach for her sword to defend herself, but the monster knocked her to the ground before she got it.

"MOM!"

"MAEVE!"

Callen and Dad both rushed at the glauxis, their weapons raised high. But they knew they wouldn't make it. The monster was right in front of her, its huge paw with four huge, sharp talons pulled back for a strike. Callen felt that his heart would stop.

Not his mom. Not his MOM!

But someone managed to get to Mom before the glauxis brought down its deadly strike. A mop of ginger hair fearlessly inserted herself right between Mom and the monster.

"Don't hurt my mom!" Callie screamed at the glauxis.

The whole village went silent. They likely expected that Callie would be mauled to pieces at the moment. Callen stopped his advance and hurriedly grabbed Dad, who was the only one still moving forward.

Dad looked at him in outrage. "Callen, what are—"

"Watch…" Callen said quietly, turning his gaze to Callie, the glauxis, and Mom.

"We're sorry your egg was taken. We have come to return it. Please, take it and don't hurt anyone," Callie said and gingerly took the egg, which Mom had been clutching in her hands.

In a daze, Mom released the egg, and Callie offered it glauxis. "It is unharmed. I can feel your baby moving around."

Callen wasn't quite as surprised as the rest of the villagers when the glauxis stepped back. But it was still an unbelievable sight. Callie placed the egg gingerly in front of the glauxis. The whole village gasped when it saw it bend down and… nuzzle gently against the egg.

Then it did something nobody had expected to see. The glauxis bowed its head at Callie.

A wide grin appeared on her face, and she said, "You're welcome!"

Glauxis then grabbed its egg with its front paws, opened its massive wings, and disappeared into the night before anyone had even realized what had happened. Everyone was staring as the monster, no, the arcane beast, flew off. But Callen couldn't help but fear—had anyone noticed the gentle glow coming from beneath Callie's tunic?

Callie stared in wonder as the glauxis flew off. He had bowed to her. She had never thought that she would feel so honored and thrilled over an arcane beast bowing to her. Okay, actually, she knew she would have been thrilled at the prospect, but she just didn't imagine that could ever happen.

Yet it had said, "I thank you for your efforts, child. I shall not bother your village any longer", bowed and then left. All of her efforts to search for the egg had paid off, because nobody in the village had been hurt. It had come mighty close.

It took a moment for Callie to realize that the whole village, all the fighters that came outside at least, were staring at her. Chief Aemon was also among them. He was still holding a sword in his hand, and his expression was… Well, Callie didn't know quite what it was, but she felt that the deep furrow of his brow and slack-jawed mouth was not a good sign.

"What just happened?" The chief stepped closer to Callie, sword still in hand, as he occasionally glanced at the sky. As if worried that the glauxis might still return.

But before she could open her mouth, Mom's arm was already wrapped around her, and she had been pulled to stand behind her.

"We returned the egg to the monster, that's it," Mom said, albeit her voice sounded a bit shaky.

"Arcane beast," Callie dared to quietly correct her.

"What? Oh, yes. Arcane beast," Mom agreed. Callie felt incredibly proud at that moment. She had just shown the whole village that arcane beasts weren't just mindless monsters and murderers. That killing them in cold blood wasn't right.

Chief Aemon waved his hand in the air, gesturing aggressively. "Arcane beast? What are you talking about, Maeve? Wh-What even was all that? Where did you get that egg? Was that Calluna's doing again?"

His voice seemed to grow angrier with each question, so Callie shrank back behind Mom.

"Aemon. Not now. It's already nighttime. Callie is tired, as are all the other villagers. The threat is gone, so I suggest we all head to sleep for tonight and figure out the whys and hows tomorrow," Mom said, her voice regaining the usual calm confidence it carried.

"She's right! It's been a long day. The most important thing is that nobody got hurt," Dad hurriedly joined Mom's side, as did Callen.

Callie was effectively body-blocked from the rest of the village by her family. Had she done something bad again? Why did the villagers look so mad?

No, of course they wouldn't understand right away. It was almost a certainty that Chief Aemon would demand some punishment again. But her family was standing firmly in front of her, protecting her, so she didn't feel that scared.

"I…" the chief began, then rubbed the bridge of his nose. "You're right."

He turned around and addressed the villagers. "The glauxis is gone. Two sentries are to remain up at night and keep watch. The rest of you—head home. We'll figure out what happened tomorrow."

Callie could feel the villagers staring at her. She could feel the many questions they had. But her family hurriedly led her home, pushing anyone who dared to come too close out of the way and promising to answer tomorrow. There was a twinge of nervousness in her when they finally got home.

"D-Did I do something wrong?" Callie asked.

Mom looked at her sternly at first, her eyebrows furrowing, but her expression quickly softened, and she pulled Callie into a tight hug. "You silly child, there are lots of things you did wrong. So many things I'm angry about. But you saved me and the whole village."

"I've been trying to do that for a while now," Callie said, returning the hug and enjoying Mom's warmth.

"Oh, we're going to have a very long talk about that tomorrow!" Dad grumbled, then crouched and hugged Callie and Mom both. "But tonight, I'm just going to say thank you."

Callen joined the group hug, giving everyone a hearty squeeze. "As long as everyone is safe, that's all that matters."

They remained that way for a while, until finally Callie was released from the embrace and ushered to go to sleep. She knew that she was in trouble. Tomorrow, Callie would have to explain everything that had happened to her parents.

The mark, the dragon too, since the two were connected. Her meeting with the glauxis. Perhaps, she could keep Wiggles and Snowglow hidden a little while longer. But magic would definitely require a proper explanation.

Actually, speaking of magic. Callie rolled up the sleeve of her tunic and saw that a third petal had appeared there. She had felt something happen when the glauxis bowed to her, but to think that it was another petal. This basically proved that her magic, the petals that she grew, were closely linked with her relationship with the arcane beast.

The more arcane beasts she understood, the more petals she would grow, which likely would also strengthen her magic. Although Callie believed that there were still a lot of things she didn't understand about the glauxis, she still eagerly wrote down what she knew.

Name: Glauxis

Locations:

Far north

Appearance:

Large lynx-like creature with white fur, owl head, and large wings

Abilities:

Poison claws

Flight

Notes:

Egg stolen returned; left the village; most caring parent

Callie fell asleep, holding the paper in her hands and content that the worst-case scenario had been avoided. But there was one question that plagued the back of her mind. Who had been the thief?
