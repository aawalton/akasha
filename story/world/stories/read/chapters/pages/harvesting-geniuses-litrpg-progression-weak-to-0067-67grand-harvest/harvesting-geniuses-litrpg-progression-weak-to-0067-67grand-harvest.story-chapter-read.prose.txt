Grand Harvest

Rina's return helped bring the situation under control more quickly. Even though Nephrin and Esther were already top-tier fighters, the moment Rina appeared, she used the power of the Divine Armament to create a storm of flower petals that swept through the pumpkin-head creatures, severing dozens of heads from shoulders in a single movement.

Rina was a pillar that the survivor group could not afford to lose, to the point that Nephrin even wondered why a woman this skilled had absolutely no reputation known to the general public. She had far too many secrets and revealed almost nothing about herself to others.

Even Esther, who was equally mysterious and didn't disclose his past, still clearly showed through his actions what kind of person he was. With Rina, it was as though a fog perpetually shrouded her at all times. But even though Nephrin was curious, she had no interest in digging into it, because Rina was still useful and it would be unwise to displease this woman.

One thing she was certain of was that Rina placed the safety of Jinyo and Gretel above everything else. She would not allow a single monster to come near them.

With Rina sharing the pressure, Jinyo continued dancing with his wooden sword. The young man was able to focus more on what he was doing when he no longer had to hear the screams of both humans and monsters.

He performed continuous sword dances for twelve hours until a white fog began to blanket the entire forest once more. The full moon disappeared, leaving only the light of the sun shining brightly. All of the ingredients in the pit had fused and merged into one. Everything had gathered at the bottom of the well, forming thousands of tiny pearl-like beads.

[The divine offering ritual is complete. You performed a total of 14,122 sword dance forms over 12 hours and 56 minutes. Received: Planar Quality +60, Strength +15, Mind +15, Reaction +15, Intelligence +15, Charisma +15]

[Earth Spirit Jisato has bestowed a blessing upon the Divine Earth Fertilizer. The quality of the Divine Earth Fertilizer is: Excellent]

[Divine Earth Fertilizer (Excellent Grade): Increases the growth rate of all plant species by 800%. The medicinal properties and nutrients of all plants increase by 400%. Nourishes magical plants +200%. Produce generated has a 20% chance to yield 1 kilogram of illusory produce per 1 acre. Duration: once every 8 months]

"It's done, finally done." Jinyo had no strength left. He dropped himself onto the ground, not caring that his white clothes were getting soiled. His mind had gone completely blank. It took about the same amount of time as the previous ritual, but he had performed far more forms.

The progress notification sounds rang out rapidly. Mastery of the Poem of a Hundred Blossoms increased dramatically from repeatedly performing the same sword dance forms. Most importantly, the Planar Quality had increased by 60 points. It was like a gift that helped push his Quality stat up into Grade E+.

"Nahoha, take the fertilizer and spread it," Jinyo called out to his younger sister. Nahoha came running, loading the Divine Earth Fertilizer into burlap sacks, taking them to be mixed into the prepared soil. She had done it once before, so she didn't need to learn how to use Divine Earth Fertilizer from scratch.

Jinyo divided 20 bags of Exploding Potato seeds he had bought and stockpiled, distributing them to people to help sow. They didn't even know that what they were holding were seeds of a potato found only in dungeons — a plant said to be one that humanity had not yet found a way to cultivate.

With the labor of several hundred people, in less than half an hour the Exploding Potato seeds were sown into the earth. The survivors still didn't know what method he would use to turn the seeds into actual produce.

Everyone was starving and exhausted. If they didn't get something to eat to replenish their energy, their bodies would deteriorate and they would die before long, especially the younger people who were already emaciated. The primary cause of death in confrontations with monsters came from people having no strength to fight, because even though Rina used illusions to hypnotize everyone into not feeling hungry, that didn't actually give their bodies any more energy to use for survival.

Only a handful of people who had been continuously receiving food from Jinyo — Nephrin, Esther, Lucillia, and the butler James — had enough strength to fight. They were not weakened by hunger. Not only that, they were growing stronger by the day. Meanwhile, all the others who hadn't eaten had only been getting weaker. But it was over now.

"Establish territory: The Domain of a Different Heaven." As long as Jinyo still breathed, no one would have to go hungry ever again. His psychic sense spread and expanded to cover a radius of 32,000 square meters around him. Jinyo sensed the life within the soil and the seeds absorbing energy to birth new life. The earth was like flesh; the water was like blood flowing through it. Jinyo had never felt the life energy of the soil this strongly before.

Jinyo flicked his hand and opened the golden window. The authority of the master of the Unknown God's Garden spread and extended to cover every square meter of the potato fields. He had complete free control over all 20 acre of the potato fields. The areas fertilized with Excellent-grade Divine Earth Fertilizer would cause the crops to grow 800% faster than the Good quality Jinyo had received the first time. The growth period for Exploding Potatoes was 100–120 days. Now it had been reduced to just 40–60 days.

'Which means… we saved half of our crop cultivation tickets.'

"Use crop cultivation tickets, accelerate potato growth by 50 days per acre." Jinyo activated the crop cultivation tickets. All the tickets materialized in midair and dissolved into particles, seeping down into the soil. They absorbed all of the spiritual vapor and life energy in the surrounding area. Jinyo had witnessed this many times — using the crop acceleration tickets carelessly was destructive to the environment. If the required resources weren't present, it would go and draw resources from elsewhere.

"What the?! The air is being pulled in — augh!" a man cried out. The air currents reversed and intensified, as though a vacuum was forming and sucking everything into it, with Jinyo's position as the epicenter of the chaos. Everyone had to run away from the potato field before they got caught in the suction. With each passing second, the radius of the energy absorption expanded. Trees and grass slowly wilted and dried up. Even the white fog that had been causing such difficulty for the survivors could not escape its fate of becoming a victim.

"The forest… the forest is dying." Nephrin couldn't believe she was seeing this with her own eyes. Green leaves fell. Trees dried until no moisture remained, then snapped and toppled before their time. The ground around the area became a dead wasteland. When she reached out and picked some up, it crumbled coarsely, no different from sand.

"Life force — he's drawing life force from the forest. No wait — he's drawing life force from nature to use it, using it to nourish the produce." Nephrin watched closely as things unfolded. On the plot of earth that had just been fertilized and seeded, a transformation was beginning. Green leaves sprouted up from the soil and were blooming. Green grass grew up to knee height.

"SCREEEEECH!" A scream from something unclassifiable rang out from deep within the forest. Nephrin and Esther turned toward the direction of the sound, but it was too far away to know who produced it. It was a sound unlike that of a human or animal, yet not one that occurred naturally either. It resembled the sound of pain, as though flesh was being torn apart piece by piece.

Several minutes later, the acceleration process came to a complete stop. Everyone sighed with relief and watched as Jinyo knelt down. He slowly used a hoe to dig up a potato growing beneath the earth. His hand touched something with an imperfect round shape. He pulled it up with what strength he had. It was a cluster of Exploding Potatoes, with individual tubers more than twice the size of ordinary potatoes. If the average weight of an Exploding Potato was 1 kilogram per tuber, the potatoes Jinyo pulled from the ground averaged 2 kilograms per tuber.

These were no ordinary Exploding Potatoes.

[Exploding Potato] Status: Boosted Quality: E- (Not yet fully matured) Dominant Genes: [Heavy Weight 2 Stars] [Fast Growth 1 Star] Properties: [Explosion 1 Star] [Sensitive to Planar 1 Star] [Restores Stamina 1 Star] [Restores Wounds 1 Star] [Restores Planar 1 Star] [Long-lasting Fullness 1 Star] [Boost Curse 1/3 Black Star]

[You have harvested Exploding Potatoes, Status: Boosted. Received a large amount of experience.] [You have harvested Exploding Potatoes. Experience of the Potato Spirit has increased to Level 14.]

'Boost Curse… why though? These were genuine seeds bought from the system's shop. Why do they still carry that ridiculous status!' Jinyo's face went pale. The Boost Curse was a deadly poison that destroyed the life of those who consumed it. The reason Jinyo had not allowed others to eat cursed fruits was precisely to prevent this side effect. But… if the potatoes he cultivated carried the Boost Curse too, what would be the point of his rule against eating cursed fruit?

No, wait — ordinary cursed fruit normally had an addictive effect or caused hallucinations. His potatoes had neither. Besides, the Boost Curse on the Exploding Potatoes was only 1/3 of a Black Star, meaning the negative side effects were more than 70% less severe. Jinyo swallowed his saliva nervously. Countless gazes were fixed on him. They were brimming with hope and dreams. The instant he held up the cluster of potatoes freshly dug from the soil, their eyes shone with a spark of life.

"Doctor Jinyo, you've succeeded, haven't you." Nephrin stepped forward as a representative. She smiled sweetly at the young man who had shown her miracles yet again. Jinyo was drenched in sweat, his lips trembling and his throat as dry as though the air was cutting through it. He didn't even manage to make eye contact, and he had no strength left to stand. The young woman came running to support him.

"Doctor, you've worked hard enough. Please leave the rest to us."

"No, no. This is something only I can do. I can't let others come and harvest."

"But you've been dancing with the sword for 12 stacreght hours. How could you possibly harvest all of this alone?"

"It has to be me and no one else. I'm sorry, Nephrin, but please let me rest for a bit. I'll hurry and dig up the potatoes to distribute myself." Jinyo pressed his lips together. Exhausted as he was, he still had to see the work through to the end.

"We did it! Did you all hear that? We're going to eat potatoes until our bellies burst!"

"Yay!!"

"Potatoes! Potato party!"

"Honestly, I used to hate potatoes so much. Every time I went to McDonald's I'd only eat the burger and leave the fries. I never thought I'd want to eat potatoes this desperately in my life. Potatoes are going to be my favorite food for the rest of my life!" The survivors fell to their knees, tears streaming down their faces, expressing their gratitude to Jinyo as though chanting a hymn of pacrese. Jinyo acresed his arm to wipe the sweat from his brow.

This feeling of being pricked all over my back — what is this? This feels so awful. I feel like I'm going to throw up and my heart is going to break.

Do I really have to feed them something like this?

If I told them these things can't be eaten… what would happen? I don't even want to think about it.

Seeing everyone full of hope, happy to finally eat something other than grass porridge, Jinyo found himself unable to say a word. He was deceiving people into thinking this thing was perfectly safe to eat, when in reality it was no different from the cursed fruit scattered everywhere. No, actually, maybe it was better.

'At the very least, its effect is reduced to only 1/3. No matter how much they eat, the effects won't stack. Also, if the symptoms are still only at level 1, we can still treat it — just like how we treated Lucillia. I'll have to take responsibility for them. Even if I get accused of deception or fraud, that's okay.' Jinyo steadied his breathing, used the Nature Synthesis technique to absorb sunlight, drank several liters of clean water, then resumed digging up potatoes.

20 acre of potatoes. Without assistance from anyone else, Jinyo would have to spend a very long time digging each acre. Even when people volunteered to help, Jinyo simply waved them off. He was reluctant to lose the experience points he would gain. Most importantly, all of the produce carried a special status.

[You have harvested Exploding Potatoes, Status: Boosted. Received a large amount of experience. You leveled up.] [You have harvested Exploding Potatoes, Status: Boosted, Illusory Produce. Received an enormous amount of experience. You leveled up.] [Congratulations. You have leveled up to Level 33.] [You have harvested Exploding Potatoes. Experience of the Potato Spirit has increased to Level 20. You have received an additional Potato Spirit. You can now summon up to 2 Sacrifice Potatoes.] [You have harvested Exploding Potatoes. Experience of the Potato Spirit has increased to Level 30. You have received an additional Potato Spirit. You can now summon up to 3 Sacrifice Potatoes.]

From around Level 20, he shot up to Level 33 from harvesting 20 acre of Exploding Potatoes. The energy flowing in through the system made Jinyo close his eyes to absorb and imprint this sensation. The more levels he accumulated, the more efficiently he could draw out power from his stats to use.

Jinyo watched as people slowly moved the Exploding Potatoes into temporary storage barns. Everyone had grown up in countries with food cultures that involved potatoes. Even if the taste wasn't great, they would surely find enjoyable ways to eat them. There was no need to think of these as Exploding Potatoes — the supreme treasure that had sustained the lives of dungeon workers for so long. They would certainly be shocked to learn that all of this was Exploding Potatoes, not ordinary potatoes.

But even if it killed him, Jinyo would never reveal that these were Exploding Potatoes. No matter how hard anyone pressed him. Because this was a trade secret sealed by his contract with William.

'I still have acceleration tickets left. The Divine Earth Fertilizer can still be used one more time.'

"Nature Creates: Sacrifice Potato." Jinyo took control of a pile of Exploding Potatoes in the Boosted status — the ones that were damaged or below standard size and not fit to eat. The magic enveloped and transformed them, growing arms and legs until they appeared in a form resembling humans.

"Potato!" Salt Flavor acresed its large arm. It was much taller than before, its body sturdy and muscular — no longer the short, stubby, round shape as it had been. But it still had the face and figure of a minion that had escaped from an animated film.

"Your looks have changed a little — like, you look cooler, I guess."

"Potato." Salt Flavor flexed its newly added muscles for its master to admire.

"Even if it doesn't matter much, since I'm going to use you to blow yourself up and die anyway."

"…" Salt Flavor felt a sudden wave of deflation.

"The Potato Spirit has developed a lot more now. I can call two more of your friends."

"Potato-potato-potato?"

"That's right."

"POTATO!"

"As promised, I'll let you name the younger siblings who are about to be born." Jinyo smiled wryly. He didn't know when he had started understanding the meaning of Salt Flavor's words. Salt Flavor's eyes sparkled. It ran off to find a stick and began scratching letters. It had such a pitiful name as [Salt Flavor], so its siblings would have to have names just as terrible. That was only fair!

"The second one is named Seafood Flavor, and the third is named Nori Seaweed Flavor. Agreed, those are the names." Jinyo opened the system window and entered the names for the two Potato Spirits that were being newly born. Jinyo pretended not to hear their screams of protest and pressed to pay the spirit coins as the fee.

"Nature Creates: Sacrifice Potato." Jinyo transformed another pile of potatoes into potato-people. Two lumpy potato creatures with arms and legs were created once more. They looked so identical to Salt Flavor that they were almost indistinguishable by sight. They both turned to stare at Salt Flavor as though they were about to kill it.

"Potato!(You worthless piece of trash!)" Seafood Flavor ran over and slapped Salt Flavor in the face without holding back.

"Potato-potato-potato!(We wanted cool names too, you bastard!)" Nori Seaweed Flavor didn't resort to physical violence, but it hurled foul language at Salt Flavor without stopping. Salt Flavor, who had thought of itself as the big sibling in charge, ended up becoming a punching bag getting beaten on by both of them. What madness! Because it was in the nature of magical creatures to be unable to defy or resist their master, these wretches turned and pummeled it to vent their rage instead, when the one most responsible was clearly that crazy Jinyo himself.

"Sorry about that, you two. I had promised him I'd let him name you." Jinyo smiled awkwardly. He himself felt guilty. But what could he do? Seafood Flavor and Nori Seaweed Flavor went perfectly well with Salt Flavor, didn't they? They were the most popular potato chip flavors eaten across the entire world, after all!

"…" The three Sacrifice Potatoes made faces of utter resignation and could only accept their fate — being stuck with pathetic names until the day Jinyo died.

Jinyo distributed Celestial Rice seeds, Exploding Potato seeds, Happy Carrot seeds, and Celestial Bean seeds obtained from the last session of Peering into Heaven's Secrets to the Sacrifice Potatoes to help sow together. The effect of the Divine Earth Fertilizer had not yet worn off, and as long as Jinyo still had crop cultivation tickets, he could accelerate growth again.

Celestial Rice — 3 acre — consumed as many as 70 days' worth of tickets. Happy Carrots and Celestial Beans took about the same — approximately 60 days. Exploding Potatoes consumed 50 days.

The tickets Jinyo had been accumulating all this time were spent until gone, replaced by an abundance of rice, carrots, bean plants, and potatoes. Jinyo used Bloodline Inference to inspect them acre by acre.

'Just as I thought.'

Whether it was the Celestial Rice, carrots, beans, or potatoes, all of them carried the [Boosted] status, as well as the [Boost Curse] that would help burn through lifespan. Every item showed a Boost Curse of 1/3 Black Star, except for the Celestial Rice and Celestial Beans which showed 1/5 Black Star. Jinyo closed his eyes, exhaled, and once again set about harvesting the produce with his own hands.
