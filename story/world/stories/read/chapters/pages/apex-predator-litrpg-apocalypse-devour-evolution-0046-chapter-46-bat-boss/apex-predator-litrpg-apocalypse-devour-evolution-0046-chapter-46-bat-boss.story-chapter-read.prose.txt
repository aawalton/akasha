When Don entered through the doorway, it immediately iced shut. Ice literally materialized to block his escape.

Great. Dramatics. I love it.

Don looked around in the pitch black cavern. It was massive for one thing, but that wasn’t its most distinctive feature.

The whole damn room went up.

The cavern wasn’t horizontal like you’d expect. It was a vertical shaft, maybe a hundred feet across and way, way taller than it had any right to be. The walls were jagged with ice and stalactites, like a frozen throat. Way up top, barely visible even with Night Vision, was a narrow, circular ledge—like a roost.

And all the way up there, perched like some smug death pigeon, was the boss. Don couldn’t make out more than a shadowy outline. But it was large and it was watching Don.

The air was colder here. Thinner too. Every breath scratched at the lungs like sandpaper. But what really stood out were the bridges. Dozens of them, spiraling around the inside of the shaft, connecting narrow outcroppings and crumbling ledges. Zig-zagging across at different heights. Some were stone. Some were ice. Just bare thin ledges with no railing on either side.

It was like a vertical maze built by something that hated people.

Perfect terrain for a flying bastard. Don could already tell the thing was going to stay in the air, swoop at him, then vanish up into its little perch. Made sense. It owned this space.

Don looked down from the platform. Pitch black darkness for hundreds of feet. Who knew how deep this abyss was. Falling there would mean certain death.

The first thing before Don moved a single muscle was obvious. He needed to upgrade his Night Vision immediately. It was costly, at 720 Evolution Points, but he absolutely needed it. It was pitch black in the cavern and he only had his vision. The Echo Location would have been a fine choice, if it wasn’t for the sonic attacks. Don was sure the boss was a giant bat, vastly more dangerous than the mooks in the cavern behind him. It would attack with sound, and Don needed to block his ears with his psychics.

So he had to rely on his vision.

[720 Evolution Points spent]

[Evolution Upgraded!]

[Night Vision (D-grade)] -> [Night Vision (C-grade)]

[Night Vision (C-grade)]: Your darkvision has significantly improved. You no longer need external light to see in the darkness. Your ocular tissue has transformed into that of a true Evolutor. [+6 to Mental]

It helped. The Mental boost was actually pretty nice. Don hadn’t expected it to double, but it made sense, since the evolution was already at C-grade.

His darkvision was now approximately five hundred feet with clear vision of details, and a thousand feet with a little bit blurrier outline. Don craned his neck and looked up. He couldn’t even see the ceiling.

But now he could see the boss. It was perched a hundred yards in the air on a little platform. Out of habit, he used Identify.

[Snarlak, The Black Scream, Level 15]

A massive hunched black bat, with similar features to the lesser bats Don had fought. Brutal talons, muscular legs, giant wings with a span of probably fifteen feet. With its mass and wingspan it could reach absolutely terrifying speeds. And the head was large, full of nasty teeth that all dripped with black goop.

It was watching Don with its head tilted, clearly waiting. Don looked around. The only way forward was the bridge. It was four feet wide and the moment Don would step on it, he would be completely exposed to attacks with very little room to maneuver.

Don took the scene in until he understood the room.

He would have to climb, and Snarlak would attack him. And the higher he went, so would the boss.

It's going to try to drop me into the abyss. And if that doesn’t work, it will tire me out.

Don had two jobs. The first job was simple, but also the hardest. Stay on the bridges. If he fell down that was game over. The second job was to keep the bat pinned down if he managed to grab it.

Don took a deep breath. The bridge was maybe a hundred and twenty yards across. He would have to sprint like a motherfucker. Fighting on this bridge was absolutely not an option.

Gathering his full strength, Don used Sprint.

Instantly, the cavern answered. A deafening screech echoed from above, sharp and high like shrapnel in his brain. Don immediately took a few percentage points of damage, and he stumbled, and almost slid off the damn bridge. He activated the vacuum bubble around his head. Muffled silence. The screech still shook the bridge beneath his feet, but he wasn’t badly disoriented. However, he could feel the sonic waves pounding on his psychic bubble. He would have to breathe soon.

Then came the wind.

Don saw the movement before his Survival Instincs blared up. A blur in the shadows—fifteen-foot leather wings flared, talons stretched forward, the full mass of Snarlak diving straight for him.

Don bolted. Full speed. The bridge rattled under each step. He kept his arms wide behind him for balance, his weight centered in a forward hunch. No room to dodge. No room to stumble.

Snarlak was fast. Faster than anything he’d fought. And it was deadly accurate, as it was coming straight at him.

Don waited for the moment. Just before impact, he dropped low and slid. Skidding on the slick icy bridge, Don shot forward, feet first.

Snarlak missed.

The boss bat slammed into the bridge behind him with an impact that cracked stone. Chunks splintered off and dropped into the dark. Don didn’t stop to watch. He was already on the second platform. The second bridge was wider and made of solid stone. But it was steep and long.

Don pushed hesitation aside. He had to move now when Snarlak was not in the air yet.

Don locked eyes with the monster. It was filled with rage, something black frothing at the mouth.

Snarlak screamed again. The massive wings beat and tossed rubble and ice into the abyss as it rose back into the air.

As Don ran up the steep bridge, Snarlak darted past him. Its brutal talons crunched into the bridge, collapsing half of it. Don risked it, and removed the vacuum bubble. He breathed in that sweet sweet air. He would need it for this. The terrible monster screeched again, but it was for echolocation, not attacking.

Don leveraged his Sprinting and augmented his speed by pushing himself forward with Telekinesis. The bridge before him started to crumble away.

Don vaulted up in the air. His leap took him ten yards forward and he managed to land safely on the other side. He ran towards the platform as the bridge behind him crumbled into nothing.

Don landed in a forward roll, came up running, and made it onto the next platform.

It was a slightly larger one, the floor mirror-clear ice. It was just as slippery as Don had feared. That was annoying. It meant he wouldn’t get a good sprint off. At least the next bridge was level. But it was made of the same crystal ice. The distance was manageable, only forty yards or so to the next platform. But if Don just ran straight ahead, that was a great way of getting killed stupidly.

He looked up at Snarlak. The giant bat was back on its perch. It spread its wings and screeched at him. The distance was large, but Don still needed to erect a psychic vacuum to shield his ears from the attack.

Don screamed back at him. There was nothing elegant about it. This was not an attack. This was a fuck you straight back at the bastard.

Snarlak tilted its head. Don could tell it was fucking pissed. The giant bastard twitched like it wanted to tear the air apart. It hadn’t expected someone to dodge its talons. It definitely hadn’t expected to get screamed at. It looked down with something close to rabid fury. Like it was thinking: how dare you. Then it screeched again and launched itself into a dive.

It tucked its wings against its body and divebombed at Don again. It was going to crash into the platform.

Don readied himself. This would end badly for one of them.
