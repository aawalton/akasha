Hey, y'all. Here's an update with me.

Overpowered Wizard 3 is out on Amazon now for eBook. The audiobook is going to take a little longer, but at least Book 1 and Book 2 of the audiobook is available.

When it comes down to Book 4, I'm slowly working on it and posting it on Patreon. It's been slow going, but I think if I work on it daily, it'll reach it's conclusion and then I'll post it on RR.

With that said, I've transitioned to another story to hold it down for me on Amazon. Path of the Slayer went up on Patreon and then went straight to Amazon. And so far, it's doing well, very, very well.

I've come to realize that I'm probably not going to do anymore new works on RR. For those who've supported and read the story and even left comments or praise, thank you. But I think this platform isn't meant for me anymore. Or maybe it has never been meant for me. All of my stories on RR get torn down and looked at as objective failures, and sometimes I do things to make up for it on RR that doesn't really work for me as a writer.

So I'm going to put more focus on Patreon and Amazon, especially Amazon. I write more naturally when I think of my stories as whole books instead of web serials, and it works out much, much better for me. If you have Kindle Unlimited or just plain kindle, I invite you to try out Path of the Slayer on Amazon.

That's all and thank you!

Path of the Slayer

Blurb:

"They laughed when he got stuck at Rank 0. Then he discovered the hidden path monsters and rankers fear most.

Arden gave everything he had to rise as a guild adventurer, pushing his warrior training and artificer talents to their limits. His dream: becoming the best in the empire. But for four soul-crushing years, his Rank 0 Epiphany remained locked at 99%.

Now, with his dreams fading and health collapsing, Arden undertakes one final quest, a simple goblin extermination in the city’s sewers. A routine mission, until he discovers a rare Hell Portal, the only path to unlock his true potential.

Beyond lies limitless power, many unique paths, and realms stretching far past the borders of his empire, all waiting for him to explore…if Arden can survive the slaughter.

In this System, there can only be one Slayer."
