Aqua

It doesn’t take long before the two of us get within sight of the Hunter’s City. And it really is quite a sight.

The city is just like the other Hunter’s Cities I’ve seen so far, but without a dome. With the city structures within it all being damaged to various degrees.

But the most unusual part of the city isn’t the destruction to the city that’s floating just barely above the ocean floor.

What’s most unusual about it is the enormous amounts of electricity running all throughout the waters around it. Along with the massive turtles spanning between ten to twenty meters just roaming around the city.

Thousands upon thousands of massive electricity coated turtles.

So many of them…

And to top it all off, there’s a single enormous turtle that is over twice the size of the others just sleeping on top of the Hunter’s Guild building at the center of the city.

Putting that thing aside for now, the other shock turtles are mostly hiding in their shells while floating around the city or just on top of the city’s damaged structures. But there are a few with their arms, legs, and head poking out of their shells. And those have glowing greenish blue eyes that are emitting greenish blue electricity.

Also, those very turtles have greenish blue scales on their limbs and head from what I can see. Although of a different shade than their eyes.

Their eyes are a tiny bit more green than blue. Meanwhile their scales are a bit more blue than green.

“This is as far as I can go,” the tour guide says, making me nod my head as I continue swimming towards the fallen Hunter’s City. Leaving him behind.

And when I get close enough, I begin summoning my abyssal servants one after another. Including the enormous one.

Alright. Time to hunt.

All Across the Universe

Trillions of people tune into the broadcast as soon as the shock turtles appear on the screen. With every last one of them hoping for an incredible show.

And they’re not the only ones as Jaleth also watches from a safe distance in the ocean.

But every last person watching shows an equal amount of awe when they see dozens of different abyssal servants appear one after another. Including the enormous dead maw of the sea along with dozens upon dozens of undead all appearing along with it.

Unlike the last time the dead maw of the sea appeared in the ocean though, this time it is summoned at the bottom of the ocean. With its legs all on the ground and its size spanning larger than the city next to it.

That’s not all though as while the large shock turtles are all beginning to wake up and stick their heads out of their shells to look in the direction of the abyssal servants and the massive mouth that appeared next to the city, various other things all begin to happen at once.

Signs of a mental ability’s usage fill the water as the monsters are all coated in a deep bluish purple aura, making their movements all begin to reverse and act strange. Then a bright blue light shines from Aqua that immediately make the turtles all loud out loud hissing grunts as they continue moving in unpredictable manners thanks to Aqua’s reversal skill.

After that, the currents all across the city begin to move in random ways, further disorienting the shock turtles before the water takes on a much deeper hue as the pressure increases.

And that’s the last thing Jaleth sees before a pitch black ink fills the water all around them and the shock turtles all grow even more stunned in the eyes of the viewers watching the livestream. Viewers who can have the livestream show the water without the ink.

The viewers all then watch as the stunned turtles are pushed by the undead forces sent by Aqua off of the city. Where they eventually begin to sink down into the mouth of the dead maw of the sea. Turning into food for the enormous creature.

All while Aqua’s level rapidly begins to rise in the identify results of those watching with Aqua just floating in the distance watching it all happen.

And the undead completely ignore the electricity thanks to their nature as undead.

But by the time a little over half the shock turtles are eliminated, the ink begins to fade, and the effects of the mental attacks disappear as well. Leaving the shock turtles stable enough to defend themselves and swim away from the massive maw.

Meanwhile Jaleth clearly shows his shock as he openly gapes at what is now visible to him again. Where he sees less than half the shock turtles remaining already. And a big part of him wonders if the other humans of the world can take down Aqua anymore.

Because he begins to believe that it’s not possible. Even if they all worked together.
