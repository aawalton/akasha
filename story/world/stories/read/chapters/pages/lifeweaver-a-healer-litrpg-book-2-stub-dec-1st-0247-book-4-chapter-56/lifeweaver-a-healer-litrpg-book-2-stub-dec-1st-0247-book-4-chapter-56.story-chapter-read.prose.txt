I must begin by offering my sincere apologies for the ramifications of what I am about to share with you all on Earth.

Luke stopped dead in his tracks. A lingering feeling of dread had been building in his chest ever since he parted with Guide, and that surged to the forefront at the words appearing in his interface. Something had happened, and now the steward of Earth would share it with them all at the same time.

As some of you are aware, more systems underwent the integration process at the same time as yours. Seven of them, to be precise. This is an unprecedented number, and it has stretched our resources thin, keeping you all safe while you grow stronger. The Singer system perished not long ago, leaving six. Now, that number is five. The void, our common foe, engulfed another system, overwhelming their defenses.

A whole other system gone, just like that. Luke shuddered, but it had nothing to do with the cold air and wind up on the mountain. If he and the others had failed in their task to protect the weave from the void creatures, they would have suffered the same fate. If that enormous void being had chosen to attack, rather than remain placid, Earth might have been the now-dead system.

Outside help is difficult to provide, but The Greater System does all it can to provide despite being under constant threat. You were brought into the fold to aid all against the enemy. Now I must ask more of you. Dungeons are our main tool in keeping those who dwell in the void from reaching us. Through dungeons, we siphon their power and claim it as our own. With the recent loss of the Singer system and now the Muridae system, their share of the burden must be shouldered by others. By you, on Earth. You show particular promise, and I am proud of your accomplishments. Again, my apologies.

No Singers and Muridae, whoever those were, around meant they couldn't deal with their fair share of portals. Instead, that would have to go to.... Nausea struck Luke when it dawned on him what Guide was talking about. Shouldering more of the burden meant those portals and dungeons ending up on Earth instead.

Starting now, you will see more portals opening. The dungeons within will all provide a greater challenge than before. Attempts at handling them without at least a party of six are discouraged. More experience will be provided, and the rewards for completing quests have been increased. A second Tutorial Dungeon is now open where Integrated under Level 25 can fight without risk of death. Touch an Orb to access it. All children under eighteen years of age and everyone else who does not wish to integrate will be placed in protective stasis to ensure your safety to the extent we are able. Unfortunately, we can no longer keep monsters from attacking non-Integrated. All of you are needed for this greater challenge. You must last until we can welcome you into The Greater System. Our continued survival lies with you and the other new systems.

Damn. That was quite a lot. Luke was about to shout for Guide but found he didn't need to. They were already in Guide's office, which was larger now to accommodate a few new faces.

"Hey Luke," Penny said, giving him a nervous wave from a few seats over. "So this is where you hang out?"

Luke nodded to her. "Something like that. Glad you could join us."

Some more faces he recognized. Sam was at the far end, staring down into her lap. Ella was there too, despite her previous proclamations. Nick, Curtis's brother and the guildmaster of Phoenix Phalanx, sat between Hannah and Popov, and a few other faces Luke didn't recognize filled the remaining chairs.

"That was some news right there," Luke said to Guide, who sat behind his usual desk, this time without his usual calm aura. When he spoke, it was in a monotone that seemed to have been scrubbed clean of any emotion.

"The Muridae system was ripped asunder by a void creature we have not seen before. Despite being old to us, it is old, very old, and powerful. Though we offered what support we could, there was no keeping it at bay."

The room was quiet for a long moment as everyone processed the news.

Popov was first to speak. "What of siphoning its strength?"

"Falzyxi, that is its name, has resisted our efforts to do so."

One of the new ones, a woman in her early thirties wearing a green dress with white spots, raised her hand. "How do you know its name? Did you ask it?"

"All those who dwell in the void project their names at all times. It is a matter of listening."

Luke asked what he figured was the most important question of the hour. "Is it coming here?"

"Yes."

Crap. "When?"

"Soon. That is why I brought everyone here. You must be ready."

"Is it as strong as the other void being?" Luke asked.

"Measuring the relative strength of those who dwell in the void is most often an exercise in futility, but in most ways, no. Falzyxi is not as powerful as the one you are thinking of. Few are."

"I barely know what the hell I'm doing here," the green-dress woman said.

"You who find yourself here are on the brink of seeing the system for what it is, but have not yet passed that threshold."

"We will teach you," Popov said, indicating himself and Hannah, who looked a little haunted.

"Better do it quickly," Dimitrios said, squeezing his hands into fists. "Luke and I cannot do this alone."

"You said you can't bring us into The Greater System yet," Luke said. "Why?"

"A seed needs time to flourish. No one is able to alter the minimum time necessary. Earth is already at the minimum. All of you are."

"And the other new systems? Can't we work with them now? There has to be others who can do what we do there?"

"There is," Guide confirmed. "But you cannot."

"The Singers were able to get here," Luke argued.

"Yes."

Since Guide didn't appear willing to elaborate, Luke dropped the subject. Pushing it would just be a waste of time, time they didn't have. "When are the dungeons getting harder?"

"Now."

"Do we have some way of being alerted to dungeon breaks?" Popov asked. "More attention from the void is the last thing we need."

"We'd still need to get there to fight them," Luke grumbled.

"Just go there," Dimitrios said, frowning.

Luke turned to look at him. "What do you mean?"

"You touch the great constellation. You must have some way of traversing it."

"Only to people I've healed, with any precision. I can find portals and go to them, but not specific ones with a dungeon break, I think."

"You will figure out the solution to that problem of yours, I'm sure," Popov said, turning to Guide. "Anything else?"

Guide studied them. He looked tired, a far cry from the picture of authority he had been at the start of Earth's journey. "I would like to pose a question to you all. This is not for you to answer now. I only ask that you carry it with you."

"What's the question?" Penny asked, her voice tinged with nervousness. She was still so young.

"How far are you willing to go to protect Earth and all other systems? How much of yourself are you willing to give?"

"Everything," Dimitrios said without even taking a second to consider the question. The utter confidence in the answer made Luke think of Mateo, and he wondered how the man must feel with his family going into protective stasis. What would he do?

"Everything is more than you could ever imagine," Guide said.

With that, Luke returned to the mountain. Messages rolled down the entirety of his interface, interrupting his thoughts about the question Guide posed before dismissing them.

Milla (Guild): "We will divide our guild into semi-permanent parties who will tackle all dungeons together."

Milla (Guild): "Lana and I will go on a recruitment drive."

Milla (Guild): "Our moving plans are still in effect, but be ready for shifting priorities."

Penny: "Hey. That was... something. I think I'm getting a handle on seeing the Everstorm and even touching it. I'll let Popov help others, since I'm pretty sure I'll figure this out on my own, like you guys."

Luke: "I believe in you, young Padawan."

Luke: "Just don't enter the weave... Everstorm on your own the first couple of times, okay? Cool name for it, by the way."

Penny: "I know, right?"

Hannah: "Hey. You got a minute?"

Luke: "Yep. Are you coming to me or the other way around?"

Hannah: "I'll come to you."

Mateo: "Luke, mi hermano. My family is gone. I weep alone."

Luke: "They're protected, Mateo. It sucks, but they'll be kept safe this way."

Mateo: "I do not trust this entity. It keeps things from us. Still, I will fight."

Luke: "Are you coming back to The Band, then?"

Mateo: "I will. Danger calls, and I must answer."

Luke: "Love it!"

Hannah appeared and looked around. "You're in a dungeon?"

"Right," Luke said, blinking. "I kind of forgot about that."

"You forgot? It's pretty cold."

He was about to answer her, but she cut him off by throwing her arms around him and pressing the side of her face against his chest. Luke hugged her back. "Everything okay?"

"No," she said, the words muffled by the robe. "Well, yes, but no."

"Tell me."

She chuckled. "There's nothing to say. I'm just overwhelmed with the whole other system just being eaten and us having to pick up the slack. It's going to be hectic, and I'm barely holding on as it is. Now we're supposed to fight this Fai-something, and I can't even reliably see the system thingie yet."

"We'll deal with it together," Luke said, squeezing her narrow frame. "It'll be fine."

"Doubt it," she said with a tired chuckle. "Popov isn't working out."

"What do you mean?"

"His way of doing this isn't mine. I'll never be able to do it the way he does," she said as she looked up at him with those large, earnest eyes. "I think I'd be better off with another teacher."

He kissed her, and they stayed like that for a little while before he thought to ask. "Me?"

"No," she said. "I think I'll need to go there, like you told me Dimitrios does."

"I'm sure he'd be happy to show you the ropes. Just don't go all crazy and charge the void beings."

"Don't you know you're not supposed to call a girl crazy? Least of all your girlfriend."

"We're all a little crazy here," Luke said. He was about to speak again, but stopped himself. Even in the dungeon, he felt it.

"It's starting already. I have to go."

Hannah nodded and released him. "Be careful."

Dimitrios: "Let's goooooo!"
