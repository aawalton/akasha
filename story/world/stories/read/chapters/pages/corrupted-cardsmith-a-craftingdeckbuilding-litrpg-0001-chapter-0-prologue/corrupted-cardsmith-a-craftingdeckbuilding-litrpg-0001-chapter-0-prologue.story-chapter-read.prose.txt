Prologue

A brief flash of purple light flickered within the guildhall as an immaculately dressed man stepped through a halo of fading light. There was a dark purple card floating above him that pulsed briefly before absorbing the surrounding halo and dissipated inside the man’s chest.

Sporting a thin mustache, the stranger had neatly trimmed short blond hair, pale gray eyes and an elegantly chiseled jawline. It was hard to judge his age accurately, but to an onlooker’s eye, he appeared to be in his late thirties. Dressed in tightly fit black robes, the stranger exuded a natural aura of authority as he passed through the hall.

People walking through the area acknowledged him with a quick nod, while others halted in their tracks to give him a bow of respect. The stranger ignored them all as he confidently strode his way ahead, before stopping at a large decorative door guarded by a single giant of a man looming over him.

“…”

The stranger stood there with his pale gray eyes calmly gazing at the towering guard.

“Tch.”

The guard grunted before tapping a brown card strapped to his belt and stepped aside. The massive door opened without a sound, allowing the stranger to step forward into the hall.

“You took your sweet time, Renald. The Leader has been waiting for you.”

A husky feminine voice sounded to the stranger’s right as a woman shrouded in silky indigo robes walked over.

“I had to pass through the Sunspire’s territory to get here to the 20th floor, and they wouldn’t let me pass unless I gave them all the details of why I was making my way to the Starhaven guild. Of course, I didn’t tell them the truth, but I want to know what all this haste is about, Leana.”

Renald’s eyebrow twitched with annoyance as he followed behind the woman. Despite knowing the way to the Leader’s throne, he let Leana lead him. Even with his volatile nature, he understood the leader of Starhaven commanded fear; a fear that extended beyond the Sunspire’s Archbishop.

As one of the few rankers to evolve to Tier 6 and reach the Card Luminary stage, Starhaven’s leader stood at the forefront of humanity’s crusade against the dungeon.

Renald was a powerful Tier 4 ranker in his own right, but even amongst the other Tier 4 Cardists, he was special because of his [Epic] class card: Diviner.

He couldn’t see the complete future, but his class card allowed him to take small glimpses a few seconds into the future. This made him a formidable opponent to face, as combined with the rest of his fate cards, only a handful of other Tier 4 Cardists could contend against him.

The pair arrived at the enormous, empty metal throne in the center. The seat bore the magnificent Starhaven crest, the mark of the man whose power reigned supreme in the dungeon.

“The leader’s not in the guild at the present. He’ll talk to you through the comms card.”

Leana pulled down the hood covering her face, revealing beautiful dark locks of hair. Her icy-blue eyes glanced at Renald before she stepped back. The throne glowed with an enigmatic gold light as a hazy illusion of an armored man coalesced on top of the throne.

Seeing the figure appear, Renald quickly got down on one knee and whispered, “Greetings to the Card Luminary.”

A small smile played on Leana’s lips as she stood on the sidelines, her gaze fixed on the guild leader, a figure she clearly admired.

“Renny! How have you been, chappy? Sorry man, the tree-huggers in Verdura wanted me to help them out with something in their kingdom, so I had to leave urgently.”

Renald’s eyebrow twitched at the leader addressing him as ‘Renny’, but he didn’t dare correct the man. As famous as Starhaven’s leader was for his legendary class card, he was equally infamous for his hoodlum personality.

Leana let out a defeated sigh. If she could somehow fix the leader's rowdy personality, it would clear her plate of cleaning the mess he created. She recalled the aghast expression on the Elf Queen’s face when the leader called her the ‘Fancy Tree-Hugger’ to her face in the presence of her subjects.

“It’s alright, Card Luminary. What can I do for you?”

Renald maintained his decorum.

“Lily, hand it over to him.”

With great eagerness, the leader’s image spoke from its throne.

“Don’t call me Lily, you lug!”

Leana cursed in mock defeat, but eventually summoned a shiny silver card from her shoulder, where her storage card was equipped. The moment the intricate silver card appeared, Renald’s gray eyes widened in shock. He’d heard of Tier 6 spell cards before, but this was his first time seeing one.

“It can’t be…”

When his sight landed on the words etched into the card, Renald swiftly pivoted his head to the figure on the throne, “A-Are you sure you want me to use this?”

“Let alone the fact that you’re the highest evolved ranker with a compatible class card, we’re desperately in need of information. The Akarsha dungeon’s integration into our world took us by storm, and in the countless years of clearing the dungeon floors, the Leader has gathered certain vital information.”

Leana placed the silver card on Renald’s palm, causing the card to flash brightly as it fused with the man’s [Epic] Diviner class card. The Leader silently waited for Leana to continue.

“Our world will soon face a terrifying calamity and we need you to use that oracle spell card to find out who the enemy is.”

Leana explained while the illusion of the Leader distorted slightly on the throne. It was unknown whether it was because of the comms connection or the leader’s turbulent emotions.

“Very well.”

Renald nodded as he channeled his lumen into the silver card. His lumen reservoirs were running a bit dry because he didn’t have a chance to replenish it after using the portal card, but it was pointless to complain before Starhaven’s Guild Leader.

The silver card thrummed as an intense silver light exploded into the area and hastily seeped Renald’s pale eyes. The Diviner let out a painful cry while streams of silver liquid poured down his cheeks from his retina.

Leana almost stepped forward to help Renald before being stopped by the Leader’s sharp warning. The system restricted cards to specific classes for a reason; it was fatal for non-compatible classes to force those cards into their decks.

Fortunately, they didn’t have to wait for long, as Renald’s screams eventually died out while the silver light gradually receded into the card. The oracle card was now noticeably dimmer than before.

Renald panted, out of breath, crumpling to the floor. His eyes looked like they were about to close, but his mouth quivered as if he wanted to get his words out. The Leader’s avatar on the throne leaned forward at the same time that Leana rushed to Renald on the floor to hold him up. He was completely drained of lumen, but he should be fine after a day or two.

“I-I saw… I s-sa…”

Renald struggled to form a sentence; however, Leana guessed what he was trying to tell them.

“You saw the enemy? Who is it?”

She wiped the gathering beads of sweat on Renald’s forehead, a trace of urgency hidden in her voice.

Renald nodded in acknowledgment before bursting into a series of coughs. After a few seconds, he squeezed out his words.

“Cardsm…ith.”

Immediately after, Renald’s eyes rolled back into their socket and the man passed out of consciousness.

Leana turned her head to the throne, her fingers trembling. They finally had a clue as to the one who will eventually destroy their world.

It was a Cardsmith.
