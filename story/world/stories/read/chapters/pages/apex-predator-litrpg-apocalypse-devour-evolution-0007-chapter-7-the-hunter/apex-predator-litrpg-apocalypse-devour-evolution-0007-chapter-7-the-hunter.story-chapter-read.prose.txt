Before Don moved any further, he spent his two Free Attribute Points. At this point the sprinting was so high speed that he felt like his claw-work was slightly sloppy. So far he had way more Power than he required, so he figured he would upgrade his finesse. That is why he put one point in Agility and one point in Mental, to increase things like reaction speed and accuracy. With all of his new cool stats, Don took a look at his full stat sheet. It made him proud.

It was amazing to look at the progress. Don had earned all of this, and nothing short of death could take it away from him. A mere night had passed and he had far surpassed human limits. He was now stronger, faster and sharper of mind than he had ever dreamed of being. And he was just getting started…

His musings were stopped by something frantic emerging from the bushes. It was a wide-eyed man in an office shirt and tattered pants. He was bleeding, with an arrow sticking off his back. He collapsed on the forest floor.

“What happened?” Don asked. He looked at the arrow. It had penetrated deep.

The office worker coughed copious amounts of blood on the grass. “It’s the Hunter,” he whispered.

“The what?”

“He hunts people,” the man said between coughs. ”Thinks this is a game… Run… Run!”

“I have healing potions,” Don said, but when he crouched to help the man, he was already dead, his glazed eyes looking somewhere no living could see.

Something flared red in his mind. It was urgent and severe. An undeniable sense of danger. Death was coming. Don listened to his instincts and climbed up the nearest tree in a dash. There he sat, shrouded in the leaves. His heart was beating fast, and the same red sense kept insisting that he was in danger.

[Evolution Acquired: Survival Instincts]

[Survival Instincts (F-grade): You have an uncanny ability to sense danger. +1 to Mental]

Don was pleased by that, but he wasn’t sure why the sense was still flaring up. Just in case he climbed higher in the tree. The red sense of danger faded into a low hum in the back of his mind. It was still unsafe, but not urgent. Don watched and resolved to stand absolutely still.

A man emerged from the foliage. He was wearing a green hood and sturdy leathers. The clothes were clearly modern, but they were fashioned after a medieval sense. On his shoulder hung a bow. Wooden and simple.

“There’s two options. He found that gear in the Tutorial, or he came here with it. I don’t get the medieval fair vibe from this guy. Prepper?

One thing was sure. This guy was the reason for his Survival Instinct blaring up. The Hunter knelt over the body of the office worker and yanked the arrow off his back. Then he looted the pouch, but scoffed after looking inside.

“Useless scum,” the Hunter muttered.

Then he looked around and knelt right where Don had been standing. The Hunter pressed his face on the ground and sniffed. Immediately he took his bow and nocked an arrow on it with one swift motion. This guy was an experienced bowman.

Don didn’t dare to breathe. He only watched the Hunter from the safety of his high branch. Something was telling him it was a bad idea to attack this guy. Well for one thing, the archer was clearly ready for an ambush. But there was something else. Don used Identify on him.

[Human (F-grade), Level 9 Hunter]

This guy was level 9 already?! That was crazy. He must have some pretty special circumstances. Don supposed it made sense. When he had killed the priest, the experience and other rewards had been substantial. If this Hunter guy was dedicating his time hunting any human he saw, of course he would level up fast. One more level and he would get a class.

Don slightly shifted his weight and the branch he was sitting on shuffled. The Hunter immediately aimed the bow in his direction and watched, still as a statue. Which was coincidentally, exactly, what Don was attempting to do.

A part of him wanted to attack. This guy was bad news, and he would likely only get stronger. But he was three levels higher. That was 50% stronger. And Don didn’t have the jump on him. So he resolved to stay absolutely still.

[General Skill increased: Stealth!]

[Stealth Level 4]

“Come on System. Make me hard to see.”

Maybe the prompting worked, but it probably didn’t. Either way, the Hunter lowered his bow and stomped off. Don released the breath he was holding and slowly exhaled.

He waited in the tree for a few minutes just to be safe. The Hunter could be waiting for him. If Don was going to fight this guy, it would be on his terms, where he would be the one hunting.

There was something thrilling about that thought. Don hadn’t set out to hunt humans. He found that abhorrent. But whatever kind of world you had. Traditional modern earth or a new System-based reality. There were always people who were a cancer. Don had met a sleuth of them. They somehow managed to always get into positions of power. Like this Hunter guy. Complete psycho and very powerful.

Well, Don was powerful too. He was enjoying his growth and looked forward to becoming more powerful. But before now, he hadn’t realized that the competition was growing stronger too. They were driven by something. Don found that the first time he was driven too.

I hated feeling afraid and that the System had to warn me about this guy. Next time I meet the Hunter, I want the System to be still, because it knows I have nothing to fear. Then I will decide what to do with the Hunter.

But now was not the time for that. Now was the time to become powerful. So Don got down and continued to look for prey.

Don found another pair of Barbtail Gibbons and he disposed of them with quick efficiency. This time it was even easier. Both his Sprinting and Unarmed Fighting were clearly improved by the Meta-Awareness Attribute. He could maintain high speed while running and still deliver accurate strikes with his claws. His skills or level did not increase with these two kills, but both of them yielded 16 Evolution Points, bringing his total to 92. Don had decided he would not evolve his Night Vision for now. The faintest gray light of dawn was already coming, and soon Don would not need the evolution. It had certainly served him well, and he would soon upgrade it. But he was more interested in pumping up his Claws another notch, to gain access to the significant Power and Agility increases. But most likely that would be his last upgrade for a while, unless he didn’t unlock new evolutions.

It seemed that the evolutions triggered naturally when he did things, as emergent features, but they were also a reward for reaching racial milestones, like level 5. Don assumed he would get another choice of evolutions at level 10. Then he would be halfway to choosing his class. He wondered how his special circumstances of being an Evolutor would affect his class. If this System worked with basic video game rules, he would assume the classes were something along the lines of warrior, thief, mage, healer etc. But with his Evolutor-race, they could be something wildly different.

Don breathed in the cool forest air. There was a faint mist around, and a soft wind blew. It was peaceful and refreshing. Don had always liked the outdoors, and now he had ample excuse to spend some more time in nature.

“Seems like this new world fits me in more ways than one.”

Suddenly Don heard a scream somewhere nearby. It was a young woman. This was no battlecry, but a call for help. Don followed the sounds. Most likely there was experience to be gained, and maybe even his first alliance.
