She sat on the bench where Bron had sat two days ago with the token in his hand.

The jellyfish drifted closer. Thunder, quiet, felt in the chest.

She had been thinking about names.

Bron said: maybe you give it a name when you figure out which one you’re doing. Staying because you ran out of reasons to go, or staying because you have reasons to be here.

I think I know which one I’m doing now.

Not because everything is resolved.

Not because the gap motor → Aethelgard is explained.

Not because I know what the third clip is, or what the pre-server plant wanted, or what T.S. is going to give me, or what happens when Alondra finally tells Miles.

Not even because an account that doesn’t exist on any server just said: I remember you.

But because today Miles said: the room needed to be ready.

And I was the room.

And it was ready.

And I’m still here.

Whatever is coming — and something is coming — I’m still here.

She looked at the jellyfish.

“Lumen,” she said.

The lightning moved. Warm. Once.

Thunder, quiet, like yes.
