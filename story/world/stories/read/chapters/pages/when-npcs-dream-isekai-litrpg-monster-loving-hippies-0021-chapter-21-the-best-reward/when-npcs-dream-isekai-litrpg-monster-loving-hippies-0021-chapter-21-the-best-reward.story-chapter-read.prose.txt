Sounds good,” I agreed. “I’ll go with Samar and Kim toward the mines. You two can circle around the other side of the hill.”

Still nothing.

Everyone stared at me. Yeah, I knew it—I was the one who had to go in.

Great. This was turning into the dumbest rescue quest ever.
