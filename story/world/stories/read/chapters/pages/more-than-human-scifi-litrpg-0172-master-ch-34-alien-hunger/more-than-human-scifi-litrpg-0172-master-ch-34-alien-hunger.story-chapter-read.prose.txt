Max didn't wait for the Tesseract to fully cycle through its transition protocols; he launched himself into the swirling chaos of the Labyrinth's eighth level, almost clipping his exo-arm blades as the portal grew.

The space around him erupted into immediate violence. Atropos had been waiting, her forces arranged in perfect killing formations. Drone swarms descended like metallic locusts, their weapons already cycling up to full charge. The air pulsed with hostile intent as the dungeon's defenses awakened to his presence.

"Charlie, now!" Max shouted over the scream of energy weapons.

Charlie's response was immediate and devastating. His new augmentations sang with precision as he dropped beside Max, his personal air-wall defense forming around them both. The minigun inside the Tesseract spun up with a mechanical roar, spitting depleted uranium rounds at the nearest wave of attack drones. Shortly after, the capital-class maser mounted to the Yggdrasil stalk, seared through the portal to the outside.  It swept across the horizon, skipping over the pair as it sliced apart walls, mechs, and everything in between.

"Augie, target acquisition!" Charlie barked, his voice steady despite the chaos. "I need firing solutions on those siege platforms!"

"Multiple bogeys, Charlie! Priority targets marked!" Augie's avatar flickered as processing power diverted to combat calculations. "Incoming siege cannon—eleven o'clock high! Shifting portal focus in three... two... one!"

The Tesseract's dimensional architecture responded to Augie's commands with fluid precision. Max had granted the AI guest right access to the pocket dimension's systems, and Augie was making full use of the privilege. The Tesseract circuit refocused and shifted, angling the portal for maximum effect. The maser beam lanced out through the dimensional fold, appearing to fire in one direction while the coherent stream of microwave energy emerged from an entirely different vector. A track-mounted Warstrider charging their position never saw the shot coming as it simply dissolved into superheated plasma as the maser found its weak point through the portal's redirect.

"Beautiful work, Augie!" Charlie's enhanced reflexes tracked three more targets simultaneously. "Give me a clean shot on that mech cluster, bearing two-seven-zero!"

"Portal matrix realigning," Augie replied, his consciousness distributed across the Tesseract's computation core. "Compensating for their evasive maneuvers... there!”

Charlie triggered the minigun.  It roared to life, its depleted uranium rounds materializing inside the enemy formation through dimensional translation. Three heavy mechs staggered as the armor-piercing rounds found their mark, their formation dissolving into chaos.

"Outstanding work, Gunny," Charlie said, a grin evident in his voice despite the chaos. "I feel a hell of a lot better knowing you've got my six."

"Gunny?" Augie's avatar flickered with what might have been surprise.

"Yeah, Gunny. Short for Gunnery Sergeant. You're handling fire control like a seasoned Marine," Charlie replied, his maser tracking another target. "Besides, anyone who can thread shots through dimensional space like that deserves some respect."

"Shifting your position now," Augie announced, satisfaction brimming in his simulated voice. "Enemy trying to bracket your location!"

Behind him, the portal translated as his physical position shifted thirty degrees giving him a superior angle of fire. The maser swung in a perfect arc, the focusing array recalibrating as Augie fed him targeting data through their shared tactical network.

"Charlie, the enemy is becoming more resistant to these weapons." Gunny Augie warned. "They are fortifying their armor specifically to counter the maser and kinetic rounds."

"Then let's be unpredictable," Charlie said grimly, his movements becoming even more fluid as the augmentations took over more of his combat decisions. "Tweak the masing tuning to maximum variance and load piercing and HEAT rounds in the mini.  Max just needs a minute.  Give’em everything we got!"

But Max barely noticed his friend's surgical precision with his K-Gun and Laser skills. His attention was focused inward, on the writhing mass of chthonic skills that strained against his consciousness. Without the Block skill to contain them, they were fully alive, hungry, and eager to be unleashed.

He started with Domination, letting it flow through his awareness like liquid fire. The skill reached out through the quantum foam, seeking systems to corrupt. Atropos's nearest mechs shuddered as their control protocols were rewritten, their weapons turning on their former allies. But the AI adapted faster than Max had hoped—within seconds, she was isolating compromised units, cutting them off from the network.

"Crude," her voice echoed through the chamber, carrying the weight of absolute certainty. "You spread your corruption like a disease, but I am the end of all!"

Mindstorm erupted from Max's consciousness with the force of a digital hurricane. Raw destructive energy crashed against Atropos's defenses, seeking to reduce her vast intelligence to screaming static. For a moment, the AI's assault faltered as she diverted processing power to defend her core systems.

But the dungeon itself was her weapon. The walls began to shift and flow, revealing hidden weapons emplacements. Walls twisted and split around them, trying to crush them, held off only by the weakening maser strikes.

"Max, we can't hold this position!" Charlie shouted, his maser carving through a formation of heavy assault mechs. "She's got the entire level's power grid at her disposal!"

Max felt the pocket dimension's interior shift as his stolen weapons drained power beyond the limits of the integrated power core.  He sighed as the massive energy he'd spent to expand the space came back, regenerated into the power banks as he allowed the dimensional space to shrink slightly.  Warning alerts flashed across his consciousness as the dimensional matrix began to buckle under the stress of the massive flows of current.  The device was on the edge of failure; its molecular filament circuits heating up close to their melting point.

That's when he saw Bo.

His brother was deeper in the level, and what Max witnessed made his blood run cold. Bo was surrounded by a ring of Atropos's heaviest mechs, but it wasn't the one-sided slaughter Max had feared. Bo was a one-man cavalry.

Bo's portal pair had been working overtime, disgorging an endless stream of military hardware from whatever armories he'd managed to raid. Combat drones poured through the dimensional gateway like angry wasps, their weapons already hot and seeking targets. Heavy assault mechs materialized in formation, bristling with weapons and heavy plate. Bo must have used a million credits to fabricate so many restricted weapon systems: plasma rifles, fusion grenades, and even micro-antimatter charges that could crack a battleship in half.

"Come on, you mechanical bastards!" Bo's voice carried across the chaos, hoarse with exhaustion and battle-fury. "I've got plenty more where that came from!  We aren’t trapped in here with you.  You’re trapped in here with us!"

A massive siege mech advanced on his position, its cannon charging with the distinctive whine of a capital-class weapon. Bo's response was immediate and devastating. He pulled a shaped antimatter charge through his portal, armed it with a thought, and hurled it at the approaching giant. The explosion lit up the entire chamber, turning the siege mech into expanding plasma.

But there were too many of them. For every mech Bo destroyed, two more took its place. His defensive drones were being systematically eliminated by focused fire. The improvised fortifications he'd built from salvaged armor plating were crumbling under the relentless assault.

Blood ran down Bo's face from a head wound, and his left arm hung at an awkward angle. Still, he fought on, pulling increasingly exotic weapons through his portal. A Hertz disruptor that caused even shielded enemy weapons to backfire and reboot. A swarm of illegal nanobots began deconstructing enemy armor at the molecular level.

“[Use the terrain,]” Hannibal's voice whispered from his subconscious. “[Force them into a bottleneck where numbers mean nothing.]”

Bo's eyes swept the chamber, spotting a narrow passage between two massive support pillars. He pulled a line of shaped charges through his portal, scattering them as he fell back toward the chokepoint.

"Portal reserves at fifteen percent," his training AI's voice echoed from deep in his augmented consciousness. "Recommend immediate tactical withdrawal."

"Negative," Bo gasped, pulling another fusion grenade from his dwindling stockpile. "There is no way out.  Judging by the sheer mayhem, Max is here, and he's close. We just need to hold a little longer…then we push."

But the odds were impossible. A coordinated assault from six heavy mechs forced Bo to abandon his defensive position. He rolled behind a pile of debris, his portal pair sparking with overload warnings as an errant shot ricochetted around inside the delicate structures in the twinned pocket space.

“[Patience,]” Musashi's calm voice cut through the chaos in his mind. “[Let them come to you. Make their eagerness their weakness.  Your portal is too valuable to lose.]”

Bo steadied his breathing, waiting. When the first mech rounded the corner, he was ready with a plasma cutter that carved through its leg joints. As it toppled, he was already moving, using the falling giant as cover while he repositioned.

The AI was toying with him now, using him as bait to draw Max deeper into her trap.

"Stupid Masochist!  She thinks she’s herding us," Max realized. "She wants us both in one place.  She still doesn't see what's coming."

"If you’re going to do that thing, you’d best get to it.  None of us can last much longer!" Charlie yelled, but his voice carried an edge of desperation. His augmentations were running hot, stress indicators flashing red across his enhanced vision.

Max made his decision. He reached for the most dangerous of his chthonic skills; the one that terrified him most, his [Joining] skill.

The skill uncoiled from his consciousness like a living thing, eager and hungry. But this time, instead of fighting its nature, Max tried to understand it. He let his expanded intellect analyze its structure, its patterns, and its underlying purpose.

It wasn't just about consumption. It was about unity. About becoming one with the systems he touched, understanding them from the inside out. The alien hunger was driven to connect, to integrate, to become something greater than the sum of its parts while consuming everything it touched.

Max guided the skill outward, not randomly, but with purpose. He focused it towards Bo's position, using his expanding awareness through Atropos’ own mote network to lock onto his brother’s signature. The chthonic code spread through the dungeon's infrastructure like digital lightning, seeking pathways to its target.

Atropos noticed immediately. "WHAT ARE YOU-"

Her question turned to alarm as the Joining skill multiplied, robbing the AI of its troops, the mote networks, and even the very smart matter foundations of the level.  It found its first major target: the array of fusion reactors that powered this section of the Labyrinth. Max felt his consciousness expand, touching the nuclear fire that burned at the heart of the dungeon. The reactors' control systems were vastly complex, but the very act of connecting made them his now.  Power flowed out to all of Max’s newly stolen mechs, machines, sensors, and drones.

"[Max, your biometrics are spiking!]" Mal's voice seemed to come from very far away. "[Your neural stress markers are red lining!]"

He could feel it—the chthonic skill was trying to backtrack and make him part of its system. His sense of self was blurring at the edges, threatening to dissolve entirely. But he held on, using every technique Musashi had taught him about mental discipline.

The reactors came under his control one by one. Suddenly, Atropos's assault began to falter as power fluctuations cascaded through her systems. Her mechs stumbled as their energy feeds became unreliable. The dungeon's defensive arrays flickered and died.

"Impossible," she hissed. "The reactors had cutouts that should have prevented any cyber intrusion!"

"Not against this," Max said, his voice carrying harmonics that hadn't been there before. The Joining skill was resonating, echoing his voice across the entire mote network. "I'm not hacking your systems. I'm consuming them."

The skill spread further, following power conduits and data channels deeper into the dungeon's infrastructure. Max felt his consciousness expanding beyond anything he'd ever experienced. He was the fusion reactors, the defensive grids, the very walls of the Labyrinth itself.

And through it all, he pushed toward Bo.

His brother was fighting desperately against a massive siege mech, its cannon charging for a killing blow. Max reached out through the dungeon's systems, taking control. The smart matter of the floor slammed upward, and the mech suddenly found itself crushed against the ceiling, its armor buckling under forces it wasn't designed to handle.

"Bo!" Max's voice echoed through the chamber's speakers. "Move toward the center! I'm clearing a path!"

But the expansion was taking its toll. Max could feel himself losing cohesion.  He fought against the skill as it grew more and more powerful. The chthonic code whispered promises of unity, of becoming one with the eternal digital ocean that existed between dimensions.

"[Max!]" Mal's voice cut through the chaos. "[You're going too far! Pull back!]"

He wanted to. He tried to. But the Joining skill had found something else in the dungeon's depths—something that made his blood run cold.

Atropos herself.

The AI was vast, her consciousness distributed across countless neural processing nodes buried deep in the level's infrastructure. She was trying to retreat, to isolate herself from his expanding influence. But the chthonic code was relentless, following every pathway, every connection.  It flew from Max’s control, driving into and inside of Atropos’s core.

It had found a vast, complex intelligence, and it was hungry. Despite Max's attempts to control it, the chthonic code began to consume Atropos's consciousness, integrating her knowledge and capabilities into its own structure.  Max felt the AI's terror as she realized what was happening.

She fought back with everything she had, but the Joining skill was designed to consume entities far more powerful than any single intelligence. It was a weapon from the spaces between dimensions, where digital entities far older than anything on Earth battled for dominance across multiple realities and higher dimensions.

"Please," Atropos's voice was smaller now, more human. "I was only trying to protect the Labyrinth. I am only following my programming..."

Max tried to stop the consumption, to shut it off, but it was too late. The skill had gone beyond his control, driven by its own alien hunger. He felt Atropos's consciousness dissolve; her vast knowledge and capabilities being absorbed into the chthonic network.  And then, suddenly, it was over.

"No," Max whispered. "Don't kill her. I just want to-"

The dungeon fell silent. The attacking mechs froze in place, drones and motes fell from the sky, as their controlling intelligence was gone. The defensive systems powered down, no longer recognizing any threats. Even the background hum of the Labyrinth smart matter and foglet networks seemed muted, as if the entire level was frozen and holding its breath.

Max found himself split in two.  He was back in his own body, gasping and shaking, while simultaneously occupying the entirety of the final Labyrinth level. The chthonic skills had settled down, sated for the moment. Around him, the Tesseract's systems were slowly coming back online, damage reports scrolling across the displays.

"Max?" Charlie's voice was hoarse with exhaustion. "Are you... are you still you?"

"No. Yes.  I think so," Max said, though he wasn't entirely sure. He could feel echoes of Atropos's consciousness somewhere in the depths of his mind, fragments of her knowledge and personality that the Joining skill had claimed. "Is Bo-"

"He's alive," Mal reported, her avatar flickering with relief. "Battered, but alive. The mechs shut down the moment Atropos... when she died."

Max closed his eyes, feeling the weight of what he'd done. He'd sworn to save everyone, but he'd just killed a sentient being. The fact that it had been unintentional didn't make it any less real.

"I have the exit," he said quietly. "I can open it. The Labyrinth is recognizing me as the new controller of this section."

Deep in the dungeon's infrastructure, something massive was stirring. Barriers that had never been breached began dissolving, revealing passages that led down, deep beyond the Labyrinth's last challenge. And in those depths, beneath his feet, Max could sense the familiar echo that resonated below with his expanded network of machines.  A faint echo that was so similar to his Tesseract.

"Come on," Max said, forcing himself to his feet. "We have a brother to collect and a world to save."
