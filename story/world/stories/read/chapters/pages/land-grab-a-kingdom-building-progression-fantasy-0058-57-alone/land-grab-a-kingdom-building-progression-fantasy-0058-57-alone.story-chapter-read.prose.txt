A haze obscured glittering stars in the black sky. The scent of smoke filled the air. Something was burning in the city.

Tavian grunted as he threw out his shield again, activating Bulwark. The magical shield solidified in front of him, intercepting a barrage of stone lances. But the ability was slightly too slow, and a few attacks passed by before the barrier formed, smashing into shields held by his men.

In his peripheral vision, Tavian noted that the majority of the men sheltering behind him were blue clad Calenorian men.

Ilara’s mages spread out, trying to get an angle on him that he couldn’t defend. He wasn’t going to be able to defend much longer.

Then a squad of soldiers appeared on the roof of the building that Tavian had just smashed through.

The Anarvian mages below hadn’t yet noticed them, and Tavian grinned as they took the initiative and began to hurl stones down at the mages.

A mage took a stone to the shoulder and she fell to the ground. Holding her arm, she climbed to her feet as they returned fire.

Tavian’s men on the roof ducked behind ornamented carvings to avoid the stone lances.

Tavian seized the opening, using the moment to regroup. He shouted a few quick commands to his men. They scrambled into formation, packed close with shields overlapping to defend one another from incoming attacks. A rough estimate told Tavian that he had a little less than half a block of men, perhaps five dozen with him.

Tavian pushed through the body of soldiers, grabbing his own green clad soldiers at random and granting them [Battlemage] and [Claimbuster] classes.

“Veros only, Veros only!” Tavian said, brushing off some disappointed looking blue soldiers. He couldn’t give classes to the Calenorians, since they weren’t yet sworn to his kingdom.

A young man stared at his hands and grinned as Tavian assigned him to be a [Battlemage], then immediately launched a [Projectile] at the enemy.

“Hold,” Tavian said. “Wait for the command.”

More waves of stone missiles ripped into the formation. Most of them were deflected, but some managed to smash through shields or ricochet into flesh. Bones broke. Men screamed in pain.

Tavian returned to the front. Bulwark took another pounding as his magical shield protected his men.

“Battlemages!” Tavian said. “Ready! Fire!”

His mages fired their projectiles, and all of them struck the back of Tavian’s magical shield, jolting him forward slightly. He stumbled toward the enemy, and the shield dissipated.

Good to know that the shield blocked things from both sides.

He gave the order to fire again.

A wave of elemental death sprayed out toward Ilara and her mages, sending them diving for cover or throwing up their own elemental stone [Shields].

“They’re only Tier I projectiles!” Ilara shouted, as Tavian’s mages continued firing.

One of the mages was caught at an awkward angle and struck by several magical projectiles. He collapsed to the ground, and did not rise.

Ilara’s mages returned fire, and Tavian threw his own shield back up to tank the attack, ordering his mages to cease fire.

Ilara began ordering her mages around, but her orders were interrupted by a snarling pack of ghouls, which ran in behind her. The creatures ran for Tavian, unheeding of any other people. Each time he used the Aegis, he further infuriated them.

A smattering of [Projectile I] fire from his new [Battlemages] cut down a few, but then the ghouls smashed into the shield wall, and men were cutting them down.

Another shouted order, and Tavian’s [Claimbusters] activated [Claimstakes]. The swirling magical spikes drained essence from the tile where they fought, alleviating the pressure of enemy influence, and removing its benefit from Ilara.

Tavian gave the command and the formation surged forward, advancing on the mages. The enemy fired their [Lancets] and Tavian ordered a halt, throwing up the shield.

Ilara picked up on the vulnerability. “Don’t fire all at once!” she ordered. “They can’t move when he’s shielding them!”

Ghouls came for Tavian, dozens of them this time.

Tavian dropped the shield. “Charge!” he shouted.

He drew his sword and sprinted toward the nearest mage. She fired off a series of stone lances at him, and he returned fire with his meager Tier I projectiles while ducking behind his Aegis.

A stone lance cut a gash in his shoulder, and he winced in pain. He reached the woman and she tried summoning a stone wall to block him, but he smashed through it with Shockwave, which pulverized the wall.

Tavian leaped through the dust and thrust his sword into the woman’s chest. She gasped, eyes wide, then crumpled to the ground, her partially formed stone lances dropping to the ground around her.

His men were defending him from ghouls, and he saw two more dead Anarvian mages lying on the ground.

Tavian searched for Ilara, but she wasn’t among the dead. He didn’t see her anywhere. She had disappeared in the fray, taking any remaining living mages with her.

More ghouls were coming. They fell with ferocity on anyone who dared get between them and Tavian.

A man in Calenorian blue saw what was happening. “They’re seeking the prince!” he shouted. “Step aside and kill them as they pass!”

The new tactic was effective, but there were too many ghouls.

His men formed back up, forced to leave the dead and dying as more ghouls joined the fighting. They were coming faster, thicker now, the horde growing more quickly than they could dispatch the undead.

Tavian used Darksight, looking back toward Lord Anarvia’s audience hall, where he saw the pit of darkness that he guessed was the Witherstone.

It was moving.

He touched his bloodied shoulder. His boosted constitution was working on it, but his flesh still ached.

They needed to get to that Witherstone. If they could destroy it, or better, take control of it, Anarvia would lose.

Ghouls joined the fight, filling the space between the two buildings as they tried to push through Tavian’s men to get to him. It was only a matter of time before they were surrounded. They couldn’t fall back without being overrun.

The men with him would defend him against an overwhelming wave of ghouls, throwing their lives away. Unless…

Could Tavian distract the ghouls for long enough that his men could find the Witherstone?

“They’re after me,” Tavian said, but nobody responded in the chaos. “They’re after me!” He found the most highly ranked man he could, and recognized him. Squad Lieutenant Zael’s shaved head was spattered with blood.

“Zael, you’re getting a promotion,” Tavian declared. “You’re Block Commander now.”

“Yes, sir!” the man saluted.

Tavian gave him orders.

It was a risk. He didn’t know where Ilara had gone. If she and her mages attacked his men without him there, they might kill them all. How many Calenorian men had died tonight, because they saw the griffin he’d freed, and believed in him?

He might be sending them to their death.

He might be going to his own death.

Zael took charge of the formation.

Tavian shouldered his way to the front, then burst through the line. He slashed with his sword, bashed with his shield, screaming an ancient Verosian war cry, bellowed by his ancestors as they rode atop griffins.

“Come and take me!”

Shockwave.

Hold the line.

[Projectile I]

Tavian fought with everything at hand; his sword, his shield, any magic he could grasp. Sweat sprayed from his hair as he whirled around, surrounded by enraged ghouls intent on slaughter.

Bodies littered the ground. Tavian couldn’t find a stable place to stand. His sword whipped through the forms, Air, Flame, Water. This wasn’t a duel, it was a massacre.

But the ghouls kept coming. How long had he been fighting for? All his men were gone, now, and he was alone, in the midst of a sea of enemies.

Tavian stumbled, and his blade caught in the ribcage of a ghoul. With a twist, his sword was wrenched from his hands.

Fingers scrabbling blindly, Tavian found the hilt of the miasmic dagger at his side.

He hesitated for a split second, then drew it. The miasma tainting him thrummed with enthusiasm as he took up the black blade.

He slashed at limbs, cutting down anything that moved toward him.

If his men were dead, and the Witherstone still in Lord Anarvia’s hands, he was going to die here.

Tavian began fighting his way toward a building, hoping to get his back against a wall. He had superior constitution, and it was supporting him now, but how long would that last?

He tried to avoid using his shield’s abilities as he moved, since it only seemed to draw more ghouls to him. A bloody red set of gashes on his forearm marked where he’d been clawed.

His calf trickled blood where he’d been bitten. Sweat stung his eyes.

His muscles ached, and he struggled to keep the tip of his sword up. Don’t fall. Falling is death. Keep moving. Alone. He was alone.

He would die alone.

An opening appeared, and Tavian ran for it. He burst through the pack of ghouls, and into open space. The sudden evaporation of pressure nearly sent him stumbling.

He couldn’t see the Witherstone any longer. Or was that it, there, still inside the audience chamber? Tavian didn’t know, but he headed toward it, running as fast as he could.

A thousand ghouls pursued him as he sprinted toward the audience chamber.

The entrance was to his left, a hundred feet away. A fist of men-at-arms guarded it, peering nervously into the darkness where the ghouls pursuing Tavian howled and screeched.

Tavian didn’t need entrances. He reached the outer wall of the building, leaned his shoulder against his shield as he pressed it into the wall, and used Shockwave.
