Risha stared at the stone wall, examining it for any kind of button or seam. There was nothing.

“How does killing you open this?” Risha asked.

“I do not know. Many have tried to trick their way in. All have failed.” Nyx walked up to the wall in the form of a small black cat.

Risha drew Crimson, and channeled her spirit through the sword. It hummed with energy as she drew it back.

The sword struck the stone, and the world shook.

Slowly, the sword cut through the stone, carving forward until Risha pulled it free. Before her eyes, the stone healed, reforming as if she’d never struck it.

Risha let out a breath, then sheathed the sword. She turned away from the wall, and made her way back towards the hall.

Nyx followed her as she made her way past the slain creatures of darkness. The half-moon on the spirit’s head and the stars along her body lit up in the pitch black of the room, illuminating featureless faces and long claws.

Risha stopped at the staircase and looked up, “How do we leave?”

“Mmm. Now is a good time to learn the skills I grant you.” Nyx turned into a dark mist, and flowed into Risha’s body. “I can guide you into the spirit realm. Simply ask.”

Risha nodded, “Take me.”

A rip of darkness opened in front of Risha. It expanded into an inky portal.

“I am a spirit of eternal void-night, the stars and the darkness are my domain. When you wish it, I will bring the darkness to your aid, Risha. Step through.”

Risha didn’t question the spirit as she walked through the portal.

She stepped out into the void surrounding the star, and the portal closed behind her. A small floating purple light hovered in the air in front of her, the tiny spirit radiating the energy of the goblin shaman.

This must be the guide back home that the shaman had left her.

With a burst of energy, Nyx leapt from Risha, and attempted to swallow the small spirit.

“Nyx!” Risha called, then sighed in relief as the spirit phased through the cat.

Nyx crouched, ready to pounce again, as the stars on her body gathered light to them.

“Nyx, that’s an ally.”

Nyx paused, then stood up and calmly licked her paw as the energy she’d been gathering dispersed, “A wisp as an ally? These creatures are fiends, leading spiritwalkers to their deaths, and feasting off the release of their souls.”

Risha looked at the wisp, double checking that it felt the same as the ancient shaman. “This one is bonded, I think.”

Nyx narrowed her eyes, then nodded, “I see.”

Risha looked at the wisp, “You are leading Risha home, right?”

The wisp bobbed up and down, then started into the void of stars.

Risha followed the wisp, Nyx walking beside her protectively.

Eventually, they returned to a star uniquely familiar to Risha. The wisp stopped there, then disappeared to her eyes.

Risha stepped into the star, and reappeared in her garden of broken blades. Nyx followed her curiously.

“This is not what I expected of you, gob- Risha.”

“It is a reminder.” Risha answered as she walked to her tent.

“A reminder?” The cat paused at one of the purple flowers. She sniffed it before sneezing.

“Of Risha, the Grand Mother. Of those who died for her,” Risha answered. She opened the flap of her tent, and stepped inside. “I’m returning home now.”

The cat tilted its head, then dispersed into a mist and entered Risha.

Risha left the spirit world.

***

Risha woke to the silence of late night, broken only by the soft muttering of warriors who valued silence so close to battle.

She rose from her bed, and made her way to the fur cloak. She reached out, and ran her hand across the soft garment.

“Impressive enchantment. It’s a pity that it is now useless to you.” Nyx’s words made Risha look up.

“What do you mean?”

“I can let you step through the shadows without any enchantment.”

Risha dropped her hand, “What else can you do?”.

“Many things. I am a tool you must learn, Risha. Call to the darkness, ask it to serve.”

Risha held up her hand, then called with her spirit.

A portal of inky darkness expanded under Risha’s feet, then long tendrils reached out, following Risha’s desires. Power filled Risha’s mind, power that rested at her fingertips, waiting to be used, to fulfill her every wish.

Risha smiled, and her tent turned into a pitch black that even her eyes could not pierce. She looked around, all sensations of the world gone. Only the soft touch of Kliks on her shoulder told her that the world hadn’t disappeared. With an effort of will, she let the power go.

Her mind cleared as the portal closed, and Risha stared into the distance for a long moment, some part of her desperately missing the rush of power that had filled her.

Risha lifted her head, then made her way to her chainblades. She belted the swords to her waist, and placed the fur coat over her shoulders.

Igni warmed her as she stepped out into the cool night air. Kliks jumped off her shoulder and carried her through the trees above the swamps. She stopped on a tree at the edge of their camp, where a familiar Sunset Fox looked into the distance. Out there, not too far, an unsuspecting tribe of Scaled Warriors kept their fires hot, peering into the darkness. Kliks shrunk back down and climbed onto Risha’s shoulder as the goblin stood on the branch next to the fox.

Sun-Chaser glanced over to Risha, and bowed her head in greeting. Risha nodded, then looked back at the distant fires.

“Do you feel it?” Sun-Chaser asked.

Risha nodded, “Three have been touched by the Spark. As best I can tell.”

“Can the two of us hold them?”

“We will.” Risha answered, feeling Nyx and Igni waiting for her call. “Trust in our people. Glo’s distraction may call one of them away. Has he sent word?”

“They’ll start before first light.” Sun-Chaser answered.

“Kliks.” The spiderling straightened, “Let the others know we’ll be moving in an hour.”

The spider jumped from her shoulder, and took off through the trees.

“So soon?” Sun-Chaser asked.

Risha nodded, “We need to get closer before the light gives us away.”

***

True to her word, they moved through the trees an hour later, creeping ever closer to the camp. Goblin Assassins stalked ahead of them, killing their scouts before they could alert the camp, then a short time later, the night sky lit up with fire as drakes and Draconic riders clashed in the sky, far in the distance.

Still, Risha waited. Clouds forced the first light into a blue twilight, perfect for Risha.

She held up her hand, then lowered it.

Arrows and fire fell on the unsuspecting enemy. Webs covered their camp, stopping their drakes from flying, and their own serpent beasts struck from the waters.

Three creatures immediately lit up with power, rising above their people as the elements gathered to them.

Risha drew her swords, and threw them forward, pulling herself into two of the Spark-touched.

Sun-Chaser watched the Grand Mother fly forward and turned her gaze to the last of the Spark-Touched.

She stepped forward, her feet resting against a cushion of air as she raised her hands towards her opponent.

The Scaled Warrior turned towards her just as she activated her ability.

[Five Spirit Tails: First Tail - Spirit of Wind]

Her tails flared out behind her as the wind answered her call, billowing around her and making her fur rise. The wind took shape, a mighty bird of coiling air that dived towards the Spark-Touched.

The Spark-Touched raised his hand, and a wall of earth met the bird,

The wind dispersed until Sun-Chaser twisted her hand and commanded it.

Blades cut through the wall, chopping it to pieces, only to reveal an empty patch of earth.

Sun-Chaser paused, then activated [Spiritual Truth]. Her gaze tracking the spirit of the Spark-Touched. Her power gave her the briefest warning before the man leapt out of the swamps next to her.

His spear came for her throat.

Sun-Chaser pushed with her palms, combining fire and air. An explosion threw her away from the Spark-Touched, singing his scales while leaving her fur lightly ruffled.

The spearman stabbed his spear into the ground, stopping his momentum. The power of the spark flowed through the ground, clear to Sun-Chaser’s eyes. The spark’s power took control of the earth behind her, and spears rose from the ground as she flew towards them.

[Five Spirit Tails: Second Tail - Spirit Of Blades]

With a swipe of her hand, the spears cut in two and she slid across the ground to a stop. She raised her hands, and cut towards the Spark-Touched.

Blood spurted out from the Scaled Warrior as his spear blocked only one of the slices. The power of the spark flowed through him, healing the wound in seconds. He stomped into the swamp, and mist rose into the air, blocking her vision for the briefest of seconds.

His spear pierced the mist, aiming for her throat.

She sliced forward with the power of her spirit, cutting into him, but not stopping him. Time slowed as the spear inched closer to her throat, and she decided to activate her newest ability.

[Flame’s Embrace]

The spear pierced her, cutting into her spirit, but not killing her as her body turned into a spirit of fire.

She reached out with her hands, wrapping his neck as he stared in shock.

Blades shot forward from her hands, piercing his defences and cutting through his neck.

His spear dropped as his head rolled into the swampy waters.

Sun-Chaser turned to the battle, feeling as the physical realm slowly lost its hold on her and she slipped into the spiritual realm. With a mental command, she deactivated her ability and returned to her physical form.

Blood trickled down her neck, but she didn’t care as she called on

[Five Spirit Tails: Fifth Tail - Spirit Of Storm]
