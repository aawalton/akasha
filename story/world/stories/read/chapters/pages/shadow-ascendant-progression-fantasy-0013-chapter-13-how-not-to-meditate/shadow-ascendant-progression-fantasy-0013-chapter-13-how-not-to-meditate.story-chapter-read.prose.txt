Move, I thought, and the mana obeyed. A stream of pure mana released from the back of my head first facing no resistance.

The other four channels followed it. Mana from the wrist and ankle mana nodes went to my fingers, splitting into five and then releasing.

I spread my fingers apart to let the mana flow more easily, to give it more space.

The next step was forming a uniform layer, and that, at least for my head, happened almost unconsciously. The mana I released spread over the region above my neck with ease. For the rest of my body, though, I had to guide it deliberately. Maybe the head really was special?

In a few seconds, the silhouette appeared to be surrounded by a thin, glowing layer of mana. Half of my work was done; all I now had to do was spread my mana. Which was simple.

"Argh!…" I yelled, exerting all the strength I had, while still focusing on my goal of spreading the mana rather than the weird scream I just made.

Instructor Mirayel’s voice cut in. My eyes fluttered open, and I spun my gaze towards him. His eyes were dark, filled with a layer of deep violet. The white part… no, his whole eyes had disappeared.

Instructor Mirayel’s eyelids fluttered once, twice. The violet veil dissolved, and his familiar black eyes emerged beneath.

"Did you enhance your vision?" I asked.

"To see how you perform," he replied flatly.

"How did I perform, sir?" I followed enthusiastically.

He raised his hand, his index finger and thumb spread. "The widest, your mana spread was two inches; the thinnest, it was one. Your mana was too condensed… which I think is my fault, should’ve reminded you." He said, tilting his head.

The mana layer around me was uneven? But how, what I imagined was perfect—the layer around my body, was uniform. I even managed to cover the most distant region with the same amount of mana…

I bit my lip, unsure where I had gone wrong.

"Sir? How much distance does my mana need to cover for the spell to work?"

"At your level? Ahh, a foot should suffice…"

"I’ll die of mana loss!" I exclaimed, this was no overexaggeration; making just a single layer took quite a bit of mana from me. Making one six times thicker? I’d probably die.

It even sucks mana like a staff! I mentally exclaimed, probably with a wide, idiotic grin on my face.

Instructor Mirayel’s eyes squinted a bit, "you don’t hold the staff at its crystal; it would suck your mana…" he then sighed; "I know you like the sensation, but the staff isn’t a tool to play, he said."

A "tch" escaped my lips, a small, involuntary noise of frustration.

"You look like a proper mag— ahhh, apprentice mage now! Good grip, Arnos," Instructor Mirayel said, with a head nod.

"Ahem," he cleared his throat. "So, Arnos… as you might’ve already noticed, the staff’s magic crystal acts as a core and attracts mana?"

"And that even if you are not actively trying…" his gaze turned to the staff, "your range is currently 2 feet."

"Yes, sir, it’s still sucking mana, I said. It’s not ticklish anymore though…" I added.

My voice sounded mournful.

His eyes turned to me. "Would you like a demonstration? It might make it clearer."

I nodded, my excitement spiking.

Immediately after the green crystal began flickering brighter and brighter with each moment, he was pouring his mana inside the staff, increasing the intensity slowly.

A pulse of dark magic moved from his hand to the crystal, and after it, the mana began to take physical form.

Dark violet… glow surrounded the staff. I could see as it released from instructor Mirayel’s palm and entered the crystal. It was beautiful, and…

You just have to spread your mana as far away as possible, not vertically though…" he shook his head. "That’s something the staff does on itself, nothing to gain from holding it idly."
