Despite Kat advising Adam to get more stamina, he had decided to try and fuse Warrior with Gram, in order to obtain his fourth fusion evolution. He had no guarantee it would work, but it seemed a better investment of his points.

My needs for stamina are not that great right now, considering the special effect from Solbor’s Elphin Agility evolution.

From his few tests, it was clear that the intention behind how he moved was the only thing that mattered to the Elphin Agility’s effect. If he charged the dummy with the intent to attack, it would consume stamina, but if he ran towards it without his weapon drawn and no thoughts of violence, the movements didn’t consume any stamina at all.

He’d also finally gotten around to testing the ‘Golden Fortress Crossbow Bracer’. All of his projectile boosts worked with it, which gave him the ability to produce a barrage of explosive bolts to cover a large area, which he hoped would help against big groups of enemies, though it wasn’t possible to test in the player house, since he couldn’t copy the dummy.

When he powered up the bolt with the Endless Hunt boost from the barrage scattershot attack, the bolt created an explosion large enough to fill half of the interior of the player house. Adam and Kat only avoided the scalding flames by being at the exact opposite end of the house when Adam fired the powered-up projectile.

There’s no way I’m swapping to a different build now, he decided, quite happy with how the projectile build had shaped up at this point.

After buying the Warrior Class from Nelly, which took three rerolls to appear, Adam went over to Lucca’s stall and sold his hobgoblin pendant and mantle of atrophy. He decided to keep the aspirant’s cowl, since it was useful for detecting mana, even if he couldn’t use its main function of absorbing it without harming himself.

That alone makes getting a legendary worth it. A shame none of us could grab the legendary armour without using it.

I guess if Seo-Yoon unequips her legendary mask, she can always just sell it.

Since he now had more than enough to fully evolve the Warrior’s sword, he went over to Thea and bought the eighteen upgrades he needed.

I picked Frontliner during my tenth loop, so I’ll pick Soldier this time. Hopefully the evolutions don’t matter as to whether or not it can fuse with Gram, if such a fusion is even possible at all.

In his next set of options, he was surprised to find that Soldier wasn’t actually a strictly defensive path for Warrior.

Though I suppose that makes sense, given that the Colossus evolution of Frontliner is actually defence-oriented.

Centurion seemed the one most likely to have good synergy with Gram, so that’s what Adam picked, but Man-at-Arms possibly had evolutions that could be similar to the Light’s Chosen Class that Ellen had used in Adam’s stage seven group.

< Tower of Frenzy — Unique Skill ( Frenzy ) Take double Damage in exchange for doubling Attack Speed for 30 seconds | Obtain a second sword for dual-wielding >

Tower of Frenzy gains a second two-handed sword? Adam noted in surprise. From the two holograms, the Tower of Frenzy showed two large-bladed single-edged swords of dark metal with red leather handles. Blade Savant featured an enlarged gladius, which seemed like it was stretching historical accuracy a fair bit, though the Centurion’s sword was quite similar, despite centurions historically using a large shield and one-handed gladius. The blade was bronze-gold and it had a thick circular cross-guard above its long brown leather handle.

Although Adam didn’t plan on using the Warrior at all, the Blade Savant sounded the most powerful, and he was curious if it would fuse with Gram to make something truly overpowered.

< Centurion => Blade Savant >

After he was done, Adam had just 1,242 points left, so he went to the centre of the island with Kat following after him.

“You should spend the last points on upgrading your stats,” she told him.

“That’s what I’m doing,” he replied.

“Then why are you leaving the market?” she asked.

“You’ll see.”

As they reached the glass tower, white scales fell down from the sky above and Kat was frozen in time along with their cubes.

Adam paused at the sight while Luvicidix landed behind him.

“Why is she frozen?” Adam asked the moth.

“Player Kateřina Černá has not obtained the Self-Devouring Eye and thus is not eligible to view my upgrades,” Luvicidix answered.

“I haven’t gotten the Eye either,” he pointed out.

She smiled at him. “You are your own little enigma,” she replied, “but the All-Seeing System asked me to appear before you, so you are allowed to see me.”

Adam sighed. “Well, whatever, I’ve got a few points I’d like to invest, can you show me your upgrades?”

“Certainly!” she replied and gestured with her black-banded arms.

“I know! Thank you!”

“I cannot say,” she replied.

“Would you tell me if I found all of you?”

“I would!”

“So, there are still more of you to find then?” he asked.

She smiled at him instead of replying with the usual phrase.

“I cannot say.”

“Can you tell me if I give you a vanity?” he asked.

Her smile widened into a grin.

“Right, you can’t say…”

“Definitely, but I guess at some point I’ve gotten used to it. Although, Meilo was definitely a lot more forthcoming, as was Yenna.”

“I am scrutinised deeper than them, so I must be careful with what I reveal,” she explained. “Now then, since you have no more points, I am afraid I must leave you. Good luck in the Tower of Mirrors!”

“Thanks,” he replied lamely as she took to the air and disappeared into the clouds above.

Kat blinked as the time-freeze lifted. She looked around in confusion.

“What just happened?” she asked.

“The same as when we visit Alivida and Meilo,” Adam explained.

“Was I not allowed to witness what you saw?” she asked.

He nodded. “I did what you wanted though. I’ve got more stamina now.”

“So, it is a hidden merchant?” she asked.

“In a way.”

“How many points of stamina did you get?”

“Ten,” he replied. “Although, thanks to my glass tree effect, it is actually more like thirteen points.”

“That is basically the same as a single epic upgrade,” she remarked.

“Well, this boost lasts forever,” he replied.

“It is a vendor that sells upgrades for time-loopers,” she realised.

“Bingo,” he replied.

When they returned to Adam’s player house, the Blade Savant’s sword started to glow with a golden light and so did the mythical sword Gram in its weapon rack.

It worked!

Adam skipped through the cube’s announcement to look at the new fusion.

“If I use the skill while on Interim Island, will it drain my health until I die?” he asked his cube.

“That is a relief,” Kat remarked with a laugh.

“They still allow you to jump off the edge or harm yourself directly,” Adam said, which took some of the mirth out of her expression.

After accepting the fusion, Adam now just needed one more to unlock the third weapon slot. Unfortunately, he had spent the last of his points.

Still, it was worth it.

And I’ve got sixteen mastery shards to spend.

He wasn’t sure how much the next weapon slot might cost, so he wanted to hold off until after stage ten.

“Do you think you will beat the Tower of Glass?” Kat asked Adam seriously.

“I’m feeling better about my odds,” he replied. “I still need to talk to Sarah Ayala, just to make sure we’re on the same side. I also don’t know who the vampire girl will be replaced by.”

“She had to die, no matter what,” Adam said. “Hopefully her replacement isn’t another Flayed Lady or Nwetrou worshipper.”

Kat nodded. “I will attempt to talk to the people in my next group as well.”

Since they didn’t want to intrude on each other’s conversation, Adam and Kat sat by different tables as they queried the tavern. It was a bit strange, since Adam could see the people that appeared from her queries, but the System had apparently accounted for situations like these, since their individual search results were contained to their sides of the basement restaurant.

While Kat investigated the people she would encounter in her stage ten, Adam finally sought out Sarah Ayala.

When she appeared opposite him, none of her weapons or relics were visible, and she looked kind of defeated. Her caramel skin was sallow, the whites of her big brown eyes were red, and her black hair hung down around her face in a dishevelled manner.

As he watched her to try and figure out what was wrong, he noticed how she was holding something in her right hand.

Then she looked up and met his eyes.
