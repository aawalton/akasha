Somewhere in the hidden room within the secret realm withinthe hidden realm within the Four Corner Sword Realm, the former owner of the sword and the fat rat were watching the final battle of the trial unfold.

“Wow, I can’t believe she did that.” The fat rat said, its beady eyes wide with surprise. “How did she even get that evil sword in the first place?”

“I have no idea.” The former owner said, a smile creeping on his lips. “I thought it was forgotten by time, but to think that it was in her possession…”

“How does a Phoenix even do all of this?” The fat rat asked. “Even with the system, given her age, she should not have unlocked so many secrets. Hell, how did she even read it?”

“Maybe she got Universal Language early?”

As a former powerful being, the sword owner was privy to some secrets of the universe. He knew that humans mostly played a secondary role in this rat race, and knew many more things that nobody else should.

One can figure out a lot of things after living for many millennia, but that wasn't the only secret he had.

If one were to poke his brain, they would find that even one third of his knowledge would be enough for their brains to overheat.

But that wasn’t something he was going to talk about, and many secrets have already been taken to the grave with him.

“Universal Language?” The rat repeated the question, thinking. “It is indeed possible, but it’s rare. Not exceptionally so, but to get it this early, the system would really need to like you.”

He chuckled. “Maybe she is favored by the system then.”

“I hope not. Someone like her… I fear the world will never be the same if the system decided to back her up.”

“Wouldn’t it be more interesting? Not to mention that you would be the one serving her.”

“Certainly, you would say that, but all I see in my future are headaches. Especially after you forced me to pull that stunt. Speaking of, it seems like she… What? What the fuck?”

“How interesting…” The man commented as he looked at the image before him, watching the Phoenix refine the Yin Qi with her cultivation technique.

“What technique is that?!” The rat was genuinely in awe after sensing the Phoenix’s cultivation.

“Something not from this realm. It looks like our little friend is full of surprises.”

“Oh… Oh no…” The rat paced around, climbing up on the man's body and settling on his shoulder.

“Master, if she's a transmigrator, then things make more sense, but Master, I don’t want to deal with them. They are a Calamity!”

The man chuckled. “Don’t be silly, Squeaky. They don't simply become calamities just because you say they are. Not all transmigrators are Calamities.”

“That’s because most of them die before they can become one! But this one… she’s worse than a cockroach.”

“Then why worry? Serve her well, and you will rule above all other sword spirits once again.”

Squeaky gulped. “But how am I supposed to compete with that devil sword?”

“That’s a good question. I can’t really tell you, but I am confident that you will figure it out. Maybe she will manage to surprise you again.”

“Master…” Squeaky cried, but the man paid him no further heed.

“The fight has ended. I will go down there and have a chat.”

“WHAT?! Master, no!”

The man patted the rat’s head. “What’s the point of holding on so much to me? Let go, Squeaky; she will treat you well eventually. And don’t blabber too much; the less she knows, the less ire she will draw.”

“But Master, I…” Rats dripped down the fat rat’s little face, its beady eyes shining despite only being a spirit. “I can’t. I-”

“Squeaky.” The man said sternly. “I've decided. I’ll weaken that Spirit for her, and I will disappear. This will be the last time the Four Corner Sword Realm opens. Please, don’t look for me. She is worthy of inheriting my legacy.”

After saying that, the man’s figure disappeared, and the rat was forced to sit there and watch as the last remnant of the master he loved so much disappeared.”

“I wasn’t expecting you here,” I said, looking at the figure of the former owner of the sword. He looked even more dashing than his other figure, though in a plain, somewhat normal way. It was hard to put into words.

“It’s no big deal. I just came to say a few things before I disappear.”

“Disappear?”

He nodded. “It is most unfortunate, but I have no choice in the matter. I’ve been waiting for a successor for too long, and I think I’ve found my inheritor.”

“I'm not a human; doesn't that pose a problem?”

He shook his head and looked at the word. “No issue at all. You will be able to inherit everything in this place, granted you qualify at the end of the Four Corner Sword Realm.”

My eyes narrowed. “And what’s the catch?”

He smiled eerily, and I could tell that whatever it was he wanted me to do would be difficult.

“I have a promise to fulfill, to a woman.”

I raised a hypothetical eyebrow.

“She was very special to me; I'm not sure if she’s still alive after all these years. I have a Jade slip with all the information in my inheritance; if you are to get the chance, please give it to her.

“And one other thing, please forgive Squeaky. I’d put him up to this.”

“Squeaky?” I paused, thinking about it for a moment. “The rat?”

The former owner of the sword chuckled. “Yes, the rat.”

“So you put him to this? Why?”

“It was a test. Honestly, I wasn't expecting you to defeat me. Maybe wound me at most, but to burn away all the Yin Qi in my body? That was certainly something. A spectacle even. I was most impressed.”

“But why did he kill himself?”

“There was no point in continuing, since you had more resources than him.”

“Phew,” I whistled. “I hope you didn't do that in life.”

His eyes narrowed when I said that, but the amused expression on his face never disappeared. “Of course not, though I've done things I'm not proud of.”

I did the best impression of a shrug I could. “Whatever it takes to survive.”

“Indeed. Well, I suppose that was everything. Whether or not you deliver my message is up to you.”

Once that was said, he turned his full attention toward the sword and made a grasping motion toward it.

At first, the sword began to shake, resisting the pull the best it could, but it was currently only a Foundation Establishment level treasure. Could it really resist such an existence, even if they were but a mere fragment?

Lightning gathered around it, concentrating into multiple powerful bolts that shot toward the man almost immediately.

These bolts could deal great damage to the same figure earlier, but now they were like raindrops on a hot surface.

They didn't even get within five inches of him before they disappeared. The sword tried, to no avail, to get rid of his grasp on it, but it just wasn't possible.

It approached the man reluctantly, and once his hand was upon its hilt, the sword shivered like an abused pet.

It struggled, trying to break free of the man's power, but there was no way for it to do so.

Despite the insane quantity of lightning striking everywhere, to the point that even I was scared of it.

Despite it all, the former owner of the sword had no reaction.

He'd whispered something I couldn't quite understand, and the lightning around began calming down and retreating into the sword.

Seals appeared all around the blade, smashing into it countless times.

It let out shrieks of pain and agitation, yet remained unable to do anything except suffer the agony of having its spirit broken.

I couldn’t help but feel some pity for the poor thing, but after remembering all the times it had almost gotten me killed, I couldn’t care anymore.

It wasn’t like he was going to kill it, but just knocking it down a peg or two.

The seals covered the blade at some point, and I wish I had better ears to cover, as the metallic vibration accompanied by the soul shriek made my mind and body shudder.

Within minutes, the aggressive, bloodthirsty thing had become docile like a dog.

I'd never seen something like that, and I was incredibly impressed by the technique the man had displayed. It wasn't every day that you could witness that.

However, it was clear that doing that took a toll on the remaining soul energy he had.

“Why?” That was a valid question, as far as I was concerned. This guy I didn’t know suddenly wanted to offer me such great benefits, and I didn’t see why he wanted to. Even if he was looking for an heir, it can’t be that easy to just give up on the last vestige of consciousness you had left.

“Because I know you will do what I want you to. I do not care about anything else, and that was my only regret after dying.

“I see.”

Maybe I understood what he’d felt in that moment; there was a deep sense of loss that I couldn’t quite put my finger on. I knew why, of course, but…

Sighing, I nodded. “I thank you. I didn’t get your name from, uh… Squeaky.”

He only shook his head as he did the last few seals on the sword, and finally, it stopped screeching.

That was a good day to hear it finally be silent. The man flicked his wrist and sent it my way, seals still in place.

“Once it accepts you as your master, it will lose a seal. For every subsequent realm you grow in your cultivation, so will the sword. Right now, this artifact has been damaged, so its power can only rise up to the peak of Nascent Soul.

“If you want to allow it to further grow with you, you would need to refine it and repair it.”

“What? It’s been damaged? Wait… Do you know what this sword is?”

“Yes, and no. As long as you master it and wield it well, I am confident that you will uncover its truths. Although… I don’t know how you’d manage that, given that uh… You don’t have hands.”

Was he being awkward?

It was the first time since I’d met both his clone and his spirit that I’ve seen him be… awkward.

“I’ll manage.” I gratefully grabbed the sword and stored it within my dantian, grateful that I won’t need to use other factors to keep it at bay. “Thank you.”

“I hope you will fulfill my task, and that you will take care of Squeaky. Don’t hold it against him.”

I shook my head. How could I not do what he’d asked of me?

“How will I know how to find her? And how to tell her where the message comes from?”

“Hmm…” He remained lost in thought for a moment, staring deep into nothingness. Then, after a minute, he smiled. “You’ll figure it out. Grab the inheritance first.”

I could only hypothetically roll my eyes, as I didn’t quite know what to make of his pseudo-divinations.

“Very well. If fate will it.”

He chuckled when I said that. “Fate…”

Then, he just… vanished. I didn’t know if I’d blinked or if something else happened, but one moment he was there and the next he was just… gone.

And with that, the trial ended. I knew that because the annoying Squeaky mechanical voice sounded out once again in my fucking ears.

“Congratulations! You have officially finished the trial! You have… A lot of rewards!” The entire world turned dark as it said that, and a small light appeared in front of me, but it didn't do much.

Finally, for passing the fifth trial, you have obtained… a bag of holding!”
