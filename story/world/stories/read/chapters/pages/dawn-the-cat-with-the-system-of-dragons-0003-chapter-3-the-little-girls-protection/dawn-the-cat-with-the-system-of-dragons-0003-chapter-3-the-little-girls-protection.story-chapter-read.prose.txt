— Before the dogs returned to the dilapidated house —

The sky was clear, the sun was at its peak, and the weather was extremely hot and humid. Dense trees and extensive undergrowth made the temperature inside the forest somewhat manageable, but inside the town’s border, it was the opposite.

The workers pulling the wagons and carriages, the street vendors, the town guards, the adventurers, the rangers, and everyone outside were in discomfort due to the current weather conditions. But this was the peak working hour, and everyone had to go on with their daily lives.

Outside the town’s gate, there was a huge line of people waiting to enter, and guards were checking each one thoroughly, especially the adventurers covered in blood and carrying freshly hunted monsters and animals.

Every adventurer was wearing armor, and they were carrying various deadly weapons. The line outside the town was filled with them. Among them, a mother-daughter pair was waiting in line. Both had dark maroon hair and eyes, and both of them were covered in dirt.

The mother was wearing black pants, black boots, a white full-sleeved shirt, and light leather armor. She was carrying a sword at her waist and a long elemental staff on her back. Her daughter was wearing a long white frock with chainmail over it. She was only carrying a short elemental staff.

After waiting in line for a quarter of an hour, it was their turn to enter. “Mrs. Pagonis, you have returned! How was the training session?”

“Cinthia, why don’t you answer the guard uncle?”

“So, how was it, Ms. Cinthia?”

“Umm, uncle, it was baaad. The forest is very dirty. I don’t want to go anymore.”

“Hahaha… Don’t worry, young lady. You’ll get used to it.”

“Umm.”

“Anyway, you can go inside. Take care.”

“Okay, see you tomorrow, Mr. Markou,” Elenora replied, and they went inside the town.

“Cinthia, from now on, we’ll practice magical techniques in the forest, okay?”

Cinthia was old enough and had already learned a few magic techniques, so it was about time she started learning some practical skills. So, for the first time, her mother, Elenora, had taken her into the forest. Though the forest was extremely dangerous, the area outside the town’s border was relatively safe, and it was the perfect place for practicing new techniques. And more importantly, it would prevent the little girl from destroying their house.

“Hmm… But mama, why can’t I keep practicing on our lawn?”

“Controlling offensive magic is hard, dear. You’ll never be able to gain experience practicing on our lawn… and without experience, you won’t be able to get into Magic School.” Though what Elenora said was true, she ended up hiding the real reason behind her decision.

“But I dooon’t want to go to school.”

Expected. “Dear, don't you want to make a lot of friends? You’ll not be able to make friends if you don't go to school. Both mama and papa went to school, and it was very fun. I’m sure you’ll love it.” There were no kids in their neighborhood, so most of the time she stayed home with her parents. So, Elenora wanted her daughter to meet new people.

“Hmm…” Cinthia didn’t answer, just simply nodded with a sad expression.

Elenora knew her daughter wasn’t convinced yet, so she decided to coax her instead. “Look, dear, there is nothing to be sad about. You’ll be able to have a lot of pastries in school if you get a good score on your entrance exam.”

“Pastries?”

“Yes, you’ll get a lot of them if you do well in the entrance exam.”

“But what is an eetaance exam?”

“It is not eetance; it is entrance. It’s e, n, t, r, a, n, c, e, en-trance, okay?”

“Umm.”

“Anyway, it means you’ll have to show what you’ve learned.”

“If I show what I learned, then I’ll get pastries?”

Finally, “Yes, but you’ll have to do better than other kids to get pastries.”

“Ooooo… there will be more kids like me?”

“Yes, dear. There will be a lot of kids just like you.”

“Then I’ll be able to make friends?” Now, Cinthia was truly interested.

“Yes, dear, you’ll be able to make lots of friends and have pastries with them.”

“Ooooo…” Inspired by Elenora, she began imagining various things about school while looking here and there.

Suddenly, Cinthia heard a loud yelping sound. She turned her head and saw a dog rolling on the ground. Elenora also heard it, but she only glanced at the dog, then continued walking straight while holding Cinthia’s hand. For her, local animals causing trouble were an everyday occurrence.

Cinthia was curious, so she kept looking at the animals. She also noticed a black cat running away fast from the injured dog.

“Mama, mama…” She pointed towards the animals, but before she could finish, she saw something unbelievable.

After reaching the first dog, the cat sidestepped, attacked the dog’s ears, and then jumped towards the dog standing behind. Simultaneously, the dog standing behind also pounced toward the cat. The cat collided with the dog’s face, and it fell to the ground.

The first dog seized this opportunity, opened its jaw, grabbed the cat from the side, clamped it hard, and began shaking it violently. “Meeeeeeeeeooooooooowwww!!!”

“Meow.eow.eow.eow.eow.eow.” The cat immediately began scratching the dog’s jaw hard despite the bone-crushing pain.

Hearing the cat’s loud scream, even Elenora turned around and looked back. Cinthia immediately let go of her mother’s hand and started running towards the animals.

The cat’s relentless attack made the dog throw it on the ground. But before the dogs could attack again, the cat immediately rolled its body, got back on its feet, and arched its back while its tail pointed at the sky.

Elenora reacted quickly and ran towards her daughter. But Cinthia suddenly stopped, pointed her staff at the animals, and began circulating her elemental energy. Seeing this, Elenora chose not to grab her daughter forcefully.

“Meeeooooooow, eooooowww, eeeooooooow.” The cat started hissing so aggressively that the dogs stepped back a bit.

Cinthia wanted to drive the dogs away fast, but after her training session, she was hurting all over her body. She barely had any elemental energy to form her attack magic. Seeing this, Elenora raised her palms behind her daughter and started releasing her own energy.

“Cinthia, absorb the energy around you and then start forming your magic.”

“Umm…”

Cinthia quickly absorbed all the energy from her surroundings, formed a small air vortex using her staff, and then began using even more energy to create a small stone inside the vortex. And finally, she elongated the vortex and launched the stone with high air pressure. The dog that was closer to the cat got hit hard and fell down.

Then she formed another air vortex, created a small stone in between, elongated the vortex, and launched the stone towards the dog standing behind. This dog also fell down.

“Cinthia, why don’t you use the pebbles on the ground instead of creating stones?”

“Ooo!!!”

Cinthia picked up a handful of pebbles, created an air vortex with her staff, elongated its form, and put all the pebbles in the vortex. All the pebbles were launched towards the dogs at tremendous speed. But the pebbles were thrown in every direction.

“Don’t just put pebbles in the vortex... Maintain its current form and keep adding more pebbles from the center back of the vortex, otherwise pebbles will not hit the bad doggies.”

“Umm.”

While maintaining the elongated form of the vortex, she crouched down and started putting the pebbles into her magic formation one by one from the center back position of the vortex. The pebbles began launching automatically as she was putting them in. “BAD DOGGIES!!! Don’t bully the kitty.”

“You’re not aiming properly. Keep the aim towards the dogs, or you may hurt the kitty.”

“Umm.”

After a few seconds, the dogs began running away with their tails between their legs. The barrage made them fall down many times, but Cinthia was ruthless and didn’t stop, even after seeing them fall and hearing their yelp.

In the end, Cinthia fell on her butt due to extreme exhaustion. Elenora immediately picked her daughter up from the ground and held her in her arms. “Cinthia, are you okay?”

“Umm…” Cinthia then looked away and pointed towards the black cat. “Mama, look, the kitty is fine… she is walking away.”

“You did a great job saving the kitty…”

But suddenly, “Haaa!!! The kitty fell!!! Mama, the kitty fell!!! Let’s gooo…”

Elenora looked at the cat and remembered how the dog was clamping it and shaking it violently. It must have been injured then…

She ran as fast as possible and reached the cat. There were visible teeth marks on the cat’s body. It was bleeding profusely from those wounds, and moreover, the cat wasn’t breathing properly. She immediately put her daughter down. “Cinthia, don't move from here, okay? Mama needs to heal the kitty.”

“Umm.”

Elenora took her magic staff from her back, picked up the cat with one hand, and used her energy to create water and poured it directly over its body. But there was too much sticky dirt between its fur. “Ugh… was it playing in mud?”

She needed to work fast, so without wasting any time, she created an air vortex and began using the water creation technique inside it. After a few seconds, the water level inside the air vortex rose, and the water began moving along with the air pressure, just like water moves inside an oceanic cyclone. She then directed the vortex towards the wounds and began using its pressure to wash them properly.

After finishing up, she used her energy to create a thin, transparent barrier over the cat’s fur. This not only stopped the bleeding but also prevented dirt from entering again.

Now that the immediate problems were resolved, Elenora put the cat down on the ground, crouched beside it, closed her eyes, and began transferring her energy into the cat's body to scan it from the inside.

She found ruptured arteries causing severe internal bleeding and several fractured ribs, whose fragments had pierced the cat's left lung. Moreover, when the cat was inhaling, the blood from the ruptured arteries was entering the left lung. It'll die if not treated immediately… At least, the holes in the lungs are small… It’ll not be that hard…

Since the cat had already lost a lot of blood, and any more internal bleeding would kill the cat, she began transferring her energy to the affected areas.

First, she solidified her energy to create tiny barriers to cover the holes in the lung to prevent blood from entering. Then, she created straw-like barriers to connect all the ruptured arteries temporarily and prevent more blood loss. And finally, she created big pipe-like barriers to hold the fractured ribs.

And finally, it was time for the most complex procedure.

First, she started with the blood that had entered the lung during inhalation. She used liquid manipulation technique to move all the blood inside near the biggest hole. And just when the cat began exhaling, she dissolved the barrier of the biggest hole, used the exhaling pressure to push out the blood from inside, and then she solidified her barrier directly on the hole again. This prevented the blood from entering the lung during inhalation. Little by little, all the blood from inside the lung was removed, and she sealed the hole again with her energy.

Now that the lung’s immediate problem was resolved, it was time to focus on ruptured arteries. She dissolved the barrier around one of the arteries and used liquid manipulation to transfer the blood from one broken end of the artery to the other broken end. Simultaneously, she was also mixing the spilled blood with natural blood flow. This allowed her to send all the blood back into the arteries again. And finally, she sealed the arteries with her temporary straw-like barrier.

Now it was time for the most difficult part. She decided to focus on the arteries one by one.

First, she blocked blood flow from the arteries by contracting the diameter of her straw-like barriers. This also allowed her to hold the arteries tightly. After stopping blood flow, she contracted the length of the barrier to pull the broken ends of the arteries closer to each other and hold them together.

Then she began directing a huge amount of energy towards the damaged arteries so that damaged cells absorbed the energy and automatically sped up the self-healing process.

This immediately enabled blood clotting, and it closed the damaged part temporarily. Then cellular replication began in the inner lining of the broken arteries, which started forming new cells in the damaged areas. Elenora’s energy transfer also helped accelerate this process. As new cells were forming rapidly, the artery began healing at a visible rate.

It took her half an hour to heal all the ruptured arteries, and she deactivated the barriers around them. Now it was time to focus on the bone fragments inside the left lung.

Just like before, instead of fighting with the biology of the cat, Elenora decided to work with what she had.

When the cat began exhaling again, she immediately dissolved one of the small barriers covering one of the lung’s holes, carried a tiny bone fragment using air manipulation, and used exhaling pressure to move it through the same way through which the fragments had entered. Then she solidified her energy on the hole again and made sure to guide the fragment using air manipulation to move it back to its original position. Since previously she had created barriers around the fractured bones, she had to simultaneously dissolve the barrier to position the bone fragment to its original place and recreate the barriers around the fractured bone again.

This process took quite a while as there were many little fragments scattered inside the left lung.

After finishing, she again began transferring huge amounts of elemental energy to forcefully activate the rapid healing process in the bones. Slowly, the blood clots formed in the fractures, and soft tissues began forming, bridging the gap between the broken fragments. After a while, the soft tissues turned into a hard structure, and the excess part of the bones began breaking down until the bones regained their former state.

Elenora deactivated barriers around the bones and turned her attention to the holes in the left lung. Since the fractured, fragmented bones had only created tiny holes, it was easy to heal using a similar process she used before. She maintained the barrier position on the holes and transferred her energy to the injured cells. It drastically accelerated their natural healing process, and a small blood clot began forming in the holes. After blood clotting finished, new cells began regenerating, and new tissues began covering the damaged tissues. After some time, all the holes in the lung closed properly, and she deactivated the tiny barriers.

After healing all internal injuries, she turned her attention to the external wounds. But external wounds had already started healing because of the massive amount of energy she had transferred earlier. The cat was also breathing naturally. Only a few partially healed wounds remained.

Hmm… the rest of the external wounds would heal naturally… But I have already done a lot of work… why not just finish healing to the end?

Again, she transferred her energy to the damaged cells, and pink skin began forming under the blood clots. After a few seconds, the blood clots became extremely dry and began falling off. Elenora used her finger to remove them entirely. The new skin underneath was bright pink. She kept using more energy until the new pink skin darkened and solidified. And finally, the healing process was over.

Ohh right… It might develop a severe infection in the future… To stay on the safer side, she began transferring her energy to the cat's blood to raise its immunity forcefully and remove any impurities that may have entered the blood earlier.

After a while, Elenora sighed in relief. “Finally…”

The whole time, Cinthia kept standing beside Elenora quietly. She was observing how her mother was continuously transferring an excessive amount of elemental energy. She didn’t know what exactly her mother was doing, but she knew that her mother was doing something important, so she didn’t disturb her.

“Mama, mama, is the kitty okay?”

“Yes, dear. The kitty will be fine now... Now let’s go back home.”

“Mama, wait… Let’s wait until the cat opens its eyes.”

“Hmm…” I have already healed it, and there shouldn’t be any issue… but… Let’s just wait and confirm its condition. It’s not like anything will change, but… “Okay, dear, let's wait until it starts walking.”

“Yay!”

Elenora moved the cat into the shadow of a nearby tree and began waiting several steps away. After an hour, the cat regained consciousness and started looking here and there.

HISSS!!! When the black cat saw two humans standing a few meters away, it jumped back in shock and hissed loudly. Cinthia got startled, hid behind her mother, and began peeking at the cat from behind her mother's legs. Elenora held her magic staff just in case the cat started attacking. But the cat didn’t attack and kept glaring at them in confusion.

Elenora held her daughter’s hand, turned around, and started walking in the opposite direction, never looking back at the cat again. But she kept observing it using her senses and the surrounding environmental energy.

After they had gone a bit further, the cat began walking towards the dilapidated house and went inside. Elenora didn’t miss the place where the cat entered and smiled slightly. Okaay… so its den was attacked by the dogs…

“Mama, why did the kitty hiss at us? Didn’t we just save it?”

“Dear, the black kitty was injured by the dog, and it lost consciousness. Don't you remember? I am sure it doesn’t know what we did.”

“Ooo…” Cinthia looked down with a sad expression. Observing her, her mother understood what was going on in her daughter’s head.

Of course… She sighed. Having a reliable familiar wouldn’t be bad… It might help her in the future if nurtured properly… Though it does have an aggressive personality, it shouldn’t create a huge problem if trained properly…

“Sweetheart, do you want to have the kitty?”

“Can I really have the kitty!!!”

“Of course, you can…”

“Yay!” Cinthia jumped in happiness.

“Let’s come back later to give it food... If the kitty likes us, it’ll surely come to us.”

“Okaay…”

“But before that, there are rules you have to follow.”

“Rules?”

“Yes, there are rules you have to follow… When you give the cat food, you must not get closer to it. You’ll have to step away after giving it food.”

“But Mama, why? Won't the kitty like it if we come closer and give it food?”

“No, darling… Wild animals always stay on alert. If you suddenly get too close to them, they might attack. So, you need to show them you're not dangerous and you're only providing food... That’s why you just need to give it food and then step back just enough to stay within its sight. You need to show clearly that you are only there to provide food… Do you understand?”

“Umm…”

“Good... You'll have to repeat this every morning and evening... The only difference is that each time you provide food, you should stand a little closer than you did the last time. By consistently doing this, you'll earn the kitty's trust within a week, and you’ll be able to get closer little by little.”

“It will take a week!” Cinthia said sadly.

“You can’t rush it, dear… You have to be patient.”

“Umm…”

“Okay, then let's buy food for her.”

“Umm… let’s gooo…” Cinthia got excited and began pulling her mother’s hand while walking in front. Elenora smiled and let her be.

After a while, Elenora and Cinthia reached the inner part of the town, and they went straight to a meat stall. There, the seller was using skewers to roast small pieces of meat. The whole street was filled with its aroma.

“How much for the roasted meat?”

“50 hyls for each skewer… If you take a full bag, you’ll get six skewers for 250 hyls.”

“Hmm… Give me a full bag.”

“Great… wait a bit… let me roast it properly for you.”

“Sure.”

After taking a bag of roasted meat, they went back to the dilapidated house. Elenora removed all the meat pieces from the skewers and placed them near the broken wall and backed away a good distance along with her daughter.

Even after about an hour, the cat didn’t come out, and the little girl was getting restless because of it. She didn’t know what to do.

“Don't worry, Cin, the kitty is just scared. It will come out eventually.”

“Umm…”

The black cat finally came out after three whole hours. It walked very slowly, looked here and there, sniffed the area, and it finally found the roasted meat lying on the ground. After sniffing and checking the surroundings, it started eating.

“Cinthia, did you notice where the cat came from?”

“Umm…”

“You’ll need to come back at the evening and give the kitty food… all right?”

“All right…” Cinthia replied in a childish tone.

By evening, adventurers who went out during midday began coming back into town, and the streets of the marketplace became crowded because of it.

“Cin darling, don’t you want to feed the kitty anymore?”

“Ohh… sorry, Mama, I’ll go now…”

Silly girl… “Here, take this bag. The money to buy roasted meat, a small container, and a water pouch is inside. After buying meat, go to the broken house, make the meat pieces small by hand, and place them on the ground. And use the container to pour water for the kitty, okay?”

“Umm…”

“Don’t get too close to the kitty; it will be scared. Place the meat and water on the ground and back away.”

“Umm… I’ll come back fast...” Saying this, she ran out of the house.

She sure is in a hurry… Elenora sighed.

Cinthia bought meat skewers from the same stall and went to her destination. Just as her mother instructed, she separated the meat pieces, made them small with her hand, placed them along with the container, and poured water for the cat.

After a few minutes, the cat came out of her hideout. It noticed the girl standing far away and began looking at her vigilantly. After determining that the girl was not dangerous, the cat began eating the roasted meat. Seeing this, Cinthia was delighted.

After having the meal and water, the cat went back inside, and Cinthia also went back home.

The next morning, Cinthia went back to the dilapidated house with meat skewers and a water pouch. This time, she had strapped a big container on her back using her father’s belt, and was also carrying two small buckets full of water.

Last evening, when the cat came out, it only ate its food and went back inside. Remembering this, Cinthia had a simple thought. The kitty doesn't go out much, so how does it bathe? Mama tells me to bathe twice a day, and I like bathing… Hmm… Kitty must like bathing too, but it can't bathe because it doesn't have water... I get it! I'll give it enough water to bathe properly… But Mama will scold me… hehe, I will sneak out water for the kitty…

After reaching the dilapidated house, the little girl put roasted meat pieces on the ground, put both big and small containers beside the meat pieces, and filled them with water. Then she walked away to a safe distance carrying the empty buckets. This time, the girl was standing a bit closer than the previous day.

The cat emerged again but didn't immediately approach the meat. It simply observed the girl. Cinthia didn't move and started looking here and there to show the cat that she wasn't dangerous.

After observing for a few seconds, the cat realised that the girl was not an immediate danger and began eating the roasted meat. But it never took its eyes off the girl. After finishing, the cat drank water from the small container and went back inside, ignoring the big container.

“The kitty didn’t bathe!!!” Cinthia sighed heavily. “Maybe it will bathe later…” Saddened by her wasted effort, she went back to her house, leaving both containers behind.

For the next three days, Cinthia continued following her mother’s plan.

She kept getting closer to the cat little by little whenever she went to provide food. She also understood why the cat had such intense wariness. It wasn’t hard for her to hear the mewling sounds. Clearly, the black cat was raising its litter. And, more importantly, the nearby dogs were relentless.

Every time the dogs got a chance, they came closer. The cat didn’t go out to fight them anymore; Cinthia was doing most of the work. Every time she noticed the dogs getting too close to the house, she chased them away with passionate fire in her eyes.

By the fourth day, the little girl realized that the black cat had exactly five kittens hidden within the rubble. The different mewling sounds confirmed it to her. The cat had also begun approaching the girl, not close enough for her to pet it. But it was more than enough for Cinthia to feel reassured about her relationship with the cat.

On the evening of the fifth day, the black mother cat decided it was time to venture out and search for a new hideout.

Though she was getting food and water continuously, she felt the dogs' relentless returns made her current spot unsustainable. Her kittens would soon start walking, and it would be very hard to protect them later, especially in these circumstances. The girl chased them away every time she saw them, but this den wouldn't remain safe forever against such persistent threats.

Since the distant houses offered no safer alternative and the streets were too exposed, she headed into the nearby forest. Forests were inherently dangerous, but the continuous harassment from the dogs made it a more viable option than her current spot. And it was better to move out sooner than to face the dogs when her kittens grew up.
