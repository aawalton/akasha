"I hope Brother Xiao Fan is alright..." Fatty Li muttered under his breath.

Ning glanced sideways at him.

He had returned late after placing his bet, and by then his original seat had already been taken. In the end, Fatty Li had simply dragged him over and plopped him down beside him without ceremony.

At the moment, Fatty Li looked genuinely anxious. His brows were tightly knit, his eyes never leaving the arena as his lips moved faintly, almost as if he were praying under his breath.

Then he pulled out a BLT.

Ning blinked.

By the time he looked again, the sandwich was already in Fatty Li's mouth.

Ning blinked once more.

A second BLT had appeared in Fatty Li's hand, freshly unwrapped, while the first one was somehow... gone.

"...What?"

Before Ning could properly process what he'd just witnessed, the same thing happened again. Then again.

Fatty Li ate with terrifying efficiency. There was no wasted movement, only a smooth transition from hand to mouth to disappearance that Ning's eyes were beginning to have trouble following.

At this point, Ning was fairly certain this qualified as a supernatural ability.

If there were a novel about this world, Fatty Li would definitely be some kind of gag character.

After all, his movement speed clearly increased whenever food was involved.

"Brother Ning?" Fatty Li suddenly asked, pausing halfway through another sandwich. "Is something wrong?"

Ning coughed lightly. "Nothing. I was just... wondering if you can really eat all that."

Xin Fu had brought back enough food to stock their stall for the rest of the day.

Fatty Li had bought all of it.

"This much?" Fatty Li looked down at the pile beside him before answering proudly, "It's nothing."

He took another bite.

"When I'm worried, I eat to cope," he continued between chews. "So don't worry. I'll definitely finish it."

Ning slowly nodded. "I see."

Fatty Li then proceeded to demonstrate exactly what he meant.

Whenever he took a bite, his anxious expression disappeared and was replaced by pure bliss. The moment he swallowed, however, his brows knitted together again as he stared worriedly toward the arena.

"Brother Xiao Fan should be alright..."

Another bite.

Bliss.

Swallow.

Worry.

Chew.

Bliss.

Ning watched the cycle repeat several times with almost mechanical precision. At this point, he wasn't sure which was more impressive: Fatty Li's eating speed or his ability to regulate his emotions through sandwiches.

As expected, anyone who managed to stick close to a protagonist was bound to be somewhat weird. First Sun Jing, and now Fatty Li.

Of course, Ning never once considered the possibility that he might also be weird.

...

Inside the arena, the formation barrier hummed to life once more, sealing ten figures inside the massive bluestone platform. For a few moments, nobody moved.

Unlike Ning's earlier match, where most of the contestants had immediately decided to gang up on him, the atmosphere here was much more tense. The reason was obvious.

Xiao Hong.

As one of the top four contenders in the prediction rankings, his reputation had already spread among the newcomers. Most of the disciples sharing the arena with him were only at the third or fourth stage of Qi Condensation, and none seemed particularly eager to draw his attention.

Xiao Hong stepped toward the center of the arena, clasping his hands casually behind his back as he looked over the others.

"I'll make this simple," he announced. "Anyone who gets between Xiao Fan and me, leave the arena now. Otherwise, don't complain when you're carried out."

Classic, Ning thought from the spectator stands, resting his chin on his fist. Certified young master behavior. Threatening an entire arena full of people before the fight even starts. Truly a time-honored tradition.

The other contestants exchanged uncertain looks. Even the slowest among them could tell they had stumbled into some kind of personal grudge, and nobody wanted to become collateral damage.

Xiao Fan didn't bother responding. He simply adjusted his stance and kept his eyes on Xiao Hong.

The chime rang, signaling the beginning of the match, and the tense arena immediately erupted into motion.

Two disciples lunged at each other in hopes of securing a quick elimination, while another retreated and hurriedly formed hand seals for a defensive technique. Swords clashed, spells flew, and within seconds the platform had descended into the usual chaos of a free-for-all.

Xiao Hong ignored most of it and started walking toward Xiao Fan.

A disciple happened to rush across his path.

Xiao Hong flicked his wrist.

A condensed beam of qi shot from his fingertip and struck the disciple's hastily raised shield. The defense shattered on impact, sending the unfortunate cultivator flying backward into the formation barrier before he dropped unconscious onto the stone.

"Flies," Xiao Hong muttered without stopping.

On the other side of the arena, Xiao Fan had also begun moving.

Compared to Xiao Hong's direct approach, Xiao Fan kept near the edges of the fighting and avoided becoming surrounded. He dodged attacks by the smallest distance necessary and only struck when someone overextended or exposed an opening.

A disciple tried to ambush him from behind with a heavy iron saber. Xiao Fan shifted aside, caught the man's wrist during the downswing, and used his own momentum to pull him forward before driving an elbow into his chest.

A crack sounded as the disciple dropped onto the stone.

Ning's attention sharpened.

Xiao Fan really was good at this.

It wasn't simply a matter of speed or strength. He rarely made unnecessary movements, never stayed engaged with one opponent long enough for someone else to surround him, and constantly adjusted his position according to the people nearby. Even his attacks seemed chosen more for efficiency than appearance.

For someone who had supposedly spent most of his life being looked down upon because of his low-grade spiritual root, his actual combat instincts were ridiculously good.

Xiao Fan didn't linger over the fallen disciple either. He moved on at once, slipping between two other contestants and subtly forcing them into each other's paths. One panicked and swung, the other instinctively retaliated, and Xiao Fan was already gone by the time they realized what had happened.

Meanwhile, Xiao Hong seemed to be growing increasingly annoyed that other people were still standing.

Another disciple attempted a wide-area technique, perhaps hoping to force everyone away from him. Xiao Hong jabbed a finger forward, sending a thin stream of qi directly into the half-formed spell. The technique detonated before it could fully take shape, knocking its caster off his feet and sending him tumbling across the arena.

The number of contestants rapidly dwindled. Between Xiao Hong simply overpowering anyone unfortunate enough to approach him and Xiao Fan taking advantage of every opening he could find, the others didn't last long.

Ning almost felt bad for them.

In the grand scheme of things, they had probably entered this arena expecting an ordinary first-round match. Instead, they had somehow been placed in the middle of a protagonist's personal grudge match.

Truly, the life of a background character was difficult.

Within a few minutes, only three contestants remained.

The last unrelated disciple stood awkwardly between Xiao Hong and Xiao Fan, looking from one to the other as if reconsidering every decision that had brought him here.

Xiao Hong solved the problem for him.

Without fully turning around, he sent a wave of compressed qi over his shoulder. The attack caught the disciple in the ribs and knocked him across the stone platform, where he remained.

Now only Xiao Hong and Xiao Fan were standing.

Xiao Hong rolled his shoulders and finally gave Xiao Fan his full attention.

"So the trash managed to survive the clearing."

Xiao Fan lowered his center of gravity and adjusted his footing.

"You should feel honored," Xiao Hong continued as qi began gathering around his body. "I'll make sure you remember exactly where you belong."

Ning sighed internally.

And there it is. The mandatory pre-boss-fight trash talk.

Xiao Hong moved first.

He crossed the distance quickly and drove a fist toward Xiao Fan's head. Xiao Fan raised his forearm to redirect it, but Xiao Hong immediately followed with another punch, then a low kick that forced Xiao Fan backward.

The difference in their raw strength was obvious.

Xiao Fan could redirect some of the force, but every blocked attack still pushed him back. Rather than trying to compete directly, he kept changing angles, slipping past the heavier strikes whenever possible and only counterattacking when Xiao Hong left an opening.

Xiao Hong didn't give him many.

A sweeping kick forced Xiao Fan backward before a heavy palm slammed into the place he'd been standing. The bluestone cracked beneath the impact.

Xiao Fan regained his footing and immediately stepped back in, driving a compact punch toward Xiao Hong's ribs.

Xiao Hong managed to block it with his arm, but the impact still forced him half a step backward.

His expression changed.

Ning noticed it too.

That punch had been far heavier than something from an ordinary fifth-stage cultivator.

The two collided again.

Xiao Hong continued pressing forward with his superior cultivation, forcing Xiao Fan to give ground, but the longer they fought, the less effective his attacks seemed to become. Xiao Fan was learning his rhythm. The first few attacks had forced him to block directly, but now he was redirecting them, and a little later, he was avoiding some entirely.

Even his breathing was becoming steadier.

Ning narrowed his eyes.

He's adapting.

"You little cockroach!" Xiao Hong snapped, throwing another heavy punch.

This time, Xiao Fan stepped inside the swing instead of retreating. His elbow snapped upward and caught Xiao Hong across the jaw.

It wasn't enough to seriously injure him, but it forced him back several steps.

The audience erupted.

Xiao Hong's face darkened.

"I don't care what kind of medicine you swallowed to reach the fifth stage of Qi Condensation," he hissed. "I'll show you the difference between someone like you and the true inheritance of the Xiao Clan!"

Qi rapidly gathered around his raised hand.

"Crushing Stone Finger!"

Ning immediately leaned forward.

This was what he'd been waiting for.

An Earth-grade martial art.

Ning himself was still primarily using mortal-grade martial arts, so naturally, he'd researched the difference. Mortal-grade techniques were generally simple and focused on refining a limited number of moves, while Spirit-grade martial arts were more complete, often containing combinations of attacks, transitions, movement, or defense.

Earth-grade techniques were where things became much more interesting.

Their techniques contained deeper principles that could grow alongside the cultivator, and sufficiently high mastery could even help someone begin touching upon martial intent. That was one of the reasons major clans guarded such inheritances so carefully.

As for Heaven-grade martial arts, Ning had only found vague descriptions, and most of those sounded like the authors had been competing to see who could use the most impressive words. Though, the consensus was that this type of technique could grow with the user.

For now, Earth-grade was more than enough to interest him.

Golden qi condensed around Xiao Hong's finger until even the surrounding air seemed to tremble. Cracks spread beneath his feet as he poured more qi into the technique.

Ning watched carefully. He wanted to see the difference for himself.

Xiao Hong thrust his finger forward, and a concentrated beam of golden qi tore across the arena.

Xiao Fan didn't dodge. Instead, he planted his feet and drew his fist back as his qi rapidly gathered around his arm.

Ning's eyebrows rose.

Oh?

Xiao Fan punched.

[Heaven-Shattering Fist]

There was nothing particularly elaborate about the attack. Xiao Fan simply drove his fist forward, concentrating an enormous amount of qi into the strike.

Then the two techniques collided.

A violent explosion shook the arena, cracking the reinforced bluestone beneath their feet as the impact sent dust and fragments of stone outward. Even the formation barrier rippled from the force.

Through the dust, Xiao Hong's voice rang out.

"How is this possible?! That's an Earth-grade technique! After you were stripped of your status, where could an outcast like you possibly find a complete Earth-grade manual?!"

His shock quickly turned into anger.

"It doesn't matter! Your cultivation is still lower than mine!"

Xiao Hong forced more qi into his technique, and the golden light pressed forward, gradually forcing Xiao Fan back. His legs bent beneath the pressure, and blood began seeping from his right arm as his muscles strained to maintain the technique.

Xiao Hong laughed. "Know your place!"

Xiao Fan gritted his teeth. "No."

His fist moved forward.

Ning's expression changed as he noticed something shift in Xiao Fan's technique. The qi that had previously been straining against Xiao Hong's attack suddenly stabilized, becoming noticeably more concentrated around his fist.

Did he just improve it? Right in the middle of the fight?

Ning stared.

Of course he did.

Xiao Fan took another step forward. "Break!"

Cracks appeared across the golden technique before the entire thing shattered. The backlash struck Xiao Hong's extended hand first, forcing his arm aside, and Xiao Fan's fist continued straight through the collapsing qi before slamming into his chest.

Xiao Hong flew backward and crashed into the formation barrier. Blood spilled from his mouth as he dropped onto the stone, unconscious before he could even attempt to stand.

For several seconds, the arena was quiet. Xiao Fan remained standing near the center of the damaged platform, breathing heavily as blood ran down his right arm.

Meanwhile, Ning was watching all this like a wife who had just seen her husband run into a wall with an erection only to break his nose first.

Extremely disappointing, but not surprising.

He had genuinely wanted to see how Xiao Fan would overcome an Earth-grade technique through finesse, timing, insight, or some other method Ning could actually learn from.

Instead, this guy had pulled out another Earth-grade martial art and punched through it with an even stronger attack.

Ning couldn't replicate that at all.

The outcome of the fight itself hadn't been particularly surprising. Too many things had favored Xiao Fan from the beginning. The only real surprise was the martial art he'd used.

"How did Xiao Fan defeat Xiao Hong?!"

The crowd finally erupted.

"What technique was that?!"

"That martial art is one of the five incomplete Earth-grade techniques! It's so fragmented that everyone who practiced it suffered qi deviation. How did Xiao Fan master it?!"

Ning glanced toward the speaker.

These people should seriously consider applying for jobs at the Martial Pavilion.

Then a strange thought occurred to him.

Wait... doesn't that include me? I recognized the technique the moment he used it.

Ning decided not to think too deeply about that.

"Brother Xiao Fan really won!"

Fatty Li, who had been glued to the arena throughout the entire fight, suddenly grabbed Ning and pulled him into a crushing hug.

"...Fatty Li, too strong!"

Ning struggled to pry him off. Even with his body refinement, Fatty Li's arms barely moved.

Something was clearly wrong with this guy's physique.

"O-oh, sorry!" Fatty Li hurriedly released him.

Ning adjusted his robes and took another look at Xiao Fan before closing his eyes.

Ning casually watched the next few matches, mainly to observe the strengths and weaknesses of his potential opponents. At the same time, he made sure to show his support for the members of his association. After all, connections required constant maintenance, even if the gesture was mostly symbolic.

Sun Jing.

The top-ranked newcomer, and the person most people believed had the highest chance of winning the entire competition.

Some people said his thought process was difficult to understand. Clearly, those people had never met the guy standing beside him.

Love and Friendship.

Yes. From now on, he would fight with the power of love and friendship by his side.
