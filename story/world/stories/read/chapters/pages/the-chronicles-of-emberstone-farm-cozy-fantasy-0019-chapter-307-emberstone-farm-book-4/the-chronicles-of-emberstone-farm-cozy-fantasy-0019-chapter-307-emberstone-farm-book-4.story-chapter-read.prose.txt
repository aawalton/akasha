I read the instructions and thought about when to travel as I continued watering the crops with the others.

Yawning, I looked up at the blue sky studded with fluffy cumulus clouds. The air was getting warmer, and we no longer needed to wear our coats when we went outside. A thinner jacket was all we needed. The trees that had lost their foliage over the winter were putting out bright green leaves, a process that Eggy found fascinating.

Right now, he was talking to a tree, admiring its energy. “Mr. Tree, you’re growing a lot! You should grow biiiiiig like me.”

I smiled at him and finished watering my part of the field. Today we planted a nice batch of [Soybeans], which we would sell to other farmers who wanted to grow them, too. Interestingly, the System information about it had changed.

[Soybean:

Revered as one of the Five Sacred Grains, it is a very useful legume with nitrogen-fixing properties whose beans can be made into many useful products such as soy sauce, soy milk, soybean paste, bean flour, and various kinds of tofu, tofu skin, and soybean oil.

Special note: The plant exhibits heliotropism by tracking the sun's movement during the day and nyctinasty by folding its leaves into a drooping sleep position at night. Thus, it is not eligible to become Star-Touched.]

“I hadn’t realized it doesn’t benefit from the event,” I said to my apprentices. “But I guess it’s okay. We have to plant it because of the demand.”

“Maybe there will be a sun-related event. Then the [Soybeans] should have an advantage,” said Kharli.

“Even if it doesn’t become Star-Touched, [Soybean] is always good. I like tofu!” said Mo. “They say it makes your skin smoother.”

“Really?” Lari looked intrigued. “That means it will sell for a higher price.”

We chatted a little more about our current task, and then, when we finished watering the field, I told them about the new quest.

“Baby, come here. Do you want to go flying on Mom’s ship?” I knew he would say yes, so I didn’t wait for his answer and turned to my apprentices. “Please go and tell Fengying we’re going out. We’ll need a packed lunch. Send a message to Shuye to ask if he wants to join us. I’ll tell Baiyu myself.”

“I’ll race you!” With a cheeky grin, Mo immediately ran off.

“No fair! You didn’t say go!” said Kharli before she chased after her.

Lari didn’t waste his breath complaining and ran.

“Wait for me!” Eggy joined in the fun, his little legs pumping for all they were worth.

I laughed and followed after them at a more sedate pace.

***

I had hoped to finish the quest today, but it was not to be. Prince Baiyu had urgent business to take care of, so I decided to wait until tomorrow. In the meantime, we started one of the quests that I had passed over before, since I didn’t want to be thought of as a witch who grew poisonous plants. Now that my position in society was secure, it was time to build my own scary garden.

[The Forbidden Physic Garden:

The dose makes the poison. Everything on earth can be harmful if too much of it is eaten or drunk. Contrariwise, in the hands of a skilled pharmacist, even toxic substances can be medicinal if the right dose is given.

Create a walled garden with at least five poisonous plants.

Reward: Farming Exp]

My plan was to grow and harvest a test batch of these special crops for expert alchemists and pharmacists to make into medicine.

There was an attached file that listed all the plants and their medicinal uses, but I was not about to risk messing around with the most dangerous plants, especially since the System’s text file said that these were special varieties whose toxic properties were stronger than their regular cousins.

For this quest, I chose [Angel’s Trumpet], [Devil’s Trumpet], [Lily of the Valley], [Deadly Nightshade], and [Oleander] because they seemed the least dangerous.

My apprentices thought that the [Angel’s Trumpet] and [Devil’s Trumpet] sounded the most promising.

[Angel’s Trumpet:

A fast-growing shrub that produces large, trumpet-shaped hanging flowers that are prized for their beauty and pleasant fragrance. Every part of the plant is highly toxic if ingested.]

[Devil’s Trumpet:

A low-growing, bushy plant that produces large, upright funnel-shaped flowers with a sweet scent. Every part of the plant is highly toxic if ingested.]

“I don’t know what an ‘angel’ and ‘devil’ are, but they must be powerful.” Kharli stirred the [Angel Trumpet Seeds] with a gloved finger.

Eggy took my hand and started swinging it. “But Mom, poison is bad! Why are you planting bad plants?”

I grinned. “Because… I am a bad Mom!”

Growling, I bent down and pretended to gnaw on his hand like a wild beast.

“Bad Mommy!” Eggy gave a delighted laugh.

I stopped and nodded at the others, signaling them to explain while I straightened up and patted my child’s head.

“I know! I know!” Mo raised her hand. “It’s like the story of Big Ears from ‘The Merry Brotherhood of the Marsh.’ He got poisoned by the Qi Dispersal Powder, and the Heavenly Physician saved his life by giving him the Feigning Death Pill.”

“Fighting poison with poison.” Kharli smiled and nodded. “I remember that.”

“And there was a part where the Original Seven cured themselves by drinking Purple Cloud Poison when they had gut worms from drinking bad water,” said Lari.

Of course, this all went over Eggy’s head completely, but it didn’t matter. His eyes shone, and he left my side to take Kharli’s hand and say, “Lily, tell me that story.”

I frowned. “Say the magic word, baby.”

“Please, Lily, please!”

Kharli laughed and agreed to read a story to him later.

“Okay, I’ll get started. Eggy, you stay outside the field with Mr. Bear.” I detached my puppet from my belt and threw him into the air. When he landed, he was as tall as Eggy and clad in a [Strawberry Print Bunny Suit].

“Bunny Bear! I love you.” Eggy immediately pounced on the puppet and kissed him.

I waited until they were beyond the field’s boundary before placing the nine stone [Protective Steles] around it in a circle.

[Protective Stele:

These white jade pillars are carved with spell formations that purify the air and soil.

Spell Stone consumption: 100 per day]

[Spell Stones] were a basic item in Adventure Incarnate. According to the game lore, the players used them to cast magic.

This requirement was the other reason I hadn’t planted this type of crop. The total cost for all the pillars was 900 [Spell Stones] per day, which was fine since I started off with 2,147,483,647 of them, but it seemed wasteful since I wasn’t sure if they would sell for much.

The cylindrical white pillars had a domed convex top and pulsed with a golden glow that ran through what looked like veins. When the last one was placed, they started emitting a low, thrumming sound that died out a few minutes later. I waited a few minutes more, but nothing more happened.

“Ready!” I called out to the others.

They waved their understanding, and we all picked our starting spots.

Three old oaks sat at the far corner, their branches still filling out with the new season's leaves, pale green against the blue of the sky. Two sparrows were darting in and out of the lower branches of the oaks. I began my work there, swinging my [Hoe] down into the ground to till the soil with Eggy at my side.

The soil was dark and damp from this week’s rain. Each swing of my tool released a wet, earthy scent. My boots left deep imprints as I walked. Eggy sometimes used a stick to poke at the ground curiously, but mostly he just watched me and cheered me on. Once the row of tiles was tilled, I fertilized the soil with [Mysterious Goo] and planted the seeds. Then the two of us watered the plants. Mr. Bear was also holding a watering can, but his was merely decorative. Puppets could not farm.

[The Forbidden Physic Garden: Quest Complete

Rewards: Farming exp]

“Great!” I said to Eggy. “The quest gave me some nice exp.”

Eggy clapped his hands and gave me a kiss on the cheek as his reward.

Prince Baiyu arrived and Eggy threw himself at his father who picked him up and swung him round and round.

“Dad! We’re growing bad, bad plants.”

Prince Baiyu didn’t ask me about what Eggy said since I had told him about the poison garden before spring even started. He just smiled and kissed us.

The kids had also finished their work and joined us.

“Do you want to come with us to see the animals?” Lari asked Eggy.

“Yes! I will take all their hearts.” Eggy really loved the [Sweet Heart Gauge] I had added.

After they left, Prince Baiyu eyed the pillars with an unreadable expression on his face. He cleared his throat twice and seemed to be about to say something, but stopped before he did.

“What?” I asked.

His lips twitched as he pointed at the pillars. “Those…”

“Yes?”

“Don’t they look…?”

I rolled my eyes. “What? Can you please just spit it out?!”

“Well.” He put his arms around my waist. “My dear, I’m just imagining all the jokes.”

“Jokes?” I still didn’t understand.

“People will say there are massive throbbing shafts erected here!”

I looked at the pillars once again, and my mouth fell open in shock. “No way!”

Shuye had lunch with us, and we set out for the [Grotto of the Silken Clouds] right after the meal. The ship I was using only had a few seats, so only a few of us could use it. Our party consisted of me, Prince Baiyu, Eggy, Lari, Kharli, Mo, and Shuye.

We went to the launch field south of the house. I summoned the ship and poured a glass of peach juice for everyone as I proposed a toast.

“To the moon and back!”

A magic circle appeared on the ground and flashed twice before it disappeared and was replaced by the [Heavenly Palace Rocket Ship].
