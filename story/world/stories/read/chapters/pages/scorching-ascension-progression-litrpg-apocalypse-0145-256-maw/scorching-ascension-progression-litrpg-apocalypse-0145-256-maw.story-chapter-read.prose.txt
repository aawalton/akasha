Now that the heavy heat was much more bearable, Ethan found it easier to see the attacks coming. Even though they crossed the distance separating him from the archer almost instantaneously, the sharp sight of [Sphinx’s Gaze] coupled with his recently improved control over [Draconic Wings] made dodging the attacks a breeze. Still, closing the range was still a bit risky but by now, he was close enough to return some fire. And what better way was there to deal with ice then fire?

Cocking his arm back, Ethan called for his flames, focusing them into a powerful—though hopefully not lethal fireball—and he was shocked at how easily and quickly the fire coalesced between his claws. While he was used to how the fire-aligned Draconic set made the flames more responsive, and how the title Arcanum of Flames bolstered his affinity with fire, empowering the flames further, he was still surprised by how intensely the spell had formed.

It felt as easy and natural as flexing a muscle. And it was all thanks to the freshly upgraded [Child of Flames].

Not wanting to put any more mana in the [Overloading Fireball], he lobbed it at the archer whom now he could see better while at the same time, three more fireballs began glowing above his brow. The archer was a red-haired woman in furs and pelts that certainly didn’t match the local weather. She had a dangerous-looking crystalline long-bow and as she pulled on the string, an arrow took shape in a second. The stranger took her time aiming, then shot her arrow and before she could reset her arm, Ethan’s fireball detonated mid-air, intercepted by the projectile.

Still, that was one less arrow coming at him, and it allowed him to close in the distance.

Ethan sent all three weakened fireballs at her feet, and dove right into the maelstrom of fire and super-heated sand, only to jump away when a wave of bone-chilling air blasted him back. A thin layer of ice crunched under his scales as he landed and skidded to a stop at the bottom of the small dune, and as the smoke and dust parted, a smoking but mostly unharmed figure stood at the center of an already melting flower-like formation of jagged ice.

“That’s not the proper way to say hello, is it?” she grinned at him, bow pointed low. While he was pretty sure he heard a couple of English words in there, he was almost sure that the woman sounded Scottish, and seeing his [Universal Tongue] kick in to translate almost got a chuckle out of him. Still, it confirmed that he wasn’t the only individual from Earth within this rift.

[Human - Level 85 (II)]

“Neither is shooting arrows at strangers,” he shot back with a smile as he spread his wings, ready to hop back in the air at a moment’s notice.

She nodded, still smiling, but she still took a second to glance around as she spoke. “American aye? How are things over that side of the pond? Not too…”

She trailed off, frowning, and her posture tensed. Half a second later, Ethan realized why.

The ground was rumbling. Softly at first, but soon, it was joined by uneasy buzz that filled the air, and before he could even parse what he was feeling, Ethan realized he was cringing away from the discomfort. In the same way that nails on a chalkboard could cause a physical reaction, the buzzing was doing the same to him but on a deeper level. He could feel it in his spirit. The spellforms were shuddering, and with every second, the buzzing was growing louder and the feeling was getting worse.

As soon as he realized the effect it was having on him, he leaped into the air, only for the buzz to turn into a shriek, and Ethan found himself falling back on the sand and by the time he recovered, his spellforms began failing him.

The eyes were the first to go. They were then quickly followed by the harpy’s talons, [Breath Gland], and even the skeleton, making Ethan feel uncomfortably light for the first time in a while. One by one, the magics enhancing him were ripped away from him, until barely five seconds after the buzzing had first reached his ears, only his scales and claws were left and even then, he could feel the former barely hanging on, with his bare skin showing in a few parts, mostly down his limbs. As for the stats, he didn’t think he lost them, but he couldn’t tell for sure and he didn’t dare look away from his surroundings.

Not yet, at least.

As for the woman he’d been speaking to, she looked frailer. She must have been using something to enhance her form as well but with the soul-wrenching buzz that filled their ears, she was only left with a t-shirt and pair of jeans, with only her bow still in her hand and just as suddenly as the sound had come, it cut off.

The soft whistling of the wind was the only sound that reached their ears for a moment but somehow, the silence turned out to be worse. What had made that sound? Something was certainly still coming, and as Ethan tried to summon his dispelled form, none answered his call. Somehow, it was as if his control over them had been damaged. As if he was trying to press a button with a soft noodle and just like him, the woman was cursing up a storm.

Their gaze met. She was just as bewildered as he was, though before they could speak or assess the situation any further, the ground erupted between them. Like an explosion had gone off, the sand geysered up and was quickly swept away by the wind, but the shadow that fell on Ethan only grew darker as a pale, train-sized monstrosity of a worm swayed under the desert sun. Slowly, its conical maw opened up, and dozens of antenna-like tendrils squirmed in the daylight, reaching out tentatively as it tasted the air for something. Ethan couldn’t see teeth, but somehow, that made the creature even more unnerving.

[Pale Maw (II) - Level 102]

While Ethan was definitely stunned by the sight, it wasn’t enough for him to forget himself though while the initial buzz had reduced to a nearly inaudible hum, it was still doing something to him. As he called for a new, full-powered fireball in his hand, the mana moved sluggishly through his body. As if a heavy blanket had been draped all over his soul. Gritting his teeth, he urged the magic to coalesce faster. He even tried to tap into the Zenith state, but that felt like it might bring the whole house of cards tumbling. He was barely keeping control of what he had as is.

Flames flared between the bony claws of his right hand. He could feel the strain of calling for a new spell. He could feel how over-extended his spirit felt at maintaining its grip over his two remaining spellforms, but he didn’t let up. Even when the worm’s head snapped toward him, having detected his presence. Cursing, Ethan urged his mana to move faster. He needed a few more seconds. But as the worm cocked its head and readied itself to lunge, an icy arrow bloomed on the side of its face, and keening wail filled the air though thankfully, it seemed to be one of pain. It didn’t worsen whatever suppressing effect was taking place, so as it twisted about to dislodge the boulder-sized clump of ice from its neck, Ethan threw the fireball high and let the homing function adjust the path, and as the glowing orb of bright flames reached the zenith before turning back down, he shouted. “Run!”

The woman didn’t waste time. She whirled and bolted in the other direction, most likely trying to gain some distance before she could activate more of her abilities, and Ethan was glad to see it. He was willing to stay behind and buy her a couple more seconds, but he wasn’t kidding himself. With nearly twenty levels of difference and most of his abilities out of commission, he didn’t like his odds with this fight, and as he attempted to channel his Prime Manifestation, he found it to be just as sluggish as his mana. In fact, it somehow felt even less responsive compared to his mana, and that made his throat dry up.

Was the worm projecting its own Zenith state over them? Was that what the suppression was? And why was it so… heavy?

The fireball detonated with a muffled bang, but that was normal. Ethan hadn’t leaned into the concussive aspect of the Fireball. Instead, a searing wave of red and orange filled the sky as the top two thirds of the worm were engulfed in intensely hot and bright flames, at which point he also turned tail and ran, a grin on his lips.

He was glad he’d come to this place. It seemed there was still plenty to learn. For now, he needed his wings back to assess the situation, and then he’d come back in for round two. Because there was no world in which he wasn’t going to make the most of this worm. It had shown him plenty he didn’t know. It had exposed some vulnerabilities and to get over those, nothing was better than practice.

Ethan had just found himself a powerful sparring partner. Whether it liked it or not, it was about to help him improve.
