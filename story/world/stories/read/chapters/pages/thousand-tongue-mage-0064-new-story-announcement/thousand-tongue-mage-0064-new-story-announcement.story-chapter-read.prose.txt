Not a chapter, but a new story announcement! With the end of Storm Strider, The Exorcist Doctor is taking its timeslot with five chapters a week for the first month, and it's a base-building progression story set in the Plagueplain Front, so you can expect:

- Slow burn progression with action, base-building, crafting, and romance elements

- Gothic Victorian setting with exotic toxins, morphing weapons, biomagic, and dangerous drugs and artifacts

- Smart and slightly insane plague doctor male lead

- Clinic reconstruction, upgrading, managing, hiring, and expanding

-Lots of worldbuilding, moments of peace, and moments of darkness

The story is set in Year Eighty-Four, twenty-one years after the current events of this story, so who knows, you may see a few familiar faces from Thousand Tongue Mage (and the other stories) over there! The link, cover, and synopsis to the new story is in the author's notes below!
