To all the readers who have been with me on this journey, thank you so much for being here.

As of writing this, I have 10,073 views, 458 average chapter views, 34 favorites, 182 followers, and a 4.39 rating. The most impressive thing is that this story has reached Rising Star on the Royal Road platform.

This is my first novel. I never imagined that I would reach this point with my debut work.

People who have read my threads and posts on the Royal Road forum may know this already.

When I started writing this novel, I had only one motivation. That is to write a story that I would personally love. This came from a few bad experiences with my favorite novels.

One was abandoned after 1,000 chapters. One was abruptly finished after completing 1200 chapters. One novel’s genre changed entirely after 1,300 chapters. And in one web novel, after finishing 2,000 chapters, the author rushed to finish the story and crammed the next 2,000 chapters' worth of story into the final 100 chapters.

In the end, I got so frustrated that I started writing a novel just to read it myself. I decided to continue the story whenever I felt like reading something new.

Funny enough, after finishing just three chapters, I got so excited that I posted them on Royal Road without much thought. But then I started loving the process so much that I simply kept writing. I have mentioned in one of the author notes as well.

And now I've finally reached my first goal, i.e., making it onto Rising Star on Royal Road. This is all thanks to you guys. And getting 10k views in 60 days was a totally unexpected outcome.

I've read in many forums that reaching Rising Star is all about luck, but I truly believe I reached here only because of you.

The fact that you are reading it and enjoying it enough to wait for the next chapter is the biggest achievement of all. I never thought people would read this story, let alone love it.

Thank you so much for being here.
