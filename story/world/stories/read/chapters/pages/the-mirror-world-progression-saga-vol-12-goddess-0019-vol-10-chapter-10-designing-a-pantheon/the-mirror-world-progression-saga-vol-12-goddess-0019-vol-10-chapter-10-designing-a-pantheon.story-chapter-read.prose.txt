Next up on Samantha’s itinerary was the Pantheon business of the new Iconic Skill and the shared Servitor Line. The Pantheon in general was seeing a large influx thanks to conversions from the newly ‘acquired’ Primal Mystic, whom she’d relatively rapidly brought into the fold not long after squaring away the immediate after-businesses of the Rivermount conquest.

Opinions came from every side in commentary about presumed needs. This had to be balanced with the presumption of lower levels for the foreseeable future, at least in all but the Archservitor, who’d immediately start (and link to) the lowest deity level of them all, which was unsurprisingly Samantha herself.

The primary need and highest demand utility-wise continued to be the newest thing Samantha herself had revolutionized: flying transport. By far, her Angel-Pegasi were her greatest number of Servitors, and they were constantly taxed in using their efficient combination of speed and low-energy transport. She could literally never have enough of them.

With the energy of the Pantheon able to be poured in by her other, older, higher resource allies, Servitors could be created without drawing from her limited, in-demand pool, and furthermore, not restricted to her maximum limits (based on Followers), but that of the Pantheon Follower totals.

Taking some of the weight off of her Angels was prudent and quickly agreed upon. They’d make some more flying mount Servitors!

Hyper-specialized, she wanted them to have Flight, Enhanced Speed, and Great Size (primarily for load-bearing benefits). Aside from that, they’d represent the Freedom Domain of the Pantheon, which was a decent support specialty that countered curses and other debuffs, as well as synergistically specializing in movement-type buffs.

She went with the concept of the Phoenix — giant colorful birds with a fiery motif and personifying the rising sun of the revolution. Ironically, they mostly wouldn’t be front-facing war units; these majority lower-levels would be kicked back further in safer zones to relieve transportation burdens on higher-level Pegasi. Over time, of course, they’d gain levels and move up, but wouldn’t have as much of a stealth or scouting specialty, regardless.

For their Archservitor, she went with the name Bennu, after the bird-like Egyptian god. At a much higher level than the ones rapidly being created by multiple Pantheon members, Bennu was already faster than any Angel, and downright massive, basically a flying bird-tank.

As for the rest of the names, she left them mostly for the other deities of the Pantheon to decide. Her general society of control was so large, it was functionally the size of a city, probably more like a busy barony, so she could hardly keep track of everyone’s names in a personal sense.

Next up for consideration was the Pantheon’s third Iconic Skill. Their first two were Diplomacy and Favored Weapon: Staff, the latter of which was fine for spear use and had already been a great boon to low-level soldiers. Any given member of any deity or the Pantheon could make use of it, so largely the whole army had a system of teaching new recruits through it. This was pretty fantastic as a dual use for actual staff users — such as spellcasters and travelers — to have some additional self-defense.

The staff or spear was a quite ubiquitous weapon in her society. In Geirkos, fancier and fancier ones, with mythical creatures at the top, were becoming quite the status symbol, particularly among the artisan and merchant classes. The young nobility had taken to similarly styled scepters, men and women.

Thus, the consideration of the Iconic Skill was not to be taken lightly, transformative as something so ‘accessible’ was. She considered what she wanted most to represent. Diplomacy represented mutual compromise, making deals, and organizing (to some extent). The favored weapon was the ‘carrying the big stick’ part of protecting oneself.

She wanted more about practical self-sufficiency. Ideally, something in the individual sense as well as in the group sense, though that was rather a tall order. There was the potential of improving agriculture in some way…

Improving basic individual efficiency could do everything. I think I know a good candidate for this, even though it isn’t terribly useful to me, personally. Maybe a smidge?

Endurance

You’re good at long-term physical tasks and avoiding fatigue. Reduce this degradation by 10%, and add +1 Effective Levels to resist long-term physical draining (such as from certain poisons or diseases), or any fatigue-related deleterious effects, long-term or otherwise.

The percentage-based reduction seemed pretty nice for improving the average person’s life in the semi-primitive society of Calrenazzod. The march of soldiers would be less inherently draining, and the farmer could get more done in less time.

Why not make this machine more efficient? A somewhat sardonic thought.

When she tested the waters on it, everyone seemed supportive enough. Well, it wasn’t exciting, after all. Redberry shrugged and said, ‘sure,’ Zadkiel and Levi did not seem to care either way, and Myst called it ‘smart.’

She made the selection and checked out the new stat block for the Pantheon:

Name: Celestial Horizon

Level: 4

Symbol: A winding white path leading up the planet to a starburst on a horizon inset in a blue sky

Edicts: Fight against tyranny and oppression; support independence and self-sufficiency; promote mutual good, understanding, and friendship between reasonable parties

Anathema: Spilling the innocent blood of sapients; working in support of tyranny

Unlocks: Priestly Ordination, Priestly Transfer, +6 exp [Deity members], Grant Prayers

Domain(s): Freedom, Unity

Iconic Skills: Diplomacy, Favored Weapon (Staff), Endurance

Servitor Lines: Phoenix (Freedom, Flight, Enhanced Speed, Great Size)

Cool beans. One thing down, baby! Just a million more to go…

As she was dealing with some procrastinated approval measures Jeeves had very insistently stuck her with, she got a sudden communication from Red. “Moth. Do you… have a moment?”

Samantha was stunned for a split second, even from this level of a vague request from her. Rare, to say the least. She replied, “For you, I always do, Red. What about?”

“I could use some advice.”

Stunned again, she nonetheless replied quickly and excitedly, “O-of course! I’ll come ri-” She felt Jeeves glaring at her spiritually. “Ahem! I’ll come over in just a few minutes!”

Redberry drummed her fingers on the wooden handrail and chewed her lip as she stared out at the wilderness beyond the walkway, feeling quite embarrassed about the whole situation. It was that much worse to be asking Samantha for advice. But she couldn’t avoid it. She had to.

Her mind shifted back to the moments sometime after the battle, with Zadkiel…

Atop a tall tree branch, Redberry watched from afar as the remnants of the enemy army had surrendered, and allies were tossing cheers, her own dryads among them. She did a little more than watch, too — she extended her spiritual senses, her greater presence, and breathed in the glee of her Servitors and Followers. She did such an ecstatic thing only rarely, but when else would she dare but here and now? What victory was sweeter in any timeframe she could put to memory, her so far from her lands yet conquering her enemies?

She smiled and her eyelids drooped, and she even swayed slightly, at the punch of the collective elation. It had been a good fight. Somewhat draining, but quite satisfying. Earth elementals were always fun to rip apart. Was there anything she was better at than destroying them, ‘rooting trees’ being her specialty?

Sex, perhaps.

It was in that unfortunately timed moment that Zadkiel popped up right next to her, surprising her and making her jump, such was her absorption in the revelry. But he didn’t even notice.

“Haha! Look at that!” the giant horned beast-devil cried, grinning with shining eyes as he looked ahead at the captured view of celebrations, his blood-stained, gauntleted fists on his hips. “What a sight to see.” He was dirty, sweaty, bloody, and cut up all across his body, with his long, dark hair matted and wild, looking all-in-all like fresh hell out of a storm of death.

It was so fucking hot.

Feeling an answering heat pulsing within her, Redberry growled deep in her throat and, without even thinking about it, grabbed the demon king in bonds of wood that made his eyes pop and his smile turn into the baring of fangs, with an instinctual snarl uttered of his own. Nonetheless, whatever moment of realization and opportunity for resistance was one he waived and let pass, even with an obviously unclear understanding of what exactly was happening.

She brought him to his knees in front of her — so giant was he that this was not that much lower than her standing. “I believe I made a promise,” she said, her voice mixed between fierce aggression she was desperately trying to wrangle, and simplistic lust. “Have you come to dare seeing it due?”

Zadkiel’s expression was priceless. Glorious. To think, such a powerhouse of a god, so eager, so thrilled, so ready to respond in whatever way she liked. “Oh, have I. But what is so daring, I wonder?”

With another snarl, Redberry grabbed his horns, pulled his head back, and kissed him. He tasted of sweat, blood, and electrifying glory. A fire lit inside her; she felt like she was detonating. His hands strained mightily against the bonds, eager and denied to touch her, as she suddenly found herself fighting with everything inside herself not to envelop him in the tree and-

“Hey, Red!” Samantha called.

Redberry jumped out of her skin and spun around. Quickly, she got control of herself and took a deep, deep breath. “Moth,” she said woodenly, with a nod.

How did I not even feel her enter the place?!

Samantha, clad in her tantalizing dressed-down ‘safari’ outfit — as she called it — was looking back at her akin to how one would a wild animal she’d petted often, suddenly acting strange and unpredictably. All too accurate, really. “Are you… okay, Red?”

Clearing her throat, Redberry flashed a smile and nodded. “Yes. Change is in the air, is all. I believe I could use a… a confidante, right now.”
