A tinkling sound accompanied Jade as she and her friends stepped across the threshold of Ashar's Illysport shop, emerging into a surprisingly luxurious gallery. Sets of armor hung on racks lining the walls, and shelves were arrayed in neat rows showcasing potions, arrows, weapons, and other items. A mezzanine level formed a ring overhead, making a second level with yet more wares on display.

"Welcome!" The tan-skinned, handsome shopkeeper greeted them, moving from behind the counter to shake their hands warmly. "I was hoping you'd stop by!"

"Good to see you again, Ashar." Jade said, looking around the shop with interest. "This is quite an upgrade from your place in Estelhelm."

"I just finished pulling it all together after the move." He grinned, spreading his arms. "Is there anything in particular you're looking for? I've heard some rather intriguing stories about what your group has been up to since our little demon adventure."

"Did they ever find out you were involved in that mess?" Naomi asked, crossing her arms and giving him a suspicious look. "I seem to recall you vanishing right before the guards showed up to arrest us."

"We were never arrested, they just asked us some questions." Luis said.

"It definitely felt like being arrested."

"As far as anyone in Estelhelm is concerned, I simply happened to be in the right place at the right time to step in and help your group deal with the blood demon." Ashar shrugged modestly. "Enough people saw our chase through the streets that I thought it was time to make the move up here, though. Before anyone stopped by my establishment with some awkward questions."

"I still don't see what they have to complain about." Tara huffed, lowering the hood of her cloak and letting her long, white hair spill down her back. "The city had a major demon infestation, and we fixed it."

"We did cause a lot of property damage along the way." Luis reminded her. He turned to Ashar. "We aren't going to have to pay for that, are we?"

"Not if you don't go back to the city."

"Wonderful." Jade rolled her eyes. "I'm with Tara, they really should be more grateful. No one else was doing anything about it."

The two women shared a nod. Naomi groaned.

"What?"

"I think those two just formed an unholy alliance. We'd best watch out."

"Anyway." Jade said, turning back to Ashar as her friends continued to bicker. "We're looking for some armor upgrades. All of us are C rank now, and our protective enchantments are still level D."

"Ah!" Ashar snapped his fingers and took her by the arm, leading her towards the rear of the shop. "That, my dear, I can help you with."

There were two ways to get their defenses up: Buying a new set entirely or upgrading an existing one. Of their group, Jade was the only person whose armor had any upgrade slots. It was custom made, and without the ability to rank up, the order wouldn't have been worth it. Everyone else's gear was more generic and was better off replaced outright. Ashar took Jade through the various options, showing her the upgrade runes she could apply to her armor as well as the new options for the rest of her team.

"These prices aren't as bad as I thought they'd be." Jade commented during a pause in Ashar's tour of the armor section. "I thought C rank gear would be more expensive.""The duke is covering some of the cost of equipment upgrades for wayfarers from your home world. Ever since word started spreading that the demon threat was connected to the Labyrinth Cores, everyone wants to do what they can to make sure those are taken care of as quickly as possible."

"That's something, at least." Jade grunted, perusing through a small selection of runes.

Most of the upgrades available were simple increases in the potency of the protective barrier that surrounded enchanted armor. Some more expensive ones came with elemental resistances, but Jade wasn't interested in those. The demons she'd encountered favored fire damage, and she already had a racial resistance to that type of damage. Instead, she zeroed in on a different armor upgrade.

Item: Agility Rune (Armor) (Level C)

Trait: Increases defensive barrier to level C.

Trait: Increases Agility by 4 points.

"This one." Jade said, pointing at it.

"Ah, good choice. It should fit well with your build." Ashar nodded, retrieving the rune for her. "Would you like me to install it?"

She agreed, following him to the workshop in the back of the store while her companions continued to browse through their own armor options. Even though she'd fought alongside Ashar in Estelhelm and he'd seen her rather unique armor, Jade still felt a twinge of embarrassment as she handed over the bundle of leather straps and buckles to him. Ashar took it without comment, pulling out a wand and performing a simple ritual to properly integrate the rune to the existing enchantment woven into the armor.

"By the way." She said while he worked. "Siora mentioned that you might have a lead on a component for a potion that she's trying to make?"

Jade tried to make it a casual question, even as her heart beat a nervous rhythm in her chest.

Please…

"Ah, she did say something about that." He said, pausing and looking up. "The phoenix tear, yes? That's a potent ingredient, may I ask what it's for?"

There was no reason to lie. Jade spent some time explaining her family situation, her brother's condition, and the difficulty that their world's medical technology had with treating him.

"I'm sorry." Ashar said softly, his normal half-grin sliding from his face. "I should be able to procure it for you. I'll make it a priority."

"How much will it be?" Jade asked, trying to do some mental math as she factored in the purchases their group needed to make. Armor, consumables, maybe some new weapon upgrades-

"Don't worry about it."

"Huh?" Jade blinked in surprise. "What do you mean?"

"I'll get it for you. A couple of friends in the business owe me a favor." He flashed her a smile. "I can have it by the end of the week."

"Ashar… isn't this very expensive and rare?"

"It's not especially common, no." He said with a shrug.

"Then why-"

"If what your friends tell me about the fight you had in the corespace is true, then your group risked your lives to stop a demon invasion no one even knew about. Now, it sounds like you'll be doing that again. Personally, I'd rather not have a demon army marching through Illysport, it'd be bad for business." The dark-haired shopkeeper winked at her. "And if this helps you stay focused, it'll be good for everyone."

Jade could taste his sincerity. There was no deception here, no hidden agenda. She surprised both of them by pulling Ashar into a tight embrace.

"Thank you." She whispered, giving him a squeeze. "If there's anything I can do for you, ever, just let me know."

"Hmm." He mused, regaining his composure as she released him. Ashar bent down to resume his work and then paused, as if struck by a sudden thought. "How about you let me take you out to a nice dinner, sometime?"

Jade blinked in surprise. That wasn't what she'd been expecting. Did that mean…? She felt a blush color her cheeks and she glanced away, embarrassed.

"I… well…"

This was not a topic of conversation she had any skill with. Why was it always so hard to get the right words out?

"Only if you'd like to, of course."

"I would." She said quietly, bobbing her head once.

Ashar grinned.

"I thought a succubus would be more accustomed to this kind of conversation."

"In my defense." Jade said, a grin of her own pulling at the corner of her lips. "I'm still pretty new at this."

The party returned to the Coral Lantern high in spirits and low on coin. Everyone had acquired a substantial upgrade, with everyone save Siora boasting level C armor among other enhancements. While the elf wasn't high enough in level to equip magic as potent as the rest of the group, she now had access to a combat oriented spellbook that would allow her to support the party from afar during a fight. She didn't have the endurance that the rest of them possessed, but even Siora had been training hard. The additional stat points that came with reaching level 10 would allow her to keep up with the party, at least in a support role.

The common room of the inn was a little busier in the evening than it had been earlier in the day, but it was still far from anything Jade would consider crowded. She spotted Ellie and Aylin sitting at a small table together and moved to join them. All of them together did a lot to fill up the small inn, and they had to pull up chairs from the other table to all have a seat.

"Good to see you again, Aylin." Jade said to the robed mage with a nod.

"Likewise." Aylin stood to greet each of them while Ellie watched the reunion in silence, taking small sips from a mug.

"We heard you're interested in joining the next expedition to a core." Luis said enthusiastically. "It'll be good to have you back with the team."

"I'm looking forward to it as well." Aylin gave him a tight smile before turning her attention on Jade. "It hasn't even been two weeks, and yet I've heard many rumors of strange events surrounding the local Earth cores. You wouldn't happen to be involved with them, would you?"

"Ahh… that depends on the rumors."

The half-elven mage sighed, rubbing her temples with thumb and forefinger. Aylin's long, straight black hair hid her ears, but her slightly angular face and slender figure hinted at her heritage. She gave Jade a discerning look that felt as though it were piercing straight through her body.

"Why don't you take me through it from the start?"

"Are you sure we can trust her?" Tara asked, looking Aylin up and down. "She's in the Mystic Order. None of them took me seriously when I tried to warn them about the demons in Estelhelm.""We can trust her." Luis assured her, and Jade nodded in agreement.

"We worked together for a few weeks on the second floor. She knows what she's doing."

"More than any of us." Naomi added.

"Hey, I know what I'm doing." Luis protested.

"And what's that?" Tara asked.

"Saving the day… and looking good doing it."

They all laughed, and even the stoic Ellie cracked a smile. Aylin just shook her head, trying and failing to keep a serious expression on her face.

"Of all the people to be tasked with saving the world…"

Jade and Fenrin took turns relating their latest adventure to Aylin just as Parker and his rhyssit companion joined them at the table. As expected, the mage had a lot of questions, especially regarding details of the ritual the demons conducted. Tara fielded those, sharing several pages of notes she'd made following the battle. Finally, Aylin sat back, a contemplative look on her face.

"This is troubling." She said at length. "I need to go to the Order with this. Our scholars and researchers may be able to uncover a better way to disrupt the dimensional gateway they're trying to make."

"Can they actually do it?" Jade asked. "Open a gate using the Labyrinth Cores, I mean. Not just bring a big group of demons through, but a gate that entire armies could use."

"I can't say for sure." Aylin said, shaking her head slowly. "This level of dimensional magic is beyond anything I've ever seen, or even heard of. But if they're putting so much effort into this, then we have to assume they know what they're doing."

"I still can't believe the Labyrinth is holding demon armies prisoner." Fenrin cut in. "I've lived here my whole life. My entire people have. How could no one know?"

"Clearly, someone or something didn't want us to know." Aylin said grimly. Word of these events had travelled with Fenrin and Tara but the news was still fresh, and Jade could feel Aylin's worry. "Jade, all of you, thank you for what you did. I know there were sacrifices-" Jade glanced at Fenrin. The half-elf pretended not to notice, a stoic expression plastered on his face. "-but we'd be in a far worse position without your efforts. I'll make sure they aren't overlooked."

"Thanks, Aylin." Naomi said, touching her lightly on the arm. "You'll be coming with us to the core, then?"

"I will. The corespace wouldn't let me in if I were in your party, not with how many outsiders are already in it, but Parker and Ellie's group can accommodate me."

"We're happy to have you along." Parker nodded, speaking for the first time. The large man stood, surveying their eclectic group with a discerning eye. "Since you've worked with Jade's group before, it will make preparing for the raid on the corespace easier."

"You're thinking we should do some group training before we go in?" Jade asked.

"Absolutely. You never go into a fight with an untested team, not unless you want everything to go wrong."

"Good idea, but I'm not sure any amount of training will fix that." Naomi laughed. "Trouble can't stay away from this one."

"And now that Tara's on the team…" Fenrin added.

"Hey!" The tek'kalla protested. "There's nothing wrong with my methods. They're carefully calculated to produce ideal results."

"Like that building you almost burned down?"

"Almost burned down, Fenrin. The key word is almost."

Jade caught Aylin studying the tek'kalla as her teammates continued to bicker. Part of her worried that the mage would judge Tara as harshly as she'd done with Jade when they'd first met, tek'kalla certainly didn't have a good reputation among the people here. The mage caught Jade's eye, and she gave a slight shake of her head. Jade nodded, giving her a quick smile. She didn't need to taste the mage's emotions to understand the message. Aylin wasn't going to make the same mistake twice.

In the end, they decided to wait a few days for any more Earth wayfarers to find them in the city. Most of the front runners knew that Illysport was a potential meeting point in the Labyrinth, and it was possible more would join them here soon. They would use that time to establish group tactics and devise strategies for taking down the core, and to mentally prepare themselves for the trials ahead.

Jade returned to her room at the Sunken Treasure tired but hopeful. They had a plan, and even better, they weren't alone anymore. The burden of saving Earth wasn't just for she and her friends to bear. All around the world, other wayfarers were fighting with them. Meeting Parker and Ellie inside the Labyrinth was a much-needed reminder of that simple fact.

Exhausted, Jade flopped onto the bed without even bothering to undress. She was surprised by an odd crinkling sound beneath her, and fished out a crumpled envelope. Jade lit the bedside lamp, frowning as she opened it to reveal a letter inside.

"Tomorrow night. The overlook at 7th street. We wish to discuss an arrangement of mutual benefit. Come alone."

The letter was signed by 'The Ebon Eye'.
