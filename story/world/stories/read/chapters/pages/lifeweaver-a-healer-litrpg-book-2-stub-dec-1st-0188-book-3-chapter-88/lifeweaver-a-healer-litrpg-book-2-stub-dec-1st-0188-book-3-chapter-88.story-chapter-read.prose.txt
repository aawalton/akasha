Skills. Going through the list was a calm, comforting experience while in meditation. Considering the different options brought images of past experiences to the forefront of Luke's mind. If he'd picked this one earlier, would this person have survived? With that option, would everything have been different? He pushed those thoughts away and focused on the words themselves.

From the list of available skills, he looked over two that protected him from manipulation.

Reject Intrusion [Active. Mana Cost: Moderate]: Experience in delving into others has given the Lifeweaver knowledge in the act of keeping others out.

Weaveguard [Toggleable - Mana Cost: Low]: Shield the core of your being from outside influence.

Similar in description, but he thought the latter referred to protecting his Meta-Heart while the former was more along the lines of guarding his mana channels, maybe. Neither sounded particularly necessary for him. If he had something for his mind, that would have been an instant pick.

Instead, Luke turned himself into more of a weapon. Now that his reach with Threads of Mana had increased to a point where they worked better as weapons, he'd experienced a point of failure in them several times. Their durability. While their strength had increased, it was still far from where he needed them to be. To remedy that, Luke picked his first skill.

Strengthen Thread [Active - Mana Cost: High]: Use mana to strengthen the weave.

As soon as he selected the skill, he knew he'd made a good choice. Even moving a thread along his own mana channels in that state of meditation, it felt solid, strong. Malleable Needle of Life had become an excellent tool in combat, but the needle was too small to deal meaningful damage in a lot of cases, like with the greater demon he and Penny struggled against. An option to fix that stared in his face, and Luke selected it with his final point.

Weaver's Whip [Active - Mana Cost: Low]: Turn a thread of mana into a weapon.

The description made the skill sound lackluster, but there was something about it that made him think it could be more than what the interface told him. Descriptions in the system did not explain things terribly well, and he thought this skill would be no different.

Coming out of his meditative trance, Luke grit his teeth against the onslaught of emotions, memories, and hurt that came on like a punch in the gut.

"No!" he barked at the empty wall. He hadn't worked on his willpower like a crazy person all this time just to end up a blubbering mess. He'd done things no person should ever have to do, but it wasn't anything he wouldn't do again, damn it! Yes, he'd killed. Yes, he'd failed those he loved, and a lot of other miserable things, but he had done his best. There was no doubt in his mind. Willpower. Willpower. Willpower. Luke. Would. Not. Fold.

Boon of Potential grants you an attribute point (+1 Willpower).

Boon of Potential grants you an attribute point (+1 Willpower).

It eased a little. While he wished it would all go away, Luke knew it never would, but this was enough. Manageable, in a word.

"The Singers will pay for this," he told the wall, despite being unsure if perhaps this wasn't for the best in the long run. Losing your humanity was not anything the Integrated of Earth signed up for. Luke just hoped his friends and other fellow Integrated were doing okay.

Taking deep, slow breaths, Luke extended a thread of mana. It looked no different from before. He extended it to the rough bed and grabbed a leg before lifting it off the ground. The thread held. He'd known it would. Picking Weaver's Whip had granted him some knowledge in its use, and he'd learned that it could indeed do more than the description hinted at. Activating the skill, he brought out a second thread and slammed it into the bed. The thread sheared straight through the wood.

Luke stood there for a moment, watching the now-broken bed. "Damn."

That had worked even better than he had hoped. What if he'd picked this skill right away? Wouldn't that have changed things? No! Stop! He was done with that. No more self-wallowing allowed. People needed him, and they needed him now.

Stepping back out into the corridor, Luke soon encountered another demon. This one didn't attack, not right away. Instead, it stood there, looking confused to the point that it was almost comical.

"What?" Luke asked.

Then he realized something. He recognized this demon.

"Wait," he said. "Didn't I kill you already?"

It was the first demon he faced when the system event started, the one who crawled out of the hole in the ground, together with the many lesser demons.

To Luke's surprise, the demon answered, its voice a rumbling baritone. "How are you here, human?"

"You thought those portals were one-way only? Sorry to disappoint."

The demon narrowed its eyes, and Luke readied himself for intercepting a charge that didn't come. Instead, the demon turned and set off running. Luke didn't miss a beat. He followed at top speed, charging through the corridor. The demon fleeing could only mean one thing: it was going to report Luke's arrival to someone, and that someone was undoubtedly the one Luke was after.

Following wasn't difficult. The demon was rather slow, but Luke kept his distance. His limbs still felt a little sluggish after the wave wiped out all his mana, but they warmed up as they traversed a bunch of different hallways, stairs, spacious chambers, and even a ritual room or two. They passed a lot of other demons and even some cultists like the ones who summoned the demon for the group to fight back in Lilineese-land, or whatever they called it.

Luke had killed the demon he was chasing. He was sure of it. So what the hell was going on? That bastard's corpse was still in Luke's inventory, even! There was just the one conclusion he could reach with the evidence presented to him. A demon dying on Earth did not mean it was dead. Either it returned here, to Infernal Flux, or it never left in the first place, and what they fought up there were copies, avatars, or something in that vein.

As he considered the implications of that, the demon he was chasing sprinted through an exit. When Luke followed, he emerged into an underground cave, of a sort, except it was large to a mind-boggling degree. Small, orange-glowing lights far up above made it look like the night sky, and down the slope, far in the distance, he saw a city that had to rival New York in size, if not bigger.

The path to that city, on which he chased the fleeing greater demon, was filled with other demons. Lots and lots of demons, and they all stopped to stare at him.

"Well, shit," Luke said, turning to see if he could perhaps go back inside and find another way forward. There was nothing there but a sheer rock wall. "Double shit," he grunted, turning back to the now approaching demons. This was not going to be pleasant.

The quickest ones ran on four legs. Two broke from the pack and charged Luke, screeching almost like twisted cats. A whirl of threads saw the left one’s head taken clear off at the neck, while the right one lost a front leg and a chunk of its side. Luke rolled and, rather than pull the threads back, he drew in infernal mana from his opponents, draining them and accepting the horrible pain in his Meta-Heart. It did not want to accept infernal mana, but since it didn't have a choice, it did. The pain resulting from it was like a dull ache and a shooting, stabbing sensation, both at once. To Luke, who had learned to accept that pain, it was nothing at all, not compared to when he had taken on the greater demons.

The first two demons died in moments, but more were coming. Taking out his staff, Luke got ready to take them on. Some looked mindless, like the lesser demons, but not all. Some even stood aside, observing him rather than attacking, while others crafted spellweaves that promised even more pain and hurt.

"Bring it on," Luke said, meeting a charging demon head-on with a downward-arching blow. The attack put the demon off balance, and it didn't anticipate a thread of mana snaking out of the weapon to latch on around its neck. Weaver's Whip came in handy again as Luke decapitated the opponent just in time to weavestep left, avoiding a torrent of orange sludge summoned by a female demon standing on top of a boulder. Two strides, a leap, and another weavestep saw that demon dead.

Luke was getting warmed up now. Combat was just what he needed to turn off his bleeding emotions for a moment. More of them were coming. Hundreds. If that was what he needed to go through to finish the system event, then that was what he would do.

"Come on, then!" he shouted as he used a thread to push a sword blade out of its path, allowing Luke to step forward and elbow the attacking demon in the face. Threads did the rest. Once that demon died, three others took its place.

One used some sort of cannon, and it blew Luke's forearm right off in a single, deafening explosion of force. Weaver's Renewal restored him right away, and the demon paid with all the mana in its system. With the incoming infernal mana, rage built, and Luke savored it, along with the pain.

"I'm not a monster," he whispered as he threw himself at the next demon.
