Hello, Keres readers!

I have some news from another corner of my writing life.

While I continue working on the planned rewrites for The Saga of Keres, my other fantasy series has reached a major milestone. A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea, Book One of the Of Sand and Silver series, releases on November 10, 2026.

The story first appeared on Royal Road as A Lullaby of Sand and Silver. After more than a year of editing, rewriting, renaming, and expanding its world, I am preparing the revised edition for publication—and ARC sign-ups have opened.

To avoid any confusion: this ARC campaign is for A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea, not a revised edition of The Saga of Keres. This announcement does not change the availability of Keres on Royal Road.

I am sharing it here because many of you have spent a long time following the Keres cast. I would love to invite you to join the next stage of my journey as an author.

The two stories have different casts, settings, and tones, but they are part of the same larger multiverse—much like Brandon Sanderson’s Cosmere or the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Every story I write exists within a shared cosmology governed by the same underlying forces, including Aether and Keres, which serve as sources of magical fuel, and the same outer gods and cosmic structure.

The Saga of Keres leans into steampunk, global conflict, kaiju-scale threats, and the struggle between magic and science. A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea follows a smaller road journey with a cozier heart, but plenty of danger still waits along the way.

The stories can stand on their own, but readers who look closely may recognize shared foundations, recurring metaphysical principles, and connections between worlds that are not always obvious at first.

Both stories feature:

Complicated characters carrying old wounds

Unusual forms of magic

Found families and people searching for belonging

Mentors, apprentices, and unconventional relationships

Ancient secrets and dangerous artifacts

Characters caught between survival, revenge, redemption, and purpose

Quiet human moments surrounded by conflicts much larger than themselves

If those elements drew you to The Saga of Keres, I think you may also find something to enjoy in Azhra and Ithráin’s journey, and perhaps notice how their world fits into the same greater multiverse.

Softness gets you killed. Azhra learned that before the contract that cost her an eye.

Azhra, a young Azqára assassin, survives by striking first and trusting no one. But when a job goes wrong, she flees into the wilderness wounded, half-blind, and clinging to the sole thing she has left: her mother’s lullaby.

The wizard Ithráin finds her and offers the one thing Azhra fears more than violence: kindness.

Under his care, she becomes his apprentice and begins building a life she never expected to have. Then someone breaches the Vault in the Skyborne Archive. A thief steals something ancient—and something far more dangerous binds itself to Azhra.

Azhra has spent her entire life trusting the instincts that kept her alive.

Now she must decide whether those instincts are worth dying for.

A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea is Book One in the Of Sand and Silver saga, a cozy-but-dangerous fantasy adventure about found family, cursed artifacts, and a feral girl discovering that survival and living are not the same thing.
