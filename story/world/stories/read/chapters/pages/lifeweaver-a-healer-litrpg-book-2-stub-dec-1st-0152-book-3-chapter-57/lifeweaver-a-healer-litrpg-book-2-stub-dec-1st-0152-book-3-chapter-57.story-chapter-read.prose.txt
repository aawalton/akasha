Perhaps that last remark was a little too cocky. Rather than charge straight at him, as Luke anticipated, the fairy turned and ran, naked feet slapping against the pockmarked asphalt. Glittering dust trailed her, shaken loose from the still disabled fairy wings.

Pissed at himself for failing to hold her back, he followed, doing his best not to breathe in too much of the fairy dust. Some still got into his nose. Luke couldn't stop sneezing while he ran.

"Stop!" he shouted.

She didn't stop. Bitch.

While she ran at an impressive speed, Luke was faster. He pushed himself to close the gap between them, using weavestep to round corners and leap over obstacles like broken-down cars or random heaps of debris from damaged buildings. Soon, he had her again. Or at least he thought so. Just when Luke was about to latch on with a thread of mana, her wings fluttered, spewing more of that stupid dust right into his face. To make matters worse, the damn stuff was poisonous. She probably hoped he'd just fall down and die after inhaling so much of it, but no such luck for her.

That flutter of her wings was a problem, though. It meant the severed mana channels were healing. Perhaps she'd downed a health potion. One possibility was her having some sort of regenerative skill, too. Either way, Luke needed to catch her soon, before she regained the ability to fly off and kill again.

Weavestepping through the weave again was an option, despite the proximity, but that would leave him in a bad state. Luke had already used it too many times that day. He needed to rest.

Also, he refused to lose a footrace against a fairy.

Needing to end it, he used another tool at his disposal, and this time it wasn't threads of mana. Reaching into his inventory, he grabbed a monster core at random and took aim. The throw took her in the back of her head, and she toppled forward, face-planting the pavement. A snap told Luke she'd broken her nose, but she still got up, snarling, with blood pouring from her face. Just then, she regained the use of her wings.

Unfortunately for her, it was much too late. Luke was on her with the metal quarterstaff. The first hit took her right in the face. After that, she was no longer snarling. The second made sure she no longer moved. Luke didn't need a third.

Luke righted himself and stood there, looking down at the now-dead Integrated. Searching within himself, he looked for feelings of remorse, guilt, or apprehension. It surprised him a little to find there were none. He'd seen this woman for the first time no more than two hours ago, and now she was dead. Justice. Luke had done what needed to be done. Nothing more than she deserved.

A cold wind blew in from the left, ruffling his hair. The robe he wore didn't react, as if fluttering under a minor gust was beneath it. He looked up at the buildings surrounding him. The windows gaped empty and dark. He wondered if people huddled in there, or if they'd all left, everyone who wasn't Integrated. Even most of the Integrated had left at that point, he figured.

Tired all of a sudden, he turned and walked off, not even bothering to pick the monster core back up. It was late, and it had been a long day. Enough was enough.

Since he still needed little sleep to keep going, there was no need to hurry back to the guild headquarters. Walking was fine. Soothing, even. Luke didn't look back at the dead fairy. She deserved what she got. Still no remorse to be found, but an anger simmered in him, deep beneath the surface. Anger at the waste. Earth would need every Integrated soon enough. Killing each other wasn't just counterproductive. It was a goddamn travesty. That didn't mean killing her had been wrong. Oh, no. Her actions removed any other alternative. The fairy's choices led her to the end she'd received. Even so, Luke had to make a conscious effort to stop clenching his jaw and hands as he walked through the still night.

It took the better part of an hour to calm down. Once his mind stopped fixating on what he'd just done, his thoughts drifted to another set of assholes he'd almost forgotten about, and the simmering rage found new fuel.

Luke: "Hey Milla, have you found anything on that guild that attacked us? Luminous Lark, was it?"

Luke: "I'll leave that stuff to you. Getting close to Level 40. Reaching that before everything goes to shit will probably be a better use of my time."
