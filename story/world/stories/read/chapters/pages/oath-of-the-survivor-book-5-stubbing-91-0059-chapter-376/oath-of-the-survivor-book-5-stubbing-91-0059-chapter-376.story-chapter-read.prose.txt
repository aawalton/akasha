Kyle fought and killed more than his fair share of oversized opponents in his life.  The reality was that size was an advantage across the universe, and getting used to fighting colossal creatures was a skill he’d needed to develop early on.  From the mutated arthropods on Earth to the monstrous hodags, he’d gotten a good sense for how to deal with them.  At least, he thought he had.

Mangalica defied every expectation.  Despite his size, he was extremely quick.  Each swing of his blade was precise and calculated.  Every time Kyle thought he was out of range or at a safe angle, Mangalica’s flexibility took him by surprise.  It was a small blessing that the monster didn’t seem to fold in the mana-resistant properties of the hodag it just absorbed, nor the unique venom that he found so difficult to heal.  If Mangalica had, Kyle would likely be dead already.  As it was, their brief exchange resulted in Kyle taking some nasty cuts across his arm, chest, and left leg.  He was working on establishing a connection with Parasitic Resonance, but that would take time that he just didn’t have.  Fortunately, he wasn’t fighting alone.

Duroc came crashing down like a silver meteor, the aura of power building in his shield unlike anything Kyle had seen from the orc before.  With an entirely uncharacteristic roar, he slammed the shield toward Mangalica, releasing the stored energy all at once.  Mangalica turned to meet the blow with another pressure wave from his blade; an exchange he lost.  Though much of the force was taken out of the shield bash, the force still slammed him into the ground hard enough to make a crater, with the second layer of the skill throwing him nearly half a kilometer away.

Kyle swore as his connection with Parasitic Resonance was lost.  Even though he was grateful for the moment to breathe, it was the only skill in his arsenal that really had a chance to put the mad orc down.  Mangalica’s physical attributes had been boosted to a level that was approaching the peak of C Grade, well beyond Kyle’s ability to overcome through standard means.

“We have to make the most of it,” he whispered.  Healing Impetus flowed across his injured body as deep green scale armor wrapped itself around his body, the living armor another prototype from his and C.H.A.D.D.’s time on Kertan Six.  Finally, he withdrew the small wooden icon from the World Tree Seed, bathing the world around them in more golden light.

“You look like hell,” Duroc said as he limped over toward Kyle.  The orc was missing an ear, and had numerous deep cuts on his arms and legs.  His armor was cracked in several places, with a particularly nasty bit of damage on his left side that still oozed with blood.

“That makes two of us,” Kyle said, reaching out to cast Healing Impetus on Duroc.  “Any bright ideas for how we beat him?”

Duroc looked in the direction where a charging Mangalica was fast-approaching.  “I’ve got a couple of tricks up my sleeve.  It looks like you do, too.  Let me take the lead, I can slow him down.”

Kyle nodded.  “Sounds good.  I’ve got a skill that can work, but I’ll need time to let it sink in enough to do the job.”

“Just like with the centipede back on Earth, huh?” Duroc asked.

“Something like that,” Kyle agreed, eyes fixed on the monstrous creature approaching them.  In his final moment, he shot a quick mental note through Pack Leader’s Instinct.  C.H.A.D.D., look for any weaknesses.  There are bound to be vulnerabilities in the assimilation process that we can exploit.  Phaegos, I need you to store up as much of the Death-affinity energy as possible without hurting me.  Our best bet right now is to flood him with it all at once as soon as we break into his full mana network.

Kyle received affirmations from both, and immediately felt the discomfort in his stomach as Phaegos began consolidating power.  He was confident that the deadly energy would be effective, the question came down to whether or not it would work quickly enough to stop Mangalica from seriously injuring himself or Duroc.  Tightening his grip on the golden talisman in his right hand, he focused.  They would hold out.

His comrade-in-arms had already positioned himself in front of the charging orc, feet firmly planted. The moment Mangalica began to swing his blade, Duroc burst into motion, closing the distance between them while thrusting up with his spear toward Mangalica’s eyes.  Mangalica responded by twisting his neck to the side, taking a gash along his cheek as the blade dipped and was brought back in a half-hearted slash.  Duroc parried the off-balance attack with his shield, and landed two more solid strikes with the spear into Mangalica’s sword arm.

Mangalica retaliated with a massive punch with his free hand, forcing Duroc back.  The flesh around the spear holes began to wriggle, and Kyle expected to see the high-speed regeneration at work.  Instead, shards of sharpened stone erupted from the wounds, widening them and causing a sickening grinding noise as bone caught against rock.  Duroc used the opportunity to pull some small spheres from his belt, and threw them at the staggering War Chieftain.  The moment they came into contact with Mangalica’s body, the space around them began to warp.  Mangalica swung his sword at them again, and Kyle was surprised to see that its speed and destructive power were markedly lower than before.  Focusing on the spheres with Identify, he understood why.

Each of the small stones was imbued with Duroc’s gravitational mana, and the moment they struck Mangalica they bound to his energy and activated, pulling him to the ground with immense gravitational force.  Each one of the renegade War Chieftain’s movements was impacted, buying them exactly the time they’d need.  Without hesitation, Parasitic Resonance connected to Mangalica, and Kyle slowly began the process of breaking through his passive defenses.  His impressive Willpower meant it wouldn’t be an easy thing, so Kyle had to lean on the advice Phaegos had given him.  Rather than fighting aggressively for control, he would infiltrate carefully, not making any large moves until it was time to strike all at once.

While Kyle focused on his skill, Duroc took full advantage of his opponent’s slowed mobility.  Mangalica still far outstripped both of them in terms of raw physical might after this boost, but the sluggishness of his movements caused by the stones was enough for Duroc to turn the tables.  Every swipe of his arm or swing of the sword was deflected flawlessly, and wounds were accumulating as Duroc’s newly enhanced spear found opportunities to counterattack.

The few times Duroc got into a really dangerous spot, Kyle used Aura Force to help him reposition or to slightly alter the trajectory of Mangalica’s attacks.  Under normal circumstances, his skill wouldn’t have been enough to have much impact.  Fortunately, these weren’t normal circumstances.  The talisman clutched in his hand burned bright with energy; countless hours’ worth of mana stockpiled within now being used to overcharge each of Kyle’s abilities.  The item wasn’t designed to be a simple mana battery, and it’s incomplete form meant that it wasn’t particularly efficient.  Furthermore, using it this way set back the creation of his planned staff by months, if not more.  The strain alone was immense.  But it was working.  While the amplification wasn’t near the level of Mangalica’s unorthodox methods, it was enough to keep Kyle in the fight.

Bit by bit, they clawed back momentum until it felt like the tides were finally turning.  Duroc maintained a delicate balance of offense and defense in close range, while Kyle focused on patching Duroc up and allowing Parasitic Resonance to spread across Mangalica’s network.  Even though the stones amplifying Mangalica’s gravity were beginning to run out of power, Kyle was confident they’d have enough time to finish it.  As if coming to the same conclusion, Mangalica stepped back, a look of hatred in his eyes.

“You bastards ruined everything,” the War Chieftain spat, a dribble of blood coming out of his mouth.  “But this is enough. At least you’ll die for what you’ve done.  That will be enough to satisfy me.”

The moment he finished speaking, Kyle saw a glass vial filled with dark liquid appear in his hand.

“He has a storage item?” Kyle blurted.  He’d already launched a wave of Aura Force to try and break the beaker, and Duroc sent a shard of crystal to do the same.  Both were too slow.

Mangalica downed the drink, vial and all, and swallowed.  Everything went still for a moment, until he let out another roar, his aura spiking.  The stones binding him crumbled to dust as his power reached a new zenith, and he lunged forward with an overhead swing right at Duroc, the air itself splitting with the force of the blow.  There was no doubt in Kyle’s mind that even a glancing blow would be fatal.

A silver barrier exploded into existence a moment later, Storm Shelter only recently having become available again.  Even empowered as it was by Heart of Creation, it buckled under the raw force of the attack.

Duroc looked up at the mad orc with a slack-jawed expression.  “I don’t understand how he can have so much power left in reserve.”

[THE PRICE FOR THAT POWER IS COSTLY,] C.H.A.D.D. offered as another blow rocked the barrier.  [I BELIEVE THAT THIS POTION WAS MEANT TO AMPLIFY THE EFFECTS OF THE ASSIMILATION PROCESS, AND THE BACKLASH WILL LIKELY BE FATAL.  THAT MAY NOT HAVE BEEN THE CASE BEFORE ABSORBING THE LAST HODAG, BUT IT ALMOST CERTAINLY IS NOW.]

A third strike left cracks across the barrier, and Kyle swore.  “How soon will that be?”

[NOT SOON ENOUGH FOR STORM SHELTER TO BUY YOU THE TIME.  HOWEVER, IT ALSO REVEALED FLAWS IN THE ASSIMILATION PROCESS.  I BELIEVE YOU CAN USE THOSE.]

Without another word, C.H.A.D.D. highlighted what almost looked like small seams within Mangalica’s mana network that had been agitated by the potion.  All it took Kyle was a quick look, and he nodded.

“Thank you, C.H.A.D.D.  Duroc, get out of here.  Your work is done, now it’s my turn to finish it.”

He and Duroc exchanged a meaningful look, and the orc finally nodded.  “Just don’t die on me Kyle.  It’s a bad look to have a Courier finish a job then get killed before they can get home and receive payment.”

Kyle gave a tired smile in return, focusing on his tenuous connection with Parasitic Resonance.

Are you ready?  He sent to Phaegos.

I thought you’d never ask, the entity responded, anticipation clear in its tone.

Mangalica swung at the barrier one more time, and Kyle knew that Storm Shelter wouldn’t be able to hold out.  He ducked low as Duroc stepped back, the skill’s last stand barely enough to let them dodge.  Kyle dove forward, hitting the ground hard.  Every tendril of Parasitic Resonance he could muster was extended, focusing their effects on the seams in Mangalica’s mana network that C.H.A.D.D. highlighted.

For a moment, everything slowed down as the mad orc raised his blade for another blow.  Kyle could feel the gentle discrepancies in Mangalica’s energy.  The imperfect absorption of at least half a dozen hodags, not to mention the powerful variant, left traces.  With a thought and force of Willpower, he focused on those traces, compressing and tearing as he released all the conceptual Death that Phaegos stockpiled.

Mangalica let out a violent convulsion, the blade still falling and catching Kyle across his back, though without the same power the orc used before.  Kyle grimaced as he felt the blade bite into him, but kept focused on his work.  Just a little more.  Somehow, the War Chieftain was still standing, even though he was staggering back.  That’s when Kyle realized his miscalculation.

“The potion…” he whispered.  In his haste to finish the fight, he hadn’t considered the way the potion bound to Mangalica’s body.  While destroying the seams and flooding him with Death would kill him, the potion was acting as a binding agent, holding him together longer than should have been possible.  He was grievously injured, but still able to fight.

Mangalica staggered backward, dark ichor freely flowing from his wounds as his body began to rot from the inside out.  And yet he still held his sword.  Kyle took a deep breath as he pushed himself to his feet, willing the scale armor to mend itself and bind up the wound.  Just one more-

Before he could finish the thought, a flash of silver knocked Mangalica off his feet, and Duroc slammed into him from the air, his spear piercing directly through the enemy War Chieftain’s skull.  The collapsed in a tangle, and Duroc was thrown away by Mangalica’s violent thrashing.  For a moment, he almost thought that their fight would continue.  And then the massive orc stopped moving, and Kyle felt the telltale sensation of his attributes growing.  He’d reached Level 60 in his Unbroken Survivor class, and was ready to step into mid-tier C Grade.

He locked eyes with Duroc, who was breathing heavily, and then stumbled as the pain in his back started to come to the forefront.

Maybe we’ll rest first, he thought, slumping to the ground as he used his remaining mana on Healing Impetus, doing his best to patch up the injury.  After all, there were big decisions to make.  For now, he just offered a weary smile.  They’d won.
