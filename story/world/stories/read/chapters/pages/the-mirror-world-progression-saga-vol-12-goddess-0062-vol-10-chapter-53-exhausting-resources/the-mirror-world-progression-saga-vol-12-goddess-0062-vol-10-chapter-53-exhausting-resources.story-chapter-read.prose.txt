Things weren’t going well among the elites, to Odysseus’s perception. The undead champion was now pressing on the offense as his animated armor wraiths swooped in, their prowess near to his. Individually, the best of the alliance side could handle them and fend them off, but nothing was putting them down, and they kept dealing Entropic-style wounds that couldn’t be healed. The physical variety, not the rarer pure elemental damage, but it was problematic enough. Everyone was getting little cuts, and those wounds were stacking up in many cases.

I’m not doing enough. I expected… I don’t know what I expected. Less futility. They can’t touch me like this, and I can barely touch them. I suspect even that is an illusion…

He watched as Servitors failed and men were wounded — and soon evacuated as their usefulness in the battle was expended. The same couldn’t be said for wounded minotaurs. They got more ferocious and harder to put down the more they were wounded. The ensuing stalemate was at least something of an anchor for the alliance side, but how long would it last? Meanwhile, the wraiths still picked them apart, slowly but surely.

Even the Avatars of deities took dings. Disheartening to those gathered, but Odysseus knew they were little different than most fighters, just tougher. He’d been trained on this eventuality and faced high-tier spirits himself. In fact, attrition was a great tactic against them. They almost always fought emotionally, and these two were no exceptions. They would inevitably make mistakes.

This battle needs Samantha, but she has her own problems.

Odysseus moved in once more, everyone else almost in slow motion due to his multidimensional senses. He aimed to keep protecting others. Stall.

It wasn’t enough, but in the end, he was just another soldier heeding orders here — wasn’t he?

Knowing she had to push for any temporary bonus she could, and that things were very close, Samantha quaffed a very priceless potion she’d been saving: Potion of the Legendary Charge, giving a unique +4 (Legendary) bonus to literally everything, ignoring wound negations, and upgrading all wound categories dealt, for only ten seconds. It was a one-of-a-kind prize from Zephyr, and it made her sick not to use it on offense, but here she was. Her instincts had kept her holding it back, even against the liches.

Perhaps Azure can brew a lesser version? She filed the thought away for another time — if she’d live to see another time, that is.

Meanwhile, a Resemblant cast another temp boost for +3, and chimed in her head with the warning, “This is a one-off for the day. You can’t take any more. There is one more lesser bonus possible, for two ELs.”

The attack came, and she resisted it. Good.

For the ten seconds she had, she began to carve a circle in the ground, working awkwardly with her eyes closed, but spirit sight was plenty for these purposes. [Abjurer: Ultimate Protection Circle] would give a continual +3 against any form of harm, and even absorb wounds once every 5 seconds. If she could last out to the point where that was relevant enough to rebuff them, great. Though the shamanistic buff Great Toad’s Untouchable Imperiousness only lasted 30 seconds, Resemblants could saturate her with endless similar ‘spell curse’ effects for -2 to ELs.

The attacks had not been coming exactly every second; instead coming sometimes with gaps of a second. It had been something shy of twenty seconds since they began. In her world, that felt like minutes passing.

Another Resemblant buff gave her +2, and boom, she resisted the next mental strike. It was weaker. One more second. She couldn’t rush the circle, either. A full ten seconds of real time, to pour into the ritual…

There were no more temp bonuses from spells. The spell curses the Resemblants were waiting to cast wouldn’t stack with the shamanistic buff, so that wouldn’t help.

The next attack was slightly higher than the last. Samantha used [Rogue’s Luck] and hoped for the best.

Pain raked her mind. Superficial damage — she’d tied them. She kept her focus. Three total seconds on the circle. Etching into the ground, the magic aura glowed bright blue to her perceptions. All around her, the eyes of her Resemblants glowed similarly. Ghosts of her realm, watching their mistress with worry and impatience.

Another mental scouring and pain. Minor Wound, downgraded by [Timeless Coalescent]. Not only was it not limited and would continue doing that, but Superficial Wounds, at least by default, didn’t stack and upgrade. She could take an infinite number of them without real cost — aside from the trauma, but this level of pain was like the brush of grass on her skin by now. She could almost laugh at it.

Four seconds on the circle…

Boom, boom, boom. Everything repeated, and wouldn’t you know it, they came every single second! She gritted her teeth at the anticipation of pain each time. It was definitely giving her a migraine. No matter. Her will held; her will was supreme!

Seven seconds on the circle…

Finally, what she expected came. The ramp up, the feel of a more intense strike. No need for [Alter Fate] with My Future Now available. She dumped 70% of her current MP into it for a +7. Easy resistance!

Eight seconds on the circle…

An even stronger attack came; obscenely strong. The assassin’s strike. She panicked a bit, not for this one, but for what would come after. If they had another, she was doomed.

She had little choice — she utilized [Alter Fate], her last one, to force failure.

Nine seconds on the circle. Almost complete. She’d almost made that glowing mystical frame around her, with the inside of it filling with additional symbology. Only a bare fraction was left.

Another strike came. Not weak. It hit her like a migraine dialed to eleven, threatening to scramble her brain, and firing through the ‘nerves’ of her essential existence. She resisted it, holding on by the force of her prodigious will. Her whole body shook, and she suppressed a scream.

You have suffered a mental Minor Wound (downgraded from Major). You are at -1 Effective Levels to all actions for 5 seconds. You have resisted additional pain effects. You’ve maintained concentration.

With a final gasp, with tearing eyes from the strain of the effort, Samantha finished the spell circle, which flared into existence around her just as the potion duration died.

Mercilessly, another strong attack came, but the circle wiped it out completely.

But one second later came another, right at the last of the shamanistic buff’s duration. It ripped into her, feeling like a hook into her brain, into her very consciousness, trying to pull it out.

You have suffered a mental Minor Wound (downgraded from Major). You are at -1 Effective Levels to all actions for 5 seconds.

Two Minor Wounds have upgraded to a Major Wound. You are at -1 Effective Levels to all actions for 24 hours (healing will not remove due to mental echo). You are stunned for 5 seconds. You have resisted unconsciousness from the pain.

Samantha was on her knees without even realizing it. Still in the circle — barely. Whatever the bastards were doing, it included enhanced pain.

A Resemblant cast something to blunt the next effect. It wouldn’t be enough.

I don’t have enough… there’s just too many… too many to face… thousands… What am I? One woman.

A mind dagger plunged into her head and ripped. She almost screamed — it came out as a wheeze, pulling breath from her lungs. Her fists were shaking, clenched. She would not scream. Not for them.

You have suffered a mental Minor Wound (downgraded from Major). You are at -1 Effective Levels to all actions for 5 seconds. You are stunned for 5 seconds. You have resisted unconsciousness from the pain. No other effects, but additional wounds can upgrade.

The pain was ramping. They were jabbing into some hollow and worrying it, much like a serrated knife through a hole carved into the guts. Damage she couldn’t even properly fathom was dealt. Something was lost as it was ripped away.

Some blessed delay — she didn’t know how long — but then, yet another strike came. Pain like fire-wreathed lightning blooming and propagating somewhere in her deepest core self. She bit half of her tongue off in her mouth. Something triggered, regenerating it instantly. Ironic, somehow. The extra piece of tongue fell out along with coughed-out spraying spittle. A sound came out of her she barely recognized as hers: a feral growl.

Her fingers were clenched around compressed dirt. It felt like all that tethered her. Her mind was inexorably being stripped away.

Too many. The weight of the entire Dominion on my spirit! They know who I am. They know. Just Samantha. Just one woman; just another victim.

Another attack was automatically blocked by the circle. It didn’t matter. Another came. Weaker. Strong enough, though.

You have suffered a mental Minor Wound (downgraded from Major). You are at -1 Effective Levels to all actions for 5 seconds.

Two Minor Wounds have upgraded to a Major Wound; Two Major Wounds have upgraded to a Critical Wound. You are at -2 Effective Levels, or -20% to Effective Levels (whichever is worse), for all actions. You are stunned for 10 seconds. You have blacked o-

You have resisted unconsciousness from the pain.

From her scoured and ripped up mind, weakness spread to every part of her, even as her body remained entirely impeccable. Something in her wanted to flop in the dirt, writhe, spew obscenities — crack like an egg — but an inner sliver within kept it from happening, kept the body poised. Shaking, drooling out of her mouth, teeth clenched, snarling like an animal… but fallen no further than her knees. Awake; aware.

She heard a scream. It wasn’t her, though.

No screams for them. I’ll die a fighter. Eyes will see, but who could remember?

Another attack came, and she lost her conscious self somewhere in the damage, the messages of the System all blurring together.
