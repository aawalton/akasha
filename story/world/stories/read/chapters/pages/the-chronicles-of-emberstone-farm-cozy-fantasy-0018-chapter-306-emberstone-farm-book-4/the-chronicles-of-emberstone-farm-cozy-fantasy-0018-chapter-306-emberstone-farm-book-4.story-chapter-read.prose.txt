“What!” Eggy poked the vials with a finger. One was a plain glass container full of red powder. The other was a very fancy violet and pink crystal container with a gold stopper shaped like a rose containing a clear liquid. “Dragon blood? Dragon tears?”

Since we didn’t really need people with strong backs to haul water for everyone every day, the people we hired were those who had more specialized skills.
