Thessalanor nods as I finish summarising what I’ve learned from Elarion. “So, did you personally treat any of the patients suffering from Energy poisoning?”

“I did,” he admits. “I’ve never seen a case of Energy poisoning that severe from consuming such a small portion of food.”

“Then you’ve seen Energy poisoning before?” I ask next.

He nods, a faint, knowing smile touching his lips.

“There’s always the odd Warrior who thinks consuming a Core all at once will help them progress faster, or a non-Classer who believes that consuming a Core will grant them a Class. I’ve even seen a case where a merchant of ill-repute convinced a poor soul that the beast Core was actually a Class stone and that the ill effects were just the Class settling.” He shakes his head in condemnation.

That last comment catches my attention.

“Wait, Energy poisoning can be caused by non-Classers consuming Cores?”

“Yes,” Thessalanor replies, looking slightly puzzled. “That’s…fairly common knowledge among Healers.” A moment later, his expression shifts in understanding. “Ah. I forgot you haven’t had a traditional Healer’s education, my lord.”

I incline my head slightly in acknowledgement of his apologetic tone. I would guess from his words that the wider population are far less aware of the dangers.

“And you’ve never seen a case where someone absorbed a Core without suffering Energy poisoning?” I press, my mind flashing to Loran.

“No,” Thessalanor immediately answers, then hesitates. “Though…generally people only come to me when they’re already unwell,” he points out. “So it’s possible that others have absorbed Cores without suffering unfortunate symptoms.”

“What are the symptoms?” I ask next. I don’t think that Loran has been suffering in any way, but I suppose I might not know what to look for.

Thessalanor shrugs lightly.

“At first the symptoms are generally mild – headaches, a dry mouth, a general sense that something is wrong. It varies from person to person. However, like most illnesses, if ignored, it worsens. Heart irregularities are common, chest or joint pains, difficulty breathing… That’s usually when they come to see me.”

“And if they don’t?”

Thessalanor’s expression turns grave.

“That’s when I tend to see permanent changes.” He pauses as if remembering, then continues. “One patient developed eyes that could see almost perfectly in complete darkness, but became completely blind in anything brighter than a moonless night. Another’s skin hardened and tightened, restricting his movement until he could barely move with any speed or dexterity.”

“Out of interest,” I start slowly, a theory forming, “was the first a scout, and the second a warrior who used a shield?”

Thessalanor’s eyebrows shoot up.

“Now that you mention it…yes, I believe they were.”

“Hm,” I murmur, pieces falling into place.

The last time I examined Loran’s internal matrix, I noticed a clearly purposeful change. It’s no longer just stray tendrils extending in random directions. There’s structure forming: pattern, cohesion.

I still can’t see exactly what it will become, not yet. But it’s clearly developing in a positive direction. When I spoke to Loran afterwards, he didn’t seem like he knew exactly what he was doing, but he was tapping into what felt right and what didn’t, just as he’s being particular about the Cores and only using the ones that feel right to him.

And it seems to be working. He’s already reported that he feels stronger, faster. His knife work has improved noticeably – Maxen and Master Ulim, the carpenter who took him as an apprentice, have been particularly pleased with his progress.

But what if Loran and his burgeoning internal matrix isn’t actually anything new? What if anyone who absorbs a Core potentially starts growing an internal matrix?

Beasts have an instinctual understanding about how they should shape the Energy that runs through their systems, long before they’re able to understand what they’re doing. What if humans don’t? What if they just…let the Energy run riot? My own experience with Pure Energy made it very clear that it isn’t necessarily benign when left to its own devices – perhaps that’s what ‘Energy poisoning’ is.

The Class system offers a means by which Energy can be put to use, but what if it’s not the only option for humans, just the safest one?

I remember Nicholas talking about people who tried to absorb Energy intentionally, and how very few of them had good results. Hopefully that won’t be the result for Loran, and if it isn’t, perhaps that will only be because he’s had guidance – because he’s able to access his internal matrix and see the effect the Cores are having.

I feel like I’m on the cusp of something here, though I can’t help but wonder something: if Energy poisoning does offer the chance of enhancement equivalent to the Class system, why is there so little information about it? Surely it’s not that no one has ever succeeded – even if it’s a chance in a million, all it takes is a million people trying to find that one who makes it.

A chill goes down my spine as I wonder whether the information could possibly be being censored. I want to dismiss that thought, but my experience of Moriax so far prevents me from doing so.

In fact, the more I think about it, the more I realise that it’s actually likely. After all, this kind of information could send the current system toppling.

At the moment, the Great Houses and the King are at the top of the hierarchy because they have the best Classes, the biggest stockpiles of Class and Skill stones, and wealth goes along with those. A peasant revolution would never work in this world where the King could single-handedly crush an army of those Classless peasants.

Yet if those peasants were themselves able to absorb Energy and twist it to empower themselves? If the common person didn’t need to beg, borrow, or scrape for access to magical Skills?

How far would those in charge go to suppress information of this magnitude?

I swallow.

I could be barking up the wrong tree here, I remind myself. Maybe Energy poisoning isn’t Energy running rampant instead of being directed to creating an internal matrix. Maybe there’s something special about Loran that allows him to make a success of something that almost everyone else would fail at.

But I suspect not.

And if not, then what do I do? If the method Loran’s following proves to be a success, can I live with myself if I don’t offer it to others, despite knowing it’s putting a target on my back, on the backs of those I love?

Or what if more and more people start suffering Energy poisoning – can I just stand by knowing that I have a possible method wherein they can transform weakness to strength?

I breathe in deeply, reminding myself that these are all very much theories. For now, Energy poisoning is causing problems. Even if it’s not inherently a bad thing, there’s no way I can give the kind of guidance Loran has had to everyone in the settlement, not even every non-Classer. Especially since, right now, it requires a Dominate-derivative Bond. Tiria has been working on developing a spell that is able to map some else’ internal matrix, but so far she hasn’t had any success.

Whatever the long term effects of Energy poisoning, for now, we need to figure out how to create food that everyone can eat.

My mind recentred on the issue at hand, I look at Thessalanor again. He’s been waiting patiently while I process my thoughts.

“What treatments do you use for Energy poisoning?” I ask.

“In this case, or in general, my lord?” he asks for clarification.

“Both,” I reply. “Anything you can tell me may help us solve the problem we’re facing.”

Thessalanor inclines his head and begins.

“For those who came to me this week, I used a purging draught to clear their physical systems of as much of the ingested material as possible. This prevents the contaminant from continuing to affect the digestive system. Without that step, I would be working against ongoing contamination.

“Next, I administered a specialised tablet designed to absorb excess Energy. It sits in the stomach and draws Energy from the surrounding tissues. It can only be left in the system for up to four candlemarks at a time or it causes its own complications. After that time, I used another emetic to evacuate the stomach and remove the tablet.

“After that, I kept the patients under observation for twenty-eight candle marks. If symptoms persisted, I repeated the process. In these cases, we only needed two cycles, since the Energy, though potent, had not been in their systems for long. Indeed, according to Lord Elarion, they presented symptoms unusually quickly.”

“What is the normal timeframe?” I ask.

“Typically between three days and a week after ingestion,” Thessalanor replies promptly. “Sometimes it’s late enough that the purging draught does nothing and we must rely purely on the tablet – often resulting in a poorer prognosis.”

Humming thoughtfully, I focus on internalising the information.

“Should I continue with explaining the general treatment methods, my lord?” he asks.

“Yes, please.”

“The overall process is much the same,” he continues, “but the longer the Energy has been in the system – or the higher the level of the core – the longer the absorption tablet must remain active.”

He hesitates, then adds more quietly, “Any permanent changes cannot be reversed. Mutations such as altered eyesight or hardened skin persist.”

“Not even through regrowth?” I check, though I suspect I know the answer.

He shakes his head. “Even when afflicted limbs are removed and regrown, the mutation returns with them.”

I nod slowly. That aligns with what I saw in the Alcaoris hatchlings. They, too, suffered from Energy overload and their internal matrices had been damaged, or, in Noir’s case, outright destroyed. I wasn’t able to heal their physical deformities permanently until their internal matrices had been untangled. I suspect the case is the same here.

“And you have no direct way of sensing the Energy within a patient?” I ask. “Or removing it more precisely?”

“I do not,” Thessalanor admits. “And as far as I know, no Healer does.” He shrugs faintly. “These cases are most common among non-Classers. Those with Classes can usually absorb Cores safely, provided they do so gradually and within their limits.”

I hear what he doesn’t say – that with the majority of the patients being among those without money to pay for Classes in the first place, there’s been little incentive to research further.

“Then how do you feel about making history?” I ask with a wry smile. “I want you to develop a treatment that allows you to sense Energy levels within a person – and to control them. Ideally, we’ll be able to remove harmful excess while preserving what is beneficial, but, for now, I’ll settle for simply removing it.”

Thessalanor pauses, searching my gaze for something, perhaps wondering whether I’m serious.

“That might take a long time, my lord,” he warns, his voice slightly hoarse. “Scholars have tried for years to achieve similar objectives without success.”

“I know. But if we don’t do this, the consequences will be worse,” I reply gravely. “You won’t have to do it without help. I’ll assign Mage Tiria to assist you – she has a great deal of knowledge that may be useful. And I’ll assist where I can.” I pause for thought, then add, “In the meantime, I’ll speak to the cooks. If we can remove the Energy before it enters the body, that may be the safer route for now.”

“Remove the Energy–” Thessalanor cuts himself off with a sharp laugh. A moment later, he looks apologetic. “Sorry, my lord.”

I wave his words away, a thought bubbling evanescently to the surface of my mind.

“This tablet…if it were placed in a container of Energy-rich substance, do you think it might draw the Energy out in the same way as it does in the body?”

Thessalanor considers the idea carefully.

“I have only ever tested it inside someone. It may not work without a living environment.”

“But you think it might be possible?” I press.

He hesitates, then tilts his head slowly from side to side. “I suppose. We don’t fully understand how it works at all, but it may function similarly in a liquid medium that mimics the stomach.” He looks intrigued at the thought.

I nod slowly, considering the factors. The stomach is acidic, warm, and damp. Any of those may be deciding factors for the tablet. Or it might be that the tablet functions only when it’s inside a living being. But if it works, this might be an easy solution to our problem.

“I would like some to test.” Then another thought occurs. “Actually, how much of these tablets do you have? Can you produce more when you run out?” The last thing we need is a solution dependent on ingredients from Moriax.

Thessalanor sends a warm smile at River.

“If you had asked yesterday, I would have said our supply was limited. The base is clay, which is readily available here, but the active ingredient is a plant I feared we would not find on this continent.”

He gestures toward River.

“However, she and her master believe they’ve identified a suitable alternative. We haven’t yet tested it on a patient, but it reacts identically to the other in the alchemical tests we’ve evaluated it with.”

I grimace slightly as the next course of action presents itself to me. I’m reluctant to do this given, as Elarion had said, the treatment certainly isn’t pleasant. But we need to know whether this will work.

Reaching out through the Alliance Bond, I contact Elarion.

We might have the beginnings of a solution, at least for the short term. But…we need volunteers to suffer Energy poisoning and treatment to see if it’s working as it should. I almost hold my breath – I know it’s a big ask.

Elarion is silent for a long moment, though I know he’s heard. Then I practically feel him sigh.

I will ask, he promises heavily. Are you sure this will work?

Not at all, I tell him honestly. But we have to find something that will – and this test has to happen sooner or later.

Then I will at least make the test worth it on my side, he decides.
