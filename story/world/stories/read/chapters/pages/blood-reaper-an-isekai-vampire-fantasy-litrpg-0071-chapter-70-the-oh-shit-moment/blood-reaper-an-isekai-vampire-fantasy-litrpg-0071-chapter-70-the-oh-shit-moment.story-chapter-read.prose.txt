The lakeshore stretched before him, a desolate expanse rife with blackened grass that crunched underfoot like charred bone. The lake’s obsidian waters remained unnaturally still despite the breeze that whispered through the surrounding forest. Ancient pines stood sentinel a good distance from the water’s edge, their needles rustling secrets to one another while their gnarled roots snaked into the dark soil. Rocks punctuated the landscape, the clusters of boulders worn smooth by time providing the only cover in this exposed battleground. Ace stood at the heart of it all, and he had just made a thrilling discovery.

This was it—the break Ace and his team needed to take this guy down.

Through the hivemind connection he shared with the other party members, Ace relayed a single command:

Target the connections.

Shadow constructs darted around them—four twisted parodies of Raven’s form, each one trailing tendrils of darkness back to their creator at the lakeshore. Each duplicate moved with unnatural speed, blinking in and out of existence only to reappear in another spot entirely. The real Raven stood motionless amid the chaos, eyes vacant, body little more than a vessel for the shadow magic that had consumed him.

High above the battle, the System perched on a thick tree branch, her legs swinging with childish glee. Her eerie giggle cut through the sounds of war as she observed their struggle.

“Oh, this is delightful!” she called down.

Ace ignored her, focusing instead on the shadow threads—and, more importantly, the plan. Through the hivemind, Ace transmitted his plan to the others. Not in words, exactly, but in flashes of intention and targeted awareness.

Four constructs. Four of us. One each.

The response came instantly through their shared connection: a chorus of affirmative pulses that made it clear everyone understood.

Ace nodded sharply.

Go.

The team erupted into action as one entity—four bodies moving with a single purpose, their movements perfectly calibrated to complement each other. The air crackled with focused intent as each team member channeled their specific abilities.

They had what they needed for this to work: Ace’s hivemind, Marcus’s telepathic enhancement, and the element of surprise.

They could do this.

They could end this.

One by one, in rapid succession, everyone attacked the nearest construct. As much as he wanted to protect his team, to look out for them amidst the battle, he had to trust them.

They had come this far, and they could hold their own without him.

The nearest construct lunged at Ace, its shadow claws extended like liquid obsidian knives. He caught the attack in his peripheral vision—a blur of darkness cutting through the air, half a second from tearing through his throat. Ace channeled everything into his legs and jumped into the air with a burst of power that used the full scope of his buffed Strength stat. His enhanced muscles catapulted him fifteen feet straight up while the construct’s attack sliced through empty space.

The shadow entity howled in frustration as it skidded to a stop, and then it slammed both fists into the spot where Ace had stood seconds before. Raw shadow energy erupted from the impact point. The ground didn’t just break—it disintegrated. Rock and soil vaporized on contact, leaving a six-foot crater that glowed with residual shadow energy. Tiny wisps of darkness spiraled upward from the depression like black smoke, the air around it dropping twenty degrees in an instant.

Yikes.

Good thing that hadn’t hit him.

Ace hit the ground rolling and landed in a tight crouch at the crater’s edge. A loud crunch from under his boots kicked up spirals of shadow smoke around him, and it rolled over his feet like a low-hanging fog.

Alright, enough defense.

Time to fuck shit up.

Quickly, he racked his brain for the perfect plan. Shadow Realm Immersion would be too risky against an entity that thrived in the Shadow Realm—not to mention he would take 200% damage if he got hit while phased.

Nope. Shadow Realm Immersion wasn’t an option, but he could still use Shadow Mark.

He grinned.

Ace summoned six Duskblades simultaneously, their obsidian edges glinting with deadly purpose as they hovered around his head. Each one rotated slowly, ready to be let loose. They all pointed directly at their target—the tendril. With Shadow Mark, he chose the six perfect spots along the tether that would snap the damn thing in half.

Oblivious, the enemy ran toward him yet again, this time with its clawed hands extended.

Ace, meanwhile, remained motionless as he waited for the perfect moment. When the construct finally lunged, Ace launched all six Duskblades at once—not at the creature, but in a circular pattern converging on the shadow thread.

The blades shot outward simultaneously, leaving trails of shadow essence that reminded Ace of black comet tails. They flew in a spiraling formation, not toward the creature’s body but clear into the thread connecting the beast to Raven.

The first blade sliced into the tendril’s base, as close to Raven as it could go without the sentinel noticing—a carefully planned trick to make the creature think Ace had missed.

The second cut through the construct’s midsection—a distraction to keep its focus off Ace’s true attacks, each subsequent blade striking at calculated intervals along its length.

The third, fourth, fifth, and sixth were his real attacks. The air itself split apart as they sliced through the wind, and a cold chill iced the sky in their wake.

The blades sliced through the tendril from multiple angles, each severing another chunk of the connection. As the construct faltered, momentarily disconnected from its host, Ace manifested a seventh Duskblade, took aim, and launched it directly at the weakest point of the surviving tether.

The blade plunged through, and the shadow construct didn’t stand a chance.

It froze mid-lunge as its connection to Raven shattered. Its form rippled like disturbed water, its outline blurring as its substance lost cohesion. A high-pitched wail erupted from where its mouth should’ve been—not sound, exactly, but a pure vibration that rattled Ace’s bones. Darkness pulled inward from its extremities, its limbs collapsing into its torso as if sucked through an invisible drain. Its chest cavity cracked open, revealing a churning void that devoured it from within. The creature’s mass compressed into a dense ball of writhing shadow no larger than a fist, hovering for one suspended moment before collapsing with a thunder-crack that left behind a brief flash of shadow—a hole in reality that sealed itself instantly.

Where the construct had stood, only a faint scorch mark remained, wisps of dark vapor curling upward like the last breath of a dying thing.

Two down.

Three remaining.

With a quick scan of the battlefield, Ace took in the others’ onslaughts. He spotted each of his team members, one by one, to make sure they were safe.

To Ace’s left, Marcus had locked onto his target. The man’s eyes flared with concentration, his consciousness latching onto the shadow tendril with psychic claws. The thread writhed under his attack. Power built around him in waves. In a single, brutal blow, he unleashed everything. The blast tore through the thread, and the connection snapped with an audible crack. The shadow construct convulsed, its form destabilizing without its master’s control, and it exploded into wisps of darkness.

Mind Leech, Psychic Rupture, and Terror Cascade, all used together in perfect unison.

Well done.

A pulse of gratitude from Marcus came through Ace’s connection.

At the same time, Tara was approaching her target differently. Her Echo Staff hummed with energy as she activated its magic—magic she had only just begun to understand—and her eyes glowed a fierce crimson. She spun her staff in a tight arc, its tip leaving trails of golden light in the air as it connected with the tether. Her staff vibrated on impact, recognizing the corrupted shadow entity and amplifying its damage accordingly. The thread shriveled at the point of contact, its darkness burning away like paper touched by flame.

The construct screamed—a sound no living creature should make—before collapsing into nothingness.

Nice! Ace thought to her.

From across the battleground, she caught his eye and grinned with victory.

Meanwhile, Olivia approached her target with lethal grace. Her movements flowed like silk in water as she activated her own Skills and created three perfect duplicates of herself. These blood-formed copies mirrored her dance-like movements as she positioned herself alongside the shadow thread. In unison, Olivia and her duplicates balanced atop the shadow tendril itself and ran along its surface, treating it as a tightrope leading back to Raven. Each step built momentum, her feet barely touching the dark path as she glided forward. The shadow construct swiped at her, but struck only one of her duplicates, which absorbed the hit before dissolving. When she reached the critical junction—where thread met host—Olivia unleashed the final burst of her magic. Her body twisted into a brutal yet beautiful spiral, her blade slicing through the shadow connection with such perfect timing that the entire thread transformed into a shower of dissolving shadow particles.

Four constructs. Four clean kills. Perfect execution, and it all happened in the span of mere seconds.

At the sudden loss of all his constructs, Raven’s body convulsed violently. Each severed connection seemed to have hit him simultaneously—four pieces of his corrupted soul, torn away at once. He vomited another torrent of shadow essence that slithered across the ground like snakes before evaporating.

His spine wrenched backward at an unnatural angle, his vertebrae cracking audibly as they shifted beneath his corrupted skin. Bones threatened to puncture his corrupted skin as his body bent nearly double in the wrong direction.

The scream that erupted from his throat started out human but warped midway, plummeting two octaves into something ancient and terrible. Black fluid erupted from his mouth, his eyes, and his ears in pressurized jets, hanging suspended in the air for a few moments before dissolving into shadow mist.

He dropped to one knee, his fists pounding craters into the charred lakeshore as tremors racked his frame. The lake water recoiled from him, pulling away in a six-foot radius. His face twisted through expressions of shock, rage, and something worse.

Fear.

It was a moment of weakness Ace couldn’t let slip through his fingers.

He charged across the lakeshore, his boots digging furrows in the earth as he pumped every ounce of strength into his legs. A surge of Duskblades manifested around him, humming with anticipation.

Ace raised one hand to Shadow Mark Raven.

This was almost over.

Just as he was about to mark the sentinel, Raven’s head snapped up, and his void-black eyes locked with Ace’s. The sentinel’s eyes had changed—the amber irises were now completely gone, replaced by bottomless voids.

Without warning, the corrupted guardian pressed his palms flat against the ground, and shadow essence sparked around his fingers like black lightning.

Oh.

Not good.

Before Ace could change course, Raven unleashed a concussive pulse of pure shadow energy that erupted outward in a perfect circle. The force slammed into Ace like a freight train, lifting him completely off his feet and hurling him backward through the air. He slammed against a granite boulder at the edge of their battleground, and the impact drove the air from his lungs. The massive stone cracked behind him, chunks breaking free and raining down around his crumpled form. He struggled to remain conscious through the explosion of pain across his back and skull, and his Duskblades blinked out of existence in small spirals of smoke.

His head spun, and though he blinked in a desperate effort to stay awake, darkness crawled along the edges of his vision.

The System clapped her hands from her perch. “Oh my, that was quite the show! The Sergeant looks so dizzy!”

Ace managed to open his eyes a crack, just in time to see Raven’s body convulse. The sentinel’s back arching at an impossible angle. When he straightened, the shadow essence leaking from his wounds changed direction, flowing back into his body instead of out.

Lifeforce Siphon—but on steroids.

“Shit,” Ace said under his breath.

Though Lifeforce Siphon was supposed to drain life from others, Raven was absorbing the very shadow energy Ace had tried to use against him. His wounds sealed with corrupted shadow essence, the darkness hardening into new flesh. With each moment, the shadow aura around him intensified, turning the lakeshore into twilight.

Ace pushed himself to his feet and staggered, trying and failing to stay upright as he sought cover.

Fall back, he ordered through the hivemind. Regroup.

But it was too late.

Raven’s hands slammed together, channeling the absorbed energy into an explosive wave of shadow. This one was far more powerful than the one that had hit Ace, and the blow sent all of them sailing backward.

Everyone landed hard against the ground, but Marcus took the worst of it. The wave threw him against one of the boulders in their rocky outcropping with a sickening crack. He collapsed in a heap, blood streaming from a gash in his forehead, and his body twitched in agony.

The psychic backlash rippled through the hivemind, sending spikes of pain through Ace’s skull. Their mental link weakened, and for a moment, he lost connection with all of them. It flickered like a dying flame before reigniting, barely a whisper of what it had been.

“Scattered like insects,” Raven hissed, his voice deeper and less human than before. His gaze locked onto Marcus’s prone form. “The mind-reader first. You’ll never rebuild your connection without him.”

Ace pushed himself to his knees, his head spinning.

Tara, get to Marcus!

Tara scrambled across the rocky terrain, her Echo Staff glowing with healing energy as her feet threatened to give out from under her. Olivia flanked to the right, racing to intercept Raven, but the corrupted guardian was unnaturally fast now that he didn’t have to control the constructs. He vanished in a swirl of shadows, and he reappeared seconds later directly in front of Marcus’s broken body.

Shadow essence coalesced around Raven’s right hand, forming a blade of pure darkness. The weapon hummed as he raised it for an execution strike. Marcus groaned, struggling to move.

Raven attacked—and in the final moment before impact, Marcus jerked sideways with a desperate cry of pain. The shadow blade missed his heart by inches, plunging deep into his shoulder instead. He screamed as shadow corruption erupted from the wound, spreading visibly beneath his skin in web-like patterns.

“Impressive reflexes.” Raven yanked his blade free and took aim a second time. “But you’re just prolonging the inevitable.”

As Marcus writhed on the ground, his psychic link to the team flickered like a dying light. Ace felt the hivemind connection weakening further, their collective awareness fraying with each passing second.

“Yay! Death!” The System’s delighted giggle echoed across the lakeshore.

Maybe she was right—because Ace would never make it in time to help.
