When Lexie opened her eyes to stare at the familiar red sky, she released a sigh of relief.

She’d made it back. For a second there, she’d thought she might not. Provoking an Orcan horde was probably not the brightest idea she’d had, and though she’d fought well, they’d been just as terrible as they were rumored to be.

Inhumanely strong. Outrageously bloodthirsty. Hacking at and hitting themselves just for a chance to get at her.

There had only been about fifty of them there, but it had certainly been an exercise. Lexie was right about the orcs naturally messing with magic. It wasn't just Fae magic either. It was all magic.

Some cards didn’t work as well. They malfunctioned or were less effective than they should have been.

The Orcs somehow broke through her VOID shield and remained unburned by her flames.

It didn’t make sense, but that was how the Orcs operated. That and their superhuman strength was what gave them their edge, and Lexie had gotten a taste of just how much of an edge they had when they’d ripped off her arm.

That had hurt like hell.

She sat up and flexed it now, winding it around her shoulder. Thankfully, Pvilycht had done a good job putting it back together, and it only smarted now.

She’d held out against the Orcs for as long as she could, seriously injuring about thirty of them. She didn’t kill them because it didn’t feel right to do so, and that worked against her because they healed fast and they came back stronger. The horde landed stronger and more painful attacks, and Lexie exhausted all of her magical skills before she finally threwin the towel and teleported out of there. She had no idea how long she’d been fighting, but it had felt like a while, and now that she was awake, she thought she felt more chaos inside her, felt that familiar battle readiness and hunger that was bubbling to the surface.

Oh yeah. She was definitely stronger. Not back to her normal strength yet, but beyond what she’d been on Earth.

With her brand new cards, she was ready for anything.

Pvilycht was sitting next to her with his legs crossed as he focused.

“That is how Neqal used to treat me. Was I his girlfriend?”

Lexie snorted. “Maybe.”

“Then I’m glad I was liberated from that. I do not want a possessive companionship. I’ve made a lot of friends in the library, and I enjoyed their company, but I had to leave them behind when I abandoned my disguise.”

“Sorry about that.” Lexie thought that might happen, both that he would receive a lot of help and that he might be a tad too noticeable. She knew that people were more likely to help good-looking people out, but it was sometimes a challenge to find the balance between good-looking and distractingly pretty. Lexie had thought she'd found that balance. She'dfashioned him after Theo Firebringer, who was good-looking but also the most approachable person she knew. He wasn’tdistractingly pretty to her, but now she remembered how much girls used to go crazy over him on the NET.

“The chaos is merging with the bodies of the infants, and they believe that it’s turning them into super soldiers. They haven't had much success with it. Most of the embryos have died. Only a handful survived, and there’s one that they call Saint-0, who actually finished the program successfully. It’s a name in all the files, and they're attempting to dissect what about the soul is so important."

"Yes."

Irina had told Lexie it was a child soldier. Had she deliberately lied, or did she simply believe at the time that it had been a child soldier?

“What about soul experiments? Are they doing that to the infants, too?”

To merge the training of a child-soldier with the unkillability of an Eldritch-infused embryo? Also, wouldn't the eldritch portion make them vulnerable to saintess and Lightlark attacks? Wasn't the entire point of their souls that they were completely invulnerable to most other systems set to take out the Eldritch?

Could she bind them to her dungeon somehow and keep them from escaping? Or would they merge with the soul of the dungeon, like Aiden suspected? How would they do that? What exactly made them so powerful?

No. Because in case the Emperor really did have his secret soldiers on sleep mode, that would just be giving him a reason to activate them, and it would certainly let him know that they were onto him, which might chase the research underground.

Once she was done, she rested for a few more minutes or hours, counting out all her new cards and making sure the eclipse card was attached firmly to her soul.

Then she teleported back home.
