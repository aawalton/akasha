Seb looked around as he waited for Anya to stop laughing. They were in a Training Hall, he recognized the underlying patterns of the Construct, however it felt wrong. The halls back in Holding Syl felt exactly the same as every other room, while here it felt as though they were-

"Are we in the Depths?" he asked.

"No, but well done," a woman, Mistress Sarna he assumed, answered as she stepped to the middle of the room.

"Most take a few minutes to realize what feels off," Master Leo added in a low rumble as he joined her.

Seb felt Anya come to full attention as they took in the pair. Sarna was slender to the point that she almost looked as though she'd vanish if she turned the right way and tall enough for her limbs to look spider-like. She'd crossed the room as though she'd danced to an unheard song while Leo had stomped his way forward, almost as wide as he was tall, even at a distance it was clear he'd maybe come to Anya's chin if they stood face to face.

"How though," he asked. "The density, the Energy in the air, the flow through the room."

"Expensive Engineer work," Leo said. "Very expensive and very few are qualified to do it."

"Adjustable Energy Density," Sarna said. "Perfect for training duelists who need to understand how it will change their combat style."

"Duelists?" Anya asked.

"Of course. Tournaments are duels. Exchanges between Elites are duels. Any battle against another Awakened is a duel of sorts."

"Bah," Leo said. "You and your duels. Duel this duel that. It lets them train at the same density as when they push, shows them what they're actually facing if they were to try to go further levels down."

"Duels are-" Sarna began then took a deep breath as her limbs shook slightly. "Nevermind. I am Mistress Sarna. I'm responsible for ensuring students are ready for duels and tournaments. This lout is Master Leo, all he cares about are the Depths."

"All I care about? All I care about? If it weren't for those Depths you want to ignore you wouldn't have the strength you do."

"They don't need to hear us bicker," Sarna said. "We're here to evaluate your combat ability."

"You expect us to fight you?" Seb said.

"Not today lad." Leo answered. "We need to know your roles and your fighting styles. See how you handle your basic katas, what your training drills and routines are. Basic evaluation."

Seb knew he was staring blankly at the pair. He had suspicions about some of what they were asking, he knew what katas were at least. Anya seemed just as confused, or at least not certain how they were expected to answer.

"Roles?" Anya said. "There's only two of us. I was taught the roles but they always assumed groups of five or more."

"You made it here as a pair?" Sarna asked, continuing on as they both nodded. "And yet you… Who sponsored you?"

"We're our own Holding, newly founded," Anya answered. "I was raised in Holding Lys on Earth. My partner is an Adult Awakened."

"Earth? Is that a town?" Leo said.

"SmallPond," Sarna answered. "At least from what I recall. Feudal?"

"That's what Marten called us," Seb answered.

He saw the teachers exchange glances. Saw the slightest flicker of reactions between them. Finally Leo turned and stormed off, muttering incomprehensibly.

Did we say something wrong? he sent.

Why do they keep calling us feudal?

Your mother's holding is a little…

Seb!

It is.

Fine. But don't need to say it.

"Let's go with the simple version," Sarna said. "Fighting style or Archetype, and how do you train."

You first, he sent.

"Monk. Original Specialization was Mystic if that helps. I get their focus, deal with them head on."

"Stalker. Witchblade Specialization. I guess the right term would be Skirmisher? I stay to the edges, attack and hinder where they aren't looking for me."

"Witchblade? So hindrance focused?"

Should I mention? he sent.

They're supposed to teach us, if they don't know they can't.

"Hindrance and Bladesong focused."

"Bladesong? That's not something you hear mentioned often."

"Our Seer said the same."

"Likely an effective combination then. And training?"

"Against each other? Our Hall is great for static targets, Depthsborn are great for fighting, but for the rest it's been one another," Anya said.

"Show me. Everyday Husks, use your preferred weapons."

~ * ~

Seb drew his sword as Sarna left the field. Looking over at Anya he saw her waiting with a smile on her face, her robe forming around her. It wasn't technically a weapon, but it was part of her fighting style.

Variant? she sent.

Simple?

"You can start when ready."

"We were just agreeing on keeping things to simple sparring." Anya said before lunging towards him.

Seb had already moved. He knew how his wife fought, she knew how he did. The point wasn't to injure one another, although they would certainly come close, it was to push. She took the lead, lashing out time and time again as he kept his distance.

Each strike was slipped past, each attempt to force him to stand and fight woven around with a smile. He knew he wouldn't win by wearing her out, at least not before they got told to stop. She knew he wasn't trying to. No techniques, nothing more dangerous than their bodies and the lightest of imbuements to make his blade useful.

Their thoughts remained separate, their movements coordinated yet without the meld that took things further. He shifted to the attack, sweeping in on her and turning the spar into a true dance. Both leading, both following, neither ever coming close to landing a blow, despite each strike flying true.

Seb sped up, Anya already a step ahead as he let the blade land at their feet to pulse sporadically. The effect not meant to disable her, simply balance the things between them so they remained matched. He struck, their bodies sliding along one another as they reset on opposite sides from where they had stood a second before. He grinned, feeling Anya's joy at their efforts. He swu-

"Enough!" Sarna called.

Instead of freezing they let their last movements carry through without force and came to a rest side by side.

"War bond?"

"Both," Anya said.

"You pass. I'll show you to your quarters."

Seb's blade flew into his hand before vanishing, banished to join his War Husk.

"We could fail?" he asked.

"If you were useless in a fight? If you'd clearly made it this far through provided Energy alone? Yes."

"Does that-"

"No. Never," Sarna answered before he could actually ask. "Officially every applicant who enters the school does so as an elite Combat Pather."

She gestured to another portal, once again a hallway clearly visible through it. Seb was nearly certain this had to be a Seed. He also knew they couldn't ask, because they would have to explain how they knew the difference.

"Step through and be welcome to Scattered Creek. Your suite is on the third floor, the door marked 3a. Normally you'd have the entire floor to yourselves, but you only get half. Don't try to go into 3b without an invitation."

Seb turned towards the portal, Anya on his heels. A suite meant space to themselves, to relax and think about what they had found out so far without further distractions.

"Dinner will be in 4 hours, the Dean expects everyone to attend," Sarna added as they stepped through the portal.
