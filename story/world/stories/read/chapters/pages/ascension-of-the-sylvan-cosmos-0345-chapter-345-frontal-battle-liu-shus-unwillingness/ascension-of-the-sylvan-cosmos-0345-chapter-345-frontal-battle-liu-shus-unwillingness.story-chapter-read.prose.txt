When they encountered the fog, the debuff caused many army members to change their expressions. <Blood Sacrifice Curse> was an annoying curse that would slowly stack up and become a big hurdle when sieging a location. From the space ahead, the army suddenly saw several creatures appearing from nowhere, moving at a very high speed and looking quite grotesque. Ignatia had been charging by Flor’s side, so she spoke aloud at this moment toward the four Sylvans. “Don’t lose yourselves.”

The four murderous Sylvans regained some clarity in their eyes. After hearing Liu Shu’s scream, they felt their hearts hurting for their lovable World Tree. “<Vinebloom Sylvan Body Liberation>!”

“<Cyclamen Sylvan Body Liberation>!”

“<Belladonna Sylvan Body Liberation>!”

The four of them charged into the mass of Level 40 <Corrupted Spawns> like four cannon balls.

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

[-245,500]! [-440,840]! [337,850]!...

[-154,840]! [-164,500]! [-158,800]!

“This…” Looking at the information, the person was stunned. Lyara urged him. “Don’t stall. Tell me straight.”

Lyara’s face gained a trace of shock for the first time. “Less than forty? A Sylvan is about to become a Third Energy Cycle powerhouse before forty?”

This kind of leveling speed was ridiculous; averaging more than two levels per year was an absurd concept. After all, Sylvans had hundreds of years of lifespan on average! Lyara crossed her arms with worry and commented. “Perhaps, these Sylvans much more of a threat compared to the World Tree. Can we kill them?”

“Lady Lyara, our cursemancers are focused on dealing with the World Tree and can’t move away. Our personal troops are just too lacking to give a decisive blow, much more now that they are in their transformed state.”

Flor and the others cleaved through the constantly replenishing <Corrupted Spawns> and arrived in front of the hidden town. Cinderielle and Ignatia attacked simultaneously, sending two massive fireballs that collided with the air and blew the illusion surrounding the town.

BOOM!

Lyara could be seen from their position, looking at them with a smile. “Well, I should say congratulations on reaching the barrier. Of the three curses, the third one was the most harmful one. Cutting her control over her own energy, Liu Shu was barely maintaining the only skill she had used, the <World Tree Calling>.

The reason for the ten serpents not having participated in combat was because their bodies were about to be forcefully returned. The ten Arboreal Winged Serpents were holding with their wills from being forcefully transported back and succeeding thanks to Liu Shu’s stubbornness not to lose control of this one connection.

“I refuse to surrender!”

Honestly speaking, any other World Tree at the <Second Energy Cycle> would’ve long succumbed to the effects. The only reason Liu Shu was able to resist was one of her skills: <Cerulean Bloodsap (B- Rank Level 3)>

<Cerulean Bloodsap>: Your sap has now become a panacea. It can cure most poisons and ailments that don’t outrank you while also serving as a naturally purifying and healing substance. The world around you will now be deeply influenced by your essence, and all flora will receive several growth boosts.

BOOM!
