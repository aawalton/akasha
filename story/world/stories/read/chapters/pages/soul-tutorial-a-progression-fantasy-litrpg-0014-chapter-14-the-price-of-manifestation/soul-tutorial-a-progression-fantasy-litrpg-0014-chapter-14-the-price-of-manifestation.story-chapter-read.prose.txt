His heart felt like it was on fire; his lungs burned. A devastating impression was left weighted in his chest. He could barely stop himself from shivering, taking small steps closer to the slime.

“I.I.. I need to kill it fast before it does anything,” Luke barely mumbled under his breath.

He rushed toward the slime while gathering as many souls as he could manage inside himself. 3 souls disappeared from the sky and appeared in his chamber as he willed them to orbit. Like old gears, it itched and barely started. Slowly, they worked ever closer to a circular motion.

But it was enough to send a weak flow through his chest, pushing that power further to merge into his right fist, while the slime stood there. Before Luke could ever give it a chance to move.

His fist collided with the dense metallic surface, leaving behind a serious dent. Luke took this chance again to slam his fist against the already dented slime, which only caved in more but didn’t kill it.

The energy from his fist had diminished a little, but it still did considerable damage compared to not using the energy at all.

"Shit, I should have condensed the energy a bit more into my arm,” he shouted.

How could I have made such a dumb mistake?

Backing off to gather the rest of the energy he had left. He spun the souls faster while glaring at the silver slime with the remaining attention he could manage.

Just staying there left Luke uneasy; he didn’t want his legs to get trampled and crushed under the weight of that slime again. He shivered as an eerie feeling overtook him; his chest quaked as the souls spun faster under the stress of Luke's thoughts.

He charged right back in with energy piling towards his arm; the silver slime gleam quivered. Luke was wary of it trying to roll into him, which only made him more observant of its every action, allowing him to notice the slime wobble slightly, halting his charge.

He nearly lost his balance while trying to shift his body away from running into the slime head-on. The slime, somehow, in that quick second of him almost stumbling, rolled towards him with unimaginable speed.

Thankfully, Luke was already taking action before it fully moved in his direction.

Without further ado, he quickly dodged out of the way, sending a small flow of every as quickly as he could back into his legs. His legs flourished with energy just enough to take him out of the slime’s direction.

But that wasn’t the end of the slime as it rolled back towards him, making a sharp turn under his gaze; the slime's movements almost seemed slower than usual. Luke, with his energy levels still low, was dedicating a lot of his time to trying to regain his footing. Gathering all his strength in one leg, he launched himself away from its path.

It thought I would die that easily. The slime was dumber than I thought.

Luke's thoughts raced with excitement at making it out of that ordeal alive, not that he hadn’t almost died twice to the other slimes on multiple occasions.

The slime came to a stop where it had originally appeared.

Before feeling a tremor under the slime, he took a moment to analyze the situation, with difficulty gathering soul energy into his arm. A burst of air exploded in front of him, then a gust knocked him over as the island violently shook. Shielding his eyes with his arm, he endured the dust storm.

Luke stared at the slime, grateful he was still quite far from where it landed, or he would have lost a limb. His soul energy moved along with his will as he tried his best to regain his footing.

Looks like the silver slime is more active this time around…oh, great. He thought while sighing.

Luke barely got to his feet in time to watch the slime hurl itself towards him, speeding up as the distance closed up fast. He barely registered how fast the slime was moving before his body reacted instinctively.

With all the energy already in his right arm, he condensed it even more. As the energy layered over itself. Like threads combining to create something. His energy merged, strengthening his skin, muscles, flesh, and bones. Until his entire arm changed to teal in color. In that moment, time froze as his arm stretched out with the energy in a singular point.

The souls in his chest disappeared, turning into vapor that floated around his arm.

His arm almost resembled the ethereal arm, but was far stronger, as the energy continued to condense. Then a sensation of pain overwhelmed his body; his skin became paler before the slime slammed into his arm.

In the singular point of collision, the dent on the metallic slime enlarged until a gaping hole the size of Luke’s forearm pierced through the whole thing.

The slime core cracked but held together by only a thread, but its inside released a disturbing odor and a grey liquid which corroded the ground. The slime stood there with its body losing mass. As the metallic sheen reflected his body, revealing his destroyed right arm, broken in 7 places, with the teal color still present.

Luke still stood with blood flowing from the right side of his body; the blood trickled onto the ground. As he watched the silver slime gradually die.

Only one thought ran through his head.

I had done it.

Luke laughed with blood in his mouth. His smile appeared to reach from ear to ear. His laughed continued to echo throughout the floating island.

“I had done it.” He screamed at the top of his lungs. The silver slime turned into particles with two silver souls floating in the sky.

But it wasn’t time to celebrate; his devilish slime slowly vanished as he lost feeling in his arm. He still had to figure out a way to survive from these wounds with the low points he had.

But thoughts of despair never left his mind.

Floor 2 left me like this even after I figured out how to use soul energy.

Then.. then what about the next floor?

Then the next floor after that.

Luke’s thoughts fell into a cycle. Tears poured down his face realizing the cruelty of this place.

What if my best isn’t good enough?

The thoughts of his friends, his mom, and his classmates. Resurfaced in his mind. All the people he had known and wondered about. How much more suffering would he have to go through to get out of this hell?

Luke bit his lip, trying to get himself back into focus.

I just need to survive for now.

His thoughts raced, but he approached the pedestal, trying his best to place his destroyed hand on the surface. His blood flowed down the pedestal while the veil appeared.

No… no… no. This can’t be happening to me.

Luke took his hand off the pedestal and put it back on. Repeating this multiple times before he accepted the reality in front of his eyes.

Even through all the pain messing with his head, Luke willed restore to work even when he only had 4 points. But they had actually disappeared as it became cheaper.

Noticing how the wounds on part of his hand were being healed before his very eyes.

With the broken arm shifting back into place, but wasn’t ready to be used in any fight just yet. The bleeding on his side had mostly stopped. But the hue of his arm was still there, even though faded out.

While the lightheadedness was still in full effect, Luke didn’t waste any time willing floor 0 to begin. The energy churned while the green slime appeared.

Luke looked desperate as he rushed forward with clenched teeth. He could only use his legs the best he could to kill enough slime to get a full restore. His body felt heavy, and his vision sometimes even blacked out as he continued fighting.

But he continued on as the souls in the sky increased slowly and surely. Luke was panting on his last breath, trying to kill a slime directly in front of him. He barely jumped out of the way with the last of his energy as the slime shot green acid where he had been.

Luke used his stump to lift himself off the floor, even putting pressure on his broken arm to find some balance. But even through all that mess, he still landed a kick directly into the slime using the full force of his body.

Cracking the core in a way that the slime turned into particles, his eyes grew heavy, and he could barely even find any excitement in finally achieving the rest of the 6 points. He stopped right before the pedestal as he passed out, his arm stretched out, touching the base.
