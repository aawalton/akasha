A few weeks ago, my publisher got an email from Amazon telling us they were going to start offering audiobooks through KU and asking if we wanted to enroll in the program. We said yes, enrolled all of The Fractured Tower and Duskbound in it, and sat back to twiddle our thumbs. Well, it's gone live today. If you have a Kindle Unlimited subscription, you can now, at no extra cost, download the audiobooks for TFT 1 and 2, as well as the entire Duskbound trilogy. Keiran is a bit trickier as the ebook was published by Timeless Wind while the audio was published by Royal Guard. Hopefully we can figure something out there and that will become available soon, too.

There is a small disclaimer: it looks like anyone who published through Kindle Direct Publishing now has the option to enroll in this program, but their stories will be AI narration if there's no proper audiobook available. That is not the case for my books. You should receive the exact same audio files you'd get purchasing it off Audible (or borrowing it through their All You Can Listen program in the case of Duskbound 1), but this is a fair warning for how the program works for other authors who don't have eligible audiobooks but who enroll in the program anyway.

Happy listening!
