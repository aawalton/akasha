Hello all!

I'm really excited today to announce the release of Prismatic Spiral! This is a spin off story of Blood Curse Academia taking place across the eastern sea on an archipelago caught in a timeloop and trapped under a dome. While it takes place a few years in the future, there are no spoilers for Blood Curse Academia. You might see a few familiar faces pop up, but none of our main cast. The main character is Mitsuko! Yes! The villager from book 3 that temporarily joined with Kizu and co! This might come as a surprise for a few of you, but there's a lot to dive into with her that I'm excited to explore and share.

This story is my love-letter to Majora's Mask and Windwaker. Two stories that had a profound impact on me growing up. You'll likely see their influences very clearly even in the first 10 chapters I just released today. It also has a side of Under the Dome and Mother of Learning influencing it.

Don't worry about this slowing down the releases of Blood Curse Academia. I currently have all the way to the end of book 4 of BCA written and I plan to start writing book 5, Tournament, in January. If all goes well, Blood Curse Academia will be completed in 2026 with 6 books total.

Also, as a bonus, every 100 followers that Prismatic Spiral receives, I will drop a bonus chapter on Blood Curse Academia (up to 500 followers). That's five bonus chapters heading your way here!

I'm extremely excited about the release of this story. I hope you all enjoy it as we explore a different side of Blood Curse Academia's world.

Thank you all for reading. I'll be releasing the next chapter of Blood Curse Academia in just a bit as well. (And probably some bonus chapters when I wake up)
