Deep within a locked room in a private laboratory within the Starhold University, a man lay chained to a bed. Needles and tubes stuck out of every inch of his skin, and even his eyes weren’t spared.

A team of alchemists moved around him in thick white suits, with full body protection. The temperature of the room was well below freezing. They prepared one concoction after another, diluting substances and readying them for delivery into whatever part of the man’s body they were destined for.

Thick tubes were set to siphon the blood out of the man’s body, upon which it would be imbued with an extremely dense nutrient concentrate and chilled to a little above 0°C before being injected back in.

Freddy lay there, patiently, waiting for the treatment to finally begin.

An immense flood of stolen life force coursed through his veins, courtesy of Bloodshed, who was currently busy slowly spreading poison mist through a C-class rainforest realm. It had been a bit of pain to prepare for that, given the danger in the local interspace, but nothing he couldn’t handle with his abilities back.

There had been some risk of the kill squad showing up when he was there all by himself, but if anything, he was disappointed they hadn’t. After all, what better way for them to appear than exactly as he was about to do an extremely beefed up Blood Sacrifice.

The influx of healing would persist for about a day, until Bloodshed ran out of targets to replenish its blood energy from. The treatment would be done long before that happened.

It was always strange to feel such an intense burst of lifesteal. Sure, it was only 1%, but 1% or what? The slow death of a rainforest’s worth of biomass was inevitably going to deliver a crap ton of life force.

“We are ready to begin,” Henry said, motioning for the others to begin.

Freddy felt a sudden drain on his blood reserves from several different points on his body, and then, a moment later, its return in the form of a near-freezing fluid with enough nutrients to feed an entire zoo for a year.

His body temperature rapidly plummeted, but that wouldn’t last long.

He would be administered a little shy of 2000 different substances, and in considerable volume. The sheer number of interactions and chemical reactions that would take place inside his body was off the charts. With the flood of lifesteal, anything negative would be nullified, while anything positive would stick around.

This was basically as close as anyone could get to cheating physique progress.

But, naturally, as with any other kind of shortcut, there was a catch.

A fire flared up within his chest, slowly spreading across every cell in his body. It was as if an army of fire ants was chewing on every nerve in his body, and even with his tolerance, if he didn’t have a forearm-thick bundle of tubes inside his mouth, he’d be screaming.

The fire wasn’t just coming from the sensation of countless nerves going off.

It was also literal.

With the sheer volume of exothermic reactions taking place inside his body, if it weren’t for the chilled blood, he would quite literally burst into flame within less than a minute. On the plus side, it would make for a great workout for his fire resistance earrings.

Naturally, the ring was also there, waiting on his finger, greedily sucking up the ambient aura that would no doubt be overflowing with what he was about to do.

The hours dragged on, and before long, he got used to the pain. It was bad, but he’d felt worse. Besides, there was only so much feedback his nerves could deliver before his brain concluded that it was better to ignore them.

He felt his body slowly shifting, his skin being stretched taut as his muscles grew almost fast enough to see with the naked eye. Like a big balloon getting slowly inflated, he grew in size, the fire only getting worse as time passed. With the volume of his body going up, cooling it became slower. Thankfully, his fire resistance earrings had already grown in power a bit, and were keeping him away from becoming barbecue.

Approximately fifteen hours in, the results started to diminish. The changes in his body had slowed down to a crawl, and after perhaps another hour or two, it would be over.

“I believe that concludes it,” Henry declared, raising a hand. “Please wait for us to—”

Freddy raised his arm, tearing through the chains like they were paper mache. He reached for the thick tubes sticking out of his mouth and yanked them out. He slowly pulled everything else out—the very moment he extracted any tube or needle, the wound that should have been there sealed as if it had never even existed.

While the lifesteal had slowed down a bit, it was still going strong enough to nullify such small wounds in an instant.

The alchemists watched with barely concealed awe as he got up, his hulking form entirely transformed from the harmless pretty boy he had been just a day prior. In response to some substance, his hair had grown long enough to cover the floor in a thick, black carpet.

His wavy locks draped over his nude body like a curtain.

His muscles bulged. He was a bit bulkier than he’d been during the defense of Repentawa, but it wouldn’t be a problem. The substances hadn’t targeted just his strength.

Taking a deep breath, he felt the immense difference. From how his lungs absorbed oxygen, to the way he perceived smells and temperature. His eyes, ears, and the surface of his skin exploded with feedback.

He stretched his muscles, feeling the way his joints and muscle tissue folded out of the way, more flexible than anything he’d experienced before.

Spreading his fingers out, he slammed his fists closed, making a loud clapping noise.

He released a satisfied groan, his voice coming out a touch deeper than before.

Freddy could see in the alchemist’s gazes that it had finally sunk in for them: who he was—what he was.

For his part, Freddy finally felt like himself again. Being all slim and pretty hadn’t necessarily bothered him, but he couldn’t deny that it had impacted his confidence. Now, that impact was gone, and he stood there, not a single qualm about who was looking.

For their own sake, he hoped nobody made any jokes about his looks. Frankly, he wouldn’t even mind getting banned from the university. Hell, he’d fight a Lord if he saw one.

Okay, there, slow down, now, Fredster…

“Are you feeling alright, Mr. Stern?” Kale asked, taking a cautious step closer. “Any adverse effects?”

“Big rush of euphoria,” he noted. “I should probably stay here for another day, because I feel like I’ll likely get into a fight if I try leaving in this state.”

Kale and Henry nodded in unison.

“As expected, then,” Henry said. “Very well. We will monitor you for another day. Let’s move to the waiting room.”

Freddy spent the next day restlessly pacing and snacking on food pills. Even with supreme healing, not every side effect could be avoided. The psychological impact was the greatest, but he felt persistent hunger, unbearable itchiness, and he kept reflexively swatting at the tiniest noises, even reacting to smells.

At one point, he nearly slapped Kale’s head off because the man had coughed.

Thankfully, his body quickly got used to its new baseline. After getting some sleep, he was back in balance, and free to go.

The walk out of the university went about as expected. Thankfully, he had refrained from killing or seriously injuring anyone, even if there had been plenty of volunteers. Several passersby made comments about his altered physique, and as expected, everyone presumed it was merely cosmetic and not functional.

With all the willpower he could muster, he ignored them.

And then went straight to a dungeon.

He had scheduled a slot ahead of time, one for a rather tough dungeon appropriate for an average party of low-level three-stars. It was a kind of hellish city, with several chambers of red-skinned demon-things who may or may not be an existing alien species, but they could just as well be made-up by the dungeon.

Regardless, they were no trouble.

After effortlessly slaughtering hundreds of them, he made it to the final chamber. There, an arrogant-looking fella sat on a throne, nearly two heads taller than Freddy, and a whole lot bulkier.

No words were exchanged as the monster grabbed a giant sword and rushed at Freddy.

He met the sword with a casual swing of Sanguine Avenger, and then, in the opening following that, delivered a punch to the monster’s stomach. A double-starred Tsunami Strike blew Freddy’s hand up, and the healing from the damage delivered patched it right back up as if it had never happened.

The monster flew to the other end of the room at the speed of an arrow, a massive, bloody hole torn through its stomach, the impact against the wall shattering bone. It slid down, and the instance dungeon ended, a portal for the exit appearing beside the throne.

Freddy was awarded a cool non-unique cursed sword for his efforts, but really, that wasn’t the actual reward.

He left the dungeon with a wide grin on his face, the feeling of power infusing every inch of his being.

Taking a dip into his ethercosm, he floated over to Bloodshed. “And?” he asked. “Did you figure it out?”

Bloodshed nodded. “The ring went up to +17%, and the earrings went up to 12%.”

The smile threatened to split Freddy’s soul projection in half.

The ring had gone from a mere +2% Physique Boost to 17%. That was an insane difference, and it only went to show just how extreme an effect the treatment had had. The Sunjade Earrings had only gone up another 5%, but for what was basically just an unintended consequence, that was more than good enough. Not to worry, though—he would be setting himself on fire soon enough.

The ring’s growth was utterly insane, however. Just this much had already taken its cost up to the highest echelons of what could be publicly purchased. An increase of this scale for everything regarding his physique was simply remarkable. This was a boost on the level of a minor talent. His strength, speed, durability, stamina, reaction time—when all of that was increased, he didn’t get just 17% more powerful.

At that point, he was pretty much entirely certain that, not counting those with a talent for it, he was likely the most powerful two-star on New Earth regarding physique alone. And frankly, he probably wasn’t too far off from being the most powerful two-star overall.

“And to think I’ll soon be going up to the third star…”

The mere thought was intoxicating.

There were only another two weeks of waiting until the fated day arrived.

And until then, he had a few things to take care of.

First, he located another suitable passage realm to unleash Bloodshed on. And then, as promised, he set himself on fire.

Using potent alchemical substances, he repeatedly burnt most of his body to a crisp until the earrings stopped growing in potency. They plateaued at 35% Fire Resistance. That was a hilariously overinflated value, and Freddy was almost tempted to try selling them just to see what kind of chaos he’d create.

With this much added fire resistance, any fire arch who got their hands on them could deliver a whole lot more power. Just because one had the fire affinity, it didn’t mean they were immune to fire by default. Building proper fire resistance was hard, pretty much on the level of Hundred Wet Hells.

With these earrings, any run-of-the-mill fire arch could train up Fire Resistance, and then develop abilities of significantly greater power, without having to fear burning their hands and feet to a crisp if they turned up the heat too far. And the best part was that even if they lost the earrings, they could keep most of the power increase.

Basically, it was a tool that could help an entire faction of fire archs reach a new level of power.

As for Freddy? Well, he had just that much less of a reason to fear getting blasted by a fireball. It would still suck, but a lot less. It had gone from a critical weakness to just a weakness. That was progress.

With that out of the way, he only had two more things left to settle.

Given he was going up to the third star, he would no longer be able to participate in the two-star category for performance matches. If he was planning on doing something about his Inquisitor persona, now was the time.

But before that, he had something he’d been putting off for a long time.

He had to go have a conversation with Vladimir.
