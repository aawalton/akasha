Me too, he almost added, but wisely held his tongue. Is this world riddled with portals or something? There’s people getting isekai’d left, right, and center. Man, I swear, I talk about my world less than this guy, and I’m actually from another world…and I talk about my world a lot, so that’s a high bar.
