I read the message to the others at breakfast, chuckling gleefully at the thought of the random event bonuses we would get. The Nine-Star Alignment event had just started and would continue for another sixty-four days. Apart from being an auspicious event that would boost the chances of multiple births in farm animals and double harvests of animal products, there was also a bonus growth effect and a possible quality upgrade.

[Astral Growth Surge:

Trees grow 20% faster. Fruit and Nut trees 20% harvest increase.]

[The Blessing of the Auspicious Guest Star:

10% chance for crops to become "Star-Touched" variants that sell for triple the price.]

“Mom, a star will come and touch the plants?” Eggy was excited at the thought.

“The star’s light will touch them. I don’t think a star will fall,” I said.

“I’ve heard of guest stars,” said Prince Baiyu. “They’re said to appear and disappear unpredictably and portend great changes, both good and bad.”

I thought it could be because of my parents’ impending resurrection, but still, I explained it as a natural phenomenon of a star’s life cycle when it explodes. “The light from the explosion will reach our night sky soon, and it could even be visible during the day.”

“Stars explode?” Mo was perplexed by the idea. “Aren’t stars celestial officials of the heavenly court?”

“I don’t know about the heavenly court,” I said.

“Maybe the guest star is the birth of a new heavenly official,” said Kharli. “Teacher is calling people up from the underworld. Maybe the celestial gods are also calling people up to the sky.”

The concept of supernovas seemed more believable to me, but who was I to say they were wrong? This world had gods. Perhaps the “guest star” really was the birth of a new celestial god. Either way, this event was beneficial to the farm, and that was what mattered to me. They speculated about it a bit more, but talk soon turned to the Deer clan’s festival.

My apprentices had grown again, and it was time for them to get new clothes.

“Yes, Fengying sent me the revised budget. You guys can pick your own fabrics and designs this year and order what you like for next year.”

Unlike on Earth, where people mainly bought clothes ready-made, here we needed to buy cloth which was then embroidered and sewn into custom clothing. Prince Baiyu’s family made most of their money from the silk industry, and he had an encyclopedic knowledge of everything related to making garments. The highest-end stuff needed to be ordered years in advance since there was a great demand for them, and certain items, such as a fully embroidered set of clothes, would take an expert at least two years to make.

“Our prince needs to be dressed in the best cloud brocade,” said Kharli.

“What, this little rascal?” I pinched Eggy’s cheek. “But shouldn’t kids wear comfortable clothes?”

“I have some singing silk cloth that would be suitable for him.” Prince Baiyu patted Eggy’s head. “Or maybe he can use some of the clothes I wore when I was a child.”

Eggy giggled. “I want Dad clothes!”

Lari, Kharli, and Mo would use the materials we already had and buy some new fabric at Anwei, the nearest city, today after we finished our work. Prince Baiyu handed them the new Emberstone Farm jade tablets that arrived today. They had their names carved on them and functioned as a form of identification at the shops in the city, kind of like an ancient form of credit card. The bills would then be sent to the farm.

“I want to finish quickly!” said Lari. “The shops in Anwei are calling my name.”

We all laughed. After the meal, Prince Baiyu left to work in his office, and the rest of us went south of the river.

“Come, baby, Mommy will show you her marvelous machine.” I took Eggy by the hand, and then a large spell formation appeared in the corner of the field we were going to use. It flashed twice and disappeared, leaving behind a [Combine] in its place.

[Combine:

A heavy-duty farming machine used to efficiently plow the soil, sow seeds, and harvest crops, dramatically speeding up your work. Just remember to keep its fuel tank topped off to ensure it keeps running smoothly.]

“Machine?” Eggy looked up at it in awe.

“Yes, baby. This is our machine!”

Lari, Kharli, and Mo stood at one side of the field, giving it plenty of berth.

“Since you guys are in a rush, I’ll use the [Combine] to plow, and you can do the fertilizing, sowing, and watering,” I said to my apprentices.

The [Combine] was basically a small [Tractor] with different attachments. This time, the plow was attached to it. A boxy red engine compartment sat atop giant black wheels, and there was nothing else like it in this world.

The driver’s seat was big enough for two. I climbed up on it and patted the space beside me. “Do you want to sit with me?”

“Yes!” Eggy joined me and looked around curiously at the steering wheel and dashboard, fascinated by the levers, dials, buttons, and glowing lights. He pointed at everything and said, “What’s this? What’s this? What’s this? What’s this?”

“Later, baby. We’ll work first, then we’ll play. Brace yourself, the engine will be noisy.”

Though there was a steering wheel and everything, as with all System vehicles, they were only for show. The real controls were in the System. I checked the fuel gauge to confirm that I had enough left to run it, and then selected the appropriate tiles I wanted to plow on the System map before mentally clicking on the [Start] button in the System menu.

With a loud metallic groaning sound, the [Combine] started to lurch forward, digging deeply into the earth and turning the soil as it moved through the field. Its faint vibrations shook my body, but not in an unpleasant way. At first, Eggy was alarmed at the engine sound and covered his ears with his hand, but after seeing that nothing bad happened, he clapped his hands and cheered it on. “Machine! Go!”

Though the [Combine] moved slowly, it didn’t take long before we finished plowing. I let my apprentices do the rest of the work planting [Tea], and kept an eye on them while Eggy played with the dashboard as I explained what the gear levers, steering wheel, buttons, etc. did.

When my apprentices were done, Eggy was still playing with the steering wheel and making engine noises with his mouth, but I hopped off the vehicle and told the others of my plan.

I had some energy left, and all the talk of clothes earlier had given me an idea. The crops we planted were all for eating or drinking, since they were the most basic kind, but [Cotton] and [Flax] seeds were also available.

“I think we can grow those plants and sell the seeds to the White Tiger clan.”

“Only to the White Tiger clan?” asked Kharli in surprise.

“Well, they make their money from fabrics. I don’t think my in-laws would want me to create a competing industry. Our spirit plants will undoubtedly produce some sort of spiritual fiber,” I said.

“Teacher, I want to make cotton and linen clothes for ourselves.” Kharli’s eyes sparkled. “Our young master will look so cute!”

I smiled. “Do you guys have some energy left?”

They did, though not a lot, so we decided to plant a small amount of [Cotton] and [Flax] as I had suggested. I called for Eggy, who reluctantly left the [Combine].

We walked a little to the east, where there was a small clearing.

“This is the life,” I said as I inhaled the flower-scented air deeply. “Sweetie, look at this tree. You were born in the winter and only saw the frozen ground and snow, but now spring has come.”

“Spring has come,” Eggy repeated.

I pointed at the tree in the middle of the clearing. “The trees’ dead, bare branches are all budding with new growth. All the plants and trees that were sleeping in winter are waking up!”

Eggy turned into his dragon form and flew up to a high branch, poking at the new leaves. I let him be and gestured for the others to start planting.

I took out my [Hoe] and plowed ten tiles, fertilized them with [Mysterious Goo], and then sowed five [Cotton] and five [Flax] seeds. Eggy flew back down from the tree, turned into his human form, and helped me water them with the [Pink Bunny Watering Can] from the Cash Shop that I gave him.

[Pink Bunny Watering Can:

A cute pink mini watering can shaped like an adorable bunny.]

“Seedies, you should grow biiiiig like me,” he said to the ground, a serious expression on his face. “Be good seeds.”

And just like that, we finished all of today’s tasks. The kids rushed off to shower and change for their trip to the city, and Eggy and I went to relax in the hot spring.

When we had planted enough [Tea], we moved on to growing vegetables for our own use. We would sell many of the seeds, of course, but they were primarily for the Demon Chef to cook his delicious dishes that we all loved to eat. Food made of spiritual plants boosted health, and those prepared by a top-tier cultivator who knew how to enhance its properties, like Deming, were even more effective. Thus, even though there was greater demand for [Tea] and [Rice], we always planted all of the other kinds that we liked to eat.

“Like the veggies you’re growing in the [Greenhouse],” I said to Eggy.

“I’m making carrots for Bunny Mommy.”

“Hats!” I said.

In perfect synchronization, we all put our Emberstone Farm-branded farming headgear on.

“Come with me, young master. I will teach you the secret of [The Three Sisters],” said Kharli.

[Companion Planting: The Three Sisters

The Three Sisters is a Native American method of planting corn, beans, and squash together in the same mound. The corn grows tall and provides support for the beans to climb. The squash spreads along the ground, providing ground cover and preventing weeds from growing. This method of planting results in a bountiful harvest of all three crops.]

She used the System to make a mound of soil and fertilized it before showing him how to plant the seeds, with the [Corn] in the center, then the [Beans], and the [Squash] on the outer edge.

Using the System, the seeds were all planted at the same time, but for normal farming, they should be planted sequentially from the center outwards, waiting for each crop to grow before planting the next.

“These are siblings who help each other grow,” said Kharli.

Eggy used a stick to drill holes that he filled with the seeds. “Like Riri, Lily, and Momomomomomomomo?”

Mo laughed and pinched his cheek. “Yes, that’s right, Egigigigigigigiggigy!”

“The last step is to water the plants,” I said, handing him his favorite [Pink Bunny Watering Can].

After Eggy finished watering the mound, he stayed in that section of the field helping Kharli while Lari, Mo, and I spread out to work on our parts.

I planted [Tomatoes], [Carrots], [Onions], [Peppers], [Lettuce], and [Radishes] together on the northwestern section, and [Peas], [Pumpkins], [Beets], [Spinach], and [Parsnips] in the southwest. Then due west, I planted a special crop, [Glass Gem Corn].

[Glass Gem Corn:

A decorative variety of corn that has multicolored translucent gem-like kernels. Ancient farmers used to call this “Dragon’s Teeth,” and it was reserved only for the kings’ and queens’ tables. Legend tells of a poor farmer who stole an ear of corn from the queen of the Fair Isle. When she found out, he was forced to labor in her fields for ten thousand years. They say he is still there. Also called “Forbidden Corn.”]

This variety of [Corn] yielded less than regular [Corn] at harvest time, hence I had never planted it, but I used it this year since I thought Eggy would like to see it.

I kept an eye on my exp and called the others over just in time to see the System fireworks explode all around me.

[Congratulations, you just advanced a Farming level!]

[Your Farming level is now 64.]

“Awesome! Group hug?”

They all smiled and hugged me.

“Congratulations!” they all said.

We went back to work, and soon we were finished.

After showering and changing, we met at the second courtyard, where they showed me the results of their shopping spree in Anwei. There were the usual silk and cotton bolts of fabric, thread, ribbons, etc., but I was taken aback when one of the maids triumphantly showed me something amazing.

“Princess, would you like some dragon’s blood?” she asked me. “I also have a vial of dragon’s tears.”
