My first lesson in keeping a dog and a catgirl at the same time was that they probably need some time to get to know each other. I learned this at the cost of moderate damage to my tables, cabinetry, and flooring as the two of them snarled, barked, clawed, and scratched their way around the place in a whirlwind of chaos.

Using dog treats, coercion, force, and whatever means necessary I finally managed to separate the two and get them to sit, peacefully, across from one another. I was breathless and rather scratched up at that point. Max was chewing on his treat and Frannie was enjoying a shiny hairpin that Leyanna had forgotten.

“Ok you two,” I said when I had regained my breath and some measure of calm, looking between them. “We are not doing this again.”

Max whined and looked up at me, waggling his eyebrows. Frannie meowed.

“Frannie,” I said, “I want to give Max a hug…” and Max, I wagged my finger at the retriever. “You be a good boy and let her, okay?”

I took his silence for agreement and Frannie, understanding the instructions, cautiously crawled over and hugged Max around his neck. For a second I expected another round of fighting but then I heard the rhythmic thump-thump of Max’s tail and knew he was enjoying it. Frannie nuzzled against his head and meowed happily.

I sighed, mentally exhausted. “Okay good. Now don’t forget this moment, because next time you both cause me that kind of chaos I’m going to kick both of you out for a week and enjoy my life as a single, petless, bachelor once again.”

Max whined and Frannie mewled, both looking up at me with wide eyes.
