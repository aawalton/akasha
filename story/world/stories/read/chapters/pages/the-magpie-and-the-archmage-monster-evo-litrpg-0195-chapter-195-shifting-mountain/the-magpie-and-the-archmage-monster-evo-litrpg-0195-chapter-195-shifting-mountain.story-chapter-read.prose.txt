"We haven't even gone up the mountain yet!" Liora complained.

"I suspect we'll be seeing a lot of corrupted creatures. Liora, fly up!" Renir called out.

Liora didn't need to ask him to know what he wanted. She flew high and then dropped, using her Meteor Strike. But not just Meteor Strike. She combined it with Purify.

[You've successfully formed the skill <Purifying Meteor>.]

She hoped that this would be enough to deal with the beast. It looked massive and powerful, and being corrupted meant that it had powered up even more. They had to deal with it before the creature managed to get its bearings.

It was difficult to choose a proper place to strike because of the dark rocks protruding from its back and the massive horn on its nose. But there was a small unprotected spot on its neck. Liora aimed for that with her beak.

CRASH.

Liora struck the rampaging beast, who was too oblivious to pay any attention to her. It had been hoping to maul poor Caspian to pieces, poor merman looking as pale as a sheet from fear.

Her aim was true, and the monster screamed as Liora's attack dealt severe damage, destroying several of the corrupted crystals on its back and leaving a horrible wound in its neck. But it wasn't enough. This thing was too damn tough. Liora was about to strike with the Purifying Lunge when suddenly a beam of light shot out from Renir.

It went straight through the corrupted monster's skull.

[You've assisted in defeating <*&%%$ER^^(*&^^>. Experience gained.]

The black mass of the monster converged into a single blightstone, which Renir quickly doused in bright, purifying light, turning it into a beautiful glimmerstone.

Liora blinked in surprise. She knew that Renir would have unlocked a bunch of his magic with two seals removed in quick succession. But she hadn't actually expected him to be… useful.

"Wow… You can actually do stuff now!" Cee said, mimicking Liora's thoughts.

"Of course, I can! I can use more of my magic now. Although it is still severely limited," Renir said, opening and closing his rocky fist. "I need to be careful and conserve my mana when possible."

Liora landed and absentmindedly grabbed the glimmerstone, putting it into her inventory. "How much can you do now?"

"Hmm… that's a good question. I'm afraid I won't be able to tell you until I've tested it out. Large, mana-intensive spells are out of the question. Any higher forms of magic, like teleportation, are too. Barriers and protective magic, I can create but likely not for too long."

"What does it matter if external magic doesn't work in the mountains?" Caspian pointed out.

"Will your healing spells work?" Cee asked nervously.

"Yeah, good question. What's the point of us dragging you along if you can't even heal us?" Liora asked.

"Some healing spells might not work, but I believe my songs won't be affected," Caspian said.

"He is right," Renir nodded. "Sound-based magic isn't affected by the mountain, but it technically doesn't use external mana. Mana is already being imbued with your voice and carried by sound waves—"

There he goes again.

Liora rolled her eyes. Renir and Caspian both went into a detailed lecture on how sound-based magic worked, which she promptly ignored. Instead she focused on getting the saddle right. She had to withdraw it from her inventory in the exact right spot on her back. It took her a few tries to get it in the right position.

Once it was in place, she settled the still yapping Renir in the saddle with the help of Cee, who appeared to be listening to them, albeit not too closely. Smokey yawned, already getting sleepy. But they had to press on, up the mountain.

Ideally, they could find a base of operations, so to speak. They needed a safe place to rest in the mountains while they figured out exactly what was going on. Ideally, they could solve the issue within a few days, but Liora doubted it would be so easy. Especially with how cautious Renir sounded.

If only all of them could fly. Then this whole mission could be a lot easier.

But for now, they had to continue the best way they could with one annoying mage yapping on Liora's back, and an equally annoying merman yapping in her talons.

"I need a vacation from all this…" Liora sighed.

"You already had one, just recently!" Cee protested, sitting atop Smokey as they took off to the air.

"Yes, but I had to deal with Aenir, who is even worse than Renir in some regards," Liora grumbled.

"At least he's handsome," Cee shrugged.

"Eh, not my type."

"What is your type?"

All of a sudden, both men went quiet, listening intently to Liora and Cee. The absence of their rambling became instantly clear.

Liora glanced at the merman, then at Renir on her back. "Are you two done discussing the intricacies of sound-based magic?"

Renir did a noise similar to a cough, although it sounded more like a rumble with his new deep voice. "Y-Yes. It appears we got a bit sidetracked. Do continue your conversation."

"This is girl talk. Boys aren't allowed to listen!" Cee declared.

"Girl talk? You're a spider and a bird," Caspian shot back.

"We are still girls at heart! Aren't we, Liora?" Cee chimed.

"Most definitely. Most beautiful girls out there," Liora agreed.

"D-Do you remember what you looked like?" Renir asked cautiously.

Liora and Cee shared a glance. The spider rubbed thoughtfully at her mandibles, seeming deep in thought. "I… I can't remember."

Liora thought about it too. She could remember the flashes of her memory. She remembered living in a small studio apartment. It had been an incredibly noisy place, located not too far from a local bar. Sleepless nights were a common occurrence.

But her appearance… She tried to remember looking at herself in the mirror. Was she fat? Was she thin? Did she have long hair? Short? Black or blonde? She couldn't remember any of it. All she got was a headache.

"I can't either…" Liora muttered.

Renir nodded. "It doesn't really matter. If you were to gain a human form, then you would likely look completely different."

"Who said anything about me wanting a human form?" Liora grumbled. "I never said I want to become human again. Or even humanoid."

"I could have been an arachne already, but the divine evolution was just too tempting!" Cee wailed. "Next evolution, I'll gain at least partial humanoid form. For sure. Liora, you should too."

"If it's a weird harpy thing like before, then hell no. I would rather become a fully sized dragon."

"You might want to consider a more humanoid evolution. At higher evolution tiers, your evolution will start influencing your personality. Especially if you choose a truly powerful race like a dragon. You might think they are cool, but dragons can be ruthless. I don't think that ruthlessness would suit you."

"Since when did you care if I took anything that wasn't the most magical option available?" Liora pointed out.

"Since… I'm just thinking of your best interests," Renir said. It felt like he had wanted to say something else, but it was unlikely that she would be able to coax the truth out of him. Not with others listening. Maybe she should ask him when they were alone.

For the time being, they continued up the mountain, following the flow of the corrupted river.

A few corrupted creatures appeared, but none quite as big or as powerful as that corrupted rhino thing. Renir shot them with a Holy Light before anyone even managed to react. Liora would purify the blightstones and add the resulting glimmerstones to her collection.

But the closer they got to the mountain, the weirder things became. The corrupted river looked like it was being isolated, craggy, almost impenetrable. Some herd of purple mountain goats with strange glowing horns were trying to get past the crags.

At that moment, it felt like the spikes along the crags grew even larger, as if trying to force the goats away.

"Did that spike just move?" Cee asked.

"I thought I was imagining that!" Liora called out.

"Oh, you haven't seen anything yet. It's about to get much weirder," Renir warned.

Liora had no clue how it could get weirder than a part of a spike moving on its own. But the higher they flew, the more of such movements she saw. A rock shifting there. A crag opening over there.

Not to mention the overall scenery growing increasingly… bizarre. Regular rocks got replaced with increasingly sparkling ones. The shrubs and greenery also got increasingly alien and sparkly. Colors seemed to shift into strange, unnatural alternatives with pink tree leaves and purple grass.

The corrupted river was barely visible. It seemed like the mountain was trying to restrict animals' access to it. As if it was trying to protect everything from its filth.

But Liora wasn't even thinking about the corruption anymore. Her Treasure Sense was going haywire. Everywhere she looked, it felt like there was treasure. Every pebble, every leaf, every stick seemed valuable.

"Renir, what is going on with this mountain? Is there a mountain of treasure buried underneath?" Liora asked.

"Not quite. I think it's better to say that the mountain itself is the treasure," Renir clarified.

"What does that mean?" Cee asked.

"I think you all have noticed by now how the scenery shifts here all the time. It's because the Titanspike Mountains, or rather, at their very core, are a deposit of very special ore. It absorbs all of the surrounding mana; however, this ore cannot hold infinite amounts of mana. So over the years—"

" — the mana got absorbed into the surrounding rock, changing its properties!" Caspian announced triumphantly.

"Exactly! Finally! Somebody who actually understands what I'm talking about," Renir cheered.

"So the whole mountain is made up of this mana-filled rock or whatever?" Liora asked.

"Exactly. In a way, this mountain is a dungeon, except it isn't like a regular dungeon, which is completely upheld by the dungeon core. Rather, the whole mountain itself is the core, making it by far the most unusual and also the most dangerous place on the continent," Renir said.

"Wow…" Cee said.

Liora, however, still tormented by her Treasure Sense, could only feel nervous as she flew over a canopy of trees which looked to be made entirely of emerald crystal.

"So if the mountain itself is so amazing and unusual, doesn't that make the monsters here unique too?"

"Yes, and they are fiercely protective of their home… The mountain and its inhabitants will try to expel us, especially when it's already been agitated by the corruption," Renir said.

At that moment, Caspian, who was in Liora's grip, changed to his merman form.

"Sorry, I need to rest in the water again," Caspian said, looking quite tired.

"Wait, where are we supposed to find water for him to rest in? The only water I saw was that corrupted river, and we can't even get to it, because the mountain is covering any opening to it…" Cee said, worriedly looking at the merman.

If they could access the river, then Liora could have kept it cleansed for long enough for Caspian to rest there. But with the mountain leaving only faint traces, it would be impossible to get him to the river.

But the things that Renir said got Liora thinking. The mountain didn't like strangers, but what if… what if those strangers did something that the mountain appreciated? Like cleansing the river that had been corrupted. It was clear that this mountain had at least some rudimentary sentience or, at the very least, an instinct to recognize what was and what wasn't harmful.

Liora flew towards the crags that were covering the corrupted river. Only a very tiny opening remained, only large enough for a bug to fit through. But it would be enough for her Holy Light.

She shot the bright ray of holy magic straight into the hole, hoping to hit the river below. She peeked in and saw that a small part of the river did indeed get cleansed.

For a while, nothing happened, but then the mountain began to shift.

"Yes, it worked!" Liora rejoiced.

But she celebrated too quickly. Jagged spikes erupted from the craggy ground, shooting straight up at her.
