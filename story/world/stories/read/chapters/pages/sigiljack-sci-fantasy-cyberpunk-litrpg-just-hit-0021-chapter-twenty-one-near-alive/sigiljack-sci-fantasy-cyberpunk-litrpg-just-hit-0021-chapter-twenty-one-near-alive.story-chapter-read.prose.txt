The waypoint pulsed like a heartbeat in his HUD, dragging John deeper into the guts of the Undercity.

The space had fully shifted now. Sewer stink giving way to something more oil and steam laden.

Nothing but maintenance corridors now, narrower but cleaner. And yet still crooked, like someone bent the place into its shape and hoped no one would notice.

The more time John spent in it, the more he thought the Undercity really wasn’t anything like a sector of its own. It was more like a crazy-god's scrapbook of civilization’s leftovers, all stitched together with rust and mana and ideas that hadn’t aged well.

He walked slow. Deliberate.

And breathed in. Things were calm for now and he intended to take advantage of that to get back to full strength.

Athena sensed his intentions to replace his lost ki without him needing to say, or think, a word to her.

[Skill Activated: Focused Breath Lv. 1][Skill-Energy Remaining: 3.][Mana-Pool Remaining: 4.][Ki Points Gained: +1 (3/4).]

Ki was a powerful resource, but one that didn't just generate on its own--unlike its individual components.

He repeated the process of activating [focused breath] once more, until his Ki stores were back to being full.

And just in time. He stopped walking.

Something metal.
