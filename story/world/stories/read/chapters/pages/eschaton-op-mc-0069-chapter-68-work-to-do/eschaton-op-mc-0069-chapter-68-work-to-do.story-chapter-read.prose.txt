Before Sergeant Callisto can pull herself off Richardson, the Stryker rolls back onto its wheels as Mach pulls it up.

“Callisto! Are you alright in there?“ Mach calls over the radio, clearly concerned.

“Ayup,” she spits out, slamming back into her seat. Richardson immediately starts working on treating Sandra’s badly broken nose. “We’re good, keep fighting, don’t worry about us!”

A wasp dives for Stryker 2, containing Thor and his squad, and Mach punches it. It explodes into a shower of gore with a pop, covering Stryker 2 with the sticky yellow innards of the wasps. As the bulk of the swarm closes in, Mach begins to do something Callisto can only describe as dance.

He almost slides around the battlefield, smooth steps followed by quick slashes. It looks almost choreographed, like an action scene from a movie. Wild strikes, seemingly aimed at nothing, carve into wasps that dive into them.

“Holy shit, Sarge. You weren’t kidding about Mach, were you?” Thomas marvels.

A few wasps jolt back on buzzing wings, their near-suicidal rage broken by Mach’s relentless assault. He throws his huge sword, and it spears through three of them. The wasp shish-kebab sails off into the flower forest. Seeing him without a weapon emboldens the monsters, and they swoop down.

He grabs the first one and rips it in half. He uses the pieces as projectiles, knocking down two more, one of them right behind Matterson’s Bradley.

“Tom! Back up, run it over!”

The heavy IFV slams into full reverse, and backs up over the stunned and twitching bug. It goes under the treads with a screech and is silenced with a squelch.

Mach’s sword reforms in his hand, and swipes up like a golfer, slicing through the ground and remaining stunned wasp effortlessly. He throws his sword again at a wasp diving for the Bradley, and its corpse goes sailing off into the forest just like its compatriots.

One of the last few wasps dives for Mach from behind him. He whips around and backhands it. The wasp’s head explodes in a fan of viscera, and Mach dodges the uncontrolled body as it slams into the ground. The Strykers blast the last ones out of the sky.

“Well, that looks like all of them,” he says, looking around.”I don’t hear or see anymore. Is everyone all right?“

“Alright, everyone sound off if you’re not dead,“ Lieutenant Matterson says over the radio.

“Stryker 2, all good.

“Stryker 1, one minor injury. Sandra broke her nose when the vic rolled. Doc’s looking at her now. She’ll be fine.” Callisto says over the radio.

“Alright, good to hear,” Matterson says. “Let’s get some distance from here and then we’ll do some science or whatever. Vehicles, move out.”

The convoy doesn’t go too far from the failed ambush site, but at the very least they’re upwind of the many, many pulped insects. The remains caked on the three vehicles and Machina himself are already horrific enough.

Eric is the first one out of the Bradley, and he gags at the smell.

“Oh, God, that’s awful, what the fuck.”

Callisto has to clench her teeth and fights to keep her breakfast down. She succeeds, but barely.

“Get to work everyone! Come on, come on, I want the full suite of samples done real quick. Jericho, crack open the sample case. Take a bit of the bug guts.”

“I can take care of it, Sergeant, the smell doesn’t bother me.” Mach rumbles.

“No, Mach, we’ve got it. You fight monsters, we clean up and do science shit. This is our job, you do yours.”

Jericho pulls a cell from a crate full of dozens of empty ones. Mist pours out and onto the ground as the dry ice meant to keep it cool vaporizes. He looks a little green as he scoops sticky globs of bug guts off Stryker 2.

“Oh, God,” he groans. He barely screws on the lid before doubling over and hurling into the grass. “That is the worst thing I have ever smelled,” he says after recovering.

“Mach, is that shit toxic?” Lt. Matterson asks.”Fuck, dude, I can smell that shit through the goddamn hatches.”

“I don’t think so?” Mach replies.

“You don’t sound sure.”

“I’m not a biologist, Tom. I can tell you none of the chemicals I recognize are poisonous or toxic, at least in a mundane sense.”

“Well, shit, I guess that’s gonna have to be good enough. Second Squad, suck it up. We still got work to do.”

Even the normally stoic Thor looks a little unsteady, but Second Squad pulls it together under the aggressively determined Sergeant Callisto. They rip up more soil samples, roll up fallen petals, and even chop down a flower. Machina spirals out from the convoy, on the hunt for monsters.

The driver for Stryker 1, a young woman by the name of Torrez, clambers out of the driver’s hatch. She thought ahead, and is wearing one of the gas masks they brought along. She takes a look at the damage done to Stryker by the wasp that attacked it and the ensuing rollover. She almost slips on viscera, but catches herself on the RWS.

“You good, Torrez?” Callisto calls out. She gets a thumbs up in response.

“Yeah, Sarge, all good.” Her voice is muffled though the gas mask.

“How’s the damage?”

“Eh, looks to be cosmetic. Mostly. The slat armor is all fucked up here on the nose, but that’s what it’s for anyway. Nose is banged up too, got a deep gouge, but no breach. We’re good to keep rolling.”

Callisto wipes her brow and sighs. “Whuf, this Fracture is hardcore. Are Cat-2s normally like this?”

“Yes and no,” Machina replies over the radio, somehow able to hear her from almost a hundred yards away. “*That’s why we’re here, focusing on science instead of resource extraction. We think we know and understand what Fractures are and how they work because we can give them a number. That’s not the case. We’re lying to ourselves about them. They do not conform to our understanding of reality in any way.”

“Then how the hell are the National Guard clearing and extracting from Fractures already?”

“Because they send in platoons at a time with heavy armor support, Empowered, and are accepting of casualties.” Lt. Matterson cuts in.

“Is it really that bad, sir?* Eric asks.”The news makes it seem like everything is a-okay. Well, beyond the rationing measures I guess.”

“Some places are doing better than others. Denver is doing pretty alright thanks to Mach here picking up the slack. The Bay Area over in Cali is getting hit hard. They have a lot of coast that’s hard to defend, and there’s some damned nasty things coming up out of the water. Same goes for most of the coastal cities to be honest.”

Second Squad quiets down, looking amongst each other. Callisto claps her hands together.

“Come on people, we got work to do! You wanna help? GET BACK TO WORK! Eric, I see you slackin’ over there, move it, come on, come on!”

Eric grumbles as he chops up a flower, the stem of it as thick as a tree’s.

“I’m workin’ on it, I’m workin’ on it.”

Sticky sap pours of the severed stem like water. It splashes onto the ground, and the dirt drinks it up. Jericho rushes to get a few cells over to his brother, and they collect a few gallons of it.

“Nice thinking, Jericho,” Callisto commends. “Remember, everything and anything weird looking you don’t recognize goes in a bag or in a can. Eggheads back at base will know what to do with it.”

After a few more minutes of tearing apart the landscape, Second Squad piles back into their vehicles.

“Okay, Mach, where to next?” Lt. Matterson asks. “See anything interesting?”

“The drone was taken out by the wasps, so I’ve lost the bird’s eye view. I did see a lake or a pond over that way before it went down.” Mach says, pointing towards arbitrary east.

“What do you think is over there?“

“*Not a clue. Saw some big lilies and cattails. You said you liked fishing, right? Might be some killer Bass in there.”

“That’s what I’m fuckin’ worried about,” Sandra mutters. “I did not sign up to get eaten by a giant fish.”

“Okay, Mach, you’re the boss. Let’s go fishing.”

The convoy piles on the speed, plowing through the grass. After several minutes of driving, they finally close in on the pond.

“Odd,” Machina comments. “Should not have taken us that long. My internal systems say we traveled nearly fourteen miles. From the drone’s mapping, it should have been less than half of that.”

“It was a cheap, expendable drone. Maybe the camera wasn’t that good?”

“Maybe, but off by that much?”

“Well, Fractures are fucky, like you said. Is it a problem right now?”

“No, this isn’t the first time I’ve encountered spatial warping. I just didn’t expect it to be so significant in a Category-2.”

“Well, alright, Second Squad, dismount. Sergeant Callisto, you know what to do.”

Second Squad piles out of their vehicles, and once again begin the process of sample collection. The instruments go down and begin whirring, humming, and making all sorts of noises related to high-tech gadgetry. Machina hacks a pass through dense cattail reeds that stretch high into the sky. They shake the earth when they hit the ground after being thrown aside by Machina.

The corndog-like tips are massive, easily as large as an 18-wheeler. Some of them fall apart in the air, and the cloud of brown seeds drifts away on the constant breeze that ruffles the grass. Second Squad rips up tuffs of seed from them, and cram them in the few remaining crates they have.

It takes him several minutes to carve a path to the side of the lake. The water is a beautiful cerulean, and it sparkles like a gemstone. The light wind barely disturbs the water, and it gently laps at the muddy bank.

“Wow,” Eric breathes. “Damn if these Fractures ain’t pretty.”

Mach rumbles, his version of a chuckle. “This one is. You should have seen one I cleared at the NAVSEA Command in the first week. That was horrific.”

As Mach tromps closer to the lakeside, his normal heavy step seems lightened, and he doesn’t sink in the mud as far as someone so heavy should. Instead there’s a bright blue glow underneath his boots.

“Hey, Mach,” Callisto asks. “What’s that blue glowy shit. Is that normal?”

He nods. “Yes. It’s part of the cloak. It allows me to stand on weaker surfaces without breaking them. I can actually go indoors now if I want. I didn’t expect it to work on mud, but I suppose when it comes to Morgan, I should expect above and beyond, hm?”

“A gift from Morgan, huh?” Jericho asks leadingly.

“Yes.” Mach replies. “I got her something too, of course.”

Jericho only gives him a shit eating grin before Castillo curses at him until he gets back to work.

“I swear to God Jericho, I will kick your ass if you don’t get it in goddamn gear!”

“Sorry about him, sir,” she says to Machina. “Your business is your own.”

“….Right,” he says awkwardly. “I’m going to check out the lake now. I’ll be back.”

He walks into the picturesque lake and quickly disappears from view.
