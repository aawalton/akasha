Twitch

This Knight elf was a hard fucking nut to crack. On top of being stronger, faster, and able to use invisible force or some shit to push someone away just when they were going to land a good hit, he had a personal magic shield thing that blocked most else. Luckily, the shield couldn't stand up against heat and now that Zach was done with his elf and had come to help out, the tide was turning.

Tiny and his crew weren't completely useless, either. Though Twitch would never be caught saying it to his face.

None of it would've meant anything if not for their enhanced Gifts. Twitch had shrugged off a dozen hits by now that should've ended him. Man, he could get used to this kind of power. Too bad it was only supposed to last a few hours.

Nevertheless, they were whittling the asshole down when Thomas, who'd been doing who-knew-what, screamed like he was dying.

The sound went through Twitch like a knife. He couldn't explain how, exactly, but all of them felt it. Even the damned elf.

The battle stopped for just a second. Twitch turned to see Thomas collapse and Courtney step casually up to the control panel.

Zach grabbed Twitch with a hand that felt stove-hot to the touch and shoved him in that direction. "Help him, we got this!"

You ain't my boss, Twitch almost spat back, except the look in Zach's eyes was terrible.

Thomas looked and sounded like he was dying, Zach and Jo were the ones with the Gifts to finally finish the elf, and Twitch... Twitch was the best one to deal with Courtney.

Fuck.

He had to move fast, too. She was punching buttons and the ring portal thing was making a humming sound. If backup came, they'd be boned.

Twitch ran over and took in the situation at a glance.

It wasn't a pretty sight. Well, the loot around Frederic's dead corpse was kinda nice but Thomas was down, writhing with green foam coming out of his mouth and veins blackening all over his body.

Courtney stood over him, pretty and unbothered as could be while punching buttons.

"What are you doing, babe?" Twitch asked.

She startled, saw it was him, then smiled. "We have to get out of here. Frederic's down, but I can report his failure to the main family. It's okay, they won't blame you. They'll keep you safe, and give you the resources to grow strong."

She'd halfway pitched this before, back in the dungeon. He'd even considered it. Why not? More power and backing of a powerful force meant no one would be able to imprison him again. Twitch had done deals with the devil to stay safe before. It wasn't pretty, but it was what it was. He and Courtney had hatched a plan to get away the second the coast was clear. No one would get hurt in the process

He stuck out his neck for her and she returned the favor by bolting out on her own. He then got a quest from the System which put it in black and white that her little family was the enemy of humankind.

And now, by the looks of it, she'd poisoned the guy who had broken Twitch out of slavery. The same guy who had saved his life before in the Grizzly dungeon. Sure, none of it had been completely selfless on Thomas's part, but he had also been the only one in longer than Twitch could easily remember who had shown him a measure of kindness.

Twitch hated to do it, but when he looked at the facts as they were, it wasn't so hard of a decision to make.

"Sorry," he said, "I'm done joining up with stupid causes."

He reached out.

Courtney's hazel eyes went hard and she shifted out of the way like her bones were made of rubber.

That was fine, he wasn't aiming for her.

His hand landed on the control panel and he sent a jolt of electric mana straight through to fry it.

Thomas had given him that skill shard, too.

Courtney shrieked in outrage and came at him with one of those rusty swords the prisoners had been carrying around. What happened to that Corruption dagger she'd been so proud of?

Behind him, Twitch heard and felt a hot flash of fire fire as the fight with the remaining elf ramped up. As for his fight... well, he didn't enjoy it nearly as much, and he made it as quick as he could. With him able to instantly heal from every wound as long as he was moving, she couldn't do much to him anyway.

Twitch turned away Courtney she died, noting the clink of loot falling from her. Thomas was still alive, then.

He looked back at the main fight just in time to see Jo come out of stealth, or whatever she called it, just as the elf was reeling from a one-two-three strike from Tiny, Zach, and Sue.

Jo's sword finally pierced that stupid magical shield thing and struck the elf with her sword right between where the armor protected neck and chin. High levels didn't mean shit when your head was half cut off.

It was done. Finally.

Welp, no rest for the wicked. Twitch knelt by Thomas. His breaths came in ragged gasps, black track marks were all over his body, still spreading like he was actively rotting from the inside. Even the tips of his fingers looked black like a drowned corpse. Um, this was really bad. How were they supposed to heal the healer?

"We got a problem here!" Twitch yelled. "Anyone got a healing crystal?"

In an instant, Zach was next to him. Must have teleported. "Shit, they got him with Corruption mana. A healing crystal won't help." He knelt down, a hand hovering over Thomas's core, then stopped, looking lost. Far as Twitch could tell, Zach had some sort of Gift with mana that went beyond fire like he claimed. Right now it was telling him nothing good.

Pursing his lips, Twitch stood and let Jo take his place. She immediately knelt next to Thomas's other side and called his name without much response. Then her tears started. "Zach, what are you waiting for? Take him to the Dark elves!"

"I... I can't. His core is completely pulped. If I moved him magically, I think he'll just fall apart. Thomas? Shit, dude. What do we do?" There was no response and he repeated in a smaller voice. "Thomas?"

Twitch backed off. Those were the guy's friends, and he'd already seen which way the wind was blowing. They should be with him in his last moments.

He angrily wiped at his own eyes, took a breath, and in an attempt to focus on anything else, looked to Antonin who stood awkwardly to the side. Twitch had seen glimpses of him here and there in the fight and was glad he had kept out of the way.

Antonin met his gaze and said, "Stabbed." He mimed a sharp gesture going into his own back.

"Yeah," Twitch said. "Figured something like that. Courtney did it?"

Antonin nodded and Twitch felt marginally less bad about ending her than he had before.

Then Antonin opened his hand and showed the handle of Courtney's dagger. Only a stub of the green blade was left. Weird little guy. Why did he pick that up? To protect himself? He supposed the stub end was still sharp.

Wait...

"Hey," Twitch snatched it away from Antonin and turned back to the others, holding it up. "Looks like Courtney broke off her little dagger in his back. She told me before this was Corruption mana."

"What?" Jo exchanged a look with Zach. Together, they helped turn Thomas onto his side. The guy had gone very still, which wasn't a good thing.

The shirt was ripped and underneath was a mass of blood and clots. Or was that necrotic flesh? The smell said yes. Twitch didn't blame Jo at all for turning away and gagging. Corruption mana was disgusting. He couldn't believe Courtney was proud to have that inside her.

Or that she had offered it to him as a source of power once this was all done, and he had seriously considered it. Yeah, he'd dodged a bullet. He had to get smarter about who he trusted.

Well, Thomas and his friends hadn't done him wrong yet.

Determination rose up and Twitch pushed down his own nausea to come and help Zach and Jo. Though they weren't doing much. She was crying and he seemed mostly stunned.

Zach had his hand over the wound and looked painfully uncertain, and suddenly young. "Yeah, something's here, like a few inches deep. We should get it out, but would it even help at this point? It's in his core. He looks so bad, dude. I..." his voice cracked, "Maybe I should get him to the elves, but if the portal to their side isn't open--"

Ignoring his babbling, Twitch firmed up his nerve and reached right in the wound with his bare fingers to fish for the blade. The wound itself was rotten, and it was about the worst thing he'd ever done in a lifetime of doing horrible things for terrible people.

Somehow, though, touching the Corruption mana blade was even worse. That was a shock down to his soul.

Twitch ripped it free and tossed the thing away like it burned him. He couldn't imagine that inside him, ugh.

Either ripping it out must have hurt enough to be a shock, or having it removed was a relief, because Thomas gasped out a breath and his eyes cracked open.

"Thomas, heal yourself," Jo urged.

"No, wait, he's gotta Purge," Zach said, resting a hand on his shoulder. "C'mon, bro. Get that crap outta you."

Thomas nodded once weakly, put his hand to his chest... and then started to convulse for a completely different reason.

Needless to say, the room cleared out quick and they reconvened in the hallway. Tiny's team was gone, fuck knew where, but Twitch was confused.

"Look, I know he's the healer, but no one said that's his Gift, right? I've seen two day old corpses that looked better. Can someone even come back from all that?"

Zach blinked rapidly and didn't answer. His gaze was on the doorway of the room they just left.

Jo shot Twitch a chiding look, then put a hand on Zach's shoulder. "If anyone can," she said, "it's Thomas."
