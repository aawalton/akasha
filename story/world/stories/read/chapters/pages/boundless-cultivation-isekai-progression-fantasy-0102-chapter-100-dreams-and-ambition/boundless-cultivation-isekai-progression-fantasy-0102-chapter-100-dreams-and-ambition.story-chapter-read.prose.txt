Inside the Cultivation Sanctuary - The Crown Chakra Island

Alaric meditated on his crown chakra, tempering his soul and preparing it for when it was ready to partially awaken. Among all the chakras, it was the most elusive to master properly.

In his original world, only a handful of chakra cultivators had been documented to partially awaken their crown chakra through willful effort. However, there existed many sages and saints throughout history who had their crown chakra bloomed since birth.

The crown chakra was associated with contradictions where logic and reason ceased to work as reliable tools. However, Alaric had already bloomed his crown chakra once, and he had grasped many fundamental truths that guided him towards his goal.

His insights matched the guidelines of his old master - the surest way to partially awaken the crown chakra was to accumulate good karma. However, it wasn’t something ordinary humans had control over.

Some higher force beyond human comprehension kept track of karma. It wasn’t possible to manipulate this higher force into generating good karma.

Thus, nobody could predict a timeline for blooming their crown chakra or even estimate how far they’ve progressed in their journey. It was the same for partially awakening the crown chakra.

There was another method to hasten the progress - to forcefully direct an immense amount of energy inside the crown chakra. But Alaric hoped he never had to face such a situation again, especially when they dictated his doom.

There is nothing I can do for now. Trying to force it open will negate my efforts. He inhaled a deep breath and slowly opened his eyes, beholding the beauty of the snow-capped mountain peaks that glistened golden under the bright rays of the artificial sun.

Alaric was grateful for his cultivation sanctuary. It allowed him to meditate while relishing the beauty of these breathtaking landscapes, all from the comfort of his home.

Until he managed to awaken his other signature ability - Lucid Inner World - this served as a nice alternative.

Of course, he had already started the appropriate training to awaken that ability. The first stage involved him having lucid dreams at will.

So he practiced remaining hyper-aware of his every waking moment and performing reality checks at regular intervals to verify whether he was currently inside a dream world or not. These included counting his fingers, attempting to breathe through his nose while pinching it, or pushing his fingers through his palm.

The dream world wasn’t bound by the rules, limitations, and logic of the waking reality. So, many of these reality checks failed inside it, alerting the user to their current state of consciousness.

Once this practice became a habit, practitioners could instinctively perform a reality check while dreaming and become lucid inside it when it inevitably failed.

The next step was to remain calm and ground oneself inside the dream world long enough to stabilize it. Otherwise, too much excitement could lead to detachment from the dream body and result in awakening in the real world.

It was one of the most common reasons why inexperienced lucid dreamers failed to go beyond the initial stage and thoroughly manipulate their dreams.

Alaric also maintained a dream journal where he recorded every minute detail he could recall from his dreams. This step might not seem that important, but it was absolutely crucial.

In fact, he had already perceived a stark difference in his dream quality ever since he had started maintaining a dream journal.

His dreams had turned more vivid, extended in length, and he could recall more of them since his conscious mind had grown more attentive during his dreamful sleep. Additionally, he had detected having multiple dreams a night.

Now, he simply needed to gain lucidity during a dream and acquire the special ability.

The power of lucid dreams couldn’t be underestimated since it allowed cultivators to form a direct connection with their subconscious mind. Additionally, it enabled them to tap into resources that were not possible to access in the real world.

But in order to unleash the real potential of the dream world, a cultivator must attain an ability similar to his Lucid Inner World.

Attaining an ability that allowed cultivators to willfully manipulate their dreamworld wasn’t something too complicated. It simply demanded a tamed mind, discipline, and the knowledge of proper dream manipulation.

Nothing a talented cultivator couldn’t accomplish if they really tried their best. However, most cultivators simply underestimated the usefulness of acquiring such an ability.

The fact that such abilities demanded substantial time and dedication to master didn’t help their reluctance. Not when they could focus on acquiring and mastering abilities that were deemed more suitable for their purposes.

At least, this had been the case in his previous world. Alaric wouldn’t bet it wasn’t the case in this new world as well.

In Alaric’s case, his extraordinary affinity for the third-eye chakra - which governs the dream realm - further boosted the strength of his ability and his odds of success.

A sudden chime in his mind pulled him out of his reverie. Alaric promptly checked the system notifications, and what he saw caused his face to break into a grin.
