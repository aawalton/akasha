A while ago, just when Vector and Arthur had met for the first time in front of Vantara's gate…

One man mixed within the crowd gathered at the gate looked abnormal, filled with worry. His eyes flicked left and right, jaw clenched, sweat beading on his brow—not from emotion, but from fear. Not because Vantara might be attacked, but because an unknown force had appeared, and the plan, Break the Fang, might have been compromised.

His name was Seran.

He wasn't a Venyar.

He wasn't even from the Novastra Empire.

Seran had disguised himself as an herb gatherer who had come to collect herbs in the Naga Forest from Ironfang Hill City. In truth, he belonged to a mysterious organization—the Ancient Ones—helping Lionheart invade Novastra, and he was one of their spies.

His job was simple: observe and report about the terrain and surroundings of Vantara. It also included noting any anomalies, so the plan could be executed with ease and preparations could be made accordingly.

Now, watching the events unfold before him, his blood ran cold.

He had heard moments ago, when a scout was reporting to Arthur, that members of the Resistance had come knocking on Vantara's door.

Out of precaution, he had come to the gate to collect any news if possible. The appearance of the Resistance members—with battle-hardened, disciplined soldiers and a group of people who were clearly craftsmen, even from afar—had solidified his suspicion that the plan had somehow leaked.

And if not—

Why would members of the Resistance come here with such an extensive lineup?

All for the purpose of strengthening Vantara to resist against the Lionheart invasion. It all indicated that Operation Break the Fang had been compromised.

So… the Resistance knows about the invasion, Seran thought nervously, watching Vector with narrowed eyes. If I don't report this back... everything will fall apart. The plan to destroy Ironfang Hill City… and the Novastra Empire… and finally to acquire access to that land.

But what bewildered him even more was the title 'Astrum'. Why hadn't he heard of it before? He knew every household title that controlled the Novastra Liberation Front like the back of his hand. But he had no idea that any of them held the Astrum name.

And the number of people Vector was leading was not something a normal Resistance force could dispatch. It could only be done by an influential family within the Resistance.

But this wasn't the time to focus on that.

He clenched his fists, waiting.

I need to slip away once the villagers begin to disperse.

He planned to escape when everyone standing around the gate began to leave. Otherwise, it would draw unnecessary attention. In the chaos of dispersal, and with evening approaching darkness, it would be the perfect opportunity to leave the village.

As Arthur and Vector began moving deeper into the village, murmurs filled the air again. Seran slowly began backing away, blending into the crowd. Soon, people started leaving. Some talked about Arthur's adventures from years past, others speculated whether they were friends of the village chief, while some even wondered if the matter was something else after seeing the people who had entered—clearly craftsmen, blacksmiths, and even healers could be identified.

This chaos was the perfect moment for him to slip away, before the newly arrived force got an inkling of his presence.

Under the guise of returning to his hut, Seran quietly made his way toward the village's southern perimeter after slipping away from the gate. He thought their vigilance would be lax now and they wouldn't notice his movement, assuming the newly arrived force would increase their vigilance only after they settled themselves.

…

What Seran didn't know was that Sebastian had already alerted Vector to the possibility of spies within Vantara after they arrived. Taking Sebastian's advice, Vector had informed Tristan to spread his scouts.

And with the presence of Vector's biggest reliance—Sebastian, a peak Third Stage warrior—if there were any anomalies, if spies were lurking in Vantara, he could easily discern them with his eagle eyes.

Sebastian had taken precautions before any problem could arise.

It was due to this precaution that Sebastian noticed the anomaly. A person whose eyes gleamed with fear and panic. After inquiring with the hunting team, his suspicion rose even more when he learned the man wasn't from Vantara. And his strength as a First Stage warrior… all led to doubt in Sebastian's mind.

The subsequent unusual and suspicious behavior hardened his belief even more. The suspect, after exiting the gate, began moving toward the southern path that led toward Ironfang Hill City. It was clearly an unusual time to leave Vantara—in the evening, and right after they had entered.

After following at a distance from Vantara, Sebastian decided to act.

He instantly appeared behind the person and said in a low voice, "You should stop your disguised act now…"

Hearing his voice, the person, instead of stopping in response to being caught, instantly accelerated his pace.

But was a First Stage warrior capable of evading the clutches of Sebastian?

No.

"You're not going anywhere."

Sebastian accelerated to catch him, moving like a phantom. His movement was so fast that leaves and trees around him began shaking intensely.

With one move, the person tasted soil. His mouth filled with blood and earth.

Panic surged through him as he looked at Sebastian. He reached for the small poison locket around his neck, a final measure in case he was discovered.

But to a Third Stage warrior—one at the peak of it—his movements were laughably slow. Sebastian saw that he was trying to commit suicide. With a flash of motion, Sebastian snatched the locket and threw it away. The person attempted a desperate struggle, but Sebastian had already immobilized him, arms twisted behind his back, spine locked with perfect technique.

"Let's find out what information you hold," Sebastian said coldly, dragging him back toward the village.

…

Twilight hours were slowly giving way to the darkness of night as Vector followed Arthur, with Marcus, Tristan, and Rhea following a step behind him. Arthur led the group with determined steps, guiding them to the prison where the suspected spy had been locked away under Sebastian's watch.

Vector had just received the information that Sebastian had captured a suspect.

Sebastian had warned him that spies might be lurking in Vantara. Vector had believed it too. But he hadn't expected that with their entrance to Vantara, they would catch a spy instantly.

Thinking of this, he moved forward a step ahead to catch Arthur. "Village Chief… please deploy scouts around the village. Marcus and Tristan will help you."

With a slight pause, he added, "And give the command of scouts to Tristan…"

Arthur's pupils quivered, but still with determination he responded, "Yes, Young Master Vector. I will arrange it immediately and have Tristan take command."

Vector looked at him. "Chief Arthur… don't misunderstand me. It isn't that I don't believe you. But Tristan is very proficient in information warfare. Even Sebastian respects his cleverness."

"The idea of Sebastian staying back to observe Vantara was also his suggestion. Otherwise, we wouldn't have been able to capture this spy."

A sigh of relief passed through Arthur's lips as Vector explained his intent. Vector knew Arthur's worry—that Arthur thought he probably didn't fully trust him, which was why this arrangement was made. Though Vector had started to believe him from the moment Arthur had shown the Astrum seal, something that couldn't be given casually.

But Vector knew that earning the Venyars' acknowledgment would take time. He believed he was capable of earning it in very short order.

Vector continued, his voice hardening. "The village must appear normal for now. We will work in the shadows. If more people or spies arrive, let them move freely, but they'll find nothing. All real preparation must remain hidden, silent, and out of sight."

His voice dropped lower, yet with a firm authoritative tone. "No one, absolutely no one, outside Vantara must suspect we've uncovered the Lionheart Empire's plan. Let them think they're still hidden. That will be our greatest advantage."

Arthur and the three nodded grimly, fully understanding the weight of his intent.

He reminded them again to keep the village appearing normal as he gave his final instructions. "Outsiders may visit under the guise of trade, travel, or curiosity, but in this forest, it's far more likely they're spies."

The Lionheart Empire wouldn't strike blindly. The spy they caught was proof enough of that.

"Act normal," Vector instructed. "And if any new visitors arrive, observe them and then act accordingly. Let them see only what we want them to see."

After giving this series of orders, he reached his destination.

Before them stood a two-story stone building, cold and utilitarian. Its walls were damp with age, wrapped in vines and thick with the scent of moss and old metal. Two-armed guards stood watch at the entrance, their expressions rigid. At the sight of Arthur and Vector, they saluted without hesitation.

Vector and the others entered the building, led by Arthur. The first floor held the regular holding cells for petty criminals. But what lay beneath—the underground dungeon—was for heinous and habitual criminals.

Vector was surprised to see its layout. A village had a prison structure that couldn't even be found in lowest-tier cities. It was probably due to dealing with adventurers, he thought.

The group descended through a narrow stairwell; the atmosphere around it felt ominous, full of fear and dread. Two guards stood beside the iron-reinforced door. They gave a sharp salute and pulled the lever to unlock the dungeon gate, the sound of iron gears groaning in the silence.

Inside, a dim hallway led to a series of reinforced cells. The stench of blood and sweat clung to the walls. A guard was already waiting to take them to the traitor's cell where he was being held.

"Sir Sebastian is already inside, interrogating the prisoner," he reported.

The guard led them to the cell where the traitor had been imprisoned. As they entered, they saw Sebastian standing before a bound man, his posture relaxed but his eyes cold.

Vector approached.

"Any progress?" he asked, his gaze shifting to the prisoner.

Sebastian responded with a nod to Vector. "His name is Seran. So far, he hasn't revealed anything useful. But don't worry, Young Master. By tomorrow, at the latest, he'll spill everything."

He handed over a seal, one known to everyone. "And this…" he continued, "...is a seal from Ironfang Hill City. It's likely Seran was stationed there and came to survey Vantara's surroundings. Likely for Operation Break the Fang."

Vector looked at Seran when the operation name was mentioned. "Does he know about the operation? Truly bizarre… how can a lowly tiered member have knowledge about this operation when they were maintaining it with such strictness?"

He said this because they also had some of their members enlisted in the Lionheart army, but they had no idea about it. One of them was even in a high post within the army.

Yet this spy and the traitor killed in Carnatic City both had knowledge about it.

…

Sebastian looked at Vector's pinpoint observation that the spy knew about the operation.

He knew the reason for it too, but he was in an emotional struggle about whether to reveal it or not. After calming his mind, he decided to reveal it when the Elders of the Astrum forces arrived in Vantara. With Vector coming here to the Naga Forest, someone from Ironfang Hill City would surely arrive within a few days. They might even arrive in two days if nothing was holding them back.

His eyes looked at Seran, the member of the mysterious organization. The reason he was sure of it was because Seran bore the same seal—front side structured with a vine-wreathed ancient gate, and the reverse side had a circular structure representing Ethereum—as was with the traitor who had attacked Vector in Carnatic City.
