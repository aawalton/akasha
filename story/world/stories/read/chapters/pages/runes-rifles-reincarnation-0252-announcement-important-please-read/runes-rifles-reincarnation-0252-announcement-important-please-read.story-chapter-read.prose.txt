Got a little announcement for y'all.

First of all, I'm sorry.

RRR is still on hiatus, and it'll likely stay that way for a while.

When I started writing this story, I set a goal for myself: one million words.

I've made it about halfway to that goal. However, as I've matured as a writer, I've come to realize that it isn't the number of words that matters. It's the quality of those words.

Unfortunately, after taking a step back, I realized I didn't put enough effort into maintaining this story's quality.

It has plot holes the size of the Grand Canyon, as one of my reviewers so eloquently pointed out.

If you haven't seen that review, go look for it. It's genuinely my favorite review I've ever received.

Anyway, I'm not happy with how disjointed the story became after I let my ADHD take over and lead it down paths I never planned.

For instance, the entire dragon saga was never part of the outline. That idea came to me the month before the story was supposed to launch while I was lying in bed with COVID.

Honestly, the only part of the story that went according to plan is the latest volume, the fourth volume, where Jin Shu treks through the Southern Region and reaches the hidden realm. (That was originally supposed to be the second volume...)

So, suffice it to say, I'm not thrilled with how the story has turned out.

Now I have two paths ahead of me.

I don't want to abandon this story, and I won't.

But...

I don't really have the motivation to finish it in its current form.

That's where my two options come in.

Option One: Continue the story as it is, but at a much slower pace.

That would probably mean one chapter a week at most. More realistically, two or three chapters a month. (Max)

This would give me more time to focus on writing my other stories while also giving me enough time to make sure every new chapter is the best it can be.

Option Two: Rewrite the story... again.

For those of you who don't know, Runes Rifles Reincarnation is actually a rewrite of my very first story, Reborn into a Cultivation World with a Gun.

With this option, I'd take the story apart piece by piece, keeping what I love and removing what I don't.

It would mean a much longer wait, probably a year or more, but it would also result in a far more polished and cohesive story.

Now I'll leave the decision to all of you, because this story is as much yours as it is mine. I may be the one writing it, but my loyal readers are the reason I wanted to tell this story in the first place.

Let me know in the comments which option you'd prefer.
