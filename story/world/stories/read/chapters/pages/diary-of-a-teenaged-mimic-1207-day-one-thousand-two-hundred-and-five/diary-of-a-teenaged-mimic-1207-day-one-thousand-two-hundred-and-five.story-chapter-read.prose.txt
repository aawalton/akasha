Dear Diary,

I think I might get more contemplative when I'm depressed. Okay, when I'm depressed and not actively self-abusive. Although I guess even then I'm contemplating a lot, it just focuses on contemplating all the different ways I've fucked everything up, and how much of a fuckup I am.

Guess I'm growing up, able to take shit seriously without having to lie to myself about it. Not to mention the whole 'not treating myself like shit all the time', which is also part of growing up. I mean, along with being a trauma response. But seriously, nobody expects a human sized sack of runny diarrhea to amount to anything. At any rate, been thinking about what's going on, and realizing that I'm not the best possible solution to every possible problem is... Not the greatest realization when I'm already kind of at a low point, but also, I think, a sign that I'm both maturing and healing.

I'm maturing, in that I'm realizing that there are things I'm good at and things I'm not good at.

I remember being surprised as all fuck that I'm good at actually doing unto others. I mean, yeah, all the repressed rage at the injustices of the world, sure, but as anybody whose learned anything about anything will tell you, rage can give you some boosts in power, not to mention making it so you don't hold back at an inopportune moment, but it doesn't do shit about knowing where or how to actually hit somebody. When it comes to one on one combat against another Human Adjacent person, I'm pretty sure the best in Atlantis are the Norfolk Duelist's Guild. And I cut through all of them and didn't even wind up dead at the end of it. Shit, I wasn't even down for the count for more than a day or two, and that was mostly my own stupid fault for not getting my lung piercing looked at sooner.

I'm not exactly surprised by the fact that I've become a good mom. Okay, maybe a little. But mostly I'm surprised that unlike all forms of shit wrecking, nobody trained me to do mom stuff. Which, I guess, isn't all that uncommon. Okay, I know back in the day there were classes on that shit, but everybody who took them when it wasn't, like, a school requirement for young women who had crotch goblins lurking in the school day care wound up getting mocked for doing so. Which is dumb as fuck given the importance of the task in question, but while people are capable of great things, they're also capable of some serious dumbassery. Also, while I never took a class on parenting, to some degree everybody gets mentored a little bit, if only just by watching what our parents did.

Although... I guess that's not quite true. We watch, but we don't always understand. Sometimes, if our parents aren't around to ask, it takes a long time for us to realize that some of the shit they did isn't not working because we're doing it wrong, but because it never actually worked for them. We were just too little and ignorant and, just maybe, innocent to realize that it wasn't working. Or that it worked for little rugrat us, but they were killing themselves by inches making that shit happen.

Long story short, I know, far, far, too late on that score, I've realized that I'm a good mom, and I think not surprised by that means I'm maturing. I've put like three years of serious near daily effort in being a good mom. My active training in shit wrecking, even including how much I've learned by teaching others, is maybe a quarter of that at best, because I sure as shit don't do that shit on the daily. I do it three days a week now, but it was only once a week last Season, and there have been some Seasons where I haven't even done that much.

I think I'm healing because, along with realizing that while I'm good at some things, I don't have to be good at everything. I can trust my partners to do the parts I suck at when it's important, and never hold it over my head in any real kinda way.
