Chapter V.LX (5.60) - Bean Spillage

“How did the blanket work?” Kizu asked. “Can you just carry it around everywhere? And when did you study a lava monster? You know that could have been helpful back when we were tracking Necro back in Hon. We could have used that in our fight against the witches.”

Ione took a long swig of her drink. Kizu watched her throat move as she gulped down the fresh water.

Across the cafeteria, students eyed the two of them with a mixture of awe and trepidation. Ione’s recent display over at the tournament grounds granted her a new respect from the student body. She very rarely publicly displayed her skill with summons and most only knew her from the F classes.

Kizu watched as a boy walking by realized he’d noticed him staring. He promptly tripped over his own feet and sent his tray’s contents across the cafeteria floor.

Milk, baked beans, and bread splattered everywhere.

Kizu stared. He didn’t even know why the student even had a tray. Everything should just appear on the table after an examination and selection from the menu. He must have been moving across the cafeteria for some reason. Rather than clean up his mess, the boy scampered off.

“This is exactly why I don’t normally display my summoning,” Ione said with a sigh. “The last thing I want is a bunch of boys goggling me all day.”

“I thought you stopped participating in the weekend contests because you didn’t want to fight your sister?”

“Well, yeah. That too.” Ione blew a wild strand of hair out of her eyes. “I can’t do the blanket strategy normally because summoning circles are single-use and expire after a day or two. I spent a few hours stitching that circle, mostly because I wanted a large physical representation of the new circle to easily study. It’s by far my most complex summon to date and will take time to memorize. Still, got to say, the monster definitely proved worth the effort.”

“A monster you discovered where? And when?” Kizu pressed.

Ione studied the ceiling as she chewed on her lip. “I might have… gone on a scouting mission with Basil.”

“A scouting mission?” Kizu blinked as understanding accompanied with horror dawned on him. There was only one place he’d ever seen lava. “Into the World Dungeon?” he hissed.

“I figured I’ve been down there enough times that it wouldn’t be an issue,” Ione replied defensively. “Plus, we didn’t go far and even brought your atlas along, just in case.”

“You brought my atlas?” Kizu thought back to where he stored the book on the shelf over his hammock. He hadn’t actually paid it any mind these last few months. It hadn’t been relevant. He supposed that if Ione had taken and replaced it in the same day, he likely would have been none the wiser about it.

“It was fine.” Ione waved her spoon in front of her placatingly. “I escaped completely unharmed and Basil barely took a few burns. And we came out with a fantastic new summon! I’ve actually been waiting over a week to show it off to you! Been torture keeping it secret. Its entire anatomy is built of molten rock, which makes it entirely unique in my arsenal. The only thing vaguely similar to it are the tortoises we saw up in the tundra of Tross. They have shells constructed of ice, though they also have flesh and blood like their less-magical reptile cousins as well. Still, they helped me lay down a foundation of understanding when I went to examine the new monster. Instrumental in studying another, more alien, elemental beast.”

Kizu set his head in his palms. So much of this could have gone wrong. He had no idea if the bloodspawn had spotted them wandering around. They supposedly avoided the upper layers due to the extreme heat, but that hadn’t stopped him from encountering them there in the past. Then there were just the natural dangers the World Dungeon imposed everywhere. The fact they’d encountered a massive magma monster was enough evidence alone that they’d navigated around extreme danger.

“What’s the thing even called?” Kizu muttered into his hands.

“Cherufe!” Ione replied gleefully. “I found records of them in a book up in the Living Library. Powerful apex predators that consume through burning organic material. Their greatest weakness is their lack of speed, which they make up for by remaining completely camouflaged within magma streams. You have to lure them out to get them to react. They’re also meant to be omens of great volcanic disasters.”

Kizu’s head shot up. “They, what?”

Ione waved off his concern. “It’s all superstition. There is no evidence they actually cause volcanic eruptions."

“Ione, half of the field of divination is built on superstitions. There’s nearly always a reason why these sorts of stories crop up.”

“Likely those stories are told so locals report the monsters to authorities immediately as a means of ensuring they kill them to make certain they don’t murder more people or breed in the area. Also, their body parts are extraordinarily valuable if you harvest them before they cool off. Additional incentive to convince people to report them.”

“You don’t know that for certain though,” Kizu said, exasperated. “And have you noticed where our school tournament is currently located? On top of a volcano.”

“Kizu, these monsters don’t just spring up out of nowhere. He’s likely been living in that magma river for decades, if not centuries. Trust me, we’re completely safe. In fact, we’re more than safe because I have an awesome new summon to use!”

“Yes. Your new summon is very neat,” Kizu reluctantly admitted. “Can you sustain more than one at a time? And will you always need a pre-made circle ahead of summoning it or can you draw it on the fly like your others?”

“Hm.” Ione dipped her spoon into her pudding. She twisted the utensil, disemboweling the dessert. “Only one cherufe. But I can have smaller summons alongside it like the caldrops I used in my fight with your ex. As for sketching out the circle… well, it’s certainly trickier than something like that mimic we encountered over spring break. It doesn’t help that I have to personally create the summoning circle by hand for results and that the effectiveness of the circle expires after like a day of disuse.”

Kizu nodded along and listened as she rambled about how the consistency of the lines altered the monster’s mass in unique ways. While powerful, Kizu noted a lot of problems with this new summon. Not something he believed they’d be able to rely on in a pinch. But maybe with planning they could still find a way to use it. A giant fire monster could potentially serve as a massive boon when confronting bloodspawn and Otochi.

“This is disgusting. Kizu, are you responsible for this? I demand you explain yourself. This is no way for a representative of the Student Council to present himself.”

Kizu’s eye twitched as he heard Ione’s twin speaking over his shoulder.

Sene sat down on the bench beside him, glaring daggers.

“Well? What do you have to say for yourself? Some of that brown sludge got on my boot!”

“I am not responsible for the spilled beans behind me,” Kizu replied flatly. “A student tripped and dropped his lunch. And you’re the academy’s highest ranked elemental student, just create a bit of water and wash the beans off your boots if it bothers you.”

“Speaking of water—”

Kizu and Ione both groaned in unison.

Sene glared at them and then cleared her voice before continuing. “Speaking of water, I have some good news. The kitchen staff agreed to use their spatial enchantments to set up a network. This expansion of what already exists in the cafeteria will make it so anywhere on campus, you can summon a glass of water to hand. It will have a twenty minute cooldown, because they’re worried about students abusing the system and placing too much strain on the enchantments. After all, should those enchantments fail, we would no longer have the ability to summon lunch to our tables during mealtimes. An absolute hypothetical disaster we must avert. But this is certainly a step in the right direction in the Student Council’s mission to hydrate the campus!”

Kizu narrowed his eyes and crossed his arms. “And?”

“And what?” Sene’s smug smile faltered. “It’s a victory. You should be overwhelmed with gratitude that I performed this feat of negotiation on behalf of our Student Council.”

“What is it you want from us?” Ione clarified, understanding what Kizu was asking. They were both waiting for the other side of Sene’s visit to strike them.

Sene huffed. “And nothing!” She stood. “I simply assumed you’d wish to join me in celebration. If I’m not wanted here, I’ll go to where I’m appreciated instead. I’ll see you at our weekly meeting.”

Her angry stomping cut off as she slipped on the spilled beans. Her arms pinwheeled up as she slid. Thankfully for everyone, Sene reflexively steadied herself with wind elemental magic. On currents of winds, Sene drifted out of the cafeteria, though her tantrum’s effect was rather dampened by the beans.

“She’s off to see that boy again,” Ione said once the cafeteria calmed down and everyone returned to their own business. “She’s been visiting him daily.”

“Do you like him?”

“Eh. Name is Barney. From what I’ve spied on, he seems decent enough, though a bit odd. But he’s a mage that’s really good at divination and keeps dispelling my summons whenever they get too close.”

Kizu poked at his noodles. “Have you tried just… talking to him like a normal human instead of spying?”

“Nope. Why would I do that?”
