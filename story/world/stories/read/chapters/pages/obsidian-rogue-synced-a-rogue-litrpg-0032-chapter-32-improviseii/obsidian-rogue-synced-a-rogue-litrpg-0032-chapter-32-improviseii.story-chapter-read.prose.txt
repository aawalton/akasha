With his mana finally replenished to a reasonable level, it made sense for him to attack the krupi again. Levi now fancied his chance of taking on the beast. The fact that his void tear was able to cut off the krupi’s arm was enough to buoy his confidence, and unless the beast had some way of healing its wounds that he wasn’t aware of, then Levi was sure that the monster would be suffering from blood loss because of the arm his void tear had severed.

After an injury like that, it wasn’t much of a surprise to Levi that it hadn’t left its cave. The krupi would be a lot easier to kill by anything around the same level as it, which meant the next move was Levi’s to decide.

Waiting for the krupi to exit its cave was a wasted strategy, as Levi didn't know if it had some sort of way to exit the cave without using the cave mouth. While he didn't think that was the case, it was still a possibility. That was why he was going to have to risk returning to the cave rather than waiting for it to possibly die from blood loss.

Fighting the krupi would still be difficult, since his blade couldn’t pierce through its scales. It was a setback that he’d found a way through, but he couldn’t expect the krupi to fall for the same trick twice—at least, not immediately. Levi shrugged his shoulders at the thoughts before proceeding to the cave. Regardless of how difficult the scales were to pierce through, he’d kill it. That much was certain.

Before Levi could change his mind, he walked back into the cave, keeping his sole dagger bared right in front of him. As it was, the krupi still hadn’t attacked him, but there was no doubt in his mind that it was still here because Levi could hear its labored breathing coming from deeper in the cave.

It was obviously still suffering from his last attack. A fact that didn’t relieve Levi nearly as much as it should’ve. If anything, he tightened his grip on his dagger. Fighting a beast on the verge of death could go any way; the krupi would be unpredictable. So, it’d be up to him to kill it as fast as possible. If not, Levi risked the chance of the monster taking him down with it.

A loud growl reverberated through the cavern; it was undoubtedly the krupi’s way of letting Levi know that it knew he was here. He‘d expected it to happen sooner or later because of the distinct way the salve on his bandaged arm stank.

The warning growl was all the motivation Levi needed to heighten his senses. Getting caught off guard by the krupi would most likely result in his death; an outcome he very much preferred to avoid.

Even as he continued to creep deeper in the cave, Levi couldn’t help but wonder how he was supposed to take on a beast that was alert to his presence. He’d discarded the idea of using a void tear as his opening move because the krupi would most likely avoid it, which would result in a waste of mana.

Levi cleared his mind of such thoughts as he finally came face to face with the krupi. The monster was leaning against the cave walls, putrid black blood dripping from its wound and onto the floor in a rhythmic pitter-patter. Its fangs were bared in his direction in hatred. Levi had read the emotion clearly, as though the beast had a human face.

The hate wafting off the krupi in invisible waves was more than warranted, as Levi had managed to strike a blow that would massively hinder the Krupi’s movement for as long as it’d live, which wasn’t going to be that long because Levi planned to end its suffering today, if everything went according to plan.

With the confidence of someone who had managed to land a critical blow against his foe, Levi locked eyes with the krupi and then grinned savagely at the monster before sprinting towards it. He could see the hesitation in its eyes as he closed the distance between them. The Krupi seemed to have grown wary of him, since his void tear had severed its arm. It openly battled its animalistic instinct to engage the man who was heading straight for it. The krupi’s survival instinct warned it to steer clear of Levi.

It was no secret that the tide of the fight had tilted in Levi’s direction since the last time they’d fought. Both had recognized this fact, and now the beast warred within itself, but a beast was always going to be a beast. The animalistic side of the krupi won out, evidenced by the way it pushed off the wall with its remaining arm in preparation to attack.

The krupi reached for Levi’s arm as soon as he got within range, but he’d anticipated such an attack.

Without breaking his stride, Levi easily sidestepped the krupi’s clawed hand before delivering a counterattack of his own by slashing at its exposed side with his dagger, which bounced off harmlessly once it made contact with the krupi’s scales.

Levi was undeterred by the failure of his dagger to pierce through the krupi’s scales; in fact, he’d expected it and factored it into his plan. With a grunt, he pulled back after his failed attempt and increased the distance between him and the Krupi, much to its annoyance. The beast growled in his direction, and the sound made Levi widen his grin.

Even though it was their first clash after the break, it seemed like he was getting closer to his end goal. He needed the beast to be beyond annoyed for his plan to work, and for that to happen, he had to keep this up.

The pair were now somewhat equal; the krupi had the single arm, and Levi had the single dagger. It wasn’t really a fair comparison, but in terms of basic means of attacking, they were matched.

He had a feeling that the krupi wouldn’t necessarily agree with his assessment, and to be honest, he didn’t care much about how the monster felt, especially as it’d soon die by his hands. Its apparent weakness due to blood loss made the matter of its death a foregone conclusion.

Levi still had to pay attention to its movement regardless of its current state. The krupi was a formidable foe despite having lost an arm. It still had a hand equipped with claws, armored scales, as well as sharp fangs, a lethal combination to face and one that could kill him if he slipped up.

He danced around the krupi with grace, poking and prodding at any openings he saw. His movement was graceful, due to the many years he’d spent as a thief. He’d learned to have quick feet, as well as avoid being seen. What he was doing now was basically the same thing with a little tweak; rather than not being seen, he was avoiding being hit, with maximum success so far.

While his attacks were ineffective against the krupi due to its scales, they still managed to enrage the krupi. He could tell that he was getting close enough to making the krupi lose every shred of hesitation that seemed to hold it back from going berserk.

To add dry twigs to the flames that were the krupi’s anger, he tweaked things a bit by changing the way he dodged its counterattacks. Rather than dodging immediately, he saw the attack, he waited till the last second, just to give the krupi a false sense of hope.

Even  as different as they were in terms of their specie, Levi could very easily interpret the look on its face every time he narrowly dodged an attack; it said almost. It was enthralling to watch, and Levi knew that the only reason he could repeatedly execute such a thing was because of the krupi’s blood loss, which had impacted its speed.

Once again, he attacked. This time, he went for Krupi's knees. His blades bounced off harmlessly, confirming that it was scaled just like the rest of its body. He felt not a single shred of disappointment as he pulled back, narrowly evading the krupi’s half-swipe aimed at his head.

For his next attack, Levi tried something different. He dashed towards the krupi, his dagger arcing up as he slashed at its eyes, only for his attack to be deflected as the krupi slapped the blade aside before using its powerful right leg to kick Levi into the jagged cave wall.

His broken rib flared up at the harsh impact. Levi cursed as he gritted his teeth. He’d gotten careless, and the krupi had managed to launch an attack of its own.

He’d thought he’d have been able to end the fight early by using his dagger to stab the krpi’s eye and quite possibly have the blade go down to its brain, killing it instantly, but that hadn’t been the case.

What he hadn’t factored in was the ability of the Krupi to anticipate the attack aimed at its eye. It was a bad decision on his part, and rather than waste time berating him self, he pushed himself off the floor, shrugging off the pain that raked his body from being rag-dolled into a wall.

Levi turned his attention to the krupi, which was heading towards him at full sprint. It seemed that his failed attack aimed at its eyes was enough to finally make the krupi go berserk. With how fast it was charging at him, Levi knew that he couldn’t let this opportunity pass him by.

[Void-step (II) Tear]

This time, the tear appeared right in front of the krupi, who had been sprinting so fast that it couldn’t avoid it entirely. A quarter of its head had already crossed into the tear before the krupi stopped its charge. That was enough for Levi, who quickly closed the tear with a thought.

The monster abruptly fell to the floor, a few steps before Levi, with a quarter of its head missing. The missing part reappeared a second later, dropping to the floor from a void tear that appeared on Levi's right-hand side.

Levi held his dagger in a vise grip and kept his eyes fixed on the body of the Krupi, which was now sprawled on the floor— with its brain now decorating the cave floor— just in case of any last-minute surprises, and once the body didn’t move after a few breaths, he dropped to the floor, panting.

His battle with the krupi had lasted for a while, and the adrenaline that had fueled him during the entire fight had begun to wear out. Now he struggled to catch his breath, his body struggling to handle the multiple injuries that he now carried.

In spite of his current struggle, Levi couldn’t help but smile as he stared at the corpse of the beast.

Being able to kill the krupi with two attempts was something that surprised him because he hadn’t even expected to survive the first. He’d believed that he would’ve died at the start and now he was staring at its corpse.

“It seems like I can accomplish a lot more if I use my brain.” Levi chuckled, his eyes on the Krupi’s exposed brain.

Fighting the Krupi opened up his brain to new possibilities. Like him discovering how he could use void-step to kill. Levi could only imagine how invaluable such a skill was, and he knew that it would only continue to grow with each level he gained.

With a grunt, he got on his feet, walked towards the corpse, and kicked the krupi. It hurt him physically to do it, but it was well worth it as he now had confirmation that the monster was well and truly dead.

The confirmation lifted his spirits; it felt extremely good to take down a monster with the odds in its favour. And he couldn’t wait to see what he’d earned from the system for defeating his second e-ranker on the same day.

What do you know? Adversity does have an upside.
