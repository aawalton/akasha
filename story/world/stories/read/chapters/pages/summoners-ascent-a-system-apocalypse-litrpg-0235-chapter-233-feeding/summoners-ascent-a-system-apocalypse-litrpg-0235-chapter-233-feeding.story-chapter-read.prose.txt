With the first strike against the large core, the street around Ashton began to scream. It was like a loud hiss or yelp of pain coming from all the bits of flesh that were overgrowing through the area.

The part of the road that Ashton was standing on, where those thick root-like tentacles were slithering out like skinned snakes, was reaching up to him, as if trying to stop him. But of course, that didn't work.

As he fought these aspects of the 'Growth', Ashton had noticed a few traits that it had. And one of them was the most important in this situation.

While it was capable of pushing through it, the growth's flesh was still able to feel pain. It often reflexively flinched back, or briefly retreated in pain before counterattacking.

It was like this whole area was a single massive monster, with its entire focus being to take down and kill all the intruders. Just that the actual basic environment here wasn't particularly powerful and couldn't do much damage. Its main purpose was probably to restrain people and then let the sentries do the actual attacking. Not that Ashton stood still long enough for the environment to latch onto him until now.

And though he did have to stick close to the core to keep attacking it, Ashton was defending himself quite easily. The salamander's fire was flying around him in a spiral. It was so hot that even Ashton would struggle to just stand here in the centre of it if he wasn't using the sylph to push the heat directly away from him with a protective layer of wind.

Instead, the flesh that kept trying to grab him pulled back as if afraid of the pain. And even the few times where it did push through, the flesh would immediately get burnt and the muscles within it would tense up, just to be further consumed by the flames.

And like this, Ashton was able to persist, surrounded by flames as the road, the trees, and the buildings around him were screaming, trying to stop him from further damaging the core.

At some point, some sentries came. It was an actually rather large group. Simids, lizardmen, goblins, orcs; they had all been taken over and were trying to attack Ashton. But since he was powerful enough to break the weak points on their bodies in a single strike, Ashton could dispatch of them without having to worry.

The bigger problem was more that, while Ashton was strong enough, his 'staff' was lagging behind in that aspect quite a bit. It was just a stone rod that Ashton had the gnome create for him repeatedly, but it would break within just a few attacks.

It wasn't exactly sturdy, and was really just kept together by magic. Through Ashton's mana and his techniques, that magic was interrupted very fast, and the heavy impacts of the attacks were enough to do the rest.

And that was fine enough in general; Ashton had enough of a strength gap with most monsters in the area that he didn't have to go all out anyway. It slowed things down a fair bit and wasted a lot of mana, but it was still faster than not fighting himself at all.

The only issue right now was that, with all the flesh covering the ground, it was almost impossible to find a gap the gnome could take advantage of and pull up some stone. And without any existing momentum, forcing the stone through what was essentially dense muscle on the ground was hard as well.

So instead, whenever Ashton's weapon broke fighting against the sentries, he would jump back and instead focus his magic on the gnome for a while. He channelled the Helping Hand and increased the magic power that flowed into the summoner's staff to boost the gnome's earth magic.

The shards of the stone weapon that Ashton was wielding until now slowly came back together, each slotting into the place they were in before as the stone fused back into one solid piece and Ashton could fight again.

In the meantime, Ashton just focused on dodging, and trying to figure out more about the sentries in general. He never encountered anything like this on Lumia, so he wanted to learn more if he could. How exactly did this spread, was this dangerous, could it infect living being? That sort of thing that Ashton had no idea about so far.

He had inspected lots of things within the area with the Pen of Insight, so it should be fine. Plus, maybe if he was lucky, he could ask the Salesman for some kind of book on things like this. Or when he went to sleep the next time and encountered another one of his old friends in his dreams, maybe they could know something.

Ashton figured that they probably kept up with the worlds that met the same fate as Lumia; the 'Growth' must have come from one of those.

Things like this made Ashton nervous. All the bodies of the sentries were slowly being pulled apart, like the growth was cannibalising itself.

All these cores that were being created were feeding into his larger one, and even this seemed to be connected to something greater beyond here. Ashton didn't know where exactly it was headed, but the ground was pulsating. Like mass was being moved from this core to the next, like a network bound together, ready to create something terrible.

And then, the large core in front of Ashton finally broke open. It was like a flood of pus flowed out from the inside. Ashton assumed that the sentries around here would collapse and the trees would wither and begin to rot, similar to the other cores he had already destroyed, but this time, that wasn't the case.

Instead, as Ashton focused on that broken-open core, he saw something else inside of that glass-like sphere. Something that began to move, and slowly came outside.

It was a humanoid figure, for the most part. The proportions were wrong, but otherwise, it looked like a human with its skin peeled off down to its muscles, if those muscles were beginning to melt off its bones. No... as if those muscles never got the chance to properly wrap around them.

Its limbs were too long, its face contorted. The ribs were too large and too round, and its spine protruded and was sticking out through the muscle fibres.

The monster stepped out of the core. Its breath was heavy and strained and loud. Ashton knew what that sound was; something was wrong with its lungs. They probably weren't developed enough.

"They're eggs, huh?" Ashton whispered as he started to run toward the creature born from the growth.

Despite its malformed body, it was strong. Ridiculously strong. As if its muscles were made of steel fibres. Of course, Ashton's first response was to blast it with flames. That seemed to do something, at least.

The creature screamed in pain and lashed out, trying to hit Ashton. With its long, deformed limbs that seemed to almost melt and stretch as it moved, it almost grazed by him here and there, but Ashton dodged, pushing himself away with the sylph's gusts.

He continued to take down the other sentries whenever possible, trying to buy as much time as he could while the gnome was repairing the staff. He could barely hit that thing more than two or three times without the stone shattering apart.

Strike after strike, Ashton tried to whittle down on it, its body bubbling and burning to a crisp more and more, but it just kept going, all the whilst screaming in agonizing pain.

"Can't you just die already? Fuck," Ashton groaned loudly. This thing didn't have a weak point like the others. It didn't have one of those bulbs that he could break. This one was like a flesh of mass that was continuously moving no matter how much Ashton broke it down.

Even when its bones should be shattered and its muscles too torn for it to move, like it was hanging on a puppeteer's threads, the monster continued to move as its body slowly melted back together. But with every injury, it was becoming less and less what it was before.

It was becoming more wrong. Like the mistakes were accumulating. If Ashton kept going, then surely at some point, it would be fine. But somehow, even the fire wasn't doing much more than causing it pain, apparently. It didn't seem like the torn and snapped and carbonised muscles were doing anything at all to slow it down.

"Okay, I've got to find a new tactic," Ashton whispered as the staff shattered for probably the twentieth time in this fight. "I don't have enough internal energy for attacks that could really tear it apart yet... gnome, do you think you could turn the staff into some kind of spear instead? Cutting it apart should be more effective."

The small mole did as asked, though it seemed like it was taking a bit longer to get the shape right, and properly sharpen the stone spearhead. But as Ashton focused back on the monster, he noticed that it wasn't trying to fight him anymore.

No, instead, it was squatted on the ground. It was eating. Tearing apart the tendrils of flesh surrounding it, and the corpses of the sentries, stuffing them all into itself. Not into its mouth, no, just into its body. Into any open, pulsating wound on its body.

"Yeah, no, let's not do that," Ashton ran forward with the unfinished stone spear in hand. Instead of letting the gnome sharpen the edge, he coated it in water and turned it into ice. It wouldn't be very strong, but it should be sharp.

Ashton swung it at the monster, managing to cut into its muscles. It was shallow and the ice splintered off immediately, but with a few more slashes, Ashton was able to get rid of a chunk of flesh.

Though, by then, it had already gained three times that much by 'feeding'.

It didn't even seem interested in fighting anymore. It was just doing nothing but eating. The sentries seemed less interested in Ashton, as well. Instead, they were just all walking at the monster.

They were walking into it, pushing their bodies against the creature, as their masses fused together.

"... Well, at least I feel like the Spawn will like what that thing is turning into..."
