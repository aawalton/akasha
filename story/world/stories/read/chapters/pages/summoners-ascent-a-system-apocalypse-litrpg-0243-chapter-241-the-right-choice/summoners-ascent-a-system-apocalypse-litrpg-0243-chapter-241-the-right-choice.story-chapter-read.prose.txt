God-kin.

Ashton figured that this was what Jonah, Clementine's father, was becoming. From the first meeting, he already had that rock-like demeanour carved into him. He really didn't know where these guys came from, but they didn't seem to be like goblins or lizardmen, where they were the people of another world that was taken over by the council.

Each god-kin was a powerful individual, and you barely ever saw two in one place, unless they were fighting each other to the death.

Ashton didn't know exactly where they came from, but he did hear some rumours and stories from celestials.

Celestial beings had different grades as well. Just like insects existed on earth, things similar to insects existed in the celestial realm. There were animals, people, dangerous magical beasts, and then there were those that were a step above that. Angels. One of the most powerful types of beings in the celestial realm.

They were basically the celestial realm's version of elder spirits. Most of the celestial Ashton knew were either at the level of animals or regular 'people' when they were summoned, though that might have changed, as it did with the spirits that showed up in his dreams and Alice.

But the important connection between god-kin and angels was that, as per the stories Ashton was told, was that there was a war between the celestial realm and hell.

During one of the battles, a powerful demon decapitated an archangel, the leader of the celestial army, and laid their body down at the peak of a mountain to let their golden blood flow into the valley below. The stone of the mountain that was drenched in the archangel's blood and the grief they felt for their own death, soon sprung to life.

These beings, borne from the blood that the people of the world the battle took place in considered closest to god, were dubbed 'God-kin', the children of god now walking a material world.

However, god-kin were beings that considered themselves as superior to all else. They had a narcissism like none other, believing all life to be below them. Whether it was demon, angel, or even other god-kin; the individual thought themself to be the greatest being in existence.

And they were vindictive, and terrible. Those that would not bow down to them and grovel were not allowed air to breathe.

When the battles between hell and the celestial realm were over, the god-kin were left behind within that world, and it soon fell to ruin, being split into small countries ruled by the powerful god-kin.

When the council came to that world, they must have thought they hit the monster jackpot, because there were few more monstrous 'people' than god-kin, even without changing their instincts.

And maybe that was why right now, Jonah was able to act like a normal person, at least compared to the other Marked that Ashton had encountered.

But this wasn't good. God-kin, as beings descending from celestial blood in some way or another, whether they were really just stone come to life or not, had access to certain innate magic.

For one, god-kin gained more power as the faith that people had in them increased, another reason why that was the name they were given. Celestials didn't normally gain power from faith directly, but the narcissistic god-kin most certainly did. They had access to a magic that simulated the process of how many actual gods were born and gained their own powers, and many of them have tried to become gods this way themselves.

Not that any of them succeeded. You had to be special to become a god; you had to have a vessel that could contain 'faith' of others, which god-kin simply did not have. The magic they used actually counter-acted that. After a while of receiving faith, it was possible to develop such a vessel, but god-kin actually used up the power created by others' faith to strengthen themselves. Essentially, they were unknowingly forgoing their long-term goal to gain benefits in the short-term.

Well, considering that Jonah was using that magic as well, at least Ashton didn't have to worry about this guy having any sort of divinity in him. The chance was slim to none, but it wasn't impossible. Ashton didn't know how people's existing faith might act now that it was given access to mana all of a sudden.

That being the case, this was good. The ways that a god-kin could strengthen themself was essentially just boosting their existing abilities, so Jonah would be physically strong.

The bigger issue was that a god-kin was able to use more than just that strengthening magic. Looking down from within the veil, the people sitting in the pews were praying with an intensity that he had never seen in any church. It was like they were begging some higher being for mercy; to be freed from the suffering of this new world. They were fanatical.

And Jonah stood at their front, convincing them it would work. This was the other magic, which was meant to turn people into nothing but cattle to be used by the god-kin however they wish.

Ashton was pulled back to his body and quickly put the world fragment away.

"Okay, we should start getting ready," Ashton explained. "When the sun is down, we'll infiltrate the cathedral and take him down. He's using magic on basically everyone in the camp beside himself right now, and from what I know, it's pretty exhausting for the affected people, so they'll head to sleep soon."

Clementine nodded. "And he'll be weaker at night? So... it won't be as much of a fight?"

Ashton held forward his summoner's staff, and cast a silence spell around them, so that they could talk without fear of being spotted. "Something like that. He's turning into a god-kin, just as I thought, so a sort of 'celestial' monster. Celestials use the power of the heavens, and of the celestial bodies, of which the sun is the easiest to use. I doubt that a man like your father would have taken the time to study the stars or put any meaning into something like astrology, so he'll be taking most of his power from the sun as well."

Clementine nodded along, starting to bite her nails as she did. "Okay... so... what exactly do I do?"

"Well... just stay quiet, I guess? Follow me, do what I say. Especially when I tell you to move or something. I'll be telling you that for a reason," Ashton explained. "Infiltrating a place like this is actually far easier than you'd think, don't worry. Though... there is something else you didn't tell me yet."

With a flinch, Clementine's eyes shot away from the cathedral and back at her friend. "Hm? Wait, what did I not tell you yet?"

"Do you want to talk to him first, or not? Doing this by surprise would be easiest, but it doesn't have to be. If you want to have a conversation with him first, then that's okay. I can take him down either way."

It seemed like Clementine hadn't really considered this yet. What exactly was it that she wanted to do here? Why did she ask to join Ashton on this? She could have just let him go alone. Sure, she said at first that she wanted to go and see what her father was like, but she believed Ashton when he said that there was only really one way to become a Marked at this point in time.

And if that was the case, and if he was leading what was literally just a cult, then maybe, in this world... it would be better if that monster of a man didn't make it past tonight.

There were times in the past where Clementine had wanted him to just disappear anyway. But... killing him? Killing her own father? Sure, she wasn't doing it with her own hands, but it was her best friend doing it. It was like she was holding the knife at this point, getting ready to stab it through her father's chest.

"... I think... I think I want to talk to him? Is that okay?"

"Of course it's okay."

"But what if I change my mind?"

"Then you change your mind," Ashton replied. At this point, however, he had decided to kill Jonah whether Clementine gave her 'permission' or not. It didn't matter if she wanted him to live. Jonah had to die, and he had to die tonight.

Bad things would happen if a Marked became the official leader of a camp, worse things than were already going to happen.

Even if it ruined his friendship with Clementine, he didn't care.

"... Alright." Clementine nodded.

"Just relax. Tonight... won't be easy, okay? Take all the time you need to process this." Ashton knew that this wasn't an easy decision for Clementine to make. Even if Jonah had been a terrible father to Clementine for her entire life, he was still her father.

He just hoped that she made the right choice, because otherwise, this would all be far harder.
