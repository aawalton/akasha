Everyone knows I’m from the Liu Clan. I came first out of a thousand disciples in the sect examinations, and I’m the most renowned newcomer in the sect. Ideally, I shouldn’t have to hold back against this brat Tez. But, if I beat the shit out of him, the victory would be meaningless. I have to consider how far the absurd slanders have spread around me as well. Well, I’m only reducing the capacity of my Qi Reserves.

Either way, I’d win.

Shuren didn’t move from his spot.

He stood with both hands behind his back, and only with a slow movement did he bring the left hand out, waving his index finger, beckoning Tez with a smirk.

“Come. I’ll allow you the firs-”

Tez covered the distance between them in a breath, raising his sword high up to aim for a downwards cleave. But when he slashed down, he was met with no resistance.

Jeers and boos resounded from the crowd, shaming him for his sneaky tactic. But Tez wouldn’t care.

Only Shuren’s body, moved to the side, was staring down at Tez with cold eyes.

The gaze of a predator.

He twirled the Ruyi Staff in his hands with deftness, and shifted his left foot back. With a wide arc, the staff over his shoulder slammed right into Tez’s torso, slamming him a few metres away.

“This is how you do an ambush, fool,” Shuren taunted while bringing the staff in a javelin’s position.

And with those words, the air sliced as the staff flew towards Tez, but this time, Tez managed to squarely deflect the staff with his cold steel sword, rebounding the staff away.

Shuren had long moved, grabbing his staff back while closing the gap. He watched as Tez raised his weapon in an awkward, amateurish stance, his sword straight up in the sky.

Shuren gradually walked forth, holding a hand to his chin as he observed Tez’s position. “Do you… even know your sword stances?” As he talked, his right hand held the end of his Ruyi Staff with a single-handed low guard stance, almost holding it crudely like a sword.

Shuren sent out a simple, and casual sweep at his torso, keeping an eye on Tez’s movements. But Tez needed to pay all of his attention, transitioning his hilt around the side of his head with the blade pointed down toward Shuren; a hanging stance.

With difficulty, Tez managed to parry Shuren’s sweep, backing away once more.

Shuren almost laughed from the ludicrous performance. He’s putting on a play. There’s no way he’s this scared to take the offence. He’s overly faking his awkwardness.

Shuren strode forth with both hands leaning back, firmly grasping his Ruyi Staff over his right shoulder. With a wide slam, it clashed directly against Tez’s blade, pushing Tez back slightly. The heel of his leather shoes dug into the ground, and even his legs started to quiver.

I’ll break that trash weapon. See if you can fight then!

Shuren stabbed towards Tez with a feint, shifting his foot back to rotate his staff behind him, aiming for a wide arc sweep against Tez once more. He even bridged the gap closer with a side stance, once more beating against Tez’s rusted blade with a strike.

“Are you that scared, Tez? Or is your offence that pathetically weak? It didn’t seem like it when you beat my cousin to an inch of his life!” Shuren yelled on purpose, riling up the crowd to side alongside him.

This was the game he had to naturally play, if he wanted to slowly break away the slanders and hearsay surrounding him. Naturally the spectators in the height of their emotions would more likely listen to him.

People were more inclined to be invested emotionally with a good story.

If it were just Shuren completely demolishing Tez without even some playful banter, they might even fall for Tez’s side out of pity in terms of gossiping and rumours.

He had to play it up for the audience as well. To sway them to his side.

He had long learnt these simple tactics from his time amongst the Immortals in the Granxian Continent.

Shuren needed to take a deep breath.

They both kept their distance, Tez still holding his sword with both hands in a sloppy cross guard, slowly walking backwards in a defensive manoeuvre. Meanwhile, Shuren held his staff with a reverse gripped back stance. His staff had begun reverberating from the Qi he had gradually cycled within it, and he slowly strafed around Tez with the playful demeanour of a lion.

“I remember when you bought that sword for a single copper. Interesting how it turned out to be such a treasure,” Shuren brought up with such ease, but made sure to slam his staff against Tez’s sword, before continuing his leisurely walk.

Tez could only clench his jaws, narrowing his eyes at Shuren. It may seem like an arrogant display, but he's already figured out the nature of my fighting style just off that single exchange we had a few weeks ago. Gauging the extent of my offensive capabilities, how ridiculous… But I can use this. Tez thought, ready to play his little game of hostile banter.

“Is it your hobby to spend time stalking orphans of Cloud City? You did the same with those Vulfoan kids as well, didn’t you?”

“You… Everyone knows my father sponsors a school for the refugees from Yestervaul. Yet you would resort to such baseless slander?” Shuren snorted, waving away his lies. “It was almost as if you knew that the sword was special. Like you had an ability to gleam the true nature of it.” This time, Shuren threw a casual feint with a swing. Tez rotated his body at an angle to deflect it, but Shuren easily drew his staff back, instead stabbing Tez’s chest once more.

Shuren’s chest began beating faster.

“And now, within two weeks, you went from Stage 7 to Stage 9. It’s quite obvious you’ve found a backing, alongside a powerful Legacy. Yet you still pretend to be a clumsy ignorant orphan.” Shuren watched as Tez’s facial expressions eventually faltered, swallowing his spit and staring daggers at him.

“But you’ve made one major mistake. There is always a price for acquiring power with such speed.” Shuren examined the tight grip Tez held on the hilt of his sword. With a single-handed, overhead slam, he clashed once more with Tez, who barely managed to block it with a high guard. Shuren took a deep breath.

“You haven’t even learnt how to Qi Tether, have you?” This time Shuren moved, bearing his fangs at his prey. He slowly and casually broke down Tez’s defences, engaging with a mix of long sweeps, wide swings, and quick strikes.

He’s purposely retaining a defensive stance, without even taking the openings I give him. Yet, I doubt he’s doing this out of fear.

Shuren quickly backed off, though sweat began to trickle from his hair down his face, and he felt his blood pumping faster around his body. He had even begun to take deeper breaths.

And a strange sensation, like a hole in his Spirit Meridians was formed, slowly leaking out its contents. Even the limited capacity of Qi he had as a Stage 7 had dropped much lower than he’d expected. Shuren couldn’t help but furrow his eyebrows when he considered the reasons why.

So he sought to put it to the test.

Once more, Shuren entered within Tez’s range, but this time he feinted into a back stance, anticipating Tez’s sloppy slice. Shuren dodged it, with a giant smirk on his face. He even had the time to beckon with an open palm, provoking Tez to swing some more.

At the spectacle, the crowd went wild. Bets continued to go in, and it went from betting who would win to how fast Shuren would beat Tez.

“I made bets for you to survive over a 100 hits, so you better not kick the bucket Tez!”

“Xiaobo you idiot, you’re wasting your spirit stones. Look at him! It doesn’t matter if he has a strange sword, he’s not going to last any hits.”

Luo stood amongst them, staring a hole into Tez’s sword. “That sword, I should take it after he comes back. I didn’t expect it to be an actual treasure. I can just force him to say he gifted it to me, that’s right.” A malicious smile bore itself on Luo’s face. “Yes, it doesn’t matter if he beats that fool Wei, if I can just use Sarya to force him…”

Tez seemed to take the bait, continuing to slash and stab towards Shuren, but he weaved and dodged each of them with ease.

It’s that sword. It has a strange effect. I’ll need to make some space to disarm him.

This time Shuren slipped his foot backwards, and within a blink he brought out the Ruyi Staff in a low guard stance. With the staff almost getting heated from being charged up, he slammed Tez’s waist directly, causing him to fly back a few metres.

He guarded with his Qi just in time, otherwise his hips would’ve fractured.

But in that instance of Tez being in mid-air, he had sheathed his sword back in.

Within a single breath, Tez entered a mid-air ready stance, bringing his right arm back with the sheath of his antiquated sword, with his thumb carefully resting underneath the hilt. And even during the scorching midday, steaming cloudy breath drew out of Tez’s mouth as he exhaled. His thumb began trembling, like the weight of the sword was far too heavy for him to move, and even his left hand that hovered over the hilt struggled to move to remove the sword.

Then Shuren saw it. The flow of his Qi, over 90% of it gravitated towards just the thumb of his left hand.

A cold purple light gleamed for a moment across the Duelling Colosseum.

And that antiquated, rusty weapon disappeared in Tez’s hand. As if it never existed in this very world.

And though his body sensed nothing, his instincts told him to dodge the unseen move.

Shuren’s eyes constricted.
