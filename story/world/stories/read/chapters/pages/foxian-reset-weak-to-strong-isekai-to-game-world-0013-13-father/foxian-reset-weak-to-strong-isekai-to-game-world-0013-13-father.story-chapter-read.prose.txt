Father fell to his knees. Kae was so engrossed in the battle between them that she didn't even bother to pick up the loot around her. She rushed over and stood beside her partner. There, they faced the broken man on his hands and knees.

"I failed as a husband, as a father, and as a follower of Rexia. How many people did I damn to hell?"

Silvia moved up to the demonic Father. From what Kae remembered of this scene, it had been completely different. Gerado would fade away, they would pick up what he dropped, and continue the quest. But everything in this was new, as if the story had been crafted specifically for Silvia.

"I don't think you were a failure, Gerado. I'm sure your wife loved you, and so did your daughter."

The acolyte slowly approached with her rod still tightly gripped in her hand. The zombified Father looked up at Silvia. The gentleness in her eyes warmed the heart he long thought lost...

"R-Really?"

Silvia waited, turned back to Kae, then back to the man before her.

"This game is weird. I'm not sure why you can talk back to me and even answer me. But, I understand you, Father Gerado. I wish I could see my loved one again, too."

Father shook his head as his broken body started to wither away in the Yale Forest wind.

"Oh, young one. I did horrible things to those who followed me for those loved ones. You're right... I am selfish, despite how much I love them."

He looked at Silvia, like a mentor passing his wisdom to the young acolyte just starting her long journey. Silvia bent down and looked Father Gerado in his worn, tired eyes.

"Maybe thinking about the good times we had with our loved ones would have brought peace to us, Father. Maybe..."

The Father smiled softly.

"Y-You know, maybe you're right, young acolyte. I should have looked at the good… and not everything I lost because they were gone."

Father Gerado vanished, and the truth of the Yale Forest was laid bare for the duo to witness. A grieving father, desperate to see his loved ones again, had done atrocious things to those who had faith in him. His wish for his family to be brought back alive created a place full of the damned...

But the ending to this story was different than the one Kae experienced. That was likely due to the [Sentient] buff... At least, that's what Kae wanted to believe. Silvia stood up and turned to Kae...

"Huh?"

Kae was surprised to see... tears in Silvia's eyes.

"Silvia?"

"Oh, sorry, Kae. Ah… I'm crying right now."

Kae walked over to her character. Silvia closed her eyes and covered her face in shame. Those tears, coming from the person outside of the character Silvia, reflected her emotions in the game world.

"I-Is everything okay?"

Kae reached out and... gently touched her shoulder.

"N-No, it's not okay. I-I didn't expect to… relive any of that right now."

Silvia fell to her knees. Kae did the same with her, getting down to her level. They were safe on this map for the time being. Still, Kae couldn't believe her eyes, watching Silvia's tears fall to the dead grass.

"Nnggg…"

Silvia coughed, clenched her teeth, and closed her eyes again. It made Kae's heart twist to see such emotions. It was one thing to experience Father Gerado's pain during the story previously. But... It's been a long time since she'd experienced another human's raw feelings of pain and anguish.

"I-It's okay. Take your time,... Ah, I'll be here, Silvia."

Words like those rarely came from Kae's lips outside of Keostar Online. They even stumbled around awkwardly in her head before she let them out. But now, as part of this world, Kae at least wanted to try to do what she could never do outside of it.

After a moment of crying into the palms of her hands, the acolyte lifted her head and turned back to her new friend. Her face was flushed red, burning with sadness and guilt.

"This all just reminded me... of..."

The acolyte sighed deeply before opening her heart up.

"You see, Kae, my father... Ah, my dad passed away a few months ago. And Keostar Online was his favorite game. He played since day one."

Silvia sat down and held her knees. There, she hid her face in her thighs as she confessed the turmoil in her heart.

"Oh, I'm sorry to hear that."

Kae rubbed her back, even though Silvia couldn't feel it in the real world. But she was hoping it would give her a little comfort, if anything.

"I've been so lost for so long... So, I came on Keostar Online to see why my dad liked this game so much. I couldn't find his passwords, though. So, I started a new character instead."

Silvia glanced over at Kae as her tears kept falling down her cheeks.

"I-It hasn't been easy, though. T-There have been so many questions that I've had about this game. B-But I can't ask him anything anymore. A-And that realization started to set in this morning."

Silvia confessed with a trembling voice.

"That must have been hard for you, Silvia. It sounds like to me that you just want to get closer to your dad somehow."

The ash-haired acolyte lifted her head slightly and looked Kae in the eyes. The fox's stomach tensed up at this. Everything about Silvia... reminded her of that day she wanted to forget about.

"Y-Yeah, yeah! I just wanted to be closer to my dad. And, slowly, I'm starting to see why he likes this game. I just wish I played it with him when he asked me to."

Silvia hid her face in her hands. The shame of it all was catching up to her again.

"I wish I could have stopped being stubborn and... tried this silly game out with him while he was still alive."

She let out a soft whimper in the cold forest. It was clear that Kae needed to do something to cheer her up. So, she grabbed her hands and looked her in the eyes, too.

"Hey, I bet he'd be happy to hear that you like this game too, though, Silvia. A-Ah, do you mind if I ask what his character's name was? if you know, that is."

Silvia paused for a bit longer than expected. Awkwardly, Kae waited there until the eyes of the acolyte lit up. The corners of her cheeks rose slightly in a warm smile.

"M-Melkio Kelster. Dad wrote it down at his computer desk. I wanted to say his name right. T-This is the spelling of it."

"Huh, Kelster."

The image of a burly man with dark short hair and bulky armor came to mind. The memory of a paladin standing over the sunset of a certain guild map rushed into Kae's mind.

"Was he a Human Paladin, right, Silvia?"

"Yeah, he was! The guy with super bulky armor and... stuff."

She perked up, nodding feverishly. To her, it must have been a silly-looking character. But... Melkio was pretty busted, from what Kae remembered.

"I remember him. We used to be in the same guild together around five years ago."

"Really?!"

Kae nodded, a hint of nostalgia took over her heart...

"Yeah. Melkio... was a smart guy who talked a lot about fishing, reading, and…"

The corners of Kae's lips rose gently as that tender old memory that faded into the obscurity of the past resurfaced in her mind.

"...About his child and how smart they were in school."

Those bright eyes lit up, and a flush of embarrassment washed over Silvia's face. There was no doubt about it from Kae's perspective... Everything Silvia felt had merged and manifested within the game.

"Huh? H-He said that?"

"Yeah, I remember we had this chat for hours about his family. I didn't realize how old he was until we opened up to the guild about it, though."

Kae thought about that day, when they all had an after-party after a raid. She was on her main, Ray Belmont. Around 25 players were sitting in the open field, chatting about life outside of Keostar...

"Today, my kid made the honor roll again. I don't know where they get their brains from, but it ain't me though. I nearly flunked out of school."

Melkio jeered, laughing all the while.

"Geez, how old are you, Melkio?"

A guildmate asked as he fixed up some gear in his menu.

"Probably old enough to be YOUR dad. But I don't care. I plan to give them a big present this year, though! I'm getting my kid a gaming PC. It's going to be better than mine."

Again, the older man laughed. Ray just sat there, enjoying the relaxing mood in the corner.

"Well, if you do get your kid a PC, get them into Keostar, Melkio. We need more overpowered players to steamroll high-level FAs with."

Ray said as he finished gearing up his own character.

"Ray's at it again, trying to recruit anyone to play this game. He's always doing that."

Melkio laughed, and Ray just shrugged.

"I only want people to know how fun this game is. And if your kid is anything like you, Melkio, they will love it."

Melkio paused for a moment, then spoke up.

"Anything like me? ...Yeah, I'll try my best, Ray!"

Kae opened her eyes as she finished her short tale. The memory of Meliko tinged in her heart. How she missed the simplicity of those guild days. The days when she needed people to fight and win in this world.

"I remember that year... Ah, dad gave me a PC, and I didn't use it. I was too busy doing other things to give it a chance."

Silvia paused for a bit, her feet curling up together as her eyes slanted bitterly.

"You know, I just turned this PC on a few weeks ago. It's been sitting there for that long. How I wish I had used it when my dad was still alive."

"Silvia."

Silvia didn't respond for a while after that. So, Kae just sat there with her on the peaceful lakeside. She wanted to be there for her friend when she came back...

"Sorry, I had to step away from the game for a bit. Are you still there, Kae?"

"Yep, I'm still here. How about we warp back to town? It sounds like you need a break."

Kae opened a blue portal with a [Warp Stone]. They both stood up and entered it, taking them out of the Yale Forest.

The field was twilight, worlds different than the dreary forest. The fox could feel the sun wash on her face, a much-needed sensation after being trapped in that darkness for so long. The horizon of the vast world looked before them, reminiscent of that day five years ago with her old guild. The orange dawn never looked so captivating to Kae before.

"I'm sorry for dumping all that on you, Kae. You've been dealing with my outburst a lot today. Haha..."

Silvia tried to sound strong, but... it fell flat in the end. Awkwardly, Kae grabbed her fluffy tail and smiled back at her.

"Don't be. We are playing a game. But sometimes life just comes up, and we need someone to talk to about it."

The acolyte nodded.

"Sometimes, it's hard to put feelings aside, and it all just comes out in ways we don't expect."

Silvia stopped talking for a moment, and Kae assumed she stepped away again. But suddenly, she perked up and asked…

"W-Why are you so nice, Kae?"

"Am I?"

"Y-Yes! You're very nice. And I want to keep playing with you if I can. I... I want to keep seeing why Dad liked this game so much."

Kae put both hands on her hips. She might not be great at talking about other people's emotions, but... she could show someone a good time while playing Keostar.

"Then, when you log back in, I'll be there to play with you! You're part of my Rexia killing crew now, like it or not!"

She giggled.

"Yeah. That's right. You have that wacky lore…"

She paused...

"But then again... Rexia did show up and..."

Before she could fall back into the despair of it all, Silvia washed her mind of that strange battle and turned back to her fox partner.

"You won't die, right? I mean, you said if you die, you really die and won't be this character anymore."

There was fear lingering in her words. So, to show confidence, Kae pushed out her chest and grinned with that cocky smile the Goddess above started to hate so much.

"I have no plans to die. I need to kill Rexia to make this game better."

The acolyte chuckled.

"You better not. If you die, I'll hunt down your new character and make sure we stay friends... at least."

The acolyte rubbed the back of her head and gave Kae a wave.

"That's my lore, Kae. I want to fall in love with everything my dad loved about this world. I... I want to tell him that I loved things about Keostar, like I did one day."

Silvia confessed.

"It's a great lore, Silvia."

With a deep sigh, Silvia rubbed her tears away and smiled back at Kae.

"Bye for now. I'm going to thank my dad for the PC like I should have all those years ago... And go to sleep. I'm freaking exhausted now."

With an exasperated sigh, Silvia slumped her shoulders.

"Bye. I'll see you when you want to play again, Silvia."

"Bye! Thanks again! I... I had a lot of fun!"

Silvia logged off.

Kae waved, and once Silvia vanished, her hand stopped mid-motion as she thought about it all...

"Melkio stopped playing a few months ago. I didn't know it was because he passed away. Honestly, I stopped talking to him years ago."

The fox closed her eyes...

"Yeah, around the time when everything became more efficient... to just do it alone."

Kae pondered the journey it had taken to reach that moment. To be on top of the world...

"Melkio liked this game and wanted it to be better, too. So, I can't just change this world for myself. But for anyone else who likes it and wants it to be better. To be a better escape from reality..."

She looked up into the twilight sky, worlds different than the gloomy one of Yale Forest.

"I want to play with Silvia again sometime. I had a lot of fun, too."
