“What the fuck…” I begin.

Standing atop rubble and holding a suit by the neck is a man of large proportions. I recognize his attire and his mask immediately.

He’s one of the WHA’s six heroes… Starman.

“Ah, you’re finally back!” Starman booms, he looks at the suit in his hand and then back at us, “Oh, this is… well, I can explain.”

He releases the suit and sets him down slowly, dusting off the man’s shoulders as he heaves for breath holding his throat. Starman turns and gives us a thumbs up and a very practiced smile then slaps the man on the back, so hard that he tumbles down the rubble. Starman’s smile flickers for a moment.

“Welcome home!” Starman says with raised arms, “There’s much to discuss.”
