[Contract | Flower Pixie - Level 6 -> Flower Pixie - Level 7]

Species – Orc

Time of Death – July 5th, 14:07

Cause of Death – Fracture of C3 to C6 Vertebrae

Circumstances of Decay – Location F3, Application of Serum AB-09

That way, when a dead body is found, they can figure out how long it's been since somebody died. Helps find bad guys," Ashton explained. "Though, I think here, they have a different purpose."

The main differences that Ashton saw on the labels that were of note were the 'Circumstances of Decay'. 'Location F3' was obviously just this place here, so that never changed, but the most important part was the 'Serum'.

Were they trying to figure out a way how to take control over the changes in how fast bodies rotted? That actually seemed quite useful.

It would help in researching the bodies of monsters safely if they could slow the decay down, and at the same time, with the disposal of killed monsters if they were able to speed the process up.

Pretty damn clever, actually. For now, Ashton inspected a couple of the corpses, trying to focus on whatever this 'serum' was so that the Pen of Insight might tell him a little bit later. Maybe he could help those guys out.
