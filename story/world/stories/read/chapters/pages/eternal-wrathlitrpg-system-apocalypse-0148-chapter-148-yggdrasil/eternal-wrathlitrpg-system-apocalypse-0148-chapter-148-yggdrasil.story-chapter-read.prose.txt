Several ice spears spawned around him, shooting at the infested flowers.

“Oi! It’s dangerous. Don’t stand so close to them!” James reached out for the Count. But it was too late. The cockroaches swarmed from the flowers, engulfing the Count. The flowers kept spawning cockroaches. His death throes were drowned out by the skittering of their legs.

“…That’s the worst way to go,” James muttered, praying for the Count’s soul to rest in peace.

He then burned away the remaining cockroaches with his fire breath. He also protected himself with an ice dome. After the area was cleansed, a vial of yellow liquid was left behind inside one of the flowers. He then looted the Count’s body. The cockroaches had eaten every last part of him; only his sword and armor were left behind.
