"Bea...trix?" Arne asks in awe, his piercing blue gaze taking me in hungrily.

My eyelids suddenly start to droop, and I note that my limbs feel very heavy and weak. Shifting like that...flying...it was the most exhilarating feeling, but it seems like it took a lot out of me.

You stayed in your avian form too long Wanderer, you will need to build your endurance, Freyja scolds. You are lucky that you shifted back just in time. If you had waited just a moment longer, you would have been lost. No longer human.

My eyes widen at the revelation and I shudder.

I would have lost my humanity?

"Fuck! Are you alright?" Arne mistakes my involuntary body shakes for shivers and wraps his arms around me tightly, rubbing my shoulders to bring them warmth. "I was so worried, Light Elf, you disappeared before my very eyes!"

"I...sorry about that," I answer, my voice hoarse and raspy, like I haven't used it in ages. "I was...taken away to perform a test. By Heimdall."

Arne's gaze briefly darkens at that, but then he softens it as he roves it over me again.

"I see...Are you harmed?"

"Just a scratch...it's not too..." I pause and frown, looking for the slice I received from my encounter with the corpse. It's not on my arm. I brush my fingers over where it should be on my bicep, but there's nothing but smooth skin. "Huh..that's odd. I guess I'm fine after all."

Arne studies where I touched my arm, then me again, before bringing back the lighthearted grin I've become fond of.

"I am glad to hear that. Now that's sorted..." he starts, "would you like to tell me where your clothes went and where this lovely cloak came from?" he adds, brushing his fingertips along the soft plumes.

I tug the cloak around me, trying to cover myself better, but Arne's grin only gets wider. He makes no effort to help me out of the tub and we are still entangled together. Those piercing blue eyes glitter in amusement as they watch me struggle.

He tentatively reaches for me, stroking a thumb along my chin, and I freeze.

"It's not too late you know...to change your mind," Arne says as his gaze drops hungrily to my lips. "You could choose me..."

SQUUAKKK!!

Huginn flies in abruptly, and we become a mess of feathers, limbs and hair as Arne whips his head back and attempts to swat him away. Huginn dodges and pecks at Arne's fingers.

"Arghhh! Blasted bird!"

Thanks, Huginn! Perfect timing.

I use the opportunity to untangle myself from Arne and step out of the tub. My legs feel wobbly, and I stumble a bit on unsure feet, having to grab the edge of Arne's bed for support.

That's...not great. Another side effect from shapeshifting?

Satisfied with his interruption, Huginn flaps over to my shoulder, nuzzling into the crook of my neck. A disgruntled Arne huffs as he starts climbing out of the tub after me.

"Ack..." Arne spits out one of Huginn's feathers. "Are you sure you are alright, Beatrix? You seem a bit unsteady on your feet," he adds, picking off more feathers off his shoulder and tunic. His top braid is also disheveled, fly aways poking out each and every way from the skirmish. I've got to admit, the sight is quite comical and I fail to hide the beginning of a smile.

"Yes...I think so."

Arne notices my grin, huffs a laugh of his own and smooths his top braid with his hands in a dramatic fashion.

"Good. Now...Are we going to talk about the fact that you were a falcon just a few moments ago?" he adds, rising an eyebrow.

My grin falters.

"Ah...sooo you did notice that, huh?" I say coyly.

Arne snorts. "Aye, I sure did Light Elf." Those sparkling blue eyes turn calculating. "A spoil...from passing your test?"

After a moment, I nod. I don't think I should go around freely telling others about this new ability just yet, but I also can't lie to Arne about it when he literally saw it with his own eyes.

"I'm still...getting used to it," I say, honestly.

Arne laughs merrily.

"That's wonderful, Beatrix! I can't wait to see my brother's face when you surprise him with it." He starts rubbing his hands together menacingly. "Oh...have I some ideas that should really make him fall off his horse," he adds with a wink.

"I could do what I did earlier with you...that seemed to work."

"Ayee woman...." Arne retorts, pointing a finger at me playfully. "I suppose you are right though. That was pretty startling, well done. Now..."

Arne grunts as he leaps, back first on his bed, and laces his arms above his head to use for a pillow. He crosses his legs and gazes up at me expectantly.

"Get changed, and then tell me more about this test that you performed and how it went. I want to know everything!"

I laugh, warmth filling my belly and heart, and I indulge him, because honestly, I really wanted to tell someone about it.

Twin blades whistle through the air simultaneously, metal heels rotating over wood knobs in rapid arcs, before sinking into the targets with a satisfying crack that echoes throughout the field.

"Damn...." I can't help myself from saying in appreciation, as I admire the results of Torsten's double axe throw 30 feet from the target. Not only did they hit at the same exact time and stick, but they are equally level and within the same circle.

Now that's fucking precision.

"Aye...weh call that one theh kill shot," Torsten says.

Arne whistles behind me in satisfaction. It's been a couple weeks since Froðe left for the scouting trip with Sigrid and the others, and I've begun to get anxious about his return, wondering if he's alright, though Arne has assured me this is normal for scouting missions and of course Froðe can handle himself. In the meantime, Torsten and Arne have been keeping me busy with my training, and insisted I was ready to include axe throwing in my repertoire today.

"But dinnae worry Lass, weh'll start yeh with somethin' more simple."

Torsten walks over to the target, and wiggles each axe up and down to dislodge it from the wood. Wow, they were really in there. A solid chunk rips off from the movement and falls to the ground as he wiggles out the second one.

Nice and sharp. What could posssibly go wrong? I wonder with morbid curiosity.

My analyst brain tells me I should be cautious, maybe even afraid? But I can't help but feel drawn to wield the twin blades, eager to have the wooden handles in my hands so I can hurl them at the target. There's a hunger inside of me, a desire to become a deathly tool myself that is so strong it makes my heart race in excitement.

Again, I ask myself, what am I becoming?

"Al'rght Lass, weh'll start yeh with one first," Torsten calls over to me, motioning me over and breaking me from my thoughts. "Le's have yeh hold it weth both yer hands, over yer head, butt of the blade sitting between yer shoulder blades."

He demonstrates, his massive arms overhead while the back of the axe sits flush on his back, just between his shoulder blades. He has one foot out in front, like he's about to take a step.

"Think abou' releasin when 'at eye level, othr' wise yeh will release too soon an' it won' stick."

I nod, studying his form as he takes a step, most likely with his dominant foot, and releases the axe in another swooping spiral that sings through the air before it hits true, in the exact center of the target. We are now about 15 feet away.

Torsten once again walks up and dislodges the axe, before his lumbering gait brings him back toward us. The blade flashes in the late afternoon sunlight as he hands the axe over to me, wooden knob first. I grasp it, my right, dominant hand on top with thumb up, left hand below, just like he showed me.

"Make sure yeh step first, then let 'er fly!"

My eyes narrow in on the target in front of me, and I take in a deep breath. Then, after taking a step, I throw, exhaling with a grunt while I 'let her fly.' After rotating almost a full circle, the butt of the axe hits the target and ricochets off towards the ground.

I frown down at it, mocking me from the grass.

"Yeh released too soon! Try again!"

So I do. Again. And again. And again. But still nothing sticks.

"Now yer steppin' too late, Lass!" Torsten admonishes.

In my frustration, I throw so hard that the axe ricochets nearly back to my feet, causing me to leap to the side awkwardly, earning me a snicker from Arne. I whip my head around to shoot daggers with my eyes, but that only makes his grin grow wider, his hand only partially concealing it in a playful manner, blue eyes twinkling. I narrow my eyes into slits, and he laughs.

"Ah, Torsten, if you will, let me step in."

I let out a sigh and my shoulders slump, but Arne gently pushes my shoulders, backing me up a step or two, then coaxes my arms back into position. Once the butt of the axe is on my back, Arne pushes down on my elbows, forcing it lower.

"That's it Light Elf, now, use your step to gain power and momentum to start your throw. Eye your target, and release at eye level," he whispers in my ear, a little to closely, in my opinion. "Also..." did he just get closer? Goosebumps pebble my skin at his breath tickling my ear. "Remember to breathe, Beatrix."

"I'd breathe better if I wasn't worried about accidentally chopping off your nose as I throw this!" I snap back, which causes Torsten to elicit a booming laughter.

"Aye, giv' er some space, Arne! Woul' want that 'andsome face of yers to get hacked!"

Arne smirks, but backs away, arms crossed, then nods at me encouragingly. I take another deep breath, step, and hurl the axe, noting that it makes a full 360 in the air this time before lodging itself into the third ring of the target. Ah, so it was a distance thing. And timing, my analyst brain notes.

"About fucking time!" I breathe, but I'm grinning ear to ear. Torsten whoops and throws his fist in the air. Arne slaps me on the back.

"Good Light Elf. Now, do it again...and when you get the hang of it...we'll have you throw one handed," Arne says.

A couple hours later, my arms feel like jelly and I can no longer lift them above my head, but remarkably, I'm able to do my own version of the double axe throw. Juuuust not as far away of course as Torsten, still at my 16 foot mark, and not as precise, with one blade a few inches higher than the other, but honestly I'll take it. It was enough to earn a celebratory bear hug that nearly crushed my ribs from Torsten, and a resulting cackle from me as he spun me around and around in the air.

They offer to walk me back to the Tavern but I politely decline, eyeing the feather cloak I have safely draped on another nearby target. There's something I need to work on first.

Again, even though I can't seem to replicate it since that one night.

Torsten grunts, but nods knowingly, and saunters off to whatever duties he has left for the day. I'm sure he wishes he was on the scouting mission, but Froðe insisted that he stay behind to keep an eye on me and further train me, so he took Sigrid instead to be his right hand. From what I know of her fierceness, she will do just fine. A twang of jealously still claws at me, though I'm not sure if it's for how capable she is as a shield maiden in comparison to me or if it's because of her beauty and her proximity to Froðe.

Stop being stupid, I scold myself.

Arne, glued to my side as always, lingers and continues to offer me very unhelpful feedback on how to shape shift.

"Have you tried picturing yourself as the falcon?" he asks for like, the umpteenth time.

Seriously?! Do you really think I haven't???

Huginn, returning after some foraging, plops down out of the sky and unceremoniously swoops down at Arne's head, earning a unpleasant yelp from him.

"Ack! Why do you always do that?!"

Ekk Ekkk EKK! Huginn croaks back at him, dropping an opalescent pebble on the ground. Another one of his many treasures, I'm sure.

I smirk. I fucking love that bird.

Turning my attention back inward, I sigh and close my eyes. Just like every day in the past couple weeks, I try to recreate the exact steps I took to shape shift the night of Heimdall's test. But it doesn't work. Not even a tingle is felt.

I'm at a loss..and starting to lose the high that I felt from that double axe throw in the face of this defeat.

"You may not want to hear this Light Elf...but there is someone in our clan who would be more qualified to help you in this endeavor."

No.

"Perhaps it is time to look past what happened...in the past? Move on and make up?"

I open one eye and shoot Arne another glare.

"Absolutely not."

Arne shrugs. "Is she not the one responsible for helping you strengthen this connection with Freyja? She would know how to help."

Freyja. For whatever reason, she also has been silent since that night. Dormant. I know she's still there, but I don't feel her words anymore.

Arne is not wrong, but I don't want to admit it out loud. Skuld and I, we haven't been on good terms since our blow out weeks ago. I haven't visited her block house since. Thankfully, Iona and Sorcha have come to visit me each day which I am grateful for. Iona also looks happy with her new lifestyle, glowing even, these days. It's been lovely to witness. And although Skuld and I are on bad terms, Sorcha talks of her nonstop, enamored with her new friend and protector.

"I could...go with you and help act as a intermediary," Arne offers with a sly grin. "I hate to see two beautiful women so at odds with one another."

I roll my eyes, but I consider his proposal. Skuld would likely know what I'm missing in my shapeshifting training, and how to reawaken my connection with Freyja.

"Alright...I'll think on it. But first, I need a trip to the Bath House and some food."

Arne stands up enthusiastically. "Couldn't agree more, Light Elf!"

"Ah, Ah, Ah..." I hold a finger up, "By myself, Arne."
