A glorious battle they’ve waged nonetheless.

The king of the fae began dispatching death knights on his path to the champion, though he was not quite ‘in the play’ they were arranging. Nonetheless, it was clearing space.

Gelmak watched the greatest warriors of the humans, naugites, and fae move in before him, striking to kill but knowing them to effectively be feints against such a super-capable foe. Entangling vines from Redberry slammed and wrapped, but were soundly destroyed in the counter defense. Swords were parried or merely scraped on armor. Explosions of magic threw up some earth, causing distractions and pivots. One of his brothers of the north, a heavily-armored minotaur going by the handle of ‘Squasher,’ moved in with mace and shield, scoring another graze.

The fists of Zadkiel finally came for the champion. The undead champion dodged one fist and then another, but the barrage was part of a furious charge with the wraith already off-balance, and a third strike came on the inside of the sword’s guard, punching the groin plate nearly square with a thunderous impact. The wraith was thrown and scrambled to recover.

The zone of recovery was right into Gelmak’s reach, the timing right as he was charging and already wound up.

Now is my chance.

The hammer came down. Gelmak moved his axe in the way of the obvious desperation parry, preventing it from stopping the unerring swing of the hammer. The sword and axe connected first with a tremendous clang and the spark of clashing magics. The undead champion used the last of his off-balance effort to push with all his strength, to throw Gelmak off, and even with the sort of force that would completely toss even a strong warrior completely to the dirt.

Gelmak Por, however, was not merely a strong warrior: he was among the strongest on the planet. Moreover, he was buffed with several layers of assisting enchantments. When he braced his muscles, when he growled with fierce determination, and his hooves dug into the ground, he met force with equal force and wasn’t moved.

The hammer fell and could not be avoided, right into the wraith's vulture skull helmet. The impact from the specialist weapon had to be among the greatest raw forces unleashed in a singular moment. The sound was like a clap of lightning, and the beak of the helmet was completely shattered, as the force carried through and sent the undead champion slamming into the dirt. Sadly, the wraith was slippery enough to avoid the carry-through that would've smashed the skull in further, and Gelmak had to pull the heavy strike short, into the beginning of a long recovery.

Gelmak was primed to follow up, but noticed the whip curling at him at the last second. He pivoted to move his axe in the way… and realized he wouldn’t block in time.

Oh dear. I might be dead.

A blur of movement interposed itself, and there was the capable human fighter, Odysseus. He threw his sword into the whip ends nearest, arresting their momentum. As he did so, he released the blade and threw himself bodily backwards into Gelmak. Gelmak received the impact and allowed it to knock him backwards as well.

Narrowly, the graze of the last whip-heads flaring out missed Odysseus. Gelmak finally caught himself before they both tumbled to the ground, flinging the human to the side so he could make space for himself.

Meanwhile, the sword had been entirely disintegrated, sacrificed as it was.

Despite the striking whip, the wraith was still on the ground, and getting swarmed with follow-up attacks with that whip rather out of play temporarily. Another minotaur with a maul managed to stop a retreat with a slammed down strike, smashing and denting a boot. A sword slashed the helm, sending more damaged material flying. Zadkiel pounced, punching a dent right in the chest plate.

The armor piles left of the slain death knights rose, as a new filling spawned within them in erratic fits: smoking voids. Each a pantomime of the undead champion, they also carried swords much like his, which they shifted to hold like greatswords.

“Oh, that’s not good,” Zadkiel muttered.

In furious assault after furious assault, needing extensive backup each time, Samantha systematically executed the lich support units. It wasn’t easy; it was probably great credit for another quest, though she was ignoring those messages until it was all over. Eight out of twelve were destroyed, and high casualties were dealt to their support structures. Their oomph in helping out the champion was seriously hampered, that was for sure. Meanwhile, the Counter Crew was doing well. Without question, the Antimages had expended a ton of their resources, but they had more, and seemingly had gotten over the hump.

A sudden change on the main front made her pause and turn to see. All the death knights they’d put down had been animated and were attacking again — or their suits were. Counterattacks weren’t working. Their insides were the same void as the champion, and they wielded blades of what appeared to be a lesser Entropy effect, damaging armor and flesh without much recourse to heal it back. The same effect was now on the champion’s blade, but he was dancing away, playing defensive… which was going to be quite difficult to deal with on top of the rest.

Time to be the relief, I suppose. They can’t be as strong as he is.

As soon as she moved, she felt a sudden shift. All of a sudden, a vast slurry of dispelling magic came at her, from dozens of angles at once. She had no choice but to desperately readjust and help block it, as the rest of the Counter Crew scrambled to do the same.

She didn’t quite have enough for multiple nasty buff-stripping effects — but Mr. Agatolis expended something at the last moment, something she’d never seen, that enshrouded her in a small, spherical Antimagic shield. All of the dispels fizzled against it.

In her mind, Mr. Agatolis said, “Ten seconds only. You have to hide before then. Minimize the angles.”

Right on the heels of all this, she felt worse coming, designed to immediately follow the sudden targeting, which they could count on because she’d been exposed for many minutes now. It was an attack, a pull of Sympathetic strings from afar, tugging and ripping for her mind. Incredibly strong — too strong for her resistances, threatening to end her. The antimagic was totally bypassed.

Of course. The traps have been sprung. So it begins.

The first mental rip was unraveled and canceled thanks to her newer protection spell, Trilogy Fusion Soul, and its special wound negations. And then a second later, another strike raked at her mind, from a separate source. It did the same thing, at the same intensity level. It was also countered thanks to the second wound negation.

Quickly, she realized what they were doing — multiple coordinated rituals enhancing mind-based attacks, one after the other, to overwhelm her defenses. She had no idea how many they could wage that could wound her, how many teams they had, or whether they could attack multiple times at this level.

We’re about to find out.
