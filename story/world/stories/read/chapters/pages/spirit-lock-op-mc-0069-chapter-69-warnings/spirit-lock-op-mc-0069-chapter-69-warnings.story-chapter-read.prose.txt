Or worse.

That said, it was easier said than done. If more people came after him—

They would die.

This rift wasn't a political playground—it was a nightmare that rifters in his last life failed to conquer. There were over three hundred deaths within the rift, and far more during the cleanup. This wasn't a place to play games. He had to treat every move with absolute deference and care.
