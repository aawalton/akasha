Aura definitely was a combination of qi and shen, he could feel the flow of the energy in the array eroding his consciousness nearly as fast as it burned away his energy. It was fine. Her ability to read the energy flows had always been better than his, perhaps that was the reason for it, or perhaps it was a spear daoist’s instinct. It wasn’t as simple as smoothing a path or yanking a stray bit of aura one way or the other. Aura was damned difficult to get a grip on. You could steer it, with some care, but controlling it was orders of magnitude harder. He felt like he was trying to control thousands of streams of mercury with thousands of hair sized hammers.

The array wasn’t nearly the largest Tian had ever seen. Not even a hundredth of the largest ones. It was, however, the most complex one he had ever been personally involved with. A bit like a human body. He didn’t need to know the why behind everything so long as he had a pretty good idea of how it was supposed to look. He had better spells now, but none he had properly practiced. It was time for the Demon Pulling Art to step forth once more.

The demon pulling art relied on minutely controlling one’s vital energy to draw out harmful qi inside a body. Usually the body’s own, or an infection, or a poison. You just had to match your qi to what you were pulling out, then subtly tweak it as it shifted through different organs, as each organ would have different qi profiles, and those profiles were by no means consistent through the organ in terms of density or elemental distribution.

Really, a brute force job luring streams of aura around was comparatively simple. Exhausting, yes, due to the way the aura ground down his mental energy. That, and maintaining more than ten streams at a time. It was less exhausting than trying to bash away at everything, though, and that counted for a very great deal.
