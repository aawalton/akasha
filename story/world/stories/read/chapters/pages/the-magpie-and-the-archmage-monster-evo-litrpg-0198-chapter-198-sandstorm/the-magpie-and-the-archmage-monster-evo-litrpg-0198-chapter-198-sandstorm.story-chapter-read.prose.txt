The song actually started slow. If it could be called a song, because it had no words.

Rapid tututututu.

Soft electric beat.

Tututututu.

More and more. Stronger and stronger.

Until the beat of Sandstorm by Darude was echoing through the ravine. Renir was hesitant to do anything at first, but then Liora could feel him pour mana into her.

He guided it through her body and let it free in tune with the beat. With each tututututu, a burst of crackling electricity sparked through the air.

It wasn't a perfect method. Far from it. Liora could feel some of the electricity discharged into her throat. It hurt. But Renir quickly adjusted his magic flow until they were both in sync.

Liora flew over the battlefield, blasting the music as loudly as Resonance Mimicry allowed her, while Renir discharged electricity through her voice along with the beat.

The bats convulsed and fell like flies hit by bug spray. Their screeching stopped, which brought everyone else out of their stupor.

Smokey savagely tore at every bat that he could reach. With his powerful jaws, it was like tearing paper. Bits and pieces of bat flew everywhere.

Cee managed to reach Caspian and put out the flames of the surrounding webs with her Aqua Genesis. It appeared the merman got slightly singed in the process. Nonetheless, he didn't loiter around and instantly began singing.

It wasn't the same song as before. This one was made purely to protect himself and Cee from Liora's electrifying Resonance Mimicry.

While she was trying to direct the sound waves at the bats, sound wasn't exactly something she could order not to go somewhere. Caspian, Cee, and Smokey also got affected by Liora's attack, even if not as badly as the bats she was directly chasing.

Smokey also didn't appear as affected as much, but it was likely before his Elemental Resistance trait. Elemental magic in general wouldn't work on him, and fire and heat wouldn't cause him any damage at all thanks to Heat Nullification.

While Caspian protected himself and Cee from the electric damage, Liora and Smokey continued dealing with the remaining bats. Some of them died from the previously inflicted burns. The majority were torn to shreds by Smokey, who looked quite angry at the bats.

But Liora also got a good chunk. Ignoring the pain caused by the electricity, she tore the bats to shreds using Raptor Lunge. Fun fact: she could use Resonance Mimicry and Raptor Lunge at the same time, although by the time she got close to any bats they were already heavily injured.

These guys couldn't handle electricity at all, even the little bit that was carried by soundwaves.

With that, the last of the bats was defeated. Liora landed next to Caspian and Cee. The web was still lightly burning in places Cee hadn't doused. Smokey was having the time of his life, crunching on the remains of the bats.

Liora wondered whether they should stop him, but decided against it. He needed a lot of food. They would save the stuff in their inventory for later. One never knew when they might not have ready access to food, especially for a large ravenous wyvern.

"So… that went well…" Cee said, as she sprayed Caspian with water.

"Well? Well? That was an absolute disaster!" Renir shouted.

"I-It could have been worse," Liora croaked out. Her voice had gone completely hoarse, likely from the damage done by the electricity.

"You can't even speak properly. Holy shit, Caspian, please, heal her," Renir said.

"Yeah, we definitely need to think of a better plan next time. We're lucky those bats were so weak to electricity. If they hadn't been… well, it is safe to assume the result wouldn't be so great," the merman said as he walked up to Liora and touched her neck feathers.

She felt a warm wave wash over her body, healing her hurting throat.

"Thank you! Now I'm going to go and gather glimmerstones," Liora said. "Smokey, don't you dare to eat them!"

The wyvern lifted his head, letting out a confused squeak. He chewed thoughtfully and spat out several glimmerstones.

"Smokey, no! Bad wyvern! You can't eat glimmerstones!" Cee said, jumping to her pet's side.

Meanwhile, Renir kept shouting profanities, cursing them out for their ineptitude. Well, except for Caspian, since he wasn't a fighter.

Liora ignored him, instead snatching glimmerstones from the bats. There were 28 in each bat, 25 bats in total, which resulted in roughly 700. Or it should have been 700, but 20 or so were missing, likely inside Smokey's gullet already.

"How are we splitting the glimmerstones? Does Smokey need anything, because if so then he gets 20 less," Liora grumbled once she had collected everything and returned to Renir's side.

"No. It is the master that needs to purchase the skills for pets, although in Smokey's case, I'm not sure if it's a good idea…" Renir said, looking thoughtfully at the wyvern who was still gorging himself on bat meat.

"He's nearly cooking with that fiery breath of his. No new skills for the wyvern until he learns proper control," Caspian huffed angrily.

"Do you need glimmerstones?" Liora asked, hoping that he wouldn't.

"I certainly wouldn't say no to some. I could improve my healing abilities with them. Perhaps obtain new ones," Caspian said thoughtfully.

"Just split it three ways, Liora, okay?" Cee said, likely already aware that Liora wasn't eager to give up the glimmerstones.

It was still better than when she was travelling with Serena's group. At least Smokey and Renir didn't need glimmerstones. Liora looked suspiciously at Renir, who still looked rather annoyed.

"You… You don't—"

"No, Liora, you can keep them. You gaining levels is far more important," Renir waved her off.

"So you can use glimmerstones now?" Cee asked.

"To an extent. My menus are still limited, but I can open them now. It appears I even have a few free spots for new skills, but for the time being I'll keep those options open. I can always borrow glimmerstones from Liora if I need to."

"No, you cannot!" She protested.

"Shush. That's not what's important right now," Renir said firmly. "The important thing is that the teamwork we currently have is atrocious! Absolutely horrendous. If it wasn't for Liora's ability to think quickly and willingness to endanger herself, then likely you all would be dead right now."

"We? What about you?" Cee accused.

"If you haven't noticed, I am made fully of rock. Those bats feed on blood. They would have no interest in me whatsoever. The problem is that these bats are very common in the mountains. Creatures here usually have very powerful internal mana, which courses through their blood. These bats—"

"Nobody asked for a biology lesson, Renir!" Liora snapped.

Renir turned to him. "You're as impatient as ever. The point is that these bats don't need much blood to survive. One creature of Cee's size is enough to feed the whole group of bats. Hence why they are so common in these mountains. And let me tell you—they are the weakest creatures living here. The weakest."

Liora, Caspian, and Cee shared a glance. If these guys were supposed to be the weakest, then what would happen against anything stronger? The way to the peak was still a way away. They would have to fight much stronger creatures to get there.

Not to mention the freaks of the Night King. If they were at the peak of this mountain, it meant that they could get past all those monsters with relative ease. After all, one wouldn't set up a base of operations in a place where the monsters would kill everyone.

So they would be by far the most dangerous threat. If they barely escaped with their lives from the bats, then what chance did they have at beating them?

"We need to train… No, not just train. We need to evolve. We need to be ready," Liora announced.

"Exactly," Renir nodded sagely. "We have a reliable backup plan for dealing with bats, but it clearly hurts Liora. It should remain a last resort. Fighting while hurting yourself isn't reliable, even with a healer around."

"We need to position ourselves better next time. Smokey shouldn't be anywhere near the webs," Cee said.

So they continued discussing the battle, thinking of ways to improve. It was far from Liora's favorite activities. Talking about more effective ways to murder something. But improving their teamwork was crucial, so she paid attention.

The next day, they, with a completely new plan, searched for a new group of bats. Well, Liora searched while others remained in their position in one of the mountain ravines.

Cee had her web ready with Caspian in the center. This time, however, there were two escape tunnels left for the merman, should things go awry.

Smokey was positioned a good distance from the webs, lying among the rocks, doing a rather good impression of one himself. Thanks to the peculiarities of these mountains, Smokey's dark red hide didn't stand out all that much.

Renir was on Liora's back. In most fights, he wouldn't be, since he hindered quite a bit. But in this scenario, when they knew that they were fighting bats, everyone agreed it was best for him to remain on Liora's back. Just so they could use electrifying resonance.

Yes, it wasn't an official system move, but everyone agreed it was a good name.

They began the same as the last time, with Liora luring the bats after herself, but this time the goal wasn't to lure them into the webs. That obviously wouldn't work since they could easily spot the webs with their echolocation.

What these poor bat bastards didn't spot as they chased after the delicious morsel that was Liora was the wyvern lying close to the ground. The moment Liora had lured the bat swarm close, Smokey took to the air and started spewing flames all over the beast.

They obviously tried to attack him with their sonic scream. But Caspian sang his song, neutralizing their attack. Smokey could keep blasting flames, undisturbed.

Some of the bats, having suffered severe burns on their wings, fell straight into Cee's web. But this time it was made from tidesilk. Tidesilk had very interesting properties. It didn't burn, but it remained sticky.

This meant that any bats caught in it would get stuck and continue burning until they were dead.

The rest was easy. Cee would jump around, biting into whatever bats she could with her mandibles while Liora would use Raptor Lunge enhanced by Renir to have a lightning attribute.

Unlike their first attempt, the second attempt at dealing with the bats was far more successful. It wasn't perfect. Cee and Liora did get hit with a few of those nasty blood crystals, but it wasn't anything Caspian couldn't heal.

One of the last bats almost struck Liora with a nasty barrage of those blood crystals, but Liora managed to dodge at the last moment, thanks to Renir's warning.
