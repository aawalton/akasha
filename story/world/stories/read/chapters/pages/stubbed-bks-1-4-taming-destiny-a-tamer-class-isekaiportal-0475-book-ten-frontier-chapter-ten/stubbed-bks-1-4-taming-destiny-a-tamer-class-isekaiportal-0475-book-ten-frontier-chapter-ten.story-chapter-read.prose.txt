Congratulations!

You have advanced a Skill past Journeyman. Meditation is Master 1. On the edge of catastrophic destruction of your internal matrix, you have made a significant breakthrough: you do not need to be in a specific state to be able to manually control any of your resources. Mind, body, and spirit, they are all yours, at all times.

As a result, you are increasingly able to multi-task – to access your deeper being even while aware of your environment. This comes at a cost: different states are optimised for different resource manipulations; multitasking is, by nature, inefficient. However, as a result of your greater understanding and control of your own resources, if you do choose the ideal state to manipulate a resource, you will gain far more efficiency even than before.

Light Meditation – Extra Energy gain: 60 - 95% -> 90 - 140%.

Medium Meditation – Extra Energy gain: 135% - 180% -> 200 - 270%. Stamina replenishes 25% faster.

Heavy Meditation – Extra Energy gain: 205 - 265% -> 310 - 400%. Mana replenishes 50% faster.

Deep Meditation – Extra Energy gain: 280 - 380% -> 420 - 570%. Health replenishes 50% faster. Basic soul injuries mend with sufficient time.

Further exploration of what you are capable of in each of the states may reveal new avenues to pursue.

The path to Sage is long and hard; do you have the will and inspiration to stay the course?

I close the notification box thoughtfully. Now that I’m focusing on it, I can feel what it indicated about not needing to be in a specific state to access a particular resource. Even while I stand here, I can see my Core space whenever I blink – and can feel it even when my eyes are open. I’m certain that I can work on repairing the Energy channels that were damaged or destroyed even while I walk around in Light Meditation as usual. But I don’t need the notification to tell me that that will be far less efficient or effective than if I slide into Heavy Meditation in my Core space – I can feel it.

Still, I suppose it could be useful for meetings – I could work on something that’s relatively simple while still participating in the discussion.

As for the resources associated with each of the states, I’m curious to see that stamina is linked to Medium Meditation. I haven’t experimented all that much with that resource, despite my best intentions. Perhaps that’s why it’s only a twenty-five percent increase while the other two are fifty percent.

I’m also pleased to see that the passive Energy gain increases of each state have increased by so much – approximately fifty percent to both minimum and maximum. Though I note that there isn’t any indication that they will increase as I rank up the Skill like there was at Journeyman. Maybe that's linked to what it says at the end – I need to explore further to discover new avenues of development. I suppose it’s unsurprising that a Master of the Skill is expected to develop it in his own way.

Still, whether or not I figure out how to increase the extra Energy it gives me, this is already a boon. Since I spend most of my time in Light Meditation these days, that means I essentially gain fifty percent more Energy per hour.

Closing the box, I observe that the three enchanters are still busy examining the muzzle and talking quietly amongst themselves. I glance at my watch again, seeing that several minutes have ticked by since the last time I looked. Nerves writhe in my belly.

To distract myself, I open up my status – and am pleased to see that my Energy absorption rate has jumped from one hundred and thirty-four units per hour to one hundred and ninety-five. Not quite a fifty percent increase, but maybe that’s because the increase works on the base rate rather than the rate bolstered by Willpower Leverage.

Looking down the other Skills, I note that Energy Manipulation has increased by a level and is now Master 7. Not unexpected considering what I’ve just done, but appreciated nonetheless.

Bond Teleport has also changed too, though not all for the good. It’s increased in level – from Beginner 9 to Novice 8 – but there has been no notification about the rank up, and the Skill itself is greyed out, as if even my status isn’t sure whether it exists anymore.

“Definitely need to figure out what’s going on with that,” I murmur to myself, troubled.

Even as thoughts and ideas tumble through my mind, I send my magic through my body to check for physical damage. Unsurprisingly, I find it – burns, charring, and the beginnings of something that looks like a mutation in my fingers, a bundle of cells that feel wrong. I get to work, soothing the burns, and removing the mutated cells with a strong sense of distaste.

Out of everything, my right arm is the worst affected – it’s almost sucked dry of health energy, and drags at my mana eagerly even though that isn’t the right kind of energy to replenish it. I immediately put my new Mastery to use, converting mana into health in a way that I only ever used to be able to do in Deep Meditation. I can feel how inefficient the process is in comparison to going into the right state, but that I can do this while remaining completely aware of my surroundings is a real boon.

It’s while I’m still healing my arm that Master Mirena approaches me.

“My lord, is it a good moment?” she asks politely.

I tilt my wrist and look again at the watch face. Just under a quartermark until dawn.

“It had better be,” I reply grimly. Then, seeing her uncertainty, I sigh. “Yes. Please tell me what you’ve found.”

“It’s a fascinating set of enchantments,” Lizil replies immediately, her eyes alight. “Nothing like the style most enchanters use in Moriax. Each enchantment is so interwoven that if one goes wrong, they all do – but conversely, they empower each other. Very complex to get right – and potentially explosive if the slightest aspect is wrong.”

“I’m sure that’s very interesting,” I agree, trying not to snap as I feel the pressure of time. “But did you find any way of breaking it?”

“Apart from catastrophic damage that overrides the indestructible enchantment, the only way I can see is for someone to overwhelm the ownership of the blood-bound enchantment,” Lizil replies in measured tones.

“That’s possible?” I ask with some surprise. From what Layton indicated earlier, I didn’t think it would be.

“Theoretically.”

I eye Lizil. That ‘theoretically’ isn’t reassuring. Then again, the whole ritual to empower Bond Teleport to drag Kalanthia here was also entirely theoretical, yet it worked. I’m even still mostly in one piece afterwards.

She sees my look and relents, giving me a little more explanation.

“Blood-bound enchantments are rarely used in Moriax – they’re very finicky things. Generally they’re only applied to devices or equipment that will only ever be used by one person, as transfer is not always possible and depends on the nature of the device or piece of equipment. In this case, the nature is that of binding, control, and dominion. I would theorise that this renders it open to being forcibly taken by someone stronger.”

I nod. That makes sense – something that dominates is open to be dominated in their turn. Like how my Battle of Wills used to impose a penalty if I failed it – which was removed as I moved ever-further away from pure domination.

“So, theoretically, how do I do that?”

Lizil shrugs.

“Bleed on it would be the start, I’d say. After that…I have no idea.”

Not exactly reassuring, but at least it’s a place to start.

I nod thanks at Lizil and then set my jaw and step forwards. No damn muzzle is going to be the reason my settlement is devastated by a Tier four. Not when I can do something about it.

But I need to prepare for the muzzle to fight back. Since it’s currently sitting against Kalanthia’s skin, that’s a worrying prospect.

I meet her golden gaze.

“I’d like to put something underneath the muzzle – is that alright with you?”

It is tight to my skin already, Kalanthia replies doubtfully.

“It won’t be entirely comfortable,” I admit. “But I’d like to have something between you and it in case it does something when I start trying to take over its ownership.” I wouldn’t put it past the makers to build in something like that, even if Lizil didn’t mention anything. Anyway, for all I know, just the clash of my magic with the muzzle’s will make it burst into fire or something.

Kalanthia eyes me for a moment, then dips her head closer. I know just the thing to use – the skin of the hot-air balloon. Not only is it thin and strong fabric, but it’s already soaked in my mana from the last time we used it. Though my Inventory strips Energy out of things, Nicholas gave me a special packing cloth ages ago that protects what it’s wrapped around and I’ve been using that since filling the balloon fabric with mana takes ages.

It’s a matter of a couple of minutes to work the cloth underneath the muzzle, and Kalanthia looks more than a little uncomfortable afterwards.

Alright, I say to myself after quickly running through things in my mind and checking that I haven’t missed anything obvious. Lizil said to bleed on it. To attempt to force it to acknowledge me as its master. I can do that. I push away the misgivings that remind me that I haven’t done anything like this before and that we only have a very limited amount of time before a Tier four nunda starts demanding entry. Already, the lights in the cavern ceiling have begun increasing in luminosity, indicating that the first rays of dawn’s light have appeared on the horizon.

Pulling out my knife, I pause for a moment and then put it away. What do I need a knife for?

Putting my hand on the muzzle directly instead, I use flesh-magic to open a painless slice on my palm and then urge my blood to well up and enter the muzzle leather.

The leather resists my magic stubbornly, my blood rolling over its surface like a bead of water on teflon. Interesting.

With the control I have over my own blood, I don’t let any of it drip to the ground, even if it’s a challenge to keep it on the leather at all. But it’s not a failure – already, I’ve learned something about the muzzle that I hadn’t seen when I was just inspecting it with my magical senses and my Skill.

Lizil is right – blood is the key, and the enchantment is the lock. At the moment, my blood is the equivalent of a wrong-shaped key, rejected by the lock. The paths to success are clear: reshape my key, reshape the lock, or break the lock entirely.

Breaking the lock seems like it might be the quickest and easiest solution, but I quickly discover that that isn’t the case. As Lizil said, all the enchantments are interwoven and the indestructible enchantment protects the blood-bound enchantment as much as it prevents the leather from being broken.

So, instead, I start sending my blood out to cover every inch of the muzzle, searching for the crack in its defences, the flaw that I can exploit.

After a few minutes, the muzzle is completely covered with my blood and I feel nothing but frustration. There is no flaw that I can feel.

Markus! It’s dawn! Bastet warns me urgently.

I just need a bit more time, I tell her, hoping desperately that that is true.

He’s unwilling to wait any longer – he’s already moving and the clouds are gathering again. He’ll burst through them.

Bring out a carcass for him to eat. Get Lathani to ask for stories about his territory. I don’t know – have the samuran hatchlings dance a jig if you must. But keep him away, I reply, frustrated anger boiling inside me as I glare at the muzzle.

And then my eyes narrow as I feel something.

We’ll do what we can, Bastet replies, pulling me out of my thoughts.

Don’t put yourselves at risk. Worst comes to worst, we activate all our remaining shield devices and defensive scrolls to buy us time, I tell her. But please – I need to focus. Speak to Maxen and Loran if you need anything from the humans.

I will, she replies, not sounding happy, but accepting nonetheless.

I send her a flicker of encouragement, then pull my thoughts away entirely to concentrate on the muzzle, new urgency running through me.

There had been something earlier when I glared at the thing in frustrated anger. Trying to repeat the moment, I bring to mind all the reasons why this bloody thing needs to come off now. Because it might be the reason a Tier four attacks us in lethal rage. Because it’s stopping me from going and helping my companions. Because it’s hurting someone I care about.

If looks could set things on fire, the muzzle would have been immolated long ago. Unfortunately, it’s not so obliging, but a grim sense of satisfaction goes through me as I get a reaction nonetheless.

There.

A flicker, as if the enchantment had briefly quailed away from my anger.

Lizil spoke of overwhelming the ownership…this isn’t all that dissimilar from a Battle of Wills, is it? I think to myself, feeling a surge of confidence – I know how to do those.

The only problem here is that the leather has no eyes. Then again, I don’t think it’s actually sentient either. The principle remains the same; the execution doesn’t. But I may have an idea of how to accomplish it – through the same means that Bastet uses to protect her territory without a physical fight.

Concentrating deeply, I press my domain outwards, focusing it into my hand. The enchantment flickers again, then returns to resisting me. But that flicker was heartening – it just means that I haven’t brought enough power to bear yet.

Knowing that my domain is powered by my Willpower, and that Willpower is strongest when fueled by necessity, I pour my need for this enchantment to give way into my mental pressure.

My domain spreads out wider. I almost lose control of the whole thing when I notice something with surprise – my domain is spreading through my blood to encompass the whole of the muzzle, rather than just the few centimetres that I normally manage.

Heartened by the realisation, I redouble my efforts.

The enchantment flickers with increasing frequency. It’s like I’m pressing against a wall that at first felt like concrete, and now feels more like a collection of loose stones.

Taking inspiration from Bastet’s own domain battle method, I begin pressing more firmly in certain points, doing my best to destabilise the wall.

It holds…until it doesn’t.

With a quiet hiss, I feel it as the enchantment folds, discarding its previous imprint and accepting my own signature as its governing principle.

Pulling my domain back slowly, I realise I’m panting. Opening my eyes, I see that my blood has entirely disappeared, absorbed into the leather. Closing my wound, I touch the muzzle, fear and hope a maelstrom in my chest.

But I needn’t have worried. With almost an anti-climactic click, the muzzle detaches the moment I send it the mental instruction to open. It slithers to the ground in an untidy pile of leather straps.

Kalanthia is free.
