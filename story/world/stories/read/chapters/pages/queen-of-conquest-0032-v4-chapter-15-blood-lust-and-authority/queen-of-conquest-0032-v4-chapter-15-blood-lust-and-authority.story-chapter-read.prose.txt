"Your Grace," one of the followers said. Her voice was young and feminine, though the look in her eyes screamed of hate far beyond her years. I'd seen her before, hadn't I? The blue hair was vaguely familiar. "How many attributes do you have?"

I sniffed and offered her a wry smile. "I have three."

I'd originally hoped that Lilliana's heart would provide me with a new attribute, but even with the senses of a gold realm core, I hadn't been able to locate it. The Soul Weaver energy had drowned out or replaced whatever her base attribute had been, at least to the extent my current skills could analyze my internal attributes. There was a slim possibility she'd always carried the Soul Weaver attribute, but given how the main system had created it through a merging of my lunar and necromantic attributes, I didn't believe it.

I shook the thoughts away and refocused on the seedling cultivators staring up at me with wide eyes.

"Three…" the girl repeated, the word barely more than a gasp. "And your affinities?"

I shrugged, motioning for the seated girl to put her hand down. “A good rule of thumb is that the number of strong affinities are around thee to five times the number of attributes. You might find yourself with more affinity toward earth than water, but without either being of a high enough affinity to bother.” The girl opened her mouth but I shut her down with another wave. “You will understand once you begin forming your rings and approaching the silver realm.” I cleared my throat, this time gesturing for them all to stand. “Now, I will demonstrate the strongest power you will have access to as a bronze realm heart cultivator. Many of you won’t be able to use it until you reach the silver realm, though it is possible to use it earlier. It is simply extremely difficult it is to master.” I waited a moment for them all the get back to their feet. “I will demonstrate. You must first flow your energy into your core and condense it as tight as you can. You won't be permanently condensing it like you would to create heart rings, but you still need to condense it. As tight as possible. Make your energy even more dense than it might be to create a heart ring. Then when it can’t compress anymore, expel it from your core like a wave. The more dense you compacted the energy, the more powerful the result.”

I slowly moved the energy through my meridians to my heart core, demonstrating the process to their energy senses.

Once the energy was decently compressed, I clapped my hands together. A small flicker of excitement rose in my chest and I grinned. “Let’s see who can stay standing. This is called Authority.”

Killing them would have been bad, so I dampened my Authority deliberately, compressing my energy far less than I was capable of. Still, the sheer density of a gold realm core was more than enough to drop every one of them before they could blink, before their minds could even catch up.

A golden halo erupted around me and spread across the central field. To the bronze cores and the coreless alike, it must have felt like the world crashing down on their bodies, a heavy physical weight like gravity pulling them not to the ground but into it, and then through it

First came sounds of surprise, then grunts of pain. It lasted only a handful of seconds before I dispersed the Authority, unwilling to permanently damage anyone.

A moment of silence stretched as some struggled back to their feet. Others rolled onto their backs, gasping. A few had passed out. If that didn't drive them toward spending more time building a core, nothing would.

”Wake them up,” I snapped at the conscious discples, gesturing toward the unmoving. “The rest of you, stand up. We're not done."

Those laying down shot to their feet, remembering who it was waiting on them. When all the unconscious were finally woken, in ways ranging from gentle to violent, I couldn't help the slight twitching of my lips framing a smirk.

"Next. Who here has heard of bloodlust?" A few hands rose. I pointed to the surprising figure of Gronch, the half-orc who'd had my back in the Sealrite arenas. "Explain it."

”It’s the ability to physically manifest your desire for blood,” he said simply. “But I do not know how to do it.”

"Many cannot," I said. "Unlike Authority, bloodlust isn't acquired through cultivation. Reaching the bronze realm is merely the prerequisite to use it. Bloodlust comes from the weight of your soul, the very essence of your existence. And unlike Authority, it inflicts damage not to the body but at the core level. Only those who know what it means to have their life threatened could even begin to develop a Will strong enough to manifest bloodlust." I grinned, and those who could see it cringed back. Even Gronch. "It feels like this."

I would only shake their hearts. Their cores. It would hurt, but no lasting damage would occur. Rather, their cores would strengthen from the experience.

Probably.

I released my bloodlust, thick and heavy into the space around me. No one dropped to their knees or face-planted into the grass. Instead they gripped their chests as though mid-heart attack, faces twisting into expressions of abject terror and pain.

I knew from experience that while I emitted bloodlust, they wouldn't see me as me. I would become a monster in their minds, the most evil, cruelest demon their imaginations could conjure. A natural predator to their new reality as prey.

The more bloodlust I released, the heavier the air grew. It thickened with the reality of death and the promise of pain.

Unlike Authority, bloodlust couldn't be dispersed. Only restrained. It remained always active once learned, held back by nothing but one's Will. I explained as much while pulling my bloodlust back with each word, until the seedling cultivators lay scattered across the ground, clutching desperately at their hearts and cores, chests heaving for breath amid rivers of sweat in the cool spring air.

"Bloodlust, while more primal, is less controlled," I said. "It doesn't dominate your foe so much as drive them to panic, to experience an abject terror of reality. That can make them unpredictable. Volatile. Often, especially against an enemy at your approximate level, that's one of the worst outcomes you can force. Pushing your enemy into a survival mindset is rarely wise. Authority is simply better in most one on one situations. It brings physical force down on them and dominates, slowing their movements and their thoughts. If your Authority is stronger, you will almost always come out on top."

I was about to end the lesson and set the seedlings to practicing cultivation under my watch when a familiar tigerkin stepped forward, the crowd parting around her with worship in their eyes.

"Can you explain the next level, my lady?" Nida asked. She wore her full silver battle armor, and I noticed, having missed it the night before through sheer exhaustion, that she'd grown. Both in height and in power. When I'd initiated her, she hadn't yet seemed a general. Now she fit the title perfectly. "What happens when you reach the gold realm? Many of us saw you fight the Hydra. There was something... more in the air. Something greater. But it was difficult for me to grasp, as if it were a thought on the tip of my fingers."

What you felt was Dominion," I said, frowning slightly. I hadn't wanted to explain domains this early in their cultivation journies. Looking too high too soon made one miss the rock underfoot. But a little information likely wouldn't hurt, so I continued. "When one reaches the middle levels of the silver realm, some few develop the ability to create a Domain and battle for dominion over an area. At the gold realm, this becomes guaranteed. It grows to be as natural as breathing. An extension of yourself." I hesitated, then added, "No Domain is the same. Each carries variance shaped by the cultivator's identity and character. The path you carve after reaching the gold realm is, in effect, your own unique cultivation journey."

"Can we experience your Domain?" Nida asked, eyes literally glistening with excitement and challenge.

I thought about the request, not sure I wanted to enact a Domain. Among higher realm cultivators in Ordite, expanding one’s Domain and claiming dominion in a city was tantamount to both claiming the territory and challenging any other higher realm cultivators within it.

But… it was my city and the King had departed for the moment. Or so I’d been told.

Darrow’s knight was a new high realm and he was subordinate to me by title, so his threat to me was nominal. There were other high realm cultivators in the city who may take offense, but would anyone dare take up the challenge in my own territory after being royally decreed as the Duchess of House Alistar?

No. Definitely not.

I shrugged. "If you desire it, my paragon, so be it."

Two options lay before me. The first was the Domain I’d used against the Hydra. That had been the first time I’d used Shen energy to create a Domain. I didn’t quite understand what kind of Domain I'd created from the new energy. It had formed some kind of responsive Domain that reacted almost intuitively to its environment, a typing which I'd never encountered before.

The Domain hadn't had any link to my attribute energies, nor did I sense a link to any of my possible affinities. It had simply reacted to the circumstances, almost reverse magnetized as it had somehow fought to repulse the Hydra’s Greater Domain.

Which would normally have been impossible for a lesser domain to impose on a greater one.

The second was to erect a heart energy-based domain. It should share strong similarities with my domain before the Soul Transference, though I couldn't predict how Soul Weaver attributed energy would interplay with it.

There was technically a third option that involved some level of experimentation by attempting to form a Domain with a combination of heart energy, mana, and Shen energy…

I dismissed the idea. If the resulting domain went awry, there was serious concern that the consequence could kill most of those around me.

“Heart energy base it is,” I muttered under my breath, low enough none else would hear.

I reached deep into the thrumming core. My heart began to pound, to stomp, to roar along a beat of its own making, its own desire, as it stirred, stretching into the far edges of my Will and the very borders of my existence.

As expected, the first signs of my Domain flared to life. I resisted the pressing urge to let it draw on my necromantic attributed energies. Every time I'd allowed that, the ability twisted into something darker. Corrupted. Tainted. And if that weren't reason enough, when the black energy would manifest during Domain expansion it would appear as a dark halo, warping and devouring the lunar light trying to shine through.

Without it, lunar light exploded around me as a visible aura, pale and otherworldly, almost dimming the morning sun overhead. That was the normal result, mimicking closely what happened when I manifested the same in Ordite.

Then my Domain shifted in a way entirely outside my control, warped by the Soul Weaver energy, as I knew immediately. It drew on something else, something ancient, yet still undeniably from within me.

The entire central field plunged into ethereal silence. The wind stilled under this domain of life and death. Even I, standing at its center, felt the Domain pulling at my life essence, dragging at both my heart and mana cores in a slow, hungry drain.

The effect didn't stop at those with cores. The Paragons suffered too, their faces draining to the same pale white as the moonlight radiating off me, some dropping outright. Most of the non-Paragons followed them to the ground. Only Gronch and the blue-haired girl still fought to stay upright.

With every passing second, those around me weakened while I grew stronger. And it wasn't only my heart core, I realized with a quick inward glance. My mana core roared alongside it, greedily pulling mana from the surroundings and everyone within the Domain's reach.

The power was forceful rather than subtle, but carried beneath it an underlying grace my Domain had never possessed before. I felt the energy and mana in the air all around me, beckoning to be commanded.

All of it was mine.

The Domain I had wielded on Ordite was nothing like this. That one had strengthened me, solely me, by conjuring a faux moon and activating the empowering nature of lunar energy. There'd been a faint pull on opponents' core energy, but nothing more than pressure, and foreign attribute energy had little use in battle regardless.

This Domain didn't attract and empower like my old one, nor did it repulse like the Shen Domain. It dominated. Everything within belonged to me, to control, to take. I stood as the sole adjudicator of life and death.

Soul Weaver attributed energy flooded my heart core past the point of comfort and kept flooding it, commanding the energy of those within the Domain to flow ceaselessly toward me.

It would accomplish little against something like the Hydra with its near-bottomless well of energy, or against a singular opponent strong enough to push back. But in the midst of a crowd?

An army?

How many lower cultivators could I incapacitate in a single expansion, while standing at the heart of a vast military force?

The thought cut off sharply when one of the mortal cultivators released a choking sound, as if the air itself was being stripped from his lungs.

I'd held the Domain too long. I withdrew it back into my core, and released them all from my dominion.
