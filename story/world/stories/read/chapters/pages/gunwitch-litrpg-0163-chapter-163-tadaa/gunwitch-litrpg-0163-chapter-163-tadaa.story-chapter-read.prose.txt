Vasil hacked up four slimy runestones. He smacked his lips and proudly nodded at the drool-covered pebbles. “Clover, Wave, Sol, and Poison. As you requested.”

He was always serious. Actually, no he wasn’t. That one time when he tried to convince me to turn into a horse. His favorite joke. Maybe this was another one of his jokes.

I put my face closer to his. “Hmmmmmmm.”

“Yeah.”

“I told you I have to grab them with my tongue. My tongue is wet and sticky.”

“Why would I waste a skill point on something like that?”

Obviously this discussion wasn’t going anywhere. Vasil probably wasn’t lying to me. He didn’t have a sense of humor, after all. Not that this was funny. It was very unfunny. And gross.

I sighed, grabbing the first slimy runestone between my thumb and index finger. “Fine. Whatever.”

“That was then, Bogumir. This is now. I wouldn’t do that again. It was itchy and helmets mess up my hair. No, a helmet won’t do.”

I prayed to all the gods that Ola wouldn’t return with a helmet. That would make me look extremely stupid. Surely Ola wouldn’t return with a helmet. Ola knew what she was doing. She found those dresses, after all. She was a professional. She knew what a woman like me needed. She’d probably return with something beautiful.

But what if she did return with a helmet. Or even worse, what if she returned with an ugly mask like Karn’s Face? It was a blessing that it got destroyed during the fight with Bogumir’s squad, else I’d probably have kept wearing it and kept being ugly. What if the mask Ola would bring was even worse than that? What if she brought Karn’s Brother’s Face? If that existed and was uglier. Bogumir would laugh at me. Maybe Vasil would even laugh at me. And they’d be right.

No, they wouldn’t be. I simply wouldn’t make the runeword then. I’d wait until I found something that wasn’t an ugly mask. I’d settle for a pretty mask as long as it didn’t cover my hair. What was taking Ola so long? I started to get nervous.

Bogumir must have noticed because he had a wicked grin on his face. He knew it. He knew what Ola was going to bring me. That son of a bitch! He knew it! He knew she had Karn’s Ugly Brother’s Face mask! That’s why he was so smug about it! He knew I was screwed and couldn’t wait to gloat about my ugly mask.

Ola reappeared in the door and slowly walked up to us, hands behind her back. I looked around. I could still run. If I wasn’t there, then she couldn’t give me Karn’s Disgusting Smelly Brother’s mask. I didn’t want a smelly ugly mask!

Too late now. She was too close to run. Bogumir’s grin grew even larger. I resolved to chop his head off and hang it from my belt again. See if he’d still be laughing then.

Ola was in front of me now. A bead of sweat rolled into my eye. I tried to blink it out. The salt stung. This was it. This was the end of my life.

She pulled out something shiny and held it in front of me. “Tadaa!”

I flinched and instinctively covered my face with my hands. I didn’t hiss like some kind of vampire or angry cat. I didn’t do that. But inside I might have done it. Outside also, maybe.

I slowly moved my hands so I could see my doom…

Ola proudly held the most beautiful tiara I’d ever seen in my life. It looked to be made of pure gold, with a big, shiny red ruby in the middle and smaller ones next to it. Relief washed over me. Not today, Karn’s Malformed Pungent Sibling, not today.

I gently took it from Ola’s hands and might have danced in place a little bit. “Ola, you are the best of the best. There is being grateful and then there’s what I am right now. There may be no word for it, but I am it.”
