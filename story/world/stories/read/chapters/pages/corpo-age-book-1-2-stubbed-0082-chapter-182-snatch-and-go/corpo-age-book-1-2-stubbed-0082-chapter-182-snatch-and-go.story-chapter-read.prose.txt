I guess regular vibro blades aren’t going to cut it…

If he’s taking action directly, does this mean I’ll be meeting him after this? It’ll be nice to finally get a name instead of just referring to him as my guardian all the time. It makes it sound like I’m a minor who needs babysitting or something…
