The demon seemed hesitant to meet the berserker's charge, but it no longer had the option to avoid the clash as it had lost that opportunity the moment it had decided to dive at him, losing its wings in the process.

Nathan swung his blade at the pit fiend, barely paying attention to the spurt of black blood that splattered across his face as the monster moved out of the way, narrowly avoiding a fatal strike aimed at its chest.

The pit fiend heaved as its clawed hand touched the open cut on its chest, then it shrieked at him as it swung its massive spiked mace at the berserker.

Both fighters met halfway, mace to sword. Each side pressed the attack, with Nathan doing his best, though there was little visible evidence. Still, Nathan was definitely feeling the effect of the contest of strength the longer the contest went on, and it wasn’t long before the demon hopped back, its eyes narrowing as its labored breathing intensified.

The first real clash between the fighters had only lasted a couple of minutes, but it was enough to ascertain what Nathan already believed—He and the monster were about the same range in level.

Although Nathan was betting on himself to be the one with the higher level, considering the amount of damage he’d been able to inflict on the monster in the opening stages of the fight. And it wasn’t until his eyes locked in on the Pit fiend that he understood that the demon knew but no longer cared about that piece of information.

Damn. Do all demons in hell run track and field? Nathan thought as he hopped back, narrowly avoiding the strike, a grin plastered on his face. “You're going to have to do better than that if you want to kill me.”

The demon did not respond; it only resumed its charge against Nathan. A wild claw attack to the berserker's face, which Nathan easily dodged. Unfortunately for the berserker, the attack was also accompanied by the swing of its spiked mace, which was delayed a millisecond longer than the swipe had come. He was too slow to evade the surprise attack that hit him square on his left knee, displacing his kneecap for a moment before it returned to its natural position.

The strike had done noticeable damage to Nathan, but the berserker simply gritted his teeth as he tightened his grip on his sword.

Obviously, fighting the demon now had become a lot harder than he had intended at the beginning. But seeing this seemed like it was the only chance for him to go toe-to-toe with the demon, and so he relaxed himself, his eyes bloodshot from the trauma of his kneecap. He refused to let it bother him as he simply went for the strike again, fighting the demon, which was proving to be a lot harder than he had anticipated.

But he persevered, creating a dangerous, endless black arc as he slashed at the demon again, the blade managing to dig into it's chest.  The demon pushed back with a horrifying shriek, black blood seeping onto him from the tip of his blade.

The pit fiend seemed determined, judging by how quickly it repositioned to press him.

It was unfortunate the way that he was moving, as his left knee had already begun to swell. His movements were becoming stiffer and stiffer. And each time he tried to move towards the demon, it felt like he was ripping more tendons.

Inflammation was already kicking in, and it was a bitch.

The spikes around the mace made his injury much worse than it should have been. Especially as the spikes had managed to dig in a little deeper than expected into his skin, which meant his constitution wasn't as high enough as it should have been to handle the hit from the monster that close and unprepared as he had been.

In light of the aftermath of that attack, he decided to use the demon's trick against it.

Nathan swung at the demon's face with his fist, correctly anticipating it would flinch. And as soon as it did, he swung his sword towards its left thigh, tearing off a good amount of its flesh before the monster was able to distance itself from him.

How do you like that? While the fight with the demon wasn't as straightforward as he would have preferred, he understood the risks that came with fighting, and he knew he could handle it.

Black blood dripping from the tip of Oblivion reflected the crimson light above as the berserker slashed at the demon once more, a frown spreading across his face as the demon evaded his attack once more. It turned out this demon was a lot faster than the Xharnton, and, as it was, he’d struggle to handle it in his current state.

Luckily for Nathan, his strike on the demon's thigh seemed to have just as much effect on the flesh of the demon as its spiked mace on his knee had had on him—of course, the severity of the two injuries was different.

From the look of things, this fight wasn't going to be a battle of attrition as it had been with the Xharnton. If anything, Nathan fully expected it to be one where the fighter with the fastest hands would win.

Nathan couldn't hesitate or decide to buy time. He had to finish this fight as soon as possible.

He shot towards the demon once more, punching at its torso. The blow connected cleanly, his fist hitting the demon's midsection, knocking it into the maze wall, just as a loud shriek ripped from its throat.

Nathan charged at the downed demon—the berserker intended to kill it as soon as possible.

The demon spotted him and quickly threw its spiked mace straight at the charging Berserker. Nathan easily sidestepped the attack, but that split-second movement was enough time for the demon to get up to its feet and brandish its claws in his direction.

A sort of last stand for it, but its actions weren't enough to make Nathan give up.

He managed to slash at the demon with an Oblivion. It was a vicious attack that should’ve severed the right hand of the Pit fiend, but it used a clawed hand to catch the sword before quickly releasing it, a terrifying shriek leaving its lips as a trail of blood ran down its open palms.

Nathan quickly caught on to why it had shrieked.

From what he’d noticed from using Oblivion, the sword had the ability to start the process of sucking souls even on the tiniest of cuts. So it was only fair that the demon would leave the sword as soon as it had injured it.

That was enough to stamp the fact that Oblivion was beyond what it could handle.

The strike was targeted at its other thigh, but the demon proved to have learned its lesson as it moved backward, putting distance between them as it continued to breathe heavily. Its massive frame vibrating with visible tension, and Nathan could tell it was beyond frightened by what oblivion could do. But the demons' fear did little to sate Nathan’s bloodlust.

He needed the pit fiend to be dead at his feet so that he could calm his raging heart. And he knew he had to do it soon before his knee got any worse.

Nathan locked eyes with the pit fiend even as he focused most of his weight on his right leg, as his left was still proving to be unstable—and it was in his best interest not to burden his injured right knee with too much.

The demon was the first to make a move as it pushed off the floor with a heavy boom, its spiked mace coming down in an overhead swing as it sought to clobber the berserker from above.

Even though its wings were now out of commission—courtesy of the berserker—it still believed that it had a better shot of taking him on from above than from level ground.

To Nathan, it really didn't matter. He just needed to kill the Pit fiend.

The demon swung at his head with its spiked mace as soon as it came within distance, forcing the Berserker to put up a hasty cross guard as he deflected the attack, which pushed him back several feet, forcing him to grunt in pain as his bad knee took on the brunt of the attack. I guess the demon is smarter than it looks.

Steeling himself, he straightened his spine and made his grip on Oblivion a little looser than he would have liked. He locked eyes with the demon, and then he charged. He swung in a vicious arc with Oblivion.

The black blade moved in a blur as it slashed at the demon's chest diagonally, only to be deflected by the demon's mace.
