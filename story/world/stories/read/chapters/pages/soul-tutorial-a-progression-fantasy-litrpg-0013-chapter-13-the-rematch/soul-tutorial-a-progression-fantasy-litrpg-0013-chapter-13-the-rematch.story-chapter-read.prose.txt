Luke wiped away the moist blood that still stained his face, stretching his limbs before he got up. He felt a kind of soreness in his body that he hadn’t experienced before. Reaching points in his body he didn’t know could get sore, even the souls he held within his core had vanished off somewhere. Maybe staying inside him used up all its energy.

But he brought his exhausted body back over to the pedestal. As he leaned on it, he thought of the previous encounter with the slime. And how soul energy helped quite a bit, even with the passive effect.

He wondered how much more he could push this ability. If he had no more souls to use, wouldn’t he only have his weak body to rely on, especially without a power source unless he killed enough monsters to refill it?

Pushing it to the back of his mind, he checked the amount of points he had. It was hard for him to keep up with the constant spending and gaining of it all. At the looks of it, he only has 3 points. Only signifying the importance of having a balance of what to spend and save, just in case something happens.

“I guess I have to get used to soreness at some point” Only being able to live with the pain for now, either waiting it out until he recovered or fighting his way to a restore. Not wanting to sit around on the island the whole time. Wanting to retry floor 1 with the new use of soul energy, using the moment it took for it to appear, he got himself ready.

Forcing his legs to move toward the slime once it was fully manifested, he tried something new instead of having the souls stored inside him beforehand. Why not will it toward him while he was sprinting towards the slime?

This turned out to be Luke's worst idea yet, as the souls gathered within him. He paid too much attention to the souls’ movement, making him lose sight of the slime as it rushed towards him, sending a spike speeding towards his face. Leaning his head out of the way, he sidestepped to the right, tilting his body forward to gather enough strength in his legs before his leg sped towards it, but it connected with nothing as the slime backed off.

This gave Luke the time to gather himself and spin the souls lightly inside of him; it was still crude and wobbly, where the slightest blow could knock it off course. But that didn’t stop him from trying to use this power even when the souls were chaotic inside his chamber. Luke took that energy, flooding his legs with it, they stung even more, and the soreness beforehand only made it worse. Even with the pain pounding him, he still used the energy as well as he could.

Applying just enough to increase his speed, he kicked off once again towards the slime. The slime hopped backwards while reshaping part of its body into a spike toward where Luke was coming from.

But that gave Luke an idea: instead of just flooding his legs with power, why not try to circulate the energy in a way that could even provide some kind of protection against the slime spikes. Including his constitution, he leveled up, which would make him more or less impenetrable if he could get everything right.

He made a significant error when trying to put this to the test; concentrating that energy into his legs and simultaneously forming a barrier demanded excessive focus.

This mistake allowed the blue slime to take advantage, as it slipped through Luke's guard. One of its elongated spikes stabbed him in the shoulder.

The pain he endured on the first three floors made the latter discomfort seem insignificant, like a paper cut that didn’t really hurt but only annoyed him.

As Luke yanked the spike free with all his might, channeling energy from his leg into his arm, he attempted to lift the slime with it. However, the slime pulled the spike back towards its body, causing it to slip from Luke’s grasp.

Feeling unrestrained he blitz to the side of the slime with the energy still held within his legs and use that as a driving force once again to send his knee into the slime with energy condensed into it using the generated speed in that distance to crack what seem to be the core of the slime and killing it instantly, turning into particles as the souls appeared in the sky once again.

However, that undoubtedly brought about some repercussions; his knee was littered with gashes from the slime spikes and acid. He approached the pedestal with a swollen knee, feeling hungry and exhausted. His stomach growled as soon as he arrived, pushing aside any concerns about the points. Besides recovering from his wounds, healing helps him with all his daily needs.

Feeling refreshed, he was dying to taste food again. The thought of fried chicken floated around his head. But putting that thought on the back burner, he felt his chest heat up as souls faded from his body; feeling like some part of his body was still sore even after the restore had. Only noticing how detrimental the aftereffects of soul energy were.

But he needed the soul energy to even make it past floor 2. Taking some inspiration from the blue slime he just fought, he turned his head, looking at his stump, visualizing some ways he could project soul energy from it someday. Since his left arm was gone, it could actually be easier to project energy through it, with the constant flow of energy just wanting to flow out of it during practice.

So he now sat with 0 points left, needing another 5 points for backup purposes. He just wanted peace once and for all. He didn’t know what would happen if he did nothing for an extended period, and he didn't want to figure that out if something bad would happen from doing that.

Deciding he needed to take a break from using soul energy for the moment, he challenged Floor 0 again to see where his body was at that moment. Visions of the silver slime flashed around in his head.

The green slime appeared before him. Luke took his time to approach, avoiding the obstacles. He could only think of ways he could beat the silver slime with the new power he had.

I could try projecting some claws from my fingertips, but it was all imagination.

Only now realizing how formidable an opponent the slime was. Crushing his entire right leg in that one fight. Which barely lasted over a few minutes. He didn’t really even want to try it without raising some of his attributes.

But he wanted to prove himself against it, body low to the ground. He picked up his speed, trying to apply his body weight to his arm, already shooting towards the slime core. It barely moved the slime a couple of inches before he backed off with a corroded fist.

Missing the feeling of having a branch with him, he continued onwards, flicking his fist out just fast enough not to burn his entire fist off. Using mostly his legs to send damage coursing through its body.

The slime backed off as its body leaked acid. Luke barely made it a few inches before the slime before something shifted. The slime body widened as it curved itself; a ball of acid gathered in front of it. Before shooting out towards Luke.

He barely moved his upper body out of the way as the acid splashed behind him, eating away at the ground.

I wondered when the slimes were going to use that again.

Only finding it more annoying to drag the fight out again. Noticing that only a few out of the many green slimes he fought could shoot out a substance, but it wasn’t without Luke inflicting some harm to it beforehand.

Gathering his wits, Luke took the moment of it trying to recollect its body to send his foot into the core that was more exposed, cracking it, leaving it destroyed. The slime slowly turned into nothing. With more souls gathered in the voided sky.

He made his way back to the pedestal, breathing heavily, slumping his body on the pedestal with his hand placed on top. The veil appeared before him in all its glory.
