Chapter 387

The Lord of the Moon had been growing weary.

In a way, that had been the case for longer than he could recall

For a while, taunting, harassing, and slaughtering these pathetic creatures as they flocked together in their cities, waiting for extermination, had been enjoyable. He had managed to regain some of that feeling he had lived for back in the day. Spending his days hunting inferior beings and feasting upon their energies had been all he wanted.

It had been glorious.

But even all those years ago, with time, that too had grown old. Rather than giving him a sense of fulfillment, it had become a chore. It couldn’t be all that his life amounted to. One day, in the distant future, when his time was up, his memory would die with him.

What he had needed was a legacy. An undeniable, everlasting mark.

But then, why stop there? Rather than a mark on the universe, why not simply make the universe itself the mark?

However, confident as he had been in his own power, strife between himself and the other Primal Entities—or Worldbreakers, as they were apparently now more commonly known as—had made it… difficult to carry such a grand plan to completion.

So, he had decided to simply wait it out, deep in a stasis slumber. But before he had gone away, he had left behind a fragment of his will to, someday, be found. So while he might not have been there in the flesh to instigate his own return, as part of him had remained to influence other, lesser beings and push them into servitude.

Oh, and how it had worked. The fools.

When he had first come back to his senses just a short while ago, he had been confused. No, confused didn’t even begin to describe the way he had felt.

Eons ago, when he had closed his eyes and gone into hibernation, the world had been a different place. The cosmos had been a different place. Not just regarding the worlds in the universe or the lifeforms living on them, but in the way it felt.

The Ak’ki was completely foreign to his system and senses and for the first few days, he had been unable to use it at all. His grand return had nearly ended entirely due to his own miscalculation. Of course the cosmic energies would have shifted during those many eons.

Fortunately, his biology, primed for survival from millennia of existing at the peak of power, had not taken too long to adapt to the new environment, and soon he had begun to gather vitality in order to climb back to his former self.

That had been the first and deepest weariness.

The second was very current and significantly more urgent than the first.

The poison of this cursed heir of Toxin was eating away at his strength.

“Enough,” he said, setting off a reaction within his body, his children swarming about him and enveloping him. “I was hoping for a worthy adversary, but… alas.”

Seeing the situation turn against him, Eik fired devastating attack after devastating attack at the Lord, and it did do damage, but not enough to stop the Lord’s transformation or the darkness that continued to flood the space around them and steadily cut Eik off from reality.

Hundreds of liters of Profound Toxin along with more than a thousand high-powered Living Manifestations ran on auto pilot against the fiend while Eik launched himself toward one of the last few spots of vanishing light.

Like hell he was about to let himself be imprisoned in here with that creepy ass bastard.

“You are not going… anywhere!” the Lord rasped, his voice even more alien coming through the armor which still hadn’t stopped undulating and expanding outward. Although the four-armed bastard was near invisible against the backdrop of the depthless blackness, Eik could tell that he was nearly twice the size he had been before already.

Tendrils shot out of the darkness and wrapped themselves around Eik’s ankles first, then slithered up his legs to constrict around his hips and then chest. At a thought, he shifted into Toxic Liquefaction and then back into human form in an instant, free of the bindings.

But as if the escape had been predicted, more tendrils were already reaching him and wrapping around him again. He repeated the escape twice more but each time he did, it slowed him down.

At this rate, his path to freedom would be closed off before he could make it out.

The fourth time he was bound, rather than simply transforming into liquid, a shell layer of crystalline toxin, no thicker than a couple of centimeters, snapped into existence tightly across his entire body. It had barely formed before he Accelerated it.

It hurt like crazy, the kinetic energy crushing him in the same way that he had finished many opponents before. But he survived, and because he didn’t have to shift forms, he could instead create a new layer of shell. Another layer went on top of that one, and when the tendrils again wrapped around his body, he detonated only the outer layer, the inner shell largely protecting him from harm.

He did that once more before a grunt of irritation sounded from the center of the darkness, already far behind him.

A moment later, as he was forming a new outer layer of shell, a beam of black suddenly broke through the crystal on his chest, piercing his torso through and through. Eik gasped as blackened feathers were ripped off, the glow running along the channels on their surfaces stuttering and dying as they disappeared in the darkness.

Blood bubbled up in Eik’s throat as miserable pain spread across his breast. Tendrils of darkness shot forth, this time getting a firm hold of his body and pulling him further into the dark while the remnants of the black beam—yet another parasitic swarm—dove into his wound.

It was as if their first fight was repeating itself.

The last of the windows to the light outside finally closed up, and Eik was blind.

“And thus, you have reached… your end.” the voice of the Lord of the Moon whispered all around Eik, as if it was coming from the darkness itself. It might well have.

Eik felt another rod pierce him, this time through the side of his stomach.

“That's not a prison, bitch!” Eik snarled, finding it difficult to speak. Abruptly, his feathers lit up brightly as power surged through his tired body. “This is a prison!”

Below them, lighting up the darkness suddenly, appeared a circle of blue haze. It measured about forty meters in diameter and immediately began to hum with a screeching, discordant grind. In the same moment, above, a single speck of Profound Toxin widened into an intimidating, roiling sheet of liquid which matched the circle below in size and parallel position perfectly.

Before the Lord could react at all, a barrier of hyper reinforced crystal connected them and completed the azure death cage and trapping him in an even smaller space within his own domain.

With their extremely condensed size, the ability that combined the normally enormously vast Apocalypse Canvas and Extinction Zone skills was operating with a focused intensity that became immediately apparent.

It took less than a second for the bottom part made up of the Extinction Zone, to whir from nothing into a churning engine of death, pumping lethal, toxic aura into the tiny field. And that didn’t need any wounds to invade the Lord’s body.

“Extinction Canvas,” Eik breathed through gritted teeth, Profound Toxin already working at full power in his wound to exterminate the parasitic infection that was eating away at his vital force to feed it to their disgusting master. The connected Apocalypse Canvas above was also launching the first few hundred mid-sized projectile. “It’s called that because I will cover every square centimeter of its walls completely with your life blood.”

Rather than offer even a single word of response to Eik’s improvised, and really quite embarrassing attempt at mid-fight trash talk, the Lord of the Moon didn’t hesitate a moment to attack, lunging for him at extreme speed.

Only the light produced by Eik’s own body, toxin, and the glowing boundaries of the Extinction Canvas provided enough light to even see it coming.

But Eik had expected it and narrowly ducked the violent attempt, but when he whirled to put distance between them, something seized him roughly by the bunched feathers running from the base of his skull and down his back. He was yanked backward, over the Lord’s crescent head, and hurled against wall of the Extinction Canvas like a snowball against a street sign.

A rod slammed into his bicep and pinned him to the azure barrier. Imbued with his will, the crystal walls were incredibly sturdy and only allowed for a part of the rod equal to the length of Eik’s finger to penetrate before it was stopped. Instantly, the broken wall closed around the rod and rebuilt the spot even thicker than before. That didn’t leave Eik with a very good way to get the thing out of his arm though.

Far past hesitation, he ripped his arm free in one, vigorous movement, tearing through flesh and bone alike. God damn, that hurt a lot.

“Shit!” he cursed under his breath and directed his attention back to the Lord. “I knew I hated those fucking feathers!”

Layers of shell were already appearing in front of him for protection as he rushed back, but no more projectiles came. The Lord had left the center and was hammering at the opposite wall while simultaneously avoiding or shattering shards from the Apocalypse Canvas.

Parasites were already scattering across the surface and eating away at the obstacle, but the extreme regenerative speed of the crystal and deadly environment inside the Extinction Canvas made for slow progress.

“Give it up,” Eik said as he practically teleported behind the fiend, slicing deeply into what seemed to be the Lord’s trapezius muscle.

Just as the effect of the toxin were really beginning to show, the Lord’s suit of parasites seemed to roil violently across his skin once more.

In a double counter, he weaved yet another sphere of blackness, smaller again that Eik’s Extinction Canvas. Immediately, the mounting effects of Eik’s skill were nullified and he instead found himself swarmed by a parasitic swarm that moved faster than any before.

Frantic, he reached for Extinction Canvas once more, building it even smaller than this second black sphere, while the undulating mass of darkness ate away at his tissue and energy. At something like ten meters in diameter, it churned away and absolutely loaded toxin into the Lord, tiny wounds on his body now refusing to close and allowing even liquid Profound Toxin to invade.

When the Lord, for a third time, began constructing a field of darkness within Eik’s second Extinction Canvas, Eik Accelerated about half of the toxin already in the fiend’s system. Since the first moment they had begun to struggle to imprison each other, ruthless attacks had been flying non-stop, but none of them could focus enough to properly breach defenses.

The Accelerant rocked him violently and disrupted the cohesion of his transformed body, but it wasn’t enough to prevent the blackness from swallowing them.

While he might not be able to see well, in a space this small, Eik could easily navigate using just the Profound Toxin that was all around, and with a quick reach for Toxic Liquefaction, he practically teleported through the liquid and appeared behind the Lord, driving a short blade into the back of his neck.

Had they not been X-ranked, that would have finished the fight.

But instead of falling dead to the ground, the Lord of the Moon reached back with one arm from each side, seizing Eik by the torso.

Crap.
