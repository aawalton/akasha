Anya set her luggage down as she waited for Seb to step through the portal behind her. The building in front of her couldn't be the school. There must have been some sort of mix-up and they'd arrived at the wrong location. The building was too small, too plain, too ordinary to be an elite training hall. Where were they supposed to train, or even interact with anyone.

Seb?

We're at the right place, right time, he sent back. I wasn't picturing an old one-room schoolhouse though.

A what?

Images flashed over the band, memories of things he'd seen as a child in books or movies. It wasn't a perfect match, but close enough she had to agree with him.

"You're right on time," a man said as he stepped out the front door. "Lady Lysanya and Lord Sebastian of Holding Syl, yes?"

"Yes," she answered. "And you are?"

"No one important. Or rather no one you need to worry about."

"So this is the school?"

"Oh no. The School is not here, nor anywhere nearby. You are in the right place however."

"Why are we here?" she asked.

"To get to the School of course. Everyone who attends a training hall must be met by a Doorkeeper. I am the most convenient one for you."

"Will our visitors have to pass this way?" Seb asked.

"Visitors? Oh, you mean family or retainers? They will arrive at the guest area directly. But you are attendees and must be greeted."

"Then we greet the Doorkeeper," she said.

"Greetings and salutations. Alas I am not the one who greets you, only sends you onward to your proper place."

Deep breaths, Seb sent.

If he doesn't tell us what-

"Enter the school, you will pass through to your evaluator and they will let you know where to go next."

"You said the school wasn't here."

"No, the School isn't here, the school however is right there," he answered as he pointed behind him. "Now hurry along, you aren't the only ones I must let through the doors today."

I guess we- Seb began before her frustration cut him off.

She wasn't mad at him, she wasn't even upset at him. She just wanted a straight answer and it seemed this Doorkeeper wanted to cause nothing more than confusion. Fine, they'd enter the schoolhouse and see what came next.

She picked up her luggage, the packs bulkier than anything they ever used for the Depths, but still likely missing something they'd find themselves needing, and crossed the distance between the portal and the school. Seb followed behind her, his thoughts calmer, more focused, yet still filled with irritation.

~ * ~

Anya might have missed the portal transition as she stepped through the door if Seb hadn't warned her. It felt like stepping through any doorway, yet a quick view of the Energy he sensed told a different story.

We can see through, it isn't a portal, she sent.

We could see through Valene's office door.

But you can't see through portals…

We can see through our Holding, even though it's on the surface and in Deep Earth.

But that's a Seed portal, not a normal portal, she sent back.

She nearly stumbled, feeling Seb's thoughts and body following closely. Seb was sure it was a portal, so it must be a portal. But it behaved like their Seed. Had Valene's office been part of a Seed?

She pushed the thought away. It didn't matter. Right now what was important was whatever this evaluation was, not whether they were inside a Seed. Her eyes took in the room, a single table with Seer stones set into it and two men, one greying, the other so thin he looked like a skeleton sitting behind it.

"This will be quick," the older of the two said as he rose. "I am the Dean Jean of the Scattered Creek Training Hall. I'm aware of how my name and title go together and I do not have time for comments about it."

The Dean stepped around the table, straightening his jacket as he did. Anya found herself looking him in the eye as he walked past them, their heights almost identical. He didn't so much look old, though his grey hair made it clear he was, as weary.

"This is Seer Marten, he will be responsible for your knowledge curriculum. Once he validates that you are indeed eligible for entry into the hall."

"Come closer," Marten said, gesturing to the table with a bony hand. "I know you feudal types rarely deal with Council Seers but I'm sure you're familiar with having your Energy Pattern verified."

Feudal types? Seb sent.

Not used to Council Seers?

"Seer Marten," Anya said as she stepped over. "Our Seer was a Council Seer until she joined our Holding."

"Of course she was." Marten all but sneered. "Many find their way into Holdings. Enticed by the opportunity to show off their knowledge and lay about rather than do proper work."

"She was-"

"I'm sure she was someone of note, someone important. After all, she did join you and you are young elites. Place your hands on the stones."

He..! she sent.

Seb placed his hand on one of the stones set into the table. Anya took a deep breath and did the same, refusing to let this Seer Marten provoke a reaction. He didn't know who Fay was, he didn't know who they were.

"Lady Lysanya Syl," Marten said. "Lord Sebastian Syl. Partners? Interesting. Newly established Holding less than a decade ago? So you found a Core while you were Diving and made the best of it. Formerly worked with Seer Liaison Phelann… Her? I'd heard she'd finally vanished, finally given up her place as a protege and researcher but still."

"You knew Feh-lan?" Anya said, feeling odd as she mispronounced the name.

"Knew of her more like it. Kept to herself, never a word for anyone. Always too busy with her work, too focused on whatever OverSeer Valene asked of her."

"Valene? He's still around then," the Dean said.

"He is," Anya answered. "We last saw him a few months ago."

"You? You spoke with-"

"Did they pass?" Jean cut Marten off abruptly.

"Yes sir. She has a handful of days before we'd need to determine exactly when she hit level 15. He has, this can't be right, a year and a half."

"If they've passed we are done and can deal with the next group."

The Dean turned and gestured back the way they had come.

"Congratulations. Welcome to Scattered Creek. Normally you would need to complete your induction evaluation to determine where you will reside, however in light of your group size and constraints put upon this class you will be in the Coordinator Dorm."

"Yes Dean," Seb said.

We don't get a choice? she sent.

It's a school, I'm sure they choose for everyone.

"Master Leo and Mistress Sarna will perform your evaluation and then direct you to your quarters. We will be unlikely to speak again before graduation as long as you remain in good standing."

"Off you go," Marten said, waving them away impatiently. "You're done here."

No, Seb sent.

He-

-is trying to provoke us.

It's working.

She felt Seb's emotions shift, a mixture of glee and focus she'd only rarely felt when they were faced with a frustrating goblin. They both turned to leave, collecting their luggage, only for Seb to pause and glance back at the table.

"Thank you for your time Dean," he said lightly. "Seer Marten, OverSeer Valene was our guest a few months ago. We were having a small get together to celebrate."

Before anything more could be said Seb had stepped through the portal. Anya caught a glimpse of the shock on Marten's face in the second before she followed him through and pushed the door shut behind her, slamming it before anything more could be said.

Seb! she sent as her shoulders shook in silent laughter.

Got him?

You- yes.
