Ashton instinctively pulled his hands in front of his mouth, trying not to make even the slightest noise. It took him a couple seconds to realise that there was no reason to do that.

No matter how much noise he were to make, even if that were possible within the veil anyway, it wouldn't be able to reach the physical realm. Maybe if Ashton cast a spell from here, he might be able to do so, but that would have to be a very, very deliberate choice.

He looked back down at the wyvern. Usually, for living beings to appear within the veil, they had to have an extremely strong soul. Sure, maybe if he tried to or once his 'veil mastery' skill improved a lot, he could see regular people, but this?

This wyvern was an artificial creation. A monster that was made and dropped here by the interdimensional council. Frankly, these guys shouldn't even have real souls. They were artificial things, barely good enough for something like a necromancer to access. At least... that was Ashton's understanding of it until now.

It wasn't like he had ever studied souls all that much. Maybe Millie would be able to tell him more about that, if her ability, at its core, really was to let her see the essence of souls.

But whatever the case was, he knew that there was no way a normal monster should have a strong enough soul to cause something like this, even if it was planned as a region boss in the future.

Ashton stared at the form of the sleeping monster, standing right in front of it. Like this, sleeping peacefully, it almost looked cute. Slowly, Ashton put his hand onto the creature's snout.

'If only you weren't a bloodthirsty monster that's out to kill everyone in this town,' Ashton thought, mentally wanting to scoff.

But... why were the elementals so scared of the wyvern? It wasn't like this thing could actually do anything to them within the veil, was there?

Whatever the reason, Ashton was glad that he came here to investigate. With this, he at least knew where the wyvern's nest was, and could act accordingly. For now, he should just stay away from here until he had a good idea for how to take it down.

However, as Ashton turned, he felt shivers run down his spine. He didn't even know it was possible to feel that sort of thing without a body, but apparently it was.

The crimson wyvern woke up. It shifted slightly and opened its eyes. And when it did, and Ashton turned his head back toward it, he could swear that the wyvern was looking straight at him. Almost like it was trying to gauge him, and see what it was dealing with, the monster's deep gaze pierced right into Ashton.

The wyvern's eyes were intense, and far clearer than anything else about its body, at least for the first few moments of it waking up.

As it laid there in front of him, slowly pushing itself back up, it was practically shaking off any semblance of 'blurriness' that every other part of the veil had due to the constant shifting and changing that happened.

This wyvern was clear, almost exactly like when Ashton first saw it earlier this week. But something was definitely different about it. When he met it earlier, Ashton could feel intense hostility directed at him. He could feel such an intense fear that he threw himself into a pile of corpses to hide from this creature.

But now? There was none of that. Neither in any subtle 'bloodlust' sort of way, nor in its body language. Even when it was finally sitting up fully, the wyvern was still staring right at Ashton, though it wasn't in the way that a beast would look at its prey.

It was in a weirdly... dignified way. Still beastly, still wild in every sense of the word, but Ashton had encountered many magical beasts in Lumia that gave off this exact sensation. Rulers of their domain that would never slaughter mindlessly for fun, but simply live in tune with nature and their natural instincts to sate their hunger and protect themselves.

That was what Ashton was feeling. And there was no way that an artificially created monster could ever give off that sort of sensation... was there?

And just as Ashton asked himself this, something struck the wyvern like a bolt of lightning.

It was a powerful strike of energy that almost knocked Ashton straight off his feet, but he knew what it was. Just the other day, he interfered with something like this, when he essentially forced the 'Eight-Legged Queen' to shut up and turned off their messages for at least a moment.

This was something from the 'system'. Or rather, the Interdimensional Council. The bolt of lightning struck the wyvern, and crackling energy flowed through its body. And almost instantly, its demeanour changed. Instead of sitting there, upright, the monster began to prowl.

Like a beast getting ready to jump at its prey that had already escaped once, it was baring its teeth.

Ashton knew that he was safe here; nothing could happen to him. Beings from the physical plane couldn't affect those on the astral plane. At the same time... they shouldn't be able to see them, either.

The moment Ashton moved back slightly, the wyvern snapped at him. Without a moment's hesitation, Ashton leapt backward, moving out of the way of the monster, but it didn't let up in the slightest. Before he even landed, the beast leapt at him again.

Ashton reached the far wall with that first jump, but even so, the distance was closed in an instant. Ashton kept moving around, bouncing from wall to wall within the courtyard, and the wyvern followed behind him every step of the way.

The way it was moving, and the way it was latching onto the walls, it was clear that nothing but destruction was left in its way. For just a brief moment, Ashton was worried, in case someone was hiding out in the flats, but that thought was abandoned almost immediately.

There was no way anyone could live in this building with the crimson wyvern right there. They would either succumb to the pressure or would fall victim to the monster as it was looking for food. This place was utterly abandoned. It just had to be.

Ashton kept climbing up higher and higher along the building with each jump, barely able to dodge the wyvern's fangs. And then, in the end, Ashton jumped off the top of the building and pushed himself as high as he could. In the astral plane, you weren't tied to physical limits, so Ashton could practically fly here if he was able to imagine it.

Of course, he wasn't acting like he could out-fly the wyvern, and he wasn't going to start a chase through the entire town. Ashton had to contain this here, and make it so that he could safely lose the monster.

The wyvern tried to snap at Ashton one more time, and the moment that its maw closed shut, he slowed down and pushed himself back down. He tried to speed up as much as he could, but the wyvern's dive couldn't be underestimated.

Like an eagle, it was able to descend in almost an instant. But since Ashton wasn't tied to gravity, he wasn't just falling either, and could push himself down at a speed where he at least didn't have his legs bitten off.

He slammed through one of the windows on the ground floor, and the wyvern, unable to fully get rid of its momentum, broke through the outer wall. That alone shook it momentarily, but it wasn't enough for Ashton to escape. He ran into the hallway, and watched as the wyvern squeezed through and ripped apart the walls like they were made of paper.

The ceiling as well was starting to crack and crumble. Ashton was hoping to lure the wyvern inside so that it would get stuck somewhere, but that didn't seem to be an option.

Seriously, why was this thing so powerful? How was anyone supposed to kill this monster?

'I have to change my plan,' he thought to himself. Getting the wyvern stuck wasn't going to work, at least not here. But since the wyvern could cut through the walls ridiculously easy, Ashton figured he should take advantage of that. Even if it meant destroying a place filled with numerous of his memories.

Ashton started weaving through the different flats, pushing through rooms and making sure that the wyvern was causing as much destruction as possible.

All that was left behind in the places that were once someone's home, someone's life, was dust and rubble. With every step, it felt like a dagger being pushed deeper into his guts. It was one thing to take what you needed to survive from a home, and another to destroy it completely because of a mistake you made.

If Ashton had just listened to the fact that the elementals were as scared as they were, then this wouldn't have happened.

And when finally, the building started to buckle in under its own weight, Ashton watched as the wyvern was crushed. This wouldn't be enough to kill it—definitely not—but it should give Ashton the time he needed to get away from here.

Of course, Ashton was fairly safe. While he could see the effects of the wyvern on its surroundings, the astral plane didn't change as rapidly as the physical plane, so he was able to get out of the way fast enough.

He could sense the elementals that he had contracted; they had already moved out of the way, but Ashton still quickly rushed over toward them and made sure that they all left together and were fully safe. He doubted that the wyvern would go after elementals, but this wasn't something that he wanted to risk.

Once he had built up some distance, hiding in a direction where the wyvern wouldn't cause much destruction if it did manage to follow him, Ashton decided to just wait for a while.

If the wyvern managed to free itself, he didn't want to guide it right to Calibur.

'What the actual fuck?' Ashton thought when he was actually able to calm down after a while. 'How was it able to see me?'

Whichever the case, Ashton clearly had to be far, far more careful. He expected there to be no dangers here in the veil, but at the same time, it wasn't like he knew all that much about this.

There were elementals here, so what if there were other creatures hiding out that he didn't know existed?

Plus, if the wyvern was able to see him, what else could?

Of course, there was clearly something special about the crimson wyvern, but that didn't mean that it was completely unique. Surely, many constellations were able to see him here as well. Ones like the 'Observer in the Veil' would definitely be able to watch him intensely even in here.

He had to get back. Once he woke up and traded with the Salesman, Ashton had to ask them for books about the veil and astral projection. A lot of his basic assumptions might be wrong, so he just had to be completely sure.

But for now, once he made sure he wasn't being chased, and that the elementals were safe again, Ashton decided to head back to his body.
