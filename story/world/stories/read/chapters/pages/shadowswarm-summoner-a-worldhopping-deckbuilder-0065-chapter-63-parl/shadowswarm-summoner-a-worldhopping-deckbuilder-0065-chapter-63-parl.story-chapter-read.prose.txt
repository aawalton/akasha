Chapter 63: Parl

If there was one thing Ambrose was tired of, it was stinky moles and their stupid rustic accent, and this entire damned world. Why couldn’t they have gotten stranded on a beach world, or just someplace with sunlight? He was tired, sore, and just plain weary. The joy of getting new cards, the hope he’d found in dealing with Javon, all dashed by a stupid, fat, smelly mole with bad teeth and a chip on his shoulder.

“Wait a minute,” he said, getting to his feet. His imps flew to him, and the Timer continued, but there was a possible chance they could reset things. “Before you kill us, do you even know who we are, or what we’ve done?”

Boss Parl paused for a second. “I don’t reckon I care, human. I got the call from Raynor to take you out, so here I am.”

“Oh, well then, you’re definitely going to want us alive,” he continued, reaching down to pet one of his [Hateful Imps]. He sensed distress at first, and then an odd sort of contentment. “Raynor’s got business with us, on account of us being his mortal enemy’s agents.”

“Ambrose!” Jessica chastised, shocked or disappointed in him.

It didn’t matter, he just needed to buy time. The guards on either side of them were content to just form a wall and block their progress. It formed a little arena, with them and the boss-man in a thirty by thirty foot ring. He was big, taller than Raeleq by at least a foot, and his body was way more muscular than the other molekin had been.

He was also the only one Ambrose had seen wear that much jewelry, and the gaudy chains and rings on those big claws just looked goofy on him. It was the display of wealth from someone who only had a vague idea of what wealth looked like. Even the Adams in decline wouldn’t have been caught dead wearing any of that, but telling Parl that might’ve been a mistake.

“You got ten seconds to keep my interest, before I start squashin’.”

Ambrose looked around. His friends were scared, but resolute. And more than that, they trusted him. After all they’d been through, they trusted him to either make things better, or at least not make them worse. He smiled and brushed dust off his shirt.

“We’re here on a secret mission from Charles Vanderborn. He told us to keep it low key because of Raynor, and we’ve been trying to do just that. Honestly, you’re getting in the way of the Emperor’s will.”

Boss Parl chewed on that for a second, nose bouncing in place. “Yeah, that’s what the call what came said. The scouts didn’t believe it, Raynor didn’t believe it, so I don’t believe it.”

Raeva snickered loud enough to be heard.

The massive molekin’s head jerked around in a flash. “Somethin’ funny to you, dead-girl?”

“Yes,” said Raeva honestly. “It’s just…you aren’t really king of this world at all, are you? Raynor is. You’re just a local puppet, there to babysit the world for him.”

It wasn’t the right time to get him mad, but Ambrose appreciated the effort just the same. A few of the guards even laughed, which set Parl off worse.

“The hell point you trying to make here? This is my world, and I watch over it for the people who got themselves multiple worlds.” He was oddly petulant for someone about to try to hurt them.

“Does that mean Raynor would just pick someone else if we killed you?” Jessica asked, catching on to the game.

“Yeah,” said Raeleq, “it doesn’t seem like a very stable position. Aren’t you afraid of being replaced?”

“Maybe don’t antagonize him?” Dexi’Lynn croaked, making herself small.

“No! No I am not! I am not gonna get replaced. The hell are we even talking about?” Boss Parl gnashed his teeth menacingly. Steam practically blew from his ears, but it was too late. Ambrose’s imps disappeared entirely. There hadn’t been fighting for too long, and combat ended.

The mole understood, and let out a frustrated, garbled cry of rage.

Round 1

Cantrip: [Vampiric Kiss], Spell: [Shred Mind], Creature: [Spiritsnipe Imp], Creature: [Festering Wretch], Spell: [Thought Tap]

The molekin’s face froze, twisted in hate. They had a chance now. It wasn’t much of a breather, but at least they had whole access to their decks again. This meant it was time to take it seriously. Beating Parl wouldn’t necessarily save their lives, but it would at least be some kind of blow against the Empire. Even if it was, as they said, a temporary one.

It also meant a chance to use his shiny new card.

“Careful,” Jessica yelled, “He’s got two affinities, Wild and Primal!”

Which meant at least Affinity Level 6 or 7. Maybe higher. They could handle it.

A line of four summoning circles formed in front of them, but Boss Parl chose to just dive in. His claws shone red for a split second, and the next thing Ambrose knew, he’d cleared the distance and slammed his colossal fist into Raeleq’s face. The younger Ransa buckled and staggered backwards, blood freely streaming from his nose.

None of them had weapons out or could do anything but use their fists while their summons finished. In that time, he whirled around and backhanded Luthor.

Naturally, he’d been the only one to hold off on summons and went with his cantrip.

The blow collided, and the spell detonated in proportion with the power behind it. It was enough to blow everyone back, and shatter the claws on Boss Parl’s left hand. The arm Luthor used to block dangled uselessly at his side.

Ambrose realized then, that they might be out of their league. None of the guards were joining in. There had to be a reason for that.

The [Spiritsnipe Imp] was a lean, small, sleek thing with a single curved horn in its forehead and tiny, useless looking wings. It took off like a thrown javelin and launched itself at Boss Parl’s considerable ass, piercing into him and then disappearing in a puff of smoke.

Things Paused once more, and information flooded Ambrose’s mind at dizzying speeds. He saw then, Boss Parl’s entire deck, as well as his Affinity Levels and stats.

Boss Parl Henderson

Level 5 Primal, Level 4 Wild Wizard

Hand Size: 5, Mana: 13, Combat Deck Size: 8/8 slots filled

Cantrip: [Alpha Strike], Cantrip: [Crater Jump], Signature Creature: [Tunnel Wyrm], Spell: [Bulk], Spell: [Immolation Armor], Creature: [Spider Matriarch], Spell: [Rumble], Spell: [Jolt], Creature: [Primal Bat], Spell: [Bristlebark Armor], Creature: [Behemoth Beetle]

Ambrose was floored by the information. In the dwindling time he had left, he could see what each spell did, and roughly how fucked they were. More importantly, he saw what was in Boss Parl’s hand and had a chance to get rid of it.

He chose [Immolation Armor: Enchantment. Set yourself aflame in the fires of your rage.

Spell Effect: Radiate fire in a five foot radius around you. 5 Mana, 5 Mana Ongoing]

Things sped up, only now he had Boss Parl’s attention. A raptor gnawed at his leg, and he barely swatted at it. “Oh, you afraid of burning, String Bean? Don’t worry, I gotchu.”

He ignored Luthor’s [Spirit Warrior] entirely, and made a beeline for him.

Ambrose had just enough time to be really, truly terrified before the molekin wrapped a meaty claw around his midsection and squeezed.

All the air left his body in an explosive, heaving gasp, and the next thing he knew, Boss Parl stomped his way into frightful momentum and hurled him with all his might.

He tried to suck in air, but that squeeze had knocked something out of him. Ambrose spun wildly in the air, getting more disoriented by the second with each flip and turn. By the time he thought to use [Cloud Cushion], it was too late.

He flipped around and saw what was beneath him. Bedrock Spire’s many layers stretched out beneath him. He activated the card and slowed a little, but not enough. Ambrose continued to tumble forward too fast, and it wasn’t long before the lowest level of the city passed beneath him.

Leaving just a drop of three miles, straight into magma below.

Chapter 64: Big Boss Beatdown

Raeleq’s incredibly sore jaw dropped. There Ambrose went, flipping and twisting through the air as he pitched off the side. From a million miles away, Jessica screamed in anger and grief, but there was no time to do anything about it. There was no time to do anything for Ambrose, or maybe even themselves at this point.

But he still had to try. Warm blood still streamed from his nostrils, but he’d suffered a broken nose before. It hurt like a bitch and soon the dimness of Stonskarr would be a blessing. Assuming they survived the next ten minutes. Ambrose bought him several precious seconds. Enough for [Reggie] to finish summoning.

The assembled guards screamed their approval. How many of them had Ambrose taken out before Parl showed up?

As soon as he could, Raeleq directed the dinosaur to attack the mole while he summoned up his [Ironwood Club], like he should’ve done from the start.

Boss Parl let out a deep, ugly laugh, and turned on them. A green light streamed around his body as he cast his next spell. Armor materialized, made of some kind of thick, knotty wood with brambles and thorns wrapped around it. It covered him from the neck down, stopping at his wrists and ankles. [Reggie] snapped forward to take a bite.

The dinosaur’s teeth closed over those brambles and crunched on the armor. The mole grunted in discomfort, but Raeleq felt the pain and distress in his Signature Creature scream in the back of his mind. He released, and blood dripped from his maw. It didn’t seem to belong to the mole at all.

Luthor stood and cast a healing spell while their foe decked [Reggie] in the jaw. It wasn’t enough to kill the creature, but it made him reel and take another couple of punches.

Raeleq cried out in anger of his own and slammed his club into Boss Parl’s back, right where he imagined the bastard’s spine would be. The second the club hit, the impact shot up his arms and nearly made him black out.

It was like hitting the side of a mountain. However strong he was, the armor enhanced it and sent the pain back his way. He barely held onto the club, and went to try again.

Boss Parl turned and caught it in his damaged claw. He yanked, and it ripped out of Raeleq’s hands. “Yer used to being the big and strong one, huh?” the molekin practically purred. “How’s that workin’ out for ya now?”

“Raeleq, get back,” Raeva shouted, spell forming in her hands.

The molekin ignored her, a recovering [Reggie], Jessica’s [Astral Viper], and even arrows from the [Ancestral Archer], which stuck out of the armor uselessly. With another big, nasty grin, Boss Parl swung the [Ironwood Club] into Raeleq’s stomach.

The guards cheered once more, screaming and jeering from the sidelines.

He crumpled to the ground. Never before had he known such pain and helplessness. It was like a button that turned off his breathing and ability to move. Raeleq lay there helplessly, struggling to suck in air and twitching while the malicious molekin raised the club again. This time, he knew, it was going to be a killing blow.

It was the worst time for the Pause.

Round 2

Cantrip: [Ironwood Club], Creature: [Pack Raptor], Spell: [Rose Charm], [Control Overgrowth]

Laying on the ground, pain stretched out thanks to the Pause, Raeleq saw death descending on him. How cruel, to see it coming and be able to squeeze an extra ten seconds out of it before it ended. Would his ancestors laugh at him, or treat him gently?

Wait. Out of the corner of his eye, there was a red light.

The fight resumed.

Raeva’s spell finished, and she launched a [Flame Lance] into Boss Parl’s exposed back. The spell pierced the armor and partway and detonated, destroying it entirely. Unfortunately for them, the thorny bramble exploded outwards, and she took a burning branch to the face for her troubles.

The molekin budged, stumbling forward a step. He dropped the club next to Raeleq’s head and turned around, irritated. This time, when he punched [Reggie], the dinosaur disappeared.

He took that chance to cast [Rose Charm], choosing the healing mode. A gentle pink light washed over him. It tickled, but the spell took time to charge, and things were looking grim.

“You bastard,” Jessica screamed, tears flowing freely. She cast what had become her go-to spell and launched the molekin in the air with [Lift].

It was a bad idea.

Boss Parl hung in the air for a second, still mildly on fire, but obviously unconcerned. The tell-tale glow of magic gathered around his feet and bottom. He didn’t fall so much as launch himself downwards, butt first.

He collided with the ground, leaving another huge dent and blowing everyone back. Raeleq rolled over to the borders of the arena, where a guard stopped him with a kick to the back. It hurt, but the [Rose Charm] finished casting and the physical damage reversed almost all the way. The pain and struggle to breathe remained, but it was enough. The card deactivated upon use.

Raeleq jumped to his feet and ignored the man shoving him forward as he cast [Pack Raptor] and jumped back into the fray.

His sister got back up in time to have to throw herself to the side to avoid being stabbed by huge claws. Luthor managed to get his sword and shield out and tried to bury the blade in Boss Parl’s back. It sunk in a few inches, but mostly, it got stuck and pissed the mole off.

“Yer all a bunch of annoying fuckers, aren’tcha?” he growled. “I’ve had my fun, but I think it’s over.”

The [Astral Viper] reared up and struck, but Boss Parl just let it. Starry fangs sank into the meat of his forearm. He waited, then grabbed the snake by the neck and squeezed. A second later, it popped out of existence. Then he pointed a broken clawtip at Jessica. “That was yours, huh? You miss your friend? You wanna join him?”

Everyone knew what that meant, and acted at once. The [Pack Raptor] appeared at his side, and Raeleq directed it at Parl. The same went for the [Ancestral Archer], still loosing arrow after arrow into their foe. A [Kindling Phoenix] arced high, readying for a dive.

None of them reached him in time.

While she might not have been very strong, Jessica loved battle for the sense of clarity it gave her. Her busy, overactive brain finally focused, and the only things that mattered were her instincts, her sharp senses and finely tuned body, and the help of her friends.

There was none of that clarity now. Ambrose was gone, and everything hurt. It was like this blaring siren in her head, inescapable and deafening. When Boss Parl grabbed her, part of her was almost relieved. She was exhausted, enraged, sad, and also still a bit high.

But her instincts were strong, and she cast her cantrip at the last second, right as he wound up.

The molekin swung her around at a dizzying speed that would’ve been fun in other circumstances. [Weakness] went off just in time, sapping the strength and momentum from him. When Boss Parl released, Jessica flew only five feet before hitting the warped metal ground.

It hurt, but it didn’t hurt as bad as falling into lava would.

She used the momentum to roll to her feet, right as the round ended.

When he came back down, [Crater Jump] ended the raptors, and the fight. Raeleq had trouble getting up after that.

The guards grabbed them all, and Jessica didn’t have enough fight left in her to try to break away. Some had weapons out, ready to execute them at a moment’s notice, but they waited for the word.

And him.” She pointed to Raeleq as well. “You need the two of them. The rest you might as well toss off the side. And there’s another person here on their side too.”
