His body stilled as his eyes quickly snapped to the right to see a massive slitted eye staring right at him.

Archie’s right arm moved before the thought formed in his mind. His hand blurred forward, Hyper Impact, and the massive eye vanished, leaving behind a perfectly circular hole punched through the leaves and creating a similar-sized hole in the tree directly across from the creature’s body.

A half-second later, a compressed blast of air erupted from the arc of his swing, shaking every tree around him hard enough that bits of moss and bark that clung onto their limbs nearby began to drop off in clumps, while the tree caught in the crossfire let out a groan as it began to loudly splinter.

The branch under him bounced from the shock as he just now noticed that there were two slimy, prehensile antenna-like things inches from brushing against his neck, whipped backward like wet ropes as they were attached to the head of the creature that snuck up behind him.

Twin thuds hit the ground not a few seconds later as his mind fully came back to him at what had just happened.

*[Thousand-Eye Moth Lv 100] has been slain – XP has been given.*

Archie raised an eyebrow as he stared at the corpse while ignoring the throbbing of his right arm and shoulder as he had so suddenly activated Hyper Impact without bracing for the kickback it normally gave him.

The creature was massive, easily bigger than he expected for something that got that close without him noticing. Its wings folded in on themselves, scattering dusty scales everywhere.

“What in the kaiju…?” he muttered under his breath as he stared at the camouflaged giant moth, blood pouring from the massive wound in its lower right wing. And now that he was able to see it from a distance, he saw that it had three massive red, slitted eyes in the remaining corners of its wings.

He reactivated Vital Sight and quickly began to scan the area around him again, making sure another oversized insect wasn’t about to try the same thing. After a few breaths of nothing moving except branches settling, he turned his attention back toward the stronghold.

Down below, the remaining Bi-Beak Dodos were sprinting toward the courtyard with the cluster of dome-shaped buildings.

Then something else came into view.

“…Uh oh,” Archie muttered as he caught sight of what they were running toward – a familiar creature he hadn’t seen in quite a while.

[Emberlash Eudromaeosaur Lv 106]

“Okay, this confirms it,” Archie said, already pushing off the branch and harshly landing just beside the corpse of the Thousand-Eye Moth and tucked it away into his Spatial Storage Cube for core harvesting.

After doing so, he sprinted across the uneven terrain, leaping over broken slabs of stone and the occasional toppled pillars semi-covered in moss and grass.

As the screeching and whip-cracks of fire grew louder near the courtyard, Archie’s lips began to split and grow into a smile, almost entirely washing away the lingering surprise from his earlier jump scare.

The thought he’d had when he first saw the ruined stronghold was proving to be real.

By the time he vaulted through a cracked archway, he got a first clear look at the fight happening inside.

The Bi-Beak Dodos and the Emberlash Eudromaeosaurs were tearing into each other. Dodos were hacking up placenta-wrapped creatures and using them like blunt projectiles and or distractions, while the Eudromaeosaurs lunged with snapping jaws and flinging fire with their flame-coated tails.

“This is definitely the dungeon I saw in the Group Tutorial,” Archie said while weaving past a dodo that had just been kicked into a wall. “Dungeon of the Lost Valley.”

“Which means,” he said while stepping forward into the courtyard, “I can fight the Primalsaur again.”

Archie’s excitement was obvious as his fist slammed into the head of the nearest Emberlash Eudromaeosaur. With Adrenaline Rush pushed to 7%, its skull simply erupted, spraying chunks of bone and gore across the two Bi-Beak Dodos beside it — and coating his gauntlet-clad fist in the process.

They barely had time to jerk their heads back in shock while their powerful, yet thin legs barely kicked up off the ground before Archie had already activated Savage Charge, crossing the gap between them in a heartbeat after he’d killed the Eudromaeosaur and unleashing a flurry of blows into their bodies.

Each blow showing visible fist formations atop their bodies, and by the time his fists struck them a total of six times each they had died as the force behind his punches practically turned their insides to liquid and burst their hearts, but Archie did not stop until their bodies looked as though they had been tenderized – minorly surprised at just how springy their flesh was.

While it wasn’t anywhere near as springy as the Amphislimes were, their flesh was a lot more springy than other creatures that he’d punched.

Could that be an effect of how they store creatures – the material of their bodies must be spongy? He wondered, thinking of a pink, ever-hungry character from a game he used to play back on Earth, while he swept the three bodies into his Spatial Storage Cube.

After storing away their corpses, he shot toward the remaining horde of Dodos and Eudromaeosaurs with unrestrained glee.

*[Emberlash Eudromaeosaurs Lv 106] has been slain – XP has been given.**[Bi-Beak Dodo Lv 106] has been slain – XP has been given.**[Bi-Beak Dodo Lv 106] has been slain – XP has been given.*

The nearest Eudromaeosaur shrieked and lunged, jaws gaping wide as tufts of flame licked between its teeth. Archie ducked under the bite and slammed his shoulder into its scaled chest, hitting hard enough to send the creature skidding back several meters on its side.

Before the dinosaur could recover, he planted a boot on its neck, pinning it to the ground. It thrashed beneath him, trying to shake the human off — right up until Archie brought his other boot down on its head, stomping it into paste.

*[Emberlash Eudromaeosaurs Lv 106] has been slain – XP has been given.*

*Your Race has reached Lv 103 – Points allocated, +15 Free Points**Your Class has reached Lv 105 – Points allocated, +10 Free Points*

A shadow swept over him — one of the Dodos had leapt into the air above him, showing off its massive clawed feet that were large enough to wrap around a basketball.

Archie sidestepped away from where it would land on him and caught it by the neck mid-air, using its momentum that carried its legs in a useless arc as he drove it straight down, beak-first, into the ground. Its second beak snapped open in shock just before he repeatedly smashed his fist to its skull until all that was left of its head resembled that of the Eudromaeosaur.

Another Dodo sprinted toward him, screeching wildly as its feathers puffed up and its second beak unnaturally widened as it readied itself to swallow him whole.

Barely managing to dodge out of the way, as it was just about to pass by him, Archie front-kicked its right knee inwards, bending the limb the wrong direction, and toppling it sideways before he followed up by hammering his fist down into its temple.

Sending up an eruption of blood that coated his upper torso and head.

“Oh, I’ve never seen these creatures before,” Archie heard Vul reply from within his Soulscape, along with Volos and Arsenic letting out cheerful chittering cheers as they watched him kill what they were going to be eating later today.

Before Archie could address Vul, a whip of fire skimmed past his face and let loose a loud crack as an Eudromaeosaur had flanked him. Seeing that its attack had failed, it struck again.

Archie ducked, letting the flaming tail slice through the air above him, then punched up into the dinosaur’s stomach hard enough to lift the creature onto its hind legs. Feeling its ribs broken beneath his fist, Archie let the Eudromaeosaur fall to the ground and stomped down onto its head, stopping after feeling its skull give way beneath his heel.

Two more Dodos approached from opposite angles — one going low, the other high.

Archie pushed off the corpse beneath him, flipping backward and landing behind two Dodos that were charging at him. Just as they skidded to a halt where they had seen the human, thick green, thorn-wreathed vines shot from Archie’s arms and wrapped around their necks.

With a soft grunt, Archie yanked his arms back, snapping the heads of the Dodos back as their bodies were flung backwards towards Archie.

Catching the back of their feathered heads with his open palms, in one fell movement, Archie smashed their heads together like overripe melons.

Storing them away with a mana string, his head flicked to his left as he caught sight of another Dodo sprinting straight at him with its enlarged second beak stretching open, ready to swallow him whole.

Archie weaved in before it finished widening, ducking under the rim of the gaping fleshy maw, and punched up into its throat. The beak convulsed around his arm, trying to close, but before it could attempt to swallow Archie’s fist, it would need to deal with the explosive force of him activating Hyper Impact.

The bird didn’t even have time to spasm before it violently exploded, showering Archie with even more gore and feathers.

Pivoting on his heel and immediately sprinting toward the nearest horde of Emberlash Eudromaeosaurs and Bi-Beak Dodos, uncaring if any of them had seen what happened to the members of their pack.

I wonder how tough those other dinosaurs are now, Archie mused to himself as he tore out the core of an Emberlash Eudromaeosaurs after his left hand speared through its chest.

Emberlash Eudromaeosaurs cores were perfect for him to experiment with creating new gauntlets and or other experiments.

As the Emberlash Eudromaeosaur’s core vanished into his Spatial Storage Cube, two more of the Eudromaeosaurs snapped their flame-coated tails toward him, whipping out a rushing arc of fire the length of a bus at him.

Archie dropped backwards into a limbo, feeling the heat skim the tips of his hair as the arcs of fire sliced past him. As the arc went passed his head, it took the head clean off a Dodo thirty meters behind him.

The Dodo wasn’t even able to react as it had been mid-lunge onto a downed Emberlash.

As the arc passed over his head, Archie snapped upright and shot forward – his boot digging into the dirt for a brief moment before he launched forwards like a bullet into the ribs of the closet Eudromaeosaur.

His fist speared into its side, breaking through scale, cartilage, and bone until his fingers wrapped around something wet and twitching in its chest cavity.

“I wonder those tanky dinosaurs that shot their plate-like scales at me would have nutritious innards,” Archie mused to himself as he yanked hard, ripping a lung clean out of its chest cavity.

Without giving the lung any mind as it hit the ground with a wet slap, Archie took out his Mana Revolver of Magma from the Spatial Storage on his thigh and aimed it at the other Eudromaeosaur.

With his eyes faintly tinging molten red, Spirit Fusion activated as he accessed Vul’s Magma Affinity and fired three clean shots at the second Eudromaeosaur.

The first round struck its neck, melting through scale and flesh as the creature reeled with a strangled screech.

The second shot missed by a hair and slammed into the stone wall of the nearest building. The wall began to deform and melt like candle wax almost immediately.

The third round struck exactly where he aimed, sinking into the Eudromaeosaur’s upper neck and melting straight through it. Before it could even release another strangled screech, the Eudromaeosaur collapsed in two thuds — its head and body hitting the ground separately as the neck connecting them melted into sludge almost instantly.

“Damn, I really gotta work on my aim,” Archie muttered, annoyed he’d missed something barely two meters from him.

He took a step toward the next cluster of creatures, but stopped mid-stride.

His eyes flicked to the half-melted wall of the structure he’d accidentally shot.

Archie’s brows rose in genuine surprise, and a small, involuntary sound of interest slipped out of him.

“Oh?”
