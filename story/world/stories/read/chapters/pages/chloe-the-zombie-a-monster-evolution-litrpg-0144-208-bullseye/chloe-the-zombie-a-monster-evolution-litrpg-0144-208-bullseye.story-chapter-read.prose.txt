Administrator P threw back his monstrous head and burst into laughter, again. Chloe seriously couldn't believe this guy. Like, okay, he was a giant murder bird administrator and all that, but did he seriously have to be this obnoxious about it?

"Once I've finished dealing with you, I'll return to Ishgria and take care of her myself," he gloated, black lightning crackling between his feathers.

Chloe stared at him from her rather unfortunate position on the floor, her leg still pinned beneath that super-annoying shadow arrow. "Okay, first of all, rude. And second of all, you are, like, so obsessed with my mom, it's kinda embarrassing."

Seriously, did this guy have a life outside of threatening people? Then again, something about what he'd just said got Chloe thinking. She remembered how Administrator P had looked when he'd first appeared in the tunnel, before transforming into his giant monster form. His cloak had been all torn up and scorched, and one of his legs had kept twitching like it was having some kind of personal crisis.

Chloe's expression brightened as a very entertaining possibility occurred to her. "Wait, wait, wait. You were, like, totally messed up when you showed up here. Does that mean my mom kicked your butt before you came down here?"

After Chloe said that, Administrator P went completely still. Chloe blinked at him. Oh my gosh. She totally did, didn't she?

"NO WAY!" Chloe blurted out, her eyes widening with genuine excitement. "Mom actually kicked your butt!"

Administrator P's grip tightened around his weapon. "Enjoy your amusement while you still can,"

And just like that, the weapon in his hands began shifting again. The shaft elongated, its dark metal bending and reshaping itself until it formed a bow. Black energy gathered between his fingers, condensing into an arrow while thin threads of lightning crawled along its surface.

Chloe's smile slowly disappeared. "Oh, you're mad-mad."

The arrow's tip settled directly on her, and Chloe had a really, really bad feeling about where this was going. Considering that the last one had literally pinned her leg to the floor, she wasn't exactly excited about getting shot with another one. Okay, Chloe, maybe insulting a really strong enemy wasn't the best idea you ever had.

But before Administrator P could release the arrow, something strange happened. Chloe felt it first: a sudden, prickling sensation that crawled across her skin, followed by an overwhelming surge of magical energy. The shadows behind Administrator P began to writhe and twist, growing darker and denser.

Chloe's eyes widened. Then the darkness exploded.

A massive torrent of shadow energy surged forward, and something enormous burst out of it with a ferocious snarl. Chloe barely had time to register a pair of glowing eyes before a gigantic wolf made entirely of shadows launched itself straight at Administrator P.

The wolf slammed into the bird monster from behind with enough force to send both creatures hurtling across the tunnel. Black feathers erupted into the air as the two collided with the wall, the thunderous crash sending cracks racing through the floor. The wolf's enormous jaws snapped shut around Administrator P's upper body, while tendrils of darkness coiled around his limbs and dragged him deeper into the struggle.

Chloe stared, and she probably should've been more concerned about the fact that another mysterious creature had just appeared out of nowhere in the middle of a fight that was already going horribly wrong, but, honestly? Compared to the giant murder bird, this new development was looking pretty cool.

Then, slowly, the realization hit her. That big wolf was actually Sierra.

Okay, that actually made so much sense, because who else could it be? It's been a long time since Chloe saw Sierra in her big wolf form, but she didn't forget. Anyway, for now though, Chloe still had a teeny-tiny problem. Namely, she was still stuck with a creepy shadow arrow pinning her leg down.

She looked at the cloak draped around her shoulders. "Cloaky?"

The fabric shifted ever so slightly. Chloe smiled hopefully. "Hey, buddy! Could you give me a hand here? Or, like, all of your hands? Because I'm pretty sure this is a situation where more hands would be super helpful."

Chloe stared at her cloak, and she felt like her cloak stared back at her, metaphorically speaking. "Cloaky? Are you seriously doing this right now?"

The cloak rustled, and finally, finally, something emerged from within its folds. A shadowy hand stretched out from beneath the fabric, followed by another, then another, until a small collection of dark, wispy hands had gathered around Chloe's trapped leg. Their fingers curled around the arrow's shaft, tugging and twisting at it from several different angles. The shadow hands pulled harder, until the projectile finally came loose with a sudden, unpleasant pop.

Chloe yanked her leg free and scooted backward, rubbing the spot where she'd been pinned. She quickly checked her leg, and while it had a hole on it, that hole quickly regenerated in a few seconds. Whew, good to know that Administrator P's shadow arrow didn't have any other funny effects.

Suddenly, she heard another loud crash echoed through the tunnel. Chloe whipped her head around just in time to see Sierra slam into Administrator P again, her massive shadowy jaws snapping at his neck.

"Oh, right!

Chloe scrambled to her feet and pointed dramatically at the ongoing fight. "Cloaky, buddy, I need you to help me fight that thing! Like, right now!"

The cloak fluttered, but every single shadow hand promptly disappeared back inside it. Chloe slowly lowered her pointing finger. "Excuse me?"

For a few seconds, absolutely nothing happened. Then a single shadowy hand cautiously emerged from the cloak, holding something out one of the laser swords Cloaky had taken from Commander Sirius.

"Oh! Chloe exclaimed, accepting it. She turned the weapon over in her hands, admiring its sleek design. "That's actually really cool. Thank you, buddy!"

The shadow hand gave a little wiggle, and then it vanished back into the cloak. "Wait. No. NO, NO, NO. That's not what I meant!"

The cloak remained perfectly still. "I said help me fight him! Not give me a sword and then clock out for the day!"

The cloak gave a faint rustle, which Chloe was fairly certain was the magical equivalent of telling her to deal with it herself. She rolled her eyes so hard she was surprised they didn't get stuck. "Fine! Whatever! I'll do it myself!"

Honestly, she should've known better. Cloaky was a very useful companion when it actually felt like being useful, but getting it to cooperate on demand was apparently a completely different story. Still, Chloe glanced down at the laser sword in her hand and couldn't help admiring it.

The weapon just looked so cool. She could already imagine how cool she'd look swinging it around, preferably while doing something heroic and not getting thrown into a wall for the fifth time that afternoon.

But, she did remember how Administrator P was really only affected by shadow attacks so far. Then again, this was a laser sword, and how was she supposed to ignore something that cool?

Okay, so maybe this isn't going to work. She thought, giving the weapon an experimental twirl. But, like, I won't know until I try, right?

Chloe tightened her grip on the hilt and turned toward the fight just in time to see Administrator P finally get fed up with Sierra. The bird monster staggered backward, his feathers ruffled and several patches of it torn apart by the wolf's relentless attacks. Sierra lunged again, her massive jaws snapping toward his shoulder, but Administrator P suddenly thrust out one arm.

Black lightning erupted from his palm and struck Sierra directly.

Chloe's eyes widened as the enormous wolf was lifted clean off her paws and hurled backward. Sierra crashed into the opposite wall. Sierra didn't move for a bit, but eventually, the shadow wolf's limbs twitched, and she began struggling to get back up.

Meanwhile, Administrator P slowly turned his monstrous head toward the fallen wolf, black lightning crawling over his feathers. The Administrator seemed thoroughly annoyed now, and Chloe had a really bad feeling about what he planned to do next. Well, Chloe was not going to stand around watching Sierra get blasted into next week while she waved a sword around and hoped for the best.

"Hey, over here you oversized feather duster!" Chloe shouted, raising the laser sword.

She focused her magic, and a pair of dark wings manifested from her back. Then, she activated the sword.

A brilliant blade of concentrated light sprang to life with a sharp, hum, bathing Chloe's face in its glow. "Ohhh, yeah. That's the stuff."

Okay, she might have been in serious danger, and her opponent was a gigantic monster who could probably turn her into zombie confetti if he really wanted to. But she had wings and a laser sword. Really, what more could a girl ask for?

Chloe crouched and launched herself into the air, her wings beating hard enough to send dust swirling across the tunnel floor. She shot forward, accelerating toward Administrator P with her sword held firmly in both hands.

Administrator P's head snapped toward her. Black lightning immediately gathered around his outstretched wing, crackling and branching into jagged tendrils of darkness. Before Chloe could close the distance, a torrent of black electricity erupted from the monster and came screaming toward her.

Chloe brought her laser sword up, and the laser sword met the incoming lightning. The entire tunnel flashed with blinding light as white energy and black electricity collided along the sword's edge. The impact jolted Chloe's arms, nearly wrenching the weapon from her grasp, while the force behind the blast sent her hurtling backward through the air.

Her wings beat frantically as she struggled to maintain her balance, but the torrent kept coming, pouring against the laser blade with relentless force. Every second felt like trying to hold back an actual thunderstorm with a glorified glow stick.

But despite how scary things were, the sword held. Chloe gritted her teeth and tightened both hands around the hilt, pushing back against the black lightning as it drove her farther and farther from Administrator P. Her arms trembled from the strain, and the pressure against the blade was so intense that she could barely keep her wings spread.

In the next few seconds, the monster's attack finally began to lose some of its forward momentum, but not before it forced Chloe all the way down toward the ground. She angled her wings, trying to slow her descent, and managed to touch down on the floor with a heavy skid.

Okay, so maybe charging a terrifying, oversized bird monster with a stolen laser sword hadn't been her most thoroughly researched life decision. But in her defense, it had looked really cool in the movies.

Then, suddenly, the black lightning flickered, its stream breaking apart into scattered tendrils before vanishing altogether. Chloe was startled by the abrupt change, but she soon spotted the reason almost immediately.

Sierra had latched onto Administrator P with extreme prejudice. Her jaws were clamped around one of his twisted limbs, while her claws raked viciously across his feathered body. Every time the Administrator tried to wrench himself free, Sierra dug in harder, snapping at him whenever he turned to strike her. The creature's wings thrashed wildly, and his long, beaked head whipped from side to side as he struggled to fend off his attacker.

So, Administrator P was now distracted, and Chloe had a sword. An extremely throwable-looking sword.

With a sharp twist of her wrist, Chloe drew her arm back and hurled the laser sword straight at Administrator P. “Surprise!”

The glowing blade spun through the air, its brilliant edge tracing a bright streak across the gloom as it hurtled toward the monster. Chloe imagined it burying itself in his chest, followed by an incredibly satisfying explosion and perhaps some dramatic background music.

However, Administrator P was apparently not interested in cooperating with her cinematic vision. Even with Sierra tearing into him, the monster snapped one enormous wing outward. Black energy erupted from its feathers, striking the spinning sword with a deafening crack. The weapon was knocked violently off course, tumbling through the air before clattering against the far wall and skidding across the floor.

Seriously? She couldn't even have one cool movie moment?

Well, that's fine, it wasn't like she'd put all her hopes into the laser sword or anything. That would have been embarrassing, and Chloe was many things, but she wasn't dumb.

Administrator P finally managed to wrench himself partly free of Sierra's grasp. He flung one wing backward, releasing a burst of black energy that struck the wolf in the side. Sierra was hurled away, her body dissolving into a swirling mass of shadows before reforming several feet away. She skidded across the floor, claws carving dark grooves into the concrete, but immediately lowered her head and prepared to charge again.

While Administrator P was still focused on Sierra, Chloe planted her feet, drew her arm back, and threw the shadow spear. The spear flew very fast, and Administrator P twisted at the last second, but it was too late.

The creature staggered as his entire body quickly collapsed into a churning mass of black vapor. His beaked head dissolved first, followed by his wings and elongated limbs, until Administrator P finally reverted back to his original, non-bird monster body.
