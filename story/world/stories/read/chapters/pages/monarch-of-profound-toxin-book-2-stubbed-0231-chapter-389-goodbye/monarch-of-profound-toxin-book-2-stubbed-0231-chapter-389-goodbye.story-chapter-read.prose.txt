“Wow, that’s… such an over-the-top name,” Eik muttered under his breath, as his eyes scanned the messages. “But you won’t hear me complaining.” Without hesitation, he chose Divine Toxic Omnipotence—the only option available.

And as if the world had been waiting for him to finish up, a sudden surge of aura hammered into him, dust and debris on the ground below lifted into the air by the sheer pressure of the energy. From the depths and from the pillars, a hollow, grinding rumble gradually grew louder and more piercing, vibrating the organs in Eik’s weary body.

Divine Toxic Omnipotence was an ability that gave him a… strange sensation.

Most new abilities somewhat revealed their effects by rousing something within his body or spirit. But that was not the case with Divine Toxic Omnipotence. As a matter of fact, it felt like it awakened everything but his body.

Suddenly, he was keenly aware of everything. Not just that which he could smell, hear, or see. And not just that which was nearby, but everything. The entire world of Toxin.

Had this ability been granted to him any earlier, his head would likely have exploded from the flood of information. Every leaf on every tree, every pebble of every mountain, and every cloud across the world was known to him.

Even the deepest depths of the world were as clear as a black line on white paper. And the ocean of toxin above the clouds, surrounding the world… It was even vaster than he had thought. For all intents and purposes, it was endless. Trying to comprehend its size, he felt like he was going to throw up.

In fact, so absorbed was he in the sensation of the omniperception, that he almost missed it when the Lord returned to the surface. The ground erupted as if by a volcanic eruption, and Eik felt every grain of soil as it was launched skyward.

Even if he closed his eyes, the Lord of the Moon was frighteningly noticeable. To Eik’s new eyes, he was a lifeless void plastered onto a world full of vibrant life and indescribable beauty. Like a blemish that marred the very world.

The fiend was enormous. He stood at least forty meters tall. Was that his original form, perhaps? What he had been all those eons ago, when he had been at the peak of his power. Even in proportion to his now much larger size, the crescent on his skull had increased significantly in size.

He now had a total of eight arms.

There was no doubt in Eik’s mind that, even with how terrifyingly powerful he was, the Lord of the Moon in front of him right now was nothing but a fragment of what the Worldbreaker must have been in his heyday.

That fact made it all the more urgent that the monster was dealt with before he could regain anymore of his former strength.

Enormous, semi-transparent wings like those of an insect had sprouted along his shoulder in two pairs. Occasionally, they would flutter, the unconscious movement carving up the ground hundreds of meters around the lord and sending up dust storms.

Through his new connection to Toxin, Eik found that he could will the world to force the buffeted dust back down with sheer atmospheric pressure.

Wait. Could he control… everything?

At a thought, the blue-green clouds above suddenly began to churn, whirling themselves into what looked like the beginning of a massive tornado, seemingly for no reason.

And that was right. There was no reason. Except for his wish for it to happen, that was.

A mountain in the distance suddenly cracked before detonating and sending rocks and ash hurling into the stratosphere. All Eik had done was order an increase in the subsurface pressure.

It was near effortless.

Suddenly the name Diving Toxic Omnipotence didn’t seem quite as ridiculous anymore. Had a single evolution actually just made him into a damned god?

Now that he was noticing it, his omnipresence wasn’t just that. He could sense all things, yes, but for each thing he sensed, there was something else as well, deeper within the aspect of the world. A potential to influence.

That turned out not to be limitless, however. When he tried to turn the air around him into a horse, or make a lake say the words “It’s awfully humid down here, you know,” he found it to be impossible. But as far as he could tell, nearly anything that could happen, he could trigger, so to speak.

Before he could react, the Lord was in front of him. A strike to his already wounded abdomen sent him rocketing into the ground at a diagonal angle, his body carving a several kilometer long groove as he tore through everything.

Coming to a dusty stop, Eik laid there on his back and stared up into the clouds for a moment. Maybe getting an ability that overwhelmed the senses so completely that it basically hauled him out of his own body wasn’t ideally timed in the middle of a fight to the death.

“Shit,” he muttered and climbed to his feet with a grunt. Even the blood thirst he shared with Profound Toxin had given significant way to the weariness that absolutely dominated his body.

When he looked up, the fiend was already there again, approaching with extreme speed and force that far exceeded what he had been capable of before this final transformation.

This time, he saw it coming, and Eik raised a foot enveloped in super reinforced crystal to catch the darkness-infused blow perfectly.

With a sickening crunch, his leg broke horrifically at the shin and knee. He barely had time to gasp before a flat rod of black hammered into his skull, robbing him of consciousness.

How in the world was the Lord of the Moon this powerful? Eik had thought that Omnicide, his ample consumption of grade 7 Legendary Mystery Medicine of a few different varieties, along with the constant flow of regular grade 7 potion improved by Supreme Vessel, would have at least tipped the scales a little bit. And in a way it had. He had become able to push the bastard to the point of pulling out his ace. But what the hell was that worth if he was still powerless in the end?

It was a strike up and under his ribs that woke him back up as he jetted upward, nearly hitting the clouds. From this height, he could see what he had already sensed.

Quadrillions upon quadrillions of parasites poured from the entire lengths of the massive pillars that had sprouted from the ground and reached the sky. Apocalypse Canvas and the Profound Toxic beasts had split their attention between the Lord and the pillars but both were too tough to destroy outright.

In mere seconds, parasites had covered more than ten percent of the land in the vicinity while miasma darkened the sky rapidly.

And Eik’s new senses informed him of every single mote of Toxin lost to the voracious appetite of the parasites, and it would only be a matter of time before all of that energy would be delivered to the Lord of the Moon.

“You are on the brink,” the Lord said when he appeared in front of Eik once more. He was still hurt, but his transformation must have boosted him enough to ignore that for now.

“Of what, death? No shit, asshole…”

“A premature metamorphosis like this,” the fiend said, gesturing to his new body. “will cost me precious months of recovery. You forced my hand, and I will make sure you suffer appropriately for it.” Eik’s stomach turned at the malicious grin.

A spontaneous attempt to imprison the Lord in another reinforced Extinction Canvas failed miserably when the beast shattered the barriers almost instantly.

Eik felt hopelessness permeated his mind as he felt the parasites consume everything. Even this world that he had inherited he could not protect.

Had the meteoric rise to power since his initial Awakening been nothing but a warm-up for this most decisive defeat? Had it all been simply to generate hope ripe for the shattering? Would his fate be that cruel?

When Spaghetti Jones returned, the Lord dealt with him swiftly as well, even while batting away all of Eik’s other attempts to cause him harm. It was useless.

Swordplay did nothing. Apocalypse Canvas did nothing. Profound Toxic beasts did nothing. Profound Toxin in liquid and gas form did nothing. Nothing did anything…

Was what a fucking loser would say!

Struck by a wave of fury, fear, and dumb bravery, will to fight surged through Eik’s veins once more, as if out of the blue. Heh. Out of the blue was right. The azure bastard always did have a way of manipulating his emotions. Sneaky little devil.

Taking the Lord completely by surprise after his moment of despair, Eik managed to drive a spike deep into the giant’s abdomen and Accelerate it immediately.

“Vermin,” the fiend growled, seizing Eik by the leg and hurling him into the ground like a projectile shot from a tank.

He threw a couple of dozen rods of darkness on top, but the earth seemed to grow around the crater, massive boulders rolling in to cover the human from harm. Each rod caused extreme damage, but the debris kept coming.

Waiting, the Lord watched Eik climb back out. He looked different. Resolute. Despair replaced by confidence. What a pathetically erroneous shift.

“You know, I’ve just realized,” Eik began with a bright smile full of mischief. The clouds above began to quietly part. “My imagination has been way too narrow.”

The Lord looked up, curious but not alarmed. Now, what was this farce?

Eik’s features were gradually bathed in brighter and brighter hues of azure, the white in his teeth reflecting dazzlingly as he grinned. “As long as I have my imagination, anything is possible!”

“You damnable—” the Lord hissed and started to move, but it was too late.

The heavens were falling.

And Eik welcomed it with open arms.

Divine Toxic Omnipotence was right. Not just above them, but all over the world, the unfathomably vast ocean of pure, concentrated Profound Toxin crashed down upon Toxin’s surface.

The weight of it alone flattened mountains and erased landscape as if it had never been there. Even if it hadn’t been the deadliest substance in the Unified Mass, there would have been nothing left.

But the Lord of the Moon still lived, even if there was nothing he could do. In a very literal sense, there was nowhere safe on Toxin now. The Lord could have swum straight up for hours and still not have reached the outer surface of the ocean.

Disappearing into the endless ocean with Toxic Liquefaction, Eik was unreachable, even with the Lord’s mighty power. But he couldn’t take any chances. It was simply too dangerous. Not even the smallest sliver of a chance that the beast made it back to Gimleh and Eik’s family could be allowed.

There were so many precious people at home, relying on him. Their safety depended on him. And he would never allow himself to shirk that responsibility.

Yes. They would all be just fine, so long as they had each other.

What was a little sacrifice when he knew that?

Eik sent his voice blasting through the vastness, reaching the entire world. No, it almost felt like his voice might even reach beyond this world.

Here, on Toxin, there were none but the Lord to listen, but Eik’s words were gentle and loving, as if they were meant for someone else, far away. In the same moment that he spoke, without hesitation, Eik triggered Accelerant.

“Goodbye.”

Unrestrained and with endless fuel, it ignited everything in a world-ending blast.
