Elion fell to the ground. His butter knife clattered onto the cobblestones beside him. His armor was gone, replaced by his clothing. He tried to pull up the Praxian menu but received no response.

He groaned, tasting blood in his mouth. The air stank of smoke and sweat. His head pounded.

Venya lay on the ground across the courtyard, unconscious.

The courtyard warped.

A black hole formed over the Altar, twisting and bending light around it. The air sizzled and hissed as a shining gemstone was lifted from the rubble of the Altar.

The stone hovered in the air for a moment, halfway between the ground and the black hole. It was large, far larger than any of the Ascendency Stones that Elion had seen, though it seemed akin to them.

It slowly ascended into the distorted vortex. The image of the gem twisted, stretching out around the hole. Elion got the impression that he could see the back of the gem, unfolded before him like origami.

The gemstone flickered and flashed, sending out flares which twisted around and were absorbed by the black hole.

Then the gem vanished.

Prator crawled away from the black hole, his armor gone, his bonds vanished.

Venya crawled back, pressing herself against one of the pillars holding up the balcony.

Keyla’s laser rifle fell to pieces, clattering onto the ground.

Warlocks and Aterfelian Guard fled the courtyard. Elion saw Kile clutching his false leg in one hand, scrambling to escape the scene.

Another Aterfelian collapsed to the ground, machine parts tumbling from his head. Laser rifle pieces lay strewn across the balcony.

One warlock scrambled to the the furthest corner of the courtyard. His wand suddenly bust to life, purple runes glowing again. He cast a bolt of energy at the black hole.

The energy bolt fizzled, dissipating half way across the courtyard. A deafening hum shook the ground. A black gash darted through the air, like a tear in reality.

This struck the warlock in the chest. The man crumpled, folding in on himself until he disappeared.

Elion shook.

He knew what this thing was. He had seen something imitate that distortion field before.

“Tephalian,” he breathed, edging slowly away from the anomaly.

The courtyard fell silent. All eyes watched the mesmerizing ripple of shadow and light hovering in the center of the courtyard. Elion bumped against Keyla’s leg. She held parts of her laser rifle in her hands, and the rest were strewn around her on the ground. She didn’t seem to have noticed, her gaze locked onto the gaping hole in the courtyard.

In the distance Elion heard a helicopter.

Do they even have helicopters in Kylios?

“What does it want?” Elion whispered,

“I don’t know,” Keyla mouthed, words barely audible.

The thrumming of helicopter blades grew louder, drawing attention. Eyes turned to the sky.

A warlock trapped on the damaged balcony coughed.

The black hole again sent a spear of torn reality through the air, striking the woman and twisting her in on herself. Her arms and legs stretched out to impossible lengths as she collapsed inward.

The chopper crested the roof of the building, flying low and fast. Hanging from twin rotors, the squat, ugly body of the craft pulsed with turquoise light. A hatch on its side stood open, a golden figure gleaming within.

The black hole launched another attack toward the helicopter.

The man inside sprang out of the aircraft and raised a massive shield, taking direct blow of the black streak of shredded reality.

Elion waited for the man to collapse in on himself.

He did not.

The air around him warped and hissed, but he remained intact.

He plummeted through the sky, and a set of six massive wings unfolded behind him, directing his fall.

Like a golden missile he streaked earthward, clad in a full suit of golden armor. He plunged head first, his shield held out before him, his sword pointing downward.

More spears of darkness flew from the black enemy. The barrage increased in intensity as the armored man descended.

The man slowed, pushing down against the lances from the back hole. A golden shimmer glowed in the air around him, a transparent membrane of magic which wrapped forward, enclosing the attacks of the enemy in light.

As portions of the courtyard fell into the man’s shadow, Praxis returned.

Elion’s armor snapped back into existence around him. He climbed to his feet, picking up his knife.

Warlock wands shone with light. The pieces of Keyla’s laser rifle snapped back into place, rubber-banding from the floor to her hands.

A net of golden, shimmering light enveloped the black hole; a billowing, undulating mass, extending from the tip of the Knight’s sword. The net constricted, consolidating into a tight orb.

The Knight stepped atop it. He discarded his shield. It puffed away into mist as it fell. He raised his sword over his head with both hands, and brought it down, point first, stabbing into the golden net of light.

The energy contained in the net disperssed. The net deflated, shrinking rapidly down to the size of a glowing baseball. The knight bent and picked up the sphere, tucking it into a pouch at his side.

The man dismissed his helmet and scanned the courtyard.

“Zev!” Elion exclaimed, dashing across the ground to greet his uncle.

Sweat dripped from Zev’s face. His hair was plastered to his head. His arms and legs trembled. He wiped his forehead.

“Elion?” Zev said, surprised. “What are you doing here?”

Purple light glinted off of Zev’s face. Panicked warlocks summoned portals, escaping the wreckage of the mansion.

Prator moaned, dismissing his armor. Zev looked around him again, his eyes unfocused. He seemed confused, disoriented.

Venya staggered to his feet. Her glossy hair had caught fire, part of it singed and melted.

Zev’s eyes locked onto Venya, and focus returned to the man’s face.

“You,” he hissed.

Fear drained the color from Venya’s face.

“It’s true,” she gasped. “You have returned.”

“I have returned,” Zev agreed. “Tell my brother I’m coming for him.”

Venya dropped her broken wand on the ground. She gestured with her hands, working magic without the aid of the stick.

Purple ribbons swirled behind Venya, a fractal pattern growing increasingly complex. Her knees trembled, wobbling atop her high heels. The shoes now seemed impractical and weak, rather than commanding and elegant.

She dove backward into the portal, unleashing a powerful blast as she did.

Zev’s movement was a blur as he lifted his shield, deflecting the blow. He skidded backward across the cobblestones, stopped only when he impacted the far wall.

“Zev!” Prator cried. “Zev! You have returned!”

“Prator?” Zev asked, seeing the man for the first time. “I heard that you abandoned your oaths.”

"Abandon my oaths?" Prator said, aghast. "Never. I could never." He picked himself up, dusting rubble from his shoulders.

"Yet here you are, working alongside Venya," Zev said.

Prator fell to his knees before Zev, pleading. "You have to understand," Prator said, "I've done what I had to, to survive. I was ready to bring the boy to you. We were going to come to Erod to fight alongside you."

Zev looked to Elion for confirmation.

Elion hesitated. He did suggest that, he thought, but he decided to turn me over to Venya.

"Is this true, Elion?" Zev asked. "Was he bringing you to Erod?"

Before Elion could answer, a blast of laser light flew past his shoulder and struck Prator directly in his left ear.

The man was thrown to the ground by the strike, his ear blackened, white hair burning. His  Aurelian enhancements, seemed to protect him from the worst of the strike. He scrambled away as Zev whirled, activating his shield.

Prator began summoning his armor. Two more lances of turquoise laser light blasted into Prator as his golden light started to form around him. He fell to the ground on his back, holes blasted in his chest.

Selna emerged from the darkness of the hall nearby, Keyla's laser rifle in her hands.

"He is a liar," Selna said, "a deceiver and a traitor to his oaths."

"Who are you to judge?" Zev demanded.

"My name is Selna Aerden. I trusted him, but he betrayed us."

Zev looked at Prator's body, the golden mist of his partially formed armor dissolving as the man gasped for breath, his eyes rolling. Blood and tissue spattered his face. The craters in his chest smoked.

“If he abandoned his oaths, he should be tried before an Aurelian court and stripped of his power,” Zev said, raising his sword and turning toward Selna.

"Kill me if you must," Selna said. "I only wish that I had killed him sooner—the first time that I had the chance to."

"Stop!" Elion cried out.

"Tell me what happened! What did he do?" Zev asked. "Why do you harbor such malice against one who walks the Path of Dawn?"

"Don’t hurt her Zev," Elion said.

"Be quiet, Elion," Zev said. "I want to hear it from her mouth. What happened?"

“I loved him,” Selna said. “I would have fought for him. But he didn’t trust anyone. His paranoia forced him to enslave us.”

Selna’s hands trembled. “I would have married him of my own free will, at first. But he forced me. I bear his child.”

Keyla gasped.

Zev frowned.

“He must be tried before a Dawn Court,” Zev said, kneeling beside Prator.

A woman ran into the courtyard. Elion did not recognize her.

“Zev!” she called. “We don’t have a lot of time; we parked out back.”

“One moment, Isalyn,” Zev said. He used the ability Elion recognized as Save a Friend.

“Just leave him to die,” Selna spat.

“If we leave him behind Gorman will get him,” Keyla said. “He might be able to patch him up and control him.”

Elion looked around.

"Where did Tuft go?" Keyla asked, echoing his thoughts.

Snickers prowled out of the dark shadow, and Elion stooped to pick up the cat. The cat dodged around his grasp and trotted over to where Prator lay on the ground.

Prator’s eyes were still alert, sweat on his face, glancing from person to person. “Forgive me," he gasped, stretching his arm toward Selna. “I should have trusted you.”

Snickers sniffed at Prator. Then the cat shifted, morphing, stretching, and transformed into the grizzled old figure of Tuft. The man knelt beside Prator and began working his healing magic.

"He may yet live," Tuft said. “I can save him.”

"Don't," Selna shouted, raising her rifle. "I'll kill you, too."

"Mother," Keyla grabbed Selna's arm, trying to snatch the rifle away from her.

"You don't understand," Selna growled.

"Give up the weapon, Selna,” Zev ordered. "We will bring Prator before a court, and if your story can be verified, he will be stripped of his power." Selna allowed Keyla to yank the laserarm from her grasp.

Zev turned to Tuft. "You can heal this man?" Zev asked Tuft.

"Perhaps," Tuft said. "I can heal his body, though the mind is a different matter, and if he truly did what she says that he did, there is an illness there."

Zev grimaced.

Shots rang out from outside the mansion.

“Come on, let’s go!” Isalyn cried.

Zev stooped and picked up Prator in his arms. He strode across the courtyard. “Lead the way.”

Isalyn lead them down a hall toward the back of the mansion.
