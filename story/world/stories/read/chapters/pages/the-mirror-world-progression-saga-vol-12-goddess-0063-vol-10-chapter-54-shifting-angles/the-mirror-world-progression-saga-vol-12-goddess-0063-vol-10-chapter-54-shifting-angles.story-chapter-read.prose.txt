Azure had been keeping the skies clear of annoyances delivered on the elites, just as Bast was doing the same on the ground. Fairly easy.

As the mental attacks first launched on her lover, Azure struggled not to rush to help her immediately. The struggle only continued when Samantha retreated to the backlines entirely, and the scouring began to feather her with pain. Still, Azure resisted.

I can’t. More is coming. I need to be tactical… if I want to save her life.

She’d been firmly told this by Ana, and then, independently, by Dax of all people, for the cold, hard tactical reasoning: doing a full heal was best saved for the most severe damage.

And then, even from the lips of Samantha herself, agreeing to be helped despite the disaster at Merrington, obviously struggling with the admittance. It had been the final eye-opening moment, her lover telling her ‘only as a last resort’ and to ‘act with wisdom, not emotion.’

How much more did she need to have something hit home for her? She steeled herself to do what was necessary.

And then, she did the unthinkable: she connected with Dax mentally, individually, and said, “I won’t act until you tell me. Direct me. I can’t make the choice right, myself. I know I can’t. I’ll rush to her too soon.”

Dax seemed perturbed to say the least. “Azure, I don’t exactly relish being reviled and despised for this sort of choice, and that’s if you don’t turn me into-”

“I won’t harm you regardless. Please. You have to do this. I can’t. I’ll fuck it up. I’m begging you, Dax.”

A mental sigh. “I’m going to regret this, but fine. When I say ‘when,’ you go, damn it. And not before. Just don’t blame me for what comes out of this.”

“I won’t. It’ll be my fault.”

“Mmph. You need a distraction — aside from that, we need help. Abandon the sky and go help fight the wraiths.”

“With pleasure, General.”

Azure entered the fray with Deathsting, swooping in with dazzling arrays of spell dagger fire to break the advances of the armor wraiths, and Deathsting tackled and grappled one before it could react, rending the armor and preventing counterattacking from the sword.

Meanwhile, Samantha took a painful wound that made Azure wince internally. Not enough, yet, certainly. Maybe that was the worst she’d get, and she wouldn’t even need Azure?

She knew it wasn’t true, yet she hoped for it all the same.

The battle in front of her hadn’t gone well. They’d ripped up pieces of armor, and it seemed to inhibit the wraiths, but not stop them. Entangling worked temporarily, which was what Redberry was doing constantly to mitigate and control the battle to some degree. Between that and dealing with the champion’s whip, she was extremely harried.

Worse, the few liches that remained could repair the armor. Only a handful of the Counter Crew seemed able to block these, and they were increasingly burdened with other things, so it slipped through here and there. Someone was keeping the armor of the champion from being repaired, though.

Samantha took another wound that agonized her. She fell to her knees, stunned.

“I think the goddess is in trouble,” Ash reported anxiously.

“Yeah, this isn’t looking good,” Constance replied just as anxiously.

Azure couldn’t help but feel the worry seeping into her bones. She touched minds with Dax and asked, “Now?”

The answer from Dax was quick. “No.”

The distraction left Azure open to a sequence of attacks from wraiths, and one nicked her with an entropic blade… she flinched in time enough to have it slide instead of cut or crack her crystalline form. She was narrowly unharmed. Unlike most, just a graze wasn’t guaranteed to wound her.

She retreated…

Another wound hit Samantha’s mind, and Azure almost had a seizure from the echo of the pain. It didn’t hurt her, not literally, but the psychological effect was almost worse.

“Get the hell out of there, Azure,” Dax commanded in the main gestalt, in disgust. “You’re too distracted; that’s worse than useless.”

“I need to go to her!” she cried out loud, even as she made it echo into the gestalt. It was panicked and desperate even to her own ears. “Now!”

“Not yet, damn it! Azure, listen to me: we want her to survive.”

The impulse was like a taut rubber band stretching between her and her lover. Everything in her wanted to let it catapult her into Samantha’s arms. Shouldn’t she? She didn’t even need to help her tangibly, what if-

She grimaced, clenching her teeth. Resisted the pull. The right timing, one use, a long cooldown, to keep her alive — to keep her alive!

Just barely, she saw the undead champion angling toward her. As she retreated, something insane occurred with a sudden flash: an energy bloomed from all the armor wraith and from the champion, as they moved much faster, turning into blurs of motion.

Again, by the narrowest of margins, she dodged the blades of wraiths that came first, and she even detonated daggers right into the chest plate of one, blowing most of it apart. They were coming at her all-out, not bothering with defense.

She didn’t see the flash of the whip at all. Instead, she saw a body practically teleport itself in the way, and get the whipcrack of that insane lash right in the body.

Stunned, she watched it begin to disintegrate even as it hit the ground. She saw who it was just before it was completely gone: Odysseus.

No! Why?! Why would you fucking do that for me?! You stupid fucking idiot!

Letting out a furious war cry, Azure spun around and flung a slew of daggers — but hidden among these, by supernatural sleight of hand, was also a potion of exceptionally potent acid. She hoped desperately that it was dangerous to the armor.

But even with the undead champion entirely out of position in his mad dash to come for her, the prodigious speed of the wraiths under that buff was just too much. The champion had multiple suits move in the way and take the explosion of acid, as the champion slipped away, intent on pivoting for others who were rushing to defend Azure, Redberry, and Zadkiel, among them.

That fucker wants to make use of his buff…

The avoidance seemed well-founded, as the acid that splashed on the armor wraiths dissolved every bit of what anchored them to the world. The black voids housed within them simply dissipated, as if dispersing with the wind.

Three of them had been dispatched, and a dozen damaged by the acid.

Despite this encouraging development, all the while, Samantha had been getting her mind ripped apart. Azure sensed something like a small, tight marble of sanity amidst a storm of debris.

How could we allow this? She’s taken this on; made herself the nexus of all of our misery, and she’s just eating it. All for us.

She screamed. For her frustration with herself, for frustration with the battle and the wraiths, for anger at the horrible sacrifices they had to make again and again, and — most of all — for Samantha, she screamed loud and deep enough to shake reality. Deep enough that The Lover within screamed with her, pulsing with animus in every direction, in every atom of being.

But she didn’t lose her mind, as she might’ve, once before — she couldn’t afford such a luxury while her duty remained so crystal clear and pure.

Azure was already collapsing herself into the vicinity of Samantha and her circle when the pointless call from Dax came: “Now, Azure!”
