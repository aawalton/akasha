Well, fuck! I’m dead!

Yes, actually. I’m not exactly sure what brought me to this situation. Because as shitty as my life was, I didn’t think I would die so soon. No job, no family, hell, I lived on the streets!

But I didn’t think that one day I would go to sleep on the dirty rag I called a bed and wake up here.

I look around but find nothing. It seems I am now on an endless grass plain that stretches into the horizon. It reminds me of the grain fields back at home, before they were seized.

“Hello?! Is anyone there?!” I call, hoping for anyone to answer. I begin to walk forward and drudge along the grass, which is almost as tall as me. And at six feet tall, I am not short, I hope.

Nope. I am alone.

Sighing, I sit on the grass. Could this be heaven?

I certainly deserve heaven. Once I dreamed of becoming a doctor, because I thought doctors helped people. But no. My family died helpless, because the doctors would not save them. Because we were poor, and we could not pay. And after our house burned down, who helped me? Nobody.

Dreaming is a privilege, I realized! And from then on, I understood that the world I lived in was rotten to the core, as was everyone in it.

I get up. The sun seems to be setting in the sky, and the chirping of bugs is beginning to pick up like a chorus in the background.

At least there are other living things here.

For a while, I stare at the night sky, finding that there are no stars. Then, I finally decide to get a move on.

I might be a bum, but who knows? Things can always change. This place is already more impossible than everything I’ve seen in my entire life. So I start walking, striding through the grass, hoping to find anything.

***

I have found something!

In the middle of the endless grass plains, there is a statue. It is human-sized, and it also sort of looks like a human. I can’t make out the details on its face, but it is currently in a kneeling position.

Brushing away the dust and moss coating, I try to take a closer look. What is this thing, exactly?

I tap it once. Two times, and even thrice. But it doesn’t budge.

Scratching my chin, I frown. “Excuse me?” I ask, as politely as possible. “Who are you?”

Of course, nothing happens—

Vrrmm….

A low humming sound emanates from the statue as it begins to glow. Gasping, I step back quickly, but it does not stop. Clicking and whirring noises fill my mind as a halo of golden light traces itself over the statue.

Click.

The noises stop, as the statue crumbles. The light floods into me, filling me with…

What is this? What is this feeling?
