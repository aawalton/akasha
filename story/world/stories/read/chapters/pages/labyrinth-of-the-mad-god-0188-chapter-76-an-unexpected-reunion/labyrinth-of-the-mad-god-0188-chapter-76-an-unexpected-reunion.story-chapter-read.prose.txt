When Nick stepped back into his body, he sat on the floor with his legs crossed and set Blightbane across his knees. After a moment of consideration, he decided to start with his Journeyman trials, since he was confident that he could clear them.

They wound up being even easier than he’d anticipated.

His Acrobatics test had two parts. First, he had to navigate an obstacle course that would have been impossible for any pre-System human. He balanced on wires, leapt along moving platforms, and crossed a dozen types of rough terrain before the finish line came into view. While the core of the trial was gauging his agility and spatial awareness, he had to contend with time limits and psychological pressure too.

During the early portions of the test, he was chased by a tornado that grew larger with every breath he drew. The later sections featured falls that would deposit him into roiling lava, bubbling acid, or an ocean of shattered glass. But Nick was no stranger to executing precision maneuvers in hostile environments. He knocked the obstacles down one after the next without breaking a sweat.

The second half of the trial involved using Acrobatics in combat, although unlike Evasion, the test didn’t focus on avoiding his enemy’s blows. Instead, he had to reposition across a range of hazardous terrain before a horde of hungry critters caught him—vicious canines that were relentless but not particularly quick.

In the end, he finished so far ahead of his pursuers that their presence no longer felt threatening, earning a brand-new bonus for his efforts.

Augmented Balance

You will have a significantly easier time keeping your balance and maintaining your bearings while performing rapid movements.

Augmented Balance was straightforward and guaranteed to be useful in both combat and everyday life. It was a solid reward for a Journeyman trial, and he had no complaints.

The Vital Energy Manipulation test was a little trickier, forcing him to use the skill in ways he hadn’t considered before. Nick aced the mana-control portion. His extensive experience with free casting was sufficient to see the task through, which involved overcoming various challenges by projecting the appropriate frequency of energy. The trial only involved types he was familiar with, and he had to apply pure, force, gravity, and darkness mana to erase, shatter, collapse, and extinguish a selection of barriers whose construction gave him some ideas that he would mull over later.

The stamina portion was harder. He had to burn vital energy to accomplish daunting physical feats. Even still, something felt off. Nick could have completed the course without using stamina at all, although he did his best to pass the test properly instead of leaning on his expansive attributes.

He wasn’t expecting there to be a health portion, although he should have, considering the skill’s name. He hadn’t made much headway with health manipulation, though he’d learned how to sense that form of vital energy over the last few months. It was too late to turn back, leaving him with no choice other than adapt on the fly and hope his grasp of the underlying principles that bridged all forms of energy manipulation was sufficient to see him through.

The trial involved being exposed to lethal poison and using health to counter the toxin before he succumbed. His heart slowed as his body broke out in a cold sweat, followed by a pounding headache as the substance spread through his system. Although his breathing was labored and it wasn’t his idea of a good time, the poison didn’t seem anywhere close to as dangerous as the warning conveyed. He wasn’t sure it could kill him, even if he let the toxin run its natural course.

When Nick focused, he felt his tissue fighting back, which led to a breakthrough in health manipulation. Since time wasn’t an issue, he used the ordeal as a teaching experience. Through trial and error, he learned how to channel his health to counteract the vile substance. When the last traces were purged from his bloodstream, the test ended and he added another upgrade to his collection.

Energy Sensitivity

You will have an easier time sensing the vital energies inside your body and directing them with your will.

The upgrade was exactly what he’d been hoping for—something that would assist in perceiving and guiding all three forms of vital energy. Nick frowned when comparing the challenges offered by these tests to ones he’d undertaken before reaching the quarantine zone. His old life had been filled with games and puzzles, and those instincts were telling him something was wonky after obtaining Dreadeater and all the milestone bonuses that accompanied his early evolution.

Whether his situation was so unique that he’d created a loophole or there was a maximum difficulty for the Journeyman stage, the trials weren’t calibrated to his astonishing new capabilities, and he wondered if these tests would ever challenge him again.

That all changed when Nick told the System he was ready to attempt his Adept Sword test. The trial provided all the difficulty he’d been anticipating and then some, along with an unexpected reunion with a pair of old friends.

He blinked, and when his eyes opened, he was sitting on the surface on another world, with none of the sensations that accompanied passing through a portal or rift. He rose to his feet and turned in a circle while assuming his stance, on guard against attacks arriving from any direction. Blightbane had come with him this time around, and he was glad to be facing this challenge with a familiar weapon in his hand.

He didn’t sense any immediate threats, so he examined his surroundings while confirming that he couldn’t use magic or draw on any of the powers granted by the System, although his attributes were unaffected and he could still channel stamina freely.

Nick was standing on the crest of a sprawling hillock. It was low, flat, and overlooked a prairie filled with rust-red grass that stretched on for as far as his eyes could see. The sky was a lustrous turquoise, the sun was just a little redder than Earth’s new star, and the air was filled with the buzzing of insects and the scent of alien wildflowers in full bloom.

Since there wasn’t anything but untamed wilderness below, he turned his attention to the rest of the hilltop. He didn’t spot any people, but there were some buildings in the distance that seemed strangely familiar. When he approached them, a beaming smile lit up his face. The structures didn’t just seem familiar—he had seen the ranch-style house and adjoining workshop before, though the environment he’d found them in had been far stranger.

When the front door opened, Nick caught sight of someone he’d been hoping to meet again one day. A delighted chuckle escaped his lips, having never imagined their reunion would take place inside his trial of mastery.

He raised a hand in greeting as he approached the diminutive woman wearing a crimson robe with a floral print, the master who had initiated him into the School of the Ever-Surging Blade and set him on the path of the swordsman. He ran his gaze across her delicate blue scales, fine features, and the slender sword sheathed at her hip—filled with a fierce and rising excitement as he anticipated the contest to come.

“Rita.” He bowed to his mentor and waited for her to explain what was happening.

She didn’t say anything at first, returning his gesture with a nod before circling to examine him from all sides. When she was finished, she came to a stop in front of him and let out an appreciative whistle.

“Someone’s been busy. I can’t believe you’re the same whelp I found hanging from a cliffside, barely able to decide which end of the blade to hold.”

That wasn’t how Nick remembered their first encounter, but he was too happy to be here to quibble. “You seem to be expecting me. I assume it has something to do with being a member of your faction. Why didn’t you tell me that we would meet again?”

“Rules, and I wanted it to be a surprise. I’ve been looking forward to this moment. It’s too bad you won’t remember any of this.”

“Actually, I will.” He explained that he had a unique ability to retain the contents of his tests, a revelation that made Rita’s eyes widen and her smile grow.

“Well now, that changes things. We need to begin the test soon, but we can speak for a few minutes first.” They sat down on a bench overlooking the rusty prairie.

“This is a true-to-life representation of my home—not that surreal world of giants where time runs fast. I can tell you’ve gone through a great deal since we last spoke. Give me the condensed version before I determine whether you’re worthy of call yourself an Adept of the Ever-Surging Blade.”

Nick trusted Rita with his life and shared everything he had experienced since their last meeting, which had occurred during a skill-training reward he’d earned while climbing the Tower of Rizzen during the Fate of Earth quest.

“By the gods. I knew your planet had fallen into Taltos’s grasp, but I never dreamed that you would become embroiled in a war that threatens the universe itself. The gains you’ve obtained are incredible, but you need to climb the peaks of power faster if you are to have any chance of surviving the calamity threatening your world, let alone being caught in the crossfire between the System and the Labyrinth of the Mad God.

“I wish I could provide material aid, but I have no way to reach you until the System secures a path through the dead zone separating the maze from integrated space. All I can offer is my fervent prayers and a final lesson, although I can tweak the reward if you manage to pass this exam.”

“I assume that because the System let you conduct this trial, it will just as hard as a normal test.”

“No, Nick.” Rita issued a bloodthirsty grin that he had become intimately familiar with during their time together. “It’s going to be one hell of a lot harder. You’ve grown so much, but mastery of the blade has nothing to do with astonishing attributes and unusual powers.”

They rose from the table and walked to a clearing behind her manor, facing each other and assuming their stances.

“Now then, show me what you’ve learned since we last crossed swords. You will pass the trial if you land a single strike and lose if you take lethal damage. I’ll match my output to your Sword skill and stats, but within those constraints, I’ll be coming at you with everything I have.”

A savage grin split Nick’s face as he flourished Blightbane. “I was hoping you’d say that. I wouldn’t have it any other way.”
