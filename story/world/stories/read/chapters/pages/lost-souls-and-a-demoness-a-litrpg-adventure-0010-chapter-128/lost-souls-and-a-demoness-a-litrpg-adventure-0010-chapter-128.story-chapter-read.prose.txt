"Jade…"

The soft, melodious voice reached Jade, so quiet she could barely hear it. She was adrift among a sea of twinkling stars, floating in the darkness between them. It was impossible to tell if the glimmering lights were near and small or large and distant, there was no reference in the empty void. Where was she? What was this place? Jade tried to recall how she had gotten here, but her mind was fuzzy. She felt strangely warm, as if bathed in an invisible light.

"Jade."

The voice called out to her again, stronger this time. Closer. This time she latched onto it, looking around, trying to figure out where it was coming from. The distant lights sparkled, and one of the stars below her grew brighter.

"Who are you? Where am I?" She called into the darkness. There was no panic, no fear. This place was strange, but not hostile. Nothing would hurt her here.

How do I know that?

And who was calling her?

"You must find me, Jade."The feminine voice echoed across the great, dark expanse, and the star below her pulsed in time with the words. There was something familiar about it, something Jade couldn't place.

"Where? Why?!"

"To protect your world. To protect the homes of everyone."

"Is it the demons?" She asked, a spike of alertness clearing away some of the lingering fuzz in her mind. "What do you know about them? How do I stop them?"

"Find me…"

The voice was more distant now, fading away.

"Where?!"

Jade woke with a start as a peal of thunder rumbled outside, shaking the shutters of her small room. She sat up, unusually energized for the early hour. Her racing heart began to slow, returning to a normal rhythm as she took in her surroundings, finding nothing out of the ordinary. So, it had been a dream? It hadn't felt like one, she could remember it so vividly.

"Who are you?" She whispered aloud to the empty bedroom.

There was no reply.

"So, like I was saying last night, I'm really only missing two ingredients for the restoration potion for your brother."

Siora bounced alongside Jade and the rest of their group as they made their way through the cramped, crowded streets of Illysport. Everyone but Tara was with them, as the tek'kalla needed to attend a meeting at her embassy before she could join them. The rain had finally let up, clouds parting to allow brilliant rays of morning sunshine to bathe the city in a pleasant warmth. Deciding to eschew Earth fashion in favor of the more local elven garb, they were all clothed in the flowing, loose-fitting garments they'd picked up in Lysara.

"Huh? Oh, right." Jade shook her head, clearing her thoughts. "You said Ashar had a line on one of them, right?"

When last they'd seen the merchant in Estelhelm, Ashar had mentioned he was planning on relocating to the more centrally located Illysport. Apparently, he'd already finished doing that.

"You alright? You've been distracted all morning." Naomi asked, munching on a purple fruit she'd picked up from a street cart.

"Yeah… Just had a weird dream."

Naomi eyed her suspiciously.

"It didn't involve me, did it?"

"What? No. Why?"

"You keep looking over at me. Am I on your mind?" Naomi asked with a grin.

"You're imagining things." Jade said, rolling her eyes.

"I'm pretty sure you're on Ashar's mind." Siora piped up helpfully. "He's asked about you a couple of times."

"Ohhhhhh? Please, do tell."

Naomi sidled over to the elf, pulling her close.

"Can we get back on topic?" Jade asked, feeling her face heat up. "You two can gossip later, I need to know what you've found for my brother."

"Right, right." Siora nodded, patting a crestfallen Naomi consolingly on the shoulder. "Ashar's shop is in the central market district, we should stop by later today. I visited last week when I was looking for ingredients. He didn't have anything on hand but once I told him what I was looking for, he said he had some ideas on where to find it."

"That's great! And the other ingredient you're missing?"

"Hmm… That's both simpler, and more complicated."

Jade and Luis exchanged a look.

"I don't think that translated right, chica. How can it be simple and complicated at the same time?"

Siora cocked her head, processing Luis's words. Jade had to steer away the easily distracted elf from a lamppost before Siora wandered straight into it. The elf didn't acknowledge the near miss, simply following Jade as she pulled her along.

"Well… it's simple in that I know where to find what we need. But I don't know how we're going to get it without making people upset."

"Ohh… that makes sense." Luis nodded in understanding.

"Are they important people?"

"Jade!"

"What?" She gave Naomi an innocent look.

"I don't think that's the first question to ask."

"Are you talking about the grove?" Fenrin asked Siora, ignoring the two of them.

"Yes." She nodded. Then, in response to the confused looks the rest of the group gave her, she elaborated. "In Lysara, there is a sacred grove with a holy tree of Lady Elaria, goddess of Growth. Growth is a powerful restorative essence, and an ingredient that bears a touch of her power would be the perfect way to bind the elixir together."

"I've been there before." Jade said, remembering the tranquil pool and slender tree that grew from it. "Master Rhystin had me… meditate… there once."

She stumbled a little over the word, not sure what she should share. Jade had never spoken to her friends about the vision she witnessed at the shrine to Elaria. There, she had relieved her final moments as a gymnast, the terrible injury she'd inflicted upon herself, and the moment her lifelong dream slipped through her fingers. The pain of that day had felt raw ever since she'd left that place, like a wound reopened.

No one seemed to notice her discomfort, and Luis kept talking.

"So, what do we have to do to get something from there?"

"I'm not sure… It's a holy place for our people. The priests don't let anyone touch the sacred tree."

"They might make an exception for you, Jade." Fenrin pointed out. "You were the one who stopped the village being attacked by that giant undead."

"This is the best way to help my brother?"

Siora nodded confidently.

"I could make do with lesser ingredients, but I wouldn't be as confident."

Jade set her shoulders.

"Then I'll find a way to get it for you."

They arrived at the Coral Lantern, the inn that other Earth wayfarers were known to be staying in. The building was on one of the upper levels of the city, accessible by the many elevated boardwalks that crisscrossed the streets below.

"Hasn't anyone in this place heard of railings?" Naomi said, shying away from the edge of the platform as they crossed towards the inn. It was smaller than the Sunken Treasure but looked better kept. "That's a two-story drop."

"They must not have personal injury lawsuits either." Jade replied dryly, glancing over the edge as they stepped up to the inn's front door. For the most part a fall would only land you on the roof of the building below, but there were places where it would be a much more perilous drop.

"Eh, that's what healing magic is for." Luis laughed, pushing open the door and leading the way inside.

The main room of the Coral Lantern was smaller than the inn they'd stayed in the night before. There was a bar on the far side of the common area, but only a couple of tables for patrons. A set of stairs went both up and down, no doubt leading to rooms for patrons. One of the tables was occupied by two humans and a rhyssit poring over a map and conversing in low voices, and a fellian woman sat on a stool behind the bar reading a book. She looked up as Luis entered, greeting him with a friendly smile and a swish of her cat-like tail.

"Welcome in! Looking for a place to stay?" She asked brightly, standing up and setting her book on the bar. "Ah, wayfarers." She added, her eyes flicking to the marks on the back of their hands as they approached.

One of the men at the nearby table looked up, his attention caught by the word. Now able to see his face, Jade recognized him.

"Wait, you're…" She couldn't place the man's name, but she knew him. "You were at that L-ReF meeting, right? The one they brought all the wayfarers to?"

"That's right." He said, standing up. He was joined by his two companions, an athletic woman and the frog-like rhyssit. Jade noted the rhyssit's hand was glowing with the same wayfarer mark as the rest of them. "Jade, right? Want to join us? Not many have made it this far yet."

"That's why we're here."

Jade moved to join them, followed by the rest of their group.

"Ahh… I'm terrible with names. Mind telling us again?" Luis asked, much to Jade's relief.

"Not a problem." He laughed good-naturedly. "I could use a refresher too, and there are some unfamiliar faces." His gaze swept over Fenrin and Siora, lingering on their slanted ears. "Did you take race changes? Or are you locals?"

"Locals. We're both from towns on the second floor." Fenrin answered, stepping forward to shake the man's hand. Oddly, the half-elf extended his left hand instead of his right. "I've joined their party to help with my own trial."

"I'm not a wayfarer." Siora explained, looking abashed. "But they did a great service to me and my people, so I'm doing my best to support them with my alchemy."

A round of introductions followed. The man was Parker, a former army ranger. While Labyrinth stat increases were slowly improving all of their bodies it was clear by his tall and muscular frame that he'd been in excellent shape before becoming a wayfarer. His human companion, Ellie, was in equally good physical condition. She was quieter than her companion, content to let him do most of the talking.

"We teamed up with a local too." Parker continued, after everyone was introduced. "Urgraan's village had a goblin problem that we helped take care of, and he's agreed to give us some aid."

The squat rhyssit slowly bobbed his head in agreement.

"Will help with cores. Good experience. Can complete trial faster and repay kindness."

Urgraan spoke in a deep croak that rumbled from the depths of his chest, throat sac pulsing with the words. Like the other rhyssits Jade had seen, he wore only a loose-fitting jacket that matched the mossy tone of his skin.

"You're going after the cores too?" Jade asked.

"That's the plan. Someone has to do it. We actually passed one on the way here, across the lake in a swamp quadrant. But since it's just the three of us, Ellie thought it'd be a good idea to get some help before we took it on."

"Bosses are dangerous." The woman said quietly, speaking for the first time. She had an eastern European accent Jade couldn't quite place. She was still staring down at the map, not looking at any of them.

"From what I gathered, some of the people she teamed up with on the first floor fell to the first boss they encountered." Parker confided quietly, leaning closer. "It shook her, but she's tougher than she looks."

"We've had some close encounters of our own." Naomi nodded sympathetically.

"Yeah, we almost died destroying the first core we encountered."

"That was your group?" Parker stood up straighter, giving each of them a closer look. "We've heard about that. Something about demons being involved?"

"It's a bit of a long story." Jade rubbed her temples, trying to decide where to begin. "It started in Estelhelm, where we met another member of our party, Tara…"

Jade went on to explain their adventure in the second-floor city, how they met Tara, and how she had uncovered a demon plot to steal valuable magical materials. Luis picked up the story from there, detailing how they discovered that the demons were planning to use the Earth cores for a dangerous ritual that involved their home. From there, they related their battle in the corespace, the destruction of the core, and how they'd narrowly prevented a demon invasion. Jade could feel the weight of it all pressing down on them as their new allies learned the true gravity of the situation.

"Now we have a quest to find the next demon ritual site on the fourth floor." Jade finished, sharing her interface window with the other party. "It's in another one of the corespaces, but we'll have to get up there before we can investigate."

"A demon invasion…" Parker sat down heavily, holding his head in his hands. "Like we didn't have enough to deal with already."

"Our goal hasn't changed." Ellie said, finally looking up. Her eyes burned with a fierce fighting spirit. "Destroy the cores. If demons are there, they're just another enemy to get through."

"She's right." Fenrin agreed. "They need the cores to summon the rest of their kind from wherever they are. If we destroy them quickly enough, their armies will never get here."

"Demon discovered in town three days ago." Urgraan croaked. "Killed by Mystic Order mage. She said demon problem is growing. Now we know why."

"A Mystic Order mage?" Naomi perked up. "Did you catch her name?"

"Yeah, we talked to her yesterday. It was Aylin, right?" Parker looked to Ellie for confirmation, who nodded once. "She'll be here tonight. Said she was interested in Earth wayfarers."

Jade grinned.

"We've met her too. If we can get her onboard, I bet we'd have enough people to take on that core you found."

"Oh yeah? How did your group destroy the one you found? Is it difficult?"

Jade recalled the bloody, desperate battle, and her grin slipped a little. It was an experience that she wasn't eager to repeat, but they didn't have much of a choice. This had to be done.

"Not if you have enough firepower." Luis said, smoothly stepping in when Jade didn't reply. "And we have someone who's perfect at bringing the heat."

As if on cue, the door to the tavern swung open with a loud thud and Tara stepped in, dressed in her black combat bodysuit. The tek'kalla cut a dramatic figure, framed by the light spilling in through the open door.

"Something needs blowing up?" She asked, crossing her arms and blowing a loose strand of hair out of her face. "Count me in."
