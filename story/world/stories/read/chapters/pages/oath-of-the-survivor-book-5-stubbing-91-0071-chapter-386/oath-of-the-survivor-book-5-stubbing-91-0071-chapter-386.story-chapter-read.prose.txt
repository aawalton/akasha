Most of Kyle’s opponents were straightforward brutes and beasts.  DeRosa was anything but.  As much as Kyle hated to admit it, the man was brilliant in his own ways.  The reconstruction of Nierburg was just one testament to his skills.

However, DeRosa also had some major disadvantages.  For one, unless he was much better connected than expected, he’d have no way to know an attack was coming until it was already underway.  Beyond that, he had no information about the types of people coming for him.  While DeRosa might have some basic information from the skirmishes on Kertan Six, Kyle, Skippy, and Tillienne all achieved their C Grade evolution since the last skirmish.  Kyle didn’t know about Skippy, but even with Tillienne only in the early stages of C Grade, her combat abilities already outstripped Reynolds’ from their initial fight.  Adding in the other elite forces, Kyle felt good about their odds.  If worst came to worst, he was at least confident in their ability to make a structured retreat.

A quick check of his nav bracelet let Kyle know where Marcus was waiting, and a few short jumps later, he walked into the office space Marcus was renting. We’ve got some big projects coming up, and with your impending absences I wanted to ensure we connected.”

Arhades had mentioned he’d be receiving an invitation to the Courts, but he hadn’t heard anything from the velgian since their lunch with Tillienne.  “When, exactly, is that supposed to be happening?”“Damned if I know,” Marcus replied, turning around.  “All I know is it’s an invite you’d be a fool to refuse.  The Alabaster Court heartlands are supposed to be some of the most beautiful places in the whole universe.  Not sure how long you’ll get to spend there, but it’s a rare enough opportunity that you have to take it.”

Pragmus wants local laborers for both the farms and processing plants, and I’m going to need people to help with drone maintenance for the new mines we’re looking to open up.  All this to say, we’re short on labor.”

Put differently, people who made bad choices, and need a fresh start.  The Gray Conglomerate is happy to send them away, but we’ll need to figure out a vetting process.  I’m not going to put somebody with a history of embezzlement in charge of managing the funds.”

I’d like to have Garth more involved, but if we can get one of his associates to get a draft to my people, they can workshop it.”
