Soulblade : A LitRPG Adventure (Sybil, Book 2)

The Grand Checkpoint has fallen.

Having just gained her freedom and started her journey, Sybil faces the looming threat of Soulblade; both an infamous criminal organization and an ancient relic. Meanwhile, Armag Hollow, the land she resides in, is in peril.

With the Grand Checkpoint gone, the Beasts of the Dark Hollow threaten to infest the land, all while old machinations come to life and insanity pervades the land. Plans of old are set in motion to achieve upheaval; all to kill the Mayor, the sickness that plagues the land once in for all.

And once that is done, a new era for Armag Hollow will soon follow.

Will Sybil be able to rise above the tides of fate, or will she drown within them?
