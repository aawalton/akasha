Henry moved like a bolt of lightning, illuminating the cave with a flash of white as one of his arms trailed behind him. With how close he was to his target, Lightning Dash was arguably faster than Blink. Somehow, it was even faster than an actual blink, so much so that if it wasn’t for his accelerated cognition, Henry himself would have needed a fraction of a second more to process what his senses were telling him. But to his perspective, the movement was smooth and easy to follow, and it helped that he could see himself from above. It was like all of a sudden, a blue line had been drawn between where he’d been floating and where Gar’Dak was. He had hoped he hadn’t given his target a lot of time to react and already, the limb he’d trailed after him was accelerating forward, magic coursing down its length as Evershifting Strike took hold. And if he had been reduced to a bright line, then his limb couldn’t even be seen.

So it was surprising when right before the impact, he saw Gar’Dak’s armguards appear in front of the man’s chest.

Henry’s arm hit the man with enough power to make the water boil around the impact. For this one, he had prioritized the [Heavy] and [Cutting] effects, as he had enough speed and not enough time for much more and with how sudden and fast the attack was, he expected his arm to slice right through the A-rank. Instead, there was a sharp pop that was immediately followed by a deep rumble as Gar’Dak sank a couple of inches into the cave’s wall, trailing some red in his wake.

“[I need to get faster,]” muttered Maurice in his ears, slowly lowering the claw he’d intended to strike the Merfolk with.

“[Should get easier once we get you to the next rank. I got nearly 30 points in every stat when I evolved,]” Henry calmly explained as he dodged out of the way of the ghostly shark, while Maurice gasped in his ears.

“[30?! That’s awesome! I mean, at this point I can see things just fine, but I can’t react fast enough. I don’t think my Clawminds are at the level of your Octominds. Plus, there’s only two of them.]”

“[They’ll get there,]” Henry said, his attention still mostly set on the two remaining Merfolk. He had considered dealing with the pets first, but then again, there was a small chance that some of them might be doing this against their will. If once Golden Mask was out of the picture and they continued coming after him this intently, it’d be another thing, but for now, they were easy enough to dodge and keep occupied with mere clones. Henry was nowhere close to running out of Avatars. Especially with him having put the teleportation network on hold.

Less than a second after having been put through the wall, Gar’Dak exploded out, his mask removed to reveal a snarl of a sharp, shark-like teeth. Interestingly enough, the Merfolk’s eyes were somewhat like his mask, a clear, glowing gold that seemed to illuminate the area in front of him. The Merfolk opened his mouth, most likely to say something, but an empowered tranquilizing Toxic Ray caught him in the guts right as a pair of limbs threatened to cut off his head.

Henry taking part in the fight didn’t mean that his Octominds were out of it.

“[Get a bubble ready, Maurice. I want you to land a good hit before I move you away and I fry him,]” Henry instructed as the Merfolk dodged under the swinging limb, grimacing as the Octomind’s arm whizzed right past his face. Gar’Dak flitted away, his glowing gaze narrowing as he tapped his stomach with his fist a couple of times, likely trying to get the blood flowing down there and regain some feeling. But of course, Henry and Maurice weren’t facing this Merfolk alone, so while Maurice worked on preparing a nasty-looking dark bubble between his claws, Henry continued his routine of dodging and observing, opting this time to send a handful of clones to Sel’Veth so that she’d get off his back.

He was still wary of the teleport-blocking artifact, but while Shark-teeth over there could take a hit and keep going, Sel’Veth was hard to touch. Henry had managed earlier when he’d embedded a little of his mana-siphoning venom, but she was much warier now, constantly rebuffing his Avatar attempts with barriers and spells. As for Veilwatch’s Mark, she had neutralized it already, somehow, though Henry was still getting plenty from the rest. By now, he’d even scored a couple of Toxic Rays with that special concoction on the pets, so he could keep going for a while still, and while he’d much prefer to wrap this encounter quickly, the two Merfolk were not that easy to bring down.

“Come back here, beast,” snarled Gar’Dak, and for a beat, Henry sensed the magic trying to worm itself into his ears, but a Dispelling Pulse as well as a little flex of willpower shook it away. Still, Henry grinned inwardly, and his mana surged toward one skill nexus in particular, one that would be getting a nice dose of Predation and Unfettered Echoes in a second.

“Right away, Master,” he shot back, and a quick glance at Maurice told him that the crab’s little void bomb was almost ready—

Then an Octomind died.

Huh?

Henry narrowed his eyes, staring at the bleeding form of the Apostle as it slowly dissolved away. The best perspective was that of the nearby Octominds surrounding Gar’Dak and through them, he could see that a foot-wide hole had been driven through the Octomind’s heart and parts of its brain. The submind in question was shunted back into his mind, losing its form, and a moment later, the picture rippled, and it was as if a veil had been lifted as the illusion was pulled away to reveal Gar’Dak holding a spear right where the Octomind had been.

“Aspect of the Last Lantern.”

Gar’Dak smirked for a beat before he swung his glowing red fist at another Octomind. Henry’s projection dodged, but still, as the fist slipped an inch away from the Apostle’s skin, the red aura latched on to the Octomind and violently imploded, tearing flesh and blood out of the projection. Then a kick finished the job, and a second Octomind was returned to him.

“Looking forward to doing the same to you,” the Merfolk taunted, baring his teeth as if what he had done had any permanence whatsoever. His Octominds would only need a couple of hours before they’d be able to resummon their form. But Henry didn’t bother correcting him and well, he also didn’t want to lose all of his manifested Octominds either.

“[Ready, Maurice?]”

“[Yes,]” the crab sent back, and that was all Henry needed to hear. Immediately, he pulled the four remaining Octominds back and gave them each a dollop of Unfettered Echoes, which they channeled straight into Telekinesis. Gar’Dak, who’d been lunging toward one of them, instantly froze as a nearly physical nimbus of purple magic surrounded his form.

Then Henry moved.

It must have looked dire to the two Merfolk. For Sel’Veth, she raised a hand toward her companion, suddenly alarmed, but she was forced to abort whatever it was she’d been attempting as a couple dozen Avatars suddenly blasted her with a pain-causing Toxic Ray. The Lionfish venom wasn’t usually very useful, but this should prevent her from intervening. As for Gar’Dak, well, he grinned, baring his teeth in a feral smile. Henry blinked a few yards away, then without prompting, Maurice sent his bubble forward.

The Merfolk’s smile slipped, and Henry noted his corded muscles flexing as he tried to force himself out of Henry’s Telekinesis. But for the first time in a while, the backlash from Telekinesis was more than manageable. Maybe because he was A-rank himself, or maybe because the skill was being bolstered by his Rupture Casting and the Aspect of Unfettered Echoes, but there would be no breaking out.

Not until Maurice’s bubble detonated, that was.

“Don’t you fucking dare,” the Merfolk snarled. “Wait. Wait wait w—”

The bubble made contact, then Gar’Dak’s voice was swallowed along with everything in a five-foot radius. The bubble at first expanded, a sphere of pitch-black darkness, then collapsed upon itself with a cold sweat-inducing pop, instantly filling the surroundings with blood and stone as the ground below was instantly reduced to mist.
