“Overseer Hydra-99, please step forward and speak your accusations.”

“Topper Esquiardo, is there anything you’d like to add?” the woman said.

Esquiardo stepped forward. He wore similar robes as the woman’s, and he sported a new wound across the side of his face, cutting through one of his eyes. It gave the once classy man a harsh look.

I also recovered the asset we lost in the Floor of Trials,” Esquiardo replied calmly. “As for the rest of your accusations, I admit I have failed. My best wasn’t good enough. I will accept any punishment, but that doesn’t make this liaison’s actions any less unlawful.”

“Immediate recycling of the guilty liaison, and portion of my rewards delivered as if the mission succeeded,” Esquiardo replied.

Esquiardo bowed as he returned to his spot. The woman cleared her throat. “Liaison Quirk, how do you respond to these accusations?” She waved a hand.
