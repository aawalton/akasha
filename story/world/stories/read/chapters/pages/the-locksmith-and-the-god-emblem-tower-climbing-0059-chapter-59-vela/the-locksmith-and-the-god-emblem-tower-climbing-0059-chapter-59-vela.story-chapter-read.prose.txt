Side by side, Goldman and Vela turned to see Ogien, a look of disdain covering his face. Was he already like this, or had we somehow managed to get to him? The compass man added inwardly with a bright smile.

"...I'm sorry, Mr. Goldman." Vela said, his calm and tranquil tone turning on.

"The important thing is that you found your way back." Goldman dismissed that. "Now, I won't stop you from using your candelabrum, but it would be good if you—"

And before he could finish, he saw a faint flame emanating from the three candles that stood atop the staff that somehow remained intact. Vela continued staring at Ogien, who was wiping the dust from his palms.

"It was foolish of me to think I could solve this alone," Vela turned with a slight smile. "Wasn't it?"

"...The conclusion of your life can't be summed up by him, Vela." Goldman said, and then paused. Thoughtful, then paused for a second. "That doesn't stop you from paying for your sins either."

Whatever they may be. Goldman added inwardly, refraining from bringing up what had happened between the two. It was clear there was a connection from the way Vela referred to him. Even more so judging by the fact that their Emblems were similar. In any case, it wouldn't be Goldman who would make him open up; that would have to come from the monk himself.

"You're annoying, you know?" Ogien said, narrowing his eyes at Goldman. "I thought the problem was that little monk over there. Well, I'm tired; let's finish this quickly."

And he unleashed two jets of flame backward as he leaped towards the pair, who remained standing. Given up so soon? Ogien wore a smug expression.

He was prepared to attack them with another concentrated beam of fire. What happened next, however, wiped the smugness from his face as quickly as a bandage being ripped off. Suddenly he found himself feeling dizzy as he spun in the air, unable to control his movements.

Vela, focused, seemed to climb his own staff, which somehow resisted his weight as it was twisted in a curious way. Carefully, so as not to touch the candles that glowed with flames, Vela grabbed the staff for support and jumped. In front of him, a man thrashed in the air like a fish out of water. The monk, in a precise movement, then grabbed his staff from below, just after jumping, and prepared a devastating attack on the floating criminal.

Smack! Using the tip of the candelabrum, Vela struck his back, pushing him down. However, the man seemed to remain in the air, as if an invisible plane prevented him from plummeting. Vela, seeing that Goldman was still holding him, delivered a calculated kick to the same spot he had hit with the tip of his candelabrum.

Before he could deliver another blow, Ogien, even more enraged, created two jets of fire in front of his palms, aiming to break free from his stasis and push himself out. Around him, the flame mysteriously turned against him as if it had rebounded off... something. Falling on his face and clothes, the man was still freed.

Goldman, despite managing to turn his own flames against him, could feel his Emblem tiring him quickly. I can't do this all the time... Losing his concentration, he abandoned the attack while coughing in the small amount of smoke that had been created by the collision of their Emblems. Realizing the advantage he had, Ogien quickly stood up and attacked Goldman. He created a wall of fire, blocking Vela, who was almost caught in the middle.

With no time to react and stop the flaming fist that was about to make contact with his face, Goldman closed his red eyes and shielded his face with both arms.

He waited for a punch that never came. How... And when he opened his eyes, he saw the back of a floating door that seemed to defy the laws of physics. The man, following the laws of the Emblem, appeared falling a few steps from Vela. Confused, the man frantically controlled his flames to remain stable, his hands turning almost pink from the injuries caused by his own fire.

"Hey, up here, beggar!" An animated voice came diagonally from Ogien.

The man was on a platform that had been generated by his doors. Despite his appearance, he was quite exhausted. Not to the point of almost dying like on the previous floor, but he felt the effects of his fight with Matthews quickly taking hold. Speaking of his brother, he was next to Harry, who was smiling broadly.

"Ogien!?" Matthews exclaimed. "But he... The information I had received was that he had died..."

"The fire never dies, skelly man!" Ogien declared, copying the Locksmith's smile.

Matthews, only realizing at that moment that he was no longer wearing his hood or glasses, felt naked. What calmed him was Harry's soothing attitude. Besides, Ogien didn't seem to be afraid of him. It was strange to consider the reaction of a legendary criminal, but for a moment, the general was glad not to be feared.

Harry spotted Vela and Goldman and waved to them both as if it were a friendly reunion. The Locksmith noticed certain injuries on both of their arms and quickly assumed the battle had been fierce. Well, it's a good thing I brought him... He added inwardly.

Boom! A splash of flame and sparks blew out of Ogien. As he spew a dark smoke, which gave contrast to his already fleshed out hands, the now flaming man was screaming, both for pleasure and for pain.

"Wow!" Harry took a step back as he looked to the others. "You guys didn't told me he had the fucking Flame Emblem!"

"We didn't told you anything!" Goldman said as they all stared at Ogien. "We need to take him down, now!"

"You don't abate a god." Ogien replied, his voice now surprisingly calm but distorted, as if the fire itself were speaking. "You can only beg to be bathed in its glory. I am that glory. I am that flame."

Seeing as things were getting serious, the Locksmith prepared to, once again, redirect the figure with his doors as he awaited for his next move. The man, however, kept talking.

"...Do you know how hard it is to control such a power?" He asked, raising his hands. "To behave as if we're all equal whilst knowingly understanding it is all but a lie? This skill—this Emblem, it is a piece of the outside. Of something that has dreamt for so long. We're all inside his dream, aren't we? But you refuse to see how the engines work. Its constantly twisting and swirling."

"What the fuck are you blabbering about?" Vela cursed, to the surprise of Harry.

Wait, he can do that now? Harry added inwardly.

"You dare to insult a man of God—"

"I dare nothing," Vela cut him off, raising his head towards the second sun. "For you are no man, nor God."

Vela, still wearing a warm smile, chuckled to himself as he eased the trip on his candelabrum.

"Curious to see such a blasphemous phrase coming from a... monk?" Ogien opened a wicked smile. "I am a force of destruction, child. The fire, it speaks to me. It orders me to—"

In that moment, Vela activated his Emblem and threw his candelabra at Ogien's shoulder like a javelin. "It orders you nowhere. Don't act like you understand what it means to feel the flame."

The flame tore through Ogien's fire as if it briefly surfed through his skin. The monk's weapon then went through a smaller door and fell right into Vela's hands once again, as he received a nod from Harry, who fiddled with his glasses.

"But I..." The man, perplexed, touched his face with his flaming hands, oblivious to the pain it caused. "Renji!? How did you survive? Y-You're that monk, are you not? The one who w-with the rituals and shit! How do you survive?!"

"I don't survive," He said, taking a step forward as everyone prepared for a fight. "I live, unlike you. You sniveling. Wretched. Miserable. Piece. Of. Shit."

And with a warm smile, Vela enhanced the flames on his candles. Ogien's face contorted as he let out a flaming screech an rushed towards Vela in an explosion of fire.

"I'll incinerate you, here and now!" He said.

Before he could, though, he fell through another door on the left of Matthews who hit him with his a great punch to the jaw. Ogien, still conscious, rushed once again, ignoring the other's attacks.

Swoosh! A wall of flame surged all around him, begging to be left alone. Matthews, however, didn't care. The skeleton simply sighed, slightly twisted his neck and threw himself into the flame arena. He then punched a whole in the fire, quickly breaking down the wall only to see Ogien's terrified expression on the other side.

Matthews, enduring the heat, jumped on top of the criminal who was now covered in flames. He gave him another blow and began to hold him so Vela could attack without any problem. Argh...! How strong! The general thought to himself as the fire covering Ogien seemed to take on a solid form.

"Quick!" Matthews told Vela, who was walking against the current of hot air pushing him away. "I can't hold on much longer..."

Before Vela could help him, Ogien exploded once more, finally throwing Matthews away. The skeleton fell against a door.

"This is my last one, brother," Harry said with a tired smile. "I won't push myself too hard."

And although he found it strange how quickly Harry had tired to the point of almost developing Emblem Exhaustion, he simply agreed as they looked at the dome of fire that had formed, only hoping that Vela would manage to get out of there alive.

The heat spiraled across the monk's skin, who tried to ignore the pain. He forced himself to remember the green fields of his old monastery and his brothers and sisters whom he loved so much. Why couldn't life have continued like this? It's the Emblems. Vela came to a conclusion. For every Goldman, there will be 3 men like Ogien. He ruminated on the purpose of this system and the nefarious motive on which the Astronomer relied to show them to the world.

"...I've waited so long for this moment, you know?" Vela said, his tone calm but his words heavy. "You took a lot from me."

"And I wish I took more!" The man bellowed, his face covered in flames, only his basic features slipping through the fire.

"The monastery was all I ever had."

"Then you had nothing!"

Once again, Vela ignored his words.

"What has a man like you ever accomplished in this life? Besides the damage, of course."

"I accumulated power. Power you will never feel or understand. The power of destruction. The power of control. The power of power."

"That is the fire, you say?" Renji raised his candelabrum. "Let us see who is worthy of said fire then, shall we?"

Renji's candelabra lit up inside the dome like a second—no, like a third sun. Through the facade of heat covering Ogien's body, the monk could feel the layers of agony taking over his being. Even so, he continued speaking and acting. He's strong... I have to admit that. He thought with a smile. In quick leaps, he dodged Ogien's flames like a rabbit dodging a wolf.

Now in hand-to-hand combat, they exchanged blows and kicks, each carrying a mass of fire as dense as a thin membrane of entropy.

In an agile movement, Vela, using an insane sense of balance, knocked Ogien down and pointed the candelabrum at him.

"...How!" He struggled on the ground. "How is his fire stronger than mine!? That doesn't make sense, damn it! I-I have the symbol of fire. I control them. All of them!" And it was stronger. The Emblem of Fire had more destructive power. But when it comes to Emblems, a clear mind is important.

It was there that the monk realized he would never leave alive. He was so badly injured that he barely recognized the pain as it devoured his skin. If I'm going to die, I'll die on my own terms. He remembered Petrikov's words, and although he didn't understand them at the time, now they made more sense than anything.

Renji emptied his mind and let his subconscious take over.

"Thank the fire for being the cause of your death," Vela said. "For there is only one thing worse than dying in the dark; it is living in it."

He let his Emblem take over.

"The brightest flame is the one that burns fastest."

A hole opened in the dome, just enough for Harry and Goldman to glimpse what was happening inside. They then saw Vela smiling as his tears evaporated in the heat.

...That's your story, Vela. Harry added inwardly. Don't let him take over.

And then the monk let his candelabrum gather all the fire around it as he directed it toward Liam's face. The fire of a lifetime. The fire of love.

"Goodbye, Mr. Vela." Renji said with a warm smile.
