The spear wielder thrust once more, eyes squinted from the bright glare and scalding heat coming off Ethan’s right claws, but it was easy enough to dodge. His claws went for the throat once more, like a ball of raging fire, but it was parried at the last moment by the terrified lizardling, knocking it off course before it screeched in pain as the flames licked its scales. Which opened it up for a perfect kick to the nethers.

Its clawed legs left the ground for a moment, eyes wide in surprise as it couldn’t even inhale as its knees buckled, and Ethan cut into its neck.

Whenever he tried to pull it closer to his claws and concentrate it, he could only keep it stable for a few seconds before it blazed outwards.

Still, he was slowly getting better at it.

As if he’d been hitting the gym for a week and this was the first day that he wasn’t feeling sore.

At least they couldn’t surround him.

Shit.

Ethan pulled the flames back and made sure his t-shirt wasn’t on fire. There were darker spots, but that was it.

I’m going to need a change of clothes soon.

Turning his attention back to the small crowd ahead of him, Ethan couldn’t help but feel excited.

It would be better in a few minutes. Maybe an hour or two. Ideally, he’d also find something to eat. All of this strenuous activity was making him hungry. And sleepy. But he wasn’t about to fall asleep in the middle of enemy territory.

He’d made that mistake once, and he wasn’t doing it again.

Assimilate [Extended Enhancement (Tier-1)] from spellcore?

You have to spend fragments to make fragments.

If he was to rank them, he’d have to put Toughness at the top. That one just straight-up made it so he wasn’t bleeding out right now.
