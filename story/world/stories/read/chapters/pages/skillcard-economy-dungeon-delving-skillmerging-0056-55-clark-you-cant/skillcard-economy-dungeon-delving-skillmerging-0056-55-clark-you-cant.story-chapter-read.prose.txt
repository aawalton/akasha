The building shook once again. I desummoned Rotary Rend and assimilated Obsidian Hide, feeling that deep tingle in my chest.

I need to think about this. Right now, Rotary Rend is my ace. The most explosive card in my arsenal. Even with the repercussions, it is very useful. I don’t want to lose that explosiveness. First, lets get out of here, then sit down and do what I should have done long ago. Analyze my previous merges and try to find something common between them.

I tried channeling my magika but noticed I was almost drained. With the repeated hopping using Shadowstride and the use of Rotary Rend, my magika was fizzling out. To let my magika reserves recover, I walked. Past the broken walls and disheveled offices, I reached the stairs.

Running down, I reached the bottom floor and saw what Tina had left behind. The giant toads had been cut apart, their guts and blood coating the floor. I walked through the middle of it and reached the parking lot. There were giant toads, snakes, salamanders, and frogs. All dead. Cut apart, melted, or burned into ash.

I noticed the footprints in the blood and followed them until I reached the old hotel hallways.

“Toby,” I called, noticing everyone standing together. Tina was there too.

“Eww, you reek.” Toby pinched his nose and scurried away.

I looked at Tina, who was standing beside him and was covered in more blood than me and probably smelled worse. I guessed the latter part, as I couldn’t differentiate my smell from hers.

“Tina is standing right beside you,” I pointed out. “And she doesn’t smell?”

“No, she doesn’t,” Toby said with a stern face.

I thought love made you blind. I didn’t know it also turned you anosmic.

Tina laughed awkwardly at that.

“So,” I steered the conversation toward what was important. “Why are you just waiting here?”

The way to escape was just past the hallways. It didn’t make sense for them to wait here.

“There’s a slight problem,” Tina said awkwardly.

“What happened?” I frowned.

“A monster got in, and it’s gold rank,” Toby answered.

“I saw it myself.” Tina said.

“If a monster got in, let’s just leave through the main exit.”

“The basement exit collapsed, the first floor—” Toby was explaining how all the exits were busted when Tina said there might be more in that place, but my mind had already drifted away.

In all this, they had forgotten.

“What about the receptionist?”

“I don’t know.”

There was no guilt in Toby’s voice.

I ran towards the door, but Toby jerked me back.

“Where are you going?”

“To save her,” I stated the obvious.

“Have you gone mad?” Tina exclaimed. “It’s a gold-rank monster.”

“Toby, we were the ones who gagged her and left her there.” I couldn’t believe how unbothered Toby was. Like it wasn’t his fault at all. “We have to get her out of there.”

“You cannot do anything,” Tina said sadly. “It has used some kind of skill and turned those hallways into a labyrinth.”

She walked towards the door and opened it. The walls were old and torn, just like before, but they kept going and going endlessly. The end was not visible.

“It is not your fault,” Tina said sympathetically.

It is exactly my fault!! You and all these people are here because of me. She is in there with a monster because of me. It is because of me that we are in danger. It is my fault.

“Just let it be.” Even Toby’s voice had grown sympathetic. “Let’s find another way out.”

No. No.

“Clark,” Tina said. “We cannot do anything.”

Clark, don’t do that. Clark, you can’t do this. Clark, you aren’t strong enough. Clark, listen to what I say.

Why? What was the point of me growing this strong? It is just like before. I have to listen to those stronger than me because they know more.

Why? I wanted to save the NPCs. They refused. I want to save this girl, and they refuse.

I did not say a word. I channeled my magika. My shadow rippled and started consuming me.

“Clark, no!” Toby shouted.

I fell into my shadow and emerged far away.

Inside the labyrinth.

The shadows fell apart. My magika was gone.

Shit, was I really this low?

I composed myself and stood straight with the help of the wall. Looking at the endlessly repeating hallways made me remember Life’s Last Hope. I missed Pyiari and Lania and everyone else I had met there.

I moved forward.

It would take time for my magika to regenerate. So, with no choice at all, I walked.

There's something wrong.

It had been about fifteen minutes since I started walking and that should have been enough for at least some of my magika to regenerate.

I tried reaching out of it, but couldn't grab like there was a wall between it and me.

I couldn't use my magika.

And I was stuck in a labyrinth with a gold rank monster.

I am fucked.
