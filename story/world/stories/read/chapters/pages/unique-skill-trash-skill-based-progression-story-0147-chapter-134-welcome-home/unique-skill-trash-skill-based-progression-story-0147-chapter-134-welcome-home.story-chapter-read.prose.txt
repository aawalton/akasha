Chapter 134

Welcome Home

Some places have a history, stories about how they were born, where they come from, who used them and how. Yet, the truly powerful places are those that you don’t have to see, to be told of their significance, for you feel their history by stepping into such a place.

The Ancestral Cathedral was one such place. A place that was so steeped in history, power, and traditions that I felt them as soon as I exited the carriage and began walking on the stone steps leading up to its massive archways.

Maybe it is my deep seeded connection to the Earth element, but just stepping out and walking along the road to the stone steps told me of a vast network of catacombs beneath this great structure. Catacombs that spoke of both hope and horrors, as each step seemed to echo back into my mind, showing the underground workings, and paths that once served as both salvation and a final resting place for the dead.

Dozens of smooth stone steps going upward told of a newer level of power and prestige. For what lay below was solid stone as well, but it had blemishes and gouges that spoke of a harsher life with minimal care, before the recent allocation of funds were made available. This too spoke of the history to me, revealing that there were darker parts of the past that people were trying to hide or distract away from.

Then there was the Ancestral Cathedral itself, which had clearly been worked and reworked multiple times until smooth grey granite stones lined the walls of the cathedral. A long marble pathway led forward to the base, where nine sacred statues stood, each depicting a glorified form of the original seven healers and their sacred lineages that came from each.

Walking forward I could see statues that were larger than life and twice as beautiful staring back at us from a central atrium. The statues were designed to be in a crisscrossing pattern depicting the founders for each major bloodline that served as the bedrock for all healing lineages.

There were statues for each major healing family: Amador, Bloom, Convictus, DiVoratore, Piedad, Voto, and Zabal.

Of those statues present I noted a distinct disparity between males and females, with females serving as the founding family member for all but the DiVoratore and Voto lineages. The rest were all females, all impressively tall, large chested and perfectly proportioned. Honestly, after staring at the statues, I could almost see why everyone who was a healer chose to cover themselves in the black hood and cloaks, as there was no way we could look that good. Even the males were all impressively tall, and muscular.

Then began a pattern of silence, where each person in the long procession would go forward, placing their hand upon a corresponding crystal linked to each family statue.  Amador was amethyst. Bloom was jade, Convictus had a ruby, Piedad was a diamond, and Zabal was Sapphire

As for the males, theirs were aquamarine for the DiVoratore statue, and opal for the Voto family statue.

All were beautiful, and all seemed to shimmer with hidden power. Power that would be activated when family members for each awakened bloodline stepped forward and touched the stones. Just being in this procession, I felt an intense pull of just how small I was in the grand scheme of things. Perhaps that is why the statues were made so impossibly tall, staring down at you with improbable proportions and beauty that would be impossible for anyone to truly reach. It was symbol, a metaphor, for how much these people have grown to mean to the people of this world. From the seven main statues, there were exit hallways with smaller wings devoted to the minor families of each.

As people made their way through, it was clear that they would be claimed by one of the seven main families, based on how bright their light showed. A full glow meant to stay, a partial glow meant to exit and try your hand at the offshoot and sub-branches for the major families. Having gotten out of the carriage with the Matriarch, I knew where she was. As a Livanathan, it was clear that she didn’t have the namewrights to one of the original seven, but watching her, I saw how she had a reaction to the amethyst stone at the Amador family statue. The faint glow from the stone directed her to the right, and out of my sight entirely.

Then walking forward, I felt myself flowing in lockstep with Madame Bloom. While I couldn’t quite see her, that didn’t stop me from feeling her next to me. Her whole presence, the distance and pace of her stride, and her steadfastness to stay by my side all told me what I already knew, she was walking with me until our paths separated. Going with her, I watched as she made no reaction to the amethyst stone not reacting to her. Then I looked on in awe as the jade stone in front of the Bloom statue glowed brightly, letting everyone know that we were home, that she was home.

At the sudden glow, two attendants who had been standing in the shadows a moment before stepped forward. One held a line of country flags, with the Empire’s flag being the one on top, followed by the Nostrylvanian flag, and a few others that were easily recognizable. Within a second, Madame Bloom pointed, and immediately picked the Empire’s flag. A dark background with a crown colored by nine trailing colors, representing the original nine founding families of the nation, two of which were now a dimmer shade of light purple and hunter green denoting the loss of those two families.

At the choosing of the Empire’s colors, musicians came out and began playing the Empire’s song, which echoed grandly in the tall round cathedral, while the flag was pulled up to wave just below the head of Lady Bloom herself. Just low enough to be respectful that these lineages mean more than a country, but also just high enough that it could be assumed the statue of Lady Bloom herself was staring at the flag raised before her.

Only once that mini-ceremony was over, a minute at most given how quick everyone was from start to picking the flag, to hoisting it up, everything was quick and precise.  Then it was my turn to try my hand at the jade stone.

Placing my hand on the jade stone, I could feel a slight cool tingling sensation going within my mind.

Family recognizes family: You have been marked as an honorary member of the Bloom family.

Sometimes choosing who will be your family, is just as important as knowing where you come from.

At the same time as I saw the message appear in my mind, the jade gemstone before me glowed lightly for a moment, before winking out entirely. I assume this was something to do with the fact that I was currently listed as a foster child of the Bloom household, and thus had Bloom as my last name, while still having the DiVoratore bloodline awakened within my veins.

Regardless, it was clear that had I been meant to go down a corridor, I would have been directed, and since I was not, I was urged to go on. Taking those first few steps alone felt odd. As if a warmth that had been guiding me all this time was now missing, as Madame Bloom stayed behind by the statue of her ancestors. Meanwhile, I moved forward, touching the ruby stone for the Convictus statue, only to have it lie completely dormant. Then taking a breath, I turned around to go back toward the right-hand side of the wall, and head back to the DiVoratore statue, that stood prominently next to the Bloom statue. For a moment, I wondered if it would be more effective to have us all go in statue by statue but realized that this back-and-forth movement allowed for a more snake like pattern, allowing for all of us to be in here at once and seemingly stay out of everyone’s way. This was how I found myself striding forward, each step seeming to echo under my feet, as I felt a well of power and serene calm calling out to me from the stone beneath my feet.

Going forward to the towering thirty-foot-tall statue of a man who was larger than life, long hair, and warm smile filling his face as he rested with a sword that looked remarkably similar to the one I used wedged into the ground by his right foot. While his left hand reached up to the heavens and seemed to be summoning forth a ball of energy. Standing under his massive frame, I didn’t feel intimidated. In fact, quite the opposite, as I almost felt like I belonged. Taking a breath, I reached out and placed my hand on the giant aquamarine stone, and I could instantly feel the difference between this reaction and the last one from the Bloom statue.

If what I felt before was a cool tingling sensation, this one seemed to be the opposite as I felt a wave of warmth erupt in my body as if my very blood was stirring to life. That’s when the system gave me a two-word response that made me wonder exactly who or what was powering these systems. For once I placed my hand onto the stone, I was met with but two simple words. Words that let me know, that while my parents and grandparents might have been willing to get rid of me, my family, or at least those seeming to look over the actual bloodline had a difference of opinions.

Seeing the message, I was glad that I had the veil over my face so that others couldn’t see my almost immediate reaction to the stone. A stone that seemed to glow brighter than the one had for Madame Bloom. A glow that all but demanded that everyone in the immediate area stop and turn towards me, if only to recognize the source of the light before them.

Just as before, two people came forward immediately, presenting me with options for flags to choose from. Even through the tears that were forming in my eyes, I could easily make out the Empire’s flag. Choosing the flag was easy, then as had been shown earlier, trumpeters and musicians came out to play the national anthem while our flag was raised. Looking around, I could see that a few other national flags were raised, one Nostrylvanian flag was waving from the Voto statue, a few were waving from the Convictus and Zabal statues, but only two received musical accommodations. I assumed this was something about being recognized in your home country, that the others likely could and would receive musical honors at their own countries, but not here. Not in these sacred grounds where space was at a premium. The fact that there were people here to help raise the foreign flags at all was apparently enough.

With the country flag raised, I was then directed by a staff member to stand off to the side. I wasn’t touched, instead it was clear that as a recognized healer, I had deferential status. Again, no one dared to speak here, as this place was entirely quiet. Instead, the respect was shown in the overly dramatic bows, a hand directing me to stand into a portion of the cathedral that only now did I notice seem to glow with an odd greenish-blue hue to my senses. Seeing the light, I could tell that it was empowered with something deeply magical. I also mentally noted it as roughly the same area where Madame Bloom stood, when she too was being recognized by the Bloom family statue.

Stepping into the pattern, I felt an instant surge of static electricity, as power slowly flowed into me. Stored up over hundreds of touches and times that people have come to this very statue, placed their hands over the stone and apparently had mana drawn from their bodies to try to activate a connection with the system. I found this out and understood it all, as the finer details of what I was seeing and why this place seemed to glow to my senses became apparent with the next system prompt.

World Quest Triggered: Finding Ancestral Places of Power (1 of ??): Whether by luck or choice, you have found a sacred spot where a family member of your bloodline fought against darkness and shed blood to preserve humanity for another day. Journey the world to find more similar places of power, and create your own spots while you are at it. The world is dark, but lighter with you and those of your bloodline still walking the righteous path.

Benefits Possible: Convert Collected Life Mana for Additional Perks.

Collected Life Mana Available: 3,472.

For a moment I was confused by what I was seeing. Only after staring at it for a bit did I realize that this was a sort of system hosted shop. One that required mana to use, and for a second I didn’t know where nor how the mana came. At least not until the next healer passed by, placing their hand on the stone at which point the value of collected mana changed by one.

Seeing the number, I realized that over three thousand people had come to these lands, made the pilgrimage and added their own power to this statue to give me the opportunity. Just knowing that, and taking in the full gravity of such an occurrence, I paused for a moment. Then looking around, wondering if anyone else would come to claim a spot at the shop, I wanted to wait for a moment. But then, part of me just wanted to see what options were available.

I mean, what could a random shop have? Right?

Then I saw the selection, and realized I might not want to waste time waiting for others who clearly hadn’t come in a while.

Skill badging Available: …

Skill Evolution Possible: … (multiple options)

Bloodline Skill Evolution Possible: …

Magical Affinity Improvement: …

…

The list went, and that’s when I knew my notions about waiting for others were gone, as I wanted some of the effects that were being offered.
