The needles in Zane's body were endowed with a delicate but quite potent curse.

It was a curse built to contain vast quantities of essence and Law, a curse meant to contain Empyreans of even T4 caliber.

And it worked—the needles stifled Zane utterly when they'd been plunged into his back and neck.

But the moment his eyes shone silver, this changed.

The curse within was built to contain and nullify—and in that moment it experienced an influx of Zane's Supernova-white essence, edged in silver. Essence it was built to handle, except that essence simply didn't stop.

It pumped in, and in, in a rush, as though spilling out from an infinite wellspring—so much Law and essence, and it seemed the more the needles took, the more the man had to give...

They were built to stab into a man's reserves at his weak points and neutralize.

But it was as though the floor had just fallen out—

They were trying to neutralize what was functionally infinity.

In the space of a second the curses visibly dulled in the Astral Plane, made inert. Simply overwhelmed by that Supreme, and that Supreme, silver-tinged essence—the needles forced from Zane's body...

As he lay there, still crushed under the steel, it wasn't merely Zane's eyes that shone silver.

A silver gloss shimmered over the entirety of his physique. His aura was edged silver too, visible in the Astral Plane and the physical...

Then there was a bar.

A bar of pure silver shining clear in the Astral Plane over his fallen body. It almost seemed a second Health bar, visually. It was visible in the physical realm too, some extension of his spirit.

Then Zane burst to life.

The essence flooded his physique, not just a new fuel, but in quantities like never before—

He flexed. The flash was brilliant in the Astral Plane; he exerted, fiercely, stepping down.

Twelve Iron Gold were simply thrown right off him, and with violence. The rest had to brace, stumbling back.

Then he was free.

For just an instant—the Gold had no capacity for surprise; they attacked instantly, clearly aiming to restrict him again. But his axes were in fists, and he demonstrated a new capacity.

A familiar series of Star-Crushing Slashes split reality. A one-two-three-four, cleaving through one of the few Class-I tanks left, and three lighter Class-II units—but it wasn't just his sheer essence output that changed; the Slashes came stunningly fast, like there was almost no cooldown between one, and two, and three—

The Skill had somehow been pushed past its limit. Supercharged with essence.

And then he whirled, and did the same to the Iron Gold behind him, and several realizations seemed to dawn on the Gold at once—

There was no cooldown to his physique either.

It was like his muscles refreshed instantly from the attacks, like he could simply blitz out an infinite series of Star-Crushing Slashes, forever, in this state—

Yet another datapoint entered the Iron Golds' mechanical minds, the handful that survived this first assault...

They saw that silver bar over Zane's head shrink, just slightly, in the few seconds he was active. It seemed to shrink at the same rate regardless of if he was attacking or standing still. They might've felt relieved, if they could feel that emotion—it was an out.

But then silver flowed out of the fallen Iron Golds—the stuff of souls, flowing right into Zane.

And that silver bar extended significantly.

The Iron Gold processed all this with mechanical efficiency and responded. Ion-beams burst out at close range, lancing Zane in the back—then 'containment' Golds clamped spiked steel girders on him, drilling into the flesh, injecting a special, paralysis-oriented lightning straight into his major muscles.

In a split second they'd responded with this assault, and they processed that silver 'Health bar,' jumping down a fraction, as he took the damage—but then it jumped right back up. And as the paralysis lightning tried to dent his Health again, it kept shooting back up, the end of the bar quivering violently—

A look at his physique confirmed the deduction.

His body's sheer regeneration capacities—in essence, and in life—had reached such a point that all this could only suppress his Health, and even then, temporarily.

The rest of the Iron Gold were frozen trying to parse this—trying to make sense of the escalating data they were taking in, even as they coordinated instantaneously with each other. They weren't frozen out of surprise—it was simply that attack plans needed clear parameters...but it felt like they were trying to attack the infinite.

Their minds simply didn't understand what to do with this.

Zane spared them their dilemma with six more Star-crushing Slashes, the last a fan of blinding brilliance that stretched several hundred miles in width.

Then there was merely one enemy left. An enemy that'd touched down just as he finished the last of the Iron Gold.

It was one of Stegar's bodies—the 9th, the lancer in Knight-Forme. The very lancer he'd exchanged with at the start of this battle. It'd been nearby when Zane's Supreme activated, and it'd been one of the bodies that'd helped to subdue Zane in the first place.

It telepathically received the knowledge of its subordinates, and it seemed to grasp the implications of the situation instantly.

The tubes running down its body instantly shone white with Destruction; then ethereal tubes shone in the Astral Plane, extending from its back, like it was drawing from some larger power source—its other bodies.

Then it drew its lance and exchanged with Zane a single time.

In that exchange, it landed that giant, hard lightning lance—now white, like the color of an ion beam, loaded with well over a pillar of Destruction.

It landed hard—slicing Zane's new silver bar, which had just lengthened by almost 50% from all those Iron Gold kills, down to half its maximum size.

Then that lance pumped in white Destruction-lightning, and that constant channeling seemed to barely hold Zane's bar at bay, which jumped back and forth at its end—Zane gritted his teeth; it seemed the first attack, in this new form, he had to respect at least a little.

But his response came an instant later and ended that.

The fight lasted a single drawn-out exchange. And again it was the speed of the slashes—six of them, in a blinding immensity, then another six, back-to-back, with no break—that distinguished it from all that'd come before.

And after the exchange—and the fight—concluded, that silver bar jumped back to its maximum almost instantly. In less than a second.

In that moment, his soul was simply pouring in too much vitality and essence into his body and his Skills; any essence used in this state was instantly regenerated, any fatigue accumulated, or damage taken, the same—

The Iron Gold had grappled with this issue.

It was almost as though you had to either kill this version of Zane in a single blow or simply outlast the duration of whatever this state was—but that silver bar shrank at a set rate every second. And every single soul Zane reaped only seemed to contribute to its length... that was the impossible problem.

Stegar's was only the latest addition.

It was a fraction of a soul, not the full Prime, but it contributed as much to the silver bar as a handful of Iron Gold.

Zane had hardly finished his task here when he stepped. Then he was gone—an instant later he was in front of his fallen Master. There was Reina's avatar kneeling there too, alongside a few Empyrean World Tree healers—her eyes widened at the sight of him, but she seemed to recover her composure quite quickly.

"He's in critical condition, but I'm directing the rest of the elixir's energies into stabilizing him," she said. Several new Blessings already shimmered over the Sage's form, and even now the healers were chanting, hands spread, golden Ancestral Spirit elementals at their fingertips.

Zane watched them, feeling slightly helpless, but she caught his look—"He'll be fine," she assured him.

It took a moment for Zane to understand that. Then—

"Malzareth," he said to her. "Where is it?"

"It's void-walked away," she told him. She seemed to recognize the urgency in his voice and blinked. "Zane, are you—it's with its Primes, all of them."

"Reina," he said. "Where is it?"

She had just a split second's hesitation, but she visibly made a decision. "It's critically wounded. The Primes have retreated with it, as far as we can tell; they're guarding it—there. Traveling almost directly north-northwest as we speak."

She gestured to a sea of shattered reality just beyond the wardlines. "And that line of Endbringers—they're trying to block any attempt at void-walking right now, on our end. They're trying to cut off any pursuit."

Zane took a very quick view of the situation.

The bulk of Malzareth's armies still seemed to be trapped behind the wardlines, in wild retreat. Their Empyreans were stationed along the wardlines.

Several of Dragonspire's top-end elites were still down; their souls were dim in the Astral Plane. They'd unleashed their Supremes. Few seemed in fighting condition to Zane. But that second tier of elites, like Haxorax or Lain or the Constellation King's daughter, were all fiercely attacking down the lines—trying to make a breakthrough.

None of them were successful so far. The few that came close were pursued into the void and shot down right after.

The bulk of Dragonspire's forces right now simply weren't set up for this kind of chase.

"In less than a minute, I'll need you to dispatch someone who can void-walk to me," Zane told her.

And then he stepped away, heading right for the nearest cluster of Greater Endbringers, before she had a chance to reply.

He knew she wouldn't be comfortable with this; she would've preferred a better plan—any plan—but time was of the essence, and he'd felt just how critically Malzareth was wounded.

And right now, frankly, he wasn't at all interested in thinking about anything else.

He needed Malzareth's head. He needed it desperately. He just needed her to trust him.

He went for it.

He was greeted by a line of high-tier Mirror Drakes and Archknights, and he didn't bother fighting them optimally. There was no subtlety to his approach. Their essence-soaking properties worked against them here, since right now essence was nothing to him.

A flurry of slashes—six, six, six—wiped the field. They were overwhelmed by the sheer essence intake even as they fell. By the end of it his muscles were still like brand new and his essence, which had dipped to a third of its maximum, again refilled almost instantly.

An instant later Reina called out his position in his portal charm, and a tall, silver-armored figure landed in a crouch. It was the Silver Winds' Lord-Commander—older but classically handsome, still pale but surprisingly still half-full of essence, judging by how he felt in the Astral Plane. Zane remembered the man drew a fair portion of his attacks from his elementals, which then drew from the World Tree's reserves.

"Sir Consort," the Lord-Commander said with an incline of the head.

He extended an arm, and Zane grasped it. Then the man took out a dagger, which lit up with Destruction; he slashed open the void.

They stepped into a tunnel of endless night.

And there, Zane caught sight of his foe.
