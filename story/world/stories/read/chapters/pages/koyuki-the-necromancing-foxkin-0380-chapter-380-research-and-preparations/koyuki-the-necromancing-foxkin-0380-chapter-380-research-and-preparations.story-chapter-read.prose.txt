She had joined me in the library and was doing her own research. Sadly the Frost library did not have a lot of information about necromancy, they did have quite a bit about monsters though.

"It's your choice, I am not sure if there would be any synergy. Kaito seems to be able to use Goro's frostfire in some way. I guess it is like a joint casting for inborn powers? I am not sure if you could combine your powers with a vampiric horse. Maybe you could drink from it in an emergency? Actually, can you drink undead blood?" I wondered.

"True but it would be an undead, we can add affinities if we prepare it properly. I have to admit I am not sure how we'll cycle anything in a crawler, it's why I am doing some research on them. Even if we stay with water, cycling through the corpse has helped to improve my undead so far." I stated.

"Can you… Can you add that afterwards?" Rose asked.

"Um, I am not sure how? I don't think I can truly change the type of an undead once raised." I pondered that for a moment. "You can turn a zombie into a skeleton, originally I really didn't like zombies but I have changed my mind. Proper zombies are quite decent if the corpse is not too rotten. Undead can also evolve, slowly, but that is not something you can control too much I think."

"No… Wait, you are a tier three and I taught this technique to Helena, Skadi and Cassie. Perhaps I could teach you to cycle mana? Actually, what do you do with the bit that leaks from your core?"

"I am not sure, it is neutral after all. We are not talking about something like light mana." I said.

"Living creatures all have mana, right?" Rose asked.

"I think so. I was taught that all people have some mana, most just don't have enough to do anything with it. Once you have a core large enough to use spells you are considered a mage. Some people who are almost mages might have some minor passive benefits though, even if they will never be able to cast a spell." I replied.

"Maybe neutral mana is part of living creatures but undead replace that with necrotic mana?" Rose theorised.

"Maybe but you can use neutral mana, like for telekinesis." I pointed out.

"True and it doesn't hurt me but it might not nourish me either. Undead specifically need necrotic mana as food, right?" Rose asked.

"Devouring just absorbs mana though… Or does it? Do you think devouring turns whatever mana is inside the target necrotic? Can devouring convert non-neutral mana into necrotic mana?" I wondered.

It was a fascinating question but I was still in the process of trying to teach Rose proper circulation. I switched to necrotic mana and tried to push that into her.

"I can feel that, I think that could work." Rose murmured, then she frowned. "I can feel how you are trying to circulate it. I think what comes from my own core just flows in all directions without purpose. I wonder if that is natural or if the necromancers made my body that way?"

"Good, that technique is also what we will use for your horse. It will be a joint casting so normally I could do most of the work but if we do a vampiric undead I will need you for the blood. I can only circulate water myself." I said.
