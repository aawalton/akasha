The Mirror Dragon was one of exactly two Primes with over 900 Shards of Destruction or Creation. The other was the Meat King itself, which had survived an onslaught half of Dragonspire had participated in.

It represented a significant step up from the Primes he'd faced so far.

He approached it, axes unsheathed—he felt Reina positioned just a click or so away. He didn't plan on taking it lightly. Equally, he still felt quite confident.

It stood in a clearing in the middle of its floor—on a flat plain of crystal that could've been mistaken for the surface of a frozen lake. It was ringed by mountains of those diamond-like, half-mirror, half-transparent crystals.

It didn't seem in any particular hurry, letting him set up and approach. It simply regarded him as he strode up to it.

It screeched as he took out his axes, a sound with a soul attack component—it looked like a crystalline wave in the Astral Plane. He let it simply wash over him, breaking against his Pagoda, while Reina's avatar hid behind its own mountains to block.

It regarded him coolly. It seemed to want to wait on him.

But that was its decision. He saw no reason to delay any further.

The edges of his axes slicked white, and he threw for its head.

In the same moment the Mirror Dragon screeched again. A crystalline beam left its jaws—a beam that seemed solid, a pillar of pure crystal, despite the fact that it flowed like any other pillar attack.

The beam caught Zane's axe head-on.

Beams of Supernova Law and essence split nine different ways from the point of contact like light through a prism—shunting off to either side, bouncing off the mountains, refracted and reflected thousands of times as they traveled the length of the Lair. The beams shifted as his axe climbed—rising undeterred, splitting the beam with ease, almost striking to the base—

The Mirror Dragon was quickly forced to abandon its beam. The axe sailed by its head, embedding itself in a distant mountain.

It seemed the first exchange went to Zane.

The Mirror Dragon gave him another screech, and he detected in it a sense of annoyance.

He wound his axe back, preparing for the next exchange. He got the sense it still wasn't convinced, that it wanted to have another chance at him.

But it didn't do that.

Instead its eyes exploded with silver, and Zane had to frown—even as the air around him solidified, growing crystalline, and all around him chunks of reality itself took on a slick color, as though made of glass. The effect continued for miles all around.

It was the very concession he'd been hoping to gain—making it use its Supreme.

The Mirror Dragon's Supreme made it incredibly difficult to wield essence and Law-based attacks, since they'd all be instantly reflected. Among the Supreme’s other quite difficult traits. Which was why, in game-planning this, Command had emphasized that it'd be quite useful to activate it early—before the final fight.

The Mirror Dragon had to know this was a key resource.

He was just wondering what its plan was when the answers stepped into place.

The Mirrorworld was a land of planes and mirrors—and on two of those flat, transparent planes, two figures stepped into view. Stepping out of seemingly nowhere.

One, a hulking, stitched-together creature wearing a crown of bone.

The other a twenty-foot-tall mech made of a dark silver, peak Origin-grade material—a mech unlike any he'd seen so far.

The Meat King, and Stegar's 1st Body.

And then the two of them stepped into reality, shattering the planes they stood in.

He'd expected a surprise, but two top-end Primes, he had to admit, was a slightly bigger surprise than he'd thought. Mostly because he hadn't thought hiding that scale of a surprise was possible. It'd snuck past the Scryers' Guild too—though he supposed that was because the Primes truly weren't there.

They were hiding in the Mirrorworld.

Which might’ve explained why they hadn't been seen anywhere else—in Cromm's Lair, or in the higher floors.

It was a functionality that wasn’t possible to predict, simply because it’d never been shown before.

"Zane—just, careful," said Reina, her voice tight. “I still don’t think this is everything.”

He nodded. He was already flooding his body with new essence, priming his physique for all-out combat. At the same time he felt two new Blessings strike him from behind, one to do with Vitality.

He didn't panic.

Neither did Reina, from what he could feel. Though she'd felt surprised at first, understandably.

She'd drawn a little more Creation to her hands. He knew she wasn't under the impression she could make a fight-altering difference in this fight, especially since she was just in her avatar form, but she likely kept ready just in case.

And then Cromm's eyes went silver too. The second surprise of the fight, back-to-back.

Its skin stretched and thickened, transmuting to something stiffer and harder, something between skin and bone, an extremely high-grade material; its entire physique took on a very pale color, like that of a body just past the point of death.

Its entire physique also erupted with vitality in the Astral Plane. Its form shimmered with that deathly-wise essence the Bloody Starved had wielded, especially its hands and its teeth.

Its muscles were instantly flush with that energy.

He was still trying to understand why it'd done this right away—just what, precisely, the nature of this supposed trap was—when it stepped.

A single physical step, no essence. It looked light, but reality fractured around it.

And then it was gone.

The next image Zane saw of it was with its fist towering over his face—a line of after-images between where it'd been and where he now stood.

He was forced to take the blow on his forearms. A fist the size of a hammer landed on Zane's body.

He skidded back several feet, his heels breaking up the crystal. He registered the feeling of it, the smarting sensation, with some surprise.

There was a split-second of incredible coldness—an instant where it tried to take his Vitality, but it was just an instant, and Reina's Blessing helped blunt the effect.

What he was surprised by was the relative strength he felt coming at him.

At the same time the other two Primes attacked. Stegar raised its arms, which folded into ion cannons. A fan of mirrors appeared around it.

Dozens of thick ion beams raced through the air.

Zane simply stepped, keeping a tight circle around the Meat King, moving behind it—letting it absorb the entirety of the beams. Beams that had almost no effect on it at all as it landed; a handful of points of damage taken, if that. Which confirmed that it was essentially immune to essence attacks in this state.

Which was also why chains wrapped Zane's fists as he moved.

He landed just as the newly Supreme'd Meat King was turning to face him.

He simply gave it a single, solid THWACK to its side.

And he felt—again to his slight surprise—a squelch beneath his fist as the force visibly punctured, rupturing a small organ.

In the Astral Plane, he felt its vitality take a visible hit.

His brow creased. Cromm let out a breath as it took the attack; it finished wheeling to face him. In this state it was significantly slower turning than it was in straight lines, he noticed.

Even as it turned to him, he treated it to a tight, short, hard left hook to the jaw. No essence, since it'd only rebound in close quarters off the mirrors; just pure, simple, physical force.

He saw its head snap all the way to the side.

It stumbled, making a noise of clear confusion.

Zane hit it two more times, throwing it off-balance even as it stumbled.

His eyes hardened.

Are—

Jab, snapping its head back—

You—

It snarled, swiping a windmill of a blow that would've crumpled one of Stegar's lesser bodies.

He ducked it and ripped two blows to its midsection—feeling his fists sink in a solid seven inches each time, folding it in half—

Serious?

This was the same creature that shrugged off attacks from half the Galaxy—that rampaged through all of Dragonspire?

***

This was the same creature that had given Reina and her armies fits just months ago.

It hadn't changed since then. She was certain of that.

Throughout this mission her attention was split between her avatar with Zane, and her true body overseeing the war. Although in moments like this she unapologetically focused on this fight.

She told herself she had to be—its result might very well decide the war.

She'd also been struck by the surprise ambush, not least because she'd expected Cromm and Stegar's 1st Body to be hiding in the Throne Room, so they could assist their master. On the surface it simply didn't make sense to fight Zane this way.

And then she'd seen the Meat King strike, saw Zane block, and felt relief—at the same time she saw his bewilderment.

Then she saw him counter.

It was impossible to know for certain, before matching them up, how this fight would go.

Since even though Zane had the Creation shard advantage—likely 200 or 300 extra shards, after maxing out his full 1024. Natural physical aptitude made a difference. Bloodlines, passives, stats, Titles, even its Empyrean perks—it all made a difference. It could simply be that the other latent aspects the Meat King had would overwhelm his shard advantage.

It was also essentially impossible to understand exactly where Zane's body was in camp, as much as she'd tried. They simply didn't have the equipment or the personnel for the task.

By his reaction she felt like she'd expected this result more than he did; she had to smile slightly at that.

She knew him well enough to know. Sometimes he just didn't know what his body was capable of.

Her avatar's eyes dazed over for just a second as she switched the bulk of her attention to her main body, to give the orders—"Sage Noughtfire, let's get the net set up in Sector XVI? Yes—now. We’ll be ahead of schedule.”

Her eyes snapped back to focus, and she took a quick breath.

She knew there was little she could do at this point in this fight. She cast a quick short-term shield Blessing, refreshing the Skill that blunted Cromm's life-draining abilities, but she knew it likely wouldn't make a difference. The result was out of her hands.

It was a role she couldn't stand at times—this semi-passive spectating, even if this was only a side duty for her. She found it especially difficult at times she saw him under pressure.

This wasn't shaping up to be difficult at all... although she didn't let herself celebrate just yet.

She saw him slip a stumbling claw of a fist, and then paste Cromm with a one-five-six combination that almost sent it reeling to its knees. Then step swiftly behind it, letting it take its allies' attacks once again; he caught two beams on his chains, dispersing them without trouble, before rounding on Cromm again...

He'd lost all respect for his opponent.

And as he stepped up, continuing an assault that delivered generational amounts of damage to the Meat King's supposedly invulnerable Supreme-body, it was clear that everyone on that battlefield knew it.
