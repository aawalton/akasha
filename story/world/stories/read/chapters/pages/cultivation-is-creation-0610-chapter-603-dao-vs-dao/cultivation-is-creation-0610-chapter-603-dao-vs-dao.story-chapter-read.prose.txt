Now that the power of a dao had been activated, I stayed back because it was better to see how this Peak Lucid Lawbearer used his dao in battle rather than rushing forward to my death, even if I could come back to life, it didn’t mean I wanted to experience dying.

Not everyone had the same worry as me, Wu Kangming’s sword came out of its sheath in one smooth motion, and he closed the distance to Yuan Shi in three steps to dissect him in two from crown to groin.

“Good form,” Yuan Shi murmured, shifting his weight slightly to allow the sword pass by him. “But your grip tightens during the follow through and that creates an opening.”

As if to demonstrate that, his hand came up and tapped Wu Kangming's wrist, causing the sword cultivator to stumble backward. I could see Wu Kangming’s knuckles turning white from trying to stop his sword from falling out of his grasp.

Taking advantage of the distraction, Xie Ling attacked from the opposite side by forming ice around her hands and shooting it forward in dozens of razor-sharp projectiles that were faster than bullets.

The ice pierced through Yuan Shi from twelve different angles simultaneously. His left shoulder sprouted three frozen spikes, two more went through his right leg, and I counted seven direct hits to his torso.

"Your ice cultivation has solid foundations, but you're treating the technique as pure offense," he said, looking down at the spike embedded in his chest. “Ice can also represent stillness, patience, the moment before movement. You're missing half the dao, little girl."

As he finished saying that, the ice dissolved back into spiritual energy and flowed into Yuan Shi's body where his injuries were, until a fraction of a second later, he was standing there completely unharmed.

This only seemed to frustrate the ice beauty because her eyes narrowed and she created an ice formation that acted as a cage that grew around Yuan Shi from multiple directions simultaneously.

"Better!” Yuan Shi exclaimed, examining the prison. “You're thinking in three dimensions now instead of just forward vectors. But I should point out that you’re still working within conventional parameters."

He placed both palms against the ice, and with a pulse of his spiritual pressure pulsed, the  cage shattered as if time had reversed to before the ice had formed, when it was water, and when it was simply water vapour, until there was nothing left.

"The Dao of Beginning allows me to reset things to their initial state. Your ice returns to water, which returns to spiritual essence, which returns to nothing."

Before he could continue what he was saying, golden threads of spiritual energy spread across the ground in geometric patterns. This was a formation. It turned out that Li Na hadn’t just been standing there watching the battle with her mouth open.

"Ah, a compound formation," Yuan Shi smiled, standing in the centre of the array. "Clever use of offensive and supportive form—"

He was cut off when golden light erupted upward in columns that would incinerate anything caught inside. Even thirty metres away, I could feel the sheer power of the formation. Honestly, I hadn’t known that Li Na had something like this in her pocket.

However, when the light faded, the smirk on the girl’s face was wiped clean when she saw Yuan Shi standing unharmed in the center of a scorched circle without even a speck of dust on his robes.

"The formation work was excellent, and I can tell that your understanding of spiritual geometry is quite advanced," he praised. "But formations require time to build and activate. Against an enemy who can manipulate beginnings and endings, that delay is fatal."

With a gesture of his hand, the golden threads pulled back into Li Na's spiritual energy reserves, and all the power she'd expended returned to her. I actually had to blink to confirm that I was seeing this right and that it wasn’t some illusion. I had never seen an enemy do something like that in the middle of a battle.

"There you go," Yuan Shi said. "I've reset the formation to before you cast it. You're welcome to try again with different parameters."

Before Li Na could come to terms with what had just happened, Wu Kangming appeared in front of Yuan Shi once again, but this time, instead of powerful strikes that sought to destroy, he used quick precise cuts aimed at joints and vital points.

A second later, Yuan Shi's robes accumulated dozens of small tears as the blade found its marks, causing blood to appear from his arms, legs, and sides. This type of injury would disable any normal cultivator, but Yuan Shi’s laugh reminded us that he wasn’t normal.

"Much better," he said even as the blood flowed back into his wounds and the skin knit itself back together. "You adapted your technique mid-combat. That's the mark of true skill. But to wound me, you must damage me faster than I can reset."

Wu Kangming, Xie Ling, and Li Na exchanged glances. They were reassessing their approach. Yuan Shi was treating this life and death battle like a classroom demonstration. Every technique they used became a teaching moment. Every attack was an opportunity for him to explain what they'd done wrong.

That was when I saw it. A pattern in how he moved. Yuan Shi always turned to face whoever attacked him directly. His defensive techniques activated in response to incoming spiritual pressure. He was reactive rather than proactive.

I activated Blink Step.

The world compressed around me and I materialized directly behind Yuan Shi. My right arm was already transforming as I appeared. Red sun energy flooded through my meridians and my flesh reshaped itself into that monstrous thorned appendage.

I drove the transformed arm forward with everything I had. The thorned vines punched through Yuan Shi's back and emerged from his chest. The red sun energy began devouring his dream qi immediately. I could feel it consuming his spiritual essence and converting it into more red sun energy.

Yuan Shi's spiritual pressure flickered, and his form became slightly less solid.

The red sun energy was working, but for some strange reason Yuan Shi didn't scream or panic.

"Fascinating," he said, turning his head to look at me over his shoulder. "This energy is consuming my spiritual foundation directly. No…it’s…it’s actually devouring the dream qi itself."

He reached back and his hand closed around the thorned vines of my transformed arm without any apparent concern for the damage they were doing to his palm.

"What is this energy?" Yuan Shi asked. "It doesn’t feel at all like dream qi. A higher tier energy perhaps? Yes, that makes sense. It treats my dream qi the way a predator treats prey."

My arm was still embedded in his chest and the red sun energy continued devouring his spiritual essence, but Yuan Shi just continued examining my transformation.

"The structure is remarkable," Yuan Shi continued. "You've combined plant-based techniques with this higher energy source. The vines provide physical form while the energy provides the consuming effect. Very innovative."

His spiritual pressure surged. Dream qi flooded through his body in quantities that shouldn't have been possible. The red sun energy devoured it but more kept coming. Yuan Shi was pouring spiritual essence into himself faster than my arm could consume it.

His hand tightened on my transformed limb. Spiritual energy condensed around his fingers and then exploded outward. The blast sent me flying backward. I tumbled across the black stone and came to a stop twenty meters away.

My arm was back to normal. The transformation had been forcibly canceled. With a grit of my teeth, I ignored the pain that shot through my meridians from the backlash.

Yuan Shi stood in the center of the step. The hole in his chest closed. Even the dream qi that my red sun energy had devoured was somehow being restored. I watched his spiritual pressure return to its previous level within seconds.

I frowned. I'd hoped the superiority of red sun energy over dream qi would allow me to permanently damage his spiritual foundation, but it seemed I was right about Daos transcending energy sources. The Dao of Beginning allowed him to reset even damage caused by higher tier energies.

"Create an opening for me," Wu Kangming said quietly as he landed beside me. "I can end this in one strike."

"Only a dao can defeat another dao."

Wu Kangming didn't reply, instead he just stared at Yuan Shi.

"Alright," I said, deciding to trust him since I knew that he had never made a claim that he hadn’t been confident in fulfilling.

I pushed myself to my feet. My spiritual reserves were still mostly full. The red sun energy consumption had been minimal since Yuan Shi ended it quickly. But I needed to create an opening that would last long enough for Wu Kangming to attack. That meant I needed more power than dream qi could provide.

I channeled energy through my meridians, not red sun energy this time, but pure spiritual energy from the cultivation world.

My dream qi responded to the qi like a loyal subject receiving orders from its king. The two energies merged. My spiritual pressure expanded outward and reached a level similar to a Lucid Lawbearer's. The black stone beneath my feet cracked from the intensity.

"You're full of surprises, aren’t you?” Yuan Shi's eyes widened slightly.

I didn't bother responding. I activated Blink Step and closed the distance instantly. My fist came up toward Yuan Shi's jaw. He blocked with his forearm. The impact released a shockwave that shattered stone in a ten-meter radius.

We engaged in hand-to-hand combat. Yuan Shi's technique was clearly superior. Every block was perfectly positioned. Every counter-attack targeted my weakest points. He had tens of thousands of years of martial arts experience and it showed.

But I had qi backing my dream qi. My strikes carried more force than he expected. When his block should have deflected my punch, it broke through anyway. When his counter should have disabled my arm, I powered through it.

Ice spears appeared from my right side. Xie Ling was supporting the attack. Yuan Shi had to commit his spiritual pressure to defending against the projectiles. That left him vulnerable for a fraction of a second.

I took advantage immediately. My fist connected with his ribs, and I felt something crack.

Golden formations appeared beneath Yuan Shi's feet thanks to Li Na. The arrays activated and chains erupted upward. They wrapped around Yuan Shi's legs so that he couldn't move, giving me the chance to press the advantage.

My right arm began transforming again, but this time instead of creating a monstrous appendage, I shaped the red sun energy into precise vines that wrapped around Yuan Shi's torso in a specific pattern.

The Qi Disruption Array. I'd designed it to disrupt qi circulation but I was confident it would work on dream qi as well since both were spiritual energies that flowed through meridians and pathways. The fundamental principle was the same.

The vines completed the array formation and I activated it.

Yuan Shi's spiritual pressure destabilized immediately. His dream qi began fragmenting. I felt his attempts to gather energy for techniques fail as the array disrupted the flow before it could complete.

His Laws weren't forming. The Dao of Beginning required energy to manifest in reality. Without it, he couldn't invoke his dao principles. Yuan Shi frowned, and for the first time in the entire fight, he looked genuinely concerned. He tried to break the array, but it held firm, in fact they actually tightened.

"Clever," Yuan Shi said. "You've created a suppression formation using an energy source I can't simply overwrite with my dao. The higher tier energy prevents me from resetting it back to its beginning state."

That's when Wu Kangming appeared beside Yuan Shi with his sword already mid-swing.

But this wasn't a normal sword technique. That I could tell immediately. The blade cut through the air and reality itself seemed to part around it. The spiritual energy surrounding the sword wasn't dream qi. It wasn't even normal cultivation world qi.

This was something else, something that transcended energy types entirely.

"Impossible," Yuan Shi’s eyes widened. "That's the Dao of—"

Wu Kangming's sword completed its arc to pass through Yuan Shi's torso horizontally. The cut was so clean that Yuan Shi's body didn't even seem to realize it had been severed. Then the top half of his body slid sideways and fell, hitting the ground with a heavy thump. As for the bottom half, it remained standing for a moment before collapsing.

"A dao strike," Yuan Shi whispered, looking up at Wu Kangming with an expression of wonder. "You're barely past Oneiric Sovereign and you've already touched a dao principle. How remarkable."

His body began dissolving until he was gone completely, the number floating in the sky changed from 10 to 9, and like that, step ninety-one was complete.
