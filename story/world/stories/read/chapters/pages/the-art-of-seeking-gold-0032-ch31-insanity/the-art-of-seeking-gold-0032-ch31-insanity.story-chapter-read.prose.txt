Amy could hear someone breathing. Blood was dripping onto the floor. She waited for another creature to come through the wall, but nothing did.

Then Libris fell.

She looked down at it.

The gold was fading, leaving her able to see what had happened to the cover.

It was black. There were cracks all over the book, and smoke was coming from its binding. Amy could smell the burnt paper from where she stood.

"Libris?" Amy whispered. She reached for the book with trembling hands.

There was no response.

Her breath stopped, and her tears be—

"It's still there," a voice said, but it didn't come from the book. It came from... everywhere. "Barely, but there."

Amy looked around in confusion and growing alarm. The light looked different. So did the others.

Crow was kneeling with his hand against the stump of his arm.

Blood hung below it without falling.

Iris had stopped halfway through turning toward Amy. There was dust in the air that didn't move either. It was like someone had paused a video, and Amy was the only one still in it.

She knew this. She'd been somewhere like this before. Right when everything began.

"Hello again, little mistake."

It was then that she realized the Goddess was sitting beside her, having appeared out of nowhere, or perhaps she had always been there, and Amy simply hadn't been allowed to see her until now.

She was just like the first time, skin pale as snow, long white dress, eyes shaped like dots depicting galaxies, and hair flowing like liquid gold. Just as she remembered her.

Her hand reached down to pat Amy's cheek, cleaning out her tears. The touch was almost motherly. It made Amy's skin crawl.

"Well," the Goddess said, examining the water on her hand. "Have you learned your lesson?"
