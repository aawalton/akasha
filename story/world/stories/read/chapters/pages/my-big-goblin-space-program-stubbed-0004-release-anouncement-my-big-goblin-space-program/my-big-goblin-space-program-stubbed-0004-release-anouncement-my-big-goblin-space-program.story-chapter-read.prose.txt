Hey everyone! I'm back from Denver, and today is the release date for My Big Goblin Space Program book 3!

If you followed it here on Royal Road, you can now get polished ebooks, paperbacks, or fully-narrated audio editions to complete the trilogy! If you're seeing this anouncement then you're probably already someone who followed the story. If you are unfamiliar, this is a kingdom/civilization building story about an astronaut reincarnated into the body of a goblin king who uses his tribe to speed-run from the stone age to the space age in order to reach the moon of his new fantasy planet.

This series has a lot packed into it. It's got primitive survival and technology, Mad Max convoys, tons of fun characters, goblin jet dogfighting, kaiju hunting, and more. It's definitely worth your time, and Mary Cruz did a lot of awesome work on all three book covers, so they'll look great on your bookshelf once you're done re-reading them.

In the electrifying conclusion to this zany isekai adventure, it’s boom or bust—and the only way out . . . is up!After some very bad luck on Earth took his legs and then his life, things finally began to turn around for astronaut Chris when he was reborn as a goblin king on a system-controlled planet. And perhaps most shocking of all, he discovers that some of his longtime frenemies are far more like him than he ever realized. But none of that is about to stop him from his dream of walking on the moon.
