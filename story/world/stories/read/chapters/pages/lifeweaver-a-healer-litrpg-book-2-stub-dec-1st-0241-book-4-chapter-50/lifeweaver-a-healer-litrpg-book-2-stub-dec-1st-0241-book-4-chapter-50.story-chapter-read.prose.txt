A state of exhaustion seemed like a constant at that point. Challenges kept increasing in difficulty as Luke grew stronger, which meant he could never get into any sort of flow where he breezed past whatever stood in his way. On the one hand, that was just what he needed to keep growing, but on the other, it was a pain in the ass.

Pushing away all thoughts, Luke settled into the comfortable state of meditation. All sensations and chaotic thoughts bled away into a soothing river of nothingness. Mana regeneration picked up as his mind relaxed. Focus and willpower returned to him, the intangible resources he needed for what was coming next. The weave was there, everywhere and all around him. All he needed to do was project his consciousness into it, and off he would go. Even with the many incursions by the void happening around the world, Luke took another couple of seconds for himself, bathing in that cool, soothing stream of non-thinking and non-feeling, before touching a system thread.

The moment he did, Luke found the most dire situation and moved there with the speed of thought. As he gathered his will to repel the beings who looked a lot like The Deep Dweller's smaller siblings, he felt others out there. He was not alone in this task. While it took a lot out of him to push the void critters back and repair the weave, it wasn't anything he hadn't done before, and the opponents acted as expected and retreated when they realized there was no way for them to best Luke in a contest of wills. The weave had been weakened in that spot, and it took him a little longer to put it back together, or at least near to its original state. Each time he handled a thread, he got flashes of what it represented, or who. He saw an old man in a hospital room lit with candles in one thread, and a tennis ball in the other. The two had no relation and didn't intersect. Two other threads did. Touching them while bracing for the internal damage it brought, Luke saw a woman in her early fifties with shoulder-length gray hair streaked with white. The other thread, the one touching the first one, showed a man about Luke's age who was out at sea, standing high up on what looked like a shipping vessel, a sea of containers on the deck below. From that brief glimpse, Luke couldn't tell how the two were related, but he knew there was some connection between them. There was much to learn in studying the weave, but this was not the time, as was so often the case.

Moving on to the next imminent breach, Luke found the end thread closing on its own, reverting to its original state, while the void critters looked on, their many eyes expressive enough to show they were stunned by the new developments.

Even after searching a good while, Luke couldn't perceive another consciousness present, and he sure as hell didn't see one. Who was doing that?

Not that it was a simple concept to grasp in the weave.

Dimitrios was strong. Not just in fighting ability, but in wielding his own version of the weave as a weapon, adding its power to his own.
