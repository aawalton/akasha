Zaiden was right, this was a bad idea! But I was scared, no, downright petrified! I’d prolonged harmonizing with my darkness for this very reason. I didn’t want to lose myself to it. The first time I tried, I felt like I was drowning. And this was my own darkness! I understood what Wilna said about balance, everyone carrying both light and dark energy. My light energy was what made me, me. I wasn’t a bad person, no matter how much faith my codex had in me for being one. A necromancer in a saint’s body—okay, I was over-exaggerating the saint part, but that’s what this situation felt like!

Now I was expected to control darkness from another source—a dungeon core. I was already containing it, courtesy to my infernal bloodline, but it was up to me to push it that extra mile.

Zaiden had so much faith in me, and I couldn’t understand why. I get that I saved him from death, but this was a different challenge entirely.

If I didn’t use Zaiden as a conduit, would the Prince of Darkness consume me? Did I have the strength to fight him back? I managed to keep him away, but as Theron lowered toward the floor of my domain, I could feel the pressure of his aura invading me. The ceiling of my resistance was being crushed, and soon, this dungeon would eradicate my entire party, including me.

I had confidence in letting the bastard in here—I needed to keep that same confidence in getting rid of him, too….

“Freya, we are running out of time!” Zaiden cried. “Conquer his essence as your own! Do it like you would a thrall. Come on!”

Prince Theron’s fury whipped biting winds the closer he descended on us. I grounded myself and squinted my eyes, focusing on Zaiden as darkness swirled around us like a living storm. His hair whipped wildly, but his eyes remained steady, unwavering in their trust in me.

‘Hey,’ he said inwardly. ‘You got this. You saved me, Freya. You can save your friends, too.’

Okay, conquer the prince. Use Zaiden as a conduit to buffer the impact. He was a naturally affirmed fae, who somehow, knew that he could withstand the process. I feared for his life more than I feared for my own, but if Zaiden had this much trust in me, the least I could do is trust him back.

I felt the warmth of his life force beneath his palm on my head. The connection between us as thrall and master sparked to life, but this time I didn’t just feel his presence—I opened myself to it completely.

After I opened up myself to him, I triggered [Conquer] and targeted the enormous mass of shadows in the ceiling. As expected, my willpower stood no chance against him. The pushback from Prince Theron was immediate and brutal, sending shockwaves through my entire body. But instead of letting it tear me apart, I channeled it through Zaiden.

The moment the dark energy hit him, his entire body convulsed. His cool-blue eyes rolled back, showing only white, and shadows began pouring into his mouth and nose like smoke. I was tempted to break the link, but I forced myself to remain calm. He understood the risks, he knew what he was getting himself into. If I stopped the process, I’d be going against his request.

Despite the conversion looking extremely painful, Zaiden didn’t scream—instead, he gritted his teeth and held firm. I felt the darkness flowing through him, somehow becoming less toxic, more manageable. Whatever was happening inside Zaiden was filtering out the raw malevolence of Theron’s essence, leaving behind a form of shadow energy that I could actually handle.

But the cost was written across Zaiden’s face—veins of black spread beneath his skin like poisonous ivy, and his breathing became labored gasps. Still, he held on.

‘Don’t you dare let go of me,’ he managed to project, his mental voice strained but determined.

Through our connection, I felt Prince Theron’s resistance crumbling. What had been chaotic and consuming became focused, a tool rather than a tormentor. I pulled it into myself, feeling it slide through every part of me—mind, body, and spirit.

“That's it,” Zaiden shouted, his voice barely audible through the storm of energy surrounding us. “Take it all, Freya!”

I could feel Theron’s consciousness fighting against me, like a wild animal thrashing in a net. But with each pulse of energy that passed through Zaiden, the Prince’s resistance weakened. The cathedral around us began to shift, the stone pillars glowing with veins of silver light that cut through the darkness. My thralls’ cages started dissolving, the thought of actually harmonizing with this dungeon core’s darkness making my chest skip a beat. What started off as a dragon’s natural skill turned out to be a catalyst to something more, something that had the potential to make me stronger….

Through Zaiden, I could feel Theron’s memories bleeding into mine—centuries of conquest, the souls he’d devoured, the power he’d accumulated. But instead of overwhelming me, they felt distant, like watching someone else’s nightmares through frosted glass. The buffer was working, and soon, I could see the surface of the once dark ceiling of my domain.

I’d consumed Prince Theron’s essence, using my thrall as his vessel….

The last wisp of shadow energy flowed through our connection before the cathedral fell silent. The oppressive weight that had been crushing down on us lifted, and I could finally breathe again.

Zaiden collapsed to his knees, those black veins still pulsing beneath his skin, but his eyes—thank goodness—were his own again. Not only that, his amputated arm was back….

‘Zaiden?’ I called out softly, confused as to how he got his arm back. Not that I was disappointed or anything! He looked down at it confused as well, his ragged breaths mellowing out once he realized a miracle had happened.

“Unreal…” he muttered to himself, and then I felt my thralls, Dusty and Weedrill taking to my sides.

‘Zaiden, are you okay?’ I finished asking, but before he could respond, I felt something. I could sense them. The souls he’d recently contained….

I imagined the other helpless souls he’d abducted were now part of the dungeon core, but not my friends. They were still there, trapped in my domain, with their bodies suspended in limbo.

‘Lun... Nolan... Typha...’ I whispered. Their life forces, I felt them around me. They were in here somewhere and I needed to find them! ‘Zaiden, I can feel them! They are here!’ I cheered, overlooking the fact that I just might not have been talking to Zaiden after all. In fact, I didn’t get a prompt saying we’d defeated the dungeon….

All of a sudden Dusty began to growl at Zaiden as he stood up, flexing his new arm. That same arm began to emit a familiar aura, the veins beneath his skin pulsing with shadows that shouldn't have been there. My heart sank as I realized what was happening.

“Interesting,” he said, his voice carrying an echo that hadn’t been there before. “This vessel is... adequate.”

‘Zaiden?’ I asked, taking another step back.

A smile spread across his face. “Zaiden is here, dragon. Just... not alone anymore.”
