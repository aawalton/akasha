Ronan had the bow in hand as he approached the falling ship. It smashed into a building at the edge of the empty area that had housed the Governor’s tower. Ronan heard yells and shouts from a few nearby Grathen, wondering what was going on. He ignored them and focused on the downed ship, watching for any signs of movement.

The moment he saw an arm appearing out of the wreckage he let his first arrow fly. It pierced through the charred arm and then exploded, blowing the Governor’s hand off. It flopped onto the ground near Ronan, but he only gave it a brief dismissive glance before returning his focus to the downed ship.

He hadn’t yet received the kill notification and that meant his opponent was still alive, somehow. Ronan let another arrow fly right into the ship, not caring about the collateral damage as it blew up.

A loud screech sounded out after the explosion, followed by another blast that was accompanied by a flash of white light. Whatever the arrow hit must have been important, yet there was still no kill notification.

Ronan watched as a mutilated and burnt figure clambered out of the burning wreckage. It wasn’t even recognisable as a ship anymore, and neither was the governor recognisable as one of the Agrathen. It was pitiful.

Ronan raised his bow and fired one last shot. It pierced right through the alien’s head and left the other side. He hadn’t even bothered to put an explosion enchantment on it, but it hadn’t been necessary.

By defeating the governor he had conquered the planet and earned himself another upgrade to his nobility title. This one had some cool benefits, including the first ‘tangible’ buff. He thought about when he’d claimed the pillar back on his home planet and how he’d been attacked almost immediately. With this stat boost, wiping out the attackers would be even easier.

Not to mention it would make taking out other pillars easy, given that the other knights on New Earth wouldn’t be able to hold a candle to him. It was unfair, but that was just how his heritage worked.

He had been about to open up his status and distribute his stat points when he heard another chime accompanying the appearance of another notification. Before he could even read it, the world around him shifted and he felt himself being pulled out of real space.

In the blink of an eye Ronan had been stolen from the planet and appeared back in front of a familiar fireplace. He was sitting in a comfy chair in the waiting room of the gladiator staging platform.

Well, that was annoying. I wanted to say goodbye to the raptor… While he had wanted to explore the planet a little more, there was nothing stopping him from doing so in a future iteration. He wasn’t going to get mad about what he couldn’t control. Instead he started to read, seeing that he was alone in the room.

Apparently he would not only be able to meet his sponsor once again, but receive even more gifts from here. At the very least that might make up for the lacking rewards he’d earned from the actual path.

Then again, he had no idea what a gladiator shard was or how good the gladiator market might be. And he had to admit that a legendary weapon would be an incredible boon for any ordinary system-user.
