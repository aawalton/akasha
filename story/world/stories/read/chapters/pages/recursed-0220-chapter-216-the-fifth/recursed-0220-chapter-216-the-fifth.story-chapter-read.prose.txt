There it was. Despite all she’d improved, changed, the metaphor was fundamentally the same.

Whatever The Soulchasm had meant by calling her a Seed, her own evolution options confirmed it. So long as she evolved all of her names together — which seemed like a horrible idea, considering both the temporary weakness when she’d gained O̅s̫stho̲th and Yog’s confounding ability to infest names — the result would be… whatever the Eidolons had planned for her.

Clearly, they had more in mind for her than the simple murder of the cults. Why would she ever think beings that thought in such higher planes would ever have a single motive. For all she knew, they had a million or more objectives for sending her back.

But what did it mean to be a Seed?

The title itself was hardly descriptive, except for the obvious fact that she was meant to grow into something else. What? Was it right to assume that this ‘Seed’ was directly related to her being the perfect sacrifice? Or were all people with cursed mutations one of the Eidolons’ Seeds? There was no way to pin it on her nature, either. As she hadn’t seen any evolution options before she’d been sent back in time, it might have been when she fell into the black hole that this ‘Seed’ was woven into her soul.

Was it a good thing, or bad?

As much as she’d love to think this was a representation of the Eidolons’ will to cultivate her growth and overcome the cults… it could just as easily be a parasite. Nyxala gulped at the horrific thought that some unimaginable plant sprouted from her chest. From her soul. It was entirely possible that the Eidolons’ Seed was not referring to her, but something implanted. Was she nothing more than a host?

The thought was enough to scare her off evolving all her names together, beyond the questionable nature of doing so in the first place. As much as it was only one possibility amongst dozens, she couldn’t take the risk.

Besides, considering the progress of the description — from ‘not yet sprouting’ to ‘ready to sprout’ and now ‘sprouting’ — Nyxala knew it wasn’t something that would disappear just because she willed it. Whatever this Seed actually was, she was stuck with it for many evolutions to come. Plenty of time to figure out if it was worth pursuing… or a parasite, after all.

Nyxala forced her gaze away from the ghostly possibility of all her names combined into one. She wouldn’t consider it. Though, there were still other combinations she needed to consider before evolving.

O̅s̫stho̲th, Lýo̻tepͦ, and Ts̟͂tll̞ were all names she didn’t want to touch. Both Feats were fated to be joined, but the time still wasn’t right. Nyxala didn’t want either to be altered too much before then. Even if she’d not really had much use for Ts̟͂tll̞ since her fight against Lysyra. The ability to lock someone in a fight to the death was incredibly beneficial, if situational, and not something she wanted to lose.

That seemed to be a sticking point about higher evolutions: was she willing to risk names that were already good for a — likely stronger — ability of unknown use?

Yog’s name — or the one it infested — Nyxala could probably combine, but it was just going to bleed through whatever else she mixed it with. Looking upon its possibilities anyway, she was surprised to find Ỷog¦­Eipi̬nỷ was no longer Ỷog¦­Eipi̬nỷ.

Nyxala wondered how an onomastician might decipher that.

As a reward for helping her against The Soulchasm, Nyxala would toss Yog one of her new additives as sacrifice. Otherwise, she would let it evolve alone.

Though, that might not be the worst outcome. The ability to sever her limbs at will, and her taboo butcher name, were not exactly at the top of her priority to keep in their current forms. Au̲cme especially. No matter how delicious they were.

Undecided, she simply decided to check the evolution descriptions for each combination.
