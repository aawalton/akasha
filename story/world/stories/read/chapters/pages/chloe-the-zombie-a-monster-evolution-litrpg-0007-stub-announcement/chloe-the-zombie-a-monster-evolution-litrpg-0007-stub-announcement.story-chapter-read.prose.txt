Hello! If you're reading this, that means that you've reached the end of the Book 1 chapters available on Royal Road. Or, in other words, this series is stubbed. The reason that the rest isn't here is that Amazon's Kindle Unlimited requires exclusivity, so no more than 10% of the story can be available anywhere else. Those first 6 chapters aren't quite 10%, but I wasn't going to mess around and try to toe the line.

If you want to read the rest of book 1 of Chloe the Zombie, it is available now on Amazon. If you have a Kindle Unlimited subscription, you can read it for free. Here is a link to where you can find it: https://mybook.to/ChloetheZombie

The audiobook is produced by Podium Entertainment, and is narrated by the incredibly talented Ash Aarrestad! I think she did a great job at bringing Chloe to life, and if you're interested in hearing Chloe's voice, I highly recommend checking out the audiobook! (It's currently just over 18 hours of content!)
