—Inside the forest—

Dawn was running leisurely inside the forest through the undergrowth.

She was happy that her kittens and the baby creature were okay in the end. She never interacted with humans this much. The humans are kind and helpful, especially the tall one. She was the one who followed her inside the forest and healed her babies.

Dawn started liking her benefactors. She didn’t mind being touched by those humans.

While thinking all these, she was running without stopping. She needed to stay with her babies as much as possible. This was the most crucial time for them. Four of them started standing. Their motor skills have not developed properly, and they mostly wobbled. They were having a hard time standing for long.

The kittens didn’t move much from their small ditch, which was inside their den. Only stood there and ventured out of the small ditch for a few steps and went back in. Only the biggest one was able to venture into the corners of the den. But even that one was still wobbling and didn’t try to walk out of the den entirely.

While running through the undergrowth, she avoided any animals’ territories. And even if she entered, she avoided going far into others' territories. She learned her lesson. She didn't want to face the strong predators like the rats that looked weak from the outside but were actually strong from inside.

She understood this perfectly. Her trauma from the dog’s fight didn’t even die down, but now the rat family really scared her. If the rat hadn’t died, she would have surely changed her den. So she was not worried much, for now.

When she reached near the huge tree, she noticed something weird for a second.

She was approaching from the side, so there was no way to get a proper view inside. She could only look at the root. But what she saw was clear enough. Anyone would think that it was a hallucination.

A blue light was coming out from the opening.

Just as she was in the visual range for seeing her den from afar, the light disappeared. It was only momentarily, but the blue light was surely there for half a second at most.

She immediately ran towards her den, thinking that her babies had been attacked.

The moment she entered her den, everything was normal. There was nothing wrong. The babies were mewling as usual. She started sniffing everywhere in her den, but still there was nothing.

The moment of glowing blue light made her anxious. She was thinking about whether she should change her den or not. But there was nothing wrong with the surroundings. Her instincts were telling her something was going on here, which she didn’t understand.

She went to the ditch and started licking every kitten to settle them. All her kittens were mewling, but the baby creature was not mewling as much as before.

She then sat down with her kitten so that they could have their meal.

While feeding her babies and the new creature, she suddenly started feeling exhausted. She had just eaten roasted meat, but still, she was suddenly getting exhausted for some reason. She didn't think much of it and just went to sleep in the ditch where she usually sleeps.

She couldn't fight the exhaustion in the end.

What she didn't know was that her adopted baby was the cause of the massive energy drain that made her sleep. The baby was doing something unusual without even knowing while drinking milk.

After a while, Dawn’s ear twitched. She opened her eyes and started looking towards the den’s opening. She sensed something was coming towards her den just like before. She was still feeling exhausted, but it was not enough to hinder her from protecting her den.

The fight from yesterday evening taught her many things. Firstly closed space was not good for her fighting style, and secondly, her den might be disadvantageous because her baby could get hit in crossfire or any kind of accident.

So, she stood up and went out of her den and stood just in front of the opening, just in case the entity that was coming closer through the undergrowth of the forest was dangerous.

On one side, the rustling sound of movement through the undergrowth was getting closer and closer. Then a rat with red eyes appeared. This rat was a bit smaller than the previous one, that attacked the black cat’s den the previous day.

This rat was the partner of the previous rat that died. This was the mother rat whose baby was killed by the cat.

It seems like the mother rat came to look for its partner while sniffing. It took her a lot of time to find her way here. A night had passed, but the father rat had not returned. This never happened before, so she had to look for her partner. It was important.

The Dawn didn’t know what to do… She was tired for some unknown reason, and she felt lethargic. But she had to take action now that the partner had appeared.

The rat was glaring at the cat.

The dry blood stain was clear on the ground. The smell of blood was all over the place. The Rat was now sure that its partner had passed away while battling. It was frustrating.

Not only did the cat kill its youngest pup, but it also took down her partner. This was over the limit she could handle. She could not tolerate it anymore. Now it was pay time for the cat. Sadness and pure rage occupied her mind.

The rat was glaring at the cat while squeaking as loudly as possible. Her eyes were glowing very brightly.

The red glow was even brighter than before. Although she was in a berserk state, but was in total control. She was ready to launch a charge attack.

But Dawn didn’t wait for the rat mother to prepare. After knowing the attack pattern, she decided to cover the den from front with her body and attack before the rat starts charging silently.

The black cat knew that once the rat started charging, it would become impossible to intercept it from her previous experience.

Dawn launched herself towards the rat. She didn't attack in a straight line. Instead, she moved forward in an arc so that the rat couldn't charge straight.

As soon as she reached the rat, she attempted to bite its neck straight. The rat also knew the cat's attack pattern.

Seeing Dawn moving with an arc, the rat jumped up a bit, and Dawn landed on the ground. Then the rat landed on Dawn and bit her neck from above.

Dawn never thought that the rat would use the same trick she used during their first encounter. The rat copied the cat’s attack pattern and learned the cat’s movement, unlike the big rat, which died last night while using brute force to the end.

Dawn mewed loudly in pain and started running and thrashing, but the rat was biting hard and didn’t let go of her back. She was now bleeding profusely. Her pain was immense.

When she couldn't remove the rat even after thrashing for a few seconds, suddenly her vision landed on her den’s opening. An idea formulated in her head.

She started running towards her den opening. The opening was small, and she could enter and exit comfortably. But the opening was not tall enough to enter with someone on Dawn’s back.

As soon as she entered her den, the rat's face hit the tree roots hard and fell to the ground. The rat didn't even get the time to stabilize itself on the ground, and immediately saw the black cat moving towards her from inside.

Dawn’s instant strategy worked. As soon as she hit the rat using the momentum, she instantly turned around while flicking her body and lunged at the rat from inside while hissing. The rat was strong. So, she didn’t want to miss even a moment of opportunity.

She held the rat with her front paws and bit hard on the rat's neck. But instead of the smell of blood, she got an unexpected surprise.

The rat's body was so hard, and the skin was so tough that Dawn was only able to grab the neck, but she was NOT able to sink her teeth into the rat's skin.

Suddenly, the rat squeaked and started running towards a nearby tree while dragging Dawn, wanting to use Dawn’s similar strategy.

Dawn now knew that the rat mother was intelligent and learned things fast. It was not hard to understand that her opponent was a tough nut to crack.

While running, Dawn was being dragged in a weird way. She was holding the rat from both sides of the neck with her paws while still trying to sink her teeth, and her whole body was getting dragged on the ground like a bag.

Now there were two choices in front of Dawn. One was to leave her hold and try to get another chance, and the other way was to keep the hold and take the impact that the rat was surely going to make after.

Suddenly, she considered another option.

Dawn then waited for the rat to drag her near the front tree. Then, just before reaching the tree, she raised one paw while holding the rat with her teeth, and directly scratched deep into the rat’s left eye. Then she opened her grip from her mouth to get left behind before getting slammed.

Rat immediately started squeaking in pain while bleeding from her eyes and rammed into the tree trunk. There was not much space to stop and stabilize herself. Impact was hard. But the effect was not much since the rat’s skin and body were developed. But a direct scratch to the eye was very costly and painful. Even after having so much pain, the rat jumped up with her intuition.

It was a correct decision.

Since Dawn couldn’t sink her teeth anyway, attacking the weak spot was the only option for her. After leaving the rat, Dawn rolled on the ground, stabilized herself, and launched an attack again, but suddenly the rat jumped up in the air. Dawn was not prepared for this. Her fast action without stopping put her in danger again. She was a predictable opponent to the rat.

The rat knew that the cat would surely come. She didn't need to look at the black cat.

The same thing happened again.

Dawn lunged to scratch the rat’s weak spot, but the rat knew what was going to happen, so the rat jumped. Dawn again found herself below the rat, and the rat was about to land on her back again, but Dawn rolled on the same spot and turned her face towards the sky, ready to scratch the rat in the air. Her last mistake taught her what to do next.

As soon as the rat landed on her belly. Dawn instinctively scratched the same injured eye again, to deal more damage. The rat stepped back upon dawn’s belly with a loud squeak, then again got scratched on the same spot.

The rat fell on the ground and started thrashing. Dawn lunged with a hiss. Hearing Dawn’s hiss, the rat mother started running without even looking in the direction where she was running and went inside the dense forest undergrowth and kept running without stopping.

Getting injured on the same eye was not good for her.

Seeing the rat running away in pain, Dawn didn't chase. She simply stood there and then limply fell right where she was standing. It was not because of the injury she sustained, but because of exhaustion.

She was already exhausted before, for some unknown reason, but now she was drained. She didn’t even have the energy to move anymore, and the pain behind her neck made her condition worse.

After a few minutes of rest, she started walking towards her den. Since it was not far away from her place. She decided to rest in her den for the time being. There was nothing she could do about the injury when she was exhausted like this. Otherwise, she could have reached the humans who give her food to help her heal.

She entered the den slowly and went to her babies, who were mewling. They were distressed because of the noise of the battle.

As soon as she sat down in the ditch, all the babies clung to her belly. She was about to sleep again, but she started having a world of pain in her chest.

To be specific, she was having pain in her heart. It was excruciating pain. She started mewing loudly. Her babies, seeing her, also started mewing while nudging their mother. Dawn, sensing her babies clinging to her belly, didn’t thrash around the ditch

She didn't want to hurt her babies again accidentally. She just started enduring without moving much and without mewing loudly in agony. She didn’t want to hurt her babies in any way.

It was chaos inside her den.

After a few minutes, the pain subsided. She was breathing heavily, and then she lost consciousness.
