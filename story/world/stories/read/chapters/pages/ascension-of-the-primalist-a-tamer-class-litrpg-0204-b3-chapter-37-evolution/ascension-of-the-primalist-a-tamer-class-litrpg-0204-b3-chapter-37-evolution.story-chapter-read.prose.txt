A sharp, instinctive urge to protect her.

But why?

Whatever the reason, the thought of her dying bothered him. Perhaps their pack was larger than he had originally believed.

And it is my responsibility to protect its members.

Nightmare forced aether to carve new, jagged grooves into his very being with a single goal in mind: kill all three enemies in a single strike.

Yet doing that alone was impossible. So he refused to be alone.

Aether slid violently through his flesh, driven by that desperate need to become more. Pain followed in its wake and seared across his body as he bared his fangs to the wind.

If he wanted to stand side-by-side with monsters like Seth and Colossus, then he had to shatter his own limits.

Just as the two men and the panther finally broke free from the immobilizing terror, Nightmare's body rippled like a reflection in a disturbed pond of black water.

Aether erupted in a silent wave of darkness, and two perfect copies of the direwolf formed at his sides, emerging from the gloom itself. Nightmare seized control of the constructs with his mind, and all three of them pounced.

What the hell? Seth thought.

They seemed to lack the original's raw attack power and the ability to cast complex spells, yet they still served as both distractions and lethal weapons.

The second duplicate wasn't as fortunate. The imperial soldier in front of it managed to drive his blade through its chest, and the construct dissolved into drifting wisps of shadow.

The chained energy felt less like a living creature and more like a storm imprisoned within a cage—thrashing endlessly against its restraints, waiting for the chance to erupt outward and annihilate everything in its path.

Ever since first sensing that power in the Desert of Misery, Seth hadn't figured out what it was.
