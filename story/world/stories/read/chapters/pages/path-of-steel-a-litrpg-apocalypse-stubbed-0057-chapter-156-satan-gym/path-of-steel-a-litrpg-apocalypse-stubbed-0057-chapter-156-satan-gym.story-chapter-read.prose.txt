A special gym emerged through the trees.

The Bayonet gym had been what Gary expected of boxers. Punching bags, mitts, oldschool instruments, along with a sweaty, intense feeling. Everywhere you looked, beef boys were loading plates onto bars.

Gary had assumed that was the case for all gyms. Only now did he realize how wrong he’d been.

The Satan Gym was super modern. It was a massive, multi-floored building which could have easily fit into any high-class neighborhood of Earth. Windows filled its sides. Through some of them, Gary could see beef boys in yoga pants running on treadmills and sipping from their thermos. Over the entrance hung a huge sign depicting a red, chiseled devil face winking at the viewer as he raised a satan shake.

The glass doors slid open to let them in. The room was air-conditioned, and gym music played from unseen speakers. A fit lady sat at the front desk. “Can I help you?” she asked with a huge smile.

“I’m here to see Satan Jr.,” Feng Shen said as the crowd after him poured into the lobby. They barely fit.

“Of course! Please go right in. I just have to inform you that we don’t offer free trials—anyone not wearing a champion belt must have an active registration to enter.”

“That’s fine,” Feng Shen said, walking up the stairs behind her. The receptionist walked out from behind her counter, revealing slender legs in pink yoga pants, and started handing everyone paper forms. Gary stared at his.

One-time subscription: 2 aura stones.

One-day subscription: 5 aura stones.

One-month subscription: 6 aura stones.

“We should obviously get the monthly subscription. Look how cheap it is!” Goldie said.

“It’s not cheap, Goldie. It’s three times the one-time fee.”

“But imagine how many times we could come in a month!”

“How many do you think we will actually come?” Gary asked calmly. A confused look appeared on her snake face, followed by an astonished one.

“Gary! I fell victim to marketing!”

Gary walked up to the front desk and left two stones on the counter. The lady stamped the number one on the back of his hand. “Please go in!” she chimed.

“Don’t tell Gary what to do, bi—” Gary blocked Goldie’s mouth before she could finish. “Thank you,” he muttered, rushing up the stairs. Inside, it was exactly as he expected, and at the same time, the exact opposite.

Beef boys of all genders and ages flexed on machines. Some had bulging muscles. Most were middling, while a few were downright chubby and sweaty. Trainers often hung over these last ones, encouraging them or explaining how to use the instrument. Pretty women walked between the instruments, offering Satan Shakes to anyone struggling—for a price.

Everyone wore headphones blasting music so loud it could be heard to the neighboring machines. There were no phones, at least.

Gary looked around, but try as he might, he didn’t spot a single plate. Every machine was state-of-the-art, sporting integrated weights which could be altered by the simple twist of a handle.

There were five rows of machines, all extending deep into the building. At the end of it, Gary saw Feng Shen going down the stairs. He immediately paced over.

“Hey there! Would you like to try a Satan Shake? It’s exactly what you need to make your work-out count!” a cheerful girl asked him, pushing a Satan Sprinting Shake in his face.

“Thank you, I’m good,” Gary said.

“Are you sure? Sprinting Shakes make you fit AND help our gym get the latest equipment!”

“I’m sure, thank you,” Gary said. He caught a few gym bros giving him dirty looks.

Near the stairs, a sign showed that the weight-lifting and callisthenics floor was above, while the martial arts floor was below. Gary headed down. Five doors waited at the bottom, seemingly identical. He picked one. The door opened to a long set of toilet stalls, empty if not for the couple of gym bros snorting cocaine on the sink. They must have caught his stupefaction because they glared at him.

“Bro, get out,” one of them said, and Gary closed the door.

“That wasn’t the bottom floor,” he said.

“Maybe try the one with the grunting sounds?” Goldie said.

Gary located a door visibly identical to the rest, but with heavy grunting sounds coming from behind it. It was ajar, too. He opened it to reveal a massive, high-ceiling space with spotlights shedding bright light everywhere. Two dozen burly men and women were spread on a huge mat just beyond the door, wrestling in pairs. Eight boxing rings adorned the room in two columns by the side walls, while the far side was covered by a wide office space. At the very center stood a massive, six-sided battle cage. In contrast to all of the rings, the cage was vacant.

Most of the monsters on this boss were pro boxers or pro wrestlers.

Pro Wrestler, Level 30

No matter what you do, don’t let this guy grab you. Or sit on you. Or approach you, in general. He’s a blob of meat with enough fat to cushion an inch of spear and enough muscles to overpower an elephant.

These guys come from the Kamowaga gym you lost, and it’s such a shame. It was my favorite gym. You should see them, rolling around in mud in their underwear like little piggies. It was delightful, really.

Feng Shen stood in front of the office door at the far back. His spear was raised, its butt resting proudly on the floor, and his robes fluttered to the tune of his aura. He was like a spear ready to strike.

Nothing happened for a while.

“Fuck me for rushing, I guess,” Gary muttered.

“Don’t say that near the shake ladies!” Goldie said.

“Ew, Goldie. That didn’t even make sense.”

“I saw how they looked at you. They’d pounce in a heartbeat.”

Gary shook his head. “One of these days, Goldie, we’ll have a long chat about what you can and cannot say.”

“I’m a snake of ruin. I say whatever the hell I want.”

More people flitted in, joining Gary in front of the wrestling mat. Whispers spread. The wrestlers paused, and at the end of their rounds, even the boxers nodded at each other and turned to watch.

The office door opened.

Satan Jr., Level 50 (Floor Boss)

Nothing junior about this guy—unless you know his father. Satan Jr. is the current ruler of the fourth floor, running his own high-end gym and a very profitable sports shake enterprise. Desiring to diverge from his father’s legacy, he opened the next worst thing—a company!

Attacking Satan Jr. incurs the automatic hostility of every monster on the floor. You may challenge him by acquiring all four championship belts, one for each gym, in which case he will happily fight you to the death and beyond. In the event of such challenge, his rank drops to Sector Boss, and the floor’s monsters do not become hostile. Defeating Satan Jr. yields a special reward, along with stakes to his Satan Shake corporation.

During this invasion quest, you may challenge Satan Jr. by possessing at least one belt instead of all of them.

Satan Jr. was a large, jacked businessman. He wore a very out-of-place three-piece suit, making him resemble an underworld boss instead of a gym owner, along with sunglasses and a white earpiece. Twin horns jutted out on either side of a black fedora.

“Hey there,” he said, clicking his earpiece. “Feng Shen, right?”

“I’m here to challenge you,” Feng Shen replied.

“Very brave of you. Before we fight, let’s have a word at my office.”

“Why?”

“Because I asked politely.” Satan Jr. smiled, revealing sharp canines. His voice was deep and devilish, exactly as Gary would imagine. Feng Shen considered the proposal.

“Fine,” he replied.

“After you,” Satan said. He turned to the side, letting Feng Shen walk right in front of him, then entered himself and shut the door.

It was waiting time again. Gary tried but couldn’t see anything through the tinted windows. Around him, rumors abounded. People claimed they were talking about a business opportunity, recruitment offers, even that Satan was begging for mercy or assassinating Feng Shen with the help of mercenaries. Hearing that, Gary didn’t know whether to laugh or cry.

Finally, the door swung open, and Feng Shen walked out thoughtfully.

“Pleasure doing business with you,” Satan called from inside, shutting the door again.

“Wait, that’s it? They’re not going to fight?” someone asked.

“I bet he threatened to kill Feng Shen’s family,” someone else said. The surrounded beef boys and pro boxers or wrestlers exchanged knowing looks and returned to training.

“Wait, wait, hold up,” Gary said, grabbing the shoulder of a beefy beef boy who glared at his hand. “I mean, excuse me,” Gary rephrased. “Has this happened again?”

“It happens every time. Mr. Satan has never fought,” the beef boy wrestler replied.

“Why?”

“How should I know? We only see challengers step into the office for a word and then leave. Now let me train,” the wrestler said, returning to his waiting partner.

Gary fell in thought.

“What do you think, Gary?” Goldie asked.

“I don’t know… Best guess? He offered something in place of fighting. I don’t know what, but Feng Shen wouldn’t cower to threats. The question is, why? Is he afraid?”

“Maybe he’s all bark no bite?” Goldie tried.

“Maybe, but he’s a level 50 floor boss. That’s real.”

“Hey, I don’t know, I’m just a snake.”

“Only when it suits you.”

“Exactly,” Goldie replied with an innocent smile.

Gary shook his head. He rose to the first floor and was about to head out of the gym when, out of curiosity, he walked into a chest fly machine—the kind where you grab two handles to the sides of your chest and try to bring them together. As he sat down and brought his fifty-five strength to fore, he sensed the machine give easily at first, then rapidly increase its resistance until it was challenging.

“Huh,” he said, but the machine was not done. As he finished the rep, he sensed his muscles tightening and relaxing on their own as if ran through by a mild electrical current. Even the aura inside them flexed. Gary slowly let the machine return to the starting position, only to realize that his muscles felt revitalized, worked out at the perfect level of intensity.

“Woah.”

He let go of the machine and looked around with wide eyes. Everyone, even the sweatiest beef boys, wore a look of pride at their progress. Of obsession.

“If you’re doing it, do it right! It’s only two hundred stones,” a smiling woman said, offering a Lifting Satan Shake.

Gary really considered getting one. It would feel amazing with this work-out, and he was thirsty. Going the last two hours without it would be kind of dry.

Wait, what am I thinking? One strength won’t help me right now. I don’t even have money. And what was that about two hours? I have a mission to catch!

“Thank you, I’m fine,” he said, getting off the machine so quickly he startled her. She brought a hand over a pulsing chest. “Sorry,” Gary said, rushing out of the gym.

“Hey, Gary!” the front desk lady said cheerfully. “This gym isn’t normally available to climbers. Take advantage of the opportunity while you still can!”

He ran out, not replying, and noticed that of the crowd who’d entered, few had made it out.

“The gym ate them up…” he muttered.

“Hey, are you okay? You looked weird in those machines,” Goldie said.

“What do you mean? I only used the one.”

“No, silly. We were in there for hours.”

Gary looked at the sky. The sun was much lower than he expected. “Holy shit,” he said.

“That’s what I said!”

“No, you don’t understand. I have no memories. I think Satan mind-controlled me!”

“Isn’t that what he’s supposed to do?”

“That’s… Oh, leave me alone, Goldie! When’s the next mission?”

“At sunset, they said.”

“Then we need to hurry!” Gary said at the same moment that a familiar face approached him.

“Hey, Gary! It’s been a while!” Marla Jackson said.

“Where were you guys?”

“You’re at Bayonet Gym, right? We’re at Pontara. It’s all rogues at ours.”

“And all clan at ours. But why didn’t I see you on the mission?”

“They’re different. But, you get all the best ones,” she said with complaint. “We only got scrubs.”

“You are scrubs, Marla.”

“Don’t say it like that!”
