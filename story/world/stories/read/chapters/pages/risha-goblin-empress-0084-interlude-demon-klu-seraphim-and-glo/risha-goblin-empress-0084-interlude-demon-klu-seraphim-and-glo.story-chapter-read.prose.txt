She walked with the caravan, her eyes stuck on the night sky. She’d never imagined that above the rock and earth, such beauty had been hidden from her.

An Under Goblin walked by, and she pulled her hood tighter, the nubs of her horns small enough to hide from the gazes of those she knew hated her.

The goblin didn’t even look at her, his gaze focused on the edge of the path they walked. There’d only been one attack on the caravan, but security was high.

Whatever they transported to the war, it was important. Important enough for that orc to move.

Her gaze moved to the head of the caravan, where the red skinned Mystic walked, his gaze forward in a frown.

She shivered, sensing the spiritual power he wielded at his fingertips. Even with her newfound gifts, she wasn’t certain of surviving if he ever turned his gaze to her.

Her hand went to the stolen dagger at her waist. Its grip gave her warmth, a warmth that reminded her of that brief moment when the Grand Mother had held her. Ironic, since it was the same dagger she’d attempted to kill the Goblin woman with, blinded by pain as she’d been.

Her gaze found its way back to the sky, where clouds were moving to cover the skies. Even the clouds were beautiful, dark, and lit with the power of lightning.

The lightning flashed, and she saw it for only a moment.

A drake.

She looked around, wondering if anyone else had seen it, but the warriors around her continued to speak in quiet tones.

She looked back to the sky, and saw another drake, hiding in the storm cloud.

She gripped her dagger for courage, then pointed and shouted to the Mystic at the head of the caravan. “Drakes! In the clouds!”

The warriors of the caravan turned at her words, their attention saving their lives as creatures rose from the nearby waters, and arrows flew into their caravan.

She drew her dagger as one of them ran at her. She twisted under his first swing, then stepped close, her dagger sinking into the scaled warrior’s eye.

She pulled her dagger out, and stabbed the warrior’s arm, forcing him to drop his sword. She grabbed the sword as it fell, every secret lesson of her childhood fueling her swing as she twisted and cut the scaled warrior’s head off with his own sword.

He fell, but more followed.

She’d travelled at the edge of the caravan, not wanting to be recognized, a mistake she regretted as she found herself surrounded.

She glanced towards the Mystic, only to find him fighting a Lizard glowing with energy to match the Orc’s immense spirit.

She wasn’t ready to reveal herself, not yet. She wanted the protection of the Grand Mother first. She needed to show that she wasn’t a threat to her power, an ally, not an enemy.

But as they converged on her, and warriors around her fell, she couldn’t hide any longer.

She twisted around a spear, her sword knocking the wooden haft away.

Fire fell from the sky, and blood seeped into the ground, every second she hid causing more death.

She grit her teeth, then stepped away from her opponent and threw off her cloak. Black wings unfolded behind her, and power gathered to her.

Siyah, Inheritor of Freera, stepped forward as blades of black fire rained down on the enemy.
