Esther cracked one eye open and watched Jinyo get hit by continuous high-speed spikes from the Lord of the Mountains. Jinyo ran into the forest and used the trees as cover. The leather ball struck the tree—but instead of the tree snapping or bending, it was the ball that ricocheted off to a different target.

The leather ball slammed squarely into Jinyo as he ran. The Punishment Wave generated from the Perfect Rotation rattled every pulse point in his body. Jinyo swung his blade to cut the leather ball, but the leather was too tough for even an Ether Blade’s edge to bite through.

“Idiot. Running into the forest is just suicide.” Esther closed his eyes and returned to his circulating meditation.

Jinyo scrambled through the forest, dodging leather balls. Back in kindergarten, he’d loved dodgeball—but the moment the ball ended up in Naho’s hands, it became a nightmare he’d never forget. A four-year-old girl hurling a ball at every boy with dead accuracy and dominating completely? Not quite. It was a horror movie because whoever Naho threw the ball at ended up in the nurse’s office. The kindergarten had cancelled dodgeball until Naho and Jinyo moved up to primary school.

It was a terrible memory—watching peers from his year collapse face-first at the hands of his twin sister. His mother had told him: keep the good memories of things you love. Don’t be afraid of them. Jinyo had been sure he loved dodgeball because he had some good memories of it somewhere.

But right now, Jinyo was certain he hated dodgeball. Not that he’d been hating it for years—he hated it now. Right now. Immediately.

Especially volleyball. His personal ranking of most-hated sports: number one, volleyball. Number two, dodgeball. Number three, every sport involving a ball.

Wilting Blossom Palm! Jinyo struck the ball with his palm—but the moment his hand made contact, his wrist nearly twisted completely from the enormous torque. His body was battered from resisting a leather ball that had no particular business being this vicious.

‘Did I use the Punishment Wave wrong? No—I got it right. But it didn’t work.’ The ball’s rotational force. The Lord of the Mountains’ Punishment Wave was leagues beyond anything Jinyo could produce. Cancellation only worked when the two waves were of equal intensity at precisely the right moment.

Which meant it hadn’t been going all-out until now. Jinyo couldn’t even see where the ceiling of the Lord of the Mountains’ power was. His vision had gone dark—no path to victory anywhere in sight. He was trapped in a forest that was the creature’s own backyard. The trees he’d hoped would serve as cover had become weapons being used against him.

[Planar Control Technique (Basic): Proficiency increased to 26]

[Punishment Wave: Proficiency increased to Level 11]

[Sword Spirit (Basic): Proficiency increased to Level 42]

The Planar control technique he’d learned from Nephrin was already increasing his efficiency, helping him burn less Planar per Punishment Wave. Even so, it wasn’t enough to pull him out of this crisis. He needed more Planar. He wanted to break past the Common Realm and into Knight Tier.

‘If I break through the Planar threshold, it all resets to full. But there’s no way to do it…’ Jinyo’s mind went blank. He heard the overhead smack from above and immediately went flat, crawling along the ground. The leather ball bounced off a tree and slammed down—not just one this time. Two.

Jinyo rolled clear of the first but couldn’t dodge the second.

“AAGHH!” Jinyo’s scream tore through the entire forest. The leather ball ground into the part of his chest closest to his heart and lungs. Heavy enough to stop his breathing. More than a sledgehammer shattering ribs into fragments. The ball bounced back into the air. Jinyo crawled into the underbrush.

Breathe. Have to breathe deep.

His body was caked in wet earth. He didn’t know when mud had gotten into his mouth. Jinyo was sliding toward unconsciousness. He glanced sideways and caught sight of a wild fruit—blueberry-shaped but a deep, vivid purple that looked fresh and ripe. Jinyo knew exactly what it was. The Gardener’s Bag had thousands of the same thing.

“I’ve definitely lost my mind. Ha. Ha. Ha.” Jinyo laughed, low and quiet. At some point his head had filled with nothing but strange, absurd ideas. Maybe Esther had been right all along—maybe he wasn’t human, but a demon. Jinyo reached out and grabbed the Cursed Fruit, rolled aside to dodge the spinning leather ball descending toward him. Mud splattered wide and chased after Jinyo—but Jinyo was already gone.

“Hm.” The Lord of the Mountains heard no impact of ball against body. It stilled itself, listening. Its hearing was extraordinary. As long as anything living moved within the radius of this Cursed Forest, it perceived every motion. Jinyo was careening around like a headless fly. No idea what he was planning.

The Lord of the Mountains sent its leather ball up and served again—increasing the count to three balls.

Each ball gave chase like it had a mind of its own. The Lord of the Mountains furrowed its brow.

“Something feels off. Why is it getting faster?” The prey it had been toying with was supposed to weaken with every Punishment Wave it absorbed. Honestly, the fact that Jinyo’s thin frame had taken more than twenty high-speed spikes and still not collapsed was already miraculous by human standards. The human body was not designed to absorb that many Punishment Waves. Not just weakening—but becoming incrementally stronger?

While being hunted?

What trick was this?

The sound of footsteps grew steadily nearer the forest’s edge. At last, it chose to face the open directly. The Lord of the Mountains watched Jinyo leap over a shrub. In the brief time—less than half an hour—that Jinyo had spent running in the forest, he had grown visibly taller by roughly ten centimeters. His handsome face had sharpened, and his hair had grown almost to his waist.

Emerald green eyes locked onto the Lord of the Mountains a short distance away.

“You! How are you carrying a substance like that in your body?!”

“What does it matter? I’m doing what I’ve been thinking about for a long time!” Jinyo swallowed the purple berries.

[You have consumed Cursed Fruit. A special fruit. The Boost Status is a special status, previously undiscovered.]

[You have received Boost Status (G). All stat values increased by +5. You have received Dark Element (G).]

[You have consumed 50 Cursed Fruits. Boost Status has advanced to Rank F. All stat values increased by +5. Your Dark Element has advanced to F.]

[You have consumed 100 Cursed Fruits. Boost Status has advanced to Rank E. All stat values increased by +5. Your Dark Element has advanced to E.]

[Boost Status (E): While this status is active, all stat values +30. Planar recovery rate increases to 100%/200%/300%/500%. Lifespan is consumed at the rate of Planar recovery acceleration. For every 100% boost, Attack Power increases by +10% (maximum 50%). Efficiency of all other statuses increases by +30%. Duration: 5 minutes.]

[Bloodline: Ancient Flora has awakened. Awakening rate increased to 40%.]

[Congratulations. You have gained a second stat value. Evolving your pulse channels to accommodate a third Planar element.]

[Congratulations. You have broken through the Common Realm and advanced to Knight Tier 1.]

From the first day he had seen the Cursed Fruit and understood it carried a status value, Jinyo had considered: if he swallowed it, he would gain a special status just like Moonlight Status. But he hadn’t dared. He feared the lifespan cost and the addiction side effect. He hadn’t wanted to consume it while he still didn’t know a cure. But if he didn’t consume it now, he would be killed.

The addiction side effect had no power over him. Hallucination or poison had no effect on his body either. Before he knew it, Jinyo’s body was growing rapidly. He no longer looked like a man in his mid-twenties but had developed into the appearance of a man in his early thirties. In minutes. Jinyo could feel it directly—his body was dramatically stronger. His height, which had stopped growing since middle school, shot up without warning, and he hadn’t had time to adjust.

Dark-element Planar and Lunar Planar roared through his channels, competing for dominance and burying the Floral element inside him. As he entered the status, all elements shifted from E or F to E+ and F+. The breakthrough from Common Realm to Knight Tier 1 had expanded his Planar reservoir from the thousands into the tens of thousands. Jinyo was brimming with power.

“Establish Domain.”

Realm of Paradise!

Jinyo’s mind connected to heaven and earth. The Domain expanded to cover a five-hundred-square-meter radius. The Lord of the Mountains looked up at the sky as it changed. The rain clouds cleared, leaving only a post-storm sky where moonlight poured down to console a chaotic world. A young man in white caught the moonlight and drank it in, converting it into power.

“You think a little power increase will let you defeat me, Heaven’s Demon?”

“Who knows! Want to try matching wits for once?!” Jinyo launched himself forward, the Sonneteer in hand. The Lord of the Mountains sent a ball into the air and spiked it in an instant—leather ball aimed straight at Jinyo. The movement was in a straight line, and it didn’t frighten Jinyo in the slightest. His footwork shifted in rhythm, deflecting every ball with ease. He’d seen this attack pattern until he was sick of it.

“Amusing.” The Lord of the Mountains flexed its massive arm and shifted from serving to throwing. This was actual dodgeball. The throw from the Lord of the Mountains was nearly twice as powerful as a volleyball spike. The destructive force wasn’t even in the same league. By rights, Jinyo should have had no time to adapt—but:

Nature Synthesis.

Carrot Wall!

A carrot barricade materialized in an instant, blocking the path between Jinyo and the leather ball. The Lord of the Mountains’ four eyes went wide with shock.

So that was it! Because another object was blocking the way, the Punishment Wave couldn’t reach Jinyo on the other side. As long as something—anything—was between them, Jinyo would be safe.

But then—

“A flimsy wall like that. Did you think it would hold?” The leather ball spun as if it would never stop, accumulating force until it pulverized the carrot barricade. The ball shot through empty air. Jinyo slid across the muddy ground as all three elements of Planar erupted within him, competing for control.

It burned—because Jinyo had never carried more than two Planar elements before. His body was adjusting, and it was a burden he had to carry. He accelerated until he was inside striking range. The Lord of the Mountains swung a fist at him, but it missed.

It wasn’t slow at all. Jinyo was simply faster.

A massive body required massive energy to move. And it made for an easy target. Jinyo steadied his breathing and did what he had been thinking about. He suppressed the Floral-element Planar to its lowest possible level and switched to Lunar and Dark elements instead.

Sonnet of a Hundred Blossoms — First Form:

Peach Blossom Dance — Modified!

Jinyo cut in a continuous flurry. No more scattering peach blossom petals. Only the cold stillness of moonlight and the savage dark. The Sonneteer carved marks into the Lord of the Mountains’ hide, but didn’t cut deep enough to reach a vein—the skin was too thick, the body too resilient against slashing.

But physical damage wasn’t what Jinyo was after.

Punishment Wave!

Double Impact!

The tree-sized body lurched back as a blade not even the size of a hunting knife drove into it. The Lord of the Mountains’ eyes went wide—it hadn’t endured pain like this in a long time.

Punishment Wave was an attack that penetrated every defense. Immune to body size or defensive power—a high-tier technique requiring proficiency and fortune to execute. Annows had used this against humans countless times, and today, for the first time, Jinyo turned it back against one of their faithful. The higher the element’s concentration, the more damage this technique dealt. Jinyo had simply swapped from Floral to an element with greater concentration.

The Lord of the Mountains swung its arm like a whip. In Jinyo’s eyes, boosted as they were, the motion was glacial. The intelligence stat increase let him see movement in slow motion. He leaped, spun mid-air, and executed the form again.

“Boost 300%.” Jinyo set the recovery acceleration to 300%. Within the Realm of Paradise, where Spirit Essence exceeded twenty units, the Planar being consumed was replenished rapidly enough to stay full.

Peach Blossom Dance.

Peach Blossom Dance.

Peach Blossom Dance!

The Sonneteer kept swinging. Jinyo began alternating between Lunar and Floral elements. The Sonneteer only reacted to Floral-element Planar. Petals scattered—but not the beautiful pink blossoms from before. Black petals, unsettling, carrying the scent of something untamed.

Sonnet of a Hundred Blossoms — Third Form: Rose Thorn Whip!

The blade swept wide and fast in a continuous arc like a thorned lash. Strike after strike carved marks into the Lord of the Mountains’ body without stopping. The creature sustained almost no physical damage—but if Jinyo landed a Punishment Wave once every ten strikes, that was a success.

‘Why is this? Something feels strange.’ The Lord of the Mountains was far from death—yet somehow it felt no closer to victory. Jinyo was recovering too fast. Not just Planar—the wounds were healing too. None of the damage from twenty-odd Punishment Wave strikes remained?

No. Punishment Wave damage was a persistent wound. It didn’t heal easily. Even if Jinyo carried the Boost Curse the same as it did.

The Lord of the Mountains threw the ball in a continuous stream, but with the Eye of the Sage, once Jinyo had adapted, the same patterns no longer worked.

‘Accept me, Sonneteer.’

‘Show me your true power.’ Jinyo fed Floral-element Planar in, steady and unrelenting. The Floral element was lower in concentration than Lunar. He was betting that this sword would accept him. The sword entrusted to him by his mother in her role as head of the Southerncross clan.

‘Please. Sonneteer. Or is it because I’m not a woman that you won’t accept me?’ Jinyo communicated with the blade. No response came. Jinyo was deeply, profoundly sad. The sword he had inherited from his mother—and yet its true form still refused to reveal itself to him.

If the Divine Punishment can’t be used, keep striking until it can!

Jinyo dodged, struck, Peach Blossom Dance, Mirror Blossom, Rose Thorn Whip, Wandering Butterflies—Jinyo cycled through only four forms, over and over. Black petals scattered everywhere, filling the forest with their fragrance. They drifted upward toward the sky where the moon shone clear and bright.

Punishment Wave!

The Lord of the Mountains released a punch. Jinyo stepped clear of it with ease.

Bang!

Jinyo’s body was launched despite successfully dodging.

What kind of madness was this?! Another technique he hadn’t seen before!

“Enough of this nonsense. Heaven’s Demon.” The Lord of the Mountains compressed the leather ball until it became the size of a lemon—thick-walled and harder than metal. It lifted one leg, gripped the ball, and raised its arm.

A throw at twice the world record speed for professional baseball.

Jinyo used Floral Step to dodge—but the ball didn’t travel in a straight line. It curved, and slammed into the center of his body. The spinning ball compressed a continuous stream of Punishment Waves into Jinyo’s frame. Jinyo had to concentrate with everything he had to prevent blood from flowing out of every orifice.

The ball nearly stripped the life from him—but in the moment he was about to black out, Jinyo tossed a purple berry into his mouth. The sweet scent of the berry triggered a burst of endorphins that jolted him back to consciousness. He opened the Gardener’s Bag and crammed a Happy Carrot into his mouth to stabilize his mood.

Still going. Still in this.

It’s just a ball. Not even remotely scary!
