We know how many Great Families there are within Awakened Humanity. The number may change over time as they rise and fall, but the number is known.

We are aware of 3 Noble, or Royal, Families among the Fae. We suspect there are more, or perhaps less. We have only ever officially encountered members of one. Those of the main family line are easily recognized, those of the branch families could pass for human without anyone knowing the better.

Look for eyes that seem to glow, skin that seems too pale and bodies somehow both too angular and not at the same time. They do not hide who they are, they do not pretend. Why should they when they are raised to see all who are not of their main line as somehow something lesser. They view their branch cousins as of note, other fae as better than humans, if barely.

Watch for those they do listen to, that they do pay attention to. For if one such is present and looks unlike the family line? They are more than they seem.

After Cara had left they'd had a quick breakfast in the dining hall then spent the rest of the morning avoiding the topic of the fae entirely. There wasn't anything more to be said about it, not until they'd had a proper talk with Percivus at least. Bern could try to create an accident, could try to have things work out less pleasantly, but they'd be ready for him.

Seb felt the shift in Energy right before the door in front of them opened. The door hadn't actually changed, not that he could tell at least, but the Energy flowing through and around it had.

"Fay. Could you look into how our expansion portals work? Whether they can change destinations?"

"Portals always can…" she began before her eyes widened ever so slightly. "I'll have to look. They aren't a network and I thought they were direct passages, but if they can change destinations that would…"

It isn't the same, Anya sent.

Never stop saying what I'm thinking.

I don't plan to.
