April 3rd, 2037…the day the world changed forever. I was only a kid when the System came online, barely seven years old, but I still remember the first System notification, an ethereal purple text box that just hung in the air.

At approximately 1407 Eastern Standard Time, every proverbial shit in the world hit the fan. At the same exact time, every man, woman, and child got the same notification from a text box appearing in their vision, saying that the Earth had been selected to participate in the Sarlenac Quadrant Games. At the time, we didn’t know what that really meant. Hell, even fifteen years later we’ve barely scratched the surface. The System is not known for giving away secrets freely.

Moments after the first message, everyone got another notification. It varied person to person; some were designated as Hunters, others as Supporters or other specific roles. Each of us received the ability that allowed us to see the System’s interface. Within a day of the first notifications, new messages started appearing, telling us the first gates had spawned—massive rings that connected different worlds to our own through portals. Teams of scientists tested the gates to no avail. The technology used was far beyond anything we had ever seen.

Eventually, daring people entered the gateways…most never returning.

The System set a goal for Earth to complete a certain amount of gates each year, promising us rewards if we succeeded. It wasn’t long before the first gate was cleared, then the next, but the pace slowed dramatically once the casualties began to rise as tougher gates appeared. World governments drew lines in the sand, as they always had before. No countries were willing to give all of their resources to close the gates by themselves. Many would claim other countries were not giving enough.

In the end, we failed the objective that the System set for the first year. There was no reward for failure, only the horror of what came next. Approximately ten percent of the current population fell into deep comas the day after the System notified us of the failure. Most died in the first week. The healthcare infrastructure of the world could not bear the burden of so many ill. All of us who were left behind were made to watch as those we loved withered away.

The next year, some countries gave it their all, but it wasn’t enough. Earth failed again, and again. Each time, ten percent of the current population of the world fell into comas. The vast majority always ended up dead without enough staff to care for them.

Economies collapsed, and order was lost for a time.

Until the Earth united, forming one worldwide government…the United World Order.

For the good of mankind, on the fifth year, all surviving nations made a pact and began sharing resources, conscripting large amounts of the population into the military service. On the sixth year, the Earth succeeded for the first time in its mission, only after losing nearly half of the nine billion lives we had in 2037.

There is no one who has not felt the losses.

No one without a sad story to tell.

We are stronger for it now, more united because of it.

Our reward for passing was unique gates that offered special resources, weapons, armor, and things that we desperately needed, integral to improving, outfitting, and leveling up highly elite warriors and heroes of the World Hunter Association.

Each year we passed, we received off-worlders who had been through the trials on their own worlds and failed the Sarlenac Games. They came through special reward gates. With them, they brought knowledge that Earth desperately needed. These people were of different races, cultures, and some even of different species. All of them had one thing in common, though: their home worlds failed and were burned for it.

No one has ever woken from the System-induced comas. Those who wish to see their loved ones cared for must be of service to the new world government.

Fifteen years after the first System message, we still fight on.

We are not defeated even against the staggering losses. We are strong. We are relentless, and we will survive.

This is our story. Written for those who we have lost, in their memory we will carry the torch.
