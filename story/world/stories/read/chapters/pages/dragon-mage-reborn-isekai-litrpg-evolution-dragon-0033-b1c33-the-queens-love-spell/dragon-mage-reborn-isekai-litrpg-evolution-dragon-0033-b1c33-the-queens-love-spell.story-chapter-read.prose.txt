The spider queen was crafty. Not only did she make Nolan waste a good vial of healing potion when it splattered on the ground, she planned a perfectly curated attack against me to catch Nolan off guard and subdue him.

I didn’t know what pissed me off more—the fact that she pulled a fast one on me, or that she thought she could get away with it. I could get to him and break the love spell. We weren’t lifelong friends, but we shared a bond, a special bond. A bond of two super cool travelers trekking it out together in the wild! A bond that could move mountains and calm storms! A bond that was—EEEeeKKK! I shrieked as Nolan fired a slug at me. The evil spider queen cackled as I dodged it, amused that she’d effectively turned my friend against me.

“Good. Make her squirm and squeal,” she sang, standing there at the sidelines as she watched Nolan charge in at me with those terrifying gauntlets.

Okay, it was time to break in my items—Nolan was the new final boss! I equipped my shield charm which reduces magical damage by 5%, my warrior’s charm, which increases chances of a critical hit, and my willpower charm, just in case this witch tried anything funny on me.

‘Get back, Nolan!’ I shouted, backpedaling as fast as my injured leg would allow. ‘Fight it! This isn’t you!’

“Open your eyes, short fry. I work for the queen now.” Nolan’s gaze remained glazed over, that eerie devotion fixed on his face as he advanced. The spider queen’s venom had worked its way into his system, overriding his will. Those gauntlets that had just saved us from the centipede were now poised to crush me.

‘She’s controlling you!’ I tried again, dodging another wild swing that would have taken my head clean off. ‘Remember who you are! A man of the people, a top-ranked dungeon crawler! My friend!’

“I’m right where I want to be.” He smirked. My words bounced off him like pebbles against steel. The queen's pheromones had sunk their hooks deep into his mind. Nolan loaded another round into Scarlet, his movements mechanical and precise throughout the enchantment.

“Destroy her,” the queen cawed. “Bring me her heart!”

I wasn't about to let this eight-legged hussy turn my companion into her personal hitman. I needed a plan—fast. My injured leg throbbed, making it harder for me to duck and weave between his melee strikes and his shotgun blows. Nolan wasn’t giving me a second to collect myself, relentlessly pursuing me across the cavern. I had to find a way to break the queen’s hold before he landed a hit on me.

I really didn’t want to attack him, but he left me no choice. He had the Armor of Invulnerability, which protected him against non-magical attacks, so I had to stick with my skill set. Not that I thought I could do much damage to him with my [Deadly Bite] anyway. Getting close to him meant signing my death warrant. I had to keep my distance for as long as I could to save my hide.

Nolan was an amazing long range fighter. He had options for both defensive and offensive attacks. I, on the other hand, was lacking in the defensive department. My skills were based on a lot of oppressive moves which left me open for a lot of punishment. Besides my [Cyclone Shield], I didn’t have much to work with. I had to come up with other ways of winning this fight.

I glanced at the queen, who lounged on her massive web throne, watching our fight with sadistic pleasure. I glared at her before finding myself at the other end of Nolan’s barrel. I triggered my [Meteor Strike] and discouraged him from firing at me. Instead, he had to step back to avoid being buried in a shower of fiery rocks.

Finally, a moment to strategize my next move. Covered behind the veil of my attack, I downed a simple healing potion for my leg. Instant fix. Then, I equipped my Cloak of Despair, providing me with invisibility. This was my first time using it, so my durability was pretty damn good. I had a few minutes before a cool down and reset, so I was going to make the best of it.

As soon as the storm calmed down, Nolan was clueless. He had no idea where I was. The invisibility was perfect, not even a ripple in the air to give me away. I used this to my advantage and circled around him, trying to find a point of attack. His perception was sharp, so I had to be careful where I stepped. I needed to use a skill I could fully control so I wouldn’t hurt him too badly.

My [Ice Breath] was a safe enough option. I could control its intensity. When I opened my mouth, a stream of icy cold air escaped it, targeting his quick-fire hand. Nolan quickly dove out of the way and rolled to the side, before getting back on his feet and throwing a blade at me. Crap, that was too close! He almost sliced me across my face! What good was my invisibility with a reaction speed and precision like that?! I had to be more strategic with where I fired. Flanking him was best.

So I weaved and bobbed around the lair as he tried to track me with more of his throwing knives. When he wasn’t successful, Nolan resorted to the trickery of his precious Scarlet. He fired four shots, and suddenly a huge translucent gold force field barricaded me. I had to skid to a stop, but the field made contact with my arm as I crashed into it, electrocuting me.

I screamed as the jolt went through me. The electrical current left me dazed, the sensation like fire running through my veins. Just like that a chunk of my HP vanished. I managed to roll away as my whole body tingled painfully. Luckily, my invisibility remained intact.

‘Damn asshole,’ I seethed. I had to remember none of this was his fault, no matter how painful it was. The queen giggled when the field went down and Nolan readied up another round. I borrowed all the rage inside me to get right back up, and dodged his explosive pellets.

I barely made it out of that barrage. Fast on my heels, I dashed around to flank him. This time, he wasn’t going to stop me.

The queen grew impatient as I continued to thwart his efforts of locating me. “Find her!” she screeched, and Nolan acted. He reached in his pocket and then threw three white balls in the ground. Instantly, white smoke surrounded the lair. I feinted left, then darted right, avoiding going right through the clouds. When I reached my point, Nolan had no idea where I was. I opened my mouth again and a stream of ice blasted his elbow.

I was aiming for his arm, but when he pivoted, my blast caught his elbow instead. The ice crackled as it spread across his joint, freezing it solid. The gunman grunted, momentarily immobilized as he tried to break free of my icy grip. Now was my chance to apply the pressure. Another ice ball for his two legs—bim, bop, and pow, for his other arm. His limbs were frozen instantly, suspended in a bizarre pose like a macabre ice sculpture. Nolan struggled against his icy prison, his muscles straining against the freezing bonds.

“What the hell are you doing?!” the queen shrieked, her composure finally cracking. “Break free and kill her!”

‘Don’t listen to that hussy, Nolan, you’re my friend!’ I begged, but when I noticed him beginning to break free from the ice, I started to panic.

His gun was frozen and the opportunity to wrap up this fight was there, but the idea of leaving Nolan in deadly burns left my chest hollow. ‘Don’t make me do this….’ I pleaded, but Nolan remained silent. Okay, talking to him wasn’t working—maybe a little bit of pain could break him out of it? A small bit of pain, nothing too serious. So I rushed in, prepared to take a whack out of the gunslinger, until I noticed the ice suddenly thawing all at once!

“Ah, that’s better!” the queen tweeted. “My, your resources are plenty, aren’t they?”

Nolan, what the heck?!

It was too late for me to make a U turn, so I slid between his legs, just as he reached down and slammed his fist into the ground. But then he pivoted around and kicked the crap out of me, my body landing in the puddle of blue guts I tried avoiding this entire time! With all of this bright visible color on me, he rendered my invisibility useless.

Nolan charged in. I scrambled to my feet, slipping in the centipede goo. My cloak flickered, the invisibility fading. My time was up, both literally and figuratively. Fighting Nolan up close was what I didn’t want, yet, I found myself face-to-face with the love-drunk bastard. Before I could do anything, he was already on me.

I turned around and stomped my foot as he leaned in to punch me, splashing the smelly liquid on his face and blinding his one eye. I followed up with another ice blast to back him off, but it was no good–his reaction speed was too fast. His gauntleted fist connected with my side, sending me flying across the chamber. I crashed into one of the hardened webs, gasping as pain radiated through my ribs. My HP bar flashed dangerously low. One more hit like that and I’d be finished.

“Yes!” the queen hissed excitedly. “Finish it!”

I struggled to get on my feet, the taste of copper filling my mouth. This wasn’t working. I couldn’t outfight Nolan, my faith in winning this fight trickling away. He was too strong and too fast for me. On top of his natural strength and resilience, he had an arsenal of resources in his inventory to take a baby dragon like me down.

Special or otherwise, I was kidding myself in thinking I could outperform him. With my body broken, he slowly approached me, that intimidation in his gait making me freeze up. I bit down into my jaw as I begged my body to move. When he stopped and pulled Scarlet on me, I looked into his gaze and winced.

“You held back,” he said. “That wasn’t very smart of you…”

I looked up to him helplessly.

He loaded his last shot, using the shell I’d seen earlier, with the neurotoxin he told me he’d use on the queen. Before I could process what was happening, he smiled and then he… winked at me?

My eyes ballooned up, and before I could connect the dots, Nolan flipped the script. He pivoted around and fired that shot right in between that bitch’s six eyes!
