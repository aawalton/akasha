Those candle classes were quite impressive weren’t they? You wouldn’t think such a joke class would have so much power, but of course it was never really a joke at all. So let’s look at the two they forgot to check.

[Candle Enthusiast] + [Survivalist]

[Tier 1 Firetender]**

Keeping watch in the night, feeding the fire, it’s a thankless job but someone must do it. You take pride in this role, stoking even the smallest ember into a roaring bonfire.

Skill - Stoke Flames: Fires in your presence use less fuel, and you can cause them to burn hotter and brighter without consuming extra fuel.

Skill - Night Watch: Dramatically increased Awareness when illuminated by open flames.

Attribute bonuses: Awareness, Will.

Advancement: Maintain controlled fires.

See? Who knows how that Stoke Flames skill would interact with those magical candles? Well, I do, but I’m not telling. We have to maintain some mysteries, and this is about numbers.

Speaking of numbers, have you noticed how vague the skills are? “Increased”? “Dramatically”? By how much? How do the attributes affect skills? It seems hardly fair of the author—I mean, the Goddess, to hide such important details. It’s like they don’t even want people arguing about optimal builds in the comments.

Well, luckily for you, I have the means to look a little deeper.

Skill - Stoke Flames: Fires in your presence use (50%) less fuel, and you can cause them to burn (Tier value + Ego%) hotter and brighter without consuming extra fuel.

Skill - Night Watch: Awareness increased by (+1 Tier value) when illuminated by open flames.

Oh, “Tier Value”? That’s the base attribute value for the tier. The formula for it is n+n^2, but at tier zero it’s just 1 as a special exception. “Dramatically” means it uses the next tier instead. Oh, and it uses species tier, not class tier. So low tier classes can still be very powerful tools in the hands of a high tier person.

Let’s stick to the advanced view as long as we’re here, shall we? It’s obviously much better. Now where were we? Oh right!

[Candle Enthusiast] + [Organizer]

[Tier 1 Cereostylist]**

Like flower-arrangement, but with candles. You have the skills to artfully place candles in such a way that not only provides perfect illumination, but also doubles as artful decor.

Skill - Perfect Angle: Ego increased by (+1 Tier value) while illuminated by candlelight.

Skill - Visualize the Scene: You can perfectly recreate your surroundings in your mind and adjust the lighting freely within that recreation. (The recreated area can be up to a number of cubic meters equal to Awareness.)

Attribute bonuses: Awareness, Will.

Advancement: Brighten up the place.

I guess they can’t all be winners, huh? Although I bet you could really get up to some shenanigans with that second one at higher levels if you got creative. Someone must have had this class when they were making that weird labyrinth.

Alright, so that’s the candle classes done. Some of you might be wondering why so many classes either scale with attributes they don’t provide, or provide attributes they don’t scale with. Why do you think we—I mean, they—gave people more than one class slot? Synergies, of course!

A [Firetender]/[Cereostylist] would get extra Ego from [Perfect Angle], improving the effect of [Stoke Flames], likewise their [Night Watch] would increase Awareness, making [Visualize the Scene] more powerful! Classes work better together than they do alone.

Okay, what else, what else? I—that is to say, someone came up with classes for every single combination, you know. Seems like a waste not to show them off.
