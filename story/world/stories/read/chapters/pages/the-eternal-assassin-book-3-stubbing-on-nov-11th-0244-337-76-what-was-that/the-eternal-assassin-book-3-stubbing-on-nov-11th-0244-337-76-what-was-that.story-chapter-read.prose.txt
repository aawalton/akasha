While the team did have a few potions they could potentially take to help with certain situations, the unfortunate truth was that the vast majority of potions had effects that only lasted a couple of minutes. Even so, Asher made certain he handed out a few of the potions he was carrying for his teammates as their personal pack mule so that he couldn’t be blamed if they were attacked out of the blue and he got distracted.

The nine of them quickly made their way toward the hills Lanra had pointed out, and Asher prepared himself for anything. He’d fought his way through the zombie-infested Deadlands, the labyrinth-like tunnels home to The Lost Lady, and even a dense jungle filled with disease and poison capable of killing a man in mere seconds. Every single one of the liches on the Council of Death seemed hell bent on making sure nobody got anywhere near them, and he doubted Flunx was going to be any sort of outlier in that regard.

Cresting the hill, he took in the old, decaying castle off in the distance. Sitting on a cliff right up against the water, at first glance, the castle didn’t look like anything all that impressive. Only about half the size of the royal palace back in the capital, this one had crumbling ramparts and years of wear from the salt-riddled water as it splashed up against the stone. It was obvious Flunx was going for a ‘hide in plain sight’ tactic, because despite knowing with near certainty that the lich was operating out of that castle, Asher still barely believed it even now.

“You all know the mission,” Lanra said, pointing at the castle with her staff as her eyes glowed and she seemed to look at something the rest of them couldn’t see. “Get to it!”

“Let’s go!” Asher said, nodding toward Samantha. Without a word, the hunter blasted off from the ground in a straight line toward the castle as she used her second-tier effect of Flight Burst, her emerald wings manifesting on her back as she shot forward. Asher waited with bated breath, his daggers at the ready in the event that anything jumped out at her or she triggered some sort of hidden defenses. Thankfully, Flunx didn’t seem to have anything of the sort located outside the castle, and Samantha touched down safely only a hundred feet away. The moment she did so, Asher used Spatial Lock and Recall to warp everyone to her side.

“Good luck in there,” Mako grinned, twirling his spear around as he took up position around Lanra. The wizard already had her eyes closed and was muttering to herself, and Asher watched as a massive, impossibly thin sheet of tritessence began spreading out from her, wrapping around the edges of the castle as it stretched higher and higher. “Go stab a corpse for me!”

“Will do,” Asher chuckled before turning toward Rulfar. The bloodhound had already shifted into his ten-foot-tall battle form that looked like a grizzly bear crossed with a human and dumped in a vat of steroids. As he sniffed away, Rulfar frowned.

“There’s so much tritessence and gemstone dust within the castle I can barely even pick out one clump compared to another. It’s too saturated.”

“I don’t see any other ways in,” Samantha said, landing beside them after completing a quick fly around the top of the castle. “There are no windows, and no other entrances or exits.”

“We’ll just have to hope my and Rulfar’s skills can detect the worst of what Flunx has prepared,” Asher decided, wincing as he slid his eyes across the castle’s exterior. From what he could tell, each individual block making up the castle had been enchanted separately from the others, and then the entire wall itself had been enchanted even further, which he hadn’t even known was possible. A quick test with Astral Dip was all the confirmation he needed that spatial travel through the walls was locked down, and he sighed as he realized they only had one option. Unless he wanted to try and dig through what looked like three-feet-thick stone walls with his void dagger, which would take forever if he could even pull it off before he ran out of tritessence, they were going in through the main entrance.

Approaching the main door, Asher grunted as Identify gave him the same unhelpful information as it did for every other part of the castle.

[Masterwork Door] - Heavily enchanted.

Shaking his head, Asher pulled out his void dagger. Making sure not to touch the door handle, he ignored the sensation of his own tritessence being drained into the dagger before stabbing it into the hinges on the door, erasing them from existence. As soon as they were gone, he stepped back and nodded toward Moxy. Moxy grinned as she got the memo, shoving down air into a tight ball before winding up and pitching it straight at the door. The moment the ball touched the wood it detonated, blasting the door down the hall in a powerful explosion of air that swept past the rest of the team.

“I’ll lead,” Rulfar decided, uncorking a potion of Steel Skin and downing it as he stepped forward. His massive frame took on a metallic sheen that added to his already insane durability, and Asher nodded as they all fell into line behind him. With Kroult, Moxy, and Samantha in the center and him bringing up the rear, the five of them slowly entered the old castle.

The main hallway leading into the castle was smaller and less grandiose than Asher had been expecting. While the interior was definitely nicer than what the outside look of the castle would lead one to expect, it wasn’t nearly as opulent as the King of Death had kept his workshop. There was a long, simple runner spread out across the floor, and pots containing live flowers of all things every dozen feet. A random mismatch of paintings were hung up here and there, which he could only assume had been left behind by whoever had originally constructed the castle.

“How does she have plants growing in here?” Samantha muttered, an arrow already nocked as she kept her bow raised and at the ready.

“Good question. Maybe—”

A loud bang from behind them made Asher reflexively slip into the astral as he spun around, his eyes widening as he took in the dense, stone wall that had appeared out of nowhere directly where the castle door had once been. He didn’t know if it had been triggered by something they did or if Flunx had activated it herself, but either way, their one exit out of the castle was sealed shut.

Then, the second trap triggered, filling the entire hallway with fire so intense the flames danced a brilliant blue that was all but blinding.

Asher could do nothing but stand there in horror from within the astral realm as the world burned around him. While the raging inferno couldn’t so much as touch him, the same couldn’t be said for the rest of his team. The fire burned everywhere, which meant even if he managed to somehow survive returning to the material realm long enough to throw up a Spatial Lock, the flames would already be within his controlled space.

The only thing that stopped Asher from freaking out and doing anything crazy was the fact that curiously, the Eternal Mark he had placed on Samantha was telling him she was completely fine.

It seemed the magical power required for such a devastating trap meant that it couldn’t be maintained for long, as the flames died out after only three or four seconds. The moment they did, Asher blinked as he took in the scene before him.

Rulfar stood in the front of the pack with a pained look on his face as smoke drifted off his charred fur. A familiar, red glow radiated off his body, revealing he’d managed to activate his Unyielding Blood skill fast enough to save his life. Between them was a glowing cocoon of emerald feathers, and Asher blinked as he recognized one of Samantha’s newest skills she’d only just started practicing during the last week. Slowly, her wings of pure energy opened up, revealing Moxy, Kroult, and Samantha all huddled together with sweat dripping down the women’s faces.

Seeing as he was undead, Kroult didn’t seem all that bothered by the intense heat they had to have just lived through.

“Nice reaction time,” Moxy muttered, actually looking a touch shaken as she wiped the sweat from her brow and gave them a shaky grin. “I’ll admit, I didn’t see that one coming. Winged Shield, right?”

“Yeah. The second-tier of the skill lets me wrap myself up in a perfect cocoon,” Samantha nodded, her legs shaking as she raised a trembling hand and doused herself with water. “I thought I was ready for anything. But I barely used my skill in time. Even a split second later…”

“All that matters is that you did get it up in time,” Asher smiled, pulling her into a strong hug as he kissed her forehead. He tried to hide his shudder at just how hot her skin was to the touch, and he stepped back to let her get back to healing herself. “Rulfar, you good?”

“I’m ashamed to admit I reacted a tad slower than Samantha,” the bloodhound said sheepishly, motioning toward his burned hair that was still smoking. “Still, I survived, so no complaints here.”

While Samantha started hosing him down as well, Asher looked around, frowning at the untouched plants. The flowers had escaped the heat completely untouched, which somehow made it feel like Flunx was laughing at them. The skill to transform an entire corridor into a blazing inferno was one thing, but to do so without even charring the delicate flowers was something else.

“We didn’t even make it three dozen feet into the castle before Flunx nearly got us,” Asher muttered, clicking his tongue as he peered down the hall. “And if she’d locked down spatial travel in this hallway, she would have gotten me.”

“We knew we were walking into the griffin’s den when we accepted the mission,” Rulfar shrugged, doing his best to shake out his wet fur now that the flames had been extinguished. “Unless you have a better idea, we just need to keep on our toes.”

“It’s not a ton… but at the very least, you and I should switch places. Even if she’s managed to block our identification skills, maybe Secret Seeker will help me pick out some traps before we trigger them. Kroult, you have that spell that enhances reaction times. You should hit us with it now rather than later.”

“Of course,” Kroult said, walking around and laying a hand on everyone as he cast.

The spell was an excellent one, but it had a distinct downside where the longer it was active, the more twitchy and uncontrolled a person’s muscles became. They’d agreed to save it until they were deeper within the castle to be safe, but if the excitement was starting already, then it was time to speed up their plans. As soon as Kroult was done buffing them all, Asher nodded.

“Okay. Everyone stays within twenty feet of me at all times so I can throw up a Spatial Lock if needed. We don’t have time to sit back and wait for Samantha and Rulfar’s tritessence to recover, so I’ll try and pick up the slack if we get hit by another trap like that. And remember, watch out for golems!”

“I’d much rather something I can punch than any more traps,” Moxy grumbled, cracking her neck as she downed one of her potions of Sensitivity. “This lich better hope you find her before I do, because mark my words. If I end up getting to her first, I’m going to teach her how to fight like a warrior whether she wants to or not. And I have it from good authority that my lessons are not pleasant.”
