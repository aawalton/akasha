A galaxy of sparkling stars painted the dark void around me as always. In my hand rested a golden orb, its surface smooth and unblemished with any mark of an affinity.

It felt oddly weightless in my hand, even compared to the other Wills I'd relinquished.

It suddenly dawned on me that this little sphere contained the last whispers of Pirhani's existence. The fading light of a god.

I recalled the strange glitch-like vision I had when I touched her thread—or rather, when the duplicate version of me touched her thread. A woman. A normal woman. I didn't know what that meant. I hoped it didn't mean what I thought it might.

It didn't matter right now though. A battle raged back in Penumbra and I held the remnants of a god in my hand. I was going to make use of it.

"Thanks, Pirhani," I said aloud as if the last traces of her life could hear me.

Her power wouldn't go to waste.

I stared up at the affinity symbols as they formed in the sky. In the past, I'd always held the Will up to the symbol of its associated affinity, but this Will had no affinity.

So what do I do with it then?

I walked around the invisible platform, searching for some sort of clue, but nothing presented itself. To be expected. Nothing in Penumbra could ever be straightforward…

I looked at the starry skull above. Couldn't hurt to try offering it there. It was my most notable affinity, after all.

I lifted the Will up to the skull.

It cracked.

It cracked into an immeasurable web of lines. Golden light poured from within, more like a river than a mist. The river split into a number of tributaries branching off in different directions. They floated up, searching. A stream slithered up to each symbol in the sky. Shield. Flame. Snowflake. Flower. Lightning bolt. Eight-point star. Heart. Skull.

Each glowed brighter as the light absorbed into the stars.

The orb in my hand broke into glittering dust. Pirhani was finally gone for good.

[Will Successfully Relinquished

Rank: God

Affinity: None]

[Initializing corresponding augmentation to:

Strength

Defense

Agility

Stamina

Health

Volition]

My veins burned Emerald with flecks of gold, blindingly bright. The release felt like all my impurities filtered away, like my body had been renewed to a state of perfection.

[Stat Augmentation Complete.]

I awaited the falling sensation that always followed this step. The breaking of the platform that brought me back to Penumbra.

But it never came. Instead, additional text appeared in my interface.

[Initializing skill augmentation…

…

Four compatible skills identified…]

[Initializing skill augmentation for:

Aero Shift.]

[Emerald rank skill augmentation complete.

Effect: Increased speed of motion; reduced Volition consumption.]

[Initializing skill augmentation for:

Life Drain.]

[Emerald rank skill augmentation complete.

Effect: Multi-target drain permitted with 25% lower Health recovery from each target; reduced Volition consumption.]

[Initializing skill augmentation for:

Prismatic Bow.]

[Emerald rank skill augmentation complete.

Effect: Shoot three arrows at multiple targets simultaneously; reduced Volition consumption.]

[Initializing skill augmentation for:

Threads of Fate.]

[Emerald rank skill augmentation complete.

Effect: Unknown.]

[No further skills identified.]

[Skill Augmentation complete.]

I stood there in stunned silence.

All four skills upgraded?

All stats upgraded?

I couldn't believe it.

My body barely reacted as the platform shattered beneath me and I fell into the emptiness of the void.

I returned to Penumbra, my body immediately being launched backward from the pillar by a tsunami of heat. The black light cast skyward from the pillar burst into a golden hue. It lasted a long time, then dissipated and returned to normal.

I let my head fall back onto the cold stone.

Less than a foot above my vision a lupine Sin growled and dribbled bloody saliva. I rolled forward, giving myself distance, more startled than worried it would attack me. I was still inside the protective barrier of the pillar's circle, but had been pushed far closer to the edge than I expected.

I flexed my fingers and bounced on the balls of my feet. The difference in strength and agility was noticeable even from those small movements. This was like no boost in stats I'd experienced before. The trickles of improvement I'd been getting from the lower rank Wills were like grains of sand. Even the Emerald Will stat boosts felt paltry next to this.

There was no quantitative way to know how much my stats improved, but the symbols in the starry void had seemed to nearly double in brightness. And the sensation in my limbs seemed to reinforce that.

I wasn't just stronger in every facet thanks to Pirhani's Will. I was twice as strong.

An audible chuckle escaped my lips. She'd put so much effort into destroying me. Instead, she'd made me even more formidable.

I held out a hand and called my scythe to me from where it still rested in the pillar. It twirled back and I caught it in my carapace hand—fully recovered from the melting that relinquishing the Will had caused.

My Health and Volition were both maxed out now.

And I had some work to do. But not here. These Sins were a waste of time. I'd clean them up later.

I Aero Shifted past the snarling wolf and its friends, outside the pillar room, and into the lobby. The Sins had all congregated around the doorway, backs to me now. I sprinted to the entrance to the arena, saving my Volition. Even though my skills all improved and required less Volition, I had no idea what I was going to find in there and how much it would take to save my companions.

I came out into the stands. There were still far too many Unchained below. Maybe even approaching a hundred. Back in the cave, that Iron puppet master Reaper had said he had over two hundred under his control at once. He'd sent them all here. And almost half still remained.

It seemed impossible. And most that had survived this long were Emerald rank.

No wonder the damage to the city had been so great.

The Sins swarmed a corner of the arena, churning like a school of pirañas. Fighting themselves. Fighting the other Reapers that defended in a small semi-circle.

They had no idea what was about to hit them.

I gripped my scythe tighter, focused on the exact location I wanted to go. Then I Aero Shifted straight into the center of the swarm.

I exploded out of the Shift, scythe twirling. Limbs flew. Blood sprayed. In that moment, I wasn't a Reaper. I was a meat shredder.

Amethyst Sins. Emerald Sins. It didn't matter. They never saw me coming and didn't live to comprehend what ended them.

I cast Life Drain on the five closest enemies, draining their Health as I called out to the surviving Reapers. Someone yelled a reply, but I couldn't decipher it amid the chaos. I Aero Shifted in the direction it came from, leaving my scythe corporeal. It cut through legs, dropping Sins as I burst through the crowd.

I came out short of the Reaper's defensive line, afraid I'd injure someone if I broke through too close. I hacked at the last few Sins blocking me and came face-to-face with Jericho.

He grinned at me, face splattered with gore. "Nice of you to join us."

"I've been a little busy," I countered.

Jericho blocked an oncoming thorn with a small club of metal carapace around his arm. The rest of his armor wasn't manifested.

"Low on Volition," he said. "We all are."

Lancelot continued diving in and out of the melee from the sky. His prismatic sword thundered down like lightning on his foes, but was a smaller version than the one I'd seen him use against the gorilla near the lighthouse.

Calliope was there as well, using shields of ice to help protect, but they were thin and broke easily. Nuzzle fought alongside her, casting little fireballs and gnawing at the legs of any Unchained who got too close to her.

Zeus and Silas fought in tandem among several other Reapers I didn't know. Most were Ambers. A few Amethyst.

"Where's Trix?" I asked.

"Knocked back to Vitria a little while ago."

"And all the help Lancelot brought?"

Jericho laughed bitterly. "What help?"

I growled as I loosed a trifecta of prismatic arrows at a Sin charging toward Calliope.

He was supposed to come back with an army of high level Reapers. What happened?

"We need to move this fight to the pillar so you all can replenish," I said.

"We'll never make it. Especially the Ambers."

"We'll make it," I said. "I'll clear the path. I need to talk to Lancelot."

Jericho nodded and whacked his club against a beaver-looking Sin's face, cracking its buck teeth.

I moved to the center of the group where a few Reapers were nursing injuries or resting from the fight. Lancelot hovered above, his armor looking more ruby than silver from the blood.

I shot an arrow into his periphery. He glared down, but I waved him over.

Seconds later he was landing beside me with a thud. "We can't hold them off much longer," he said.

"I know. We need to get everyone to the pillar room in the colosseum."

"We tried to make that run already. Lost four Reapers and got pushed back."

"You didn't have me."

Lancelot squinted at me through the visor of his helm, unconvinced.

"I'll clear the front. You and Jericho hold the sides. Ambers protected in the center with a handful of Wills each."

"I know you're an Emerald now, but—"

"Just trust me. I've got the most Volition left of anyone."

Lancelot gritted his teeth, but nodded. "If you get us all killed, I'll put you in the ground."

"If I get us all killed, I'll already be in the ground." I lifted my foot and pointed to the lack of shadow. "There's no phasing out for me, remember?"

"Let's do it then. Show me what you're capable of."

"Hey! I just killed a fucking god! What do you mean 'show me what you're capable of?!'"

He waved me off. "Special circumstances don't count."

"Like hell they don't!"

But Lancelot was already off to inform the others of the plan. Ungrateful bastard.

I grumpily launched a few sets of arrows at Sins who were encroaching on the group, then rejoined Jericho at the crest of the defensive formation.

"Ready to go, my boy?" he asked as he sent a weak bolt of lightning into the eyes of a bird-spider Sin.

Behind, Lancelot was gathering the lower level Reapers into a tight formation in the center. Calliope and Nuzzle were with him, removed from the fighting for now.

"Any minute now."

Once, the others were settled, protected in the shell formed by the remaining higher level Reapers, I led the march forward.

"Remember," I shouted over the din of monstrous growls and howls around us. "Don't fight to kill, just to keep them off us. The priority is moving forward. And don't get left behind."

With that, I pushed forward into the churning sea of Sins. Hacking. Slicing. Chopping. I cast a Life Drain, sucking the life-force from the nearest foes in a circle around our group as we inched into the deep. My allies finished them off, incapacitating so we could progress.

Time passed in a blur of blades, shadows, and arrows. We made it almost halfway across the arena through a gauntlet of death.

Then someone screamed.

I turned around. One of the Amethyst's defending the right side had a bloody icicle sticking out his thigh. Another one, six inches thick thwunked into his stomach. He fell to the ground.

The Emerald Sin that impaled him—a snowy owl mixed with a tiger—pushed him to the side as he phased out. Two other Unchained followed through the gap in the formation. They went straight for the Ambers huddling in the center.

But the Reapers were prepared, scythes out, skills at the ready.

The ground beneath their feet turned to ice. Most slipped, lost focus. One even dropped her scythe completely.

Calliope stood strong in front of them all. She'd cast her own ice spears into the ground, holding herself steady. The owl charged toward her.

I notched a prismatic arrow and let it loose, but the owl barely noticed. Then something wrapped around my chest and pulled me backwards.

I reached out a hand, but could only watch. Everything had happened so fast. Fell apart in the blink of an eye.

It was a massacre.
