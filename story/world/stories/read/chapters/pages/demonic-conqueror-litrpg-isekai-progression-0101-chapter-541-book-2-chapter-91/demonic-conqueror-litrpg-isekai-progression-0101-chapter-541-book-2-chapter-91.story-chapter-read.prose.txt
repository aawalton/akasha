You would've been lucky to last a month. Fell Beasts were known to attack areas even with the appropriate defenses in place, let alone ones offering themselves up on a silver platter.Things were done differently in Ashora, it seemed. Fell Beasts had been created by the Ancient One, so it made sense that there would be less of them farther away from the Isles. Additionally, some Demons probably enjoyed fending off Beast incursions. Made them look strong. Needing a big wall to safeguard your territory could also be seen as a sign of weakness by rival City-Lords.None of those possibilities were what his Boon-Bearers focused on, though. Not even after Simon explained his reasoning to them directly. Katarina, Bastian, Cyna, and Esen could only interpret the lack of defensive perimeters as just another indicator of Ashora's opulence.This was a nation so safe that it didn't need walls, didn't need Warding Orbs. A true land of prosperity.
