Chapter 386

Chop was absolutely loving this.

When he had first reached X-rank all those many years ago, he had thought the rest of his life would be one long tour of battles to the death. One after the other, day in and day out, with no breaks in between.

That was all he had wanted.

A distraction from the thoughts that seemed to refuse to stop swirling around inside his head. That’s what it was.

But as time went on, he had come to realize just how optimistic his expectations had truly been. Sure, if it was a fight he was looking for, there were plenty around to be had, and it certainly was to the death.

The problem was that it was never to his death. At some point, killing helpless monsters became as devoid of adrenaline and excitement as sitting on his flat ass. Cutting the head off a kilometer long serpent was no different from slicing cucumbers in the garden for a light snack.

Very rarely, the occasional X-ranked creature would show up and provide him a battle worth getting into, but at the end of the day, all it would really be was a bleak reminder of how dull the rest of his existence was.

But right here there were so many powerful spawn to slaughter that he could barely feel the negative emotions roiling underneath. It was pure bliss.

And this too would eventually run out.

But perhaps, he found, that wasn’t quite as terrible of a thought as it had once been. At first, when he had approached Eik, it had been in order to confirm the rumors he had heard about the man during an anonymous supply run to a town on the outskirts of Alliance territory.

Apparently, this guy from a fresh world had managed to climb the ranks at unprecedented speed. Further digging had revealed even more interesting tidbits, such as the fact that he had, somehow, created a medicine that could do what regular potions did, with the crucial difference that their effects never faded, and stacked seemingly endlessly.

This meant that, in theory, and given enough time, Eik Magnasen could become a being who wielded infinite power.

And if anything could lure in Chop the Blade, certified battle addict, then it was the prospect of infinite power.

That had been why he initially sought out the creative alchemist. For power and nothing else. But with time, his sojourn had turned into more than just a quest for power, although it had taken him some time to come to that realization.

The wait for Eik to complete the recipe for the grade 7 potion and actually create it successfully had naturally led Chop to hang around with him and his family, and that had forced him to experience something again which he had deliberately been avoiding. He had thought that companionship and familial bonds were something he had lost for good when his family had been killed, but apparently, he had just needed a break of a few hundred years.

Naturally, nothing could replace the love he still felt for his wife and children, but that didn’t mean these new connection weren’t meaningful. And perhaps his newfound feelings for Eik, Ihasu, Rasmus, the kids, and the rest of them were a better and healthier way to deal with the emptiness in his heart than endless slaughter.

But that didn’t mean he wasn’t going to thoroughly enjoy these moments of fun.

With the grade 7 Potion of Mighty Strength, it was as if he had been granted a new body and was fighting for the first time. It was heavenly.

“Chop!”

As his crystal blade blazing with azure flames cleaved more than a dozen spawn in twain with a single, fell stroke, he turned and caught glimpse of Andihar emerging from behind a smoking ruin that had once been a large building more than three stories tall.

The large man looked as if he had just taken a shower in pure blood. His armor was chipped, cracked, and dented in places, but the crystal cover had nearly repaired it completely already. A mystery to all who had fought alongside him, his hair was somehow still immaculately pristine and smooth.

With a single, vicious movement that carved a deep groove into the ground, Chop whipped his blade clean of ichor and strolled over to meet the muscular elf. “What is it?”

Andihar offered a salute to the X-ranker—a gesture mirrored by the nine subordinates tailing him. “I believe we have this area under control for now, but I have received reports of several hot spots where our people are pressured beyond their limits,” he said and pulled out a large map from his bag of holding.

Chop stood at his side to get a look.

“We will open a fracture that can withstand your power and send you to a destination point around here,” Andihar said and stabbed a finger at the eastern outskirts of a large city. Chop didn’t know where it was, and nor did he particularly care. The elf dragged his finger toward the center. “Please move westward. You should quickly encounter a group fighting spawn there. Can I ask you to aid them and assist them in rescue efforts?”

“A hundred thousand,” the old man said, taking a seat on a block of rubble to wait for the fracture specialist to open the portal, blade secured in his crossed arms.

“Excuse me?” the elf asked with confusion.

“I want a hundred thousand credits for the trouble.”

“I—” Andihar stumbled, blinking. “That is completely acceptable. I earn at least ten times that in a day now.”

The old geezer frowned at that. “I want fifty million, then.”

“Please, Chop,” the elf said, a faint smirk on his lips as he pointed out where he wanted the fracture placed. “You gave me a number and I accepted. The deal has been made.”

Chop the Blade spat on the ground with a scowl as he stepped through the fracture. “It was a displeasure doing business with you, you filthy scoundrel. Next one’s fifty million, I tell ya.”

Andihar bowed deeply, a smile plastered onto his face. “I can live with that. Thank you, Chop.”

Eik had been right.

Not two seconds after the Lord of the Moon had been swallowed up by Spaghetti Jones, a surge of might unlike anything the fiend had displayed before crackled through the serpent’s leviathan body.

Moments later, pitch black parasites swarmed the membranous, azure tissue, numbering in the trillions. While their spread through Spaghetti Jones’ body could hardly be called swift, the fact that they were at all capable of moving through a beast whose internal power made for a such a highly pressured environment was a testament to the power of their origin.

Present inside the Profound Toxin that made up the serpent, Eik felt the swarm instantly. Worryingly, anywhere they made contact with the Profound Toxin, which was everywhere, they seemed to somehow break down the integrity of the blue substance and convert the potent energy contained within to vital energy for them to absorb.

Eik didn’t doubt that if they were allowed to return to their master, all of that vitality would be dutifully transferred to him.

Luckily, it wasn’t as if the parasites could instantly break down Profound Toxin, and they perished under the assault of the deadly liquid in more cases than not. But with himself as a central source of infestation, the Lord of the Moon could disgorge them faster than they could be killed.

Through his connection to the Profound Toxin within the Lord’s system, Eik could tell that while that was also being broken down into vitality by the fiend’s absorptive nature, it wasn’t happening fast enough to counteract the damage caused first.

But densely surrounded by his own parasitic horde, he was shielded from the toxin that would otherwise have flooded his wound while he was inside a beast made entirely of the stuff.

The damned bastard was taking this as an opportunity to recover from his previous injury.

Fuck that.

Mentally sectioning off the part of Spaghetti Jones’ body that held the Lord and his parasites, Eik conjured a solid sphere of hyper reinforced crystal before Accelerating everything within.

Spaghetti Jones, who had been jetting across the skies of Toxin, was momentarily thrown off course and nearly brought to the ground by the sudden force of the internal detonation, even contained by the spherical barrier as it was.

Immediately, the shield melted and allowed liquid toxin to flow in and fill out the void. But while the parasites had been obliterated, the Lord was nowhere to be seen either.

How?

Still floating around within the serpent via Toxic Liquefaction, Eik spun his metaphorical head frantically. Where the hell had the bastard gone?

Spaghetti Jones roared as a razor-sharp wave of undiluted darkness abruptly tore through the air from above, severing the serpent’s impossibly massive body completely through. The wave continued down and chopped into the earth and splitting it apart as if with a cosmic butcher’s cleaver.

Profound Toxin poured out of the mountain-sized wounds, the sturdy membrane that normally allowed Spaghetti Jones to literally dive through bedrock without spilling even a single drop of the deadly ichor ruined to the point where it was flooding the landscape below.

With Eik’s formless self still within the head, the leviathan writhed and crashed to the ground, leveling forests and mountains alike.

A second wave of darkness descended, even faster than the first, and much, much larger. It was more than enough to obliterate the serpent’s head in its entirety. But this time Eik saw it coming.

He Accelerated the hundred meters or so of Spaghetti Jones’ severed head and neck, the violent pulse of destruction that resulted meeting the darkness in a region-ending explosion that rocked the realm and sent shock waves across the entire continent.

In the sky above, the Lord was thrown back by the force. Eik, who had placed his consciousness in a flying Profound Toxic beast near the stratosphere, flowed smoothly into his human form and hurled over a dozen javelins at the fiend at full force, his glowing feathers whirling about his body.

Booming as they forcefully displaced the air, the first and second javelins hammered into the Lord of the Moon below and knocked him off course and down toward the ground, but the rest of them were avoided entirely.

Shit. Just a little more, and then he only got a single damned level.

Trailing the Lord’s path as he moved at high speed, sheets of blackness were drawn across the world as if it was paint splattered upon a canvas.

That would not do.

In a violent interception before too much of the realm could be darkened, the two titans of power clashed.

With each stroke of his crystalline katana, the weapon melted into liquid to assault any wounds on the Lord while another blade was conjured for the next slash. Wielding the same muddled rod of black that he had been using since their first battle, the Lord seemed to be growing irritated.

A pulse of aura forced Eik back for a moment, but that was all the Lord needed. “Enough.”

Squirming darkness poured out of him and enveloped his body completely before forming into something that resembled carapace. Completely devoid of light reflective properties, it was difficult even for Eik’s eyes to tell exactly what it was, but the sharp, layered protrusions made it resemble the armor of a beetle.

The surface of it undulated as if it was alive.

Goosebumps overwhelmed Eik as he was hit by the hideous sensation of the thing’s aura washed over him. It felt like a billion insects crawling on his skin and inside his flesh. And the depth of it was incomparable to before.

“I was hoping for a worthy adversary, but… alas.”
