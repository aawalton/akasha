Cultivation Realms: More will be added as the story progresses.

Each major realm has three minor realms. Such as Imperfect Heaven 1st/2nd/3rd realm. Each of these correspond to one of the minor realms. For example, Imperfect Heaven 2nd Realm for Body Cultivation is Meridian Building. Note, that the Imperfect Heaven 1st Realm may also simply be referred to as the 4th realm.

Each minor realm has 12 stages. Some group these stage into stages of three. Like, stages 1-3 are Early, 4-6 are Median, 7-9 are Late, and 10-12 are Peak.
