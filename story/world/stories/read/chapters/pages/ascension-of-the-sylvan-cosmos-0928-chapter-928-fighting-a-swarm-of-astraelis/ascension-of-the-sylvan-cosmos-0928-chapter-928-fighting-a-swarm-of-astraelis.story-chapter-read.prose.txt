So, they had to strategize to slowly cut down the herd of 40 beasts. For that, they used the starships, since they were the closest match in size.

The Mothership attacked the BOSS Monster with its cannon, a beam of light bridging the distance between the creature and them. The gigantic space creature, resembling a long-limbed, armored hamster, took the attack like nothing and roared back silently. The silent burst from its mouth caused the surrounding ternary essence to ripple.

Seraphina and the others left the ship and spoke to the 60 elites behind them. “As planned, we will take care of the big one. Coordinate with the rest, and 30 of you will each take the two Mini Bosses. As for the rest, let the starships and the army slowly tire them out.”

“<Harmonia Sylvan Chaos Transformation>.”

Seraphina and Obsidianis reached a similar height, their bodies much woodier as their elements coated them like armor.

Tempestria and Seraphina took the lead as the frontal melee fighters, and each of them chanted their skills.

“<Endless Tempest Domain>.”

“<Lightbloom Knight Genesis>.”

Tempestria’s body was instantly coated in a massive, yet perfectly controlled storm. Her Wind Aether Mana merged and summoned dark clouds around her body, her grey-colored eyes leaking faint energy.

Meanwhile, Seraphina’s body was coated in deep light while the world opened around her and hundreds of armored tree knights manifested in all kinds of shapes and forms. Seraphina followed it up swiftly.

“<Radiant Formation>.”

“Go.”

Like white streaks in a black canvas, the hundreds of knights rushed forward toward the gigantic <Astraelis>.

Verdantia’s voice boomed out from behind. “Not so fast. <Source Root Entangling>.”

Crraaaack…

SNAP!

Damage numbers appeared in Seraphina’s eyes one after another, but none exceeded one <Phoer Star Essence>, causing her to click her tongue.

Obsidianis's husky and charming voice reached them from behind the creature. “Hehe. With those attacks, you are just tickling it, Seraphina.”

Manifesting from the void and tightly gripping her giant scythe, Obsidianis swung.

Dark elements coated her, causing even the stars around her to lose their shine. “<Umbra Reaper>.”

A black line, so dark it stood out against the already obscure background, sliced across the giant beast, making it roar in pain.

[-45,120,000,000]! (-451,200)! (-4.5)!

Cinderielle’s cold voice spread out. “You both are too relaxed.”

Harmonixia’s melody enveloped Cinderielle, and as the massive star larger than the <Astraelis> lunged forward, it swelled and then multiplied three times.

Tempestria, who was about to close the distance, cursed lowly. “Warn us before you throw your skill! Do you want to kill us together with the BOSS?”

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

WHOOSH!

Crack… crack…

BANG!

If you’ve learned your lesson, go back and begin preparing to drag these along.”
