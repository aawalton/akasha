Heya all, I just wanted to make this post as a huge public thank you to MelasDelta, the author of the popular webfiction Salvos. Now, some of you might wonder what is the point of this, why make a whole post as a thank you note. Well, you see - those that wonder the point of this post - there is a magnitude of reasons but one is gratitude! The New Cover is fucking amazing too and deserves its own post, but also gratitude. Lots and lots of gratitude, for you see people. Long long long - five months ago - I was nothing but a college student with no dreams and aspirations.

Well, maybe I am overexaggerating, I just mean to say that thanks to MelasDelta I managed to pursue a career in writing and it has been the best time of my life. Now, I understand that that might sound a bit odd and make some people skeptical, but he served as a mentor someone that knew the ropes and taught them to me, moreover not just that but also supported my career to make it. If it weren't for his time and effort I don't think this fiction would be anywhere near as popular or even exist in the first place. I don't think I would've even finished my first book of Grim Reaper, let alone Blair whose second book is getting close to its end.

Hence, I want to do a big thank you to MelasDelta, for his writing being an inspiration in the form of Salvos, since if it weren't for Salvos I don't think I would be writing LitRPG or gotten the desire to write again in the first place to begin with. It spurred me to do better and make something. But also, the new cover, and almost all of Blair's covers were commissioned by him. He spent money that he shouldn't have spent just to help me succeed, and I think that speaks magnitudes of his character. To be honest when some people began to call me an alt account I felt flattered, since he is like my writing idol. I just wanted to say this publicly since I think you all should check out his work, and he is also just an amazing person all around!
