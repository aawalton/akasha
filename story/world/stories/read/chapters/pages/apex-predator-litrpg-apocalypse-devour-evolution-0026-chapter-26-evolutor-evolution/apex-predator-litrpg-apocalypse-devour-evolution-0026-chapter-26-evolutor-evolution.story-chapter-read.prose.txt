[Your performance in the tutorial pleases us. At the advent of reaching level 10 in your race, you are given a chance to upgrade your race to a superior form. For humans, this means a simple attribute increase as they ascend from F grade to E grade. Each increase in grade does additionally give an overall boost in capacity. This is not measured in attributes, but it is something the System governs.]

Don read intently as he sat down in the myriad corpses of the Grimoids he had killed. The System was getting chatty.

[But you are no longer human. You are an Evolutor and this pleases us. An Evolutor is a special-grade species which can evolve into multiple directions. Your choices are affected by your performance in the tutorial so far, but not limited to it. Your choice will affect future ability/skill/evolution and class options.]

Don nodded. Seemed simple enough. He was eager to see what kinds of options he had. He mentally ‘scrolled down’ and saw the first option.

[Evolutor Hunter:] The original choice of the Evolutors if their progenitors have not acquired genetic material. This was the path of the First Prime. Versatile, a balance between speed and strength. This style of predator hunts by chasing down its prey and delivering swift and efficient violence. Upgrades revolve around fangs, claws and musculature. +1 Skill Slot, +1 to Agility, +1 to Power, +1 to Constitution, +3 Free Points per level.

This was just… solid. It basically did what Don was already doing, and specialized in it. It was definitely cookie cutter, but it was a solid option. The increased attributes were balanced and a great addition.

The notion of a skill slot was interesting too. Don had been wondering why he hadn’t gotten a skill such as [Acrobatics] from jumping from tree to tree. It seemed that the amount of skills one could have was limited. Don was lucky to have acquired useful skills for his style preferred combat. He was a little annoyed that this wasn’t something he had the chance to know before already committing to choices.

I guess this is called a tutorial because here we learn this stuff…

Don scrolled down and read the next option.

[Evolutor Webstalker:] After the first Evolutor Prime gathered genetic material from a thousand planets, in search of perfecting its race, it repeatedly ran into variants of the spider. The First Prime consumed millions of spiders and obtained perfected versions of the spiders weapons: speed, poison, traps. This sub-species of Evolutor specializes in setting up a trap and quickly ambushing its prey when it is tangled. +1 Skill Slot, +2 to Agility, +1 to Mental, +3 Free Points per level.

This was certainly an interesting choice. Don could see many benefits in being able to use web. Not just for trap laying, but also for mobility as spiders were wont to do. However, he did not like the idea of laying low and waiting for something to wander into his traps. Spiders who hunted like this were really efficient using limited resources. They would be perfectly still for hours or even days until something got caught in their web. Then they would pounce. However, this style of hunting was not something the System would favor. The System favored excess. Don needed to adapt a style that was as fast at killing and devouring things as possible. So this was unfortunately a skip, as much as Don could appreciate the web, not that speed and poison was bad either.

[Evolutor Dominator:] A rare variant, only acquired recently by the Evolutor race. The Seventh Prime managed to Devour a solar system inhabited by psychic elves. This subspecies hunts via the use of psychic powers. While not directly as powerful as the more physical variants, the Dominators abilities are more subtle. There are many specializations in this subspecies. Mind-altering charms, psychokinetic paralysis and blindness or brute force telekinesis among the options. The Primes are waiting for someone to perfect this form. Warning: Choosing this option will permanently alter the trajectory of your racial evolution. However, with this risk, comes high potential. +1 Skill Slot, +1 Agility, +1 Energy +3 Mental, +3 Free Points per level

Damn… This sure was something. It was more of a caster based option. Don wasn’t sure what to think about it. On one hand it would be really awesome to be able to use telekinesis and make his enemies blind and afraid with just his mind. On the other hand, he just had so little information. This was also a risk, because it would lock him into a trajectory. This option did give more Attribute points than the other options, namely Mental, which was for obvious reasons the primary attribute for this variant.

The Dominator’s style was quite a bit different than what he had been opting for lately. But in truth, Don wouldn’t necessarily mind a more subtle option. Sure, he enjoyed the hell out of these brutal fights that he got into. But pain and struggle was still pain and struggle, and having an option to… not have to fight skin, tooth and nail every fight was definitely alluring.

Don instinctively turned his head to look at the dark pond below. Whatever he chose, he needed to make sure it aligned with his goal of killing the boss. The Dominator could certainly give him options.

Don was unsure about the major shift that the Dominator subspecies would entail in his trajectory. But on the other hand he needed to hem his apprehension. He also, personally, needed to evolve. He needed to keep growing and turning into something more and more that could not only survive but also thrive in the System. And the Dominator was a powerful option. Don would not let fear dictate. He would consider this option. But for now he moved on.

[Evolutor Reaper:] The Sixth Prime was the progenitor of this form. This form was not born of a single hunt or a planet devoured. The Reaper was perfected over millenia of growth. The Sixth Prime loved the hunt and would always prolong it. The Reaper subspecies evolved for attrition and efficiency. Bleed, infect, weaken, kill. This subspecies specializes in poisons, infections, bleeds, weakening effects and durability. To be offered this form is a privilege. Warning: Choosing this option will permanently alter the trajectory of your racial evolution. However, with this risk, comes high potential. +1 Skill Slot, +2 Constitution, +2 Agility, +1 Mental, +3 Free Points per level.

Another heavy hitter. This one was also considerably more powerful than the previous options, barring the Dominator. It was really the choice between those two. The spider-style was simply not a good option for his circumstances. The Hunter was a straight up upgrade, but it would keep Don the same. The Dominator and the Reaper represented what the System wanted of him: evolution. He would have to choose in which way to grow.

Would he continue on a more physical path, but focusing on damage over time effects, sapping the life force of his enemies. He could see this being very good in bringing powerful foes down. If his attacks could poison or accelerate bleeding, he could use the hit and run tactics he had developed while hunting the bears.

But on the other hand, he could become something else entirely with the Dominator option. The Reaper would always be straight forward and one-dimensional. He was sure he would get ranged options, but basically it would be limited to debuffs and damage over time effects.

The Dominator, however, was an unknown. A black box of potential. It was impossible to say what kinds of abilities he could get. Don could imagine myriad different ways psychic powers could be used. Telekinesis was so versatile. He could throw objects with it, close and open doors remotely and maybe even help pin down enemies if he could affect them directly. Not to mention all the illusions and the ability to cause blindness.

But would he need all of this trickery and subtlety if he could just inject someone with overwhelmingly powerful venom?

What if something is immune to poisons and bleeds? Well, I suppose there are bound to be things immune to mind-fuckery.

The bottom line was that both of these options were very powerful, but neither of them was quite aligned with his brutal ambush-tactics and physical power. It was a fighting style Don had started to perfect recently, but it was clear to him that he had to change. He had to evolve into something more. Otherwise he would be hunting bears until kingdom come.

“I wish I knew more,” Don muttered to himself. “Is there a way for me to learn what kinds of trajectories these options have?”

There was a silence, then Don heard a static that intensified. It felt like something was closing in on him, encasing him under pressure. The pressure wasn’t directed at him. It just was there, because the entity was there. Don didn’t see what it was. He was immediately pressed into the platform.

A delicate voice of a woman spoke. It was deep and sultry and clearly educated.

“Oh, darling,” it said. “I was worried you would never ask…”
