The idea was simple. Take a portal into an active dungeon, remove its thread without cutting it, and then take that thread and force Falzyxi’s Source through it. That way, it should siphon the void being over time until it reached the critical limit where killing the intruder would be possible. This would make the dungeon difficult, perhaps even turning into the most difficult dungeon Earth had ever seen, but Luke had confidence in the people he had gathered to deal with it, far more confidence than in his own chances of success. If this enemy was anything like the void being that had taken an interest in him, it would not be a pushover. Not at all.

Any number of things could go wrong. The foremost of them was the fact that he had no idea what he was doing, or if it would even work. Connecting Falzyxi to a portal meant bridging the gap between it and Earth, in a way, and that hadn’t worked out so well last time, even against a being that would be like an ant compared to what was coming. There was also nothing that said connecting a void being to a portal was even possible, or that it would do what Luke hoped. Perhaps it would just blow everything up, or bleed Source into the dungeon, killing everything and everyone in it. According to Guide, the Source needed to be converted somehow and turned into mana, experience, and all that good stuff, but was that necessary when siphoning it? Luke’s gut told him it wasn’t. His gut told him this would work.

A confirmation or a few questions answered by Guide would have increased Luke’s confidence, but no matter how much he reached out, the damn entity wouldn’t answer. Had he fled? Was he working with The Greater System or The Council to help Earth? Maybe. Maybe not. Either way, they were on their own with this one. At least that meant Guide couldn’t complain afterwards. Eh, who was he kidding? Guide would complain no matter what.

It didn’t take long to locate the portal they had settled on using. It was out of the way and in New York, meaning there was no one around to get hurt if things blew up. Since very few Integrated remained in what had once been the country’s largest city, population-wise, portals were sparse and far between. Soon, when The Band and Phoenix Phalanx left, along with a scattering of free agents, no more portals would open at all. Now, it was nothing more than a desert with mountains of rubble. The perfect staging ground for what would be the first real battle against the void, with many more to come. If they survived.

He sensed the others’ presence near the portal and decided he had time to say hi, since he might not get another chance. Ever.

"Hey everyone," he called as he stepped out of the weave. "Hannah? You're here?"

She shrugged with a glint in her eye. "Popov didn't want my help."

"That's weird," Luke said before being wrapped in an embrace so strong it grew difficult to talk or even breathe. "Mateo," he choked.

"My friend! Mi hermano!"

It took some doing to untangle himself from Mateo and share greetings with everyone else. Penny explained about her new class, which was completely different from her old one, even though Luke didn't quite get it. Ray and Curtis stood off to the side, talking amongst themselves, while Nick and Vicky from Phoenix Phalanx, with Martin joining them, made sure to get a few words in. Priyah fidgeted next to Lemira and Elijah, who looked like they couldn't see anything in the whole world except each other. Kenny had joined in, giving the group a second healer. Good. They'd need all the healing they could get if Luke's hunch was right.

Once the greetings were taken care of, Luke explained again what he was going to attempt and what would be expected of the gathered twelve people, divided into two parties. Since he was nowhere near certain how things would turn out, there wasn't much in the way of specifics to get into. The dungeon would be difficult, the most dangerous one they had ever attempted, but they had to complete it and claim Falzyxi's Source, or at least some of it, or Earth would pay the price, all their futures hanging in the balance.

The atmosphere wasn't as grave as he had expected. Instead, they joked and laughed. Even with the mental block gone, the Integrated in front of him had proven their resilience again and again. Risking their lives was just another day for them at this point. He hoped they would all get through this one.

"It's coming," Luke said, looking into the gray sky. Falzyxi didn't approach from up there, or from any physical direction, but tilting his head up, as if looking into space, felt right. "I'll need to go meet it."

Hannah grabbed the back of his head and pulled him down into a kiss. The crowd all joined in a cheer, putting some color into Luke's face. "Go on, then," she said with a grin.

"Yeah, well," he began, clearing his throat. "Bye."

Stepping into the weave again, bringing his physical body along, he made sure to trace the thread running from the portal as he assigned the available attribute points from his level up into Willpower, raising it to a total of 91. Every single point would be important in the fight that was coming his way, and it was fast approaching.

Luke: "Ready?"

Popov: "It is a giant. Can't you feel it?"

Popov: "You need to abandon your scheme."

Luke: "It'll work. If nothing else, it'll weaken Falzyxi. You might be able to finish it off together if we don't make it."

Popov: "It will be too late then, and my disciples will be exhausted. Falzyxi is not alone."

Luke: "Disciples, is it?"

Not bothering to reply, Luke knew Popov was in position to help, and that he would. The man was correct, though. Falzyxi was far from alone, and the void being's entourage had arrived. The weave came under siege in ten places at once, then ten more. Void creatures were everywhere, and since the main attraction hadn't arrived yet, Luke raised the hood of his robe and got right into action.

Dimitrios: "What are you doing? Falzyxi is coming."

Dimitrios: "You'll just make yourself tired."

Luke: "I'm just warming up. I'll be ready."

The first site was just a single void creature, one without much development. Having had some practice in revealing their inner Source, killing it required no more than a push of will to shatter his opponent's resistance. That allowed a single thread inside its mass. After that, all he had to do was sever a channel. Once the integrity of whatever held the Source in place was compromised, the void did the rest and consumed its spawn. Luke had time to wonder what happened to the Source. He doubted it just disappeared forever, before a force pushed against his mind, blanking it for a moment before he regained control. Even as he watched, the weave rippled like waves, as if a huge freighter had just driven by on the open sea. Falzyxi had come.

Ray: "Your girlfriend just fell unconscious. Penny too. Guessing the big bad is here?"

Luke: "Wake them and get ready to go into the portal when I say go!"

Luke: "Drag them in if you have to!"

Ray: "Yeah, yeah."

Luke shifted his position in the weave and staggered back at the sheer enormity of the being in front of him. Falzyxi's mass obscured even the void behind it. He had expected something that looked like a monster, perhaps another cosmic-horror type creature, but this thing was nothing like that. Rather than tentacles, horns, infinite mouths, or glaring red eyes, it was just a... worm. Kind of. It wriggled with what looked like glee at having arrived at a feast, but there was nothing there that Luke could sense, no intelligence, not even malice. Just hunger. Bottomless hunger. And Falzyxi fed. Oh, how it fed! Not in a conventional way. Instead, the weave just bulged inward before the end seam snapped and threads began to disintegrate around the being, as its sheer presence was enough to destroy and consume. Luke felt the pull even on himself, on his own thread as it was connected to the weave. Given even a little time, Falzyxi would feast on them all.

Dimitrios: "Come on, then!"

The Mythos was next to Falzyxi, battling against the endless stream of smaller void creatures coming in Falzyxi's wake.

Popov: "I'm going to need a little time. Almost everyone lost consciousness."

Luke: "You'd better hurry."

Luke: "I'm going in."

Even from a distance, Luke could feel the enormity of Falzyxi's will. This thing, this monster, didn't have much in the way of intelligence, but that didn't make it less dangerous. If anything, it was worse. There would be no tricking this opponent. All it wanted was to eat, and its focus on that task was like an unstoppable force. Resigning himself to becoming the immovable object, Luke hurried forward and placed himself in Falzyxi's path. He focused on keeping his own thread from shredding, and used The Healer's Moment. There was no pulling punches in this fight. Time froze, but the effect shattered under the weight of Falzyxi's enormity before Luke had reached far with a thread.

"Dammit," he muttered, and continued to push the thread forward, allowing it to reach farther by spending mana. In this battle, there would be little need of that particular resource anyway, at least if it went according to plan.

The massive void worm's approach put in reach, and even being in its presence was like struggling to swim out of a whirlpool about to pull you under. Gritting his teeth, Luke pushed on. His own thread fell apart, and he had to try again, then again. Each failed attempt to even reach Falzyxi meant it got to feast on a little more of the weave, which could mean the deaths of thousands. This was not the time to mess around.

The weave shuddered under the weight of Falzyxi's advance, threads coming apart and disappearing without any sort of resistance. Clenching his fists, Luke put more of his will behind the idea of keeping his thread stable, keeping himself stable. Dimitrios was having trouble fighting as well. The pressure was getting to him. With the Mythos forced back, Luke pushed forward. There would be no second chances here, no chance to regroup and try again. This was it. His one chance. Luke's thread reached Falzyxi without falling apart.

Surprise registered in the enormous void being, a brief flicker of a recognizable emotion, before the hunger came. That all-consuming need to feed on anything and everything rose up and crushed Luke like an ant.

Gasping, Luke pulled back on reflex, but it was no use. With that one thread, he had connected himself to Falzyxi and brought the being's full attention upon him. Rather than rebuffing the advance, the enemy had welcomed it, because it was another thing for it to consume. Just as planned. Sort of.

Luke: "Now, Popov!"

Popov: "I need more time."

Luke: "There is no more time!"

The force of this thing's will was like a natural disaster, like an avalanche crashing over Luke and burying him. Mind threatening to blank out again, he pushed back with everything he was worth, but it wouldn't be enough. Not even close. So much for creating a diversion. Knowing he didn't have much time, Luke brought forth the portal thread and slammed it into Falzyxi, along with several more threads. Weaver's Bulwark worked overtime to keep Luke alive as he focused on pushing forward rather than defending himself. It was not a move to sacrifice himself or anything noble like that. He just needed the attack to work more than anything else. This was his gamble, and he had wagered far more than his own life that this would work.

The portal thread held. At first, Falzyxi didn't react to it at all, which wasn't the sort of response Luke had hoped for. What he needed was for the enemy to divert its attention away from Luke's own thread. Or was it? His head was growing dim. Difficult to think. The furious push of the void being's will was like taking a sledgehammer to the head over and over again. Hard to remember. Another thread. This one held too. No, it fell apart just after reaching inside Falzyxi. What was Luke supposed to do in there again? A glimmer. Source. Right, that was it. The Source.

Popov made good on his promise then. Chains of green and purple light wrapped themselves around the worm and squeezed as perfect circles full of runic symbols popped into existence to deliver furious streams of fire, ice, and energy. None of it hurt Falzyxi in any meaningful way, but it didn't have to. A smaller void creature surged forward and wrapped its tentacles around Luke, but it seemed so small compared to the powerful entity in front of him. With nothing more than Source on his mind, he killed the charging little beast and pushed another thread into Falzyxi, focusing his willpower to keep it from being consumed. Reaching, slowly inching forward to keep the thread from falling apart while staying alive himself, Luke's will crawled at a snail's pace, but it didn't stop. Stopping meant failure, and failure was not an option. The distance between his thread and the Source grew smaller and smaller without the destroyer of worlds noticing, because the weave had just turned into a battlefield.

Integrated popped into existence to help Dimitrios, who had resumed his fighting against the void creatures while Luke did his best trying to stay alive. Those new, juicy humans were far more interesting than Luke, and he felt Falzyxi's will reaching for them, its massive bulk shifting. Just like that, it caught a woman Luke didn't recognize, and she didn't even have time to react before her body fell apart, consumed by the worm. It reached for another snack, and that's when Luke struck. His thread touched the Source. Falzyxi reacted in an instant, as expected, but it would do it no good. The moment Luke connected with the Source, he touched that same thread to the portal thread, bridging the gap between them before letting go.

Luke: "Hold it now!"

Popov: "My people are perishing! They must pull back!"

Luke: "Hold Falzyxi!"

Source streamed from the creature into Luke's thread and over to the portal thread, but it was slow, far too slow. As if that wasn't enough, it headed in both directions. He hadn't cut the thread, since that would disconnect it from the dungeon or the portal. Instead, he had patched in the thread carrying Falzyxi's source. What he wanted was for it to go toward the dungeon, not the portal, since that might destroy it. Luke was forced to block the Source from going in both directions while keeping it going in the other. Using nothing more than his now-exhausted will, he made it follow the thread leading away from the battle, toward the dungeon. Blocking the slow-moving Source from going the other way proved even more difficult, but he managed it without losing his grip.

Luke: "Go! Go now!"

Ray: "Geez. You're being a bit dramatic, don't you think? We're going."

The glowing stuff of life itself continued along the thread into the distance, disappearing while Falzyxi struggled to cut it. Despite his opponent being a master of will-craft, something about its Source draining made it panic and not wield it with the same sharpness. Even blunted, it took everything in Luke's power to keep it from consuming the Source-filled thread. Allowing that meant everything up until that point would have been in vain. It wouldn't be long now before Source poured into the portal, and he hoped it would continue to flow on its own at that point because it was getting more than difficult to defend it from the increasingly frantic strikes of the void worm Falzyxi while willing the Source to keep flowing.

Luke had been worried he wouldn't know when it was time for the next step in his plan. It turned out he need not have worried. The weave shifted, then shuddered, tearing at itself as if under duress when the Source finished its long path and started pouring into the dungeon. Falzyxi shrieked and thrashed as most of its massive stores of Source left it and the void pushed in, reclaiming the void worm just like Luke had hoped. What he hadn't expected was a construct of will springing into existence in the metaphysical realm of the weave and the void. It enveloped not only Falzyxi itself, but Luke as well, trapping him in there with the enemy. As the last remnants of Source left the void being, Luke felt its consciousness fading, its attempts at severing the threads growing weaker as it perished.

Falzyxi grabbed hold of Luke's consciousness. Its will was like a vice. It was impossible to pull free, no matter how much he tried. No, his opponent wasn't just buying time to keep from dying, he realized. The enemy had not given up. Far from it. Rather than letting the Source go, it was following it through the thread. Its consciousness was following right behind the Source, unwilling to let it go and doom itself. The unstoppable hunger was still there, and it almost felt as if it was eager, ready for another meal. This thing had no intention of dying, and it was heading to the dungeon where Luke had just sent his friends. The challenge for them would go from the most difficult dungeon ever to impossible, with nothing more than a sliver of a chance for survival. Unacceptable. Unacceptable!

He had seen how Falzyxi slithered away, slipping its consciousness into the thread along with the last of the Source. Before it was too late, Luke followed. If that damn thing was going into the dungeon, then so was he.
