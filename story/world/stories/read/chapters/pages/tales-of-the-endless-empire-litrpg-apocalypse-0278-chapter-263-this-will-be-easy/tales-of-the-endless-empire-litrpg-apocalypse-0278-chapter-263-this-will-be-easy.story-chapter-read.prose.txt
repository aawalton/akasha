Kael hadn’t even charged it, a mistake he would not repeat.Charged arrows moved like lightning, their force magnified tenfold. Not even advanced armor like Thalion’s could hold against one. Close combat would have to do.Engaging up close carried its risks. If Thalion had a escape token, a well-placed arrow might not be enough. A single second was all it took to vanish.
