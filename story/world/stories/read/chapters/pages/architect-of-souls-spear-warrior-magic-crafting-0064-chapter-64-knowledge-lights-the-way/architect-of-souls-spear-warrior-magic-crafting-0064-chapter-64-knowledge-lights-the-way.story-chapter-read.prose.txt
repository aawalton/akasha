Serena nodded, going along with his plan to perform more tests on Jade’s healing instead of working on Acid Resistance.

“And this food is involved?” She picked up one of the chopped carrots and tossed it to Jade, who rippled happily at the treat.

“Yes,” Ferris nodded. “Mainly, I would like us to injure Jade to the point that the slime ranch does when harvesting fertilizer. I believe watching this from inside the slimespace will help me better understand the healing process. I would also like to test feeding and hurting Jade afterwards, while they are still recovering.”

Serena spent a couple of seconds silently watching him. Ferris wondered what she was thinking. This was a more drastic injury than he had been willing to allow last time, and he hoped he wasn’t giving too much away with his requests.

“Very well,” she said, walking over to inspect the axe more closely.

Ferris unwound the magical chain from the cage around Jade’s core, wrapping the end around his waist. He lay on the floor with the slime next to him, barely touching with his hand from the top. The core was pulled to the side, making it easier to chop the slime’s body in half. There would be a lot of slime fertilizer falling out, and Ferris tried to make it easy for Serena to avoid him.

Serena and Ferris agreed to use the same signals as last time. She would follow the same procedure as last time, giving a piece of food first and waiting for signals, but the cuts she made would be as long and as deep as possible. He specifically asked for cuts with different angles.

After five of these cuts, Serena would use the axe to chop off as much of Jade’s body as possible. Hopefully, the axe was thick enough not to dissolve before the separated half fell apart, but they would have to see. Ferris was still a bit queasy about the idea, but more confident after seeing Jade heal so many times.

At this point, they expected Jade to fall unconscious. They would need a different procedure, as the slime would no longer be able to coordinate. Instead, they agreed on a set time to wait between each test.

With a plan in place, Ferris started to meditate. It was a bit worrying to think that Serena would be swinging the axe so close to his hand—and Jade’s core. She may only have one hand, but he still trusted her control… at least intellectually. Emotionally, he was a bit more concerned. The magical chain’s cage should protect Jade’s core.

Eventually, Ferris found the calm of meditation and entered the slimespace. He was standing on top of the Heartfire tower, looking out over the yellow spires towards the dome’s walls.

First, Ferris picked up the dark green core from its pedestals.

Cautiously, he approached one of the spires that extended out from the edge of the tower’s wall. There were ants around, as usual for every spire. He’d generally given them space, afraid of provoking an attack. Even if his theory about the ants being tied to Jade’s memories was true, it didn’t mean they wouldn’t have basic instincts to attack intruders. They hadn’t been hostile while the scarabs were attacking, but that might have just been due to the larger current threat.

Ferris was prepared to jump backwards at any sign of aggression, but the ants ignored him as he slowly walked up and touched the side of the spire. Calmly, he climbed as far up as he could to look out on the slimespace. He had a reasonable, if distant, view of the Core Tree’s tower, the birds’ higher tower, the Consume Pool, and the Digest River. The river was full of qi chunks, although he couldn’t see them very well from here.

Finding a good spot in the spire’s exterior, he wedged the core in as well as he could. He planned to watch the entire flooding from this point—the highest he could find. If the acid overflowed the top of his tower, he would immediately try to exit the slimespace. He wasn’t sure if he would have time, but it was the best plan he could come up with.

With a thought to Jade over the bond, they signaled Serena to start the first piece of food. Ferris easily spotted it discolor the wall to the left of him. With another signal, Serena made a deep, long cut. The wall of the dome opened up with a massive gash, and a flood of acid rushed in, but the damage didn’t extend higher than about a two-story-tall building on the wall—well below his tower.

Four more times Serena cut deeply into Jade, and Ferris saw the same results. The gashes in the walls appeared in different directions around him, but all were still around the ground level, well below the height of the towers. Serena may be cutting at different angles, but the cuts that appeared were all horizontal.

The ants would rally out of their spires in force after each cut, repairing the walls surprisingly quickly. It was impossible for him to see the details from so far away, but it only took about a minute to fully repair the wall.

In the real world, when Jade was injured, there were two parts. At first, the slime would bleed out acid, but then stop leaking almost immediately. A short time later—about the same time Ferris saw the wall be repaired—the slime’s outer layer would pull itself back together. This felt like a distinction of inner acid control, which could be done almost immediately, and healing, which took more time.

Bracing himself, he asked Jade to stretch their body out and signal Serena to perform the cut. The slime felt fine with the test, still not really understanding or caring about the concept of bodily harm.

The results of the chop echoed through the dome with a thunderous roar.

Dozens of large circular holes appeared all around Ferris, with giant streams of acid bursting in from each one. Looking around, he slowly counted thirty-six of them in total.

Immediately, a pattern was apparent. The holes were evenly spaced around the entire circumference of the dome. Ferris also noticed that the damage from the yellow scarabs was only in the area above and around the holes. The parts of the dome that had been pristinely restored all now had holes cut into them.

This supported the theory that the ants would only repair damage that went all the way through the wall, which Ferris had seen when digging out the Grow seed.

Hopefully, this also meant that hurting Jade would only ever damage the dome in these areas. If so, that would mean the entire dome wasn’t at risk of collapsing, as Ferris had feared. He knew slimes didn’t die from this level of damage, but seeing this helped calm some of those worries.

Acid flooded in a tidal wave across the ground. Many of the yellow spires—although not all—were completely submerged under the flow. He knew that quite a few of the entrances the ants guarded were now under acid. How many of the creatures were dying as the green liquid rushed down into their tunnels? Or had they retreated somewhere safe?

Thankfully for him, the rising level of acid stopped just short of the height of the towers.

After a few minutes, the waves settled down. The grey birds were flying above each section of the missing wall, but Ferris wasn’t close enough to see any details.

Despite the high level of acid, streams of ants flooded out of the yellow spires closest to each hole. Slowly, as if they were wounds closing in on themselves, the holes in the dome shrank. Thousands of ants swarmed and died along the edges. Well, Ferris thought, it can only be considered slow compared to the way Jade normally heals smaller cuts. The repair was happening a lot faster than would be reasonable for a wall in the real world.

After another few minutes, the dome’s wall was completely repaired. The inside of the dome in the slimespace, however, was still flooded.

Ferris knew that in the real world, slimes would heal quickly after any injury, but not regain their lost size until fed. While he believed the dome spiritually represented Jade’s outer body, it was clear that the dome’s size didn’t decrease when parts of the slime’s body were chopped off. In fact, the same thing happened when Jade ate a lot: they would swell larger in real life, but in the slimespace the dome never expanded. However, the Digest River would overflow after Jade overate.

The information Ferris learned from eating the Grow seed’s fruit implied that the dome’s size mattered. The ability would make the dome larger in the slimespace. Perhaps this was related to Jade’s ‘normal’ size. When excessively eating or injured, the slime was in a temporarily larger or smaller state. Later, they would eventually revert to this normal size. The Grow ability permanently increased Jade’s size… by expanding the dome?

Ferris spent about five minutes observing the flooded slimespace and confirmed that the flooding was receding. There was a slow motion to the liquid, headed over towards the drain at the end of the Digest River.

Cognizant of the time, Ferris prepared for the next test. Ten minutes after Jade fully healed their outer shell, Serena would feed Jade five more pieces of carrot, followed by a cut each time. He wanted to see how the Consume Pool and healing worked while Jade was unconscious.

This was a point of confusion for Ferris. He knew that overfeeding Jade would overfill the Consume Pool, and then flood both the pool and Digest River. He’d previously linked the flooding to Jade’s increased size after eating too much.

But now, the slimespace was filled way past the point of typical overeating, and Jade was smaller than normal. He knew feeding them after they lost part of their body increased the slime’s size back to normal, but what did that mean in the slimespace?

Foreman Carter claimed that if a slime ate too much, they would throw up slime fertilizer, then go right back to eating. Perhaps Ferris would need to set that up as another experiment to observe in the slimespace to learn more.

As Ferris watched, grey birds circled, and a large chunk of giant carrot appeared above where the Consume Pool usually was. Ferris noted that the food appeared higher than the acid, but much closer to the surface than before. Perhaps the reason the food entered the slimespace so high up was to account for the flooding?

As soon as the vegetable hit the acid, ants began emerging from several spires that were poking up above the acid. It appeared their tunnels weren’t fully flooded. With their typical bravado, the ants threw themselves into the lake of acid and swam towards the food, ripping it apart with their mandibles.

Next, Ferris watched the dome where the cuts had appeared earlier. True to form, the grey birds were circling above the same spot. It was hard to see anything through the acid, but it looked like new holes were appearing in the wall. The ants would jump in from nearby spires, and the wall was rebuilt each time. The process appeared the same as before each time until the five tests were all finished—just with a lake of acid on the inside.

It was just a few chunks of carrot, but Ferris couldn’t see any appreciable change in the height of the acid. Perhaps the level had stopped lowering for a few seconds, but he didn’t notice it rising.

When Ferris had first excessively fed Jade, they had doubled in size in real life. The Consume Pool and Digest River had overflowed in the slimespace. Everything he had seen today implied that the two were not linked; Jade’s size in the real world was not related to the height of the acid level in the slimespace. Eating did increase the slime’s size, and it did flood the slimespace, but perhaps the two were not magically linked. Other events that flooded the slimespace did not affect Jade’s size.

So then… what did Jade’s size in real life relate to in the slimespace, if anything? Ferris could understand getting larger after eating, but then what happened when Jade shrank after digesting a meal? The slime didn’t expel anything. Was it correct to think of the return to normal size as digestion?

This also showed a new potential danger. It was likely very hard to fill the entire area inside the dome through feeding… but what if it happened anyway? What if Jade was injured to create this lake of acid inside the dome, and then fed food to raise the level of acid above the towers?

The food had stopped appearing, and Ferris sat for a couple of minutes, just looking out at the slimespace. The view was foreign, a lake of acid with sporadic yellow spires poking out above it, yet it had a beauty to it. There were many details he wanted to see closer, but couldn’t.

Ferris wanted to watch the acid level lower back down, but feared taking too much of Serena’s time. He decided to wake up to allow her to leave.

After returning the core to its pedestal and waking up, Ferris first examined Jade. They appeared normal, other than being half the size and unconscious.

There were still a good number of carrot chunks left. The axe appeared undamaged, apparently unharmed by chopping a slime in two. Ferris packed everything up in his bag.

“Thank you for the assistance,” Ferris said to Serena, picking Jade up.

“Just doing my part,” she said with a friendly wave. She grabbed her large sack from the corner, and they left the room together.

One step closer to their goals.
