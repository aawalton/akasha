The ping of notifications washed over me, as did the light. For a split second I wasn’t anywhere, and then I was through. The memory of the room where I had ranked up flashed through my mind, but I shoved it away.

I focused instead on my surroundings and ignored the pings.

The sky rippled with greens and purples, making me crouch down with my spear as the ground shook under my feet. It wasn’t a drastic movement, but tiny, like a hum. My radar flared all around, spreading from my arrival, but nothing showed up. After a second I inched forward, finding a low-lying wall in a massive circle around the portal, much like on the Clan world.

Beyond that wall, everything changed.

The green and purple light shone from a massive crack in the sky in the far distance. Mountains rose in the distance, and tiny trees covered one area, while another held a lake.

Spotted all over were bright white portals, which flickered like tiny stars all over the land in all directions. Some were close enough to be just a jump between them, while others were spaced out broadly.

A shadow crossed the land, causing me to glance up. I found a massive floating land mass, higher up then the crack in the far sky, lazily moving above me. Long ribbons of something reached down and touched the ground below as it drifted. At least six of them spread out across the floating thing.

Shaking my head, I turned to the notifications even as the crack drew my attention.

[You have devoured a level 199 Ivkhavu and gained the skill Marionette Mind. Marionette Mind: You gain control of a target’s body by using voice commands. With practice, you have the potential to gain absolute fine-motor control over the target's body, as if it were an extension of your own frame.]

[Warning: this skill is monitored by the system.]

I did not want this skill. It made my stomach roil just thinking about it.

[Do you want to merge this skill with Sovereign’s Call - V?]

Yes. System willing, it doesn’t screw up how I use it.

[You have merged Marionette Mind into Sovereign’s Call. Sovereign’s Call has evolved. Sovereign’s Thread: You project a powerful, resonant aura. Unpledged entities inside your radius are drawn toward you by a siren pulse. As they draw near, your aura tests their resolve. If your Fortitude exceeds their Willpower, you gain momentary control of their mind, gaining access to knowledge, and leaving them vulnerable. For any individual pledged to you, the Call acts as a command, pulling them to your side in battle.]

[Pledged allies: Dengu, Lenna, Strange, Kabi, Holls, John, Abby, Saevel, Vienna.]

I stared at the words floating in front of me for a few moments, then shook my head. This had changed the skill, giving me potential knowledge, and entangling me with my allies a bit more. That wasn’t how I wanted my path moving forward to go, but what was done was done.

I’d almost expected the allies thing to go away. Then again, I was working with a rather large team right now.

All of whom were waiting for me on the other side of the portal.

I took one quick look around the outside of the low wall, but again, nothing moved nearby on my radar. In the far distance that wasn’t the case, but this area appeared safe. Again, my eyes were drawn up to the crack. Something in the back of my mind tickled.

It reminded me of something important.

Shaking my head, I moved toward the portal, but it flared as Lenna stepped through.

“I was just…”

She held up a hand, and nodded before sticking her arm back. “The shuttle needs more space.”

We both hurried to the far side of the safe area.

Within a second, the shuttle appeared. It didn’t fly through the portal. One moment there was nothing, and the next, it was just there.

John waved from inside the nose, and the rear ramp lowered. Everyone piled out as I turned to check our surroundings once more.

“The air feels strange,” said Lenna with a frown, stepping up next to me. Her bow rested in her hands.

I took a moment to switch to my second sight and took a step back.

All of the mana in the air streamed toward the massive crack in the sky. Instead of mana, something else had replaced it, but I didn’t know what it was. At the same time, the world didn’t feel like when I had entered a higher or lower mana space. It remained comfortable.

“What does it feel like to you?” I asked, still staring at the green and purple light.

“Reminds me of being on a mountain top…” She sighed, and pressed her lips together for a moment. “Have you stepped out of the circle?”

“I will,” I said, as I moved to the opening.

“Be careful,” she mumbled.

I carefully stepped out from between the rocks, and nothing changed. The hum that echoed up through my boots increased a little, but not much.

Lenna watched me carefully as I shrugged. Then she stepped out, before stumbling back. She tried to speak to me, but her lips moved without anything coming out.

Actually, no sound made it out of the area surrounded by the low wall.

I stepped back inside as Vienna wrapped an arm around Lenna’s waist.

“What happened?” I asked, moving closer.

John moved from person to person, before heading our way.

“It’s dangerous, but not like a world that's a higher tier. Not precisely. It was more like the tier keeps shifting,” she said, looking between me and her mother.

Her mother stepped past me and the wall. She stood there for a few moments, with her eyes closed, then stepped back. “The world is unstable.”

“It’s Ever-Changing,” I added, being completely serious. The gate guardian had warned us about it.

John joined us and held out his hand. In it were three small ear pods.

I picked one up and placed it inside my ear. “How do these work?”

“You are, like, the third person to ask that,” said Abby, from all the way across the safe zone, though her lips didn’t move. “They just work.”

My eyes went wide as I glanced at John. “Please say this helps you level.”

He smiled. “Yes, it helps my profession.”

“It fits strangely in my ear,” said Kabi, one hand touching the side of his head, “But hearing everyone is such an advantage, I will get used to it.”

“You’ll all need to step outside the circle for that,” said Saevel. “I can handle the changes, but fighting in this could go wrong quickly if you aren’t prepared.”

I hadn’t noticed that he stood outside the low lying wall.

Strange darted in my direction and merged with the shadow at my feet with a chirp. He wanted to get going.

With that, several people popped outside the wall and then back. The humans weren’t as affected as Kabi or Lenna’s people. Dengu couldn’t even last five minutes before he started scratching at his face.

He stood pouting next to the shuttle, with Lenna speaking softly to him.

“John, you and Abby were going to stay here, right? Who else is staying?” I asked, glancing around the group.

Kabi stepped again outside the walled circle, and stood next to Saevel. Lenna joined him after hugging Dengu.

Both lasted longer than before.

Vienna stepped closer to me, and we both stepped out. She pointed at the small pinpricks of light. “What are those?”

“Portals,” I said with a frown. “But I don’t understand why there are so many of them…”

That’s when the humming coming up from the ground increased.

I triggered my second-sight as a blast of mana shot out of the jagged scar in the sky, bathing a section of the land in green and purple energy.

The tiny trees were torn from their roots as rock shot up into the air, creating jagged spires and canyons. All I could do was watch as the landscape changed. Destruction was wreaked on the entire area bathed in the green and purple light from above.

Fifteen minutes later, the forest had gone and a rocky canyon took its place. The lake drained inside it, leaving a flat steaming area in front of it.

“That…” muttered Kabi.

“Who could survive that?” asked Abby. That made everyone pause for a moment.

“Those who could react fast,” I said, trying to think about what I’d do if my surroundings started shifting like that. Probably fly into the air.

The others didn’t really have that option.

My gaze went up. “The high council is up there, above it all.”

The floating island with the massive chains hadn’t reacted at all to the shifting landscape below.

Energy pulsed inside me, and I wanted to explore this world. I wanted to see how people and creatures survived in this place.

A smile came over my face, and I took a small step forward.

No one else moved.

“How about I scout ahead, and keep everyone in the loop?” I asked, turning to face the group.

Lenna nodded carefully, with a frown. Her parents glared at the floating island.

“This is how they get away with what they do…” whispered Vienna, the ear bud picking it up as though she’d spoken with a normal voice. “Not many can survive the changes here to make them face justice.”

I didn’t respond, and instead took off without saying anything.

A gentle slope down led me to a massive crack in the ground. Peering over the edge, I saw that light shone up from below.

I stepped back, taking a deep breath as another island passed under where I stood.

This wasn’t solid ground. Or, rather this was just another island.

A big one, but still floating.

Across the massive gap, there floated another landmass. It didn’t move like it was floating. With a shrug, I leaped across the area. I landed on the other side, destroying a dead bush in the process.

Dust rose from it, smelling horrible. I stepped back a few times, waving my hands in front of me. A groan put me on guard as I checked my radar for whoever had made the sound.

Then I realized it came from the gap.

Silently, I crept back and stared down.

Something crawled up the side of the island. The white skull practically glowed as giant claws stabbed out from its back into the side of the stone wall. There was no body, just a giant skull with claws coming out from it like a spider.

[Bone Horror, Claw Strike, Level 180, Prey, Unknown.]

[Warning: Uncivilized Creature Detected.]

I shivered, wishing for a bow.

This fucker could take out those near the portal. I couldn’t let it remain alive.

Glancing around, I found a pretty heavy rock, picked it up, then crept back to the edge. It didn’t notice me, or the rock, as I dropped it over the edge.

It slammed into the skull just as it loosed a claw to stab higher into the stone cliff. The rock smashed into the bone, almost knocking it free.

The second and third stones did the job, as the Bone Horror tumbled from the wall, with another deep groan echoing up from below.

It vanished far below. I waited for what seemed like a long time.

[You have gained experience from combat.]

That wasn’t nothing.

“Okay, warning ya’ll if you go exploring, there are things called bone horrors that like to climb up from cracks that lead to islands below us.”

Silence, then John's voice came through. “What the ever loving fuck? I’m gonna stay here next to the ship.”

“What level?” asked Seavel.

“This one was 180. I knocked it off the wall with a few well placed stones,” I added, when the line went quiet again. “Gonna keep moving on.”

The stink continued to get stronger the farther I went into the dusty area. Massive dead plants, completely dried out, blew across the ground.

Behind me, enough dust filled the air that I couldn’t see the other island, or the portal on the hill. Bright light flickered close by, revealing a different portal.

Strange popped out of my shadow and took over to the left, where some shadows formed from some boulders. It widened my map as he sniffed around, while I circled the small portal just floating over the ground.

It didn’t have a stone archway. Instead, the white light just hovered above the ground, almost like a cloud.

Strange chirped in panic as a massive red dot appeared on my map, right next to him.

The boulder stood, forming into a monster the size of the shuttle.

[Stone Sleeper, Level 191, Prey, Unknown.]

[Warning: Uncivilized Creature Detected.]

Rocks formed legs, holding up a nice round boulder, while more small rocks formed arms. It barely moved, inching after Strange, swinging a stubby arm after him.

Strange dodged and snipped at its leg, but it didn’t even react.

Poison wouldn’t do much to a rock.

Honestly, it was a bad match up for either of us, except for my levels.

Still, Strange danced around the giant creature, telling me to hold off. He wanted to try this fight without me.

Everything inside me wanted to attack, right now, but I had to trust the little guy.

I could only hope to be in time if he ended up needing my help.
