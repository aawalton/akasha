Materialising from nothing brought about a shocked silence that made me feel a little better about having every bone in my left arm broken and my ribs caved in by Li Na's transferred injuries.

Xie Ling, who had pressed herself against the far wall of the step, had now straightened slightly as she stared at me with wide, pale eyes. Her mouth opened, closed, then opened again, but nothing came out.

What she was witnessing was beyond words, and she wasn’t the only one thinking that. Even Song Wei, a Lucid Lawbearer, seemed uncertain of what was going on, if his silver spiritual pressure flickering around him was to be believed.

As for the one who had taken my life, Li Na, her face had drained of all blood as her hands trembled at her sides. The look in her eyes was a combination of disbelief and horror. Her breathing, which had been relaxed a few moments ago, was now coming out in short, sharp gasps that suggested she was either about to hyperventilate or scream.

The one who recovered first was Shen Bolin, his face hardened and a calculating, suspicious look appeared in his eyes. “This…this isn’t the real Hou,” he announced, sending a wave of dream qi at my direction to figure out what was going on. “It can’t be. The palace must have created a construct that has his memories and techniques after his death. It is quick. I’ll give it that. But it is a test. This must be the enemy of the ninety-first step!”

“That makes sense,” Song Wei murmured, nodding slowly. “After the Three Calamities, it only makes sense for the trial to throw something personal at us to throw off our emotional states. The real Hou dissolved into the stone. This is just another enemy wearing his face.”

Their justification for what was occurring brought a smile to my face. This was so far away from what their minds could accept. After all, everyone knew that whoever died in the trials died permanently and didn’t come back except as a construct. But there was one person who knew the truth, and that was Wu Kangming.

My eyes found his, and something passed between us in that moment.

There were no secrets between us anymore. We knew exactly who the other was. But we also knew that we didn’t have to fight right now. We had something else to deal with first. With that, we moved at the same time.

Blink Step put me in front of Shen Bolin with my right fist, empowered by real qi, already pulling back in the style of a Phantom Strike, ready to cave in his chest completely.

The Lucid Lawbearer’s eyes widened, not understanding what he was sensing – ghost using an energy that surpassed anything he thought existed. But then his survival instincts kicked in, and his Law activated with desperate speed.

"Reality Shifts For The Dreamer."

The space between us warped in a subtle way that the distance I'd closed with my teleportation suddenly expanded again. Instead of my fist connecting with his sternum, it hit empty air since Shen Bolin had been moved ten metres backwards.

But I'd expected something like this. Peak Lucid Lawbearers didn't die to single strikes. Without a doubt, they had too many defensive options, too many ways to escape or counter, which was exactly why I'd prepared a follow-up.

Red sun energy erupted from my right arm, and the transformation happened in an instant.

My arm elongated impossibly, muscles bulging and twisting as thorned vines wrapped around enhanced limbs that looked like they belonged on something that crawled out of nightmares rather than walked around as a person.

The appendage stretched forward, covering the ten meters Shen Bolin had created in less time than it took him to blink.

His face went from confident to terrified in the space between heartbeats.

The Phantom Strike, still compressed at the tip of my transformed limb, connected with his chest. The compressed qi detonated against his spiritual defenses, causing them to shatter like glass, allowing the force to transfer directly into his body.

Shen Bolin was sent flying back like a cannon, hitting the ground forty meters away. Even though he wasn’t dead, not even close, after all he was a Peak Lucid Lawbearer, he was definitely hurt. Blood was leaking from his mouth, and his hand pressed against his chest where the strike had landed. His robes were torn, and I could see the massive bruise forming across his sternum.

Even as I had been locked in a conflict with Shen Bolin, my spiritual sense had been tracking the fight between Wu Kangming and Song Wei.

The Lucid Lawbearer had his full spiritual pressure active, silver energy crackling around him in violent patterns.

"Probability Collapses To Certainty."

"Fate Follows Calculation."

"Outcomes Align With Intent."

Three separate Laws working in tandem to reinforce each other meant that Song Wei wasn't holding back, he wanted to crush Tian with overwhelming power before the situation could become dangerous.

But Tian was Wu Kangming, and he was doing better than he had any right to.

His sword moved in patterns that transcended simple technique. Sword intent radiated from the blade in waves that cut through Song Wei's reality distortions like they were made of wet paper. Each strike forced Song Wei to defend rather than attack. Each movement positioned Wu Kangming perfectly for the next exchange.

The problem was energy. Wu Kangming only had dream qi and sword intent to work with.

Dream qi was powerful in this world, but it was still fundamentally a lower-tier energy compared to what we used in the Cultivation World. And sword intent, while impressive, didn't provide the raw power needed to overcome what was almost a two-realm cultivation gap.

I watched Song Wei's silver energy wrap around Wu Kangming's sword and force it off course. The probability manipulation made it certain that the next strike would miss by crucial centimeters.

Without any difficulty, Wu Kangming shifted his stance to redirect the blade's momentum into a different angle, but Song Wei had already predicted the adjustment, so he thrust his palm forward to connect with Wu Kangming's chest with enough force to crack bone.

Wu Kangming stumbled backward with blood trickling from the corner of his mouth, and his sword arm trembled slightly from the impact's spiritual backlash, but his eyes remained narrowed and his grip on the weapon never wavered.

All of that had occurred in the same time it took for me to land the first strike on Shen Bolin. The old man pushed himself to his feet, his face red with anger, and his Crown of Clarity manifested above his head.

Unlike the crown of an Oneiric Sovereign's advancement, this one was enhanced and perfected by his Lucid Lawbearer cultivation. The crown pulsed with golden light that spread across his body in protective layers that rewrote his wounds into something that did not happen.

"You're not just a construct," Shen Bolin spat. "You can’t be. Constructs don't use energies outside this world's system, so which dream realm did you get it from. Tell me! Tell me now!"

There was no way I was going to reply to such entitled behaviour.

My silence must have made him furious because Shen Bolin's spiritual pressure exploded outward with greater intensity. The golden light from his crown spread across the entire step, creating a domain where his Law held absolute authority. His most powerful technique, the one he'd probably been saving for the final confrontation with the Dreamgod manifestation on step ninety nine.

"Dreams Become Absolute Reality."

The pressure changed beyond reality manipulation and probability bending, this was a declaration that whatever Shen Bolin dreamed would manifest as physical truth, regardless of what natural laws said.

Without a doubt, this wasn't going to be an easy fight because Shen Bolin had the cultivation advantage, the experience advantage, and now he'd activated his ultimate Law, so I’d need to use my full power to have any chance at winning.

However before we could clash properly, a wet cough echoed across the step, making us both turn towards the sound forty metres away.

Wu Kangming stood with his sword having fallen to the ground, since both of his palms were pressed against his abdomen where silver energy had punched through his spiritual defenses and torn his internal organs apart.

Blood poured between his fingers in a heavy flow that meant something vital had ruptured. He looked down at the damage, then back up at Song Wei with a smile. The expression didn't match the situation at all. He was dying, his spiritual foundation was collapsing as we watched, but he smiled like he had won something.

“Wh-what is wrong with you?” Song Wei cried out.

But he didn’t get an answer since Wu Kangming had dissolved into nothing, leaving behind a heavy silence.

Song Wei stood there, staring at the empty space where Wu Kangming had been, at the blood on the ground, and at the sword lying abandoned on black stone.

"That smile before he died," he whispered, looking left and right with paranoia. "What…what was that about?"

The smile had been his way of saying he'd be back, but I wasn’t going to tell them that, they would find out soon enough for themselves.

"Look what your desire for revenge got your friend, you dragged him into this mess," Shen Bolin spat at me as a golden domain expanded to its maximum range, covering the entire massive step in dream-made-real authority. "Now it's time for you to join him."

"Yes,” Song Wei murmured, shaking off the strangeness of the Wu Kangming situation. His silver energy wrapped around the edges of that domain, probability manipulation working in concert with absolute manifestation. “This distraction has gone on long enough. Let's end this quickly and move on to the remaining steps."

They attacked simultaneously from different angles.

Shen Bolin manifested a massive golden hand that descended from above, fingers spread wide to crush me completely. Song Wei created a silver blade that cut through space itself, appearing directly in front of me with no travel time, probability forcing the strike to land regardless of my attempts to dodge.

I ignored Shen Bolin's attack entirely. The golden hand was powerful and dangerous, but it was also slower. The real threat was Song Wei's instantaneous strike, which I couldn't avoid through normal means.

My monstrous arm shot forward to intercept Song Wei's silver blade. The clash released a shockwave that cracked the black stone beneath our feet.  My red sun enhanced strike met Song Wei's probability-certain attack with equal force.

For one moment we were perfectly balanced, neither side gaining advantage, raw power versus reality manipulation creating a stalemate. Then my left arm transformed into that same monstrous form, but this arm had something extra. The shockwave rune I'd inscribed before I joined the expedition activated across the transformed surface, geometric patterns glowing with compressed spiritual force.

Song Wei's eyes went wide as he realised what was about to happen. His silver energy tried to bend probability, tried to make it certain that the incoming strike would miss or fail or somehow not connect properly. But he was already committed to our current clash. His spiritual will was focused on maintaining the stalemate with my right arm. He didn't have enough attention left to properly defend against a second attack.

My left fist, covered in thorns and glowing red sun runes and compressed qi, smashed into Song Wei's side. The shockwave rune activated on impact and released all its stored energy in one explosive burst.

The sound was like thunder inside a confined box.

Song Wei's defensive spiritual pressure shattered, his ribs cracked audibly, and he was sent flying sideways across the steps until he slammed into the far wall hard enough to leave a crater in the black stone.

I'd severely injured a Peak Lucid Lawbearer.

But that aggressive focus on Song Wei had left me completely open to Shen Bolin's attack.

The massive golden hand descended from above, now less than an inch from my head. At this range, with this much power behind it, the technique would crush my skull and destroy my spiritual core in one motion.

But I knew what was coming so I just stood there and waited while the golden hand completed its descent.

The hand stopped, but not because of something that I had done. The reason it had stopped was because the arm controlling it, Shen Bolin's right arm, was no longer attached to his shoulder.

The severed limb hit the ground with a wet thump. Blood spurted from the stump where it had been cleanly separated from his body. Shen Bolin's golden domain flickered and destabilized as pain and shock disrupted his concentration. He retreated immediately, his left hand pressing against the bleeding wound while his spiritual pressure tried to seal the injury. His eyes were wide with confusion and fear as he looked down at his severed arm lying on the black stone.

Then his gaze shifted to something beside me.

“W-what in the heavens are you…?”
