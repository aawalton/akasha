Luke stood there, shocked, thinking back and forth about what he should do. Like drums, his heart beat loudly enough to be heard from across the floating land. He was also literally a ‘bomb’. The energy that had built up inside him hadn’t exploded yet.

His muscles tensed, as if his insides were about to burst out of his chest. Goosebumps swelled across his body.

Struggling to understand what was happening inside him. Like earthquakes, his body quaked, oscillating.

Slowly, things turned into a blur; vision swirled as if in a tornado, and he couldn’t tell where he stood. Pushing onward, disregarding the pain.

Everything went dark; parts of his body erupted. He passed out, foaming at the mouth. The sheer fear of death made him pale as paper. His body thumped on the ground. Death approached him, but it didn’t take his life. The force of death disappeared as quickly as it had stood before him.

“He isn’t dead yet.” The voice echoed out through the empty void.

He stared upward; it all felt like a dream. He tried to harness energy he didn’t even know anything about. Absorbing too much.

“How could I be so greedy and stupid?” Screaming with a hoarse voice.

Not even knowing how to release the energy.

Before Luke realized his current situation, he watched as his right hand faded out. The energy remained in his body. The power was trying to take over.

“This is so cursed,” he wept.

Luke tried his best to stand up. He finally felt proud of himself. He had received a title that fewer than 100 individuals could have. Now all he felt was pain. He could barely comprehend anything.

No coherent thoughts ran through his mind; he didn’t know how to salvage the situation. All he wanted to do was release this energy.

Luke wasn’t even standing straight anymore. He couldn’t handle the pain; screams escaped his foaming mouth. Kneeling, chest bare, covered in bloodied flesh.

The slime collided against his chest. The slime sent him flying. Coughing up blood.

Luke looked around with his hazy vision, his sense of balance completely gone. He didn’t know if he was upright or lying down. But he knew one thing: if he got knocked unconscious, he was dead.

Maybe the only thing alive was his will to live. He tried to direct the energy, to control it. ‘But could he?’

“Is all this effort for naught? Will I die here like this?” Luke said.

He didn’t even know what facial expression he wore. He didn’t know if he was still crying. All he felt was everlasting pain and anguish.

He wished for death. He hoped to be free of this torment. Surprised he hadn’t dropped dead yet. Watching all the blood gushing out of his body? Weren’t pieces of his body scattered on the island?

Hadn’t he created his own torment? This would never have occurred if he hadn’t followed the path of the Souls. Maybe power wasn’t everything. Maybe he needed a good foundation. Maybe he needed to invest in other stats.

I wonder how the strongest person in this place would deal with something like this.

Maybe they could escape this damn place. His consciousness faded, but he felt as if he were on the brink of comprehending something.

“I’m so close. I’m almost there. Just a little more!” Luke screamed desperately.

Luke couldn’t even tell if he was breathing. He had no sense of touch, no sense of smell, no sense of sight. But he could talk. But some words barely came out. The slime lunged toward him again.

Under his ragged breath, Luke said one last thing before he passed out:

“May my journey into the unknown forge a bright path.” Luke groaned quietly.

All his energy gathered toward his dissipating hand. Slammed it against the slime with all the energy he had left. A shockwave enveloped both the slime and him, blasting them away.

The force threw Luke meters away. Soft breathing sounds were audible in Luke’s direction.

The slime core turned to dust as the blob slowly disintegrated. He didn’t know how it had come to this. Luke felt dead.

Everything was silent. No bouncing of the slime, no wind, no sound at all.

Luke woke up. Just as vision settled in, a massive burning sensation spread through his body.

“I don’t have many points,” Luke cried out.

He couldn’t heal himself, but he felt like he had recovered quite a bit from his missing flesh. Daze overcame Luke. He felt as if he were going to die at any minute. His last nap hadn’t helped him recover energy in the slightest.

He could smell burned flesh around him.

Have I been fried by souls? He thought.

But this wasn’t the time to joke around. At least he had regained his sense of smell and sight. But he felt different, as if some change had occurred to his body, or maybe he was just on edge.

Right now, he still couldn’t move his body, an after-effect of using Soul Energy, he wondered.

If I’m going to use this power again, I have to start with the smallest amount. Just a couple of souls almost killed me. If I hadn’t let the energy surge through my hand, I’d be dead right now.

At least the slime was dead. If he could move, he would have clenched his fist. Instead, he wore a cheeky smirk, grinning because of this newfound power.

Doing all this to distract himself from the unimaginable pain. But it didn’t help. Luke might have been smirking, but he was also clenching his teeth.

It felt like they could break under the amount of pressure he was putting on them. All of his wounds were now scars, ones he would probably have for a long time.

But Luke felt more weakened than he should have. Barely able to even move his mouth anymore.

“How could I have possibly forgotten about the branches? God damn it,” Luke said bitterly.

I almost died today because I forgot to bring my branch. Why am I so clumsy? His own mistake irritated him. Luke had endangered himself because of another silly mistake.

It’s about time I upgraded, though.

‘Wait, a second... Why does the island look better than it did before?’ Luke scanned the area.

He saw no crevices or damage. It looked just like when he first arrived.

Actually... How long have I been gone? Hours? Days even?

How long have I been in this place? Days just went by for him as he tried to survive in such a place.

I wonder how Mom is. I really hope she hasn’t been moved to this place.

She is getting way too old to deal with a bunch of monsters they know nothing about. His thoughts drove more worry into him.

Luke shook his head.

“Let’s forget all of that for now. All I need to do is survive. Maybe I can find Mother soon enough,” Luke said, determined.

But deep down, he knew... they might already be dead. He waited on the floor as time passed by.

It’s time I got up, Luke decided.

Wounded, Luke pushed himself up and crawled steadily toward the pedestal, reaching down to grab one of the many wooden branches scattered on the ground. It was necessary for him to be serious. He had to battle a slime and win.

“Victory has to be mine,” letting out slight grunts along the way. Even though he had slightly healed. He might reopen some wounds during the fight, but that wouldn’t matter if he could get his hands on the restore using the pedestal. He needed points more than he had ever had before.

When Luke placed his hand on the pedestal, the white veil panel appeared within his vision. He willed another monster to spawn on floor 0.

Suddenly, energy condensed in the middle of the island. A majestic, new green slime materialized, still and unmoving. But Luke already felt the pressure of facing it. His experiences with slimes had affected his mental state, but he had to do everything he could to survive. His drive for power was to meet the others to escape this world. But his mind wandered.

Were his parents safe? Was the world he knew still alive? Were the people he would meet in the future reliable? Would human greed cause his death? Or would it be the monsters, the ones stronger than him? To survive, he needed to become strong.

He was in need of someone to talk to.

But right now, he has a job to do.

This slime had to die.

Something shifted inside Luke. He rushed toward the slime before it could even comprehend the situation. As people always say, the first to strike is the winner.

He raised his wooden branch and launched a devastating, blunt attack on the top of the slime’s head. Its body caved in at the point of impact, loose green sludge shooting off in all directions. The slime was knocked back as Luke stumbled slightly from the force of his own strike, leaving streaks in the island’s dirt. The creature wavered from the blow, trying to make sense of what had happened.

Luke didn’t give it time.

He rushed in again, his steps pounding against the ground. Even while injured, he still had power behind his attacks. All that training paid off. His branch bent slightly upon impact, but the slime seemed to brace itself this time. It couldn’t withstand the force. Its gelatinous body split apart from the strike, a gaping hole left in its form.

The acidic slime began seeping into the ground, eating away at the soil. Luke watched, waiting. He didn’t need to keep attacking; it was already on its last legs.

After a few moments, the slime dissolved completely, leaving behind a small pit in the dirt. Maybe the island would automatically regenerate like before.

That was a big if.

Luke returned to the pedestal, not as tired as he had expected. He continued summoning slimes, fighting them one by one. But he hadn’t tested whether he could summon more than one slime on the same floor. The panel only said restart floor 1, but maybe there was a hidden function that allowed multiple enemies to spawn at once.

That thought quickly faded.

He focused on the hunt, fighting slime after slime. As exhaustion set in, his attacks grew sloppy, forcing him to adjust his strategy. Running back and forth across the island, constantly fighting, slowly drained him. Each slime gave him a single point and caused two new souls to appear in the sky.

“Wait, a second…” Luke muttered, frowning.

If one slime was a single enemy, why was he getting twice the number of souls? It should have had only one.

Maybe these monsters followed some special rule he didn’t understand yet.

Shaking off the thought, Luke finished his fifth slime and made his way back to the pedestal. It was time to restore himself finally.
