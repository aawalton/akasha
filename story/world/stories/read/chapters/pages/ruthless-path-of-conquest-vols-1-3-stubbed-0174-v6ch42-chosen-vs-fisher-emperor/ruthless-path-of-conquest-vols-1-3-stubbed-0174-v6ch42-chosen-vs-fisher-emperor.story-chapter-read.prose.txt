Are you coming, or aren’t you? Brice asked, his voice agitated and beyond his control for a moment.

He tried to ignore the voices that clawed at his sanity as he used the alligator’s brain to communicate with the Fisher Emperor telepathically.

Feed us, Brice, they said.

Kill for us.

Go and lick a toad.

Carve out your own guts.

Take a vow of silence.

Perform a plié and do relève!

Sacrifice them.

Eat the wolf’s flesh.

Take a flying leap!

Hang yourself from the nearest tree.

There were dozens of conflicting messages flowing through his brain, all at once, every moment when he used Apophis’s power at its full potency. They played off each other, off of him, and off of the situation, and sometimes they were seemingly completely random. It was unpredictable.

The god Apophis truly was chaos itself, and when Partial Incarnation of Apophis was used, the last bit of shielding between Brice and the god’s essence was stripped away. He felt every bit of Apophis’s madness. He became the sole channel for all the god’s unfocused malevolence. It was beautiful. It was terrible. Painful. Horribly pleasurable.

There was a push toward insanity there. A desperate pull toward letting go.

Focus! he told himself irritably. Fucking focus up! Even with this power, if you don’t maintain your Will, the Fisher Emperor will eat you up.

Munch munch, said a voice into his mind that Brice was almost certain was not his own. Chomp chomp. Gobble gobble.

The maddening quality of the voices was part of why Brice had taken the quest to go after the Fisher Emperor so personally and dedicated himself to it. He genuinely resented James for having exposed him to this. If Apophis hadn’t lost his previous two Chosen Ones, Brice would never have been selected. A lesser form of blessing wouldn’t have had nearly these deleterious effects.

Or so he imagined in his more lucid moments.

Yeah, I’m coming, James replied. You’ll regret asking me.

Then Brice was waiting, standing in place, patiently using his tentacle limbs to tear away the alligator’s slowly regenerating legs once more.

The monster writhed in pain under the Chosen One’s renewed attentions. Brice only smiled.

“This is what comes of fighting against—”

“You called?” said a voice from above.

Brice suddenly felt the presence, as if it had followed after the Fisher Emperor like a shadow.

He must have some sort of high tier stealth-related Skill.

“I didn’t think this would be nearly this easy, honestly,” the Chosen One of Apophis said.

“Oh, I try to be accommodating,” the Fisher Emperor said, his voice ice.

“You’ll just lay down and die, I guess, then?”

“Why don’t you pull your tendrils from my monster’s brain and find out?” James replied. “Unless you’re arrogant enough to believe you can fight me and keep yourself tied to him at the same time.”

Of course. He’s not actually willing to die for this stupid creature. But the important thing is that he would risk himself in a fight with me. I can do the killing part for myself, as long as the Fisher Emperor doesn’t flee again. He seems to be too quick and too good at concealing himself for me to succeed in a pursuit.

“That’s fine by me,” Brice managed, realizing that the Fisher Emperor was waiting for him to say something.

The Chosen withdrew long, thorny tentacles from where they had rested, entwined with the alligator’s nervous system. Brice relished the little micro expressions that danced across James’s face as the overgrown lizard writhed in pain. There was contempt there, disgust, hate, fear, concern… a veritable buffet of negative feelings.

Brice raised one long tentacle to his mouth and gently licked off a bit of the alligator’s brain matter. There was that disgust again.

“Let’s do this,” Brice said.

“Oh, so you did want to fight?” James said. “I thought you called me over just for my good looks.”

His heart clearly wasn’t in the banter, but Brice would have applauded his effort if he wasn’t thinking about how he would flay the Fisher Emperor alive.

The Chosen One of Apophis lunged across the distance that separated himself from James, and suddenly, the Emperor had vanished.

No!

Brice just barely managed to wind a tendril around the alligator’s leg before James could pull him away.

He’s so damned fast!

The Fisher Emperor tried to tug against Brice’s impossible, divine strength, and the Chosen just smiled contemptuously.

“You’ll never break my grip,” he taunted.

Then he lunged in again, and this time, the Fisher Emperor took the attack meant for him. A flurry of serrated tentacles slashed him across the face and neck, and James staggered back, half-blinded, cheek torn open, throat gushing blood. He let go of the alligator, to Brice’s pleasure.

That’s right, focus on me, you’re not getting away!

But the Fisher Emperor’s insane regenerative abilities were working their magic again. The wounds were already healing and had almost closed by the time Brice struck a second time.

His tentacles whipped out and grabbed a neck that was somehow, suddenly, no longer there.

Brice blinked, and then the Fisher Emperor’s knee struck him hard in the nose.

So fucking fast!

But the Chosen was stronger by far. He took the hit without flinching, opened his mouth, and closed down on the offending limb.

There was a cry of more surprise than pain as Brice bit a huge chunk out of James’s knee.

That’s right, even my bones are stronger than yours right now, bitch.

The Fisher Emperor fell away, bouncing from the impact of his failed attack and weakened by the bite.

The Chosen One of Apophis kicked him as he dropped, while at the same time, more of his tendrils reached out and wrapped around James’s remaining, whole leg. Another group of tentacles tried to enter the knee wound, but it was somehow already closed by the time they got there. All of the Fisher Emperor’s wounds were closed, Brice realized, as his long tentacles explored and tried to find a good point of entry.

Then he felt a surprising surge of numbness.

As Brice looked down, the Fisher Emperor leaped free of the tentacles. For just a moment, they had been successfully paralyzed with Lightning Mana.

“You’re really not bad,” the Chosen said.

He still had one set of tentacles wrapped around the alligator’s leg—it had been a mistake to give the enemy even a moment’s opening to try and rescue Brice’s hostage—but he had three other limbs.

The Chosen turned one foot into a third set of tentacles and used them to chase the Fisher Emperor.

James used that movement Skill he kept spamming to get distance, and then Brice bent forward as a giant fireball suddenly struck him from behind.

When did he make that? How did he hide it?

It didn’t matter, of course. It was barely enough of an impact to make the Chosen react at all. As the Fisher Emperor tried to pull his alligator free from Brice’s grasp, the Chosen just grinned.

He straightened out a few of his tentacles and fired them like miniature javelins at the same time that he whipped the others in James’s general direction.

I can wear you down. As long as you can’t run away…

The other limiter, of course, was Brice’s sanity.

Eat lice!

Go fishing in that lake.

Play a video game. You have a console in your bag…

The voices kept gnawing at him, though it was easier to ignore them in a fight. But as he kept using Partial Incarnation of Apophis, his Will would gradually wear down. It was hard to say what would be eroded first, Brice’s Will or the Fisher Emperor’s body.

James dodged the tentacles that were still connected to Brice’s body and was forced to take the javelin-like tentacle projectiles straight through the chest. Three of them penetrated through where the Fisher Emperor’s heart should be, but he just smiled and seemed to shake them off, so Brice guessed James’s most vital organ was probably hidden somewhere else.

Well, it doesn’t matter.

The Fisher Emperor darted backward, pivoted on one foot, leaped into the air, rolled his entire body through the space between another two waves of tentacles, and landed, again on one foot.

Oh, he doesn’t fully trust the knee that just got regenerated yet.

Brice observed that the tentacle javelins were still sticking out of the Fisher Emperor’s chest, right where the heart should have been. He hadn’t had the chance to pull them out yet.

The Chosen whipped more tentacles around and simultaneously shot more of those tentacle javelins out from two different directions.

James managed to avoid being grabbed by the long tendril limbs again, and he dodged one set of javelins, but another group of them impaled him through the thigh.

The Fisher Emperor winced. Though Brice did not sense any actual pain coming from him, no one liked being disabled by an attack.

I just need to land one more shot, the Chosen judged. That ought to be enough.

James lunged in Brice’s direction, his arm suddenly transformed into what looked like a bone drill, and he ignored the tentacles that Brice raised in his defense.

The Chosen shot more javelins in the Emperor’s direction, but a semi-translucent green energy shield appeared around his body and deflected them.

Brice hardened his tentacles, preparing to use them as a defensive wall.

The Fisher Emperor leaped up above the wall of tentacles.

Brice looked up to track the altered trajectory of James’s attack and move his defense accordingly.

The Emperor locked eyes with him, and the Chosen found himself suddenly locked in a battle of Wills. It lasted for only a moment, and the power of Apophis easily shattered the mental attack. James fell away, abandoning his attack, his eyes bleeding.

Forget about landing that last set of javelins, that was the opening I needed, Brice thought excitedly. Detonation.

The tentacle javelins still sticking out of the Fisher Emperor’s chest, where his heart should have been, and the middle of his left thigh, exploded with all the force of multiple sticks of dynamite.

It was far from enough to blow James apart, but Brice could see through the Emperor’s chest and thigh like he was peeking through a little window.

The Fisher Emperor staggered back, his body no longer under his control for a moment.

It was then that Brice’s tentacles all wrapped around the enemy Ruler’s body, surrounding every part of him below the neck completely.

The Chosen could feel there was an object underneath the curtain of tentacles—probably that shapeshifting Ego Weapon he had seen the Fisher Emperor using before—that had placed itself between the tentacles and James’s gaping wounds.

But it didn’t matter. Invading the body was only one method Brice had of fighting James. Now that the Fisher Emperor was immobilized, there was another, better option available.

Brice channeled Soul Mana, and he pushed it rapidly out into his tentacles.

In response, the Fisher Emperor surrounded himself with that same aura, Mana with the same range of colors as a burning photograph. That was his only defensive option now. Try to push off Soul Mana with more Soul Mana.

But in a contest of who had the most energy, between a Ruler—regardless of how powerful—and a man who had made himself the vessel of a god, there could be no doubt of the eventual winner. Brice would outlast the Fisher Emperor’s laughable reserves of Mana and put the man down for good.

He began trying to crush James with the tidal wave of his overwhelming, divine-supplied Mana.

The Fisher Emperor seemed to focus everything he had on resisting, and he had a fairly deep well of power for a human, but Brice thought he could detect which way the struggle was going from the very beginning.

Slowly but surely, he was drowning James in his aggressive, invasive Soul Mana.

Then the Fisher Emperor spoke.

“Yeah, I could use a little help right now,” James said.

A terrible sensation overtook Brice—like a sort of punch to the gut for his Mana supply—and all at once, the cacophony of voices in his head that he had found so unpleasant was suddenly silenced.
