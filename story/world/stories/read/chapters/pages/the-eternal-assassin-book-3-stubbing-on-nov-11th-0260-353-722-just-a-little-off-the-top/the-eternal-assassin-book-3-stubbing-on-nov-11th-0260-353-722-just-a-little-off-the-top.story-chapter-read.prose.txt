Unfortunately, while the missing greater revenant was more than a little concerning, Asher didn’t get the chance to think too much about where it might be. The three undead that were waiting for them chose that moment to attack.

The undead archer began the assault, whipping an arrow onto her bow and firing it all within the same heartbeat. Samantha probably would have criticized her for not even bothering to aim, except for the fact that that one arrow suddenly fractured into a hundred arrows as a volley of projectiles screamed their way.

“That’s a nifty skill!” Moxy laughed as she used Air Nova to blast the entire wave of arrows off course and stop them dead in their tracks. “Here, have one of mine!”

With another explosion of air, Moxy launched herself toward the archer, already firing Air Blasts out from her fists as she shadow boxed. For her part, the archer revealed she had some form of movement skill to back her up, because she immediately began twisting and flowing out of the way of Moxy’s strikes as she took the warrior on in earnest. Every time Moxy tried to get close, the woman leapt away, somehow always staying a few dozen feet away while continuing to pepper her with arrows. Already, Moxy was beginning to take carefully placed shots that somehow seemed to bend around her pelt armor, and her blood flowed freely as she chased after the annoying archer.

“I’m going to back Moxy up!” Samantha shouted, shooting into the air and flying over toward the two of them. She was forced to stay rather low, what with the jagged shards of blood flying around in the storm just a few dozen feet above their head. Even so, they’d practiced having her flying just above the ground, so Asher wasn’t worried.

“Don’t let her pull you too far away!” Asher shouted, before being forced to shelve his concern for others as the remaining two revenants came for them.

The knight darted forward with calm and collected movements, his armor clinking with every step and his large polearm held out to the side at the ready. The slayer, in contrast, ran at them with reckless abandon, all but dragging his warhammer along the ground. His tongue lolled out of his mouth and his wide eyes matched his even wider grin.

“Asher…” Kroult began, and Asher nodded before wordlessly reaching out and yanking Kroult into his Personal Rift. While his buffing spells were helpful, Kroult had proved time and time again that he was no good in a fight. The last thing Asher needed right now was worrying about rescuing him from something, which meant his rift was the safest place for him. That left Asher, Rulfar, and Amanda against the two revenants.

“Where did your undead friend go?” the slayer called out, cackling as he spun his warhammer over his head. “I guess I’ll just have to beat him out of you!”

With that, the slayer smashed his warhammer into the ground, causing the earth to split open as a large, jagged crack shot almost fifty feet toward them, ripping the earth open and threatening to swallow the three of them up.

Guess that explains the aura that looks like a crumbling pillar of bloody dirt rising from his shadow, Asher thought as he used Distortion to easily dodge to the side. Unlike the liches, these three undead weren’t hidden from Identify and Judgement. The archer had an aura that looked like thousands of tiny, razor-beaked birds swarming around her with blood streaming from their wings, and the knight’s some sort of indestructible shield of darkened metal that radiated death and fear. All three auras gave the impression of blood or death, which Asher felt was rather appropriate considering their situations.

Already in his battle form, Rulfar launched himself to safety with his impressive strength, taking advantage of the move to go straight toward the slayer and try to cleave him in half with his battle axe. At the same time, Amanda’s armor flared brightly, and with a flash, she was suddenly standing behind the slayer, slashing with a glowing sword as she tried to flank the undead and sever his head from his body. In an impressive display of skill, the slayer somehow managed to bend and twist his body unnaturally out of the way of Amanda’s brilliant slash that left an afterimage where she cut, while simultaneously conjuring a pillar of stone out of the ground and driving it into Rulfar’s gut to stop the bloodhound before he could get close. As the three of them began to fight in earnest, Asher focused on the final enemy.

“I suppose that leaves you for me,” the knight scowled, not looking all too excited at the prospect even as he lazily spun his polearm over his head. “Those fools always bite off more than they can chew.”

Before Asher could offer a retort, the knight stopped spinning his weapon and thrust forward. Despite still being a good thirty or so feet away, the steel blade stretched toward him, and Asher’s eyes widened as he barely managed to slip into the astral in time to dodge the strike. He watched the polearm return to normal size, and the knight clicked his tongue and glared at the space Asher had just inhabited, clearly displeased that his initial strike had failed to gut him.

It’s like fighting someone else with Distortion, Asher thought, running at a diagonal before returning to the material realm and lashing out with his own thrust.

Allowing Asher’s dagger to scrape past his armored head, the knight blinked at the groove that was carved into the steel helm, scowling as he shifted his glare to Crescent.

“Mithral? And quality mithral at that. I’ll be taking that blade once you fall here.”

“Not going to happen,” Asher said, whipping a steel blade at the man’s face and using Distortion to drastically shorten the space it had to travel. Unfortunately, the knight proved himself more than capable of smacking the dagger out of the air with his polearm as he sent the blade tumbling into the dirt by his feet.

Which gave Asher the perfect opportunity to trigger Recall and pop into existence directly in the knight’s face as he followed his Eternal Mark.

The undead’s eyes widened and Asher grinned as he took advantage of his shock to try and remove his head from his body. But right before his blade could connect, Asher froze when a deep, clawing fear unlike anything he’d ever experienced before radiated from his chest and swept throughout his entire body. Without a shadow of a doubt, he knew that if he tried to follow through with the attack he was about to make, somehow, he’d be the one to die instead of the knight standing before him. The sheer certainty of this knowledge was so powerful that Asher was completely rooted to the spot, his feet practically glued to the ground. His hands trembled as he warred with himself as to what to do, too terrified to decide between stepping forward or back.

His indecision made it child’s play for the knight to sever his head from his body.

Asher blinked at the sight of his own frozen body and the knight’s polearm, covered in his own blood, rapidly rose before him as his head hit the ground. Staring up at his own headless body from this angle was more than a little jarring, the shocking sight of which helped snap him out of that overwhelming fear that had paralyzed him directly before his opponent. Before his body could collapse as well, Asher triggered Comprehend’s second-tier effect, slowing the world to a crawl as he tried to take stock of his current situation.

Okay… I’ve been beheaded. That’s probably not great. That said, I’m clearly still thinking, which means I’m not dead yet. I haven’t exactly tested this just yet, but I think this is exactly what Remote Control is supposed to be used for. The bigger issue is whatever insane skill that guy just used. Whatever it was, it explains the sensation of fear I was getting from his aura. Thank goodness I’m the one who ended up taking him on, anyone else would have been killed for real from such a surprise attack.

Having used his quick timeout to come to terms with the current situation, Asher did three things in quick succession the moment time sped back up to normal. First, he switched Permanence from Samantha to himself, using the skill’s second-tier effect to stop him from bleeding to death. Then, he focused on Complete Resilience, changing his elemental immunity to Fear to prevent a repeat of what had just occurred.

And lastly, he commanded his headless body to lash out with Crescent and Nail, surprising the knight so badly that the undead barely managed to dodge out of the way of Crescent’s blue streak. While Asher missed returning the favor and beheading the knight in turn, he still managed to drive Nail through a small opening in the knight’s armor on his arm, severing the muscles at the elbow and crippling his fighting potential.

“What?!” the knight shouted, leaping back a dozen feet and staring in shock at Asher’s headless body as it lunged after him, not letting up on the attack. “I thought I was the undead! What are you?!”

“Is ‘your worst nightmare’ too cliché?” Asher asked, surprised to find that he could both speak and laugh somehow, despite no longer being physically attached to his lungs. It was a little difficult landing hits when he was now watching himself fight from a distance and in third person, but he did what he could to keep the knight on the backfoot as he continued his relentless barrage, using Astral Dip and Distortion to both dodge those lightning-fast jabs from the knight’s polearm while lining himself up for new strikes. Now that he was watching for it, Asher noted how the knight’s eyes continued flaring jet-black every now and again, no doubt as he continued to try and fail to paralyze Asher in fear and land another devastating blow. Eventually, the knight seemed to realize Asher had countered his Fear element, as the undead roared and did something entirely new.

Exploding into a wave of freezing black energy, the knight’s body reappeared directly before his severed head, a deep scowl on his face as he hefted his polearm with his one good arm before driving the blade straight down.

Stabbing deep into the earth and missing Asher’s head completely as he slipped just his head into the astral realm.

Asher could only laugh at the sheer rage visible on the knight’s face behind his visor. He had a feeling the undead warrior before him had never had the pleasure of fighting anyone quite so difficult to kill, or even anyone capable of shrugging off his fear aura. Before the knight could free his polearm from the ground, Asher’s body was behind him, having used Distortion to chase after the undead the moment he’d used his unique teleportation skill. With Crescent in one hand and Nail in the other, Asher lunged forward, finally managing to land a direct hit on the furious undead as he sliced his blades together and returned the favor, severing the knight’s head from his body.

“Looks like I wear it a little better than you do,” Asher grinned at the lifeless head that plopped into the ground beside him. Even now, the knight’s face was frozen in fury, and Asher chuckled as he yanked the polearm out of the earth and gave his head the space needed to return to the material realm. Tossing his daggers into his rift for a moment, he looked up at his headless body and directed himself to scoop himself up, still struggling a little to wrap his mind around what he was doing. Plopping his head back onto his neck, he felt Eternal Regeneration get to work, quickly healing the wound as his skill made him whole again. Ironically, the knight’s strike had been so quick and clean that Asher was able to reattach his head in only a few seconds.
