Clementine grabbed Ashton's arm.

"Wait! Wait, just– I just need a bit more time! A few more days, just please, I just–"

"The people here don't have 'a few more days', Clem. People already suffered far too much because I let him live when I first figured out he was a marked," Ashton said, pulling his arm away. Clementine stared at his back, confused. She saw her friend's expression, and heard the tone of his voice. She heard the shame in it.

"Were you... planning on killing him today no matter what?"

Ashton stopped. He turned away, and didn't say any more to her. It wasn't like there was anything he could say in the first place. She was right. No matter what, Jonah would have not lived to see another day. Ashton was always going to ensure that.

"Ash, please! Please, just stop, just let me think, we can figure something out! We can–"

Ashton interrupted her. He used the sylph's gust magic to push her back as far as he could without hurting her, so that she wouldn't get caught up in this. He called on the gnome's magic next, pulling up stone from the floor and shaping it into a staff, as the helping hands grabbed the summoner's staff for him to continue boosting Ashton's magic.

Without a moment's hesitation, before Jonah even understood what was happening, Ashton struck him in the chest. The staff in his hand cracked. It didn't break, but it would after just one, and definitely after two more attacks. He was just a Marked, not even a full god-kin, so why the hell was he so tough already?

At least that attack seemed to have done something. Jonah was wheezing already just from that single impact, and Ashton wasn't letting off, either. He wanted to finish this as fast as he could. That was the most he could do for Clementine.

Ashton struck at Jonah again, and just as he thought, he had to repair the staff after two attacks already. Ashton was considering repairing it like he had in the fights against the growth, but there was something more efficient he could do here. While he was fighting with one staff, he would create more at the same time, so that he could simply swap it out the moment one broke.

But even though Ashton was well-prepared, Jonah wasn't helpless, either. Not only did he have a mace, a larger one than the one he had the last time Ashton was here, but he had that book. That large slab-like book in his hand was radiating magic from it, like it was some kind of magical tool. With every attack, more magic seemed to be pushed from Jonah's body into that slab.

It didn't just seem coincidental, either. Rather, it was like the damage was immediately being transformed into power.

Not that it really mattered what the deal with that item was, though. Ashton would finish this before Jonah could try anything stupid.

By now there were more than enough stone staffs accumulated that Ashton could get a little reckless with his internal energy, and decided to try something out. Although he was technically only at the lower first stage of his internal core, considering that Ashton had been able to use techniques even before this point, he should be able to push beyond what is possible for this stage.

At least, enough to start unlocking the true potential of the staff. He let his internal energy flow into the staff, and compressed it. The energy moulded itself to the physical form of the weapon. Ripples of energy would be coursing over its surface now. It didn't seem quite as stable as normal, but it was enough. This was the basis of almost all of Ashton's staff techniques. It also made a pretty handy torch if you didn't have anything else on you.

Ashton pushed the staff forward and struck Jonah's shoulder. But this time, it didn't hit him just once. The moment Ashton's weapon pulled back, another translucent, blue mirage appeared, mimicking exactly what the staff had done before, striking at the same spot on Jonah's body.

While the base attacks were already stronger, the compressed internal energy seemed to hold the staff together, and let Ashton get three or four attacks in per staff. With this, after just a few attacks, Jonah's arm was limply hanging off his shoulder.

"Unnecessarily sturdy bastard," Ashton clicked his tongue. The amount of faith that Jonah had strengthened himself with was genuinely ridiculous. It had truly all gone to him, except for the seemingly minuscule bits that he was funnelling over to the wyvern from afar.

"Why-Why are you doing this? I am merely spreading the truth! Do you not understand? I am a prophet of the new world, I–" Jonah started blabbering. He was moving around, turning and twisting his body that made his arm drag behind him in a way that should be extremely painful. Ashton's attack should have shattered Jonah's shoulder and broke his upper arm and elbow at the very least. But Jonah wasn't even flinching, and beyond the wheezing earlier, he didn't seem particularly affected by Ashton's attacks.

It was clear that they were working, of course, but it was like he didn't feel the pain they brought.

However... the more Jonah moved around, the more power was continuing to flow into that slab in his other hand. He couldn't hold his mace anymore, but that didn't seem to matter.

Was that slab taking away the pain that Jonah should be feeling right now? How ridiculous. Well, at least Clementine might be relieved to hear that her father didn't suffer going out.

"Ashton, please!" she yelled out from behind him, not daring to approach the fight. "Just stop it, I need to think! He's my father, I–"

Ashton clicked his tongue. "No, he's not. Not anymore, nor was he ever. A man who treats his daughter the way Jonah treated you is not a father."

"What if it was yours? What if your father was the one standing there?"

Ashton ground his teeth. "My father never would have become a Marked, Clem." He turned to her. "But even if he had... if he had turned into a monstrous being like Jonah is now, I would do this same thing."

Clementine stood there. Ashton couldn't see her; she was too far away. But he could figure what expression she had from the slight sounds she was making, trying to come up with a response.

"I... but... I don't believe that, I–"

"Clementine. I told you before, but I'm not the Ashton you used to know anymore. I would. I have done worse. Patricide would be the least of my sins. So don't do this. Your father has to go," Ashton said, as he felt a powerful magic surge behind him. The slab's effect must have been activated now. Ashton let himself get too distracted.

What now? Was Jonah going to strengthen himself further? Would he enter his second phase? Whatever it was, Ashton was annoyed that he let Clementine distract him. It was just hard to ignore his friend when she was crying out like that.

But when Ashton turned, the slab-like book's magic didn't affect Jonah. Instead, it spread out through the room, like a thick mist that was attempting to cloak Ashton's mind. It was a disgustingly familiar sensation. It wanted to seep into every nook and cranny of his mind, as if it was trying to latch onto his soul.

It felt the same as when the Summoner was trying to affect Ashton's mind, forcing him to do their bidding. But Ashton wouldn't let that happen. He had bene able to resist against some of the Summoner's influence at points, so this was easy enough.

For him.

Just for him.

Ashton turned back to Clementine and ran over to her.

She was on her knees, staring up at Ashton. Tears were streaming down her cheeks. "Ashton, I just... why can't you listen to me? Why can't you trust me, and give him a chance? He's my dad, no matter what you say, he's my dad! No matter what he has done, I love him, he's my family!"

Ashton's heart skipped a beat. Fucking shit.

"Clem, calm down. Take a breath. You're okay. Just work against this, you don't need to-" Ashton held Clementine's arms, but she wouldn't listen to him. She just seemed to stare blankly into space, like she didn't even see him. And then, Clementine pushed Ashton away.

"Get away from me! You murderer!" she yelled out, crawling backward. Like she was genuinely scared of him, and it broke Ashton's heart. Even if he already expected something like this might happen. The only infuriating part was that it didn't come from Clementine's heart, but from magic her piece of shit father used.

Ashton stood up and turned around. He ran back to Jonah. Ashton would finish this fast, before Clementine's head got fucked with even more.

But before he could do that, before he could reach Jonah, the doors to the cathedral were ripped open, and the cultists under Jonah's control came storming into the space.
