Using Agni Clan and the Association to spread his produce, the newer potions could reach throughout the country and even beyond if they wanted. The demand for them wouldn’t die down, and his importance would increase even more.

Anyone who has tasted honey wouldn’t want to go back to sewage water again. If anything, they work harder to make sure they can have that honey over and over. Johan banked on that integral part of the human mind.

Merilyn was a living legend. She had the position and the power, and he doubted she’d let harm reach his way after putting in so much effort and time into helping him.

Sure, he might have to make some concessions if things came down to that, but working under the Association would be way better than getting turned into a brainwashed farmer working 24/7.

“You.” Yuna pointed at him.

Since the Association would help in negotiations, Agni Clan would help set up deals with other guilds and delivery, he didn’t want Yuna to feel left out any more than this.

“It’s ok if you don’t want it.” Johan could sell them for more profit.

There was a lot to do since he had decided to roll the dice, but he wanted to take a moment and reflect.

His eyes wouldn’t leave the potion bottle. It was a cheap one, made of extra-durable glass. A bottle that held the potion he had made. The potion Yuna took with her in the Tower.

Best of all, Yuna seemed determined to become even stronger. So, he had to do his best to.
