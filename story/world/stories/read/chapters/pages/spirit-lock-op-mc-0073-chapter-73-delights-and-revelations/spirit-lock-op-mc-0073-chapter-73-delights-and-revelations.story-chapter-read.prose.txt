She moaned and leaned back and said, “Oh my God!” as dramatically as possible, triggering a wave of laughter.

To protect your veins, your body produces a compound called ingensal that coats the outer lining of your muscles, ligaments, veins, and other tissues that cannot be infused with cora. It's clear you're not a kid, you’re likely from the future, but sometimes I get the impression you’re not even from here.”

“Yes. Building trust is desirable, but the stakes are high.”

“No.” Hayden pulled a steak off the rock, cut it, and offered it to Fringe, who eyed it like a drug pipe. He accepted it against his best judgment and tried to eat quietly. His reaction was audible and comical, but the conversation was far too engrossing to laugh at him.

“Well that was terse,” Photel said.

“There’s not much to say,” Hayden replied. “I didn’t return for the rift, so I’m not sure what’s inside. That said, I know it’s special, so I stayed for it.”

Hayden fell silent as the others looked at him. He seemed lost for a considerable period before lifting the steak to check it. “No. Now eat up. We need to put as much distance between them before more people get through that rift.”

Her real salvation was Toba Akcala, who had been nicknamed “Mackerel” for reasons no one could seem to remember. He had never been to the ocean. He hated seafood. His name was Mackerel all the same.
