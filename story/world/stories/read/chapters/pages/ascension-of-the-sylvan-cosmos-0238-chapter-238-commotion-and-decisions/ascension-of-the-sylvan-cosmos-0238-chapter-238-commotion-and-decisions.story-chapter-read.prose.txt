"Shut up!"

Elowen's words were supposed to increase morale, and although her words worked on many, some people's faces started becoming uglier. Elowen didn't notice due to the helmets some people were wearing, but she was sharp enough to feel that her words weren't as effective as usual.

Moving an army of 10,000 through the forest was not easy. A formation divided into melee soldiers, ranged soldiers, and cavalry dragged for more than 300 meters wide and 200 meters long. Due to dense vegetation, those at the back could not see those at the front. So, Elowen's words reached them like an echo, which was not too inspiring.

Moreover, the soldiers were currently receiving whispers, and unlike the other time, Liu Shu was focusing on the weaker ones and those with the least <Willpower> stat.

The effects took a while to truly start showing, but they were exceptional in the most isolated units.

A man suddenly snapped at the person walking by his side. "Can you stay further away!? You are basically bumping into me all the time!"

The soldier that was shouted at didn't even touch that person, so she became upset. "What are you talking about? I haven't touched you!"

Unexpectedly, the man swung his fist.

BANG!

BANG!"

"Don't look at me like that!"

BANG!

With the commotion occurring, not many were noticed, and those who did were quickly slapped by a root, making them lose consciousness.

The sound of the flashing roots hitting the soldier's chin was very similar, so when one of them fell, the people around thought that someone around them had also gone insane and became wary.

Amapola's eyes flashed coldly and quickly as she stealthily moved in the surroundings with the 200 Rose Fairies, using their sharp and silent wind to pick up those stragglers created by Liu Shu.

Even if some seconds had just passed, the number of missing people reached a few hundred already.

Elowen turned around with a confused expression and looked to her side. "Go and see what is happening behind us. Even if we are a provisional army created from many powers, this level of undiscipline is… frankly unbelievable." That person nodded and quickly moved to the back. In the meantime, the Level 29 spoke. "I need ideas. What might be the reason? Is the World Tree involved in any manner with what's happening?"

Elowen frowned, unconvinced. "That's not an answer I want right now, Horlrus. I need other ideas!"

"While we don't know exactly what type of World Tree Tianlian Liu Shu is, we know that she is proficient in summoning arts due to that <Summoning Blessing> we've heard about. This provides a strong argument for her being an anima-focused World Tree like Lady Fortuna. If so, she might have already started an attack by making our soldiers' minds unstable."

Elowen shouted, her voice booming outward. "Deploy mind-clearing <Soulspells> at once!"

Everyone who knew quickly deployed them in the conflict areas. However, this approach had a problem. Liu Shu wasn't manipulating anything. She was using <Mind Echo> to communicate with her powerful voice in the minds of weak-minded people.

She wasn't influencing them with any energy, just words. Therefore, mind-clearing <Soulspells> did nothing to influence the situation favorably. Elowen frowned and clicked her tongue. "Is it really a lack of discipline? Don't they know that we are on a timer here!? Useless! How much time have we lost already!?"

"I am doing well. However, my sisters are feeling the strain. Their Aether Mana reserves are being sucked dry quickly due to our need for stealth. While the armors you created increase our stealth capabilities, they drain too much Aether Mana to maintain."
