With a kick, the monster that literally jumped out of the grass to eat me dies. It was only level thirty, hardly worth my time. It was quite a stupid one too, most monsters that are too low level seem to avoid me.

But there is one every now and then that just seems extra stupid. Maybe it has something to do with my hero-ness attracting monsters? At first I thought that only applied during incursions, but I have started to think it's a permanent thing. I would guess that whatever I must smell or feel like to monsters overrides their instincts to stay away from stronger opponents.

This landscape is quite nice, despite that. Just endless rolling hills and fields covered in lovely green grass. I think the system called it The Evergreen Meadow, which is pretty fitting. Especially without Ari here to blow up the lovely hills.

Speaking of Ari… it's been three days since she left. Who knows what sort of trouble she has gotten up to by this point. None of that concerns me though, as I have just been moving along towards Itolas. My guess is that I will reach it tomorrow, or maybe the day after. Depends how efficient I can be with my Plane Step.

Thus far, I don’t think I've really appreciated how useful such a skill is. It is quick and relatively convenient teleportation in what is now quite a large area. Sure, I do have to physically take a step or some similar motion. But I get the feeling that it is quite light of a requirement compared to other teleportation skills.

Putting all that to the side, I harvest the materials from the foolish monster. Nothing really good came of it, just some hide and a mana crystal to sell. However, as I am accessing my storage, I feel an odd hum, like something inside is resonating.

Focusing on the sensation, my interface points towards that compass I got. The one that is supposed to lead me to legendary treasure, and great danger of course. I pull it out of storage and it almost leaps out of my hand with how excited it seems to be.

The point is shaking back and forth a bit, but is clearly pointing towards the direction of the Snarq Mountains. I pull out my map, and it seems that it is pointing to one of the larger mountains on the outskirts of the region. My map doesn't say there is anything special about it. But I guess treasures of an ancient civilization wouldn’t be easy to find.

It isn't that far off my normal path. Maybe three more days extra, plus whatever time it takes to fight the dangers. Given the fact that I am about to enter a very dangerous mountain range that I am not sure I can survive, perhaps a bit more power would be nice.

The compass did come from a quest reward, and it seems an awfully large coincidence that I would happen to be passing by the location. So that means I must be meant to go there for some reason. Which means there is bound to be something important there.

So, I adjust my direction and keep moving forwards.

—

The ground underneath me abruptly transitions from grassland to hard sandy soil. My boots make quite a racket as I come to a stop near a large reddish rock. I have technically now entered the Snarq Mountains, which the system confirms for me.

You have now entered The Snarq Mountains | Danger Level: Moderate-Absurd

Okay, so I better stay away from whatever entity is making this palace rated absurd. The rest are only of moderate danger, which is a step above what is ideal, but shouldn’t be too hard for me. The danger level thing is based on level, from what I can surmise, and I am good at hitting a few levels above my own.

As for the mountains themselves, well those are going to be a problem. I appear on top of one of the taller rocks around, and my jaw drops. They aren’t mountains, they are a fucking wall. I can barely see the top of the main range as it reaches into the clouds and doesn't even stop then.

Oh, and they are thick too, if my map is anything to go by. Likely at least ten thousand kilometers across, by its estimation. There is no way I can cross those mountains. Damn it, couldn’t Ari have told me how she planned to get past these things? She just said it would be super easy with her guiding me…

That sounds like a problem for later though, as I can clearly see my destination already. It is a much shorter mountain several hundred kilometers away. Normally I would say it would take me a day at most to reach it. But the monsters I see dotted about make me think otherwise.

I scan their levels and not a single one is below level fifty. Though there are only a couple nearing sixty. Those are ones I will have to avoid. I have been going on the assumption that five or six levels above my own is my rough limit. A far cry from the dozen or so levels I could manage back when I was a lower level.

With a great sigh, I jump off the rock and let Dovey out of the phylactery. He was a bit sleepy, so I let him rest. But I will need his scouting prowess if I want to navigate this maze of rocks and monsters that surround the giant mountains.

“As you wish, master. I shall be your guide and even save your ass too!” The stupid bird says as he takes off into the skies.

“Be careful! There are definitely some flying monsters too!” I shout back at him, getting only a mental nudge as confirmation that he heard me. Whoops, guess I forgot to deactivate Spirit Link. Not that it matters much, the passive drain is basically nothing at this point.

I wonder if Ari would’ve done the Spirit Link thing if I asked… nope too late for such thoughts. To help push such thoughts away, I jump down from my observation rock, rolling to absorb the impact. Dusting myself off, I start to run again, letting nothing but my own navigational instincts guide me through the maze.

Oh and the map that I conveniently have. That is barely doing any of the work, though. My instincts are quite good, after all.

Master, you really shouldn’t lie to yourself.

It is not a lie. My instincts tell me to use the map, after all.

Dovey fails to come up with a response to that. Which is good, as moments later the ground a few hundred meters in front of me ruptures. A giant snake-like monster launches itself from below, seemingly intending to swallow me whole before I can react.

Unfortunately for the monster, it has tried to swallow way more than it can chew. Wait, do snakes even chew? Eh, this one is a monster, it probably chews, right?

While getting distracted by thoughts of snake anatomy, I Plane Step to the side and then use the narrow walls of this place to get some air. Never in a thousand years did I imagine myself able to run up walls like this, but damn is it fun.

During my fall, Identify does its thing, revealing that the monster is a [Burrowing Basilisk - Level 52]. I don’t have time to remember that basilisks have petrifying glares, as my eyes have already locked with its own.

Yet, I don’t freeze. I know I should be freezing, I can feel the tingle of whatever magic it is using. But I seem perfectly fine? Or wait, my new focus thing, that protects me against control and suppression and whatever, the basilisk's gaze must fall under my immunities.

Thanks to that, the basilisk is momentarily confused as I start to swing my hammer down towards it. The thing tries to move to the side, but a perfectly timed gust of wind from my pal Dovey puts me right on course.

Luxmore slams into its head with a deafening crack, splitting its skull wide open. As I tumble to the ground, I see the thing still twitching around. I walk up to the immobilized creature and smash it several dozen times with Luxmore, putting an end to it.

‘Ding’, You have slain [Burrowing Basilisk - Level 52].

I didn’t get any new levels or anything fancy, but that is to be expected. Still, the fight reminds me that while I may heal, I am still constrained by various limits. Like, if I lose my head, then I can no longer control my body until it grows back. Though without allies, getting beheaded is certainly a death sentence. Good thing I designed Stelan to be really good at protecting me from getting beheaded.

Musings about my own mortality aside, I collect the drops from the basilisk. Nothing fancy really, just its scales and one of its teeth. Everything else kinda got destroyed. Hammers are not the most precise weapon, after all.

With that monster dealt with, I continue onwards to my destination. I can’t let a little brawl get in the way of getting stronger. What kind of hero would I be then?
