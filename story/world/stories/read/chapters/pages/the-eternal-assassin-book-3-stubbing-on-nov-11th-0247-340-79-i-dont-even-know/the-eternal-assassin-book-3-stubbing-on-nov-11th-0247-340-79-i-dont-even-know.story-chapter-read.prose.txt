Not waiting to hear the dreaded message from Jameston that they’d been overrun and forced to retreat and give up on the plan, Asher ran over to the door and got to work. Just as he feared, while he was able to carve through the heavily enchanted door without issue, the door was huge, and his little slice of void simply wasn’t big enough to get through it in any sort of timely manner. The void dagger ate his skills just as hungrily as it ate anything else, which meant he couldn’t even use Distortion to elongate the blade and speed things up.

This door didn’t have hinges, but even if it had, Asher was pretty confident not even Rulfar was strong enough to push it over after erasing them.

Asher did his best to carve through the door, knowing that all he needed to do was dig open a hole wide enough that he could shove something through. Even if he couldn’t use Astral Dip to get through the door, he should be able to toss something through and Recall to the other side. Though even as he had the thought, he gritted his teeth as he realized Flunx had probably prepared for other people trying to use teleportation spells to get into the room as well. This was the heart of her castle, the bunker she’d poured who knew how many centuries into creating. He’d be more surprised if he could use Recall to get in there rather than the opposite, which meant he’d need to carve out a large enough hole for him to force his entire body through.

But even if he had enough time to do so, he simply didn’t have the tritessence.

After surviving all the traps on the way down here and relying on the dagger so heavily against the waves of golems, he was already running dangerously low on energy. At the rate the dagger was continuing to drag it from his body to feed its endless stomach, there wasn’t a chance in hell he’d manage to last long enough to carve out a hole large enough for him to fit through without taking a break to replenish his energy. And as far as he could tell the gravity in the corridor hadn’t changed in the slightest, which meant he couldn’t even drag one of his teammates out and ask them to dig with the dagger either, as they would be flattened to the ground in an instant. As Asher struggled to push those thoughts aside and simply keep digging as fast as he could, Jameston’s voice sounded in his head again, this time far more panicked.

“Lanra’s spell is failing, there’s nothing more we can do! There’s a golem out here that’s almost three stories tall, even Mako is struggling to break this one! Asher, we’re out of time, if you’re going to do something, do it now!”

“I’m trying!” he shouted, desperately doing what he could to slash his dagger back and forth and erase his way through the giant door. The longer he dug, the more of his own tritessence he lost, and the weaker he felt. Already he’d erased three feet of solid matter, and he’d still not reached the other side of the door.

Out of desperation and a total lack of other options, he tried forcing Distortion to work on the void dagger again. Digging with a blade that was only a few inches long was one thing, but if he could just expand the dagger to the size of a sword like he could with his other weapons, getting through the door would be far easier. The skill had been sitting at level 39 for ages now, and he’d used it plenty during the last couple of fights. If he could just tip it over and evolve it, maybe they could still pull this assassination off.

“Come on!” he said, gritting his teeth as he did little more than waste what little tritessence he had left trying to force his skill to work. When the dagger refused to budge, Asher switched targets, compressing space in order to shove his arm deeper into the hole he’d already erased as he continued digging. That seemed to be the last little bit the skill needed, as the moment he poked through the other end of the door, he got what he’d been waiting for.

Distortion - lvl 40

Spend 1,000,000 shards to evolve Distortion?

“Yes!” Asher shouted, staring at the inch-wide hole his dagger had poked into the wall as he hurried to sheath his void dagger and pull out one of Samantha’s arrows. Even if he was ninety-nine percent certain he wouldn’t be able to Recall into the room, that didn’t mean he wasn’t going to try. “Absolutely!”

Shards: 7,742,782

Distortion (active) - lvl 41

Tier 1 - Bend the nearby fabric of space to your whim

Tier 2 - Temporarily alter the very existence of an inanimate object

Tier 3 - Unclaimed space is yours to command

Unclaimed space? Asher read, frowning as he placed an Eternal Mark onto the arrow before shoving it through the small hole leading into the next room. What the hell does that mean?

Focusing on the arrow that had fallen into the bunker, Asher tried to trigger Recall, only to feel as though he were trying to force his way through a brick wall. As he’d suspected, the grandmaster enchanter Flunx had made damn sure nobody could warp in and out of her main room other than her.

With that plan failed and his soul all but scraping the bottom for tritessence, he took a step back, running his hands through his hair as he desperately tried to come up with a plan. Lanra and her team from the Eight Crests were counting on them making it to Flunx and finishing the job. They’d entrusted them with the most important part of the mission, and Asher would be damned if he was going to let that trust go to waste. Without any other options available to him, he turned toward his newest skill evolution, trying to make heads or tails of what it means.

“Unclaimed space is yours to command,” he muttered, closing his eyes as he tried to feel out what the skill did. Usually, skills came with an innate understanding of their purpose. Each of the skills for his Eternity and Knowledge elements had come with that innate understanding and been simple enough to understand, so he hadn’t really been forced to wonder. But for whatever reason, his Spatial element was different.

Asher didn’t know why, but the Spatial element seemed to function differently in a couple of ways from literally every other element this world had. Where the other elements each had a passive that kicked in the moment the element was absorbed, the Spatial passive didn’t trigger until all five of its skills were unlocked. Similarly, while other elements gave people basic understandings of their skills, it was up to him to figure out how each of his Spatial skills worked. It was a mystery he hadn’t been able to solve from scouring Whikoga’s library, and it was a serious pain in situations like these.

“Yours to command, yours to command,” he repeated under his breath, opening his eyes as he stared at the small hole he’d managed to dig through the door before almost running himself dry. Through it, he could just make out what looked like cabinets filled with countless rows of colorful gemstones, which meant that had to be where Flunx did most of her work. Shifting his focus less so on the room beyond the hole and more on the hole itself, Asher tilted his head as he stared at the one-inch-wide hole. He needed to make it large enough for his entire body to fit through, but he didn’t have the tritessence or the time to keep digging away with his dagger.

But what if there was another way to make it bigger?

Asher continued staring at the hole, focusing on what he needed to happen. Distortion’s first-tier effect allowed him to compress and expand empty space, making it larger or smaller depending on what he needed. The skill’s second-tier effect allowed him to make objects larger or smaller, increasing his capabilities beyond just empty space. If Distortion was capable of effecting space with the first tier and solid matter with the second…

Why not try and do both?

Asher stared at the hole as he focused on his skill, all but shoving his will toward it as he demanded it follow his orders. Space was his to command, and he had but one desire to see fulfilled at this moment. As if his origin element agreed with him, Asher felt what little tritessence still remaining in his body flow over into the hole, and his eyes widened as it began to grow. The inch-wide hole stretched and expanded as the surrounding door shrank and gave it room to do so. In a split second, the hole that he could barely even fit an arrow through had suddenly grown almost two feet wide, giving him a perfect view of not only Flunx’ bunker, but of the lich herself.

The lich sat within a complex ritual circle made out of gemstone dust, the fires in her eye sockets blazing a brilliant purple as she stared up at the roof and chanted to herself. Her body was covered in a robe that shimmered and shined in the light of her eyes, as if it had been crafted from gemstone thread. More and more gemstone dust was beginning to float up off the ground and gather around her as she chanted, and the dust seemed to be picking up speed with every syllable. Despite having no idea of the finer details involved in rituals, Asher was all but certain that Flunx was seconds away from finishing hers and escaping for good. Not willing to see that happen, he lunged forward, narrowly slipping through the hole right before his tritessence ran out and it snapped back to being only an inch wide.

“You!” Flunx shouted, the lich recoiling and pausing in her ritual the moment Asher landed in her room. “How?!”

“I don’t even know!” Asher gasped, ripping a dagger from his bandoleer and hurling it at her head from across the room. He was completely out of tritessence, but that didn’t matter at the moment. Sever the Thread was a passive skill, which meant so long as he cut off her head, the lich would be gone for good.

Unfortunately, Flunx wasn’t willing to make things quite that easy.

Even with most of her attention on completing the escape ritual that felt as if it were coming to a crescendo, Flunx wasn't nearly as helpless as he would have liked. Raising her arm, she batted the thrown dagger aside with ease, her glimmering robe functioning like armor as the dagger failed to do so much as sever a single mundane thread. Before Asher could try anything else, she pointed a finger and a wave of gemstone dust broke away from her ritual as it shot toward him. Knowing he wouldn’t be able to dodge in time, Asher instead held his breath and clenched his eyes shut as he tried to run through it, immediately regretting his decision as his skin was torn to shreds. His sea serpent leathers did absolutely nothing in the face of the dust, and he realized far too late that the gemstone dust functioned like thousands upon thousands of tiny little blades.

Still, death by ten thousand miniscule cuts was rather difficult for anyone to pull off when Asher could regenerate as quickly as he could.

Bursting out of the other side of the dust, he saw the fires in Flunx’ eye sockets flare as he raced toward her. He was absolutely covered in his own blood and completely naked at his point as his armor and bandoleer fell off him in tatters, but that didn’t matter.

He still held Crescent in one hand and Nail in the other, and he had a far better idea of where he wanted to sheath the daggers.

“Insolent—”

Asher sliced his two masterwork daggers forward, cleaving straight through Flunx’ neck and separating her decrepit head from her body. Her shimmering, robe-covered torso collapsed in the center of her ritual circle in a puddle of Asher’s own blood, never to rise up again. Breathing heavily, Asher looked down at the smeared mess of blood and glittering dust all over the ground at his feet, and he could do nothing more than laugh as he raised his daggers above his head in triumph. It certainly hadn’t been easy, and he and his team had nearly died more times than he could count, but they’d done it.

The Council of Death was down to seven.
