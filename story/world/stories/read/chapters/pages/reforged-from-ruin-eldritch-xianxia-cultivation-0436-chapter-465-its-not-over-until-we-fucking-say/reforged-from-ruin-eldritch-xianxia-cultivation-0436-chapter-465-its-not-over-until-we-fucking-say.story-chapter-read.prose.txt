The screaming stops.

Its absence is loud enough to be heard for miles, and for far, far further to those with enhanced senses.

What was a squirming, writhing sun, cradled in the palm of the Morae’s five Peaks, winks out into nothing. An objective is removed from the battlefield, and while the strongest react in nanoseconds rather than moments, there is still a single instant where everyone, as one, witnesses the change.

And there is light.

The atmosphere glows with it. A light bright enough to briefly push back the colors of the Peaks above, shining like a beacon. The five Peaks act as shadows, blocking off some of the illumination, but the glow is bold enough to replace blue skies and black clouds with the colors of a myriad sunrise.

Even the land itself, for a brief moment, goes silent. Stops its agonies out of sheer surprise, stunned by the glow.

The instant passes.

The squirming, endless beast in the sky casts its shadow, and the shadow inverts as the glow pushes it back. A million limbs reach out, vast enough to hold cities in their palms, and the weight of that light is so vast that for just a moment they are slowed.

An invisible, inverted thing crawls out of evergreen nowhere, opening itself wide and shaping its inversion to unmake everything that it knows because it is only that which it has consumed.

The land rears up in a storm of violent spasms, pockets of space and hidden musculature suddenly expanding a thousandfold in a desperate rush of Qi, deepening ontology into a violent storm of grasping tendrils.

Supreme beings, high up enough in reality to define themselves against it, to define themselves as the single greatest forms of themselves, turn their weapons inward. Mission objectives are abandoned, without needing even so much as a word between them, for they understand the greed their fellows hold and the wonder of that single, central glow.

Power enough to break the world a hundred times over rushes in towards that light from every direction.

In the light of that bloody sunrise, the night sky changes shade.

The stars change colors.

Empty holes out of reality, shining down on a world lit by a sun or roiling things in a ball of flame and heat and sex and death and starvation, gain definition. Gain color.

And fall.

Droplets.

Turned to rainfall.

Turned to LIGHTNING.

Empowered by the collapse of a realm smaller but heavier than the one it is falling in, the lightning glows with iridescent light, every shade of the bones of creation, flooded with so much Qi that the world rattles.

For a moment, [Divine Strikes Of Starry Tribulation] transcends any limitation from being incomplete, from being born out of a cultivator only at the Nascent Soul Realm, and holds back demigods of all stripes.

Raika steps out into the world again.

Her first thought is a sort of mild irritation.

The surgery took more than a few hours.

Days, maybe?

Enough time that the battlefield has shifted a hundred times over, and then been reshuffled back into new landscapes. Where there were mountains, there are canyons, and where there were fields of grass and food, there’s now fields of magma, of ash, of blood and bodies.

She’s a little late.

The irritation passes.

She’s here now. Now is when she can act.

So she acts.

She doesn’t have a lot of time to do so, after all.

She takes the collapsing bubble of the screaming sun in her hand, letting it continue shining above her as she stands on the mountaintop that was once the site of the auction that led her to most of this mess. With a push, she transforms some of the ground, expelling it out into space.

A flood of mud and muck and filth and corpses falls out of the sunrise, down to earth.

With it comes a flood of abominations, eyes and wounds glowing with plasmic vitality, spilling out and already moving. As faux-Tribulation slows the demigods above, the evolving abominations of the Heart-realm tumble into the battlefield, moving at speeds that could match even specialized cultivators through sheer force of biology. They run towards battlefields, towards sites of writhing flesh, into the heart of the Beast Tide’s waves.

Behind her, a thing of artistry and mechanism twitches, poking at the world in specific patterns, and its Intent tells her where to guide them. [PERFECTED ARTISTRY OF FUNCTION] acts as conductor to the first wave of the battle, transforming what would be a messy flood into directed rivers, efficiently moving towards the function of stopping the war.

The stars, and the lightning they’ve become, are flagging. There’s only so much Qi to spare, and [Divine Strikes Of Starry Tribulation] isn’t strong enough to contend with all the powers about. Already, pushing it so far past its limits has shredded it, opened great golden wounds in the fractal limbs that encompass the lightning, and some of the High Warriors and Divine Beasts are pushing through.

Raika lets them, redirecting the remaining faux-Tribulations to the Emperors and the Apex above. Probably pretty irritating for them too, even though she’s sure that they’ll be able to brush it aside with no trouble at all.

Besides the trouble she’s already giving them, obviously.

There is a man who Is Not There.

An inverted canyon, mountain, falling thunderhead, flesh abomination and more swings towards her.

Rather than aim at him, she takes what is Truth and… adds to it.

She has the weight for that, now. Holding the rapidly bleeding Heart-Realm as part of herself, she has enough ontological pressure to confirm things.

He’s Not There.

The attack doesn’t land, because he isn’t where it came from.

Some of the transmorphic metal of her body bubbles and bursts, boils over, evaporates and cracks, but it shields what’s left of her biology from the pressure of the act, and it buys her another second.

Some of the faster Warriors comes at her, breaking through the battles still being waged between lightning-strikes. An upside-down volcano that is the sky rushing to meet the earth like a meteorite, a jungle made entirely of ice, a single sculpture crafted to overcome all others and so detailed that cities grow in its marble, and others beside, rushing towards her. The heat of it is enough to liquify earth and air alike, and the pressure bends the sunrise down with his weight.

She opens her palm, letting go of something she was holding as–

A cube made of cubes made of paintings bubbles up out of the mountain below her, an Emperor retaliating against the newcomers and the sunrise both.

Zhen Zixiang might not be an equal to the Emperors of the Peaks, but to even a dozen Warriors, the distance is immeasurable.

[ENDLESS LANDSCAPES DEMAND ENDLESS INSPIRATION] once again blooms, redirected out from the Divine Beast that Zhen Zixiang has kept struggling with to act as a desperate counter to the chaos all around. The world is repainted in dozens of new shades, pre-prepared artworks and more drowning out the flood of incoming chaos for a moment.

Her palm is open, and she tilts it down.

A dozen of the Domain-Soul’s portraits are torn to nothingness as a flood of trees blooms out of the mud.

Millions of fresh flora explode out from the central mountaintop of the bloody sunrise, casting shadows over the battlefields. Where they emerge, their roots spread, consuming the corpses and detritus and magics all around, turning them to mulch alongside the protean mud they spawn from, spreading out further.

The country that is a battlefield begins to turn a purplish-blue shade of life, strange leaves stirring in the winds of conflict.

In the woods, metal clinks on metal, teeth scrape on teeth.

The thunderstorm of starlight above collapses as [Divine Strikes Of Starry Tribulation] collapses, sinking back down into what’s left of the rapidly decompressing Heart-realm. All the Qi and ontological weight it was wielding to strike above its weight class becomes free to redirect.

[JUDGEMENT IN THE DARK OF THE WOODS] wasn’t in the Nascent Soul Realm when it began bathing in the light of her Dao garden and the Heart. And since then, it’s consumed one lesser and one equal Soul besides.

The battlefield transforms as [JUDGEMENT] arrives from the shadows in the form of a monster.

And yet, something hits her.

A pinprick of light, no more than a pointed sunbeam of a blue star, unmakes the left side of her body, only barely redirected by the painted landscapes and the trees blooming around her. If not for that, she’d have lost her head.

She stumbles back, but doesn’t bother trying to dodge or repair herself. She can’t move fast enough to make a difference right now, and already, [PERFECTED ARTISTRY OF FUNCTION] is working on a replacement, building her back together. She stays singlemindedly focused on the war ahead of her, on the other attacks already incoming.

[JUDGEMENT IN THE DARK OF THE WOODS] is considerably stronger than [Divine Strikes…], but at this level it’s not just about strength. Most of the beings here think themselves beyond judgement and have the power to back it up. Tribulation lightning, meanwhile, as much as it’s a lesser shadow of the real thing, has properties to it that are hard to deny and harder still to endure. While the judgemental beast overtakes the field, pacifying the battles below one by one as the forests spread, the greater powers above are free to continue acting.

Some of them are already moving to strike at the green all around. The shadow of the slithering thing in the atmosphere above is fighting against the bloody sunrise, crawling back to earth, and wherever it lands just vanishes completely. The Emperors above, mostly involved with the beast in the sky and supporting their juniors, are grand enough to be able to multitask easily, and wipe her out almost as easily in turn.

She’s not spread across the landscape. She’s not decentralized. She is here, in one body, alone.

So, she gets tricky.

The metal of her body, actively regenerating from the attentions of [PERFECTED ARTISTRY OF FUNCTION], splits, part of itself growing out from the shielding of her sternum to form into a face.

“Priority acquisition confirmed! Protect the asset! For the Family!”

Feng Lan’s voice echoes out from out of her, and thus out of the sunrise, out of the trees. It’s loaded with Intent, wrapped tight in the leftover fragments of the body she’s wearing / that became her, and with his Soul devoured by [JUDGEMENT…] and a part of the trees below?

A single, massive sequoia bursts from the woods to intercept a sea of falling spears. Whipping vines stretch out from the trees to wrap tightly around a serpent-thing made of liquid purity, blue and brilliant, slowing it enough that some of the abominations she’s let loose can jump down its throat and begin to tear at it and die inside it.

She doesn’t get all of them, but enough of the High Warrior saboteurs of the Feng Clan reply with well-trained proficiency to the recognizable voice of one of their own that she survives the next laser, the next invisible flechette, the next wave of pressurized arrogance that pulverizes some of the mountain around her.

Which is great, because she really wasn’t sure she could even do that.

“[PERFECTED ARTISTRY OF FUNCTION]! Is it ready?”

“It is ready to achieve its purpose, and thus–”

“Then fire!”

[PERFECTED ARTISTRY OF FUNCTION] isn’t much of a fighter directly. It was never a Soul designed for war, and time in isolation and self-isolation haven’t changed that.

But it is very good at building things. And one of the trees in her Dao garden is of gunpowder and projectile alike.

Raika calls on something deep inside, independent of anything but her own identity, and holds the Soul in her palm closely, so that it acts as an extension of herself and vice-versa.

She’s not sure why she does it. Not sure why she knows how. But something in her sings, and it feels almost easy to match the tune.

TRANSCENDENT ART: A BULLET FOR EVERY BREATH

The same spears of orange metal that skewered what was Feng Lan fire out from inside the sunrise above her, moving so fast that the air cracks as they fire. Charged by purest plasma, through machinery she simply does not understand, the person-sized flechettes vanish into the world.

And, imbued with transcendent magics, every one of them finds their target, no matter how fast or esoteric they may be.

Pretty much all of her targets are fast enough, powerful enough, that they put up defenses in time to catch the hypersonic projectiles. The ones that don’t are the ones that trust their physiques or auras to protect them.

The flechettes hit barrier arrays, magical shields, protective talismans and techniques– and change. Orange metal glows with the depths of its Dao, reaching for higher concepts, and instantaneously shift to match their defenses and slip past them.

A few hundred Warrior Realm opponents stagger, fall out of the sky, are impaled or weakened enough that their opponents take advantage.

A dozen Emperors above are struck blows, some for the first time in millennia, leaving cuts on demigods above demigods.

The battlefield changes again as they respond to her, no longer rushing madly at her, wary now.

Into the space created by the momentary hesitation, the land howls.

The forests are cracked and overturned, trees uprooted, mountains and craters alike cracking and disgorging a sea of flesh. Tendrils of red, grey, purple, bone-white, exploding up out of every available surface, out of every hidden place that she’s spread herself to. Across the entirety of the Republic of Morae, lit by a transcendent sunrise, the forests grow crimson with supernal blood and gristle.

And then she emerges.

[TRANSCENDENT ART: SCULPT OF LIFE AND MADNESS]

The base of the mountain cracks like an egg, stone tumbling and ricocheting as boulders the size of buildings cause rockslides and craters below. The Watchful Fields Sect defends the millions in its borders with desperate acts of magic, the painted Soul-Domain of Zhen Zixiang holding the tide against the collapse, if only barely.

Out of the shattered stone comes a face.

Bulbous. Malformed. Six eyes, several holding dozens of pupils that roll and burst and reform chaotically. Malformed antlers, more like barbed wire, stretching out of a scalp large enough to hold a city atop it. It’s too large for its own good, too large for its own survival, but still it moves, forcing biology to obey as it rises.

Clouds of crimson escape it, the heat of its body vaporising its own blood, obscuring it– but then it opens its mouths, chaotically spaced across the mass and of all different shapes, and screams.

MINE

Raika looks at herself, and her heart hurts.

So this is what I am, without myself.

In the back of her head, she hears the same screaming that’s yet to stop, reflected in the real world. A self-destructive agony, a mania born of desperation and deprivation.

It’s so very lonely, and it hurts so very much.

But she also knows that there’s no way she can reach herself now. Not with words. If, even with the intimacy of direct neural connection, she still overtook all those other parts of herself, silenced so many of her own voices, then what good would simple words do?

Maybe someone better than her could do it. Someone more skilled in that arena.

Maybe not.

She’s herself, and she’s here. So she turns to face the screaming madness that she remembers being, so very recently, and opens her arms wide, one still regenerating to wholeness.

“Come take me then.”

“So it shall be.”

There is a man who is here.

Not anywhere else. Not There. A new use of his Truth, maybe; if he isn’t there, he must be Here.

It’s impossible to track him, but the trick kept him back for a moment. She turns, catching him out of the corner of her eye, standing opposite the mass of madness that is even now sloughing like an ocean up the mountain.

A spear of orange metal impales him, through the shoulder and upper rib cage. It bleeds, along with thousands of other, older wounds, long left untreated, many of them infected. His robes are a mess, his hair partially burned away, one of his eyes blood red from burst vessels.

And he is holding a nothingness that aches, because it is shaped like her. Shaped like the madness approaching.

Raika, the mass of flesh, SCREAMS, and throws itself at her.

The Feng That Isn’t swings a Daemon-Soul that matches them both.

It’s almost funny. Fuck it, it is funny. So she laughs, and aches, and acts.

The mountain drowns in flesh, sunrise, and inversion as the Jiang’Hu burns.
