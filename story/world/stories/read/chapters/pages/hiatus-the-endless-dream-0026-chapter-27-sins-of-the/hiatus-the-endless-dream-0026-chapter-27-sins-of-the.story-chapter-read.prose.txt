My vision fully came to.

I realized that what I thought was a lone statue projected many times was anything but, and that I was in the center of a lower dais surrounded by a colosseum of vicious spectators.

The more I looked, the more I understood they were all unique. Some held torches, others swords or spears. A few held rocks high above their heads, and one even aimed a rifle at me. But every single one was pointing at me in anger.

Another voice came again, and I didn't know from where.

"Bandit."

The voice grew louder. Everything else went quiet. Not silent, I could still hear my heart. I began standing again, and I swore I heard the faintest shuffle. I stomped my foot to make sure, but I couldn't hear it.

I stared up to see the ceiling of a cave, with crystal lights shining down and illuminating the almost ten-foot diameter arena. My head pivoted on an axis, and I saw a large metal gate built into the cave wall.

Screeching metal barely broke through the murky barrier around my ears, and even with that temporary protection, the sound made me flinch.

"Marauder."

Louder still, and it echoed in my ear, repeating over and over again.

I started running to the edge of the circle, not bothering to look back at whatever was on the other side of the gate.

"Scourge."
