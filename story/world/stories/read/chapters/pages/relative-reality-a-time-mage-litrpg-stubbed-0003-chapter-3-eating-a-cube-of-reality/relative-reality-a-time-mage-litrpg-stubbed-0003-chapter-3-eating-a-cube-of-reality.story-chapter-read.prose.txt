The essence floating above Rowan shattered into a fine dust. The still glowing particles of essence spun in a complex pattern around him and a tiny section of it split off and shot down one of the ten lines to the outer circle. There, it formed into a much smaller gem that floated above the point that the line met the outer circle.

The salt that had been poured into the etched circle began to glow with heat until it melted. Both the line linking the outer gem and the inner circle itself were glowing significantly brighter than any of the rest of the molten salt until, in a flash, the dust of the Reality Essence compressed down into Rowan’s chest, sinking beneath his skin. The gem that had formed at the outer circle became translucent then, as soon as the last particle of Reality Essence had disappeared, it came flying toward him and sank into his chest with nothing more than a light tugging feeling.

You have absorbed a Reality Essence.

Innate abilities unlocked.

Innate ability “Warped Space” has been unlocked

Innate ability “Flickerstep” has been unlocked

Innate ability “Timeless Body” has been unlocked

Absorption of Reality Essence has created and absorbed a Stone of Reality.

Ability “Relativity” has been unlocked.

You are now capable of absorbing solidified mana directly to sustain yourself.

You are now capable of advancing your power through the training of the Body, Mind and Soul facets.

You are now Tier 1.

Rowan gasped as a feeling of power swept through his body. He had been frozen to the spot during the entire ritual, unable to move even if he had wanted to.

Nearby, Helja clapped her hands together excitedly.

“Well done!”

Rowan sank to his knees as a sickening feeling ran through his entire body. A violent shudder ran down his spine and his right eye twitched ever so slightly. He wiped at his mouth and found blood on the back of his hand, staring at it wide eyed.

“Is that normal?” he managed to ask quietly.

Esmerelda handed him a cloth which he used to wipe away the rest of the blood.

“Oh, quite normal indeed, dear. Congratulations.”

Slowly feeling better from the ordeal, Rowan instinctively knew what his new ability did but, by thinking about it, a blue box with its description appeared.

Relativity - Body, Mind, Soul - Affliction - Blessing - Stone of Reality

Tier 1 Effect:

You are able to affect the relative time of a target. Increase or decrease the time stream of the target for a small amount of mana per second. More drastic increases or decreases use exponentially more mana. Targets with larger mass use exponentially more mana.

To escape the bonds of time is no easy feat.

Rowan stared at the description. He could change a target’s relative time?

Of course, he immediately tried it on himself. He increased his own relative speed and the world around him slowed ever so slightly. It wasn’t much of a difference but he wasn’t running it as far as it could go. He ramped up the power of the ability, instinctively feeling how to do just that. Everything slowed even further, to the point that Helja’s voice was a little deeper as it reached his ears.

“Having fun?”

Rowan sheepishly scratched the back of his neck, deactivating the power.

“Yeah, actually.”

Both Helja and her mother Esmerelda were looking at him like they wanted to ask what the essence had been and what the ability was but didn’t want to be impolite, while William just asked anyway.

“So, spit it out. What did you get? You should be able to feel what the essence encompasses and what the ability does.”

Esmerelda rolled her eyes while Helja slapped her father’s arm lightly.

“Father! You need to work on your tact.”

He just shrugged, still looking at Rowan.

“Um, the essence is Reality and the ability I got is called Relativity, it lets me speed up or slow down things with larger targets taking more to affect.”

Helja’s eyes widened as she grinned while Esmerelda looked at him like she might start dissecting him at that moment. William just looked thoughtful.

“I’ve never heard of a Reality Essence before. New one to me, Ezzy?” William asked.

“Oh dear, I’ve never heard of it either! It will certainly have some exotic abilities with a name like that though, don’t you think?”

Helja turned to her parents.

“Oh we just need to get him into my team, I have a feeling that his abilities will be beyond helpful in all kinds of situations! Portals, storage spaces, these things don’t come easily!”

Rowan, who was looking at the list of the new innate abilities now listed under his innate trait, heard her say that and paused.

“Hold on a minute, your team?”

She looked at him with a brilliant smile.

“Of course! I just started attending Birkston Adventurer Academy, see, and upon beginning our training, all adventurers form teams! Now, you don’t have any training right?”

He opened his mouth to respond but she kept talking.

“So, I’m sure we can get you through the basics quite quickly and into the academy in short order before the applications close for the year, mother?”

Esmerelda nodded thoughtfully.

“Well, yes, of course dear. I can pull a few strings to get his lack of background worked around in a quick manner.”

William held up a hand to halt both women in their plotting as Rowan went back to reading his new innate abilities. Rowan wasn’t sure about joining any kind of team. Besides, wanted to go back home when he could… didn’t he?

“Well hold on a moment, we don’t know that he will gain any kind of powers that will be useful to you dear, I think we should consider this a bit before acting rashly.”

Helja folded her arms.

“Well, you want to consider carefully now but not when you say such rough mannered things?”

William looked like he was going to reply but Rowan spoke first.

“Actually, I think I already got both of those things?”

They all looked over at him and William frowned.

“What things?”

“A teleporting power and a storage power?”

The first two of Rowan’s innate abilities were called Warped Space and Flickerstep

Innate Ability - Warped Space

You can warp the space around an item you are holding, storing it or later retrieving it if already stored. You are limited to five items. This limit increases with the Soul facet.

Innate Ability - Flickerstep

You may take every fourth step instantly, your personal time moving forward ever so slightly. Increasing the Mind facet will allow you to add or subtract one from the steps required to activate this ability.

He explained the two innate abilities to the avian family in front of him and Helja took off a bracelet, handing it to him.

“Here, try to store that.”

Rowan stared down at it and then pushed it forward. It felt like he was trying to push the bracelet through honey until with a strange twisting feeling around his hand, the bracelet vanished.

“Esmerelda dear,” William murmured.

“Hmm?”

“Have a look about getting Rowan here into the academy, would you?”

“Of course, dear.”

Esmerelda wandered off and William looked at Rowan. His casual, but somehow dismissive, expression of respect had changed to one of a hard taskmaster.

“Right, come here son. If we’re going to have you on our daughters' team, you’re going to have to work hard from here on out.”

Helja remained silent as Rowan came down the steps toward William, a protest on his lips.

“But I don’t know i-”

“Now, show me that ability that lets you take every fourth step immediately.”

William was so much like Rowan’s memories of his own father that he found himself complying, a little curious about the ability anyway.

He took one step, then two more. On the fourth he allowed the ability to activate. Nothing happened.

“Wow, that is quite an ability!” Helja cheered.

Rowan looked down dejectedly.

“Yeah, I know. I don’t know why it didn’t work.”

“What?”

“Yeah, it felt like it activated but nothing really happened.”

William came over and put a hand on his shoulder.

“It worked son, you might just not have noticed if time was still normal for you, try again but look at us this time.”

He did as William said and this time, as he took his fourth step, they froze in place. The moment his foot hit the ground again, they resumed their normal movement.

“Oh! That is so cool,” he exclaimed.

“Excellent. These are good innate abilities. As you progress different facets they will gain additional abilities as well, though only very minor changes compared to what your stone awakened abilities will gain.”

Rowan flexed a hand, feeling how he was able to retrieve the bracelet he had stored. He did so, pulling it out of thin air with the same warping feeling and moving to hand it back to Helja.

On the way over, he used the fourth step ability once more but this time he tried to hold his foot still in the air. Hoping he was able to freeze there indefinitely, he was let down as time resumed the second his foot stopped moving.

Using the ability every time he could just for the unique experience of it, he handed the bracelet back to Helja who was grinning at him.

“Fun, right?

He nodded.

“I won’t lie, most of this is breaking my idea of what is real and what isn’t but I must admit it is fun.”

William came up next to them and folded his arms.

“Good thing you’re having fun, because we’re about to work you to the bone.”

Rowan took a step back.

“Oh, um, why?”

Helja pointed at herself proudly.

“I’m Tier two, that only happens through the advancement of your facets. Un-essenced people are Tier zero and those who have just received them are Tier one, like you, unless they put in the work before getting their essence in which case they can sometimes skip to Tier two. We need to get you to that Tier as soon as possible, so father here will help you work through your facets to get there.”

“Oh, right. I’m still not sure if I want to become some kind of adventurer like you’re saying but I’d like to know what that means anyway. I saw on the Relativity ability that it says soul, body and mind. Do they make Relativity better?”

Helja looked at her father.

“Well, father here used to be an adventurer too, so he knows better than I do.”

William harrumphed.

“You know as well as I darling. But yes, you are correct Rowan, in a way at least. Your tier is a representation of the average of your facets and increasing those facets will in turn increase the power of your essence abilities, as well as your innate abilities. Your first essence ability using all three facets is quite rare and marks it as a powerful one, since you need to reach Tier two with all your facets before it will increase in power.”

Rowan swallowed, beginning to understand what they were planning to put him through.

“I think I understand what the Body and Mind facets will need, working out and mental exercises like study. Those two make sense but what about Soul?”

“The Soul facet is increased by testing your morals or dedication, son. From experience, it doesn’t matter what those are, you could be evil and your dedication is to kill as many people as possible. It doesn’t matter, as long as you stand by it through thick and thin. People who have the strength of their dedication tested harder often find themselves advancing their Soul facet quite quickly. Though, people who dedicate themselves to some kind of evil moral don’t usually live within society as others do.”

Rowan began to sweat a little. He was fine doing some physical exercise and a bit of study. He needed the first and the second had basically become part of his nature since starting university. Having his morals thoroughly tested though? That sounded like it might not just be hard, but traumatic.

“Okay, sure. What does improving each of these facets actually give me, other than increasing my tier?”

William shook his head.

“Enough of that for now, first I want to know what your third innate ability is. You should have one linked to each of the three facets.”

Rowan looked back down as he summoned the blue box listing his innate abilities.

“Yeah, the storage one is linked to my Soul facet while the other is linked to the Mind facet. The last one is called Timeless Body and is linked to my Body facet, obviously.”

William gestured for him to explain.

“Well, I don’t think it will be very useful. It helps with poisons and stuff.”

He read out the ability, frowning at it.

Innate Ability - Timeless Body

Poisons, Diseases and Ailments you are suffering from are considerably slowed down in effect. Negative effects related to time have a reduced impact on you. Increasing the Body facet increases the effect of these abilities.

“Not very useful…”

William looked Rowan up and down.

“Well you’re just full of surprises aren’t you son. Tell me, why don’t you think this ability is very useful?”

Rowan shrugged.

“Because how common could time magic be? I know I got it but this Reality Essence must be quite rare, right? The slowed poisons, diseases and ailments is nice, sure, but it doesn’t say they’re any less powerful, just that they are slowed down.”

William shook his head, looking up at the bright sky above them.

“Son, we are going to have to change up your way of thinking. Let’s start with your second point there. Slowed poisons and all the rest might be no less powerful but it gives your body the ability to deal with them before they have a real chance to do any damage. That ability should be quite weak to begin with but as your Body facet increases, it will allow you to cope with much more than usual. As for your first point, let me ask you this. Outside of magic, what are some time related effects?”

Rowan looked at him, thinking about it. He did make a good point about the slowing of poisons meaning they could be dealt with without doing as much damage. Something like a snake bite would probably be easier to deal with, which was nice. His eyes widened as he realised what William was prompting him to realise.

“Are you telling me I am going to age slower?”
