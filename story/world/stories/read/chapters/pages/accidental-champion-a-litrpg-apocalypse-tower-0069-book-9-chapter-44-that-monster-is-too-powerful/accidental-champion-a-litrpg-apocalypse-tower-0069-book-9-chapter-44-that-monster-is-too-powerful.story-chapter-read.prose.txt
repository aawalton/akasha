Amarantha stood at the viewing screen on her ship. The A Grade captain was the most powerful time mage in the entire Empire of Servitude’s armada. She had powers over time that the other time mages within the empire could only dream of possessing.

And yet, she’d never even heard of someone having the same mastery over time as Xavier Collins possessed.

She was the one who’d noticed what was happening to the first thousand ships. If not for what she’d done… well, it was hard to think about.

Now, after many waves had gone by, and the System timer had been altered and then returned back to what it should be, Xavier Collins had returned to space.

And he’d brought his drones.

Not that anyone could see them.

Amarantha: [Any luck breaking through their new concealment?]

The Communication Channel was silent for a long moment before someone finally replied in the negative.

Amarantha released a long sigh. In her right hand, she held a staff topped with a purple crystal. Her long fingers gripped loosely around the shaft.

She swayed to the left and caught herself on the hull, leaning heavily as her forehead touched the cold surface of the viewing screen. When she’d first activated her own Time Alteration spell, she hadn’t thought she would have to maintain it for very long. In the grand scheme, it hadn’t been long, but she was pushing the field as hard as she could. She had to push it hard if she wanted to be close to matching how fast Xavier Collins’s field was.

That monster is too powerful. How has he been able to maintain his shield for so long, over such a large area?

She couldn’t cancel her shield and let another of the armada’s time mages take her place. None of them were powerful enough. And she wouldn’t be able to cooldown her spell.

It’s only a matter of time now. He’ll be defeated. He can’t hide from the fleet much longer…

It was as she was finishing that thought that something changed.

She cocked her head to one side, adrenaline filling her body with renewed energy.

Something had shifted.

She’d been able to feel Xavier’s time distortion, when it had been pushed out and encompassing the entire area. But then it had all but disappeared when he’d contracted it to only cover himself. Then, that concealment had strengthened, making it impossible to detect him and his time distortion at all.

This was something else. Something other. Her mind felt sluggish as she tried to register what was happening.

An opposing time distortion…

Another time dilation field had been spread over the area, though not as wide as Xavier’s last one. And this wasn’t as powerful as Xavier’s. One of his drones? They seem to possess all his abilities, even though that shouldn’t be possible…

The opposing time dilation field was slowing time. And…

Amarantha’s eyes widened.

The field was active around the same area as her own.

Her mind’s processing speed amped up and up as she tried to understand what was truly happening. Something like this shouldn’t have been possible. How could Xavier, let alone one of his drones, cancel out her time dilation field?

That was when her spiritual sense discerned something she hadn’t noticed at first—it wasn’t merely one foreign time dilation field that had become active. It was a dozen. Every single one of those fields was working against her own, covering the entire fleet, slowing down time to an absolute crawl. They weren’t so slow that they were at the normal pace of the universe’s time stream—that much she could easily discern—but they were damned close.

Which meant—

Amarantha didn’t sense Xavier Collins or his approach. All she felt was one of his sharp bone swords being shoved through her back, as she entered his time dilation field at the exact same time as the sword made contact. Her breath caught in her throat.

The blow was a lethal one, but it didn’t kill her in a single instant as she’d expected.

The pain was unbelievable.

Amarantha was a time mage. She’d done well to stay away from pain.

She was never the one in the thick of battle like Xavier Collins was. That man was no time mage. Not a true time mage. He was a monster.

He could have made this a killing blow. Why…

The answer came to her the moment she noticed the purple smoke swirl toward her open, gasping mouth. The foreign Willpower Energy entered nose and ears as well.

She fought back, but she was already so tired, and now in so much pain. And he was so powerful.

Too powerful…

For the first time in her very long life, her mind fell to the power of another, and she knew this would be the last time any choice she made was her own.

Xavier invaded every part of Amarantha’s mind. If any of the A Grade captains in the Empire of Servitude’s armada would be aware of where the other time mages in the fleet were, it would be one of their own. The instant he took full control, he had her cancel her time dilation field.

He hadn’t been sure that his plan would work. It wasn’t often he worked against opposing time dilation fields, although as he had the ability to use spell patterns, he had experimented with what happened when he had more than one field active in an area.

Still, he hadn’t been sure this would work. And the only reason he’d been confident it might was because of how powerful his drones had become since he’d arrived on this floor. He was able to use their Soul Energy, through their Immortal Soul lenses, to directly alter reality outside themselves. And with the drones, he didn’t need to spare their Soul Energy—he could use every drip until they dried out completely if he needed to.

And he had needed to.

Xavier was beginning to realise that there was very little out there that he wasn’t capable of. After he’d taken control of Amarantha, he’d taken care of her crew. All one hundred thousand B Grades on the ship were dead, and he had the location of the other A Grade time mages in the fleet. There were more than he’d expected—at least four thousand—but none of them were as powerful as Amarantha.

The dozen drones he’d used to cancel out Amarantha’s drones were close to death as they’d used up so much of their Soul Energy, but that didn’t matter—they’d served their purpose.

He looked sideways at Amarantha. Once his drones had all but cancelled out her time dilation field, it had been easy for him to take out the ship’s shield—he’d used the hundred drones he’d had surrounding the ship to do that. The other ships hadn’t fired upon them. They couldn’t fire upon them—they were moving too slow to even realise what was going on.

Then, he’d slipped onto the ship with ease, making his way to the enemy time mage without her even being aware he was there.

Once he’d come up with a workable plan, it was easy.

Now he had the information about the other time mages from Amarantha, there was no reason to keep her alive.

He raised his chin, cocked one of his bone swords back, then swung it straight at her neck. As she was only a part of his time dilation field while his blade was touching her, her head didn’t slip off her neck. It remained there, frozen.

She almost looked like she was still alive.

The kill notification told him otherwise.

Xavier surveyed the ship. The dead A Grade time mage. The dead B Grade soldiers. He marvelled at what he’d been able to do. A smile slipped onto his lips.

Roln materialised a few feet away. The mental construct looked around at the carnage. “A piece of cake…”

He shook his head slowly. The mental construct had a strange look on his face. He didn’t say anything more, but Xavier interpreted the dead Wanderer’s look as disbelief.

Xavier had to admit he was a little surprised himself. Had he taken down the entire armada? No. But now that he’d taken out their most powerful time mage, and had a way of countering the others should they activate time dilation fields of their own…

The job was all but done.

~

Xavier boarded one ship after another, dealing with every Denizen aboard each of them, not sparing a single person among the enemy. When he’d dealt with a thousand more crews—a thousand more waves that wouldn’t be able to reach the planet—he half-expected another time dilation field to spring up.

But it didn’t.

Every single ship he boarded was a ship with the next most powerful A Grade time mage in the armada. One by one, he eliminated the true threats to his goal.

It wasn’t long before he’d eliminated every single A Grade time mage in the entire armada. There was nothing stopping him from sweeping through every single ship in the fleet.

Nothing stopping him from finally reaching B Grade.

And so, he kept moving. Kept killing.

The faces of the dead blurred.

The numbers racked up.

Millions upon millions of dead enemies. And through all of it, he still had the wave reward active from when he’d defeated the last wave, allowing him to gain more Mastery Points per kill, as the wave the S Grade True Dragon was a part of was still active back down on the planet.

Xavier reached Level 400 long before the fleet was fully dealt with. The A Grades gave him far more Mastery Points than he’d accounted for. It wasn’t merely levels he’d been gaining, either. With every ship he boarded, more tweaks to his neural pathways were made. Every spell pattern he manifested was refined, over and over. Every movement. Every cycle of energy through his pathways.

And with each level he gained, he cycled more refined Celestial Energy from his drones into his body, strengthening himself again and again until he hit the new limit, then the next new limit, then the next…

Now, he was more than eager to discover what advancing to B Grade would feel like. As a C Grade, he was already stronger than the A Grades he was facing, with and without a time dilation field on his side.

How much stronger would he become once he advanced?

Xavier sat in the middle of a vacant room inside one of the Empire of Servitude’s spaceships, surrounded by drones. Each drone had an active time dilation field, in case his own didn’t survive the imminent transformation. His legs were crossed, hands resting on his knees, and he was breathing long, deep breaths.

He brought up the advancement notification.

Congratulations, you have fulfilled all the requirements to advance to B Grade!

If you have not prepared your body, mind, and cores appropriately, your attributes, spells, and skills might be damaged, or the transformation may cause your death.

Do you wish to advance to B Grade?
