Which Feng promptly slapped aside. “Dai. Your Young Master commands you to stop.”

Dai’s qi pulsed erratically for a moment, his dark eyes seeking Feng's. The Young Master did not back down from the disciple's hate-filled gaze. Eventually, Dai stepped back, before taking in a shuddering breath to stabilise himself. It took another three breaths before his muscles relaxed, and his expression turned neutral.

“That was unsightly of me,” he said, tone regretful. “Forgive me, Young Master. I lost control.”

The lapse of restraint could be attributed to his recent resurrection, but even so, to see his Senior Brother lash out like that after mere verbal provocation…

It appeared this woman was quite close to his Senior Brother’s heart indeed.

She pointed to a large bush some ways away from her.

The Split-headed Carnivores Sect is one of the Sects of the Four Mountain Sects Group.
