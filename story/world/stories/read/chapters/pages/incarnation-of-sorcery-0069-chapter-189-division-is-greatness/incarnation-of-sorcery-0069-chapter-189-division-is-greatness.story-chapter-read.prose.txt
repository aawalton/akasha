The Great God descending upon them was Ovrega. She was one Ryland knew too well. Unfortunately well.

Strangely, in all the centuries, she hadn’t changed at all. Or maybe this was just her anti-Ryland outfit, since he had only ever met her in the moments where she had been furiously seeking his head.

She was armoured head to toe, the pieces constructed from polished sandstone and abalone. Her plumed helmet blew fire instead of horsehair. Shimmering wings stretched out wide from her shoulders, while an axe larger than she was gleamed red in her shining hands. Air was funnelling into miniature twisters all around her, and lightning danced on her skin and armour.

“There you are,” Ovrega said, ignoring the other two Incarnations present to look only at Ryland. “I’ve been waiting for a long, long time.”

Xyke’s Glyphs were still keeping the Incarnation of Growth trapped, though it required her to remain focused on containing their initial adversary. In other words, Ryland would need to face Ovrega alone so long as they intended to keep the Incarnation of Growth tamped down.

“Were you so desperate,” Ryland said. “That you ended up joining forces with the despicable Order?”

“Sheltering the meek and the weak from the ravages you intend to inflict merely seems like the right choice,” Ovrega said. True enough. It was quite difficult to make Great Gods fall for little mind games like that. “Just like what you’re doing to this poor Incarnation here. Attacking one of your own in such a manner… and you called the Order despicable.”

It was also true that they were more than experienced enough to turn any mind games right back onto one who had started it all.

“Let’s end this prattle,” Ryland said. “If you’ve come to annihilate me, then you have your chance. Only a fool would end such an opportunity. After all, unlike that idiot Jahaevo, you don’t even have me trapped.”

“Don’t I?”

Her fiery eyes turned to where the Incarnation of Growth was trapped by Xyke. Ah, yes. Ovrega knew Ryland wasn’t going to abandon his allies. He wasn’t going to let them die in his stead.

“But you’re right.” Ovrega raised her gleaming axe, which began bleeding bright blue energy. “Let’s end this.”

She flashed at him. A god’s speed wasn’t something even Ryland could match. He already knew it from his time facing Jahaevo, and from the last time he had faced the other Great Gods such as Ovrega.

So, he didn’t bother dodging or evading. He just attacked as well.

“[Continental Step],” Ryland cast.

At the same time as his firing manasoul turned unquestionably bright, Ryland was also manipulating the Conceptual Glyphs in the air before him. He turned the air impossibly solid and dense, creating a wall that even a Great God couldn’t easily break through.

She did break through, however. Ovrega smashed into the suddenly-manifested wall and burst out with an explosion, her axe still swinging with deadly intent.

But that little moment where she had been slowed down had been enough. The [Continental Step] appeared without warning, smashing into the Great God with enough force to slam her back. It lasted for less than the blink of an eye. The axe continued swinging, and it split the entire enormous foot with a slice that could have serrated a continent.

Now, the Great God was shooting straight at Ryland through the separated halves of the enormous foot. And worse, behind her was a second version of the Great God.

That was one of Ovrega’s powers. Every time she took a hit, a second version of her formed. As if one Great God wasn’t bad enough.

The two versions of Ovrega didn’t attack him. Not at first, and not together. They didn’t need to. Against “just” an Incarnation, the Great God of Division didn’t need multiple versions of herself.

So, while one rushed towards Ryland, the second aimed for the other Incarnations in play.

The first Great God smashed straight at Ryland with blinding speed. There was no subtlety about her assault, no trickery or deception. She had no need for one. Just reaching Ryland would be enough to ensure her victory.

Which was why she only crashed through an illusion brought on by [Timescape].

“Coward!” Ovrega screamed as she whirled around, locating the real Ryland in an instant. “Face me yourself instead of running away!”

Ryland was doing just that. His little trick had once again brought him just enough time. His manasoul was turning apocalyptic in its furious outburst of energy as he called on potent spell after potent spell. “[Endless Evolution]. [Eternity’s Aura]. [Wings of Salvation].”

Rifts broke within his body, powered by the divine energy he was drawing in. His second spell imbued him with monstrous energy and a cyclone of mana adding to the sheer eruption that his manasoul had become. Then there were the wings. Twin appendages reared high and wide from his back, constructed purely from compressed mana and energy.

The God fired herself at her target. And Ryland took flight.

They flashed through the Wound Corridor with such speed and power, it was like twin lightning bolts were bouncing around within the enormous region of twisted, wounded reality.

Ryland knew he had to protect Xyke and the Incarnation of Growth—which had to be a terribly odd thing to say, considering he and Xyke hadn’t been shy about suppressing it with force. But he knew how Ovrega worked. He knew exactly what her power of Division would do.

So, blindly rushing towards the second instance of Ovrega wasn’t going to help. Not really.

Instead, Ryland needed to act according to the impromptu plan he had come up with.

They bounced around the Wound Corridor like beams of light that had gone insane. It was mad. He was matching a Great God in speed. The very thought was incoherently dreamlike, even for someone like Ryland.

But he persisted anyway, even though he knew he couldn’t keep this up for long, because his real goal would be on hand soon.

“[Translocate]!” Ryland shouted.

Two mirroring rifts opened up. One was just ahead of Ryland, and one right behind where the Great God was chasing him.

As soon as he entered the rift in front of him, it closed. Ovrega couldn’t follow him in, just as he had planned. And just as he had intended, he burst out of the second rift instantaneously, right on his pursuer’s tail. His sudden disappearance had made Ovrega hesitate even if it had been for a fraction of a second.

That infinitesimally small amount of time was enough. Ryland crashed into the Great God with all the momentum and violent, bursting power he had gathered over the entire duration of his chasing flight.

The impact set loose a small nova of heat and light, the boom so loud that even the Great God’s second version missed her shattering blow.

Ryland’s momentum carried Ovrega straight to the edge of twisted reality. They struck the walls of the Wound Corridor so hard, it cracked. Reality itself broke. Golden energy and light spewed inwards in geysers of gold.

Of course, Ryland had already rifted away. Not to where the second version of Ovrega was again attempting to attack Xyke and her captured Incarnation.

No. He had gone to where he had some space to cast his next spell.

“[World Node],” Ryland incanted.

The universe spanning everything turned into a three-dimensional graph, equal regions separated by tiny points inhabited by his rifts. And it wasn’t enough. Not for Ryland. Not if he wanted to deal with a Great God. With three of them, to be precise, since a third Ovrega emerged after Ryland’s last blow.

Right. She got more copies of herself whenever she was hit. Just a small foible of facing the Great God of Division.

Ovrega poked one of the points Ryland had created using [World Node]. “And what is this supposed to do? Make this whole place suddenly your domain? You do see it’s leaking in the Overrealm, don’t you?”

As she spoke, her second version, the one attacking Xyke, completed its move. And of course, to keep the Incarnation of Growth trapped, the Glyphmaster hadn’t moved an inch.

She was quite powerfully determined that way.

The second Ovrega’s slice from far above landed. Xyke wasn’t simply sliced in half. That would unfortunately be too mundane for a Great God. Instead, the cleave just created a second version of Xyke now.

One who was immediately hostile to her originator.

Ovrega laughed at seeing the Glyphmaster jump in alarm. Xyke’s magical attention shifted to the bullish, aggressive new version of her rushing straight for her.

“There is no greater joy in the universe,” Ovrega said. “Than watching your enemies tear themselves apart.”

Great God of Division indeed.

Thankfully, it seemed Ovrega still hadn’t figured out Ryland’s proper intentions. As such, he acted.

“[Universe of Voxels],” Ryland incanted.

Lines extended from all the points making up reality through [World Nodes]. They were thin. Seemingly harmless. But that was how Ryland preferred them to be in their inert state.

“[Translocate],” Ryland incanted next.

At the very instant his spell was cast, the lines bounding the boxed region that the not-Xyke occupied expanded. The fake Incarnation was soon trapped within a void black box, much like being enclosed by [Void Seal].

A second later, the black box opened, revealing nothing but charred remains and a sprinkle of burning magma.

“What in the…?” Ovrega frowned up at Ryland. “What did you just do, interloper?”

Her second version just went back to repeating the same motions again. One might be mistaken into thinking that there was a reason they didn’t speak. But it wasn’t foolishness or a lack of ingenuity. Ryland knew that first-hand.

Rather, like most Great Gods, both Ovrega and her duplicates were frightfully arrogant.

It was that arrogance that made the second Ovrega think it was just a fluke. There was no way her attack could have ended in failure. She just needed to try again. And she did.

That led to the same situation as before, where a violent cleave created two separated Xykes once more. Ryland was even more thorough this time.

“[Translocate],” he incanted.

This time, the onrushing Xyke was swallowed up by another cubic rift. When she reappeared, she continued charging. Except, this time, she was slamming straight into the Great God.

Ovrega screamed as she was attacked. With a smack of her fist, she just made the fake Xyke explode in a shower of magic and gore. One hit. If Ryland hadn’t been already in the embrace of a spell he had prepared for exactly a situation such as this, even he might just have been intimidated at how easily a copy of an Incarnation had simply been wiped out.

Technically, he had done it too with the last one. But a Great God’s sheer, effortless power was something else entirely.

Ovrega turned to face Ryland, then pointed at him. Both of her duplicates immediately shot at him with the same speed and power that she herself could assume. He was facing a Great God. Even her copies and fakes would be terribly difficulty to fight, even for him.

Good thing he had his spell to rely on. “[Translocate].”

Ovrega never reached Ryland. He was transporting her away before she could do so, quickly sending her copies off to other regions of the Wound Corridor. Black boxes went up and down, popping up here and there all over the area, depositing a Great God every time one managed to eat up a copy before she reached Ryland.

“Enough!” the real Ovrega screamed.

She raised her axe aloft, then swung. Reality itself split in two. Well, that was one way of countering Ryland.

The entire region of the Wound Corridor broke into two halves. On one side was the real Great God, as well as Xyke plus the Incarnation of Growth that she had trapped. On the other was Ryland and the two fake Ovregas. And the real issue was that Ryland’s magic couldn’t cross the divide in reality where nothingness existed.

Or should have existed, had it not been filled with a flood of divine energy from the Overrealm.

Ryland continued casting [Translocate] on his side of things. The copies were still too dangerous, and he had to remain as alert as he had to keep them away from him with the teleporting black boxes. However, they were following their originator’s idea, simply slashing through reality to hem in Ryland.

The even bigger issue was that Xyke was finally being forced to relinquish her control over her captive in order to face a wrathful Ovrega about to attack. Thankfully, the Incarnation of Growth finally appeared to have calmed down to some extent. But still. They’d be annihilated.

Thanks to that reality-separating slash from the Great God, Ryland no longer had access to the other half of the World Nodes. He could no longer create black boxes to assist Xyke.

Unless Ryland intervened.

More slashes of unreality tore at Ryland, about to leave him entirely at the mercy of the duplicate Great Gods on his side. But that was just what he needed at the moment.

The spell circle for [Reality Scar] went up in no time, but instead of mana, Ryland drew on the divine energy flooding everything.

“[Reach of Unreality],” Ryland cast, the spell circle turning as bright as a star.

All Ryland did was tap into the divine energy and wrest control of the cracks in reality. He turned his ones into claws that slashed in a wide arc. Not only did that repel the copies of the Great God, granting himself some space, but he had also slashed into the far larger tear that was keeping him locked away from Xyke and the others.

Using divine energy to power spells was having a rough effect on him. His Incarnation form tried to channel it through him, to burn it away like it did mana as though he was a conflagration upon a star.

But it tore off chunks of him instead. It ripped into his being, almost as if it was sacrificing pieces of him to power the sorcery he was casting.

A small price to pay for even a meagre pseudo-victory against a Great God.

“[Realm Tilt],” Ryland incanted. “[Rift Tether].”

Through the breach in unreality, Ryland reconnected with the other half of the World Nodes that had yet remained. Ovrega had once again slashed through Xyke, creating a fake version of her that was barely being held back by a slew of Glyphs. But that effort proved unnecessary when Ryland’s spell came into play.

A gigantic black box formed around them all. Then it tilted. Shifted. Gravity began pulling with increased intensity from a suddenly different angle.

All the Great Gods and their creations cried out and fell.

It was really just surprise getting the better of them. Ryland knew that they were all floating and flying with the help of magic counteracting gravity, which was a force acting from a very specific direction. As such, the countering force was acting in the opposite direction.

So, by suddenly shifting the source of gravity to said opposite direction, Ryland was at least momentarily causing everyone to fall prey to the eternal pull of the universe.

Well, everyone save himself and anyone tied to him. That was where [Rift Tether] came in handy. Both Xyke and the Incarnation of Growth were stuck to him thanks to the link through a rift he had created, remaining static and floating unlike their enemies.

Ovrega quickly recovered, her axe raised aloft once more, but it was too late. “You mongrel coward! Return at—”

Ryland dragged himself and his companions straight into the breach the Great God herself had created with her power of Division. The scar that had halved the entire Wound Corridor. He led them straight into the divine energy, casting [Wound Corridor] himself in an attempt to reach safety. It worked. Kind of.
