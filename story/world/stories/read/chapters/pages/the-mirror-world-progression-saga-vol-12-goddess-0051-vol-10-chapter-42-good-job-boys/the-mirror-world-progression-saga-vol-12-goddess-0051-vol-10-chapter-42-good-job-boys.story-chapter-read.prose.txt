Gelmak Por found the ‘Cursed Old Temple’ to be a refreshing change of pace.

They’d arrived on a typical wilderness isle and sent scouts all across it until the specific location was discovered, lodged up on higher, rocky ground. They then sent a full exploratory expedition, more or less specialized in raiding ruins successfully. The area around the temple ruins was already crawling with ancient skeletal undead, and when they got inside, it was like trying to wade through a sea of decrepit bones and rusted weaponry. Fun!

For the most part, Gelmak hacked through it all in the front, swinging his axe and chopping undead apart, and passing by already tripped traps. The undead appeared to be prior victims of raids, mostly Naugite skeletons, easy to smash through, and easy to end their animation with a split skull. A few were apparently cunning warriors in life, and put up more of a fight, making for a little excitement and a few cuts on his own person to get the blood flowing. Nothing too serious, however. They were all pirates, thieves, and such, after all. Likely, the best of them had gotten all the way through and looted the place.

A couple of times, there were skeletal mages, but nothing capable of getting through his resistance. A thrown axe quickly ended their ambitions in both cases!

When Gelmak got shot full of some annoying poison darts from a reloading magical trap, the team started getting a bit more cautious and utilizing their ‘treasure hunter’ specialist. He was very knowledgeable about traps and even picking locks. That seemed like [Rogue] stuff to Gelmak, but the man had always swore up and down he didn’t have a single level in it, and had never stolen anything in his life. Odd!

Finally, they arrived in a chamber they had to clear out, but once finished, the captain and the cleromage got excited about some murals seen on the walls. Gelmak didn’t particularly get it. To him, they just looked like a bunch of smudgy lines and vague humanoid shapes, but then again, he had no eye for art.

When he asked the captain to boil down what they’d found, the sweaty, dusty, bearded man turned to him and grabbed his hand, shaking it with both of his. “We’ve done it, Gelmak! The signs match! Haha! We’ve found another skies-be-damned spot for the goddesses! We’re gonna be rich, haha ha!” He threw his hat up in the air.

Gelmak blinked, then frowned. “Oh. Rich. That’s… good. But I thought this was more about saving the world and such things? Priorities of the order and the times of the end?”

“Right, right!” the captain called, as he danced a jig with one of his scouts. “That too, lad! We’re savin’ the bloody world, haha!”

After obtaining details on the spot, direct mirror capture of the contours from various angles, mural details within the ruins, and so on and so forth, Samantha utilized these sympathetic impressions, combined with the real-world coordinates, to seek it out on the Bridge of Edges.

It wasn’t long before she found it and popped right in.

You have discovered a [Transition Point] on the Bridge of Edges, preliminary designation ‘Point 10’. Avatar position moved… Anchorage and transition in-world complete.

The hidden quest “Location, Location, Location (find and anchor to ten Transition Points)” is complete. 4(3) exp gained in [Goddess]. 6(3) exp gained in [Magineer]. 4(3) exp gained in [Saboteur].

She arrived, to the relief and ensuing cheerful celebrations of the dead-body-smelling, sweaty, beleaguered gentlemen that had conducted the harrowing expedition. Not only would they be going home, but they’d earned an exceptionally fat commission for discovering something effectively priceless to the order. Even more so after discovering these locations to be needed for Glae’s quest. They’d earned every coin, for sure.

Enjoy it while you can, boys.

More than likely, the place was connected to her sister. Was that enough? Did all of the locations need to connect to Azure’s predecessor in some way? Not all of them had coughed up visions, making it difficult to verify.

She also didn’t particularly want to try a query to her sister or the Primal Mystic. Information was always vague and cryptic at best from that one. Knowing her, she’d phrase some dismissal of it being irrelevant, laced in heavy rhetoric.

The great battle was finally here. Matrazere, to break or be broken, and decide the destiny of either side as a whole, likewise.
