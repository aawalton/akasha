Brian was blinded by the light of the teleportation. When his vision started to clear, his stomach lurched and he barely held his last meal in. He looked up to see the pleased look of a giant wolf. Judah jumped back with his trident pointed at Big Bad. Linda was nowhere to be seen.

“What the hell are you doing here!?” Cassidy cursed as she stepped backwards.

“Your world is bright but a bit harsh when smelled. My ability to travel has long been withheld. Withheld.”

“So we are to believe you simply wanted a vacation?” Cassidy asked sarcastically.

“Think as you might that I am a threat. At least I can stretch without any debt,” Big Bad said, elongating its body as if it had been cooped up in a cage.

“Do you have to speak in rhymes?” Brian asked. “It seems like it’s not the clearest form of communication.”

“To rhyme as I do is long a tradition, take it as a quirk of my disposition. But if you ask if limerick to speak is required… no.” The wolf chuckled.

Quest Complete!

Save the damsel in distress!

A young maiden has fallen into another world and been kidnapped! Objective: Survive the perilous new world, find Megan Dyer, and rescue her!

Quest rewards:

2000 XP

Crystallized Essence of Darkness

2 gold

Congratulations! You are now level 7!

- Amelia the Enthralled -

Amelia sat across from Anastasia, staring at the Elafonisi beach in Greece. The pink sands were unique and were known to draw many tourists, but the beach was empty save for the two of them. Well, three of them, she supposed. As a mental third wheel, Amelia simply attempted to relax and enjoy the view.

“Will you make your move soon?” Anastasia asked.

“Are you growing bored with your life of luxury?”

“No, but I see you whispering to your minions more frequently.”

“Ah, the build-up is the sweetest part of a performance. It is the journey that people tell stories about, not the end.” Mephistus grinned.

“And you like stories more than power?”

“I do, actually. This world has reset, and I find the early struggles to be the best stories. You begin to see who will thrive and become the heroes that the people will rally behind. They form factions, have civil wars… and then the villain comes to play.”

“You being the villain, I suppose?”

“Of course! And it is tragedies that I am the most fond of, aren’t you?”

Anastasia smirked knowingly and let Mephistus continue.

“There is something I am looking for that I allowed to escape my possession. I know the city, but it is surprisingly well hidden, or the owner is wildly incompetent. I am unsure. I mean to use it to help me reap this world’s secrets. There is no rush, however. I will find it, or I will enter this world completely and take it then,” Mephistus said as he sat up and clutched something invisible in his hand.

He smiled as he looked at the ocean and relaxed again. Turning to Anastasia, he asked, “Shall we break to the jacuzzi? The steaming bathhouses were always my favorite.”

“Let’s,” Anastasia smiled.

Mephistus confused Amelia, who had nothing but time to ponder trapped in her mind as she was. She wasn’t sure what type of weird relationship he was looking for with this Anastasia. So far he played one part love interest and one part demon on her shoulder. She was glad he wasn’t actually pursuing any real physical relationship with her. She was already defiled enough being trapped in her own body. She mentally shivered at the thought of playing third wheel to any other activities.

As far as she could guess, he wanted to set up Anastasia as a conqueror when he finally made his move. As they walked into the hijacked villa, she saw one of the taken walk up to a map and circle a building. The shadow-veiled face of a man in a tuxedo bowed to Mephistus and spoke.

“My lord, we have narrowed down a point of interest in Mekland and believe this block of flats may be of interest.”

Mephistus dismissed him with a wave. He liked information trickled in rather than a full accounting. He leaned over the map to look at the name that was circled.

“Windhaven Apartments, a rather majestic name for the tiny little rooms people stuff themselves in, if I do say so,” Mephistus mused.

“This is where your lost item might be?” Anastasia asked.

“It might be.” He smiled.

His fingers traced the map to several black squares that were circled.

“Plenty to choose from… let’s make sure to leave a few to be inscribed with less flamboyant means. I want them to feel as if they have a chance to stop what is coming.”

The taken bowed at his words.

Mephistus reached out a hand and Anastasia took it. They began to walk through the courtyards, and he asked her, “How is your craft coming along?”

“Infatuation has been easy enough for me to progress. Synchronicity is a more complicated spell, but I should have it mastered within a few days.”

“Good, good. Your talents run deep, and that one should help you lead an army with ruthless precision. I am looking forward to your performance. Do let me know if you hit any roadblocks.”

They moved not into any building but to a field with a large bubbling jacuzzi. On the other side of it was a tall black pillar surrounded by a rock garden. Several taken stood on ladders with carving blades that cut into the side of the pillar with ease.

Mephistus and Anastasia settled into the warm water and reclined. A servant came forward and offered them ouzo on ice. They each took one and sipped at the drink, enjoying the strong flavor of anise.

“Anastasia dear, they are almost done. Get ready for the light show.”

Anastasia sat up with anticipation. The sophisticated patterns on the pillar started to smoke. The black cloud moved as a snake would, twisting up the sides until it reached the top. A large crack hit the air and the sound of ripping echoed across the field. The greenery around them desaturated, and color was sucked into the smoke, turning different parts of it silver, gold, and teal.

All of it started to seep into the pillar, causing the black to look like polished onyx.

“Is it done then? Have you opened a path?”

“Not quite. This is like poking a tiny hole into an air balloon. Once we have enough hold, however, the rip will form.”

“And then?”

“And then I will invite all of my party guests. The old world and the new will have a nice little reunion.”

“I can’t wait!” Anastasia smiled.

Mephistus grinned at her enthusiasm. “Enjoy the suspense of the second movement. Let it build for the third act.”
