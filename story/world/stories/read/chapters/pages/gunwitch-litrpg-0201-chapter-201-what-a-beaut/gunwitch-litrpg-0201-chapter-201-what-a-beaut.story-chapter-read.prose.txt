Phisto rolled his eyes. “Tell him no one cares about his chest full of spiders. No one gives a single shit.”

He weakly raised a bony finger. “A chest full of spiders and one chochlik.” Then he shrugged. “And… I dunno. They were putting their webs everywhere and I didn’t know where else to put them. It’s very difficult to reach up high for me and clean all the cobwebs!”

“I literally don’t care. Like at all. Keep doing whatever you’re doing if it makes you feel good, Skelly. You do you.”

“And a chochlik,” Skelly echoed.

“May I ask one thing?”

“Yes?”

“Why the chochlik?”

Skelly gave me a sheepish look. “I thought it was going to tell everyone about my chest.”

“Riiiiight. I guess it deserved it then. Maybe.”

I didn’t really care. Chochliki were a bunch of mischievous little spirits, and if I could be honest, super annoying. It had been stupid of me to let them inside, because now they wouldn’t leave. Maybe stashing a couple of them in a chest full of spiders was exactly what they needed. Maybe then they would learn to behave and be a bit more respectful towards everyone around here. If we were lucky it might even convince them to leave, though that may be hoping for a bit much.

At this, Skelly left the kitchen, possibly inspired to go on a hunt for more spiders to fill his chest with.

“Again!” Once more I closed my eyes. This time I stuck out my tongue, trying to taste the air for where they might have put the head, since that was the one I was most excited about. “This one!” I opened my eyes and found myself pointing at Vasil, the goblet lying next to him on the table. Bogumir shook his head, but didn’t argue.

I took the goblet and held it in the light. It looked nice and sparkly with all those gems.

“Everyone ready?” I glanced around to see everyone nodding at me. I cast [Artifact Index] and read the System text aloud.

[Eternal Goblet of Cleansing]Whatever liquid is put in this goblet will never run out. Should it be poisoned, the poison will be cleansed.

“Ugh. Lame.” I said, putting it back on the table.

“Lame?” Bogumir frowned.
