After running the idea of flying up over the Deadlands and dropping an object with Eternal Mark on it into the center to Samantha, who promised she’d check and see if it was something she was even capable of doing, Asher headed over to the eastern training grounds to meet with the realm wanderers from the Eight Crests. While he wasn’t really interested in swearing allegiance to any one kingdom, he was beyond curious as to what the realm wanderers were going to offer him.

He spotted the same trio who had been waiting outside the palace yesterday sparring against one another, but they had a fourth member with them this time around. Wearing the same partially armored leathers as the others, the new woman looked far older than the other realm wanderers, closer to her late seventies or early eighties if her long, gray hair was any indication. Even so, she didn’t seem to let that slow her down, as she used her quarterstaff to knock aside Mako’s spear, twirling it in her hands and nearly braining the poor man before he could dodge. Just as he was preparing a counter, Mako’s eyes widened as he spotted Asher, and he paused as he grinned at him.

Which made him an incredibly easy target for the wizard’s staff that smashed into the side of his head.

“Lanra, he was clearly distracted!” one of the realm wanderers who had been watching from the side shouted. She had short, messy hair clipped close to her scalp, and a seemingly permanent scowl on her face. As far as Asher could tell, she didn’t have a weapon on her, but her aura was a jumbled mess that he couldn’t really interpret. “Was that really necessary?”

“I would have thought I’d beaten the urge of getting distracted out of you three by now,” Lanra said coolly. Rather than check on Mako, who was still lying dazed on the ground, Lanra turned and nodded toward Asher. “You must be The Reaper.”

“Asher is fine,” he said, trying not to furrow his brow as he stared at her. Similar to the liches, Lanra didn’t read to his Identify or Judgement. He was beginning to think wizards naturally kept up defenses against investigative-type skills, and that Richard was just too lazy to bother, seeing as his skills worked on him. “…Is Mako alright?”

“You know his name?” Lanra asked, her lips forming a thin line as she turned to glare at the stunned man sprawled across the ground. “I told you not to meet with him without me! There are procedures that have to be followed!”

“Technically, he met with me,” Mako argued weakly as he got to his feet, trying to shake off the blow to the head. “Hey, Asher! Good to see you again!”

“You are going to cause an international incident one of these days, and I am going to leave you stranded in the abyss when you do,” Lanra said, letting out an annoyed huff before turning back toward Asher. “I don’t know what Mako told you, but I assume at the very least, you know who we are then?”

“Realm wanderers from the Eight Crests,” Asher nodded. “Donvath told me you’d be here.”

“High Prince Donvath was gracious enough to permit us to speak with you directly, rather than using an intermediary to make our offer,” Lanra continued, stressing Donvath’s official title. “The Eight Crests is a large, powerful kingdom with much to offer all of its citizens. For the last—”

“Lanra, you’re great at navigating the realms, but quite frankly, you suck at reading people,” Mako said, chuckling as she glared at him again. “Look at his eyes! The way he carries himself! He doesn’t care about the history of our ‘great nation’ any more than its actual citizens do, beyond yourself. Here is a man interested in what we do. You want to win him over, talk about that.”

Lanra looked like she had a few choice words she wanted to share with Mako, along with one or two blunt ends of her quarterstaff, but one glance at Asher and she held her tongue. Instead, she sighed and shook her head.

“We want to offer you a position on our team,” she said bluntly. “We are aware that the Noala Kingdom doesn’t have their own dedicated team of realm wanderers, and the four of us are the Eight Crests top team when it comes to delving into the different realms of existence.”

“Technically we’re their only team,” Mako grinned, ignoring the look of pure loathing Lanra shot his way. “The three superpowers, like the Hojon Empire, are powerful enough to have multiple, but the Eight Crests are stuck with us. It’s why I can piss Lanra off so much that she’d never actually leave me behind on a mission.”

“I don’t know, I think she’s getting pretty close!” the female realm wanderer called out, finally strolling over with Jameston at her side, who Mako had pointed out earlier. Asher recognized the third realm wanderer as the one with the Mind element, and he did his best not to let any sort of concern show on his face as he nodded toward them. “I’m Cora, by the way,” the woman continued. “This is Jameston. He’s mute.”

Jameston offered a small wave, giving him a warm smile.

“As I was saying,” Lanra said, taking a deep breath and doing her best to ignore her teammates. “I understand the Hojon Empire was the first to offer you a deal, but knowing them and their priorities, I’m assuming it was to turn you and your Spatial element into little more than a prized cow they could milk at their leisure. We, on the other hand, want you for your skills. We’ve heard about you taking down the various high-ranking members of the Noala Kingdom’s Assassin’s Guild, and we want you to join us as we cross between realms and venture to lands unknown.”

“Other than Lanra, we’re all pretty fun to be around!” Mako said, leaning forward on his spear as he smiled. “Just wait until Jameston starts cracking jokes! He takes a bit to warm up to new people, but the guy’s hilarious!”

“We each get our own manor and surrounding grounds near the capital,” Cora explained, nodding along. “Fully staffed, food provided, basically all your essentials taken care of for free. And anything that isn’t, you can purchase with the shards that the Crown pays us. We get bonuses based on what we bring back, but we also receive a weekly stipend. Being a realm wanderer is a pretty sick gig, so long as you can survive all the dangers.”

“Of course, the dangers are fairly minimal,” Lanra added, frowning at Cora. “Some realm wanderer teams focus primarily on the abyss, as that technically does have the greatest payouts. But it is also the leading cause of death for realm wanderers by a longshot. The Eight Crests takes a far more moderate approach, focusing the majority of our trips on the wild realm. The payoffs of each trip may not be as impressive, but unlike other teams, we boast an average ratio of only losing a realm wanderer every four and a half years, as opposed to the more common eight months.”

“Again, as the Eight Crests’ only team, they can’t risk losing us all in one fell swoop like the larger countries can,” Mako winked. “It’s nice being important!”

“The team also consists of a dedicated mentalist and healer!” Lanra continued, desperate to get through her entire pitch despite Mako and Cora interjecting every few seconds. “I understand you probably view the Mind element as a bad thing due to the Noala Kingdom’s laws, but in fact, it is a massive boon to any team. Jameston has the Mind, Life, and Steel elements. He’s nearly impossible to kill, capable of healing just about anything short of actual death, and ensures that we are always in communication with one another. He’s also a certified therapist, in the event you have any lingering traumas you wish for him to help you with.”

Jameston nodded, giving Asher two thumbs up in response.

“Seriously, he has a skill that will actually let you relive your traumas in your mind,” Mako said, tapping his head. “It doesn’t sound great at first, but it lets you go back and basically do what you wished you could have done in the first place. Save the parent who died right in front of you, prevent yourself from getting hurt in the first place, stuff like that. Most people need to pay quite a lot of shards for access to someone with his skills.”

Jameston blushed slightly, punching Mako gently in the arm as he grinned.

“But you don’t have to relive your traumas if you don’t want to,” Lanra hissed through clenched teeth, looking like she was getting closer and closer to following through with her threat of leaving Mako behind in the abyss sooner than later. “It is just another one of the many perks joining the Eight Crests’ realm wanderer team would bring you. The Crown has also made clear that your first three children would be provided with whatever array of elements they would like from the royal vault, and you yourself would be allowed to select any one artifact of your choosing.”

“What?! Why didn’t we get anything like that offered to us?” Cora demanded.

“Because the two of you were nothing more than marginally talented fighters who managed to survive the application process through dumb luck and, presumably, being too stupid to die,” Lanra snapped. “He is an actual realm wanderer, born on another realm! Do either of you possess the Spatial element? Can either of you actually travel to another realm without my assistance? No? That’s what I thought!”

Throughout this whole rant, Asher watched the team, slowly piecing together the dynamic. As far as he could tell, Mako, Cora, and Jameston were all actual good friends, but Lanra seemed like the odd woman out. And not in the sense that she was the team leader and the others didn’t respect her decisions, but more so that they simply didn’t have the same history with her. As he cleared his throat, the team stopped bickering and turned back toward him. He was tempted to ask who was actually in charge, as Mako had introduced himself as the captain of the team earlier while Lanra sounded like she made the decisions, but he decided to go with a safer question. For all he knew the spearman had been playing some sort of prank on Lanra, and Asher was in no hurry to turn her ire on himself.

“How long have you all been working together?” he asked.

“Cora, Jameston, and I have been together for a little over seven years now, so we’ve got some real experience under us,” Mako said. “We used to have a fifth, but she died during our last trip into the abyss, and the Crown still hasn’t found a suitable replacement yet. Our previous wizard retired about a year and a half ago, and Lanra’s been with us ever since. We give her a lot of flak, but she’s admittedly very good at what she does.”

Ah, that explains the strange dynamic, Asher thought, nodding along with the explanation. It must be hard keeping a wizard active on the team, seeing as they’re all so old. I wonder how often they get swapped out.

“The team was designed with five realm wanderers in mind, so you would actually fit in perfectly, if you so chose to join us,” Lanra said, seeming satisfied with Mako’s explanation for once. “I appreciate your patience in waiting for me to finish with the details of our offer. Do you have any other questions for us? I am happy to clarify any points that might have been confusing.”

“For starters, I’ll admit I’m not currently looking to make any promises to anyone at this moment,” Asher began, already seeing the disappointment in Lanra’s eyes and written far more plainly on Mako’s face. “That said, I’m certainly not opposed to potentially allying with you guys and joining you on some of your future trips, if you have any interest. Admittedly, my experience with realms beyond the material and the astral is still extremely limited, so I’d love to try and work on that.”

“Forget future trips, want to join us right now and see how it feels?” Mako asked, his excitement already back in full force. “We were prepared to take you on a test run if you were interested.”

“We’ll only go to the wild realm, just to show you how a professional team operates,” Lanra clarified. “Care to join us?”

Asher thought for a moment, weighing the offer in his mind. While they were a little busy with the Council of Death, he wanted to wait for Samantha to finish her flying experiments before deciding how to move forward. And he had to admit he was more than a little curious to see an actual team of realm wanderers in action.

“Alright,” he said, grinning as Mako pumped his fist and Jameston and Cora exchanged a fist-bump. “Let’s go for a trip!”
