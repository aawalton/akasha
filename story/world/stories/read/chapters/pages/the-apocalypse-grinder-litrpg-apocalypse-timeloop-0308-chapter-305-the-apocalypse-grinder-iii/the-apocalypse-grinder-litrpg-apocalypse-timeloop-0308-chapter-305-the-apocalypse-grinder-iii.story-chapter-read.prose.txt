Ronan managed to defeat the spider monkeys all the way up to stage 4. The mini-boss there hadn’t been as challenging to defeat as Spinodoxis. Ronan reckoned that was purely because he was on land and not in the ocean, meaning that the monsters had less of a terrain advantage. However, they did use the skyscrapers like giant trees to jump around on, which made them hard to hit.

In the end he still killed it and made out like a bandit. Before entering stage 5 he used the two shards he received to attempt to push himself over the edge in all four paths. Unfortunately that wasn’t possible. In the end he had only been able to reach the third threshold for Tempered Body and Prismatic Soul. However, the moment he defeated the first mini-boss in stage 5 Ronan decided to temporarily abandon the battle against the others in order to push the rest of his cultivation paths into the third threshold too, meaning he successfully achieved his goal. Because of the distraction he ended up dying to the second mini-boss, but he didn’t mind that much because defeating the nightmare tutorial hadn’t been his main aim during that iteration.

Ronan wondered if he might fail the requirements for the fourth threshold for some of the paths. Mystic Energy and Prismatic Soul would probably be fine. The latter was a bit obscure but he kept getting bonuses to his soul weight and believed that it would be sufficient to keep carrying him through. As for the other two he would just have to see what they were when he reached that point.

With his cultivation at a sufficient level for the time being, Ronan moved onto the next part of his grinding strategy. He planned to kill two birds with one stone by improving his crafting abilities while also getting his masteries up. Along the way there were a few more skills he hoped to acquire, but that would happen as a natural consequence of completing so many tutorials.

So it was that he began the next step of his plan. This time he entered the harder difficulty tutorial. The large quantity of monsters as well as the Path of the Gladiator would be the perfect whetstone to sharpen himself against.

Beginning a new iteration, please stand by…

Ronan reached level 10 very quickly. After killing one enemy, in fact. With his experience multipliers at the level they were, gaining experience was a breeze. Actually he also wanted to test the effects of freeloader by saving up his free stat points now that he had a chance to not just auto-assign them to luck. He wondered if he could breach the level 400 mark while not in the nightmare tutorial.

Before he moved onto stage 2 he checked out his list of masteries. He had gained quite a few during the recent iterations and he wanted to figure out where he needed to focus his efforts in order to unlock some more advanced masteries by merging his existing ones.

He did wonder why he hadn’t gained a merged thrown weapons mastery yet. Surely there can’t be that many more to discover? Then again there is a whole new multiverse out there. I can’t necessarily limit myself to Earth’s historical weaponry. But I gained blunt weapons from just a few… The legacy had a big hand in that. I hope I won’t need to luck out with grimoires just to condense this list. In any case, worrying about it wouldn’t help. Only killing stuff would. For most of the masteries, anyway. He wasn’t looking forward to improving meditation and breath that much, but they had good bonuses that meant ignoring them would be futile.

As he fought his way through the endless waves of goblins Ronan tried out practically every weapon under the sun. He knew there were plenty from other cultures and nations he likely hadn’t even heard of, but if there was even one instance of a weapon in his memory, he conjured it and killed a few goblins while figuring out how it worked. The benefit of increased intelligence and acuity meant he had no problem recalling anything from his past, even things like his early childhood memories. The first time he’d remembered crying for milk as a baby was odd, but he’d learnt how to just push those memories into a corner until they were needed.

It was only four iterations later that Ronan finally received the notification he had been waiting for. That also helped him understand why it was that he hadn’t gained any merged masteries until that point. It wasn’t just a matter of obtaining the prerequisite masteries and having the system do the heavy lifting, but he needed to gain an actual understanding of the masteries themselves. That should have been obvious, given… Mastery. It was in the name. Understanding was more than just conjuring a weapon and using it, but learning how it was best used to take down an opponent.

It was only once Ronan gained an appreciation for how to use various ranged weapons, how to judge distance and travel time, understand obstacles like the wind and the parabolas of his throws, that the system finally saw fit to grant him what he had been working towards. His second merged mastery.

[Throwing Knife II], [Javelin II], [Dart], [Shuriken], [Boomerang], and [Bola] Mastery have been assimilated into [Thrown Weapons I] Mastery!

Gain a minor accuracy and precision bonus when using a thrown weapon that you are familiar with. Gain a minor damage bonus when landing a successful hit with a thrown weapon you are familiar with. Thrown weapons travel slightly faster when you throw them. Gain a 5% chance to apply a cascading [Bleed] effect when landing a successful strike with a thrown weapon.

The bonuses were decent. While some of them were made obsolete by Mark of the Eternal Seeker, there would be battles where Ronan had more than five opponents where they would still come in handy. More than that, he now had the general idea for how to gain merged masteries and could work towards earning more of them. Next on the list was a return to pugilism and the various other forms of bodily combat.
