After making their way back and leaving the cave, the cold, relentless wind greeted them once more. The warmer interior had made them almost forget about the outside temperature! As they walked through the trees that hid the cavern, Lilithra sneezed and rubbed her nose.

“Augh~ we gotta finish this quest quickly!” She complained as she rubbed her arms, trying to warm them up. Lyra giggled at her side, yet she too sneezed afterward, her ears drooping cutely in response.

Ascalon and Selorien chuckled at the interaction, as the small cub in the knight’s arms barked playfully. Ascalon’s heart melted again as he rubbed the canine’s head softly, a small whimper escaping its lips.

Eventually, they left the hidden woods and stood in a clearing, hail descending gracefully around them. It was then they noticed that their quest was going to be quite more difficult than expected, since no matter where they gazed at, it all looked the same.

“Hmm… we should separate—to search in different directions.” Lilithra suggested as she stepped forward, the snow crunching beneath her feet. While Ascalon thought that was a good idea, he was a bit worried about splitting the group in such a hostile environment—with such poor visibility, and a white landscape that seemed to expand endlessly, he had reason to.

“What do you guys think?” She asked, glancing over her shoulder, a timid smile on her lips. Selorien shrugged indifferently, speaking up.

“I guess.” He replied straightforwardly, his eyes half-closed in an unbothered expression. Lyra seemed to think for a moment, before raising her clenched fists and answering energetically.

“Let’s do it!” The cat girl chirped with clear resolve in her voice. Perhaps she was feeling the need to be ‘useful’ to her friends, as usual. Still, one had to respect her conviction! As the knight observed the scene, Lilithra’s eyes were focused on him, a longing gaze brimming with expectation urging him to speak.

“Before that, we should make sure we can meet again easily.” Ascalon commented, patting Moonstone softly. The creature was falling asleep in his arms, undeterred by the colder climate around them. Lilithra pondered for a moment, her lips pursing into a pout as her eyes darted across the landscape.

“Well… we should meet… there!” She responded, pointing toward the distant peaks that rose into the sky—more specifically, toward an almost unnatural, rocky column that would serve well as a landmark. Ascalon nodded in acknowledgment, thinking it was large enough to be visible even amidst the hailing winds.

“Very well.” The knight stated with a gentle grin, with Lilithra beaming at his agreement.

“Oh! And let’s meet in half an hour, just in case—doesn’t matter if you don’t find anything, okay?” Lilithra exclaimed, looking more serious. It was a nice idea to have a time limit, since it would be helpful to know if someone had gotten lost if they didn’t arrive on time.

“Alright!” Lyra nodded enthusiastically while Selorien yawned, waving his arm lazily as he walked away. At the sight, the Felinae parted in a different direction, leaving Ascalon and Lilithra alone.

“See you in a bit!” Lilithra chirped, a shy smile on her face as she skipped through the snow. Ascalon waved at her figure even though she wouldn’t see him, and then sighed as he turned around.

“Let us find some herbs, Moonstone.” Ascalon spoke to the puppy, who slept peacefully in his arms. With a soft smile on his lips, the knight set out, resolved to find the flowers and finish the quest swiftly!

Unfortunately, things didn’t work in his favor. He walked and walked through the snowy peaks, meeting wolves on the way, almost falling off a cliff, and even having to plow through a massive mound of snow that blocked his advance—but in the end, he found no herbs in his path.

With a resigned sigh, he looked up at the cloudy sky. He had no way of knowing how much time had passed—since the sun was blocked out—so he could only guess based on his own instinct.

“Hmmm. Perhaps it is time to go back, Moonstone…” Ascalon spoke with a hint of regret in his voice. Moonstone had woken up mid-way, behaving like a good boy in the knight’s arms.

“Woof!” The canine replied, as if agreeing with Ascalon’s suggestion. A chuckle escaped his lips as he returned the way he came, retracing his steps.

It was a good thing that they had decided on a meeting point, the landmark standing unwavering in the distance, since his footprints in the snow had already faded under the falling hail.

After a couple of minutes of sluggishly wading through the snow, he was greeted by a waving Lilithra, who stood next to the rocky pillar as she took refuge from the snow.

“Hey! Welcome back!” She exclaimed with clear happiness, causing Ascalon to grin in relief.

“I am back. How did the search go? I must regretfully say I came back empty-handed.” Ascalon spoke as he bowed slightly, apologizing to Lilithra in his mind. The girl patted his shoulder a few times, beaming at him.

“No worries! I found a few, look!” She declared triumphantly, the herbs they needed appearing in her hands briefly. “I’m keeping them in my inventory so they don’t wilt too fast!” She added, giggling to herself.

“Well done, Lilithra!” Ascalon said with evident pride in his voice, placing a hand on her head and caressing her through her hood. She let out a soft chuckle as she stood in place, relishing on his affection, her cheeks reddening slightly. Just then, Ascalon noticed a few familiar presences approaching.

“I made it!” Lyra exclaimed in resounding relief as she skipped toward them, her clothes and other features bouncing with each step. “I’m glad I didn’t get lost!” She added with teary eyes, a small smile forming on her lips.

“Welcome back! Did you find any?!” Lilithra responded with excitement. Lyra nodded a few times as she huddled with her against the rocky column, producing from her inventory a single herb.

“I—I only found one, s—sorry.” She apologized timidly, but Lilithra reassured her with a quick head pat.

“Nice! We have four so far!” Lilithra grinned with glee, perhaps enjoying the fact she had collected the most herbs so far—a bit of competitiveness wouldn’t hurt, either! As Ascalon thought that, the remaining presence reached them.

“Hey guys.” Selorien spoke with a tired voice, yawning as he made his way toward the wall that protected the girls from the wind.

“And welcome back to you too! Did you find any herbs? Hehe!” Lilithra puffed out her chest, feeling proud of herself. Selorien chuckled as he extended his hands, a multitude of flowers and herbs appearing on his palms.

“Wha—!?” Lilithra blurted out, while Ascalon and Lyra let out impressive sounds. By the looks of it, Selorien had found a minimum of ten!

“You cheater! You and your elven eyes!” Lilithra reproached as she puffed her cheeks, clearly annoyed by Selorien’s resounding win—although they weren’t competing to begin with!

“Heh.” Selorien replied with a short, smug laugh, adding to Lilithra’s irritation. Ascalon laughed heartily at the sight, since it had been a while since he had seen them interact like that.

After a brief, playful moment, everyone recomposed themselves.

“Alright… should we fight the boss?” Lilithra asked with a smirk plastered on her face, confidence more than clear in her visage. Lyra nodded hesitantly, perhaps remembering the fight where Ian had betrayed her Guild. At the sight, Lilithra hugged her tight, while Ascalon spoke.

“Do not worry—we shall be at your side.” Ascalon reassured her with a smile, the small cub peeking out toward Lyra.

“Woof.” Moonstone added, causing Lyra’s eyes to glimmer with emotion. Moonstone was darn adorable! Although who knew what it meant with that woof. Selorien, on the other hand, simply gave a thumbs up, agreeing with the proposal.

“Alright, then! Let’s do iiiiiit!” Lilithra raised both her arms in a pumped-up gesture, while Selorien chuckled at the sight. With their quest done, it was now time to take a detour and reap some rewards from defeating the boss! And so, they set out toward the distant, towering peaks.

The final stretch to the boss of the dungeon was quite the hassle, with hazardous paths, cold, unforgiving gales, and a variety of monsters ranging from high-level harpies to mountain trolls!

The latter had proven formidable foes, especially when they had tried to throw Ascalon down the chasm—though the knight responded in kind, sending one of the monsters hurtling down into the abyss.

After a strenuous fight, and a few level ups, the group took a moment to rest at a new clearing—though something kept Ascalon preoccupied.

“Are we far from the beast, Lyra?” The knight asked, a hint of suspicion in his voice, his gaze focused in the distance.

“Uhm, yeah… it’s just up ahead.” Lyra replied as she drank from a water bottle, pointing toward the path leading up the mountain.

“Is it only one foe?” Ascalon questioned, raising an eyebrow. Lyra nodded before tilting her head in curiosity, while the rest observed from the sidelines.

“Strange…” Ascalon muttered, sensing not only the monster’s presence, but something else—another aura. Somehow, it felt familiar… yet he couldn’t quite put his finger on why.

“Woof!” Moonstone suddenly barked in his arms, as if to snap him out of his thoughts. Ascalon let out a deep sigh as he caressed the small cub, his mind still wandering over to the unknown presence.

The thing was, it didn’t feel like just another beast—it was a more humanoid, distinctive presence, which made for a confusing situation. Had another Guild entered the dungeon after them? Or a lone adventurer seeking treasure? The fact the boss and the unknown presence weren’t fighting only added to Ascalon’s curiosity.

“Is everything okay?” Lilithra asked at his side, peeking at his face with a worried, frowning look. Ascalon sighed once more, this time in regret. He didn’t want to worry his friends, though he did want them to be vigilant, just in case.

“There is another presence alongside the boss of the dungeon… we should be on our guard.” Ascalon revealed, his brows furrowing in concern. Selorien narrowed his eyes and nodded, glancing toward the peaks, while Lyra and Lilithra seemed slightly surprised, but soon also nodded in clear resolution.

“Alright… let’s be careful, guys!” Lilithra exclaimed as she got up, the massive chimera-forged halberd appearing in her hands. She was quite ready, it seemed! At the sight, Selorien and Lyra rose up from their rest, their respective weapons being summoned from their inventories.

“Perhaps I worry too much.” Ascalon whispered as he saw his friends, their actions easing up his worries. Exchanging glances, everyone finally set out for the tallest peak, with unwavering, decisive steps.

But as they advanced, Ascalon’s unease deepened—the presence ahead felt larger, more oppressive, the more they shortened the distance. A sinister, foreboding feeling assaulted him.

It didn’t take long until they reached the summit: An open, rocky plateau, with pointy stones surrounding its edges and resembling a giant, natural crown. At the center stood a hooded figure, radiating the aura that had worried Ascalon along the way.

“Finally!” A womanly voice echoed across the landscape, her tone sharp and mocking. The group immediately tensed up, focusing on the stranger.

“You took your damn time, you assholes.” She continued, a smirk shining under the shadow of her hood.

“And you are…?” Selorien replied with a raised eyebrow, his irritation more than clear. After all, she was being pretty rude for apparently no reason.

But Ascalon remembered he had seen her once before—he was sure she was the one who had bumped into him a few days ago, in the middle of the street. Although fleeting, that deliberate action had been enough for him to recognize her. It was clear then, that this meeting was no coincidence.

But the strangest thing was the boss, as Lyra soon noted. “Ah!” She exclaimed as she pointed past the hooded woman. There, a humongous gryphon could be seen, pinned against one of the jagged edges of the plateau, struggling to break free from… something. There were no visible clues as to what kept it in place, but it was clear it couldn’t move an inch.

“Oh, that? I had to get rid of any distractions! Defeating it would close the dungeon, after all… and we can’t have that.” The hooded figure spoke, her voice brimming with utmost confidence.

“Answer the question! Who are you, and what do you want?!” Lilithra exclaimed with firmness, frowning in clear wariness, the pointy end of her halberd directed at the stranger. Perhaps the unusual sight had prompted her to become more alerted.

The woman laughed, at first a small giggle, soon turning into full-blown cackling.

“Ah, such weaklings demanding answers~ I should be offended, but it’s kinda funny.” The stranger let out a sigh, her head drooping slightly. “Actually, I am a bit angry.” She revealed in an irritated voice.

At that moment, the air around her shifted violently, her hooded cloak dancing slightly in the wind. In the next second, everyone could feel a crushing, powerful pressure push down on them from above, the whole plateau trembling under the weight of an unknown strength.

Lilithra, Lyra, and Selorien fell to their knees, unable to withstand the sudden force, their eyes opening wide as they struggled. Ascalon remained standing, yet his boots sank deep into the rocky ground, his alertness skyrocketing as the air crackled with tension.

“Oh? Wrath wasn’t exaggerating, huh? Good!” She growled as she raised her fists into a fighting posture, her intense voice reverberating across the plateau.

“Wrath!?” Ascalon hissed through clenched teeth, his anger rising up at the mention of that name.

His mind raced as he connected the dots. The oppressive presence, the helpless gryphon, the sheer display of wicked power, and the connection to the vile man known as Wrath… all of that could only mean one thing—before them stood another embodiment of a sin.
