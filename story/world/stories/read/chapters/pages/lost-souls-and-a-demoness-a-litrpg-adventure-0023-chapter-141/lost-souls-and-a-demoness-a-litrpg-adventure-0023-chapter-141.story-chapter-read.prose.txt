The glowing spear flew from Seraphine's hands like a bolt of lightning, aimed directly at Jade's chest. The succubus twisted to the side, and even her incredible speed was barely enough to evade the clearly telegraphed attack. Her armor's shield sparked and crackled as the projectile scraped past, burying itself in the wall behind her and throwing out a shower of masonry. The celestial was already conjuring a replacement, floating higher into the air and further away as she did.

"Go!" Jade shouted, launching herself up in pursuit.

The team leaped into motion. Tara fell back to guard the entry doors against the regular monsters spawning in the gardens. At the same time, Aki and Eita took off at a sprint to a pair of doors at the far end of the massive chamber, preparing to do the same. This had been planned ahead of time. Fighting a powerful, high-level boss was a sketchy proposition at best. If the cathedral was flooded with powerful minions on top of that, Jade doubted they'd even get a chance to retreat before being cut down. But if Tara and the Japanese siblings could hold them back, that gave the rest of the raid group the chance to fully unleash their combined powers on a singular target. Even if Seraphine was far stronger than any individual member of their team, even she wouldn't be able to shrug off their concentrated attacks. This was based on information provided by both Tara and Aylin who, despite not being any more experienced in boss fights, were well studied on the topic. Jade just hoped they were right.

Knowing that they were on the clock, Jade wasted no time summoning her aura blades. Condensing her personal shroud of mana into focused blades of stable magical energy required a level of concentration and was markedly inefficient. Maintaining them for longer than a short period of time would dry up Jade's mana pool, even with all the points she was investing in intelligence. Because of this, Jade frequently employed the technique to enhance a quick flurry of strikes, or for a single finishing blow. There were drawbacks, but when it came to dealing a lot of damage in a small amount of time, Jade didn't have anything that better fit the bill.

Despite pouring on the speed, Seraphine finished conjuring a new spear before Jade reached her. She found herself flying right into a spear thrust, only managing to avoid it with a last-second deflection that was made possible only by her high agility score. Even with her heightened speed and reflexes, it was a near thing. Incandescent energy clashed with condensed mana, and the deflected attack still knocked Jade far enough to the side that she only managed a glancing blow with her second blade. The follow-up strike was better, scoring a line in the angel's armor, but that was all Jade got in before being forced away by a warding swipe.

We have to get her on the ground!

Other than Jade, the only member of the raid group who could remain airborne for any appreciable length of time was Fenrin, using his wind magic. Unfortunately, that did nothing to fix the problem. Primarily an archer, Fenrin could fight Seraphine from the ground already. The real issue was with Parker, Luis, and Urgraan. All three were melee damage dealers, and none of them could participate effectively from the floor.

"Luis, Fenrin, wind shear!"

Naomi called the move, recognizing the problem just as quickly. Jade pressed the attack, taking a hit that sent cracks through her armor's C-rank shield in exchange for forcing the celestial lower, keeping her from ascending beyond the range they could employ the plan. Seraphine was a boss, not a foe that Jade could keep fully occupied for more than a scant moment, but it bought the team the time they needed. A blast of rushing air accompanied Fenrin's arrival, the half-elf arcing up and over the boss even as he loosed a pair of arrows that curved to track Seraphine's attempt to dodge. The unenhanced attacks did little damage, but they occupied her long enough for Fenrin to blast her with a concentrated gust of roaring wind, knocking her further towards the ground.

Jade broke off her assault, flinging herself away from the column of rushing air before it smashed her down as well. She landed with an acrobatic roll, letting her momentum carry her to one of the many columns that ran the length of the hall. Springing out of the roll, Jade kicked off the pillar and launched herself back towards the fight just in time to see Luis land his Frost Hook ability on the angel. Now at C-rank, the tether of icy energy both had greater range and lasted significantly longer. In other words, it was excellent at grounding fliers. Heaving with all his considerable strength, Luis hauled Serpahine back to the cathedral floor.

Jade arrived, meeting Seraphine with another dagger strike, this time from unenhanced blades. Neither penetrated the angel's armor, but a piercing stab from one of Parker's dual swords did. The angel showed neither pain nor emotion as she took the hit, fighting back with a mechanical efficiency that reminded Jade of the robotic constructs she'd encountered early on in the Labyrinth. While tethered to Luis, Seraphine had no choice but to fight at ground level. The team immediately took advantage of this, with Urgraan, Luis, and Parker punching, slashing, and stabbing at her with all their respective might.

That didn't leave a lot of room around the angel, and Jade fell back. Adopting a more supportive role, she dropped rune traps, cast the single-target variant of her blindness spell, and joined Fenrin and Aylin in harrying the boss with ranged attacks. Every strike that Jade landed on spots highlighted by her Vital Strike talent afflicted her with a moderate bleed effect that reduced her movement speed. Jade focused on precision over volume, generating as many instances of the debuff as she could either by swooping in from above to land a quick hit or, more often, with carefully aimed throws.

Reducing Seraphine's maneuverability was critical. She was faster than anyone in the group beside Jade, who she nearly matched. The angel launched attacks with blinding speed, often overwhelming Luis and Aylin's abilities to project shields onto allies. She wielded her conjured spears with formidable prowess, alternating between randomly striking at everyone nearby and focusing heavily on one individual, overcoming their defenses and wounding them in short bursts before turning her attention back to the others. Sometimes she would throw her spears, which surprised them by exploding shortly after impact. They adapted, spreading out to reduce the splash damage while continuing to attack with everything they had.

A warmth filled Jade, accompanied by the scent of flowers as a spell took effect on her. Her wounds closed and burns faded, the pain diminishing as Siora's healing spell worked its magic. Jade was grateful for the elf's presence, as her healing let Naomi spend more of her time boosting the party's agility with her lively music. The bonus wasn't huge on an individual level, but when applied to their entire team at once, the difference compounded. Every strike dodged was one more hit their armor's shields could take before failing, and each additional attack that found its mark was one step closer to victory.

The battle was frantic and full of close calls. Even Jade's newly enhanced C-rank armor could only absorb two hits before shattering, and that was when they didn't take them full on. They fought on a knife's edge, balancing precariously between successfully defending against Seraphine's relentless attacks and managing to deal significant damage to her between them. For Jade and her companions, only their recent experiences with the desperate life and death battles against the demons let them keep their nerves in a fight where a single mistake could lead to a debilitating wound that took them out of the fight. Their resolve, along with Parker's hardened determination, helped prop up the less experienced members of the raid party.

Just when they managed to fall into a rhythm of grounding Seraphine, defending against her spears, and landing hits on her back and sides, the angel of battle changed tactics. She stopped attacking Luis and Fenrin, allowing Jade and Parker to land solid hits on her back while she held her spear aloft and chanted a spell. Her weapon flared with light as a crackling pillar of radiant energy smashed down on their back line, scattering Naomi, Aylin, and Siora. Jade lunged, intending to take full advantage of the boss's focus to land a critical blow to the base of her neck. She thrust, but the angel was no longer there. A burst of feathers was all that remained as Seraphine flapped her wings once, launching herself towards the distant support casters. She timed it perfectly, executing the maneuver right as Luis's tether faded. Now free of the melee the boss pressed the attack, raining a flurry of piercing thrusts on Naomi before any of them could reach her.

The angel appeared in front of Naomi in a flash of light, and she felt the impact of multiple rapid strikes before her mind fully registered what was happening. Scrambling to evade the boss's spell had separated her from the others, leaving her isolated and without protection. The next thing the bard knew she was sprawled on her back, her armor's defensive field shattered. Seraphine loomed over her, silhouetted against the Core light, her angelic figure tall and menacing. She thrust with her radiant spear and Naomi rolled to the side, scrambling towards where she'd dropped her guitar. The strike caught her in the side, piercing armor and flesh and sending searing pain flashing through her.

In the past, such a wound would have been terrifying to Naomi. While the fear was still there, the foundation of training and the building blocks of experience formed a bulwark against being overcome by it. She may be a back line healer, but she could hold her own when she had to. Naomi growled, ripping the rapier free from its sheath and activating Spike Thrust. The powerful weapon talent practically launched the bard back to her feet, slamming into Seraphine's silver breast plate with enough force to crack it. Unfortunately, the motion ripped the radiant spear from her side. Naomi's vision swam as more pain clawed through her focus and she cried out, staggering back. She tried to push through it, recovering her stance even as the angel lunged for her again.

CRACK.

The strike never landed. Luis's shield formed around Naomi an instant before the spear found her chest, the translucent bubble expanding to deflect it. The force of the impact still sent her stumbling further away, but putting more distance between her and the murderous angel wasn't anything she'd complain about. But Seraphine wasn't done. The angel swept her spear in a circle, launching a wide arc of golden energy that rapidly expanded, striking the other members of the raid party as they rushed to regroup. The attack hit home, knocking them to the ground and pushing them back, creating another opening for her to resume her assault.

Except, not everyone had fallen. Luis barreled through the ring of energy like a juggernaut, catching most of the attack on his enchanted shield and simply ignoring the rest of it. He dashed forward, interposing himself between Naomi and the boss right as she launched another barrage of attacks. The large man caught several on his shield, an aura of blue light flaring around him as his mana barrier deflected much of the damage.

"Oh, no." he growled, knocking away her spear and retaliating with a quick slash of his own. "You don't get to go after my friends, chica. I'm not done with you."

Naomi seized the momentary reprieve, grabbing her guitar and plucking a quick, staccato rhythm. Bolts of fire flared from the instrument, striking Seraphine in the flank as she grappled with Luis. His blade found her again and fire flared from the impact sites, triggering her spell again for additional damage.

"I take no joy in violence." Seraphine said, so quietly that Naomi could barely hear her. Her face remained blank, as expressionless as a statue. "But you must be challenged."

She leapt back, momentarily breaking away from Luis. The angel's wings flared and she lifted a few feet from the ground, the shining spear in her grasp glowing brighter and brighter as more magical power accumulated in it. The rest of the team was almost on them, bolstered by Naomi's renewed Fleetfoot melody. They fired ranged attacks and spells, trying to distract the boss before she unleashed whatever attack she was powering up. She evaded some, while others impacted her armor. The damage didn't deter Seraphine. She raised her gaze past Luis, locking her eyes on Naomi.

Great. Of course I'm the target.

But she still had a trick of her own. Several things happened at once. The angel's spear flashed, glowing like the sun as the boss unleashed a lunging attack on Naomi. Luis leapt between them again, his aura surging in strength as he activated a powerful talent. And, finally, Naomi's fingers danced across the strings of her instrument, playing a heroic crescendo of notes as she activated her newest talent.

Talent: Star Power

Rating: C level talent

Cost: Moderate mana

Duration: 1 minute

Cooldown: 1 hour

Effect: Grants a short lived but large bonus to damage dealt, healing given, and spell potency. Cleanses all debuffs from the caster, transforming them into self-healing effects. Requires an instrument.

Seraphine's supercharged spear crashed against Luis's shield with enough force to crack the floor beneath his feet. He didn't move an inch, the telltale glow of his 'Immovable Object' talent granting him the fortitude to resist the mighty blow, at least momentarily. As sturdy as Luis was, he couldn't hold out for long against attacks from such a high-level boss monster.

But he wasn't alone. Naomi rushed forward, buoyed by a sudden influx of energy and healing as the lingering burns left by Seraphine's spear thrusts were transformed into life force. Aura bursting with supercharged confidence, Naomi darted around Luis and fired off a point-blank, enhanced Sonic Boom.

The blast of thunderous energy had so much recoil that Naomi was knocked off her feet, sliding backwards on her rear before a column arrested her momentum with a painful jolt. That was nothing compared to the thunderous force that impacted Seraphine. It lifted her from the ground, flinging the angel a solid thirty feet and sending her crashing to the hard stone floor right in the middle of their approaching team. They fell on the boss before she could recover, Jade and Urgraan landing several clean hits as the others rushed to both support them and recover their formation. Naomi pulled herself back to her feet, her insides still bursting with heightened power. It cried for an outlet, urging her to act. Fight. Win! Siora was already tending to Luis's fresh wounds, so Naomi felt comfortable focusing on offence. She resumed the Fleetfoot melody, the melody faster and louder than before, boosting the agility of everyone in the raid group to newer heights.

Nearby, Luis gave her a thumbs up and a grin, his cheerfulness only somewhat marred by the burns Siora was pouring a potion on.

"Good recovery.""Thanks to you." Naomi grinned back, a rare ferocity glinting in her eyes. This wasn't a battle they could afford to lose, so she wasn't going to let them.

Jade scored a final hit against Seraphine's side, one of her daggers finding a gap in her thick armor. And then the boss was back on her feet, another ring of burning energy blasting out in every direction and forcing back her surrounding attackers. This time, Jade wasn't caught by surprise. She launched herself into the air, leaping over the attack and swooping back in to continue their renewed offensive. Luis and Naomi had turned a potential disaster into an opportunity, and Jade didn't want to give up the initiative. Her charge was brought to an abrupt halt, however, by a flash of silvery steel.

Jade barely caught the blade on her daggers, the impact knocking her back to the ground. She stayed on her feet, her boots skidding several yards on the smooth marble before coming to a stop near the middle of the room. No follow up came. Seraphine remained standing alone in the circle of space she'd opened for herself. The weapon the boss held was not another conjured spear, but a long, glistening sword. Everyone paused, staring at the pure, star-silver blade. Seraphine unhooked the now-empty scabbard at her hip, dropping it to the floor where it landed with an audible crash, cracking the tiles beneath it. The angel's armor was rent in places, golden light shining from wounds in her flesh. They had scored more than a few solid hits on the boss, but one look was enough to tell Jade that they still had a ways to go.

"You fight adequately." Seraphine spoke, still in that strange monotone. The light beauty of her voice was entirely mismatched with the manner in which she spoke. The same was true of her otherworldly beauty and her empty, emotionless eyes. "But I have no wish to be cut down yet again."

She raised her sword, golden runes lighting up along the length of the blade, and renewed her attack.
