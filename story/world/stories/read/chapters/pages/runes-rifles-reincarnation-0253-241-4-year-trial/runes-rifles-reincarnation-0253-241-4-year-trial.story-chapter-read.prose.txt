“Dad, Momma Li Xue is calling for you!”

A teenage girl with tiger-like features — a long, swishing silver-and-black tail, and silver-and-black tiger ears — wearing a golden tiara and a pure white robe called out.

Slowly opening his eyes, Jin Shu glanced down from the mountain peak where he had been meditating. As his trance ended, his floating body gradually lowered. The moment his feet touched the summit, his qi surged uncontrollably — and the peak beneath him shattered under the weight of his overwhelming aura.

The teenager spread her wide wings and, with a powerful beat, swooped over Jin Shu, a teasing smile on her lips.

“Still haven’t solidified your realm? Why is it taking you so long?”

Jin Shu rolled his eyes. “Sorry, Your Highness, but not everyone can be like you — the great Divine White Tiger and only the second qiongqi in existence, Yin’er. Especially not after gaining an ancient technique that perfectly matches your body.”

“But didn’t you create your own technique?”

“I did. Unfortunately, I have to keep altering it after every breakthrough just to make it fit my body.”

He adjusted his still-surging qi and shook his head.

“The Adept Realm is proving slightly more difficult than I imagined. That’s why it’s taking so long.”

She nodded.

“Anyway, why is Li Xue calling for me? Did they find a way to complete the trial?”

“No.” Yin’er shook her head. “But I think they found a clue.”

“Oh? A clue?” Jin Shu nodded in approval. “It’s about time. This has been the longest trial yet. We’ve spent four whole years wandering from place to place without finding anything. I was beginning to think it might be impossible.”

His qi finally settled into its usual rhythm.

“Alright. Let’s go.”

“Okay!”

Yin’er’s body shrank as she shifted from human to feline. Within moments she was no bigger than a kitten, hopping neatly onto her favorite spot — her father’s shoulder.

Jin Shu scratched beneath her chin, drawing out a soft purr as she rubbed her head against his cheek.

He still remembered the day, more than ten years ago, when he had feared his daughter would grow into a legendary beast of slaughter.

What a foolish thought.

It didn’t matter what she became — or what she was.

To Jin Shu, Yin’er would forever be his cute daughter.

Coming to the foot of the mountain, Jin Shu entered the small settlement where the others were staying as they searched for clues.

More than ten years had passed since they first entered the Hidden Phoenix Realm. What they had expected to be a small pocket dimension had instead revealed itself to be an entire world — a perfect replica of their own, only set in the distant past.

From time to time they would encounter trials left behind by the realm’s master — Lord Qilin.

Their current trial had already dragged on for nearly four years, with no clear objective or condition in sight.

That was, until now.

It seemed something had finally changed after Jin Shu entered secluded cultivation to consolidate his realm. A clue had finally appeared.

He found Li Xue waiting near the entrance of the temporary settlement. She wore a vibrant pink robe that looked ready to burst at the seams around her chest.

Jin Shu sighed, vanishing just as she opened her mouth.

“Jin—!”

When he reappeared, he was already standing behind her.

With practiced precision, he tapped several acupoints along her back, dispersing the gathered qi throughout her body. A rush of wind qi escaped from within her robes, which visibly deflated.

“Ah… my hard work,” she pouted. “I finally perfected my wind-mimic technique. And now it’s gone just like that!”

“Haa… must you keep fooling around like this?” Jin Shu said. “You’re already thirty. Besides, you look beautiful just the way you are.”

Li Xue stared at him with lovestruck eyes.

Jin Shu smiled faintly as he looked at her. It had only been a month since he entered seclusion, and she hadn’t changed in that short time — yet it still felt like far longer since he had last seen her. Or anyone else, for that matter.

During their years within the hidden realm, Li Xue’s once youthful beauty had matured into that of a refined woman — at least outwardly. Inwardly, she remained the same playful and impulsive person he had always known.

So long as she wasn’t speaking or acting out, most people would likely mistake her for a cold, stoic beauty. Her sharp features and cool eyes only softened when she was with him.

The two of them had already shared everything a husband and wife should — everything except a child… and an actual wedding.

For all intents and purposes, however, they were already husband and wife.

Slipping an arm around her waist, Jin Shu pulled her into his embrace and pressed a gentle kiss to her lips.

“Bleh!” Yin’er made an exaggerated gagging sound. “Save the public displays of affection for when I’m not around.”

Jin Shu chuckled and released the rosy-faced Li Xue.

“Tell me,” he said. “What clue did you all find?”

She shook her head. “I don’t actually know. Wu Ling was the one who discovered it, but she hasn’t told anyone yet. They’re waiting until everyone is gathered.”

“Then let’s not keep them waiting.”

Together they walked toward the meeting hall at the center of the settlement. Voices drifted from inside — it seemed everyone else had already arrived.

Only Jin Shu and Li Xue were missing.

He didn’t immediately announce his presence. Instead, he lingered at the entrance, quietly observing the changes in everyone during the years they had spent trapped within this realm.

His mothers hadn’t changed much outwardly, aside from becoming slightly more open about their relationship.

They had never truly hidden it within the sect, but neither had they ever openly announced it. There was no grand reason for this — only Chen Ai Yun’s quiet refusal to suffer further embarrassment at Sun Mei’er’s hands.

As long as they maintained a certain level of discretion, Sun Mei’er could only act out so much in public. If their relationship were fully revealed, however, there would be nothing stopping her from saying or doing all kinds of outrageous things.

Within the trial grounds, where only a small group of younger cultivators and spirit beasts were present, they could afford to be more relaxed.

The revelation had still shattered more than a few young hearts.

As for the others, time had left far more visible marks.

Biyu, for instance, had finally begun shedding her insecurities — much like the green robes she once wore without fail.

She had clung to that color because of childhood trauma. Her father had despised green, seeing in it a constant reminder of her mother’s death. For years she had worn it almost defiantly.

But with Jin Shu’s help — along with Li Xue, Tian Li, and the others — she had slowly begun to move past that pain.

Her temperament had also become steadier. Unlike Li Xue, she had truly matured both outwardly and inwardly.

Tian Li had changed as well. The traumas that once weighed her down had gradually been washed away by the passage of time and the ordeals they had faced together.

People often said time was cruelest to women.

For Tian Li, however, it had only deepened her beauty.

Such was one of the many advantages of cultivation — a lifespan far longer than that of mortals.

The Ling Sisters showed far fewer changes, though that was hardly surprising. For spirit beasts — especially those of Divine Beast lineage — ten years was little more than the blink of an eye.

Zhu Ren, now widely known as the reincarnation of the Dagger Goddess, had fully grown into that reputation.

Despite her small stature, which often caused strangers to mistake her for a child, she was like an unsheathed blade. Even the air around her seemed to carry a cutting edge. Others instinctively gave her a wide berth — though none dared exclude her.

The one who had changed the least was likely Bing Hou. Her mastery over fire and ice had deepened considerably, yet outwardly she still carried herself with the same glacial composure.

Surprisingly, it was Ling Shi who had undergone the greatest transformation over the past decade. During the early years she had been an unrelenting nuisance. But after enduring countless trials alongside the others, she had finally become a true part of the group.

Even now she was laughing freely, trading playful blows with the rest.

As for Liu Hua, she remained almost entirely unchanged. Aside from a growing impatience to return to her younger sister, she was the same person she had been ten years ago.
