A few weeks later – or about four months after Percy first awakened his third core – he felt ready to attempt the upgrade of his mindset. It had taken a bit longer than Nephthys’ projections, because he hadn’t been able to allocate all twelve minds on the task. He hadn’t wanted to eat up all of Micky’s time, and progressing with his boosting art or affinity fusion had been no less important than working on the mindset.

‘Remember to keep the underlying structure of the previous version intact,’ the Ruby Princess advised. ‘There’s no reason to recreate the whole thing from scratch, since you’re not skilled enough to overhaul it entirely. Just focus on cutting out the redundancies, scaling its consumption up to the rest of your mental resources, and adding a few gates so that you can deactivate it or feed it your excess mana at will.’

Percy paid close attention to his familiar’s instructions, fully aware that she wouldn’t be able to join him inside his mindscape. Not directly. Parallel Minds would allow him to keep a sliver of attention in the real world to inject his brain with mind mana, another to tinker with the symbolic representation of his mindset within the starry sky, and a third to stay in contact with the reincarnated goddess. It would help, but it wouldn’t be the same as having her watch the whole procedure directly.

Eager to begin, he pulled the magical plaque out of his spatial seal and held it between his hands. Activating it, he observed the changes inside his mindscape as the artifact tried to grant him Enhanced Cognition again. It obviously failed, since Percy already possessed the mindset, but the attempt helped him recognize and isolate the mental structures associated with the ability.

Nephthys had explained the importance of choosing a symbolic representation whenever he created a new mindset. It didn’t have to be a physical object – any visible change would do – but it was important to pick something that matched his understanding and which would allow him to interact with the mindset more easily.

Percy had done that when he had acquired Shimmering Constellations and Parallel Minds under Phoebe’s and Nephthys’ supervision respectively, but he hadn’t known to do that when he had obtained Ludwick’s Compass or Enhanced Cognition.

That was something he had to change in order to upgrade the latter.

Tracing the outline of the mass-produced mindset, Percy allowed it to manifest as a thirteenth constellation inside his mindscape – albeit one that was very different from the other twelve. This one wasn’t shaped like a living creature, but rather an enormous spherical shell that surrounded the other constellations – both the two massive ones that represented his and Micky’s original minds, and the ten smaller shapes that represented the clones.

The spherical shell consisted of countless deep red stars arranged in a sparse hexagonal lattice, connected to their neighbours with thin struts of the same colour. Percy knew that his relatively recent experience of working on the Cradles had subconsciously biased his choice, but he didn’t dislike it.

Most notably, the spherical shell appeared somewhat defective. There were several missing stars and struts, with a few patches so horrifically deformed that their connections were bent and visibly tangled up.

The corners of Percy’s lips curled up as he realized that the constellation had already provided him with several clues on how to improve the mindset.

Before working on the finer details, he added a circular, two-layered gate at each of the six cardinal directions on the surface of the sphere. The inner layer was embedded directly into the network of crimson stars, so that twisting the inner gates counterclockwise would add “material” to the spherical shell, thus loosening the connections and deactivating the mindset.

Percy tested that out immediately, since he didn’t want the mindset to remain active while he worked on it, lest he accidentally injured himself.

As tension left the crimson struts, the spherical shell’s rigid structure collapsed into a semi-amorphous fabric. It also deflated by around fifteen percent of its volume, as did the twelve constellations housed within it, matching the associated reduction in Percy’s thinking speed.

It wasn’t the most pleasant feeling, as he felt like somebody had pumped his head full of tar, but he gritted his teeth and resumed working under the promise of feeling much better once he was done.

The next thing he did was to methodically carve out the deformed patches on the surface of the sphere and replace them or fill in the gaps with new sets of properly-interconnected stars.

Of course, these were just the symbolic changes inside his mindscape. Each modification was accompanied by the addition or adjustment of a tiny bridge linking a pair of neurons together back in the real world.

Repairing the entire shell took another four days – which was the longest Percy had spent working on a single mindset so far. The fact that he was only tweaking an existing mindset made this sound even more ridiculous.

Once he was done, he double-checked his work a couple of times while discussing his experiences with Nephthys, to make sure that everything was in order. Only when he was reasonably certain that he wasn’t about to fry his brain did he dare to twist the inner gates clockwise again.

The action caused the network of crimson struts to tighten, restoring the spherical shell to its original shape. Despite the fact that Percy had technically removed “material” from the connections, the constellation swelled by forty percent of its volume, stretching the struts even thinner and reminding Percy that the laws of physics didn’t apply within his mindscape.

Most importantly, the twelve person- and bird-shaped constellations inside the sphere also expanded by forty percent, not only draining the “tar” that had previously bogged Percy’s thoughts down but seemingly replacing it with a form of mental “grease” that helped his brain work much faster than ever before.

At least, that was how it felt to him.

‘Wow… Nephthys wasn’t kidding!’ he told his fellow aspect. ‘Are you feeling this too?’

‘Hurry up and twist the outer gates as well,’ Micky said, indirectly answering Percy’s question.

Percy nodded, opening the second layer of gates while redirecting his excess mind mana into his brain. He wasn’t using it for anything else, nor was he planning to register any other mind spells in the foreseeable future, so he was able to feed the resource into his mindset as fast as he was regenerating it.

He had yet to master the Dance with his new core, so his current output was about twice as much as a normal Red’s. A bit higher than that, actually, because of his Twin Heartbeat Physique and the synergistic effects of his other cores allowing him to circulate more mana through his pathways.

The spherical shell inside his mindscape lit up and swelled by another one hundred percent of its original volume, bringing the total to around two hundred and forty percent of its base size. His thoughts flowed even faster, causing the drifting motes in his surroundings and the soft flailing of his familiar’s antennae to slow down to a crawl.

[Congratulations! Your mindset has evolved: Enhanced Cognition -> Supercharged Cognition!]

Before he could appreciate his new upgrade, Percy noticed something that he didn’t particularly like, however.

Only one of the giant constellations and nine of the smaller ones had expanded this time. The pale stars representing Micky’s original mind and the mix of pale and grey stars representing the soul clone remained unaffected.

‘I’m guessing that you didn’t feel this one?’ he asked.

Micky confirmed Percy’s fears with a disappointed groan.

‘That’s not very surprising,’ Nephthys – who had clearly been following their conversation – interjected. ‘The base version of the mindset doesn’t require any additional fuel, so you can automatically pass it to your soul clones and aspects at no extra cost. Unfortunately, supercharging it is proportional to the provided mana. If you want to accelerate Micky’s mind as well, you’ll need to send him as much mana as you’re using.’

Percy frowned, knowing that was impossible.

He was already using his entire output on himself. Leaving aside the fact that he couldn’t transfer mind mana through the cords until he figured out affinity fusion, splitting his reserves with Micky or his soul clones would only reduce the benefits on his own mind by the same amount that he granted them.

‘But my mind clones don’t seem to need any additional mana,’ he realized. ‘If my output had been split ten ways, there’s no way I would have experienced such a massive improvement.’

‘Indeed,’ Nephthys acknowledged. ‘It looks like we were right about the synergy between your bloodline and the mindset. Supercharged Cognition causes your brain to work faster, while your clones simply use the same boosted organ more efficiently. It would be best to always focus the entirety of your reserves in your own head and only share the resulting processing power with Micky.’

Percy’s lips couldn’t help but part into a grin. As excited as he’d been upon discovering that his bloodline worked with his new affinity, he’d kept thinking of his mind clones as a weaker variant of his soul clones. After all, each soul clone also technically provided him with an extra mind, in addition to the ability to travel to distant planets and possess the bodies of others.

Evidently, his assessment had been incomplete, because his mind clones also came with some unique advantages of their own.

Opening his Status, Percy’s eyes drifted to the mindset section.

___

Mindsets [3,700/1,200]:

___

‘Nearly four thousand points of mental capacity…!’ Percy gasped.

Performing a few calculations – which was as effortless as blinking at this point – he quickly confirmed that the mindset worked like they had deduced.

It consumed all twelve hundred points of his capacity to accelerate his and Micky’s minds by default. It could also convert his mana – which was about two and a half times as much as a regular Red’s – into two thousand and five hundred points of additional mental capacity, split evenly between ten of his minds.

Sadly, the cognitive boost had to be multiplied by a factor of zero point four due to the relatively low efficiency of the mindset, but that was something that Percy could improve over time if he kept studying under Nephthys.

‘I’ve been thinking… When you said that a mindset specialist can improve the effect of their mindsets by a flat five percent by practicing ten times longer… did that account for the extra processing power afforded to them by their improvements?’

‘Unfortunately, yes,’ the Ruby Princess confirmed. ‘It also assumes a few promotions along the way, though there aren’t many people in the universe who can advance as fast as you, so you might still be able to improve a bit faster than the benchmarks I gave you.’

Percy nodded contentedly. With both of his mindsets working at full power, he estimated that he and Micky had access to brainpower on par with nearly twenty-seven people!

‘And this is with just a Red core and Circulation,’ Percy thought with a smirk, already drooling at the thought of his upcoming promotions.
