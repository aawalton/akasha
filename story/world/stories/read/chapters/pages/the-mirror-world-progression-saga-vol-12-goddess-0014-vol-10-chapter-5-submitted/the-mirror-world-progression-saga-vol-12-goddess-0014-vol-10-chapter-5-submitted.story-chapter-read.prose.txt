The dungeon was not Samantha’s typical mental picture of a jail, with bars and such. It was a very depressing, ‘old-school’ type of place, behind thick stone walls, ostensibly capable of shrouding someone in darkness without letting in the sunlight indefinitely. Naturally, Samantha didn’t allow that — all the rooms had shutterable light sources.

When Samantha walked in, Nyomel and Deikmorn were standing there, looking as if they’d cut off a conversation. From Deikmorn’s expression, it had been an argument.

Immediately when he caught sight of Samantha, Deikmorn began, “Please, Your Majesty, release her into my custody, and I’ll take full responsib-”

Samantha had closed her eyes and held up her hand for him to stop, so he cut himself off. She opened her eyes very slowly, ignoring Deikmorn and studying Nyomel.

Nyomel, clad in a plain gray robe, kept her eyes downcast. But she still felt eyes on her, and immediately threw herself at Samantha’s feet and bowed her head and hands to the floor. “Forgive me, Goddess!” she said in a furious, impassioned whisper. “I acted like a fool, in suspicion of you! I-”

“No,” Samantha said, cutting her off, her voice one of contained fury even to her ears. “You were right in what you did in those moments. But go on. I want to hear what else you’re begging forgiveness for. I trust you’ll figure out why I’m angry with you.”

Meanwhile, in the corner of her eye, she could see Deikmorn practically chewing rocks with the scowl on his face, looking between them. But he wisely kept his mouth shut, sensing the mood.

Or realizing she might save her own behind, here.

Nyomel did not lift her head. “Your Majesty… you are merciful even to let me speak, I know. I hoped you’d come one day, and was steeled to wait. First, I apologize for him. I didn’t ask him to do this. As you know, he is exceptionally smitten with me.” Deikmorn chewed rocks some more and growled in his throat at this, glaring at her, but she ignored it. “Principally, I beg your mercy and forgiveness for what I am: a deceiver. I did not come to you to confess, though our purposes were aligned, and-”

“Cut the crap!” Samantha interjected, feeling more and more annoyed with her. “You think I haven’t figured it out by now!? They really keep your cells completely ignorant, don’t they? You were under the orders of your masters. Or mistresses; whatever. I don’t know the layers of hierarchy, admittedly. What I do know is that before I made contact with the true head of your fucking serpentine organization, you lot were everywhere, hatching your schemes long before I even existed. Powerful enough at it, you could even hide from me, right under my nose. And I know you aren’t sorry, ‘Nyomel.’ You’re a spy; a fanatical agent. You’re totally dedicated. If the order came through, you’d go for my neck right now — that is where your loyalties lie. Isn’t that right?”

Nyomel was silent and still to this. Meanwhile, Deikmorn was staring at her with great intensity. Exactly what he felt about it seemed hidden. In any case, he didn’t seem that disturbed by the implications.

“Pick your face up off the floor, snake,” Samantha commanded. “No more posturing.”

Nyomel rose to her knees slowly but smoothly, her face serene but hollow as her eyes remained downcast. Her hands were folded in her lap. “That order would never come, Your Majesty.”

These words made Deikmorn exhale loudly and deeply out of his nose, his face a deep scowl.

Samantha, for her part, chortled. “Oh?! Gee, what a relief! I guess I have nothing to worry about, on the word of a proven liar committed to another’s allegiance!”

Nyomel blinked and looked stricken by her tone. She swallowed. “I understand. I believe I’ve outlived my usefulness. I will accept my execution without resistance.”

Samantha wore a dark grin as she looked over at Deikmorn. “Wow, dude! This one takes the cake for submissiveness! Got yourself a keeper, eh?”

Deikmorn glanced at her but cast his gaze away quickly. He was keeping a stony face, but she could tell he was struggling on the inside.

Nyomel said nothing.

If this is left as it stands, she’ll kill herself, won’t she? Holy hell, these snakes are real pieces of work.

Samantha shrugged with her hands out. “Let’s not jump the gun, hmm? What I’d really like to know is who you really are.”

Nyomel shook her head. “No one. Whatever face, whatever persona is needed. That is all.”

“Fine. What are you, then? You’re not a Naugite, are you?”

Stricken again, she squeezed her eyes shut and shook her head. It seemed to pain her.

“You’re a shapeshifter,” Samantha said. “A perfect one, enough to put my own to shame.” No response to this. “But you have a true form, or a default form. I feel that, now, in my very bones. Something like that. Hmm? I taste it on the tongue of the Goddess of Magic, as it were. And I have a feeling I know exactly what it’ll be.” She crossed her arms and glared down at the humbled figure. “Show me.”

For the first time, Nyomel’s eyes shot up and met hers. She looked horrified. “Please! Please, I beg you, don’t make me-”

“Show me!” Samantha cried, thrusting a finger at her. “Show me your true form, you fucking snake!”

Nyomel’s mouth clamped shut, even as her eyes flickered in panic. She deflated and her eyes cast down once more. In an anxious, pained voice, she cried, “I will obey, Goddess!”

With the faintest trace of magic, her body shifted physically. She shrank in size. Her skin went golden-yellow and developed scales, and her hair turned into thick tendrils that grew little snake heads.

A female gorgon was knelt before her.

Something about it tickled, however, in her magical senses, now that this being had released the prior form. It still didn’t feel right…

The gorgon’s reptilian eyes, with vibrant orange irises, shot up to hers, full of silent pleading. Her snake heads writhed and hissed in agitation. Samantha didn’t understand at first, but then those fascinating eyes flickered almost imperceptibly, quickly, at Deikmorn.

Hmm.

Looking down, this gorgon was still pretty damn cute. Exceptionally feminine, and well shy of truly monstrous. When Samantha side-eyed Deikmorn a bit, she could see a bit of warring emotions on his face. Shock, yes, but not quite horror or the like. There was a trace of fascination.

Oh hell. Even if she dies, she still wants to be admired by her lover. Damn this bitch! She’s pulling my heartstrings, now.

Those pleading eyes were asking her not to demand the ‘true true’ form, it seemed, which would likely not be so cute. But the truth was… she wasn’t sure she should trust either.

It’s true to her, in some fashion.

Samantha gave an almost imperceptible nod, to which the gorgon lady seemed to sag with relief and appreciation. A reptilian tail flicked around on the floor from behind her robes.

Good enough. Confirms my suspicions.

Deikmorn finally spoke up. “Why didn’t you have a tail? It’s hardly unheard of among Naugites.”

Nyomel met his eyes with a bit more horror to spare, perhaps at the vulnerability she’d exposed. But after a few blinks, it dissipated, and she formed a faint smile on her lips — perhaps comforted by the easy, irrelevant question he’d chosen to break the ice with. “It-… It is still uncommon, and remains a stigma in some companies.” Her voice was a lot different from the sensual Naugite woman. Higher, elegant, and rather musical. “A lot of males dislike it, no? Parting with it increased the reach of my seduction. I worked for decades in the modern Land of Naug, mind you.”

Deikmorn nodded slowly. “Well, it never bothered me. It still doesn’t.” There was clearly more being said with those words.

Nyomel searched his face and smiled a bit wider at him, though it held a trace of sadness, too. “I see.”

Deikmorn’s eyes shifted partially in Samantha’s direction. “Surely you have use for such expertise, Goddess.”

Samantha stared down at the gorgon with a frown, finally sighing grandly and rubbing her forehead. “Only if I can trust her. Aside from that, she’s always been quite helpful in battle. I already have spies. Unless you can get into Matrazere?”

Nyomel looked up at her with a resigned expression. “That would take time. I could try, but I would more likely fail due to the timeframe.”

“Eh. We may or may not try something that’s already likely to fail as it is.” Another sigh. “Look, I need to go over your head. I don’t know what the fuck that’s going to do to you, but I think you know this is too awkward to sweep under the rug. I have to determine what to do with you via your superiors.”

Nyomel, her snake heads going smooth and settling, nodded, still with a resigned look on her face. “A compromised entity is normally slated to either escape or perish, but our purpose created a complexity. Whatever my specific role, I was to help you absolutely, trumping literally all else. And then, second only to that, to obey you, literally and in spirit. I was to get closer at one time, but this was abandoned for reasons not specified, via indirectly communicated changes to my role.”

Samantha nodded slowly, working it out. “Right, so you kept that cursed crown away from me regardless of what I wanted or said. As for you staying away… that’s probably because I saw you turn into a ‘spirit-snake’ or something like that, to cast that spell during the Merrington siege.”

Nyomel blinked. “That does explain a lot.”

“Have you not received any new orders or contacted your superiors?”

“No. That’s a security risk under the circumstances, and wasn’t necessary. I would have fled after ensuring your people got the cursed crown, but… obedience to you — in spirit — would not allow that. It was more beneficial to our purpose to continue fighting, and, after the battle, submit myself for your judgment and punishment.”

Samantha began to feel just a tad guilty as she thought about how this woman had helped save her own bacon twice, and was — according to her — fanatically devoted to Samantha’s wellbeing… which obviously did, in fact, hold up to scrutiny.

But she’s still a dirty, dirty spy, damn it!

Her conviction about her own ‘righteous indignation’ on the matter was waning rapidly, and though she didn’t examine why just yet, she understood the whole thing was bothering her for separate reasons. Rubbing her neck, Samantha cleared her throat. “You’ve done enough for me to give you the benefit of the doubt, which is why I’m going to get everyone on the same page and figure this out. Okay?” A pause. “And, so we’re clear, do not try and off yourself. Capice?”

Nyomel bowed her head and hands to the floor again. All her snake heads bent down, too, going flat. “I will not defy you, Goddess. Your will is mine and I am yours, mind, body, and soul, to deal with however you desire.”
