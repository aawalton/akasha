James found himself suddenly in a bright, white environment.

The white was almost blinding after having moved through a strange, dark passage to get here. He turned his head and looked from one side to another, trying to take in his surroundings even as his eyes adjusted to the sudden, sharp increase in the amount of light nearby.

Fortunately, his senses and Vitality of the Hydra meant that everything that had been dark and hazy in the strange transition environment soon snapped into sharp relief.

And it was a relief to observe that now, he was in a place that felt completely solid, rather than surrounded by a type of Mana he still could not quite place.

The Fisher Emperor found himself in a land of snow and ice.

Freezing, he thought as both the sight and the cold air hit him almost simultaneously. But he did not allow himself to shiver. As with so many things, he could control that bodily impulse fully now. Where the hell am I? Canada? Scandinavia? Siberia?

He felt a strange sense of deja vu, though he had never taken vacations to icy places before in his life.

More importantly, a figure stood across the snow from him, roughly of the same height as James himself. As James’s eyes focused on him, the feeling of deja vu intensified. The other man wore ornate green armor that seemed to cover every inch of his skin.

Is that dragonscale?

“You,” James muttered. “I’ve seen you before.”

“Don’t waste time,” the man said in a deep voice that was obviously acoustically altered by the properties of his helmet.

“Who are you?” James asked angrily.

“We’ve already discussed this. You will not delay my purpose.”

He sprang at James with a speed that the Fisher Emperor could not remember having ever witnessed from another human.

It was all James could do to jerk his body into a fighting stance. Then the other man was upon him. James found himself helpless as blows rained down on his head, shoulders, arms, and torso. Each one struck with the force of a car doing sixty miles an hour.

This guy is so far beyond me it’s not even funny, he thought as his ribs exploded with pain. How? How was someone like this just lurking out in the world?

James mostly blocked as best he could—that was really all he could do to survive, fighting someone who was obviously stronger and faster than him—until Roscuro started helping.

The Soul Eater Orb had fortunately been transported along with James, though he had for some reason reverted to the form of a bracelet en route to the new location. Without asking what James wanted him to do or whether he needed help, Roscuro began forming small shields in midair all around James’s body where the green-armored man was punching. They took the brunt of the blows and gave James enough room to think.

I didn’t even realize Roscuro could do that now, James thought. He’s grown so much stronger.

It wasn’t enough power to keep James from getting beaten up, but diminishing the force behind every blow at least allowed James to block more easily.

I get why I had deja vu, he realized after a little under a minute of enduring the assault. I watched this beating happen in a vision from Sister Strange. But it was a vision set further in the future. That’s why I can’t do much to fight him right now. This guy has been training for longer. Not that I was able to beat him even in the vision… Okay. I have to try and survive and benefit from this fight any way I can.

James began counter-attacking, feebly and without conviction at first. He was just trying to make the green knight show more of his technique and powers, to learn anything he could from the skirmish.

He threw punches, Meteor Strikes, and Lightning Strikes, all of which bounced off of the other man’s armor. The green knight blocked some blows and simply allowed others to hit him with no apparent effect. He answered some strikes with counter attacks, heavy blows to James’s head, knees, and ribs.

James tried to conjure silk to obstruct the green knight’s movements, but he tore through James’s strongest threads in a single punch, and the fist seemed to arrive at James’s shoulder without any diminution in force.

The Fisher Emperor wanted to use more complex magic, but he never had the breathing room. It was impossible to focus completely when he needed all his physical might and fighting technique just to block hits.

Roscuro kept blunting the force of the attacks. James suspected that was why he remained alive. With that, and James’s full focus on blocking, he could mitigate the damage from the green figure’s ridiculous strength.

The Soul Eater had never shown this much initiative before, and he still remained totally silent, apparently trying not to distract James. Both facts spoke to how desperate the situation was. Roscuro could tell James’s life was in danger if this fight went poorly, and at any moment, it could begin to go very poorly indeed.

When one blow got through Roscuro’s rapid-fire defense and sank deep into James’s abdomen, it doubled him over and left him wheezing, vulnerable. The Soul Eater took the entire brunt of the green warrior’s attacks for a solid thirty seconds, forming a giant semi-cylindrical shield that covered James’s entire front and completely separated the two fighters from each other.

But sustaining such a complete defense against someone who so thoroughly outclassed James was clearly beyond Roscuro’s capabilities.

The green knight went at Roscuro with greater force than he’d been using on James, throwing hammer blows, haymaker punches that would probably have pierced through James’s body if he’d been hit by them. The Soul Eater had to just remain in place and take it, lest the attacks get through to James while he recovered.

As cracks began to form in the shield from the constant barrage of blunt force, James forced himself to his feet. He noticed as he rose that the warrior in green had a sword at his hip, but he hadn’t bothered to draw it.

James felt the disparity in the fight in his guts at that moment.

Damn. I’m so little threat to him, he hasn’t even used his weapon. Meanwhile, James had been entirely kept alive by the Ego Weapon using itself as a near perfect sentient defense system.

For the first time, James considered running away. The idea seemed infeasible, but the possibility of winning this fight seemed even less so.

The green knight had not used anything but punches and kicks so far. No matter what move or Skill James tried, his opponent was just employing hands and feet in response. James decided in an instant to try another strategy. It was a bit desperate, but so was his situation.

Roscuro! he sent. I’m all right back here. Withdraw to my arms and legs. He sent a mental image, and the Soul Eater obediently transformed into arm guards and shin guards.

The beating resumed.

James tried to accommodate the fact that he was slower and weaker than the man in green armor with martial arts. The Way of the Predator still told him how to move optimally in a fight, and he had learned his Class’s intrinsic martial art Skill well enough by now that he could even adjust for being a significantly slower and weaker party.

Gradually, fewer blows landed on target. More often, the green armored man found himself unexpectedly punching solid Soul Eater armor, which didn’t seem to hurt his hands but definitely blunted his force.

Then the green knight changed up the tempo of his attacks. He had been swinging brutally and violently, with little precision or restraint, like a brawler—careless of any attack James might attempt. The Fisher Emperor hadn’t managed to land a significant blow even once, so the lack of caution was understandable.

Now the warrior in green began fighting very efficiently, as if it was a knack he had been waiting to reveal. James found himself reluctantly admiring the fighting technique, even as he felt and heard his own ribs crack under the more accurate, but still horribly vicious, blows.

Wait, this feels a lot like—but it can’t be—can it?

The more skillful movements from the opponent finally rang a bell for James. It reminded him of his own semi-bestial, almost instinctive, martial arts. The Way of the Predator.

Could this guy have the Predator in Human Skin Class too? I guess it’s probably not super-uncommon. He definitely earns it with this level of violence.

Coherent thought became difficult as a brutal blow struck his head. James felt darkness creeping at the edges of his vision.

He started focusing only on defending his head, his neck, and his heart. Those were the key vital points that could kill him quickly. Every other injury, he was slowly recovering from as the fight went on, though he could feel his True Regeneration being tested by the sheer speed at which he accumulated them.

As blows rained down on every part of his body that he wasn’t desperately defending, James’s Scale Armor of the Demi-Hydra was smashed or badly dented in a hundred places. James felt and tasted blood in his mouth, and he wondered if this was where and how he was going to die. The warrior in green, when he wasn’t a blur of motion, seemed slightly winded but otherwise completely unhurt.

If the green knight didn’t get tired soon, James thought he might die.

He considered trying Soul Magic and actively attempting to kill his enemy rather than just defending, but James remembered that in the vision, that had been something that provoked the green knight to take the fight to a more brutal place. The vision had drawn to a close with a scene of the green-armored man whaling on Vision-James so violently that he smashed Vision-James’s arms to bloody stubs, shattered both his face and chest cavity, and made Present-James believe that Vision-James was about to die.

Soul Magic’s definitely a potential weak point, James thought. But if he’s still holding back some of that brutality, best not to try something guaranteed to provoke his full strength. I’m still not dead. Not yet. Still… standing… I can win a war of attrition. Just need him to get tired…

Even as he thought that, his thoughts began to come more slowly with the green-clad man’s repeated blows to James’s head.

The Fisher Emperor endured hit after hit, but he began to sway from side to side like a drunk.

This is it. I can’t take anymore. I’m going to collapse. Hopefully he doesn’t want to kill me. In the vision, he didn’t kill me. If he kills me… this is such a stupid goddamn way to die… I wouldn’t even know why…

James leaned his weight to one side in anticipation of a blow, but suddenly, the onslaught stopped, and he lurched forward a few inches before he caught himself.

What? What’s going on? Did he bruise his hands from beating the shit out of me? Why did he stop?

“Why are you so damned weak?” came the voice of the other man. He sounded grumpy.

“Wha?” James spat out a mouthful of blood before he could properly pronounce the word. “What?”

“You’re weaker than you were supposed to be,” the green knight replied. “This fight is meant to be a wake-up call for you. Instead, if I don’t hold back, I’ll just kill you. So how did you end up so damned weak?”

He sounded bizarrely angry that he’d been having an easy time fighting James.

“I’ve been displaced in time, I’m pretty sure,” James said after a moment. “Switched with my future self to fight an opponent I can’t beat at the present. At least, that’s my best guess as to what happened. Now please, tell me who the fuck you are, so this whole trip isn’t completely pointless.”

“We’re both time travelers, then,” the man in green replied with a bitter laugh. “You’re the wrong James Robard, not the one who needed a reality check… so, wherever he is, the future’s probably completely screwed in this timeline. Maybe not yours, though. Time’s funny that way… Tell you who I am? Sure, why not? In fact, I’ll do you one better. I’ll show you.”

The green knight reached up and removed his helmet. The face beneath was older and battle-scarred, a strange cross-shaped scar stretching from eye to mouth. But the features were easily recognizable nonetheless. James had seen that face every day for a matter of months, though it was still hard, even staring straight at it, to believe it was him.

“Abhi?” he asked uncertainly.

“Hi, Dad.”
