Don sat on the edge of the sofa, steepling his hands. He could sense Mal’asha watching him as she summoned a glass of some really thick red wine. She sipped it and twirled it in her hand.

“What is going on in your little head, darling?” she finally asked.

“Well I have to choose now,” Don said, still looking at the platform floor, replaying the intense fight in his mind.

“Would you like some pointers?”

“I know you’re going to suggest I take the Dominator, because it's your breed.”

“Smart man,” Mal’asha said.

“But I fear it's going to deviate too much from what has been working for me so far.”

“Oh,” Mal’asha drawled. “That is not how it works. You make the race and class.”

Don looked up. Mal’asha tilted her head and smiled.

“What do you mean?”

“Well, I can’t tell you everything. I can barely mention Meta-awareness, for starters. You’ve unlocked it which makes you ever so much more fascinating, but it is not a topic we can discuss, or the System punishes me for being a bad girl.”

“Uhhuh.”

“As for the general rules… I think I am fine, but let me test the waters. I so rarely bother interacting with mortals. Your skills so far are Sprinting, Stealth, and Unarmed Fighting. Quite a good collection for your circumstances. These skills will affect your choices. The evolutions you choose at level milestones will also affect your future choices. You are not at the whims of the System, the System is at the whims of the… players.

Mal’asha winced like a migraine had struck her. She scoffed.

“No mentioning players. I see…”

That prompted a million questions for Don, but he was more interested in the ones he could get answers for.

“So I can take the Dominator subrace and still be melee-based?”

“Of course, darling,” Mal’asha said. “Venaxthra, the Dominator you saw in the fight is of a frail race and has a caster based class. She specializes in throwing rocks, gravity, magic glyphs and lasers, what not. Mind you, she would still squash you like a bug with a single flick of a finger. But that is not what she has specialized in.”

“Yeah?” What would a melee based Dominator look like?”

“Neural overload, like you saw, basically a psionic poison. Telekinesis is all-around useful, if only for pinning down targets, slowing down attackers, deflecting projectiles, you name it. It is really useful, and I highly suggest you attempt to learn the skill immediately if you choose the option.”

Mal’asha tensed and looked around, as if awaiting for another smite from the System. It never came.

“Interesting. Looks like I have more free reign than I thought.”

“So I could basically become a melee based fighter that augments his powers with psionics?”

“Exactly, darling. I feel like it would fit you well. All that brawn with such a brain is a waste.“

“How badly can I screw up my trajectory if I make bad choices regarding my evolutions, skills and such?”

“Hm, good question,” Mal’asha said. “While the choices matter, there are methods of backtracking. I have in my possession an [Elixir of Attribute Reset], which is prohibitively rare. So it is possible, but very expensive. I could give you boons that let you reset skills. Those are more malleable. But you would have to devote yourself to me…”

“Yeah, I’ll take a rain-check on that.”

“Oh,” Mal’asha said and batted her eyelashes. “Don’t you trust me?”

“Categorically not,” Don said immediately.

Mal’asha broke into a throaty laugh and revealed rows of tightly packed needle-like teeth. “Wise of you.”

“So if I were to choose to later go for a caster route, would that be possible?”

“Yes, of course.”

“Would my previous melee-based focus be a hindrance?”

“If you were to go for a purist build, I suppose yes. But at higher levels you cannot get away with being a simple brute or a frail caster. You will simply die from being physically too weak, or lacking elaborate options. Specialization is good, but weaknesses need to be shored up.”

Don nodded to himself. He had to make a choice. Not choosing and waffling over and over was also bad. Not only did it waste time, most importantly, it would dilute his resolve.

And if there’s one thing I’ve learned in my short stint with the System, it’s that resolve and grit matters. I have to keep pushing forward. I will not get into the habit of second guessing myself.

But Don wouldn’t be hasty. He had a lot of direct threats to deal with right now. Morshoff, Blazeclaw and Jacques primarily. What he needed was power. Raw power. Reaper would give him plenty durability, and higher physical prowess. But Dominator was more versatile.

Telekinesis would give him a projectile weapon. Don figured he should start collecting knives off bodies or something. He was sure he had two of them in his satchels. Not much of a combat weapon with such a tiny blade, and not a particularly sharp tip, but if he could throw it with telekinesis with the fraction of the speed that the Dominator had been slinging shit… Now, that could do some serious damage.

The poisons, durability and area effects were interesting, but it seemed like the Reaper was adept at dealing with multiple smaller enemies and waiting it out. The poison gas, the toxic quills, everything made it an inescapable weapon of mass destruction. But when it came to concentrated damage, it seemed like the Dominator had more on-demand burst.

“I’m choosing Dominator.”

Mal’asha smiled. “That is a good choice.”

“Nothing else to say?” Don asked. “No gloating, no double entendres?”

Mal’asha chuckled. “All in good time. I like to keep a veneer of mystery too, darling.”

Don chuckled, finding himself just a tad bit more comfortable with the ancient eldritch god of hunger.

He leaned back on the sofa, enjoying the comfort as he chose his race evolution.

[*DING!* You have obtained a racial evolution!]

Evolutor (Spawn) -> Evolutor (Dominator)

You have acquired a skill slot!

You have acquired additional Attributes!

[You have acquired an Evolution: Psychic Sense (F-grade)][Psychic sense: Your pineal gland has activated and transformed into a third eye. You are now able to learn various skills related to psychic energies. Upgrading this Evolution increases the potency of all Psychic abilities. +2 Mental]

Don could feel a strange tingling in the middle of his forehead. Then a strange sensation opened up to him. He could sense a sort of an energy field around him. He felt like he could affect it, communicate with it. When he turned his attention to Mal’asha his mind was flattened almost instantly. A lancing pain struck him and his psychic sense turned off.

If the energy field was a gas in the air one could smell or faintly feel, the emanation of psychic energy from Mal’asha was a mountain. Solid, massive, overwhelming.

“Careful where you point that, darling,” she purred. “I wouldn’t want you to hurt yourself.”

Don was too busy blinking away the pain, so he just grunted.

“Now then,” she said and lifted Don off the sofa in a whisk of a wrist. The sofa and the floating screen vanished. “I believe my job here is done for now. We will be in touch, try not to die.”

“I could still use some information,” Don said.

“Oh, I am sure we could chat till kingdom come, but we both have business to attend to. Tell you what, darling. Defeat Jacques in the quest I gave you two boys and we’ll talk again.”

“I intend to,” Don said.

“Good…” Mal’asha said. Then her aura took a more sinister aspect, and her dark eyes twinkled. “Because if you lose… I’ll devour you.”

That made Don flinch. There was a clear murderous intent coming off her, so strong it made Don’s knees buckle. Just as quickly as it came it was gone.

Cold sweat slid down Don’s back as he watched Mal’asha saunter through a portal. Before she stepped in, she gave him a wink over her shoulder. “Ta-ta.”
