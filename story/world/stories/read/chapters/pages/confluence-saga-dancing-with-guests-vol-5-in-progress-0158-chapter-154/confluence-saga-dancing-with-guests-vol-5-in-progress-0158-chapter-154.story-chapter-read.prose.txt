Holding Inheritance Law

I used to think Inheritance Law was complicated, complicated enough I stuck with housing law instead. Then I Awakened spontaneously, I'm still not entirely sure why, and found out that although on paper Awakened Inheritances are simpler, they can occur while the ancestor is still alive.

Which makes complete sense. When someone can live centuries is the heir supposed to wait multiple Unawakened lifetimes before they inherit? Obviously not.

Sovereigns retire, go off to do whatever retired Sovereigns do and let their children take over. Or they step back, still technically in charge but never present because they no longer wish to be.

Larger Holdings establish cadet branches, which then establish their own cadet branches a few generations later and suddenly you've got a new Family with complex intermingling of lines that even Senior Seers want nothing to do with.

Technically any Holding with a cadet branch is considered a Family, because there are now multiple Holdings. But a single sub-Holding is seen as an uncertainty. It might fail, the parent might fail. Dozens of branches? That doesn't fall apart without a significant disaster befalling them.

And don't get me started on Mergers and Acquisitions. Or rather intermingling and fealty arrangements. Did you know nothing stops a cadet branch from breaking away entirely for one or more generations then coming back? Sometimes as the more senior Holding?

His Eminence slept fitfully. He had been awoken early to address the matter of training for the current generation. He had woken again to approve the dispatch of one of his most loyal as an instructor. He had tried to return to his sleep, to allow his thoughts to settle themselves, to create a path forward for his Family, for the Fae as a whole.

His Eminence dreamt. Even in his dreams his name was avoided. Once it had been spoken with awe, with reverence, with fear. Now few remembered it, fewer still realized that it was his name, and none dared speak it. Names were a fickle thing, when his Name appeared in his dreams he knew himself to be remembered.

He dreamt of war, he dreamt of peace. He dreamt of his descendants, those he acknowledged and those who time had forgotten. They did not look like him, they had eyes that gleamed and forms that Humanity questioned when they knew what to pay attention to. Amethyst, Sapphire, Ruby, even blinding Diamond eyes. Those were known, those were remembered. There had been others, there might be again.

His Eminence dreamt of loss, of sacrifice, of the cost of peace. His Name was the least of those costs, yet at the same time the greatest. If he was nameless, if he was only known by his title, then none might draw his attention. If he was nameless then his power was claimed by his title, or so most believed. He hoped to never show them the folly of their belief, he did not need more dreams.

He slept, unbothered by those who depended on him.

Valene sighed at the indication of three items requiring his direct attention. The first was simple enough, a completed report from Phelann that he'd been waiting for for… had it really only been two days? He'd expected it to take at least a week.

The note clarified things. She expected there might be some additional details to be found, that she'd be able to follow up as needed, however she would be visiting Scattered Creek in the coming days and wished to ensure he had the preliminary data. Knowing how thorough she usually was he doubted he'd need her to keep looking, at least until he needed specific details.

The second shouldn't have come to him, not as an urgent matter at least. Yes he kept an eye on Velli affairs, yes he wanted to be advised when they changed their behaviour or announced something new, but he didn't need to drop everything to read about their presenting a new batch of possible scions as eligible bachelors. It wasn't as though there were any names that- Wait. Why was Clyde on the list. Wasn't that the name of the one who had had something to do with Holding Syl?

He'd been reported lost, assumed dead. If he was back to being on their rolls then he clearly had not died, that he'd been in the Depths for close to nine years? Presented as eligible meant he'd passed the Wall, and from Phelann's report he'd been level 5 or 6 at the time of the incident. Hardly an elite pace by any standards, but still something worth taking note of.

The last item was something so rare he couldn't remember the last time he'd seen anything of its like. The fact that the incident had been reported proved the safeguards were properly set up, the fact that the report had come to him meant whoever had first received it recognized that he would need to be the final arbiter on the matter.

Unsanctioned use of self-directed technique. Initial query appears to be based on the work of Seer Phelann. No indication of intentional violation of existing regulations.Monitoring ongoing. Further alerts will trigger termination protocols and sanctions unless override provided by appropriate body.Lack of Seer Council affiliation noted. Independent research into this field requires further approval and assumption of responsibility.

What was Phelann up to? There wasn't any reason she should be digging into this discipline. There were guardrails in place specifically to prevent anyone from looking into these things without careful guidance. It was possible she'd created something by accident, that she'd put together the right combination of intent, instructions and Energy and ended up doing more than she realized.

But the details indicated that she had gotten results from it. There was no way she wouldn't have realized something strange was happening, no way she wouldn't have flagged the anomaly. Techniques didn't keep working on their own, every Seer knew that. The fact that every Seer was wrong about that fact was beside the point.

She hadn't mentioned it in any of her messages to him. Hadn't so much as brought up the question of whether such things were possible, let alone that she apparently had created one.

Scattered Creek. She'd have been going there once a month for the last six. She'd be interacting with a Doorkeeper on every visit. That shouldn't matter. She shouldn't have any knowledge of what a Doorkeeper actually was, any hint of its origins. The knowledge associated with that was deeply buried, carefully guarded and beyond the reach of anyone of her level. At least it was supposed to be.

With another sigh he acknowledged the report. He wouldn't provide approval, not yet at least. He'd have to talk with her, find out exactly what she was up to. Approval meant granting her access to the information she'd need to push forward. She'd have to make some decisions about what that meant. At least Holding Syl had a Seed and not a Core.
