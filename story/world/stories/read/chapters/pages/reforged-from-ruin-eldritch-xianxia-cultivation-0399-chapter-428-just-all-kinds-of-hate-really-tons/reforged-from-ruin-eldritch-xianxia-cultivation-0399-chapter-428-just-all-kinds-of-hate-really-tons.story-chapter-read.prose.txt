Flesh Of Liquid Form Technique: Flooded Dark

“Oh, now you want to coordinate?” Lei Hai says, throwing a glare back in his direction. “Whenever it’s something you can’t handle alone, then you’ll talk, to try to bludgeon us into obeying!”

“Don’t try to tell me what to think!”

Yu Guanyu vomits blood as Dai Yuhan shrieks, leaves spiraling out from out of water and seemingly tearing at her skull. Lei Hai manages to keep to just one knee for a moment, but where her body holds firm, her eyes go blank, her face turning slack from some sort of mental overload.

Geng He spasms, his balanced thrown off and his body convulsing on the floor. He can barely look at it without screaming, except he can’t scream at all, because he can’t even breathe.

Geng He’s lungs collapse, all of the breath ripped out of them, even as the wall closes shut and fresh air rushes in to replace what was lost.

The only saving grace is that the creatures in the hallway fared worse. Shattered shells and viscera are splattered from where the vacuum sucked a lot of the exoskeletal creature’s insides to the outside.

It stinks. It hurts. He tries to breathe but his lungs are shaped wrong, collapsed inward and emptied completely.

He might die here.

The thought is loud. He didn’t have that before. He just fought and fought until he couldn’t think of anything at all anymore, until he went blind and mad and kept cutting and fighting and reacting. Here, now, he feels his body spasming, trying to make itself function again and failing in spite of his newfound vitality.

He doesn’t die quickly, though. Too much energy in him for that. Too much enhancement that this hidden realm has offered.

So he just keeps spasming, trying to get his mind to work again.

“Wow! Sorry about that! Let a little something slip for a minute there!”

He hears the voice, but the words are basically gibberish.

Then his lungs inflate, and the broken ribcage he didn’t notice resets, and all of the exhaustion and pain in him is just gone.

“Don’t worry though! I promise it won’t happen again! I’ve got a handle on it now. At least I think so. Probably. Enough that you all should be fine.”

He turns his head, Yu Guanyu still retching to one side but trying to do the same. Geng He notices that the bloody puke from before has vanished, right alongside the plant-growths that were trying to crack Dai Yuhan’s skull open.

Growing out of the ceiling is a disembodied face. Well, not disembodied really- there’s a long spinal column wrapped in thick muscular tissue that lets it extend down from the ceiling, but the only “human” thing about it would be the face itself.

The spirit of the hidden realm (or of this temple, or of the trials, or whatever the hell it is) smiles down at them. Geng He thinks it’s meant to be reassuring but it does not do a very good job of it. The eyes are wrong.

“Your Flesh should be all fixed up! Don’t want any outside interference on the trials, right? As a little apology from me though, if you take that corner, I’ll put up a rest area with some medicine for you to take with you. How’s that sound?”

They hear the flesh ripple down the way, portending said changes, even as the creatures splattered around them also start to squelch and shift and get put back together. Slowly, but not so slowly that it’s not visible.

“Great! Good luck again! You guys are some of my favorites!”

And the face is gone, retracted with a wet sucking sound into the ceiling above.

Geng He groans.

Worst. Hidden realm. Ever.
