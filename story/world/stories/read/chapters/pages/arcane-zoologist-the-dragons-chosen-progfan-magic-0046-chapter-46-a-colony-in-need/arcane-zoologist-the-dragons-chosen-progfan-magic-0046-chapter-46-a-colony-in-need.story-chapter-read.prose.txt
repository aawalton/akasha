"Brother, Brother!" Callie called as she scrambled out of the dragon's cave.

"Callie!" Callen ran up to her immediately, checking over her body for injuries.

"I'm fine, don't worry! But you won't believe what I saw. No, actually, let's head back home. I want Mom and Dad to hear this, too," she tugged on his arm, pulling him in the direction of their home.

"What about the mushrooms?" Callen asked.

"We can get more and completely safely, but we just have to help out the glimmerhares first!"

They hurried home, where Callie explained everything that had happened. She told them of the warning she heard in the swamp, and how she thought it likely came from the dragon. She also explained that it was possible to get lantern mushrooms safely if they gave the glimmerhares some carrots in return.

Of course, Callie didn't skip on the details of her discoveries. Snowglow's thicker fur and lighter horns, and the amazing fact that glimmerhare had a whole tunnel system underground where dozens upon dozens of them lived together.

"To think that there were so many glimmerhares in the area… I thought for sure that it was two, maybe three at the max," Mom said thoughtfully.

"But there's a whole colony, and you don't see them doing anything bad to people. They just live peacefully on their own!" Callie said smugly.

"Yes… I suppose that is true, since there are so many of them…" Mom said.

"So all we have to do is give them some carrots, and they'll give us the mushrooms. Coat problem solved," Callie said cheerily, although she felt unease within her.

Mom and Dad looked at each other. Callie knew the problem they had, but she hoped that perhaps there was a small chance that they had more food than she had thought. Perhaps, they could spare some for the glimmerhares.

"Callie, we can give them a few carrots, but not enough to feed a whole colony. How much food do you think that would require?" Mom said.

Callie slumped. She knew that this was coming, but it was still difficult to hear. "Well, we can try giving what we can and see if glimmerhares would give us the mushrooms in return, right? No harm in trying?"

Mom looked at Dad. Dad nodded. "Yes, let's give what we can afford. I do have to say that I'm proud of you two."

"Proud?" Callie cocked her head.

"I'm not sure if it was a dragon or perhaps merely your gut instinct, but I'm glad you left the swamp when you believed it to be dangerous. Although I don't approve of following those glimmerhares in the cave… it appears they truly are harmless. But this doesn't mean you're allowed to follow every arcane beast you see!"

"Of course. I only followed Snowglow because I know her. She would never do anything to harm me," Callie confirmed.

Mom and Dad then collected as many carrots as they could spare for the glimmerhares. It really wasn't much. While they had managed to grow a lot this year, since carrots didn't depend so much on warmth to grow as other crops did. But since their other crops hadn't grown as well, their family would have to rely on carrots during winter.

Nonetheless, Callie and Callen went back to the dragon's cave. They brought the dozen or so carrots into the cave, and she called for Snowglow. Before long, the glimmerhare appeared from the opening in the wall, eyeing Callen nervously.

"I'm not going to harm you!" Callen said in a soft voice, taking a few steps back. "Thank you for helping us!"

Snowglow stared at Callen for a moment and hopped out of the opening. She didn't go close to him, but at least seemed comfortable enough in his presence.

This… not enough.

Callie nodded. "I know… I'm sorry, but there isn't much we can spare."

Snowglow seemed downcast.

Babies starve… Winter too soon.

Callie's heart clenched. "What do you usually do for food during winter? How do you survive?"

Gather roots, leaves, and nuts for winter. Keep underground.

It didn't take long to realize what was wrong. "You didn't have enough time to gather food this year. Because of the sudden frost."

Yes.

Callie looked at her brother and explained the problem going on with glimmerhares. She… She couldn't just accept this. It wasn't even about mushrooms anymore. This went beyond that. These poor glimmerhares didn't have enough food to last them the winter. If Callie didn't do something, then the weakest members of the colony, or in the worst case scenario… the whole colony might die of starvation.

That couldn't happen. Not to Snowglow's family. Callie had to do something about this. Surely, there must be something she could do.

Callie looked down at her hands. She had managed to grow a whole field of flowers and herbs. Wouldn't it be possible to grow carrots, too?

Although Callie had no confidence that she could just order carrots to appear in the ground out of nothing. But perhaps if she worked on seeds, or better yet, on slightly grown carrots.

"Brother, did you have any ungrown crops left in the garden?" Callie asked.

Callen nodded. "I did sow more carrots at the very end of summer, but they didn't have enough time to grow since the frost came so early. They're still in the ground, since I haven't really had the time to clear them out yet."

"That's perfect! I'll try to grow them," Callie announced.

"What? Callie, no!"

"Callie, yes! Brother, isn't that perfect? Those carrots would be lost anyway. But if I grow them for Snowglow and her colony, then it would be perfect. Besides, I do need to practice my magic somehow."

Callen looked torn, then scratched the back of his head. "Let's ask Mom and Dad first."

So they did. Dad resisted the idea at first, saying it was too dangerous to do any magic. That neighbors could discover it. But the only neighbors who could view their garden were Tristan's family. And it wasn't a clear view either. They would have to go out of their way to watch their garden.

Mom agreed to Callie's idea. "Callen will keep watch to make sure nobody sees Callie. She needs proper practice for magic. I believe this is a good opportunity."

So Callie was allowed to try to grow the late summer carrots still in the ground. Thankfully, the ground wasn't fully frozen yet, so after scraping off the snow from the top of the soil, she could see the small, withered stalks of the carrots.

Although the stalks were withered from the cold, the carrots themselves turned out to be completely fine. Although they were very small, smaller even than Callie's pinky finger.

"I'm going to try one at a time first," Callie said, and picked one of the carrots.

She pressed her finger against it. Magic would likely work better if you were touching the thing you wanted to do magic on. Right? Callie didn't know for sure, but it felt like a plausible conclusion.

Reaching for the magic within her, Callie tried to guide it to the carrot. Of course, she had no clue how to actually guide this weird energy. She just thought hard about the carrot growing big.

Earth shifted lightly beneath her finger. The carrot was growing. Elated Callie pulled out the carrot… only to find it large and rotten.

What did I do wrong? Why did it rot?

Admittedly, it was better than the time she had burned the poor dandilynx plant. But her approach hadn't been right. Perhaps she should try remembering the feeling she had when she had grown the dandilynx and the field of flowers in front of the rangers.

I felt really happy and peaceful at that time. Maybe that's what plants need to grow?

Callie tried again on another carrot. She imagined all the times she spent together with Wiggles. The softness of Snowglow's fur, and the time she saw thunderfox mother cuddled up to her babies.

The magic within her seemed to flow more freely now. Too freely, in fact. Before Callie could stop it, she could feel a chunk of the magical energy burst out of her. The carrot grew instantly, its stalks perking up and reaching higher and higher until they started to bloom.

"No, no. Stop!" Callie pulled her hand back, but it was too late.

Callen came running at her exclamation. "What happened? Are you alright?"

"I'm fine, but carrot," she whimpered and pulled it out of the ground. It was overgrown.

"That's not going to be tasty…" Callen noted, wincing. Everyone knew that once carrots started blooming, they tasted bad. It was safe to eat them, and they were manageable in soups and stews, but raw… Yuck!

The glimmerhares likely wouldn't reject a carrot such as this, but Callie felt that she could do better. Not to mention that it had used up a lot of her energy. It would be better to use the correct amount and get the carrot to grow to normal size, rather than overgrow.

"Maybe you can't get it right because it's too cold? It's a miracle you managed to do anything in this weather," Callen noted.

"Too cold…" Callie pondered. Plants needed warmth to grow.

With the overgrown carrot, she might have just burst out too much of her magic, so it compensated for the lack of warmth. But what if she tried to make it warm for the carrot as she tried to grow it? Apply a bit of heat, a bit of controlled anger to the magic.

Callie put her fingers against another carrot and tried again. She tried to imagine both happiness and anger. To let the carrot grow in warmth. But it was difficult to focus on both emotions at once. Anger quickly overtook the feeling of joy, and it resulted in an utterly dry carrot.

"That's not edible…" Callen noted.

Callie sighed, tossing the carrot aside. This was hard. Why couldn't the carrots grow just like those wildflowers? Then again, you couldn't just toss carrot seeds into the soil and expect them to grow properly.

To grow the best carrot, you need to take care of it. Make sure it has enough space to grow. Enough water. That weeds wouldn't be growing around it and blocking out the sun. They also didn't need that much heat, just a little bit of warmth, since they were rather sturdy in this regard.

This time, as Callie pressed her fingers against the ungrown carrot, she thought about all the things the carrot needed to grow. She thought of warm, gentle sun, of fresh summer days with a bit of rain, and she focused on the happy feeling she felt while growing flowers.

Her magic seemed to understand her better this time. It flowed more calmly and didn't resist as much as before. It was by no means perfect. The moment she got excited over the progress, it threatened to spill out of her. But she managed to get herself in control just in time.

"I… I think I did it!" Callie said, pulling her hand back.

"Then what are you waiting for? Pull it out!" Callen urged.

Callie nodded and reached for the carrot, pulling it out of the cold ground. Out came a perfectly ripe, large carrot.

"It worked, brother! It worked!" Callie cheered, brandishing the carrot high in the air.

"Callie!" Callen suddenly called out, his expression panicked. "Your nose is bleeding!"

"What?" Dumbfounded, Callie touched the skin beneath her nose. Looking at her fingertip, she saw that it was stained red with blood.

Her vision warped, and Callie felt very faint all of a sudden.
