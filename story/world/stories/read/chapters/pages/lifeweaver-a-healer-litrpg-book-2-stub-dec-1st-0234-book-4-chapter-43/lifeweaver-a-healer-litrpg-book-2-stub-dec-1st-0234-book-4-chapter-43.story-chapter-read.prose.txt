Guide had mentioned a few individuals having been selected for a new kind of mission. Luke had kind of skimmed over that part of his messages, since he'd been too tired to consider what it meant. Now that he had been picked for that group of Integrated, it was clear those missions could only be about one thing. The fact that he'd sensed something while he worked on Enchanting the large pile of debris and garbage should have tipped him off, but he'd been so into what he was doing that Luke didn't connect the dots. It was about the void. It was always about the void.

Appearing first in empty darkness, then in an office he knew well, Luke found himself sitting opposite Guide. This time, however, he had company.

"Luke?" Hannah asked, reaching her hand out to him from the chair to his left.

He took her hand. "Hey."

Ella was there as well. She looked calm and collected, but pale. Her eyes darted this way and that, as if searching for a way to escape.

"Ella," Luke said, trying for a reassuring smile.

All he got in return was a hopeless glance.

The other three, he didn't recognize. One was a man with wide shoulders and a square-looking face on account of his massive jaw. He was maybe forty and dressed like, well... a wizard, complete with a purple robe, a matching pointy hat, and a thick leather tome clutched in his hands. The last one, who sat on Luke's right, couldn't be older than eighteen. With dark, curly hair and deep brown eyes, his face was set in an expression of disgust? Disdain? Luke wasn't sure. His head was tilted up a little, as if he was looking down at everyone around him. Everything about the guy's aura put Luke on edge, and he couldn't help but dislike him before he'd spoken a single word.

"Welcome," Guide said. He sat behind that same worn-looking desk. Even though he looked the same way he always did, there was something about him that made Luke think he was tired. Exhausted, even.

"I want to leave," Ella said, interrupting whatever Guide was going to say next.

Guide gave her a long, interested look. "Are you sure?"

"I don't want this," she said.

Luke leaned toward her. "It'll be fine, Ella."

She shook her head. "Look, I'm grateful, Luke, but I'm out. This isn't what I want." Turning to Guide, she raised her voice. "Let me out!"

Guide waited a beat longer, then complied. "Free will is not to be tampered with. This system needs you, Integrated Ella, but what is expected of you cannot be taken by force."

Ella disappeared into thin air.

"What exactly do you want us to do?" Hannah asked, looking a little shaken by the interaction.

"He wants us to keep the void out," Luke said, before turning to give Guide a look. "Right?"

"That is correct."

The young guy said something in a language that sounded almost, but not quite, like Spanish. Guide responded in that same language, then gave the square wizard a look. A heartbeat later, Luke felt something change within his head. It was slight, but undeniable.

"What did you just do?" he asked.

"Mess with my head and I will have yours!" the wizard spat, and a bunch of symbols on his book glowed in colors of deep orange and violet.

Luke blinked. The words he'd just parsed weren't English, but a thick dialect of Russian, but he understood the words nonetheless. "Did you just put a Babel fish into our brains?"

The two strangers both turned to give him astonished looks, but probably because they understood Luke speaking.

"Language should no longer be a barrier to cooperaetion."

"Why should I help you?" the non-Spanish guy asked.

Integrated Dimitrios. Level 42. Mythos.

Inspect worked in Guide's weird little office. That helped.

Integrated Popov. Level 39. Grandmaster.

Those were some interesting-sounding classes. Their levels weren't anything to sneeze at either. Other than himself and Alan, Luke hadn't seen anyone that high.

"What I'm offering is a chance for you to help your system. Integrated Luke here," Guide gestured to him. "Is correct in his assumption that it is the void you will be dealing with. The reason you have been chosen is not because of your advanced level. All four of you have shown promise in will-based manipulation, and you seem to have an inherent understanding of the system and the nothingness beyond."

Dimitrios sneered. "You're offering experience? I can get that from dungeons."

"The destruction of the Singer system has led to conflict in The Greater System. This, in turn, has diverted resources from those responsible for your initial safekeeping. The void is always coming, and it is up to you to stop it. You can see this as training for what is to come. Your reward will be fitting, with a lot of experience diverted from other important aspects of system upkeep and Integrated advancement. Refusal of this offer is within your rights, of course, but know that it will spell the end for Earth as you know it."

"I reached Level 145, and then someone took those levels back," Luke said, shaking his head. "Was that you? Are you part of this 'council'?"

"I am not," Guide said.

Popov and Dimitrios both turned to look at him when he mentioned that level, suspicion and excitement warring for control of their expressions.

"Care to explain it then?" Luke asked.

"I am unable to do so at this time. Know that it was not my decision, but I do consider it a just one."

Luke grunted. "So experience is a resource, then? Not something there's an endless supply of?"

"That is correct."

"Why?" Popov asked. "Why these rules? It's like a game, but it is broken."

"You will learn more once Earth undergoes full integration into The Greater System. For now, I'll need your verbal confirmation that you agree to take part in the missions."

"I'm not sure what you want me to do," Hannah said.

Guide nodded, as if having waited for that cue to give a more thorough explanation. Leaning forward, he continued, "The void is forever testing The Greater System and all systems within, aiming to consume everything that is. If a break happens, it can mean the end of a system. You will not be tasked with breaks, but you will be alerted to instances where the void is testing your system, trying to find a way inside. Repelling such instances will be your mission."

The four of them sat in silence for a moment until Hannah spoke up again. "How?"

"That method is different for each of you. If you wish to share your tactics with your fellow Integrated, you may do so, but it is not for me to share that information with others."

"But how?" Hannah asked, now sounding like she was becoming exasperated.

"What kind of question is that, woman?" Dimitrios asked. "Like with everything else, we kill."

"Say 'woman' like that to my face again, and I'll rip yours off," Hannah said, the tentacles of shadow in her hair wriggling.

Popov tried a more friendly approach. "My spells can affect the astral plane and reach the limits of our system. Through these abilities, I may touch the advanced script that spells out Earth and everything in it. You must have similar abilities, or you would not be in this room."

Guide nodded. "You all have the ability. Do you accept?"

"I accept," Luke said. There wasn't much else you could do when your system faced annihilation. Also, getting experience without going through the hassle of dungeons seemed a fine idea.

"I accept," Popov echoed.

Dimitrios leaned back and sighed. "Fine."

"And you, Integrated Hannah?" Guide asked.

Luke leaned closer to her. "We'll figure it out. It's like what we tried with the portal. You'll get there."

"Sure, I guess," Hannah said. "Better not give me the first mission, though."

Guide rose to his feet and placed his hands on the desk in front of him. "Very well. Your new role comes with a title and some other advantages, but for now there are issues in need of your immediate attention. Please attend to them. I will be in contact soon."

Darkness blinked past, and then Luke was back on the ground next to his pile of rubble, with Smudge happily bringing more and more trash. The new title flashed in Luke's inventory, and he couldn't deny it was probably a good one.

System Guardian: As one of the first in your system to stand against the void in its true form, you have reached heights most can never dream of. Your reward for shouldering this burden is a permanent increase in resilience against ill effects in all things related to system shaping. You also stand as one of your system's representatives in The Greater System.

Weavestepping through the weave just became a lot easier, if he had to guess, and using system threads wouldn't feel like having your insides melted every time, he hoped. While he'd grown somewhat used to the pain, he'd rather do without.

Hannah: "That was something."

Luke: "Yeah. I'll go deal with the thing now, and we'll talk in a bit?"

Hannah: "Be careful."

Hannah: "Who am I kidding? I know you won't be."

Luke: "You get me."

He took a look at the complete mess of stuff Smudge had gathered and sighed. The small respite for enchanting was over, at least for this time. Rank 4 was so close, but he couldn't in good conscience ignore attacks from the void to create more pebbles of speed. Focusing, Luke let his mind drift into the metaphysical realm, to where he knew the void waited for him.
