Part of the deal he’d struck with the tailor in Poltar had been a lifetime warranty on his armor, meaning he could bring it back for free repairs whenever it was damaged. Honestly, it was a genius marketing idea, because anything powerful enough to damage the armor to the point where it needed repairing would almost certainly kill the person wearing it as well.

Unless that person could come back even from death.

At this point, Asher had made almost a dozen trips back to the tailor over the past couple of months, and the man had quickly gone from amazement, to disbelief, to annoyance as he was forced to keep repairing the armor he’d made. Though reducing the armor to literal scraps was a new one, and even Asher felt a little bad this time around.

Not bad enough to skip the free repairs of course, but still bad.

As soon as he was clean of his own blood, Asher yanked some clothes and his backup set of armor from his rift, donning them both along with a second bandoleer he’d picked up ages ago for this same purpose.
