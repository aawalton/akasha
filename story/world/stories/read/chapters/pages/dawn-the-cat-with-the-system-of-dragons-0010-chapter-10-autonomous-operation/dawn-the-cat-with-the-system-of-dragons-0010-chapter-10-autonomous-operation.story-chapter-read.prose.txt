—The previous day, when the big rat attacked the mother cat’s den—

During twilight, a calm atmosphere filled the forest. The orange sky was fading into the night. Everything seemed well, but suddenly, the black cat sensed something moving towards her den. She had never noticed any animals near her new hideout before, so this was very unusual.

Suddenly, a rat appeared from the dense undergrowth. The black cat had fought this rat earlier, inflicting a minor injury, but it wasn't enough to make it stay put for long. The cat knew it was here for revenge. She had met these kinds of foes many times before.

From the front, the rat immediately charged straight towards the cat’s den, which was under the tree roots. The black cat saw the rat coming directly towards her babies, and she immediately decided to intercept. She instantly launched herself towards the rat, and they collided head-on.

The sheer force of the collision threw the cat back into her den. She hit the opposite wall and fell upon her babies, who were sleeping in a small ditch. The impact of the black cat’s body from above was too much for them, and they started mewling with distress and pain.

The cat’s back and neck were hurting. During the short collision, the rat had bitten the cat’s neck, wanting to deal as much damage as possible.
