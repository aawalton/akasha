“What? What do those colors mean?” Amon asked.

Whenever the topic involved magic, he became far more invested than usual. And now that the priest had mentioned affinity toward different elements, Amon had completely forgotten everything else.

“Your mana appears compatible with most elements,” the priest muttered slowly.

Mana shimmered around Amon’s hands as he channeled it into the spheres. He focused entirely on the artifacts, trying to understand how they functioned and whether they were connected to something beyond the mortal world.

The moment his senses grasped a faint connection, Amon flinched and immediately severed it.

I had been focused on Amon’s senses the entire time. The only reason I allowed him to experiment was because I wanted to see how mortals perceived divine.

And he saw eyes.

Four golden eyes with vertical pupils like those of a snake.

It was possible to establish a connection with the deity through the artifact they created.

Perhaps Amon’s demigod-like state was what allowed him to perceive the link. Very few mortals could wield divine power, let alone possess some of their own, after all.

But if Amon could trace those connections, then so could Aurelion.

And more importantly, so could I.

If the time ever came to hunt the Night God, I could potentially use one of his relics to locate him.
