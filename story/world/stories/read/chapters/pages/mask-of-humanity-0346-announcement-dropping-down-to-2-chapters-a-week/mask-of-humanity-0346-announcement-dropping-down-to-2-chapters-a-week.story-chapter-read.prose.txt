Hey guys, I have an announcement to make. It’s unfortunate but tbh, now I actually sit back and think about it seriously, I realise this was pretty much inevitable.

What you can expect, in short:

More detail:

I have a whole bunch of reasons for this. To start with, there’s so much I want to do but increasingly I just never have the time; new stories I want to finish writing and start uploading, I need to do a bunch of edits so I can finally put MOH on Amazon (I originally planned to totally restructure the whole thing but I’ve given up on that, there’s no way I’ll ever have the time to make it ‘perfect’, but even just doing the main big edits that I feel would accomplish the most with the least time investment, it will still take a lot of work), and I’ve gotten a lot busier with work these days.

There’s also the fact I feel the story is suffering. Ever since I ran out of backlog I’ve struggled to maintain the level of quality I want, as I just don’t have the time for it while sticking to 3 chapters a week. Not just in terms of prose but also fixing stuff people point out, plotting, worldbuilding. There’s a lot of worldbuilding I want to do but I never seem to have the time to sit down and think stuff through. I thought that the one month break would give me a chance to rebuild my backlog and solve all the problems but that turned out to be just a temporary bandaid.

Then the main huge issue is my tendonosis, which is severely limiting how much I can write. It had actually gotten much better just prior to Christmas, so much so I thought I could relax a little and stop doing my tendon strengthening exercises, that they were recovered enough. This turned out to be a huge mistake as by the end of christmas they were bad again and now it’s actually worse than ever.

I thought dictation could solve this but I have quite a few issues with it. There’s the misheard words issue ofc, but the main problem is that I just write a lot better than I talk. I can write something and even on the first draft it’s pretty decent. But fingers are connected to a different part of the brain than your vocal cords, and I can very clearly see my “speaking” brain is nowhere near as practised as my “typing” brain when it comes to story writing, because my dictated stuff is of a way lower quality than typed stuff, which leads me to go and do a bunch of editing, to the point where dictating has become more just something I use for a crappy first draft which I then completely rewrite.

All this has culminated in me leaving writing chapters to the last minute to give my tendons as much time as possible. The latest chapter, I literally wrote about 80% of it in the 3 hours before it was scheduled to be uploaded. Which definitely isn’t good for the quality of the story. My best work comes when I write it far in advance then have time to read it a couple times before uploading it, tweak stuff, fix stuff, occasionally even think up a way better way and scrap it and rewrite it entirely. You would be surprised by how many scenes in book 1 were like a 3rd or even 4th iteration of an original idea, where I wrote it then was like ehhh it just doesn’t feel right and eventually worked out a better way and implemented that. I’ve had that kind of moment numerous times writing book 2 but then simply… not had any time to go through this process, because the chapter is due to release in a week.

Anyway, ultimately what this all comes to is I’ve realised that in spite of my occasional effort to Just Write A Lot and rebuild my backlog, it’s never going to happen while sticking to 3 chapters a week. At one point I managed to rebuild to like a month and a half of backlog (and all that frantic writing definitely wasn’t good for the tendons) and then it just… vanished. I don’t even know how, just life happened.

Only option is to drop down to 2 chapters a week - and there is a reasonably high chance of me going down to 1 chapter a week, at some point in the future. We’ll see. For now I’m gonna try at 2 chapters a week, see how that works for me. But if it’s still too much, I will ultimately go down to 1, which I feel sure is a rate I could manage quite easily literally forever, no chance of burning out. Ultimately I feel this slow release will result in a much better overall story, which I can also happily keep up for the years it’ll take to complete this narrative.

Also going to take a week break here, since right now specifically my tendons are at their worst and I need to rest them.

I’m gonna reduce the Patreon price a bit too, since I figure that’s only fair since you’ll be getting less chapters. Going forward I’m reducing it from £8 to £6. In terms of USD, the conversion to dollars is always a bit confusing to me (Patreon definitely puts it a bit higher than you’d expect just based on GBP-USD conversion), but I think it charges $11.50 right now, so I’d expect that to end up around $9, give or take a bit.

Sorry about all this anyway, hope you to see you guys a week from now and thanks for your support so far!
