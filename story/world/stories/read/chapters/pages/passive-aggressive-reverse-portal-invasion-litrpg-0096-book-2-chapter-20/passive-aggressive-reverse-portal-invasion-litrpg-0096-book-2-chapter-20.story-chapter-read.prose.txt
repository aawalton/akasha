The unexpected mental commentary made me jolt with shock. A barb flickered below my shield and pierced my boot. Fortunately, it got stuck in the material without entering my skin, sticking out of the boot like some punk-ass accessory.

“Puta madre!” Victoria didn’t hold back, hand outstretched as she prepared to poison whoever that was. “Show yourself!”

“Please don’t kill me.” The mental voice urged. A second later, they did as asked. I didn’t have time for a proper look, but did spot somebody looking an awful lot like a Hiscariat emerging from behind a boulder a few dozen meters back in the canyon.

Grimacing, I moved left, trying to allow myself the leeway to keep an eye on the newcomer, while still being able to keep the flow of Ceaseless at bay. I… didn’t succeed. The moment I veered too far, the incoming Fighter, even slowed, decided that Steve looked like a more appetizing target than myself. I cursed and sprinted back to the center of the canyon, even as I felt an itch between my shoulder blades.

Clearly, Victoria felt as mistrustful as I was. “The fuck do you want? You know, if I was a suspicious hija de puta, I’d attack first and ask questions later, what with you arriving right when we’re fighting for our lives.”

“Please don’t!” The mental voice did sound earnest, very beseeching. “I just don’t understand why anybody would want to throw their lives away against the Ceaseless.”

“That’s not what’s happening.” Steve shouted. “Listen, I am sorry, but you are really distracting. We need to focus on this fight. And we’re not trying to slay the Ceaseless alone. We’re just going to hold them back for a while.”

“Okay? That leads me to my next question, I suppose. Are you aware that the Ceaseless Minds are about to hit? Once the Spitters arrive, it’s usually an hour or so before a Mind shows up, taking over co-ordination of every Ceaseless in range.”

The last Spitter dropped from the poison. I sprinted forward, meeting the incoming Fighter with a perfect block of its incoming claws. Somehow, the Wave of Reflection failed to kill the beast – Perhaps the claws weren’t poised to do enough damage, came at an odd angle or something – but the beast subsequently biting down on the edge of the shield in its hurry to get at me did the trick.

I looked around, panting, realizing there was a short opening before the next group was upon us. Now, I saw the Hiscariat. She – because it was clearly a woman, with slender facial features and long, braided hair – stood at the center of the canyon, tentacles far away from the daggers sheathed on her belt.

Running back toward the others, I shouted. “Okay, you have thirty seconds to explain yourself. We’re holding this place for at least another nine-and-a-half hours or so. This is the first we’ve heard of any Ceaseless Minds, and now you’re talking about them like they’re unavoidable.”

Her tentacles waved about in the air as if they were trying to perform a full air-drum solo. “Well, they are! Anybody out here would be able to tell you! Why else do you think no Hiscariat have made headway into their territory? Two hours of expansion, three at the outside, and they fall upon us like a ticked-off Calamity. It’s not that the Minds are strong, they just allow the damn things to co-ordinate! Sometimes, we are even unlucky, and meet one straight away.” Her mental voice sounded heartfelt and earnest, frustrated even.

I growled. That made no damn sense. Fortune-Unfolds would’ve told us about it, if those Minds were as efficient as she claimed. Huh. “Listen. How the hell did you get here anyway?”

“Erm. I walked? Same way you did? Obviously, you’d cleared the area of any Ceaseless, and it was easy. I have some talents that help me move about by myself, but-”

“Shut up, shut up!” I growled, turning my back on her, as a group of five – two Ceaseless Fighters and three Scouts – advanced close together. They started glowing as Victoria’s talents took effect, slowing and poisoning the lot. “If you’re not lying, how the hell did you make it past Fortune-Unfolds and the builders?”

“Why won’t you make sense? What builders? Fortune-Unfolds is my superior. He works with scouts, not with builders. And what would he be doing out here? He rarely ever leaves the Outpost.”

“What the hell is that? Why are you lying? He is right back there, preparing defenses! Him and the builders.” Victoria shouted, pointing back the canyon.

“He was not.” The Hiscariat sounded deadly afraid at this point. “There was nobody in the canyon. Listen, can we just leave? Or else, at the very least I will have to go. You’re running out of time.”

“We can’t just leave! If we do, this whole thing falls apart!”

“Why? What’s happening? What is so important that you’ll face certain death for it?” The gesticulations of the Hiscariat’s tentacles were becoming so frantic they looked more like flailing.

“Something is seriously wrong.” I said, though for the life of me, I couldn’t have said what. I activated my Running ability, which allowed me trick the attacking Ceaseless Fighters and reach the Scouts at an unexpected speed. One Scout took itself out of the equation immediately, while another broke a leg on an unlucky Deflect.

Leaving only the Fighters as real, but slow, problems, I ran back immediately. Meanwhile, my headache was increasing. I couldn’t focus!

“I agree.” Steve pointed at the Hiscariat. “She’s trying to turn us on Fortune-Unfolds.”

“No, not that. I think….” Victoria gritted her teeth and rubbed her forehead. “Steve. Change your buff to Mental Control. Just for a moment.”

“No.” He refused instantly. “We need the stamina.”

“What are you all doing?” The Hiscariat sounded like she was on the verge of a nervous breakdown. “I… those Fighters in the distance look organized. I am leaving. May your thoughts live on in the collective.”

“Wait.” I gritted my teeth, forcing myself not to think about what was happening. “Victoria. I think that… try this!” Reaching into my Personal Universe, I grasped a small bag that I’d only seen once and tossed it behind myself. Then I hunkered down behind my shield, backing up awkwardly, as the remaining Ceaseless advanced on me at once.

Moments later, a cry of frustration emerged from behind myself. “Me cago en la leche de sus muertos! Fucking Fortune! Steve. Eat this!”

I evaded one blow, activated my Dodge skill to improve my dodging capabilities and weaved my way out of the attacks of the trio of Fighters all trying to come at me at once.

Then I heard Steve’s comment. “That little fucker is dead!”

I almost turned to stare at that. Steve? Cursing? Only, there was no time or attention for anything but survival. I weaved and bobbed, took a weak blow on the edge of my helmet, leapt backward, giving myself space to isolate a Fighter and disperse with him with a Wave of Reflection. Then one Scout, already damaged, succumbed to Victoria’s poison, and the pressure was off, as rapidly as it had emerged.

Slowly, I started backing away again, now in control. “What is going on?

I felt a wave of weakness, as one massive buff left me. Steve’s Fortitude buff which granted us all a 50% increase to Fortitude faded into nothingness, only to be replaced by… Mental Control? My mind grew sharper, more focused. Yet, I couldn’t hold back the automatic reaction. Alarmed, I said. “No! Fortune-Unfolds told us to-”

“You need a dose too.” Steve said. He held something under my nose.

I didn’t hesitate, didn’t think, just accepted the offering. It was a pill, or, rather, what a pill might have looked like a few hundred years back. There were plant fragments clearly visible inside the sticky, compressed thing, and it reeked like that Swedish moonshine my first foster family made in the backyard shed.

It tasted worse than it smelled.

Yet, just a moment later, the effect kicked in and my brain exploded.

You have received a buff.

+3 to Mental Power

+3 to Mental Control

+15 to Mental Resistance

Duration 3 hours, 59 minutes

As my mind was freed, with it came the realization that had been evading me. Fortune Unfolds had messed with our heads. Whatever he’d done, it hadn’t been complete puppeteering. In fact, neither of us had realized it was happening. Yet, in between his happy-go-lucky attitude and solid reasoning, some of his suggestions had turned into commands. Commands, backed by something stronger. Keep boosting Fortitude instead of other attributes. (Because Mental Control or Power might break his control.) Hold your ground. Turn them back. We need twelve hours! Both of these simple, yet a constant push, not to be questioned or disobeyed.

“That motherfucker. I’m gonna tear off his arms and make fried squid rings!”

Victoria nodded at me with flared nostrils, then behind me, at the oncoming Ceaseless. “I’m with you. Only, perhaps we run first, plot our vengeance later.”

Escaping the Ceaseless was easier than expected. Usually, the Scouts were fast enough they’d be able to keep up with us. However, it seemed that newcomer had been right, and something was coordinating them, because they stayed right back with the other Ceaseless, advancing as a group of about a dozen that I could see. And since they were moving at the reduced speed of the Spitters, we were able to stay at a jog and still comfortably outpace them.

That allowed us to address some of the less comfortable topics in the room. I sped up, catching up to the Hiscariat, wondering just how to start this. ‘So, I see you guys like to use mind control?’ maybe? Yeah. That’d be a nice little ice breaker.
