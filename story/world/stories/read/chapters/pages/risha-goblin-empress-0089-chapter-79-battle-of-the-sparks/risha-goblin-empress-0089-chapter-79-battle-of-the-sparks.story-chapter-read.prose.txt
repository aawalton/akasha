Spears of darkness lifted the enemy around her, holding the ten scaled warriors into the ceiling as Nyx’s shadow twisted under Risha’s feet.

The darkness retreated to Risha, dropping them to the floor and revealing a door of stone, held wide open with Klaz’zks’s webs.

They’d thought to stop them here and make a stand by closing the tunnel, but Risha’s people had been too fast for them.

Blood covered every one of her warriors, a sign of their many battles through the tunnels. [Mother’s Care] healed their wounds, but couldn’t do the same for Risha. Every cut that broke through her armor weighed on her.

She stepped through the doors, her spiderlings crawling over the walls and ceiling around her, and her warriors following a step behind.

A short hallway opened into a large hall, the ceiling outside of Risha’s sight. Pillars rose to that ceiling, their architecture carved long ago.

A creature of stone stood tall in the center of the hall, his arms holding the ceiling as he rested with his eyes closed. Risha nearly stepped back as she took in the spirit that suffused the Spark, but her heart was steeled for the battle ahead of her, and she would not give up now.

Below the statuesque Spark, large scaled warriors wearing the trophies of their many kills stood in a half-circle.

Risha recognized the chiefs of the clans from Mother Iris’s description of them. their eyes watching her like one might watch an annoying insect, but they did not move, instead looking to the creature at the foot of the Spark.

A single scaled warrior, not the smallest, or the largest, stood with his hands resting against the Spark. His scales were a dark blue, his limbs lithe with tightly packed muscles, and his eyes glowed with the spirit of the Spark he stood next to. At his waist, a single sword was sheathed.

Behind Risha, her people filtered in, and her spiderling climbed the walls and pillars, ready for the battle to come.

Yet they did not move.

Risha and the Blue Scaled Warrior stared at each other.

“A goblin stands in my way, ruining my plans. The irony does not escape me.” The voice that spoke was not the Blue Warrior’s, although it spoke with his mouth. A laugh followed the words, filling the hall with its joyless sound. “Tell me. How is it to lay your eyes on my form? To know that you are powerless in the face of my true might? Were the bindings of your ancestors not holding me, I would but flick my finger, and you would be no more.”

“Hwahwei scared Risha more.” Risha answered as Nyx and Igni left her body to take their physical forms.

“Ah yes, that child has grown, I imagine. She must, to face the waters.” The Blue Warrior paused and frowned.

Risha suspected the expression was his, not the Spark’s, and his expression as he spoke his next words confirmed her guess.

“I will give you the chance now, Goblin Grand Mother. Fulfill the broken oath of your ancestors. Serve me, and I will see you rise beyond the Ocean Mother, I will protect you from the wrath of those who only seek to destroy you.”

Risha laughed startling the scaled warriors in the room. She finished her laugh by showing her sharp and bloody teeth. “I will not give you the same offer, Spark. The only option for a slaver such as you, is death.”

Her words marked the end of their discussion.

The Statue opened a single eye, a black orb that swirled with power, and every scaled warrior in the room grew in spirit. Then that spirit covered the walls and floor of the room itself, blowing away any power that attempted to connect to the world.

Risha couldn’t move. Her spirits, Nyx and Igni, dispersed, returning to her, and the edges of her vision darkened. Around her, her warriors wavered, any magic they thought to call upon, gone to them.

The Blue Warrior smiled and stepped forward, then paused as a small sound filled the room.

Ba bump.

It echoed softly at first, then again, it filled the room. A heartbeat.

Ba bump. Ba bump. Ba bump.

Another Spark’s power filled the room, gathering to the Undergoblins who fought with Risha. They ignited in white fire, and the white light filled the room, pushing the power of spark out of the walls and allowing Risha to breathe once more.

“KOLIEKAN!” The roar of anger was followed by the disappearance of the Blue Warrior as he appeared in front of Risha.

Her swords met his, and the earth cracked under her feet as her bones creaked and cracked under the power of the single attack. Risha stepped through the shadows.

“YOU DARE TO CHALLENGE ME? YOU DARE TO ENTER MY DOMAIN? I WILL FIND YOUR HEART, AND I WILL CRUSH IT UNTIL YOU ARE NOTHING BUT DUST!”

Risha’s sharp smile widened with anger, as she stepped through the shadows, and appeared behind the blue warrior, twisting as her swords met his once more. Nyx and Igni flowed through her, giving her the strength to meet the Scaled’s power as their swords met once more. [Mother’s Ferocity] and [Pack Leader: Black] reinforced the attack further, and yet the Blue Scaled did not budge. Instead, he threw her swords back, and his weapons sought her heart.

She stepped once more, reappearing outside the warrior’s reach. Risha called Nyx to fill the world with her power. The spirit answered her call, and a sphere of darkness rose around Risha and the warrior, capturing them both in its void.

The Blue Warrior lifted his head to where the Spark stood outside their bubble, and closed his eyes. The Spark’s open eye focused on Risha.

Risha threw Crimson.

The Scaled Warrior flicked his sword, knocking the sword into the ground, his eyes still closed. Risha pulled herself to Crimson, and ducked under a swipe of the warrior’s sword. Scarlet stabbed towards the Scaled’s stomach.

The Spark narrowed his eye at her, and she stumbled, her attack faltering. A quick step brought her away from the Scaled’s retribution as she regained her footing.

Risha looked at the Spark, then spat a globule of blood at it as she activated [Tribal Hunter].

[Tribal Hunter]: You embody the spirit of a Hunter. You are hidden from the gaze of predators, your arrows are unerring, and once the hunt begins, no prey can escape you.

The Spark’s single eye widened as she disappeared from his sight. The Scaled warrior opened his eyes in surprise, but his eyes could not pierce Nyx’s darkness.

Risha stepped through the shadows once more, and this time Scarlet pierced the Scaled’s chest, drawing blood.

The wound started to heal around the sword, and Risha’s eyes narrowed.

He swept at her, and she let go of Scarlet’s handle to wrap the Scaled’s arm in Scarlet’s chain.

Igni ignited through the chain, and the Scaled screamed in pain. Risha stabbed him again with Crimson, as he tried to twist towards her. Then Crimson’s chain too, wrapped around him as he fell, still screaming.

Igni burned both chains, cooking the Scaled as the creature tried to heal through the damage.

Risha kicked him onto his back, then stomped the Scaled’s chest and pulled the chains, pushing the swords further into him as the fire burned.

He screamed.

Risha did not care.

The power of the spark burned away as the Scaled used it to heal, and Risha watched for a second before she pulled a dagger from her thigh.

She cut his neck, only for it to heal in a second. She punctured his eye, and only then did he quiet.

Igni’s fire consumed him then, the healing slowing until it stopped altogether.

She pulled the swords from the body, and Nyx’s sphere of darkness dropped to reveal a battle that continued around her.

She stepped past the battle to appear in front of the statuesque spark. A throw of her sword pierced the stone of its leg, and she pulled herself to the sword before throwing the other one to slowly climb the Spark.

Soon enough, she appeared in front of the eye, and once again, the Spark could see her.

It only had a second of her sharp and bloody smile before Crimson stabbed deep. Scarlet followed a second later, covering Risha in gore.

The Spark screamed in pain, its power leaving its people, as its spirit flickered.

Risha called Nyx to her, then channeled the power of both her spirits into the eye. They traveled through the Spark, stabbing and burning it from the inside.

It died an inglorious death.

Risha watched the spirit of the Spark leave its body of stone, escaping to the Spirit Realm.

Nyx called for Risha to Step once more, and Risha did.

Risha reappeared in the spirit realm, the Spark’s spirit running through the void of stars.

Nyx left Risha, her body expanding to eclipse the escaping Spark, as the moon on her forehead glowed with power and she grew larger than the stars themselves. Her inescapable maw collapsed on the spirit of the Spark, unavoidable, as Nyx’s fur sparkled with the light of a galaxy.

Her maw closed over the Spark’s Spirit, and it disappeared to Risha’s eyes [Tribal Hunter] no longer sensing her prey.

Nyx swallowed, and her eyes turned to Risha, larger even than Hwahwei. They watched her for a second before Nyx started to shrink into a small black cat.

Nyx hopped onto Risha’s shoulder. “Return, Risha. It is not safe to have your physical form here.”

Risha could feel the attention of things in the void, and did not doubt the cat’s words.

With another step, she returned to the physical world, her swords still in the dead Spark’s eyes.

Below her, the sounds of fighting stopped as the powerless Scaled surrendered to Risha’s people.

Risha withdrew her swords from the motionless statue of the Spark, revealing an eye that had turned to black crystal.

Risha stepped through the shadows, reappearing on the ground. [Mother’s Care] quickly spread from her, covering the injured with her power. Her eyes found the dead. Undergoblins, Kobolds, a demon, an orc.

Her care did not reach them anymore.

Risha found Klaz’zks alive in her spider form, bloodied, and with two legs missing. Still, the spider met her eyes with the fervor of victory.

A few feet away from Klaz’zks, Lolo stood over the body of another Undergoblin, crying silent tears.

Scaled lay dead, or wrapped in webs with lowered heads, and pale scales.

Risha walked past Klaz’zks, her eyes hard as she held back her anger and grief, “Gather the prisoners, and the injured. I’m returning to the surface.”

“Yes, Grand Mother.”

Her spiderlings streamed back to her as she walked past the statue to a set of stairs that led back up to the surface.

She walked them with swords in hand, bloodied, and angry.
