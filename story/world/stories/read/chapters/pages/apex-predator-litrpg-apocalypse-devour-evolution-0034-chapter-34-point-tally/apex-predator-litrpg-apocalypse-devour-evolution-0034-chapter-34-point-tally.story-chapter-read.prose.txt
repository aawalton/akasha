[New Evolution Acquired: Reactive Camouflage (F-grade)]

[Reactive Camouflage (F-grade)]

Your skin, musculature, and micro-hairs adapt to reflect ambient light and blend with surrounding textures. While not true invisibility, this adaptation greatly reduces visual detection, especially when stationary or moving slowly.

+2 Agility

+2 Mental

[Active]: Your skin adapts to environmental hues, shadows, and contrast levels. Reduces enemy detection range while in cover or low-visibility environments. Enhances synergy with [Stealth]. Costs Energy to maintain.

Scales With: Agility, Mental, [Stealth]

Don grinned happily. It was just what he had been hoping for. The acid spit would have been a nice addition, but he already had ranged options with his Telekinesis. This enhanced his stealthy ambush style and allowed him to enter dangerous areas or overall avoid fights he didn’t want to take with much more efficiency.

He wondered if the lizards would ever spawn or if he could find them anywhere else. Having the acid wouldn’t be a bad option. But maybe he didn’t need it so badly right now.

Yeah… Time’s better spent on killing monsters where there’s lots. Time to go back to the Grimoid village.

He really wanted to test his new ability, but other than getting proficient at turning it on and off, there wasn’t much to do. No enemies to detect him meant no way to explore the limits of his camouflage ability.

However, before he moved on, it was absolutely time for some evolution upgrades. He checked his evolution interface. He had 1933 Evolution Points. The lizard massacre had netted him around fifteen hundred points.

An absolutely massive haul. The Devouring Physique had done some serious work. His Psychic abilities made it relatively simple to pin down weaker monsters and Devour them alive, giving him a significant point boost. Of course his overall growth in strength had enabled this grind to be fast and efficient. Everything was boiling together into a beautifully optimized build.

Now the question is, how do I optimize further.

Don was actually not sure what evolutions he should pick here. As he squeezed a full waterskin’s worth of water in his mouth and refilled it in the creek, he thought. He washed the lizard gunk off his body, but felt kind of icky putting on the tattered rags that used to be his clothes.

I hope I get some equipment soon.

In Don’s mind the top contenders were Simian Physique and Retractable Claws. Those were his meat and potatoes, bread and butter, his… Pizza and cheese?

Maybe it wasn’t time to come up with new food metaphors, but he could really use a pizza. Not because he was hungry, but for the sheer pleasure.

But yeah, those two evolutions did most of the heavy lifting. He could upgrade both of them and still have a good chunk leftover.

There was also the case for upgrading Devouring Physique even further. It would cost 1000 points, but the upgrade would pay itself back. It was a reasonable milestone to push it to E-grade, since the next upgrade would probably cost something north of three thousand.

He could of course also upgrade the psychic ability he had gotten from his racial evolution. Telekinesis had definitely been useful as an auxiliary power even with his brawler style.

Upgrading it would help pin monsters down, thus synergizing with the Devour-upgrade.

Damn… Do I take brute power in Simian Physique and Retractable Claws, or the synergy between Telekinesis and Devour?

He checked the cost of upgrading all of the options to get a clear picture.

Devouring Physique (E-grade): 1000 Evolution Points

Psychic Sense (F-grade): 150 Points

Retractable Claws (D-grade): 600 Evolution Points

Simian Physique (E-grade): 300 Evolution Points

“Huh…” Don muttered. “I can actually almost afford them all.”

He could take all of the upgrades except for Psychic Sense immediately. And then just kill a few things to upgrade it later.

Of course the Psychic Sense was at F-grade, and thus much less powerful than the other options. Don was surprised by the 150 point price tag. It implied that the ability was more powerful than Simian Physique, which had cost 100 points to upgrade initially.

The next upgrade for Psychic Sense will cost something like 300-400 points. I’ll gain that fairly rapidly.

It would just be too satisfying to spend 1900 points for all the upgrades he wanted. Once he hunt more prey he would focus on the psionics, but they were, as he had concluded, auxiliary for now. He needed raw power from the claws and physique augments.

[600 Evolution Points spent]

[Evolution Upgraded!]

[Retractable Claws (D-grade)] -> [Retractable Claws (C-grade)]

[Retractable Claws (C-grade): You can shift your fingernails into metal-like claws at will. The claws are 3 inches long, half an inch thick and have the qualities of titanium. +4 to Agility, +8 to Power]

The power boosts were definitely adding up. Don felt stronger and faster almost immediately. He flicked out his claws. They were still black and gleaming, but ever so slightly longer. He knew the cutting power had increased, as the property had been changed from steel to titanium.

[300 Evolution Points spent]

[Evolution Upgraded!]

[Simian Physique (E-grade)] -> [Simian Physique (D-grade)]

[Simian Physique (D-grade)]:

+6 Power

+3 Agility

[Passive]: Further Increased grip strength, explosive movement potential, and blunt force resilience. Scales With: Power, Agility [Unarmed Fighting].

Again he felt the muscles wrap tighter against his already skinny frame. He no longer seemed to shrink. Instead, it felt like his bones got dense and even slightly bigger. His size and appearance remained mostly the same, but he could feel he was a lot stronger and faster and more agile now.

[1000 Evolution Points spent]

[Evolution Upgraded!]

[Devouring Physique (E-grade)] -> [Devouring Physique (D-grade)]

Devouring Physique (D-grade)]

Your digestive system processes consumed life force even more efficiently. You extract more essence from enemies you Devour. +9 Constitution +3 Power [Passive]: Slight increase in Evolution Point yield per Devour. Slight increase in chance to gain evolution traits from consumed foes. Slight Increases in Health and Energy from using Devour. Scales With: Constitution, [Devour]

Other than a slight shift in Don’s stomach, he didn’t feel any different. But the stat increase was massive, especially with Constitution. Not only was his body more adapted now because of the evolutions, the raw Attribute increases had given him a significant power boost. He had gained 9 points in Power, 3 in Agility, and 6 in Constitution. That made a difference. His Power had gained a solid 20% boost. He couldn’t wait to see how that would translate into fighting prowess.

“Alright… time to continue the hunt.”

Don made his way out of the grove back to the tunnel. After a short while he arrived between the two fiery braziers. The swamp still looked the same. He would have loved to see how his camouflage worked against Morshoff, but he knew it hunted like a spider, sensing tremors with its tentacles.

Leveraging his improved physique, his Sprinting skill and his knowledge of where he could take a step and where he couldn’t in the swampy area, he quickly made it back to the platform safely. Some tentacles almost reached him, but Don used Telekinesis to misdirect them, and jumped on the platform scot free.

He moved swiftly from platform to platform, checking around if there were any Grimoids hiding. He didn’t see any. He moved up a floor, then another, and the drums got louder and louder. They were coming from some upper platform.

I have a feeling there’s going to be a party upstairs. One that I’m about to crash.
