Down below in the suffocating darkness of the sinkhole, the situation was rapidly deteriorating into a total, bloody catastrophe.

The sheer bodily mass of the undead horde was a physical weight pressing against the fighters. Every time they swung their weapons, they had to drag the steel through a thick, tangled wall of rotting meat and rusted iron. Josh was bleeding from a deep, jagged gash across his left cheek. A rusted spear tip had managed to glance past his visor during a chaotic surge, the foul metal burning like acid against his skin. His sword arm felt as though it were made of solid, burning lead. The continuous, jarring impacts of striking bone and deflecting heavy iron were taking a massive toll on his endurance.

He slammed his heavy steel shield into the exposed ribcage of a charging skeletal warrior. The impact shattered the brittle bones, physically throwing the creature back into the densely packed horde, but two more ghouls instantly scrambled over the falling skeleton to take its place.

"Where is the covering fire?" the exhausted tank of the Iron Gauntlet party yelled over the deafening, clicking noise of the swarm.

The massive man was visibly shaking. His towering rectangular shield was severely bent out of shape, the metal groaning under the relentless pressure. A thick layer of freezing black mud coated his legs, anchoring him painfully to the spot. "The ridge has gone completely quiet! Did they abandon us? Did the horde overrun them?"

"They did not abandon us!" Josh roared back, refusing to let the creeping despair take hold of their fragile defensive pocket. He stepped forward, sweeping his electrified blade in a wide arc to keep the snapping jaws of a corrupted hound at bay. "Keep your shield locked! Trust them! They must be repositioning!"

Suddenly, the dense, swirling fog of the horde parted unnaturally. A massive, horrific shadow loomed over the defensive circle, blocking out what little ambient light filtered down from the canopy.

A Bone Goliath pushed its way brutally to the very front of the swarm. It was easily twelve feet tall, constructed entirely from hundreds of fused human skeletons. It did not merely step over the smaller ghouls and skeletal hounds blocking its path. It actively crushed them beneath its massive, iron-plated feet, treating its own lesser troops as nothing more than crunchy terrain. It carried a massive, rusted iron club that looked like a salvaged structural support beam from a ruined keep. The weapon scraped heavily against the limestone walls of the gorge, dislodging showers of loose rock and freezing slime.

The monster looked down at the tiny pocket of living fighters with glowing, malevolent green eyes. A swirling vortex of concentrated necrotic energy burned within its hollow, cavernous chest cavity.

"Brace!" Josh screamed, his voice cracking violently.

The Goliath raised its massive, rusted weapon high into the freezing air. It did not aim for the injured Iron Gauntlet members. It locked its glowing gaze entirely on Josh.

Josh quickly bent his knees, driving his steel boots as deep into the sludge as possible. He did not have the spatial leverage to dodge the strike. He brought his deeply gouged shield up, angling it slightly to deflect the worst of the kinetic energy, and desperately attempted to trigger his Titan's Anchor skill.

He was a fraction of a second too slow.

Before the high-voltage blue dome could fully materialise around him, the massive iron club crashed down.

The impact was devastating. It was like being struck by a runaway siege engine. The force completely bypassed Josh's physical strength. The blow drove him brutally to his knees, forcing his boots through the layer of thick mud and cracking the solid limestone bedrock beneath them. His left arm went entirely numb, a terrifying, sharp popping sound echoing from his shoulder joint. He tasted fresh, hot blood filling his mouth as he bit completely through his own lip.

The Goliath let out a hollow, rattling roar of triumph, raising the massive iron club for a second executing strike that would undoubtedly shatter Josh's guard and crush him into the mud.

Before the monster could bring the weapon down, a brilliant, blinding flash of white-hot plasma struck the Bone Goliath directly in the side of its fused skull.

The elemental heat was so intense it instantly melted the crude, rusted iron plating bolted to the creature's head. The massive skull shattered explosively under the terrifying thermal shock. The kinetic force of the plasma bolt sent the twelve-foot monster stumbling wildly sideways. Its massive, flailing arms crashed into the surrounding horde, completely disrupting its devastating attack and crushing a dozen lesser skeletons beneath its weight.

Josh gasped for air, spitting a mouthful of crimson onto the freezing mud as he forced himself painfully back up from his knees. He looked up through the swirling smoke and falling ash.

Directly above them, standing on the very edge of the thirty-foot vertical drop, was Brett.

The mage was flanked by the entire ranged squad. They had successfully navigated the treacherous, slippery rim of the sinkhole while fighting a desperate running battle, and were now positioned perfectly over the trapped party.

"Hold your ground, Josh!" Brett’s voice boomed down from the darkness. He had magically magnified his vocal cords, the sound carrying easily over the deafening din of the undead swarm. "We are shaping the stone! Brace for the collapse! Do not move from the wall!"

Josh did not hesitate or question the impossible engineering of the command.

"Shields up!" Josh bellowed, his voice raw and bleeding. He ignored the screaming agony in his left shoulder, gripping his shield handle with a white-knuckled intensity. "Press hard against the back wall! Interlock your edges! Cover your heads!"

The fighters and the surviving Iron Gauntlet members instantly tightened their formation. They crouched incredibly low in the freezing sludge, retreating until their steel backplates scraped against the sheer limestone. They raised their shields flat above their heads, overlapping the rims to create a solid, slanted roof of metal against the rock face. They became a hardened steel turtle shell, waiting blindly in the dark.

On the ridge thirty feet above, Kaelen the dwarven Stoneweaver and the slender human geomancer stepped to the very edge of the treacherous drop.

They did not use delicate, intricate wand movements or softly spoken incantations. Geomancy on this massive, geographical scale required brute, uncompromising physical exertion.

Kaelen roared a harsh dwarven battle cry and slammed the heavy base of her granite staff directly into the corrupted earth. The human mage fell to his knees beside her, pressing both of his glowing iron gauntlets firmly against the freezing, foul-smelling mud.

"Push it deep!" Kaelen grunted, her deeply lined face turning bright purple with the immense, straining effort of the magical channelling. Sweat poured freely down her brow despite the freezing air. "The dark mana is actively resisting the shift! It has rooted deeply into the stone! We have to crack the bedrock beneath the corruption!"

The two mages poured a terrifying amount of raw, elemental magical energy directly into the earth. The ground surrounding the massive sinkhole began to vibrate violently. The tremors started relatively small, rattling the loose stones on the ridge and causing the dead trees to sway erratically, but they quickly escalated into a deep, deafening, subterranean roar.

Down in the pit, Josh felt the vibrations travelling through the freezing mud and straight up his boots. It felt as though the entire world was tearing itself apart. The sheer limestone wall pressing against his back began to groan and pop in a terrifying, unnatural rhythm, small showers of loose gravel raining down onto their raised shields.

The undead horde surrounding them faltered. The ground was shaking so violently that the skeletal warriors and rotting hounds could no longer maintain their footing in the slick mud. The constant, crushing pressure against the defensive circle momentarily eased as the monsters stumbled and fell into the sludge.

"Break it!" the human geomancer screamed from the ridge above, his glowing green gauntlets trembling wildly with absolute exhaustion. Bright red blood began to trickle steadily from his nose, a clear sign of severe mana depletion.

With a final, explosive surge of terrestrial magic, the two geomancers completely shattered the structural integrity of the cliff face directly below their boots.

A massive, thirty-foot section of the sheer limestone wall violently sheared away from the main rock.

The deafening sound of cracking stone echoed like a thunderclap across the entire gorge, completely drowning out the shrieks of the undead. Hundreds of tons of solid rock, freezing mud, and tangled, dead tree roots collapsed outward and downward in a terrifying avalanche of geological destruction.

The angle of the spell was absolute perfection. The falling debris missed the fighters pressed tightly against the concave base of the wall. Instead, the avalanche slammed with catastrophic, unstoppable force directly into the front ranks of the undead horde.

The impact was apocalyptic. Dozens of skeletal hounds, rotting orcs, and heavily armoured ghouls were instantly crushed flat beneath the massive, falling boulders. The sheer weight of the collapsing wall buried them deep beneath the freezing earth, entirely removing them from the battlefield in a single, deafening heartbeat.

A thick, choking cloud of pulverized rock dust and foul-smelling mud exploded outward, completely obscuring the bottom of the pit in absolute darkness.

Josh squeezed his eyes shut beneath his visor, coughing violently as the thick dust coated the inside of his helmet. He kept his shield locked high, waiting for the rumbling echoes of the rockfall to fade.

When the dust and thick mud finally settled enough to see, the geography of the sinkhole had been completely and fundamentally altered.

The impossible, vertical thirty-foot drop that had trapped them was gone. In its place was a steep, chaotic, thirty-foot ramp of jagged limestone rubble, loose dirt, and shattered tree roots. The slope led directly from the freezing mud at the bottom of the pit up to the safety of the forested ridge above.

It was not a clean, paved escape route. It was incredibly steep and littered with dangerous, unstable boulders, but it was a viable path. They were no longer trapped in a vertical tomb.

However, the undead horde reacted almost instantly to the changing terrain.

They did not experience fear or confusion at the sudden geological shift that had just annihilated their front ranks. The massive swarm simply surged forward, their thousands of glowing green eyes immediately locking onto the newly formed ramp. The hive-mind commanding them recognised the exit route. The horde began scrambling wildly over the massive boulders, desperate to secure the high ground and trap their prey at the bottom once more.

"Not today!" Brett roared from the top of the ridge, his voice cutting cleanly through the settling dust.

The mage stepped confidently past the exhausted, gasping geomancers, planting his boots right onto the very lip of the newly formed, highly unstable slope. He pushed his imaginary spectacles up his nose, his pale face set in a mask of absolute, cold fury. He activated his Aura of the Hearth-Forge, completely surrounding himself in a rippling wave of intense, dry heat that pushed the freezing fog back.

He unleashed absolutely everything he had left in his vast mana reserves. He opened his hands wide, fingers splayed, and sent a massive, continuous, roaring torrent of white-hot living plasma cascading directly down the steep rubble ramp.

It looked exactly like a waterfall of pure, liquid fire.

The magical flames wrapped around the jagged limestone boulders, aggressively seeking out the corrupted flesh and brittle bone of the climbing undead. The heat was absolutely staggering, radiating heavily even down into the pit where Josh stood.

The freezing, necrotic mud coating the slope instantly flash-boiled upon contact with the plasma. It created a massive, suffocating cloud of foul-smelling steam that completely obscured the ramp from view. The front ranks of the charging horde were incinerated in a matter of seconds. The lower-tier dungeon spawn vapourised instantly into shimmering golden dust, while the corrupted native corpses were reduced to blackened, smoking ash that blew wildly in the thermal updrafts.

The living fire completely sterilised the newly formed exit path. It created an impenetrable, roaring wall of heat that forced the endless, writhing swarm to momentarily fall back, their primal instincts warring with the sheer, destructive power of the flames.

Brett did not stop casting. He knew the horde would simply wait for his mana to run dry before surging forward again.

He took a bold, incredibly dangerous step forward, walking partway down the steep, burning slope. His heavy cloak billowed wildly in the intense thermal updraft, threatening to catch alight. His dark red drake-scale gloves glowed brightly, protecting his hands from the worst of the searing heat as he channelled the massive spell. He looked like an avenging god of fire descending into the underworld.

He peered through the thick, swirling steam and the roaring, white-hot flames, locating the heavy steel shields of the rescue party waiting at the bottom of the ramp.

"Move!" Brett screamed, his voice raw and cracking painfully with extreme magical exhaustion. He maintained the massive torrent of fire, sweeping his hands left and right to keep the outer flanks of the rubble ramp completely clear of the encroaching horde. "The path is open! Get your boots on the stone and climb! I cannot hold this volume of fire forever!"

Josh lowered his heavy shield, squinting through the blinding, brilliant glare of the magical flames. The intense heat washing over his face was actually a profound relief, completely burning away the freezing, bone-deep chill of the necrotic mud. He looked at the steep, jagged slope leading to safety, then turned back to the exhausted survivors.

"You heard the man!" Josh bellowed, his voice ringing with renewed, desperate energy.

He turned to the exhausted Iron Gauntlet party and the surviving members of the rescue squad. He pointed his electrified longsword directly up the burning, steaming ramp of rubble.

"Break the circle!" Josh commanded, moving to the front of the line to protect the injured. "Move up the rubble! Help the wounded! Do not stop for anything until you reach the treeline!"

The brutal, grinding stalemate was finally broken. The claustrophobic trap of the sinkhole had been shattered by brute magical force. They had an exit, but the hardest, most dangerous part of the rescue mission was just beginning. They had to transition from a static, heavily armoured defence into a fighting, vertical retreat. They had to fight their way up a steep, unstable slope of jagged rock while physically carrying their wounded, with an endless army of the dead snapping viciously at their heels.
