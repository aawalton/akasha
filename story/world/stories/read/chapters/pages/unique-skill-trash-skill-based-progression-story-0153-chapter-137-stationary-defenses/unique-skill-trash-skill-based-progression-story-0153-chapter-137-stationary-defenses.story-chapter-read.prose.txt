Chapter 137

Stationary Defenses

It is said that fools jump in blindly, before taking in the totality of the environment around them. After beginning my movement towards the raised chain railing, in an effort to climb over it, and jump down from raised spiral railing to the next lower set of railings, that is when I realized I had acted before taking in the totality of my circumstances, making me the noted fool in this whole operation.

I should have noted that something was already in the works, as everything was a bit too smooth. The banner guards who carried our national flags so easily, then worked the inner riggings of the spire. Moving gears and levers, causing the spire come to life as we walked our way to the top, only to then bring up waist high pillars that supported a chain rope that could be used to help us balance ourselves while we waited for the ceremony to continue. Everything spoke of having plans within plans. Even the patches worn by the flag bearers caused a warning bell to rise up in my mind, as I had seen the symbols somewhere before, but that wasn’t the only oddity that caught my eyes as I began to move.

For as soon as I rushed to the chain railing, other healers down below, those that had just gotten to the wooden spiral structure began to panic. Some of the healers even looked like they were about to break ranks, ready to rush out like startled horses. That is when the Royal Order of Watchers, the wearers of the odd patch that finally clicked in my mind, came to life. Turning this ceremony that had up until this point been performed completely in silence was now switched on its head.

“Hold!” Chief Royal Order of Watcher, Aiden Lightken shouted, his voice oozing with authority. Only after hearing his familiar tone and seeing the use of his now familiar social Skill did my mind instantly identify the Chief. I watched as brown energy filled his words, words that my mind automatically fought to resist. The reason I could of course see the energy in his words was due to my unique seal that I recently acquired, my True Sight of Enigmas seal*. A seal that formed organically based on what I needed, and what I was doing while I had the seal slot open and available. My mind somehow used the open slot to convert it into this seal. A seal that is both always active, something that Mr. Yi would call a crutch in that I didn’t improve myself by its use.

The command rolled over the other healers who had been close to panicking and breaking formation. Only for their bodies to suddenly lock up. Muscles and hidden shapes covered in black cloths caught in rigid poses of fear, showcasing just how powerful the suggestion Skill being used was. The wave of energy rolled over the others, locking them into place, causing an odd macabre statue like appearance to take shape below. I even watched as the rolling mist of power barreled towards me, coating me in a dense layer of energy. That is when my mind rebelled, at its very core I could not stand the idea of being forced into submission by anyone. That is probably why my mind rebelled so violently at the blatant use of a mind-altering power.

Feeling that there was no other way out, my mind moved on instincts, following my initial plan for escaping and dedicating itself to this course of action despite any other factors. Jumping from the chain rope down, I found my body gliding forward, leaping over the gaggle of healers who were locked into place.

My jumping from chain to chain was somehow easier than expected. My body moving with fluidity and grace that I had not expected, though I felt my high Dexterity Attribute, along with several of my Skills working to help make this type of movement possible at all. Yet, it was only once I got to the second to last railing that all the elements of the battle unfolding around me became clear to my mind.

It was only now that I was near the end of my movement that my mind even began to realize why all the healers were forced on this rising spiral. A metal and wooden structure that should normally make an overly efficient death trap for just such an attack. Especially as we would all be captive audiences, just waiting to be ambushed. Apparently, those who came before me had held similar thoughts and subsequently prepared for just such contingencies. For only now that I was lower did, I see the reactions of the Royal Order of Watchers. While I wasn’t exactly certain what it took to be a member of the Royal order of Watchers, it was apparent that having an affinity for Holy imbued Skills would be a bonus in recruitment, for all of the Watchers that had been listless and part of the background of the ceremony to this point came to life and began grabbing the metal chains that they had pulled up and locked into place moments ago, and began channeling holy energy into the chains. The very chains that I had leaped from a moment ago, and the same set of chains that I found myself perching on for just a moment.

As the Watchers poured their energy into the chains, an immediate and drastic change occurred, for the chains began to glow and simmer with an intense burning golden light. Even as my momentum kept me rocking forward. I felt my mind had two options, either continue forward or fall backwards into the gaggle of paralyzed healers that I had just leapt over. In the end, momentum won out, and I found myself continuing forward, jumping over the frozen healers. While the looming dark streaks of bubbling shadows barreling forward could be seen, charging forward and slamming into a suddenly visible wall of golden protection.

Dry popping sounds erupt, as three Death Eaters are forced from their protective shadows and forced to appear above ground, as their protective dark cloaks seem to burn. Golden flames fueled by holy magic burn on the protective coating, revealing bones and burning red hearts. Hearts that beat with a deep rhythmic pattern from below their cloaks.

Seeing their attacks thwarted, I am again reminded of how foolish I had been to charge head long into battle. At the time of my decision, I felt it would be better to charge forward and fight on my own terms, only to now realize that I had broken free of the layer of protection that had been created for us. Still, I was already committed to my course of action. While the other healers might be content to hide behind barriers, I still felt a need to charge forward, to stop the threat before us. To stop the monsters that threatened all the innocent civilians around us. Perhaps it was because this was my home, a place filled with people and faces that I recognized from my time here. Fellow students and their families, shop owners who would nod at me during my morning runs, people who glared at me for my heritage and bloodline, they were all here and all part of the group that was now vulnerable to attacks from Death Eaters. Seeing the victims, I knew I couldn’t stop, my conscience wouldn’t’ let me. That is why I jumped one more time, down to the last line of glowing chains, and one final jump propelled me over the line darting towards the momentarily stunned gaggle of Death Eaters.

Coated in holy flames, the Death Eaters were just as stunned as the healers were, their bodies and minds seeming to take a second to realize that their protective coating had been damaged, that their bones were now exposed to the light of the noonday sun, and that the sun’s natural rays hurt.

I moved forward, ready to attack, only to feel slightly naked without my sword by my side. Though the sword was just an extension of me, as I didn’t need a weapon. All of my training to this point had proven that with enough force and focus, my body would be a lethal enough weapon. My high Attributes aided by my Skills were enough, then you factor into the equation that I could feel my mind activating my Seals and I knew that I was trained for this very act, to be a perfect killing machine. Well, a perfect machine against the undead. Against the living I was probably a terrible matchup.

Charging forward, I felt a burning sensation growing within my chest, radiating out to my arms and feet. Each step forward felt like it was powered by full holy energy. Mentally I understood the process, it was a physical reaction to my mind telling my body to prepare. Just as you might inadvertently take a larger breath of air before a major physical feat, so too was my body producing and swirling about holy energy through my body. A tempest of force and power surged up within me, starting at my core and working its way out to my extremities.

Fueled by a primal desire to fight and act, rather than react, I charged forward ready to strike.

(Aiden Lightken)

The entire ceremony was going tits up, as error after error in the defensive formations were being exploited. This was expected in such a backwards empire, which is why the Royal Order of Watchers volunteered to provide the primary protection for the healers, and even assembled the protective spire. A massive metal structure that costs invaluable resources, and time to assemble. While the time will not be able to recover, the cost for the resources would now be at the cost of the Empire. An Empire who apparently had no process for screening people wishing to attend the ceremony.

To make matters worse, since it was apparently an open range, the gathered masses were now being subjected to attacks by Death Eaters, a process that those on trial were now being judged for. While it was not the Royal Order of Watchers’ prerogative to tell nations how to operate within their borders, they were more than willing to offer aide in the form of resources and training, provided the Empire simply ask. Yet, they never did, and that is why there were always incidents like this.

While the Royal Order of Watchers could and should likely go to help the gathered crowd members, their primary and so far only concern was the protection of the healers. That is why Aiden felt the need to use his Commanding Skills against the healers. Many of whom were about to break formation, and expose themselves to possible attacks by Death Eaters, a loss that the Royal Order of Watchers could not allow to happen at any cost.

For the Royal Order of Watchers had one thing that guaranteed their safety against the Death Eaters, namely that all who were gathered for this mission and all who were recruited to the Order all had Seals awakened in their bodies. Everyone from the lowliest grunt, to the supreme leaders of the Order all had Seals placed within them. Seals that prevented Death Eaters from entering their shadows and infecting their spirits with their withering rot. Rot that would carve out a hole within their bodies and ensure corruption once they finally passed away, creating even more Death Eaters who would have to be put down.

The reason the members of the Order were able to get Seals of course is that they all also had at least one Healing Skill to help prevent the damage. Not enough ability to be a true healer and protector, but enough to protect those who were worthy of their respect.

That is why the Order existed at all, for they were the chosen protectors, those valiant few who were immune to the dark corruption of Death Eaters and were therefore able to stand toe-to-toe with the vile monsters. Above all else, they were created with the express purpose of protecting those blessed with the healing arts, those who were sacred enough to be able to provide life in an otherwise dying world.

For a moment, Aiden thought he had the threat contained. With his fellow Order members standing guard and powering the protective spiral, the healers were safe from immediate threats. At least, that is what Aiden thought.

It wasn’t until he saw one healer jumping down gracefully from the highest spire, the spire dedicated to the local heroes that Aiden felt a chill run down his spine as he wondered just what was happening. For a moment, he thought about using his Command Skill again, but realized that using such a Skill twice in such short succession would have a deeper effect on the younger healers who were not mentally resilient to such actions. That and by the time he felt he could target the leaping healer, she was already out of the protection of the spire, meaning that if he forced the healer to stop moving now, she would be vulnerable to the attacks of not one, but three Death Eaters.

Realizing it was too late to stop the foolish healer, Aiden chose to charge forward, only to be amazed by what he saw next.

The healer landed on the ground and seemed to produce golden bolts of electrical current that spread out from her feet, leaving glowing imprints of her passing. Then the craziest event of all happened as she charged forward, trying to reach the nearest Death Eater. Without even having a weapon in hand, the foolish healer charged forward, a recipe for disaster. A defenseless healer against not one, but three stunned, but still deadly Death Eaters.

Seeing the image before him, anyone would think that the healer would be a goner, but he was amazed by what he saw next. Stepping forward, the healer charged the closest of the stunned Death Eaters, her glowing feet stepping onto the shadowy death cloak that protects all Death Eaters. She stepped on the cloak, causing a torrent of violent golden flames to erupt upwards from the ground, and ignite the cloak immediately. Then he saw something he never thought he would see, a complete look of horror filling the boney face of the revealed Death Eater’s skull. Horror that soon proved to be justified by what happened next.
