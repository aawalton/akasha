Before even checking the changes to [Assurance], she could feel it — it had massively expanded its breadth of targets. Her realm was kinda reacting in amazement to it already.

Assurance

Power (Divine; Aura; Light, Fate, Magic, Abjurer, ‘Major Battle Victory’ Synergy). Your simple existence gives your Followers and allies powerful, supernatural faith in victory, and particularly in resisting the enemy.

This affects all of your Followers at all times, and any other allies within a radius of 7 meters per deity level, centered on you. Add +1 Effective Levels to any Active Defenses, +2 Effective Levels to resist Knockback or any Stunning, Unconsciousness, Knockdown, or Pain effect, and reduce any Wounding effects by -2 Effective Levels. These bonuses do not apply to you.

For Followers who are also in the radius as above, improve the EL effects above to +2/+3/-3.

Ah! Feels good. A continuous buff to pretty much everyone. I’m now such a… Key Icon! Tee hee.

Samantha reached out to Azure through their bond to gush about all the new and shiny accomplishments, but found her out cold, asleep. When she checked on her specific location, she frowned.

St. Deikmorn? We don’t even have a place there.

But she suddenly recalled that Azure had been exploring the catacombs under the ruins of Matrazere, looking for the inevitable ‘spot’ that was there, though it was one of the more extensive enterprises she’d gone on in that regard. The catacombs there were like a city unto themselves, and the spot felt more buried and elusive to Azure than usual.

Shit! I forgot about it. This was the second day of that exploration, that’s how extensive it is. I couldn’t spare that amount of time in escort, either.

The location was currently a bit of a pain to go to, so Samantha first pinged Jeeves & Company to direct her to who was keeping an eye on her. Servitors, naturally. Normally, Raphael would be the one, but he was busy these days in a realm that needed medical expertise.

The Angel, Camael, responded to her soon thereafter. He was obviously nervous, even fearful, which wasn’t a good sign. “I’m sorry, Your Majesty. She wanted to disconnect from things as much as possible. I didn’t want to betray her wishes.”

Feeling a growing sense of annoyance, Samantha asked with careful calmness, “What did she do?”

“She sought out strong drink suitable for her unusual constitution,” Camael reported miserably. “She found it in mixed Daenriar and Naugite company last night. Enchanted wine. At first, she purchased it and drank alone, but was drawn back by bardic performance. Singing and dancing.”

“I see.”

Camael’s nervousness only grew. “Sh-she passed out drunk at some point. She was warned repeatedly, Your Majesty! W-we had a tent and a bed made on the spot, of course! She is sound asleep now.”

Something happened in those catacombs. Azure wouldn’t go to such extremes unless something quite awful had occurred, probably in a vision. “Mmn. Thank you, Camael. Keep watch, you and whoever. I’m going to need to- wait. Wasn’t she supposed to be with Six as her bodyguard? Where is she?”

“I have no idea, Your Majesty. Six protected her within the catacombs, but they separated when exiting.”

Samantha’s eyes were closed, but she rolled them all the same. Idly, she pulled out her moistened handkerchief and wiped at her eyes and cheeks, removing blood as she did umpteen times in a day.

Not bothering to say anything more to Camael, Samantha shifted to make mental contact with Six Moons Falling. Abruptly, she demanded, “Why the hell aren’t you still protecting my betrothed while she remains vulnerable?”

Six was quick to reply flippantly, “Respect.”

“Excuse me?”

“Respect for certain stated intentions that were thankfully rather timely. To be short, you wouldn’t want me around her in the state I knew she’d be in.”

“Why, because you’d end up taking advantage of it?”

“No one is immune to temptation, Goddess. To remove oneself entirely avoids even the subtler lapses. We’re now entirely ignorant of what might’ve occurred, and ignorance is bliss.”

“You really think there’s temptation there? Does that include you?”

“If you must know? Yes. I am certainly tempted toward sexual relations with two of the most desirable and sensual beings to ever exist. Meanwhile, I think I’m quite catching myself.”

Samantha shook her head incredulously. “This late hour of existence, something you’re all too informed on, and you think some ridiculous erotic triangle, guaranteed to stir up drama, is advisable?”

“I think there’s no better time than when time is short.” Her tone became teasing, as a serpentine smile translated across. “I’m game if you are. Doesn’t danger call you, Your Majesty?”

Samantha both scoffed and laughed at the same time. It had actually been a hot minute since someone was so bold with her. “Stick to my betrothed like glue the next time I assign you to her protection — don’t just fucking leave her when you feel like it. And don’t have sex with her, either. Those are both orders. Understood? Or am I not your superior?”

There was a brief pause of consideration before Six replied. “Hmm. You are, yes. Understood, Goddess — I’ll do as you say. Does ‘no sex’ include kissing, by the way?”

Samantha disconnected even as she facepalmed in annoyance. Next time, I’ll arrange for it to be Gelmak. He’s a thousand times less shady.

Even as she told herself this, though, she knew she wouldn’t. While she trusted Gelmak like she trusted the sun to rise — understanding the rigidity of minotaur honor — he… wasn’t too bright from a social perspective. It had liabilities, especially when it came to being the bodyguard to someone as dodgy and persuasive as Azure. She tended to wrap Servitors around her finger, whatever the hell Samantha told them, for instance. She’d been doing that from the beginning.

Six would be a perfect counter if she’d stop being so damned horny. And Samantha was at least mostly confident she wouldn’t consciously disobey direct orders when it felt as if her word had been given. It was just going to be a… process. Hopefully.

Samantha sent word for a mirror walk chain to be readied, as she shifted to the Bridge of Edges and arrived at the Eleks Obelisk east of Rivermount and the ruins of Matrazere. She zipped through mirrors as fast as she could until she arrived in St. Deikmorn. So much faster was her brain than those transitions, she did ‘realm stuff’ along the way, to not waste as much time.

Gonna need to get Azure to help make the Transition Point in those catacombs after she’s sobered up. We need that quick response time as close to the frontlines as possible. There’s gotta be at least one more somewhere north, too…

She flew over to the tent Azure was passed out in, finding Camael inside, trying to stir the haggard blue beauty awake, to little avail. His wide, angelic eyes looked over as Samantha hovered close. Samantha waved him off, trying to exude calm and patience to not further spook the poor guy.

Azure was quite the mess. Her hair was everywhere, taking up practically half the bed in a wild tangle. She’d managed to get her coat sort of half-off, though a sleeve was stuck on the other hand, caught behind her back. She’d also managed to get off one boot, but otherwise slept in clothes smelling of wine and regret.

Floating over the bed, hands clasped behind her back, Samantha declared calmly, “I’m going to give you the chance to simply wake up to my voice, Azure. A count of three, m’kay? It’ll be better for you, I assure you. One…”

Azure stirred slightly, her head unburying itself and her brows knitting together.

“Two…”

The Naugite beauty made a decidedly pouty sound in her throat as she shifted again, almost as if she knew she should wake up.

“Three. Alright, you asked for it.” Calmly, she turned to Camael and said darkly, “You might wanna plug your ears.”

Camael nodded frightfully and covered his ears.

Samantha utilized [Conjure], first making some big, fluffy, cozy earmuffs over her ears that would easily protect her. Then, she conjured a small orchestra of airhorns and began blaring them right over Azure’s face.

Azure might’ve been screaming as she shot up on the bed and struggled in the veritable straitjacket she’d accidentally made out of her coat. Finally, grimacing, she got her hands up to the side of her head and clasped them over her ears, eyes peeling open, squinting terribly, and trying in vain to see what was happening.

“Staaaaaahp!” the Naugite could be heard to cry when Samantha finally did stop the horns. Finally, Azure’s eyes managed to focus on who was hovering over her, and she gasped. “Oh shit.”

Smiling grandly, Samantha threw her arms out. “Wake up, dearest!” She conjured two buckets, and they poured ice-cold water right over Azure’s head.

“Ahhhhhh!” Azure screamed in terror as the water hit her, grabbing for her life for the blanket and using it to wrap around and absorb the cold liquid.

“Are we awake?!” Samantha asked, making sure her smile was exceptionally sadistic-looking, as two more buckets appeared over Azure’s head.

“I’m awake, I’m awake!” Azure cried pitifully, holding her hands out, panting in fear as her now wide eyes craned up to the buckets. “Please, don’t! Don’t, babe, please! Mercy!”

The water sloshed a bit but didn’t spill, and Azure sighed in relief, even as she shivered.

Samantha finally sobered her expression, and her hands returned to clasp behind her back. “I take it you accept the fact that you deserved that?”

Sighing again, head hanging down, Azure shook her head. “I deserve far, far worse, my love.”
