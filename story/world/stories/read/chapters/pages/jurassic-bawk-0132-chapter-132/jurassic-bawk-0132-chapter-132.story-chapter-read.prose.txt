The final Class option had the longest and strangest description.

Soulful Master Artisan-Warrior

You have surpassed the limitations of your birth, allowing you to create Items with both skill and cunning.  Your expanded sapience will continue to develop your crafting abilities in the background, preparing you for greater works and an eventual Magnum Opus, after which your crafting abilities will diminish slightly without further investment.  Your Class Skill, Zenrage, will be changed to Battlefield Inspiration, which provides bonuses to designing new Items while in combat as well as providing additional battle “IQ”.  You will also gain a new Class Skill, Soul Artisan, that allows you to imbue Items with a small portion of your soul.  Note: Soul Artisan will synergize with your existing Skill, Imbue Soul, allowing you to add different effects to your works.  You will receive a total of twenty-five points to Strength, Dexterity, and Durability upon leveling up. You will receive a total of thirty points to Spirit upon leveling up.

Charlemagne read through the Class option twice, attempting to better understand how exactly it would work with his existing powers.  As he thought about the Class’s possibilities, an idea bubbled up from his subconscious mind.  He could use the powers that this Class granted him to create powerful defensive Items that his mates and progeny could use to defend themselves.  A dim but painful memory of his previous efforts to teach his hens how to fight still haunted him, reminding him of his failure every time he considered how best to protect his flock.  Following on the heels of that thought was the reminder that he, in fact, had been forced to consume a slurry made of his own offspring.

He had vowed never to be so weak again.

But would this Class help him achieve his goals?  He could already make magical Items.  In fact, he had just made a really good one for the pangolin.  So this Class would not change anything there.

Outlining his priorities always helped Charlemagne make decisions.  He was, after all, a simple bird.  Entertainment was not a priority.  Comfort was not a priority.  His own safety was barely a priority, and only to the extent that he was able to continue to shield his mates and offspring.  The rooster’s goals remained unchanged: assert dominance, eat good food, and make more little Charlemanges.  So which class helped him the most with that?

After much internal debate, the rooster discarded Manaforged Noble Spellsword, Primordial Chimera Progenitor, and Soulful Master Artisan-Warrior.  The Spellsword Class was very powerful, especially for someone like Charlemagne who was always righteous all the time.  However, he did not care for the Reckless Vitality Skill, which would essentially kill him over time.  The Chimera Class was equally tempting, as it promised the chance to gain Skills through eating other beings, but the rooster believed that the class had a hidden downside.  The word ‘chimera’ was translated by the Squiggles directly into Charlemagne’s brain as “a being of dubious biological provenance”.  Charlemagne had already experienced enough genetic drift and was not interested in changing his form further away from the perfection that he embodied.  Finally, the Artisan-Warrior was not focused enough on fighting for his taste.  He especially doubted that he would be thinking of crafting new Items while he was in the middle of a fight for his life.

With half of his choices having been eliminated, the rooster spent more time going over three finalists before getting rid of Blazing Berserker Tyrant.  While the class was fairly interesting, it didn’t really give him anything new beyond better fire control while in combat.  That left only two options: Arcane Warbrute Champion and Nova Avian Prime.  Charlemagne read through the descriptions again.

Arcane Warbrute Champion

When people refer to you as tough, they are generally underselling your ability to take punishment and survive. Generally, defense-focused Classes tend to have lower offensive capabilities, but not you. You can dish it out just as well as you can take it. This class passively boosts both your base defensive abilities while also providing a moderate increase in your ability to manipulate mana. Your Class Skill, Zenrage, will be upgraded to Arcane Cascade, granting you its existing bonuses as well as a bonus when contesting another creature’s attempts to control environmental mana. You will also gain a new Class Skill, Shieldbreak, which will enable you to better break through your foes’ defenses. Note: Shieldbreak can be applied to both physical and energy attacks. You will receive a total of twenty-five points of Strength and Dexterity upon leveling up. You will receive a total of thirty points of Durability and Spirit upon leveling up.

Nova Avian Prime

You are a bird. You are fire. You’re like a firebird. Note: apparently, this was a very popular conveyance at one point on your planet. Although you’re not a conveyance, you do convey a fiery death to your adversaries. Your Physique Skill, Inferno’s Embrace, will be more effective and will level up faster. In addition, you receive a bonus to manipulating fire and combustion-related energies. Your Class Skill, Zenrage, will be upgraded to Molten Rage, granting you bonuses to combat based on how high your internal temperature is. You will also gain the Class Skill, Fire Flash, allowing you to teleport short distances by transforming your corporeal form into pure fire. You will receive a total of twenty-five points to Durability and Spirit upon leveling up. You will receive a total of thirty points to Strength and Dexterity upon leveling up.

The Warbrute Class seemed to hold the most promise for a direct combat potential upgrade.  He had no doubts that he would continue to fight creatures that far exceeded him in levels, and every advantage he could get to breaking through powerful defenses was welcome.  Getting a bonus to wrestling mana away from the control of others was…underwhelming.  That being said, the Class did give him his two favorite Attributes in greater amounts: Durability and Spirit both would receive 30 points per level rather than 25.

On the other hand, Nova Avian Prime felt like it had some serious long-term benefits for the rooster’s love life.  After receiving the incredibly powerful skill Inferno’s Embrace, the rooster’s body exuded heat in amounts sufficient to cook a pre-System chicken in a matter of minutes.  He would have to learn better control over his own fiery nature if he wanted to continue his one rooster quest to populate the Earth, and the Class promised just that.

It took Charlemagne just a few moments to realize that he had already made up his mind.  Without further ado, including telling Phatagin what was about to happen, Charlemagne made his choice: Nova Avian Prime.

A thick reddish-tinted eggshell formed around the rooster as an incredible wave of fatigue swept over him.  By the time that Charlemagne was fully enclosed, he had already lost the battle to stay awake.  This time, however, no dream disrupted his rest.

Consciousness returned slowly and gently, the oven-like heat within the egg creating a cozy atmosphere that Charlemagne was loath to give up.  But, even without him doing anything, the warm air began to vent from cracks in the shell.  Cold air rushed in to replace it, annoying the rooster into wakefulness.

The Squiggles, noticing that he was awake, began bombarding him with messages.

Charlemagne stretched his wings, pushing away pieces of eggshell as he did so.  His body felt like it was bursting with power.  Every small movement felt more fluid and elegant, but at the same time, he felt the weight behind them.  He also felt healthy in a way that he had never felt before, as if the Class Evolution process had corrected a number of defects in his body that had crept in over the levels without him even realizing it.  Even his mind felt sharper…it was as if a fog hanging over his thoughts had been lifted.  The rooster was still exploring the changes to his body when he sensed two creatures approaching.

“Ser Charlemagne!” a familiar voice called.  It was Josephine, the Attendant.

“Bawk?” he asked.

“You look…well you look the same, although you definitely seem stronger.”

“A lot stronger,” Phasima noted as she floated up.  “Your new Class must be giving you a lot of Attribute points per level.”

“Bawk,” the rooster agreed.

“That’s absurd!” the Vessarian blurted, while Josephine looked equally shocked.

“Bawk?” Charlemagne asked while the pair was still recovering.

“You’ve been out for about two days, give or take.  It’s a bit hard to tell time in a Dungeon unless you pay attention, and there was a lot for us to do while you were out.  Phatagin asked me to train him, and he finally got his mana Skills to a place he was happy with.  His Class Evolution started just over a day ago, but I can’t imagine it will take as long as yours,” Phasima said.

“So, Ser Charlemagne, can you tell us what your new Class is named?”

The rooster obliged, although neither woman had ever heard of it.  Feeling generous after acquiring such an incredible boost to his power, he even told them about the Classes that he had passed over.

“I think you made a solid choice,” Phasima responded with confidence as Charlemagne wrapped up.  “It’s probably not the option I would have taken, but that’s more an issue of preference than anything.”

“I would have picked what you picked, Ser Charlemagne,” Josephine added.

The rooster’s stomach decided to interject at that moment, rumbling loud enough that everyone present could hear it.

“Well, it’s a good thing that we saved a bunch of the food from the box for Charlemagne,” Phasima laughed.

“It’s a good thing that the food didn’t disappear,” Josephine added.  “The crowd did.”

“Well, the System has no incentive not to give proper supplies during downtime.  It’s not like everyone has access to dimensional storage options,” Phasima noted.

Charlemagne ignored the banter and, following his nose, found the remnants of a great feast in one of the small rooms that stood at the top of the stands.  He dug in without hesitation, leaving his future self to deal with future meals.  Long deprived of appropriate nutrition, his body was craving everything: fat, protein, carbohydrates, vitamins, and minerals all went down his gullet to be burned in the furnace that was his stomach.  He ate with both gusto and abandon, allowing his whimsy to take him from dish to dish.  After a while, he realized that he was no longer hungry.  In fact, he was feeling rather full, and not just in his stomach.  His Ember Core felt like it was full to bursting as food turned to energy inside his body which was transformed into heat and mana within his Core.  Despite feeling no pain, the rooster was certain that something was about to happen.

Something wonderful.
