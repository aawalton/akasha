While I composed myself, Toby explained everything to Cerise.

“Yeah, we need to find new clothes. Uh-huh, hmm, got it. Do it when I ask you.”

After that, he spoke to me.

“Oi, Clark. Are you ready now?”

“New plan. Get to the parking lot and steal the guard uniform. Use that to infiltrate the upper floor. You got that?”

I straightened back up and nodded.

After leaving the laundry room, we went to the sleeping receptionist and tied her up, then stuffed her mouth with cloth so she couldn’t shout.

We did set an automatic message on her phone for twelve hours later, which would share her location with her friends as a precaution in case she was never found.

She would be questioned about Palik’s death, but no one should suspect her.

Cerise hacked the cameras again, and we walked through the hallway.

My clothes were wet again and Toby’s were bloody. We did take the dryer—which wasn’t strong enough to dry my clothes—cologne, and mouth freshener with us.

We reached the parking lot and travelled even further towards the exit.

“Hey, so did I tell you about what Cerise’s chauffeur told me?”

It was the guard who had registered me when I entered.

“Nah uh,” another guard replied.

All the guests had arrived by now, and their cars had already entered. With nothing else to do, and never imagining that the guild’s headquarters itself could be attacked, they drowned themselves in idle chatter.

“Cerise, she doesn’t live with her parents. She is living alone with her boyfriend.”

Toby stared at me. I casually shrugged. This was what Cerise got for teasing me.

“And why is that such a big deal?”

“Because her boyfriend is a middle-aged dude as old as Eric Truman himself.”

Toby looked at me with disgust for spewing such lies about his friend. I shook my head violently. I pointed towards myself and made an X with my hands, trying to tell him that it wasn’t me.

The rumor was growing out of hand.

Toby sighed and pointed towards the open window.

I used Shadowstride to sense that no one was inside, unless they were hiding in the lockers.

No one would do that. Right?

I hopped inside while Toby had to climb in.

We looked through each of the lockers until we found a pair of uniforms in our sizes. We once again changed. The dryer was silent, so nobody was alerted. Even in the blue uniform of the guard, Toby somehow looked dashing.

Toby vaulted out of the locker room. I used Shadowstride. We walked through the parking lot. This time, the cameras were live, showing us.

Instead of going to the back door, we went to the elevator.

“Are you a good actor?” Toby asked while pressing the button and calling the elevator down.

“I think I am.”

“That doesn’t sound confident.”

With a ding, the elevator arrived, and the doors opened. We went in and Toby pressed the seventh floor. That was where the entry was supposed to be.

“Cerise, do you have the key from Farnes’s phone?” Toby asked her through the intercom.

—I have had it for an eternity. You guys took so much time.

“Sorry that a Silver-rank Walker was there to greet us as soon as we got out.”

—I hear excuses. Just get there already. There isn’t long until the party ends.

“Yeah, yeah,” Toby grumbled.

The screen read seven. There was a ding, and the elevator doors slid open.

We moved with confidence. A janitor saw us but didn’t say anything. Moving past several cubicles and meeting rooms, we reached the end of the hallway.

A completely normal-looking door.

AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY

The plaque read.

Toby pushed the handle down. It didn’t open.

“Cerise, open the door,” he said.

The small red light near the handle turned green. Toby pushed it again, and it opened.

Sharing a nod, we went inside.

It was a small office room. Completely ordinary.

“There’s nothing here,” Toby said.

“No, there is.” I touched the wall just in front of me. “It’s behind this wall.”

“A secret door, but where could the button for it be?”

The wall slit open and started opening on its own. Toby and I got into a stance, ready to fight whoever was going to come out of there.

—You guys can go ahead. I opened the door from Farnes’s phone.

Toby and I looked at each other. Neither of us said anything. We walked inside as if nothing had happened.

There wasn’t a shift in surroundings like there had been with the old hotel hallways. They were the same light blue color as before, well lit. The only difference was that it was narrow, the ceiling was higher, and there were no doors on either side.

We walked for a minute until we reached a staircase going up.

We climbed up what should have been two floors but was only one.

A door appeared.

MONSTER PROCESSING

The plaque read beside it.

“I don’t think it is this one,” I said.

Toby agreed, and we went to the next floor.

PLANT PROCESSINGFORBIDDEN ITEMSNPCs

“Use your skill,” he said.

I channeled my Magicka and focused on Shadowstride.

The hallways inside were similarly narrow, but they were not as tall as before. Every few metres, there were stairs going up into small rooms. And inside those rooms were people.

“I sense people. Imprisoned.”

“Any guards?”
