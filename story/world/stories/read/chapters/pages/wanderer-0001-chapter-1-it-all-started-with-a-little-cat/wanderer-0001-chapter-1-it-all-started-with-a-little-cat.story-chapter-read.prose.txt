I press a hand to my wounded side, blood trickling down my armor as I look over my shoulder, trying to discern my pursuer's whereabouts. They're...maybe 50 yards behind. I'm moving much slower than usual, my limbs dragging and my heart racing. Things are looking bleak. I'm now certain that the blade that caused this dreaded wound was dipped in basilisk poison, so I don't have much longer unless I get my hands on an antidote fast, but I'm at least 2 miles out from the village of Cletharda. A snap of a branch sounds 20 yards away, causing me to whip my head towards the sound.

Gods...they are getting closer.

Another whistle of an arrow, however I'm not as fast this time as it grazes my shoulder, causing me to hiss and unsheathe my sword, perhaps for the last time.

I will have to face them and fight...I turn to face my pursuer.

A male hooded figure steps out from a nearby tree. His stance...it somehow seems familiar. My heart pounds out of my chest as I wait for him to near.

The figure's gait changes to a strut, with a sinister air as he removes his hood to reveal dark long curly hair, a strong jawline and striking green eyes settled on mine. Eyes that I have gazed into millions of times. Eyes that I looked into with trust and love, until now.

My heart sinks to my stomach as I gasp, my heart breaking into a million pieces. "Rolim!"

My mate. My mate...wounded me? It didn't make any sense. There has to be an explanation. Deep sorrow overtakes me, and everything feels hopeless. I must be strong and fight him, but my treacherous eyes begin to water as I take in his handsome form.

Rolim grins menacingly, the expression so horrifying on a face I have traced with my hands lovingly over centuries. He opens his mouth to say...

"I'm sorry Beatrix...." BEEP! BEEP!

Huh?

"I love you so much..." BEEP! "but I have to hurt you now..." BEEP!

BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!

My eyes fly open, and the obnoxious blaring of my phone alarm greets me, reminding me that it's Monday morning. Ugh! It was just starting to get good!

Another dream inspired by my current Romantasy binge read, A Betrayal so Heartless, one of the most popular reads right now according to the r/Romantasy subreddit. It's a great slow burn full of betrayal and new beginnings, my favorite trope. My phone alarm reads 7:30 am as I turn it off. Sure...Deyre Laeris Morrengail was betrayed by her mate...but will probably meet some hot fae prince from the kingdom of Ecrela who will be her new love interest (her ACTUAL actual mate) and she will gain some cool new ability she never knew she had that makes her super overpowered. At least she had a life that was exciting. Her day to day involved sword fighting, befriending magical creatures and meeting fae royalty. My day to day involved...excel spreadsheets, corporate bullshit and overbearing managers. I work at a well known Fintech company, Nile Inc. as a data analyst. It's an extremely boring job that sucks the life out of me.

With a heavy sigh I roll out of bed, stepping into my fluffy cat shaped slippers and shuffling to the kitchen to make some very strong black tea. Coffee is definitely needed too if I plan on surviving my 5 back to back meetings today. Fuuuuuuck. To make things worse, my boss hasn't been very happy with me lately. In our last 1:1, he said I was not showing enough ownership or initiative, but has also been clearly preventing me from taking on any feasible projects. Let's face it, he's kind of a dick and is trying to get me to quit. Annnd it's working...I just haven't found anything else yet. I scrub my face with my hands, longingly wondering what it would be like to have chosen a different path in life. Perhaps a career that's more fulfilling? Something more adventurous?

I scoop a couple teaspoons of Almond Sugar Cookie black tea into my ceramic teapot I got from a trip to Japan, then fill it with hot water from my Japanese hot water dispenser. I gaze out my window to the Seattle skyline, waiting for the tea to brew.

If I had another chance to start over...what would I do? At the age of 30, I am technically young enough to consider a career pivot. I like helping others, love animals, and have thought about teaching martial arts after getting my third yellow stripe in Wing Chun class. Honestly I would love to learn another martial art form too, one involving weapons. There has to be something out there besides excel spreadsheets. Lately I've felt like I've been floating along a lazy river, life starting to pass me by on the riverbanks.

My phone buzzes with a text. Tapping it to view the message while carefully pouring my tea, I see that it's from my friend Natasha.

Karaoke again tonight? :)

The message makes me smile, and I text back, Of course!

Blasting Pink Pony Club at the Crescent Lounge at the top of my lungs would definitely cheer me up. It always did. I really want to nail those high notes this time, too. Gotta put those years of choir in school to good use!

Another buzz from my phone. This time it's Mom.

Have a great day at work today! (heart emoji) Remember this weekend is Dads' birthday celebration.

Yep. At the Mexican place right?

Mom thumbs ups the text. Wow, look at her, using emojis.

"You got this, Beatrix" I say to myself. "You are a fierce, strong warrior, and you're going to tell that boss to go fuck himself," I add, pointing at my reflection. Yeah....right, probably not. It's a busy Monday morning in the streets of Belltown Seattle as I exit the main door of my apartment complex. Commuters are honking at each other, bicyclists barely dodging pedestrians as everyone is in a hurry to get somewhere. I barely have time to duck in and grab a coffee at the closest coffee shop, also my favorite one, a cat cafe called Whiskers and Beans. Sadly, I don't have time to play with the cats today. I make a mental note to get up 20 minutes earlier tomorrow so I can give my favorite cat there, an orange tabby named Cheeto, plenty of skritches, as we'll both need it. Cheeto, perched on a driftwood cat tree in the corner in the cat section of the cafe, lazily watches me exit the coffee shop and yawns...

Wait, did he just wink at me?

No way, I must have imagined it. Clearly I needed this coffee, I think. Nonetheless, I wink back and wave at him like an idiot as I leave. I love that cat.

I check my phone for the time, 8:20 am. Shit! I've got ten minutes to get to work. I take a big swig of my oat milk latte, it only slightly burning my esophagus as it's still pretty hot, and pick up the pace. Whiskers and Beans is about a 15 minute walk from work, so I will definitely have to hustle to get there on time. My office is right next to The Orbs, a large indoor garden fixture that our CEO likes to boast about and tourists often visit.

While waiting for the light to change to cross the street, I check my Nile Ring app, which my team uses to communicate with each other via instant messaging. Four messages from my boss. My heart sinks, because I know they are not going to be cheerful ones. As always, the messages seem to read in a cold, unfriendly manner. He says that he is still not happy with the latest draft of the data trends analysis paper I am writing and it needs more work, despite this being the 4th draft I have sent to him and getting completely different feedback every time. MAYBE if he was more clear in his feedback and actually told me what he wanted, we wouldn't go in fucking circles like this.  I inwardly sigh, and start typing a much nicer response than my actual thoughts while the light changes and I begin crossing the street.

I'm nearly to the other side when I hear a soft mewl behind me. I lower my phone and whip my head around, nearly spilling my coffee in the process. There, sitting in the middle of the street, impossibly, is Cheeto. He gazes up at me and meows again, this time in a somewhat sassy manner, raising his eyebrow whiskers. The expression is oddly human-like for a cat.

"Cheeto! OH MY GOD what are you doing there!" I screech. This time I definitely drop the coffee. The light is also now green, and cars are honking.

"Euuargh!! Hold on!" I yell at the honking cars, waving my arms frantically as I hurry over to Cheeto to scoop him up. "Come here, Cheeto, it's ok!"

As I secure Cheeto in my arms, a visibly angry Prius driver swerves around me, shouting something indiscernible out the window. Rude! I ignore him as I head back to the sidewalk with Cheeto. Meanwhile, a large truck takes a sharp left turn from the adjacent street, heading straight for us. The driver is talking to a passenger, not really watching the road. Fuck! Heart pounding, I'm still about 10 feet from the sidewalk, busy traffic on the right side not letting me pass despite the precious cargo in my arms. In a panic, I decide to turn around and try to go the other way, but the truck is almost on us. Cheeto mewls again, this time more worriedly, while I sprint and leap onto the opposite sidewalk, the truck barely missing us both and I stumble to my knees, hissing on the impact. But Cheeto is safe.

"My goodness, are you ok?!" a middle-aged woman asks as she helps me up from the ground. She has a kind face, and is the only person that seems to have noticed the almost accident.

"I better get him back to the cat cafe. Thanks for helping me up."

"Oh? Whiskers and Beans? Love that place. Of course, Dear. Now you take care of yourself!" The kind woman smiles and goes on her way.

That was close. I almost got Isekai'd. I can't help thinking of all the anime I've watched over the years that literally started with a scene like the one I just nearly fully experienced, and the main character was reincarnated in a new world following their death. I shake my head to gather myself.

I gently carry him back to Whiskers and Beans, attracting interested stares and looks from the passerby. As I walk inside, Helen, a cafe worker, is frantically talking on the phone with someone, most likely her manager. She has tears rolling down her cheek and one hand raking through her hair. As soon as she sees us, the relief on her face is instant and she drops her hand holding the cell phone.
