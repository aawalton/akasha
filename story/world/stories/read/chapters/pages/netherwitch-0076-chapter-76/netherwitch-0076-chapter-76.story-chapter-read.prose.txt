Golems

A golem is a construct similar to a phantasmal beast. The key difference is that phantasms are born naturally from the plane. Golems, on the other hand, are engineered from the ground up. For this reason, they have a nether code and structure that is comparably simplistic. Their design is much easier for mages to understand.

Virtually all golems are built around a two-part core. The blood core simulates the heart of a phantasmal beast, providing life to the golem. The psychic core replaces the phantasm’s mind. Through this means, an instruction set can be entered, governed and interpreted by a mental imprint.

This is the key advantage of golems. Phantasmal beasts are influenced by their instinct and their animal mind. Even when tamed, they act as trained dogs at best. Golems, by contrast, have a clearly defined psyche. They follow orders without cluttered thoughts or distraction. For many labor tasks, this makes them superior. However, it isn’t without cost. Cheap golems are often stupider than beasts.

Golems are typically equipped with internal spell catalysts. While these runic codes can be engineered for war, they can also be used for labor. The spell catalysts let golems carry out simple tasks that would normally require ki manipulation. In some cases, golems can mimic ki arts.

Standard Golem

The standard golem forms a body around its core using essence, making it similar to a phantasmal beast. Because life elements are complex, creators prefer simpler primordial structures such as rock, wood, or clay. Golem cores are not as tightly entangled with the body as those of phantasms. As a result, golems can usually survive the destruction of their form so long as their core is intact. By comparison, to properly harvest a phantasmal beast’s heart requires a hunter skilled at the task.

Once created, standard golems can operate 24/7. The complexity of those tasks depends on their intelligence and thus the quality of their psychic core and their instruction set. Cheap golems can be used to pull carts or move earth. More sophisticated golems armed with the proper catalysts can handle logging, mining, or other crude resource manipulation.

Golems of this sort are rarely used in war. Tamed beasts are cheaper and provide more bang for the buck.

Figment Golems

If a golem core projects its body with mana, then it is a figment golem. The advantage of this design is that the body can be trivially regenerated or replaced. Unlike standard golems, figment golems consume mana during operation. They have an activity limit of roughly one hour. During intense combat, the time frame might shrink to minutes.

Thus, figment golems are unsuited for rote labor. Nearly all golems of this sort are wargolems.

In battle, figment golems are incredibly tough. They can rapidly regenerate all wounds. Additionally, they can deploy powerful attacks. When their mana is exhausted, the golem core will simply ‘eject’ from the false body and retreat. Eight hours later, the golem can be redeployed. If a mana pool is provided, this time frame can be reduced to 30 minutes.

Heaven is famous for its figment golem armies. Their high-end designs have cores that teleport back to safety when their resources run out.

Construct Golems

The highest-level and most expensive golems are construct golems. Instead of projecting a body, the core integrates with an enchanted machine. There are a variety of designs. In some cases, the core pilots the body. In others, the cores will grow phantasmal flesh around a constructed skeleton. The tower spirit Tårn is a case of the former.

While this method produces powerful golems, it comes at a cost. The skeleton or machine body of a construct golem cannot be restored or regenerated by the core. Engineers are needed not only to repair the golem should it be damaged but also for routine maintenance.
