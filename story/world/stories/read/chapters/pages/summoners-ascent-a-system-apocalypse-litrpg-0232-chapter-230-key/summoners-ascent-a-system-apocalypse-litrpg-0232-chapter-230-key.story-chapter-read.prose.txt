"Dad? What the fuck..?" Ashton focused on the figure moving in front of him. This was undeniably his father. There was no way for it to be anyone else. His height, the thick glasses sitting on his nose. Even that coat that he was wearing; Ashton recognised it.

He already figured that this was going to be the case, but that didn't change the fact that it actually being true was quite the shock to him. How did his father create an imprint in the veil?

Ashton had pushed 'pause' on the Arcane Pocket Watch so that he could take a proper look, and then slowly twisted the dial so that he could see exactly all the things his father was doing. Though it was all happening in reverse.

Ashton's father was moving away from his office right now. It looked like he was running.

Back in the office, he was doing something that Ashton couldn't quite see. looked like the view was being blocked by all the clutter that gathered up over the years. And before he went into the office in the first place, Ashton's father was on the other side of the hallway and, just like it was described, was talking to someone.

That other person couldn't be seen in the imprint, but what did show up was the object in Arthur's hand. It wasn't there when he ran away from the office. And right now, Ashton could watch it be exchanged between the two parties as well, and watched his father's imprint fade away again.

"So that thing is what caused the imprint?" Ashton whispered. He couldn't tell what it was. It was only clear that something powerful was sitting on Arthur's palm, but not in the slightest what exactly that something was.

"Millie... what do you think? Can you see anything?" Ashton asked. He already knew the answer, but he figured he would ask anyway.

"I can just see the ghost, but I can't really feel anything from it... This isn't a person, right?" Millie asked, still staring at the spectral figure before her. "I mean, it's not a 'soul'?"

Ashton shook his head. "No, it's not. It's an imprint, just like I thought..."

Madeleine stared at the figure of the ghost just standing in front of her. "Did you just say 'dad'? Is this person your father?"

"Mm-hmm. I'm very certain of that, at least."

"I... What does he want? Is he tied to this place? Does he have lingering regrets here?"

Ashton scoffed. "No, it's not a spectre. But it's a ghost, in a sense," he pointed out. "And don't worry, it's going to disappear soon enough anyway."

There was no way in hell that Ashton would leave an echo of his father here to be turned into a test subject by Quincy. Ashton wasn't quite sure about it himself, but there were cultures that were aware of the veil that believed that such a strong imprint of a person was still somehow connected to that individual.

Like a part of their self, that piece that had experienced this moment, was forced to repeat it again and again for all of eternity. There wasn't exactly proof for it, but there wasn't any against it, either. Rather, Ashton figured it was possible. He didn't understand all that much about the nature of the veil yet, after all; nobody did.

But for now, Ashton had to continue figuring out what actually happened. What sort of power that thing had to cause a veil imprint here.

And so, he stepped into the office. He stopped the watch at the moment where Arthur was hidden by the desk and the boxes stacked up in here, and decided to just get to work. With the assistance of the Helping Hand, Ashton pushed everything to the side and quickly, and carefully, moved everything aside. Arthur's hand was pushed through the old rug on the ground.

Ashton rolled it up and revealed the old wooden flooring. This part of the university hadn't actually been renovated in quite a while, so there were still old floorboards around. One of them, while not exactly 'loose', seemed like it could be dislodged from its position, and Ashton quickly did just that, pulling the wood up toward himself.

Below that, there was a small wooden box. Too big to be the object itself. Plus, Ashton couldn't feel any mana from it in the slightest.

Actually... Ashton couldn't feel any mana from it. He could see the form of it, but the inside was completely hidden, like there was a void of mana. Even when Ashton tried to force some more in, or when he focused his channelling range as much as he could on this box, he couldn't see inside.

It wasn't exactly hard to open the box. Beside its magic-blocking qualities, it was just made of old wood, and the lock and hinges seemed quite rusted. The moment Ashton pulled the box open, he was practically assaulted by a wave of mana. It was enough to block out his view for a moment, somewhat like someone suddenly shone a flashlight in your eyes.

It calmed down soon; seemingly, over time, all the mana had just gathered in here and came flooding out all at once. Though it wasn't even that much. More than would be contained in your average magic item, but it was probably less than the mana that made up the wisp's body.

But somehow, the veil was practically warping around it.

This small wooden key; it was like the weight of its existence was enough to bend the magical world around it.

No wonder it caused an imprint back then. But what kind of person was able to control this item well enough to prevent an imprint before then..? And why the hell did Arthur have something that could block an item like this?

Ashton had the Pen of Insight inspect the key for now, and then gave it to the Backpacker.

But when the young spirit appeared, it seemed more interested in the old wooden box that Ashton was holding.

"... Can you do something with this?" Ashton wondered, and handed it to the Backpacker. They quickly nodded, pushing the box into the darkness of their hood. The wood was crushed and the metal snapped and bent, until finally, a message popped up.

[Contract | Backpacker has absorbed the [Magic Sealing Box]]

[Contract | Backpacker's Ability [Storage Item Creation] has been expanded]

Ashton looked at the satisfied Backpacker. There didn't seem to be any additional keychain hanging off the bag, so Ashton came to a different conclusion.

"Can your storage item seal in mana now?"

The Backpacker immediately nodded. From the various rapid hand-motions they were making, it did also seem like using that function also took up an additional 'Charge' for creating a storage item.

But either way, Ashton figured that it wasn't a bad idea to have something dedicated to keeping suspicious item sealed away, and quickly had the Backpacker create an item chest for it. There was space for more items in there, though for now, he only put the key inside.

And then, as the Backpacker disappeared again, Ashton held forward his staff, starting to pour some mana through it into the air to try and interfere with the veil.

Ashton's magic flowed through the tiny individual threads of the other plane and began to disrupt them. Just small ripples throughout various points that were going to essentially 'reset' the imprint in the veil.

And as those ripples moved through this space, even the image conjured by the Arcane Pocket Watch was wiped away, so he knew that this would work to stop his father's echo from appearing here again.

It was nice to see him standing in front of him, moving again, though, even if it was just through the form of a veil imprint.

"Alright. Let's go now, then," Ashton said, patting Millie's head as he walked up to the girl.

"Awe, already? Is the ghost hunt over?" Millie asked with a sad groan.

"Sadly so. We've got plenty of stuff to bring back to Calibur, don't we?"

"I guess..."

Ashton focused on the girl. She really did seem a bit frustrated. She probably hoped to have learned a bit more throughout this all. So, Ashton came up with an idea.

"Well, if you don't want to go yet... How about this? We can play a game before we go," Ashton suggested, turning toward Madeleine, who was still perplexed by all the things she had just seen. "Would you be able to give us a hand with that?"

"Me?" she replied, startled. And Ashton quickly nodded.

"Yes, please. It's simple, really. We're going to play a round of hide and seek, and I would like you to play by Millie's side and make sure she doesn't get hurt."

Confused, Millie stared up at Ashton, "What? But I'm not a baby anymore, I can walk around on my own!"

"Right, she seems... big enough," Madeleine agreed.

But Ashton shook his head, untying his mask. His lids were closed over the empty sockets, so it wasn't quite so gruesome to look at, but there were still some small scars around the edges of his eyes. Either way, Madeleine seemed a little startled.

"But you can't walk around when you're blind quite well yet, can you?" Ashton said. "Your goal is to find me without being able to see me. And I'm also going to use techniques to completely silence myself once I get to my spot. So, you'll have to fully rely on your sixth sense."

Millie's eyes widened as Ashton knelt down in front of her. "So it's magical hide and seek?"

"Exactly."

"That's so cool!"

Madeleine looked at Ashton a bit confused. "Uhm... I think that's kind of..."

"Don't worry, this isn't a trick. Millie is quite special. I'm positive she'll be able to find me, don't worry." Ashton placed the mask over Millie's eyes and tied it behind her head. "The seeker has thirty minutes to find the hider. After those thirty minutes, or when the hider was found, we swap. After it's been both of our turns three times, we leave. Okay?"

Millie quickly nodded, grinning excitedly.

"Alright? Then count to a hundred, and then you can come find me."
