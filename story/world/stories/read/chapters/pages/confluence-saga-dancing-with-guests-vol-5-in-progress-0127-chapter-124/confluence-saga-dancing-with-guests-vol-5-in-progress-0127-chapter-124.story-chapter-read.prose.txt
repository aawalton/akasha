Remember, your Husk is more than just the face you show to the world. It is the face the world affects.

We shape our Husks to reflect who we are, to show other to other Awakened. It is often imperfect, a small variation here or there that doesn't match our True Self. Sometimes this is intentional, to cover a childhood scar, a birth mark. Sometimes because we don't realize we didn't get right.

Other times the imperfection is something more, an idealization. A change that anyone who knew you before your Awakening would point to and say is drastically different. Taller, shorter, more slender, stockier. Almost everyone lies, if only by accident.

Our Husks do not gather scars, they heal and return to how they were, to how we envision them. But they do feel pain, they do bleed, they do experience the environment we expose ourselves to.

Our Husks do not distance us from our lives. They give us more leeway, let us take more risks, but we pay the price in the moment.
