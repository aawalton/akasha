Four down, four to go.

Levi took stock of the situation and had to admit he liked the new odds at four to two. And to be honest, it had to seem terrible to the young ogres who had just witnessed Levi slay the rest of their kin with relative ease. And if he were the one in their position, he would’ve tucked his tail between his legs and fled.

Safe to say the ogres were braver than him—or stupider—seeing as all four now charge straight for him, even after just watching him make light work of their kin. Then again, Levi had to admit he was a bit tired, and he'd already used up all his available casting for [Shadow Hold I] for the day, which meant he only had [Void Step III] to rely on.

Of course, he wasn’t insinuating that the ogres had any idea of his current predicament, but he had to note that it had to be obvious enough to the ogres that he was flagging for them to even attempt to attack him rather than fleeing.

Suck it in levi, job’s not over.

He wrapped his fingers around his blade tightly as he ran towards the charging ogres, and once he judged the distance to be adequate for what he needed. He activated his skill.

[void step(III) Shadow]

It happened all too quickly. One millisecond, he was charging towards the ogres, and then he was in the shadow verse, and in another millisecond, he was behind the ogre at the rear.

The movement had been crisp and flowed as naturally as ever; unfortunately for Levi, the mage had launched a fireball straight at the ogre that he’d chosen just as he emerged from its shadow.

Levi moved just in time to avoid some of the fireball catching onto his cloak.

Yeah, we definitely need to work on our teamwork.

Levi suppressed the urge to protest the mage's action; instead, he took advantage of the chaos they’d caused.

Regardless of the miscommunication, the rogue made the best of the situation by dashing towards the ogre closest to the one that the mage's fireball had hit. His blade went up in an arc as soon as he got into position, but the free arm of the ogre deflected his strike, and it counterattacked by swinging its cudgel towards the rogue, but Levi had already hopped back. He gritted his teeth in frustration. I guess the ogres might be stronger than I gave them credit for.

The clash spurred the rest of the ogres into action. Two ogres went for the mage while the other two focused on him.

Well, if he was being technical, the ogre currently putting off the flames on its body didn’t exactly have much of a choice on who it faced, as Levi was already on it in a flash as soon as it extinguished the mage's flames.

It made much more sense to Levi for him to abandon the ogre that was already prepared to face him in favour of the ogre that was currently disoriented from taking a fireball to the face. And so that was what he did.

The rogue dashed for the vulnerable ogre, sliding to its side, and as soon as he got in range, he struck at its eyes. Of course, the ogre, still trying to get its bearings, was no match for a nimble Levi who stabbed at its left eyeball, managing to get a good bit deep before the enraged ogre tossed him aside.

Levi tucked into a roll to cushion the impact of being tossed to the ground, and he quickly sprang to his feet and charged at the ogre, not interested in having his advantage slip out of his grasp.

He'd made it about five steps to the ore that just had its left eye poked out when a wooden cudgel flashed from behind him, hitting him in his right rib and careening him into the dungeon wall.

The rogue hit the wall with a heavy thud, his right side flaring up as he tried to move, and his vision became blurry as he pushed up from the floor, but he had no choice, so he persevered and pushed up from the floor. Involuntarily coughing up blood. Well, that’s not good.

Levi wiped his lips with the sleeves of his cloak before turning his attention to the ogre that had hit him. It stood proud, a smug expression on its face, its wooden cudgel positioned by its side.

As far as he could tell, he wasn’t going to be able to move as fast as he’d like because of his new injury, but he still bet on himself being able to take on the ogres.

Besides, I don’t need to be fast to take on these brutes.

He bared his dagger in the direction of the ogre and charged before he could find a reason to change his mind.

Levi didn't know if it was because he was suffering from a concussion or something, but he could have sworn that he saw the ogre flash him a taunting smile as if telling him to try his luck. The rogue shook the thought away as he closed the distance between him and the ogre, and as soon as he got close enough to it, he slashed.

[void step(III) Shadow]

That had been the plan all along: slipping into the shadowverse and then reappearing from its shadow to kill the ogre from behind while it tried to defend against an attack from the front that would never come.

At least that was how he expected it to play out, but as soon as Levi emerged from its shadow, he received a heavy elbow to the face, his nose making a crunching sound as he was once again tossed to the side from the force of the ogre's blow. But this time he managed to stop himself before he hit the floor… hard.

Levi cursed as he pushed himself to his feet. The same ogre had managed to mess him up twice within a handful of minutes. It was pitiful to be bested by a dumb beast, but then again, Levi couldn't really say that that particular ogre was dumb; if it was able to predict his moves, then it was clearly not that dumb.

The rogue’s thought scattered into the wind as the ogre now decided to charge at him, its wooden cudgel by its side.

A grimace spread across his face. If the ogre was already injuring him badly with just self-defense, then how bad was it going to be for him if the ogre actually decided to go all-out?

Luckily for the rogue, the ogre stopped in its tracks before abruptly taking a huge step to the side, and it didn’t take too long for Levi to figure out why it had done that.

The rogue quickly ducked as a fireball meant for the ogre flew past his head and ended up hitting the wall behind him, and splattering into nothingness.

He locked eyes with the source of the fireball. Xara shot him an apologetic look before returning to her own fight against the two ogres struggling to close the distance between her and them. Still, she kept hurling fireball after fireball. It looked like she was also flagging, but Levi couldn’t focus on that as he had a fight of his own to focus on.

His gaze turned to the ogre that had now resumed its charge towards him. It has good reflexes. How do I use it against it?

The ogre's anticipation was on another level than that of its kin, but it also depended on it entirely too much, and if there was something that Levi had learned on the street, it was that too much of everything was not good.

A pained grin spread across his face as a plan came to mind.

It was rough and probably had a lot of holes he could find if he poked through it, but he didn’t have the time to figure out the problems with his plan as the ogre had resumed its charge towards him. He couldn’t think too much about it; the only thing he could do was act on the plan, and that was what he did.

[void step(III) Shadow]

[void step(III) Tear]

Levi slipped into his shadow, narrowly avoiding getting a wooden cudgel to the face. He emerged from the shadow of one of the dead ogres to see the ogre swing its cudgel, extending its arm into the void tear that appeared behind him, and the rogue did not waste any time commanding the tear to close with the ogre's arm still in it.

As soon as that Void tear closed, another tear opened in front of Levi, and the ogre's severed arm, still holding the wooden cudgel, dropped from it.

The rogue closed the tear with a thought and pried the weapon free from the severed arm's fingers before flashing the injured ogre a weak grin.

A loud shriek ripped free of the ogre's throat as it finally registered what had happened. It's stump spurted blood, and it tried to stop it with its free hand, but they both knew its fate was sealed.

Time for my final trick.

Pulling everything he could muster, he flung the ogre's wooden cudgel.

[void step(III) Shadow]

Levi slipped into his shadow and reappeared from that of the ogres. As expected, the ogre had opted to catch its cudgel rather than keep its focus on the rogue.

It was a big mistake, one that Levi did not hesitate to pounce on.

As soon as he emerged from the ogre's back, he backstabbed it so much his wrists began aching, but he kept at it until the ogre fell to the floor. Dead.

Only when he was satisfied did he turn his attention to the other ogre that had been burned by Xara's fireball earlier.

Even though his hood covered his face, Levi could have sworn that the ogre flinched as soon as he turned in its direction, and then it tried to flee, but Levi had no intention of giving it the chance to do so.

The rogue pushed up from the corpse of the ogre with a stump, ripping free his dagger from the ogre's spine as he charged at the one-eyed ogre. Its long strides could only take it so far before Levi caught up to it.

The rogue jumped on it, clinging to its back as his dagger went up in a deadly arc, and flew into its remaining eye. This time, the rogue didn’t stop until his dagger reached its brain, killing it instantly.

Levi pushed away from the body of the ogre as it began to fall to the floor.

His vision swarmed with dark spots, but he clung on to consciousness even as he involuntarily dropped to a knee. Levi resisted falling unconscious as his focus shifted from the ogre's corpse to the mage still fighting the two ogres.

The ogres were creeping up past the point that Levi judged to be comfortable for the mage. Just as he was about to rush into her aid, Xara threw a disc at one of the ogres, and it exploded in a burst of fire that began to consume the ogre greedily, and soon it was nothing but ash.

The remaining ogre thought to overwhelm the mage by charging at her now that she was not hurling fireballs, but it didn’t go to plan for the ogre as a metal… thing that Levi couldn’t identify appeared in the mage’s hands and before Levi could understand what was going on, a loud bang ecohed in the chamber.

When Levi looked at the ogre, its entire head was missing, and what remained of its neck was a smokey mess.

“Ow ow ow,” Xara hissed, tossing the metal thing to the floor, and staring at it in frustration evident on her face as it turned to molten slag.

Before he could mutter a word, her eyes turned to his and widened in concern as she rushed towards him. “Levi, are you okay?”

He nodded, “I just need a minute.” The mage pressed her body against his as she helped him up, and the scent of jasmine mixed with honey wafting off the mage was enough to make the pain that wracked his body recede, but it wasn’t enough to make him forget the question burning at the tip of his tongue.

“What the hell is that?” Levi asked, pointedly looking at the molten slag.

“It’s just a prototype.” As closely pressed together as they were, Levi didn't miss the way Xara’s cheek reddened at the question. “I call it the Xistol … patent pending.”
