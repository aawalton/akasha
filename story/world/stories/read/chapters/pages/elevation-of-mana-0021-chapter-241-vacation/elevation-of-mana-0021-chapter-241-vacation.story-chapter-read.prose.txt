Atal

Writing furiously I worked on the organization, putting people and things where they were meant to go. I wanted this done, yesterday, a year ago, and that it still sat upon a desk before me irked me to no end.

“Your Majesty, we have a missive from the south,” my assistant said.

“Show me,” I held out a hand and quickly read the note. It was a request for me to look over one of their proposals for a wall on a larger settlement.

“Hand this over to the engineering corps. I built them to handle this kind of thing, not to do it myself.”

“Of course, my apologies.”As he left Chien walked into the room. My longtime assistant was smiling, almost laughing.

“I swear if you've come to give me more work I'm going to throw something at you,” I said.

“No, not at all. How are the new departments working out?”“As they should, really should have done more of those before,” I grumbled.

It was true, over the past few months I'd been building out whole new departments and corps to handle a lot of the day to day stuff, even some of the specialty stuff. While they did that I worked like a madman on getting my own work done, shifting what I could to them.

“Boss, I get it, but you really do need a break, even if only a short one.”

I gave him a hard look. “I mislike it, letting her go without me, letting her head off to an unknown land with unknown people and potentially unknown monsters. Bah! I should never have consented.”“It wouldn't have mattered and we both know that boss,” he said, and I sighed at the truth of it.

“Still, letting my only child go without me there to support her stings,” I grumbled. “She always had the ability to run home should something go truly wrong, and now...”“Now you're going through the thing every parent goes through.”I laid back in my chair. “Yes.”

“She's an adult now boss, try to coddle her and she'll just resent you for it. You know that.”“I suppose, maybe the fact that my own childhood ended with me off on my own, and...”He didn't say it, we both knew that when I'd left it had been the last time I saw my parents. I didn't want my relationship with my daughter to end the same way.

“Well, there's only one thing for it,” I said after a time. “Finishing all my tasks here. How go the new airships?”“Better and bigger than ever. The golem prototypes are going well too,” he said, finding a chair and sitting down. “I've had to make some tweaks here and there on some of the ongoing prototypes, but I'll send you the new write-ups later. They're working fine.”“Good to hear,” I said. “That at least I couldn't trust to anyone but you or myself. Or Isha I suppose, but she dislikes doing those things.”

“You sure? You should ask her help, she is about as good at making magic items as either of us.”“And she hasn't wanted to make any in years,” I replied. “Though maybe I will, have to see how she feels about it.”

I wrote myself a note, it was easy not to think about something, but it was much harder to ignore a piece of paper sitting on my desk.

“Your break boss, a day, at least that. Do it as a request from me. I'll even be here to handle anything that comes in. Heck I might even be convinced to put my own list aside for yours for a day or two.”

Chien was seldom serious, but I could see in his eyes that he meant this, even through the smile. His eyes twinkled, but behind it there were words he didn't want ignored.

“Maybe, can you handle five? Perhaps Isha and I can take the time to step out of the city for a bit.”

“That was easier than I thought. I've got it,” he assured.

“I keep having to learn the same lessons again and again, and one of those is that when your friends tell you something, listen.”

When it was finally over I moved through the palace, for it was now a palace. I still had Atal's old one, but it was being converted into a public works building while my own home was expanded again and again. Sadly as I walked the halls it seemed I would never have enough space for all the people and work that needed doing, well, unless I started to push those offices out...

No, no more thinking about work. I needed to go and find my wife, which was easy enough.

“Good afternoon, love,” I said as I moved into her rooms, she too was looking over some papers that had been brought to her.

“I see Chien found you,” she said with a laugh.

“You knew what he was going to do?”“Who do you think sent him?”“Well... why didn't you speak to me? You know I'd give you anything you wanted, within reason, and sometimes outside of it.”“Because then husband you would only have been giving me what I want, not actually thinking about what you needed or was right.”

Without even noticing it I began to laugh, having to cover my face. “Seriously, you really do know me.”“So, our day.”“Five,” I replied.

“Five? My he did better than I anticipated.” She wrote something on a piece of paper and handed it off to one of her maids. “Well then, where are we going?”“Let's go somewhere away from people.”“I can do that.”
