As it was time for the first meeting of the research project, Ferris carried Jade to Elder Harper’s office.

When he arrived, Hemon led Ferris and Jade to a small conference room inside the same building.

Elder Harper was already in the room, speaking to a young woman. She didn’t look happy at what Elder Harper was saying. Seeing him enter, both turned to him.

“Ah, Adept Ferris Bluereef, may I introduce Adept Serena Hollowfield,” Elder Harper welcomed him, and gestured to the woman.

Serena greeted him with an… almost excited look. “Pleased to make your acquaintance.” She was taller than him and appeared a few years older in age. Strapped to her back was the shaft of a short bow, likely her bonded weapon. Most noticeably, she was missing her right hand, amputated just below the elbow joint. Ferris carefully ignored it, as he didn’t care for people who stared at his missing leg.

“Elder Harper,” Ferris greeted the Elder first, and then the woman. “Adept Serena, this is my familiar, Jade.” He held Jade up, still carrying the slime.

Jade, able to feel the focus of Ferris’ attention from the bond, jiggled a little.

Elder Harper stared intently at Jade for a couple seconds. Her eyes did not flash with light like Cyrus’ had, but Ferris would bet she was using some visual ability. The Elder’s was likely more advanced, and more subtle, than Cyrus’.

Ferris was ready with an explanation of the chain wrapped around Jade’s core, but the woman remained silent.

Hemon served tea, and then left the room as the three cultivators sat at a table. Ferris placed Jade on the table and sent the slime a mental picture of the area to stay inside. Jade immediately started exploring, stopping precisely at the boundary he had sent.

Elder Harper led the discussion. “Now, the goal of this project will be to research an ability manual for Acid Reconstruction, or a similar tier one acid ability able to heal limbs.”

“We will first study the slime’s, Jade’s, natural ability to reconstruct and heal the body after taking damage.”

Gesturing at Ferris, she continued. “Next, we will use the familiar bond for Adept Ferris to develop Acid Reconstruction.”

“Last, we will use that experience to create a technical manual, through which Adept Serena will acquire the ability and prove we can transfer it.”

Ferris wasn’t surprised, but it did clarify why Serena was here. The end goal of the research project was information that would let other cultivators learn the ability, so they needed someone to verify the process. Serena, with her missing hand, had clear motivation for the project’s success.

It also explained her excitement. She was in the same boat as him, although apparently she had already bonded.

Elder Harper had mentioned Clan Hollowfield had several martial traditions with acid core weapon bonds. Serena’s bow strapped to her back almost certainly had an acid core embedded inside.

“Previous studies of this nature had better results with consistent, regular focus over the long term rather than attempting to force it too quickly.” Elder Harper looked at Serena, some unsaid communication passed between them. “Adept Ferris, being newly bonded, will need time to progress his cultivation.”

“I expect you both to meet once a week, and work together on this project.” The woman took a sip of tea, making eye contact with both Ferris and Serena in turn. “Once a month, we will meet as a group to review progress and plan out next steps.”

“Now, Adept Serena has been investigating a healing ability for a couple months now. Please present your progress and current approach.”

Serena started to speak, but then glanced at Ferris. Elder Harper saw the look and reassured her, “Adept Ferris has been allowed access to the clan’s information on tier one healing abilities as part of his retainer contract.”

The young woman shrugged, and then began, “I started by investigating any references to acid core healing abilities. The library has a tier two ability that would work, but a closer examination showed that it is unlikely to be possible to reduce the tier requirement. The nature of the ability depends deeply on concepts that cannot be used at tier one.”

Ferris had no way of determining the tier of a cultivator just by looking, but this implied that Serena was still at tier one. The knowledge that the clan had a tier two acid healing ability was good to hear. In the worst case, Ferris might be able to find a different path than delving to advance, and then heal his leg later. However, that would be very slow. His decade-long contract might expire first. Serena wasn’t waiting around for tier two either, apparently.

Ferris didn’t blame her, either. He knew it was common for active delvers to take between five to ten years to move through the first tier and break into the second. And that was assuming they didn’t have any major problems. Beyond time spent working in the soul, cultivators needed resources to advance. Delving was typically the most profitable way to earn those resources—barring some great crafting talent or super rare ability.

Assuming Serena wasn’t already close to the second tier, her injury likely prevented her from earning the resources she needed to advance. Even if this research project took years to complete, that was likely faster than her other options.

“This brought me to the clan’s records of one Janice Landsworn, and her slime familiar Gumly.” Serena glanced at Jade, who was resolutely rolling around the limits of the table. “Janice had an observed ability she called Acid Reconstruction, although the records do not detail how she got it or the resource requirements.”

“Next, I compared this to other known tier one healing abilities, namely Revivifying Flame and Regrowth.” Serena’s research seemed very similar to the information Elder Harper had provided Ferris. It made sense, as she was looking for the same ability within the same library. She likely didn’t have an Elder streamlining the search for her, Ferris thought. How long had it taken her to reach the same results?

“Regrowth works by stimulating a biological process, while Revivifying Flame forms new matter fueled by fire. From the descriptions, Revivifying Flame sounded closer to Acid Reconstruction in concept, so I have been attempting to translate the process of learning the fire ability using similar cultivation resources with my acid core.”

“Revivifying Flame has two required abilities: Burning Blood and Fire Resistance,” Serena continued. “I have already developed Acid Resistance and am investigating potential substitutes for Burning Blood.”

Ferris saw an opportunity. “I will likely need Acid Resistance as well, before we can make progress,” he said, looking to Elder Harper. “I intend to make it a priority to purchase the manual for it, but it may take some time.”

Elder Harper looked back at Ferris, and Serena smirked. It wasn’t the most subtle way to ask, but Ferris figured it helped to express honest intention to purchase the ability… although he would rather receive it for free as part of the research project.

After a moment of consideration, Elder Harper responded, “While you will need to pay for it, afterwards Adept Serena may aid you in learning the ability.” Serena lost her smirk, but the Elder continued before she could protest. “The sooner he learns, the faster the research project will go, and this will help teach you how to work together.”

Serena sighed but looked down at her missing hand. “Yeah, I suppose I can help him out, if it moves things along faster. Better than waiting around hoping I luck out on a random ability.”

“Thank you, I appreciate your time.” Ferris tried not to smile too much. He hadn’t gotten what he wanted, but firsthand advice from another cultivator may be worth more than the twelve merit points to purchase the manual.

“Was there anything else?” Elder Harper asked Serena, who shook her head no. “In that case, we will start with two tasks for this first month. First, Adept Ferris will meditate while Adept Serena injures Jade, triggering the slime’s natural self-healing.”

The Elder turned to face him. “Adept Ferris, you will observe within Jade’s soul. Jade will almost assuredly have a version of Acid Reconstruction as a natural ability, but it will be specific to slime biology. Find it, and study it, and prepare a report for us. Pay particular attention to what concepts and effects are present. If you require instruction on the nature of abilities, First Steps of the Soul at the library has a section you may refer to.”

Ferris paled at the idea of hurting Jade. The slime should be fine… but a nauseous feeling still rose up in his gut.

He evened out his breathing, and repeated his new mantra to himself: I am the master of my own thoughts.

“Second,” the woman continued, as if not noticing his reaction, “you will both work on Adept Ferris learning Acid Resistance. Any questions?”

Trying to focus, he considered whether there was anything he could add. The Adaptation ability may help, but he didn’t know how it worked yet, and both Elder Harper and Serena already knew about it from the journal. It might make it easier for him to get Acid Reconstruction, but until he knew more about how it worked, he didn’t have anything substantial to add.

When neither of them responded, Elder Harper continued. “Then, I expect the two of you to find a time to meet once a week, and then report on the progress one month from today.”

“Adept Ferris,” she said, facing him fully, “Now that you have bonded, please see Hemon to set up your job assignment.”

With that she bid them goodbye, stood up, and left the room, leaving Ferris, Serena, and Jade alone.

After the door had closed, Ferris was subjected to the full force of Serena’s focus.

“Adept Serena, it seems we will be working together. I will be in your care,” Ferris broke the silence.

“It appears so, please, call me Serena,” she said.

“In that case, call me Ferris,” he responded, glad she was willing to be a bit more informal. It wouldn’t be appropriate with Elder Harper, given the gap in both cultivation and status. In his mind, Serena had been speaking a bit too casually with someone at tier three. However, it would be nice for him to have a more casual working relationship with Serena.

Now that he thought of it, Elder Harper hadn’t given a family name when introducing Serena. Gilbert ‘Booker’ Hollowfield had been oversharing a bit when introducing himself, either as a sign of welcome or as a boast. Maybe both.

However, this was a different situation than with Gilbert. They would be working together for a long time, and Ferris might actually need to distinguish Serena from someone else with the same name.

It wasn’t necessarily a problem, but not mentioning it was… odd.

She wasn’t a member of Hollowfield’s head family, as then she would have been ‘Serena al Hollowfield,’ with the oh-so-special ‘al’ in her name. Also, people like that couldn’t help making one hundred percent sure you knew who they were.

The two of them didn’t appear related, at least. Not that Ferris even knew Elder Harper’s family name to see if they matched.

More likely there was some other reason the Elder hadn’t mentioned it. In the end, he wouldn’t even say it was rude to hide it, as he was only a retainer. Serena hadn’t even been the one to neglect mentioning it.

Still, Ferris doubted it was unintentional. Not from what he had seen of Elder Harper.

“I heard you are from another bastion?” Serena asked.

“Yes, although Bluereef Bastion is much smaller than Scalefort. I am still adjusting to the number of people here.”

“What’s it like? I’ve never been to another bastion.”

“My home is on an island, surrounded by water on all sides in the Wild. We don’t have much space, but the nearby rifts have a tendency to produce some valuable resources. They are mostly underwater, and our delvers have specialized to take advantage of them. Other than that, we eat a lot more fish. The Hollowfield compound alone is larger than Bluereef Bastion, much less the rest of Scalefort.”

“I’ve seen a couple rifts with underwater sections, but my group has always exited or gone around them. I hear there can be some unique rewards if you can get to them.”

Ferris was excited to hear that. He would need some place to practice fighting underwater, after all.

“How long have you been delving?”

“Over a year now,” Serena frowned and looked off to the side, “although it has become harder recently.”

Ferris could imagine, intentionally not looking at her missing hand. He noticed she said it had become harder, not impossible.

Still, a year wasn’t long. No wonder she didn’t want to wait for the tier two healing ability.

Looking back at Ferris, and then down at Jade, Serena seemed to focus back on her goals.

“Well, the sooner we get started, the better. How about we begin?”

Jade, who had been following a path around the table, stopped and rolled back to Ferris. Some of his apprehension must have transferred through the bond. Although, Ferris wasn’t even sure if Jade would feel pain from simple cuts.

Blushing a little, Ferris realized that he couldn’t start yet. He wanted to, but Jade’s mental body was still recovering.

“I’m afraid that I can’t today, for personal reasons.” He didn’t want to explain that Jade had died to a fish in his soulspace, or that he didn’t trust the slime not to eat him while meditating. At least the concept of privacy when dealing with cultivation was well established.

Serena looked unimpressed; Ferris could see she was as motivated as him. Something about missing a limb appeared to encourage a person.

“Perhaps,” he said, “we could meet twice this week. There is so much to do, I would hate to disappoint Elder Harper. My contract is dependent on this project.”

Her eyes darted up. If Ferris had read the situation correctly, the Elder had cautioned her not to push too hard. He wasn’t sure of why, but Serena didn’t appear to agree.

For that matter, Ferris didn’t agree. This was the entire reason he was here. He wanted to push it hard.

“Yes,” she said slowly, “if you insist. A bound contract should not be neglected.”

“Then, my schedule is currently open. What days would work best for you?”

They found times that worked and left the room, neither mentioning that the plan had shifted from twice this week to twice every week.
