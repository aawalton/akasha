For the students selected for demonic cultivation within Star Gem Academy, they had a plethora of choices in terms of techniques. Li Lang stayed throughout each lesson despite his busy schedule. For one, he wanted to keep an eye on Niu Ben to assess when or if he needed another dose of Neuraflex. Second, he wanted to collect the samples of these new students from before and after they start practicing demonic techniques.

Almost all the students had a possession-type technique, absorbing the power of Qi beasts. It was the result of the young children hearing about the heinous crimes of demonic cultivators from childhood. None wanted to become the villain that hunted down the innocent.

It was a comforting sight from what would be the first pioneers of demonic cultivation from Star Gem Academy. They were the ones who would allow Li Lang to study their experiences. It would aid in his goal of creating a demonic cultivation technique that didn’t have any of its infamous side effects.

With over a dozen specimens and exclusive access to their transformation, it didn’t take long for Li Lang to spot some significant changes once they began practicing demonic cultivation.

“This—mutations were introduced to the NRG1 gene, NRG3 gene, and many others! None of the results are consistent with each other, even if they absorbed Qi from the same type of Qi beast!”

Li Lang wasn’t too worried about the variance right now, but he was startled by the consistent mutations that were brought onto his student’s DNA by simply cultivating for a few days. The number of changes was staggering, with its results unpredictable. They could be completely benign or fatal. The only saving grace was the information that demonic cultivators rarely suffered fatal accidents from cultivating. It wasn’t like orthodox cultivators who could lose their lives if they got too ambitious with their training.

He didn’t know how this was possible, now that he saw all the mutations before his eyes, but demonic cultivation seemed to have found a way to stabilize its practitioner despite all the invasive transformations.

To Li Lang’s relief, these changes settled down after the initial phase, but he didn’t relent in his careful monitoring of their condition. Still, he had other things to attend to, so he slowly delegated his role to his peers or older students more and more as time went on.

“In a week. I won’t take long, and if anything, Wei Ping can handle them while I’m gone.”

“He’s been busy operating the formation for willpower training and the immigration efforts into the new city, so give him some slack.”

“Hmm, did you maybe want to take some of the load off his—-”

“Yes, it’s time for us to go find your main body. I’m glad you’re excited about it.”

Now that he had obtained a heavenly earth as well, he needed some time to assess the changes it brought to his spiritual sea.

It appeared calm as usual, with a ring of light floating near the top, constantly flickering. The Qi flowing into his dantian was all pulled into it before it was allowed to fall into the sea. However, they were obviously outmatched by the Primordial Star Fire in every aspect. Now, that would no longer be the case.

Hmm, its output is still around the same. The heavenly element still has to hold back to uphold the delicate balance. I’ll need to find more powerful sources of wood and metal Qi if I want to increase my rate of improvement anytime soon.

Not long after he was done managing his spiritual sea, he was abruptly awakened by someone shaking him.
