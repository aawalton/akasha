Lysandra Quinn had never been a fan of war, but she was adept at it—what with her father having been a general back on Earth. It was war, after all, that had given her the burns and scars that refused to fade.

But that was a story for another time.

The point was simple: Lysandra was an excellent strategist, and she couldn’t have resisted the idea of a perfectly coordinated, perfectly synchronized strike team even if she tried.

The thought had been simmering in her mind ever since Elder Mournwind had called them a unit, and the moment the elders left, she had seized the opportunity to lead it.

To win, the alliance needed them to function as a team—to develop a bond before the war began—and what better way to achieve that than a simple round of introductions involving discussions about their home worlds, their incredible paths, and their abilities?

It felt like the first day of high school all over again.

Only this time, it was magical.

Flicking a golden lock behind her ear, she smiled brightly. “I specialize in reality swapping and perfect illusion layering.” Her smile widened. “It is an honor to meet you all.”

Their reaction was immediate and expected.

The men smiled, charmed almost instantly by her beauty and presence. The women not so much.

Lysandra couldn’t have that. A perfect unit required both the men and women firmly on her side.

So she glanced toward Seraphine and smiled. “Why don’t you introduce yourself next?”
