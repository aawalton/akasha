Like most times he picked up new skills, acquiring them didn't come with some unexplained influx of information. How helpful it would have been if the exact use for a skill had been beamed straight into his mind. Since that wasn't the case here, he did his best to figure out the skills while they ran.

Threads of Mana was no different as far as he could tell. In the chaos of lesser demons streaming in behind them and Luke's friends running alongside him, managing to see the weave proved impossible. More practice was still needed in that regard. Needle of Life was a lot easier to figure out. Before, it had been a small tip, not much larger than the point of a needle. Now, he could change both its shape and size with a thought. The largest size was still no bigger than a scalpel, but that was a huge improvement. With how many threads Luke could manage at that point, Needle of Life had just become an even more formidable weapon.

That left the new skills. Will of the Weaver did nothing. Well, he was sure it did something, but it was impossible to gauge in that moment. Weave Integration was the same story. Neither did anything at first glance, but Luke would not be deterred. Once he got into another life-and-death situation, which happened a lot more often than he'd like, the two new skills would shine.

Explosive Reach, like Needle of Life, was more straightforward. Activating the skill removed whatever limiter kept him from reaching farther with Threads of Mana. The skill slurped up half of Luke's mana in an instant, which was terrifying, but gave him a whopping four feet of added range. That meant Luke could tap into someone through Threads of Mana at a range of just over seven feet. Now, that was some serious improvement. When he first got Weaver's Grasp, the skill that allowed him to even attempt to use Threads of Mana without touching his patient, he hadn't managed more than an inch. The mana requirement was bonkers, though, so this was not a skill to be used all willy-nilly.

"There!" Ray shouted, pointing between two tall buildings in the distance.

At first, Luke didn't understand what he was seeing. The greater demon was just too large for him to comprehend when it stepped out from behind the right, fifteen-story building, its head almost reaching the top floor.

"Damn," Luke said, feeling a little weak in the knees after the large mana expenditure. That was it. No other reason.

"Don't stop," Curtis barked, grabbing Luke's arm to get him going again. "The lesser demons are still gaining on us!"

It was true. Perhaps some stopped to try to assault The Band's headquarters through the plaque somehow, but an endless sea of them were still in pursuit. If not for Curtis's and Hannah's efforts, the monsters, on their quick legs and fluttering wings, would have caught up at that point. The problem was that the group was running headfirst into even more lesser demons.

"Is there no end to those assholes?" Ray asked between panting breaths. "At this rate, we'll never get to finish Scrubs!"

"There's no electricity!" Hannah shouted back. "How are you even watching?!"

Ray raised his nose and harrumphed. "I've got my ways."

Luke (Party): "I'm going into the building. The last greater demon, I had to decapitate to put down."

Curtis (Party): "Against that huge shit, I think we'd better go into the building."

Ray (Party): "That's a terrible idea. It looks like it could make the building crumble with a single fart."

Hannah (Party): "Ray is right."

Ray (Party): "I am?"

Hannah (Party): "In general, no, but we can't all go into the building. We've been sent to help some people. We have to find them and do just that."

Hannah (Party): "Curtis and I should go up. You three stay down here and help."

She looked straight at Luke. "They might need healing."

Luke nodded and glanced over at Lemira, who'd been silent the entire way. Her expression was one of grim determination. There was something going on with her. That was only natural, of course, since they were fighting against some of her kind, but Luke made a note to ask her about it as soon as they found a window of calm, whenever that might be.

Luke (Party): "It's a plan. Ray, Lemira, and I will approach from the ground."

While he was unsure of how they would deal with the endless waves of lesser demons, he knew Hannah and Curtis wouldn't have been able to keep their darkness and shadow up for much longer either, not without getting dangerously low on mana. As he considered that point, a solution of sorts appeared. The enormous demon pointed to something in the far distance. It uttered a single unintelligible word, deep enough that the sound reverberated in Luke's chest, and that was it. It diverted the entire lesser demon army, and they turned, yipping and growling, down a side street and disappeared. Not just those chasing after the group, either, but those in front.

With the street now far less crowded, bodies became visible near the greater demon's bus-sized, clawed feet. No, not bodies, not all of them. A few still stirred. Luke ignored the huge demon's gaze and hurried in, several threads of mana reaching forward, ready to heal.

A loud rumble sounded from above as the demon spoke, but it was impossible to make out what it was saying. Either way, Luke had more important things to focus on. Through Weaver's Perception, he sensed that two out of six were dead, with a third teetering on the brink. The three remaining downed Integrated wouldn't make it without intervention, but they had a few more moments. Focusing on the one who was worst off, a woman with enough muscles Luke thought she might be a bodybuilder or something, he set to healing the massive internal injuries. The demon must have stepped on her or something, because there wasn't much left. It was a miracle she still drew breath. This meant there wasn't much Luke could do without relying on Weaver's Renewal.

Glancing at the massive demon's leg, where Lemira's newest pet lion-monster-thing was attempting to climb, Luke sighed. He really didn't want to use up so much mana, but there was no other way, and he wasn't about to leave this poor woman to die.

Hannah (Party): "We're on top of the building now. Standing right in front of the monster's face isn't... great."

Curtis (Party): "The smaller demons are on their way to two other big ones way off in the distance. I think those are fighting."

Lemira (Party): "Demons are not known for their spirit of cooperation."

Luke healed the woman before it was too late. Groaning at losing almost half his mana, he left her there on the ground after extracting some from his patient for his troubles. It wasn't much, but every little bit helped. The others needed help, too. One was bleeding out from several long rips across his chest and legs. The cuts were deep, but with his new and improved Needle of Life, Luke was able to close them without too much trouble, and a great deal faster than before. A larger needle allowed for wider stitching. The scar wouldn't be as pretty, but that was not something he took into consideration when saving lives. As with the first patient, he took as much mana from this one as he dared. Since both were fighters, it amounted to no more than ten percent or so, but the next two looked like mages, so perhaps they had a little more to offer.

"Luke!" Lemira shouted, and he looked up to see a huge demon foot raised.

He cursed and grabbed hold of the two he'd healed before weavestepping backwards. It wouldn't be enough, so he pushed off with his feet and weavestepped again. Crashing to the hard ground while carrying two unconscious people was not pleasant, but it beat the alternative. One of the two remaining injured Integrated had just been crushed. The other lay still right next to the foot and rolled a little to the side when their massive opponent turned toward the tall building next to it, where Hannah and Curtis were in the process of attacking.

It was too far up to make out any details, but it was clear the demon hadn't even meant to kill that unfortunate Integrated, or even attack Luke. They were just collateral from a simple step.

"Are you hurt?" Lemira shouted.

"I'm fine," Luke said, getting to his feet. Stumbling forward, he reached the final injured Integrated, a young woman with short black hair wearing a workout top, running tights, and a glittering tiara. The headgear had to be a magic item, or something, to go with the wand she still clutched in the palm of her hand. This one bled internally from a number of holes in her heart, injuries he couldn't fathom the reason for. It didn't matter, though. The cure was always the same. Stitches! Her Meta-Heart was a sad, deflated thing, too, which forced Luke to part with some of his own mana to jump-start the metaphysical organ. If he guessed it right, she must've cast too many spells and drained herself of mana. Stupid.

Once he'd healed the patient and hauled her over to the pile he'd made of her friends, Luke called out to Lemira. "Can you get them somewhere safe on that thing you're riding?"

"It's a llemx!" she shouted, directing the pet toward the pile of injured with practiced ease. "And yes, I can!"

"Right!" Luke shouted over his shoulder as he ran toward the demon. "Llama! It doesn't even have antlers!"

"Llemx!"

He couldn't help but laugh. The sound and any cheer he'd worked up died when he approached the demon. How the hell was he supposed to bring down this thing?
