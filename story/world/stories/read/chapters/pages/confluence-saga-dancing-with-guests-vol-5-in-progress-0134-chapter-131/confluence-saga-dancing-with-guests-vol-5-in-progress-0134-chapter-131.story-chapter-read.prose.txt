"Fae!?" Anya said.

"Fair Folk?" Seb asked quietly a moment behind her.

She didn't ask, she knew he'd feel her confusion and clarify what he could. They hadn't said Fair Folk, they'd said Fae. Which still didn't answer what the word meant or why Gwen had said it as though it was supposed to mean something.

"How do you know that version?" Cara asked sharply.

Anya glanced at her then looked more carefully. She'd noticed the changes to Cara's hair, nearly dismissed it as a trick of the light, as something to ask about later. The light spikes within it looked nearly solid, dangerous while still looking like hair.

"Cara," Gwen said.

"No Gwen. No one uses that term anymore. Awakened shouldn't know any term, not unless they've had an encounter. He's too young to have encountered that name."

"He's not a threat," Gwen said with a sigh. "I'm sure there's a good reason he knows that word."

"Fair Folk?" Seb said again. "You mean…"

"Yes," Gwen said. "Fair Folk. That word hasn't been used among Awakened in hundreds of years."

Seb?

Stories, he answered. Shallow stories.

"Would my being Adult Awakened change anything?" he said. "Myths and stories about things that aren't supposed to be true. Stories about beings that aren't human. Fairy Tales is another word for it."

Anya tensed as Cara shifted forward. She didn't feel threatened, she didn't feel as though anything was going to happen, but the movement was the promise of a threat.

"Fairy tales?!" Cara asked before starting to giggle. "Fairy tales. They tell stories about us and call us all fairies?"

"Cara…"

"No Gwen. They would think you're a fairy, that Bern…"

Anya watched as Gwen dissolved into giggles next to Cara. She could tell Seb was just as confused as she was. They were Fae, they were concerned he knew the term Fair Folk, but when he said Fairies they laughed? This made no sense. And they were family in some way?

I don't know love, Seb sent. Hopefully they'll explain.

And you'll find out you know the word and everything else is wrong. That you were wrong and it was all a lucky guess that meant nothing.

I never guessed Fae existed, he sent back. I recognized the word, that's all.

You'll be wrong.

I'll tickle you.

She didn't answer with words, only emotions. A moment later he flushed and she forced herself to take a deep breath to try and keep calm. Hopefully whatever Gwen wanted to tell them, what she needed to explain, wouldn't take too long.

~ * ~

Seb wasn't sure what to think. No, that wasn't true. He knew what to think about Anya, about their shared moments, but there wasn't anything either of them could do about those thoughts, especially not with strangers in their room. What he was stuck on was Fae being real.

If they were real why had no one mentioned them? Why did Anya not even know the term? Why was his recognizing the word and context such a shock? He could understand Affinities being a secret until you passed the wall, but an entire culture, or, thinking back to what Gwen had first asked at dinner, an entire species, being a secret? That didn't-

Fay knew, Anya sent.

Her words broke his spiral, her irritation spiking as she continued.

She all but told us. She couldn't explain until we knew the word. We had to find out for ourselves and then she could talk.

But if she knew, how did she find out?

Seb…

Right, Seer stuff.

"Seers know, don't they," Anya said.

Seb saw Gwen take a deep breath and try to focus through her laughter. Cara's hand was covering her mouth, her shoulders still shaking as she fell silent.

"Some," Gwen agreed. "Those who have met us, or seen traces of our passing. And those who are likely to be exposed to records that mention us."

"Fairies…"

"Cara, please."

"Sorry."

"Why is it so funny?" Seb asked.

"As I said," Gwen began. "We are Fae. Different Fae groups, lineages, species, depending who you ask, are referred to by different names. Cara is a Fairy, Bern, Morgan and I are not."

"Keldren?" Anya said.

"Is complicated. Technically Fae from an outside point of view."

"His kind was designed for one purpose," Cara said.

"Cara!"

"You said family, your family can know."

"Designed? As in bred?" Seb asked.

"Yes," Gwen said with a frown. "Under the oversight and guidance of ancients who used their Paths to ensure they became what they are today. It is not done anymore, it is not considered appropriate."

Seb?

He could feel Anya's confusion. He wasn't sure he understood fully but the implication was there. Especially after Gwen's clarification that they no longer did so.

Shaped. She's saying that they were manipulated and forced into what they are.

"Why?" Anya asked.

"They needed troops. At least that's what I was taught," Gwen said.

Seb didn't want to understand, didn't want to see that answer as valid. And yet he couldn't argue it, he didn't know what had happened at the time, let alone what actually had been done. He could tell that Anya had her own opinion, that they'd likely discuss it later, or more likely ask Fay.

"Cara said you'd explain," Seb said.

"I will," Gwen said before hesitating. "I just don't know where to start."

"Family," Cara said.

"Fae," Anya said.

"What happened with Cara when she choked," Seb said.

We don't know what Fae are, Anya sent.

We also don't know what happened there, or when they reacted to you reacting to my full name, or how they knew I came up with the name Anya, he answered. Things are happening around them.

I still want to know.

You get to tell Fay you didn't let her be the one to explain.

That's- Anya sent as she shifted on her feet. Fine. We find out what's going on.

"You two…" Gwen began. "You never drop the band, do you."

"No," Seb said. "We try not to make it too obvious or ignore others."

"It's cute," Cara said. "Strange, but cute."

"Can we sit?" Gwen asked. "Did they give you anywhere to?"

"Second bedroom," Anya said. "Or we can sit on the floor here?"

"Floor," Cara said before dropping as though there was nothing to it.

"Floor it is," Seb said as they joined her.
