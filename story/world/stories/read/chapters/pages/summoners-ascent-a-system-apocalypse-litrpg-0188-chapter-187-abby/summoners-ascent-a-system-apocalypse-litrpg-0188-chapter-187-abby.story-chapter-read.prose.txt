A long, stretched out breath left Ashton's mouth toward the end of his chant, as he channelled more magic into the summoning. During the process, something came falling through that shifting, weirdly compressed space above the contract circle. Ashton saw this the first time when summoning the Salesman, but that didn't make it any less tantalizing to him.

But what Ashton was actually more interested at this very moment was that thing that 'fell through' earlier. It wasn't anything with a direct form, it was more like the shadow that passed by in the corner of your eye when you weren't quite paying attention.

***

[You have formed a contract with a [Living Abode]!]

[[Living Abode] is being registered in the [Construct Summoning] Category]

[Skill | Summoning - Level 11 -> Summoning - Level 12]

***

Almost immediately as the notifications popped up, Ashton could feel the mundane thoughts of the Living Abode. Just like the Pen of Insight when it was first created, there was barely more than the raw idea of thought and awareness, though its mind was immediately stronger. That must be because it was a Low-grade summon instead of a Lowest-grade.

"Is it done?" Millie asked curiously, and Ashton quickly nodded.

"Mm-hmm," Ashton smiled as he approached the tent, putting his hand onto the outside. Both to simply pet the Living Abode, and also to see whether or not the tent's mana pattern showed that the enchantments were active. And since they were, Ashton quickly turned to Millie with some explanations. "Now, try not to get it dirty if possible. And if you see anything like a tear or rip, let me know immediately."

"Okay!" the girl nodded excitedly, smiling up at the tent. "Nice to meet you, uh..."

"Living Abode."

"That's its name?"

Ashton shook his head. "No, it just doesn't have one yet. I explained how the mind of construct summons comes from within the veil, right?"

"Right... the same as the elementals."

"Exactly. For different reasons, both of them take a while until they can really get a name. Elementals still simply want to find themselves and figure out who they are. The same is the case for constructs, but they start a step back, since they barely understand the concept of a name when they're first born anyway," Ashton explained. "When they're ready, they're either going to announce their name to us, or ask us to name them. It's probably fine if you want to use a nickname for now, but once they get a proper name, please call them that, alright?"

"Of course! It's really mean not to. There was this one girl in the children's home until last year, she came when I was really young still because her parents kicked her out when she told them she was a girl," Millie pointed out, mimicking Ashton by petting the side of the tent. "She would always get super sad when you would call her by her old name. So I know that's just mean."

"Names are an important thing. Especially when you're dealing with magic. So that's a good stance to take," Ashton explained.

"I'll call you... Abby for now, okay?" Millie said. "Tell us when you want to call you something else."

Ashton smiled as he walked into the tent. There wasn't any change in here quite yet, but there would be with time, as the Living Abode's level increased.

The base of the tent itself had acted as the offering, as was the case with all construct summons. Everything that was inside didn't directly count, but by channelling the Living Abode, Ashton was able to change that and link all these objects to the tent. Like that, the furniture would be magically changed as the Living Abode's level went up.

That meant the stove, the chest, the bed, the table and the seating pillows, as well as the carpet were going to be magically tied to the tent once Ashton channelled the tent and activated its ability, and couldn't be normally taken out.

Of course, they could be taken out, but it needed an equivalent exchange, either in the form of another piece of similar furniture or magic stones. If there wasn't an exchange, then it would drain a bunch of Ashton's mana. But beside those things, anything that was brought in could be taken out without any issues.

And so, Ashton decided to quickly get it over with and upgrade the Living Abode for the first time.

As Ashton channelled the construct, he could practically feel every fibre that made it up. He could sense everything that was within the tent, which wasn't exactly useful right now, but would be incredibly useful as it grew in the future. But beyond that, Ashton could also sense the Living Abode's 'potential'. The ways it could grow, chambers that could form within it, and things that could be linked with it. And even beyond that, things that it could possibly form from simple ideas.

Right now, there wasn't a lot yet. The biggest real potential was that of 'expansion', which Ashton could do by supplying magic stones, at least for the time being. It would keep getting more expensive, and soon, other materials had to be used. But as more materials were introduced, the more the Living Abode would learn.

All in all, although from the outside this tent would keep changing in slight ways here and there, it wouldn't really get much larger. It would stay an unassuming tent. Well, unless Ashton did something to change that as well, but that wasn't actually in his plans, at least for now.

The first thing Ashton tagged was the bed. The entire thing, including pillows, mattress, frame, and duvet cover. Just by supplying a bit of mana, Ashton was able to connect the simple, probably a little uncomfortable, bed to the tent. One after another, he did the same with the carpet, stove, table, chest, and all the pillows.

This part was relatively simple, and there wasn't any noticeable change just yet, but there were immediate benefits. With just a bit of mana supplied, most minor damage could be repaired. If the damage was a bit more extreme, maybe Ashton would have to add in some other materials into the mix, though.

And now that there were more things that the Living Abode knew about, new potential had been formed. This time, he focused on the bed's potential specifically.

There were a lot of things he could do, but all the directions were very vague. Ashton had a pretty good idea what he could do. He could change this single bed into a different type—such as a double, a bunk, or even something crazy like one of those murphy beds—, he could change the material of the blankets, pillows, and mattress, but he had to 'teach' the Living Abode about those things first.

Right now, it was a blank slate that could develop in numerous ways. It would grow and shift and shrink and then shift some more, adapting to the needs of whoever was inhabiting it.

For now, what Ashton wanted to do was pretty simple. He really just wanted to upgrade the materials of the bed a bit. It was fine to sleep in for Ashton, but it was one of those small camping beds that wasn't exactly fitting for a kid like Millie. It was one of those rubber-covered mattresses, the blanket was thin and rough, and the pillow was fairly small.

Luckily, Ashton had brought his own sleeping stuff, since he figured he'd have to sleep at the school for a bit.

He quickly tagged basically all of his sleeping things. The blanket he used at Calibur, his sleeping bag, the blow-up mattress that he grabbed somewhere just in case, and of course the big, comfortable, soft pillow that he took with him. It had taken up quite a bit of space in the backpack, but it felt worth it anyway.

With this, Ashton was able to make some first adjustments. The blanket could be made softer, the pillow got larger and there was more than just one on the bed, and the frame and, most importantly the mattress got quite a bit larger as well and no longer had that rubbery cover.

He did have to use some of his stuff as an 'ingredient' to change the bed, but since there were leftover materials from that process, he should be able to make himself another one later as well. All the things that were part of the Living Abode could be duplicated easily enough.

Plus, the construction and upgrades didn't take the same amount of materials as they would if it was done manually, since a lot of it was subsidized by magic, so a little could really go a long way.

"Hm, maybe I should change the bedframe's material later, too..." Ashton whispered. The metal wasn't bad, but it was easier to get wood for repairs or upgrades.

"That's so cool...!" Millie whispered with an excited look on her face, quickly jumping on the bed. "How did you do that?"

"It's part of the Living Abode's innate magic," Ashton explained, smiling proudly. "Apparently, really, really long ago, the first proto-Living Abode, that I know about, was born in a super old house that was beloved by an old man who had lost his spouse. The man, who used to fix up all the damage that built up over time, at some point couldn't anymore. And the house saw that, and began to fix itself. When the old man complained about back pain from the worn-out mattress, it suddenly became springier again. As a summoner, I can take that innate ability and guide it."

"That's so cool," Millie said as she properly laid down under the blanket. "What else can you do with Abby?"

Ashton grinned, feeling a little smug that he was called cool. "I'll make some more changes once you go to sleep. By the time you wake up, this place will look a bit different."

Millie nodded, though with a slightly annoyed face. She clearly wanted to watch. But Ashton didn't know what else to do, either. Making these changes took a lot of magic, so he couldn't do too much all at once.

Obviously, as the grade of a summon went up, as did the effort that it took to actually channel their abilities.

"Hm, but I'm not..." Millie let out a long, stretched-out yawn that she was trying her best to hide, "... tired yet."

"Sure you're not," Ashton replied, smiling as he watched the girl snuggle up in the bed. Even if this still wasn't the best setup, this was definitely a lot better than what the people here at the school have had to deal with. "Then how about I just talk to you about parts of my past? There's a particular story that I wanted to tell someone about."

Millie raised her brows and slowly nodded. "Okay!"

"Alright. Well, to start with... From the view of this world, just a few hours before the system came here, I became the summon of a being in another world, against my will. I spent a really, really long time there, though when I finally came back no time had passed at all," Ashton explained as he started to get out some materials that he would need to start sewing later. "That world was called Lumia. My Summoner made things really, really terrible for me. But Lumia itself had quite a lot of beautiful parts, even if it was absolutely crazy. There were fruit as large as buildings, or snakes that would put a train to shame. Forests with trees whose crowns were hidden by clouds."

As Ashton spoke, he slowly focused on one of the messages that he had kept around him. He usually kept one message from each of the constellations around even when they weren't actively talking. Somehow, through them, he was able to tell if they were paying attention. And right now, a particular one of them was, so Ashton could continue with this story.

"One day, a long time after I first came there, the Summoner decided that they needed something from the demon realm," he continued. He figured it might be better not to use words like 'hell', at least not until Ashton had the chance to explain the concepts a bit more to Millie. "Some material, some particular plant. Something that they couldn't just summon. So instead, they just tore open a gateway to hell, had a summon grab what was needed. But just before the gate was closed, something else tumbled through. A young, then shapeless, demon."

"A demon? Was it scary?"

Ashton laughed, shaking his head. "No, no, she was downright adorable. She was a sort of blood demon. If you need an image in your head, then something like a vampire. But back then, she was just a child that had fallen into another world, and was scared beyond belief, just like I was. The summoner forced her into a contract, and I was made to take care of her," Ashton smiled, reminiscing as he started at the Daughter's message. The thread of connection to her was shaking.

"As I said before, names are an important thing to demons, you know? As they are to a lot of beings. Most of them don't have one, though. But someday, that little girl asked me for a name, and I gave it to her. The Summoner was mad that I named her, but what was done was done. After that, she changed a lot more to look like a human, surprisingly. She really ended up fitting that image of 'vampire' pretty well by the time we were all freed, even if she was still a kid to me back then."

Millie yawned again, though she hid it less this time. "What name did you pick for her?"

Ashton was focused on nothing else right now but that link to the Daughter of the Scarlet Heart.

"For a little girl who fell into a wild, crazy new world? I figured there was no better name than Alice."
