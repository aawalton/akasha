As James landed, he also controlled his bones with Full Body Control.

His arms suddenly turned porcupine-like, his ulna, radius, and humerus, as well as every bone of his fingers shattering and sprouting forth from his skin and instantly regrowing beneath where it shattered. Then the bones surrounded themselves with Fire Mana.

“Surprise!” James lunged at Zhang Chāo across the short distance that now separated them, aiming to impale and burn him simultaneously.

The Heavenly King failed to dodge aside, James’s arm blades penetrated through his stomach and arm, and—What? What is this?

His blades had clearly pierced through Chāo’s body, so why did what he was holding feel so insubstantial?

James’s eyes narrowed a fraction of a second before it happened. The figure he was stabbing popped like a balloon, revealing the real Zhang Chāo a few feet behind him.

“Surprise,” Chāo whispered.

James only heard it because the entire sky—and perhaps the whole arena around himself and Zhang Chāo—had grown eerily quiet.

“When did you—”

“When you took your eyes off me for a second.”

Even as they exchanged those few words, the Emperor was maneuvering to lunge across the short distance that separated himself from Zhang Chāo. But the angle was wrong. James had started to fall backward, so he made wings sprout from his back, using his skin as the material. Zhang Chāo took two small steps before James could start to close the distance, and the Mana that had gathered all around him suddenly dissipated.

Shit.

James felt it as the entire world around him shifted. He knew what was happening.

His superhuman perception tracked everything as the environment changed to a beautiful, picturesque setting—his own potential city of the future in Florida, back in the heart of his empire.

Then the illusion shattered.

“What?” Zhang Chāo exclaimed, his sword half out of its sheath. “Not even for a second?”

James pounced in that moment, his bony appendages still sharp and coated in fire.

Zhang Chāo was paralyzed with surprise for just long enough to suffer a flurry of bone blade stabbings and burns.

He let loose a roar at James, and then the King pushed off of the Emperor, hurling himself back. Supported by his wings, James took a single step forward and followed Chāo with Warp Step. He stabbed him again and again, perforating Zhang Chāo’s body with small wounds that added up.

The King’s body glowed, and then he seemed to explode outward, the force of the blast throwing James backward. But it wasn’t powerful enough to cause the Emperor any harm. He closed the distance with Warp Step again, expecting to inflict more stab wounds.

As he reappeared, he saw a flash of Chāo’s glowing eyes, twin bursts of electricity, and then James found himself blind. This time, it was him who took a defensive posture, raising his arms to defend against the expected surprise attacks.

But the strikes to take advantage of his sudden inability to see never came. He sensed that Zhang Chāo had retreated.

Damn it, I gave him room to recover!

As his eyes were growing back, James used his creatures from down in the swamp to get an angle on what was happening. Looking through their eyes, he saw Zhang Chāo lunging across the air toward him, the worst of the King’s bleeding at an end, sword in hand.

James held himself very still, pretending he didn’t know what was going on but rapidly gathering Mana. The accumulation of his own Mana, unlike his opponent’s, was invisible thanks to Hidden Magic.

So when Zhang Chāo moved into striking range, he was taken completely by surprise as he was suddenly yanked downward, off course, causing his swordstroke to miss its target completely.

“What…?” was all he could manage before James was descending down after him, one of the Emperor’s eyes fully regrown.

Zhang Chāo swung at James as he dropped down just above Chāo, but James flattened his wings out and pulled up slightly, leaving himself just out of range. Zhang Chāo tried to move in James’s direction, but he was under the effect of James’s Precision Gravity Control, so his footsteps in the air only gave him half of the upward momentum he expected, and he was pulled back down toward the swamp almost as quickly as he tried to leap after James.

More.

James increased the gravitational pull tugging at Chāo, and he further ramped up the pressure by directing swamp geysers to rush up to meet him, forcing Zhang Chāo to dodge and spin around the geysers rather than focusing on gaining elevation again.

As Chāo fell, James felt victory settling into his grasp.

Then the shoulders of Zhang Chāo’s armor burst open, and a pair of large dragon wings appeared.

They flapped a single time and reversed Chāo’s direction of travel.

Shit.

The wings flapped again and brought Chāo almost up to James’s height.

Roscuro, transform into a giant mace!

The Soul Eater began to move in compliance immediately.

Predictably, Zhang Chāo rose to James’s height, smiling, sword still drawn. They locked eyes.

Soul-Freezing Gaze!

James immediately felt the backlash but ignored it. He swung the giant mace that had just formed in his hands and smashed Chāo’s left wing. As Chāo tumbled away, James sensed the King begin to move again, the paralysis having worn off in less than a second. But the damage was done.

He could not fly with one broken wing.

And even though it began to heal even while the Emperor was looking at it, the wing would not become functional again quickly enough to get Chāo out of this trap.

James forced himself to keep watching as Zhang Chāo tumbled into the water, to make sure he actually landed there instead of pulling some other trick out of his bag. He reached out with his powers to take hold of Chāo with the swamp, and he began pulling him downward. He didn’t imagine for a moment that drowning would kill the Heavenly King, but the further he sank into the swamp, the more vulnerable he would be. Chāo was more powerful in the air, after all.

The Emperor took a moment to wipe the blood from his face. He had started bleeding from the eyes after he used Soul-Freezing Gaze on Zhang Chāo, but the minor injury was easily worth it.

I don’t know if it’ll work on him a second time, but I’ll keep it in reserve in case I need it.

Within the swamp, James’s aura turned every bit of water and soil into his eyes and ears.

He watched eagerly as the monsters under his control all swarmed Chāo and attacked him, ripping at his wings, his arms, his legs, his head, anywhere they could land a hit, inject venom, or try to melt him with acid.

Zhang Chāo kept his composure. He swung in a series of swift circles around his body, using his magic sword to slash all of his enemies over and over as they struck. His flesh was blackening in a few places where venom had been injected, but he shredded almost all of James’s monsters, and despite their healing Skills, James felt that none of them were piecing themselves back together except the slimes.

Even the slimes somehow seemed to take damage from contact with the sword, remaining dented as if a sword blade was still pressed into their flesh. The closest to an unscathed non-slime monster was Doppelganger, who only lost an arm to Zhang Chāo’s sword before James silently commanded the monster to retreat.

James reached out and began rapidly dropping the temperature around Chāo and making the swamp water heavier and denser. The trick hadn’t worked on Han Jianguo, but the Heavenly King was not as physically robust as his number one subordinate.

And James pulled out a trick against Chāo that he hadn’t been able to use against Jianguo.

Complete Illusion Magic… Witness your worst fears come to life.

For a few seconds, James couldn’t sense any effect.

Then Chāo seemed to genuinely lose his edge for the first time in their bout.

He slashed at the water violently, angrily, as if he was surrounded by enemies again and wanted to cast them down. Perhaps he just assumed that under the cover of the illusions, more monsters were coming to attack him. Or he might have genuinely lost his awareness of where he was and what was going on. James’s Complete Illusion Magic was incredibly powerful. There was no way to know for certain.

For a minute, the area around the King turned into a small whirlpool of flurried activity, Chāo’s blade cutting through seemingly every particle of mud around him in quick succession.

James dropped into the water himself during this sequence of frenzied movements. It was time to look for his opening to strike. Zhang Chāo would be slowed after this, by the exertion of these rapid-fire attacks and by the denser and increasingly cold swamp water all around his body.

When the disturbed water calmed, however, James sensed that the Mandate of Heaven had been replaced in Chāo’s hand by another weapon. This one was a monk’s staff topped by metal rings rather than a sword.

Worse, Zhang Chāo seemed to have recognized that whatever he’d been seeing and hearing under the influence of Complete Illusion Magic was not real.

James distinctly detected him mouthing the word “Lies” to himself.

That’s the trouble with illusions, the Emperor thought. They have to be believed to be fully effective, but the most believable illusions aren’t unsettling enough to be properly distracting. But they do have their uses. He’s tired, he’s still off-balance, he’s bleeding in the water, he’s poisoned, and for some reason, he’s even put away his sword.

Then the staff began to extend, stretching down into the swamp water.

Oh. That’s why.

In an instant, James rushed through the swamp in a sudden streak of lightning motion, Lightning Mana running through him, Roscuro transforming into a long sword as he moved. He could ignore the water resistance, because this was his domain. He was not slowed by the increased weight of the water.

James needed to either finish the fight here or at least disarm Zhang Chāo of that weapon before it allowed him to climb back out of the swamp. The King would still be at a disadvantage outside of the water, but it would be much less of one. Just as the Heavenly King was stronger in the air than in water or on the ground, James was more powerful within the swamp as the Ruler of the Dark Waters. He could feel that power surging through him now, accelerating his regeneration, rejuvenating him and empowering his strike.

The Emperor aimed a mighty slash at Zhang Chāo’s staff-wielding arm, he drew to within striking range, he swung the Soul Eater sword—and James felt a blade impale him through the chest.

It didn’t stop his own attack, which glided through the water as smoothly as a hot knife cutting through butter. The blade slashed Chāo’s arm, cutting it all the way down to the bone.

But the sword in James’s chest was an unpleasant surprise. It was the old, familiar, overpowered sword that Zhang Chāo had held onto for most of this fight, the Mandate of Heaven.

When did he…? No. I see. He never put it away.

As he looked, James saw that the Mandate of Heaven sprouted from the monk’s staff he had been looking at, growing out of it like a branch from a tree.

All along, that sword was a transforming weapon, too. I shouldn’t be so surprised.

He pulled back, just a foot or so, to get the blade out of his chest. The sword hadn’t been aimed at his heart, nor had the blade struck there, but the wound was still bleeding profusely, far more than it should.

As blood filled the water, Zhang Chāo smiled, and his staff continued to stretch down toward the ground that lay somewhere beneath the reach of James’s Dominion.

This is still my best chance to finish this, James thought. That arm is only barely attached. If I can sever it and disarm him, he won’t be able to escape the swamp.

Ice crystals were forming all over Zhang Chāo’s body, freezing swamp water that would slow any subsequent movements. He was only clinging to the Mandate of Heaven for his victory now.

I can do this. One more attack, with everything I’ve got.

James gathered a huge quantity of Mana for a multipronged approach.
