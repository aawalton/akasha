Arthur Venyar, Village Chief of Vantara, had received the report only moments earlier.

One of his hunting teams had encountered a group claiming to be from the Resistance—armed, disciplined, and led by a young man. That alone had stirred doubt within him.

The Resistance rarely interfered in remote regions like Vantara. Their operations were usually concentrated around strategic cities, supply lines, and contested borders—not tribal settlements hidden within the outer curve of the Naga Forest.

But then came the second detail that made his doubts about them impersonating the Resistance vanish completely.

They had shown a seal. And he knew that a seal wasn't something an ordinary member could carry, but rather someone who held authority within the Resistance.

Proof… that made him take it seriously.

That shifted his thoughts entirely.

Perhaps Vantara's border position had made it relevant to some larger operation. Or perhaps they sought forest resources—rare herbs, beast materials—like adventurers sometimes did.

And then came the third detail.

One among them possessed strength that could rival his own. That alone was enough. If such a force had arrived at his gates, he could not simply remain seated within his hall and summon them like strangers.

Respect demanded presence.

So, he came personally.

Now, standing before the timber gate of Vantara, Arthur waited.

Shortly thereafter, the Resistance group emerged from the thinning forest path.

Arthur stepped forward.

"Greetings," he said calmly, his voice steady yet firm. "I am Arthur Venyar, Village Chief of Vantara."

Yet as he spoke, his gaze had already fixed upon one figure.

The young man at the front.

There was something… familiar.

The build. The posture. The restrained composure. Even the way he carried himself—not arrogant, not subservient—simply assured.

Arthur's mind stirred with memory.

Years ago, when he had left Vantara to see the wider world and temper his strength through travel, he had encountered a figure whose presence still lingered in his memory like a brand burned into steel.

Mysterious.

Powerful.

Unfathomably calm.

That man's strength had left an imprint upon Arthur's mind that time had never erased.

And now—this young man bore the same features. The same quiet weight.

The same… feeling.

Before Arthur could dwell further, the young man stepped forward and bowed slightly.

"Greetings, Village Chief Arthur," he said. "I am Vector Astrum."

Astrum.

The name struck Arthur like a stone dropped into still water.

His doubt shifted sharply toward belief—but belief required confirmation. He could not assume recklessly. He knew too well that the force associated with that name preferred to remain hidden. Their existence was known only to a rare few.

Arthur had been one of those few.

Because he had once traveled beside Velric Astrum. He had become a friend—or comrade—with him, or at least, as equal as one could stand beside someone like him. But he had to leave Velric's shadow after a time to take command of his village. Vantara, being in the Naga Forest, was always prone to danger. So, someone powerful needed to guard it.

Arthur's voice lowered slightly as he asked carefully, "Do you perhaps know Velric?"

He watched closely.

If the young man knew the name—truly knew it—then his reaction would not be false.

The moment the name left his lips—

The air changed. A suffocating pressure enveloped him. It did not come from the young man. It came from the figure standing just behind him.

Arthur's instincts flared.

Third Stage.

No—

Stronger.

At the peak of Third Stage. Or perhaps already stepping into the Fourth.

His senses screamed warning.

Simultaneously, hostile gazes locked onto him from the group. Weapons did not move—but tension sharpened like a drawn blade.

Now it was clear that they were related to Velric and the Astrum family.

So, Arthur hastily waved his hands and explained to their hostile gazes, "Do not misunderstand me," he said quickly, his tone steady but urgent. "Velric is a friend. He bore the same family name—Astrum."

The oppressive aura diminished significantly, but he could still see the vigilance in their eyes.

Then—

The young man bowed deeper.

"Forgive us, Elder Arthur," Vector said sincerely. "I did not know you were a friend of my grandfather."

Grandfather.

Arthur's eyes widened subtly.

Vector continued, "Allow me to formally introduce myself. I am Vector Astrum. Heir of the Astrum Family. Grandson of Velric Astrum."

Silence followed.

Arthur's final doubt dissolved. He did not know the full extent of the Astrum Family's power—few in the world did—but he knew enough. He had witnessed at least two Fourth Stage Warriors with his own eyes while traveling beside Velric. And if two were visible… there were surely more concealed.

It was just that the Astrums preferred to remain hidden—few in the world knew that a family like the Astrums existed. One whose strength was equal to some kingdoms, if not empires.

After the formal introduction and clearing of the misunderstanding, Arthur led them inside the village.

While leading them inside, Arthur enthusiastically talked about the days he had spent with Velric and their adventures. He reminisced about those years and carefree days.

Arthur also showed Vector a token that he had received from Velric.

After all this…

He made preparations for their stay inside the village while he took Vector and the other three figures from his team to the community hall to ask about the reason for their coming here.

…

As Vector's eyes fell on the village, a wide wooden gate greeted them. It stood about three meters tall and a meter thick, constructed from thick logs reinforced with iron nails and wooden spikes. Primitive, but it was enough to ward off beasts… and delay intruders.

Vantara Village was naturally fortified by its geography. Nestled deep within the eastern Naga Forest, it was protected by the mountainous terrain and surrounding forest vegetation. The village had the eastern side surrounded by mountains. A clear forest stream flowed past the northern edge of the village—the Saraswat River—and the other sides were surrounded by deep forest vegetation. The river provided fresh water and an additional layer of defense. With only a few narrow paths leading in or out, Vantara was easy to defend and difficult to assault—a perfect location for a hidden stronghold.

The gate was guarded by two sentries who stood vigilantly at their positions, watching all the surroundings. Two watchtowers had been built on both sides of the gates. Vector and the others entered the gate with Village Chief Arthur and the elders; the guards gave salutes to Village Chief Arthur and the other elders accompanying them.

Vector's eyes drifted toward the people moving about the village, going about their daily lives. Some of them were dismantling beast carcasses and butchering meat, while others were carrying wooden logs on their shoulders. The peaceful bustle of rural life filled the air. This was the scene one would normally imagine in a village.

Inside Vantara, the villagers moved about with a quiet rhythm, but after seeing Vector and others entering with Chief Arthur and the elders, their eyes cast curious glances. The villagers began whispering about who these people were that the elders of the village were personally accompanying.

One of the villagers whispered, "Who are these people and why is Chief Arthur escorting them?"

Others said, "Don't know. Just now Chief Arthur spoke with a scout, then suddenly he gathered and summoned the elders hastily. After that, all of them went to the southern gate."

"Maybe they're old friends of the chief," someone else suggested. "He did travel a lot when he was younger. Most people in the group accompanying the chief are around his age."

Speculations continued to ripple through the crowd. Who was the group accompanying the elders? But what they didn't know was that in the group, one of the people was soon going to be their future leader. He would lead them to see the world they hadn't seen and show them technology that would change their lifestyle completely… they would live in the information era… and eventually in the galactic era.

The villagers watched as the Village Chief and elders led the group toward the Village Community Hall. At the heart of the village, encircled by open space—clearly a gathering place or village council—Arthur led Vector and the other three—Marcus, Tristan, and Rhea—inside the community hall.

Sebastian wasn't there. He had gone to inspect a suspicious activity that he had sensed around the village.

After taking their seats, Vector looked at the people, especially Arthur. He hadn't expected that someone who was friends with his grandfather would be here in Vantara, and that he would be an authoritative figure in the village. This might make their subsequent actions more effortless and efficient.

And he was more certain that Arthur was friends with his grandfather after Arthur showed him a token he had received. After seeing it, Vector was certain that Arthur was truly someone who had befriended his grandfather and was not making things up.

What Vector hadn't expected was that Arthur was a Third Stage warrior and even the elders of Vantara were Second Stage warriors.

One need to know that Carnatic City had only one Third Stage warrior guarding it. Surprisingly, this small settlement had similar strength hiding within it.

Vector looked at Arthur with a serious expression. "We have intelligence that the Lionheart Empire plans to invade Vantara Village in six weeks. Your village is in a strategic location." He continued his explanation. "They are going to establish their fort here and attack Ironfang Hill City from the rear."

After hearing the intelligence from Vector, Arthur and the other elders' faces paled and worry could be seen on them. The elders murmured anxiously. They began discussing whether they could defend against the attack, whether to evacuate, whether to abandon the homes their ancestors had built. They were also aware of the Lionheart Empire's invasion of their homeland, the Novastra Empire. But they didn't expect that Lionheart was also going to spread its reach here to Vantara Village.

Vector spoke again, which eased their worried expressions a little bit. "I can help you resist against their invasion, and…" Vector continued, and said firmly, "You don't have to face the Lionheart Empire alone."

He added, but his tone stronger now, "But there is one condition."

Vector continued with introducing the condition. "If you want protection, then Vantara must come under the banner of the 'Astrum Family.' Stand with us, and we will turn this village into a stronghold, one that no army will ever overrun."

Arthur on the side was shocked. "Really…"

"Astrum?" an elder, who was one of the Second Stage warriors, asked looking puzzled. "Astrum? We've never heard of this family."

Arthur stopped the elder from saying further. "Edward, don't be disrespectful."

Chief Arthur paused to observe Vector's expression, checking if he was offended by the words. But he was relieved seeing that it didn't look like the case. Chief Arthur then continued and asked in confusion, "Are you sure we can be under the Astrum Family?"

Vector nodded and smiled. "Yes."

Vector showed him the Astrum Family seal, which represented the only heir to the family and their Young Master. And said, "I am Astrum's heir. I have the authority to extend that offer."

He also cleared the other elders' doubts about the family, the Astrums. "That's expected that you didn't know about Astrum. The Astrum Family has always operated in the shadows, but we are one of the core powers behind the Novastra Liberation Front…"

Arthur was quite surprised that Vector revealed his family's information and worried too.

Vector paused, his voice steady and resolute. "And I, Vector Astrum, have come to this land not to conquer, but to defend it. To turn it into a fortress, one that will not only resist the invasion but strike back against it—only if it follows under my command."

A tense silence followed as Vector declared his intentions to the villagers.

Then the Village Chief sighed deeply. "We have no strength to resist the Empire on our own. If you truly offer protection, to take us under the Astrum Family."

Chief Arthur emphasized Astrum and said, "Then we will stand with you, and are willing to come under the Astrum Family."

The elders didn't question the Village Chief but actively supported him. They also wanted to save their home and they believed in the Chief's judgment.

Vector's eyes narrowed slightly, observing them as he said, "Vantara will now stand under my family's banner. I intend to make this village one of the bases for the family. A hidden military base for our Astrum family."

Arthur bowed deeply, and the other elders followed suit. Whether they swore to the Astrum Family or to Vector himself, it was all the same. He was the heir of the Astrum. His words carried the strength to influence the family's will.

He stepped forward, his gaze sweeping the elders.

Vector declared to them, showing his trust, his voice calm. "No need for this… No need. From now on, you are my own people… We are the Astrum family… We are one family."

He continued, "I promise you this… the Astrum Family will always stand with you."

Vector was shocked, though not visibly on his face, by the submission of Arthur and the elders without any resistance or persuasion. It looked like Arthur really knew more about the Astrum Family. And being friends with his grandfather, he didn't doubt where he had gotten the information. Using the family's name was really a good decision. He had intended from the very beginning to bring the village under his banner. This wasn't just a place to establish his base; it was the foundation of his influence. He never intended to compromise on this... It looked like he didn't have to use force or any other method. It would make a good reputation for him and the family toward them and the villagers.

For establishing Vantara as a base, he was resolute to make it possible.

And seeing one side of the village being protected by the steep mountain walls, his will strengthened even more. As it was the same as the one in Agastya Mountain, and seeing it gave him the idea to make a structure like that too. And here, in Vantara, it was completely feasible.

The family also had bases like this, nestled far from normal settlements where movement was negligible or very limited. And Vantara fit that category in every possible way. There was very little movement from the outside world. Only some adventurers or merchants would come here. Though the Naga Forest was very rich in resources, that was only in the inner regions. And very few would have the courage to go there and collect resources.

So, he was sure that if the Venyars did not submit to him, he would make them do it through absolute force—the army that was going to be stationed there after making Vantara an Astrum base. But seeing Arthur and the elders, he knew the possibility of using military strength had become almost negligible.

Vector felt that he had gained a formidable force for himself. He perceived unwavering cohesion and deep loyalty from Arthur and the elders toward him—not just him, but his family. Indeed, he understood that the very foundation of any organization or force rested upon the faith and loyalty of its people.
