Standing in front of the system's magical shop, Ashton crossed his arms. He was suddenly facing a pretty big hurdle that he, for some reason, had not considered before coming here.

Ashton could see the shapes of the items, and could figure out their general type through that since he knew what generally appeared in this machine, but... he couldn't read the labels.

If Rory was here, he could have him read them out loud, though with just the kids here, Ashton didn't quite know what to do. If possible, he didn't want to simply spring the fact that he was suddenly blind on them. They barely even registered the mask he was wearing, and seemed to think that he could see through it. Most people thought so after seeing how Ashton moved.

"Hm... do you three know about all of the things in here right now?" Ashton asked, leaning slightly onto his staff. The kids looked into the vending machine and then back at Ashton. Especially Joey seemed confident.

"Of course we do. There are a couple new ones, and some that we weren't able to try, but I believe we can make educated guesses," she said happily, crossing her arms with a proud huff.

"Oh? Is that so? Show me." Ashton grinned lightly, watching as Joey took out her glittery pink notebook. 'Kids are so easy'

And though Ashton was really just trying to use this to have Joey list all the things in the vending machine, but he was genuinely quite surprised to hear her describe all the items so intensely. She was making a lot of guesses, but she was absolutely right for most of it. There were bits and pieces that were totally wrong of course, but those were largely the wild guesses like 'If you eat too much of this yeti chocolate, you will grow fur'.

Ashton listened curiously to hear what they all thought the items would do. He didn't know all of them either, though he tagged the items to have the Pen of Insight write down the descriptions later. Joey was saying them all in the order they were in the machine, so he didn't have to worry about doubling up on the ones that he already inspected before.

"Well, that sounds about right, at least," Ashton smiled, taking out some coins from his item storage. Random boxes really were giving him some pretty good items, so he bought two more of the best that he could buy in here. These were just 'white' boxes, but still, there could be some really useful stuff in here.

So, Ashton bought two for now. He would fill them up with mana on the way back to Calibur and open them up once he was there.

"What, you're not buying any of the other stuff?" Sean asked disappointedly, and Ashton raised a brow.

"What else would I buy?"

Sean shrugged. "I dunno, just... something? What about the slime candy! You know, you could buy yourself a bag and eat it later, and you could share some with us..."

Ashton smiled lightly, and bought something else from the vending machine. Not the 'slime candy', but a chocolate bar each. The passive resistances they would get weren't that big of a deal, and they could share and mix them together if they wanted to, and it wouldn't change anything. The special tastes they had weren't all too strong either. Rather, Ashton figured these kids would enjoy it if they tasted even stronger.

"Can't buy you guys the candy, and make sure nobody else eats that, either. It's not 'slime candy', it's 'candy slime'. If you eat one of those, your throat and guts will start to slowly melt away. Very unpleasant," Ashton explained, handing out the chocolates. "Stay away from anything with the word 'Slime' in it, basically."

"Awe man, now I want that one even more..." Sean sighed slightly, looking down at the chocolate he was given. "But this is really cool too! Look, mine has a devil on it!"

He smugly showed his chocolate to the others, who both looked down at theirs, too. It looked like Joey got the 'Viper's Embrace' that gave poison resistance, and Millie the 'Yeti's Hug'.

"You're happy with a devil? Well, I've got a super cool snake."

"Oh, man! Swap with me!"

Ashton smiled a little. So snakes were cooler than devils? Good to know. Almost immediately, Sean started trying to snatch Joey's chocolate away, but she defended it like it was her most precious possession, and started to run down the hallway. "Hey, be careful! Don't hurt yourselves!"

There was no response, and the two of them left his channelling range pretty fast. But there was one that had been quiet this whole time, and was now just staring down at her chocolate that was sitting in her remaining hand.

"Can you..?" she asked, glancing up at Ashton, who nodded without hesitation.

"Of course."

He took the chocolate from her and quickly opened it up for her. With the first bite, she let out a slight shiver. "Oh wow, that's cold..."

"Just wait for it. The resistance will set in soon."

Millie seemed confused, but soon that moment of clarity came over her. "And now I'm not cold at all! Whoa!"

"It's neat, huh?" Ashton laughed a little. "Come on, let's see if the others tasted theirs already, too."

He started to walk forward a bit, but he was stopped. Millie leaned forward, trying to grab Ashton's cloak. With her left hand.

Ashton caught her as she slipped when the realisation set in. She dropped the chocolate, too.

Kneeling in front of her, Ashton gave it back to her and smiled. "It's okay. You will get used to it before long."

Millie scrunched up her face, clearly trying to hold herself back from crying again.

"Before... you said that you would lose arms and legs when you were trapped... Did you get used to it, then?" she asked with a slight sniffle.

Ashton still smiled. "Yes. I hated every second of it, but yes. You know, I've met many people before. Some with four arms, others with too many to even count, and some with none at all."

Millie slightly tilted her head.

"They were a talking snake. Pretty good guy, actually. Made the best tea, ever," Ashton explained with a laugh. "But the important bit is that all of them, no matter what, used what they had to do what they could. And I believe that there's a lot you could do, even if you were just a talking snake."

Millie smiled lightly, though she soon look up at Ashton's face. "And... what about your eyes? Will you get used to losing them?"

Ashton's heart skipped a beat. Sure, maybe she could guess that he was blind, but how would she know that he lost his eyes?

"Excuse me? What do you..?"

"Your eyes. They're gone, right? You haven't been looking at us at all, but you knew what we were doing. You had Joey tell you what all the things were in there... and you didn't even look at me when I fell. You use magic to see, right?"

Ashton sighed lightly. At this point, there was no reason to hide it anymore. "Yeah. I can't use my eyes anymore, but I can still see. Right now, I'm seeing the world through the wisp. I see all the magic up to a small distance around me. But I can't read normal writing anymore," Ashton laughed a little. "Was it really that obvious?"

Millie shook her head. "No, I don't think the others noticed. But..." She hesitated for a second, like she was scared to say the next thing that was welling up in her throat. "... I can... I can see things...?"

She turned her head away and instinctively held her hand in front of her body protectively.

"And that's how you were able to tell that I'm not just blind, but don't have my eyes anymore?"

Millie nodded slowly. "Yes..."

"So, what is it that you see? Can you see through my mask? Or is there something here telling you that I don't have them?" Ashton was curious. Of course, if this was a world that once had magic, then it made sense for there to be people with special abilities. The Guardian protecting her so much was as much proof as Ashton really needed that Millie was telling the truth, too.

It seemed like she could tell that Ashton wasn't judging or mocking her. He believed her, and was curious about her ability. "It's... hard to explain? It's different for everyone... And before the monsters started appearing, I could only see it with some people. Usually when they were really bad people."

"Okay. So now you can see it with more people?"

"It's almost everyone. Joey looks like she's made of paper, and when she's thinking about things super hard or talking really fast, it's literally written all over her. And Sean is completely made of stone, but he gets super nervous sometimes and his skin starts cracking. And through the cracks I can see... I don't know what it is really, maybe wool or something? It's soft anyway."

Ashton listened curiously as Millie continued to give examples for her teachers and more of her friends, talking like she had been waiting to finally get it out. If this was real, then this was a special ability that was severely strengthened by the appearance of mana in the world. It also didn't seem to interfere with her too much. She knew what was real and what wasn't.

But there was something that made Ashton just incredibly curious.

"What about me? What do I look like to you?" he asked, but Millie hesitated to answer.

"Hm, you're... big? Like, really, really big. It's uhm... it's hard to explain, you look like you're a giant, but super far away, so you look normal, I think? I don't know, it doesn't always make sense..." Millie seemed frustrated, but Ashton just smiled and kept listening. "And I can't see them, but I know that you have things living in you sometimes. They change a lot, but the... you called them elementals, right? They're there a lot. And that little guy with the backpack that pops up a lot. And in your arm..."

Millie barely just glanced at it, but she looked at the place where the mark of the Spawn of Akrazoth was. She seemed nervous to even think about it.

"That's fine, skip over that one. What did you see that you knew my eyes were gone?"

"Just holes... dark, super deep holes. And there are cracks right next to them, too. Actually, there are cracks all over you. There were a lot when you were first here, but they're way thinner I think," Millie explained, looking Ashton up and down one more time. And as she did, she glanced at something above him. "But there are other things, too. They're not part of people, but they're watching. It's like they're stars in the sky, but I can see them through walls, and they do always stick to really specific people. Does that make sense?"

"Wait, what? What do you mean? Stars..?"

"Mm-hm. They all feel different, but they're all stars. There's one super big one always floating around here, but sometimes there are more, and ones that feel really icky were here yesterday. There are four really big ones with you right now, one of them is the one that's here all the time, and a lot of small ones, but they're way further away from you. They can't hear us I think. But they're trying."
