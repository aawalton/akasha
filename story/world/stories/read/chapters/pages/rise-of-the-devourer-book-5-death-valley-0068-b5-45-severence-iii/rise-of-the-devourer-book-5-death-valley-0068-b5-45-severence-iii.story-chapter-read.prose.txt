Noah had been through some things over the years, but most of them were sharp and very present. Rebuilding himself was a tedious process, challenging in all the wrong ways, and required so much repeated trial and error for microscopic progress that he would have despaired if there’d been any other choice.

But it was this or die, so even as frustration at failure after failure mounted in his stubborn heart, he and Tony kept at it.

With the sheer quantity of high-grade mana flooding through him, anything less than exceptional was too weak to survive. But anything strong enough to withstand the environmental pressure was so far beyond Noah’s current strength that his spirit failed to register it as part of himself.

It was like trying to convince his body to treat a suit of armor as flesh and blood. He could wear it, move around in it, but it was heavy and awkward and decidedly not-Noah.

Which left him in the distinctly uncomfortable position of working to create a near-perfected peak physique capable of channelling insane quantities of mana safely… that for all intents and purposes would only end up being a golem puppet he pretended was himself. A mech suit wrapped around the only part of him to exist: his Dragon’s Heart and its veins.

Just call me a reverse Tony Stark, Noah thought when the realization hit him during one of the pauses after finishing with one mana crystal before moving on to the next.

Noah wants to be called Tony?

Noah chuckled. Or the mental amusement equivalent thereof. Different Tony. And then they got back to work.

Unlike most of his other times recreating himself, there was no all-consuming agony. Or perhaps his pain resistance was just that good by now.

Instead there was a deep-seated unease, something like nausea but pervasive and impossible to locate. It made him jittery and twitchy and did nothing to help with his attempts to turn the body they were creating into himself.

Explosions of power flared out from him periodically, but increasingly less dramatically as more and more of the mana was successfully absorbed into his new creation. His bones were already near-unbreakable, but now they’d outlast the planet. And that was before adding Dragon Shift on top of it.

There would still be the matter of integrating himself into it once they finished creating it, but for now that was a future problem.

—

Noah had finished almost half his body when Sev found him. The ascendant shattered the crystal formation with a single strike, dissipating half the crystalized mana into vapor and sending the rest raining down around what was rebuilt of Noah in an overlapping flow of tinkles and clinks.

It was almost a welcome distraction from the metaphysical wrongness of forcing his soul to rapidly assimilate an entirely new physical form.

“There you are, little—” Sev paused, staring at the half-finished Noah 3.0 lying with one arm draped over a mana crystal. “You! You survived?" he stared, as though this wasn’t what he’d expected to find. "I'm glad to see that. Now, if you'd be so kind as to stop trying to escape…"

Noah wasn't about to stop anything. He didn’t quite have legs yet, but he did have his arms. And a whole lot of mana. He snatched the shards of crystal from the pile around him and started shoving them down his throat as fast as he could move.

Sev laughed. "You think the power of this place matters against someone like me?"

Noah released a swarm of bloodwyrms beyond anything he'd ever unleashed before, hundreds, thousands, enough that the entire cave was filled with them.

Sev walked toward him, flicking aside the bloodwyrms with casual disdain. The few who survived his casual smack dissolved in the domain of his aura before they could reach him. “Please. You have no need to throw away your life so eagerly. You’ve already done the impossible, and saved me quite a bit of trouble in fact, but this fight is hopeless.”

Noah was so focused that he felt every single bloodwyrm's dissolution as though he were the creature itself, and it was that connection that showed him what Bun Bun had been trying to tell him about mana.

It was so simple he should have known it but so opaque he'd never have been able to comprehend it even if he'd guessed it already. He wasn't actually channeling the mana. He was using it, forcing it into activation points—but those activation points were throttled by the limitations of the abilities they were connected to, limited to the strength of the Path it belonged to.

It was the difference between throwing handfuls of water or placing his hand in a stream to redirect its flow. He was never going to control it fully, never going to contain what was inherently too far beyond him to be contained by what he currently was. But he didn't have to work so hard at it either.

Instead of shoving power into the abilities, he guided it through them.

A bloodwyrm spread out around him, growing as it consumed every scrap of stray mana that escaped Noah's porous vessel. It bit down on Sev's head, flickering and wavering as it both regrew itself and dissolved around him.

Tony. I need you to make a few changes. We’ll finish this another time.

Pressure grew behind his eyes and color diffused oddly, rainbow auras surrounding everything while saturation dimmed to grey.

A moment of disorientation, then he shifted the flow of power into Wyrmblood. Tony took over the guidance, pushing Noah's body into perfect health.

With the bloodwyrm absorbing the stray power and no longer any rampaging spillover tearing him apart, recreating his body took less than a second.

Noah stood, blue scales crackling with lightning as his wings expanded out behind him. Infernal fire flared around him, power flowing through him effortlessly. He laughed at the relief and sense of overwhelming strength that was now his, and shifted the primary channel into Life Siphon.

“Fine. You’re not in the mood to talk.” Sev shot forward, tearing the massive bloodwyrm apart in the process, hand extended to deliver another Noah-destroying smack.

Noah was already somewhere else, Arcane Step taking no more time than a thought. Spectres sprang out in every direction, gathering up the crystals and handing them back to him as fast as he could swallow them. He’d never felt so electrified. With this kind of power running through him, he could do anything, go anywhere. Nothing in the world could stop him now!

…except the Ascendant standing right across from him.

"You can't sustain this forever, kid." Sev stomped a pile of crystals into powder, amused again.

"I know." Noah's voice came out more confident than it had any right to.  He hadn't had time to think past finding power and somehow healing himself, but Sev didn't have to know that.

"This cavern won't last more than another few minutes." Sev kicked a second pile of crystals, shattering and dissolving them and everything in the path of the kick for the entire width of the cave.

Four of Noah’s spectres exploded from the force of the strike, though they were replaced as quickly as they were destroyed. “But I have the whole valley.”

"Then I’ll destroy the whole valley.” Sev stepped toward him, melting Noah’s aura around him as he advanced, walking through bloodwyrms and hellfire like they weren’t even there. “Or you can give up and this can all be over."

"Not gonna happen." Noah teleported away, deep into the tunnels.

Sev ran after him unerringly, smashing through walls like they didn't exist. "You're not escaping me this time."

Despite Noah’s best attempts to throw him off, teleporting first one way, then another, Sev was always there a split second later, running so casually that it looked like he regularly shattered mountains at supersonic speeds for fun.

“I’ve no interest in killing you, but if you make me cripple you in the process of bringing you in, it’s your own doing.” Relaxed, unconcerned.

Noah was devouring the guy's power as fast as he could, drawing on the Life Siphon with everything he had, but he was starting to think that wasn’t going to be enough. Perhaps Zax had been right and it would take him a lifetime to drain an Ascendant, even if he temporarily had A-Grade power to fuel him.

He couldn't afford to accept defeat as a possibility though, so he kept going. His spectres teleported back and forth, maintaining the flow of crystals to him, but he would only have another few minutes of that before the cave was emptied fully.

“Do you enjoy this desperation? Is this your idea of fun?” Sev sounded genuinely curious, and more than a little amused.

Noah didn’t dignify that with a reply. He teleported back the way they’d come, and an instant later Sev was running alongside once again, a smug cat crashing his way through a cardboard maze in relentless pursuit of his prey.

What else did Noah have? His spectres were only as strong as he was, and underneath all the A-rank mana he was currently channelling, he was still B-rank.

Sev had already proven his aura was stronger than anything Noah could throw at it. His biggest, strongest bloodwyrm, far beyond anything he'd made before, wasn't even enough to make the guy pause.

If strength wasn't going to be enough, then he had to do something else. Target his weaknesses with Noah's strengths.

He had to find a way to change the dynamic of the fight and turn it into something on his own terms. But it was already so far out of his control he couldn't think of any way to reclaim the high ground.

He needed another force multiplier. Another pencentage, another…

Tony has an idea.

What is it?

Tony shoved Noah’s attention into one of his least-used abilities.

Soul-Rune Harvest [Ancient/Unique] (3/3)

Harvest the soul of your enemies, capturing them into runes that adorn your Arcane Body, allowing you to summon phantoms and specters that will obey all your commands.

That’d be great, if I had any way to kill him.

It doesn’t say dead enemies.

Noah frowned at the 3 of 3 and it expanded into a small menu.

Current Soul-Runes:

Shadow Spectre

Axeros

The Menagerie

Noah blinked in surprise at the middle name.

“Axeros? As in, Vion’s father? The level 1000 dragon?”

Sev’s eyebrows rose and he turned to glance at Noah. “You speak of the old Great Dragon so casually?”

Noah realized he’d spoken aloud and shut his mouth. He teleported a few times in various random directions, gaining a few split seconds before Sev caught up again. And again.

I used Soul-Rune Harvest to pull Vion out of the link that was powering the undead dragon, but that shouldn’t have meant…

Tony doesn’t know what it will do either, but Noah has nothing else.

Except that. But Noah didn’t want to give in to the temptation. He was already a potential vessel for the rebirth of the Devourer, he didn’t want that aspect getting any more comfortable taking control.

So he nodded and shoved the torrent of mana toward manifesting Axeros from his Soul-Rune.

He’d never actually manifested anything from this ability since it fused into its current form, so he had no idea what to expect. He’d been focused on its potential as part of the chain of events that would allow him to safely break the Pact with the Void, not so much on the power itself for its own sake.

Honestly, rereading its information. he was surprised it hadn’t fused with Arcane Spectre. Its basic description sounded like nothing but an extension of that, just allowing him to summon people other than clones of himself.

Axeros began to appear as a phantom image overlaid atop Noah. His scaled hands split from Noah’s, then his arms, then—

Noah looked around for the rest of the phantom, but all he had were an extra pair of phantom-dragon arms sticking out from his shoulders. To their credit, they were significantly longer than his own and ended in vicious claws, but when expecting The Great Dragon King, this was a little anticlimactic. Noah already had more ways than he could count to create extra limbs.

To demonstrate, Noah summoned six more arcane arms from Runeforged Armaments, each carrying a different weapon.

Sev’s aura evaporated them instantly, but the dragon king’s arms remained.

Ah. That’s good to know.

He swiped out at his adversary with one clawed hand, hoping the dragon limb would follow suit, but it only remained where it had appeared, poised over each of his arms like a pair of clawed scorpion tails.

“Don’t just sit there, do something!” Noah jumped toward Sev, his mana crystals running almost completely to the end by now. He was out of time to think. All he had left was action.

In response to his vocal command, the oversized dragon limbs started flailing ahead of him, clawing the air violently enough that they tore chunks out of the tunnel around him, coming close to causing a cave-in.

Noah teleported directly behind Sev. The dragon claws slashed into the Ascendant’s arm as it came up to block the attack, not quite slicing through to bone, but certainly cutting deep enough that blood started to well up.

Sev looked at the blood in surprise, then ducked to one side just in time to evade the second dragon claw’s equally violent attack. “Something new to show me before we call it a day? How lovely.”

He reached up and caught the first dragon claw as it rose to prepare for another strike. The Ascendant effortlessly held the phantom limb in place, effectively trapping Noah in place. “I think we’ve played out this charade long enough.” Then he threw Noah into a dimensional storage box.

Noah had been in his own storage plenty of times, and he’d taken things out of other people’s storage, but Sev’s dimensional storage was nothing like Noah’s. It was a solid cube of silver metal, seamless and flawless, roughly the size of a small bathroom. The only other things inside were a large stone block of the right size to sit on and a black panel hanging on the wall opposite it that immediately put Noah in mind of a TV screen. It had none of the usual accoutrements thereof, but who knew what would be possible in a fantasy world. Magic power without cords could be perfectly reasonable.

He half expected to see Sev appear on the screen to continue taunting him, but it remained dark.

Noah tentatively tried moving himself to his own storage instead, but the barriers around him were sufficiently strong that he bounced off them.

Noah’s space is not connected to this space, Tony pointed out. It would be much easier to go back to Erandir.

“We’ll come back to that later. For now…” Noah walked over to the wall and pressed his hands against it. “I don’t think this is the boundary of the astral space. I’m not detecting any astral connections here.”

But the feeling of being moved into another space was unmistakable, he definitely had been moved.

He already knew without having to check, even though this space was owned by someone else, he could freely block any attempts to remove him. Or, for that matter, anything else. If there was anything important in here, he could hold it hostage.

Life Siphon continued to function, though the amount of life energy had slowed to a trickle. Guess inevitable does mean inevitable.

Noah could stay here.

“In theory, I suppose I could outlast him using Wyrmblood, stay in here for however many centuries it took to drain him fully, but I’m not sure I have that kind of patience. Besides, Zax is out there. Aurelia is out there.” And Vion and Erwest and so many others. “As long as my presence on Erandir is throwing a wrench in the cults’ workings, accepting an indefinite time-out isn’t on the menu.”

Tony was quite for a long moment as he thought it over, then, Tony thinks the dragon spirit scared the hiding man.

“He didn’t seem bothered to me.”

He wasn’t taking Noah seriously before that. After that he put you where you couldn’t touch him.

Noah’s smile slowly grew across his face. “He thinks I can’t touch him from here? Then he’s got quite the surprise on the way.”
