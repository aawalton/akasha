Chapter 115

The Hard Way

It wasn’t until I slammed the heel of my foot into a slowly congealing pool of dark shadowy liquid that I realized I might need to start carrying more weapons on me.

Not that I felt a conventional blade or other type of weapon would work exactly at this moment, for currently I was trying to squish a shadowy octopus like squish ball into oblivion. There is just one obvious problem to this trial and error, mostly error on my part as each stomp down elicits a piercing psychic screech with each stomp.

“SCRIEECH!”

Fortunately, my Divine Music Resonance Skills has advanced enough that even now, even with the screeches being projected directly into my mind, I can feel the slight differences in pain being provided. Making my method of execution slightly easier, once I find the most deafening scream being unleashed by simply rolling my heel around until I find the exact right spot that produces the notes indicating the most pain. That’s when I feel something ignite within my chest and with a forceful stomp down that exact leg, I feel a slight burst of warmth, and then nothing.

To be clear, I mean I feel nothing on multiple levels, first after that initial burst from my heart down my leg and into my grinding heel, there is nothing. No more heat, no more warmth, nothing aside from that initial surge of energy that coursed through me. A surge that momentarily drained me of mana. Mana that I am already refilling thanks to my Golden Life Ratio Mana Regeneration* Skill. Then I also feel nothing in my mind, no more scraping sounds that feel like long fingernails scratching against chalk boards, no high piercing cries of pain from an alien creature, nothing. There is just silence.

For a moment, I almost wonder if the world has somehow stopped, and that I am somehow the only one able to move. As it feels like every sound has faded away from the world. Then I almost wonder if I have gone deaf somehow by being too close to the psychic screaming inside my head.

Fortunately, in less than a second the world spins back up into life, as a cocophony of sounds and pain filled cries scream out letting me know that the world is back to normal speed, and that I can still hear.

“AHHH! My nose!” Junior cries out.

“Junior, are you okay?” Ronaldo cries out, looking up I see that Ronaldo is reaching out to stabilize a falling Junior who is still bleeding out a stream of bright red blood from his nose. Taking one quick glance down, I look to take in the remains of the once intimidating tiny ball of darkness. A ball that only now do I remember a similar shadow like substance from just two days ago. The same type of substance that I had to first defeat to enter the ghost rift in Bloom Park.

Looking down, it was clear that something had changed, the once glowing sheen that had almost seemed to be melting like vapors in the light was now gone, replaced by a dark tarlike substance that just clung to the floor. I thought about using Trash on it, but chose not to, as I instead went to go help Junior. Figuring that the slime like substance was clearly dead this time, which meant healing others injured by my actions was now the priority.

I would never be so thankful as I was later, that I ended up leaving that first tar stain right where it was.

***

(Two Hours Later)

What had started as an early release day, turned into being an exceptionally long afternoon.

As it turns out, the psychic screams of that one monster could be heard by sensitives over a mile away. I didn’t even know that there was such a thing, but apparently Junior’s reaction was signs of his having a proclivity towards being a sensitive. Which was good, as the government was always looking for people with such sensitivities, what and why I did not know, but apparently this mean that Junior now had options open for his future.

As for me, I had to explain myself a few dozen times. Fortunately, some found out that I was Madame Bloom’s foster child and that’s when things took a sudden turn. I went from being interrogated like I was the original instigator, to being asked for my honest and professional opinion on what happened and explaining in detail what happened and why. I was used to such hostilities, as my hair and eye color instantly marked me as a DiVoratore, who the locals apparently wouldn’t put past being the initial source of whatever these shadow creatures were.

Only the fact that Ronaldo, and Junior both vouched for me saved me from being hauled away right on the spot. Though it was clear that there were still some on the force that questioned my sincerity.

“You just happen to find one randomly?” The sergeant in charge of my interrogation asked.

“No,” I reply, and as I say this, the sergeant’s face grows wide with shock as if he just got me to confess to my crime. Though his face changes as I continue my explanation, “I just went to the closest one, as I was trying to inspect why the shadow was being that active.”

As I spoke, I could see the look of surprise fade on the Sergeant’s face, as it slowly fades to confusion, and finally a look of him being completely lost by my explanation. Thinking back on my words, I didn’t blame him, as I too was having a hard time describing what I saw, and was still seeing.

That’s when I decided to go for the more direct approach.

“Look, I can show you,” I offered.

“Show me?”

“Yeah, there are dozens of these throughout the street,” I note, as I have identified closer to two dozen, thanks to my never dropping my Divine Sight and keeping it active this entire time. While there are well over two dozen, over a dozen of these dark shadows are within easy grabbing distance of the street. There are a few on rooftops, gutters, poles, and other places that I’d rather avoid, but I can show the few that are at ground level and appear easy to get to. I can almost imagine someone walking by and placing them as they stroll by.

“Dozens?” The Sergeant asks skeptically. Feeling that this is getting nowhere, I gesture to the nearby chair, a chair that one of the officers used to set up some of their gear in while they quarantined the place.

“Yeah, there is one under that chair,” I state and point to the chair in question.

“This chair, right here?” The Sergeant asks, pointing at the one with his group’s gear in it.

I nod. Seeing that I am serious, the officer pauses for a moment, but then glances at my chest, well at the Bloom family emblem that is on my chest and pauses for a moment before ultimately nodding his head and declares with an almost sarcastic tone, “show me.”

Hearing him, I get up and go over to the chair in question. I gesture at the bag first, which one of the younger officers that have come to help lock down the area sees my advancement and grabs the bag, removing it from the chair. At which point, I take the now empty chair, flip it over, so its legs are now up in the air and point at the smoking and hissing black sac on the bottom of the chair.

“What? That’s a chair,” the sergeant cries out angrily. With that, I pause as I wonder if he really can’t see it? For a second I stop cycling my Divine Sight, but I can still see it right there, right on the back left leg, now hanging upside down from the support arch. For a second I wonder if this is due to my odd seal dealing with sight, but I just shake my head. I’ve already wasted too long on this, and if I don’t do something now, I will be labeled as either crazy, or go right back to being suspect number one on the police’s rather short list, apparently.

Shrugging, I reach down and just grab the black substance once again. Being my second time grabbing it, I already know what to expect, but pause as nothing happens. Nothing, no movement, no psychic screaming, just an inanimate object in my hand. It wasn’t until I remembered that I tried to rid of the first one by using my Trash Skill on it, that it stayed in a dormant state. That’s when I decide to try it again, using Trash to once again dissolve the object.

As soon as I do, there is a faint melting sensation, as I did in fact remove a small dead protective outer layer from the creature. A layer that protected the shadow from the light, and from apparently melting and kept it in a hibernating state, as it woke up and instantly cried out in immense pain, just as the first one did.

In seconds, waves of psychic cries of agony rang out from the shadowy substance in my hand. This time more than Junior and Ronaldo were deeply affected by the screech, as one of the local guards who was also noted as a sensitive also doubled over in pain. Realizing that this wasn’t my first rodeo, I sprung into action, taking the blob, throwing it hard against the ground, then grinding it with my heel again. Letting the pain roll over me, until I felt a flame build deep within my chest, then when I was ready, I focused my attention raising my foot and stomping violently into the psychically screeching mass. A burst of flame and energy coursed through my leg, then silence as once again it felt like the world had stopped. Though this time I could see the confused looks of all the security guards and officers on site. Many of the first timers raised their hands from their ears slowly, as if they too wondered if they had somehow gone deaf.

I removed my foot, showing the exact same scorched shadow stain that had remained after my first stomp.

After my reveal, the entire atmosphere around me seemed to change, going from skepticism to outright fear.

“That was there the whole time?” The one sergeant who had been interrogating me up until that point asked.

I nodded.

“Wait, that was there the whole time, as in, you let me sit on it?” The one guard who removed their bag asked, having apparently first sat in that chair before using their supply bag to hold the chair’s place.

“I guess so,” I reply not quite remembering if he actually sat in the chair or not.

As this was going on, the oddly soothing sound of hooves on cobblestone roads could be heard coming from the side alley. Hearing that sound I involuntarily let out a sigh of relief as the cavalry showed up, almost literally in this case.

There was a commotion, as officers went to intercept the approaching carriage, only to be stopped when a clearly tired Madame Bloom exited the carriage.

While she was out, I went over to provide first aid to Junior, the sensitive guard member, and anyone else who appeared to have been gravely injured by the second psychic scream. While this one had been dealt with far quicker, its effects were still noticeable to those that were identified as sensitives.

“Yes, right this way general,” One of the guard officers called out, directing Madame Bloom through the quarantined ropes and lines of officers and towards me and my now two shadow stains on the ground. Again, other than looking disgusting, I was beginning to think of these stains as almost worth keeping, almost.

“Funny how you always seem to be at the heart of most of my problems recently,” Madame Bloom lets out, a clearly exacerbated note to her words. Judging by her somewhat pained and tired demeanor I almost wonder if she is a sensitive herself? I then wonder if her being a sensitive would explain how she can seemingly see into the future, something that she has never confirmed but never denied either. Still, I feel I must reply just as sarcastically.

“Look, someone was bound to find at least one of these, as there are dozens of them along the street here,” I reply and trail my hand down the street highlighting all of them that still stand out to my sight, even with Divine Sight no longer activated.

Hearing my words, and sensing the truth in my statement something shifts in Madame Bloom’s posture. Originally, she had seemingly been just tired, feeling that she might have to cover for the not one, but now two different shadows that I detected. Yet, she seems to snap out of whatever state of exhaustion she had been in a moment ago, as her body straightened up as a switch seemed to flip in her mind as she seemed to realize a looming threat that required her full attention.

“Dozens?” She asks, her tone low as if she is trying to derive the validity of my previous statement.

“Yeah, most on the ground, but a few are up higher,” I comment feeling suddenly frightened by her intensity. That’s when she hits me with the two words that let me know today is going to be another exceptionally long day.

“Show me.”
