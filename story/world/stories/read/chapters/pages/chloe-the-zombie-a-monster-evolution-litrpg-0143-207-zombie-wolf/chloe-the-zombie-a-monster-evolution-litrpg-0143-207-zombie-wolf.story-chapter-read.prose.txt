Chloe decided that there was no need to overcomplicate things. The monster might have looked super terrifying, but underneath all those feathers and absurdly long limbs, it was still just another monster that she could beat. And hey, maybe she'd finally get to eat its brain when this is all done and over with. It's been a pretty long time since she ate a brain after all.

Alright, let's start this off with a good ol'fashioned magic blast, those always work after all. Chloe thought.

Chloe raised both hands and focused really, really hard. Fire curled around her fingers in gorgeous little orange ribbons, and chunks of earth floated up around her palms, which was honestly kind of iconic, before everything got covered in a layer of icy blue frost. Then,  she added lightning, letting it crackle between the rocks and ice as she pulled everything together into one giant, swirling ball.

The ball grew bigger and brighter by the second, until the air around her shimmered with heat and smelled sharply of ozone. The whole thing vibrated between her hands like it was about to explode right then and there, and Chloe had to really fight to keep it together. Fire, earth, ice, lightning.

A beautiful combination of destructive forces, all neatly packaged into one convenient ball of magical violence.  Chloe flashed the monster a determined grin. “Let's see how you like this!”

Chloe thrust both hands forward, sending the elemental sphere screaming down the tunnel. It tore through the air in a blaze of orange, blue, and white, leaving a turbulent wake of sparks and frost behind it. She watched it hurtle straight toward the gigantic birdlike creature, its explosive power illuminating the monster's black feathers from beneath.

The big bird monster barely even seemed to acknowledge the incoming attack. Because when the ball came close to hitting, the creature casually swatted the elemental sphere aside with one of its wings.

The resulting impact sent Chloe's carefully constructed masterpiece veering violently into the tunnel wall. Fire burst against the metal in a spectacular flash, stone fragments scattered like shrapnel, ice exploded into glittering powder, and lightning forked wildly across the surface before sputtering out. The entire tunnel shuddered with the force of the blast, leaving behind a smoking, frost-covered crater.

And the monster? The monster was fine.

Chloe stood frozen, her hands still extended in front of her, staring at the smoking wreckage of her attack. She slowly lowered her arms, blinking once, then twice. “Okay… that didn't work."

Administrator P threw back his monstrous head and laughed. And before Chloe could even think of a witty comeback, black lightning erupted from the monster's outstretched hand. Chloe barely had time to register the flash before it slammed into her, sending her hurtling like a rag doll. Her back struck the floor hard, and she could only stare at the ceiling for a little bit.

Okay, wow. That was, like, seriously not cool.

Chloe tried to move, immediately regretted it. Everything felt wrong, like someone had taken her entire body, thrown it into a blender, and then put it back together without bothering to check the instructions. But hey, she was still alive. Technically. Or, well, alive-adjacent, anyway. Chloe was a zombie, after all, and zombies were... something.

The whole undead thing came with a lot of weird technicalities, and she could unpack that existential nightmare another time. Right now, she had bigger problems.

With a grimace, Chloe pushed herself onto her hands and knees, then staggered upright, one arm wrapped around her aching stomach. And that was when she saw Sierra fighting.

The wolf girl was attacking the monstrous bird head-on. Dark shadows coiled around Sierra's hands, stretching into long, wicked claws that slashed through the air as she darted forward. One strike raked across Administrator P's side, followed by another that caught his arm, leaving dark gouges in the creature's black plumage, and surprisingly, all of this was actually working. The monster jerked back as Sierra's shadow claws tore into him, his enormous frame shifting to avoid another slash.

Since when can Sierra do that? Chloe wondered.

Chloe had known the girl had her own abilities, obviously, but seeing her go toe-to-toe with an Administrator was a whole different situation. It was impressive and honestly a little unfair. Like, hello? Could we maybe have discovered your super-cool shadow claws five minutes ago?

Anywho, watching Sierra do all the work was officially getting a little embarrassing, so Chloe decided it was probably time for her to jump back into the action. She pushed herself upright and fixed her gaze on Administrator P.

“Okay, big birdie, round two!” Chloe shouted, raising one hand toward the creature.

Electricity crackled between her fingers, rapidly intensifying until brilliant white-blue sparks danced across her knuckles. Chloe narrowed her eyes, gathering the energy into a concentrated bolt before thrusting her hand forward. Lightning tore through the tunnel with a deafening crack, illuminating the concrete walls as it streaked straight toward Administrator P’s head. The bolt struck the side of his skull with a blinding flash.

And then... absolutely nothing happened. The lightning dispersed across his black feathers in a brief shower of sparks, leaving behind little more than a few fading arcs of electricity. Administrator P didn't even flinch. He didn't stagger, or so much as turn his head to acknowledge the fact that someone had just tried to fry his brain. He simply continued moving as though Chloe's attack had been about as threatening as a particularly annoying mosquito.

She stared at her hand, then back at the monster. Oh, come on! Seriously?

Before she could come up with another complaint, a sudden movement caught her eye. Sierra had darted beneath one of Administrator P's enormous wings, her shadowy claws slashing across his side. Dark streaks tore through his feathers, and the monster jerked away with what looked suspiciously like genuine discomfort.

Chloe watched as Sierra followed up with another slash, forcing Administrator P to twist his enormous body to avoid the attack. Unlike Chloe's elemental magic, which he had casually brushed aside without a second thought, Sierra's shadow attacks were actually doing something. They weren't merely scratching the surface, either. They were getting through his defenses.

Ohhh, okay. So, shadow damage is the best plan for now.

Unfortunately, Administrator P seemed to have had enough of being scratched up by an extremely angry wolf girl. He abruptly swung one enormous wing sideways, catching Sierra before she could retreat. The impact sent her flying across the tunnel, her body tumbling through the air before she crashed against the ground.

Chloe's stomach lurched at the sight, but there wasn't much time to panic. Administrator P was already turning toward her, his monstrous head lowering as those dark, unfeeling lenses settled in her direction. Chloe clenched her fists, forcing herself to focus on what she'd just learned. Ordinary elemental attacks seemed practically useless against this guy, but shadow magic could hurt him. And luckily for her, she just happened to have at least one shadow skill up her sleeve.

Darkness gathered around Chloe's hands, curling between her fingers. The shadows stretched outward, thickening and hardening until two long spears took shape in her grasp. Their sleek, obsidian-black shafts gleamed faintly, while their pointed tips seemed to swallow the light rather than reflect it.

Chloe rolled one spear between her fingers, admiring it for half a second.  Then she drew her arm back and hurled the first spear. It shot down the tunnel with a sharp hiss. Administrator P reacted immediately and he threw his body sideways, narrowly avoiding the spear as it whistled past his head and buried itself in the concrete wall behind him.

She barely waited for the first spear to disappear into the darkness before launching the second, aiming directly for the creature's exposed torso. This time, Administrator P didn't attempt to evade it. Instead, he thrust one monstrous arm forward, black lightning erupting from his palm in a violent burst. The dark bolt collided with the shadow spear halfway through its flight.

The two forces struggled against each other, black lightning crackling violently around the spear's point as its shadowy surface rippled beneath the pressure. Then the electrical blast overwhelmed it, shattering the spear into a cloud of black wisps that scattered through the tunnel before dissolving into nothing.

Chloe stared at the dissipating fragments. Well, that was annoying, but also, kind of encouraging? Because Administrator P had actually bothered to dodge one attack and actively counter another, rather than simply swatting them away like they were nothing. Her shadow magic wasn't guaranteed to hit him, but at least he seemed to consider it a legitimate threat.

Chloe barely had time to appreciate the fact that her shadow spears had actually made Administrator P take her seriously before the giant bird launched another attack. Black lightning erupted from his outstretched wing, branching through the tunnel in jagged, vicious arcs. Chloe barely managed to throw herself sideways before the energy tore through the space where she'd been standing, striking the concrete floor with enough force to send chunks of stone flying into the air.

She scrambled to her feet, narrowly avoiding a second bolt that scorched the wall beside her. “Can you, like, stop doing that for two seconds?"

Administrator P didn't seem particularly interested in accommodating her as he lunged. The enormous creature crossed the distance between them with startling speed, sweeping his black spear toward Chloe's chest. She ducked beneath the first strike, felt the weapon whistle over her head, and retaliated with a blast of dark magic aimed at his exposed flank. The attack struck his feathers and burst into a cloud of dark wisps, forcing him to shift his weight, but before she could capitalize on the opening, Administrator P twisted its spear.

The spear changed as its shaft elongated, the pointed head splitting into a broad, curved blade. In the span of a second, he was wielding a scythe.

Chloe's eyes widened. Excuse me?

The scythe came sweeping around, and Chloe threw herself backward, but the blade caught the edge of Cloeaky and tore through the fabric. Chloe stumbled as she struggled to regain her balance. Administrator P followed with another swing, forcing her to retreat, then drove the scythe down toward her head.

Chloe rolled aside and managed to dodge the attack just in time. But across the tunnel, Sierra was also not doing amazing.

The wolf girl had gotten back up and was charging at Administrator P, dodging between sweeping arcs of black lightning. But every time Sierra moved in close, the big bird monster  forced her back with a black lightning blast.

“SIERRA!”

Chloe panicked as the wolf girl was hurled across the tunnel thanks to a direct hit from a bolt of black lightning.  Sierra struck the ground hard, skidding over the floor before coming to a stop near the base of the wall.

Administrator P's head snapped in Chloe's direction. “Oh, no, you absolutely do not get to—”

The weapon in his grasp shifted again. The spear's head split apart, its dark metal bending and stretching until it formed a curved bow. P drew back an invisible string, and a slender arrow of concentrated shadow materialized between his fingers. Its surface rippled with black energy, while thin threads of lightning crawled along its length.

Chloe's instincts screamed at her to move. She sprang sideways as he released it.

The arrow shot through the air, and Chloe twisted away, but the projectile changed direction midflight, curving after her as though it could sense her movements. Are you kidding me?! It TRACKS?!

Chloe winced as the projectile struck and pinned her leg to the ground, its shaft embedding itself in the concrete beneath her. She crashed onto her side and felt really annoyed. For now, all she could do was stare at the arrow jutting from her leg, because getting unstuck was turning out to be a pretty big challenge.

The shadow weapon seemed to have anchored itself to the very darkness around her, its shaft growing heavier and more resistant whenever she struggled. Chloe braced both hands against the floor and tugged, but the arrow refused to budge.

“Oh, that's just super annoying,” she hissed through clenched teeth.
