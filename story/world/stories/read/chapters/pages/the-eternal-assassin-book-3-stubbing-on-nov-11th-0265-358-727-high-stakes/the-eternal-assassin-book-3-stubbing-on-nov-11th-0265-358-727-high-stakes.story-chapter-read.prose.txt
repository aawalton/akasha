Not long after they finished helping the townsfolk and made sure everyone was in stable condition, Asher gathered the teams once more. The casualties had ended up being a bit higher than he’d first thought through his limited scrying, and their victories not quite as many. Groln had pulled him and the other team leaders aside, a dark frown on his face as they all shared information and went over what happened.

“Fourteen of the thirty Order members have perished,” Groln said, spitting to the side as he continued. “…along with Tambor. One of my own party members who’s been on my personal team of slayers for over a decade now.”

A quick glance back at the gathered people revealed that the weird being who seemed to be covered in clouds was nowhere to be found, and Asher’s heart went out to the head of the royal slayers. He didn’t even know what he’d do if he lost any of his teammates, and they’d only been fighting alongside one another for a couple of months.

“One of the teams made up of members of my Order went up against The Giant,” Amanda said, her voice hard but firm as she took the loss of her members in stride. “It will be expensive, but we should be able to resurrect everyone once the battle is done. The other team went up against The Warden, and while they managed to remove one of the greater revenant’s arms, they too fell before his power. My own team fought against Crescent Moon, who I personally slew without too much difficulty.”

“We faced off against Flaming Thread, and while we sustained a few serious injuries, they’re nothing we can’t heal with time,” Asher declared. “Not only did we take her out, most of my team is still in fighting shape and ready to go. Moxy will have to sit out any more battles though.”

“My own team went up against Blood Storm,” Groln said. “Tambor made the mistake of trying to pit her own Cloud element against the power of his storm, and she was annihilated in doing so. We managed to finish the greater revenant off without further injury, but not fast enough. There is no part of her left, which means she won’t be coming back.”

“Sorry,” Asher said lamely, knowing that his condolences wouldn’t bring the warrior back.

“She knew the risks when she decided to join my team,” Groln said, steeling his resolve as he turned to Mako. “We’ve already accounted for all the greater revenants, which means there’s only one undead missing. Did you go up against the lich?”

“We did… but it wasn’t much of a fight,” Mako admitted with a pained expression. “Eupher revealed himself to us, but not physically. He projected himself into our minds using his Mind element. It doesn’t seem like he was able to do anything harmful, thanks to Jameston, but he still appeared. He spent a few minutes ranting to us about destroying the Hojon Empire and even tried to get us to join him. We did our best to get him to keep talking in the hopes that Jameston would be able to figure out where his actual body was, but before he could get close, Eupher ended the skill. I can’t help but feel like Lanra would have figured out a way to find his body or trap him somehow, but on our own…”

“You did what you could,” Asher said, and Amanda nodded in agreement. Groln, however, disagreed.

“You just let him get away?” Groln asked, narrowing his eyes at Mako as the realm wanderer shrunk in on himself even further. The fact that the usually smiling, joke-filled man was currently mourning over the teammate who he had only just lost seemed lost on Groln, and Asher stepped between the towering demon and the realm wanderer before he could continue berating him.

“What would you have had them do?” Asher demanded, hoping Groln would understand what a ridiculous request he was making. “I know I sure as hell wouldn’t have been able to do anything if a lich popped up in my head. Could you?”

“I wouldn’t have just let him run away!” Groln shouted, causing everyone standing off to the side to jump and turn their way. Asher could only shake his head at the sight of Moxy perking up at the thought of a potential fight despite the state her arms were in, and he let out a sigh of relief when Amanda stepped between them all and held up her arms placatingly.

“We have all suffered loss recently,” she said, staring up into Groln’s eyes as if daring the demonic slayer to challenge her after she’d lost half her Order. Sure, she might be able to resurrect most or even all of them, but at the moment, they were as dead as anyone else. “We should focus on the task at hand to make sure we don’t suffer any more than we already have. The lich and two greater revenants are still loose within the empire you treasure so highly. We gain nothing by arguing among ourselves.”

“You’re right,” Groln said, taking a deep breath before giving Mako a slight bow. “My apologies. That was unfair of me. Tambor was not just a teammate, but a close friend. I lost my temper.”

“I understand,” Mako replied, a ghost of his old smile on his face as he nodded. “What can we do to help?”

“We need to find the lich and his remaining revenants, but now that he’s thrust in the knife, I fear he will have fled the country,” Groln admitted. “The first order of business is figuring out where he and his remaining minions ran off to. Once we know that, then we can destroy them. Tambor was my team’s tracker. Do we have any others?”

“I’ve got a bloodhound on my team, and a damn good one,” Asher admitted. “He once tracked a lich across the entirety of the Noala Kingdom. Do we have something to track?”

“My Order managed to sever the arm of The Warden before he bested them,” Amanda said, growing excited as their plan came together. “We can use that!”

A few moments later, Rulfar stood before them, holding a severed arm up to his face like it was completely normal as he closed his eyes and took a deep breath in through his nose. The fact that this was the second time he was using the severed arm of a minion to track down a lich wasn’t lost on Asher, but he decided to keep that thought to himself for the moment. He had a hunch nobody else would appreciate the oddity in their current situation.

“They’re far… and on the move,” Rulfar confirmed, his eyes remaining shut while he worked his magic. “This is more than enough blood for me to not just get a direction or distance, I can even get a clear picture of them. Eupher and the two greater revenants are together. They’re heading… west.”

“That’s the coast,” Groln frowned. “There’s nothing out there but ocean.”

“Maybe they’re planning on sailing to one of the other continents to start over and escape everyone that’s after him?” Asher ventured. Curiously, Groln and Amanda shared a look he couldn’t quite read in response to his suggestion, before both of them shook their heads.

“He wouldn’t try something like that,” Groln stated with certainty, not bothering to elaborate further. “But that begs the question. What is it he’s trying to do?”

“He wouldn’t try to leave… but there is another option…” Amanda muttered. The paladin’s usually stoic face had gone pale, and she shot Groln a desperate look. It seemed to take the slayer a moment to realize what she was getting at, and his eyes went wide as he took a literal step back in shock.

“He wouldn’t,” Groln said, shaking his head as if trying to convince himself rather than any of them. “It would be suicide!”

“It would be genocide,” Amanda corrected. “He’s a lich. He would just reform at his phylactery.”

“But he knows Zygrul has it within the void!” Groln countered. “He can’t be that stupid!”

During this whole exchange, Rulfar, Asher, and Mako watched the two of them go back and forth, confusion written across all three of their faces. Rulfar shot Asher a look, and Asher just shrugged, equally lost as to what they were talking about. From context, he could figure out that there was something dangerous about the ocean that Eupher might very well be preparing to unleash, so he made what he thought was an appropriate leap in logic based on the hints Gran and Donvath had dropped for him.

“…Is there some sort of monster he’s going to try and anger? Like a kraken capable of going on land?”

“There’s no point in discussing this further, we need to move,” Groln said, motioning toward the remaining wizards who had survived the earlier attacks. It turned out, Asher’s team wasn’t the only one that had lost its wizard. Of the original seven, only four still stood. He hoped the Hojon Empire had a lot more wizards than the other countries, because he was pretty certain losing three wizards would have been an absolutely devastating blow for just about any other one. “We need you to bring us to the coast. Straight west. Work with the bloodhound to figure out exactly where. We are to head off the trio before they reach the coastline.”

Nodding, the wizards took Rulfar aside and began discussing precisely where they should move everyone to. While they were doing that, Asher gave Groln a pointed look. “Even if you can’t or won’t talk about this big bad plan the lich might be enacting, surely if it’s this big of a deal, the Hojon Empire can spare a few more of its greater powers, can’t it? We could really use the extra manpower.”

“They are indisposed at the moment,” Groln grunted, staring hard toward the west as if he could see the devastation that was almost upon them.

“Yeah, I’m going to need more than that,” Asher snorted, crossing his arms as he stood firm. “We’re risking all our lives helping your kingdom. The least you can do is explain why only a fraction of your greater powers have been brought to bear.”

“I agree with Asher,” Amanda declared, stepping up beside him. “I have already lost half of my Order who volunteered. We deserve to know.”

Groln look conflicted, his eyes darting back to the wizards who were busy talking to Rulfar before he grunted.

“Conjure that bubble of yours that blocks sound and I’ll tell you.”

Doing just that, Asher blocked light and sound from exiting his Spatial Lock. The moment the barrier was in place, Groln sighed.

“This is beyond top secret. Only the Queen and the other greater powers are aware of this, so if anyone else starts talking about it, I’ll know the leak came from one of you three, and I will figure out which one.”

“We can keep a secret,” Mako said, beginning to look a bit worried. “What’s so top secret that only a few people in your giant empire know about it?”

Groln hesitated for another few seconds before making his choice. “At this very moment… other than myself… there are now only two other greater powers within the Hojon Empire.”

“…What?” Asher asked, scrunching up his face as he tried to recall what he’d been told previously. “I thought you guys had almost a dozen greater powers. Isn’t that what you’re famous for?”

“There are ten in total, which yes, gives us the highest number of greater powers on the entire continent. However. As of a little over a month ago, the Hojon Empire launched an expedition unlike anything the world has ever seen. Three of our top realm wanderer teams, a whole host of supporting warriors, and last but not least, five of our greater powers were a part of the expedition. All of them joined forces to take on what was once thought to be an impossible task.”

“What would require such a powerful force?” Amanda asked, her brow furrowing as she stared at Groln. “And where did they go?”

“If the expedition is led by three different teams of realm wanderers…” Mako muttered, his face paling as his mind wandered through the limited possibilities.

“Exactly,” Groln grunted, looking more than a little annoyed as he revealed the truth. “The Hojon Empire has ventured into hell itself. We’re going after a demon lord.”
