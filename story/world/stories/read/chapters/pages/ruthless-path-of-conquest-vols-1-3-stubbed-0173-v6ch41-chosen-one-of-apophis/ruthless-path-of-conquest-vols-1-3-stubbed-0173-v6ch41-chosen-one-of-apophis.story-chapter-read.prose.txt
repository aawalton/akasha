The screams of the two Rulers who had been foolish enough to challenge him still ringing in his ears, James stepped out of the shattered remnants of the barrier they had used to trap him.

He instantly felt an oppressive sense of pressure. It was the sort of energy that made his hair stand on end, that seized hold of his senses and told him that he needed to get away immediately.

The power affected him in a way that resembled the pressure he had felt when he encountered Anansi and his children, albeit that this energy was a small portion of what Anansi had shown. Unlike Anansi, though, this opponent was not in his own divine realm. Given where they were, the source of the power should just be a fellow mortal.

Impossible. But my instincts don’t usually lie…

It was hard not to think about what had happened recently. But when his instincts had been deceived, it was about making them underestimate a threat, not overestimate one and feel a sudden desire to flee.

He swallowed and forcefully pushed down his urge to run for a moment. He was a man, not a beast. He would not succumb immediately to instincts, not when fleeing blindly could put him in more danger. He didn’t even know which direction the threat originated from yet.

He looked to both sides and behind him, just to make certain nothing was coming straight for him. Then instinct tugged his gaze downward. He looked and saw the enemy, a pale man with snake-like features standing at ground level, exuding a fearsome aura and seemingly overwhelming Luna and Samuel fighting as one. In his peripheral vision, he could see his giant spider ally still fighting one of the other Rulers, though both appeared to be hampered by the presence of the intense aura.

There was no sign of the Panther Queen.

Run, James! came Luna’s voice through their bond. The fight is unwinnable. Just run!

She was saying exactly the same thing that his whole body was telling him.

He took only a split second to consider it before he did what his instincts told him to.

Warp Step.

A single step took him a significant distance from the ongoing fight.

I can’t afford to be knocked out of the melee here, he thought. I’m the only one on my side who can destroy the Holy Kings permanently. I hope Luna and Samuel will understand.

The enemy’s voice cut across the distance, mocking him.

“You thought you could just casually reject Apophis, Fisher Emperor?! You were a fool!”

James firmly ignored the taunt and continued using Warp Step to gain distance.

I apparently have yet another enemy who formed a grudge based on something I did a long time ago that’s been blown way out of proportion. Great.

He kept some of his senses trained on the fight between the Apophis worshiper, Luna, and Samuel, and even periodically turned his back to the direction he was moving in, to have a better idea of what was going on.

What James saw made him tremendously angry.

The Apophis follower plainly wasn’t trying to kill the two monsters. The fight wasn’t balanced enough for that to be a necessity.

Instead, he was using some tentacles he had extending from one limb to slowly torture them both. Samuel and Luna were trying to fight, but every other move left them with a bit of armor flayed off, a limb cut through to the bone and rendered completely unusable, and more of their blood painting the ground and their opponent.

As far as the Fisher Emperor could tell, their enemy was entirely uninjured.

Well, of course he fucking is, James thought hotly. He’s channeling the power of a god. More of Apophis’s power than I ever felt run through me, back before I rejected his blessing in favor of Anansi’s. There has to be a terrible price that chaos god is extracting from him, but it’s irrelevant right now. Hell, whatever it is will probably be reversed when the System fixes everyone’s injuries and equipment damage after the melee anyway. This is just a game to that bastard. When I can make the opportunity, I have to make him pay a more serious price.

The only question would be whether that would be now, in the melee, or perhaps in the tournament itself.

Undoubtedly, this Apophis servant would be a threat once again in the future.

By torturing James’s allies, he was trying to lure the Fisher Emperor back toward him for a fight. James must have become some sort of a sore spot for Apophis, despite what Anansi had said about that entity being largely indifferent to human affairs.

I always get attention from the fucking crazies. At least I’m guessing I’m faster than him with this Skill.

That was the only reason for the enemy to keep messing with two opponents who were clearly no longer capable of fighting back effectively. He could kill them at any time, but he was avoiding lethal wounds, instead now lopping off limbs.

If he could catch up to James, he ought to have done so already. The pointless torture was just building up the Fisher Emperor’s lead.

Which almost certainly meant that the new Chosen One of Apophis could not catch up, even if he tried, even empowered by the chaos god’s energy.

James used Warp Step again.

Then he activated Invisibility and started using Warp Step in another direction. That would hopefully further throw the Apophis follower off his trail. James was still moving away from the man, but he wasn’t planning to go too far. If he could, he would rather destroy the man’s soul, just like with the Holy Kings.

He reached out to his monsters that had not engaged in the battle with the Rulers.

Are any of you near Zhang Chāo or Han Jianguo? he sent telepathically to all the creatures that were not present close by. I need them as soon as possible. We’ve got problems where I am.

He looked back again and checked on what the agent of Apophis was doing. It looked like he was just holding Luna and Samuel down with those long tentacles he’d sprouted from one of his arms. James guessed that the two were being tortured in some way he couldn’t see from a distance.

He wasn’t entirely sure he wanted to know what was happening to them, but he needed information.

With reluctance, James opened a telepathic channel to Luna and Samuel. He braced himself to hear their cries of pain, but there was nothing at first.

No, there was something, but the Fisher Emperor didn’t know how to interpret it. Maybe the two of them were in an altered state of consciousness.

He waited a few seconds, but no words or ideas came over the line.

Finally, he communicated himself.

Luna, Samuel, report, he sent. What is your situation?

It’s nice to finally hear your voice, James, replied the Chosen One of Apophis in a voice that made the Fisher Emperor’s skin crawl. I was beginning to think you didn’t care about these two at all.

They’re useful pawns, James instantly replied. I was hoping they could tell me something about you. Who the fuck are you? What are you doing here?

I would have thought all that’s obvious, the Chosen sent. I’ve heard lots of things about you, James, but never that you were stupid. Interesting.

I understand that you’re the Chosen One of Apophis, after I abandoned that Title, James sent. I guess you're after me, because Apophis is pettier than I realized. But why here, why now?

He started to get a message from another of his creatures, and James was thankfully able to listen to it discreetly, on a telepathic channel isolated from the one the Chosen was communicating on, while simultaneously talking to the Chosen.

We found them, the message began. James continued listening and began formulating his response.

Meanwhile, the Chosen was gleefully answering James’s questions on the parallel line.

Yes, chaos can be a bit unpredictable, he sent. You never know when something will happen that’s disproportionate to what you expected, huh? You were a living representation of that when you had Apophis’s powers. You ought to have known. As for why here, why now, the fact is that it’s a big world out there. I’ve traveled it a bit. I think Earth is roughly twice the size it was before integration, just talking about surface area. Just finding you would have been a challenge for me, when the more logical use of my time was to get stronger and wait for this day. Every morning, I flipped a coin. Do I go and find James specifically, or do I go and slay another Ruler? Most times, I ended up going off and killing someone who just reminded me of you. What you must be like.

You sound obsessed, James sent. Like a dog with a bone. I didn’t think a God of Chaos would care so much about one little human.

Of course I cared about you—I mean, of course he cared about you, James, the Chosen sent. You were special. Like a little snowflake.

The Fisher Emperor’s eyes widened. The Chosen had tried to cover up a little bit, but James could swear something had happened just then.

Did Apophis just try to take control of him directly? he wondered.

Frog in your throat there? James sent.

Worry about your goddamned self! the Chosen replied at a volume that made James’s head pound.

Well, as for me, I’m sitting pretty, James sent. I was worried this snake-looking guy was going to chase me, but instead, he decided to fuck around while I made my escape. Thanks to his stupidity and arrogance, I made a clean getaway. Flawless victory.

I’m sure your two subjects here would be thrilled to hear how happy you are with that state of affairs, the Chosen sent.

Probably, James agreed in an amicable tone, forcing himself to relax. The Chosen would probably sense it through the connection even if his jaw was tight, so the Fisher Emperor tried to smile. My subjects are pretty devoted to me. You should try actually building something instead of devoting yourself to wanton destruction, and you’d see what I mean. Speaking of the two you have in your power, I’ve been meaning to ask, just how exactly are you doing this? Interfering with my connection to them?

I have them on a very tight leash, what with my tendrils entwined with their brain stems, the Chosen sent, amused. My careful attention is the only thing keeping them alive now. I’m like their life support system.

If I could get him to let them go… James thought to himself. That would solve a major problem for him with a single adjustment to the situation.

James felt a sudden spasm of pain through the bond with the two monsters.

Oh, my bad, the Chosen added. I guess I twitched the wrong way. I’ve only done this a handful of other times, you know. Spines are delicate.

Well, nice talking to you, James sent. I have things to do here, you know.

If you want them returned to you… the Chosen let the words hang in the air.

You just said they need your tendrils to stay alive at this point, James replied, making his inner voice cold and disinterested. They were useful. Then you broke them. And I’m not interested in fighting the rest of the melee with you by my side, waiting for your chance to stab me in the back.

So it’s no skin off your nose if I was to decide to destroy these two’s souls? the Chosen asked in a calm, measured tone. It would make this whole little detour a bit more worthwhile. I apparently miscalculated, thinking the lizard and the puppy dog would be things you would care about, but—

Just let him destroy us! Luna’s voice broke into the dialogue.

James saw sudden motion in the direction of the Chosen and the two monsters, and his eyes flitted in that direction. The action was almost over as soon as he looked. His three-headed wolf’s body had half-risen, but she was surrounded and permeated by tentacles that entered through multiple holes in her side, her belly, and her neck. As he watched, the wolf’s body spasmed and writhed—and then went suddenly still.

I still have your lizard, came the voice of the Chosen through the connection. James could sense a little bit of strain and frustration in his tone. Get your ass over here, now, or I’ll fucking destroy his soul, James.
