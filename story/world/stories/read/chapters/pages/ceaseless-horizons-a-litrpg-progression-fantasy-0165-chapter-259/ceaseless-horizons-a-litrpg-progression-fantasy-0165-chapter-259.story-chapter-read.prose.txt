Lev distractedly made the bubble scoop up all the humans. Kora buffed his regeneration without prompting, barely realizing she was doing so as her jaw hung open.

Long claws also allowed the Hunter to easily stick to the monster even as it thrashed violently, and while the Neraf had more than enough size to flatten cities, it could do nothing about the persistent creature that slowly tore it to shreds.

It is about… 5% stronger overall, which is quite a bit more than the 2% increase in the total number.

She and Lev shared a glance, and a silent understanding was reached. Both had an inkling of why such a thing was happening. Revealing it to others was not going to do them any favors.

Around 30%, which is far from enough to face all of the monsters. Guess I’ll have to rely on my new skill.

Through tacit agreement, the humans returned to where the stag was still deep inside its mind, fighting something that humans were blissfully ignorant of.

The Hunter also joined them, looking over the humans with approval before turning to the Neraf corpses. A tinge of regret was visible in the Hunter’s eyes, most likely because of the cores that they had no time to harvest.

Lev also wanted the cores. They probably showed some decent properties like size or toughness, both of which he could use in his armor and bubble.

Unfortunately, looting the monsters wasn’t in the cards for them. Instead, he created dozens of orbs and chucked them in a tub of normal sap. The miraculous fluid improved their conductivity with a small intake of mana, and within a few seconds of hasty treatment and enchanting, his traps were ready.

Between all of the big defense constructs, the orbs flew down and slotted themselves into the floor, ready to accept an influx of destructive and shadow mana to surprise the enemies.

Whatever little mana he could get from Kora’s buff was already in his pool. Time for preparation was up as the bubble lowered, depositing them on the barrier ground.

Beyond the disk that he wasn’t going to risk using again, it was the most mana-intensive thing in his arsenal. That would normally be fine, but he was hovering around 35% of his combined reserves, and the shadow mana was necessary for making good use of his Trapping skill. The cost was going to be relatively immense.

No two ways about it, I guess, he breathed in, then sighed.

Orbs were constantly scanned and enchanted, as Lev kept repeating the carving to practice even in the tense situation. Each minor improvement was going to count, though he couldn’t properly assess the changes from repetition.

Hah, I knew our hideout wasn’t natural.

A strong Scaled Draco, and three Earthen Destroyers. This is going to be tough.

At least Aryan’s class is going to shine here.
