“You got skills, bitch,” I muttered under my breath.

Nothing will sneak up on me, I will sneak up on it! What I would do after sneaking up on it …that was still up in the air. Punch stuff? Shout ‘boo’? My stiletto dagger was gone, lost somewhere between castle and abyss, but I soon found a pleasing stick with some weight, and hefty bonkage potential.
