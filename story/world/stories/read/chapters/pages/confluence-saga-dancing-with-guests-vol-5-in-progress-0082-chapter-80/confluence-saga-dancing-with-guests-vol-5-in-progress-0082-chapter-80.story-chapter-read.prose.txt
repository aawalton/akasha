For Combat Paths fighting up a level while trying to reach level 10 is an impossibility. Trying to do so at the subsequent Path Selection levels is a challenge but some have been known to manage.

The quantitative leap at those levels is akin to three and a half normal levels. Few dare to face more than two levels up but it can be done.

The qualitative shift of the wall, of fully manifested Affinity is the true threat. Creatures and even Awakened sometimes benefit from their Affinity prior to level 10, however without conscious intent. Intent and even intuitive understanding of one's Affinity is enough to tip the scales entirely, especially when coupled with the massive difference in power.
