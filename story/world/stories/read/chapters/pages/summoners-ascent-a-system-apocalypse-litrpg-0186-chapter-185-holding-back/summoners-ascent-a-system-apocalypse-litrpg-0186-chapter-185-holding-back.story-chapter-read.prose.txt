"I'm sorry I'm late."

Ashton looked at Millie, trying his best to smile. The girl was sitting there, quietly, just taking all of it. She didn't cry, she didn't complain, she wasn't even making a single sound the entire time that Ashton was helping her clean up.

All the whilst the voice of that teacher was still ringing in his ears. While this wasn't exactly what Ashton had in mind, he figured he should act like the guardian he signed up to be.

"So, what's the meaning of all this?" Ashton asked as he stood back up. He walked over to the teacher, immediately positioning himself between her and Sean. "Do you want to explain to me how Millicent ended up with food all over her?"

The teacher looked around for a few moments, trying to get help from the child and parent that were behind her. "Uhm... well, Sean hit his friend here out of nowhere, I wasn't actually aware that something happened with Millie..."

Ashton grimaced. "What, and you didn't even bother asking what this was all about?" he asked, briefly focusing on the boy with a bloody nose. "That's the same boy that tried to attack her with the 'Throwing' skill the last time I was here, isn't it?"

"Huh? A skill, what do you...?"

Ashton was really getting sick of the way that these people were acting. The way they were talking, and the way they looked at Millie. That wasn't just something that came from her being bitten by a zombie; these people were looking at her that exact same way long before the world began to end.

He knew it well. It was the same way that people looked at him back then. A horrendously twisted face of those that were thinking just how pitiful that little 'thing' was. How they must be rowdy and acting up because they didn't have anyone to raise them properly. How they would just end up a danger to their own precious little wonders.

"You want to know what skill he used?" Ashton sneered. He knew that this was a bad idea, and he also knew that he was going to pay for this later when he was forming his core, but he couldn't help himself right now.

Ashton held onto his metal staff tightly. He pulled all his mana into his chest, and began to shift it in that grating way that felt like he was putting a rusty fork under his eyelid. It was unnatural, and he could already feel that sinking pain in his soul as he pushed that energy into his arm. At the same time, he shifted his channelling range, to make sure that the trajectory was clear.

With a swift motion, Ashton threw the barbell forward like a spear, just as he had done earlier. But this time, something was different. The moment he released it, a wave of energy was released that sounded out with an audible blast.

Everyone nearby would have felt it in their very bones; the power in that throw. They could tell it was a skill, but they also knew that it was much, much different than that.

And of course, Ashton knew that fact well. Energy that was manipulated manually, and that which was manipulated through skills felt inherently different. And especially this rough, almost 'dirty' version of a technique must have felt particularly unpleasant to their senses.

They didn't even focus on the metal staff that was now stuck in the gym's brick wall.

"I will say this once, and only once," Ashton practically growled his words out. "I may have saved you before, but I'm not some kind of hero. I'm not doing charity work. I just didn't want to see all of your kids piled up like all the people outside on the street. But I swear that if, in the little remaining time that we are here, any one of you even speak to Millicent without my go-ahead, you will fucking wish that I hadn't saved you in the first place."

Ashton's breathing had grown heavier, and he noticed that he wasn't the only one. The breathing of the adults in the room had grown tense as well. He must have placed some pressure on them without realizing. The kids seemed fine, though, so at least Ashton's subconscious knew where to focus his anger.

He turned around and walked back over to Millie, though he didn't even have to. She was already standing behind him, staring up at him with an anxious expression. But she wasn't scared of him. She was scared for him.

Slowly, Ashton reached out his hand. "Let's go. I'll make you something new to eat."

Millie still didn't say anything, and just took Ashton's hand. On the way out, they briefly walked by the barbell in the wall, and he pulled it out with a quick tug. The two of them walked out of the now quiet gymnasium. Ashton could hear some whispers, but he didn't care anymore.

He wished this place well, but he didn't want to have anything to do with a place that would just treat a little girl this way.

Ashton walked outside onto the old playground and pulled out his tome, using it to summon the gnome. At the same time, he had the Backpacker get him something that he had been putting off on using for a while.

This was one of the first rewards that he got, and Ashton had put off on using it for a time when he could use it as the material for another construct summon. But maybe not was a good time for it. He should have the materials now, and he was actually still carrying all his potion-making tools with him, since he didn't sort those out of the backpack after waking up yet.

Ashton held onto the 'Common Settlement Selection Box' and opened the selection screen. Really, a lot of things had been added to this since he got it, and there were even more variations of the thing that he was trying to get now. He selected the one that seemed the most like what he was looking for, and once the gnome had flattened and strengthened the ground just so slightly, Ashton activated it.

The box fell apart and the particles of magic floated over to the empty space in front of Ashton and Millie, forming an actually fairly large bell tent.

Confused, Millie looked up at Ashton, and finally said the first thing that he heard out of her mouth today.

"That's it? A tent?"

Ashton smiled lightly, nodding. "It's a tent, but it's going to be a pretty neat one. Though, I think it's already quite cool." He slowly let go of Millie's hand and approached the front of the tent, pulling open the entrance to reveal what was inside.

On the ground, though there was already a thick canvas layer, was a solid Persian-style rug that covered almost the entire area. There was a small bed set up on one side, and there was even a small wood stove on the other, with its exhaust reaching up through the top of the tent. A few sitting pillows were set up around a low wooden table that had the central pole right in its middle, and right next to the bed was a wooden storage chest.

"Whoa..." Millie let out, as Ashton took the first step inside. He squatted down and took off his boots, putting them inside right next to the entrance.

"Come in."

Millie carefully followed, pushing her shoes off with her feet. Ashton had already sat down on one of the pillows.

He asked the Backpacker for a few more things, which they of course quickly got out for him. The first thing was some wood, which Ashton quickly placed into the stove, before calling the Salamander to take a nap inside the stove. It was a lot bigger than the lantern that he usually had it in, so this must be pretty nice.

And while the metal top of the stove was heating up, Ashton put the ingredients on a cutting board on the low table, and started to chop them up. Of course, he had the undine help clean his hands real quick.

"I'm sorry I'm late," Ashton said, trying to break the silence in the tent. Millie was just staring at him the whole time, so he figured now was a good a time as any to at least try and explain. "My sleep is a little weird for me recently. You saw them, right? Those cracks that are in my soul; in the image that you see of me?"

Millie slowly nodded.

"Those come from my soul being forcefully bound and injured. I'm pretty sure they're the reason why I'm stuck in the realm of dreams for a bit too long whenever I sleep. And this time was especially bad, since I forced myself to stay awake for a couple of days."

Though Ashton was trying to explain it as best as he could, he wasn't sure if this was really convincing Millie. She didn't seem impressed, at least.

"But... why?"

"Why? Why did I make myself stay up so long?"

"No!" Millie quickly shook her head. "Why did you do that in there? The cracks got bigger when you did!"

"Ah..." Ashton replied. "Sorry. I just got a bit mad and turned a little childish."

"Childish?"

"Well, yeah. I think I wanted to scare them a little. Make them think twice about how they treat others, especially in this new world. But don't worry, I wouldn't have actually hurt any of them," Ashton explained as he chopped up some herbs. The oil on the stove behind him seemed pretty hot by now, so he put in the first ingredients, starting to fry up some vegetables. Though, when Millie saw small, cubed mushrooms—rather, the myconids—that he was about to put in, she grimaced slightly. So, he just put them aside for now.

But now that he said this, Ashton figured he should point something out to Millie. "Please understand though, that things in there could have been handled much better. Trying to scare them definitely wasn't the best thing I could have done. Might've been one of the worst, actually."

"Then why did you do it?"

Ashton asked himself that as well. Why did he do that? There wasn't any reason to go so far. He could have just taken Millie away from there without any of that extra stuff. But... Ashton could feel all those stares on his skin, and he couldn't even see anyone's expression all too well. Millie had to sit there and just take it all, seeing the way that they all looked at her. Not just today either, but over the past few days, ever since she lost her arm.

A surprised smile formed on Millie's face. "Even you act like a kid sometimes?"

It was almost impossible for Ashton to hold back a laugh. "Of course. Everybody does," he said clearly. "So don't hold back, alright?"
