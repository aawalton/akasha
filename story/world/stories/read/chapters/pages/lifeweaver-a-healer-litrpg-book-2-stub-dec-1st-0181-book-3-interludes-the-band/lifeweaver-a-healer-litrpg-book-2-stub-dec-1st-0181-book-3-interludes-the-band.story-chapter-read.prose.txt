Emma

After almost getting burned to a crisp on top of a building, one would perhaps think it a good idea to get the hell away from it all, but nooooooo, Emma had to go out and play again. Such a super-duper idea. Great fun for everyone. It had started all right, with a couple of arrows here and there from a distance. Since those arrows were about as big as a small spear, they packed quite the punch, and more than one demon had fallen to her attacks without ever knowing she was there. Dead, just like that. So far, so good.

The problems started when she was on the way back to headquarters. An order was an order, after all, and it would be poor form to ignore them after just having joined. Going from rooftop to rooftop with her new jump skill made for a quick traverse of the city, and it let her see far and wide from high up. That's how she spotted a small group of Singers.

Choosing the Siegebreaker class way back when had given her some pretty incredible eyesight, if she could say so herself. Even from such a long distance, she saw them standing still and talking about something. Hitting a target that far away wasn't an issue, but she hadn't come up against Singers before. Like the little airhead she was, she decided to hell with it and went for it. The arrow struck just right, hitting the one Emma pegged as the healer straight through the chest. A killing shot. Hurray!

Except not hurray. Not hurray at all. The big fellow did something, and a ray of light broke through the clouds, centering over the dead one.

"Holy shit," Emma whispered as she watched the dead Singer come back to life. That's cheating! Stupid cheaters.

Emma blinked, counting the Singers again. One was missing. Where did it go? Movement in the corner of her eye made her jump back and avoid getting the shit kicked out of her, but the Singer charged on without missing a beat. Pain lanced up Emma's arm as the bone snapped. Rather than drop the bow, she took it into her inventory even as she used the jump skill again to get away. In the air, halfway down the building, she felt sort of proud of how she'd handled that, actually. Sure, it turned into a quick little clusterfuck, but at least she'd been able to escape.

Something snapped in Emma's back, and rather than pain, she lost all feeling in her legs as her body was propelled forward and into the side of the building she'd just jumped from. Bouncing off it, she saw the Singer coming for her again. Desperate, she used the jump skill again. Not being able to stand up wasn't great, but getting to the ground was the only way out of the Singer's grasp that Emma could see.

"Shit, shit, shit," she repeated as the cold rain assaulted her face.

Emma (Guild): "I need help! Again!"

It was far too late for that. Emma knew it. The Singer was bearing down on her even now. Once she reached the ground, she used the last of her stamina and pushed off with the jump skill, then landed in a heap on the ground.

Using a bow in that position was awkward at best, but she got a shot off nonetheless, using her one good arm and her damn teeth. Emma was pretty sure nine out of ten dentists would recommend against using a bow with your mouth because she lost a few teeth in the process.

The arrow struck the Singer's arm, blowing the limb off.

"Hah, suck it!" Emma shouted before a coughing fit took her. It was getting a little difficult to breathe, almost as if her lungs were filling up with water.

The Singer looked down on her lost limb but didn't seem all too bothered about it. Crud. Advancing on Emma, she spoke for the first time. "Where is Luke Quinn? Where is the Lifeweaver? He is mine."

"Who?" Emma asked between coughs. Her chest wasn't feeling great.

Josh: "Get ready, Emma."

Emma blinked. Who the hell was Josh?

"You will tell me where to find the Lifeweaver," the Singer said, her voice even as she approached.

Standing over Emma, the Singer raised her foot. She wore no shoes. When you're close to dying, you notice the important details, Emma thought, shaking her head. Quite nice feet, too.

The Singer struck with the point of her foot, almost like lashing out with a spear. Emma closed her eyes, accepting that she'd messed up for the last time. When no new pain came, and no darkness reached out for her, she looked up to see a guy standing over her, protecting her with his body.

"Hey there, handsome. I'm Emma," she said. "Come here often?"

"Josh," he grunted, clearly in pain. "Let's go."

With that, the world shifted around them. Emma cried out in pain as she collapsed in a heap right outside guild headquarters, then found that she couldn't draw a proper breath. Josh had saved her, but for how long?

"Thanks," she croaked. "You can get off me now."

Josh was on top of her. He didn't stir.

"Josh?"

He wasn't breathing.

Emma (Guild): "We're outside headquarters! Help!"

Someone appeared, then a couple more. Emma heard gasps, but she couldn't see past the blurriness in her eyes. Her cheeks felt wet.

Nash

"In rock, we trust!" Nash shouted as he slammed his shoulder into a Singer, sending the bastard sprawling. More and more of them were pouring into the street, coming from all directions. This wouldn't end well.

"We need to retreat," Elijah said. Light crackled in his eyes and even along his teeth as he spoke, almost like some sort of static electricity buildup. Very cool.

They'd been sent out to help a few stranded guild members. Nash had naturally volunteered. You never leave members of your band behind. That was just poor form, and it led to break-ups and worse.

"We still haven't found Triss," Nash argued as he cut the fallen Singer in two, making sure he wouldn't get up again.

Elijah swung his sword, and light arced out from the blade, kind of like with Link back in the day. The projectile cut a Singer in two, but they just kept coming.

"We have Triss, Kenny, and Linda," Elijah said through gritted teeth. "If we don't pull back, they'll die here with us."

"Shit, you're right," Nash said, looking over his shoulder. They were standing off to the side. Looking away from your audience isn't something you should try, since you're liable to get a bottle thrown at the back of your head. Nash wished the blow that took him through the chest had been a thrown bottle, but alas, it was not. As soon as the Singer retracted the spear, a glow surrounded Nash, and the wound closed before he could bleed out.

"Thanks, Kenny," Nash said before repeating himself. "You go. I'm heading in to find Tiffany."

"You'll die," Elijah grunted as he, too, received a blow. A Singer with a hammer gained enough momentum to crush Elijah's shield of light, and the weapon crunched into his arm. Elijah didn't even flinch. Rock ‘n’ roll.

"Go," Nash said.

Tiffany was in the building ahead. Singers were crawling all over it, but they hadn't found her yet. There was still time.

Activating the one skill that would give him a fighting chance, Nash said it again. "Go!"

Not waiting to see Elijah retreat, Nash charged forward and into the massing of Singers. Every step built up momentum, and with it, his body grew. Taller, much taller. That wasn't all. His skin turned grayish, like matte metal, and became absurdly resistant to damage. It was time Nash showed these invaders what his class could do, what being an Iron Saint was all about.

Discarding his weapons, Nash used his fists to pummel Singers out of the way. Bolts of fire and ice struck against him, but all they did was piss him off. Enemies either jumped out of the way or found themselves trampled, crushed under Nash's still-growing frame and the weight increase brought along with it.

Weapons struck him, but all that did was feed his resistance as his skin took in the metal and made it part of him. Juggernaut was not a long-lasting skill, but it would be enough. It had to be enough. Smashing through the door to the building, Nash crushed Singers left and right, giving his best growl as he made his way to the stairs. The door was too small, so he smashed it, pushing into the stairwell.

Ten minutes later, he found Tiffany. She was still alive, having used some sort of aura to make the Singers less likely to detect her presence. He hadn't encountered much resistance on the way up, but pursuers were hot on his heels. Going down the way he'd come up was not feasible, and it wouldn't make for a grand exit, either. Nash looked at the windows and grinned.

He extended a hand to Tiffany. "Come with me if you want to live."

She grabbed it and threw a glance at the stairs. "They're coming."

Juggernaut was running out, but Nash thought he had enough time. After losing Savannah and the old lady to that portal closing on them, he wasn't about to give up and lose another member of The Band.

Rather than just pulling her by the hand, Nash pulled her into an embrace and ran toward the window. At the last moment, he jumped and turned so his back crashed through the glass. With his incredible weight, they'd make quite a dent in the crowd of Singers below.

Laughing, Nash screamed the immortal words from Kickstart by Mötley Crüe, "Whoa, yeah! Kickstart my heart, hope it never stops!"

Milla

Lana stroked her hair, urging her to get up, to do what they all needed, but Milla found it all too overwhelming. Another pang of almost physical pain wracked her body as a notification appeared. Another death. One among many. Why had the Singers come now? The demons were too much to deal with on their own. That was why, of course. Milla knew that. They'd timed their attack just right.

Their reason for knowing was something Milla had suspected for a while, but she'd received confirmation less than an hour ago. She now knew, and that knowledge was like a dagger of ice through her heart.

"You need to get out there," Lana said.

Milla sat up, but shook her head. "How could I? What am I supposed to do? We're just one guild, and our strongest members are either away in portals or won't listen to me."

"The Band is more than its strongest members," Lana argued. "They have you, and you have all of them."

Milla shook her head, unable to summon the will to get out of bed.

Lana sighed, then drew back her hand. The slap shocked her. More for what Lana did than the pain it brought about, and there was quite a bit of stinging.

"Pull your head out of your ass!" Lana shouted, then pointed to the door that would teleport Milla to the main hall. "Grow some ovaries and get the hell out of here! They need you! I need you!"

Martin: "Milla! Where are you? We've got more injured coming in, but there's some good news too. ISG sent over Lara. You know the woman who can create dungeon spaces? With that, we can heal our people! They sent over some more people, too, for protection."

There it was. Just what Milla needed. That, and the loving slap, of course. That was something, at least.

"What?" Lana asked.

Milla brushed Lana's cheek and gave her a soft kiss before wiping the tears off her face. She must've looked a mess, but who gave a shit in a situation like this? Their defenses were solid, so she didn't think the Singers would get in. Hopefully, they would hold out against the demons, too. Perhaps the two forces would end up killing each other? One could hope. Either way, they couldn't just stay and hide. Those who could fight would. That included herself.

Coughing, Milla ran a finger down her cheek where her girlfriend had clocked her. "I'm going out there. Don't you worry, Lana. I'll put things right."

"What changed?" Lana asked.

Milla stopped and drew in a deep breath, feeling how her lungs couldn't quite reach full capacity. Luke would have to do his thing again soon, even though he'd just done so before heading out. Why had that bastard of a little brother gone into a dungeon when she needed him most? She'd make him pay for that.

"The rats have come," Milla said. "It's time to right at least one wrong."

Creating some order out of the chaos in the main hall took some doing, with a whole slew of members being severely injured. Two of those injured were gone, she saw, and Milla set her jaw to push through the grief of losing some of her people. If she didn't get this next part right, more would be lost.

Martin stood by the entrance, but hadn't allowed the outsiders entry just yet. Good, that gave them a moment to prepare. Milla gave her orders and her reasoning behind them, sharing the information Ray had passed on to her. They went from looks of horror to expressions of grim determination. Most of them, at least. Sam and Mas looked as blank as ever.

"I'll need you to work with your vitality," she told them. "The healing will have to be quick."

Sam nodded. Everyone got into position, standing around at the ready without making their tasks too obvious. Milla nodded to Martin, who, as a Lieutenant, was able to grant access to non-guild Integrated.

Peter, the de facto leader of Integrated Solutions Group, was not among the new arrivals. Too bad. Lara, the Architect, was. She looked around the room and changed the main hall space into a dungeon without asking permission.

Rather than comment on it, Milla set her jaw and approached the ISG people with a, what she hoped wasn't too obvious, forced smile.

"Welcome," she said, waving for the healers to do their thing. Some of the members of The Band were on the brink of dying. With this, they would recover. Milla knew Lara wasn't doing it out of the kindness of her heart. No, it was just an excuse to set up a zone of influence for her disgusting mental manipulation skills.

Maybe that wasn't such a bad idea, though. With Lara's help, they could calm down and make a well-thought-out plan. Together with ISG, maybe they could find a way to work with the Singers.

Milla bit down on her tongue. The pain and taste of blood gave her enough clearheadedness to issue the necessary orders before it was too late. She had thought they'd get a little more time, but no such luck.

Milla: "Now, Martin."

Martin: "But..."

Milla: "DO IT NOW, MARTIN! They'll kill everyone!"

Martin phased through Lara's bodyguards and reached out his hand. First, he placed it inside her neck, then activated a new skill he'd gained. When his hand returned to its solid, non-phasing state, it destroyed the biological matter in its way.

Lara's head thumped to the ground.

Milla booted everyone else from her party, kicking them out of the guild headquarters. If they tried to ruin the plaque outside, they'd get what was coming to them sooner rather than later.

She turned to face the room, ignoring the head leaking blood onto the carpet. "Did we have enough time?"

Kenny looked up from over by the other side of the room. "We bought them a little more time, but they won't recover on their own in time, even with potions. They need real surgeons, or a portal to a dungeon."

The moment Lara died, the dungeon withdrew from the space, meaning healing didn't work anymore. Most healing didn't, anyway.

Milla sighed. "Luke better get here soon."

The Band had just declared open war on Integrated Solutions Group. She wondered what the consequences of that one action would be. Either way, they'd deal with it the same way they dealt with everything: Together.
