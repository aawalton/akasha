There was little else of note within the ruins, though Kyle and Tillienne left no piece of rubble unturned. Suierrillax found the information far more interesting than Kyle did, but their time ran out before they were able to move to a third settlement to uncover more logs.

“You’ve been quiet,” Tillienne said as they boarded the shuttle sent to bring them back to the Hub.

Kyle felt a smile grow across his face.  He’d been worried about her after their meal together; worried that the guilt and obsession with pursuing power would lead her down a dark path.  Now, seeing her closer to her old self, a weight lifted from his shoulders.  “Well then, what would you do?”

“I’d say focus on the auction, and leave the Tribunal and your hunt to the others.  It sounds like the Tribunal will be done soon in any event, and there will need to be a full investigation about the group that spirited Valentine away.  That’ll take time, and it will be even longer for you to figure out your plan to confront him.”

“You’re not going to try and fight him?” Kyle asked, the question a serious one.  “You, Skippy, and Angela Corthus all have a claim to him too.  Hell, Angela’s might even be stronger than mine.”

Tillienne let out a long breath, leaning forward against the balcony as she looked out at the retreating planet.  “I’d be lying if I said I didn’t think about it when we found the data packet.  A year ago, I might have even tried to stake claim, using him as a measure for my own power if I could make a push to C Grade first.  Now, I realize what a foolish decision that would be.”

“Why do you say that?” Kyle asked.  He had his own ideas, but he wanted to hear it from her.

“First of all, he’s a terrible match for me as I am now.  He specializes in speed, mobility, and single, overwhelming strikes.  Our styles are similar in that way, except for the fact that he likely has the same type of regeneration as the other fungal creatures.  That means I lose the battle of attrition every time.  And whether I choose Ranger or something else as my second class, I’m not going to have the defenses to trade blows with him.”

“Anything else?” Kyle pressed.

“I need to work on letting what happened here go,” she replied, gesturing to the planet.  “It was a job, and one that went bad.  I’m still coming to terms with the fact that it wasn’t my fault.  Even if I had a better shot at winning, fighting him would be to satisfy my own pride, nothing more.  Although, this does beg another question.  How confident are you in winning if you two fought again?”

“Don’t worry about it.  I’ll get it handled.”

Tillienne sighed, then gave him a look full of determination.  “I’m serious, Kyle.  I blamed myself before for not being strong enough to help when I thought you died.  If you’re going after him, make sure you have what it takes to win.  Don’t go dying on me again.”

Kyle felt a sense of agreement from Phaegos, and anger from C.H.A.D.D.  His mind went to the grievous injuries he suffered at Valentine’s hands, and the struggle to recover.  He remembered his evolution, and the projects he and C.H.A.D.D. began.  He thought about the various constructs and prosthetics they developed.  And he thought about a golden piece of wood, inscribed with glowing golden runes.  “I won’t,” he whispered.
