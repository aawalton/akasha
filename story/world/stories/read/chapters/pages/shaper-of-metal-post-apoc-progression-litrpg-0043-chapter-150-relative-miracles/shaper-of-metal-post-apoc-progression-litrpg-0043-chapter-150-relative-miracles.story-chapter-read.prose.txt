Chapter 150: Relative Miracles

Jack held Neex in an embrace for a long while as they enjoyed each other’s closeness, both physically and through the richness of their emotional bonds, both literal and otherwise. Neex’s ‘hair’ could not help itself at some point, becoming tentacles again and clinging to his chest and neck. One even stretched out and wrapped possessively around like a little arm.

They’re very stretchy.

The feeling of closeness through the bond was like a continual glow to bask in, and Neex made it all the more hypnotic with her ‘psychic purring,’ now even more intense.

The physical reflection they’d just engaged in made for a high like Jack had never experienced, charging him, mind and body, as if he’d breathed in a new, drug-filled air. He felt bewildered in the best way by it.

I am incredibly blessed by this blue wonder appearing in my life. To think… any other shmoe would’ve just flown away from it and her in that first uncertain scenario, rationalizing that as the logical play. Why get involved? Not anyone’s business but theirs. Good thing I was a stupid meathead in that hour. Maybe it had been too long since I’d been able to play the hero. I lucked out that it ended up being true that I was, on some level.

They ended up having a seat, and Neex chose her spot to be in his lap. The loveseat-like chair was quite comfortable and, though he’d never admit it, Jack knew he was tired on the inside from the long day, both mentally and physically.

Neex sensed this, naturally. Without any verbal exchange, she turned her hypnotizing bond-powers to soothe them both to sleep, and promptly, they were out on the balcony in each other’s arms.

His dreams were quite pleasant, and he slept like a rock.

Still, as he sat up bleary-eyed and stretching, he once more — in the fog of semi-conscious sleepiness — expected Neex to be there and felt disappointment that she wasn’t.

You’re doing well, Champ!

Not exactly something a non-Non could accomplish easily, but Jack already knew rapid sequences of damage, regeneration, and heavy food intake could do in hours what might take days or even weeks for the average Joe.

So that’s what he set up and executed, working on his core and pretty much everything else over a couple of hours.

“Oh, there are a lot of changes, but they’re good ones. Mostly good… her energy is hard to keep up with. I can see she was holding back before. At least she isn’t tripping over her ‘differences’ being from an isolated plat and can just admit she’s a tenty-headed noob to our whole society; species, I suppose. And it's more interesting for me to hear her unique angles of thinking. She’s already dropped things on me that would make an alley merchant’s eyes pop, and I guarantee you she wouldn’t be going to you with them.”

Between then and the next group training session, there remained a gap to fill. Jack sent a message to Lattice, asking if she could try and show him some tricks regarding the aforementioned ‘failsafe meditation’ into the Calibration trance, specifically to not accidentally fall asleep while going into it, regardless of exhaustion.

Lattice soon got back with him, telling him he might as well be tired when they did it, and to wait until after all the training. Otherwise, he could practice on his own through the day simply meditating in preparation. She also advised him to make sure he wore himself out over the day, so it was in fact a challenge. Finally, she sent him a little list of meditation advice and potential aids, as well as recommended references.

These were probably pretty useful considering he was far from a meditation expert.

Jack subsequently ordered some crap Lattice suggested: a cushion, specialty incense, and a certain overpriced digital music purchase called ‘Energy From Waterfalls’ that was apparently nothing but high-quality waterfall sound capture. ‘Pink noise,’ well reviewed as particularly good for meditation. While Jack was around the tower, he could play it right in his head. Out and about on the Frontier and beyond, he’d need a device and some headphones for any reliability.

It was a bit amusing, but he’d heard those who traveled near the borders far from Memoria used shit like specialty music players and even disk players, as they were hardier and less prone to errors over time, which got worse the further from the tower one got. The typical cellphone had little hope. Jack was familiar with a separation between personal phone and work phone already. That was just how it was done to leave the core territory. The military had supplied his — a brick of a device that probably had a better chance to survive a crash than a pilot.

He made a mental note to prepare the right technology for his trip, so he’d have what advantages he could leverage out there.

Jack also saw something in his searches that he liked, though Lattice hadn’t suggested them: eye pillows. Boy, he could use those with his light sensitivity!

On the back of his intensive workouts that made for a decent ache useful for relaxation, he went for it and started practicing meditation.

It went… well. He didn’t have much problem getting into the zone while utilizing all the aids. Jack had always been good at turning his brain off and thinking of absolutely nothing, so maybe that helped, even if it wasn’t exactly what meditation was about.

He also easily segued it into Calibration, which could always be done if you had a little development recently, to synchronize and align training with Allotment.

Angel-Memoria gave him a half-hearted thumbs-up. “Well done, I guess. Now git!”

He was promptly booted back out into the real world.

Alright, test successful. Tonight will be the real challenge, though.

With more time to kill, Jack considered more solo practice, since he had been getting decent gains out of it. Before he left, though, he recalled the piece of Chromey he had — the highly sophisticated metal bit. He needed to study it more while he had the chance, and perhaps try to integrate some of the principles into his own manufacturing.

Once he retrieved it and sat down to analyze it more thoroughly with his powers, extending memorite feelers and going heavy with Interpret… he was once again mesmerized by its intricacy. He saw new layers within, merely in the time and development of himself between the first time he’d looked at it and now.

He became confident that the matrix of metals was anchored into angles that only existed in the sense of the System. It was reinforced by powers even passively, meaning the manufacture had been a permanent modification.

He realized quickly that fully emulating it was still as beyond him as the moon, particularly the harder outer surface. Nonetheless, he was more and more inspired by the inner, porous matrix, able to ‘taste’ it so to speak, from many angles and understand it better. Thanks to his continued development with the intricacies of Augment, he thought he was seeing at least some inkling of how to ‘anchor and branch’ better — thereby, at least hypothetically, improving base material strength.

He began testing out new manufacture off the cuff, shaping and transmuting in semblance to the inner layers of Chromey’s armor sample. Several aborted attempts later, he thought he was on the right track, as he felt a sense of stretching and ‘bloom’ within the metal that felt productive. At the most basic level, what he was doing was nothing more than creating trapped bubbles within metal.

It wasn’t long before he began to understand what made the method rare: it was tedious and slow to make such tiny pockets as to emulate what Chromey did. Jack decided to ‘poor man’ it and simply make them bigger.

He then ran into the trouble of irregularity in the process of his ‘bloom.’ Many different sizes of pockets tended to naturally develop that way, which was messy and made the material unreliable at any given point. Moreover, the method seemed to be taking him well away from the perfection of Chromey’s technology.

Instead of doing it the messy way, he wholesale abandoned it and instead began tediously forming tiny little hollow spheres, and then linking them together, thereby preserving some base ‘perfection,’ so to speak. And he felt good progress, slooow and tiring that it was.

He wasn’t sure yet how thick to make the substrate between, nor exactly how to best leverage his Augment traits with the new method — they wouldn’t work by default with an experimental, non-intuitive structure. Nonetheless, he made a small panel he was hopeful about before hitting the first licks of fatigue.

Sighing, he paused his little ‘material science’ enterprise. He needed to get the panel to a decent size before locking in the memorization through Transmute: Quick-change. Even that would take a long time, because it was extremely intricate. And even then, he wouldn’t have it quite right, because what he really wanted was to emulate the deeper power of Chromey’s method, which blurred things into supernatural territory, requiring a … fitting into a greater, effectively invisible substrate of reality.

It was a start, though, and it held tremendous promise with enough dedication. That hidden layer… it was waiting there for discovery, for attachment.

He felt that the Augment traits would eventually magnify in power through the method, even as they were practically useless, currently. He could feel that there was a ‘click’ somewhere to that perfect attachment, due to his observation and analysis of Chromey’s armor piece. It was an itch he now was on a quest to scratch. It might take a long period of tinkering and fiddling with the structure to move it along the right angles to find that click. It could be hours, days, months; he didn’t know. Hopefully he could find the time, though. If he did, he very well might advance his capabilities far in excess of what he otherwise should be capable of.

Even this would be barely scratching the surface of Chromey’s brilliance here. After all, what I’m doing is a stiff copy, and he was able to move around with complete flexibility. For all I know, there are limits based on his powerset I can’t overcome. Still… wow. I made more sense out of this than I would’ve thought possible, even getting this tiny bit.

He was on the cusp of some breakthrough or epiphany, he was sure, possibly even a relative miracle.

Not to oversell things to myself. Heh.

Regardless of what could be achieved in the future with this nerdy endeavor, more practical demands remained pressuring him, and he couldn’t continue ignoring the basics he was still learning and perfecting with every hour spent.

Oh well. We gotta train, train, train for the trials ahead. It was all for this, ultimately. All for what is gonna be my reality very, very soon: a journey beyond borders few ever cross, and where the greatest dangers we know reside. I have to be ready.
