Either they are talking about my wound, or the lizard lady is thinking about eating me.

Whatever the case, they seemed nice, and they were using their own supplies to help her leg, so she supposed that made the chances of them being traffickers lower. Then again, if they did want to kidnap and sell her off, it would probably be easier on them if she could walk on her own, so it wasn’t entirely off the table.

I should definitely not let them figure out I’m a noble. I bet a noble elf would be huge on the black market! Tressa thought.

So that’s the word!

Now they’re talking about elves, and they seem to be arguing. I wish I knew what they were saying. It could be that they are discussing whether I’m worth kidnapping or not. After all, high elves are probably worth more than wood elves. Oh! I know! I need to humanize myself! They’ll feel bad about kidnapping me if they see me as a person!

“Briadon! Don’t be rude!” Lisha told him.

“Br-ai-don!” Tressa repeated. “Lisha, Briadon, Tressa! Tressa new friends!”

“Yes, friends!” Lisha said, lips parting and showing some teeth. Tressa was glad she knew the word friends because she could have easily mistaken that toothy smile for something far more threatening otherwise.

“Friends!” Tressa agreed cheerily.

Yes! This is great! There is no way they will sell me into slavery now! I bet they’d feel totally guilty even thinking about it!

Tressa’s head snapped up. Only the merchants and other mages ever knew that!

“Yes!” she said excitedly, and now wondered if these two were merchants… But no, actually, she cast that thought off instantly. They clearly were not merchants at all. How skillfully they had wielded their weapons definitely hinted at being some sort of warriors, and based on their species, they must have traveled far. She knew minotaurs were from far to the south, and Tressa simply had no idea what species Lisha even was.

“Yes. Um. Ash wood. Good staff!” Tressa said, showing it off happily. Lisha nodded, tongue flicking as she looked it over politely.

That is… if I ever go back, she thought with a frown.
