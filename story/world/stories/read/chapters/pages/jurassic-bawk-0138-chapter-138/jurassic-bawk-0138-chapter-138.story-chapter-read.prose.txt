“Charlemagne, please do calm down,” Phatagin begged as he attempted to reason with the enraged rooster.  From a safe distance, that is.

The rooster was having none of it.  Someone had pretended to be his friend, had made him think that they were his friend, but it was a lie!  His Ignition Core raced as his emotions ran rampant, creating a deadly aura of unbearable heat around him.  Fire flashed from his nostrils as he bawked and squawked in outrage.  As the rooster raged, he imagined himself attempting vengeance against Labrissa the snake-woman.  In his mind’s eye, he could see himself finding her and tearing her to shreds before she could cloud his mind with her sweet, poisonous words.

Speaking of poison, the deadly toxins that Labrissa had injected him with had long burned away, defeated by his cultivated resistance, his Inferno’s Physique Skill, and his incredible Durability Attribute.  Charlemagne neither remembered nor cared that he had been bitten; he had been bitten hundreds of times in his short life.  But he had never before experienced a mental attack.

Phatagin finally lapsed into silence for a few minutes, allowing Charlemagne to continue throwing his temper tantrum in peace.  But, just as he was starting to get really into a foul mood, the pangolin spoke again.  This time, the rooster listened.

“I believe that you have a Special Ability Choice available.  Wouldn’t you like to spend some time making the best choice?”

Charlemagne stopped short and went back through the Squiggles’ most recent messages and found the one to which Phatagin was referring.

After taking a few deep breaths to still both his pounding heart and his Ignition Core, which was running at full throttle, Charlemagne asked the Squiggles to show him his choices.

Special Ability: Any Mana of Mine.  Mana loves you.  It adores you.  You and mana have a pretty special thing going on.  Your mana becomes harder for others to disrupt, adding additional strength to any mana constructs that you create.  Mana draining attacks are less effective on you, while your mana draining attacks become more effective in general.  Increases overall ease and efficiency of converting environmental mana into your own personal mana.

Special Ability: Hearts on Fire.  You are the ultimate pyromaniac.  You don’t just use fire: it burns for you and you for it.  You will gain a special reservoir of elemental fire that exists in an extradimensional space connected to your Core.  At any time, you may deposit or withdraw thermal energy from this space.  Increases your overall ease and efficiency of converting thermal energy into mana and vice versa.  Increases your overall ability to control thermal energy.

Special Ability: The Duality of Rooster.  Unlike Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde, there is no eternal struggle between vice and virtue inside you.  But it would be rude to speculate as to which side won.  This Special Ability will cause your attacks to damage your opponent’s soul, allowing you to harm foes that would otherwise be impervious to your attacks.  Additionally, soul damage has a number of deleterious effects, reducing the combat effectiveness of foes you successfully harm.  Note: It is still not advisable to attempt to harm event Overseers.  Yes, even now.

Special Ability: White Phosphorous Coating.  Water almost always puts out fires…but not always.  You have consumed a shadow of the legendary blade Phosphor, which burned with a fury that no water could quench.  Your body gains a special outer coating that will increase the maximum temperature that you can reach, repels water, and allows you to burn for a time without oxygen even if fully submerged, consuming a significant amount of mana instead.

Special Ability:  I Found the Blueprints.  Your mana veins have been doubly reforged, both by I Lost the Blueprint and by Inferno’s Embrace.  This Special Ability will go a step further, allowing you to obtain subconscious insight on how your mana should flow by consuming beings with better mana vein configurations than you.  After consumption, your mana veins will rapidly evolve to fully utilize the insights you have acquired.  Future enhancements to your mana veins will also be easier to achieve.

Charlemagne was once again faced with analysis paralysis, as he simply had too many good choices to pick from.  But nothing in the list would give him a defense against the insidious trick that Labrissa had used on him.

“Bawk!” he complained to the group.

Josephine, the Attendant, looked at him in confusion.

“Ser Charlemagne, if I understand correctly, you must have just hit level 75.  You’re telling me that a being of your caliber does not have any desirable choices?”

The rooster, not used to reading such long messages, spent the next ten minutes haltingly explaining his options to the small group.  Phasima, Josephine, and Phatagin listened with increasing disbelief as Charlemagne described each of the five Special Abilities.  When he was finished, there was a long moment of silence before they all spoke at once.

“That’s the craziest list of Special Abilities that I’ve ever seen,” Phasima yelled.

“You can’t possibly go wrong with any of those picks,” Josephine noted.

“I suggest that you take the one that would allow you to burn underwater.  I find that amusing,” Phatagin said with a chuckle.

Charlemagne glared at the trio.

“Bawk,” he rebuked, causing all three to hang their heads and look bashful.

“I am surprised that you think that you require mental defense at such a low level,” Phasima finally answered.

“Yeah, it’s not like you’re likely to run into anyone who can use those types of attacks, the only ones who could do that would be once in a generation…oh, right.  We’re in the Elite Systemic Games.  You must have run across someone who has mind control powers.”

“Oh, that’s awful,” Phasima replied.

“One moment, please.  What exactly are ‘mind control powers’,” the Pangolin asked.

“Well, there are generally two kinds,” Josephine explained.  “A charm-related Special  Ability or Skill affects the target’s thoughts and emotions, making them more inclined to act in the user’s favor while still believing their choices are their own. The subject won’t act significantly contrary to their nature, so a pacifist won’t start a war or an assassin to just ignore their target forever, but their judgment is skewed to a certain extent.  By contrast, there are also mind control abilities that override the will entirely, allowing the user to essentially command the target to do whatever the user wants regardless of the target’s desire to act or refrain from an act.”

“But that’s just awful!” the pangolin exclaimed.  “How can anyone be sure that they are not presently under mind control?  Perhaps there is a set of beings greater than even our deities who are secretly mind controlling us right now?  Or what if the entire universe is simply the dream of a being so vast, so powerful, that the entire universe represents one simple mental construct, and our existence is simply the byproduct of misfiring neurons?  The horror!”

“Well, congratulations,” Phasima said sarcastically to Josephine.  “You broke the little one.”

“I did not mean to!” the Attendant complained.  “All I did was answer a question.”

“Bawk!” the rooster interjected.

“Well, perhaps after the Delve is over, you can use some of your rewards to bolster your mental defenses,” Josephine suggested.  “Or, you can hire a mind control specialist to continually assault your mind until you develop a resistance skill naturally.”

The rooster considered the idea and found it to be suitable.  He did not want to wait a minute longer than he had to in order to make himself immune to any form of mind control, and if he needed to expend all of his System rewards to do so, then so be it.

But another thought bubbled up that gave him pause as he considered using his rewards to increase his mental defenses.  Wasn’t getting him angry a form of mind control?

Charlemagne put his 1000 points of Special to good use as he considered his own temper.  Many times it had caused him to act contrary to his own interests.  He thought back to his mad flights across Benin, after he had lost his hens, eggs, and chicks during the battle with the mechanical human.  Those had done nothing for him at all, but his anger had essentially forced him to continue trying long after he should have given up.  That was bad.

But, on the other hand, his anger in battle gave him real power.  His Class Skill, Zenrage, and its upgrade, Molten Rage, were both based on anger.  But rather than render him stupid, these Skills focused that anger and used it to make a difference.  Charlemagne, through his Skill, remained the one in control.  That was good.

Was his anger overall a good thing or a bad thing?  The rooster didn’t know the answer, but he felt that it would be something that he would continue to work on when he had the chance.  For now, the Squiggles seemed content to allow him to occupy a space between Floor two and Floor three of the Delve, so he should make the most of it and choose a Special Ability.

He reread all of them carefully.

And then again.

Finally, he had narrowed the choices down to two options.  Then he went back up to three, then four again.  Eventually, he worked his back down to two, but only after much soul searching.

Special Ability: Any Mana of Mine.  Mana loves you.  It adores you.  You and mana have a pretty special thing going on.  Your mana becomes harder for others to disrupt, adding additional strength to any mana constructs that you create.  Mana draining attacks are less effective on you, while your mana draining attacks become more effective in general.  Increases overall ease and efficiency of converting environmental mana into your own personal mana.

Special Ability: White Phosphorous Coating.  Water almost always puts out fires…but not always.  You have consumed a shadow of the legendary blade Phosphor, which burned with a fury that no water could quench.  Your body gains a special outer coating that will increase the maximum temperature that you can reach, will repel water, and will allow you to burn for a time without oxygen even if fully submerged, using a significant amount of mana instead.

Any Mana of Mine was a carryover from his last Special Ability selection, but it had been a strong contender then and was a strong contender now.  Despite his newfound fire powers, the rooster had not forgotten that it was his mastery over mana that drove the majority of his powers, including his oversized physique, the incredible defense and flexibility provided by his mana shields, and his most powerful attack.  Increasing his ability to use mana would help him in almost every aspect of his life.

White Phosphorous Coating, on the other hand, was a completely new ability that would allow Charlemagne to ignore the deleterious effects of water, at least up to a point.  The rooster had grown up on the shores of the Atlantic Ocean, and he knew deep in his heart that there were enormous and powerful creatures that lived in the ocean.  He wanted to eat them all.

After eliminating three of the five choices, Charlemagne read the two finalists to his followers, asking for feedback as he continued to consider which one he would pick.  A spirited debate followed, which led to all three of the others agreeing that picking Any Mana of Mine would be the best choice in general, since White Phosphorous Coating was a very situational ability, and that the amount of water needed to actually quench Charlemagne at full power was nothing short of oceanic.

The rooster did his best to listen, but, in truth, he had already made up his mind.  He finally bawked for some peace and quiet, and then told the Squiggles his choice.
