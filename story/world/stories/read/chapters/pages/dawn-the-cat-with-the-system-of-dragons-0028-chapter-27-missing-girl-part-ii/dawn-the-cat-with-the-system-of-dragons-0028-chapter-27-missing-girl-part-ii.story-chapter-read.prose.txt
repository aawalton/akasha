Moons were visible in the sky; the forest was filled with darkness, but even in this ominous atmosphere, two beings were navigating through the forest. One was Dawn, the sleek black cat, and the other was Elenora, an expert healing mage of Sylvane.

Inside the forest, the black cat was leading the search while Elenora was following from behind.

Elenora noticed that, compared to the areas near the sentient tree, the farther areas were relatively intact.

Only the areas near the sentient tree sustained major damage. There, trees were gauged out of the ground everywhere. The rest of the places were damaged, but not so much as to change the look of the whole region.

Fallen trees were here and there, but that was all.

After exiting Sylvane through the small opening of the border, Dawn became silent and was walking very vigilantly, as if she was wary of something.

She was navigating through the forest very cautiously.

Previously, Dawn was meowing as loudly as possible while sniffing, but now her demeanor had changed entirely. Elenora noticed this and became vigilant as well.

(Has she picked up something dangerous?)

Dawn was continuously walking, but for some reason, she was not walking in a straight line. Sometimes they would walk in circles, and sometimes they would walk erratically. Elenora remembered that when Dawn led her to her den below the giant sentient tree, she was navigating similarly, just like this. The only difference was that they were running back then.

Elenora understood Dawn was deliberately trying to avoid something in the forest.

(Is this region filled with monsters? But we have not encountered anything yet. Maybe she is smelling something in the air.)

What Elenora didn’t understand was that due to the cyclone, most of the normal animals and evolved animals ran far away. Some of them started coming back, but for most, it would take a few days to return.

Only a few extremely strong predators stayed in the forest. And those that are coming back soon after the cyclone are also strong. Dawn was simply trying to avoid those territories.

Dawn wanted to return to her den. It was time to feed her babies. That’s why she was trying to avoid confrontation, especially with a strong being. A confrontation meant having an extended battle. And avoiding conflict would allow Dawn to find the little girl sooner and return to her babies faster.

After two hours, Dawn stopped walking. She turned her ears here and there, sniffed the air, and started growling in a low tone. “Grrrrrrrrrrr”

Then Dawn looked at Elenora behind her, then crouched down in her hunting position, and started moving forward as silently as possible. Elenora thought for a second and understood what Dawn wanted her to do.

Following Dawn, Elenora crouched and started moving ahead. But because she was a human, she was not able to move as silently as Dawn. Her foot kept creating the rustling sound from the fallen leaves and bushes.

Observing this, Dawn immediately turned her head again and looked at Elenora and growled at her. “Grrr”

Seeing Dawn reacting this way, Elenora understood something was definitely ahead. She immediately stopped moving and started using Elemental energy to scan the whole area ahead. But she found nothing.

Dawn had superior senses. Clearly, she could move silently, but Elenora would create a little bit of sound no matter what. This was problematic.

In the end, Elenora decided to stop moving forward. This was too risky.

When Dawn noticed that Elenora had stopped following her, she slowly came back to Elenora and held her clothes with her jaw and started pulling.

“Dawn, look.” Elenora called Dawn and pointed her finger towards her foot and stepped forward. She unintentionally created a rustling sound again. Dawn realized what Elenora wanted her to notice. For Elenora, it was not possible to move silently.

This was troublesome.

Dawn sat down, closed her eyes, and started thinking about something. Elenora kept observing the black cat. “Dawn, what are you doing?”

Dawn didn’t answer and kept her eyes shut. Then, after a few minutes, Dawn opened her eyes, bit Elenora’s clothes, and started pulling in another direction.

Elenora started following her again without stopping. She knew Dawn was up to something. Since it was Dawn who gave the first hint about her daughter’s whereabouts, she decided to trust her and started following her without saying anything.

This time, Dawn was not taking a straight line towards her destination. This time, she was leading Elenora where there were almost no bushes. Her goal was to lead Elenora through the safest route.

While walking, Elenora noticed a narrow, unpaved path inside the dense forest. This was an unnatural path. She could clearly see the cleared areas. She sat down and started observing it.

Some bushes were cut longer and some were shorter, and some were simply crushed by weight. The trail was certainly created in a rush. She also noticed a lot of footprints.

Earlier, she didn’t notice the footprints due to the darkness, but now after observing, she was certain. At least eight to ten people were here earlier.

“Meeeoooow.” Dawn got annoyed and called her. She didn’t understand what she was doing while sitting down.

“I’m coming. I’m coming.” Elenora muttered and started following Dawn again.

After following the trail, Elenora finally noticed something weird at the end of the trail. It looked like dim light emanating from multiple fire torches. She was about a hundred meters away from it.

“Dawn, Dawn, come here.” She moved her hand forward and called Dawn near her.

Seeing her gesture, Dawn went back to Elenora.

She picked her up in her hand and went out of the trail. Since the enemy was nearby, walking while following the trail would make them a visible target.

She started circling around the source of light through the dense forest. There was no need to observe them directly. As long as she could go close enough for her scanning to work, everything would be worth it.

After getting a little bit closer, she started hearing multiple voices from afar. Although she could not hear them properly, it was enough to estimate her distance from them. Now she was surely closer than fifty meters away from them.

Elenora started expanding her Elemental energy to observe them. When she expanded her energy to her limit, she started sensing more than fifty people sitting in multiple lines.

She sighed and muttered, “This is worse than I thought. I have to go back now.”

(Cin, wait for me just a bit more. Your mama will be back soon and obliterate the whole army… They don’t know who they have messed with.) She turned around and started walking in the opposite direction. She did not go towards the trail this time.

Dawn, observing her, jumped out of her hand and started walking. She understood now it was time to go back. Elenora finally understood what Dawn was sensing.

Sometimes cats abandon their kittens when they are in the clutches of a very strong enemy, and it becomes impossible to save them. Though Dawn was not like that, she understood this perfectly and decided to head back just like Elenora.

But then Dawn remembered how Cinthia took care of her when she was injured, and multiple dogs kept coming back to her previous den. It pissed her off.

Initially, Dawn thought that Cinthia went out on her own, but after sensing this many people, her thoughts changed. Cinthia was surely captured by human predators. Dawn wanted to help, as Cinthia helped her in her need.

Dawn turned around, kept her head low, and started walking in her hunting position towards the enemy. She decided to leave Elenora behind and attack on her own.

Elenora didn’t understand what Dawn was doing and said in a low voice, “What are you doing? We'll be captured.”

“Grrrrrr”

Dawn turned her head back and growled in a low voice, and started walking towards the enemy again.

Elenora decided to bring all the high-level adventurers and rangers she knew. She wanted to save her daughter as soon as possible, but upon noticing an unknown army, she had to take measures.

“Come on, Dawn. If we get captured, we won’t be able to bring reinforcements, let alone save Cinthia.”

Dawn simply ignored her and kept walking toward the enemy camp silently.

Elenora sighed and started following her. (It is better to get more information about the enemy. Hopefully, the risk is worth taking. If Cinthia is not under tight security, then I will take her and run away.)

But there was a huge problem: Elenora was unable to move silently. Although she was not making much sound, in this area where no one was present, her walking kept making a slight rustling sound.

Since this would alert any enemy who is nearby and make everything dangerous, she started stepping forward carefully, hoping that no one would notice.

After a while, they reached visible range.

There were elves everywhere.

It was the elven army.

Looking at the sheer number of people, her face paled. There was no way she could rescue Cinthia in this condition. She could not even see her.

Everyone was equipped with armor and weapons. Archers, swordsmen, spearmen, mage corps, and cavalry were there waiting for a command. They were clearly prepared for a full-scale war.

(I need to get back as soon as possible and let everyone know about the invasion.)

Suddenly, she felt something on her shoulder. When she turned her head, she noticed a blade pointing at her.

“Move and you’ll be beheaded immediately.”

“Grrrrrrrr meeeeeaaaaooooo.” As soon as Dawn heard an unknown sound, she turned around and noticed several people standing behind Elenora with their swords drawn and bows and arrows aimed at her. One of them was pointing a blade at her neck from behind.

Dawn was furious seeing this. She kept growling with a warning sound.

“Ooooeee, put down your staff and sword.” The man holding the sword spoke while pushing his sword just enough to poke Elenora.

“Umphhhh.” Immediately, small drops of blood started coming out of her neck. The injury was not deep. It was clearly a warning to Elenora. If she tries anything funny, he will surely pierce her neck.

Elenroa had her staff on her back and her sword on her waist. She was not using it until now. She slowly moved her hands and unhooked both her weapons.

THUD! THUD!

Elenora noticed Dawn was about to attack.

“Calm down, Dawn. It is alright.” Elenora’s heart was pounding rapidly. Sweat was coming out of her head. She was scared. This was the worst possible outcome. With one wrong move, she would die immediately.

“Shut your beast, or I’ll kill it.”

“Yes.” She looked at Dawn with a smile on her face. “It is okay, Dawn. It is okay. Don’t be angry. We’ll be fine.”

“Grrrrr.”

“Let me move, or I’ll not be able to calm her down.”

“Haaah.” The elven man chuckled. “Don’t try to be smart. Do as...”

She cut off his sentence. “More than ten people are standing behind me, and an army is in front of me. Do you think I’ll be able to do anything?”

“SHUT UP NOW. OR I’LL KILL YOU AND GRILL YOUR PET ALIVE.” The elven man behind her shouted loudly and alerted everyone in front.

All the army personnel nearby heard his sound and turned to see what was happening, and then immediately drew their weapons.

“I’m complying. I’m complying. . . Dawn, it is okay. Calm down.”

Seeing the development, Dawn understood that if she made any wrong move, everyone would attack at once. She stopped growling and walked just in front of Elenora.

The man who was standing behind everyone commanded the elves who were standing with their weapons drawn. “Bring a net and capture the beast. And take her to the interrogation room.”

“Yes, sir.”
