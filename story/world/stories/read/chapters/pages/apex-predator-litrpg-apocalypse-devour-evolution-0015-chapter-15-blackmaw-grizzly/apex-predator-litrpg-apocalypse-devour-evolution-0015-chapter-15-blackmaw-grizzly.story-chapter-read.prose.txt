It did not take long for Don to find the big bears. It seemed like certain types of monsters were to be found in certain areas. That made it easy for Don to pursue specific Evolution Traits if he chose to. He still hadn’t tried to see if he could obtain another Trait or an upgrade to an existing one by killing a singular monster over and over again. If he found some special Trait, he would surely do so.

The bear was monstrous. The hair was thin, so the muscles bulged visibly. It was the size of a grizzly, if you added a foot in height and a hundred pounds in weight. The claws on its front paws were just as nasty as Don’s.

[Blackmaw Grizzly, level 10]

Don moved like a jungle cat— silent, steady, graceful. He wanted the jump on this thing. It seemed like he was definitely turning into the ambush kind of predator, at least for now. It just made sense. Attacking a target with heavy damage when it was unassuming gave a massive advantage. And since the beast was huge and had higher level than him, Don would take any advantage he had available.

The bear growled as it lumbered through the woods. Spruces bent in its wake as it walked between trees. When they snapped back and made noise, Don used those moments to trail behind the beast. It seemed his level four Stealth was quite enough in this situation, but building good habits was important.

When Don was a mere ten feet behind the bear, he struck. The bear heard the snap of Don’s claws opening, but it was too late. Don had struck, piercing through the bear’s leg. He twisted his wrist and sliced through the tendons. The bear roared in pain and anger. It took a swipe at Don, but he was expecting it and danced back. The bear charged, but its back leg would not hold. It stumbled forward, and Don took a swipe at its face.

It was a light graze, but it did draw blood. No eyes were popped, but the snout was bleeding. The bear, however, was not deterred. It drew back on its hind legs. It trembled slightly, but as it rose to blot out the sun it was a terrifying sight. Ten feet tall, wide as a barn door. It took a few steps forward and collapsed on Don.

The weight hit like a landslide.

Don rolled, barely getting his arms up as the bear collapsed on him. Its bulk slammed down, crushing air from his lungs. The beast snapped its teeth, inches from his face, foul breath and spit coating his cheek.

Don twisted hard, shoving one leg under its body and kicking off. His whole body screamed in protest as he rallied every fiber of his muscles. He slid out just enough to get his arm free—and drove his claws into the bear’s armpit.

The bear bellowed, furious and wild, and slammed its paw down. The impact caught Don’s ribs. He felt the crunch under his arm. Something cracked—maybe two somethings.

[Health at 61%][Debuff inflicted: Moderate Bleeding, Fractured Bones]

Don spat out blood and grabbed a handful of the bear’s thin fur, and hauled himself up onto its back using his Simian Physique. It bucked under him like a rodeo horse, but Don clung on and buried a claw deep in the beast’s back to stay on it. It roared and kept bucking violently.

Don held on and struck down with his other claw, trying to aim for the spine, but the bear rolled—suddenly and violently. It crushed Don under it again, this time with purpose.

Everything went white for a second. Cold and sharp. Don’s head felt like it was squeezed and about to pop.

[Health at 48%][Debuff inflicted: Fractured Bones]

Don coughed. Blood flecked his lips. He twisted, grinding his shoulder against the dirt until he could bring his arm up and slash.

The bear shrieked, a horrible, broken sound. One claw had torn through its lower jaw.

It reared, staggered back, and Don gasped for air. His body screamed at him. But he stood.

Staggered. Then steadied.

The bear lunged again, head low this time, jaws snapping for Don’s midsection. He leapt aside—barely. The claws caught his thigh on the way past. The bear was insanely strong in its bestial fury. Don’s skin tore like paper. Blood spurted out.

[Health at 29%][Energy at 41%]

[Estimated time until incapacitation from hypovolemic shock: 2 minutes]

Don gritted his teeth and stumbled, leg dragging behind him. The bear turned slowly, jaws flecked with red foam. Its front leg was torn, its jaw mangled, but it was still coming.

A real fight. Not a hunt. A war of attrition.

Don crouched.

Breath. Focus. One more chance.

The bear charged again.

Don ran forward.

They met like boulders smashing together—but Don ducked at the last instant, sliding under the bear’s swiping paw. His clawed hands struck up, piercing into the bear’s gut and dragging through open flesh.

[General Skill Increased: Unarmed Fighting!]

[Unarmed Fighting Level 5]

The bear shrieked and twisted. Don was tossed back like a rag, slamming into a tree hard enough to splinter bark. He felt pain like lightning course through his spine.

Everything hurt. His vision blurred. He couldn’t feel his leg. Blood pooled in his mouth. He coughed and felt like choking.

But the bear was limping now. Slower. Head lowered. Don could see it in the beast’s eyes. Fear.

Don stepped forward.

“Come on,” he rasped.

The bear rose one final time. Don ran again—not with grace, not with precision. Just raw, desperate intent. He threw himself into the beast’s chest, claws out.

They fell together.

Don’s claws found the throat and tore.

Warm blood drenched him. The bear twitched.

Then it froze and let out a low growl. Immediately after it went limp and fell on Don.

[You have slain [Blackmaw Grizzly – lvl 10] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 84 System Points earned]

Don slumped under the body and gasped. He shimmied himself from under the bear’s bulk and tried to breathe. He was soaked, shaking, barely conscious. His entire body felt wrong. Too loose. Too heavy.

The System was basically blaring at him like a fire alarm. Don didn’t need it to tell him he was fucked up. He could feel his consciousness slipping. Only with sheer will he kept his mind together and with excruciation he shuffled himself on top of the bear’s neck.

His vision tunneled. His hands trembled as his claws retracted. Blood trickled down his chin.

[Health at 12%]

[Energy At 4%][Status: Critical. Incapacitation imminent.]

With the last dregs of his strength, Don used [Devour]. The life essence had never tasted so sweet. He felt the bear’s essence fill him. He could feel the bones rearranging themselves, and the constricting agony of them pressing against his organs ease.The pain started gradually fading. Oh, it was still there. Don’s head felt like it was going to burst at any moment. His skull must have fractured. But the bear was huge and it had a lot of energy to offer. Don drank it all in greedily. It tasted like victory.

[Racial Skill increased: Devour!]

[Devour Level 6]

[48 Evolution Points acquired!]

[Evolution trait Blackmaw Bear: Consumed]

[Health at 47%]

[Energy at 22%]

[Debuffs: Moderate Bleeding, Fractured Bones, Concussion]

As Don’s consciousness began to slightly expand again, he realized something. If he had not put points in Constitution with his last level up, he would have died. The fight had been so close. Don had gotten the jump on the beast and fought as hard as he ever had. And it had been so close.

He was still bleeding and hurt. His muscles were spent and if his hammering headache was a clue, he still had a concussion if not skull fracture. So he reached for one of his satchels and produced a health potion. He drank it down like a shot and felt a warm feeling spread from his stomach to his body.

[Health at 67%]

That was better. The headache eased, and Don felt his vision was a bit sharper again. His muscles still felt like lead, and every movement was a struggle. But at least the bleeding stopped. Still, Don was vulnerable and decided to use another health potion. When Don tried to drink another one, he noticed something. There was no warmth and no System notification.

“Oh,” he rasped to himself. “They have a cooldown.”

That was completely fine. Don needed a goddamn break.

However, as he tried to steady his breathing, his Survival Instincts flared up.

“You’ve got to be kidding me…”

The direction the danger was coming from was from behind Don. With painstaking slow movement of muscles stiff from exertion, he turned and stumbled to his feet. Through the foliage pushed a large dark snout. Soon two dark eyes looked around curiously. Don felt his heart drop to his stomach. Another Blackmaw Grizzly was staring directly at Don. For one heartbeat the two just stared at each other. Then the bear let out a roar and rushed at Don.
