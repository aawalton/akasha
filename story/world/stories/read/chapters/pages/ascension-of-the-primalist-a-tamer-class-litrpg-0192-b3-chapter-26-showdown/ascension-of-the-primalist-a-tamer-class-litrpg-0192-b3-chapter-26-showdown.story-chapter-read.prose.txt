Devus stumbled out of the fading light. He was barely recognizable.

Ragged wounds covered him from head to toe, some barely stitched shut, others actively leaking blood that darkened the steel. Devus lifted his head, and Seth met the Guardian's gaze.

For the first time since they had known each other, there was no grin waiting on Devus' face. No loud confidence. No stubborn spark.

Devus's face tightened, the muscles in his jaw trembling as if he were trying to hold back a scream his whole body begged to release.

Seth's core stirred in his chest, dark emotions boiling.

Soldiers from Kastal's side broke from the sideline and rushed toward the Guardian. Priests, officers, shaken soldiers who wanted to help.

"She's injured," he said, loud enough to carry. "But alive."

By the time his squad came to a halt before the hovering portal, anger had settled deep in his chest. Around him, Henry and the others tightened their grips on their weapons while they waited for the Spirit’s signal to enter.

All because one of its favorite toys is back? Seth wondered bitterly.
