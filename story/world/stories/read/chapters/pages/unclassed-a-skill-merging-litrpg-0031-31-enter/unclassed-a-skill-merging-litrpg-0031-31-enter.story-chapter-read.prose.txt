[If _____ _____ is ineffective, this may be due to failsafe _____ causing subject to _____________ expiration cycle. Enable manual override in order to release ____ subject and disable _____. Place your head within the _____ to establish a neural ____ and then walk to an empty ____ and climb inside. Once inside, establish ____ with subject and ____ to disable failsafe. DO NOT execute manual override without a supervisor present.]

It wanted me to… put my head inside that helmet thing over there and then climb in one of those pods?

Yeah. That sounded smart. Because I really wanted to end up trapped in one of these things like her. Yeah. Not doing that. I’d search for another solution.

[You will be fine.]

I almost jumped at the message in front of me.

“Wha—who…”

[This is a part of fulfilling your quest. You will not be in any danger.]

I pointed nowhere in particular, trying to put a face to the disembodied message. “How do you know that? Who even are you?”

[I’m the one who led you here. I want you to succeed. I wouldn’t lie to you.]

“You’re…”

My brain almost broke trying to comprehend what I was being told. I hazarded a guess, feeling like a complete idiot even to be saying this out loud. “You’re the rat god?”

[Yes.]

“Show yourself.”

[I can’t.]

“Why not?!”

[Gods are governed by rules. It would put you in danger.]

“But climbing into this thing won’t?” I stared at one of the empty pods as I spoke.

[Not if you follow my instructions.]

“And there isn’t another way to disable this thing?” I asked, sounding a little desparate and not remotely caring.

[The Relativistic Flux Inhibitor requires biometric scans from three approved engineers, as well as ancillary staff to monitor for wave collapse as the subject is removed.]

“What the… what?”

[Turning this thing off usually requires hand and eye scans from a bunch of long-dead scientists. The manual override is an alternative means to turn it off, but requires you to enter the limbic construct and terminate the program yourself.]

I stared blankly into space.

[...big machine creates DREAM WORLD for girl. Dream world controls BARRIER. Go INSIDE dreamworld and I’ll tell you how to TURN OFF barrier from inside.]

I almost flinched at the bluntness of the message. At least now I understood it.

Well, mostly.

“And you’re sure I won’t die if I do this?”

[You’re in zero danger of dying.]

I thought for a second. “And you’re sure I won’t end up trapped in here either?”

[Not if you follow my instructions.]

“And if I choose to turn around and leave right now?”

[Then there’s nothing I can do to stop you.]

The message lingered for a moment. Reading it almost felt relieving.

[But did you really come this far just to quit?]

I paused at that. I didn’t know what to say. I definitely wasn’t safe if I chose to leave, and I’d be giving up on the reward I’d been offered. Did I really want to return to the mines without the Major Advancement I’d been promised?

I’d taken bigger risks with far less guarantees before. What was stopping me right now? Even if I didn’t trust the god, all refusing was going to do was leave me stranded in the underground or taking my chances up top. I didn’t have any better solutions available.

[Scan your brain and enter the pod. We’ll discuss a further reward once the deed is done.]

I didn’t protest anymore. I tried asking questions, but I didn’t receive an answer. As far as the Rat God was concerned, this conversation was over. Maybe it knew that I’d agree.

After a couple of minutes of no reply, I walked over to the strange looking helmet thing and stuck it on my head.

[Neural scan commencing.]

After a couple of minutes of waiting, the device beeped and told me the scan was complete.

Now all that was left was to enter a pod.

I climbed inside of an empty pod. It was surprisingly comfy.

Then, the glass door slammed shut around me.

A light purple gas began seeping into the pod. Before I could change my mind, I felt my consciousness beginning to slip.

When I woke up, I was somewhere completely different.
