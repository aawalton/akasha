The next day we continued our journey north. Without the merchants we could travel slightly faster but our wagon was not quite able to keep up with the carriage. It also looked a bit shabby; perhaps we could make some improvements to it in the future? For now it did its job and we made good time. As the sun began to set we arrived at Rovik.

As our carriage rolled into the courtyard the doors to the keep opened and Hans emerged, followed by a few servants.
