Jericho's transport item turned out to be a blanket. A Reaper grabbed each corner and lifted it into the air. We all clustered beneath. As it settled down upon us, it teleported us to the nearest city.

Meadowvale.

Upon arrival, we stood by a lantern outside the gate. Two Reapers and two Unchained guarded the entrance. Pirhani had taken extra precautions since my escape from the city, but the four of them were only Ambers. It wasn't enough. Between the group of us, we were able to handle them, though it cost us two Amber Reapers who were phased out to Vitria during the exchange.

I had two of the lower ranked Reapers harvest the Wills and items.

An Amber Will seemed so useless to me after the taste of Emerald. Though I doubted I'd have the luck to defeat another Emerald Sin on my own, even with the boost in stats I'd been granted. With assistance from the others here though? Maybe.

Once everyone was situated, I led the march through the gateway portal into Meadowvale.

The other side was ruin.

Nuzzle whistled sadly from my shoulder. I patted him on the head.

It was worse than when I'd left, almost as if they had backtracked to destroy any buildings they'd left standing during the first wave of their attack. Smoke billowed across the city from waves of flame. The thick scent of ash settled over everything.

The home near the gate that I'd spied on Pirhani and the Iron Reaper from was completely gone. I silently thanked fate that I'd stumbled upon that family and sent them off to safety.

No structure had more than a single face still in one piece. Wood lay splintered. Brick crumbled. Stone shattered. Bodies of unfortunate Penumbrans mangled among it all.

They weren't trying to occupy the city. They were tying to raze it to the ground.

To what end?

It didn't make sense. Was it really all for some game between the gods as Lancelot insinuated? A petty show of superiority. At this cost?

"I think you undersold the need for help a little bit," Jericho said flatly, striding up beside me. "I wasn't expecting this much devastation."

"Neither was I…"

A wall collapsed to my left as I walked down the main thoroughfare with Jericho. I paid it no heed at first, but someone in the group behind me swore. I looked back and several were pointing at the wall that had fallen.

A cloud of dust hovered in the air there. But through the cloud, a green aura burned.

"Emerald Unchained!" someone shouted.

The monster charged, bowling into the group before anyone could react. One Amber took a pair of horns from the beast's skull straight through the chest. Several others were knocked down.

A second wall collapsed on the right of the road. Another Unchained.

And another.

Three Reapers joined, striding through the rubble with singular purpose. Each of the opponents was Amethyst or Emerald.

One of the fallen Ambers scrambled away on hands and knees, but was stopped by a scythe through the center of her back. Straight into her Will. She coughed. Sputtered. The light within her cracking, then being consumed by the Reaper's blade as if she were no better than a Sin.

She died like one too.

Her lifeless body dropped to the cobblestone, empty and drained.

I stared, speechless, for a frozen second.

They… They killed her. But that was supposed to be forbidden. That's why scythes weren't allowed in the Arena. I waited for skull icons to appear over the head of the one who harvested her Will, but nothing happened. He was supposed to be Marked now, right? That's what I'd been told by multiple sources.

Instead, he twirled his weapon, smirking as several other Ambers screamed in fear. His cape billowed in a gust of smokey breeze, the symbol of Pirhani dancing on its back.

Is this because of their oath? They've gained amnesty for murder?

It didn't matter right now. Only one thing mattered.

I'd brought these Reapers to their deaths.

Behind me, Jericho summoned his spiked armor. The three Reapers focused on him next. He'd defeated three before, outside the Meridian. Except… one had been hit by the spines from the Unchained. And they were Emerald, Amethyst, Amber. This was two Emeralds and an Amethyst. But he has allies this time, he's not—

One of the Unchained swept a razor tail through our group. I dove out of range, but others weren't so lucky. Most had their legs swept out from underneath, severed at the calfs. Blood soaked the stone street.

I cast Life Drain at the Unchained. It glared at me as my black smoke consumed its face. Then it turned away, uncaring and stomped on the nearest footless, screaming Reaper.

Fuck you. Fuck you all!

I Aero Shifted toward the beast, scythe primed to go right through its skull.

That never happened.

I was knocked into the air mid-Shift and slammed against a broken wall. One of the other Unchained stood in the path I'd been traveling, giant mirror-like shield blocking the way. Pain radiated through my body. I tried to get up, but my back burned.

Felix dodged a strike from the third Unchained, then jumped high into the sky like a grasshopper, as if he weighed nothing. He disappeared beyond some buildings. At least he was safe…

Jericho and Nuzzle battled against the three Reapers, both wielding flame—Nuzzle breathing fire, keeping the foes off Jericho's back. Jericho, calling forth a flaming tornado, all while his liquid armor struck out, deflecting, attacking, striking at precise moments. He was like a one man army.

A screech pierced the air.

Everyone paused for a second, even the Sins.

I looked to the source of the noise. It sounded like a fucking dinosaur.

Perched above the demolished Meadowvale gate, as if it'd been sitting there watching this entire time, was a massive bird-like creature. Its eagle head tilted to the side, analyzing the scene. For a brief moment I thought maybe it would be on our side. A bonded creature. A sign that Lancelot was coming.

But no. An Emerald aura radiated around it and a broken skull shown above its head. Another Unchained. That makes four.

Feathered wings the size of garage doors spread from its back and it launched into the air. It circled. Spying. Hunting.

Then it swooped down with lightning speed. Straight toward me.

I Aero Shifted away, leaving the Shift in a dead sprint despite the searing pain in my back. The eagle matched me, Aero Shifting as well.

Astral Affinity. Damnit. This asshole is basically me but better.

Talons grabbed my arms. My feet left the ground.

Jericho called after me, but it was too late.

In seconds, I was high above the city. I Aero Shifted from the bird's grasp, no real plan aside from escaping its clutches. I dropped like a rock. The devastated remains of buildings grew larger as I descended. Maybe I can Aero Shift as I approach the ground. Would that break my fall or accelerate the impact?

In the end it didn't matter, because the eagle just snatched me back up again. I tried several more times, each unsuccessful. The Sin's Aero Shift outclassed mine by far.

I used Life Drain, sucking Health from the beast. It replenished my own, but other than that seemed to have little effect on the eagle. Was its Health that high that I didn't even make a worthwhile dent?

I'd been a fool…

I defeated one Emerald Sin and thought I could measure myself against that. Now I knew better. I'd been lucky. No match for these monstrosities.

And yet…

An Emerald Astral Affinity Sin right there above me. With its Will I could advance to Emerald just like I'd advanced to Amethyst. I'd been quietly hoping to find a Death Affinity Sin for my next advancement, but I was in a bit of a desperate predicament. And it seemed Death Affinity Sins were hard to come by.

I steeled my resolve to somehow fight the eagle, but that didn't exactly solve the small problem of how I was going to achieve that. My Volition was low, my skill far beneath its skill. And it had the advantage of environment, carrying me through the sky.

But why is that? Why hasn't it just killed me?

The city below was in ruin. I recognized places I'd been, despite the destruction. The narrow alley where Trix had ambushed me and forced me into the Arena—no longer an alley with the buildings on either side flattened. The bazaar, now empty aside from overturned carts and burning wares.

But amid the devastation, one building still remained standing tall. A few streets out from the colosseum, a steeple stretched into the sky. I'd never paid any heed to it before, never noticed it among the other structures. It looked like a church, or whatever the Umbral Plane equivalent would be called.

And we were headed straight toward it.

If that was the eagle's goal, then I'd make sure we never reached it.

I swung my legs up toward the bird's face. It ignored me, simply tilting its head from side to side to avoid my kicking feet. But hitting it wasn't my goal. I just needed the momentum.

On my final swing, I Aero Shifted up above the Sin, flipping in the air and landing on its back. I primed my carapace arm and Shifted it through the eagle's back, straight at its Will. The same way I'd defeated the other Emerald I faced. It was a cheap trick, but—

Suddenly, the bird was gone and I was grasping at nothing. Hovering. Falling.

Shit. Shit shit shit.

It knew exactly what I was going to do and—

Immense pressure exploded in my chest. An indescribable pain like something was trying to burst from within, but also compress my insides at the same time. I glanced up through barely open eyes, wanting to clench shut against the horror.

The eagle was there. Somehow, the hint of a wicked grin across its beak.

And it's talons were inside me. Squeezing. Threatening to rip the Will from my body. Doing the exact same thing I'd just done to it.

But two could play at that game.

I Aero Shifted away like it had, escaping the aerial prison momentarily. My Volition was dangerously low. That seemed to be a recurring theme during my time in Penumbra. Always in the most inconvenient moments. Life Drain would cost too much, but I'd upgraded Aero Shift enough that it took less Volition. I'd somehow have to end this using my old standby.

The eagle snatched me up, holding me by the arms again, as if it was satisfied that it'd deterred my attempts, asserted its dominance, and could go back to flying as normal.

How wrong it was.

I couldn't Life Drain, but I still had my scythe. And a few other tricks.

The marble I'd collected from one of the Sins a while back appeared in my hand, summoned from my inventory. I hadn't appraised the item yet, always forgetting about it when the time came. It was so small and inconsequential, like it was shoved into a shadowy corner or lost in the couch cushions of my inventory.

But Sibyl's words came back to me from when she was explaining how to activate items. That felt like ages ago now, but the words still rang true.

She'd mentioned that balls typically acted as small explosives that activated on contact. And Calliope had told me that you didn't need to have items appraised to use them, but it was wise to know what they did unless you were desperate.

Well, I'm certainly beyond desperate now.

I couldn't throw the damn thing with the eagle's claws restricting my arms, so I did the next best thing. I maneuvered the marble so it rested between the nail of my middle finger and the pad of my thumb. Then I flicked it up at the eagle's head.

It exploded in a burst of light and smoke, not damaging, but distracting the giant bird. I summoned my scythe, simultaneously letting it go and Aero Shifting above my captor one last time.

Then, I reached out my hand and called my scythe back to me. It travelled the path of least distance, swirling up to me and carving straight through the eagle Sin's neck.

The head flung back, completely detached, trailing blood and nerves. I barely dodged it and latched myself onto the body of the corpse even as it began its decay.

And we began our free fall.
