Jumping through the weave, Luke and Hannah appeared right next to Mill near a town he didn't recognize. She was in the middle of talking to a rather large group of Integrated. Not just members of The Band, either, but Phoenix Phalanx and a bunch of others Luke didn't know. After a quick greeting with everyone happy to have Hannah back, their guildmistress described what was going to happen. The plan was simple: Everyone would attack the portal and then hold it on Earth's side while a select few crossed through into Singer territory and created a ruckus. Luke and Ella, who appeared about two seconds after him, would run to already mapped portals, staying on the other side as much as possible. Once that was no longer an option, they would jump through the weave and tackle the remaining portals from Earth's side, prioritizing those where Earth's forces had retreated back to safety. It wasn't the most elaborate plan, their guildmistress admitted, but it was the best they had been able to come up with. A lot hinged on Luke and Ella, but also on those others selected to sow chaos in Singer-land.

With this particular portal, those going through would be Curtis, Hannah, Penny, Elijah, along with Nick and the new healer from Phoenix Phalanx. Lemira had lobbied hard to go, but Milla wanted her to stay on this side to better utilize her growing army of pets. Many others had wanted to go as well, but they'd need a lot of firepower on this side to protect the retreat. This wasn't a full-scale counter-invasion, after all.

"Did you talk to Curtis?" Milla asked, leaning over toward Luke.

He made a face. "Shit. Forgot."

"I'm here," came a voice from behind them.

Turning, Luke was struck by how pale his friend looked.

"You're not turning into one of the Hollows back from that dungeon, are you?" Luke asked.

Curtis scoffed. "There are many kinds of hollows. I'm not a shapeshifting monster, I think."

"You think."

"I'll let you know how things develop," Curtis said with a shrug, unable to keep the faintest of smiles from playing across his face. After that, he walked over to where Hannah, Penny, and the others were standing, and they all hugged it out.

Luke stood there looking out across the crowd, expecting pride to fill him over everyone's willingness to fight back. No such feeling arose. He swallowed hard as, instead, images of dying Integrated back on that road flashed through his mind. Nash and Mas. Nothing left to buy.

Exchanging a look with Milla, he saw her expression change when she saw him, and she walked up, grabbed his arm, and pulled him away.

"Get ready!" she said over her shoulder. "We're going in five!"

They walked off, creating some distance from the others.

"What's up? Why do you look like you're at a funeral?"

Luke looked over at the group again, then at his sister. "I'm not sure about this."

"What do you mean? This was your idea."

"What if some of them die?"

Milla turned to look at the members of her guild. After observing them for a couple of seconds, she nodded. "Some of them will die."

"What?"

"Well, what do you expect, little brother? This is war. People die in wars." She ran a hand through her hair, gathered it up at the back of her head, and performed some sort of witchcraft to create a ponytail, her fingers flicking this way and that with practiced ease. "We'll be careful, as much as we can, but we can't go into things like this and don't expect any losses. It's a damn miracle we're all still here. A lot of that is your doing, but don't get a big head about it."

"I won't," Luke promised, but the lump in his throat hadn't disappeared.

"We have healers with us, and Lana will make sure they can do their work in a portable bunker kind of thing we got from the Integrated Shop."

"Good."

"But that doesn't mean everyone will be coming back."

Luke let out a long breath, his shoulders slumping. "I don't want people to die. Maybe if I-"

"No," Milla said, directing a swift kick to his chin. "Don't you dare."

"Ow! Why did you do that?"

"You're strong, little brother, but that doesn't mean you can take on everything yourself. Maybe you don't remember, but you've come pretty close to dying yourself. You'll be in more danger than most today, so don't even think about arguing for everyone else to stay back. This plan IS stupid, but it WILL work."

"I wasn't going to..."

She just looked at him with a raised eyebrow.

"Yeah, fine, maybe I was..."

Her eyebrows climbed even higher.

Luke cleared his throat. "So, are we doing this or what?"

"That's what I thought," she said with a nod.

Milla (Raid): "I've created a raid party for this. There are separate chats for the defenders and the attackers. Use those for anything you don't have to share with the larger group."

Milla (Raid): "We are one of twenty-seven separate teams, consisting of Integrated from all over Earth."

Milla (Raid): "Before today is over, we'll have cut the Singers off from their home system."

Milla (Raid): "We have eradicated Integrated Solutions Group and made sure there are no more anchors for those tall assholes to use. After we get this done, all that's left is the cleanup of the Singers still on Earth."

Milla (Raid): "That's it! Let's go kick some ass!"

Milla: "That's your and Ella's cue to get started. Run!"

Luke exchanged a look and a nod with Hannah, then with Penny and Curtis. They'd come far together, and they would go farther yet. He was about to call out to them when Ella grabbed hold of his hand. The world shifted around them, and in the blink of an eye, they stood on top of a platform, right in front of the Singer portal.

"Fuck you, asshole!" Ella shouted, giving everyone around them the finger.

The place was crawling with Singers, but before they could do anything, Luke and Ella were through the portal. Emerging on a raised platform, they spotted two other portals. Singers were hauling supplies around, constructing things, and standing around talking. A small group of what appeared to be Integrated Singers rushed toward them, and what looked like stationary gun turrets whirred to life.

"Ready?" Ella asked.

Luke, who was well ahead of her, cut the threads leading from the two new gates, closing them in an instant. The Rusher took that as a sign to keep going. Luke hadn't seen the mapped-out geography of the Singer world, so he trusted Ella as she carried him away just as the platform they were standing on exploded with energy blasts and spells from the Singer guards.

Luke: "You'd better be careful out there."

Hannah: "Samesies."

No more than a couple of heartbeats later, Ella brought them to a stop in the middle of a busy street. Singers cried out in alarm, and Luke spun, not sure what to expect.

"Why did we stop? There's no portal here."

"Need. To. Catch. My. Breath."

Since all the Singers did was flee, they could spare a moment, he figured.

"You ran a lot more than that back on that island."

"Ran. Too. Much," she said, gasping for breath.

Examining her, he didn't see anything wrong, but plenty of indicators showed she'd pushed herself too far. The glycogen stores in her muscles and liver were almost depleted, and her lactate levels were through the roof. Along with those symptoms, micro-tears in her leg muscles, and stress hormones like noradrenaline and cortisol being elevated, it looked to Luke like she'd been taking things a little too far for quite some time. It made sense, considering the situation they'd all been in ever since the gray orbs appeared.

"I can help a little," Luke said, extending a thread of mana. Rather than go for it right away, he waited for a decisive nod. The micro-tears and lactate were simple enough to deal with, and telling the body to chill decreased her temperature a little and brought the raised hormones under control. Creating new glycogen was beyond him, but he risked mana and used Weaver's Renewal in a few select spots, and that did the trick.

"Feels weird," Ella said, testing her legs.

Luke finished up a few more things, then withdrew. "I couldn't help with everything, so your body is a little confused, I think. Should fix itself in a couple of minutes."

"Should?"

"Will."

She gave him a long look, then nodded. "Thanks."

"I put some things in place to protect you if you're attacked. Hope that's okay."

"Like what?"

"Just something to protect a few of your vital organs." Luke pointed down the street. Singers with rifles were coming. "We had better get going."

Ella steadied her breathing and took his hand again. "Right. Let's go."

They appeared on another platform a couple of seconds later. This one was empty except for a couple of terrified Singers hiding behind a crate. They let out a shriek and fled when Luke and Ella appeared.

"What's going on here?" Luke asked.

Pointing to one of the four portals, Ella frowned. "We have a team through there." She moved her hand to indicate the portal next to it. "Or maybe it was that one? When they said second on the left, did they mean from this position?"

"Shouldn't there be Earth Integrated on this side then?"

"Fuck. You're right. There should be." She held up a finger. "Give me one second."

It took less than that to reappear and point to the first portal she'd indicated. "We've got a problem."

"What?"

"Our side is losing here."

"Shit," Luke grunted, looking around. Just as he did so, turrets rose from the ground, swirling to face the duo. Using Weavestep, Luke brought himself closer to the portal she indicated. "Let's go help them!"

"We're not supposed-"

Ella's words cut off as he stepped through and emerged in a damn jungle. From the vantage point of the raised platform, he could spot a town in the distance, across from a battlefield of man versus Singer. She'd been right. Things were not going well. If anything, she'd undersold just how shit the situation was, with what had to be half the Earth Integrated force dead or injured, and the other half surrounded and outmatched.

"We're not supposed to help like this," Ella said, appearing by his side.

Luke didn't take his eyes off the battle. "I'm not going to let them die like this."

"Suit yourself," Ella said before sitting down on an overturned barrel. "I'm not much good in a fight, so I'll wait here. Don't die."

"You could help bring the injured to healers."

She shook her head. "You're a healer, aren't you? I've got my orders. Besides, I need to conserve energy, remember?"

"Fine," Luke grunted. "I'll be right back."
