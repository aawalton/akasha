My Lord,

His Eminence requests that you dispatch yourself with utmost haste to the Scattered Creek Training Hall. Your most recent pupils were accepted as students there upon the condition that we make available a Teacher should one be needed.

Your familiarity with the twins is of secondary consideration for this role. There is apparently a student capable of Bladesong who would benefit from your experience and skills as a trainer.

It is understood that this will disrupt your ongoing and short term plans for the next few decades. His Eminence extends his regret that he cannot find anyone more suitable to send in your place.

~ * ~

Anya looked between the two groups as they made their way off the field. The rules had been simple and familiar. Everyday Husks, no lethal blows, no injuries that would prevent training and fight until either surrender or the judge declares you've lost.

She could see the Blehman's pike wielder still frowning at having been told he had lost. Paul Hammerswell had pulled a blow that likely would have left him on bedrest for most of a week, and he hadn't seen it coming. The rest of the fight had had similar outcomes. Marie had managed to counter Robin, the archer, with a few well placed Energystic beams while Peter had gotten his axe caught in Nora Blehman's shield.

In the end the Hammerswells had been found victorious with Sasha surrendering, apparently he was there as their healer? She'd have expected a different purpose for him but if that was his Path that was his Path.

That was… Seb sent, trailing off into uncertainty.

Underwhelming? she sent back.

No? I mean it was chaotic, it was fairly quick, but they all seemed uncertain when…

When they faced a group who could change plans and not fight more or less predictably?

She felt Seb agreeing even as Sarna took to the field.

"Before we have another duel, does anyone have anything to say about the match?"

"Luke Blehman. Why was I deemed defeated?" the polearm wielder asked.

"Because I swept behind you and could have broken your back," Paul yelled.

"As he said," Sarna replied. "Had he not pulled his blow you would have been badly injured. You need to remain aware of your surroundings."

"That's Robin's role," Luke said.

Does he simply- Seb began as Sarna spoke.

"She was defeated. You need to retain your own awareness, specifically for cases such as that. What would happen in the Depths if she was knocked out?"

"She wouldn't be, we-"

"She was," Marie taunted. "Just admit you don't pay attention."

Again? Doesn't she get tired? Anya sent.

She could feel Seb trying not to chuckle.

Think a loss or two will change anything for her?

Hopefully, she answered.

"That being said," Sarna continued as though neither Marie nor Luke had spoken. "Both teams need to work on situational awareness and coordination. You all do excellently against typical Depthsborn, they are mostly predictable and can be handled through routine, however against an opponent who adjusts and learns along with you none of you seemed prepared."

"Our trainers have us practice against one another," Peter said.

"And I'm certain you do quite well at variations against a known opponent. That still leaves you unprepared for the unexpected."

Seb!

She felt his thoughts aligning with hers before flashing thoughts of various experiments they had performed while sparring, and then even when fighting against Depthsborn despite the added risk.

"Now then, are there any further challenges? Or will I have to pick."

Anya stepped forward, Seb a few steps behind her as she stared across the field until she locked eyes with Gwen. For a moment she saw confusion, only for it to transform into a faint smile.

"Anya Syl. We challenge Gwen and Ca-"

"Accepted," Gwen yelled before she'd finished speaking.

~ * ~

Seb held his sword loosely as he studied the pair across from them. Gwen's studded mace made him think of his weapon, large enough to wield two-handed but clearly meant for one-handed use most of the time. The tiny buckler on her other arm wouldn't protect her hand, or get in the way of it.

Cara on the other hand certainly looked the part of a guardian. The sharp glow of her hair matched the shine of a shield almost as tall as she was, her sword easily a match for his. He doubted she'd be limited in the injuries it could cause however.

Focus on Cara and try to keep Gwen's attention? he sent.

You owe me for any bruises.

At least there's no slimes.

He felt Anya's revulsion at the memory of their last fight with slimes even as they readied themselves. Hopefully the fight would be over fairly quickly, in their favour of course, but he did wonder what they would find themselves up against.

"Fight!" Sarna called out and everyone moved.

Seb tried to circle as Anya closed on Cara, only to find Gwen pacing him in the opposite direction. Why couldn't things simply be easy and she come face him directly? Sighing he moved to flank Cara as he felt Anya's Energy ripple outward, issuing her challenge and momentarily distracting the two.

Seizing the moment he threw his sword, aiming at the ground behind Cara rather than at anyone in particular, if he missed it would take that much longer to retrieve it and there was no point in that. Gwen charged him as Anya and Cara clashed, only the sound of footfalls and impacts on Cara's shield marking the intensity. He knew Cara's blade was slicing into Anya's robes, he could feel her frustration as blow after blow landed despite her best efforts, as she failed to get past the shield.

Parrying Gwen's swing, his arm ringing momentarily from impact of the haft, he pulsed the hindrances flowing through the blade. He felt Cara stumble, saw Gwen's surprise as the edge of the pulse touched her as well. It wasn't enough, Cara's shield kept flashing, kept moving as he saw Energy guiding it to keep her safe even if it threatened to wrench her arm.

Gwen retreated, standing back to back with her sworn, his sword unreachable between them. He wouldn't be able to get within range to strike, he wasn't Anya, he might be able to dance with her, to move with her as she struck out, but he knew her, they knew each other well enough that there were no surprises during the fight.

The blade pulsed again even as he recalled it, the edge crashing against Gwen's leg before it flew into his waiting hand, giving him the opportunity to strike. Despite the opening Cara's shield was there waiting even as she gasped at Anya finally landing a blow. He felt Anya wince in return, a trace of blood on her arm from the counter.

This isn't working, he sent.

It is, they just-

-have longer reach?

They circled slowly, all four rotating clockwise as they remained squared off. He could see frustration on Gwen's face, he knew she saw the same. If they'd been fighting to kill it might have been different, he might have struck more aggressively, but then again their weapons were just as deadly.

Flick? he sent.

No, don't show them you can do that. We need some sort of secret.

Limiting your techniques?

Same thing.

They must have seemed distracted. Gwen somehow rolled over Cara, lunging at Anya in a vicious flurry as Cara bore down on him. He backpedaled, staying out of her reach before managing to leap to the side and break free. He threw his sword blindly, trusting Anya's awareness to guide him. Once again Cara was there to block, the ring of the sword on her shield louder than it had any right to be.

They couldn't get past her, couldn't land a blow on her. How were they supposed to overcome someone who was clearly there to prevent harm. He was out of position, too far to help as the pair turned on his wife. She danced, pushing herself to her limits and he thought it would be enough.

He was five strides away when the first blow glanced off her, unbalancing her just long enough to break her rhythm. Four and she was lunging for freedom the way he would. Three and he knew she had no chance. Before his foot hit the ground music wrapped around the four of them, Anya's movements gaining the slightest edge even as Cara's aim seemed to waver.

He didn't slow, bodily crashing into their backs even as the sword hit his hand, every movement in time to the Bladesong. He felt them stumble away, felt them start to turn and regroup, for the first time split apart while he and Anya held the center. He found himself facing Cara only to watch her lower her blade as Gwen called out their surrender.

"What!?" Anya said even as she fought to steady her breath.

"Bladesong," Gwen said before turning to yell at her twin. "He is a Bladesinger."

"You know?" Seb asked even as he let the song fade.

"Bladesong is rare among us, yet compared to among Humanity? It might as well be merely infrequently seen."

"That doesn't explain your surrendering," Anya said.

"We were losing. Against Bladesong we stood no chance today," Cara said, her hair back to merely shiny as her weapons vanished.

"Well done, all of you," Sarna said as she approached. "Saving a surprise for a critical moment is a key part of a duel. As is working together."

Seb took a moment to look at Anya's cut, barely more than a scratch despite the traces of blood.

Bruises?

Yes, she answered. Nothing too bad though.

They moved back to their side of the arena, both smiling, their thoughts still melded. They'd won, they'd faced a pair who had clearly trained closely together, and they'd won. The Fae knew about Bladesong, they knew something of what it meant for the battle. They would get their answers, neither of them doubted it.
