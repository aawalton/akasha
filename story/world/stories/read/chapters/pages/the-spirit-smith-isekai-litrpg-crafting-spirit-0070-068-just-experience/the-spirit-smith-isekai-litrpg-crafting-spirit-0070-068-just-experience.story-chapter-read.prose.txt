I channel all of my remaining energy into one last Plane Step. As a result, I manage a ridiculous teleportation of two hundred meters in one go. Right onto the top of the finish line of our race, also known as a random hill.

Moments later, Ari comes flying past me, overshooting the hill and flying off the edge. A giggle escapes me as she catches some air before losing control and plummeting to the ground. The dull thump that she makes as she hits the ground would be concerning if she didn't have that ridiculous armor.

“You okay?” I ask after I walk over to her. She is just lying on the ground, seemingly having lost all will to move.

“You won.” She murmurs.

“It was pretty close. Some might even call it a draw.”

“Not a chance. You won, fair and square. Well, that teleportation skill is downright cheating. It's not fair that you get one and I don’t.”

“Wait, you don’t? I could’ve sworn you’ve teleported a few times.”

“Nah, that is just a really fast movement skill. Allows me to quickly reposition in close quarters, mostly. Far inferior to a true teleportation skill, as it can be interrupted, and can’t be extended by pumping more energy into it.”

“Huh, so I should feel lucky that I got a teleport skill so early then. It has come in quite handy.”

“I would bet. But you still can’t match my attacks.” She hops onto her feet, “Watch this!”

Before I can think to stop her, she already has her rifle out and anchored to the ground. I can only watch, mesmerized, as energy builds up in the rifle. With a thundering crack that almost knocks me off my feet, the attack is released all at once.

The hill that she was aiming for ceases to exist. Like, literally goes up in a column of fire so bright that I am forced to avert my eyes. When it dissipates, all that is left is a very large red rock. It is a bit shiny, and my body immediately fills with the urge to harvest it. After all, it must be quite tough to withstand such an attack.

“Oh shit.” She says just as I start to step towards it. She appears in front of me, placing a hand on my shoulder. “Orn, this is the part where we run.”

“Why?” I ask just as the world starts to shake.

I watch in horror as the shiny red boulder moves. Not a boulder then. My Identify gets to work, and says that it is a [Red Crystal Golem - Level 48]. Moments later, that fact is quickly made apparent to my eyes.

The golem is gigantic, easily ten meters tall. Oh, and it is mad. Really mad. Tends to happen when someone launches a hill-detonating explosive at the home of a monster.

A roar shakes the air, but unfortunately, Ari collapses to the ground at that exact moment. “I uh, may be out of stamina, and mana, and literally everything.” I sigh and bend down, wrapping my arm around Ari. “Wait what are you-” She screams as I throw her over my shoulder like a really big metal bar.

She is quite a bit lighter than I imagined. Which is crazy, because that armor is super thick and should not be this light. Oh well, that just makes it all the easier for me to turn the opposite direction of the golem, and start running the fuck away.

I run across the vast plains beyond the hills as fast as I can. Heck, I even activate my muscle enhancement. But Ari is slowing me down quite a bit. “Orn, uh, the golem is gaining on us.”

Glancing back, I realize that she is right. It is still about a kilometer back, but it is closing the distance. Interestingly enough, it seems to be quite tall, and its legs long and slender. What the fuck sort of golem is this? It is like it was meant to chase people down.

“Unless you’ve got any tricks, we will just have to fight it then.” I say.

“I’m like, all outa stuffs. But I think I can move now, if you could please set me down. This is quite embarrassing.” Ari says, and I stop running to set her down. After dusting herself off, she pats me on the shoulder. “You will be on your own. It is only level forty eight, it should be quite easy now that we’ve crossed fifty.”

I laugh, “Well, guess I can clean up this mess you have made. But you are gonna owe me, you know.”

“Really? Wasn’t it you that goaded me into such a display?”

“Certainly not.” I say, and before she can retort, the ground starts to shake again. The golem is here, and I summon Luxmore and Stelan. Then I Plane Step as far away from Ari as I can to draw the golem away.

Thankfully, it senses that I am more of a danger and comes after me first. It at first tries to throw some rocks at me from a distance, but I easily dodge those so it gives up. The thing is quite fast over open terrain, but as I get up close, I learn that it lacks dexterity.

I swing up into the side of its knee and my blow connects with a satisfying crack of the crystal. The thing is tough, but with enough effort I think I can break it. Seeing me dancing about its feet, the golem uses some sort of earthquake attack.

One of the quakes catches me the moment I land, and throws me to the ground. Before I can roll to my feet, the golem’s foot comes crashing down onto my right leg. I hear some stuff crack, which I am pretty sure are my bones.

Not hesitating, I pop a regeneration potion and manage to use my other leg to Plane Step away. Guess my theory about not having to be standing to use it is true. I just need to somehow make the movement of my foot stepping.

The potion works quickly and within twenty seconds I am back on my feet, just in time to dodge a swing of the golem’s arm. Using the opportunity to chant, I cast Ice Burst.

Dovey, think you could give me a boost? I ask the bird who is hovering up in the sky looking for something to do. The golem is too tough for him to actually damage, but that does not mean he cannot contribute.

A gust of wind whips up around me just as the golem is caught up in the wave of ice. It freezes the monster solid, trapping it. The wind throws me up into the air, and I quickly activate Spark Drill. Plummeting downwards, my hammer strikes the monster and a deafening crack echoes out across the plains.

The monster shatters like glass under my repeated hits. It would seem my instincts were right. The monster wasn’t actually made out of crystal, but rather a type of metal that merely seemed crystalline. As it turns out, many metals can get quite brittle when cooled down.

Suffice to say, that theory did in fact apply to this monster. As the ensuing ding is an indication of.

‘Ding’, You have slain [Red Crystal Golem - Level 48].

‘Ding’, Spirit Mage has advanced to level 49. Stat points distributed.

Huh, I didn't expect to get a level, let alone one in Spirit Mage. But I guess Dovey’s efforts somehow converted to experience? Or perhaps the magic I used counted? Either way, I will take a few more stat points for sure.

I am currently standing in the middle of the monster’s corpse. So, I take out my harvesting dagger and get to work. The material is a bit hard to collect given how it is super sharp and cuts me constantly, but I do manage. Whether I find a use for it personally, or sell it, it will definitely come in handy.

Once I get enough of it, I inspect the weird material to learn about it.

Red Crystalite Ore (Uncommon)

A strange red ore that many mistake for a crystal at first. But in reality, it is actually a metal. Despite that, it does share several properties of crystals, namely the toughness and sharpness. It would be great to turn into some sort of blade, and if one can deal with the weight, it would make great armor. It also has high inherent mana conductivity, making it great for all sorts of magical gadgets.

The material is quite useful in its ore form already. But proper processing can of course improve its properties even further.

Yep, definitely quite an interesting material. I got a whole bunch of it, so maybe it would even be good for practicing some metallurgy. I should probably learn how to make metals and alloys at some point, even if I am not the best at it.

For now though, my attention is drawn to a shiny orb that I found in the middle of the golem. My inspect skill returns a result almost instantly.

Major Crystal Golem Core (Rare)

A core used in the formation of a golem. This core is of exceptionally high quality, obviously created by a master golemancer. Its primary purpose is in the animation of a golem, given the size of the core, it can be used to create quite a large one. It could also just be used as a generic core ingredient for equipment or other such things that are not golems. Though do be aware that using golem cores in standard crafting can cause unusual effects.

Traces of malice lay within the core, however. Thus it will need to be purified before being used to create anything.

A golem core, huh? I haven't heard much about golems, but I guess this implies the existence of golemancers. Or at least those that can make golems somehow. I bet this core is worth quite a lot of gold. I could also use it in my own crafting, and part of me really wants to find out what those unusual effects are.

“Orn! Are you okay?” Ari shouts from just beyond the monster’s giant corpse.

I Plane Step to escape and appear next to her. “Yeah, got a bit dinged up, but nothing a potion couldn’t fix.”

“Did you really use the cold to make the golem brittle? That’s genius! Like seriously, that is a plan I would've come up with. Who knew you were so smart.”

“Well, uh, I guess I just have experience. You know, from dealing with metals and stuff.” I try to remain calm, but the compliments definitely go to my head. Though it isn't lost on me that Ari managed to sneak in an ego boost of her own.

She slaps me on the back. “Well then, guess we better hope that experience applies to more situations.”

“You could also just be more careful with your resources. Oh, and not blow up random hills just to prove your power.”

“Pfft. But what fun would that be? Plus I’ve got you here to back me up.”

I sigh, but it quickly turns into laughter. Yeah, this whole traveling thing is going to be a whole lot of fun. But something tells me this isn't the last bit of trouble we are going to get into.
