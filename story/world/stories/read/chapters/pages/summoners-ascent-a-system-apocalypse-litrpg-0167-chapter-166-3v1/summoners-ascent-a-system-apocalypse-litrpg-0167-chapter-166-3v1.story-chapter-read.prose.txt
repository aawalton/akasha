Ashton stretched lightly as he walked a few steps away. The car park had been cleared out relatively well, since they were using the cars as foundations in front of the walls to build platforms onto, letting people look out and check on the situation outside.

That left them with a relatively large usable area that couldn't be seen directly from the classrooms where most people were gathered right now.

"You want to fight us? I'm not exactly in the business of beating up blind people, you know?" Chris scoffed loudly. "This would be pretty unfair."

Ashton just replied with a laugh. "I know, and to make it more fair, I won't even be using any magic. Not even the magic that's letting me see that smug grin on your face right now."

Chris raised a brow. "Wait, but your eyes are... You're saying you can still see?"

"It's complicated, but a little, yes. But for this fight, I'm going to be completely, actually blind," Ashton pointed out. This was actually a pretty good opportunity for him. There was no way those three could actually beat him in a fight, so he could push his limits in a quite safe way.

"... So why would we do this? We can just take the kids and–"

"If you win, I'll personally help you escort the five children to the university." Ashton was getting ready, giving his items to the Backpacker. He even gave them his shoes. Fighting barefooted was a bit more comfortable considering these circumstances.

Chris, Mickey and Bart started discussing things. Clearly, they figured this was going to be pretty easy for them, so they didn't have to talk long to come to a decision.

"Alright, sure. That would make things much easier," Chris said, slowly putting his helmet back on.

"Perfect! And in return, when you lose, you guys are going to go and only come back when you're ready to follow the rules," Ashton replied, but the three barely even listened. "Oh, and on top of that, I'm getting the armour you're wearing."

Chris stopped. "Huh? What do you mean?"

"I mean that I'm going to get your armour. You know, the big hunk of metal you're wearing for whatever reason."

"Dude, I need this armour, it's part of my class, why would I–"

"You're an Armoured Swordsman, right? The armour you got in the beginner gear should have been a hint, but they don't wear full suits of plate armour. The one you'll get when the quest is over is going to be much better for you, and it will be at least uncommon grade, so you're going to give this armour up soon anyway."

Chris looked around at his friends, not sure what to say. He was clearly confused how Ashton knew his class, but Mickey dismissed him.

"Just shut up, he's not going to win anyway. If he doesn't use that weird magic or whatever, how would he beat the three of us?" Mickey asked, and Chris nodded.

"Okay, you're right. Let's do this, then," Chris agreed. "We've been up all night, I want to just grab those brats and get out of here."

"Great! Everyone heard it, right?" Ashton asked, and the teachers all mumbled in agreement, though they weren't sure what to think. They knew that Ashton was strong, but most of them hadn't actually seen him in action. They were probably thinking that most of his strength came from his summons.

"Backpacker, could you hold this? Don't put it away. Poppy and the treasury pixie are still in the pockets," he asked as he pulled off his cloak. He still wasn't wearing a shirt, and right now, since he took off his shoes, only had some slightly ripped up cargo pants on. And the mask covering his eyes, of course. He really looked like a mess.

But even so, he happily grabbed the barbell and dismissed all his summons beside Poppy and the Backpacker back into his vessel.

"Holy shit, what the fuck happened to you...?" Mickey asked. His voice sounded shaky. But why..?

"Oh, is it because of the scars? Aren't they cool?" Ashton replied with a laugh. "Yeah I don't know, you just accumulate these if you do what I do. But I've made some pretty strong potions, so most of these don't have lasting damage. Anyway, let's get started. It's a little chilly today, don't you think?"

Ashton took out a coin from his system window. "When this coin hits the ground, we start. Alright?"

The three agreed, and seemed like they were getting ready. From the sound of it, although he didn't participate in the fight Ashton saw through the pocket watch, Bart was going to join in as well. He was probably going to fight like a grappler, at least that's how he always was in the past, so as long as Ashton could avoid him that was fine.

And now, Ashton flipped the coin high into the air. He picked up his staff and got ready. The coin landed just a second later, with a slight 'clang' as it hit the stone ground.

The first sound that Ashton could hear was the shaking of Chris' armour as he ran forward. Hidden behind that were some quieter footsteps as Mickey got into a good position, and then some knives rubbed against leather straps.

There was a slightly stronger sensation of mana from Mickey's direction as he started to use a skill. There was a quiet whooshing sound as a knife filled with mana was thrown at Ashton. He slightly tilted his staff and diverted the knife, though he barely had to do so anyway. It would have barely hit the side of his left leg if Ashton hadn't moved at all.

The second knife fully just missed Ashton, pulling past his shoulder. There was a very simple reason why these two knives were ineffective. It was the same reason why Chris was trembling and uneasy while swinging his sword. Ashton was sliding over the ground with minimal movements, avoiding every swing of that sword with ease, for two reasons.

One, because Chris' armour was so loud that Ashton could guess every tiny movement. And two, because Chris was scared.

He wasn't scared of Ashton, obviously. He was scared to hit Ashton. This was the reason why Mickey missed with the knives, too. If they managed to hit Ashton, who wasn't wearing any layer of protection at all, it could get lethal very fast.

These guys were assholes. Ashton didn't like them at all, but in the grand scheme of things, that was just because of the feelings Ashton had from before being dragged to Lumia. If he had met them after that, they wouldn't even have annoyed him.

The three clearly didn't like Ashton either, though he had no idea why they hated him so much. They were empty feelings that came from a simple clash of personalities, from what he could guess.

And they weren't the sort that could kill someone for a reason like that. At the end of the day, they were still humans. They weren't evil... they were just assholes.

Ashton continued to dodge with minimal effort, and in the end, positioned himself so that the crowd of teachers was somewhere behind him. This essentially sealed away Mickey's throwing completely.

With that, Ashton thought maybe he should go a small step further, and slid away ever so slightly less. Chris' sword cut vertically across his chest, from his right shoulder to right below his left pec. Warm blood flowed down Ashton's chest. It was a wound that barely even scratched the skin, all things considered, but it definitely looked flashy. Enough to make Chris' movements stutter.

It was enough for Ashton to push the barbell under Chris' armpit. He could hear the sound of metal scraping against metal, until suddenly that sound stopped and his staff was slightly lodged in place. The armour was protecting the side of Chris' chest, but to allow for more freedom of movement, there obviously wasn't any metal covering certain parts of important joints. But through the slight overhang that was created from the armour's construction, there were plenty of such things to take advantage of.

With the staff relatively solidly in place, Ashton pulled Chris closer toward him. Right now, if he was right, the sword should be pointed right at Ashton's abdomen. Chris freaked out and quickly pulled the blade away, lowering his dominant hand.

Ashton hooked his foot against the flat of the sword and traced it up to Chris' wrist. Twisting his body, Ashton pushed forward one end of his staff and kicked Chris' hand back up. The staff was now under Chris' armpit and on his elbow, and Ashton quickly pulled down on the front end of his staff while continuing to push up against the man's arm with his foot.

Honestly, it was a relatively slow movement that could have been countered by a more experienced and level-headed opponent, but Ashton knew that he had the leisure to take his time.

Fighting while completely blind was a dangerous thing, so he wanted to engrain every single experience fully in his mind and body.

Chris felt the strain on his arm. It wasn't the side that had been injured before, so it didn't hurt quite as much, but that also meant the only hand he had to attack Ashton right now was that injured one with its limited range of movement.

Ashton heard some heavy, dull footsteps approaching fast, like a runaway train aimed right at him. Those grunted, suppressed breaths belonged to Bart.

With a smile, as soon as Bart came scarily close, Ashton dropped low to the ground and slid down under Chris' arm, pulling his staff up once he was behind him.

Chris yelled out in pain as he dropped his sword and Ashton pulled his staff away from him. With a swift motion, he swept the barbell over the ground until he found the sword's hilt and guard, pulling it toward his feet. With a swift motion, he pushed the hilt onto his foot and then kicked the sword up, right when Bart was trying to grab him.

But Ashton swung his staff around and pushed against Bart's belly to force himself further away.

Bart tried to grab the barbell, but Ashton spun it around its axis to make it harder to hold onto while pulling it away, and then used the staff to vault himself up. He pushed his staff back up above his head and hooked it into the sword's guard once more, pushing it straight down with the hilt forward.

"Bart, watch–" Mickey yelled, trying to warn his friend, but before he could finish the sword had already hit Bart's arm and a loud crack followed.

He didn't seem to notice the pain of the injury directly, and just tried to grab Ashton again, but the moment he wanted to put any strength into his right arm he groaned and pulled back.

Ashton caught himself and pushed the staff into the ground to stabilise himself as he swept Bart off his feet.

Now, the only one left in a fighting position was Mickey. He had been shifting around the entire time, trying to find a good angle to fight from. It made him a little hard to keep track of when there was so much going on, but when he yelled just now, it made things a lot easier.

Ashton ran at him and heard another knife being pulled from some kind of leather sheath. Instead of throwing it this time, though, Mickey seemed to just hold onto it, and then stayed completely silent. He must have realised that Ashton mostly kept track of everyone through sound.

This would have worked, if it weren't for the fact that, at this distance, Ashton could sense Mickey's mana perfectly fine as well. Ashton vaulted over him and landed with the staff hitting the ground first, then kicked against Mickey's back and pushed him toward Chris.

He had been trying to recover and pick up his sword, but soon, Mickey's head slammed into the hard metal armour. Mickey laid flat on the ground a moment later, as Chris also once again lost his balance.

And with all three of them on the ground, Ashton approached them with a light skip in his step.

"I think that's my win, right?"
