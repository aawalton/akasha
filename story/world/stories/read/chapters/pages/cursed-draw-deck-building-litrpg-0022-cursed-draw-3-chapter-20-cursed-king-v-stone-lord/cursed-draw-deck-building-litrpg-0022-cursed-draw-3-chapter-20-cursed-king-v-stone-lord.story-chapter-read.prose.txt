While Alley couldn’t read the text that denoted what Sage of the Stone Palace actually did, he had seen the ape-man creature played before and had a rough understanding of its capabilities.

So when Vern Manifested Tectonic Hammer Alley was prepared for the creature's Power to jump significantly, as the ape improved Relics that were targeting it. By Alley's calculations, it wouldn't matter, so long as the white-bearded man didn't get an opportunity to target Sage of the Stone Palace with his Hall of the Underlord spell at the start of his next turn, the sage would already have been defeated, and likely so would Vern.

Tectonic Hammer appeared with a lot more pomp and ceremony than anything else on the field, which made sense as it was a unique Card, but was still a little amusing since it was a Relic and not a Creature. In fact, Alley wasn’t sure he had ever seen a unique Relic other than this one before.

Obviously used to and prepared for the hammer's entrance, Vern took several steps back until he was deep in the cut-in-half tower that was the result of the ongoing land manipulation spell.

Alley didn't blame him when, rather than appearing in the usual blue light of Manifestation, the ground violently split open on Vern's side of the field. The chasm the Card created was only about ten feet by ten feet, but molten rock hot enough that Alley could feel it burst out of the split earth, launching a solid twenty feet in the air.

The liquid earth fell back down with a mighty splash, and the earth closed again. Where once had been a blast of superheated lava hovered a hammer that was almost as tall as Alley. The head of the  weapon was a collection of dark and heavy-looking square plates of iron, along with  circular discs. The handle that made up most of the hammer’s length was made of grooved stone, which would serve well to grip, if it wasn’t split by occasional and thin veins of magma.

The amount of strength a person would need to really wield such a weapon was absolutely absurd. Alley thought you would need to be somewhere near Soren’s rank, and even then, it was probably a heavy and awkward thing to use.

Alley shook his head and refocused on the match itself; it wasn’t important if the Relics his opponent used wouldn’t be practical under normal circumstances. What was important was what they did in the context of being Manifested, and this particular Relic was nothing to take lightly.

​

​

​

“I target Sage Of The Stone Palace.”

The wizard-ape raised a hand, and the hammer immediately appeared in its grasp. The creature’s weight didn’t shift at all, even though it was now holding up a hammer taller than it that likely weighed more than it too.

Alley repressed a wince as the creature’s Power jumped by two thousand, a thousand more than the hammer innately granted. It left the ape-man at four thousand one hundred Power. Not enough to challenge Alley’s Necrotic Fiend, but all Vern needed to do was protect The Sage of The Stone Palace with the Crystaline Barrier that would spawn at the end of his turn, and he could target the ape-wizard with Hall of The Underlord, and it would be in a position to wipe Alley’s side of the field out in no time.

Alley was in position to completely take over the match before that happened, which was a good thing too, as a longer match would not favor Alley after the way he had poisoned himself. Besides, he really wanted to go drink water and then lie down until his head stopped spinning and his body stopped aching. A hot bath somewhere in there sounded good, too.

The white-bearded man redrew to replace his Cards and ended his turn without Discarding. Once again, Shard of the Living Mountain activated as Vern ended his turn, creating yet another Crystaline Barrier.

‘This had better work.’ Alley thought to himself as he dragged the tip of his hook across his left palm. He had already slipped his cloak off, and it sat in a heap at his feet. Alley reached down with his bleeding hand and grabbed hold of the cloak, which instantly reacted. The ragged and dark garment instantly wrapped itself  around the wound and began to undulate like a living thing as it drained Alley’s blood.

Keeping a close eye on his Vials Alley immediately began fighting the cloak once he had regained a full Vial. Still poisoned by the use of foreign Resonance and then weakened by the blood loss, getting the Cloak of The Bloodless One away from him proved to be almost enough to make Alley pass out. Thankfully, the cloak instantly returned to normal the moment none of it was touching Alley’s wound.

Stumbling away from the cloak that now sat inert on the arena floor, Alley considered his situation. If he took a hit from this point on in the match, he wouldn’t have the stored Life Essence to heal himself during the match. Not to mention, with the way Ally felt, he wasn’t confident in his ability to dodge an attack if something made it past his other defenses.

Well, it was time to see if all that he had spent to win this Challenge would pay off.

“Wraith Master!”

The Creature appeared in a surge of blue light. Wraith Master was a hooded figure in black robes that hid its dessicated skin. Once its Manifestation was complete, the Undead creature raised a hand to its head height with a palm facing upwards. An orb of teal colored energy appeared in the open palm. Within the orb appeared images of the four spells in Alley’s Crypt.

‘Hunter’s Descent, Bone Shield, Soul Exchange, and Bone Weaved.’

The Undead spellcaster released the orb, which briefly floated in front of it before slamming down into the ground. A second later, four pale ghosts with skin as white as the robes they wore and  faces covered by blank masks that had large holes for the eyes, yet revealed nothing but darkness beyond.

​

​

The four Faceless Wraiths were pitifully weak,and Wraith Master was hardly a heavy hitter in its own right. In terms of power for cost, even four of the wraiths and their master were hardly worth two Vials, especially since those Vials had cost Alley a lot of his blood. It wasn’t really  about the Power of the Wraith Master, and the ghosts it created represented that mattered; it was the way the Card interacted with other Cards in The Crown of the Cursed King.

Cards like Shadowed Eater.

Suddenly, the creepy mass of shadows and rags crouched down over something Alley couldn’t see, shoveling whatever it was hidden in the shadows, the Eater began to make an extremely unsettling chewing sound. Finishing after what felt like an uncomfortable amount of time, the Shadowed Eater stood back to its full height, when it did its Power leaped from twenty-four hundred Power to six thousand four hundred. It’s own ability plus the negative effect of Hall of the Underlord makes it much more Powerful than it had begun.

The sole Crystaline Barrier could no longer be relied on to keep Sage of the Stone Palace alive, which meant Alley was about to leave Vern with no creatures on the field.

Moving on to his War Phase, Alley redrew a Card which proved to be useless. It was a Water Resonance Spell called Split The Seas, and there was no possibility of Alley playing another off-Resonance Card today.

It didn’t matter; first, Alley attacked with Necrotic Fiend. The horned monstrosity raised its hand and summoned a flaring orb of Purple Undeath Resonance and hurled it at Sage of the Stone Palace. To Vern’s credit, he held his nerve and didn’t use his Crystaline Barrier.  That still meant he had to endure Necrotic Fiend having the chance to attack him directly, as it had just defeated another creature.

The white-bearded man once more braced himself and activated his stone-skinned ability. No doubt it protected him from a great deal of the magical assault, but four thousand five hundred Power was a big hit to take at the best of times.

The ball of purple Resonance that the Necrotic Fiend  hurled at Vern blasted the man off his feet, even with his  defensive ability active. A second later, when the stone faded away, the older man looked much worse for wear. His shirt was mostly scorched away, and his skin looked like it had been hit with scaling water. Alley couldn’t tell how many from this side of the field, but a significant chunk of the Vials that hovered around the older man’s arm emptied to begin an accelerated healing. The damage and cost to regenerate from it had been significant, yet it was still the right move to hold off on using the Crystaline Barrier.  As bad as being struck by Necrotic Fiend was, enduring an attack from the empowered Shadowed Eater would have been  much worse. Alley still attacked with the creature, knowing that it would be negated simply to keep clearing the Barrier Relics.

As predicted, Alley’s opponent sacrificed his Barrier to keep himself from being attacked again. Ending his turn, Alley Discarded Split the Seas, and waited for Vern to try and rebuild his side of the field. The older man took around a minute to look at his Cards and breathe heavily, holding the injured side of his chest.

Eventually collecting himself, Vern finally played something.

“Maiden Of The Mines!”

Alley hadn’t seen this Card before, but judging from the way Vern’s eyes briefly flashed shining blue, it allowed him to look into his Library to some degree or another. When his eyes returned to normal Vern shook his head in disgust. He took a few more seconds to observe both sides of the field.

“This is ridiculous,” grumbled the older man. “You look like you are about to drop dead, but I’m the one who has to surrender. I Yield!”
