Hey there!

Over half a year has passed since I last posted anything on this story. Well, that changes today. I have two announcements for you.

First, the obvious. Book 4 is now out on Amazon. Like always, every bit of support is appreciated. You guys are what made it possible for me to get this far, even if AUOBE never became a hit on Amazon. I am still proud of it as my first-ever original series. So yeah, you will find the link to book 4 below. I will also attach all the other books there.

Now, for the second thing.

I have been thinking about this for a while, and I've finally decided that leaving AUOBE as it is would be a shame. Unfortunately, I am incapable of writing it full-time, not with its frankly poor performance after publication. I just don't have the time. Still, I would like to finish this series one day, or at least write one more book for it. That's why I came up with a little compromise.

You see, I am currently working on another project that isn't Virus: Origin of Blood or AUOBE. I plan to release it on RoyalRoad in like 2-3 months, maybe later. Now, once the book is released and has had some time to establish itself here, I plan to return to AUOBE. It won't be a big return, mind you, but a chapter every week or two? That I can probably manage. There would be no fixed schedule, that's for sure. I would simply be posting when I have the time.

I know that might sound unfair to all of you who have followed AUOBE since the beginning, but that's life. Writing is not only my hobby, but also my job, so I gotta do what I gotta do.

And that's it. Thank you all for reading. Now go check out book 4 of AUOBE on AMAZON :D
