The cries of dozens of people continued to fill the cathedral. It was a sound that would tighten the heart of any that heard it. Cries of grief that were forced out through the magic these people were forced under.

Some of them fell to their knees, despairing, but others' sadness manifested differently. A few ran at Ashton, trying to attack him, while others took to hurting themselves as if to atone for letting Jonah die.

The magic that was affecting them of course wasn't released instantly. There was something else that was needed for that.

A potion of mental clarity. It was a potion that could remove external influences on the mind and protect one from encroachment. Ashton didn't have everything he needed to make one of these on hand, but he should be able to get the rest soon enough. Mass-production wouldn't be possible, but it should hopefully still do the job even diluted to make sure everyone here could improve.

But for now, there were other things that Ashton had to do. While he made the potion that Millie used to wake him up, those 'magical smelling salts', he made another potion as well. One that did the opposite.

Ashton took a small bottle from his pocket and tossed the contents into the air. Through the undine and sylph, Ashton dissolved the liquid into a mist that he quickly spread throughout the room. The people that, until now, could not stop their tears, were almost immediately put to sleep. Ashton kept the potion away from Clementine.

She was the only one that Ashton could give a moment to try and process this all. But when he approached her, the moment he got close enough for her to notice, Clementine glared up at him.

"How could you?!" she yelled with a voice of poison that stung deep in Ashton's skin. It was a pain that he had come here expecting, but hoped he wouldn't need to feel. "You killed him–you killed my dad, I–I told you to stop!"

Ashton swallowed the boulder in his throat. "You knew what we were coming here for, Clem. I–"

"Don't 'Clem' me! What the fuck is wrong with you? You said that you would do what I wanted, but you just–you were going to kill him either way! Right? You were!"

Ashton just stood there for a moment. He wasn't sure what to say. She was right. He had lied to her.

"Yes, I was. I'm sorry, but that man... especially the way that he was now, has hurt so many people. Look behind you. Everyone here is bruised and bleeding and barely hanging on," Ashton explained, grinding his teeth. "And this isn't a world where we can just lock someone away and be done with it. Some people can't be redeemed anymore."

Clementine didn't look back, and just kept staring at Ashton. "But–But he wasn't all bad, he's done nice things for me! For you! He was nice sometimes, right? Right?"

Ashton sighed, kneeling down. "Clementine... Just because someone knows how to act nice, doesn't mean they're a good person. Everyone knows how to be nice when they need to be."

"But he... he was my father, he..." Clementine shook her head. "He was a good man, he just didn't know how to show it, and he..."

Almost like trying to convince herself, Clementine looked over at the lifeless body of her father laying not too far away from her. It was there, unmoving, and it would never come to move again. Tears were flowing down her cheeks, gathering on her chin before falling to the marble floors.

"I... My head, I just... I loved him, but I also hated him, but... the love is so much louder, it's like..." Clementine leaned forward. "Like it's the only thing the voice in my head knows how to say..."

"It'll be okay... it's magic, it's going to wear off at some point."

Clementine, again, shook her head. "This pain is going to wear off? These complicated feelings? Before all of this, I didn't want to talk to him, but I didn't want him dead, I... I wanted to see if there was a path where we could live alongside each other, where we could be a normal father and daughter, but now... now we... we just can't..."

[[Oldest Unbeliever] glares at you for the crime you have committed]

[[King of a Godless World] is furious with you]

[[Smallest Shadow] refuses to let you get away with this]

Ashton noticed these messages pop up beside him. The 'Oldest Unbeliever' and 'King of a Godless World' were constellations that he didn't know, but the 'Smallest Shadow' was deeply familiar. It was just like Ashton thought. It was the 'Coalition of Small Constellations' at it again.

"Don't you guys have something better to do?" Ashton barked at the air. "Sorry that I've messed with your plan to tame the fucking wyvern, now buzz off. You know you can't touch me anyway."

[[Smallest Shadow] tells you to be careful of the enemies you make]

"Yeah, yeah, buzz off," Ashton clicked his tongue, focusing back on Clementine while starting to ignore the messages the coalition kept sending. "I know they're sending messages to you as well. Don't listen to them, okay?"

"But they..."

"No 'buts', just focus on me, okay? Come, stand up," Ashton said, reaching out his hand toward his friend. Shakily, she pushed herself up, refusing the help.

The aversion that Clementine felt toward Ashton at this very moment was stuck in the air like an unmistakeable stink that you weren't able to get rid of no matter what. But it was an aversion that came to her not purely out of her own will; she was fighting against it herself. Which was hard, when even when she wasn't dealing with such mental influence, she still had deeply conflicting feelings toward what Ashton had done.

"Just... don't," Clementine practically whispered, walking up to the large body in front of her. In this moment, it appeared more like some kind of statue that had fallen down to the ground, or some grotesque art installation. It didn't look like the man she knew. Even before, when he was alive and moving and yelling, he didn't look like her father. Though he certainly sounded like it. Even if his voice was deeper and rougher and more imposing, and the way he spoke carried more grace and influence, something about the way he spoke was just like what she knew from him.

And now he was dead. He wouldn't come back, either. Or maybe he would; at this point, there was little that would surprise her. Clementine didn't know how to feel about that one, either, honestly. Now that he was already dead, would she want him to come back if he could?

Clementine sat there for a moment.

"Could you... leave me alone? Just a little?" she asked. Ashton agreed without hesitation. It was the least he could do.

"I'll, uhm..." he thought for a moment. "I'll see if there is anyone else here that wasn't brought into the cathedral. But first..." Ashton walked over to the item that Jonah's body was still clutching. The tome. It was clearly a dangerous item that he wasn't sure he wanted to just leave out here in the open. He would check it out later, and for now, gave it to the Backpacker to stash away, before heading out.

The rest of the people in here would be knocked out cold for a good while longer, so it was fine to just go and leave Clementine on her own for a bit.

And then, Ashton went outside. There were some adjacent buildings that were part of the church's property which Ashton wanted to check out. He had noticed that people had gathered largely there, while almost the whole of the cathedral was left empty at this point in time.

The last time Ashton came here, people were sleeping inside, but it looked like that changed pretty fast. There seemed to be some people actively staying outside out in the open, rather than taking shelter in the cathedral now. There were some tents set up, and tarps that were pulled over bushes. There were people sleeping there, and they seemed to be in a much better state than the ones inside.

When Ashton walked through the buildings, it didn't exactly seem like they were overflowing already; rather, there were plenty of empty rooms, and the ones that were filled up had plenty of more space to set up for others. Though... the people that clearly lived in these spaces were gone, probably sleeping in the cathedral.

There were just a few exceptions here. Children, for one. There were others that were so deeply injured that they clearly couldn't move anymore, or were barely hanging on.

Ashton tried to give them first aid, and even had the Spawn of Akrazoth take care of a couple of them when it seemed like there was something the demon could do to help.

He handed out some food, and tried to help. It didn't seem like this place could persist as a proper 'camp' after this, so the next quest wouldn't acknowledge it as one, either. The people here would have to move soon, and Ashton wanted to try and keep them safe.

He should have some Calibur people try and get them in the morning. Or anyone that would accept help, at least.

But there was one thing that worried Ashton.

Clementine's mother was nowhere to be seen.
