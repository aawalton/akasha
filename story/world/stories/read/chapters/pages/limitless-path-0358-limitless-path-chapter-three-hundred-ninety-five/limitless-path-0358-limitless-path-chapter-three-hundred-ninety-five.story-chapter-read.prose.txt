Bjorn took the lead in entering the room, making sure there wasn't some mechanical monstrosity waiting for them. His concern was good as it was always great to maintain a state of caution and awareness, but he needn't have bothered. The room was empty of beasts, empty of traps, empty of any threat to their safety and well-being. Even if there wasn't much of anything in this final room, Beth would already be quite happy with their gains. Bringing the whole team along had shown massive benefits, especially in discovering and accessing the hidden floor, where she had made a massive gain.

Beth was initially a little disappointed with the contents of the room, having hoped for piles of gold and diamonds, but that disappointment disappeared rather quickly when they realized what exactly was in the room. There were seven items in the center of the room on a high table, though that table would be a nice height for Bjorn. Beth assumed that at least some of the facility, based on what they had seen, was constructed for giants or titans more on Bjorn's stature versus the stature of the mechanical titans.

Beth examined all seven items, noting that two of the items were crystals of some kind, which turned out to both be skill crystals. Apart from that, the other five items were four pieces of equipment and an artifact. The pieces of equipment were a ring, a sword, a chestplate, and a set of gauntlets. The chestplate and the gauntlets didn't match, at least in terms of style and appearance, though it was still possible they could be part of the same armor set. Beth dismissed that after examining the pieces and finding out that they were not part of any set, though they were quite rare and valuable pieces. The ring and the sword were not parts of any set, either, just very good items that more than one of those present could use. The artifact, meanwhile, was something useful in enchanting, not an inscribing tool exactly, but a tool meant to lay out patterns on work pieces and align multiple different runes together. It wasn't likely to be as valuable as the other items, but it was still something an advanced enchanter would love to get their hands on.

"Should we head back before discussing the loot?" Beth asked.

"Now, hang on a minute," Beth said, after finishing stashing the seven items in her necklace. "What's this about a big enough storage space?"

"If you had a big enough storage space, we could take the upgrade core, which I would prefer to do, especially as I didn't see anything about there being one here," he explained. "Which means it's one of only a couple hidden ones scattered throughout the universe. Likely why the Path put a portal to it on a new world to help inspire exploration."

"I have enough room," Beth said confidently. "As long as I don't have to put it in my storage at that size. It won't fit."

"Well, if it won't fit in your storage at that size, then you don't have room," Bjorn said, scrunching his brow and frowning as he stared down at Beth.

"No, no. I have enough room, but I can't just instantly move it in there," Beth explained.

"Ah, your gate," Bjorn said, face relaxing into an aware look as he came to a realization.

"Yeah. Can we move it piece-by-piece or something?" Beth asked.

The rest gathered around while Bjorn started typing, entering a huge number of commands into the panel. He hit two buttons and stepped back with a satisfied nod, but frowned when the panel flashed first white then red. He stepped forward again, frowning as he typed away furiously for a minute before tapping two keys and again getting the panel flashing white then red. His frown deepened and he started typing in a different rhythm, moving through different screens as his brow lowered and his eyes squinted.

"Hmm. No, no. This isn't right…" he muttered. "Where's the unseal? Why'd they change the matrix…"

"Having problems?" Beth asked, watching him work.

"Almost all the commands are changed or non-functional," he responded in a growl. "I don't understand what they did to this system."

"So, it's not supposed to be like that?" Beth asked.

"No. Many of our old technologies were made to be portable, or able to be torn down and set up again somewhere else. I should be able to put this machine into a travel mode and then get it to collapse, but none of the commands are working," he explained, frustration in his voice as he continued typing.

"Is it because it has energy in it?" Beth asked.

"No, no. That's not the problem. That's why we could only pack it away for five to ten minutes, but it should still shutdown and collapse," he answered.

"So, what if we try draining it?" Beth asked.

"Pointless," he said. "This would have been seeded with very concentrated, pure energy. We couldn't even pay an arm and a leg to buy something like that these days. Not in the Milky Way. And then it has gathered and concentrated energy for thousands or tens of thousands of years. If we just shunt all the energy away, it makes the whole thing worthless. Unless you don't mind waiting fifteen or twenty thousand years for it to have even a moderate charge?"

"Okay, point taken," Beth said, holding up her hands briefly in a calming gesture.

"Looks like we aren't getting this," he said with a grunt. "I could try to reprogram it, but that would take days, and I don't know if I could get it to repack even then."

"Well, we know where it is," Val said. "Unless you're telling us it will move or self-destruct or something?"

"No, it will stay here. It will keep slowly gathering energy forever, or until it breaks down, but these things were built to last to just shy of forever," he said.

"That means we can still bring people here and use it, right?" Val asked.

"Okay, cool. Next question; how do we get out of here?" Beth asked, thinking of the long shaft back to the twelfth floor.

"Elevator," Bjorn grunted, leading the way back out of the room with the energy core to the initial room. The others joined him, standing around him as he accessed something on his communicator. It wasn't long after that that a platform formed under their feet, or perhaps had always been there, rising up into the shaft they had fallen down at a rapid pace. It might have been too much for a normal person, but both the acceleration and speed were nothing to this group.

Once back up on floor twelve, they started their climb back out of the facility. Val told them, and Bjorn confirmed, that there were no other teleporters or doors in or out of the place apart from the one on floor one. They had to hoof it back up to the top the manual way, though Val said she could likely get the elevators working if given just a bit of time. It wasn't really worth it, as the group could move very fast, and waiting for Val to tinker with the elevators for possibly an hour or two would be even slower than just running up the stairs. Besides, they didn't have to deal with any enemies along the way and Bjorn had done all the smashing he could handle, so their progress was extremely quick.
