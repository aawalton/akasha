All the deer—male, female and fawn—had deformed antlers. Some twisted together into single horns while others jutted out at unnatural angles.

The claws on the bear too were long twisted things and its teeth extended out of its mouth and curling in on themselves.

The first deer to spot them let out a loud braying sound, and all the others let off on their attacks on the bear and as one circled around to charge at the new arrivals with the bear following in their wake, joining in with its former assailants.

Kelia shot bolts of ice into the charging herd while Mettius fired bright purple bolts of energy. Caleb sensed a new aspect in the bolts he’d never felt before.

Magnus drew his bow and fired a barrage of arrows far faster than Caleb had ever seen a man shoot.

Caleb and Bog ran into the gap, spear held before him. Once he was a few meters in front of Kelia he planted the butt of his spear into the ground and using his Imperium held the tip up chest height for the oncoming deer.

Bog didn’t stop beside Caleb, instead running to meet the charge with heat building up in his mouth. As the deer got within range, he spat molten steel right into the face of the lead buck. This deer, the largest of the herd reared up in pain, goring the deer beside it all along its side. Both deer collapsed and were trampled by those behind them.

All the ranged attacks together worked to down only four of the charging monsters. The survivors parted around Bog and the dragon continued on to meet the bear bringing up the rear.

The herd reached Caleb, and the lead deer dodged out of the way of his spear only to receive a blow to the chest with his hammer. The sickly deer died on impact, but the momentum was so great that it sent Caleb flying backward, forcing him to let go of his hammer lest he get dragged under the falling deer. The second deer in the charge was equally as unfortunate. It hadn’t seen the spear dodged by the first deer and ran right into it, taking it through the chest. Held in place by Caleb’s Imperium, Caleb felt the massive force of the impact and strained against it to keep the spear steady.

Corrupted Deer (error) has been killed.

The weapon held in place, and passed right through the deer, maintaining its position to catch a second disfigured monster. The force of this second blow was too much for Caleb, and the spear broke within its victim, depriving Caleb of the weapon.

Corrupted Deer (error) has been killed.

Caleb willed the notifications to stop appearing.

By then the deer were around and past him. They were focused on the ranged attackers, and Caleb saw to his relief that Magnus had drawn a spatha and shield while Mettius held a long thin blade. Kelia had forsaken her ice magic and now held her bow.

The first deer to charge each of the men were downed by quick surgical blows. Magnus moved with the speed Caleb now expected from him, while Mettius moved somehow even faster with a speed he’d not shown on the march out here. Caleb sensed a second new aspect from the man and saw that whatever he was using rapidly drained his mana.

Kelia stood between the two, shooting any deer that she could get a good shot at. It took multiple arrows for her to kill a creature. Avis too had joined the fray, swooping down at any deer that drew close to her master and attacking the faces. The deformed antlers served as a good defense against the hawk however and Caleb’s enhanced sight could see the blood that covered the bird’s wings.

Bog met the bear with a crunch of breaking bones—none of which were Bog’s. Despite the horrible sound, the bear attacked Bog as if it hadn’t broken a dozen bones. It swiped at the dragon’s metallic hide with its claws which skidded off ineffectually, and bit down on Bog’s neck only for its teeth to break under the force of its own bite.

Bog returned the bite, tearing out the side of the creature which had already been covered in wounds from before the battle. Despite that, the bear kept fighting.

After killing his third deer, Caleb took a blow to the chest from the antler of a fourth. The blow sent him flying back to his back, denting the armor badly and knocking the wind from him, but once again his extra constitution kept him from serious harm. These deer, as horrible as they were, were not much stronger than the normal deer. Whatever had driven them to this monstrous state hadn’t enhanced their strength as much as their constitution.

On the ground, Caleb rolled to dodge the cloven hooves of the stampede. He pulled on his hammer, lodged as it was within a deer chest, to pull him to safety. Once beside the deer, he climbed to his feet, using the opening the corpse created in the chaos to stand. A deer with the antlers twisted into a single horn saw him then and lowered its antlers to charge him.

Caleb bent over and grabbed the antlers of the dead deer his hammer resided in while also grabbing hold of his hammer with his mind and lifted it to intercept the charge. The corpse flew up into the air and the charging deer’s horn pierced deeply into the body, Caleb diving out of the way just before impact.

Dropping his control as he dove, Caleb saw the corpse collapse bringing its attacker down with it, though this deer was not dead, only stuck pinned to the corpse and unable to free itself. Three arrows from Kelia finished it off, all fired into the chest, any one of which would have slain a regular deer.

Caleb looked around to see that the herd was all laying dead or dying, the only thing remaining was the bear. Bog lay on his back wrestling the bear off him. Steam rose from the dragon as the tide of blood coming out of the bear’s many wounds cooked on the hot surface of the dragon.

“Hold still Bog!” Kelia shouted and not free of their own enemies now, Mettius, Kelia, and Magnus bombarded the bear with bolts and arrows until it collapsed on the filthy dragon.

“You’re going to need a bath,” Caleb said to his dragon as he clawed his way out from under the beast.

“You too,” Kelia pointed out, and Caleb saw he too was covered in the abnormally dark blood of the slain deer.

Caleb checked his notifications only to see more kill notifications without any skill increases.
