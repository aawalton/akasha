In a snap, Harry opened his eyes, and the inertia that had gripped the world seemed to have vanished. He felt the wind brush through his hair and the air fill his nose; he realized the lack of such basic elements while he was with Raven. He also felt a mental connection, something only a Emblem user could have noticed.

He let his body absorb the world while, in addition to all this, he turned around and finally realized the chaotic situation he was in.

"Locksmith!" Daniel's voice could be heard from the left. "What are you doing? Get out of there!"

In front of him was Alexander. The white glove on his left hand had disappeared. Directly above them all, a colossal, tapered object witnessed Alexander's destructive plan.

Within a few meters, Vela, Manes, Brun, and Madeline all appeared unconscious as they lay on the rocky ground. Brun and Manes seemed fine, but Madeline had a deep cut on her thigh, and Vela shared a similar injury. Vela... Maddy...! Harry screamed in his mind, noticing the state of his companions.

Goldman, also fallen, seemed to still be conscious, even if exhausted. Around the entire dry area, a furious vortex of water swirled as if the ocean itself were starving.

"Why are we under the island!?" Harry heard himself shout this to everyone involved, only to see Alexander's face, still startled by the young thief's appearance. Before he could do anything, however, he saw Harry fall against one of his doors and approach Madeline and Vela.

Daniel, undeterred by the unique moment, continued summoning his arrows in an attempt to take down the false butler. At that moment, Daniel and Harry were the only things between Alexander and the destruction of the island. Harry didn't know this yet, however.

"Maddy! Are you okay?" He shook the girl's body, but she didn't respond. "Vela? Damn it, wake up already, for fuck's sake!" He yelled, not caring about the commotion in the center of the place.

"They're okay, Harry!" Goldman shouted from the other side, trying to get up. "I mean, they'll live..."

And then he coughed violently as he lost his balance and once again collapsed towards the hard ground.

What the hell is this... Harry, who had been crouching, also stood up and saw Alexander now smiling at him. Despite the apparent confidence of the leader of Prism's group, it was clear that he too was one step away from succumbing to exhaustion. Was this all after the explosion?

Daniel continued dodging and struggling not to be pushed into the water. Alexander was repeatedly trying to reach the unconscious bodies, but was often stopped by Daniel and Goldman. After hitting Goldman hard in the stomach, the man could barely stand properly. Still, that didn't stop him from trying.

Now with Harry in the mix, he realized he couldn't eliminate them one by one. Screw it. I don't need to do the dirty work... He pushed Daniel to the ground once more and reached the edge of the island. Since he was tall, he only had to extend his right hand to touch the edges. As if lifting a goblet, the man then looked around and decided to snap his fingers into nothing.

Snap! A wave of energy was expelled from his body in the movement, but was quickly sucked back into his fingers. He then continued the same movement, using his Emblem, but before the energy was lost, he managed to pull it back into his body.

As if his body had a life of its own, it began to grow and shrink rapidly. Pulsating and agitated, the limb seemed to scream. It turned red, some bubbles appearing and then bursting. The energy then spread through Alexander's left arm and advanced throughout his body. His veins bulged as the energy passed over him.

Finally reaching the man's right arm, Harry only had to look once to realize the damage it was doing to him. He seemed to want to scream at every moment, yet at times he seemed to be in jubilation. But what the hell is he doing? Harry tried to understand the reason for that mechanism.

How can he be doing this? Although the second Emblem is usually amplified, being even more evident in Triangular shapes, like Alexander's Snap Emblem, Harry hadn't imagined that something like this could be done. He's accumulating the remaining energy from the pulses he releases from his snaps... His body won't hold out much longer if he remains in this state.

He then looked at Daniel, who seemed as confused as he was. He tried to see Goldman's expressions, but the man didn't seem to have noticed the event that was taking over.

Suddenly the ground trembled. Could it be the sea getting closer to the island? Or was something about our presence irritating the Metas in the region? But no. Harry then noticed the source of the vibration; it was Alexander.

They all then felt an unimaginable pressure coming from the center, as if the world were about to explode at any moment. Alexander had found a way to use the energy he was storing, giving his body a rest, but not his mind. Using two Emblems at the same time, especially one of them being triangular, certainly required a lot of attention.

Somehow, he wasn't just expending that energy, but reusing it in the vibrations he could generate in the terrain. Thus, holding onto the lower point of the island, the man used all his concentration to...

"He's going to blow up the island!" Harry shouted to everyone who could hear. "Maybe that's a pressure point or something, I don't know, but he's going to do it."

Amidst his desperate pleas, Alexander seemed to laugh weakly once more. Daniel then tried to summon one of his arrows behind the man. Before it could hit the criminal, however, even the arrow seemed to break under the pressure.

Already irritated by the past complications, Daniel ran to try and reach Alexander, only to be redirected to the other side by one of Harry's doors, who was approaching the man.

"Before you—" Harry then received a slap across the face. It was so abrupt and powerful that Harry forgot what he was going to say for a second. Daniel then grabbed the man by the tie and lifted him up.

"What the hell are you doing!?" He yelled in front of Harry, who had his arms raised.

"Wait!" Harry shouted with a weak smile. "I saved you, you idiot! If you had gone through that accretion disc, you would have turned to mush by now! The pressure is too high, you wouldn't have survived."

Damn, I shouldn't have called him an idiot...

"How can you know that, you—" And then he yelled again, but in the corner of his vision, he saw Brun's body, which seemed to be waking up from whatever attack had left her unconscious. He looked with resentment and fear at his father, who continued to hold Harry. He then looked once more at the thief in front of him and let him go.

Harry proceeded to straighten his tie once more. "I have a plan, okay? You'll need to trust me, but it'll work, I promise."

"...If it doesn't work, she'll die," Daniel said, his voice sorrowful.

"I know, big guy," Harry said with a grin that stretched from side to side. "And that's why I won't fail. Do you trust me?"

The man sighed deeply and asked with a small smile, "...Do I have another choice?"

Harry then had to jump to bump Daniel's shoulders. He really is tall, huh? He turned to see Alexander still holding back screams as cracks began to appear at the edges of the island. Like a virus, they were spreading, determined to bring everything down.

"Bring Goldman here with the others. Tell him not to interfere with Alexander's... thing."

"Why—" And then Daniel shut up, simply nodding as he went around to help Goldman get up.

As the waves seemed to grow more violent, Harry decided to accelerate a part of his plan.

"Hey! Alexander!" His shouts barely penetrated the barrier Alexander had created for himself and just barely reached his ears. "You hear me, don't you?"

For a moment, however brief, Harry noticed a hint of recognition on Alexander's face, who briefly stared at Harry before refocusing.

"You know all this you're doing?" Harry continued to shout with a smile. "It won't do you any good! Raven will leave you here, did you know that? I just had a little chat with him!"

At the mention of the mysterious figure's name, it even seemed as if Alexander gave up on everything. The muscles in his face relaxed as the energy seemed to subside. Even the pressure around him had lessened.

"...What did you say?" He asked, still maintaining his pose. "H-How do you know that? How do you know his name? You son of a—"

And he shouted for the first time. A raspy, bubbling tone, as if even his vocal cords trembled with the power within his body. If you don't concentrate, you might implode... Although it wasn't Harry's main plan, it could happen.

"You left Cal and Gale up there while you did all this." Harry taunted the man. "Besides, you got scammed by a supernatural entity and killed one of your former members. What future is there for you?"

"You know nothing!" The man yelled back as he took a step forward, his hand still on the rock. "I've helped them countless times, more than they could ever repay me! That was their final goodbye. It was their plan in case things went wrong. They knew of the risks!"

"Yeah, because you manipulated them, you asshole!" Harry replied. "Do you really think they'd be willing to die for a butler who uses only one glove!?"

"You fucking lunatic, I'm going to... kill... I'm..."

His eyes darted from one side to the other, unsure of what was happening inside his own body. Splat! Liters of blood were fell to the ground as his left hand exploded. Alexander, not even gasping from pain, collapsed with his eyes wide open.

Fuckin' hell... Harry added inwardly as he took a step back in disgust. That was brutal. But the damage was already done. Colossal creaking sounds permeated every inch of the island as the thread holding it together seemed to snap.

Away, floating above the raging waves, Raven could be seen pointing at his clock. He then showed his palm to Harry. Five minutes. Will that be all?

"Shit!" Harry said to himself, a smile still in his face. "Okay... Okay. Okay! Let's go guys, let's finish it up. Come with me!"

Harry then advised for Daniel to help Goldman and Brun. Giving support to the old woman and Goldman, Daniel also summoned his arrows and picked up Vela and Manes from the ground. In front of them, the Locksmith made a sequence of doors leading upwards. We just need to get out. And although momentum carried through the loops as they restarted, they would all fall on soft sand, so it wouldn't be much of a problem.

As soon as he started creating his doors, Harry remembered the sound of his speedy heart beating nonstop. Well, Emblem Exhaustion wouldn't carry through loops, so fuck this. The young thief then proceeded to create dozens of doors as he and Daniel fell through, jumping as if they were gymnasts. Vela and Manes, being carried by a couple arrows, also followed. They were soon getting out of range of the island.

This was, of course, a crude and rudimentary plan. If the island's fall wasn't exactly as Harry had planned, they'd be on the mercy of the doors, which was scary to say the least.

What Raven had said, though, was still fresh on his mind. The loop will end sooner. He thought to himself. Let's hope five minutes have passed.

Harry looked down as all around him people were standing on closed doors. Beneath them, the floating island fell into the waters. Before that, though, the Locksmith swore he saw two figures being propelled into the air, right to the hatch. The Locksmith closed his eyes and sighed deeply as the breeze took control over his hair.

Time suddenly felt solid.
