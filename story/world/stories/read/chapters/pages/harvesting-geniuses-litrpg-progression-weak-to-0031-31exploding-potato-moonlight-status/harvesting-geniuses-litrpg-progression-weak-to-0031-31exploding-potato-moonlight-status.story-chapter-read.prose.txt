The senior butler stationed at the family mansion was a former government official who had retired due to health reasons and chronic illness. Grandfather had paid handsomely to recruit him in exchange for his loyalty. But even though he was just a butler, everyone in the family had to show the senior butler respect—as if he were an elder sharing their bloodline.

Only those with Grandfather's permission could summon the senior butler. Sven was one of them—because he contributed to the family through business management. In contrast, the other third-generation descendants had no right to use the senior butler.

Sven had ordered the senior butler to gather information. As a former official with extensive connections, it wasn't difficult to dig up data from the training center's security cameras or police footage showing Jinyo's old car racing down the streets. It wasn't hard for them—with enough money, officials would turn a blind eye to digging up information on insignificant nobodies like Jinyo.

On a night when everyone else slept, Sven stayed awake until four in the morning. He couldn't sleep without knowing something. Fortunately, the senior butler met his expectations. Otherwise, Sven would probably have had a heart attack from anxiety.

The senior butler entered his room. His expression was more grave and cold than when they had met that evening.

"Why such a scary face, James?"

"I must apologize, young master. The investigation into that seal symbol has made no progress. It appears that lady's organization is far more influential than I anticipated."

James readily admitted his failure. He had worked for the government and had connections with numerous special agencies. Yet even so, he couldn't uncover anything about the organization with that strange seal.

"Even someone of your caliber couldn't find that agency?"

"Correct. If it's not a fictional organization, then it must be one so influential it can block my network's access. Not knowing anything might actually benefit you more, young master."

"No... I need to know. I nearly died, you know. I can't pretend ignorance—that goes against my life philosophy."

Sven hated having things outside his control, and a threat so great it nearly ended his life and his sister's—who was the family's future. Among the third-generation heirs, besides Sven, only Luciria could serve as a pillar for the next generation. His cousins weren't that capable. He didn't want to imagine how disastrous the Halfrin family's future would be if both Sven and Luciria died.

Above all else, if he didn't know what had nearly killed him, how would he face tomorrow?

"No matter how terrible it is, I want to know. James, you must continue investigating. Consider it a personal request."

"Understood."

James accepted the willful young master's request. It couldn't be helped. Sven had just narrowly survived, and his mental state was unstable. It was natural to be paranoid and want peace of mind. His job was simply to follow his master's orders. Even if the path the young master walked led to hell, he would simply descend to hell alongside him.

"As for the matter of the man named Jinyo, I've already investigated. His history is quite interesting indeed."

James handed over a file to Sven. Even in this era of widespread smartphones and computers where sending digital files would be more convenient, some matters required information kept on paper—because it was easier to destroy completely.

"A foreign scholarship student. Finished his doctorate at age 24... sued for intellectual property theft. No wonder he's working as a contract teacher in a rural town. So he was blacklisted from research institutions. What a waste of talent."

Sven couldn't connect why a genius who finished his doctorate two years early would do something stupid like stealing intellectual property. For the research world, it was a huge embarrassment. Being alone with no backing made exclusion easy. No wonder he'd hit a dead end.

The last heir of an ancient family. Single, unmarried, but with two children born through a genius gene program.

Jinyo's history made Sven feel like he was reading a novel. But what interested him most was the travel log—starting from when Jinyo entered the training center.

Jinyo had acted normally until the moment he first saw Luciria. Then Jinyo began hovering around Luciria at a distance. His behavior resembled a disgusting man stalking a girl half his age.

What was interesting was that Jinyo had chased his car desperately from the training center all the way to the underpass where the accident occurred.

Suspicions bloomed like mushrooms.

"Strange. He really did follow me and Luciria. Then that woman came to join him shortly after. How did they know in advance that I'd encounter that monster? Do they have some way of detecting when we're being targeted?"

"I don't know, sir."

"Hmm..."

Sven went to the computer and pulled up video files from the dashcam. Fortunately, his car had captured the grasshopper demon's battle with Jinyo perfectly. Sven watched the low-quality footage on the computer. It didn't make his hair stand on end like seeing it in person, but no matter how many times he watched, he was impressed.

"What do you think, James?"

"He's a young man with a bright future."

"Do you recognize the techniques he used? They seemed very effective against that monster."

"I don't. But if you give me time, I'll investigate until I find out."

"Please do. Oh right—I'm going to the training center tomorrow. It would be great if you could come along."

"Understood, young master."

James finished speaking and left the room, leaving Sven to spend his private time piecing the story together. Even though Jinyo was injured, Sven believed Jinyo would appear at the training center again tomorrow. If Sven wanted to get closer to the secret of that monster, staying close to Jinyo was the best option. Bringing the senior butler along would keep him safe. At the very least, James's skill was several times greater than Joey's and Adam's.

The next morning, Sven had slept only two hours. The dark circles under his eyes made him look like a panda, but nothing could stop him from going to the training center. Everyone in the family must have been shocked—he had just narrowly escaped death, yet instead of staying at the mansion with its high security level, he woke up and ran outside to seek excitement at the training center.

Sven stood waiting for the car until a young girl walked through the mansion gates and stood beside him. Sven furrowed his brow, his head filled with question marks.

"Lucy, why are you here?"

"I have to go."

"Go where? You can't go to the training center. Didn't you hear what that woman warned? You're still not safe."

"Grandfather ordered it. No matter what, I have to go."

Luciria answered Sven's question. Her voice had no ripples whatsoever—as if devoid of emotion. The young man's heart went numb. He was certain he had warned that Luciria might be in danger. The fact that Grandfather still sent Luciria to take the Hunter exam at only twelve years old, even after being targeted by an inexplicable monster—in Grandfather's eyes, his granddaughter's life must not be as important as the family's honor.

Jinyo didn't go to the hospital as Sven's group thought. He asked Rina to take him back to the family mansion. His Primordial Plant Body was too mysterious to explain to doctors. If he didn't want to get entangled with experiments from strange research institutions, it was best to keep the secrets of his Primordial Plant Body and Nature Synthesis Technique hidden.

Naho ran to fetch twenty kilograms of Immortal Rice stored in the warehouse and cooked it for her beloved brother, whose face looked as haggard as a dying corpse. Eating Immortal Rice mixed with soy sauce and carrot omelet—he had to thank his special body for recovering at an inhuman speed.

"No wonder you refused to go to the hospital. You're becoming less human by the day."

Rina looked at Jinyo with a piercing gaze. After eating just five kilograms of rice, Jinyo had almost recovered from his injuries. The only thing that hadn't healed was the broken bones from the car crash. Come to think of it, Jinyo had barely been hit directly by the grasshopper demon even once.

"Big bro, will you be okay tomorrow? Tomorrow's exam is really important, you know."

Naho worried about her brother.

"I'll be fine. I want to open a school next month. Even with a teaching license, I can't open a school without a Hunter license."

"Sigh, my brother just went to take a teaching exam, so why did he have to run into monsters? I really don't understand. Wasn't that thing from the other day bad luck enough? Brother, I think we should go to the temple on our next day off. Maybe making merit will reduce your bad luck."

"Ha ha."

Jinyo shoveled Immortal Rice into his mouth. He didn't dare say that he'd gone looking for death himself. He could have stayed put comfortably without any trouble. Changing Luciria's fate hadn't helped him at all either—and he'd even gained that highly suspicious Hostility toward Heaven.

The system warned him to avoid leaving the Garden of the Sword for now. But Jinyo couldn't do that. Tomorrow had an important exam.

Ryse and Gretel had already gone to bed. Jinyo dragged himself to the cottage. The Garden of the Sword's Spirit Essence had really increased by 1 permanent unit. It seemed the system gave especially good rewards for eliminating Enemies from Heaven.

He had received a 100-Day plant cultivation ticket—enough to turn seedlings into rice ready for harvest. Plus, within these 100 days, there was a chance to produce stat-enhanced crops.

The only stat that could appear was [Moonlight].

Jinyo wondered what would happen to his body if he ate stat-enhanced produce again. And if others ate some too—like Naho or Rina—would they get the same results as Jinyo?

Jinyo sat using Nature Synthesis Technique. At midnight when the moon was most beautiful, Jinyo strolled from the cottage to the Exploding Potato field.

"Use 100-Day plant cultivation ticket on Exploding Potatoes."

Jinyo clicked to use the plant cultivation ticket. Spirit Essence in the Garden of the Sword flowed into the Exploding Potato field.

From observation, Jinyo knew the plant cultivation ticket wasn't without side effects. Accelerating plant growth drew upon the Spirit Essence produced in the Garden of the Sword. If that wasn't enough, it would pull natural energy from around the garden.

Jinyo had seen hundreds of trees around the mountain—even grass wouldn't grow there. It was as if it had become a land of death. This made him more careful about using them if he didn't want the environment damaged by his power.

The Spirit Essence accumulated over many days was enough to grow the Exploding Potatoes.

Among the tens of thousands of potatoes buried underground, there had to be some with special status. A 1% increased chance was significant. Statistically speaking, for every 10,000 potatoes, there should be 100 with stat bonuses.

"Pop! Come help dig potatoes!"

Jinyo called for his servant sprite. Pop ran over with farming tools, its eyes sparkling at being summoned by its master. But when it found its master standing in the Exploding Potato field, its enthusiasm dropped to nearly zero.

"Don't make that sour face. Experience points! Think of the experience points!"

"..."

If the master had asked it to dig carrots, it would have been happier than gaining experience points.

Jinyo, who had an exam tomorrow, sat using Nature Synthesis Technique under the moonlight, letting Pop harvest experience points to its heart's content alone. Jinyo only received a small amount of experience as the garden's owner.

Pop ran over and tossed him a potato plant with a fresh white-yellow color. Its skin was so white it seemed to glow from within. Jinyo knew immediately—this was the produce with special status.

"Great job, Pop. For every 100 potatoes, I'll give you 3. Okay?"

"!!!"

Pop's mouth fell open. Such good fortune existed?!

Pop became even more enthusiastic about searching for stat-enhanced Exploding Potatoes. But it wasn't easy—even with a 1% increased chance, it didn't mean exactly 100 special potatoes would be found. It was just statistical probability.

Regular potato yield was 25,000-40,000 per acre. But Exploding Potatoes only yielded 15,000-25,000 per acre.

Jinyo hoped Pop wouldn't disappoint him.

The young man brought the Moonlight-status potatoes inside. He cut them in half and baked them with cheese.

The first time Jinyo had nearly lost his mind. He had barely savored the taste of stat-enhanced produce. But today his consciousness was crystal clear.

Jinyo used a spoon to cut a piece of cheese-baked potato. The fragrance of potato and rich cheese hit his nose like a straight punch. He sprinkled a little salt before putting it in his mouth.

The potato with its soft, delicate flavor harmonized perfectly with the rich cheese. The sweet fragrance that practically melted in his mouth attacked his nerve cells, awakening his body. Jinyo trembled at the nostalgic taste. It was as if he had returned to childhood again—the day he first ate cheese-baked potato at school. The impression of that cheese-baked potato flavor was unearthed.

"Delicious!!! So delicious I want to turn back time!"

He didn't know what inspired him to say that. Even Jinyo, who wasn't particularly fond of potatoes, was impressed to the point of wanting to cry. Wouldn't people who truly loved potatoes like Naho or Ryse cry rivers?

Above all else, his body reacted to the special produce.

[You have eaten Exploding Potato with Moonlight status. +0.1 Moon Element, +0.1 Charm, +0.1 Strength. Moonlight Status activated for 30 seconds.]

"!!!"

Jinyo opened the status window and found that Moon Element (G) had begun to change. It had become (G+). Did this mean just eating produce with stats corresponding to his element would develop that element's power?

"It's this easy? This is obviously a cheat code."

Jinyo regretted that Moon element wasn't his main element. Moon element was extremely rare, and martial arts or magic related to Moon element were so expensive Jinyo couldn't possibly afford them.

Jinyo made cheese-baked potatoes using 20 Moonlight-status potatoes. Though he felt a bit guilty, Jinyo chose to eat them all himself. But he set aside 5 each for his children, his sister, and Rina.

The message "+0.1 Moon Element" popped up repeatedly. The more Jinyo ate, the more benefits he received. Until finally, he succeeded in changing Moon Element (G+) into Moon Element (F)—equal to the Flower Element (F) he already had.

This meant the damage from Double Impact would now be more powerful. Not only that—when Jinyo tried using Nature Synthesis Technique, his planar cultivation speed doubled from before.

The more intense the elements, the faster cultivation?

No—at least according to standard curriculum, it shouldn't work that way. In textbooks, the more elements you had, the slower cultivation became, because you had to nourish two elements simultaneously.

Jinyo had seen a greedy friend at military school train in two elements. In the end, that friend never reached his dream and was expelled mid-term for developing too slowly.

Therefore, this phenomenon must only occur with Jinyo alone.

Jinyo felt refreshed. His body overflowed with two types of power. If he could control it well, it would become an ultimate weapon—equivalent to holding two swords in one hand.

Something only he in the entire world could do.
