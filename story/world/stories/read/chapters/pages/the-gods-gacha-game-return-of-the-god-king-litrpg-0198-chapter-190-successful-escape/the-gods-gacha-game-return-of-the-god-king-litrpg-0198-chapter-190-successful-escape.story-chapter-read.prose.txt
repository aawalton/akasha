No other choice. I have to go all out!

You have used Grant Plausibility.

You have greatly enhanced your combat capabilities for fifteen minutes.

You have greatly enhanced your skill proficiency for fifteen minutes.

A total of 240 Plausibility has been consumed.

Using Grant Plausibility to enhance my combat capabilities while maintaining Fabled Vessel was something I had never really tried before, because doing so would consume an enormous amount of plausibility. But since I still had quite a bit to spare and had no other choice, I activated the skill.

The effect was immediate as I felt every aspect of my power strengthen drastically. My body felt lighter, my perception sharper, and even the flow of Selene’s borrowed power inside me seemed to respond more smoothly. With this, I should at least be able to fight the enemy before me.

Hyunwoo’s sword came down again like a descending executioner’s blade. As our weapons clashed, instead of being pushed back instantly, I managed to hold my ground this time. My daggers crossed against his longsword as sparks burst outward from the collision.

His brows furrowed slightly. I twisted my body and slipped past the follow-up strike, my figure vanishing for an instant with Moonveil Step and Predator’s Veil before reappearing behind him. Then I activated Moonlight Blade in tandem with Phantom Slash, causing my daggers to light up as multiple afterimages appeared at once while I slashed toward his back.

But Hyunwoo reacted instantly. His sword spun backward with terrifying precision, intercepting my attack before it could land.

The two of us exchanged a rapid series of blows, blades colliding repeatedly as shockwaves rippled through the air. Hyunwoo’s expression finally changed slightly. It must have come as a surprise to him. After all, this was probably the first time a Gimmel-rank divine warrior had managed to fight him to this extent. Even if he could tell that I was relying heavily on buff skills, the fact that I could keep up with him at all was clearly unexpected.

But even then, the gap between us remained terrifying. This guy was faster than Kaela, stronger than Boris, and highly likely to be far more adept in magic than both Lucian and me. Furthermore, his proficiency with the sword even somewhat surpassed Erika’s. And the most terrifying part was that he hadn’t even used a single skill against me. It felt like I was fighting someone who had gone through countless battles.

However, that balance was also what kept me alive. If he were just slightly faster, I wouldn’t have been able to react. If he were just slightly stronger, I wouldn’t have been able to even barely defend against his attacks.

At the end of the day, he was still a peak Daleth-rank divine warrior and not a He rank. If he were a He-rank divine warrior, I would have been killed in a single exchange.

Hyunwoo’s eyes suddenly sharpened. “You’re interesting.” Then his left hand rose slightly, and a terrifying heat gathered instantly as blue flames appeared.

My pupils shrank in recognition. Hell Flame?!

The next moment, a sphere of blazing blue fire erupted from his palm and shot toward me like a meteor. A deafening explosion tore through the battlefield as the Hell Flame detonated, flames surging outward in a violent wave that engulfed everything around me.

It scorched the ground and shattered the nearby stone walls as heat blasted across the battlefield. Even with Moonveil Step and Predator’s Veil, I barely managed to throw myself away from the center of the blast. My body rolled across the ground as fragments of burning debris rained down around me. Pain surged through my arms, and the lingering heat from the Hell Flame clawed at my senses.

Damn… that attack was insane!

Through the curtain of blazing fire and rising smoke, I could already feel Hyunwoo’s killing intent sweeping across the battlefield as he searched for me. I quickly realized that this was a good chance to escape. Without wasting a single second, I activated another skill—Echo Construct.

A faint ripple of energy spread outward from me as a duplicate of my figure—or in this case, Black Phantom’s—emerged within the smoke, perfectly mimicking my posture and aura. The decoy staggered forward, clutching its side as if injured, before suddenly launching itself toward the nearby alleyway. Exactly the kind of desperate escape someone in my position would attempt.

Meanwhile, I activated Moonveil Step again, my figure fading into the shadows as I moved in the opposite direction. A moment later, the sound of steel cutting through flesh echoed faintly from behind. It appeared that he had taken the bait.

Nevertheless, I didn’t dare slow down. Even with all the buffs stacked on top of each other, the gap between us was still terrifying. If he had realized the trick even a few seconds earlier, I would already be dead. My body slipped across rooftops and through narrow streets as I pushed Moonveil Step and Wind Rush to their limit, putting as much distance between us as possible.

The construct has lost energy and dissipated.

Still, I knew this wasn’t something I could rely on twice. Hyunwoo wasn’t an idiot. Once he realized what had happened, he would immediately understand the trick. If I encountered him again… this kind of deception would never work a second time.

Luckily, I managed to escape safely and put several kilometers between us in less than a minute while running nonstop. Of course, I made sure not to head in the direction of the supermarket, in case he had intentionally let me go just to follow me back to my base. That would only bring disaster by drawing the attention of such a terrifying divine warrior…

Hyunwoo slowly lowered his sword as the last traces of blue hellfire faded from the shattered street. His sharp gaze swept across the battlefield once more, searching for the divine warrior he had just fought. Yet there was nothing; the opponent’s presence had completely vanished.

He let his senses spread outward across the surrounding streets. Thanks to the many perception skills he possessed, his awareness extended far beyond normal limits, sweeping across rooftops, alleyways, and distant buildings. But still, nothing.

“…Interesting.”

Most divine warriors would leave behind at least a trace of their aura after such a chaotic escape, yet this one had erased himself completely. Hyunwoo slowly exhaled and relaxed his stance; it seemed the enemy had truly slipped away.

He could continue searching, of course. However, the city was vast, and the chaos of the battlefield had already disrupted too many signals. Chasing blindly would only waste time. Besides… he already had enough information.

A faint blue translucent screen appeared before his eyes as he reviewed the fragments he had managed to extract earlier with his status-prying skill.

Black Phantom

Rank: GimmelPatron God: Velkhar (God of Deception)…

“Velkhar?”

His brows furrowed slightly as he found the information suspicious. Not only was the name Black Phantom clearly a nickname rather than a real name, but the patron god listed was also Velkhar. A divine warrior capable of fighting him to that extent shouldn’t be some obscure figure who had never appeared before. Hyunwoo had spent years studying the major forces involved in the divine war before his regression. He knew the prominent divine warriors on nearly every side.

And yet… there had never been a high-rank divine warrior with a name like that on either Istellia’s or Velkhar’s side before. That meant this information was most likely fake.

To begin with, if he were truly a divine warrior under Velkhar, the God of Deception, he wouldn’t have acted like that. The opponent might have appeared to specialize in illusions and deception, which certainly fit Velkhar’s authority, but that also meant the information itself could easily be fabricated. If that was the case, then the most likely truth pointed elsewhere.

“Istellia.” The Goddess of Imagination. “So there’s someone like that on her side…”

Hyunwoo’s eyes glinted faintly as he pinpointed the faction the other party most likely belonged to. That would explain many things, including his strange skills, his unorthodox combat style, and the bizarre buffs that allowed a mere Gimmel-rank warrior to temporarily stand against him.

But more than that… Has the future changed?

Hyunwoo sheathed his sword slowly as he turned away from the ruined street. The fact that the future had changed bothered him slightly. Fate itself was always changing, and the future was never truly fixed. But the more the timeline diverged, the less reliable his prior knowledge would become. When that time came, who knew what kind of changes and consequences might occur?

But there was no need to worry about that now. Perhaps saving his friends had already altered small variables within the grand scheme of things. If that was the case, then encountering this mysterious warrior might simply be another ripple within the changing future. And if that person truly stood on Istellia’s side… their paths would cross again.

“Huff… Puff…” Warm afternoon air rushed into my lungs as I finally slowed down.

For several minutes, I had pushed Moonveil Step, Wind Rush, and Predator’s Veil to their limits, racing across rooftops and empty streets while keeping my senses stretched behind me for any sign of pursuit. Only after confirming that Hyunwoo’s terrifying killing intent had completely vanished did I dare reduce my pace.

My boots touched down on the edge of a deserted rooftop, and I staggered slightly. Then the familiar System message appeared before my eyes.

The possession has ended.

Your power has returned to normal.

Assassination Arts has leveled up.

Moonlight Blade has leveled up.

Your [Basic Dagger/Knife Mastery Lv.10] has evolved to [Intermediate Dagger/Knife Mastery Lv.1].

You have acquired a skill: [Moonveil Step Lv.1].

Your Dexterity has increased by 5.

Your Stamina has increased by 1.

You have used up 538 Plausibility for the possession.

The sudden loss of Selene’s power felt as if all my strength had been stripped away. The sharp clarity that had filled my senses moments ago faded rapidly, leaving behind a heavy exhaustion. I had clearly maintained Fabled Vessel longer than I should have, but it was better to be cautious than sorry.

I leaned against the cracked concrete wall of the rooftop and let out a long breath. Even my arms felt heavy now. Using Fabled Vessel while simultaneously maintaining Grant Plausibility had drained far more from my body than I expected. The buff from plausibility had kept me standing in that fight, but now the backlash was catching up to me.

My heart was still pounding from the battle. That guy… Lee Hyunwoo. Just thinking about the clash made a cold chill crawl up my spine. Even after stacking multiple buffs and possessing Selene, I had only barely managed to survive against him. If the fight had lasted even a little longer—or if he had decided to go all out from the start—I would probably already be dead.

I glanced down at my slightly trembling hands. To think that a peak Daleth-rank divine warrior could be this strong. No… he might even be far beyond that. His combat sense alone was monstrous. Every movement he made felt as though it had been refined through countless battles. And the worst part was that he hadn’t even used many skills.

“…What kind of monster did I just run into?”

I clearly remembered that his class was “Demon Hunter,” which was merely a unique class rather than a legendary one. However, there had been another word inside the brackets—one starting with the letters “re.”

“Don’t tell me… He is a regressor…”
