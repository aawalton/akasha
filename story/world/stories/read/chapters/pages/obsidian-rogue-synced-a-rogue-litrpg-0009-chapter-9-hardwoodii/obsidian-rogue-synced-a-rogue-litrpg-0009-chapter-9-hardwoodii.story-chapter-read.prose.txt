Levi flung himself to the dirt floor as fast as he could, the rogue barely saving his skull from being caved in by the gremlin's long cudgel. His newest injury flared up at the jarring movement, and he forced himself to hold back the tears that threatened to spill from his eyes. A wave of relief washed through him as he lay on the dirt floor, panting from his close shave with death. He'd somehow managed to evade the attack from the chief gremlin, who was still seated on its mud throne.

The gremlin recalled its weapon after the missed attack; its beady black eyes stared down at Levi from its mud throne. Its expression was blank as though it hadn't even registered the amount of damage it had done to Levi within the short amount of time that had passed. The krul chief gremlin's expression was mirrored by that of its sole remaining kinfolk. The other gremlin looked like more of a statue than a living thing; its lack of movement was eerie.

Pulling his focus to the present, Levi gritted his teeth as he managed to prop himself off the floor with his one good arm and did his best to put some distance between himself and his foes. Beads of sweat rolled down his forehead as his current reality set in. Sure, he'd been somewhat fine with dying a while back if he could kill the gremlin that had slashed his side deeply. But now, as he stood to his feet, eyeballs locked to the that of the gremlin, Levi knew for a certain time that he wanted to live.

Today was the day he was supposed to begin his life's journey. He'd planned to travel to different places, explore various foods, meet different people, and, most of all, have the opportunity to participate in the high-level tasks typically found only in big cities. But somehow his syncing had turned out to be more of a curse than a blessing. It now seemed like he wouldn't have any of the myriad of things that he'd imagined...No, he surely wouldn't have any of that if he gave up now, but if he fought with everything he had, then maybe he'd be able to achieve his dreams.

It couldn't be his destiny to die in this dungeon today. It would make for a good story, sure—the rogue who'd died the day he'd synced. In death, he'd be used as a tragic lesson to all those who would think to clear a dungeon all by themselves. He'd be ridiculed in death, his name attached permanently to stupidity. It wouldn't matter his reasons for going into the dungeon alone, and he doubted that they'd be able to piece together the truth of what had pushed him into the dungeon. They wouldn't know, they couldn't know, and... There would be no need for them to wonder.

I'm going to survive this.

His fate wasn't sealed in stone. Sure, he was battered and bruised, outclassed even, but the fight wasn't over. And as far as he was still drawing breath, he'd do everything in his power to keep it that way. He'd risk everything, a broken shoulder, hand, or whatever the gremlin would attempt to break; he could find a way to heal as long as he could clear the dungeon.

Defiance shone in his eyes as he stared right back at the krul chief gremlin. The pain from his dislocated shoulder pushed to the dark recesses of his mind as a more critical feeling came to the fore — the primal urge to survive. He acknowledged the fact that he wasn't going to get out of this one cleanly; heck, he was already sporting a serious injury on his right shoulder. Still, he kept it at the back of his mind that he was going to be hurt, maybe even receive a worse injury than the dislocated shoulder.

He broke eye contact with the Krul chief gremlin as his eyes drifted to the non-fatal but disastrous injury. His first proper view of the injury made him wince; the bone of his arm seemed to have moved out of its position on the right shoulder joint, with the bone jutting out at an outward angle. The injury was so severe that even looking at it caused him pain, and Levi was forced to grit his teeth. He used the overwhelming pain as fuel for his resolve. His shoulder might be out of position, but he still felt like he had a chance of making it out alive and a chance... A chance was all he needed.

A deafening shriek boomed within the small room, the sound reverberating within his bones and causing his teeth to vibrate. Levi turned to face the source of the sound, his gaze locking onto the form of a still shrieking krul chief gremlin. The bigger and bulkier gremlin let out what Levi believed to be its version of a war cry as it finally came down from its mud throne. Its eyes locked onto Levi's as the guttural sound left its throat. The threat within the shriek was easy for the rogue to deduce, even though he didn't 'speak' gremlin.

"Come at me, you ugly bastard' Levi said, the dagger clutched in his only good hand bared in defiance.

The chief gremlin pointed its cudgel at him, the crude and monstrous weapon gripped tightly in its right clawed hand. Its beady black eyes, showed no hint of anger, hate, or any discernible emotion. The Chief gremlin was going to kill him simply because it was in its nature, or maybe because Cerpital had made it so; he didn't know, and he didn't plan on driving into that particular train of thought at the moment. He needed to be entirely focused on what was to come.

The preview he'd gotten from the krul chief gremlin's cudgel making contact with his right shoulder made him understand that he'd have to avoid getting touched by the weapon. Despite its size and length, the gremlin had wielded it with such ease, which meant that the weapon wasn't a problem for the gremlin. That was just another unfortunate piece of information that he'd managed to glean from their short clash.

Unfortunate or not, it was a valuable piece of information for him. At least he now knew to stay far away from the weapon that could badly injure him. Apart from the krul chief, the other gremlin seemed to have come alive as it now actively stared at Levi. Its clawed fingers twitching as though waiting for the order to attack. An order that didn't have to wait long for. The Krul Chief gremlin let out a short, guttural shriek before charging at him; its sole remaining kin followed behind.

Levi readied himself for their attack, his left dagger positioned for both defence and attack, his knees bent for explosive movement, and his eyes vigilant as he sought out weaknesses that he could exploit. The rogue stood on the balls of his feet, anticipating multiple scenarios and prepping for them as best as he could. If he were lucky, he'd be able to kill the chief gremlin in a single hit. But as far as today was concerned, luck like that wasn't going to be on his side.

The chief gremlin swung its cudgel at the rogue's legs as soon as it came within range, but Levi had anticipated an attack of that type, so he had no problem evading the attack as he moved backward. The chief gremlin didn't react much to the rogue evading its attack; it only paused in its charge, letting its underling go past it, headed for the rogue.

The underling gremlin attacked from the side rather than head-on. A clawed strike targeted at Levi's limp arm, unsuccessful as the rogue moved out of harm's way. Narrowly avoiding another injury to the arm. The underling was undeterred by the rogue's evasion; it simply pressed the attack while the Krul chief gremlin watched. Levi kept an eye on the chief gremlin even as the underling prepared another attack, which was headed his way. He wouldn't be caught off guard by the same trick it had used earlier.

His unresponsive right hand made everything he had to do twice as hard. He couldn't just rush at his opponent's head like he had earlier; no, he had to bide his time until the perfect opportunity to strike presented itself. Trying to feint with one working arm was, more or less, inviting unnecessary problems, which was why he refrained from doing so. Instead, he focused on evading the attacks the underling attempted, much to its frustration.

Levi risked tiring himself by going this route, but he preserved, dodging attack after attack that the underling threw at him as he stuck to the plan that had come to him when the krul chief gremlin had stepped off its throne. It was mad as it was brilliant, and the only thing left to do was for him to play his part and hope that the plan worked out.

The previously quiet underling was now shrieking in frustration, the rogue's antics pushing it towards the edge of insanity. Its attacks became wilder, less thought-out. It was desperate to peel Levi's skin with its claws, but for some reason, it just couldn't. The injured rogue avoided every strike, his dislocated shoulder flaring up with each movement he made, but he didn't stop moving because if he stopped, he was as good as dead. Already exhaustion had started to catch up to him from his constant movement, just as it was the underling.

Its movement had lost some of its sharpness; it swung a little slower than when they'd started their little dance. And Levi's movements weren't spared from the effects of exhaustion seeping into both their bones. His dodges were getting slower. When he'd been able to evade the underlings' attacks with ample time, he now evaded it's attacks narrowly. In short, the odds of winning this fight were tipping towards that of the gremlin, with Levi currently enduring the exhaustion that had come from all his movements for the entire night. To be honest, he didn't even know how he was still standing, how he was still fighting.

Just a little bit more, I need to hold on for just a bit longer.

Levi repeated the words in his head, his body protesting the amount of stress he was putting it under, but he kept going regardless. His plan was the only way he saw himself getting out of the dungeon alive. A guttural shriek pierced his thoughts, the sound causing the rogue to grin weakly. The Krul chief gremlin had finally decided to rejoin the fight. The bulky gremlin charged straight for Levi, its cudgel in tow.

Alright, here comes the tricky part.

The sheer madness of his plan made it seem incredulous, but Levi had faith that it'd work out, and if it didn't, then he'd be dead, and dead men didn't have any regrets. So Levi got to work, angling himself closer to the still-attacking underling. He continued to dodge its attacks, keeping an eye on the charging Krul chief gremlin. His plan hinged on his ability to pull off this next part successfully.

Still dodging the attacks the underling threw at him while keeping his body close, he waited and waited until the attack of the charging chief Krul gremlin came, and then he moved. Levi did the easiest thing he'd had to do since he'd begun his fight with the chief's underling — he dropped to the floor like a sack of flour. The chief gremlin's attack, aimed at his chest, hit the neck of its underling with a resounding crack!

That's it.

Everything happened within a few breaths, the underling neck snapped from the force of the blow as it was tossed aside like a rag doll, Levi scrambled to his feet and threw his dagger at the chief gremlin's chest, the blade only sinking an inch deep into the monster's sturdy chest before coming to a halt. The chief gremlin looked at the dagger sticking out of its chest before looking at Levi with disdain. Confusion washed over its features as the rogue rushed at it, putting everything he had into one single attack. He used his good hand to punch the blade hilt deep into the gremlin's chest, the momentum behind his attack pushing both of them to the floor.

Levi used his last burst of energy to roll off the chief gremlin, his dislocated shoulder flaring up, but he tuned it out. Not a moment later, dread saturated his body as he glanced at the gremlin out of the corner of his eyes and saw that it'd begun to raise its cudgel again, only for it to abruptly drop the weapon on the floor with an audible thud! The chief gremlin was dead; he'd done it.
