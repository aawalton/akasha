Chapter 64

The first stop the next morning was on Main Street. Enchanters of Serenor, A fancy store owned by an important family from Leiveen.

According to Elke, they sold the broadest selection of enchanted goods in the entire city of Anvakar.

The sky was clear, warm sunlight touching the blooming trees on the broad main shopping street of the dungeon city. A slight breeze flowed past and Veron connected to it. The wind, as was often the case in Anvakar, pulled him down towards the depths.

We'll go there soon enough. With new gear and armor.

Veron immediately felt a little tense when they entered the imposing stone building with large display windows on its front. The ceiling was high, rich carpets and bright, likely magical chandeliers decorating the expansive entrance hall. Elke didn't seem to mind at all, while Isaac seemed intrigued. Ailis was the only one in the team that seemed to share some of Veron’s discomfort.

Shortly after their arrival, they were led into a beautifully furnished waiting room and served tea.

Elke had already been in touch with them, so they quickly brought a selection of enchanted items.

Three of them, the slightly disinterested attendant presented and explained. The man was tall and lean, his black hair slicked back. He wore a black vest over a white shirt.

He first held up a simple looking wand with a metal tip. “This is a cooling rod enchanted with the essence of the Wennafox. It is normally used to cool drinks but if more Focus is used, it can easily freeze small quantities of liquids.” He held the tip into a glass of water which turned slightly dull in the span of a few seconds before it started freezing with a slightly crunching sound. He left the wand inside.

Veron could see that Elke itched to touch the ice but held herself back.

The attendant moved on to a bowl shaped container made of steel. It was quite broad, about the size of a medium cooking pan. “This item is normally used to keep bottles or food at a cool temperature, at a party or a ball. The steel is enchanted with the essence of the Silvian Eagle, a rare bird that lives in the north of Felrak.”

The items look so mundane. I would’ve had no idea that they were enchanted.

The attendant poured water into the large bowl and then touched it to fuel it with Focus. It took a little longer for the water to freeze but the quantity was higher too.

The last item was a nice looking piece of blue cloth, enchanted with the essence of a Winter Wisp, a weak elemental spirit of ice. “Its cooling properties can help soothe pains, but if fueled with enough Focus, it can be used to freeze a small amount of water too.”

He demonstrated with another glass of water, this one taking significantly longer to freeze.

“The enchanted cloth is valued at three gold, the Silvian Eagle bowl Is valued at six gold, and the cooling rod, due to the rare essence of the Wennafox, is valued at ten gold.”

Elke seemed torn, glancing between the three items.

“If you wish to test the items, you may do so,” the attendant said and stepped aside but didn’t leave the room.

“The rod is clearly the best,” Ailis commented.

“And the most expensive,” Elke said.

“Well, the bowl is way too big,” Isaac added. “And while the cloth kind of works, it just seems annoying if you have to pry off the ice every time and shape it first.”

“I'd have to shape it with the rod as well.”

Veron raised his eyebrows. “Maybe not. Depending on what you want, couldn't you have a crafter make some kind of molds? Then you can just fill them with water and use the rod.”

“Maybe,” Elke said. “I think Sonny would enjoy making something like that. But it's a lot of gold for something so... ineffective.”

“What do you mean ineffective?” Veron said. “You'll get easy access to ice! That would be amazing, no? You could train your Harmony whenever you like.”

“That's true...”

“I also don't think ten gold is all that much for what you get out of it,” Isaac said. “Though I do wonder if you could find it somewhere else for cheaper.”

She shook her head. “These are specialty items. People in Anvakar normally don't buy things like that, so nobody else sells them here.”

“What about negotiating the price down?” Ailis asked.

Elke shrugged slightly.

Ailis motioned to the attendant. “Sir, is ten gold for the cooling rod the lowest you can go? We're an iron team, and we’re ill equipped as it is. Elke can't use her Harmony in the dungeon without an item like this.”

“I'm afraid the prices are non-negotiable. We only sell the highest quality at the Enchanters of Serenor.”

Of course. I wonder who we would have to be for a better price, or what we would have to offer them. Maybe a rare essence?

Either way, Veron didn't think it mattered. With the gold they had made, Elke could afford any of the three items, and he hoped she would get the best one.

They spent the next ten minutes testing the items and playing around with them. The bowl really was just too big, and just like Isaac commented earlier, the ice rag was pretty inconvenient to use. So it came down to the rod, and it looked like Elke was torn.

“I mean it’s exactly what I wanted but ten gold… I could live off that for… a long time.”

“Yes, but you wouldn’t be able to use your Harmony,” Veron said. “Wasn’t this exactly what you wanted?”

“Yeah. What else is gold for than to spend it on things to improve your life?” Isaac added.

“Savings do improve your life in the long run,” Ailis said. “But as it allows you to work on your Harmony, it’s an investment into yourself more so than just spending it on a luxury. I agree that it’s a lot. But we’re adventurers. And I’m sure we’ll find more treasures.”

Elke looked at the rod and tried it out again. She touched the ice it produced and sighed.

Fifteen minutes later, the group left the establishment.

Elke held the rod in her hands, fixated on it and with a slight smile on her face. “That was so expensive,” she whispered.

“Let’s get some water so you can freeze it,” Veron said.

“We should,” Isaac said.

Elke looked at them and smiled, then nodded.

While they walked, Veron remembered that he could just shape some molds for Elke out of rock, instead of having anything like that crafted by someone. So that's what he did. Veron made a mold for two small spheres and one for a set of small spikes.

Feels like I’m making ice trays for fancy cocktails.

They found a fountain on the way to the Shops and Elke got to work, her remaining discomfort at spending so much gold visibly fading. She soon held up a frozen spike and smiled, looking at the team with wide eyes. “It's ice!”

“Fuck yeah!” Veron answered.

Isaac clapped and Ailis raised a fist in joy.

“I can finally work on my Harmony… all the time,” Elke said, as if she still couldn’t quite believe it.

She froze a few more pieces. “My Focus is getting low.”

Veron shrugged. “Good thing it’s not a relic then. Should I continue? Or maybe Isaac, I might need my Focus at Kere’s later.”

“I can freeze some more stuff, if you’re okay with me using the rod,” Isaac said.

Elke was happy to hand it over.

“We will have to show all of this off to Ellie,” Isaac said as he used the item to freeze more water. “How long until our appointment?”

“We have another hour,” Veron said.

“Should we look for potions in the meantime?” Ailis asked.

“We could just hang out here for a while, maybe get something to drink?” Veron asked.

They watched Elke poke and move around the melting ice she held in her hands. She had a broad smile on her face, urging Isaac to freeze more.

“Maybe, yeah,” Ailis said.

Liviana Kere was ready for them at eleven o’clock. She led the group to a spacious back room where various items were already prepared. Evan was with her again.

“Can I get you anything to drink? Or should we get started immediately?”

“I'm eager to try on my new armor,” Isaac said.

Veron gestured for her to start.

Liviana smiled. “Then we shall start with Isaac.”

There were three armor stands with plate armor, a large table with clothing, and another table with a few pieces of armor. Liviana went to the latter.

“I don't think much of an introduction is needed, seeing that you chose the pieces already.” She picked up the helmet, a polished piece of dark steel with a movable visor, all of it sleek in design. It seemed weighty when she turned it around in her hands and opening the visor made a smooth sound. It had an open slit broad enough to let the wearer see with almost their full field of view. “Larsen’s pride themselves in long lasting and practical designs, though I admit that while it's supposedly not their main focus, their pieces always look stunning too. Not much to say here, at your Path level, this is some of the best armor you can buy. The helmet is from the Cavalry Collection, and the cuirass is from the Duelist line.”

She set down the helmet and picked up the chest and back piece. The metal seemed steady and light at the same time, the magical lantern light giving the dark steel an almost golden sheen. “The weight should barely impair you, and we can adjust the fit slightly. At the Adequate stage, you should hardly even notice wearing any of these pieces.” She showed off the pieces to protect the thighs, shins, and feet. “If the final fit is uncomfortable, we can change out or adjust the buffcoat that you chose.” The coat had a tan color and the texture of leather. “In combination, it should be a comfortable wear, protecting your vitals completely and protecting your arms and shoulders from slashing attacks at the very least. Do you want to try it on?”

Isaac beamed and joined her.

Evan walked over and started explaining the various pieces, straps and where to connect everything.

Liviana continued seamlessly, walking over to the first armor stand while addressing Ailis. “These are ordered with quality and price increasing from left to right. The first piece is rather standard and what I consider an efficient and suitable set of plate armor for an adventurer at the Adequate to Durable stages.”

The metal was a dull gray, polished but with far less of a sheen than the Larsen pieces. It comprised of a cuirass and pieces that protected the arms, legs, feet, shoulders, and hands. There were clear weak points near the joints. The helmet had a nose guard and an additional piece that would be connected to the cuirass to protect the chin. “This set was made at the Bolt Smithy. While it certainly won't win a prize for aesthetics, you won't find a better set at the price range.

“While the other two sets boast more comprehensible protection, my opinion is that medium range Bolt plate it's more than suitable to deal with first and second layer threats. The whole set costs five gold and thirty silver. If you intend to buy comfortable and fitting arming clothes, it would top out at around six gold.”

She went on to the second set. It looked sleeker in design, had a helmet with a movable visor, and smaller weak points at the joints. “This is a Tolner set, a local maker just like the other two. You can tell the joints are better protected, and the helmet covers almost the entire face. The alloy used is a slightly higher quality than the first set. All of it combined makes for a good piece suitable for at least the first three layers of the dungeon, however the smaller weak points and visor will make it more difficult to get used to, and your movements won't be quite as free and smooth as with the first set. I would take into account the choice of your weapon. This set costs seven gold, going up to around seven gold and seventy silver if you want new arming clothes that fit well.”

“I also allowed myself to showcase something slightly above your stated budget. It's a set by Victoria Smith which I would sell for nine gold. Do you want me to present it?”

Ailis considered. She glanced at Elke and then nodded to the shop owner.

The last set was by far the sleekest in design, and the metal sheen looked impressive. The helmet had a visor too and chain mail connecting to it that fell towards the cuirass to protect the neck. Small pieces of mail protected the weak points at the joints.

“All the mail is removable and it should protect from slashing and light piercing attacks. With how well designed it is, your movements should be less impaired than with the Tolner set, though it will still take some time to get used to. For the Adequate stage, this is one of the best sets of plate armor you will find in Anvakar. I dare say it can compete with the big names from Norkamm. I think the same consideration as with the first two sets applies. You'll carry a little more weight, will give up some field of view, and you'll sacrifice a bit of maneuverability, all for additional protection. Which one do you want to try first?”

Ailis went and touched the first set, lifting the arms and then the helmet, then going on to the second. Elke joined her.

“While she decides where to start, why don't I show you what we prepared for you, Veron,” Liviana said and led him to the table full of clothes. “Here we have several sets of high quality linen clothing that should fit you perfectly,” she said and showed him the pieces. “They are plain but very well made. At thirty silver per set, they are on the expensive end, but they'll last you a lot longer than cheaper ware. Feel free to try them on. The main pieces I prepared however, are the padded gambesons and arming pants.”

She showed them off, the colors dark brown or tan, the garment thick. “The arming points sewn into the fabric allow you to connect your stone pieces which should improve your mobility even as you increase the weight and density of your armor. The padding, which consists of cloth and horse hair will heavily reduce the impacts you feel from blunt attacks. I have several thicknesses and qualities here, though as you are an Elemental Path, I would recommend the thicker variants, knowing you won’t be bothered as much by heat as any other Paths. The prices range from fifty silver to two gold. Take your time, try on a set of clothes, put on a gambeson and a pair of arming pants on top, and then I’ll help you connect your armor. I’ve also got something for your helmet, I’ll show you afterwards. And then we can try the shields and weapons.”

“This is great,” Veron said, touching the various pieces. “Thanks, Liviana. I’ll have a look.”

She smiled and went over to Ailis, asking her which plate armor she wanted to try first.

Veron first checked the normal clothing. He had bought a few new sets in the last few weeks but just touching these, they felt both more comfortable and more robust than what he wore. He first took off his armor piece by piece, noticing a box full of rocks sitting under the table.

How considerate.

There were dividers in the room and he quickly went over to put on a set of the linen clothing. It always surprised him how much of a difference well-fitting and high quality clothing made. Sometimes it made him feel self-conscious, but the pieces here looked plain. He admired himself in one of the mirrors set up in the room and smiled to himself.

I look like a well-dressed peasant.

Next, he went and checked the gambesons, seeing at the same time that Isaac was already finishing up his new look. Liviana meanwhile was helping Ailis put on the first set of plate armor.

Veron first chose the lightest variant, buttoned it closed, and checked how it felt to move around. He added arming pants above his linen ones afterwards.

I already feel cushioned.

Veron tested the thicker variants without his armor and found that the middle one felt the most comfortable.

Isaac joined him then. He looked downright regal with his high class cuirass and helmet, giving Veron a slight bow. “Isaac Aldana, at your service.” He opened the visor of his helmet to reveal a bright smile. “All of this feels so much better.”

“I agree,” Veron said.

Liviana came over then and showed him how to fasten his armor to the gambeson. “You could connect it with rock directly but I would recommend incorporating straps into the armor, for some flexibility.”

It took some time and Focus, but Liviana knew which sections to focus on, where exactly to incorporate the thin straps, and how deep to inlay them into the armor.

Veron was almost out of Focus when they were done, and then she showed him how to connect everything to the gambeson.

Ailis and Isaac moved around in the meantime, testing their new gear and trying out using their weapons.

When Veron had fastened his armor to the gambeson and padded pants, he rolled his shoulders and immediately knew what Liviana had talked about on the previous day.

With the rock so close to his body and the increased strength that his Path provided, it was easy to get used to the set he had made, even without any arming clothes. He had adjusted as well, to the weight and some of the awkwardness that heavy armor came with. Of course he had simply smoothed out everything with his Harmony, but he realized now that everything he had done was to adjust himself to the armor. Not really the other way around.

Now that he had near full body padding and straps that kept all the pieces in place, he hardly felt like he was wearing any armor at all.

All of his movements felt comfortable, and it felt so much lighter, though he was sure it was a combination of his recent Path advancement and the padding that now helped distribute the weight a lot better. He jumped slightly and then got his shield and mace to test.

Veron tried a few attacks and blocking, even letting Ailis strike his armor repeatedly.

He found the impacts heavily dulled, as if he was wrapped in padded clothing and rock armor, which of course he was. Compared to how he’d felt during his first few spars with Hayden, it really did feel like night and day.

Maybe I should even try the heavier padding. And once I get my Focus back, I'll have to start to add weight.

Veron smiled as he tapped his chest with a fist, barely feeling any of the impact.
