The townsfolk gathered in the center of Chalaco, anxious about what was happening around them. The usually hot desert was colder as the purple-hued sand danced around, blowing Kae's long hair to the east. As they all approached the group, they marveled at the palace before them. The structure was pale, firm, with gold trim like the sands of the desert.

From the building, a tall man came out. An older man with short black hair and a darker complexion walked out of a nearby building.He had a red sash covering the left side of his eye, while the other eye burned like brass and gold. With a rugged beard and a fierce look, he looked aged from war. In a rough voice, he addressed his people.

"Everyone, calm down, please."

His voice was like that of a lion, mirroring his tall, bulky build. Older yet still with the aura of a fierce beast.

"What's the situation, Elder Cain?!"

The crowd erupted in shouts and pleas. But with a wave of their elders' hand, the villagers settled down as he prepared to speak.

"The Fallen Angel, Perry, and other monsters have been relentlessly attacking our town. We've attempted to contact the city of Mages, Fiador Hallows, to no avail..."

The gasp of the worn-down villages took over the group. Murmurs of hope were cast aside as the collection started to grow rowdy. A woman cried out.

"What about my child? What about the Thieves Guild? Shouldn't they protect us instead of the Hallows?"

A wounded woman in the crowd shouted out.

"Has Rexia forsaken us?"

A young man cried, pleading for the Goddess above to listen.

Kae, Palona, and Dareth looked around. Even with the barrier around them, buildings and homes were damaged, and the people looked no different. Clearly, the monsters have been getting inside and wreaking havoc. The barrier was nothing but a forethought at this point.

It would only be a matter of time before the "Enlightened" beast ran through the village and destroyed its people.

"This is awful."

Kae murmured.

"What are we going to do, Elder Cain?!"

Another man howled at their leader. Elder Cain, the chief in charge, adjusted his red cloak. The words leaving his lips were wary, knowing the feelings of those around him before he spoke.

"We plan to send another group to the Thieves' Guild."

Reluctantly, Cain declared. The crowd gasped as he held his hands up, palms out.

"Now hold on! It's the only way-"

"The last group didn't come back! Elder Cain, there has to be another way than sending our friends and family into the desert!"

"He's right! We can't keep sending people to their deaths!"

The crowd agreed, pleading for another way. The elder's eyes swept over the crowd, then rested on the unique three.

"Wait, who are you three? Are you part of the rescue crew? Human, elf, and fox?"

The merchant pointed to himself,

"Are you talking to us?"

Dareth asked, cautiously.

"I am."

The elder replied. To Dareth's surprise, the NPC answered him.

"What the heck?"

The merchant whispered.

Palona stepped forward, taking over the discussion though.

"We came to the desert, attempting the Thieves Guild trial, but were attacked by the black tornadoes and Perry, who seems stronger than ever..."

Palona explained, crossing her arms. She turned to the broken crowd.

"Fear not, hurt ones, Rexia has not forsaken you. She loves you all!"

Palona reached out to the people, even though their sunken faces didn't inspire hope back.

"Whatever is happening here, Rexia sent us here to help. So please, don't give up on the Goddess just yet."

Hearing the people's anguish stirred something in Kae. These weren't just NPCs anymore; they looked scared, wounded, and desperate for answers. These... were people.

"Palona is right..."

Kae spoke up, walking to the group.

"We may not have known the situation before, but now that we see it clearly, it's our responsibility to help."

The fox's tail wagged as she walked through the crowd, toward Elder Cain. The older man was tall and formidable. Kae remembered the short bio about Cain, the leader of Chalaco. Another NPC that Kae never considered meeting, let alone talking to, as a real person. Cain wasn't a True Hero, but he was a powerful soldier who fought countless battles. Another hero in Keostar.

"You mentioned sending people to the Thieves' Guild? I take it they didn't reach it?"

Kae asked, and Cain groaned with a nod.

"We believe that to be the case. We've been stuck in a cycle, trying to get out of this bleak situation."

Cain explained. He rubbed his beard, thinking of his options.

"We were already planning to go there. If you believe getting there can help us solve this incident, then we'll do it."

The Foxian said, confidently. The elder was hesitant at first, but the groans and pain of his people made him accept any help. So, he nodded firmly.

"Regarding the Thieves' Guild… It's located further north in the desert."

Cain groaned as he looked back down at the Foxian.

"We sent a few men, but they never returned. We worry for their safety, along with the Guild's."

Cain looked out into the distance as the weather weighed heavily on his words. Dareth stepped forward.

"So, you need us to make contact with the Thieves' Guild and see if they can aid Chalaco?"

The Baron asked. He put his hands on his hips and gave them a sharp, confident smile.

"I'm down for this mission. This is getting exciting."

Dareth beamed.

"Yeah, it is different, isn't it?"

Kae whispered to Dareth. The last fight with Perry was rough, but she learned his pattern. She knew more than last time, meaning she could strategize and come out on top.

"My name is Kae Loredom. That Baron is Dareth Darkmere, and the elf is Palona Vertra. We will accept this mission to find a way to the Thieves' Guild for support."

The fox promised. Looking at the fox, the elf felt a new sense of determination herself. Palona walked up on stage and turned to the hurting men and women.

"We don't know why the monsters around here have been so aggressive. But I promise you, Rexia has not forsaken any of you."

With her great sword, she held it high. Even in the darkness of the storm, it glimmered with a little bit of hope for the beaten village.

"So, have faith! I promise you that I, Palona Vertra, will find a way to protect all of you as Rexia's sword!"

The might in her voice was unyielding. As her words touched the ears of those who never met her, they washed over the people, blanketing them with anticipation.

"Please, help us, Miss Palona!"

A worn man cried out.

"Thank you! Thank you!"

Another woman praised Palona. It seemed the group took a liking to the elf Knight. Kae smirked, thinking about how reliable she was. They had only just met, but she was starting to grow on the fox.

"Despite my having to kill your 'sister,' I think we can be good friends one day, Palona."

She mused silently. Dareth smiled, a glint of excitement in his eyes. He turned to the fox.

"Ever since I met you, Kae, things have become a lot more interesting."

The fox grinned back.

"Maybe there's more to this game than you thought? Come on, you two. We'll have to prepare before we venture to the Thieves' Guild."

Kae was beginning to realize that this journey wouldn't be as simple as defeating Rexia and changing the world. As she looked at the crying children, wounded men, and women, it settled in. For the first time, she saw the people here not as game characters, but as individuals truly suffering.

Palona walked back down from the stage and made her way to Kae.

"Are we going to prepare? The longer we wait, the more these people suffer."

The elf said, glancing back at the tired villagers.

"Yeah, I was just speaking with Dareth about that. Let's go to the local weapons-and-armor shop. There should be some local gear that will help."

Her tail meandered from side to side.

"Perry is using thunder-based spells. So, I plan to make his overall damage deal far less than before."

Most gears were designed to be off-balanced like that. Just another part of the world that Kae knew like the back of her hand.

"Dareth, Palona..."

Kae perked up.

"This is our new operation. As we move through the desert, we'll encounter stronger monsters and maybe even Perry again. We'll need to have each other's backs. That means no grieving me or Palona, Dareth."

Dareth raised an eyebrow but smiled despite the reprimand.

"You don't have to worry about me. I'm actually curious to see where this goes."

With a finger in his ear, he slanted his eyes.

"When will you actually believe me?"

Like a child, he complained. But Palona, like his mother, crossed her arms and glared at the childish merchant.

"We say this because this isn't a game to us, Dareth. If one thing goes wrong, Kae and I will die permanently. As hard as that is to believe..."

The elf trailed off.

"Despite whether you believe us or not, Dareth, this game is more real for us than it is for you. So, let's make a truce for the remainder of this mission."

The fox stood tall against the human Trade Baron.

"We are a team and act as one. Because right now, the game is harder."

Saying those words drew in another layer of excitement to Kae. She'd always wanted a challenge to test her skills and knowledge of Keostar, and here it was. But not only that. In a way, this will be the isolated test of a world where people need to work together... to succeed.

"Oh, so you're trying a hardcore run? Both of you?"

Dareth said, impressed.

"Guess you really are veterans at Keostar Online. I'm willing to try it, sounds fun."

Kae noted the spark in Dareth's eyes. Despite his previous complaints about the game being boring, something had changed in this time. He seemed motivated now, and Kae couldn't help but feel cautiously optimistic that Dareth would pull his weight.

"First order of business is making sure we're prepared. Let's head to the shops and gear up. "

The motivated fox pointed at the merchant first.

"Dareth, your stats are the strongest, but Palona might be stronger in skills than you. My knowledge of this game should guide us. Let me lead the way for now."

Dareth nodded.

"So, you're going to be our strategist, Kae Loredom."

The Baron asked.

"You know it. That's what I'm good at."

With that, the trio made their way to a multi-purpose shop. Being stuck in this strange world of the purple desert, Kae would have to work with what they have here. Thankfully, there was an alchemy shop that she desperately needed.

"Dareth, have you created elemental armor yet?"

Kae opened a shared menu with the merchant.

"I haven't yet. Are you going to make thunder armor?"

"Yep. Using [Alchemy Scrolls], you can get from Dark Beetles and other enemies, and you can enchant your armors with an element."

The farming from the Swordsmen Guild wasn't just for EXP but for the 1.2% drop rate of [Alchemy Scrolls]. Just another piece of information Kae had in the back of her mind for later.

"I can take my current [Wooden Helmet] and select what element I want on it. But I have to use [Beetle Shells] as an ingredient since the Dark Beetles have thunder resistance. I wanted to use these for when I got better gear, but time is of the essence. What a waste of good scrolls..."

The Foxian pouted. Her tail was darting around, stabbing at the sand on the ground. Palona scratched her cheek. She was grossed out by the [Beetle Shells], but watching Kae complain was amusing, at least.

"You really sound like this is a nuisance to you."

The Knight prodded.

"It is annoying. Perry is making me waste my scrolls for this. I had a plan for my early game…"

Kae merged her armors using alchemy. Her character lit up faintly as the effects took place. A sharp animation of dancing electricity resonated on her gear, enchanting her with thunder resistance.

"This won't make me immune to thunder attacks, but it will make it less effective. Good for someone as low level as me."

Dareth was amused once again. He nodded as he watched the effects take place on Kae's gear.

"It's good to know. How did you find those scrolls again, Kae?"

"Some enemies drop them. The Swordsmen Guild has some easy enemies for them, but the drop rate is extremely low."

The fox looked back, excitedly. Her eyes were shining like the new morning's sun.

"I can show you later. Maybe we can grind there for a while and get a bunch of scrolls together."

The thought of staying in a dark sewer for days on end made the merchant want to kill over… Dareth sighed.

"It sounds… like a lot of farming, Kae."

Kae nodded fiercely.

"It is a lot of farming, but very rewarding. Isn't it fun when hard work pays off? That's the whole point of multiplayer online games, right?"

She looked back at the baron trader, eyes glimmering like diamonds shining in the purple hue of the sand. Dareth looked away, one finger in his ear, and he groaned softly.

"I'll think about it, Kae. Maybe… Maybe it is worth it, I guess... if you put it that way."

After the group stocked up on [Red Potion], [Strawberry Juice], and a new item, [Mint Leaf], to restore [Stun] and [Shock] status effects, too. Together, they walked towards the edge of town.
