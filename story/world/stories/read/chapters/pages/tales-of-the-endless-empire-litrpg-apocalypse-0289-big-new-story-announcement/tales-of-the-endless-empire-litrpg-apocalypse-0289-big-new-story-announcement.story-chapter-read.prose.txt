Synopsis:

"Good Sukuna" saving a world where evil has thrived for thousands of years

My name is Tiberius. Once, I was known as the greatest emperor the world had ever seen. Now, I am nothing more than a soul bound within an amulet. My citizens were slaughtered, and my empire was drowned beneath the ocean’s surface. Only I remain, clinging to the memory of our grand dream of freedom, waiting in my prison beneath the waves for someone to set me free.

Centuries passed. Humanity grew weak, overshadowed by elves, orcs, dwarves, and dragons. An invasion loomed on the horizon, and in desperation, the king sent out his subjects to search for the Amulet of Tiberius, though now I am remembered only as the Mad Emperor. (Yes, they really did me dirty there.)

Ancient texts claim the Mad Emperor would be bound to obey the will of whoever holds the amulet. Such a master would become the most powerful being alive, strong enough to terrify the other kingdoms and halt a war that could cost millions of lives.
