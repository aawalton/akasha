The night was young, the sky was clear, and thousands of stars were clearly visible to the naked eye. But what seems to be real is not always real, especially when gods are involved.

A Soul Bringer, a subordinate of God, was flying through the skies of the mortal realm while concealing his presence from human eyes. He was wearing his formal attire, an elegant silver armor with a black cape on his back and a sword at his waist. He was holding a small glowing orb, containing the soul of a deceased human.

Since his job was finished for the day, it was time to return to the heavenly realm. After reaching high above the ground, he began chanting a spell, and soon, the space in front of him began distorting in a circular motion. From the center of the distortion, a circular portal formed. The portal had a dark tunnel inside, which was connected to another portal at the end, his destination. He stepped inside, closed the portal, and went to the other side.

After reaching his destination, a huge circular hall came into his view. The hall resembled the Roman Colosseum from inside. Its inner wall was lined with hundreds of portals connected to different realms. Each portal was a few meters apart, and in between, torch sconces with white flames were placed.

Countless Soul Bringers were present there, and every one of them was wearing the same formal attire, the elegant silver armor with a black cape on their back. Some were waiting, some were entering the portals, and some were exiting the portals with a crystal orb in hand.

At the center of the hall, a heavenly being was sitting gracefully on a majestic chair. He was wearing black armor with a red cape on his back. He was reading a massive handwritten tome that was hovering just above his lap. The tome was continuously emitting a golden haze, and its pages were turning themselves as the heavenly being was reading it.

All the Soul Bringers who came back after collecting the designated souls were waiting for their names to be called by the heavenly being and present their crystal orb to him. The heavenly being was not only determining how a soul would be born, where it would be born, what challenges it would face, but he was also deciding how much the soul would suffer during its lifetime. Though how a soul would deal with its circumstances and where it would take its life would be entirely up to the soul. If a soul becomes determined enough, it would surely be able to overcome its predestined fate. The heavenly being always made sure to leave such ways for every soul.

Just like every day, everything was proceeding normally. But this time, something unexpected happened. The heavenly being called a name again, and the Soul Bringer stepped forward, knelt down, and presented his crystal orb with both hands. This was the Soul Bringer, who came from the mortal realm of Earth.

The heavenly being waved his hand upward, and the orb began hovering in the air and moved just above the hovering tome. Pages of the tome turned themselves rapidly, and the heavenly being began checking the soul’s karma. As he finished reading, his blank expression shifted into a solemn one. He looked at the orb, then looked at his tome, then again looked back at the orb, and again he looked back at the tome.

“Soul Bringer, what have you done? Do you understand the consequences of your actions?”

The Soul Bringer realized something was wrong. “Forgive me, good Commander, for I do not understand your meaning.”

The heavenly being was at a loss for words. Looking at his subordinate, he realized this was likely unintentional. “This is not the rightful soul. How did you manage to retrieve the incorrect one? There should be no way for such an error to occur.”

The Soul Bringer was shocked. He didn't know what to say. He didn't know how he had made a mistake even after checking again and again. “I truly brought the soul guided by the threads of destiny. There should be no error... at least, none on my part.”

”Yet a wrongful soul has been presented before me. Did you even verify it correctly? Hah... No matter. Restore this soul to its rightful vessel and deliver the true soul this time. I have examined the vessel’s current state. His last rites are in progress. Return it with haste, else you must endure grievous consequences.” The Commander was irritated, but he kept himself composed as he issued the command.

The Soul Bringer became tense and replied with certainty. “Yes, Commander. I shall amend my negligence and bring you the rightful soul.”

Several hours later, the Soul Bringer returned holding the same crystal orb. His face was pale, and he was sweating profusely. He walked slowly to his superior. “Commander, I have failed my oath. The current of time in the mortal realm moved too swiftly for my approach, and I could not place the soul within its rightful vessel.”

The Commander could no longer hide his anger. “Are you serious? Do you understand what you have caused? Not only were you negligent, but you also moved with the lethargy of a mortal! Face the consequences. I shall not aid you in any way. Your fate rests in the hands of His Majesty.”

The Soul Bringer was horrified. The only thing he could think of was apologizing to His Majesty directly. “Please, allow me to speak to His Majesty and inform him of my error. I shall take responsibility.” Perhaps an honest apology will at least lessen my punishment.

They were discussing the matter so tensely that they did not even notice the crystal’s soul had started fading into oblivion. It was not only fading, but was also turning into white smoke and disintegrating into nothingness.

In the heavenly realm, an underdeveloped soul cannot stay intact. So the temporary vessels, the crystal orbs, are used to prevent the dense divine energy from disintegrating and absorbing the souls. However, there is a limit to how much pressure a temporary vessel can handle. Before that limit is reached, the souls are always sent back to the mortal realm. Even a vessel transfer is impossible due to the dangers involved in the process.

But now, the crystal orb’s limit was about to be reached. Once it happens, the soul would disintegrate completely. In the ancient era, mortals used to develop themselves through meditation and inner cultivation. But in the current era, mortals have forgotten such things. No mortal being practices them anymore. Consequently, their souls have become extremely weak. And this soul was no different.

Everyone in the Heavenly Realm, including the Soul Bringer, knew this well. When he noticed the disintegration process, he immediately started sprinting toward His Majesty’s throne room. He knew that disintegration was an irreversible process, and only His Majesty was capable of making the unthinkable possible. He sprinted without looking back or saying a word to the Commander.

After reaching his destination, the Soul Bringer met the guard stationed outside. “Please, ask His Majesty to grant me an audience. It is a matter of utmost urgency.”

The massive doors to the throne room opened with a thunderous sound. The vibrations were enough to be felt just by standing near the door. The guard went inside to seek permission, and it was granted. The Soul Bringer entered immediately.

The throne room was incredibly majestic and vast. It had no ceiling, but instead, billions and billions of stars in outer space were visible above. There were two rows of pillars lined straight from the gate to the dais, and in between, a long red carpet was placed. Each pillar had four torch sconces on all sides. The white flames within the sconces were so bright that the entire room was shimmering with heavenly elegance.

On both sides of the carpet, many guards were standing in line. Each one was clad in elegant golden armor with a red cape. They carried massive shields on their backs and swords at their waists. They were the Celestial Guards, one of the most fearsome fighting forces in Heaven.

Just before the dais, there was a slightly raised platform, and in its center, a short cylindrical altar with an intricate design was placed. On the dais, His Majesty was sitting on a colossal throne, looking directly at the Soul Bringer.

He walked up to the dais, knelt, and bowed down. Before he could offer his greetings, His Majesty asked sternly, “What has happened that you request my audience with such urgency?”

“Your Majesty, I have committed a most grievous error. This has brought a wretched soul to the brink of dissolution. I know not if anyone, save Your Majesty, can save this soul. I beg you, extend your mercy to this poor being and allow me to atone for my own transgressions.” Knowing the soul inside the crystal orb was already vanishing, the Soul Bringer kept the details short. He presented the crystal ball with both hands while keeping his head bowed. He dared not look at His Majesty.

“Make haste and lay the soul upon the Altar of Rebirth.”

“Yes, Your Majesty,” the Soul Bringer replied. He stood up, walked up to the altar before the dais, and placed the crystal orb on it. Then he stepped back and knelt just like before.

The soul was already halfway disintegrated into nothingness. His Majesty stepped forward and approached the altar while summoning a hovering tome. The tome materialized out of thin air, its pages turned themselves just like the Commander’s tome. This tome was far more majestic, and its pages were shimmering with a dense golden aura.

His Majesty touched the crystal orb and read the contents of the page with an amused expression. “For many a year, my eyes have not beheld a soul with so many virtuous deeds. You are truly worthy of praise, good soul, especially for keeping nobility within your heart during your blackest hours. I shall bestow upon you a gift in recompense for my servant’s folly and in honor of your kindness. This is a boon I have not granted in many an age. Employ it wisely for the welfare of the realm you are soon to enter.”

His Majesty closed his eyes while facing the cosmos above. A golden light descended from above and began merging with the disintegrating side of the soul. Immediately, the soul started regenerating. And simultaneously, the air surrounding the altar swirled into a cyclone, creating a pressure that pushed everyone back. Even the Celestial Guards were struggling to keep their footing.

It took some time for the swirling golden light to merge with the disintegrated side of the soul. Once the golden light was merged entirely, the soul began glowing with a golden aura. Gradually, the white light emitted by the soul also transformed into a soft, radiant golden glow. And the cyclone around the altar died down entirely.

Then, His Majesty raised his palm and began using his energy. Immediately, a small portal formed just above the altar. His Majesty waved his hand upward, and the soul rose out of the crystal ball and entered the portal. “Soul, I hold high expectations for you. Let me not be disappointed. I shall observe your deeds.”

Inside the portal was an unending multicolored tunnel. The tunnel looked as if it were created with vibrant waves of Aurora itself. The soul traveled through there for some time.

Exiting the tunnel, the soul found itself in a sky filled with lightning and thunderous sound. Below the sky was a tall mountain range filled with deep trenches.

The soul changed its direction and traveled downwards and disappeared into the deepest part of the mountain range.

—Several months later—

In a vast forest, where trees were so dense that even the moonlight was hardly reaching the ground, five armored men were surrounding a beautiful woman. She had white hair, pale skin, and she was carrying a white, wingless infant dragon in her arms.

“Lady Asphine, please surrender that abomination. Running away will only make things worse.” The leader of the armored men requested politely. But his deceitful expression gave away how serious he was.

Abomination, huh? Is that what they think about my child? The lady glanced at the baby with a grim expression and asked, “Lord sent you, right? Could you please pretend that you never saw me? This is my humble request to you.”

“My Lady, you know we can't do that... Please don't make our work harder than this. The Lord has already been informed. If you don’t come with us, he may take drastic measures.”

She sighed. “It has finally come to this, huh? There is nothing to talk about.” The lady replied. Immediately after, she created a white colored barrier around the baby and began charging her body with a huge amount of lightning energy. Her energy was so potent that surrounding tree trunks, undergrowth, and fallen dry leaves started burning due to continuous miniature lightning bolts.

She wants to make it a fight to the death!!! The leader was shocked by how decisive she was. Her intention was clear. Seeing her rising energy level, all five men got alarmed and began using their own energy to intercept.

She held her baby with her left arm and then transferred all her energy into her right hand claws. This forcefully condensed her energy to an extreme level, and lightning from her claws began bursting out violently.

I knew she would do this. “Prepare to engage. You’re free to use your artifacts.” As soon as the leader commanded, Asphine charged towards him, aiming her claws directly at his face. Simultaneously, the leader raised his palm, transferred his energy, and created a condensed fire explosion magic to counter her attack.

But instead of making a direct collision, Asphine jumped very hard, dodging his attack, and reached the clouds almost immediately. The leader's explosion magic went straight to his subordinates, blasting all of them away.

After Asphine saw the explosion below, she distributed the lightning energy from her hand, charged her body entirely, created a lightning blast to change her direction towards the horizon, and began traveling through the sky with the speed of real lightning. From below, it seemed as if she had converted herself into lightning itself.

"How are we supposed to deal with a lightning dragon?" The leader sighed and began looking after his subordinates.
