The battlefield was carnage, bodies lining the ground. Screams and warcries filled the air, the sharp clang of metal on metal unending. Blood mixed with the murky waters of the Scaled swamps, turning it a sickly brown.

Risha’s blades met the towering shield of a scaled warrior, only to bounce off.

She ducked under the swipe of its claymore, twisting around to land a shallow cut on the creature’s arm. Its shield punched into her, throwing her backwards and she stepped through the shadows to dodge its following strike.

Appearing to the creature's side, she threw her sword. Crimson’s chain slid through her hand, puncturing the warrior’s ribs.

She pulled herself to her sword, and Scarlet’s blade cut through the large warrior’s neck.

Deep into the enemy lines, more of the scaled creatures thought to approach her, but she only had eyes for the rainbow scaled warrior who still watched her.

“Igni. Burn them.”

The spirit howled at her command, erupting from her in an inferno of flame that devoured the approaching warriors.

The flames swirled around Risha, it’s head taking on the form of a wolf’s head. It too watched the rainbow scaled warrior.

Finally, the scaled creature gave Risha a smirk. It reached down, and the earth rose up with the water of the swamp, forming into a staff, an icy curved blade forming at its end.

The glaive shimmered, carrying with it an intimidating force. Risha looked at the glaive, trying to place the strange sensation.

Slowly, inevitably, the scaled warrior stepped forward.

His allies marched with him, towering warriors that circled him as an honor guard.

Risha showed her bloody teeth, and Igni returned to her. She channeled spirit through her chain swords, igniting them with a purple fire that lit her skin with its eerie light. Igni joined her flames, flaring them with his own power.

Then they were upon her.

Spiderlings streamed from her robes, meeting the Scaled Leader’s honor guard.

Risha took advantage of their surprise, and threw her Crimson’s blade. The Scaled Leader’s glaive swung, deflecting the sword into the muck behind him.

Risha pulled herself to the sword, and Scarlet’s fiery blade met the shaft of their leader’s glaive.

Elemental magic met Risha’s spiritual fire and Risha was pushed back a step.

Risha slipped under the point of the glaive, dodging a thrust as she pulled Crimson’s blade back to her.

Her swords cut, only to be met by both ends of the glaive. Their leader met her with the skill of a highly leveled warrior, his glaive spinning through his hands as he met each cut and strike with force that made her step back.

Her flames refused to gain purchase on the strange glaive, whatever magic that filled it fighting against her weapons with a strange pressure.

Risha thrust, only to slip on the swampy floor they fought on.

The leader took advantage of her misstep, his blade seeking her stomach. She stepped away through the darkness, then stumbled as part of her armor fell to the ground, sinking into the murky water.

Risha stumbled, as she looked down at her stomach. Brown and blue veins spread from the cut in her stomach, infecting her and slowing her.

Igni left her swords, and rushed to the wound, burning away the magical infection. Risha bared her teeth from pain, and looked up into the confident eyes of the Scaled Leader.

“You are what he fears? And to think, I, the lowliest of servants, was all he needed to defeat you.” The scaled warrior stepped forward.

Risha raised her sword, then stumbled from pain. The strange magical infection was slowed, but not defeated, and even Mother’s Ferocity wasn’t enough for her to ignore it.

Risha still stood, the strength of her many battles forcing her to stand against the tyrant in front of her. She bared her teeth, “Risha is not defeated, dumb stupid fish.”

“Your insults mean nothing to me, goblin. I live for his purpose, not mine own.” The warrior raised his glaive, and energy flowed into it from the earth. It glowed with the elemental energy of something beyond Risha’s power. Something she’d witnessed before.

“Stupid lizard blindly follows a Spark?” Risha asked as she raised her own weapons. The movement screamed with fire in her stomach, but she still raised them. She shifted into [Pack Leader: Black], the strength of the Black Lupus Kobold giving her what she needed to hold her swords up.

“He is no mere Spark.” The lizard said, lowering his glaive. It glowed with power that froze Risha’s skin. “He is the Great One.”

The Lizard stepped forward, and the swamps rose around them. Earth and water collapsed towards her in two tsunamis of the elements. The water sharpened into ice as the earth glowed with strength.

Risha tried to step away through the darkness, only for the magic in her stomach to hold her in place. She raised her swords, ready to meet the power of nature itself.

A spear pierced the collapsing walls of earth and ice on one side, spirit spreading through the tsunami and shattering it to pieces. The spear and the elements fell to the floor in a rain of muck. On the opposite side, webs of purple spirit wrapped the wall of elements, holding them still as spirit battled against the elemental power of a spark.

A goblin landed in the murky water next to his spear, he was bloodied, but his gaze was strong as he pulled his spear out of the swamps.

A black spider empress crested the webbed wall of elements, standing on top of it imperiously.

The scaled warrior raised his glaive, and placed its non-bladed end against the ground. “Your Grand Mother is weak. Look. Look at how she falters in front of a fraction of the Great One’s power.”

Glo snorted, “Look at this ugly lizard, Risha. He’s a stupid, ugly dumb dumb.”

Risha nodded, smiling despite herself. “Very dumb dumb. He can’t even fight Risha on his own.” She shook her head, “Glo, can you kill this ugly lizard?”

“Glo will happily show you his severed head!” He gave Risha a wink, then smiled with his sharp teeth as spirit gathered around him. He turned to the Scaled Leader, “Since you have help, Glo will call upon help of his own.”

He tapped his spear against the ground, then familiar symbols filled the air. Symbols similar, and yet different from what the ancient shaman had shown Risha when she bound Igni.

Slowly, a portal to the spirit realm opened behind them.

A plated boot stepped out of the portal, followed by the spectral visage of an armored warrior. His eyes were simple points of purple fire. Behind him, ancient monster warriors stepped from the portal. Shamans, archers, knights, they carried armor and weapons that even Risha’s people didn’t have, all too similar to the runed panther bones she’d created with the help of the Ancient Shaman.

“You can leave this to me, Klaz’zks, Risha.” Glo said to the black spider who watched from atop her webbed tsunami.

The black spider bowed her head, then disappeared to help elsewhere on the battlefield.

Risha trusted her general and immediately sat down, focusing on the energy that spread through the wound in her stomach.
