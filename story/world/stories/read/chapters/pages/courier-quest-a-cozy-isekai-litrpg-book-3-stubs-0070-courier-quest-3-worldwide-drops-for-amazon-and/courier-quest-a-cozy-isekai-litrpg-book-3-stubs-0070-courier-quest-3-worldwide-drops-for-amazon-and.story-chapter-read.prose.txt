So we're still a few months off, but the final book of Courier Quest now has a release date: November 10th!

I had wanted it to drop in late September or early October but, alas, the scheduling fairies were out of town that day. Instead, the wonderful Giancarlo Herrera and Hannah Schooner will be reprising their roles as narrators for the series. They'll be breathing life into Trevor, Adabelle, and the rest of the Courier Quest world in time for a simultaneous release!

That said, only one is available for preorder today, and that's the eBook (link above.) Book 3 will remain here on Royal Road until November 9th when I'll delete it in preparation for the launch.

Thanks for sticking around and reading about Trevor and the hijinks he got around to in his new home. It really warms me that this cozy little series became as popular as it was. Until next time!
