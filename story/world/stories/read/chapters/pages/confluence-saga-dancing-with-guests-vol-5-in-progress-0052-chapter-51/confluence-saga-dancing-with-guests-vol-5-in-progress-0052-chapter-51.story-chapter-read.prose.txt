An Awakened's True Self, Everyday Husk and War Husk will all gradually recover after injuries. Healers can accelerate this process and ensure that it goes smoothly, otherwise the body heals exactly as well as an Unawakened would.

With the help of a Healer these repairs can be accelerated and not require active use of the Husk or True Self while healing. The Energy-based repairs can proceed passively however they do require one hour per day in the form for the process to continue. Skipping this time will slow healing, while spending too long will cause the benefits to disperse before healing is completed.

Truly severe injuries may require follow-up attention from a Healer, or even full-time use of the form despite the pain and suffering it may cause.

They left the warehouse almost an hour later. Seb had been ready to leave only a few minutes after George left them alone but Anya refused. First she'd had to go and settle up their fee for the dive, she insisted on paying them their due even though they had not been in the company's territory. Then she had arranged that the Staging Point pattern be forwarded to Holding Syl as soon as an exploration team could be sent to obtain it and verify their claim, and that access to the area be sealed off until further notice. Finally she had come back to tell him she'd loaded the car and that she'd be driving them home.

To an outside observer the drive home was spent in silence. They hadn't said a single word or even looked at one another the whole way. As far as they were concerned they spent the whole trip together. She spent a portion of it complaining about how little she enjoyed driving, an equal portion of it telling him in no uncertain terms that he was not to drive no matter how he felt. The rest of the ride however? They spent it in faint smiles and mental reassurances that all was well.

The next few days were an enjoyable exercise in patience. Anya fussed over him tirelessly, reminding him to stretch, refusing to let him cook, sitting next to him and watching in concern as he spent his hour in his War Husk and suffered through the pain. More than once he felt her start to stabilize him when his breath caught, ready to catch the slightest change in his injuries and keep him from backsliding until they could make it to a healer. The fact that he was clearly improving made her efforts sweet, the fact that neither of them were happy with the progress his True Self was making was entertaining. A year ago he would have been amazed by how quickly he was improving from what had clearly been severe internal bleeding, but after their words when he had woken up in the clinic? Every day it took to heal was an eternity.

Yshe had confirmed on the second day that a Staging Point Access would be usable within a week even if she had to slap it on the nearest wall. Skall's Depth Administration had sent over the pattern and confirmation that yes, unfortunately, it was a brand new area of the depths and that they would seal it of as per Holding Syl's request. They'd spent a few hours scrolling through the EDN trying to find answers as to how they might finally develop some more useful techniques, and what George meant when she said they should talk to a Seer. Despite Seb's experience searching for nuggets of knowledge in a sea of data neither of them made heads nor tails in finding anything they were looking for. It was as though the EDN was designed to only show you the most mainstream and common information unless you knew the exact queries and efforts to go through to find the important data.

They'd decided to wait for her before entering, it was going to be her space after all.

He'd made it as clear as he could that he was fine and that if he felt otherwise she would be the first to know. He knew part of her behaviour was revenge for how he had prevented her from doing anything while injured, she'd said so herself the first time he tried to help in the kitchen.

"This is too much." Phelann made her way through the room, trailing fingertips across the built in shelving and coffee machine before making her way to the desk and simply staring.

Is it? he asked Anya, not wanting to comment without context.

You saw the Seneschal's office in Holding Lys, and the Seer's closet where they recorded your Energy Signature, she answered, her tone heavy with surprise.

EDN feeds flickered up from the desk then vanished, two for visitors and two for the occupant. Phelann started to sink into her chair only jump back up and take a good look at it.

Seb, sit, Anya sent as he wandered towards the coffee machine.

I love you Anya. I'm not going to fall apart. I'm fine.

I'm getting better, I'll be better soon. A few more days. Then no more having to be careful how we touch.
