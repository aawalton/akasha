Awakened Vaults are a matter of Energy or Energy-infused materials. Condensed Energy is used as storage. Energy Relics are tangible items that can be converted into and reconstituted from Energy Patterns. This makes Vaults a perfect storage medium, able to be accessed based on Energy Pattern Records without needing to physically transfer materials.

An Inheritance Vault is a Vault set in place for a child who must be granted access by her parents or godparents.
