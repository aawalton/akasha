Ashton dropped through the ceiling, moving through a few different floors. It seemed like some people saw him and were startled or stunned, and some even drew a weapon instinctively, but he disappeared before they could do anything.

Plus, he was just smiling and waving at whoever noticed him, so they calmed down. People here seemed to have gotten used to Ashton at this point in time.

He soon got to the floor with Sam's office and practically skipped inside, moving through the door. But Sam was nowhere to be seen.

"Oh? Guys, do you know where Sam went?" he asked, stepping up to Jacky and Paul who were squatting in front of the boxes, sorting through them.

"Ashton? When'd you–" Jacky turned around, surprised. She stopped speaking the moment she looked at him, seeing the translucent, colour-less figure of Ashton in front of her. "... What the actual fuck, Ashton?"

"Hm?"

"Did you die upstairs, or something? Are you trying to haunt Sam?" Jacky asked with a groan. That was a much more casual reaction than what Ashton expected, though he was glad that she wasn't making a big deal about it. Well, Paul was doing that for both of them, just completely stunned the entire time.

"Nah, I'm just astral projecting, all good. But where'd he go? I want to go scare him, Sam hates ghosts," Ashton explained with a happy smile on his face. Just imagining his best friend jumping like that would be great. Was that... normal? For a moment, Ashton thought that that might not be the healthiest thing to do in a friendship, but on the other hand, these sorts of small pranks were pretty normal between them.

Plus, Sam would most likely come by a ghost in the future, so he might as well be mentally prepared for that.

"Uhm... I think he's downstairs, checking on the progress on the field? He said something like that, I think," Jacky explained, slowly turning back to the boxes.

"Coolio, thank you very much," Ashton smiled as he stepped forward through the window. He floated through the air, searching for Sam. The way things were right now, Ashton could actually see the world at a distance. There was still no colour, and only shape, but still; it was pretty great.

It looked like a few people spotted him floating there, but Ashton quickly moved away and toward the field, soon floating in front of his best friend.

"Okay, and the crops with the monster-fertilizer are still growing well? There are no issues?" Sam asked, flipping through some notes that had seemingly been handed to him.

"They're lookin' pretty good, yeah. Maybe better than the ones with the normal fertilizer we picked up."

Ashton silently nodded. Of course that was the case. Monster corpses were stock-full with mana, and when the scavenger insects took them apart, their excrement basically created a pretty decent fertilizer on its own and filtered the mana into the soil even more effectively.

Monsters weren't the only way to get that much mana into the soil, but it was probably one of the easiest. Crushing up magic stones and mixing them into the soil was a good idea as well, but it wasn't actually as efficient as the corpses those stones came from.

"And with the speed that everything's growing at, can we support everyone here?" Sam asked, the most important thing right now. They could subsidize things pretty well right now with things scavenged from stores, but those would run out sooner or later. They had to be self-sufficient by then. The farmer raised his hand to his neck anxiously.

"Maybe? But even then, we're probably cutting it close. We'd definitely need to expand a lot more to be safe, especially if we keep taking on more folk like we have been," the farmer explained. "But we do have a big issue right now, and if we don't solve that, we'll be fucked beyond belief, mate."

"Hm?" Sam let out surprised. Even Ashton was actually pretty curious to hear what he was about to say, since he wasn't quite sure what the problem was.

"The fucking pests. We've been doin' our best to keep 'em away, but there's tons of little fucks that won't leave the plants alone. Fist-sized bugs, snails the size of yer yead; they ain't monsters or anything, or at least not the aggressive kind, but they're going to ruin our crops. The insecticides that've been picked up for us ain't keeping 'em away, either."

"Ah..." Ashton let out, surprised. He put his hand in front of his mouth, not wanting to be caught.

But it was too late. Sam and the farmer already looked up, seeing Ashton floating upside-down above them.

Sam flinched and dropped the notes before stumbling backward. He was barely able to catch himself. "What the actual fuck, man?!"

With a click of his tongue, Ashton crossed his arms. "Fuck. Ruined it."

"Wait, Ash, what's going on? Why are you... you're not dead, right? You're not like a ghost?"

"Nah," Ashton flipped right-side-up and groaned. "Astral projection. But anyway, you. Is it really that bad? Don't farmers and gardeners have ways to keep pests away?"

"We do?" the farmer asked, startled to be spoken to by what, despite his denial, was obviously a ghost.

"Wait, don't you? Shouldn't you guys have like a... plant health skill, or something?"

The farmer's eyes focused on something in front of him.

"My skills are 'Planting', 'Harvesting', and, 'Farmer's Eye'. The first two ain't useful for that, but the Farmer's Eye makes like, stuff I'm looking for glow. Seeds that didn't germinate, blemishes, diseases, or somethin' like pests that are actively attacking the plants," the farmer explained. "There ain't anything that lets me actually stop the pests."

Ashton sat cross-legged in the air. "Huh. That's weird. I could swear there was a skill like that. Though, I guess, I only really met farmers after they've chosen a path, or gotten a class for synergy." He thought about it for a moment. "Who's the highest-level farmer here?"

"That'd be me, sir ghost," the farmer nodded.

"... Dude, we've met. I'm Ashton. This is some magic stuff, I'm not a ghost." The farmer nodded in agreement, though it was clear he didn't really get it. Ashton sighed. "Whatever. How long do you think it'll be until you reach level 10?"

"Uhm... maybe another couple days? When the next batch can be harvested, and I can do that and then re-plant more. That should let all of us level up, I think a couple of us might reach level 10, actually," the farmer explained.

"Good. About time. Just keep track of what sort of paths everyone gets, and discuss it with the other farmers and gardeners so that you don't all go down the same exact route. I'm sure that at least some of you should get a good skill to help against the pests," Ashton replied. "But I'll also work on some potions that can help against pests, or help plant growth and health in general. Been working on some of those anyway."

"Thank you, sir ghost!" the farmer exclaimed with fast nods, clearly happy to hear all of this, and Ashton just let out a long sigh while turning back to Sam.

"Fucking ruined my prank. You couldn't have just acted like I wasn't there, huh?"

"Fuck off," Sam flipped his friend off as he walked away. Ashton grumbled and let mana flow back into the world fragment. The moment he did, Ashton could feel his astral body be pulled back to the location of his physical body, like he was being strung along by a rope wrapped around his waist. It wasn't the most comfortable experience, but it was all pretty fast so he couldn't complain.

He stood up and stretched slightly. His body felt fine, and so did the flow of his mana. There really didn't seem to be any issues with this item.

"Perfect. Fucking perfect," Ashton said with a broad grin. This was an item that would completely change the way he could act in the future. Just scouting out different areas would be so much easier. Sure, there were people that could detect him; if Millie was able to sense him just with her innate ability, then people with specific detection skills should be able to do so as well while he was 'spying' on an area.

Plus, his astral body was basically his soul, so if there was someone that could not only detect him but potentially attack him, that would be insanely bad for him.

Well, either way, Ashton doubted that there was anyone that could do something of the sort right now. Maybe amongst certain types of magic-casting monsters, but even then, that would be rare at the moment. Later on, it would be a lot more risky, probably.

But this was perfect for finding some monsters that were a good match for Alice's contract. And it would be amazing for the infiltration of the cathedral later.

This item was an absolute, great success. Though, he did wonder... at the end of the day, this was still just a stone. A crystal. Something that could be shaped and further attuned.

What if Ashton used this to make an item out of?
