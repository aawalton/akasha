Neither the crowd above nor the warriors below had expected so many contenders to be culled within a blood-splattered handful of heartbeats.

Edge’s explosive counter, Mel’s catastrophic combo, and the gladiators’ astute ambush on the fighters clustered in the center of the ring had exacted a devastating toll. When the last body hit the floor, seven warriors remained: three intact teams and a lone spearman whose partner had fallen during the ferocious melee.

They formed a loose circle around the fighting floor, catching their breath and steeling their resolve before the Duos’ Clash arrived at its adrenaline-drenched finale. A fleeting moment of calm ensued as the contestants sized each other up—the eye of the storm in the hurricane of violence that was oh so inevitably coming.

Edge took stock of his situation while getting ready to make his move, combining everything he’d witnessed thus far with the wealth of information provided by his skill senses, which were sharper than ever after evolving to stage three.

Mel’s reservoir was down to the dregs, but Rejuvenation had erased her injuries, and Second Wind kept her stamina from running in the red. She signaled that she had enough energy for a single Titan’s Axe. The guardswoman was saving her mana for a finishing move and would watch his back in the meantime.

Melenia had put on an amazing performance, and now it was his time to shine. He still had plenty of magicytes sloshing in his tank thanks to Skill Bond’s efficiency upgrade. With its reduced cooldown, using Overdrive was back on the table, and first place felt so close that he could reach out and grab it.

That being said, winning the fight wasn’t going to be a walk in the park. Every gladiator still standing was an arena veteran—consummate warriors with countless tricks up their sleeves. Most were near the peak of stage two, with synergistic skillsets geared toward defeating human opponents.

One of the pairs included a mountain of a man carrying a tower shield that could have doubled as a castle door. The massive slab of steel was six inches thick and modified to enhance its resilience across the board. Combined with the potent blend of defensive skills the gladiator was packing, Edge doubted he could dish out anything that would penetrate that shield, and anyone who bore the brunt of its bulk was in for a bad time.

The shield bearer was paired with an archer whose arrows were bolstered by a range of spells. He’d seen shots split into multiple missiles midflight, adjust their trajectory to remain on target, and punch straight through high-grade armor. And something tells me she still has more surprises in store.

The next duo was a woman who could sprout plants from the seeds filling her belt pouches. They didn’t need soil and grew to maturity in the blink of an eye. Each species was a different type of living weapon, and her magic could control them freely. A mesh of wrist-thick vines formed a perimeter around her position, and thorny stalks protected her flanks, weaving back and forth like a squadron of serpents. A third variant was covered in colorful buds, but it wasn’t doing anything, as far as he could tell.

Her teammate was a summoner who manifested minions formed from living shadows. Their inky profiles bore the forms of hideous monsters and beasts. The constructs’ bodies were highly malleable, and his instincts told him they would be fearsome opponents. There were already a half-dozen on the battlefield, and Edge had a hunch that the man had revealed only a fraction of his full strength.

The final fighter was a ritually scarred warrior whose partner had been taken out by Titan’s Axe. He was glaring at Melenia with vengeance gleaming in his eyes, and Edge intended to deal with him first. The gladiator was holding a heavy, perfectly round shield. It had a bladed band running along the border, which he suspected could be used as a projectile in a pinch.

The furious fellow was wielding a spear whose shaft had been heavily modified. It could change shape in the blink of an eye, and Edge had watched it extend thirty feet to land a killing blow. The unusual weapon was incredibly flexible, bending to reach over enemy shields or target unarmored areas.

“The archer and the summoner are the biggest threats,” Mel whispered, “although that oversized shield looks like trouble too. I doubt anything will get through the big bastard’s guard unless you close the distance, so we should deal with that duo last.”

“I’d like to eliminate the gladiator who’s staring daggers at you first, but that archer isn’t going to wait for an invitation. Her partner doesn’t seem to have any long-distance attacks, though there’s a chance he’s holding one in reserve.”

That was all the time for reflection they had before the combatants exploded into motion and the Duos’ Cup arrived at its gore-studded climax.

Edge and Mel sprinted along the arena’s wall, heading away from the summoner and toward the lone spearman. He had a bad feeling that after Melenia’s cataclysmic combo, the gladiators would try to eliminate his team first. Sure enough, a barrage of ranged attacks came flying their way—mostly from the archer, though the summoner’s team unleashed a volley of elemental projectiles as well, revealing they weren’t limited to a single strategy.

Foebinder executed a beautiful series of blocks, batting a half-dozen missiles out of the air while Magebane severed the mana holding the spells together. Even so, Edge took an arrow to the shoulder and a firebolt to his legs, but it was nothing he couldn’t handle with Harden and Aegis. Before the plant and shadow specialists flooded the fighting floor with their lethal creations, the archer switched targets, firing at them from the flank and forcing them to defend.

That just left the enraged spearman charging for Mel—retribution radiating from his scowl like heat from a bonfire. Acting on instinct, Edge asked the twin chains of oblivion to guard her position… a fraction of a second before the spear shot out in a streaking blur.

Foebinder tried to block, but the incredible impact batted it out of the way. The shaft was diverted, but it bent back around, guided by a skill he wasn’t familiar with. Melenia raised her axe and got ready to Swap, but the move had caught her flat-footed, and there simply wasn't enough time.

At the last possible second, Magebane was there.

The right chain caught the spear’s point between its links and pulled hard, driving the tip into the ground. It was weak against physical attacks, and the impact shattered the manifestation, taking Magebane out of commission until it had a day to recover.

Edge conveyed his heartfelt thanks through their bond, firing water bullets, Vacuum Blasts, and Glitter bombs in a flurry to prevent the warrior from striking again. He opened his mouth and issued an Intimidating Roar, flaring every power at his command while his avatars amplified their output.

The spearman staggered when the aural attack broke over him, grimacing when he spotted the lethal barrage headed his way. He pushed through his fear with an effort of will, swerving and blocking and spinning. In a remarkable display of dexterity, he evaded every attack as explosions erupted on all sides.

Edge was just getting started.

He intensified his relentless bombardment, casting spells in time with his pounding pulse as he crossed the final few feet. Before the fighter had time to recover, Foebinder flung the scapula shield, knocking the gladiator off balance before looping it around his torso and gripping it tightly, pinning his arms to his sides.

Edge channeled flaming mana into Opulence with Elemental Blade, causing the weapon to burst into flame. The instant he was in range, he thrust while activating Devastating Discharge, causing the glaive to erupt in a pillar of blaze as it struck with meteoric force.

The warrior never had a chance. He was eliminated in an instant, charcoaled remains soaring across the arena to land in a sizzling heap, leaving six gladiators contending for the throne. His fight with the spearman lasted less than a minute, but the battlefield had changed dramatically in the interim.

“By the reaper’s blade,” Melenia cursed as her eyes went wide. “This isn’t a brawl anymore—it’s a siege.” Edge couldn’t fault her assessment. He had brought two allies to the party courtesy of Manifest Chain, but the summoner’s duo had hundreds.

An army of shadow beasts and grasping vines filled one side of the fighting floor, crashing into the shield-bearing giant like waves breaking against a boulder. The warrior was a fearsome foe, but the ravenous tide was rising. Edge watched in awe as dozens of ink-black serpents, tigers, and jobos converged in a tsunami of talon and fang.

He didn’t see how anyone could endure that many attacks, but the towering gladiator had no intention of receiving the minions’ charge. While his ridiculously girthy shield was impervious armor, it was a devastating weapon too. A pair of shadow tigers were obliterated when he took his first swing, maneuvering the multi-ton slab of steel with astonishing speed.

The warrior continued his rotation, using the momentum to spin as he extended his right fist. When his arm came around, Edge realized it was encased in a gauntlet so thick it was almost spherical. It struck with the force of a battering ram. A brutal backhand annihilated a leaping jobo, and when the fighter completed his next turn, he grabbed his shield with both hands and ignited his core.

Edge thought a high-rank Skill Bash was coming, but the giant’s magic was even more impressive. In addition to multiplying the force of the slam, the skill injected earth mana into the stone below his boots. A portion of the impetus came along for the ride, causing stone spikes to erupt from the ground with incredible force. The magic spread in a cone from the shield-bearer’s position, impaling a score of summons and shredding a phalanx of tendrils along the way.

The beefy bastard was one hell of a brawler, but that didn’t mean he would prevail. His mana-costly moves were impressive and incredibly effective, but there were hundreds of constructs headed his way. A river of plants was spilling across the battlefield, and the summoner was manifesting more minions with every second that passed. He must have a limit on how many shadows he can control at once, but that’s one of the best skills I’ve ever seen.

Instead of focusing her efforts on their ever-growing army, the archer was trying to take out the construct-conjuring duo before the situation spiraled out of control. She was firing missiles at a ridiculous rate, supplementing the arrows in her quiver with projectiles forged from mana granted material form.

Some of the shafts replicated midflight, creating clusters with a spread like a shotgun. Others swerved at impossible angles, as if gravity and momentum were suggestions rather than natural laws. A handful of arrows released thunderous cracks as they shattered the sound barrier, prioritizing speed and penetration over coverage and clever tricks.

The plant-manipulator and shadow summoner worked in perfect harmony to endure the archer’s relentless fusillade. Her attacks were versatile, numerous, and incredibly lethal, but not a single shot made it through. Every time an arrow came near the opposing duo, one of their creations flung itself into the missile’s path, sacrificing itself to neutralize the threat.

Edge watched in astonishment as a wall of vines reached out to repel a volley of cluster attacks. After absorbing the shots, the living curtain split around its creator and formed a barrier behind her back, catching a shot that had flown high and curved around a bare second before it buried itself between her shoulder blades.

A supersonic arrow tore the vines apart, but the shadow summoner stepped up to the plate. There was one humanoid construct mixed in with the beasts, and Edge sensed that the gladiator was manipulating his own shadow to create the effect. The shadow man reached out to block the streaking shaft with its bare hands, causing the arrow to disintegrate on contact.

The thorn-coated stalks were flinging their payloads to harass the archer, firing high so the barbs fell from above. The shield bearer was doing his best to block them, but he couldn’t turn away from the advancing army every time, and dozens of thorns bit into the archer’s flesh as the brutal battle wore on.

The only variable Edge couldn’t figure out was the flowers. The beautiful buds were almost as numerous as the grasping vines and their barb-chucking cousins. They’re gradually growing bigger, but I have no idea what purpose they serve.

His assessment only took a half-dozen heartbeats, but that was long enough for both teams to notice he’d defeated the spearman. Although they were focused on eliminating each other, they had no intention of giving his duo free rein. A mixed contingent of vines and shadows had broken off from the main group and was headed his way, and the archer started firing occasional shots in his direction.

He worked with Foebinder to block the missiles, slashing through vines while Melenia dealt with the shadow beasts. Edge sent some Vacuum Blasts the other way whenever he spotted an opening, sending his opponents scrambling to evade attacks coming from multiple sides. They were holding their own for now, but he couldn’t come up with a plan to take out either duo without exposing himself in the process, creating a three-way stalemate while the audience roared their approval.

That was when the situation shifted in the blink of an eye. The shield bearer and archer were in danger of being overrun when the giant made his move. He used his spike slam combo twice in succession, destroying every construct within twenty feet while raising two jagged, waist-high walls from the stone floor. The warrior arced his shield over his head to block attacks from above as his partner fell into formation behind him.

Both gladiators took off at a dead sprint, heading away from the summoned army and toward Edge’s side of the fighting floor. “I’m sick of fighting grass and shadow puppets!” the warrior roared. “Chain main, I challenge you to a duel while our partners hold back the swarm. The loser helps the winner defeat the other duo, then surrenders.”

Edge wasn’t sure if it was a smart play, but he was eager to test himself against another worthy opponent and decided to go with the flow. “Why not?” he yelled. “I accept!”

The four of them pulled back to the arena wall. Mel and the archer spread out to give the men some room. The women went to work dealing with the summons, as the vines were slow and would take a few minutes to reach this position.

Foebinder had dropped its monster-bone shield. It gleefully accepted the custom polearm Edge retrieved from his vault—thrilled to put Beelo’s training to the test before thousands of screaming spectators. He grinned at the bloodthirst flowing through their bond, his own hunger for carnage rising as he got ready to make his move.

It made him realize that when the stakes weren’t life and death, he’d come to enjoy the thrill of combat, particularly when the spectacle took place in front of a cheering crowd. It made him feel alive in a way he’d never experienced before, revealing a facet of himself he’d never imagined existed until arriving in World’s End.

“Belver.” The hulking gladiator nodded his head and assumed his fighting stance. “Thank you for accepting my offer. I was starting to get bored.”

“Edge.” He flourished Opulence and bowed. “I know exactly what you mean. Let’s give them a show they will never forget.”
