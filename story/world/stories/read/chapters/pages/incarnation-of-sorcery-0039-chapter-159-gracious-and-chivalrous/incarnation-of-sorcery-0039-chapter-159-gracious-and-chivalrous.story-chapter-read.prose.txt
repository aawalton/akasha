Ryland pulled out Chivalrous from within Gracious’s own body. He didn’t let either Order mage, no matter how much they might be screaming, cast any spells. Not while he was on the case.

[Endless Evolution] was still active, so he just needed to cast the correct transformation. “[Limitless Reach].”

His arm morphed again. Its spectral form changed, the glowing bones and sinew replaced with a sinuous limb constructed entirely from compressed red energy. Ryland thrust out his arm, sending the limb and its burden flying with the speed of a cannon blast straight towards the distant ground.

Ryland’s goal had been to separate Chivalrous from Gracious, and he was successful in that regard. He couldn’t land an actual blow on the second Order Mage, however.

“[Open Canvas],” Gracious the First growled.

Immediately, the area where Ryland had been hoping to slam Chivalrous into suddenly turned liquid and watery. Reality itself turned into a slow waterfall, one that erupted with a huge splash of riotous, liquid colours as he thrust Chivalrous into the mess.

“[Repaint Realm],” said Gracious the Second.

The problem was that Ryland wasn’t just facing one mage. It was two mages stuck in one body, two people casting spells simultaneously that he had to deal with. And that was with Chivalrous on top to add an element of surprise. Ryland might have removed that element temporarily, but it wouldn’t last.

Especially not with Gracious being an annoying pest all the time.

Their current spell had recreated that last magic which had turned the world into a painting—[Closed Canvas], from what Ryland remembered it was called—but just at a much smaller scale. One where Ryland was immediately struck by multiple different kinds of magic from all around.

“[Absolute Null],” he incanted.

It all disappeared. Every single bit of magic aimed at Ryland dissolved into sparkling motes of nothing. Even the world around him became normal again. It was no longer a painting.

“[Imprisoned Land],” Ryland cast next.

Rifts popped up. One appeared right behind Gracious, pulling their smudged body straight into the rift from which the strongest gravity field in all the Realms emanated. But it wasn’t the only one. At the same exact time, Ryland had incanted a second rift far beneath, spreading out wide to capture both the first rift and its trapped occupant as well.

Since this had another expanse of the same gravity as the first, it pulled the first rift straight down. Gracious was now trapped in a sandwich of impossible gravity.

Unsurprisingly, that didn’t hold for long. Chivalrous emerged from that waterfall of paint, looking like a watercolour monstrosity, and then burped.

Gracious emerged out of Chivalrous’s expanding maw with a groan of disgust.

“Oh, how I hate that sensation every single time,” he said, scowling up at Ryland instead of the mage whose maw he had just popped out of. “You’ll pay for your transgressions, traitor!”

While they might have annoyingly returned far quicker than he’d have preferred, he had granted himself a good deal of time. Enough to prepare the next slew of spells he needed to end this once and for all.

Because both Order mages had popped out into a dark world enclosed by [Void Seal].

Just to ensure it worked as Ryland intended, he cast another spell to go with it. “[Temporal Lock].”

His Soul Glyph of Time flared, making sure that his targets’ timestreams were now conjoined with that of the space bounded by [Void Seal]. Now, both Chivalrous and Gracious were trapped within the city-wide space Ryland had created.

Even using a Wound Corridor wasn’t going to help.

Admittedly, Ryland wasn’t actually feeling annoyed. There was a whole different kind of feeling when it came to taking on powerful mages. Overcoming the challenges they presented, fighting off not just their magic but at the tricky little ways they sought to use it, was often enough to keep him a little on his toes. Metaphorically speaking.

By this time, both Chivalrous and Gracious were trying to break free. They had already discovered the terrifying truth that their Wound Corridors no longer had any effect. A series of other spells was crashing into the nothingness doorway keeping them trapped.

“If we can’t break out,” said Gracious the First.

“Then we’ll barge right through on our own!” said Gracious the Second.

They did just that. Their smudged body turned into a blur as they charged straight at the black boundary of rifts maintained by [Void Seal].

Ryland simply floated there and watched as Gracious slammed right through the dark rifts in space. The Order mage entered through one location, then simply popped out of another at the exact opposite end.

Gracious looked mightily annoyed. Then they turned to Chivalrous. “What are you waiting for, you glutton. Go eat the rifts already!”

Ryland blinked. He hadn’t quite anticipated that.

In truth, his fascination was warring with his intention to just take care of the mess right here and now. But the former impulse won the day. Ryland actually just took a moment to check just how Chivalrous could actually “eat” the magic of [Void Seal].

For this wasn’t any typical, tangible magic. Ryland’s rifts were specifically doorways. A medium of nothingness between different Realms, allowing easy passage from one to the other.

This was exacerbated when they were all connected together to form one vaguely spherical enclosed space as [Void Seal] normally created. There was no longer even the crackle of golden energy bounding the rifts. That boundary might have been tangible enough to latch onto for the purpose of consumption, but it didn’t exist now.

And yet, Chivalrous succeeded in his endeavour. Ryland’s eyebrows actually rose at the sight.

The Order mage opened his wide maw and bit out a chunk of the rifts keeping things closed. Ryland saw how. Just before his teeth sank into [Void Seal], the rifts gained a slightly different colour and consistency. To most people, the shift would have been imperceptible. But Ryland had centuries of familiarity with his rifts. He knew everything about them.

No surprise that he also knew when they were tampered with.

Sure enough, a turn of his head revealed how Gracious the First was muttering a spell under his breath. A spell circle was turning his rift into something else an infinitesimal moment before Chivalrous chomped down.

How sly. Gracious could have escaped by themselves already, had they just used their spell to turn the rift into a painting and turn it into an exit. Instead, they wanted it to look like Chivalrous had broken through Ryland’s magic, which meant Ryland would focus on the big, gluttonous Order mage while Gracious made their exit. Commendably sneaky. If only Ryland hadn’t caught on to it.

Both Chivalrous and Gracious escaped, shooting through the opening since Ryland was apparently content to just let them go. They didn’t stop to consider that there might be something wrong, too busy and too tempted by the seeming taste of freedom they got.

The Order mages stopped as soon as they went outside [Void Seal], though. For beyond it, there was another [Void Seal], concentrically enclosing the first.

“We’ve had enough fun,” Ryland said. He raised a hand, dispelling the first [Void Seal]. “It’s time we ended this little game. [Universal Collapse].”

The world did indeed start to collapse. Ryland’s spell fell upon them all with a fury, drawing the bounds of the [Void Seal] tighter around them with increasing speed.

Both mages had already started casting their magic yet again. Chivalrous with his consumption magic and Gracious with his repainting powers. But this time, Ryland was actively modifying the Subglyphs on [Void Seal] to ensure that none of the rifts were broken, eaten, or were otherwise bypassed one way or another.

Ryland himself disappeared once he saw both his opponents were stuck inside. He could rest assured that they were more or less dealt with now.

Looking on from the outside of a collapsing [Void Seal] was certainly interesting. Huge expanses of reality destroyed and reformed, the glimmering black exterior shifting and reforming as the collection of rifts tried to condense to a singular point regardless of what was inside.

Then the collapse stopped. In fact, a moment later, it started expanding again.

Ryland frowned. What in the world was going on inside for something like that to occur? He decided to peek in with [Omniscience].

He had to admit to a reluctant admiration for his opponents’ ingenuity at what he saw within the formerly collapsing rifts. Chivalrous had opened his maw wide once again, swallowing down the rift. Since [Universal Collapse] would try to cover all space within the rift and crush it, Chivalrous’s little trick was creating a bit of a paradox.

The Order mage’s stomach was expanding rapidly, creating more space for the rifts’ boundaries to cover in an effort to collapse them. But Chivalrous was faster than Ryland’s spell. This meant that, instead of closing down and crushing the space within, it was actually growing.

Ryland sighed. He was once again fighting off his impulses of fascination to focus on ending the threat the Order presented for good.

But just as he raised his hand, Gracious spoke first.

“Wait, wait!” the First said. “We’ve seen the error of our ways. We want to negotiate.”

“I don’t negotiate with inhuman monsters,” Ryland said calmly. “But tell me—what exactly have you learned? I highly doubt you’ve suddenly learned that your endeavours to resurrect a Calamity are entirely heinous and need to stop immediately.”

“Well, no, of course not. That’s preposterous. We’ve just learned there’s no further point in continuing this battle.”

Ryland tutted, done with the stalling. “[Dragongod’s—”

“Wait, wait! You said you’d spare us if we told you what you wanted to hear!”

Ryland paused. “Go on, then.”

“How do we have any guarantee that you’ll keep your word?”

“You don’t. You must simply trust me. I will either eradicate you now, or eradicate you later. Your choice.”

“Gah! Fine. Here’s your titbit. This world isn’t as free of Living Mana as you think.”

Ryland considered that statement for a while. He had known that the Demiurge’s bragging that Rhel was rid of Living Mana thanks to his policies and the efforts of the Rhellians had been a bit far-fetched. It was natural to assume there was Living Mana out there, even if it wasn’t in as dangerous a state as it tended to be on other Realms like Vyrd.

But why was Gracious speaking of it with such pointed significance? Was it real, or was it a mere distraction?

“Worthless,” Ryland said. “But at least you tried. I will give you five seconds to cast whatever spell you wish and take whatever preparations are necessary.”

“Curses!” Gracious cursed.

They began casting their spells. Magic burgeoned forth, creating ripples of power that threatened to make [Universal Collapse] simply shatter apart, reversing the effect of Ryland’s spell thanks to the paradox that Chivalrous had created.

But Ryland was ready with his own series of spells.

“[Oneself World],” he incanted to create a bubble of stable atmosphere around himself. “[Translocate].”

The subsequent rift created a rent straight into the cosmos. Its vacuum dragged them all out into its expansive embrace, ripping them away from Rhel. Placing them in the crosshairs of what Ryland intended to do next.

For the Order mages’ part, they had both cast powerful, multilayered barriers, glinting with indomitable energy. Spell circles were compressed upon it with blinding intensity. There were so many there, it was difficult for even him to read.

Ryland wasn’t daunted. His magic came alive, his Incarnation form able to take note of the exact Conceptual Glyphs he needed to manipulate. So, he latched onto the Glyphs of Tangibility giving them form and reversed them all, opening the pathways for his magic to reach his targets.

And Ryland did it just as he finished casting the spell he had started earlier. “[Dragongod’s Endbreath].”

A maw that could have bitten off a quarter of the Rhellian moon floating not that far away from their current destination burst out of a rift large enough to cover an ocean. Twisted bone and rotten sinew cracked and splintered as the planet-scale draconic jaws opened wide.

What came forth next was a stream of light, heat, and sheer obliteration that could rival exploding stars.

The universe turned a dizzying, blinding pure white, glowing with enough intensity to momentarily overtake the sun in Rhel’s atmosphere. Ryland’s gargantuan energy blast crashed into his targets with impossible fury and power.

It was so much energy being blasted out in that instant that even [Void Seal] was broken. Ryland was certain both Order mages were screaming out, though he could hear nothing in the emptiness of the cosmos surrounding them. Especially not when that titanic beam of power was taking them straight to the massive blue moon.

Ryland fully expected it to impact the satellite’s body with enough force to shatter it entirely. Of course, he had no intention of destroying Rhel’s main planetary satellite. So, he just created another rift with [Translocate]—

A shield appeared around the enormous blue rock floating through space. It was enormous enough to cover the entirety of the moon’s surface, a sea of vaguely familiar golden panels barricading Rhel’s satellite. Ryland forgot all about his rift for the colossal beam from the Dragongod he had called upon collided with an impact that broke the light already flooding out.

[Omniscience] was amazing for stopping his eyes from getting vapourized. Ryland was left wondering just how much more intense would the explosion have been if he had been on an actual planet with an actual atmosphere to intensify the fallout even further. As it was, he was still buffeted by the series of massive shockwaves, his barriers actually straining a bit to contain the impact.

He supposed it was his own magic more or less bouncing back at him, so it wasn’t really that alarming.

What was more interesting was the actual shield that had stopped his magic from striking the moon. What he couldn’t get over was the fact that it was magic he knew. Magic he had seen before.

And just to hammer home that point, the barrier spell’s caster appeared before long.

“Take it easy, Ryland,” Mavar said, his voice magically echoing across the space despite the lack of a breathable atmosphere. “I didn’t give you free rein to destroy entire moons.”

“I would have stopped it in time,” Ryland said. He put no extra effort into his words. Mavar would hear, as was proven by the shift in his look. “I had a rift ready to go before I saw that you had a shield up. A very powerful shield. To protect a simple moon.”

“Do you have no idea how many deleterious effects we’d have to suffer if you destroyed the moon?”

“I’m aware.” Ryland squinted just a bit, ensuring that Mavar understood his glance was sharp and missed nothing. “But it’s rare for a moon of all things to be so well-protected. Almost as if you believed it would be attacked by someone or something strong enough to harm it.”

Mavar was only silent for a moment. A single point in time when he decided that the truth wasn’t worth it. “Be that as it may, you’ve done enough for the time being. You have succeeded in your endeavour. The mages from the Flourinus Order have been eradicated. We have no further business here.”

He wasn’t wrong. Even as he said it, Ryland received the notification proving Mavar was right.

[ Achievement Recognized: One Mage Army

You’ve defeated two Empyrean-ranked opponents despite being merely Titan-ranked.

Rank up from Titan [19] to Titan [31].

Reward: Glyph of Void [Tier 25]. ]

An intriguing Glyph, but Ryland set it aside to deal with his current situation. Though, there wasn’t much to deal with. It was a simple impasse with the Demiurge himself standing before him, weirdly protective of his strange moon.

Ryland finally sighed and nodded. “Our business on Rhel has ended. I still can’t fathom what you were truly aiming for all this while, Mavar, but I wasn’t prepared for you to give up everything you’ve built for centuries. Not without something to gain first.”

The Demiurge apparently decided it was best not to answer Ryland just then. Clearly, the probes wouldn’t help if he refused to cooperate.

So, for the time being, Ryland just cast another [Translocate] and left.
