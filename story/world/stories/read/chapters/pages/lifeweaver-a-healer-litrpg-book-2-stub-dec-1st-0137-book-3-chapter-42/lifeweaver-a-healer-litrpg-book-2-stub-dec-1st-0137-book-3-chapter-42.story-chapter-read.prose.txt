Luke couldn't believe his eyes. "Lee?"

One of the shadowed shapes separated from the others, and Luke could hear him swallow hard, even from a distance. "Lifeweaver?"

It was the Torturer from back in the dungeon where they unearthed The Deep Dweller, the one who'd attacked Luke. It hadn't ended well for him then, and it would end no better now.

Luke approached. Though unafraid, he made sure to check the stores of healing mana squirreled away inside his Meta-Heart. They were full. "What are you trying to do here?"

"Fuck off!" the one at the front called out. A big guy with buzzed hair and face tattoos, Luke saw when the light from a passing spell flew over the top of the buildings. Once that was gone, the street fell into darkness again, since the power was out in what appeared to be a city-wide outage.

"You're messing with the entrance to my guild, so I don't think I will fuck off."

"Chella, this is the guy I told you about. The Lifeweaver," Lee said, backing away. At least one in that group had found some bit of sense lying about somewhere.

"You're such a little bitch, Lee. If you don't stop right there, I'll beat the shit out of you once I'm done here."

Lee swallowed hard and looked from the one he'd named Chella and Luke, as if trying to figure out who he was most afraid of. The moon was out, casting everything in shadows, but at least it wasn't pitch black. Seeing a person was a good start when attempting diplomacy, after all. Not that Luke was in a diplomatic mood. Nor did he need light to see by, not with Weaver's Perception showing him the location of all five of them, as well as their relative strength. That wasn't all the skill showed him.

"The short one in the back has a heart condition, and the girl has cancer," Luke said, keeping his voice calm. "What if I heal you and then you go on your merry way?"

"C-cancer?" a feminine voice rang out from behind the first line of brutes.

"Yup," Luke said.

Challa brought up a thin but muscular arm and pointed straight at Luke's chest. "Don't listen to this asshole. We're Vikings, for fuck's sake! When we die, we'll end up in Valhalla!"

"...What?" Luke asked.

"We've come to plunder!" Challa shrieked, throwing himself at Luke, his other arm now raised over his head, an axe poised to chop down through his skull.

The attack was quicker than Luke anticipated, but lacked grace and finesse. Stepping aside, Luke threw a hook into the asshole's jaw, but found it was tough, almost like he had struck stone. Rather than cry about his wrecked knuckles, Luke used the thread of mana he'd extended and pulled Challa forward and off balance. A moment later, most of the group in front of him was in attack-mode, but it didn't matter.

Three wisps of thin color streamed out from each of them, one blue, one red, and one white, as Sam and Mas pulled all the fight out of each of them. Priyah held Challa while Savannah loomed over the others, wearing her mecha-like armor of see-through material she'd assembled with nothing but her mind.

"Was coming out for smoke," Sam said, her eyes glowing a little with the three colors she was in the process of draining from her victims. Mas's eyes matched his sister’s, but, like always, he didn't say a word.

"Hey," Luke said, noting that Lee was gone. He'd slunk away the moment Challa made his move.

"Evenin'," Savannah said.

The two in the group who hadn't attempted to join the fight stood off to the side, trying to be invisible. Once Sam and Mas had drained what they deemed a sufficient amount of health, mana, and stamina from the group, they sent them on their merry way, all except for Challa.

"What were you doing to the plaque?" Luke asked the woman with cancer. She was young, perhaps not even out of her teens, he saw now that she stood in the dim light of the Conduits' eyes.

"He," she said, pointing at Challa, who was still held by Priyah, "got paid to destroy it."

"Who paid you?" Savannah asked, her tone of voice showing just how displeased she was that they'd decided to accept the job.

"I don't know."

Luke believed her and turned to the guy. Older. Balding. Ugly and afraid. "What about you? Do you know?"

"C-can you heal me?"

"If you tell me."

"I'll tell you everything I know, but you have to heal me first."

Luke took a step toward him and must've struck a menacing figure, the hood of his robe pulled up in preparation for the fight. It made it difficult for anyone to get a good look at him.

"Tell me now."

The middle-aged guy flinched and raised his palms in surrender. "Fine, fine. Take it easy, geez. It was some man in a suit, you know the type."

"No," Luke said. "I don't."

"An agent. Some kind of agent, I'm sure. I don't know anything more than that."

"Agent?" Priyah asked, straining a little to keep Challa under control.

Luke reached into the man with a thread of mana and took care of the issue. It was nothing more than a minor systolic heart murmur. The guy could have gone through his life without it being a problem, but he didn't know that.

The guy whimpered as he ran off, leaving Challa and the young woman. Luke turned to her, thinking their Viking ring leader could wait for a moment longer. Priyah had him well in hand.

"What's your name?" Luke asked her.

"Ana," she said, her voice a whisper.

"Do you know anything else about this agent who paid you to mess with my guild, Ana?"

"I don't."

Luke raised an eyebrow and took a step toward her, realizing a little too late that she didn't see his face, which might make him come across as a little more intimidating than intended. The way she flinched back helped him come to that conclusion. Lowering the hood, he tried again. "Is there nothing you can tell me?"

"I swear! Didn't even come along and meet the guy! It was just Challa and Nils."

Savannah snorted. "Nils? What kind of name is that?"

"Why are you with them, Ana?" Luke asked. "Are you an asshole?"

Ana relaxed a little when Luke lowered the hood, and it seemed like she wouldn't be on the receiving end of any retaliation for what they'd attempted. When she spoke again, her voice was a little less cowed. "I'm an asshole sometimes, I guess. Not like him," she pointed at Challa, "but sometimes. A girl has to eat."

Priyah: "We could invite her to the guild."

Luke: "No. Milla said I wasn't allowed to pick up more strays."

"It was a guild," Ana said without having to be prompted again.

"What?" Luke asked.

"I could see you messaging each other about what to do with me. Maybe you want to rescue me, yeah? Like I'm some damsel in distress? I'm not. Challa is a butthole Viking cosplayer with a violence fetish, but I can take care of myself. If you'll just heal me, I'll be on my way. That little weasel Lee said you can actually do that sort of shit, no?"

"I don't care about any of that," Luke said. "What was that about a guild?"

She shrugged. "Challa mentioned it."

Challa strained under Priyah's hold, as if he were redoubling his efforts to free himself. It was futile.

"If you want me to heal you, you'd better start," Luke said, growing tired of her attitude. While Nils's ailment hadn't been such a huge deal, her own was no joke.

"That's what I'm doing, isn't it?" Ana said. "I don't know anything else."

"Ever heard of the name 'Phoenix Phalanx'?"

Ana shook her head. "No. Don't know any guilds."

Priyah: "She needs our help. Can't you see she's putting on a brave face?"

Sam and Mas had grown bored, had their smoke, and then left without saying a word. Savannah had stayed behind, shifting her weight from huge mecha leg to huge mecha leg. "We could beat her up a little."

"You're going to beat up a cancer patient?" Ana asked.

"You're not a cancer patient," Savannah argued. "You're just a cancer person."

"What about Challa? Does he know the name of the guild that hired you?"

Ana nodded. "I'd guess so. They offered to let us join them."

Luke turned to face Challah. "Priyah. Make him stand up and look at me."

Rage filled the big guy's face when it came into view. So uninspiring. Luke sighed, told Priyah to close her eyes, and then got to work. He didn't like doing it, but he wouldn't spare someone who held back information Luke needed. If a guild was after them despite everything that was going on in the city, the country, and the world, he needed to know more about them because they were obviously idiots who needed a good talking to.

Challa was not strong-willed and broke almost right away when he realized there was no other way out for him. Once Luke got what he wanted, he instructed Priyah to let him go. The Viking limped away, throwing fearful glances over his shoulder. All rage had bled away from him.

Ana's face was white when Luke turned his attention back to her. "Luminous Lark? You never heard that guild name before?"

She shook her head in denial.

"You sure?"

She nodded.

"Fine," Luke said, extending a thread of mana that entered the young woman's mana channels through her arm. From there, it wasn't difficult to clear away the cancerous nodes in her chest, as well as the satellite nodes, which had spread throughout her body. It took time, but it wasn't anything groundbreaking, nothing he hadn't done before.

Being bored when curing cancer in less than half an hour was a little strange. Luke saw that. Of course he did. It was just that so many impossible things had been happening lately that the enormity of what he'd just done didn't strike him like it once had. Having healed so many people, fixed them, and cured their diseases and injuries, wielding his Lifeweaver powers had become second nature to him. The woman needed healing, so he healed her. Of course he did. Luke would have done the same even if she hadn't helped them. Wouldn't he?

When he finished the process, Luke withdrew. "Done. No more cancer."

"Just like that?" Ana asked, raising a black eyebrow so high it almost met her bleach-blonde fringe.

Once Sam and Mas left, it grew more difficult to see, but Luke's eyes had grown accustomed to the low light of the street.

"Luminous Lark?" Savannah said, disgust in her voice. "Why is a guild full of nerds coming after us? The Band hasn't even existed for twenty-four hours."

Luke ignored her and answered Ana's question instead. "Yes, you're no longer dying of breast cancer. Congrats."

She gave him a long, unreadable look before nodding again. "Thank you."

"You're welcome," he said, turning to walk away. He still hadn't found enough enchantment materials, after all. "Priyah. What you do with her is your business. I'm sure Milla can put her to work if you decide to bring her in."
