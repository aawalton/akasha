Xavier hovered in the void.

A hundred holes in reality surrounded them. Each hole led to another universe—another version of the seven-hundred-and-fiftieth floor, where an S Grade True Dragon waited, frozen outside the most powerful time dilation field Xavier had ever wielded.

Around him, ninety-nine other versions of him were enveloped by that time dilation field. They flapped their wings, turned their heads, and took a moment to examine the holes in reality and everything that had happened since fifty of them had gifted their own version of Galadsea’s energy to Xavier.

Those fifty versions of him were clearly in a lot of pain. Their energy channels would be quite damaged, though not as damaged as Xavier’s had just been. As their minds were all open to one another, Xavier consulted Roln about an idea. The mental construct nodded along at Xavier’s words. The dead Wanderer already knew Xavier’s thoughts—already knew what he was getting at.

It wasn’t merely the Xaviers who were connected by the psychic link—it was the mental constructs, too. Roln was able to share every single one of the refinements he’d just given Xavier to help heal his energy channels to the other Rolns that were in the other Xaviers’ heads.

This allowed the fifty versions still healing their energy channels to heal them far, far more rapidly than would have been otherwise possible.

Xavier blinked, watching in awe after that information had just been shared. The wheels in his mind rapidly turned, and he wondered just how powerful this new connection between all of them could make them if they could now share things like this…

Though they didn’t strictly need to wait for those fifty versions of him to heal, caution dictated that it was a good idea to do so.

Several more hours passed inside the time dilation field. The only thing to do while they waited was either interact with one another. The psychic link made it incredibly easy to transfer their thoughts from one to the other, and one of them suggested they should create a shared mental space. Xavier wasn’t even sure who’d suggested it—it almost felt like they all came up with the idea at the same time. He supposed he wouldn’t have been surprised if they had.

The way they were all connected felt like some strange sort of hivemind, but a hivemind that didn’t have any one controller. They were interacting with one another on a level he hadn’t realised was even possible before.

Each Xavier sent a single part of their mind into the mental space. They ended up creating a mental space that looked identical to the tavern at the bottom of the Tower of Champions. The conversations ranged on all sorts of different topics, and they weren’t the only ones there. Roln materialised. All of the Rolns.

At the same time, outside the mental space all one hundred of them were acting as though they were one.

When the fifty versions of them healed, it was time to initiate the next phase of their plan.

The last fifty versions of Xavier—including Xavier himself—each created a single bone arrow. This bone arrow had 10 of the remaining 28 bits of energy that had been gifted to them inside it. Xavier still had the energy at his disposal, as he’d only placed the energy gifted from the other versions of him into his time dilation field.

Creating these bone arrows required each of them to recover. Time passed, but it didn’t matter how much to any of them, as nothing was moving forward outside the field.

When they were ready, each still wielding the bone bows they’d made, they nocked the arrows. Roln had assured Xavier that he should easily be able to loose a physical object through a hole in reality from the void to a universe. A Void Being wouldn’t be able to do such a thing, as they were nothing but spirits, but a living being?

It would be easy enough.

Time to test that theory.

Xavier steadied his breathing as he pulled back the string on his bone bow at the exact same time as the other forty-nine versions of him did. He was worried about this part. Worried that the S Grade True Dragon would be able to push the attack away just as it had let all that destructive energy from the explosion of the Empire of Servitude’s armada flow right past it.

But this was a very different attack.

The S Grade True Dragon had stumped him, multiple times, but Xavier hadn’t been thinking outside the box enough. It wasn’t until that bastard of a thing had created its own time distortion that Xavier realised he needed to think bigger.

That he could no longer hold back.

There were unimaginable powers at his disposal, powers that gave him unlimited possibilities. All he needed to do was think up the right plan, and he would be able to achieve anything.

He was quite sure of that. Now, he just needed to prove it.

His thoughts made a sliver of confidence build up within him. He wasn’t the only version of him feeling that confidence. In fact, they were all feeling it, and with their combined psychic connection focusing on it, Xavier suddenly felt like he couldn’t lose.

He released a breath at the same time as he released the arrow. The forty-nine other versions of him released their arrows at the same time.

They had all targeted the same S Grade True Dragon—the first S Grade True Dragon, the one that resided in the version of the seven-hundred-and-fiftieth floor of the tower that Xavier was connected to.

This was an important part of the plan.

The arrows didn’t move. Not until Xavier extended the time dilation field he had created. So confident he was that this would work, he didn’t bother creating a secondary time dilation field to test this with.

The arrows soared through the hole in reality, aligned so perfectly that none of them interfered with the other as they flew.

When they made it into the universe, Xavier sighed a breath of relief. But he knew they weren’t in the clear yet.

Xavier edged the time dilation field closer and closer, until it finally touched the dragon.

The S Grade True Dragon didn’t know what hit it.

All fifty bone arrows slammed into it at once, hitting the exact same spot on the top of its head.

The arrows were not rebuffed.

The destructive energy that had been placed within pocket spaces inside the arrow heads exploded. All that energy pummelled straight through the defensive energy that surged up from deep within the S Grade True Dragon. It punched a hole through that energy, through the dragon’s scales.

Through its skull.

Then straight through its brain.

For a moment, Xavier worried when a kill notification didn’t come. Then he remembered where he was. The System had trouble reaching into the void, so while he resided there that kill notification might never come.

But he didn’t need the kill notification to know that the dragon was dead. He could see it, clearly, with his Immortal Soul lens. There wasn’t even the tiniest bit of energy remaining inside that thing.

Relief on a level he’d never felt filled his mind—he realised it was the combined relief from all one hundred versions of him. That relief was dwarfed by the elation that came a split second after.

He’d done it!

No—they’d done it.

The S Grade True Dragon had been defeated!

Well, one of them had been, anyway. There were still ninety-nine S Grade True Dragons they needed to kill, and they couldn’t combine their powers enough to kill all of them.

Fortunately, they didn’t have to.

Xavier looked over at all the other versions of himself. Every single one of them had a great big grin on their faces. Once again, Xavier Collins had done something everyone told him should be impossible—even Roln, who knew he’d done the impossible before had told him to run what felt like a million times.

That would have been the logical choice. But it hadn’t been the right one.

He just hovered there in the void for a little while, contemplating their victory. The next step of the plan had to be executed by him—the original him—for this to all work out. He cracked his neck, stretched out his wings, and released another long sigh of relief.

Then, he summoned the Spirit of Time again. That six-month cooldown meant nothing when he could use Pocket Time Stream to connect to whatever time stream he wished. That was how he’d managed to accomplish all of this in the first place. The other versions of him wouldn’t have been able to summon the Spirit of Time otherwise.

The beautiful Otherworldly spirit appeared in a flash of brilliant light. The woman looked around with a raised eyebrow then gave a small shake of her head. You… did it.

Surprised?

Once upon a time, I would have been. But I think it would have been more surprising had you died, considering just how far you’ve made it.

Xavier inclined his head.

It was time to make another unfolding—a lot more unfoldings. He flapped his wings idly, doing some math, wondering just how many he would need. Perhaps nineteen hundred more unfoldings would be enough. Though technically he would only be creating only one unfolding. In fact, they all would.

Could you create another unfolding? he prompted the Spirit of Time.

Anhelina smiled. As you wish, Xavier Collins.
