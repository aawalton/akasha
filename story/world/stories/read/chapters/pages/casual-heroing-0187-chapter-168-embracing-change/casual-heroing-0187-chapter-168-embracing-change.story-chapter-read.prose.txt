Flaminia watched with a smile on her face. The [Mage] had been out of her element at the beginning, showing a complete lack of culinary sense and skill. But there had been an eagerness and a willingness to adapt that had brought them closer than even the gossip they had talked about on their breaks. She could see how careful Lucinda was about not upsetting the patterns of the magic flowing from her to the steak—Flaminia had learned much about discipline during these thirteen days too, and she had been handsomely rewarded for it.

Lucinda had learned from Flaminia the nuances of cuisine and how closely they resembled the nuances of magic; Flaminia, on the other hand, had learned just how talented the redhead was and how to tame her own ego and listen to someone who had never spent any meaningful time in the kitchen.

Their work was a well-choreographed dance, a silent conversation. Lucinda's hesitations were met with Flaminia's bold actions, and Flaminia's uncertainties were steadied by Lucinda's measured precision.

Yet, below this synchronicity, a subtle tension lingered. The work they had performed had unearthed something that had been buried deep inside their heart of hearts—a certain reluctance marred them both. After all, they had mixed magic and cooking, venturing into uncharted territories.

What would she do if she didn't get anything interesting from level 40? Something she had worked her entire life to achieve?
