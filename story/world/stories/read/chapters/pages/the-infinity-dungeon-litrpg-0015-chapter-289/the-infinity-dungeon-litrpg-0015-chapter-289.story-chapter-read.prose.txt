Chapter 289

Michael’s demand hung in the air, the words like solid chains seeking to bind the man in front of him. He waited patiently, feeling Icarus’s grip on his arm tighten as the little elf was beginning to lose their patience.

“My cross?” the pope asked, echoing the question.

Michael nodded patiently. “I know it’s a Faith artifact, your holiness. You can’t hide such things from me.”

“It’s powerless!” the man argued. “De Ponti used all of the Faith–”

“It’s not, and you know it. Faith is gathering again already. I guess having more than a billion believers does that. Don’t play games with me, pope Damasus.”

The man froze, his whole face immobile save for a slight twitch at the right corner of his mouth that he could not manage to get under control. With mechanical movements, he lifted the thin golden chain from his neck, revealing a thick cross made of pure gold.

Michael touched it, saying nothing for several seconds as a frown slowly grew on his face.

“Ah, this would explain it. Interestingly enough, I can’t use it,” he said.

The pope was not surprised. “You are not of the Church.”

“I guess the Don couldn’t either, then.”

“You are correct.”

Michael chuckled. “And even without it, he managed to cause so many problems. Imagine if he had magic on top of everything else. This makes me wonder how he even came to know the existence of these things.”

He snapped his fingers, and one of the shield’s hexagons became transparent. The mirror was now fully visible, glowing with reflected magic and ominous PAC energy that turned the air around it into a thick miasma.

“You see those shards? If I try to touch them, I get instant PAC corruption. Anyone else just dies and turns into a devil. If I try to move them, they explode. What you will do now is take one of them out of the shield.”

The pope swallowed. “Did you not just say that if I touch it, I die?”

“Well,” Michael shrugged. “Someone is using the shards alright. The way they worked has changed after the Don’s death, and I have a hunch. Take one. I will not repeat myself again.”

Despite that, the pope still tried to argue. “Are you sure?”

“Take it.”

He finally did as asked. Plunging a hand inside the shield, he retrieved one of the shards and took it out. For some reason, the shard was still emitting ludicrous amounts of PAC energy, making Michael doubt his hunch for a moment.

But then he noticed that the pope was not screaming, convulsing or otherwise dying in horrible ways.

A pity… Icarus whined in his mind.

Michael hummed in thought. On a whim, he withdrew his Domain from a small area around the pope, and suddenly the shard calmed down. There were still traces of PAC energy coming from it, but only barely detectable. The same did not happen if he withdrew the domain from the other shards, meaning that the pope had to be touching it for it to calm down.

“Try to hide it using your Faith,” Michael ordered.

As the pope concentrated, the shard seemed to vanish from all senses except sight. Michael grinned.

“There, now I can’t detect it. This confirms it, only Faith users can safely wield and hide a shard.”

“Can I put it back now?” the pope asked. His voice was shaking.

“No. Slash the veil.”

His eyes went wide as saucers.

“Don’t worry,” Michael said. “We are in one of my strongest Domains, it’s safe. Try to think about Faith devices as you do.”

The pope gingerly lifted the shard up until it was almost level with his face. By now, his movements were completely mechanical, lacking any sign that he was not an automaton as his mind had retreated to total apathy to protect itself. With a quick, jerking motion, he slashed down and the air rippled and tore apart.

Michael watched the veil, carefully observing any change. A thin, surgical cut appeared, and immediately PAC energy rushed to widen it, ripping it apart and damaging it even further.

Through the opening, they saw a room full of riches and gold coins. A rush of magically-rich air followed, and then the portal snapped shut.

Was it me or did that place smell like the dungeon? Michael thought.

It did! Icarus replied. Was it our dungeon, or was it another world’s dungeon?

The portal suddenly collapsing had left the pope rather shaken. It made sense, because while Michael could speed up his mind using the infrastructure of the Origin System, anyone else would have seen the opening appear and vanish in a blink of an eye.

“You are clearly doing something wrong,” he said. “Well, didn’t we talk about sacrifice? What does the shard want?”

“Are you sure–”

“Dude, stop questioning everything and just concentrate. What does the shard demand?”

“...souls.”

Michael grinned. “So they do exist! Nice. Can’t give them to it, but try with this gold magical coin.”

They tried several times, and each time Michael increased the amount of coins he offered to the shard. After a few tries, the pope managed to get the hang of it, no longer needing to slash the air to tell if the sacrifice would be accepted. It was a good thing, because the veil was tearing apart the more they tried.

Eventually, with more than two dozen platinum coins hovering in the air, held there by Michael’s magic, the shard finally accepted the sacrifice. The aperture into the strange mountain of riches was stable.

Next to them, the containment shield around the mirror brightened.

“Fuck, is that a dragon?” Michael exclaimed.

The creature was enormous, and its magical signature was enough to obliterate him. Before it could be roused from its sleep, Michael plunged a hand in the portal and retrieved a handful of stuff. Gold, jewels, strange artefacts and a single cross-shaped object.

“Close it,” he commanded.

The pope did not need to be told twice. As the portal collapsed, the shard released some PAC energy that quickly began to rush toward it. Michael quickly intercepted it with his spiral, and restored his Domain after the pope put the shard back under containment.

Clapping his hands, Michael began pacing around the room. “Well well well, we have some interesting data. Even though it seems like the shard can be tamed and bargained with, it’s been modified so that it sneakily releases PAC energy with each use. It’s not a coincidence that the biggest release of PAC coincides with an action that weakens the veil.”

The pope was not listening. Instead, he was staring at the containment shield next to them, utterly transfixed.

“Some of the PAC even entered your body!” Michael said chipperly.

This got a reaction from the pope. “Can you… can you cleanse it?”

“Lower your defenses. And done,” Michael said.

“Thank you. What of the shield? Will it hold?”

“It should,” Michael said casually. “For now.”

Another fifty percent increase, he thought gravely, none of the casual tone of his real-world conversation remaining. A steep price to pay to perform an experiment.

Did you notice that the shard the pope was holding did not change its PAC output? Icarus supplied. It only did when you put it back, matching the others.

It’s probably a safeguard, Michael replied. Made not to kill the users until they have done their job of weakening the veil. Now, one last thing.

“So, for the last trick,” he said. “I will show you what happens when a weakened veil and PAC energy interact. After that, you will be ready to accept the Origin System as your new religion, pope Damasus. Of that I am certain.”

Michael’s Domain suddenly withdrew. The PAC energy still lingering in the air rushed to the weakened veil, no longer held together by the Domain. A clawed hand swiped at the air, tearing a hole in spacetime that led to the terrifying sight of the Netherworld. It lasted mere moments, and then the Domain snapped back in place, closing the portal and severing the claw.

Michael picked it up, and burned it with his spiral. “It’s the third time this has happened. They must be getting pissed.”

The pope was shaking.

“This is what’s happening to the world, pope Damasus, in no small part because of your actions and choices. Now you will help me fix this fucking mess.”

“How?”

“Let me present you, your new god. The Origin System. Accept it into your heart, and be changed by the light of redemption.”

The change took place in mere moments. A new form of energy flowed into the System, but there would be time for that later. The question that burned in Michael’s mind was another, and he made the pope try to touch a shard again. This time, even though the shard did not kill him outright, he could not tame it and corruption spread quickly. The System ran on Michael’s Domain, which the shard simply rejected, no matter how much the pope tried to tame it with his faith.

Good, Michael thought. Anyone with the Origin System will be unable to use the shards without alerting everyone.

Or turning into a devil, Icarus added.

But they will do it loudly.
