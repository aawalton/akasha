With simple hand-signals that they agreed on beforehand, Ashton had Clementine follow him. She was already moving close to silently because of the Silent Step Shoelaces that Ashton had given to her, but those only worked to a certain extent.

They were able to erase the sound that your footsteps made directly, but if you stepped on some fallen leaves or some sticks, that would still make a sound just the same. Similarly, there were other things about Clementine that still made sounds. Her breathing, or her sliding against something with her arm; all of those things made sounds, and she wasn't exactly trained to control her breathing in that way.

She did have decent breath control, being a runner and all, but the focus there wasn't silence.

Ashton was doing his best to counter-balance the sounds she made. By controlling the sylph, it was possible to disrupt soundwaves to some degree. Dulling out vibrations coursing through the air like that was relatively simple. Worst-case, Ashton would figure out something with the silence spell. It wasn't mobile so it was something he could, at best, use in a real emergency, but that was fine. It was okay to take it slow. It was more important to not get caught, and to not draw the attention of and involve the people entrapped into this cult.

They scaled the shabby, low wall erected around the cathedral. It wasn't exactly hard.

"... How the hell are they keeping monsters out?" Ashton whispered under his breath. There really hadn't been anything nearby that should be able to stop them, nor did god-kin have any particular ability to counteract the system to stop the attacks of monsters. But there were some possible explanations.

One, they had so many children here that there was a pre-emptive protective zone here as well, similar to the primary school, though that seemed unlikely.

Another possibility would be a powerful magic caster creating a perception-blocking barrier around the entire camp, which obviously wasn't the case. Even Ashton wouldn't be able to do something like that yet.

That left just one possibility at the moment, and it was probably the most annoying one.

A constellation was helping out here. A lot of constellations, particularly the more powerful ones, were allowed to affect things like this to some degree by basically spending their power of 'influence' like some kind of currency.

They couldn't make monsters not hostile directly, but they could create certain safe zones on top of already existing ones.

But... already? This seemed already early. So, either, they were dealing with a particularly powerful constellation, or... multiple constellations that linked their powers together.

Somehow, a bad premonition came to Ashton as he was unable to hold back from forcing its way into his mind. He really hoped that it wasn't the case, but those guys had been silent for a while anyway, so it was possible.

As Ashton and Clementine climbed down the other side of the wall, Ashton used his tie to spread his perception into the veil. Things weren't completely visible to him the way they were now, but he was able to at least sense the veil beyond his channelling range. While you couldn't generally see people in the veil unless they had particularly powerful magic, there were still ripples of magic subtly influencing the veil.

It usually wasn't exactly clear whether it was just the natural flow of mana in the world making that happen, or if it was people, monsters, or animals, but there were some patterns you could see in the movement to be able to tell things apart.

... It was better than being fully blind, at least. Especially when you mixed it with your other senses, and Ashton's existing sixth sense, it did increase his options a little.

But then, Ashton sensed something. Not too far from here, toward the entrance of the cathedral. There was someone with a lot of energy standing there. They were staring right this way.

Clementine opened her mouth slightly, but Ashton immediately moved and pressed a hand onto it before she could make a sound.

Without hesitation, Ashton grabbed the wisp as it floated underneath his cloak. Though it was subtle, the wisp still gave off too much light to let them hide in the dark veil of the night, so it had been hiding away within the cloak, which was able to block the light out perfectly well, and practically seemed to fully absorb each and every ray into itself.

This cloak was made to hide contracted beings within its confines, after all, so that made sense.

Ashton quickly wiped away the spell within the wisp, and placed something else within it. It was the 'silence' spell. Usually, it was a stationary spell that couldn't be moved, but maybe it was different in this case.

The moment he decided to activate the spell now housed inside of the elemental, Ashton noticed a small field of silence forming around himself and Clementine.

"Don't move, don't touch anything. Your father is there, looking this way," Ashton stated, though he wasn't sure why that was the case. The two of them should be perfectly hidden behind some bushes right now. And if Ashton could barely sense Jonah, then it had to be impossible the other way around for now, right?

"Wait, what? Does he know we're here?" Clementine asked, startled. Ashton shook his head.

"No, I don't think he does."

"Are you sure? You don't think your outfit is a bit conspicuous for sneaking around at night? What if he saw you climb over?"

Ashton silently stared at his friend. "I once infiltrated a spies' guild while I was actively glowing."

"... But that was back when you were stronger, right?"

Ashton sighed. "Yes, fine. But no, I don't think he's looking at us, just at something in this direction."

He quickly focused, thinking of a map of the town. The way Jonah was staring was... the place where the wyvern should be nesting right now.

Ashton felt the pit in his stomach grow. Another attempt at helping the wyvern grow stronger? This would end tonight, no matter what. It had to.

Ashton and Clementine continued to sit there for a while, trying their best to hide and not be spotted. There weren't able to move away from this spot right now, either, since this area was quite open outside of these bushes. If Ashton had contracted a tenebrun already, then he'd be able to cloak himself and Clementine in darkness and move around much more freely, but that sadly wasn't the case yet. The ink to contract a tenebrun was a little annoying to make, same as with a lumen, and other more complex elemental beings, even if they were still lesser beings.

It took a whole twenty minutes for Jonah to turn back around and move back into the cathedral. He had been releasing some magic for a while now, and it had been moving like it was being siphoned away to somewhere else.

A regular god-kin would never just give their magic to something else, but... maybe it was different for Jonah, since he still wasn't a full god-kin. Or, there was some other even more annoying plan at play.

"Okay, let's get moving. We'll move along with the silence spell," Ashton told Clementine, though she looked at him confused.

"But I thought it can't move?"

"Not normally, but in this case..." Ashton focused on the wisp, which had split in two. When the silence spell was active in one of them, the other wasn't able to move much at all. At a snail's pace, basically as fast as if someone were crawling. However, that wasn't the case for the one it wasn't active in.

So, Ashton activated the spell in one, had the other move forward a tiny bit, basically just directly to the other side of the first wisp, and then swapped through which one the spell was active in. Like that, by oscillating the spell between one wisp to the other, Ashton and Clementine could move forward completely silently, if only relatively slowly.

But right now, since they were much closer to the cathedral, it was important to be extra careful not to draw any more attention.

Ashton was trying his best to spread out his senses beyond his channelling range, even if it already took a lot of concentration to move along with the wisps like that.

However, without needing to worry about making any noise, they were able to get to the cathedral quite easily. They took a side entrance instead of the front one, moving through the hallways around the building to try and find Jonah. But when they moved around, and Ashton started being able to sense Jonah again... he was just standing there, right in front of the altar, as though he was waiting.

God-kin didn't require sleep, but Jonah wasn't a full god-kin just yet. He was going to need to rest at some point, and right now should be the best time. He should be exhausted from using so much magic during the service earlier, and especially after forcing out so much magic to be siphoned away.

But he just stood there, silently staring ahead in calm expectation.

Ashton deactivated the magic in the wisps, and slowly stepped forward, walking out of the door at the side of the nave and faced the altar.

"Ah, Ashton. There you are. I was wondering when you would finally come crawling out," Jonah said with a laugh, like he had been expecting Ashton. It might just be a bluff, but considering who was most likely helping him out, it wasn't unlikely that he was warned in some way.

"Yeah, yeah, shove it up your arse with the rest of it," Ashton waved Jonah off and turned to Clementine, who slowly followed behind him. "Your daughter's got something to say to you."
