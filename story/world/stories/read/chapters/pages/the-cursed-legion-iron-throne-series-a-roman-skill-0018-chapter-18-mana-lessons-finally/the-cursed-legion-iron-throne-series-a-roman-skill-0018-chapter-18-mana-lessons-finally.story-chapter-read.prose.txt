The Captain had them settle down after he was done pressing the need for hard work and grinding to unlock your full potential without wasting any drop within their body. It was necessity in order to keep up with those that were filthy rich and those that were incredibly lucky and talented.

Max didn’t like being the center of attention that the Captain kept pointing toward. As though the lesson did not include him and Olir.

He shook his head as he sat down next to Arnie and Caleb..

His chubby cheeked friend was giving him a massive grin.

Max smiled back and turned to look back at the speaking Captain.

“...the very essence of this world. It is what helps life bloom in even the most inhospitable places. Volcanoes, dead deserts of sand, rock, ice, and snow. Places you could not imagine. It fills everything around you down the dirt you sit on now. The only difference between magical items and others without that classification is the quantity and quality of the mana within them.”

The Captain raised his hand and balled it into a fist.

The Ledger detects a surge of Body Tempering Mana!

…

Max dismissed the notification as he stared at the Captain’s glowing hand. Flecks of white light orbited around his fist, fading into it and out seamlessly. That was the first time Max had ever seen mana that could be used offensively up close. Of course he had seen the healers and parlor tricks done by the actors, but it was nothing compared to this.

“It can be manipulated, gathered, moved, and used to strengthen, speed, recover, or fortify the body. Many of the captains have unlocked the ability to use the Body Tempering skill through years of dedication and focus. The Optio is the strongest amongst us excluding the elite knights of our lord Centurion. As for him, the lord Centurion, he is the strongest individual in our entire Cursed Legion with the ability to use his mana internally better than even the Optio, but also match the mages and Royal Praetorian Knights in external manipulation. He is a legend walking among men.”

Me having six magic means I can learn to do that too, right?

The Captain continued. “Any questions?”

Max raised his hand instantly.

“Go ahead.”

“How do we learn to use mana, internally or externally?”

“In a rush, aren’t we,” the Captain said with a smile. “That is the goal for today. I will help you unlock the Mana Gathering Skill today, the very foundation upon which you shall base your ability to manipulate the heavenly energy. After is the formation of a core to house the mana, then learning to call it forth, how to move it within your own body, and lastly how to manipulate it with the skills you will eventually unlock once you have a class at level 10. Until then, we will strengthen that foundation.”

The Captain waved him forward.

Max hesitated for a second, then he rose to his feet and wound his way between the rest of the students to stand before the captain. The man was a giant now that Max stood closer to him, not as big as the Optio, but still massive nonetheless. Max only came to his hip.

The Captain kneeled in front of him. “You asked about it first, so, I will go through the process with you first as well. Face your legion brothers and sit cross legged—”

Max did as he was told. He kept his head down to not meet any of their eyes.

This was a far more important matter that he did not want to be messed up because Arnie was making a silly face or Joel giving him a look so intense and full of hate it could have skinned him clean. Nothing was worth losing this opportunity especially with the help of the Ledger just waiting for him to use.

He could already imagine how much easier all the training would become if he only moved Recovery of the Air Gladiator up one to four percent only, much less anything better than that.

“—I will use my own mana to unlock your first mana channels. Close your eyes and follow the threads and wisps of heavenly energy as they move within your body and settle into the space where your core will eventually form. Let me know when you unlock your Mana Gathering Skill. You have sixty seconds before we will need to retry, though you will have to get back to the end of the line for another attempt. Are you ready?”

Max nodded and closed his eyes.

“I will begin now.”

Max felt the Captain touch his shoulder. Heat spread from his fingers and penetrated into his body.

The Ledger detects an external source of Mana attempting to infiltrate your body!

Warning! Warning!

Do you wish for the Ledger to rebuff the attempts?

Yes?

No?

Note that rebuffing strength is directly correlated with Ledger skill level and your personal mana reserves.

Max clicked ‘No?’ and kept focus. He could feel the heat slowly move through his body until it latched onto something he hadn’t known existed within him. A single tube or blood vessel that he assumed was the aforementioned mana channel, or one of them at least.

Sweat dropped from his face, tracking down his cheeks and dropping into his lap as he kept his full attention on the movement of the wisps of golden mana move within him through his shoulder, up and down his arm left then right arm, over his chest, and finally settling in his sternum. The five wisps locked into place in that area and began revolving around each other in a tight spiral, leaving trailing light that gave the impression a core was already there.

Max stared in awe for what felt like hours at the orbiting wisps, mesmerized by their bright luster and symmetry.

Time seemed to move in slow motion. Everything faded away into the background including Captain Hector’s lecturing voice. Nothing seemed to be worthy of breaking his attention from this moment in time and place. Those wisps were everything he had ever wanted and imagined.

“Once upon a legion’s march, stood a legend…”

He could still hear his mother’s voice as she read her bed time stories.

Mages casting spells that won entire battles.

“...waves of power descending upon the mountain whole. Feasting upon the carcass of Angothal the Dread and cleansing the world pure…”

Legends that stood before the darkness and remained firm and steadfast.

“...eyes of the bedlam bride. Legends stout and unwavering as the mad threatened their stalwart minds shattered…”

Heroes of old rising once more to fight the hordes in the bleakest days… Men and women who sacrificed their lives, knights who stood stalwart knowing that doing so meant death for them and life for others who would never remember them.

It was these tales that had shaped his earliest memories and molded who he was before the cruelty of the world took grasp of him. And now it all sat there in his chest lazily orbiting–

Skill Acquired

Mana Gathering - Level 1

Max gasped as he was ripped away from the orbiting wisps. He saw them vanish forever, leaving him empty of them.

One moment, the world, in its entirety, had been warm and perfect. The next found him sitting on hard ground, dozens of faces staring at him, a cold filling his limbs, and the Captain, with his hand holding him, still speaking his lesson as though nothing had happened.

I… I almost… No. I did unlock it.

…

Theft Tier (2) - Level 1 (2)

Battle Focus - level 3

Mana Gathering - Level 1

…

It was there on his status page and yet… Max felt empty and devoid of something vital.

“...most of you will have slightly worse success. Those with only one magic stat will have the hardest time. Those with none aren’t likely to unlock it, but the Optio will have a few of the other Captains attempt to help them. We aren’t hopeful of great success, but you never know who over reaches. Olir, much like Max here,” he patted Max’s shoulder with his heavy hand. “Ten to fifteen seconds is normal.”

Ten to fifteen seconds? It felt like I was in there for hours at the minimum. How is that even… I need to be careful if I can’t actively tell the time I end up using when I work on gathering and manipulating my mana in the Ledger. I can’t afford getting caught or worse… the Optio makes me run even more laps because I slept in.

“Up you get. Wait in your seats when done or in line. Starting from the far right. Don’t waste my time and stay focused.”

Max shook his head and got up. He walked back to where he had been seated and plopped down.

“How was it?” Arnie asked.

“It… I can’t explain it, but when I broke free, it felt like the world became darker and emptier without the mana. That.. Being in there and staring at the wisps, it was intoxicating.”

Caleb leaned closer and whispered. “Do you think we should do the same? The focusing on the wisps the entire time until the end?”

Arnei scooted into their little huddle as well.

“I’m not sure, but that was what happened with me,” Max took a moment to really consider what had happened to him with his eyes closed and doing what the Captain had told him to do. “I couldn’t take my eyes off the wisps. It made me remember distant memories and why I was who I was…”

Max gulped, remembering how vividly he had heard his mother’s voice as though she had been sitting on her chair beside him. He had heard the flip of the pages and how her voice cracked when speaking about the eyes of the bedlam bride and the Legend that stood strong before the waves of madness that threatened the empire.

He took a deep breath and closed his eyes to remaster himself.

“Everything alright?” Arnie asked.

Max nodded. “Yeah… I’ll be fine, it was just very vivid.”

Caleb cleared his throat and nodded toward the Captain.

Hector was standing up and looking toward them. “I said, Arnold. It’s your turn, boy. Hurry up and stop wasting my time. You all only have a limited amount of time with me every week. Don’t waste it whispering to each other like old women sharing gossip.”
