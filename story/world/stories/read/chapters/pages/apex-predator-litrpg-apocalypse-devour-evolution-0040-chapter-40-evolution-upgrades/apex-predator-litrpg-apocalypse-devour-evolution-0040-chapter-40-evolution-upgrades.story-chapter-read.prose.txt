It took Don a few more minutes to hunt down the remaining Grimoid hobgoblins. Since their numbers had dwindled so dramatically, he didn’t need to be so careful. He sliced through them with supreme efficiency and even managed to Devour three of them alive, resulting in extra Evolution Points as well as a level up.

[You have slain [Grimoid Hobgoblin – lvl 12] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 45 System Points earned]

[’DING!’ Race: [Evolutor (Dominator)] has reached level 12 – Stat points allocated, +3 Free Attribute Point]

Speaking of Evolution Points, he now had 3059 points. They were seriously starting to rack up. But just upgrading the Devouring Physique would cost that much. However, now was not the time for it.

Don had a gut feeling that the king would be a tough opponent. He had plenty points to spare, but he would need to crank up as much offense and defense as possible for this fight.

But when Don looked at the prices of his evolutions he was vexed. Shit was getting seriously expensive.

Devouring Physique (E-grade): 3000 Evolution PointsNight Vision (D-grade): 720 Evolution PointsPoison Resistance (F-grade): 80 Evolution PointsPsychic Sense (F-grade): 150 Evolution PointsRetractable Claws (D-grade): 2400 Evolution PointsSimian Physique (D-grade): 1200 Evolution PointsUrsine Vitality (F-grade): 250 Evolution Points

“Oh,” Don said to himself. “I’m starting to see the logic here.”

The upgrade from F to E grade doubled the initial cost. Then the price was tripled from E to D. The jump from D to C would require quadruple the previous points. It wasn’t exactly like that, but that was the general rule. The Retractable Claws had gone from an initial 60 points, straight to 200, a little bit over triple.

“I guess that’s only fair considering how many points I’ve started generating.”

But there were definitely some obvious choices here. He had completely neglected the Ursine Vitality. It was simply due to not having to fight overwhelmingly strong enemies, so the Constitution wasn’t something that Don had been prioritizing. But the king was powerful. He would need to be able to take a hit from that massive club. And besides, the Evolution seemed like it was a really good one.

The Psychic Sense was another one he was supposed to upgrade earlier, but hadn’t had enough points. It was time to bring those both up to par.

“Holy mother of Constitution,” Don muttered. “Those are some sweet stats.”

Don felt his body shifting. He wasn’t sure, but it seemed like he grew a little. Just an inch, something subtle. But he felt overall sturdier, stronger and… bigger. It was a nice feeling. His muscles felt full and ready.

750 points spent. He still had plenty to spare. Next Psychic Sense got the same treatment.

[150 Evolution Points spent]

[Evolution Upgraded!]

[Psychic sense (F-grade)] -> [Psychic sense (E-grade)]

[300 Evolution Points spent]

[Evolution Upgraded!]

[Psychic sense (E-grade)] -> [Psychic sense (D-grade)]

[Psychic Sense (D-grade)]

[Psychic sense: Your pineal gland has activated and transformed into a third eye. You are now proficient in wielding psychic energy. You have acquired the ability to resist mental effects from enemies. Upgrading this Evolution increases the potency of all Psychic abilities. +6 Mental]

Huh. Another one where the description changed quite dramatically. Now it gave him the ability to resist psychic effects and said that he was proficient with his psychic abilities.

Don had to test to see what that meant.

With his mind he picked up one of the hobgoblin spears from the floor. He was startled by the ease at which it levitated in perfect stilless. Don inhaled and then pushed. The spear flew towards the wall like the world’s longest bullet and absolutely shattered against the wall. No human could throw a spear that hard. And Don could do it with his mind. With pretty significant accuracy to boot. Don decided to push the ability.

He took two spears off the ground. This one required a bit more concentration. He launched them in tandem, pushing his hands forward. They flew towards the wall and connected, biting deep into the wood. There was a tsok-tsok and then a twang as the spears reverberated, expelling their kinetic energy.

Definitely not as much raw power, but the fact that he could deal real damage with two spears like this, meant that he had gotten a serious upgrade to his psychic abilities.

As Don approached the king’s hall, he kept picking up spears and throwing them around. Just for fun, and of course for Telekinesis practice.

As he walked, he considered his options. He still had almost two thousand points left. He really wanted to level up the claws, but they were unfortunately too expensive. So that left him with the option to pump either the Simian Physique, Ursine Vitality, or Psychic Sense.

Additionally, he also had a total of six free Attribute points from his level ups. It had been foolish to not spend his points from level 11, but he had been too absorbed with solving the castle. In a fashion this was actually better, because now he had a much better idea of what Attributes he might need against the king, than before, when he was just about to enter the castle.

Don liked having all these options. But he needed to make good choices. Don figured regardless of how he was going to spend his Evolution Points, it was safe to put the Attributes in Power and Agility. He didn’t have any specific tactics relating to his psychic abilities and the Mental stat, and the Ursine Vitality had sorted his Constitution out. So he put 3 points in Power and 3 points in Agility.

That just left him with the points. Don sat down in corridor near the kitchen area and emptied a water skin. He had been in the castle for a few hours now. Devour of course kept his Health and Energy topped up, but he needed to take a little rest, to let his mind and body relax. He needed to be at a hundred percent against the king. This was his first dungeon boss, and he didn’t know how strong they were. Morshoff seemed like it was extremely strong, and Blazeclaw, while not a dungeon boss, was an absolute unit.

Don considered his options and was disappointed that not only could he not afford the claws upgrade, he could not afford to bump up both Simian Physique and Ursine Vitality. He would have to choose one.

It was a question of whether he wanted explosive power and offensive ability, or if he wanted to be able to take a hit.

The monstrous speed at which the king had thrown his club at the kitchen door played over and over again in Don’s mind. If that had been a direct hit at him, how badly would he have been wounded?

Was it better to take the explosiveness to be able to dodge those attacks, or reduce his margin of error. If he pumped his Constitution, that could make a difference between a devastating blow and a glancing blow. However, the same logic applied to Power and Agility. If he was fast enough, no matter how powerful the boss, it wouldn’t be able to damage Don.

This was a matter of risk appetite.

In the end, Don would rather avoid damage, while also dealing more damage. So he opted for the Simian Physique.

Whatever bulk he acquired from the Ursine Vitality, was sucked in by the Simian Physique. He still felt fuller after the bear upgrades. But his muscles roped around the bone together and he felt really light on his feet. Explosive and ready, like a boxer.

And just like a fighter before his match, he was getting antsy. The nervousness was getting to him. He wanted to release it. Just go and do it.

Before he did, he took a hefty boost of confidence from looking at his stat sheet. It sure as hell was coming up nicely. Especially his Constitution had come a long way. He was confident he could tank a hit from the boss now.

Homass, King of Grimoids, rose up from his throne and grinned.

“I’ve come for the crown.”
