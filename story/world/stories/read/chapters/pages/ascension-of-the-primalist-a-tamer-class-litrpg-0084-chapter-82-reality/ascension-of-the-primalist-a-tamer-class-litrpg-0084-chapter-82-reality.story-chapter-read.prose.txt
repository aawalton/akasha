As if a single person was responsible for all this. Professor Reat, the Champions of Chaos, you… everyone seems to be fine with this outcome."

"They could pass a law saying commoners simply aren’t allowed to awaken anymore," Elena continued, pressing her point. "They could ban you all from the academy, or use guards to constrain small towns into forced labor. It can always be worse."
