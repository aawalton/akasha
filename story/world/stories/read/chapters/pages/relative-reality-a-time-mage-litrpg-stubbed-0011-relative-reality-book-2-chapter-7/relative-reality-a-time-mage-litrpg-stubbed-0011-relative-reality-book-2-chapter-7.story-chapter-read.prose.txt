Rowan took Ester by surprise, hitting her in the back of the head so hard that it interrupted the spell she was casting. As Ester stumbled, a burst of light spread out from her body, shoving Rowan back and lightly burning him. Gritting his teeth, Rowan continued on the offensive, using Relativity to keep on the healer.

Noticing that his healer was being attacked, Felir called to his team and turned around to deal with Rowan.

Felir’s team consisted of only four members, one less than Rowan’s own. With the already ramped power of Melody’s songs playing, the small advantage that Felir and his team had on Rowan’s flipped with his and Elise’s arrival.

The frontliner of Felir’s team, called Fabien if Rowan remembered correctly, that wielded sword and shield turned his attention from Arne to the commotion behind him. He raised his shield and used some kind of power to come charging at Rowan. Deftly dodging the bulldozer of a man, Rowan continued his assault on Ester. He simultaneously avoided the magic that Felir threw at him and positioned on the other side of Ester to Fabien, delivering machine precision punches into the joints and unarmoured spots of Ester’s body.

Draining mana at an unreasonable rate, Rowan felt temporarily invincible. The three members of Felir’s team couldn’t even touch him, let alone hurt him. Only Ester’s periodic waves of light managed to hurt him, but he was on top of her after each wave almost immediately.

Meanwhile, Arne was now only dealing with the greatsword wielding member of Felir’s team, Dray. Now with only one opponent, Arne was handily holding him down. Roots grabbed at his legs and the spikes of Arne's shield wrapped around his weapon and hands, while Arne repeatedly bashed him in the face with his free hand.

Elise was hidden somewhere setting up her devastating combination of curses and triggered abilities, and Helja was taking the moment of reprieve that Rowan had bought to rest and recover some mana.

Feeling his own mana supply begin to run low, Rowan disengaged from his all out assault on Ester and retreated to Arne’s side. At the same time, Dray managed to get away from Arne’s grasping roots to return to his own team, Ester desperately healing up her team to health once more.

There was a slight pause in the fight then, as the two teams each held their ground against the other. Each second brought healing, mana and stamina to Rowan’s team, so he decided to delay if he could.

“You don’t want to do this Felir, Hendrik and his team are likely on their way now. You won’t win this fight quickly, and if you leave us both exhausted, he’ll win!” he said.

Felir looked at his team, then to Melody and her two Afterimages as they restored Rowan’s entire team. He cursed.

“We’ll be back,” he said darkly, then turned and ran. His team followed in a quick retreat, in formation and on watch for Elise.

Behind, Rowan heard Helja sigh in relief.

“Good timing, they almost had us there,” Helja said.

Turning around, Rowan noticed that Helja was pretty badly injured. She had a cut from hip to shoulder, presumably at the hands of that greatsword. The wound was slowly closing up thanks to Melody’s healing.

“They jumped us nearly as soon as we got here. I don’t know how they knew we would come here, maybe they got lucky, but I think one of them must have a kind of tracking or perception we don’t know about. With Elise’s Silence stopping Melody’s songs, we barely held them off. Arne had to use his Form of the Deep to heal us.”

“Damn,” Melody said, breathing heavily, “it’s like they knew you two wouldn’t be here.”

Rowan narrowed his eyes at the retreating team, wondering if they had somehow listened in on their pre round planning.

“Well, Elise and I managed to take out one of Hendrik’s guys. Thankfully they kind of stumbled across us so we managed to get it done quickly. Things could have gone real bad, real quick if we hadn’t been back so quickly,” Rowan said.

“They really are retreating,” Elise said, appearing beside Rowan. He hadn’t even noticed she had followed Felir.

“Good. We’re at an advantage over both other teams now, so let’s plan carefully.”

Helja, who was still wincing at the gash in her torso, let out a long groan.

“Now that we are the strongest of the three teams, we should be worried about them teaming up to take us out. Elise, do you think you could take out one of Felir’s team like you did with Hendriks?”

Elise nodded.

“I’ve cursed their healer.”

“Alright. Melody, consume those Afterimages. We’re going to have to reposition.”

Melody did as requested, releasing a burst of health, mana and stamina recovery. Heja’s injury closed over, and Rowan felt refreshed.

They moved out of the courtyard together, carefully moving away from where they knew both other teams to be. Rowan felt tense, prepared to be attacked at any moment. There was a good chance that the next team to make a move would get both themselves and their target killed. Elise was the only person in the entire arena that could potentially assassinate a member out from the middle of their team and get away with it, so that was the play they would make.

With the courtyard compromised, Helja led them to a backup position. It was the second floor of a building missing its roof. There was only one way other than flight to get up to the second floor, and Arne stood guard at it. The rest of them took a moment to collect themselves.

“Should I go with Elise again?” Rowan asked.

Helja pursed her lips.

“I don’t like the idea of her going out there alone, but I don’t like the idea of being without you both again. If Felir really can track us somehow, that could be disastrous.”

“We all go then. Let Elise do her work to kill Ester, and ambush the rest of them. If we’re quick enough, we should be able to take out the whole team without Ester before Hendrik can get there.”

They all waited as Helja considered. While they were a team, and decided things as a team, the past months of training had solidified Helja’s position as their leader. If she made a decision, they would follow through on it. She had their trust.

“Alright, let’s do it. We can’t wait for them to potentially team up to take us out. Now that we’re the strongest, it’s on us to make the first move.”

Gathering together, they set out. There wasn’t a time limit on this final fight, but Rowan also had a feeling that the instructors wouldn’t allow the teams to all wait the others out into a standstill. He knew that they would eventually reveal the positions of each team to the others, but if the standstill continued after that they would surely intervene further.

Their plan was a good one. Rowan could accelerate Elise enough that she could reliably deal with Ester. One of the discoveries that they had made over their months of training however, was that Rowan could not accelerate his teammates too much. His passive, Timeless Body, apparently protected Rowan from more side effects of his own powers than he realised. If he sped up one of his allies too much, it had a deeply nauseating effect on them. Thankfully, with Elise’s stealthiness, he didn’t need to help her out too much.

They followed Elise’s directions, guided by the curse that she had placed on Ester. Tracking wasn’t a part of the curse, but there was a kind of intrinsic connection to one’s own abilities that let her know the vague direction they needed to go.

Once they got close enough, Elise closed her eyes and focused. The second part of her innate ability Soul Reap was an enhanced soul sense. It was hard to sense other souls at their rank, especially at any kind of distance, but Elise’s enhanced sense allowed her to locate Felir’s team.

Much like they had done, Felir had holed up in a dilapidated structure with good defensive positioning.

Sneaking up on the building, Elise used Silence, attaching it to herself, and then Elusive Repositioning. The team was wrapped in a field of silence, then became both fast and invisible. With the combination of powers, they snuck up on Felir’s team. They were gathered in a small group, Fabien guarding the entryway and the team behind. Arne ran full speed into Fabien at the same time that Rowan used Relativity on Elise. Fabien was sent flying and Elise slipped past Arne to sneak attack Ester. Arne pushed into the building and Rowan engaged Dray to distract. Melody began her same songs, while Helja began pulling air from Felir’s lungs.

Ester died first, falling to Elise’s dagger. Rowan once more thought about just how terrifying Elise was, but quickly focused back on the fight.

Dray was a skilled opponent, much more skilled than Rowan was, yet slow. If it came to a direct fight, Rowan would have lost quickly, but that wasn’t his goal. Rowan led the man in circles, retreating before the massive blade as he drew the man away from his team. Noticing what Rowan was doing, Dray tried to get back to Felir and Fabien. Rowan jumped forward and delivered a quick punch to the back of Dray’s knee, sending him stumbling. He followed up with a kick to the man’s side, but the attack was blocked. Rowan barely jumped out of the way of the greatsword, held in a half sword style, as Dray tried to slam the hilt into Rowan’s head.

Their little game of cat and mouse continued as Helja slowly drained the life out of Felir. Not going down easy, Felir gave as much as he got back to Helja. Unfortunately, without a healer, life was not easy for the noble. Elise joined the attack, and took Felir down with a few more strikes of her savagely efficient dagger. The final two members of Felir’s team didn’t last much longer, one falling to Helja’s wind blades and the other to Arne’s fiery axe buried deep into his back.

Elise was a precision tool, a point of leverage to pull apart their enemies from their weakest point. Felir’s team was by no means unskilled, nor were they weak. Yet with a well placed strike, Elise had turned what should have been a slog of a fight into a quick mop up of the other team.

She was also staring at Rowan, obviously having heard his thoughts.

He smiled, expressing his gratitude. Surprisingly, he got a small smile back.

“Where is Hendrik and his team?” Helja asked.

Rowan shrugged.

“Elise kept her sphere of silence up for most of the fight, maybe they didn’t hear the fight in time?”

Helja frowned.

“No, there’s something going on. The arena isn’t that large, they should have at least seen light from Ester’s light burst as she died. Even the flame of my abilities. Especially if they’re still in that building you two found them in.”

They left the building littered with the dead of Felir’s team, and went back out onto the street. Hendrik’s team was still nowhere to be seen. Full on mana, and without any further injuries, they went out in search of their enemy.

Elise’s soul sense found them exactly where she and Rowan had left them, holed up near the top of the tall intact building.

“What are they doing in there?” Rowan wondered aloud.

His eyes were drawn up as he saw Hendrik step onto the ledge of the rooftop. There was a smile on Hendrik’s face.

“That’s disconcerting,” Melody said.

“A little, yeah.”

The rest of Hendrik’s team stepped up beside him. One of them was holding the corpse of the team member Elise had killed. Hendrik held a hand over the body, and a subtle glow started to spread from his body. Then, all at once, the entire team jumped off the roof.
