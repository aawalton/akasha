Lacking Merit

“She cheated, she attacking before the match had even been called,” Herra cried out angrily, trying to point at me, with her gnarled hands, as her healing abilities slowly worked their way through her old bones.

Watching her magic at work, I thought something was odd about the process. Even with a blade in her gut, Jolia seemed to be healing herself much faster. Keira was being aided by Moira to have her arrow removed from her back and lung. Meanwhile only Icky who was still lying flat on the ground was having a worse time that Herra was currently. Looking at Icky and the look of pain on her face, I grimaced a bit, I did hit her hard, and while the blow was kind of cathartic at the time, the aftermath made me feel a twinge of guilt.

Fortunately, Pierre who seemed like he had experienced the whims of Herra for a lifetime in the Bloom household just sighed and closed his eyes for a second before replying. It was clear that this was a learned technique, as I could almost see the way he forced his body to relax, despite having his ruling be challenged right before him.

“I assure you, that Laday Catarine waited until I dropped my hands to start,” Pierre replied.

“Are you blind, she was upon us before you even said go. There is no way she could have done so had you been doing your job of maintaining proper balance with the match field,” Herra continued. At her words, I wondered what exactly she was talking about? Was there actually a way that moderators could force a restart? Or maybe even a handicap to those who started early? At the very least, her words seemed to imply this was the case. Though I don’t remember seeing any particular form of magic being used, though maybe the lack of magic was part of why this somehow made sense. The fact that there as no response by Pierre meant that there would not be any visible signs of odd magic being used.

That said, Pierre was a saint. He took the comment like a department store manager who had leveled up by dealing with over a hundred Karens in order to explain why there were no more free samples to provide. As he stood there, a look of cool dark brown calming energy washed over him. That’s when I realized it had to be a Skill of some kind that he was using. Was this some form of Meditation? Some odd Skill for practicing keeping your cool while surrounded by over entitled jerks? If so, I could see why household staff would need and be able to easily level up such a Skill. I would be losing my ever-loving shit, if Herra spoke to me in such a way. Yet, he just stood there with a calmly defiant tone.

“If you’d like, you could always challenge the perpetrator to a one-on-one duel to settle any lingering animosities,” Pierre stated, giving even more details about duels. Apparently after a team duel, individuals could still request an individual contest.

“No, I don’t need another duel to challenge a cheat, I need these results annulled,” Herra continued, and suddenly I wondered if there was some sort of tracking mechanism?

“Your requests for nullification of results have been noted, realize that should you proceed with this complaint, and are found to be in error the penalties implemented for you will be doubled,” Pierre stated. As he did, I could see a faint silvery glow coming from him and stretch out to Herra. Once the silver cord seemed to latch onto Herra, she responded with more fire and vitriol about her loss.

“Yes, I am certain she cheated, there is no way she could have gotten to us had she waited until time. She struck down Ithica before time was even called,” Herra was nearly frothing with rage at this point, while Pierre’s stoically calm demeanor seemed to state he somehow expected such an outcome.

“Very well, you are objecting to these results, and wagering your own honor and reputation in relation to these accusations?” Pierre called out, a note of inevitability filling his words as I could sense that this was somehow bigger than any of us here. For a moment I wanted to protest, to defend myself, but Pierre seemed to realize this and held up a hand to stop me. Seeing the gesture, I just bit my lip, as I felt that this was somehow going to go in a way I had not expected. Then before I could make heads or tails of anything, silver bands extended out from Pierre linking Luna, Lucia, and me in one group, with Herra, Ithica, and the others in a second group.

Then in less than a moment the silver cord glowed brighter for a moment, before winking out of existence, but not before I could feel an intense burning sensation.

“The system has judged the complaint and made its final ruling,” Pierre declared coldly, his voice seeming to lack any remorse or compassion at all. That’s when I felt it, a faint burning sensation deep in my chest, right where the silvery cord reached out and seemed to make contact with me, before pulsing and disappearing completely.

“What was that?” I asked, as I instinctively covered my chest in the exact spot where I felt the inner fire burning. Though I was apparently not alone, as I saw Herra, and the others also do the same. A glance out of my periphery showed that Lucia and Luna both had the same reaction. With that, I realized that this was likely something from the system, telling us that we had a new notification.

Seeing the look that everyone else had, I quickly pulled up my status panel and looked for any changes. That’s when I was surprised to see all the subtle changes that had somehow taken effect on me.

Name: Catarine Bloom

Age: 16

Class: (Unique) Culmen Vitae* (5/5) (Maximum Potential 7)

Magic: Life (5/7) – Class Linked Only, Holy (2/7)

Seals: (7 / 7) – Holy Sword of Slaying seal* (Heart, Left and Right feet), Divine Notes of Precision seal*(Left Hand), True Sight of Enigmas seal* (Eyes/Mind), Sigil Activation seal* (Right Hand).

Binding Oaths:Oaths Currently in Effect: Bloom Hall Honor Duel.

Bloom Hall Honor Duel: *While At Bloom Hall*: Victory results from sanctioned Team Versus Team Duel against Ithica, Keira, Moira, Jolia, and double effects against Herra.

While I had seemingly done nothing to open up my available Class slots, I had apparently maxed out my seals. Somehow, I managed to convert three seals to further empower my Holy Sword of Slaying seal*, while also having my Sigil Activation seal registered for my right hand. It took me a moment, but I realized that it was the seal that I had worked on last night, while I was clearing out main street with the magical paint and sigils. But the most glaring change was the Binding Oaths section.

I didn’t realize that the details of this duel were somehow enforceable by the system, but I guess it made sense and seemed to fit with why everyone was taking this so seriously. Even Herra’s protests likely might have had a chance to

“The system has judged, and found your objections lacking,” Pierre declared, then with that he turned and began walking away. His part in this trial of watching over two teams dueling was apparently now complete, and he no longer wanted to try leveling his Meditation Skills against Herra’s inevitable onslaught.

Everyone was shocked, Lucia and Luna both seemed to blink at the descriptions they were now reviewing their own status pages. Seeing their shocked faces, I turned to face the others, and that’s when I could almost see system force their oaths into effect as each person suddenly turned towards me and smiled nicely. Well, as nicely as some of these old crones could. Imagine a demonic fence trying to smile and that’s what some of the efforts looked like, faces strained in hard and unnatural ways that were not natural to observe at all. It was kind of frightening, but ultimately I could see how this effort even seemed to supersede their healing factors, for they were seemingly denied the ability to use any Skills while smiling. Or maybe it was that they were so lost with trying to remember what a smile was, that they couldn’t even let a Bloodline Skill continue to heal them while they tried to smile. The end result was that there was a distinct lag in healing, before their timer ended and they were once again allowed to heal themselves.

Herra seemed to have the hardest time, as she clearly had to hold that forced fake smile for a full ten count before she was released and once again allowed to use her own bloodline abilities to heal her broken hands.

Seeing that the oaths were binding, I felt both vindicated, and somewhat concerned about this additional functionality that the system seemed to allow. That said, it was nice knowing that the system seemed to track the oath, and whether it was followed or not.

Even Icky, smiled, though her smile was more of a blood-filled toothy pained smile that she only managed to offer while tilting her head up towards me from the ground. Watching her pathetic state, I found myself going over to her and using my Streamline Healing* on her. Within seconds, she managed to relax her head slightly, as bones in her shoulders and spine realigned and adjusted themselves back into place.

The process was slow, and painful as popping bones and painful grunts were slowly released from Icky, but within minutes the major injuries caused by my two strikes to her body were mostly healed and she was able to roll onto her right side, and extend her left leg that slowly twisted its way back into place. I say it was able to twist its way back into place, but only through Icky’s sheer will and determination as she was the one who seemed to understand that she needed to push the bone back into is correct position. A fact I was grateful for, as I did not want to touch her that much.

Finally, by the time I was done with Icky, I saw the other aunties who all looked visibly cowed. Going over to Keira first, I began healing her, fixing the internal injury caused by a high velocity arrow cutting and carving its way through her right kidney, missing her lung entirely and landing a bit lower than I originally expected. Still that had to be painful, and was also a good excuse for me to also use my Recuperatio Vitae* Skill to help her replace the blood she lost and would continue to pump out through her now functioning kidney.

After that, it was Jolia who was far worse, but was seemingly better at using her bloodline ability to heal herself than the others were. While her injuries were more severe, she was the one who required less overall help, though I did help her regenerate her blood, and help her body seal up and keep her intestines restrained finally.

With that, I stood up, looking for anyone else, only to see that Herra had apparently left sometime during my healing efforts, leaving only a slightly flustered Moira behind. Seeing that she was the last one left, and that she managed to be the only one who was not injured during the fight she just smiled and replied, “thank you.”

“Yes thank you,” the others all replied as they gingerly made their way off the arena floor, leaving behind just the three of us: Lucia, Luna, and me.

I nodded and was almost confused with what to do now that all the action was over. My blood still felt the high and thrill of combat, but I needed to calm down, they were weaker opponents clearly not used to real combat, or worse, veterans who still thought of themselves as being in their prime, and able to teach younger people the ropes of life, only to find their bodies were failing them. For these reasons, I tried to calm myself and realize that I had been lucky. If these were real opponents I would have had a much worse time, and likely not had nearly as much of an advantage. As it was, I was able to get a quick start, that I followed up with mercilessly, but it could have easily gone far worse, had I not been the first to make a move.
