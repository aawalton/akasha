Ben shot an arrow out of the air about three feet from a monkey.

Lowering his bow, the archer on the ground froze for a moment. The guy stared up at their floating platform they stood on with his mouth hanging open. Watching the gears turn in the man's head, Ben saw him arrive at the only logical conclusion available.

"Are you insane?"

"Extremely," Ben replied to the man's shout, though he highly doubted the guy actually heard him given how far in the air they were.

Snorting in disbelief, the archer immediately notched and fired another arrow at the horde.

Ben knocked the projectile out of the sky before it even got close with a small [Mana Cannon]. And he blasted the guy's immediate follow up shot into splinters a second later.

Taking a short breather before they came here had definitely been the right call. Ben had run his cycling technique to get some mana back into himself while completely recharging his healing mark as well. And Alice and Ethan had spent that time going around to get more people onto their cause. By the end of it, they had managed to gather like ten people.

And now they were here standing on a platform of gravity Alice had made, their plan in motion.

Ten people wasn't exactly a lot of people. But it was still more than enough to maybe stall for a few minutes.

Dozens of people down below were trying to kill the rampaging beasts as they smashed into the barrier, but Ben and his tiny crew were actively shooting down their attacks, stopping them from hitting the beasts as they lunged into the barrier.

Tracking another wave of projectiles from the angry archer, Ben swatted them away before they could land.

"Ethan. How long?" He asked.

"Depends on how fast the monsters can break through. A few minutes, perhaps. I think we only need to hold for that long."

"That's a lot of time, but we can hopefully do this."

Shifting his weight awkwardly on their invisible platform, a guy in heavy chainmail stared down at the dozens of defenders below. He was one of the people Alice recruited.

"This better be worth it. If my points drain for this…"

"They won't drain, believe me, they will climb, just watch them."

Ethan replied, not even stop deflecting projectiles, using some kind of talisman that duplicated and shot down. It was paper, but it was incredibly tough and could explode, as they smashed into the projectiles and exploded, stopping the attacks.

"You watch them! I'm watching the dozens of people down there deciding whose face to remember after this! If I'm implicated outside the rift because of this, will it even be worth it?"

Alice scoffed and shot the panicking guy a deadpan look.

"Then smile. Don't be like this, you knew what was going to happen yet you came anyway, so don't chicken out now. Give them something to remember. And check your points!"

Checking his system interface, the chainmail guy suddenly snapped his mouth shut. His score was definitely already ticking up, even though he wasn't an active participant yet.

Ben was racking in the points.

Some of the others had realized that their points had increased, and one of them was the shifty guy that Ben remembered seeing. He'd summoned a ring of rocks around himself, and started firing them off like bullets to intercept the spells flying up from below.

His job was pretty much shielding them for now, and he was doing it spectacularly.

And Ethan and Alice kept calling out reassurances to the rest of their tiny crew to keep morale high.

Ben though had been busy firing [Mana Cannon] over and over, though it was growing a bit tedious given that he could only shoot one at a time. Still, he dispersed a fireball before it could strike the rampaging beasts throwing themselves at the barrier.

But a bunch of the nobles playing defense on the ground had finally figured out that their attacks were being deliberately sabotaged from above. So they sent someone up to deal with the problem.

Riding a swirling vortex of wind, a single scout floated right up to the edge of their gravity platform.

Ben narrowed his eyes. He knew that arrogant face instantly. It was the exact same spoiled noble kid who had tried to bully him into giving up his staff, and he looked awkward, but glaring at their platform and finally seeing with detail what they were up to, the floating archer lowered his weapon slightly in sheer disbelief.

"You. I knew it'd be you. What are you doing?"

"Holding a platform," Ben replied, and the archer glared at them in complete disgust.

"You're protecting the Tide. Days we've bled keeping those things off the monolith, and you're— do you have any idea what happens to you when the rest of them realize what I'm looking at?"

Leaning casually against his wooden staff, Ben gave him a deadpan stare.

"Roughly."

"You don't! The objective says protect the monolith. It's the one thing in this rift everyone agrees on. You're not clever, you're wrong, and when this is over nobody will protect you."

Well, he wasn't wrong. The objective did say that, but doing the objective wasn't their goal. After further talking with Ethan, he'd told them some other reasons this might be beneficial, and Ben had to admit… He was tempted.

It was no longer something he had to do for Ethan, but something he wanted to do for the rewards alone. After all, didn't the system say it rewarded based on performance?

Then breaking the rift's narrative had to count for something.

Ben swatted a stray fireball out of the sky with a quick flick of his staff and sighed in annoyance, giving the archer a side eye.

"Are you going to attack or what? Please go away if you aren't."

Glaring daggers at their group, the noble looked at them all one by one.

"Be warned, nobody is going to bother coming up here just to talk again." With a seething glare, he dropped back down toward the ground.

Alice chuckled. "He is terrified of us."

Ben honestly couldn't blame the guy.

Flying up here completely alone to confront ten people on a floating gravity platform was just incredibly stupid. So logically, the noble wouldn't want to actually start a fight he couldn't win.

…but then again, Ben and his tiny crew were currently sabotaging the attacks of dozens of nobles who vastly outnumbered them.

So they really weren't the ones to talk about fighting smart.

We're all basically idiots here.

Betting on the chance Ethan was right.

Ben shrugged at Alice and turned around and went right back to blasting incoming spells out of the air.

After the archer probably told the nobles below what was going on, it basically became a game. Ben and his crew just kept raining death from above, trying to starve the nobles below of their precious monster kills.

The nobles naturally started adapting their tactics. They launched synced volleys to overwhelm Ben's defenses, and some clever mage even started throwing attacks blindly around the platform's rear side to keep them busy.

But utilizing his active [Mana Sonar], Ben tracked every single incoming threat.

Pushing his limits, he fired intercepting shots, he even started blasting multiple [Mana Cannons] from his staff at the exact same time. And naturally, doing that cranked the mental load on his [Focus] up to an agonizing degree.

He had never done that before, but it was now doable due to [Focus] evolution. He knew he hadn't been utilizing [Focus] properly, so he had thought of ways to use his skills better. Multiple mana cannons was one such thing.

And it was definitely working.

The monster hoard was growing thicker around the outer barrier. And thanks to the relentless sabotage, the nobles were visibly failing to thin the herd. So every single person down there now had a grievance against them, most likely.

Hitting a breaking point, the weakened monsters finally smashed right through the defensive barrier. So the defenders on the ground were immediately forced to start fighting the beasts in brutal, close-range combat.

Ethan tapped his weapon against the platform.

"Alright, they're distracted. Time to drop down and make sure none of them try to pull a fast one while the lines are broken."

Laughing like an absolute maniac, Cassian stepped right off the edge of the platform and plummeted straight down toward the battlefield to go pick fights with someone higher Tier.

Shaking her head, Alice quickly wrapped herself and Sera in a layer of gravity magic before gracefully floating down after him.

So Ben was the last one left on the platform. Igniting a continuous [Fire Burst] beneath his feet, he stepped off the edge and began a controlled, hovering descent as well, the platform disappearing behind him.

Ben honestly thought things were progressing pretty smoothly for once. But he had completely forgotten about one massive, highly annoying variable.

Flickering into existence right behind Ben in thin air, Theodore wrapped an arm around his neck, and flashed a wide smile.

"Leaving so soon?" Dimples widened. "Won't you tell me what you guys are doing."

Ben slammed a [Mana Cannon] into his face.
