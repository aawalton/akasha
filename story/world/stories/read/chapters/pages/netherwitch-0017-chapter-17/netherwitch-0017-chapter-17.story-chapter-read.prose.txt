Duels. Joint battles. Sylvia fought foes on the docks, this time without the advantage of ambush. She faced demons in the halls of the Academy and challenged wolves on the streets of Orasul Lunii. She died. She died. Then she died some more. Every death was a new experience. Every failure was followed by a chance to review the battle and revise her tactics. Again and again. Day after day. Week after week.

Then she opened her eyes.

With her last blank skill book, Sylvia had mastered the art of Magical Combat. She’d been holding onto the System item for weeks for this moment, waiting until the database was filled with enemies and tactics. Now, she was rewarded with a practice the System named excellent.

The wait was well worth it. One book and she was confident she could defeat two of her prior self.

Over the last year, Sylvia had dedicated five skill books to combat. The combination was: All About Wind Blade, How to Fast Cast, Waltz of Flowers, Basic Combat Magic, and finally Magical Combat. No longer was she weak and inexperienced. She was a power house.

Reminding herself of this, she checked her image in the mirror.

Reflected was a delicate, silver-haired girl with pale skin and an adorable allure. Dark fabric hugged her curves, a wide-brimmed hat resting on her head. Sylvia adjusted the accessory, admiring the snazzy crook along with the silver moon ornament hanging from the tip.

“I’m a witch,” she insisted. “Today, I’m going to kick apples and take names.”

“Don’t think about it,” she insisted. “No one will care what you look like after you’ve murdered your first few demons.”

No one would care before, either. Appearance was less important than power. This principle applied in the netherworld and in video games. Sylvia’s shoes and dress might seem impractical, but the stats didn’t lie. They were high quality pieces of equipment. She was, without question, an armed and armored badass.

Feet scrambled on a roof nearby. A dark-skinned goblin dropped over the edge. Gavin the Black had a smarmy smile, his sharp nose raised high.

“Little witch, I thought that might be you.”

Sylvia’s gaze flicked to the smaller goblin. Then her eyes rose to the West Tower. The fires were fading, but she could still see the side that had been smashed in by the explosion.

Earth Spike

Runes: 13

Mana: 40 to 100; 33%

Attack: 280 to 400

Penetration 40 to 100 pierce, 50% multiplier, 15% DRR

Max Range: 20 meters * Mysticism

Causes a flower of sharp stone to explode from the earth. The earth, in this case, being any thick and solid surface. This may include walls and ceilings, making the spell extra tricky in caves and castle hallways. The point of emergence is set in the second before earth spike erupts. The shards can twist toward the target. To dodge, the target must escape the point of origin by one to two meters.

Because the spell is easy to evade by those with ether sensitivity, it’s recommended to use earth spike in combination with other spells or after reducing the target’s mobility.

Like most earth magics, earth spike has high defense penetration. The spell is excellent against slow and armored opponents along with large, fixed assets. The mass of the stone can push through guards and thick material, assuming it can even be blocked. Most demons find it difficult to parry spells that come from below.

Water Knife

Runes: 23 initial, 11 chain

Mana: 30; 33%

Attack: 210

Penetration 50 pierce, 100% multiplier

Max Range: 150 meters

Velocity: 75 m/s

Water knife might better be named water bullet. This magic forges a projectile of hardened water that pierces the enemy. Water knife can be chained, allowing fast iterations after the first casting. The bullets may be held for up to three seconds, allowing for various rhythms of release.

Water knife can curve along the trajectory of flight after being fired. Unlike wind blade, however, it may never curve back onto itself, nor veer more than 80 degrees off its initial direction. The spell’s overall rate of turn is limited to no more than 180 degrees in every second of flight.

Despite this, water knife remains quite tricky to dodge, as the bullet can jitter when manipulated by a skilled mage. This allows the spell to displace itself up to half-a-meter along the column of its path. This feature is independent of the spell’s ability to change its overall trajectory.

Contrary to most spells, the energy that can be placed in a water knife is fixed. Because of this, the magic is considered highly technical, challenging a mage’s casting speed and control rather than their raw power.

Flame Shot

Runes: 9 (14 variant)

Mana: 30 to 270; 13%

Attack: 60 to 540

Penetration 10 pierce, 100% multiplier

Max Range: 60 meters

Velocity: ~35 m/s

Area: 1 to 3 meter radius

One of the simplest, effective combat spells in existence. Flame shot forms a ball of fire that can be lobbed like a common material projectile. In order to maximize the range, flame shot must be thrown at a 45-degree angle, with the caster moving in tandem to enhance the velocity. The normal variation of the spell has minimal control. When cast at maximum range, it can skew 5 meters in any direction at most.

Like most fire spells, flame shot easily accommodates great amounts of energy and ether. Mages can put about twice as much energy into the spell as they can into ordinary attack magics.

Despite flame shot’s drawbacks, it remains a rather popular spell in the netherworld.

A 14 rune wind dual-element variant of this spell exists. By adding 10 wind ether, the spell’s velocity and trajectory control can be greatly improved.

Cremation Hex

Runes: 17

Mana: 15; 33%

Power: 0 to 250 fire damage

Duration: 0 to 60 seconds

Despite the name, the cremation hex is a blood element curse, not a fire spell. The cremation hex forms a runic chain that embeds itself in the target. The duration is approximate, as the actual span depends on the comparison between the caster’s Spirit: Integrity and the opponent’s ability to break down the curse.

The cremation hex creates channels that absorb fire ether, burning the target from the inside out. This completely bypasses all defenses, causing direct and horrifying damage to the blood essence. Each curse is sufficient to channel around 100 ether into the body, but is consumed after triggering. Multiple curses have an additive effect while compounding the duration by the resultant.

Independent curses can be braided into a super curse with greater power and function. Each thread beyond the first increases duration and function by +25%, to a maximum of 200%. However, this rarely comes into play as mages of sufficient skill have almost always moved onto the advanced magics.
