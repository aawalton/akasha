Chapter 191 / B3-36: Juggling Plans

Feeling a bit down due to the animosity between the pack of akhluts and the cù-sìth, Joe trudged his way back through Seaside. With a series of heavy sighs, he climbed the hill from the shore up towards Founders Plaza, passing by dozens of theaters and eateries.

Even though it was too early to see a play, there were plenty of places to grab a bite of food from. Joe bought a woven-reed bowl of doughy balls fried around pieces of fruit called daydahs. He bought a double portion so he could share with Mojo. The tasty blobs were a bit like jelly-filled donut holes, more filling and less sweet than those from Earth’s donut shops.

Even though the theaters wouldn't open their doors for a few more hours, there were plenty of street performers out and about. Joe and Mojo stopped to watch a juggler spin objects through the air. The lanky elf was wearing a colorful costume with plenty of ribbons to accentuate his movements. Fararannul the Fabulous was very charismatic. He had gathered a small crowd of rapt onlookers. To engage his spectators, the elf frequently lobbed a ball to the watching children, who would then toss it back into the spinning cluster of props, with varying degrees of success.

When the man noticed Mojo, still riding Joe, he flipped an apple to the broodling instead of a juggling ball. Both Joe and the performer were a bit surprised when Mo perfectly tossed the fruit back into the flow of whirling items rather than taking a bite. The purple-eyed elf sent another object to the broodling and again received it right back into his hand, flawlessly on beat. Mojo ruffed, which elicited a giggle from Fararannul.

With that, the games began.

The colorfully dressed performer picked up the pace, and soon Joe stood at one end of a helix of hurled objects. He could feel Mo manipulating the air, a bit like Hah’roo’s tricks with the winds. This allowed the little guy to draw the objects along as he shifted to a better perch on Joe.

“He’s great!” Fara exclaimed as coins rained into his basket. An even larger crowd was forming around them. While the juggling was really quite impressive, Joe felt that more folks were coming over because the two of them were just having so much fun. Much to his chagrin, they even began to work Joe into the act. Mojo moved to his head and did flips while he juggled with the elf who was narrating the exchange with a loud performer's voice. Mo wasn’t overly heavy, but it was not all that much fun to have his little feet slapping onto Joe’s crown over and over again.

“See how Mojo works the {Air}. That is not me, folks. This marvelous little wind mage may be better at this than I am,” the entertainer announced with an exaggerated sad face. “Soon, I shall be supplanted by this fuzzy wonder. But, I may just know his weakness.”

Fara used a bit of magic to pluck the daydah basket from Joe, juggling the rest of the items one-handed. He added one of the pastries to the flying collection. This time, Mojo took the bait and popped the dough ball into his mouth with a loud smack. This earned a good laugh from the crowd. More daydahs came and went. The pair ended the performance on the last bit of dough. This one Mojo caught, and instead of chomping it down, he expertly lobbed it into Fara’s mouth. The colorfully dressed elf swept into a bow and then extended arms to his costar. Mojo chittered and ruffed happily, executing a decent bow of his own.

While watching Fara reset his gear and Mojo was getting attention from the kids in the crowd, Joe realized his blues were long gone. The hostility between the packs was a problem that needed to be solved, but Joe felt he was no longer on the clock. Recently, he had been rushing between Cauldrekon, Founders Day, and the Vale of Lament. With those now behind him, he could now devote some time to other activities.

Since he also had to get [Anyone] off cooldown, he had a window in his schedule where he could take it easy, avoiding activities that would earn him exp.

‘You know what, Hawking? I think a week of downtime sounds just about perfect.’

Do you wish me to inform you if your downtime activities are likely to result in significant experience?

‘Please do. Are there any out-of-combat tasks you think would be big exp earners?’

The primary actions associated with one’s classes yield the most non-combat experience. In your case, these would be healing and warding.

‘Aw, man. I wanted to study warding. In order to learn more wards, I have to raise my [Basic Warding] ranks.’

Or increase the rarity of the skill. You have the common skill [Basic Warding]. If you were to advance the skill to the uncommon [Intermediate Warding], you would be able to learn more formulas with your existing ranks. Though the act of learning new formulas in and of itself will give you experience and skill ranks.

Please note that your worries are somewhat disproportionate to your current situation, Joe. You need 1,235 points of experience to reach level 18. [Anyone] will be available again in 8 days. You would have to perform an excessive amount of healing or warding to accumulate 1,235 experience in that duration.

‘What about healing Margen? If Mercy Suku includes me in the ceremony, will I get experience for that?’

I stand corrected. Depending on your level of participation, you are correct in assuming such an act could be detrimental to your plans. The removal of [Mythic Mummy Rot] from a [Living Legend] in tandem with a sainted [Divine Servant of Murrcee] is likely to result in significant experience for you.

‘Oh, for crying out loud. So how the heck am I going to get my level seventeen trait?’

I am confident you can discover a way to overcome this challenge should you devote some time to finding the answer.

‘No clues this time?’

You don’t need my help with this one, Joe.

‘Boo. Kidding. Thanks, Hawking.’

Joe extricated his pup from the gathering gaggle of children, promising to bring him back later in the week. He finished his trek to the guildhouse in much lighter spirits. Stepping into the entry hall, he spotted Kenda and Puqmup. Both had dark circles under their eyes. Joe doubted either of them had seen much, if any, sleep since yesterday’s assault on the Vale of Lament. He hit them both with [Effereous Endurance] as they lifted their bleary eyes to see who was coming in the front door.

Kenda threw him a warm smile as the gray aura around her dissipated.

“Whoa!” gasped the gnome. “What a nice spell you got there, Joe. There are plenty of anti-fatigue skills, but this one has a hint of encouragement tucked into it. It’s a boost and a hand up all-in-one.”

Joe had picked up [Efferous Endurance] back in the farming community of Crowfield. Since then, he had come to realize how different the {Beast} spell was from the standard [Reinvigorate]. Both gave a boost to stamina recovery, but [Efferous Endurance] was created to stimulate beasts of burden. He could imagine how the extra dose of motivation would be a perfect way to get plowhorses to churn another field or oxen to cover a few more miles. Even though it was slightly less effective on people than [Reinvigorate], Joe liked the little rallying kicker enough that he never regretted buying it.

“So what’s the plan?” Joe asked. “When is the council announcing the big surprise? On the way here, I heard some rumors about the Big Man’s return. People were scoffing at the gossip, but it shows that the tales are getting traction. I doubt it will stay a secret much longer.”

“Septimus wants to make a huge reveal out of his return,” Puq scoffed, “But the rest of us are just happy it has happened. It will be this afternoon, 5-Bells-Day. They want you there for it, Joe.”

“Do I have to?” Joe was not at all a fan of being the center of attention.

“Yes. Yes, you do,” Kenda exclaimed. “Yuk and Taylyn already beat you to bowing out. Besides, we want one of our own up there, somebody we can cheer for. Not to mention, it’s good for the guild. Never hurts to remind Fort Coral of what we do for the community.”

“Okay,” he sighed. “Before then, I am going to need you, Kenda. Both in an official and unofficial capacity.”

“Ooh. I like the sound of that,” she smirked back, trying and succeeding in getting a blush out of Joe.

“We also have something we’d like you to take a crack at, Joe,” the guild-quartermaster stated. “As you know, the scattered items from the Erlkings trove are cursed. We have Vexor trying to unravel the bane, but curses are not his specialty. We’d like you to see if you can do better.”

“Better than Vex?”

“Every form of magic has its idiosyncrasies. I know you are just starting out with curses, but your class might help you in ways that even his encyclopedic knowledge won’t see. We just want you to give it a try. It’s a bit time-sensitive. Sooner rather than later, someone is going to go out to that valley and start bringing those cursed items back to the city. Even if it's just to ship them off for sale somewhere else, we really don't want to be the source of a spell-plague.”

“Which means that while we are going over those official needs of yours,” the skill savant interjected, “we should keep an eye out for any good curse-breaking advancements.”

“Very well. I will leave you two to it. Joe, before you go, come by the guild shop, and I will give you a cursed item that we recovered from the Vale to work on.”

“Will do, Puq,” he replied before following Kenda to one of the studies. Just after she crossed into the room, Kenda paused for a second to look around. Instead of stopping short, Joe took the opportunity to move forward and wrap his arms around her waist, hugging her tightly to him. She leaned back against his chest and folded her arms over his, squeezing him back.

“I was thinking about you all night,” she sighed. “Leaving you two down there in the cave was the hardest decision …”

“It was the right one,” he affirmed, enjoying the feel of her in his arms. “I was thinking about you last night, too. Can you come over tonight?”

“I can, but I’m surprised you’re free. I would have sworn House Jarlscarp would have snatched you up for their gala tonight.”

“Oh, I did get that invite. Do you think I should go? I really don’t relish the idea of being paraded around for a bunch of affluent elites.”

“If it were House Tarken or Vaylish, I’d say skip. You won’t enjoy those. Those two houses are pretty full of themselves. But House Jarlscarp made its fortune constructing this city and somehow never really lost their hands-on mindset. They hold three Crafters Balls every year, and any member of that crafting guild is made welcome. I’ve been to a couple of Kiln Balls for the glaziers and potters, and let me tell you that noble house does not skimp on good beers and food. Those galas have been some of the most amazing nights of my life.”

She turned around in his arms to face him. “You are going to have to get unveiled to the city one way or another, Joe. I’d take the invite that you will have the most fun with. Under that light, Jarlscarp would be at the top of my list. The Crafting Clan are pretty straightforward folk. Like most dwarves, they will want to get you crazy drunk, but there will be no hidden agendas, like you might find with the other noble houses.”

“Will you be my date?”

Just watching her face brighten caused Joe to pull her tighter. Kenda kissed him before replying, “I would love to. We are going to have to get you something better to wear.”

“You read my mind. That was one of my unofficial asks.”

“Can’t wait to find out what the other ones are. Let’s get the official stuff out of the way first. Party up with me, so I can see what has changed with you.”
