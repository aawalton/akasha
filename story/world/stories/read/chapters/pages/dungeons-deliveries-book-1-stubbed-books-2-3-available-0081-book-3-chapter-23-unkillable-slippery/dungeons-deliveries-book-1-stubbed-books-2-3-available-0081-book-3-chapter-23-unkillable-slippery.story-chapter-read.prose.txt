“Bah, that’s all you got? Put your damn phone away!” Alex mocked as he booted the man in the butt, sending him sprawling into the mud.

Brody jittered with [Elegy’s Exit] through the air, ending up behind the man to jump on his ankle. It splintered under his foot, sending the man into wails.

“Nice.” Brody grunted, huffing and extending his hand for a fist bump.

Dandelion’s voice called out, “He—,” before dropping away.

Time immediately slowed around Alex. [Relative Voyeur], his time dilation Title, activated when he was in mortal peril. The screams and clashing turned down several octaves, and Brody’s fist grinded millimetre by millimeter through the air.

Alex grinned, craning his eyes to search for the source of his incoming death. The first few times it had activated since they’d landed next to the Falls, he’d floundered and panicked. It didn’t guarantee he’d make it out alive. Time still moved around him, just far slower than his thoughts. But he realized something about it all. As long as he kept calm and assessed, he could always figure something out and live.

Hopefully…

Ah, lovely, just a wall of spears.

A box of cast spears headed slowly towards him, not two feet to he and Brody’s left. They moved silently through the air, inch by inch, to tenderize him and his clone.

Can’t have that, now can we?

Glancing quickly, he spotted Dandelion atop The Expediter, beaming as a tiny pellet flew out of her tiny guy toward some poor bastard. Mapping the way out, Alex hoped that Brody wouldn’t be too bothered by sending him away to the unspace momentarily.

It’ll just be a moment, buddy. DON’T BE MAD.

Alex made his decision, activating the second part of [Illusory Copy], and blinked away in slow motion to take Brody’s place. He tried his best to ignore the existential dread of being torn from reality, then being shoved through a tunnel that touched…everything, including bits that seemed to call out to him, before being quickly unfurled in the blip in space where Brody had been.

CRUNCH

WOOF

Seer?

To not go back to the unspace and rely on Alex to pull him out. And now Brody was yelling at him that he needed Alex to do this.

[1. Aid the Other in his want]

Of course he would help Brody. Fear of losing control of the skill, his marbles, and possibly his life didn’t enter the equation.

“I’ll find it.” Alex said, and blinked away, leaving his friends to fight without his help.

His enemy was preparing something grand, trying to understand Emilio’s true might.

His unseen target would learn soon.

Emilio languished to the sounds of young Gnomes growing their strength, and pushed his gaze across the sky of his region to check in on his Companion. Alex would make it through. Emilio was certain of that. Though upon assessing, a deep growl rumbled in his belly.

The drones’ violence impressed him. Dandelion did too, and Emilio was surprised to see her dropping enemies with ease. Perhaps she should be elevated amongst the ranks, rather than just rewarded handsomely. Alex, true to his form, scampered around searching. A presence he’d ignored sparked in the sky, likely watching the battle below as well, and Emilio yanked his all-seeing eyes up into the sky to assess the potential scavenger. He couldn’t help himself, as these days finding appropriate hunting in his region proved difficult.

As his gaze landed on the flier, he felt something new flutter in his chest.

Startled, Emilio broke his vision away and came too on the bed of coals to find himself purring deeply.

Why did his heart hammer as it did? Why did he not need to supress the instinct to hunt the flier? It was something he had to constantly fight against now that he was allied with animals of all types. Why was he purring so?

Emilio grunted, carefully extending his sight back to the Falls to gaze upon the flier that felt oddly cat-like.
