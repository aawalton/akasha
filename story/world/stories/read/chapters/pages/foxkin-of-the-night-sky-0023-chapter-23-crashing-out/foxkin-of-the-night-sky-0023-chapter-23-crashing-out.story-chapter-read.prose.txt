I failed to convince Older Sister that cake is, in fact, extremely healthy and should be a permanent staple of my diet once I exit the Tutorial.

Tragic.

I will try again tomorrow.

After all, I am nothing if not persistent. And no being in the universe can withstand my puppy-dog eyes more than once.

Good for them.

As expected.

My tail gives a small, absent flick.

Unacceptable.

Fine.

Nearly two hundred.

Good.

At my current speed?

A pattern.

Charming.

And kill.

New monsters appear—stronger ones, smarter ones—

This isn’t practice. This is optimization.

Twenty minutes after I fully commit to becoming a fox-shaped missile fueled by pride and spite, I level up.

Another hour—another.

Skill selection.

Once.
