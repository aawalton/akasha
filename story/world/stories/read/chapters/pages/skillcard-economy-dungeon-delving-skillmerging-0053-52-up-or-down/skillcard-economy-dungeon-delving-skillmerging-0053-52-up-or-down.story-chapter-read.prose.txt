Rift break.

An event when a Rift is not cleared of monsters and becomes overloaded. The monsters burst out into the real world.

Something that had not occurred in the last three years. That event was happening now.

The ground shook with a loud boom.

“Cerise, are you all right?” I asked, panicking.

—I, fuck… yes, Father. I got it, Father… I am alright. You guys focus on escaping.

“We should head to the top floor,” Toby suggested. “With how many Walkers there are, that should be the safest place right now.”

—Don’t. It’s a Gold-grade Rift.

“A Gold-grade Rift?” I couldn’t believe what I was hearing.

“How can they let a Gold-grade Rift overload?”

—They didn’t… It opened just now.

“What?” Toby and I gaped at the same time.

How could a Rift that just opened overload? This had never happened.

My gaze fell on the small rooms in which the NPCs were trapped.

Or it had. We just weren’t told.

—There are flying beasts attacking the top floor. There are many Silver and Gold-rank Walkers, but even more normies. The real powerhouses can’t go all out because of that. The building is already falling. Go down and escape.

“What about you?” I asked.

—Don’t worry about me. Father will not let me die… even if it means sacrificing everyone else in the process.

The last part was a mumble, not meant for anyone to hear.

—Take care of yourself. There are already monsters coming inside.

“We need to get out of here,” Tina said. She hadn’t heard what Cerise had said, but had inferred it from the explosions and our echoing questions. “I believe you had an escape plan before coming here.”

Toby nodded.

“I will go ahead and scout then,” I said, channeling my Magika.

“No,” Tina said. “It’s better to stick together. If we encounter something we can’t kill alone, that would be bad.”

“As Tina said,” Toby agreed. “Stick together. If we encounter a creature we can run from, we run. If we can’t run, we try making an opening to run.”

Tina and I agreed with Toby.

“What about them?” I asked, pointing towards the NPCs.

“We… We can’t save them,” Toby said.

“What? We can’t just leave them,” I argued.

“They won’t move,” Tina said. “The researchers here had been trying that. They don’t eat. They don’t drink. They are like living corpses. We won’t be able to move them from here.”

I looked at the NPC in the next room. Even after all these explosions, he hadn’t even flinched.

“What about the others?” Toby asked.

“We are also here!” someone shouted.

“You guys finally remembered. Now get us out of here.”

We walked forward and found that all the rooms after Tina’s were also filled with everyone who had been captured. They looked weak and must have been sleeping until now because of that. The explosion had awoken them.

I looked around and found Hanz there too. He banged on the glass.

“Get us out of here!” He had tears in his eyes. “I don’t want to die here. Get us fucking out of here, man.”

I felt bliss by watching the crying man.

“Let’s get them out of here,” Tina suggested.

Out of all of us, only Tina was able to cut open the glass. I could have done it with Rotary Rend, but I wasn’t looking forward to breaking my fingers.

“Why are you so useless?” Toby remarked.

“You are literally standing here doing the same as me.” I grumbled.

“No, I am supporting my girl. Go, Tina. You can do this.”

“Shut up,” she shouted back.

“She just loves me so much.”

Five minutes later, everyone was out.

They had already started making small groups with people they knew. Hanz and his friends were hugging and crying on each other’s shoulders.

“Everyone,” Toby said loudly, gathering everyone’s attention.

The little chattering stopped.

“Everyone here was captured by the Phoenix Guild when Life’s Last Hope closed. So if you find anyone from Pheonix Guild, know that they will try to eliminate you. There is also a Rift break going on, so there are monsters outside. We have to escape on our own.”

No one argued with Toby, or rather, with Tina, who stood beside him. Everyone knew who she was. A Gold-rank Walker who didn’t belong to any guild. And a fighter class at that. If they wanted to escape this place, they needed to stick with her.

I noticed that Hanz was staring at me. I gave him a wave. He glared harder and kept staring until one of his friends made him stop.

“Are there any Walkers among you?”

Around a dozen hands shot up.

“Are there any Silver-rank or above?”

All the hands fell down.

Toby sighed and turned around.

“Getting this many people out of here would be hard,” he said to Tina and me.

“But we can’t leave them,” I said.

“Yeah,” Tina agreed.

“I was not suggesting we leave them," Toby sighed. "I was just saying how hard it will be.”

“That doesn’t matter. We have to do it,” I said.

“Don’t worry. I will burn every monster that comes our way.” Tina said with a grin.

Toby chuckled and turned around.

“Everyone follows my orders and does exactly as I tell you to do, and I promise to get you out of here. Any questions?”

No one asked anything. They didn’t even want to know who was saving them. They just wanted to escape.

“Good, then let’s go.”

As we left, my heart ached at leaving the NPCs behind.

I am going to save you.
