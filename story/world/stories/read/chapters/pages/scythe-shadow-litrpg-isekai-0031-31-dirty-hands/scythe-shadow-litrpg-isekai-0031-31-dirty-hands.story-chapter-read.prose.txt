I stayed there, sitting against the lighthouse wall for a long while. Nuzzle had climbed down from his hiding spot and slept in a ball on my leg. The little shit even licked my cheek on his way down in an attempt to cheer me up.

I assembled the scythe in my right hand, then retracted it. Then assembled again.

Retracted.

Assembled.

Retracted.

Over and over.

Lancelot was one of them.

The task Valciferous had given me just became even more monumental. If I couldn't convince the Shepherds to give up their views—and let's face it, that was always the least likely possibility—I'd have to fight Reapers like Lancelot.

Lancelot, who basically one-hit KO'd a Sapphire Sin right before my eyes.

I stroked Nuzzle's head with a finger.

If I wanted to stand a chance against that, I was going to need a veritable army at my side, on top of the personal growth I already planned to pursue. The god wasn't exaggerating when he said I'd need to unite the realm. And from everything I'd seen so far, the majority of Reapers preferred to work in isolation—like Lancelot and Zeus—or small groups of two or three—like Jericho and Calliope.

Calliope… I'm so sorry… My eyes betrayed me, locking on Allium's decapitated head. It lay on the ground amid the gore from the gorilla hybrid's stomach. His eyes were open, still watching me—judging me—blaming me—even in death.

I stuck my scythe into the ground and pulled myself up. Nuzzle woke and crawled up atop the blade. I wouldn't be seeing Calliope again after this. How could I face her? I'd most likely had my last drink with Jericho, too.

I'd come here to get away from them. To forge this path on my own, but it was becoming clearer by the second that this wasn't something I could do alone.

Originally I'd thought maybe I could be friends with them to make this second existence more bearable. But I'd still kept them at arms length. Why create connection when I've got one foot out the door? I'd never intended to stay. Getting back to Vitria was my goal and they knew that. Yet they'd still helped me up until now.

So selfless. Unlike me.

It didn't matter now.

I had to maintain my focus on the goal.

The blue bar of Volition in my interface had about a third remaining. I'd have to take care of that before I could do anything. That kangaroo Sin the gorilla backhanded off the cliff would be ripe for harvesting.

I descended the stairs down toward the beach, my resolve growing with each step. I could do this.

From up here I could see the spot the Unchained had descended on Allium. The sand was splashed with red, looking like a sanguine bomb had exploded there. Even at this distance, I could see nothing remained of the other Unchained that had attacked with the gorilla. Perhaps the dormant Will was still there, but nothing else.

Damn, that would have been me if Lancelot hadn't intervened.

Once on the beach, it didn't take long to find the corpse of the kangaroo-human hybrid. I approached cautiously as I opened my dark eye to the Sin's Will.

[Identity: Sin

Rank: Amber

Affinity: Aegis]

This was the first Aegis Affinity I'd encountered, as far as I knew. I was curious to see which stats it augmented when I relinquished it.

I stepped forward, then paused. I hadn't even noticed, not thinking it possible.

The Amber light of its Will still burned.

The body twitched and the aura around it flared back to life.

I stumbled back, but a dark orange hand burst from the sand and snared my leg.

Not this again.

The Unchained rose.

It could barely move, but still took a step toward me and fell, its human leg twisted at an unnatural angle from its tumble off the cliff. Shoulder, too, twisted awkwardly, parts of bloody bone jutting out from its skin.

It had me constrained, but I refused to panic, unlike Nuzzle who sprinted circles around me frantically swiping paws at the ghostly appendage. I knew how to get out of this situation now. There was no rush. Not with the state of the beast and the distance still between us.

Even now, as it took another wobbly step, the mangled leg fully snapped to a grotesque 90-degree angle at the shin. The shadowy hand around my ankle vanished, a few wisps of mist all that remained of its presence. The Sin lay on its back in the sand, breathing heavy, wild rage in its eyes.

I stepped forward and the kangaroo swiped a clawed hand at me, but was no where near close enough to do any damage. It hissed and spat bloody spittle, a crazed expression tainting is half-marsupial, half-human face like a feral beast.

How could anyone want to shepherd these monsters? How could anyone view them as anything other than what they were? Pathetic manifestations of human sins—greed, lies, corruption, lust, hatred. These didn't have a place in the world.

In my world.

I decapitated the Unchained Sin with a single swipe of my blade.

Except… I didn't.

Blood gurgled from the shallow slice in the beast's neck. It's eye's widened frantically, thrashing its limbs. Air wheezed through the severed windpipe in a red mist.

I cut again, but didn't go through. The scythe was awkward to swing, it didn't hit where I expected. Not a weapon for slicing like this.

Who the fuck designed this thing?

Fuck.

A leg thrust up and knocked me backward onto my ass.

I grabbed my scythe again and straddled the soul, holding its arms and legs from hitting me.

"Just die!"

And I sawed at the neck, gripping my blade low. Warm blood sprayed. I squeezed my eyes shut, unsure if the liquid running from them was tears or blood. Back and forth. A metallic taste on my lips. Back and forth. The visceral sound of slicing juicy meat. My hands were slick and sticky. I kept loosing grip on the blade.

"Fuck fuck fuck fuck fuck."

Why hadn't I just stabbed the stupid thing? Literally anything but this.

I fell forward as the spine finally severed.

The beast lay motionless.

I let my scythe fall to the ground and tried to calm my breathing. I sat there on the corpse, focusing my gaze on the dunes towering above, my chest heaving up and down, lungs filling and emptying. I could feel the blood dripping down my arms and face, seeping into my pants.

"Shepherds my fucking ass…"

I rolled off and lay next to the corpse.

Nuzzle crawled onto my forehead and began licking the blood from my face, being sure to scour halfway up my damn nostrils with his sandpaper tongue like a good lizard friend. I had no idea if the little guy was consciously cleaning me off, or if he had a vampiric taste for blood. But I figured if it were the latter, he'd have just dove headfirst into the body of fresh meat beside me.

Once Nuzzle was satisfied with his work and tucked himself into a ball in the warm sand, I got to my knees and looked at the kangaroo hybrid I'd completely annihilated.

The entire upper body was a wreck from its chest to its mouth. In my frantic struggle to get it over with I'd cut this way and that with no regard for form or efficiency. Strips of flesh and chunks of trachea and esophagus hung in different directions out of place, like some dark-ass Picasso shit.

I truly hated myself in that moment. Not because of what I'd done, but how I'd done it. The brutality… Almost like I'd lost control.

Affinity: Death.

The red sand looked far too similar to a bloody carpet. I pushed the memories down. That wasn't me.

This wasn't me.

I would do what needed to be done to get home, but I couldn't lose myself in the process. I had to come out the other side as Miles, not Zephyr.

I prayed to whatever gods would listen that it was possible.

For Avery's sake. For my own sanity.
