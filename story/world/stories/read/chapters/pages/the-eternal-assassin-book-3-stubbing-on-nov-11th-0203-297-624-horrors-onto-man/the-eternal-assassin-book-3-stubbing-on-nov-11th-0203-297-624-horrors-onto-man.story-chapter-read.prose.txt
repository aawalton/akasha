Time seemed to slow down without Asher even triggering Comprehend’s second-tier ability as he stared at the blank-faced man before him. While the too-large muscles and obvious magical experimentation was worrisome, the fact that the man was somehow still alive chilled Asher to the bone. He couldn’t even begin to imagine what the last few years had been like for the farmer, and his heart went out to the man as he took in Grant’s vacant-eyed stare. Then, before he could make so much as a single move, Grant did so first.

Turning and sprinting away from Asher and Kroult.

“Grant!” Asher shouted, cursing as he took off after the shockingly fast man. “Kroult, get back to the others! Tell them I’m going after Grant!”

Asher didn’t know if Kroult even bothered to respond as he was already using Distortion to compress space and chase after the man who had been turned into some horrific magical experiment. While Asher could move twenty feet in a single step, Grant was fast. The bulging muscles in his legs clearly weren’t just for show, and Asher clicked his tongue as he realized he wasn’t just not gaining ground, he was actively falling farther and farther behind with every second he chased after the missing farmer. The only good news was that while Grant was fast, he certainly wasn’t quiet. Asher didn’t have any issue following the sound of his pounding feet as the man raced through the cave system.

What does this mean? Asher thought, whipping around a bend in the tunnel and just barely catching sight of Grant before the man darted around another bend himself. If The Lost Lady does this sort of thing with each of the men she kidnaps, does that mean she’s got a dozen or so living people enthralled under her? God, is there a part of Grant that’s still conscious under all that, forced to watch as his physical body follows her demands like some sort of twisted puppet?

He’d thought discovering Kroult’s prison would be the most horrifying thing he’d ever witness, as he hadn’t been able to imagine being unable to move so much as a single muscle for centuries on end. But was that better or worse than being trapped in one’s own body and forced to watch as someone else went ahead and moved your muscles for you?

I don’t know, and I don’t want to even think about it, Asher decided, gritting his teeth as he realized Grant had pulled a little farther away from him again. If he hadn’t picked up cardio a while back due to Moxy’s incessant prodding, he had no doubt he would have already lost sight of the farmer.

Turning another bend, Asher realized he had a chance to make up ground as he spotted the lengthy tunnel before them. Grant was almost a hundred feet away as he ran at a dead sprint, but Asher had something the man didn’t have. Imbuing the Eternal Mark he’d been prepared to use on the chalk for their trap instead into one of his daggers, Asher threw it forward with all his might, even compressing the space before them to make it fly faster. Grant was far away enough that he didn’t stand any chance at actually hitting the man, but that was fine.

He just needed to get close.

All of Asher’s training paid off, and he grinned as his dagger caught up to Grant, passing him about a dozen feet to his left. Before the farmer could change course, Asher triggered Recall, warping forward and popping into existence off to the side. The moment he did so he threw up Spatial Lock, trapping Grant inside a giant bubble with him.

“You’re not going anywhere,” Asher said, pulling out Crescent and Nail as he held his masterwork daggers before him. Seeing as Grant wasn’t an undead, his living wood dagger wouldn’t have any special benefits this time around.

For his part, Grant surprised him by seeming to realize he was trapped almost instantly. Rather than try to keep running, the farmer turned and lunged at him, his face remaining eerily blank as he moved. He didn’t utter so much as a single word as he swung a fist at Asher’s head, and Asher moved to dodge and sever Grant’s outstretched arm before hesitating at the last second, deciding instead to trigger Comprehend and speed up his thoughts. Staring deep into the man’s lifeless eyes before him, Asher argued with himself.

If there’s even the slimmest of chances Grant’s still in there… Is it okay to kill him? His class is still farmer and his aura seems human enough, which I can only take to mean he’s not completely gone. Despite everything The Lost Lady did to him, there may be a way to turn him back. If healing magic exists that’s powerful enough to resurrect those who have literally died, there has to be a way to fix this.

At the same time, I can’t risk messing up our attack on The Lost Lady just to save one single person, or even a handful of them. She’s a powerful wizard, there’s no doubt about that. If we give her any handicaps, it might be us who get turned into whatever happened to Grant next.

Making his decision, Asher let Comprehend come to an end before following through with his attack. But rather than cleave Grant’s arm in two, he dropped his dagger at the last second, grabbing a hold of Grant’s arm as he lashed out toward the man’s chest with his other weapon. Just as he’d hoped, Grant focused on twisting out of the way of his blade, ignoring his outstretched arm that Asher was still holding for a moment. That single second was all Asher needed to shove an Eternal Mark into his forearm, and Asher grinned as the first step in his plan fell into place.

Right up until one of Grant’s muscular legs shot up out of nowhere and smashed him across the face.

Asher was sent flying backward, knocked out of his own Spatial Lock as he bounced and tumbled across the cave floor. The moment he left its confines his skill came to an end, and he spat out shards of broken teeth as he spotted Grant turn and sprint away once more. Rather than try to take advantage of his wounded state, Grant fled just as he had the first time he’d spotted them.

Why would her servants be so cautious… I don’t get it.

Shaking his head, Asher wobbled as he got to his feet, holding a hand against his head as he realized Grant had given him a concussion. He was grateful that Eternal Regeneration healed him completely whenever he was damaged, because according to what he remembered from his first-aid training back on Earth, each subsequent concussion made the next one all the easier. From the sheer number of times he’d been whacked in the head, without his skill, he’d be at risk of getting a concussion from simply nodding too hard at this point.

As he walked around and picked up his daggers, Asher focused on the Eternal Mark he’d placed on Grant’s arm. The farmer was blitzing his way through the cave system, not slowing down in the slightest as he got farther and farther away. It wasn’t long before he reached a portion their team hadn’t gotten to yet based on the distance between them, and Asher whistled as he realized just how vast the cave system had to be.

With any luck, Grant will lead us right back to The Lost Lady. Sure would be nice to finish up in here within the next hour or two rather than spend a week wandering the caves.

Before heading back to his team, Asher took the time to clean up the blood and bits of broken teeth he’d spilled across the cave floor from being kicked in the face. There wasn’t much he could do about the blood that was no doubt already on Grant’s leg, but he saw no sense in leaving any more of his body parts in the lair of a known lich than was necessary. The Lost Lady’s third element was still unknown to them, and there was no telling what other spells she had to work with.

Asher still recalled the scene of Rosh being flayed open before his very eyes as a Blood element user with a particularly nasty skill proved he could harm the man from anywhere in the world. He’d needed a significant amount of blood to make his skill work, but even so, Asher understood just why the Blood element was listed as one of the forbidden ones in the Noala Kingdom.

Once he finished cleaning, he went to Recall back to his team, only to pause as something strange caught his attention. Samantha and Moxy’s marks were still right where he’d left them, but Kroult’s was moving at an alarming speed.

Away from the rest of the team.

“Kroult, if this is some sort of foolish attempt to run away the first moment you get alone, I swear to God…” Asher muttered, closing his eyes as he scried the lich. The moment he did so, he shot Kroult a silent apology, realizing he’d overlooked one ever-so-slight detail.

Kroult was decent at buffing the rest of them, and he was intelligent. But he had about the same combat potential as an undead paper bag.

Which evidently made it extremely easy for another one of those over-muscled minions of The Lost Lady to immobilize him and take off with the lich slung over his shoulder like a prized pig.

Asher watched for a moment as he witnessed the hedge wizard summon electricity to his hand and try to shock the minion into letting him go, but one of the runestones embedded in the right half of the man’s ribcage glowed a bright yellow and the electricity was nullified completely. Kroult then changed up his tactic and pressed a glowing-red hand that burned bright enough the air shimmered around it against the minion, but a separate runestone embedded down near his kidneys lit up red, negating that spell as well.

Well how about that, Asher thought, watching Kroult continue to struggle, trying anything and everything he could think of to break free. Sure would be helpful to get the chance to better examine those runestones and figure out what they do.

Luckily for us, a willing test subject has just appeared.

Unlike Grant, this minion was far older. Despite the body that still looked like it belonged to a professional bodybuilder who’d gotten his steroid dosage horribly wrong, the man’s head was covered in a thick layer of white hair that stretched down past his waist. Asher could only guess he’d been in The Lost Lady’s clutches for the better part of a century, and while Identify and Judgement didn’t work through Eternal Mark’s scrying effect, he had a feeling the man who once called this body his own was long gone. Not wasting any time, Asher prepared himself before triggering Recall.

Without a word, he flickered into existence directly behind the minion and scythed straight through both his legs with a slash from Crescent.

Kroult was sent flying as the older man tumbled to the ground, somehow managing to turn his fall into a roll before popping up onto the stubs of his legs. Despite the fact that he’d just had both his legs sheared off right above the knee, the man’s face was completely blank, without so much as a hint of pain even as he stood directly on his own gaping leg wounds. Asher didn’t miss how there was next to no blood pouring out of the wounds, similar to how The Lost Lady herself didn’t bleed when struck.

[Lost] - 1/3 elements

Identify - Lvl 34

“Lost,” Asher muttered, giving the man a pained grimace as that all but confirmed it. The man didn’t have an aura either, and along with his odd-sounding class, that was all the proof Asher needed to decide putting him down was the nicest thing he could do for the poor man. “I’m sorry you had to suffer all these years, but don’t worry. You’ll be free soon. I promise.”

If any part of the man heard what he was saying, there wasn’t any sort of indication. Instead, the man hurled himself at Asher using his muscular arms like some sort of gorilla, his blank eyes staring past Asher’s own as if he didn’t really see him even as he reached out to wrap his fingers around Asher’s throat. Flickering into the astral, Asher let the man soar straight through him before turning around and elongating Crescent.

With a heavy slash, Asher cleaved the man in half from top to bottom, hopefully freeing a soul that had been trapped for who knew how long.
