Far from the Storm Sect, within the ashen reaches where the Soul Sect stood, a certain Calamity sat alone in his cultivation chamber, reading the latest reports from the outside world.

Having secluded himself within those walls for the past five years, the reports were his only connection to the world beyond them.

Runic eyes aglow with violet light, his gaze moved methodically across the parchment.

The Storm Sect had won the Balance War without losing a single transmigrant.

Their enemies, however, had not been so fortunate.

The Earth Sect had lost two transmigrants in one fell swoop, leaving itself vulnerable to old rivals.

The Blood, Serenity, and Glass Sects had been forced to pay immense sums of resources to recover their arrested transmigrants.

The thought made him chuckle.

Arrested.

What a ridiculous term.

His fingers twitched as he continued reading.

The Solar Sect had lost one of its most promising cultivators.

And the Illusion Sect—

Severian paused, his brows furrowing as he read the final line.

The Illusion Sect had possessed only one transmigrant, last he knew. If they had truly lost her in this war...

His expression darkened.

Malverin would be devastated. She had always grown attached far too easily.

A quiet sigh escaped him.

Perhaps it was time he paid his little sister a visit.

The thought lingered for a long moment before another sigh followed, carrying with it a faint trace of gloom.

There was a reason he had secluded himself within this chamber for the last five years. A reason he had neglected both his sect and his family in pursuit of a breakthrough that seemed only to drift farther away with each passing day.

Lips tightening, he lifted his gaze toward the heavens, toward the looming sense of an ending that seemed to draw closer with every passing day.

Then his attention shifted beyond the heavens themselves, toward something far more distant.

Something whose very existence defied the law of stagnancy.

Even now, he could feel it.

That slow, steady expansion that had refused to stop for reasons only the Storm Sect understood.

For a moment, he simply watched it. Then a faint smile touched his lips.

Interesting.

For the first time in years, the path ahead no longer seemed entirely closed.

It appeared the time had finally come to return to the outside world. After all, the answers he sought were no longer within the walls of this chamber.

They were waiting in the Storm Sect.

Or perhaps, more accurately...

They soon would be.
