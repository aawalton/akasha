Samantha stacked her dimensional sack, the special pocket space within her staff, and her general person with whatever crap might be useful in the coming conflict, including an overage of spell components. She also included two of the highest evolution regen potions, now quite ridiculous in potency, though limited in number.

Pinnacle Potion of Troll’s Blood

(Pure Alchemy — Transmutation, Regeneration) Provides regeneration of any wounding. You are immune to Bleeding effects, and cannot die from wounds that aren’t Lethal in severity. Heal 1 wound category every five seconds. Reattaching severed limbs will allow reintegration in a matter of seconds; otherwise, they will regrow over the course of a minute.

Provides +3 Effective Levels to resist physical Stunning or incapacitation effects, and to anything related to pain.

Make a comparison class resistance test against any temporary effect that would hinder healing, with +5 Effective Levels. Magical fire, burning, or acid wounds (specifically) also count for this purpose, but add an additional +3 ELs to resist. Tie or success ignores the effect, and you will heal normally.

Duration is 10 minutes.

Now it would even resist certain lesser entropic effects, though even without the comparison, it was limited. Still, that was a big plus in terms of mitigating strain on the duties of healers.

Though the potions were only for their elites, various lesser versions of it were in the hands of nigh every sort of champion and lieutenant. The whole vanguard had the lowest level, though they’d be keeping these for after various buffs wore off.

In concluding as many loose ends as possible — fuck if all of them would ever be done, but whatever — she used her last free ‘Minor Divine Enchantment’ that had been around forever. She had no real need for a minor enchantment anymore, and had never intended to use it in that fashion. She’d kept it around because it was something of a ‘gamey’ way to affect things at a far distance, to help out a Follower in need. The likelihood of that being relevant had reduced and reduced with each increase in influence and breadth of power, and now it approached zero as her order marched on the capital.

She created a protective anklet — these were pretty rare but had been churning out from Heaven due to the access of the rare modifier (Blessed Stance), that only they could claim, artifact-wise. She didn’t need that due to having it from a common long-term ‘combat readiness’ buff, and half the time she was flying and couldn’t make use of that portion of it. She didn’t assign it to anyone, either, leaving it to the Brain Trust to get it to whatever champion could make use of the bonus.

She also recalled she had to distribute the experience from the partial quest credit. 4 became 12 into Magineer, bringing the running total to 79 out of 170.

And then came deciding the capstone ability for her staff, Foolmaker. Options were extensive but full of things she didn’t need, like ‘Independent,’ or the final upgrade of Spell Absorption. In regard to the latter, she just had too many other counters, and Ozzy was right to replace that portion of the artifact. The Parry, Plus Ultra enchantment could negate single-target spells or abilities far better anyway.

She thought about some of the ‘magic-boosting’ options available, but the greater specialty of her staff as a weapon was already extreme, and her new class had made it even deadlier. On top of that, because of the Magi’s Rapport enhancement, her staff use basically was magic. It was melee or ranged, and at some point, some way, she knew she’d need it for the hard targets. Nuking shit wouldn’t cut it, and [Spellfyr: Charged Strike] still had the most stacking of bonuses and options available.

Thus Samantha decided she wanted to improve the ‘smacking things’ aspect of her staff. Despite the humble-sounding benefit, her smacks were more like mountain-cracking catastrophes; her bonks were brain-disintegrators.

Recalling the few times she’d had someone cancel her success-ensuring effects, and anticipating it could happen again at the higher levels she was operating at, she wanted to bolster her totals. She was mixed on whether she wanted wounding severity or accuracy, and then she found something that split the difference in a big way.

Presaged Demise: Assign a single marked target within line of sight at any time, at will. From this point, animus and anticipation builds perceivably between you and the target until you attack them. After 1 second, add +1 Effective Levels to the use of this weapon, both to strike them and to the Wound Severity of a successful strike against them.

Add another +1 to both after 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, and 10 seconds. At each stage, the target will sense the animus growing.

Presaged Demise resets after any wound-dealing type of attack you intend to land, regardless of the use of the weapon or if the attempt is successful. It also resets if you change targets. It does not reset on feints, but the bonus is also not applicable (however, the animus can reasonably psyche out foes).

This is obscenely powerful yet has the nastiest of trade-offs: time. Nonetheless, it synergizes with some of my other abilities, and the potential for mind games with feints and baiting out defenses is profound. This is the best way to hedge my bets against a strong enemy.

Briefly, she fantasized about setting up the ultimate killshot of compounding temporary attack boosts, and obliterating the Sage in one epic strike.

Okay, maybe not, but someone is going down this way.

Sighing, Samantha made the selection and locked it in.

Final-Final preparations… the long-maligned Avatar Upgrades. Her total levels available were now 15, which was improved from 11, and ranks 5 and 6 were available, for 10,000 or 50,000 FE each, respectively. She took the maximum of rank 6 in Mental Resistance, and split the difference into Magic Resistance 4 and Quick 5. Favored Weapon was an exception to increase, too, thanks to the old quest reward that allowed another selection at -1 of maximum rank. So she bumped it from 3+1 to 5+1.

In total, this cost her a ‘mere’ 84,000 FE. Considering her running total was up to 825k before that, it was immaterial. Before Matrazere’s fall, she’d been at a total near to what she’d just spent. Clearly, there were a few benefits to rapidly becoming the deity of a world religion. Who knew?

She reviewed her statistical bio one last time. Over 57k Followers. Over 6500 Servitors, constantly added to in spurts over time, and heavily lopsided to Angels, Faerie Dragons, and Resemblants, the Sirens and Cath Sith in smaller numbers as special supports. Even still, the total size of the alliance army dwarfed this. The clerics had taken to a policy of actively recruiting the most capable first and foremost, to best leverage and focus the benefits.

Big numbers, in any case. It’s fit to grow. I suppose I could’ve held back and waited, but the Dominion never expected to be in this situation. They’d prepare more every day, too. We decided a blitzkrieg was best, and that’s what we’re doing.

There was little left to do before hitting the battlefield. Excitement warred with butterflies, and they also warred with the sheer dread of death and destruction on the horizon. This was perhaps the final day of Calrenazzod.

She looked around her personal quarters, memorizing it all, from the carelessly discarded ‘signs of lived-in conditions,’ to the paintings on the walls, to the cherished furniture and interior design — that could use a re-touch, but that was fine, totally fine, let’s not go there.

Floating up the tower the long way, she memorized more and more along the path of the stairs. Itha Ranon hadn’t exactly been her first home on Calrenazzod, but it had become the homiest of homes, by far. It felt like hers, and it was hers, both as gifted honorably by another, and as virtually remade in her image. After all, it would still be buried under the sea if she hadn’t raised it up.

It isn’t this home I fight for, as I’ve had many, and ultimately don’t belong in any one place. But I do fight for a sense of home — for comfort, security, and lasting peace. Hypocritical for all the bodies at my feet, but I aim to make up for it. I won’t stop fighting for a greater end to misery, even on the other side of eternity. I’ve visited it, and I’ve suffered it, yes. I still refuse to believe it is our lot. Things are misaligned; the construct is wrong and must be set right. Somehow.

She thought of all the people who’d died already, and those that would suffer and die again.

I’m tired of playing games with people’s lives. When this is done, when this farce is over… If it’s really up to me, I won’t do it again. I’ll stop this madness. No matter what, I will: I’ll fix everything. I swear I will…

Samantha found Azure up on the balcony, though the day was a spoiled one, with a bruised sky and rain likely at any moment. Ah well.

Silently putting her arm around her wife, she looked out and enjoyed possibly the last view of the place they’d get, such as it was.

Finally, Samantha chortled suddenly.

Azure looked at her quizzically. “What is it?”

With an impish grin, Samantha replied, “Guess what my Days Lived value is at, on the great day of reckoning, Blueberry…”
