Although I was a bird… So… I didn’t exactly have a face anyway… I didn’t think so.

I’d never seen one in silver form, though, but that didn’t mean much. Blue, yellow, pink, whatever it was, it didn’t matter as long as I defeated it.

[Mimic Elemental Phoenix Heir, Lvl 40.]

Seeing my name like that was a little imposing, and the level was awesome to look at. It was hard to imagine that I’d gotten this strong not even a year into becoming a Phoenix in this world. Was I really that awesome?

Maybe that would allow me to break through from my Kindled Core to whatever was next. Having a core at such a low rank was definitely bottlenecking my potential, and I didn’t want that to happen.

But then again… I doubted I would have.

How annoying, I should have bought another one-

Whoosh.

A kick I was far too familiar with flew straight at my face, which I dodged thanks to Phoenix Reflex. My doppelganger had gotten tired of me just staring at it, apparently, and came to attack me with the signature Iron Claws.

They weren’t imbued with any fire, though, which I found a little disrespectful.

That was, unless they couldn’t actually use any fire, in which case, good for me, I could get rid of it easily.

The clone chained the missed hit with a Blade Wing and Side Step straight into another attack with Iron Claws.

I dodged every hit, but that thing’s speed was insane. Was that how fast I was? No wonder I was pissing a lot of people off…

I was beginning to get pissed off!

With a thought, I activated Elemental Wave and took to the skies, raining down Pyro Breath on the debuffed clone.

It only took a superficial hit and rose to the skies as well, charging me with Aerial Dive and attacking me further with the same few basic skills over and over again.

What limitations does it have?Curious, I shot out a normal fireball conjured out of the Four Calamity Lotus Flame, but it was slow enough for an easy dodge.

However, the moment it was behind it, I made it explode, sending tiny amounts of fire everywhere, including on its body.

The Four Calamity Lotus Flame began to eat away at its body, but the clone didn’t seem to care.

It did frown, which was weird, and then just waved its wings, getting rid of it.

Finally, something changed within the clone and… It summoned five tornadoes made out of Four Calamity Lotus Flame…

Wait, wait, wait, wait… That’s cheating!

I flew as high as I could, watching the tornadoes spread out and clash with each other, creating a cacophony that was soothing to the ears in a strange way.

If that attack had hit me… I would have been done for! What the hell!

And why didn’t I think of that!

That was definitely an attack I was going to use in the future, but I thought these types of clones couldn’t use anything original!

How did it just invent a skill? Well, sure, it manipulated the fire using a skill, but creating a shape like this was completely different.

It was definitely cheating…

I poured lightning into my body and strengthened myself with Strengthened Constitution, then charged at it with Iron Claws.

The clone of me dodged to the side, and when I went in for another attack, Heaven’s Protection shattered right below my talons.

As if that wasn’t enough, the thing had the nerve to hit me in my stomach!

I… definitely felt that. Oh, did I feel that. All the air got knocked out of my lungs, and I smashed into the ground.

Just before I rolled away to dodge another fire attack, notifications covered my eyes.

One I wasn’t expecting at all.

[Congratulations!]

[Skill: Dragonbone Tempering(Expert) has become Dragonbone Tempering(Master!)]

I’d almost forgotten about that skill, since it wasn’t really obvious. It increased my toughness, sure, but it didn’t really change anything.

Much like the rest of the passive skills.

I definitely had to do a spring cleaning and figure out what to do with them; doing so during one of my meditation sessions could have been the play, but…

Ah well, I always forgot.

Now, though, I felt the pain from the kick induced by myself lessen by a considerable amount, and I was more than happy for that.

But if even Dragonbone Tempering upgraded, what other skills could I get to the maximum proficiency?

Hehehe…

Oops-

I used Side Step to dodge out of the way of another Aerial Dive, then counterattacked with one of my own Iron Claws.

It dodged, of course; we both had Phoenix Reflex, so there was no way for me to hit it without catching it by surprise.

At first, I was annoyed, thinking that it would take a lot of time to defeat it, and it would have been a waste.

But now… Well, there was definitely a lot to be gained by fighting myself!

And I was all in for it.

First, the most important skill would be Phoenix Reflex. There were just too many situations where it would be useful, and times when it saved my life.

But the clone of me had to cooperate, so it would only hit with physical attacks.

Thankfully, it seemed that it only used the same ‘tier’ of attacks as me, so as long as I didn’t provoke it with fire spells, it should be fine.

Argh, but time was so short; if only I could stay here forever and upgrade all of my skills, what better training could I have than myself?

I wanted to scream!

Paralyzing Screech!

Oh, that was a mistake.

The clone of me also screeched, and for the first time in my life, I’d sensed the effects of that skill, and… It wasn’t nice, not at all.

At least we both got that off our chest, and just in time because the clone charged at me again.

Clack!

I got sent flying backward, but the Dragonbone Tempering skill and Pain Tolerance skill alleviated a lot of the pain.

And yes, that was yet another skill I could upgrade, since I was doing enough damage to myself to actually feel the pain, but not enough to kill me straight away.

And so, I’d started a very strange dance of getting hit, dodging, getting hit, dodging, getting hit… and so on.

When I had to regenerate my health points, I would dodge, training my Phoenix Reflex skill, and when I was back at full, I would get hit, training my Pain Tolerance skill.

Time passed like that, but I knew that I couldn’t have a standstill forever.

After a certain attack, I turned around and jumped, turning my tail feathers almost solid as I executed a perfectly timed Tail Whip on the thing.

It was a skill I hadn’t used in a long time, and hence, it was lagging.

The clone fell backward, unable to dodge it in time, but rushed back right away, closing the gap with Side Step, and hitting me with Tail Whip as well.

[Congratulations!]

[Skill: Pain Tolerance(Expert) has ranked up to Pain Tolerance(Master)!]

Finally!
