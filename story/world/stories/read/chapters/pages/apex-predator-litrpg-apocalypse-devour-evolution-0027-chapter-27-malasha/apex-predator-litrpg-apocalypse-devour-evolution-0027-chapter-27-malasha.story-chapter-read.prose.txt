Don tried to crane his neck to see who was talking behind him, but the pressure was overwhelming. He slid his hands under his elbows and pushed. It was like trying to push up a a truck, but Don did manage to create some space.

“So fierce…” the woman said from behind him. Don could hear the faint clacks of high heels on the platform.

Don’s Survival Instincts were absolutely blaring. This wasn’t just dangerous, this was certain death that was inescapable.

“Who… are… you…?” Don managed to say.

“That would be a long history lesson, darling,” the voice said. “But I’ll give you the short version. I am Mala’lasha, The Endless Void, The Seventh Prime, The Maw Which Always Feasts, Ender Of Worlds, Matron Saint of Predators and Hunters. I am The Eternal Hungerer.”

Don couldn’t help but to chuckle. “That’s the short version?”

The woman laughed airily. “Believe me, it is. Despite my dashing looks, I am old. And if you are hungry and nigh immortal, you tend to accrue titles.”

“Uhhuh,” Don said between grunts. It seemed that the pressure was mounting. “What do you want?”

“To talk,” she said. “You are very interesting. I am currently watching half of Earth’s tutorials. Around seven hundred thousand of them. There are only a handful of humans even close to how interesting you are.”

“Are you going to keep crushing me against the platform?”

“Ah,” the Hungerer said sweetly. “My apologies. You should have brought it up earlier.”

The pressure released and Don groaned and coughed weakly. Then he got up and turned. What he saw surprised him.

In front of him stood a beautiful woman, inhumanly tall. She was completely white. White of hair, white of skin, clad in a white dress that looked expensive and clung to her feminine form. It left her slender shoulders bare. Her arms were spindly and thin, and she moved with slow and deliberate grace, giving Don a little pose.

Her face was angular and immaculate. No blemish, her skin like porcelain, her lips full, although bereft of color. Only two sinister details revealed monstrosity beneath the veneer.

First were the eyes. There was a strange beauty to them. They were completely black and half hidden behind lazy eyelids. There was a depth as deep as space in them. These were watchful eyes that assessed everything with cool calculation. There was amusement in those eyes, but it did not hide the pure predatory intent.

The second monstrous detail was the mouth. It was too wide. Her features were beautiful, but the wide mouth crossed almost the entire face, giving her a reptilian aspect. This however, somehow fit her face. Her long and graceful neck turned slowly to give Don a full view of her horrible beauty.

“Had enough of an ogle, darling?”

“I…” Don stammered. “What are you?”

“That is a complicated answer,” Mal’asha said lazily.

“You said you are the Seventh Prime. You are an Evolutor, like me?”

A slow grin spread on Mal’asha’s wide mouth, revealing hundreds of tiny sharp teeth. “I like a man who listens. However, calling me an Evolutor is like calling a black hole heavy. Technically true, but woefully inaccurate.”

Don recounted what Mal’asha had said so far as she watched her with those eyes like abyss, a small smile playing at her pale lips.

“You’re that crackpots god,” Don said.

“Ah yes, Jacques,” Mal’asha said. “He is also very interesting. It is because of you two, that my attention is dragged here. You are both just so… perfect in your own ways.”

“So you pitted us against each other?” Don more stated than asked.

“It was quite easy,” Mal’asha said and started slowly circling Don, like a shark to a stranded ship. Her white heels clacked on the wooden platform. “I merely promised him my favor. He could become an Evolutor, just like you and me, if he defeats you.”

Don felt a chill when Mal’asha circled behind his back out of his view. He wouldn’t give her the pleasure of showing his discomfort and turning.

“So you’re collecting champions?”

“Of course I am. We all are…” Mal’asha said and stopped behind Don. “You are remarkably calm, to be in the presence of someone as powerful and brutal as me.”

Don shrugged. “If you want to kill me, I can’t do much about it.”

“How very true,” Mal’asha said. Then she immediately appeared behind Don, her spindly hand cradling his neck, her breath hot in his ear. “But you will find I can be nurturing too…”

Don let out a shuddering breath. Reflexively he flicked his claws out.

Mal’asha untangled and reappeared in front of him. She laughed and watched him with twinkling black eyes.

“So fierce. I like you Don Damon. But those primitive weapons will be of little use against me.”

“It’s the best I got.”

“So far…” Mal’asha said. “But that is why you asked for help. To explore your options.”

A black screen with white text appeared in front of her and she flicked through it. “Ah, yes. You have been presented with very interesting options. Both the Dominator and Reaper are excellent evolutions. I am surprised the Sixth allowed you the option. But you are indeed special.”

“You’re the Seventh, right?” Don said. “So you offered me the Dominator.”

“Clever little beast,” Mal’asha purred.

“Don’t call me that.”

“Clever, little or beast?”

“Don. My name is Don.

“Very well, Don. I will allow it, but you must call me Mal.”

“Mal…?”

Mal’asha smiled. This one was warm and genuine. It looked somehow uncanny on her face. “I like that.“

There was a moment of awkward silence. Don just stood there, mostly confused. He was still afraid, but it seemed like this eerie god of predators was surprisingly… human.

Looking at Jacques and her, it seems like the System really favors crazy people. What does that make me… I seem to be a crazy-magnet if nothing else.

Mal’asha moved with liquid grace and in a moment she was next to Don, leaning a spindly hand on his shoulder. She smelled of something otherworldly. It was intoxicating, but subtly so.

“Now then,” she said. “Let us focus on your question. You wanted to know more from your racial evolution options.”

The black screen Mal’asha had tapped before appeared before them, as well as a very expensive looking large red sofa. Mal’asha pulled Don next to her on the sofa between velvet pillows with golden tassels.

“I have arranged a bit of a show, if you don’t mind, darling,” she purred. “I have to say, I was wondering if you will ever accept help.”

“Couldn’t you have just imposed yourself on me like you literally just did?” Don asked.

She laughed a throaty low laugh. “How uncouth of me. But you will find me pushy and will have to live with it. As for your question, no. There are certain rules even gods must abide. You are so headstrong, Don. It is remarkable. The pleas for help from humans are constant. Either they ask the System for help, or their old gods, or anyone who is willing to listen. Gods can answer if they so choose, but only after help is requested.”

“Huh,” Don said. “Never thought much of it. I just figured the System is what it is, and I have to deal with it.”

“Quite so,” Mal’asha said and smiled. “Remarkable. Where as Jacques is devoted to the hunt,  you have a far more rarer trait than devotion.”

“Yeah?” Don said. “My winning smile.”

“You do not smile, darling,” Mal’asha said and pouted. “At all.”

“I’ll try to remedy that,” Don said dryly.

Mal’asha laughed. “I should hope so. But as for your trait. You are what it truly means to be adaptable. You don’t have an ounce of quit in you. You don’t ask if you can do something. You ask how you can do it. And then you execute. There is no hesitation in you, and that is something I ravenously want…”

Don gave Mal’asha a sideglance, but she only kept smiling and staring into Don with those black abysses of hers.

“Now then,” she said. “Enough play. Observe.”

The screen floating in the air flicked to life. It showed the top-down view of an arena, like a colosseum. The floor was sand and some scant vegetation, but there were some conspicuous bushes here and there, as if for cover. There were also crumbled buildings here and there. The arena resembled a small village next to a forest, a mix of urban and nature.

Mal’asha spoke a command in some unknown language, full of hisses and clicks. Then two platforms opened up from the ground. On top of them were creatures, who Don instinctively recognized as Evolutors.

One of them was four legged, black as ebony, with strange green tubing surrounding its body. It was large and muscular, like an oversized panther. On its snout was a horn and the tip was glistening with a green toxin. The skin looked bulky and blocky, like the beast had steel platings studded under its skin. It moved with deliberate slowness, clearly displaying great physical strength.

The other creature was more slender, and completely white. It vaguely resembled xenomorph from the Alien-franchise. Large head sprouting backwards in a tube. Skinny, wiry musculature. Four black eyes that Don immediately recognized. There was the same kind of lithe grace in its movements as Mal’asha had. Instead of moving, it rose in the air and a faint bubble of white energy surrounded it.

“The white one,” Mal’asha said nodding at it. “One of my offspring. The Dominator type.”

“What is this?” Don asked.

“A demonstration,” she said. “Between a level 100 Dominator and a level 100 Reaper. Care to place a wager?”

“I’m absolutely not betting against you,” Don said immediately.

“How deliciously smart…” Mal’asha said. Then she turned towards the screen to issue a command.

She spoke a clipped hiss, which Don could only assume meant a single thing. “Begin.”
