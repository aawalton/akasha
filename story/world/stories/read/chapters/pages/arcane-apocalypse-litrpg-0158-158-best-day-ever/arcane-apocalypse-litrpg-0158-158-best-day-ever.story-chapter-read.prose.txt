Mia truly was spoiled for choice when she could finally get started on really consolidating her gains so far. Ten empty Spellbook pages she may have had, but she still had to decide what to prioritise first. She could only absorb a single rune at a time, and she also had to go through all her Rank 0 spells to upgrade their runes to Rank 1.

With Spirit Bond and Spirits of Duality both added to her Class Skill, she finally felt like she didn’t have to hold back so much. After all, her runic model was practically useless while it was digesting a rune, so it would have been stupid to absorb new ones when she could expect to be ambushed or get into combat in the near future. But with her new Subskill and with Gabe’s addition to their temporary home’s list of protection, she finally felt safe enough to keep eating and upgrading runes throughout the day.

Mage Armour was the first of her new spells, already at Rank 1, although without any of the Rank 1 extra enhancements added to its spell circle. With her witchy hat worn atop her head, she’d gotten that spell in a little over a day.

But still, Mia had made the perfectly reasonable and rational decision to first get a handle on using only her new Subskills in combat by having a spar. Maybe she had some ulterior motives, like showing off her awesome new pseudo-spirits. Although she’d be lying if she said she didn’t enjoy sparring with Camie.

Even now, as they stood across from each other in an old school gymnasium, a large hall the size of a football field, she could feel her heart thundering against her ribcage as a large grin crept up onto her face. The way her pretty vampiress was looking at her, those glowing crimson eyes narrowed into slits. Camie’s unfairly curvy body coiled up like a spring, her entire being radiating a dangerous aura of a predator getting ready to pounce, all of that focused solely on Mia as nobody and nothing else in the world mattered … it did things to her. Good, wonderful things.

“You look … better,” Mia mused aloud, giving her girlfriend a long look while trying to ignore the way her stomach fluttered just from those crimson orbs gazing at her with such focus. “More relaxed, ever since the Rank-Up.”

“It’s been good to me,” Camie said, a radiant smile lighting up her face. “We never actually got to talk about the Traits I chose, did we?”

Mia’s cheeks gained a new heat, making her scratch at them awkwardly. She did recall rambling to Camie and Lina about her awesome, wonderful new Traits before the Ritual, talking their ears off during breakfast. They didn’t have time in the end for someone else to also list what they chose, ending up just debating about spells and magic in the end.

“No,” Mia said after a moment, making the vampiress’ smile turn rueful.

“I got nothing that will make me all that much more powerful, nothing like most of the Traits you got,” Camie said. “Instead … I bought everything that would give me more control over myself and my stupid vampiric instincts.”

“Really?” Mia asked, her eyes widening before she grinned and hopped over to Camie, wrapping the taller girl up in a hug. “I’m happy for you. It shows, too, that you look much more relaxed now.”

“I am,” Camie said, softly returning the hug and placing her chin atop Mia’s head. Her arms tightened around Mia, the embrace growing firmer, a bit more possessive, too, making Mia grin. “Wanna see the Traits?”

“Sure,” Mia said, raising an eyebrow in curiosity.

“I wrote them down for you,” Camie said with a smile, grabbing a small notebook from her back pocket. Mia took the small notebook and quickly skimmed through it, her expectant expression falling as she read, giving way to growing horror.

“What … are these?” Mia said, her voice quivering with an emotion she couldn’t name as her eyes lingered on the second set of Traits. Was it horror? Anger? A mix of both or neither?

“Probably why vampires are loathed and feared in equal measure in the Mystic Realm,” Camie said, sounding tired and annoyed in equal measure. “I thought you should know. Any vampire you meet could have these.”

“I see,” Mia said, shaking off the horror of it all. This first of the not chosen Traits explained quite handily why any vampire would want to kidnap her and drain her dry: so they could steal some of her Bloodline Traits. The rest? Those made her wary. Her mana was good at resisting magic that affected the mind, but she didn’t know whether it could help her resist everything that ‘Hypnotic Aura - Fae’ could throw at her. “Thank you. For sharing these with me.”

“I just want you to be safe,” Camie murmured into her hair, her voice a bit muffled. “But to get back to our previous topic, besides being relaxed, there is also this … expectation. I think I’m ready.”

“Ready?” Mia mumbled.

“For more,” Camie whispered, and Mia could almost hear the blush in her voice. Embarrassment warred with something much more delicious, a mix of hope, expectation, affection, hunger, and maybe even a bit of lust. No, that was more than a bit of lust. “I got three Traits, all of them very powerful, and all of them are focused on enhancing my Willpower and making my instincts more controllable. I’m finally starting to feel human again and not-“

Camie’s voice hitched, the final word refusing to come out as her body shuddered. Mia’s arms tightened around her waist, knowing exactly what Camie was thinking, having heard her mumble in her sleep sometimes and being able to read between the lines.

A monster. A bloodthirsty beast. A wanton murderer. There were many more, all of them titles Camie had used before to describe most of her ancestors whose Bloodline memories she’d been forced to witness. Mia had always been able to hear the unsaid parts. Carmilla had been terrified she’d turn out just like them, despite fighting tooth and nail to avoid that fate.

Mia didn’t have the words to console her girlfriend. But it didn’t feel like it was needed, anyway. Not this time. Mia, having constantly shown only trust and affection to the vampire, always being there for her when she needed it, always there to listen when she needed to vent or a shoulder to cry on, was the best she could do.

“Thank you,” Camie whispered softly, her voice quivering with emotion. “You’ve always been so good to me, and I can finally give you what you want?”

Mia blushed, her stomach doing happy little flip-flops. But there was a slight problem.

“What I want?” Mia whispered, surprising even herself with how … sultry she sounded, almost like a purr. But it also carried a dangerous note.

Camie froze, making Mia wiggle out of her hold just enough to tilt her face up to stare into the silly vampire’s confused eyes.

“If you don't want to do something, even if you aren’t worried about your instincts, then we won’t do it. You don't need any excuses. If you aren’t comfortable with something, that’s reason enough not to do it,” Mia said soothingly, putting on a soft smile. Then her lips snapped into a teasing smirk, seeing the look of obvious denial on her pretty vampiress’ face. “But if that’s not it, then you should be honest.”

“Honest?” Camie squeaked, her face matching the colour of her hair as her eyes fluttered about, looking everywhere but at the petite halvyr grinning up at her. She looked a bit overwhelmed, and considering Mia had been restraining herself a lot, keeping to chaste hugs and cuddling, which Camie initiated most of the time, wasn’t a surprise. Mia hadn’t really been actively flirting before, not really. She’d made sure to act more like a little sister, or maybe a best friend to Camie, keeping her hands to herself and her gaze from wandering too much.

“Honest,” Mia said, then stood on her toes, tilting her face just so she was staring into those beautiful ruby eyes from inches away. Their faces were so close she could feel Camie’s hot breath on her lips. “It’s not just about being able to give me what I want. It's about you being able to finally do what you want to me.”

If Mia were being honest, a part of her felt worried Camie had nothing like that. The girl was still adjusting, just acting the part of the ‘girlfriend’ to stay close to Mia, too afraid to ask to just be friends in case Mia threw her out if she did. She wouldn’t, but Mia knew that emotions didn’t really do logic.

So when Camie’s blush went nuclear and her arms around Mia’s waist tightened, her eyes glinting with something hungry, Mia grinned, relief flooding her being. That look was unmistakable, and the hunger she saw had nothing to do with her blood.

“You-“ Camie almost growled, then her magic flared, the energy Mia had come to recognise as vitality surging up and filling her head. She blinked, the fierceness lessening in her eyes, but not leaving entirely. The vampiress sighed, though there was an unmistakably fond note to it. “You horrible minx.”

“Am I?” Mia giggled gleefully, feeling like she was floating, or maybe on drugs or a sugar rush. “I take it you have something in mind then?”

“Maybe?” Camie said evasively, swallowing a lump in her throat. Her gaze flickered downwards to Mia’s lips, still close enough that she could probably feel her breath on her skin. Her eyes flickered back up, meeting Mia’s gleeful pair of sapphire orbs.

“You can have it,” Mia said, then slipped out of Camie’s grasp as the vampiress went stiff as a board. “Once you catch me! We came here to spar after all, and we might as well set a prize for the winner.”

Camie gave her a slow blink, arms slowly falling to her sides, while Mia hopped away, putting some distance between them while continuing to giggle. Her heart was hammering against her ribcage, thundering away like a war-drum. She never knew she had it in herself to act like that, to flirt so freely and shamelessly.

But she supposed she just hadn’t found someone she felt comfortable enough with before, whom she also wanted so badly. She felt so alive in that moment, and it rose to new heights when Camie pounced, finally giving chase to her fleeing girlfriend.

The silly girl hadn’t even thought to ask what would happen if Mia won, not that such a thing was really a possibility without them having set a time limit, and Mia limiting herself to using only her physical capabilities alongside the White Guardian. Unfortunately, the Black Knight wasn’t really … spar capable. If that thing swung its swords, it was to slaughter its master’s enemies.

Her White Guardian appeared a moment later at her side, then got left behind as Mia continued dashing away. Her agility and overall Body attribute had made her quick, though not nearly quick enough to make this game of catch all that challenging for the vampiress, even if she didn’t resort to Blood Boosting herself to be even faster. Thus, a distraction was needed, which in this case was a two-metre-tall giant wearing heavy plate armour and brandishing a tower shield the size of a large door.

The pseudo-spirit wasn’t that fast, but it still managed to intercept Camie, using its shield to swat the vampire trying to leap over its head out of the air. The vampire gave a yelp of surprise, probably having seen the White Guardian’s speed and deemed it too slow to react to her leaping over it.

Mia grinned, dismissing the pseudo-spirit with a thought as she stood on the other side of the gymnasium from where Camie was prying herself out of a Camie-shaped indent in the wall. Damn, that spirit packed a punch, and it had something about it that made it unnaturally good at intercepting anything that would seek to harm its master.

Camie huffed, giving Mia a dour look as she dusted herself off, then pounced again, crossing a dozen metres in a second as her backup Vitality burned through her body.

Mia summoned the White Guardian to her side once more, and a mental prompt had it swing its tower shield just so Mia managed to springboard off of it, leaping over the startled vampiress who crashed through the dissipating pseudo-spirit’s afterimage a moment later.

Mia landed on her feet, then broke her momentum with a roll, her greater flexibility and agility making such basic acrobatics easy to learn. She bounced back to her feet, her pseudo-spirit appearing once more, and a single step of its stupidly large legs took it right in front of Mia just in time to intercept a blob of Blood Magic. Probably Camie’s attempt at a non-lethal version of her magic. Did she somehow sabotage a Blood Bolt spellcircle to make that thing?

A faint nudge came back her way from the White Guardian, and Mia followed the guidance despite her shock. Wasn’t this thing supposed to be lacking any sort of intelligence? Then why could it nudge her to take a step to the side?

Camie came swinging around the tower shield, slipping under a white gauntleted fist primed to knock her away. It could have taken off her head, but Mia’s subconscious influenced the pseudo-spirit, making it hold back from doing anything that could cause permanent harm. One of her arms snapped out to grab Mia, but fell just a few centimetres short, her fingertips barely missing the grinning halvyr’s shirt.

Had she not followed that nudge, Camie would have had her wrist in a vice by now, and it would have been game over from then on. Another nudge came, and Mia followed it too, marvelling at the way she and the White Guardian swapped places, their feet moving in perfect synchrony as if they were a pair of dancers perfectly in tune with each other.

The White Guardian’s arm met Camie halfway as the vampiress came charging right back in, its white gauntlet snatching the redhead off her feet by the scruff of her neck. Mia locked eyes with her girlfriend, her lips peeled back into a toothy grin as those crimson eyes widened, somewhere between surprise at being caught and indignation at being treated like a feral cat.

Her hands were once again almost able to grab Mia, but then the White Guardian moved, taking a step forward, and then it chucked Camie away with an underhand throw that nonetheless had the redhead fly through the sky with an indignant squawk. She flailed a bit at the top of her perfect parabolic arc, nearly four meters from the floor, twisting her body around in a distinctly feline way, making it so she landed on her feet.

Their little game of cat and mouse continued for minutes, five, then ten, and it wasn’t stopping even as they crossed the quarter-hour mark. Mia’s growing exhaustion from the constant dodging, running and adrenaline was offset by her getting more and more comfortable following the White Guardian’s little nudges. Her Body stat increased her stamina, but it was still finite. Her pseudo-spirit didn’t have a conscious mind or intelligence, but it had skill and experience in spades and was powered by an unyielding will. These little nudges were its way of sharing some of that skill with her. To protect her better.

Unfortunately, she doubted that the Black Knight would have anything similar. That thing was a human-shaped, psychopathic murder-blender leashed to Mia’s will. Maybe if she found something, it couldn’t be defeated without her help … thoughts for later.

But that wasn’t enough. The White Guardian could only offer suggestions; it couldn’t take over her body, couldn’t truly give her its own skills. It was Mia’s job to follow through, to mimic the movements the pseudo-spirit showed her … and she eventually stumbled. It was the exhaustion; her feet were dragging a bit more than they should have, even though she had cut down on the more acrobatic movements a few minutes ago. Her shoe caught on something, and she nearly face-planted.

The White Guardian caught her, but in doing so, it ignored Carmilla for a moment, and that was enough for the vampiress to stab a spear of blood magic strength through its head.

The White Guardian stiffened, its arms lowering Mia to the ground with janky movements, and then its form dissipated. The pseudo-spirit snapped back into its place, stuck to the side of Mia’s Spirit like an overstretched rubber band, making Mia twitch from momentary phantom pain. It would need a bit of time to recover, maybe a few minutes, or up to an hour, based on the vague feeling she got.

“Got you,” Carmilla said, grinning as she stood over Mia’s collapsed form. She was flushed, panting lightly, her crimson hair now a wild mane and her clothes ruffled all around. Mia didn’t look any better, though she was breathing much more heavily. She leaned down, scooping Mia up into her arms.

“You did,” Mia said, giggling softly as she rested her head on Camie’s shoulder. She sent the taller girl a teasing, if tired, grin. “So then, what lewd act of depravity am I to be subjected to?”

The way Camie’s cheeks turned pink, then a rosy red, was absolutely delightful. Mia wasn’t worried. She knew Camie; whatever she was going to ask for would be stupidly wholesome. There was no way she would ask for anything more depraved than some extremely vanilla stuff.

“A … kiss?” Camie said uncertainly, her eyes unable to meet Mia’s own as she fidgeted shyly despite holding the halvyr in her arms. Mia smiled, happy to be proven correct so quickly. “A proper one.”

“French?” Mia hummed, her heart racing with excitement. “Tongue and all?”

“Eh-“ Camie stared, taking a moment to process the question, then she gave a slow, extremely hesitant nod. Her eyes were fixed on Mia’s face, searching for even an iota of dissatisfaction. They found none.

“Let me down,” Mia requested, and it was promptly fulfilled. She turned to Camie, arms wrapping around her waist as she stood on the tips of her toes to whisper. “I’ve never done it before, but I’m more than willing.”

A proper kiss, to be exact. She was a bit too old not to have had her first kiss, especially since Mark and Gabe had made it their mission in the early years of high school to get Mia a girlfriend, always acting like her wingmen. It never really worked out, but she had a few clumsy, chaste kisses here and there. Though she was a teenager back then, and far from sober on most of those occasions.

Camie gave a jerky nod, eyes wide; she even forgot to breathe as her arms came to rest on Mia’s shoulders. Mia tilted her face up, eyes closing just a moment before her lips pressed against something soft and warm. She failed to fully suppress the grin that threatened to break out on her face as she felt Camie go stiff as a board.

Mia could both hear and feel Camie’s thundering heart threatening to hammer its way out of the vampiress’ chest. The hands resting on her shoulder tightened, then went loose, those arms wrapping around her neck instead to pull her in deeper.

Mia felt fingers slide into her hair, holding the back of her head as those soft, velvety lips parted. This was nothing like any kiss she’s ever had before. Comparing those empty things, void of any emotion other than some lust, to this was impossible. This was a proof, a proof of her feelings, and of Camie’s feelings for her. This was a way to communicate without words.

It was a slow, clumsy thing. For one of them, this was probably their first kiss, and for the other, it was the first proper one. They made up for the lack of experience with enthusiasm, and they both had plenty of that.

By the time they parted, Mia was panting, slightly delirious and dazed from both the lack of air and the chaotic swell of emotions swirling in her heart. It was overwhelming, and she had the urge to both cry and laugh, though both came from a place of so much joy her body just couldn’t figure out what to do with it, or how to express it all.

On the other hand, Camie looked numb, as if she were in shock. Her eyes were glazed over, and she touched her lips absently, only for a radiant smile to light up her face a moment later.

This, Mia decided, was the best day of her life.

So far. She promised herself.
