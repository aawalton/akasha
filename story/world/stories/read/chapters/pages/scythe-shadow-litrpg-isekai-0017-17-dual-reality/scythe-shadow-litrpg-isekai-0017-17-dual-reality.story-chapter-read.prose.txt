I forced a laugh, but went a little overboard trying to pretend and actually did hurt my ribs this time.

“I’ll be fine," I said, changing the subject, not wanting to think about my adoptive parents or my past. "I don’t think the injuries are as bad as they seem.”

They certainly weren’t as bad as they should have been if I'd truly been attacked by that lion zombie beast. God, that felt like ages ago at this point.

Avery rested her cheek on my shoulder. I leaned in to her warmth and let my head fall on top of hers, stray strands of hair tickling my lips.

“I’m just glad you’re alright,” she said. “I was worried.”

“I know. I’m sorry.”

She placed a hand on my chest, being attentive to avoid my ribs this time. “I love you.”

“I love you, too.” My breathing finally calmed and heart slowed to match. I took a deep breath. It was going to be okay.

I can do this. I can live like this and in Penumbra. Calliope said she'd been doing it for fifteen years. Jericho even longer. If they could do it, so could I.

The door opened again and a nurse entered the room. She stood shorter than any of us, but stocky as hell. Looked like she could have been an MMA fighter during her off hours, could lift a car over her head with a little pump of adrenaline. Her eyes lit up when she saw me, then shifted to Avery.

“Oh, thank the good Lord,” the nurse said. Her voice was high pitched, not at all what I’d been expecting. “I needed some good news this morning.”

Avery ran across the room to the woman, and they embraced briefly. “Joy, why are you still here?”

Joy? A nurse named Joy? As in… Nurse Joy?

“I had to know what happened. My flight’s not until the afternoon, anyhow. The whole floor is rooting for you two. And Derek, of course.”

My brother waved. “Just happy to be acknowledged.”

Joy avoided eye contact with him and shifted her feet, then straightened up and grabbed a clipboard from the end of my bed.

I glared at Derek and gave him a look that I hoped would say, ‘ARE YOU FUCKING KIDDING ME RIGHT NOW?’

He shrugged with a sly smile.

“Miles,” Avery said. She was at my side again. “Nurse Joy was looking after you all day yesterday."

Great to know my nurse had her mind completely on her patient while she looked after me…

Nurse Joy asked me some questions about what happened and took notes. I left out the part about a monster attacking me and added false detail to match what Derek had told me. Avery sat, listening intently to my every word.

How easy the lie came. Even as the three of them stared at me.

Nurse Joy shined a light in my eyes and confirmed I had sustained a concussion and minor skull fracture from my fall, but that it should heal fine.

“You might experience nausea, dizziness, some vomiting,” she said. “Depending on the severity, hallucinations and lucid dreams. We usually only see those things in cases where there has been damage to the brain. You seem fine so far, but you were unconscious for quite a while, so we’ll get you in for an MRI this morning.”

“Hallucinations?” I asked.

“I don’t mean to scare you. Just want to inform you of all the symptoms you might experience so you’re aware.”

It wasn’t fear I was feeling.

Could it all have been a dream? A hallucination? The Reapers. The Umbral Plane. Jericho. The Sins. I've give-up any hope of that.

The nurse said a few more things, but I was hardly listening. She placed a barf bucket on the table beside me, just in case. Avery answered some questions for me.

This was an out. I didn’t want brain damage, but…

I paused.

What did I want?

I kept trying to find reasons for Penumbra to be imagined, but why? If there truly were Reapers constantly fighting against sin to improve humanity, wasn't that a good thing? It meant there was hope. The world wasn't destined to get darker, but brighter. And I could be a part of that solution. Maybe this was my chance for recompense. I could directly help make the world a better place. For everyone.

I'd still have my life with Avery. All it would cost me was a little sleep.

I'd been selfish, imagining this a nightmare because it interrupted my life. But it was so much more.

I have to know that it's real for sure though. It would be an agonizing wait until I could sleep again. Would it be weird to ask for more sedatives? They’d probably put me on some addiction watch list.

Nurse Joy had finished speaking and was already at the door before I noticed. "If all goes well today," she said. "We can have you home this evening." She smiled and left the room.

Avery leaned in to me. "Isn't she the best?"

I grunted, glaring at Derek, but he just grinned and got up from his chair. "I'll give you two some privacy."

Before the door even fully shut behind him, Avery was already climbing into the tiny hospital bed beside me.

"I'm glad you're okay," she whispered.

I put an arm around her. "Me too. Thanks for coming all the way here to be with me."

She settled in, resting her head on my shoulder. “You know… before we met, I never thought I’d be a relationship person. I didn’t like the idea of being tied down to one place, one person. I wanted to explore everything life had to offer. That first night of college, Veronica and I stayed up til three devising plans, dreaming of what could be.”

I chuckled softly. “Well, Veronica certainly lived up to part of that plan. How many guys did she sleep with in college?”

“And don’t forget the girls,” Avery added, a smile in her voice.

“Oh, how could I… Remember that time we were supposed to be doing the psychology homework together and instead she and what’s-her-face spent the entire time making out up on my bunk?”

She laughed, a sweet sound that always made my heart flutter, pushed the dark thoughts into the shadows. “Oh, honey… They were doing far more than making out.”

My eyes widened. “I didn’t even change those sheets for like a month afterwards!”

Avery squeezed my arm. “I’m teasing. But my point is, I didn’t think I wanted… this.” Her eyes met mine. “I thought I’d backpack across every continent. Conquer the world. All by myself.”

“I didn’t know that.”

She shook her head. “I don’t regret it. Not a single bit. I just didn’t know I wanted this more.”

I pulled her in closer. Taking in her scent, the feel of her against me. It was calming.

“You know, there's nothing stopping us from doing both. We’ll conquer the world together,” I said.

We lay there for some time in silence, simply enjoying one another’s presence.

This.

This was what I needed right now.

No worrying. No passion. No thoughts outside of this moment. Just comfort and peace. I loved this woman and she loved me. My fiancé. I could still hardly believe it. I didn’t deserve her. Her gentle soul, her kindness, and caring for all things.

We were happy. We would still be happy. Nothing had changed. I'd just be a slumbering vigilante. Ridding humanity of sin. Making the world a better place for us. For our eventual family.

That was something I could do.

Calliope had said to partition myself. Keep Zephyr in Penumbra and Miles in Vitria. But that was wrong. This was the life that made it all worth it.

It'll be Miles' life that Zephyr fights for.

After a day or being poked and prodded by doctors, I was finally cleared to leave and Avery drove me back to our apartment. I spent the car ride contacting all my friends and letting them know I was alright.

By the time we parked it was already dark. Gratefully, Avery didn't baby me too much. She had an uncanny knack for knowing when to help and when to give space.

I stared at the bed, Avery already snuggled under the covers. A thrill ran up my spine. A mix of anxiety and anticipation. I'd be back in Penumbra shortly after closing my eyes. This would be the first time I went back by choice, knowing what awaited me.

"It won't bite, you know," Avery said, pulling my side of the covers over.

"You never know. I heard one of those electronic bed frames folded right in half, snapped someone up like a crocodile while they were sleeping."

Maybe a little too reminiscent of my misadventures with a zombified lion beast at the lake.

"Ooh, now I'm really worried."

"You should be." I climbed into bed beside her. "You never know what will come get you in the night."

She melted into my shoulder. "Good thing you have me to protect you, then."

I kissed her on the forehead and relaxed my body.

It took about ten seconds to fall asleep.
