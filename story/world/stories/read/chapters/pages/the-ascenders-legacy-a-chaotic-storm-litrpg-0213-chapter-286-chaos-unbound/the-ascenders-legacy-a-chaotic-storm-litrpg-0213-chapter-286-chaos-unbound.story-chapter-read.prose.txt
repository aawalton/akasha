A battle against him was like a battle against one with foresight, only at a much higher level, because where a time awakened could see only a few seconds or minutes into the future depending on their strength, Sebastian could alter aspects of that future to his favor, navigating probabilities and parallel realities to ensure he came out ahead.

It was a frightening ability, but Aodhán wasn't completely without a plan either.

From the little he had observed, Sebastian thrived on rational, structured combat where cause and effect followed clean lines. If he could muddy those lines—or even better, completely destabilize them—then he could effectively render Sebastian blind.

And he had just the idea for that patternless chaos that would turn Sebastian's ability on its head.
