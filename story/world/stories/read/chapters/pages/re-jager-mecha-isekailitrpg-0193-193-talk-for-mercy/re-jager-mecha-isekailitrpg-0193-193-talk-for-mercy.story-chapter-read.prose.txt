“I am Lieutenant Hans Hoffman of the 1st Anti-Demon Unit, attached to the Ygeian Expeditionary Force. We are here to offer you a chance to parley. Come to our custody, and you shall not be harmed.”

“What are you—”

“I am Lieutenant Hans Hoffman of the 1st Anti-Demon Unit, attached to the Ygeian Expeditionary Force. We are here to offer you a chance to parley. Come to our custody, and you shall not be harmed.”

“Are you mocking me?”

“I am Lieutenant Hans Hoffman of the 1st Anti-Demon Unit, attached to the Ygeian Expeditionary Force. We are here to offer you a chance to parley. Come to our custody, and you shall not be harmed.”

Cowardice couldn’t believe what she was seeing. This thing, this machine, and the man inside it—it was simply advancing toward her like a lifeless creature. Did it not fear death? Pain? What was it?

It was no angel, like the one who nearly killed her earlier, no, this was merely an underling. Yet such boldness…

“Is this…but a professional formality for you?” Cowardice muttered, the pain in her body flaring as she turned fully to face him. “Does my weapon…”

She tried to pull her molten flail, but her muscles ached so much that it was impossible.

“Do I not register an ounce of fear in you?”

“Surrender, and this will end,” the monstrosity demanded before her. “It’s a simple decision, Cowardice.”

“I…I am not a coward.”

“Your knees…”

She narrowed her eyes painfully.

“What of it?”

“It trembles.”

With those words, Cowardice felt her energy collapse, as her body hair, whatever remained of it, rose and a cold tingle shot up her spine.

The 1st Anti-Demon Unit, the Ygeian Expeditionary Force, and this man—Lieutenant Hans Hoffman—they were an immense professional force.

Was it true then, that they who had ascended from the mere frivolities of common mankind, now beaten again by mortals?

On her knees, she stared at the machine as it stopped. Out of it came a young man in uniform she didn’t recognize. He looked at her coldly with his blue eyes, as he brandished a gun resembling that of a short musket, yet jet black.

“If you’re asking about formalities,” the man said, as he aimed his gun at her. “Consider this an extra term once you accept being in our custody.”

“P-please,” she squeaked out. “I surrender.”

A barrage of gunfire struck her, and before she knew it, she was blind to everything.

Then, she found her body taking in shot after shot, more than a hundred bullets, each of them interspaced in waves of thirty rounds each. When all was said and done, she had practically no AP left to resist. After every attack she tanked, especially from the feared ‘Angel of Justice’, now, everything left was gone.

She felt her body collapsing like a pathetic sack of potatoes on the fields, her blood flowing warmly on the ground below.

“You made the right choice, Lady Cowardice.”

Then, she felt her consciousness fading.

It had already been a week since the woman named ‘Calamity of Cowardice’ was captured by mankind. Her arrival leaked to much of the general public rather quickly, even when the Ygeian Expeditionary Forces tried its best to keep the matter hush-hush.

Unfortunately, it almost caused a bout of panic and paranoia in Astelia and the rest of Asfaleia, but the Imperial Army managed to largely subdue the public unrest.

Now, it was decided that the Calamity of Cowardice would be kept on a newly renovated and upgraded fortress—Fort Knox. They had diverted immense resources, equipment, weapons, magical artifacts, and precious specialized mages just for the project of containing one monster.

But it was all worth it. They have already observed her for the past few days. Every time her body tried to heal itself with its powerful demonic abilities, it was unable to do so with the amount of damage their measures inflicted, and continued to inflict.

In other words, she was effectively sealed.
