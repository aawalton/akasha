But it would all end today…

Frost soared for his coronation under the nimbus cloud when the icy peak froze beyond its past, as the bumps on his head finally broke skin and the curved horns of obsidian crowned him, as the raging twister of snow and ice shards complemented his rise. The mountain quivered under their howls, the cracks raced on the ice, but snow had kindled them, and they could not exist without Frost’s accord. Thus, when he glanced down, when he willed it, the beasts crumbled and collapsed into snowflakes, and the peak regained its quiet.

By the time Frost ended his rite and hovered down, his anxiety of heights still making waves in Ewan’s soul, marring his pristine regality, the last potion for the Elementalist subtype had modified Ewan’s heart and etched the spell circuit for his current step—the absolute smoothness between the Anima flowing inside him signed the conclusion of the ‘Heart of Anima’. Now, only the ‘Body of Anima’ remained for the Step-1 Elementalist, and though they’d amassed a lot of blood for its baths, the task still towered over him, for it would be years before its fruition would greet him…

“Nana,” Ewan said, keeping the injector gun away after unloading the empty vial as the last traces of the Lightning-Anima numbed him, arcs crackling on his skin. “Can you make a wing-type artifact for Frost? It doesn’t have to be functional for combat, just make it feel real.”

“Does he still want wings?” Nana asked, glancing at the royal imp who alighted on the icy peak and sauntered down to them, his tail wagging despite his poker face.

“Yeah, his yearning for it became stronger just now,” Ewan said, beckoning Frost into a pat and a stroke with a smile. “Good job.” He petted his crown, and Frost’s tail wagged and wagged, his lips almost tugging into a grin, nigh baring his pearly fangs.

“I’ll need specific feathers and the proper types of bones…,” Nana mumbled, lost in her thoughts, humming, and bobbing her head. “It needs to give him stability in air, so he won't be so anxious anymore… Also speed…”

“What were those twisted beasts, Boss?” Kidd asked, lounging beside the fishpond, tossing the larvae he caught in the forest to feed the fish, and they crowded for him. They ate with relish, so Kidd stared at the larvae in his hand and tried one too. And he retched as he bit…

Sindra…

“Snowdoll,” Ewan said under his breath, raising his hand, as Frost rested in the rune. Snow swirled around him as the Ice-Anima frolicked, and the flakes blended and crammed together to fashion a bear in front that reached his shoulder, and it growled for his command. Its four proper limbs held its intact body, even its eyes gleamed with life—Ewan’s creation far surpassed Frost’s instinctual birth of the distorted existences. Nevertheless, Frost had barely started with the skill, and it would smoothen over time when he acquainted with its usage.
