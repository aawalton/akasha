Ronan held his conjured short-spear at Louri’s throat. The red-skinned demoness had tried to influence him with her laugh, but his mental resistance combined with the effects of his branded amulet allowed him to resist. “Explain why you just tried to manipulate my mind or I’ll finish what I started down there.”

The demoness blinked, then held up both hands. “What are you talking about? I didn’t—” She paused and then sighed. “I can explain. It’s my class. I wasn’t trying to affect your mind.”

Ronan narrowed his eyes, but was willing to give her a chance. It definitely wasn’t because of her looks. “Go on.”

She glanced at the glowing spear. “Impressive skill you have there. I would appreciate it if you didn’t hold it so close to my neck.” Ronan didn’t move an inch. “Fine. Men. My class, as you might have seen, is one that relies on sound and other methods to influence the minds of my opponents. However, when I chose it, I didn’t realise that it would seep into every part of my being. Even my unconscious actions can cause others to fall under my charming spell. That you can resist shows your strength.”

He thought about her response for a moment. Then he let the spear dissipate, causing Louri to let out a sigh and instinctively reach for her neck. “You’re a scary one. You can take one of those idiots’ cells for yourself. Their possessions are yours by right, but I doubt they had anything worth taking. They spent all the money they earned on food and… fun.”

Ronan didn’t need an explanation to know what she meant by that. “That’s an interesting class you have. I haven’t heard of a class causing one’s every action to carry an effect. I might need to research that sort of thing so I’m not caught off guard in future.” He didn’t need to worry about her being a threat even if she did try to actively manipulate him. Ronan was certain that his resistances combined with his amulet would let him resist her charms.

“Well, it has its uses. It helped me to survive in here and get to the position I have now. Who knows what the future holds. What is your name, strong human?” Her twin tails flicked from side to side as she spoke. Ronan felt the subtle tug in his mind, but was able to easily ignore it.

“Ronan. Ronan Steele.”

“Nice to meet you Ronan. I am Louri. I am one of the vice-leaders of the argir faction. The other three seem to be in need of having those positions filled, but I am not sure you would be interested in such a role, am I right?” She tilted her head and smirked as she asked the question.

“Who knows. If they offer me the right price I might join one of them. For now, I need some rest.” He turned his back to her and walked around the balcony, trying to figure out which of the three cells would be most comfortable.

In the end he chose the cell in the middle, the one directly opposite Louri’s. It was a coincidence, but it worked out nicely. Compared to his last cell which had just been a metal box with a hole to do his business, and an uncomfortable bed mat, this one was practically drowning in luxury.

There was an actual bed, complete with some sort of squidgy mattress that moulded to the shape of his body. It was firm in the right places, and soft in others. He almost fell asleep on the spot, but wasn’t certain he was safe yet and held back the urge. There was a proper toilet and even a shower and sink, both with running water. He could even see the leftovers of some sort of meal the previous occupant of the cell had been tucking into before it was killed in the battle royale they’d just had.

Better than all of that, however, was the fact the cell had a door that he could operate from inside. It completely shut off visibility from the outside, and he had to assume that besides himself, only the guards could operate it. That meant he would be able to cultivate freely.

There was one thing he could do without worrying if he would be attacked in the immediate future. The many notifications awaiting his attention blinked in the corner of Ronan’s vision. It was time to reap the rewards of his methodical kills.

I know I’ve earned a similar amount overall in the past, but in one go!? He fell into the rubbery mattress as he read through it again. I’ll have to think carefully about how to spend them.

It worries me that I have yet to receive an upgrade for sorcerer, too. If that is a pattern that will continue with my race… He was at a loss for the first time in a while.

Shelving the choice for the time being, Ronan moved onto the next notification, dismissing the racial evolution menu. He had expected it to be his class evolution, but was surprised to find something new.
