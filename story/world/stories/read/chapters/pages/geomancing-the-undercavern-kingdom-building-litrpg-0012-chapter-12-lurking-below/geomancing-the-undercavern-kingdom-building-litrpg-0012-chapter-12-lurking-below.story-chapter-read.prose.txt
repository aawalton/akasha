Great, just what I needed.

Come on, damn it—just a little more.

I’m out of time.

This monster wasn’t as powerful as the Chimera, but still, he sensed a relatively powerful core pulsing with mana within it.

Carmack couldn’t see a thing. He just had to wait and hope it couldn’t or wouldn’t attempt to break the stone he was buried beneath.

The cores of powerful monsters often grew strong enough to pass down magical traits, and it was clear that this monster had some kind of mana-based senses, along with its screeching spell.

Carmack could sense the feeling for him. It no doubt knew he was there, but was confused.

Thankfully, it doesn’t appear particularly intelligent.

Great. So it appears the beast has decided to stay and keep me company.

This wasn’t what he had planned. Then again… it hadn’t been intelligent enough to figure out where he was.

I suppose another monster core couldn’t hurt, Carmack thought as he began to meditate. Who was he to throw away a fine meal that presented itself to him on a platter?
