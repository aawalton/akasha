The second bloodrune, equally as important as the first; Gift. Blood when freely given, holds great power. Those who must take blood can only enhance the power of their bloodrunes through even greater anguish. A path that leaves you closer to demon than human.

Blood Rituals Of Patari by High Mage Baramal

I stare at the small building. It's the largest house I've ever owned, with two stories, and twelve bedrooms. Surrounding the plot of land is a simple wooden fence. It's not endless, but it's still way more land than I'd ever expected. Space inside a stronghold is at a premium, even if this one didn't have enough people to take up most of it yet.

Trees take up the space inside the fence, a small grove with birds whistling to each other, and squirrels chittering. It surprises me to see the small pray animals so at peace. I think Tanya will like it here.

When I'd asked some of the Vishten about getting a new home, they'd been extremely confused. It turns out, they'd already planned on gifting me this place.

I raise my hand, channeling the skill I gained as a Lord of Kolas's Stronghold.

Claim Land.

A wave of mana sweeps through the place, stopping at the simple fence, then dispersing.

I lower my hand, then open the same screen I'd had in Stronghold Deragun.

Lord Of Kolas’s Stronghold: Leo Grayson

Kolas’s Stronghold’s Claimed Land: 2 Rooms, 1 Estate

Stronghold Deragun’s Claimed Land: None

Vassals:

Keve, Level 32 Warrior

Daron, Level 28 Scout

Grildor, Level 39 Warrior

Tanya, Level 21 Unique

Shar, Level 23 Scout

I'm careful to not mess with any of my vassal's names. I don't want to bring up their stats by accident. Instead, I spend a few minutes trying to access my claimed land. Focusing on a name had brought up that person's status, but land doesn't seem to be as straightforward.

Eventually, the screen I'm looking for comes up.

Structure: Leo Grayson's Estate

Defenses: None

I read through the screen twice, doing my best to understand it. The coins are the wrong numbers for my actual assets. No, those are Old World coins that I'd kept out of curiosity, or as gifts. Modern coins aren't made from their actual materials as far as I understand, but rather worked stone. The Talisman paper is interesting, and I wonder why that's the only other old world material it detects on me.

Finally, my gaze goes back to the functions. Focusing on them doesn't bring up any information, and I spend a good few minutes trying before sighing.

My original plan was to have a home for our group, but the other options do have meaning to them. I dismiss Academy out of hand, I would've taken the Owl's Academy if I was interested in that. Fortification sounds useful, but if something makes it past the walls of the Stronghold, I don't see it lasting. Temple as well, is not what I'm looking for.

That leaves me with Guildhall, and Mansion.

My first thought is Mansion. After all, I'm looking for a home. But…

I'm not alone. This isn't just a place for me, or at least, I don't want it to be.

I've heard of an Explorer's Guild, up near one of the northern strongholds. But I'd never had any desire to join. My Master had been extremely against them for a number of reasons, and I trust his judgement on matters like that. The Arcanists Guild definitely hadn't improved my opinion of guilds, but at the same time… The mercenaries are more than just mercenaries to me. I've trusted them with secrets beyond imagining, and they've saved my backside far too many times.

So this decision, I'm not just making for myself, but for my friends.

I select Guildhall.

The mana of the place changes, the Stronghold itself working to make the place something different.

I glance at the upgrades now available to me, only to see most of them costing Mana Stones. I've seen them before, of course, but I had no idea they held any kind of importance. Some of the upgrades require other materials as well, like the coins or talismans, but everything need is at least one Mana Stone. There seems to be three categories of upgrades. Defensive, Structure, and Function. Sadly, none of them are really available to me right now without Mana Stones.

I close the blue screen, and enter our new home.

The main room has a couple tables and chairs set about, with a back kitchen visible through an open door. Exploring the place shows four bedrooms on the main floor, and eight taking up the second floor, all of them fully furnished by the Vishten. There's a small cleared space in the back, with a stream lazily making its way through on the border of the fence. Past the fence, there's just more empty forest.

Being mildly selfish, I take the largest bedroom on the first floor. It has an attached office, which I rather like. Being a guild, I imagine that makes me the Guildmaster.

I'm collecting titles like Tanya collects animals.

Happy with my exploration of the place, I make my way back to the front, and let out a sharp whistle.

Shar comes flying over moments later, landing on one of the trees in the grove. "Could you let the others know they have a new home? I think they'll like it."

Shar flies off.

Everyone settles in rather quickly to the new place, and for the first time, I see almost all of Tanya's animals out and about. There are less reavers than I expected, only three of the big spiderlike creatures. Asking Tanya tells me that she let the rest of them go into the wild. I seriously hope she did that nowhere near here.

Still, the Shadow Horses like the open space, and owls quickly fill the branches above.

Looking at my Upgrades list later shows me that a few more options have appeared in the Structural list with the arrival of my allies, including an Owlery, and something called a Beastmaster's Kennel. I'm about to close the list when another new addition catches my eye.

I glance out the Shuttered window to where Keve meditates with his back against one of the trees. Cultivation is what the book had called nonhuman magic, and I actually do know where to find Spirit Wood, or at least, if Merchant Gerald hasn't sold his entire stock.

The smell of food from the kitchen makes me turn back to the group as they explore the place. An interesting advancement for another time, when I actually have the materials to make it happen.

The extra space for everyone seems to do them good, it'd been a while for all of us since we'd had some privacy, and the cramped space of the other house hadn't helped. Tanya finishes her cooking, and we all eat dinner together, playing some cards before we start turning it in.

Tanya hands me several vials of Circuitry Ink before going to sleep herself.

I take the vials back to the office attached to my room and set them on a small desk. A yawn reminds me of how tired I am, but I ignore it as I stretch my body.

The break is nice, in all honesty. No more undead, demons, or behemoths. For a little while, we get to be a strange group of friends all working towards a common goal.

My gaze lands on the four vials of circuitry ink, then I slide a familiar ring on my finger. Array needs both nature and life aligned circuitry, two alignments I happen to have. So I'll make one vial of each ink.

Infuse Circuitry Ink.

My profession's newest skill activates, draining my mana as the color of the circuitry ink starts to subtly change white.

The skill finishes, and I lift up the bottle to study it. I can't identify it, since it's technically not an Artifact, but I think the change worked.

I pull off the ring, then place the Gardener's Gloves on as I grab the other bottle. This time, the ink takes on a green hue. Another success, I think.

I take off the gloves as I look at the last two bottles.

Then I hesitate for several seconds, and grab a bottle.

My own, hazel magic flows into the bottle, changing it and infusing it with whatever alignment my natural magic has.

The ink turns fully hazel, taking to my mana in a way it hadn't taken to the other alignments. I hold the bottle up when I'm finished, studying the strange color.

A knock on the door brings me out of my thoughts.

I stand up, making my way over.

I open the door to find Eyla standing there.

She smiles when she sees me, her face relaxing with the emotion.

"Can I come in?"

I blink in surprise, then nod, opening the door fully for her. She looks around the mostly empty room. My backpack sits next to the desk, still packed, and the four vials of ink are the only objects out. Any other personal items I have are on Jasper, or carefully held by Merchant Gerald.

I move past Eyla, suddenly a little nervous about having her here. "What brings you over here?"

She watches me, trying to hide a smile as I start putting the vials up on a shelf, mostly to keep myself busy.

"We haven't talked much since we got here. And I get the feeling that if I let you hole up in your new room, you'd disappear for days."

I run my hand through my hair, then reach down to my backpack to take out some of the circuitry pens. "Sorry, yeah. I'm used to… being alone."

She leans against the desk, watching me with an unreadable expression. "That's okay."

I pause, then set the pens down on the desk slowly, right next to her. I look up into her eyes, the icy blue staring back at me. Usually, I can read her fairly well, but right now, for some reason, the look in her eyes is a mystery to me.

"I…" I stop, the words not coming to me like I thought they would.

"What's wrong?" She frowns.

"Don't step away." The words come out without me meaning them to.

She tilts her head in confusion.

I clear my throat, then look up to the ceiling, as if Sehara could give me guidance. "I want you near, even if… even if it seems like I'm far away."

The confusion lasts for a second before it melts away under a caring look. Slowly, her hand reaches out, gently touching mine where it rests on the table, next to the pens.

I grab the hand tight. For a second, I feel like I'm drowning, but the feel of her soft skin grounds me, bringing me back from a storm of emotions that lies under the surface, always ready to explode, forever trapped. I can't let them out, because if I do, people relying on me die.

She leans towards me, and wraps me in a hug. "I'm not going to step away."

My arms wrap around her lower back, and I close my eyes as my head falls into her shoulder.

"You're never alone." Her voice is soft, gentle.

I breathe, almost on the edge of something dangerous. I can't help myself as I squeeze her tighter.

Her hand runs through my hair, and a feeling of safety overcomes me for the first time in… a long time.

After a few minutes, my grip lessens, and I pull away.

Her hand grabs mine, faster than a snake after its prey. "Come on."

She pulls me into the other room, then onto the bed, then we lay there, holding each other until sleep catches up to both of us.
