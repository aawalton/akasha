With the System's arrival 100 years ago, Earth was transformed into a deadly progression realm. Countless wars were waged, millions of lives were lost, and chaos descended. Humanity had lost. The insects had won.

Forced to live out like the insects that had ravaged them in fortified colonies with no connection to the outside world, one S-rank hunter refused to believe they were the only ones to have survived. Other settlements had to exist beyond the endless forest; he was sure of it. They called him mad.

That had been a month ago, when he had led a team of A-ranks beyond the walls. Nobody had survived for more than a week outside, so in a twisted way, that was an accomplishment in itself.

After countless days of slaughtering hordes of insects, they had finally emerged into a silent clearing. To Kain's exhausted team, it was an oasis, a moment of peace. But Kain knew this peace was merely an illusion. They were standing on a graveyard.

Around him, his team moved in the scout's silent aura skill, resulting in no sound coming from their steps.

"How much farther?" whispered Maya, the scout, dark circles shadowing her eyes from days of minimal rest.

"Can't be far now," Kain replied, studying the disturbed vegetation. "The tracks are slowing. We should be coming into an established territory."

A faint vibration tickled the edge of Kain's [Hunter's Sense], a warning only an S-Rank hunter would be able to sense. His lips tightened.

We're not alone.

"Something's close," he murmured, voice low but firm. The others froze in their tracks, weapons drawn and eyes scanning the darkened expanse.

A blue rectangle of text appeared in Kain's vision, bold and cold as it hung in the air before him:

[Alert: Target Acquired – Titan Spider (S-Rank)]

[Distance: 73m]

[Current Danger Level: ???]

[Warning: Anomalous Energy Signature Detected]

His grip tightened on his bow. "It's close. Form up."

The others obeyed instantly, drawing into a tight circle. Kain's eyes flicked toward the surrounding trees, analyzing every shadow and flicker of movement. S-Rank. Even for an S-Rank Hunter like himself, this was no simple beast.

Question marks on its danger level? Damn it, we might have overshot here.

"The System's reading is... fluctuating," muttered Elise, the team's healer, fingers already tracing glowing sigils in the air. "Like it can't properly categorize what we're tracking."

Unlike most hunters, who relied solely on combat instinct, Kain had spent countless nights studying the biological archives, analyzing patterns in apex predator behavior that others dismissed as irrelevant theory. The colony elders had often chastised him for "wasting time on dusty records" when he should be honing his physical skills, but that unconventional knowledge had saved his team more times than he could count. His mind raced through documented weaknesses of arachnid-class entities, calculating optimal strike points even as his body prepared for combat.

Kain's hunter's sense flared to life:

[Critical Weak Point Detected: Third joint on left leg]

[Analysis: Trace elements of extinct Void Weaver lineage detected]

Void Weaver? The term triggered a distant memory—fragmented texts from the earliest days of the Integration, tales of creatures that could manipulate spatial dimensions.

They had all but disappeared in Kain's timeline.

Suddenly, the forest floor beneath them began to change. Silver strands, previously invisible to the naked eye, caught light from above and began to glisten. "We're in its web already," Kain realized aloud. "Not physical strands—it's some kind of energy field. A trap."

"I'll draw its attention," Kain said, nocking an arrow tipped with venom harvested from a Razor Wasp. Preparing a charge for a most vital connection, his shoulders began crackling with power. "Hit the legs if you can. The third joint on the left side shows vulnerability. Don't let it pin you."

The group replied with confident nods. They were professionals; this is what they trained for. In this ecosystem, the Titan Spider stood as more than just the dominant predator— it was the terrifying result of the harsh evolutionary processes employed by the System. The silver threads started to tremble, producing a deep hum barely audible to the human ear, but Kain felt it; he felt it vibrating through his chest cavity. The forest canopy swayed side to side, the branches rustling, and yet there was no wind. Something was up there.

[Spatial Distortion Detected]

A shadow moved; it was faster than the eye could track. Kain shot his arrow into the darkness, but it did not find its mark as there was nothing to hit.

The forest exploded into chaos as the Titan Spider phased into existence, its massive form materializing as though stepping through an invisible doorway. One moment there was nothing but wind-stirred leaves, the next—a nightmare given form. Kain's [Hunter Perception] activated instantly. Time seemed to slow as his enhanced senses cataloged every detail of the creature. His pupils dilated, absorbing available light with unnatural efficiency, mapping the spider's anatomy in milliseconds. Points of weakness flared red in his vision—joint connections, sensory organs, the slight irregularity in its third left leg.

"Defensive formation alpha!" Kain commanded, his voice cutting through the initial panic with practiced authority.

His team responded instantly. Years of hunting together had forged them into a single organism with multiple limbs—each member understanding their role with perfect clarity.

Kain's hands moved with preternatural speed, nocking not one but three arrows simultaneously. His muscles, enhanced by years of system-regulated physical evolution, barely registered the 300-pound draw weight of his composite bow. He released the first arrow—a standard penetrator—then the second and third in such rapid succession they appeared to leave the bow as a single projectile. Each arrow struck with surgical precision: the first penetrating the creature's left sensory cluster, the second finding the gap between armor plates at its thorax junction, the third severing a minor hydraulic channel that controlled its forward-left appendage.

Black ichor sprayed in pressurized jets as the spider released a multi-toned shriek that vibrated the very air. The creature staggered, its left side momentarily compromised by Kain's opening salvo. But instead of retreating, the spider's exoskeleton rippled with unnatural energy. Faint purple light pulsed between its armored plates, and before Kain's eyes, the damage began to reverse—ichor retracting, severed tissue reconnecting.

"It's regenerating!" Kain shouted, already reaching for his quiver. His fingers selected a specialized arrow by touch alone—the shaft notched with identification patterns only an S-Rank hunter could distinguish at speed.

"Elise, suppression field! Derick, engage but maintain five-meter clearance! Maya, circle pattern three!"

Kain leaped backward, his enhanced leg muscles propelling him twenty feet up to a sturdy branch. From this vantage, he activated [Hunter's Focus], his most powerful non-Dao ability. His heartbeat slowed to a crawl, each pulse precisely controlled. Oxygen efficiency in his bloodstream increased by 300%, neural pathways optimized for a single purpose: the perfect shot.

The specialized arrow began to vibrate in his fingers, responding to his focused intent. Unlike standard ammunition, this was a Resonance Arrow—designed to disrupt system-enhanced biological functions through precise vibrational frequencies. The technology represented years of research by the colony's best minds, refined through countless hunting expeditions.

Below him, his team executed his commands with flawless precision. Elise's hands traced complex patterns in the air, creating faintly visible geometric structures that slowed the spider's regeneration. Derick advanced with his shield, each step calculated to draw attention without overcommitting. Maya became a blur of motion, her twin daggers leaving phosphorescent traces in the air as she executed the complex movement pattern designed to confuse the spider's tracking capabilities.

The Titan Spider's legs crashed into the forest floor with spear-like force, creating small craters with each impact. Its crystalline eyes rotated independently, tracking multiple threats with an intelligence that transcended instinct.

Kain exhaled, releasing the Resonance Arrow with perfect form. The projectile cut through the air with a distinctive high-pitched whine, its specialized tip beginning to glow with accumulated energy. It struck precisely where the creature's main neural cluster should be—a target no larger than a walnut, hidden beneath layers of armored exoskeleton.

For a heartbeat, the spider froze completely. The Resonance Arrow's specialized payload detonated inside the creature, sending disruptive waves through its nervous system. Its legs spasmed uncontrollably, mandibles clicking in what might have been pain.

"Now!" Kain roared, already in motion. He dropped from the branch, shouldering his bow mid-fall and drawing the twin hunting knives sheathed across his back. The specialized blades—forged from materials harvested from A-Rank monsters—gleamed with lethal purpose.

He landed on the spider's back, driving both blades deep into the junction points where the legs connected to the thorax. His enhanced strength allowed him to pierce the creature's armored exoskeleton, severing critical nerve clusters with surgical precision. The spider bucked violently, nearly throwing him clear. Purple energy began to emanate from between its armor plates, crackling with spatial distortion.

"It's shifting phase!" Kain warned, executing a perfect backflip off the creature's thorax, one knife still embedded in its flesh, the other in his hand. "Distance, now!"

The air around the spider shimmered as reality itself seemed to warp. The creature's form became partially translucent, limbs disappearing and reappearing at impossible angles. Kain landed in a three-point stance, already calculating their next move. But the spider had adapted with terrifying speed. It phased partially into existence directly above Maya, who was mid-dodge.

A leg materialized through her shoulder, solid reality replacing the space her flesh occupied. Her scream cut short as the spatial disruption tore through muscle and bone.

"Maya!" Elise abandoned her position, rushing toward their wounded comrade.

"Elise, stay back!" Kain commanded, already sprinting to intercept. His enhanced muscles propelled him forward with inhuman speed, but the spider was faster.

It phased again, reappearing directly in Elise's path. Crystalline fangs dripping with purple venom descended toward her exposed neck. Derick hurled himself forward, shield raised to intercept.

Too late.

The spider's mandibles closed around Elise's torso, lifting her struggling form into the air. Her healing sigils flared brilliantly as her Dao fought to counteract the venom flooding her system, but the creature's toxin overwhelmed even her specialized abilities.

Kain reached for his emergency quiver—the three specialized arrows he carried for situations beyond conventional tactics. His fingers closed around the shaft of a Phase Disruptor, a theoretical weapon developed by the colony's research division but never fully tested in field conditions.

"Get clear, Derick!" he shouted, nocking the experimental arrow. The arrowhead began to pulse with an unsettling frequency that made reality itself seem to shudder.

Derick, grief and rage contorting his features, ignored the command. He charged the monster head-on, his warhammer crackling with stored kinetic energy. The blow connected with devastating force, momentarily staggering the spider.

As Kain's Phase Disruptor arrow flew through the air at impressive speed, warping the air around it, victory seemed possible.

But the creature's adaptive intelligence had already analyzed their tactics.

It phased partially out of reality, the hammer and arrow passing harmlessly through its now-incorporeal form. In the same movement, its leg materialized inside Derick's chest cavity.

The tank dropped without a sound, blood pooling beneath him.

The battle turned into a chaotic whirlwind driven by desperation. Kain moved with practiced efficiency, but the spider was relentless, its spatial abilities making conventional combat tactics nearly useless. Maya was still alive, but barely. "S-system's wrong," she whispered, blood bubbling at her lips. "Not... just S-Rank. It's something... else."

Before Kain could respond, the spider phased directly above them, its massive body blocking the light. Multiple legs struck simultaneously, one piercing Maya's already wounded body with lethal precision. One leg smashed Kain backward, his quiver sent flying; his bow became useless.

The spider towered above him with its mandibles clicking in eager anticipation. Kain drew his last weapon, one of the specialized knives he managed to hold on to.

Kain stood alone, his quiver gone, his breath ragged. Blood dripped profusely from a scrape across his torso, but he refused to fall, refused to die.

The purple energy between the beast's plates pulsed rapidly, creating a distortion field that made the creature appear to exist in multiple planes simultaneously.

[System Alert: Connection Unstable]

[Anomaly Detected: Target utilizing unknown System manipulation]

[Warning: ???]

Kain smirked bitterly. "Not today."

He charged forward with a roar while wielding his dagger to strike at the creature's vulnerable spot. The joint glowed red with vulnerability. He moved his body to evade both legs based solely on survival instinct.

For a moment, victory seemed possible—the dagger's tip mere inches from the critical joint.

Then, the spider simply... vanished. Kain's forward momentum resulted in him flying into empty space.

[Spatial Anomaly Detected]

The spider reappeared behind him, its movement impossible by conventional physics. He witnessed only the creature's jaws opening fully as its fangs pierced his chest and released burning toxins into his blood.

His final thought wasn't of fear but bitter irony. All his years studying the archives, all his knowledge of apex predators, and he'd still underestimated what lurked beyond the colony walls.

The elders were wrong, he thought as consciousness slipped away. There are things out here they never prepared us for.

[Life Signs Terminated]

[Commencing Data Archive...]

[Initiating Transfer Protocol...]

Kain's first sensation was cold. He gasped as his lungs seized up, completely losing their ability to function.

His eyes flew open as he lay on moist soil while fresh rain and scorched wood scents filled his nostrils. The sky above him displayed an unsettling violet color while flashes of light moved across the heavens like lightning.

Where the fuck am I?

A blue rectangle appeared before him with a flickering light.

[System Reinitializing…]

[Welcome to the Integration]

His heart raced. The Integration? That was a term he’d only read about in ancient colony records, a moment whispered about in legend—the beginning of the system apocalypse a hundred years before his time—seventy-five years since he was born into the colonies.

[Species: Human - Level: 1]

[Class: None Selected]

[Dao Compatibility: Determining…]

Kain staggered to his feet, his body unsteady and unfamiliar. He glanced down, expecting the calloused, scarred frame of an S-Rank Hunter.

Instead, he saw a lean, unblemished, wiry form, like the body he’d possessed in his youth.

This isn’t my body.

The realization hit like a blow. He had died. He had felt the spider’s fangs pierce his chest, its venom burning through his veins. Yet here he was, alive and whole but impossibly in the past.

[Integration Complete]

The sky rippled as the world seemed to shudder. Screams echoed in the distance, and Kain turned toward the source. People were running in chaos, their faces pale with terror. Trees twisted and grew unnaturally tall, their bark cracking with luminous veins of green. Insects, small and harmless just moments before, began to swell and shift, their forms grotesque and predatory.

A new message appeared:

[New Era Detected]

[New Dao Available]

Before Kain could even begin to process this new information, his thoughts escaped him—

A colossal centipede the size of a small car burst from the undergrowth while its numerous legs churned up the earth in a charge toward him.

Kain's lifetime of hunting experience instantly activated his survival instincts. He dove aside, grabbing a steel construction pole that had snapped during the Integration from the ground as the creature’s mandibles snapped where he had been standing.

The centipede attacked him with its huge mandibles closing, and its black eyes gleamed with predatory spite. As Kain stumbled backward, his heel snagged on a moss-covered log, he dropped his makeshift weapon—his only hope of defense. His body crashed onto the ground while his breath escaped in a harsh gasp as the creature charged toward him, making the chaotic sky disappear behind its massive form. The centipede towered above him; all Kain could see were the enormous mandibles gnashing toward his face.

Kain placed his hands in front of his face in a last gesture of protection against his seemingly inevitable second demise. "GET OFF ME!" Kain wrenched out at the top of his lungs.

Something deep within Kain tore open. Raw and uncontrollable energy exploded from his core. Tendrils of lightning burst from his body with explosive force and struck the centipede as it lunged forward. Electric arcs glowed around the beast while its massive form shone brightly before its armor turned to charcoal, where it burned. The beast fell to the ground, convulsing while the smell of scorched flesh permeated the atmosphere.

[New Dao Discovered: Elemental Hunter – Awakened Lightning Dao]

[Effects: Minor affinity with lightning. Bonus to tracking in storm conditions]

Kain lay still for a moment, staring at his trembling hands as faint tendrils of lightning continued to dance along his fingertips. The moss covered log beside him smoldered, charred beyond recognition.

As his breath steadied, he sat up, his thoughts racing. "Elemental Hunter," he muttered, the words of the system's prompt echoing in his mind. He closed his eyes and focused, piecing together fragments of ancient colony texts he had studied long ago.

The irony wasn't lost on him. In the colonies, children were tested at age five for dao affinity, then systematically channeled into rigid vocational paths based on those results. He couldn't even remember what his original affinity had been—it was deemed irrelevant once he'd been classified as a Hunter.

The colony's resources focused on only a handful of dao paths considered tactically essential; countless others were left to atrophy, their potential diminished through neglect.

Yet here he was, awakening to a Lightning Dao—one of the elemental affinities that colony records spoke of with both reverence and fear. The professors had spoken of the Elemental Hunters—warriors who wielded the forces of nature itself. They were humanity's greatest weapon in the early days of the Integration.

And they had been systematically hunted to extinction.

Kain was a good student; he'd spent many hours with the teachers in his colony, learning about all the ins and outs of the past. Something about the past deeply intrigued Kain, he found it resonated more with him than his time.

While other hunters drilled combat forms, Kain had pored over fragmentary records of pre-Integration society—a world of inexplicable machines, global connectivity, and resources so abundant that people fought wars over abstractions rather than survival.

It had all seemed so alien to him, yet strangely compelling, as though a part of him had always belonged to that vanished world.

Kain’s fists clenched. He had read those stories in awe and sorrow, never imagining he would walk the path of those ancient warriors. But now, with the crackle of lightning still pulsing faintly within him, he realized that the Dao he had awakened—his Dao—might be the key to rewriting history.

A blue rectangle flashed into his vision:

[Level Up! You have reached Level 2!]

The notification brought an unexpected steadiness to Kain’s frayed nerves. His breaths slowed as he processed the words. He was still an F-Rank Elemental Hunter, but advancing even a single level meant something. A sense of progression. Growth. The system wasn’t just rebuilding him—it was offering him a path forward.

Another notification appeared, sharper and more significant:

[Class Advancement Available: Choose a Subset Path]

[Options for Elemental Hunter - Lightning Subclass]

[Stormcaller] – Master the tempest. Harness lightning to perform area-of-effect attacks against multiple enemies. Strong against groups.

[Thunderblade] - Utilize lightning to empower weapon strikes with destructive melee capabilities through the Thunderblade ability. Exceptional speed and agility in close combat.

[Stormstrider] - Use lightning speed to improve your movement capabilities. Gain unparalleled evasion and traversal abilities.

Kain frowned, his analytical mind already at work. Each option carried immense potential but also came with inherent risks. [Stormcaller] offered raw power, but the thought of trading precision for chaos left a bitter taste. [Thunderblade] promised brutal efficiency, but committing to close-range combat was dangerous, especially against creatures like the centipede.

The [Stormstrider] option captured his attention because its speed and adaptability features could protect him in this dangerous and unpredictable world.

Cross-legged on the wet earth, he remained motionless while distant roars and screams continued to fill the air. The subtle hum of lightning coursed through him as a constant reminder of his newly acquired power.

His home's walls had stood tall and unyielding to block the external horrors. As an S-Rank Hunter, he protected his people while exploring beyond the walls to defeat the system's monstrous creations and collect vital resources for the colony.

The colony elders frequently recounted tales of the Integration period, which included chaotic circumstances and desperate situations and how heroes emerged to confront those challenges. The Elemental Hunters stood out as legendary figures because they possessed unmatched control over natural forces.

And yet, their power had made them targets. With their cold, alien intelligence, the insects had systematically hunted them down, eradicating what they perceived as their greatest threat. By the time Kain had been born, the Elemental Hunters were little more than myths.

And now I’ve become one.

Kain’s eyes moved to the constantly changing sky overhead. Jagged forks of lightning ripped through the purple-colored sky. Kain's memories returned to his former existence in the colony, where survival dictated every decision he made. The S-Rank Hunter’s success depended on combining offensive and defensive techniques while utilizing his team’s abilities to enhance his performance. But now? Now, he was alone.

His jaw tightened. "No one is coming to back me up this time."

He returned his focus to the choices before him. Before his death, Kain would have immediately chosen strength over all else, but this time, it would have to be different.

Rationality dictated caution, yet ambition demanded he seize every advantage. [Stormstrider] triggered survival instincts by providing enhanced movement and escape capabilities in this unpredictable environment.

Finally, he exhaled and made his choice:

[Selection Confirmed: [Stormstrider] Your speed and evasion are enhanced. Gain access to Unique Skill: Lightning Dash]

[Lightning Dash]

Move quickly over short distances by harnessing lightning speed. Lightning Dash serves as a tool for fast movement, dodging attacks, or decreasing distance to opponents. The faint feeling of electricity danced around his legs as he tested his new power while standing. Through mental activation he triggered his power and immediately bolted forward covering multiple meters at superhuman speed.

The sensation was exhilarating. The system’s design fused with his instincts and transformed him into an evolved entity.

His thoughts remained shallow as the ground started to shake beneath him. The atmosphere became dense with electric energy that matched the intense power flowing through his body. The violet sky overhead appeared to undulate and wrap back into itself. The earth released trees that grew with impossible speed because their thick twisted trunks supported dense impenetrable canopies. A vast forest emerged quickly before him, extending to the horizon.
