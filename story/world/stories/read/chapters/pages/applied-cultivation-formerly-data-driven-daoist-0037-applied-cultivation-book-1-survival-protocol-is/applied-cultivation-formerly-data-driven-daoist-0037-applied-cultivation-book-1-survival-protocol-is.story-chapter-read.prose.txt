Thank you, all of you, for reading and commenting and theory-crafting and reviewing and for every piece of interaction you have done on Royal Road. If I didn't have so many regular readers, there's no way in hell I would have found the motivation to keep writing. My writing pace is nowhere near fast enough; many authors write full-time and can pump out seven chapters a week. I can't with my full-time job, but I wish I could. If I could write for a living, then that would be a dream come true.

This publication may very well be an important step towards that dream. If it succeeds, I may have to become a full time keyboard-monkey feeding the anons online their daily neurotransmitters.
