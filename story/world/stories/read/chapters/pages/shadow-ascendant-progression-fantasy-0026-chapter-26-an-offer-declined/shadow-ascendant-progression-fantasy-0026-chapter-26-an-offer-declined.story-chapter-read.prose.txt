The battle dome was a strange construct… if I had to assign a single word to describe its purpose, it would probably be show-off. Yes, that’s the word I’d use.

It was a massive circular arena with enough seats to host an audience as large as ten thousand! Ten thousand—yes, the arena could hold the population of a small town within itself. Though that’s not why I call it a show-off.

I mean, Colosseums aren’t that rare, right? You can easily find one in any big city.

The reason I call Cellera’s battle dome a spectacle of show-off is because of that… the flowing hemispherical metallic dome that covered the arena, though incompletely.

Incomplete, yes… I didn’t stutter. The metallic dome was smaller than the wall’s circumference and left a uniform gap all around its boundary.

The heavy dome wasn’t supported by any wall… no, instead it was held aloft by magic—by a sage-class air affinity spell cast by Headmaster Azembor.

The dome wasn’t just an arena; it was a symbol of Cellera’s power. It was built to showcase the school’s advanced status, essentially a giant advertisement. Honestly, I can only imagine how impressed I would’ve been as a kid. It was truly a spectacle.

Upon reaching the center of the arena, we were asked to form a line, keeping two hands’ distance on each side. While the students formed the line, Instructor Serena walked back toward the arena’s main gate and went inside, leaving the students to themselves, and everyone did what they did best… yes, chattering about useless things.

"I really don’t want to learn enhancement magic. I mean, girls aren’t supposed to be ‘wild’ and ‘monstrous,’" Maya—the one I met in the dungeon the other day sobbed.

Her words earned a few giggles from the students nearby, and a few comments too… something I didn’t bother paying attention to as I looked to the left.

Beside me stood Seraphina, chatting with her minions about their previous dungeon expedition, from the sound of it.

"Luring monsters is the way," Seraphina said confidently, brushing a stray strand of violet hair from her face. "Unless the monster’s a Minotaur or hmm… a goblin king. The ones we face are mostly feeble, even a weak fireball could take down two goblins if aimed right." She chuckled.

Luring monsters, huh? The words echoed in my mind as my eyes met the girl beside Seraphina. I tilted my head slightly, now looking down at the grass.

The grass swayed with the soft breeze, it was comforting to see. It would’ve looked far better if not for that damned dome blocking the cloudy day’s light, I thought, staring up at the metallic shell.

My eyes dropped back to normal, staring at the dome wouldn’t blast it off; it was pointless anyway. That’s when I heard footsteps behind me.

I looked back, hoping to see Instructor Serena and our temporary healer, Chloe Valmont. I couldn’t have been more wrong.

Toward me walked Mabel, one hand hiding her giggle while the other hung at her side. Her walking toward me wasn’t the problem, it was Mabel, after all, she’d probably say something weird and leave. Totally not problematic—that is, if not for the person beside her.

Marcus Levile walked with her.

Hmm… maybe they wanted to talk to Seraphina about something? I thought, turning my eyes away. Why would Mabel bring him to talk to me, anyway?

"Hey, Arnos!"

I blinked. Mabel used my name, meaning they were definitely walking toward me… but why? Turning around with a small smile, I murmured, "Hey."

Not sure what else to say.

"Marcus wanted to talk to you," Mabel pointed at him. Doesn’t she know we shouldn’t point at people, especially not royalty!

"I… I’m flattered," I replied, trying to keep my stuttering to a minimum.

I looked toward Marcus. His sharp brown eyes focused on me. He dropped his gaze, then lifted it again. Was he sizing me up? Was he going to offer me a job? As a table or something? I mean, royals are meant to be sadistic, right?

"You don’t have to be so uptight with me, Arnos. I just have something to discuss," Marcus said, giving a small smile as he looked down on me.

And by looking down, I don’t mean looked down on me—no; he was just slightly… okay fine, half a foot taller. What do royals even eat!?

"I am n…not uptug, Lord Levile," I replied.

Wait, did I just say uptug?

"You’re not…" Marcus murmured, his eyes shifting to Mabel next.

I glanced at her too—I mean, she should’ve been mediating this conversation to begin with.

"Pffftt… one second, you two, just—pffftt, haha," she laughed, both hands covering her mouth as if hiding something.

"It’s not funny," I said, slightly frustrated.

"It’s not, yes…" Mabel exhaled dramatically before turning to Marcus. "Arnos doesn’t really talk much to just anyone; you need to know him to get a reaction. And he stutters when he’s terrified."

Did she really have to say the last part?

"Oh, is that the case? Well, Arnos, I hope I’m not making you uncomfortable… and if I am, we can always talk later."

"No, no, Lord Levile, I’m fine. Mabel’s exaggerating, I’m just not used to talking to big shots— ahem, I mean, ahh…"

"He’s even addressing you with honorifics. Oh, poor thing. How scared must he be of your scary aura?"

"I don’t have a scary aura."

"You don’t?" Mabel asked, eyes wide in mock surprise. "Well, I was under the impression you did. I mean, why else would Arnos, ‘a classmate,’ address you as Lord Levile instead of just Levile or Marcus? It’s obvious he’s scared."

"You’re messing with me, aren’t you?" Marcus asked.

Mabel smiled, waving her fingers. "With both of you, actually." Her gaze turned to me next. "Marcus was actually looking for a sparring partner, someone with fire affinity strong enough to stand against him."

"He is?" My eyes turned to Marcus, who simply nodded. "Then… I don’t think you’re in the right place, Lord Levile. I’m not really as good as she might’ve made me sound," I murmured, shooting Mabel a glance. What nonsense had she filled his mind with?

"I never said you were good, exactly," Mabel said, pouting. "I just recounted the event, nothing more."

"And what exactly did you recount?" I pressed.

"About the fireball you cast against the bunnies. I was talking about our dungeon expedition with him, and then he heard it and went, ‘Wait, really? He’s surprisingly good with fire affinity!’"

"Did you tell him about the part where I got unconscious?" I asked.

"I did, he’s still interested." Mabel replied.

"Getting unconscious in itself is a testament to your strength." Marcus chuckled.

It is? Wait, how? "I’m not sure I get what you’re trying to say, Lord Levile. Could you please elaborate"

Marcus blinked. "You know how people with less mana don’t get unconscious on depletion, right? They just get in Mana-less state. Getting unconscious means your mana level is somewhere around B-tier, which is already impressive for a first year, if I may say."

"Ahh, that’s what you meant. Well, I don’t really think it’s that impressive, half the class already has B-rank or higher mana."

"Being above half the people isn’t impressive?" Marcus’s brows raised.

"I didn’t mean it like that," I raised my arm.

"Haha, you can relax around me Arnos. I’m not that serious."

"He’s a softie," Mabel said, eyes closed and arms crossed as she nodded in approval.

"Don’t badmouth me, Weeble!" The softie… I mean, Marcus yelled, getting a few amused glances from people around us.

"Ahem, well anyway, Arnos, coming to the main topic, I really want you to be my spar partner for the tournament. It won’t be a one-sided deal, of course; I could even pay you in weapons or potions, it’s only fair. So what do you say, would you?" he asked.

"I’m not really sure."

"Do you not want to?" Marcus’s eyes widened slightly, as if he didn’t expect that answer from me.

"No, that’s not what I mean, lord. While I’m not bad with fire magic, I’m not that good either. Most of my energy is spent on learning dark magic, as you might have— no, why would you know, haha." I laughed awkwardly.

"You don’t attend fire magic classes, is that what you’re trying to get at?"

"Yes," I nodded.

"Do you not go to a private tutor to learn it during your holidays?"

"I… ahh…" I lowered my voice. "No, I self-study, and currently I’m two spells behind the class. The only spells I know are Fire Bullet, Fireball, Wall of Fire, Firebolt, and… Quick Step, that is, if you consider it a spell.

"So you’re more focused on your other affinity, dark, right?"

"That I am…" my eyes widened. "Wait, you know about it?"

"About what?" Marcus blinked.

"My other affinity, did Mabel tell you about it too?"

"No, that she didn't." Marcus shook his head. "Other than me and Emily, only you two have dual affinity. It’s only natural that I would know about it, right? Or should I have not known about it? he asked, a smile spreading on his lips."

"No, that’s not what I meant. I just didn't expect you to."
