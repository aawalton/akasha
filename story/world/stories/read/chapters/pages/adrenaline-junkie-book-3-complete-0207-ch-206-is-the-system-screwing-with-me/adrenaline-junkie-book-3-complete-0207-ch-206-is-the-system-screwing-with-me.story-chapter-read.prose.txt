Archie stared down at the walkie-talkie in shock. His mouth opened for a moment before closing.

…I’m sorry, what?

No, seriously – what? A fresh wave of confusion lapped over him, which was both cold and disorienting.

I did–no, he corrected himself, the person I took over and three others brought an entire city to ruin? Why does this sound like the literal opposite of that shield isekai I watched a couple years back?... Why? What would make someone become a terrorist and do all of that?

He blinked slowly as he was once again lost in his own thoughts and assumptions; while doing so, he had subconsciously released the clutch and slowly began moving towards the city of Creavenboor.

While he was still reeling from the Handler’s revelation, grappling with the fact that whoever had this body before him was, apparently, a literal terrorist, he conveniently forgot that he himself was already branded one by the Netharim Sovereignty after the whole Border Wall fiasco.

Unaware of his spiraling inner monologue, the Handler’s voice calmly continued: “You, along with Agents XI, XIII, and XX, will now be fully instated as Intelligence and Field Operatives for Molta.”

“Normally, after completing a long-term mission such as the one in Greeber, we would grant a month or two of downtime for rest and resupply. However, since you are already near Creavenboor and extremely ahead of schedule, and we have just recently gained crucial information in regards to a potential traitor, I’d like to offer you a preliminary mission. This would serve as both reconnaissance and lead into a future coordinated operation involving you and another Agent – Agent IX. This mission is expected to last one month. Do you accept?”

There was a pause.

Archie tilted his head, the glint of moonlight catching on the metal of his gorget as he thumbed the walkie. He didn't know what the hell he was agreeing to, but if this Handler was buying his act, then the best play was to keep leaning into it. Information was power, and right now, she had all of it, especially considering how he had less than a week to gather information about himself to complete the first Questline Mission the System gave him.

“Sure,” he replied casually. “I don’t mind at all. But, uh– what would the role require of me? Would you want me fully embedded with the locals? Or would you rather I stay in the dark, hidden from all?”

He paused and decided to add in a little levity in this situation, “Or full-on trench coat, fake wig, mustache, and the like?”

The Handler let out a soft, polite breath – too light to be a laugh, but it was a win for Archie as with this one shitty joke, he was able to see if his handler was a hardass or not.

“Yes, of course. One moment, Agent XVI. I’ll send the mission parameters to your communicator. Your pay is being routed now to your associated Bank Card.”

Archie had barely mentally questioned, Wait, what Bank Car– ? Before the Spatial Storage in his agent tattoo had an item rumble inside it once again.

Reaching back into the tattoo’s hidden storage, he pulled out what looked like… a completely normal, utterly bland white card with a matte finish.

He squinted. “...Huh.”

Injecting a trickle of mana into it triggered a shimmering wave across its surface, revealing elegant etched lettering:

Gates BankAccount Holder: Archibald RavenbowReceived Payment: 15 Gold, 23 SilverTotal Holdings: 2 WG, 34 G, 28 S, 5 C

The elegant etched lettering then shifted into some sort of Identification Card after a couple of seconds.

Identification CardName: N/AKingdom of Birth: N/ARace: N/ASex: N/AGrade: N/A

Archie’s expression did not change. He stared at the card.

Then he blinked.

And blinked once more.

And whispered, with no small amount of disbelief:

“…Archibald Ravenbow?”

He stared harder, as if sheer willpower could erase the name and rewrite it into something – anything – less ridiculous. Archibald? What the hell kind of name was that?

Is this some kind of joke? Is the System screwing with me? Or is this just my luck?Why couldn’t it have been something cool like ‘Blade Stormsong’ or ‘Kaz Voidwalker’?

He let out a very deep sigh, I can’t believe I’m a terrorist with such a shitty name. Why couldn’t I just be called Archie in this world?

Just then, the walkie crackled again. “Agent XVI, are you still there?”

Archie snapped upright, both from his Handler’s voice and by the massive tree trunk that suddenly loomed in his path. He veered sharply, just managing to swerve around it.

“Y-Yeah. Still here,” he answered quickly. “Just… reviewing the, uh, payment. Much appreciated.”

“Excellent. I’ve uploaded the mission details. The parameters will auto-sync to your communicator, and the white card will serve as your encrypted ID, credit storage, and mission credential key – all will appear to be normal to you, but anyone else who checks it will Identify you as Creed Burrows of Amwell.”

He held up the card between his fingers in confusion, People use bank cards as IDs? That sounds stupid.

Another ding yanked him from his train of thought as the walkie’s screen flickered. A fresh wall of text began populating across its cracked digital display, but before he could catch even a word, a Quest Panel popped up in front of his face.

Side Quest – First I.F.O Mission:Objective: Infiltrate the city of Creavenboor under the guise of a tourist. Gather intelligence on Baron Alric Velstrome, who is suspected of betraying the Kingdom of Molta to the Kingdom of Lagos. Investigate all known properties, warehouses, and hidden storehouses owned by Baron Alric Velstrome. Search for signs of illicit dealings, correspondence with enemy states, or any indication of treason against the crown. If enough conclusive evidence is discovered and upon receiving the "Go Ahead" from your Handler, eliminate Baron Alric - it would be preferred if it looked like an accident. During the course of the mission, if you venture into the Primeval Forest of Creavenboor and encounter a Griffin, engage, eliminate the beast, and preserve its remains.Reward: Tutorial Points will be given based on your accomplishments & findingsTime Limit: 1 MonthNOTE: You are expected to report findings weekly with your Handler. Should your cover be compromised, you must escape immediately - leave no traces behind.

DO YOU ACCEPT THIS QUEST?

That’s new, Archie thought, narrowing his eyes as they flicked back to the Reward section.

The System seemed to be offering him Tutorial Points as a reward, but the walkie’s screen told a slightly different story. Right below the same objective summary, the reward section listed 10 Gold minimum, plus performance bonuses based on “findings and accomplishments.”

He gave a low whistle.

If the currency in this world worked anything like the coinage on Fractal – and the fact that the card noted two whole White Golds in his account already suggested it might – then this mission wasn’t just important, it was lucrative. And lucrative meant dangerous.

Before he could dwell on that realization, the screen abruptly switched again – back to the glowing words: Caller: HANDLER.

Her voice returned a moment later and sounding a lot graver. “With your records, it has been calculated that if you are caught, you would have a 17.8% chance of escaping the city. Agent XVI… Would you still accept this mission, and accept the risks of doing so without receiving any backup, while knowing this?”

Archie squinted. I thought I already said yes…? It’s a dangerous mission with high stakes and no safety net… Why wouldn’t I accept it? Who would choose a safe mission over a dangerous one?

He blinked at the still-hovering quest panel like it might clarify itself, then furrowed his brows slightly before realizing that he probably had to say that he accepted to get the Quest Panel in front of him to vanish and to “officially start the side mission”.

“Yeah, I accept,” he said aloud, and after doing so, the Quest Panel vanished from sight and words appeared atop the white card in his hands, filling in the previously N/A fields.

Identification CardName: Creed BurrowsKingdom of Birth: Amwell Race: HumanSex: MaleGrade: D

Like, why couldn’t this be my name normally? Archie sighed. It’s so much better than Archibald, hell, why couldn’t my name just be Archie?

More clacking of keys echoed faintly from the walkie, and something scribbled across paper in the background – or maybe it was parchment? A quill? Pen? Who knew anymore?. From what he’d seen in Greeber, albeit while it was falling apart and along the way here, this world looked like it was experiencing Earth’s post-industrial era as well as its Middle Ages at the same time.

Then her voice came again – brighter this time, almost chipper.“Fantastic! Keep in mind that while assuming your tourist identity, you must act like a commoner. Do not reveal your status as a Marquess under any circumstance. And, under direct orders from the Director, you are not allowed to consume any alcohol during this assignment.”

Archie’s brow twitched. Marquess? Isn’t that like nobility or something? I guess that makes sense considering my Questline is called Amnesiac Noble.

But before he could question it any further, the Handler continued, now sounding practically celebratory: “Nevertheless… congratulations on passing your second and final trial, Agent XVI. I wish you the best of luck on your mission. Viva la Molta.”

Archie blinked, stopping his bike from cruising towards the West Gate by lightly pressing his feet atop the dirt. Second trial? How did I pass it? When did it begin? Was it because I didn’t care about the mortality rate?

“…Viva la Molta,” he repeated back to his Handler as he assumed it to be some sort of patriotic good bye saying, but weirdly similar to Earth’s Viva la Italia.

Archie looked thoughtful for a moment after the Handler ended the line and static began to spill out of the walkie- talkie’s speakers. And as he powered it off a realization came to him: Am I in a mob?

As though to answer his question, his Main Quest popped up in front of him.

Main Quest – Amnesiac Noble: The OriginObjective: You are the son of a Marquess. After being told time and time again to cut down on your alcoholism, you did not listen. As such, you’ve been punished by being drafted into the I.F.O. division for the Kingdom of Molta.Reward: 500 Tutorial PointsTime Limit: COMPLETED!

The words “COMPLETED!” sparkled for a second too long before fading out with a little jingle that reminded him of the victory screen from an old gacha game that he played for an hour or two after it kept trying to get him to enter in his credit card.

“… So, the character I isekaied into is an alcoholic nobleman who got black-bagged into a black-ops division?” he muttered under his breath. “How does that make sense? Did he have a previous background in this type of stuff? Are only nobles allowed to do things like this?”

“… I have so many questions,” he muttered out loud before putting it out of his mind for now;, he had a niece to find.

Glancing down at his Mana Bike and the numerous guards off in the distance guarding the West Gates of Creavenboor, Archie let out a sigh before hopping off of it and storing it away into the Spatial Storage that resided within the XVI tattoo.

Considering what my Handler said, it might be for the best to keep my bike out of sight, Archie mused to himself as he began to sprint towards the West Gate of Creavenboor.

“…Wait– if Camilla’s not with Henry, does that mean I’m stuck handling this mission and babysitting her at the same time?” Archie muttered, the weight of the realization hitting him right at that moment.
