Spitting some blood onto the dirt, Ben stayed on the ground and watched Theodore's figure shrink higher up the staircase. His body was an absolute wreck.

Pushing mana through his system, he kept flooding his body with [Regeneration].

Painful.

It was painful, not being able to do a thing when met with someone so much stronger, but at the same time Ben knew not to act, yet, as Logan had taught him. He was weak. The game was rigged against him, so he had to play like a weak person.

And so he just focused on himself, eyes tearing away from Theodore that kept climbing farther and farther away.

A massive shadow suddenly blotted out the light overhead.

Looking up, Ben barely had time to register the absolute unit of a man standing over him before a hand clamped onto his shoulder.

Hauling him upright like he weighed absolutely nothing, the giant easily set Ben on his feet. Up close, the guy was just a walking wall of old scars stacked onto dense muscle. But there was zero aggression coming off him.

Popping out from behind his massive body, a tiny girl suddenly appeared. She barely reached the giant's waist. But she had this little button nose and big eyes that seemed to do all her talking for her.

"My sister will heal you now." Cassian stated.

Planting herself directly in front of Ben without even asking for permission, she grabbed both of his hands, and he let her, not sensing any malice from these two.

Besides, what could he even do in his state anyway?

Warmth flooded through his veins, healing far stronger than anything he'd ever felt knitting his body back together. Blinking in surprise, Ben watched his bruises and scrapes rapidly heal closed under her skill.

But even with her help, he noted the [Healing Mark] on his chest stayed torn since that specific repair required his own mana to feed it. He'd wondered if someone else's life mana routed through their healing skill would work.

But guess not.

He observed the healing magic work its way through his system with [Focus] to learn all he could, absorbing everything. While he did so, he was suddenly reminded of that nasty goblin poison he'd gotten.

He'd desperately needed a healer back then.

Now he just wanted to chuckle. He'd already scrubbed those toxic remnants right out of him using his own [Regeneration]. In fact, he hadn't even tried, given that the skill did it by itself.

"Thanks."

Beaming up at him, the girl gave an enthusiastic nod, but she didn't say a single word. Cassian crossed his arm as he watched her work and now that she was done he finally spoke up again.

"I am Cassian. This is Sera. We are siblings. She keeps me breathing."

Tilting his head up toward the monolith, Cassian stared at the spot where Theodore was still climbing.

"That man is dishonorable. He smiles when he breaks things, where I laugh when things almost break me. We are not the same."

Turning his head back to Ben, the giant offered a surprisingly genuine, battle-hungry grin. And just like that, the air around him shifted. Whatever he actually came over here for, it had absolutely nothing to do with Theodore or the stairs.

He wanted Ben.

"I saw your mana attacks earlier. When you were fighting the Tide. You are strong."

Cracking his knuckles, the giant stepped forward with zero malice or scheming in his posture. At least none that Ben could detect. He just wanted a fight.

"So what do you say, friend?"

A spar, eh?

###

Cassian looked visibly deflated when he walked off.

At least the giant didn't push it or get angry, he seemed like a genuinely nice guy. Giving a single nod, he'd simply turned around and walked away.

Watching him go, Ben shifted his gaze downward, and realized the tiny healer was still standing right in front of him. She was staring up at him with her big eyes. And she clearly wanted to say something to him.

Waiting patiently, Ben expected her to just speak.

Instead, she reached up and pinched two fingers at the corner of her lips, dragging them across in one smooth motion like she was sealing her lips shut. Then she tapped her throat and shook her head once.

"Ah. You can't talk?"

A nod.

Then she dropped into a sudden squat and started drawing in the dirt with her finger. But then she seemed to have thought better of it, scrubbed it out with her palm, and jumped back to her feet.

Hm?

She pointed directly at Cassian's retreating back. Then she pointed at Ben's chest. Bringing her tiny hands up, she started doing this weird, repetitive motion with her fists that could have either been fighting or aggressively kneading bread dough.

Ben frowned.

"You want me to fight him later?"

She huffed. Putting her hands on her hips, she shook her head wildly and gave him the most exaggerated look of disappointment a human being could possibly produce. So he just tried again.

"You want me to bake bread with him?"

Slapping a tiny hand over her face, she dragged it down in pure exasperation.

Groaning silently, she repeated the fist bumping motion, this time pointing at her own smile, then at Cassian, then back at Ben.

"He's happy when he fights?"

Crossing her arms into a giant X, she practically vibrated with frustration.

Marching right up to Ben's shins, she grabbed his hand and shoved it toward Cassian's back. Then she grabbed her own hand and shook it vigorously like a business deal.

And then it finally clicked in his tired brain.

Ben just stared at her in total silence.

"You want me to be his friend?"

Nod nod.

She beamed.

Ben's frown deepened.

She flashed a cheeky grin. Reaching up with both hands, she jammed a tiny forefinger against each side of his face. And she physically stretched his deep frown upward into a forced smile.

Then she just spun around and sprinted away after her brother, happily waving bye-bye over her shoulder.

What a strange girl...

Rubbing his cheeks, Ben finally took a moment to actually look around the clearing for the first time since hitting the dirt.

And he immediately noticed the scattered monster corpses littering the distant tree line.

A Tide had come somewhere out there while Theodore was busy dragging him up the monolith. So he had absolutely no idea how the defense had actually gone down or what he'd missed.

Sweeping his eyes over the remaining crowd, he scowled.

Where the hell had Alice and Ethan been through all of this? And what happened with the Tide?

###

"The Tide split into multiple fronts and pinned us down on the far side of the clearing. And it's only getting worse." Ethan explained.

Finding them hadn't exactly been difficult, and now he was getting the lovely information.

"They weren't even targeting us. The entire horde was deliberately pushing toward the monolith. They were literally crushing themselves to death against the barrier without a single care for their own survival."

Processing that deeply concerning tidbit, Ben just sighed.

"The Tides have grown significantly stronger, huh."

"And they'll definitely keep growing. Meanwhile, the time between the waves keeps shrinking. Alice and I have been swamped every single round."

Ben could easily tell something was seriously bothering Ethan. But before he could even begin to poke at Ethan, a highly agitated blur shoved right into his view.

"I can't believe that absolute psycho!" Alice threw her hands in the air. "I just learned he dragged you all the way up there and threw you off!"

"I technically dropped myself." Shrugging, Ben enjoyed watching her eye twitch. "Gravity really did the heavy lifting."

"That doesn't make it any better!"

"He's just deeply misunderstood."

Keeping his tone flat, Ben leaned into the bit.

"Honestly, there's nothing actually wrong with him. The poor guy is just incredibly mana deficient. It's gotten to his head. Once we get him some more mana, everything will totally be fine."

Staring at him in sheer disbelief, she let out a sudden, startled half laugh.

Covering her mouth, Alice lost all of her furious momentum. But then she quickly recovered and jammed an accusing finger right into his chest.

"I literally told you so."

Leaning in, she leveled a flat glare at him.

"I specifically named him a complete maniac to your face on day one. And look exactly what the maniac did during our very first time sharing a rift."

"Fair enough."

Well, not like it was his fault, but when Alice was in one of her moods, it was best if he let her be. So waving a hand dismissively, Ben redirected the conversation toward other matters.

"But at least I met some decent people. That giant guy's tiny sister named Sera just came over and healed me up."

Blinking in surprise, Alice instantly forgot all about Theodore.

"Since when do you let random strangers heal you?"

Tilting her head, she openly inspected his remarkably unbruised face.

"That's surprisingly well adjusted of you."

"I didn't really have a cho—"

The sentence completely died in his throat, because the entire base of the monolith suddenly went quiet where before there had been whispers and chatter, and Ethan had grabbed their attention, nodding toward the monolith.

Ben craned his neck upward along with every single other person in the clearing.

Way up near the very top of the monolith, Theodore had stopped moving. For the absolute first time since he started climbing, Theodore's casual little stroll was broken. He'd slowed down, sure, but he hadn't stopped before.

But now...

Ben could easily see the strain in the man's posture even from down here.

Taking a single step forward seemed to take a lot out of him, and taking another strained step froze him completely. And then the monolith threw him off.

Plummeting down from such a height, Theodore fell for a long time. It was a massive enough drop for the entire clearing to collectively suck in a breath.

And then the air around him rippled as he disappeared and reappeared on the ground, bracing his legs as he landed.

Everyone was silent.

Staring right at his face, Ben noticed the blood dripping from his nose.

Throwing his head back, Theodore let out a genuinely delighted laugh. It was the first time since stepping foot inside this rift that the man looked legitimately interested in anything.

He kept his eyes locked on the very top of the monolith as his laughter died down.

"Isn't this fun, Benjamin?" Theodore said, gaze falling to meet his eyes.

Ben refused to say anything, his mind focused on something very simple.

Theodore bled like anyone else.
