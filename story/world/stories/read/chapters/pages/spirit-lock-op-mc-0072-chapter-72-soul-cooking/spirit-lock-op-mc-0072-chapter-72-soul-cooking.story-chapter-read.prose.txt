Most rifters just ate it to lick the salt, then left the rift to obtain more food before returning. Those who did eat it cooked it overnight over a bonfire and then ate the burnt jerky as survival rations. The fact that they were going to try it the first day was bewildering.

That said, if he could make it work, they would all kowtow. They’d eventually have to eat it, so saving their rations would be a boon.
