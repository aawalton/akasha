Thanks to the awesomeness of Mr. White, Chloe and Sierra eventually went somewhere that wasn't a dark tunnel. Instead, they entered this metal-ly area with a whole lot of pipes and stuff like that. The emergency lights above were constantly spinning and flashing though, which made the whole area look a lot more intimidating than it otherwise would have been.

But like the bestest horse he was, Mr. White suddenly stopped. Whoa, someone dangerous must be close. Even I can feel it.

Chloe hopped off Mr. White and looked at Sierra, who soon did the same. The wolf girl then hurried over to her. "Chloe, do you feel that?"

She nodded. "Yeah, probably an administrator. I can feel their mana all the way from here."

And boy, could she feel it. The mana was practically prickling against her skin, like thousands of tiny needles warning her to get out of there. Not that Chloe was particularly surprised. Given the situation, running into yet another Administrator seemed like it was just bound to happen sooner or later.

As if on cue, something suddenly exploded through the ceiling. Metal fragments and dust rained down as a figure dropped from the hole above, landing in front of them with a CLANG! The impact was so intense that it sent a whole lot of dust and metal parts skittering across the floor. Well, that's one way to make an entrance.

The woman slowly straightened up, revealing a suit of crimson armor that covered her from head to toe. The armor was reinforced with overlapping plates of a darker red, each layer fitted over the other like the scales of a dragon.

Then there was her face. Or, well, the demon mask covering it. The thing was downright terrifying, with a scowling expression that looked like it had been carved specifically to communicate just how much the wearer hated everything in existence. That everything in existence probably also included Chloe too.

The woman took a step forward. "I am Administrator V, and I have been tasked with—"

"Yeah, yeah, I get the whole deal." Chloe cut in, waving a hand. "You're an Administrator, I'm an intruder, and you're here to kick my ass or drag me off somewhere unpleasant. Blah, blah, blah. But can't you just, y'know, let me and my buddies go?"

"You're an idiot." Administrator V said.

"And you're a big meanie!" Chloe shouted back.

After that though, Administrator V raised her hand, and Chloe patted Mr. White on the back so he'd get to running away. Sierra ran with Mr. White as a fiery laser cut through the ceiling in a straight line. Weirdly enough, Chloe was able to dodge the laser by standing where she was. Heh, maybe Administrator V thought I was gonna dodge and tried to predict my moves.

"Did you miss me?" Chloe asked, wagging a finger at Administrator V with the smuggest grin ever.

Administrator V was not having any of it though. She let out this super angry growl, and suddenly, flames started swirling around one of her hands until they formed a spear made of fire. And then, she charged straight at Chloe.

Now, any normal person would probably be freaking out at this point. But Chloe just stood in place, as there was a trick that she wanted to pull. A trick that, in hindsight, would've helped a lot in a couple of fights before this one. It's time for Wraith Form!

Right as Administrator P's spear was about to go straight through her chest, the woman and her weapon passed right through Chloe's body like she was made of smoke.

"Is that the best you've got?" Chloe asked.

Administrator V spun around, and the look on her face? Priceless. Apparently, she wasn't exactly thrilled about her attack doing absolutely nothing, so she tried again. The administrator readied her spear, charged, and then went through Chloe's body.

She lunged at Chloe from every possible angle, swinging and thrusting that flaming spear like her life depended on it, but it was literally giving absolutely nothing. Every single attack passed straight through Chloe. Honestly, it was almost kind of embarrassing to watch, and it was definitely funny to see.

Eventually, Administrator V seemed to realize that maybe, just maybe, repeatedly trying the exact same thing wasn't going to magically make it work. She backed away several steps, before she started chanting something that sounded eldritch-y and kinda evil.

Well, guess I should start doing something. Chloe thought.

See, Chloe had been perfectly happy to stand there and let Administrator V embarrass herself for, like, the next three to five business days. But the second her opponent started doing all that creepy chanting nonsense, it was go time.

"My turn!"

Chloe dispelled her Wraith Form and focused on conjuring her Divine Blast. She brought her hands together, and a ball of light began to form between her palms, growing brighter and brighter as she channeled her magic into it. And based on vibes alone, Chloe decided that three seconds or so of charging was probably enough.

She released the spell, sending the ball of light straight toward Administrator V. It slammed into her opponent's chest with a burst of light, knocking her backward and sending her flying. Wow, that worked better than I thought!

Honestly, this was a pretty good start to a fight, all things considered. Maybe this whole fight would be a lot easier than her previous encounter with Administrator P. And Chloe did get a lot stronger after defeating an administrator after all.

Administrator V finally stopped flying when she hit a wall, there was a whole lot of damage on her armor, with big chunks of it just outright gone. Chloe figured that maybe a couple more of those Divine Blasts of hers should finish the job. So, she did what she was supposed to do and charged up another one.

But well, things unfortunately didn't turn out to be so simple. When Chloe fired off her second Divine Blast at Administrator V, Chloe could swear that she saw the Administrator smile as she got up. Administrator V tanked the full hit from Chloe, and more of her armor shattered as her Divine Blast erupted in a flash of brilliant light.

When the dust settled, however, Chloe saw Administrator V was… naked? Okay, uh, as more of the dust cleared, it became clearer that she wasn't naked, but she wasn't wearing much under all that armor. She just had bandages wrapped around her chest and pants, which reminded Chloe of her days as a simple dungeon zombie. Pretty sure I had more clothes on me even back then.

Administrator V's mask then started to glow as she now manifested not one, but two swords made of flames. Well, an enemy powering up and showcasing different abilities was usually a bad sign, so Chloe decided to pull a trick she hadn't used in a while. It was time for her to use blood magic!

Chloe focused her attention on Administrator V,  concentrating on the blood flowing through her opponent's body. She began manipulating its flow, twisting Administrator V's limbs and forcing her body to move against her will.

She pushed harder, tightening her control as she forced Administrator V closer and closer to the ground. Her opponent's legs buckled, and she began sinking toward her knees. Come on… just sit down for a second!

Chloe was this close to making her opponent kneel properly when, all of a sudden, flames erupted across Administrator V's entire body. And before Chloe could even process what was happening, Administrator V launched herself straight at her with her blazing swords. WHOA!

The good news was, Chloe still had Wraith Form so she turned invisible and intangible before Administrator V could hit her. Or at least, that was the plan. Because Chloe soon felt her head being separated from the rest of her body as, somehow, Administrator V's fire swords managed to hit her.

Ignoring the fact that that was totally unfair, since her skill should've made her intangible, it also meant that this Administrator fight was gonna be a lot more annoying than she thought. Chloe caught her own head with one head, turning the head over to Administrator V. "Hey! How did you do that?"

Administrator V ignored Chloe's words and prepared to charge again. But luckily, before Administrator V could take another step, the sound of thundering hooves suddenly echoed.

Mr. White, the most awesomest horse in the entire world, burst into view with brilliant blue flames roaring around his body. He lowered his head and barreled straight toward Administrator V, and the administrator barely had time to react before she got hit, hard.

The impact sent her staggering backward, and before she could regain her footing, Mr. White's  hooves came thundering down around her. She was knocked off balance and trampled beneath the charging horse, which felt really good to see.

But apparently, Mr. White wasn't the only one who wanted a piece of Administrator V. While the Administrator was still struggling to recover from her impromptu meeting with an angry horse, the shadows nearby her suddenly stirred.

Sierra materialized from the shadows as she lunged at the fallen Administrator. She closed the distance in an instant before unleashing a flurry of vicious swipes aimed at her opponent. One claw swept toward Administrator V's shoulder, followed by another aimed at her side, and then a whole lot of other places too.

But, Administrator V wasn't about to let herself get turned into claw chow. Despite having just been introduced to the wonderful experience of being trampled by Mr. White, she managed to bring both flaming swords up in time to defend herself, sending sparks and embers scattering through the air.

Sierra attacked again, striking from one side before darting toward the other, but Administrator V kept her swords moving, parrying each blow. And just like that, Sierra's assault was being held back.

Well, at least Administrator V was having a much harder time now, and Chloe was definitely going to take advantage of that. She still had a lot of tricks she can pull, and this one was surely going to finish off the Administration for good.

Chloe plopped her head back onto her body, then closed her eyes and focused. Because as her body regenerated, she prepared to use her True Eyes of Dominion.
