Through the journey, Ashawein grew closer to the beauty, Othenia, and learned much about the lands she walked. Thanks to Othenia’s presence and royalty, they were never accosted by sapient denizens, and even most beasts. Occasionally, they helped a tribe with some trouble for heroes. Though Ashawein ached to get through her challenges, having so many long roads ahead, she never refused a request, so much so that it seemed to amuse Othenia, who teased her that she’d grow old before getting halfway through them, fulfilling every wish as she was.

“Nonetheless, I will fix whatever trouble I can,” Ashawein replied, somewhat sheepishly, “so the people here will know kindness and worthiness from foreigners.”

Othenia had many questions along the way, digging into Ashawein’s history and her opinions about the lands, including the Indigowood. Ashawein, as usual, stated plainly her intentions: to rally them against the storm to come, and preferably to go and meet it elsewhere, so the beauty and sanctity could remain preserved.

She wasn’t sure, but underneath the amusement for Ashawein’s idealism, Othenia did seem pleased.

As they came to the edge of the territory, and the mountains loomed, the challenge of the chimera was at hand…

It was oddly gentle, the transition Samantha felt from being Ashawein, into taking control inside her skin. Perhaps it was just that she had become so much more advanced since her previous dives of such nature that her mind and soul found such transitions trivial to bridge, perhaps due to the sheer speed at which she thought and processed things.

The journey blurred at the final points, so Samantha briefly pondered all she’d learned and experienced through her previous life.

I was a giantess. Heh. Pretty damn cool. And she seems more prescient than any other life — this alone would be enough to make her a key anchor. There is even more to her, though I don’t fully understand it. Her name, that meaning, that is the root of it: One Who Shows Heaven and Awakes the Creator Within. I believe it may be rather literal…

Samantha felt herself inside the body of the giantess, now — a ten-foot-tall, majestic being of blue-gray skin, rippling muscles, and hair like literal wisps of a cloud trying to make a break for it to the sky. Dressed in golden armor otherwise reminiscent of a Greek Bronze Age period, she radiated physical dominance from her greater body, even as she projected wisdom and restraint from her peaceful countenance.

Next to her was the heart-stoppingly attractive form of the half-dragon, Othenia, a mix of mainly humanoid beauty synthesized with draconic and even faintly demonic highlights, including wings, huge curving back horns, and a tail. She was a combination of lighter blue and darker with her skin. Ironically, though extremely tall at over eight feet, to Ashawein, her form was rather short and cute, while not being so short as to feel completely diminutive.

Azure, you’re going to get big-time regret for not being in that body. Knowing you, you’ll go to find it in the Hall just to ‘partake’ of whenever these two started tangling in the sheets. Hehe… I’m a little tempted myself. Mmph. She really is corrupting me, isn’t she?

Othenia pointed a claw up at the mountains, and a just-visible ghost of a path up it. “There is where we must go, and the cave within. It will be quite dangerous to enter the chimera’s lair. They tend to lurk in ambush, even there.”

The first thing ‘Ashawein’ did was shrink and disguise herself as a gnoll. She turned and nodded to Othenia. “Do as I do. And no, we’re not going in there to get ambushed like a couple of rubes. We’ll dangle a carrot and bring the thing to us, where we will ambush it. After all, it likes to eat tasty gnolls. Can you summon expendable entities that can also be disguised, or preferably, shapeshifted?”

The draconic beauty frowned at her oddly for a moment, then began shapeshifting into a gnoll, briefly looking at herself in distaste. “I dislike not having wings, you know.”

“Forgive me, your worshipfulness,” Ashawein said, attempting as best she could not to make it sound sarcastic, though she was definitely being sarcastic.

The rather feminine-appearing gnoll raised an eyebrow. “Begging, are we? Interesting.” She waved a hand. “And yes, I can summon minions that will not die true death, but at best, they will be cloaked in illusion. You should know that most chimeras have a set of eyes that can see through those. Their senses are quite acute.”

“Hmm. Noted. I’ll handle the minions’ physical changes, then.” I think this is what she’s good at, after all. Hell. Preparing traps? That’s my wheelhouse, too!

As her plan for creative, deadly things to create germinated in her brain, Ashawein asked, “Othenia, what’s something believable a less-than-wise gnoll group might do out here?”

Othenia replied, “Seek medicine. Two rare herbs grow in these forested hills, one to combat a troublesome mange they develop, the other regarded as beneficial to weak hearts. To draw someone to danger, it would perhaps either be a bad case on a child with the mange, or some elder suffering. Technically, most now journey far west for this, but it is further, so the measure is feasible.”

“Perfect! Let’s seek out a good ambush spot, and I’ll make some traps…”

They found a location that had the right bushes. Before Othenia did her summoning, Ashawein began designing some spell-traps to place, to hit the chimera with them as soon as it took the bait.

Othenia watched her work and said, “Just be wary of default summons and spell phantasms. This chimera can dismiss lesser varieties. Though I suppose you wouldn’t have learned the Gate rune. It limits the radius but prevents dismissals, as well as hardening against, and even sacrificing itself first, in the event of a dispel.”

Ashawein felt her gnoll eyes widen hungrily. “Show me.”

Othenia scoffed. “It is not possible to absorb such a thing so quickly-”

“Please? I can reverse engineer anything. I’d at least like to know what I’m missing. Pretty please, with sugar on top?”

Othenia frowned doubtfully, but finally sighed and relented, beginning to move her claws into patterns of spellcraft. “I can’t resist such shameless begging as that, and from the pride of the cloud giants, no less…”

The rune that was displayed was a truly magnificent thing — a spell ‘additive’ or modifier of brilliant, subtle integration, something like a mushroom sprouting from the roots of a greater power, i.e., what it took to perform powerful spells.

“Incredible!” Ashawein cried. “Like a mushroom sprout!”

The uncannily attractive gnoll blinked in surprise. “Yes, that’s exactly right. How did you-”

“Sh-sh-sh!” Ashawein exclaimed insistently, waving a hand as her other hand began weaving a spell. “I need to act immediately on this inspiration!” Feeling the royal scowling at her for the shushing, Ashawein ignored it and wove the new spell, consisting of four animating, scaling ‘bear traps,’ and protected by the new rune additive, though it would be confined to a pentagram circle.

She was able to tie that same circle into another triggering spell, which she adapted from something she already knew, to save time: Pillar of Force. More or less, she upgraded it to a higher level of potency, and it became automatically persisting, instead of requiring continual focus. That meant it had the potential to ‘stun lock’ a foe inside it, slamming it down over and over like a giant machine hammer.

Ozra would be proud.

“Interesting work,” Othenia commented as she observed. “I believe I can create a larger circle around it, which will-”

They were interrupted by a great roar, which propagated out as a destructive sonic blast slamming into them and bowling them over, from the giant lion head of the chimera that had suddenly appeared on their flank as they were absorbed in their arts. For a creature dwarfing even her natural form as a cloud giant in its size — and fat-bellied besides — it had been deadly quiet.

Shit, she did warn me about them being great ambushers…

Samantha, as Ashawein, tumbled through multiple trees, breaking them on her way through, finally stopping herself with telekinetic force, though she was still significantly disoriented, with the world warbling topsy-turvy from the sonic attack.

The chimera could be seen charging after them, its central head the monstrous lion, its secondary head a vulture with a long neck, and its ‘tail’ another head ending in something like a dragon-cobra, looping around from the back and ready to strike.

On the plus side, its advance carried it right into her two new traps, triggering a glowing pentagram circle to appear underneath it, and the spells to bloom a moment later — hopefully, the potency was enough to at least distract it long enough that Ashawein could recover…
