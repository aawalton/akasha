“I’m so sorry, Gary!”

“I’m so glad you’re okay!”

His friends piled up on him. Alicia, Cog, even Asim. The hired beef boys were there too, bawling their eyes out for abandoning him.

“No one abandoned me, guys,” he said. “I told you to go.”

“Yeah, and we left!” Alicia said. “I’m so sorry you had to through this alone…”

“I don’t know you, but I appreciate you,” Delilah said.

Only the two summoned bosses, Evil Shrek and the Jester of Hearts, didn’t speak. They stood on a distant patch of grass. Gary had realized the balls vanished when he spawned them, so he couldn’t take them back.

Feng Shen and the boxing champ running out of the gym had caused a sensation. When they returned with an injured and broken-through Gary, whispers started spreading. None of the three had bothered explaining to the crowd, who now stared at Gary with new eyes.

“What is it like?” Asim asked.

“What’s what like?” Gary replied.

“Core Formation.”

“Oh. It’s amazing, actually. All the aura I had in Birth of Aura is just the little I use to keep my body moving, and I now have access to a new reservoir of vaster, stronger aura. It’s a huge change. I have no idea how someone with this kind of power could lose to someone still in Birth of Aura.”

“If they’re super weak, I guess.”

“Yeah.”

“What percentage of core saturation did you reach?” Cog asked.

“Uh… Ninety-three, I think.”

“Really!?”

“Yeah. I had all the necessary treasures on me, and I think the pressure also helped.”

That was a lie. Gary hadn’t had any of the necessary resources, but he did have the Seed of Eternal Flesh and all its mysterious interactions. Which reminded him, he still needed to look into that.

“I’m going to cultivate, guys,” he said.

“Absolutely! The next missions are due in a couple days, you have to acclimate to your new realm,” Alicia said.

“Something tells me it will be interesting,” Delilah said, giving everyone a playful smile.

Gary left his friends, mercenaries, and summons behind to sit by himself at the far end of the grass field. He had a ton of merit points to spend, too, but learning what the hell was wrong with him came first.

He dove into his body, which came even easier now. A spherical core floated in the center of his abdomen, vibrating in tune with his entire body. Gary watched as its edge undulated, rising and falling. Like it was breathing. Panting.

The core was dark red, with a gradient as if one side of it was heavily shaded by something—though there was no light inside Gary’s body. He’d heard that when a cultivator got a core, looking at it felt like looking at yourself. The harmony between core and cultivator was absolute, and many even used staring at their core as a calming meditation ritual.

That wasn’t the case for Gary. His core was ominous, hungry, greedy. It took his black lightning and assumed it for its own means. The destructiveness, which Gary aimed at vengeance, was twisted as the vessel of an insatiable hunger.

Gary didn’t like that. He didn’t like it one bit. The seed had given him power, letting him achieve a powerful breakthrough despite the terrible conditions, but it had taken control in return. This core wasn’t entirely Gary’s, and he knew it. It felt like he and the seed were playing tug of war with his black lightning as the rope.

Can it stop me from using my aura, if it wants? Can it use my power for itself or take over my body? Can it make me explode? Gary thought, breaking into a cold sweat. The seed is clearly intelligent, or at least it can take its own initiatives… What the hell is it?

Suddenly, he missed Master Colorful. The old expert would have some way to tame the seed or put it to sleep. As it was, Gary couldn’t even touch it inside his soul.

Until I find a solution, I just have to live like this, he thought, sighing. At least it makes me powerful. Is this the price of vengeance?

He let go of darker thoughts and refocused on his core. It was powerful. So powerful it made him shiver. Incomparable to before. In Birth of Aura, his aura had been a thin, delicate thing which he guided with utmost precision. Now, he could blast his enemies with vast quantities of dense, explosive aura. Proper control, in turn, would make him a menace.

Gary suddenly realized he hadn’t checked his stats after breaking through. Well, he’d glanced at them, but not really taken in the changes.

Gary Steel #00301, Level 28

Floor: 4

Clan: Heaven Reaching

Strength: 56

Speed: 76

Vitality: 116

Fortitude: 8

Wisdom: 6

Aura Volume: 34

Aura Control: 34

Core: Void Lightning (Legendary)

Vibrations: Black Lightning, Seed of Eternal Flesh (U)

The Fortitude, Wisdom, Aura Volume, and Aura Control stats seemed as arbitrary as ever. Gary assumed that the volume and control being equal was a sign of a well-completed breakthrough.

His physical stats, though, were coming along nicely. His strength still rose by the Muscle Sap he consumed daily, though it had slowed down, and his speed had increased by twenty-four when he poured all free points into it after his breakthrough. Vitality remained sky-high, but hadn’t it been 104 last time he checked?

The most striking addition, of course, was his core.

Void Lightning, legendary… It must be really powerful, but what does it matter if I can’t control it? What’s this Void, anyway? It doesn’t sound related to the seed, but it must be.

Wait. Wasn’t the seed supposed to unlock new powers once I broke through?

Seed of Eternal Flesh, Unique

The Seed of Eternal Flesh enhances the wielder’s body, greatly increasing robustness and regeneration. Additional properties unlocked as the wielder advances in power.

Current Perks:

+4 Vitality per level up

Void Drain (personalized)

That explains the absurd Vitality gains. 4 points per level up… Wow. If I earn eight by default, then four extra is like an extra half level every time I level up.

The Void Drain explains why my core is called Void Lightning, I guess, but what does it do?

Gary got up and ran into the woods. He only stopped when he was far away and certain no one was following. Activate Void Drain ! he thought, but nothing happened.

Hmm.

A squirrel watched curiously from the distance. Gary smiled and waved at it. It waved back. He almost laughed before forcing himself to focus.

He concentrated on his core. It pulsed sharply, hungrily. Draining… Gary leaned against the ground, picking up a leaf. He covered it in his aura and, for lack of better options, tried to “drain” it.

Nothing happened. He experimented with every way he could think of, from visualizing the drain to embracing the hunger in his core. It refused to work. Even focusing on the name itself gave no further System description, and Quirk refused or couldn’t help him. Half an hour later, Gary finally gave up.

“Don’t worry. It will come when it’s time,” Goldie said.

“I’m not so sure, Goldie,” he replied.

Disappointed at the power, Gary settled into his core. It was maddeningly powerful. He could draw out its power by merely willing it, a roaring stream which blasted outside his body. Controlling it required much finer skill than before, since it was so potent and explosive, but thanks to Gary’s work on his aura control, he could just barely do it.

His aura pulsed, and a new, strengthened aura field blasted out from his body, reaching a full sixteen feet in radius. “Awesome!” he said.

“Isn’t that too much information?” Goldie asked.

“Yes, but it’s a matter of practice.”

Maintaining his aura field at maximum radius, he thrust. The aura flowed from his core into his spear, suffusing it with power, then erupted alongside his thrust. A fierce aura strike shattered the trees, and Gary scoffed. With proper control, he would have pierced them instead.

Adjusting to this new amount of power wasn’t a quick process. Still in the forest, and touched by its serenity, Gary settled down, dancing with the spear as he used to do in the past. He performed each fundamental move hundreds of times, until he grew familiar with using it with his new power, then started blending the moves together in a dance of the singing spear.

It was hard for the first few hours, but then his mind found peace. He sank into the dance. The aura streaming from his body came smoother and smoother, his control growing better, guiding it instead of just blasting it out. He learned to control its output and flow. To regulate its explosiveness. By the time he left meditation, while he still wasn’t fully accustomed to his new realm, he was well on his way to it.

“Hmm,” he hummed, lying on the grass. The breeze was pleasant, refreshing—the feeling of grass on his back was heavenly. He’d shredded his robes during the previous chase, leaving him topless. Thankfully, his space ring still contained the underwear of the Core Formation spear-wielder who sold him the ring in the first place, so he wore that under his torn lower robes. All those pairs were clean.

Shadows journeyed over Gary’s face, the footprint of tiny clouds high in the sky. He reached up as if to touch one of them.

“Did my eyes change again, Goldie?” he asked.

“Not right now,” she replied, leaving his sleeve to inspect them. “They changed before, though. When you were practicing harder.”

“Yeah…”

When Feng Shen and the boxing champion found him, Gary’s eyes had been black with red irises. Demonic. They’d switched back when he relaxed, and this meditation session confirmed that the transformation happened when he channeled his power over a certain threshold.

Like the falling hair thing, there was nothing he could do about it.

“It just keeps getting worse…” he muttered, rubbing his hands over his face.

“Hey, I like you, no matter the color of your eyes!”

“Thank you, Goldie, but I’m not sure everyone will share the sentiment…”

“I think they’ll like you even more. Those eyes make you scary, and scary means strong. They’ll respect you!”

Gary considered that for a moment, then chuckled. “I guess you’re right,” he said, standing up and looking at the night sky. “I think the gym is still open. Wanna go look at merit rewards?”

“We’re going shopping? Yay!”
