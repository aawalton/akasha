We remember this being harder.

Before. When I was part of a party. When I was still formless and young.

The Depths were challenging, dangerous, somewhere to be cautious and alert. They were a maze I did not understand, an eternity I believed to be everything.

We met by accident, we met by disaster. Now we are learning again, finding out who we are.

People are difficult. Society is strange. So noisy, so cluttered, so many. Not that many, the school isn't that many. It's too many though, and they all treat us as different.

We are different. Of course they treat us that way. They see the difference, they think they understand.

Two did not. They greeted us, spoke to us. Did not pry. We know they had questions, we know they were uncertain. But they were… friendly?

Because there was no time, they were interrupted, we all had to listen.

Cara kept pretending to sleep as she felt Gwen climb into her bed and settle back to back with her. They each had their own bed unlike Anya and Seb, both were wider that either of them were tall. There was no reason they needed to share, no reason for Gwen to get up from her bed and lie behind her.

They'd talked but gotten basically nowhere. She understood why Gwen insisted they keep working with Bern for a few more days. She'd made it clear she would rather be sent home, but for Gwen's sake she'd do her best.

Which was the whole issue. Everything she did was for Gwen's sake, everything she chose put Gwen first. She didn't have to, she'd end up obeying no matter what. She could be stubborn and make Gwen command her. She could be uncaring, complete her duties without complaint but never going a single step beyond the minimum.

She wanted Gwen to be happy. Gwen wanted her to be free to act how she wanted and not feel trapped by the geas. She'd even implied that once Cara was free she'd be able to go her own way, to be whoever she wanted to be. But she knew what she wanted, at least she thought she did.

I know you're awake, Gwen sent.

We need to sleep.

Climbing into bed and putting your back to me isn't talking.

I agreed we'd keep trying to work with Bern.

And what we said this morning?

Cara tensed, her thoughts racing. She knew her emotions were bleeding through the band, she knew Gwen would be getting every unfiltered reaction. And for the first time she didn't try to prevent it.

You've all but said you want me to go my own way once you release me.

I thought you wanted to be free.

I want to be free of the geas, she sent back. Not of you. I want to be able to stand at your side, not a half step behind you. I want to know that we're together because we choose to be, not because we need to be.

Cara felt Gwen's emotions spill through hers. There were no words for the moment, there was nothing except the jumble of uncertainty, of hope for something more.

Together?

Yes Gwen. she sent. Together. I don't want to go anywhere. I don't want to Dive with someone else, spend my days with someone else, work with someone else.

A Royal and a Fairy though, you know what they would say.

We grew up as sisters. We've spent more time together than apart. I know everything about you, you know everything about me.

They'd judge us.

Cara rolled over, bracing herself on her elbow to look down at Gwen. The tent was nearly too dark to see in, but she could see enough to picture the rest. Gwen's eyes wide with confusion, with doubt, with questions. Questions Cara couldn't ask herself, not honestly. That she'd had to hold at a distance.

For Diving together? For working as a pair? she sent.

She felt Gwen's emotions slam shut at her words, the proximity turning cold and distant before she'd finished expressing the thought.

Gwen?

If that's what you want we will make it work.

No, what's wrong.

I thought I was Your Gwen. That is what you thought wasn't it? What you felt?

Those were her words, her thoughts. She couldn't deny it. But Gwen was acting as though she'd taken them back, that she'd changed her-

I love you Gwen, she sent, forcing the words past her shock and fears. I love you and I don't know what that means for us. I can't, you can't. Neither of us know where we stand because I am your lifesworn and geas'd.

What do you mean?

Really? You're asking why the geas might complicate things? Why I don't know where we stand?

No, I understand it complicates things. I mean why can't we know.

Because the geas will influence it even if we don't want it to. Because until I know it isn't affecting me… Until you know my reactions are completely my own…

She let herself fall onto her back then rolled so they were lying back to back, shoulders and hips pressed against one another as firmly as if they were pushing against a wall.

Cara?

It means I'll still love you, still want to be by your side, no matter what. But that we can't be anything more. We can't find out who we are until that day.

She didn't close off her emotions, she didn't try to ignore Gwen's. She could feel the pain of acceptance flowing between them, the pain of knowing that they couldn't know.

I love you too, Cara.

They'd talked. She just wished it didn't feel like the conversation hurt more than the uncertainty.
