Time.

The bane of any gamer. There is never enough, no matter how much planning or hard work, we always need more. It might sound a bit melodramatic when speaking of a hobby like gaming, but it is still a true sentiment. We all get older, have less time for fun activities and move on to other responsibilities.

Many games are just fine to play, but MMORPGs are a different story. They are a different monster all together, requiring significant time and more often than not, scheduled gaming sessions. Like it's something you have to plan for.

Finding a game that truly speaks to you can be just as difficult as it is to find the time to play it, but once you connect with a game and get people to join with you, the excitement and sense of adventure just keeps building.

Just a step into another world, just a window in which we can poke our head in and get a glimpse of a different way of life. MMORPGs are truly an amazing concept, but having all the circumstances come together for a player to actually put a few hundred hours into it is a rare thing.

For some people though, having time for an MMORPG is a necessity, a way of life. Realizing that people and games come and go isn't easy to deal with but nothing feels better than an event big enough to draw your friends in.

One Gamer with the account name 'Azure_Sky' just joined the voice chat program called Vent-cord where an old friend waited.

"Sky? That you?"

"Hehehe."

"What's up, Sky!?" The account named 'Glacial_Touch' said.

"Touch! I am doing fantastic, how the hell are you?"

"Just peachy, especially with that news!" Touch replied.

"Right? I couldn't believe it! Still can't!"

There was only one reason why they were meeting, a large event had indeed come. The two gamers hadn't played in a while, but that was nothing new. Sometimes people grow apart and sometimes reconnecting is as simple as a new event in game. But this event was something big enough to bring them back together.

"I mean a whole new line up of servers, a full restart, AND nothing is race or expansion locked?! Are you kidding me?!" Touch said excitedly. “And on your birthday? What an amazing gift!”

"I know, I know, the big 24. A fantastic birthday gift from the gods of gaming. Have you been able to get a hold of Dark or Ivy?" Sky asked.

Glacial_Touch sighed, "Yeah, but they aren't sure if Dark can get the time off from his new job. You know how it is."

"Ah, well it will be epic if they can make it...I got a buddy I made recently that is looking for a starting group who would be a perfect 5th. That is…if you are able to join?”

“Oh, I am definitely in. There is no way I am missing this!”

Zenith Affinity was without a doubt a unique MMORPG. One in which you can only have one character on any specific server. This would normally be met with outrage in any other game, but this one had a uniquely 'tough' development team that only ran the game how they wanted and they almost seemed to enjoy punishing those that went against what they wanted.

It didn't earn them the best reputation in the form of newer players, but it also formed a uniquely dedicated and fervent player base. One that would be playing this game for years to come.

"I might have someone as well, but we need to double check Ivy and Dark...please just message them. Hopefully you can annoy them enough to agree," Touch said with a smile that Sky could hear.

"Hah! Alright, hold on."

Sky wrote up a message with extremely persuasive arguments, tagging their other friends in it. The message had two parts and was very simple: "Ivy, you don't have to heal." and "Dark, you can tank."

"So, who is this other player you have?" Touch asked.

"Just this guy I have been running PvP with, he is really good at different affliction builds that are super difficult to take down. He said something about wanting to make a bleed-blight warrior or something...I can't remember, but the guy is crazy good at PvP. We actually won a couple of the duo Blitz Tournaments together."

"He got an account name?"

Knowing where this was going, Azure_Sky linked his online Zenith Affinity profile, "Yup, 'Dread_Sloth'. Here is the link to his profile."

It would show all of his characters on any and all different servers, as well as the various achievements they had on each character. Before Touch could fully investigate, the sound of two new guests entering the Vent-cord channel echoed through both of their headsets.

"Hello?" Sky asked sheepishly.

"SKY! What the hell kind of arguments were those?" A male voice spoke out.

"Uhhh, Dark, that you?"

Account name 'Dark_Hero' spoke up, "Who else? Course it's us and we will be what we choose to be. Should we decide to play..."

Sky laughed, "That's what I like to hear!"

"We didn't confirm anything Sky! Calm down!" Account name 'Wild_Ivy' said, recognizing the excitement in Sky's voice.

"Yeah, of course not," Sky agreed, diplomatically.

"How are you guys doing?" Touch asked.

"We're doing great, Touch! Good to hear from you!" Ivy said.

“Happy early birthday, Sky. 24 now?” Ivy asked.

“Thanks! If you guys join tomorrow you won’t even have to get me any gifts!”

Dark laughed, “Okay, I’ll just go ahead and toss what we already got you!”

“Hey!”

Laughter on Sky’s behalf was a common thing.

"So, you guys are updating or what?" Dark asked. Referring to the new patch and the reset server, you had to update the client to access the new servers.

"Yup, you?" Touch said.

Dark didn't respond, trying to leave it as a mystery.

Sky couldn't help but smile in delight. The feeling was coming back. That euphoria and excitement that comes with a new play-through with friends, the rare and fleeting feeling that only happened at the rarest of occasions! IT WAS BACK!

He couldn’t officially confirm it, but Sky had a feeling that it was going to happen.

Sky did his best to hold the waves of excitement inside, "So, have we thought about classes? I would like to go mage again, you know I love AoE.”

“Going flame mage again?" Ivy asked.

"Ehh, it's fuuuunnnnn, but I don't know... I am ready for a different one. Was thinking of going solar mage this time since there is a build that increases their affinity to an obscene amount."

"Solar mage huh? What race?" Dark asked.

"Celestial without a doubt, the racial bonus to the solar affinity is exactly what I need. Racial stat stacking is key for top tier AoE wave clear. I would be Solar, Spatial, and Gravity," Sky explained. Not all races could be every class, and some were even locked to a single race or gender. For example, the Valkyrie class could only be played by Celestials, and only female ones at that.

Dark laughed, "Fair enough, let me know where you line up that boob slider-" His voice was cut off by a solid whack as he and Ivy played right next to each other IRL, "Ow!"

"Well, stop dreaming about Sky's new character...it's creepy," Ivy said. "I am still going as a druid healer but from my last two minutes of searching for a build and I am looking at an interesting Water, Light, and Holy Druid build. Really good cleansing, like obscenely good, though I have to find a way to deal with curses."

Dark grunted positively, "AoE healing, a ridiculous amount of cleansing, and solid crowd control to boot. Yeah Ivy, that looks like a strong build. What about you Touch, monk again?"

"Nah, I am going Rogue, I want a bit of stealth this time," Touch said.

"What, Dark, Poison,....something?" Dark asked.

"No, poisons kind of got nerfed a bit, but Ice got buffed when they added buffed physical last update. So right now it is great for Dark, Ice, and Spatial or Void, causing obscene crits, stealthy utility, and a whole host of single-target debilitation's. But choosing Void or Spatial will depend on our 5th. If we decide to play, obviously," Touch said the last part quietly, but loud enough for them to hear.

"We need one more though right?" Ivy asked, knowing the full party size is 5.

"Yeah, I have one that can take a sort of off-tank affliction-brawler position, he’s really good, but I am not sure how well it will be without another support class. I was doing some PvP with him recently and we have been on a tear, if he decides to go with that I can switch to an enhancer of some sort," Sky explained.

"Why don't you invite him in, Sky?" Touch asked. "And I am also messaging a friend that might be able to make it, just in case yours can't."

"Alrighty, one sec."

Another sound of someone joining, "Hey guys..." A very relaxed male voice spoke up. He had a sort of surfer-dude accent and tone.

Ivy and Dark muted themselves which meant they were probably laughing.

"Hey Dread!? What's up dude?"

"Skyyyy! My dude, good to hear you. This the group you were talking about?"

"Yup! We have Touch, Dark, and Ivy - Single Target DPS, Tank, and Healer respectively."

"Yeah, and she was in that golden laurel guild, or whatever their name was."

"Saints, something or another, can't remember," Sky replied.

Touch must have recognized it, "Oh! Golden Saints?"

"That's them! Huge RP guild and what not, but anyways, I think I'll be starting with them," Dread explained. RP meaning that the guild focused on Role Play along side other content. The server they're joining was also a Role Play server, indicating that issues and interactions should be dealt with 'in character.'

"Awww, alright. Well, join our guild at least?" Sky asked.

"Yeah, actually, Lyssa, that's her name by the way, said that the Golden Schmucks or whatever aren't starting over as a guild as only a couple of 'em are restarting. So we might just be looking out for your Zenith Star banner," Dread said.

"Yeah! I will get a party together, sounds good?" Dread said.

"Hell yeah! And don't forget...100 for 100!" Sky cheered.

"100 for 100!" Dread replied with just as much enthusiasm.

"You got it."

"Anyways, what race are you all going as?" Ivy asked.

"Celestial!"

"Yeah, I know Sky..."

"Sorry."

"Nothdren or Dark Goliath," Dark replied.
