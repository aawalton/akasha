I experienced the hours that followed as nothing but a series of moments, flashing before my eyes. A caved-in skull here, Pharus ripping rotten meat and rusted iron apart there, and teal fire draping over everything like a shroud.

Who knew how long later, long past sunset, there was a sudden lull in the fighting. I spun in place, Pharus blazing with blue fire, ready to decimate whatever stood in my path, but there was nothing left to fight. Pharus sputtered, and in this second of cold, I managed to reign myself in.

My senses returned, and I found myself panting, both hands trembling around Pharus’ handle, standing in a perfect circle of pure destruction. There was so much blood. Dead bodies everywhere. I hadn’t killed all the Ferals. That would have been impossible. Hundreds of them still gathered around the slaughter. There were still so many…

But they stayed away from me, and that was enough for now. By God, it was enough. I somehow felt that they feared me. That they’d leave me alone. They’d leave the house alone. Sure, they already started snapping at each other, and soon, they’d start to mutate, but I’d bought us time. I couldn’t think past that. Not now. They stayed away. I was good. No more need for rage.

It took me a concerted effort of will, but I managed to switch off Pharus before I sunk to my knees, swayed, then fell forward. I managed to brace myself against the ground with outstretched arms. They were still wrapped in chains, trailing wisps of blue fire.
