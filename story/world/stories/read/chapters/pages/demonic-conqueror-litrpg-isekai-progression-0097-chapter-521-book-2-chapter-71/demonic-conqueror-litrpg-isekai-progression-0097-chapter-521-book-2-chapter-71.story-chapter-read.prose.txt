He couldn't kill the targets too quickly, because Queen Ashora's detractors would wise up if they started dropping like flies. He couldn't kill them too slowly, because he needed to make an immediate dent in their pro-war coalition. He couldn't be caught, couldn't be seen...the list of potential failstates went on.His dominion over the Severed Isles was its own separate issue. The early days of a regime change were crucial for solidifying his power base. If he spent too long prancing around Ashora, people back home might try chipping away at his fledgling administration while its foundation was still being built.So unless he wanted to trudge back to Proxia Village every time he needed to visit the Isles, he would have to force the Demon Lords to transfer authority of their cities before he killed them. That way he could activate Subjugate Territory and expand his teleportation network, letting him easily hop over to put out any fires that had sprang up in his absence.Teleport to Ashora, travel to a new city, kill a Demon Lord, Subjugate their Territory, return to the Isles, address any issues there, teleport to Ashora again, rinse, repeat.
