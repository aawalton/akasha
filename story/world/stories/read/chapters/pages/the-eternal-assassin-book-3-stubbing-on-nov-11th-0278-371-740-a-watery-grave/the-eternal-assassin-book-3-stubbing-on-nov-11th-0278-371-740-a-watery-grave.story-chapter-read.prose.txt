Asher’s eyes widened as Sefta’s head shot toward him, the warrior moving far faster than he thought she could. Even knowing she was under the lich’s influence and was probably going to try to attack him like he almost did her, he still barely managed to slip into the astral before she reached him.

Unfortunately, despite slipping into the astral in time, Asher received a rather painful reminder of just how his skill worked. While the restriction didn’t usually come into play during battle, there was a limit to how much mass he could phase through.

And the giant head of a sea serpent was far past that limit.

Asher shouted out in pain when Sefta’s jaws clamped down on where he’d been moments before, tearing deep into his abdomen and crushing bones all along his body as he was nearly bitten in half. Despite the fact that she couldn’t know where he was, her attack landed without issue even with his body residing in another realm. Sefta seemed too enraged to fully understand what had just happened, however, because she flicked her head and hurled his mangled body off her back, sending him soaring through the air. The unexpected movement within the astral clawed away at his energy, and Asher gasped when he was forced to drop his skill before he lost consciousness.

Crashing into the lake, he did his best not to think about the enraged and incredibly dangerous sea serpent who would no doubt be chasing after him in mere seconds. Yanking a potion of Lesser Restoration out of his Personal Rift, Asher downed the liquid as fast as he could. He barely even managed to finish before he spotted the giant, gleaming eyes of Sefta’s serpent form rocketing toward him, and his blood froze while he reflexively triggered Comprehend’s second-tier effect. As the world slowed to a stop, he stared at the sight of Sefta’s giant teeth coming back around to finish the job.

Damn it, he thought, knowing he only had a couple of seconds to try and come up with a solution. Submerged in the lake as he was, Astral Dip was off the table. If he came back to the material realm while underwater, his entire body would simply cease to exist. Sefta’s body was too large and too fast for him to try to dodge with Distortion, so that wouldn’t work either. He could temporarily slip into his Personal Rift or Recall to safety, but each of those options came with risks. If he tried hiding in his Personal Rift, Sefta would probably be able to sniff out the exit portal and block it with her massive body, which would trap him inside until she moved. And if he Recalled, by the time he healed up and got back out here, he had a feeling the lich would be long gone. He wasn’t willing to give her a chance to run away and try destroying another city.

There was only one potential way out of his current situation, and it relied entirely on a magical interaction he’d never gotten around to trying.

The world sped back up to normal speed, and Asher tensed his body as Sefta shot forward. Placing an Eternal Mark on the empty potion bottle in his hand, he hurled it up and out of the water, Recalling to it right before her jaws could close on him a second time. As he popped into existence a good twenty feet above the surface of the lake, Asher tried to activate his boots of Levitation. They allowed him to hover above solid ground, but he’d never tried using them above water before. As it turned out…

They didn’t work.

Asher cursed as his broken body began falling right back down into the water once more. His only option was to start marking and hurling daggers one after another to keep himself up in the air with repeated uses of Recall, but that tactic would run him dry on tritessence in no time at all. Still, it was a preferable alternative to entering Sefta’s domain while she wanted to rip him in half, so he prepared to do just that.

Right up until he felt a strange sensation in his core almost insisting that he try using Astral Dip again, and he realized nearly getting bitten in half by a monster in the material realm while he’d been in the astral had finally given him the last little push he’d needed.

Astral Dip - lvl 40

Spend 1,000,000 shards to evolve Astral Dip?

Despite the fact that he was about to crash into a lake. Despite knowing it would utterly erase his entire body if he returned to the material realm while surrounded by water. Asher didn’t hesitate. The moment he saw that notification, he knew he was going to roll the dice. Dropping the one million shards needed for the skill’s long-awaited evolution, he tensed his body harder than ever before. A small wave of energy radiated out of him while he slipped into the astral realm just like he had a thousand times before.

Only this time, something was different.

Asher blinked in shock as he looked down at himself, quickly noting the fact that he was no longer falling. The surface of the lake was only a few feet below him, and the water rippled as Sefta swam about just below its surface. Slowly, his body righted itself, floating about in midair without the use of his boots of Levitation. Laughing at the sensation of drifting about under his own power, he looked at his latest notification.

Astral Dip (active) - lvl 41

Tier 1: Temporarily slip into the astral realm.

Tier 2: Amount of time within the astral realm correlates directly with movement.

Tier 3: Become one with the astral.

Shards: 18,318,762

Asher’s breath caught when he read over Astral Dip’s third-tier evolution, and almost without meaning to, his body drifted higher up into the sky. Rather than his usual flesh-and-blood body that simply hopped over into the astral realm when he used his skill, the third-tier evolution had transformed his body into something else entirely. His form was misty, almost ghostlike even, and he quickly discovered he could float about without encountering more than the slightest drain of his tritessence.

Laughing at this newfound freedom, Asher experimented for a moment, spinning himself upside down and looking down at Sefta’s angry serpent form as her head burst out of the water where he’d been a few seconds ago. As if lamenting the fact that she’d lost his scent, she reared her head back and roared, thrashing her massive body around hard enough to cause waves to start rising up onto the surface of the lake.

This is insane! Asher drifted another few dozen feet up, grinning stupidly to himself as he realized he may have very well finally overcome his fear of heights. He knew in his very soul that most of the limitations that came with Astral Dip didn’t apply to this new form of his. Regardless of how far he moved, the energy drain was almost nonexistent. He was all but certain he’d be able to phase through as much mass as he wanted, regardless of if it was five feet or five hundred feet thick. He still wasn’t able to utilize Distortion in the astral realm, but that was fine. He had the power of flight now, even if he was only drifting around at a speed that would make Samantha scoff at him before rocketing away.

However, there was one significant drawback to the skill’s latest evolution which he noticed almost instantly.

When he attempted to slip back into the material just to fall a few feet and try catching himself in the astral realm again, Asher frowned at the discovery that he couldn’t return to the material realm. Or more accurately, his body was too dispersed and incorporeal for it to be a simple process. In order to return to the material realm from this state, it seemed as though he had to remain perfectly still and will his body back together. An act that took quite a significant chunk of time and his energy to do so.

Not to mention the fact that the process was completely visible from the material realm, if Sefta’s head snapping up to stare directly at him was any indication.

Asher clicked his teeth and stopped trying to reform in the material, drifting a bit farther away from the enraged sea serpent just to be on the safe side. The power to drift around in the astral realm for almost as long as he wanted and go pretty much anywhere was a massive upgrade to his skill, but the downside of returning to the material realm taking time and being noticeable meant he wouldn’t be walking a mile through the astral realm and popping back into the material to stab anyone any time soon.

Still, he could work with this.

Despite Astral Dip suddenly becoming the world’s greatest scouting skill, Asher avoided drifting over to the lich to see what she was up to. Gran had already proven months back that there were ways for people to view him in the astral realm from the material, and if there were any other people in the world who had the power to peek into the astral realm on command, it would be the very wizards who were actively hunting him. Instead, he angled himself upward, slowly but surely drifting up into the sky. He could only ‘fly’ at the speed of a brisk jog, but that was fine. All he needed to do was get high enough that he could return to the material realm without worry.

Once he was high enough that he could barely make out Sefta’s large body, he focused on returning to the material realm, tensing his body and watching as his incorporeal form slowly solidified under his command. Becoming solid again took almost a full ten seconds, during which time he couldn’t move a muscle and anyone could have walked up and shoved a knife through his throat had he been on the ground. The moment his skill ended, he began plummeting down toward the lake as gravity toggled back on like a switch had been flipped.

While returning from his incorporeal state to a normal body again had drained him of almost half of his entire energy pool, and it hadn’t seemed to do anything for the injuries he’d taken before slipping into the astral, Asher didn’t care. He fell toward the surface of the water with a big smile on his face simply due to the fact that he’d taken another giant step closer toward the Stage of Power. As he fell, the potion of Lesser Restoration he’d drunk kept working its magic in tandem with Eternal Regeneration and his healing artifact, repairing the worst of the damage Sefta had inflicted upon him. With his daggers at the ready, Asher narrowed his eyes when his target quickly came into view.

While Sefta was currently swimming back toward Poltar as fast as her serpent body could travel, which was more than a little concerning, the lich hadn’t moved from her spot in the center of the crater of water. Asher knew that the moment he finished off the lich her skills and spells would end, so he focused on his primary target, pulling his arms in as he shot like a missile straight for her. Without her Rage skill clouding his mind, he was calm and coolheaded as he prepared to end things with a single strike.

Unfortunately, the lich wasn’t caught quite as flatfooted as he’d hoped.

A giant fist exploded out of the water, shooting up to meet him like a geyser. Rather than let it connect and splatter him into paste, Asher hurled one of his daggers down and to the side, triggering Recall and dodging the fist as he continued falling. A second fist exploded up toward him, and Asher tried the same move, only to nearly be bitten in half when a sea serpent leapt out of the thick channel of water, lunging through open air while it tried to bite him. He narrowly dodged that surprise attack by dropping through a tunnel of compressed space, ignoring the sea serpent that was now falling down to the surface of the lake alongside him. He had eyes for the lich and the lich alone, and no amount of tricks would stop him from killing her for good.

As if sensing the same, the turquoise fires burning in her eyes flared as the lich changed tactics. She didn’t seem willing to run, as that would require her to stop her mass-rage ability she was using in her attempt to destroy Poltar, but she could certainly try to protect herself from him. A dozen streams of water erupted out of the walls of the crater surrounding her, spinning together and forming a sphere of rapidly moving currents with the lich and her glowing staff protected in the center. Based on how her dress was drifting about, it looked as if the interior of the sphere was filled with water as well, which meant slipping into the astral to bypass the outer currents wasn’t going to work. It wasn’t as if the lich needed to breathe, and she watched him continue to fall as her flaming eyes somehow continued burning despite being submerged.

With only a few seconds before he slammed into the outer edge of her barrier, Asher did the only thing he could think of.

He pulled out his void dagger and hurled it straight at the lich below him.

Air resistance meant nothing to a blade that consumed everything it touched, and water was just as meaningless to it. Still, the raging current making up the edge of her barrier did crash into the hilt of the blade after it pierced the water, knocking the dagger off course and sending it spinning wildly down past the lich rather than straight through her head.

Of course, that was perfectly fine. Astral Dip might not let Asher reappear within a solid body of water, but Recall had no such restrictions to its power.

Recall - lvl 36

Right before he splattered against the outside of the lich’s barrier, Asher triggered Recall, teleporting to the Eternal Mark he’d placed on the hilt of his void dagger right before throwing it. Before the lich could so much as recoil in shock, Asher slashed forward through a compressed tunnel of space, using Crescent to slice the lich’s head clean off her body.
