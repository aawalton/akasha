I walked toward the battlefield of blood on the beach—the location of Allium's final moments.

Red, sticky liquid from the kangaroo Sin's death still dripped from my face and coated my clothes. Its Aegis Amber Will now rested within my scythe and it had dropped a small, clear marble with a pinwheel of color in the center. Instead of putting the marble in my inventory, I tossed it casually as I walked. Nothing like a blood-soaked Reaper strolling along the beach tossing a—

Oh, fuck me.

The marble fell into the sand by my feet.

I'd been so shocked by the appearance of the Unchained and scared of dying that I'd completely forgotten about items. I had that Notice of Resignation in my inventory that LITERALLY could have teleported me away to safety.

I'm such an idiot… Ah, well. Still alive. And at least I now have the tiniest lead on the Shepherds, now that I suspect Lancelot is one of them. It wasn't all for nothing, at least.

I picked up the marble and put it in my inventory this time. I'd need to bring it to Sibyl at the colosseum for appraisal. More urgently, I needed to relinquish this Will and replenish my Volition.

The one—and I mean ONE—good thing about not being able to phase back and forth to Vitria was that I didn't take that Volition hit from the process. Each Will I submitted would last that much longer.

The signature beacon of black light denoting a pillar pierced the sky to the south, so that's where I'd be headed. But first, I wanted to see if that third Sin left behind anything useful.

I paused at the edge of the red blood carpet on the sand, closed my eyes and took a deep breath. Each step was a march through memory and pain. The same horror of seeing the Sapphire gorilla leaping over the dunes resurfaced. The agony of watching Allium ripped limb from limb. Blood squelched between my bare toes, seeping up through the beach with each footfall, leaving red puddles in every footprint I left behind.

I wanted to be sick.

In true Nuzzle fashion, the little lizard hopped from puddle to puddle, splashing about like a toddler in the rain.

Bits of flesh and shards of shattered bone littered the scene. Half a leg floated at the edge of the stained water.

And there, in the center of the mess, the motionless body of that third Unchained Sin. I couldn't even tell what creature it had been, head caved in, fur matted and torn. As I walked around, I saw the entire body from the torso down was missing. Entrails hung from the open chest cavity.

Nuzzle skittered up to it wagging his tail, then boinged like a cricket straight into the torn stomach.

"So much for not being a vampire…" I mumbled.

The body wiggled and squelched as Nuzzle swam around inside. He popped back out a few seconds later holding an eyeball in his mouth.

"Gross, buddy."

He dropped it by my toes and sat down, tongue lolling.

I tapped it off to the side. "I don't want it, thanks for thinking of me though."

The lizard chased after it, brought it back and sat down again, tail going like mad.

"Oh, hell no. We're not playing eyeball fetch. That's where I draw the line."

He pouted with puppy dog eyes, but I didn't give an inch. Then he promptly shrugged and swallowed the eye whole.

I focused back on the dead Sin, trying not to think about what sort of creature I'd accidentally bonded.

I opened my Second Sight, hiding the details behind a dark veil, focusing on the prize within. A dormant Will. The eight-point star symbol orbited the solid orb. Astral. Another Affinity matching my own.

I eagerly slid my scythe into the Will. The symbol spun, burning itself into the orb. Cracks formed. Purple light broke through the cracks—Amethyst light!

My first Will above my own rank and I didn't even have to fight for it.

I wasn't sure I could have two Wills harvested and residing in my scythe at the same time, but it seemed to be working. The colored mist seeped into my blade and morphed into a purple aura, then dimmed, joining the orange of the kangaroo's Amber Will.

[Will successfully harvested.

Rank: Amethyst

Affinity: Astral]

What remained of the body mummified and vanished. Some of the blood in the sand drained away, apparently belonging to this Sin and not Allium. I picked up the item left behind and added it to my inventory.

[Cast Iron Pan

Effect: Unknown]

With that accomplished, I had no desire to stay anywhere near here. I could still feel Allium's open, dead eyes staring down at me from the lighthouse. But first, I wanted to make sure I wasn't followed.

I walked toward the dune leading back to the Meadow, making hard footprints in the sand, and climbed a portion of the way up. I was pretty sure in real life there was a rule about not climbing dunes. Erosion or some shit. No such signs in Penumbra.

If anyone came upon this scene, hopefully they'd assume any survivors travelled back toward civilization.

I walked backward, returning to the bloody sand, then headed south in the ankle-deep water to avoid any trail. Maybe I'm being overly paranoid, but better safe than sorry.

It took about twenty minutes to reach the pillar, which stuck straight out of the sand near the water's edge. A half circle of ice coated the sea around it, while the sand around it twinkled with frost. I approached, leaving Nuzzle back on the warm sand. He seemed rather averse to the pillars. Either he didn't like cold, or something about the bizarre energy of these things didn't sit well with him.

Honestly, it didn't sit well with me either. Light should never be black.

As I neared, the blood on my skin and clothes froze, making them stiff and crinkly. I still hadn't cleaned myself. That would be next.

I summoned my scythe and inserted it into the pillar.

Thankfully, the gorey graveyard was long out of sight and around a bend in the coastline.

I relinquished the Aegis Affinity Amber Will from the kangaroo Sin easily enough, offering it to the shield icon in the starry sky. It rewarded me with an augmentation to Stamina and Defense. As I came back to myself, the Amethyst aura of the other Will still glowed from my scythe.

I guess you can only release one at a time.

I inserted my blade back into the pillar. It pulsed.

[Will Relinquish Commencing.

Rank: Amethyst.

Affinity: Astral.]

[Will Relinquish Failed.

Rank not compatible.]

Huh… Well, that was unexpected.

I tried again with the same result before giving up and joining Nuzzle outside the circle of cold. So, this Will was just going to sit in my scythe until I ranked up to Amethyst? That was kind of a bummer. I was hoping that relinquishing a Will higher than my own rank would provide bonus boosts or skills. Of course I wouldn't be so fortunate…

Ah, well.

I walked toward the sea, removing my shirt, boots, and pants, tossing them into the sand as I went. My cloak wouldn't come off, of course. The sea was up to my chest when I finally stopped walking. Nuzzle followed, paddling along beside me. I didn't even realize he could swim.

I dunked under the water, enjoying the shocking warmth—considering the ice a few feet away. The water bloomed red around me as I scrubbed my face and hair; arms, legs, and chest.

Once I'd purged the stain of death from my body, I floated on my back for a while just staring up at the sky. It was bright where we are, but I could see dark clouds rolling in to the south—the first I'd seen since I got here.

Not foreboding at all…

After floating there a little longer, I went and retrieved my clothes. I wrung them out, soaking and twisting repeatedly until the water I squeezed out wasn't quite so bloody. The Amber cloak remained dry and bloodless throughout it all—even seconds after dunking into the water.

As if the darn thing wasn't peculiar enough already.

I lay the clothes on a flat rock nearby to dry and found another rock that would make a decent seat for myself. Nuzzle lay splayed out on his back beside me, basking in the sunless light.

We were finally allowed a little rest after all that…

For about three and a half seconds.

A distant, agonizing scream pierced the air. Nuzzle jumped three feet off the rock, hissing like a feral cat, and a row of inch-long spikes extended out his spine. Goosebumps formed on my arms.

It wasn't a scream of fear or anger.

No.

This was sorrow.

Incomprehensible sorrow.
