When Callie woke up, she couldn't understand where she was. It didn't feel like her bed, and she felt herself… rattling. It took a moment for her to realize that she was inside a moving wagon. She tried to sit up, but found that both her hands and legs were bound.

"Wh-What is going on?" Callie asked fearfully, looking around the place.

Wait… The inside of this wagon was familiar. This was Silas's wagon. She recognized the large shelf and the couch, although she was currently on the floor, with a blanket underneath. It was cold, but oddly enough Callie did have a coat on, even though it was too large for her.

"Oh, you have woken up. Good morning!" Silas's voice resounded from the front of the wagon.

Callie felt even colder. He sounded just as friendly as before, which didn't match the situation at all.

"You've kidnapped me!" Callie blurted out and tried to squirm free of her bounds, feeling panic rise within her stomach.

"Hmm… Yes, I suppose I have," he replied nonchalantly, his voice still sounding friendly.

Callie didn't understand how this could happen. When did this even happen? The last thing she remembered was going to sleep. Some noise had startled her awake, and something had forcibly grabbed her. She had tried to resist, but her assailant had pressed something against her mouth and nose. Everything from that moment felt as if she had been stumbling through fog. She couldn't recall it properly.

"Wh-Why are you doing this? L-Let me go!" Callie demanded, her voice trembling.

"I think you know very well why," Silas replied, his voice growing colder. "Your snooping has ruined many plans I had set up in Greyfen. First, you free that glimmerhare. It was very hard to get one in a trap, you know. They are surprisingly easy to startle and usually don't even wander around the forest."

"But it was one glimmerhare. I was willing to let that one go. But then you went ahead and found the glauxis egg." His voice was low and angry. "I was going to get a lot of money for that egg. A LOT of money. I had no clue how you managed to find it. I thought I hid it well. But fine. Let's say I wasn't careful enough."

"Still, I couldn't shake the feeling that there was something unusual about you. The whole village searched for that egg, yet you were the one to find it. I had to find out more. So I set up a little trap for you."

"A trap?" Callie gasped, struggling against her bonds.

"Yeah. The thunderfoxes, remember? That one I set up specifically for you."

"F-For me?"

"Yes. I wanted to see if you could find the mother. The rangers were an unwelcome development, but even so, you completed your task," Silas laughed, sounding… delighted. The noise made Callie tremble with fear. "But even so, you came through. You actually found that thunderfox. I have no clue how you did it, but at that point I was certain you had some unusual ability to find arcane beasts."

"I have no such a-ability!" Callie tried to deny it.

Silas chuckled. "Yeah, it is hard to believe. You're just a little girl. But you finding the drownhart proved everything I needed to know. Although admittedly, I'm still mad you freed it. I had been chasing that drownhart for a long, long time. But it's impossible to get to it during summer."

"So I waited until winter, for the swamp to truly and completely freeze over. Then I set up my trap. You even helped me out by finding those lantern mushrooms for me. Thank you, Callie!"

Callie felt sick to her stomach. She didn't want to listen to him anymore. But she couldn't get free. She had to get free somehow. Maybe with magic? But that was dangerous. He already knew too much.

"But then you ruined it all. Got to the drownhart before I could. You even brought your mother along. Had it been just you alone, I would have snatched you up right there, but I'm not foolish enough to challenge the best hunter in the village."

Silas sighed. "Even worse, you went ahead and figured out that it was me who did all those things and snitched on the village chief. I had run away real fast, but there's no way I would leave empty-handed. Good thing that your family seemed so distracted with other things. I could snatch you up real easily. I suppose people in the village don't worry much about security."

"What do you want me for? Are you going to hurt me?" Callie asked, feeling her breath grow ragged. She could also sense her magic starting to swirl within her uncontrollably.

Temper your heart. Temper your heart!

Callie tried desperately to keep it under control. She couldn't let it out. She couldn't reveal her magic to this man!

"Hurt you? Heavens, no!" Silas laughed. "Why would I hurt you?"

"Because… I ruined your plans?"

"Yeah, you did, and I am mad about it. But if I get you in exchange for losing the drownhart and glauxis egg, then I think it'll be worth it."

Callie swallowed hard, her throat feeling dry. "What will you do with me?"

"I'm not going to do anything to you. It's about what you will do for me. You'll be finding me all the monsters. With you, I won't need to track those things for months or wait for the right circumstances. You'll lead me straight to them."

"NEVER!" Callie screamed.

Silas merely laughed. "Oh, dear, sweet Callie. You don't get a choice. We'll be going far, far away from this region. To make sure your pesky parents and brother don't interfere, and I'll be making a fortune."

Tears began to well in her eyes. He was taking her away from her family. No, no, no! She didn't want to be away from them.

"Take me back! Take me back!" Callie screamed in panic.

"Shhhh! I know it'll be a difficult change to undergo. But you're still young. You're adaptable. You'll learn to love my way of life. You'll get to experience all the best things in life, unlike in that pathetic village where you didn't know whether you would even survive the winter. So long as you obey me, everything will be just fine."

"No! I won't obey you! I won't!"

"It's okay. Scream out your frustration. I felt frustrated too. You'll see that it'll all be fine in the end," Silas said in a sweet voice.

Callie couldn't believe her ears. Did he really believe that she would just go with him? That she would seek out arcane beasts on his behalf so he could kill them, exploit them, and sell them to dragonbloods. No, Callie wouldn't accept this. She wouldn't let herself be used like it.

Terrible anger rose within Callie, and her magic responded in turn. Any thoughts of stopping her magic were gone. If he thought Callie would calmly, then he was wrong. She would take this man down, even if it meant that she would go down with him.

"I won't let you!" Callie announced and allowed her magic to run wild.

She had been keeping it under tight control the whole time. Always careful not to show it. Always careful to restrain it. Always careful to make sure it didn't hurt anyone. But not anymore. Callie allowed her magic to run free and run wild.

Fire erupted from her hands, instantly burning the rope that bound her legs. It exploded outward with a violent WHOOMP, charring much of the shelves, the bench, and ceiling of the wagon and making Silas yelp in pain.

"What the hell?!" The man exclaimed, looking into the cart.

Callie had already sat up and used her magic to free her legs. The man gasped, having witnessed the display of magic.

"Y-You're a dragonblood. You can do magic…" he said, and began laughing. "This is even better than I thought. I'll be set for life if I bring you the dragonbloods."

Callie did feel a bit of fear for a moment, but only for a moment. It was quickly forgotten as rage burned within her. It was begging for her to release it.

"You will not bring me anywhere!" Callie announced, and let the fire loose again. WHOOMP!

It raged through the wagon all around Callie. She could feel the heat, but the fire didn't hurt her. Instead, it swirled around her in a small hurricane, burning everything in its path. Silas was forced to jump off the carriage.

Callie stopped the flames, although much of the wagon was burning now. She ran to the back and jumped out, running back towards the village as quickly as she could. Which wasn't very quick. The snow was deep, and even with the wagon trail, she couldn't move very fast because of the large clothes that were weighing her down.

"Don't be stupid, Callie. I know you're a smart girl. Let's talk it out," Silas said, but Callie didn't miss the small blade he held in his hand.

Callie felt herself shiver from fear. How could she hope to defeat an adult? Even with magic, it wasn't like she had a limitless supply. Also, Callie wasn't sure if she could attack Silas. He was still a human. Callie didn't want to hurt anyone. That was not who she was.

"Let me go home!" Callie demanded, and let a stream of fire into the snow between them.

Silas took a step back, but clutched onto the blade more tightly.

"No, Callie. I will not. You have cost me too much money, but at the same time you are the most valuable thing I have ever come across. A child at your age, able to wield such powerful magic. I don't know how your family managed to hide that, but the secret is out now. You got no place to run or hide anymore."

"Leave me alone!" Callie demanded, her heart thundering so hard in her chest that it hurt.

"Be reasonable. You will live a much better life. You'll be powerful, well-fed, and happy."

"I will never be happy without my family!" Callie screamed.

Silas clicked his tongue. "I suppose there is no choice then. I wanted to do this peacefully, but you are forcing my hand. You will be coming with me one way or another. I'm sure the dragonbloods won't mind too much if you're missing a limb or two. We'll say that a monster got to you."

The blade glinted, and Silas jumped forward towards Callie, ready to strike. She screamed and released a powerful plume of fire all around herself. This forced the man to stop and melted all the snow all around her.

But her magic was running low. She felt herself gasping for breath. Fire magic was strong but it ate so much of her energy. One more blast of such fire would be all Callie could manage.

"Getting tired?" Silas sneered. "Even magic has its limits, and you're no exception. Last chance, Callie. Come with me peacefully, and you won't get hurt."

"NEVER!" Callie shook her head.

Silas once again jumped forward, and Callie once again released a fire all around herself. He backed away, but smiled. Callie was out of energy. She was gasping for breath and barely able to stand on her feet. Tears welled in her eyes.

Was this really all she could do? Would she really get separated from her family and never see them again?

"Maybe I should tell the dragonblood about your family. Have them punished for hiding you for so long?" Silas sneered, coming closer to Callie.

Tears ran down her face as she shook her head. What could she do? What could she do?

"Get away from my sister!" Callen suddenly yelled, startling both Callie and Silas.

At some point during the fight, he had managed to get right up to them, riding on Gale's back and holding Mom's sword in his hand. Callen didn't hesitate. From atop Gale, he swung at Silas, forcing the man to back away, closer to the burning wagon.

"You will not hurt her!" Callen positioned himself between Silas and Callie.

Callie scrambled to get to him, but Silas jumped forward, intent to cut Gale's leg. Gale reared up, neighing loudly. Unfortunately, Callen hadn't been holding onto the reins rightly enough and fell off, thumping into the snow.

Silas didn't waste the opportunity and jumped to strike at Callen. Callie screamed, but Callen managed to defend himself with the sword, and even push Silas back a little.

"I will not let you take my little sister!" Callen announced and stood bravely to face the older man.
