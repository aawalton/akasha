After a couple of hours, Luke came back to the pedestal, ready to purchase a restore. Wounds covered Luke’s body from head to toe, comprising of corrosive flesh and a few gashes that went along his arm, which had gone deep. Blood flowed down his forearm, dripping off his fingers, leaving behind a trail.

In that short timeframe, he had killed many green and even blue slimes. Even when weakened, he still needed to train, even if he had just come back from death’s door. He fully understood that taking breaks would not benefit him. Aside from the absolute necessity of it between returning to the pedestal and taking a short breather.

Pressing his hand on the pedestal, the veil presented itself before him. His eyes darted toward the restore price, costing him only 5 points for his current condition. Sometimes, even reaching an astonishing number of 14. Depending on his injury, he could sustain less overall damage. However, he might need to spend more points. He often fought many battles and killed many slimes, which saved him points overall before he would go for a restore.

The points vanished, with the same blessing of energy removing all his wounds. He could only take a breath of fresh air after the wonderful feeling.

“If only this place could provide some more leniency,” Luke grumbled under his breath.

But his eyes shifted towards the amount of points he had amassed.

An incredible 100 points stood before his eyes.

It didn’t take Luke long to consider his options, pouring almost half of what he had into constitution. It felt like the best option to keep himself alive in battle.

Constitution: 4 → 9 [48 points]

“Maybe I will finally be immune to all that acid those slimes keep shooting around." He chuckled while staring at the rest of the points he had. He ended up with 62 points remaining.

Shoving roughly a quarter of the original amount into soul resonance, he had a feeling that it would help him when he tried to get the souls under control. He remained anxious about not being able to access his soul chamber, and without it, he couldn’t truly control or fully use its power. But he focused on just his attributes for now.

Soul Resonance: 3 → 7 [25 points]

Luke wanted to save at least 10 points and poured the rest he could into physique.

Physique: 4 → 6 [12 points]

Luke peered back at his remaining 15 points.

Should I upgrade agility?

He hadn’t really encountered any issues with his speed falling behind that much during his fights, but it wouldn’t hurt to increase it at least once.

Agility: 3 → 4 [4 points]

Luke watched his points disappear one last time, leaving him with 11 points. He felt a shift in his body as the attribute effects finally took place. Luke almost passed out while falling toward the floor. He barely stopped himself as an overwhelming power filled him, as if he could accomplish and crush anything within his grasp. His heart beat rapidly.

His muscles bulged and twisted. He watched as if things were crawling underneath his skin as it wiggled around his body; points of his body convulsed, like a heartbeat. But he strangely felt no pain; the effects slowly died down. Before his very eyes, his muscles, which were barely defined, gained a tiny edge that wasn’t there before. His skin became rougher, as if nothing could pierce it anymore, and he felt a deeper connection to the souls in the sky, which suddenly became known to him.

Now he had finally gone through his first big change in his attributes. Luke studied his status while nodding his head at his accomplishments.

He was abruptly brought back to the present, realizing he had gained more souls than he’d lost from using it in all those previous battles. But he still found this to be an excellent opportunity to work on many things, like fighting more while being single-handed. He hadn’t given it much consideration in the way he fought, and with this new attribute, maybe he could finally do something new.

He could even lessen the amount of burden he had to put on himself by finding a better way to fight or add subtle elements of soul energy. But that was clearly the end goal for him.

So fighting against these slimes as his only opponents was really his only choice. A few hours of fighting green or blue slimes a day without the use of soul energy might be something he would implement into his current schedule if he even had any.

Especially the rewards from just one day of fighting compared to previous fighting sessions, which all ended in him only gaining 20 points, he could only blame it on constantly needing restores, and he was after experience instead of points.

It would be a slow grind in the future to even get some of his attributes up, but it was all he could do. He got to this floor without even investing a lot of points in himself.

But he would gradually figure out he could only advance on some floors if he had high attributes as a foundation; maybe he would even somehow meet people in this weird place, or at least he hoped.

He found it impossible to explain his thoughts about this place.

Thinking back to the people who were ahead of him in harnessing energy, maybe he could learn something from them that would really help in bringing himself past a few floors. But getting to the point of being able to even chat with them would be the first step.

Truly being stuck here really made him question why they sent him here out of all people. He rubbed his hand across his head and felt nothing but his rough scalp. He couldn’t really tell if he could grow hair anymore, with how it all got burnt away.

The fire slime literally burned everything off besides the few rags that hung from his waist.

Without further ado, Luke pressed harder on the pedestal surface as he summoned a blue slime. Time to test his new, increased attributes.

Luke stepped toward the blue slime that stood there, waiting for the slime to notice him as he closed the distance between them. The slime suddenly perked up to see Luke launching himself towards it as fast as possible, stopping only a few steps away.

Luke repositioned himself as he lowered his body into a stance, right foot slightly ahead of his left one, shoulder-width apart. With his right arm held up in a particular way. He pointed at the slime.

The slime leapt towards him as it formed parts of its body into spikes. But Luke was ready. He briefly dodged the strike as he redirected the slime's attack with his right arm, twirling around to face the slime going in the outside direction.

Luke approached while punching his arm out with all the strength he could muster. Like a bullet, his fist collided with the slime; parts of its unguarded side exploded as gel shot out everywhere. A fist-sized hole leaked fluid from its side.

Without even missing a beat, he shifted his leg forward and spun on his left foot axis, swinging his leg directly towards the blue slime core, cracking it the moment his leg collided.

One mistake of the slime had cost it its life, as Luke stood there panting, gathering his wits back. He walked towards the pedestal, making his way past it and instantly falling asleep on the ground.

Luke woke up shortly after with sore muscles. His right arm felt inflamed, and it sometimes twitched uncontrollably as he got up. Wiping away the dust that was left on him, quickly stretched his limbs. He stood before the pedestal once more, eyes darting towards his points rather than towards the sky.

What to do next with these points?

Spending was good and all, but he still had to at least save some for an unexpected situation. Without much thought, Luke just ignored the points and got back to fighting the slimes for the day.

The only thing that could stop his boredom in this empty void was occasionally staring out at the sky to watch the souls float suspended above. His heart yearned for more power, as each soul represented a power source he could rely on. Sighing, he just paced around the island in his off time, as the inflammation of the wounds from his continuous slime fights continued to eat away at him.

Not minding such pain, he took it as a testament that he needed to train harder to gain more strength.

Luke lay on the floor as he even tried to peer back into his soul chamber, only for it to resist his attempts. This would have been the second day for him not to get a response or at least seeing what’s going on.

The fire slime is currently his most dangerous challenge so far, and not knowing what else lay ahead, really put pressure on him, not knowing how much he could handle or even survive in this place.

Emotions rushed through him; a darkness in his chest he couldn’t get rid of swelled up, his eyes tearing up as he wiped them away. Weeping away on the ground, finding it hard to even breathe.

His lungs burned; he barely registered that something within him had changed as he passed out crying.
