The creature growled again as its legs coiled up under it.

Just before it launched itself, I triggered Sovereign's Call. The yellow in its eyes flickered for a moment, shifting to a deep green.

I pressed my lips together as a notification popped up.

[Sathos, Champion, Shadow Eater, Level 269, Predator, Unknown.]

[Current Ranking: 1]

What the heck was a Champion?

I didn’t let my confusion stop me as I leaped off the island from next to the portal, and slashed at the Shadow Eater’s chest. While I didn’t want to fight it, if it planned on hunting Lenna, I was damn well going to change its plans.

She couldn’t stand up to a creature this level, and I wasn’t going to leave her to die here, no way.

Three thick golden lines sliced into the beast’s chest, cutting through the thick spiky fur that coated the creature. The strike knocked it back, and Sathos stumbled on the hole in the wooden platform.

Its eyes flickered back to bright yellow as he recovered from my Call.

My spear pierced through its center first.

Or at least, I thought it did.

Instead, two clawed limbs clenched at the shaft, stopping the tip from cutting into its already bleeding chest.

We both hung there for a split second, then I forced the spear to grow.

Sathos wasn’t expecting that as the shaft shot forward, along with a bright blue triangle tip that appeared out of nowhere.

“You die!” the beast said. Two additional limbs clawed at me, but they hit my shield that appeared a split second ahead of the claws. The shield shattered, but it gave me time to kick off of Sathos while his arms grasped at the empty air my spear left behind as it vanished into my inventory.

I flew backward, and landed near the edge of the raised dirt.

Sathos was gone. Nothing appeared on my map, so I focused on my tracking skills and the mouthwatering scent from his wounds hanging in the air.

He leaped in my direction, coming at me from the left, but I didn’t react at all.

At the last second, my spear appeared again, butted against the ground as he almost impaled himself completely. At the last instant, his legs stretched out like a putty and took most of his weight.

Claws failed about, then suddenly grew longer as he hung there. One made an impact with my side as I held the spear up, but my armor tanked it.

The wounds in its chest suddenly changed, and I rolled back, tossing the spear into my inventory again. A hole remained where I had injured it three times now. It was like I could see through Sathos, to the other side. The weird spiky black fur coated the sides of the opening.

Then, it flung its tail at me. Hundreds of thin threads shot through the air in my direction.

I dodged, rolling across the ground to the far side of the small island.

The thin threads curved to follow.

Eyes wide, I dove into the water and headed for the bottom.

The needles lost momentum in the water, then vanished.

I swam around the island, trying to figure out when to exit, when something crashed down on top of me. Six limbs tried to hold me in place, but my shield snapped around me.

My spear appeared in my hands as I stabbed up, letting my shield fall as the tip cut into Sathos’ spiky body.

All six limbs grappled onto my body, squeezing. My armor held up as the blue mana tip punched deeper into the center of its body. Dark liquid flooded the water, making it a little harder to see.

Pain rushed through me as Sathos squeezed tighter, desperate to win this battle of attrition.

That was when Strange leaped out of my shadow and swam for the island.

Seconds later, Sathos twitched a few times, giving me enough room to claw at one of his limbs. Golden lines sliced through the end, and it detached.

Suddenly free, I snagged my spear into my inventory before bursting from the surface of the water and leaping onto the island.

Sathos clutched his sliced off arm as the dark spiky fur started to coat the cut end of it. “You will die for this!” he yelled.

He leaped at me again, but I dodged to the side at the same time that Strange called into the air.

Sathos’ head snapped to one side and his tail flicked out, sending more of those thin needles in all directions.

Strange vanished into the shadows near the base of the portal, while I swiped in the air. Three golden lines sliced through the dark fur, and that lovely smell filled the area again, making my stomach rumble. My mouth watered.

He cried out and stumbled his landing, given the shortened two limbs. This time, I dove closer with my claws flashing.

I dodged under one arm that scratched at me, while the other hit a sliver of a shield I formed.

I latched onto his shoulder with my teeth, pumping as much poison into his body as I could while my claws dug into the armpit area. Black ichor drenched the ground as he screamed.

Three limbs tried to grapple me, but that only let me cut another one off. He tried to jerk back, but I went with him, teeth still attached. I wasn’t going to let go if I could help it, not until he was dead.

“Let go!” he yelled, bashing an arm into my back, and I finally snapped out of it as crushing pain knocked me sideways.

Another limb hit the edge of my hip, sending me into the water, which sprayed everywhere from my impact.

A chirp from Strange kept him off me as I scrambled to my feet. I leaped away again, trying to get my head on straight and clear the distractions being thrown off by my stomach. This fight was taking a lot out of me, and I’d have to eat soon.

Sathos’ bright yellow eyes followed my every move, and I triggered Sovereign's Call again. Deep green took over, and he stopped moving, his mouth partially open.

Strange darted away from the area, vanishing again into the shadows.

I aimed at his neck as I pivoted, leaping at the Shadow Eater. The golden lines reached him before the rest of me did. Black ichor went everywhere again, as the bright slashes cut deep. The yellow reappeared in his eyes as he suddenly clutched at his wounds.

“So close…” His words were cut off as both my claws sank into his chest, knocking him back. Water lessened the impact as his back hit the ground, yet his tail snapped out and slammed into my face.

Blood filled my mouth as something crunched, and there was pain. For all that was holy, the pain. My sight went dark, but I somehow kept digging with my hands, even as everything inside me tingled. Second sight flipped on, and my claws tore into Sathos’ neck even as his tail snapped out again.

Then, he suddenly went slack.

[You have gained bonus experience from combat for surviving against a level 269, Champion Sathos.]

[You have gained a level x 10.]

[You have gained territory.]

[Your ranking has increased: 1]

None of that mattered as I mentally shoved the notifications away. I leaned closer and dug deeper, trying to find the core. Everything moved in slow motion, like time had slowed down in the worst possible way.

Mana surged out of the healing crystal and into my body, lighting up my second sight. It cut off too soon, but the energy stored in the other crystals followed. It barely helped, as the back of my neck burned.

My face tingled, and I couldn’t even feel my eyelids. Nor could I smell anything.

Small rushes of energy filled me, highlighting various areas I hadn’t even realized I hurt. A few ribs had been broken, plus something wasn’t right with my hip. None of that compared to my face… and my neck.

Finally, I touched the core, and it was in my mouth before I knew it. A bright sweet flavor took over everything. Almost like a raspberry, but heavier somehow. I chomped down and struggled to swallow as my second sight flickered at the motion.

The tingling in my hands vanished, leaving no connection to them at all. My entire body went limp as energy exploded inside my stomach.

Second sight went completely dark. Then the energy suddenly went out, as it raced along my veins. The ding of a notification echoed in the back of my mind, but I couldn’t see it. Or move.

Strange crawled next to me and paused, not touching but supporting me with his presence. Concern drifted along the bond between us, but I couldn’t respond. I held onto the bond as everything else vanished.

Light bloomed as energy surged again, and I shifted out of my devourer form without meaning to. I hadn’t even realized I’d shifted into it. I violently shivered, then Strange nudged me.

I blinked once. Then again.

The portal stood in front of me, still dark, but the carcass that rested at my knees was gone. A thick coppery smell coated everything.

Strange stared up at me. “Alpha?”

“I… I’m okay…” I didn’t feel okay. Everything still hurt, and for some reason I felt very naked. Even while wearing my clothing. I swallowed hard, and my stomach growled again, but my kill was gone.

Food appeared from my inventory and I gobbled it down, just trying to center myself. It didn’t take long for everything to stabilize, and I sat up.

The stars glimmered overhead.

I opened the notifications.

[You have devoured a level 269, Champion Sathos and increased your divinity.]

[You have gained the skill: Devouring Shadows. Devouring Shadows: Your shadows are hungry.]

[Your monstrosity has increased by 10.]

A shiver went up my spine as the voice that echoed inside my head reminded me of the first notification about becoming a devourer. This had the same ring. Nothing like the descriptions the system gave me for my divinity upgrade. This wasn’t that. Not at all.

I scrambled to my feet and dust went everywhere. It coated my boots, my clothes, and my body, but slowly drifted off in the breeze as the enhancements caused it to fall.

Strange leaped free of the cloud, inching closer to the portal.

An outline of where I’d crashed to the ground remained filled with the dust. It stirred as a breeze blew through the area.

I swallowed hard, then started coughing.

Icy cold raced down my spine as the realization sunk in.

I did that.

My shadow did that.

I almost died.

The crunch of the impact echoed inside my head, a reminder of what had happened.

I shook my head and ran my hands over my face. My face had healed.

Somehow, I’d increased my divinity and gained a skill. One that reminded me of what Noseen had done to a bug long ago. Not only that, but my monstrosity had climbed drastically. Not a little, not a bit, but by 50%.

Last time that had happened, I’d gained all sorts of things.

Not this time.

Strange chirped again, drawing my attention. He stood in the middle of the archway of the portal. “Leaving?”

That had been the plan.

Lenna would know what to do.

I triggered second sight and it didn’t work. My lungs froze, then I forced myself to take a deep breath. I tried again.

Same result.

I closed my eyes for a few moments and just breathed.

Snapping my eyes open I used second sight. Everything around me changed as mana flickered into view, along with the looping patterns. I touched the edges of it and gathered mana to my fingers. The looping pattern brightened, then the portal flared to life, taking Strange away.

Silence filled the area. Nothing stirred.

Concern pulsed along the bond between Strange and I, snapping me out of the daze.

It twisted to panic.
