Duchess had come from Heaven’s Falls, so she knew how the aerocline worked. She’d also fought Fabulosa and had time to lay a trap. Fabulosa’s charge-into-combat philosophy worked until it didn’t. The tactic made sense in certain situations, but it had always been her default mindset.

Fabulosa had laughed when Audigger boasted she could handle her—“Did you hear that one, Patch? She says she has me!” Her words echoed Tardee’s before I rope-a-doped him out of the contest.

But there was more to Fabulosa than overconfidence. Her last words, “Make us proud,” also rang in my head. She had grit and wouldn’t want me stunned into inaction.

Mourning her loss was a luxury I couldn’t afford.

I forced myself to think. As the floor of the universe dropped out from under me, I reassessed my worldview. Witnessing the breakup of the last power couple, Audigger and Duchess may have been inevitable at this late stage.

I needed to move. Toadkiller had only been a day away, but after this ruckus, it seemed evident that he’d force his way onto a skiff as soon as he found one. The idea of opponents waiting for chartered departures seemed like such a ridiculous assumption.

I grudgingly admitted Audigger’s knockout simplified the situation. Racing two players to Oxum was easier than three. And who knew what complications Audigger could induce on these rickety airships?

The leaderboard feature revealed Darkstep wasn’t the overlord I expected. While he still might have high skill ranks, unless he picked up something comparable to Applied Knowledge, I didn’t see how he could have accumulated enough power points to pose a threat.

Duchess still had her shrunken head, but if she had been truthful about never being to Oxum before, it meant she wouldn’t have a large NPC alliance working for her.

At least Duchess had the sense to avoid bragging about how she killed Audigger. Now, two sets of gear rested hundreds of yards outside the harbor. I’d go after them, but with the lower aerocline’s deadly fog, dragging the bottom for items wouldn’t be possible with Toadkiller hot on my tail. I noted the general coordinates. Perhaps I’d be able to use grappling hooks to snag someone’s gear after this Oxum business ended.

Right now, it seemed best not to hint at the gear’s location. If Toadkiller knew where to look, he could send his demons to retrieve them. But if Duchess wasn’t blabbing about them, then I wouldn’t. It was anyone’s guess if Darkstep saw what happened—or if he cared.

Instead of seeking help from others, I turned within. With only one power point, I regarded my menu of potential powers.
