Tristan and Callie hurried to decide where they could hide the egg as they ran home. Anywhere within Callie's home was quickly dismissed. The house itself was far too small to hide the egg, and neither was the chicken coop. The stables were a bit more hopeful, but it was likely that the egg's presence would make the horse nervous, as it had with cows and goats.

This also eliminated Tristan's barn and his house, since, besides their ginger cat, they had another cat which was much more easily frightened. Not to mention the frightened cow and goats.

"I have no clue where to put this thing," Tristan said, sounding annoyed as he carried the rather heavy egg.

"We only need to hide for one night. Tomorrow, I'll return it to the glauxis," Callie said.

"Even if it's one night… Where do we put it?" Tristan asked.

"I… I don't know… I don't exactly have egg-sized hiding places available…" Callie said.

"Callie!" Somewhere in the distance, she heard Callen's voice.

"It's your brother!" Tristan froze immediately. Callie could feel herself break into cold sweat. Not only Tristan's mom but Callen was looking for them too.

"Don't you have some hiding place? You said you had a hidden base somewhere in the forest, didn't you?" Callie prompted, remembering how much he had gloated about building it himself.

"It's off-limits to girls!" The boy said stubbornly.

"Do you have any better ideas?!"

"Callie!" Callen's voice called again.

"I… Fine! But you can't tell anyone about it. Especially not other girls!" Tristan warned.

"I won't tell!" Callie had no issues promising that. "Just hurry!"

Tristan looked around, then motioned Callie to follow. They didn't have to walk long before they came upon what looked like a crudely made shelter, which consisted mostly of spruce branches. It was situated behind a larger spruce tree, right between two large pines.

Despite its simplicity, it was actually not that easy to spot, and the only way to get inside was by crawling beneath the spruce branches. Even for Callie, who was rather small, it was rather difficult, and they had to proceed doubly as carefully with the egg.

However, once inside, she was amazed to see how cozy the place was. Small, but still spacious enough for Callie and Tristan to fit. The ground was lined with moss and ferns, and there was even something like a small table, which looked like an old log used for chopping wood.

"This is pretty cool," Callie admitted.

"I know, right!" Tristan said proudly.

Despite that, Callie was nervous about leaving the egg here. "Are you sure nobody will find it?"

"Did you notice it before?" Tristan asked.

She had to admit that she hadn't. Not like she passed through this area often, but the secret base hadn't caught her eye.

"Okay, let's cover the egg and hurry home, before we end up in trouble," Callie said.

She and Tristan used some spruce branches and ferns to cover the egg and hid it by the log. It was rather obvious that the plants were placed there to hide something, and bits of white eggshells were peeking through. It was far from a perfect hiding place, but it was the best they could manage at that moment.

"Callie!" Callen called again.

"Tristan!" He was joined by the voice of Tristan's mom.

"This is bad! We have to go!" Tristan urged Callie, and they hurriedly left the base.

She didn't feel good about leaving the egg unattended, but being punished again would hardly help the matter. She would just have to hope that nobody found it.

Tristan and Callie both hurried towards the voices that were calling them, since both seemed to be coming from the same direction. Callie couldn't help but throw a last glance back at the hidden base when she noticed someone in the distance.

She stopped just as her heart did. Has anyone seen them? Who was that? The figure seemed familiar, but she couldn't see the face from afar. It took her a moment to realize that it was actually Mister Blackthorn. Was he coming from the same direction where the egg had been hidden? It seemed like they had narrowly missed him.

"Why are you stopping? Come on!" Tristan grabbed Callie's hand and pulled her away.

Callie allowed herself to be dragged away by Tristan, but she couldn't help the horrible feeling that was settling in her stomach. Why was Mister Blackthorn in the forest close to the area where the egg had been?

A tiny voice in her head asked—could he have stolen the glauxis egg? But she hurriedly dismissed that thought. He was her teacher, her brother's teacher, and a hunter who had protected the village for many years, even before Callie's birth. What reason would he have to take the glauxis egg and endanger the village?

She didn't get to ponder the matter for long because soon Callie and Tristan ran into Callen and Tristan's mother.

"There you are! I have been looking all over for you, you rascal!" Tristan's mom, Mara, glared at her son. Her tough demeanor softened a little when she noticed that he was holding Callie's hand.

"Why are you together with him?" Callen's glare, on the other hand, intensified, and for a moment, Callie felt that he would melt Tristan into a puddle with his stare alone. She didn't even know her brother could have such a scary look on his face.

She inadvertently took a step back. Callen must have realized that he was glaring and hurriedly smiled. "Are you alright, Callie?"

"What were you kids doing?" Auntie Mara asked, placing her hands on her hips. "It's almost sunset!"

"I… I was just showing Calluna my secret base," Tristan stammered out.

"Is that sooo…" Auntie Mara snickered, looking amused for a moment. Then her expression turned stern again. "Even if your secret base is nearby, you shouldn't stay in it too late, especially together with Calluna. Her family is worried sick, you know!"

"What's the big deal?" Tristan muttered, but when his mother glared at him, he hurriedly added. "I-I'm sorry."

"As long as you understand. Go along with Calluna and Callen and explain to their parents why she was back late. I wouldn't want her to get into trouble on your account," Auntie Mara said.

"Okay…" Tristan said, glancing at Callen somewhat nervously.

"That would be helpful. Thank you very much," Callen said, but his thanks were clearly addressed to Auntie Mara, not Tristan.

As soon as the woman was out of earshot, Callen pulled Callie's hand free from Tristan's grip. "You stay away from my little sister!"

"Why are you suddenly being so mean?" Callie looked at her brother. Tristan had actually been very helpful. She wasn't sure if she would have even found the egg without him. Not to mention the hiding place for the egg.

"Doesn't he always bully you?" Callen asked.

"I do not!" Tristan protested, which earned him a glare from Callen.

"He was very helpful to me today!" Callie said. She wanted to explain to her brother about the egg, but they were already coming up to their house, where Mom and Dad were waiting, both looking rather angry.

Tristan explained that they were at his secret base, which was not too far from his house.

"Since when are you such fast friends?" Dad asked suspiciously.

"Hush, dear!" Mom elbowed Dad gently. "Let kids be kids!"

Dad didn't look satisfied. In fact, it looked like he had a great deal to say. But Mom had the same expression on her face as Auntie Mara, and she ultimately waved Tristan off with a smile as he headed home.

"Next time you go to play with Tristan, you come tell us, okay?" Mom said with a smile.

"Y-You're not mad that I stayed out so late?" Callie asked cautiously.

"I understand how it is to get caught up playing with a friend. As long as we know where you are, it's all good," Mom said.

"But Maeve—" Dad protested.

"Hush. She was right next door with the neighbor. No need to get so riled up. Let's head inside and eat dinner," Mom said and ushered everyone inside.

Callie didn't quite understand why Mom appeared so happy that evening, but she wasn't going to complain about it. Even if Dad and Callen looked rather sullen. They were so weird.

Nonetheless, after dinner, Callie was determined to tell her brother the truth. She knocked on the door of his room, receiving a rather lackluster response.

"I-Is it a bad time?" Callie asked, peeking inside the room.

Callen came up to the door, looking apologetic. "No, no! Come in!"

As soon as the door was closed, Callie couldn't help but let some of her excitement out. "Brother, you won't believe what Tristan and I managed to do today!"

"Tristan and you…" Callen mumbled. "Did you forget how he bullied you before?"

"That's not important right now. We found the glauxis egg!" Callie said in an excited whisper.

"You what?!" Callen's eyes opened wide.

"Yeah! Although he did see the mark on my hand glowing since I accidentally used magic to find the egg…"

"He saw your magic mark!" Callen clutched at his head in despair. "What if he tells someone?"

Admittedly, Callie was worried about that, too. It was a very real possibility. But her mind was too focused on the glauxis egg to worry about that right now.

"We can worry about that later. The point is that we found the egg, and we can return it to the glauxis and have it leave the village in peace."

"That's good… yeah. That's great!" Callen said, somewhat in a daze. "Where did you even find it?"

"It was buried in the forest. I don't think I could have found it without Tristan."

Callen gritted his teeth. "I still can't believe you went to him for help, instead of me."

"I didn't go to him for help. He just happened to be there when I sensed the egg with my magic," Callie said.

"Was he stalking you?" Callen asked suspiciously.

"Brother, please focus! We need to return the egg to the glauxis. I'm worried that the thief might discover that we dug it out of its hiding spot and will find it again. We hid it in Tristan's secret base, but it's not exactly the best hiding spot…"

"You mean the twigs he arranged behind the spruce," Callen noted.

Oh no… If her brother knew of the place, then it likely wasn't quite as well hidden as Callie had thought.

"Brother… Somebody will find it! What will we do then? The thief might find it!"

"Surely whoever the thief is wouldn't be going to check his hiding spot every day. That would be rather suspicious, don't you think?" Callen prompted.

Callie paused for a moment, remembering what she saw before. "But I saw Mister Blackthorn in the forest."

"What?" Callen narrowed his eyes. "You're not suggesting that he actually took the egg. Why would he endanger the village like that?"

"No, no!" Callie shook her head. "I mean… I don't know. I did see him coming from around the same area where we found the egg."

"Are you certain about that?"

"It happened quite fast… so no? But isn't it weird that he was there right after we found the egg?"

"Callie, Teacher Blackthorn wouldn't steal the egg! He has no reason to do such a thing. In any case, we need to worry about returning the egg to the glauxis, not finding the thief, do we not?"

Callie nodded. "I can't go out anymore today. But could you check on the egg? Make sure it is safe?"

"I'll check on it first thing tomorrow morning, okay?" Callen said, gently patting Callie's head.

"But what if the thief finds it?"

"What egg thief would believe that some kids unearthed his carefully hidden egg and then placed it in their secret base? That sounds rather unlikely, doesn't it?"

"I suppose…" Callie muttered; she didn't feel much calmer.

"Okay, off to sleep you go. Tomorrow I'm going to return the egg to the glauxis!" Callen announced.

"You meant we right?" Callie said.

"Oh, no! You are not going anywhere near that monster," Callen said firmly, and the look in his eyes was just as firm.

"I am going! I'm the one who can talk to it," Callie argued.

"Did talking to it help you last time?"

Callie flinched back. Her last encounter with the glauxis didn't go quite as smoothly as she wanted, but in the end, nobody had gotten hurt.

"Even so, I am going!" Callie said firmly. As if to emphasize this, a dog barked somewhere in the village.

"No, you are not!" Callen protested, narrowing his eyes at her. More dog barks resounded.

Callie opened her mouth to speak, but the barking got so intense that Callie and Callen both couldn't help but look out the small window. It was as if all of the village dogs had suddenly woken up.

"What's got them so riled up?" Callen asked.

Callie shrugged her shoulders and was about to return to the argument when she heard a loud, deep hoot echo through the village, which instantly made the dogs go silent.

She recognized this call. It was the glauxis.
