It was early morning. The sun was about to rise. One side of the horizon was orange, and the other side was dark blue. Dew formed everywhere on the ground, grass blades, tree leaves, and dry branches. The atmosphere was cool and soothing. People were going out of their homes.

While everyone was preparing to start a new day, Elenora was seeing a legendary beast during its growth period. She was too shocked to think straight. And no, this beast was not legendary for its strength, it was legendary for having the strongest core at the weakest stage.

For Elenora, she felt as if she was seeing a child holding a legendary sword.

When she read about the core, she stopped petting the cat, leaned her body back just a bit, and looked at the sky in resignation. “What have I gotten myself into?”

While looking at the sky, she started muttering. ”Don't be surprised, don't be surprised, don't be surprised, don't be surprised, don't be surprised …. The cat has a nexus core, and it is about to become a strong beast, that’s all. It’s nothing serious.”

Elenora closed her eyes and took a deep breath, and calmed herself down, “huuuuhaaaaa …. Okay… I'm okay now… ”

Feeling the hand getting moved from its body, the black cat turned around only to find Elenora looking at the sky, muttering something.

Seeing this, it started meowing while looking at her face. “Meow… meow… meow…” This brought her back to reality.

Elenora looked down and saw the cat looking at her. The screen was hovering in front of Elenora’s face. As usual, the screen moved following the cat’s face. Now she was seeing the blue screen from the backside with all the text written backwards.

Seeing the screen like that, Elenora got a bit annoyed. “Huuuh… What do you want?”

“Meow… meow… meow… meow.” The cat kept mewing. Elenora didn’t understand what the cat wanted.

When the cat saw Elenora doing nothing, it moved forward towards Elenora’s leg and touched the lunch box with its paw, and looked at Elenora again. During all the movement, the screen kept its position intact in front of the cat’s face.

Seeing the screen keep its position exactly as before, Elenora started wondering. (Why does the stat screen keep moving like that?)

“Meow… meow…” While looking at Elenora, the cat mewed again and moved its paws up and touched the lunch box again. Elenora understood the cat’s gesture perfectly.

Earlier, Elenora put the lunch box on the ground to heal the cat using both hands, but later she forgot about the cat’s food entirely. Realising this, she put her fingers on her forehead and started rubbing it in annoyance. “Huuuh… Of course… You want food…”

She picked the lunch box, opened it, and put it in front of the black cat. The cat, without wasting any time, started eating.

As soon as the cat started eating the meat, the screen following the cat’s face entered the ground. “Why did it move into the ground!!!” Elenora was shocked.

She wanted to read more, but she couldn’t even if she wanted to. Elenora didn’t even know what to think anymore… looking at the cat, she understood what was happening and gave a look of resignation.

“So you’re unable to manage your stat’s bracelet, huh?”

Elenora started looking for the bracelet on the cat’s body. She wanted to move the bracelet so that she could read the stats. But there was nothing on the cat’s body. This was strange.

She immediately held the cat from both sides and picked it up. The cat hissed “hiiiissssssss”. It was hungry, and Elenora picked it up as soon as it started eating. The cat was not expecting this.

Ignoring the hissing cat, she turned the cat in every possible angle with her hands to look for the stats bracelet. But even after that, she found nothing sticking to the cat’s body. (There is a stats screen, but… where is the stats bracelet?)

“Hiiiiissssssssssssssssssssssss.” The cat hissed again. It was preparing to scratch Elenora’s face while it was being held with both hands.

Seeing that the cat was about to be aggressive, Elenora put the cat on the ground. Then it went straight to its food and started eating as if nothing had happened. The stats screen also entered the ground again.

(Maybe the bracelet is in the cat’s body... But… there was nothing when I scanned earlier… I should scan again, just to be sure.) She gently put her hand on the cat’s back and started using her elemental energy to scan it again.

Last time while scanning, she had noticed the core, but she thought that it was still forming. This time, she scanned the Core properly. It was denser than an elemental core, it was a Nexus core for sure.

After thoroughly scanning the whole body, she found nothing. “Where is your stats bracelet?? … … …”

She didn’t understand what was happening and kept looking at the cat. Suddenly, the realization dawned upon her. “Wait… …”

“You… You… YOU’RE A SYSTEM USER?????” Elenora asked loudly in shock and disbelief.

The black cat simply ignored Elenora kept eating. It was too hungry.

She finally sat on the ground from her crouching position and started looking at the cat. She was unable to digest all this information.

After finishing its meal, the black cat went near Elenora and started rubbing its neck against her knees. The screen was also moving, following the cat’s face.

Elenora braced herself, picked up the cat with both hands, and put it on her lap. She started petting it. The cat also purred. This was the only way to read the whole thing without getting hindered.

Her eyes went to the core stat again. She started reading from there. She was now sure that nothing could make her disbelief anymore. But it was the biggest mistake ever.

“Core level one, Enhancement level one, Natural Abilities scratch bite high-jump long-jump dodge, special abilities nothing, unique abilities aegis user temporary, physical attributes.” Suddenly, she stopped reading. “Hmmm!!!”

She leaned towards the screen and started reading the line slowly. “Special… abilities… nothing… unique… abilities… aegis… user… temporary… !!!”

Elenora paused as if her brain had hung up while working.

After a few seconds, she started reading again. BUT she had to read the same thing again and again. “Special abilities nothing, unique abilities aegis user temporary… … special abilities nothing, unique abilities aegis user temporary… … special abilities nothing, unique abilities aegis user temporary… …”

After reading the same thing multiple times, she looked at the sky again and muttered. “Of course… … … you have Nexus core, and you're SUPPOSED to have unique abilities… … Of course you’re SUPPOSED to have unique abilities.”

She looked at the screen again and started reading the same line. Subconsciously, she was trying not to miss anything. The stats were too unusual. “Special abilities nothing, unique abilities aegis user temporary, physical attributes, health… waaaaiiiit a second.”

“What is an Aegis user?? And why in the lord’s name is a unique skill mentioned as ‘temporary’???”

After a few seconds, Elenora realised something.

“Wait… don’t tell me…” She started reading again. But this time, she decided to read nonstop, no matter what. She wanted to make sure that her hunch was correct. Now, she had enough of being shocked and in disbelief.

Different attributes were mentioned after the unique abilities section. There was nothing special written after that. The only difference was that the numbers were higher than usual street animals. Given that the cat had a unique ability, this was not unusual.

But what unusual was that there was no mention of the system anywhere on the stats screen. Also, a unique ability was mentioned with ‘temporary’ written beside it. This only meant one thing.

Cat’s unique ability was the system itself.

Elenora never heard about the system being a unique ability. The system was supposed to come from contracted spirits. But here, she was seeing something entirely different. It was totally unexpected for her.

The cat suddenly stood up on her lap and started walking away towards the town’s border. It was going back to its den. Seeing this, Elenora sighed. “Huuuuuuh…”

Looking at the cat’s back, she realised. She needed to hide the stats screen. If anyone else noticed the cat walking with the stats screen, it would surely make the cat an instant target.

Elenora wanted to remove it, but she had no idea how to make it disappear.

Suddenly, Elenora got an idea. She was not sure whether it would work or not. Still, it was better to try rather than leaving it for the world to see.

She started running towards the cat. “Heeeyyy, stop… wait a second… Dawn… Dawn…”

The cat, seeing her, got startled, and it started running away. Elenora was trying to catch up, but the cat was too fast. They soon reached the Sylvane’s border. The cat turned around and started hissing.

“Hiiiiissssssssss.” It knew that Elenora was its food provider, but seeing her running towards her fast made the cat instinctively run.

Elenora stopped a bit further away from the cat and sat on the ground. “Don’t worry Dawn… I’m here to help you...” Elenora moved her hand near the ground, looked at the cat in the eyes, and showed her palm.

She was waiting for the cat to come. After a while, the cat moved forward, walked straight toward Elenora, and started sniffing her fingers.

As the cat walked forward, the screen passed through her hand and stopped just below Elenora’s elbow.

Her hunch was correct. The screen was made up of condensed elemental energy. That’s why it was passing through everything. This made Elenora’s work easier. She immediately started absorbing all the energy nearby.

Her goal was to absorb the stats screen entirely, along with all the elemental energy in the surrounding area. This way, the cat would become safe for the time being.

Now, Elenora was sure that the cat didn’t know how to use its unique ability. Otherwise, it would have deactivated the stats screen just like other system users. The unique ability must have been activated unintentionally, and it created the stats screen using the cat’s energy. Since the cat was not intelligent enough to understand the system and didn’t know how to use elemental energy, the black cat was not able to remove it.

It was not hard for Elenora to figure out.

While absorbing elemental energy, Elenora started getting exhausted. A huge amount of elemental energy must have been condensed to create the stats screen. It was too much for her to absorb. Elemental energy in her body was now overflowing.

She needed to spend her own energy to absorb the stats screen’s condensed energy. She moved her hand inside the stats screen and started absorbing the condensed energy, and with the other hand, she started releasing her own energy.

After a while, the color of the stats screen started desaturating. Parts of the status screen were also disappearing.

Seeing this, the cat was shocked. It was not expecting Elenora to kill its foe to oblivion.

Although the stats screen was not threatening, it was hindering her ability to see everything. Her hunting ability was also entirely hindered.

Before coming to the town, the cat had tried to hunt a squirrel. But its prey saw the blue screen and ran away. The cat stayed hungry. In the end, it decided to come to the town early and wait for Elenora and Cinthia to come and provide food.

The black cat didn’t move, only observed the entire process. It saw how Elenora used her hand to absorb the screen. It finally understood how the whole process worked. The cat simply needed to put its paw inside the screen and absorb it. Easy.

After a while, the stats screen was gone. There was nothing left.

Now, Elenora was sweating heavily. Her whole body was aching. Absorbing and spending the energy rapidly made her extremely tired. She didn’t even have the strength to stand up straight.

Without caring about her clothes, she lay on the ground. She needed rest before she could move again.

The cat, seeing Elenora like this, meowed and started rubbing its face and neck affectionately against Elenora’s face. “Meooood… meooow… meooow”

“Okay… okay… Stop… You don’t have to worry about me… Go… your children are waiting, no?” Saying this, Elenora petted the cat’s head and gestured to go away with her hands while smiling.

The cat understood what Elenora meant with her gesture and climbed the the town’s border and disappeared to the other side.

While lying down, Elenora started evaluating all the things she had noticed until now.

After a while, she muttered. “That Despicable monster… Is it trying to destroy a country?... … … uuuuuuHuuuuh…” She sighed with a long breath.

As soon as she muttered this, a storm covered the whole forest of Hylas. The town also was not spared. People started running to take shelter from the strong wind. Many things blew away in the air.

Monstrous amount of elemental energy started spreading everywhere, it was very dense. The source of the huge amount of elemental energy was in the forest not far from the town.

Elenora, feeling the energy, started sweating heavily. For some reason, she knew why the storm started suddenly and why she could feel a huge amount of elemental energy even from this much distance.

“If you’re listening… I’m sorry… Respected Lord of the forest, I didn’t mean to say that to you directly.”
