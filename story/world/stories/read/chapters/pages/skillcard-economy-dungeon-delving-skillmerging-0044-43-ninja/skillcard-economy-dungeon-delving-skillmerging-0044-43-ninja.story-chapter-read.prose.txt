Footsteps.

Thugs rushed in like sheep into a barn. They surrounded me in a circle.

The man who was a little too proud to show his hairy chest stepped forward once again.

“What do you want?”

“Those people you have inside those crates.”

“There is no one inside the crates, only goods.”

I sighed, “Why bother asking if you are not interested in listening? Just come at me already I have other places to be.”

“Shoot him—”

Guns got pointed at me and triggers were pressed. I hopped between them, taking down each gunner one by one, dodging the rogue stabs and slashes one after another.

A minute later all gunmen were down.

“Jump at him,”

I touched the ground with my left palm. No, that will too much. My fingers. Still too much. My pinky. Perfect.

I changed my focus from Shadowstride to Rotary Rend.

The floor cracked, the cracks travelled far enough to engulf everyone, while maintaining a safe distance from the crates. The ground shook, concrete broke and the earth opened.

“What the fuck is this guy?”

Everyone fell into the pit I had created. While falling, I changed my focus back to Shadowstride and hopped to the first floor.

I went to watch down the railing when suddenly was hit by a burst of pain.

I looked at my pinky. It was torn and twisted. Red and violet. It hurt. I ignored it or tried my best to.

I looked down.

There was a ten meter deep crater in the middle of the warehouse. Some were buried under rubble, some had gone unconscious from the crash. I hoped no one had died.

Someone tried standing. A piece of rubble fell on his head and he went down.

I used Shadowstride to get to the crates. I sensed something approaching. Something big and fast. A bull? No. I turned and saw the man who had been blissfully sleeping after drinking, charging straight at me.

“You,” BigBelly growled. “What did you do?”

“Made a nice pool for you guys.”

BigBelly screamed. He covered a distance of fifty meters in two seconds. I vanished and emerged at his back and punched him.

He didn’t even flinch.

He swung his hand back. I ducked and punched right in his biceps while springing back up. He did flinch this time. He recovered in a second.

“Finally a challenge.” I couldn’t believe myself but I was grinning. Had I always been this way or changed after growing stronger? Whatever it was, I was enjoying myself.

I vanished once again from the face of the bull like man. He frantically looked around for me.

“Above you,”

I jumped at him from the first floor. He reacted fast, shielding his face using his hands. I vanished again emerging in front of him.

I punched him in his big belly. Then punched him again and again and again and again. I was no boxer and had already forgotten MMA, so the punches were sloppy, but they packed enough strength behind them to take down a bull. When I stopped using him as a punching bag, he fell on his back.

I was breathing heavily now. This had taken more from me than it should have, but it was done.

BigBelly shot his eyes open.

Me and my cursed tongue.

“I will kill you,” he screamed. Steam rose from his body. His fat belly shrunk a bit. He stood up.

Some skill that burns fat for healing? Stamina? Strength?

He came it at, faster than before.

Definitely strength.

Both of us were sloppy in our punches as if never trained. I had trained in MMA but had forgotten it, he had never trained because his punches were packed with strength.

I got hit for the first time tonight. It was strong, so strong that I slid a few inches back and my hands ringed where I had just blocked.

Can’t let him punch me again.

His belly had gone flat now, and the excess fat in his hands, legs, and neck was burning away. More steam rose from his body.

Looks like he won’t be able to keep this up for long.

A faster and stronger punch came for me. It was not slowed down as before. He had grown strong enough to match my speed.

I hopped to the first floor to catch a breather, he came there in an instant.

The fuck is this guy.

He was trying to climb the railing, but it gave up under his weight. I kicked him in the face. He fell. I emerged from his shadow, and punched him in the back. I heard bone cracking but didn’t know if it was my hand or his back.

I hopped to the first floor again and dropped at him. Smashing my knee into his chest.

I moved away from him, observing him and as expected, more steam rose from his body. He vanished. Something hit me in the cheek and I went flying.

Shit,

I used Shadowstride to emerge behind him. I crashed into him with the momentum he had sent me flying.

I hopped away before he could react. He came at me in an instant.

Too fast.

There was too much steam, through it, I saw that he was almost out of fat. I was liking the fights, the feeling of beating opponents and absolutely owning them. But the man in front of me was stronger than me and I had absolutely no desire to fight him and get beaten.

But, it wasn’t like I couldn’t beat him. I didn’t need to fight him to win.

I hopped away. He ran and appeared in front of me. He swung his fist at me, and I was gone. I repeatedly use Shadowstride to make distance between us, staying close enough so that he could see me then hopping again before he could strike me.

The amount of steam was decreasing and he was getting slower.

We kept at it for a whole minute until BigBelly was nothing more than a husk. All his fat had burned away. He was a living skeleton now.

He fell and did not get up.
