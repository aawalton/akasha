“You can tip the scales of this war at any time. You’re breaking the balance.”

His tone turned vicious.

“Anyone with talent can become a powerhouse if given enough time. You're making that possible for too many humans.” His fingers twitched slightly, crackling with faint energy.

“And we will not allow it.”

Sora was doing everything he could to remain calm and not immediately jump at the demon's neck. The threat to his life didn't bother him, as he didn't fear death. However, the look the demon was giving to Kara was infuriating.

Before he could move, however, he heard someone else speak.

"Enough with your bullshit Abbott. I didn't come all the way here to chat with these trash." Norj, the level 260 Dark Elf player spat.

Abbott grinned at Norj's words, and spoke in Sora's direction, "Oh I almost forgot. This guy's not here because he fears your future. He's here because of what you did in the past."

Norj replied to his ally in anger, "Shut it!"

Pointing at two players, he said, "You, and you, take Kara and Salim. I'll handle Ralph."

Turning to the remaining players, he said, "Everyone else, get in position. Make sure they don't run anywhere."

Sora clenched his fist in anger.

'Another group of enemies that aren't underestimating us...'

Covering himself and his group with thousands of layers of [Veil] to stall for time, he sent a frantic thought transmission to Kara.

'Accelerate us!! Push it to the limit!!'

Not waiting for her answer, he immediately took something that was stored inside his body.

Sora looked at the item in his hand and sighed.

'I should have prepared more drops.'

He regretted his lack of preparation in the three days before update. While Kara, Alius and him farmed levels in Nigeria, Sora had created drops of [Blood Essence]. He had formed one every time his level hit the limit.

However, the quantity he had farmed was lacking, as every time he extracted a drop, he felt the purity of his bloodline thinning, and his overall power slightly reducing.

He observed small stack of drops in front of him and immediately started absorbed all of them.

'Less than a hundred drops...'

The first choice would increase his level and bloodline density. The second choice allowed him to control it and do whatever he wanted with the drop of [Blood Essence].

'Zriko, the werewolf I met earlier chose to burn it to gain an explosive increase in power. But for now, I'll choose the other method.'

Selecting the first option, Sora who was level 186, felt a massive amount of experience being absorbed, and saw his experience bar filling up.

'Just a single level? There's no way...'

Sora absorbed another drop of [Blood Essence]. Then another, and another.

He watched as the notifications he received were less and less frequent.

'15 drops to reach Level 190, and additional 30 to reach level 191 ... It's barely having any effect as the essence was extracted when I was level 180... '

He handed the remaining drops to his friends, keeping only a few for himself.

After a while, he saw their new levels.

They had used around twenty drops each, reaching a level close to his.

'Once you'll burn a drop of Blood Essence, you'll experience immense pain, and your bodies will be crippled for weeks.' He communicated with them, warning them of the after effects of what they were about to do.

Before they could give any answer, Sora felt a terrifying aura slicing through his [Veils] at a frightening speed.

"They're coming," he shouted.

Eloj turned to the man behind her and complained, "Raymo, why is Kro's maid so helpful, yet you, my butler didn't tell me about anything this whole time?"

It seems that his real name was Sora Hashino, and he was bo-"
