Unique Skills are rare but not unheard of. The easiest way to gain a Unique Skill is by simply being a progenitor. Pathbearers on newly Integrated worlds often gain a wealth of Unique Skills as they are the first to develop them, but as their dimension matures, they usually find their skills lowered in tier as the overflow of mana is converted to levels. This usually accelerates their development, but it also weakens one dramatically in other ways.

To retain a Unique Skill, one must continue building an unparalleled legend—experiencing and surviving events and battles so extreme or rare that no other Pathbearer might replicate the act. As such, an enduring Unique Skill is as rare as it is dangerous to gain.

Another way of acquiring a Unique Skill is to be bound to a Unique Path—an occurrence so remarkably extreme in circumstance that the likelihood is a magnitude less than individually gaining a Unique Skill itself through individual endeavor.

Standalone Skills are a magnitude harder to gain than even Unique ones. They are a recognition by the System that a skill is beyond any possibility of recreation by another—or that only a single Pathbearer might hold this skill at any point in Integration’s history. These abilities are usually tied to a Unique Divinity or a dimension that has been destroyed beyond recreation. More unusual circumstances portray magical lores that are too alien for other monsters or individuals to learn beyond a single surviving Pathbearer of a vestigial civilization.

Matters, however, change when a dimension exceeds the Legendary-Tier ambient mana threshold. At this point, the maturity of individuals and empires, along with the presence of the Animancy Skill—and other Legendary Magical Skills—will allow certain Pathbearers to engineer novel struggles for their young and their future. It is at this point in a dimension’s history that artificial unique skills become more and more possible.

And should someone fuse Animancy with a skill that allows them to extract or alter another Pathbearer on a deeper, fundamental level—like a surgery or social-engineering skill, for instance—then new avenues toward uniqueness open, though the gaze of Chorus might be drawn.

Such might present another set of variables to consider should one desire power above stability…
