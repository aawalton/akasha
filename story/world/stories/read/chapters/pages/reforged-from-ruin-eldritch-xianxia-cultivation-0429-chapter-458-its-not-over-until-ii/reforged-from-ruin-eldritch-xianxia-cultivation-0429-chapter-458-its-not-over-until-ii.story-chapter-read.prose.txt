Inside of that inverted sun, the pyramid holding Raika’s greatest victory against the Feng falls into a new realm.

It tumbles, slamming down onto multiple hillsides, cracking its frame in microscopic ways from weight thousands of tons and facing the consequences of gravity. Shreds of flesh flap uselessly in the air as it impacts against one surface, then another, before finally coming to a rest in a crater of its own making.

For a few moments, there is silence.

Thousands of eyes from thousands of cultivators take note of the sudden obelisk that has fallen amongst them. When no immediate danger presents itself, they turn away again.

In this place, there are more important things than falling buildings to take note of.

The world inside the inverted sun goes quiet again. Well. Quiet by certain definitions. To those who can hear, the silence is agonizingly loud.

The wind whispers of detonation and impact. The ground whispers of the dance of fluid in physics, blended with chemistry to generate motion and mechanism. The sky above whispers of stars and starlight, of terrible tribulation and blessed horizons. The grass bends and sways with the words of sword-law, the severance that unmakes one into two, and its edges scrape against each other with words of conflict.

There are thousands of people here, many of whom can easily detect the madness occurring outside. And yet, none move that do not have to.

Some understand how valuable the experience before them is, and refuse to let it slip out of their grasps. Others are too overwhelmed to think very much at all, trance-like from the world they have found themselves in.

The world screams pained sounds of enlightenment, and those who infest the holy corpse sup of the music it makes.

From within the pyramid, green begins to emerge.

Weeks of struggle culminate with roots and branches, at long last, breaching the material surrounding them. Hyper-dense supermaterials of bone and metallic ingredients deform under the pressure from within as the roots of a forest spread wildly, crawling into the cracks that they create to spread them exponentially wider.

It does not break all at once. It takes minutes, then hours, for each weakness to be properly assessed, for every inch gained to be sensed for danger. The thing within the pyramid is not a fool, not willing to risk everything and spend itself on a potential trap, and so it waits, gaining inch after inch of new freedom.

And yet, when nothing interrupts the roots, when no pressure crushes it back down, when nothing reaches through layers of physics to tear apart chunks of what it is, it acts.

With its roots expanded into every part of the prison chaining it down, the Feng within the pyramid flexes all at once and shoves out.

The pyramid shatters.

The size of a proper hillside and made entirely of materials engineered to be as close to impenetrable as a demigod could manage, the inverted prison detonates with tremendous violence. The force required to break it open, even weakened, takes the full and proper might of a Warrior, exhausted though it may be.

What emerges from within is not easily discernable as human.

A vaguely humanoid shape rises up out of the shadows of the prison, glowing with its own colors, casting illumination as its own sun. In place of clear shapes and connected anatomy, there are whorls of sword-violence, twirling whirlpools of Cuts shaped into a person-esque form, its edges tinged with hints of flesh and black robes between the silver. From its light and its shadows blooms a forest, hundreds of thousands of trees exploding out of the prison and expanding three-dimensionally in every direction, filling the air around the entity.

The world screams with the emergence of a demi-divine presence, capable of toppling cities and lesser countries, worthy of standing against the Heavens.
