Even wounded, the hunter was fast. Rex could sense his aura closing in on him.

I can’t escape. Not like this. I need something. Anything.

He considered climbing a tree. From there, he could shoot the hunter from afar. But what if he dodged the arrow again? Compared to the other hunters', Rex’s arrows looked like they were flying in slow motion. And he had a feeling that the hunter chasing him down was the strongest of them all.

It wasn’t just that, though. He was in pain. His chest and stomach thumped, and he could already feel the bruising settling in. In his current state, he wasn’t sure he could climb one of the nearby trees fast enough.

Screw that. Too risky. I need to find somewhere to hide. Somewhere easy.

Scanning the ground, he tried to push himself to run fast, one hand clenching his stomach.

“Oww. Damn. Hurts.”

Even wearing a tunic hardened to iron, taking a hit from a Freedman sucked.

What’s that? That might work!

Rex spotted an old trunk. It had rotted out, and a hole had been dug into it. It wasn’t big, but it was big enough for his skinny frame, which was perfect since he didn’t want the hunter following.

He squeezed through and scuttled as far as he could, pressing his back against the inner wall of the drunk, and drew an arrow. There was only a couple of meters between him and the entrance, and if the hunter followed, good luck dodging his arrow in such a confined space.

This is as good a spot as any. Come at me, you asshole!

Rex tried to settle his breathing as he aimed at the hole. He was ready for a fight, but in the best-case scenario, the hunter didn’t find him, and he tried to dim his aura.

The few seconds that followed seemed to drag on forever. And he clenched up the moment he heard the man’s heavy steps crunching through the growth and labored breath.

“Where are ya? Show yourself! I can feel you! You won’t get away. Not from me. Not after all you’ve done, ya little bastard!”

Rex tried to be as still as he could. But he could hear and sense the hunter getting closer.

I’m right in here, waiting for you.

He marked his arrow with [ Corrosive Touch ], and as he drew the string back taut, he felt weak.

Shit! That fatigue… I’m low on mana. I only need one good shot, though.

Whack!

The entire tree trunk shook and groaned.

“Found ya!” The hunter roared as he began to chop into the wood with his machete.

The hunter was strong, the wood was rotted through, and every swing he took chopped a chunk out of it.

“Haha! Nowhere to run now! I’m gonna enjoy this!”

He kept chopping, and soon rays of light pierced through the gaps of the cracking wood. Then suddenly, a massive chunk broke loose, bringing the two of them eye to eye.

Time seemed to slow in that moment, and Rex’s body sprang into reflexive action. He spun toward the hunter before his mind could even catch up, and loosened without thinking.

It was an awkward shot and not perfectly on target. But the hunter was barely a meter or so away, and in a blink, it had struck his shoulder.

“DIE!” The hunter had time to raise the machete into the air, but before he could bring it down on Rex, the acidic damage had done its job, melting through his shoulder, and his arm literally fell off his body, still gripping the machete.

For a moment, Rex thought he had done it. He had bested the hunter. Killed multiple Freedmen by himself. Then, he realized that the first arm he had wounded looked good as new. Not only that, but burns spotted across his body, which must have been caused by the shamans, were spotted by what looked like healthy, freshly grown skin.

He was healing.

“Arrgh. My goodness. FUCK! My arm!” The hunter shouted and stumbled backward. “And after I already used my rejuvenation potion! You little cunt! I’m going to struggle you to–”

An arrow thudded into his chest.

Panting, Rex nocked another and fired again, and again.

“You!” The hunter staggered forward, reaching out for Rex as another arrow pierced his throat.

He tried to speak, but blood just splattered out, and another arrow snapped his clavicle.

Even as the hunter tried to stop the fountaining blood from his throat, another arrow hit his chest.

And then another.

And Rex wasn’t done. He reached back for an arrow, but his hand found air.

Out?!

A madness came over the hunter’s eyes, and he released his throat and lunged for Rex. The two of them fell backward into the tree trunk, the hunter landing atop Rex.

He was bigger and stronger, and his blood was pouring all over Rex as his rough hand searched for Rex's face. His fingers were trying to gouge as he fought the man’s hand with both arms.

Rex wiggled, trying to move his face away from the man’s hand, but he was pinned down and unable to move much.

He realized that while the hunter might be short on breath, at this rate, he might not outlast him.

His mana was drained, and there were no good options, only desperate ones.

As the hunter scrambled, pushing himself against Rex, trying to grab at his face, he felt his breath. The man’s face was right there and within reach.

Rex released the man’s arm and stopped trying to defend, instead reaching out for his neck.

While Rex couldn’t cast his spells directly, he could use his Feat to create a spell circle on any surface he wanted. And in truth, it only took a second longer than casting spells directly.

The moment Rex grabbed hold of the man’s neck, he used [ Rune Scribe ], drawing a spellcircle across the man’s neck in an instant and activating [ Corrosive Touch ] before falling unconscious as he drained the last ounces of his mana.

At best, that kid is level 3. There’s no way he’d have handled half my boys alone.

But if it were the local lord?
