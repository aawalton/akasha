I’ll check in on him soon. Johan said it’ll be a few days before we really know what’s going on, so no point in dropping by early.

Her hand fell on top of his own as she presented a beautiful smile his way, “That’s more than enough, Cass. More than enough.”

He put his other hand on hers, nodded, then added the rest of the money to the pouch within his shoulder bag and made his way to the elevator.

Cass didn’t feel bad about the current state of his kidnappers. Okay, a little bad. But not enough to really push himself to do anything. His empathy had a strong boundary that drew a thin line with people who dragged him unwillingly across the territories.

Also, Cass knew for a fact Pellin wasn’t a thief, but that didn’t mean he wasn’t a borrower. Just one who doesn’t warn you when doing the borrowing.

Is happy the right word? No…ambivalent.

A bit of research proved his earlier planning right; there was a pure trend in what he could expect on certain days of the week. The Liberty Loaders collection rotated its Quest assignments, making it easy to slot in pre-made templates with all of their anchors.

But he also made some changes.

While the Quests were easier to predict and outline, he now had more information about Advanced Quest Formatting. Though Cass wasn’t using the AirScript Quill, drafting unto vellums from an outline still followed the same process. It took a little extra time compared to his old process, but he more than made up for that by using anchored templates.

Enough so that he was done before closing time yet again, receiving his two Vellum bonus before heading out the door.

Exiting the tower, Cass spotted a smiling Adya and a rowdy Bella.

Neigh, stomp, stomp.

Adya patted the draft horse's side, “What’d she say?”

He swiftly mounted up, his hands encircling her waist, “Apparently, I owe her several days' worth of Riding Quests.”

Bella kicked off as Adya called back, “That’s not so bad, right?”

“Mmm.”

Adya pushed Bella a little faster as she quickly looked over her shoulder, forcing Cass to adjust his grip. A heartbeat passed before she said, “It’s that rude man, Noah.”

“Ah. He’s tailing us back to the apartment to find Pellin. He told me earlier that Pell stole his gun.”

“Strange to hear that word. Nobody has seen a ranged weapon like that since not long after the Reshaping. You know, I haven’t seen Pell at all today. He’s probably out doing something Engineer’y.”

“Lucky for me that he’s not here to complain.”

Rather than head to the apartment, tipping off Noah to where they lived, Cass had Adya take him to a stableyard with a big, open riding yard. Ten minutes later, he laughed as Bella tried and failed to buck him.

“Oh, what a big, mean horse you are,” He called out tauntingly. “Can’t even throw me end over ass, now can you?”

He could feel that she took that personally, but he also felt her body shift as she prepared to rear toward the sky. Clenching his thighs, Cass shifted up and wrapped his hands around her neck. At their maximum height, he saw Noah hiding near a hay bale, ducking upon being spotted.

What an idiot.

“Face it Bella, you’ll never defeat me! This is the-” Cass yelped as she reached around and bit his leg, then pulled him off and threw him mercilessly to the ground with a neigh.

Adya was seated on the yard fence as she began to clap, “Nice one, Bella.”

Cass held up a finger, “I call foul.”

After dropping off Bella in the small yard attached to the apartment, they ate a special dinner with Cass’s salary earnings. The outdoor restaurant wasn’t the best in the Fifth Ring, but it had an amazing ambiance, with a woman singing and a small band accompanying as they ate.

Ignoring a fuming Noah seated at another restaurant across the way, Cass reached over and held Adya’s hand for a moment as the most recent song petered out, then their real mission for the evening began.

In between Alan’s training and Adya’s finding small work scouting the city for Orla, she spent much of her time just meandering around town. That meant she had a clear eye as she used Profile to analyze items and people in need within the ring. So on this particular evening, she took him to a small, dark area on the southern side, where there were not many people by his request.

Holy shit, I didn’t think she’d hit him. I was just going to explain that Pellin is his own person, with his own goals. Good God, that was a hard kick.
