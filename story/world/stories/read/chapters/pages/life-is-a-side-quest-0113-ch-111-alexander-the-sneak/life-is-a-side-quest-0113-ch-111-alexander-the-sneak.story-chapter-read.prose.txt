After they cleared the lightning plane, they dropped the cube. Min Min insisted they get some samples of the cube from it, so the prince used an ability to slice a few of the corners off. Min Min picked them up greedily before turning to Alexander.

“You picked up the sphere core, yes yes?” She asked, excitedly.

“I did.”

“Have you examine looked at it?” Her eyes questioned with a sparkle.

Alexander sighed and pulled it out for them to see. Once he held it up, Flick jumped on the orb and encircled it with his whole body. He squeaked with indignation that anyone but he would dare lay claim to such a valuable item.

“Flick…” Alexander started.

“Loot share!” Min Min chided the dragon.

The dragon snorted, sending a few sparks out of his nose.

“I do owe you some coinage for your stash Flick. I will make sure you get a fair cut.”

Flick didn’t seem satisfied, but he still backed off for the others to see.

Greater Water Elemental Core

Rare

A crafting item that contains a dense water affinity.

Alexander read off the description and Min Min let out an excited squeak.

“It seems a bad poor place for a water type monster.” Wensley pointed out with a thoughtful expression.

“Was the cube set up to protect it by the storm wraiths? Maybe they held this creature in some sort of reverence?” Alexander pondered aloud.

“No way to know for sure certain.” Min Min said, still enthralled by the find.

“If you want I can give you some resources you feel would be worth the value of this. If I don’t find a use for it Flick we can store it in your stash.”

Min Min thought about it. “Yes, yes. Many lots of experiments instead of one. This may be agreeable.”

Flick chirped from Alexander’s head.

“I’ll pull them out and hand them off before I leave this plane. I hope y’all will be able to make it back safely, maybe I’ll leave you with some of my single use enchanted items to keep you safe.”

Min Min and the prince glanced at each other before the prince responded, “How gracious kind of you! I am certain I could protect Min Min with my prowess, but it seems a fine good payment for her guidance.”

“Very well then. Let’s continue, the city is not far from here?”

“We are near close Alexander! Not long now.”

After half an hour, Alexander settled down on a hill that Min Min pointed towards. He still had a terrible time making out anything past thirty yards away. The shadowy outlines and depth perception tricks played on him as he flew. So he had to lean on the mice for any type of scouting info.

Min Min pulled out two large lenses and clipped them on her goggles before scanning the area.

“Oh… this is much concerning…”

“What?”

“There are many lots of bad people gathered. A thousand strong at least.”

“Are they all spread out or concentrated into a group?”

“Some together, some scattered they are busy bustling.” Min Min shared.

“Are they gather mobilizing an attack?” The Prince asked. “Should we warn Velvet Burrow?”

The group looked at each other in concern.

“We first need information. Can you disguise yourself enough to infiltrate that army?”

“We are to stature small and the mice folk do not work with the Warlock. Where is your portal gate located?” Min Min asked Alexander.

“It was in front of the pet store. When I went through it the building housed a variety of strange animals all in separate cages.”

“The twisting rooms likely. I have rumor heard of this place but do not know it by sight.”

“It was across from the bookstore, I believe he called it an arcanum.”

The two mice shivered at the name.

“You are a big, but skinny thin Giant… would you mind some extra weight?”

Alexander trudged into the town covered primarily by his yellow rain poncho. The overall look took the form of a hunchback with a beer belly, though his stomach may have been more akin to a rampant tumor. The group of fiends he approached was poorly organized, but there were a string of people blocking the dirt streets between buildings. A group of men who looked like lizards stood a little straighter as he approached.

“Halt you mutilated bug eyed colorful bastard.” One of the lizard folk called. “The rest of them chuckled.

“Did your mother never teach you how to dress? Or did you rob a fruity hedge witch?” Another lizard man with a nasty scar on his face called.

They wore minimal clothing, but the scales on their bodies likely made up for the lack of armor. Alexander kept walking forward right up to the man that called him to a halt.

“I suggest you make way, I am a powerful sorcerer who is vital to organizing the military operations gathered here.” Alexander said, waiving grandly. He flourished his hands, casting a minor illusion to crackle sparks in the air that floated around his hand.

You have gained a level in Intimidation!

Intimidation level 5

You have 2 points to distribute to your Intimidation skill tree.

The lizard man backed away and waved the others to do the same. Alexander didn’t even break pace as he walked into the town. He was glad nobody would be scrutinizing his extra weight under his poncho.

Alexander felt proud of his performance. He mentally acknowledged that his ability to perform had improved compared to his early attempts at deception.

“Yes, very discreet your costume disguise choice.” Wensley mocked, in a whisper.

“There was not much of a choice. It’s the only thing baggy enough to cover the two of you.”

He was forced to wander up and down several streets before he got his bearings. His short sightedness didn’t help. He managed to set eyes on the outline of the mountain he had once journeyed up in his last trip to this world. Mentally tracing his steps based on its outline and the buildings, he managed to find the row of shops.

Unfortunately, the space in front of the pet store was crowded with horrifying mutilated monsters. They seemed to be on some sort of rowed display. Many of the specimens had a reddish liquid covering that seemed to undulate randomly around their bodies. Some had extra appendages that didn’t seem to match their overall physical makeup. A few people of differing races examined the caged and chained animals.

He felt that he recognized one of the monsters from his first encounter, the feathered lion which originally had its own majestic form now looked like a scene out of a horror movie. Feathers that floated and moved like they were underwater were now stiff and jagged. They twitched menacingly as it growled at its prospective buyers.

Alexander whispered what he was witnessing to his passengers.

“It is a form of evolve mutated blood-curse.” Min Min whispered. “The Warlock has an Arcanum full of evil knowledge from other world places. They use ritual spells and blood infused resources. Forbidden it should be, but they would not care.”

“It’s truly terrifying.” Alexander muttered. “Should we wait for them to clear out on their own? It will likely take time to find the exact location to set your device. I don’t want to leave an open door to my world.”

“We set one device to infuse portal with magic, then after passing through another to disperse ruin the portal.” Min Min suggested.

“It would be to dangerous to draw attention here, so we just have to hope they move on their own.”

“Let us wander find information then.” The Prince suggested. “Study your enemy.”

“I think I agree, no need to rush a delicate operation.”

As they walked through the encampment, Alexander noted that there was a large variety of races making up the army. He saw what looked to be a dwarven race with white hair and beady black eyes fitting armor to a humanoid with a sinister wolf-like face. As he watched, a dwarf tugged on a strap, accidentally pinching and tugging on its hair. The wolf kin lashed out, striking the dwarf on the side of his head. To his surprise, the dwarf didn’t even flinch. The other dwarves outfitting troops nearby turned as one and jumped the wolf kin and swiftly pummeled him unconscious. When they returned to the other soldiers to resume fitting their armor, the rest of the beings being outfitted were on their best behavior.

“The drink tavern, to your left.” Min Min whispered, glancing through the poncho over his shoulder.

Alexander nodded and walked over to grab a seat. His back turned to a table of rough-looking badger kin. They talked crudely of different unsavory activities before turning their conversation to their next conquest.

“Now, you remember the women folk on the other side?”

“Oh, yeah. Those were good days. I can’t wait to revisit those screams… nothing quite like finding a city of unsuspecting weaklings.”

Alexander had a sinking feeling in his gut. He continued to listen, but they didn’t give any specifics of their plan. He wondered at how time would work here if they were having memories of his world.

“Wensley, how long have you been in this place?” Alexander asked.

“It’s hard difficult to say.” The prince said quietly with some weight in his voice. “We have sleep cycles, but time feels like a small thin concept. Memories are sometimes a heavy fog. We do know that we banned kit child bearing over a millennium ago because we could not sustain feed the growing population.”

Alexander felt Min Min shift uncomfortably on his chest.

“The Master of Earth was cruel evil to banish us here. We feel half alive and many of the good decent races have become slowly mad with resentment.” The prince sighed. “But we mice kin did hold to our community.”

Alexander was appalled at the revelation.

“Your people are far stronger than I realized. I would help you if I could.”

Min Min sat up. “Alexander, the truth is…”

“Hey!” A voice shouted from over his shoulder. “What are you muttering about over there with no drink!? This isn’t a charity!”

Alexander stood and turned to see a goblin-faced woman in an apron causing all eyes to turn on him. He was about to use his invisibility, but had a feeling that would leave two mice folk floating in the air.

“What kind of fruit are you dressed as?” A drunken badger asked.

“I think he wants to share some drinking money with us boys.” The table muttering near him laughed as five beast kin stood to join in the fun.

Alexander began to walk away. Despite the evil intent of these people, he still didn’t want to use force or draw attention. One of the badger folk grabbed his shoulder. He felt the rip in his thin plastic poncho. The badger was surprised to see the face of a small dragon peer out from under Alexander’s hat and spew lightning into his face. He shouted and convulsed as he tried to swat at his attacker.

A part of his brain clicked as he realized his naivety. This was a war that they planned, and regrettably all moral compunctions must be adjusted in wartime.

He made a sign with his hands, turned, and cast gusting wind at the group. The spell pushed through the group, shoving over tables and beasts alike. The others at tables cleared away, leaving him plenty of space.

“What the hells?” The drunk badger muttered, trying to get to his feet.

Alexander turned and strode away, hoping no peacekeepers would follow him. Though he supposed there were no peacekeepers in a group like this. He heard whispers as he left earshot.

“What was that? Did you see something under his coat?”

“It seems we will need to find new disguises.” Alexander muttered.
