Pushing his anger away, as well as the small voice of fear of ending up sharing this poor bastard's fate, Luke deepened his focus and extended a thread of mana. The void creature didn't react. Pulling out the almost-empty monster core with the slow care of a documentary presenter not wanting to startle its subject, he changed the thread, so it exited through Luke's own palm, headed into the core, and then into the creature. Once that was in place, it was time to see just how much resistance this thing would put up.

It didn't take much to direct the flow of Source into the thread. The void creature didn't move or stop trying to gnaw through the end seam. Not until Source hit the core. A powerful burst of will slammed into Luke, who struggled to keep himself together while knowing his physical body had just been flung across the street. The creature shrieked a thousand shrieks from a thousand mouths as it wriggled its tentacles, lashing out at the weave while pushing with remarkable strength against Luke's gathered will. It pulled on its Source, keeping it from the core like its life depended on it, which it did. Take Source away from the void creatures and nothing but void remained. You can't build something out of nothingness. Forcing Source away from his target was far more difficult than destroying it, and it devolved into a tug-of-war with Luke trying to force-stuff Source into a container and the void creature trying to keep it inside itself.

Perhaps it was Luke's imagination, but he thought he sensed something inside the chaotic opponent outside the weave: fear. The damn thing feared for its life. That was new. He hadn't even seen that in the two more developed ones he and Dimitrios faced. Perhaps there was some way to use that against it, but in that moment he couldn't see a way. Taking Source was a pain in the ass. Yes, he was the stronger one and more expert in wielding his will, but it wasn't by much. Using that method against Falzyxi would be... improbable. If others helped, maybe they stood a chance, but he just couldn't see anyone else being ready for it. The others, Dimitrios included, were behind with this stuff. Perhaps Popov would be able to do something using his spells, but Luke didn't see it working. Since this wasn't the solution he had been hoping for, alternatives were needed, and they were needed soon. He had an idea, but it would be one full of risk, not only to himself but to his friends. A lot could go wrong even if everything worked out the way Luke hoped. Even so, he saw no other way. Time to get started.

After killing what had once been the Integrated Dimitrios dubbed Philosopher, Luke reached out to his friends and explained the plan. Like most of his plans, it was simple but not easy. Siphoning Source from Falzyxi was an absolute necessity if they wanted Earth to still be around after it was all said and done. If doing so by using empty cores wasn't possible, they would just have to use the one method they knew worked: pulling it from the void being and putting it into a dungeon.

Explaining what he wanted to do took some doing. Milla followed along as best she could, but her focus was on getting Earth back on its feet and back into modernity by working with a bunch of other guilds in New Belgium. What he needed from her was a sign-off to use members of The Band in his little operation,' as she called it, just to tease him. She OK'd it, of course. He didn't need her word before approaching the others, but it didn't hurt to play nice. She was his sister, after all. Gathering the party after that didn't take long. There was no shortage of friends willing to risk themselves on dangerous schemes. Luke wished he could join them, but he would be needed in the weave, doing the actual siphoning, and it wouldn't be long before it was time. Luke could feel the approach of Falzyxi. It was a little strange, since he hadn't been able to track the other void being's movements, but he chalked it up to having grown stronger.

What he didn’t expect was to face opposition from Popov. The man was irritable from the moment Luke appeared, and judging by the many Integrated scattered around the outside of his small home, he had perhaps taken on more than he could manage in teaching everyone. Hannah had left to go try her luck being taught by Dimitrios, leaving him with little in the way of help. And here was Luke, coming to ease his burden by taking one of his pupils.

“Of course I’ll come help,” Penny said with a beaming smile. “This stuff right here is too much like school, and I’m so done with school!”

“She is near a breakthrough,” Popov grumbled. “Penny is very promising. Her workings are nearing my own in refinement. I cannot spare her. It is for the greater good, you understand? The void approaches.”

Penny leaned closer to Luke. “My spells are way better than his.”

“Your harebrained plan is sure to fail. Toying with forces beyond our understanding will lead to doom. Where will that leave those who follow you through this foolish quest? Let us face the threat of Falzyxi together instead. All of us must be enough.”

The more agitated the Grandmaster became, the more he spoke like some wizard out of medieval times. Perhaps the class he chose was getting to him.

“It’ll work,” Luke said.

“Hubris is nothing more than the tall cliff from which you will fall.”

Luke didn’t feel like arguing. He already knew the path he’d staked out for himself and his friends was a risky one, but it couldn’t be helped. Conventional attacks would not work on the coming void being. Only he and Dimitrios had killed a void creature, and those had been small ones, nothing compared to Falzyxi. The damn thing had eaten a whole system. Once everyone who Guide gathered were strong enough to stand on their own, Earth would have a chance, but that was not now. They weren’t even close to ready. Luke’s plan was the one chance they had to keep going and get stronger.

“Falzyxi won’t be alone,” Luke said. “You and your students can help by holding off the other void creatures.”

“And when you fail? What then?”

Luke, who had already started walking away, turned back. “I won’t. It’ll work.”

He waved to Sam, but she was in a meditative trance and didn’t see him. Ella wasn’t there. Time was running out for her to come around, if she was going to. Another void being appeared somewhere in the weave, so Luke nodded to Penny. “See you in a bit, then?”

“I’ll go pick up Mateo and meet you there.”

Luke frowned. “Mateo? Are you sure? I don’t mean to be an asshole, but is he strong enough?”

Penny laughed. “You think he just sat around with his family? You’ll see.”

“Of course he didn’t,” Luke said with a smile. “It’ll be the whole crew back together again, then.”

“Hell yeah, mister!” Penny said as lightning crackled around her body. Her hair stood on end in all directions. A flash followed, and she was gone.

“Be careful,” Popov said. He hadn’t moved. “We will lend whatever aid we can, but I will not allow you to die, Lifeweaver. Like it or not, we need you.”

Dimitrios: “You're taking that one too. Your girlfriend ruined my nap, so you owe me.”

Luke: “I’m on it.”

Luke stepped into the weave. This time, he decided to try shifting his physical form into the metaphysical realm, like Dimitrios, to see what difference it made. It wasn’t difficult to accomplish, but a wave of disorientation struck him the moment he made it to the end seam. Having his body there, seeing his arms and legs, felt alien in a way he hadn’t expected. How strange to feel weird about having limbs.

The void creature seemed to share in Luke’s confusion. It just sat there, all alone, looking around.

“What’s wrong with you?” Luke asked, using his actual voice. “You lost, little one?”

That seemed to set it off, and it tore into the end seam with wild abandon.

Stop.

It stopped. Luke’s practice was bearing fruit. The creature struggled against his will-infused command, but he held it fast, not letting it wriggle out of his control.

Boon of Potential grants you an attribute point (+1 Willpower).

This one was a tad more advanced than the recently turned Integrated he dealt with last, and he wondered how it would compare in a contest of wills over its Source. Pulling out the almost empty monster core, Luke reached in and grabbed hold of a thin tendril of its internal power, the power that ruled it all: The Greater System, the void, and everything else.

Like last time, the creature reacted in an instant, breaking the will-based bonds by summoning a furious resistance. It didn’t want to die, and having some outside force touching its Source turned it from an apex predator into a skittering, fearful animal. That lasted no more than a second, though, before its more conscious mind took over. Again, they pulled. Luke knew he could win if he asserted his will with more force, but tiring himself with Falzyxi drawing so close would be a poor decision. Instead, he reached forward to end his opponent’s terrible existence. It lashed out rather than pull away, catching Luke in the arm with enough force to break bone. A tentacle wrapped itself around the limb, and its many mouths began feasting on Luke’s flesh, as well as his mana. Crying out with a mix of pain and surprise, he scrambled to regain control. More tentacles struck out like whips, going for his head, but by then Luke had recovered. Rather than defend himself, he went on the offensive. A slice with Needle of Life through its Source channels was enough to end it, and the void claimed the creature soon after.

Congratulations! You have reached Level 54!

Healing himself, Luke wondered what the hell had happened. Those who did not belong in the weave should not be able to enter it like that, not by just crossing over the end seam to attack. The thread. That had to be it. In connecting himself to the creature, Luke must have bridged the barrier, allowing his opponent to do the same through the bond he had created. He would have to be careful about that in the future, very careful, or he would be granting those who wanted to consume the weave direct access to it.

In fighting Falzyxi, Luke would have to leave the weave and enter the void for his plan to work. That was not part of what he had imagined, and the thread of it was a tough complication to accept, but it was a pill he would have to swallow if he wanted to rid Earth’s system of the coming infection.

With the enemy dealt with for the moment, Luke returned to Earth, considering the differences between entering the weave with his physical body and the way he usually did it, with just his consciousness. So far, it seemed much the same. Falzyxi was approaching, but the preparations were underway. Did that mean he could get a few more moments to work on his enchanting?

Warning bells rang in Luke’s mind as he sensed the weave being under attack yet again. It wasn’t Falzyxi, not yet, but the interruption meant his enchanting would have to wait. With a sigh, he returned to the weave once again.
