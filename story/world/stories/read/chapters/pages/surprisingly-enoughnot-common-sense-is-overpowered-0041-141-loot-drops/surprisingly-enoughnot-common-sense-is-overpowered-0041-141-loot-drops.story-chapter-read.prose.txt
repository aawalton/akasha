Loot drops.

They were a classic RPG trope, the ultimate wet dream of every loot goblin.

But seeing something like that happen in real life?

That was shocking, to say the least.

“So...” Ning broke the silence, staring at the pile beside the corpse. “What exactly just happened?”

“Uh... I don’t know,” Xiao Fan admitted, looking equally bewildered.

It was true that he had occasionally found valuable things on beasts he killed before, but this was the first time the loot had literally appeared out of nowhere.

Ning studied his expression carefully.

It didn’t look fake.

So either Xiao Fan had somehow caused it without knowing, or the rabbit itself had possessed some strange ability.

Of the two, only one had an explanation Ning could actually work with.

If he had been wearing glasses, they would’ve flashed brilliantly.

“Bloodline ability.”

Xiao Fan looked over. “What?”

“Spirit beasts can sometimes awaken abilities hidden within their bloodlines,” Ning explained, turning his attention back to the corpse. “It’s rare, but beasts with powerful ancestry, sufficiently high cultivation, or some unusual fortuitous encounter can manifest all kinds of strange abilities.”

Xiao Fan’s attention immediately returned to the dead rabbit.

“You think this thing had one?”

“Given how absurdly powerful it was?” Ning shrugged. “Yeah. I’d say it fits.”

“Huh...”

Xiao Fan looked between the corpse and the pile of items before slowly nodding. “That actually makes sense.”

“It probably had something similar to a storage pouch inside its body,” Ning continued. “An internal space created through its bloodline. Once it died, the space collapsed and expelled whatever had been stored inside.”

The more Ning explained it, the more reasonable it sounded.

After all, his second home was practically the Scripture Pavilion. He had read enough strange cultivation records to know that bizarre bloodline abilities were hardly impossible.

There was a brief silence.

Xiao Fan stared at the mangled corpse with unusual intensity.

Then he coughed lightly.

“Uh... Ning?”

Ning looked over. “Yeah?”

“I’ve got a favor to ask.”

Ning raised an eyebrow. “Oh? A favor? And you even asked nicely.”

No, Ning was not still salty about Xiao Fan’s earlier rude comment.

Anyone saying otherwise was lying.

Xiao Fan ignored him.

“I want the demon core and the body of this Red-Eyed Spirit Rabbit. You can take everything else, including all the other rabbits.”

Ning glanced toward the enormous corpse.

The demon core of a late-stage spirit beast was already valuable, and if the rabbit really had awakened some unusual bloodline ability, its body would be worth even more to the right buyer.

On the other hand, Ning would receive everything else from the battle, including the dozens of weaker rabbits they had killed.

The division was reasonable enough.

“Is that all?”

Xiao Fan nodded. “That’s it.”

“Sure. Take it.”

Xiao Fan paused and looked at him. “Really?”

“What, you want me to haggle?” Ning laughed. “You were the one getting clawed in the face while I stood far away shooting arrows. I’m not going to cry over a small difference.”

Xiao Fan snorted. “Fair.”

Besides, Ning had already gained more than enough from this mission.

With that settled, he finally turned his attention toward the strange pile that had appeared beside the corpse.

There were two stalks of Moonlight Grass, both close to reaching First-Tier quality, several pieces of beast bone, a few chunks of preserved spirit-beast meat, and a handful of other miscellaneous materials.

And finally...

A spirit stone.

Ning’s gaze locked onto it immediately.

The stone was almost completely unblemished. Most of the spiritual energy within had already been exhausted, but what little remained was astonishingly pure, far beyond any low-grade spirit stone Ning had handled before.

He picked it up carefully.

“This might explain why the rabbit grew so quickly.”

Xiao Fan leaned closer.

“For a spirit beast to advance that fast, it would need a huge amount of spiritual energy,” Ning continued. “A late-stage Red-Eyed Spirit Rabbit appearing out of nowhere was already strange. If it found this somewhere and absorbed most of the energy inside...”

“That would explain it,” Xiao Fan finished.

He examined the stone for a moment before frowning.

“This definitely isn’t ordinary. Shame there’s barely anything left.”

Ning nodded.

A spirit stone with purity like this couldn’t possibly have been low-grade. At the very least, it had once been middle-grade, and there was a chance it had been even higher.

“Yes.” Ning sighed. “What a waste.”

The loot was decent, but nothing that would suddenly make him rich.

By the time they finished sorting through everything, the fighting on the other side of the field had ended as well.

Patriarch Mi approached with several guards. His face was still pale as he looked toward the corpse of the late-stage rabbit.

“I never expected something like this to appear here,” he said solemnly. “If not for the two of you, our family would have suffered greatly.”

His fear was understandable.

A group that large, backed by several mid-stage beasts and a late-stage leader, was already approaching what could be considered a minor beast tide. The Mi Family might have been able to deal with the original problem, but what had appeared today could easily have devastated their spiritual farm and spread into the surrounding village.

“We just got lucky,” Ning replied with a polite smile.

And in a way, they had.

The rabbit had been far stronger than Ning initially expected, but the two of them had also happened to be unusually well suited to fighting it.

Its greatest advantage was speed, and Ning possessed the perception needed to track its movements. His poison had gradually worn that advantage down, while Xiao Fan had enough raw strength to confront it directly whenever it came close.

Even then, both of them had nearly exhausted themselves bringing it down.

Calling the fight easy would be ridiculous.

“You’re being too modest,” Patriarch Mi said earnestly. “The two of you have truly broadened our horizons.”

He hesitated before bowing deeply.

“Please allow our family to host you both. You should rest and tend to your injuries. We will gather the remaining beast corpses and salvage whatever materials we can for you.”

He bowed once more.

“Thank you for saving us.”

Xiao Fan waved a hand. “It was just a job.”

Ning glanced at him.

“For someone who keeps saying that, you sure do a lot of saving people.”

Xiao Fan clicked his tongue. “Don’t start.”

Ning grinned. “I’m just calling it how I see it.”

Before Xiao Fan could come up with a response, Ning stretched his arms over his head and started walking away.

“Where are you going?”

Ning yawned without looking back.

“I’m exhausted. Imma take a nap. Bye.”

After all, you can never lose an argument if you are not even there.

...

Ning, now dressed in clean clothes, sat quietly in one of the Mi Family's guest rooms, his right arm wrapped in fresh bandages.

Two days had passed since the battle, yet the Mi Family estate remained in a state of chaos. Servants and guards moved constantly between the damaged spiritual fields and the main compound, tending to the injured, counting their losses, and assessing just how much of the harvest could still be salvaged.

Ning, meanwhile, was conducting an equally important assessment of his own.

"Let's see the damage."

Ten black boar tusk arrows.

Two qi-replenishing pills. Twenty spirit stones gone.

Twenty well-made standard arrows.

A ruined robe, along with several other miscellaneous items.

Ning sighed as he finished going over the list, then glanced at his gains again.

"...Hmm."

Maybe it wasn't so bad after all.

Setting aside the most valuable loot from the late-stage rabbit, the corpses of five mid-stage rabbits and fifteen early-stage ones should easily fetch him at least two hundred spirit stones. More importantly, there were some things that couldn't be measured in spirit stones. Fighting a late-stage demon beast head-on had given him the kind of combat experience that ordinary sparring simply couldn't provide.

The only real downside was his injured arm, and even that wasn't particularly serious. After applying the medicinal ointment provided by Patriarch Mi, the wound had already begun closing, while his body-refinement technique had made his recovery noticeably faster than it would have been otherwise. At this rate, he should be completely healed within a few days.

As Ning leaned back in his chair, a knock came from the door.

"Come in."

Patriarch Mi stepped inside and immediately glanced at Ning's bandaged arm. "Brother Ning, how is your injury?"

"It's fine. It should be completely healed in a few days."

"I'm glad to hear that." Patriarch Mi visibly relaxed before taking out a storage pouch. "I've also brought the spirit stones for the demon beasts."

Since Ning had no desire to haul around sacks filled with rabbit corpses, he had simply sold his share to the Mi Family. He accepted the pouch with a smile and briefly checked its contents.

“There are three hundred spirit stones inside,” Patriarch Mi explained. “Two hundred for the demon beast corpses. As for the mission itself, given how badly the situation exceeded what we originally reported, I’ve increased the compensation to one hundred spirit stones, along with Red-Striped Barley and honey from our family."

"One hundred?" Ning looked up in surprise.

"Yes. The situation changed considerably from what was described in the original mission, and none of us expected a late-stage demon beast to appear. Increasing the reward is only fair."

Patriarch Mi's expression tightened slightly as he spoke. Even two days later, he clearly hadn't made sense of how a late-stage demon beast had suddenly appeared in his family's spiritual fields.

If Ning knew what the man was thinking, he would have simply pointed toward Xiao Fan's room and said, Look at that guy.

"I appreciate it," Ning said instead.

Patriarch Mi hesitated for a moment before taking out a scroll. "There's one more thing. Consider this an apology for my son's earlier behavior. Since you cultivate the Pure Qi Sutra, I believe it should also be useful to you."

Ning accepted the scroll, and his expression changed slightly when he recognized what it was.

"I hope you can forgive his immaturity," Patriarch Mi continued. "I've already disciplined him."

After watching Ning and Xiao Fan join forces to defeat a late-stage demon beast despite both being only mid-stage Qi Condensation cultivators, Patriarch Mi had already begun regarding the two as potential Foundation Building seeds. There was little reason to allow his son's earlier behavior to leave behind unnecessary resentment.

“Young people do stupid things sometimes.” Ning waved the matter aside before glancing down at the scroll again. “That said, this is genuinely useful to me, so I’ll gladly accept it.”

Patriarch Mi finally relaxed. "Thank you."

After speaking for a little longer, Ning suddenly remembered someone. "By the way, what has Xiao Fan been doing?"

"Oh, he took the corpse and demon core of the late-stage rabbit. He's been staying inside his room for the past two days."

"I see..."

Ning's eyes narrowed slightly.

...

Demon Subduing Sutra.

It was one of the greatest secrets Xiao Fan possessed.

Hidden within the ring left behind by his parents, the inheritance was far more valuable than its unassuming appearance suggested. It was only through the Demon Subduing Sutra that Xiao Fan had managed to repair his spiritual root.

The mysterious master he'd told everyone about had never existed.

That story had simply been a convenient lie.

After all, claiming that some wandering expert had healed him was much safer than admitting that he'd obtained an inheritance capable of solving a problem even his family's elders couldn't handle. In the cultivation world, possessing a treasure far beyond one's ability to protect it was rarely a blessing.

Inside his sealed room, Xiao Fan placed the late-stage demon core beside the enormous rabbit corpse and carefully carved a star-shaped pattern into the floor around them. Only after checking everything several times did he sit at the center and begin reciting the incantation recorded within the sutra.

The atmosphere gradually grew heavier as demonic qi stirred within the room. The entire scene looked like some demonic ritual.

The Demon Subduing Sutra was divided into two primary aspects.

The first allowed its cultivator to refine the power contained within demon cores and use it to accelerate cultivation. That alone was already extraordinary. Demonic energy was chaotic and corrosive to human meridians, which was why cultivators normally processed demon cores through alchemy or other methods before daring to absorb their power.

The second aspect, however, was what truly made the inheritance heaven-defying.

Demonic bloodline refinement.

Rather than merely extracting energy from a demon beast, the Demon Subduing Sutra could refine portions of its bloodline and incorporate them into the cultivator's own body, allowing them to gradually acquire characteristics and innate abilities belonging to the beast itself.

For the past two days, Xiao Fan had been preparing for precisely that.

He had stabilized his cultivation, adjusted his condition, sealed the room against outside disturbances, and prepared himself for the body's inevitable rejection. Refining a demonic bloodline into a human body was not something that could be rushed, and failure would not merely waste a valuable corpse and demon core. The resulting backlash could leave him severely injured.

Even so, Xiao Fan had no intention of giving up the opportunity.

He looked at the late-stage rabbit before him and smiled faintly.

Who would ever imagine that a mere mid-stage Qi Condensation cultivator could use demon cores and corpses in such a way?

No one.

And that was exactly how he preferred it.

...

Yeah. That guy's cheat definitely has something to do with demon cores.

Elsewhere in the Mi estate, Ning rested his chin against his uninjured hand as he considered what he'd learned.

As far as he knew, Xiao Fan wasn't an alchemist, artifact refiner, or practitioner of any profession that would explain his unusual interest in demon beast corpses and cores. Yet whenever the opportunity arose, the guy seemed particularly interested in getting his hands on them.

That narrowed the possibilities considerably.

Perhaps he could directly absorb demonic energy to accelerate his cultivation, or maybe he used demon cores to temper his body beyond normal limits. He might even be able to refine a beast's bloodline and inherit some of its natural abilities, purify demonic essence into something compatible with human cultivation, or convert the cores into some special form of energy capable of bypassing ordinary bottlenecks.

Ning had no way of knowing which answer was correct, but as a transmigrator, his imagination wasn't particularly constrained by what cultivators considered normal. More importantly, every single possibility sounded exactly like the kind of unreasonable cheat a protagonist would possess.

Otherwise, why would Xiao Fan want demon cores so badly?

Ning smiled faintly as another piece of the puzzle surrounding his protagonist-shaped friend seemed to fall into place.

Anyway, whatever Xiao Fan's secret actually was, Ning had learned one important thing.

He had caught that guy lacking.
