Or they're making me feel like something I'm doing is helping, while some other mechanism at work is causing the progress, which is kind of a downer thought, so I'm gonna set it aside as a possibility, but not one I'm gonna emotionally invest in.

If I keep telling myself I've got control over my emotions, maybe it'll happen. In the meanwhile, taking some degree of control over the actions I take in response to the emotions that hit me is an achievable goal that I think I can measure. Okay, I can measure it when I'm on the slow glide into or slow recovery from a depressive crash, which is the best I'm gonna get, I think.

"Like this."

I chuckled. "Okay, yeah, or when it's fun." Then I realized something, and put a little extra warmth into my smile just to reassure them both. "But Siobhan's super not fond of it. Not completely incapable of ignoring it, or we wouldn't have little Aoibh, but barring some kind of need or emergency," I dropped back to girl mode, "she prefers this."

"Ew."

I shot back an animated image of what she'd sent me, with the Maw as backdrop. "Sure, Darling. You've done all the Big Work, you deserve all the Big Prizes."
