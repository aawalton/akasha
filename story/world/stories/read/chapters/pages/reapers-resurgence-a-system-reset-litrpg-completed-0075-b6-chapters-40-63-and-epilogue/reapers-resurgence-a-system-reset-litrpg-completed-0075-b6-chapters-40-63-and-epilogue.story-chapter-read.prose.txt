Next up, here's something about my mindset when I started this series. I started writing Reaper’s Resurgence with a few things in mind.

For one, I wanted to write a series where everyone who is already in a System has to face a System Reset. One where the levels of all beings in the System are completely reset, bringing them back to square one.

I have never seen a series with that sort of premise before, so I wanted to write one of my own like it.

And now I have.

Another source of inspiration for the series are those series I’ve seen before that are almost entirely just battles and watching numbers move upwards. A series meant for the people who just want to pass the time and read the simplest form of Progression Fantasy. Just watching the protagonist rapidly progress in strength over the course of the story without much drama or emotional conflicts or any of that.

A much simpler series than most of my other series in regards to characters and their interactions with each other.

And now I have one of those as well.

Lastly, I thought the idea of a power revolving around glitches would be cool. So I made one.

And it is cool.

So yeah.

I hope you all enjoyed Reaper's Resurgence, my second completed series! Which still leaves nine more ongoing series left :P
