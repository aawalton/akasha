The first Book of The Legendary Poet has been stubbed on Royalroad and published on Amazon. You can read the eBook for free with a Kindle Unlimited subscription, or buy it outright if you don't have KU.

Book 1 has gone through a lot of editing to make it read smoother and to match what I always wanted the beginning of this story to be.

If you enjoy it, a review on Amazon would help a ton, even a short one. It's one of the biggest things that helps a self-published book get seen. Any other support, whether that's a rating, a comment, or just spreading the word, means a lot too.

RoyalRoad and Patreon are both staying active. I'll keep posting chapters here as usual, and future books will get stubbed and moved to Amazon the same way as Book 1, just spaced out over time.

Thanks for reading this far, and for sticking with the story; I really, really appreciate it.
