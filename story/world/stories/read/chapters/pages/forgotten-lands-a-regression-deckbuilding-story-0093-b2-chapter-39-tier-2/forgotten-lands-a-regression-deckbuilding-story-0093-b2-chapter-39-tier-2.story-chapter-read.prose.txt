Under normal circumstances, upgrading the Heart Deck to the next Tier was far from easy. It required him to complete a mission related to his Class Path to advance to the next Tier. Usually, those missions would take several weeks or days if one was extremely lucky because they required extensive research about the chosen Class Path.

But that was not all.

Knowing much about your Class Path wasn’t enough to advance to the next Tier. It was also necessary to upgrade your Class Cards to a specific level and gain a certain understanding and mastery of your Class Path’s heritage. Some even say that becoming more attuned to your Class Path was necessary to advance your Tier. For most people, that part wouldn’t be an issue because they chose the Class Path that best suited their fighting style and personality. It would only become an issue upon reaching a higher Tier. At least for those who didn’t suit their Class Path perfectly.

“The Primal Soul Lord’s knowledge and memories!” Zayn blurted aloud, his eyes widening.

In the first place, Zayn spent weeks researching the Soul Lord’s Class Path using the books and tomes from the Tower of Nature. And that was long before he integrated the Class Path into his Heart Deck, procuring the extensive knowledge and memories of the Primal Soul Lord. It was a reward for integrating the Class Path, but Zayn noticed only now that it was far more valuable than he’d initially thought.

He thought that leveling Soul Sense and Soul Link to Level 50 and attaining a fairly high mastery of using the Class Cards would be the main gains acquired through the Primal Soul Lord’s knowledge, in addition to the information about the Primordial Pantheon. But that couldn’t be more wrong.

Zayn burst into a loud laughter.

It was only obvious that he knew enough about the Soul Lord’s Class Path to advance to Tier-2 without any additional studying. The creator of the Class Path’s knowledge had been implanted in his mind!

“This is much better than anything I could have hoped for!” Zayn exclaimed before continuing to laugh. If he’d been stuck at the Peak of Tier-1, that is. He would have to spend much time upgrading his Tier, just like everyone else. But time was something Zayn didn’t have right now. His plans pushed him to move forward, not to spend several weeks stuck to reading…again.

The separation of Tiers was usually considered an indicator of power, but for most humans, it was a measurement that determined their Foundation Orbit’s limit. Every Class Path had a different Foundation Limit, and only by completing the Class Advancement missions and learning more about their Class Path would it be possible to reach the next level. It was similar to the evolution of monsters.

Monsters could only enhance their strength to a certain level before their Foundation couldn’t be improved. They would have to evolve and break their limits to attain more power.

That being said, Zayn didn’t know the Foundation Limit of his Class Path. He could only guess that it was quite high and that he must have surpassed it by accident. Otherwise, he wouldn’t have reached Tier-2. His Foundation Orbit would feel full and stuffy, and he would have reached his limit instead of breaking through it.

A bubble overflowing with excitement burst inside him and spread through Zayn’s entire being. He was happy and didn’t feel stressed for once. It felt great that something that ought to be annoying went his way rather than obstructing his path.
