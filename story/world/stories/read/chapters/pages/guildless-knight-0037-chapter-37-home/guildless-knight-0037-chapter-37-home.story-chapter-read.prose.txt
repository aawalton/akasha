Ais smiled for a moment and then turned around. "I'll be going then," she said, walking toward her room.

Alan glanced at her, then down at his empty plate. On a normal day, he would have taken extra, but considering the fact that he was supposed to fight a high-ranking monster, he avoided it.

Grabbing his plate, he walked towards the kitchen. Noya was cleaning the dirty utensils in the sink. She turned her gaze to him, then back at her work.

"Place it here, Master Alan," she said, gesturing at the left side of the sink, to which he nodded.

With his plate out of the question, Alan walked back into the guest room. His sword, Ferrust, and his leather belt with the attached scabbard were placed on the table in the room. Alan grabbed the belt first and wore it in his pant, afterwards he placed ferrust back into the scabbard.

With the sword now at his side, Alan's attention shifted to the potions he got from Ais.

Not having a belt modified to keep potions, he simply placed them in his pocket, hoping they wouldn't break, and walked out of the room. Walking up to the table he grabbed the quest form before walking outside of the Blackwell mansion.

Alan then began his journey back to his rented house. It was on the border of the city and also quite close to the dungeon of Alcia.

Its proximity to the dungeon was one of the main reasons Alan had chosen to rent the place. His home was roughly a 20-minute walk from the Blackwell mansion and just two blocks away from Alcia's church.

It was also just two streets off the main road connecting Alcia's dungeon and main market. Upon reaching the church, Alan turned right onto a narrower stone road that led to a four-way intersection.

Seeing his familiar street ahead, his pace quickened slightly, and within moments, he stood in front of his modest home.

Alan walked up to the door. It was a wooden one with a silver-coloured door knob. Reaching for the door knob with his right hand, he swiftly moved his left hand to the back pocket of his pants. He turned the knob to the left, not hoping to open it, but it opened up with a creak.

"Don't tell me I forgot to lock the gate. Seriously, how can I be such an idiot!" Alan scolded himself. He glanced around himself, hoping no one heard him—not wanting others to know he could make such a mistake, since he didn’t really trust himself to not repeat it.

Walking in, Alan closed the door firmly behind him. He stepped slowly from the entryway into the living room, turning his head as he scanned for any signs of a break-in.

There was a single window in his house, and that was against the wooden table kept in the centre of the room. Alan walked up to it, still looking at each corner carefully. It doesn't seem like anyone broke in, he thought to himself, his gaze dropping to the ceramic plate with the peeled cover of a tangerine he ate two days earlier. The living room had a green carpet all around over the wooden floor.

The floor was slightly elevated in comparison with the stone entryway. Letting out a small sigh, he walked into the kitchen area. Taking some water in a glass, he took it in his hand and splashed it on his face. He placed the glass next to the sink and began rubbing both his eyes.

Alan walked into his room. It had a similar look as the living room, but the paint on the wall was slightly more lively. Instead of a simple white paint on the wall and wooden ceiling, it had a lightish green colour on the wall with the door and the one directly against it, while the two others had white paint.

His bed was opposite to the door. It was a double bed with black-coloured wooden legs and frame, with a rather nice and fluffy mattress. It was also the only costly thing in his house, since Alan preferred a nice place to sleep the most.

Alan walked to his bed, his hand still rubbing both of his soaked eyes rather rigorously. She would have chewed me for this, he thought to himself as he took his seat on the bed, remembering how Alisa used to scold the habit of his.

Alan opened his eyes. His room was dark. He stood up from the bed and walked towards the cupboard. The floor creaked as he did. Alan always felt that the contractor supposed to build the house did half-assed work in his room, but not like he had a say.

Alan opened the cupboard. There was a rather weird smell. "Ah shit!" he exclaimed, looking at a white shirt placed on a hanger. "Don't tell me it didn't dry completely," he complained, throwing it out with the hanger on his bed.

Then he crouched down to reach for his spine protector kept in the corner compartment. Stretching his arm, he grabbed it and pulled it out.

"It really is quite heavy," he mumbled, holding the spine protector in his arm.

It was a metallic armour piece that every adventurer or those who were involved in fighting wore… everyone except for Alan, that is. He hated the spine protector with all his heart, not liking the restriction in mobility it caused.

Alan walked back to the bed and threw the shirt he was wearing.
