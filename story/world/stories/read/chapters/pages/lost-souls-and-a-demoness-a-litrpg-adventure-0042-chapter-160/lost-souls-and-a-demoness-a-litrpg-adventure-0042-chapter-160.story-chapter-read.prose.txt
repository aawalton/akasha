Third Floor - Grasping Shadow Zone, Silent Eclipse Quadrant, City of Gremnegar

Vylith flexed her hand, slowly opening and closing it. Even for a being like her, regrowing a limb was no simple matter. It would take time before it felt right, before she'd be able to move it with lethal precision. Not a great deal of time, admittedly, but the delay was irksome. The wraith demon was not used to defeat, and the humiliation of having been forced to flee rather than completing her mission stung worse than any wound of the flesh.

The thought of returning to Zertrolk in shame never even occurred to her. Not only had he tasked her with this important mission, but Vylith could never allow one who had bested her to live. The wraith demon wasn't one to let emotion cloud her judgement, but there was nothing wrong with a little extra motivation. She closed her hand into a fist, squeezing tightly. Now, it was personal.

"I see you're doing better."

Vylith remained where she was, standing before a window that looked out over a city shrouded in shadow. She neither responded nor turned to face the newcomer, opting to survey the dark cityscape that sprawled below the fortress palace of Gremnegar.

"And just as talkative." The newcomer continued, her voice thick with sarcasm. "Are all demons as antisocial as you, Vylith?"

"Mind your tone, vampire." Vylith said, keeping her words short and clipped. She was in no mood for idle prattle. "You may be allied with my people, but I do not tolerate disrespect."

"Yes, yes, you're very frightening." The other woman finally stepped into view, joining her at the window. A slight smile played on her lips. "Consider me repentant."

The vampire, Ves, had long dark hair and pale skin. She wore a sanguine robe with a plunging neckline, stitched with golden thread. Privately, Vylith felt the outfit was a little on the nose, but she'd always found vampires to be a self-indulgent lot. She understood the Lady Ira's decision to ally themselves with the Blood Witch, of course, the demons had a limited presence outside their prison, and the additional resources they provided had been invaluable to the cause. She just wished that she didn't have to deal with any of them.

Coming here had been her idea, though, so Vylith reined in her instinct to vent her frustrations. It wouldn't do for her to cause a diplomatic crisis with their most important ally mere days before their plans finally came to fruition.

"Why are you here?" She asked instead, keeping her tone neutral. "Your queen has already supplied the aid I sought. We are… grateful… for your continued support, but I am near ready to depart again. I need no further service."

"She sent me to get an answer out of you." Ves said bluntly. "You never explained how you received your injury, nor why you sought our aid to restore it. Surely you have demons proficient in blood magic, no?"

"Not many are outside our home world. And I could reach your city more quickly than I could our own forces."

That was technically true, the node she used to transport herself to Gremnegar was marginally closer to the city than any of the other transit devices were to the demon ritual sites. The difference in overall travel time was minimal, however, but Vylith was loath to voice her unwillingness to return to her people. The fewer people that knew of her failure, the better.

"The magical resources spent on your restoration were not insignificant." Ves reminded her. She smiled again. "As allies, we are of course happy to render aid as you navigate a changed and dangerous cosmos. But the queen does demand an explanation."

Demands? Demands? Only a small handful of creatures had any right to demand anything of her, and none of them were on this plane of existence. At least, not yet. Ves took a reflexive step away as a spark of fury flashed in Vylith's eyes but, again, she restrained herself. Causing issues here would only draw attention to her failure. No, she needed to be… diplomatic. Her skin crawled at the thought.

"Would you like her to visit you personally?" Ves asked sweetly. "I'm sure I could arrange an audience. You've caught her attention. It's not every day that a powerful greater demon graces our fair city with her presence."

That made Vylith flinch. She had been in the Blood Witch's presence only once, some years ago when Zertrolk was attending to matters pertaining to their alliance. The immortal queen's aura could only be described as domineering, and her very gaze had paralyzed Vylith, leaving her shaken. Only the Lady Ira's dark presence could match such an aura, and the wraith demon was not too prideful to admit to herself that she had no wish to repeat the experience. Perhaps… some manner of explanation was warranted. If only to avoid an unpleasant encounter.

"I was wounded in battle, naturally." Vylith sniffed, not taking her gaze away from the city below. "Commander Zetrolk assigned me an important task. A task that still needs completing, which is why I will soon be taking my leave."

"And you embarked on this venture alone?" Ves asked. "I thought all of your efforts were being put into establishing the dimensional gate."

"This isn't unrelated to that goal. When circumstances give rise to certain individuals opposing us, I am sent to remove them."

"Ah… Then, if you're still in such a hurry, I take it this person survived your attack." Ves mused. "How unfortunate."

"She won't get away a second time." Vylith said coolly. "My information was incomplete. If I'd known her aura was celestially infused, my approach would have been different.""Celestially infused?" That got a raised eyebrow from Ves, and for good reason. The gods of the cosmos were long gone, and few remained who could claim ownership of even a trace of divine power. "Were you after a priest?"

"Hardly." Vylith snorted, amused. "A fellow demon. A rogue succubus, not beholden to our cause."

The sharp look Ves suddenly shot her caught her by surprise. Tension bled into the air, and the vampire beside her no longer looked amused.

"A… succubus, you say?" She said slowly, extending and retracting her claws. "Was she a Wayfarer?"

Despite her general disinterest for matters beyond her own, Vylith was curious. What could a vampire of Gremnegar possibly know about her quarry? By all accounts, Jade and her group had barely even made it to the third floor. She gave a brief account of their battle and provided a description. Less than halfway through, Ves cut her off.

"That's her." She said coolly, fire burning in her eyes. "That's the one."

"You've met?"

"We fought over a divine relic in her possession. At first, I thought she was one of yours. She managed to make me retreat to my sanctum."

So, in vampire speak, she'd killed her. Vylith held back an amused snort. Vampires and their pride. But now her interest was piqued. Perhaps this creature could be of some use.

"Is that how she obtained her divine powers?"

"Not if they're celestial. It was a death splinter."That made more sense. The Blood Witch would have no interest in a life or renewal splinter, but a death one…

"Where did they get it? That is no common artifact.""I don't know, and I don't care." Ves growled. "What I do know is the next time we meet, I'm putting her down. The stain of my failure will not be washed clean until her body is nothing more than an empty, bloodless husk. I'd already be after her, but the Queen has had me busy with other tasks… like minding you."

"The succubus will be dead before you can lay hands on her." Vylith said coolly. "In the morning, I'll be recovered enough to go on the hunt once more. This Jade is the type who thinks she can substitute experience with ferocity, and her companions aren't much different. She's out of tricks now, and I know how she fights. She won't be getting away again."

"It sounds like you're the one who got away from your battle, not her."

The glare Vylith levelled at Ves could have frozen a lake. The vampire just smirked, unintimidated.

"In that case, I'd like to join you."

"Join me?" Vylith scoffed. "I don't need your assistance. The matter is well in hand."

"It never hurts to have some backup. There's a whole group of them, and only one of you. You may be stronger than any of them, but they work well together. It might not be as easy as you suppose."

"You're barely level thirty." Vylith pointed out. "Your presence would be a hindrance at best."

"Maybe, maybe not." Ves's lips were still curled in that annoying smirk. Vylith suppressed an urge to punch the woman. Diplomacy was harder than she'd imagined… maybe she shouldn't have skipped the communication seminars held by their succubus infiltration agents prior to departing the home world. Distracted by her inner battle, the wraith demon almost missed what she said next.

"How do you plan on tracking them?"

Vylith grimaced. Divining someone's location in the Labyrinth was possible, but it was generally time and mana intensive. Tracking magic was unreliable in a world filled with several interconnected subdimensional layers.

"Haven't figured that part out, yet? Well, you're in luck." Ves continued, holding her hand palm up. Blood oozed from her skin, pooling into a sanguine orb that hovered above her fingers. "No one can hide from a vampire who's tasted her blood."

"Oh?" Vylith asked, her interest renewed. "You can find her?"

The vampire closed her hand into a fist, popping the blood orb. It sank back into her skin, flowing along her arm like a living creature before finally absorbing.

"Easily."

For the first time, Vylith smiled slightly.

"You queen won't object?"

"Not if I'm helping one of our trusted allies achieve an important goal. We haven't forgotten what Lord Vastrozun has promised us. My Queen will probably want me to see what I can glean about demon activities from you, though. We know you haven't told us everything."

"You know everything you need to.""Perhaps. But not everything we want to."

"Fine. Lead me to this succubus, and I'll think about answering some of your questions."

"Wonderful." Ves said, her smile turning predatory. She turned, sauntering back towards the door. "Let's get started."

Vylith followed her through the darkened halls of the Blood Witch's palace, her mind already churning through tactics. She opened and closed her fist again, annoyed at the distracting sensation of a limb that was still not quite moving in the way she expected. What a humiliating injury. But no matter. Jade and her friends weren't going to get lucky twice.
