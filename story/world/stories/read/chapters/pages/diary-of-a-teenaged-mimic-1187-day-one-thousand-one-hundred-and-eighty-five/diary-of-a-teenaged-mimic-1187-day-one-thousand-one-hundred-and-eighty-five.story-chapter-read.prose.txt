Dear Diary,

I realized another thing about being an adult today. Although honestly it's one of those things that I think has been building up over time and I just put words around it today.

I've mentioned before how much boredom is underrated. I've talked a lot about the difference between waiting for the other shoe to drop and waiting around for nothing much at all. I've definitely talked a lot about how I wind up doing a dozen things in a day, and how my ability to Co-Locate helps with my executive dysfunction.

Okay, maybe I haven't really done a deep dive into that, but I'm sure I've referenced it. Or thought about referencing it. Eh, whether I've mentioned it before or not, part of my own personal executive dysfunction is related to FOMO. When I say FOMO, I'm not really talking about the whole 'oh, I've got to have the latest thing' or watch the newest show, or go to the hottest eateries. It's not even about someone missing a trend and not being able to talk to people about whatever it is. For me, in this case, I'm talking about wanting to do something, maybe even needing to do something, but not being able to act on that because I'm waiting for the opportunity to do something else.

I read about that, the whole idea of 'opportunity cost', but for whatever reason it never combined with my knowledge of my own executive dysfunction until now. I mean, yeah, I've had the more traditional 'I have one thing to do and I can't do it because my brain has decided it is harmful and has no payout' executive dysfunction, but I'm not sure what else to call it when I'm watching a stream waiting for some kinda drop, but I'm hungry and I'm out of toilet paper, but I can't call for food or order some TP to be delivered because if I do, I'll miss that drop. Shit, that's kinda easy mode, because even if I'll probably forget to eat and have to hop in the shower after next time I take a shit, there exists the possibility that I might actually remember and get the food or the toilet paper after I get the drop.

When it was two things that each had some kind of time limiter on them, and I had to decide which one to do, I was definitely gonna wind up sitting there doom scrolling until it's too late to do either one, just so I didn't miss out on either of them. Yeah, objectively I know that's some shit right there, but subjectively it wasn't something I had under control.

I mean, I still don't, but with being able to Co-Locate, it doesn't matter. Okay, nowhere near as much.

Thing is, that's a huge part of what adult life is. Doing a whole assed laundry list of shit every single day, day after day, just doing what it takes to keep the world working. Which, now that I can do it, I feel a whole fuckin' lot better about myself. I may not be anywhere near perfect, but I can look at the food on the table, the clean clothes on our kids, and even the progress my students and Cadets are making and say 'I did that, I'm helping', rather than getting on my own case about all the shit I'm not doing.

Yeah. I'm helping, and it's surprising how much that means to me. Guess Mister Rogers got to me early and deep. Not that I have any kinda bad feels about that.
