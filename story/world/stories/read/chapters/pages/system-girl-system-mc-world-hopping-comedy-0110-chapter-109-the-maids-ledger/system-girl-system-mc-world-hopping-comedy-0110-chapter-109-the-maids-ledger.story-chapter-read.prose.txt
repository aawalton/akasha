Mira gripped the leather-bound notebook tightly against her chest as she walked down the polished hallway of the inn. Her steps were silent, a ghost navigating the dark corridors.

Usually, inspecting the maids' quarters was a morning task. She would review the ledger while assigning new daily chores, nursing a cup of strong tea. But tonight, Ren had insisted on keeping Lily in his own room.

Mira’s heart ached with a dull, heavy rhythm. She felt a profound, suffocating guilt. Even though the kidnapping attempt had been a massive misunderstanding fueled by a cursed battlemage, it didn't change the fact that she had failed.

One question repeated in her mind, a relentless, self-flagellating loop.

Why did I leave the window open?

Now that she knew Lily's true identity, and the monumental, world-shifting significance of it, she should have been vigilant. She shouldn't have been sleeping so soundly. It was no wonder Ren had lost trust in her and decided to keep the child under his own watch tonight.

Mira stopped walking. She shifted the notebook to her left arm, freeing her right hand. She curled her fingers into a tight fist, burying her short nails into her palm until it stung.

No. She forced the thought away. If I work harder, the Master will trust me with the young lady again. I will prove my worth.

What Mira didn't know was that a certain communication module was leaving tonight. Ren wanted Lily with him when that happened. He wanted Ana to be able to say a proper goodbye to her, and more importantly, Ren needed the emotional support of holding his daughter after the goodbye was over.

Mira arrived before the maids' resting quarters. This was a large, communal room designed for the maids to take breaks, gossip, and change into their uniforms.

Mira composed her emotions. She smoothed the front of her crisp apron, put on her best, most terrifying poker face, and pushed the door open.

Everyone froze.

The room was relaxed. Some of the girls were sitting on the floor playing cards. Others were partaking in late-night snacks. A few had slacked off during the day and were desperately trying to fold a massive pile of laundry in the corner, thinking they had time until tomorrow morning to finish the task.

No one expected Mira to walk in at this hour. She was normally busy tending to the Little Lady in the nursery and usually changed into her nightclothes in her own private quarters after putting the young mistress to bed.

The maids who were folding the laundry felt their souls leave their bodies. The maids who were playing cards scrambled, desperately trying to hide the small, sweet snacks they were gambling with in hopes of securing a larger ration.

Mira stood in the doorway, taking in the sight, memorizing the faces and the exact expressions of the guilty parties.

"Line up," Mira ordered, her voice quiet but razor-sharp.

The maids scrambled, forming two neat lines on each side of the changing room walls.

The room was designed practically. Shelves and lockers lined both sides, with sturdy wooden tables set up in between them for folding clothes or eating. At the far end of the hallway, there were even a few bunk beds for the maids to rest if they were feeling unwell. The Lord had spent so much gold on their wellbeing, and this was how they chose to repay him.

Mira was furious. These people didn't know how incredibly good they had it. Just the fact that the Lord provided sweets and snacks daily as a standard part of their included meals was a luxury unheard of in this kingdom. And they were gambling them away like cheap copper coins. It almost made a vein pop in Mira’s forehead.

Mira brought out her handy notebook. The sight of the leather binding made some of the older maids flinch in genuine fear. Their daily performances were recorded in those pages. This book would determine their year-end bonuses, and more importantly, this determined the exact amount of sweets they would receive for the following week.

Mira uncapped a small ink pen. She marked a few maids' names with a sharp, aggressive slash. The ones who had started their own gambling ring right under her nose. These five would not receive any extra snacks the following week.

Then, Mira looked at the girls who were still frantically trying to smooth out the wrinkled laundry. They received a small cut in their year-end bonus as they were clearly slacking off during active work hours.

With the punishments doled out, Mira started calling the names from the notebook, asking for status reports on the progress of their daily work.

The two little girls were first on the list. Raina and Larena.

Larena was given the task of feeding the pups. Her job was simple enough. She just had to deliver the massive bowls of raw meat and vegetables prepared by the kitchen out to the courtyard.

There was a stark contrast between the workloads assigned to the two little girls. The older girl, Raina, was given way more work. She was ordered to dust the heavy tomes in the library, help in the kitchen to peel giant sacks of root vegetables, and help the outside group clearing dead leaves from the massive garden.

Her workload was almost as heavy as one of the older, adult women. Everyone on staff thought Mira had incredibly high expectations for Raina because she was being trained to be her eventual predecessor, similarly to how Sylas was training Liam in the ways of a butler.

But the truth was quite petty.

Even though everyone else in the household had forgiven the girl for spilling Lily's secret in the alleyway, Mira kept a close, unforgiving eye on her. She still couldn't bring herself to completely forget the absolute terror of that night. Hence, this was her spiteful way of taking revenge. The girl had to work twice as hard as the others to prove her loyalty. Mira will always be vigilant around her moving forward. Even if everyone else lets their guard down, Mira can forgive eventually, but she will never forget.

Then, Mira moved down the line to the other girl who had an unusually larger workload than the others. Kiren.

Gone was Kiren's pristine outlook and polished, immaculate appearance. The girl looked haggard. She looked tired beyond measure. She didn't even have enough time to comb her hair properly in the mornings. Mira was intentionally putting her through the wringer.

Mira stopped in front of her. She looked the panting, exhausted woman up and down.

"Kiren," Mira said, her voice dripping with ice. "You must look the part of a dignified maid of House Fenris at all times. Even the children get it right."

Kiren stared at the floor, biting her tongue. She couldn't tell why this Head Maid had it in for her so badly. Yes, the maids' gossip network had eventually revealed her past misgivings at the Greenwood estate, so the other maids all gave her the cold shoulder. But they all also understood she was just a cog in a brutal system, helpless to stop the Lord. Why was this Head Maid out for her soul?

The only thing Kiren could come up with as plausible was that this maid was pissed off because Kiren might be gunning for her position as Head Maid. It was a dream Kiren had all but given up on when she overheard the gossip between the other maids about Mira's interactions with the Lord of the house. Apparently, the Lord fancied Mira, which meant it was an impossibly steep uphill battle for Kiren to even try to get the Lord's attention now. To make matters even worse, she had thought the Lord to be a mere chef upon their first meeting at the food station and was quite rude to him.

But Kiren was wrong.

Mira wasn't too attached to the role of Head Maid. If you asked her, she couldn't herself point out exactly why she hated Kiren so much. She justified it internally by focusing on Kiren's fake demeanor. The act she puts up. The constant vying for attention and admiration at any opportunity she gets. Mira saw straight through her fake face and manipulative personality instantly. Even the way this girl was using the two sisters as a bid for more sympathy disgusted her. Mira convinced herself this was the root reason behind her intense dislike of Kiren.

But the truth was much deeper.

Mira, too, had overheard the whispered stories about Kiren in the laundry rooms. How she seduced her old master and regularly visited his bedchambers to secure her position. This knowledge coupled with a memory from a few days ago solidified her hatred.

It was the moment Ren finally addressed the new staff, explaining who he was and how things worked around the estate.

Mira stood behind Ren on the veranda. She watched the crowd of refugees. She saw every elf had hope in their eyes. The desperate hope of buying their own freedom. But when Ren announced his title, Mira's gaze had locked onto that one girl. Kiren.

She had greed and raw lust in her eyes. And that rubbed Mira the wrong way.

Mira didn't know why she hated the idea of any other girl getting close to Ren, but she did. Mira could tell Kiren had initially mistaken him for a common cook. But then, the exact moment she realized the man was the Lord of the estate, her whole demeanor changed. Her posture shifted. Her smile turned predatory. Now she was hunting for every opportunity to get close to him.

Mira was fiercely determined to give Kiren assignments that would lead her as far away from Ren as physically possible. Especially a manipulative woman like Kiren, who would just use him for status.

Mira looked down her nose at Kiren. She snapped her notebook shut. "Do better next time. From tomorrow, I am increasing your workload."

A few maids in the back of the room were visibly struggling to stop themselves from jumping up in glee and celebrating. They were enjoying Kiren's current predicament a bit too much, as they were the ones originally from the Greenwood estate who had suffered under her rule.

This group of original maids were still very well-behaved. The luxury and ease of the Fenris estate had yet to melt away the harsh discipline they had learned within the Greenwood walls.

Mira moved to this group next. Seeing their flawless work and steady progress, she looked back at the original staff, as if to say, Look at them. Learn something.

Mira went as far as to gently pat the head of one of the girls. Laurana. The older sister of the two. Mira was quite fond of this girl. Not only was she a ridiculously hard worker, she even stayed up late to help the others complete their tasks. She was kind and deeply caring to her fellow workers.

Mira knew if the Lord asked one day who would be best to take over the mantle of Head Maid after her, she would suggest Laurana over anyone else, even though she had just known her for three days.

The girl's sincerity and fierce dedication had moved her so much, Mira even let her stand guard at the Lady's crib in the evening during her naps.

Mira was quite fond of the two sisters in general. They woke her motherly instincts.

Mira wrapped up her little inspection, choosing to change out of her uniform in the communal maids' quarters instead of her own room. She wouldn't be sleeping at the main estate tonight. She was returning to her parents' hut in the village, since the Mistress wouldn't be sleeping with her tonight anyway.

Mira, dressed in her normal, casual clothes, walked through the quiet garden. She saw the pups sleeping on the lawn, twitching their paws as they dreamed. The fireflies had come out, twinkling all around her in the dark.

Mira was struggling. Her heart was not at ease. She kept telling herself: you can't trust anyone with that. Learn from your past, Mira. Don't entertain those thoughts and feelings. Be professional.

But she couldn't deny reality. Recently, her gaze would linger on him longer than intended.
