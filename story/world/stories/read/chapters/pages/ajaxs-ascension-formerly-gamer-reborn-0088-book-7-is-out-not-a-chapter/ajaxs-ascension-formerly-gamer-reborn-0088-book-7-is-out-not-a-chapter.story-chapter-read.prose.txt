With his Duchy getting off the ground and in capable hands Ajax is heading into the Wild Lands looking for an edge as well as adventure. For the first time he will be truly on his own with nobody from Gryndor there to watch his back.

Outside the humanoid lands Ajax will find strong creatures that have been able to grow with the law of the jungle waiting for their next prey as well as encounter the Ascended races whose power dwarfs anything he’s encountered so far.

The question remains will Ajax be able to convince any of them to form a bond or will they just kill him now that he has exited the protection the humanoid lands granted him?
