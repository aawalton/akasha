Anny sat nearby on a bedroll, watching with less engagement than he expected. Her grey eyes seemed distant, as if she was staring through him rather than at him. Her fingers drummed against her knee in an absent rhythm.

‘Still dissatisfied with the plan? Or considering the heart again?’

"Mmh?" Her eyes snapped back into focus, blinking as if surfacing from deep water. She straightened slightly, rolling her shoulders. "Yeah, go ahead."

Deciding to leave it for now, Nova reached out and grasped the first cluster of kilosouls. The familiar warmth spread through his palms as the concentrated power began to travel toward his core. Unlike his previous absorptions, this time he could feel his body straining to contain the influx of energy.

‘Ooh, now even the troll enhancement is struggling. I hope this doesn’t mean I have to stay in the Unification form constantly…’

Fifty thousand. One hundred thousand. The numbers climbed steadily as sphere after sphere dissolved into his core. His muscles began to ache from the sheer pressure of containing so much raw power.

One hundred and fifty thousand. Two hundred thousand. The air around him began to shimmer with heat as his essence reserves expanded further, reminding him of the time he had used Cardinal Darius’s soul. Sweat beaded on his forehead despite the cool dungeon air.

The formations around him flared to life, responding to the massive energy signature he was generating. He had created them to protect the room in case the breakthrough had unexpected effects, as an extra safety measure.

Three hundred thousand. The pressure became too much for his child's body to bear. He was forced to activate the Unification skill, feeling his bones stretch and his muscles expand as he grew to the form of a young adult. His clothes strained at the seams but held, designed to accommodate the transformation.

Then, finally, the barrier shattered.

One million Soul Power.

The sensation was indescribable. It felt like every cell in his body had suddenly awakened from a deep sleep, like he had been living in a shadow of himself and only now stepped into brilliant sunlight.

This was the barrier separating the masses from the kings and leaders of the world. The threshold that marked true power.

‘Now I am starting to approach the power I was once used to. But my previous life is still far off.’

"Aah, this barrier felt much more significant than the previous ones. I feel great," he exhaled, rolling his shoulders as the last of the energy settled into place.

"The fragment is as it should be?" Anny asked nervously, leaning forward on her makeshift seat. They had been worried that the floor would prevent his fragment from manifesting somehow, but it was a risk they had to take.

Nova nodded, looking at the system interface in front of him. “Yeah, it’s right here. And wow.”

"You lived for a long time then, right?”

It was like watching water flow through invisible channels, carving temporary riverbeds in the landscape of his soul. Each pulse of energy that moved through these phantom meridians left them slightly more defined.

“Anny, let me feel your body.”

Nova blinked, realizing how his request had sounded. A faint flush crept up his neck as he clarified. "I've gained access to qi, so I can use it to check what's going on inside of you. If anything is strange with your beast powers, I should be able to resolve it."

More grumbling followed, though he couldn't make out the specific words. Despite her obvious discomfort, she pulled the fabric over her head without further protest. Nova sat down behind her, noting the pale expanse of skin marked by faint striping patterns where her beast powers had left their mark.

He placed his palm against her back, feeling the warmth of her skin and the steady rhythm of her breathing. Her muscles tensed at the contact, a reflexive response she couldn't quite suppress.

Slowly, he changed the essence held in his arm into qi, feeling the old but familiar sensation course through his muscles. The qi felt much heavier than the essence, somehow adding weight to his arm.

"Are you ready?" His voice was softer now, recognizing the vulnerability of what she was allowing him to do.

Nova sent the qi flowing through her back, spreading throughout her body in branching networks that mapped every detail with perfect clarity.

Over a thousand different beast powers thrummed within her flesh, each one leaving its unique signature. He could sense the changes they had made to her body—strengthening bones here, enhancing muscle density there, and overall purifying and condensing the powers in harmony.

But beneath it all, he could feel the powerful heart, which had been further enhanced by the vampire blood. Her pulse grew faster and more erratic with each passing second.

Then, something familiar reacted in her core. Something stretching after his hand, almost like a hand reaching out to grab his—a presence that was unmistakably, intimately hers, yet somehow more than just her physical self.

‘Hmm, I’ve never seen something like that before… Her soul?’

The increased heartbeat, the warm skin, and the irregular breathing were all signs that he knew the cause of. And if this was truly how her soul reacted to his touch, it was no wonder she had been struggling with her feelings.

"...Anything?" she asked quietly, the question barely audible. Nova could sense through the qi that she had tried to speak three times before managing to get the word out—her throat had closed each time, overwhelmed by the intensity of the connection flowing between them.

Unlike the gentle exploration he had performed before, this required surgical precision. The qi moved like molten metal through her system, seeking out the points where beast powers had twisted and misaligned during their initial integration.

Each correction sent waves of heat radiating through her flesh. Nova guided the energy with patient techniques, slowly and methodically turning imperfections into seamless integrations.

Anny's breathing grew deeper and more controlled as the qi worked through her system. Nova could sense the moment each misalignment was corrected—her muscles would relax slightly, tension she hadn't even realized she was carrying melting away as the beast powers found their proper places within her body.
