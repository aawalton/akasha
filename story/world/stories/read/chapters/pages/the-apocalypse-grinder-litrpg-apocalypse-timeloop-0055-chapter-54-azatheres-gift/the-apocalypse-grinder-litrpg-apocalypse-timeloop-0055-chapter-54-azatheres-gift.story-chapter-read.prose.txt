So Ronan decided to focus on the only important part—the blessing of chronos. His ancestor had mentioned a gift before vanishing. He assumed this was it.

Thus he immediately pulled up the second message, hoping it held a better explanation. He also didn’t want to consider the horrifying implication that there were beings out there which could reach such an absurd level of power.

How was their fledgling planet supposed to survive in such a universe? How had it done so until now!?

After a quick scan of his newly configured status, Ronan was left confused and disappointed. If anything, all that had actually improved was the section marking his heritage.

Where before it had been a rather confusing mess which the system was unable to comprehend, it was now clearly labeled and his number of lives was marked in a more concise manner. That and the new section, blessings, which contained Azathere’s ‘gift’.

The rarity of the blessing did intrigue Ronan. Boundless sounded like one of the higher rarities, possibly nearer the top end. Even the name itself made it sound as though the blessing had no upper limit to its potential.

However, that still created more confusion for Ronan. If that was the case, why were the effects it provided so minimal? He soon wanted to facepalm at his lack of foresight as he focused on the blessing and obtained a full description of its effects.

Unsurprisingly, the final parting gift of a being with a four digit level was much more than a simple cosmetic change in his status. So much more.

Blessing of Chronos (Boundless): The power of the Chronos Clan flows in your veins, empowering the base fibres of your being. Time is just another inconvenience to those of your heritage, and the most gifted who receive this blessing are true examples of that. Upon beginning a new iteration, receive a detailed breakdown of your accumulated chrono enhancements. Memory and personality leakage is vastly reduced per iteration. Cultivation talent is increased by an accumulating multiplier based on your completed iterations. Gain the skill [Reverberating Path (Epic)]. May the rivers of time and eternity give way before you, chosen son of Chronos.

A tapping on his shoulder broke Ronan from his dumbfounded shock. “Are you alright there, Ronan? You suddenly started breathing heavily and your hands were shaking,” Keith asked with a concerned expression.

Realising how tense his body was, Ronan relaxed and flashed a grateful smile at his companion. “All good, Keith. Thanks. Just checking out my status after all… you know.”

The response seemed to placate the guy, because he nodded in understanding and began to walk away. “I’ll keep watch on our surroundings for a while. Get some rest, mate,” he shouted over his shoulder.

It was a solid shout. However, there was a zero percent chance Ronan would be able to fall asleep right now. Not after reading what the blessing of chronos did.

The sheer power that was now his to claim was… World shaking didn’t even begin to cover it. Ronan actually appreciated that the blessing didn’t give him some sort of instant power up. That might have spat in the face of his efforts during the tutorial.

Instead it seemed that the seemingly ‘cosmetic’ changes to his status were actually going to become extremely relevant and useful in the days ahead. Not only that, but many of the benefits granted by the blessing would grow in power as he progressed, meaning they would never become obsolete.

Boundless was truly a fitting rarity for it.

The message at the end was something he hadn’t really seen from the system before. Then again, he didn’t think this blessing had anything to do with the system, given the disdain his ancestor had shown for it.

Unfortunately due to their growth nature, most of the boons weren’t immediately accessible to Ronan. Instead he checked out what his new skill did, excited by its epic rarity tag.

Reverberating Path (Epic): The power of the Chronos Clan allows the wielder of this skill to fix a point in time, causing all future iterations to begin from there, rather than the moment their heritage awakened. [This skill can be upgraded as the wielder develops their heritage].

Unlike the rest of his gains, which were still clouded in a lot of mystery, Ronan immediately understood this skill. The moment his heritage awakened had to be the beginning of the tutorial, since that was where all of his previous lives had begun from. Reverberating Path would allow him to move that start point forwards for all future ‘iterations’ as his heritage seemed to call his lives.

He wasn’t sure he wanted to use it yet. It did say that overwriting his base path was impossible, but the idea of making Terry’s death at least semi-permanent was something Ronan couldn’t stomach.

Now that wasn’t to say he would get himself killed at the earliest opportunity to try and fix that. In fact, now that Ronan had confirmation he would return to the beginning of the tutorial when he next died, he was even more determined to make the most of this life.

The further he progressed now, the quicker he would progress in his next iteration. That in turn would make defeating Magriz’al the Hobgoblin Champion easier, hopefully preventing any deaths in future iterations.

He could certainly see the utility of the skill. Having to complete the tutorial every time he died and ‘respawned’ would be a pain in the ass.

For now though it wouldn’t be seeing much use. Ronan was about to move onto reading the next of the remaining messages from the system when another small change to his status caught his eye.
