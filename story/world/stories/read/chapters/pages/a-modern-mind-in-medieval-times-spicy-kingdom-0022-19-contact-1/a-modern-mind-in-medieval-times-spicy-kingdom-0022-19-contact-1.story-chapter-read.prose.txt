As I skulked through the shrubbery, a clearing came into view, with two well-used large tents set up in its center. Twenty or so men with rather unkempt features, wearing a mishmash of well used armor, were sitting around them. Lucky for us, their horses were tied at the other side. They would not be alerting the men of our approach.

Judging by the tents’ size, I surmised there might be a dozen or so men inside. With the element of surprise on our side, we could easily handle that many.
