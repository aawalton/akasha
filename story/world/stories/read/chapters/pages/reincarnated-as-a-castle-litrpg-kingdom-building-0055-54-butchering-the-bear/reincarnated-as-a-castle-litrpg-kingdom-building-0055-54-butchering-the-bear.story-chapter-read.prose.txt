I opened the Daily Equipment Shop again. Six F-tier items appeared. Still no E-tier from this shop yet.

But I wasn’t looking for any E-tier items anymore. I was specifically hunting for knives or daggers.

Currently, we only had two knives with the [Butchering Lv.1] skill. I wanted to find more so all seven goblins could join in on the task.

That meant… we needed five more knives. They didn’t all have to have the Butchering skill though. Just regular knives would do the trick just fine.

However, there were no knives in the current shop rotation.

I still had my free Daily Weapon Shop refresh today. And I could only afford two, maybe three more refreshes with points if needed.

But given how utterly shit my luck tended to be, there was a certain chance that I would fail to find all five knives from those refreshes.

(Please… please, gacha god, please give me a knife!)

I prayed a total of five times for good luck. After all, a ritual or at least a sincere prayer was always needed when you gacha!

But then, as I was about to click the refresh button, an idea suddenly came to mind.

(Wait a minute…)

(Do I even need a knife in the first place?)

To test my theory, I bought an F-tier bone spear from the shop, then gave it to Gopuro.

"Puro!" I called out to him.

"Yes? Big Boss?" replied the dude after receiving the spear.

"Please break this spear into a knife."

"Yes?"

Gopuro tilted his head in confusion.

But then, as his eyes landed on the big chunks of bear carcass I had just taken out of the inventory, "Ooooh!" he gasped, as he seemed to get what I was trying to do now.

(Wait… since when does Gopuro get this smart?) I wondered.

Anyways, the guy laid the spear across his legs, then bent it with all his strength.

Crack!

The wood snapped cleanly! Only the short handle and the sharp bone tip remained, turning it into a makeshift but functional knife.

I quickly checked the spear’s description again. And voila! It had changed!

[Broken Spear Tip]

Rank: F

Description: The tip of a broken spear. Can be used as a knife.

Effect: None.

Special Effect: Grants user the skill [Backstab Lv. 1]

(IT WORKED!)

It did lose one ATK stat due to being broken, but it should still be fine.

Now, moment of truth.

"Gopuro, use the spear tip and butcher the bear!"

"Yes!"

Gopuro moved toward the carcass, bent down, and with the spear tip in hand, started cutting.

Seconds later, he perked up, gave me a thumbs up and a bright, toothy smile!!

(HELL YEAH!)

I felt like a genius.

And so, using the free refresh and one more paid refresh, I was able to obtain two more bone knives and two more bone spears.

This time, I figured that I didn't need to go as far as breaking the spear. Just removing the tip and reattaching it onto a short wooden handle was more than enough.

Now, with everyone now having their own tools for the job, we got to work. I made the quests, rewarding everyone with 4 Powerstones each once they completed the task.

With newfound determination and the encouragement from the big reward, the seven goblins focused all their might, working at impressive speed. Their hands moved quickly, cutting up the carcass. My inventory started to fill with meat, fat and bone in no time.

Later on, while they were still butchering, I reopened the Daily Equipment Shop. Because there was something else that had caught my attention previously.

(Hehe, what a find!)

Apparently, that paid refresh had landed me one E-tier weapon!

A shield!

From the look of it in the preview, it was heavy heater-style shield made of dark iron. Though I wondered how big would it be once I get it out of inventory.

[Big Iron Shield]

Type: Armor

Armor Type: Heavy

Rarity: E

Description: A big shield made from iron. Very heavy, but exceptionally strong and durable.

Effect: +2 ATK, +3 DEF

Special Effect: Holder gains Skill [Shield Charge Lv. 1]

[Shield Charge Lv. 1]

Type: Active

Description: Charge forward while holding your shield, ramming into enemies with great force.

Effect: Deal normal-type physical damage and knockback enemies in your path. Has a chance to stun on impact.

Mana Cost: 10 MP

Cooldown: 20 seconds

Cost: 2 Iron, or 90 points.

It was literally the perfect item for Gopugiga!

The big guy was already the tankiest and strongest among my citizens. Giving him this heavy iron shield would turn him into an absolute frontline monster.

Now that I knew an E-tier item from the Daily Equipment Shop usually cost around 90 points, and potentially less if I had the right materials, I could finally compare that to how many points I would need to spend to get an E-tier from my [Item Combination] tab.

It costed 10 F-tier weapons plus a 50-point fee to create a single E-tier item. Meaning, buying E-tiers directly from the shop would always be much cheaper.

But given the rarity of E-tier items — only a 5% chance of appearing with each refresh — I figured I should buy out the shop as much as I could, stocking up on F-tier gears as well.

This way, I’d never run into a situation like this again, where I suddenly needed specific tools for a job and had none available. And beyond that, what I needed most right now wasn’t just points, but proper equipment too.

I needed to gear all my seven citizens up as fast as I could.

****

Four hours later… everyone looked a bit tired, yet they remained focused and moved with surprising efficiency.

There was still around 20% of the massive bear carcass left to be processed. And somehow, by constantly rotating the knives with [Butchering Lv.1] among themselves, all my citizens had now acquired the skill.

Very good.

I was deeply satisfied with the ongoing result. I wondered if anyone would be able to get Butchering Level 2 soon.

That asides, now that everything was running smoothly like a well-oiled machine, I figured this was a good time for a little chit-chat.

After all, I was very curious about what had happened to them. Were these guys the same as Gopuro and Gopuran — fleeing their tribe after it was taken over by a stronger one? And how on earth did they end up being chased by such a gigantic, dangerous bear?

So I asked.

Everyone suddenly stopped working for a brief moment at my question.

Then…

“Yes, we were running away too,” said Gopumi, a short goblin girl with bright brown eyes, a low ponytail, and freckles scattered across her green cheeks.

Her voice was quiet and tinged with sadness, yet her hands continued moving skillfully, separating the tough hide from the meat.

“But it wasn’t just us. There were many others too.” she muttered.

“Hoh? So where are they now?”

“Dead.”

Her answer came cold and flat.

I was a little taken aback by how straightforward and emotionless it sounded. But as I looked at her, then at the others, I noticed a clear shift in their expressions.

Their eyes now burned with a deep-seated thirst for vengeance.

“I see…” I replied carefully. “So you guys are the only ones left alive?”

“Yes.” Gopumi nodded, her knife never stopping.

“Big Boss,” Gopuro interjected. “Make us stronger! We want revenge!”

And as soon as Gopuro said that—

“REVENGE! WE WANT REVENGE!”

All the other goblins joined in, their voices rising into a powerful, unified chant that echoed through the courtyard.

Now that I was seeing this fierce determination burning in their eyes, how could I possibly refuse them?

“Absolutely! Follow me, you will become strong! You will get your revenge!”

The moment I said that, the group erupted into loud roars and howls of excitement. They were completely fired up, knives and broken spear tips raised high into the air.
