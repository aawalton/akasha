Anya took a moment to remember their questions. Seb's ability to see through Husks and to memorize Energy patterns were both explained, at least partially. His ability to connect with her through the wall that one time however was- She immediately distracted herself before she went too far down that thought. She was almost certain that she knew what was happening but now was not the time to bring it up, not until HE stopped being so stubborn.

"I believe you've answered all our questions. Thank you for that."

Anya? What about-

I think I figured it out. We can talk later, she sent back before he could finish the question. She'd have to explain to him what the typical benefits of Bonds were, if he didn't figure it out after that then she wasn't sure what it would take.

As the three of them made their way to the training hall Anya made sure Seb couldn't see her smile. She didn't doubt he'd soon realize she was happy for no apparent reason but that couldn't be helped. Bonds were two way affairs.

Phelann took a few moments to tinker with the newly installed EDN Terminal then gestured for them to step towards it.

"One at a time please. I've enabled the Path Interface. When you approach it will detect your Energy Signature and allow you to make your selections. Details are only provided up to your selection point. You will only see Descriptions of the selections you can make."

"After you My Lady," Seb said as he gave her space.

Taking a deep breath Anya stepped forward. Almost instantly the terminal lit up and the feed filled with information.

"I don't even get to choose my Archetype?"

"Unfortunately a large portion of your Path is determined by actions and experience," Phelann explained. "Time spent training, sparring and diving without first making an explicit selection is in itself a selection."

Before she could answer Seb stepped over, guiding her to look at the feed once more as he spoke. "It fits you. The way you fought Rios, you're a natural. You told me you forgot to use Energy against him but that your teachers were impressed. This sounds like the Archetype that does that."

Anya took a deep breath, forcing herself to look back over the information without the frustration at the lack of choice.

"You're right, you both are. This fits me and it fits what I've done so far. But the Specializations," she trailed off before nearly snarling the next words out. "I will never take Bruiser or Protector. I will not be them."

She considered the last two choices, conflicted between them and unwilling to choose quickly. "But am I truly a Monk? Am I in balance? Or do I focus on Energy, on finding myself?"

Turning to look back at Seb she stepped away from the interface, her options fading as she did. "I need to think about it before I pick. You need to see your choices too."

With a nod Seb took her place, the feed coming alive once more.
