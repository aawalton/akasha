Maximiliam Anderson (MC)

A former stressed office worker, who has been having a constant dream of being a God-King.

He’s in his early twenties, around twenty-two years old. He has a tall stature (6 feet 3 inches) and black hair.

MC’s past life is the God of Creation, which is none other than the God-King. He was the creator of Divine Will.

He will use his knowledge and class to exploit the System and rise to the top.

He’s bald and very muscular. Basically, a tanker that can go against anything head-on, which will make him unlock the signature skill “Adamant Flesh.”

Michelle

A young, slender, and gentle-looking, blonde-haired woman, a few years younger than Maxim, which means she’s around 19. She’s the daughter of a lumberjack and lives near the forest.

She’s not from Earth, so she’s not familiar with modern terms. However, she knows more about anything fantasy-related, though her knowledge is pretty shallow.

Her class is currently Magic Lumberjack.

Important Character:

Raphael

She’s an angel (an archangel). Barely half the height of a typical teddy bear, the being has an adorably round face with rosy cheeks. Feathery white wings flutter behind it, each as delicate as the finest silk. A faint halo hovers above its head, spinning lazily like a ring caught in an unseen breeze.

She has large, sparkling eyes. She wore a tiny white tunic that shimmered with gold-threaded accents that somehow made it look even more like a plush toy.

She’s a bit sadistic and can kill divine warriors on her whim, but she’s not evil either. It’s just how higher beings treat lower beings.

Istellia

She’s the Goddess of Imagination as well as MC’s patron god or goddess.

Her appearance is a young girl—no older than eighteen in appearance—with impossibly long silver hair that cascaded like silk. Her eyes were a brilliant, unnatural crimson, glowing softly with unfathomable power. She wore an elegant black dress, adorned with black lace, white frills, and a large violet ribbon on her waist. At her collar sat a gemstone brooch, pulsing faintly with divine light.

She possesses a black lantern, likely a divine weapon in disguise.

She’s unsettlingly beautiful… She resembles a porcelain doll brought to life by sheer divine will. Everyone who sees her always has an urge to kneel.
