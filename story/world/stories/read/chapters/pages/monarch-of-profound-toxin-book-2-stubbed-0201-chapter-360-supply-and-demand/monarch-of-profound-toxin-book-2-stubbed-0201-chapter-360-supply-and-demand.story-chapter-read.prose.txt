Chapter 360

“Wh… What’s this?” Meri stammered as she stared down at the piles of body parts and harvested innards.

Most of the ichor had been drained at this point, but a little bit here and there still trickled out and slowly pooled on the table. Whenever it spread a little too thickly, the table seemed to vibrate slightly and swallow the entirety, leaving no trace that it had ever been there in the first place.

A self-cleaning table, huh. Damn. After this whole shitty situation with the Lord of the Moon was over and done with, Eik was starting a new business.

Oh, hell yes! He could see the headlines and articles flashing before his eyes already.

Eik Magnasen, X-ranker and owner of the glorious elixir merchant Eik’s Excellent Elixir Emporium, has unveiled a fascinating new concept that will revolutionize daily living. If you thought your B-rank body made cleaning up at home negligible, then get ready to lose your mind all over this new table and have it cleaned up automatically before your body even stops twitching!

And of course, it would not only be tables. Any sort of furniture could be made out of crystal. The table was just the main seller. Chairs, step ladders, plates, mugs, and utensils. Freakin’ trash cans!

Imagine that! The world’s most effective garbage disposal system. Banana peels, empty cans, crumpled paper, or that old bike with the bent wheel? Easy, quick, and total annihilation.

Not to mention that, as the X-ranked Awakened he now was, he could guarantee the most durable craftsmanship available anywhere in the Unified Mass. Even a hundred meter tall Andihar Dayarunar wouldn’t be able to break his own, brand new tea table even if he tried.

All including a sweet, smooth, glittering design. This was going to be an all-time bestseller!

But then came the issue of naming the company.

Eik’s Enduring Everclean Equipment Enterprise?

“—ik?”

Was it time to drop his own name from stuff he founded? Were people becoming a little tired of it? Was he out of touch…?

No, it’s the people who were wrong!

“Eik!” Ihasu put a hand on his shoulder and spoke loudly into his ear, snapping him out of his stupor. Around the table, everybody was staring at him.

“What?”

“Are you okay? Where did you just go?” Rasmus asked, serious concern on his face now.

“Oh, uuh, I just had an idea. You know how I can get swallowed up in my own thoughts. Nothing new, but I do think I’m still slightly affected by my injury.”

“An idea? We can use anything at this point. Do tell, please,” Mn’Toakh prompted, her eyes revealing expectations that he knew full well Eik’s Enduring Everclean Equipment Enterprise would never be able to live up to.

He cleared his throat. “Ah, no, it’s… It’s nothing, really. Just a silly little thing that struck me for a moment.”

Meri repeated the question she had asked earlier. “What is this? It feels… frightening.” Even without an active, living aura, the monster had been so unimaginably mighty in life that its mutilated and brutalized corpse alone retained enough power to make a person feel sick to their stomach.

Absentmindedly, Eik flicked the forehead of the severed head with a finger. “This is a spawn I fought after I met the Lord of the Moon. It was pretty strong.”

“Pretty strong?” Andihar repeated, his eyes never leaving the pale, warped face of the beast. “So, that means it’s…”

“It’s an X-ranked monster,” Chop finished, pinching the thing’s cheek hard. “It’s been a long time since I last saw a monster this strong. Well done,” he told Eik with sincerity.

“I could have handled it easier if I hadn’t already been injured by the Lord of the Moon.”

“This is…” Meri trailed off, her fingers hovering mere millimeters from the creature’s skin but not daring to actually make contact.

“I think the best thing to do for now is focus on the creation of a grade 7 potion. I think I’ll be able to turn the tide of the battle if we can just figure out how to make them,” Eik said gravely.

Ganyak hir Gawak didn’t look quite as confident. As he looked over the parts on the table, his frown seemed to only grow deeper. “Say this thing does contain the properties we need for the recipe, you only have this one specimen, right? How will you make enough Legendary Mystery Medicine? It’s not going to be nearly enough.”

Eik jaw tightened. That wasn’t a fact that he had missed. “In that case, we’re just going to have to make do with what we have. Then I’ll focus on regular potions with half going to me and half going to Chop.”

“You seriously think you’ll be able to create a grade 7 potion?” Chop asked. Stars were practically flying out of his eyes at the thought of it.

“Yeah, maybe,” Eik said with a nod, both Meri and the guild master looking considerably more glum.

The Pristine Blade Saint stared at Eik, then at Meri hir Gawak and Ganyak hir Gawak, and then back at Eik. “Well, then what the hell are you waiting for? If you can finish it quickly, I will give you all the treasure I have. And let me just be very clear here, I have a lot of treasure!”

The guild master chuckled, but it didn’t reach his eyes. “We will certainly do our best,” he said before sinking deep into the cushions with a trembling sigh. He caught Eik’s questioning eye. “No, it’s just… I think I finally understand—really understand—what you mean when you say that the fate of the world weighs heavily on the shoulders,” he clarified, waving his hands above his head. “There’s nothing but air here, but I feel like I’m being crushed.”

Meri didn’t look much better, Robert holding back tears as he tried not to cry out in pain as his much stronger girlfriend practically pulverized the bones in his hand and fingers with a tight grip.

“Try not to think about it,” Eik told him. “Or, if you want a tried and true method from a man who had been feeling this way for a while, make a joke out of everything and lose all ability to take anything seriously. It works wonders. If you don’t take it seriously, it can’t crush your spirit. Clever, right?”

“You’re doing it right now,” the old man said with a soft smile.

“Oh, yeah, you can bet your ass I am. Chop’s been doing the same thing for a few lifetimes, I believe.”

Chop just raised the sandwich he had been making and nodded affirmatively.

“I suppose I’ll give it a try, then.”

“Good man,” Eik said. “Well, then, please pull in as many people as you need. Any resources you need, any equipment, any expert, any whatever, I will pay for it all. Any public or political resistance you meet, drop my name liberally and see it fade into the wind. Living Manifestations will keep you safe throughout. Do not hold back and just get this done, please.”

Both of the alchemists nodded solemnly and headed out, leaving the rest of them in the living room.

“All right, I suppose I should check my levels.”
