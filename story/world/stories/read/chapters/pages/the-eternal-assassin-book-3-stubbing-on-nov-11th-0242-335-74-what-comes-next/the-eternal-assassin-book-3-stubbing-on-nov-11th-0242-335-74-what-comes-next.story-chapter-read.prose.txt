That night, Asher and Samantha laid in bed next to one another, each silent for their own reasons. After leaving the coalition meeting, Asher had met back up with his team and ran them through everything that had happened. The others had been excited to hear that they’d be jumping right back into the thick of things come tomorrow, but that didn’t hold a candle to their reactions when he dolled out ten million shards to each of them. While Moxy and Rulfar immediately ran off to evolve some of their skills that had been sitting at level 40 for a while now, Samantha had gone all but comatose, simply standing there as she stared at what Asher could only assume was her shard total. He knew her hang ups surrounding money had gotten a lot better over the last few months, but he supposed handing someone such a large fortune would result in a bit of a relapse.

Eventually, Samantha had recovered enough to ask if they could call in for an early night, and he had agreed. If they were going up against a lich potentially in the next couple of hours, getting a little extra rest certainly couldn’t hurt.

As he laid there, enjoying both the comfort of the rich, palace mattress and the warmth of his fiancé pressed against his side, Asher stared at his own interface. He’d been on this world for only a couple of months at this point, and he was proud of just how far he’d come in that time.

Almost immediately upon receiving his ten million shards for killing four liches, Asher had turned around and dumped all of them straight into evolving his Identification resistance to the second tier. Seeing as they were going to continue meeting with powerful representatives from different nations, and he would presumably be meeting with stronger and stronger people as time went on, that was one large purchase he didn’t mind making in the slightest. Doubly so when he saw what evolving the resistance actually got him.

Resistance: Identification - Tier 2 - Gain superior innate resistance to identification. Know when someone is using such a skill on you or attempting to do so.

He had figured out rather quickly with Rulfar’s help from his Scent skill that the warning was a slight scratching sensation in the back of his chest that was hard to ignore. It didn’t allow him to pinpoint who was trying to learn more about him, but it was still a nice warning system.

Other than his resistance evolution, the last week had been devoted entirely to training while they waited for the representatives from the coalition to make it to the Noala Kingdom. Whatever time Asher hadn’t spent being blasted around by Moxy or practicing going against a larger, strong opponent as he fought Rulfar had been focused on his new blended skills.

Remote Control was easy enough to work on, as all he needed to do was chop off a part of his body and experiment with using his skills through the severed limb before eventually reconnecting it with Eternal Regeneration. But Eternal Knowledge was a good bit trickier. At this point, Asher had scoured all of the different markets in the capital, Whikoga, and Dormaul. He’d pretty much run out of objects with powerful emotions tied to them that he could use to view into the past. He was excited to get the skills up to level 20 so he could evolve them, but he still had a ways to go.

Almost as exciting was the fact that two of his most-used combat skills were just about ready for their level 40 evolution. Distortion was sitting right on the edge at level 39, and Astral Dip wasn’t far behind at level 38. He was all but certain the skills’ latest evolutions would be massive boons to his fighting style, and he couldn’t wait to see what was in store for him. Shards weren’t any sort of issue after their massive windfall, and the more liches they continued to kill, the more shards they would continue to rack up.

One of the first things that had been ironed out regarding the bounty pool for the coalition was how the bounties would be paid out when Asher was required to be the one to land the finishing blow on any given lich. Even if another team did all the hard work and defeated a lich, they were instructed to call him in to finish the job. To make things easy, Donvath proposed that if the event ever arose where Asher literally did nothing but pop in to make the killing blow, he’d be given a fifth of the ten-million-shard pool, while the remainder went to the team that did all the actual work. Asher didn’t want to risk angering any of the people who had come all this way to help them, and he certainly didn’t mind getting a free two million shards for doing all of five seconds of work, so he’d readily agreed to such a compromise.

While he daydreamed about having so many shards he could fill a large swimming pool with them and dive in like a certain cartoon duck from his childhood, Samantha poked him in the cheek.

“Asher…” Samantha whispered, poking him again. “...Are you still awake?”

“Yeah. Just basking in the sensation of being ridiculously wealthy,” he chuckled, pulling her closer as he kissed her forehead. “What’s up? Finally done freaking out about all the shards?”

“I think so… But to be honest, that wasn’t the only thing I was shocked by. I only had a few skills left that I needed to evolve to the second tier. I just entered the Stage of Bleed.”

“Wait, what?” Asher asked, recoiling at the news as he sat up and smiled at her. “Samantha, that’s amazing! Congratulations! What were they?”

“Water Walker, Wet Warrior, and Flight Burst,” she answered. “Water Walker now allows me to share the skill with others in close proximity, Wet Warrior gives me a strength boost along with a speed boost while I’m wet, and Flight Burst can now be triggered before I even have my wings manifested. That weird wave of energy that swept over me after evolving the last of my skills to the second tier… it was a pretty odd sensation.”

“No kidding. I had the same thought,” he admitted. “Still, this is great! Now it’s only a matter of time before you start earning your new blended skills. I wonder what a Forest and Wings skill would end up looking like.”

“I didn’t even know elemental advancement was a thing, and now here I am in the Stage of Bleed,” Samantha muttered, pulling him back down to use as a pillow as she laid her head on his chest. “It’s just all so wild to me. Not even a year ago I was just an ordinary hunter trying to make enough to keep our family home in our possession and raise my little brother in a small town on the outskirts of the kingdom. Now I’m mentally preparing myself to go up against another millennia-old lich, because we’ve already killed the first couple of them. Every time I think I’ve gotten used to all the new craziness in my life, I do something insane like evolve the last of my fifteen skills, or am handed ten million shards to do whatever I want with them. Sorry, I know you’re probably sick of hearing me talk like this by now… But you sort of signed up to hear all this when you asked me to live forever with you.”

“I think one of the neat things about being immortal is that we have all the time in the world to talk about whatever we want,” Asher smiled, running his fingers through her hair and feeling some of the tension bleed out from her wound-up muscles as he did so. He still cringed a little bit at the memory of him accidentally asking Samantha to marry him, but it was hard to wish for the event to never have happened when he thought back to just how happy and excited she’d been at him popping the question.

They still needed to have the conversation with Brian, and Asher was admittedly a bit more nervous about that than he was about the liches they’d be going up against before long. Despite knowing Brian thought he was awesome and loved hanging out with him, he couldn’t help but worry that hearing how he and Samantha were going to get married might trigger some sort of primal fear in Brian at having his sister taken away from him. Still, there was no sense in worrying about anything out of his control, and Asher did his best to put that thought aside for the moment.

“Oh! Now that you’re in the Stage of Bleed, we should swing by Opal’s shop and grab a new artifact for you before we head off on the hunt,” he said, glad he’d remembered. “You’ve got your enchanted bow, your cloud shoes, and your Amulet of Perception. Any thoughts on what you want for your fourth artifact?”

“Probably not too much use thinking about it before we get there and see what’s available,” Samantha pointed out. “That said, maybe something to help deal with lots of little enemies? After our recent battle through the disease-riddled insects guarding the last lich, I realized Moxy is really the only member of our team who is suited for dealing with swarms. Her Air Blasts work pretty well, but when she’s not there, neither of us really have a good way of fighting them.”

“We should probably ask Polly about whipping up a few blasting potions for similar occasions,” Asher admitted, yawning as he snuggled deeper into the mattress. “I know it’s sort of my fault for forgetting or being too busy, but we really should try to remember to use our potion supplies more often.”

“Normally we get jumped by opponents and don’t really have time to prepare,” Samantha pointed out. “I wouldn’t really say it’s your fault.”

“Sure, but you have to say that, seeing as we’re together and all.”

Giggling, Samantha gave him a tender kiss, before letting out a sigh as she leaned back and stared up at the ceiling. “...What happens once we’re actually done with the Council of Death? Taking them down has been the only thing driving us for so long, and it seemed like an impossible goal. But here we are, having almost reduced their number by half already and with a real plan to finish the job. What comes next?”

“Reaching the Stage of Power and shaking Donvath by the head until he reveals whatever big secrets he and Gran and the rest of their strange pact seem to be keeping from all of us,” Asher said without hesitation. “According to what little they told me, the Council of Death isn't even their biggest concern. There’s more going on around the world. A lot more. Gran’s already made it quite clear that they’re all but banking on me basically being their magical chosen one and helping join their fight, despite not giving me any details as to what the hell I’ll even be fighting against.”

Gran was an ancient healer who wandered the continent who Asher had met on two occasions now. Both times he’d been all but blown away at the purity of her aura and the soft sense of power she gave off. It wasn’t just the fact that she was capable of resurrecting the deceased if she had even a small part of their body to work with, something about the woman just gave off the vibe that she knew more than anyone around her and was capable of impossible feats.

“I wonder why they wouldn’t make an exception and just tell you if you’re that important,” Samantha muttered.

“I think the pact they all swore is magically binding somehow. The only reason Gran was even able to reveal as much as she did is because she’s one of the few ‘Grandkeepers’ of the pact. Whatever that means. So to answer your question, I suppose that’s what comes after the Council.”

“Sounds like we might have a little bit of a lull in between finishing off the Council and hitting the Stage of Power,” Samantha pointed out, giving him a teasing grin. “Is that when you’re planning to go back to your old ways and finish off the Assassin’s Guild?”

“It’s not a bad way to pass the time,” Asher laughed. “Hell, maybe I’ll even pop into some of the other countries and see if they don’t have their own versions of the Guild that I can pay a visit to. I bet the Hojon Empire’s got to have a pretty wild darkside if they’re such a powerful country.”

“Just be careful,” Samantha muttered, giving him another quick kiss before yawning and closing her eyes. “Even if you can’t really die for good anymore, I don’t exactly like waiting a whole month for you to be given a new body. And you’ll lose all your artifacts too.”

“Don’t worry, I’m not planning on throwing my lives away,” he promised, following her example and doing his best to get some rest as well. If there was the potential of him killing two additional liches tomorrow, he wanted to make sure he was rested and ready for it.
