Alex walked to his side of the station, and before long the clock hit 0. He gave the panes of glass a final glance before walking toward his opponent.

The Station was, once again, ‘Standard Arena.’

Karnation_, by contrast, instantly began charging an attack; he drew his bow, and Lightning began surging from his fingers into the shaft, concentrating at the steel-conductive tip.

The O2 Guilder was of average height, decently built, and suited up in light armors.

Karnation_ was seeded 29, and likely a little underrated, given he was provisionally rated. And by how carefully he was proceeding and prepping, and the way he was watching Alex, it seemed like he’d picked up at least an idea of Alex’s skills from watching Alex’s previous duel. He didn’t seem interested in taking Alex lightly at all, despite Alex’s lower ranking.

Except Alex had also been watching Karnation_’s duel, and he felt like his mental model of the guy, and his capabilities, was likely accurate. Even if there wasn’t much data to go off of; Karnation_ had finished his previous duel quite quickly.

Four Mana Spears appeared behind Alex, slicked with Swordforce, Swordforce that accumulated as the seconds ticked by; five was possible, but he wanted to keep a little willpower in reserve.

All the while, Alex kept walking at the O2 Guilder, his face still rather calm.

He also primed a little Force mana in his body, ready to activate.

For a few seconds, neither side struck; Karnation_'s  patience broke.

Karnation_ cried out as a final bit of lightning mana surged up his arm, infusing the muscles, and he let his super-charged arrow fly.

If Alex hadn’t seen how Beckett’s Lightning Spear worked up-close, he might’ve been tempted to try to dodge this. But he’d kept his senses on that arrow, and it felt quite similar; I’m 100% sure that Lightning in the tip will leap into my body if it’s anywhere close.

Instead, Alex did something Karnation_ wasn’t expecting, judging by the guy’s expression.

Alex threw up 3 consecutive Mana Shields, and this supercharged arrow struck—CRKC-CRSHH!

There was an expulsion of light and sound, which faded quickly.

Were you expecting it’d break all my Shields? Alex wondered. And hit me anyway, for my arrogance?

2 of his 3 Shields were broken.

But these weren’t any plain Mana Shields; these were Uncommon Rarity Mana Shields, with increased shatter resistance and proficiencies.

Alex glanced at the glass. He wanted to make it rather clear, for all those watching, what his Shields actually were. Because, by his ranking, it seemed potentially unclear before, after seeing his fight against Caide.

If he was playing for optics—I might as well commit, right?

He kept walking forward; Karnation_ seemed kind of struck, for about a half-second; to his credit, he recovered fast, teeth clenched, like he was thinking, okay, fine.

A little Lightning trickled down his body, especially his legs. He was already drawing another arrow, eyes flickering around the station.

Alex struck.

A Mana Spear whipped at Karnation_. Alex charged it heavily with Force. The speed, and the suddenness of it, also seemed to surprise Karnation_ a little, but the guy was ready, and with a surge of Lightning down his tendons, blitzed out the way.

A second Spear, and Karnation_ did the same, except he also fired several slightly charged, very quick arrows at Alex; a little more quantity and speed, now that quality and power failed. But these, Alex could safely dodge without issue.

Alex’s Spears regenerated behind him, and he felt like he’d seen all he’d needed to see. His vision of this fight—if the idea was to showcase a few abilities he really hadn’t been able to last fight—was suddenly very clear.

And without any more thinking Alex executed.

Alex threw a Force Slash right in front of Karnation_, forcing him to a halt. The Guilder’s eyes widened; Alex hadn’t actually shown this Skill before, at least in Rising Stars.

Force surged through Alex’s muscles, especially his legs, even as Karnation_ was trying to grind to a halt.

Karnation_ barely had time to draw his daggers, and Alex was already slashing. His first slash, struck against unsteadily held steel, almost forced the daggers out of Karnation’s hands.

Three lightning-fast slashes, and he succeeded; Karnation_ was stumbling back, gasping; Lightning surged down his legs, and clearly all he wanted was to get away. He burst into a backpedalling step.

And this next bit was a bit willpower intensive, but Alex’s eyes narrowed. It’d likely get what he wanted to across.

CLANG!

Karnation_’s back struck a Mana Shield—a Mana Shield Alex willed about 20% larger than his typical shields—also about the maximum he could push its size with pure willpower.

Karnation_ seemed momentarily discombobulated, and in the same instant, three more Mana Shields filled the air—adjoining the first, finishing a square of hardened mana, a prison of Pure mana. Alex was surprised when Karnation_ sank to a knee; he must’ve hit his head pretty hard.

Karnation_ glanced up, just in time to see the first Mana Spear descending.

The O2 Guilder could only stare; CRSH-CRSH!!

As four giant Mana Spears crashed through the prison’s open-air ceiling, in quick succession. Finishing a perfect victory.

Is this what the world wants from me?

1-0, WINNER—_1arcstyrion_!

elo increased!

747? -> 795?

At the same time, Alex felt a ping!, to his surprise, and his body flooded with joyous warmth.

He’d also Leveled.

The walkways had cleared quite a bit since his and Caide’s first fight; the few people who were still there were staring, and the walkways were silent. Including Caide, whose lips were pursed.

The stations reset, and Alex was teleported back to his side, as was Karnation_, whose instantaneous reaction seemed to be pure bemusement, like he wasn’t even sure what’d happened to him.

That… might’ve been a little much, Alex reflected; he felt a flicker of guilt.

He’d created that prison assuming the Guilder would react a little quicker; all he’d wanted was to freeze the guy for a second, but with how things played out it obviously wasn’t necessary.

Whatever the case, his little display did, at least, seem to change a few minds when it came to his talent.

Rankings updating…

_1arcstyrion_ (49) (41 -> 29) (+12)

Lv. 6, elo 795?

Alex began making his way back to the walkway; he passed Karnation_ as he went.

“Good fight,” he said, feeling a little apprehensive; he was a little concerned Karnation_ would think he’d humiliated the guy on purpose.

But Karnation_ said, “What the hell was that?”

The O2 Guilder grinned. “You’re fucking insane, dude. What the fuck?”

Alex was glad, at least, Karnation_ didn’t seem to be taking it badly.

Back in the walkway, Alex breathed out, ignoring the stares—including Caide’s, which was lingering on him a little—and finally checked his notifications, which had built up. The first was from the Caide fight.

Skill evolved!

Gives the user innate proficiency in sword-fighting, as well as innate compatibility with all swords-based Skills.

This Skill has achieved slight proficiency (1).

This Skill has achieved slight proficiency in sword spellcasting (1).

All his spellcasting abilities with his new sword had significantly increased the instant he unboxed that spellswords support. It’d definitely helped in that last Mana-Shield-cage-plus-Mana-Spears assault, with all he’d had to wield. Its effect wasn’t that it made any individual attack better, but rather that his willpower, when cast through his new sword, went significantly farther.

He wondered how much better it’d feel still if he was wielding a genuine staff; he liked spellswording, but he was also kind of curious.

Alex was pretty certain almost all of the experience gain happened in the Frigid Knight and the Caide fights, and this just accounted for that last bit of necessary experience.

He was surprised his Shield hadn’t gained a level; he was up to 3 multishields now pretty easily, and his limit right now, if he cast with his sword and focused his will, was 4.

You didn’t just get a new support level for every extra multishield; the requirements for every subsequent level doubled. Alex was already at level 1 for the multishield support, which required 2 shields, so the next level, Alex was pretty sure, required 4.

Which he’d already cast. He pursed his lips—So why isn’t it giving it to me?

I mean—it’s also true that it didn’t just give me the hard constructs support the instant I could make hard constructs. It seems to require a certain level of, like, ability with it. Before it’ll click.

Still, I have to be really close. Surely.

Alex was thinking about this because his Mana Shield, right now, was this—

Mana Shield [Uncommon] (2/3)

Constructs a circular shield out of Pure-affinity mana at the user’s desired location.

This Skill has achieved basic proficiency (2), including a slight degree of shatter resistance (2).

This Skill has achieved multi-shield up to two shields (1).

After a second level of multi-shield, all he’d need was a single support level before his Mana Shield was also at Rare.

Caide had wrecked Alex, and he wasn’t even the first seed in the Rising Stars Cup.

And if Alex was real, an aspect of his performance just now, besides the optics reasons, was because he wanted to redeem himself a bit after his first performance.

But Christ, I need to get so much freaking stronger.

He wasn’t certain if it was realistic to win, but—surely it’s in my powers to not be humiliated the next time I face a Caide-tier enemy?

Or even Caide himself, again.

Alex knew it was kind of silly, thinking of things in terms of if it’d be humiliating or not; he smiled a bit to himself. It wasn’t that serious, objectively.

It was just, he did have his pride.

In that way he supposed he was like Bella, a little.

He also had a few ideas as to how, precisely, he might still increase his abilities in the next few hours; luckily, like with Mana Shield, there were several aspects of his powers he was pretty sure were decently close to evolving.

He wasn’t sure what rank talent he was, in all this; there seemed to be plenty of quite talented folks here. What he wanted, ultimately, was to make certain he’d put in a performance he was proud of.

He smiled a bit.

Okay, that’s—Kumbayah shit. I might not be sure what rank I am, but I’m sure as hell still higher than 29.

Alex watched as Caide faced off against lilly_xd, and finished her in less than 5 seconds in a blitz of golden light. His expression never changed, which felt kind of like forced nonchalance to Alex.

Caide crushed Karnation_ in a rain of golden shards, again in the very first assault; Caide did say good fight to Karnation_, inclining his head, when Karnation_ said it.

Such a prick.
