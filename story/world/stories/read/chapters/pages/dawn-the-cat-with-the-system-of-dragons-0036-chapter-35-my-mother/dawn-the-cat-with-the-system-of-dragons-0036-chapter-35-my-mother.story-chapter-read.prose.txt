Just before the explosion, the infant dragon and the five kittens were sleeping soundly inside Dawn's den.

BOOOOOOOOOOOM!

But suddenly, the loud sound of the explosion reverberated inside, and the infant dragon jolted up in shock and fell on his chin. Ouch!

“Miew miew miew miew miew…”

After the initial shock, he noticed the subsiding light that had brightened the den. But within a few seconds, everything became dark as usual. What the hell!!!

For some reason, he knew how dangerous the source of light was, and his anxiety rose so much that he could feel his own heartbeat. He turned his head to find his cat mother, but she was nowhere in the den. Where is my dumb mother??? Did she get caught up in it???

The kittens also realised their mother wasn't with them, and they began meowing even louder. “Miew miew miew miew miew miew miew miew miew miew miew miew miew miew miew…”

For God's sake, SHUT UP! “EeeUUU UUU…”

Why do these idiots keep mewing inside my ears? Mom, please come back soon. I can’t take it anymore… Why do I have to rely on a dumb cat?!... Of course, why am I even asking? He sighed internally.

He waited for about ten minutes, but still, Dawn didn’t come back, and he started having a series of negative thoughts. Did she really get caught in that explosion… No no no, no one should be dumb enough to get caught up in an explosion like that. Who knows? Maybe she did. She was dumb enough to jump here and there for no reason… Wait, did she create that explosion? She does have the power to change body structure… No, no, that's too far-fetched; or is it? Haaa, don't think about it; nothing makes sense… Come on, stop it already, you guys. How much are you guys going to push me here and there?! “Euuuuuuu…” Dumb kittens…

The infant dragon didn’t even notice how used to he had become to riding the waves of wiggling kittens. He kept thinking while he was being toyed with. Sometimes he was between the kittens, sometimes he was above the kittens, and sometimes he was under the kittens. But nothing stopped his chain of thought.

It is so hard to accept this absurd situation… He sighed. Maybe I should start treating the cat as my real mom… Mom, Mama, Mommy, Mother; How do I even call her??? “Meouu uuuu uuuee uuuuuu uuuuu???” I can’t even speak in this body, so why am I even worried about what to call the cat… I'm an idiot, for sure.

Whatever… Mom, please don't get caught up in that. I don’t even know how to stay alive without you. Becoming a wild cat is surely not easy… It would have been way easier if I were a pet of a rich family, but… He sighed.

If I'm not wrong, animals raise their babies for one or two years, then they leave them behind after they grow up… At least I am safe for a year or two… Just leave it; thinking about the future will not help me in any way.

How annoying these big ass kittens can be!… It is better to stay out than to listen to these continuous alarm clocks. Maybe I should look at what's happening outside. It is better than staying here…

He tried to get away from the kittens, but getting onto the ground was a task in itself. Kittens were continuously trying to find cover under each other’s warm bellies. When one of the kittens pushed him to the ground after getting under him, he tried to stand up. But he was having a hard time. His limbs were shaking as if he had just finished his leg day in a gym.

In the end, he stopped trying and began crawling with his limbs before another kitten tried to get under him. Even the small ditch where he was lying with the kittens seemed too big for him. Moreover, the kittens were too restless.

After a lot of effort, he was finally out of the ditch. Using all four limbs, he began crawling towards the opening. It took him several minutes to reach the opening. He poked his head out to see outside, but there was nothing else apart from the dark, silent forest.

Isn’t this forest a little too quiet? Maybe I should stay in; it's better to be safe than sorry… But venturing out just a bit shouldn't hurt that much…

Just when he began looking left and right to assess the surroundings properly, he felt something beside him. And when he turned his head towards the entity, Cooome ooon… again! What do you want? “Uuuu uuuu?” The biggest kitten was walking behind him with wobbling steps, and four kittens were raising their heads to look at both troublemakers.

Follow the oversized black cat, not me. Go away… “uuuu uuuu uuuu…” He tried to push back the kitten with his head, but it turned out to be a bad decision.

The kitten was not yet developed enough to walk properly, and it immediately lost control of its body and fell on top of the infant dragon. Then it began biting his back for some unknown reason. WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING??? STOP IT, STOP BITING ME YOU A*******… “UUUUU UUUUU UUUUUU…”

In the end, the dragon gave up trying to save himself, rolled his body, and began fighting back. But with the body of an infant, there was so little he could do. He tried to bite it back, but even though it was just a two-week-old kitten, it was the biggest among all the babies, and it had enough strength to overpower him. It kept biting his back, rolling on the ground while holding him, and kept scratching his entire body. The infant dragon tried to do the same, but he ended up screaming in pain throughout the playing session.

Half an hour later, Dawn came back with a disheveled look. She was tired of everything she had just faced, and now, she just wanted to take a rest. But what she saw after coming back was totally unexpected. Outside her den, the biggest one and the smallest one were wiggling and rolling on the ground while hugging each other. Moreover, both were mewling in pain.

“Grrrrrr MEEEOOOWWW…” Dawn's threatening scream stopped them immediately. Both of them looked at her as if a grim reaper had just warned them.

But what Dawn didn’t know was that she had unintentionally released her own elemental energy with killing intent for the first time, which made her eyes glow golden.

Both babies were scared of Dawn's wrath, especially the dragon.

In anger, she walked towards the biggest kitten, picked him up by his scruff, and took him inside her den. Then she came back, picked up the dragon by its nape, and began carrying him inside. But before she could reach the ditch, she felt a sudden electrifying sensation in her mouth, and she dropped the dragon.

She was confused. She tried to pick up the dragon again by its nape, but again she felt the harsh, electrifying sensation on her tongue. She couldn’t even pick it up and had to back away.

In confusion, she stepped forward and looked at its body. What she noticed next literally blew her mind. She saw hundreds of tiny sparks generating from all over its body parts. This was not something she was expecting. In disbelief, she stepped back, and all the sparks from its body disappeared, as if the tiny sparks had never been there from the beginning.

For the first time, she got a hint about this baby's true nature. When she adopted it, she knew it was different, but she never imagined the baby would have an innate ability. And she knew very well what kind of animals had such abilities.

The infant dragon was looking at Dawn with tear-filled eyes. It wasn’t even able to look at Dawn straight in the eyes. Moreover, he kept anticipating what would happen next. When Dawn looked at the dragon’s face, her anger vanished completely. Her eyes also stopped glowing with excessive elemental energy, and only the natural glow of the dark environment remained in her eyes.

Dawn got closer to the dragon’s face; the infant dragon immediately closed its eyes, and again its body began generating electrical sparks. In the end, she stepped back and saw the electrical sparks reducing rapidly. She understood that the infant dragon was scared of her.

Dawn sighed heavily in exasperation and prepared herself to be electrocuted by the infant dragon. She got closer and tried to lick the dragon’s face to calm it down. But she couldn’t, and she had to back away again.

She decided to try again, but this time, she hardened her resolve and began licking the infant dragon’s entire body despite being electrocuted through her mouth. It was hard to continue at first, but after licking the dragon’s fur several times, she got the hang of it and began licking his fur aggressively.

After getting two minutes of a soothing session from Dawn, the infant dragon finally calmed down, and it stopped producing sparks. When he finally looked at Dawn, who continued licking his body, he was even more shocked. She really did get caught in the explosion before!!!

All the glossy fur on Dawn’s coat was partially burned; some of her fur had hardened, and some of her fur had curled into a hard substance. Moreover, after being electrocuted by him, the remaining fur stood straight, making her look scarier than she actually was.

“Meouuuu meouu uuuuuu…” Stop licking me already… Dawn immediately stopped licking and began looking at him as if lamenting something deeply.

After a few seconds, she picked up the infant dragon by its nape and put him in the other corner of her den. Just beside the ditch, she began digging the ground where five kittens were mewling continuously. Then she picked up the dragon and put it in the new ditch.

Then she went to her other babies and began licking them roughly, just like she did to the infant dragon. After a while, all the babies calmed down, and she positioned herself in a way that she was able to cover both of the ditches, and then she went to sleep for the night.
