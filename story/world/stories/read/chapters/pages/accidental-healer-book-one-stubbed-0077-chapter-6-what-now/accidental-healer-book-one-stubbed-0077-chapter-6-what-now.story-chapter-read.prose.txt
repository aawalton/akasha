“Layton asked for us to complete the objective?” Durkil asked slowly, either confused or pleased. Norso wasn’t sure. He’d never been great at reading people, and the fur covered Guildians only made it harder.

With a huff, he turned away, and stretched his arms high above his head, extending them out wide and sucking in a deep breath that puffed out his chest.

“Let's go then. I’d like to see more of what this world has to offer.”

Norso didn’t say anything.

Since coming to earth, things had not gone at all how they were meant to. Both his leader, along with his longtime childhood friend were killed. But for some reason Norso wasn’t angry about that.

He wasn’t angry at all.
