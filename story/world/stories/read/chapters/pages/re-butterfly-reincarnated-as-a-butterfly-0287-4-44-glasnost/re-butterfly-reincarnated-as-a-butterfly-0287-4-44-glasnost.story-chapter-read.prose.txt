Do they actually mean it? Rosslyn thought in Adon’s direction.

Duke Sebas intends to follow through on his word, the butterfly replied instantly, including King Janus in his transmission. I should point out that he is the sort of person who does not consider his promise to be a binding commitment but more like something he plans to do for now, until he comes up with a good reason to do something else.

Typical demon, then, the Queen thought. That is part of why no peace treaties with the Empire have ever lasted. Please thank the Queen for her reasonable stance and willingness to compromise, he thought.

There was a part of the Queen, Adon sensed, that wanted to turn this into an opportunity to gloat, to rub the demons’ faces in their relative weakness, to treat them as already defeated enemies, and to take out all her resentment and rage on those present, for the loss of her country, her father, and the lives of so many of her people.

Espionage could have told her everything she just said, but we have never considered the Kingdom to be competent at spycraft, Sebas thought. What else are they hiding up their sleeves? Why did they even assent to a duel between their King and our champion if they knew they could wait us out? That simply confirmed, in the Duke’s mind, that there was a highly placed spy somewhere within the Empire. Someone who could penetrate their layers of internal secrecy and also get information to the Kingdom despite the fact that the Empire was in a war posture towards it. Someone who Claustria would want to keep alive and useful even in the event that war suddenly ended.

The humans simply stared him down. There was a collective feeling of simply refusing to indulge the Duke’s regurgitated propaganda. He looked as if he wanted to continue, but under the collective gaze of the nonbelievers, he frowned and sighed and stopped talking.

“How would you describe the challenge you face with the monsters?” Rosslyn asked after a moment.

“They are a horde of mindless beasts that we hold back at great difficulty and sacrifice, to the benefit of all who inhabit this continent,” said Kastinov.

The Dukes genuinely believe the monsters are a mindless horde, Adon sent to Janus and Rosslyn. Despite the intelligence that we know exists driving them. It does not seem to be something that was deliberately kept from them, considering their level of prior exposure to the monsters. I’m guessing that the Monster King has deliberately concealed his existence from them.

How do we handle their ignorance? wondered Janus. The fact that we have the alternative of allying with the Monster Kingdom, even if theoretical and only used as a cudgel for negotiation purposes, is not obvious if they genuinely believe these are just dumb beasts.

Rosslyn, maybe I should tell them the things they don’t know, Adon sent to the Queen only.

Just if you feel comfortable, she thought after a moment. It will probably smooth the interaction, but remember that anything you tell them is another piece of information that we can never take back once spoken.

Nothing any of the demons revealed in their inner reactions contradicted Adon’s expectations. They did not know about the Monster King. They were not faking the lack of knowledge unless they had the capacity to fool his Telepathy more generally.
