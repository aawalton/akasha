Diving Basecamps - Master Leo's explanation

~ * ~

Seb woke up to the sound of someone pounding on the door to their quarters. He wasn't immediately sure what time it was, other than much too early for the amount of noise being made. Feeling Anya start to wake up as well, her irritation at the sound clearer than any coherent thoughts, he slipped out of bed, pulled on his robe and went to confirm who was at the door.

Whatever was done to set up the quarters prevented his aura from reaching through to the hallway. He was aware of everyone inside their rooms, he could sense people in the hallway when the door was open, but that didn't help in this case, he had to open the door first.

Opening the door he stifled a groan as Sandra barged her way in, barely even looking at him as she went to the counter holding the coffee machine. Fay and Briana trailed in after her, both looking as upset to be up this early as he felt.

"Briana," Sandra said. "Here's the machine, make it make good coffee."

"Good morning to you too Sandra," Seb said.

"It's morning but there's no coffee. Fay said we couldn't go to the dining hall without you so we're going to have coffee here first and then more coffee there."

"It is too early for you to be this excited," Anya said from the bedroom door, her own robe wrapped around her.

"I want to see everything though. Some of the Constructs here aren't like anything used in your Holding. I'm sure Yshe would love to see and explain what is going on."

"We'd still like to sleep for a reasonable amount of time though."

"I slept plenty. The bed was comfortable and the room was quiet."

She wasn't this bad the few nights she slept over, Seb sent. Was she?

Nothing new to explore?

Seb turned towards Briana as she fiddled with the coffee maker.

"Thoughts?"

"There's a few small things that'll help it work better. It'll still be sub-par, but not horrible."

"Maybe if she's got coffee to focus on she'll-"

"Already brewing," Briana said with a smile. "She'll be the test subject."

Seb realized Gwen and Cara were about to join the group and turned towards their door, calling out to them the moment it opened.

"Sandra wanted coffee, and I'm guessing bacon once we get to the dining hall. She was too impatient to wait for a reasonable hour so woke everyone up."

"Why bacon?" Cara asked.

Seb felt Anya's resigned reaction to the question. The answer was obvious, at least to anyone from Holding Syl, but it caught Sandra's attention despite her focus on sounds the coffee machine was making.

"Because family breakfasts always have coffee and bacon. Eggs too, but bacon is more important."

"Why?"

"Because if people aren't paying attention you can grab their bacon, or they grab yours. And good bacon is good."

Maybe you should? Seb sent.

"Seb made eggs the first time we had breakfast, and we had coffee the first time we spoke. We added bacon to breakfast because Sandra is right, bacon is good."

"You let her grab your bacon?" Gwen asked.

"Taking the last piece or pieces is a challenge, everyone tends to want more. Sandra especially likes stealing un-eaten pieces."

"Then why not make something else that everyone can have their own portions?"

"And not have bacon as family?" Sandra said, sounding shocked. "Anya and Seb take turns cooking the bacon and eggs. It's what they do when we get together when they come home for a break from Diving. Then they kick me out because they need to deal with Holding business."

"You cook when you're taking a break?" Gwen asked. "Don't you have retainers or servants who-"

Seb started to chuckle. He smiled and shook his head before managing to answer.

"Fay is family but also our Seer, we have an Arbiter who comes to deal with that side of Holding Business. Then there are three retainers, one of which is a Healer, who run our Dive Administration. That's the entire Holding."

"Hey!" Briana said. "What am I then?"

"Affiliated to the Holding and maker of the best coffee we can find?" Seb said quickly.

"I suppose I'll accept that."

"This coffee isn't bad," Sandra said, sipping the mug Briana had handed her.

Seb took the next mug and tasted the coffee. It wasn't great, the dining hall had better, Holding Syl had better, and he wasn't even going to think about comparing it to Kaf-Fay's brew.

"Better, much better."

"There might be something to improve it further," Briana said. "But not with what we have here."

"Bri," Fay said. "They don't expect you to make it perfect."

"They deserve something better than this, and that's not to mention the thought of them serving it to guests."

"We don't expect any special beverages," Gwen said. "We understand the limitations this place imposes on our Hosts."

"Hosts?" Briana answered, clearly confused by the choice of word.

Seb saw Gwen hesitate and look at Anya.

"Do they not know? I would have thought Lord Percivus would have clarified."

Fay answered even as Seb worked his way through the question, Anya's thoughts mirroring his.

"Lord Percivus has not yet answered beyond saying there might be precedent."

Gwen forgot that they weren't aware? Seb sent.

Or assumed they'd been told since.

"Is there a reason we can't tell them?" Anya said

"Seer restrictions," Fay said.

"They would need to swear that they would not share the knowledge," Cara said.

"You didn't have us swear," Seb said.

"It is Anya's ancestry and you are a bonded pair."

"I want to know!" Sandra said. "Is it a secret? Is it a surprise?"

"Sandra," Anya said. "Drink your coffee."

I doubt Briana will say anything, but Sandra? Seb sent.

Seb! She's not that bad. She understands that there are things you do not share.

"I want bacon," Sandra said. "Can we go get food now?"

"Some of us still need to get dressed," Seb said. "We were woken up by banging at the door."

"Sorry."

Seb looked at Gwen and Cara. Gwen still seemed slightly flustered by the reaction to her referring to them as Hosts and he suspected both would want to change, their current attire looking more thrown on than intentional.

"Will you join us for breakfast?"

"As long as we are not an imposition," Gwen said.

"More bacon!"

"We'd be glad to have you. Especially if we can get an answer from Lord Percivus."

"Yes," Gwen said after a quick glance at Cara.

Seb nodded and ushered Anya back into their bedroom, aware of Gwen and Cara returning to theirs as Sandra turned to get more coffee. He was glad that the drink didn't seem to have any effect on her, he'd hate to imagine what she'd be like if caffeine energized her further.

No, Anya sent. Absolutely not. If coffee did that to her I'd refuse to be around her, even if she is my sister.

I didn't say anything.

You didn't need to. We, well you, but quite vividly so we, pictured it.

Seb shut the door behind them, intentionally picturing other things as he answered.

Wow.

I love you Seb, she sent back, the words filled with a mixture of warmth and amusement.
