Charlemagne was feeling pretty good as he dropped off the captain he’d stolen onto the deck of his ship.  For one thing, coming up with the idea that the captains might be interchangeable showcased his slowly developing mental capabilities.  While Charlemagne was still a long way away from understanding the concepts of “fungibility” or “interchangeable parts”, he was making progress.  But, more important to the rooster’s mood was that he had gained a lot from eating the other Knight.  The Squiggles had even given him a very encouraging message saying so.

The rooster queried the Squiggles regarding some of the terms included in the message, specifically “Life Fruit”, “second-hand” and “life-aspected mana”.  The responses he received were underwhelming.  The only answer that gave him any relevant information was for the term “second-hand”, which he found insulting.  “Second-wing” would have been a more appropriate choice of words.

After dropping off the captain and ordering him to set a new course, Charlemagne returned to the other Knight’s ship to “inspect” and guard its food stores.  The simulated sailors hunched over in terror as he descended into the hold.  He didn’t bother with them at all, since they were not attacking him and would provide no benefit if eaten, at least according to his Special Ability.  He was, however, drawn to a small cask that appeared to be in much better shape than the rest of the comestibles stored in the hold.

Ignoring the boxes of ship’s biscuits, salted pork, and various dried goods, the rooster pecked through the top of the cask and took a deep breath.  A heavy, sweet, and sharp scent rose from the nondescript brown liquid contained within.  Intrigued, Charlemagne shoved his entire head into the cask and began to slurp down the liquid.

It burned, but in a good way.  So he drank the entire cask.

After the rooster was finished with his liquid lunch, he found himself ready for a nap.  So, without further ado, he found himself a nice nook down in the dimly lit hold and passed out.

He was rudely awoken by being plunged straight into the ocean.

Flapping his wings in alarm, Charlemagne found that his leg was caught on something.  Upon closer examination, he realized that the “something” was quite large and possessed many sharp teeth.  He had been attacked by a shark.

The rooster, still groggy from the rum and an interrupted power nap, kicked his trapped leg.  But, despite his ridiculous strength, Charlemagne had no real leverage and remained trapped.  He was considering his next move when the shark decided it was time to dive deeper.  The water, which had already extinguished his flames, grew even colder around him.

Charlemagne finally woke up fully as the shark attempted to get a better grip on the rooster, managing to punch through his feathers and skin with its razor-sharp teeth.  There was only one thing he could do in such a situation, and Charlemagne acted without delay.  He blew the shark’s entire head off with a hastily-created mana ball.

A huge cloud of blood erupted from the headless shark, filling the rooster’s nostrils with the tantalizing scent of iron.  The beast had not been that powerful, but it was perfectly adapted to its environment.  Charlemagne couldn’t wait to eat it.  There was just one problem, however.  He could already sense another five sharks on the way, and he would rather not have to kill them all just to enjoy his meal in peace.

Grabbing onto his latest victim’s corpse with one of his claws, the rooster pushed himself toward the surface of the ocean with all his strength, using the power stored in his Ignition Core to rocket him toward the surface.  Reaching out with his magical senses, he located the others, finding to his pleasant surprise that they had caught up while he was napping.

A moment later, Charlemagne burst through the surface of the water and continued to ascend, dragging the shark along with him.  A few flaps of his wings allowed him to adjust his trajectory as he slammed down onto the ship’s deck, the body of the shark beneath him.

The deck broke, causing the shark to plummet through and crash into the upper part of the hold.  Thankfully, nothing important was stored directly below the breach, just some ballast stones.

Ignoring the shocked looks from the others, Charlemagne began ripping huge chunks of flesh from the shark and devouring them.  The skin was very rough, but the lean, meaty texture was satisfying.  There was a bit of a bitter aftertaste, but it wasn’t exactly unpleasant.

The door to the captain’s cabin burst open and the captain emerged, swearing up a storm and threatening instant keelhauling to whoever had just damaged “his” ship.  The rooster looked up just long enough to lock eyes with the erstwhile officer before returning to his meal.  The door to the captain’s cabin shut moments later.

After cramming down about half his body weight, the rooster slowed down.

“Bawk,” he said around a big mouthful of shark meat.

“I’ll try some,” Phasima agreed, “but only if you cook it.”

Josephine nodded in agreement.  Phatagin appeared dubious of the proposition but eventually nodded as well.

“I suppose if it is properly cooked,” he said.

Charlemagne pulled off a large chunk of flesh and tossed it onto the deck before sending a gout of flames washing over the deck.  The timbers blackened slightly under the heat but otherwise held up.  The shark meat ended up more than a little blackened, however.

Phasima created a small circle of ice underneath the meat, which swiftly rose into the air on four legs.  Having no need for a chair, she floated over to her makeshift creation.  A three-pronged fork and a knife appeared in her hands, and she dug in without hesitation.

“Oh my goodness, it’s perfectly seared!” she exclaimed.  “I thought for sure it would be a charred mess.  How did you manage to cook it evenly?”

As soon as the words had left her mouth, Charlemagne received a new message.

Skills Gained:

Cooking 1

Charlemagne knew that many species preferred their food charred by heat, but he was still somewhat surprised by the Squiggles’ message.  After all, he hadn’t really done all that much.  Nevertheless, he tore off two more large lumps of shark flesh with his beak and engulfed them in flames.  Using one of his mana barriers as a tray, he set them on the table.

“Bawk,” he urged.

Phasima was kind enough to make forks and knives for Josephine and Phatagin, but it turned out that neither needed them.  The former had already brought out her own utensils, while the latter had to specially prepare each bite.  The pangolin, despite having received several genetic enhancements that expanded his ability to obtain calories from sources that were neither ants nor termites, still struggled with the mechanics of ingesting food items.  Before his alterations, Phatagin, like all pangolins, ate through a thin slit at the end of his long, tapered snout, using his sticky tongue to capture his prey before consuming it.  And, although his snout had received a bit of a size upgrade, he still had to be very careful.

“Squire Phatagin, do you have to make everything that you eat into a noodle?” Josephine said as she turned her nose up at the pangolin’s eating habits.

“Yes, I do,” he answered.  “Unless you would prefer that I choke to death.”

The Attendant muttered something under her breath that sounded shockingly impolite before turning her attention back to her meal and cutting herself a decent-sized bite.  But, before she could lift the bite to her mouth, she suddenly made a strange face and excused herself.

“I think this would go better with salt and lemon juice,” she announced.  “I’m going to see if I can find some.”

Without further ado, Josephine slowly descended further into the hold and began rummaging around.  Her departure seemed to have prompted the crew into action, because a trio of the very one-dimensional sailors walked up to the remaining three diners carrying a variety of tools.

“Th..the cap’n wants us fer to fix the deck,” one of them said, her knees knocking together in abject terror.

Charlemagne looked up from his meal and nodded.  When things around him were broken on the farm, the humans always showed up with tools and made it better.  That was the proper way to handle things that he owned.  He would allow this.

“Bawk,” he said to the others as he casually flung the shark’s carcass back into the water with his beak.

“Yeah, I guess we should let them get to work,” Phasima replied as she and the table began to float toward the back of the ship.  I’m pretty sure that we can find a better spot to eat.  Maybe somewhere a little less rolly,” she added as she looked down toward where Josephine had disappeared.  “I think that Josephine might still be seasick.”

Charlemagne was about to respond when he felt a strange sensation begin to well up from deep within him.  It wasn’t just from his body: he could somehow sense that the feeling was growing from whatever connection that he had forged with the Squiggles.  It felt like something that had been buried for a while was coming to the surface, some reward that had been withheld.

Unfortunately, that sensation only lasted a few moments before the pain hit, as his Inferno’s Embrace Skill kicked into high gear and began resisting whatever changes that the Squiggles were making.  The rooster fell to the deck twitching in agony as he received a slew of messages.

Congratulations, you have consumed a sufficient amount of high-quality shark flesh.  Your skin will be more resistant to damage, and you will be able to hold your breath underwater for longer periods of time.

Congratulations, you have consumed a sufficient amount of flesh from an apex predator from the animal kingdom.  Synergy with apex predator detected from the plant kingdom.  Synergy detected with <ERROR>.  Partial memory wipe detected.  Bit Bleach Achievement detected.  Synergy detected with unknown catalyst.

Congratulations, you have consumed a sufficient amount of high-quality organic material to initiate a species upgrade.  Species upgrade may not be delayed and will take place immediately.

Charlemagne’s vision collapsed into a single point as his entire existence turned into searing pain.  His soul felt like it was being stretched beyond its limits, and he feared that, at any moment, it would tear itself asunder and he would simply die.  He fought back instinctively, pushing back against the forces that were trying to pull his essence apart while reinforcing his soul with both mana and heat.  His suffering lasted for what felt like hours as the struggle continued.  He heard snatches of conversation around him, although he could not make out anything that was being said.  Finally, mercifully, the wrenching sensation ended, and he allowed himself the luxury of falling unconscious.

When he awoke, his throat was parched and he had a pounding headache.  But that meant that he was still alive, so Charlemagne gladly accepted the pain over the alternative as he reviewed his notifications from the Squiggles.

Charlemagne yawned, drawing gasps from the others as he unfurled his wings to their full extent.  Then he opened his eyes and looked around.  He could already tell that his species upgrade had been a huge success.  His Ignition Core rumbled with a deeper and more primal frequency than before, hinting that it had reached new heights of power.  He also felt much more in tune with the body enhancements that he had been granted through Inferno’s Embrace.  Even his eyesight had grown more powerful, allowing him to see small objects far off in the distance.  Like the ship that had just appeared on the horizon.

“Bawk,” Charlemagne hollered to the others as, with a hop and a few flaps of his wings, he accelerated past Mach 1.

The other four watched in stunned silence for a moment as the newly recovered rooster blazed through the sky like a meteor.  Finally, Phatagin broke the silence.

“Perhaps Charlemagne sensed something?”

“Maybe another ship?” Josephine suggested.

“Yeah, that could be, but…do you think he even noticed that the ship got upgraded?  I mean, it looks totally different,” Phasima said.

Josephine looked around at the ship, which now sported additional sails, a larger deck, and more cargo space, with additional crew commensurate with its new size.

“I’m pretty sure he didn’t,” she concluded.
