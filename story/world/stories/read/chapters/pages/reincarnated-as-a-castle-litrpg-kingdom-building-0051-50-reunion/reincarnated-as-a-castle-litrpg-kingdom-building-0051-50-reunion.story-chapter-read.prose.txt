For a moment, Gopuran could only stare. Her eyes widened in disbelief as she looked at the massive bear corpse sprawled across the forest floor. Even now, she couldn’t quite believe it.

She and Gopuro had actually done it. The two of them had taken down that giant beast on their own.

Honestly, the entire fight had been suicidal from start to finish. What Gopuro had done near the end still made my nonexistent blood boil. One tiny mistake—just a fraction of a second too slow—and both of them would have died right there in the mud.

But in the end, only the result mattered.

They were alive. The bear was dead. And the five goblins had been saved.

That was enough.

As furious as I still was at their recklessness, I couldn’t deny it—I was genuinely proud of them. My first citizens. They had fought bravely when it truly counted.

"HELL YEAH! WE DID IT!"

Gopuro let out a triumphant roar, jumping in place before throwing his arms around Gopuran in a fierce, blood-soaked hug. She was completely drenched in bear blood now, so much so that she now looked like a demonic creature painted entirely in red.

But then—

“ACK!”

Gopuran suddenly froze.

Her face twisted in pain.

Right.

The side effect of [Enraged Lv. 1].

It was kicking in.

The brutal backlash had finally arrived.

“Well… good luck, you two.”

The words had barely left my mind when the goblin girl’s legs gave out completely.

She collapsed onto the blood-soaked ground with a heavy thud, unable to move even a finger.

A few seconds later, Gopuro suffered the same fate.

“URGH! FUCK!”

He dropped dramatically beside her, cursing as his entire body locked up. After that, he lay there motionless, unable to move even a single muscle.

For the next full hour, both of them were completely helpless.

Thankfully, no other beast wandered into the area during that time. If anything had appeared then, they would have been easy prey and it would have been terrible.

It seemed that the deafening noise caused by the bear’s rampage had already frightened off and driven away any nearby creatures.

****

Later on, as soon as the paralysis finally wore off, the two sprang back into action. Sure, they were still suffering from the -50% stat penalty, but it was not like they had to fight another bear. If anything, they said they were just a bit more tired, but still able to perform their tasks.

Gopuran immediately set off to search for the five goblins they had saved, while Gopuro stayed behind to deal with the bear’s corpse. I switched my [Vassal’s Eyes] over to him at once.

By “processing,” what he was doing was pretty straightforward: he simply used the iron axe to chop the carcass into massive chunks.

Even so, the thing was still absurdly huge.

Judging by its size, the total weight had to be over two tons. There was no way the two of them could haul the entire body back to the castle before sunset. Worse, every second we stayed here increased the risk of attracting another predator.

The thick smell of fresh blood alone would be enough to lure something dangerous from deeper in the forest.

(Fuck… is there really no way to bring this thing back?)

Just then, Gopuran returned.

And she wasn’t alone.

The five goblins had made it back safely.

Among them were two males and three females. One of the males was the evolved goblin we had seen earlier. Standing beside Gopuran, he was a full head shorter than her.

Super Forest Goblin was clearly the superior evolution line here.

The moment the five saw the dead bear sprawled across the ground, they froze in shock.

Their eyes widened.

Their mouths hung open.

Clearly, none of them had expected to see that monster defeated.

But then their eyes landed on Gopuro.

“Brothers?” Gopuro tilted his head, looking at the evolved goblin in confusion.

“BROTHER!!!”

The five goblins rushed toward him all at once and nearly tackled him to the ground in a wave of frantic hugs and joyful cries.

A moment later, they were practically dancing around him in celebration, like long-lost siblings reunited after years apart.

Of course, because Gopuro was now much bigger than all of them, the scene looked more like a giant green dude celebrating with a group of excited children.

It was strangely wholesome.

Beside them, Gopuran’s eyes began to glisten with unshed tears. She wiped at them quickly before stepping forward to join the reunion.

I let them have the moment.

They had earned it.

But after a little while, I pulled my focus back.

Alright, enough celebration. We still had work to do!

And now that we had five additional goblins here, an idea quickly formed in my mind.

I instructed Gopuro to keep chopping the bear into manageable pieces.

Meanwhile, I had Gopuran quickly collect the most valuable-looking parts: the massive paws, the heart, and the severed head.

Those, along with the five goblins, would be taken back to the castle first.

This way, I could sell the monster parts to the System for Castle Points and convert some of that into Powerstones.

And with those Powerstones…

I would be able to evolve these five goblins as well.

If that worked, we’d have more strong laborers capable of hauling the rest of the bear back in multiple trips.

*****

Moments later, the citizenship granting process went smoothly.

One by one, the five goblins stepped forward, accepted the System’s offer, and were officially registered under my rule.

Just like that, I had taken in five new citizens. Hell yeah!

Meanwhile, the bear parts had sold for more than enough Points to cover the cost. After converting some of them into Powerstones, I distributed the glowing crystals among the new recruits.

Crunch after crunch, they devoured them like candy, eyes sparkling with delight as the power flowed into their bodies.

Then the leveling began.

A series of bright chimes rang out in my mind as their levels climbed rapidly.

Level 6.

Level 7.

Level 9.

And soon enough, all of them had reached Level 10. Except for the evolved guy. He was already level 13.

Nice!

The four remaining goblins then evolved.

A series of bright glows later, they changed form!

The three females turned into absolute beauties, just like Gopuran!

However… the one dude… he looked… weird?

For some reason, he evolved into a completely different form than Gopuro. Instead of being muscular and broad, he was now slender and much shorter, with spiky hair that made him look exactly like some kind of anime protagonist who just received a dramatic power-up!

I quickly checked his status…

Still Super Forest Goblin, though!

But then, as I scrolled down, I was utterly confused yet intrigued.

Instead of having most attributes put into Physical Attack or Physical Defense like Gopuro, this dude was somehow able put most of it into SPEED!
