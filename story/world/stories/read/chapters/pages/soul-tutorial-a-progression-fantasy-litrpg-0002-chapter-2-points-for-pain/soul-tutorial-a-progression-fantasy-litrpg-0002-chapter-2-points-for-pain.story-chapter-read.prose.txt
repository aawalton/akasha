Luke lay flat on the ground, stirring from his unconscious state. A killer headache pounded against his skull, making it impossible to decide what to do next. Pain radiated through every inch of his body, and passing out hadn’t helped in the slightest.

With gritted teeth, Luke turned his gaze toward the pedestal, the only thing that might help him right now. He tried to push himself up but collapsed instantly; a fresh wave of agony spread through him.

His legs were useless.

With no other choice, Luke used his arms to drag himself forward. He gripped the rough soil, clawing his way toward the pedestal inch by inch. It was miserable; he still had 2 meters to go. He looked around and found nothing that could help him.

His body was so numb he couldn’t even gauge how bad his injuries were.

But he kept going.

Time passed, and Luke was now just inches away. Behind him, a bloodied path marked his struggle, deep grip marks carved into the soil, dirt smeared all over his battered body. His clothes were in shreds, offering no protection. Carefully, he lifted himself, trying to minimize the pain, but his chest throbbed as if swords were piercing into him.

With one final push, Luke reached the pedestal.

He lay directly in front of it, pressing his trembling hand against the smooth stone surface. A familiar screen flashed before his eyes.

Three new things appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Name: Luke Holloway

Points: 0 → 5

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Restore: 5

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[Attributes]

Physique: 0

Constitution: 0

Agility: 0

Intellect: 0

Sensory: 0

Soul Resonance: 0

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

In the top left, he saw points, specifically five of them. He wondered why he had so much when he had technically only beaten 1 creature. Also, a new restore tab had been added.

Luke’s face lit up. A wide grin stretched across his lips, revealing his teeth. If restoring cost five points, that meant he could fix his broken body right now.

But how would he do that?

He tried pressing the tab, but nothing happened. His smile faltered, shifting into frustration. He furrowed his brows, trying to think. Then, an idea hit him.

Focusing intently, he imagined selecting the restore tab.

The screen responded instantly.

Points: 5 → 0

Luke felt a wave of relief wash over him as his points depleted. His wounds mended before his eyes. The pain faded. For the first time in what felt like forever, he felt weightless, as if he were lying on a soft cloud where nothing could hurt him.

But that was just an illusion.

Luke wouldn’t let death creep that close to him ever again.

Drowsiness overtook him. His eyelids grew heavy, his body relaxed, and he collapsed with a soft thud, sending dust swirling around him.

Shortly after, Luke woke up; drool leaked from the corner of his mouth. His eyes fluttered open, his vision still adjusting. He lifted his upper body, resting his arms on his legs.

For a moment, he just sat there, lost in thought.

The memory of almost dying sent a shiver down his spine. He turned his gaze back to the pedestal; his eyes flashed with curiosity.

Luke stretched, and cracking sounds echoed through his body as he stood up. He took a step forward, the ground rough beneath his bare feet. Beyond the island, he saw only pitch-blackness, except for two small floating specks in the distance.

Nothing had really happened since his fight with the slime. His brows furrowed as his mind wandered, searching for answers.

But he pushed those thoughts aside. He’d figure it out, eventually.

What was strange, though, was that this island, the atmosphere, the way the air felt, functioned just like Earth.

Luke now stood directly in front of the pedestal, reaching out his hand. He hesitated for a second. As he rested his hand on the tilted surface, the screen reappeared.

[FLOOR 0]

[RESTART]

[FLOOR 1 — NEXT FLOOR]

[SUMMON]

This time, he noticed something he had missed before. Floor 1 has been unlocked, but there was also a restart option below floor 0.

Luke had a feeling he knew what this meant.

He focused on the restart option, watching as the surroundings distorted. Small particles gathered at the exact spot where the slime had been before.

His jaw tightened. His fists clenched.

‘I have to fight this goddamn slime again?’ He thought, ‘Fine,’ he growled.

This time, Luke grabbed a random branch from the ground. It felt lighter than the last one, less dense. It would probably break after a few hits. But that didn’t matter.

Luke took a deep breath. Then he ran.

His body flapped with each step, weight shifting side to side, slowing him down slightly.

‘I need to train more. I swear this is going to kill me,’ he thought.

He was half a meter away when the slime stirred. But Luke was ready.

He lowered his stance, shifting his body weight.

The slime lunged toward him. Luke dodged, hopping to the side.

The moment the slime landed, Luke swung down. His strike connected, only sending a wobble through the creature’s body, but the damage was minimal.

Once again, the slime sprang into the air. Luke intercepted it mid-air with his arm. Interrupting its leap toward him. But the slime took the chance to delay a jump to spray some acid toward him.

Luke’s heart skipped a beat. The acid splattered just inches from his feet.

“No way am I going through that again.” He said sarcastically under his breath.

Luke backed up fast. The memory of his burnt flesh flashed in his mind; his body shuddered involuntarily. The moment of hesitation gave the slime an opening; it lunged toward his position.

Luke dodged right, his foot leaving a deep imprint in the ground. His shoes were gone from the previous battle. The slime didn’t wait; it only took this chance to attack again, using the ground as a cushion, bouncing itself through the air.

Luke kept moving, dodging, and waiting for an opening.

The fight dragged on. Small craters littered the ground. The slime looked weakened, but Luke was exhausted. He moved sluggishly. His branch was nearly destroyed; this one had lasted even shorter than the last.

Gritting his teeth, Luke grabbed another branch.

Footprints covered the battlefield, marking every step he had taken. He was still dirty from his previous fight. The only thing Luke wanted right now was to be clean.

But first, this needed to be over.

Luke sprinted forward, his branch raised high.

The slime tried to dodge, but it was too slow.

Luke leaped, swinging downward with all his strength. The branch landed directly on its head, sending a shudder through its body, cracking the shiny core that was suspended in its body.

The body lost its form. Slowly, it deteriorated into nothing.

Luke crashed to the ground, exhausted. But this time, he didn’t feel despair.

A wide smirk spread across his face; short giggles escaped his lips. He had won. He had actually won.

Time passed. Luke finally pushed himself up as he made his way to the pedestal. Before leaning on it, he placed his hand on the surface.

Points: 0 → 1

He eyed the five attributes on the screen, each with a 1-point purchase tab beside it.

Luke considered his options. His body still felt heavy from the fight. Agility? Physique?

Agility would let him move faster and dodge better.

Physique would most likely let him end fights quicker with brute strength.

After a moment of thought, he made his decision.

Luke had tried physique.

If he could land an attack, he needed to make it count.

If he were going to survive, he needed strength.

Physique: 0 → 1

Upgrade cost: 1 point

Points: 1 → 0

As he glanced into the darkness again, something new caught his eye.

Not two. Four.

Four specks floated in the sky.

Luke narrowed his eyes.

‘Does this have to do with the death of monsters?’ He thought.

Then, in an instant, Luke found himself dazed, staring off into the distance. His eyes locked onto the four specks, and for a moment, he swore he felt himself grow just a little stronger. The thought sent a rush of excitement through his mind.

A grin spread across his face as he imagined the endless possibilities of what this place held for him.

Needing rest, Luke picked a spot near a pile of branches, just 2 meters from the pedestal. He grabbed one and used it as a headrest, though it was anything but comfortable. His body ached, and irritation crept into his skin as the rough ground pressed against him.

No matter how many times he shifted positions, the soreness from battle made it impossible to find relief. He furrowed his brows as the discomfort in his feet set in; his constant dodging had taken a toll.

Exhaustion finally got the better of him. His eyelids quivered, his features softened, and he gave in to sleep, resting on his side.

Sometime later, Luke woke up, unsure if it was night or day. The surrounding darkness seemed to lighten ever so slightly, suggesting some kind of cycle in this strange place.

He groaned as he pushed himself up, steadying his stance. His body still felt sore, but he noticed something: he felt stronger. Putting the point into the physique had made a difference.

But his thoughts drifted back to the lights in the distance. Could soul resonance connect to them?

Luke stood tall, though the sting in his heels made standing uncomfortable. Before heading back to the pedestal, he tested just how much stronger he had become.

His maximum push-up count before had been twelve. Now, he wanted to set a new record, even without training.

He dropped into position and powered through the first 10 with ease. That alone was a shock. Usually, by this point, his arms would be shaking. Yet he pushed out two more, then two more after that. He collapsed trying to complete the fifteenth, but a wide grin stretched across his face. It wasn’t anything too impressive, but it was actual progress.

Determined, Luke set a goal to grow as strong as possible. Before he restarted the floor and faced another slime, the boy ran through basic calisthenics. Push-ups, sit-ups, squats, anything he could do without equipment.

When finished, he approached the pedestal. The screen appeared before him, and he took a deep breath before focusing on the restart option. If he wanted to get stronger, he needed to repeat floor 0 as many times as possible.

The slime spawned, but this time, the air around Luke felt heavy. He locked eyes with the creature; his body radiated determination. Having grabbed a branch from his resting area, he charged forward.

His feet pounded against the ground as he launched himself into the air. His arm raised, he felt the air shift around him before he brought the branch down with full force. The impact sent vibrations through his hands, but the slime barely had time to react.

Luke didn’t let up. He struck over and over; the branch slowly dissolved in the creature’s acidic gel. Even as his weapon weakened, he refused to give it a chance to fight back.

Luke’s attacks came from a low stance, using the ground to fuel his strength. Within minutes, the slime was nearly dead, but Luke’s own body was reaching its limit.

He stumbled back, gasping for air, keeping his eyes locked on the slime in case it tried anything.

Then, without warning, acid began spilling from the creature’s form, sizzling as it hit the ground. Luke felt the heat radiate from it. He steadied his breathing, knowing one misstep could end his training session. He had learned from his first fight to always be ready for the unexpected.

He carefully maneuvered closer and gripped what was left of his weapon. He studied the slime, looking for an opening. With one last strike, the branch snapped in half against the creature, ending its life.

As the slime dissolved, Luke’s gaze flickered to the darkness. Two new specks had appeared.

He exhaled; exhaustion crept into his limbs. The adrenaline faded, replaced by a deep soreness in his hands. He clenched his fist, feeling the strain in his grip. His lips pressed into a thin line as he stepped toward the pedestal.

The screen reappeared. Everything still costs one point, except for strength, which now requires two. He only had one point, so he chose Constitution.

Constitution: 0 → 1

Upgrade cost: 1 point

Points: 1 → 0

Instantly, he felt something crawling under his skin; his bones creaked. It was a strange sensation, as if he had become an immovable wall.

Before he restarted, Luke gathered every branch he could find, sorting them by durability and density. A few of them looked sturdy enough to last through a couple of battles.

‘It’s time,’ Luke said as he stretched his arms.

The floor restarted, and Luke launched himself into battle. He fought slime after slime, winning more and more matches, but fatigue set in fast. His movements grew sluggish, and he barely dodged some of the acid attacks. He had to finish this last fight quickly.

He switched tactics, moving in a zigzag, trying his best not to be predicted by the slime. When he swung his branch, the creature dodged. He nearly left himself open, but corrected his stance just in time.

Luke circled the slime, waiting for the perfect moment. He had noticed something, each time it sprayed acid, it weakened itself. It was a small but valuable piece of information.

Finally, he spotted his opening. The slime wobbled to the right, and Luke struck. His weapon grazed its side, but the creature retaliated. However, Luke would not let history repeat itself.

Instead of swinging again, he did something different, he ran. It had confused the slime enough that Luke saw it and swiftly shifted back towards it.

Gathering every ounce of strength, he sprinted toward the slime and thrust his branch straight into its core.

For a moment, everything went silent.

Then, darkness.

Luke’s vision blurred. His body felt like it was being shut down; the exhaustion consumed him. He couldn’t make out what happened next; his mind collapsed in on itself. Before he could even process it, he blacked out.

When he came to, every muscle in his body ached. He gasped, struggling to breathe. His body refused to move, ignoring his desperate attempts to rise.

He lifted his head slightly, scanning the area. The slime was gone. He had won.

Relief washed over him, but it was short-lived. His body still refused to move. Panic crept in. His heartbeat quickened; sweat formed on his brow. What if he had become paralyzed?

Then, slowly, he felt something; his fingers twitched. A shudder ran through his limbs as sensation returned. He trembled, pushing himself up inch by inch.

Luke staggered toward the pedestal. As he raised his head, his eyes widened.

A grin stretched across his face.

His point count had increased.
