Thalion tried to ignore the implication that he might die and instead focused on himself.

The days leading up to the third System Event passed quickly, and Thalion spent most of his time meditating. He also spoke with the Echovore god many times about the soul and how to defend against mental attacks. Under the Echovore's guidance, Thalion hardened the outer shell of his soul body to absurd levels and created countless tiny pockets loaded with energy. Those pockets would act like mines, and the moment another influence triggered them, they would detonate and bathe his soul body in his own energy, washing away the enemy's influence.

Quite a neat trick.

It wasn't the ultimate defense but rather a failsafe in case someone managed to overwhelm his soul for a brief moment. He still had his soul core, which could cleanse him of hostile influence, even though the Echovore considered it a crude solution and insisted it would be better to unwrap the core entirely and create a new one from scratch.

While in his Eclipsari form, Thalion consumed everything with a darkness affinity that the gathering parties brought back, along with a large number of crystals. In his human form, he practiced several combat styles with Saranya, who remained a relentlessly strict instructor. He also continuously trained his blood and fire control since his human form would be the one he spent most of his time in during the upcoming event.

Skipping the hunting expeditions and focusing entirely on himself had definitely been the right decision. The steady progress calmed his mind, and when he stood on the plaza during the final minutes before the event alongside Ricarda and the eighth daughter, he actually had a good feeling.

The tension was naturally high, considering the System Event they were about to enter. The eighth daughter looked confident and eager, while Ricarda was practically radiating negative emotions. Seeing her breathing heavily while glancing around as though hoping someone would come and save her wasn't exactly inspiring.

Still, when it came to elves, Thalion didn't have much sympathy. Ricarda was also a Chosen, which meant she would have to face situations like this many times in the future. She should also have a relatively easy matchup, provided she didn't end up in the same bracket as Ryan.

To be fair, Thalion also had the feeling that the third System Event demanded a creative and alert mind. If he had to name the craziest of the three creators behind the System Events, that title unquestionably belonged to R-87.

"Oh, I just remembered one thing. For R-87, round stones are the best stones," Thalion said, and not a second too late, as the notification appeared on his status screen.

It was time to go and face the other victors of the previous two System Events.
