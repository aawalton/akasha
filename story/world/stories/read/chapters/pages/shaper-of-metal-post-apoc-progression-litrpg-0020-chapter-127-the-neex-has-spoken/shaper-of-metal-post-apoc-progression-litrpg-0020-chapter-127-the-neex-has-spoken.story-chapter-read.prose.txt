Chapter 127: The Neex Has Spoken

As Jack mentally chatted with Neex, he went to the kitchen for something to drink and a snack.

Neex suddenly seemed nervous about something. “So, Jack, I put in a… request. To Memoria. Something I was asking about for a while. And it was approved.”

Jack’s eyebrows rose curiously as he slapped a simple sandwich of meat, cheese, and bread together. “Go on. What is it for?”

“To see New Babylon before I leave. The city.” Her mental voice was suddenly laced with wonder. “The hordes of people shuffling around. The varied locations. Th-the ‘night life,’ I believe it's called. Which will persist for months.”

Chewing on his sandwich, Jack said, “Actually, they’ll flip the big lights on in a couple of weeks, for a poor man’s sun as if we were more equatorial, so it doesn’t get too depressing. Jury’s out on whether it's all that helpful. But now is the time, I suppose! Hey, that’s great, Neex. I guess you’re really one of us, now.”

“Yeah! Yeeeah…” Again, the hesitation.

Rather than just ask what was up, Jack paused to activate his morning-fogged brain and consider what the deal was. Once the hamster wheel began to spin, it quickly hit him. “So, do you need a guide? I’d be happy to take you.”

Pleased relief flooded the bond. “Yes! Thank you! Mmn. You know the city so well! I must see everything with you, Jack. A night on the town with Jack! Can we go now?” Anxious excitement bubbled up.

Blinking, chewing, and pondering, Jack pinged Mini just to make sure all was well. Mini answered quickly. “It’s fine, Jack, but you need to go in disguise. Not as Jack Laker. You can choose something subtle and an alias.”

Jack then replied to Neex, “This is early for most people, but I believe we can go after I get ready and then pop into CP for a disguise. Hell, this is great, actually! Perfect way to celebrate. Good idea, Neex.”

“Yes! I will be ready!” There was a sudden, long pause. “I-It’s a date-see-you-then!” Mental voice vanishing into something strangled with each syllable, she quickly closed the connection at the end.

Jack choked a bit on his sandwich, from initial surprise and then shifting to amusement at her hurried delivery. He shook his head.

Alright, then. The Neex has spoken, and it’s a date.

He went through his morning routine to get ready, showering and eating a bigger breakfast, pondering exactly what Neex was thinking about the day. He knew he wanted to keep it light and fun to cool those nerves of hers. It was difficult to know what someone from such an alien culture thought about these things, but the very way she delivered her outlook said she knew exactly the connotations of a ‘date’ in ‘Skyhuman’ culture, and wanted one. Considering all the media she was consuming, it shouldn’t be very surprising.

Jack liked the idea, too, though he was way more relaxed about it. All too soon, she’d be enchanted by the city, anyway. Jack loved it enough himself to know its effect, especially on a newbie. Anyway, it was important that she enjoyed it, and he planned to ensure it.

It certainly was… the broaching of a subject. She’d taken what was likely a leap for her. He’d have to start asking questions at some point to figure out where the cultures intersected and what expectations were.

He dressed casually, but not too casually, picking out a nice button-up shirt and a ubiquitous coat against the windy, chilly baseline of New Babylon, especially when the sun wasn’t around. He was tempted by the dark side, that was that musky cologne he’d abused before, but resisted the impulse. Well, he had no clue what Neex would prefer there, after all.

The rest concluded, Jack put in a request for a disguise and was quickly directed to Central Processing and then Operations. Amusingly, heading into public was technically a mission.

He parked himself in a chair in the familiar ‘disguise salon’ where he’d become Agent Steel for his last trip into the city. It was quite a bit different this time, as the salon was quiet and nearly barren but for one disguise tech in her full-body get-up. There was also no rush, and the tech emphasized this if he wanted to be particular about his disguise. And he did.

He had to have a conversation about what he wanted, first finding out they could do a ‘mimicry improvement’ instead of a static voice change. Basically, he could fake his voice better when he wanted, or drop it. That seemed ideal, so he went with that. Looks-wise, he heard different technical options spouted, and ultimately decided to rely on the tech’s expertise. He just asked for the minimal modification required to throw people off. Keep his face familiar, for Neex’s sake.

When it was all done, he looked in a mirror and blinked a few times. If he squinted, he looked like himself. Only focusing more did he seem like Jack’s ‘brother.’ His build was already way off from anyone who thought they recognized him. His eyes were the exact same, but that was because he could wear shades and see perfectly fine. Preferable, really, due to his relative sensitivity and what he’d have to look forward to in the varying bright lights on the streets and rooftops of New Babylon.

Remembering something his previous tech had said to him as he was looking in the mirror before, at Agent Steel, he went with the name, ‘Mikey’, for a false name.

He did everything else he needed in Operations, such as snagging a false ID, a new wallet, and stowing his prior personal belongings in his locker, transferring what was permissible from one wallet to another.

Finally, he exited an elevator on the rooftop inside Nimrod and approached a generic Q-23 vessel. Standing in front of it was a woman unlikely for the environment, smiling widely as she watched him approach. She was wearing a white sweater dress belted high at the waist, long brown boots, stockings seen at the window of the thighs, a light coat, and a big, wide-brimmed hat. She even had a matching purse. Long, purple hair cascaded down one side of her face. A face vaguely reminiscent of Ira and Neex.

Wow. She’s astoundingly pretty.

The woman held out her hand as he neared, revealing white lacquered fingernails. “Hi there, Soldier! I’m Nichola, and I believe I’m your date today!”

Returning her smile, Jack took her hand lightly. “Then I’m in luck. I’m Mikey. You look fantastic, by the way.”

Nichola beamed. “Thank you!” She twisted herself, lifting a leg backwards as she acrobatically gestured at a boot, turning it this way and that. “Look at these boots! Ahhh! So cute!”

“Yes, they are.”

“I am in love with many human boots! It was very difficult to decide on merely two.” A pause, as she cleared her throat and looked sheepish. “I was also told you can’t mix. ‘You can’t hedge your bets on boots,’ Bo said. They must match.”

Jack chuckled. “Aw, who says? You can wear whatever you want.”

“What about the fashion police?”

Jack blinked. “Fashion police?”

“Yes, I read they will come for you if you do such things as clash patterns, wear too many accessories, chase trends that don’t suit your body features, and so on. While two different boots weren’t specifically mentioned, I believe that falls under the clashing of patterns. I would not wish to be arrested or fined for illegal violations of protocol.”

Jack burst into laughter that he had to contain at Neex’s confused look. “Nee- er, Nichola, um, I’m afraid that’s just a figure of speech, not literal. Heh. There are no such police.”

Nichola deflated as her eyes twitched. “Oh. But Bo- Crap! I should’ve investigated that more. I guess I was in a rush…” She cleared her throat again, then smiled. “Anyway — you look great, too!” She suddenly turned her body as she curled her arm around Jack’s. “Let’s go to New Babylon Proper!” She bounced on her toes.

“You got it, fashionista!”

Jack and Nichola approached the vessel arm in arm. When they got closer, the doors opened automatically. Jack moved to help Nichola in, but she just adroitly leapt up into her seat like a monkey. Stifling his amusement, Jack less adroitly moved in and took a seat across from her. They strapped in as the doors closed.

“Where to, sir?” came the masculine voice on the intercom. Jack noticed the barrier between the cabin and the cockpit was dark, so he couldn’t see the pilot.

Before answering, Jack prepped his new mimicry tweak, summoning a slightly different, smoother voice, and with an Origin accent. “Trudy’s Car Rentals and Sales,” Jack finally replied.

“Roger that, sir. Sounds like I’m gonna have an easy day.”

“Yeah, I feel the itch. I’m sure you get it.”

“That I do, sir. I’ll now deactivate the intercom two-way for absolute privacy in your cab, which you can verify with your interface. Buzz me as needed.”

“Sounds good.”

As the vessel lifted off leisurely, Nichola raised an eyebrow and asked, “Are we renting a car?”

Jack nodded. “That’s right. Might as well fly in style, mm? You can pick the car.”

Her eyes widened. “Really?!” She smiled and clapped her hands together. “Oh, something sleek and colorful! Ah, shucks, I forgot… the current season should have more subdued colors reminiscent of Fall. F-frag.”

“I think that’s just for clothes. You can pick a bright color if you want.”

“Really?!” She fist-pumped. “Yes!”

“So, is there anywhere specific or in general you’d like to go first?”

“Where there are lots of people! Eventually. But, wherever! Any ideas?”

“Hmm. In this timeframe, The Runner’s Strip in Downtown isn’t at its busiest, but will be plenty stacked for lunchtime. Those with the bennies to be vertically mobile and in no hurry probably hit Origin for breakfast and coziness. Old, historic Mulberry Heights. Actually, you’ll absolutely want to see it, so that sounds like the best bet to start, though… Well, my first idea isn’t the busiest, but there are the Mulberry bushes. Most of them will be picked, but I know some spots. They’re as ripe as they get, right now.”

“Oooh…” Nichola had been listening intently, and now she sighed contentedly. “Savoring berries, right off the trees of your first peoples… I would like that very much. You’re sure it's okay?”

“It’s tradition. I’ll buy you a signature basket, too. I know where to get the authentic ones. A nice memento.”

His date was so pleased by this that she curled inward with her arms and scrunched up her body, smiling gleefully. Her hair twitched. “Yes, please.”

Jack couldn’t help but smile. He had a feeling Neex would have a whole purse full of mementos by the end of things. Boy, did it feel great to suddenly have massive amounts of disposable income!

The vessel dropped down from the top of the tower to the NB Proper, and a ways off from Downtown Atalanta, touching down in an open lot area full of various vehicles on display.

Jack took Nichola around immediately to look at the vessels, and a salesman moseyed up soon thereafter, asking after what they were looking for and guiding them. He also asked if ‘Mikey’ was buying, renting, needed a pilot’s services, and so on.

A sky blue beauty ultimately caught Nichola’s eye, so much so that she literally ran over to it and marveled. It was of a typical 21st-century Corvette style, which Jack could very much appreciate. Behind retractable covers, it had ‘false’ wheels that spun with decorative blades inside, and sleek black highlights for the secondary colors of the frame. The racecar-like interior was just as ‘ooh’- and ‘ahh’-inspiring.

Honestly, he would’ve found it hard to walk away from even if he was alone. Nichola’s fascination made it a done deal. Naturally, it wasn’t cheap to buy or rent, but he locked it in, went inside for the paperwork, and got the keys. Everybody was smiling when all was said and done.

After they got settled, Jack cleared his throat and turned to his date to ask, “Would you mind a bit of freespace flying real quick? Before we hit Origin.”

Nichola gave him a thumbs-up, grinning. “Even I know the look of a boy with a new toy. This vessel wants to go fast, doesn’t it?”

“It’s absolutely begging for it.”

Among other things, this might be the last chance I get to do this for a while.

Jack felt an old, delightful pleasure as he activated the MALPP drive and took the car up and away. Interestingly, it connected to his System interface directly, feeding him a slurry of information on other vessels in the area and the nearby non-crossing zones, though he knew most of them by heart already.

He registered his intentions to Nimrod and flew them out from the main platform. Out and out, picking up speed, and angled away from the majority plane of all the other platforms. Far enough out, there were high-speed zones and routes, and you could register a zone and route as yours to go nuts on. That’s exactly what he did at the first one he could get to. The coveted circle all the way around the tower, like you owned the place.

The tower in the distance, he ramped up speed progressively through the open air, fast enough on the acceleration to feel it and make Nichola squeal a bit. Mostly in delight.

He flew as fast as he had in a long time. It was damned faster than his cherished cabby car, and ultimately faster than he’d be willing to push it.

A giant waste of energy — until he looked inward and found the primal glee of engaging in such power over the air. And it was countered even more when he looked across as the other one strapped in the chair, eyes wide, laughing gleefully, amazed, her eyes flickering around in wonder at the sky and clouds and moving shapes, with her arm having reached across the space to grab his shoulder tightly.

That’s what I’m talking ‘bout, right there.

After a while, he slowed it down and began to drift back inward to the city. Looking over at Nichola, who was perhaps panting just a little bit, he smiled wryly and said, “Something tells me you wouldn’t mind that ride again sometime.”

Giggling, she nodded. “I still can’t believe I’m flying!”

“That’s what we Skyhumans do, after all.”

“You especially, Jack! Er. Mikey, that is.”

“Why don’t we just drop the false names in private this time around? We can handle it.”

“Oh. Yes, I think that’s a grand idea! I’ll allow it.”

“That’s my Neexy.”

She smiled at him silently and warmly, then turned to look out the window. “Berries, then breakfast, perhaps?”

“There’s this place where you bring your own berries as one of the toppings for waffles, or preferably, their fluffy, ridiculously delicious french toast.”

“Oooh! I have no idea what that is, but fluffy toast sounds wonderful!”

“Oh, you’ll love it, trust me.”

Jack brought them to the Origin Platform, moving suddenly at a comparative snail’s pace to comply with speed requirements in the airspace. Over to the colorful, greenery-saturated Mulberry Heights district, laced with little shops, old homes, and endless dwarf trees. Most of them were bare this time of year, though some fallen and smushed berries lay underneath some of them, and birds were plentiful in picking around.

“Wow!” Neex exclaimed, looking out the window at everything. “The buildings are very colorful and unique! Is that in the shape of a giant berry?!”

“Ah, yeah. A cute place, I guess, but the food is meh. I heard some people say it used to be better. The original owners are gone or something like that.”

“I see.”

Jack took the vessel into a sheltered area surrounded by an old wall in a square, almost completely covered inside with trees. He made a ‘tsk’ sound when he saw the single damned space a vessel could touch down was occupied, so he had to go further out to park legally on a road.

No biggie. Just gotta walk.

After parking, they walked around a decaying, vine-strewn wall with seemingly no opening. The lighting could only be described as ‘moody’ here, and there were only a few people in the area. They came to an arch, but it was apparently filled with stone. Jack grinned as he took Neex’s hand and tugged her along through an actual hologram that passed inside. She made a startled sound at first.

“Oh!” Neex laughed as she turned back around once they were through, where the street could be seen. “I should’ve known!” She giggled. “Quite an adventure we’re on, so far.” She looked down at their held hands.

Jack made to pull away, but she gripped tighter to forestall him. He smiled and relented. “That we are. That hologram has been there since before Mom would bring me here. Somehow, it’s a secret that's stayed reasonably well-kept. Anyway, let’s go get you that basket.”

He took her past endless rows of Mulberry bushes that were all but hidden from the streets outside the square. Birds flew out of their way as Neex whispered apologies to them. Whoever else was here, he couldn’t even see them, though he caught a flash of the vehicle parked. Likely, they were collecting a huge horde of ripe mulberries somewhere.

An area under a tree was nothing more than a small stand and a chair. No one was there, but the few things offered had their prices written, and instructions to leave money in a box built into the cart. Jack put the required tender in the box and told Neex to pick out a basket.

Each of the handmade, topped wicker baskets had a design of a berry bush on the front and a butterfly of different colors on the hoop.

“Oh my gosh,” Neex exclaimed, leaning down and inspecting the handiwork. “They are absolutely beautiful!”

Jack nodded. “Used to be an old lady here, but she passed away, and her daughter does it now, though she isn’t here as much. Does the detail just as well, though — maybe better.”

Neex picked out one with a blue-and-purple butterfly, taking it in her hands solemnly, and bowing her head, muttering something in her own language, before finally standing and nodding to him. “Now, let us pluck the berries!”

Jack smiled. “You got it.”
