It had taken Asher and Rulfar a couple of hours to deal with the remaining minions, find Grant, and get him back to his village. Yet by the time Asher Recalled back down to the rest of his teammates, Moxy looked pretty much the same as she had before.

Permanence - lvl 29

“Still no access to your Life element?” he asked, raising an eyebrow as he took in the sight of Samantha gently spraying water on the warrior’s chest with Soothing Spring.

“Nah, not yet,” Moxy grinned, at least looking a little better than she had when they’d left. A bit of color had returned to her face, which was a strange sight seeing as she still had a gaping hole in her chest. “Like I said, overtaxing my body like that takes a while to come back from. That Permanence skill of yours is pretty wild.”

“It definitely has its uses,” Asher said, sharing a small smile with Samantha before clapping his hands together. “Well, Grant’s been returned to his people and The Lost Lady is no more. With Zygrul having run off to the void and Kroult on our side, that brings the Council of Death down to only nine members. Sort of wild that we’ve managed to slay three liches in the span of a few weeks.”

“Nine members left,” Rulfar muttered, scratching at his stubble as he grunted. “Though things only get trickier from here. The King of Death and The Lost Lady were the only two liches who didn’t bother to go into hiding. Whoever we attack next is going to tip our hand that we’ve got insider information.”

“But we only need to kill two or three more before we can get some countries to throw in with us and go on the offensive,” Samantha pointed out. “Especially with Kroult on our side. I bet they’ll be more easily persuaded if we reveal we have a defector helping us.”

“I feel as though I should reiterate that I do not have any information about the Council that you would not already know,” Kroult pointed out.

“Yeah, but they don’t know that,” Asher grinned. “You’d be something of a mascot. Whatever it takes to get them to help us.”

“Great plan guys, love what I’m hearing,” Moxy said, weakly waving a hand in the air. “Though if we’re done here, maybe we can head back to the palace and get me an actual healer? As much fun as being able to see the ground through my chest is, I wouldn’t put it past Runt to get himself killed in the next few minutes, and if his skill goes, I go.”

“Fair enough,” Asher chuckled, throwing up a Spatial Lock around all of them and triggering Recall. The world twisted and shifted around them as the cold, stone cavern they were in was replaced with his and Samantha’s bedroom back in the palace, and they all let out a slight sigh of relief.

“I’ll take Moxy to find a healer,” Rulfar said, scooping her up carefully in his arms and grimacing as he stared at the giant hole in her chest. “...A healer in the palace who won’t ask questions. Kroult, can you come with to keep your Remove Pain skill running?”

“Of course,” the lich said, casting his minor illusion over himself and tugging the hood of his cloak up just to be safe. “Lead the way.”

“I told you two, I’ve got an evolved pain resistance,” Moxy argued, frowning as she tried to raise a hand to punch Rulfar in the chest, only for her arm to start shaking and give out before she even got halfway. Even hours after using her powerful blended skill, she could still barely move. “Damn it, this is why I hate this skill!”

Asher rolled his eyes as the door closed behind them, leaving him and Samantha on their own. Turning toward her, he went to crack a joke about finally being able to see through Moxy when Samantha tackled him.

“Samantha?” he wheezed, barely having managed to keep himself from reflexively slipping into the astral realm at the surprise attack. It took him a moment to realize Samantha was shaking with her arms around him, and he blinked at the sudden sobs that racked her body. “What’s wrong?”

Rather than answer, Samantha continued sobbing against his chest as she squeezed him tight, and Asher did what he could to simply be there for her. Letting her get it all out of her system, he ran his fingers through her hair, doing what he could to comfort her. Eventually, her sobs slowed to the point where she could speak, and she took a deep, shuddering breath as she looked up at him with tears in her eyes.

“It… it was horrible,” she sniffed, clearing her throat as she shook her head. “I could feel the hairs forcing me to move and fight how the lich wanted me to. Being made to attack you like that… somehow having someone else access my tritessence and skills… I didn’t even know something like that was possible!”

“I’d bet most people don’t know it is,” Asher admitted. “These liches have had hundreds, some of them thousands of years to research and improve their crafts. The Lost Lady may have been the only person in the whole world capable of such a feat.”

“I hope that’s true,” Samantha whispered, placing her forehead against his chest as she let out a weary sigh. “I knew we’d be going up against unimaginable monsters… but with how strong our team is, it’s easy to forget sometimes. Sometimes I just feel like I’m dragging the rest of you down. All I managed to do during that fight was let myself get captured and slice off your leg!”

“For starters, Moxy also ended up getting captured, so I wouldn’t worry too much about that one. And I seem to recall you taking two of the minions on at the same time while the others only took one apiece. And losing my leg? You inadvertently helped me unlock my second blended skill, so I should be thanking you for that! Though I’ll admit I’m not really sure how I’m supposed to go about leveling this one…”

As useful as Remote Control had been in that moment, the skill sort of sounded like it required him to start lopping off random body parts and throwing them around to work on it. It wasn’t the first skill that required him to get injured to improve by any means, seeing as he’d had Eternal Regeneration from the very beginning, but it was still confusing. And speaking of Eternal Regeneration…

Asher frowned as he looked at the level 39 skill sitting silently in his interface. It was a somewhat funny complaint, but despite how close to failure their mission had been against The Lost Lady, he really hadn’t suffered any serious damage to his body that had needed healing. Other than Samantha slicing off his leg, which had been so cleanly done that reattaching it had been a breeze, he’d escaped without any serious injuries. The Lost Lady’s freaky pain attack that had put him down for a bit had been designed to incapacitate, not destroy. Which meant despite everything they’d just gone through, he was still waiting for the skill to tip over to the third tier.

Asher spent the rest of the evening sitting with Samantha and comforting her as she talked about what it was like to lose control of her body like that, or about their lengthy battle with The Lost Lady overall. He knew he needed to head over to Donvath at some point and check in with the High Prince to figure out what it was he wanted to talk about, but Samantha was more important. He felt bad for not realizing just how shaken up she’d been about the incident, but seeing Moxy’s condition had forced that to the back of her mind for a while.

Eventually, Samantha fell asleep in his arms, and Asher carefully extracted himself with the help of Astral Dip, smiling at his fiancé as he pulled the covers over her before heading out to find Donvath.

“Alright, what’s this big, beautiful news you had to share with me?” Asher asked after being let into the High Prince’s office. It was already pretty late at night, but as per usual, Donvath was still hard at work. “And shouldn’t you have a team of scribes or something to help you with all this. I swear, that stack of paperwork gets larger every time I come in here.”

“I have a team to help with all this,” Donvath said, sighing as he looked up at the towering stack that threatened to crush him at the slightest bump of the desk. “This is all the stuff that’s important enough that only I can deal with it. They handle most of the boring, day-to-day stuff. How was the hunt?”

“The Lost Lady is taken care of,” Asher said, unable to keep the proud smile off his face. “That brings the Council of Death down to nine members.”

“If I wasn’t witnessing it happen in real and future time, I’d say I wouldn’t believe it,” Donvath admitted, putting his quill down. “By the way, I’ve taken the liberty of setting up Samantha’s first therapy session for her. The therapist will come to your bedroom first thing in the morning, so make sure you’re decent. And don’t worry, she’s my therapist as well, so no need to bother hiding anything from her.”

“What? A therapist?” Asher asked, his heart going out to the poor hunter as he realized she might have been even more affected by their recent encounter than he’d thought. “...What happens in the future?”

“That’s the same as taking a giant handful of sand, throwing it into a raging river, and asking me ‘what happens to the grains of sand?’” Donvath drawled. “I told you before, there’s not one single future. The paths are constantly in a state of flux and chaos. However, there are enough futures where things start getting worse before they get better, and it isn’t until Samantha starts seeing a therapist that she improves. I’m just jumping ahead a little bit here to speed things up. Call it a perk of working together.”

“Alright. Thank you,” Asher said, choosing to trust Donvath like he had so many times before. “Wait, does Moxy need to speak to one too? She also got caught up in that hair-possession attack. Kroult did as well.”

“Moxy is a special case,” Donvath said with a blank face, and only then did Asher recall that the High Prince really didn’t like the boisterous warrior. “Trust me, she’s fine. You’d probably have better luck trying to make a rock feel something than her. She’s all battle, all the time. And Kroult is already seeing the therapist. The man was sealed in a metal tomb for six hundred years after accidentally becoming an undead monster during an event that took from him the only person in his entire life that mattered. You think the first thing I did after getting him scanned via truthseeker was anything but shove him in a room with a therapist?”

With how soft spoken and collected Kroult seemed, it was easy to forget just what horrors the man had gone through. “In that case, what was the big important thing you wanted to talk to me about that you couldn’t mention over sending stone? I’m pretty tired as well, but I wanted to check in before I went to sleep.”

Despite the fact that Donvath had assured him ages ago his private office was probably the most warded room in the entire kingdom, Donvath still pulled out his amulet and tapped on the gemstone in the center. As a large, black sphere popped into place around them, he sighed as he leaned back in his chair.

“I actually managed to figure out who it was that sent the Shadow Squad after you.”

“You did?” Asher asked, startled by the update. After witnessing the trio of assassins who had come after him completely incinerate themselves, he’d really thought finding out anything about them would be next to impossible. “How?”
