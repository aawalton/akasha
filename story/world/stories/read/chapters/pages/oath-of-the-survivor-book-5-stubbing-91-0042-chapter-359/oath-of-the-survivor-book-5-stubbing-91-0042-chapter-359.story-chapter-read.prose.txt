The roads leading outside the main city were entirely empty, with signs plastered near the exits warning of deadly beasts, and encouraging people to stay inside.  Even more impressively, there were several high-tier D Grade guards posted at the gate.  They weren’t orcs, and a quick glance showed Kyle that they were Collective hires, likely from one of the Guilds.  Moreover, all of them had a distinct feeling in their aura that they’d defeated C Grades before.

Walking up, Kyle raised his hand in greeting, also flashing his Courier’s Guild credentials.  “Hey there.  My job is taking me along this road.  Are these general warnings, or tied to a specific creature?”

The guards shared a look, and a human with a massive greatbow finally spoke.  “C Grade monsters set up a nest a couple hundred kilometers away.  They’re hunters, though.  Territory is large, and they have lots of prey here.  No disrespect, friend, but this job is probably not worth it.  If any of those monsters show up here, our job is just to hold them off long enough for the City Overseer to join in and repel them.”

Remembering the impression of weakness his own aura gave off, Kyle just smiled.  “I appreciate the warning, but I think I’ll be fine.  Say, does there happen to be a bounty on these creatures at all?”

The other human shook his head.  “None that I’m aware of.  Maybe the smaller villages have one.  Seriously, though, don’t do something stupid.  There are multiple C Grades in the nest, and you’ll be throwing your life away chasing a few credits.  It’s not worth it.”

“I won’t do anything foolish,” Kyle said with a guileless smile.  After all, was it really foolish if he was confident in taking out the nest?

Of course, Kyle’s first priority was getting to Duroc’s village, but if he was making good time, and the hodags were on the way…

He raised a hand in farewell to the guards, Kyle took off at a brisk jog.  The brisk jog in this case being faster than most mid-tier D Grades could move with their boosting skills active.  Through Auric Perception, Kyle felt that there was an identification skill of some sort tracking him, likely having been used by the archer.  It acted like a mark that followed him, and was one of the well-defined upgrade paths for most E Grade identification techniques.  He still had plenty of time before Identify would get an upgrade, but this was a path worth keeping in mind.

Still, while under observation, Kyle didn’t want to show his real travel speed.  The moment he felt the mana from the mark disperse, he activated Aura Force.  A plane of force was projected in front of him to cut the wind.  Each footfall was coupled with small blasts of power to increase his speed without sacrificing mobility and control.  It wasn’t his top speed, but it was incredibly efficient.  By keeping the domain of Aura Force tightly contained, he reabsorbed the majority of the mana, as opposed to burning it away like he did when he flew.

Like this, Kyle crossed the red, rocky ground in a blur.  There were plentiful shrubs and wild grasses springing out of the ground, and Kyle saw a surprising amount of diversity in the various animals grazing across the land.  Large, plodding lizards grazed on tough shrubs, while smaller mammals grabbed seeds underneath, running and hiding between the cracks in the ground.  None gave off a powerful impression, though seeing the slow-moving reptiles helped Kyle understand how the hodags managed to get such a deep infestation here.  Creatures like these were the perfect prey, after all.

Just over an hour after they left the city, C.H.A.D.D. flashed a warning.  [DR. MAYHEW, I BELIEVE I LOCATED THE HODAG NEST THE GUARDS REFERENCED.  APPROXIMATELY TWENTY KILOMETERS TO THE EAST.]

Kyle considered for a moment.  They were making great time, and it didn’t seem to be that much of a detour.  “How far ahead of schedule are we?”

[AT YOUR CURRENT PACE, I EXPECT YOU TO ARRIVE AT DUROC’S VILLAGE IN APPROXIMATELY TWO MORE HOURS.]

Kyle felt a rumbling hunger from Phaegos.  “Let’s make a quick detour,” Kyle decided.

The drone obliged, highlighting a path toward the hodag nest as Kyle abruptly changed course.  As they approached, Kyle could feel the familiar presence of C Grade hodags, a little surprised to feel three of them at the same place.  Either this is a better environment for them, or whoever is guiding their evolutions has refined their process, Kyle thought with a frown.  In either event, Kyle was more than prepared to thin their numbers.

Using Aura Force to climb skyward, Kyle soon found himself looking down on the nest.  As he suspected, there were three C Grades all basking under the warm sun, while a cluster of D Grades milled about, occasionally snapping at each other as they feasted on the carcass of one of the large lizards.  Taking a stabilizing breath, Kyle pulled his shirt down over the exposed wood of his left shoulder, then willed Model: Cannon to attach.

The familiar weight of the prosthesis threatened to pull him off balance, though Kyle quickly adjusted.  Marcus had some recommendations for each of the prosthetics, which had been one of C.H.A.D.D.’s projects while Kyle was training.  Model: Cannon was not designed for prolonged use.  It was meant to deliver massive opening blows.  Marcus had identified that at a glance, and recommended improvements exclusively to improve the firepower and accuracy.

Now, the massive razorpine shell was nestled inside a rocket of the enhanced bamboo, with a two-part propulsion system.  Kyle would initiate the first with a blast of Repel, which would then trigger the razorpine pod to activate, increasing both accuracy and power.  All this to say, as Kyle took aim, he was excited to see just how much the improvements worked.

The moment before he released the attack, he activated Genesis Engine.  As he did, the C Grade hodags stirred, but it was too late.  Kyle heard wood crack as the munition was sent down toward the ground, and felt the shockwave as it exploded into one of the C Grade hodags.  A massive cloud of red dust was kicked up in the wake of the attack, and he felt one of the C Grade auras disappear.  Pushing himself down into the cloud, he relied on Auric Perception and Synaptic Barbs to locate his remaining opponents.

A tendril of Parasitic Resonance soon found its first mark; one of the D Grade hodags.  To Kyle’s surprise, however, the skill reacted differently.  Rather than the usual connection to the creature that he’d need to manipulate, the moment the tendril connected a flood of death-affinity energy entered it, killing it instantly.  Kyle was so shocked, he didn’t react in time to avoid the swinging tail that caught him square in the torso, launching him out of the cloud of dust as he coughed up blood, feeling at least a couple of his ribs broken.

What was that?  Kyle thought at Phaegos, picking up the entity’s frustration.

It’s not working how I want it to, Phaegos complained.  Try again.

Since when can you use death energy? Kyle asked, bewildered.  As he did, he placed a hand on his bruised torso and activated Healing Impetus, feeling the cracked ribs start to snap back together.

Since you let me absorb it, Phaegos replied.  Now get back in there so I can keep trying.

[STOP ARGUING AND DEFEND!]

Kyle refocused just in time to feel mana gathering within the two remaining C Grade hodags.  He recognized the attack they were bringing to bear, and activated Storm Shelter, weaving in the concept of immutability.  An ear-splitting roar shook the ground as twin shockwaves barreled toward Kyle, pulverizing everything in their path.  Until they met Storm Shelter.

The blasts of sound and force slammed into the barrier, causing a set of cracks to form across its surface.  It was still a D Grade skill, after all, trying to defend against the strongest offensive skills the hodags could bring to bear.  And it held.  Still, Kyle found himself a bit disappointed.  Should have reinforced it with Aura Force, he thought.  The defensive skill was still incredibly powerful, growing more so as his attributes climbed.  Qualitatively, however, it was beginning to fall behind what he’d need as he increased in power.  After Parasitic Resonance, you’re next.

Dropping the barrier, Kyle dashed toward the hodags before they had a chance to make another attack, the echo of their first roar still faintly reverberating through the steppes.  Parasitic Resonance reached out to the D Grades, killing them instantly as it had before.  You know you aren’t getting to eat anything when you do this, right? Kyle thought at Phaegos as he closed in on one of the C Grades.

‘Thank you for helping me upgrade my skills’ was the correct response.  Now let me practice.

Kyle grunted as he vaulted over another swinging tail, finally unleashing Aura Force broadly to redirect the second tail strike while also deflecting a storm of needle-like hairs.  Activating Cultivate, Kyle launched a quickly-grown tangle of vines at the C Grade whose tail he’d deflected, binding the tail to the other hodag’s forelimb as it tried to swipe at him.  Parasitic Resonance reached out to the hodag with the bound tail, and as before the deathly energy began to infect it immediately.  However, rather than killing it instantly, the hodag held on.

Phaegos, end it.  We’re not here to cause suffering.

I’m working on a breakthrough, the entity protested.

With a growl, Kyle took full control of the skill, quickly overpowering the hodag’s Willpower and snuffing out its life with a twist of force.  The remaining C Grade roared at him as it tried to bite him, and Kyle allowed it to approach, only to equip himself with Model: Spear and pierce directly through the creature’s maw and into its brain.  The weapon was impossibly durable and sharp, improving both attributes alongside Kyle’s Willpower.  It was an anticlimactic end to the creature that had once been so dangerous to him, and a testament to Kyle’s own growth.

Stepping away from the destroyed battlefield, Kyle turned his attention back to Phaegos.  What are you trying to do?

I told you, I’m working on upgrading your skill.  And I can’t do that if you don’t let me practice.

I remember what it was like fighting that energy.  We aren’t going to go around experimenting and torturing a bunch of monsters to find the right upgrade.

I’m not saying we torture anybody, Phaegos shot back.  But you need to understand that you are only leaning into a small portion of what Parasitic Resonance can do.  If you are serious about upgrading it next, trust that I understand its nature far better than you do.  And let’s not pretend you haven’t caused your fair share of pain and suffering with the skill, even as it stands today.

That’s different, Kyle thought with a growl.  When I didn’t have a choice, I did what I had to.  I didn’t intentionally prolong any suffering I didn’t have to.

Kyle could feel the entity’s frustration building, just as he felt his own temper begin to rise.  Before they could get into it any further, however, C.H.A.D.D. flashed another warning.

[A C GRADE PRESENCE IS APPROACHING QUICKLY, DR. MAYHEW.]

Sure enough, a spot appeared on the drone’s map, moving toward them at an alarming speed.  Looking around the destroyed area, Kyle had to admit that they weren’t exactly subtle in their attack on the hodag nest.

“Is it the overseer?” Kyle asked.

[IT IS COMING FROM THE WRONG DIRECTION, BUT I WILL NOT RULE OUT THE POSSIBILITY ENTIRELY.]

All in all, Kyle decided the best course of action was to wait and meet the C Grade here.  If they were friendly, all the better.  If they were hostile, better to deal with it in the wilderness than around a city.  Fortunately, they didn’t have to wait long.  A diminutive gray form soon appeared atop a nearby stone, a wide grin exposing sharp, pointed teeth.  “It’s been a while, Kyle.  Now, should we spar here, or wait so that Duroc can be the referee?”

Letting out an exasperated sigh, Kyle couldn’t hide a smile of his own.  “Skippy, it’s been a while.  Good to see that you caught up.”
