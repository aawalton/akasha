Freddy Jones was having a bad day. A very, very, bad day.

“Look, Freddy. You know we don’t wanna have to do ya like this, but the Boss can’t have ya going back on your word. Ya know that right? And ya remember what that means to have too, right? Your word that is? Like when ya say you’ll pay back a 100,000 Zeni by Grada the 5th. Well guess what? It’s already Bahna the 21st, and the money? It ain’t nowhere to be seen Freddy.”

“I-I’ll have it soon! I promise! I’ll have it soon!”

Freddy might have been slightly more in control and less panicked if he wasn’t currently being dangled by his feet in the dead of night above a deep dark pit. He had no idea what lurked below but whatever it was, it sure wasn’t no Threadian. That should have made things ‘ok’, most things didn’t eat threadians. Not a lot of taste in yarn.

Judging by the splatters of stuffing still being flung up the walls by the last poor bastard, this thing wasn’t so picky.

“Now ya see, I believe ya. I really do. But the Boss… oh the boss ain’t so much a man of faith as me, ya know? He ain’t gonna just let ya waltz on around. He’s been nice and good to ya, hasn’t he? Got ya right out of that jam with the Pricklins and we both remember that whole ‘Porcelain’ fiasco. I personally lost an arm for that one for ya.”

Angry Murry stared at him and Freddy nearly choked on his own stuffing as he saw a flicker go through his cold gem eyes. Murry was a good sort really, no nonsense. He let ya have all the rope you could want, just so ya could hang yourself with it. Big-un too. From a line of Rock Dolls with Gem for eyes. But there was a reason he was Angry Murry. Nice… right up until he ain’t.

“Now, ya told us, you had it figured out. We gave you that cash with the expectation there’d be no problems, ya know? Well, the Boss sure did at least. What happened Freddy? Where’s the money? You ain’t a whoring man and you sure ain’t no gambling man, so What. The Hell. Happened?”

Freddy reflected for a moment. Something he rarely ever did in his life. He lived alright, his job wasn’t too bad, and his ties with the local mafia were mostly only skin deep. Hell, he’d call em friendly most of the time. Didn’t ever do no wrong by ya as long as you never did no wrong by them. Sold some drugs, ran the red line district, all the classic stuff. And sure, more than a few gamblers end up dead in a ditch but even then, the mafia preferred to cut em up a bit, get their pound of thread and stuffing, and throw them away like last week’s trash before actually killing ya.

The problem, like all things usually was in his soon to be too short life, was with him.

“...it was a woman.”

“Huh? What, did it get stolen? Ya know we woulda helped ya get it back ya idiot. Sure, sure, there’d still be some debt, some interest, but we wouldn’t be hanging around old George’s pen if ya hadn’t avoided us for the last two months straight. Running was the last straw, ya know? Thought Jerry was gonna shoot ya and ya know how much he hates using that thing.”

Freddy’s stuffing jumped into his throat. He did know how much Jerry hated using guns. He wasn’t above it, but it was the tool of cowards and the weak, something Jerry hated being. To think he’d pissed him off that bad? That was almost worse than being hung by his feet over a pit of death. Jerry held grudges for a long time.

What a horrible day. Especially with what he was going to have to say next.

“...I gave it to her.”

“You what? You gave it to her? But why?”

If Angry Murry had looked volcanic at the start, then confused, he now looked straight up baffled.

“You don’t make the sorta money to pay this back. Not this quick, not as no shopkeeper's assistant. If ya wanted to trade your life for money, we woulda just offered you a job ya couldn’t refuse. If ya really wanted to die, we might’ve given ya something and at least made it painless. Why on Vega-”

Angry Murry froze and then looked straight at him with a deadpan face. His voice raced out, practically building up in a buried storm of rage at stupidity.

“Freddy Holsteric Jones. You did not get and give a 100,000 Zeni to fuel your dang human fetish did ya?”

“It’s not like that! Come on Murry! She was just so beautiful and those legs! You don’t know how great those legs were! Their skin is so soft Murry! And she needed my help and did I tell you how beautiful she was? Humans always feel so nice and soft, especially when-”

Freddy suddenly found himself flying through the air like a rag doll as he crashed into the side of a dumpster.

“Freddy, you damn idiot! I thought ya had more brains than this?! God, ain’t no whoring man but a man in shallow love ain’t no better, ya hear me?! And where, pray tell, is this wonderful and ‘soft’ human woman now huh? And what did she need 100,000 Zeni for?”

Freddy gulped as he slowly righted himself, feeling his thread practically tremble the way Murry was looking at him.

“Well… her family was sick… and…… she….. Needed some medicine and a deposit…. She was only going to…….borrow……it.”

Murry didn’t even look at Freddy, simply stared up at the night sky in apparent anguish. A groan came out, like all the suffering in the world was upon him.

“Alright. Well, if you still believe that, you’re dumber than ya look. Either way, you want us to kill ya now or later? It’ll hurt a little but it’ll be worse if ya go past the deadline. Boss will have to make more an example of you.”

“Wait wait wait wait! Look, I know, I know I was wrong! I- I see that now! But I’ll have the money by the deadline! I will!”

“How Freddy? You gonna rob a bank? Hey, it’s your life and your choice. It was nice knowing ya either way, but, well, this ain’t friendlies, this is business, and you’re bad for the business. But hey, alright, give it your best shot. See ya again in two weeks. Oh, and Freddy?”

Murry had turned to walk away but looked back and those gem eyes sparked with some of that real anger. The kind Freddy had seen pull a porcelain doll off him and beat them to death with his own severed arm.

“Don’t skip town. Please. I don’t wanna have to hurt ya like that.”

Freddy grimaced as Murry walked away. He shook, fear racing up his spine, until he got it under control.

Now.

How on Vega was he going to get that money?!
