Leveling his Cards using 41 Soul Essences might be considered foolish by most but strengthening Root Strike increased his combat prowess drastically. His Foundation was not further reinforced, yet his combat prowess changed entirely. Killing stronger enemies using Root Strike at Level 5 would be much easier, resulting in more loot given the disparity in their Foundation.

Even hunting groups of monsters with similar Foundation would be much easier, resulting in more drops than before.

But that was not the only reason Zayn decided to integrate so many Soul Essences into Root Strike. Demonic Treant’s Set effect combined the Card Level of all linked Cards. By increasing Root Strike’s Level to 5, Zayn increased the power of Rapid Plant Growth, and Iron Bark as well. The Cards were still at Level 1, but their effects rivaled their state at Level 7.

The reason Zayn chose to increase Root Strike’s Level instead of Iron Bark and Rapid Plant Growth was also quite simple. Leveling up Cards was rather easy. All one had to do was use a Card repetitively to increase one’s proficiency. However, there were a few rules at play – rules that were written down in the Intermediate Guide for Forgotten Lands’ Players. Two particular rules were quite simple, but they affected a Player’s leveling strategy drastically.

1) The higher a Card’s Level and Rarity the higher the difficult to increase one’s proficiency.

Blink had to be used more often than Scent of Treasure to reach the next Level. Simultaneously, Root Strike had to be used far more often than Rapid Plant Growth to receive a level-up.

2) Using a Card for its designated task elevates the increase in proficiency. The harder the task the higher the increase in proficiency.

Zayn could use Blink dozens of times without gaining a Level Up. However, by doing the same mid-battle, Zayn was likely to gain twice, or thrice as much proficiency. The same applied to Rapid Plant Growth. It was possible to use Rapid Plant Growth without a target. Repeating the same task would grant some proficiency but it couldn’t be compared to the increase in proficiency provided by accelerating the growth of Moonlight Lover.

Since Zayn was in the Arylin Forest and searching for herbs and other plants to nourish and harvest, it was much easier to level Rapid Plant Growth. Therefore, he discarded the thought of artificially leveling the Card. It was much easier and cheaper to do it naturally. The same applied to Iron Bark. It was just a little bit different with this Card. Iron Bark possessed a passive effect. Even though it would take a while for Iron Bark to level passively, it was only a matter of time. Furthermore, Iron Bark’s effects were at the same level as the other Cards of the Set. Artificially upgrading it seemed like a waste. It would be much better to leave Iron Bark alone and wait for the Card to level up naturally.

One way or another, the potency of Zayn’s Card Set reached new heights after upgrading Root Strike. However, Zayn was not yet satisfied. He had to keep leveling his Cards to be fully prepared for the next step in his plan.
