One of the biggest hurdles that higher-realm beings face is the issue of consciousness and identity. This can manifest in any number of ways, but the easiest and most visible is when one forms their Soul.

While it is you, and is a part of you, this does not stop a Soul from being, effectively, a new branch of yourself. It does not think with the brain you have, but experiences varying degrees of sentience in its own existence. That sentience is your sentience, but what that means can be wildly discordant from one cultivator to the next. In the Divergent Paths, there are as many ways of existing with a Soul as there are of making one, and even the simplest divide between “body” and “spirit” cultivators is a vast one. Some choose to confront the existence of a Soul by fully identifying themselves as singular, consuming the nascent new organ to transform the existing self into something new. Others treat the Soul as something altogether more separate, acting as an individual mind that can make decisions for and with the “host”, or “original” form. Still more treat the nascent consciousness as an extension, an addition to a new whole, and sometimes, the Soul is formed in such a way that its “mind”, if it can be said to have one in the first place, simply does not overlap with what most consider consciousness or any sense of self.

But to change the self is, inherently, to change the mind.

If one has the ability to alter memories, how much of the self is tied to the idea of memory? If one has a mind that can think faster, in more directions, and in stranger ways than another, how does the idea of consciousness overlap, and where does it not?

All of this doesn’t even get into the idea of clones or multiple bodies, techniques which are very common above a certain rank. Some establish hierarchies, some create “lesser” versions of themselves, some even fight for dominance directly once the job is done. I once knew a cultivator who was capable of creating a seemingly indefinite number of duplicates, dividing their mental capacity and powers with each new body created (which they used primarily as a tool to control an overpowering and harmful level of cognition), who, upon reconstitution, simply did not know or care which version of themselves survived and which was permanently erased, so long as information was preserved. I envy that level of suicidal ideation and self assurance. I pity the concern on the face of his wife when I inadvertently described the process to her.

-Restricted treatise on higher challenges of consciousness by Researcher Wei Xiong, pre-sanction for treason, unsanctioned demonic cultivation, and endangering the welfare of the state.
