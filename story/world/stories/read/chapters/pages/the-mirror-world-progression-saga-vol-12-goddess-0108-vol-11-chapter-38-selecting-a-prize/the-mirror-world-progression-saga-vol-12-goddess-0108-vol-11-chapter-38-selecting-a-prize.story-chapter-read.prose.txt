“This is a magical sword of great power,” the very cultured curator related in his nasally voice, gesturing with a smooth, manicured hand at the display. “With additional bonuses against supernatural foes. My Lord Alegon Glaelychus, himself, used just this weapon in his youth, making it glorious indeed.”

Samantha suppressed a yawn — that someone who didn’t need sleep did so said something — and nodded along, as she barely glanced at the sword. Naturally, she couldn’t even identify it, like every other item in the joint, because Glae’s museum-like vault of ‘artifacts’ had them all in crystalline cases of numerous protections, including against information. One had to either rely on the curator’s information given or ask to open a case, which she’d done once… and had to wait minutes of her time for it to be unlocked!

Never again! I can’t wait an eternity just to pick some stupid toy.

Naturally, she was there to fulfill Glae’s bargain of getting an artifact of supposedly equal value, but in each case (literally), it wasn’t something she personally had much use for. Perhaps not surprising, as Glae wasn’t big on empowering wizards.

She was certain he had a stash of other, likely better shit somewhere, and this was just him being petty and insincere about the deal, if technically honoring it. The same went for having a curator show her the goods rather than handling them personally, as she should have been due.

Whatever he says or whatever front he puts on, he is jealous of my popularity. I already seem to be getting credit for the ritual, even when I correct people who were there! I swear, if this holds out, he’ll be a bit justified in hating me.

She’d mostly noted artifacts of minor help to others in her order, rather than personally of benefit.

Samantha listened to the curator drone on about more pointless items.

His somehow even more nasally voice continued, “This lovely gem piece is a versatile, bountiful energy source only recently replaced in one of the master’s devices. Though it can be tricky to tap into, as you can see, it has a custom housing suitable for integration…”

Samantha stopped hearing as she yawned.

He made me do it. Actually, that felt really good, though.

Just as she was going to cut him short and decide on an enchanted forge hammer a dozen items back, similar to what Glae used in the ritual, her eyes caught on to what the curator was describing. She did a double take, as something within her uncannily recognized it.

Samantha pointed, brows knitting together. “Hey! That’s mine! What the hell is he trying to pull here?!”

The curator’s blink was like molasses. In offended confusion, he touched a hand to his chest and said, “I beg your pardon, Madam? The master has possessed this item for as long as I’ve been here. I assure you, on my honor, this is his, though in his wondrous grace, you may select it if you desire.”

Samantha held her finger out, turning it to him, her mouth open. She stopped herself, making her cheeks blow out. “Puh. Uh, sorry. My mistake.” She dropped her hand and cleared her throat. “Please go ahead and open the case. I do have an interest in this item.” In fact, she had to stifle her excitement at what she believed it was.

The curator collected himself and nodded, turning to the crystalline case and pulling out a wand from his belt. “Just a minute… I do remember this lock’s sequence. Ahhh… yes, here we are…”

Samantha took a deep, calming breath, trying not to tap her foot as she waited impatiently. She once again cast her mind back to her realm, to make use of precious, precious time being squandered.

Glae, though he’d made himself personally unavailable, had delivered some helpful information, basically a summary of what he believed he’d ‘knocked out’ with his ultimate Antimagic World Spell. The Sages had some exclusive buff deeply embedded in their inner circle, which made their top people generically more effective on multiple fronts, and also slowed aging. This was quickly corroborated by some deaths of the very old in the Dominion, though information suppression made the extent unknown. Particularly in the Capital — the confirmed deaths were outside it, where things didn’t get buried as easily.

Glae also noted that he strongly believed some rules of the inner workings and effectiveness of Entropy magic had been removed, stealing away some of its renowned potency on Calrenazzod. Investigations into exactly what the World Spell was were inconclusive, so it was guesswork for the most part. With luck, it would improve their chances, in any case.

“Success!” the curator declared, pulling out a pyramid made out of numerous bronze panels, with the center including a large blue gemstone inset into it.

As soon as Samantha’s naked eyes fell on the gem, she grabbed the object out of the curator’s hand and said, “I’ll take it! This is my choice! Yes!”

The curator blinked. “Ah. Very well, I’ll just-” He suddenly made a strangled sound, cutting off, his mouth dropping at what Samantha was doing.

Making gleeful noises, Samantha ripped apart the pyramid shell to take the gemstone out. Dropping the carcass of the bronze pieces on the floor, she patted the curator on the arm, then flew away with her prize, calling out, “Thanks so much, I’ll see myself out! Nyahahaha!”

The curator looked down in horror at the debris on the floor, letting out one small, squeaky sound of dismay, as gremlin-like laughter echoed through the hall.

At the top of the tower of Itha Ranon, Samantha held the new glittering gem in one hand and her as yet incomplete D.I.V.A. crown in the other, smiling in sheer joy. One mere inset piece in the almost perfect crown remained! On the back, there was a crisscrossing crack, with the empty socket in the middle.

“Here we go, baby!” Allowing the crown to float in front of her, she placed the stone in its socket.

The crown flashed and remorphed, its luster and shape improving, its gold and jewels becoming shinier, all in all becoming complete, without tarnish. Perfect.

So shiny!

The quest “Restoration of the Crown” is complete. 20 FE added. 7(5) exp gained in [Goddess], 10(5) exp gained in [Magineer]. Artifact enchantment “Mana Portals” added to the artifact, D.I.V.A.

Yes, yes, yes!

Samantha excitedly checked the details, including refreshing her memory on the rest. Though the tower had been used for many, many rituals since its rise, and the crown used to enhance them in some cases, it had been a while since she checked the newest stat spread and developments in the ‘notes,’ which had various ‘problems’ when she’d first obtained the crown.
