When they arrived at the inn, Edge stepped into the shower while his friends worked their magic in the kitchen, and Lanna got the party started in the inn’s common area, along with everyone who wasn’t a core member of the crew.

He listened to the music blaring from below as the hot water washed away all the mud, ash, and blood matted in his hair, along with varieties of grime he couldn’t even begin to identify. When he was clean, he dried himself with Repel Water, and Mel took her turn, stopping long enough to smack his ass along the way.

A throaty chuckle escaped his lips as he stepped into his bedroom and retrieved some comfortable clothing from his vault. He was looking forward to joining the party, but before he did anything else, it was time to take a tour of his new-and-improved inner world.

He cast his consciousness into his core, grinning when he saw how much it had grown to accommodate the upgraded components. His central chamber still took the form of his father’s workshop, but the ceiling was higher, and there were several new alcoves that prevented the recent additions from cluttering the place and kept the overall vibe true to his childhood memories.

While his avatars followed in his wake, Edge took a lap around his core, starting with the devices that made his magic possible. Not every improvement was listed in his profile, a detail that was emphasized when he stopped in front of his reservoir. The tank was twice as big as before he’d evolved, and the machinery compressing the magicytes it contained was considerably more complex.

The brass and other metals had been replaced with celestial dolomite and gobium, granting the surfaces a lustrous sheen. He judged that its capacity had tripled, which explained why he’d been able to use his powers so freely during the Duos’ Clash. Combined with the efficiency bonus from Skill Bond, he could pull off quite a few combos without running out of fuel, even with more Rare and Epic powers added to the mix.

Moving on, the array of conduits, pipes, and ducts leading from his reservoir to his reactor had grown significantly more substantial. The tubes that permeated the wall of his central chamber were wider and more numerous, linking his reservoir to his mana pores and Disruption diffusor.

Edge had the sense that the energy transfer between the various aspects of his core was smoother than ever, providing a modest mana-efficiency upgrade on top of everything else. Filled with a fierce and rising excitement, he followed the conduits into the alcove housing his reactor.

When he’d evolved to stage two, his reactor had swelled to the size of an industrial furnace. Now, it was bigger than the common room of Trapper’s lodge. The device was still formed from jet-black metal, with a bulbous grill leading to a fathomless realm of roiling flame. In addition to the various magitech components, glittering jewels and inscrutable runes graced its surface. He had no idea what they did, but he had the impression that Absorbing skill experience from the powers he Extracted would yield a better rate of return.

The heat had grown so intense that he would have burst into flames if he were standing here in the flesh, even with Aegis of Nullification. However, there was a new device embedded in the wall that prevented the heat from leaving the alcove, which meant he didn’t have to worry about his inner world becoming a sauna.

Next, he walked over to the nook containing his skill slots.

The marble pillars had multiplied to match his core’s upgraded capacity, but that was only the beginning of the changes. Instead of two rows of nine, the columns were arranged in three concentric circles. The white marble surfaces had been smooth before, elegant in their simplicity. Now, they were covered with lifelike engravings that depicted the power slotted into each pillar’s socket, in a style that reminded him of the images etched into the walls of Skill-Eater’s temple.

The elaborate circuitry embedded into the sockets comprising the top of each pillar was denser and more intricate than before. He had a hunch that his skills would be a little stronger as a result—another bonus from evolving that wasn’t included in his updates. He admired the changes while Sea flew over to perch on his shoulder, then continued with his tour.

He walked past the wall where his Spatial Storage Rune was housed, which took the form of a spacious bank vault with a heavy metal door. The neighboring surfaces were blank, and he thought they represented his expanded implant points, although only core runes and implants that were bonded to Skill-Eater were embodied in his inner world, like his Auxiliary Skill Slot.

When Edge willed it, a secret door in the brickwork swung open, which he’d discovered when Earl taught him how to unleash Disruption surges. Outside was a realm of seething mist, symbolizing the innate antimagic barrier flowing across the boundary of his body. He followed the current in the ocean of flowing fog to its source, the magitech device known as a Disruption diffusor.

Like the other components, the machine was bigger and more complex, releasing vapor from two additional vents that hadn’t been there before. As he’d noticed earlier, the conduits leading through the floor and into his reservoir had widened considerably, which meant he could draw more magicytes through them to create a stronger surge.

Whistling in appreciation, Edge made his way back to his central chamber. The last two stops on his list were the black door leading to Skill-Eater’s domain and the spacious area encompassing his skill village. He was hoping to have a nice chat with the ravenous lord, so he decided to visit the latter first.

“Holy shit.” His eyes went wide when he realized it wasn’t a village anymore. He came to a stop at the boundary of his central chamber, running his gaze across a swath of real estate the size of a city block. He’d acquired a skill garden at stage one, which had grown into a bustling skill village at stage two, so he had expected the area to expand again. But this exceeded his expectations by an order of magnitude. What he was looking at after evolving to stage three could only be described as a skill kingdom.

Each of his powers now commanded a considerable territory, complete with diverse places to rest, various forms of recreational and training activities, and artifacts that were so strange he couldn’t even begin to divine their purpose. He spotted everything from houses to towers to caves opening from the sides of miniature mountains.

A fair chunk of the kingdom was empty right now, since he had a lot of empty slots to fill, and some of the structures were under construction, but even so, it was an incredible sight. There are rumors that skill villages improve at higher stages, but I’ve never heard of anything like this, even in speculation and legends.

Edge planned to explore his kingdom at length, but he was eager to join his friends and decided to check out a few regions at random before heading back to his body. Chibime’s home had turned into a perfect replica of Trapper’s lodge, including all the upgrades he had purchased with contribution points before leaving for World’s End. He smiled as he took in the true-to-life detail, wondering how the rest of the crew was holding up.

Shadow Play’s home was a dark cave, and the area in front was interesting. It resembled an obstacle course with countless features to scale, traverse, or weave your way through. Beams of sunlight and deep shadows checkered the complex terrain, which made him realize this setup was intended for stealth training on top of mobility drills.

His final stop for today was the domain of his newest skill. Tri-State Sentinel had a circular plot that was divided like a pie chart. There were three cottages in the middle, with doors opening to a central courtyard shared by each aspect. He would explore the details a little later, but he had the sense the arrangement symbolized both the unity of purpose and unique facets forming the triple avatar’s identity.

There were roads dividing each territory, and the center of his kingdom held communal areas where his powers could interact. He spotted several spacious parks—each featuring a different type of terrain—along with what looked like an ice-skating rink, swimming pool, and climbing wall.

The most interesting addition was a recessed pit leading to a fighting floor, which looked exactly like the Grand Arena of World’s End. Edge chuckled as he imagined his skills conducting their sparring sessions down there, idly wondering if they would split into three teams instead of two now that a new Epic avatar had joined his collection. These changes were incredible, and he looked forward to spending time in each avatar’s area while working on their skill bonds, but for now, he left his kingdom behind and made his way over to the final feature of today’s inspection.

The black door that led to Skill-Eater’s realm had grown to over twenty feet tall. Instead of smooth metal, the surface was adorned with intricate etchings depicting her history. It included scenes from the distant past, when Skill-Thief and her friends had explored the cosmos together. He ran his fingertips across panels portraying the lords’ arrival on Ord and the golden age that followed when they helped the natives make peace with the spirits of the land.

Next came her tragic battle with Boundless-Well, resulting in her transformation into the ravenous lord bound in endless black chains. The adjoining image showed events he remembered from his vision. The remaining eighteen lords had intervened during her fight with the kaiju World-Breaker, preventing the monster she’d become from devouring all life on the planet by turning Skill-Eater into a core and sealing it in a rune-bound reliquary.

The final scene featured Edge himself as he battled alongside Skill-Eater and the chains of oblivion, overcoming the dark passenger and dragging it kicking and screaming below the waves. There was room for more images on the far side of the door, which made him wonder what the future would hold.

Now that I’ve evolved, she should be awake more often. I wonder if she’s in the mood to talk. He learned the answer when he raised his fist to knock, only to have the portal swing open before his knuckles came down. It seemed it was time for a face-to-face chat with the ravenous lord and discover what changes had occurred beyond the boundary of his inner world.
