Yes. It was beautiful, but it was not complete. Ninety-seven was not ninety-nine no matter how much he wished it was.

Max sat on the hard packed earth of a grey and cool day. Autumn had started to swing through the land in this area, causing the trees to slowly edge the lines between vibrant greens to rusty red and gold. It didn’t help that the air carried a cold underlying threat that had the legionaries provide a longer and thicker version of the shirts to wear.

It also didn’t help that none of their workouts had changed schedule. If anything, they became more intense.

The Optio stood a bit further than usual after driving them into the ground multiple times with endless running and physical trainings. He was in a deep conversation with Senior Casius, their voices were distant and only fragments reached them that made little sense so separated. Something about a northern pass and supply routes.

Max had not tried to parse through it either. He had better things to do.

Don’t let go! It’s so close!

A wavering wisp lethargically bobbed its way closer to the pseudo core within him just like the ninety-seven before it. Though, his expertise and experience doing the same action for almost one hundred times did make the process easier. It wasn’t anywhere close to the race that Captain Hector caused, but it was marked improvement.

He smiled as it broke through the mana channels and into the massive void where the rest were currently revolving in a perfect orb. Max pushed it forward until it moved by itself, caught by the current of energy that was already moving in a constant motion.

One more! That’s all I need—

Max winced as a splitting headache cut him off.

He opened his eyes and looked down at himself, covered in sweat, like he had jumped into a river, and shivering from both the chilly air and the exertion that he kept forcing upon himself. There was no time for a break unless his body collapsed into a fit of sleep. Otherwise, the only thing he could think about was unlocking the Mana Manipulation Skill so he could finally use his Patron Inheritance properly.

I can do it…

Max struggled to stop his shaking hands.

Just one more. Endure and everything will fall into line—

“Max?”

He blinked, losing focus, as he turned around to look at Arnie sitting next to him. The boy sat a few feet away with his knees pulled to his chest and arms wrapped around them. His eyes were distant and his customary brightness had been dimming for some time now.

Caleb and Max had noticed it happen.

The large chubby cheeked smile was always there, but it looked… forced? Fake? They weren’t sure how to explain it other than it was not Arnie’s usual intensity. His eyes did the most telling though. The struggled to focus on them during conversations, constantly drifting away and never locking onto an object for long.

Caleb straightened from the other side of Arnie.

“Yeah? What’s up?” Max asked.

He met Caleb’s gaze without giving the far off looking Arnie an indication, not that he would have noticed. The two of them had made a promise to keep an eye out on their friend in case he did anything stupid to himself. They couldn’t really pry into his matter either. When he was ready, he would speak and any attempt to push him would only make Arnie retreat further back into isolation.

Said moment seemed like it was now.

“Does your status page have a family name too?” Arnie turned to look at him.

Max frowned deeply.

He pulled up his status and stared at the strange name that sat there untouched and undiscovered by anyone except his mother and himself for all he knew.

He nodded. “Mhm. Fome. I’ve… I don’t know what it means, to be honest. Have you heard of a Fome family before? I haven’t, that’s for sure.”

Arnie looked away from him, his gaze dropping to the floor as they went unfocused once more. His grip tightened around his knees, pulling himself closer and making himself smaller. Arnie looked… small. Diminished compared to his usual self even more now.

But it was enough.

Caleb and Max both understood what was happening as the realization hit them as well.

All three of them were orphans. Their suffering and pain was stitched from the same clothe and materials.

Arnie had family out there just like Caleb and Max did. A father, mother, cousins, maybe even siblings that he had never met. Real people with real faces, voices, hate, love, pet peeves, joys, and everything else someone their age should be experiencing. People who had the same exact family name as theirs and accepted them.

Somehow, during all the training and mana work, that thought had finally crept through the endless amounts of walls Arnie had put up since the first days he could think to protect himself from this aching pain he suffered now. Each one of them had those walls.

Max gulped because he knew what the thoughts Arnie was fighting against right now.

What if they are still alive and waiting for me to find them? What if I had grown up with them? Would someone have celebrated my Patron Inheritance unlocking? Would I be wearing fine clothes and eating real food? Would I be playing in the street now with kids my age without all the dangers and harsh lessons we had to learn early or suffer the consequences?

Would I be here, in this Cursed Legion, at all?

Max would be lying to say he hadn’t had the same thoughts. Only the constant need to improve, move wisps, use his Patron Inheritance, make his Skill Shard better, and the constant gain of attributes had kept him from falling into this same pit. He wasn’t sure what he would have done otherwise.

It was either lose himself in the grind, or lose himself in sorrow.

Max grabbed Arnie’s shoulder and shook him hard. “Arnie, look at me! I said—”

“I’m looking! I’m looking! Stop shaking me so damn hard! I said—”

“I consider you a brother! We are family and that is enough for me!” Max stopped shaking him and stared hard.

Arnie frowned deeply as he stared back. “Yeah… Thank you, Max, but you know its not the same. It can’t be the same.”

“I know, but its all we have. Don’t forget our promise. I definitely won’t let you. I refuse, no. We refuse to let others decide our fate for us. If I end up dying, then let it be on my terms and my path. Not someone else's. If I have to be stuck here in a Cursed Legion, then I’ll be damned if I didn’t take advantage of it fully to become the greatest person I can become. There are enough people out there that want to see us fail even though we’ve never met them before to allow ourselves to be down!”

Arnie opened his mouth to speak, but could not utter a word.

For the first time in a long while, his eyes cleared and he focused directly on Max’s face. That was enough. Yes, the edges of the wondering and thinking would still be there, but it was weakened by brotherhood and their friendship. And it would be kept down for as long as they were together.

Arnie straightened and unlatched his death grip on his other hand. “Yeah! You’re right, Max! W-We’re legionaries now. Tough, mean, strong, and stoic legionaries! The most powerful ones too!”

Max laughed.

“Only thing stopping us is our own commitment and efforts!” Caleb joined in.

All three of them join in the laughter.

“Back to grinding, then.” Max turned away and refocused on his wisp gathering.

The other two boys did the same.

Max closed his eyes and latched onto the final one in the air around him, it was sitting beside him, waiting for this very moment. He guided it into his palm and lead it through the mana channels in his body all the way through until he finally found the void once more.

It drifted into it spot and clicked into place.

The whole pseudo-core trembled as the final piece locked in. it flashed a few times in a brighter light, before settling into the brightest version of itself. That was all that happened and nothing else.

Max wasn’t going to lie to himself, he was expecting more. Maybe all his mana channels becoming illuminated? A flash of light escaping his eyes and body? A new found power coursing through his entire body? Maybe even a single pulse and vibration.

All he got was a couple of flashes and then it settled like nothing had happened before this.

He began to wonder if he had missed something—

Long nights of sleeplessness, grinding constantly, struggling day in and out, eating like a madman, training and then filling resting periods with mana work, and so much more… it finally came to a hedge and he received his reward.

He blinked as the system notification faded away.

The clearing still looked the same. The gloomy sky did not change, the cool wind did not suddenly become warmer, the packed earth did not become softer, and Arnie and Caleb were still the same exact friends he had just moments ago without anything making them different.

In the distance, Optio Ignatius stopped speaking mid sentence and turned to look at him.

Senior Casius joined him with a massive smile. He reached into his robes and pulled out a small journal and opened it. He began his finger over it as though it was a pen, mana leaking from it and filling the page. Max blinked in wonder at what was happening. He couldn’t imagine how many wisps each drop of ink like mana would require.

He shook his head and looked away before his expression could betray anything.

Instead, he opened his Ledger and fought the smile that threatened to bloom on his face. Soon enough, he would finally take his first steps into bettering his recovery Skill Shard and he would gain more stats in an loop of growth that he would ride to the very peak.
