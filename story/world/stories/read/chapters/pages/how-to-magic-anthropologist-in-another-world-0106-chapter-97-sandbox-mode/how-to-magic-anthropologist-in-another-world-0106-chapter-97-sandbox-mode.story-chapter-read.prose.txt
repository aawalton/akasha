If I could figure out how to do that, it would make the sanctuary considerably more useful.

I wish I could tell you I fell asleep immediately.

While my limit of three slots has already been reached, I should be able to free up a slot once Ko’i goes about making me a custom Soulbook.

What should I ask for?

Should I just pick Flight?

Or maybe something like Second Wind so I can train my kindred side even more.

Success.

Participating in the sanctification ritual and watching the Soulit village youths consume souls were both new experiences. To be fair, every time I came here, it was with Nina, and we always had things we planned to do.

No, this wouldn't do.

Well, this was a simulation of star mana. I couldn't actually teleport my real body from here.

"I wish I could teleport a kilometer both north and south at the same time."

Hmm. This was interesting.

Nothing happened.

I didn't even lose any mana.

My wish wasn't about all of outer space. It was about teleporting directly above me.

Could I actually go to outer space?

Yep, definitely dreaming.

"I wish I was teleported four hundred kilometers upward."

Right. Air expands in a vacuum.

Next time, I should exhale completely before exposing myself to the vacuum.

My Freelancer dorm room.

Sara's Soulbook shop.

The cobblestone, the empty fixed stalls, the familiar buildings, and the road stretching ahead.

This couldn't be a coincidence.
