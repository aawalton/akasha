Before he could escape from our grasp, Xie Ling's spiritual pressure exploded outward.

“What Freezes Cannot Escape.”

Around Xu Kong’s fading form, ice formations coiled around him, but the technique did more than physically lock him in place, it was targeting the concept of space there itself. As his body flickered, for a second, I thought the technique was about to fail because he was already more absent than present.

But Xie Ling poured more spiritual energy into her Law, causing the frozen construct to wrap tighter around the spaces where Xu Kong was still partially manifest. This forced his form to solidify by maybe five percent; however, it clearly wasn’t going to be enough to keep him there.

"Now," Xie Ling said through gritted teeth.

Not needing to be told twice, Li Na activated her ability, targeting the parts of him that were still solid.

“Arghhhh,” Xu Kong cried out as massive internal damage manifested throughout his body.

The injuries forced the rest of his translucent form to solidify as his body instinctively tried to heal the damage, but that only allowed Li Na’s sacrificial technique to end him, leaving behind nothing but scattered qi when it was done.

I didn’t even get the chance to release a sigh of relief at seeing the number change from 7 to 6 when the Nine Deaths Transfer hit and I experienced organ failure across my entire body simultaneously. Kidneys, liver, heart, lungs, everything shutting down at once.

The darkness came faster this time, but the resurrection also felt slightly easier because I found that I wasn’t as disorientated.

"I'm learning something," I said to Azure internally while the others recovered from the battle. "Each dao we encounter shows me a different fundamental principle. Zhong Ji taught me that acceptance of endings is part of the natural cycle. Zhou Xuan demonstrated how patterns repeat across all scales of existence. Xu Kong showed that absence can be just as powerful as presence."

"These are basic Taoist concepts," Azure replied. "The Tao Te Ching discusses similar ideas. Wu wei, the action of non-action. The usefulness of emptiness. The eternal return of all things."

"But understanding them intellectually is different from experiencing them through combat," I said. "These manifestations are forcing me to confront these principles as living reality. I’m not just studying abstract philosophy."

We didn’t get to finish our conversation because step ninety-five materialized its defender.

“My name is Shi Zai,” the middle-aged-looking bulky man announced as everything about him radiated permanence and physicality. "I comprehended the Dao of Reality by understanding that dreams and illusions are less real than physical existence. What you can touch, what you can break, what persists despite all attempts to change it. That…that is what truly matters."

With a stomp of his foot, the black stone beneath us became more solid as though the dream-like quality of the palace faded in his presence and everything felt heavier and more concrete.

"In my domain, dreams cannot override reality," Shi Zai continued. "Your techniques will work only if they produce genuine physical effects. Your spiritual manipulations are useless before me."

As if to demonstrate this, when Li Na’s golden threads wrapped around his legs, he broke them easily by simply stepping forward because spiritual energy couldn't hold him due to not being real enough in his perception.

To make matters worse, even when Wu Kangming's sword struck Shi Zai's shoulder, it bounced off. It seemed whatever the manifestation’s perception of enhanced reality was, a blade couldn't cut through it. It was likely because it was a creation from Tian’s dream technique if I wasn’t mistaken.

Instead of using spiritual techniques, I decided to transform my right arm with red sun energy and attack physically. It seemed to be the right course of action because when the thorned vines wrapped around Shi Zai's torso, they began devouring his cultivation base immediately.

"Better," Shi Zai smiled a brutish smile as his spiritual pressure surged, tearing the vines apart with pure strength. "You're using actual matter enhanced with energy rather than pure energy constructs. That can affect reality."

A frown appeared on my face as the transformation cancelled from the backlash.

“Do not be dishear—” he didn’t get the chance to finish because Wu Kangming appeared before him again, but this time there was that that strange distortion around the blade again, it was the same dao-infused edge he'd used against Yuan Shi.

When it struck Shi Zai's chest, this time the blade cut through the enhanced reality like the concept itself was irrelevant. After all, the dao strike transcended the physical versus spiritual divide that had been created. Even though it wasn’t enough to kill him, the strike caused him to stumble backward, weakening the domain he had created.

"Now," Wu Kangming called out.

Acting immediately, Li Na activated her sacrificial ability while that brief window of vulnerability was still present, causing Shi Zai's body to erupt with internal damage. The brute of a man collapsed before he could restore his reality enhancement.

The number changed from 6 to 5.

Wu Kangming took the transferred injuries this time. He died quickly from massive brain haemorrhaging and resurrected within seconds. Like me, he was adapting to the process faster than I expected, and I couldn’t help but wonder if he was gaining any inspiration from repeated deaths. Then again, with his protagonist status, that was basically a given.

The next step, step ninety-six brought Meng Xian, the one known as the Dream Immortal.

From her appearance, it was obvious why she was named that. She was the embodiment of spiritual energy, her form constantly flickered between solid and ethereal, and her features were beautiful but indistinct, like trying to remember someone from a half-forgotten dream.

"I achieved Half-Step Dreamgod," Meng Xian said softly. "I could have taken the final step into true divinity, but I chose not to because I feared losing my humanity in the ascension process."

That came as a surprise. Most cultivators were more than happy to sacrifice their human qualities in order to climb the mountain of immortality. And my surprise only grew when she demonstrated what she'd refused to become.

For just a moment her spiritual pressure expanded to touch something beyond Lucid Lawbearer, beyond even what I understood as possible within normal cultivation realms.

"This is what I could have been," Meng Xian said, her aura making my soul tremble in awe. "This is what I rejected. Was I wise or cowardly? I've had fifty thousand years to contemplate that question. I have yet to find an ans—."

Fortunately for us, Li Na's technique killed her before she could show us more of that terrifying power, but it was obvious to everyone that the Nine Deaths Transfer had been closer to failing than the previous uses.

It was my turn to take the injuries this time. My cultivation base stopped existing for several seconds because death came as complete spiritual foundation collapse, which was a terrifying experience, but then like before, the Dream World's resurrection restored it.

The next step was step ninety-seven.

This time we faced a man named Chen Jue, and his appearance was shocking even by the palace's standards. Every injury he'd ever received was visible on his body. Scars covered his skin completely. His right arm was a spiritual construct replacing one he'd lost. Burns, cuts, stab wounds, everything layered over each other until his flesh looked like a battlefield.

"I refused to accept limitations," Chen Jue roared. "They all told me that I had no talent. I proved them wrong by becoming a Lucid Lawbearer anyway. They claimed that I couldn't defeat stronger opponents, but I did it anyway. They ridiculed by declaring that my wounds were fatal and that I would die a dog’s death, but I survived anyway. The only thing that killed me was choosing to stop fighting because there was no one else to defeat."

When his Law activated, reality shifted to respond to him. "I Will Not" meant he simply refused outcomes he didn't accept. Death, defeat, limitation, all of it could be negated through pure determination.

Li Na's technique hit him and he should have died as his body erupted with fatal damage, but he refused to dissolve, so his spiritual pressure just stayed together.

"No," Chen Jue said despite bleeding from his mouth. "I decide when I die. Not you."

That may have been true had he been facing only her, but like before, Xie Ling activated her own Law. As reality was torn between the two laws, the effects of Li Na’s technique became more prominent, the stubborn cultivator lasted another full minute before finally collapsing.

"Never surrender. Never accept defeat. Keep fighting until you choose to stop."

Those were the last words he uttered.

This time Wu Kangming took the transferred injuries, and when he came back, he had a contemplative expression on his face. "That one taught me something valuable," he surprisingly said when he saw our questioning looks. "Determination can transcend cultivation advantages if the will is strong enough."

The next step was step ninety-eight, and it brought the final pair before we would face Shen Mohai himself. Yan Sheng and Yin Huang, husband and wife, they’d achieved Lucid Lawbearer together and died together. How romantic…

Yan Sheng was crowned in flames, radiating heat and light. Yin Huang was crowned in shadows, absorbing illumination around her. They moved in perfect synchronization, each attack from one complemented by defense from the other.

"We are one," they said together. "Two bodies, one dao. Where light falls, shadow forms. Where shadow recedes, light emerges."

Their combined Law activated and the step transformed into a battlefield where light and dark contested for dominance. Fire and shadow spiraled together in patterns that were beautiful and deadly simultaneously.

Li Na activated her ability but targeting one wouldn't work, so instead, she aimed for the bond between them. Yan Sheng and Yin Huang collapsed at the same time with their hands reaching for each other as they fell.

"Together," they whispered with their final breath as their fingers touched. "Always together."

The number changed from 2 to 1.

Li Na turned toward me and Wu Kangming. The injuries she'd inflicted on herself to kill both manifestations were catastrophic, much worse than when she killed the three calamities.

"Both of you," she forced out, coughing up blood. "I need both of you to take the damage.”

We nodded as the Nine Deaths Transfer activated and split between us. My ribs cracked and my left lung punctured. Wu Kangming took damage to his legs and abdomen. We both collapsed simultaneously. Just like before, the darkness came, then the light of resurrection, and finally we stood up, both restored to perfect health.

"One more step," Li Na whispered, looking absolutely exhausted despite being physically whole. "There is only Shen Mohai left."

The number 1 floating in the sky confirmed that she wasn’t wrong. It was finally time to face the Dreamgod Ascendant who'd created this entire palace fifty thousand years ago.
