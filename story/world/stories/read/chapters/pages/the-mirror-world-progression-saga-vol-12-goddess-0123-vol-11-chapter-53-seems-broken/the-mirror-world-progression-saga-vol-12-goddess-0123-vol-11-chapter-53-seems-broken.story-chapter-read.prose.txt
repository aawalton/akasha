The spam in her head when Samantha returned to Calrenazzod was insane, and the ensuing sense of great transformation was overwhelming, as so much new potential surged through her like white-hot energy.

Before she dealt with any of it, she checked on the realm and, fortunately, found it not just as she left it, but advanced along with sterling success. The march of the war, expansions on a dozen fronts, the developments in the tower, the management of Heaven — all of it was hunky dory despite four-ish days passing.

She felt nothing but pride for all of them. And most of all, for Azure, who’d done well with the crown on her head, apparently. She’d done something Samantha was bad at: expend stockpiles of wealth and resources. If it got more things moving, she was all for it. She approved of all the things suddenly taken out of her hands, now. She practically rejoiced!

Still quite overstimulated and gaining her bearings, Samantha had immediate demands: lover, couch, head on lap.

And so she found herself in their private rooms, not in Itha Ranon, but on Heavenpeak, with Samantha laying her head on Azure’s lap as she stretched out on the couch, her bare feet crossed over the arm. Azure played with her hair, and Floofa had found a seat in her lap to get scritches and pets. For a long, perfect span of time, nothing was said.

Peace was enjoyed.

Finally, Samantha felt the pressures bearing down on her and sighed. “I’ve got to get back, yeah?”

“Mmngh.” Azure grunted something extremely not an answer.

“Well, time for the fun part, first.”

“Sex?”

Samantha laughed. “No. Review. That’ll be for later.”

“Bah! I might keep your crown hostage until the goods are delivered.”

“Ohhh! Pfft. Be my guest. I could get used to not having that!” It was only partially true — she had use of its powers beyond Itha Ranon, after all — but the meaning held in spirit.

Azure groaned. “It’s no fun if you don't care about it…”

Samantha reached a hand to pat her wife in mock consolation. “There, there…” Meanwhile, she began delving into the changes she’d been dealt from the recent challenges.

First, she checked the final tally on all the classes.

Goddess, level 13 [0/-]; Final Deity

Divine Thunderer: 8 [2/90]

Saboteur: 5 [56/60]

Magineer (Abjurer/Conjurer+5): 16 [28/170]

Bard: 4 [27/50]

She giggled about ‘Final Deity,’ and when she shared it with Azure, they both giggled.

“A shame about the levels freezing, though,” Azure commented.

Samantha shrugged. “I’d have been lucky to get to another level at this point, and I can already sense a massive boost in tradeoff. Aside from that, experience isn’t wasted, but diverted, and the multiplier eventually pumping into the others might be better if there were an actual long-term reality to speak of. Anyway! Let’s see about [Divine Thunderer]...”

It seemed a very offensive class that leaned on most defense coming from ‘innates’ or another class — which made a lot of sense for the focal class of a giantess hero. In addition to simply replacing [Brute] for fighting purposes, it had three primary battery skills and abilities it operated off of: [Charge!], [Flurry], and [Righteous Indignation]. They were obtained as levels came, and then mostly cycled upgrades to them on level-ups.

Charge! (Expert)

You move up to a 1-second period of additional movement equivalent to your full-speed sprint over that period, and then perform a devastating singular melee strike. It has no accuracy benefits, but adds your full [Divine Thunderer] levels to existing totals for Lethality Grade purposes. This adds as a raw value, not as a modifier.

This attack dismisses wound negations and severity-reducing abilities before assessing wounding, reducing Lethality Grade by 2 levels per wound negation, or by 4 levels for a severity-reducing ability. In the latter case, only do so if the wound would be more severe. This will only ‘drain’ a severity-reducing ability if it is a charge, daily function, or the like.

This skill has a 20-second cooldown between uses.

Flurry (Expert)

So long as you dedicate your full, normal action period of 5 seconds to only melee attacks and movement, add +1 Effective Levels to all attacks, and add in an additional full-strength strike.

Righteous Indignation (Expert)

As a battle goes on and your foes are still around, your mind, body, and soul grow indignant of their audacity to survive you.

After 30 seconds of battle with a foe you’ve attempted to kill or subdue at least once, add +1 Effective Levels to all active actions you take, as your blood, fiber, and soul are inflamed. After two minutes, add an additional +1. If a foe survives a wound dealt by [Charge!] or another wounding-enhancing cooldown ability, add an additional +1. If you take a wound, add an additional +1.

Any ‘triggering’ foe slain or subdued by you will reset relevant bonuses. Taking a wound will only reset if also healed (it will not reset from healing alone).

Samantha whistled at the high bonus and benefits of [Charge!], in particular.

Azure was also perusing the details. “Seems great to me. Some similarities to [Berserker], but way more enduring. It basically crams in all of the best offensive ability concepts. A strong counter to [Knight], though they might still be able to take one charge, if they’re defensive-focused.”

“Not my charge, though,” Samantha said confidently, “not with all the stacking bonuses. At this point, I could blitz down anything. The pre-stripping of wound negations and wound-mitigating abilities — which is fucking unheard of — means [Alter Fate] can one-shot nearly anyone.”

“Mmm. I dunno. High-level [Knights] could, maybe? Or just massive creatures with bloated wounding totals.”

Samantha just shrugged, barely listening. She noticed she had a free skill allocation as one of the level-up benefits. With [Defensive Strike] sitting there, she opted to upgrade it to Expert on the spot. She was pretty sure Ashawein had it even higher, technically. Expert was a big upgrade, though, deepening the potential trade-off, changing ‘one target’ to ‘three foes,’ and allowing a new ‘feint’ option.

Defensive Strike (Expert)

You weaken the severity of an attack to improve your active defenses against up to three foes in front of you. Reduce Accuracy/Wound Severity by -1/-2, -2/-4, or -3/-6, to add +1, +2, or +3 to defenses, respectively.

Alternatively, you may perform a ‘Defensive Feint’ similarly, foregoing any chance at wounding entirely, to drastically improve your defenses. In this case, it will only be of benefit if a foe’s attack comes immediately after. On a success, they are likely to attack through a false opening.

Cool.

“Alright,” Samantha declared with a deep breath. “Time to see what Ozzy crammed into [Timeless Coalescent]. I can feel the fucking power surging from it already. This is big, big, big…”

Floofa suddenly popped up to stand on her hind legs, putting on a serious face, yet striking a rather odd, silly pose with her hands out. “Time keeps on surfin,’ surfin,’ surfin’... into the futuuuuuure!” Her hand motions soon became clear to be surfing wave motions.

Samantha and Azure burst out laughing.

“Very nice, Floofa!” Samantha said in praise. “I think the lyrics are off, but whatever! Those are better!”

Floofa beamed with her fists on her hips.

Samantha turned her attention to the new enhancement, which made her eyes go wider and wider:

Everyone, Everywhere, Anytime: You may add full [Goddess] levels as the base to any task or contest. For multiclass purposes, you may add full [Goddess] levels to any primary sub-class operation. It is only reduced for ‘off-class’ operation, at the same percentage the sub-class would suffer (for example: hand-to-hand combat via [Wizard]).

Likewise, add full sub-class levels (reduced as necessary, as above) to any [Goddess] class operation that would otherwise already apply for multiclassing actions.

Samantha laughed in disbelief as her hands covered her mouth.

“That’s fucking broken,” Azure commented, shaking her head. “Who needs more levels when you have that? It should be called ‘Queen of All Trades.’ Pfft. Missed opportunity, Ozzy!” Comically, she looked upward and shook her fist.

Still smiling, Samantha shrugged. “Technically, I almost had this for arcane spell operation, thanks to [Spellfyr].” When she checked, the rule was still there, perhaps as an oversight — two-thirds Goddess levels added to [Magineer] — though the new enhancement made it obsolete. “So for arcane spells, the base caster level is now twenty-nine… which is higher than a max-level wizard adding some other sub-class also at twenty, as that is only twenty-seven.”

“And you can get higher, maybe.”

“Yeah. Dicey, even with the multiplier, but possible.”

“What’s the improvement for your martial prowess? [Divine Thunderer] and [Goddess]? Better than before?”

“Yeah. Operating off of [Magineer] was shit for smashing things. The penalty is a quarter reduction, but there are worse aspects, some just the intangibles of what you can feasibly pull off creatively. It’ll never ‘overpower’ someone, for example. It limits circumstantial benefits, translating to general performance. [Goddess] full levels were just better.

“And now, I can’t even add [Magineer] to [Goddess] to do melee through this ability, as that’s not a primary act. So I could add a third from Magineer, as before, or full from [Divine Thunderer]. So that’s level twenty-one. That is definitely higher than before. The other option would be [Magineer] plus [Goddess] at a quarter penalty to both. The same total, it looks like, but the intangibles and flexibility make the other the vastly superior option. I can even switch styles! Go from [Goddess] base to [Divine Thunderer] base interchangeably. Few would ever suspect that. A possible tactical advantage.”

“And weapon bonuses close the gap compared to spellcasting.”

“Offensively? More than, I think. Single-target, melee is even more desirable now, just not always feasible. But we’ll see! There’s still more to review!”
