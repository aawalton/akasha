Alexander wandered the forest, searching for Congo packages whose contents had been strewn about. He grabbed an open box and began picking up whatever items he could find—a pack of crayons, some deodorant, a pair of overpriced headphones—trying to collect them all before they blew away. Every time he managed to gather more than a few items and place them in the box, a strong wind would blow through the trees, knocking the box out of his arms and scattering the contents on the ground once more. Alexander screamed in frustration; this was utter torture. It was so aggravating that it physically hurt his legs. He screamed again, this time from the pain in his legs as he awoke.

Alexander grabbed his legs and found them wrapped in roots over his sleeping bag. Glancing left and right, he saw that he and the rest of the camp were on the edge of the glade rather than in the center where they had started. His shouts woke several other camp members, who had also been ensnared by roots. Bubba started barking next to him as Johnny awoke and began struggling.

Turning back to his legs, Alexander said, “Floating Disk.”

His invisible disk had no space to appear, so he felt the mana fail to materialize and return to him. He watched as more roots moved quickly to subdue him, crushing his legs further.

“FIREBALL!” a female voice shouted. Alexander saw a massive explosion against a tree trunk nearby. A tree burst into flames, followed by a wave of heat that slammed into Alexander’s face. Any closer, and he likely would have suffered burns.

A tree shrieked and swayed, causing Alexander to recategorize the trees as monsters—monsters that likely did not like fire.

Alexander had saved a spell for an emergency, so he pulled up his skill menu and selected the fire spell he had mentally placed on his “to consider” list. A book fell into his hands and he quickly selected to learn it.

“Firebolt,” he said, pointing just past his legs. The roots shrank back as the flames licked at them.

“Firebolt,” he repeated, pointing slightly closer to his legs.

This time, they released enough for him to slip his legs out of his sleeping bag. He moved to help Johnny, but Johnny had disappeared. Alexander saw the beagle running to the next closest person, and Johnny… grew up next to his dog out of the dirt and started hacking at the roots around Judah’s legs with an axe. He was helping the noncombatant first—a wise decision.

Brian, upon waking in his now-crushed tent, threw his knife out of the open flap and teleported a short distance from the roots. He looked around, spotted Alexander, pointed in a direction, and sprinted that way.

Alexander ran to find Cassidy still wrapped up, shouting, “Firebolt!” This time he aimed at the tree roots rather than the trunk. He dropped down beside her and said, “Firebolt,” aiming at the roots closest to her leg. They shrank back immediately, creating an opening, and he followed up with “Floating Disk” near her leg. He managed to lift the roots upward and pull her backward out of the tangle just as Brian jumped up beside him to assist.

Johnny managed to free Judah as well, and they all found themselves grouped a dozen yards from the trees.

“What were those spells?” Cassidy looked at Alexander accusingly. “That didn’t seem like support.”

“Where is Linda?” Judah asked.

They split up and searched for her, wary of any roots they saw along the way. As Alexander looked about, he listened carefully and heard a sound about ten yards into the forest line. Rounding a tree, he found Linda lying on the ground undisturbed, snoring loudly. A small circle had formed around her where the roots had pulled back, similar to the clearing. She was sound asleep and snoring. Alexander bent down next to her and shook her; the strong smell of oils emanating from her made his eyes water.

“I found her!” Alexander yelled to the group. He shook her more vigorously. “Linda, wake up!”

She snorted and started awake.

“Ah, is it… is it my turn to watch?” She groaned. “Just a second… I’ll get my Vigilance oil. It will wake me right up.”

“Grab all your things.”

Alexander helped her gather her stuff, and they returned to the glade to find the rest of the group pulling together their equipment that hadn’t been dragged underground by the roots. The fire from Cassidy’s spell provided enough light to work by.

“Who was on watch?” Brian asked the group agitatedly. Judah, obviously ashamed, raised his hand slowly. “And who was with you? Cassidy, weren’t you on watch as well?”

She hesitated a second before nodding.

“I was just so tired! I’m a city girl. I don’t hike twenty miles a day for fun. I take a cab like the rest of the civilized world!”

Johnny chuckled.

“Well, it seems primitive, but I feel we need to do something about the fact that you put the rest of us in danger. No breakfast this time for either of you, and if it happens again…” He trailed off menacingly, as if there were something worse he could do to them. Alexander doubted he had any idea what that would be. Johnny voiced the question on Alexander’s mind.

“What should we do about this, boss? Looks to me like this circle is a trap of some sort. False sense of safety before the forest creeps up on you.”

Alexander looked at Linda and asked loudly enough for the group to hear, “Linda, what oils were you wearing when you went to bed last night?”

“Oh! You see, I do a nightly regimen of oils before I go to sleep—all proprietary blends made exclusively by Naturoleum.” As she recited her list, her voice took on a musical cadence. “Preservation on my skin to keep it young and soft, Deep Dream to give me the REM sleep required for a true night’s rest, Strengthen to increase my immune system response, and lastly Bug Me Not, as we are in nature and nobody enjoys mosquitoes.”

Alexander turned to Brian. “The trees left a circle around her.”

Brian nodded in understanding.

“We will buy those oils from you and use them every night before we go to sleep as well, at least while we are in this forest. Thank you, Linda. Let’s get away from the trees again and decide what to do as we make breakfast.”

Everyone seemed to agree—until they reached the center of the clearing. A wave of exhaustion hit Alexander again, and it seemed the rest of the group felt it too.

Cassidy’s whine came first. “I am so tired. Can we sleep for another couple of hours before we continue?”

“That sounds reasonable,” Judah said supportively.

“Yes,” Brian nodded. “We will rest while you two take your turn on watch as you were supposed to.”

“I can watch as I feel fine,” Johnny piped up.

Everyone looked at him, confused about where his energy came from.

“Do you have a buff to your energy levels or something?” Cassidy asked with a hint of jealousy.

“Not that I’m aware of.”

Alexander thought about it for a second, then decided to be cautious.

“Detect Magic.”

Enchanting magic detected. Would you like to attempt to identify the spell? Yes/No

“Yes.”

Identification successful. Spell name: Minor Exhaustion.

As he succeeded on the identification, his glasses started to see the tiny threads of magic interwoven into a net above their heads. Examining it, he felt that if he had time to study it, he could understand the pattern and its relationship to the effect. It almost felt familiar to Alexander. Something in the back of his head.

Is it similar to suggestion?

He hadn’t been able to see a pattern on suggestion, but learning the spell had been a mental download of sorts. As he pondered the pattern and pictured suggestion moving through it, he felt something in his head click. It wasn’t a large revelation, but maybe a clue.

This glade is made from the collective magic from the Devouring Grove. The center of which is concentrated with the sleep spell. Minor Sleep Creatures will feel compelled to sleep while in the affected area. Will not affect those with high wisdom.

You have gained a level in the intermediate skill Arcana! Arcana level 9: You have 2 points to distribute to your Arcana skill tree.

“Oh,” Alexander said aloud. He looked up to see everyone staring at him. He cleared his throat. “It seems the forest made this glade to be a trap, as Johnny said. There is an area where people will feel an extra level of exhaustion.”

“Then why doesn’t it affect Johnny?” Judah asked.

“It seems that people with high wisdom are resistant to it.”

“Ah yes!” Judah nodded knowingly.

“So you are saying Johnny is wiser than all of us?” Cassidy asked incredulously.

“Well, I suppose I know a thing or two,” he said, eyeing Cassidy.

“Like how to milk cows and shoot defenseless animals?”

“Oh, sugar, I reckon your rural ignorance is just as great as my urban ignorance,” Johnny said with a sly smile.

“What is that supposed to mean?” She huffed.

“Well, what is your numerical value for wisdom, Johnny?” Judah asked.

“I’d like to know as well,” Cassidy said, crossing her arms.

Johnny looked around at everyone, hesitating. “Well, not sure exactly what the numbers mean… but my wisdom is a bit higher than most of the other numbers, at 19. Care to tell me yours, cher?” He looked at Cassidy.

She uncrossed her arms and grabbed her stuff. “I am finished with this tiresome conversation, in this literal tiresome circle.”

She huffed and marched toward the outer edge of the circle, stopping near the trees she had blasted with her fireball spell. Alexander had considered that one as well, but it never made his list—it seemed impractical and far too hazardous to bystanders.

They picked up their gear and moved away from the center of the glade. As Alexander moved to the outside of the ring, he felt the exhaustion lessening with every step. He mused internally about how the sensation was literally the reverse of the norm when hiking.

“Okay,” Brian said. “So here is the game plan. Judah and Cassidy are forgiven for the results of the spell, and Linda and I will make breakfast while the rest of you finish getting some rest. We are going to need all our strength to get out of this forest as quickly as possible. Agreed?”

While Brian waited for a reply from the group, a dozen shrill voices reverberated around the glade: “Agreed… agreed… AGREED! Agreed…” They seemed to be mimicking Brian’s voice, though twisted—too sharp or too light and airy. It made the rest of the group nervous.

Brian sighed heavily. “Ok, new game plan. Get ready to fight.”
