This floor really is a lot simpler. The enemies, made to be such by this minor monolith, always crawl from the water. They’re usually around level 30, with some champions reaching the 60s. But the champions are easy to pick out, since they’re the only ones casting proper skills.

Isn’t that a strange thought? These monsters seem a lot stronger than their levels would make me assume. They’re tough, and fast, and hit hard. But they often don’t cast skills - or only very instinctual ones. Maybe some of them have [Bite] or [Cut] or something, but if so, they’re not very creative with them.

Which, well, is kind of expected. Shame, though, because deconstructing skills is one of my ways of earning essence. I guess my best work is still as an anti-person special weapon. Weird thought, that.

But I disregard it as we walk further along the beach, occasionally drinking from my refilling phial. I’ve still not figured out the enchantments on that item. They’re extraordinarily clever, and even after breaking another one, I’m not done. Hopefully, I can… legally acquire some more, soon.
