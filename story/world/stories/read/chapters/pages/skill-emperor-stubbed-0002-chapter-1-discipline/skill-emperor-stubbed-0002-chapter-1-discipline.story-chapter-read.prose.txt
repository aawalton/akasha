Heroic Class? <Emperor>? The words assault my mind without meaning, so I clutch my forehead, heaving and falling onto the pile of dust where the statue once was.

I find that it has completely crumbled, leaving nothing. Even all the light within it has flooded into me.

But that doesn’t matter now. My heart beats quickly against my chest as my pulse rises. In my previous world, there was nothing like this! This is ‘magic’, right? Back in my world, only a select few could use supernatural powers.

I wasn’t one of the lucky ones.

Glowing golden runes appear in the air before me, shifting around and stabilizing with my movements. I blink, trying to get a better look at them. In my vision, they swim around before changing into a language I can understand.

Name: Jace Kessler (Rank 1)

Titles: None

Bloodline: None

Bloodline Skills:

Class: Emperor (Heroic)

Class Skills:

Skills:

It looks like I don’t have any skills except for my Class skills, but that is alright for now! I am beginning to understand the <Emperor> Class! Or at least, I think so. While I’m not used to this, I can sort of figure out the implications of it.

When I get skills, I should be able to name them! And when I name them, I will be able to summon them.

In this way, I can build an empire of sorts. The thought makes me shiver, because I do not want to become like one of those emperors in my previous world, who treat people as slaves and livestock. Just the thought makes me nauseous.

Instead, with the <Emperor> Class, I have been given a chance to pursue my dream! That dream being, to help people.

It might sound like a stupid dream.

But I am determined to keep to it.

“First, I need to find a way out of here,” I mutter while looking around. The statue dust has almost completely dissolved, and it seems to be midday since the sun is high in the sky.

The only problem is, how the hell do I do that?!

Should I go looking for something else? Maybe I’ll find something like the statue, to get me out of this place. I think that maybe because of how unlucky I was in my previous life, my luck has somehow skyrocketed wherever I am now.

So, I begin walking.

***

“FINALLY!” I shout at the horizon. “FINALLY, I FOUND SOMETHING!”

In my entire life, I’ve never felt this sort of happiness before. And it's all because the color of the grass has begun to change. Instead of staring at endless green fields, the fields have now become sort of amber, like the green is fading away.

Of course, that isn’t much. But it looks like I am doing something right, at least.

***

I hear the sound of running water.

Again, not exactly a profound discovery, but still amazing nonetheless. There is a slight trickling in my mind coming from far away, so I know the water is there. Of course it could be a mirage, but I want to think optimistically.

***

I’m not sure how many days have actually passed at this point, even though I can clearly see the days passing! It might be a side effect of not having food or water, who knows.

All in all, it is surprising that I can survive without any of the basic necessities. It isn’t like my body has changed or anything. I feel pretty much the same, I think. At least my build isn’t all that different, and my hair color hasn’t changed—still a strange white color.

From now, it is clear that I won’t be able to find what I want soon. So, I should start getting working out.

Ermm… there’s only a single problem with that.

How the hell does one work out?

***

I think I’ve gotten the hang of it! Well, think is the key word here. I can’t find any weights or anything, so I just do pushups and situps and the sort. I think I tried doing it with one arm as well, but that didn’t go very well.

The grass is entirely amber now, and the trickling of water is louder.

I finish my set of pushups—I can only muster about thirty in a single sitting now, and look up to see runes encircling my body!

Adrenaline rushes through me as my eyes widen.

[You are able to unlock a new skill: <Discipline>]

[Would you like to unlock the new skill?]

<Discipline>? I frown. What kind of skill is that? In my previous world, they didn’t have ‘skills’, but the supernaturals had different abilities. For example, I once saw a person create a giant ball of water in the air above them to put out a fire! Although that fire was in a rich residential area…

What I mean is, <Discipline> does not seem like a skill that does something.

Still, there is no harm in accepting it!

“Yes,” I say to nobody in particular.

[You have unlocked the skill <Discipline>!]

Suddenly, an otherworldly feeling rushes through my body. It is similar to when I recieved the <Emperor> Class from that statue, but it is somehow different. At the top of my mind, there is an urge. And that urge is to work.

So I do. I immediately fall back to the ground, drilling my workout routine into my bones instead of moving forward. My hands scrape and dig against the ground with every movement, and suddenly there is no restriction within me.

I see now, what ‘skills’ are.

They are like executables. Or functions, something like that. I’m not too used to tech and programming, okay?

As I keep exercising, some mysterious energy begins to build up around me. It is unlike anything I’ve ever seen before—wispy like flames, but also white as paper.

[<Discipline> Lvl 1 ➜ Lvl 2]

It leveled up! Another surge of energy washes over me.

Looks like I know what I’ll be doing from now on.

***

Movement has been slow lately, because I have been juggling two things at the same time. But seriously, I think I’ve fallen in love with working out! I know it sounds stupid to say because I’m just a novice at all this, but there’s a lot of enjoyment to be found with it! Seriously!

Happily, I trek across the grass plains with sweat dripping down my skin.

… Wait.

There, far in the distance, what is that?

I lift my right hand and use it to block the glaring rays of sunlight coming down from above. I squint, trying to figure out what it is…

Water!

That’s the water source!

It is like a tiny slit in the land, but I can clearly see the bubbling stream within it. Excitedly, I begin to run toward the water, ready to take a bath and freshen up. It has been terrible, and if I kept on going like this I think I would get an <Odor> skill.

I finally reach the small stream, and the runes pop up again.

[<Discipline> Lvl 2 ➜ Lvl 3]

Maybe this level up is because I’ve finally reached a goal I’ve been striving for?

I have no way to test the purity of the water or anything like that, but it looks crystal clear, meaning it’s probably fine, right? I dunk my head into the water and then rip it out, gasping from the cool sensation on my face.

My skin sizzles, and I realize just how hot the sun can be.

I jump into the river, which isn’t particularly large. I could probably swim across if I tried a bit.

As I do, more runes pop up.

[<Discipline> Lvl 3 ➜ Lvl 5]

[<Discipline> has become eligible]

I freeze.

Now, how did something like that happen? It leveled up so quickly. Does it have something to do with the water? I sense no abnormalities, though!

I’m still not sure exactly how this works, but I feel much stronger now.

And the skill is ‘eligible’ now, as well. That means I can name it, right?

“How am I supposed to use <Designate Name>?” I think aloud. “It isn’t like there’s a tutorial for it…” I place a finger on my lip as I usually do when thinking hard. I think back to the wisps of white energy that curl around my body during particularly intense workout sessions.

That is some kind of magical energy, right? Could I use it to power <Designate Name>?

Closing my eyes, I give it a go.

Within a minute, I feel something tugging at my body! I use <Discipline>, which further enhances my senses and strength. Overall, the increment is small. But the power of <Discipline> is to build this up over time.

I wait a few minutes until—

Crack!

Something snaps, and I open my eyes to see light.

[Which skill would you like to name?]

[Eligible skills: <Discipline>]

“I will name <Discipline>.”
