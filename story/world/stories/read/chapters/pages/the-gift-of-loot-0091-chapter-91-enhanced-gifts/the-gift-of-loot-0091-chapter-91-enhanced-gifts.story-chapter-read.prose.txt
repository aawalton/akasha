Thomas stared at the pile of ash that had been a guard a few seconds ago. No doubt about it: Zach's Gift had been enhanced but good. It had been like the fire had teleported inside of him.

Jesus Christ on a pogo stick.

That realization only lasted a fraction of a second because his gaze snapped to the Corruption crystals and the skill shard that lay beside the ash pile.

They weren't in a dungeon, Thomas had nothing to do with the kill, and yet the guard had left behind loot.

Jumping Jesus Christ on a goddamn pogo stick.

He had no time to think about how utterly screwed he might be now that his Gift was out in the open for anyone to see, and that apparently his team could use, because at that moment another guard attacked. He swung a sword glowing with green Corruption right at Thomas's ribs.

Just seeing it made his mana want to recoil. Thomas did not want the Corruption blade to so much as scratch him. He caught the blow halfway using his Mutable shield. However, the sword strike must have been a feint because the guard didn't hesitate a second and aimed a punch right at Thomas's jaw.

Instinctively backing from the Corruption mana he could feel roiling inside the guard saved him. Thomas jerked his head back out of the way before he fully registered the punch was coming.

The strike missed and Thomas bashed him with his shield. He just wanted to get the man—especially the Corruption—away from him.

The guard went flying back a full two yards and landed hard on his ass, clutching at his very visibly broken arm.

Some old Looney Tunes bit flashed in Thomas's mind about not knowing his own strength.

Then he didn't have to worry about that guard anymore because his throat opened as if an invisible lady with a sword had passed him by. He fell with blood fountaining, and a moment later another Corruption mana crystal and, weirdly, a Water crystal lay by its head.

Thomas braced himself for his own mana to burn him. He hadn't meant for that man to die; he had just been protecting himself. Jo had done the work, but he still felt slightly bad about the man's death. Would that trigger his Burden?

He waited for a second but a fat nothing happened. Huh.

Then his attention was yanked to the side as he heard a snap of bone followed by Twitch's scream.

He'd just taken a heavy mace hit to his shoulder. Thomas started toward him but he'd only taken a couple of steps when Twitch's very obviously broken shoulder and collarbone snapped back into place all on its own. No healing mana required.

Twitch then used his newly reconstituted shoulder to roughly shove the butt of his spear into the guard's gut. Thomas was too far away to hear the "oof" from the guard, but he practically felt it. While the man was hunched, Twitch slapped a hand down on his shoulder. Arcs of electricity shot out, practically tasing the man where he stood. The guard dropped.

No loot. The guard was still alive, and Thomas was grateful for it.

He hadn't heard an alarm go off, but reinforcements were coming anyway. Thomas was kept busy defending himself.

While he couldn't outright kill, he had never been physically stronger. In fact, he had to hurriedly put the brass knuckles away because punching somebody or shield bashing them was more than enough to bring them down.

While fighting, he was distantly aware of Wellspring mana trickling from his level four core into his body, specifically into shoulder muscles and down his back. His Mutable shield felt light as a feather, and though he breathed hard, he found he wasn't out of breath.

There was a burst of heat, the sound like a whip crack, as if the air itself was splitting, and three more guards were sent flying into a wall with reddened, burned skin on their hands and faces.

Thomas glanced at Zach. His hologram had completely fallen apart under the strain. Little licks of candle-top flames were dancing above his head. They were orange at the bottom and shaded to dark at the top. Zach caught his gaze and grinned wildly back. But there was none of the volcano demon in there. His eyes were sane; he was still in control. By the looks of things, he was getting a handle on his new power enough to disable and not destroy.

Twitch ran through a small group like an electrically charged bowling ball. His body snapped back instantly from any wounds he picked up. Son of a bitch didn't even have to deal with the Burden to do that. Thomas was jealous.

Thomas would have checked on Jo, but he saw no signs of her, which was the point of her Gift. Just dead guards wherever she passed. Unlike the others, she gave no quarter.

Really, their enemies didn't have a chance.

Finally, there was one guard left and, clearly seeing which way the wind was blowing, he tossed down a pike and held up his hands. "I yield! I yield! Don't kill me. I got a kid!"

Jo seemed to appear out of nowhere, but also... not? The second Thomas finally was able to see her, it felt like he'd always been able to, but his mind simply hadn't registered it. Her Gift of Hiding was so weird.

With sword in hand, she glared down at the guard. "Sit down and keep your hands where I can see them."

The man practically collapsed on his ass, hands up and out. The fear in his face was real, and Thomas didn't have any worry he was going to restart the fight any time soon.

He wasn't the only one. There were quite a few guards either on the floor groaning or lying very still with their empty hands up in surrender.

Thomas was careful not to look at them closely because he hated seeing their injuries, especially when he knew he could help. Because he didn't have "stupid" stamped to his forehead, he could exercise some self-control... but he still hated to see it.

Instead, he switched off the shredded remains of his own hologram disguise and focused on the crazy amount of loot that was scattered near the real bodies. That was one easy way to tell who was actually alive versus who was playing possum. Most of the dead had at least one Corruption mana crystal near them, but he saw a rainbow of others. It looked like they mixed and matched Corruption with other types. Then, of course, there were the skill shards that used to be people's Gifts.

He wasn't the only one who noticed. Twitch had given up on his disguise too and bent to pick up a skill shard. "What the hell. You guys ever seen this happen outside a dungeon? Wait, are we still in a dungeon? Like, a dungeon within a dungeon Inception shit?"

"Later," Jo said, clipped.

"What do you mean, later? Look at all this free loot! Man, I'd never seen so much —"

He glanced at Thomas, and his mouth snapped shut. Thomas didn't think he was mean-mugging him. He was just severely freaked. It wouldn't take much for anyone to connect those very short dots. Aside from his newfound strength, he was the only one not showing an active Gift. Forget Twitch, half of these guards were still alive. Shit, shit, shit.

Zach broke in, pitching his voice hard and commanding with a sort of drill sergeant's bite that Thomas hadn't heard from him before. "Drop your weapons and get in the far corner. You play stupid or you play dead, and you're not, and I'll fry you right where you stand." His hands erupted with the black-topped flame that looked positively evil.

People got moving, even if a couple of them had to crawl to do so.

"What are we going to do with them?" Thomas asked in a low voice.

Matching his tone, Zach answered. "We'll put them in one of the cells. Totally ironic, don't you think?" He flashed a grin and half-nodded. "Get the loot."

Thomas didn't need to be told twice. It might be really screwed up, but that was what he really wanted.

Pulling out one of the storage bags, he started collecting. He focused on the skill shards and stepping around the Corruption mana. He wasn't touching any of that with a ten-foot pole.

Twitch started collecting, too, though he had no visible trouble touching the Corruption crystals. He also looked about as giddy as a kid on Christmas. "You see all this, dawg? I mean, what the fuck. Hand me one of those storage bags, would ya? I'm not leaving any behind."

Thomas had an empty one, thanks to consolidating the loot back in the dungeon. Good thing, too, because he didn't want that Corruption mingling with his stuff.

"I mean," Twitch continued, "I haven't spent much time on the outside since getting put away, but this isn't how it is nowadays, right? People just don't drop goodies like they're monsters. Unless..." He glanced at Thomas with new concern. "Shit, do they? The Guard were selling off indentures. I thought they were just coming to places like this, like dungeon mines. But were they being farmed?"

"They were what?" Thomas's thoughts briefly stalled out at that little revelation, but he couldn't focus on that. Now was the time to plant the seed for a good excuse. "Uh, no, I've never seen this happen before. It must be something the elves have done. Maybe the dungeon's leaking energy or something."

Sure, blame the elves. He didn't feel bad about it.

Antonin came out of the shadows where Jo had told him to hide. He carefully stood by Zach's side, staring around with wide eyes.

Jo cut in. "Work fast, boys. We're on a timer, and I'm not the only one who wants to get out of here."

She was right. Plus, Courtney and Tiny weren't going to wait for them to finish shopping. Thomas focused on collecting the last of the skill shards, which were probably people's Gifts, and he didn't want to think too closely about that. All went into the bag. He'd catalog them later. Then, with one last sweep of the room, he nodded. "I'm done."

"Yeah, yeah," Twitch agreed, throwing the bag over his shoulder. "Me too."

Thomas couldn't help it. He glanced around once again for something shiny and forgotten just in case, then stopped as he noticed something. His blood went cold. "Anyone see where Frederic went?"

Zach, Jo, and Twitch stilled and looked around. Sure enough, he was nowhere to be seen.

Jo turned to Zach. "What's his Gift?"

"I don't know," Zach admitted. "I didn't see him leave, either. That fight was crazy. Antonin, did you see?"

"No," the man said softly.

Thomas held up his hand. "Let me look." Closing his eyes, he concentrated fully on his Health Sight and swept his perception through the mine complex. There were bunches of life signatures gathered together. Those, he assumed, were the prisoners. As far as he could tell, there was nobody alone. Even the guards who had been positioned at the mouth of the entrance were gone. Presumably, they were here.

Opening his eyes, he shook his head. "I don't see anybody by themselves. Either Frederic ran off and died somewhere, or he ran off to go get help."

"I could've sworn I stabbed him through the heart," Jo said. "But I guess that guy doesn't have one. He's probably gone to the portal room."

If they didn't have a quest on their hands, Thomas would have told Zach to just get them out of there, even if that meant forgoing looting the inventory room. However, they did have a quest, not to mention the prisoners who were waiting to get fed to the dungeon. He couldn't just leave those people behind.

"Let's work fast," Thomas said.

They marched the remaining guards out. Thankfully, most of them were ambulatory, and the ones that weren't were helped by the other guards. Many threw dirty looks at Zach as if they were personally offended that he had turned on them, but Zach didn't look like he noticed or cared.

The first cell wasn't very far down a side tunnel. It was empty, having been the one that she and the rest of Team Alpha had been lodged in. Jo had a very satisfied look on her face as they ordered the guards in and shut the door.

Thomas didn't forget for one hot second that they were on several different timers: The quest, Courtney and Tiny who could leave the dungeon at any moment, Frederic's whereabouts, and maybe if some other guards had managed to shoot out a call for help.

He knew all that, yet... Releasing the poor people who had been imprisoned was one of the most satisfying acts he had done since he first stepped into the Demon Chicken dungeon.

The people were traumatized and cautious and so, understandably, a little freaked out when they first showed up, but that fear turned into joy as the cell doors opened and Thomas was able to disable their ankle monitors with Severing Radiance. People shook his hand and hugged him, crying. This was the stuff he liked about being a healer. In a way, it touched on why he also liked loot so much, too: Enriching himself and enriching others.

It was up to Twitch and Zach to subtly shuffle them off, and Jo's no-nonsense presence to keep everybody on track and keep moving from cell to cell.

Thomas wasn't the only one affected. He glanced at Twitch, who was distinctly misty-eyed.

"It's good being on the other side of this," Twitch said simply, and Thomas let him have his moment. The idea was to get everybody to the portal Jo had talked about, but some of the prisoners didn't want to wait for that and broke straight for the entrance. Desert or no desert, they wanted the hell out of here. Thomas couldn't blame them, and nobody tried to stop them. With the last cell emptied, they made their way to the portal room.

Thomas had seen one of these in the Dark Elves' village. He didn't want to get into a copyright pissing match, but the wide ramp leading up to a stone ring held eerie resemblances to a Stargate. Right now, the portal ring showed a blank blue within it, giving off a distinct 'standby mode' appearance.

"There is some crazy teleportation mana happening here," Zach muttered, and pushed his way to the front to a very alien-looking portal control panel. Then he started pushing buttons.

"Uh, you know how to use that?" Thomas asked. There probably wasn’t a self-destruct button. Right?

"Sorta. I don't know what these do, but I know what the mana's doing, you know?"

Thomas was about to suggest Zach maybe possibly stop doing that before he blew something up, but just at that moment, the blank blue field in the ring suddenly switched to the image of a park-like setting. A very Earth-looking park-like setting, at night. Probably to the east somewhere.

"Got a good connection," Zach said in satisfaction. "Go, go, go!"

Some people didn't wait, going right through.

One man hesitated. "Where is this?"

"Not here," Twitch said dryly. “Ain’t that enough?”

"Who cares, I'm outta here," declared one teenager, practically diving in, even though walking would have carried him through just fine. He hit the park's lawn ungracefully, then scrambled up and ran away.

That was good enough for the rest of them, despite a couple of grumbles. Once on the other side, people broke into a run. Or in one notable case, flew.

Finally, the last prisoner was through, and Zach pressed the X button on the panel, which shut down the portal. They all looked at each other.

"I'm kind of waiting for a video game notification to pop up and say the quest is complete," Thomas said. "Do we need to go through the dungeon and get a new scroll for an update?"

"The dungeon," Jo repeated, her expression darkening. "What do you want to bet the System counts Tiny, Sue, and Holton as prisoners who need to be freed before we can claim this place?"

"Oh man," Zach muttered.

"Well," Thomas said, brightening. "We can go back to the dungeon entrance and wait for them to come out and hope they'll be in a mood to talk... Or we can find that inventory room, steal all the good stuff, then wait at the dungeon entrance —"

That was when the portal flashed into blue light again, and this time there was definitely a swirl pattern of magic that accompanied it. Someone had activated the thing, and it hadn't been from their side.

"Crap, dudes! We have incoming," Zach yelped.

Everybody started to get into a fighting stance, but Jo growled, "Nope, stick with me."

She grabbed Thomas's arm with one hand, yanking Antonin in close with the other, and moved them all together so close that their shoulders touched.

The next thing Thomas knew, it felt like a wet blanket had been thrown over him. He had been under the effects of Jo's Gift before and had felt exactly nothing, but now its power had been enhanced. He felt exactly like he had when he had been very small, playing hide-and-seek with his brothers. He'd hidden under a blanket, thinking that would conceal him. Only this time, it looked like it worked.

Thomas glanced to the right and left and didn't see his team at all, even though he knew they were there. Presumably, no one could see him either.

"Don't make any noise," Jo's voice came from next to him, and she sounded strained. Enhanced Gift or not, she was perfectly hiding a group of people.

She had moved just in time because the portal flashed into a truly blinding-looking landscape, as if everything were drawn in shades of yellow and white. Thomas had to squint his eyes, and even then he could barely make out details of the world beyond.

Then two real Light elves strode out.
