Thatch knocks on a stone wall. And, indeed, as he promised, it sounds hollow behind it. There’s no door to access it, though. Just something that’s perfectly sealed up. How interesting. If only we had Bay with us. Surely she could have blown it up. Tragically, as it is right now, I need to actually do the work and figure it out.

[Observation] hums to life and I kneel before the hollow part of the wall. There’s little lines of mana flowing through the stone, but it’s hidden. [Observation 23 > 24]

We walk, bit by bit, and three more traps trigger. The door behind us closes once everyone is in the tunnel, smooth stone sliding back into place. I breathe, and am once again reminded of the hole right through me. Even now, all my mana is busily getting used to redirect my blood vessels.

Efficiency helps me work through it, though. My oxygen absorption seems to be more efficient, so I still feel alright, even though I should definitely be feeling winded at this point. After a few dozen steps in the narrow tunnel, it opens up into a simple stone-brick room - fully warded.

There are more papers strewn about, partially covering the floor - most of which is a nice, soft rug, except for the trapdoor.

Instantly, we swarm forwards. I check the inscriptions, roughly at first, and find that they seem to mostly be warding-related. Keeping mana in and out. It’s an air-gapped room. Completely sealed.

Which means no banshees can make their way here. “All safe,” I say raggedly, and one by one, everyone starts taking a look around. Once more, my eyes are drawn to that desk in the middle. My curiosity spikes. Now what could that be?
