Death Valley was disturbed. Vast powers had woken and now clashed with enough force to shake the ground. Roars echoed, painfully loud, even this far away. Noah tried to stay calm and centered, focused on drawing in and filtering the overly-strong mana, but his own mind was equally disturbed.

Mana: 4%

Noah's head hurt. His everything hurt. His meditation was fragmented and ineffective. He'd underestimated just how much Zax's wards were making it possible to absorb the local mana at all.

Zax is dead.

He couldn’t quite bring himself to believe it, didn’t want to believe it. But as minutes turned to hours without the dragon appearing, he found it harder and harder to deny.

When Sev woke him from his attempted meditation the first time, it was with a hand over his mouth, crouched to shush him as he pulled Noah into a darker corner. “Unseen will protect us as long as we don’t move,” he whispered, and stood blocking Noah from view as Ascendant power rolled over them.

Noah fought against the overwhelming suppression that was the Ascendant’s aura with all the mental strength he could muster. It wasn’t enough to break any of its effects, but it did net him another level of his resistance.

The Ascendant hunting them didn’t even glance at their cave, but stalked past outside without slowing. Still, it was minutes after she left that Sev finally relaxed and nodded for Noah to resume his meditation.

“I don’t suppose you have any handy exercises for internalizing mana a rank too high for you?” Noah asked as he settled back down. “My familiar said I just need to subjugate it, but I haven’t mastered that method yet.”

Sev laughed softly. “You would chain yourself to a hurricane and expect it to turn at your will?”

“Well, no, not without a lot more wind magic or associated artifacts.”

“So why would you imagine mana is any different? You don’t need to beat it into submission, just dilute it with something else.”

“I don’t have much in the way of anything else,” Noah began, then stopped. “Would D-Grade mana crystals work?”

"D-Grade? You know how the scaling of power grades works? It would be completely useless."

"Then I've got nothing."

"You've got yourself. And, more importantly, you've got me."

Noah laughed weakly. "What, are you going to suggest some kind of dual cultivation thing?"

"That's a good term for it."

"I don't know anything about you." Which made Noah frown as he realized, as if for the first time, that he had no idea who Sev was. "Why are you helping me? Who are you?"

"I already told you. I'm from the guild, here to escort you to safety before the Ascendants hunting you can finish you off like they did your friends."

"But you're the same level as me, what use are you going to be against Ascendants?"

"I kept them from noticing us, didn't I?"

"Ye...es..." Sev had done a good job filling in the protector gap, but Noah still couldn’t quite let go. Grief and denial washed over him all over again. What would he do without Zax? Keep moving, obviously, but it'd taken everything he had, every trick he could possibly throw at it, just to escape with his life.

He'd lost Aurelia, lost Zax, all he had left was Sev and without the man's concealment powers he'd have been caught again already.

"We can't stay here forever," Sev said, beginning to lose patience again. "Do you want my help or not?"

Noah couldn't think why he was so reluctant, maybe he was just too tense with everything going on to relax regardless of who it was.

No, he thought. If Aurelia had been here, he could have. Even if it just meant holding her while they grieved for Zax together. Even afraid, even alone... it would have been better, easier, if she'd...

“You're clearly in no condition to be making decisions. I'm getting us out of here. You can come back and get yourself killed after." Sev grabbed his hand and tugged him to his feet.

The moment his hand touched Noah’s, something shifted. He’d been too numb and overwhelmed to notice the first time, but the prolonged contact was enough that the sleepy Devourer perked up and shoved against Noah’s consciousness hard.

Dizziness assaulted him and he practically fell on top of Sev the moment he had his feet under him.

Sev let out a disgusted noise, then stopped trying to be nice and hoisted Noah over his shoulder like a sack. “Why do I even bother?” he grumbled, and took off running.

The prolonged contact took everything that had been muddling him on and off and turned it all the way up. Static filled Noah's head, buzzing and hungry and testing at the edge. His memory felt unstable, like he couldn't even remember the current moment, it just slid past him before he could even perceive it.

They were moving past walls he knew, walls he didn’t, canyons a blur. He had to move.

He was so empty. It was hard to focus, which only made him that much more desperate to maintain some semblance of autonomy.

Tony is concerned, Tony mentioned, his voice oddly distant and hazy.

"Noah is also concerned," Noah mumbled in response. They were moving the wrong direction, back toward the upper valley, and without Zax to help and protect him he doubted he'd make it this deep in again any time soon. "Stop, go back."

Sev didn't answer.

Noah gathered what little mana he'd been able to absorb and activated Arcane Step, blinking out of Sev's reach back in the direction they'd come.

The man spun on him, and Noah teleported again.

He’d started to grow used to the feeling of teleporting through the heavy denseness of the local atmospheric power, but it was still like shoving his way through mud.

Dazzling light surrounded him, blocking his ability to see anything but directly below him. He didn’t know why he was running, only that he had to get back on track. Keep moving forward. Or, turn back and let the Devourer take over.

He wasn’t going to do that.

Noah is going to die. Tony’s voice echoed from some vast distance, urgent.

I know this is a dangerous path but I can’t stop now.

No. Noah’s body is overloaded. Tony can fix some of it but the mana is too far beyond Tony’s understanding.

Hazy impressions of having eaten the A-Grade mana crystal came back to him, and he finally recognized the static running through his body as too-strong mana that tore its way out of him leaving myriad cracks and channels in its wake. Tony was repairing the damage as quickly as he could, but it was depleting Noah’s own mana faster than it could be replenished.

He wasn’t capable of guiding it in such small amounts, the traces left over after he'd burned through the bulk of it were too fast and slippery to keep hold of.

"Is that what's happening? The mana is physically burning holes through my memory?"

Tony... is not sure. Tony is also confused.

Noah could still feel the weight of the Ascendants hunting them, presences so far beyond his comprehension that to even breathe in the same general area as they existed was a struggle. It stacked atop the weight of the local mana and the heaviness in his own body from overstrain, and made him question if maybe he was wrong. Perhaps Death Valley wasn't someplace for a B-Ranker to survive on his own.

"I was hoping you'd be at least a little cooperative," said a voice from behind him, and when he turned, it was Sev who he'd somehow forgotten about again. Now the man was angry, and light gathered in his hands and hovered about his head like the aura of an eclipse. "It would have been so much more fun to ruin your life slowly, but if you're not interested in playing along, I guess we can do it the quick way."

Noah had no time to react. The attack came at him so fast even with all his agility boosts combined he couldn't perceive it until after it hit. Sev’s open palm crashed through his armor, shattered his scales, and sent him flying. He slammed up against the stone wall of the ravine with enough force it made his previous crater look tame.

Noah collapsed in a limp pile, his unbreakable bones rearranged through his largely pulverized body. He had no thought of movement, only the overwhelming static of confusion and pain. He couldn't have moved even if he'd tried.

His Abyssal Awareness remained blinded by the dazzling lights, making his surroundings almost unperceivable. He could barely even perceive himself, or whatever was left of himself.

Master! Tony screamed in his mind. Noah! Wake up!

Noah isn't available at the moment, Noah thought dizzily.

Sev was still saying words, something about vengeance and honor, but Noah’s perception was overloaded enough with . They were sounds that his nonexistent brain and pulverised formerly-ears couldn't register, only angry vibrations that didn't quite translate.

Tony will help. Determined, focused, with only the slightest edge of desperation. Noah's chest burned as Tony coalesced inside what used to be his ribcage but was now a collection of sliced organs and disconnected bones.

It might have been one of the most uncomfortable experiences of his life if not for the fact he was already so far beyond being able to describe the experience's awfulness.

Noah became aware of the string of energy drawn into his body from his attacker, the residual thread of his Life Siphon ability that he must have activated at some point even though he couldn't remember it.

He grabbed hold of that thread and pulled as hard as he could. Anything that wasn't acknowledgment of his own current condition. Sev's life energy resisted him strongly enough that he almost reversed the draw entirely just by reflex. It would be so easy to let himself be drawn in, follow the thread of least resistance...

Do not! Noah! Stay with Tony. Come back to me. Stay yourself!

Noah felt that voice small and distant, weak and pitiful compared to the vastness that was Sev's power, but where Sev's presence evoked confusion and uncertainty, Tony's felt warm and familiar and correct.

He distrusted the confusing creature that had tried to save him or kill him or both, distrusted him all the way to his soul.

He pulled away, maintaining the siphon in the proper direction, and fled toward Tony.

*Abyssal Symbiote has activated Arcane Step.*

His awareness snapped back into existence. Tony was in his tiny eyebat form, his tiny claws holding Noah's heart as he fled the Ascendant behind them, leaving the rest of his body behind.

We will not be able to escape for long, Tony warned. He was burning through Noah's mana and each Arcane Step took them further toward depletion.

Mana: 2%

They could survive, for now, but...

Noah had to admit, Bun Bun had been right. A dragon did not hide and run. Whatever powers had been brought against him, Noah should still throw himself against them even if it meant crushing himself in the process.

Channel even if you can't absorb.

Oddly enough, being nothing but a heart and a soul made it easier to understand things that having health and power and agility distracted from.

I have a plan. Noah mentally shoved the image of the destination he had in mind to Tony, and the eyebat shifted direction.

Tony bobbed in agreement as he flew. Tony will help rebuild. It will not be pretty.

Doesn't need to be.

Noah relaxed into his awareness, focused all his available attention on maintaining the siphon. He was sure only that perpetual inflow of energy drained from the Ascendant was keeping him going, just enough to offset everything else and sustain his existence and Tony's rapid teleportation.

Fortunately, it took a lot less energy to Arcane Step a tiny Tony with just Noah's heart. So drastically less, in fact, that Noah worried about just how long it was going to take him to have a functional body again. He'd had his body reconstructed in one way or another over and over, but never quite torn down to actually nothing like this.

He really, really hoped his plan would work, because if it didn’t, he had nothing left to try.
