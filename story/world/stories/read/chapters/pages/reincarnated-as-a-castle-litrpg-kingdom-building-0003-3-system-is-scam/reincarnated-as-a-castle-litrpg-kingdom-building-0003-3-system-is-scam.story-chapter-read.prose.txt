(Hah! So I actually get rewarded for being struck by lightning? Nice.)

Unfortunately, before I could spend too much time appreciating my newly earned achievement, another problem demanded my immediate attention.

There was now a giant hole in my roof.

Rainwater poured through the opening nonstop, splashing onto the stone floor below and slowly forming puddles inside the castle. Thankfully, there wasn’t any furniture to ruin, but still, the whole thing felt strangely invasive. Like the storm had broken into my home.

(Hmm… don’t I have a skill for this?)

“System, open.”

The familiar blue screen appeared in my vision almost instantly. After scanning through the menu for a moment, I found the entry I was looking for.

[Self-Repair (Level 1)]

(Now then… how exactly do I use this?)

At first, I tried the most obvious approach.

(Self-Repair!) I shouted mentally with as much determination as possible.

Nothing happened.

Right. Maybe screaming skill names like some rookie anime protagonist wasn’t the correct activation method.

So instead, I focused directly on the damaged section of my roof. I imagined the broken stone and shattered slate slowly pulling themselves back together, piece by piece, until the structure was restored.

A moment later, the System responded.

[Not enough materials! Self-repair process failed.]

[Required Materials: 1x Stone, 1x Wood]

(Huh?)

I stared blankly at the message.

(Materials? Where the hell am I supposed to get materials?)

Sure, there was an entire forest surrounding me, packed with trees and loose stones. But there was one tiny issue with that.

I couldn’t move.

Not even an inch.

Then suddenly, a thought occurred to me.

(Wait… shouldn’t I already have materials?)

The lightning strike had blasted chunks of my roof apart. Those pieces had to land somewhere nearby, right? Maybe I could recycle them back into myself somehow.

Quickly, I switched to my external vision and scanned the courtyard through the pouring rain.

But strangely enough, I couldn’t find a single piece of debris.

(Huh?)

(Where did everything go?)

Now thoroughly confused, I checked beneath the window I had destroyed several days earlier during my brief and tragic drumming career.

(It should still be there…)

But the result was the same.

Nothing.

The broken window pieces had completely vanished. No shattered wood. No fragments of glass. Not even a tiny splinter remained.

A cold realization slowly settled over me.

(So let me get this straight…)

(If parts of me break off… they just disappear?)

(Completely erased like they never existed in the first place?)

(Damn it… then how am I supposed to repair myself?)

That realization genuinely terrified me.

Sure, I could survive one storm. But what about the next one? And the one after that? If pieces of me kept disappearing permanently every time I took damage, then eventually I would be worn down bit by bit until nothing remained except ruins swallowed by the forest.

As panic slowly started creeping back in, the System suddenly displayed another message.

[If insufficient materials are available, Points may be used instead.]

(Oh!)

Right.

I still had the ten points I earned from the “Wrath of the Gods” achievement.

“Yes, please,” I replied immediately.

However—

[Repairing damaged roof section.]

[Cost: 10 Points.]

(HUH?!)

(ALL TEN?!)

I nearly lost my mind.

That was the price just to repair one tiny section of roof?

Instantly, every survival instinct I had developed from years of gaming started screaming at me.

No.

Absolutely not.

I knew this trap.

After all, this “Castle Points” currency felt exactly like the kind of premium resource you were supposed to hoard carefully in gacha games. You never should spend everything on the first shiny thing that appeared, because the next limited-time banner might drop a super-rare SSR waifu with broken skills or a game-changing item that could completely shift your progression.

My years of suffering through Miyoyo games had prepared me for moments like this.

(You can’t fool me, System.)

(And besides… do I really need to repair the roof right now?)

Honestly?

Not really.

I was already soaked anyway. At worst, some mold and mushrooms would probably start growing inside me eventually.

But weirdly enough, I didn’t completely hate that idea.

A tiny ecosystem forming inside the castle actually sounded kind of interesting. At least something would finally be alive around here.

So after thinking it over carefully, I decided to hold onto the points for now.

(Let’s save them first. I should check what else the System can do before wasting everything on roof repairs.)

“System, open shop.”

Almost immediately, another notification appeared.

[Do you wish to unlock Shop Level 1?]

[Unlock Cost: 10 Points.]

(Eh?)

(System… are you serious?)

That would drain all my remaining points too.

I hesitated.

But before I could decide, the System chimed once more.

[Ding! Achievement Obtained: 10 Days in Azef!]

[Reward: +10 Points]

(Ooooh. Now that’s some perfect timing.)

For the first time since my reincarnation, it felt like the universe had finally decided to throw this broke-ass castle a bone.

“Alright,” I said excitedly. “Take the ten points and unlock the shop.”

[Ding! System Shop Level 1 Unlocked.]

[Points -10]

[Remaining Points: 10]

A brand-new screen unfolded before me, glowing softly against the darkness of the storm outside.
