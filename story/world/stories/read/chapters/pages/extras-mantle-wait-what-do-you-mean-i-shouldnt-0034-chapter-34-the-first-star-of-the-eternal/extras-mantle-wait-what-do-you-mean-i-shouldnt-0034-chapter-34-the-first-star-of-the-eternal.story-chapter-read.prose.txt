Jin's consciousness returned like a bucket of ice water dumped over his head.

He was standing in the white expanse, completely naked, covered in what looked like black sludge that stank worse than a week-old gym sock filled with rotten fish. The stench hit him first—a nauseating combination of sulfur, decay, and something metallic that coated his throat.

"What the—" He looked down at himself and his stomach lurched violently. The black gunk was everywhere, coating his skin like toxic tar. "Oh god, what is this stuff?"

This is revolting. Absolutely revolting.

His nose burned with each breath. The novels never mentioned anything about smelling like Satan's personal armpit after he spent three days at a heavy metal concert.

His stomach cramped. Jin doubled over and retched, but nothing came out except bile that burned his throat. His body felt wrong—like it had been disassembled, put in a blender, then reassembled by someone working from a rough sketch.

My bones feel hollow but somehow heavier at the same time. His muscles twitched and spasmed like they were trying to remember how to work.

Congratulations, young Harvest.

The dungeon presence's voice carried warmth and approval, which would have been more comforting if Jin wasn't currently covered in black gunk.

The initiation was successful. What you see covering your form is the physical manifestation of mortal limitations being expelled. A necessary, if unpleasant, part of the process.

"Necessary?" Jin spat, trying to get the taste of bile out of his mouth. The metallic tang lingered. "This is like being dunked in a septic tank! There has to be a more dignified way to do this!"

Seriously, couldn't evolution have found a cleaner way to upgrade the human body? His skin crawled where the substance touched it.

The purging of impurities has looked the same for ten thousand years, young one. Even the greatest Sage Emperors experienced this... humble beginning.

"Alright, alright." Jin wiped his mouth with the back of his hand, then immediately regretted it. "Just please tell me you're about to shower me."

The white expanse around him began to shift and change. Walls materialized from nothing, wooden floors appeared beneath his feet, and within seconds, Jin found himself in what looked like the world's coziest log cabin.

A fireplace crackled merrily in one corner, casting dancing shadows across comfortable furniture. Snow fell gently past frosted windows, and the air smelled like pine needles and hot chocolate instead of whatever ungodly substance had been covering him.

This is actually perfect. Jin's shoulders relaxed for the first time since awakening. How did it know I'm a sucker for cozy winter aesthetics?

His gear appeared on him instantly—clean, warm, and comfortable. Jin sank into the plush sofa with a groan of relief, his muscles finally unclenching.

"Much better. Did you do some magic on me to remove all that gunk?" he muttered, then looked around with genuine appreciation.

I gotta learn that spell. His mind was already racing ahead. There is no flexibility with sorceries... hopefully by this dungeon's end, I'll be ORDER I entity, then I will begin on my path to be an unparalleled mage!

Just wait. His hands clenched with anticipation. Demons or enemies with Essence's edge, I'll stack tons of buffs, enhancements, and nuke them all!

Apologies, but we would have to interrupt your amusing evil thoughts...

"Huh? How did you—"

Haha, there's no mind-reading young harvest, at least not with you. Your mind was formidable before the initiation on this path... now? I doubt anyone but a mind path archmage would be capable of doing that.

"Then..."

One advice, young harvester: practice your poker face. Anyone can read what's going on in your mind like an open book from your face.

Jin's cheeks heated. "I'll keep that in mind."

Shit! He touched his face reflexively. I thought Rudy was only pulling my leg. Am I that easy to read?

Jin sighed and stretched lightly, marveling at how different his body felt. Lighter somehow, but also more substantial. Like he was more real than he'd been before.

Okay, enough procrastinating. His pulse quickened with anticipation. Time to see exactly what that trial did to me.
