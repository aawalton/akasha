Dear Diary,

I know I've gotten kinda contemplative over my time here and now. Today I'm kinda wondering why.

I mean, I'm not precisely upset about it. Sometimes it kicks off my depression, yeah, but more often than not I think it's managed to turn that depression into some useful insights or even just let me spiral to a quiet restful state instead of crashing so hard I'm utterly useless for some indefinable amount of time.

Of course, I think maybe it's because of what I told Siobhan back before our date. Back when I first showed her what I really am. I know back then I thought of it as who I really am, but I've realized something between now and then. Who I am hasn't really Changed. I mean, okay, yeah, I've Changed, but every Change has just been natural growth and maturation over time. Self-discovery and shit like that.

Okay, yes, being isekaied isn't a natural kind of Change, but all the others since then have just been me learning more about myself. Either learning about my new physical form... Huh. I dunno if it's a physical form or metaphysical form or whatever, but learning about that, learning about who I am as a mom, as a partner, and as a person, and I think maybe most importantly of all who I want to be.

Well, who I want to be, who I think I should be. Maybe who I think I need to be.

Because yeah, I've got the feeling that at some point the avalanche that I've become will at some point... I don't want to say 'die out' or anything like that. Both because I'm trying to come to terms with being immortal and also because some part of me never really thought I'd live into my twenties, and I don't want to fuck that up now. But at some point I think the world, this world that I've found myself in as the incarnation of Change, will find a balance. Yeah, shit Changes, but that doesn't mean that nothing is durable, that nothing is reliable.

There's a word for it. Dynamism, maybe? The idea that eventually, with everything Changing all the time, people and societies and even the world itself adapts. Adapts not just to the Changes, but the fact that they're happening all the time. Instead of being the oak tree or the rock or the big solid wall, the world becomes the tumbleweed, or the bird in flight, or the willow tree, I guess. Yeah, it's a scarier way to live, but at the same time it's the only way to grow, to become more than we were, because for whatever reason I don't think Entropy was in that box with me.

Fuck, I wonder if Indech or one of his kids is technically Entropy. Daelbeth might be, I think flame is kinda entropic. Not sure.

At any rate, and yeah, my head is all over the place because I'm kinda sliding to that resting state after the ladies washed all that sand out, but along with having to adjust to being immortal, I'm also having to adjust from being fundamentally utterly incapable of altering the world in any meaningful way to being the, as noted, literal incarnation of Change. I can and in fact will Change the world no matter what I do, so I've got to make sure that not only are any conscious Changes I make to my world and myself ones that are, on balance, good ones, and by that I mean they leave the world a better place than I found it, I need to make sure that whatever my subconscious 'perfect paradise' is doesn't involve anybody getting consequences they don't deserve.

I almost said 'anybody having a bad time', but let's face it, at this point Tallulah's plate licker has earned herself some time in the oubliette at the very least.

Tabitha? I believe Hat is ready to make a deal with us.

"Maze? Could you take over for me here for a second?" When she took over showing the Troll girls how to simplify fractions, I stepped over to help some of the newer kids with simple repetitive stuff like times tables, then Co-Located one of me to the oubliette. "Has he been in there this whole time?"

Before she could answer, the Fae themself answered through the grating. "The bed is soft, the food is good, and none seek to harm me. Were I not in dire need of entertainment, I might stay here a fortnight without complaint."

Tallulah just shrugged, so I looked down into the oubliette through the grating. Something looked off until my sense of scale registered that Hat couldn't be more than three feet tall, and was probably closer to two. Struck by sudden curiosity, I asked, "do you mind if I Inspect you?"

They just stared at me for a long ten count, then shrugged. "I find it odd that you'd ask, but with you treating me so graciously I choose to agree to your request."

"Nice." I extruded the Shape out of my cheek, which had them blinking at me, and I got what I wanted at a glance. Adult Gnome. "No offense intended, but one of my kids is part Gnome, and nobody really knows anything about them, so along with helping the Archmagi with their Shaping research, you mind awful much if one of them asks you some medical questions?"

They pursed their lips. "Gnome is..." They shook their head. "I sense no intend to offend from you, but know that most of my kind would take offense by one not of my kind using that term."

"Oh. Shit. Well. That's unfortunate. You mind answering those questions?"

They shrugged. "So long as none experiment on me, and I'm free to seek some form of entertainment when my interrogators have no need of me, I see no difference in them asking about my Shaping or my health."

"Okay." I looked at Tallulah. "I'm guessing you've already worked out the details of the contract with them?"

She shook her head. "I thought it best that you be here for the discussion." She smiled at me. I'll handle the negotiations and wording, but if you're here, listening, Hat may feel us bound more strongly by the agreement.

I mean, we're not gonna fuck him over, right? At her raised eyebrow, i tried to clarify. We're going to deal in good faith with him, hold up our end of the bargain, and all that good stuff, right?

She nodded so slightly I wasn't sure I'd seen it. As you wish, let it be so.

"Okay, Hat. I'm here to witness and make sure we don't promise anything we can't deliver, but Tallulah's my legal expert, so she's gonna bargain this out with you, okay?"

If I'm not a slave in truth by the end of this day, I'll count myself lucky indeed." He paused, looked up at her, and said, "shall we begin?"
