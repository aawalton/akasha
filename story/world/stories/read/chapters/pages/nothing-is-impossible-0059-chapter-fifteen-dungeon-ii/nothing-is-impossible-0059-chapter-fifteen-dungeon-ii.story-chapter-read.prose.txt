Lars looked between the two of them. “You guys know each other? Great! That will make our lives much easier.” Atlas tried to say something, anything, but Lars just whisked them away, heading straight for the tier 3 portal. “It’s time to kill things!”

Atlas just followed. Not really knowing how to feel. Here was the girl he had dated for two years, the girl he had wanted to marry. But now… Atlas felt lost. He had changed so much, and he very much believed Lesli had too. Otherwise she wouldn’t be wearing mage robes and be adventuring in another world.

Atlas felt torn. On one hand, he still had feelings for Lesli, how could he not after so long of being together? But on the other hand… He loved growing stronger, and he felt like that was far more important to the current him than any relationship.

As he wrestled with himself internally, they reached the tier 3 portal, an attendant looking over the six of them and nodding before letting them all go through.

Atlas felt his body jolt painfully and he found himself standing on a white pedestal some feet above an even brighter white ground. He glanced around and saw everyone else was also standing on pedestal’s similar to his, and they hopped off without giving it much thought. After some deliberation, Atlas followed suit.

Lar’s clapped his hands. “Okay people, let's get this show on the road. I’ll take point, Atlas on my left, Jay on my right, preferably with stealth active. Dane, summon one of your ranged creatures if you would, and stay in the back to help buff with Nex. Lesli, if you could stay with them, that would be great.” He looked like he was about to leave it at that but then he noticed Atlas.

“Oh, Atlas, is this your first dungeon?” Atlas nodded, and he went on. “Then here is what you should expect. Be on the constant look out for traps, calling them out if you see them. This is a tier 3 dungeon, so there are three floors. At the end of each floor is either a miniboss or a puzzle, it depends on the dungeon and floor. At the end of the final floor is the boss. Kill the boss, we get rewarded. As I already said, you will be on my left, and when we start combat, let them first charge me then have at them. Sound good?”

Atlas furrowed his eyebrows before nodding. “So it's similar to a video game?”

Jay shrugged. “In some ways, yes, that is a great way to think of it. Just remember this is real life and you can die here. You can’t respawn, and if you mess up, it could lead to one of us dying. So a video game… just with much higher stakes.” He said, flashing Atlas a grin.

Atlas blanched, coming back down to earth. When Jay said it like that… he felt a lot more pressure than he had before.

Atlas took his position to the left of Lars, and Jay went opposite him.

Dane started to chant in a strange language, and Atlas felt like he could understand the intentions but not the actual words. He was… summoning something? After at least a full minute of chanting, there was a golden flash and a creature appeared in front of Dane.

It appeared to be some type of huge rodent, and it looked slightly similar to a squirrel. It was deep black fur with golden markings on its body, and it stood at a towering seven feet tall. It had a long bushy tail and carried a large rock about the size of a bowling ball. It was covered in more of the golden markings and glowed slightly.

Atlas used Master Scan.

Atlas froze at being compared to Lesli, having to confront those emotions once again in very little time. He mentally wrestled with himself for a few more seconds before steeling himself and pushing it away. It was time to kill some things.

Atlas followed Lars as they went through the first door, finding themself in a tunnel that looked to be 15 feet wide and just as high. The tunnel was not straight, and winded back and forth every so often, making it impossible to see all the way down it, even with enhanced eyesight. They started down the tunnel, with Jay taking the lead temporarily to look for traps, as he apparently had a skill to help with that.

There was only one trap that they ran into, and Jay quickly deactivated it, cutting the tripwire that would have activated the trap with his daggers. His daggers were sharp enough that he could cut the wire without applying any pressure, therefore not activating the trap.

They eventually came to a fork in the tunnel, with three paths to choose from.

Lars turned to look back at all of them. “Any preferences on which one we take? Or will we just pick one at random?”

Lesli shook her head. “Manasight is telling me that the middle path has the highest concentration of mana, so that path is most likely the way to the next floor.”

Lars nodded, and they continued down the path in silence. Atlas felt a sudden spike of dread and instantly reacted, summoning his scythe and activating Strength Form, swinging in an arc above him and killing three kobolds that had attempted to ambush them, jumping out from behind a bend in the tunnel and trying to land on top of them.

They died in a spray of blood and gore, but the fight didn’t end there, as a group of 10 or so of the creatures charged them from further down the tunnel.
