This time, even as his temple spiked in pain he forced himself to keep focusing. The cone of mana that exploded from his knuckles was even narrower than before, almost forming a lance.

In an instant, the goblin’s spine, flesh, and a portion of its lungs behind them were vaporised by the crackling mana. It collapsed to its knees with a garbled screech. In its final moments it tried to stab at Ronan, but its dagger splashed uselessly into the mud.

Having a huge mana pool was great, but if he couldn’t recover it once it was drained what was the point? Combining regeneration with the boost from selecting the mage class, he hoped he might be able to utilise all the waiting periods to practice and still have mana left over to fight the goblin hordes.

Wait a minute. 26 Copper Credits? Ronan realised in confusion. The reward for completing the stage was higher than it should be.

Seeing that Keith had actually stood up for himself this time around, Ronan felt confident in leaving him to handle the first few lesser goblin warriors. His priority would of course be the shaman.

That fleshbeast is horrific and the stench alone would make me never want to fight one again, but if I want to make the most progress then perhaps I should let the shaman summon it, he realised. It would give me extra experience and mastery that I sorely need.

Ronan kicked out like lightning, cracking the goblin’s shin. It wailed in pain but was soon silenced when a lethal one-two combo of punches caved in its ribs and then liquefied its heart.
