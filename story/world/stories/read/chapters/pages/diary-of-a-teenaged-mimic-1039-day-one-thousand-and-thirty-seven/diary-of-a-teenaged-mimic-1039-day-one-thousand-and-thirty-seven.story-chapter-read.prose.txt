Dear Diary,

I really don't know what to say about that whole assed revelation yesterday. I don't know what to think about it, either. I mean, I'm gonna. Say shit. Think about shit. I'm not sure whether I could avoid doing either one if I tried.

Okay, I know I can keep my mouth shut on occasion when I'm fully in control of all of my faculties and I'm at my most mature and one of my ladies has applied some form of covering to my mouth in the form of one of their important body parts that I'm unlikely to nom off and swallow. I can't tell whether to find it really touching that they enjoy my word salad so much they'll leave my mouth free even now that I've figured out how to turn things up to eleven with that 'Gift Ecstasy' Skill or to feel super smug about the fact that they'll forgo word salad to let me get to Gifting some Ecstasy.

As for not thinking about shit, that pretty much requires similar input. Okay, it requires me to have some kind of stressor, because eustress is still stress, that completely overrides my inevitable penchant for chewing over every fuckin' thing that happens to me. Weird, I can sorta remember that I didn't do that nearly so much right after I arrived. I mean, I still did it, but I was mostly just, I dunno, cataloging the new place I found myself in. Focusing on keeping my head down and not looking like a target.

Surviving.

Yeah, I think something about the Battle of the Bay is what really knocked me out of that. Yeah, that shitty self-help book full of stupid quotes is what I attributed it to at the time, but the thought that I had summoned up one of my own personal icons of power from back in the day and used it to obliterate something that the Gods themselves had to focus on bowel control when they heard about it definitely showed me without too much doubt that I am no longer constantly on the raggedy edge of dying from being expendable.

I am well aware of how much ass I'd kicked before that, but that one was kinda big. I set up the whole endgame scenario, it went to shit because the Mother of Hole Spawn was, as noted, something the Gods themselves feared, and I won anyway.

To be fair, Dragons and Undead are the two things we Deities have trouble enforcing our will upon.

Shit, I'd thrown down with Kaiju and felt injury and pain, but never a thought of 'this is it, this is how I die again'. All kinds of worries about the mortality of my loved ones, but never really for myself. Shit, I think prior to that I wasn't admitting to it anyhow, but it was lurking there somewhere under the surface.

That's yet another reason Yule hit me so hard, I think. I'd been riding that high of 'I am eternal' so long that the clear indication that I am in fact metaphorically two farts and half a sigh from dead shook a deep certainty that I hadn't even realized I had.

Because at the end of the day, we're not lumps of meat, metaphoric or otherwise. We're thoughts and dreams and memories. People, I mean. Not even talking exclusively about Human Adjacent people here, either, because my Kraken dreamed so hard of a Deity that they brought one into being. Twice, if you count getting me out of that fuckin' box. Eh. I guess I can use 'people' to talk about sophont beings and Human Adjacent to talk about the people who are, for lack of a better term, mostly humanoid. Because sure as shit there are gonna be both Human Adjacent folks who are less humanoid, like maybe Centaurs or Naga or shit like that, and other non-Human Adjacent sophont beings. I'm including most Deities when I say that, too. But compared to what I ought to be, I am... Shit, I ought to be, I dunno, Steve Rogers or Kal El or Jonathan Osterman or some shit, and compared to what I ought to be, I'm fuckin' Stephen Hawking. One fingertip interacting with the world and shit. No, I do not get nosebleeds. Yes, I kinda wish I did, because that would be so much better than that sense of, I dunno, un-becoming. It's like falling asleep, maybe, except I'm not actually losing consciousness. It's almost like if I fully gave in to it, I'd totally cease to exist.

I kinda wonder if I ever let go that far, the rest of me would wake up.
