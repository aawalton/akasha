So when I tell you that I have never seen anything stranger than the room I awoke in. I really want you to understand the gravitas of that statement. This place was strange in a way I didn’t really have words for.

The walls were a pearlescent white that somehow shone with such a bright purity it hurt my eyes. If the oddities had ended at the walls, I would have thought myself in some ascended realm or heavenly antechamber. As only janitors of the higher realms could ever hope to maintain such clean whiteness.

Not only were shining white walls not the end of the strangeness, they were barely the beginning. I lay naked on some sort of cushioned table, big enough to barely fit my torso and my head. My lower body and limbs hung awkwardly over the sides. The air around me tasted dead somehow, like I had entered a region so desolate that no Qi permeated the land.

Above me floated what looked like a cluster of glowing purple gems. It hovered in the center of the room just below a ceiling that was the same unnatural white as the walls. As I stared at the gem cluster, it seemed to move, to rearrange itself. I narrowed my eyes suspiciously at the thing as it gave off the distinct impression it was somehow watching me back.

Around me were dozens of similar tables, but on each one lay an infant wrapped in fine-looking blankets and shielded by a strange glass dome. Beside each was a strange esoteric box, made of a material I didn't recognize and displaying shifting glyphs in a language I couldn't read.

A trio of two men and one woman dressed head to toe in muted deep green robes? They looked like robes, but pulled tight enough to act as a shirt and pants despite a lack of a belt. Their faces were covered from the nose down in some sort of thin white mask. The tallest of the men was armed with a bizarre and obviously esoteric dagger. The weapon’s shape was cylindrical, ending in a single thin stabbing, needle-like point.

I think a dome like the others must have adorned my own table at one point, as shards of glass littered my naked body, and the three masked people only seemed to be noticing my presence now. Though they immediately gathered together to approach me. Placing a hand on the identical glyph box that sat next to my cot, I steadied myself and sat up.

I realised almost immediately that something was wrong with me. My body was shorter and thinner than it should have been. I wasn’t a baby like the room’s other occupants, but nor was I the powerfully built man I had been before awakening here. Running a hand through my hair, I found it a scraggly mess, barely long enough to reach my eyes. It might have been less alarming than the lithe and youthful body I found myself in, but not feeling the long and lustrous black mane I was accustomed to was oddly disheartening.

Slipping off the table onto the floor, I stepped between the shards of shattered glass and inspected the room around me more closely. While I had never seen anything at all like it before, the proliferation of infants gave some context. This room had to be a nursery of some sort. Granted, one the likes of which I couldn’t imagine if you described it to me, but a nursery all the same.

At least that’s what I thought before the strange living gem cluster above me launched a beam of light directly into my head. It was reminiscent of the energy attacks certain cultivators and sorcerers are known to favor, but very thin and far faster-moving than any Qi-based technique I knew of.

The beam hit me before I had even registered what was happening. Now, in my defense, I had just woken up, not to mention I had found myself in a body not my own. So It is fair to say that my reflexes weren’t exactly finely honed that day. Still, I know what I saw, and that beam of energy was fast.

Evidently, it wasn’t an attack, as while my head felt slightly warm where the energy had struck my forehead, nothing else happened. At least that's what I initially thought, soon, I realized the gems must be the home of some particularly powerful spirit, ghost, or demon that now had me in its grasp. It was the only explanation for my sudden inability to move.

When I say I couldn’t move, I want you to understand that I couldn’t even breathe or blink. I was utterly frozen in place, staring straight ahead…surrounded by babies. As though this alone hadn’t added to the surrealness of my situation enough, the spirit or whatever it was began showing me what had to be the most pointless illusions I have ever been subjected to. Before my eyes appeared words? Why?

Unable to move, I had no choice but to stand there and read this nonsense. It didn’t tell me anything I cared about, the scrolling words talking about my superior physical state, something about an infant's body being unable to contain my soul. I don’t really remember, as pretty soon I felt my mind glaze over at all this writing. In the past if I needed to read something long and boring like this, I would have a beautiful girl do it, ideally one with honey in her voice and a cheeky “Fuck me” smile.
