Every feather on Liora's body was shivering. The ritual circle was complete. Renir was standing in the very center of it while the sun shone down on him almost perfectly from above. They had maybe a few more minutes until they would have to begin.

The antkin soldiers were gripping their spears in their hands, ready to jump into action at any moment, while Liora fluttered in the air.

She was extremely jealous of Caspian at the moment, who was still sleeping in his basin. They did try to wake him for the ritual, but the merman was so exhausted he wouldn't wake. Then again, he had already done his part in brewing the potion.

Apparently it had been a really complex one. It was doubtful anyone without experience in potion brewing could have done it. Since Caspian didn't have any role during the ritual itself, it was alright to let him sleep.

Although having a healer around while the gate to the damned underworld opened would have been helpful.

"Cee, it is time!" Renir announced.

Liora took a deep breath. Everyone tensed, keeping their eyes on Renir. Cee glanced around one last time, Smokey flicking his tail left and right nervously.

Finally, the spider touched the ritual circle with her leg, activating it. It lit up with bright blue light. If the sun hadn't been so bright already, it would have blinded everybody.

The ritual circle crackled for a moment, the light flickering. But after a while it stabilized, and the sand below the ritual gradually turned dark.

"The potion, Liora!" Renir commanded.

Liora already held the potion in her talons. She pulled out the cork and flew up to Renir, pouring the potion over his rocky body. The nasty purple liquid flowed down his shiny surface, covering it. The sand beneath was completely black at that point, making the blue shining lights of the ritual circle all the more vibrant.

Seeing his perfectly beautiful and shiny surface get marred by that nasty sludge set Liora on edge. She wanted to polish him clean. But she couldn't interrupt the ritual.

Suddenly, the ground beneath the ritual began to shift. As if the sand was being sucked in somewhere. Initially, it was slow, but before long all of the sand was being sucked somewhere as if somebody had pulled out the plug in a bathtub.

Liora was about to swoop down and grab Renir, but oddly enough he remained where he had been, just floating in the air. No wait, not in the air. He was standing on Cee's web; she just couldn't see it as clearly because it was shining.

As soon as all the sand was sucked into a hole, Renir began to recite the incantation. Curious, Liora positioned herself in a way that she could peer inside the hole. It was hard to see beyond the shining ritual circle, but she could tell that there was a whole dark landscape in that hole. Likely something straight out of hell with a green river flowing somewhere far below, emitting an eerie green glow.

Renir continued reciting the incantation, and while the obvious gate to hell was creepy as hell, it seemed like everything would go well. The potion on his surface was beginning to bubble, which Liora assumed to be part of the ritual.

But then a loud, unearthly scream came from the hole below. Liora plummeted to the ground, having lost control over her wings in the short moment she heard the scream. Everyone on the ground had either backed away or were crouched down.

The ant soldiers recovered first, clutching their weapons and stepping closer to the ritual circle. Liora was next, flying closer to Renir, readying to grab him should the need arise. But she was too slow.

A black tentacle appeared from the hole below, wrapping itself around Renir. Before anyone could react, the tentacle had pulled Renir down into the hole below.

"RENIR!" Liora screeched and dove after him. She momentarily shifted to her magpie form, so as not to ruin the ritual circle with her body, and flew after the tentacle.

The air instantly shifted the moment Liora had flown into what was supposed to be the underworld. The air was hot and heavy like before a thunderstorm, but at the same time it was chilling like the cold northern wind. It was the kind of feeling you get while being sick with a high fever. You were simultaneously too hot but also freezing.

The thing that had grabbed Renir could only be described as an unholy abomination. Its body was elongated, almost like that of a snake, but it was fat in some places and thin in others. There were countless tentacles coming out from seemingly random places, while the mouth with several rows of sharp teeth sat in the middle of this mass, screeching.

It didn't take a genius to deduce that this was a demon.

It was pulling Renir towards its mouth, clearly intent to swallow him. Perhaps Liora wouldn't have even spotted Renir, because the tentacle was so thoroughly wrapped around him, but she saw distinct flashes of light. Once. Twice. Thrice.

He wants me to recite the incantation? Now?!

Liora had no clue how the ritual could even work if they were pulled into the underworld, but she knew that now wasn't the time to question Renir. He was counting on her after all.

She began chanting the accursed incantation, relying purely on her magpie memory and Resonance Mimicry skill. It helped her to chant it loud and clear, resonating across the dead landscape. A series of soul-wrenching wails echoed from the green river as if the souls of the dead had replied to her chant.

But Liora had no time to be creeped out by this place. In addition to chanting, she released a strike of Holy Light at the tentacle monster, striking its body. As expected, it was super effective, and the monster screeched.

The loud, disorienting scream made Liora pause in her chant, and she felt that the magical power that had been quietly gathering within her dissipated into the thick air.

Renir flashed three times again, urging her to start anew.

But the tentacle demon was angrier than before, shooting its many tentacles at Liora while still pulling Renir towards its mouth. Liora chanted while she shrank down to magpie size. If one of the tentacles got too close to her, she would shoot it with Holy Light, forcing it to back away.

But unlike the corrupted creatures who would pretty much be destroyed by exposure to holy magic, this creature merely didn't like the holy magic; it wasn't actually doing any significant damage. Kind of like one not wanting to get mud on their freshly cleaned clothes.

Only in this case, it was the reverse. The nasty demon creature didn't want to get cleansed. But being clean wouldn't kill it.

Perhaps, if Liora didn't have to chant the damned incantation, she could combine Purify with Wind Blast to make this thing back off completely. But her mental capacity was full, not to mention that she was still dodging all the tentacles.

Renir was almost within its mouth. Liora shot Holy Light straight at him, sending the gem flying out of the monster's grasp.

"LIORA, we have to get back to the circle! Ignore this thing!"

Easier said than done.

The tentacled demon raged, all of its tentacles shooting towards Liora. She was still chanting as she shot Holy Light after Holy Light, pretty much in every direction with no regard to actually striking the monster.

She managed to snatch Renir before any of the tentacles, but at this point, the chaos was so pronounced that she was barely able to keep up with what was going on. Liora transformed back into the dracoryx form and, with the powerful flaps of her wings, flew at the ritual circle.

It looked like a small opening in the underworld sky, completely alien in this landscape. She could see Cee peering into the opening nervously, Smokey behind her.

Somehow the eyeless demon could tell that Liora was about to get away with Renir in tow and concentrated all of its tentacles on her. She wove to the left, to the right, up and down, often folding her wings to avoid the tentacles.

A tentacle grabbed her just as she was about to reach the surface. The chant was almost complete.

But Liora refused to let this demon ruin everything. She used a powerful dose of Purify on the nasty limb. This time the monster instantly shriveled in on itself, all of its tentacles retreating.

It gave Liora the chance to fly back into the desert, squeezing between the ritual circle in her magpie form, just as she finished the chant.

"Let go!" Renir commanded, and Liora released Renir straight on the ritual web. The gem fell, and for a moment she feared he might just fall through.

But he hovered in the air, his now purple surface bubbling more intensely. How did that potion not come off with everything that happened? It was almost as if he had been dyed into that disgusting color. He wouldn't remain that way, would he?

But the light of the ritual circle concentrated on Renir, and she could hear cracks coming from his body. The purple mass around him seemed to bloat larger, with more cracking coming from it.

"ARGH!" Renir yelled out in pain, a horrible sound.

But then one of the tentacles emerged from below the underworld. The damned demon had recovered.

"No, you don't!" Liora shot towards the tentacle like a bullet. She combined her Raptor Lunge skill with Purify.

[You've successfully formed a new skill <Purifying Lunge>.]

She slashed her talons, glowing with intense light, right into the tentacle. It tried to retreat, but Liora wasn't going to let it. It had chosen to stick its tentacles where it shouldn't. Liora decided to make it regret this decision.

Without any mercy, she slashed with her talons, separating the offending appendage into pieces. The demon in the underworld screeched and pulled the remains of its tentacle back down.

"And don't come back!" Liora shouted, throwing the nasty tentacle pieces she slashed off into the underworld. There was no need to sully this world with filth.

The ritual light grew more and more intense. So intense that Liora couldn't even see Renir anymore. It appeared that sun rays from above were making the light brighter, while some kind of energy was coming from below.

Without her Prismatic Twilight Vision, Liora likely wouldn't have even noticed this, but there was definitely faint light energy coming from above and some dark energy coming from below. It converged around Renir and swirled around him.

For a while, all they heard was the cracking of his body. Liora nervously looked at the still-open underworld portal. The tentacle monster had retreated for now, but what if it was just preparing for another attack?

Liora was ready to use Purifying Rend at any moment.

Suddenly, the light around exploded into blue fireworks, and the ritual circle began to burn away, the blue flames going from inside out. The gate to the underworld was also subsequently closing.

Just a bit more.

But obviously, the demon hadn't given up. Tentacles shot out from below at lightning speed, much faster than before. Liora was about to strike at them when, all of a sudden, the tentacles seized.

The ritual circle was burning up, but before it could fully disappear, something shot out of the gate. Not the tentacled abomination. Not fully, at least. Several of its tentacles had been cut clean off and were wriggling in the desert sand.

But up in the air flew a demon. A real demon. He had large curved horns on his forehead and bat-like wings almost as large as Smokey's wings, and a thin tail swishing left and right. The demon had light purple skin, and his eyes were an unnatural red.

His fangs gleamed in the light as the demon's mouth spread into a grin.

Oh shit!
