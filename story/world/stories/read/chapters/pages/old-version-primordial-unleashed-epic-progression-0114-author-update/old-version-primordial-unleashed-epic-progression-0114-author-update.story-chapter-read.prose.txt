Hello readers! Thank you for reading so far into this series.

I am currently concentrating on re-writing and editing Book 1 so that it is ready for Amazon release. There are many new scenes added, plot and characters expanded on, and some major changes.

One major change comes at the halfway mark, where a lot of reader feedback indicated that the twist was poorly foreshadowed and unsatisfying. I have come to agree with this sentiment, and so re-writen chapters 25-28 thoroughly, and added an extra chapter in there to flesh out the pacing.

Now, instead of receiving a random, unexplained and unresolved vision of a cyclops, Skippii is indeed the subject of an enemy invocatinon, which seeks to ensanre his soul; the hook for such an invocation was planted early during the struggle with the heretic magus at the farmstead in chapter 3; it comes to fruition here, and causes Skippii to go beserk once he comes face to face with Cosmipox, the dark god, himself.

The resulting damage is much more severe than in previous drafts - many legionnaires are hurt by his outburst.

This re-write accomplishes a few things: it establishes the 'big bad' earlier on in the book, and has a through-line of logic to explain why it happened, and is eventually resolved.

There are a few major changes being made to Book 1, and generally a thorough edit of the prose to bring it as close to perfection as I can before Primordial unleashed Book 1 is published in February 2026.

As such, I have not had as much time to work on Book 2. Furthermore, I wish to edit what I have written so far in order to bring it up to scratch.

So, it is with a heavy heart that I must say that, on this cliffhanger of a Schism in the Heavens, that I put chapter releases on hiatus until I have the publishing of Book 1 out of the way and done with.

Once I start writing new chapters again, they will  be uploaded here immediately. I am expecting that to happen in February.

Thank you for understanding, and thank you to all the comments which help me improve this series before it goes to Amazon for publishing, and my fate is sealed!
