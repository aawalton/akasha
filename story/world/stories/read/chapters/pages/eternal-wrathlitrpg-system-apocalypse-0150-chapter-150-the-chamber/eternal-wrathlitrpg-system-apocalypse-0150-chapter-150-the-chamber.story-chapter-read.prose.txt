The path narrowed when James entered—barely two meters wide. It meandered, forming twists and turns as the group traveled upward. The group glued their eyes to the ceiling, the floor, and the walls, expecting monsters to jump at them at the first opportunity. The skittering of the black cockroaches inside really strained their nerves.

“I am getting claustrophobic here,” Kouki muttered, trying to crack a joke but failing.

“Watch out!” Andrew yelled. He ducked down, shielding his body while James raised his shield against a liquid that squirted out from one of the corridors. Some of the liquid splashed onto his shield, while some landed on his limbs. Thankfully, James was in the front, so Kouki and Andrew were spared the toxic liquid. James felt a stinging pain in his flank.

[You have been hit by a corrosive liquid (Level 3).]

The system notified him of the sudden attack. Then, from the corridor, two treemen emerged. Unlike the ones before the gate, they didn’t use shields. One had an elongated mouth—it was the one that had shot liquid at James earlier—while the other was dual-wielding two swords.

James ignored the stinging pain and breathed fire at the ambusher. He then lunged, his sword glowing golden as it carved an arc against the sword-wielding treeman. Andrew was also quick on his feet. Despite the narrow passage, his spear glowed golden as he launched it at the acid spitter.

The spitter managed to fire several more shots at James before a golden flash embedded itself in its head. Kouki then lunged at it, bisecting it from the side into three pieces with a quick sword skill. It was perfect teamwork—no plan was needed. They worked together seamlessly despite the new environment.

“Fuck…” James felt his body burning as the acid bored a hole into his flesh.

“James. Should I buy you a potion?”

“Nah. I can fix this using my biomass,” James muttered. He then looked over at the treeman, activating his Chimera skill. The treemen from the gate had been too damaged for his Chimera to do anything, but this sword-wielding one only had a hole in its chest.

[Would you like to modify your body? Y/n]

[Available body parts:Arboreal Knight’s arms (Regeneration, Fire weakness) (Arm slot)Arboreal Knight’s legs (Regeneration, Fire weakness) (Leg slot)Arboreal Knight’s head (Regeneration, Fire weakness) (Head slot)]

[Would you like to spend 300 coins to repair your Armor of Stillness?]

“Ugh… fire weakness. I didn’t know that. They didn’t seem to have such a weakness…” James groaned. Regeneration was a good thing to have, but unless he had the entire body, it wouldn’t matter.

“Yes. Fix my armor. By the way, my firefur has fire resistance. Will I lose it if I add these arboreal body parts?”

[Negative. Your body will still be fire-resistant. However, once injured, the fire weakness will take effect.]

“Great! Then let’s start with the legs!” James blinked. He had expected his Chimera to disregard the fire resistance his fur had, but apparently that wasn’t the case. With this, the demerits of the new body parts were essentially nonexistent.

Black motes of light spread out from James and engulfed the treeman. The other treeman—the one with the acid-spitter technique—was too damaged for his Chimera to plunder, but he could still get biomass from it. All in all, he got around 300 biomass in addition to his new body parts.

“Let’s move on,” Andrew said. His eyes darted toward the countdown looming in the corner of his vision. Only 24 hours left. They had to clear this dungeon in 24 hours.

They didn’t know how far they’d walked. The paths all looked the same to them. Sometimes the path curved upward, like climbing a hill; sometimes it curved downward. Then, after a while, the path split to the left and right.

To the left, James found a treasure chest. His heart leaped with joy as he approached it.

“We don’t have time to open it…” Andrew groaned. “There might be traps.”

“Just ten minutes. Besides, we might find something important there,” James replied. Using the usual ritual, James attacked the chest with his ice magic. The chest opened before James could even approach it. Arrows laced with green goo at their tips shot out from the chest. Despite James being a good three meters away, one of the arrows still flew toward him.

The arrow hit his shield, and the green goo actively corroded it. Then came the skittering. Numerous black spots crawled out from the crevice of the chest. James could feel his heart jump out of his chest. The damn cockroaches were inside!

“Fuck!” James cursed. He immediately used both his ice dome and fire breath in panic as the cockroaches skittered, hopping and leaping toward him. The others immediately took a step back.

After exterminating all the bugs, he peered into the chest and found a crystal orb inside.

[Congratulations, you have obtained an Arboreal Key.]

“What is it used for?” James asked, but the system returned his question with silence. He started to miss its information classified catchphrase, but apparently, the system had stricter limitations in hard-difficulty dungeons.

“It sounds important,” Kouki muttered.

“Yeah, you probably found a key of some sort,” Andrew nodded. “Nice work, James. Let’s take it. We’ll probably need it later.”

As they dove deeper, the path twisted more and more, splitting and merging every ten minutes. They fought various monsters at every twist and turn.

“Watch out!” Andrew threw his spear at a humongous larva with a turtle shell for a skull. Despite using his spear skill, it bounced harmlessly off its skull. The larva charged forward, and James had to block it using ice walls. Several ice spears stabbed into its back and belly. Thick red blood gushed from the wounds before it died.

In any other environment, this larva would have been harmless, easily beatable. One only needed to attack its flank. But in this narrow tunnel, it proved to be a rather tricky opponent.

James frowned as he saw the bug bleed to death. Unlike bugs on Earth, this one had blood and flesh inside—just like mammals.

James was astonished that this single larva alone gave him over 1,000 biomass.

“Damn, that count wanted you guys to eat that? Well, that larva was easily five times bigger than a cow…” James scrunched his face. Nutrition-wise, he got the idea, but he wasn’t willing to risk it.

“I could have sold it for ten coins, James. I’ll let the shop eat it instead.” Kouki said.

“Well, I’ve eaten several exotic foods in Brazil and SEA. Some grubs are delicious,” Andrew replied, “but I dunno about eating a flesh-eating bug. I’ll pass. Let’s continue.”

Over five hours later, they finally reached a chamber where a door with three concaves resided. There, a large cockroach the size of a car stood guard. James didn’t waste any time. With a roar that represented his hatred for these creatures, he threw his axe at it before it could even react. Just like the larva, the head was immune to damage. But since his axe had elemental damage imbued into it, frost spread as the axe bounced off its hard exoskeleton.

“Come in, Ralph!” Kouki motioned for his dragon, who had followed them from behind. Unlike the treemen, the frost was not enough to kill the massive cockroach—but it did slow it down. Kouki released a few flying slashes at it. The frozen parts of the bug broke off with each slash, revealing pulsing crimson flesh inside.

James followed up with his own flying slashes, but unless he hit the frozen parts, his strikes merely bounced off. Knowing he needed his axe, he leaped toward the rampaging cockroach.

“Watch out!” Andrew and Kouki leaped to the side as the beast tried to run them over. Its mandibles clicked like a clock. Thanks to his jet thruster, James reached his axe in a second. But as he was about to use his Boomerang skill again, the cockroach squirted glowing green goo from its rear.

James felt dread. He quickly cast an ice shield. But unlike normal acid, this one was also glowing hot, and his ice shield melted. James recast his shield and even raised his beetle shield.

Thankfully, that was enough to kill its momentum the acid spit dropped to the ground, still oozing smoke. A blue glow shone from his axe. James locked onto the massive target further away. Another strike at the cockroach, and it should be over. However, contrary to his expectations, the cockroach flapped its wings. As it landed on the ceiling, several smaller larvae emerged from above. Those larvae crawled onto the wounded cockroach, merging their bodies with it and regenerating it.

“Shit, there is no end to this,” Kouki cursed.

“We’ll have to kill it in one blow. James, on your mark!” Andrew prepared his spear.

The cockroach was a monster—it didn’t retreat and instead charged toward the enemy it found most dangerous. As it observed its surroundings, it clicked its mandibles and rushed at James. James met it head-on. Activating his skill, V-Slash, he slammed his axe into the monster.

The cockroach wasn’t wounded, but two hits from elemental weapons had frozen its front solid. Then a golden meteor slammed into it, shattering its head on impact. Kouki followed up with another sword skill at its torso, bisecting the beast in two.

“We won!” Kouki yelled.

James was about to complain to Kouki for destroying the corpse—there might have been parts he could plunder—but as he approached the cockroach, a purple barrier shone around it, protecting it. James tilted his head, wondering why the system protected it. Meanwhile, Andrew moved toward the gate.

“Good work, everyone. Let’s see…” Andrew inspected the gate. “Damn it. We need two more of those orbs.”

James frowned. It looked like they would need to search every nook and cranny of the tree. James glanced at the countdown—sixteen more hours before the crimson moon shone above them.

“We must hurry,” James muttered.

They all nodded and departed, leaving the protected corpse behind.
