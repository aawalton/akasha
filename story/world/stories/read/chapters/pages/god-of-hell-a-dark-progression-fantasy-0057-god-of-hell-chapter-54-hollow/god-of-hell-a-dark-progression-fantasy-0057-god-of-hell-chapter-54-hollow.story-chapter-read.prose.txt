Chapter 54: Hollow.

The day was normal. The sun shone down brightly upon Dolore, the wind blew hotly, and the birds fluttered casually underneath the purple clouds.

Gunther looked at the hole in the ground. The grave and the casket within it. Selvas was being buried today. And the day was normal.

There was a wrongness to it, as if the Inferno was mocking him. He was the town healer; he’d seen several die, pets, men, women, children—entire families wiped away in a night at the hands of some disease that ravaged through the town one season.

This was different somehow. It was cruel to think, cruel to know, cruel to hold on to. But Gunther didn’t care. This, Selvas’ death, was different. And it angered him that the Inferno refused to recognise that.

“He should be here,” Alfie hissed. He was standing next to him now, the giant looked smaller somehow, like he’d had half of him burnt away and discarded. “He should fucking be here,” he repeated.

But Nero wasn’t. He hadn’t been the one to bring back Selvas’ body, and he hadn’t stepped a foot in Stradale in the week since her death.

Gunther was under no illusions that the boy wasn’t suffering through a sea of despair, maybe he even had good reasons for not sending a letter or message of any sorts. But Alfie was right. He should be here.

Nero stood in the Hell Gate chamber. He looked right, up, and down. But he never looked left. That was where Selvas had died, and he knew the stain of her blood would still be on the walls and floor.

In one hand was the heart of a Hollow, the one Defrid had sent to kill him, only fitting then that it be the thing that brought retribution to her doorstep.

In the other was a blade, Cain’s. That one he’d found when he got down here.Wrapped around it was a note that read ‘Make her pay.’

That he would do. That, he had no choice but to do.

“Are you ready?” Ember asked, voice a low thing.“I am,” Nero answered, and there was no other truth that could ever have been as strong as that.

Nero pulled the lever. Stepped into the circle. Looked to the left. And then his world flashed white.
