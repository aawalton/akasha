/The fourth floor is the Graveyard.

That’s its job, metaphorically and physically. Dead climbers end up there. Corpses have a way of ending up there, and some think the tower keeps record of every single climber who’s ever died in it.

I say that’s crud. But there is some truth to it.

If you die on the fourth floor, it spawns a gravestone with your name, and brings your rotting corpse back out to haunt the living. This means that in addition to the normal undead, who’ve long forgotten who they used to be, we get people who know how to cast some magic, or with physical skills.

It’s the graveyard, because the fifth floor is the end of where most people can go. It’s where reckless idiots go to die, and when they do, their bodies get thrown down here. They get consumed by the rot that plagues this place, and they rise again.

So, climbers. If you’re on the fourth, find a city fast. Ascension wells always drop you near cities, because water goes bad fast, and food is scarce here. Find some walls. Sell the corpse-prizes. Grow until you earn your authority.

And then climb higher./

-Windwrought, level 85 Galebringer, entry in the Climber’s Compendium.
