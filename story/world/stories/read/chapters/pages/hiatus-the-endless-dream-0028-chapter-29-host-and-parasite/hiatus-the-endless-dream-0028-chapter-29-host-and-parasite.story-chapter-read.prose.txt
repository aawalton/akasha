I kept my hand firmly on Theon's back, and I hoped that through the silence, he would still feel its warmth. There was paltry solace in the act, but when faced with absence, I liked to think something was preferable to nothing.

The boy was young. Thoughtful. Malleable in mind. A blessing and a curse, and ultimately, what had me bend my will towards action. It was a cruel thing that I had done, but I had been cruel all my life, and would've been crueler still to leave him in the world we were born to.

The human mind was not built for only joy, yet it was what God enforced all the same. Ceaseless, unequivocal joy in all things, even those horrid to others and oneself. I felt joy when my wife died and when my son was killed. There was purpose and pride when I churned children into the waiting arms of the priesthood. I did it all with a smile. I was neither courageous nor wise. There was always something mixed with the joy, disgust, shame, and fear, but it never overpowered the happiness, so I continued with what was easy. I smiled still. Listened to their confessions and sent them to the Mourning Circle for absolution while calling it a mercy.

I believed it was a Fiend's word when I found a crack. A small space, of no importance, on the side of a random room I entered in the crypts. The feeling didn't follow strongly, but what I felt underneath the constant elation became impossible to ignore—despite my consistent efforts. I screamed at the tear, prayed for forgiveness, and cursed my fate. There was even a point I contemplated taking my life as recompense.

Heretics were rarely outright killed; instead, they were sent to the Mourning Circle and given a trial with a group of their peers. Those who survived became Visitors and were banished from the world. Those who died returned to God to be reborn and reforged anew. Most never attempted to live. They sought the embrace of God. So I waited until someone I knew who would always fight was branded as a Heretic. Then revealed myself and the unknowing Theon as unclean as well.
