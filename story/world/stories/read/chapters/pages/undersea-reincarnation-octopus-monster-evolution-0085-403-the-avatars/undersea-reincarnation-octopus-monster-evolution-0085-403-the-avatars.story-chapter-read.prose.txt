Henry’s awareness bloomed out of the cove like a tidal wave that grew stronger and wider with every clone it reached, propagating out of them and spreading his reach further and wider. As soon as his consciousness reached them, his clones changed, transforming as Henry’s new abilities granted them much more power than what they used to wield. Henry already knew instinctively what the change was, but he still loaded up the details while simultaneously, he took a deep breath and took stock of his state.

He would need a long break after this.

He could already tell the fusion of his Aspects had severely strained him, and he could tell he had plenty of what he could only describe as sore knots in the spots where the fusion hadn’t gone so well, which would need time to even out and heal before he dared push the Aspect to A-rank. But the worst was behind him, and as information flooded through his mind through his eyes and perspectives, he knew this had been the right call.

Unfettered Echoes of the Trickster (B) - Level 7

Cornerstone: Avatars of the All-Seeing Legion

Passive: The Eight Apostles

Your evolved Octominds unfold into eight sapient projections, each benefiting from Trickster’s Legion and each capable of exerting nearly as much power as yourself. Their presence greatly sharpens your awareness, deepens your insight, and augments your cognitive speed and casting prowess. The Apostles can act on preset triggers or tactical intuition, and all maintain an intrinsic connection to your mind. You may bind passives or abilities to each, allowing specialization—to heal, to navigate, to act as a decoy or to defend, and much more. They act near-independently, yet in perfect, seamless harmony with your core self.

Passive: The Trickster’s Legion

You may act through all of your projections—Avatars and Apostles included—to perceive, communicate, use your skills, or switch places with them. All of your projections hold your memories and a fragment of your spirit, creating a failsafe in case of the destruction of your original body. The more spread out your spirit is, the longer the recovery would be.

Passive: The Trickster’s Dancing Constellation

Every projection summoned acts as a relay, increasing your zone of influence. The closer and better connected the relay is, the easier exerting your influence would be. The connection to an avatar may never be truly severed, but the further away it is, the harder it would be to sense or reach it. Stranded Avatars would continue the tasks they had been assigned, or would go dormant until a stronger connection is re-established.

Active: Avatar of the Trickster

You may create a projection of yourself that benefits from The Trickster’s Legion. The projection can be an Arcane Avatar or a Lifeblood Avatar. The Arcane Avatar is a clone made of magic and mana that can act as a powerful relay and caster, wielding your skills nearly as powerfully as yourself. It can interact with the physical world, but it wouldn’t have the weight and physical power of the Lifeblood Avatar, which would excel in healing, physical combat, and stealth. The Avatars can switch from one form to another in exchange of mana or lifeblood, but keep in mind that this process is not instantaneous.

It was a little ironic how if Henry had gone through this upgrade a couple months ago, his clones—no, Avatars—would have been much more useful to Velistraine. Then again, what had happened had likely pushed Henry toward these results.

At least I’ll be ready the next time something of that kind happens, he told himself. And once the Aspect moved on to the next rank, its reach might hopefully breach the limits of this world. Or maybe he’d need to figure out a way to cut a portal open. He might need to find a creature that can do that… or maybe he should try chewing on the turtle’s shell some more?

Or experiment with my teleportation…but that’ll need to wait a little, he immediately remembered, sensing the deep aches he could feel all over his spirit. Henry’s musings were cut off as he sensed movement to his right, and he looked up from the little pool to find Maurice slowly peering at him from the side. When their gaze met, the crab threw his claws up. “[Henry! You’re back! How long have you been up? Why didn’t you tell me? Did you do it? Did it work? Tell me!]”

“[I just finished, and I’m beat,]” Henry said, raising an arm before he paused, then dropped it. He was about to summon his new Avatars to see what they looked and felt like, but… it’d be better to give himself at least a few minutes to recover before he started engaging the Aspect and skill he’d just finished creating. “[It worked, but I need to let it all rest a little. How long have I been gone? I hope you didn’t get too bored?]”

Now that he was out of the intensely focused state he’d been in, Henry was feeling a little guilty for leaving Maurice alone. He hoped it had been only a couple of days, but he worried it might be worse and just like he’d feared, Maurice confirmed it a moment later.

“[Oh I don’t know. I stopped counting after 10 days. Might have been 16 days or something like that. I even worked on my skills and stuff while I waited, though before we leave, I gotta make sure these guys are safe. Can you leave a clone with them?]”

Henry stared past Maurice at the crabs peering at them, then with a sigh, he nodded. “[Of course, buddy. Sorry I took so long.]”

Over the next few minutes, the two shared a meal that Henry practically inhaled. It was some fatty tuna they’d been carrying for a while, then Henry dug into some fruits and even some cheese Maurice had been stashing.

“[Do you think Velistraine’s clone got the change?]” Maurice asked when Henry finished telling him about the new clones’ capabilities. Unfortunately, Henry couldn’t tell. Not yet, at least. But once he recovered, he’d see if there were any changes. If he could somehow manage to reach them one way or another. For now, it was time to take a little rest, and to speed things up a bit, Henry closed his eyes and suffused his body with the energies of Life Surge, which he bolstered with the essences of Lifeweaving and a tiny bit of Unfettered Echoes.

Henry inhaled sharply, eyes shooting open. The bit of Lifeweaving he used roiled and shone within his soul, pulsing with echoes that made it feel as if Henry had used all of his Lifeweaving essence and then some, to the point that to his bare eyes, he was glowing with green lights, outshining the lights of the day.

Would you look at that, he thought, chortling to himself.

Clearly enough, the new essence worked as a potent amplifier, which Henry believed was perfect. He had Predation for the offense, Lifeweaving for healing and altering his body, then the rest could be filled up by the new Unfettered Echoes. Henry wanted to continue testing it and see its limits, but he refrained. He’d stretch his limbs after he got some rest. After some much needed sleep. Then the two of them would move out and depending on how Henry felt, he might take his new abilities out for a test run.

As Henry drifted off to sleep, he wondered how far he could push these new abilities. He hoped they’d be enough for what was to come.
