Salzer was a benevolent king and master of all six elements: light, dark, fire, water, earth and air. His kingdom prospered for generations under his wise rule. With the help of his mighty Elemental War Hammer, the lands of Elestria flourished with abundant harvests, clear waters, and gentle winds that carried the sweet scent of everdew blossoms throughout the realm.

Salzer did not hoard his elemental knowledge but shared it freely with his many children. In the grand citadel of Oluminara, he established the Academy of Elements where they learned to harness the fundamental forces that shaped their world.

“Balance, equilibrium—the cycle of life depends on harmony between opposing forces,” he often told his offspring when they gathered in the Chamber of Oluminara, where the six elemental pillars housed the aura of energy that fueled their land. “When one element dominates, chaos follows.”

The Elemental War Hammer was a conduit to the elements, a powerful weapon that channeled all six elements in perfect harmony. Yet, whispers had begun to spread through the kingdom’s outer reaches. Strange disturbances in the elemental balance—rivers flowing upstream, fires burning cold, and stones floating weightlessly into the sky. The common folk called them “Salzer’s Anomalies,” believing them to be merely tests of faith from their king toward those who doubted his power. But Salzer knew better. Each night, as he meditated in his private sanctuary atop the highest tower of Elestria, he could feel the subtle fractures in the elemental weave. Something—or someone—was pulling at the threads of equilibrium….

An imbalance, a disturbance… the truth would not be uncovered until it was too late. King Salzer’s son, Prince Theron, had been siphoning from the pillar of darkness….

For months, Theron had secretly studied the forbidden texts of the dark hidden in the academy’s restricted archives. Unlike his siblings who embraced their father’s teachings of balance, Theron believed true power lay in specialization. Why master six elements weakly when one could command a single element with unrivaled strength?

The darkness called to him, whispering promises of knowledge and power beyond imagination. Each night while the kingdom slept, Theron would steal from the Chamber of Oluminara, placing his hands upon the obsidian pillar that pulsed with shadow energy. He had learned to draw the darkness into himself without much disturbance, until the scales tipped over and unleashed unrecoverable chaos.

The once fruitful and flourishing land of Elestria quickly succumbed to darkness. Crops withered overnight, their life force drained by shadows that crept through the soil. The everdew blossoms, once vibrant and fragrant, shriveled into black husks. Rivers turned murky and thick, while gentle breezes became howling gales through the branches of dead trees. The potency of darkness rooted deeply within Prince Theron that even his father couldn’t stop him. The fall of Elestria was inevitable. King Salzer felt his connection to the elements weakening as the darkness spread like a plague across his beloved realm, allowing chaos to bury Elestria and its prosperous people into the ground.

The intro to the dungeon had its very own story behind it. I guess the cores in tier five dungeons came with some awesome backstory, because the lower tiers Nolan and I raided didn’t have anything like this.

“There is no way we are sticking around for dungeon raiding,” Typha retorted, and then searched around for an exit. “Look, over here,” she said as she neared the other end of the circular temple. “An exit.”

Or was it?

Typha was sure reckless. She was going to make Nolan’s blood pressure spike up. I couldn’t really blame her. The girl had always relied on her klepto ring to give her what she wanted, and right now, it was useless. She was so dependent on her stolen items that her survival instincts were shot. So when she walked into the end of darkness and no one stopped her, I made my move. Nolan called out for me, and I stopped. But, it wasn’t because of him—it was because Typha disappeared and then reappeared behind us!

“Is this some kind of joke?” she hissed, realizing she’d circled back.

“It’s a loop,” Lun replied. “Zev must have placed a binding spell in the temple that forces us here. The only device that would break it is sitting right in front of us—entering a dungeon. The dungeon core would negate his magic.”

I had to test it out for myself, and test it out I did, running from one end of the temple to the other. I was shocked!

“You can stop running laps, lightning foot,” Nolan called out to me.

‘This is so weird!’ I said, then skidded to a stop.

“We have no other choice but to raid this thing,” Nolan added.

“Are you insane, or just stupid?” Typha retorted. “There is no way a half-excuse of a gun mage and a baby dragon can defeat a tier 5 dungeon! With no items!”

“Egh, you’re forgetting about me,” Lun said as he raised his hand.

“Gun mage?” Nolan questioned with an eyebrow up.

‘I still can’t evolve, can I?’ I asked.

‘No, not here.’

‘But this area is wide enough,’ I protested. ‘I mean, how big is the impact zone?’

‘It gives off intense heat, and I don’t know how this place would react to a dragon’s transformation,’ he said, and then he looked over to Lun. I saw a light bulb pop over his head before he asked the lizard mage, “Hey, can that scepter of yours bubble us? Typha, Zaiden, yourself and me?”

“Huh? As in a force field?”

“Yeah. Freya is going to transform, and we need protection.”

I gasped happily.

“We are letting a dragon transform?” Typha commented.

Nolan looked over to her. “Problem?”

She rolled her eyes at Nolan. “Are you sure you’re human?”

“Not all dragons are out to hurt you. Some of them are decent, decent enough to suggest rescuing the thief that stole her things, even though she didn’t deserve it.”

“I can do that, but if we are going to face this dungeon head on, I need a mana restoration vial,” Lun said. “A few, actually.”

Nolan handed Lun some, Typha’s eyes on me the entire time. Did a dragon steal her lunch money or something? Why was she bent on hating my guts?

It didn’t matter. I wasn’t going to let her ruin my evoluuuuution!

Gosh, I was so excited, I couldn’t stand it!

“Perfect!” Lun said after Nolan handed him some goodies. “All right, now. The force field bubble will only last for a few seconds, under thirty seconds for sure. If I extend it any longer, I’ll run into integrity issues. That’s plenty of time for you to evolve. As soon as I see the first whip of spiraling air, I will encase everyone in a protective bubble, just in case the temple answers back to this show with raining chunks of rock or somethin’ worse.”

“We move to the far end and give Freya here some room,” Nolan said as he adjusted Zaiden over his shoulder. Lun and Typha followed him closer to the dungeon entrance, and I moved further back to the phoney exit to keep them as safe as possible.

Well, here goes nothing!

“Ready when you are, dracapod!” Lun shouted.

I pulled out my evolution stone as my heart raced. This was it—again. Second time’s the charm! I repeated the process, the same results happening as before. A hot radiant light began to pulsate around me. The heat was intense, radiating outward from my core in waves that distorted the air around me.

Through the haze of my transformation, I caught a glimpse of Lun’s scepter rising. He was ready to drop a barrier as soon as the real deal started.

It was strange–even though I was flippin’ thrilled about transforming, I was nervous. I looked over to Nolan who glanced back at me with a subtle smirk and a nod. ‘You got this,’ he said, being the supportive friend he was.

I closed my eyes and a whip of energy boomed in my ears. It was loud and fast like thunder, forcing me to open my eyes again. But when I did, I saw nothing but a prompt screen in a field of pitch blackness:

Evolution Stage 2 In Progress

Please select an option from below for stage 2: Dracuna

(as a multi elemental dragon, you have different phenotype evolution paths available to you)

Earth Elemental Phenotype: Bastodon or Wyvern

Frost Elemental Phenotype: Hydra or Sea Serpent

Fire Elemental Phenotype: Drake or Dragon

Air Elemental Phenotype: Caelym or Ether (Ether: fae, wyvern, dragon)

Space Elemental Phenotype: Cosmyrim

Oh crap, this was hard!

I didn’t know which one to select. They all had their pros and cons, their unique strengths and weaknesses. So there lay the big question; what evolution path did I pick? Did I want to be a Bastodon, with its earthy brown scales and horns that could pierce through the toughest armor? Or a Wyvern, a creature said to be able to withstand any backhand slap from Mother Nature? But then, the cool Hydra was appealing too, possessing multiple heads that could attack simultaneously.

For a regular dragon, this process would have been so much easier, because there wouldn’t have been so many choices available. If you breathed fire, your only options were drake and dragon. There was nothing else in between. But maybe I was overthinking this too much….

Hmm, the name Cosmyrim sounds pretty badass, but I’m not a fan of the whole centipede look….

Not only that, I wouldn’t be able to fly.

I wanted to fly, damnit!

‘Okay… so ol’ fashion it is. Can’t go wrong with common ground.’

Huh, well, I sure didn’t feel anything. I looked down at my body and, yep, still small.

Hmm, I wonder if—oh wait, something was happening! I could feel my stance changing, becoming broader and larger. Then a sudden, pain in my head—crap, it was intense! My head felt like it was ready to explode, and for a second, I thought that maybe I was getting a little ahead of myself about this evolution business!

My body prickled as if thousands of tiny needles were making their way out from beneath the surface. I looked down to see that scales were beginning to reform, my limbs stretching and my torso expanding. My claws extended and sharpened, my scales hardened, and my back hunched over as wings sprouted from my shoulder blades. I was being rearranged, where even my mouth extended into a longer snout filled with razor-sharp teeth. I fell on my knees, gasping from the intensity of the ordeal. I let out a mighty roar, and everything went black.

My dracapod form was gradually dissolving, the juvi form of a dracuna taking its place. My body convulsed again, the internal change that was happening in rapid-fire successions nearly taking my breath away. My lungs… they were no longer a single united organ but now six separate entities within my chest. My body was radiating heat from the endeavor, heating up to a level that made the ground beneath me scorching red like lava. The entire process felt longer than thirty seconds, and the first thing that crossed my mind as my aura began to taper down was Nolan’s safety.

Before I knew it, the transformation was complete.
