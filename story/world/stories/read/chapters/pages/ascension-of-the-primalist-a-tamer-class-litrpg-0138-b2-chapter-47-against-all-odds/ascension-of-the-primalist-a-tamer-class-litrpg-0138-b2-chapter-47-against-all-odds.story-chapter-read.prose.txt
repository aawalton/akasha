The Dark Elemental Executioner moved.

It flowed across the stone floor and closed the distance with terrifying speed. Seth braced himself, his core raging like an inferno in his chest and flooding every fiber of his being with primal energy. The creature's shadowy claws lashed out.

Seth met the strike with his gauntlets, and the impact sent a jarring shock through his bones. He was stronger than he had been a week ago, yet this creature was on a whole other level.

Another slash came, this time for his throat. Seth ducked so the claws hissed past his ear and threw a counter. The Dark Elemental slid back, causing Seth's fist to meet only empty air. The next instant, the creature launched a series of simple slashes with the apparent goal of testing his defense.

Seth was being toyed with, the space between him and the claws shrinking with every parry and dodge. It was only a matter of time before he was hit.

'Colossus, now!' he said while being pushed back.

Aether surged from his necklace, and the scorpion materialized beside him. Colossus wasted no time and slammed his pincers together. The layers of Quartz Armor crystallized over his body, and at the same time, the shimmering brown barrier of Desert Aegis expanded around him and Seth.

Seth lunged forward, aiming a punch at the Elemental's face. Taken aback by the scorpion's emergence, the creature dissolved into a dark streak and flowed away. Seizing the opportunity, Colossus charged, and a chitinous hum echoed from his maw the moment the Elemental reformed.

Halting, the creature turned its featureless head as it felt the spell's compulsion. A cold malice surged out of it; it was partially resisting, but it also seemed to accept the challenge, as if daring the scorpion to withstand what it was about to do.

Its forearms began to writhe, then shadows within them coiled, hardened, and sharpened, until the two scythe-like blades of solidified dark energy erupted once again from its limbs. The creature then rushed toward Colossus as Seth ran toward them.

The next breath, the Elemental became death itself, its shadow weapons moving in arcs too fast for eyes to follow. The first blade struck Colossus' armored carapace; Quartz Armor, which had withstood the claws of countless Elementals half an hour ago, shattered with a shriek in a single attack. Continuing on its unstoppable path, the scythe sheared through the chitin beneath as if it were a simple piece of parchment and carved a deep gash across the scorpion's side.

Colossus let out a screech of agony that echoed in Seth's mind. The scorpion's Desert Aegis flared, the brown aura pulsing while the spell tried to slow the onslaught, yet the Dark Elemental Executioner's second scythe was already descending. It dug into Colossus' shoulder, tore away a large chunk of his shell, and sent a spray of blood into the air.

Already dashing toward them, Seth pushed aether into Huntbound Rush and activated his Swift Eagle Bracelet. Colossus could not withstand such strikes. Certainly not for the full duration of the elemental's spell.

'Colossus, get back!' he shouted through their bond.

As the surge of energy shot through his legs, Seth cast Beastmaster Rage and surrendered himself to the cold fury of his core.

A red veil fell over his vision as threw himself between the creature and his wounded companion. The stone of Iron Fists materialized around his gauntlets, accompanied by the azure glow of his Protecting Belt. The Dark Elemental swiped one of its blades at him.

The scythe sliced through the air, and for a beat, it seemed to rip space itself. Seth ducked underneath the lethal swipe, then lunged in, driving his fist into the creature's abdomen and forcing it back.

With a hiss, the Dark Elemental countered, its blades turning into a flurry of slices. Driven by his core, Seth weaved through the slashes. All the Agility from his spells and artifacts allowed him to turn into a ghost, darting in and out of the whirlwind as he searched for an opening.

Yet something quickly caught his attention. The Elemental wasn't dissolving into a streak of darkness to evade his attacks as the others had done. With the scythe-like blades extending from its arms, its body seemed locked into a more solid state, forcing it to rely on speed to dodge and its weapons to defend itself.

Seth barely had time to register the weakness before one of the blades connected. A single cut completely drained all the aether from his Protecting Belt.Behind him, Colossus could only watch, being too slow to participate in the clash. Seth's Iron Fists screeched with each deflection, the stone on his gauntlets being sliced apart and forcing him to pour a constant stream of aether into the spell just to regrow what was being actively destroyed.

Then, as suddenly as they had appeared, the shadowy blades finally dissolved and retracted back into the Elemental's forearms.

The onslaught was over.

An abrupt silence fell over the city, broken only by Seth's ragged breathing and a pained hiss from Colossus. Wasting no time, Seth charged back in to throw his own attacks. For now the advantage was theirs.

No longer having to deal with the penetrating spell, Colossus joined the fray. Air seemed to blur around the massive scorpion as he charged and slammed into the dark creature with an explosion of aether that enveloped a five-yard radius. The Elemental stumbled back, its form sputtering like a flame in a gale, momentarily stunned.

Seth didn't know what had just happened, but he wouldn't waste such an opportunity. His gauntlets crackled with the teal aether of Phantom Punch as he lunged in and drove his fist into the Dark Elemental Executioner's torso.

The Undead spell crashed against the creature's body and the Elemental staggered. The creature flickered violently, but it did not crumble or shatter. Its resilience was unnatural; the thing seemed to absorb the kinetic force in a way that defied logic. It felt unreal that an assassin-like creature could also withstand direct attacks with such ease.

But like he'd done with all the others, he had to break through that thing.

Seth glanced at Colossus. A large crack ran across the scorpion's shell, and blood leaked out of it. Anger flared in Seth's chest at the sight, and he gritted his teeth as he turned back toward the Elemental.

"Let's kill it!" Seth shouted.

The scorpion surged forward again to engage the elemental, his pincers clamping down on its limbs while his three tails whipped and stung, buying precious time. Seth coordinated his attack with his partner's, ducking under the claws to batter the creature's body with every blow he could land. Yet even while restrained, the creature still dodged and weaved through most of his punches, dissolving into a sleek streak of darkness whenever it needed to slip away.

The difference between them and a Peak-Iron was stark.

Yet slowly, Seth and Colossus began to gain the upper hand thanks to Beastmaster Rage and Desert Aegis. The Dark Elemental was forced back more and more, the shadow forming it flickering, unable to land a clean blow. Just a little more and they would put it down.

But then, the creature's form solidified once more. A silent roar left its maw, and with a surge of aether the shadowy scythe-blades materialized again. They seemed even sharper and darker than they had previously.

Shit, Seth cursed, looking at Colossus next to him.

The scorpion was battered to the brink of collapse, his shell covered with a web of cracks. He'd taken the brunt of the fight—every strike, every blast—to buy them time, and Seth had thrown everything he had at the Dark Elemental, but it hadn't been enough. Now, the creature would be back on the attack. And those shadow blades would cut them to pieces.

The Dark Elemental turned and slashed its scythes, unleashing a crescent of pure shadow that tore through the air. Colossus cast Sandstorm Dome right away, and the swirling barrier of golden dust erupted around them. The defensive spell intercepted the attack in a deafening explosion that shook the entire cavern and dissipated.

We can't win, Seth thought, his mind racing to find a plan. Not in a drawn-out fight. We have to finish it in a single blow.

His gaze flicked toward Colossus, and his stomach tightened at the sight of the scorpion's shattered armor and bleeding chitin. 'Colossus, retreat. Get close to Nightmare and stay out of its reach.'

'No.'

Seth almost thought he'd misheard him. 'What?'

Instead of retreating, Colossus lowered his battered body and charged toward the Dark Elemental with his new spell.

"Colossus!" Seth shouted.

'Kill it!' the scorpion bellowed through Link. 'I'll give you the time!'

The Dark Elemental, unable to dissolve into a streak of darkness, moved to the side to dodge Colossus' attack. Its scythes then flashed. One carved through the remnants of his Quartz Armor and tore into the chitin beneath, sending a surge of pain through Link that made Seth's jaw clench.

'Get back!' Seth rushed to support him. 'Or I'll pull you back into the necklace!'

'Not yet!'

Another blade struck Colossus across the side. Chitin slid open, but the scorpion refused to take even a step back. He threw himself back into the fight, snapping with his pincers and lashing out with his tails despite being far too slow to keep up with his opponent.

'Colossus, that's enough!'

'Just kill it!' Colossus begged, pain bleeding through every word. 'I'll retreat at the last moment. I promise!'

Seth wanted to force the issue, but he couldn't. Colossus had made his choice, and every second Seth spent arguing with him was another second his companion was being torn apart.

So Seth seized the rage of his core and drew on the last reserves of aether from his Fine Storing Ring. He pushed a torrent of power into his right arm and channeled it all into the grooves of Phantom Punch with a single intent: for this blow to kill anything.

Aether began to infuse and hiss around Seth's gauntlet. A skeletal hand of pure energy took shape, the ethereal fingers forming first, followed by the wrist and then the arm. The spell was being forged with more strength through sheer will.

Through Link, Seth could feel Nightmare still absorbing the Seed and the pain of Colossus.

The spell had to kill that thing.

This victory would let them live one more day and strengthen all of them. It would push them another step closer to their goal, another step closer to tearing apart the Faertis and the other greedy Houses. To overthrowing that horrific king. To avenging his parents.

They had to win.

Seth's Well dropped toward ten percent. 'Colossus, now!' he shouted through Link. 'Get back!'

But the scorpion didn't. Instead, Seth felt a grim certainty through their bond an instant before Colossus deliberately turned and exposed his wounded shoulder.

Seth's blood ran cold as he charged. 'No, don't—'

The Dark Elemental took the opening. Its shadow scythe plunged into Colossus's chitin, punching through his shoulder and pinning his body against the stone. Agony exploded through Link.

'COLOSSUS!'

The scorpion shrieked, but even through the pain, his answer came immediately. 'Now, Seth!'

For an instant, Seth understood. His companion had known exactly what he was doing. He had known the strike might kill him, and he'd taken it anyway because he believed Seth could finish the Dark Elemental if it wasn't able to dodge.

Seth lunged and closed the distance in a heartbeat. The Dark Elemental, still anchored by its own weapon, snapped its head toward him as if sensing something amiss, but it was already too late. Seth threw everything he had into the punch: his weight, his rage, his purpose.

The world fell silent as the skeletal fist shot forward, the teal aether leaving ghostly trails in its wake. The Dark Elemental tried to twist away, to bring its other scythe up to block, to do anything that would save it, yet it was already doomed.

The spectral knuckles smashed into the dark creature, and its form rippled violently like a reflection on water struck by a storm. A silent, aetheric scream tore through its body as Phantom Punch kept advancing, relentless and absolute.

Then came a sickening crunch—a sound that one might imagine when a soul broke. The Dark Elemental froze. The scythe-blades around its arms vanished, and an instant later its entire form shuddered before collapsing in on itself in a fading shower of dark motes that drifted away in the cavern air.

An obsidian crystal clattered to the stone floor, the last echo of the predator that had once ruled over this place.

Almost simultaneously, the adrenaline that had sustained Seth vanished. The full weight of his injuries and exhaustion slammed into his body like a punch. His legs buckled. He collapsed to his knees, then fell forward onto the ground and his vision tunneled to black. A pained groan escaped Colossus' maw, the shadow scythe having left a gushing wound in his shoulder.

Fighting through the weakness, Seth plunged a trembling hand into his Endless Pouch and took out one of Marcus' potions. He moved to the scorpion's side and poured the crimson liquid between Colossus' mandibles. A weak thought rippled through their bond.

'Inside… take me inside the necklace.'

Seth complied immediately. The moment Colossus disappeared into the teardrop necklace, Seth pushed his consciousness into the domain. There, he saw Colossus crawling painfully toward the glowing Heart of Brown-Peak Mountain. The brown shell covering the giant scorpion began to crack and fall away in large chunks, revealing the raw flesh beneath.

For a heartbeat, a chill wave of panic seized Seth, thinking that his companion wasn't going to make it, but then he understood: Colossus was shedding his old exoskeleton and forming a new one.

Through their bond, Seth could feel a new carapace, even more powerful than before, forged not just from aether but from his partner's unwavering will to protect. To endure. To be the shield his family needed.

Though he was battered and exhausted, Seth forced himself to his feet. Then, his attention was drawn to the single object left behind by the vanquished elemental. The obsidian crystal pulsed with an inner light, and even from a distance he could feel the pure, dark energy inside. There was no way it was a mere beaststone.

A spell-shard.
