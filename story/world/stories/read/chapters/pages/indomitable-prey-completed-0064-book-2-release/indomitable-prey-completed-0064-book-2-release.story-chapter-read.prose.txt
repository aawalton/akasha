Lamb has survived as prey in a new world. Now he needs to become the hunter.

After escaping mercenaries and uncovering the power that sent them, Lamb vows to support Vera in her quest for vengeance against Duke Ryonic.

But to reach the warlord, they’ll have to cross the Dragonspine mountains, a journey few low-level adventurers return from. That means evolving his class, gaining levels, and deepening the bonds with his companions.

As the mountain clans gather to celebrate another winter endured with an orgy of gladiatorial violence, Lamb has an opportunity to do all three. Succeed, and he’ll emerge from the peaks stronger than he could have imagined.

Blocking his path are warriors, beasts, and the mountains themselves, and in Tsanderos, none are merciful.

Don’t miss Book 2 of this gritty isekai LitRPG series about a lost outsider learning to adapt, survive, and rise in a beautiful, dangerous land.
