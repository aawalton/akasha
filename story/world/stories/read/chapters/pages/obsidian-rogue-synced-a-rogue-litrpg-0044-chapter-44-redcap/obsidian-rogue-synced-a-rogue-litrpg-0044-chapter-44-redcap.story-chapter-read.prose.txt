Levi bolted upright, his heart hammering in his chest, with his eyes scanning wildly for threats. It had been a long journey, and so they had decided to camp by the road for the night in fear of being attacked by bandits or monsters.

He had dozed off in the wagon, and so had the mage if her closed eyes and even breathing were anything to go by. Levi tore his gaze away from the sleeping mage as he exited the wagon. He hadn't had a nightmare in years, and it felt more like a gut warning than one. So Levi had to wonder what his gut was warning him about.

He continued to scan the area for threats, his eyes going to Symon's tent, where the sound of snoring was echoing from. The gnome’s tent was pitched close to the wagon with the matas tied down to trees to the side.

It took a moment for Levi to shake off his grogginess, but once he did, he began walking throughout the makeshift camp.

Once again, his eyes drifted to the matas, which were both tied to different trees, fast asleep on the floor. Then he turned his gaze to the sky, believing that there was a reason why he had woken up.

A nervous neigh sounded to his right, and he redirected his gaze to one of the mata that had been asleep just moments ago. Levi had to wonder if he had been the one to wake it or if it had sensed something he was missing, as matas were known to be sensitive creatures.

That thought seemed to hold more weight as the second mata also neighed and got up on all fours from the floor where it had been asleep moments ago.

Levi stilled his breath as he listened, trying to gain a sense of what had roused the mata. a slight sound, carried to his ears, prompting Levi to pull out his new daggers. There's something definitely out there.

The forest was dark at nighttime, but it was more of a natural darkness as compared to the one he had experienced in his last dungeon. So it was a lot easier for him to pierce the darkness with his improved perception.

There we go. Levi thought. His scanning finally paid off as he spotted a short creature stumbling towards their makeshift camp. Redcaps.

Redcaps in the stories he had been told were notorious, bloodthirsty fey with hats soaked in the blood of their victims. They were roughly the same size and height as a gnome, and were known to move in packs, which was why the sight of only a single one stumbling towards the makeshift camp had Levi on edge.

He suspected that there had to be more of them just hiding beyond the tree line and out of his line of sight, but a part of him had to wonder if he was just overthinking it, and it was just a single red cap that he could quickly slay and go back to sleep.

Powerful neighs drew Levi’s attention as both mata turned their heads to the left, causing Levi to turn in that direction. He couldn't stop himself from cursing as he spotted multiple redcaps circling the camp.

To confirm his lingering suspicion, he turned his head to the right, where he spotted a few more red caps. Levi did a final scan. he turned backward, and sure enough, multiple redcaps seemed to be coming from that direction.

They have us surrounded. With confirmation of Redcaps coming at them from all sides, Levi knew that this was not a fight that they could run away from, even if they wanted to, which meant he had to wake the others up at the very least to improve their chances of all getting out of this unhurt.

Of course, Levi knew that if he had been on this journey alone, the Redcaps themselves wouldn't be much of a problem for him, as it would be very easy for him to escape uninjured, even if he could not slay them all by himself. But as he was here with the gnome, mage, and the two matas, he had to at the very least consider their well-being, and for everyone to survive the redcaps, he needed them all awake and alert, as that was the only solution he could think of that guaranteed them slaying all of the redcaps while keeping their bodies intact.

Levi hurried towards the tents that Symon had put up himself and rapidly rapped on the tent pole, simultaneously shouting at the gnome inside. “Symon, get out, we have company!”

“What?” The gnome asked, slightly annoyed and sleepy, as he popped his head out after hearing the rogue's words.

“Get into the wagon, redcaps are attacking,” Levi said, “I and the mage will handle this.”

Not waiting for Symon to grasp what was happening, Levi hurried to the wagon and shook the mage awake. “We'd better be under attack,” the mage groggily said as she blinked herself awake.

“Congratulations, you’ve used one of your wishes. We are under attack.” Levi said, the hint of a grin on his lips. “Redcaps are circling the camp. guard Symon and the wagon, and I'll do my best to make sure the matas remain unharmed while I decimate as many Redcaps as I can. Your fireballs should be able to handle the rest of them.”

Successfully delivering a heads up to the two, Levi spun and darted towards the closest Redcap, who was holding a wooden pike, just as the others. The barbaric weapon, which was comically oversized as compared to the red cap itself, was dyed red, probably from the blood of its last victim.

The redcap shrieked as he thrust with his pipe towards the rogue, who skittered around the weapon until he was close and personal with the red cap.

Without missing a beat, Levi struck at the eyes of the redcap with both his blades and watched as they pierced its eyeballs and went down to its brain, killing it instantly.

A hissing sound echoed in the forest as Levi pulled out his daggers, and steam wafted off from the empty eye sockets of the dead redcap as its body reacted to the iron that had pierced it.

What do you know? They really are allergic to iron.

Not even waiting for the body of the dead redcap to hit the floor, Levi moved, eating up the distance between him and the next redcap so quickly that it hadn't even been able to properly thrust its spike before Levi's left dagger exited the other side of his throat, causing it to gurgle as it fell to the floor.

Individually, the redcaps themselves were the most harmless creatures as far as synced monsters were concerned in Rinesta. But they more than made up for that with the sheer numbers they attacked.

Levi expected them to be around fifteen to twenty redcaps altogether, circling the camp.
