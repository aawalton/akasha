By the time Adrian and Rose began eating their fill in the tavern, Bella reached the Blackwell mansion. The mansion was spread over a wide area, its rectangular perimeter easily covering 10 acres. The exterior walls of the mansion were made up of black marble, and the leftover area was divided into a battle arena and a garden, while the two shorter sides were covered with arrays of flowers.

This building was made by Blaive Blackwell, or the previous guildmaster of White Tiger Guild, with the reward he received for clearing the 30th floor of the City of Alcia’s dungeon.

Bella stepped down from the driver's couch and looked in the direction of Blackwell mansion. Her eyes moved across the building’s wall, finally stopping at the huge main gate of the building. A subtle smile spread on Bella’s lips as she remembered her past memory.

I bought this! Well, bought won’t be the right word since I got this built instead, but it’s ours… Blaive Blackwell had said, his lips spreading into a wide grin as he played with his black hair. Marcielle surely gave him a hell of a beating that time, Bella thought to herself, as her smile transformed into a small chuckle.

Bella looked at the black gate and walked in its direction to check whether the gate was opened or not. As she touched the iron bar of the gate, a question briefly crossed her mind. Could I have saved them had I been more experienced in conserving mana? She questioned herself, remembering the tragedy of the 40th floor.

The tragedy that had pushed two S ranking adventurers to their death.

The smile on Bella’s face vanished as her brain began to process a question that she had already asked herself several times before. Her hand passed through the empty space of the door and reached for the metallic hinge lock. Bella carefully unlocked the door while making loud metallic noises as she opened it.

In moments of Bella making noise with the metalic frame of the door, a loud running sound came from the mansion. As Bella finally unlocked the door, the brown wooden gate of the mansion was opened simultaneously as well. A silhouette of a girl with curved ears was visible, the girl quickly dashed toward Bella in a pace some would call teleportation. With wide arms, the girl jumped on Bella, embracing her in a hug.

The girl wore a characteristic black and white maid outfit, she had furry brown ears that matched her neck-length hair. "Hey Bella!" Noya chirped with enthusiasm, as she looked at Bella who was now lying on her back.

Bella took a moment to process what had happened to her, leaving a disapproving sigh, she began to speak. "You shouldn’t jump like this on anybody, Noya, I could’ve gotten hurt had I not been careful."

"Are you ever not careful though?" Noya teased, shifting to the right to give Bella space to stand.

"Well, I wasn’t careful right now or I would have sidestepped and took hold of the back side of your cloth and fallen over you instead," Bella said in a matter-of-factly tone.

"Maybe i shouldn't have jumped at you, but it’s your mistake to not care to visit…" Noya began to reply, though her attention quickly shifted to the carriage. She took two sniffs, and her brown eyes widened with enthusiasm as she noticed a familiar scent, a scent that didn’t belong to Bella, certainly. Noya stood up, her ears twitched as she tried to hear any sound. "Is master Alan with you?" Noya questioned, she practically blurted the question in an incomprehensible voice.

"Yeah, he is," Bella replied with a nod, her eyes drawn to the vigorous wagging of Noya’s tail.

"He’s in the carriage, isn’t he! Does he think he could hide from my expert nose?" Noya said with a prideful smile, as she rubbed below her nose with her right index finger. Noya then turned her head toward the carriage about to walk towards it.

Bella grabbed Noya’s shoulder before she could step away. “He’s unconscious, but he’s going to be fine,” she said, careful to finish her sentence so as not to cause Noya a heart attack.

"Unconscious, but how?" Noya questioned, her eyes widening with concern.

"He got a burnout during our recent mission," Bella replied with a neutral expression. "Both Ais and Alisa might be late too since they are most probably busy with the aftermath and paperwork."

Noya took a moment to process everything Bella had informed her, and she quickly dashed toward the carriage. Opening the gate, she looked at Alan, her eyes drifted at all the wound marks left on his pants and shirt. What kind of monster was he fighting to get this hurt? she mentally questioned, well aware of Alan’s capabilities.
