Thomas's entire perception and his very sense of self, narrowed down to the blade pierced through his kidney. Since it was in him and his mana was freaking out, he became viscerally aware the blade was actually pure, hardened Corruption mana. Just like when Zach had been hit with those Water mana arrows.

Then his spirit itself was wracked as the introduced Corruption started tearing apart and corrupting his current mana into more of itself. That process was rapidly accelerating.

Thomas's mind flashed to the YouTube video his nephews had shown him: The kid who'd mixed Water and Fire mana and blew out his own core.

Also, let it not be forgotten that stabbing him right through a vital organ must have cut an an artery or something, because he was almost instantly bleeding out.

It was... a lot to take in.

Thomas went down hard, reaching frantically and failing to grab the Corruption mana blade lodged up inside him. The angle was bad, it had broken off deep, and he was quickly losing the ability for precise motor movements.

Vaguely, he was aware that Courtney had let out a shriek of her own. Good to know she suffered a little backlash from possibly fatally wounding him.

Unfortunately, it had been a sharp slap to Thomas' total car wreck. Maybe it was because she wasn't the high level Raveth had been, so she wasn't impacted by the Healer's Burden, or maybe her own affinity to Corruption mana partially insulated her. Either way, in a few moments she stepped over Thomas like he was already a corpse and no longer a threat (fair) and went straight for the control panel.

Frederic sneered something douchey that Thomas couldn't entirely catch because he was a little distracted. Then he moved to the side and gestured to the air. A shimmer went up in front of him that shielded Courtney, the control panel, and Thomas, who was bleeding out all over the floor.

Some kind of an illusion to hide them? Had to be, because no one had yet reacted, and Thomas would have liked to think that even with his team busy trying to take down Lavaris... someone would have said something.

He was dizzy and sick and his whole back felt wet and hot with blood. His fingers scrabbled uselessly at the wound, but he already felt numb like he was wearing mittens. Strength was literally being pumped out of his body with every heartbeat. The Corruption mana was certainly doing its dirty work, but bleeding out would kill him first.

Purging wouldn't work. Considering what the Corruption was doing to him, he could convince his body it was a toxin but he couldn't just purge out the entire blade. It would have to be removed first. Recising his core on the fly was pointless. He'd yeet his healing ability and still be stuck with the foreign object feeding him pure Corruption.

Okay. So he was dying, but he could slap a bandaid on the problem.

Get a grip, he told himself, and made a decision. He could buy a few minutes, and he'd fight through every one of them.

Gritting his teeth, Thomas forced an absolute flood of healing mana from his core straight to the wound. No point holding back anymore. Much was immediately converted into Corruption mana, which was then converted into even more corruption in a cascading line that was leading directly to his core like blood poisoning... but enough healing got through to seal the slashed artery, knit the kidney back in one piece, and replace the lost blood.

Unfortunately, the price of fixing the wound sealed that Corruption blade inside his body.

That was a problem for a few minutes from now.

Eyes snapping open, Thomas grabbed Courtney's ankle. Unfortunately, she had no Wellspring mana to yank around, so he did the job physically and swept her leg out from under her.

Her Gift of Rubber kicked in and she started to twist unnaturally. It wasn't enough to keep her balance and she fell, her chin hitting the control panel on the way down.

Having a partially stunned body land full force on top of him might have hurt, but his mana felt like it was on fire already. He was beyond pain.

Two things made him get to his feet.

He had to keep Frederic and Courtney away from the control panel, and therefore calling for backup.

He'd only need to do it for the few minutes he had left. A glance told him his team were winning their fight, then someone else could take over.

Frederic grinned at him and pulled out a sword he'd probably gotten from Courtney. Thomas had been a little distracted. "You don't look so good."

Thomas could only imagine. Even physically healed, he was wheezing and couldn't stand up straight, but he could place himself squarely in front of the control panel. That was all that counted.

"Hey," he yelled to his fighting team. "Little help here?"

"They can't hear you," Frederic said and pointed to his chin, to the glimmery wall in the air. His grin widened and made him look not a little crazy. "It's agony, isn't it? Accepting Corruption when your body isn't prepped for it? I took it as a level 1, but I can't imagine anything higher. What level are you? Two? Th--"

Thomas literally did not have time for back and forth with the creepy fuck, so he just barreled forward at him.

Frederic was no fool. Scratch that, he probably was a complete clown-shoes wearing fool for partnering up with the Light elves, but he was a well trained one.

He flicked out his short sword with expert precision as Thomas sort of stagger-charged at him.

Thomas's Combat Foresight told him where to expect the hits, and he took them, while absolutely flooding his System with Healing and Wellspring... which definitely increased the rate of inner Corruption but, who even cared at this point? He was holding nothing back.

One slash meant to disembowel him was sealed before the blade finished sweeping across his belly. The second upward thrust to his arm probably meant to sever a tendon, though it didn't have time to register.

Frederic didn't have the opportunity for a third hit before Thomas was on him. He meant to use the Purge or the Bridge or just rip him apart with Living Architecture. Whatever it took because surely Healing mana was just as corrosive to Frederic as Corruption was to Thomas.

The instant information Living Architecture returned to him at the point of contact was still pretty damn shocking.

Jo had been right about stabbing him straight through the heart before because Frederic's heart was currently not beating. Something--probably a skill fueled by Corruption mana--was forcing his circulatory system to run. Only his blood was just alive in theory and simply carried oxygen and basic bodily signals to keep muscles and nerves firing, and that was it. His cells were getting no other nutrients or support. Most except for the brain and lungs were already gone.

He was about as close to a living zombie Thomas could imagine. Already dead, except for the life support of Corruption mana, which was finishing the job on its own anyway.

Sometimes final death was a mercy. Thomas had done it for the boss bear in the Grizzly dungeon. It was even easier to do the same for Frederic now that Thomas's own Healing mana was actively tainted by Corruption.

That gave him a pathway in.

There was no backlash from his Burden. Frederic fell like the corpse he already was, only this time loot scattered out from him.

Unfortunately, the moments it took to deal with him had given Courtney a chance to recover.

Thomas discovered this as he started to turn and was met by a quick rabbit-punch to his diaphragm. Air exploded out of him. Even then, he fought through it and used his greater size and weight to shove past her and put himself between her and the control panel.

He had no more weapons, no shield, just himself but she'd still have to go through him first.

That was the moment where the Corruption mana, which had been stretching like an infection from the blade through his System, reached his core.

Thomas briefly lost control of himself, screaming, clawing at his own stomach. He wanted to rip it out. It was beyond pain, beyond soul-deep violation; his mana was being converted into its opposite and something that was the antithesis of himself as a person. His veins blackened and he could taste Corruption like rot in his mouth as it permeated everything. The healed wound reopened and instantly started to fester. Inside, his core sizzled and was being eaten away as if in a vat of acid.

Thomas fell to his knees, then all the way down as he started to convulse. Courtney casually stepped around him and started pushing buttons on the control panel.
