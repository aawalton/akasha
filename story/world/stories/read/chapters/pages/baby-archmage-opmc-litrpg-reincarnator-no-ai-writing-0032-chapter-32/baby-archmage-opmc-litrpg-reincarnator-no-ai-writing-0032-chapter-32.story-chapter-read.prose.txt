We were back on the road, traveling along a dirt road on the side of a jungle. I leafed through the leatherbound spellbook, at which everyone took turns ogling. Even though I knew most of the kinetic spell stuff by heart, it was good to check if I had missed a detail. The book detailed several variants of the Basic Kinetic spell. Kinetic Shield spell was a clear formula and the spell was easy to cast. The description detailed a formula for compressing kinetic energy into a barrier that would linger in front of you for a breath before vanishing.

The problem with it was that it was not a continuous spell. I didn’t want to get blasted just because I bungled the timing of the spell once. Did the writers of this book not understand how fast fights happened? Maybe they were just masters who could cast a spell instantly. I was getting fast, but not that fast. I was merely an aspiring magician, and I wouldn’t want to try to block my water spells.

Now, I had many gifts. My Skills, Attributes and Perks were aligned really nicely. I had talked to Piraka and mother about that buildcraft, and they were both astounded how airtight my build was. I had nothing to compare to really, but apparently my build didn’t have any secondary Attributes; they were all central to what my skillset was trying to do.

Perception and Focus gave me sufficient speed of thought, when combined with my two other magical Attributes, that I could cast Water Bullet with a second or two of preparation. This was when I just used the basic kinetic components with my cultivation.

And even with all these advantages I still wouldn’t want to try to time a Kinetic Shield to block a high velocity projectile.

So I needed to turn this spell into a continuous variant. There were a lot of components that I needed to master about the spell. I wanted to be able to manipulate the size, the durability and naturally make it continuous. Since I had the advantage of being able to draw mana from my environment, I could keep the shield spell on me at all times. That was the goal.

Maybe continuous spells were too expensive to cast, if most magicians were like Elder Piraka and couldn’t directly draw atmospheric mana, the math might not math for continuous spells. But mother and Alik were using the ambient mana for their Mana Absorption too, so I wasn’t sure. Maybe that was why it was an Epic Skill?Oh… the Skill could absorb and use ambient mana! I had kind of taken it for granted, since it was so close to Mana Manipulation. But I had a Unique variant of Mana Manipulation, which was likely a result of my Mythic Skill.

I did need to start with the basic Kinetic Shield first. That would give me the baseline before I started making my own adjustments. I fell into a meditative state and connected with the mana. I mostly ignored the chatter of my companions and fell into that happy place where there was only me and magic. This was how I spent our days of travel. The spellbook was open on the page of the Kinetic Shield, which I cross-referenced as I worked on the formula over and over again.

Was that too much to ask? Pfft, let a baby dream.
