“Hm... now this is what the smart humanoids would call a conundrum!”

Rusty had two options before him. He could either unlock the Library of Knowledge within the Soul Forge or the Training Chamber. The former would allow him to conduct research, as new books would usually appear whenever he interacted with something.

During his evolution, he had noticed that every enchantment he examined and tried to analyze eventually appeared in the library. Through it, he would be able to copy them all and make both his enchanted weaponry and his body stronger. However, the usual drawback remained. His body was made of twilight metals, which made incorporating other elemental spells much more difficult.

'It would do wonders for my shop, but...'

Rusty was not interested in becoming a crafter first. At heart, he was still a fighter, and crafting was only a means to an end. It gave him better gear, provided a steady income, and allowed him to support those around him, but it had never been his true goal. That goal was to become more powerful, although lately he had begun to wonder if his motivations were starting to change.

At first, he had been alone, confused, and guided only by the two spirits trapped within his body. Then he met his first companion, Gleam, along with the group of orphans under the little spirit's care. At the time, he had not expected to grow attached to any of them, but when the orcs broke through the gate, he had rushed back without hesitation to protect them.

Then there was Gwen, someone he still did not know whether to consider real or merely an illusion created by the system to grant him more power. Slowly, he had grown fond of her as well, to the point where he willingly spent precious system resources just to ensure she survived until their next meeting.

All of these relationships had slowly changed him, and he was no longer certain what he would do if they ever came into conflict with his ultimate goal of evolution.

'Now then, before Gleam gets here... what should I choose? I should probably look through my status screen and check the new skill I acquired as well.'

Rusty stared at the window before him, considering everything the Soul Forge had to offer. The ability to research the world in greater depth was certainly tempting, especially since it would complement the knowledge he already received from Aburdon and Alexander. Even so, the prospect of training against illusory opponents was equally enticing.

'I wonder... it never let me level up any skills during training before, but what if this time is different?'

A new possibility entered Rusty's mind. Dangerous people surrounded him, and in truth, he had no one powerful to rely on. His only allies were himself and Gleam, both still D-Rank creatures. If he unlocked the Training Chamber, he could spar against opponents far stronger than himself and discover their weaknesses, just as he had with the Ice Elemental.

That seemed far more valuable than any book. A book might describe an enemy's weak point in perfect detail, but it could never recreate the chaos of real combat. Knowing exactly where to strike with his halberd meant little if he could not land the blow while avoiding the countless skills, feints, and tricks his opponent would employ.

With that in mind, the choice became obvious. He reached out and pressed the floating window, unlocking the Training Chamber.

Once he did, his status screen flashed a few times before settling down. It was similar to the previous two times, appearing as a separate option. Just like with the Soul Forge and the Summoning Chamber, a new one had been created, and he could even enter it right away. However, knowing that these things always seemed to have hidden timers, he decided to save it for later, after he found a worthy opponent to practice against.

‘Now then…’

With that in mind, he glanced at his full status screen, which looked quite imposing.

One thing he noticed immediately was that his Durability had already reached C rank. That probably meant no weapon below that rank would be able to inflict much damage on his metallic body. However, that was not even the best part. Something else had caught his attention. His overskill points had increased.

‘Oh? Have I already maxed out some of my stats? Does that mean I'm already one of the strongest D-rank monsters? Could I actually be as strong as a C-rank monster with all the overpoints I have?’

His mind raced with possibilities as he noticed that both his Strength and Constitution had reached two hundred and twenty-five. Any remaining points had been converted into overpoints instead. The normal D-rank cap was two hundred and twenty-five, and anything beyond that was stored separately from the main attributes.

‘Those humanoids were right about taking things slow and steady. It's best not to evolve too quickly!’

The body he now occupied felt noticeably stronger than before. Soon, his attention shifted to the new skill he had received upon reaching level one as a Superior Enchanted Twilight High Steel Armor.

‘Hm... this one feels underwhelming... but maybe not.’

Reinforced Monster Core L1

Passive Skill

The monster core has been reinforced with a durable layer of twilight alloy, making it significantly harder to breach or destroy during combat. Reduces damage taken from critical strikes targeting the core by forty percent. In addition, the core is twenty-five percent more resistant to magical attacks.

The core was still there, though it looked slightly larger than before. A thin shell of twilight metal now encased it. After removing his helmet and giving it a few experimental pokes, he was convinced it would not break easily. More importantly, it was now more resistant to magical attacks, his greatest weakness.

‘It can be leveled up further... does that mean…’

It was a passive defensive skill with levels. That probably meant the only way to improve it was to let the core take direct hits, something that was far more dangerous than he liked.

‘Perhaps if I have Gleam attack me with a weaker spell, it won't be too bad…’

He had already endured a few lightning spells, along with attacks imbued with frost. Whenever mana-based attacks struck his core, strange jolts rippled through his body. Even for a monster made entirely of metal, the sensation was deeply unpleasant.

“(˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶)”

Just as he was contemplating his new passive skill, the door was pushed open by his favorite white ant friend. Gleam hopped inside and landed on the helmet he was holding.

"Oh, Gleam, how have you been?"

"(ᵕ—ᴗ—)"

"Oh, some people came looking for me? What did they want, and how long was I evolving?"

"( • ̀ω •́ )"

Gleam hopped off his helmet onto a nearby workbench covered with a few of his tools, then began explaining.

"Oh? The new mercenary leader was looking for me?"

The one asking for him was Galiena, the leader of the mercenary group he had recently joined. The Orc attack was over, and he had been told he would have some free time, so he had assumed no one would come looking for him.

"(˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶)"

"I see, so this evolution took almost two days to complete?"

That was useful information for the future. If he attempted the next evolution, he would probably need to find shelter for up to three days.

"Rolo came up with a good excuse for me? But she came back half a day later? Hmm..."

Rusty had just finished evolving and planned to visit the local dungeon to test his new skills. However, to maintain his secondary identity as the second in command of the mercenary group, he needed to answer the summons.

'I wasn't planning to use it yet, but I might as well...'

After thinking for a moment, he turned to Gleam with a request.

"Could you watch over my body for a while? It shouldn't take long."

"(╹ -╹)?"

Gleam looked confused for a moment but then wiggled her antennae in agreement. Rusty sat down and selected the option to enter the newly unlocked training chamber. Moments later, he was transported into an empty room with nothing but a console rising from the center.

"It looks a bit different, and there doesn't seem to be any way out... but I bet there's a timer, so I don't need to worry about that!"

He stepped up to the console before him. At first it was awkward to use because his thick, armored fingers were not made for delicate precision, but he eventually managed to operate the glowing interface and quickly became proficient at navigating it.

The training chamber was remarkably intricate. It could recreate various locations he had visited, including forests, bandit camps, and even the dungeon where he had been born, turning them into battlefields. This allowed him to train in a wide variety of scenarios. As Alexander had once taught him, making proper use of the terrain could often allow someone to defeat an opponent who was stronger.

"Who should I test this body against... the Ice Elemental? Or perhaps an older enemy?"

After selecting a simple grassy field without any obstacles, he browsed through the list. He could type the name of the opponent he wanted or simply scroll through the available options. The monsters were categorized by type, and one of the more unusual choices caught his attention. Himself.

“This might be the best way to test the difference then... I select you, old me!”

His own body had its own category. He could even select the corroded bronze body the dungeon had originally summoned him into, making it one of the best ways to compare himself to his old self. Soon, another living armor appeared before him, one forged from twilight metal, the same body he had used during his adventures with Gwen.

He summoned his usual weapon, a halberd, and this time wielded it without a shield. It was the improved version forged from Superior Enchanted Twilight High Steel, while his illusory reflection carried his old lightning halberd and wore a body made from regular Enchanted Twilight High Steel.

The reflection tilted its head as a faint purple glow burst to life within its eye slits and it settled into a ready stance. It advanced just as he did. The construct possessed all of his old skills and was almost as capable, but it had one major weakness. While it could perfectly mimic every tactic and attack he had known before, it could never do anything beyond them. It was an older version of himself, forever frozen at that point in time. That made it the perfect training partner for testing his new body.

The air cracked as the simulated armor released a burst of stored twilight energy, using it to propel itself into a lightning-fast thrust. Rusty met the charge with his own weapon, but instead of deflecting the attack or dispersing the lightning magic it carried, he deliberately let it strike to test his new core.

“Ow...”

His current helmet lacked any defensive enchantment against lightning, so when the energy surged through it, he still took damage, just far less than he would have before evolving.

“It’s bearable, but it still makes my body lock up...”

The phantom armor lunged again, immediately taking advantage of his brief stiffness. It swept the heavy blade of its lightning halberd in a horizontal arc aimed straight at his exposed neck.

Rusty forced his locked joints to respond and raised his arm, not to avoid the blow, but to let it strike again. This time he wanted to test the durability of his body armor. The halberd crackled with lightning as it slammed into his armored forearm. The force of the impact sent him sliding backward, but just as he had expected, the damage inflicted by the D-rank weapon was minimal.

“Just some scratches. This body really is C rank now. If that’s the case... let’s see how this weapon performs.”

After confirming how much punishment he could withstand, it was time to counterattack. Both he and his illusion activated their twilight energy and launched into a flurry of blows. As expected, he was now both faster and tougher. Every clash favored him. The old halberd slowly chipped away against the superior alloy of his new weapon, and even when the phantom managed to land a hit, it inflicted far less damage than before.

Rusty brought the heavy head of his new halberd down in a crushing overhead strike. His duplicate raised its weapon to parry, but the superior weight and density of the new alloy shattered the phantom halberd on impact. The blade continued through the fake armor, cleaving it from shoulder to hip before the illusion burst apart into a cloud of fading mana particles.

"Not bad at all."

It was now clear that his evolved body was much stronger, and the weapons he wielded could probably deal serious damage to even a C-rank monster, something he was already quite close to reaching with his overpoints.

"Now then, if I fight someone else, how much time do I have in here?"

He had only spent around fifteen minutes fighting his copy. As he approached the console, he noticed something new. Various statistics were now displayed on it, something that had not been there before.

"A threat level? Interesting..."

After fighting the old version of himself, a new entry had appeared beside it in the selection menu.

"Threat level: Low-Intermediate?"

He immediately understood what it meant without any of his guides having to explain it. The rating reflected how much trouble he had experienced against his copy. If every opponent had a similar rating, choosing future training partners would become much easier.

"What about those Ice Elementals..."

He quickly navigated to the section containing his recent encounter with the Ice Elemental. Even though he had not fought it this time, its threat level was already listed. It was marked as High. The summoner who controlled the two creatures, however, was rated as Extreme.

"This will be handy..."

Knowing how dangerous an opponent was before facing them would be invaluable. While there were skills in this world that allowed people to analyze an enemy's status, they were far from reliable and did not always work.

"Okay then, who's next..."

After fiddling with the interface for a while, he noticed a timer in the upper right corner of the panel. He had around forty minutes remaining, which meant he could stay here for a full hour. With that in mind, he selected the Ice Elemental again. Even though he was still weaker than it, he wanted to know whether he stood any chance against such a creature if he ever encountered one in the wild.
