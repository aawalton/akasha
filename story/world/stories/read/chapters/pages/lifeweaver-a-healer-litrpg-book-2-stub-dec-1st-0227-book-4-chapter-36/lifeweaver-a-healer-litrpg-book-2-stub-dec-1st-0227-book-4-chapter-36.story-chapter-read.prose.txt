Standing over the now dead Singers, with humans screeching as they fled up the road, Luke wondered what the hell had just happened. He'd received a bunch of messages without the invaders noticing, but the moment he sent one himself, they were all over him. An item, he decided. Or maybe one of them had a strange skill that provided information. Too bad for them it didn't tell them how utterly outclassed they were.

Dot: "Luke... I'm in Chicago."

You have blocked an Integrated from messaging you.

He reached out to the weave and stepped through it, deep in thought. Healing himself, he nodded to Milla. "This is the replacement?"

"How is Ella?" Milla asked.

Luke wasn't sure what to say. "...Not great."

"Yeah," Milla said as she made a face. "Penny told me what happened." Luke saw her pushing the disturbed expression from her face as she turned to their guest. "Yes, this is Raphael. He has the same profession as Hannah."

"Raphael," the older, Italian-looking gentleman said as he reached out a hand. His voice had a faint accent.

Luke shook it. "Luke. You know how to find the portals?"

"I do, but I would like to hear more about what happened to this Ella."

"Trust me, you don't," Luke said, turning back to his sister. "Closing portals is still the plan? Ray told you about the attack?"

"The big guns? He told me, but we don't know anything about the where or the how. Not sure what we can do about it just yet, except hope your little buddy finds more."

"This is all feeling very inefficient," Luke grunted. "I mean, how many portals are left? Hundreds?"

"How many have you closed?"

Luke shrugged. "I'm not sure. Ella closed a few. Maybe thirty? Forty?"

"We had two hundred and forty-three mapped at the start of this."

He groaned.

"You have a better idea? I'm listening," she said. "Singers all over the world are retreating to protect their portals. Our attacks are having an effect."

A thought struck Luke. "Maybe that's how we do this."

"You have that look again," Milla said, with more than a little worry in her voice.

"Give me a few minutes to try something," Luke said, waving for Raphael, who had been standing there unbothered, to follow over to the couches.

Once they were seated, Luke reached over to Raphael with a thread of mana. "Don't worry, this won't hurt."

"What are you trying?" Milla asked before checking her wristwatch. "We need to get going."

"I have a hunch. Or maybe not a hunch, but something I've been thinking about for a while, something that should be possible. I just never get the chance to try things since all I do is run around from one fight to the next, putting out fires and dealing with catastrophes. Just give me a moment to try this."

Luke closed his eyes. While the system weave was accessible to him at all times at this point, what he was about to attempt was something new, uncharted waters. For that, he wanted calm and to focus. When using Weavestep to move through the weave, he always used an echo thread. Those threads glowed, making them easier to distinguish from the near-infinite others just like it in the weave. But, other than that glow, it was just another thread, wasn't it? If that was the case, shouldn't Weavestep work with any old thread?

If that was the case, and Luke believed it was, the obvious problem that followed would be to find which thread to use. That was at the forefront of his mind when he submerged himself in the weave. Holding on to Raphael through a thread showed what he had hoped for. A thread. The idea that a single Integrated could be reduced to a thread was stupid, of course, but the idea still held merit. Raphael was not connected to the weave and all it represented through just a single thread, but to Luke's still-new senses, that was how the multitudes showed themselves. A single person, a single thread. That was his first time seeing a non-echo thread and knowing who it belonged to. His first thought was morbid as hell, wondering what would happen if he cut through it. Focusing on something more productive, Luke held the thread close, inspecting it with the full force of his focus.

Boon of Potential grants you an attribute point (+1 Focus).

There it was. Once he found what he'd been looking for, it was so obvious he didn't know why he hadn't seen it before. An Integrated's thread was different to his senses. More robust than most other threads in the weave, in a way, but also more fragile. The revelation rippled through the weave like a wave that brought with it unprecedented change. Threads changed color, or perhaps gained color was a better way of putting it. Threads belonging to those who had touched the gray orb stood out, blue like threads of mana against the otherwise grayscale background. So many of them appeared that it boggled the mind. When Luke first laid eyes on the weave, he perceived the individual threads for a moment, but it had been too painful, too big, to keep in his mind. Instead, it had defaulted to a sort of zoomed-out version where individual threads became less obvious, and the larger pattern appeared. Now, having gained experience, he saw both versions at the same time, and the pattern made by Integrated threads was beautiful in a way he couldn't being to describe. Luke saw all of them. Every single thread. The echo threads were all in there, glowing brighter blue now, but with this deeper understanding, Luke could pluck out any one thread and hold it for a moment. This was it. This was what he had been looking for, what he needed.

He dropped the thread connecting him to Raphael, and that specific thread dropped away, joining the pattern. No matter how hard Luke tried, he couldn't bring it back, couldn't distinguish it from the other threads. That meant he couldn't just find a specific Integrated in that whole beautiful chaos and go there, but at this point, he was certain weavestepping to a random Integrated would be possible. Even so, he tested it, choosing one at random. Opening his eyes as he appeared, Luke found himself in what appeared to be a large cell with barred windows, where the bare concrete wall was lined with devices he knew all too well. Integrated were being held, stuck in machines that drained their mana to keep them subdued. Singers were all over the place, and they had sounded the alarm the moment Luke appeared.

"Oh, hell no," Luke said just as the captured humans realized an Integrated on their side had appeared.

Milla: "Luke? Where did you go?"

The door opened, and a large group of Singers rushed in with weapons raised and spells at the ready. Luke stepped up to meet them. A Singer at the front thrust a spear right at the center of Luke's chest, but a thread of mana was enough to push that to the side even as Luke ducked forward and under a blade slash. It brought him to the spear-wielding Singer's chest, and he pushed with his shoulder, knocking that one into the one behind it. Both of them toppled over. Luke weavestepped on top of them, past the other Singers, and lashed out with Weaver's Whip, severing their heads from their shoulders. The threads glowed orange with infernal mana, and Luke couldn't help but feel a moment of satisfaction when he saw fear in the Singers' faces.

"First time seeing mana change color?" he asked before he leapt at them, shouting.

Their moment of hesitation was their undoing. It wouldn't have mattered, seeing how weak they were, but their lack of nerves made the whole process quicker. Taking all their mana into himself before ending their lives also meant more time wasted, and Luke's meta-heart bulged and strained with the effort as he cut mana-draining devices and restraints apart. Each captured Integrated received a measure of mana to kick-start their regeneration once they were free, with Luke having the presence of mind to keep the untinted mana separate from the infernal mana, giving the former rather than the latter to the freed humans.

"Where are we?" he asked a woman who looked like she'd been held there for quite a while. Her long, dark hair hung in front of her face, greasy and unwashed.

When she pulled it aside, he saw she was a lot younger than he had initially thought. "I don't know," she said, her voice hoarse from disuse. Even so, she spoke with an Australian accent.

"It's an old prison," an older guy said as he massaged his bruised wrists. His accent was much thicker, to the point Luke was surprised he hadn't ended the sentence with 'mate', but maybe that was just a stereotype. "There are others here too, mate," the guy added.

Luke struggled to keep a straight face when he nodded and asked, "I don't have much time, so do you want to help me free your friends?"

Their roar of approval told him they did.

Luke: "I think I've found a solution to our problem. I'll be right back."

Milla: "You've always had a flair for theatrics, little brother. It's pretty annoying, not going to lie."

Luke grinned as he stormed out of the room with a group of fifteen Integrated at his back. Like he'd told them, time was short, so he didn't hold back when he launched himself off the ground with a thread to land in a clump of arriving Singers. Their mana fueled his attacks, and he got into a good rhythm of draining then killing, draining then killing, and even took a few meta-hearts along the way. Those, he'd feed to Smudge once the now-not-so-little voidling returned from his excursion into the woods around Hannah's old haunts. Perhaps feeding the metaphysical hearts of those he killed to a pet creature from the void should bring up some ethical concerns, but Luke found he didn't much care. They deserved no quarter. If they surrendered, maybe he'd let them go back to their own world, but so far, none had even attempted to lay down arms.

The prison wasn't all that large, and the number of Singers and their strength was laughable considering how many Integrated humans they watched over. Random chance had brought him to this place. Just the luck of the draw, but after freeing more than fifty people, he let them know what The Band and the rest of the Earth Defense Pact were up to, and received applause for it. That was awkward as hell, so Luke escaped through the weave.

At first, he'd considered going back to Milla right away, but there was one more thing he needed to make sure worked before he could call at least the setup to his plan a success. Luke appeared right next to Penny, who was soaring high up in the air and in the middle of calling down rolling bolts of lightning on a large contingent of Singers.

Penny looked down at him with a puzzled expression as he fell screaming. "Luke?"
