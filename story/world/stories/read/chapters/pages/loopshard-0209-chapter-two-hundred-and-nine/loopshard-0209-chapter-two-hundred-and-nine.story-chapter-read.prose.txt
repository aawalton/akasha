The effects are too good. There has to be a stronger weakness than what is listed outright.

Chien would benefit from it, since he lost his headband and has so far gotten hit in every major fight… Of course, it likely would be no different to him than if I put on a First Light mask, given his patron’s opposition to the Forlorn Shadow.

“Keep him still,” Adam said. “I’m going to try something.”

The force making him violent is more powerful than me.

Adam would love to believe it was the latter, but given that Chien had heard ‘the angel’ talking to him, encouraging him to violence, it made him unsure.
