Whispers spread through the audience as a scandal unfolded before their eyes. Such investigations were never public, and the details were often hidden even after an investigation had concluded. Tomorrow, this would be the talk of the entire city!

El’Shenawy was brewing Muscle Sap and is concretely connected to the Cult of the Broken Compass, whom we have determined to be the dealers. The entire ring has been laid out. I can’t target Colorful now, but so be it. Gary Steel humiliated me today. He must fall—immediately! Elder Sui thought. He could already see his triumph. Accolades would rain on him from above for succeeding where a senior inspector failed, and the shame of today would be forgotten. He might even get promoted to senior inspector himself! They might invite him to the clan headquarters!!

Snapping back to the present, Elder Sui realized that Whitefang hadn’t spoken yet. His brow was deeply furrowed as he stared at something in the booklet.

This will paint him in such a bad light… Elder Sui thought, hiding his ugly grin. It’s unbecoming of an Elder, but since it helps me, I can push a little more…

This way, the people will know the truth, and the rumors against our clan will be limited...”

Agree, agree! Elder Sui begged inwardly. If Gary Steel says the wrong thing now, incriminating Colorful is still possible!

Whitefang still wasn’t responding. Elder Sui finally worried a little. “Senior Inspector?” he asked.

Whitefang’s knuckles were white around the booklet. He was gripping it so hard he almost tore it apart. He raised a fierce gaze.

“Branch Elder Sui Deng…” he said harshly, abandoning all decorum. “Do you know what you’ve brought me?”

“Evidence,” Elder Sui said.

Check this part right here! It’s a signature! A signature belonging to someone named Zink! Do you know who that is, Sui Deng?”

All you did is connect that private lab to the four brothers, and even prove they were running it themselves! Who knows if Asim El’Shenawy was even aware of this? Did anyone testify that he entered this lab or registered it in person?”

Of course, Elder Sui had tried to get this testimony. He’d just failed. Everyone involved said they didn’t recall who had registered this lab or entered it. The signed testimony he’d mentioned before only involved the booklet being found in the lab. “It’s in Asim El’Shenawy’s name, and he’s disappeared for days. If running away isn’t guilt, I don’t know what it is,” he said.

“You know how lax those labs are! All they care about is getting paid! We get fake names there every month—it could be any disciple of the Witch Coven!”

There were many people from the Witch Coven present, but nobody elected to speak. Even their Branch Elders stayed silent. This was, indeed, the case. The security at their labs was full of holes on purpose. The only laws respected by the Coven were their own.

“We’ll post a warrant for Asim El’Shenawy to question him, but this is nowhere near proven guilt, let alone guilt that transfers to someone else just because they’re rumored to be friends!” Whitefang said. “Since the booklet was there, I’m inclined to think he wasn’t involved in the first place! The brothers just used his name because he’s a drafter with no connections, one of the few to enter the Coven this year!”

“Um, excuse me…” a hesitant voice came from somewhere in the audience. Everyone looked to find a short, slim, sharp-faced, brown young man dressed in black robes with dark green trimming. “I’m Asim El’Shenawy…” he said, buckling under everyone’s pressure.

Asim! What are you doing here? Why are you speaking!? Gary thought, but he kept his face calm.

Whitefang leapt through the arena to land close to Asim. The nearby spectators rushed to make space. “I’m Senior Inspector Whitefang of the Heaven Reaching clan’s Enforcement Hall. I possess the power to detect lies. May I ask you some questions?” he asked directly.

“Are you the Asim El’Shenawy who’s a member of the Witch Coven, a drafter, and a friend of Gary Steel?”

“Yeah,” Asim replied.

“No.”

If you want me to arrest the disciple of another faction with such non-existent evidence, you must be dreaming!” Whitefang said, turning back to Asim. “Were you aware of the existence of a Muscle Sap ring?” he asked.

“No.”

“Were you aware of what was going on in the lab that was booked in your name?”

“I didn’t even know that lab existed. But no, I wasn’t aware.”

“I saw them in the Floor of Trials, but we never spoke…”

“I was hunting monsters to level up. The invasion quest is in three days.”

Asim looked down. He was clearly wearing black robes. “Uh… Yes?”

“Silence!” Whitefang barked over him. “Sui Deng! Today, you have publicly shamed the clan, you have failed in your duty as Head of the Clan Floor’s Enforcement Hall, and you have attempted to frame a disciple out of personal grudges. Additionally, of your own admission, you withheld evidence from the main investigator—myself—for several days. If you had handed me that booklet earlier, I might have been able to act more strongly against the brothers in the city. This was all done to gather prestige for yourself and to satisfy your personal interests. The acts you have committed are not only immoral and despicable, but they are also punishable crimes according to clan and imperial law! Branch Head, do I have permission to arrest Sui Deng so he may be tried at the clan headquarters’ Enforcement Hall?”

“You’re making a mistake! Don’t you see they’re playing us? They must have colluded with the brothers to get that booklet and lead us to this situation! Asim El’Shenawy is an alchemist—he must have ingested a Dishonesty Potion! The witches are covering for him, don’t you see!?” Elder Sui raved desperately.

He had no idea how this all played out, or whether Asim truly did ingest that potion and so managed to fool the inspector, but in all honesty, it was nice for things to work out for nice. He had no power to wonder, anyway. All he wanted right now was sleep.

“Thank God. How are you?” Colorful said, landing next to Gary.

“Ambitious,” Gary replied. “Watch me, Master. I’m about to thank you for everything you taught me.”

Before Colorful could reply, Gary had leapt to the stairs and leapt again, all the way to the VIP stands, landing solidly on the stairs amongst Branch Elders and other high-level figures. Even with everyone restraining their auras, the combined pressure made him dizzy.

You threw everything you had at me, and I still made it through. Even though I cultivate black lightning and hold revenge in my heart, I will let bygones be bygones, and I will forget about everything you did to me. After today, you and I will be as if we have never met. We will be even.”

Gary had been walking while speaking, arriving in front of the chained Elder. Elder Sui—with his long white hair, bloodshot eyes, and furious snarl—didn’t reply. Anything I say lowers my reputation further…but, so be it. The family will bail me out and move me to another branch. It will be fine, he thought.

No matter which branch he was transferred to, this event would hound him forever.

Slapping the Elder was risky, but he didn’t do it for no reason.
