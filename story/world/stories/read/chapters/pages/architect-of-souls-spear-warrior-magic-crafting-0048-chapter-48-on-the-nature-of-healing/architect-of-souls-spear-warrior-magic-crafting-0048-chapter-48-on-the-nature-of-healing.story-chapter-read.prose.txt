Serena had once again arrived first and was waiting in a room with the same table, cot, and chairs as last time. When Ferris carried Jade inside, his eyes immediately went to the knife she had placed on the table.

“Ah, Ferris, welcome!” She immediately stood up and waved to him as he entered.

“Serena, thank you.” Ferris walked over to sit on the cot, Jade still in his arms. “I am happy to say that I am ready to begin today.”

Looking around the training room, Ferris considered his uncle’s secrecy. The research project included discussing details of his cultivation aloud with Serena. He would need to be careful to only say aloud secrets he was willing to allow others to know. At least here, it was likely only Hollowfield listening.

“Excellent!” Her eyes lit up with excitement at his words. “Now, Elder Harper said that we should trigger Jade’s healing ability while observing it both physically and spiritually. I will watch and record the process after causing a cut, and you will see what happens inside Jade’s soul.”

“I am a bit apprehensive about hurting Jade, even knowing they will heal…” Ferris said. “It feels like someone kicking my bonded weapon.”

Serena frowned. There were few insults greater than causing intentional harm to another cultivator’s bonded weapon. Walking up and kicking it would be taken as a death threat.

“I know,” Ferris continued, “that we have to do this. I just want to take precautions. Despite Jade’s body being able to heal, the core is still vulnerable.”

“Yes,” Serena nodded, apparently happy to be back on track, “I have done this experiment several times with the slimes at the ranch, and never unintentionally killed one.”

Ferris paled a bit at the word ‘unintentionally,’ but Serena just continued without noticing.

“This blade is magically enhanced to be stronger, and can resist the slime’s acid. I have made long cuts through a couple of different slimes’ bodies. From the outside, the slime’s body looks all the same. But once you start cutting, there is a clear difference between the outer shell and acidic interior. The outer shell is harmless to touch, but it appears to be selectively permeable. When the slime eats, the shell molds itself around the food, keeping the liquid sealed inside.”

“That sounds like we should test feeding Jade as well.”

“Way ahead of you.” Serena smiled, and pulled a handful of carrots out of her bag. She placed them on the table and began using the expensive magical weapon to chop them up into bite-sized pieces.

Jade locked onto the food, immediately recognizing it. The slime started rippling in anticipation. Ferris looked down at his familiar and considered.

“I will explain what we are going to do to Jade before we begin, but… I am not sure how they will react to being attacked. They are still freshly bonded, and learning. I won’t be awake to give instructions.”

Ferris unwound the magical chain from around its cage, still clamped protectively around Jade’s core.

“For that reason,” he said, “I intend to restrict Jade with this. Please stay outside of Jade’s range to prevent any accidents.” Ferris was more worried for Jade than Serena. She would easily win a fight, but there weren’t many non-lethal ways to stop a slime. If it came down to a choice between her life or Jade’s, Ferris expected her to choose her own. It would be much better if she could simply step back out of range. The cage around Jade’s core would also provide its usual layer of protection, which Ferris appreciated.

Serena rolled her eyes a bit.

“Worst case, I could just run away, but that is fine.”

“Thank you for your understanding,” Ferris said.

Serena nodded and continued both chopping and her explanation.

“Now, here’s where it gets interesting. When I sliced the slimes with a small cut, three distinct things happened. First, some of the inner liquid immediately leaked out. Second, about three seconds later the leak stopped, but the wound in the outer shell still existed. Third, around ten seconds after that, the outer shell started closing over the cut, taking various times to fully heal. Usually, it was about a minute.”

Ferris got excited, looking at Jade. To keep the liquid inside the body… there must be active control of it!

This brought to mind all the ways Jade manipulated their acid body: rippling, bubbling, and swirling. Jade could also roll around and grip things… All of this, the slime somehow did without muscles or tendons. There must be some magical control of the acid that allowed manipulation.

If Ferris could figure it out, and enhance it, then could he use his clan’s Raging Stream Jagged Point method? Probably… not. His uncle had said the clan methods required massive scale. Still, it could still be a basis for a future manipulation ability, or something to help with the spellcasting ability.

“Next, I tried a large cut that separated and removed most of the slime’s body before they could heal. I assume you have seen how the slime ranchers harvest fertilizer?”

Ferris nodded, remembering the slimes hung in a cage while a rancher used a wide blade to chop off the slime’s body.

“I caused a similar grievous wound, and the slime acted differently than the small cut. They immediately became unresponsive and wouldn’t move around for a few hours. Just like the smaller cut, the inner acid stopped leaking after about three second. However, the outer wound took about three times as long to heal.

Looking down at Jade, Ferris felt embarrassed at how little he understood the slime’s body. The idea that Jade’s outer shell was different than the inside made sense in retrospect, but he hadn’t thought about it before now. He had tried to examine Jade, but Serena’s methods—both brutal and inquisitive—showed he had a lot to learn.

“One more thing,” Serena said, waiting until she had his full attention. “I once tested how long a healthy slime can continue healing small cuts. There were two points of interest.”

“First, after about a minute of quick injuries the slime started excessively leaking acid. They shrunk to about half normal size before it stopped. At this point the slime was unresponsive just like after a single large dismemberment. Additional cuts still healed like normal, and the slime once again recovered in a few hours.”

Ferris looked down at Jade. He’d always heard that slimes ignored physical attacks that didn’t hit the core, but apparently there was more to it. A limit implied that there was something fueling the healing, like qi or physical stamina. Did too much healing just make the slime tired, or was it something else? What happened to cause the shrinking and loss of control of their inner acid?

“Second,” Serena continued, “I continued to give small cuts while the slime was in this unresponsive shrunken state. It took a while, but after about an hour the slime appeared to lose the ability to keep their shape. They melted down to just a slimy core.”

Ferris shuddered a bit at the thought of that happening to Jade.

“Surprisingly, the slime didn’t die,” Serena continued, “It took about a day, but it slowly grew back up to the shrunken size. I fed it food, and within a few hours it was back to normal.”

At least the slime hadn’t died from overuse of its healing, Ferris thought. This matches what I heard before: as long as the core is intact the slime will survive. Just a series of limits to learn and plan around.

Ferris wrapped the chain around his waist and enclosed Jade’s core within the cage. The only thing to do was to learn more. “Jade can signal you when I am ready. Please only make small slices, and wait for another signal between each cut.”

They worked out a system where Jade would rock left and right to signal for food, or up and down for the cut. Jade was happy to show off the signals, especially when Serena rewarded them with a piece of carrot. Ferris laid down on the cot, with Jade held in his lap.

“Jade,” Ferris said over the bond, “Serena here is a friend, she is helping me.”

“Serena has food, she can be my friend too!”

“Yes, but she is helping me test how your body works.”

Jade swirled a bit in confusion.

“She will feed you food, and I will watch how your Consume Pool ability works in your soul.”

“Yes!” Jade started rippling at the mention of food, and Ferris wasn’t sure if the slime really listened beyond that.

“But then, we also need to test how your body heals. She will make some small cuts. It might hurt, but she won’t touch your core. Please, don’t attack her.”

Jade stopped rippling, and went back to swirling.

“If Ferris says so, I will try…” Jade hesitantly said.

“Okay,” Ferris said aloud to Serena, “let’s try a cut while I am awake.”

Serena used the knife to make a small incision in Jade’s body, only cutting the length of a fingernail deep before pulling the blade out. Ferris watched in fascination as a small stream of green acid bled out, then stopped just as Serena had claimed. About ten second later, the wound magically began closing back up. It took another half minute or so, but soon the wound was gone as if it had never existed. The green acid, however, had leaked down onto Ferris’ shirt.

He jumped up, noticing the wet green spot. Ferris had been distracted watching Jade heal. With a groan, he set the slime on the floor. The acid was much less potent than when inside Jade, but it would still cause irritation and burns over time. Hopefully the shirt wasn’t ruined. Thankfully, the ground of the training hall was an absorbent sand meant to be easily replaced. Some abilities practiced here were very destructive, after all.

Awkwardly, Ferris decided to just lay on the ground, holding Jade next to him where the acid could drain into the sand. Thankfully the slime hadn’t reacted at all to the cut… it was only Ferris that had jumped. Serena simply sat down in the sand next to Jade.

“Well, I think we are ready. I will meditate and observe the spiritual effects of Jade’s healing,” Ferris said, awkwardly closing his eyes under the young woman’s gaze. He tried to focus on everything that had happened that morning, and not on Serena, then let all thoughts drift away. Uncle Winton had given him so much to think about, but in the end, he still needed to focus on learning Acid Reconstruction for any of it to matter…

As usual, he eventually opened his eyes next to his Heartfire in the soulspace. Jade had not joined him this time, as the slime stayed awake to signal Serena. Ferris’ body was touching Jade, so he was able to go to the slimespace by touching the core pedestal. Ferris took the bone spear, not wanting to go anywhere unarmed.

The slimespace was as lively as ever, with the birds above and the ants below. The massive chain cage was in the Consume Pool, with its giant chain extending until it was sheared off instead of wrapped around in a ball like usual. Ferris found the best vantage point he could from atop the Heartfire’s tower, then asked Jade through the bond to signal for a piece of food.

A swarm of birds gathered, circling the acid waterfall as a giant piece of carrot appeared. Just like when Ferris had tested Jade eating the rice ball, the same series of events played out. This time, he wasn’t on the ground next to the pool, and noticed movement off in the distance.

Ferris asked Jade to signal Serena for another piece of food, and then carefully watched. It was hard to see clearly, but he spotted something like a discoloration of the dome.

It took about five minutes to walk up to the side of the dome where he noticed the oddity. There wasn’t much else around, other than a couple of the yellow pillars. Approaching the edge of the dome, Ferris asked Jade to signal for another piece of food.

This time Ferris was able to watch the process up close. The vibrant green dome typically had an incandescent glow, lighting up the entire area inside. Now, the glow disappeared in a large circle, suspiciously about the same size that the Consume Pool enlarged a piece of carrot.

All the nearby birds converged on the darkened area, flying frantically around it. Ferris wasn’t sure how, but he felt they were related to Jade’s Consume Pool ability. This was only the second thing he had seen the birds interact with, but both happened when Jade was eating.

The glow returned to the dome as the darkened area shrank from the outside in, and the grey birds dispersed. Ferris heard the expected splash of the carrot hitting the Consume Pool behind him.

He spent some time watching Jade eat five more pieces of carrots, and the same process would play out every time. The dome wall’s glow would darken, the birds would gather, the darkness would shrink away, the carrot would appear above the acid pool, and then the birds would leave. From what he heard over the bond, Jade was having a ball.

Elder Harper expected him to prepare a report on this process, based on the instructions from First Steps of the Soul. The jade slip had made special note of the strange vibration Ferris felt around abilities. He only noticed it when trying, otherwise it faded into the background.

Here around the dome, watching it darken and lighten as Jade ate, Ferris felt… nothing. No vibration. No strange feelings, like being near a meridian or otherwise.

He did feel the vibration near the Consume Pool, and this dome was clearly related as the very visible magical glow reacted when Jade ate. Yet, there was no vibration to be felt out here.

The second instruction from the jade slip was to focus on conceptual parts of the ability. All abilities would have concepts, which would have spiritual representations. For example, his Air Passage had a breeze in it representing its concept of ‘breathing.’

The breeze changed direction, in time with Ferris’ breath. This was also an important detail for understanding the Air Passage, as the flow of air entered carrying qi and was expelled stripped bare.

Most of this he had intuitively understood, but having First Steps of the Soul explain it didn’t hurt.

After he understood the concepts behind an ability’s representation in his soul, he could work to change it. Perhaps, for some strange reason, Ferris wanted to change his Air Passage to heat the air he breathed. He could build a fire in the trench, heating the air as it passed through. This might, with some careful testing, change the ability.

However, if the change didn’t align with the concept, it wouldn’t work. ‘Breathing’ naturally encompassed the idea of bringing something into the body, which made it work well for gathering qi.

First Steps of the Soul warned there were rules, some understood and some not. Trying out different modifications to an ability was a core part of growing stronger. More drastic changes usually required resources, which usually contained their own concepts that could be added to the ability.

For Assimilate Object, eating the fruit had already taught Ferris some of its concepts. The frame represented Jade’s body, which the artificial organ would anchor onto. That’s why he felt confident using the chain demon’s fort would strengthen the defensive potential of the ability.

For the Consume Pool, Ferris was less sure. He felt pretty confident the pool itself acted like a stomach, dissolving food into nutrients.

But whatever was happening out here with the grey birds… he didn’t understand. The dome itself had always reminded Ferris of Jade’s slime body. Perhaps this part was related to the semi-permeability of the slime’s outer shell?

Ferris decided it was time to continue the experiment, and asked Jade to signal Serena to perform a cut. He watched the wall with anticipation, wondering how the healing ability would appear.

After a few seconds, a huge tear appeared in the dome.

Ferris wasn’t directly under the hole, but he was still far too close. He screamed and tried to run as a wave of acid blasted in, drenching anything nearby—including Ferris.

Covered in the liquid he dropped his spear, fell to the ground, and tried to roll the acid off. Unfortunately, the area around him was already flooded.

Indifferent to his struggle, the acid worked quickly. Ferris soon lost consciousness, the agonizing burning turning to cold numbness.
