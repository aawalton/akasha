Turning his body away from the group, Elion pointed the rifle at the ground. He thumbed the safety a few times, flicking it on and off. He inspected the powder feed mechanism, but couldn’t figure out how it worked. A label beneath a dial on the side of the powder hopper was labeled ‘power.’

He’d never seen a firearm like this before, with separate feeds for bullets and for powder. *I don’t think any guns on earth work like this.*

Prator’s mouth moved, but Elion couldn’t hear or understand him. He took out one earplug.

“Arnolfo is out there,” Prator said, speaking to his guard. “He’s being blockaded by that army, who appear intent on starving us out. As far as I’m concerned, that’s an act of war. They need to know who they’re dealing with here.”

Prator reached the barricade and jumped atop it, slashing with his sword as reinforcements figured out where they were being attacked and swarmed to defend.

The Aurelian atop the wall in full golden armor made a striking figure against the dark night sky.

Elion took aim through his scope at a man’s head, just visible over the top of the wall.

Prator continued fighting as cargo hauling arachnatronics, most of them not more than eight feet tall, scrambled up the barricade and past Prator, scuttling down the road toward the New Kairn Tol gates.

One of the spider-mechs transporting cargo was struck by a bolt of blue light. It staggered to the side, but continued on it’s path. Elion searched for another target through his scope.

So far, none of the soldiers on the barricades had managed to return effective fire against Prator’s guard. Each of them remained far back from the fighting, picking off soldiers with their rifles.

Elion was the closest one to the barricade.
