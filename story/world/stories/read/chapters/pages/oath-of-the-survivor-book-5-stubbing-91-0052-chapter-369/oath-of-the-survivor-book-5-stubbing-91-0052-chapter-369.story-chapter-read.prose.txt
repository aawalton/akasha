Kyle nearly missed the trail on his road back to Duroc’s village, his mind racing as he thought about the implications of Marcus’ message.  With all this business concluding, he’d soon be able to get back and visit Garth, Frank, and the other refugees from Earth.  With some luck, and financial support from Corthian Mining, Kyle hoped the logistics for the relocation would run smoothly.  Relocation was never easy, and anything they could do to help streamline it would be a huge benefit.  It would be worthwhile work.  Still, he needed to be thoughtful.  Kyle was adding items to his to-do list markedly faster than he was checking them off.

He owed Marcus and the refugees time and energy to get them established in their new home.  There was an endless amount of planting to be done between crops, trees, and simply getting enough roots in the ground to help keep the damaged land from collapsing into the tunnels below.  Kyle also had some work outstanding for the Pillar, who weren’t pushing him too hard yet, but would certainly want their job completed sooner than later.  He also had to keep leveraging his network to pursue Gregory Valentine’s location, follow-up on the mysterious forces backing Ger’Sinh, and make a visit to the Alabaster Court to meet with their Lorekeeper.

All of that was going to have to wait until after Kyle dealt with the DeRosa issue.  If Benjamin was right, and the fungal creatures were being controlled from this moon, he had every intention of stamping out DeRosa permanently.  His decision to let DeRosa live after first meeting Corthian Mining was one of his greatest failures and regrets.  The moment that transportation was arranged, Kyle intended to go.

His train of thought was finally interrupted when C.H.A.D.D. gave him a nudge through Pack Leader’s Instinct, a note of concern piercing through his musings.

“What’s going on, C.H.A.D.D.?”

The drone was quiet for a moment, considering its words before it finally spoke.  [WHAT WAS YOUR IMPRESSION OF MANGALICA, DR. MAYHEW?]

“For one, he’s powerful,” Kyle said.  “I doubt he’s far into mid-tier C Grade, but he’d be a tough opponent.  I was also surprised at how much he’d invested into Willpower.  It feels like an odd choice for a primarily physical combatant, doubly so without any real investment in Intelligence.”

[WHAT ELSE?]

Kyle considered his words for a moment before continuing.  “I got the sense that he was barely containing his bloodlust.  If we’d stuck around any longer, I fully expect that there would have been violence.  It was almost like he was half-feral.  Though that could also be a result of the C Grade evolution.  He looks a hell of a lot scarier than the other C Grade orcs that we’ve come across.”

[HIS UNIQUE APPEARANCE IS CERTAINLY NOTEWORTHY.  ANYTHING ELSE, DR. MAYHEW?]

Kyle frowned as a last idea came to him.  “I don’t know how he would fight with his sword.  His grip on the blade seemed too tense, and the blade looked too heavy and bulky for easy use.  His fighting style has to have some more to it.”

[THAT IS NOT AN OBSERVATION I HAD CONSIDERED, THOUGH IT FITS WITH SOME OF MY OTHER HYPOTHESES.  YOU AND I WERE IN AGREEMENT THAT MANGALICA WAS SOMEHOW CONNECTED TO THE APPEARANCE OF THE HODAGS ACROSS THE ANCESTRAL PATHS, AND I BELIEVE THAT I HAVE GLEANED A BETTER UNDERSTANDING OF HOW HE ACCOMPLISHED IT.]

By now, Kyle’s full attention shifted to C.H.A.D.D., and he nodded for the drone to explain further.

[TO BEGIN, MY SCANS OF THE PALACE FOUND SEVERAL AREAS THAT WERE WARDED BEYOND MY ABILITY TO DETECT.  THIS DREW MY ATTENTION, AS NONE OF THE OTHER VILLAGES POSSESSED ARRAYS POTENT ENOUGH TO PREVENT MY SCANS.  GIVEN WHAT WE SAW FROM MANGALICA, I HAD DOUBTS THAT HE WAS SOLELY RESPONSIBLE FOR SUCH INTRICATE WORK.  THAT IN TURN LED ME TO FOCUS ON BETTER UNDERSTANDING THE SURROUNDING STRUCTURES, AND I BELIEVE I FOUND A WORKABLE EXPLANATION.]

C.H.A.D.D. then pulled up a scan of the palace, highlighting the restricted areas.  One, predictably, was near the top of the structure, where a large room was isolated.  Between its prominent position and size, it was clearly a private office or bedroom for the War Chieftain.  The others were tucked away below the ground, though as Kyle looked at the surrounding structures, pieces began to fall into place.  A long tunnel extended out from the far side of the sealed room, which was also connected to a separate set of kitchens and medical facilities.  There were also large brooms, pitchforks, and shovels set outside.

“He’s hiding stables,” Kyle breathed out.

[THAT IS MY SUSPICION AS WELL.  THE AREA BELOW THE PALACE WOULD BE A GOOD PLACE TO SECURE AND NURTURE THE HODAGS.  AND, IF MANGALICA IS A BEAST TAMER VARIANT, THE INVESTMENT IN WILLPOWER WOULD ALSO MAKE SENSE.  HOWEVER, I BELIEVE THERE IS STILL MORE TO THE PUZZLE.  IT WAS FAINT, BUT MANGALICA’S MANA NETWORK HAD SOME IRREGULARITIES TO ITS FLOW.]

“How do you mean?” Kyle said, frowning.  He hadn’t noticed anything to that effect, but trusted C.H.A.D.D.’s sensory capabilities over his own Identify.

[THE CLOSEST COMPARISON I COULD MAKE WOULD BE TO THE AMAGLAMATIONS WE ENCOUNTERED ON ER’MITHREN; ALBEIT A DEGREE LESS INTEGRATED.]

A chill ran up Kyle’s spine.  “You’re telling me that he isn’t just taming the hodags.”

[THAT IS CORRECT.  I SUSPECT THAT HE HAS DONE SOMETHING TO ABSORB THEM; LIKELY ACHIEIVING SOME TYPE OF VARIANT EVOLUTION IN THE PROCESS.  FURTHERMORE, IT APPEARED TO ME AS THOUGH MUCH OF THE POWER WAS CURRENTLY BEING RESTRAINED.  IT WOULD BE WISE TO ASSUME THAT HE CAN BRING MORE OF THAT POWER TO BEAR.]

“The abominations were made from mostly E Grades and unawakened, and were strong enough to fight at the peak of D Grade in power.  If his increase in power is similar…”

[THE METHOD SEEMS TO BE LESS REFINED THAN WHAT WE ENCOUNTERED, AND I WOULD EXPECT THAT THE INTEGRATION PROCESS IS MORE DIFFICULT WHEN USING HIGHER-TIER CREATURES LIKE THE HODAGS.  ALL THIS TO SAY, I DOUBT IT WILL BE ENOUGH POWER TO JUMP TIERS TO B GRADE, BUT I WOULD EXPECT TO SEE A MATERIAL INCREASE SHOULD MANGALICA USE HIS FULL POWER.]

“We need to tell Duroc,” Kyle said, pushing even more mana through Aura Force as they flew over the craggy ground.

A couple of small hodag nests were scattered along the journey, but they didn’t even slow down.  Even if Mangalica didn’t participate in the Tournament of Ascension, Duroc would have to face him soon.  The sooner Kyle and C.H.A.D.D. could share what they knew, the sooner Duroc would be able to start working on countermeasures.  The hodags were a problem that would have to wait for another time.

Even flying at full speed, it took Kyle just over a day to reach Duroc’s village.  The guards appeared to be on high alert, flagging him down as he approached.

“Courier!” one of the orcs called out.  “You’ve returned faster than expected.  The War Chieftain is out on a hunting expedition, but should return in the next few days.”

“I have news he’ll need to hear,” Kyle said curtly.  “Where can I find him?”

The guards shared a look before responding.  “We can send word to him, why don’t you go back to the palace and get settled in?  We’ll have an attendant get your chambers prepared.”

Kyle was about to continue to argue, but saw a glimmer of understanding from one of the guards.

“Very well,” he said, continuing his flight toward the structure at the heart of the village.  As promised, an attendant was waiting for him, though she was shifting uncomfortably.

“You have news for the War Chieftain?” she asked with a ghost of a warble in her voice.

“I do.  If you know where he is, I’m happy to go to him,” Kyle replied.

“I’ll…  I’ll be back shortly,” she managed, giving a shallow bow.  “Can you find your way to your chambers on your own?”

“Assuming they’re the same as last time, yes.”

The attendant bowed again before taking her leave, and Kyle watched her go with a frown.  Once she was out of earshot, C.H.A.D.D. quietly spoke.

[DO YOU WANT ME TO SEE WHERE SHE GOES, DR. MAYHEW?]

“Everybody is acting suspicious, that’s for sure,” Kyle muttered.  “Keep an eye on her.  I doubt that anybody here would try to betray Duroc or try to hurt me, but we’ve never regretted being careful.”

[THE SUSPICION SEEMS WELL-FOUNDED, GIVEN THAT SHE HAS NOT GONE TO ANY OF THE COMMUNICATORS, AND HAS INSTEAD OPENED A SEALED PASSAGE THAT LEADS BENEATH THE FIRST FLOOR.]

“Did you detect it before?” Kyle asked with surprise.

[I NOTICED WHAT APPEARED TO BE A CELLAR.  I DID NOT DETECT THE OTHER CHAMBERS EXTENDING BELOW IT UNTIL NOW.]

“Do all of these villages have secret areas hiding behind wards?” Kyle asked himself, shaking his head.

[IT IS LIKELY, DR. MAYHEW.  IF WE CONSIDER THAT EACH WAR CHIEFTAIN IS EFFECTIVELY A HEAD OF STATE, IS IT REALLY SO SURPRISING THAT MOST WOULD HAVE A MEANS OF EGRESS PREPARED, OR OTHER SECRETS LOCKED AWAY?]

“When you put it that way, it makes a lot more sense,” Kyle conceded.  “Should we work on making a secret base when we settle on Kertan Six?”

[I DO NOT BELIEVE THAT SUCH A THING WOULD BE NECESSARY FOR YOU, DR. MAYHEW.  THOUGH A SECLUDED GARDEN WOULD BE… APPRECIATED.]

“The World Tree Seed isn’t enough anymore?” Kyle asked.

[THERE ARE DIFFERENT TYPES OF CREATIONS, DR. MAYHEW.  THE WORLD TREE SEED ALLOWS US TO CREATE FOR CONVENIENCE AND UTILITY.  A PROPER GARDEN WOULD ALLOW US TO CREATE AND CULTIVATE ART.]

“Then we’ll make sure we leave space as we design our home,” Kyle said with a chuckle.

They waited for nearly twenty minutes, until C.H.A.D.D. noted the attendant coming back from the sealed pathway in the palace’s basement.  She was making a beeline for Kyle’s chambers, and with a quick nudge of Aura Force he opened the door just before she reached his room.

He tried to hide his smile at her look of shock.  “Where is Duroc?”

The question snapped her out of her surprise.  “Please, sir.  Follow me.  The War Chieftain has given you leave to go to him.”

“I thought he was out on a hunting trip?” Kyle asked with an eyebrow raised.

The attendant gave a furtive look down the hall.  “Sir, please just follow me.  We can speak openly later.”

Kyle sighed.  He’d formed a good idea as to what was actually going on, but he still didn’t care for the deception.  And, on the off chance he was incorrect, he wanted to make it very clear that the consequences for betrayal would be severe.  “Lead the way.  And understand that I’m here to deliver a message to Duroc as a friend.  If anybody tries to stop me from doing so, I’m going to protest.  Vigorously.”

The orcish attendant blanched at that.  “Understood, sir.”

She gave a shallow bow, and Kyle followed her through the complex, to the basement, and then through the secret tunnel in the basement.  Once inside, with the door closed behind them, she turned to Kyle and offered a deeper bow.

“I apologize for the secrecy and misdirection.  Only a select few of his most trusted are aware of the War Chieftain’s whereabouts, and the story about a hunting party was to help explain away his absence.”

“When the reality is that he’s in some sort of seclusion in the village itself,” Kyle finished with a nod.  “I can appreciate that, and why you may not want it getting out.”

“Exactly right,” she said, a note of tension leaving her shoulders.  “And I would also ask that you keep what you see here in confidence.  The facilities beneath the palace are not public knowledge, and the War Chieftain is showing significant trust by allowing you to see him here.  You’ll be the first outsider to enter these halls in generations.”

“The secret is safe with me,” Kyle promised as they began to navigate the narrow hallway to a stairwell that descended even farther below.  Along the way, Kyle got a feel for the layered warding runes inscribed on each stone.  It wasn’t particularly precise work, but it made up for inefficiencies with sheer volume.  The people of Duroc’s village had invested an incredible amount of mana into the wards through the years, and they’d paid off nicely.

Finally, Kyle reached the bottom of the stairwell, where a massive stone door stood closed.

“The War Chieftain is just beyond this door.  I will wait here, Courier.”

“Thank you,” Kyle said with a nod, pushing the door open with a grunt of effort.

What awaited on the other side was a wide cavern with dark gray stone and deep purple crystals lining its edges.  At the center of the space was a swirling furnace of magma and a massive anvil.  All throughout, shards of crystal, stone, and metal flew in different directions, forming a constant storm of shrapnel.

And, floating in the middle of it all, was Duroc.  The orc was suspended in the air nearly a dozen meters above the forge, and as Kyle stepped in, the storm of shrapnel slowed as the massive orc descended to the ground.

“It’s good to see you, Kyle!  I hear you have some important matters to discuss.  Please, come in.  But first, did you happen to bring any snacks?”
