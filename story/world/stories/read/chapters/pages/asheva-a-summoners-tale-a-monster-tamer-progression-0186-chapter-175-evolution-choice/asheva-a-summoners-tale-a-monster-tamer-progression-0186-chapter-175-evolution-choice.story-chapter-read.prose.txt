‘Twin-Buds Lotus’, a higher species that shed its ability to heal and adapted a combatant stance against the harsh condition it survived in—it excelled in blood-element spells and boasted a higher affinity than its counterparts.

‘Bloodthorn-Lotus’, an advanced species that inherited the convention of ‘blood’ in its name and chose the path of healing and recovery for its evolution—nature crowned them with the highest survival rate, they were the last to lose against extinction.

‘Lotus Tree’, the giant species that rejected the world and hid behind their hefty barks—they ditched combat and recovery and focused all their affinity with Blood-Anima towards defense.

The paths lay before Iris, none better than the other, as she waited for Ewan. They all had their own positives and negatives, and only preference could sway his choice. Since his birth, the mystic element obeyed him, and he reigned over anyone and anything that was capable of thoughts.
