If we keep standing here, more and more people will come to greet me. In fact, it’s good that Yuelan is underestimating all of you.”Chen Ren nodded. Being underestimated was always a gift. People with lowered guards revealed far more weaknesses.None of them lingered any further. Princess Yanyue led the way, her guards parting the crowd effortlessly. To Chen Ren’s surprise, much of the massive wall was hollow from the inside—built like a fortress with long corridors carved through its width and entire rows of rooms embedded into the stone.According to Princess Yanyue, the inner wall was practically a miniature fortress city. All of it existed to accommodate the strong and wealthy cultivators who explored the Corpse Lands.
