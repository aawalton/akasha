Chapter 179 / 24: Going the Wight Way

Margen still had a lot more healing to go when Joe heard Yuk shout a telepathic warning.

‘Six of them just blew past us. Sorry, they were going so quickly, we only heard them half a second before they ran by us. We’re coming!’

“Damn it!” Joe used a [Casting Claw] to scoop up the hide from beside the reclining swordsman and throw it on top of a hastily summoned [Grimoiriguard]. He then willed the orange paw to follow the other force digit he had already sent shooting down the hallway in the direction Yuk had gone. As the clawed constructs zoomed away into the darkness, Joe brightened the [Glowstone]. The first paw passed right between the half dozen bounding dead. Joe willed it to stop short and exploded a knee with [Dispel Noxa]. The other amber fist nailed one of the necrotic half-beasts straight in the gut, folding it over and stopping it dead in its tracks.

Joe hexed the next two with [Rattlebone], causing the wolf-headed monsters to trip and fall into the path of the ghasts behind them. Unfortunately, the trailing two wights bound over their allies and kept coming.

Woeful Marauder: Level 23: Undead (Dirge Wight): Skirmisher: Dexterity

These wolf-face dead were similar to the Scarified Harriers, but they were bulkier. Joe was given the impression more of the Tolkien-esque wargs than true wolves.

The first undead claws to reach for him were intercepted by a stunningly powerful [Heavy Hand]. The reflex spell, empowered by Joe’s new might, smacked away the wight's arm with so much force that the creature spun sideways, causing it to trip over its own feet. It staggered past Joe, falling and skidded up to Margen. As Joe blocked the second feral ghast with his hairy-coated shield, the founder effortlessly swept DoomHerald in an arc, bisecting his ghast into two separate pieces.

“Don’t undo all my work, sir. Stay put, please.”

“I promise. Minimum effort. And knock off the sirs, kid. I’m no general anymore.”

Focusing on the pack in front of him, Joe placed a [Dispel Noxa] inside the head of his attacker. Through trial and vile error, he had found that targeting the back of the undead skulls destroyed the wights and mostly prevented the explosions of putrescence. Revolting fluids leaked out of eyes, mouths, ears, and noses, but rarely the full spray of splurting blobs of rot.

Whether it was because Margen’s life force was so low or because the man was sitting in the middle of the {Phoenix} flames, none of the creatures went after him. They all piled on Joe. Since [Steadfast] had never been stronger, they had no chance of knocking him off his feet.

Five ghouls, with Yuk when he got there, took longer than Margen had needed against twenty, but the pair came away from the fight in much better shape. Joe had to cleanse the taint from the wights’ claws, heal a little, and recharge his friend’s stamina, but after that, they were as good as new.

The founder still needed far more attention before they could get moving again.

An hour of healing later, interspersed by two more small fights, Joe had gotten the big man back as much health as they could reasonably expect, given the state of the mythic-level mummy rot. Joe’s stomach was empty once again from the retching that went hand in hand with these purges.

“That should do it. Now stay back from the fights, sir,” Joe ordered as politely as he could, even though he was frustrated and nauseous. “As you can see, we are not quick, but we can take them.”

The old warrior scowled at Joe for the ‘sir’ but the big man was beginning to realize that the honorific was a reflex that Joe was not easily going to overcome. “I understand. I’ll stay out of it unless things get too hairy.”

“Oh, yeah. Speaking of which. Here, you should take this back.” Joe passed over the hide and willed it into the shape of a long hauberk, big enough to fit the man’s broad frame.

When they had gotten far enough away from the wretched scent of where they had treated Margen, Joe pulled rations from his Dimbag. He was ravenous. He extended a pack to the blade-master, who grinned widely at the offer. [Ring of Sustenance] or not, it must have been years since he had experienced the simple joy of eating. Margen tore into the biscuits as well as the dried fruit and meat.

He placed a second wrapped ration on the ground, and Yuk swarmed over it with as much gusto as the founder was displaying. As Joe dug into his own meal, a low rumbling purr seemed to issue from inside himself. It seemed the wildness had been craving food as well.

As he walked and ate, Joe considered whether or not they should party with the legendary hero. It wasn’t worth it for experience, as the undead here were far too low to benefit the founder. On the other hand, it would allow Joe to better watch the state of the rot and Margen’s health.

Just as he was about to make the suggestion out loud, a thought struck him, and he clamped his mouth closed. There was no way they could party up. The moment Margen joined them, he would realize Yuk was not a familiar, and then questions would start, especially since they had been lying to him. Joe mentally breathed a quick sigh.

‘That was close.’

The trio followed the twisting passages, looking for a way back into the zigguat. After about twenty minutes, Margen hissed a warning from behind Joe.

“Behind us. I hear footfalls.”

“Ok, switch,” Joe ordered and sent, ‘Yuk. Dead charging from behind.’

‘Coming.’

He stepped around his huge ally and readied himself. He summoned his shield for mental and magical attack, but left the hide on the founder. Joe could fix his own damage much easier than he could Margen’s.

“Widen your feet, Joe,” the old warlord coached. “You have a two-hander’s stance there. Did you learn with a specific weapon?”

“Staff,” Joe blurted back, as he brightened the golden rock and surrounded the founder in a [Halefire]. In the brighter light, his enhanced night-vision could pick out pale forms bounding toward them.

“Yeah, that makes sense. You should be fighting in a shield and strike pose. You need to relax your posture. Roll your shoulders. Loosen up,” the fighter suggested. “Be open to pivoting toward either hand, instead of working them together like you would with a staff.”

Joe adjusted to meet the warrior’s advice as much as he could in the seconds before they were engaged. This pack had a surprise Joe was not expecting: an arrow lanced from the squad of bestial dead, passed through [Grimoiriguard], sinking into Joe, high on his chest. Thankfully, he had [Bonemail] going on his gambeson, which gave the padded armor more physical defense than a steel breastplate. The head still penetrated, pinging into his collarbone, but it failed to go any further.

“Uh. That’s not much of a shield, son.”

“Magic only,’ Joe hissed, yanking out the missile and healing.

In the midst of the dozen or so enemies, Joe saw a weasel archer. He swooped his hands together like a conductor, commanding the orange force fists to crash into either side of the snouted head. The blow was not lethal, but it temporarily took the bow-bearing dead out of commission.

‘Can you get any archers, Yuk?’ Joe asked, as he ducked an attack while raking his talons through rotting skin.

‘Got em.’ Yuk sent back as the frontline of ghasts practically tackled Joe.

“Ditch the fake shield, soldier. You are treating it like it’s going to stop their claws. I’ve seen how tough your arms are. Get into a pugilist stance and block.”

The wolf-wights were no longer stronger than he was. Joe let [Heavy Hand] shove them away as he began to punch and slash at the mass of scabby bodies around him. The pose Margen had him adopt felt steadier, allowing him to deliver more powerful blows.

Joe leaned back from a swipe attempting to blind him, and countered, his new Strength breaking the deader’s arm. He pivoted away from another strike, slicing his claws down a long muzzle of another ghoulish wight. Instinctively, he kicked out, shattering the monster’s knee.

Your actions have awarded you with a skill option. Do you wish to learn [Unarmed Combat Expertise]?

[Unarmed Combat Expertise] - Common - Strength: You are skilled at fighting without external weaponry. Ranks with this skill increase your ability to bypass defenses as well as parry incoming attacks. Passive. {Might}

‘I already have [Versatile Combat Expertise], Hawking. That covers fists,’ Joe sent back, trying not to lose the rhythm of the fight he was embroiled in.

I know this. Do you wish to learn [Unarmed Combat Expertise]?

There was an odd feeling of pretense to that last message.  Joe realized Hawking was trying to help him, but didn't want to outright say so. ‘Ok. Do whatever you think’s best. I’m really busy here, bud.’

You have learned [Unarmed Combat Expertise].

Skill Upgrade: You learned a skill that synergizes with a skill you already possess. [Versatile Combat Expertise] and [Unarmed Combat Expertise] will be combined to become the uncommon skill [Brawler]. You have an open point of Dexterity.

[Brawler] - Uncommon - Strength: You are skilled at unarmed fighting and using common simple weapons, such as clubs, hatchets, knives, as well as improvised weapons such as rocks, bottles, etc. This style of combat includes several Fighting Dirty maneuvers. Ranks with this skill increase your ability to bypass defenses, increase damage, and parry incoming attacks. Passive. {Might}

Suddenly, he knew how Margen was trying to get him to fight. His footing was close, but reflexively, he shifted it to be much better. Joe unleashed a flurry of claws, punches, and kicks, throwing back his attackers.

[Versatile Combat Expertise] had him trying to use his rather poor agility to thread strikes through openings in the wights’ defenses. [Brawler] made its own openings. With his massively improved brawn, Joe was pounding away on the throng of undead. Every time [Heavy Hand] came off cooldown, Joe used it to send another lupine marauder flying off its feet.

Joe went from being mobbed to becoming a junior Jackie Chan, knocking enemies about, never allowing them to regroup and overwhelm him.

He didn't even need to worry about finishing the dead off. True to his word, Margen stayed back, but he was not inactive. Every couple of seconds, DoomHerald lanced out in surgically precise strikes; each blow immediately dropping a ghast. These whisking slices and thrusts were nothing like his previous hurricane of destruction. Joe could tell that the swordsman was being very conservative with his efforts.

[Swift Strike] fused with the Strength-based [Brawler] better than it had with any other skill he had used it with so far. Joe found he could easily vary the speed of his strikes, lancing his hands in the way of oncoming attacks and lashing his claws into the dwindling pack of the rotting marauders.

As the last wight fell, Joe’s ears picked up more howls coming their way. Playing to a hunch, he used that free moment to pull [Haybreaker] out of his dim-bag. He made a few experimental swings and instantly felt the difference. [Versatile Combat Expertise] had accepted the heavy baton. [Brawler] clearly liked the truncheon. It felt good in his grip.

As the next wave came into view, Joe was tempted to see if his new combat skill could handle it on its own, and then realized how stupid of an idea that was. This was not the place or situation to hot-dog a fight. He fired curses first and used his amber force paws to soften up the charging corpses.

Only when they finally closed did he get to work with [Brawler]. Between his coordinated skills and the sundering enhanced billy club, he completely tore the hand off the first set of claws to reach for his face. The weighted rod destroyed joints: elbows, shoulders, and knees. He left a wake of disabled dead behind him for Margen to decapitate.

Joe found that [Brawler] felt more like a fighting style than just a skill. He was more aware of the battle around him. [Brawler] seemed to nudge him into using his other skills more effectively. He found himself utilizing [Sidestep] far more often than he had in the past. Joe slipped between wights, tangling them up with each other. He shifted to better positions while not leaving an open path to the ancient warrior behind him. He could feel when to use [Heavy Hand] to knock an enemy off its feet and when to save it to deflect a blow.

Joe had enjoyed sparring with Hah’roo and Kendell, but he had never enjoyed a real fight before.

Until now.

‘Nice pick,’ Joe complimented the ethereal watcher.

I thought you’d like it.
