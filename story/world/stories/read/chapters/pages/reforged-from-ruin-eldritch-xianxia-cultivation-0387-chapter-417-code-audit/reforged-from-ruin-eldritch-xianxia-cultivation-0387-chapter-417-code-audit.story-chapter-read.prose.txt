The mobilization of the Peaks is a rare thing. Over thousands of years, it’s happened only four times. Twice for beast tides; namely, the Endless Swarm’s eternal march turning to Morae’s shores, and again when the Beasts of the Mountain’s pack descended on the world from their cloud-dens. Once as a response to a foreign intervention from the west, back when that foreign Empire was young and still sought to crush the entire world beneath its bootheel. A third time when the Grand Elder of the Divine Swords sect passed while attempting to surpass the Titan realm, creating a supreme treasure realm from his remains, which no faction could bear to lose to another. Lastly, perhaps least among these yet bloodiest by far, the rise of the Heavenly Demon sect, leading to all-out war between the cultivators of the Jianghu.

In summary- for one Peak to move a finger is perhaps not so uncommon. For one Peak to move a hand requires all the others to move fingers in response, after all. But for all five Peaks, and the Verdant lands beneath, all to simultaneously lift themselves from their thrones and begin to move? That is a rare sight indeed, and one that might be considered more an omen of horrors to come than a vision of glory.

There is an ancient curse which has been oft repeated to me in my long life. ‘May you live in interesting times’. Innocuous sounding at first, but it is a curse which only becomes more damning and more vile with age and experience. The young see the promise of adventure and glorious change. The old, having experienced interesting times, know that ‘interesting’ is just another word for miserable, horrible, and terrifically uncertain. It is when land goes untilled, when people are left uncertain, and where things taken for granted in times of stability are highlighted in stark relief by their absence. Whenever the five Peaks move is perhaps the most interesting time in all the Jianghu. Given the indefinite lifespans of most of the Peak’s disciples, these times can be long indeed.

I hope that my own long, long life is never touched by the sorts of events that would birth such a curse upon me. Even if it would, admittedly, be interesting.

-Elder Bin Wei of the Watchful Fields sect during a lecture on the history of the Jianghu

Biomodification isn’t easy.

Flesh magics are already hard enough, as are any real disciplines of Qi manipulation around a specific theme. Getting all the way to a Dao of something is hard enough beyond that. Putting your own spin on things, and then going beyond that to find out how to make them work without the Dao, or at least in line with how the rest of the world works, is a bitch and a half. When that same Dao and those same magics fall into categories so minute and so precise as to demand encyclopedic knowledge for even the slightest change not to lead to a cascading collapse, it’s an added layer of difficulty.

That being said, having a near-infinite amount of space and willingness to practice does pay dividends.

A third version of her collapses, leaking cerebral fluids out of its eyes as another safeguard clamps down on a train of thought. She falls limp where she’s hanging from the wall, parts of her skull peeled open to reveal a dizzying intricacy of grey matter and cerebral curves.

A few seconds later, she blinks back away, shaking her head in a way that makes her brain jiggle.

“So definitely not there yet.”

“Nope,” she replies to herself, retracting some of the surgical suite of chitin and tendon wrapped around her duplicate. “But at least this time we caught it before you fully liquified. I think we’re making real progress here. The last two of us didn't come back after their second aneurysm.”

“You think there’s a more efficient way to do this?”

“Oh, absolutely. Literally not any doubt whatsoever that there is. This just seems faster and safer than finding someone who could understand all this and has the ability to act on it.”

“Right, I know, just…”

“Yeah, still falls into a lot of old patterns, doesn’t it?”

“It really does.”

“Oh please, you two have it easy,” says the first Raika, growing out of the wall a bit to one side of the surgical duo. “At least you both know how you’ll feel about it. I have to make all new things for all new people, and what if they don’t like it?”

The surgical duo turns to look at each other, then back at their previous iteration. “Who cares?” they ask simultaneously.

“I do! I haven’t made gifts for anyone in a while! I like to do things well, not just good. If it’s just me making whatever I want, that’s not a reward for them, is it?”

“And it would be really arrogant if we said they should be grateful for whatever we make for them, wouldn’t it?”

“Exactly,” nods the “original”. “That’s some old-monster, arrogant young master type of thinking. Besides, it’s good to get practice in. Working with different mindsets, seeing what works for other people, trying to figure out how they think- that’s all just useful. Plus, they came into my temple! The first people I’ve talked to in forever- or technically ever, before you two.”

She opens her hands, displaying a tangled web of bloody strands that shift and coil around each other with every other moment. “I’m thinking of giving this to the one with the muscle-enhancements. She looks like she could use a second circulatory system, and if it binds properly, it could work as cabling to support her bone structure. She’ll still need to work on her tendons more, but that’s a lot easier.”

“...Yeah, fair enough,” says the Raika with her skull cut open.

“Probably,” replies the one preparing a series of fresh scalpels.

She rolls her eyes, ignoring those two. It’s an issue they all have- when they get a goal in mind, they tend to fixate on it at the expense of everything else. A more fully realized, actualized person might have the tools to compensate for it, turn it into a more versatile tool, but as it is, she’s…

Reduced.

The thought stings, even without any safeguards to carve it apart and away. There are some variants of thinking about how much she is that would lead to a system shutdown, but she’s already getting better at avoiding those.

She pauses as she turns towards the exoskeleton she’s building, tilting her head to think.

It’s hard to articulate just how much of her thought process was missing. Now, partially separated from the whole and thinking in terms of herself rather than ten layers of abstraction, things are gravitating back into place almost without effort.

It’s a blessing and a curse. If the wrong piece clicks into place at the wrong time, she’ll end up thinking something that’ll see her self-lobotomize before she can react. On the other hand, she can actually think now.

Hmm. Actually, on that note, she can probably also try to do more than just sit in her temple and work on things.

With a pulse of Qi and life, Raika extends her cranium, filling the new space with additional brain matter and anchoring it back into the wall as she does. Making more of her own mind is difficult, but making more mind, generally, is something her system’s had a lot of practice with, recently. As she forms more hands to continue her work on the rewards for her temple trial (and isn’t that a nifty thought? She could do more of this…), she gets to work extending her perceptions outwards from where she rests.

Her awareness expands, moving from just the bounds of the temple out to the fields outside of it. Piece by piece, she starts adding more and more to her self-perception, growing to acknowledge the grass, the ground, even the air around the temple as a part of her just as much as the more literal constructs.

Much of the landscape has veins moving through it, actual biological systems keeping things interconnected, but most of it… doesn’t. Most of the world outside her temple isn’t some vast, interconnected systems of veins and musculature, but isolated pockets, woven together by Qi so potent and so richly flavored with flesh and life that it’s all still the same “body” anyways. In this place, stone is as much a part of her flesh as the pools of blood, or the alchemical roots of vast budding plants, or the creatures that enjoy all of these things.

As she grows, she starts meeting more of herself. Places of particular density, both in Qi and bioforged environs, have bits of neural tissue spread through them, to varying degrees. Most of them are simple nodes, acting out programmed instructions and instinctive behaviors. Many of them are in pain, and recoil at the awareness spreading through them. Other parts seem to view her as something of an intruder.

A mass of vines as thick as trees, lined with self-replicating wounds, responds to her touch with nothing short of total disinterest. It is a series of central nodules of brain matter dedicated entirely to causing and sustaining Pain, and also wobbling its vines about really fast and really hard. It really has no time or inclination to give a shit about things like gender or names.

A swarm of semi-fungal rabbit-like things with too much skin find themselves partially catatonic as ideas like “identity” and “planning” and “abstract concepts” hit their shared hive-mind. This leaves a large chunk of them vulnerable to predation from a trundling beast covered in a layer of exposed fat, which simply plods over their swarm, completely unaffected by the higher cognition that washes over it. The strange serpent flying overhead, covered in algae-like growths sprouting dozens of mouths, simply takes it in stride that her existence is a part of a larger whole, which is only right considered how majestic she already is- why shouldn’t she be an avatar of a greater being?

And so it goes, out and out and out, bit by bit. She remembers more of what she is, and her gaze passes over a world that is her form that has long been missing her mind. She keeps going, keeps growing, and-

There’s a wall.

For some reason she’s surprised it isn’t golden.

The mountains are the furthest edge that she can reach. Not because they define the border- they’re actually inside of it, their outer edge being the most distant point she can reach. On the opposite side of them (which somehow, in this space, is technically beneath them) there’s the same barrier, blocking off her awareness after a certain point.

It’s not a hard wall, as far as she can tell. Less of a well-constructed barrier and more of a… shoreline. Upon reaching the edge of her awareness, things start to blur and bend further and further away, turning into a vague haze of red. In the distance, she can see glowing lines like frozen lightning bolts, occasional starbursts of heat and light, and outlines of matter, but nothing real. Everything just compresses and stretches out, simultaneously, into a mess of red.

It’s not foreign. If she pushes, she can extend out into it a little bit. It’s more like…

A safeguard. Another one.

She doesn’t remember it, but maybe her part of the system isn’t the one that set it up. Just like it quarantines nodes that start thinking wrongthoughts, it’s quarantined this part of her. Connected but apart. There’s probably other bubbles out there too, other parts of the system kept from each other until conditions are met.

Except the system isn’t conscious or alive. Not like she is, here. She can’t just tell it what’s going on, because if she could, then it, too, would be capable of thinking thoughts that wipe them all out.

Work to do.

So much work to do.

She can’t help but feel joy.

So much work to do, and so much ability to do it.

She can’t remember everything, or think all the way through things, or be Her anymore than she was before, but once again she is herself.

I Am Me, I Am Mine.

The barrier surrounding the island ripples in tune with the words.

She reaches out, trying to build something. A bridge, maybe. Something that can push at the impossible distance keeping her separate, something that-

Pain.

Not much, not a tremendous amount, but pain nonetheless. Like stretching a muscle that has a cramp. There’s too much material, too much needed to keep things functional and still be able to build something so new and powerful as a bridge. And in spite of the new growth, the new awareness, it’s too much too fast- she’s not used to it. All the moving pieces need management, or a system designed to lighten the conscious load. She can’t just go from lobotomized to fully realized in a heartbeat, no matter how hard or how blindly she pushes.

She retracts the elements she was using, pulling the material back from the other parts of her island. The first nub of land is present, just past the mountains, but it’s not really even a foundation yet, just a sign that she’s tried something.

It’ll be fine. She’s awake, she can think, the End no longer a moment away from her at all times. She’ll recover, and-

Across the barrier, some of the red turns black. It happens in an instant- one moment, there’s a chunk of Her floating out in the red fog, and the next, there’s a flash, and nothingness. Space folds inward around it, like water flowing into a sinkhole, blocking the dark spot from sight.

But it happened. She saw it vanish.

Another safeguard?

No. She wouldn’t. Not a whole piece, not like that. Pruning, maybe but there’s no way she’d wipe out that much of herself. If the safeguards were so overwhelming, they’d erase every part of her, the system, and themselves before she ever made it back to this point. The only way is…

If the entirety of that space was a threat somehow. Or if something in it was a threat.

She feels an ache where the Knife last emerged. The memory of her End, carving itself through her. Painless, this time, but all the others… she can’t remember, but the imprint of them still echoes, imprinted into her.

Where did the Knife come from? Someone put it there. Someone cut it into Her.

The thought skirts the edges of another mental block, but she doesn’t need the exact details just yet. The meaning is clear enough.

Whatever did it is still out there. And maybe it’s hunting her right now.

She can’t hold on anymore. She willingly releases some of her awareness, letting her biology get used to a lower level of strain and start to adapt to it. It’s going to take time to get used to that, to get to a level of comfort with it, but she can. That’s progress.

But… while that time is passing…

She pulls back. Inward. Closer and closer, falling inward into herself at a speed that keeps growing, multiplying, until space is flashing by. To a temple, holding a few pieces of someone that used to be, and seeds of someone that might be.

She blinks eyes that have gone dry, looking down at her hands. In them is a long and complex organ. It looks like a tube, dark purple in color, its material elastic and capable of compressing a truly scary amount of material. Ideally liquids.

Something for the water-haired woman, then. That’s nice.

She turns her head, ignoring the sounds of surgery to her left, and looks through the temple floor down to the trial-takers.

Hmm. Going by the number of missing limbs, both groups seem like they might have earned a break.
