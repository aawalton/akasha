Well, Chloe's plans had just been made for her. She knew where Mom was now, she knew that Mom was alive, and, most importantly, she finally knew exactly where she needed to go. After spending so long wondering whether she'd ever see her mother again, there was no way Chloe was going to let something as silly as the lowest floor of Tartarus probably being extremely dangerous get in the way.

She turned toward everyone with a bright smile, still clutching Z's flip phone. "Okay, so now that we know where Mom is, we're going to go down there and save her! Then we'll all get back out of this place together, and everything will be great."

"I agree. We have already come this far, and knowing Lady Ishgria's location gives us a clear objective. If she is in danger, then assisting her is certainly preferable to simply leaving her there." Z said.

Raenof stretched his shoulders, wincing slightly as some soreness from his imprisonment caught up with him. "I suppose I can hardly complain about another rescue mission after you lot went through the trouble of rescuing Sierra and me. Besides, if we're going to be wandering around Tartarus anyway, I might as well make myself useful."

Sierra nodded quickly, her ears perking up as she looked at Chloe. "If your mother is there, then I'll help too."

Chloe's smile grew wider as she looked around at everyone, because as far as she could tell, everyone understood what they needed to do next; they were going to find Mom, bring her back, and then somehow everything would work out in the end, just like it was supposed to. Z seemed perfectly fine with the idea, and both Raenof and Sierra had readily agreed to help, so Chloe was already starting to feel exited.

Well, at least until she noticed that Greg hadn't said anything yet, and when she glanced toward Liz, she found her standing quietly with her arms folded, while Percy remained a short distance away with an unreadable expression. At first, Chloe didn't think much of it, assuming they were probably just taking a moment to consider the plan, but the longer the silence stretched, the more she began to realize that there was something different about the way they were looking at her, and that little burst of excitement in her chest slowly gave way to a creeping feeling that perhaps not everyone was quite as ready to rush off and save Mom as she was.

"Uh... guys?" Chloe asked out loud.

Nobody answered immediately. Chloe tilted her head, confused. "What's wrong? You guys aren't seriously saying we shouldn't go, are you? We finally know where Mom is, and she's on the lowest floor, so all we have to do is go down there and find her. I mean, sure, it'll probably be dangerous, but we've already fought Administrator A, and we won, so how much worse could it really get?"

Greg rubbed the back of his neck and gave her a weary look. "Chloe, as much as I respect your mother and as grateful as I am that you've gone through all this trouble to find her, I'm going to be honest with you here: going to the lowest level of Tartarus sounds like a bad idea."

Chloe blinked. "But... Mom's down there."

"I know she's down there."

"And she's probably in trouble."

"I understand that."

"So we have to go and get to her!"

Greg sighed. "Listen, I'm not saying we shouldn't care about your mother, because I obviously do, and if there were a reasonable way for me to help her without immediately throwing myself back into the middle of whatever nightmare is happening down there, I'd gladly do it. But I was just rescued from a prison, Chloe. I haven't even had time to properly stretch my legs yet, and you're already asking me to march straight toward the most dangerous place in Tartarus."

Chloe's eyebrows drew together. "But if we leave her there, she could get hurt!"

"I know."

"Then how can you just sit here and say it's too much trouble?"

Greg's expression softened slightly. "Because sometimes knowing someone needs help and actually being capable of helping them are two different things. I'd rather admit that now than go down there pretending I'm ready, only to become another person everyone has to rescue."

Chloe opened her mouth to argue again, but Liz stepped in before she could. "Greg isn't wrong."

Chloe looked at the goth angel. "What?"

Liz folded her arms, her expression calm but unusually serious. "I'm not saying your mother isn't worth rescuing, Chloe, but Greg has been imprisoned here for who knows how long, and he only just got out. He's exhausted, he's probably still recovering, and none of us even know what we're going to find on the lowest floor."

"But—"

"Let me finish," Liz said gently. "Percy and I already helped you get into this facility, helped you find your friends, and stayed with you through the fight against Administrator A. We could have walked away a long time ago, but we didn't. Now we've found Greg too."

Chloe stared at her. "That doesn't mean we're done."

Liz sighed. "Maybe it does, Chloe. At least for us."

The words hit Chloe harder than she expected. She looked from Liz to Percy, searching his expression for some sign that he disagreed, but Percy stayed quiet.

"But... you can't seriously mean that." Chloe said.

Liz's expression softened. "I'm sorry, Chloe, I really am."

"Please, Liz, I need your help, and Percy's help, and Greg's help. What if something happens to Mom while we're standing here? What if she's already hurt? What if she needs us to come and help her and we decide that getting her out is too dangerous?"

Chloe's voice cracked slightly. "I've been trying to find Mom for so long. I don't want to finally find her and then just leave her there."

The room fell quiet. Chloe lowered her gaze, staring down at the little phone in her hands. She could still hear Mom's voice in her head, still remember the way she'd said her name.

Chloe swallowed hard. "I just… want my mom back."

Z stepped closer. "Chloe."

Chloe looked up as Z placed a hand gently on her shoulder. "I understand why you want to go after her immediately, but you need to calm down and think about what everyone is saying. No one here is telling you that saving your Mom doesn't matter. But, we can't always force people to go along with what we want.."

Chloe sniffed and wiped at the corner of her eye. "But if we don't go..."

"I'll come with you Chloe, but everyone else should also be free to make their own choices. Just try not to be too upset for now." Z said.

Chloe puffed out her cheeks and looked away. "I'm not upset."

Z stared at her, and then Chloe sniffled. "Okay, I'm a little upset."

Raenof, who had been watching the exchange, finally cleared his throat. "Well, if I may offer a suggestion before this turns into an emotional argument, perhaps we should remember that there is one rather important detail everyone seems to be overlooking."

Chloe looked toward him as Raenof spread his hands with an almost innocent smile. "We're all standing here discussing whether we should go charging into the lowest level of Tartarus as though we've actually decided what we're going to do once we get there. Personally, I would prefer to have at least a vague idea of what we're all going to get into before anyone makes decisions they might regret."

Chloe nodded, realizing that Raenof had a point, and after a moment, Z gave a quiet nod of her own, while Liz shrugged as though she didn't particularly disagree with him, and Percy and Greg both turned their attention toward Raenof. The elf took a moment to look around at everyone before finally settling his gaze on Chloe, a faint smile appearing on his face.

"Well, I suppose I should start by saying that when I first met Chloe, she was... quite a different person from the one standing in front of me now. Back then, she was rather airheaded, surprisingly naive about a lot of things, and seemed to possess an almost supernatural ability to overlook even the most obvious pieces of common sense."

"Hey!" Chloe shouted.

Raenof chuckled. "Let me finish, Chloe, I'm not saying that as an insult, although I suppose there are certainly worse things I could say."

Chloe puffed out her cheeks but reluctantly let him continue. Raenof coughed once before speaking again. "The point I'm trying to make is that you've changed considerably since then, and not because someone simply told you that you needed to grow up, but because you've been thrown into one difficult situation after another and somehow managed to keep moving forward through all of them. You've fought things that were far stronger than you, you've put yourself in danger for people you barely knew, and, more importantly, you've repeatedly chosen to help others even when there was very little in it for you."

Raenof's expression became a little more serious as he continued. "Now, I'll be honest, I don't have much to gain from helping you find your mother. I'm not particularly invested in whatever conflict is going on, and considering what I've already been through, I could certainly make an argument for leaving this place behind and looking after myself. But, you've saved my life before, Chloe, and whether I like it or not, that makes it rather difficult for me to simply turn around and walk away when I know you're going to put yourself in danger. I suppose what I'm saying is that I can't help but root for you."

He then looked around at the others, his expression becoming more thoughtful. "That said, I also understand that everyone here has their own circumstances, their own reasons for being involved, and their own limits. I don't think it's fair for me to decide that everyone else should be willing to take the same risks that I am simply because I happen to feel this way about Chloe. If you want to continue helping her, then you should do so because that's what you want, and if you don't, then I can't exactly fault you for choosing otherwise."

For a moment, the room became weirdly quiet, and Chloe found herself looking from one person to another, unsure what any of them were going to say next. Raenof hadn't exactly told them what decision to make, but somehow that almost made the silence feel heavier.

Eventually, Z was the first to speak up. "I will help Chloe find Lady Ishgria. Personally, I have many reasons for doing so, but the most important is that I owe Lady Ishgria a considerable debt, and I have no intention of abandoning her while she is in danger. If Chloe intends to go to her, then I will accompany her."

Chloe's eyes brightened. "Really?"

Z nodded. "Of course."

Before Chloe could say anything else, Sierra suddenly perked up as she looked between Chloe and the others. "I... I'll help too."

Everyone turned toward her. Sierra fidgeted slightly with her hands, uncomfortable with having everyone's attention on her, but she forced herself to continue. "Chloe has done a lot for me too, so... um, I don't think I could just let her go alone after everything she's done for me. I'm still scared of this place, and I'm not sure how much help I'll actually be, but if you're going to find your mother, Chloe, then I'll come with you."

Chloe smiled warmly at her. "Thanks, Sierra."

Sierra gave a small nod, her tail giving a hesitant little wag behind her. Raenof then turned his attention toward the remaining three. "Well, that leaves the three of you. Out of everyone here, you three probably have the least personal connection to Chloe, so I think it's fair to say that whether you continue with us is entirely your decision. You don't owe Chloe anything simply because the rest of us have decided that we're willing to help."

Liz scratched the side of her head, giving Raenof a slightly suspicious look. "You know, when you put it like that, it sounds an awful lot like you're trying to guilt-trip the three of us into coming along."

Raenof shrugged. "I'm merely stating the facts."

Liz stared at him for a moment before letting out a quiet sigh and turning toward Percy. "Well, Percy, what do you think?"

Percy didn't hesitate for very long before answering in his usual quiet voice. "I'll go with whatever you decide."

Liz rolled her eyes. "Of course you will."

After that, Liz glanced toward Greg. "Well, Greg, I'll go with whatever you decide."

Greg slowly turned his head toward her, staring at her for a moment. "You're aware that you and Percy are both old enough to make your own decisions, right?"

Liz nodded. "I know."

"Then why are you looking at me like I'm supposed to decide whether you two are going?"

Liz scratched the side of her head and glanced away. "Because I don't wanna."

Greg stared at her, but Liz shrugged again. "I mean, I can make my own decision, obviously, but if someone else is willing to make it for me, then that's one less thing I have to think about."

Greg slowly rolled his eyes. "You're unbelievable."

"Thanks."

"That wasn't a compliment."

"Doesn't matter, I'll pretend it is." Liz said.

Greg sighed and fell silent again. For a while, he didn't say anything at all, and Chloe found herself watching him as he thought in silence for a really, really, really long time. . Every so often, his expression shifted slightly, but whatever thoughts were running through his head remained impossible for Chloe to read. She wondered if he was thinking about Mom, about his time working for Divinity Inc., or perhaps simply about how ridiculous it was that, after finally being rescued, he was being asked to head straight back into the place he'd spent so long trying to escape.

The silence stretched on long enough that Chloe began to wonder if Greg was ever going to answer. Until finally, Greg exhaled and looked toward her. "Fine, I'll go help you find your Mom."

Chloe's eyes widened. "Really?!"

Greg gave her a tired look. "Yes, really. Don't make me repeat myself."

"But what changed your mind?" Chloe asked. "A second ago you were saying that going to the lowest floor of Tartarus sounded like way too much trouble, and now you're suddenly okay with going down there with us, so what happened?"

Greg's expression became strangely unreadable for a moment, and then he gave her a faint smile. "I'll tell you all about it after this is over."

"After this is over?"

"After this is over," Greg repeated, already turning away. "Trust me, Chloe, that's a story you'll have plenty of time to hear once we've got your Mom back."

Chloe looked at him for another moment before grinning. "Okay!"
