“What civilization do you think the investigator will be from?” Kyle asked, looking out at the approaching planet.  It was almost entirely black, with pale green clouds slowly swirling in the atmosphere above.  The notes indicated that it was a C Grade world, though there was no mention of any wildlife on it.

[YOU AND I BOTH KNOW THAT THERE IS NO VALUE IN SPECULATING, DR. MAYHEW.  WE SHOULD FOCUS ON THE TASK AT HAND.]

“Oh come on, you have to admit that you’re at least a little bit curious,” Kyle pressed.

[THE INVESTIGATOR WAS ASKED TO STAY ANONYMOUS FOR A REASON, DR. MAYHEW.  SPECULATING AT THIS JUNCTURE WOULD BE NEITHER HELPFUL NOR ADVISED.  EVEN IF WE WERE TO GUESS CORRECTLY, WE WOULD ONLY DAMAGE YOUR CHANCES OF BEING FULLY CLEARED BY THE TRIBUNAL.]

“Fine,” Kyle relented, watching the green clouds play over the planet’s surface.

Their time on the phytan space station had given Kyle and C.H.A.D.D. some time to rest and consolidate a bit of what they’d learned, though any real progress was overshadowed by a lingering anxiety about the Tribunal.  Old wounds and were reopened as he saw their rejection from Nierberg reflected in the Tribunal.  Whoever was behind it fought to once again strip away his home; his sense of place.  And while he trusted Jarberry, Suierrillax, and Marcus, his faith in these systems to find the truth was nil.  For organizations like these, the most convenient and compelling story became fact.   Particularly when the truth required work to uncover.

Kyle’s thoughts were cut short as their shuttle descended into the eerie green clouds, approaching the planet’s surface below.  He walked to the communication equipment and sent an inquiry to the space station, expecting like the others that he’d be covering much of the descent on his own.

Courier,

This environment isn’t hazardous for the shuttle.  It’s going to be more of a problem for you.  This probe has an organic core that will need to be saturated with the ambient energy, and then manually sealed away.  You are welcome to rest a bit in the shuttle if needed, but it’s imperative that you’re present to seal it at the right time.  We tried automating this one and sealing it remotely a few times, but the delays in the processing time caused problems.  We do have some additional cores on the shuttle if you need to attempt it again.

-Roole

Kyle checked his nav bracelet, and frowned when he saw that there was nowhere specific that he’d need to place the probe, just that it would need to be stuck into the ground somewhere outside the shuttle.  What kind of world is this?  The data packet was vague, just saying that the environment itself was particularly dangerous, and to exercise extreme caution while exposed.  The green clouds made him think it was likely some sort of radiation or poison, but now he was second-guessing those assumptions.  Whatever made this world so deadly, it did so in a general enough way that very simple exposure could get the Pillar scientists the data they needed.

As the shuttle gently landed, he grabbed the probe, took a deep breath, then stepped outside. Immediately, he felt his body under attack.  His eyes burned, his throat dried, and his skin cracked.  It was as if his entire body was just trying to shut down.  Reflexively, Kyle activated Adaptive Regeneration and channeled a Healing Impetus through his body, rallying vital energy against the mysterious force.  The discomfort abated somewhat, and he put pressure on his mana network to get a better sense of the scope of the unexpected attack..

[WHAT IS HAPPENING?  DR. MAYHEW, WHAT IS CAUSING THE DAMAGE?]

“Not sure,” Kyle said, swapping out his artificial organs with another set as they began to rot away.  Activating Identify, Kyle got a better sense of the ambient mana around him.  It was qualitatively C Grade energy, but rather than being passive mana, it was all infused with singular intent: death.  This energy sought to rot away and kill any living thing within its domain, and now, that meant Kyle.

“It’s using the concept of Death,” Kyle said, channeling Heart of Creation infused with the concept of Adaptation through his healing skills.  Using this aspect of his Devourer’s Anatomy still put some strain on his body and mana network, but focusing on concepts with strong compatibility helped to alleviate it somewhat.  If the mana in the air was trying to get him to rot away, he’d simply have to adapt and overcome it.

Getting just a short distance away from the shuttle, Kyle primed the probe and stuck it into the soft, depleted soil.  The very ground itself gave off a subtle aura of decay, and the moment the probe was in the ground a notification came through his nav bracelet, highlighting its progress as the deadly energy slowly invaded the probe’s core.

C.H.A.D.D. was furiously at work creating new artificial organs within the World Tree Seed, despite the dozens of backup sets that were already prepared.  Through Pack Leader’s Instinct, he could feel the drone’s anxiety spiking, but he felt something else as well.

This energy is quite interesting, Phaegos sent through their link, interest and avarice coming through their bond in equal measure.  Would you be so kind as to allow me to experience some of the mana?  I will do my best to contain it, of course.

[DR. MAYHEW, THAT IS A VERY BAD IDEA,] C.H.A.D.D. warned.  [IF THIS ENERGY GETS INTO YOUR MANA NETWORK, IT COULD BE DISASTROUS.]

It would be dangerous, Phaegos agreed.  Yet, is that not perfectly aligned with our path?  Besides, there is a reason that you have been sent to collect samples from these places.  Think about it; your employers find the resources valuable enough to pay you a royal sum for your work.  Shouldn’t we also find a way to take advantage of these opportunities?

[THAT IS GREEDY, DR. MAYHEW.  WHAT IF THE MANA MANAGES TO INVADE THE WORLD TREE SEED?  WHAT IF IT KILLS OFF YOUR ARTIFICAL ORGANS?  YOUR OTHER CONSTRUCTS?  THERE IS A REAL POSSIBILITY THAT THIS ONLY CREATES SETBACKS, DR. MAYHEW.]

I am not suggesting that you draw in an irresponsible amount of the mana.  And I certainly agree that there are risks.  However, you are no longer a mere D Grade wandering about a broader universe.  You are an exceptional existence, and unless you choose to retire peacefully, you will find yourself in conflict with other apex creatures.  Some of whom can most assuredly leverage the same type of intent and concept you are facing now.

Phaegos stopped for a moment to let the message sink in before continuing, the impression more reserved.

I will not tell you what to do.  But if you truly want to grow powerful enough to stand up to the forebear and demand answers, shying away from an opportunity like this is anathema.  Have confidence in our power.  And drone, do not think that I’ve forgotten the risks you took, even prior to finding the World Tree Seed.  You call me a glutton, but you and I have more in common than either of us readily admits.

That seemed to quiet C.H.A.D.D., and deep inside himself Kyle knew what he wanted to do.  One thing was certain to Kyle; the intent infused into this environmental mana was not natural, at least not wholly so.  Just like the first planet, where the conceptual conflict was too perfectly matched, the acute pressure toward death felt more manufactured than natural.  After all, in a world without any life in the first place, how could a powerful intent like this manifest?  And, to Phaegos’ point, this provided an opportunity to face this concept now before he had to face an expert.

Death magic was not widely seen or practiced, but Kyle had read enough in his studies to know that it did exist.  It was a rare and powerful affinity, and one that entirely opposed his entire path.  Anything he could glean from the experience now could be literally life-saving down the road when he eventually encountered somebody with both the desire and power to leverage it against him.  So, steeling himself, he activated Parasitic Resonance.

Of course, he wasn’t going to be too foolhardy.  He only allowed a couple of tendrils to taste the mana in the air, drawing small portions of the energy into himself, then immediately clamping down on his own mana network before it had the chance to spread across his body.  He felt the tissue near the effected areas begin to wither, and pushed the still-empowered Adaptive Regeneration to counteract its effects.

As he won the smaller battles, he allowed Parasitic Resonance to draw in more of the mana, pushing himself to heal more than before.  The experience was surprisingly less painful than he’d expected, more of a tingling feeling than an agonizing stab, even though he knew that it was still incredibly dangerous.  It took more Willpower than Kyle felt it should have to simply keep the energy contained in one spot.  Its desire was to spread, and Kyle felt it drawn to the wellspring of vital energy housed inside the World Tree Seed.

Fortunately, thanks to his connection, Adaptive Regeneration was able to help the seed fend off the passive exposure it had.  On a whim, he also activated Infuse, and found that the seed stirred with even more energy, fighting off the conceptual Death with very little effort.  He’d never tried to use the skills this way before, and he felt the concept of Adaptation working in both the World Tree Seed and across his body as he kept Adaptive Regeneration running.

When Heart of Creation stopped empowering the skill, causing it to need a brief cooldown period, he shifted to Healing Impetus.  This time, he didn’t use his Devourer’s Anatomy, instead reflecting on the concepts of Immutability and Timelessness.  He still didn’t fully understand the latter, but his mind’s eye kept focusing on the natural treasure from Er’Mithren.  What he could fathom was how each of the concepts interacted with Death.

Adaptation fought against it by growing and changing faster than Death could invade and destroy.  Immutability provided resistance to the concept, restraining and rebuffing it.  By contrast, the Timeless concept simply didn’t allow Death any purchase.  If there was nothing to decay, nothing to stop, the very notion of Death had no place.  Just like the metals and stones that ignored the ambient energy of decay, a truly Timeless body would be unaffected, even in this environment.

Though the Timeless concept was beyond him, Kyle still felt himself making progress against the hostile energy trying to snuff out his life.  Phaegos was greedily devouring the energy that Kyle allowed to enter his mana network, and he slowly expanded the amount that he drew in, giving Phaegos more and more of the power to consume as he pondered the crystalline treasure in his mind’s eye.

Kyle continued to reflect on the idea of Timelessness, activating Healing Impetus while cycling between the concepts he already understood.  They were all connected in different ways, and he felt he was getting close to a breakthrough.  Suddenly, a jolt of pain snapped him from his focus.  At the same time, Kyle felt a sense of alarm wash over him from C.H.A.D.D., and a single word from Phaegos.

Oops.
