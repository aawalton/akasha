Having left one of his Eternal Marks on the door leading down to where Kroult had been held captive all those centuries, the next morning, Asher Recalled straight into the heart of the former Deadlands. He had no interest in hiring another pegasus courier for another four-day journey to Nunait, which meant he was going to have to make his way over to the kingdom in question from here.

Unfortunately, despite his team having played their own vital role in the eradication of the Deadlands, they’d decided Asher would be accepting the reward and attending the ceremony all on his own. It just so happened that today was his and Samantha’s one day per week that they got to spend with Brian, and while Asher was disappointed that he’d be missing out on the energetic twelve-year-old dragging them around the capital, he agreed with Donvath that doing something more public today was probably for the best. Having spent such a long time in his battle form during their fight against the undead, Rulfar had once again gone off to hibernate for a few days to recover his energy. His blended skill between his Bear and Strength elements that allowed him to maintain his battle form for so long had a cost like any other, which meant the bloodhound wasn’t able to join him. And Moxy…

Asher figured he would save all of them an international incident and not bring just the battle-manic warrior with him to the ceremony. Moxy was more than fine with the decision, declaring that she’d rather spend her time beating up the captain of the royal guards anyway.

Alone in the deadlands once again, Asher shivered as he looked around at the empty hallway he was in. Fallen skeletons lay scattered all along the ground, and tapestries woven from sinew that depicted horrible scenes of undead feasting upon small towns hung from the walls. It had only been about a day since he’d actually taken down the King of Death, which meant that the bulk of whoever Nunait was sending over to take care of the cleanup probably hadn’t arrived yet. Asher didn’t see any signs of that paladin he’d spoken to yesterday anywhere, so with a shrug, he turned and headed out of the building.

Part of him was tempted to go through the different rooms and see what he could find that might be worth taking, but the King of Death’s workshop was a massive structure. Asher knew if he went down that road he’d end up spending the entire day hopping from one room to the next killing undead abominations, and while that did sound sort of fun, it was far more important that he heed Donvath’s advice and show himself to the world. The thought of a second Shadow Squad hunting for his head and stumbling upon Samantha and Brian of all people while they were out enjoying the day was enough to send a spike of cold dread through his chest, and Asher wasted no time in getting out of the building. Of course, even after he’d made it outside…

He had quite the trek ahead of him.

Damn, I wish Samantha and her wings were here right about now…

Shaking his head, Asher did what all people without wings did when they needed to get from point a to point b. He lowered his head and began running.

Thankfully, with Distortion in his back pocket to create tunnels of compressed space and his Belt of Strength giving him a much-needed advantage, Asher was capable of running far quicker than the average person. On top of which, Complete Resilience helped keep the fatigue at bay, and Eternal Regeneration worked to repair his sore muscles in real time. All that to say, Asher had achieved the point where he could pretty much keep up a hard run just about indefinitely.

Still, thirty-plus miles was thirty-plus miles he needed to cover in order to get out of the Deadlands, and Asher didn’t have anything like music or a podcast to listen to as he ran and leapt over the countless decaying bodies of zombies that littered the former Deadlands. Instead, he thought about the future.

Such as how to best go about taking down the next lich that stood in their path.

Of the remaining ten liches in the Council of Death, only one of them was somewhat publicly known. Iphiane. Or as the locals liked to call her, The Lost Lady. While the general populace wasn’t aware that she was a lich, they did know that some form of vengeful spirit lived within a sprawling cave system. One that clung to her undead corpse and occasionally ventured outside every few years to hunt down and retrieve strong men who reminded her of her husband. While the rest of the Council kept a low profile, the King of Death and The Lost Lady were the only two who had public personas, and that was the reason why Asher and his team were starting with them. They wanted to try and hide the fact that Zygrul had left them dossiers on the Council for as long as possible, which meant avoiding going straight for any secret bases or marked locations that didn’t exist on any map.

Once everyone was recovered, their next mission would involve heading deep underground.

Asher spent the majority of his run brainstorming what exactly they would need to prepare for such an assault. Watcher, one of the powerful Servants created by the King of Death, had destroyed almost his entire potion supply, which meant they had plenty of room to replenish their stocks with whatever they might find useful for a sprawling cave system. Hilariously, the only potion that had survived the undead’s rampage had been the experimental potion of Bright Sight that he’d snagged ages back. He’d originally picked it up to be a part of his plan to take down the Head of the Assassin’s Guild, but that had been put on hold ever since they’d gone to ground.

The biggest worry he had around the hunt for their next lich was the fact that of all of them, Samantha would be the most handicapped. An underground system of caves meant she’d be grounded despite her Wing element, and her Forest element would yet again be all but worthless. On the plus side, there shouldn’t be any greater demons involved this time around like there had been with Zygrul, which meant they wouldn’t have to worry about their artifacts being dampened. Though as to what horrific threats they would face, he could only wonder…

In what felt like no time at all, Asher realized he was nearing the edge of the former Deadlands, and he blinked as he realized he’d gotten pretty lost in thought. A quick glance up at the sky revealed he’d managed to cover thirty plus miles in only a couple of hours, and he chuckled to himself at the absurdity of the situation. That might not have been too impressive for any marathon runner given magical assistance, but Asher had not been a fan of cardio back on Earth. He’d added running to his daily workout routine at Moxy’s suggestion, but even so, it was tiny things like that which continued to surprise him unexpectedly.

As dead, blackened ground shifted almost instantly to lush, green grass and vibrant life when Asher passed the border back into Nunait, he fingered the seal of the sun, going back over what the paladin had told him.

Bring the seal to one of the guards at Preaset’s walls, the capital of Nunait, and they would take care of getting the ceremony prepared. Sounds simple enough. Only…

Where the hell is Preaset?

Luckily, Asher had Incorporated a large map of their continent some time ago. Because it was scaled up to cover the entire continent, there was quite a lot missing from it, such as anything beyond major cities or significant landmarks. However, the capital of an entire country was definitely important enough to land on the map, as was the splotch that had been marked off as the Deadlands. Using that, Asher oriented himself in the right direction and took off running once more.

That million and a half shards we spent on the Wing element for Samantha… Greatest purchase of our lives.

The sun slowly rose over his head as one hour passed after another, and Asher tried not to think about how Samantha could have covered this entire distance in a fraction of the time.

I guess ‘runner’ isn’t a class, because if it was, I’d definitely have it by now!

Eventually, Asher managed to find a small town where he was able to confirm he was heading the correct direction, and the people living within even pointed out the actual road for him to head down. Hilariously, he was capable of moving faster than a horse-drawn carriage at this point thanks to him not needing to take breaks beyond recovering his energy from all the uses of Distortion. So rather than kick back and hire this world’s version of a cab, he just kept trucking on. Seeing as he was going to be officially revealing himself to the world anyway, and anyone important already knew about him and what his Spatial element could do, Asher didn’t even bother keeping his blatant bending of the fabric of space hidden as he ran down the road. He ignored the startled cries as he passed carriages in a single step, waving an apology as he wove in and out of the road that was growing denser the closer he got to this nation’s capital.

As the sun slowly began to approach the horizon and the sky turned a warm red, Asher finally reached his destination after an entire day of running full tilt. He was equally surprised and thankful that the capital of Nunait was only about a hundred miles from the edge of the Deadlands, and he took a moment to simply lean against a nearby tree and catch his breath as he took in the outer wall of Preaset.

Based on the giant, glowing sun motif etched into the side of the wall, he was definitely in the right place. The traffic was smaller than what he usually saw flowing in and out of the capital of the Noala Kingdom, but that was to be expected. While the Noala Kingdom was on the smaller side compared to most countries on this continent, based on the map he’d Incorporated, Nunait was almost the smallest country. From what little he knew of the small country, it had actually been formed in response to the Deadlands themselves. Neither of the two larger countries that had once shared borders with the Deadlands had wanted to deal with the zombies and abominations that occasionally wandered out of it, and yet neither of them had been strong enough to reach the center and eradicate whatever was causing the deadly phenomenon. Instead, they worked together to sell off a chunk of the surrounding land, helping create a smaller country with the sole purpose of keeping the Deadlands contained. While Nunait had certainly grown into its own over the past fifteen hundred years, Asher was admittedly a little curious as to what would happen to the smaller country now that their initial reason for existence had been wiped out.

Still, if that hadn’t been their goal from the start, they wouldn’t have set up the five-million-shard bounty in the first place. With that in mind, Asher did his best to clean himself up and pretend like he hadn’t spent the last twelve hours sprinting across the land as he made his way over to the city walls. There was only one path leading into the city and one leading out, which meant his options were simple enough.

[Guard] - 1/3 elements

“Welcome to Preaset,” the guard said, smiling at him after waving a wagon carrying bushels of hay through and motioning for him to step up. “Purpose for entering the city?”

“I’m here to collect a bounty,” Asher admitted, grinning at the guard’s confused face before Asher reached out and handed them the metal sun seal he’d been given. The moment it fell into the guard’s hands, the man’s eyes shot wide open, and he gaped as he stared at Asher in shock.

“You’re the one who finished off the Deadlands?”

“Yep. And it wasn’t easy for the record,” Asher added, deciding he didn’t need to mention how he’d technically died while doing so. That made his accomplishment sound a lot less impressive in his opinion.

“Please, wait right here!” the guard said, motioning for another guard to come forward and take over the line of people waiting to enter the city as he ran off. Asher stepped to the side and happily waved at a few curious faces who were now watching him as they waited for their turn. The guard hadn’t exactly been quiet in his exclamation, and already Asher could hear whispers traveling through the thin crowd as people turned and tapped their neighbors, gesturing excitedly in his direction.

I guess being an unknown was fun while it lasted, Asher thought, letting out a small sigh as he prepared himself for what was to come. Before he could get too depressed thinking about the anonymity he was leaving behind, there was a flash of brilliant, blinding light, and a familiar paladin appeared before him, clearly having used some form of teleportation skill. She wore the same pure-white armor emblazoned with a golden sun that she’d worn during their meeting in the Deadlands, and the moment she spotted him, she literally beamed. Her very eyes were a soft, glowing gold, and light seemed to genuinely radiate from her skin.

“Asher!” she said, stepping forward and holding a hand across her chest as she gave him a formal bow. “On behalf of the Shining Order, welcome to Preaset!”
