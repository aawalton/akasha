Henry didn’t float down to meet the humans.

It might seem rude, and he might come off as standoffish, but he didn’t know them, and he didn’t trust them. Most importantly, Henry had sensed the avaricious drive emanating out of both of them as they rushed toward the scene. It was clear that they had intended on capitalizing on the situation. And that hadn’t changed when the first of them had arrived, just as Henry finished off his bird.

The older man, a level 19 A-rank, still had the glint of greed in his eyes when he first looked up at Henry, and he might have actually been about to take his shot, as Henry sensed the mana build up in the man’s chest. He had been preparing to activate a skill. It might have been a simple Identify. It might have been an attempt at kill-stealing Henry’s eagle. And it might have been an attempt on Henry, Maurice, or one of Henry’s avatars.

Whatever it was, Henry wasn’t about to let himself be pushed around. If they were introducing themselves, he might as well get the message across, and Henry did just that.

Right as the man arrived, crouching in preparation to activate whatever it was he’d been channeling, one of Henry’s Octomind appeared behind him. It was like it had faded into existence, its arcane ghostly form looming over the unaware A-rank. It tapped him on the shoulder, casually, and in the millisecond it took the alarmed man to turn, a sharp Dispelling Pulse rippled out and flushed the nascent skill out of his system before it could be brought to bear.

The man flinched, eyes wide, and Henry turned to face the boar. Dispelling Pulse, coupled with a dash of Unfettered Echoes, had picked up the man’s ambition and broke its back over its knee. Now he just had to send the same message across. To the boar, to the snake, and to the human who’d just gotten here.

“[They hadn’t lied,]” he sent to Maurice, thinking of the intel he’d heard about the Reach. “[It’s really busy here.]”

“[I know, right?]” Maurice sent back, his joy clear as day, both on his face and through his voice. “[I love it!]”

Henry… didn’t hate it. He sort of did well in chaos. This was like their first days in the Current all over again. Opportunities were all over the place. He just had to be ready to pluck’em.

***

Once the opportunistic monsters were dealt with, and once Henry had impressed on the human duo that he was no easy pickings, he greeted them, and as they recovered Henry had one of his Octominds appear above the two eagle carcasses and stow them away under the gaze of the strangers, right before he had the projection fade away into nothingness by a smooth transition from a camouflage to invisibility. To really drive in that at any time, any number of summons could appear.

It really did help that Henry’s Octomind had managed to dispel whatever the man had tried to cast. That had really set the tone. As for the man in question, he finally smiled, dipping his chin a little.

“Greetings, travelers. Apologies for the brusque entrance. We were just worried a fellow patrol might have been in a bit of trouble. I’m Omar, and this here is my colleague. We are on patrol out of Wolf’s Den. Are you new to the Reach?”

Henry raised a brow, not missing the subtext of the question. Was he interpreting it right? Was the man trying to discern if he was already affiliated with a group in particular? Well. Honesty might serve him best, here.

“We are new, yes,” Henry said, producing his Emerald Isles ID and flashing it for a brief moment before he tucked it back. It was a pretty, square-like green gem, kind of cut like an emerald. “From First Landing.”

“Oh?” Omar exclaimed. “Hammond here’s from First Landing. What are the odds? I myself am from Cloversdale.”

Henry had no clue where that was, but he just nodded. As for this Hammond fellow, he was examining Henry and Maurice, though naturally he didn’t recognize either.

“Small world,” Henry noncommittally said with a smile. He was interested in eventually checking out the settlement, but he was in no rush. In fact, he’d rather leave and continue his hunt slash training as they made their way toward the orb. “It was nice to meet you, Mr. Omar and Mr. Hammond. When we come by Wolf’s Den, we’ll make sure to sing your praise.”

Omar’s smile slipped, but only for the briefest moment before he recovered.

“Of course,” he said, nodding, then his smile widened as he leaned against his stick, face turning a little to the left as if he was about to impart some precious secret. “You know, we’re always looking for new allies. I’m sure our Warden would love to meet you both. High B-ranks who can down A-ranks don’t come by often. How about I show you around? I promise it’ll be worth your while.”

Henry kept his face neutral, but inwardly, the invitation annoyed him.

Already, the currents of the local politics were dragging him in.

Henry would rather not ally with any of the local forces, as that could make his traveling harder. Allying with this Omar and his Warden meant that whomever they didn’t get along with would start looking at Henry sideways, and he wasn’t interested in that.

Now what he needed to figure it is whether these two were going to press the issue.

Henry considered for a moment while behind him, Maurice continued to roll around, as if he had been stuck in some invisible wheel while he stared and listened to the strangers.

“I appreciate the invitation. But I’ll have to decline for now as I’m rather having fun exploring a new continent.”

The beat of silence that followed was telling. The normal, completely non-suspicious response would have been something in the vein of ‘of course! Have fun’. But the calculating look he saw from the older man told him that this would have some repercussion. Nothing as severe as a bounty or anything. But likely, this Warden he spoke of, and maybe a couple more individuals will learn of Henry’s presence. Maybe of what he had just done here. Then what they would do with that information was up to chance.

The beat lasted for nearly three second. Then Omar’s smile widened. “Ah, the wonder of seeing these wild lands for the first time. I envy you, you know?”

Putting his fists in the small of his back, Omar stretched, a benevolent smile on his lips. “Mr. Henry, it was very nice meeting you. I hope we’ll have the opportunity of working together in the future. Please, don’t hesitate to come visit. Ask for Omar, and they’ll drag me out from wherever I happened to be, and I’ll make sure you see the best of what Wolf’s Den has to offer. Trust me, First Landing isn’t the only spot where one can have a good time. I’m sure we could even get you a very good price on those eagles and their talons.”

What followed then was a couple of pleasantries, then the two human disappeared into the brush, heading eastward. Henry tracked them for a while, hovering far above and watching through an Octomind as he didn’t want to risk discovery.

“You think they’ll cause us problems?” Maurice asked after a couple of quiet minutes. The crab was still enjoying himself, and Henry could understand why. He’d finally downed his solo A-rank. Henry hadn’t contributed at all.

“Probably. But they’d have to keep up with us,” Henry said. “And I don’t intend on letting them.”

***

With the ring back on his finger, Henry and Maurice flew away, following the golden path toward the orb. They didn’t stay long into the clearing. Barely a couple of minutes after the two humans had left, they departed themselves. And while the two briefly debated going invisible, they decided not to in the end.

They wanted a dumb A-rank to ambush them again.

So the two flew, enjoying the scenery as they did, while Henry’s network-rearranging Octominds continued its work. Already, the link to First Landing was back, and it was fully functional. The relays were well within their optimal range, so Henry could literally teleport there within the minute. As for the link to Thalis, it was close. His avatars were traveling fast. It only took a couple of minutes for each to reach the edge of its range, at which point it would spawn a new avatar, which then becomes a relay point. Already, the avatar had made it to the Current, so it was only a matter of hours until the link was established.

But those two weren’t the only areas Henry was connecting to his network. Because after a pretty sustained sprint of nearly eight hours, the two human A-ranks made it to their settlement.

Wolf’s Den was… different than what Henry had pictured.
