Ashton looked down at the hole in the ground. It led down into a deep, pitch-black darkness. The plants that grew on the edge of the hole led down inside, appearing as if they were being swallowed up completely.

"So it's down there?" Oka asked curiously, peering down into the darkness. Ashton slowly nodded, smiling with relief.

"Mm-hm. If the information is correct, at least," Ashton was relieved beyond belief. He had been looking for this place for years, and it almost felt like he wasn't going to get there.

The object he should be able to get from here wasn't exactly on the 'list' of things that he had to get for the Summoner; he already had gotten all of those multiple times over, far more than the Summoner even asked for. But this item was something that Ashton knew they had been looking for for a long time.

The Summoner was a dangerous, unpredictable creature, and Ashton had no idea what they were going to do if they suddenly got angry or upset by something that he did. He had to get on their good side and make sure that Oka could be protected somehow.

To summoners, contracts were something extremely important. Maybe, if the Summoner was in a good enough mood, it might be possible for Ashton to form a contract where Oka could be kept safe.

And it wasn't that they were weak, or couldn't take care of themself; it was just that the Summoner was an overwhelmingly powerful being that Ashton couldn't even imagine taking down. Even if he became powerful enough to take down some of the other creatures that Ashton had encountered during his time with the Summoner, taking them down seemed like a hopeless endeavour.

Ashton wouldn't even be surprised if they somehow forced the very concept of death into submission, so that they would never be able to pass on from that amalgamation of chaos they called their body.

But... they did have emotions. Most of those emotions were some form of anger, but every once in a while, it did seem like they could get happy. And if Ashton took advantage of that... he might be able to protect Oka properly.

"Okay, I'll go down first," Ashton said, walking up to the edge of the hole. "Once I'm down, I'll let you know if it's safe to follow."

Oka let out a long, stretched-out sigh, shaking their head. "You know exactly that this is my specialty. I still think you should let me go first here."

But Ashton wouldn't have it. "Sure, but this is also something that I'm insisting on trying to get, so I'll take the brunt of the danger."

"I know, I know, but exploring ruins... that's what I've done my entire life."

"Which is why you'll be taking the lead once we're down there, but at least let me make sure it's safe right by the entrance," Ashton suggested. Well, it wasn't really a suggestion. From what he understood, this place was protected by some extremely powerful beings; ones that Ashton wasn't exactly sure he was able to beat all on his own.

His internal core was close to reaching the third stage, at least, and his techniques had improved a lot, so he should be able to take care of any of the beasts or creatures hiding down there. He hoped, at least. He had also prepared a ton of potions that were going to come in useful for this.

But for now, he just had to get down there.

"Fine..." Oka let out a long sigh, crossing their arms with a smirk. "But if you get hurt or get caught in a trap, don't tell me that I didn't warn you."

"Yeah, yeah, as if that's going to happen."

Oka raised their brow. "Are you forgetting what happened in the 'Achack' ruins in that rainforest just a few months ago?"

Ashton stared up at Oka and narrowed his eyes. "That's just because you were distracting me."

"You sure it's not because you were drunk?"

"Oh come on! It was our honeymoon," Ashton laughed. "I'm allowed to let loose sometimes, right? And I didn't get hurt in any of those pitfalls either."

Oka smirked and leaned down, placing their forehead against Ashton's. "Of course you didn't. Just make sure to be a bit more careful this time around."

"Yeah, yeah," Ashton smiled, placing his hand against Oka's cheek for a moment. "Anyway, I'll see you down there, alright?"

Just smiling at Oka and waving slightly, Ashton jumped backward into the hole, and could only hear his spouse sighing as they stood at the entrance for a little longer.

The fall was quite long, but it wasn't that big a deal. Ashton sent out a pulse of internal energy to sense how deep the hole was, though the distorted response that he got didn't exactly make him particularly confident.

So instead, Ashton took a stone from his pocket that he grabbed above, and threw it downward. It should have reached the ground pretty fast, but... there wasn't any sound of it hitting anything.

Ashton had been falling for quite a while now. At least thirty seconds. There was no way this hole could be that deep; no civilization in this world had ever created their towns this far underground.

"A barrier, then," Ashton said to himself and grabbed something from his bag. He held the small vial between his teeth for now, and then swung his staff around. After sending out a pulse of internal energy specifically meant to destroy magical formations just like this one, he could feel a shift in the energy around him. It wasn't quite as 'isolating' as before.

But it wasn't destroyed; only slightly shaken. So, Ashton spat out the glass vial and smashed it with his staff. The moment he did and the substance inside made contact with the air, it exploded out into a massive mass of foam. It created extreme heat and steam as it expanded, and that steam seemed to cling to the magic of the barrier placed on this area.

The weakness of the barrier revealed by Ashton's initial technique was quickly taken advantage of, and it spread throughout the entire magic pattern around Ashton.

By the time the foam had dissolved into steam completely, and the ambient temperature made Ashton feel like he was in a real oven, the barrier fell apart, and Ashton reached the ground.

He looked up. The hole he entered through was only a few dozen metres up.

Ashton sent another pulse of internal energy out around him to get a good idea of what was around him, and could only sense the old ruins he had been looking for.

"You can come down now!" Ashton yelled out, and before he even knew it, Oka was rappelling down from above.

They reached the bottom soon enough, and then quickly looked around. They grabbed the small lantern by their hip and activated it. This was a magic item that they had brought with them from their world. It broke in the transfer, but Ashton managed to repair it some time ago.

Instead of creating light through fire, it basically created an artificial source of light within its centre. It was really like a ball of light was now floating inside of the lantern.

And the neat part about this item was that by just adjusting a few panels, it could be used to light up a relatively large area for a campsite, or be used to illuminate the entirety of a deep tunnel. It could even be used underwater without trouble.

A truly wonderful item of magical engineering that Ashton barely even understood, even if he fixed it. It was one of the handful of things that Oka still had from Karros.

The lantern lit up the area around the pair perfectly. They were in a relatively small room with a collapsed ceiling. Most of the rubble had slid down into the cave that was right adjacent, so it was actually relatively clean here.

A long hallway stretching out in front of them, leading away from the room. Ashton could see some sigils placed on the stones that made up the walls. Those were most likely what held up the barrier, though there really wasn't anything keeping up the power here.

It was likely that it was deeper inside the ruins. And by the time Ashton came to that conclusion, Oka was already following the trail left behind by those sigils and runes, trying to use them as breadcrumbs to lead to what they were looking for.

"When you're looking for powerful items like this one, it's often possible that they act as the power source of the ruins. Or, at the very least, that they're nearby that power source. Especially in underground spaces like this where it's hard to spread out, they usually place the most important parts first, and then add whatever else they may need later," Oka pointed out, looking around. "It's likely that this was some kind of storage room. It looks like it's pretty far out, which is why the magic keeping the structure together was weaker here and allowed that hole in the ceiling. Either that, or this was meant as an emergency escape path. Would explain why it's right next to that cave, as well."

Ashton nodded. That all made sense, though it wasn't like he didn't know that already. Oka had already explained all of these principles to him dozens of times over. But it was okay; he was as excited to hear them talk about any of it the first time, as he was the twentieth.

The rest of the ruin exploration wasn't anything all too special. There were a lot of traps, some collapsed hallways and rooms, but surprisingly, there wasn't anything around protecting the space.

Powerful magic items always attracted magic beasts. Were they kept out by the barrier, then? Though it didn't seem like the sort that would keep them out, and more like one that would trap them and break their mind so that they could be used as tools or fodder for whatever else was down here.

It was actually pretty nerve-racking that there was nothing here at all. Especially as they kept moving deeper into the ruins and spotted the traces of living beings. Claw-marks on the walls, or moss-overgrown bones. There was something here. Long ago, but after this place fell to ruin.

"That's weird..." Oka whispered, running their hands over the marks on the walls. "These look... familiar."

"Hm? How so?" Ashton asked, taking a look himself. To him, they really just looked like the marks of any old beast to him. Sure, there were some differences for each sort, but there wasn't anything particularly recognisable about these.

"Just... a gut feeling I have, but I think this might be..." Oka responded, before sharply turning to Ashton. Their face was completely pale. "This is bad! I told you about that 'thing' that was in the ruins when I fell here, right?"

"Wait, are you saying that it's the same creature?" Ashton asked. It sounded a bit ridiculous, but it wasn't impossible. If Oka could fall here, then that creature could as well. It was still a stretch, but Oka's gut feelings were basically never wrong.

"But... does that mean that this place has something to do with your world? Or something with moving from one world to another?" Ashton's heart was almost beating out of his chest. Frankly, he had abandoned the idea of using Oka to find a way out of Lumia a long time ago. "I guess it makes sense, I mean, it's an item that that thing is looking for, so it's not too far-fetched for it to be related to other worlds..."

However, while Ashton was getting excited at the possibility, Oka shook their head.

"No, Ashton, we can't beat that thing! That's not something you can fight! We have to leave! Right now, we have to–" Oka tried to grab Ashton's arm to drag him away, but he was still stuck in his thoughts and wouldn't budge. "Seriously, stop it, just come with–"

Oka stopped speaking. Ashton was confused, looking up at his spouse. "Wait, what's wrong?" he asked, and slowly looked at the place she was facing.

Right there in the hallway, peering around the corner, was a creature that Ashton could only describe as an abomination. A thing that made his stomach turn upside down, that made him want to rip off his skin because even that pain would be better than having to be in this creature's presence.

Whatever it was, it was the same species as the Summoner.
