Kill All Gods And Rule The Universe

Lord Dread was the first lich in history—and the greatest magical prodigy the world has ever known. The Gods themselves feared his potential, and so they banished him for it.

They should have been more thorough.

After freeing himself from a prison of countless millennia, Lord Dread awakens to find his powers diminished… and his body reborn as that of a baby girl. Born into an aristocratic family and given a rare chance to relearn magic properly this time, his ambitions quickly return: reclaim his former strength, rebuild the dark empire that once challenged even the Gods, and finally take revenge on the divinities who struck him down.

This would all be much easier if girls could inherit land.

Fortunately, Lord Dread has never let a minor obstacle like that stop him.

If you’re interested in giving early feedback—on pacing, hook strength, tone, or anything else that stands out—we’d really appreciate it.

Thanks for your time, and we hope you enjoy it.— A.C.Prologue
