Ethan blinked, touched the spot where her hand had left a burning imprint, and slowly met Fauna’s shuddering eyes.

No words passed between them. And all who looked on could be forgiven for thinking that Fauna’s hand had practically left her arm to strike at Ethan of its own accord.

The next moment she turned and ran from the room.

“Faun!” Tara shouted after her. She made to give chase, but Ethan held up a hand.

“Let her be,” he said.

Tara turned to see that Klax was standing cross-armed, staring after Fauna just as she had been.

“She has a right to feel that way,” Ethan continued. “And I have no right to tell her otherwise.”

The team let silence reign again while Tara debated running after her sister.

“You know…she’s never gonna go for this idea,” she murmured. “Not after…everything that’s happened.”

“The time has come for us to make a choice, it seems,” Klax nodded gravely. “A choice between our personal wishes and those of the world. Ethan has made the only choice that he can make. It’s the right choice. And I am with him.”

“Who’s gonna tell her that?” Tara scoffed. “You, or me?”

“No,” Ethan said. “I will.”

He rose, unfurling his wings and shaking himself off. This conversation had exhausted him more than any recent battle.

“But not right now,” he said. “Take some time. Rest. I’ll do the same. Let’s meet back here in five hour’s time.”

“Five ho – but don’t you have to save the world and shit like right now?”

“Kaedmon can wait,” Ethan replied, already retiring to his own chamber in the monastery.

But he stopped just before he left.

“I want you each to think about this course,” he said. “And I want you to tell me, honestly, if you think it’s for the best. This decision can’t be made lightly, and if even one of you tells me, even after all this, that my place should be here on Argwyll, then I’ll stay.”

Both of them looked puzzled at that. It seemed that both the Lycae and Minxit had believed Ethan was giving them an order.

But now they saw they were being given a choice.

“Leave Fauna to me,” he said before he left.
