Again with the sorry. Her ear twitches. She knows that word means something different to Dylan Thorogood—she’s already wondering how she might prudently remove it from his vocabulary. “Why’s that?”

But something in her thrills to it. To the idea of…

Choosing a different door. Choosing something for yourself.

Thinking is harmless. She nods.

Xamika is lighter-than-air, and she feels vaguely terrified, and she feels very brave. “You’re on.”

She wipes her palm on her skirt before she takes it and shakes it.

“Starting tomorrow,” he says, and his hand is so huge it could fold hers in completely, but his touch is so infinitely gentle. “When 9:40 in the morning comes around, I’m yours.”
