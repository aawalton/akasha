Chapter 126

Almost a Clear Victory

Cumbersome, lazy, and ambling, those are the words that I would now use to describe the team of Bloom family members who were coming at me. These were the same words that were used to describe me and my efforts at first, when I asked for honest feedback from Mr. Yi. At the time, I thought they were rough and used them as motivation to continue to push myself further through hellish training sessions.

Yet, watching the senior members of my fostering family now, I could see that they were accurate, at least when describing this group. Perhaps it was the fact that I had just been a member of a team of combat veterans not days ago, seeing how they all moved and reacted as a team. Each person in that team was individually active, and at first glance appeared as if they were moving on their own. As if their actions had no impact on the others, but really, they were spreading out, creating attack paths that allowed for lines of sight for members in the back to be able to adjust their actions accordingly. In a way, I was blessed to have that rift experience first, as it showed me what a truly capable team was. Even though I was clearly the weakest, the entire team went into that operation knowing that to be the case and reacted accordingly. Protecting me by moving about me in ways that allowed me to help rather than hinder the process.

By contrast this group was clearly overwhelmed by the incident they staged, clearly thinking that age, numbers, and the fact that four of the members had Classes would be more than enough to take on the three of us. Two of whom did not even have a class, making the potential for disparity a relevant issue. Duels like these were not uncommon, as it was often the elders who demanded duels of their juniors, knowing that the gap in powers and abilities of the Classless, or lower-Class wielders of the Upper-Class wielders was a chasm too wide to pass. I could also see this as the old Crone’s last attempt to put me in my place, figuring that even with a superior class, I only had this class for a week, as of today.

My mind lets me see all the reasons and potential future justifications that were given as to why these elders would choose now to make such an obvious power play. But I dismissed them, and instead treated them the same way I would any future challenge presented. I attacked it mercilessly and without restraint.

My first target was Icky, who left herself wide open, especially as she only went with a dagger. A concealable weapon, but one that did not serve to counter the weapons of her opponents. Me wielding a large but blunted stone sword, and Lucia and Luna both wielding longer spears that they were clearly comfortable with wielding. Also, given her short reach, Icky was thrust to the front, giving her less distance to have to make up at the start of the conflict. The only unfortunate thing for her and the other aunties was that they were too busy waiting for Pierre to signal the start of the fight, than to the fact that the fight would start and they would need to spring into action.

Within one magically enhanced stride I had closed the distance and was to my right, Icky’s left and about even with her. By my second stride, I had shifted forward, planting my left foot into the ground and used my toes to help me pivot and shift my momentum into a spinning motion that I further used to enhance my strike at Icky. I wanted to aim for her knee, but something in me caused me to halt just a moment, and instead, I went for the meaty part of her hamstrings, twisting the blunted blade ever so slightly at the last second to provide as much area of impact to the overly exposed hamstring. Treating my blade more as a smaller disciplinary paddle than an actual sword. Even with the shifting of direction, the blunting of the strike, and even holding back from my full force, I could hear a distinct crack and popping sensation as my blade still pushed into and then through thick muscle fibers in her leg. Then the blow met bone, stopped for a fraction of a second, before  the force even pushed through that obstacle as well.

She began falling almost instantly, but I had already pulled my blade back, and wanting to make sure she was out had my blade up and thought about hitting her in the back of the head, where my instincts and training directed my blow, but again at the last second I realized that might be too much force for her. I knew she was weak, but not this weak, as such I went easy on her. Once again I adjusted the flight path of my sword again, going low, aiming under the head, under the very fragile and vulnerable neck, and instead twisted the blade and let the width of my blade strike her along the shoulder blades. Just like before, there was a cracking sound, as my reflexes took. Icky was falling forward, her body caught in a stunned pose as she seemed to be seizing up, but I could tell from a glance that she was still alive. While her posture let me know that she would not be a threat going forward.

With her gone, I turned my attention to the four other crones who all looked in various shades of shock. But it was their complete lack of coordination that struck out to me. Herra, the dominatrix lady with the whip, and Moira the bow wielder were in the back, behind Keira and Jolia, the two sword wielders. But rather than flanking out, spreading wide and allowing Moira to have a clear shot at me, the two sword wielders showed that they had never worked together as a team, for they just charged straight forward. This forced Moira and Herra to both hold their fire as they clearly didn’t trust themselves to attack through the small gap provided by Kiera and Jolia’s advance.

The end result was sadly underwhelming. Kiera went low, swinging from my left side, and aiming at my waist, while Jolia made an overly large downward striking motion, which in hindsight might have been a good attack pattern for the duo, had they timed their attacks to land simultaneously. However, Kiera had no wind up on her attack, while Jolia’s attack looked like she lost balance by the sudden upward trajectory to gain force and momentum on her downward swing. The result was just as I mentally planned out. My mind treating the whole sequence like a dance, of openings and closings. As she swung, I took one step backwards to the left, thereby letting Keira’s blade pass right in front of me. Then I immediately began stepping forward once, catching up to the swinging sword with my own, and placing my vertical blade against her horizontal strike, giving additional force and momentum to her now unimpeded swing. Then I take another forceful step forward, again getting myself closer to Jolia, and her overly exposed stomach. A stomach that is nearly undefended thanks to the overly dramatic strike she had wound up for, and then seemingly lost balance at the top, before regaining her balance and continuing the strike. But it was already too late, as I continued moving forward and even past Jolia, going to Jolia’s side, all while forcing Keria’s blade into Jolia’s stomach. The blade cutting deeply, a clear sign that this was not meant to be a friendly spar. Taking a quick glance at the blade, I could see that this was all orchestrated to look like an accident. For Keira did have a blade that had a blunted tip, but the shaft of the blade was apparently sharp as ever, a fact that Jolia was proving right now.

I stopped my dance of blades, having taken one step backwards, and three steps forward, I managed to redirect one opponent into disabling their ally. Jolia began slumping over almost immediately, dropping her blade as she immediately began clutching at the blade that Keira had sliced into her abdomen. Then in an act of pure panic, Keira had desperately pulled the blade away, unwittingly slicing her blade deeper into Jolia’s stomach. A fact that caused both Jolia and Keira immense pain, as Keira seemed to forget for a second that she was still in a Team Duel and broke off a hand from her blade to reach out a comforting hand to grab at Jolia who was dropping to her knees and grabbing her guts as if her life depended on it, which it likely did. I knew they all had the Bloom bloodline awakened, but that could only heal you so much.

With a quick swipe of my blade, I could easily crack a distracted Keira over the head, an act I was about to do, when I realized that Moira now had a direct line of sight to me, and was already aiming her shot at me. Though I felt slightly better seeing that her hand trembled slightly as she now seemed to take in the fact that I was not as weak and defenseless as she expected.

Seeing that Moira with her bow was the more immediate threat, my mind went back to dancing. Fluidly moving and strutting back two of the three paces that I had just moved forward. This put me squarely in front of a kneeling Jolia, and within the direct firing path of Moira, who could easily aim over Jolia to strike me. Her hands still trembled, but I saw the moment her killer instincts took over as I raised my blade diagonally to my left, preparing my blade for a slicing motion that could easily cut into a distracted Keira who had her hand on Jolia’s shoulder, or into Jolia herself who was clearly distracted with her own injuries.

I could almost see the moment resolve took over, as Moira’s hands stopped their trembling and seemed to straighten, as she focused. That’s when I began to make my own attack, swinging my sword at a clearly distracted Keira in a move that could easily be a death blow, at an exposed neck and shoulders of Keira, a person who seemed to be too nice to attend a duel. Seeing her, I could also see why she was so poor at fighting, as she didn’t seem to have a mean bone in her body. I even found myself feeling sorry for her, as she genuinely didn’t seem like she wanted to be here, but somehow found herself obligated to be here due to pressure from the others, that and maybe a deep seeded bond to Jolia, someone whom Keira clearly cared deeply for.

That’s when Moira made her move, her grip firm, her eyes determined, she released the arrow, but it was already too late. Her arrow was fast, far faster than any of the Blooms we were fighting could be, but my eyes still caught its movement and trajectory. After training with Mr. Yi and seeing his speed, my eyes became adjusted to such movements.

This is when I again began to dance, my body still powered by Life Reinforced Movements*, I moved. My swinging blade struck Keira, as I began taking a half step to my left, the movement took away a bit of the force from my swing, but I wasn’t trying to make any killing blows. Instead, I once again went easy, snapping the broad side of my sword against Keira’s shoulders. Taking her body that was already at a precarious angle leaning over, and forcing her further to my right, inadvertently causing her to stumble and trip over Jolia, at the same time an arrow struck her back.

There was a moment’s pause as everyone seemed to take in the carnage. Keira even seemed to hover in air, turning back to look at a now clearly startled Moira whose eyes went from showing a look of clear resolve one second, to a look of shock and panic the next.  Then gravity took over, dropping Kiera onto an already injure Julia, a sight that caused both Moira and Herra to be terrified and take an involuntary step forward. It was clear that these were not seasoned warriors, nor people who had experienced combat in any real form. These were people who grinded their Skills in safety and privilege, likely taking up bureaucratic healing roles that made them have an over inflated ego. That or, they just didn’t expect to run into me.

Regardless of the reason, I used their moment of confusion and hesitation to charge forward. Swooping and twirling around the two downed swordswomen, and moving to strike Herra first. My sword crashing into her exposed hands that gripped her whip, the only real weapon at this range.

My blade crashed with a dull and sickening crack, as my stone sword made quick work of crushing her delicate and pudge coated fingers. Fingers that showed a life of luxury in this world, versus the calloused strife that most had to suffer through. Then a quick sweeping kick to the back of her right knee caused Herra to drop to the ground, flat on her back like a turtle, as she clutched her broken hands desperately to her chest.

With the fourth down, I turn my gaze to Moira, who still had not drawn a second arrow from her quiver, my actions too fast, or her attention too distracted. Regardless she turned to see me, and in a moment of clarity, she raised up both hands, her left holding up her empty bow over her head as she dropped to her knees and declared, “I yield!”

My body so conditioned to follow through with the attack, had already swung back and was ready to provide the final strike, only to stop as I saw my last opponent kneeling and staring up at me with eyes full of panic and fear.

I locked gazes with her for a second, only for her to turn away in shame as she saw the look of pure and focused fury within my eyes.

For a moment, I wondered if this would be enough, then finally after what feels like an eternity, but was only a few long seconds of battle, I heard the commanding voice of Pierre call out.

“Match, Catarine Bloom’s team are the decided winners of this Team Duel,” Pierre cried out, his words sounding resolute and official. Hearing the phrase, I let my battle lust fade, as I stopped cycling energy to continually empower my body. Then I felt the weight of what I had done.

Turning back, I began walking over to see a stunned Luna and Lucia just staring at me, eyes wide with different emotions. Lucia looked like she was in awe, while Luna, well I couldn’t exactly place how her emotions seemed to make her.

Seeing the look of ambiguous fear, contemplation, and others racing across Luna’s face, I felt panic. Had I frightened her? Had I somehow made her fear me? For a moment I thought the worst thing in the world had happened and I had completely ruined any chance at a normal friendship with Luna. Then as if realizing things had gone too well for too long for me, the other shoe dropped almost immediately.

“She cheated! She crossed the line before you declared a start to the match!” Herra cried out, trying to point at me, despite her fingers still undergoing the long and painful process of self-healing.

Hearing her words, I knew this would not be an easy night. Next time, I should take the rest day, when I get one.
