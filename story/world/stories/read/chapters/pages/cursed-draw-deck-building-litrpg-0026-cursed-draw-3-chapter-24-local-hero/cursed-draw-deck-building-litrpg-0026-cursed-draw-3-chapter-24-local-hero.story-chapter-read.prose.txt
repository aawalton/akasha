Darius was breathing hard to the point that he would have been embarrassed if his family were here to see it. His father had always made a point about controlled breathing, and ‘not panting like a dog.’ Darius was hardly the black sheep of his family, but when it came to looking a certain way or maintaining self-control he definitely cared about it a lot less than his parents or younger siblings.

There was logic behind what his father insisted upon, and Darius would never disrespect him by suggesting the older hunter had a form-over-function kind of attitude. It was just that the eldest son of the Knots family had his own philosophy that lent more towards embracing the wilder side of his profession. Of course, he hadn’t completely abandoned what he’d been taught and never would, and that usually extended to keeping control of his breathing. Today, however, the big game hunter had simply exerted himself beyond his ability to maintain the proper form for inhaling and exhaling. He wasn’t alone in having his mouth hang open as he panted. Across the other side of the circular Dealer-generated arena, his opponent was equally exhausted.

Darius had spent a lot of time thinking about this particular match-up after seeing it on his schedule, and yet he still hadn’t expected it to turn into an endurance contest. The man whom Darius had found himself standing across from was Captain Korshei of the Skalean Militia. He was the tall blonde man that had acted as the Guarantor for Darius’s group at the somewhat surreal trial that had taken place after the three of them had fallen into the dragon’s tomb with that tour guide.

Having seen the captain fight a match already, Darius had known this was a dangerous opponent, but he’d assumed either he would overcome the strange nature of Korshei’s Deck and get the win, or he wouldn’t be able to and he get easily defeated. Either way, it had seemed to the young man that the match would be a short one. Oh, how wrong he had been.

Captain Korshei’s Cards and equipment had been just as powerful and unusual as Darius had predicted. Darius had never actually fought against a Resonance launcher before, but it wasn’t exactly a difficult weapon to understand. It was a tube that launched miniature harpoons at you. In the case of Korshei, they were even smaller than those Darius had seen at the Deep Hunter's League back on the Silver Sea. The projectiles flew through the air with both speed and power, yet the hunter would have been able to deal with them easily enough if it weren’t for the Deck that went along with the launcher. It was called The Petty Tyrant, and it was the only Trials Deck Darius had ever seen that made use of Authority Resonance.

Honestly, he wasn’t sure how a Trials Deck could even make use of Authority Resonance, since Trials Decks were all about self-enhancement, and Authority Cards seemed to affect their targets rather than do anything at all to the person playing them.

The match had begun with furious use of Cards on both their parts as Darius blitzed forward to get in spear range while the lanky militia captain did his best to stay well out of it. Darius’s opening Hand had consisted of ‘Dawn shot, Display The Trophies, Primal Forge, and Brood Queen’s Venom.’  Even as he sprinted to cross the thirty-foot arena of blue energy, Darius activated Dawn Shot.

​

Usually, Darius would follow up Dawn Shot by throwing his spear at the enemy, today though the hunter could not afford to risk disarming himself. Instead, he ripped a five-inch knife from his belt and sent it spinning through the air with a flick of his elbow. The thrown knife wasn’t going to end the fight, regardless of the empowerment from Dawn Shot. Darius simply hoped dealing with the ranged attack would buy him enough time to close the gap before Korshei could make use of one of his weird Authority Cards.

Things initially seemed to be going perfectly when the captain tried to duck out of the way of Darius’s thrown blade, only to have the projectile curve impossibly through the air to still intercept the man. The knife struck him just below the collarbone. The blade lodged deeply and was enough to elicit a wince from Korshei, but not enough to stop him playing a Card of his own and firing the Resonance launcher with intense accuracy.

“Command Pace: South!”

The Card wasn’t some kind of perfect mind or body control, so while Darius could still make a last-second effort to defend himself, he was compelled to turn to the south and take a single step. It not only stopped his  momentum in its tracks had Darius not been wearing his Frostdragon Scale cloak, it may very well have ended the match then and there.

Unable to stop himself from spinning about and taking that single step. It left him facing away from Korshei, who had perfectly timed his shot from his Resonance launcher to coincide with the Authority Card’s activation on Darius.  The miniature harpoon had struck the hunter in the lower back with enough force that it would have punched through Darius’s leather armor far too easily. The dragonscale cloak, on the other hand, was almost impossible to puncture. The harpoon still hit him hard enough to send Darius stumbling and gasping in pain.

It wasn’t enough to stop Darius from immediately bursting back into motion as soon as he had control of his legs again. He might not understand how the Authority Card had worked, but it seemed obvious that the best defense was going to be applying pressure to Korshei.

With a pair of movements so smooth they almost seemed simultaneous, the militia captain pulled the knife out of his upper chest, discarded it, and reloaded his Resonance launcher from one of the many small harpoons he had on a bandolier across the unwounded side of his chest.

“Command Pace: North!”

​

Darius stopped dead and spun to take the step to the north he was being compelled to take. Rather than try and fight against the compulsion, the hunter leaned into the command, as the moment he took that step, he regained full control. Korshei obviously had a lot of practice timing the shots from his launcher so that he would be firing directly at where his target was about to be. It was a danger and a puzzle everyone who had faced the captain up till this point in the tournament had failed to overcome.

Darius couldn’t blame the string of defeated foes Korshei had left in his wake. There simply was no way to get accustomed to an enemy who knew exactly where you were going to be because he got to pick that location. That being said, the hunter didn’t need to feel accustomed to an opponent to defeat them; he only had to make the right decisions regardless of how comfortable he felt.

“Primal Forge: Sword and Shield!” Declared Darius, knowing that he wouldn’t be able to get out of the way of the harpoon that accompanied Command Pace: North in time.

​

Darius’s spear split into two liquid halves, one in each of his hands that quickly reformed into their temporary existences. In his left hand formed a roughly circular shield seemingly made from a tortoise shell. His right now bore what Primal Forge considered a sword. Darius was less generous with his description of the weapon; in his mind, it was less like a sword and more like a sharp club. The weapon was a fairly flat piece of wood with a dozen jagged teeth of bone and rock protruding down its front to act as a ‘blade’.

Able to protect himself with both shield and cloak, Darius avoided significant damage even when subjected to the manipulations of Authority Cards. It led to two minutes of brutal fighting as the hunter did his best to corral the captain. He didn’t manage to actually keep Korshei locked down in one place, but staying mobile cost the taller man several chunks of flesh torn off by Darius’s sword.

Even with his increased defenses, Darius didn’t have it all his own way. The militia captain was a fine shot and continued to create opportunities for himself to land shots with his Command Pace Cards.

Korshei had never actually introduced his rank, which, while not against the rules, felt a little rude. As such, Darius didn’t know for sure how many Vials he was up against, but judging from the speed and strength of the other man, they were of the same or similar ranks.

By the time Darius’s spear returned to normal, his tortoiseshell shield was punched through by five separate harpoons, and both men had burned through around half their Vials just to keep themselves from bleeding out.

Darius had redrawn after having played Dawn Shot and Primal Forge, leaving him with ‘Scent The Blood, Bonetooth Necklace, Display The Trophies, and Brood Queen's Venom.’

They weren’t useless Cards exactly, but in a duel on flat ground inside the confines of a Challenge Arena, they weren’t what Darius had hoped to draw either. Scent The Blood in particular had the chance to prove useful in the match, but unless Darius knew for sure his opponent was running low on Vials, it was potentially a waste of a Vial he could be using to keep himself healthy. Something that was a major factor when so much of this fight was about trying to close the distance whilst under attack at long range.

Darius would have loved to banter a little with his opponent if he only had the time. Instead, as things stood, the pair of combatants were far too busy trying to impose the range they wanted while doing their best to rip the other man to shreds.

That was how he had ended up gasping for air with steam rising from his wounds across the arena from an opponent who was in much the same condition. The fight had gone for a total of seven minutes and had left an awful lot of both men’s blood on the arena floor. In some ways, Darius had enjoyed the fight, though in the sort of way that he never hoped to repeat ever again as long as he lived.

In the end, an exhausted Darius had managed to seize victory by kicking one of the miniature harpoons from the floor of the arena up and at Korshei with enough force to throw the man’s aim off. He closed the distance between them one final time as he did it, and finally ran the man through with his Ancient spear.

Looking around at the crowd that was most definitely booing him, Darius winced.

“Please don’t die on me.” He said between heavy breaths as the arena around him collapsed.
