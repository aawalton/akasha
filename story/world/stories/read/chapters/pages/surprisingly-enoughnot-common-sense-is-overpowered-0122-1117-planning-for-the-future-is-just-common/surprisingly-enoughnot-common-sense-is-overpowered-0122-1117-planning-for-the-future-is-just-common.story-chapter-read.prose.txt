Fu Xuan chose an open training field some distance behind her residence for the demonstration. Old trees and medicinal gardens surrounded the field, and several formations lay beneath the ground to contain any damage from training or experiments.

Fu Xuan stopped near the edge of the field and gestured ahead. "Go ahead. Use your strongest attack."

Ning nodded and walked forward.

If she wanted to understand the Infinite Circle properly, there was little point in demonstrating it with an ordinary Fireball Technique, and there was only one attack Ning had ever created that truly pushed the technique to its limit.

He reached into his storage pouch and took out his bow.

The last time Ning had used it seriously, the weapon had been thrashed in the Heavenly Origin Tower. Ling Yinran had sent it for repairs afterward, but naturally, his senior sister hadn't stopped at simply fixing it.

The original limbs had been reinforced with a layer of dark ironwood, increasing the draw weight far beyond anything an ordinary person could handle, while the old string had been replaced with braided tendon from a Peak First-Tier spiritual beast. Several thin silver inscriptions now ran beneath the surface of the limbs as well, so subtle that they were almost invisible until qi flowed through them.

There were four of them.

The first reinforced the limbs and distributed physical strain across the entire bow rather than allowing it to gather around the center. The second helped conduct qi from his hands into the arrow, reducing the amount that leaked into the bow itself. The third was a simple stabilization inscription meant to suppress the vibrations produced when an excessive amount of spiritual energy was released at once.

The fourth was much simpler, but Ning appreciated it the most.

Normally, a bow this powerful couldn't be left strung indefinitely without slowly warping the limbs and weakening the string. It had to be strung before use and unstrung afterward, which was hardly convenient in a fight. The fourth inscription solved that problem.

Ning fed a thread of qi into the bow.

With a soft click, the tendon string slipped free from the small groove where it rested along the lower limb. The inscription flashed, the limbs bent inward on their own, and the string snapped neatly into place between the two tips.

Ning gave it an experimental pull, feeling the tension in the string.

Senior Sister really understands my needs.

He looked down at the reinforced limbs and smiled slightly.

LOVE 2.0 was a considerable improvement over the original.

Behind him, Ling Xin's eyes immediately brightened when she recognized what he was preparing.

"Oh! He's going to use LOVE."

Fu Xuan turned toward her. "Love?"

"Yes." Ling Xin pointed toward Ning with complete seriousness. "That's what he named it. Whenever he shoots someone with an arrow, he's shooting them with LOVE."

She lasted approximately two seconds before laughing.

"Isn't that funny?"

Fu Xuan stared at her disciple for a moment, then slowly turned toward Ning.

Looking between the two of them, she found herself wondering, perhaps for the first time in many years, whether there had been some previously undiscovered flaw in her teaching methods.

Whatever it was, apparently it had already spread to the next generation.

Completely unaware that the well-behaved and sensible disciple impression he had once wanted to maintain had fallen apart yet again, Ning selected one of his better arrows and nocked it.

Lightning gathered first.

The Lightning Cry Arrow Technique circulated through Ning's meridians, and blue arcs crawled over the arrowhead before spreading down the shaft. Ning fed qi through his fingers at the same time, causing the silver inscriptions hidden within the bow to awaken one after another as they carried the excess spiritual energy toward the arrow.

He kept the bow lowered.

Well, that was the easy part.

Ning drew out the cold qi produced by Jade Skin and Icy Meridians and guided it toward the arrow.

This was something he had been experimenting with ever since the Heavenly Origin Tower.

During the battle against Lei Shen, he had somehow managed to put lightning, crimson flame, and ice onto the same arrow without having the entire technique tear itself apart. However, much of that had only been possible because of the Tower Spirit's assistance, especially when it came to preventing the different energies from conflicting with one another.

So, naturally, Ning had gotten to work.

After all, having experienced the terror of a tri attack once, how could he simply give it up?

Of course, he hadn't managed to recreate it completely. But he had made some progress, and awakening spiritual sense had improved his control considerably.

The main problem was maintaining the cold qi outside his body while simultaneously controlling Lightning Cry Arrow.

That was where the Worm Overlord's energy came in.

The strange energy had already adapted closely to Ning's body, and when guided alongside the cold qi produced by Jade Skin and Icy Meridians, it helped the cold qi maintain its form after leaving his meridians. It couldn't resolve the conflict between ice and lightning like the Tower Spirit had, but it gave Ning enough control to work around the problem himself.

The pale-blue qi emerged and began gathering around the lightning.

Ning circulated Spiral Arrow.

Rather than allowing the two energies to touch directly, he set the cold qi rotating around the central bolt of lightning. As the rotation accelerated, the two formed separate layers, divided by a narrow gap that constantly shifted as Ning corrected their circulation.

The arrow trembled briefly before settling.

Ning watched the structure through his spiritual sense, paying particular attention to the boundary between the two energies.

It was nowhere near as effortless as the Tower Spirit's golden seal had made it look.

But this time, Ning was the one holding everything together. So, it was progress, nonetheless.

Fu Xuan's eyes narrowed slightly. "Interesting. You said Spiral Arrow was something your disciple created himself?"

Ling Xin nodded. "Yes. Though he says it was just something that came naturally with his qi control."

"That makes sense. He's using its rotation to keep the two energies separated while maintaining them within the same attack."

"I helped with that, you know," Ling Xin said proudly.

Fu Xuan understood what she meant. Her disciple possessed a Pure Fire Spiritual Body. The qi Ling Xin produced naturally carried a trace of special fire qi, greatly enhancing her fire-attributed techniques while making techniques of opposing attributes, especially water, considerably more troublesome for her.

"Mostly it was the timing," Ling Xin explained. "Which one to establish first, when to bring in the second, and how quickly to feed it in."

"Well, it seems you did your job as a master, at least." Fu Xuan smiled.

Meanwhile, Ning was ready to launch the attack.

[Infinite Circle: 91/100]

After his advancement, completely filling the Infinite Circle took approximately twenty days.

This was the highest his accumulation had ever reached.

Ning began drawing upon the accumulated qi.

The silver inscriptions across the bow flared.

The stabilization inscription activated first, suppressing the violent vibrations running through the limbs, while the reinforcement inscription spread the rapidly increasing strain across the entire weapon.

Even with both inscriptions active, the bow creaked.

Ning raised it and began to draw.

The spiritual-beast tendon resisted before slowly yielding beneath his strength. His shoulders tightened, qi reinforcing muscle and bone as the dark ironwood limbs bent farther and farther back.

Half draw.

Purple-white lightning screamed along the arrow.

Three-quarter draw.

The rotating ice qi became a pale blur around it, freezing moisture from the surrounding air.

Full draw.

The ground beneath Ning's feet cracked.

The bow bent into a deep arc, every silver inscription shining as the weapon struggled to contain the combination of physical tension and spiritual energy running through it.

Then Ning released.

THRUM!

For a mundane archer, the energy stored within the bent limbs would have been almost everything behind the shot.

For Ning, it was merely the first push.

The bow launched the arrow, Lightning Cry Arrow accelerated it further, and then the enormous quantity of qi supplied by the Infinite Circle drove the entire technique forward.

The projectile disappeared.

A purple-white streak crossed the training field, wrapped in a pale spiral of frost.

Then it struck.

BOOOOOOM!

The target vanished beneath an explosion of lightning and shattered earth.

The central bolt punched into the ground first, branching arcs tearing outward through the soil before the rotating ice qi collapsed around the impact point. Frost exploded across the surrounding earth in a rapidly expanding ring, the Worm Overlord's energy mixed throughout the discharge, and finally, the enormous quantity of qi forced into the attack broke free.

The shockwave followed.

The formations surrounding the field activated almost simultaneously.

Lines buried beneath the earth blazed to life, throwing up translucent barriers just as the remaining force reached the edge of the training ground. The nearest barrier bowed visibly beneath the impact before slowly returning to shape.

For a moment, there was silence.

As Ning's robes settled from the shockwave, he turned around.

"So, how was it, Grandmaster?"

"That attack..." Fu Xuan paused, unable to completely hide her surprise. "Is that really something a Seventh-Stage cultivator should be capable of? It seems I underestimated it."

It was fortunate she had reinforced the formations around the field beforehand.

"Hehe. I didn't know Master could make that face." Ling Xin grinned.

She was also surprised by the sheer power behind the attack, but recovered considerably faster. With her spiritual body, producing attacks beyond what should normally have been possible at the Seventh Stage wasn't exactly unfamiliar to her.

Fu Xuan extended her spiritual sense across the ruined field, examining the sheer impact of the technique.

"This power..." She considered it for a moment. "It's comparable to the full-power attack of a cultivator approaching the peak of late-stage Qi Condensation."

Ning blinked.

That was somewhat better than he had expected.

Fu Xuan, however, was considering something else entirely.

Unlike Ling Xin's spiritual body, which granted her explosive power beyond what her realm would normally allow, the Infinite Circle had even greater potential for growth. Its limit seemed to depend largely on Ning himself, and as his body, dantian, and control improved, so too would the amount of power he could accumulate and release.

Perhaps, eventually, he might even be capable of threatening an established Foundation Building cultivator.

Of course, that would depend heavily on the opponent and whether they were foolish enough to stand still while Ning prepared this ridiculous attack.

Yet the possibility existed.

Fu Xuan looked at the crater again.

Perhaps she had been too quick in her judgment of Ning earlier.

He wasn't a genius in the same way Ling Xin was. His talent for cultivation couldn't compare to his master's, but Fu Xuan was beginning to see that the way his mind worked wasn't so different.

Talent wasn't always about learning faster or cultivating quicker. Sometimes it showed in what a person thought to do with what they had learned. Where others followed an established path from one step to the next, geniuses were often the ones who wondered what would happen if they went somewhere else entirely.

Ning certainly seemed to have that tendency.

More than that, what struck Fu Xuan most was his curiosity.

During their alchemy lessons, he hadn't simply accepted everything she told him. He asked why, proposed different methods, and occasionally even questioned whether some of her explanations applied under different circumstances. He remained perfectly respectful throughout it all, but Fu Xuan could tell that simply receiving an answer was rarely enough for him.

He wanted to understand it, test it, and see what else could be done with it.

Perhaps the Infinite Circle itself was the clearest example.

Most cultivators who created a unique technique like this would guard it closely as a trump card. Ning had instead explained its principles to his master and was now demonstrating it openly before Fu Xuan.

Simply put, his desire to improve the technique outweighed his desire to hide it.

Of course, that kind of curiosity wasn't always a good thing.

Fu Xuan's gaze shifted toward the master-disciple pair.

"That was around eight out of ten," Ling Xin declared, her eyes sparkling. "It needs more fire. More bam! How about adding an Explosion Pill? A modified one, of course."

She pointed enthusiastically toward Ning's arrows. "Attach one near the arrowhead. No, several smaller ones might be better. Then they could detonate after impact. Your arrow already goes boom, so if we add an alchemical explosion afterward, it'll go boom twice."

Ning seriously considered it. "That could work, but we'd need to make sure the pill doesn't destabilize while I'm charging the arrow. The lightning alone might trigger certain formulas."

"How about a layered shell?"

"An insulation inscription would probably be safer. A shell alone might not be enough."

"Yes, that's true." Ling Xin nodded thoughtfully.

"Before the two of you accidentally invent something that destroys my field completely, there are several problems with what you just demonstrated."

Ning immediately abandoned the Explosion Pill discussion and turned toward her.

Fu Xuan walked closer. "First, the attack takes too long. Against an opponent willing to give you time, that doesn't matter. Against anyone experienced enough to recognize what you're doing, you'll be attacked long before the technique is complete. Your concealment technique can compensate for that under certain circumstances, but relying on favorable circumstances isn't a solution."

"That is true." Ning exhaled slowly, still catching his breath from the shot.

For his stealth-archer style, the preparation time wasn't as crippling as it might have been for someone else, but it was still an obvious weakness.

"Second, you're still treating the different energies as separate techniques that happen to occupy the same attack. Spiral Arrow keeps them apart, but the structure is fragile. If one layer loses its rhythm, everything becomes unstable."

Ning's expression turned thoughtful.

That was the biggest difference between what he could do now and what the Tower Spirit had accomplished. He had found a way to keep the energies from interfering with one another, but he still couldn't truly combine them.

Fu Xuan's gaze settled on him.

"Third, and more importantly, there is the Infinite Circle itself."

Ning's attention sharpened.

"You emptied the entire accumulated reserve just now, correct?"

"Yes, Grandmaster." That was all the load he had accumulated over the past several days, released at once.

"Can you control the rate of release?" Fu Xuan asked.

"It's still a work in progress," Ning admitted, then continued, "but I've thought of a few possibilities that might help."

Ning briefly explained the three methods he considered most promising. He could construct a formation-like seal to regulate the output, divert part of the accumulated qi into a smaller secondary circulation, or deliberately alter the Circle's stability until it naturally shed a controlled amount of excess qi.

Fu Xuan nodded. "Not bad. All three are worth exploring."

She thought for a moment before continuing. "There are other methods as well. You could divide the Circle itself into independent layers, establish several smaller circulations around the primary one, or create opposing currents that separate a portion of the qi through differences in rotational force. With sufficient control, you could even alter the circulation route during release so that the Circle divides and reconnects without ever truly stopping."

Ning listened carefully.

Each method sounded increasingly ridiculous, though they were not without merit.

"However, don't try any of those yet," Fu Xuan added. "Your current body and qi control aren't sufficient. Inside the Heavenly Origin Tower, you could afford to fail hundreds of times until you found the exact balance. Outside it, you only need to make one mistake in your dantian."

Fu Xuan continued. "Wait until your body cultivation has advanced further and your control over qi has become perfect."

"That's what I thought as well." Ning nodded.

Naturally, he also understood this. That was why he wasn't going all-out experimenting with the Infinite Circle.

Now that he thought about it, there were too many things he still needed to experiment with regarding the Infinite Circle, not only the automated cultivation but also this.

Fu Xuan took out a jade talisman and passed it to him. "I recorded what should be useful to you. There are also some things unrelated to this particular problem, but they should help you develop the Infinite Circle in the future."

Ning sent his spiritual sense inside and immediately understood what she meant.

The talisman contained information on qi circulation, pressure and flow, methods of dividing and reconnecting currents, several formation principles that could be adapted to the human body, and references to techniques that accumulated or continuously cycled spiritual energy. There were even notes on strengthening the dantian and meridians, along with several cultivation resources that might help him endure greater internal pressure in the future.

What surprised him most, however, were Fu Xuan's observations of the Infinite Circle itself.

She had recorded its general structure, rotational changes, points of instability, and even how the flow had shifted immediately before and during his release. Some of the proportions were remarkably close to what Ning himself had observed through Know Thyself.

Ning slowly lowered the jade talisman.

Fu Xuan had seen him use the Infinite Circle once.

How did she figure out this much from a single demonstration? Old monsters in the cultivation world sure were scary.

Of course, Ning would never voice this out loud. While his grandmaster might have the appearance of a wise old woman, it was still not his place to call her old.

Fu Xuan waited until Ning had finished examining the jade talisman before speaking again. "Controlling how much you release is important, but that isn't the problem I'm most concerned about. The Infinite Circle never stops accumulating on its own, does it?"

"No." Ning understood where she was going with this.

Fu Xuan continued, "Your current limit is twenty days. Under ordinary circumstances, that gives you plenty of time to release the accumulated qi before it becomes dangerous. But what if you're unconscious? Sealed? Poisoned? Or injured badly enough that you can't circulate qi?"

"Then the Circle would continue accumulating until it exceeded what my dantian could safely contain," Ning admitted.

This was the other flaw he had already noticed. As his cultivation advanced, his dantian strengthened, and his control improved, the amount he could safely accumulate would naturally increase. Eventually, reaching his limit might take months rather than days.

But that only reduced the likelihood of something going wrong. And with Murphy at play, chances were that there would still be some way for it to go wrong.

"That is the problem I intend to address today," Fu Xuan said. "Your master has been pestering me about it ever since she learned how the technique works."

Ling Xin immediately frowned. "Because it's dangerous."

"Even your master thinks it's dangerous, so you should know how crazy this technique is." Fu Xuan ignored Ling Xin's protest and continued. "Especially considering what happened after you left the Heavenly Origin Tower. You were unconscious for three days, then spent another seven unable to properly circulate your qi."

Ning's expression stiffened slightly.

The Infinite Circle had still been accumulating during that entire period. That was already uncomfortably close.

"At the time, your estimated safe limit was only around fifteen days," Fu Xuan continued. "If your recovery had taken several days longer, we might have discovered what happens when the Infinite Circle exceeds its limit in the least desirable way possible."

Ling Xin's frown deepened. "You could have exploded. Maybe tracking Worm really needs to be put into my plan."

"Master, I don't think that's necessary anymor—"

"You could have exploded."

Ning gave a hollow laugh.

When she put it that way, it did sound rather bad.

Fu Xuan reached into her storage pouch and placed a small jade bottle on the table. "This is the temporary measure for this problem."

Ning accepted it curiously. "A pill?"

"A Threefold Returning Origin Pill. It's a top-tier Second-Grade pill, but its use is quite niche, so it's very rare."

Fu Xuan briefly explained its effects. Unlike an ordinary medicinal pill, its essence would not be consumed immediately after entering his body. Instead, it would form three dormant medicinal seals around his dantian, each capable of activating independently when his spiritual energy became dangerously unstable.

Once triggered, a seal would temporarily reinforce his dantian, suppress the offending spiritual energy into a dormant state, and preserve as much of it as possible until he regained control.

In other words, even if Ning fell unconscious while the Infinite Circle approached its limit, the pill could forcibly stabilize it without simply dispersing everything he had accumulated.

"And it can do that three times?" Ning asked. This was simply too good.

"Under ideal circumstances, three. If the situation is particularly difficult, one activation may consume more medicinal power." Fu Xuan paused. "Don't treat that as permission to test it. From what I have seen, you share quite a few traits with your master."

"Ehehe!" Ling Xin rubbed her head. "You don't need to flatter us, Master."

"Ah, Master, I don't think that was meant as a compliment." Ning admired his master's selective hearing. She had the talent to simply focus on the things she liked. In a way, one could say her Dao heart was terrifying indeed.

Fu Xuan ignored them. "This pill was never designed for your Infinite Circle, so consider it an emergency safeguard. Once its medicinal essence is exhausted, it's gone. More importantly, suppressing something and solving its fundamental flaw are two different matters."

Ning let the words sink in.

The pill could keep him from exploding if something unexpected happened, but ultimately, the Infinite Circle still needed a way to protect itself without relying on him being conscious.

But for now, this would be a great stopgap measure.

"Swallow the pill now. I need to guide you through refining it," Fu Xuan said sternly. She then turned toward Ling Xin. "You will help me with this."

"Yes." Ling Xin nodded, then looked at Ning. "My disciple, leave it to me. I will take care of it."

Ning really wanted to be reassured by his master. He really did, but the shine in her eyes kind of scared him.

Still, at this point, all of this was for his own good.

Ning sat down in the lotus position and swallowed the pill without hesitation.

Fu Xuan placed two fingers against his back, while Ling Xin moved to sit opposite him.

"You'll maintain his meridians and dantian," Fu Xuan instructed. "Don't interfere with the medicinal essence unless I tell you to."

"I know. Even I wouldn't do that inside my dear disciple's body." Ling Xin was offended. She was perfectly well-behaved, thank you very much.

She placed her palm against Ning's chest as a cool and remarkably gentle stream of qi entered his body. Unlike Fu Xuan's qi, which immediately began manipulating the medicine with extraordinary precision, Ling Xin's spread throughout his meridians and settled around his dantian, forming a stable layer around them.

"Focus," Fu Xuan said. "I'll separate and refine the medicinal essence. As for you, follow the circulation I show you with your own qi. Don't try to help me, and don't move ahead even if you think you understand what I'm doing."

Ning had, in fact, been planning to do exactly that. "Yes, Grandmaster."

"Good. The seals need to be formed with your qi mixed into them."

Ning closed his eyes and concentrated on the flow of qi Fu Xuan was guiding through his body.

The Threefold Returning Origin Pill had begun dissolving. Dozens of medicinal properties emerged almost simultaneously, some sinking toward his meridians while others naturally gathered around the dantian. Several opposing strands began reacting the moment they came into contact.

It was at that moment that Fu Xuan's qi moved.

It divided into dozens of fine threads, separating one medicinal property from another with a degree of control that Ning could barely follow even with his spiritual sense. Some were merged, others suppressed, while several were deliberately circulated through particular meridians before being drawn back.

At the same time, Ling Xin adjusted her qi whenever Ning's meridians reacted to the medicine, keeping the pathways stable without once interfering with the medicinal essence itself.

The longer Ning followed Fu Xuan's guidance, the more familiar the whole process began to feel. In a strange way, they were performing alchemy inside his body. His meridians served as the channels through which the medicinal properties were circulated and refined, while his dantian had effectively become the furnace in which the final medicinal seals would be formed.

It was an interesting thought.

Then the framework of the Infinite Circle trembled.

The disturbance was tiny, barely more than a ripple in its circulation, but Fu Xuan noticed it immediately.

"Ling Xin."

"I've got it."

The qi surrounding Ning's dantian tightened. Ling Xin stabilized the area surrounding his dantian and isolated the disturbance before it could affect the Circle's rotation.

Fu Xuan never stopped working.

Ning also realized there was another purpose behind his demonstration.

Even nearly empty, the Circle was sensitive enough to react when such potent medicinal power approached it. If several days' worth of strengthened qi had still been rotating inside, refining the pill around his dantian would have been much more troublesome.

Under Fu Xuan's guidance, Ning's own qi gradually joined the process. She led him through each circulation while handling the transformations that exceeded his current control, until the chaotic medicinal essence had been completely refined into three distinct portions.

"Now the first."

The first portion condensed around his dantian.

Dozens of medicinal strands intertwined under Fu Xuan's control, gradually forming a complex pale seal. Ling Xin kept Ning's dantian perfectly stable throughout the process while Ning followed Fu Xuan's circulation and supplied his own qi to complete the final connection.

Similarly, the second and third seals were successfully placed without further trouble.

Nearly half an hour later, Fu Xuan withdrew her fingers.

Ling Xin waited several breaths longer before removing her hand as well.

Ning examined himself through Know Thyself.

Three pale medicinal seals now rested around his dantian, completely dormant. The Infinite Circle rotated normally between them, while the enormous medicinal power contained within the seals showed no sign of interfering with his cultivation.

Only then did Ning open his eyes and slowly let out a breath of turbid air.

Temporary or not, it felt good to have one less problem hanging over his head.

"You should thank your master for that pill," Fu Xuan said.

Ning turned toward Ling Xin. "Thank you, Master."

She waved dismissively. "It's fine. You're my disciple. This is nothing."

Ning looked at her for a moment. The fact that she was brushing it off so casually only made him more curious.

"How much did it cost?"

"It doesn't matter," Ling Xin said immediately. "You're my disciple."

That answer told Ning enough.

He shook his head. "A debt is still a debt. I'll find a way to repay Master eventually."

"You can start now," Fu Xuan said.

Ning looked at her suspiciously.

Right now?

He quickly went through everything he possessed, but couldn't think of anything that would be particularly useful to Ling Xin.

Fu Xuan seemed to know what he was thinking.

"Not with spirit stones." She continued. "As Ling Xin approaches the Purple Mansion realm, there are certain breakthrough resources within the sect that would be particularly suitable for her. Her talent and achievements are already exceptional, but she isn't the only genius in the sect."

Ning immediately began paying closer attention.

"Normally, a disciple's accomplishments have little bearing on their master's cultivation. But when several candidates are competing for the same limited resources, everything becomes relevant. The quality of one's disciples reflects upon the master who taught them."

Fu Xuan glanced at Ling Xin. "And your master has never bothered cultivating a faction of her own. Her relationships with the other alchemists are hardly worth mentioning either."

"They just don't have that spark, you know," Ling Xin tried to justify herself.

"That attitude is precisely why I'm explaining this to your disciple instead of you."

Ning understood what was going on.

Fu Xuan was quietly paving the road ahead for Ling Xin.

Ling Xin, however, didn't seem particularly concerned.

"Disciple, just do your best and do what you want." She gave Ning a reassuring pat on the shoulder. "Your master won't be stopped by a hurdle like this."

"I know you're carefree," Fu Xuan said, sounding thoroughly accustomed to her, "but you should at least think further ahead. Not only for yourself."

Ling Xin raised an eyebrow.

"You still have your niece to consider. Her talent isn't as exceptional as yours, and you know that better than anyone." Fu Xuan's expression grew more serious. "Especially after the promise you made to her mother."

Ling Xin fell silent.

The carefree expression on her face faded.

After a few moments, she sighed. "I know. I'll take it seriously."

Ning glanced between them. Senior Sister Yinran's mother?

He filed the question away for later before nodding.

"Grandmaster, don't worry. I'll do my best. It's the least I can do." He smiled slightly. "That's what being master and disciple is about, isn't it?"

Thinking about it, Ning felt he had been rather fortunate with his master.

In cliché cultivation stories, sects often seemed like remarkably poor places to learn anything. Elders were constantly suppressing talented disciples, senior brothers and sisters were picking fights over minor grievances, and masters had a habit of disappearing whenever their disciples actually needed them.

Of course, that was usually because the story followed the protagonist.

For most cultivators, entering a proper sect was probably one of the best opportunities they would ever receive.

Techniques, resources, protection, guidance, connections—these were things independent cultivators had to fight for themselves.

Ling Xin had done more than that.

If his achievements could make her road a little easier in return, Ning saw no reason not to try.

A master taught and supported the disciple. Once the disciple became capable enough, naturally, he should be able to support his master as well.

"That's good to hear." Fu Xuan smiled, her expression softening slightly.

She then added, "As for your cultivation, I won't tell you to stop learning broadly. It may suit the Infinite Circle better than I initially thought. Just remember that your time and attention are limited. Learn and experiment as you wish, but don't collect techniques simply because they interest you. Think about how each one fits into the path you're building."

"I'll keep that in mind, Grandmaster."

...

On the way back, Ning continued thinking about Fu Xuan's advice.

His grandmaster was right. He couldn't keep spreading his attention everywhere. It was time to min-max a little.

The alchemy competition came first, so for the next three months, alchemy would take priority. His Scorching Purge was already quite close to advancing, so mastering it before the competition would be a nice bonus. He also needed to find more spiritual liquid for Pure Eyes to complete the Clear Sight stage.

Ning was still arranging his plans when Ling Xin suddenly stopped.

"Anyway, Disciple, you go on ahead. I just remembered I have something to do."

Ning glanced at her before nodding. "Alright. Take care, Master."

Ling Xin waved and disappeared in another direction.

Ning sighed lightly and continued walking.

His schedule for the foreseeable future was actually quite clear. First came the alchemy competition, then the yearly courtyard battle. Sometime within the next two years, there would also be the Spirit Tempering Pond opportunity, followed eventually by the Secret Realm.

It was quite eventful, if he said so himself.

Still, considering everything he had gained recently, reaching the Ninth Stage before the Secret Realm no longer seemed impossible.

More importantly, he needed to be properly prepared for the Spirit Tempering Pond. Of all the opportunities ahead of him, that was probably the one he couldn't afford to waste if he wanted to increase his cultivation rapidly.

With that thought, Ning changed direction and headed toward the Hundred Whisper Pavilion.

It didn't take him long to find Tang Jin.

"I want to commission you to find a technique for me."
