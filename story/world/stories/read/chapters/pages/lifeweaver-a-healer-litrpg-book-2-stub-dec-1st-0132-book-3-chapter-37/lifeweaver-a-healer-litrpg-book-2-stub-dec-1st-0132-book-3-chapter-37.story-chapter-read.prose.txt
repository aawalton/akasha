After a brief round of introductions right there on the platform, where each person gave their name, class, and level, Milla got going.

"We're all gathered here in New York because it is far more intense than almost any other city in the world. This will give us everything we need to grow stronger and position ourselves as one of the top US guilds!"

"Top of the World, baby!" Nash shouted.

"Er, yeah," Milla said, stumbling over her words a little before finding her stride again after the interruption. "Before the guild can even get going, we're going to need a guild stone. It is a costly item, but you've all been kind enough to pitch in, which means we're almost there!"

Luke plucked the stone he'd demanded as payment from Peter and handed it over to Milla, who gave him a short, exasperated look before turning back to the small crowd. "Guess we have a stone!"

A cheer rose, but Milla only let it go on for a few seconds before raising her hands and gesturing for them all to quiet down. "That means we can put all those credits to good use elsewhere. A guild headquarters!"

While Nash led the group in another cheer, Milla leaned over to Luke and whispered, "Thank God. I was not looking forward to roughing it and squatting in some abandoned apartment building."

"You can buy a Shelter for a whole guild?" Luke asked.

She nodded. "Of course. It's expensive as hell, but I think it'll be worth it."

"Your call, guildmistress," Luke said, giving her a wink.

Milla raised her arms again. "We'll find a nice, central location to start. Then, once everyone has a room instance, I'll be handing out communication gems. You need to have that with you at all times so I can reach you and direct you to a party and portal."

This went on for a little while longer. Milla explained her idea of how to structure the guild into semi-set parties where you learned to work as a team, while some of the more experienced members floated between parties to lend a hand. Some members wouldn't be fighting at all, but would hold administrative positions and help Milla, who would keep everything together and at least somewhat structured. "Now let's get going! Where is my beautiful girlfriend?"

A girl off to the side gave a little wave, looking uncomfortable with the idea of getting the attention of everyone in the crowd. There was something about her that made Luke sure the pretty, girl-next-door kind of girl with shoulder-length light brown hair was just his sister's type. Perhaps something to do with her body-language? No, he realized, Lana just looked a lot like the kind of guys Milla dated in her teens. Interesting. Perhaps there was something there he could use to tease his sister later. Grinning, Luke decided to give that some serious thought.

It took them the better part of the day to find a good spot for their guild hall living quarters and get everyone situated. To Luke's surprise, their new home didn't form a structure right in the open. Instead, it was a sort of metal plaque you put on a wall. When you touched it, you were transported into a decent-sized entrance hall, kind of like the lobby of ISG headquarters, with more plaques leading to different rooms, some of which were personal sleeping chambers. All rooms were bare for the moment, except for beds, but Milla described how she envisioned the place looking once they had the funds to spare for furniture and the like.

"What if someone breaks the plaque out on the street?" Luke asked Milla.

"They won't," she answered. "They're indestructible. Now come here, and I'll show you something neat."

The entrance hall wasn't bare, after all. A table stood right in the middle, where someone had spread out a cloth map of the city. A few points on it glowed.

When everyone had gathered around, Milla explained. "Touching mana to any of these points will transport you to that location. It's sort of like a teleporter that only we in the guild can use."

"Sounds expensive," Ray said.

Milla gave him a quick smile that showed she had indeed not forgotten about his antics when he and Luke were younger. "It was. Don't break it."

Nash nodded with his usual level of enthusiasm, a lot. "What about the guild name? How about 'The Band'? Great, isn't it?"

"We'll get to that in a moment," Milla said.

"How does this work?" Hannah asked, her eyes glowing with interest. "Why are they all subway stations?"

"It's a collaboration with other Integrated, as well as the DIA. Teleport destination plaques are almost as expensive as this house we're in, but you can share them with others. People are banding together."

Hannah touched a finger against the spot nearest the guild headquarters and spoke in a reverent whisper. "Cool."

With that, she was gone, disappearing into thin air.

Milla gave Luke a questioning look, and he could do nothing but shrug. "She likes to travel."

"Surrounded by weirdos," Milla said as she withdrew the guild stone, raising it into the air so everyone could see. "It's time for the main event! Let's create a guild!"

After pushing through the throng of people crowding in, she hurried over to a corner. A moment later, she looked up at everyone. "We need a name."

Luke didn't care what they named the damn guild, and the amount of time it took for the decision made his skin crawl. Sitting still was such a foreign thing to him after running around like a maniac all day, every day, and most nights, since he touched the gray orb and became Integrated. He wished he were in a dungeon fighting, leveling, or even training his enchanting more, but it was important for him to be present in this moment and support his sister. Starting the guild was her idea after all, even if it was he who'd got the ball rolling. How his sister had afforded all this stuff for the guild was a mystery, or was the guild stone that expensive? No, even with the money she'd gathered from everyone, it wouldn't be enough. She had to have some sort of secondary source of funds. Curiosity piqued, he decided to ask her about it later when she wasn't standing in front of a group of people shouting guild names at her.

There was one thing he could do, though, even while standing around. Extending Threads of Mana, practicing to increase their reach. Wearing the robe, it was simple enough to wrap the thread around his own body under the thick fabric, going around and around, rather than pushing it forward.

Elijah seemed to have the same idea. That light of his kept moving around his skin, or perhaps even below the skin. Underneath his clothes, it was difficult to tell, but it popped up around his neck a few times. More people were doing much the same, with Tiffany extending an aura through the room. The name suggested she used something to wash away everyone's fatigue, but it was difficult to tell if it made any difference. Savannah kept forming and then dissipating armor pieces around herself, as well as a small claw-like thing that she used to scratch her hair. As time went on, more and more people caught on and joined in the practice, pushing themselves to improve. A feeling of pride came over Luke. He was proud of them. This group of Integrated had come from all over, deciding to band together and fight whatever was happening in this ghost town of a city.

"Enough!" Milla shouted, startling everyone. "Enough. We have enough suggestions. Since we can't agree on anything, we'll do it another way. Once you've accepted the invitation to join, you can add your suggestion to a list and then cast your vote."

The invitation appeared before Luke's eyes that instant, and he accepted it without hesitation. Just like she'd said, a list of suggested names appeared, and he had to cast his vote:

The list, like so many things in life, went on and on. Judging by the number of entries, almost every single person in the room must've added an option. Some were obvious jokes, while others were far too try-hard for Luke's liking. In the end, he picked one he felt he could live with, one sort of placed in the middle on the scale between stupid joke and way-too-into-role-playing-games: Radiant Dawn. Yes, it was a little much, but there were worse options in there. A lot worse.

Having made his choice, Luke waited for everyone else. It didn't take long until one option came out on top, far higher than all others. 'The Band' won with five votes. The most any other option received was two.

"Hell yeah!" Nash shouted. "Let's go!"

Luke couldn't help but burst out laughing. Of course, that was the winning option. With no other groups being as large as Nash's party from ISG, it meant that their combined vote was more than anyone else could hope to match, with so many different opinions going around. Still laughing, he raised a hand and mimicked Nash's metal-horns. "The Band!" he shouted.

A chorus of voices rose into the air. "The Band! The Band!"
