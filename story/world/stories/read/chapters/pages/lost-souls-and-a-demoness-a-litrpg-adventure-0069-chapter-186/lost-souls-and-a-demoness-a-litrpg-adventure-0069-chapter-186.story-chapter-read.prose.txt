For the second time in her life, Jade breathed in a stream of radiant fire. Ira's association with demonkind had done nothing to dim the heat of her celestial heart, and all that remained of her brilliant essence flowed from her dying body straight into Jade's spirit. It seared through her body like molten rock, erupting against the opposing darkness that suffused her demonic body. Jade squeezed her eyes shut against the pain, clenching her fists and digging her claws into the hard ground. It was just like it had been with Seraphine. An overwhelming brilliance flowing into her body, burning its way through to her spirit. But this time, she didn't black out. Jade's spirit already held a kernel of radiance, and it welcomed this new power in a way her body could not. Perhaps if she'd drained more of Ira's life force, she wouldn't have been so fortunate, but the angel's spirit was all but spent. Jade had only inhaled a tiny fraction of her power when Ira's breath faltered, and then stilled.

The flow of blazing starlight petered out, and Jade jerked back, eyes wide and gasping for breath. Her body practically vibrated with power, and her skin was covered with blotchy red burns that stung to the touch. Despite the pain, she felt stronger. While the essence she had just absorbed didn't mingle well with her nature, it still contained a substantial amount of life force. Ira's energy had healed her, albeit poorly, and the worst of her injuries had eased. Jade drew in a tentative breath, finding the stabbing pain in her chest was gone. Her ribs still ached, but the sensation was dull, and moving didn't seem to aggravate them.

"Thanks…" Jade muttered, casting one last look at the dead angel lying beside her. Whatever her role in this had been, Ira had given Jade enough strength for one final push. The battle continued nearby, but her friends were flagging. It was time to end this.

Jade surged to her feet. Or rather, that was her intention. It ended up being more of an awkward stumble, but she made it up. Her spirit buzzed with power, draining Ira had even partially refilled her mana supply, but her body remained at its limit. An infusion of celestial essence was no miracle cure, and that was doubly true for a succubus. But pain or no, she was back on her feet. It would have to do.

"Aaghh!"

Luis let out a cry of pain just as Jade started back towards the fight. Vastrozun had finally landed a clean hit, smashing through his guard and slashing Luis across the chest with his massive greatsword. Luis staggered back, his flaming blade scoring a groove into the flagstones as he dragged it with him through the spray of blood spattering the stones underfoot.

"Luis!" Naomi's music faltered for a moment, but the bard recovered. She switched to a healing melody, her frantic notes at odds with the soothing balm of the magic.

The demon lord made to follow Luis, but Fenrin leapt in to intercept him. Siora rushed in as well, dredging up the last vestiges of her power and pressing her hands to Luis's back. There was a flicker of green light, but it quickly faded. She had nothing left to give.

Jade took a trembling step forward. Her legs were still unsteady, but she was feeling stronger with each passing moment. The maelstrom of conflicting powers she'd pressed together within her spirit was stabilizing, channeled through grooves previously burned into her soul.

I can do this.

Vastrozun looked absolutely terrible. Like Jade, his skin was covered with angry burns where the Light essence had seared through his body. The collapse of the cathedral on top of him hadn't done him any favors either, and the ongoing battle had left him with numerous other wounds. Dark blood dripped to the stones beneath him, sizzling and steaming against the rock. His steps were heavy and his movements slow, but his eyes still burned with fury. He knocked aside Fenrin's blade and kicked him with a snarl, launching the half-elf into the half-collapsed wall of the cathedral. Then, the demon swiped his hand, conjuring a bolt of crackling darkness and flinging it towards Naomi. The bard dove out of the way, leaving the magic to hit a bolder behind her where it burrowed a hole straight through the enormous stone as if it was paper.

Jade cursed, retrieving her blades and moving towards their foe as quickly as her aching legs could carry her. If Vastrozun's magic was coming back, they were in real trouble. Forget about the incoming demon swarm, they had to finish this before the effects of the divine relic faded. Mana condensed along the lengths of her daggers, forming into twin spikes of brilliant energy that shone like beacons in the swirling smoke and ash. Radiant energy crackled down Jade's arms and into her weapons, etching a web of fresh burns in their wake.  Her aura bucked and writhed in her mental grip, its radiant aspect searing her flesh each time her control slipped even for an instant. But that wasn't going to stop her. Jade closed the remaining distance between her and the demon lord, propelling herself over Luis's stooped figure with a mighty flap of her wings and swooping down upon him with weapons raised.

"Stay away from my friends!"

In the end, that was the answer. Their ideals may have been what drove each of them here, but they weren't what kept them pushing through to the very end. Jade was past that point, now. Her body was at its limit, and her mind wasn't far behind. All she wanted to do was just lie down and give in, responsibilities be damned. If it had just been her, she would have done just that. But it wasn't. Her friends were here, and they were in danger. And she was not going to let them down.

Vastrozun reacted slowly to her approach. Jade didn't know if it was because he'd already counted her out or if his own wounds were finally taxing the demon lord to his limit. Whichever it was, he didn't manage to bring his guard up in time. Both of Jade's radiant mana blades tore through Vastrozun's chest, slicing an "X" shaped cross into his front. Where her weapons had barely penetrated before, the enhanced radiant power blazing within her manifested bit deep into his flesh, gouging jagged lines that bled dark smoke into the air.

"Graargh!"

Vastrozun let loose a roar of pain that shook the plateau, staggering away and clutching at his chest. Jade landed hard before him, and she dug her boots into the hard ground, coiling like a spring. This was her chance. Now! She kicked off the shattered remains of what had once been a smooth courtyard, launching herself in an explosive pursuit.

She wasn't quite fast enough.

This was not the first time the ancient warrior had been in danger of defeat in his long life, far from it. One did not survive the centuries of battle and bloodshed that he had without unshakable discipline and a healthy respect for the strength of his opponents. Even as he staggered back Vastrozun brought up his palm. In it, another sphere of void-black darkness warped the surrounding air, fully charged and aimed straight at Jade. She spun in the air, trying to twist away from the blast of energy--

Naomi hit the ground hard, narrowly avoiding the bolt of death the demon lord had suddenly thrown her way. She landed awkwardly, banging her head against a protruding rock as she cradled her precious instrument, doing her best to protect it.

"Agh!"

Stars exploded across her vision and, for a moment, all sound snapped off. Her hearing was replaced by a high-pitched ringing, which slowly faded as the rest of her senses came back online. No, faster! Naomi dragged herself upright, leaning against a freestanding column to support herself. Her entire right side was cold and numb, like the attack she'd just dodged had blasted her with arctic frost. She fumbled with her guitar, frozen fingers stiffly plucking at the strings as she struggled to resume her melody. Luis was down, and Tara and Fenrin were out of the fight. Jade was somehow back on her feet, but Naomi doubted that even her prodigious willpower would keep her going for long. She had to do something!

Something flickered in her core. Her spirit stirred, and she felt her dwindling supply of magic crackle in response. Had the demon's spell messed with her mana? This was bad. The other demons were getting closer, they only had moments before--

The other demons!

Naomi glanced skyward. In just another minute or two, they would arrive. She could make out individual demons clearly now, their wings flapping fiercely as they drew ever closer. But, more importantly, they could see her.

Naomi grinned, suddenly filled with manic energy. With a thought, she opened her spirit to an ability she rarely had opportunity to make use of and resumed playing with newfound intensity. Power blazed through her body, and she pushed every single drop of it into the music that blasted forth from her guitar.

Naomi's supercharged "fleetfoot" spell hit Jade like a surge of adrenaline. It was just like that time back on Earth when they'd been fighting the wyverns that had escaped from the Labyrinth while a camera crew focused on the bard. Everything seemed to slow as the magic sank into her, amping her agility beyond simply "enhanced" and well into the realm of "superhuman". She observed the exact moment Vastrozun released his spell and timed her dodge perfectly, using her wings to push herself back to the ground and kicking off a slanted slab of stone, deftly avoiding the deadly magic. It was a maneuver she would never have been able to attempt without inhuman reflexes, but with the aid of Naomi's spell, it was no more difficult than one of her old vault routines.

Whoosh!

The spell whipped past her head, missing her by less than a foot. Something within the darkness reached out to her as the spell flew by, and an icy grip closed around Jade's heart. But then it was gone, the spell flying off into the distance, leaving her with nothing but a bone-deep chill and a few strands of flash-frozen hair.

"This is why we'll win." Jade grunted, diving back in to resume her attack. The demon lord was caught off guard, and she landed another clean hit to his side. "We fight together. We trust each other." Another slash. Another dodge. Jade's limbs were filled with lead and her muscles burned with protest, but Naomi's magic propped her up, burning like fuel in her veins. "But you have no one!"

She pushed in hard, going for the kill, but Vastrozun's eyes flashed. He kicked her away again, sending Jade skidding back several feet. She caught herself just before slamming into Luis, who was somehow still on his feet. She spared half a moment to check on him and their eyes met. Something passed between them then, a kind of communication that could only be shared by those who had stood strong together against death time and time again. It clicked, and she knew what she had to do.

Jade charged at Vastrozun again. This was it. She would finish it here, while she still had the strength to fight. The demon lord raised his hand once again, and this time his clawed fingers crackled with dark lightning. His spirit was stabilizing, and his ability to draw upon his magic increased by the second.

"Too late." He growled through a mouthful of dark blood. "You missed your chance."

There was only so much support and healing magic could do to prop someone up. These next few steps were it for Jade, and she knew it. Her strength nearly gone, she raised her weapons one last time, her radiant aura blades flickering in and out as her focus slipped. But that was okay. In her heart she now held a truth that had eluded her past self, to her own ruin.

"Maybe," she gasped, "but we win anyway."

You didn't have to do everything yourself. It might seem like that gave you more control, but that strength was an illusion. The greatest heights could only be reached by putting your trust in those who walked alongside you.

Jade swung with everything she had, but not at Vastrozun. Instead, she guided her gleaming blades straight into the umbral power gathering at his clawed fingers. Light met darkness with predictable results. The explosion blasted Jade away, lifting her from the ground and tossing her into a pile of rubble near where Siora knelt, utterly exhausted. Vastrozun withstood the eruption better and kept his feet, but only just. Jade could see his wavering silhouette through the billowing cloud of smoke conjured by his interrupted spell.

That's right. She thought to herself, somehow pulling herself to one knee. I'm not the hero of this story.

A line of silver sliced through the smoke and, suddenly, Luis was there, the angel's former sword held firmly in both hands. Blood coated his cracked and shattered armor, but his steps were strong as he stampeded closer, empowered by several overlapping effects. He struck with a single, smooth motion that left an arc of glimmering embers in its wake. Vastrozun raised his sword, but it was too late.

"Haaaaaa!"

Luis's stolen blade sliced in a clean, unbroken arc, falling with the speed and power of a meteor descending from the heavens. It connected. His sword sliced cleanly through the demon lord's neck before smashing into a nearby column, the force of the blow driving the blade two thirds of the way through the solid stone. Then, without fanfare, Lord Vastrozun's severed head fell to the ground. His body followed a moment later, collapsing with a heavy thunk that shook the surrounding stones. For several long seconds, everything was still. Jade stared at the corpse, still partially obscured by the smoke, utterly unable to think. Then, slowly, it sunk in.

"We… did it?"

"Jade! Luis!"

Someone shouted their names. Tara? There was a rumbling sound, followed by a loud series of hisses. A shadow darkened a nearby patch of the courtyard as their tek'kalla airship appeared just past the edge of the cliff, its rear ramp opening as Serik eased the ship into position.

"Hurry!"

Naomi was helping Fenrin onto the ship. Tara took a step closer to Jade, but she waved her back.

"No! Get on!"

It was as if the world had snapped back to full speed, and she was struggling to catch up to it. The demon host was almost upon them, they had to get out of here. Jade rose to her feet and took an unsteady step, Siora by her side.

"Come on, Jade. We can make it!"

"Luis?!"

"Right behind you!"

They were running together, Siora helping to support Jade as Luis caught up with them. The ship drew nearer. They were going to make it in time. The demons were closing in, but they still had a few more moments. Jade's heart swelled with hope, filling her tired limbs with new strength.

Then, they felt it.

Vastrozun's body held divine energy, essence he had stolen and made his own. That immensely powerful force had just lost the will that had kept it bound for so long and was further destabilized by the infusion of disruptive Light essence he'd been ravaged with. That energy was still there, but now there was nothing holding it in. Jade felt the building eruption with her magical senses and knew it was over. It was too much power, and they were too close. A mere fifty feet separated them from the waiting ship, but it may as well have been fifty miles. She opened her mouth to shout for her friends to go, only for the wind to get knocked from her lungs as a massive force slammed into her back.

"Obstrego!"

Luis's spell enveloped the three of them in bubble shields an instant after he grabbed Jade and Siora and heaved. The spell guardian had pumped more points into strength than anything else, and it showed. He flung the two of them like shotputs, launching them toward where Tara waited on the open ramp, hand outstretched.

"No!"

Jade looked back at Luis even as she spread her wings, righting herself in the air and pulling Siora as close as their overlapping shields would allow. Their eyes met for a moment, and he gave her an apologetic smile.

Then, the world was consumed by a titanic explosion of icy darkness.
