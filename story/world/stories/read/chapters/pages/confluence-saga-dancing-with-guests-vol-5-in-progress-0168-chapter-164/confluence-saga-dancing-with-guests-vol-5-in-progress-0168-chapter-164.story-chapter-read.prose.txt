Former Velli Insider Promises Tell-All Expose

Verner Infilestimi claims to have worked directly for the Velli family for 249 years. Two weeks ago he left their employ and immediately promised anyone willing to listen a look at what working for Family Velli was really like.

Two days ago Verner vanished. His accounts were closed, his residence cleared of all belongings. No one knows where he went. No one saw him pack up and leave.

No friends or family have come forward to report his disappearance because no friends or family have been identified. We were promised a story and instead were handed a mystery.

Those of you who have followed our search for the truth behind the Great Families are familiar with the difficulty in finding answers. You know how rarely an opportunity like Verner comes along.

Those of you who have only just joined our search will soon learn that Verner is not the first to vanish before telling his story.

As always, we remain your Anonymous Archivist.

Anya forced herself to take a deep breath as Gwen finished explaining what had happened with her mother. The argument had sounded unpleasant, especially the parts that had reminded her of the frustration she had felt towards her own mother. The implications about what Morgan had interrupted however, that was enough that she-

Breathe, love, Seb sent.

Her mother..!

Breathe.

She took another breath and looked at Gwen sitting cross-legged on the floor facing her. She looked at Cara, squatting just behind her shoulder, tense enough for it to be obvious without having to look at her hair for clues. She felt Seb's presence next to her. Getting angry right now wouldn't do any good, she couldn't go after Lady Phila even if she wanted to.

She'd been furious at finding out Bern had treated his sister that way, she'd decided to deal with him once she had the chance. Lady Phila wouldn't be back for at least another six months, not to mention she was high enough level that there was nothing she could do- No, nothing Gwen could do. This wasn't her fight…

Seb, she sent. Stop being reasonable.

She asked us for help, not to solve the problem.

That doesn't mean you have to find a point of view that makes her decision understandable.

She could feel his reasoning, recognize his attempts at framing the situation to at least recognize what might have led there. She didn't want to recognize that, she didn't want to have a degree of sympathy for someone who would act that way. Then she felt a clear shift in his thoughts. He still understood and could reason through how it happened, she could still feel the same through their bond, but it didn't matter.

Your Affinity?

I guess? he answered. Even before I Awakened I tried to see and understand the point of view even if I didn't agree with it. Now it becomes a shape that fit into the current.

So understanding doesn't-

If Gwen asks us to help 'deal' with her mother, Seb sent, a momentary vision of Clyde, of that thing that had tried to control her, flashing through their thoughts. It won't be a problem.

Anya realized they'd lost focus, that Gwen was still waiting as Cara grew more and more visibly agitated.

"Sorry," she said as she reached out to squeeze Gwen's hand lightly. "I started to think about what you told us and we had to remind each other that trying to attack Lady Phila would be suicidal, not to mention impossible."

"You..?" Gwen said, her eyes going wide. "But she didn't do anything to you."

"What she did was wrong," Seb said. "I understand Naming is something Fae do, at least sometimes. We didn't grow up somewhere where it was a part of life, which probably makes it seem worse. But doing it to your own daughter just because you disagree with her? Just because she has an opinion you don't like? No."

"It gets used as discipline, to guide upbringing," Cara said softly. "It isn't a geas, it isn't always present, but…"

"Cara," Gwen said. "They never did with us."

"Rarely did, not never. Maybe never the way she was about to, but Lady Phila was always faster to compel than your father."

"I…" Gwen began, what little color left in her face draining away. "I forgot. I didn't want to remember."

Anya looked at Cara and saw the pain in her eyes. There had to be something they could do, had to be some way they could help. She watched as Cara shifted onto her knees and pulled Gwen into her arms, holding her while all but glaring at Anya.

"I'm sorry," Anya said. "I know the words don't help, don't change what happened."

She saw Cara nod in response, heard what sounded like Gwen sniffling before turning her head to look at them without moving away from Cara's embrace.

"I don't want to see her again, especially not alone."

"No one is saying you need to," Seb said.

"She'll demand it, expect it."

"Say no?"

It can't be that easy, Anya sent, thinking back to the Court culture she grew up in.

"Fae Royals don't refuse their parents's requests, not when they aren't able to stand as peers," Cara said.

"Just like Sworn don't disagree with their charge or tell them what to do?" Anya asked.

"Exact-" Cara began then flushed. "It isn't the same."

"You're both right," Gwen said as she slowly pushed herself away from Cara to sit upright, her hand lingering on Cara's arm despite no longer being held.

"She can insist, I can say no. We aren't at home, we aren't among the Fae."

She felt Seb's question a moment before he asked it, too briefly for her to point out this wasn't quite the moment.

"You said you needed our help. I'm not sure how we can."

"You have," Gwen said. "You are. Being able to talk, to know you don't feel her actions were appropriate…"

"Not having to act according to expectations and demands," Cara added.

"Is bacon as good without Sandra around thinking she's stealing it?" Gwen asked.

Anya saw the smiles start to appear on Gwen and Cara's faces as she nodded.

"Probably. I'll let you know after breakfast tomorrow."

"Why… Oh!"

"Don't worry Mistress, your bacon will be safe with me," Cara said.

"But who will protect it from you?"

Anya laughed as she felt Seb holding back from commenting. The problem wasn't really solved, the pain and anger wasn't gone, but in this moment it didn't matter. They could talk about bacon, they could smile and enjoy themselves and forget about Lady Phila.

Cara shifted her shoulders as she sat back against the bed frame. She had a few moments before she could stop pretending. Pretending she would doze off sitting here, between the door and Gwen's rest. Pretending she needed to be vigilant and couldn't sleep more soundly than she could ever remember managing before.

By before she meant before becoming Anya and Seb's Guest, before realizing how much strain Bern's proximity had put her under every night for years, before meeting people who didn't look at her as Gwen's Sworn and assume that's all she was.

She wasn't complaining, not really. She liked the change, but change meant exactly that. She still wasn't sure how to act around them, how to act around Gwen around them. They didn't care, she was sure of that. They didn't look at her pushing Gwen to act, scolding Gwen for acting, being there for Gwen as a person, as anything unusual.

"You're not sleeping on the floor."

"I know," she answered. "I'm just pretending."

"Pretending?"

"That nothing changed. That I need to be keep watch and you need me to protect you while you sleep."

"Cara…"

"No. I know. It's all just different," she said, pushing herself to her feet only to sit on the edge of the bed.

"Am I wrong to trust them?"

"I don't think so," she said then shook her head and turned to look down at Gwen. "No, you aren't. You saw them with her family, with each other. On his own I might worry about Seb, he makes me think of Morgan, but after seeing them together? No."

"Good. I'm glad you agree."

"When are you going to ask them?"

"Who said I was going to."

Cara tried not to roll her eyes as she reached out to form a band. Some things could be said, others needed to be known. Feeling the connection establish she did almost nothing to mask her emotions, focusing entirely on the moment, on the situation they were finding themselves in.

You asked for help against your mother-

Lady Phila is-

She is still your mother, no matter what comes to be between you, Cara sent back sharply before her thoughts softened. You asked for their help, you're choosing sides, yours and theirs, and they don't know.

There are other options.

That's still choosing a side.

After we dive, after we know if we can work with my brother, after we spend time in the Depths with them.

Cara nodded before stretching out on the bed, next to Gwen without ever touching. She held on to the band, kept the connection despite the silence that stretched. She could feel Gwen doing the same, aware of one another yet separate. Cara knew some Sworn formed bonds with their charges, knew the time, effort, and constant proximity could help create something more. They didn't have that, they had the band when they dared use it, they trusted one another, cared about one another, but they were still separate.

She didn't notice when their breathing synchronized, when their bodies relaxed and hearts started to beat as one. She didn't notice, except for the awareness that they were together as the band dissolved into sleep.
