[Strong Arm] - Fireball? Firebolt? Potentially dangerous. Overlap with extant skill.

[Retaliation] - Uncertain. Does it combine conceptually or mechanically? Fire shield? Fire buff? Stronger while on fire? Consult with Talla and Anna.

And so on. She seems convinced that the potential of a spell remains flexible until it’s actually created, at which point it becomes “registered” with the [World Engine] and can no longer change. Except when one of our other headmates tries to use the same spell, which both requires the assistance of the person who made the spell in the first place and only results in a variation on the spell already created.

Of course, that’s all just theory based on one example, but Vi wants to get as much information as she can before experimenting with anything irreversible. Personally, I think she’s being a bit too cautious, but it would be hypocritical of me to tell them how to explore magic after deciding not to do it myself.

I still have mixed feelings. I was the one who pushed for magic in the first place, and I still think it’s super cool! It’s just that when I think about trying to tap into that terrifying, infinite expanse again...no.
