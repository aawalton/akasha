Cass breathed in and out. He’d stayed awake, studying Command Center through the night as he waited for the daily reset. Like he’d noticed the first time, the amount of detail was astounding. He could even see people lying down in their beds.

But it was also creepy…

Because he could also see people in their beds doing… other things. Each time that popped up, he quickly moved on, deciding that area didn’t need a closer look, no matter what emotions it brought out in him. His remaining time until the clock wound down was spent wondering if there were any perveted QuestWrights in the world.

Then, it arrived.

[System Alert]

Your quests have reset for the day.

Quota activating…

0 failed Quests recorded

+1 to total Quest limit

Current available quests: 154/154

Advance your reputation or gain new titles to increase your daily allotment.

“Well, would you look at that. Not too shabby, huh!” Cass yelled to the empty room. In a much more moderated tone, he said, “So, as long as I don’t fail too many Quests and go past my node-buffed limit, I’ll get one extra a day. Man, that Logistics Path was a really good choice for my first one.”

He looked at the next line in his notifications.

Cassio Vale, will you accept a new Exponential Daily Quest?

“Why yes, I would like to do a new Quest at no charge.”

[The Exponential Daily Quest]

Cassio Vale will work on his posture by maintaining an erect carriage for at least 20 minutes.

This must be done before the day is over.

Cassio Vale

Liberty Guildtower

QuestWright

“Erect…” He paused. “Erect…carriage. What a weird way to word something. First man-purse, and now this? Is the System making fun of me?”

He couldn’t rule it out or in. From what he could tell, there was some driving force behind how the System worked, but as for what that was, Cass had no idea.

Remembering a quick lesson Dev had given them about dealing with the stress of the body, Cass went over to a nearby wall, placed his rear against it, then moved his feet in and stood up as straight as he could. There was no timer in his vision for this, but after a long amount of time passed with him staring at the opposing wall as he further studied Command Center, it came in.

Exponential Daily Quest Complete]

Time remaining: 23 hours, 38 minutes, and 2 seconds…

Reward: 4.8xp

Happy with his choices, Cass knew he had some work to do before he could sleep. Shucking his robe on a nearby desk, he reached into his screens and activated an Intermediate Stalwart Quest. Then, breath slowly calming down from the workout, he copied it and did it again, and again.

Like always, Cass ended on Exponential, but it disappointingly wasn’t a large leap from the previous day. With a quick moment focusing on a change to his System screen, he took a look at the results.

[3x Daily Quests Complete]

Rewards: 36.1xp, 11 Stalwart Tokens, 7 Survivor Tokens

“Much better grouped up like that.” He said with a nod. “So, I spent two Quests there. One for my first Stalwart, and one for the Stalwart Exponential, and I’ve already used both of my copies for the day. I’ll have to do Tempered later tonight when Ana can help me out again.”

Liking the plan, Cass stretched his worn-out muscles. The Constitution upgrade still more than paid for itself, as the tight, painful feeling throughout his body receded after only a few minutes. Taking a breath, Cassn looked at his desk and made a decision.

He fell asleep, his head resting on his hands as the sun rose, the map untouched before him.

Dreams of Adya and his friends being eaten by Skreels filtered into his unconscious mind. It was so graphic that even within the nightmare, he felt sick. Thankfully, it was brief, but things didn’t stop there.

It faded away only to be replaced right away. In the new dream, he was forced to dance and move erratically. It took him a moment to see the strings attached to his limbs. Looking up, he could only see a shadowy figure controlling his body and forcing him to move. Just as it was about to end, one string wrapped around his throat and began to choke the life out of him.

He woke up in a sweat to find the room thankfully empty. He’d forgotten to put his robe back on and wouldn’t have liked the situation that would’ve brought upon him.

This place sucks…He thought to himself. But only for a moment. Because another thought quickly followed, I’ll just have to make it better.

Ana was smiling as she dropped by with his breakfast only an hour or so later. She gushed about the second Quest arriving, then gave him a light talking-to about the first one requiring so many push-ups. After she left, he ate, still studying the map and making a few notes in his pad.

The early morning meeting came all too soon as he stepped out, not at all surprised to see Colin Schwartz already at his desk within the empty Scriptorium.

“Whoa,” The other Junior QuestWright said, “Did you stay here all night?”

“Yep,” Cass confirmed, “It was my first day, so I had a lot of studying to do.”

“Studying…right.” The man shook his head, then looked back at the papers splashed across every inch of his desk, “Look, I’m sorry if I was rude yesterday. New guy comes in, don’t know him, don’t know his family…it just makes me a bit nervous.”

“Hey, I get it, and no worries,” Cass replied with a smile. “Everyone starts somewhere, and maybe we started a little roughly.”

“Yeah, exactly.” He looked up, “Redo?”

“Redo.”

“Cool.” He leaned back in his chair, “So, how many Quests did you get through yesterday?”

“148,” Cass replied, “That’s what took me so long.” He laughed to himself, “It was a pain in the butt figuring out where all the shops were.”

“Wait, wait, you really did all 148 of them?” Colin asked with big eyes. “Seriously?”

“Yes, I did. Why?”

“Because we’re expected to get through however many we can in the day’s allotted time. Most of the Apprentice and Initiate QuestWright’s can do that, but once you start pushing into the second Tier, that gets hard to do. And on your first day,” Colin gave him a respectful nod, “That’s surely something. What do you think your efficiency rating will be at?”

Cass laughed again, “I have no idea.”

“Hah!” Colin chortled with him. “You’re not so bad, Cassio. Look, as long as you stay above 60 percent, you should be fine. Well,” He rubbed at his eyes, “You should be. Senior QuestWright Mirelle, for as strict as she seems, is the nicest one in the group. Maybe she’ll take it easy on the new guy.”

That did not happen.

“QuestWright Vale, you achieved a 71% efficiency rating on your Quests. Do you want to tell me why it was so low?”

Cass raised his hand, waiting an interminable amount of time before she nodded at him, “Because I’m new, in a big city, and I was given 148 Quests to complete on my first day? I thought I just needed to stay above sixty-”

“Minimum standards lead to minimum results, QuestWright.” She said, interrupting him. Cass tried to look at Colin, but when their eyes met, the other man looked away.

So, not a bad person, just anxious and has no balls. Got it.

Profile updated as the Senior QuestWright continued, “You are required to complete the Re-Quests you receive before the day is over, or that’s a Docking. That doesn’t mean efficiency will be allowed to slip through the cracks.”

“How many Quests are the others required to complete daily?”

“That isn’t material to your assignment,” Mirelle stated flatly, “You have been assigned 148 Delivery Quests to be completed daily. If you can’t achieve that and this becomes too stressful for you, your Calling can be changed by request.”

Cass tilted his head, “Change my Calling?”

“Of course, the Guild is not without empathy. If you find that being a QuestWright is too much for you, please let me or any Guildmaster know, and we’ll schedule it right away.”

“I see.”

And he did. It wouldn’t shock him to learn that the other QuestWrights didn’t have to stay late because they weren’t required to reach the almost maximum amount of Quests they could complete daily. That was specifically a Cass situation.

They’re trying to burn me out so I won’t be in here anymore. In fact, they expect it. That’s why they put me in that room, and that’s why they’re throwing so many Quests at me. Well, thank god for Templates.

You’re not going to make me give up, Liberty. If Dark Guilds and monsters couldn’t do it, bureaucracy and snooty tactics won’t either.

“I don’t think that will be required,” Cass replied evenly.

The Senior QuestWright looked at him a moment longer, as if waiting for him to change his mind, then began calling on others with warnings and Dockings. No bonuses were given before she walked away with a long, sharp stride.

Cass went back to his office and, not long after, Quirrel showed up with a new stack. He looked tired.

“Here you go, Cass. Twenty new Quests for the day’s work.”

“Thank you, Quirrel. I promise to be faster today.”

And he was. The templates were still too new, and he lacked a clear picture of what was required for each Quest, but just by the habit of creating over a hundred Quests a day, he sped up. On the second day, Cass finished his assigned Quests just before seven p.m. On the third, he got it down to six.

After three days of completing Quests in Liberty, the templates started to show their worth. He noticed in his notes and through the extensive use of Profile’s tracking, that the majority of Delivery Quests each day rotated through different locations in the fourth ring. First, it was Liberty Loaders C, then D, then E, and on the fourth day, F.

Each place had a slightly different focus depending on the locations immediately surrounding it. Sure, they all delivered dry foodstuffs, and at times, barrels of water. But while the south was dedicated to raw materials, the north took in imported objects from the outside. The longer he worked at it, the more Liberty’s picture began to clear up.

At least, from a QuestWright’s perspective. Because even as he got faster, his efficiency stayed about the same, much to Senior QuestWright Mirelle’s continued rage.

“You had three failures yesterday, that’s a Docking.”

“Your efficiency hasn’t gone up a single percentage point since you arrived here.”

“You should really consider changing your Calling, QuestWright Vale. For all of us.”

On the outside, he showed the same contrition and anxiety he’d presented to the Senior QuestWright on the first day. But every day that Liberty tried to break him down, he found it easier and easier to shrug off. It was like a negativity baptism, hardening his internal purpose to always point toward hope.

On the fifth day since Cass had begun working in the Scriptorium, he woke up as early as ever. He hadn’t been idle, still continuing his Tempered and Stalwart workouts day by day, even re-assigning the non-Stalwart Exponential Quest to both himself and an ecstatic Ana. His gift to Quirrel had to wait, as he hadn’t completed all of his Quests on-time and received his free Vellums just yet, but he knew it was coming.

And all the while, as Liberty pushed, Cass grew, and the days passed by; the fire that had begun in his chest burned ever more fiercely.

Opening his eyes to the unadorned ceiling of his room, he pinged Bella’s location as he’d done every morning since arriving. Following Ana’s advice about saying important things aloud, he kept the more dangerous thoughts to himself.

Only another day or two, or I think. Then, we’ll see who’s here. Getting out of the Tower is the hard part. Every time I’ve tried to go near the entrance, someone has popped up out of nowhere to guide me back to ‘safe’ areas.

They’re trying to keep me trapped here. To force me to quit.

Hah, they don’t know me very well. Like that would ever work.

Smiling to himself, he pulled up his screen. Four days of non-stop work had seen it go through remarkable changes, and he thought it was high-time to unlock a few more of the strange choices within Cornerstone.

Before he checked his personal node to see how much experience he could spend, Cass scrolled through the new notifications first.

[System Notice]

You’ve gained .3xp for Ana Renae’s Quest Completion.

Details can be found in the Quest Ledger.

Various members of the Liberty Support Branch view you more favorably

Various members of the Liberty QuestWright Branch view you less favorably

Various members of the Liberty Warrior Branch view you more favorably

Cassio Vale, will you accept a new Exponential Daily Quest?

Pushing aside the Daily Quest for a moment, Cass remarked to himself, “What a strange city.”

He was still sad about the discovery that Cornerstone’s Daily Quest didn’t reward System Reputation, but there wasn’t much he could do about it. Shaking his head, he pulled up his node.

Calling: Junior QuestWright: Cassio Vale

Level: 13

Experience Accrued: 6197.42

Experience Required for Next Level: 502.58

Unspent Experience: 2247.32

Available System Reputation: 520

Available Quests: 156/156

Total Stalwart Tokens: 53

Total Tempered Tokens: 8

Total Survivor Tokens: 59

Two more tokens before I can unlock the second Tempered step. It may also be the time to unlock a new Stalwart. They tend to build up quickly, and there are still plenty of options. Okay, no problem. As for Cornerstone…

Cass had decided on the second day to always keep a ready bank of experience in case he ever needed to purchase anything. He’d wisely understood that in his current hostile environment, of which Battlefield Memory continued ticking every day, having some withheld for emergencies was a wise course.

Not to mention, with every day focused on learning how the massive city worked and the huge time restriction he had, it was smart not to add anything new to his plate. No matter that he spent any free time locked up in his room or office working on it, Cass still hadn’t made any progress advancing the Quill’s tutorial. Taking on a new set of abilities just wasn’t wise.

Re-considering all of those facts, he opened Cornerstone and scrolled through the minor node options again. Taking a breath, Cass purchased three minor nodes for one-hundred and eighty experience. They lit up as always, but as he hadn’t completed the ring, nothing else appeared. Swapping screens to activate the new purchases, on a whim, he turned on Investigation as well.

>Administrative on<

>Delivery on<

>Investigation on<

>Message off<

>Scouting on<

>Stalwart on<

>Strengthening on<

>Tempered on<

>Training on<

[System Notice]

New functionality has been added to the Cornerstone path.

Would you like to update all active Exponential Quests?

It updates the Quests already out there! No way! Cass thought in exuberance, trying very hard not to yell out. Yes! A thousand times, yes!

All Exponential Quests have been updated with Investigation, Strengthening, Tempered, and Scouting options.

Days required to complete all dailies: 8

Initial Quest experience now set at 3.5

“What the hell does that last part mean?” Shaking his head at the System, which hadn’t once responded to him since unlocking his gate, Cass got up and started the day.

Burning through only a single copied Stalwart, he finished an annoying Administrative Daily that popped up. requiring him to make his bed perfectly, activated a Tempered, then stepped out.

The Quest didn’t take long. Ana was already in the room, having adjusted to his sleeping, or lack of sleeping, rather early on. He performed his normal tricks with haste as he’d already adjusted to the new Quest-type, then dodged some pillows and took a quick shower.

Ana was cheery as always, and his porridge tasted better than any he’d ever had that wasn’t made by his father. Telling her that he would be back on time that evening, she handed him a sealed cup to drink from for the day, then he made his way to the Scriptorium.

The Clerks at the front desk smiled as he passed, with Jessica’s expression a little on the strong side, and he passed through the intake room without any issues. The door to what was slowly becoming his sanctuary for the world opened silently, and then he found his problem.

Seated at one of the previously empty desks was a grumpy-looking Maggie. Beside her was a similarly grumpy Noah, his hand slowly moving away from the metal object at his side.

“Finally, ya brat. What took ya so long?”
