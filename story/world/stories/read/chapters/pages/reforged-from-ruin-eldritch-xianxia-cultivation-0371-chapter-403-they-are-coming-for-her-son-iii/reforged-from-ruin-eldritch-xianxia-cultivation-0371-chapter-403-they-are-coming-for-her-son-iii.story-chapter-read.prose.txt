A thousand years of Death, wrapped in the illusion-that-is-true of a laughing giant.

The sky goes from black to a pitch-perfect white. A coloration so profound, so alien, that it’s like the sky was always a canvas, and only now has the paint been pulled back to reveal it. Alabaster white, void white, a color that is no-color, and it colors the world below it just as much as the sky.

The only thing visible in it are the shapes in it. Everything is precisely the same shade, but the sharp lines of a single astral body stand out brighter than all the rest.

There’s a sort of afterglow in the air, right over where Kai Ra sits. Rai Jin, standing beneath and behind her, glows nearly as bright as she does, as if he’s reflecting more light than the rest of the world, right alongside her.

The Cold Sun shifts in the sky.

Just an inch. Just a millimeter. Less than any other time she’s seen it move.

Slowly, just behind it, an eye looks down at them, so bright and so cold and so empty and so dark that it can’t ever be anything but the END of a world.

Approval.

“You Have No Place Here.”

Empty robes glare, empty of eyes but looking out with an army of ghosts. Behind it, a corpse sits on a throne.

It is not sitting in the same position as before. It has moved. Only very slightly, only the smallest tilt of the head… but it is not the same as before.

The eye of the impossible thing beyond the sky meets his, and it is even emptier than the Holiest of Holies. It is an Echo, multiplied by a billion into a single necromantic demigod- the eye, on the other hand, simply is.

“It Is Not Your Time.”

Something pushes back.

Not words. Not anything so potent as that, not anything so world-ending. Just a small, quiet thing, a puff of air, less than even a laugh.

Amused, nonetheless.

Amusement. The sound of it soft enough to be barely audible, and powerful enough to make the sky tremble.
