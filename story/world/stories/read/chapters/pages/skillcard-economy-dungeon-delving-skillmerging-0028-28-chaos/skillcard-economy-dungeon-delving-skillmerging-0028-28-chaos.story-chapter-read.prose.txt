Rotary Rend

When the gladiator Varnav faced the Iron-Shelled Basilisk, his heavy sword bounced off its scales, and his iron spear shattered on impact. Backed into a corner and stripped of conventional weapons, he remembered the violent cyclones of his homeland that could tear ancient trees from their roots. Realizing that the greatest destructive power lay not in a straight line, but in a violent spiral, Varnav twisted his broken shaft with all his might at the exact moment of impact, drilling through the impenetrable armor and tearing the beast apart from the inside.

Recorded in the Akashic system in the year 211.

Rotary Rend allows the wielder to impart a violent, high-speed centrifugal force to their weapons or body parts. This rotational momentum turns a simple physical swing or thrust into a devastating, drilling impact capable of tearing through defenses.

I was suffering from a massive predicament right now. Do I merge Shadowstride and Rotary Rend together or not? These two skills were so fundamentally different that I didn’t have the slightest idea what might come out. Would combining them give me a Gold card? There was no guarantee. Would whatever new card I got be better than using these two separately?

I thought hard about it and ultimately decided against merging them. They worked perfectly fine individually—the supreme mobility of Shadowstride and the raw, destructive power of Rotary Rend. It was way too big of a risk. I would only think about merging them if I found myself backed into a corner with no other options.
