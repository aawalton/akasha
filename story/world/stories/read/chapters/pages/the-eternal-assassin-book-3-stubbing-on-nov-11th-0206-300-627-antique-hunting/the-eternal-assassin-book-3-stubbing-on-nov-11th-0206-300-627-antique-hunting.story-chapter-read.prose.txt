Down a hand and needing to wait until Richard figured out if saving Grant or any of the other minions of The Lost Lady was even possible, Asher decided to take a day to rest up and recuperate. He’d first intended to spend it monitoring Grant from the Eternal Mark he’d placed on the man, only to discover that The Lost Lady must have somehow figured out what he’d done. After noting how the mark had finally stopped moving, Asher had thrown his awareness over to it, only to find Grant’s severed arm lying on the floor of one of the tunnels.

Despite the fact that they’d lost Grant for the moment, Asher wasn’t too worried. While the loss of potentially popping in directly next to The Lost Lady and killing her before she knew what was going on was rough, they should still be able to track Grant down at the very least. Rulfar only needed a few drops of blood to work with, an entire arm would be the equivalent of a giant neon sign directing them precisely where to go.

Unable to monitor Grant and not willing to do anything too risky with a hand missing, Asher went out into town with Samantha at his side, deciding to do his best to enjoy a rare day of just wandering around and enjoying the other’s company. The two of them hadn’t really gotten a chance to talk after his accidental proposal the other day, and based on how Samantha had beamed in response to him asking if she wanted to go on a date, it was pretty obvious she wanted some time for just the two of them as well.

“Is it on me right now?” Samantha asked, holding his arm against her side as they walked through the main market of the capital. Window shopping was a fun date idea regardless of the world one was on, even if the stalls didn’t actually have any windows.

“Yeah, it is,” Asher confirmed. “Permanence is doing its thing. You’re not getting any older.”

“Huh. I mean, not that I don’t believe you, I just sort of expected to feel something, you know?” Samantha asked, frowning as she looked down at herself. “Immortality doesn’t really feel like anything.”

“Think of it this way. Do you normally feel yourself aging?” Asher asked, laughing at the curious look on Samantha’s face. “And you don’t need to ask me all the time. I’ll keep the skill active on you any time I’m not actively using it on something else. It’s largely passive, so it’s simple enough.”

With that, the two of them carried on through the market. As they walked, Samantha eventually shifted from holding onto his entire arm to taking his hand in her own. When their fingers entwined, Asher could feel the cool metal of Samantha’s ring, as well as a strange, almost pulling sensation from deep within the band.

Glancing down at the ring, Asher gave into the feeling, and the world twisted and rippled around him as he suddenly found himself standing in an entirely different market, watching himself as he purchased the simple piece of jewelry for Samantha over in Whikoga. Hilariously, his past self was also missing an arm from his old battle against Eight, and Asher smiled as he watched the sheer joy and happiness on Samantha’s face as he picked out a ring and matching earrings for her to wear. Almost as quickly as the vision had come, the world rippled again, and he was back in the capital once more.
