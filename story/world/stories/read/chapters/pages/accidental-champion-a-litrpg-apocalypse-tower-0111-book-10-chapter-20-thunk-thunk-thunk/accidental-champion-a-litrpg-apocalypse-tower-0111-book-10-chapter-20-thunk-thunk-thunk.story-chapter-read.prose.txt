The first lion-octopus-nightmare beast pushed off all six of its furry, tentacle-like legs when it was still forty feet away. The intense gravity in the dungeon didn’t seem to have any effect on its ability to jump at all. It soared through the air as easily as a fruit bat gliding from one tree to another.

Thunk, thunk, thunk.

More bone arrows slammed into the beast, now hitting its exposed underside—an area that was often a great vulnerability, as it didn’t tend to be as thick. But as with all the other arrows he’d launched at the two Void Dungeon beasts, these didn’t do a damned thing. Or, at least, they didn’t appear to. He couldn’t sense how much health these things had, but both the beasts now looked like lion-octopus-porcupines because of the number of arrows not lodged into their strange flesh, and it hadn’t affected their movement at all.

Yet Xavier was still smiling, even as the first lion-octopus-nightmare beast reached him.

Personal System Integration has reached 6%.

Xavier was keeping a close eye on his energy expenditure. The rudimentary System that was currently installing into his brain was eating up a great deal of it with every percent it reached. Creating the bone bow and the arrows had been negligible, and it also hadn’t been enough to do anything against these things.

He leapt to the side, rolling along the ground. Though he hadn’t been in this intense gravity for long, his body was already adjusting for it. Xavier had gone through so many shifts and changes over the years, gaining more and more strength and speed, that he was used to compensating when things changed.

Though he wasn’t used to compensating down. Usually, he moved too fast, pushed off too hard. Now it was like everything was reversed and he was suddenly about as weak as he’d been when the System had called his physical abilities “middling.”

Except, that wasn’t really true. That was just a lie his mind wanted to tell him. Just because gravity was pushing so hard down on him that he had trouble moving didn’t mean he wasn’t incredibly skilled. Even under all this pressure his movements were fluid, fast, and as efficient as he could make them, not wasting an ounce of energy as he made it back to his feet following his roll.

Personal System Integration has reached 7%.

Xavier wanted to take this fight cautiously, even if he wasn’t usually the cautious type. He had a few dozen spell patterns manifested inside him and ready to go if things became dire, but considering he had no idea how much energy he’d have left after the System finished installing, he was hesitant to use any.

That, and he worried it would only attract more beasts to him.

So instead of casting his old reliable spells, he was midway through growing a bone spear as he came to his feet. His bone bow was deposited straight into his Storage Ring. It hadn’t been damaged, so he could use it again later if needed.

The bone spear felt natural in his hands. He might have gone for dual wielding swords or even the old faithful scythe, but from the look of these beasts he figured a spear was the best bet.

Xavier whirled around. His senses felt incredibly dulled. The All-Seeing Eye couldn’t see these beasts at all. He had to rely more heavily on his natural senses than he had in a long time to keep track of the beasts’ positions. As he whirled, he’d found the first lion-octopus landing just where Xavier had been. All six of its boneless legs now had spikes extended from their ends. Spikes that were faced downward, slamming into the spot he’d just been.

What surprised Xavier most was that the spikes didn’t make a mark on the stone. But while they didn’t leave a mark, he could feel a slight vibration from the beast’s landing.

That thing must be damned heavy.

Xavier faced his bone spear forward as the lion-octopus shifted positions. But a mere thrust wasn’t going to be enough to do the damage he needed. Xavier wanted to incapacitate this thing as fast as possible. Killing it would be even better, but he’d settle for slowing it down before the second lion-octopus-nightmare beast reached him.

He extended his wings, feeling the strain. For a moment he wondered if he would even be able to fly in gravity this intense, but he wasn’t about to let such doubts stop him. He was the ruler of ten sectors. He’d taken down one of the top ten most powerful beings in his entire universe.

In his universe, he was incredibly powerful, and it was only a matter of time before he ruled it all.

Of course he could do what he needed to take down these two pitiful Void Dungeon beasts. Derrida hadn’t sent him here to die, after all.

Personal System Integration has reached 8%.

At the same time as his wings flapped, Xavier’s legs flicked up and landed on the wall behind him. He braced himself on the wall as solidly as he could then used a kick-off and another flap from his wings to give him as much momentum as he could muster to slam the spear straight into the underside of the lion-octopus-nightmare beast as it leapt at him a second time, all six of those extended spikes aiming for him.

He’d intentionally made the spear extra long—comically long, if this weren’t a life-or-death situation—and it hit the lion-octopus-nightmare beast well before it could reach him.

Xavier felt a massive amount of resistance as the spear hit the beast’s flesh. The arrows he’d loosed had all been able to lodge themselves into this thing, so he wasn’t expecting that. But it made sense as he observed none of the arrows had gotten more than an inch or two deep—the thick, furry hide the beast had must be more like armour than anything else, and while the arrows had penetrated that, it looked as though they hadn’t gotten any farther.

But that resistance didn’t last for long. The spear sunk through the hide and into the beast’s flesh, going deeper and deeper, covering the bone spear shaft with a disgusting, orange sludge that smelled fouler than a demon’s bowels.

Personal System Integration has reached 9%.

Xavier wrinkled his nose as the beast slid farther down the spear, more and more of that orange sludge released. It was screeching again, but this screech wasn’t one of desperate hunger, it was one of absolute pain.

The lion-octopus-nightmare beast didn’t look as though it was going to stop sliding closer to him, and all those spikes were still aimed well and truly at Xavier. Xavier released the spear and leap to the side once more. As he rolled along the ground and heard the beast slam into the solid stone wall. It was still screeching, but he had no idea if a wound like that would be fatal. He also worried that neither of the beasts had tried to cast any spells on him, just as he hadn’t cast any spells on them.

It made him wonder if the Void Dungeon was trying to converse energy in the same way that he was.

If so, that was vital information.

It’s probably just testing me. Feeling me out. Or maybe it can’t expend too much energy on this particular floor of the Void Dungeon. Hell, let’s be honest, I just have no idea how this place actually works.

Personal System Integration has reached 10%.

Xavier sighed. That damned Personal System was taking far longer than he would have liked. The second lion-octopus-nightmare beast screeched. An angry screech. He didn’t know how sentient these Void Dungeon-created beasts were, but that screech sure sounded like it was pissed off because of what Xavier had just done to its kin.

Or maybe it was the Void Dungeon itself that was pissed off…

Another bone spear grew out of Xavier’s arm. He’d just gotten it gripped in both hands as the second beast was almost on him. He angled the spear upward and took this one straight in the throat.

“Urgh.” Xavier coughed violently as some of that orange sludge spurted straight onto his face and into his mouth. If he thought it had smelled bad, it tasted about a million times worse…

Personal System Integration has reached 11%.

Xavier did the same move as before, releasing the spear and launching into a roll and out of the way, hearing the second beast fall to the ground where he’d just been.

The first octopus-lion-nightmare beast had made it back to its many feet and was heading toward him across the solid stone floor.

As it closed the gap, Xavier felt something from his Immortal Soul lens. It was the first time his spiritual sense had picked up anything in this place, anything but himself that is, and it was coming from the first of the two beasts. He didn’t need to sense the energy to see it, however.

Every one of the six spikes that had extended from the lion-octopus-nightmare beast had started to glow. The glow had started a fraction of a second ago, and in an instant the spikes were launching forward. The beast shot the now-glowing spikes straight at Xavier—something he hadn’t even known the damned could do—and there wasn’t time enough for Xavier to get clear of them. A bone shield grew from his left forearm as he moved.

Thunk, thunk.

Two of the glowing spikes slammed into the bone shield. There were another three thunks as some of the spikes hit the wall. But before those three thunks there was a heavy thud.

The glowing spike had made it cut into Xavier’s reinforced robes, pierced his flesh, and lodged itself straight through his upper thigh, stopping only when the point had emerged on the other side.

Xavier was already experiencing pain from the installing of the Personal System—another notification announced it had reached 14 percent—but the pain he felt now drowned out the pain in his head. His Immortal Soul lens wasn’t only sensing the spikes, either. There were lines of energy reaching from each of the six spikes back to the beast. Five of those glowing lines disappeared when the spikes didn’t hit their target.

But the spike that had slammed into Xavier still had its energy line connecting it to the beast. This energy line pulsed brightly in Xavier’s spiritual sense. With that pulse came even more pain. And not just pain.

A reduction in his energy.

The spike was helping the Void Dungeon-beast feed off Xavier’s energy, making the reserves in his cores dwindle down even faster than they had been because of the Personal System’s installation.

Rage filled Xavier at this having happened. He’d been willing to treat these things with kid gloves right up until now, but if they could feed off his energy…

No longer did he worry about how much energy he was using. No longer did he worry about attracting more of these beasts.

It was time to show the Void Dungeon what he was made of.

He needed to put these bastard things down.

Xavier pulsed Soul Energy, aiming his Immortal Soul lens halfway down the line of energy that connected the spike to the lion-octopus-nightmare beast, scattering the energy there on the subatomic level. He ended up having to pulse more Soul Energy than he’d imagined would be necessary—a full 10 percent of his current reserve—but he severed the line of energy all the same.

For the first time since he’d arrived in this dungeon, Xavier burned Reality Energy. It infused every single one of his muscles, and while he could still feel the massive intensity of the gravity around him, it wasn’t nearly as powerful as it had been a moment ago. As he flowed forward, two bone swords grew from his wrists. He grasped them in his hands. Energy pulsed into the bone swords. Energy that would help him cause a great deal of extra damage.

The lion-octopus-nightmare beast’s tentacle-like limbs whipped out, a second set of spikes forming where the first had been as it screeched. The spear was still lodged firmly through its abdomen. It almost moved like a frightened, wounded animal, but behind that pain and fear Xavier saw something else—

Rage.

Rage like he’d felt from the screech of the second Void Dungeon beast.

Whatever these things were, they weren’t just hungry, they were angry.

As the tentacle-limbs flashed out, spikes reaching for him and beginning to glow, Xavier’s swords flashed out in tandem, slicing through the limbs with ease. He flowed like water as he moved, the Reality Energy he’d burned making every one of his movements faster, crisper, stronger.

The Void Dungeon beast’s limbs fell to the ground until all six slapped onto the stone. Then Xavier leapt, his large black dragon wings propelling him forward, and he slammed both swords straight into the beast’s skull. The swords met resistance, but nowhere near as much as when he’d used the spear.

The Reality Energy made him so much stronger.

The swords pierced through the beast’s skull and straight into its brain.

There was no kill notification, but Xavier didn’t need a kill notification to know this thing was dead.

Something pulsed, a sharp burst of energy he could feel through his Immortal Soul lens. For a moment Xavier worried this was some sort of self-destruct, that when he killed one of these monsters, they would go boom.

But that wasn’t what happened.

The energy pulsed up through his bone swords, into his energy pores, and snaked its way through his channels and straight into his brain.

Personal System Integration has reached 35%.
