Cara opened her eyes. She was in a bed, not her bed, and she was alone. She sat up, needing a moment to recognize the room she was in. Anya's spare room, the visible clothing draped at the foot of the bed made it obvious.

She was alone though, Gwen wasn't there. Her geas twinged, she was supposed to be guarding Gwen, she wasn't supposed to be sleeping so deeply that she didn't know where her charge was. Gwen was safe though, she remembered Gwen saying so right before she…

She'd collapsed. She'd pushed herself to be strong, to stand up to Bern. He'd pushed back, she'd had to back down. The injuries hadn't been bad enough to take her out though, not on their own. She was still sitting in bed, she hadn't moved, hadn't felt the need to move and make sure Gwen was safe. That wasn't like her. She'd been hurt before, woken up from actual injuries and needed to find her even when she could barely walk.

Every time she'd needed to rest and Gwen didn't, where she'd had to be separated for her own health, someone had been there to watch over Gwen, even if not officially. Someone who she knew could handle whatever happened better than she could. She was in their dorm, in their spare room. She wouldn't be there if Gwen hadn't brought her there. So Gwen was with them. Anya and Seb could handle Bern, they'd handled her more easily than she wanted to admit, and she knew they were holding back Bladesong if nothing else.

Would Gwen stay with them? She realized her hand was on the doorknob, she'd moved the moment she pictured Gwen leaving their side. She knew that wouldn't happen, but her geas didn't. She had to confirm that Gwen was still there, still in the common area outside the room.
