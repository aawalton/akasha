"Honestly, it's kind of weird how well game terminology fits up with how magical girls actually fight," I comment offhandedly. Consequently, we end up in the roles best suited to complementing our team, and the DPS, tank, support triad standard to multiplayer games itself exists because it is the most efficient and logical division of labor available to groups playing to win."

Holy moly. That was not off the cuff. That was well thought-out. Those were the words of someone who has thought about this before! That means…!

"Oh my gosh," I squee. "Su-san, are you a gamer!?"

The closet furry blushes.

"…It is not an uncommon hobby for Earth Guardians," she admits. "Those of us without families need some way to spend our time when we are recovering from combat or training. One of the internet cafes back home knew I was an Earth Guardian and let me play for free. My old team spent most of their time in the library, however."

"Oh, that's neat," I say. "How are they doing? If that's okay to ask."

"They are alive, if that is what you mean," Amaterasu says flatly. "I simply volunteered to come here because our area was asked if we could spare reinforcements given the issues with your Dark Rebellion."

"Do you miss them?" I ask, mostly just making conversation.

The more I talk like a normal person, the more they might actually consider me a person! You know, hopefully. Amaterasu doesn't respond immediately, though, a small frown crawling its way onto her face.

"I'm not sure," she admits after a while. "We never really got along."

My response to those unexpectedly depressing words gets cut off by yet another unexpected burst of joy.

"Allocated time expended. Please proceed to the convergence site," Uma'gabo reports, and then vanishes again.

Oh shit, we're just going for it. Fair enough, I guess. Though come to think of it, I've never actually seen Amaterasu's transformation. It's pretty standard, as magical girl transformations go: a blinding light obscures all details, the clothing all explodes, and a fancy new outfit replaces the old in a showy sequence. Near the end, however, are a bit of extra additions: namely the glowing silhouette of her body growing wolf ears, an adorable fluffy tail, and shifting the structure of her legs to a fully digitigrade walking style.

Well! That last one is new! That's totally my fault, isn't it? I'd apologize, but now she has toe beans, and as we all know I am huge fan of beans. I could never truly bring myself to regret this.

"Yᴇᴀʀɴɪɴɢ Fʟᴀsʜ Lɪɢʜᴛsᴘᴇᴇᴅ Aᴍᴀᴛᴇʀᴀsᴜ!"

She doesn't seem to have any trouble standing upright once the transformation finally ends and the glow of the room returns to normal, and nobody else seems terribly surprised, so I'm extra sure she's been this way ever since our little incident. Nothing like having the representation of your true soul maimed and coming back fuzzier to indicate that there's absolutely nothing going on in your subconscious that needs to be addressed.

"Let's go," Amaterasu orders, gripping onto Veritas and Aurora via each other's wrists and flying to the teleporter room. Castalia and I follow (by which I mean I am once again lifted up like a shark in a tornado and brought along in Castalia's wake), and soon enough we're rushing toward a rapidly darkening part of the sky.

"Thank you," Castalia randomly says to me.

Yeah, she's stronger than anyone, but it's not just raw power that let her save my life. She's so much more than that.

I'll make sure she understands how special she is, one way or another. That girl deserves all the friends.
