Asher’s mind went blank as the corpse screamed at him, his vision going blurry and his fingers loosening their grip around his daggers as the world almost seemed to zoom out in a way. Everything seemed so strangely small, from his very own body to the situation he was currently in, almost like it didn’t really matter. Rather than try to tear his hand free or stab at the corpse holding him, Asher merely stood there, blank faced as the beings within the mist clawed at him and pulled him down.

But before he could be fully submerged within the swirling red mists, a glowing arrow smashed into the corpse’s face, knocking it back slightly yet somehow failing to penetrate through its withered skin. Even so, it was enough to stop the creature from screaming, and the world stopped expanding around him. The seemingly endless whispers of the mist made it almost impossible to think, but Asher managed to hold onto enough consciousness to understand what he needed to do.

Triggering Complete Resilience’s purge effect, Asher gasped as his mind snapped back to his body. With a start, he lashed out with Crescent, the masterwork blade gleaming in the moonlight as it arced forward and connected with the creature’s throat.

Before stopping dead in its tracks.

Asher stared in shock at the withered corpse before him, the monster still clutching that pulsing gem to its chest as it recovered from Samantha’s arrow and stared at him once more. The crimson mist pouring out from its eyes began flowing faster and faster, swirling up around the two of them and blinding him as countless hands continued dragging him down. At this point, only his Belt of Strength was giving him the strength he needed to fight off the dragging hands and remain on his feet. He didn’t know exactly what was going to happen to him if he lost his footing and fell, but he figured it wouldn’t be good.

Enough of this!

Tossing Crescent into his rift, Asher grabbed his void dagger and ripped it free from its sheath, not sparing the time to be careful with the deadly blade like he normally did. His entire world had become nothing more than clutching hands and deadly crimson as the mist swirled all around him, but one of the hands was far more solid than the others. Without hesitating, he lashed out where he knew the corpse’s arm to be, the small slice of void serving as his blade severing clean through the oddly durable flesh like it was made of paper.

The almost-silent whispers that had been growing all this time erupted into a cacophony of screams so loud Asher nearly fell to his knees as he stumbled backward. He felt warm liquid dripping down the side of his head, and he gritted his teeth at the realization he was bleeding out his ears. Yet despite the fact he wasn’t able to hear his team shouting at one another or even the flapping of Samantha’s wings any longer, he could still quite clearly hear the screaming in his head as whatever monster controlled the mists suffered from his blow.

I doubt this thing even can bleed to death… Which means one cut’s not going to do it.

Lashing out in a straight line of compressed space toward where the corpse had been all this time, Asher stabbed forward, aiming to drive his void dagger straight into the monster’s head. Yet based on the lack of change to the screaming and the still-swirling mist, the corpse had wisened up and moved. With a curse, Asher triggered his Boots of Levitation, forcing himself up and out of the churning mists to buy himself a moment to breathe and regain his sight.

A quick glance at his friends showed they weren’t having much more luck than he was when it came to their own fights. Moxy and Rulfar were fighting all but back to back, blood dripping from their own ears as they no doubt relied on Kroult’s mind-linking spell to predict what the other would do as they cleaved and punched through an endless army of misty figures. Kroult himself was huddled between them, the fires in his eyes burning brightly as he did something. Asher wasn’t sure what exactly he was doing, but the figures seemed to slow as they approached the three of them, giving Moxy and Rulfar the time they needed to fight. Whatever the spell was doing, it was the only reason the two of them hadn’t been utterly overwhelmed already.

Samantha was shooting around in the air, banking and twisting as fast as her wings would let her. A number of misty beings were chasing after her, flying far quicker than they could walk as they chased the hunter through the sky. It looked as though she’d already run out of arrows and was focusing almost exclusively on evasion, every now and again lashing out with a wing and dispersing one of the figures as it got too close.

It was only at that point Asher realized the corpse’s seemingly indestructible hand was still clenched around his wrist, and he frowned as he grabbed at it and failed to pry it off.

What the hell is this thing made out of?!

Deciding to leave the hand attached for the moment like some cheap Halloween bracelet, Asher turned his focus back to the churning pool of mist below him. What had once been a pool of relatively calm mist now swirled and shifted, revealing hands and howling faces as they reached up and tried grabbing at his ankles. He didn’t know why some of them seemed capable of flight while others were trapped on the ground, and he didn’t care. The running theme of the wild realm seemed to be that nothing had to make sense. Ignoring the grasping hands, he leaned on Secret Seeker to try and help him figure out where the corpse had run off to, but the skill came up empty. If he couldn’t find the corpse again, their only option was to retreat and regroup, and there was no telling if this portion of the wild realm would remain the same when they returned.

As Samantha’s shadow shot by overhead, Asher cursed as he realized he was an idiot. Here he was trying to come up with the best way to find the one solid corpse in a pool of mist, when they literally had an artifact that would do just that for them.

“Samantha!” he shouted, sighing as he realized that wasn’t going to work. Even if their ears hadn’t all been burst open, the howling in their heads was loud enough that he couldn’t hear himself think, let alone what anyone else was saying. As he felt her intent to bank and evade to the left a split second before she even did it, he realized Kroult’s connection spell may have given him a solution to that problem.

Focusing on Samantha flying around overhead as hard as he could, he thought about driving the void dagger into his own chest, even going to do so before stopping the motion at the last second. Just as he’d hoped, Samantha nearly gave herself whiplash as her head yanked around to look at him, her eyes wide and panicked until she realized he hadn’t actually gone through with what he’d been intending to do. Now that he had her attention, Asher pointed to the artifact in her hand and tried to mouth the word as he gestured for her to toss it to him.

Luckily for him, Samantha was rather exceptional at charades, and she nodded. Spinning through a few misty figures and breaking them all apart, she shot by overhead, dropping the artifact into his waiting hands.

Asher quickly shoved his fire dagger into his rift before he surpassed his artifact limit, before snatching the sensor out of the air. While Kroult’s warming spell was enough to keep them alive outside of the depression filled with freezing mist, the cold was far more concentrated down in the hole. Deciding Complete Resilience would just have to be enough, Asher deactivated his boots, falling back into the sea of howling faces and clutching hands as the artifact pointed him in the right direction.

Before he even touched the ground again hands clawed at him, and Asher swung the void dagger around, scything through misty beings and dispersing them with the slightest of touches. The void dagger actually seemed slightly worse than his flaming dagger at keeping the beings at bay, but Asher didn’t have any other options. Even as his blood froze in his veins and his body began to slow, he forced himself forward, knowing every step he took was one step closer to ending all this chaos. The depression was only so wide and the corpse didn’t seem to be moving around, which meant all he needed to do was reach it and finish it off for good.

Smiles better appreciate the hell out of us after all this…

One shaking step after another carried Asher through the howling mists, the errant slashes from his dagger growing less and less frequent as his body continued to slow down. He would have cursed his past self for making him immune to death energy instead of cold if it weren’t for the fact that he could quite easily feel the sheer concentration of death energy contained within the mist-filled depression. If any living thing took even the slightest dip into the mist like he was it would have died in a fraction of a second, evolved resistance be damned. It almost felt as though the King of Death’s powerful Death Aura that had stretched out for miles upon miles had been concentrated and poured into this one-hundred-foot wide hole. At the very least he was extremely confident that this gem was exactly what they were looking for, and he didn’t have the slightest worry that it wouldn’t be enough to keep Smiles running on his own.

Yanking a potion of Lesser Restoration from his rift and chugging the thing down, Asher focused on the brief spark of warmth from the magical liquid as he pushed ever onward, knowing he was nearing the edge of the pit. Richard’s artifact did exactly as advertised as he felt the thing directly ahead of him, and even if he couldn’t see more than six inches in front of his face as the figures howled and tried to drag him down, that didn’t matter. Having been counting his steps ever since dropping back into the mist, Asher realized he was roughly twenty feet from the edge. There was nowhere to hide and nowhere to run, and with a shaking, frostbitten arm, Asher reached up and slashed out through a compressed tunnel of space, making sure to aim high so he wouldn’t accidentally cut through the gem they were doing all this for.

He couldn’t actually feel the void blade connect with anything, but seeing as the howling screams all stopped at the same moment, he felt pretty confident he’d managed to find his target.

Like a switch had been flipped, the churning, crimson mist collapsed all at once, sinking to the ground and dissipating into the soil like water in a desert. The beings chasing after Samantha in the air faded away with one last barrage of whispers, and the ones that had crawled out of the depression and had been swarming Moxy, Rulfar, and Kroult did the same. Asher breathed heavily as he took in the sight of the one solid corpse lying on the ground, the top half of its head having been sliced clean off thanks to his void dagger. The being still had the pulsing gem clutched to its chest with one hand, the other still firmly lodged around Asher’s wrist.

“Thank God,” Asher muttered, feeling his ears repair themselves in real time now that he was no longer being bombarded with screams. Raising his shaky, frostbitten fingers, he grinned and gave his team a wave with the arm that still had an undead fist clasped around his wrist.

“This might be coming a little late… but does anyone need a hand?”
