Thalion watched with wide eyes as the bronze-colored dragon god stepped out of the statue. Had he been trapped in there the whole time? He looked exactly like the statue—tall, muscular, with backward-curving horns and glowing eyes. The dragonkin looked at Thalion expectantly, who could only stammer:“Wanna have a drink now?”The dragonkin threw his head back and laughed heartily. His laugh sounded exactly like the voice Thalion had heard in his mind, except now it was real, no longer just a telepathic message.“Haha, I like you. But enough fun. I can’t stay like this for long, and my mental transmissions while inside that thing were recorded. Now I can mostly speak freely. There are just a few names I can’t mention. This system is owned by a massive empire ruled by incredibly powerful gods. The gods inside this system are nothing compared to them. However, a few things Amun said weren’t entirely accurate. For instance, the god who originally created the system was murdered a long time ago. No one really understands the full details, which has led to some weird, unfixable issues, like the rule that you need to return spatial rings, and so on.There must always be one operator inside the system, someone to talk to people and make adjustments, especially when system rules are broken. Just so you know, I’m not here by choice. I rose to power far too quickly for the royals’ liking, so they imprisoned me here for five terms. And believe me, that's way longer than you think. One term is about a thousand eras. Every thousand eras, the system harvests the ‘sprouts’, which means every living being. The buildings and artifacts remain, though. That means, if you're old enough, you can find some pretty advanced stuff.By the way, this is my fifth term, so I’m almost free,” the dragonkin said, summoning a chair beneath himself and sitting down, noting how Thalion's eyes were practically popping out of their sockets.“Ah yes, sitting after trillions of years stuck in stone... you have no idea how good that feels,” he said with a deep sigh, eyes closed in contentment. Then he continued.“Now, back to the important info. Another thing Amun Ra got slightly wrong was the claim that the system spans multiple dimensions. That’s not quite true. It all takes place in the same dimension, but the system contains four pocket realms, each of equal size and power level. When the system nears harvest time, it opens the gateways, so to speak. That’s when the powerful gods begin fighting over territory to gain a power boost which ensures a high-quality harvest. At their level, quality matters far more than quantity. That event, by the way, is coming very soon. I don’t have an exact time, but it’s always a massive system-wide event. Even weaker beings like you can participate. Right now, we need to talk about the important topic: how to destroy the system’s seal and why you can actually trust me,” the dragon god said in a calm, relaxed tone.“Hmmm,” was all Thalion managed to get out. He sat completely tensed up in his chair, clearly overwhelmed. The dragon god didn’t seem to care and went on.“As I said, I’m imprisoned here and I don’t exactly like the royals, as you might imagine. Five terms is an absurdly long time to be stuck in a statue. I do wonder what my wife has been up to in the meantime...” He trailed off for a moment, slightly lost in thought, before catching himself and continuing.“What I’m trying to say is: I don’t give a damn how the royals’ harvest turns out. One escapee means nothing in the grand scheme of things. That black crystal you received, the one that teaches how to open portals to other dimensions, also came from someone who managed to escape in my second term. He was eventually killed in a war between gods in a water dimension, if I recall correctly. But that’s not the point. The point is, no one really cares if one weakling escapes."What’s important and what no one has ever managed before, is destroying the seal. I can’t see it, so I can’t tell you where it is on your soul body. You’ve seen some of the other dimensions. They’re much harder to survive and grow in compared to the system, which continues to nurture you. Make sure all your forms are in top condition and aim to place in the top one hundred in all three event quests or reach the top ten in one. If you manage that, you’ll definitely qualify for a divine class. If you don’t want to go that route, you can of course try to escape, but I doubt life will be any easier for you outside the system. Any questions so far?" the dragon god asked, eyeing Thalion’s expression, which looked like he had just bitten into a lemon.Ensure your favorite authors get the support they deserve. Read this novel on Royal Road."Well, yeah, there’d be one or two. First... why are you so sure no one gets hunted down after leaving the system?
