Sasha had heard about Johan from Yuna during their University days, albeit only in bits and pieces. Her senior wasn’t one to open up easily, but even she had times when she needed people to talk with.

However, Sasha had always believed that there should be something special about the guy, since her senior hadn’t talked about anyone else but him.

But the reality let her down when she dug into his information.

So, when she saw his name plastered all over the internet, thanks to Shadowman, it surprised her to the core. Still, the interest wasn’t so deep that she’d try to spy on him.

That was more on account of her senior, who had started to worry about this guy extensively; it had started to show on her face, too, and she would make a comment on it every few hours.

He has a tendency to get into trouble even when he tries to avoid it. Her senior’s words were spot on.

Seated before Duran, whose PR team definitely needed a huge raise, Sasha glanced at Johan. Since the initial words, about five minutes had passed.

They had only taken seat before Duran, his face getting more twisted by the second, and the intimidation from the four hunters in the room kept on rising not so subtly.

They were all towering hunks of muscle and rough faces. Duran himself looked like the typical musclehead with a slightly handsome, tanned face.

Johan’s face hadn’t moved much since the initial shock. He had come here with good intentions, trying to help senior Yuna, not that she needed any. But she thought it was an admirable attempt.

However, the situation didn’t help.

“Would you like anything to drink? We have an amazing barista from Rome and an excellent tea brewer from Unified Han.” Duran’s secretary, Albert, asked in the awkward silence.

He was also quite famous alongside Duran, but his competence was real. There was no doubt that his managerial skills were the reason for Duran’s high-class image. And, this slick looking greasy haired guy wasn’t so simple either; his gaze, hidden behind the square glasses, only looked at people like an appraiser would look at items.

A pure profit-seeking bastard.

“Stop wasting my time, just sign so we can get this over with.” Duran leaned back into his golden chair.

And his four bodyguards were A-Ranks. Sasha glanced at the contract, and Albert tried to explain it too.

In a nutshell, S.M. Foods, the company under Duran, wanted Johan to exclusively sell the vegetables to them. They were trying to get him before the market put an actual price on the items, and it exploded in popularity. Sasha didn’t know about the vegetables, so she couldn’t tell if they were trying to finesse Johan or not.

Well, that truly didn’t matter in the current situation.

Duran’s magik was on full display. He was a B-rank Hunter, at least that wasn’t a lie. Along with the other hunters in the room, any normal non-combatant hunter would fold under such pressure and sign.

To Sasha, however, this was but a breeze. She had stayed alongside S-ranks and A-ranks for as long as she could remember, and even made her first trip to the Tower recently.

Even now, she wasn’t worried. Her drones had already taken position around the Hotel building, and the display projected on her retina showed her that Renolds had already entered the building.

She had told him about this visit, just in case something happened. Carefully, she slipped out the white flower hidden under her blue shirt sleeve, onto her palm. The flower would form a barrier around them for a minute as soon as it felt her magik power, enough time for Renolds to handle the people in the room.

However, the reason she hadn’t made a move yet was because of Johan.

She initially expected him to panic, but his face had stayed still.

Frozen in fear?

No, she would’ve noticed if that were the case. His heartbeat, breathing, and even his eyes, nothing looked out of order according to the Hunter phone’s monitoring system.

There was no way this guy came here without a plan, she could feel it. Sasha wanted to see what made him so confident; that’s why she had Renolds on standby in the room right below them.

Is it the skill he uses to vanish from the Hunter Mart? It had shocked her quite a bit when that happened at first; she flew into a panic thinking he had been kidnapped, but since he arrived back shortly after, she decided not dig into it anymore after Senior Yuna’s orders.

Could he take her with him using that skill? If he did use that skill to get away and leave her behind, she’d be quite disappointed.

He’s smiling. But under the sharp glares of five hunters, he calmly pulled out his phone. Almost too calm.

When he opened the phone app, Sasha thought that was a smart move. If he could call the Agni Clan, the ones he made a deal with, this would blow over.

It seemed that Albert had noticed that too, but when he arrived near Johan, probably trying to stop him, his jaw slacked open.

The reason for that fear followed soon after. It wasn’t a voice call, but a video call.

“Hey deary, you’ve been calling me too often these days.”

The voice of the Association President, Lady Merilyn Faust, was a unique one. It had softness and playfulness, but with a level of intimidation that was hard to forget. Even more so when one could see her calm smile and covered eyes.

Normal people called it hubris, but Hunters called it instinct.

Any decently powerful Hunter who had heard her voice couldn’t help but tense up. Because they could feel it, the fear gripping their bodies. The fear of Merilyn Faust, the strongest person in the country.

Even S-Rank feared meeting her eyes when she made official appearances.

The image of when Senior Yuna met her for the first time, during the graduation ceremony, was still fresh in her mind.

She’s a monster. Yuna had said, trembling like a child.

And this bastard just called such a person like it was a normal Tuesday!

“I just missed you, especially after dealing with such unreasonable people.” Johan’s tone was light. He almost chuckled, it seemed.

“Well, you can call me anytime. But I’ll get mad if you only call me when you are in a pinch.” Lady Merilyn showed a playful smile.

“Sorry about that, I’ll make it up to you soon.” Blood drained from the faces of everyone with each casual word; even Sasha felt scared.

“Fufu, I am looking forward to that.” Even the mouse became terrifying when it stood on a Dragon’s shoulder. “Now, who is it, the one giving you trouble?”

Especially, with a Dragon as infamous as Merilyn Faust.

***

Stepping into the lobby from the elevator, leaving behind the pale Duran and Albert, Johan could only sigh.

If only he hadn’t raised the flag. Well, wouldn’t it be amazing if real life were so convenient?

Who would’ve thought that Duran would turn out to be such a big fraud? Media Manipulation wasn’t a lie after all.

It made Johan tremble, wondering what else he had been manipulated about in all those years. Well, he doubted it would be anything worse than what he learned about rifts.

Still, calling Merilyn may have been overkill. But the sight of the people in the room made it worth it. This was what people made connections for.

His initial plan was to call Lucas or Adrian in case someone tried to strong-arm him into a deal, but he decided to go with Merilyn because he didn’t want something like this to happen again.

No doubt Duran’s team wouldn’t leak this, but Johan had recorded the entire thing on his phone, and Sasha said she had the video recording too.

Once the reporters get their hands on this, it’d blow up all over. People eat up stories like these.

He would ask Sasha how she got the video, but she was too busy being impressed by him.

“That was cool.” Her springy steps reached the door; it was almost like she pulled off a great heist.

“I must admit, I didn’t expect a bold move like that from you,” Renolds said, following him to the driveway before the Hotel’s entrance. Totally ignoring the passed-out staff along the way.

“When did you even arrive?” Johan had many questions as he sat in the car waiting for them.

This Hotel boasted about its high security, saying not even A-Ranks could infiltrate it.

“Is it important?” Renolds tilted his head, taking a seat beside him. “Just know that I came with good intentions.”

Johan looked at the unconscious guards along the way and shook his head. That might’ve been the best answer he could get out of this guy.

It was for his protection, and he felt glad, but also vexed in a way.

As the car left the Hotel’s main gate and entered the Highway, he finally relaxed into the heated seat. Facing intimidation from four A-Ranks was no joke; the old him would’ve melted in place.

But staying in proximity of a Guardian Beast had made him more resistant to strong aura, paired with the daily run-ins with the S-Ranks, getting used to it was a matter of time.

It still felt like yesterday when he trembled at Lucas’s sudden appearance. Now, he won’t even feel shocked if he met Yi Do-Yun the next day.

Human adaptability was indeed next level.

“Send me the voice file, no wait, I’ll take it myself.” Sasha had unfolded her phone into a paper-thin tablet and started working on it.

“I am more scared of you than Duran.” Johan couldn’t wait to get a different Hunter Phone.

“Don’t worry, I won’t peek at your search history or research folders.” She giggled like a goblin.

“That’s the least of my worries, you know?” He only shook his head as Sasha’s fingers moved at blinding speed across the screen.

That’s when the driver, the same guy who had driven them here, joined in.

“As much as I’d love to see my brother get dragged through the mud, I’ll have to ask you to stop that.” The guy had a rather suave voice.

Johan hadn’t heard someone else that screamed rich this much since Eugene.

But before that, did this guy just call Duran his brother?

Sasha didn’t even look at the driver, who removed his hat and mask, revealing deep brown hair and lavender eyes. Her eyes focused on Renolds, who spared no effort to hide behind Johan and failed spectacularly.

“Well, the little lady asked why you sneaked out. And Agni Clan also sent out a message, spotting you and Johan leaving the Mart.” Giving up in the end, Renolds shrugged.

“I am not helping you again.” Sasha pouted, closing her tablet.

“Please don’t be like that. I need help with the taxes.” Renolds’ joking plea went unanswered.

“Still, why is this one here?” Johan finally looked at Duran’s younger brother, although he was much more pleasant to the eye, compared to his musclehead brother; he didn’t want to see this guy either.

“That cuts deep, even more than being left on read.” Seemed like this guy also liked theatrics. But it didn’t take long for him to start speaking. “Name is Bruce Seong, by the way.”

“I was called regarding a leak of information from my camp, information about your magik vegetables.” He looked at Johan through the rearview mirror. The lane was empty, even then, he drove at a slow speed. “I happened to get the information from a nurse in Agni Clan’s Hospital, and Albert bought it off from one of my people. Mr. Adrian was pretty pissed on the phone, quite charming when the silent ones get angry.”

His tone was quite condescending, but Johan had more to worry about.

Privacy didn’t exist in the Hunter World. He had learned that lesson thoroughly from his short time in this circle.

I have to change my plans asap, or it won’t be long before I get kidnapped or worse. Johan gulped. Lucas’s basement didn’t look so bad anymore.

But there seemed to be a bigger problem on hand.

“You missed the exit for Sector A,” Johan pointed out the car window. He may be a son of a big family, but he should at least focus on what he did.

“Oh, we aren’t going to Sector A.” Bruce chuckled.

“Huh?” Johan looked between Bruce and Sasha, who had a hand on his shoulder.

“You are in big trouble, Johan.” The sympathetic expression on her face didn’t soothe any of his worries.

Two hours later, when the car stopped at the door of the Eternal Peace Guild, Johan understood how fucked he was.

He tried to run away on the spot. But when Sasha, a girl half his size, pulled him through the automatic doors like a fish, he decided to just give up on everything.

In the end, he had to put out the ultimate move from his arsenal. A move so strong and lethal that he had always reserved it for the worst of situations.

Yuna didn’t like attracting attention, so if he gave an overdramatic apology, like the one where he confessed his mistakes in a dogeza while others watched, she’d cave and spare him the sermon.

It’ll all be according to the plan. Johan smirked.

He ran in as soon as the door to the guild master's office opened, but as he was about to show his perfected knee slide into a dogeza, Yuna had grabbed him by his collar.

“Eh?” Johan blinked in horror, hanging midair.

“Don’t even try it.” She carried him to the sofa like a stray cat, eyes sharper than her words.

In the end, he couldn’t escape the sermon. Looked like he had to come up with a new tactic after all.

Yuna had grown too much.
