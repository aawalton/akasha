Quite a few messages popped up in Luke's interface the moment he returned to Earth and cut the thread to the pink portal behind him, but it wasn't before he'd escaped the Singers and almost reached the guild that he had the time to make his way through them. Milla informed him of Lara's demise and how the Architect had met her end, followed by a warning to stay vigilant in case Integrated Solutions Group attacked. Stifling a chuckle, he approached the plaque leading into guild headquarters. If ISG attacked this group full of frustrated powerhouses, they would never know what hit them.

Lemira: "You're back! We are in dire need of aid, Luke!"

Luke: "Yeah, I was just going to the guild."

Lemira: "Don't. Come to me. Your companions are doing their best against the onslaught, but they grow weary!"

Luke: "What friends?"

He had a sneaking suspicion already, but Lemira confirmed it.

Lemira: "Penny and Mateo have arrived. I owe them my life."

"Hey, guys," Luke said, waving to the rest of the group. "Who wants to go kill some demons with some old friends of ours? Penny and Mateo need our help!"

Ray perked up at hearing those names. "They're here?" He looked up, shaking his head. "That explains the rain."

"What about the Intruders?" Curtis asked. He'd returned to himself at that point, but was even grumpier than usual.

"Demons first. Lemira says they'll take over if we don't exterminate them."

Curtis narrowed his eyes and shook his head. "I'll keep an eye on the Intruders. You deal with the other monsters."

Nick sighed and looked after his brother as Curtis disappeared down the street. "I'd better go with him."

"He'll be fine," Ray said. "Don't put your guys at risk."

"You're right," Nick grunted, shaking his head. "I just don't want to see him push too far."

"We'll make sure he stays out of trouble," Ray said.

Nick gave him a doubtful look, then shook his head and reached out to touch the plaque. "I need to have a conversation with Milla about what we're going to do with ISG. You kids stay out of trouble."

"We could use your help with the demons," Luke said.

"I'm not in the habit of running headfirst into things without a plan. Once I've got a handle on things here, we'll see where we stand."

"Fair enough," Luke said, understanding the guildmaster of Phoenix Phalanx's reasoning. He hadn't read Lemira's messages, so he didn't have a clue as to the seriousness of the situation, and Nick was responsible for a lot of people other than himself.

Once Luke and Ray were by themselves, Luke sighed. "What a mess."

"What was that pink portal about?" Ray asked.

"It taught me a few things."

Ray frowned. "Like what?"

"Want to see?"

"Yeah," Ray said with a grin. "Show me!"

"You asked for it!" Luke said with a laugh as he extended a thread of mana to Ray's Meta-Heart. "Here we go!"

Since he was able to weavestep with another person, it made sense that it should also work when traveling the weave using that same skill. Just to be safe, Luke readied himself for Ray needing a whole lot of healing, but it turned out the passenger wasn't affected at all. By this point, Luke could use Manipulate Thread and use echoes to move through metaphysical space without missing a beat. The pain was still there, and some internal damage, but he'd become an expert in healing that particular ailment these past couple of weeks, and dealing with pain was second nature at this point.

They both emerged on top of a building right next to Lemira, who looked frightened out of her wits, and he couldn't blame her. Another massive demon was right next to the building, throwing punches and swatting the air as if trying to catch an annoying fly, except this fly bit back. Bolts of lightning slammed into the demon's chest and face again and again, each one followed by immediate thunder that made the building under their feet quake. Each time the darkened sky lit up, someone flying around the demon came into view, looking as if she rode the storm like some goddess out of myth. Penny. Luke grinned.

Ray leaned over and puked, but the rain would wash it away in minutes.

"Lemira," Luke said.

She jumped with a shriek, and her llama appeared under her, as if she had readied herself to flee before turning. "Luke? You move faster than I anticipated."

"How does she have mana for all that?" he asked Lemira, pointing to Penny. Words of power boomed with the lightning as she chanted, calling the storm forward and bending it to her will.

"You would need to ask her, but I believe she is in need of help, and soon. Mateo is on the ground, struggling against waves of lesser demons. Penny's lightning is damaging the demon, but the danger of her being struck is too great."

"I'll help her," Luke said, looking over to Ray. "Feeling better?"

Ray shook his head, dry-heaved, drew in a deep breath, then got to his feet. "That wasn't fun."

"Can you help Mateo?"

"Yeah, yeah," Ray said, walking over to the lip of the roof. Rather than make some sort of ninja-like assassin move and leap down, he lay down on the wet, cold metal and rolled until he fell out of sight with what sounded like a sigh.

Luke turned to face the massive demon again. "How many crystals have you gathered?"

"Three," Lemira said, folding the llama back into her stable with an air of embarrassment.

"I have two," Luke said. "How many do we need?"

"Nine would be my guess. One for each layer of hell."

He groaned. "That'll take forever. We don't even know where to find them."

"I know."

"How?" he asked, turning to look at her again.

"They call to me. The infernal side of me longs to join them and feel that power blossom. Some will want me on their side, while others would prefer my death."

"But you won't go to them," Luke said. It wasn't a question.

She shook her head. "I won't."

"You like it here, I take it?"

Lemira turned and faced him with an earnest expression on her face and took a step forward. "You've welcomed me into your home and introduced me to the guild and your friends. I want you to know I would never betray such a kindness."

"Of course," Luke began.

"But," Lemira continued, "but that was the initial arrangement. This system event. I don't know how, but I'm sure my father set this in motion somehow. Infernal Flux holds much sway in The Greater System, and my presence here might very well be what allowed for demonkind to invade Earth, despite my best efforts to block it."

"You were supposed to help them come here? To betray us?"

A much stronger bolt of lightning slammed into the demon's face, right between its eyes, and the monstrous being reeled back with a roar that thrummed in Luke's chest. Small globs of orange sludge bled from the resulting wound. Rather than fall toward the ground, those globs rose into the air, then shot forward toward Penny, chasing her through the air. Dodging them, she soared past on a strong, shrieking gale.

"A little help?" she shouted without slowing down. "Shithead!"

"I was supposed to anchor Infernal Flux to this system using an item. Not much different from how the Singers arrived here, would be my guess. But I destroyed the item. I thought that was it, but my father must have seen that as a possibility and accounted for it somehow."

"What an asshole," Luke said with a smile. "As long as you didn't have anything to do with it."

"Y-you believe me?" Lemira asked.

"I do."

She shook her head as if exasperated. "Why?"

"Why what?"

She pointed at her horns, now more visible than ever with how wet and matted her hair had become in the rain. "I am half-demon in origin. You do not know me, and when I have probed your friends with questions, all of them mention you being betrayed again and again."

"Yeah, well…"

"Well, what?" she asked.

Luke shrugged, but felt he owed her some sort of answer, even if he hadn't quite put the jumble of feelings and thoughts into any satisfactory order just yet. "It's... I'm feeling... I have been feeling cold. Do you know what I mean? Like I'm having trouble with empathy, with caring. That's not who I am. Not who I want to be or to become. So, yeah, maybe I am a little too trusting, but I've seen the alternative, and it is not the kind of future I want."

She looked at him for a long while before nodding. "I think I understand, but know that I will work to earn that trust. You won't regret it."

"No," he said, "I don't believe I will."

The Fallen Shepherd was at the forefront of his mind since the revelation that the bastard was still out there. Where Luke couldn't say, because he'd never healed him, never touched the corruption in that way, but there was no doubt in his mind. Relian would turn up again sooner or later. For the moment, though, there were a few more immediate concerns to focus on.

A twinge of pain shot up his spine even as he started toward the edge of the roof, and he healed a spot within himself that had fallen into decay. Penny avoided being caught in the massive demon's huge jaw, but looked to be about spent. How she'd accomplished this much, Luke couldn't begin to understand, but it was time she got a little help. It was time Luke saw how much infernal mana he could learn to withstand before it burned him from the inside out.
