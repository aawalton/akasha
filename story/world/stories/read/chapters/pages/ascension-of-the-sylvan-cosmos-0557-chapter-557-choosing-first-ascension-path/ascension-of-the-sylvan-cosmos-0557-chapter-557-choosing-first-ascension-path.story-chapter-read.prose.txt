For now, Liu Shu kept this option as a sure option. Still, she knew that she would start struggling right away after reading the next two. “Alright, System, shoot the next one.”

Liu Shu’s mind short-circuited when she read this Ascension Path. “...What? What is this?” She knew that this path would be strong the moment she read the description of her <Universal Tree> title. After all, it said that she had awakened something called <Eternal Core>.

Therefore, since the very beginning, Liu Shu had intended to choose this path regardless of its specialness. However… “System, didn’t you say that anything related to time was strictly prohibited? What am I reading here, then?”

[Tianlian Liu Shu has achieved the ability to gain an Ascension Path without system limitations. Therefore, many of the prohibitions can’t be applied. Since Tianlian Liu Shu originally had the <Eternal> attribute sealed within her, it is impossible not to offer this option.]

Liu Shu heard the usually cold and emotionless tones, but she could swear that there was some sourness in them. “...You sound resentful for some reason, even when the tone hasn’t changed.”

“Whoa!” Liu Shu almost jumped in fright. “To think that there would be an option that could tempt me after reading the <Eternal> species… This path is also incredibly strong. Especially the <Resurrection> skill… It’s extremely tempting.”

What Liu Shu feared beyond her own strength was her family dying. So, this species was something that she would love to acquire. With it, she would become semi-immortal, and she would be able to grant such semi-immortality to those around her.

The more she thought about it, the more tempted she became. “But…” Looking at the <Eternal> option, it was clearly a special path that couldn’t appear during other Ascensions because only during this one was she able to ignore System restrictions.

“Ugh…” As she mulled deeply, she noticed something unusual. “...Wisdom, why are you so silent? Wouldn’t you usually have recommended me a path to take?”

[Wisdom: Tianlian Liu Shu must choose her own Ascension Path without outside interference. It is not a place for a <Skill> to dictate or suggest anything. Please, trust yourself and choose.]

Liu Shu listened to Wisdom and smiled faintly. Clearly, Wisdom had a path in mind that it wanted to recommend quite badly. However, they were holding back and leaving Liu Shu to make her own choice. “I see.” Liu Shu smiled. “Thank you, Wisdom.”

The skill didn’t answer and left Liu Shu to ruminate by herself. After thinking for a while, she made a summary.

“Dimensional Universal Tree is a path that focuses on ruling space itself. It is a path that will help me expand the quickest.”

“Eternal Universal Tree is clearly a path that focuses on oneself. The individual strength this path provides is probably the highest, and it also gives me access to the prohibited element that, according to the system, I have sealed within me.”

Liu Shu thought back to all those years when she revealed her affinities. ‘At that time, the System broke down and hid one element. Clearly, it was trying to reveal the Eternal Attribute, but it forcefully sealed it because it went against balance.’

“Finally, the Reincarnation Universal Tree is one that focuses on developing life. With it, I will influence living beings the most and become part of them the best. It will most likely help me when things go wrong, allowing me to revive those dear to me. In a way, it is the most group-oriented path.”

Liu Shu hesitated. “Expansion, control, or life. I must choose one.”

In her mind, the voice of the unknown being echoed. The being that told her never to forget those around her. ‘But… can I really give up the ability to manipulate time for that?’

Time, which Liu Shu always lacked, was within the reach of her branch tips. Just a simple word from her, and she would accept it.

“... But the problem is… what if everyone gets killed?” Liu Shu feared that, in the future, she could face someone so strong that her life would be forfeit. If that was the case, what was the meaning of reincarnation? “If my family gets killed, won’t reincarnation be a curse rather than a blessing? I can see myself falling into an endless cycle of revenge, destroying all my enemies time and time again until all are defeated.”

With a sigh and complicated feelings, Liu Shu decided. “Sorry, gentle voice. This time, I want to be slightly selfish. I can protect them better the stronger I am. There is no need to avoid strengthening myself just to follow your words. My canopy is their eternal sanctuary, and I will become their guardian. System, I choose [Sapling Eternal Universal Tree]!”

[Are you sure?]

Liu Shu, who hardened her heart, instantly hesitated when those three words popped in front of her. “... Yes.” Inside her eyes, strength recovered, and she shouted. “Unlock my innate attribute and give me power to wield it!!”
