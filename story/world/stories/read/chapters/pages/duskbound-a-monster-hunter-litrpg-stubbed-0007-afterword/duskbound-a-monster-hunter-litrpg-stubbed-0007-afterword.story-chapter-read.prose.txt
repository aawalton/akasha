And with that, this series is officially over. It started as a writathon project that I thought I'd wrap up in 100k words before returning to the tower climber that was supposed to be my next story once Keiran ended. That... uh... did not happen, obviously. In the original version, there was no plot with the parasite monsters and the story would have ended once Velik found Chalin and killed him. The reason the chapters are shorter than my normal ones is also because of writathon--55k words/35 days of writathon = 1.6k words per chapter.

So what's next? Well, thanks in part to Audible's massive delay, book 2 still hasn't gone live in that format. It's been submitted for two weeks now and they haven't approved it, so...
