The low rumble grew in intensity. After a moment, it ceased, and all fell silent.

"That's it?" Ray asked, just as it picked back up with far more power. The street running from left to right cracked with a sound so loud Luke shied back, covering his ears. A building farther down the street shook and began to crumble as they watched in horror. Another followed, and soon both buildings were gone, reduced to enormous hills of rubble.

Milla: "Luke! What's happening out there? Is it them?"

Curtis: "What did you do this time?"

Lemira: "It has begun."

Luke (Guild): "This is a system event. I don't know what it means, but be prepared. Stay out of buildings."

Hannah: "I guess this means band practice is canceled."

Milla (Guild): "I have confirmation now that all portals closed when the earthquake hit."

Something caught Luke's eye, and he peered over at the wide crack in the middle of the street. Movement.

"Is that a hand?" he asked Ray, pointing. "Did someone fall in?"

Ray squinted and craned his neck. "Can't tell from here. I'll go check."

Luke: "Hey, Penny! How's Chicago? Any earthquakes?"

Penny: "Nope. Electricity is gone, and there's no Internet. I'd rather deal with earthquakes, honestly. Why?"

As Ray approached the spot where they'd seen movement, something emerged. It was a small humanoid figure, like a goblin, except its skin was ashen with thin streaks of a glowing orange Luke had hoped never to see again. Ray slammed a dagger into its head, killing it, but two more climbed up, then three after that.

Luke: "Looks like we have a situation on our hands here, Penny. I'll talk to you later. Stay safe."

Penny: "Situation? What situation?"

Luke didn't have time to answer. At that point, Ray was on the brink of being swallowed by a mass of what appeared to be small demons. They reached no higher than his hips, but they seemed to be endless. Rather than fight his way free, the Assassin disappeared into stealth.

His interface was drowning in messages about more appearances of the little bastards, with Milla calling for all members to gather at guild headquarters. Since Luke was already there, it was up to him to make sure members of The Band weren't overwhelmed the moment they reached what they thought would be safe harbor.

One message stood out from the others.

Lemira: "Oh no. Luke, I think they are coming. The demon realm draws near!"

"Ray!" he shouted, running toward the monsters. "We need to deal with these assholes!"

The increased activity in New York had been well underway before they returned to the world of the Lilineese and fought, then made a deal with Lemira's demonic father. But could this be a coincidence? Luke didn't think so.

Luke: "Are they coming for you?"

Lemira: "...I had not even considered that."

Luke: "Well?"

Rather than wait around for a reply, Luke closed the distance to the demonic horde boiling out of the street. Several lay dead at that point, killed by the stealthed Ray lashing out and feeding souls to the dagger he'd claimed when they last went up against demons. Ray's fighting style was ill-suited against many weaker opponents, but he still fought on, staying out of sight. That meant all demons focused on Luke instead. Great, just great.

Still not at the top of his game after coming within an inch of his life, and then stepping through the weave twice, Luke gritted his teeth and took a defensive position, holding up his arms in a boxer's stance as he stopped and waited for the small monsters to crash against him.

Lemira: "This might be my fault."

Luke: "Either way, we'll deal with it together."

Luke (Guild): "I'm outside guild headquarters. Lots of monsters here, just FYI."

Then they were on him. Unlike goblins, who looked like green-tinted humans with smushed faces, long ears, and spindly limbs, these small demons had a far more beast-like appearance. They wielded no weapons. Instead, each thick finger ended in a wicked-looking claw. Their arms and legs were thick with muscle, and there was no sign of intelligence in their orange-glowing eyes, just a ferocious need to hurt and maim. With elongated jaws and mouths full of long, pointed teeth, Luke knew he'd need to look out for bites as well as being raked by their clawed hands and feet. Worst of all, for some reason he couldn't quite explain, were their heads. Other than the shape of their lower jaws and the obvious lack of a nose, they appeared far too human for his liking. It was like all humans stuck in hell were now being allowed a break from eternal damnation, a break where they were allowed back into their old world to kill everyone and everything.

It was only when he felt the first one's teeth clamp down on his arm that Luke realized he was in real trouble. Demonic mana was not like a human's or even a monster's. Taking it within himself burned with a pain far worse than anything physical. Drinking it in put his soul under an open flame.

Cascading Threads slammed into the first demon and branched out to many others, and then more still, until Luke's mana fell too low for comfort. Only then did he pull them apart with a surgeon's precision, severing their infernal cores from the rest of what passed as mana channels in these types of monsters. Not all demons were monsters, Luke had to concede that with Lemira and her father proving that, but the ones now dying in droves before him were.

Around thirty demons died in the first wave, but that was far from enough. More surged out of the crack in the road. Luke steeled himself, healing the many small wounds from pointed teeth and sharp claws by sewing them shut using Needle of Life. This was not the time to waste resources by using healing mana, even if he had plenty stored in his Meta-Heart.

Nash (Guild): "What are we supposed to do about our nakama? I'm not leaving them in there!"

Milla (Guild): "Get back to guild headquarters for now. There's nothing you can do from there."

Tiffany (Guild): "How did that happen?"

Emma (Guild): "Shot a bunch of bastards. No one told me they bleed lava!"

Elijah (Guild): "We are close to being overrun!"

Milla (Guild): "Diverting some people to help you as well, Elijah!"

Fighting while keeping an eye on the messages streaming down his interface was impossible, so Luke waved them away with a thought as more demons crawled out of the hole in the ground.

What looked like a clear bottle filled with pink-tinted liquid soared through the air to land right at the lip of the rift. To Luke's surprise, it exploded on impact with enough force to pry loose a good chunk of road as well as kill a bunch of demons. They shrieked as they fell back, but the sound cut off as the falling stone and debris crushed them.

Ray appeared on top of a bus stop, and he hurled more bottles at the approaching enemies and the hole itself. "Step back a little!" he shouted. "My aim is terrible!"

Not needing to be told twice, Luke moved back and watched in fascination as bottle after bottle struck demon and ground alike, ravaging both.

"What is that?" he shouted back to Ray.

"Explosion potions!"

"Well... yeah," Luke muttered, stepping left to avoid a blow from a demon that had made it through Ray's bombardment. An elbow to the neck took care of that little asshole, but another one followed, slashing Luke across the chest. Long tears in the robe were all it managed before Luke tore its core apart.

While Ray kept lobbing his alchemical warfare into the pit, Luke got down next to the first demon, seeing it wasn't quite dead. Not being able to restore mana would be too much of a hindrance if the system event meant more and more demons popping up in the city. Sure, Luke could take from other Integrated, but everyone would need every available drop, if the sinking feeling in his gut was anything to go by. That meant he needed to find another way.

Prodding at the downed demon's internal core with a thread of mana, he breathed out in an attempt to steel himself for what he was about to attempt. Infernal mana was not compatible with a human's Integrated, at least not at first glance. It was time Luke took a closer, more thorough look.

He drew a small measure into himself, taking from the demon's infernal core and absorbing it into his own Meta-Heart. At first, nothing happened as the infernal mana mixed with his. A sense of calm came over him, but it was brief. Bile rose in Luke's throat, and he put his hand against his chest, a terrible ache radiating from his heart.

"Crap," he said, going down to a knee, barely able to breathe. "Not good."
