The whooshing sound of the automatic door closing was loud in the nearly empty library. The place felt like a pocket of sanity in a world gone awry.

Just outside those doors, the distant sounds of battle and the clashing of monsters could be heard, even here at the outskirts of town. The fluctuations in the ambient mana had made their way all the way to the edge of the barrier surrounding the library.

The defensive shield she and Maus had cobbled together to slip through the barrier had been crude but effective, protecting her from whatever effect sucked the souls out of living creatures the moment they touched it. She wondered if, with some refinement, the same method could become a proper defensive technique against spiritual energy.

The fight itself was just inside Scarlet’s active range of perception, and she was hoping once she turned the town into a Safe Zone, enemies like that would show up on the Mini-Map in her Interface.

Scarlet looked at the man still sitting in that seat behind his desk, sipping hot tea from a heavy ceramic mug and slowly flipping the pages of what is definitely not a normal book.

According to Investigate, the Knowledge Tome was called: Beginner's Guide to Spiritual Energy and Those Who Wield It. Volume One of an encyclopedia detailing the most common spirits, beasts, and creatures that can be found in the greater Network.

It reminded her of a few of the books she'd picked out from the System Store. She wondered if they'd be a decent bargaining chip to use with the man, depending on how this conversation went.

She decided to use Investigate on him too.

At that, his aura rippled with confusion and discomfort, evidenced by the slight frown that crossed his face before his expression smoothed out once more. Like somehow her action had tossed a small pebble into still waters, and the effect had once more settled down.

Fascinating, she thought as she went over the highlights of the Stat sheet in front of her.

Marion Kim.

It was satisfying to put a name to the Face

He was a human at a remarkable Lvl. 6. Apparently Lvl. 5 was a big threshold for a lot of people. He had middling Perception, middling Strength, middling Dexterity, and middling Mana. Those middling Stats, however, were belied by the genuine power of his aura, probably due to his Vitality, Intelligence, and Willpower being incredibly high.

Well, that and his two Traits. Serene Soul, which she had no base knowledge of, and Shadow Affinity, which likely gave him an innate predisposition towards shadow-aspected mana that improved his abilities to sense and manipulate the element.

He truly had, an intelligent build if he chose to undergo the racial transformation into an Umbros.

Scarlet had learned a lot about the three primary energies, from the book she'd purchased in the System Store: Body, Mind, and Soul: A Beginner's Guide to Mana, Psionics, and Spiritual Energy.

Mana, psionic energy, and spiritual energy - often called the energies of the body, mind, and soul - were the three primary forms of energy acknowledged by the System.

All humans had Mana on their Stat sheets because all humans possessed an organ Scarlet had taken to calling the Mana Heart. It was both physical and metaphysical, gathering, circulating, purifying, and managing mana within the body.

Not all creatures had a Mana Heart.

Some possessed similar structures within the mind or soul that functioned as conduits for psionic or spiritual energy instead.

Scarlet very likely had some sort of mental construct that allowed her to access psionic energy, while Maus's reconstitution had brought him back with a construct that allowed him to access spiritual energy.

Because humans (and apparently spatial mice) didn't have either construct as a racial trait, neither appeared as a Stat.

Scarlet could develop her psionics, of course. But outside of Titles, Traits, and Skills, the System did not directly interfere to help her push beyond her natural ceiling.

The Umbros were different.

They were the primary race within the Umbral Specter Faction, likely due to the Monarch of Shadows being one. Umbros naturally possessed the construct required for spiritual energy. Accordingly, where Mana appeared on every human's Stat sheet, Spirit appeared on theirs.

Much like humans, while every Umbros had the Spirit Stat, that didn't prevent them from learning to use mana. It simply wasn't an innate racial trait.

Despite Scarlet sensing his discomfort when she first used Investigate on him, the man's emotions remained like still waters.

His Skills included Spiritual Energy Manipulation (T2), Meditation (T1), Shadow Communion (T2), Spirit Ward (T2), and Enchanting (T1).

All five of his Skill Slots had been occupied, and quite well in Scarlet's opinion.

Perhaps Spirit Ward explained the death trap outside.

Scarlet had also been offered the Enchanting Skill, though she'd decided against taking it for now. It would almost certainly help with some of her more complex enchanting projects, but she'd rather hold off until she reached a natural dead end. Her Stat Saturation was proof enough that there were benefits to practising enchanting without System assistance.

"What was that?" The man asked, a hint of curiosity rippling through those still waters.

"An investigative Skill," she said.

She answered honestly without revealing it was literally the Skill Investigate, one of the furthest along in the Observe Skill-Tree.

"What does it do?" he asked, curiosity spiking sharply before settling into something milder.

"It can provide me information about, well, most things."

"Most things?"

"Concrete objects, primarily," she clarified.

"Primarily?"

"Indeed."

He smiled then, something more complicated than amusement rippling through his aura before fading back to those same still waters.

Looking left to right along the librarian's desk, Scarlet shrugged and leapt gracefully over the barrier between them, tucking her cane beneath her arm before resting it once more on the floor as she landed without a sound.

Despite the borderline unendurable trials she’d survived to get them, sometimes Scarlet really enjoyed her new physical Stats.

She pulled up a chair and sat beside the man, angling herself to face him.

Manipulating the surrounding mana, Scarlet spread an invisible wave across the immediate area, forming a privacy barrier. It was among the first constructs she'd learned to weave.

"You appear well?" she asked. She meant it too. He looked much more spirited since the last time she’d seen him.

It was nice sitting with Kim. She didn't feel the need to smile or be anything other than exactly what she was. He had a soothing quality to him, probably something to do with that Trait.

That made her a touch uneasy, but considering she'd just bought an artifact to negate any spiritual attack he might try, Scarlet wasn't worried.

"I have been, thank you," he said. "You appear well yourself." he inquired.

Small, brief smiles flashed across both of their faces in unison causing a rare, genuine one to grace Scarlet’s.

She had a feeling about Kim, one of those her intuition sometimes sent her. She could look for someone else for the Umbros Soul Orb, but she didn’t feel it necessary. She had a feeling they’d make a good pairing.

"You're quite a high Level," Scarlet said, not hiding that she could see it.

"Is 6 a high Level?"

"Yes, there's a threshold at 5."

"Oh." Kim nodded. He'd closed the book, his finger acting as a marker.

"You can absorb that, you know. The Knowledge Tome. It’s a System artifact."

"Yes." He smiled, amusement passing just as fleetingly through him as every other emotion. "I am enjoying reading it."

At Scarlet's look, he continued.

"I find it settles the mind."

That actually startled a laugh out of Scarlet. This man had perhaps the most settled mind she'd ever seen outside of someone catatonic or comatose, and even some of them had intense enough brain activity to cause emotional fluctuations. Scarlet grinned.

"It must be working then," she said. Then her grin stiffened.

Sure, she had a good feeling about this guy, but good enough to laugh? To smile foolishly and feed him compliments? No. That was abnormal.

She checked her body. Did a full scan, then did the same with her psionics.

That's when she realised his passive erosion of spiritual energy wasn’t some passive attack she was ‘studying’.

It was a destabilisation so delicate and so profound, she was almost embarrassed by her crude display when fighting Tareth.

She knew he hadn’t used psionic energy to lower her guard, so just what was happening here? Was this Spiritual Energy Manipulation?

No, she would have sensed that. Whatever he was doing was definitely passive.

Scarlet looked down at her finger where the Ring of Spiritual Warding sat.

[Ring of Spiritual Warding – Tier 2: Provides passive protection against active spiritual attacks, spiritual erosion, spiritual corruption, and some forms of soul damage. Provides limited warning of hostile spiritual phenomena. Works against power levels at or below Tier 2.Defending against spiritual attacks erodes durability.]

She could only think of two reasons the ring wasn’t protecting her. Either she was delusional, or whatever Mr. Kim was doing wasn’t considered an attack.
