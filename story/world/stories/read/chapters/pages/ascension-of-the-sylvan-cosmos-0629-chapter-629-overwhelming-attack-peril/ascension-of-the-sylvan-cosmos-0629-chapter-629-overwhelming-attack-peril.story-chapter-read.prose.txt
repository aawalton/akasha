BOOM!

Her aura skyrocketed, her stats doubling while her ternary essence reserves almost tripled by the loopback feed of her new stats.

Liu Shu’s face became ferocious as she moved her branches and canopy. She wanted to absorb the entire attack with her bare body.

100,000 cores… 200,000 cores… 300,000 cores…

Damage accumulated like crazy, enough times to kill almost anyone at the <Sixth Energy Cycle> in just a few breaths. However, Liu Shu stood tall and embraced everything, taking in all the pain and energy from the wild explosion and avoiding any deaths on her side.

If this attack were to destroy Liu Shu and burrow into Ceperet, the number of secondary-cause deaths that would occur would be a backlash so significant that all people in the ships would be reduced to Level 0! It was basically a suicide attack!

Cael glanced at the massive cloud of dust and heard a whooshing sound. Looking up, he saw an enormous branch of the World Tree falling from the sky.

Boom!

The branch struck the ground, followed by many others. The rain of World Tree parts was despairing.

On the ship, Altherion was nervously looking at his System. ‘...No message?’

“Anyone! Has anyone received a kill message?”

The one in charge of the cannon shook his head. “I only received a message of about 54,000 cores of damage dealt, Lord.”

“Only fifty-four thousand?” Altherion was incredulous. The attack from the World-Type could probably pierce through a small moon. Yet the damage was only a few tens of thousands of cores.

‘What kind of sick defense does this being have?’

The cloud finally dispersed, and behind it, Liu Shu's body appeared.

Her lush green leaves were scorched, many of her branches having snapped off. Her gorgeous body was black, as pieces of carbonized wood slowly crumbled. The previous lush World Tree full of life looked a breath away from scattering together with the wind.

Such a heartbreaking sight made the hearts of all from Ceperet lurch, their eyes reddening as they clenched their fists. Flor’s eyes were locked onto Liu Shu’s head, wanting to see any movement. Anything at all. ‘Please, baby, move. Tell Mommy that you are okay.’

Ignatia was frozen, her compound eyes only focusing on Liu Shu’s sorrowful figure. Honestly, just one of those beams was enough to make Flor and the others shudder, not to mention the 15 Moon-Type beams and two horrifying World-Type beams. At that moment, some of them thought that the world was truly ending.

Verdantia and the others were staring blankly at Liu Shu while a terrifying aura was about to awaken at the bottom of their souls, like an ancient beast about to open its eyes—a beast with endless hunger. Whatever Liu Shu’s next movement was, it would dictate a rather significant change in these four.

Everyone, both ally and enemy, held their breath, and the World Tree twitched, with a weak voice spreading out.

“You… didn’t… kill me…”

The creaking of wood around her neck echoed, giving the sensation that it would break down at any moment. Yet, the smile on the half-scorched face of the World Tree didn’t falter.

“Big… mistake… Heh. <Eternity Strand…”

All the falling branches, all the scattering ash, and all the pieces of Liu Shu scattered around began flowing back. The world was kept the same, but Liu Shu’s body started recovering at a speed visible to the naked eye.

In Liu Shu’s eyes, the healing numbers popped so fast that they created a giant column, while from the outside, it looked as if all that happened was being forcefully undone.

Such a stupefying thing made all enemies fall into despair.

A few seconds later, Liu Shu’s body recovered to its peak, her dim green eyes regaining their strength as her aura skyrocketed.

BOOM!

Her body, fully recovered, emitted endless vitality, her lush branches and gorgeous body taking over the space around her. With a smile, her voice boomed out, spreading with unequaled intensity!

“Your cannons are unable to finish me off. Now, Altherion, it’s time for your death!”
