After leaving that small gift to the “top-ranked heroes,” Liu Shu glanced at the leader of Etheria Cathedra. To clarify, that attack was meant to be “healable,” as she made sure not to damage them too badly. Any recovery skill of B Rank and above would do the trick easily.

After all, while Etheria Cathedra was better for her plans, those “heroes” were not called as such lightly. Most of them had good hearts and truly believed that they were chosen ones to protect the common people. Naturally, like in all human organizations, there were corrupt heroes here and there who looked down on those who didn’t Level Up.

The leader of Etheria Cathedra, kneeling in respect, glanced up slowly at the wooden Avatar Liu Shu, manifested and sat in a wooden throne. The blue-glowing sap flowing through those visible veins gave the whole thing a majestic and unreal beauty.

Liu Shu looked at the woman with an indifferent and divine look and spoke. “You’ve been chosen to lead this world in the right path. From today, you’ll be my Saintess and lead this world to a future where everyone will have the same chances to Level Up. I will make sure that Level 99 is not the limit, and also open everything and spread you beyond the stars.”

The woman’s eyes trembled, her blood rushing in excitement. This had been her deepest wish, and while her method had been quite twisted and cost several lives, it also helped this world be above the rest in this Solar System in one thing: quality.

Few people Leveled Up in this world, but those who did were chosen due to their outrageous talents. From what Liu Shu had secretly absorbed with her roots, there were records of humanoids reaching Level 99 in less than 10 years.

That speed was outrageous even in Ceperet, Liu Shu’s home planet, not to mention here.

Once she got to know of this information, Liu Shu expanded her Divine Tree Eye together with World Tree Wisdom and scanned all active heroes.

To her utter stupefaction, she saw at least five people with 20 overall stars (S+ Rank), forty with 19 (S Rank), around 90 with 18 (S- Rank), and a few hundred with 17 overall stars (A+ Rank), which was Liu Shu’s current tier.

Not to mention, due to Etheria Cathedra’s threat, talent bloomed to the point that C+ Tier was considered the minimum to become a low-ranking Hero. That kind of selection criteria was not far behind what Liu Shu used to select her soldiers!

Due to her <Breakthrough Berries>, almost all her army had varying tiers from B+ to A Tier. As for S+ Tier talents? Liu Shu recalled that there were around 10 of them across her entire Golden Sap Empire!

‘It’s honestly quite ridiculous. If this entire civilization decided to actually take arms and Level Up collectively, their potential would be high.

There weren’t only humans. Beastkin, Sylvans, and Humans. Those were the “accepted” races as intelligent species. As for World Trees and Life Trees, they were actually not considered special.

The reason? Governments that took control realized their potential and made sure that no World Tree or Life Tree could reach Level 75. Due to this, there were World Tree striker groups, led by one of the five S+ Tier talents.

As a consequence, this S+ Tier talent was riddled with negative <Titles>. It was to the point that Level 74 Liu Shu would’ve been able to deal with him because of the negative multipliers against World Trees.

The woman who had been trembling in joy spoke up. “L-Lady Heavenly Willow, this servant is delighted to serve your purpose and bring a new dawn to society!”

Liu Shu escaped her ruminations and refocused on the woman in front of her. “Your Villain name has been Etheria. Is that your given name as well?”

“This servant has long discarded my own name. In my divine page, the name Etheria is written as my True Name.”

Liu Shu blinked. ‘Divine page… ah. The System Interface.’ Her eyes were curious as to why this woman was so religious, so she scanned her System Page in detail.

As if she was feeling it, the woman trembled under Liu Shu’s analytical gaze.

‘...Wow. Look at this. No wonder she acts as a servant of the divine. She has a special <Title> with her.’

***

<Witness Of The Gods>: You saw a shadow of their majesty by chance once when you were young. +500% Aether Mana and Anima Energy regeneration. +10% Stats. +1 Tier.

***

Liu Shu pondered, scanning when such an event could have happened through her collected intelligence. Her mind focused, discarding and analyzing information like an advanced supercomputer.

‘Gods… Gods… Something that happened around a century ago…’

She frowned deeply, and suddenly, her blue glowing eyes flashed. ‘Could it be…?’

Liu Shu scanned the woman’s system page once more and noticed one <Passive Skill>.

***

<Divine Revelation (SS- Rank)>: You will be able to sense a divine existence once and gain powerful bonuses from it. If you later become a divine follower, you will gain further enhancements.

***

“SS- Rank… Such a skill should be able to detect things even if they happen far away… How far? It probably will trigger even for nearby solar systems…”

That being the case… “Is it because of me?”

Liu Shu, when she was incredibly young and was choosing a species for her summoned creatures, went through an overwhelming event. That day, the sky parted, and a serpent eye large enough to cover the sky looked down upon her. Back then, just for witnessing such an event, Flor went through several Level Ups and Tier Ups.

‘It’ was most likely able to see through either her potential or her destiny to an extent, and, as a result, it forced a contract on her.

“Did that Arboreal Winged Serpent’s presence back then activate her <Passive Skill>? Our Solar Systems are relatively close, and I doubt that being’s presence failed to reach nearby Star Systems.”

Her eyes, when she looked at Etheria, became subtle and more profound. “Then… will it become angry if I steal a potential follower?”

Honestly, if she could communicate with it, she would want to ask. However, since there was no chance to do so, Liu Shu decided to be a bit mischievous and selfish and take her for herself. ‘Moreover, Elyne is starting to need some help in managing the growing Eternal Church. Etheria is a perfect person suitable for that.’

So, with that in mind, Liu Shu opened her mouth and spoke. “Today, you will receive my blessing. Here, my children will descend to strengthen you and those you choose. Remember, my name is Tianlian Liu Shu, the Heavenly Willow. Those who follow me belong to the <Eternal Church>.”

Rambling more would decrease her mysteriousness, and for now, what she needed was Etheria’s blind faith, not becoming close like she did with Elyne. Later, she could slowly become closer to Etheria.

Meanwhile, Etheria was trembling in delight. She had always waited patiently, and while she was considered the strongest in the world, she avoided appearing in public. The reason was simple, more than appearing in public, praying and hoping for the god who gave her strength to appear was much more important. Even Etheria Cathedra was something she created to gather those who were dissatisfied.

She pointed the direction, destroyed all who challenged her, and gathered those who wanted to go against the current system. That was her role. A massive and unmovable pillar that not even the strongest opposing force could shake.

And now, that existence was about to receive Liu Shu’s buffs.

First, Liu Shu raised her hand. “<Temple Seed>.”

Six seeds fell from the sky like shooting stars, landing around the hidden headquarters of Etheria Cathedra. Liu Shu, naturally, used her rune knowledge to reinforce all the concealing methods.

Next, Liu Shu poured her blessings because this world had already become “her domain” the second her roots took over.

A moment later, Etheria heard countless pings in her head as the system went haywire before her eyes.

A pillar of ternary essences surrounded Etheria as her comprehension, talent, affinities, stats, lifespan, and even her <Destiny> were boosted.

That wasn’t all.

[Congratulations! You’ve been appointed as the Heavenly Willow’s <Eternal Saintess>. Your <Passive Skill: Divine Revelation (SS- Rank)> evolves into <Passive Skill: Divine Eternal Saintess (SS- Rank)>. (Passive Skill can evolve further).]

<Divine Eternal Saintess (SS- Rank)>: You’ve gained the recognition but not the trust of the Heavenly Willow. When fighting for her cause, you’ll gain 20% Stats. Liu Shu used World Tree Wisdom to see through the details, and her composed face almost crumpled. Thankfully, she could maintain a dignified appearance. ‘This woman… her talent as a religious leader might be beyond Elyne’s.’

The woman with golden hair and eyes looked up, and as she did, her eyes changed. The golden color shifted, turning to a gorgeous purple. Her hair stayed the same, but Liu Shu was sure that once she became an <Eternal Avatar>, that would change as well.

“My Lady, I can see your grandeur! This humble Etheria will follow your gospel and spread it to this world and beyond the stars!”

Her words were actually not a lie. When she transformed, Liu Shu’s World Tree body flashed in her vision for a moment, forcing her heart to pump so fast in delight that she almost had a cardiac episode.

Looking at her flushed face, Liu Shu secretly gulped ‘Honestly… with all her personal stat boosts, her Overall Star Tiers, and my blessings… She is an absolute monster even at Level 99. She can probably beat up most low-level <First Ascension> powerhouses.’

For a moment, Liu Shu even had the idea of placing her at the same level as Aionthra. After all, Etheria’s current strength was more than enough to fight her Level 99 past self! A human at Level 99 with enough strength to beat Liu Shu at Level 99… ‘Ridiculous.’

Liu Shu walked down her wooden throne and caressed Etheria’s face. “Do a good job, Etheria. I trust you.”

“Y-Yeesssh!” Etheria’s nose flowed with blood, and then the woman fainted with a blissful smile on her lips, flopping onto the ground like a puppet whose strings were cut.

Liu Shu, her hand stuck in the air where Ethera’s face previously was, coughed awkwardly. ‘Did I go overboard?’
