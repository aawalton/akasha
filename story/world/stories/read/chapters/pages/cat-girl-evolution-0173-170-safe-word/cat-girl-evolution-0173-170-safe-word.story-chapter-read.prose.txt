Round Two Metztli is a whole different beast. It literally feels like phase two of a video game boss battle, where she’s got some new abilities and attacks and stuff.

Jane can’t grapple her anymore. She tried, but as soon as she got her arms wrapped around, stone spikes shot out to try and impale her. Most of them weren’t quite strong enough to damage her, but they made her bleed, and they made her lose her grip.

Wesley isn’t actually that affected. The whole demon is wrapped in stone, but the stone isn’t actually any more durable than the demon’s skin already was. His sword cuts through the stone like butter, hitting the flesh beneath just like before, but sadly, none of his attacks are strong enough to do real damage. He hasn’t really done much more than defend and cut either, which makes me think his abilities are relatively one-dimensional. He did get randomly stronger earlier, which I think is a blessing from his goddess, but again, his strength all goes into defense, rather than offense.

Endir, the new wizard guy, is very strong, though, and is exactly what we needed. He’s clearly not a pure offensive specialist the way A is, but even his ordinary attacks are as strong or stronger than her fully-charged ones, and since he can cast them so quickly, he can chain them together, blowing up some of the armor and following it up with an immediate lightning strike.

He seems to know magic of every element, and spends the first part of the fight testing out each of them, seeing if the demon has any weaknesses. It only has one, light, and it’s not very extreme, and he ultimately decides that his non-light spells are stronger, despite the weakness.

The real benefit to having him along, though, is his knowledge of earth magic. His raw power is utterly eclipsed by Metztli’s, but he doesn’t need raw power to provide utility. Sometimes when Metzli sends stone spikes flying, they crumble to dust before they hit their targets. Other times, when Jane jumps in to punch it, the stone armor suddenly becomes brittle, shattering under her fists and letting her hit the flesh underneath.

Even despite all this, though, the fight isn’t really going our way. It’s not going Metztli’s way either, but we still can’t do much to her. Whatever damage we do gets healed quickly, and even when A fires one of her bullets at its knee, it’s still not quite enough to bring her down. She’s taking more damage than us, but she also has that aura Trait, and if this is a battle of attrition, she wins. Plus, there’s always the risk of her just submerging again if things get dire.

She seems to have realized this, and has mostly given up on fighting back. She’ll still attack when given the opportunity, of course, but for the most part, she’s just walking toward Dryadil. Endir sent out a telepathic message saying he thinks she’s going to try to get some experience and level up or something like that, which would be bad for many reasons.

All in all, it’s not looking great for us. We just don’t have the raw firepower to take her down. Endir says he might be able to do some real damage if he can charge up an attack properly, and Jane says she could too, if she could just get some proper leverage, but it’s just really hard to coordinate. What we need to do is get it on the ground again where it will be vulnerable, but we need to do it quickly enough that it can’t sink into the ground again, because if it does before we can separate Metztli from the main body, it will just recover and come back.

I’m not sure why she doesn’t just swim through the ground if her main goal is to get to the city. Abby says it’s either something to do with the mana cost, or more likely, Dryadil’s roots. It’s good for us that she doesn’t, but the threat of submersion still lingers.

None of this is really my problem, though. With the rock armor, I am suddenly much less useful than I was before. I can’t actually do real damage unless I go full hulk mode, and I don’t want to do that again. And this part isn’t my job, anyway.

My job is to wait up here, and as soon as they separate Metztli from the main body, I eat her. So while everyone else fights, I just hover, re-shaping the maw that got severed when the ground ate me. This time, I use almost exclusively the extra flesh that Jane helped me pull off my body. It’s the strongest flesh I’ve ever used, and is therefore a bit more difficult to shape, but nothing debilitating. By the time two minutes have passed, the maw is ready.

There’s still a big issue, though. Metztli can just rip through again like she did the last time I tried to eat her. Even without the rest of the demon body, she’s still got a Tier 6 body now, and even made of her flesh, I doubt the maw will be strong enough to withstand it if she tries to rip through again. I need to somehow remove her arms. Or maybe just immobilize them…

Cutting off her arms is almost definitely beyond me, and that’s not something I can really ask of the others when they already have to do so much just to get her separated. So, trapping them is my only option. Fortunately, I think I have the perfect way of doing that.

While I work, the battle rages on below, but it’s changed slightly. Jane is no longer trying to grapple at all. Instead, she periodically plants herself, then launches straight into the demon’s back, punching through its stone armor and trying to knock it over.

Wesley has taken up a position in front of it, and is trying to stop it from moving. He’s actually much more successful at this than attacking. His divine blessing or whatever is especially good for blocking, so with his feet planted in the shallow mud, he is almost immovable, and there’s a massive wall of mana rising as high as the demon is tall from his shield.

Metzli tries punching through it, and I see the barrier ripple, but it doesn’t break. She punches it twice more, but it seems more indestructible than her own body, so she turns to try to go around instead. Wesley slides to the side, blocking her path again, so she tries jumping over, which almost works, except Jane flies in from the side, crashing into her and knocking her off course.

She bounces off the top of the shield, and somehow manages to land on her feet, making the entire swamp shake. Jane pounds away on her back for a second or so, but then Metztli reaches back to grab her, and Jane doesn’t quite move away in time. The massive demon hand clamps down on two of her arms, and makes to fling her. If she succeeded, Jane probably would have been out of the fight for a few full minutes, but Jane instead seems to shed her arms like lizard tails. The arms go flying off into the distance, while Jane falls to the safety of the ground while new arms grow into their place.

I hear one of Endir’s mana batteries curse as the demon starts moving again, and Wesley is forced to move with it to block its progress. It definitely would have been nice if that had taken Metztli down, but fortunately, that wasn’t the main plan. Neither Endir nor A participated in that, as they are saving their efforts for the main plan.

A few more seconds pass of Metztli struggling to get past Wesley, and then I hear a voice in my head.

“Now!” shouts Endir.

Wesley’s shield drops, causing Metztli to stumble slightly, and at that exact moment, a projectile slams straight into her knee, making her stagger back a step. The knee looks completely undamaged, but I know that’s just A using the gun’s targeting feature to ignore things she doesn’t want to damage. A second bullet follows less than a second after the first , and the knee hyper-extends backward.

Metzli tries to move the demon hands down to cover the damaged knee, but A is faster. I don’t even know how it was possible, because from my angle, it looked like Metzli successfully blocked it, but A somehow threaded the needle, firing between the enormous fingers, and hitting right on the kneecap for the third time in a row. When the third bullet hits, it bends the knee backward, folding it in on itself like a piece of paper, and making the demon fall.

It catches itself on its hands, but only for a split second, because at that moment, Jane comes flying in from above, stomping down right on the demon’s back. What she did shouldn’t have been enough to knock it down, since even with all her compressed flesh, she doesn’t weigh that much, but it makes the demon’s elbows buckle anyway. The beam is white, but it looks prismatic due to whatever ridiculous effects that much energy has on the atmosphere.

His attack hits right below the nape of the neck, completely disintegrating the armor and burrowing deep into the flesh. The stone armor across the demon’s entire body shatters and starts to melt as the beam continues boring into the demon for a full three seconds, and when it settles, there’s a charred hole three meters across that cuts almost all the way through the demon’s body, completely severing its spine.

It’s still not enough, though. Despite not having a functional spine, the demon can still move somehow, and it starts flailing its arms, trying to get rid of its attackers while the earth rises up around it to pull it back under.

We expected this, though. As it sinks, Jane darts to the top, standing at nearly four meters, her maximum height. Metztli, who had previously been fully encased in stone, is now exposed, and Jane grabs a hold of her, wrapping her up from behind with all three pairs of arms and pulling hard. The giant demon hands move to grab her, but too slowly. At the same time that she grabs Metzli, Wesley appears standing beside, sword raised high to the sky and charged with as much mana as he could manage in the seconds since he dropped his shield.

“No! Stop him!”

Then, Wesley’s sword successfully severs the topmost vertebrae from the rest of the demon’s body. Jane jerks backward, pulling just out of range of the arms as the clasp around where she had just been standing, and the newcomer comes with her.
