[Congratulations, Verde!]

You have met the requirements - 10,000 / 10,000 Divinity Points. Rank ascension has been initiated.

A new Rank requires the selection of an accompanying intrinsic skill… Pantheon entry must be authorized… Your Dominion will be removed from the mortal world and anchored beyond the veil… Awakening sequence has been initiated!

The yellow notification window glowed in front of me, bright enough to overshadow even the changes unfolding in my realm. The mist that usually covered my plane began to bubble as if it was boiling. The space itself trembled, the vibrations reaching the Crimson Guardian’s manifestation and sending the stars drifting around it into uneven, chaotic sways.

This is stronger than before.

The tremor didn’t fade as it had in the past. If anything, it grew. I tried to move to the guardian, but I remained rooted in place.

I looked down. I had no legs, yet that had never stopped me from moving. But now my lower form, the ghostlike shape that made me look like a crimson Casper, was sinking into the mist, blending with it as if merging into something else. I froze. Panic rose in me instantly.

Was I about to dissolve into my own realm? Was I going to lose the form I had only recently gained and stop existing as an individual? I didn’t want to become one with the space I had been living in.

As though the mist heard my thoughts, it surged toward me. It moved like a whirlpool, and I was the center, pulling in everything around me. Yet I felt nothing. No pressure. No weight. No pain. No sensation at all.

Then the flow slowed, and I saw that my shape was changing. My form was less transparent now, and the hazy lower half pulsed with brief flashes of light, like distant lightning trapped inside it.

That was when the pain arrived.

It didn’t strike at a single place. Instead, it spread everywhere at once. My head, my body, even the realm around me throbbed with the same overwhelming ache, as if the entire plane shared a single pulse and each beat sent a fresh shock of pain through me.

My vision blurred. My awareness got hazy. I blinked against my will, and every time my eyes closed, it felt like something tried to pull me away. I forced myself to stay conscious as much as I could, but the pain choked my thoughts until only instinct remained.

“Aaargh… it hurts so much.”

The words tore out of me and roared across the entire realm. I tried to gather my divine power to ease the pain, but it wouldn’t respond. Or rather, my ability to command it had vanished.

Then another golden notification appeared.

[Congratulations, Verde!]

No anomaly detected. You are permitted to ascend and become a true deity. Estimated duration of slumber: 6 months.

Your new Rank has been generated. A skill uniquely tailored to your nature has been created. Certain restrictions placed upon you have been lifted.

I tried to read the text, but the letters kept jumping around, sliding out of place whenever I focused on them. I had no idea how long I struggled. Seconds, minutes, or perhaps more, there was no way to tell. But eventually, I reached the part that mattered - raising my Rank this time would put me into a slumbering state for 6 months.

The meaning hit me immediately. Just the thought of what awaited me if I allowed it pushed the pain aside for a moment. Six months unconscious… I couldn’t afford that. Not with the Night God coming. Panic rose through me, and I forced myself to read the rest as quickly as I could.

Clarity didn’t last for long.

The pain came back with a force that felt deeper, heavier. My head felt ready to split apart. Forming even a single coherent thought became difficult.

[Warning: pain is exceeding the allowed threshold!][The slumbering state is being initiated…]

No…

I tried to argue with the system. In my mind, I reached for any justification I could make - anything to convince it I could endure this. I would have taken any amount of pain if it meant staying awake. Six months unconscious was unthinkable.

“Please…” my voice, which didn’t come from my body, sounded rough and weak.

The space around me shuddered again. But this time, instead of another wave of pain, it felt like a warmth spreading through the mist, familiar and strangely comforting.

[Congratulations, Verde!]

Your Angel responds to your will and seeks to awaken. However, an Angel cannot be born without a defined role or purpose. Their entire existence (shape, instinct, and power) depends on that singular mandate. They will pursue it eternally, prioritizing it above all else, save for their god’s direct command.

Your Angel already possesses either a role or a purpose. (cannot be determined.)

Cost: 5,000 Divinity Points.

I didn’t fully understand what the text said, but the moment I grasped the meaning of the first few words, my mind was already made up. In my current state I had maybe ten minutes before I lost consciousness, and if the system was right, then I’d be gone for six months.

That meant I wouldn’t be able to protect the Velmoryns if something happened, wouldn’t be able to nudge them in the direction I’d been planning for months, and, most concerning of all, I wouldn’t be able to return Karla to the mortal realm once she regained consciousness. By now, she had less than three months left, and without me controlling my divine power, I had no idea whether she would survive once the cocoon protecting and healing her finally dissolved.

My priority was, of course, the Velmoryns, but Karla added the last piece of weight I needed to commit. The problem was that reason had already begun slipping from me. I could think, but only in messy fragments.

“Protect what’s mine…” I managed, though the next surge of pain stripped my voice away. First, I had lost movement and control over my divine power, now speech and the last pieces of coherent thought. Whatever followed would likely take me out completely.

The moment I voiced what I wanted most in that moment, the safety of the Velmoryns and Karla, my divine power burst through the realm. The mist reacted as if pulled in and began to flow toward the sphere where the Angel had been slumbering.

Then the mist shifted into red winds which burst out like a rising tornado, curling around the sphere and lifting it from the ground. The crimson deepened with each passing moment as pulses of scarlet lightning spread from within.

Even in my own realm I could hear the roar of those winds, along with the sharp sound of glass cracking. Each crack was followed by another pulse of red, louder and heavier than the last, until the sphere finally shattered and the crimson washed through everything like a tide.

My vision blurred. The shapes and edges smeared together as though they were melting into one another. The mist blended with every object that didn’t stand out in color, and the entire realm distorted into a haze. Only the stars and the Crimson Guardian remained clear enough to recognize. Perhaps it was because their image was still fresh in my mind, or perhaps their crimson was simply too vivid to mistake for anything else.

And then I saw it. A figure stepping toward me, or at least that was what I assumed, because it continued to get closer. It moved slowly, unhurried, as though time meant nothing to it.

I wanted to scream at it, to force it to come closer so I could see its shape clearly and give it a command. I needed to make sure it would fulfill the purpose for which I had summoned it. But I couldn’t call out. I couldn’t even form a clear thought. My mind felt like water I was trying desperately to shape and give it a form.

The time it took the Angel to reach me felt endless, and by the time it finally came close, I was already half asleep. That was when I noticed that it did not shimmer only in crimson. There was gold in its light as well, woven through the red.

“My liege,” it spoke. Or rather, the thought brushed gently against what remained of my mind.

The sensation was soothing. As soothing as collapsing into warm bedding after days of exhaustion, easing into a rest the body had begged for.

“Please rest without worrying about anything. Your servant will protect your Kingdom.”

I was pleased that the Angel at least seemed to understand what it was supposed to do, but I still felt as though I needed to give it more information. I needed to better direct it. The problem was what to actually say. No matter how hard I tried to recall what my plan had been, or what I had been worried about only a moment ago, nothing came back.

My perfect memory was gone. The thought of Karla slipped away as if it had never existed. Whatever I had intended for the Velmoryns scattered with it, along with anything else I might have been trying to hold onto.

I couldn’t tell whether the Angel sensed my inner turmoil or simply acted on instinct, but it leaned closer, and the blended silhouette of crimson and gold wrapped around me. The warmth of it settled over my mind like a blanket I had been reaching for without knowing it.

“Rest, my King.”

Its voice reached me one last time, quiet but safe, and I followed it without resistance, drifting into the darkness.
