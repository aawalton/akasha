The Healer's Moment awarded Luke a much-needed breather. He ignored the somewhat mangled state of his body after taking a punch from the demon. The healing mana in his Meta-Heart would take care of that for him. Instead, he focused on offense and snaked threads of mana through Simean's arm and into its mana channels. This time, he was careful not to draw mana from the monster and into himself, since that'd made Luke's insides burn. Not a pleasant experience, and not one he wanted to repeat. The objective was to do maximum damage before the skill wore off and the demon threw Luke off.

To that end, Luke surged forward with threads of mana, cutting through mana channels as he went. The anatomy of a demon was nothing like a human's. Simean didn't have a single organ, for starters. Instead, some sort of burning sludge churned inside the demon's chest. It hadn't been his blood then, or perhaps it served multiple purposes. There was no use in trying to hurt the monster through that avenue, at least, so Luke searched on as he went. Simean did have a skeleton, but no heart or lungs, and where the Meta-Heart should be, Luke found nothing more than a higher concentration of the same sludge. Perhaps it burned a little hotter, but that was it. That begged the question: How the hell was he supposed to hurt the thing?

Its head, Luke decided. Few creatures made it far in life without something resting on their shoulders. The damn thing didn't even have a brain! Just more sludge! It was a wonder it even had mana and not just more of the same. How it pumped mana around its body, Luke had no clue. No, wait, he did have one, he realized. The demon's sludge pushed into the mana channels, converting itself into mana. An interesting application, and it meant Simean wouldn't run out anytime soon.

Four threads of mana, all with Needle of Life activated, cut into the demon's throat, working to sever its head. Luke even made decent headway before The Healer's Moment timed out and time resumed, having carved through a good third of its flesh.

Burning sludge poured out through the massive hole in Simean's throat, and the demon gurgled as it brought a hand up to stop the flow. At this point, Curtis, Hannah, Nick, and Dirk were poking holes in the monster, making it bleed all over the place while Clementine sounded like she was performing an exorcism, waving a damn bible over her head as she screamed until her face was red and blotched.

As expected, Luke couldn't stay on the arm, but made it to the ground without further injury, and reached out to heal Nick again, who had ended up with a large burn on his cheek, since the nun seemed to have better things to do than heal. As for the demon, it wasn't done just yet. By the look of things, it thought it was being assaulted by a horde of flies, because it swatted the air with its free hand rather than retaliate against the humans, and Luke caught on to what was going on. It was George. The middle-aged office worker had found a way inside the demon's mind somehow, and was attacking it with visions, or something to that end.

He must've decided Ariel and Ray didn't need his help, and in that, he was right, because every single cultist was on the ground, dead or dying. Most had succumbed to Ariel's poison abilities, but several were cut to shreds, having tasted Ray's blades.

Little by little, the group whittled down Simean's health, relentless in their assault. The dangerous job wasn't without injury, of course, but with Luke once again having taken up healing duty, things were under control for the most part. When Dirk found himself showered in burning sludge, Luke had to steal some mana from Curtis, or risk running out, but the Reaver was almost topped off, even after all that fighting and having taken several blows from their opponent. The guy was a damn mana machine.

It seemed like the demon had some sort of self-healing. The wound in its neck stopped spewing sludge almost right away, but it put Simean off-balance, at least, allowing the others to get a few good hits in. The monster screamed in defiance, threatened, and fought for its life until it turned toward the stairs to find the cultists dead. That, more than anything, seemed to affect the creature, and it was then that it took a step backward, retreating for the first time in the fight.

All in all, Luke found himself unimpressed by the monster. Sure, it was tough as hell and packed a mean punch, but so far it hadn't cast a single spell, if you didn't count the burning sludge it kept pouring everywhere when wounded. This was it? A literal demon should be more dangerous, shouldn't it? And a Level 35 one at that.

Ray appeared by Luke's side. "Did you just jinx us?"

"What?"

"You did, didn't you?" Ray said, pointing at Luke's face. "I can see it on you! You're thinking the fight should've been harder!"

"...Kind of."

"Well, knock it off!" Ray shouted, heading into the fray again.

It was about that time when Simean formed its first spellweave, far too quick and practiced for Luke to have a chance to react in time and slice it to bits. A powerful shockwave of force pushed everyone off their feet and back as sludge once again poured out to cover the demon. No, not everyone had been pushed back, Luke realized. Clementine still stood there, waving her hands in the air, screaming at the top of her lungs. Since she hadn't even stopped to catch her breath during the entire fight, Luke had sort of tuned her out. Now, since she refused to retreat, she was in danger, sludge pooling around her feet, pushing up against her barriers.

"Get up!" Ariel shouted, holding out her hand to Luke, who took it and got to his feet as the others rose to join them.

"Can't believe this asshole has a third phase," Ray muttered, not even leaving stealth.

"What?" Hannah asked.

"Like in games," Ray explained. "Sometimes you've got to kick a boss's ass several times before it goes down."

"Let's not repeat our mistake from before! Attack!" Nick shouted, charging forward. Hannah picked him up off the ground with an extended tentacle of shadow. "Not you."

"Put me down!" Nick shouted.

Curtis let out a short, intense laugh before catching himself.

"You don't have the range to hurt it without damaging yourself!" Hannah shouted. "Keep back!" With that, she put him down and ran past, heading toward Simean by herself.

"Sit down and watch, little brother," Curtis said, following.

"Sibling rivalries bore me," Ray said, appearing. He was sitting on the ground, playing with one of his knives.

"Are you coming?" Luke asked, waving for the Assassin to get up.

Ray shook his head. "You heard Hannah. I'm short-ranged too. Looks like you'll have enough help, though."

And he was right. Ariel and George were approaching together, and Dirk had taken out a bow. He pulled back the bowstring and multiplied himself before letting go. Rather than one arrow soaring toward the target, twenty slammed home, then twenty more.

"Awesome," Ray whispered before catching himself. Glaring up at Luke, he added. "Don't tell him I said that."

"I'm going, too," Luke said.

It looked like the demon was about to transform again, but before it did so, Simean seemed to have something else in mind, because the monster cast another spell, slamming into place within the blink of an eye. Rather than doing something external, the demon directed it to its own chest. At first, it didn't seem to do anything, but soon it became apparent what Simean's goal with the spell was.

The dead cultists stirred. Luke half-expected them to stand up and fight again. It wouldn't be the first time opponents resurrected each other, but that wasn't what happened. Instead, the cultists' bodies rose a little off the ground, and something emerged. Wisp-like small clouds of unknown origin rose into the air, floating almost like threads before coming together in the air above the stairs. It lingered there for a moment, then shot toward Simean and entered the demon through the area on its chest where it'd summoned runes earlier. Was this their purpose? To harvest souls?

Milla: "Luke, how many people did you talk to? I'm drowning in applications!"

Luke: "Later!"

Milla: "Geez. Rude much?"

The souls did something to Simean's transformation, speeding it along as the monster once again got up to stand on a single set of legs. It was tall, much taller than before, and before the sludge returned to its body, two massive wings formed, spreading out to either side.

"Flight is going to be a bitch," Luke muttered, thinking how they would get the demon back down to the ground before it had even risen into the air.

A cracking sound rang out in the chamber, followed by Clementine's screams. The sludge had broken through her barriers and was now eating away at her feet and ankles. Before she fell, Nick was there, having charged forward before, heedless of Hannah's words. His speed forced the sludge to part, and he grabbed Clementine by the waist before pulling her along and out of the fire.

Simean took to the air with one powerful push of its new wings. Spellweaves formed in front of the demon as it laughed and bellowed, "I shall harvest your souls, mortals!"

The distance was far too great for Luke to reach and destroy the spellweaves, but he made it to Nick and Clementine in time to pull the oncoming balls of sludge apart, hoping the others would be able to protect themselves. Judging by Clementine's wailing, she wouldn't be doing much more fighting, at least not for the moment. Pulling mana from her Meta-Heart, Luke used it to restore her feet with Weaver's Renewal. Once the pain had passed, Clementine lost consciousness.

"Shit," Nick said.

Luke pointed to the gate. "Take her aside. I'll take over the healing."

Ray appeared, his entire arm seared off right at the shoulder. His face was pale, but he did his best to appear and sound unaffected. "A little help?"

"Hold on," Luke said, grabbing Nick's arm to stop him from going. "If Clementine isn't going to be fighting, she won't need all that mana."

Healing Ray brought the nun down to around twenty percent, and Luke didn't dare take any more. His own mana was at forty percent. Things weren't going so great. Prolonged battles where he couldn't re-supply his mana were a weakness, to be sure. They'd need to end this soon, or they would have to go without healing.

"Thanks," Ray said, testing his re-grown arm, as if he'd just hit his hand on something. Luke knew his friend was shaken. There was no hiding that from Luke, not after they'd known each other for so long, but he didn't say anything. If Ray needed to look tough, then Luke wasn't going to take that away from him. They shared a look, and then they were off, heading toward the fight with, at least on Luke's part, no plan on how to tackle their now-flying opponent.
