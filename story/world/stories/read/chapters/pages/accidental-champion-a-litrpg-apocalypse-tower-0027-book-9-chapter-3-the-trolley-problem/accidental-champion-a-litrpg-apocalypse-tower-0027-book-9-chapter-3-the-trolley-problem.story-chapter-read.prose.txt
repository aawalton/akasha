The woman had a strong mental defence, even for a Denizen at peak B Grade, but it was no match for Xavier’s ability. He shattered the mental defence on contact and took control of her mind in an instant.

Or almost an instant.

As the spell had tunnelled into her brain, he’d felt her call out in panic to the other members of her team. The twin Denizens whirled, but to Xavier with his more powerful time dilation field wrapped tightly around him, they weren’t fast enough. He’d already flapped his wings and pushed himself back from the time mage who was now in his control.

The twin Denizens, staffs gripped tightly in their hands, glanced around then settled their gaze on the time mage. Shock turned to confusion and annoyance as mental communications were sent her way.

Mental communications Xavier was now able to intercept.

[What’s the matter, Alica?] Somehow concern and annoyance seemed to leak into the man’s voice equally.

[Thought I saw…] Alica shook her head, a piece of Xavier’s consciousness in her mind making her do it. [Never mind. I must have imagined it.]

The twin who’d communicated to her—Dybal, Xavier gleaned from the woman’s thoughts—narrowed his eyes then gave a slow nod. [I’m feeling it too.] The man shuddered. [I didn’t expect Xavier Collins to go down so easily.] He rotated in the air to look at where the drone had been obliterated. [The way he took out Sovereign Fouran… I thought we might actually have a challenge on our hands.] He grinned, chuckled. [Suppose I shouldn’t have expected much, they’re all weak in these backwaters.]

Xavier fingers twitched, aching to draw Bones and teach this man a lesson. Teach them all a lesson. Xavier Prime was feeding him more information about what had happened to Palini’s family, how the kidnappers had treated them, making anger well inside his chest.

Palini hadn’t been among them. Xavier read through Alica’s mind, searching for the information, learning everything he could about her and the hit squad along the way. He’d been right—all three of them were peak B Grade, and those two were twins. Dybal and Lydad. They’d chosen a synergistic mage class similar to the Defender line but more concentrated.

The class let them to combine their powers, and it had an extra boost that allowed them, once per month, to cast a spell that was three times stronger than they could usually cast—that was the spell they’d just used on him.

Xavier took in this information with different parts of his mind while he tried to pinpoint the thing he needed. He frowned as he searched, thinking it should have been easier to find. His frown only deepened as he kept looking. A sigh escaped his lips as he figured out the problem.

The hit squad didn’t know where Palini was. They didn’t know anything about that side of the operation. The Denizen they were taking orders from had ensured all information would be compartmentalised. Everything was need to know in case one of them got captured.

It was no secret that Xavier Collins was able to read minds.

In fact, many of his abilities weren’t secrets. That was why they’d sent a time mage in the first place. They knew they would need one to deal with him.

Xavier gritted his teeth. The twin Denizens had paused to search the area with their spiritual sense, not willing to take any chances before they returned through the portal, a little wary of Alica’s inexplicable moment of fright. But they would have gotten a kill notification when they’d killed the drone, confirming “Xavier’s” death, so they didn’t appeared worried about having failed their mission.

That was something he and Xavier Prime had spent a good bit of time working on when their respective Palinis had contacted them. They’d known it was a trap and both were confident Palini was under duress. Whoever had enough resources to trap a man like Palini—who was far more powerful than a normal B Grade was after Xavier had spent time training him—would likely have him somewhere safe until this was all over.

Which meant simply killing the hit squad who’d come for him wasn’t the right move. Palini would be lost forever or used as a bargaining chip.

Xavier wouldn’t allow that to happen.

This was a problem he’d known he would face. Even with powerful time dilation fields and thousands of drones, he couldn’t be everywhere at once. The people who worked for him, and the people he cared about, would be vulnerable. Not just to widespread attacks from another sector ruler’s army, but to targeted missions like this one.

It had already happened before, back on Earth, when The Collector had sent an agent to kidnap those close to him.

So, Xavier and Xavier Prime had ensured that when the decoy drone was killed by the hit squad there would be no chance of them knowing it was a drone. That way, Xavier could follow the hit squad back to wherever they were holding Palini with his concealment active.

This had been a difficult problem to solve. When he’d killed the Arakashinai drones, the fact that they were drones came up in the kill notifications. Even Identify was enough to reveal that kind of information.

So they’d carefully crafted another Create Drone spell, one that wouldn’t leave a trace of information about the drones actually being drones. A spell that would make it out like that drone was the real Xavier Collins.

And, of course, they’d tested it out, going as far as killing each other’s drones to receive kill notifications about them.

The twin Denizens’ spiritual sense search didn’t turn up a single thing, and Xavier’s concealment was strong enough that their scrutiny failed to reveal him. Xavier had every opportunity to take control of the twin Denizens. He was fast enough and powerful enough to control all three of them, but he quickly realised that there was someone on the other side of that portal paying close attention to what was happening. Someone who was coordinating the mission as a whole.

The twin Denizens were already wary after Alica’s reaction when Xavier took control of her mind, if that happened to the other two Denizens, the man coordinating the mission would feel it and became highly suspicious.

Striker: [Return to base. Immediately.]

The man had a deep, commanding voice, strong even through the Communication Stone, and the words came out like a growl.

The twins glanced at each other. Xavier could tell they wanted to say something, but the second they’d received that communication he could finally sense a hint of fear wafting off them. The three Denizens communicated a brief, [Yes, sir.] through the linked Communication Stone channel then turned to make their way back through that portal.

Xavier watched them hover toward the portal in what to him looked like slow motion. A hit squad of three peak B Grades who’d been sent to take him out, and they were afraid of whoever was on the other side of that portal.

And from all he’d learnt from infiltrating Alica’s mind, he knew exactly why.

Striker, their immediate boss, was A Grade.

Whoever had set this up was more powerful than Xavier had thought.

He stared at the portal. The whole point of this plan, creating the decoy drone and letting it die, was so he could get to Palini—wherever the man was.

Now, Xavier doubted he would be strong enough to break him free. If he had the strength he’d possessed when he’d killed the World Destroyer, he wouldn’t be worrying at all. But this wasn’t his territory. He couldn’t use that power here.

And he certainly couldn’t take it with him through that portal.

As he hovered there, watching as the first of the hit squad floated through the portal back to the base where they’d come from, a few dark thoughts entered his mind.

How important is Palini to the cause, really? To saving the universe? I can take the Orin sector without him…

Isn’t the universe more important than the life of one man?

Xavier sat with those thoughts for a moment as the second member of the hit squad floated through the portal. The thoughts made sense. It was rational not to risk everything for the life of a single individual. This was like the trolley problem, but on an even more massive scale.

But the trolley problem had always pissed him off.

Why did you have to choose?

Why couldn’t you do both?

Xavier threw off a quick message to Xavier Prime, updating him on the situation. Xavier Prime told him to wait, that he would join him, but Xavier refused to let that happen. So far, they hadn’t produced anymore unfoldings in the void. Out of all the different Xaviers in the infinite universes, they were the only two versions that anyone knew had a shot at saving the universe.

It would be foolish to risk them both.

I’ll keep in contact if I can. That way you can learn from anything that happens on the other side.

Xavier wasn’t going to let himself believe that he would fail. He had put himself in worse situations than these and come out on top. Just because there was an A Grade on the other side of that portal didn’t mean he couldn’t handle it. But he also wasn’t going to allow himself to be naïve anymore. He flapped his wings, his concealment still strong around him, and flew toward the portal. Alica, the time mage whose mind he had full control over, was hovering in the air in front of the portal. Xavier had delayed her entering so that he could go through first.

When he reached the portal, he froze.

He looked at Alica, looked at the portal, and thought about his concealment.

The concealment had worked perfectly against the various senses and detection methods of the hit squad. But he had no guarantee that it would work against whoever was on the other side of that portal. Even with access to Alica’s mind, he didn’t know the specifics of the security measures that were in place inside the base.

That was information she didn’t need to perform her mission, so she didn’t have it.

What he did know was that there was a good chance the A Grade on the other side would be able to break through his concealment.

Xavier hesitated for no more than a fraction of a second as an alternate plan occurred to him. He couldn’t think about it for too long. There wasn’t exactly much time to execute it. His time dilation field was faster than the time mage’s, but not by much, and from the speed at which they’d received the communication from Striker back at their base, there was another time dilation field in place on the other side, one that was on par with Alica’s.

If he was going to change the plan, he needed to act fast.

Thoughts raced through his mind—

This is insane.

I can’t pull something like this off.

But if it works…

He moved. His time dilation field might not have been all that much faster than his enemy’s, but his mind and body could move fast enough to compensate for that.

He hoped.

Xavier turned from the portal and faced Alica. He tilted his head to one side. This next move wasn’t his first choice, and the plan was a crazy one, but it was the best chance he had at saving Palini and coming out of this mission alive. The more the other parts of his mind sifted through the information he gleaned from the time mage, the more he realised this woman didn’t know very much.

She didn’t know what sector she came from.

She didn’t know who her boss’s boss was.

She didn’t know who she truly served.

All she knew was enough to perform her mission—and from what he could tell, the same was true of the twin Denizens who’d already made it back through the portal.

A dozen contracts were etched into her soul, and someone had played with her memories, erasing a great deal of them to make her and others like her the perfect agent, for if she was captured, she wouldn’t be able to give information she didn’t possess.

And she didn’t know anything.

There was a small part of Xavier that couldn’t help but admire the planning that must have gone into creating someone like that, but mostly he was horrified by the prospect.

He doubted these people went into this line of work voluntarily.

Which means she might be innocent, her only crime not being strong enough to resist being controlled by whoever did this to her…

Xavier gritted his teeth. That was the case for the majority of enemy combatants he’d encountered since being integrated into the System. He’d killed millions—more—who’d been forced to fight him.

Now wasn’t the time for his conscience to get in the way.

Xavier drew Bones in the form of a sword. In one swift strike he cut off the time mage’s head and reaped her soul—not into his soul reserve, but into Bones. Then, he shifted Bones into a stylus, summoned a drone and handed the stylus to it, then he worked as fast as he could.

He was about to perform his first Class Grafting.

On himself.
