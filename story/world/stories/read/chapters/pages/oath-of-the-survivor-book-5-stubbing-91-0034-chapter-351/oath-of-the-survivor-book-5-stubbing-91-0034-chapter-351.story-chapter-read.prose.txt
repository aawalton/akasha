[CAN YOU PROVIDE ANY MORE MANA, DR. MAYHEW?]

“I’m working on it,” Kyle sighed as he kept Genesis Engine active, funneling power through their connection into C.H.A.D.D.’s garden within the World Tree Seed.  The separate pocket of space was bathed in a golden glow from the skill, and Kyle kept Cultivate active constantly to keep providing new seeds and growth for the drone to manipulate and organize.  Of course, C.H.A.D.D. wasn’t satisfied with just that level of support, and was taking all the mana Kyle could provide through Parasitic Resonance to make adjustments.

Though the drone was loath to admit it, Kyle knew that the destruction of parts of the garden were actually a good thing.  Their understanding of how to use the World Tree Seed had been rudimentary at best when he and C.H.A.D.D. first encountered the seed, and much of what they’d accomplished amounted to building on flawed foundations.  Even C.H.A.D.D.’s Knotted Vine Core; built off the template of the Pyrocore Sphere they found during Phase Three of the Praticum, was inefficient compared to what it could be.

Unfortunately, as was often the case, the enemy of greatness was “good enough.”  Even as the duo began to identify gaps in their use of the World Tree Seed, it all functioned well enough to keep them from wanting to really dig in and make changes.  There were plenty of other priorities during their time stranded on Kertan Six to distract them.  Plus, neither Kyle nor C.H.A.D.D. were big fans of disruption.  Now that there was no choice but to do some rebuilding, they were giving it their singular focus.  Between their better understanding of the World Tree Seed and the meaningful improvement to Kyle’s attributes from being a good distance into C Grade, the work was moving at a blistering pace.

After only a focused week of work, the garden itself had been largely redesigned.  Each of the various plants and constructs C.H.A.D.D. grew were overseen by a unique new chaddling variant, born from the absorbed essence of the Scourge.  It was the plant monster’s ability to decentralize that allowed for the initial creation of the chaddlings; an ability that only improved now that they’d finished absorbing its essence.  These new variants were immobile and utterly useless for combat purposes, but they had a singular intent woven into their being: growth.

Bathing in the golden energy of Genesis Engine, they kept control of the different zones of the garden, removing the need for C.H.A.D.D. to spend much conscious effort at all.  The only things the drone refused to delegate to them so far were the stockpile of artificial organs for Kyle, the Knotted Vine Core upgrades, and the creation of new chaddlings.  The delegation allowed C.H.A.D.D. to focus much more of its energy on these endeavors, with impressive results.

Before, the Knotted Vine Core would shift through various formations, allowing the drone to manipulate plant matter which usually took the form of growing layers of various bark over vines or growing plants in unique and unusual shapes.  Now, the core had been split into three separate balls of vines.  The first still governed the ability to weave in bark and thorns to reinforce anything the drone built.

The second of the cores was designed to take advantage of the unique environment within the World Tree Seed, and was almost entirely engraved with runes for mana storage and amplification.  Effectively, the drone had a power battery that would continue to store and produce energy as long as the materials would hold out.  Given these vines were created from the Scourge itself, that upper threshold was well into C Grade levels of power.  Eventually, once it was fully charged, its presence would mean that Kyle wouldn’t need to provide as much energy on an ongoing basis for the basic upkeep of the garden.  Instead, he could put that mana to work to enhance some of his own projects.

The final core was an interesting one, with plates of wood and vines present in equal measure.  Rather than provide immediate utility, it served as a testing ground whose sole purpose was to gauge seeds for compatibility with Kyle’s Graft skill.  This core tapped directly into the archived information stored within the World Tree Seed, and acted as a lab where interesting seed combinations could be tested.  Most of the successful combinations they’d found so far were related to mundane combinations, though Kyle felt like he was getting closer to a breakthrough when it came to combining awakened seeds with mundane ones.

The most exciting new creations, at least to Kyle, were variants on the pogrit seeds.  Keeping all the nutritional content, he’d been able to mix them with various fruits and vegetables to provide a wide variety of different flavors.  While he had no intention of being stranded again any time soon, having some variety in his diet when it inevitably happened again was a major quality of life improvement.

All of this was exciting enough, but alongside all of the creation and updates came something he should have expected: levels.  As Kyle combined a handful of fern spores with some birch tree seeds, he felt another threshold get crossed.  The World Tree Tender class finally reached Level 60, and Kyle had a final skill selection to take care of.

Giving C.H.A.D.D. another surge of mana, he deactivated Genesis Engine and entered meditation, quickly allocating his free attributes to Willpower before looking for the typical three gray orbs representing his skill selection.  He frowned when he saw them.  There were three, as usual, but they were clustered together, their borders overlapping.  As he focused on one, a rush of information entered his mind, as all three appeared at once.

Aspect of the Tree: Branch, Root, or Bark

Looking at the description, he was surprised once more as, rather than a simple impression of the skills, he felt words come to him.

The World Tree Tender accomplishes more than simply nourishing the gifts of nature.  As they shape the tree, it shapes them in turn.  For the Union Symbiote, this truth is more profound than any other.  Now, the World Tree Tender may bring an Aspect of the Tree into themselves, greatly empowering a piece of their connection to the World Tree itself.  As the World Tree Tender progresses, more Aspects of the Tree will be woven into the path.  May the garden grow into eternity.

The first of the aspects he had to choose from was the Branch.  A vision of an expanding golden tree was before him, its branches reaching out endlessly into the sky.  The instinctive knowledge he gained from the vision was that this aspect would greatly empower his ability to create and manipulate constructs made from plants.  The effect looked to be entirely passive, and he got the impression that it would improve with each grade, making his control of external plants leagues better than it currently was.  Already, he knew that C.H.A.D.D. would be metaphorically drooling at the potential this aspect provided.

The second he looked at was Bark.  This time, the vision of the tree’s trunk was before him as a thousand different environments changed around it.  The tree stood unbothered through them all, its bark seemingly ignoring the dramatic shifts.  Similar to his Iron Will, Kyle understood that this Aspect of the Tree would provide him a passive boost to durability, though this one appeared to be specialized in stopping environmental and mana-infused energies as opposed to the emphasis on physical toughness.  Another difference in this case was that it would be almost entirely focused on Kyle’s skin, rather than improving durability all throughout his body.  Between Bark, Iron Will, and his Enhanced Carapace with the Ferric Augmentation, Kyle knew his resilience would be at a level very few people could dream of, even at C Grade.  Moreover, the synergy with his Unbroken Survivor class was incredible.

Lastly, he saw the Aspect of the Tree: Root.  His vision changed to see golden roots, some buried in the soil, others digging into the Between itself.  Everything was nourishment; drawn in, refined, and used to fuel the tree’s growth.  Mana from all around the tree’s roots was drawn in passively, continually feeding it as it expanded.  This aspect would create a minor domain around him, allowing Kyle to increase his passive mana regeneration, while also giving him a constant conduit to atmospheric mana he could refine.  He also expected it would lightly drain energy from other hostile effects as well, absorbing power thrown at him and passively refining it.  If C.H.A.D.D. was excited about Branch, Phaegos would want him to go for Root, synergizing well with the Parasitic Devourer’s other abilities.

As a capstone for his class, the skills certainly didn’t disappoint.  Frankly, Kyle had a gnawing anxiety that his final skill selection would be as lackluster as some of his others had been so far, making this a pleasant surprise.  Looking at the overlapping gray orbs, he also had a surprising realization.  I’ll get all three, eventually.  Rather than his other skill selections, this was a progressive expansion.  He could choose an Aspect of the Tree now, one when his class evolved to D Grade, and the last when his class evolved to C Grade.  He could only hazard a guess at what would happen beyond that, but the idea that he’d progressively unlock all three was exciting.  Each of the Aspects of the Tree had synergy with the others, and would blend in perfectly with the Unbroken Survivor class.

The question now boiled down to which of the abilities was least necessary for him in the coming years.  Given his proximity to his second class evolving, he’d be able to unlock a second Aspect of the Tree fairly soon.  His other skills would certainly offer some interesting upgrade possibilities, but none of them were as immediately enticing to him as getting a second Aspect of the Tree.

To his surprise, the answer came to him in short order.  Bark was powerful, and would be a perfect addition to his other defensive abilities.  At the same time, it offered the fewest new features to round out his kit.  It would, however, be an excellent choice as he entered high-tier C Grade and upgraded Aspect of the Tree alongside Iron Will.  Root was also very interesting, and could have some powerful synergies with Parasitic Resonance, Storm Shelter, and their eventual upgrades.  At the same time, it wasn’t something that was necessarily urgent for him at this point, nor did it provide a lot of new capabilities.

Branch, by contrast, leaned the heaviest into what the World Tree Tender did, and what Kyle would need to keep doing to progress his second class.  Higher-quality manipulation and control of the plants he was using would impact every aspect of his burgeoning combat style, and would allow him to work with C.H.A.D.D. on some truly incredible projects.  Adding to the notion that it could help solve the Graft problem, it was an easy decision.

Feeling the instinctive knowledge settling into place from the skill, Kyle took a breath and turned his attention back to the space within the World Tree Seed.  With a tentative push, he reached out toward one of the prosthetics he and C.H.A.D.D. had been repairing, Model: Shrike.  Almost immediately, he could feel the flaws in the way they’d grown the prosthetic.  The wood was too rigid, limiting range of motion.  Furthermore, the bladed leaves from Er’Mithren had room to be bound more tightly into the wood to allow for better mana conductivity.
