Realizing that the God of Beggars had joined Noah’s side, further lowering my chances of victory, immediately soured my mood.

Whatever progress I had made recently suddenly felt insignificant compared to facing multiple deities. Even if I had secured Athena on my side, or at least ensured she would not fight against me, two gods still remained formidable opponents. And I knew absolutely nothing about this God of Beggars.

When my attempt to activate Guidance failed, nothing unusual, of course, I shifted the Window toward Karla and Ninali instead.

The elf had chosen Ninali to enter the Goddess’s dungeon.

I was not surprised.

Not only had the two grown close to something resembling friends, but Karla was also eager to mentor her. She saw enormous potential in Ninali, who, after switching to fire magic, was progressing at a frightening pace.

And in return, Ninali trusted Karla more than the velmoryns. She had even shared her most intimate thoughts with her, things she had once been reluctant to admit even to herself.

Now the two stood before the violet portal.

It shimmered beneath the curtain of vines, swaying from the gusts created by the swirling magical energy.

“Is there nothing else?” Karla asked.

“No, I already told you everything,” Ninali replied with a faint smile. “I’m starting to think you’re asking simply for the sake of…”

“No,” Karla said firmly, interrupting her. “No one knows what awaits inside. And being forced to face what troubles you most is by far the cruelest trial.”

“Do not worry, I cannot be broken so easily. Even if the trial proves dangerous, I brought two fruits from the Tree of Life. And Eshma evolved as well.” Ninali chuckled softly as she removed a stray green leaf from her hair. “Besides, what worries me is not the trial, but what happened to Avenor.”

“What do you mean?”

“He would never admit it. But ever since he entered the dungeon, Father seems dissatisfied with him. What if He…”

“Lord ordered me to explore this dungeon, remember?” Karla cut her off. “You are following His will, not indulging your own desires.”

Despite healing Avenor after the successful campaign against the spider mutants, and even forging a weapon for him, some velmoryns had noticed the change.

Ninali was one of them.

Unlike Aria, who noticed it through the tasks Avenor received, Ninali had sensed the difference through his behavior and attitude toward me.

And honestly, it was not difficult to notice.

Before the link had been severed, Avenor constantly praised me. Now he rarely spoke about me at all, and whenever he did, he sounded reserved and cautious.

I did not mind.

The change in Avenor’s standing had not harmed me. If anything, it strengthened the velmoryns’ belief that no one stood above my wrath. If I was willing to punish one of my blessed followers, then what would happen to an ordinary believer who sinned?

It was complete nonsense, of course. But it was nonsense that benefited me, so I had no reason to correct it.

The two women exchanged a few more words before Ninali finally stepped into the portal. Violet swallowed her as her figure disappeared into the swirling vortex of magical energy.

That moment was the main reason I had been observing them.

I wanted to see whether our connection would be severed, just as it had been with Avenor. Unlike him, Ninali was not immune to my divinity. So even if our link was cut, I was confident I could restore it later.

It wasn’t.

I could still clearly feel Ninali and even hear her thoughts.

Unfortunately, I could no longer see through her eyes.

The Goddess clearly had no intention of allowing her dungeon to be observed from the outside.

After Ninali’s figure disappeared, Karla sat down on the moist soil and pulled two giant eggs from her Veilspace.

The moment my precious creatures appeared out of nowhere, I tensed. What if the Veilspace dimension harmed them? How could Karla take such a risk?

It was one thing when they were in a dormant state, but after absorbing Kurraghal’s life force and being fed Karla’s mana, they had awakened. What if storing living creatures inside Veilspace was harmful…

Guidance.

[Reviving Extinct Species]

The eggs are almost ready to hatch. The beasts inside await a stimulus.

A stimulus? So Karla’s mana was not enough?

I had assumed that once she fed the beasts enough of her formless magical energy, they would hatch. But now I was no longer certain.

Perhaps they required the same kind of stimulus as angels. After all, they were not ordinary mortal creatures.

Still, there was nothing else I could do. I had no idea what kind of trigger they needed, nor was I about to cause cataclysms just to test whether it would force them out of their shells.

Literally.

Besides, Tekla had finally begun the next stage of Eriavel’s development, and I fully intended to monitor it closely.

I cast one final glance toward Karla. She had already begun feeding mana into the eggs, silvery energy gently coiling around them as they hungrily absorbed every trace of it.

Greedy bastards…

It was a good sign. The more they consumed, the stronger they would become.

Finally, I attached one of my threads of thought to Ninali, making sure I would not miss any change in her emotions. I was not certain whether I could help her in any way, but at the very least, I could try.

The Window shifted toward Tekla.

She stood beside Lucas, who was glaring at the five velmoryns in front of them as though he were moments away from charging at them and slaughtering them all.

“This mark,” he began, raising his hand to display my symbol etched into his skin, “was given to me by our Lord. I alone carry it!”

For the first time since… well, ever, Lucas spoke with genuine emotion. His voice was filled with pride as he displayed the mark he had received after my second blessing.

I still had no idea why it had formed back then, and Guidance had refused to provide any explanation.

But perhaps my Rank had simply been insufficient at the time.

Maybe now it would work…

A blue window flashed before my eyes as I activated Guidance.

[Mark of Unrelenting Faith]

Although all Velmoryns receive your soul mark, those who surrender their lives completely and place absolute trust in you may unknowingly form a deeper bond.

This bond grants no immediate power, yet it stands as the ultimate proof of faith - a testament to the adversity they endured and overcame through sheer will and unwavering trust in their god.

Believers who possess this mark and reach Platinum Rank may awaken semi-divine classes belonging to your domain.

Seeing how worked up Lucas was, Tekla gently placed a hand on his shoulder. It calmed him immediately. Lucas glanced at her, then looked away as a faint red painted his long, pointed ears.

“You have all shown the willingness to become the first members of the Crimson Root,” Tekla said as she stepped toward the five velmoryns, smiling softly while they lowered their eyes before her. “But I will ask one final time. Even if you do not receive the same mark as Lucas, even if you are given the most dangerous quests, are you certain you wish to take this step?”

“I am, Priestess!”“I am, Priestess!”“I am, Priestess!”…

All five answered at once.

I quickly scanned them.

Four men and one woman, all melee fighters. Unlike the others, the woman had no class. She was also the only Bronze Rank among them. The rest were all Silver.

Even after the merger of the tribes, Gold Ranks remained extremely rare.

Should I bless these five?

I considered it briefly, but quickly dismissed the idea.

If I blessed them, it would completely undermine the purpose of the Crimson Root.

Tekla had designed it as an order for the most devout believers. Warriors directly under her command, those who would raise their blades only against enemies of the faith.

Their missions would be the most dangerous, and unlike others, their successes would not bring them glory most of the time because their objectives had to remain secret. That was why the members of the order needed genuine devotion. If their motivation became personal gain, they would never last.

Which was precisely why I could not grant them blessings.

If the main attraction of the order became receiving my blessing, then everyone would want to join. Who would refuse guaranteed favor from their god after all?

“Lucas, guide them to the order’s den. I shall join you after blessing the women carrying our future. They must be cared for above all others.”

Lucas nodded and led the group toward the temple while Tekla turned and walked toward the Crimson Guardian.

That was yet another pleasant piece of news.

After nearly a year, the fifth commandment had finally produced results. Quite a few women within the tribe were pregnant. With their lives becoming more peaceful and their god clearly favoring reproduction, the velmoryns had begun to multiply at last.

This is nothing. Just wait until I make Athena play cupid…

As Tekla walked toward the center of the settlement, Dirion hurried toward her.

“Priestess,” he said, practically beaming. “The council requests that you bless our new Vael.”
