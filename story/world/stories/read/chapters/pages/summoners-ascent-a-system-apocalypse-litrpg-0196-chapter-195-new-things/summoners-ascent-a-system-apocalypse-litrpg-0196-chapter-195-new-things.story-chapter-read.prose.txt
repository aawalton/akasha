Sure, demons didn't exactly have the same lifecycle as humans did, but since Alice was copying humans due to proximity to Ashton, she was, for some reason, reaching similar milestones.

"What's that?" Millie asked, squatting down next to Ashton. She was standing next to the monsters' dead bodies without even flinching, completely unbothered.

"It's something made from one of the Backpacker's abilities. This one has all the magic stones from orcs," Ashton explained. He opened the top of the pouch and turned it over, watching as a large pile of pale magic stones fell to the ground. It was far more than should fit into this bag. "Neat, right?"

But you see, these magic stones are created because most beings aren't able to naturally process mana in their bodies. But they're still filtering that mana, and changing it ever so slightly depending on, well, a lot of factors. Age, health, size, but the most important one is species."

Millie looked at the stone in her hand. "Really? Why is it important?"

So, when it comes to more advanced and precise uses of these stones, using the right combination of strengths and weaknesses can be pretty important."

They circumvented certain monster camps, dealt with fights, and even encountered a small group of survivors here and there.

Ashton got pretty excited with all the new and more varied monsters that had been popping up. Like the 'Red Wolves'; even before, they had somehow taken on a vibe that was more like that of a stray dog, but now they actually started looking more like different dog breeds.

According to Millie, they still had that red, almost blood-stained looking fur to them, but they were definitely different to before, and before long, they might even get new species classifications. Like a 'Red Retriever', or a 'Red Bulldog'.

Similarly, the humanoid monsters were showing up with more 'modern' looking equipment. They were things that looked like they had been carried around by them for years, but the quality was definitely like something from earth. Shirts, shoes, even the weapons. They weren't just items that the monsters had scavenged anymore.

Clearly, the system was adjusting to the world as time went on. The same way that new shops and items kept popping up that Ashton had never seen before, the same could be said for monsters.

And then, there was one that Ashton had truly never seen before. A new species of one of his favourite monsters that he was generally pretty excited to see, since every single one of them was ridiculously useful.

It was a slime.

It was the most disgusting slime that Ashton had ever seen, but it was a slime nonetheless!

Ashton had spotted it as him and Millie took a shortcut through an alleyway, and something had started to move within one of those large industrial rubbish bins. When it crawled out and over the edge, with an uncomfortable squelching sound, it pulled tons of old and rotten rubbish out with it. Pieces of plastic were sticking out of its body, and it dragged along the stench of the worst garbage you've ever smelled, and even if he couldn't see its colour, Ashton was immediately reminded of a puddle of sludge left behind at the bottom of a rubbish bin.

As it kept moving, it continued to pick up things from the ground. It stuck to the side of the bin as well.

"Oh, it must be a variation of a trapper slime," Ashton said curiously. He picked up a stone from beside him and flicked it at the monster. The stone was stopped even though he threw it with quite some force, and was slowly enveloped by that sludge. "Interesting..."

Ashton quickly tagged this monster through the Pen of Insight, and then threw another stone, this time using a throwing technique.

The slime's core was quickly pushed out of the monster's body. Though the form of a normal slime would have collapsed pretty fast, this one was slowly settling into a flattened form with bumps of rubbish all throughout it.

"A 'Trash Slime', huh?" Ashton said with a smile as he quickly approached the monster's corpse, if a dead slime could even be called that. The smell was truly, utterly terrible. Ashton pulled out some of the tall grass that was growing between the cracks on the ground, and held it into the slime. It was dissolving it pretty immediately, though it was quite slow.

Slime species tended to have one of two focuses. First, dissolving something extremely fast. In that case, they were usually focused on a single specific material. Like organics, metals, stone; things like that. And second, being able to dissolve practically everything at a somewhat slower pace, in which case they usually had methods to maximize contact.

Either they were particularly fast or large, or they were sticky like this one. Usually, trapper slimes were great materials for crafting things, since you could make some of the best glue in the world with them that could adhere to literally anything, but Ashton was a bit worried about this specific sort.

He wasn't sure if he could filter this slime enough to get rid of the smell. In that case, that would definitely narrow down what he could actually make with this.

Well, either way, Ashton would collect it for now. Slimes seemed to struggle with dissolving things like rubber, glass, and most types of plastic, so with a pair of rubber gloves, Ashton was able to get rid of all the stuff stuck in it before collecting the slime in one of the Backpacker's new glass bottles.

Ashton threw the gloves away immediately, and he didn't get any on himself, but when he walked back up to Millie, she pinched her nose while looking up at him.

"Don't come too close, you stink," she grumbled.

Ashton laughed. "What, you don't like the smell of hot rubbish?"
