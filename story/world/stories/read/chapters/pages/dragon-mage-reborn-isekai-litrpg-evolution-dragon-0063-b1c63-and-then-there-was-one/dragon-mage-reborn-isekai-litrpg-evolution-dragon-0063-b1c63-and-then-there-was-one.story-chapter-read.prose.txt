My blood ran cold. ‘Theron.’

“In the flesh. Well, his flesh, to be precise.” He snickered. The Prince of Darkness took in a breath of air and reveled in the sensation. “I must thank you, Freya. For accelerating my release. Do you know how long it’s been since I’ve had a proper body? Decades of existing as pure essence, and now...” He flexed Zaiden’s restored arm again, shadow energy crackling between his fingers. “This fae’s resilience is remarkable, I’ll give him that. Then again, you had a lot to do with it, didn’t you? You… infernal dragon…. You dark child….”

‘Let him go!’ I snarled, my claws extending instinctively. ‘His body is not yours to take!’

“Oh, but it’s not my body, dearest. It is yours. He is your thrall after all, yes? Even so….” He cracked his neck and his eyes turned pitch black. “Any prison is better than that of a dungeon core.”

I winced.

“I’d almost forgotten what it feels like. To be free from the limitations of that blasted core!” Theron continued, rolling Zaiden’s shoulders as if trying on a new coat. “Do you have any idea what it’s like? Being bound to a single location, forced to operate by those ridiculous rules?”

I kept my distance, watching him carefully as Dusty growled beside me.

“I couldn’t leave my designated area. Couldn’t improvise. Everything—everything—had to follow the system’s parameters.” He sneered as he paced. “There were specifications I had to abide by, until ten years ago, when things in my dungeon became a bit more… lenient. All of a sudden, I was able to abduct souls, powerful bodies of pure energy that I could manipulate. Slowly, I created that vastness that became my domain, and there, I built my small empire. The more adventurers who crawled through my home, the more powerful I became. In the hope that one day, I may grow powerful enough to break through the shackles of this place, and become whole again. Now, thanks to you, I am reborn! Liberated!”

‘And a slave to my command,’ I reminded him bitterly. ‘You’re still my thrall.’

“Our arrangement is more complicated than that. Zaiden is your thrall, and he is my vessel. While that makes me bound to his body, I am not bound to serve you. I am merely a passenger.”

He was right. I hadn’t conquered him, not like I had Zaiden. However, I did absorb his essence, and part of his darkness was mine to command.

He had to know that, which meant he was bluffing. While I was hoping that his impression would remain dormant in Zaiden, it was clear that wasn’t the case. I needed to get rid of him somehow, and quick.

‘You’re not as clever as you think you are, Prince,’ I said, stepping forward despite every instinct telling me to run. ‘You may be riding along in Zaiden’s body, but your essence is still mine. I consumed it, which means I can control it.’

Theron’s borrowed face twisted into an amused grin. “Is that so? Then by all means, try to expel me.”

‘You would like that, wouldn’t you? In Zaiden, you’re limited. If I released Zaiden from my thrall, then you’ll have free range to do whatever you like.’

“What difference does it make? You have my power, but you have no idea how to use it.”

‘I might hate the idea of using dark magic, but I promise, I can still wipe the floor with you.’ I took flight, prepared to fight him one on one. ‘I made a promise to Zaiden. I’m taking you down.’

Theron laughed, and the sound was all wrong coming from Zaiden. “You’re welcome to try, little dragon. But I wonder—can you bring yourself to harm this body? To risk damaging your precious thrall?”

I didn’t hesitate. I didn’t give him the moment of weakness he was fishing out from me. Instead, I ordered my thralls to attack. My dire wolf and my dire wasp launched themselves at Theron simultaneously. Dusty went low, aiming for his legs, while Weedrill descended from above with his sting attack.

But Theron moved with inhuman speed, shadow energy erupting from Zaiden’s restored arm. The darkness formed thick flexible tendrils that sent both my thralls flying backward into the stone pillars.

I followed up with a flank attack, unleashing a spicy [Flame Breath]. He sidestepped my aerial assault and countered with a blast of concentrated shadow. The two energies collided in midair, creating a violent blast that forced me to fly back.

“You think too small,” Theron taunted, stalking toward me as I regained my balance. “This domain you’ve created—it’s barely a fraction of what you could wield if you embraced your true nature.”

‘I know what you're trying to do,’ I growled, circling him warily. ‘You’re trying to corrupt me.’

“Corrupt?” He laughed again. “There’s nothing to corrupt. You’re already dark, Freya. You’ve always been. It’s in your blood. The necromancy, the thrall-binding, your ability to extract my power—you think these are accidents? You think the light in you is your true nature?”

I snarled and dove at him again. I tested my speed on him while incorporating my breath attacks. It didn’t help that he knew how to shadow-port too, gliding in and out of my vision whenever he wanted to. It also didn’t help that he was a quick healer. These skills were from the prince, and not necessarily Zaiden, and even though I’d consumed his essence, I had no idea how to do any of those things.

Luckily for me, it was still three against one. This time, we worked more like a team. My dire puppy could teleport too, keeping the prince on his toes. My dire bug was helping me flank him, doing a perfect job of distracting him as it acted like a complete nuance zip-lining back and forth above his head. I managed to rake across his chest, tearing through Zaiden's shirt and drawing blood. But the wounds sealed themselves almost instantly, shadow energy knitting the flesh back together.

That was fine—his stamina was going to die down soon enough.

I used my [Vine Slash] to reel him in by his ankles. When the poisonous vines snapped tight around his legs, I yanked hard, sending Theron crashing to the cathedral floor. Back and forth like a rag-doll I gave the prince the business, finally noticing the exhaustion on his face. Unfortunately my juggling him around made him extremely angry, and he exploded in a burst of shadow energy that shattered my vines and sent me flying backward into a row of pews. I tumble-weeded until my momentum slowed down enough for me to catch my footing. When I did, I looked up to find Theron in the air, shooting black darts of energy at me. I dodged them with difficulty, each one tearing through stone and wood where they struck, leaving smoking craters.

“Enough games,” Theron snarled, gathering shadow energy in his palm until it formed a pulsing orb of darkness. “You want to see what real power looks like? Allow me to demonstrate.”

He hurled the orb at me, and I barely managed to evade it. The sphere struck one of the cathedral’s pillars instead, instantly consuming it in darkness before the entire structure crumbled to dust.

I stared at the disintegrated pillar. That could have been me.

He’s going to bring down the entire cathedral, I realized, scanning the grand ceiling above us. Already, cracks were spreading where the support had been, dust raining down.

But this is still my domain, I reminded myself, narrowing my eyes at Theron. The cathedral around us trembled slightly as I focused my will on it. The broken pews began to shift, splinters and fragments rising into the air.

I used my domain against him. Dusty and Weedrill fought with me against the prince’s barrage of shadow manipulation attacks. Dark energy was certainly his specialty, using everything from shadow camouflage to projectile shadow spikes that consumed everything they touched. I had to concentrate, pushing my domain control to its limits.

The cathedral continued to respond to my will, marble floor tiles cracking and rising to intercept his attacks, stained glass windows shattering and reforming into glittering shields. The floating wooden fragments I’d summoned formed a barrier around me, absorbing a blast that would have torn through my scales.

Theron was relentless with his dark magic, which didn’t make much sense to me. Just when I thought his stamina was running dry, he picked back up again. I was willing to bet he was tapping into Zaiden’s life force somehow. He was pretty good at extracting those when he was a dungeon boss in his own domain. The last thing I needed was this asshole sapping his vessel’s essence to put me down.

I had to separate him from Zaiden somehow. There had to be a way to exorcise Theron’s consciousness without harming my thrall. Think, Freya!

Wait, I was a necromancer….

I could manipulate souls. That’s what necromancy was, at its core—the control of life and death, the manipulation of spiritual energy. And what was Theron now but a soul invading a body?

‘Dusty, Weedrill, keep him busy!’ I commanded, diving behind the altar. I needed just a moment to concentrate, to reach into the darkness I’d just absorbed. My thralls obeyed, and my plan was to use the prince’s own powers to trap him inside my domain. I understood that I couldn’t induct him into my thrall, and I also understood that I couldn’t take on his full power without Zaiden as my conduit. But the dark prince’s essence, his spirit, I could try to trap it in this altar, just like the dungeon core trapped him in the dungeon.

I placed my claws on the altar, feeling the cold stone beneath my scales as I closed my eyes and focused. My thralls were being worked up right now, but I had to tune it out, diving deep into the well of darkness I’d absorbed from Theron.

The power was intoxicating, its raw potential trying to pull me in. I could feel it whispering promises of limitless ability, of dominion over life and death itself. But I wasn’t here to embrace it like the prince had. I was here to use it against its former master.

‘I call upon the darkness that binds,’ I whispered to myself, the shadows around my claws beginning to pool and swirl on the altar’s surface. I was shocked to have triggered it without losing myself, Zaiden serving as a perfect conduit. ‘By the power of darkness that flows through my blood, I command this altar to become an eternal vessel.’

The stone beneath my touch began to transform, black veins spreading through it like ink in water. The altar vibrated, and I felt the necromantic energy responding to my will. The altar was becoming something more—a spiritual anchor, a prison of stone.

In front of me, I could hear Theron’s frustrated roar as Dusty managed to sink his teeth into his leg, while Weedrill’s bubbling spit ate at his skin. The poison from my vines was also finally slowing him down, allowing me to finish the crucial part of my binding ritual.

I continued, pouring more of my energy into the altar, ‘I bind the wandering soul to stone, separate the passenger from the host. Theron, Prince of Darkness, I call you forth!’

The altar beneath my claws erupted with dark energy, sending a beam of shadow straight toward Zaiden’s body. It struck him square in the chest, and he went rigid, his back arching as if electrified. Theron’s scream tore from Zaiden's throat—a sound of pure rage and disbelief.

“What are you doing?” he howled, fighting against the pull. “You cannot—”

‘I can and I will,’ I snarled, doubling down on my focus. ‘You’re right. I am stronger than I give myself credit for. Your power inside me is my tool to wield.’

The beam of shadow intensified, and I could see something beginning to separate from Zaiden’s body. A writhing mass of darkness, vaguely humanoid, fighting desperately against the pull of my makeshift prison. Theron’s essence was being torn from his host, strand by strand.

But the prince wasn’t going down without a fight. Even as his spirit was being extracted, he fought me. I strained against his resistance, feeling the altar crack beneath my claws from the immense spiritual pressure. Theron’s rage manifested as violent waves of dark energy that rippled through the cathedral, shattering more windows and sending chunks of stone cascading from the ceiling.

“You think you can cage me again?” Theron’s voice echoed as the writhing shadow pulled toward the altar. “I have tasted freedom! I will not be imprisoned!” The extraction was taking everything I had. I poured more energy into the binding as I noticed symbols begin to carve themselves on the surface of the altar. Binding sigils glowed with purple light, growing brighter as more of Theron’s essence was pulled from Zaiden’s body.

“STOP THIS!” Theron’s disembodied voice thundered through the cathedral, the walls trembling with his fury. But I could hear something else in his tone now—fear.

I gritted my teeth, pushing through the strain. My wings flared wide as I channeled more power into the binding. His aura was like a torrent of wind trying to push me back. As much as I tried, I felt like I was slowly losing the battle, until I felt a familiar aura permeate around me—it was Nolan.

I couldn’t see him but I could feel him, his energy anchoring my own. His essence mingled with mine, lending me his strength. His presence reinforced my will.

‘Nolan,’ I whispered inwardly, with unshed tears stinging my eyes. ‘Help me finish this.’

His spiritual energy merged with mine, creating a surge of power that intensified the binding. The beam connecting the altar to Theron’s essence brightened until it was almost blinding. With one final, desperate pull, I yanked the last of Theron’s consciousness from Zaiden’s body.

The Prince of Darkness screamed as his essence was completely torn from his host and pulled into the altar. The stone cracked and reformed around him, trapping his spirit in its core. A final pulse of dark energy swept through the cathedral as the binding completed, and then silence fell.
