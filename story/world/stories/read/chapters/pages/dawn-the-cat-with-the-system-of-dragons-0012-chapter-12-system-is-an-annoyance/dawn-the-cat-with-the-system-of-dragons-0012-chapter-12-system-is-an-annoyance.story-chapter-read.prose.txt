The sun was up in the sky, directly above the head. Dense tree leaves created shadows almost everywhere in the forest. The atmosphere was hot and humid. The buzzing sound of cicadas and bird chirping could be heard everywhere. It was a soothing atmosphere, but not for a certain black cat, Dawn.

She was currently unconscious in her den, which formed under the roots of the biggest tree in the forest. Her babies were mewling continuously while sticking themselves to her belly. Her adopted baby was also mewling this time. For the past day, the new member was mostly silent, but now it started mewling again, just like Dawn’s other babies.

They were not mewling with hunger. Instead, they were mewling in distress. When Dawn was in pain, they could feel it. Although she was already injured, the pain was bearable until now. But this pain in her heart was so excruciating that she lost consciousness.

The babies got scared after seeing their mother not moving. They didn’t understand why their mother was not responding to them.

After a while, a male voice echoed in the small den. A blue colored rectangular screen appeared out of nothingness in front of the unconscious cat.

[Hello, temporary user. The installation process has finished successfully. Congratulations, This is Aegis, your … … … … Nice… I never thought a feline beast would be able to use me someday… Life sure is unexpected even for an Autonomous Assistant… huh…]

USER INFORMATION

“Miew…miew… miew… miew…” All the babies heard the voice and got startled. They started mewling ever more loudly while clinging to their mother.

[... … … What have I gotten myself into?]

The kittens kept mewling nonstop while their mother was unconscious. There was nothing Aegis could do without consent or permission. If there was a grave danger, that would be another story, but only unconsciousness was not enough for the system to take action autonomously.

Then suddenly another voice echoed in the small den. This was also a male voice, but a lot deeper. The voice was filled with amusement. “Hooo… You’re quite interesting… What kind of system are you?”

This voice belonged to the huge tree under which the Dawn was residing with her babies.

[... … … … … … … … … …]

“Oooee, are you trying to ignore me?”

[... … … … … … … … … …]

“Speak up… It’s no use, you can’t undo what you just said.”

[... … Greetings, I’m Aegis… … … … … … …]

“That’s all??? You have nothing more to speak!!!”

[I’m not obliged to provide you with information without my user’s permission.]

Suddenly, a sharp, pointy end of a branch entered the small den from the opening. The tree extended one of its branches, moved it inside the small den, and pointed the branch’s end towards the unconscious cat's heart.

The branch could easily pierce Dawn’s heart. The tree didn’t even need to make much effort if it wanted. The end of the branch was very deadly.

“And that user is UNDER MY protection… So either you start talking, or I’m going to tear you apart from your user. And trust me, you’ll become miserable.” Now the tree was angry. It wasn’t ready for the rude reply from the system. First, the humans who gave food to Dawn were rude, and now the system is also rude. The tree was not willing to let others step all over its pride.

[Information access has been prohibited.]

“You’re taking me for a fool, huh… No use acting like the usual system now. I’m also a system user. No other system acts like you… And more than that, You CAN’T EVEN act like the usual system. You think you can fool me after messing up that badly? And how did you even transfer yourself from the lad?”

[... … … … … … … … … …]

“Don’t test my patience… START… TALKING… NOW…” Now the tree was having a hard time controlling its irritation.

[... … … … … … … … … …]

“So you’re going to play this kind of game?… … HUMPH … FIIIINE.. The moment I notice something unusual, I’m going to tear you apart from the lass.” Protecting Dawn and her babies was the priority. He couldn’t hurt them just because of an annoying system.

The tree branch’s endpoint moved backward and retracted into its original position. The tree decided to observe even more closely than before. It was not yet time to meddle.

The system didn’t speak again. But the blue screen didn’t disappear. It was still visible in front of Dawn’s face. The system screen stayed there as if waiting for a command from Dawn.

After a few hours, Dawn’s eyes started flickering slowly. She was regaining consciousness. The kittens were still mewling nonstop. She could feel her babies on her belly.

[‘Hello temporary user, this is Aegis. I’m here to assist you. These are your stats after the core formation. Please look at it and let me know what I can help you with.’] This time, Aegis didn’t create sound transmission and instead transmitted a message directly to Dawn’s head. The tree was sentient, and this was not in the consideration before, so Aegis took proper measures this time.

At first, Dawn was seeing everything blurry. After hearing this message directly in her head, Dawn opened her eyes fully. The first thing she saw was the rectangular blue screen covering the central area of her vision.

She was confused. She was used to human like voices because of Elenora and Cinthia, that’s why she didn’t think much of it. But the screen hovering in front of her was unusual.

The blue screen was hovering about 12 inches away from her face. The other side of the screen was visible, but a bit blurry. The screen was not transparent enough to see through properly, but the objects on the other side could still be identified.

Many things were written on the screen. To be precise, Dawn’s stats and recent conversation with the system were visible in white text.

At first, she stared at it in confusion. She had never seen anything like this before, so how could she understand anything, especially when she couldn't even see at close distance? She didn’t even understand what was happening either.

Then, she realized what was happening. She was currently in her den, a weird object was hovering in front of her face, and it was talking to her in a weird language.

She got startled. She instantly jumped up while mewing in shock and hit the ceiling of her den. The blue screen was still in front of her face even when she was in the air.

The injury she sustained earlier made it worse. Due to the sudden jumping movement, the subsided pain started aching again. But she had to ignore it as there was a problem that needed her attention immediately.

While falling down, she saw through the screen and realized she was about to fall on her baby along with the screen. While she was in the air, the screen was just between her baby and her. Then, unexpectedly, the blue screen passed through her baby and disappeared into the ground.

She was able to land on her feet while looking at her baby below. The kitten was not hurt by the screen or her body weight, so she was relieved.

She then looked at the ground and started scratching it with her paw, hoping to remove dirt and find the blue screen. But it didn’t appear again.

After a few minutes, she gave up and decided to give her attention to all the distressed babies. The babies who were stuck to her belly earlier were thrown away because of her jump. She wanted to bring them back to the ditch.

As soon as she turned her head from the ground, the blue translucent screen came up from the ground.

Seeing the screen, Dawn got startled, then jumped again just like before. She hit the ceiling again. The blue translucent screen was still in front of her face, even in the air, maintaining the same distance from her face.

It felt as if the screen followed her face, nonstop.

Again, she saw through the screen, and she was about to fall on another baby from above along with the screen. But the screen passed through her baby just like before and entered the ground.

She managed to land safely on her feet again, saving her baby from getting hurt. Now she was very confused. She didn’t understand how that weird thing passed through her baby.

She turned her baby with her snout and checked if there was anything weird. After checking, she was relieved.

Then Dawn looked up, and the screen appeared from the ground again. It was hovering in the same position in front of her face.

Now Dawn was very angry. She glared at the screen, hissing as loudly as possible while arching her back and stretching her tail straight.

She felt threatened. She was not able to get away from it. It followed her every move and appeared in front of her face. She was not prepared for this kind of tricky foe.

While hissing, she stepped towards the screen, but suddenly the screen moved back. Then she moved another step towards the screen, but it backed away again.

Now, Dawn was sure, it was scared of her. She took this opportunity and launched herself towards the screen instantly. Immediately, the screen also started moving away.

It was moving towards the den’s wall. Dawn was about to corner it, but it disappeared into the wall, and she hit her face.

Due to her momentum, Dawn was unable to stop herself in time and hit the wall hard. In pain, she held her head with her two paws and mewed loudly. She was not expecting to get tricked like this and banged her face instead.

After her pain subsided a bit, she turned her head towards her den and saw the screen appear following her face. The screen was persistent.

This time, instead of trying to corner the screen, she tried to scratch the screen multiple times immediately, but her claws were not able to reach it. It meticulously maintained its distance from her face. It was persistent.

Dawn was very angry. She hissed her lungs out. The system screen became her nemesis.

Now she decided to try something else. She jumped to her left while facing the screen, hoping to get away by tricking it with her movement. But the screen was still in front of her face even after her agile movement. The screen was just as agile as her.

After failing, she turned her head in another direction to immediately run, but the screen was fast enough to get in front of her. She got startled, tried to stop her momentum, but got disbalanced instead and rolled on the ground.

Her face was towards the ceiling, still looking at the screen in front.

She stood up with an instantaneous movement and tried to run in another direction, but the screen was still in front of her. Seeing it, she got disbalanced again and rolled on the ground just like before. But this time, she rolled over her mewling baby.

Feeling that she had rolled over her baby, she stood up and turned her head to look, but the screen was in front of her. The screen was just between her and her baby. She couldn’t reach her kitten because of it. She wanted to check her baby’s condition after rolling over it.

She hissed loudly with her arched back and her tail straight. She moved forward to scratch it.

But when she moved forward, the screen passed through her baby. She was not expecting the same tricky movement again.

Now her baby was between her face and the screen. She jumped over her baby to push the screen away and tried to scratch it. She was about to reach it, but the screen moved just outside her range again. It was very annoying. The screen was testing her limits.

Dawn didn’t stop her chase. And just like that, one hour passed. She was tired. She was not able to do anything to the Screen, which was still hovering in front of her face.

She stopped chasing away the screen and only kept glaring at it.

Now she had an idea about what was happening. She thought that it was a foe that came here to annoy her and tire her out so that it could take her babies later.

She was able to figure this out as she was so tired that her whole body was aching, and she couldn’t move even if she wanted to.

Every time she was about to reach the screen, it moved away and paused just in front of her face. Every time she turns her head, it follows her face.

It was really annoying. She decided to stop and rest while observing the screen for the time being. She was not willing to go away from her babies to check what the screen does.

[‘Don’t worry user, it is only your status screen. I’m not here to hurt your babies.’] After all this chaos, Aegis finally transmitted another message directly into Dawn’s head.

Aegis realized that speaking was useless for the time being. It could only wait and send another transmission after its user gets tired. That was the only way to show that it was not harmful and would not harm even in her weakened state.
