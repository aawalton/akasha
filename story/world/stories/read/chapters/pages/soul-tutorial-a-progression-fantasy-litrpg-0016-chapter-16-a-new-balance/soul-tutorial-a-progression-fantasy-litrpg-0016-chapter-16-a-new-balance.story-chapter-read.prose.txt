Luke gripped the pedestal with all the strength he could muster, commanding the veil to appear before him. Then relief swarmed his whole body as he felt rejuvenated once more. Having to go through 3 restores within a short timeframe showed its effect, as it didn’t feel as great as it had a couple of hours ago.

But he didn’t really pay a lot of attention to it and instead looked towards his inner chamber to see if anything had happened to his soul after all.

A shroud of smoke slowly dissipated, for him to see a white sphere with golden threads spreading across it, but it still had a slight hue of red to it. Yet there wasn’t much he could do after all. It at least appeared to be better, as the whiteness took over the dark. Leaving only the red hue to cause him some uneasiness.

The only way forward was to hope this wouldn’t hinder him later or delay his search for his mother.

With everything out of the way, he might as well try spreading his wings a little, going back to what he did at the very start of all this mess: his trusty exercise.

This time, he could do a lot more and with higher intensity, trying to help build his endurance this time. So, getting on the floor and resting his body weight on one arm, he could hold on to his one-arm pushup stance.

Surprisingly, he maintained his balance well, even though he had lost his left arm. He really missed it, being able to have both arms placed on the floor, having that extra arm for support.

His daily life lost something irreplaceable when he could no longer use both arms, and it’s only when you lose something that you understand what you had going for yourself.

Warming up his muscles a bit, he continued with regular leg workouts, mainly just squats.

Luke shifted his focus back onto the pedestal, wanting to test himself a bit more with his current attributes.

Thinking floor 1 would be just perfect for this situation and he couldn’t get rid of the painful moments he had just experienced a little while ago. He fought to get his mind off things. The phantom pain still lingered in his chest, and the fright of using soul energy only grew even though he knew he had mostly recovered.

Watching the energy fluctuate as the blue slime took shape, Luke stayed there and thought of ways he could make different choices in this fight that he couldn’t in his last one.

But remembering that he didn’t want to push his soul energy too much, since soul energy burnout was possible. He was still happy about one thing: the achievement he received from it all that finally gave him a bonus to constitution, even if the percentage was low.

One more drawback to using soul energy was the wave of exhaustion he felt afterwards, hoping he could find a solution to everything in the future… Then an idea came back to Luke, instead of flooding his limbs with a massive amount of energy. He could concentrate a slight amount to create an energy shell around his limbs.

With one more method to train, without getting into the blue slime range, Luke pulled on the souls as 3 settled inside of him. He willed it to orbit around his own soul for the first time after the incident.

It felt different this time, though, like they were even more difficult to control at the moment. They shifted awkwardly as they tried to steady themselves, but it’s not like he can choose not to use the energy if he wanted to reach a higher stage, so even with pushing through, he tried his very best to start getting the generated energy into his limbs.

The drawback was worse than what he had thought it would be; pain flared up in his chest, even when there were no visible wounds on his body.

So, using the bare minimum he could, he tried his best to get the energy into his fist to at least have the power to crack the slime’s core.

Not being able to do anything else for the moment, Luke rushed towards the slime in a stalking motion with his right leg in the lead, shuffling his way towards it swiftly.

The slime didn’t notice him as he positioned himself almost directly in front of it and launched a surprise attack, striking its core with a slightly empowered fist, though it proved insufficient.

The slime used the shockwave to bounce backward, while shifting its body mass to create a spike that struck out towards his face.

Luke moved his head back while repositioning his body to land another hit with his right fist.

The slime redrawn its spike as Luke tried to wait out its next attack. The seconds ticked by as Luke glared at the slime, not wanting to wait any longer, he rushed in.

Leaning forward, having already shifted his body weight.

The slime lunged forward, swiftly aiming for his neck, trying to stab straight through it, but Luke wouldn’t allow it lying down.

Concentrating the energy back into his arm, he blocked the spike aiming for his neck, but it left quite a big gash along his forearm.

Without waiting for the slime to react, he stepped forward, swinging his arm directly into its body. The energy in his fist shifted, causing an impact that immediately broke the slime core, ending the fight.

So it took almost 2 charged fists of soul energy to kill a blue slime, and he honestly felt very cheerful, with a bright slime flashing across his face.

He still had to accept that he could only use a limited amount of soul energy at the moment.

So Luke went back to check his chamber to see the aftereffects of using soul energy in that fight. The golden lines still had the hue of red to them. But he could tell parts of his own soul had changed to a darker shade of gray.

Scrunching his eyebrows at the sight of it, his heart beat quickened uncontrollably.

Is this permanent damage? He thought.

Compared to how dark it was back when he was experiencing burnout, it currently looked quite healthy in his eyes. Something that would fade out to white in around a few hours. Luke couldn't be too sure, only knowing that if he kept up this level of treatment with his own soul, it was going to turn black sooner or later.

But he had to calm down; at least he didn’t push his soul too far that he would have to go through that pain again.

While looking at the gash he had in his forearm, deciding he could at least still kill green slimes without soul energy. But now he also had to worry about how to better spread out his points for his attributes.

He couldn’t rely on soul energy for everything if this stuff could happen; maybe with a mixture of high attributes and excellent control of soul energy he could do whatever he wanted, but it was too early to actually figure out how he should really start on that path.

Barely knowing anything at all about the system or what awaited him on this journey forward.

Luke could only bottle up his fears and continue on with what would get him to survive this ordeal…Points.

The energy shifted again to reveal a green slime, so he wasn’t worried he couldn’t take this thing on, but when it was time, he would have to try floor 3 at some point. Maybe a challenge that might just be worse than anything he had done before.

He just hoped it wasn’t at the level of the elite slime, shivering at the thought of fighting something so huge and strong again. Even though it would be a different outcome this time, he could put up a better fight against something that came from floor 0.

So, putting his focus back on the boring green slime in front of him, signaling the start of his point-grinding again. He wished he had put more points into physique instead, if he was going to rely on his body more than soul energy at this moment.

Haters couldn’t be choosers. He thought.

So it didn’t stick with him long; before he knew it, he had already grinded enough points for another 3 restores, but not without sustaining some injuries this time around as well.

Looking back up at the sky with a smile at the constant growth of his souls, he wouldn’t run out anytime soon at this rate.

He was practically building up an empire of power cells. With quick counts, it numbered over 60, which was more than anything he had at this point, but he couldn’t take advantage of everything just yet.

While being injured, he couldn’t risk spawning the elite slime but he could now think about what he should invest his points in.
