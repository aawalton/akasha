Soulbound Steel: An Isekai LitRPG Medieval Fantasy Adventure

Pulled through a portal at a Renaissance fair into a world where spiritual power determines your worth. Eryk Ragnar is told he has none. Zero affinities to his name. Nothing the Net recognizes as useful.

What he has is stubbornness, a dark sense of humor about his own odds, and the categorical, relentless refusal to treat any situation as unwinnable enough to actually stop.

Oh, and a 'blessing' from a god of death. One that comes with a power most people in this world have never seen before, and a debt he hasn't been asked to repay. Yet.

The world is harsh and unforgiving. The enemies are real. The problems keep piling up.

But Eryk is simply not one to back down from a challenge.

In a world where kindness and cruelty walk side by side, he works to destabilize powerful factions, fight for what he believes in, and destroy anyone who stands in his way. And maybe even make some friends along the way...

What to expect:

- Weak to strong MC

- Exciting power system combining classic LitRPG with Xianxia tropes

- Guild politics and hardcore progression, where power and respect do not come easily
