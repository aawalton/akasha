Unlike the others presented, Clyde seems much calmer, much less prone to the well-known Velli arrogance. He seems content to listen to guidance, to follow the instructions of the interviewers and assist however possible.

The only exception being when asked how his time in the Depths was and how he felt about his return. Unfortunately we cannot reproduce his statements, our editors feel the statements go too far and Velli representatives added their own clarification that Clyde's sentiments towards those named individuals does not represent Velli policy.

Seb paused as they stepped into the Coordinator dorm. He'd anticipated Gwen and Cara being there, they'd already been gone for some time before when Fay and Briana had herded Sandra off to their quarters for the night. He hadn't kept close attention to when exactly the rest of the Fae had left, but it had been long enough that their table had been tidied in preparation for breakfast.

Lord Bern is talking with them at our doorway, he sent.

So he isn't in our room.

No, none of them are. I think they have the door closed and Cara is leaning on it.

So we can't get into our rooms without interacting with them?

Seb shook his head, letting the band carry his sense of impossibility as he headed for the stairs. They'd have to at least greet Gwen's father once more. He hoped the current discussion was civil, the sense he'd gotten from the fae table at dinner was one of tension and little else.

He knew the moment Cara noticed their approach, she went from leaning against the door, as far from Lord Bern as she could get, to taking a half step towards the stairs before relaxing as their eyes met.

"Seb," she said as she saw them. "Anya."

"Cara, Gwen. Lord Bern," Anya said as they all faced one another.

"Lady Anya, Lord Seb," Lord Bern said. "My daughter has been filling me in on the details of your interactions with my children."

"Has she now?" Anya said, her tone calm despite the uncertainty Seb could feel passing between them.

"I merely explained how you were unaware of your heritage prior to your arrival. How we've trained together beyond the required practice on the dueling grounds. How your duels with Bern and Keldren have unfolded, regardless of what he would want our parents to believe."

"She also believes she owes you a debt for the safety you have provided her and her Lifesworn. That Cara's continued ability to perform her duties is almost entirely due to your taking on responsibility, and Hosting them."

"Father! I said I was thankful-"

"Gwen, you're not usually one to exaggerate or mislead. I know how much you care for Cara, how deeply it would have affected you to know her well-being was at risk."

Seb thought he caught a glimpse of Gwen blushing, or of Cara's hair hinting at rose-gold, before Lord Bern had closed the distance with them and was looking Anya in the eyes.

"I suspect my daughter's opinion of you will prove true. Even should it not I owe you my thanks for keeping her well. My partner and I do not always agree, including on whether children should be held accountable for the actions of their ancestors that they know nothing about."

"I bid you all goodnight. Gwen, I trust you will associate with your family during our visit, even if you must excuse yourself at times rather than escalate with your brother."

"As you wish, Father," Gwen said with a nod of her head.

Once Lord Bern had made his way far enough up the stairs to be out of sight, even if not out of his awareness, Seb turned to the others and gestured to their rooms.

"Shall we?"

~ * ~

Anya gave Seb just enough time to shut the door, to ensure their privacy before turning on Gwen.

"What did he mean about my ancestors?"

"I don't know," Gwen said. "I told you I'd never heard of any Royals with Emerald eyes."

"You assume it has to do with my eyes then?"

"Anya," Cara said. "Royals have gem-toned eyes. The colours follow family lines. Even when two lines associate with one another, as in Gwen's case, the tones remain distinct."

Anya took a deep breath. Dinner had been all she needed at the time, a chance to reconnect with family, to pretend to forget about the insinuations and opinions Bern had had this whole time.

"So her green eyes are a line that doesn't exist?" Seb asked.

"That no longer exists," Gwen said. "New lines don't simply appear, at least not that I know of."

"So your parents know something about an Emerald line. Lord Percivus knows something."

"He likely knows much more than they do," Gwen admitted. "Our family is not the most relevant within the various branches. The longer we remain as successful Elites the more…"

"The more access Lady Phila will have, the more significance others will give her opinions."

Anya snorted and shook her head.

"Your success reflects on her, so you must succeed at all costs? Or at least one of you must?"

She saw Gwen wince at the words, at the bitter tone she'd injected into them. She'd been told more than once growing up that her behaviour, that her appearance, reflected badly on the Sovereign. That she was somehow responsible for issues that she had nothing to do with. Her mother had never said anything of the sort, nor had Tomas, but that had not prevented whispers.

"…yes," Gwen said after a moment. "She is not satisfied with her current station."

"She'd feel she still deserved more if she were named Heir," Cara muttered.

"Cara!"

"Yes Mistress?"

"Don't you Yes Mistress me. If she heard you say that-"

"She never will, she'd have to ask me my opinion of her and force me to say things she does not wish to hear."

"Be careful. I don't know what I'd do if you faced those consequences."

Are they..? she sent.

No idea. If you feel like asking and dealing with their reaction we might find out.

Anya didn't answer. They'd gotten involved in Fay's relationship, or at least in helping Fay realize it was a relationship, and it had worked out. They hadn't known Briana nearly as well at the time. She didn't see Gwen or Cara as her sister, but she did care about them. They clearly cared about one another, for all that Cara was supposed to listen to Gwen and do as she was told there were times Anya was sure Gwen was the one following instructions.

"You'd put yourself at risk," Seb said. "You'd do everything you could to keep her safe."

Seb!

"No," Cara said. "She wouldn't. She couldn't."

"I would," Gwen said.

"But then I'd have to protect you, while you protect me, while I…"

"Then you'd better not take any risks, because I can't release you from the Geas if you do something stupid first."

"Speaking of the geas," Anya said. "Do your parents not have Lifesworn?"

"Technically," Gwen said. "The school is not seen as a threat. None of the students are a threat even if they wanted to be. The faculty might be, Mistress Sarna probably would be if it came to it, but with Lord Percivus here the risk would be seen as minimal."

"And none of them are active Divers," Cara added. "They aren't non-Combat, but none of them has trained seriously since before the twins were born."

"Then how do they protect their charges?" Seb asked.

"They don't," Cara said. "Not really. Their geas is relaxed. Few retain the geas past level 25, and those that do usually make it as lax as possible."

"Why then?"

"Strong enough to handle most typical threats. If someone goes after them at that point a single guard likely won't change much."

"I'm not waiting that long," Gwen said sharply. "I told you I'd do it as soon as I can and I mean it."

Cara turned to face Gwen, stepping closer until they were almost nose to nose.

"They'll find reasons to prevent you," she said.

"They won't be able to stop me, I won't let them."

Anya cleared her throat and managed not to laugh as the two jumped and turned to face her.

"Seb and I are going to head to bed, unless you have something you want to talk about that involves us."

She saw Gwen glance at Cara then look at Seb before taking a deep breath.

"How did you lie about Seb knowing where I was for every second when Cara was recovering?"

I told you, she sent.

"They lied? You weren't safe? You. I. But."

I trust them, Seb answered.

"I didn't lie," Anya said quickly. "Seb knew exactly where you were the whole time."

"He couldn't see the bed," Gwen said. "He wouldn't have been able to see me when I was checking on her."

"There's no way in or out of the room," Cara said. "Nowhere you could have gone except next to me, so he knew."

"No," Seb said. "I didn't need her to be in eyesight to see where she was."

Just tell them.

"Explain," Gwen said.

"I can see Energy Patterns the same way some Seers can. And I don't forget them, so once I've seen someone's pattern I know it when I see it again."

"That still doesn't explain it."

"One of my Gifts lets me interact with Energy at a distance. Which lets me see the patterns at a distance."

"Interact?" Cara asked.

"I see the patterns, I can do the few Seer-adjacent things I am capable within the area. I can manipulate Energy, but only faintly unless I shrink the area to only slightly more than twice my arm's reach."

"That's how you got past my shield with that one shard," Cara said. "The angle was impossible."

"Just barely impossible," Seb said. "I was sure you hadn't noticed. It was only a hand's width."

"It was impossible and I knew it. I just didn't understand how or why. It shouldn't have mattered, I should have blocked it."

Anya started to relax. It was Seb's Gift, his secret, not hers, but it still felt dangerous to share, to let others know. She'd been taught you didn't discuss Awakening Gifts, not that Holding Lys knew all that much about them. Even Fay had made it sound as though the details of your Gifts and your Path were something private, something that wasn't typically shared.

"I'm going to figure out how to block you even when you cheat," Cara said.

"I'm sure you will, but not tonight," Seb said before stepping towards the bedroom.

Anya slipped under his arm, glancing back at the pair to say goodnight before pushing the door shut behind her.
