"Why was there a hobgoblin there?" he asked himself with a slight whisper. He didn't know how far his throw went, but did that mean that there was some kind of goblin village in that direction? The fact that it wasn't some kind of elite was the most worrying part about it.

Hobgoblins only appeared in large groups of goblins, and would act as their leader. In large group of monsters, the leader was usually an elite. So this meant that the hobgoblin was either subservient to a stronger, even smarter monster, or there were so many goblins there that there were multiple hobgoblins.

Goblins were cowards, but they were also extremely prideful. They could be forced into submission by other species relatively easily as long as there was a threat to their life, but they would never be able to admit that they were worse than one of their own. So for one of them to work under another one of their own kind would be an extremely bad sign.

And frankly, considering the track record of the monsters in this town, Ashton wouldn't be surprised if there was some ridiculous monster trying to form another large colony of some sort.

But nearby? Sure, that was a strengthened throw that would frankly put the 'throwing' skill of most people at the moment to shame, but that didn't mean that it went for miles and miles.

It was annoyingly close, and Ashton couldn't even check the community to see if there was any info about it already. Though... at least right now, he could ask Millie to read things out for him. Plus, with her, he would have to return to camp a lot more frequently anyway. He couldn't take her with him, at least not right now, not without the right safeguards or while there were no ways to protect her properly yet.

Ashton had effectively zero defensive summons, though he should get his hands on one or two at this point in time.

But for now, there were a few other things that he had to try out. First, he had to figure out a few things, and as it had been a while, decided to check out his entire status.

The stages of cores themselves were fairly clearly defined, and there was an immediate change in quality that practically ensured that each stage was easily distinguished from one another. In general, there were around ten major stages, and then within each stage, there was a differentiation between 'Low', 'Mid', and 'High' grades within each stage.

And that wasn't just a rough assignment of proficiency within each stage, but there was actually a rather distinct sensation when you moved from one stage to another. It was like a minor version of moving from stage to stage.

For now, Ashton wouldn't be able to use most techniques that he had learned or developed in Lumia, though the most basic and fundamental could be used right away. That 'throw' for example was nothing special, since Ashton just forced out some energy. The core-shattering one that he used to kill the Draconid would still be a bit hard to use at its full potential, but most of that technique is about shifting the energy's frequency at the moment of impact, which was Ashton's forte anyway.

The main techniques that Ashton could use for now would follow this pattern. Generally manipulating energy for basic strengthening of actions and shifting the pattern or frequency of his energy to cause minor effects.

For one, there was a particular technique that he had mimicked through the sylph before. Slowly, Ashton moved his internal energy into his throat and shifted the frequency, beginning to speak.

"Test, test," he said. Rather than the mostly smooth, relatively average-pitched voice that he had until now, his voice was more rough and deep like Sam's. "More tests, another one..."

Ashton kept speaking and adjusting his voice until he sounded exactly like Sam. "Heh... that's going to be fun," he laughed to himself, once again adjusting his voice in the opposite direction. Without having to change anything else in the slightest, Ashton now sounded like Clementine. After continuing to practice a bit to get used to it, he released the voice change technique and coughed slightly. It was a little straining, not massively so, but enough that if he wasn't careful he would go hoarse pretty fast.

Next, he made his internal energy flow into his limbs. His legs, specifically. There were a lot of body-strengthening techniques, though many of them were far too basic. People would just stuff their muscles full of internal energy without a second thought, and while it worked, it was deeply inefficient. And, especially at this early stage, Ashton couldn't do 'inefficient'.

Instead, he made sure to intertwine his energy with particular muscles, shifting the energy's pattern to specifically help with the muscle extension.

Annoyingly enough, due to the Summoner's curiosity, not to mention all the body-building infodumps that Sam would get into, Ashton managed to learn a lot about each and every muscle in his body and could apply that knowledge well.

He knew what each muscle did and how they were used and what their purpose was, plus he knew the location of basically every muscle strand in his body.

It was a little awkward at first, and it meant that if he wanted to strengthen himself this way, he needed to manipulate his energy precisely at the moment the muscle was used, but the more Ashton's body got used to this energy, the more natural and 'automatic' it would become.

But for now, Ashton still had to put some more effort into it. He squatted down, making sure that by now his energy had reached all the muscles he needed for jumping, stabilisation, and landing, though said energy was inactive in most of those places, and then quickly extended his legs.

Ashton leapt up, strengthened not just by his higher stats but also by the internal energy. This obviously wasn't at the level of how he could jump in the astral plane, but this was more than enough to jump over the tall walls of this school in one fell swoop without even worrying about it.

As Ashton came back down, he made sure to switch his focus on the energy in the muscles that would help him catch his fall safely, mitigating the impact on his bones and joints, not just through the muscles but also by strengthening said bones and joints themselves.

The landing was maybe a little clumsy, but he was safe and barely any sign of physical strain.
