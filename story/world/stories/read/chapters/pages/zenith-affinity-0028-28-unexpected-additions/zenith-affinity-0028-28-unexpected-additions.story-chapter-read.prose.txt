The gates of Osten opened with the dawn, and 40 mounted members of Zenith Star [★] rolled out of the town like a war party searching for prey. The sight had people stopping what they were doing to watch the guild move through the town, a display of shock and awe. It wasn’t that they were the only ones using mounts, and even the guards used the local horse breed, but to find so many incredibly large and legendary creatures in one spot was a sight to behold.

Sky led the charge on Zazed, her blue-and-gold tiger the size of a horse, who gracefully jogged through the town. Despite his size, there was a terrifying silence in each of his steps, the massive paws not making a sound as they impacted the ground. When they left the gates, he sprang forward in a massive leap, drawing a yelp of excitement from Sky.

The race was on, and everyone took off in a stampede of fantastical creatures. Cheers and excited laughter rang out from the Zenith Star members as they felt the wind on their faces and the power of their mounts. After a good quarter of an hour, Sky had fully adjusted to the motion of Zazed, allowing her to move with the steps, making it easier for both rider and mount. She began to look around at her party, all of whom were leading the guild on the trip back to Ostbury.

Kesa rode Suijin, the elemental guardian of water and sentient fox-like being that flowed along the rest of the party like a living body of water. He had no difficulty keeping up with Zazed, and didn’t seem to breathe as he ran. Next to Kesa, forming the flank behind Sky, was a Dark Hero on a massive black warhorse. It was so dark that it seemed to pull in light as it ran, fully attuned to the shadows. The summoning skill unique to Paladins created an attuned warhorse that was size-appropriate for the race that summoned it. Of course, Dark Hero was a dark goliath, who are naturally large, meaning that his warhorse was massive, making the largest of basic horses from Earth look like ponies in comparison.

Behind them, Atticus rode on the mount they got from their first dungeon, Karne, Zaphid Stormhorn. The massive insect had a strange movement style with its six legs that kept the head and saddle almost perfectly level as it moved, presumably to keep the massive C-shaped horn level and ready to strike out. The giant stormhorn beetle appeared to just glide across the road as it moved, easily keeping pace with the others, and it looked ready to take off as the saddle sat ahead of the hidden wings.

While the rest of the party acquired their mounts by either drops, affinity tests, or summons, Zedra rolled for his mount from someone else in the guild, winning the right to bond with Nyx, the Shadow Frost Panther. She fit Zedra’s lithe and graceful vibe easily. Smaller in stature than both Zazed and Suijin, Nyx still had incredible power behind her movements while cloaked in a shadowy aura that left the certainty of a cold death in her wake. She would be a fantastic companion to someone who needed stealth as he scouted for the guild.

The trip back to Ostbury was fairly uneventful, the most excitement coming from the jump scare Zazed left on a group of adventurers escorting a caravan to Osten. Zazed leapt from the crest of a hill, imparting the fear any giant predatory cat would on a sentient being, and only when he rushed past with forty other riders did the group get a chance to move again. The paralyzing fear of recognizing a being higher up in the food chain had that effect on many.

Perhaps the strangest part of the entire procession, however, was the massive azure sea shepherd turtle, Cobalt, who acted as the rear guard of the line of mounts. This wasn’t a land-based creature, with massive fins and a huge platform that could seat 10 people mounted on its massive shell.

The entire creature was akin to a barge that floated along the ground. To move around, the turtle continuously conjured a wave of water, which he used to sort of 'surf' around on. It made for a smooth and comfortable ride, and the platform seemed like it could be customized a bit further for comfort.

If nothing else, it was amazing to see a creature with such a large mass floating down the road on a wave of water. Ren stood up front where the reins connected to the bridle, straddling the seat which looked like it could be on a jet ski. It was comfortable and easy to use.

The group quickly approached Ostbury; the former journey by foot now took just a couple of hours by mount, where it had taken an entire day running at full speed. The gates to the city were open in the midday sun, revealing the city within to the approaching guild. The large group of quick-moving riders seemed to provoke a response from the city guard, something that never would have happened in game.

What else could be expected from the guards upon seeing a group of mounted people approaching the gate? All of whom didn’t slow down until they reached the entry line. By that point, bells began to ring, and guards began to file out as the gates closed. The guild looked around in confusion.

Sky asked, “You don’t think we caused this, do you?”

“I hope not…maybe we just approached a bit too fast?” Dark replied from his huge dark horse.

“But it’s not like there aren’t higher-level adventurers that show up on epic mounts, right?”

“I think most will use some form of teleportation or flying mount for the tier 4 adventurers. As far as those who are essentially level 50 and less…I have no idea,” Dark replied.

“So, you think because of the low levels around this area, they don’t get many people, or I guess guilds that are fully mounted?” Sky asked.

“Must be.”

“I have definitely seen people riding on some pretty fantastical mounts, though none were players, obviously,” Kesa interjected. "Plus, there are caravans where adventurers bring mounts. I think it's just how fast we approached."

The Captain of the guards approached the group, though the complete lack of hostility, compliance with the entrance line, and clear confusion made for a strange interaction. The Captain asked for their Adventuring Association Identification Cards, and they called off the alarm with a warning.

They moved through the city as a line of mounted adventurers, all representing Zenith Star [★], causing shouts of surprise and interest as Cobalt acted as a street cleaner. The pink stones shone through the dirt and grime since the last rainstorm, and looked almost gleaming as Cobalt inadvertently cleaned them. It took another 15 minutes to cross the city to the Adventuring Association's campus. Guild chat was easy to access while riding, and the plan was to have everyone meet up in one of the larger gathering areas for a guild meeting.

The plan was to meet the other parties and players that had applied to join the guild, all while outlining the plan to move to the Crystal City over the next few weeks. Aralen had wrangled everyone staying in Ostbury together as the rest of the guild rolled in on mounts.

“Whoa! Look at that!” A random player said.

Zazed growled and chuffed at the group of players who were waiting to join the guild, as Sky hopped down. “Hello, everyone!”

Aralen stepped forward, smiling as she inspected the various mounts. “Well, you weren’t joking about a giant tiger, were you, Sky?”

“Did you doubt it?” Sky laughed, pulling the guild mom into a hug.

“I did no such thing!”

Sky wasn’t the only one to pull the woman into a hug, and Dark even went to her son, who everyone called ‘Smash,’ exchanging some knuckles. He was still extremely shy, but it looked like he had been leveling up through exploration, now representing level 12 in the guild roster screen.

Exploration and crafting were alternate means of leveling, though the efficiency was lacking. It took an extraordinary amount of time to level via quests or dungeons, and one had to visit landmarks and interesting or historical sites to gain experience.

Everyone in the courtyard was either a prospective guild member in a party or a solo player looking to find a place to call home. There were even a couple in the second tier, with one party that stood out like a sore thumb.

“So, introduce us to everyone here,” Sky said after mounts had been de-summoned.

Aralen gladly did so, and Sky and Zedra shook everyone’s hands and invited the smorgasbord of races and classes to the guild. Everyone seemed happy to join and weren’t confrontational or anything that raised a red flag. The entire group of players was below level 25

That left the final group, with someone that everyone recognized from the tournament. Sky, having won the tournament, spent some time in the awards ceremony with the seeming leader of the party and went to speak with them.

“So, that leaves you guys. I see your guild tag is now a party tag. What was it before? [Advent]?”

Arnas Delmont, the winner of the Tier 3 Tournament, stepped forward. “It was, Lady Sky.”

“Just Sky is fine.”

He nodded. Sky inspected the man, casually standing in light blue voluminous robes with a beautiful staff strapped to his back. He had something akin to a turban, though it was a small headwrap that looked different from anything Sky had seen on Earth. More fantastical, if that was possible.

He had dark brown skin and a huge black beard with icy blue eyes filled with wisdom and power. “You can call me Arnas. Would you allow me to introduce my party?”

“Of course.” Sky smiled at the overt politeness.

“This is my beautiful wife, Allyra Delmont, a sorceress.”

Sky shook the beautiful elf’s hand. She got the impression that this was one of the elves that lived in the desert on one of the southern continents, with her tanned skin and darker hair color. She was beautiful, but her eyes began to reflect her attunement, as they sparked with a fiery orange color.

“Wonderful to meet you, Sky.”

“You too! You weren’t the one he faced in the finals, were you?”

“Oh no, she was a witch from our former guild. I am not a battle junkie like my husband here.”

“Nothing wrong with that,” Arnas defended.

Sky and many others in the guild agreed.

“Hector and Serena Delasalle, or the twins as we like to call them, Shield Guardian and Cleric respectively.” Arnas gestured.

“Good to meet you both.”

“Last but certainly not least, we have Shangxi, our Shaman.”

“This one is Shangxi. Honored to meet you, Guild Master.”

The kainen had a curious accent that came out buttery smooth. It was the type of accent that no one could accurately describe, but would be recognizable as something vaguely mixed between Middle Eastern and Spanish, but in English. Most natural-born kainens(NPC's) had an accent similar to that, but his was exceptionally clear and deep. He had dark black fur with beautiful sand-colored stripes that went up to either side of his face.

Sky smiled wider. “An honor to meet you as well, Shangxi.”

Her smile fell as she brought her attention back to Arnas. “Alright then, the elephant in the room.”

His smile also fell, and he nodded.

“You were part of the Adventuring Association's own guild, with the tag [Advent], with your whole party, I assume. The last time we spoke, I sold some information to you, which is now common knowledge. Yes?”

Arnas nodded again. “I indeed passed the information to the guild.”

“Right, so they know how to farm Zero Aff metal now, and you…don’t regret giving me the gold?”

He laughed, “I do not.”

“Okay, before I ask the main question, why are you and the rest of your party still capped at level 74? With the information I gave you, and the casual handing out of gold…why haven’t you crossed into tier 4?”

“I think part of that question could be answered with the main one you want to ask, but for now, we didn’t cross because we wanted to join you on the journey that your guild is going on. The Crystal City, am I correct?”

“That’s right,” Sky remembered speaking about it when she pitched the guild invite to Selena Blackmore. “So you left [Advent] and formed your party? [Venture]?”

“No, we have been a party together since tier 1. I don’t know how much you know about the Association, but [Advent] is the largest guild in the world, and it essentially works for the Association. When we were young, all of us wanted to be a part of it, choosing the name Venture as a nod to that.”

“And now…you left the guild?”

“We did. It turns out that politics are quite frustrating for all of us, and the release of information about sponsorship not being a necessity to advance anymore- well, it didn’t make people too happy. Our own sponsor, a noble within Ostbury…dropped us immediately, and is currently attempting to sue us for breach of contract.”

Sky’s eyebrows rose, and she looked over to Zedra. He also looked surprised, but spoke up, “Was that part of your contract?”

Arnas shrugged, “It is hard to say. There was a clause about helping others to avoid sponsorship with the potential of breaching our own, and when we released this information…rather publicly-”

He was cut off by his wife, “He announced it loud enough for the entire Ostbury Branch of the Association to hear, only after sending notes out to a dozen other branches.”

“As I said, rather publicly. We either had to leave the guild, and preferably the continent, or face repercussions.”

“And I am sure that joining Zenith Star doesn’t come with any sort of strings…like funneling the information you will discover back to the Association,” Sky noted.

Anras nodded, looking to craft his next words into something honest and delicate at the same time. Shangxi replied before he could find the words, “There are many friends we have made along the way; you must understand, Sky. Making it to level 74 as we have has taken nearly a decade of adventuring and waiting at thresholds as our sponsors get what they can from us. But we wouldn’t have made it without the friends we have made along the way.”

“What does this have to do with telling the Association things that we might want to hold onto?”

It was the stoic Hector's turn to speak up, “We wouldn’t be telling the Association; we would be giving information that could help other adventurers. And if it is important to the sanctity of Zenith Star, we will not be telling anyone anything. Does that sound reasonable?”sa“The problem is that there are fundamental things to joining a player guild that we don’t even know the implications of releasing to the wider world could be.”

“Haven’t you already accepted non-players into your guild? That was part of the reason we wish to join you,” Allyra questioned.

“True, we did, but they had a serious hatred for sponsorship from the start,” Sky nodded over to [Pink Lotus].

“It’s true. Standing on our own, grasping our future and rising through the tiers would never have been possible without Sky and Zaria’s parties. We would have still been running shitty quests over the years until we could save enough to buy the ascension discs. The moment we joined Zenith Star, and I mean before the guild actually existed, they ensured that we ascended to tier 2 and even ensured that we were fully equipped,” Alisha said sincerely.

“Fully equipped?” Arnas asked.

Alisha looked to Sky, receiving a nod. “We didn’t even understand at first. We had no idea how much the players know about this world, and it’s in ways that none of us could even comprehend. They just…created items for all of us, doing what they call ‘min-maxing,’ where they maximize the benefits for each of our classes and minimize the weaknesses and wasted stats to achieve perfect efficiency. To be ‘fully equipped’ is to have gear in every slot with statistics that maximize the benefits to your class and race. Did you know that you, as a wizard, have certain statistics that enchanted gear could help all of your casting abilities?”

Anras looked curious. “It is important to have gear, but finding or creating enchanted items for every…slot is an obscenely expensive endeavor.”

Alisha laughed, but it was Helga who spoke up, “True enough, but you should have seen it; they came together like a fucking swarm of bees and kicked out a set of gear for every damn one of us. Not only that, but Sky here even helped Sera here learn to enchant, which she wanted to do for a long time. They might not have every ounce of street smarts, but they know things about adventuring that none of us, including you tier 3 folks, could figure out.”

“I see.”

“This conversation seems to have gotten away from us. It went from us needing to find reasons to accept [Venture] to the guild, to you guys giving them reasons why they should join us. We still need a reason to accept them when they have admitted to being willing to give information to other people who we don’t know,” Zedra said.

“But you have already given plenty of information away for free, have you not? Turning people into ‘players’, the implications of these new dungeons and the destruction and benefits they could bring?” Anras questioned rhetorically.

“Yes, we gave that information away, but events are coming that I know for a fact haven’t been spoken on, not to mention how much farming we will need to do to get everyone fully geared at each step, you know?” Sky said.

“Okay, then let’s sign something or do something. We want more than to just be some informant or something, regardless of the people we can help. I will tell you this honestly, and I want you to understand this is something that all of us in [Venture] fully agree with. We are not just looking for a place that we can use to get ahead, or gather information, or anything like that; we are looking for a place to call home. A guild that we can become a part of where a stupid choice to accept a sponsorship from a local won’t have an impact on our future. We want to explore, to become stronger, and see what you players are actually capable of.”

People started talking, quietly at first, but Sky stared at Arnas, then she looked to Allyra. His wife nodded, followed by the siblings Hector and Serena.

“Arnas is correct, and this one agrees. We wish to learn from each other. I happen to have many connections in the city of Itzatl, and I have been in the jungles before. That is the path you must take to get to the Crystal City, and this one would find great honor in providing knowledge of the path to come,” Shangxi said.

Sky smiled, looking to the leaders of each party in the guild and even to Aralen, who smiled with a nod. “Great, welcome to Zenith Star Guild [★], players!”
