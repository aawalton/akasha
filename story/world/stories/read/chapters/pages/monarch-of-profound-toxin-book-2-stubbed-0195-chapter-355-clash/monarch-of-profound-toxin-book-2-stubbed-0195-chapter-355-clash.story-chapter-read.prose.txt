Like a meteor, he was sent back to the ground where he crashed through the earth, still completely unable to see anything.

Before he had even come to a stop within the crust of the planet, something powerful pierced him painfully through the stomach, spreading sickening warmth from the wound site and throughout his abdomen.

Running on pure reflex, Eik clasped the object in both hands, fingers tightening hard enough to cause vibrations in the air. But he still couldn’t shatter it. Even just touching whatever it was that was firmly stuck in his stomach burned terribly.

When the nauseating warmth began to spread to his chest and groin, he began to feel sick. Shouldn’t True Toxic Harmony have prevented this kind of sensation? Even though he still could not see anything whatsoever—not even the bright glow of his own skin—he could still tell with absolute certainty that his personal buffs continued to very much be active.

With a howl of exertion, he finally ripped out the object and instantly exploded up through the hole his body had made, launched by the power of Accelerant, dust and soil still falling into its depths as he rocketed upward. Something plunged down to strike him on the way up, but despite the loss of his vision, Eik managed to sense the aura of the presence and conjure two small blocks of crystal on his hip and shoulder to spin him out of the trajectory of a devastating attack.

This fight was already a lost cause if he couldn’t get his vision back. Sure, as an X-ranker, his other senses were more than enough to beat nearly any opponent with playful ease, but against the Lord of the Moon… Well, the horrific injury that had already been inflicted upon him was enough to make him worried.

But just as he was about to fire off a Death Star in desperation, the darkness began to melt away in front of his eyes. It hadn’t been long since his vision was lost, but the sun still stung his eyes.

Outside whatever veil of pitch had been cast over the world, the day still carried on.

A glance down told him the stab wound was probably even worse than he had initially feared. Not unlike the symptoms of his own ability, a spreading discoloring was slowly claiming his body. Even his clothes and the pieces of his Armor of the Scourge were being affected, the glittering brightness of Profound Toxin somehow suppressed by the darkness.

With a groan, he planted a knee in the dirt where he had landed, a glowing, blue substance slowly seeping from the wound that seemed to refuse to close. Was he… bleeding? Bleeding Profound Toxin?

How the hell was that supposed to make any sense?

He was already circulating Panacean Quintessence, but the wound appeared to be growing larger nonetheless. Wow, that was… concerning, to say the least.

Messages made themselves known on his forearm, etching the familiar, glowing script into his skin.

[Acquired Living Manifestation — Lv. 272]

[Acquired Living Manifestation — Lv. 273]

[Acquired Living Manifestation — Lv. 274]

[Acquired Living Manifestation — Lv. 275]

[Acquired Living Manifestation — Lv. 276]

Not bad. Goo’s daily shenanigans had already been netting him a steady number of levels, but it would seem that the extreme distance he had traveled via the little Living Manifestation he had planted in the spawn’s ear had been significant enough to warrant several levels on top of that.

He wasn’t going to complain about that.

[Acquired Monarch’s Will — Lv. 386]

[Acquired Monarch’s Will — Lv. 387]

[Acquired Profound Toxin — Lv. 492]

Just surviving that initial attack had apparently been enough give levels in both his primary and secondary skill.

He was going to complain about that. That didn’t bode well at all.

Before he could begin to agonize over it, a figure landed in front of him. Eik grimaced.

It would seem that not only the servants, but the master, too, enjoyed humiliating and playing with their victims. The god damned Lord of the Moon was grinning from ear to ear. Razor-sharp teeth as white as new chalk stood in stark contrast to his pitch black skin. A terrible, rumbling giggle rolled up from deep within the Worldbreaker’s throat.

“You think this is funny, do ya?” Eik snarled.

The Lord of the Moon began to approach and Eik rose to his feet, flexing his jaw to keep the pain from showing on his face. It wasn’t supposed to hurt this badly. At X-rank, nothing was.

And Panacean Quintessence wasn’t doing jack shit to make it better either.

Without a word, the Lord lifted all three of his right arms and waved to something behind Eik, and before he could turn to look, a heavy presence suddenly arrived and landed by the Lord’s side. Like all other spawn, this one was pale white.

Hunched over like a crooked, old crone, the thing offered a bow at the hip, black, greasy locks of hip-length hair bobbing and falling over its face as it greeted its master. In Eik’s mind, the spawn of the Lord of the Moon had always been the definition of the words ugly and creepy, but, somehow, this one probably took the cake.

It hadn’t been long since the resurrection of the Lord of the Moon, which should mean that the spawn had also only been alive for a short while, and yet, this freaking thing had the nails of a human garden mole. Cracked and uneven, dirt and grime seemed to have penetrated all the way in to the root.

At a silent command from the Lord, it turned and cast a glance at Eik for the first time, slightly baring black, crooked teeth that would make a veteran dentist weep with despair. Eik was convinced that he could smell its breath despite the more than thirty meters of distance between the two of them.

In contrast to its brethren, this one seemed to have neither grin nor frown to show. Instead, its disgusting face was an expressionless mask. In fact, it looked utterly uninterested in Eik, or anything going on around it for that matter.

But most notable of all was the power it exuded. Coming off its body in roaring waves, Eik felt genuinely affected by an aura for the first time in a long time. Even its brethren that had been watching them since the beginning finally reacted as they were forced to their knees by the pressure.

With no outward tells, Eik abruptly activated a vast Extinction Zone, offering no chance to react in time to completely avoid Profound intoxication. The S-ranked spawn around them quickly succumbed and fell into unconsciousness, but while Extinction Zone was wide-reaching and could affect all targets within it simultaneously, it was probably one of Eik’s least potent toxic delivery methods per affected individual.

The unwashed, corpse-looking bastard of a spawn studied its own body with some curiosity as the toxicity began to build up inside it, while the Lord instantly made his exit. The moment the first motes of Profound Toxin entered the six-armed fiend’s system, he leaped into the sky to get out of range of the ability.

Unwilling to let the enemy he had finally found get away, Eik launched himself upward in pursuit. But the stomach wound, which stubbornly refused to get better in spite of the otherwise phenomenal self-healing properties of his X-ranked body, was affecting him more than he’d like and the spawn intercepted him in mid-air.

Reflexively, a multi-layer of reinforced shells of crystalline toxin sprung up between them as a fist hammered toward Eik’s face. Each shattered plate melted around the beast’s hand and arm to once more solidify and hamper movement. Behind the final shell in the row, Eik waited with an azure katana held point forward so he could sink it in between the spawn’s exposed knuckles.

Whether it was stupid, careless, or just didn’t consider him a threat worth being careful about was difficult to tell, but Eik was grateful regardless, because he honestly hadn’t expected the ugly thing to fall for such a simple trick. Ichor sprayed out between the small gaps between skin and blade—Eik’s cue to pump out more of his deadly payload.

The entire katana disappeared in through the wound as Eik conjured eight chunks of crystal divided across the front of his body and sent himself back down toward the ground. His eyes never left the Lord.

He couldn’t let him get away.

Letting his body be carried on the explosive force of the Accelerant, Eik altered his plunging course and set off after the Lord, the domain of the Extinction Zone following him. Unfortunately, the X-ranked spawn was hot on his tail as well.

Layers of shells continuously blocked and obscured Eik from vision, but with his focus primarily on the Lord of the Moon up ahead, it wasn’t a sustainable plan to simply keep the spawn at bay without fighting back.

But the Lord was getting away—and fast—increasing the distance between them with every second that passed. Having to evade the X-ranked sweaty grandma on top of the injury that just kept getting worse and worse, it felt like a hopeless chase. The bastard had already disappeared from sight long ago, only the few motes of Profound Toxin still left in his system giving Eik an idea of where he was.

“Get back here!” Eik roared and amplified his voice with the full force of his aura, the sound shaking the world like a planet-spanning sub woofer. “I will take you down! I will murder you, you fucking piece of shit! Don’t you get it? This is your chance to kill me before I can become a threat!”

Wordlessly, Eik screamed as frustration ignited white-hot rage in his chest.

After all this time, they had finally found the Lord. So many people had risked their lives to fight his spawn and track him down. So many had died. And now they finally had him. And what does Eik Magnasen, glorious X-ranker and the supposed hero of the Unified Mass do?

Well, of course he lets the cowardly vermin skedaddle out of there scot-free right in front of his nose, leaving him to fight yet another subordinate.

“Coward!” he howled, unsure if the sound would even reach the Worldbreaker so far ahead of him. “You think it will go like this the next time we meet? I will make you beg!” The promised was almost tangible as it permeated the world.

Why?

Why was the Lord of the Moon retreating? It didn’t make any sense. The Lord would undoubtedly know about him by now, and the fact that he was one of the biggest threats to whatever plan he had for the universe.

So where the hell was the logic?

Where was the sense?

Sensing the approach of the Lord’s spawn, Eik’s ire only grew more intense.

Fine, if the Lord himself was out of reach for now, then this filthy pest would have to be the victim of the wrath of the Supreme Divinity of Toxin.
