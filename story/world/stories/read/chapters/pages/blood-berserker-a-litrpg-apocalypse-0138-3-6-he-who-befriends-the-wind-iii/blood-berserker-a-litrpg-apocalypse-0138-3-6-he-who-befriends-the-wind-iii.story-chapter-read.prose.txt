Good, it hasn't given up, Nathan thought. Nathan could appreciate the willpower of the Wyvern to keep going even through what it had just endured from the Blood Berserker. Or maybe it wasn't just willpower. Maybe the Wyvern still held the illusion that it controlled the fight and a bit of hope that it could win in a battle against the Blood Berserker.

Either way, Nathan couldn't care less. This fight was his to lose, and he did not lose.

It was only now, as he stood against the Wyvern, unyielding even through the popping sound of the charged lightning bolts that made up the cage, that he truly understood the essence of a blood berserker.

His heart pounded in his chest, exhilarated. The feeling of the battle was better than any high he had ever consumed in his life. This feeling… intoxicating. This was it. There was no mistaking it. This was what it meant to be a Blood Berserker.

Nathan would not be confined to a cage made out of hesitation, lightning, or even his own fears. He would expand, evolve, and dominate, because that was who he was at his very core, or who he had become.

Somewhere along the line, he had forgotten why he had taken to the apocalypse. He had forgotten it was because he needed to be the best, to take his chance and his place at the very pinnacle of whatever the fuck this apocalypse was. That conviction he’d had at the start had begun to wane as he focused on building his stronghold, but that could no longer be an excuse. Nathan knew that he needed to evolve beyond what he was because the him of today wasn't enough.

He’d needed the help of whatever the Potato Peeler was, and the shame that thoughts of that brought him had been tucked away as he focused on other things. But no longer. Nathan embraced the shame of needing the help of a being that so far outclassed him that he wasn't even on the level of an ant compared to it.

He readily embraced that shame, then squashed it, converting it into willpower and conviction. He could only go up from here, and up he would go.

Nathan snapped out of his thoughts as he sprinted toward the Wyvern. Lightning cage or not, this was his moment. He wasn't the one caged, and while the Wyvern didn't know it, it wasn't the predator in this cage. He was. And Nathan definitely planned to make it pay for trying to keep a king locked in a cage.

Nathan was ruthless as he was relentless, judging by the way he pounced on the beast.

His aura had just been released, and yet he put his full weight upon it. He did not know how to control it. He did not know how to enforce it, nor reinforce it, or make it better, but he trusted it to do its part in this fight.

Oblivion went up in a dangerous arc as it slashed toward the head of the Wyvern, forcing the creature to bare its sharp rows of teeth in Nathan's direction as it sought to clamp down on the black blade. But that was nothing more than a feint, as Nathan quickly pivoted to his true target, the Wyvern's injured wing. With the grace and the ruthlessness of a skilled predator in the jungle, Nathan slashed at the wing, severing part of the Wyvern's wing with that single strike. And while Oblivion hadn't gone quite all the way through, it had done serious damage, and more likely than not, rendered that wing useless, effectively grounding the Wyvern.

"All right, now we fight on the ground, my terms, non-negotiable," Nathan said to the beast.

The Wyvern simply hissed. No doubt it couldn't comprehend the words of the creature in front of it, but all it knew was that that small, insignificant creature had done significant damage to it. And the creature's blood boiled, but not as hot as the berserker's, who had already moved. Not content with almost severing its wing, Nathan slashed again, blood spraying all around the dirt, some even sizzling as it made contact with the lightning cage. But Nathan did not relent until Oblivion was all the way through.

The black blade was unmarred, save for the red blood coating it to its hilt, making it look, in some ways, like King's Wrath. But it was impossible to mistake the black blade for anything other than the creation of hell that it was, judging by the way the Wyvern withered from the cut. No doubt the infernal blade's special effects were taking a toll on the Wyvern, giving it double the pain. But Nathan was starting to grow indifferent to its hisses, and besides, he knew not to let his guard down against a beast, even one as badly injured as the Wyvern was now.

Still, he kept his body poised and his eyes alert. With adrenaline pumping into his veins, his body was running at over 200%, with his eyesight so sharp it felt like he could see the wind itself, which was why he was alert to the Wyvern's tail, currently hurtling toward him. The berserker was unfazed by the attack because he knew it was reactionary rather than planned. The Wyvern had so quickly gone from a clever predator to a cornered animal.

And the one thing about cornered animals was that they were unpredictable and sloppy, their need for self-preservation overriding every hunter's instinct they had, which made it easy for Nathan to counter.

He stabbed Oblivion into the dirt. Once more, Nathan was trusting in his own abilities, and so he waited, timing it for the perfect moment, he grabbed the Wyvern by the tail, narrowly evading its barbed end, which still managed to graze him, the pain noticeable but insignificant in the grand scheme of things.

As Nathan held on tightly to the creature's tail, he pulled every muscle in his body taut as he dragged the creature, sending it careening into the nearest wall of lightning. The creature was relatively unharmed, judging by the way its body slammed into the wall of lightning but did not spasm, which made sense to Nathan, as it was now clear that the creature could not be harmed by its own element.

Still, the creature definitely felt something, judging by the way it struggled to right itself. It gnashed its teeth, its two hind legs bending as it tried to launch itself toward the berserker. But Nathan was relaxed, even though he was a bit hyped.  He ripped Oblivion free of the dirt, sending debris flying just as the creature launched itself at him. With a one-handed grip on the black blade's hilt, Nathan was ready for its attack.

But at the last second, the Wyvern pivoted, which caused Nathan to raise an eyebrow even as his legs already moved, pumped up to explode in the direction the creature had chosen. But that turned out to be a mistake, as the smell of ozone reached Nathan's nose, the wind tugging at his soul, but Nathan had already begun the motion of moving. He couldn't turn back. The Wyvern had caught him in its ploy, its feint effective, as lightning fell from the sky, easily penetrating the ceiling of the lightning cage as it struck the Blood Berserker head-on.

Lightning arced throughout his body, making his muscles twitch and spasm, ending his momentum, as the berserker struggled to regain control of his muscles, gritting his teeth as the lightning flowed through him and into the dirt. It had happened in just a fraction of a breath, but the damage was undeniable, as all the blood on Nathan's body instantly dried up, his skin smoking. But that was the extent of the lightning's effectiveness on the Blood Berserker.
