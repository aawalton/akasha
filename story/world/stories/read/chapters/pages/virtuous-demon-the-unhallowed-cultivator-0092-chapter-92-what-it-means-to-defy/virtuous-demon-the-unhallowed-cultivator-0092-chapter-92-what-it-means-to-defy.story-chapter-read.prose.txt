When the sounds in his brain went off, Tez only had a strange, lingering thought in his mind. A feeling not from excitement, nor from relief. It was…

A twisted fear, like he was being strung like a puppet.

Unlocking a perfect skill like this when I should’ve lost…

This is… Really convenient.

Too convenient.

“That kid is not bad. He is surely a good talent for our sect. Old Wan, isn’t it about time to end this little duel?” Elder Spiritweaver said, drinking a cup of tea in hand.

Swordfall observed with dark blue eyes, having not mentioned a word the entire time amongst the elders. Not even the wind picked up his long black hair, drowning out his shoulders like a curtain.

“This was… quite the duel. If it weren’t for us, I’m afraid one of them would have died.” Elder Harmony commented, having long discarded her drink to her spatial storage. It wasn’t something she could enjoy with tea. She stood up, ready to handle the injured. “Shuren’s the clear winner, obviously. I’ll go so-”

“No, it’s not over.”

Swordfall commented for the first time.

Grand Elder Wanshang butted in, observing the collapsed boy. “Indeed. Both are still conscious. We won’t allow them to kill each other like the other sects, but we won’t coddle them with safety until the very end. They need as much experience as they can get, no matter how bloody and ruthless.” He focused on the twitch in Tez’s finger, rousing to wake.

Boy, you tried to use outside influence to involve yourself in our matter. I’ll allow it, just this once, as you sought to protect our disciples. But there is always a price to pay. If you don’t pledge your loyalty to us… Then your worth will diminish sharply.

Originally curled over in pain, Tez moved his bloodied body with care so that his back was firmly against the floor. He stared at the sky, looking at the foreign sun.

Perhaps he could never feel at home.

In this world, he could get strong enough to destroy mountains with a punch. In this world, he could get strong enough to even conquer the sky. In this world, he could even gain longevity, becoming an Immortal cultivator.

Yet, after all these years of being here, he couldn’t even protect a single girl.

He even chuckled out loud for a moment, even when the crowd was waiting for Grand Elder Wanshang to end the duel.

Most likely, he already had enough evidence, so the sect would punish Wei instead of him. With the help of that letter, at least his friends will be free from scorn and ridicule, and even the hazing from others.

He had done enough.

“Still conscious? Good. Tez, you vagrant from afar, perhaps you don’t know the customs of this land. If you dare renege on our agreement, you will be ridiculed no matter where you go.” Shuren’s grating voice said, walking towards the fallen boy.

Tez could only click his tongue, rolling his eyes to the side.

It irritated him to no end. The price for defending Sarya came with Wei’s eldest cousin coming to defend him. Even if he beat Shuren, he wouldn’t be surprised if the Liu Clan still came for him. It was so ridiculous, so cliche, so typical, but Tez knew it would end up like this in one way, ever since he knew what their surnames indicated.

There was only so much the rules of the Seven Clouds Sect could do.

He should’ve avoided them. He should’ve used his prior knowledge to manoeuvre around this world and its gimmicks. All the tropes and cliches he’d known, he should’ve used them to his advantage.

Even if Liu Shuren isn’t a fool, he still has his obligations. I can’t fault him for that.

But in truth, the world didn’t run like that. These people were all real, with their own twisted customs and traditions and beliefs and ideologies and-

“Get up. It’s time for you to publicly reveal your sins. Or will you run away? You still have it in you right?” Shuren stood over Tez’s body, with a gaze like he was staring at trash.

“Your pride as a man.”

Was it because he was the one who injured his cousin? Or was it because he was a no name peasant?

Tez closed his eyes with frustration, making sure not a single tear leaked out.

Not here, not now.

It frustrated him to no end. Must he run away? Should he just let them do as they wish, oppressing and abusing others just because they were stronger than him?

Even if the world was like this, Tez hated it. He wanted to reject it. He’d seen enough with his time as an orphan in this world.

A chubby Outer Disciple began to yell out. “Tez! Just forfeit! Stop getting yourself hurt any more! ” Chen yelled out, breaking the murmurs amongst the crowd.

Xiaobo yelled out in retortion. “Get up, Tez, don’t let that banana bastard let you down! At least for a few more hits, don’t give up!”

Fatty Chen… And whoever the hell that other guy is. Man, whatever, this son of a bitch is really pissing me off.

He used the only least injured part of his body, his left arm, as the anchor to raise himself. His wobbling legs barely held onto itself, as they propped Tez’s body still. Stings crackled throughout his entire body, the most excruciating pain he had ever felt in his life. Parts of his meridians had been blocked or completely run dry of his cycled Qi.

“Tell everyone. That you crippled your fellow disciple, leaving him at an inch of his life. That you ignored the rules of the Seven Clouds Sect that our Sect Leader made to ensure harmony. That you were too ashamed to hold a public duel, instead attacking him outside of the sect.”

Tez could barely open his eyelid completely, but he glared at Shuren.

“Everyone’s listening,” Shuren said, making sure the spectators and elders were paying attention.

I just have to play my role. That’s what it wants. But… Can I accept masquerading around all for the sake of the System? I’m purposely being ignorant of it, but I’ve never had a shred of freedom the moment I gained this second life.

Tez forgot it all.

He accepted the deal to gain a second life.

And there was always a price to pay.

With a maddened gaze, Tez brought both his arms wide apart, even if they were trembling. Even if just holding them up felt like the twisted agony of swords digging in his skin.

“That’s right. You’re absolutely right, Shuren! I only wish I couldn’t end the pathetic life of Liu Wei! He was truly a bastard, worth nothing more than the scum beneath my fee-”

Tez felt a sharp force as Shuren grabbed his collars, with a clenched fist ready to smash his face in.

Tez sneered. “What, I’m following my oath. As per our agreement, I must publicly announce to everyone why I did it. Or do you want to renege on your oath?”

Shuren scowled heavily, letting Tez go and stepping back.

“Everyone in the Evening Clouds knows. It’s an open secret, so why hide it?!” Tez said with crazed vigour, pointing at everyone amongst the spectators.

Those who saw it all. Those who knew it all. Those who pretended to be ignorant, as if they knew nothing at all.

Perhaps he himself was also like one of them.

He looked up, not to the jeering crowds, but straight to the Elders of the esteemed Seven Clouds sect. With utter disappointment.

“You all love it don’t you?” He yelled out winith a hoarse voice. As if the world decided to let his words be heard, both the outer and inner disciples had shut up. Whether it was because of the shock he dared to yell to the Elders of the sect while saying so, or at the surprise that Tez still continued to stand after the beating he took, it didn’t matter.

“Must be so fun watching a man with the backing of a Rank 7 Clan beat down a mere orphan, right?” Tez stumbled. Forced to a corner by a successor of the magnanimous Liu Clan, one of the most powerful Sword Path establishments in the world. Even if Tez refused, he would’ve just been punished for crippling a waste of human scum like that bastard Wei.

He raised his bloody arms even wider, as if in defiance to the world. He yelled louder and louder, even infusing his vocal cords with Qi. “It must be fun right? Letting these guys get away with bullying the weak and oppressing Silvents and Vulfoans just because they look slightly different to you, right?!” When he saw Sarya getting beaten by Wei’s garbage group of worthless human beings. When he saw the girls who claimed innocence and slandered Sarya, snickering in the distance enjoying the show. When he saw Arene come back bruised for defending Sarya’s reputation.

Not even in his previous life had his blood boiled to such an extent.

The dull-eyed receptionist gradually removed his hand from his chin, concentrating on Tez.

“It must be fun, right? Just because you’re strong, or your dad is strong, or you’re just related to someone strong, you get to do as you wish in this world. Hypocritical bastards, even at the Seven Clouds. Am I wrong?!”

No one would dare to insult the Seven Clouds while staring down the elders, not even the most arrogant of disciples would dare commit such an offence. Yet in his madness, Tez refused to yield.

“I get it. I get it all. The strong rule with an iron fist, and the weak obey. Mortals kowtow to cultivators, and cultivators kowtow to Immortals. Even those immortals kowtow to the heavens. It is the order of this world, the law of cultivators.”

Chronotua Blade Nullus (Sealed) lay on the ground. Camouflaged by the Nebulous Treasure Hashin’s Ring (Fractured).

An artifact eternally bound now to Tez’s soul. Even if he wasn’t at the Path Foundation, the System granted him the ability to unequip and equip multiple weapons. It provided him with the ultimate storage space, even if the space was limited right now. One of his biggest advantages as of this moment.

“I reject it all. Why should I watch as my friends get beaten, and just leave it because of the beliefs of this world?!”

Everyone heard him.

The spectators were confused at his words.

The elders glared at Tez.

The dull-eyed receptionist sighed.

The young man with silver hair, sitting amongst the disciples, only had a light smile.

And Shuren changed his expression. For even if he had to defeat Tez, he now understood his conviction. And there was a light in his eyes, as if he had just realised what had truly been going on in the background.

Tez had long forgotten the quest he had.

Right now, he just really wanted to fight back.

To defy the sect, defy the ways of this world.

And most important of all, to defy this System!

That’s right. Even if I’m just an ignorant imbecile, being played around by the System and shitted on by these cultivators, I won’t forget my ideals. The values that my teacher fought for, even at the end of my world!

“I fight so that my friends, no, so that all of us weak people have a chance to make a stand in this world!”

Equip.

A reassuring presence weighed within Tez’s left hand.

“I’ll be the last man standing!” Tez declared to the world!

Both of his hands now held his sword.

He only yelled out one word.

“SHUREN!”
