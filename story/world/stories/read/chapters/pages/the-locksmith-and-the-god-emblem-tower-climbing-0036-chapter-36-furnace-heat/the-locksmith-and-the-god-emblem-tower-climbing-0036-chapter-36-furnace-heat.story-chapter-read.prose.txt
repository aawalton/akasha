As if straight from hell, the heat haze enveloping Cal was only thinner than the flames of Vela's candelabrum. The effect, however, wasn't merely sensory, and as Goldman had just felt, it was real.

Shirtless, the broad man protected his belly, which now resembled a portal to a volcano. A black, coal-like substance melted under the pressure of Cal's body, protected only by mere metal bars. From within the furnace, and even from his skin — which now resembled brick in its patterns and color — a plume of steam rose.

Relatively far from the furnace-man, Madeline was seen bickering with Harry. The two, clearly arguing about something, inadvertently denied help to Goldman, who had come from the air and was preparing to strike Cal. Absorbing him like a sponge of energy, Cal retaliated with a precise blow to his chin, throwing him to the left, but not knocking him out.

"...I don't need you all the time!" Madeline said in a shrill voice, a paintbrush in her hand. "Even more so after—"

At that moment, she saw the clash between Goldman and Cal, and immediately fell silent. She simply glanced at Harry, who then realized the futility of their argument. Not that he could do much; just thinking about opening a door made his heart explode. In any case, he wouldn't abandon them.

Going over to the two, she gripped her paintbrush so tightly she almost broke it. With a shape in mind, she reached Goldman and, before Cal could strike another blow, painted a wall of paint suspended in mid-air. She made it even stronger by adding layers, creating a concrete wall. Then, with Goldman as backup, like an abstract artist, she cut out meaningless shapes in the air. Corrosive as acid, the paint burned Cal's slightly rocky skin, who screamed in agony, fighting against the air.

It was curious how many people thought that a Triangular Emblem had only one application. Certainly, they weren't as versatile and flexible as the other two types, but they could surprise if used correctly. In Madeline's case, the ink flowing from her brush had various applications depending on its shape. If she made quick, unassuming cuts, they gained speed and changed their properties, becoming a fiery cut that tore through everything in its path. If, however, Madeline painted a more filled shape, it gained more robust properties, becoming a shield or a platform. If, by chance, she decided to paint something round and asymmetrical, she could create a kind of viscous, sticky foam, perfect for hindering enemies.

At its core, the Triangular Emblem shape allowed the symbol in question to have different abilities, like Brun's manuscript and Madeline's inks. If either of them lost these objects, however, they would lose their primary source of damage or information.

The only way to know what the artist would choose was to base it on the context of the fight itself, but Cal, having the disadvantage of fighting an unknown Emblem, hadn't realized this. The good, or bad, side, depending on the perspective, was that they also didn't know what Cal could really do.

"What the hell is this...!" He yelled while wiping some kind of goo from his face, the heat quickly melting the material.

As soon as he caught sight of Madeline and realized it was the girl who was delaying him, he lunged at her. Thump! Goldman then struck him with a knee, pushing the man back, who lost his balance and almost fell. "Get the fuck out of my way!" His voice was distorted and amplified due to his Emblem, making him sound like a real monster.

Cal engaged with Goldman in a fist fight, but somehow it felt off. His fists sometimes bent in weird ways as he always missed his attacks. Do I have a concussion? Does this have to do with the painter? A trail of reasons skimmed through his mind clearly as though the heat didn't bother him.

Goldman, avoiding his punches effortlessly, would often counter attack with a right hook followed by a a knee hit on the metal bars in his stomach; an attempt to stop his Emblem somehow. Taking a step back, he then floated and threw a nail right on his shoulder, piercing the man's stout skin.

Giving Madeline room to land more blows, the girl always stayed a few steps behind, and in moments when she seemed too close, she was prepared to create a barrier. She made symbols and shapes in the air like a true artist. The battlefield brimmed with what looked like blood on the grass as she used Vermilion Red and a Fern Green with elegance. Colors clashed as waves in the air as Madeline kept on going, a witness to a dream of her own making.

Traveling on the wind as if it were part of nature, a precise arrow struck the exact spot where Goldman had thrown the nail. Cal cried out with intensity, his skin turning more orange than before. He grabbed the arrow and snapped it in half, leaving a part of it still stuck in his shoulder.

Daniel appeared, also jumping in the air, using a tree as a support. He gave a quick glance at Goldman, who nodded and turned to Cal. The man then pushed in the same direction with even more force, for only a brief moment, orbited said tree. Picking up momentum, he dashed to Cal and kicked his face. Before falling, Goldman slightly pulled Daniel, careful not to make the man injure himself. Goldman then created a polar field on Cal's trousers and pushed himself to the man, delivering a skull-breaking hit. Unfortunately, though, he was still standing.

As if they were bond together, they continued their interchanging attacks. Goldman would pull himself to Cal, punch or kick the man and go back, and as soon as he was done, Daniel would pick even more momentum and do the same, sometimes using his arrows as knifes. Cal acted like a black hole as Goldman and Daniel kept on hitting nonstop, two herculean and intrepid figures who, by all means, should've defeated the man long ago.

Harry then observed the state of the man. The lines in his skin more defined, the heat which now felt like a fever; the man was growing in power. His crooked smile could be seen behind the heat haze as Cal's skin was totally orange.

Goldman and Daniel didn't seem to notice it as they kept their intermittent combat. And as fascinating as it was to observe such cooperativeness in action, Cal was ready to counter-attack.

"No!" Harry called out from behind, still grasping at his heart like an old man. "Stop! That's what he—"

But the warning came too late. Like a fat mantis, Cal grabbed Goldman's leg and smashed him into the ground, interrupting Daniel's next movement. As he was pulling and pushing Daniel constantly, the sudden attack broke his concentration and made Daniel stumble in the air, unsure on what to do next. Luckily, Madeline was attentive and created a small platform on the air for him to stabilize himself.

"Can you feel it!?" Cal bellowed and grinned with half his teeth missing out. Steam rose from his mouth as the fireplace inside his torso crackled with the sound of snapping coal.

Goldman then managed to slip out of the man's grab as he looked, horrified at what he and Daniel had done. The furnace man, now standing nine feet tall, had a ragged and heated breathing. His arms, whose skin was now harder than obsidian, were like two cannons, the pressure in his grip bone-breaking.

That's why the other idiot was whipping the hell out of this guy... Harry thought, unsure if he should participate in the fight. He's converting damage into energy, into coal. We can't keep up a fight with him for long; only a knockout would finish it.

"Daniel!" Goldman yelled to the viking, who was now the nearest. "Don't fight him. We gotta wait until he runs out!"

Thankfully, Goldman, even if too late, had figured out what to do in order to defeat Cal. Daniel simply nodded and tried to flee. The furnace man, however, had other plans for Daniel. Mimicking the speed of a horse, Cal rushed towards the brute as he broke all the barriers Madeline created like a bull.

By a few centimeters, Daniel evaded the man's grasp by propelling himself to a nearby tree. To his surprise, when he looked back, Cal was in the air with the force of a jump, ready to crush Daniel with his strength. Before he could, though, he then suddenly entered an illusory door and appeared on the ground, far from everyone.

Shit... He felt his heart beating faster, but they needed to keep going. Trying to slow him down, Madeline hit him with a mix of acidic and slimy paint, which only seemed to anger Cal even more. His voice, now totally altered by his Emblem, sounded like a train at full speed, each step covering a few meters.

Goldman, seeing as he was the closest, stopped in his tracks, facing Cal like he was a toreador.As Cal gave him an terrifying smile, he lunged to Goldman, who crouched and created a polar area on the ground and one on his trousers. Using the same technique he used to hold the mountain, when Cal fell on top of him, Goldman pushed him to the other side, vaulting the gigantic man.

Rising with a grunt, the impact seemed to have slightly reversed his transformation. He had shrunk a few inches, and his aura of heat seemed to have cooled. Perfect, if we continue like this for the next few minutes... Harry thought as he inadvertently smiled and admired Goldman's courage. Even after seeing him lift a mountain, he was still amazed by his virtues.

How can she live like this? Harry added inwardly as he panted, running frantically. Always using his doors for easy movement, he rarely ran. In this case, however, it was life or death. He looked back and saw Goldman delaying Cal while Madeline, uselessly, threw her sticky ink on the furnace man's feet. As he seemed to shrink every second, Cal bellowed once again and, using his last resort, paused for a second and jumped as high as a gorilla — if gorillas could fly.

He could've tackled Goldman, Daniel, or even the Locksmith, but he was aiming at Madeline, who gave a horrified shriek as she saw him. Harry then created a passage in front of the man and another one nearby. The furnace man then fell and hit the ground in a huge Thud!

I... I'm sorry, Maddy. Harry then collapsed on the ground, his heart beating unnaturally fast as if a woodpecker were hitting it. His vision turned black as the warm darkness invited the Locksmith into a hug.

Madeline dearly held Harry on her arms as she grimaced. Don't fuckin die, you idiot.

Behind her, Daniel and Goldman surrounded a now weakened Cal, his disgusting smile turning into a scowl. One of the man summoned an arrow into his grip as the other cracked his knuckles.

"How much coal for you guys to let me go away?" He said. "Come on, everyone has a price."

And then his vision turned to black.
