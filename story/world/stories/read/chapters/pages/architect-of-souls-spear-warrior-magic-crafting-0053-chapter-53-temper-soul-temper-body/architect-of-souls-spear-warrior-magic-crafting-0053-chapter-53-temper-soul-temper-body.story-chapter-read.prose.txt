Ferris looked over his new gear, preparing to set out for the chain demon’s fort.

There were always new groups of demons around the fort. This made more sense if the light atop the four towers was attracting them as Uncle Winton suggested.

This could be useful later, but for now, Ferris wanted to secure the fort to defend his oath statue. It would also be good to check on the statue and ensure that nothing had changed over the last couple of days.

He collected his new bone spear and crossbow, then strapped the shovel to his back. With all the equipment, plus his new dagger on his belt, Ferris was starting to feel like a fully equipped delver. He was still missing armor, but it felt good to be better prepared.

Jade and Ferris set out of the chain fort, following the drag marks. They had never reverted or changed, so at least that was one question answered. Small changes like this appeared to be permanent. Several groups of demons attacked them along the way, but nothing surprising. Ferris had to re-teach Jade their battle tactics and the game to eat around the chains, but once again the slime took to it all with enthusiasm.

At the fort, they tackled the tedious task of clearing out all demons in sight. It took hours, but felt rewarding.

Ferris stopped feeding the leftover chains from the demons to Jade. The slime still couldn’t avoid eating most of them along with the rest of the corpse, but they resolutely kept practicing. About ‘four out of ten’ left each time, in fact, as Ferris helpfully informed Jade. The slime was quickly able to improve their score from three to four, but they couldn’t seem to go beyond that, even after dozens of demons.

Instead, Ferris began collecting a pile of chains inside the fort. Sometime in the future, he hoped to see if he could use Uncle Winton’s qi rope transfer technique to move them. He left them in a pile underneath the oath statue. Normally, he wouldn’t have bothered, as anything left around would be stolen, but Ferris was hoping the oath statue would protect them. It had kept anything from stealing the lanky demon’s corpse, after all. If it didn’t work, at least he would have learned something new.

Once there were no more demons in sight of the fort, Ferris turned to his true goal for the night: the blazing towers. Each of the four corners of the fort had a bright fire atop it.

Climbing up one of the towers, Ferris once again saw the single large red-hot chunk of rock in a stone brazier. It was a bit strange that it could support a fire bright enough to light up the area for days, but there were many strange things in the soulspace.

The ‘impurity’, as Uncle Winton had called it, was about the size of a burning log. Ferris couldn’t touch it directly while on fire, so he got under it with his shovel and tossed it off the side of the tower. It fell to the ground with a thump, the fire still blazing.

Back on the ground, Ferris began shoveling dirt atop it. Soon, the fire was smothered under a small mound of dirt, and Ferris went on to do the same to the other three. It was odd to see the fort with only the light from his lightstone. It gave the area a bit more of an ominous feeling. Hopefully, there would be fewer demons around now.

After uncovering one of the smoking rocks, Ferris took a closer look. It looked like a large, lumpy coal-black rock, although not as heavy. Strangely, while still very hot, it no longer burned. A log would still be smoldering on the inside, but these rocks were solid.

Once all four were uncovered and had cooled enough to touch, he was able to tie them together with a spare chain into an oversized bundle. It was heavy and awkward to carry, but Ferris was able to lift it up onto his shoulders and slowly lug it back to his Heartfire.

After dropping the heavy load, Ferris groaned. It was much later than he should have been up. With a sigh, he left his soulspace and immediately fell asleep.

The next morning came too soon, but he managed to pull himself out of bed. Ferris almost missed being unable to cultivate while his mental body recovered.

Jade, on the other hand, was happily rolling around. The slime was definitely a morning person, excitedly waiting for Mother Lily’s food.

Breakfast was a rice bowl with sweet yellow fruit on top. Ferris wasn’t sure what kind of fruit it was, but it had a bit of a stringy texture. It wasn’t bad, but not his favorite. Too sweet.

It was halfway through the week already, and Ferris hadn’t started any of the three workdays he needed. He’d wanted to do them earlier than last week, but couldn’t while recovering. He couldn’t today either, as he was scheduled to meet Serena in the afternoon. Hopefully, always working the last three days wouldn’t become a pattern. He’d also intended to work an extra day to cover the cost of Jade’s extra food each week, which hadn’t happened…

Speaking of the extra food, Ferris took Jade down to the basement. The first delivery had arrived, and twenty large bricks of bundled straw were stacked against the wall. He’d paid for forty, but the rest would arrive in a few days. These twenty bricks should be enough to fill Jade—and hopefully the Digest River—for at least three days. Jade happily ate two bundles, and Ferris promised they would have more after lunch and dinner. The slime still remembered the dismantle game, and apparently was able to apply what they learned last night with the chain demons to the straw.

Jade was visibly larger than before, but not as much as last time. Hopefully, splitting the six and a half bundles into three feedings would fully saturate the river with qi, and Ferris could reduce the quantity each day until he found the limit.

After eating, Ferris and Jade set out for the bathhouse. It wasn’t as busy in the morning, but there were still people coming and going as they arrived.

Yesterday, Ferris had confirmed they had a special room set aside for his first order of business that day: purging impurities. Uncle Winton had warned him it would be unpleasant, and to only do it in a designated room. Ferris wasn’t sure what he needed to do yet, but it would be better to experiment in the bathhouse’s room rather than where he slept.

An attendant led him to a small room far away from the baths. It had a tile floor, with a shower built into the center of the ceiling and a drain in the floor. There was a lever for the shower, and a brick of soap was provided. The room didn’t look very pleasant to cultivate in.

Lying on the floor, Ferris held Jade as he meditated. While usually he had to focus on clearing his mind of distracting thoughts, today he had to be careful not to accidentally fall asleep. If it wasn’t for the cold tile, he might have lost that battle.

Instead, he found himself with Jade in his soulspace, next to the Heartfire. The bundle of four black impurities was where he had dropped it last night.

Untying the chain wrapped around the four black rocks, Ferris lifted one up to consider it. It was slightly larger than a watermelon, although not as heavy as a typical rock that large. Examining it in the light of his Heartfire, the surface was an inky black that felt almost sticky to touch, like partially dried paint.

Uncle Winton had told Ferris to figure out how to purge the impurity himself. That likely meant one of two things: that it wasn’t important to do immediately or that Ferris had everything he needed already. Perhaps both were true. If purging the impurity required special tools or knowledge that Ferris didn’t have yet, his uncle would likely have said so. If Ferris got stuck, he couldn’t ask for more help until the next time he visited, so his uncle must have felt that was an acceptable time to wait.

Uncle Winton had said to think on the word ‘purging.’ The impurity was described as a mental representation of toxins and waste in his real body. Removing them from his soul would then be reflected in his real body.

Feeling the sticky rock in his hands, it reminded him of one of the problems he encountered while refining spirit gems. One of them had a section with sticky black gunk stuck to the crystals. He had needed to use the magical broom to scrub it clean. Perhaps he needed to wash it to make it clean? He had heard people use the term ‘body cleansing’ before in relation to impurities.

Taking off his shirt, Ferris ripped off a strip of cloth and tried to wipe off the rock with it. The sticky black rock was hard to scrub, and nothing came off onto the cloth.

It would be helpful to have some water and soap, but the only liquid Ferris had was qi and acid. Qi was limited and valuable, and he was hesitant to try acid—other cultivators didn’t have it, so it shouldn’t be necessary. He might try it later to see what happens. For now, he wanted to look for a more standard solution.

Ferris dipped his cloth into the Qi Pool, soaking a small amount up. Once more, he tried scrubbing the black tar, but with no success. The cloth just stuck to the sticky surface of the rock, and nothing came out.

He’d had similar problems with the gunk on the crystals, but the glowing broom from the spiritual refinement device had seemed to magically remove it. He could try making a scrubbing brush and giving it the intent of cleaning, although the qi would be wasted if it didn’t work. Was there a way he could test using the magical broom in the refinement spiritual device?

The device’s gate wouldn’t let him take the tools out, but maybe he could bring the impurity into the spiritual device? The only problem was, he couldn’t take it out of the Ox Building, and his uncle was insistent he do this in the room at the bath house.

This made Ferris think: he had taken things between his soulspace and a spiritual device before—like the bone spear to defend himself while refining spirit gems. What if he took the rock out of his soulspace… and just left it out there?

Ferris had been trying to ‘cleanse’ the impurity, but the word his uncle had used was ‘purge.’ Instead of trying to scrub it, shouldn’t he be trying to get the rock out of his soulspace completely?

Lifting the rock once more, Ferris imagined leaving the soulspace, but not waking up. His mind drifted in that emptiness next to the warmth of his soul. In this place, he didn’t have a body, but he tried to focus on the thought of letting go of the rock.

Something… separated from his mind in a way he had never felt before. Ferris could now feel a new presence in the vast empty space next to his mind and soul. Ferris couldn’t see things in this place—he had no eyes after all—but he had a kind of strange sense. This new presence had a feeling of filth.

Ferris decided to wake up, and was immediately hit with an awful stench. His body was seeping a black, oily substance. As the putrid odor hit him, he lost his breakfast onto the floor. The tangy-sweet fruit tasted much worse coming back up.

After a few minutes of wrenching, he started to take his clothes off. They were now covered in black, wretched oil. After he dumped them in a corner, he grabbed the soap and pulled the lever for the shower. Water came down from above, and Ferris spent several minutes scrubbing.

Stopping the shower, Ferris sat back down. He didn’t feel clean yet, but at least he could breathe again. He still had three more of the rocks in his soulspace, and he believed he had found a correct method to purge them. He also felt a better understanding of why people referred to this as ‘cleansing.’ All of that gunk had been inside him?

Returning to the calmness of meditation was difficult, especially with the lingering feeling of the oil and the taste of vomit in his mouth. It took him longer than usual. About half an hour later, he once again felt his soul. The presence he had felt after releasing the impurity was gone, and Ferris entered his soulspace.

Looking at the three black rocks, Ferris considered his options. He had been told that purging them would improve his body, but not the specifics. Uncle Winton mentioned that Ferris would need to purge hundreds of these impurities before reaching the second tier.

In the short term, an improved physical body didn’t directly help Ferris. He couldn’t delve until he fixed his leg, and that would take months if he was lucky.

But I want it, he thought. All my life, I’ve watched cultivators casually outclass everyone. Either in training or just doing simple tasks around the bastion. Won’t it be awesome to be that strong myself?

The reason Ferris hesitated was a comment from his uncle: that burning an impurity in his soul was a spiritual technique to attract demons. He wasn’t sure when the next time he would find more, so maybe he should keep them in his soul to use and experiment with.

At the chain demon fort, attracting demons was a problem. His oath statue was still there, and Ferris wanted fewer demons around it.

However… it could be useful to have a way to attract demons in the future. Ferris was already considering using demon corpses as material for building bricks. He would need a lot of them, after all.

At the same time, Ferris was afraid of the idea backfiring on him. When things had gone wrong with the oath statue, the source of the problems was his intentions. He hadn’t wanted the oath statue to take root, and then it was stolen. If he set up something to intentionally attract demons, would he get more than he wanted?

In the end, Ferris decided to purge all of them. Uncle Winton claimed he would need to deal with hundreds, so acquiring more later should be achievable. For now, his mentor had told him to work on purging, so that is what he would do.

With a grimace, he awkwardly picked up the three remaining impurities and left the soulspace. It still felt strange to let go of something while in this meditative emptiness, but it was easier the second time.

Bracing himself, Ferris woke up to another round of filth and cleaning. It was definitely worse the second time, with three times as much gunk, but he was better prepared for it. Thankfully, there was a lot of soap, and he spent a long time scrubbing and rescrubbing.

Feeling more raw than clean, eventually, he had to stop. Ferris was then faced with a conundrum: his clothes were covered in the black oil. He wasn’t sure if they would ever come clean.

In hindsight, he should have disrobed before purging the impurities. He now noticed there were hooks along the wall to hang his clothes on.

Sticking his head out the door, Ferris was able to embarrassingly flag down an attendant to help him. The man was at first confused to see a naked Adept waving at him, but soon turned to barely contained laughter at Ferris’ predicament.

Thankfully, the bathhouse occasionally had someone show up needing a change of clothes, and they had a simple outfit they could sell him. They even helpfully disposed of his old clothes for him.

Leaving the bathhouse, Ferris finally started to feel his embarrassment go down. Thankfully, he didn’t know the attendant that helped him, and the man likely didn’t know anyone Ferris knew. If this had happened back home, the entire clan would’ve known within a day.

At first, he was relieved. He would’ve been the subject of gossip for a week, and his sisters would’ve kept bringing it up for years. Yet, as he was walking back to Lily House, a sense of homesickness overwhelmed him. The relief turned sour in his heart.

He stopped along the side of the path, and just held Jade close for a minute, missing home.
