I pull out my map and assess my situation. Based on some rough maths, I could make it to Itolas within a day. That is still my short term goal, as ideally I need some info of how to actually cross the gigantic mountain that stretches up beyond the clouds. I get the sense that I can'tfw just walk over it.

However, I think it would be best if I spent some time getting powerful. There will be more people in the city, and thus it might be easier for any hunters to find me. So far it seems they can’t field anyone much higher than level sixty, and maybe the rare seventy. Which makes sense, as those more powerful than that are going to need much greater incentive to take up such jobs.

My understanding of this world is quite limited. But from what I have observed I think I can deduce a few things. Mostly that the higher levels are quite rare amongst humans. I would think that people that can hit those levels in the first place are the ones that can break the one star barrier. Naturally, those that push themselves to such heights are seeking to go even farther, and thus leave the realm to pursue further power. Though of course, some stay around to basically act as big bullies. Not all that reach such power are pure of heart, though I would hope most are.

With all of that in mind, I have about two weeks of supplies left. So I figure I can spend that time grinding out some monsters and hopefully learning my first apprentice tier spell. I do have to keep in mind the time limit to reach the capital to attend the banquet. But if I get stronger, then I should be able to travel way faster, right? Especially as my spirit increases. That vastly improves the effective speed of Plane Step. If I can understand a bit more theory, perhaps I could even upgrade the skill, or at least make it even more efficient. Right now my reasonable limit is around a hundred meters… but imagine if I could push that to five hundred meters, or even a kilometer.

It is a lot to do in such a short time, and I am sure that for any normal person, it would be literally impossible. But I am not a normal person, and I think I am strange even by hero standards. Not that I have met any other heroes, but I kinda just get that sense. Might just be arrogance though. Or wait, would it actually be low self esteem?

Blah, all this philosophy stuff sucks. Time to go kill some more monsters.

—

You know what they say about not kicking a hornet’s nest? Well that applies to a colony of level sixty plus dust motes too. Though this was totally not my fault, how did I know that hole was the entrance to their burrow? Heck, how was I supposed to know they had burrows in the first place?

As I run away from a cloud of hundreds of dust motes, I come to realize that they are truly horrifying. They look like meter wide cuddly balls of fuzz, and they can float idly along through the air. But that is how they lure you in. Once you are close enough, you realize they can spew acid. You know, because why wouldn’t the cuddly balls of fuzz be meat-eating predators. Normally they wouldn’t attack things strong enough to fight back like me, but these ones have been corrupted by the malice and warped into monsters that don’t have such inhibitions.

Luckily, I don't have to run very fast as they are quite slow. However, I don’t think they are going to just let me get away, thus I need to kill all of them somehow. On one hand, that is a lot of experience. But on the other hand, they can spew really not fun acid.

The problem is that I have no good area of effect attacks. Spark Drill could work if there weren't so many and I could get the energy required. But with so many of them chasing me, there is no way I would have the time to set that up. Perhaps if Dovey wasn’t recovering and could distract them. But he is out for four whole days like a truly lazy bird.

Ice Blast definitely is an option, but I have learned from previous encounters that the dust motes are quite ice resistant. I think it has something to do with them basically being clouds of dust. There is too much chaos or whatever to freeze them effectively.

Yip! Luma chimes in, and a figurative light appears above my head.

Of course, your Light Cannon! It could work, I think. I say to Luma and she yips back again. The problem is that based on the description, it isn’t very precise, which means we have to be really close to the dust motes.

Oh well, I have done more risky things in the past. So with far too little hesitation, I turn around and start running towards the cloud of dust motes. As I do, I pull Luma out of the phylactery and she precariously holds onto my shoulder. Despite the awkward position, she still manages to stand nice and tall like one would expect from a proud spirit fox.

Once we are within about three hundred meters of the dust motes, Luma tenses up. “YIP!” She yips with enough noise to nearly deafen me, and it even echoes through some of the nearby ravines. But moments later, I feel an immense energy gather within her body, it feels like spirit energy, but like it is far denser than I usually feel. Just what sort of power is Luma harnessing? It can’t be her own energy, as she doesn’t have nearly that much.

It doesn’t matter where the energy comes from though. It doesn’t change the fact that I can feel it gather in front of her snout before she releases it with her Light Cannon ability.

The attack is so bright that it outshines even the sun itself. I watch in awe as it flies across the open space and impacts the cloud of dust motes. The sheer amount of energy obliterates every last health point they have in an instant, and I am deafened as hundreds of dings fill my mind.

Woah, that is a lot of dust motes killed… but damn that is also a lot of experience for Luma. She got three whole levels, and even cleared fifty. This is good, it is like we are all getting stronger. This seems to be some milestone for Luma too. She has been stuck at forty nine for what feels like forever, for some reason. Or well, I guess the reason is that Combat Form ability she unlocked. Maybe great spirits have thresholds like cored beings have specializations? Though quite different in effects, of course.

Theories aside, Luma seems really happy and jumps off my shoulder and does a little dance on the ground. She calms down and then looks up at me, tilting her head, as if asking permission.

I giggle, “Oh come on, just try it out already. I ain’t going to tell you to stop.”

“Yip!” Luma yips cheerfully and I feel a tug on my spirit reserves. Interesting, an ability that requires energy from myself as well? I let her take what she needs, and it ends up being about four hundred points, which is quite a fair bit. But when she starts shining, I know the price is worth it.

Pop.

My jaw drops at just how worth it it was. Honestly, if I didn’t see Luma standing there a moment ago, I would think that the terror standing in front of me is a completely different being. She is about the size of a wolf now, and has long shaggy white fur that looks quite comfortable. I can tell it is also very tough though, maybe even more so than Stelan is, if my intuition is correct.

Of course, with Luma’s new form, she also gets some pretty nasty claws, and some downright terrifying looking fangs that she is currently baring at me in what I think is a smile.

“Woah Luma, it would seem you have gotten an upgrade.” I say once a bit of the shock wears off. In response, Luma launches herself at me, and tackles me onto the ground. “Oi, you are too heavy for this!” I say while my entire body is being crushed. She seems far heavier than she should be, must be all that fur.

“Yipoof!” She barks with almost enough force to make my ears bleed. It would seem her yips have also grown a bit more powerful. She nuzzles her snout against my face and I just kinda accept my new reality as her human cushion. She stays like that for perhaps three minutes.

Pop.

I suck in a deep breath as the weight suddenly disappears. “Yip?” Luma asks from my chest, having seemingly reverted to her ‘super duper adorable form’. There must be a time limit–scaling with how much energy I use for the initial ability, I’d bet.

With my arms now free, I reach up to scritch right behind her ears. “Aww, who's such a good fox? Guess this means you can finally fight alongside me and Dovey, huh?”

“Yip yip!” Luma says as she excitedly dances on my chest. She is also bigger in her adorable form too. Though I think that is just from the normal growth she has been experiencing. Now she is what I'd expect a several month old fox to be–around one and a half kilograms, give or take some. Not too large, but a bit too big to sleep in my hood, not that she has done that in a while, thanks to me having to travel in my armor.

This is all happening way too fast. But somehow I find myself not bothered by it as much as I should. Guess this world has just gotten me used to ridiculous shit like my super cute spirit fox transforming into a terrifying battle fox. Perhaps a part of getting stronger is just getting used to more and more ridiculous shit. I really hope that is not the case, though, as I don’t know how much more absurdity I can take.

There is only one way to find out though, and that is to continue my grinding. This time though, I have Luma to help out. In other words, it is time to put those new claws of hers to the test.
