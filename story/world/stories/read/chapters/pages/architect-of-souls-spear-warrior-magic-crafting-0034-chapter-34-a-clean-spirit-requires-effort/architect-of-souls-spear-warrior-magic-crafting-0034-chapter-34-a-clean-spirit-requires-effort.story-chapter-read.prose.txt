Ferris looked over the room. He would be meditating here for the rest of the day, refining spirit gems.

He needed a place to tether Jade, and the only two items large enough were the table and the cot.

With a little rearrangement, he was able to lie down on the cot while touching both the refinement device and Jade. The chain was wrapped around the length of the table and clasped around Jade’s core. Frankly, it was better than his setup at Lily House, as Ferris wasn’t on the floor.

Closing his eyes, Ferris meditated. This district, and its focus on enchanting, made Ferris think about magical weapons. He hadn’t been able to bond a spear, but could he buy a magical one? Although, a magical spear would, in the end, be a pale imitation of a true bonded weapon…

He had heard that some magical items with special abilities took too long to use in battle unless the cultivator had an ability to help. Ferris wondered, was it because he had to meditate to enter the gate? Gilbert at the library had hinted that some cultivators could access a jade slip without a spiritual gate device, perhaps it was something like that?

Theodore had also raised questions in his mind. The idea that monsters left behind spiritual corpses was intriguing.

As always, time allowed his mind to clear and reveal his soul. Ferris, familiar with the jade slip reader from the library, sought the connection to the gate.

Walking through the gate, Ferris found himself on top of a large wall. Immediately, he felt the strange sensation of existing in his mental body in a place that wasn’t his soul. It was the same as when using a jade slip reader; there was just something indescribable missing compared to his soulspace.

Unlike the darkness of his soulspace, a soft glow filled the sky much like the Wild surrounding a bastion.

The wall he was standing on was several times wider and taller than the fort in his soulspace. While his fort was made of stone, this wall felt like pure metal.

Looking around, he could see the wall formed a large circle. There were six towers spaced evenly around, with some kind of ballista pointing inward. He couldn’t see any gate or entryway along the base of the wall, but there was what appeared to be a box attached to a pulley he could lower himself down on.

Inside the wall was a maze of glowing rocks. Yellow crystal-like formations scattered around the floor, along with large black chunks stuck to them. The black material must be the pollution Theodore had referred to.

Next to where Ferris had arrived, there were several racks proudly displaying magically glowing… tools. A shovel, hammer, pickaxe, rake, push broom, and more. Each emitted a shining light that put everything Ferris had made to shame. Just from a glance, these tools would likely be stronger than his weapons—even if he hadn’t lost them all. Imagine, Ferris thought with a chuckle, running around killing monsters with a shovel.

Next to the tools hung a couple large bags and a lightstone unlike any Ferris had made. It was brighter and attached to a necklace. Its presence here, just sitting around, suggested that it wasn’t as temporary as Ferris’. Either Theodore made it and placed it here earlier today, or it would last much longer than half a day—perhaps even permanent?

Looking around again, Ferris didn’t see Jade. The slime usually entered the soul at their Heartfire, just like Ferris. He’d hoped entering the gate would bring Jade along with him, but unfortunately not.

Ferris left the refinement device through the gate, and once more found his soul. Entering his soulspace, he quickly traveled to the slimespace to find Jade waiting for him. Picking up the slime, he tried leaving his soul, asking Jade over the bond to accompany him.

Walking through the gate of the refinement device a second time, Ferris was relieved to find Jade still in his arms. Next time he would see if they could enter together from the beginning.

Looking at the tools, he grabbed a shovel, pickaxe, bag, and the lightstone necklace. He didn’t really need the lightstone, as there was plenty of light shining down from the sky, but he wanted to inspect it. He also wanted a weapon, but embarrassingly the pickaxe was better than anything he had in his soulspace.

He briefly considered taking the tools back to his soulspace at the end of the day, but that would be stealing from Hollowfield. Even if it wouldn’t break his oath, his mother had raised him better. Also, Theodore would know when the next person using the device reported them missing, and who had taken them. He could probably get away with ‘borrowing’ them: use them while working, and then return them before leaving… Ferris wasn’t sure how easy it was to break the tools, or if that would even damage them.

He wanted to understand the rules and consequences better before bending them, but if there was no harm done, his oath wouldn’t mind.

Ferris and Jade approached the box and pulley system he’d seen. Stepping inside, they found a lever currently pointing to an up arrow. Experimentally, Ferris switched the lever to point at a down arrow.

The box shook a little, and then began descending. Fascinated, Ferris was unable to figure out how it worked. At the bottom the box gracefully slowed to a stop, and they exited.

Looking around from the bottom, Ferris felt like he was in a giant pit. The metal walls towered around him, much taller than they needed to be. The crystal maze in the center was dwarfed by the protection surrounding it.

As they approached the center, Ferris realized the crystal looked very familiar. In the slimespace, the crystal formation the Core Tree was growing out of looked exactly like a green version of these crystals.

With a sinking sensation, Ferris realized the black rocks also matched the ground on top of one of the towers in the slimespace. This ‘pollution’ that he was clearing to refine the spirit gem existed in the slimespace as well: the black rock ground around the Core Tree.

Theodore had been surprised to explain how to refine spirit gems… did he expect Ferris to have experience from the slimespace, clearing out the black rock that was around the core crystal and tree?

Further, why did a spirit gem look like Jade’s core, spiritually speaking? There were some differences… like the color and sheer scale. The color might be due to the aspect of the core, but the crystal maze in the spiritual device was many times larger than Jade’s crystal in the slimespace.

Importantly, could Ferris ‘refine’ Jade’s core by removing the black rocks from the green crystals? This strongly suggested it, but… what if it hurt the slime? Theodore had also said monsters spiritually develop around a gem, which remained after they died. Ferris assumed the pollution was akin to a corpse—like dead flesh—but what if it was a part of Jade? The book on familiars hadn’t mentioned this at all.

If the black rock was spiritual remains of a monster, did that mean a different monster had Jade’s core before? Or was it a natural result of a monster’s life, like dead skin flaking off?

Ferris approached a crystal and felt it. Close up, it looked and felt slightly different than the dark green crystal formations in the slimespace. The porous black rock, however, was exactly the same.

The black rock was fused onto the side of the crystal. Tentatively, he hit it with the pickaxe a couple times. It took a bit of strength, but eventually the black rock broke off and fell to the ground. It was a bit too heavy to pick up, but Ferris was easily able to break it up with a few more swings.

Inspecting a chunk of the black rock, Ferris wondered if Jade should eat it. Theoretically, that would make a copy appear above the Consume Pool, which would dissolve away along with the original in Jade’s body. After Jade ate food, something was left behind in the slimespace floating down the Digest River, but he wasn’t sure exactly what.

If ‘refining’ a gem worked by removing this pollution, would letting Jade eat it harm them? Would it leave something bad in the slimespace, hurting the clams in the Digest Rivier?

Or, if the black rock was a kind of ‘spiritual corpse,’ would the Consume Pool and Digest River abilities transform it into something useful—just as they did physical corpses?

Ferris wanted to experiment. Hemon had advised him to find a job assignment with inherent advantages, and feeding Jade was a big one.

The problem was, Ferris was in a very vulnerable position. He had just found a way to harvest from the Digest River. What if the black rock tainted the qi chunks sitting at the bottom, or harmed the clams? He needed to at least gather everything that had accumulated before risking it. Ferris decided not to feed anything strange to the slime that day and relayed the restriction to Jade.

Despite this, Ferris didn’t want to abandon potentially useful material. Theodore had said to clear it away from the crystals, but he said nothing about where it went afterwards. The tools were obviously valuable, but the gunk he was clearing away was not. Ferris could take the black rocks and store them in his soulspace until he had time to experiment. Worst case, Theodore would note that the ‘pollution’ was gone and ask for it back. The man might be annoyed, but then he should have specified when Ferris asked what to do with it.

Settling in for a long day of labor, Ferris started clearing the black rocks. Jade couldn’t help, as the slime would have to eat the rocks to clean. Instead, Jade explored the area near Ferris, systematically mapping the space around him as he moved. According to the bond, the slime was having a ball.

Some of the black rocks were attached to crystals, while others lay around on the ground. Ferris wasn’t sure if the rocks on the ground affected clarity, or if it was just the ones touching the crystals, but if they ended up being useful, he wanted them all. Later, he could test not collecting any at all—just knocking them to the ground—and see if the gem appeared clearer in the real world. Ferris doubted it, as why else would the sack have been provided?

The sack also turned out to be more than it appeared. After a while, Ferris realized that it wasn’t filling up as fast as he expected. Somehow, it was larger on the inside than it should be. Thinking about it was odd, and made him a bit dizzy to look and feel around inside.

The lightstone necklace… was hard to evaluate. The sky was too bright to really test it out, and any light was washed away.

The work was hard, swinging the pickaxe and then picking up the rocks. He didn’t use the shovel as often as he’d expected, and he didn’t see much use for some of the other tools. Perhaps different gems required different tools. He took a couple of breaks to recover, but it was hard to tell how long it took to clear the crystal maze.

After at least an hour, he couldn’t see any large pieces left and decided to stop. There were pebbles and small chunks, but gathering them would take significantly longer. The bag had never completely filled. Somehow, it didn’t weigh as much as it should. It was heavier than when empty, but not nearly enough to account for what he put in.

Nothing had attacked him, and he had tried to stay on guard, but it was easy to see how a cultivator could get ambushed while distracted. Unfortunately, Jade was not the best lookout, despite the slime’s excitement to explore.

Returning to the box and pulley system, Ferris and Jade were able to rise back up on top of the wall. He left the pickaxe, shovel, and lightstone back on their stands, but attempted to take the sack full of black rock out of the refinement device’s gate… and failed. Something prevented him from moving the bag through the gate.

Perhaps this was why Theodore hadn’t said anything about the expensive-looking tools. There was some protection keeping them here. It was a bit frustrating, as he just wanted to use the bag to transport the rocks.

Ferris sighed. There went his idea to use these tools to work on his soulspace. He left the magical bag in the spiritual device and went to fetch his qi crafted bag. Thankfully, he was able to bring his own bag in without any problems.

It took six trips to transport the black rocks, but each was very quick. Ferris dumped the rocks in a pile near the Heartfire, excited to have something new to experiment with.

Moving items through the void between his soulspace and the gate device was strange. It was like they stayed with his mental body, but didn’t have the same material form while in transition. Ferris didn’t understand it, but it worked.

After waking up, Ferris inspected the yellow spirit gem. It absolutely sparkled with an inner light, completely different from the cloudy glitter from before. It was now translucent, and he could see clear through it.

Removing the gem from the clamp, Ferris lifted it up to look closer. He could still see slight imperfections, but it was like he was holding a different gem than before.

Jade got excited over the bond and rolled over. The slime really wanted to eat the gem, but Ferris quickly stopped them.

“You can’t eat gem,” he held it high up, just in case.

“Gem feels delicious, why can’t I eat?” The slime may have memory problems, but their communication still improved each day. Jade had to constantly relearn concepts, but they were getting better at comprehension of words beyond emotions.

“Gem does not belong to us. Stealing is bad, we are not thieves,” Ferris replied, attempting to give the idea of ownership.

Jade stopped and swirled in confusion. The notion that things had owners had never occurred to them. The slime usually either ate something or ignored it.

Ferris carried Jade down the hall, where a mundane attendant took the yellow spirit gem and exchanged it for a cloudy blue one. There was a clock nearby, and Ferris saw that he had made good time. At this rate, he could finish all four gems with two hours remaining in the shift.

After slotting the cloudy blue gem, Ferris returned to his cot. Looking at Jade, he wanted to see if they could enter the refinement device together—instead of the slime entering the slimespace.

“As we meditate, follow me,” Ferris sent images and feelings over the bond to Jade. He tried to relay the idea of staying with him instead of entering the soul.

Jade swirled a bit but was eager to try. Entering meditation, Ferris tried to go slower and stay connected over the bond. Somehow, he started to get the impression of another presence following him towards the spiritual device’s gate. It was Jade, he was sure of it through the bond.

Walking through the gate, Ferris was excited—yet not surprised—to find Jade next to him.

Although he expected Jade’s presence, there was a different surprise waiting for them within the refinement device’s walls. The crystal formations were similar—except blue—but now there was water up to his knees filling the bottom of the pit. It completely covered Jade, and the slime could barely move around in it. After splashing around a bit, Ferris was worried that an enemy would swim up and kill the slime before he noticed. Regretfully, he had Jade wait on top of the wall to be safe.

It took Ferris significantly longer to wade around while breaking and collecting the black rock. He knew he missed some of it, lost in the water. At least he knocked off all that he could see fused to the crystals.

It was a bit odd, being separated from Jade, but also not. They’d spent almost every waking moment since bonding together, only really spending time apart for a brief time in the soulspace. Now the slime was far away, up on the wall, but also constantly present in his mind…

When he was done, it only took five trips to transport the black rock to his soulspace. In the real world, the blue gem now shone with the same brilliant light as the yellow one had, although maybe not as clear. It was hard to tell, as the previous one had a lighter color. The attendant accepted it without comment, and then gave them a red gem. Ferris noted that it had taken significantly longer to refine the blue gem, and half the shift was over.

Ferris and Jade took a brief break for lunch. Mother Lily had packed sandwiches again, although surprisingly they weren’t bird meat. The grocer must have been out, Ferris joked to himself, forcing her to find something else.

It was some kind of red meat, smoked and heavily peppered. The mustard sauce was still a bit tame for his liking, but an improvement overall. Once again, the sandwich’s bread was light and fluffy… perhaps baked using the techniques from On the Preparation of Food?

As they ate, Ferris tested Jade on some of the numbers from breakfast, which the slime still remembered perfectly.

After eating, they started on the red spirit gem. As if to mock Ferris’ wish for ‘more heat’ in his lunch, the area around the red gem’s crystals was sweltering. It was like being near a bonfire, although thankfully not too much to handle.

Somehow, Ferris’ mental body sweated as he worked, even though he never drank water or ate in the soulspace. Jade barely even noticed the temperature.

Ferris was able to refine the red gem almost as fast as the yellow. He took a couple of extra breaks due to the heat, but it didn’t slow him down—other than being miserable to work in. He got another six loads of black rock from the gem afterwards. It glimmered as brightly as the others after being refined, but it was hard to compare them with the different colors.

The last gem for the day was light blue. Ferris was wary from the moment the attendant presented it, dreading another slog through the water. Thankfully, inside the spirit gem, it was simply cold. The hard work swinging the pickaxe kept him warm, and soon the cold barely troubled him. It was chilly, but not freezing, and much better than working in the heat. Just like with the heat, Jade didn’t notice the cold.

There was one tricky section, near the middle. Generally, the black pollution would separate from the crystal with a solid hit from the pickaxe, but the blue gem had some black gunk that was sticky. He could pull most of it off, but it left a layer of black tar adhered to the crystals.

Eventually, Ferris had to return to the wall and try a couple of other tools. He found a large push broom that cleared the gunk off like magic, sticky tar flaked off easily in large pieces.

No, Ferris thought, it isn’t like magic, it is magic. The broom glowed brighter as he scrubbed up and down.

Finding the correct tool had taken some time, but not nearly as long as wading through the water in the blue gem. He only got five loads of black rock from this gem, although it looked the brightest yet.

There was about an hour left in the work shift when Ferris turned in his fourth gem. He considered starting on another, but quickly rejected the idea. He was tired. He was used to long days of physical training, but working in his mental body drained him in unfamiliar ways.

Happy to be done, he returned the refinement device and left the building. The Ox statue still stood tall in the waning light as he passed it on the way out. Ferris couldn’t help standing a little straighter as he moved past it.

He’d worked like an Ox that day, after all.
