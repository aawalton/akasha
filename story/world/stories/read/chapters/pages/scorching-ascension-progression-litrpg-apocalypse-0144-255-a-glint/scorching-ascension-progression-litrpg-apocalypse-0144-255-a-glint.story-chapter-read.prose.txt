The second upgrade wasn’t as dramatic as the first. At least not outwardly, aside from a few crackling bolts that scorched the cement below him and the bit of wind. But inwardly?

It was as if Ethan’s body and spirit had become some sort of funnel, gulping in all the surrounding mana to fuel whatever change was taking place. His flesh burned, as if every muscle fiber was being wrung. His bones ached and pulsed with pain. It started with his chest. With his ribs. The pain pulsed and spread, incredibly sharp as if his bones were being shattered and reassembled in the fraction of a second it took his brain to process the pain. Ethan found himself trembling, not even remembering to breathe until the wave passed, leaving only ache and fatigue in its wake. Ethan labored for breath, teeth bared as he tasted his own acrid sweat. No, it wasn’t even sweat. It tasted too foul for simple sweat. But he couldn’t focus on that, and instead of worrying about it, he just closed his eyes and kept breathing. The energies continued pouring into his body and while the worst had passed, the experience definitely rivaled some of his most painful upgrades. The magic kept pouring into him, washing away the worst of it, changing him, and eventually, the rate at which the mana was being sucked into his chest slowed, and his breaths came through a little easier.

But even that was no mercy.

Ethan immediately recoiled from his own smell. Thankfully, the water was nearby and so, he took a quick dip and used it to collect himself and recover a little, which didn’t take too long. The residual aches and fatigue quickly seeped away and after he pulled himself out, he ignited his scales. The difference was instantly obvious.

His regular flames had always been somewhat heftier. Ever since he’d gotten the initial form of the trait. It was as if they were a mix of smoke and fire. Somewhat more physically present than regular flames, but without being something as physical as water, which allowed him to use the fire for shielding and whatnot. But after the upgrade?

Well, they still weren’t as material as water, but they were significantly closer to that state as they’d ever been. The fire swirled about his frame, obeying his will seamlessly, and when his scales were dry, he collected the fire he summoned into his palm, condensing it in the form of an orb whose weight he could feel against his palm.

Lobbing it in front of him, he willed it to spread into a barrier, and the flame obeyed, creating a semi-translucent panel of super-heated, rippling air.

Ethan grinned, and wondered how effective the new fire was at healing him. And while he wasn’t in the mood to injure himself to test, he’d bet he just had become much more difficult to put down. Between the healing flames and the upgrade of [Enduring Brawler]?

“I guess I’m as ready as I’ll ever be. As for the rest of my resources…”

Eyeing his interface, Ethan only had a couple thousand fragments left, and plenty of essence, though he might need the latter in the rift. Especially if he came to need his Prime Manifestation. All in all, it was good enough for now. As for his loadout, it was the same as what he had been using the last few days, so he kept it as is and so, with all of his preparations done, Ethan pulled in the System’s warning once more and after taking a deep breath he accepted its conditions.

A ring of glowing, golden symbols flared around him and by the time he turned to examine one of the rotating symbols, two more rings appeared, slowly orbiting him, growing brighter with every second until Ethan found himself squinting and before he knew it, there was a flash of bright, blinding lights and then—a stutter. Barely for an instant, Ethan felt discombobulated, as if, while teleporting from one spot to the next, there was a lag or a stutter in the middle. But he had no time to try and understand what happened. One moment he was in the cool, comfortable air of the dock and the next, a wave of searing heat buffeted him and drew a hiss of pain out of him as the bottom of his scaled feet burned. But the worst of it all was that he still couldn’t see. There were still dark and bright spots in his eyes as he took to the air to spare his feet whatever surface it was he had landed on.

Then he felt the heating metal on his wrists.

Ethan flared his flames around him, cursing as he blinked the bright lights away. He even pulled some of the flames to his eyes and activated [Heal] to accelerate whatever it was that was going on with them. Thankfully, that was effective, and the spots quickly subsided to reveal a golden expanse of dunes in all directions and above, three massive suns blazed in a bronze sky, bearing down on him, making it so the air never stopped rippling with the sweltering heat.

“What the…”

Ethan was at a loss for words. By now, he had experienced quite a few rifts and even explored a desert-like ecosystem the day prior. But it was nothing like the place he found himself into. He could barely handle the heat, even with his resistance to it. The air was full of mana and he knew that he couldn’t afford to stay under the three suns for long. Hell, he didn’t even know if he had enough water.

Where was he supposed to go? He could see no monsters roaming about. Nothing to fly toward but as he was about to pick a direction, the message arrived, and Ethan took a second to read it.

Rift challenge: Be one of the four finalists, and defeat the sealed guardian.

Rift restriction: None of the prime forms can be used. If either bracelets are removed, the participant will be eliminated and extracted out of the rift.

Rift reward: 1 special rift token. 3 mid-grade primagems. 1 item token. 1 tier-3 spell token.

Ethan’s brows instantly furrowed. “One of the four finalists…?”

So he wasn’t alone in here. Were they from Earth? Or was he about to encounter his first non-Earthers? Ethan was definitely curious, but he pulled his gaze back up to the restrictions. No prime forms meant no horns, no lance, and no wand. He wouldn’t have their abilities, either. No empowered spells. No charging with the horns. Ethan was still trying to catalog what he wouldn’t have access to when he caught the glint from the corner of his eye.

“Shi—”

With a curse, he ducked, feeling the cold blast the arrow left in its wake. With a beat of his wings, he flew higher, scanning the ground until he picked up the glint in the distance, only for it to shine brighter as another glacial arrow shot toward him.

“I guess I’m not alone in this one,” he grinned, which looked more like a grimace considering the unbearable heat bearing down on his scales. He glanced down at his arm and frowned. His scales looked dry. Almost desiccated. They had that pale luster to them, like a crocodile’s who’d been too long out of the water and into the sun.

Then it clicked, and Ethan groaned. “Oh. I’m just stupid”, he chided himself as he sent his senses inwardly, and finding the switch, he flipped the Draconic Set’s affinity from water to fire.

The change was immediate. A sense of relief spread out through his scales, and a content sigh escaped his throat as he flew higher, eyes still on the barely visible sniper in the distance and as he did, he cracked his knuckles, the Ossyrath claws glinting dangerously in the light. That explained how sensitive to heat he’d been ever since he’d stepped in here. His own flames weren’t that difficult to resist, even with the water affinity but clearly, that wasn’t an option in here and while the suns were still dangerous to stay under for long, it was much less of a problem now. So he focused on the current situation he found himself into.

So. This was some sort of competition. No prime forms was the limitation, and he had to keep both of the rune-engraved bracelets on his wrists safe until only four people were left, at which point they could challenge the boss. Looking down at them, they were a band of gold, around an inch wide. They were pretty snug too, which was good for him. Because unless someone really got a hold of his wrist or cut off his arms, it would be tough for his opponent to remove them.

“That’s straightforward enough,” he smiled, cracking his neck and with a push of mana, he accelerated toward the one who had attacked him.
