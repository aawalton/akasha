I exited the cave first.

Nuzzle catapulted onto my chest and licked my neck, warm saliva dripping down my shirt. So gross…

"Yeah, yeah. Missed you too, bud," I laughed, unable to help myself. He'd waited here patiently just like I asked.

We'd had to travel slow on the way back to avoid making too much noise and being found out, despite the urgency of leaving before someone else arrived. If the guards in there were waiting on a contact from the Meadow, they would've been coming down the same tunnel we used. And if they were anything above an Amethyst we'd be dead.

Or… I'd be dead at least.

And somehow that was less concerning than what we'd observed down there. Someone had found a way to control Unchained Sins. That alone was unbelievable. Even in my short time in Penumbra, I understood the gravity of that discovery. No one had mentioned anything like this, as if it weren't even in consideration as a possibility. That Reaper had been an Iron, so this skill was probably something only he had access to. That was dangerous. A lot of power for one person.

And worse, it begged the question… Had the ones that destroyed Silas' village—and killed Allium—escaped? Or had they been set free?

A chill ran down my spine at that last thought.

"We have to get to Meadowvale," Silas said as he stepped out from the shadow of the cave.

Nuzzle scurried up to him and sat on his boot.

I agreed. Whatever was going on here, regardless of the intention, it was far from safe. I had to update Valciferous on these findings.

"And who do you think you are?" a new voice questioned. It came from above.

A man stood atop one of the stoney outcroppings opposite the cave. The sky edged toward twilight, creating a colorful glow silhouetting the hooded figure. His Amethyst cape waved in the slight breeze, catching the light.

He extracted his scythe and asked again. "Who are you?"

Silas tensed beside me.

"On guard. Waiting for you," I said, trying to keep my composure. "Some of the surviving locals are wandering about. Just keeping an eye."

The man hopped down to our level, pointing his scythe at Silas. "What's the kid doing here?"

Silas backed up and shifted behind me. I could hear his breath quicken.

"He's new. I'm showing him the ropes."

"What was he saying about going to Meadowvale, then?"

Ah, shit. He'd been up there longer than I thought. Maybe even before we got out if he saw Nuzzle and waited. I guess time's up…

I shrugged and threw a Life Drain at the man, the shadowy tendrils snaking into his eyes and mouth. He yelled in fury and fought through it. His scythe came within inches of my chest. I backed away, bumping into Silas and urged him out of the way. This was no fight for an Amber.

Some sort of shield popped up before the man. Whatever item he'd used it interrupted my Life Drain.

I cut off the skill and released my scythe just in time to deflect another swing as he charged forward. I responded with a swipe at the head, which he ducked under. I had to be careful to avoid hitting his Will. He'd be dead. But I'd be Marked. My situation was already precarious enough without that added complication.

I could phase him out by finishing off his Health, like I had with Kaiju in the arena, but he'd know who I was. Know that we were at this cave. When he returned, whoever he worked with would know too. It was a difficult dance. Needing to defeat someone, but not being able to remove them permanently without endangering yourself.

I Aero Shifted away from another strike only to feel a piercing pain in my abdomen. A scream left my throat as my opponent pulled a bloody, glistening ice spear from my side. I fell to a knee holding the wound as fresh red liquid poured between my carapace fingers.

Fuckkk. It hurt.

"I've battled enough of you Astral Affinity assholes," the Amethyst Reaper sneered. "You all rely too heavily on your Aero Shift."

My Health tanked.

And a scythe swung toward my neck.

I couldn't react in time.

The blade clanged against something, stopping inches from my throat. A stone shield hovered before me. Shards broke off and clattered to the ground, but it held.

What? But, how?

Then I realized… It was a skill from Silas.

The boy yelled like a maniac and charged the Amethyst, his Amber cloak flowing behind him as he summoned a ball of fire in his off hand. He barraged the enemy with a flurry of attacks, forcing him to backpedal. He caught him off guard, but it wouldn't last.

I could already seeing the foe dodge and deflect easier, formulating a plan to end Silas. Once he could attack it'd be like swatting a fly.

And I had no disillusion that he'd allow Silas to phase out. He'd assume we've seen too much. He'd go for the Will.

He'd kill him.

I forced myself to stand through the pain.

The Amethyst Reaper was already battling back. He cast a patch of ice under Silas' foot, then shoved him. Silas fell, all his skills breaking. The scythe swung toward his chest. Toward his Will.

I was right.

But I wasn't going to let him die.

I Aero Shifted forward, pushing through the pain, reaching out with my carapace arm. I caught the blade in my hand, the point tickling my cheek. A little too close.

Then I Aero Shifted forward again, still holding the enemy Reaper's own scythe.

It went straight through his neck.

Warm blood sprayed as I zoomed past, speckling my face like bugs on a windshield.

I came to a stop ten paces away on one knee. Breath heaving. Heart pounding.

The Reaper's head toppled to the side and fell to the ground not unlike the boy who'd gone white. Though for drastically different reasons. Then his cloak swirled, pulling the decapitated head and body away into the vortex.

I sagged.

Heard Silas crying.

We were both spent. Physically and emotionally. From what we saw… From this…

But we were alive.

I crawled over to Silas, trying to ignore the searing fire in my side from where I'd been stabbed through, and hugged him tight.

We both settled after a while and Silas procured a pair of healing items from his inventory. I didn't really appreciate hand-outs, but in the case I'd make an exception.

We made our way north toward the nearest entrance to Meadowvale on horseback. Silas knew of a stable near his village, which had luckily been unharmed by the Unchained. He bought us each an undead stallion. Mine was white like Allium, but it was just a horse. No playfulness, no intelligence, personality.

Silas had neglected to mention he was wealthy. I guess when your father is a self-appointed mayor in Penumbra that comes with the territory. I told him I'd repay him someday, for the horse and the Health rejuvenation item he'd given me, but he declined.

The pace we travelled was brutal and constant, aside from a few breaks to take out marked Amber Sins along the road. Nuzzle's saliva kept splattering me in the face as he sat atop the stallion's head. I tried to push him down, but he kept climbing back up. Eventually, I gave up and suffered through the lizard's mouth rain.

It wasn't enough to distract me from what we'd witnessed though. Unchained enslaved and caged like animals. Cowed beneath the skill of that Iron Reaper. But who was he? What was the goal of all this?

We passed by another abandoned village. The destruction here appeared several days older. We didn't get close enough to see anything besides the swarm of crows sitting on the remaining roofs, rotting flesh hanging from their mouths. That sight and the scent of death were enough to keep us at a wide perimeter around the place.

I patted Allium on the neck—no. Not Allium. Just an unnamed white horse. Allium was dead because of whatever was going on in the cave, whether intentional or not. I'd be gone too if it wasn't for…

Lancelot…

Lancelot was there at the lighthouse. But why? I'd never questioned it until now. Calliope had mentioned it was odd he was in the Meadow. He'd been right there where the Unchained were, as if he was waiting for them.

And now there was an Iron Reaper here as well, in secret. It couldn't be a coincidence. The two highest rank Reapers, both out here in the lowest stage zone.

There was no reason. Unless…

The Shepherds. This had to be related to the Shepherds.

I pushed the horse harder, the urgency of the situation driving my commands.

Soon, the wall of Meadowvale appeared on the horizon.

Soon, I would speak with a god once again.
