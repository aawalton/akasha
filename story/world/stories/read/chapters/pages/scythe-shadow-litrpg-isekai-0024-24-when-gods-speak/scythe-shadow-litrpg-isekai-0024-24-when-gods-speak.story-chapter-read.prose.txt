I stared up at the god with my best 'F-you' glare, as a pair of spear-wielding Penumbran guards directed me toward a tunnel. His golden-eyed gaze followed me.

It was him.

The one who spoke to me as I was being re-formed for this world. He wore the same white and gold robe, the same golden antler crown.

My view ended as I was led into the short tunnel.

The guards gave me adequate space on either side. The one to my left kept glancing at me then back forward, gripping his spear like a vice.

"Everything alright?" I asked.

The guard practically jumped out of his skin. "Y-yes, of course!" He paused. "Are you really a Death Reaper?"

"I have the Death Affinity, so… I guess?"

"Fucking metal, man."

I played coy, hoping to get more information. "Is that uncommon?"

The other guard spoke up this time as they guided me toward a set of stairs. I still had no idea where they were taking me. "Uncommon would be an understatement."

"There's only ever been one before now."

"Only one?" I asked. The old man—what was his name? Renauld?—failed to mention that.

He nodded solemnly. "At least that anyone remembers from tales passed down."

"What happened to him?"

"No one knows."

The guards stopped at the top of the stairs before an ornate white door with gold ornamentations, including a branching antler crown. One knocked, and a few seconds later a deadbolt clunked unlocked.

"Good luck," the nervous guard said as they both turned back down the stairs.

I opened the door.

The room was small—more of an anteroom than a living space—but adorned with luxurious pristine white upholstered furnishings and an elegant coffee table. Eight tapestries lined the far wall on either side of another door matching the one I'd already passed through. The tapestries each depicted what apparently passed for a god here, similar in appearance, yet distinct. Sewn behind each figure was a scene that seemed to depict one of the various zones I'd seen on the map on The Meridian's ceiling.

I spotted the god I'd met on the farthest left tapestry, a verdant meadow sprawling behind him.

A glass of wine had already been poured and a platter of smoked meats and cheeses sat on the table.

On the sofa off to the side lounged a Sapphire Reaper dressed in tight leather, a whip looped at her belt.

“You,” I said.

“Me.” She smiled and skewered a slice of cheese with a long fingernail before sliding it between her ruby painted lips. “I'm afraid we were never properly introduced. I’m Trix. You’re Zephyr. And you’re a lot heavier than you look.”

“What do you want with me?”

“Right to the point. I can appreciate that.” She had another sip of wine, taking her time to swirl the drink and smell the aroma. “You may have noticed someone special in attendance during today's battle."

"I did." I eyed the door on the opposite wall, fairly certain it connected to the balcony I'd seen the god watching from.

"What you may not know is that most Reapers in Penumbra swear fealty to one god or another. Valciferous is my liege, my employer, my companion. My god."

"I met him once before."

"Yes, you did. That's why he instructed me to find you."

"Did he instruct you to kidnap me and throw me unprepared into an arena too?"

She grinned, licking her teeth as she skewered another piece of cheese. "Of course. A test of your mettle and ingenuity. Anyone can succeed with enough preparation. Few can react unaware."

"If he's trying to recruit me, he's not doing a very good job."

"Oh, it's far too late for that, honey."

The far door creaked open.

Valciferous, the god, strode in, slow and steady as a gentle breeze.

"Trix," he said. "Are you done torturing the lad?"

"Torture? Me?" She put her hand to her breast innocently.

"Leave us now."

Trix hesitated. "My Lord, are you—"

"Yes. I'm quite sure."

"I…" She stood, bowing low. "Of course. Thank you, my Lord."

Valciferous waited until she was gone and the door latched behind her to continue.

"Welcome to the Umbral Plane, little scythe. I imagine your experience thus far has been satisfactory? You certainly made it to Meadowvale faster than I expected."

Satisfactory? That's one word for it. "I had help."

"Indeed. You won't need to rely on them much longer."

I stood, picking up my glass of wine and taking a sip, trying to appear like I wasn't freaking the fuck out on the inside. It was impossible to read his golden glowing eyes and lack of facial expression. "Why am I here?"

"You already know. To extinguish Sin."

"That's why Reapers are here. Why me specifically? Why did you make me Death Affinity? I didn't ask for this?"

The god laughed—a heavy sound that echoed off the walls and made the tapestries flutter. "Make you? You were affiliated with death long before I summoned you here. No. You were always Death Affinity, weren't you, little scythe?"

The muscles clenched in my throat. No… I—I'm not. I—

Memories flashed through my mind.

Blood on a white carpet.

A knife dripping red liquid.

In my hand.

No!

I'd moved past this. I…

"Only a Death Affinity Reaper can acquire the skills to end sin forever," Valciferous said, bringing me back to the present. "We nearly got there before. Were so close to putting an end to everything. But that fool Zenith couldn't handle it, even as an Iron."

I blinked, not understanding his rambling.

"But you're here now. You'll do better. Of course, I'm getting ahead of myself. Look at you. You're an Amber, you're not ready. And Hell knows the Sins aren't going anywhere."

"You're a god," I interrupted. "Why don't you just end sin yourself?"

"Ah, yes. I forget the naiveté of you Reapers when you first arrive. Very well then. An explanation. I can not interfere in this world. I'm not omnipotent and can only see so much in a world so vast. So it's up to my scythes. It's up to you. I've been waiting for another Death Reaper to awaken for too long. The time is right. The Unchained are becoming more numerous, more aggressive. The Sins know we are winning, so they're going to extremes to fight back. A significant contingent of Reapers have betrayed us and joined them. Shepherds, they call themselves, as if Sin were their flock."

My mind was still reeling from the information overload, from my flashback, from all of it. But I paused. "You said joined them? There are Reapers fighting for sin?"

"It's abhorrent, I know." He took the wine glass from me and poured it on the white couch, leaving a far too familiar red stain. "But the symbol of Death strikes fear, demands loyalty, contains power. It's already a legend in itself. The people in both the Umbral and Vitrial Planes need a hero, Miles."

I cringed hearing my true name in this place. From this being.

"And you need to go home, yes? That is your desire. I can feel it in you."

Home…

"I can give it to you. Work with me and I can return you to the Vitrial Plane. Permanently. I can even erase all memory of this place if you so desire. All you have to do is swear an oath to me. We'll put an end to human sin and self-destruction together."

It was so tempting. I had to be here anyways. Killing Sins, harvesting Wills. But I'd be beholden to him. A god… There were so many unknowns around that.

"And if I refuse?"

Valciferous shrugged. "I may have selected you, but you were not my only option. You nearly died moments ago. I have no qualms letting that happen again. I must say though, you certainly announced your arrival to the Umbral Plane with aplomb. Better than I could have hoped for, if I do say. It makes sense to ride the wave so to speak."

I blinked, not quite comprehending the last few sentences. Too focused on what he said before that. "I… almost died? When?"

"Did no one tell you? Shame on your companions. Why do you think scythes aren't permitted in the arena? A scythe to your Will breaks the bond. With no bond, there is no soul. Not here. Not in the Vitrial Plane."

In the arena, Kaiju had pointed his scythe directly at my chest. I'd been inches from death.

"Your opponent, had he succeeded in ending you, would be Marked of course. The rest would take care of itself. I'd be down one Death Affinity scythe and have to begin my search over again, but such is the way these things go. I much prefer to stay the course. Do you not agree?"

I sat down.

I almost died.

I'd come close to using all my Volition and dying before, but this… This felt so much more visceral. More real.

I would have never seen Avery again. I watched the life we could've had together evaporate before my eyes. Possibilities erased from existence. Happiness torn asunder.

This being, this god, had no hesitation letting me die if I declined his offer.

In the end, there really was no choice.

I was made a Death Reaper. So a Death Reaper I'd be.

"What must I do?"
