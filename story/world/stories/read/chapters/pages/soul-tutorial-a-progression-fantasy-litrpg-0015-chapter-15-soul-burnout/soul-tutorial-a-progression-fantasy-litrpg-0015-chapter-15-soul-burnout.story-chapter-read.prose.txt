Luke lay on the floor with pale skin; the coolness flowed off his body. As warmth embraced him, bringing back his skin’s youthfulness. Before he passed out, he had spent his points on a restore.

But his body still shivered until the rejuvenating effect fully took place. After that intense fight, even though it was a better outcome overall compared to fighting the silver slime without soul energy.

His body was still left partially destroyed after taking the brunt of the attack, and even after having to gain enough points for a heal, left him near death.

But he had finally done it; now it was just the next floor that he had to worry about, floor 3. But he hoped that, with a bit more training with his soul energy he wouldn’t be so desperate again to lay everything on the line just to make it through.

Tired from all the fighting, exhausted from training just to end up more wounded than before, everything was alright in the end. He wasn’t just doing this to survive but to find his mother. Keeping her safe was all he wanted.

He knew nothing if she was here in this world or currently back on Earth. But figuring out that truth will only happen after he gains strength.

He looked toward the pedestal, wondering if anything would change. Despite having a lot of energy, his soul was utterly exhausted. He didn’t want to just take a break with an inevitably stronger opponent waiting for him on the next floor.

Luke made his way to doing what he always had: fighting a green slime. But not much had changed at all. He finished the slime quickly, finally grasping some proficiency in fighting with only one arm.

Concerning himself with fully learning the way they attack and bend their bodies. No longer concerned with an elite green slime appearing after killing a lot of its brethren. With being able to control soul energy better than he did before and actually being able to inflict some damage. If only he could pull out that ethereal blade once more.

But time will tell what happens in the future. But with having a bit more energy left to spare after the restore. Killing green slimes was the only way he could keep himself from his dark thoughts. But his mother’s face and nostalgia kept trying to flood his mind.

Mother, I can only hope you are still alive out there.

Having killed slimes for a while longer, his knuckles and arm were burned and bruised. Luke went in for another restore that took the usual 5 points. But he still had a surplus. With restoration flooding him, he got down on the floor, ready to stare at the sky before he fell asleep.

So if the beginning started with a green slime, then blue, then silver slime. What was it going to be next?

I find it odd that only slimes are appearing; I expected a more challenging foe by now. It doesn’t seem worthwhile to just defeat these slimy creatures.

But there’s not much he could do in this boring new world, except continuously smashing his fist against the gelatin body that stood in his way.. or even some metallic protection.

I think it’s time I just put more points into constitution.

Luke needed more stamina, and he couldn’t keep getting injured like this.

But his thoughts finally seem to have tired him out. Little by little, his eyes grew heavy before his head rested on his arm, peacefully going to sleep for the first time in a good while.

It didn’t last long before his eyes shot open, with a burning pain fluctuating in his chest. Luke immediately got up and tried to use the pedestal for a heal, but it fell short when the pain didn’t disappear even with the rejuvenating energy he felt throughout his body.

"What the hell is happening” he screamed.

The pain only grew, becoming worse, and without another thought, he tried to peer inside himself and see if there was a problem with his chamber.

It felt harder than usual to even do that with the constant burning in his chest.

Am I going to die? He thought.

With all the focus he could muster, he sat down on the ground. This time actually getting to see what had happened.

Compared to his usual white soul with hair-sized lines of golden color, the lines had turned blood red and his soul was struggling against a black outline consuming the white slowly.

He couldn’t comprehend what was happening inside his own body; from the outside, everything looked completely fine, as if nothing had happened at all.

But the burning pain didn’t disappear just because he could see what was happening. It only got worse over time, affecting not only his head but his heart felt heavy.

Tears flowed from his eyes as he tried his best to suppress the pain. How could he? It was as if his very soul were on fire. Luke passed out for a few seconds only to be woken again by the pain. This repeated over a few hours.

Why could the system heal everything else but not soul damage? Couldn’t it do something about this? I thought the system was omniscient.

Wait, why did he even think this? He didn’t know who or what the system even was.

He forced himself to believe the system was of unimaginable power; his thoughts got distracted again as the pain exploded in strength. His eyes shed tears of blood, his body completely tensed up as it started convulsing, muscles caved in, making his body look crushed, blood poured out of his pores.

The world before him turned red; his body expanded and deflated like a balloon. He could only cry and scream at the top of his lungs with his lungs losing all oxygen.

Was this it? He thought.

But as the worst of it came, it all disappeared the very next second. He was left lying in his own pool of blood, staring at the dark veil that surrounded him.

Peering into the very souls that make up the vast emptiness above him.

Title Unlocked: Withstand Burnout!

Effect: 5% more constitutional effectiveness

Luke tried his best to read those words that had just appeared before him in golden coloring, but he passed out before he could. The pain was stronger than anything he had ever faced before.

He woke up again, but this time he could not move at all. His body was completely strained to the point of no return. He could only wish he had been closer to the pedestal if had known he wouldn’t be able to move anymore.

But the golden panel still hovered before his eyes, and without further ado, he tried his best to read it with his hazy vision. Surprised to learn that this had all been just a burnout; if a burnout was this powerful, he wouldn’t want to do anything more with soul energy.

That alone almost made him want to give up on that path, but he knew it was his only way to survive in this world currently, or the only method he had.

Tears welled up in his eyes, fearing the future of being put through even worse pain, but he couldn’t give up yet and could only brace himself for the future.

Deciding he couldn’t stay in this singular spot forever; he didn’t know how he could access the pedestal from here. He wished he could just pull it up before his eyes.

But at the looks of it, he couldn’t, or just not yet at least. He was about half a meter away, and if he just crawled a little closer, maybe he could reach it if he fully extended his arm. But at the current state of his body, he didn’t think he could do much of anything except endure the pain.

While the pain in his soul might be gone, he was still under enough shock to make anyone back on Earth faint.

Actually, maybe the wounds he had right now would kill the average person, but Luke doesn’t even think he has gotten any stronger.

But he could only shrug his way through the pain and continued to look towards the souls in the sky to grasp at anything he possibility can.

Why can this world hold souls, but what even is this place?

Would I be stuck here forever?

How many floors are left to go through?

How strong would I be if I stayed here as long as possible?

Actually, why was I even sent here?

Not being able to remember clearly whether all the people back on Earth got sent here or just the unlucky ones.

Was there a requirement?

He hadn’t been in contact with anyone before this incident took place, so he doesn’t have anyone to really care for besides his mother as his only blood relative.

If he was supposed to die someday, he wanted to at least be able to keep his mother safe, the same one who raised him to be who he was today.

A grin dawned on his face, or at least he tried to, as it turned into a devious smile with bloodied teeth showing.
