*At the Safety Spires*

A wind rose in the living room, marking the beginning of something grand. It went ignored, save for the gathered gashapong around the potted tree. A plastic piece of toast no larger than a marble and no less sentient than anyone else was the first to speak with the young tree in garbled noises.

The rest of their squeaky chanting from the pot was washed away under Giat and Snu’s moans of pain.

A [GooBerry] monster hovering over Giat and Snu clasped their melty hands together. “Do you need me to kidnap some Adventurers?”

“Perhaps a wing of a hospital?” An [Almost Rabid Squirrel] chittered from her perch. “Wheel ‘em’in’ta Leather Spires and yeh grind ‘em’t’a’pulp? Spot’a murder would help?”

“The prison’s always good eats. No one will notice us taking a couple during the Tax Guild Stone hunt.” Stevie, a [Tangled Morphdame], who appeared as a little girl, but was really a flesh-eating larva in a human shaped shell, thought aloud.

“UGH. No kidnapping.” Giat and Snu groaned together, streaming sweat despite being bundled and cuddled under a thick blanket.

The first residents of The Safety Spires, monster’s seeking refuge and help for their internal torments, hovered and fussed over the Boss sisters, who appeared quite ill. Pale and sunken-eyed, their teeth chattered as if deep in a fever.

“Taking’s a nasty bit of business for all Bosses,” Vrshkeuc slurped on a little crab apple, her gremlin feet thrown up on the table, small body clothed in a velour track suit.

“No Adventurers coming to delve the Leather Spires for a couple days, and now the hunt is distracting everyone,” she waved her fruit around. “They’ll be fine. Been times where the girls have gone weeks without feeding the Taking. Nothing quite like a dungeon to spark the taste greed in Adventurers, and when they come back, probably tonight, they’ll get their feeding. Feel bad for those poor souls. The Glimps are getting restless.”

“Th-th-the long-longer it goes,” Giat’s teeth acted on their own as she spoke to the crowding monsters. “The wo-worse it gets. We-we’re OK f-for now.”

Britanni, who sat next to Vrshkeuc and fiddled with an iPad to go over the Safety Spires’ hundreds of applications, sucked her filed teeth and looked up.

“If only they would figure out what fed their Taking,” Britanni said sarcastically. “It’s totally not obvious.”

“Sh-shut up,” Snu moaned, clenching her stomach as a wave of murderous nausea. “Y-you don’t k-know, a-and neither d-do we.”

The resident monsters hung on the Boss’s words while Vrshkeuc and Britannni shared a knowing look.

“Right,” Britanni nodded, going back to filtering out the scammers. “Two succubi sisters and two boys start dating. Two Succubi sisters who are in obvious love with a couple doorknobs, no offense. There’s just no way they might be able to help.”

“SHUT UP.” Snu and Giat yelped together again. “It’s not an ‘admission of love’!”

“For sure, Mistresses. As you say,” Vrshkeuc snorted before sighing and inhaling the crab apple, core and all. “Some people just have all the luck when it comes to love.”

“What happened to Zanta? He seemed…active.” Britanii asked without looking up.

Vrshkeuc shook her head as she lounged deeper into the chair. “Meathead through and through. And not the fun kind.”

Another gale fluttered out from the potted tree, and another round of squeaks roused, beginning to harmonize as the gashapong—

“St-stupid boys,” Snu forced her stutter to stop, sitting up and wrapping herself into a blanket cocoon. “My Mark on his is going crazy now too. Do they ever stop getting themselves into trouble? Alex won’t even answer my messages. I’m worried.”

“Feels in my Mark on Brody that he’s…having fun but also terrified? I’d be jealous, but they’re surely together getting up to no good.”

“You’re not worried about him? What if they’re in trouble trouble? You know how these hunts get.” Snu turned to Giat.

“Worried?” Giat laughed. “Why would I be worried? Snusana, fuck, feel your Mark on Alex. What do you feel?”

Snu thought for a moment as she felt around her succubus Mark.

“Fear, excitement, frustration…speed?”

Giat tsked. “You were always the nice one, I swear, even though you have double the kills I do.”

“Mistress Snu just don’t like to play with her…food the way you do.” Vrshkeuc chimed in.

“Exactly,” Giat continued. “Fear I feel too from Brody. But the fun kind. Well, fun for them, until you push and push and bring them right to the brink of dea—”

“Ew, stop it, please,” Snu interrupted, gesturing around the room. “They don’t want to hear this, and neither should you be speaking like this in front of the gashapong.”

The [GooBerry] monster cleared their throat. “I would like her to continue, please.”

“Don’t be weird, Googina, this is a therapy center, not a smut forum. And yes, we’re putting you back on the Hedonists Anonymous rotation.” Giat reprimanded, causing Britanni to snicker.

The Boss sisters nuzzled in together while a gentle breeze blew across the room. Googina went to fix some tea with Stevie to supervise, and Britanni shot the shit with Vrshkeuc about boys.

Holed up in The Safety Spires to comfort the monster residents during the hunt mayhem, the gargoyles guarded The Leather Spires while the Glimps played pickleball with stiff socks and goopy eyeballs of Adventurer’s past inside.

Not a one noticed dozens of plastic toys crowded around the budding tree in the pot at the front, meditating as good as they could for a bunch of gashapong.

“Point is,” Giat finished after calming down her sister. “Worry when their fear turns sour. These two might be bone headed, but they always figure it out.”

“You’re right,” Snu nodded, grinning at her sister. “They always have it under control.”

*Somewhere over Lake Ontario”

“WE’RE GOING TO FUCKING DIE!” Alex yelled over the gushing wind, yanking the handlebars upwards. “SHOOT IT AGAIN, DANDELION! WE’RE GOING UP!”

“Need another good shot, and I’m running out of stored ammo! Let it attack!” She laughed back, swinging one handed from the roof to get into position.

The Expediter swam up almost straight into the air, the thin pipes that veined the body glaring bright. Brody clenched his hand tight on the seat to ensure he and Tank wouldn’t fall backwards into the cage, which was a lesson that had only taken three reminders. Thankfully the cage stopped them from plummeting into lake Ontario, though that didn’t make Tank landing on him any easier. The metal plated pitbull was dense.

CAW! CAW! CAW!

Tilting his hands forward, Alex levelled the vehicle.

“Block, Acorn! Now!” Dandelion screamed as the putrid Skill fired.

Green flames roared beneath the flying vehicle in a cone, going to engulf them from the bottom up. But hanging by a single pinky from the bottom of The Expediter was Acorn, managing to cling on despite their speed, and the only reason they still lived.

The wordless gnome folded his body in and activated [Unrelenting Block] at the perfect time, extending it as always into his [Indominable Plastique Shell]. Rather than encase his person in a temporary impenetrable barrier, the shell of plastic took on the ability and morphed it.

WOOF!

“HA, you like that, Tank? BACK, YOU MANGY BEAST. None can break through his barrier.” Dandelion hollered, already swinging down to where Acorn hung.

A translucent barrier shot out in a circle from the shield, growing the unbreakable range to a five foot by five-foot wall.

Acorn stared into the flame from behind his Skill and shield, aweing at the damage his [Twice Returned Damage] took. While nothing could break through it, he could feel the awful power from the monster. Without his protection, he knew that they’d be falling and crisped to a mutated pulp into the lake.

The flame cut off, and the monster chasing them swooped away in a wide circle, cawing the whole way.

Big as a car and filthier than an alley shoe, the [Gluttonous Seagull] had spotted its next meal after a simple high-speed rip from Pearson Airport. With thousands of monsters, Adventurers, and civilians beating the hell out of each other in search for a single stone, a glowing motorcycle shooting over the horde hadn’t been that out of place. Dandelion had only needed to brain a few that possessed more bravado than luck.

And with Brody’s grunted directions and pointing, Alex aimed their ride towards their destination. Niagara Falls.

How Niagara Falls is considered Toronto is beyond me. Stupid geography. It’s halfway round the lake!

“It’s sweeping again?” Alex scanned the horizon as they flew above the churning waters that allegedly mutated swimmers and freshwater lobsters alike. Rumor was there were some the size of boats, worshipped by hippie cultists and kept well fed on lake yachters who decided that three drinks was enough lubricant to plunge into the filthy water.

“It’ll be back.” Dandelion grunted as she and Acorn planted themselves atop The Expediter, scanning for the beast.

It had learned a lesson or three from Dandelion’s firepower and seemed more interested in murder rather than where they headed. After she pinged one of its ruddy feet clean off, the monster began opting for dive attacks before scampering off in a flurry of feathers.

“We got to be flying two hundred, two-fifty kilometers an hour. How far are we from the Falls? And Tank is sure the Tax Guild Stone is there? The drones must be on their way, and we don’t want to send them on a goose chase.” Alex asked as he turned his head so Brody could hear.

Brody pinged Tank, who’s head was off to the side so that he could enjoy the wind. The monster that kept annoying them wasn’t a bother. If things got bad, he’d just eat it, simple as that. He could smell a million scents, he was being hugged a bit too tightly by his master, and his new pack was headed in the direction of his old master’s dig site. He loved digging! He loved car rides! What a good day!

“…Yeah,” Brody grunted. “Bit ahead. Tank is sure…”

Now Alex did turn to face Brody as they soared through the air on a magical motorcycle.

“What? What’s wrong?” Alex asked.

“A lot of…messages,” Brody smirked. “From Snu.”

“Don’t read my messages!” Alex called. “You were just supposed to phone the drones!”

“Whatever you…say, Booboobear.”

“Hey! That’s privat—”

“Guys, shut up,” Dandelion interrupted, pointing with her gun. “Here comes our friend and…I think he brought company.”

Alex swore. “Damnit. Seagulls are weirdly enough pack animals. Filthy little bastards. Filthy BIG bastards. How many?”

“Uh…a dozen. Two dozen.”

“Crap. Crap! I don’t have any attack skills, and neither does Brody. Tank does, and a real nasty one, but he can’t climb the cage like you two. Shit!”

Dandelion and Acorn looked at each other, and even without a mouth, she could tell that he was grinning.

Both Gnomes hooked a finger on the corner and threw themselves over so that they faced Alex and their backs were towards their flight direction.

“Brody?” Dandelion laughed.

“Mm?”

“You think you can get Tank to bite Acorn’s shield? About a dozen times?”

Alex gasped, revving the engine accidentally, and he turned around to face his clone and Tank.

“Oh my System, Brody. Acorn is going Gnome Nuke mode.”

Dandelion’s laugh crested above the dozens of caws of [Gluttonous Seagull]s.
