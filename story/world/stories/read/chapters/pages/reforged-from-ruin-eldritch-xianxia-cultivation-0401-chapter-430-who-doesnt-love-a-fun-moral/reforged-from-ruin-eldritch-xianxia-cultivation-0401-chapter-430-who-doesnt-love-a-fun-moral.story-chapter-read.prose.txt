Two days is a lot of time.

It’s also kind of no time at all, especially not when you’re biologically immortal. All three of the elders seem completely unbothered to stay floating outside in front of her space-fold.  Maybe they’re just enjoying all of the life-Qi she’s pouring out, getting some boosts to their own vitality, though she’s not sure how that works with them being the way they are. Inhuman and alien, but they are still flesh, at least to some degree, and she’s got great Fleshy goodness, so they’re probably benefitting.

Between that and keeping her contained inside a formation, they’ve mostly just stayed still in their encampments, a few miles around the crater above and below where the plateau used to be. Close enough that a Warrior realm movement technique can have them right back on her within milliseconds, far enough not to be blatantly annoying.

They have some of their allies with them, the more powerful ones perhaps, or the ones they want to help grow from the energies around her as well. She’s not exactly sure, and she doesn’t really care.

More of her focus is on the formation surrounding her.

It manifests as a dome of Qi, invisible to the naked eye but brilliant to her synesthesia. There’s so many symbols on it, literally millions of them. They interlock, shift into place and then disconnect, transforming into more complex letters and symbols and then breaking apart into new ones. It’s beautiful, like a constellation flowing in and out of focus, partially alive, partially something like a river’s currents.

She has absolutely no idea what any of it means.

And luckily, it’s not all that important! Hasn’t even touched it, actually. There’s no reason to expand outwards yet, especially not through the mundane world. She’s got some vague plans about trying to make a bridge through that vague inner space around her “edges”, where she saw some of those strange lights and distant shapes, some of them fading away, some of them abruptly being snuffed out. They’re only tentative, though.

Frankly, she’s got a hell of a lot going on already, and she’ll either have time to do more or she won’t.

The pyramid remains intact. Contained and functional, in spite of how messy it is, getting refined more and more with every few seconds. New frequencies of screaming, fresh doses and types of Pain, infusions of Flesh and Space to keep warping the interior and diverting more and more Qi into generating more empowered bio-materials. Still not enough to kill the Feng inside it, but as it continues to ramp up its attempts to cast formations and adapt to her methods, she keeps wearing it down, bit by bit, and the process as a whole continues to feed her more of its concepts.

It’s delicious. Genuinely. It’s like… tasting the flavors of an entire forest all at once, tinged with the themes of shadows and magics, aged, empowered… delicious.

And hard to describe.

Then there’s the boulder.

Using what she’s learned in the last two days to refine it properly, it’s gotten even more efficient. Her inner world grows and grows wetter, ideas of water and depth and cold and liquids. More Qi, yes, but she can generate more Qi already. She can brute force it.

This feels different, though. Like it’s… becoming part of the environment.

It also generates more Qi, but differently. Usually her process is to just sort of grind Qi together, the most forceful and constant use of it she can manage with her biology, and it interacting with itself makes more Qi. If someone were using it to cast something, the Qi can be spent to cause an effect, but even in that scenario, a little bit more gets created.

But having forests and waters and other ideas, other states of matter, blending against her… that’s a kind of friction too. Kind of. In a different way? It feels different, but also the same.

She’s generating more Qi around areas she’s infesting with that foreign Qi than before. There are some ways where it fights her, and that kinda makes more but not as fluidly, not as consistently, not as well, so she avoids those. Changes them into different forms, imitating nature in some ways, doing things her own way in others.

There’s a system to it. She’s figuring it out, bit by bit. In fact, if she figures out a way to add more boulders, there’s probably even more combinations and tricks to make even more Qi, that she can eat, that she can be a part of, that can be a part of her.

So she’s been watching the disciples. Looking for one that fucks up.

They’ve mostly been good, though. She’s been keeping track and while there are lots of them that absolutely do suck, lots that she very much dislikes, but none quite as bad as the one that led her to her water-obelisk. None that she can entirely justify putting to sleep and using so directly.

She’s trying, though.

When one of them stabs another in the back, leaving them for dead and taking some of their supplies, she watches. When two start fighting, filling the trial-tunnels with thunder and knitted ash, she watches closely to see what the victor does, and holds back when they end up not killing their opponent outright. Three disciples corner a weaker one, beating them and taking their storage rings- but they stop and leave just before it would lead to a crippling.

It’s hard to find the right criteria because she doesn’t know the right criteria. It doesn’t feel right to just grab one of them and transform them, but it did that one time, and she wants more of them, and some of them get close. She doesn’t really care about them, that’s not it. They’re interesting, and they give her great ideas, acting as lovely inspiration, but she doesn’t really know them as people, and doesn’t want to. They’re just sort of… there.

They’re not important. They’re barely even real.

But it feels weird to just pick them up and turn them inside out. And she’s not sure why.

No safeguards slamming down, no active subversions breaking down her brain when she tries to think things through. Something behind said safeguards might be causing it, some trickle-effect of the existence of her memories even if she doesn’t actually have them, but that’s just a theory.

So! The thresholds.

If they murder someone in cold blood, she can use them. If they assault someone, she can use them. If they torture someone, they’re free game. Anything less and she starts to feel… off.

Maybe she should push through it, though. Maybe. She has to push through her safeguards, after all, and maybe this is some kind of stranger, subtler blockage she doesn’t remember putting in place.

Two days without finding even one viable target has that possibility looking more appealing, but then someone triggers her awareness to zoom in.

In one of the rest areas, seven disciples face off against each other, though one in particular is a bit further apart than all the rest.

The body language, the smell, the hum of Qi and the Intent to cause harm… it’s the perfect seedbed for a fresh conflict. Considering how few of the disciples smell like they want it, it also indicates some of the unfairness (is that the word? Maybe?) that she’s looking for, tempting her all the more sweetly.

The attention of a world turns inward, highlighting a cavernous chamber filled with flesh-fruits, padded cushioning, a small spring, and the possibility of murder.
