“There must be a mistake,” Kayline said. “He authorized us to—”

“Go,” he ordered. “I need to prepare.”

Kayline’s stomach sank when she saw his unfeeling expression. There was no warmth or comfort. He radiated a chilling aura that broke Mayle’s rage and cast the room into silence. Kernant and the guards reflexively entered a defensive position when they felt it.

“Hayden…” she whispered. “I hope you know—”

“Your father made his decision, and will bear the consequences,” he declared. “Now go.”

Kayline’s heart plummeted. “Hayden…” Her eyes welled with tears as Kernant and the guards dragged them away. Not only was Hayden losing a full line of protection and access to all the catalysts he had invested in, but now he would also become an enemy of their father. Her romantic feelings toward Hayden had long since passed, but her bond with him had become inseparable. The thought of being enemies was too much to bear.

Mayle watched him leave with a pounding heart. She had never seen him furious, but now that she had, she feared for Ritrix and the Golden Belt as a whole.

Karva was also feeling that chill as she watched video footage of the swirling ball of mercury bending the clouds eighty miles west of Junolyx University. It was unlike anything she had ever seen—twice the size of the largest grand rift she had worked with. It was clearly special—special enough to bring someone from the past to enter it.

As a famed treasure hunter, she naturally wanted what was in it—whatever it was—but she was afraid to enter.

Video footage showed Hayden’s shuttle boldly approaching the Golden Belt star fleet. Somehow, Hayden had been bold enough to call intergalactic leaders to a conference and then ditch them when the rift opened. Now, his shuttle was boldly confronting the second-largest military in the universe. That was a level of brazenness that Karva couldn’t even comprehend.

“What’s the situation?” Karva asked as Ana and another man walked in, both wearing grave expressions.

“The conference is in a panic,” Ana said. “Everyone agrees that Hayden entering the rift is a security risk, but Stella is boldly insistent that she is the only one with the authority to stop him—something she’s refusing to do.”

“En route.” Another screen in the control room flipped to show Carian’s team.

“I didn’t give the order.”

Karva threw her coffee cup, shattering it. “Then who did?” she screamed.

“The Morine Domain.”

Karva froze. “What?”

“The Morine Domain ran communication interference to convince Stella’s fleet that Carian’s ship was part of Hayden’s security detail. Then, they ordered us to follow them.”

Everyone paused when the screen showed Golden Belt’s Star Fleet allowing Hayden’s shuttle to pass through. It was surreal to watch it happen, and even more surreal to watch Carian’s ship follow after them.

“And you just complied?” Karva asked. “From a foreign celestial?”

“We didn’t have a choice,” the man said.

“What do you…” She turned to him. “Who are you?”

“I’m Nisan Kara,” he said. “I’m the head diplomat of the Crimson Sickles.”

“They want what Hayden is after—and they want us to bring it to them. If we succeed, they will give us a galactic chain and boundless resources. If we fail, they’ll protect us. But if we don’t comply, they will dismantle us. It’s non-negotiable.”

Karva looked at the screen showing Carian’s ship, then back. “That’s bullshit. Contact Stella’s team and tell them what’s going on. It doesn’t matter if we get dismantled; it doesn’t matter if we face prison—we’re not committing treason. Now, go out there and play whatever games you need to unfuck this situation. Got it?”

“Oh, thank God you’re here,” Carian said. “I’ve been calling you. What should we—”

Carian’s face paled, but she saluted. “Understood.”

Karva cut the call and pointed at Ana and her team members. “Go out there… and do whatever the fuck needs to be done to mitigate this disaster. And for the love of God, figure out why the Morine Domain is breaking universal law in front of the Vast Four!”

Her team saluted and ran out of the room, leaving Karva to process what was happening.

Hayden had unilaterally destroyed the Crimson Sickles’ plans and tanked their security market by making Junolyx untouchable. Karva couldn’t even fathom how a normal person could wield so much power, but she found out the hard way and corrected course. There was no way she’d attack him further, but for some reason, the largest celestial in the universe, an empire that spread over three billion galaxies, was forcing them to get involved.

Karva took deep breaths and pulled out a small bottle of Xanax. She didn’t like medicine; never took the stuff. But if there was any time to curb her temper and keep calm, it was right then. So she opened the bottle and popped a pill—nearly taking a second—before closing it and watching Hayden’s ship fly toward the rift. Throughout it all, she wondered what in the hell drove Halkan Morine to do something so aggressive.
