After a little more back and forth, it became clear that Dot didn't have the first clue as to what was going on in her own guild. Like before, she'd been sheltered, protected, and used for healing pretty much from the moment she woke up until she collapsed into bed. There was no end to the patients. Chicago was far better off than New York in regard to monster spawns and, as far as she knew, portals were still opening up, which meant no system event for them. Good thing, that, since what Earth needed now was not more demons.

Luke believed her when she said she didn't know anything about ISG's betrayal. Knowing what he did about her, he'd never suspected she might, but the pissed-off response he got when asking confirmed it. Also, Dot detested Luom.

The topic of the Singers and their invasion lingered at the back of his mind as the trio made their way through endless hospital corridors, killing not-nurses and not-Relian-doctors. The first time they came up against one of the Relians again, Luke couldn't help but feel a tinge of fear, but that subsided in a matter of seconds when it turned out they held nothing of The Fallen Shepherd other than his appearance. Instead, they served as a constant reminder of Luke's failure to kill the man. What was the real Relian doing at that very moment? Had he already escaped through the Singer portal? He was on their list, after all.

"We have to get out of here," he muttered, not for the first time.

Dot sped up, so she walked right next to Luke, overtaking Luom. "Agreed, but how?" Her eyes widened at the approach of another couple of not-nurses. These were pushing a hospital bed with a pregnant woman in it.

"Don't know," Luke said, holding up a hand. "It's not a real woman."

"How can you tell?"

"Weaver's Perception. She has a monster core. Nothing more than a distraction."

"Weaver's what?" Luom asked, eyes gleaming with interest.

Shit. So much for not sharing information with the enemy. "Nothing," he said, stepping to the side to allow the hospital bed to pass them.

Dot's gaze followed the pregnant woman as she disappeared, but Luke urged her on. The plan, such as it was, consisted of heading down each time they found a set of stairs. They kept close to each other, hoping it would keep the dungeon from separating them. So far, it had worked.

They'd descended at least ten floors without any noticeable difference in their surroundings, and Luke's annoyance was reaching new heights when something, at last, changed. Another Lifeweaver made their appearance.

Integrated Rem. Lifeweaver. Level 2.

This one wasn't a Singer, nor a human. It walked on four legs, was covered in long, pristine, grayish fur, and its head looked like that of a ferret. Rem the ferret Lifeweaver spotted them from a distance, across the bustle of not-nurses and fake patients in the corridor.

Luke raised a hand in greeting, but the moment he moved, Rem twitched and fled.

"What was that?" Dot asked.

"A Lifeweaver," Luom said.

Dot glared at the Singer. "I got that much."

"So if it isn't just humans and Singers here, does that mean we have to make friends with every species?" Dot added.

"Or eliminate them," Luke said, frustration growing again. This was taking far too much time. Hurrying his steps, he found another staircase. Wavering on whether to follow Rem or go down another floor, Luke decided to stick with the original plan. The likelihood of catching up to that skittish creature wasn't great.

Descending, he wondered about the point of this dungeon. As far as he'd gathered, each dungeon had some sort of reason for existing, like a job that needed doing or an item Earth's Integrated needed. That should go double for pink dungeons, or destiny dungeons, as Luom called them, but the quest wasn't a whole lot to go by. Was he supposed to ally himself with all other Lifeweavers in existence? That couldn't be it. If nothing else, there had to be uncountable systems out there, no? If you narrowed it down to new systems, like Earth, that might be more plausible.

"Hey Luom, how long ago did you say your system's integration happened?"

"I'm not sure I shared that with you, but I don't see the harm. The Singers integrated thirteen months ago, to the day."

"You've had more time than us," Dot said.

Luke nodded to himself as they emerged into another identical corridor. "The Lifeweavers in here are probably from somewhat recent integrations. You spoke of a 'joining' earlier. That's where our system opens up to other ones?"

Luom stopped and bit her lip, considering her answer.

"You want us to come with you or not?" he urged. Luke had no intention of fulfilling his part of Relian's deal with the Singers, but Luom didn't know that.

"Yes, of course," the Singer said.

Dot crossed her arms over her chest. "Then spill the beans?"

"What are beans, and why would I spill them?"

"Just answer my question," Luke said.

"Yes, fine. You are correct in your assumption, as far as I know."

"Your Custodian told you?"

She nodded. "That's right."

"Custodian seems a lot more helpful than Guide," Luke grumbled as he located yet another set of stairs. These only led up, so he ignored them.

"I don't know about that."

Luke turned to look at Luom. "What do you mean?"

"I've heard you have something called a boon. Those sound powerful."

"You didn't get boons?" Dot asked her.

"We did not."

That was an interesting point, and Luke pocketed the information for the future. Right now, they knew little about those beings, what they were capable of, or what sort of restrictions they operated under, but he knew that would change at some point.

Dot pointed down the corridor. "Over there!"

"What?" Luke asked.

"That furry thing again, but it ran away."

"At Level 2, I'm surprised the bizarro nurses haven't killed it."

"Too fast, maybe?" Luom suggested.

This time, they followed. Luke restored his Meta-Heart to full capacity, pumping it full of the healing mana he'd spent surviving the fight against Trypten and not-Relian's minions. He glanced over at Luom, who shot back a smile his way. After what Luke had done to her friend, it didn't make sense for the Singer to be so pleasant and cheerful. Luke didn't trust it. Not one bit.

Turning a corner, he stopped dead in his tracks. The Level 2 Lifeweaver who had fled from them twice now had run right into someone. That someone made sure little Rem wouldn't run ever again. All that remained was a blood-covered furry mess lying unmoving in a pool of more blood. Over Rem stood the one responsible, a new figure.

Luke wasn't sure how he'd explain what it looked like. The best he could come up with was the cursed love child between a dolphin and a human. In shape, it was somewhat humanoid, except with four arms and a head that sat right on its shoulders. The upper body was short, but the legs more than made up for it. It was naked, or at least Luke thought it was, with the glistening skin of a sea creature. Even the toes and fingers were webbed, giving a clue to its aquatic nature.

Integrated Wilska. Lifeweaver. Level 31.

"You killed it," Luke said.

Wilska let out a shriek, its lower jaw chattering, and charged forward. Before it had taken two steps, something tripped it. Not something. Threads of mana. Its bulk falling to the floor, a human Integrated stepped forward and on top of its back.

Integrated Yuze. Lifeweaver. Level 27.

He was a short guy with buzzed hair, hard eyes, and a glare that told Luke this one didn't mess around. The dark-green military fatigues added to the presentation.

Less than a second later, a Meta-Heart appeared in Yuze's hand before it disappeared into his inventory.

"You're from Earth, right?" Luke asked. Best to make sure, he figured.

Rather than answer, Yuze looked up, set his shoulders, and charged with one hand held out in front of him, a thread of mana extending from it. "Chong a!"

"Come on!" Luke shouted at him in exasperation, even while bringing out his staff.

When Yuze got close enough, Luke extended several threads through the staff as he swung it, matching the blow to his opponent's incredible speed. The idea was to take advantage of the weapon's superior reach. That didn't work, not at all. Yuze kept going straight forward, but at the last moment, the bastard weavestepped past the staff and ducked under Luke's arm, heading straight for Dot.

The Healer's Moment was off cooldown, so Luke used it right away. This was not the time to mess around, not when this mad newcomer threatened someone whom he still considered a friend.

Speed and momentum had carried Yuze past the reach of Luke's threads, forcing him to use Explosive Reach. With that skill active, the threads made their way into Yuze's mana channels. Judging by their width, this guy walked around with a lot of mana, but not at that moment in time. His Meta-Heart wasn't even half-full.

Time was running out, and Luke had to make a decision. Either he disabled Yuze to later attempt some sort of peaceful understanding, or Luke killed the guy. Thinking fast, he weighed the options and decided that he couldn't take the chance, not with an opponent who possessed all the tools of the Lifeweaver trade. A single Healer's Moment from the guy could be enough to kill both Dot and Luom. Having decided, Luke made his move.

Killing a Lifeweaver is a challenge. As long as the Meta-Heart pulsed away, providing mana, you could come back from almost anything, a point Luke had proven again and again. Thus, the Yuze's Meta-Heart had to go. When extracting one that insisted on healing itself, overwhelming force was the only way to get the job done. Using eight threads, half with Needle of Life affixed in the shape of various tools used for cutting and piercing, and half for pulling with all the might he could muster, Luke thrashed and heaved for all he was worth.

Yuze made weak attempts at pushing The Healer's Moment back using his will, which was impressive on its own, but it didn't help him. With Luke's own will set and his focus on the task at hand, it was like a mosquito trying to ram a rock wall.

Time resumed. Luke stood there, Yuze's Meta-Heart in hand, with two pairs of eyes staring at him in shock and horror.

"What have you done!?" Dot shrieked, throwing herself at him.
