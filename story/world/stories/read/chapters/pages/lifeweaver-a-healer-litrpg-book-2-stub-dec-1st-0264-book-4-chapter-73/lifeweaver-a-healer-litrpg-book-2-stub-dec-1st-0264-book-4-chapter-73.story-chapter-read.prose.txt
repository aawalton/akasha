Earth's weave was in a sorry state. Falzyxi did not come alone, and many of the smaller, less advanced void creatures had thrived. They fed on the end seam of the weave and had broken through in many places. As if that wasn't enough, the higher void being's mere presence turned the weave brittle, allowing others to flow in and attack with crazed abandon. Earth's forces, a larger number of Integrated than Luke would've expected, were putting up a fight, but they were hopelessly outmatched in both strength and experience. Things were dire. Dire enough that Luke found himself jumping in, ignoring the burning pain of void mana having taken hold of a good third of his meta-heart.

Luke: "Dimitrios! Where the hell are you?"

Luke: "Popov! What's going on?"

Not waiting for an answer, Luke moved through the weave to the nearest Integrated even while he extended threads to two more. She was on the brink of death, with what looked like a big chunk of her belly ripped away by a thousand tiny mouths. Restoring her with Weaver's Renewal by taking some of her own mana, Luke braced himself. The other two he had reached out to were hurt but not dying from their wounds, but they were about to receive fresh wounds. Now, Luke took those on instead, even as he pushed void creatures back with sheer force of will.

Back off.

There was no time to heal himself. Stored healing mana would need to deal with that task while he repaired the weave as best he could, pushing void creatures out and killing those who dared come too close. They were used to dealing with Integrated who couldn't put up much of a fight. It was a good thing for Luke, since that meant a clean cut through their Source channels was often enough to put them down.

Dimitrios: "I am injured. Perhaps you could lend aid?"

Dimitrios: "Popov has been silent since you left. Haven't had the time to check on him. His students are doing the best they can in one place, but there are also many others."

The message made Luke expand his awareness of the weave, and he found exactly what he was afraid of. There were many other spots where Earth's weave was being overrun. This appeared less and less like an attack by Falzyxi alone, and more like a coordinated effort by the void to destroy Earth. They'd turned the action up to eleven, and the Integrated weren't prepared for it, even if they were putting up a valiant effort.

Luke helping would alleviate much of the pressure, but he was needed elsewhere. Earth needed a heavy hitter, though, so the best course of action was getting the ones who should be available back into play. Weavestep brought him to Dimitrios, who had returned to the physical realm due to injuries. Injuries quite a bit more severe than expected.

"What happened to your arms?" Luke asked, grimacing at the intense ache in his chest as the void stirred and more of the Source within shone through. Dimitrios was a mess. One arm was gone at the shoulder and the other at the elbow, with gaping wounds in both places. Teeth marks stood out red and angry on his face and on the side of his head, where an ear was gone.

To answer that question, Dimitrios vomited blood. Great. Using Weaver's Renewal again in such quick succession would require more mana than Luke had at his disposal, but nothing else would work without leaving the patient a cripple for the rest of his life, and that was unacceptable.

"You're coming with me," Luke said, reaching down to grab Dimitrios and stabilize his condition with a thought and a thread. Another Weavestep brought the two to Popov, who was bent over a table full of rolled-out parchments stained with coffee and thick tomes full of text and symbols that didn't make much sense.

"I'm preparing!" Popov yelled without turning to look. "Leave me alone!"

Luke reached forward with a thread of mana and entered Popov's mana channels. The response was immediate. The Grandmaster pushed back with his will, threatening to destroy the thread, but Luke didn't have time for it. Shoving Popov's will aside, as if physically tackling a child, he took what he needed, and there was plenty to draw on.

Dimitrios got to his new hands and feet, coughing, after a Weaver's Renewal that drew almost half of Popov's mana.

"You're needed out there," Luke said, a little out of breath. "Both of you."

Popov emptied an impressive-looking, ornate mana potion the size of a tennis ball and shook his head in exasperation. "I'm working on it! You can't just come in here and help yourself to my mana!"

"Dimitrios would be dead now if I hadn't."

"Thank you," Dimitrios said, having gathered himself enough to stand.

"Things are looking bad out there," Luke said, as if that wasn't obvious to everyone. The weave itself was shuddering under the strain, and would collapse if they didn't get everything under control soon. "I don't know where all those Integrated out there are coming from, but they aren't enough."

Popov slammed his open palm on the table in front of him, then crushed a piece of paper in his hand. "Falzyxi may be disabled for the moment, but that creature is very much alive! Your plan failed! Just like I predicted!" He drew in a deep breath, and when he spoke again, he had restrained the worst of his anger and replaced it with a sad weariness. "We have paid a heavy price, but now that you are back, we can restore order. We could have used Penny and Hannah, but your actions have led to their demise. A loss that we will feel in the battle to come."

"I need to go back to the dungeon," Luke said. "Falzyxi kicked me out, but we can still beat it in there."

Popov turned, his face ashen. "You're joking? After seeing what we're dealing with out there? You're going back?"

"This sounds like it's going to go on for a while. Better I join the others and fight. Bye-bye, now," Dimitrios said and disappeared.

"I'm not leaving my friends in there to die, and it's still our only shot at killing Falzyxi."

"You let me deal with Falzyxi," Popov said, narrowing his eyes. "In the being's disabled state, I have ways of pushing it back into the void."

"No. No way. That's not enough. If we don't kill it now, it'll just return!"

Popov grunted. "That's the best we can hope for in this situation. Next time, we will have been able to prepare."

Luke took a step forward and grabbed the front of the wizard-looking, pain-in-the-ass-being Russian's robe. "If you drive it away, that will sever the connection with the dungeon." Luke leaned in a little closer. "I'm pretty sure that would kill everyone in the dungeon, no matter what. Don't you fucking dare."

Popov brought a palm to Luke's chest and gave a gentle shove. "I will do what my conscience demands of me. The deaths of your friends will be a light burden to bear compared to the lives of everyone else on Earth."

"You. Don't. Touch. Falzyxi."

"Again, I'll do what I think is right." There was steel in his demeanor, as well as in his voice, but it softened as he continued. "But I'll hold off until there is no other choice."

Luke nodded and reached out to the weave. Time was running out in more ways than one now, so there was no point wasting more of it. He had received a bunch of messages from Milla about the guild and their progress setting up in New Belgium, but that ranked low on the list of things needing his attention at that moment. There were a bunch of other people reaching out to him as well, most for healing, but that would have to wait as well. There was healing to be done, yes, but he needed to focus on the ones fighting in the weave, the ones standing between humanity and the void.

As he reached another field of battle, he saw Dimitrios having that particular plate well in hand, so he continued on to another. Here, Smudge poked his head up and demanded release. Unsure what to expect, Luke let his companion out while he healed a group of Integrated who had all fallen back to regroup after facing a void creature a step or two above the lowliest bastards. Before moving on, he helped end those particular void creatures as well.

With battles going on all over the place, there was just no way for Luke to help everyone, and he was needed elsewhere. People would die, but once he had healed threads in the places that threatened to unravel the weave itself, he returned his attention to Falzyxi and the dungeon. He couldn't save everyone, not this time.

Finding the stream of Source was not difficult once Luke returned to the unmoving husk of Falzyxi. It was like a blazing stream of blue-glowing lava surging through see-through tubing at an incredible but constant rate. At that point, it had eaten away a good chunk of Falzyxi's internal resources, but there was still a lot remaining. If they failed in the dungeon, the void being would get it all back, with everything it had gathered added to it. They couldn't allow that to happen.

The closer Luke got to the Source, the more the void in his meta-heart stirred. It was getting excited, but also apprehensive, like a kid in line for a big water slide. There was this instinctive knowledge in the void matter telling it how coming into contact with that much Source would obliterate it. The Source enclosed by the void in his meta-heart was like a drop in comparison. Sharing in that apprehension, Luke reached out to the Source, knowing that one misstep, even a brief lapse of concentration, would be enough for him to cease to exist in the coming moments. But there was no choice, no choice at all. Was there?

Luke extended a thread of mana, giving every ounce of his willpower and focus to the sole task of keeping himself shielded from the Source as he attempted to rejoin his friends and enter the dungeon for the second, and hopefully final, time.
