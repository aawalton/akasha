Chapter 103 – Training the System

The next two days passed quickly. Fang Zhao was training like a maniac. Li Yao and Huang Niuniu would spar with him, with Yu Han joining in sometimes. The three of them combined couldn’t take him down.

Though when Yu Han maxed out his instincts with Thousand Petals Awareness, he came close.

Can I call it Ultra Instinct? Nope. No, I won’t steal names. I could totally come up with a better one.

He could force a draw with Li Yao. Huang Niuniu refused to spar with him.

How about, Synaptic Bloom? It matches the theme of the original name.

It wouldn’t show up on his dao record. But his gains to the psychic art had gone through the roof recently. For now, he could max out his sense of touch better. He wanted to find a way to up his reaction without upping the pain. With sight and sound, he could only last a minute or two while combined with touch.

The end goal?

Max out all his senses. Then see if he could affect other senses not listed in the arts manual, like balance and heat.

They used blunted training weapons. Fang Zhao used none. With bare-handed martial arts, he could deflect swords, spears, and whips alike.

He had shown interest in collecting other Martial arts too. Already, he could link the seven he knew together adequately.

Let’s see if I can somehow make copies of the other martial arts I saw in the rookie village library. He didn’t remember the exact number, but there were around forty, right? He was curious if Fang Zhao could master so many martial arts. Yu Han didn’t know imperial script to a workable level yet, but if it was just transcribing characters, he could do it.

Yesterday, they picked up their third share of pure qi assimilating elixir. The hidden realm excursion lasted four days, and with the three days of settling in after that, Yu Han was finally ready for his night soil collection duties.

The donkey cart rocked left and right. Huang Niuniu bit her lips as she wrote numbers on a Yu Han brand new notebook. She was plotting their future expenses, like a matriarch in charge of the household finances.

But rather than having a husband and kids, she had three idiots, one crab, and an old cat.

“Our coveralls actually cost more than what we have,” she said. Her voice a bit down. “We’re not that rich. Is smuggling not good business?”

One Coverall set cost 5000 elite-grade spirit stones. That was equal to 500,000 mortal-grade ones.

“We don’t own the coveralls,” Yu Han said. “If we leave the Night Alchemists’ Yard, we’ll have to return them.”

“What if I ask Senior Brother Wen really nicely?” Huang Niuniu made a face. “Like this.”

“He has a fiancé.”

“I can flutter my eyelashes!” Huang Niuniu sulked. “Mother taught me how.”

“Tan Ruoxuan can do that too,” Yu Han said. “And she can put you in jail.”

“Good answer. Definitely what I wanted to hear.” Huang Niuniu lightly poked the butt of the donkey with a stick. It made a snorting sound and picked up its pace.

“Tan Ruoxuan said they used up most of their money on a landed property in Great Barrier City,” Yu Han said.

“That’s should’ve been ours! I was almost kidnapped.” Huang Niuniu was indignant.

According to Fang Zhao, there were artefacts that collected this extra pure qi. The pure qi would lose its mysterious properties, but could still be used as neutral spiritual energy, like in spirit stones.

A bump on the road shook Yu Han’s vision.

How did the Romans make roads again?

The Johan part of Yu Han didn’t want to openly share knowledge. However, the basis of a courtyard, if he decided to make one, had to be knowledge.

In The Dao of Characters (Review by Liang Fuyu), the author claimed that one of the most important foundations of the art of formations, for a cultivator, was a strong mind origin. Other foundations were talent, clan, and teacher. They would also need to acquire a trait called Inner Awareness. The earlier the better.

One way to determine if one’s mind was up to par was changing the dao record to display information that better suited the cultivator’s needs.

This could be accomplished even when one was below Level 21, their essence still not shown in the dao records. Same went for Inner Awareness, which a cultivator would naturally acquire during the breakthrough to foundation building.

Yu Han’s mind origin was his highest stat. He’d been trying to change how his dao records showed information.

Mentally, he grabbed hold of the ‘feeling’ or ‘sensation’ he got when he had to concentrate on parts of the dao record to show additional data. Such as with his arts panel.

As always, Thousand Petals Awareness rose the fastest. With the Synaptic Bloom practice, it shot through the roof with no signs of slowing down.

Li Yao, Fang Zhao, and Huang Niuniu had better base fighting potential than him. But with Synaptic Bloom, he too had his trump card.

Deep Sleep with Echoing Dreamscape was best for training mental skills. Cultivation arts, psychic arts, and perhaps spiritual arts would also benefit from this.

His current focus was training the various senses with Thousand Petals Awareness while cultivating. If he could grasp the feeling of whatever it was that flowed in his body during cultivation, would he get the Inner Awareness trait?

Ox Tail 72 Sweeping Forms rose quite nicely too. He was close to levelling up.

Would it suffice though? It was, after all, an art for tempering the body. Not really offence focused.

In the hidden realm, his performance in fights was the worst unless he used Synaptic Bloom. Fang Zhao was a genius, and Li Yao was skilled. Huang Niuniu was a bit silly, but her Lotus Razor and Pale Spine were no jokes. Not to mention her quick thinking when the Redgrass Beak Viper boss had appeared.

Yu Han, with his halberd, could only act as a basic minion after Synaptic Bloom ran out. It was as clear as day when he defended Fang Zhao.

One trump card is not enough.

Even if Ox Tail 72 Sweeping Forms was meant to train the body rather than to kill, he had no doubt that Fang Zhao could use it better, even with one arm tied behind his back.

Yu Han didn’t want to invest much time in it. His weight loss journey had been stable. Right now, he was around 137 kilograms. Still nowhere near his target, which was below 100.

I definitely gained some muscle, though.

He didn’t really have an aesthetic preference. Not Yu Han, at least. Johan would prefer visible abs over useable ones.

Still, too much fat.

He would continue his daily grinding of the Ox Tail 72 Sweeping Forms, combined with endurance and weight training. But I need a better offence.

Let’s see if I can find a more offensive martial art with halberds. If not, I can change weapons. What would suit Synaptic Bloom the most? If I don’t use it as a burst, I could fight longer.

Formations. Arrays. Runes. Glyphs. Diagrams. Talismans.

Those could be a way forward too.
