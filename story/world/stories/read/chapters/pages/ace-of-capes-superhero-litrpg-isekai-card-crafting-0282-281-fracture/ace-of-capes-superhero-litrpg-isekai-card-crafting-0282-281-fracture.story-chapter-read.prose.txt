“Okay.” Lexie had just watched her mother die while being in the mind of the sociopath who'd murdered her, so she wasn’t the best she’d ever been. “If you’re hungry, we have some leftover pot roast at home. It’s really good. Oh, and some brownies as well.”

“I don't think you understand. The cookies are really, really good,” Lexie emphasized, and Irina gave her a weak smile.

“I’ll take your word for it. I’m vegan.”

“Oh.”

“Yes.”

Her father was typically more hospitable than this, but he wasn’t in the mood, so it seemed like that task would have to fall to Lexie for now.

"Indigo used to be a part of District 8. District 9 stole it. "

The three Developers worked hand in hand with necromancers and blended their arts in order to create the system that we have now. It was the pride of the entire world, a purely human invention. As much as the association likes to pretend it’s impartial and apolitical, the truth is that the Association is based in District 9, which was the District that was attacked. Necromancy research struggled but ultimately became more advanced as a result of the restrictions placed on us.
