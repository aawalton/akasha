Just in case, she chose to pick a few samples and use her spatial control to move these few structures to the asteroid belt. From what she saw, of the nine Solar Systems, seven of them had planets with advanced intelligent life. The others also had worlds, but the life there was wild and non-intelligent.

“Because it is the edge of the galaxy, it seems that nearby powers had not felt the need to colonize these Solar Systems… Or they did, but they just didn’t have either time nor resources to colonize them?”

From what Liu Shu knew, of these nine Solar Systems, three of them were of the Covenant, another two of the Nexus, and the final four were of weak, unknown powers.

‘Well… I could intimidate non-True World Trees in my early 100s, now that I am at Level 220, and with Experience to spare, I am sure a single glance might suppress them.’
