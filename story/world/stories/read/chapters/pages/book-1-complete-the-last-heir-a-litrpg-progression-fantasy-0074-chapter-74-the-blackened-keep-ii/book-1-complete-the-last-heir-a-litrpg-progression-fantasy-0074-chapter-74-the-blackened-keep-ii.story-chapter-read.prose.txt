All three of Rowan’s whips sharpened, the edges flattening into makeshift blades.

The humanoid demon crouched down, bursting forward like a predator finally pouncing on its prey—but its two companions weren’t to be outdone. The spider slithered across the ceiling, its legs finding purchase in the cracked stone while behind them the raging hound’s snarl reverberated off the walls.

Rowan braced himself, transferring his shield to the front and infusing it with more mana. The flames grew, covering his entire right flank. He felt the thrill start to pump through his veins, and with barely a thought, Rowan lashed out with his spells.

Two of his makeshift blades slashed an X towards the approaching creature while he thrust with the third.

In response, the Demon turtled up, a hard, chitinous shell forming on its forearms and moving up to its shoulders.

This was what made Changelings so dangerous. With their assortment of stolen skills, they could adapt to almost any situation. But their powers came with obvious drawbacks.

Having more tools for the job wasn’t always the best course of action. Sometimes, having just one was enough.

Rowan’s lashing blades impacted the Demon’s arms, cracking the shell and forcing it to slow. With a grunt that was equal parts anger and effort, the creature shrugged off his spells, taking a nasty burn across its side in response.

His third whip sharpened to a point, but the Demon managed to shift its torso to the side, turning what should have been a killing blow into one slightly less so.

The sharpened whip penetrated through its upper arm, burning through muscle and bone alike.

An agonized scream tore out of its throat, raw and guttural, echoing through the chamber like a wounded beast.

Rowan went to bring his other two blades around but he was forced to redirect them towards the ceiling. Up above, the spider quickly crawled towards him, a viscous fluid dripping from its exposed fangs. He wasn’t exactly sure what kind of venom it was, but Rowan had a feeling that coming in contact with it wouldn’t be a pleasant experience.

The humanoid Demon tried to buck off Rowan’s third whip, hissing in pain as the flames burned brighter. When that didn’t work, the shell covering its forearms grew, covering its palms. It grabbed hold of his spell, glaring at him with a murderous expression, pulling with all its might.

Rowan didn’t have time for a tug of war, but he still managed to curl his spell around to hook the creature's arm. Its pained howl grew in pitch as flames licked at its exposed skin, charring it black and filling the chamber with the acrid smell of burnt flesh.

Rowan brought his other two blades up and thrust at the slithering spider. It dodged with alarming ease, dodging to the side to avoid his deadly spells.

The humanoid Demon, realizing it wouldn’t be able to get rid of Rowan’s whip that easily, came to a decision. A wicked-looking blade grew across its other arm. With a rage-filled glare, it brought it down onto its mangled limb, cutting through burnt muscle and charred bone alike.

Rowan didn’t even have time to gawk as the creature freed itself from his grasp, throwing itself forward with wild abandon as its severed arm fell to the ground.

On instinct—or something more—he tugged at the Wind mana filling his Core. It answered like a storm finally breaching through a barricade, flowing through his channels so quickly that Rowan wasn’t sure if it even completed the circuit for [Gust]. But even still, the spell manifested.

Rowan managed to raise his hand just as the Demon got in range, already swinging its bladed limb towards his throat.

The sight made his heartbeat quicken, but there was no fear clouding his mind, only a firm, unshakable resolve.

A vortex of highly condensed Wind erupted from his palm. It impacted the charging monster straight in the chest, throwing it back with the force of a thunderous gale, straight into the waiting hound.

But he didn’t start celebrating yet.

The spider-like Demon hissed, spewing a viscous green liquid from its fangs as it dodged Rowan’s whirling blades. He brought his shield forward, trusting in the spell to take care of whatever his opponents threw at him.

The creature's venom landed on Rowan’s shield, sizzling as it tried to eat through his Intent. A few weeks ago, it might have even managed it, but as he was now, Rowan dealt with the Lesser Demon’s attack with relative ease.

The glow intensified as he pushed more mana into it, burning away the venom, forcing the spider to engage from up close if it wanted a chance of breaking through.

At the sight of its attack being so easily overcome, something almost akin to surprise rippled through the creature’s body. Its spindly legs faltered for just a moment.

Rowan capitalized on the opening, sending all three of his whips lashing toward the Demon.

Two slashed from the sides while the third thrust forward like a spear, repeating his earlier maneuver. Unlike the humanoid Demon, this one didn’t try to meet his spells head-on—it had already seen what they could do. Instead, it opted to retreat.

Shadows pooled around its legs, racing up its body like living ink.

[Shadow Step]. Rowan thought. Didn’t think I’d ever see a spider do that.

His whips wouldn’t reach in time, so his Intent shifted.

The large hound snarled as Rowan’s [Gust] swept through the corridor, pressing the wounded humanoid tighter into its fur. Rowan barely spared them a glance, adjusting the stream of Wind with a flicker of control. The spell curved upward, cutting through the air with far greater speed than his whips could manage.

The spider tensed as it noticed the approaching spell, the shadows surging faster up its limbs. Rowan sharpened his focus, forcing the wind to accelerate—

But he didn’t need to force it.

He asked, and it listened.

The [Gust] splattered the Demon against the ceiling, breaking its skill and holding it in place. Even the boost from [Storm Touched] wasn’t enough to make it a deadly spell against a monster of this level, but Rowan didn’t need it to be. He had something else for that.

All three of his whips sharpened to a point, impaling the Demon through its hardened carapace. The sound it let out was somewhere between a whimper and a hiss, but as Rowan’s whip pierced through its skull, all noise died out.

He withdrew his spells, and eight spindly legs loosened from the ceiling. The creature's body fell to the floor with a wet thud. Hissing blood pooled beneath it, a combination of its venom and the residual heat from Rowan’s attack.

He grinned.

That’s one down.

Rowan dropped [Gust], once again shrinking his shield and turning three whips towards his two remaining opponents.

“...what?” he muttered, eyes widening at the sight.

The humanoid Demon was tearing into the hound's back leg with wild abandon, biting and clawing as the other monster just took it, gulping down its flesh.

Rowan realized what was happening a moment later.

The bastard’s healing.

Its severed arm slowly regrew, sinew stretching like hot wax, reforming itself piece by piece. First came the bone, jagged at the edges before it smoothed over, knitting itself together with sickening cracks. Muscle coiled around it in writhing strands, twitching as it reconnected.

Then came the flesh—pale and cracked, reforming in uneven patches until, finally, its fingers flexed, curling into a fresh set of claws.

The humanoid Demon shuddered, eyes intent and a vicious expression on its face.

Meanwhile, the massive hound barely reacted, standing rigid as its own wounds began to close. The jagged tears that marred its thick hide moments ago knitted together in rapid bursts. Blood, dark and sluggish, still dripped down its leg, but the gouges faded.

Should've guessed they had some kind of regeneration skill. His fingers twitched. I just didn’t expect it to look so revolting.

The humanoid Demon lifted its head, its newly reformed arm stretching as if testing its strength.

Can’t have that.

Without hesitation, Rowan cast [Firebolt].

From one heartbeat to the next, the spell flared to life, his mana flowing like a stream downriver and his Intent honed to a fine edge. Rowan didn’t overcharge the spell. In fact, he did the opposite. There was no reason to waste mana.

He flicked his wrist, sending the spell flying.

The humanoid snarled out something in a tongue that grated on Rowan’s ears. Va’khir, the language of the Damned Plains was grating and unnatural heard aloud, and he understood why humans couldn’t speak it.

Their anatomy was just unsuited to the task.

Rowan felt the hairs on the back of his neck stand on end, but [Iron Will] kept him focused.

His [Firebolt] whistled through the air, headed straight towards the two demons barring his path forward.

With surprising speed, the hound moved, positioning itself between the humanoid and the rapidly approaching spell. Its thick hide grew even thicker, its muscled legs bracing against the cracked stone.

The explosion that followed was muted compared to what Rowan was truly capable of, but it was still enough to force the Demon back, burning off its fur and charring its skin.

A surprisingly normal yelp left the hound's throat as Rowan’s spell detonated, the sound echoing off the walls.

Deciding to capitalize on the opportunity, he sharpened his focus, drawing more mana through his channels and casting [Tailwind].

The spider was dead, the hound distracted, and the humanoid was behind cover—meaning that for at least the next few seconds, Rowan could attack without fear of reprisal.

A subtle pressure built at his back, wrapping around him like a warm embrace. Rowan crouched low, firmed his resolve, and sprang forward.

He blurred across the chamber, moving faster than a mage his level had any right to, his abnormally high Dexterity only adding to his speed. The Wind surged with him, matching his movements as he propelled himself forward without resistance. His strides lengthened, and for a moment, Rowan felt like he was gliding—weightless.

Then he got in range.

His whips lashed through the air like coiling serpents, their edges sharpened to a fine point as he launched them towards the disoriented Demon.

With the speed he was going, stopping wasn’t an option—not unless Rowan wanted to lose his footing. So instead, he moved left, flanking his opponents.

The hound didn’t have time to react before Rowan’s whips carved into its flesh. The first lash struck its hind leg, a burning arc that seared through muscle and sinew, severing it to the bone.

The beast howled, its balance crumbling as it staggered to the side.

Rowan didn’t let up.

His second whip came down like a branding iron, raking across its side and flaying the thick hide clean off in a smoking, ragged strip. Charred flesh split apart beneath the head, and the scent of scorched fur filled the chamber.

The hound roared in fury and pain, turning to snap at him, but Rowan was faster.

His third whip lashed across its snout, splitting it open down the jawbone. Teeth shattered, molten embers catching in the open wound as flames chewed through its face.

The Demon reeled back, but Rowan pressed forward, his whips a storm of fire and fury. They lashed, raked, and tore, peeling away thick muscle, stripping flesh from bone, reducing the beast to little more than a smoldering, twitching husk.

With a final downward strike, he split its spine, the force of the blow crumpling its already shattered body. The hound gave one last gurgling snarl before the light left its eyes, its body steaming against the cold stone floor.

Rowan exhaled, flames flickering in his wake.

With its barricade gone, the last Demon caught his gaze, just the slightest hint of fear visible in its strange, multicolored eyes.

Unable to hold back, Rowan grinned.

Two down, one more to go.

With the Wind still at his back, he threw himself forward, ready to end this fight and see what else this Rift had in store for him.
