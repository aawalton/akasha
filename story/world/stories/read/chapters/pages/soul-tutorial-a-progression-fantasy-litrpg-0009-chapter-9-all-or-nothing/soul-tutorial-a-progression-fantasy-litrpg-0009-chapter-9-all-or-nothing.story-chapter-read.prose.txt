Luke stood a few steps away from the silver slime, glaring at it eagerly. All he desired was to kill it quickly, contemplating how he could do it. Maybe he could try using soul energy again, but he didn’t want to risk death ever again, at least not for now.

Taking his time to walk around the slime, Luke finally decided to just do as much damage as he could in an instant. He had no other plan floating around in his head. The dent he had made before had been the only progress he’d made so far.

Luke planted his feet and quickly advanced, using the momentum from rushing in to empower his right leg as he went for the spot he’d dented before. A metallic clang rang out, but he had to keep trying. Maybe if he hadn’t put points into constitution, Luke might have really broken his shin.

He questioned whether he should hit using his shin or his foot. But that wasn’t the point right now; the dent certainly had enlarged a little. He didn’t even know if he should call it a slime, with how strong that defense was. It was a blob, but that appearance couldn’t fool him. That thing felt like a block made of steel.

However, the slime was considerably rounder than the rest of the blobs he fought, though Luke didn’t really think that mattered much. But the silver slime wasn’t just gonna stand there like a dummy the whole time. Luke stared in confusion, his jaw dropped as the slime leapt into the air and landed exactly where it had been.

He was unsure of its weight; all he was certain of was that if that slime had landed on him, he would have instantly died, flattened out like a pancake. Luke felt the entire island tremble and sway when the slime had landed. He had lost all balance, tumbling toward the ground. He tried to get up, but he still felt the tremors reverberating out from the epicenter. Luke still couldn’t find the balance until all the residual energy of that jump had been fully exhausted.

But if that’s all the slime had, then it was just a sitting duck.

All I have to do is wait it out and slowly kill it. He thought while giggling.

Maybe this will be the easiest point farm of my life, but maybe Luke shouldn’t have thought it would be so easy. Luke took his focus off the metallic slime, trying to clear the dirt off himself.

The silver slime inched away from its spot, getting closer to Luke, sending smaller tremors throughout the island; lifting his head up, he stopped himself from exploding out in laughter, exhaling.

Was it even supposed to harm me, with how ridiculously slow it is?

Luke watched in bewilderment, smiling until it picked up speed, hurling directly towards him, quickly catching him by surprise. Covering that short distance in a matter of a few seconds. Luke tried to dive out of the way, but, like always, he had to get hurt in every fight.

It had nicked him on the right shin, and that’s all he saw before the slime continued in a straight direction. A wave of immense pain jolted through his body, originating from his now crushed right shin.

He didn’t even know why he had underestimated the slime to begin with. Every new slime was always a challenge to him. His new constitution stat increase could do nothing against the weight of the steel behemoth of a slime.

Luke watched in horror as the slime turned back around, rolling its way towards him. All he knew was that he didn’t want to be crushed again. Struggling to stand up, the boy tried crawling his way out of the rolling slime’s path. With his increased strength, he barely turned his body out of its direction swiftly. He watched as the slime finally slowed down, becoming stagnant once more where it had been before.

Luke looked back at his leg, his face scrunched, trying to prevent himself from screaming, tears already present in his eyes. The amount of pain he was in was unimaginable; every twitch of his body sent a jolt of pain to his receptors.

The wound was extremely bloody, and muscles and flesh were scattered across the ground a few steps away, where they had been crushed. Staring at the slime, he saw his blood smeared along its round body. He knew he couldn’t heal his injury right away. But Luke had to get up; that was the only way he could win against the slime.

Not having an arm was all right for him; at least he could still move and dodge and use his lower body to fight back. But losing the mobility in his right leg had made him a sitting duck. Not having any way to fight back was exactly how he was going to end up dead.

Luke very well needed to finish the slime before it was too late. He strained himself, trying to the best of his abilities. Blood vessels were visible along his forehead, and his face became a brighter hue of red. He could barely remain standing on one leg, and his other was still giving him constant waves of agony as it hung to the side. It was true suffering; he moved as swiftly as he could towards the slime. If he were going to win, he had to end it now.

Every hop was painful as he clenched his teeth. With nothing to support him, he fell towards the ground. Even without the slime having to jump and literally cause a practical earthquake, he was losing his balance. Luke decided to just crawl his way to the slime instead, fully knowing the short time he had left. He left a trail of blood pouring out of him, looking at his arm, which pulled him closer to the silver slime, becoming paler by the minute.

The slime could flatten him if it rolled or jumped on him. Yet, Luke still pulled himself towards it as his reflection grew bigger on the slime’s shell. He didn’t wish to depend on soul energy for salvation a second time, as it would defeat the purpose if he couldn’t save himself by his own power.

Luke grunted while getting back on his feet. Even though his right leg was in excruciating pain. He plummeted downwards with all his might. Extending his elbow, he pushed against the dent that was already there.

Much to Luke’s surprise, it inflicted surprisingly extensive damage. His elbow still throbbed with pain at the fact that it had snapped under all his weight. But the dent’s size increased decently. He rushed to do it again, as he gradually felt a shift coming from below the slime.

He felt the earth tremble once again. Just the fact of its getting ready to jump had sent enough shivers running down his spine. Now he wondered why he hadn’t felt the previous tremors. Was it because I was so deep in thought? But at this moment, he didn’t care. Pushing his thoughts aside. He went to do the same thing with his broken elbow.

He shoved himself with an unimaginable amount of force towards the enlarged dent. His elbow was completely shattered, almost making Luke pass out from the pain. He heard it crack under the pressure again, and in that moment, he bit his lip while focusing his full attention on the slime.

His eyes darted along the crack his elbow was still stuck in,

"T-The slime…isn’t…disappearing,” Luke said with a trembling voice, looking to see if there was anything he could do to slay the slime. Time seemed to freeze before his very eyes; the ground shook with even greater power. He could only try his best to hold on to the slime. Vibrations rang out with rhythmic patterns as if gravity had changed. The slime jolted; with everything Luke had, he pushed his left leg against the ground. Using the force, he pulled himself up and held on for dear life. Before he knew it, he was meters in the air with the slime only slightly below him. Watching with teary eyes as the slime descended towards the ground. Luke remained in the sky, feeling as if his heart was stuck in his throat, but Luke kept it in.

He could only watch the ground rapidly grow bigger. Luke set a goal, if he died, he would take the slime with him. The boy took this chance; even with his broken elbow, he tried his best to hold it out in front of him. Praying this wasn’t the end for him, he had to try something. Nose-diving, his elbow held out as best it could, slamming into the dent he had scanned from above. The instant he made contact, his arm caved in. He could only watch in horror before everything went dark.
