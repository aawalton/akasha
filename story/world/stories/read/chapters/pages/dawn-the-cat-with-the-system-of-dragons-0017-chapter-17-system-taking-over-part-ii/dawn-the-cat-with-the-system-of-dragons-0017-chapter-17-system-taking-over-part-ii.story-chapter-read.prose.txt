Dawn, the black cat with golden eyes, was currently standing inside a huge cyclone, talking with the guardian of the forest of Hylas. But Dawn was behaving differently than usual. Her appearance was also slightly different.

Her golden eyes turned into a glowing sky blue color. Her claws were covered with a blue transparent layer. The layer looked like a weaponized extension for her claws, ready to obliterate any foe. These were the result of the system taking control of her body.

Since Dawn was not intelligent enough to use a system, the system decided to exploit a loophole and took control of her body to solve all problems once and for all.

The system said it wanted to assist its user, but in reality, there was no need to assist Dawn with anything.

Since Dawn was worried that her babies might have been swept away in the cyclone, the system decided to use this opportunity. The system knew her babies were okay. Guardian of the forest was taking care of her babies. So there was no way the guardian would let a disaster take her babies away, especially when the disaster was caused by the release of huge amounts of elemental energy.

The system knew that the cyclone was not natural. Cyclones never start abruptly like this. There are always signs of impending disaster. Abrupt cyclones can only be caused by someone strong like the guardian, but why the guardian created the cyclone was still unknown to the system.

Even after observing everything and knowing all this, the system still intervened.

Initially, when the Aegis system was installed, it decided to observe its temporary user for several hours. And all Dawn did was try to hunt down the status screen. It took Dawn almost half a day to understand that the status screen was not harmful.

The status screen would have been removed easily if its user had thought about the screen being closed, but instead of thinking about closing the screen, Dawn tried to hunt it down. Aegis was not expecting this. In the end, Aegis decided to show Dawn how to use the system by taking over Dawn’s body

Ideally, Aegis wanted to make Dawn intelligent, but it was not possible yet. Becoming intelligent is a long, continuous, and rigorous process. It required developing elemental circuitry or, preferably, Nexus circuitry in the brain. It required years to accumulate elemental energy.

Sometimes the process takes hundreds of years, because to have Nexus circuitry, one needs to develop dense elemental circuitry all over the body, including the brain. There was no way a normal cat like Dawn could do it fast.

Thankfully, Dawn had accumulated elemental energy over the years and had already created circuit pathways throughout her whole body naturally.

This means if Dawn is able to absorb and use a huge amount of elemental energy in a short time, elemental energy will accumulate throughout the pathways, and elemental circuitry will develop fast.

This would also shorten the time to develop Nexus circuitry, as Nexus circuitry only develops when elemental circuitry becomes dense enough for Nexus to reside and not flush out when using elemental energy through the elemental circuitry.

But there was no way to get an astronomical amount of elemental energy in a short time to create her elemental circuitry through her pathways. So Aegis decided to wait and see how Dawn develops. It was important to protect Aegis’s original host.

When Aegis system talked to the guardian tree, it finally got what was happening. The system decided to use the tree’s released elemental energy to create elemental circuitry in Dawn’s body, including her brain, to make her intelligent.

For Aegis, this much elemental energy was like an abandoned mine full of diamonds, waiting to be taken by someone.

Aegis system decided to take the shortcut. The shortcut was to absorb surrounding elemental energy to hunt down high-level evolved animals. Then, take elemental cores from the evolved animals and utilize them along with surrounding elemental energy to create dense elemental circuitry.

This would significantly decrease the time to create nexus circuitry naturally.

Initially, Aegis only wanted to show Dawn how to use elemental energy and use the stats screen, but after talking to the guardian tree, Aegis changed the whole plan immediately. Hunting monsters would not only allow the system to show how to use elemental energy, but also make Dawn intelligent and strong by building elemental circuitry immediately.

Until now, Aegis was using Dawn’s elemental energy to redirect the wind to stay on the ground. As soon as the guardian replied, Aegis stopped redirecting the wind around Dawn’s body, and the wind of the cyclone instantly threw Dawn into the air and took her high above the ground along with everything else.

Now, Dawn was more than a few hundred feet above the ground. If anyone sees her now, they would think that the cat was a little too calm while being thrown up into a cyclone.

Dawn was seeing everything clearly. She saw her babies being trapped, she heard the earlier conversation, and she also saw how she went up into the air suddenly. If Dawn was the one controlling her body, she would surely scream her lungs out.

Unfortunately, she was not able to scream. Her body was currently being controlled by Aegis, and it was too calm for some reason.

Then Aegis spread all four feet in the air and created a big blue colored square sheet just below Dawn's belly to glide in the cyclone. Square sheet’s corners were connected with each pawn, and from afar, Dawn looked just like a big flying squirrel.

Had Aegis created wings like shape, it would become hard to navigate due to strong air flow. So instead, Aegis used elemental energy to create a thin sheet below the belly to glide through the wind.

Seeing Aegis doing all this, the tree guardian realised why Aegis told him to release more elemental energy and make the suction radius bigger. This would surely make the cyclone more deadly, but it would not let Dawn fall to the ground.

Now, Aegis was looking here and there, trying to find a perfect prey. When Dawn was on the ground, the system noticed many evolved animals being enveloped in the cyclone. This was why the system decided to hunt down monsters. Monsters who are unable to control themselves and are too scared to take action would become easy prey.

Soon, Aegis saw a black wyvern rolling in the sky, trying to regain balance in the air. It was quite a distance away from Dawn.

The black wyvern was flapping its wings nonstop, but it kept getting unbalanced by the strong wind. Wind was not letting it leave the cyclone, no matter how much it tried.

In the end, it lost its balance entirely and started rolling in every direction.

Observing the wyvern, Aegis took action. There was no shred of fear in the eyes, as if everything was being calculated.

Both the wyvern and Dawn were circling around the cyclone.

To reach the wyvern, either Aegis has to fly through the cyclone or reduce the circling speed by reducing drag. Aegis chose the latter option.

Aegis started gliding in the wind of the cyclone and used a little bit of elemental energy to redirect some of the air to reduce air resistance around Dawn's body to slow down the circling speed.

The wyvern was behind Dawn. And slowing down the circling speed worked very smoothly.

After a while, Dawn was close to the unbalanced wyvern. It was roaring in fear, while being rolled in every direction.

Aegis was aiming to land on the back of the wyvern. It was the safest option. If Dawn lands on the belly of the wyvern, it would surely attack and obliterate Dawn within seconds.

Aegis started gliding, matching the wyvern’s speed, hoping to land in the perfect position. But the wyvern was rolling around too fast, so it was very hard to aim for the perfect spot.

To be on the safer side, Aegis started absorbing a huge amount of elemental energy from the cyclone and spent everything in Dawn's claws, which were covered with a long, blue colored weaponised extension. Aegis started enhancing the claws’ strength and sharpness to the maximum level possible.

After being fully prepared, Aegis finally moved towards the unbalanced wyvern. Dawn landed right above the wyvern's head, covering both of the wyvern's eyes with Dawn's belly. It was a total blunder. The aim is to land on the wyvern’s back, not covering its eyes with Dawn’s belly.

The wyvern got startled. It was about to grab Dawn with its hand-like front leg. But immediately, Aegis used Dawn's claw-like extension to tear into the wyvern's skull through one of its eye sockets, and tore it off towards its nose from inside.

Aegis had spent a huge amount of elemental energy on the claws, so it was not hard to tear apart the skull from the inside. Everything happened within the blink of an eye, and the wyvern died immediately. There was no struggle.

Aegis then made the claws' length shorter to move easily to the wyvern's chest. Aegis didn’t let go of the dead body.

After reaching the chest, Aegis then made the claws sharper by spending elemental energy and inserted them into the ribs. Then tore out a small elemental core, which was just beside the heart.

After taking out the elemental core, Aegis put the core inside Dawn's mouth and swallowed it.

The core of the wyvern contained a huge amount of elemental energy. Immediately after swallowing the core, the elemental energy started overflowing. Aegis bounced off the wyvern's body and immediately started manipulating all the elemental energy inside while absorbing more energy from outside.

Then the system started transferring a huge amount of energy throughout Dawn’s body to enhance every fiber and bone without any exception. The goal was to temporarily enhance Dawn’s body and take it to the limit before creating elemental circuitry. Otherwise, Dawn’s body would not be able to handle the circuit creation process and would be obliterated immediately.

Previously, Aegis continuously absorbed elemental energy from the cyclone to spend where it was necessary. But now, not only was Aegis absorbing elemental energy from the cyclone, but Aegis was also absorbing energy from the wyvern’s core, which was currently inside Dawn’s stomach.

After enhancing Dawn’s body to the limit, it was time to redirect elemental energy from both sources and create the circuitry throughout the pathways.

Dawn was seeing everything, and she was too shocked to react. Even if she wanted to make any expression, she couldn’t, because her body was currently controlled by Aegis.

Dawn had never felt this much overflowing power in her body. Temporary enhancement by Aegis took everything to the limit of how much enhancement Dawn could handle temporarily.
