Hey everyone, the day is finally here!

After many days of nervously sweating the official release, as of today, I can officially call myself a published author for the very first time! Book 1 for The Eternal Assassin, Rise of the Reaper, is now published on Amazon and available to be read in all it's fancy glory.

This includes:

-Kindle Edition: Containing four thousand more words than what was on Royal Road, because I couldn't help myself during editing haha

-Audiobook: Narrated by the wonderful Garrett Michael Brown

-Paperback: Which is probably the most surreal of the three for me as an author. I'm going to be able to touch my own story. Crazy stuff!

This is my very first launch of a published story, so I'm stepping out of comfort zone a tad here and humbly asking you guys for your support with the launch. My dream is to become a full-time author, and I'd love to be able to devote even more time to coming up fun stories and bring them to life. Technically there are some crazy things that could theoretically happen if the series blows up on Amazon, such as hard covers, comics, or more, and man that would be pretty exciting to see the story come to life in new ways!

If you happen to be in a position to be able to help with the launch and would like to, there are a few simple ways you can go about doing so:

-If you have Kindle Unlimited, please think about downloading the book. All downloads count as sales, and if you decide you want to try rereading the now professionally edited version of the story, that would be just icing on the cake! I've been told Amazon is being wonky in terms of ratings and reviews these past few months (because of course), so if anyone does decide to give it a read or even just download it, any and all ratings/reviews would be massively appreciated.

-Simply spread the word about the story! In the grand scheme of things, not a whole lot of people read all the awesome stories here on Royal Road. However, a lot of people read what's available on Amazon. Word of mouth honestly goes a long way, so don't be afraid to tell people about this stab-happy man with a fancy dagger collection. Here are some promotional posts I've put up on r/LitRPG and r/ProgressionFantasy that you're welcome to share if you'd like.

Regardless of everything above, I just want to give you guys one more massive thank you for all the support you've shown over the last year the story has been running. Telling Asher's tale has been an absolute blast, and his story is far from finished. Obviously the daily postings here on RR aren't going to change as Asher is kind of in the middle of things at the moment haha, so I hope you all continue enjoying the story!

You guys rock, and I shall see you with tomorrow's chapter!

(P.S., Yes, my official Pen Name on Amazon is W.D. Swanson, as my publisher told me I should come up with something that sounded like a real person. Though I'll give you guys three guesses as to what the 'W.D.' stands for!)
