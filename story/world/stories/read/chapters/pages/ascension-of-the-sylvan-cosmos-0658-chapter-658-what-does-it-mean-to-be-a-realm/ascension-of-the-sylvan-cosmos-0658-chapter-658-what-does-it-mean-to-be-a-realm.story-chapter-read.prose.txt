After beefing with the System a little more to relax, Liu Shu decided to stop bullying the poor thing and focus on her changes. ‘The <System> said that I’ve become a <Realm>, but what does that mean exactly?’

Liu Shu pondered, spreading her senses around herself. Once she did, she noticed a subtle difference compared to before. “I exist… but I don’t at the same time.”

It was such a weird thing to say that Liu Shu paused after completing the sentence. Still, after thinking for a while more, there was no simpler way of explaining her current change compared to before.

She observed herself a bit more and then decided to try and spread her roots. Once she did, her face gained a subtle expression of confusion and, paradoxically, understanding. As her roots moved through space, she could see they were now drilling right under the main spatial layer.

Previously, Liu Shu used her spatial prowess to expand the sub-dimension, and her roots followed. Mixing those powers with the help of her subconscious allowed her to maintain her terrifying, constant spreading speed, even when her main body was not much larger than before.

However, when she moved her roots now, both of those actions seemed to have fused and become rather effortless. “This feeling…”

A sense of nostalgia hit her, and she noticed that what she felt when she spread her roots now was not much different from what she felt when she spread her roots in soil before. Moreover, thanks to her passive skill that allowed her to absorb all types of energy, the void had become part of her energy sustenance.

Liu Shu couldn’t help but relish it, her mind sinking into the feeling.

Outside, Flor’s group waited for Liu Shu to contact them. However, even two months after the “shadow” disappeared and something seemed to be completed, they heard nothing from Liu Shu.

Flor asked, worried. “Should we call her?”

They were standing below Tree’s shadow, all wondering if something had gone wrong. Tree was the one who answered, her soothing voice reaching them. “I believe that she should have finished whatever she was trying to do. The flow of essence in her roots is much tamer compared to before. It is even calmer than before she began trying to open her <Realm>, as she explained.”

Elyne asked, curious. “Lady Tree, your affinity with space is profound. Can you tell what has happened? The other day I tried it, and I can fly through Liu Shu’s body. It’s as if she has stopped existing.”

Tree pondered, her mind finding it difficult to put into words what she felt. “Liu Shu is now… deeply connected… to her dimension.” Tree spoke slowly, trying to find the correct words. “No. More than connected… She has merged with it.”

The Life Tree’s words made them ponder. Tree continued. “If I am correct, Liu Shu should’ve managed to put the <Realm-Touched> title to use.”

Of those present, no one managed to unlock this particular <Title>. Therefore, they didn’t pay it much attention at first. Now that Tree mentioned it, they reviewed the effects.

Everyone got thoughtful, and they decided to try it. If she didn’t answer after they called her, they would wait for Liu Shu to contact them from her side.

Tempestria had an idea and looked down at her Sylvan daughter, Arlyn. “Love, call for your mom. Tell her that someone is bullying you.”

Arlyn, who was nibbling on a delicious-looking red fruit, blinked. Then, without any shame, she shouted at the top of her lungs. “MOM! BAD PEOPLE ARE BULLYING ME!”

Her shrill shout left the adults present speechless.

Almost immediately, everyone felt a boundless presence manifest around them as pressure like no other descended from the sky.

Liu Shu’s cold and murderous voice reverberated, imposing and threatening. “Who dares bully my daughter?”

Arlyn smiled widely, not feeling an ounce of fear within her mother’s all-encompassing aura. “Hehe. Mom, Mommy is bullying me! She doesn’t let me eat <Delicious Berries>!”

Tempestria’s smiling face froze. ‘Daughter! You dare sell out your mother!? Look how I make red flowers bloom on your lower cheeks when we go back home!’

Liu Shu, who was suddenly awakened due to her daughter’s call of distress, couldn’t help but pause. Her senses swept the area, and she connected with Wisdom to know what had happened during the last few hours.

After she absorbed all the information, her threatening aura dissipated like smoke, and a chuckle escaped her. A small root rose from the ground, gently poking at Arlyn’s cheek. “Silly girl. If your mother doesn’t let you, then there is a reason for it.”

Arlyn pouted, and when she looked up and saw Tempestria’s expression, her pout froze. ‘Uh oh… I’m going to get spanked.’

Tempestria, understanding her daughter’s gaze all too well, smiled menacingly. ‘Yes. Yes, you are.’

Not a word was exchanged, yet mother and daughter exchanged a conversation full of ups and downs.

Tree chuckled and asked softly. “Little sapling, how did it go? Did you manage to complete what you wanted?”

Hearing her mom’s voice, Liu Shu’s motherly gaze changed to a childish one. Her roots were quick to climb up Tree’s trunk and also around Ignatia’s and Flor’s bodies.
