James and Zhang Chāo each threw a punch.

Each man sidestepped, pivoted, and kicked—then backed off again, avoiding contact.

For the first few seconds of their fight, they were mirror images of each other.

James allowed himself a quiet laugh. Then he and Chāo renewed their dance. For the first ten minutes of the fight, both men used normal martial arts—normal being a relative term, considering that James fought using the Way of the Predator, a martial art that the System had given him. But neither of them used flashy Skills or even employed Mana to enhance their punches.

As the Emperor blocked a punch with his arm and countered with one of his own, he couldn’t help but smile. There was some nostalgia to this. Neither man was even hitting at full strength yet. It reminded him pleasantly of Orientation. Simpler times, when hitting harder, dodging an attack, or being armed with a dagger was all that mattered in a fight.

The dance continued, growing more intense with each passing minute as both men revealed more of their strength, speed, and technique. Zhang Chāo, James guessed, had been a martial artist even before the System. James had no way of knowing this. It was just a feeling.

The System also didn’t create new martial arts styles for every person it initiated, and unlike James, Chāo clearly didn’t belong to the Predator in Human Skin Class.

James kicked Zhang Chāo in the shin, but Zhang Chāo didn’t allow the blow, which must have been painful, to keep him from punching James in the stomach. That was also mildly painful, though again, both men continued to hold back, such that no blow truly did more than sting. They were feeling each other out, looking for weaknesses, learning on another’s rhythm.

The slow escalation of force and speed continued for those first ten minutes, until Zhang Chāo threw a particularly violent-looking chop. James’s superhuman instincts detected danger, and he recognized he was not positioned well to dodge it. For the first time, he made an unnatural movement, using Full Body Control to twist his neck out of reach—a twisting movement that would have been physically impossible for a pre-System human.

So much for nostalgia.

Zhang Chāo grinned triumphantly, and James wrinkled his brow.

I suppose that was a sort of competition between us, he thought.

“All right, call that a point for you,” James said. “Savor it. You won’t be enjoying more.”

“We’ll see—”

Zhang Chāo’s words choked in his throat, and the grin vanished, as a wave of power struck him. The ring on James’s hand glowed with its golden aura.

Dominion.

A fraction of a second after the wave hit him, Chāo was dancing backward and using the same Skill. It was almost the same time, exactly the same Skill, but wildly different effects.

You are still just a King, Zhang Chāo. I am an Emperor. Feel the difference in your bones, and give up.

James gritted his teeth, as his surging aura struck the rising wave of Zhang Chāo’s Dominion. The terrible clash shook the air around them, and both the audience and seemingly, a few of the other fighters in different rings, became riveted on the contest of strength.

You cannot win.

Zhang Chāo clenched his fists, and James saw his teeth were gritted as well as the Heavenly King’s lips parted slightly with the effort he was exerting. The Fisher Emperor pushed a little harder pouring a bit more energy in.

Then James felt it.

Chāo’s whole body shook. He took a single, quaking step back. Then another. Then the King leaped into the air, jumping back and getting distance from James’s aura.

The Emperor grinned this time.

Zhang Chāo leaped on the air as if it was a solid surface, moving up more quickly than James’s aura rose. The power of Dominion suffused the ground and spread to surround the ring more quickly than it climbed into the sky. And the Heavenly King continued to flee upwards until James had to crane his neck to look at him.

“That’s a point for you, then,” Chāo called down once he seemed to be in a stable place, standing on a platform of solid air.

It’s more than that, James thought. Losing the aura contest puts you on a timer. As my Dominion extends into the air, and it gets harder for you to escape, it will be increasingly difficult for you to even move around, let alone fight. But of course, you already know that, don’t you, Chāo? I’m sure you’ve used that on your fair share of opponents in the past.

Zhang Chāo scowled and added, as if he knew what James was thinking, “My aura is more powerful in the air, anyway!”

And it did seem to be true. The Emperor’s aura was still slowly but surely pushing the King’s aura back in the air, but it was happening much more slowly than it had at ground level. It would take time, though the outcome was inevitable.

I could try to stall, or…

As James weighed his options, Zhang Chāo glowed brightly where he stood in the sky. His body whirled with multiple colors of Mana at once, so intense it was almost blinding. He certainly knew he was on limited time.

The wind began to shift, and James frowned.

He’s changing the weather, so I probably need to go to ground.

He reached out with his power and instantly liquefied the entire surface of the ring and the surrounding ground, turning the stone and soil into swamp.

Overhead, thunderclouds were forming, far more rapidly than was possible naturally.

If he’s using lightning, being underwater won’t be much of a defense. All right, so I take the fight to him.

Zhang Chāo’s eyes had begun to glow white, lightning bolts crackling around the edges.

James quickly released the half dozen surviving monsters he had left from the previous fight from his body into the swamp, then turned his attention back up to Chāo. Tornadoes appeared to be taking shape beneath the thunderclouds he’d conjured.

Well, those won’t do you a lick of good unless they’re something more than wind and water. In case you didn’t know, I’m the Ruler of the Dark Waters. Just like you’re stronger in the air, I’m more powerful with water around me, whether it’s swamp or atmospheric moisture.

James raised one hand, and a dozen geysers of swamp water burst up out of the ground. It was a move he had copied after seeing Samuel use it during the melee, but unlike Samuel, who required a magic trident and a Skill to do it, James’s Ruler powers had advanced far enough that he could perform such maneuvers casually.

Zhang Chāo dodged around the geysers fairly easily—the distance they had to cross, including moving through areas where Chāo’s aura was still dominant, made them far too slow—and pointed at James.

The tornadoes finished forming and rushed down toward the Emperor’s position, and a bolt of lightning blasted from the extended finger toward James as well.

With a flick of his fingertips, James intercepted the lightning with two swamp geysers, lined up in front of him like two walls.

The water from the first geyser exploded on contact with the lightning bolt, filling the air with gray steam. But the second geyser absorbed most of the shock wave and blocked the diffused electricity well enough that James only felt a moderate breeze from the explosion.

You’ll have to do better than that.

Then the steam was sucked up and away, into the rapidly closing tornadoes.

Still not good enough.

James waved his hand, and a relatively small wave of swampwater rose from beneath his feet and struck the tornadoes. The tempests pulled the water up, and it rapidly suffused every part of the tornadoes.

Then James seized control of them—the water was full of his Mana and aura, so he retained control over it even when it was surrounded by someone else’s conjured wind and water—and hurled the tornadoes back in Zhang Chāo’s direction.

The Heavenly King wrinkled his nose at the apparent inefficacy of his attacks.

As the tempests neared his position, his entire body glowed and then disappeared. James recognized what was happening, or what he thought was happening, and he used Lightning Strike. The Lightning Mana running through his body allowed him to move faster. He raised his arms in front of his chest like a shield. Roscuro was still in bracelet form since James hadn’t been using the Soul Eater’s power throughout the fight, and James didn’t have enough time to order the Soul Eater to transform.

A fraction of a second later, Zhang Chāo struck in the form of a massive lightning bolt. It struck the Soul Eater bracelet, singed the skin all over James’s arms, and probably caused a great deal of pain that James could not feel.

But it failed to blast a hole through James’s body as the King had probably hoped it would, and it couldn’t paralyze James when he had Lightning Mana already flowing through him. Nor did what would have been overwhelming pain for someone else matter, since James had complete control over that sensation.

Zhang Chāo bounced off of James’s body, raised his hand, and called out, his wordless cry piercing the sky.

Roscuro, shield! The Soul Eater transformed almost as rapidly as it had received the command.

A giant bolt of lightning, large enough and powerful enough to vaporize a tank, struck the shield just as James had raised it over his head. The energy washed over James and the ground all around him, harmless to the Emperor behind the Soul Eater shield.

Zhang Chāo lunged in, sword drawn, but James sidestepped and threw a punch that almost hit him in the throat.

The King darted back, out of melee range again, and then the swamp surged up to try to grab his feet. Zhang Chāo leaped into the air just in time to avoid it. More geysers of swamp water surged up, trying to grab Chāo, exercising their master’s will even as he was still locked in place, blocking the improbably large burst of lightning.

At least that seems to be everything that his thunderstorm had in it. A single blast of lightning, channeling his full power, and I can see the sky is starting to clear up. He’s using at least as many heavy energy output attacks as me, arguably more, but I can keep doing this. If he makes this into a war of attrition, he’s playing right into my hands.

James tried to keep an eye on Zhang Chāo as the King retreated back into the sky, but Chāo dodged and weaved too many times as he tried to avoid the geysers chasing him. The Emperor definitely lost sight of him several times, and in the end, his line of sight was almost fully blocked as the swamp formed a massive wave trying to swallow Zhang Chāo and everything around him.

Despite being unable to see what was going on, the Emperor couldn’t help but smile as the lightning onslaught finally lost its force.

I endured the worst you could dish out, Zhang Chāo. I can’t imagine you have an attack more destructive than that one that you just haven’t used yet. Not when you’ve been running from my attacks for half of this fight. Have you noticed? Aside from when we’re fighting in melee range, I haven’t had to move at all. Not a single step from me since you took to the skies, except when you lunged at me with your sword. That has to hurt your ego a bit. Come down here and prove you’re the better man. You know you want to…

As James was smiling, the wave crashed down, struck against a spherical shell of aura, and fell back down into the swamp it had come from.

Now that he could see Zhang Chāo, James saw he was gathering a large amount of Mana around himself again. This time, it was a rainbow of colors. James thought he recognized what kind of power his opponent was using.

As Chāo prepared to use this Skill, he moved in what felt like a ritual dance style.

He’s not trying what I think he is, right? On me? Fucking arrogant. Give it your best shot. I’ll make you pay for every second you spend in this stupid dance.

James raised one foot and placed it down in front of him.

Warp Step!
