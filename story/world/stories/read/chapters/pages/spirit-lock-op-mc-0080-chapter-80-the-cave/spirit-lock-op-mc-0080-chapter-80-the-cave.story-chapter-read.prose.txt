Carian led the charge up the path to the mountain. Hayden’s entire team was still alive, surrounding his body. That made her grit her teeth. They’re all still alive? she thought. Half her team was dead—more than half of the total combined forces. Yet Hayden’s entire team was still alive.

Hayden wasn’t exactly at fault for what happened. The Sickles went to that mountain of their own volition. Yet it was likely that Hayden would have awakened that beast, and even if she didn’t, she felt it was sinister somehow.

Carian was confused and lost and angry and she knew she wasn’t in the state of mind to make decisions, but she was still furious to see them all alive.

“Leave,” a man she’d come to know as Photel—known best by his moniker, ‘Acid Rain.’ He had no mercy as he stared at them. “If you don’t, you’re going to lose what little forces you have left.”

“Not with Calid here,” Carian said. “He may be injured, but he’s still enough to wipe you all out with one strike.”

Another man she’d come to know as Fringe activated his Bubble spirit.

“I’ll break that before the attack,” Carian said. “We have thirty-six people here, all elites. There are over a dozen B-ranks, four As, and an army of Cs. You’re going to die. So let’s talk.”

“Do you think we’re stupid?” Photel asked. “If we live, you’re toast.”

“Not necessarily. Our only job is to keep Hayden alive. To that end, we planned to kill you and take over his security, as powerful politicians tasked us with. But at this point, we’ve realized we’re at a serious impasse. If Hayden dies, we’re dead—all of us. ‘Cause the Vast Four won’t pardon you for failing to protect him.”

“Unless we die fighting a colossus and you destroy the evidence,” Kalia, as Carian would soon know her, said. “That’s what’s gonna happen if he dies, right?”

Carian’s expression didn’t betray her, but the expressions of those with them did.

“Look,” Fringe said. “We’ll talk. But until this kid lives or dies, you need to leave. If you don’t, we’re takin’ half your team with us.”

“You won’t touch us.”

“That’s bold of you to say,” Kalia said. “Ella’s dead. Sylvia’s dead. Most of your A-ranks are dead. It’s you and Calid, and Calid’s lost a lot of blood. Are you really counting on him?”

“Calid’s healed. And he only needs one strike to wipe you out.”

“We’ll see about that,” Photel said.

A geyser of water shot up underneath their feet. A split second later, Photel snapped his fingers and lightning erupted from the water. It wasn’t just a low voltage. It was an entire storm that shocked half of her team.

Her shadow cat, Koben, teleported behind the team and prepared to strike, but an arrow shot out of nowhere, piercing through its body. She landed on dry ground and called it back. Where did that—

A cave mouth suddenly appeared, and twenty high-ranking rifters flew out, striking in formation—a seasoned team of mercenaries. Carian’s team and those in the back were immediately overwhelmed by the barrage of attacks. It turned messy on both sides—a flurry of wind and fire and electricity.

The ground buckled and cratered and cracked under the impacts, and someone on Hayden’s team had a bomb spirit that exploded around her.

That wasn’t to say that the ambushers weren’t hurt. The Crimson Sickles lost their best members, but those who remained were still powerful. Carian crushed three of the ambushers with gravity, and Koben blinked through the battlefield, cutting them down. Arrows shot at the groups, breaking barriers and killing them.

It all happened in thirty seconds—a true bloodbath that scarred the landscape forever.

Fringe stood in the center, sweating profusely as he fought to stay conscious. His barrier was the only thing standing between Hayden’s team and annihilation.

“Kill Hayden!” Carian screamed.

A massive barrage targeted Hayden specifically, breaking down the barrier. To Carian’s amazement, Fringe survived the onslaught—but his barrier disappeared immediately after.

“Well fought,” Carian said. She lifted her hand, but abruptly stopped when she saw Lina Iska fly out of the cave, followed closely by Kayline. She hesitated, but Calid screamed, “Kill them! We’ll add them to the—”

Living Arrows shot after Calid, each streaking like missiles.

Carian panicked. Killing the Iskas was a matter of life and death. All the Sickles could do was kill them and pray they could successfully destroy the evidence.

“Sorry,” Carian said, glancing at the sisters. She lifted her fingers and snapped. A gravity ball summoned above the group—preparing to crush them. Fringe put up a barrier. It was admirable, but it was useless—

Or so she thought.

The barrier obtained a deeper hue, making it stronger. She increased the pressure, and the ground around the barrier cratered. She did again. What’s going… She paused when she saw Lina. She had her hands on Fringe, and under the Hawk Eyes spirit, she could see that she was feeding him mana and easing his core. What the hell is that?

Carian tried to increase the pressure, but a streaking silver line cut her off. She barely dodged, catching a glimpse of a blonde rifter attacking her.

It was Kalia. She slashed her katana at Carian.

Carian dodged, but an aura blade continued onward, shearing through her side. What the hell was that? she screamed.

That was just the start of the terror.

Kalia shot an arrow at her. Carian dodged, but the silver arrow stopped by her face, and then shot a blade out of it toward her face.

Carian ducked the attack, only for a new blade to shoot downward from the last blade. It was like a labyrinth. It was like a three-dimensional game of snake, where the blades formed a long network, each capable of creating new blades. It was wild and fast. It shot out three blades a second, following Carian around. “What… the… hell… is… this!” she screamed as she dodged.

It was over.

Hayden didn’t just get some mercenaries. The universe was vast, and he somehow managed to import top-tier experts who rivaled them on all fronts. They even had Acid Rain, who killed half their A-ranks in a siege attack—he was no weaker than Calid or Ella. Then there was the sniper, who was keeping Calid from attacking. It was over.

Or so she thought. The striking blue arrows that Hayden’s sniper was using suddenly stopped.

Finally! Carian thought. The sniper was using Mind Split for five splits for the last hour. It was shocking that they could keep up. That means—

The skies suddenly darkened, and wind crashed around them.

-

Lina saw the coming hurricane and panicked.

“Help Hayden!” Kayline ordered. “I’ll protect you!”

“What? If the barrier goes out—”

“It’s going out anyway! That’s Calid—the Living Hurricane. Move!”

Lina’s eyes welled with tears as she fell to the ground by Hayden’s feet. His cloud of tora had reduced to a third, but it wasn’t enough. Please let this work… she thought as she put one hand on Gluttony and the other on Hayden. She then sucked tora from Gluttony into her core, processed it slightly, and then poured it into Hayden’s core.

Gluttony opened its eyes weakly and then squeaked gratefully. He then sucked in more.

It was overwhelming for about thirty seconds, but then something extraordinary happened. Hayden’s core shone, and Gluttony glowed just as brightly. The little mouse’s head slowly grew two tiny, two-pronged antlers.

It’s evolving… Lina thought in wonder.

Suddenly, the tora sucked into Gluttony, and it came out twice as processed as before.

“Hurry up!” Fringe screamed as the storm picked up. “It’s about to make landfall!”
