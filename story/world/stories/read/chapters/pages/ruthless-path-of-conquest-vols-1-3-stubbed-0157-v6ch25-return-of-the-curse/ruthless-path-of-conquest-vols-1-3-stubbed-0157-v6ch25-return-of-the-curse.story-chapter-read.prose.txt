If you or any of your agents harm any member of my family, your entire nation with all its peoples will be erased from this world. You will not become martyrs. You will not be mourned or honored in death. Your name will not echo through time. You will simply vanish.”

James saw that the Holy Kings all felt the weight of his words on them. Their reactions were visible and visceral. The white gloved man clapped one of his hands to his mouth, and James could see he was suppressing the urge to vomit.

I guess Curse got upgraded, James thought. I don’t think it was powerful enough to do this to Rulers before. Well, suffer, bastards. This is what you get for probably trying to harm my family at some unspecified point in the future.

Vinny!

Show off!

If you leave my people alone, I might kill off all of you, but I won’t touch any of your people or your families. We can keep this conflict civilized, or we can make this a very dirty war.”

“Yes, by hiring assassins,” James said, rolling his eyes.

I could really use a Skill like that, James thought. He knew Han Jianguo would never trade it for anything James had, though.

[All Rulers, please prepare to proceed to the Victors Tournament, whether as participants or as honored guests. That’s a pretty insane prize. I have to win if possible…

We will restore all bodies and equipment to their pre-melee condition once the melee reaches its conclusion.]

So, they’re asking all of us to sign up to literally die, James thought. All but one participant is guaranteed to… but it’s only temporary? Well, I guess my life’s been in the System’s hands before. More than once.

Maybe this place could be a solution to my Holy Kings problem…

What you’re asking for is entirely possible, master, Roscuro replied. Aren’t you worried about pissing off the System’s representatives again, though?

It’s not even against the spirit of the rules, he added in his head. It doesn’t go against anything that was said. I’m really doing them a favor. If they didn’t want people using this strategy, they needed to change the rules a little and do things differently. Assuming my plan works.
