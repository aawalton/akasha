Who...what? Yes! It's another author update!

First, you should know the next chapter is a monster. Titled Temper Temper, it starts a small reunion arc with a lot happening behind the scenes. Some things about Liberty will be revealed, as well as the Cass Crew, as I call them in my head. A bit of alliteration there. I had a great time writing it, and kept things simple. It took me a bit, and it's a double chapter, so we'll have a one-chapter skip that follows before diving into the reunion scenes. You'll understand as you read through. It also starts a major shift in the series.

Second, did you all know I'm a mentor? My previous mentee, Everheart, is the author of Progenitor's Path, a solid story that is doing very well at the moment. My new mentee, Shawn DePasquale, is already an established writer, though in Comic Books and Screenplays. I realized, as I read his work, that his faults were my strengths, and vice versa. So in a moment of inspiration, we began to talk about what we could do if we co-authored a piece of literature. I presented several book ideas I had been kicking around, we merged two, and came up with MetaJoy.

Now, technically, that means I'm writing 3 book series at the same time (though MetaJoy is a singular book with the potential for a sequel, ending already written). To keep the burnout from accruing, I spend a lot of my time working on Support Your Local War Mage, just developing backgrounds for Systems and characters, while with MetaJoy, we're rotating who writes which chapters. I wrote the original, located here: Link, While Shawn is just about done with chapters 2-3 as he went on a writing spree.

The writing style and plot design of MetaJoy are so far outside the bounds that we decided to have a non-synopsis, synopsis (blurb). If I release any part of the blurb to the public, it'll probably ruin the story. Just know that it's weird, experimental, and very, very fun. Chapter releases will be random and whenever we have time to write them, though I'm kicking around the idea of releasing them in batches of 5.

Now, on old writing news, Symphony book 3 is out today!

Ohhh, look at the glory. The beauty. Man I love these covers. When you put all three next to each other, it looks super cool. And an extra super cool note, each of those musical elements added in there are actual songs. If you can figure out what the song is, it gives you a preview of what the book is about.

I really, really hope you'll give the series a shot. While Cass focuses on being a beacon of hope, Walker, the protagonist of Symphony, focuses on literally building a better world, with one of the craziest endings you can find in any LitRPG book series. It's stated as science fiction, but by book 3, it has shifted over to fantasy (intentionally). I also included a touch of every subgenre within LitRPG, expanding the scope of the story and putting forth a plot that can't really be predicted.
