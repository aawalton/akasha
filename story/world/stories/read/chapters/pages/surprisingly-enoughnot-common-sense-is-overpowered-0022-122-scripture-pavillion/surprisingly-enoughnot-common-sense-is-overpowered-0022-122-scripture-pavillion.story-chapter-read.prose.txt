[Jade Skin and Icy Meridians]Draw upon yin and frost qi to temper the body. Refine the flesh until it possesses the resilience of jade, while icy currents cleanse and strengthen the meridians, allowing them to endure greater flows of spiritual energy. With each level, both body and meridians are tempered further, eventually attaining a physique of jade and frost.

It can be practiced up to the Foundation Building stage.

Price: 10,000 spirit stones.

“Finally found it,” Ning murmured.

Before coming to the Scripture Pavilion, he had already asked Fang Zhu, Wei Zhusang, and several others for recommendations. After comparing their suggestions with the techniques available to him, Jade Skin and Icy Meridians had stood out as the best fit.

Its greatest advantage was its effect on the meridians. While it tempered the flesh as well, its true value lay in gradually strengthening and refining the meridians, allowing them to endure greater strain and more violent flows of spiritual energy. Better yet, the technique could accompany him throughout Qi Condensation and even into Foundation Building, so he wouldn't need to replace it anytime soon.

Most importantly, many of the resources required for its cultivation were spiritual plants.

And Ning happened to own a farm.

Compared to the brute-force body cultivation methods that demanded large quantities of beast blood, medicinal baths, or other expensive materials, this one suited him almost perfectly.

“Welp! That took a while.” Ning stretched his shoulders and glanced around. “Since I’ve still got some time, might as well check out a few martial arts.”

For the next several minutes, he wandered leisurely between the shelves, pulling out the occasional scroll and skimming through it. He wasn’t necessarily looking to buy anything; with so many techniques gathered in one place, simply seeing what kinds of martial arts existed was useful for broadening his horizons.

Eventually, however, a rather peculiar title caught his eye.

The Turtle Breathing Art.
