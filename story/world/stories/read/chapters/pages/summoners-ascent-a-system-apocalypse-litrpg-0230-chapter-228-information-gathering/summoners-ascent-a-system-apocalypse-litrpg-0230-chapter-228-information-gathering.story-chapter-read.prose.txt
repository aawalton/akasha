Ashton walked around the university's campus. It hadn't even been that long since he had been here, so it didn't really change all that much. Well, in terms of the base-construction, at least.

There were some parts that had changed considerably just in the last couple of weeks, obviously. The formerly grassy areas had been repurposed to turn them into fields, and some of the larger open areas were littered with tents or other temporary housing solutions.

In general, that was all well and good, if it weren't for some minor facts.

First, none of the fields were used to grow food. All that was in the soil were seedlings for certain herbs. Specifically the ones that Ashton had told Sam to post about in the community.

They were mostly ones with healing properties. Well, Drakewood's university did have some pretty damn solid botanical tracks, so it made sense for them to try to raise these. But usually, that should happen alongside some level of food production. But every single one of these fields was dedicated to herbs.

And second, this university should have plenty of rooms to turn into temporary housing without forcing people to sleep outside where they were basically the ones that were the most at risk if monsters did manage to make their way inside.

Just like at Calibur this morning, it was possible for monsters to fly over the walls or also just climb over them, and in those situations the people that were trying to sleep out here had no way to defend themselves. Ashton had sensed the inside of the rooms that he had walked past earlier. There were plenty of spaces that weren't being used efficiently for this situation. Sure, it made sense that they would want to keep some of the laboratories so that they had safe environments for the things they were researching and trying to produce, but Ashton walked past empty rooms.

Not just ones that didn't have any people in them, but ones that were fully devoid of any semblance of purpose. No chairs, no tables, nothing. And then there were the massive lecture halls.

From Ashton's understanding, some of them were still being used for... lectures. For large meetings of people just talking about their new findings or discussing the newest situations in town. And that alone was fine; but you could do that just as well outside in those wide open areas and let people sleep in safe and dry indoor spaces instead.

Ashton just wasn't able to comprehend how you could prioritise such utterly unimportant things over the basic necessities and lives of other human beings. Well, it was better than it was in Lumia, but that wasn't a high bar to cross, either.

At least most of the people here seemed to be 'okay'. There weren't many folk that Millie pointed out to Ashton as feeling 'uncomfortable', or having any weird things connected to her view of them. There were a handful that did make her nervous, obviously, but for the most part these just seemed to be normal people.

"Uhm..." Millie started, tugging on Ashton's cloak. "There's someone staring at you."

Ashton raised a brow. "Oh? I've been getting a lot of stares though, right? I don't look like most people do right now."

Millie grimaced slightly. "Yeah, but it's a different type of stare... You know, the type of stares that older people have sometimes when they look at pretty people." Millie's apprehension to the topic made it pretty clear what kind of stare it actually was. It was one of the most convenient sort for gathering information.

"And where are they?" Ashton asked, and Millie groaned and pointed in their direction. And so, with a bright smile and an annoyed little girl by his side, Ashton approached the person that Millie was talking about.

It was a young woman seemingly around Ashton's physical age. Most people here were on the younger side, anyway.

"Hello there," Ashton cocked his head ever so slightly and showed a warm smile toward her. He could sense some particular mana signatures in the bag she was carrying; various potions, specifically the ones from shops. "Sorry, you look like you know your way around here. Do you happen to know where the potion research area is? I've got a few things I'd like to talk to them about."

The young woman's eyes widened. "Potions? Are you an alchemist as well?"

"You're an alchemist! Interesting," Ashton smiled broadly. "No, no, I'm a summoner, actually. I just happen to know quite a lot about potions from personal experience and would like to share that knowledge with others."

The woman raised her eyebrows surprised and excited "You do? How do you–" she stopped herself. "Wait, did you say... summoner? As in 'the Summoner'?"

Ashton shivered slightly. He didn't like being referred to like that. It was the same way he spoke about that creature, and it felt like a pit formed in his stomach. But well, he couldn't show that in this context. "The very same. I'm Ashton."

He stretched out a hand, and the bewildered alchemist shook it. "Madeleine... So what exactly does it mean to be a summoner? I've seen the posts in the community, but... is that all true? Like, you use real magic?"

Ashton smiled and thought for a moment. Which of his summons would be the most awe-inspiring? He could summon the Drunken Bear already, since he needed them to carry the rest of the boxes later anyway, but they were a bit more intimidating than Ashton wanted, at least for a first meeting.

To start out with, the Backpacker might be a good choice instead. Maybe they weren't awe-inspiring, but it was a good way to start. Madeleine saw the small figure appear from behind Ashton and hand him a bottle of water.

"Thank you very much," he said, before splashing the bottled water into the air. Madeleine flinched and already tried to stop the water from hitting her, but it never came back down anyway. The droplets came together into the undine, who swam through the air around Ashton's head.

Just as he thought, the undine was always a great choice to try and impress people.

Of course, he could have also just used the wisp, who was already summoned and currently sitting atop the staff, but well, the wisp's true magnificence came out when you were able to cast spells through it. Otherwise, it was just a floating sphere that Ashton used to kill monsters, and that wasn't the sort of awe he wanted to show off right now.

"This is real magic...? It's not just some kind of trick?" Madeleine asked, and Ashton quickly shook his head.

"It's the absolutely real thing. Amazing, right?" He smiled back at the woman in front of him. He could barely tell the details of her expression, since Ashton's channelling range was quite extended, but from the fact that Millie wasn't hiding her annoyance, Ashton was able to guess that this was working. "Anyway, that being the case... the potion research?"

"Huh? Oh! Yes, yes, of course, right this way," Madeleine nodded. She cleared her throat and turned, starting to guide Ashton across the campus to the science building; the place with all the different lab rooms.

Millie tugged on his arm and called him down. "Yes?" Ashton asked, and Millie just grumbled loudly.

"I don't like her. She didn't even pay attention to me..."

"I know, and I'm sorry. But this is the best way for us to find that ghost, alright?" Ashton pointed out with a smile. From the way Chris was talking about it earlier, it was probably not supposed to be spread around. Ashton also had the Backpacker check the community and they didn't seem to have found any posts, at least not yet. Chris wasn't exactly the kind to just want to make small-talk for no reason, so there was definitely something to all of this.

Sure, it could just be a dumb rumour, but in this world, any such rumours would obviously be followed up on more. There was no way that nobody would post something to warn others about potential ghosts showing up.

So, the more likely situation that Ashton figured was going on right now was that this was another thing that Quincy was trying to monopolise; knowledge of ghosts.

Different sorts of ghost-like or undead monsters would inevitably show up sooner or later, if they weren't wandering around at night already anyway. It was insane to try and keep that sort of information to yourself now that the world has come to this.

It didn't even make sense to want to keep this to yourself. Unless you were a necromancer, why would you want to monopolize this knowledge? But Ashton highly doubted that there was a necromancer here of all places. The most likely option was that there was something related to this 'ghost', this imprint in the veil, that Quincy wanted to keep a secret.

Some kind of information that Quincy didn't want to leak out. And that was exactly why Ashton wanted to see it.

"I heard some rumours about this place," Ashton pointed out, catching up to Madeleine with a skipped step.

"Rumours?" Madeleine asked, clearly growing nervous already. Ashton doubted that she made the connection to ghosts; rather, there was probably a lot of sketchy stuff going on here.

"That there is a ghost roaming these halls. I'm pretty interested in magical beings, as you can imagine. And I happen to know a thing or two about spectres, so I might be of help, too," Ashton explained as Madeleine led him and Millie into the potion lab.

Madeleine tensed up. "Ghosts? Don't be ridiculous. There's no such thing as ghosts."

"Just as there's no such thing as magic? Or goblins, and orcs, and giant man-eating spiders?" Ashton asked with a laugh. "Ghosts are very much real, and I'd like to meet this one. Do you think you could help me with that?"

Madeleine slowly shook her head. "I don't think I can do that..."

"Are you sure?" Ashton asked, smiling. He already got her to drop the 'ghosts aren't real' thing right away, so this should be quite easy. He took a step closer to Madeleine. "I could definitely make it worth your time."
