Despite everything—despite having been pushed into this—Daruk felt a spark of excitement stir within him as the announcer called out loudly,

“Daruk Brystion of Ragnarok, winner of the Sigma 25 Grand Tournament!”

The crowd erupted with cheers, their voices loud enough to shake the heavens and the earth. Fireworks filled the sky, love poured from the crowd too tangible to resist. Trumpets blew all across the arena. The citizens of Ragnarok raised flags. Students from the 5th academy raised flags. People screamed. People cried.

Energy filled the arena until it overflowed.

It was more adoration and praise than Daruk had been prepared for, and despite hating himself for putting on this show, Daruk let himself bask in the excitement—if only for a moment.

His gaze took in the crowd, his heart pounding as millions chanted his name over and over.

It was exhilarating, and for a brief moment, he admitted that perhaps it wouldn't be so bad to accept this. To be content with it.

But he entertained the thought only a moment before turning his gaze toward the royal pavilion, where the dukes, high nobles, and royals sat, clapping politely.

All four crown princes of Lutia were there with their entourages, and when his gaze locked with theirs, they smiled.

Smiles they had never given Aodhán.

Smiles that would have been twisted into snarls were Aodhán the one standing here.

The thought crushed what little excitement had stirred within him, and Daruk felt his heartbeat settle instantly, like a stone sinking beneath still water. Resentment curled in his gut, but he pushed it aside, a neutral mask slipping over his face.

Dining with devils was hard, but he had come this far already. He would see it through.

Drawing a slow breath, he stepped forward and bowed. “Your Highnesses,” he greeted, his voice echoing all across the arena.

Prince Magnus Ragnar smiled proudly. ““You have done exceedingly well, Daruk Brystion. But then again, it is the least we can expect from a double inheritor like you. You truly are one of this continent's finest gifts—a grand example to your generation. I look forward to seeing how much more powerful you will become.”

Daruk grimaced, not liking the sound of that at all.

He didn't raise his head. “Thank you, your Highness.”

“Thank you for this victory,” Prince Magnus continued. “You have brought glory to Ragnarok—to your academy. I am truly proud of your achievements. And now, with the power vested in me... tell me, what is it you want?”

The cheering crowd stilled immediately and Daruk finally raised his head.

The rewards for winning this tournament were no mere gifts. They offered more credits than he could hope to spend in a dozen years, more treasures than he knew what to do with, more power, influence, and assets than he could ever have gathered on his own.

Daruk wanted all of it.

But there was one reward he wanted more. The request he could make of the prince himself.

His eyes hardening, Daruk raised his voice. “I want to travel, Your Highness. I want to see the world.” He drew a steady breath, choosing his words carefully. “I want to see the continents beyond Lutia. To visit them. Expand my horizons.”

Cheers rose from the naïve crowd, loud and enthusiastic as they waited for the prince to accept such a simple request. Some even shook their heads in disappointment, discussing how they would have asked for something grander. Something better.

Daruk barely heard them. His ice-blue gaze remained fixed on the prince, whose expression had gone utterly still.

Then he grimaced.

Daruk's heart stuttered.

Confused whispers spread through the arena as the seconds passed without a response from the Prince. Daruk's hands sweated then froze over to stillness.

Tension settled over the arena.

Then, after what felt like an eternity, the prince suddenly burst into laughter. “Is that what you want to waste a request on?”

The tension shattered immediately as the arena joined him in laughter.

"This is a once-in-a-lifetime offer, young man," the Prince continued. "Why not ask for something else?"

"I don't want something else," Daruk replied without hesitation, a cold anger rising within him. "This is what I want… Your Highness."

The title tasted like bile in his mouth.

Apparently, they tasted like insult to the prince's ears as well.

Prince Magnus grimaced. “That’s not a request I can grant you.”

"Why?" Daruk nearly spat, Embody Ice working overtime to keep his tone flat and inflectionless. “You're the crown prince. You can command someone to take me.”

His voice rose a fraction before he forced himself to breathe.

“You said I could ask for anything,” he said a second later. “This is what I want.”

He paused. “It’s just an adventure.”

But it wasn’t just an adventure. And it seemed the princes had caught on to that.

Prince Magnus stared at him without a word, and then someone else responded. It was the crown prince of Sunstone.

"We cannot grant you that request." His eyes glinted in a way that said you're too powerful to lose, and his next words confirmed it. "Lutia can't let you go. You will either ask for something else or forfeit the reward entirely."

Daruk raged, fury rising within him. He should have known this would happen. He should have known the powers that be would never let him leave this prison of a country.

He should have left with Aodhán when he had the chance.

His anger sharpened into something cold—merciless. He took a step forward, but before he could say or do anything, Aldric was suddenly by his side, a beaming smile on his face as he bowed.

"Forgive my brother, your Highnesses," he said smoothly. "He seems to be a bit overwhelmed. Let us take some time to think it over, and we’ll get back to you.”

Tension eased immediately, and Prince Magnus smiled. "Take all the time you need."

Aldric bowed once more and pulled Daruk away, and despite his rage, Daruk allowed himself to be pulled away. Cold fury raged within him, tears pricked his eyes, his jaw tightened in disappointment.

But when they reached a corner, rather than berate him as Daruk had expected, Aldric wrapped his arms around him.

He said nothing. He did nothing. He simply breathed, and the tears Daruk had been holding back pooled… and fell.

"It's alright," Aldric whispered, knowing exactly how his brother felt, perhaps even more than Daruk himself did. "It's alright."

They stood that way for nearly a minute before Daruk finally pulled away, his neutral mask back on. Without looking at his brother, he whispered. “I’ve gotten too emotional these past few days. Something is wrong.”

Aldric smiled wryly. "Perhaps you're becoming more human."

"No," Daruk glanced at him. "It's not that. I—"

"You think something is wrong with your abilities." Aldric said. "You believe if you were with Aodhán you would have found the answer. You believe if you don't figure it out, you're heading for a limit."

Daruk let out a shuddering breath and closed his eyes. “I don’t know what to do.”

Aldric nodded. "We'll figure it out together. Aodhán would be sorely disappointed if he finds you limited when he returns."

"You don't know that," Daruk shook his head. "You don't know that he will return."

Aldric smiled and ruffled his brother's hair. "Sometimes, Daruk, you forget that while you endured more with Aodhán, I know him more than you do." His smile brightened. "Far more than you can even imagine."
