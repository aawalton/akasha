'I can't afford to waste time. Every hour counts if I want to hit Level 50 before the next reset.'

Sora woke at 4 a.m., full of energy. He geared up in silence, grabbed the sleeping Hiyoko and left the inn quietly. The streets of Barcelona were still draped in shadows, the city still sleeping. He went to the [Auction Hall] to buy supplies for his trip.

His first destination was the [Combat Training Center]. Barcelona being the biggest city in the region, it had perks smaller cities dreamed of—namely, a library of classes that was unmatched. Sora knew exactly what he was lacking. In his previous cycle, his body had struggled to keep up with his mana-fueled movements.

'I want a body that can actually house my speed without falling apart,' he thought.

He had become addicted to high-velocity combat. The movement skills he'd honed left him hungry for more—he wanted a class that would push his physical limits to the edge.

The center was one of the few places with early-morning activity. A few players and NPCs were already moving and hitting training dummies. Sora approached the central console, but as the seemingly endless list of classes began to scroll, his head began to hurt. The sheer volume of data was staggering. There were thousands of paths, each with its own niche.

"Need help?" a voice piped up from behind him.

Sora turned to see a kid, maybe twelve or thirteen, watching him with a curious look. The kid stood out in the near-empty hall, especially given how intently he was observing Sora.

"Uh, yeah," Sora admitted, glancing back at the console. "There are way too many classes. I'm having trouble finding something that fits."

The kid chuckled. "I figured. You looked like you were about to break the interface!"

"You’re used to seeing people in my situation?"

"More than you'd think," the kid said, stepping closer. "I know a lot about the classes here. I even sell booklets summarizing what each one does. You tell me what you're looking for, and I'll help you find something. Gotta make a living, right?"

Sora appreciated the hustle and smiled. "Alright. I'm looking for a class that focuses on body strength. Something that can push my physical limits but still has good potential for growth."

The kid nodded, rummaging through his bag. He pulled out a small stack of booklets. "I got a few that might interest you. Take a look."

Sora read from the first page, and turned the pages one by one. All basic skills were introduced, and many things were summarized for Sora to understand quickly.

[Martial Titan]

Focus: Extreme physical strength and endurance.

Main Abilities:

Titan Strength: Grows stronger with each training.

Infinite life: Body cells regenerate as long as the energy supply exists.

Body Forging: After recovering from damage, the body gets stronger.

[War Monk]

Focus: Combines martial arts with chi control for balance and power.

Abilities:

Inner Focus: Boosts strength and reaction speed.

Stone Palm: Devastating strike that breaks defenses.

Body Meditation: Rapid health and stamina regeneration.

[Blood Berserker]

Focus: Uses pain and blood to fuel the body in battle.

Abilities:

Bloodlust: Grows stronger the more damage taken.

Berserk Mode: Massive boosts in power at the cost of defense and health.

Regenerative Rage: Converts damage into health.

[Iron Fist Disciple]

Focus: Master of hand-to-hand combat with lightning-fast reflexes.

Abilities:

Unyielding Grip: Crushes through armor and weapons.

Lightning Reflexes: Counters attacks with perfect timing.

Fist of Fury: Rapid combo that grows in power depending on the amount of hits.

[Juggernaut]

Focus: Tank-like resilience and resistance. Focuses on Stamina, defense and crowd control.

Abilities:

Unstoppable Charge: Smashes through enemies and obstacles.

Endurance Mastery: Greatly increases toughness.

Reckless Smash: Unstoppable strike.

[Weaponless Warlord]

Focus: Uses their body as the ultimate weapon.

Abilities:

Bone Shatter: Powerful blows that break the enemy and the user’s bones.

Limitless Endurance: Never tires in battle.

Warrior's Resolve: Heals wounds by converting battle energy.

[Flesh Shaper]

Focus: Can reshape and enhance their body for battle.

Abilities:

Bone Blade: Grow natural weapons like claws or spikes.

Muscle Surge: Temporarily boosts strength and speed.

Chitin Armor: Hardens skin to form armor.

[Void Guardian]

Focus: Enhances the body using void energy to become untouchable.

Abilities:

Void Infusion: Temporarily strengthens the body.

Phantom Stride: Phase through matter to become untouchable.

Gravity Crash: Amplifies weight and impact of attacks.

Sora's eyes lit up as he read through the descriptions. These classes were exactly what he had been hoping to find—each one with the potential to make his body the perfect tool for combat. They all sounded promising. Too promising.

"These are all solid," Sora admitted, impressed by the kid's knowledge. "But what are these descriptions? Untouchable? Ultimate Weapon? Extreme? Aren’t you exaggerating?”

The kid grinned, “Maybe… Slightly?”

Sora smiled, “I need something that can make my body able to adapt to high speed. I want to be fast, not just strong."

The kid thought for a second before saying. "Then you'll probably want to focus on either the War Monk or the Void Guardian. Both classes have movement techniques that make them really fast, but in different ways." He added : "For infinite growth of your body, nothing beats the Martial Titan. But that one is known to hurt a looooot !! Of course, if it is speed that you're pursuing, you're looking in the wrong section my friend, the potential of the classes in the assassin, and Fury section is way higher in terms of speed. Let me show you two more booklets about speed related classes"

Sora nodded, feeling a surge of excitement. "How much for these booklets?"

"Fifty gold each," the kid replied. "But I'll throw in the advice for free."

Sora laughed, before paying. "Deal."

Sora took a look at the last 2 booklets :

[Stormrunner]

Focus: Mobility through electricity. Stormrunners harness the power of lightning to enhance their movement.

[Flash Reaper]

Focus: Pure burst speed. Flash Reapers have the ability to harness explosive speed, using it to overwhelm enemies in a short amount of time.

The kid continued, “Of course, everyone knows the drawbacks of these classes. They are too extreme, sacrificing too much to achieve their goal, which means their users lack in all other areas," the kid reminded kindly. It seemed he added these two booklets to show off his knowledge or earn an extra 100 gold coins.

Sora didn’t bother with reading the details of these classes. “This isn’t what I’m looking for. I want to focus on my body and gain speed through my body level.”

Sora thanked him, paid, and stayed in place to think. After a few minutes, he ended up choosing the [Martial Titan] to lay the basis for a strong body before pursuing anything else.

‘During this life, I’ll build up some endurance, and push my body beyond its limits, to see if this leads anywhere.’ He had a smile on his face as he got his new class. He soon left the center.

He had prepared thoroughly for the trip ahead. His bag was packed with a month's worth of supplies, enough to last through any unexpected detours or extended battles. Alongside that, he carried an assortment of items for different situations—tools for all his level 1 harvesting jobs, healing potions, [Trump Cards], and food for both himself and Hiyoko. Most importantly, he had an updated map that marked every monster zone from Spain to France, especially the ones near his destination: Alès.

From Barcelona to Alès, it wasn't too far. Around four thousand kilometers. But with the inevitable detours and avoiding high-level zones, the total distance stretched to about six thousand kilometers. Even with his impressive stats, he missed his [Mana Amplifier] skills, [Speed Surge], [Amplify], and [Mana Shield]. Without them, he felt naked. Weak. Slow.

Stepping into the first low-level zone outside the city, Sora couldn't shake the emptiness left by the absence of those abilities. His body felt sluggish, his movements lacking the explosive speed he had once mastered. He realized quickly that the first thing he needed to do was recover those skills.

Sora stopped in the middle of a field, focusing on his mana. He recalled every aspect of [Speed Surge]—how it felt to channel mana into his body, how fast it made him. He remembered how, at his peak, he had modified the skill so that he could channel it to specific body parts, enhancing only what was necessary. It had become more than just a buff.

"I can do this again," he muttered, gathering and guiding his mana.

He pictured the skill in its entirety, concentrating the energy into his legs, feeling the familiar hum of power building up inside him. And when he tried to activate it... it worked. The system labeled it as [Fake Speed Surge], but he was overjoyed. He let out a sigh of relief, feeling some of the weight on his shoulders disappearing.

"Fake or not," he said to himself, "I recreated it." The real work began now.

Sora knew how to fix the fake skill. It wasn't the first time he'd faced this. The key was to constantly refine the mana flow, study every interaction between the mana and his body, and improve the efficiency with which he controlled it. He didn't deactivate the skill. Instead, he left [Fake Speed Surge] running, analyzing the way mana coursed through him. Every movement, every adjustment in his posture, was a chance to learn.

As he kept moving, he felt the flow becoming smoother. In less than twenty minutes, the system updated, notifying him that his skill had shifted from Fake to [Alternate Speed Surge].

He beamed a smile at Hiyoko who was cheering on him. "Thank you."

With [Alternate Speed Surge] active, he was much faster than before, and as fast as the real [Speed Surge]. But it wasn't enough. He knew that an alternate skill wasn't the true peak. He needed to push it further, turn it into something flawless.

‘I’ll do it later.’ However, he wasn’t in a hurry. ‘I need to get [Amplify] and [Mana Shield] back first.’

During the next hour, he recreated both alternate versions of his skills and began working on the final step : perfecting them. He focused on his training, and his skills.

He soon forgot about Rachel, about the players he had to kill, and even his parents.

The first thing he did was activate [Alternate Mana Shield] and [Alternate Amplify]. His goal was to keep them active while focusing on the third skill.

For [Speed Surge], he sprinted across the field like a madman, dodging between trees and rocks, using his mana to guide his legs faster, sharper, until every step was perfectly synchronized. As he moved, he experimented with the mana structure, refining the skill, until it felt like his body and mana were aligned.

Hours passed as he trained, only his body limiting his speed. The sun began to rise, but Sora did not notice. He started hunting monsters, and every bit of progress he made was made with increased power, and increased damage.

And by noon, something clicked.

He stopped to focus on his energy. Every part of him, from his shoulders to his feet, were trembling. As he directed the energy, and drove it through his body for a few minutes, the system informed him of his achievement.

[Skill Unlocked: Speed Surge (Perfect)]

Sora activated the skill and felt an insane increase in speed, "That's more like it."

With [Speed Surge] mastered again, he moved on to the next skill, [Mana Shield], and [Amplify].

As time passed, and sun rose the next day, Sora relaxed.

He had done it. In a single day, he had recreated and perfected his three [Mana Amplifier] core skills—[Speed Surge], [Mana Shield], and [Amplify]. Now, they were perfected and reached a new peak.
