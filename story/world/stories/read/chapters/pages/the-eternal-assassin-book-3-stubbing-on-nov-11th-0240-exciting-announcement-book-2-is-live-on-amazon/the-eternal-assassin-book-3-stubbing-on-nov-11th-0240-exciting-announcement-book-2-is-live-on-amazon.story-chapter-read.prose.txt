Hey everyone, the day is finally here! Again!

Three months has flown by in the blink of an eye (seriously, I'm getting old over here), and book 2 for The Eternal Assassin, Fall of the Guild, has officially been published. It is available to be read in all its fancy glory.

-Kindle Edition: Professionally edited and much cleaner final version of book 2's content

-Paperback: Hold the story in your own hands and smell that fresh assassin-y smell! (I realized that was probably not the best choice of adjective as I wrote it, but you know what, I'm sticking with it)

-Audiobook: Unfortunately, I have been informed that the audio for book 2 has been delayed by a little bit, so no link to that one just yet. Turns out the one downside of getting a popular narrator to do your audiobooks is that they are really busy. I'll let you guys know as soon as that is available!

The launch for book 1 went better than I could have ever hoped for, and I really appreciate all of you guys who helped make that happen. Because of that, I am stepping out of my comfort zone yet again and humbly asking you guys for your support with this launch as well. I know I mentioned before that if the series does well we could potentially see some crazy stuff happening down the line like hard covers, comics, or more, and while it's definitely a baby step, I can confirm we have already taken our first step toward something unexpected! Aethon has informed me that the series is in the middle of being translated into German to reach further audiences, which is definitely not something I ever expected to happen! And that's after only a few months!

If you happen to be in a position to be able to help with the launch of book 2 and would like to, just like with book 1, there are a few simple ways you can go about doing so:

-If you have Kindle Unlimited, please think about downloading the book. All downloads count as sales, and if you decide you want to try rereading the now professionally edited version of the story, that would be just icing on the cake! Amazon's ratings and reviews continue to be as wonky as ever, so if anyone does decide to give it a read or even just download it, any and all ratings/reviews would be massively appreciated.

-Simply spread the word about the story! Like I mentioned before, Royal Road is a lot smaller than Amazon (shocker, I know). Word of mouth goes a long way, so don't be afraid to tell people about the super-cool Reaper and his attempts to greet people with the pointy end of his daggers. Here are some promotional posts I've put up on r/LitRPG and r/ProgressionFantasy that you're welcome to share if you'd like.
