For a long, disturbed moment, Samantha and Palthennius looked at each other. The Heaven Knight seemed reluctant to meet her eyes, but he did, as if he felt it was suddenly necessary after his revelation.

His blue eyes were cool and calm. Resigned. His soul was bared to her, and it was a powerful one indeed.

Danger and poise filled the air, like the energy of two halves of a planet facing each other between them and pressing, promising a sudden storm of violence at any moment.

Samantha saw horror and pain in those eyes. They stretched back far further than one life. The shadow over him cast across the same vastness of time she’d seen in so many others. This was indeed a man on the edge, one who’d been foe and one who’d been friend. A man of deception, a soul with many faces. On all sides of the war, the actions between them shaped his destiny. He would always react. Fuck things up with him, and the other side would get him. One side would suffer.

Staring into his soul, Samantha developed a little, cruel smile.

They fucked up.

The voice within her spoke again. ‘I brought all that was left to fracture into dust back under our flag, their dry and abused hearts thirsty for the meaning you deprived them. The meaning they took for granted without me. Do you see? See how they leapt for my bosom at my faintest whisper? You offered them… nothing. Absolutely nothing, through all that time. You took what was free for your winning, and you just smashed it, as I knew you would without me.’

Palthennius was far from relaxed at seeing that smile and the absent look, unsure how to react to the pleasure of a goddess he instinctively regarded as something like a predator. He remained an animal in a cage with an open door, and Samantha was in the way, an unclear agent of aid or abuse.

Oh yes, he saw something horrifying in her eyes, right then.

Samantha cleared her throat and slowly raised her hands up flat, turning the smile into something more friendly. “You’ll have to forgive my alarm. What you just shared… makes you one of the rather scarce top-tier dangers on this planet. Nonetheless, I am inclined to have your aid, in principle. Trust is difficult, as I’m sure you can guess.”

Palthennius nodded slowly, gradually managing to tear his eyes from hers to write. ‘I WILL SWEAR WHATEVER OATH YOU REQUIRE, AS I KNOW DEITIES TO BE INCLINED TOWARD, THANKS TO THE SYSTEM-ENFORCED CURSES THEY CAN LEVERAGE ON BREAKING. IT MAY SEEM I AM AN OATHBREAKER TO BE HERE, BUT THEY BROKE THEIRS FIRST. WHAT THEY CONSPIRE HAS NO LEGALITY. THEY’D LIKELY DENY AND COVER IT UP AFTERWARD. SOMEHOW.’

“We shall see, my son. So, it’s true, then? They plan to turn everyone into zombies?”

‘YES. AN ANCIENT CONTINGENCY NEVER USED, OR SO THE INFORMATION WENT. BEING YOUNG DESPITE MY RANK, A LOT WAS KEPT FROM ME THAT THE MORE EXPERIENCED KNEW. I WAS WARNED BY SOMEONE HIGHER WHO KNEW MY FATHER. A CHANCE LOYALTY. A MONKEY WRENCH IN THEIR PLOT TO MAKE ME THE CHAMPION OF THE UNDEAD. A DEATH KNIGHT.’

“Damn. That… yeah. That would’ve probably sucked for us. Just a bit.”

‘INDEED. IRONICALLY, MY BIGGEST WEAKNESS IS PHYSICAL DURABILITY. THAT WOULD FIX IT. I BELIEVE I AM A HUMBLE MAN, YOUR MAJESTY. BUT ME, AS THAT, TERRIFIES ME IN THE SENSE OF THE POWER POSSIBLE. IT WAS ALMOST CERTAINLY A BIG PART OF THEIR ENTIRE MOTIVATION FOR THIS MOVE.’

“Well, egg on their faces, now! Their gamble cost them. Out of curiosity… Do you need to stay away from there? To keep them from doing this unwillingly?”

Pathennius paused, looking at her in some surprise before jotting down, ‘YOU ARE ALREADY ENTERTAINING SENDING ME AGAINST MATRAZERE, YOUR MAJESTY?’

“It’s simply a matter for consideration. Wishful thinking, even.”

‘WHATEVER THEIR PLOT WAS, I DON’T KNOW, BUT IT WOULD REQUIRE SOMETHING SPECIAL IN PREPARATION. THEY WERE DELIBERATELY FORCING ME TO STAY THERE. PERHAPS THEY WOULD DRUG ME, OR SIMPLY HAVE ME REPORT TO THE RITUAL ROOM, ALREADY IN PROGRESS. WHAT WOULD I DO, BUT OBEY? BUT NOW, I DON’T SEE HOW IT IS POSSIBLE. I’M NOT IN THEIR POWER, AND IF KEPT HIDDEN, THEY WON’T EVEN EXPECT MY RETURN. THEY CAN’T JUST FLIP A SWITCH, OR THEY WOULD HAVE ONCE THEY KNEW I’D CAUGHT WIND OF THINGS. INSTEAD, THEY TRIED TO CAPTURE ME WITH A TOO-LARGE HORDE OF FOES I SAW COMING A LEAGUE AWAY. I FLED, LEADING THEIR FASTEST ON A MERRY CHASE.’

Samantha smirked. “Yeah, I hear you’re pretty damn slippery.” Palthennius huffed in rare amusement and nodded, looking down. Samantha continued, “Alright, I’m going to assign some Servitor guards here and get this tent better warded, and we’ll take this whole matter slowly. I think you need to rest. If you’re burning to provide something, I suppose you can write.”

With a wave of her hand, Samantha conjured some notebooks and pens and made them all fully permanent to boot. “Get a little rest in, at least. We’ll handle things later. I have a… guy, for oaths and contracts. It works out better that way.”

Palthennius glanced at her and nodded appreciatively, then wrote. ‘I APPRECIATE THE LEAP OF FAITH YOU’RE MAKING. I HAVE NO INTENTION OF BETRAYING YOU. I’VE ALREADY MADE MY BED.’

“Mmn. I have to ask… if you have any ethics at all, how did you do what you did for so long? For them.”

Palthennius was a long time in answering this, but finally held up the notebook. ‘THE DOMINION PORTRAYS RIGHTEOUSNESS IN THEIR ACTIONS. AN ADHERENCE TO A HIGHER, DIVINE LAW. MANY THINGS ARE CALLED ‘UNFORTUNATE REALITIES OF SOCIETY,’ SUCH AS DEALING WITH CRIME. PUTTING DOWN REBELLIONS. ALL FOR THE GREATER GOOD. PRESERVING ORDER, WHICH HUMANITY NEEDS. THESE PRINCIPLES ARE HOLY, PRESCRIBED FROM HEAVEN, WHICH IS COMPOSED OF THE COLLECTIVE HIGHER MIND OF US ALL, OF WHICH THE SAGES ARE BETTER TAPPED INTO. NOT THAT I’VE BELIEVED THAT BEYOND MY YOUTH.’

Ironic. The higher plane is where I reside. He resides somewhere else. “Mmn. It’s about what I expected you to say. But what changed?”

‘DOUBT WORMED ITS WAY IN. I BECAME JADED YEARS BEFORE THIS WAR. THINGS I’VE SEEN SINCE WORE ON ME EVEN FURTHER. OUR HANDLING OF THE PEASANTRY ALONG THE WAY. FIGHTING AZURE. LOSING. SEEING THE WHOLE OF THE SUPPOSEDLY SAGELY LEADERSHIP PANIC AND COMMITTING WRONG AFTER WRONG. MY RESPECT, FAITH, AND SENSE OF JUSTICE IN WHAT WE DO WANED FURTHER. DESCENDED INTO BITTERNESS. BY THE END, MERELY DUTY HELD ME, AND THEY THREW EVEN THAT ON THE HEAP TO ROT. THERE CAN BE NO EXCUSE FOR WHAT THEY AIM TO DO, NOR FORGIVENESS. A LINE HAS BEEN CROSSED.’

Samantha nodded slowly without saying anything.

Palthennius wrote a bit more. ‘WHAT IS TRUE OF WHAT RUMORS EXISTED ABOUT YOU AND THIS REBEL CIVILIZATION YOU DESIGN, I DON’T CARE. THEY WERE CERTAINLY MIXED. I DON’T EXPECT GOODNESS HERE. I AM NO LESS JADED TO DELIVER MYSELF TO SPIRITS AND DEMONS. I WAS DOOMED AND DAMNED ALREADY. I AM HERE TO STOP THE SAGES IN THEIR HEINOUS PLANS, AND FAILING THAT, TO PUNISH THEM AS MUCH AS POSSIBLE FOR WHAT THEY’VE DONE. IT'S AS SIMPLE AS THAT.’

Samantha smiled faintly. “You don’t actually think we’ll win. You believe we’ll fail at Matrazere.”

Palthennius met her eyes and hesitated, looking away briefly. He shook his head and wrote quickly, ‘I DON’T THINK IT’S POSSIBLE. APOLOGIES, YOUR MAJESTY. THE SUN RISES IN THE MORNING, AND MATRAZERE HOLDS.’

“But you’ll fight anyway, to whatever degree you can. To the death, if I let you.”

Palthennius met her eyes unflinchingly this time and simply nodded.

A knight ready to fall on his sword for the last sliver of his sense of righteousness. I guess we’ll see. He’s the type to find it difficult to die even if he tried.

“Don’t be alarmed when the wards go up,” Samantha said. “Otherwise, I’ll leave you to some quietude for now, and discuss you with my leaders. I’m sure we can arrange for a voice-changing enchantment or item, and otherwise hide who you are in the ways you require.”

The notebook was scrawled simply with, ‘THANK YOU, YOUR MAJESTY.’

Samantha smiled cheerily as she half-turned to go. “You’re very welcome!”

News of who they had in that tent set the Brain Trust on fire with alarm and excitement. Ash said ‘no’ in disbelief like five times, slightly more drawn out each time. Dax wanted to put up a force wall dome over the tent and station minotaur guards around it, which Samantha shot down immediately as way too obvious for spies. Canmore wanted to duel him, which Dax shot down immediately. Tashome wanted to talk to him about his damn sword, of all things.

I swear, I run a madhouse!

Samantha calmed them all down and got them to conspiring with their resident lawyer-cat, Sancus, to draw up all the oaths and contract-type shit they needed. Palthennius was indeed something like having a live bomb in the camp — if he suddenly decided he didn’t like them, the damage he could do in an ambush could be catastrophic. He might have the potential to assassinate Dax, for instance.

As such, they needed a pretty nasty contract if they wanted to utilize him. His very thoughts would need to trigger alerts and responses. He was too damned fast and deadly. They’d need forewarning if he turned on them, and a nasty curse to debilitate him. These were non-negotiable if he wanted to fight. She could believe he was truthful all day, and her vibes were nearly sacrosanct, but he was exceptionally high level, in fact, level 20 in one class, she was pretty sure. On top of it, he had powerful illusions and cloning powers, which meant he was a tip-top deceiver.

He’d gotten himself out of bloody Matrazere of all places, when he was probably the only one they truly cared deeply about not allowing to escape. All of their efforts had probably been for him, and they’d still failed. It was kind of ludicrous. So… no, they’d definitely need to act with an abundance of caution with him going forward, however much of an irresistible asset he was.

She was absolutely going to use him, though. Since when did she resist dangerous temptations, after all?
