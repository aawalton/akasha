When Liu Shu started building the <Temple Of Destiny>, she focused wholly on building it. She even closed the <Core Room>, not allowing anyone to bother her. Liu Shu knew from the <Temple Of Summoning> that each of the Temple-Level structures was going to be essential for her future achievements.

When creating a tree structure, Liu Shu first needs to create a seed by refining different materials and using her unique type of World Tree Aether Mana to stimulate it. Once she did so, the seed would gain life and would start growing. Most of the highest-level materials would be used during this first step.

The second step was transforming the other materials into essence and slowly and gently pouring them into the tree structure in a specific order and at a specific pace. The process needed to be done slowly and carefully since a single misstep could affect the final outcome. Thankfully for Liu Shu, <World Tree Wisdom> helped a lot by neatly compiling the information about it and her previous experiences.

Therefore, until now, Liu Shu has yet to fail in building a tree structure, even when, theoretically, the success rate was not one hundred percent.

Her Aether Mana and Anima Energy constantly poured into the building, and the seed sprouted, growing at a tremendous pace. The tree didn’t just grow vertically as its body and bark expanded around it, creating a structure from the bark. The combination of tree and building was mystical yet pleasant to the eyes.

The <Temple Of Destiny> had beautiful white and pink tones of bark highlighting the corners and borders of the building. Overall, the main color was a light brown, and the bark was also smooth and polished. The canopy of the massive tree was filled with Peach Blossom like leaves and with white flowers.

A few months passed when Liu Shu finished the building and nourished it to her own level. Once she did so, an aura of power swept around the entire city, originating from the <Temple>.

<Temple Oracle Runic Field>: The <Temple Of Destiny> will be able to have an appointed <Sylvan Oracle>. The <Sylvan Oracle> will receive several personal blessings, and they will be able to grant buffs and curses affecting <Destiny>.

Liu Shu read all the messages slowly, and her mind became tangled. ‘Ugh… both of them are so good!’ She knew how important powerhouses were in the World. For example, a single Third Energy Cycle would be able to deter a group of high-leveled Second Energy Cycles from attacking even when they could win. After all, sacrifices were almost inevitable facing such a disparity in powers.

Before she continued thinking, she asked a very important question. ‘System, can a World Tree be chosen as the <Sylvan Oracle>?’

[No. Only a being from the <Sylvan> race can be chosen as the <Sylvan Oracle>.]

Liu Shu grumbled. ‘Tsk. What a shame.’

With that option out of the list, Liu Shu became much less excited about the second option. ‘If I choose the second Runic Field, I will be able to create a powerhouse. Most likely, they will grow as fast as I do, and they will become extremely powerful, being able to affect thousands of beings as my influence and the influence of the <Temple Of Destiny> grows. However… what if this person is killed?’

Liu Shu sighed in annoyance just thinking about that situation. ‘If that person is killed, all the resources spent on them will be for naught. Of course, this logic applies to all powerhouses. However, this one is specially nurtured to be part of a central building of my domain. The resources are naturally incomparable compared to others. Not to mention, that person will for sure be someone I care about, so their death will also hurt me emotionally… I might even develop the risk of being afraid to develop attachment towards them, creating a strange wall between.’

She then looked at the other option. ‘Meanwhile, the <Blessed and Cursed Bark Runic Field> will benefit us greatly. <Blessed Bark> will surely be a high-ranked material that will follow the <Temple Of Destiny>’s <Tier> as it develops. Adding it to accessories and other types of small items is neat and can create another attraction that makes other powers reluctant to attack me. As for the <Cursed Bark>...’ Liu Shu smirked maliciously. ‘Heh. That’s an item with many possibilities.’’

If Liu Shu had to summarize both options, it would be a balanced option against a high-risk, high-reward option.

She pondered deeply, and eventually, she decided to go with the <Blessed and Cursed Bark Runic Field>. She reasoned that the benefits of this one field would be much more tangible, and she would also be able to aid her closest people equally. Not to mention, the <Cursed Bark> was a fascinating material with a myriad of uses for schemes.

Moreover, as another drawback of the Oracle option, she mused that if she chose one of the four Sylvans, the other three would nag her to death! Then, if she decided for a Sylvan outside those four… Liu Shu felt a strange shiver of fear spread through her body. ‘They will bully me until I ask for forgiveness!’

After the reasoning was done, she informed the system. ‘System, I want the <Blessed and Cursed Bark Runic Field>.’
