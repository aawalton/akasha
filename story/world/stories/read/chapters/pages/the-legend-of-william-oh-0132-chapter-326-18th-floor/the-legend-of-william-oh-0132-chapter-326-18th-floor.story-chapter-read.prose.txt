Sammohan came away with about six dozen dimensional pearls, and Will did him a favor and cracked them all open, being one of the few people who could open them without risking his fingers to dimensional ripples.

The majority of the pearls had nothing, but two had weapons with minor dimensional enhancement, and the last one had a belt buckle that made whatever belt it was attached to weightless, which, after some experimentation, included anything strapped to the belt as well.

That was valuable for merchants and scouts who liked to keep things light, but it wasn’t earth-shattering.

It would sell fairly well down in the lower Floors, were the lower Floors not currently falling apart.

Despite not having a strong need for it, Will bought the buckle and spent his free time studying the miasmatic structures inside the slotless item.

The buckle seemed like a seed, and when it was attached to a leather belt, that seed would sprout roots that wove their way through the belt, creating nodules on the belt-loops.

Curious, Will tried attaching objects to the belt itself and not the loops, and the weight negation was drastically diminished.

Will took sketches of the miasmatic structures and when he was done experimenting, simply replaced his belt buckle, because why not?

After a few minutes, Will switched back, because not being able to feel the tomahawk on his belt made him distinctly uncomfortable.

Eventually, he gave the buckle to Beth, who used it to decrease the weight of her gun, which was much heavier relative to her own size.

In fact, after some experimenting, she looped the belt tight around the stock and made the slugger weightless, which played strangely with the weapon’s natural inclination to resist movement.

Once everyone was dry, they set off to the 17th Floor, where the trapped Climbers were still preparing to head back down. Will informed them that the Coil was ending and the lower floors were getting more dangerous, but they wanted to go home more than they were concerned about possibly dying, so there wasn’t much Will could do to stop them.

They gave the group some Relics and supplies before clearing another Key Site and moving on to the next Floor.

As both a Lord and a scout, Will went through the glowing orange Door to the 18th Floor first.

Going from the chilly grasslands of the 17th Floor, the temperature increased by a few degrees, but the moisture did as well. Will was in a young forest, the sun was high overhead, There was a muggy smell of rot.

Will’s nose tingled and he idly rubbed it.

The tingle came back and Will rubbed it harder, still keeping his eyes open for any sign of an enemy.

The tingling turned into a burning itch, which demanded Will’s attention.

What the…

The tingle spread from his nose into his nasal passages and down into his lungs, causing him to double over with a harsh cough.

Will summoned his internal map as he felt pressure begin to build in his face.

What is going on!?

Nodules inside Will’s lungs were visibly growing, where specks of dust so small that he wouldn’t have been able to see them with his naked eyes had stuck in the mucus membranes.

These rapidly growing nodules extended through his sinuses, and when he had take a breath to cough with his mouth, it had gotten in his mouth and lungs.

I’m going to choke to death.

Will spotted the other doors open only a few seconds behind him on his map, watching as the others stepped through.

“Don’t breathe!” Will shouted with the last bit of his voice before it was choked off.

Need a solution.

Will grabbed Sommahan, Jorn, Loth, and Beth, dragging them towards Mason as quickly as possible, his vision gradually turning red as his throat began to close off.

I should be able to hold my breath for over half an hour, but not even having a chance to take a breath is cutting that time down to a third.

And these growths aren’t stopping, Will thought, breaking out in a cold sweat as he watched the cancerous masses combine and gain size inside his own lungs.

Will stopped in front of Mason, whose hand was clapped over his mouth and nose.

Bad air. Will gave the climber-sign before diving into his friend’s chest, dragging everyone with him.

Will rolled to a stop in the grass field, immediately turning his attention to his inner map.

I need to cut these growths out of my lungs and restore my ability to breathe, Will thought, turning Phantom Hand’s armor into a scalpel.
