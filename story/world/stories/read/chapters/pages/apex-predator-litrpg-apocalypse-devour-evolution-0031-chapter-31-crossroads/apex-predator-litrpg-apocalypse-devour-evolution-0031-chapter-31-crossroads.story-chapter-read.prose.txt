Don’s concentration was starting to wane. He had been at this for almost two hours. His Energy was at 50%, but his mind was so fatigued. It felt like he was running an ultramarathon, while dieting while studying for medical school. The headache was extending to his eyes, and he kept blinking.

Thank god for eyebrows, I wouldn’t be able to see anything with this sweat.

This wasn’t exactly something Don articulated in his mind, but he was pleased with his eyebrows.

Two blowpipes hovered in front of him. He had managed to suspend them in the air for at least two minutes now. He dropped one. Then turned the other sideways. Then he threw it with his mind. It clacked on the shack hall.

Okay, yeah, that works.

He was close. He just needed one level. With his mind, he started dragging broken spearheads on the floor towards him. Some of them had too much shaft attached, so Don snapped them. Once he had a dozen, he started throwing them at the shack wall with his mind. First from a foot away, then two feet. Tsok, tsok, tsok.

After forty more minutes of throwing the spearheads at the wall with his mind it finally happened.

[Racial Skill Increased: Telekinesis]

[Telekinesis Level 3]

“Fuuuuuuck…..”

Don slumped on the floor. He wished he had something to Devour. Even a puny Vulpin would do. Anything to take the headache and fatigue away.

For a few minutes, Don just rested. He felt like taking a nap, but that would be the most foolish thing he could do in a goddamn dungeon. There were bound to be more Grimoids around. Not that they seemed to want to bother him while he was training. Still, Don wouldn’t take stupid risks. He shuffled and took the water flask and poured half of it on his face.

Worth it.

The fatigue was fading, and that was enough momentum for Don to get up. He had work to do. So much work. That psycho Jacques wasn’t taking naps, that was for sure. Idly, Don wondered how many people that bastard had killed, but quickly pushed the thought away. It really wasn’t any of his business at the end of the day.

Only his progress is. I have to win. And to do that, I have to push forward.

Don picked up one of the spearheads with his mind and levitated it to his hand. That felt awesome. Telekinesis was awesome. Imagine never having to reach for the remote or your phone? Amazing. Too bad remotes and phones were off the menu for the foreseeable future, but if Don could do this with knives, spearheads and arrows, that was a win.

He slid the spearhead under the cinch of one of his satchel belts. Then he took five long steps away from the shack. Around four yards. With his mind he drew the spearhead and it soared through the air.

Tsok.

The spearhead twanged faintly as it vibrated against the wood. That was a good hit. The blade bit deep. The speed was good, the accuracy was good. All that training, and especially that level 3 made a huge difference. Don wasn’t sure if he could call the power increase exponential, but the gap certainly widened with every level.

Don threw another spearhead with this style. Then he tried blowpipe darts. They were tiny and light and throwing them was really easy. He could use telekinesis to pick them from an open satchel and throw three at a time. The speed was solid. Not excellent, but definitely useful in combat. Then Don threw some darts and spearheads while running around. Seemed to work fine after a few times.

“Alright,” he said. “I can work with this.”

It was finally time to look at his evolution choices. Don wasn’t sure if his telekinesis training had done any difference regarding the choices, but damn it, he sure went the extra mile to cover his bases. And so far that had worked out for him in the new reality.

He considered using his three Free Attribute Points, but he wanted to see his evolution options first. Considering how close his fights got, optimization definitely mattered.

Don opened up the Evolution choice interface and started scrolling through the options.

The first one was straight forward, but it was good.

[New Evolution: Ocular Precision (F-grade)]

Your visual cortex adapts to high-speed movement and object tracking. Even under high-stress conditions, your aim and perception remain stable. +2 Mental +2 Agility [Passive]: Improves projectile targeting accuracy and depth tracking. Reduces aiming penalty while moving.

Just a generally good thing to have. Basically it did what Mental did, but it doubled down on it. Solid Attribute boosts too. It would most likely work whether Don was using his body or psychic energy to throw objects. It was basically a coordination buff, something that shouldn’t be underestimated in combat.

[New Evolution: Psionic Core (F-grade)]

Your nervous system adapts to the strain of psychic output. Neural connections across your brain and spinal column thicken and reinforce to better handle ambient and directed psychic forces. +2 Mental +2 Energy[Passive]: Slightly increases psychic ability control and energy stability. Reduces Energy loss from sustained Telekinesis.Scales With: Mental, Energy, [Telekinesis]

Well, Don was now pretty sure his training had paid off in this respect. This was a really solid option if he wanted to lean more into the psychic side of his abilities. He couldn’t deny that having new abilities in his kit was exciting. He was yet to gauge how powerful they would be in actual combat, but this sure would make a difference.

However, it was, just like the Ocular Precision, basic. Sure, the evolution upgrades seemed balanced. Something less powerful was cheaper to upgrade with the Evolution Points. But he would rather try to squeeze all the advantages he could from the System. He moved on with the choices.

[New Evolution: Devouring Physique (F-grade)]

Your digestive system begins processing consumed life force more efficiently. You extract more essence from enemies you Devour. +3 Constitution +1 Power [Passive]: Slight increase in Evolution Point yield per Devour. Slight increase in chance to gain evolution traits from consumed foes. Slight Increases in Health and Energy from using Devour. Scales With: Constitution, [Devour]

Now this was something different. It wasn’t flashy. It wasn’t psionic, it wasn’t anything that directly helped him in combat. But damn, was it good. Don had to look at the big picture. Meeting Mal’asha had put things in perspective. He could live up to hundreds of years, devouring planets and stuff. It was all very high-minded. What if this was the last time he was offered this? He would go on to devour thousands or even millions of beings, and if he neglected to take this, he would leave a staggering amount of resources on the table.

However… It gave him barely any combat power right now. And he needed to live right now. One might think he should just auto-pick this one, but if he took something that gave him an immediate combat edge, that would also snowball, it would just be more subtle. He would kill things that much faster, be that much safer. All of which would lead to him killing everything in the future with more speed and efficiency with less downtime and hesitation.

But he really liked the idea of increased scaling for the rest of his life…

Reluctantly Don scrolled down to the last option.

[New Evolution: Psionic Venom (F-grade)]

Your claws exude a toxin not of flesh, but of mind. On contact, psychic venom seeps into the target’s nervous system, disrupting cognition and emotional regulation. +2 Mental +2 Agility [Passive]: Claw attacks inflict [Psychic Poison I] — a stacking debuff that causes disorientation, delayed reactions, and reduced resistance to psychic effects. Particularly effective against targets with low Mental. Scales With: Mental, [Unarmed Fighting, Telekinesis]

“Oh no…” Don groaned. “This one is good too.”

This was more on-hands than the passive psionic buff. The passive psionic buff would probably be more useful in the long run though. When Don was throwing boulders and stuff like that. But as long as he was primarily fighting with melee, using this venom could be huge. He could further enhance his hit and run tactics. And apparently the poison would reduce his enemies’ ability to resist his psychic techniques, such as telekinesis. He could eventually just take a swipe at an enemy and push and pull them around as he pleased. Just being able to lift a melee-based enemy off its feet would be a massive advantage.

“Okay… Do I take the immediate advantage or the scaling one?”

He was really feeling the Devour upgrade. Just being able to get more juice out of every kill would stack up fast.

Do I actually have to pull some napkin math out of my ass here? Math? In my apocalypse?

Math aside, he would feel really stupid getting his innards ripped to pieces by a mutated flaming hyena or whatnot if he didn’t pick the immediate, direct power up two hours later.

“Goddamn it… I’ll just have to choose and live with it.”
