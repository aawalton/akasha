“Welcome, everyone, to the second day of the finals!”

The crowd erupted. They were packed as ever. Rollo Frollo looked around, beaming a pearly white smile at the thousands of spectators he’d charmed.

“Only eight finalists remain! They’ve all earned twenty drops of Muscle Mass and two thousand aura stones, but those who win today will secure even more rewards! And then more! It’s the clan that never stops giving, the goose with the golden spears!”

A few laughed. Gary sank in his seat, sighing with exhaustion. He struggled to focus. The training had taken a toll on him, and while he’d managed to sleep, one night couldn’t balance weeks of continuous strain.

“You know what I just realized? This might be your last night ever,” Cog said.

“Cog!” Alicia said, slapping his shoulder.

“What? I’m just saying, maybe we should go out or something.”

“I need to train precisely so it isn’t the last night,” Gary replied tiredly.

“Right. You’re an all or nothing kind of guy. I dig that.”

“Please welcome our first challengers: Lin Xin versus Wei Roy!”

A man who looked part-Asian stepped out to meet Lin Xin. While their battle was ongoing, Gary received a message.

Chloe: Hey Gary! Asim and I couldn’t make it today, but you have our full support. Don’t you dare lose!

He couldn’t quell the disappointment in his heart. This could be the day he died, and his friends didn’t even show up…

Ok, he replied, then returned to watching. Exclamation rose from the crowd. Lin Xin had erupted with a new level of power, capitalizing on a single misstep to dominate her opponent. Snow battered him alongside spear strikes until his back was frozen against the wall. A spear stopped inches from his throat.

“Lin Xin! Lin Xin!” the crowd chanted.

“The winner is Lin Xin!” Rollo Frollo shouted, his voice failing to pierce the cries.

She withdrew her spear and bowed to her opponent. “Well fought,” she said.

Wei Roy had to do the same. “Thank you for your guidance,” he said, clearly upset. As both jumped to their seats, Rollo Frollo kept speaking.

“The young prodigy of the Lin family triumphs again! She only joined the clan four months ago, but with her heaven-grade talent, her rise has been meteoric! Can she surpass the previous generation? We’re about to find out! Tomorrow, she’s facing Feng Shen in a pitched battle of generations. Some say that could be the real final. Don’t miss it!”

“What a jerk,” Alicia muttered. “Feng Shen hasn’t even won yet. It’s like his opponent doesn’t exist.”

“There no way he’s losing,” Gary said.

“That doesn’t make it nice!”

“Speak of the devil!” Rollo Frollo exclaimed. “Ladies and gentlemen, I present you the number one seed for this tournament! Boasting an invincible record, all his matches ending in a single strike, the man who stood head and shoulders above his peers in every single clan ranking… Feng Shen!”

Feng Shen jumped lightly into the arena and bowed with precisely the depth he ought to. The crowd cheered.

“For his opponent, I give you Sway Pond! The only woman left besides Lin Xin—and not for long—she wields the power of ripples to suffocate her opponents in a never-ending, ever-climaxing barrage!”

A woman of flowing blue hair leaped into the arena, cupping her hands at Feng Shen. “I just had to get matched against you,” she said with a bitter sigh. “I can only blame my luck.”

“Luck is part of a cultivator’s strength. More than that, however, there is skill and strength. You have trained for as long as I have, and your talent is not inferior to mine, yet you cannot meet me in combat. That is not luck. If you want to blame something, blame your lack of diligence.”

The crowd was stunned for a moment, then erupted into jeers. Rollo Frollo laughed. “The number one disciple doesn’t frequent harsh words! We can only assume they’re warranted. Hopefully, today’s loss will be a lesson for poor Sway Pond!”

The woman frowned deeply.

“Fight!” Rollo Frollo exclaimed, and she immediately burst into motion. She thrust rapidly. She hadn’t moved from her original position, however, so her thrusts met only air. Feng Shen stood far away. Where her thrusts ended, the air rippled like water, and those ripples merged to create an area where they absolutely dominated.

“Her signature move, the Ripple Realm!” Rollo Frollo exclaimed. “In the area of her ripples, the ambient aura is completely warped. Even vision is hindered. If someone enters that area without understanding its patterns, their strength will be greatly restricted! All her previous opponents had charged her before she could form it, but Feng Shen…is just sitting there!?”

The crowd cheered hard. Feng Shen stood still, letting Sway Pond charge up to her full power.

“A risky move from the head disciple! Once the Ripple Realm is complete, even he might not escape unscathed…and here it is! It’s fully formed! We have a battle on our hands, ladies and gentlemen!”

The crowd erupted again. A ten-foot area around Sway Pond swimmed with clashing ripples, spreading out in a dozen different alternating patterns at once. Gary got dizzy just watching it.

Feng Shen finally prepared his spear.

“Ripple Rush!” Sway Pond said, running ahead while still thrusting her spear at the air. Somehow, she made the ripples clash in a way that pushed them forward, following her charge.

“It can move!” Rollo Frollo exclaimed. “We didn’t know that! Feng Shen might have intended to wait until she ran out of energy, but that’s not happening! How will the head disciple react? I see that her charge is kind of slow, so he might be able to keep his distance!”

“Shut up!” Sway Pond yelled at him.

Feng Shen leveled his spear at her. He waited calmly. His aura rose but remained contained within his body like taut rubber. The ripples approached him. They licked at his spear. Sway Pond slashed.

Finally, as the ripples were about to overwhelm him, Feng Shen attacked. His thrust was so fast Gary barely caught it. At its apex, an explosion of black and white appeared, creating a rift which grew towards Sway Pond and nullified the ripples through sheer power. The Ripple Realm collapsed. The air was torn apart. Sway Pond brought her spear to block, but the rift exploded on her shaft and sent her rocketing back the entire arena’s length until she smashed into a wall. Sand rose and flew. The audience gasped, then erupted into wild cheers.

“How is this the power of the Birth of Aura realm!?” Rollo Frollo exclaimed, even his voice colored with shock. “This is genius! Pure, unfiltered genius!”

The crowd kept screaming and howling his name. Their cheers rose to the high heavens.

“FENG SHEN! FENG SHEN!”

The sand cleared, revealing a woman barely on her feet. She wore a self-pitying smile. “I accept my defeat,” she said.

“Well fought,” Feng Shen replied, cupping his hands.

“What a magnanimous man! He didn’t struggle at all, yet he still had kind words for his opponent! When power and kindness combine, that is truly the king of a generation! Tomorrow we’ll find out whether he can be the king of two generations!”

The fighters departed under clapping and cheers. “You did well, Sway Pond!”

“Good luck next time!”

“You rippled my heart!”

Alicia squinted in the direction of that last cheer. “Perverts,” she said.

“I forgive him because it was funny,” Cog said.

Before the cheers could end, Sui Duang and his opponent had jumped into the stage. Rollo Frollo didn’t comment much. Sui Duang wasn’t as nonchalant as Feng Shen had been, but his victory wasn’t hard either. The opponent was quickly sliced up by Razor Wave and forced to admit defeat.

Gary had been watching to glean information, but all he managed was to make himself anxious. He took a deep breath and gripped his spear.

“We can do it, Gary!” Goldie exclaimed from his sleeve.

“For the last match of the day, we have something special!” Rollo Frollo announced. “Oracle Jay is the clan’s fifth-ranked disciple, wielding the mysterious power of foresight! He can read his opponents’ move before they even make it, and so far, not a single person has managed to penetrate that power! His nickname is the Bald Prophet. Why Bald? Because he’s hairless!”

Oracle Jay begrudgingly leaped on stage. Muffled laughter came from all around. His fur was growing back, but he still looked terrible—nowhere near the dignified feline he used to be. Everywhere on the stands, furry people of various species stewed in fury.

“Facing him is the man who made him bald—Gary Steel, aptly called the Baldinator!”

“Good luck,” Cog and Alicia said as Gary entered the arena. He was immediately surrounded by clapping, cheering, and yelling. Endless light fell from the ceiling. Standing on the sands felt vastly different than sitting in the crowd, and Gary was momentarily disoriented. He raised a fist. The crowd cheered harder. Banners unfolded and people clanged their drums in his name.

“Baldinator! Baldinator! Baldinator!”

A projection appeared over the arena, showing scenes from Gary’s battle against Oracle Jay in the preliminaries. “Gary Steel defeated the Oracle early in the preliminaries, but he still managed to reach the top eight! Look how pretty the Oracle used to be—nothing like the pink, fleshy man we see today. This isn’t just a battle for revenge, but also for honor! Fate has brought these two together again. Who will emerge superior? There’s only one way to find out!”

People stomped on the stands, shaking the arena. Oracle Jay and Gary exchanged hard glares.

“You will pay for what you did to me,” Oracle Jay said.

“Watch me do it again,” Gary replied.

“Ohhh! A wild taunt from the Baldinator, who promises to reset the Oracle to complete hairlessness! The animosity between these two is real! Thankfully, it’s the Oracle who’s the hurt side, because Gary Steel is addicted to Duels of Honor!”

Gary’s brows twitched. “I’m not!” he yelled, but his voice was overpowered by Rollo’s.

“Fight!”

Oracle Jay drew his spear with a cruel smile. “You’re mine today,” he said.

“Sorry, I don’t lean that way,” Gary replied, drawing the Golden Bolt. Black lightning arced.

The two stared at each other for a moment. The crowd fell quiet. The tension climbed. Gary charged, activating his aura field as he reached the oracle. He pretended to thrust, then stopped a few feet short and discharged black lightning all around him. The oracle had already jumped away. Gary charged again. “Nail Gun!” he yelled, but his countless thrusts were evaded. The oracle’s smirk remained. A single thrust came against Gary’s, timed for a precise instant where he was off-balance. Gary was grazed. The crowd cheered.

The Oracle stopped ten feet away, hopping lightly in place. “What’s wrong, Gary Steel? Come at me. Show me the power of the Baldinator,” he taunted.

Gary frowned deeply.

“How does Oracle Jay’s power work, Master?” he’d asked the previous day.

“Knowing that won’t help you. Act as if he can really read your thoughts,” Colorful had replied.

“But then I’m helpless.”

“No, you’re not.”

Gary gritted his teeth. Damn them all. He put his spear away and charged again, just running ahead full tilt.

“There is it! The Baldinator’s trademark Teeth-First attack!” Rollo Frollo yelled. “This is what took down the Oracle last time! Gary Steel just charged in, took all hits, and shoulder-rammed his opponent into hairlessness! How unbecoming of a spear-wielder—but will it really work twice?”

Oracle Jay’s grin widened. His feet moved in a pattern as if flowing on the sand, taking him away from Gary at the same speed he approached. When he thunderstepped, the oracle predicted his direction and turned smoothly the other way, dodging everything. Even when Gary thunderstepped straight ahead, the oracle jumped into the air, then smoothly glided to a new position without losing speed.

“I must thank you, Gary Steel!” Oracle Jay exclaimed, laughing. “If it wasn’t for you, I wouldn’t have developed this technique. I call it—Jay Walking!”

The audience groaned collectively.

“Wow, that hurt my soul!” Rollo Frollo said.

Gary grunted and dove in, but while he was a little faster than his opponent, Oracle Jay was slippery like a fish. He just couldn’t catch him. Thrusts poked at him throughout. They weren’t particularly strong, but they were made against his high momentum, so even grazing blows hurt. Moreover, they were hard to dodge, always aiming where he could least react.

Gary chased down the oracle for a while. When he stopped, the audience exclaimed in shock.

“He was beaten black and blue!” Rollo Frollo exclaimed.

Gary panted hard. He’d taken several glancing blows and a few real ones. Thankfully, his Vitality far outstripped his opponent’s Strength, but even he had a limit. Everything hurt. His arms were heavy. Worst of all, his chest rose and fell rapidly, his endurance sapped by the constant sprinting and thunderstepping.

“What’s your next trick, clown?” Jay asked, still hopping lightly. He’d spent way less energy than Gary.

Gary thought hard. This isn’t working. What else can I do?

How is he reading my mind?
