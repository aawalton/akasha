He literally couldn’t see anything.

Finally, the nature of his barriers brought him to the current dilemma. Fire and lightning worked pretty well, and with shadow mana being necessary to stay alive, he had to make a conscious choice about primary and secondary elements for his skillset.

When the strength of mana types was considered, he also had no qualms about adding more basic elements into his arsenal if they fit. In short, base elements were just as strong when combined with the versatility of his class.
