Samantha frowned at Glae’s revelation. “Summons? What kind? How many are we talkin’, here?”

Glae shook his head. “I don’t know the numbers, nor details, nor if it will happen at all. I just suspect this eventuality, as I can’t discern the spells — they’re not even spells. Spells will do nothing here if arcane in origin. But like magi would bring ancient magic items with divine magic contained in them, wizards love their backup plans. The legacy summon contracts were one such.

“The Sleeper would not have passed on the knowledge, and likely banned it entirely. The rumors are always of hideous, eldritch things. Demons, in other words — the true kind rather than the semblances of these days, that merely emulated them. I know of only one contract, myself, which is for an imp. I used it experimentally to test my antimagic, and it cuts through every prevention known.

“They are specialists of access, you see. Agents of chaos fit to invade and disrupt. Only a demon’s specific circle can ward it. There is a massive one of those around this area, but I doubt he’d bother with such minor beings. He should know far more powerful versions. Based on how comprehensive his contingencies are on the World Spell, I suspect he added them into the mix, too. It’s one of the few things so important to him, he’d break his general principles to protect it.”

“So, this could be really, really bad, in other words,” Azure said accusingly.

Glae shrugged. “I’m sure the collective might gathered here can handle it. If not, you’ll all perish, and either I manage to finish the ritual, or I don’t. Such is life.”

Azure sniffed and scowled at him balefully. “I hate you.”

“The truth hurts, doesn’t it?”

Samantha paused, waiting for a quest pop-up, but none came. “Drat. The quest is either hidden or won’t start until some other trigger.”

Glae gave her a withering gaze. “Such as, perhaps, offering up the artifact that is the most important foci?”

Glaring back at him for a moment, Samantha finally sighed and turned to Azure. “Give it to him.”

Grimacing distastefully, Azure nodded stiffly and thrust out her hand, after which the First Page, a humble clay tablet, appeared out of a warped slurry of air distortions.

Glae was prepared the instant that it did, gesturing and enshrouding it in a protective containment bubble. Samantha winced when she sensed the intensity of its protection — she wasn’t sure she could pierce it without Fate manipulations, which meant Glae had a mountain of bonuses, or there was a flat level restriction, perhaps both.

“Finally,” Glae whispered with intensity. “Aeons of degradation lifted; aeons of torture relieved…”

The artifact floated down to the center of the ritual platform, and the bubble expanded to encase the platform as well as Glae himself.

The quest “History in the Taking” is complete. 18(13) exp gained in [Goddess], ??(10) exp credit gained to a sub-class of choice, 10(5) exp gained in [Magineer], 7(5) exp gained in [Bard]. +66 Maximum MP added. [Sacred Treasure Finder] Status added.

[Bard] level 4 reached. [Musical Talent] upgraded.

Samantha was happy to see it, though a bit distracted by the current incident. Nonetheless, of course she allocated everything into [Magineer]. It leveled her! That would be a huge difference if a bunch of demons showed up.

[Magineer 15] gained. +50 Maximum MP added. Bonus to [Magical Invention] already present. You may upgrade a [Magineer] Class Skill instead.

That, I’ll worry about later.

Try as she might, though, she couldn’t resist peeking at the new Status. She could already feel its strong significance like an enchanting tingle in her bones and nerves, particularly touching and flaring in her senses.

Sacred Treasure Finder

You’ve discovered and secured a Sacred Treasure in your world (Calrenazzod)! This is among the rarest and most precious of accomplishments, making you a bit sacred yourself. Add +2 Effective Levels to any contest engaged on Calrenazzod, as a unique (Sacred Luck) bonus. Increase this to +5 for any matter related to information gathering, detection, investigation, analysis, and so on.

This Status is permanent and is not affected by the ownership or future conditions of the item. Only one individual in a world may claim this Status at any given time.

Whoa. Another unique bonus, and this is to… almost everything. Every action isn’t a contest, exactly, but most are. This is kinda Holy Grail level shit, here.

There were other interesting insinuations. She knew the quest itself differed in the reward based on whether one ‘found’ it or ‘possessed’ it, which likely referred to the specific nature of the agreement of the quest, as it involved Glae. Likely, the benefits were greater for an owner, but it seemed to put a target on your back to some degree, too. If she had to guess, right now, Glae and she were sharing a portion of a total bonus between the finder and the owner. Once he destroyed it, though — hypothetically — he’d lose his.

Destroying it has a certain benefit, ironically. If the artifact doesn’t exist, there’s no point in taking me out to open up the Status. It becomes no longer obtainable.

Glae was still preparing the ritual, in which three strong-smelling, highly reactive alchemical cocktails floated above the platform and poured into different sections of the rune’s hollow etching. One boiled and fumed on contact with the air, another glowed brightly and intensely, and another swirled and stirred chaotically, as if fighting to escape. Each was tamed, however, as it flared upon contact with the rune. Into the grooves, each flowed and solidified like metal suddenly cooled from the forge. They fit the rune’s mold perfectly and were set.

Glae made several emphatic hand signs and chanted slowly, drawn out in a harsh tongue, his voice suddenly far deeper, with supernaturally echoing undertones, like he had become the gong of a drum the length of the horizons. The very mountains around them seemed to stir and resonate from it.

Giantkin speaking to their brothers.

Samantha peeked over at the minotaurs to see their reaction. Their heads were bowed solemnly, their eyes closed, listening, perhaps to what they considered the work of ‘cousins.’

It wasn’t something easy to interpret fully, but she understood that it was a calling and a focusing. Moreover, something began to pool and charge the air. It wasn’t mana, that was for sure. In fact, it was quickly beginning to interlace the environment with deliberate interference and rapidly intensifying.

“Glae, uh-” Samantha began nervously.

But he wasn’t listening, entirely absorbed and focused on the task in front of him. It took her a moment to realize it was rather tangential to the ritual. He’d connected it, but this was about blocking interference.

Her warning before the suppression zone dropped was startlingly brief. The four antimagic towers in the distance erupted in instantaneous overlapping effects, wiping out everything arcane in one breathtaking moment. Spells — even contingencies — ended, arcane artifacts deactivated, and all mana went from full to absolute zero in a heartbeat.

Supreme Antimagic Zone in effect. Enhanced Null Mana Zone in effect. No arcane magic is possible, regardless of Effective Levels. No mana can exist, even inside containments.

Samantha felt punched right in the gut from this, her skin tingling in what likely would’ve been pain if she still suffered such things.

Not sure why I didn’t expect it… ugh. Too many fascinating distractions, I suppose.

A whole smorgasbord of her capabilities had just been blocked out, and it felt like a slap in the face to the Goddess of Magic. Even ‘Mana Font’ had been rendered useless. The spell she always had going, Trilogy Fusion Soul, was under some sort of unique ‘suppression’ of its effects, though it had almost hilariously refused to be dispelled entirely due to its rules. Nonetheless, she had numerous divine powers still operating, and what was keeping her hovering was one such thing: [Matter, As Spirit], which could not be dispelled, period.

Azure wasn’t so lucky — she yelped as she fell the remaining distance to the ground, though her agility was up to the task, and she performed an acrobatic roll that put her into a crouch. Glaring at Glae, she called, “You fucking asshole!”

It was not an effective taunt, as Glae didn’t seem to hear, still lost in his antimagic, alchemical rituals, as he layered the environment in alien protections of his unique ‘Nega Ith’ arts. His Antimages were also working, layering more independent ‘antispells’ in preparation for trouble. Many were using divine items as supplementation.

In her experience, antispells could do some generic protections, and were useful for some unique layering, but they were incredibly weak when it came to countering raw physicality. This was their Achilles heel, even more than divine magic, which they could quite potently counter, if not as spectacularly as they could arcane.

Meanwhile, Samantha got various reports from her Servitors and Followers about blown protections, contingencies, and deactivated items. The minotaurs were quiet, but that was no surprise — they did not use anything arcane, generally.

Samantha said nothing, just punted things to Jeeves to begin arranging divine-only protection layered in compensation, which fortunately were quite extensive and potent from her side of things. Otherwise, she primed her senses to try to understand as much as she could, getting the rare chance to see Glaelychus’s fullness of power on display. She recorded in mirrors and had her Resemblants watching, too. All in all, it was terrifying, humbling, and informative, though it would require copious study to unravel.

Mostly, Samantha just hoped today wasn’t the day Glae decided to stab her in the back.
