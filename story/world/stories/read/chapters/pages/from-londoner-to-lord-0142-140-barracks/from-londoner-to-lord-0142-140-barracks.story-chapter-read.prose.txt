"Well, we don't have a tanner in the village," the majordomo replied, "since there was never enough animal hide for them to tan into leather - mainly because the previous baron never allowed hunting at a large scale here. Even with six new leather armors we still won't have enough of them to properly equip all the new guards, but they will still help a lot."

But when it did happen eventually... only his brothers themselves would know what they must surely be planning to capture the Duke's seat.

Thinking about his brothers made him wonder how his newfound sister was doing. Astela was the only one who supported him against their older brothers, and it had been months since he had heard from her. But it was also true that without any modern communication methods in this world, that was likely a normal duration to talk with people who didn't live in the same city. Coming back to the present, he looked at others and tried to present the future threats to himself without openly giving his own reasons behind this.
