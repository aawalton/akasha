The next morning, Edge began searching for the skills he intended to merge with Enhance Vision.

He wasn’t sure where to find Enhance Olfactory, although it was a common skill for beasts in the wild, but one of the birds he’d fought during his first trip through the Sea of Trees had Enhance Hearing in its collection—a giant eagle that was massive enough to pick Blue up and carry her away.

He journeyed to the region where Trapper’s crew had encountered the creature, then spent the rest of the afternoon stalking his prey. He eventually came across an immense nest in the crook of a colossal branch. Judging by the feathers and bones piled nearby, it belonged to one of the eagles he was hoping to find. He used Play to erase his trail, found a spot where the shadows were thick, and remained in that form until the predator returned.

Defeating it alone would have been an impossible task during their first encounter, but Edge had undergone some explosive growth since arriving at World’s End. He hit the big beast hard right from the start, combining Maelstrom, Blast, and Repel Water to keep the bird off-balance. When it tried to fly away, he sent the twin chains snaking out to wrap around its ankles.

The stage-three avian soared toward the heavens, trusting the elevation to stay his hand, since most landbound creatures couldn’t survive a fall from that height. Unfortunately for the colossal creature, Edge wasn’t landbound anymore and had no need of the beast’s wings to return to the ground below.

It realized the error of its ways when he switched on Dragon’s Greed, bolstering his glaive with electric energy from Elemental Blade for good measure. The eagle sensed his magic and began a plummeting dive, hoping to snag its hitchhiker on the canopy below, but it was already too late.

Edge used the chains to swing up and over, then let loose with the single-blade version of Quadra Slash. Opulence sheared off a wing like it was made of wax, crackling with current as it scored a debilitating blow.

He let go and Glided to the ground, trusting the predator’s insane natural Durability to help it survive the fall. He landed beside the dying creature, claiming Enhance Hearing before it drew its final breath. The eagle wound up having Enhance Vision too, but there was no reason to steal it, so he finished the bird off after claiming a single skill.

You have accumulated sufficient experience to cycle up your core. Your new cycle is: 2.

You have 7 attribute points awaiting allocation.

He decided to stick with the same distribution until he hit cycle four, which should leave his Speed in a good place even without Fear the Seasons. When the potentia finished working its magic, he plucked the feathers and butchered the remains, giving him enough creds to cover his expenses when he made it back to town.

Shortly after he stopped for lunch, his skill senses pointed him toward a stage-one boar with an oversized snout that was foraging for mushrooms among the loam. The piglet didn’t have a chance. He caught it without breaking a sweat, claiming Enhance Olfactory before letting it go. The parts wouldn’t sell well, and he already had a vault filled with frozen meat.

Wearing a beaming grin, Edge stepped into his central chamber and pulled out his Triple-Merger Token. He asked his newest avatars to gather round, then voiced his intentions to the System. “I want to use my token and merge these three skills.”

When those words left his lips, the disc rose from his hand and started spinning, producing intense flashes in every color of the rainbow along with miniature bolts of lightning. The impressive display continued for several minutes before the special effects faded away, leaving a single avatar floating where three had been only moments before.

The originals looked like the body parts they enhanced. Edge thought the new avatar would be a bunch of sensory organs floating in formation, like what had happened with All-Seeing Gaze, but that wasn’t the case. Instead, the avatar took the form of a bank of mist that kept changing shape, shifting from eyes to ears within the span of a breath, followed by tongue, nose, and skin.

There wasn’t any pattern he could discern, and the avatar seemed friendly enough—excited to check out his kingdom and meet the rest of the team. After introducing himself and welcoming the skill to his collection, Edge summoned his Guide to read the description in full, supplementing the vision he’d experienced when the joining was complete.

Congratulations. The skills Enhance Vision (Common, Rank 2), Enhance Hearing (Common, Rank 1), and Enhance Olfactory (Common, Rank 1) have successfully merged into a new skill.

The long-distance bonus from Enhance Vision has been preserved.

Your new skill is: Enhance Physical Senses <passive> (Rare). Rank 1. Path: Long-Distance Vision.

Dramatically increases the acuteness of the user’s taste, touch, smell, hearing, and vision. Each augmentation may be switched on and off at will.

He pumped his fist when he reached the last line. The results were even better than he’d hoped. In addition to being elevated to Rare, which would make Enhance’s even-rank upgrades considerably more powerful, the merged skill boosted his sense of taste and touch too, which he was eager to explore when he made it back to town.

An enhanced tongue would make food taste even better, particularly mana-berry-infused delicacies, and turbocharging his sense of touch was bound to come in handy from time to time. Although, if he was being honest with himself, Edge was most interested in trying it out with Mel during their quality time between the sheets.

While exploring those enticing possibilities would have to wait until he made it back to World’s End, there was plenty to test here and now. Alive with the thrill of acquisition, he ignited his core and switched on Enhance Senses, taking it slow as he got used to the surge of sensory data flooding his brain.

It would have been completely overwhelming if he hadn’t had so much practice using All-Seeing Gaze. However, he was used to being bombarded with raw information, even if the contents were slightly different from what his ocular power provided. After ensuring he could handle boosting all five senses at once, he explored his enhanced organs one at a time.

He began with his eyes. Instead of doing anything fancy with mana or Disruption, Enhance Senses offered a straightforward improvement to the acuity of his eyesight. He could make out fine details from considerably further away, and his close-range vision had been sharpened to a lesser degree. It made it easier to scan his surroundings for anything out of the ordinary. His perception of color and contrast was a bit better too, making his visual world richer in addition to clearer.

Edge focused on his ears next. He had played around with Enhance Hearing after defeating the first wave of the beast stampede, then placed the power in an auxiliary slot. This skill was considerably stronger, likely because it was Rare instead of Common. He took in the complex soundscape of the Sea of Trees in crisp, clear detail—everything from the wind stirring thousands of leaves to the hoots and cries of beasts in the distance.

The changes were more pronounced when he focused on his nose, since he had never experienced a skill that boosted his sense of smell before. It wasn’t just a matter of the odors around him growing stronger, although that was a major component. The skill increased the range of scents he could detect in addition to boosting his nose’s raw sensitivity, letting him pick up dozens of odors he had never encountered before.

This must be what beasts experience all the time, although I still can’t compete with a canine or boar, even with Enhance Senses running. It was amazing how vivid the scentscape of the forest was. It would likely take months or even years before he could fully appreciate and apprehend what he was smelling, but he was certain the information would come in handy sooner rather than later.

Just for fun, he tested his upgraded tactile sense, gasping as the enhancement hit him. He could feel the individual threads of his socks and undergarments. Perceive the breeze kissing his skin with an intensity that took his breath away. This is definitely going to be useful, but it will take a while to explore the possibilities.

Thoroughly satisfied with his progress thus far, he began making his way back to the borderlands, planning to continue his hunt for the rest of the day and return to the free city the following morning. Edge hunted until the sun went down while practicing with Glide and Enhance, camping in a hollow trunk whose opening was too narrow to squeeze through without using Shadow Play.

He resumed his journey several hours before sunrise and entered the borderlands shortly after, unaware that while he was searching for prey, he was being hunted too.
