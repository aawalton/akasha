This was a kind of in-between section of the serial. I’ll spoil some stuff in case you’ve not read the original, so spoiler warning from here for book 7.

I think at the time, I had some semblance of a plan of where things would go with the Cerithil Hunters and the Taleen, and there was quite a bit of setup for that here. Originally, Ilea just kinda goes for the keys on a whim. Well, she knows there’s something about the One without Form, and she wants to help out but it felt very just like something happening – I tried to make it feel a lot more intentional in the books. Her involvement with the Hunters, her choice to look for the keys, and her communication with Isalthar and the Hunters. It should feel like a more intentional choice, more drive for all of them really, but mostly Ilea.

Of course I adjusted almost all dialogue/generally matured up the emotions a little. A bit less random edge/lolxD vibes without losing too much of the voice. I’ve felt with the edits in general, Ilea feels more grounded in the world. The story is taking itself a little more seriously, and I think that original randomness was less intentional and more just me losing my mind a bit from time to time, trying to write 5 chapters a week and creating some semblance of cohesion. There’s a certain charm to that but as I’ve said before too, I think that fits more with the serial format and less with published books.

Tried adjusting the flow again too, as sometimes the serial just lulled and felt stuttery. Ilea had grown very powerful by the end of the Krahen Isles stuff, so I was thinking of what to do next.

The main thread that we found in book 7 were the Teleportation gates and of course the Accords. I made significant changes to both, not in terms of where things go but how we get there. Originally, Ilea wasn’t particularly interested or involved in either. The Meadow pretty much made fun of Iana and Christopher for not figuring out this super easy thing immediately, so everything becomes kind of a joke. The Accords just kinda happen because I thought something like that would be cool but I don’t think it really flows naturally in the original and feels more like a teenager setting up his lego avengers because now they have to fight together!

Now, both happen more in tandem. One is the consequence of the other. The Meadow is important but the Taleen gates prove a massive challenge to it too. A challenge that the enchanters, the Meadow, and Ilea face and solve together – She considers the implications a lot more too, or with a lot more groundedness at least. The tech they’re working on, if they succeed, would change the world as they know it. And with it, it lays the groundwork for the Accords.

And while Ilea thought the Accords was kinda cool in the original but mostly didn’t want to be involved/seemed to think her responsibilities there to be more of a burden she should carry, without really wanting it, she’s a lot more present now. I think it’s a logical next step for her, for all the places she’d discovered and learned to love, places she could return to, places where she’d made friends and connections. I’m very happy with how I managed to adjust the vibe of all that, and of the various places, especially the Meadow’s domain. But let me know what you think of it, if you do get to read it.

Her space magic also goes more and more nuts in this one and I’ve made her a lot more excited about that. Because being able to teleport around the world is really fucking sick.

Really happy with the cover for book 7 too. I feel like we’re really getting into four-mark territory at this point :). I’m quite a bit ahead in my first pass edit and I’m liking how things are starting to come together.
