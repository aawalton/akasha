Edge and Mel went streaking for a duo standing near the arena wall, who were prioritizing defense during the opening seconds of the Clash.

The women were equipped with long spears and heavy shields, and a forcefield shimmered in the air above their heads. The barrier blocked energetic attacks, the shields handled projectiles, and their long weapons would make short work of anything that entered melee range. It was a solid strategy—far safer than entering the chaotic melee underway in the center of the ring—and it was clear that the duo expected to endure anything their opponents dished out.

They were about to learn the error of their ways.

Thanks to Mantle, Boon, and their top-tier traits, Edge and Mel’s attributes were superior to those of anyone else standing on the arena floor. They danced between the barrage of flaming bolts and acidic spews the spearwomen fired from the ends of their weapons, closing the distance in a streaking flash.

The woman sensed something was wrong and activated their ultimate abilities—a pair of synergistic powers that bolstered their defense. They formed a shell with their shields, with the spears poking through, ready to absorb the duo’s charge and counter with precision strikes.

Edge took the gladiator on the left, and Mel darted right. She drove her boots into the ground while bringing Extinction whipping around, transforming her forward momentum into a wicked horizontal slash. The primal weapon had been heavy to begin with, and Helena’s modification made its mass swell in proportion to its wielder’s strength. Even without a skill bolstering Mel’s swing, when the mastercrafted axe collided with the spearwoman’s shield, she went flying back, slamming into the wall before falling to her knees.

Meanwhile, Edge came to a skidding stop and sent Opulence screaming toward his foe’s unshielded side, activating Quadra Slash and flaring Mantle and Greed along the way. His avatars lent their assistance to the endeavor, blending with the bonus damage from his traits as his offense soared to lethal new heights.

His opponent was an expert shield bearer. She used the heartbeat before impact to reposition, placing the heavy slab of armor at an optimal angle to block the strike. Both women’s ultimates were going strong, and she’d bolstered her defense with yet another skill.

It didn’t help at all.

The gleaming glaive struck the shield and kept right on going, cleaving through metal, leather, and flesh in turn before erupting out the far side of the gladiator’s body, quinquesecting her torso into five ragged chunks, armor and all. He knew she’d been portaled to safety before his blades reached her organs, but the arena simulated the fountain of gore that followed.

Her partner wasn’t so fortunate.

Mel Swapped beside the dazed warrior, bringing her axe screaming down with meteoric force. The woman released her shield and rolled out of the way, dodging the blade with inches to spare… or so she thought.

Melenia had anticipated the move and cast Force Cleave before her axe struck the stonework. A wrist-thick force blade erupted from the bearded weapon, straight between the woman’s legs. It struck where the armor was thin, carving the limb from her torso before the arena’s magic teleported her to safety.

The couple fell back-to-back while assessing the battlefield, having scored first blood in the blink of an eye. The gladiators who were targeting them hadn’t been idle, and there was a mixed volley of spells and projectiles headed their way. When the attacks entered the periphery of All-Seeing Gaze, their secrets were revealed. Edge sent everything he was sensing to the chains of oblivion, coordinating their movements in perfect harmony.

The duo exploded into motion, dodging a fair fraction of the attacks and trusting their metallic allies to handle the rest. He Hardened his body while Repelling water-based projectiles, occasionally slicing an arrow out of the air. Mel Swapped so quickly that she appeared to be strobing, kicking her stones, and conjuring Force Walls to make her movements difficult to predict.

That left Foebinder and Magebane to deal with anything the duo couldn’t evade or was too dangerous to let through. They were boosted by Warlord’s Mantle and his stage-three evolution, pulling off moves that wouldn’t have been possible only a few minutes ago.

True to its nature, the left chain focused on physical attacks. It moved the scapula shield so fast it blurred before his eyes, displaying incredible dexterity and pinpoint precision. Stone spears fired at a fair fraction of the speed of sound exploded when they collided with the dense wall of monster bone. Foebinder used the momentum to reposition, turning the shield in time to deflect what looked like a lightning bolt made of molten silver.

Meanwhile, Magebane reared back and brought the ivory scythe flashing around. Disruption congealed along the cutting surface as the weapon’s innate properties synergized with the right chain’s antimagic touch. The Gardener’s blade severed a pair of raging fireballs, causing them to sputter and die like candles in the rain.

Magebane yanked on its anchor to adjust its trajectory, drawing the spell-severing scythe through the center of a summon that resembled a great, glowing whale. The ethereal beast wavered out of existence as the duo circled the ring, breaking line of sight with their assailants while looking for their next target. It was astonishing how much the chains had improved, and Edge’s body moved like a dream after equipping his new traits.

While those were amazing developments, that wasn’t the only good news. Thanks to Skill Bond’s upgrade, his magic was significantly more efficient, and his reservoir was sitting near the brim. Combos made from skills that get along should be stronger too. I can’t wait to see them in action.

Out of the corner of his eye, he watched Bison succumb to a spell that manifested a ten-foot tsunami of roiling lava—reduced to charcoal and ash when the searing tide broke over his head. His partner was trapped in the stone floor nearby, suffocated by the same skill that had defeated Edge before. Nasty. He winced in sympathy as he blocked an arrow with his buckler. I’ll have to watch out for that one.

By now, ten teams and four individual fighters had been eliminated, leaving twenty-six gladiators standing in the ring. The weak and unlucky had been removed from the board, and everyone remaining was incredibly dangerous in their own way. He met Mel’s gaze, only to see his ambition mirrored in her eyes. They were ready to take out that pair of earth mages that had slaughtered Bison’s team and eliminate the threat they posed.

This was one of the scenarios the duo had prepared for, and they confirmed their strategy with hand signals while crossing the arena floor. Edge was taking on the lava-caster while Mel engaged the gladiator who could manipulate stone. As they expected, the pair spread out when they saw him coming. Their skills affected a wide area, and they didn’t want to catch each other in the crossfire.

When he began his final approach, Edge stowed Foebinder’s shield and armed the chain with an enormous iceblade, flaring Elemental Blade as frost billowed into the air. He switched Aegis of Nullification to aura mode, paying the combo penalty to protect all four members of his team from elemental attacks.

The lava-wielder didn’t want any part of what was headed his way. He ignited his core and flared his spell, manifesting a wave of molten stone that was twice the size of the last. The gladiator took aim and set the skill loose, using the lava as both weapon and shield to keep Edge from closing the distance.

For her part, Mel charged straight at the stone manipulator, who was activating several skills at once. The warrior grinned when the ground in front of the guardswoman turned to mud, leaving her no time to stop or move out of the way. The smile curdled to a frown when she kept right on coming, using Wall of Force as a bridge to cross the hazardous terrain.

The man darted back to open some room as Melenia came sprinting in to land the finishing blow. She cursed when the stone beneath her boots gave way, sending her plunging into the concealed trap the warrior had placed behind the obvious threat.

His skill was significantly more versatile than he’d let on. While it could turn rock into mud, it could also grant stone the constancy of wet clay without changing its appearance in the process. The gladiator controlled the effect with impressive precision, leaving a set of stepping stones to cross while luring his opponent into the muck.

The second she fell, the warrior added a barrier on top, shoving her head below the surface and trapping her there. Edge flashed a grin of his own, excited to see what happened next. However, his partner wasn’t the only one who had a surprise in store. He pumped extra mana into Elemental Blade and Warlord’s Mantle while sliding to a stop, watching the left chain fling itself toward the lava headed his way.

The tsunami of molten stone was as ponderous as it was powerful. It was still fairly close to its conjurer and about thirty feet out from Edge’s position—a bit less than Foebinder’s maximum length after its latest upgrade. It meant he had about two seconds until his battle reached its climax, which gave him plenty of time to watch Melenia make her move.

The stone-manipulator had turned his way, prepared to help his partner finish Edge off now that the guardswoman was out of the fight. That left the man completely unprepared when Mel materialized behind him, Swapping with one of the stones she’d tossed in preparation for this moment.

She had lost to his combo once before, and there was no way that she was falling for the same trick twice. His partner had gone into this fight expecting to wind up in the mud one way or another, intending to use her opponent’s expectations against him this time around.

The gladiator sensed the mana in the air, but it was already too late. Before he had time to cry out, Extinction came down like the talon of a titan. Mel deliberately avoided a lethal strike to the skull, angling the blade to claim an ear, arm, and leg before the arena teleported her target to safety. It was the most brutal move Edge had ever seen. That’s going to be expensive to heal. Being trapped in stone must have really pissed her off.

Thanks to Gaze, Edge could track Foebinder’s motion at the same time. He had already activated Quadra Slash on the crystalline weapon clasped in its coils, creating the combo he’d named Dragon’s Claw.

When the living links drove the quartet of oversized iceblades into the superheated stone, several events happened at once. He Hardened his entire body while unleashing Devastating Discharge, which Elemental Blade had acquired after advancing to rank five. Mel hit the dirt and cast Wall of Force, using Extinction to shield her body.

Edge had tested each aspect of Devastating Discharge over the last few weeks and consulted with the avatar living in his village. He’d learned that when used in conjunction with an iceblade, the high-rank magic manifested a large volume of ultracold ice. Even so, it was only a slim fraction of the mass contained within the wave of molten mineral.

The intense heat vaporized the ice Discharge injected in a fraction of a second, just like he’d planned. Before the lava-wielder had time to react, the rapid phase change produced an enormous steam explosion from deep within the tsunami of superheated stone.

Edge dove to the ground while deactivating Manifest Chain and flaring his defensive skills for everything he was worth. Meanwhile, the wave of lava erupted in spectacular fashion. It covered its caster and his partner in the blink of an eye, eliminating the duo before they knew what hit them. The eruption flung superheated stone across the arena floor, causing the magitech barrier protecting the audience to appear with a pop of displaced air.

The molten rain pummeled the gladiators, damaging gear and searing exposed flesh. It killed several of them outright and left all but a handful with wounds ranging from painful inconveniences to debilitating burns.

Edge was struck several times, but thanks to his myriad protections, none of his injuries were severe, and Rejuvenation handled them with ease. Mel took a few hits when one of her force walls gave way, but her weapon was guarding her vital areas, and she signaled that she was fine.

When Edge turned his head the other way, every fighter in the ring was staring at him with murder clear in every crook of their features.

“Shit.” Mel followed his gaze. “We’ve drawn too much attention. We may have bitten off more than…”

Before she could finish her sentence, half the fighters formed an unspoken truce, fanning out to surround the dynamic duo. The audience went wild when they realized what was happening, clapping and yelling as the sold-out exhibition match entered a deadly new stage.

Edge’s creative use of Devastating Discharge came with a heaping side order of unintended consequences. He had killed four warriors outright and wounded many more. Some didn’t seem to have taken it personally and resumed the melees he had so rudely interrupted, but ten irate gladiators were headed his way—scorched, seared, and eager to settle the score.

Mel issued a bloodthirsty chuckle as she fell into formation behind him. “You managed to take center stage faster than I expected. If we survive what’s coming, I’ll flip the table like we planned.”

Over the course of that brief exchange, their opponents arranged themselves in a curved line, standing far enough apart that they wouldn’t get in each other’s way. Instead of charging straight in, the warriors attacked from range, intending to whittle the duo down before going in for the kill. He sensed their reactors flare to life as they unleashed their strongest attacks, including some impressive skill combos and ultimate abilities.

Crossbow bolts, fireballs, and an assortment of other projectiles came streaking his way—packed full of volatile mana he deeply desired not to experience firsthand. He flashed a hand signal, telling Melenia to fall back while he dealt with the pressing threat. Edge had already resummoned both chains, armed them, and primed his wind domain. He switched Maelstrom on with an effort of will, tracking the panoply of destruction headed his way with Gaze’s combined mode.

Powerful gusts erupted from his position, blowing missiles off-course while he Hardened his vital areas. He cast Glitter as fast as he could, filling the air with sparkling flakes to obscure his position. He dodged while spinning like a dervish, coordinating his efforts with Manifest Chain so his allies knew which attacks to intercept and which to ignore.

Edge ducked and weaved in a flurry of motion, taking an occasional hit to his diamond scales while using Maelstrom to propel himself faster. Foebinder blocked the strongest strikes, sending shards of bone flying from the massive shield as the Mantle-boosted chain deflected any shot strong enough to penetrate his defense.

He channeled Disruption into Magebane, causing the Gardner’s scythe to swell with antimagic as the right chain severed spells in every color of the rainbow, protecting him from the most dangerous energetic attacks headed his way. It let elemental magic pass through, since Aegis and Mantle could blunt the brunt of the damage, although it still hurt like hell.

Edge had never been targeted by so many top-tier skills at the same time. Despite their best efforts, several deadly strikes broke through the living links’ defense. The first was destroyed by Wind Ward, who he’d asked to hold itself in reserve for just such an emergency. A screaming vortex of wind shredded a dense ball of blue plasma that burned far hotter than anything else he’d seen, singeing his skin despite his resistance. An ultimate-bolstered javelin shattered a section of his scales and would have punctured a lung if his Organ-Guard hadn’t kicked in.

Although he was taking the brunt of the onslaught, Mel had quite a few shots headed her way. The redheaded warrior Swapped back and forth in a frenzy, flinging her stones every time she teleported to keep her opponents from deducing the pattern to her movements. She took several hits that sent blood running down her beautiful body, but she signaled that none of her wounds were serious and he should focus on his ordeal.

Eventually, the elemental magic filling the air overpowered Edge’s domain despite its upgrade, and Maelstrom faded away. However, the skill had served its purpose, buying enough time for the other gladiators to fall upon the impromptu alliance from behind, dissolving their teamwork as the melee returned to a brutal free-for-all.

The battlefield changed in the blink of an eye. One heartbeat, ten warriors were looking their way. The next, they were engaged in a desperate struggle for survival. It gave the duo room to breathe and time to play some of the cards they’d been holding close to their chests.

Melenia didn’t waste a microsecond.

She’d been saving the bulk of her energetic reserves for an opportune moment. Edge needed to buy his partner a few more seconds, so he taunted the trio of gladiators that was headed her way.

“Cowards and weaklings! Where is your honor? Where is your shame? There is merit to prey coming together to battle an apex predator, but to fail so spectacularly only compounds your disgrace!”

While he baited his opponents, he let loose a bombardment of Vacuum Blasts, asking the avatar to fire them in grenade mode instead of seeking individual targets. The thunderous racket merged with the roar of the crowd, who found his provocations to be prime entertainment.

As he’d hoped, Edge’s efforts were sufficient to draw the advancing warriors’ attention. The rest were dealing with enemies who hadn’t taken part in the spontaneous truce, which meant no one was watching Melenia. Thanks to the dense flows of mana filling the air, they were slow to sense the incredible surge of magic emerging from her core to coat Extinction’s blade.

As he put everything he had into surviving the consequences of his distraction, the muscular guardsman completed Titan’s Axe, causing the cutting surface to swell from gigantic to titanic between the drawing of one breath and the next. She took aim and flung the mastercrafted weapon as hard as she could, guiding its path with the skill’s rank-two upgrade.

Mel flared the spell hard, making the primal blade even bigger than it already was as Extinction abruptly accelerated. The immense weapon spun end over end, soaring toward the gladiators, lambasting Edge from beyond their field of view.

Several sensed Mel’s magic coming at the last possible second, but there wasn’t time to get out of the way. The axeblade collided with the first warrior like the tooth of a primeval god, bisecting her torso with no appreciable resistance as it continued its lethal journey.

Five fighters were mowed down like wheat before the scythe as the weapon curved around and began its blood-soaked voyage back to her hand, but Mel wasn’t finished yet. Along the way, she fired not one, but two lethal combos using Force Cleave, causing massive mana blades to tear into the gladiators who thought they’d escaped her wrath.

It was one of the most effective attacks Edge had ever seen, but it came with a heavy price. She didn’t have enough mana to use other skills and couldn’t block while Extinction was in the air. Her armor was coated with blood, one eye was swollen shut, and her left arm dangled at her side—broken in at least three places.

The sight filled him with incandescent rage, but he didn’t succumb to his fury. Instead, he switched on Rejuvenation’s aura mode for the first time while defending her position. His magic reached out and infused her body, causing sundered flesh to sew shut and broken bones to mend. She grunted as her arm reset, letting out a shuddering sigh of relief as her grisly collage of cuts and bruises faded away.

“Thanks.” Mel grinned while popping her knuckles. “That spell is amazing. I feel great, but I drained my mana to the dregs. I might be able to pull off another skill or two if the fight drags on, but it’s up to you to take care of the rest.”

“No problem,” he replied while flashing a maniacal grin. “You’ve done more than enough. Watch my back and let your reservoir refill until it’s time for the final exchange.”

Edge cast his gaze across the fighting floor as he asked his avatars to lend a hand—ready and eager for round two.
