Luke woke with a start, not understanding what had just happened or where he was. A loud banging sound turned his mild headache into a throbbing demand to lie back down and keep from moving at least until the world stopped spinning around him.

"What?" he croaked, realizing some sort of lump was right next to him. Again, he didn't quite understand what he was seeing. Then, when the utter darkness on the floor stirred, he saw that it was in the shape of a dog. "Smudge!"

"I think he's in there," a voice called out. Milla? The banging returned. "Luke!"

"Step back."

That second voice belonged to Curtis. Luke looked around, realizing he was on the floor of his Shelter. He'd returned to himself after everything that happened in the midst of The Greater System Weave and the void beyond. That's about when he realized what Curtis's comment must've meant, and he called out. "Wait!"

It was too late. The door flew off its hinges, crashing into the bathroom with a crack that spoke volumes about the kind of damage he'd just done. As Luke got up, swaying a little, he saw water running along the floor.

Curtis stepped through the open doorway, looking around the Shelter. "Your door is broken."

"Luke!" Milla cried out, following close behind. Lana was out there too, peering in with ill-concealed interest, along with Lemira, who grinned and waved, and even Nash.

"What?" Luke asked as he sat down on the bed with a heavy thud.

Milla glared at him, her previous worry turning into anger, and splayed her hands. "Where have you been?! Do you know how worried I was?"

"I was looking for Hannah?" Luke said, looking around and trying to judge everyone's demeanor. "What's going on?"

"You've been gone for four days," Milla said, her voice low now, dangerous. "We found your Shelter this morning by pure chance! I thought the damn Singers got to you before they left!"

Luke blinked. "Left? They left?"

"Four days, Luke! What the hell?"

Checking his interface, he saw a lot of missed messages. A whole lot, but all he could do was shrug and point to Smudge. "I guess I got kind of knocked out pulling this little guy free."

Milla turned to look toward the floor at the end of the bed, then frowned. "What is that?"

"His name is Smudge."

"It's like a dark splotch on the floor."

"He's alive, like I told you. I'm going back to my own stuff now," Curtis grunted. He left the Shelter without another word.

"He's part void, I think," Luke said, watching Curtis leave.

Lemira entered instead. Three people were just about the maximum capacity without it feeling far too crowded.

"You have a companion?" she asked, getting down on her knees in front of Smudge. "How peculiar. May I stroke it?"

"I think so," Luke said. "To both questions."

She brought her hand closer, but then pulled it away, shaking her head. "I had best not. My skills are unable to provide any information about this... Smudge. You say he is part of the void? Having even the smallest part of that here is not a comforting idea."

"Enough about the smudge!" Milla called out. "You were gone. Unacceptable, Luke!"

Luke eyed her. "Did something happen while I was gone? You said the Singers left? Where did they go?"

"Some went South toward Philly. We think they're heading to Washington, maybe. Some went west. Penny is following them to keep a watch from the air and see where they're going. Lots of Singers everywhere in this great nation of ours."

"Guess we’ve got the portals for ourselves, then," Luke mused. Other than the headache, he felt quite rested and wouldn't mind heading out for a dungeon. It would be interesting to see if Smudge could follow. Even if he couldn't before, Luke thought he might be able to now with the heart thread keeping the two of them connected.

"What's that look?" Milla asked.

"What?"

She glared at him and stuck a finger right in his face. "That look. Like you can't wait to run out and disappear on me again."

"Well..."

"We've got people who need healing, mister! They've been out of commission for days!"

"Yeah, yeah. I'll see to them first, obviously," Luke said. "It's just that I got a bunch of levels just now, or a few days ago, I guess. One more and I'll hit 50. Maybe I'll get something cool then, you know?"

"Healing first!"

"I said I would," Luke grunted and stood. "Stop nagging me. You're not my mom."

She gave him a look.

"Yeah, I heard it when I said it," Luke groaned, waving for them to get out so he could leave. "Come on, Smudge, let's go."

But Smudge just lay there, still on the floor, not moving an inch. Instead, he let out a low whine. Stopping dead in his tracks and turning around, Luke frowned. "What's wrong?" The voidling couldn't answer in speech, but it sent a pitiful whimper, and Luke felt it through the heart thread like a chasm of exhaustion, horrible solitude, and a raging yearning that needed to be filled with... Smudge's stomach let out a long hunger growl that made Luke stop and give the damn thing a deadpan glare. "Are you seriously begging for food? That's what's wrong? You know you're not a dog, right? Not really?"

In answer to that, Smudge began wagging his tail.

"Luke," Milla said. "Once you're done playing with your dog, maybe you could come help save some lives?"

"I'm coming," Luke said, throwing Smudge another glare. As they left, he threw his companion a bunch of monster cores to snack on. "Don't eat them all at once!" he ordered, knowing they would be gone before he even made it to guild headquarters.

"You feed it monster cores?" Lemira asked, a strange look on her face.

"Yeah? So what?"

Before she had a chance to answer, Nash pushed his way forward and gave Luke a high five so hard it would have broken a normal human's arm and shoulder in a good number of places. "Way to not be dead, Luke!"

"Thanks, Nash. Good to be back."

Mateo was there and threw his arms around Luke in a crushing hug. "I was so worried, my friend. Next time, you must take me with you on your journey!"

"I'm not sure I can, but thank you. Too bad Penny has left already."

"She'll be back," Mateo said with a grin. "She rides the wind, so she is never far away. Como un suspiro danzando en el viento."

"Right," Luke said. "You know I don't speak Spanish, right?"

"Yet you feel it in your heart, I'm sure," Mateo said, gesturing to something Luke hadn't spotted, since there were so many people outside his now somewhat broken Shelter. "Your chariot awaits!"

"You brought a... school bus?"

It was a yellow one, perhaps even the same exact model as the ones he'd taken to school way back in grade school.

"We needed some way to transport the injured. Guild headquarters are kind of far, and we figured we had found you when Nash and the others found it after exiting a dungeon," Milla explained.

"The injured are in there?"

Luke hadn't needed to ask, since Weaver's Perception told him everything he needed to know. He didn't always keep it active and extended, but lately, he'd found that the massive amount of information no longer overwhelmed him like it once had. Perhaps it was a level thing, or maybe he'd just grown used to it, almost like a sixth sense.

Six injured with a varied set of wounds waited inside. A few would not make it much longer, so he hurried to the open door and stepped inside. If you took away the smell of injured people, the bus even smelled the same as when he was a kid. What a surreal experience.

"Hey Priyah," he said, despite her being unconscious, now understanding the somewhat ragged and worried expression on Nash's face. This was a recent injury, one she must've sustained in the dungeon Milla mentioned. A long gash cut along her entire thigh and had even shattered her knee. They'd done the best they could to staunch the wound with a bandage, but she was bleeding right through it. It was a serious injury, with the femoral artery having been nicked. If whatever attack did this had been just a little deeper, it would have cut right through it, and Priyah would have bled out in minutes. Now, she'd held on for long enough that Luke could fix her right up. Rather than mess around with needle and thread, he used healing mana, closing the wound as he cut off the bandage with Needle of Life in the shape of a pair of scissors. Luke could even make the scissors move. What a novelty.

The healing brought out a strange sensation in his chest, almost like heartburn, but he ignored it, moving on to the next patient in line. To get to the other one who was near death, he had to step past two others. This guy, he knew well. Well, maybe not so well, not yet anyway, but this wouldn't be the first time he healed him.

"Luke," Elijah said.

Luke nodded to him. "Elijah. Not a horn stuck through your entire skull this time, at least."

"That would have been awkward," Elijah agreed.

Instead, his right arm and right leg were gone. Not cut off with a weapon, but torn off. The stumps were in threads and, to add insult to injury, his body was ravaged by sepsis.

"Good thing Milla found me, Luke murmured as he sat down next to Elijah and put a hand on his pale, sweaty forehead. "I'll fix you right up."

When Luke pulled on his Meta-Heart for more mana to chase away the infection causing the sepsis, that strange pain returned. This time it was worse, almost like a...

"Am I having a heart attack?" Luke asked, clutching at his chest, struggling for breath. It sure felt like he was having a heart attack.
