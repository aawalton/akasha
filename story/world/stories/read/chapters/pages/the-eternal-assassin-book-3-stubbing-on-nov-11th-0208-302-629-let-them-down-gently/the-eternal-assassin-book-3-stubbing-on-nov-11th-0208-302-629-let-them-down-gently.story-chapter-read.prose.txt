Asher knew firsthand from his efforts at chasing down Grant that the minions were fast. Because of that, he wasn’t surprised in the slightest when the six remaining men who had been turned into nothing more than bad magic experiments raced across the cavern toward him. However, as much as he hated to admit it, Asher had long since come to a rather humbling understanding.

Of his original team, he was easily the slowest member.

“Let’s go!” Moxy shouted, laughing maniacally as she went screaming past him and smashed her knee into the face of one of the older men. Her method of covering ground quickly with her Air Nova and riding the dense explosions of air was haphazard and dangerous beyond belief, but it wasn’t like that was going to stop her. As Moxy and her chosen opponent went flying, a streak of emerald shot past him next, Samantha’s wings flaring as their razor-sharp edges aimed straight for two of the minions simultaneously. Unfortunately, these two managed to react in time, each one diving underneath the deadly feathers before turning to chase after her as she banked hard and worked to remain airborne within the cavern. That left three of the minions still coming straight for him.

Right up until a charging Rulfar in battle form went stampeding past him, roaring loud enough to shake the cavern as he lunged forward and smashed into one of the test subjects. He’d had to return his axe to its sheath for a moment in order to run on all fours and join the fight, but Asher didn’t think that was going to stop him from tearing the poor man apart.

Just as they’d planned, the team divided up the different minions flawlessly, and Asher grinned as he confirmed he’d been left with the only two of the original eight who had any chance of ever becoming human once more. Of course, just because they weren’t empty husks like the rest of the minions didn’t mean they wouldn’t do their damndest to kill him.

“I’ll do my best to take you guys down easy, but I’m sure we can stitch a few limbs back together again later if we have to!” Asher said, lashing out through compressed space as he tried to sever mustache’s right leg. The man had an aura that felt like a dense ball of darkness weighing heavily on his surroundings, and he reacted faster than should have been possible, twisting to the side even as he ran to dodge Asher’s strike before reaching down to try and grab his wrist. Asher barely managed to yank his arm back in time thanks to Kroult’s spell enhancing his reactions, and he frowned as he realized extending himself like that might not be the best idea against these strange, superhuman warriors.

That was just about all the time he had to think, as the two of them were upon him shortly after. Asher fell on the backfoot, focusing on defense as he watched the two minions fight side-by-side near flawlessly. It was almost as if each one instinctively knew how the other was going to attack, and Asher didn’t miss how their runestones seemed to pulse in tandem with one another as they alternated striking at him. One would try to draw his focus to blocking a punch to his kidneys, and the other would instantly strike with a kick to his head at the same time. Or when one would dive low for his legs, the other would try to anticipate how he’d dodge and try to lunge forward and wrap his fingers around his neck. It was all Asher could do to flicker through the astral and utilize Distortion to keep dodging, slowly but surely getting a feel for their unnerving fighting style as he waited for an opening.

I have to admit, taking an opponent down without actually killing them is a lot harder than what I normally do, he thought. He grimaced as he was forced to block a devastating kick to his side with the flat of his dagger, his hand going numb for a moment before Eternal Regeneration healed him. Add a second one on top of that… Trying to be the hero sort of sucks!

Even as focused as he was on keeping his face intact, Asher couldn’t help but steal glances around the cavern at this team while the others fought. Kroult was hanging back near the entrance, staring up at the strange plants growing from the ceiling for some reason.

Moxy was barely managing to keep up with the speed and power of her opponent, and loving every second of it by the looks of things. Asher caught her right as she finished healing a broken arm, before turning one of the furs covering her arms into a gauntlet of fangs and driving the thing hard into the minion’s stomach.

Samantha was zipping about in the air, weaving in and out of the same strange clumps of plants Kroult was looking at as she rained down arrows that punched deep into the cave floor down on her two targets. For their part, the minions were grabbing loose rocks and hurling them up at her with terrifying force, but Samantha had trained against Moxy of all people trying to knock her out of the sky. Regardless of how hard the rocks were thrown at her, they weren’t nearly as scary as Moxy’s bullet-like Air Blasts, and she managed to avoid or block each throw with her wings.

Rulfar was having the hardest time against his opponent when it came to landing attacks, though he was also the one who seemed to be the least bit in danger. The blank-faced man he was fighting was much faster and far more agile than the bloodhound, managing to dodge all of his powerful attacks easily enough, but his own blows were practically shrugged off from Rulfar’s massive frame as if it were a child striking at him. If the man had any sort of weapon on him things would have been different, but as it was, taking down a humanoid bear with human intelligence with one’s bare hands was no easy feat.

Asher was reminded of the risks of looking away from his own fight when one of the minions finally managed to land a blow on him. When one leapt into the air and did a literal spinning kick of all things aimed at his head, the other one surprised him by snatching up a hoe on the ground and swinging it toward his side. Asher hadn't anticipated the strike, and while he managed to slip into the astral in order to prevent the full metal head from sinking into his side, a good two inches of the blade still stabbed into him before he could fully escape.

“Rude,” Asher muttered, glancing at his side and confirming the wound wasn’t anything dire. He didn’t dare remain in the astral for long as he didn’t want the two minions to run off and gang up on any of his other teammates, so he darted forward and returned to the material realm behind the man with the hoe, trying yet again to drive Crescent into the muscle of his shoulder and disable his arm.

Unfortunately, whatever The Lost Lady had done to enhance these guys, their reaction times were off the charts. Asher swore the moment the tip of his dagger made contact with the man he dove forward, leaping out of the way before Asher could get his blade more than a half-inch into his flesh. And while his wounds couldn’t be healed, that didn’t stop their freaky flesh from almost zipping itself shut.

Flesh wounds ‘heal’ almost instantly, even if it’s not real healing. That means like it or not, I’m going to have to get a bit more surgical.

Asher could practically hear Mornamol taunting him in his head, asking if he even had the skill required for such a thing. Gritting his teeth and trying to ignore his rendition of his mentor, Asher took his first real risk of the fight. The man with the dark aura weighing down around him went for another powerful kick, only this time, Asher let it connect with his side rather than block it. He felt a few of his ribs snap under the impact, but in exchange, he lashed out with his dagger, slicing cleanly through the tendons behind the man’s knee and ensuring he wouldn’t be able to put any more weight on the leg. Despite his big talk from earlier, he was more than a little hesitant to lop off actual limbs of the people they were trying to save.

But he knew better than anyone that ordinary healing potions could easily fix severed muscles.

While that minion stumbled back, the other one suddenly went on the offensive, lashing out with a flurry of rapid strikes to force Asher onto the backfoot once again. This man had an aura that felt like a decaying tree that had once stood tall but had long since fallen. A sensation that was depressing enough Samantha probably would have shed a tear if she’d been able to see it. Now that it was only one on one, Asher managed to sneak in another strike, compressing space and driving his dagger up underneath the man’s shoulder, severing those tendons and causing his arm to go limp.

With one minion unable to walk correctly and the other down an arm, that was the beginning of the end.

Asher fought with clinical precision, willingly taking small blows here and there in order to slice critical muscles and tendons everywhere he could. The Lost Lady’s enhancements actually worked for him in this regard, keeping the two of them from losing barely more than a few specks of blood despite the dozens of cuts Asher was making across both their bodies. Eventually, he was left with two men struggling to move with access to little more than their core and necks, their arms and legs fully decommissioned until he chose to dismiss Sever the Thread’s effect and someone came around with a healing skill. It had cost him another few broken ribs, a sprained jaw, numerous nasty bruises, and two dislocated fingers, but none of those were injuries Eternal Regeneration couldn’t heal right up.

As he yanked his fingers back into place with a hiss, Asher checked in on the rest of his team. Moxy had long since taken down her opponent, bashing the man’s face in somehow by the looks of things. She’d already run over to help Rulfar out, and the two of them were finishing off his target. Samantha had managed to cleave straight through one of her minions with Razor Wing by the looks of things, the two halves of the man still struggling to move on their own but more than out of the fight. She was busy trying to do the same with her second opponent, but it seemed as though he’d learned from what happened to the first one, because he was being quite cautious anytime she banked around and got close to the ground. Based on how one of her arms was trailing limply behind her when she dove, it looked like she’d taken an unlucky hit from one of the rocks at some point, which explained why she wasn’t trying to use her bow.

And for whatever reason, Kroult was still staring up at the plants growing from the ceiling, a deep frown on his face as the fires burned in his eye sockets.

“Kroult?” Asher called out, keeping an eye on Samantha’s fight to make sure she was alright as he did so. “Are you good over there?”

Rather than answer him, Kroult remained motionless, his head turned up toward the ceiling even as his eyes flared brightly. In fact, Asher had only ever seen them flare that brightly once before.

When he’d been in the middle of being kidnapped by The Lost Lady’s minion.

It was only then Asher noticed the impossibly thin strands stretching down from the cavern’s ceiling and weaving their way around and even in the lich’s body. Kroult was trapped in place, unable to do so much as twitch as the strands held him steady. He hadn’t looked all too closely at the clumps of what he’d assumed were plants before launching their attack, but Asher’s eyes widened as he finally realized what was actually dangling from the cave ceiling all around them as it began creeping down toward them.

It was hair. Millions upon millions of strands of hair.
