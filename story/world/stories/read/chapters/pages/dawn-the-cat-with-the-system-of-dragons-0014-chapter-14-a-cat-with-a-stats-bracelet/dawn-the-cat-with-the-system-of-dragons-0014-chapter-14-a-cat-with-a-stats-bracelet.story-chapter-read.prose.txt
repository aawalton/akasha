Sylvane, a town located at the edge of a dangerous forest, was becoming busy. It was early morning, and everyone was preparing to get started with a new day. The surrounding atmosphere was cool and calm. Anyone would love to stand on an elevated place, just to look at the serene nature and its beauty.

Currently, Theodoric was standing on the roof of the Ranger Association, looking at the forest nearby. His wife Elenora was also there with him, looking in the same direction.

They were looking as if searching for something, but there was nothing out of the ordinary. Even if they can't see what was there, they still knew that a very dangerous being was residing somewhere in that direction. It was hiding behind its space distortion magic.

This being was so strong that it was able to keep hidden for centuries, even when the top-level Rangers and Adventurers were residing in the nearby town.

Theodoric said with a calm expression. “Are you sure we don’t need to investigate?”

“Yes… That monster clearly said, it doesn’t want to be bothered… I don’t want you to poke at the hornet’s nest… … Who knows how many evolved animals and monsters are being controlled by it?” Elenora didn’t want to bite more than they could chew. Years of working in the Adventurer’s guild had taught her this.

“Hmmm… That’s a logical possibility…”

“Let’s just wait and see what happens.” Elenora didn’t want to take action unless necessary, especially when dealing with a strong entity.

“But aren’t you worried? The cat didn’t come last night… Cinthia was also sad.” Last night, Theodoric found out that when Elenora and Cinthia went to give food, the cat never came. In the end, they left the food there and came back.

“Hmm, I know… Anyway, it's time to go back.” Saying this, Elenora started walking towards the stairs.

“Where are you going?”

“Obviously, to wait for the cat to come and feed it.”

“Already?” It was not time yet, so Theodoric was not sure why his wife was going so soon.

“Yes… The cat didn’t come last time. Maybe it will come early today.”

“Fair point… But are you going to bring Cinthia there?”

“Umm hmm… she slept late last night, I'll just let her sleep”

“Okay then… see you at home…”

“See you.” Saying this, Elenora went away.

It was early morning, and the market was not open yet. So instead of going to the market, Elenora went back to her home, hoping to find something else.

She had vegetables, fruits, and rice, but there was no meat in her kitchen. She was not sure whether the cat would eat fruits or vegetables, so she started pondering what to do.

After a while, she went to the Adventurers Guild, which was not far away from her home. Even if she couldn’t find an open meat store right now, she would surely get an evolved animal meat from the guild.

She didn’t mind paying a bit extra. In the end, the cat may be something special. Otherwise, why would a monstrous being like that take care of the cat? This was her hunch after working for so many years in the adventurer’s guild.

Elenora entered a back room in the guild’s building and greeted a man. “Hello, Simon.”

The man was currently standing near a table, processing huge chunks of meat with his knife. Hearing a familiar voice, he turned around.

“Ho hoo… long time no see. How are you?” Seeing Elenora after a long time, Simon was happy. She used to hunt monsters and bring him the spoils regularly.

“I’m fine, and you?”

“I’m good, I’m good. So, what can I help you with?”

“Umm… can you give me some edible meat? I don’t need anything high quality.”

“Whaaat!!!” Hearing her say she needed edible meat from him was shocking, given that she could buy it from the market at a lower price. “You want to take edible meat? from here? You know I’m a guild’s butcher. Right?”

“No, no, no, I don’t want to take meat for my home. I want to take it to feed a stray cat. Market is not open yet, so I came here...”

“Just wait for the market to open. Why bother taking from here?” The guild only deals with evolved animals or monsters, so the price is always higher than the market price. And more than that, she wanted to feed it to a cat, not an important figure. It was totally illogical from his perspective.

“I’m busy today, I don’t have much time, so I came here.”

Hearing Elenora say this, Simon was not sure what to reply anymore. “You… really are something else…” Simon looked at Elenora with a strange expression and said again. “Anyway, wait a second, let me give you something.”

“Thanks”

“Hmm…”

Simon went to the inner room and came back after a few seconds. He was holding a couple of small pieces of meat in his hands. He put it on the table.

“Here, it is evolved rabbit’s meat. Its price is fairly affordable compared to other evolved animals.”

“Okay… How much is it for?”

“485 Hyls.”

“Hmm...” Elenora picked multiple coins from her pouch and gave them to Simon. “Here is your 485.”

“Perfect.” After receiving the money, he started walking back to the inner room.

“Wait… Do you have something to carry this?”

This is why he was suggesting buying from the market. Simon looked at Elenora and said, “Again. I’m a guild’s butcher, not a meat store butcher. Adventurers don't buy meat from me. I buy whole animals or monsters from them. How am I supposed to have a carrying bag?”

“Of course… Huuuh… What should I do? I forgot to bring a carrying bag.”

“Huuuh… Wait…”

“What?”

“Wait.”

“Okay”

After a while, Simon came back with a lunchbox in his hand. “Here, take it… After feeding the cat, wash it, return it to me before evening.”

“Nice idea… you’re awesome.” Elenora was delighted. She was not expecting Simon to give her his lunch box instead.

“I know I’m awesome. Now go. I have work to do.”

“Sure.”

Carrying the lunch box, Elenora was walking towards the old dilapidated building near the border of Sylvane. It didn’t take her long to see the broken wall.

“What. Is. that? Is that the black cat?” Looking from afar, Elenora was not sure what exactly she was looking at. It was early morning, and the surroundings were still a bit dark, so it was a bit hard to notice.

She could see a pair of golden cat eyes glowing in a blue rectangle. The pair of eyes was just above the only standing wall of the dilapidated building, looking at her straight.

At first, she was not able to decipher exactly what it was. Since the rectangle was also glowing with dim blue light, Elenora was able to see the shape of the cat’s face.

Then, the pair of golden eyes came down from the top of the wall, and she became sure who it was. It was surely the familiar black cat. But there was something weird about the cat this time. Something weird was stuck around its head. Moreover, it was glowing.

As soon as the cat jumped down from the top of the wall, it started running towards her at a slow pace. Elenora was also walking towards it.

“!!!” When both reached closer to each other. Elenora was shocked.

Nothing was stuck to its head. Instead, it was actually a stats screen hovering in front of the face. The blue screen looked similar to the stats screen produced by a stats bracelet.

Since Theodoric was a Seasoned Ranger, and he had shown the stats screen produced by his bracelet, she was familiar with this.

She never thought that the tree would give the cat a stats bracelet. Now, she was interested in the cat’s stats screen.

“Meow… meow… meow.” When the black cat reached near her leg, it started mewing.

Elenora then walked behind the cat and sat on the ground. She started petting the cat’s head from behind and started reading the stats.

“Why is the screen so detailed?… … … Name Dawn. Is the name supposed to be shown? … Current Status Slightly Injured …. What? You’re injured again!!!” Elenora was not sure why the cat kept getting injured again and again.

Reading the injured status, she immediately held the cat’s body from both sides and transferred her elemental energy to the cat’s body to scan it.

The cat didn’t move. It knew that Elenora had the ability to cure.

Thankfully, the cat didn’t have any major injuries. It only had a bite mark on the neck. Although the bleeding has already stopped, the infection has started spreading.

Seeing this, Elenora first started transferring elemental energy to the cat's blood to enhance the immune system. This way, the cat’s immune system would stop the spreading impurities.

Now it was only a matter of time for the impurities to be removed naturally. Then she started working on the torn muscle and skin.

Since it was not an emergency, there was no need to spend her energy on building new tissue. Instead, she decided to enhance the body's natural healing ability.

She started transferring huge amounts of elemental energy to the affected area. This allowed the healing process to be enhanced, and the injured area soon changed into a dried scab ready to fall off within a day or two.

While looking at he cat, she said. “You really have a lot of energy … … How do you even get injured every other day? … Huuh.” She was not expecting to heal someone early in the morning.

After healing the cat, she continued reading.

“Level 16, … You are level 16!!!” She continued reading the stats.

“Species cat, Age 6, Evolution status, Core status nexus core has developed, core level 1, wait … !!!” She looked back at the core status again. She read it a few times in disbelief.

She then looked at the black cat and said, “You…. You …. You have a Nexus core!!! Not an elemental core?”

Elenora didn't know what to think anymore. Nexus core only develops from an element core. And the development takes hundreds of years, but here she was seeing a 6 year old cat with a Nexus core.

She then looked at the sky above and muttered. “What have I gotten myself into?”

She then continued reading the cat's stats.
