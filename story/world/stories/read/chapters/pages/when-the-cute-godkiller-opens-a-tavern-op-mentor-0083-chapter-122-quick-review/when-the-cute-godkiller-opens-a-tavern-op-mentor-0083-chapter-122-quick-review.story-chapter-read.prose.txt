Chapter 122 - Quick Review

With everything on the immediate agenda now out of the way, save for the farewell party with the Gaiamaxims Guild, Shallies intended to re-establish the training routine for her students. Sending them straight to the Decade Battlefield would be unwise. Of course, before any of that, it all had to start with some re-evaluations. She had expected them to have significantly improved, simply based on the reports from the sub-raid.

There was also the topic regarding formally accepting Arun’dul to the Shalliesters Party. With that, they would be only one step away from being a genuine four-man group.

As evening arrived, she invited them over for dinner to discuss those matters. She specifically hadn’t told them about her plans to send them to the Decade Battlefield, just yet.

“For now, let’s talk about those two things,” Shallies concluded while enjoying a generous helping of cottage pie. “Actually, three…”

“Our new levels, Arun’dul,” Tayin enumerated while she munched on a chicken leg. “And what?”

“Umm… I’m right here,” said the young seraph, drinking some mango juice.

“Yeah, pretend like you don’t know that we’re planning a fancy welcome party for you.”

“Some of the things we talked about before I became your mentor have been slightly changed,” Shallies intervened. “Well… adjusted, is the better term.”

“Adjusted?” Ren asked, pausing with a chicken leg held right in front of his face. “Because of our narrow timeline for the Divine War?”

“Yep. With the imminent arrival of the tournament, there’ll be a lot more people involved who’ll know that I’m your mentor. Mostly the other gods, and their apprentices. I’m telling you now, so you don’t get surprised later when Stachie doesn’t hide your identities. The common folk, of course, we’ll still keep in the dark.”

“Doesn’t sound like a big deal,” Tayin said, turning to Grace. Everyone had expected the half-orc to join the conversation by now. “You with us?”

“Ah, yeah, I’m just curious about the box Aunt Mallee gave to Shallies,” she replied, barely touching her food. “I’m amazed they kept that one a secret for so long.”

“Apparently, it’s been passed down for generations,” the halfling added, gesturing the implied question to Shallies. “They were planning on passing it down to me next year, but there’s no need for that now.”

“Kurfurul loved this place,” the Godkiller explained, nodding towards the half-orc. “But I’ll expand on that some other time. I’ll let everyone see what’s inside the box, don’t worry. Eat your food.”

“Yes, ma’am,” said Grace.

“Kurfurul sounds pretty close to Nefurul,” Arun’dul said, not missing the irony of his name somewhat rhyming with them. “Are they related? Are we related?”

Shallies shook her head. “I guess we’re talking about more than three things now. But no, they’re not related at all. Nefurul was a goddess beyond the ancient times, before there was even a System. Kurfurul ascended after. Their names just have something to do with the ancient tongue. ‘Furul’ meant to ‘have fun’ or ‘fool around’.”

“Ohh…” said everyone.

“Nobody uses that language anymore, though.”

“Not since the Gone Old, right?” Tayin asked, excited. “Did they use the same language back then?”

“There are resemblances.” Shallies sighed. “But we’re getting off topic.”

“Right. New levels, and Arun’dul’s welcoming party.”

“I don’t really need a welcoming party,” said the young seraph.

“We’re having one!” everyone answered back in full synchronicity, including Shallies.

It caught Arun’dul completely off-guard. “Yes, sorry!”

“Right, so since you won’t be able to utilize your new levels any time soon, Arun’dul, I won’t bother checking your profile,” Shallies started, turning to Grace. “This time, we’ll start with you.”

The half-orc complied and opened her System Console.

[Name: Grace Allturia][Species: Half-human, Half-orc][Class: Mana-Edge Sentinel (Advanced 18), Mana Weaver (Base 27)]

[Attributes:][Strength: 6][Agility: 40 (+5)][Constitution: 20][Mana-Affinity: 45][Focus: 27][Unallotted: 0]

[New Skills:]

“That’s a total of twenty levels from the sub-raid,” Shallies commented, studying the half-orc’s choices. “You’re rounding out the rest of your Attributes, and your weaves. Good choice taking Multi-Weave and Combined Weave.”

“Yeah, I realized that I can only use one Manaweave per person, so I took Multi-Weave to triple that,” Grace added. “Combined Weave showed up after I took that, which makes casting three weaves at the same time a lot easier, so I thought it was a no-brainer.”

Shallies didn’t bother correcting the half-orc. Technically, it was possible to bypass all those limitations, but it would require strings of complicated invocations and a high amount of Focus. Choosing the skills was more practical. There hadn’t been any Mana Weavers crazy enough to skip out on those basic skills, and their ascended counterparts would require those anyway.

“I have those,” Arun’dul commented. As a Guardian Armorer, which was the Hero class ascension of the alternate route for Mana Augmentor that Grace was planning to take, he had a lot of experience with weaves. “Really handy.”

“Ah, that’s right,” Shallies started, turning away from the rest of Grace’s profile after glancing at the rest of her hidden stats and confirming that there were no significant changes. “Arun’dul, you need to make sure you share with the others what you’re able to do with your current situation.” She turned to Ren and Tayin. “Basically, just treat him like an Advanced class holder, even though he’s a Hero class doubler.”

“So basically an Ascendant and Armor Weaver,” Arun’dul concluded. “I’ll give you more details later.”

Shallies nodded. “Great. As for you, Grace, you’re headed on the right path. There are some direct offensive skills you can get from Mana Weaver, but you don’t really need them. Sticking with these choices would suit you better.”

The affirmation made the half-orc smile. She retrieved her System Console with a silent celebration, feeling confidence build in her choices and the direction of her career. Shallies intended to nurture that further. But for now, she turned her attention to the other two.

“You next,” she said to Ren and he quickly obliged.

[Name: Ren Salvatur][Species: Human][Class: Shield Knight (Advanced 18), Disciple (Base 35)]

[Attributes:][Strength: 40 + 4][Agility: 20][Constitution: 36][Mana-Affinity: 25][Focus: 25][Unallotted: 0]

[New Skills]

“Sixteen levels since the sub-raid.” Shallies nodded. “Since you’re the highest level out of you three, that’s expected.”

“I’m actually surprised we got this many levels from a single sub-raid,” Tayin commented.

“That’s because parts of it were Feats of Heroism.”

“What!?” The three exclaimed all at once.

“Arun’dul got a lot more, since he was part of the main raid,” Shallies continued. “But whether it’s the main one or the sub one, a raid is still a raid, and it still deserves Feats of Heroism. Not to mention, none of you three skipped a day.”

“Yeah, Mana Energy really helped us out on that one,” Ren added. “We basically have no down time.”

Shallies crossed her arms proudly. “Aren’t I the best mentor?”

“Best of the best,” Tayin said.

“Goes without saying,” Ren followed.

“Could not have a better one,” Grace said last.

They all turned to Arun’dul.

“I’m going to have to suffer through this all the time, am I?” said the young seraph. “I’m learning a lot, though, to be honest. Even if I’m not a mentee. So, yes. Best of the best.”

“Welcome to my world,” Ren said, earning a light slap from Tayin to his shoulder.

Shallies smiled. “Alright, enough of that. I haven’t even commented on your choices yet, Ren. Boulder Posture and Condition Resist. Clearly you’re looking to stay put now, rather than move.”

“I struggle a lot with large monsters throwing me around,” the Shield Knight started. “It takes a bit to return to position, and we lose some precious seconds. If I can stay in place, it’ll be a lot easier not just for me, but for everyone.”

“But what about your armor?” Shallies asked, reminding him of his Full-Plate Armor of Unhindered Mobility +1.

“I’m getting a new one, courtesy of our sub-raid rewards. Danilo and Yor’ylin are working on it.”

“We’re getting new ones too,” Grace added. “Well, I’m upgrading my original Mana-Edge.”

“I’m getting a new cloak,” Tayin proudly followed.

“I already got mine,” Arun’dul said last.

“Wow, quick learner!” Grace said, giggling. “We didn’t have to stare at you this time.”

“Yeah, yeah.”

“Alright, alright,” Shallies said, returning Ren’s System Console. “It looks like you know what you’re doing, so just going with that.”

She extended a hand towards the halfling. “You last. Give.”

“Yep!” Tayin quickly navigated through her own console and shared the details.

[Name: Tayin Laughlap][Species: Halfling][Class: Chronospark (Advanced 18), Star Gazer (Base 34)]

[Attributes:][Strength: 10][Agility: 20][Constitution: 25][Mana-Affinity: 50 (+3)][Focus: 40][Unallotted: 0]

[New Skills]

Tayin had gained seventeen levels from the sub-raid. She was technically only one level behind Ren now. As far as Shallies was concerned, there were no mistakes with the halfling’s choice of skills either. They were simply just a bit biased.

“Looks like you all decided to pause your Advanced classes at Level 18,” she started. “Good decision. You’ll have two Advanced classes soon at this rate.

“As for your skills, Tayin, everything looks good to me, except for Cosmic Flame Lance. Given that you’re a Chronospark and a Star Gazer going towards the Astral Lord route, you might find it difficult to invest in elemental-based skills in the future as you go deeper into your Chronospark class.”

“I’ve given it some thought,” the halfling said, looking determined. “I know this might be trying to overachieve things, but you said you’re letting us inherit skills from our alternate classes, right? So I thought, maybe I’d utilize those options to strengthen my elemental-based spells, while I keep up with my main skillsets. I didn’t even know there was a skill that specifically combines skills from two different classes!”

Looking at Ren and Grace’s confused expressions, Shallies decided to pass the explanation to Arun’dul with a gesture.

“There are some skills that only show up once you have the required combination of skills from different classes,” he started. “Guessing from the name, Cosmic Flame Lance, it’s a skill that requires one from a Mage and another from a Star Gazer.”

“More than one, actually. Cosmic Flame Lance requires Flame Lance, obviously,” Shallies continued, taking the reins. “It also requires the Basic Spells Mastery from Mage. Then Shooting Star and Cosmic Infusion from Star Gazer. After that, you can use a Skill Point from either class to learn the spell.”

The Godkiller gave the explanation some room to breathe. It was a mouthful, after all. She was also expecting at least one of them to find the hidden implications of that information.

It took Ren a minute to figure it out.

“Does that mean, there are skills or spells that only show up if we have one or more of them from three specific classes?”

Everyone stared at him. Even Arun’dul.

“We only have two classes,” Tayin said, trying to bring her fiancé back to the real world.

“But we have a mentor,” he said simply.

They all slowly turned to Shallies for clarification.

The Godkiller only responded with her scheming smile.
