A crisp autumn breeze stirred the hair on Nick’s brow. Warm sunlight caressed his skin. The buzzing of insects and chirping of songbirds provided a cheerful chorus that contrasted against the heavy-metal symphony of his heartbeat and the raging river of adrenaline electrifying his veins.

He didn’t notice any of them.

Every mote of his gray matter, every scrap of his soul, was focused on the woman flashing a predatory grin. With a languid grace, Rita reached to her waist and unsheathed her sword. It was the most fluid motion he’d ever witnessed, revealing a familiarity with the weapon she’d forged that made his flourish feel clumsy by comparison.

The feral anticipation gleaming in her eyes, however, was a mirror to his own. Both master and student were looking forward to the impending clash of blades. She had initiated him into the School of the Ever-Surging Blade, an offer that went above and beyond the System’s reward, and Nick intended to prove Rita’s faith was well-placed. Although, if he was being honest with himself, he was even more eager to pit his skills against someone who had mastered the art of the blade.

She made a subtle motion with the finger-wide sword gleaming in the sunlight. The gesture was equal parts an invitation to take the first move, and a warning that if he didn’t, she would end the fight in a flash. Within the System’s trial of mastery, he didn’t have any skills or abilities to rely on. Nothing besides his superhuman attributes and the raw talent he’d tempered in the time they’d been apart.

He wouldn’t have it any other way.

In that moment, everything fell away except the weight of Blightbane in his hand and the warrior in front of him. Time slowed to a glacial crawl when he began his advance, entering a trancelike state of hyperfocus as he brought his full strength to bear, determined to break through Rita’s guard at least once before the blademaster cut him down.

He knew that nothing he tried would surprise her. That any attempt at deception was doomed to fail from the moment of its conception. The weaponsmith was centuries older than himself, with a lifetime of combat experience and a deep dedication to her martial art.

She could read every move he made like the words on a page, but he still had a chance to prevail. He could already tell that she was tailoring her bladework to his attributes, and this was only his second trial of the Sword. If he was worthy of advancement, an opening would appear despite the disparity between their combat prowess. A subtle flaw he could exploit, created not by circumstance but by design.

With his Mind, he could track every move Rita made with total clarity—visualize the terrain, their bodies, and the blades in their hands as effortlessly as breathing. His Creativity conjured countless possibilities, ways to transition each parry and slash into a dozen different moves to capitalize on this snapshot of the ever-changing battlefield.

His Dexterity provided complete control of every muscle in his body, transforming his momentum into a tool. With his Toughness, he could fight for hours without running out of breath, and his Strength sat so high that Blightbane felt as light as the memory of yesterday’s dream.

He opened with a simple slash—stance like water, ready to flow into any form depending on how she responded. She raised her blade in a straightforward block that sent their weapons crashing together, the chime of metal-on-metal reverberating across the hilltop.

He followed up with a thrust that she diverted to one side, weaving his way between the barrage of blows that followed as Rita began her counterattack. He would lose within seconds if he let her dictate the duel’s momentum, trading parries in a frenzy instead of trying to evade.

The sword master unleashed an endless series of strikes. Each slash was as unique as a fingerprint, born of the present moment’s features instead of adhering to preset forms. Every move was based on one of the fundamentals she’d taught him, the martial wisdom instilled in the six katas she’d demonstrated during their time together.

He memorized everything to reflect on later. This encounter was a lesson as much as a test. The warrior probed his stance for weakness, searching for a gap to slip through but finding none. It took everything he had to keep her slender blade from cleaving his flesh all the way down to the bone.

She nodded her approval and took her assault up another notch, adding feints and kicks that revealed that while the sword was Rita’s killing tool, her body itself was the weapon.

Faster and faster the frenetic onslaught grew, each attack flowing into the next like oil over water. Like silk fluttering in the breeze. Nick met her fervor in kind, going above and beyond as he integrated the information each move imparted into the defense Blightbane wove.

Their swords flew so fast it looked like they were encased in strobing hemispheres of steel. Sparks fell like rain as the Legendary weapons met a dozen times for every wild beat of his heart.

It was his best performance ever, and he was improving as the adrenaline-soaked exchange wore on. Even still, Rita was ramping up the difficulty faster than Nick could compensate, and she licked her lips when his vitae began to flow. For all her merits, the woman had a bottomless pool of bloodlust seething beneath her skin and an unsettling fascination with watching him bleed.

He lost all awareness of time and self, consumed by the deadly dance taking them from one side of the hilltop to the next. He was at one with the ground below his feet, the sword in his hands, and the remarkable individual driving him harder than he’d ever been pressed before. His killing intent resonated with her own, each will seeking to dominate the other but unable to obtain the upper hand.

Nick’s wounds were piling up, and his best wasn’t good enough. It seemed that defeat was inevitable, but his gut was telling him he had even more to give. That an untapped ocean of potential was churning inside him. He dug deeper than ever before, inventing new moves on the fly that combined everything his master had taught him with every trick he’d learned while fighting for his life during the endless ordeals life had flung in his path.

He had never felt so alive—apotheosis lighting up every molecule in his brain as he transcended his limits and unleashed a combination of strikes so sharp they seemed to sever space itself.

He concluded with a lightning-quick thrust, threading the needle of his master’s defense a fraction of a second before she plugged the gap, Blightbane brushing her hair before she spun out of the way.

Rita had always been fascinated by the color of Nick’s blood. While he didn’t share her interest, he had to admit that he was curious what hue filled her veins. He learned the answer when a single drop of green gathered on her left earlobe, evidence of the millimeter-deep scratch his masterstroke had left behind.

They both knew the trial was over but didn’t let up for a second. They weren’t done with their duel by a longshot, and the action only grew more intense as he absorbed the techniques her onslaught imparted.

In the end, she unleashed the most beautiful series of slashes he’d ever seen. Nick managed to block the first and parry the second, but after ducking beneath the third, he found her blade pressed against his throat, drawing a single drop of blood to even the score.

He laughed as she sheathed her sword, following suit as he collapsed to the ground—indescribably elated and utterly spent.

“Not bad, Nick. Your style is rough around the edges and your bladework has gaps that you plug with magic, but all in all, you’ve progressed a remarkable amount in a short span of time. I’m impressed, and I’m honored to call you my student as well as a friend.”

The praise warmed his heart. What happened next made him happier still. Rita’s voice took on a formal tone as she said, “In addition to reaching Adept with the Sword, I hereby acknowledge you as an outer disciple of the Ever-Surging Blade.”

A profound sense of accomplishment swept through him as she offered a bow that was deeper than before.

“The test is officially over. I can draw this out a little longer by spending some of the faction points I’ve banked, but there are hard limits to how much I can tweak the System’s trials. Our meeting was only possible to begin with because you accepted me as your master and were initiated into my school. Before you go, I’ll teach you some tricks you weren’t ready for before and offer advice on how to make the most of your unique situation. We need to move fast, but before we start, there’s someone who wants to say hello.”

Nick had a hunch who Rita was referring to, and he was looking forward to their reunion too. Sure enough, the door to her workshop slid open. Through it emerged the first friend he’d made who wasn’t from the planet of his birth. A teenaged girl made of living wood approached him and bowed. He returned the gesture in kind, and even though her face was unable to express her feelings, he could tell Kiki was just as happy to see him as he was to encounter her again.

The combat doll was wearing a necklace made from the stones Nick had traded her, a deal that let him practice his other powers on the side while receiving Rita’s instruction. His instincts told him she had improved a great deal since they last crossed blades, and though there wasn’t time for another spar today, he hoped they would have an opportunity one day. He thanked Kiki for everything and invited her to watch as Rita imparted one last lesson and a few choice words of wisdom.

“I’ve enabled your other powers.” His master led him into the workshop’s weapon-testing area. “Now then, show me that Concept and those intriguing techniques you told me about. I’m especially excited to see the move you call Sonic Slice.”

Since time was of the essence, Nick demonstrated each of his powers in turn, describing his skills, titles, techniques, and abilities as he went through his paces. He ended with a full-power Sonic Slice that rocked the room, demolishing a test dummy to Rita and Kiki’s delight.

“Your Concept is amazing. The All-Devouring Blade suits you perfectly. I’d love to go head-to-head and see what it can do, but that will have to wait until your Master-level trial, at which point, I won’t be pulling any punches at all. Before you go, I’ll show you the Adept katas for the Ever-Surging Blade, since you will remember them. There are only four, but they are considerably more advanced than the others you know.”

With that, Rita executed all four katas in fast-forward, trusting his perfect recall to let him study them later. She offered advice all the while, making helpful suggestions for how to blend his bladework into his overall combat style.

All too soon, their time together came to an end. Rita pulled Nick in for a fierce embrace, and to his surprise, Kiki did too, making a gesture that implied she had high expectations for her fellow disciple. As the world faded away, Rita’s final words reached his ears.

“Farewell Nick, I hope we meet again in the flesh. But if we don’t, know my thoughts are with you. I will always treasure our brief time together.”

When he found himself back in in his room, Nick pulled up his profile with a smile on his face. He’d picked up a bonus for completing the trial, but that wasn’t the first item listed in his updates.

You have learned Parry.

A new skill may be acquired in 30 days, when Skill Master’s cooldown expires.

Rita’s instruction had taught him even more than he’d thought, teaching him a combat skill he’d been hoping to pick up. Gaining Parry was a considerable windfall, but it wasn’t the only surprise on the floating screen.

You are now an outer disciple of the Ever-Surging Blade. For increasing your faction rank, you have gained a new combat art: Blade Sense.

Blade Sense (Combat Art, Passive)

Sense any weapon in your hands as if it were a living extension of your arms.

He hadn’t realized that becoming an outer disciple would confer an additional power. Nick was thrilled to have picked up a passive skill that would help him take his techniques to the next level.  His day only got better when he saw his bonus for passing the trial.

Lightning Draw

Gain a significant boost to the speed and force of your slashes when drawing your sword from its scabbard.

He had reached a point where he could pull off Sonic Slice without using his Half-Length Scabbard, although it still gave the technique extra oomph. Lightning Draw, however, meant a drawing slash would be his trump card going forward, and he couldn’t wait to see it in action.

Immensely satisfied with how his day had unfolded, Nick closed his eyes and fell asleep, ready to immerse himself in the scientist’s legacy bright and early in the morning.
