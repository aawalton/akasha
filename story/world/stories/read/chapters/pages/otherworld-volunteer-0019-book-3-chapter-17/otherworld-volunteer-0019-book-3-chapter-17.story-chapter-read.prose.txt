Noah sighed. It seemed as if he wasn’t given an option. The only thing left was to embrace the power that the king offered. He was right. He couldn’t refuse. He might as well use it to become strong. To be free. To protect those he cares about. Krall, Gluttony, Thraz. That was what he wanted.

“Okay, then what do I need to do?” Noah asked.

“Accept my powers. Don’t block them. You will feel it enter your Pathways. Let it run free.” The king moved, green clouds billowed around him.

“Wait,” Noah said, interrupting him. “Jumping Pathways is a good way to get yourself killed. I assume you know this?”

“Don’t worry. I will be using my own lifeforce to bolster your Pathways. They won’t break.”

“Alright, but which one will it overwrite? I have 3.”

“Only time will tell,” He said, shaking his head.

It seemed even he wasn’t certain what it would be. But Noah could theorise. The strength he had was obviously from the warrior path. The shadows he used – the Hallowed Mist – were capable of creating physical beasts to protect him in a ring. He didn’t use stealth, either.

Noah deduced that it would overwrite his Might Pathway. He was happy about that. Might was still only an Uncommon Pathway. He didn’t know how powerful this King's powers were going to be, but they had to be at least of the Epic tier. If it was Legendary… A smile crept up on Noah’s lips.

Maybe being forced a Class wasn’t such a bad thing after all? Maybe he could forgive him for hurting Krall. Maybe…

“I’m beginning,” The king said.

The green mist billowing around the king shot towards Noah. He tried his best not to flinch, but from his fight before, he found himself flinching anyway. He took a single step back as the Hallowed Mist seeped into his nose, ears, and mouth. Noah choked as the mist pervaded every inch of his body, to the very core of his being.

Looking inward, he witnessed the sight of the green mist surrounding his Might Pathway. Beasts appeared of various shapes and sizes. Some of them resembled dragons, others hounds from hell with large horns and dagger-like teeth. And then they attacked. It was violent, and sudden. His Might Pathway didn’t stand a chance as it was obliterated in a single blink of an eye. Noah felt like something shattered within his body. It was an indescribable sensation.

It was like cardboard boxes going against an industrial bulldozer.

Just what Tier is it going to be? Noah thought in shock. Even when his Epic Pathway - Shadow of a Monstrosity took over his previous Pathway, it wasn’t so simple. It happened slowly, and the pain was unbearable. This happened so fast that there was no pain to even register.

And then it became apparent as to why in the next few moments.

Destroying his Might Pathway, the Hallowed Mist took its place. At first, it was just the same old mist. Hazy, and without form. But it changed. From vapour, to a solid. It rotated in a circle, and finally, the image of a winged-serpent eating its own tail formed

Ouroboros?

The beast looked at him. Not directly, but in his consciousness. Its eyes told Noah that it was looking down on him. As if he didn’t have the bloodline worthy of carrying its might. It didn’t last for long.

From deep within his soul, a small, but mighty growl rumbled through every part of his being. The serpent lowered its head. As if in fear for its life.
