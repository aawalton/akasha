I can’t be playing around like that next round, or after, Alex decided, wincing.

Rankings updating…

Favorites to podium and, of course, to win prizes. Which was ultimately what he was here for.

The favorites were several Awakened, almost always previously known quantities, all ranked in the top 10ish in talent; he kept hearing names like ‘Clearwater,’ ‘Zhang,’ ‘Rose,’ ‘St. Claire’ and such.

If Alex was reading his standing right—it seems like I’m in that second tier right now? Relatively high-ranking, rising talents?

And there aren’t that many chances to display my talent, maximum 4 rounds, maybe even less…

If I want to break into that first tier—and I really think it’s possible—

I need to display my talent. Like, I need a statement win.

Such was his thinking as he saw who he’d likely be fighting next.

‘Sage Greer?’ He’s an O2 Guilder. Initial rank 23, talent ranking 19.

He was up against ‘PrimordialWarrior,’ ranked in the 30’s; a warrior.

Sage was absolutely decimating this PrimordialWarrior.

Alex went to watch the fight, since he saw it was upcoming and he really didn’t have any data on this ‘Sage.’

Alex quickly learned a few things, listening in on the crowd.

It seemed like Sage was one of O2’s most promising recruits this cycle, and was largely speculated on to be an Ivy Liu-like situation; once he was eligible for transfer, the global guild X1 was supposedly interested in picking up his contract, and likely for a sizable sum, in the 7 figures.

Sage’s talents were, apparently, very high in Blood, as well as Life. Which he showed off, to their full effects, by absolutely butchering the poor guy with a jagged-edged spear; the spear was enchanted, it seemed like, so its wounds wouldn’t easily close, and whatever magics Sage was utilizing seemed to badly exacerbate the bleeding.

Sage’s skin was also faintly glowing with this bright light, Life mana, which also seemed to be getting stronger as he drew more blood.

And the rubies on Sage’s set of bloodred armor also seemed to be growing in mana, as well.

He’s also a bit of a showman, it seems like.

As PrimordialWarrior weakened, he grew sluggish and a little clumsy; Sage started dancing around him, playing to the crowd, allowing a few slashes to sail right by his neck, before slicing in three or four slashes, making Warrior cry out and flail. Warrior was already gushing blood like a fountain.

As the fight finished, Sage’s talents ranking increase from 19 to 16—despite his facing a significantly lower ranked opponent; it was like his display was being rewarded.

Alex’s eyes narrowed as he watched it all; it seemed like he wasn’t the only one feeling as he was.

‘Fucking Outworlders,’ said one—a guy in his late 20’s, from the Institute, likely a peer mentor—as Sage slashed a final time, turned around, and bowed—timed, so that the bow occurred as Warrior collapsed in a shower of blood behind him. ‘Another Main Character Syndrome brat, God these next few weeks’re going to be insufferable.’

Alex considered it. It wasn’t the first time he’d seen folks talking about this.

Outworlders, at times—at least, with certain people—seemed to occupy a strange place in society at times; celebrated for their talents, but certain folks also had rather mixed feelings about them. Or Us, I guess. For, well, plenty of possible reasons.

And there was this persistent cliche, that, well—an Outworlder woke up in this world, with all these new talents and magic, and believed they were suddenly the center of the universe.

He recalled Beckett had been kind of embarrassed when Alex had accused him of it, teasingly, before; at the time Alex hadn’t realized why.

Alex walked back to the resting area, turning the fight over in his mind.

Suffice it to say he had zero intention of being another showcase on Sage’s highlight reel.

When we fight I can’t allow that fight to drag on, at all.

It has to be decisive.

Or… things could get really ugly, really fast.

As Alex reserved a station near the corners, he checked a ping! he’d received earlier, mid-fight against the Time assassin; a ping! he was pleased to see, and was very much expecting. Finally.

I have an hour, at least.

As he began to work—he’s an O2 Guilder. Which is the same Guild as Karnation_, which means—I’ve seen his capabilities, but he for sure has an idea of what I can do. And O2’s a big guild, they’ve probably scouted my capacities regardless.

Alex also hadn’t really displayed himself at 100%; and that was simply because there wasn’t a fight, so far, that actually required it, besides the Caide fight. But the same was likely true of Sage Greer. Alex wasn’t going into this with any illusions it’d be easy.

Alex began with his Shields, using Willpower to force them as wide as he could; with all his new Intelligence and Wisdom on his gear, it wasn’t all that taxing. It wouldn’t compromise him for future fights.

He was also kind of thinking, brow creasing—am I also guilty of this? Like, Main Character thinking?

Alex had certainly seen aspects of discourse around Outworlders that were plain venomous; he wasn’t going to just accept all of it. Except this aspect was clearly a real thing; he’d just seen it. And given the circumstances, it did kind of make sense? Alex, at least, could see why certain people would start treating this world as a fantasy rather than what it was—a real world, with real people living in it, and real consequences. PrimordialWarrior was a real person Sage was humiliating, with feelings as valid as Sage’s own; is it just that he doesn’t see it that way?

I get this feeling, like, it isn’t even like he was thinking he’s above Warrior; he wasn’t thinking about Warrior at all.

As for Alex, he was pretty certain he didn’t feel like he was above other people, but did concede he had a sense of pride, which…

Actually, now that I’m thinking about it—is that even new?

It might be the case that it doesn’t change you, it just reveals who you are? Like that one quote about power.

Or is that like, a bit cute?

I definitely had a sense of pride, Alex was realizing, as he reflected. He was self-aware enough to realize that, at least. Even if it wasn’t as ‘activated,’ maybe, before.

I feel like I have changed, a bit.

Maybe I should watch a few more of these top seeded fights.
