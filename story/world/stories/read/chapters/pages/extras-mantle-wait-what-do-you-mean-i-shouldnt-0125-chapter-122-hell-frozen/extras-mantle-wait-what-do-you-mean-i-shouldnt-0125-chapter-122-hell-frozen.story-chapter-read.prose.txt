The bomb detonated with a sound like reality tearing and had carved a hole in space, which folded everything nearby into a crushing singularity point with indiscriminate hunger.

Dark bolts of lightning crackled outward from the event horizon. The arcs were black with white highlights, and they ate through the hall walls, ceiling, and the floor like acid through paper, leaving deep gouges that smoked and hissed in their wake.

Jin was thankful he wasn't anywhere near the vicinity—because unlike Priest Kiyon, he didn't have an Underlord body to weather that kind of punishment—but that didn't change the fact that he was caught by an attack and was getting crushed to death between the blast doors and the dark shadow-ball.

The shadow-ball was grinding away at his layered armament shields like a battering ram.

The first barrier shattered like glass. The second crumbled half a second later, fragments of essence scattering as the attack punched through with barely diminished momentum.

« First layer gone. Second layer crumbling. Third layer holding but fracturing fast— »

Jin forced his focus inward, leaning harder on the Sovereign's Indifference to filter out the spike of adrenaline trying to flood his system, and flared his Helldiver's Mark shield with a surge of essence.

The transparent shield manifested just in time to catch the shadow-ball's full force.

It held.

For exactly two seconds… the shield's surface spider-webbed with cracks and pulsating red flashes.

« Blink is about to trigger automatic— »

Space twisted, and Jin was ripped away from the shadow-ball's impact point and slammed back into reality two meters to the right.

He landed on the floor in a graceless roll as the Helldiver's Mark cracked apart into fading crimson fragments.

Pain spiked through his ribs.

A sharp sting bloomed across Jin's arm as the field alchemy injector automatically deployed and punched a dose of healing compound directly into his bloodstream.

Fortunately, the pain was already receding as the potion began its work, pulling together his wounded body.

Jin exhaled slowly—even with the Sovereign's Indifference muting the worst of it, attacks from an Order IV entity were on another level entirely—and pushed himself upright.

His armor hung in shredded strips across his torso. The self-repair enchantments were already fighting to knit themselves back together, but the process was slow compared to the rate of incoming damage.

« Jin. » Angel's voice was calm but insistent. « The engine's running hot. You need to vent accumulated charges soon… »

I know… but that’s the only thing that would bring the enemy down.

He exhaled once again and got his breathing under control.

« Essence is at 78%.»

Jin manually had the injector punch in another dose with his right hand—his left arm felt like a dead weight hanging from his shoulder, and that he could not have—and poured essence into his Armament skill, layering fresh barriers in overlapping positions around him.

The potions worked well, and he regained his mobility in his left arm.

Just then, the singularity collapsed with a sound like a thunderclap, and when the twisted spatial distortion snapped back to normal reality, Priest Kiyon was still standing.

He staggered, one hand pressed to his chest where his attacks had caught him, blood pouring from the wounds all over his body in thick streams that painted his ridiculous robes black and pooled on the floor.

"YOU!"

The word came out as a roar.

Kiyon's eyes—bloodshot and wild—locked onto Jin, and dark-red fire erupted from his body. The flames wrapped his entire frame in burning armor that crackled and hissed, drowning out the other ambient sounds.

« Underlord flames… »

Jin tightened his grip on Iron Howl and settled into a stance that favored mobility.

The priest's eyes burned violet-black behind the flames. His face was a ruin—skin charred and peeling, one side of his jaw exposed where flesh had been burned away—but his expression was pure rage.

"You..." Kiyon's voice came out doubled now. One layer was human and ragged, scraped raw from screaming. The other was something else… a low chittering resonance that scraped against Jin's nerves like nails on glass. "You dare!"

"You dare strike a servant of the Darkened One! You dare wound what has been claimed by divinity!"

The flames pulsed with each word, growing brighter and hotter until the stone floor beneath Kiyon's feet began to glow dull red.

"You think you can protect that mind from ME?" Kiyon's remaining hand lifted, fingers splayed. "I'll pry it out piece by piece until there's nothing left but a hollow shell begging for mercy!"

The violet glow in his eyes deepened to near-black.

« Jin, tune out his voice! » Angel snapped. « He's layering psychic influence into his speech— »

A fine mist of whispers seeped into the edges of his awareness, trying to slip through the cracks between focus and instinct. The intrusion was subtle—barely perceptible—but once he noticed it, the whispers multiplied.

Images flickered across his thoughts.

Vienna burning in the distance, ash falling like snow. Rudy's severed legs lying in pooled blood. Faces of civilians he'd shot without checking their status first, their eyes wide and surprised as the bullets found them.

Guilt.

Shame.

Hooks.

The whispers reached for his mind, hoping to claim it, but were welcomed by his star and were devoured ruthlessly.

Silver chains erupted in his inner sky, rising like serpents to intercept the psychic fragments and store them in the Mantle's crushing grip.

And just like before, backlash ripped down the psychic connection to its source.

Out in the physical hall, Kiyon jerked as the feedback hit him, but he'd learned to brace for it this time, and even reduced, the backlash made him stagger half a step backward.

Jin wasn’t sure if the man would double down on mental attacks or would go all-in on spells.

And so Jin took the initiative.

Time to set up the battlefield.

Jin deliberately formed incomplete, chaotic, and unstable sorceries in his mind’s eye and began embedding them into every bullet.

He had faith that with [Spellshot Synthesis] and [Spell Weaver’s matrix], he would be able to hold the unstable magic.

BANG.

BANG. BANG.

Two more followed in rapid succession.

BANG-BANG-BANG-BANG.

He fired in rapid succession, the gun kicking like an automatic rifle in his left hand as he blurred across the hall.

The bullets screamed towards Kiyon, but they weren't aimed to kill.

Some impacted Kiyon's shield directly—high-density rounds designed to force a reaction—and the priest blocked those reflexively, his barrier flaring bright where they struck.

Others went past him.

They slammed into walls, ceiling, and floor in seemingly random placements. Jin made sure the ones aimed at Kiyon were dense enough to demand attention, weak enough elsewhere that the priest dismissed them as poorly aimed shots.

Kiyon's expression twisted into something approaching contempt as he watched Jin miss.

Good.

Jin didn't care whether they hit the target.

He cared about where they landed.

Jin poured more charge from the [Thermokinetic Engine] into his legs and blurred forward, closing the distance.

He shaped his [armament] skill construct into a spear point and drove it forward.

The priest's smirk widened.

The spear collided with the barrier.

Both constructs shattered on impact, essence scattering in fragments that the Engine reached for and drank with never-ending hunger.

« Engine at 67% capacity. »

Jin leveled Iron Howl and fired twice at point-blank range with just raw kinetic force wrapped in basic sorcery.

BANG. BANG.

Both shots hit Kiyon's hastily reformed shield and stopped cold.

Jin didn't much care about the damage. He cared only about keeping the priest reactive instead of proactive.

He knew… should give the priest time to think and plan… he would infinitely suffer.

Kiyon snarled and drove his fist wreathed in his underlord flames toward Jin's face—the transcendent fire promising to burn through aura and flesh with equal contempt—and he barely got Essence's Edge up in time.

He poured dense essence and accumulated charges into the dagger, layering multiple Armament barriers along its length as the blade caught the punch at an angle, and Jin focused on deflection rather than blocking.

With an impact that rattled his bones, he managed to redirect the trajectory past his shoulder.

The arm holding the dagger went numb from the wrist to the shoulder. But the Engine drank almost eighty percent of the force and flames behind the blow, converting punishment into fuel that flooded his system with power.

« Engine at 87% capacity. »

Jin bit down on the pain and redirected some of those charges into his legs.

His kick landed on Kiyon's chest, shattering through his outer shield and only getting stopped by the innermost one.

But even that shield spider-webbed with cracks and force behind the attack launched the priest flying backward.

« Engine at 91% capacity. »

Jin breathed heavily—he was starting to feel the burn and liquid fire in his body even after the pain suppression—and called upon his Cryo affinity to keep him from fully burning while urging the Engine to drink more and more from each exchange.

The lord flames in the vicinity that his engine devoured filled the charges at an alarming rate, but they also spiked intense pain through his being. He could see his veins heating bright red beneath the steaming flesh.

« Engine at 95% capacity. »

« Engine at 97% capacity. »

« Engine at 98% capacity. »

« REACHING PHYSICAL LIMITS! »

Jin's essence perception triggered, and he immediately blinked backwards, through conjured darkness, as bolts of dark-red flame rained down from above.

He reappeared two meters left and had to immediately dash again as more bolts rained down on him with supernatural accuracy.

Kiyon hovered fifteen feet in the air, his flowing Underlord flames forming new robes around his burned body.

His face was twisted in something between rage and ecstatic fervor as he spread his arms wide and called down divine judgment in the form of concentrated firebolts that hammered the hall floor like artillery.

"Run, little mortal!" he shrieked. "Run and see how far you get!"

The bombardment intensified.

Each bolt was laced with Kiyon’s lord flames, and each impact required Jin's full aura just to tank without being obliterated, and there were hundreds of them raining down.

Jin dashed.

Dodged left as a bolt carved through where his head had been.

Rolled forward under a cluster of three that detonated behind him.

His aura flared and compressed around his body in desperate layers, each shield barely absorbing a single bolt before shattering under the superior firepower.

The laughter that echoed through the junction was unhinged—Kiyon's voice cracking as he poured more and more power into the bombardment—transforming the hall into a killing field.

Fortunately, every attack fed the Engine, and unfortunately, Jin had no doubt Kiyon could keep up with the bombardment for hours.

Jin's body was steaming now, heat distortion wavering around him as he'd stepped out of a furnace. With the charges past the safe limits, he had to actively pump charges into his attacks and weapons, distributing the load to prevent himself from being burned to ashes.

He was in pain—agonizing, bone-deep pain that even Sovereign's Indifference couldn't fully suppress—but he ignored it and focused only on dodging and shooting more unstable bullets at irregular intervals.

BANG. BANG. BANG.

« Engine at 111% capacity. LIMITS CROSSED! DISCHARGE! »

« Engine at 121% capacity. LIMITS CROSSED! DISCHARGE! »

Within thirty seconds, Jin was reaching his physical limits on how much punishment he could evade.

His muscles screamed. His essence channels burned. Every breath felt like inhaling molten glass.

The priest had concentrated more and more firepower on pinning him down, narrowing the safe zones until Jin was forced to move in a space barely ten feet across while flames hammered everything around him with unrelenting fury.

Kiyon's plan was obvious: kill Jin through attrition, wear him down with overwhelming firepower until exhaustion made him slow and slowness made him dead.

And Jin would lose that fight one hundred percent if he let it continue.

But by now, the hall was packed full of his payloads. Hundreds of bullets lodged in the walls, floor, and ceiling… all waiting for activation.

« Engine at 129% capacity. LIMITS CROSSED! DISCHARGE! »

« Engine at 132% capacity. LIMITS CROSSED! DISCHARGE! »

Heat pooled behind Jin's sternum, but he held it down.

Kiyon's next attack broke the pattern. Instead of another wave of firebolts, the priest raised both hands and threw a lance wrapped in his underlord flames towards him.

Okay.

Jin exhaled and clapped his hands together.

« O threads once sown and hidden from sight—marks laid in silence, bound without voice—answer now and rise as one; let distance collapse within the weave. »

Every embedded bullet activated simultaneously.

The hall was flooded by waves of chaotic and unstable essence from the detonation of his spellshots.

These waves acted as a disruption in the ambient essence, which created an essence-dead zone where all spellcasting failed.

Kiyon's lance was guttered and dispersed into just underlord flames, which were then devoured by his engine before it traveled three feet close to him.

Jin lurched forward, barely maintaining his stance as power recoiled and tore through him.

“What did you do!!!” Kiyon yelled as all the enhancement and support spells reinforcing him collapsed, and he dropped from the air like a puppet with cut strings, hitting the floor hard.

For the next couple of minutes, while his spellshots lasted, this hall denied any spellcasting.

Jin pulled a dark-red potion vial from his spatial storage.

He only looked at the berserk potion for a second before tipping it down his throat.

The liquid burned going down, flooding through his chest and spreading to every extremity with vicious speed. His muscles swelled as artificial enhancement overrode his limits. His aura surged from high Order III to peak, pressed against the threshold of breakthrough to the next rank with barely controlled fury.

Pain that had been muted by Sovereign's Indifference vanished entirely, replaced by cold aggression that demanded violence as its only outlet.

He needed an overwhelming force.

« Engine at 133% capacity. »

« Engine at 149% capacity. »

« Engine at 159% capacity. »

Kiyon was still on the floor, his hands moving in rapid succession, and Jin’s mystic eyes revealed the man had almost stabilized the chaos in the ambient essence.

Jin frowned; he wasn’t aware that this was something one could manually do.

Ordnance: Voidflare Charge.

A second essence-dead zone erupted… this one deeper, localized, and far more vicious, specifically tuned to disrupt entities up to Order IV by forcibly collapsing their essence channels from the inside.

Kiyon roared as he pushed both his arms towards the bomb with shields over them, which flickered in and out of existence, and every time they did, Kiyon spat out blood.

His armor of Underlord flames fragmented into dying embers. His aura—that crushing weight of superiority that had dominated the fight's opening—cracked and splintered as the Voidflare's effect ripped through him.

The priest was brought to his knees by the chaotic storm, his body shaking as his power was being stripped away layer by layer, and his furious shouts reverberated through the hall.

Jin pulled himself upright—groaning as muscles strained under the combined weight of Engine charges and Berserk enhancement—and let the Sovereign's Indifference fall away for the first time since the fight began.

Color bled back into the world, and with emotions came the fury he'd been storing since he'd awakened in this world.

The Regulator was gone, overridden by the Berserk state.

Angel was manually directing the flood now, diverting what he could into safe channels, but even that wasn't enough.

And as Jin walked closer to the priest with only a couple of meters between them, his Mantle of Harvest erupted.

Chains—silver and burning with white flames—exploded from his back and pierced straight into Kiyon's vulnerable form, bypassing the chaotic storm. The previous chains he'd left anchored in the priest's mind flared and latched on as well, creating a web of connections.

"Harvest."

Vitality flowed back.

Essence poured into him in a torrent.

Power—raw and stolen—surged through the chains and fed directly into the Engine.

« Engine at 161% capacity. »

Jin activated Mareth’s echo.

Iron Howl hummed with power, racing from Jin’s core to its barrel, where a brilliant white sphere formed.

His arm trembled violently, bones creaking under the strain, as even with all the enhancements, the power of hell was a calamity, and right now, Jin was holding back that calamity from taking the entire bastion over.

To flatten the land.

To turn everything into ash and silence.

Holding it.

The Engine.
