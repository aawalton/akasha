Have I slept? Will thought, eye twitching as he scanned his memories.

“Joint training. The top boys have earned the right to receive ‘lessons’ from you publicly.”

“Well, I’d hate to disappoint,” Will said, grabbing a towel and wiping the liquified miasma off his hands. That, Bri could see, since it was glowing like crazy.

“How are you not hideously deformed?” Bri asked.

“I don’t think I’m human.” Will replied, changing out of his Wizard clothes and into his ‘Lord’ clothes.

“That makes sense.” Bri replied, scanning him from top to bottom.

‘Wizard’ clothes were plain coats resistant to miasma and corrosion with plenty of pockets to hold things as he moved from experiment to experiment.

‘Lord’ Clothes were what people typically thought a Lord should be wearing in his off hours. A comfortable vest and undershirt made of cloud wool with gold accents, some trousers of the same material dyed black, and opal crocodile leather shoes gifted from Carrie Envar, lord of the 14th Floor.

Will still thought the shoes were a bit too much, but they had turned out to be the most comfortable boots he’d ever worn, so he was willing to overlook their gaudiness.

“How do I look?” Will asked once he was dressed, holding out his arms.

“Hot.” Bri said with a smile. She frowned for a moment, then pulled out a comb and straightened his hair, studying her work like an artist.

“…Nah.” Bri messed Will’s hair up again, then stood back, nodding to herself.

“Buttoned down works better with a splash of crazy-hair.” She explained.

“As always, I defer to your opinion in matters of style.” Will said, stepping off the balcony and flying down to the ground.

Since Will’s tower had been destroyed two weeks ago, he’d been able to use the anchorite he’d been accumulating in his warehouse structurally, instead of just for accents. Now each floor was floating, separated from the last by an open-air staircase that hovered in midair.

It looked cool as Abyss, and Will loved it.

Will found Ria and the guard kids doing their physical training. They all had a Strength score, but Strength was a multiplier. A weak little girl with a Strength would always be weaker than a large man with the same Strength.

That meant there was incentive for his guards to build muscle. They were currently working out in the practice yard, taking advantage of untuned Anchorite to simulate weights that were thousands of pounds.

Despite all of his guards receiving the same sacrifices, they each had chosen their own Primaries, because Will had no idea what the Class combination offered.

Their Class Sacrifices were Ethereal Metallic Slime, Roc, and Kincaid.

They had good stat growth and lots of Abilities that allowed them to control lightning and metal.

Best of all, Will didn’t have to pay for any of their Sacrifices, since he killed all of them himself.

It was a bit of mixed bag. Some of them seemed to have chosen their primaries wisely, while others…

“Check this out, I can eat lightning!”

One of the young men unhinged his jaw and a second shot lightning directly into his mouth. True to the guy’s words, the bolt of lightning writhed for a moment before disappearing like a noodle getting sucked in.

Kincaid Ability? Good or not? I shouldn’t judge before I find out the details.

“Do you get permanent buffs from eating lightning?” Will asked as he arrived beside the guard.

“Milord!” The two kids started and gave him a hasty bow, quickly echoed by the other young guards at the training ground.

The lightning shooter elbowed the one eating the lightning.

“Ah, no. Um, no I do not, it’s a temporary boost. Milord.”

“I have a friend who gained a small, stacking bonus every time he broke his shield. Keep your eyes open for an upgrade of that nature.”

“Yes, milord.” The young man said, eyes fixed on the ground.

What did I do to them to make them so meek?

“So, Ria tells me you boys have earned some joint training with your Lord?” Will asked. Behind the kneeling young men, Ria nodded.

“So what kind of lesson do you boys want?” Will asked, rolling up his fancy sleeves. “A lesson to get better at fighting…or a lesson about what a Lord can do?”

“Give me everything you got, Milord!” one of the young men said, rising to his feet and meeting Will’s gaze.

“The latter lesson then. What’s your name?”

“Aaron.” The young guard said as the other kids moved away, giving them space.

“Alright, Aaron. Start whenever you’re ready.”

“Does milord…want to wear any of his Kit?” Aaron asked.

“I’ll be fine,” Will waved off his concerns, equipping the Coiled Dimensional Serpent Amulet.

The young guard shrugged and a moment late a disc of liquid metal formed in front of him. Will watched the strings of miasma create, control and shape the liquid metal.

This is the kid who was swinging around the liquid metal earlier. His control has gotten a lot better.

Will watched as a second Ability triggered. From what Will could identify on sight, it was some kind of lightning Ability, wrapping around the disc of liquid metal. The Ability shot forward, creating a sort of…ribbed archway, creating a path through the air connecting Will and the metal disc.

Will could counter it, but he was curious to see how it worked.

It was obviously meant to guide and accelerate the metal disc, so Will reflexively shoved out with Miasma and diverted the path of the archways to go up and over his shoulder.

An instant later, the metal disc accelerated at a blinding speed that caught even Will off guard, moving faster than even Phantom Hand was capable of. Faster than Will could even react.

…up and over Will’s shoulder.

That could’ve killed me!

Will didn’t let his surprise show on his face, instead standing there with his arms crossed.

“…You missed.” Will said when he recovered his poise.

“Ooooh!” The other kids hooted, seemingly eager to see the top performer taken down a peg.

“Since you asked, I suppose I should stop holding back.” Will said, his eyes narrowing menacingly.

…He was absolutely going to hold back.

Dead guards were of no value.

Will snipped the control threads on the liquid metal, sending it careening away.

Aaron made a wise choice to dismiss his summon and create a new ball of metal rather than try to regain control of the distant metal while Will was so close.

Will snipped the control threads of the new one as well, then gently shoved the sphere into Aaron’s chest, causing the young man’s eyes to bulge as his own Ability struck him in the chest.

Will couldn’t help but give his advice.

“You need to develop finer perception of miasma. This is largely dictated by Focus, but there are a few sensory organs that could give you the edge you need. You can check the warehouse this evening. Until you can sense Miasma, my Abilities will be invisible to you, which will place you on the defensive.

If someone has demonstrated the ability to break the control over your Abilities, don’t just summon it again directly in front of your chest. Throw some sand in my eyes, make a distraction, anything to increase my cognitive load enough to give you time to use your Abilities.”

Aaron shot a hand forward, and Will saw a simple thread of lightning miasma arc out. Will cut it near the base, then tapped his toe on the ground.

A pillar of earth caught Aaron in the crotch.

“Keep yourself light and mobile. Advanced movement techniques can negate terrain advantages. Find them wherever you can.” Will called as Aaron rolled back to his feet.

Will frowned as he spotted Travis among the audience, looking absolutely pissed.

Will walked over to Travis, continuing to counter and redirect the young guard’s Abilities as he moved, keeping the beating within reason.

Sure, Will said he was going to teach them what a Lord could do, but there was a certain level of swift defeat where nothing of value was learned. He was obligated to toy with the kid for a bit first so that Aaron could understand he was being toyed with.
