I wanted to share some exciting news about another fantasy project of mine, Of Sand and Silver.

The first book, A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea, is about to be published, and things have been getting quite interesting behind the scenes.

Don't worry. I'm not about to bury you under twenty paragraphs of ISBNs, Amazon listings, distribution headaches, and my increasingly questionable sleep schedule.

I have a few updates to share, including some good news about book prices, early reader reviews, and a little surprise involving the cover artwork for Books Two and Three.

And I'd love your opinion on that last one!

We're less than two weeks away from the November 10 release of A Lullaby of Thieves and Tea!

Since my last announcement, the Goodreads reviews have continued coming in. We've gone from 20 ratings and 18 reviews to 35 ratings and 30 written reviews, with a current average of 4.82/5 stars as of October 28.

GOOD NEWS ABOUT THE PRICES, TOO!

I've managed to lower the retail prices for the physical editions! So if you checked the listings before and thought they were a bit expensive, you might want to take another look.

And here's a little tip: you can also order directly from me through my website, potentially for an even cheaper price!

Well, depending on where you live, that is. International shipping has a wonderful habit of ruining a perfectly good bargain. So I'd recommend comparing the final prices, including shipping, before ordering.

I've also started contacting bookstores here in Central Florida where I live about potential author signings and literary events. Nothing confirmed yet, but I'm working on it!
