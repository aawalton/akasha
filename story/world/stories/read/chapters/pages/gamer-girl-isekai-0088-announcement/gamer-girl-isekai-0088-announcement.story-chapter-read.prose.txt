📜 Synopsis:

Over fifteen decades, Silenos has become an experienced magician of House Shaiagrazni, revered for his intellect and power. But when he dares summon a fearsome Entity, things quickly go awry, and he's transported from his sleek tungsten chamber into a cold, barren stone room in an unfamiliar world. There, he's surrounded by a crowd of ignorant savages who call him "Savior" . . .

Ensharia, a paladin in this backward nation, leads him to its royal rulers, who beg his help fighting off the Dark Lord's forces. An army of rotting abominations is pouring over their border, far outnumbering their defending troops. There's nothing for Silenos to gain by assisting these people, with their primitive technology and weak magic, against such superior warriors. But he decides they need a great leader such as himself.

When Ensharia accompanies Silenos into battle and witnesses his mastery of the dark arts, she's horrified. But when Silenos's necromancy saves countless lives, she resolves to put her misgivings aside. As they plunge into a sea of carnage, Ensharia will find her endurance and spirit tested like never before.

Meanwhile, before Silenos can reach the heights of greatness he strives for, the former commander himself will have to learn how to fight. He'll confront barbarians, revolutionaries, and the stirring of not only his hunger for unlimited power but also emotions long locked away . . .
