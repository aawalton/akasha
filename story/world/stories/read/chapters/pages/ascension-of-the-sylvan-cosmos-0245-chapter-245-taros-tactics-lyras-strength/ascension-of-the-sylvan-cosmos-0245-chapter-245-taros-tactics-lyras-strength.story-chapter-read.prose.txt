On Lyra's side, the armies were in a balanced situation. Taro was keeping the enemy in check while Lyra was slowly advancing in formation. Both sides were spending Aether Mana and Anima Energy at a regular pace.

Lyra looked toward Taro and squinted. He had not attacked even once, making her doubtful of what he was planning. While she had been fighting with her army, it was clear that she was making too little progress. 'At least I've managed to fill the moat, and now we can start attacking the doors.'

Looking sideways, she called a group of turtle men that she had on her side. These people were from an allied clan that owed her something and had come as a way to return the favor. "You twenty will advance while being protected and carry this item to the door. Once you place it down, retreat and use your defensive skills. If you manage to blow up the doors, I'll count the favor as paid."

Lyra nodded. "Understood." Then, she shouted. "Open path!" Those were the keywords for the Aethermancers to create the defensive path.

In an instant, around 400 people moved in perfect coordination right as the twenty turtle men rushed forward.

As soon as he finished explaining, the people on the wall finished charging their more powerful attacks and released them en masse toward the weakened army. Lyra saw the enemies on the wall redoubling their efforts at attacking her people and her face fell. 'Are you really going to ignore the door!? Madman!'

Taro's eyes swept the battlefield and saw several attacks striking true and managing to either kill or severely injure many people. He nodded and ordered. "Evacuate the wall above the doors and command them to hide in the reinforced defense sites near the entrance."

The assistant quickly nodded and set off, carrying his orders.

Lyra scoffed and focused on the door. Seeing that the twenty turtle men had done it, she smiled and shouted. "Spear Formation! We are going to breach into the city soon!"

The army moved with her words as the commanders repeated her orders. The soldiers moved from a stretched-out line to a more compact formation. It was clear that they were preparing to breach through the doors.

BOOOOM!

However, it seems that it wasn't completely out of our expectations. The protective areas are still standing." Seeing the walls in such a miserable condition, he felt a bit of heartache. 'Repairing this will cost a fortune.'

Both armies readied their bodies, tensed their muscles, and then pushed forward into a frontal clash.

CLANG! CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!

A large amount of a black substance flew and fell on the enemies. Because they were so close, unlike Folter, they failed to react and were completely bathed in it. Instantly, several fire spells and arrows landed, igniting the fluid.

WHOOSH!

A firestorm surged on the chokepoint, spreading back toward the people who started screaming in pain as they burnt alive.

Taro looked over and smiled. "Finally. Activate the <Humming Array>!"

As soon as his orders spread, hidden people nearby rushed forward and placed their hands against the wall. Their energies sunk into the wall, lighting up several formation lines that soon started vibrating at a very low frequency and high intensity. For humans, this would create slight discomfort and nausea. However, for Sylvans, it was a bit more disrupting than that.

Lyra was about to use her frost powers to stop the fire when a strident sound pierced her ears, making her widen her eyes and hold her head in agony. "Ah! What is this!?"

HUMMMMM~.

The vibrations of infrasound affected all the transformed Sylvans, forcing them to hold their ears. Their bodies felt like they were trembling from the inside out, making some of the weaker Sylvans puke out of reflex.

Lyra glared at the walls and shouted. "<Moonlit Flowerbloom>!"

Her back opened, showing several bell-like white flowers that aimed at the walls and fired a ray of pure frost. As the ice sunk into the walls, the array began to stop its vibrations, slowly returning the combat capability to the Sylvans.

Taro frowned and commented. "Roll down the stones. I didn't know Lyra had this attack. Is it something new she got recently?"

Several spherical boulders started rolling down from the middle of the street that led toward the door. The allied army parted without problems since the enemies were still dealing with the fire and couldn't really push through.

At first, Taro had thought that the Sylvans would rush and attack the walls. However, he had prepared reinforcing <Arrays>, which would take away their attention for a long time. In the meantime, he would thin down the enemy army, and when they restarted their attack, he would use the boulders to run over a few more people.

Sadly, that plan was completely invalidated by Lyra alone. At the end of the day, Lyra was a Level 29 who could easily fight the average Level 30.

Taro saw the large boulders rolling down the slope and striking into the fiery mess, creating more confusion and fear in the people who were surrounded by flames.

'I wonder if they can finish before we lose here. I have a few more tricks up my sleeve, but I don't think I can hold Lyra back for more than two more hours…'

Lyra and the other Sylvans finally made the walls stop vibrating, and Taro's heart tightened. 'Too quick. Even if I call for help, it might be too late by the time it arrives. There are at least 100 transformed Sylvans in the enemy group…' He frowned and sighed. 'Too many will die, but I really don't have any other option.'

Taro was about to tell his assistant to inform Delia that he needed two of those serpents to fight Lyra together with him. However, before he gave the order, he heard a powerful and penetrative beastly roar coming from the sky.

"Hm?"
