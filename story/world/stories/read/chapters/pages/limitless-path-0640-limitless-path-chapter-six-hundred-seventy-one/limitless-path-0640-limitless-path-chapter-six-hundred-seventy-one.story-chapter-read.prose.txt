“Maybe we should just throw our valiant diplomat out to the wolves?” Beth asked then with a raised eyebrow. “Did you know she’s the heir to the kresnik clan? And the former leader of the Young Scions?”

“Right. There are also people who lose their homes or lots, typically by buying beyond their means; very few people can afford to fully purchase a property here. Even teams with vast wealth often have to either rent a place, which is somewhat rare, or take a loan to make a purchase,” she continued. “Let’s just say…not every team is as lucky, or skilled, as every other. Some teams fall onto hard times, some teams are driven to hard times, and some teams disappear, an event that is, unfortunately, more common as of late.”

Even the loss of two or three members of a team can be devastating. This is far more true when jockeying for position on Millennia’s Echo and in the City than elsewhere; a team that losses a member on another world won’t feel it nearly as keenly, operationally speaking, as they would here. Some teams have one or two members that are key anchors to the team, wherein the rest of the team is more of a support unit for those one or two individuals. Such a team can afford one or two losses, if those losses are secondary members, as harsh as that is to say. What they cannot afford is the loss of one of those pillar members holding the whole team together.”

“Yes, that’s certainly true, but they wouldn’t be ones selling any property,” Isoldorra replied, making Beth blink for a second.

“Of course, you’re speaking specifically to our situation,” Val replied smoothly.

“That’s correct,” she nodded. “Your best bets are, roughly in this order, to go for a team that is collapsing, as mercenary as that sounds, to purchase an ‘extra’ unit from someone who has more than one, and finally to work together with someone more closely to earn housing.”

“What about the CRA?” Beth asked, curious what Isoldorra’s response would be.

“What about them?” she replied with a raise eyebrow, Beth thinking how she kept her eyes closed made her expressions even nicer. “If you mean to imply, what about housing from the CRA, then all I can tell you is good luck. There are waiting lists for everything here, including every suite and apartment in the several CRA Halls in the City. Even the other CRA Halls on the world have waiting lists, and they are not nearly as in-demand as the local Halls. If you mean any other housing they may have, I would be highly doubtful; I am not a CRA member, beyond Copper, of course, and do not have much insight to their inner workings. However, what I will say is that even Diamond Emblems aren’t given preferential treatment here, to my knowledge.”

To be brutally honest, a recommendation from Illiem and your current performance is not enough for us to consider shuffling people or freeing a unit for your lease or purchase.”

“Not totally surprising,” Beth said, cutting off something Andrea had been about to say. “You had something, Andrea?”

“Enough,” Beth replied, though there was no heat to her voice.

“At the least? I don’t like the sound of that,” muttered Val.

“Things here are not cheap. It is, however, a full purchase of a unit,” Isoldorra said. “You will, of course, have to pay the property tax on the unit, but the unit will belong to whoever wins the purchase free and clear.”

Beth exchanged a look with Sera before exchanging just as pointed of a one with Val and then the rest of the group. It really circled back to what Isoldorra had commented on at the start of the conversation; Beth’s team was incredibly strong. On top of that, they had her gate, which they really used a lot for farming experience and the quests for True Rebirths, but it had quite a lot of money-making potential. She had thought they were pretty good on money at this point, but now she was planing on pulling in some more money for the auction.

It would take multiple remarkable performances in a row, which is certainly possible, don’t get me wrong, but you would still have to commit quite a lot of time and effort to working for us to get there.”

Farming for money was not always the most thrilling thing, but it was something they had known they were going to need to do in earnest for a while now. Maybe Mortaine or somebody had a job they would pay really well for that had a bunch of rare materials along the way, or maybe they could hit up Fallon and see if he knew of any juicy spots or jobs. “How does one get into this auction?” Val asked then, giving Beth a bit of a side-eye.

“Yeah, yeah, should have asked.”

“That won’t be an issue,” Isoldorra said quietly, waving a hand in a negative gesture. “We can easily secure a pass for your team, if that is what you want?”

“Please do so,” Beth said firmly, several other team members nodding in agreement.

“Very well. I will have a pass for the auction for you by the end of the day. Would you like me to send it somewhere, or would you like me to contact you so you can pick it up when it’s ready?” she said.

“Of course. Feel free to use any of our representatives, including Illiem, to not just sell items, but arrange for consignment,” Isoldorra said with a small smile.
