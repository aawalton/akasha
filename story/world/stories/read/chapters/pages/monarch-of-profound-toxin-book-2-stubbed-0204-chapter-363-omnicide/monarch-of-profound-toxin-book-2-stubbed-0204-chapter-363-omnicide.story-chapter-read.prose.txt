Chapter 363

As if he himself were the epicenter of a Death Star detonation, blue flared outward from Eik’s body.

Well, actually, this outburst didn’t seem to be a destructive explosion in the same as Death Star was. It was more like a forceful externalization of his power. The ground he stood on wasn’t blown away by a wave of force, but instead pulverized and held in place. The soil and the air vibrated so intensely that the sight of Eik in the center was distorted and stretched to an extreme degree.

Then, without warning, his body began to change.

“Ho woah, wait, wait!” he yelped as he was raised into the air slowly, suspended as if by hundreds of invisible strings. Taken by surprise, he fluttered and spluttered futilely, unable to return himself to the ground. “Hey, something’s happening, dude!” he called, directing his words inwardly. “I’m changing! Stop, for fuck’s sake!”

Despite his own alarm, Profound Toxin didn’t reflect any of the concern, and within a few seconds, a sense of familiarity and calm washed through him like a cool glass of water on a hot summer’s day. “I’m not going to turn into some formless blob for the rest of my life, am I?” he asked, still not completely convinced.

More calm, now filled with reassurance. Well, okay, then.

Trickle by trickle, the energy that had been expelled from his body during that first release now returned to him, flowing into him through his skin in luminescent blue motes of light. Gradually, the rate of absorption hastened and Eik felt himself nearly burst with power.

He wasn’t actually going to burst… right?

The first thing to visibly change in his physique was his size. He had already been pretty tall for a human man but with this transformation, he was quickly overtaking even Heath’s enormous frame. Growth ceased when he had just rounded the three meter mark, but was nowhere near the end of the transformation. And it only got weirder.

It was an exceedingly concerning sensation to feel it happen in real time, and Eik found himself wishing he could have been put into a state of unconsciousness to avoid the experience.

Running a finger down his face and jaw, he could feel his features grow more angular by the second. From his head, shoulders, knuckles, elbows, hips, and lower back, among other places, long, slender protrusions like feathers sprouted in thick clusters.

Along their surfaces ran countless tiny channels through which blindingly bright Profound Toxin flowed like blood flowing through external vessels. They didn’t cover him completely—far from it, actually, but rather looked like primitive, natural decoration. Certainly no good for flight.

An interesting point was that they didn’t grow down his limbs and trunk, but rather up, forming a formation that resembled a crown around his shoulders with his further feathered head in the center.

What the hell was going on here?

Pressure pushed through his gums as his human canine teeth extended to twice their original length, tapering to sharp points that would surely have hurt his tongue if it hadn’t been so much stronger due to his X-ranked body.

Even weirder was the tail that suddenly began to extend from his coccyx, growing long enough to sweep the ground like a broom. And the feathers sprouting along its length only contributed further to that image. Then it grew longer still, eventually stopping once it was almost twice as long as his body from head to toe.

Instinctively, his mind and body knew exactly how to move the long appendage, and he lashed it around his body in a spiral pattern.

Damn, this was weird as all hell.

When the horns came, he gave up on surprise and just let it happen.

Like his feathers, the horns also had smooth channels running up their lengths, Profound Toxin flowing along. At about a hand span, their structure divided once, and then again, and again, multiple times.

So, not horns, but antlers? Or were they still horns? Was that how that worked? One of those not all fingers are thumbs but all thumbs are fingers kind of thing? Not all horns are antlers but all antlers are horns? Whatever. He had more important things to think about. Namely, everything.

Eik had seen large stags in the deer gardens around Denmark many times before and he had always thought that they must have been walking around with perpetually stiff necks on account of their ridiculously sized headpieces. But, now that he had a pair himself, they really didn’t weigh anything, as it turned out.

Well, now he felt kind of stupid for pitying those grass-chewing giants.

And with that, it seemed to be over. Slowly, he was lowered back down to the ground. Was that… it? He’d turned into a very large man with feathers, antlers, and a tail? What the hell was this supposed to be?

And it was named Omnicide? Had something finally gone wrong?

[Natural trait evolution triggered — Profound Toxin: True Toxic Harmony]

[Profound Toxin evolutionary trait True Toxic Harmony elevated and absorbed]

[True Toxic Harmony elevated and absorbed into Profound Toxin — Omnicide]

Fuck… True Toxic Harmony had arguably been the most powerful personal buff in his arsenal, and now it was gone, completely without asking him for his opinion. This Omnicide crap had better be worth it or he was going to be pissed for real.

“Be honest here, man,” he said out loud, directed at Profound Toxin inside his spirit. “Did you screw me on this? Don’t tell me I just lost a big chunk of my power in favor of being able to turn into a god damned bird man…”

The blue bastard just used one of its strongest and most annoying abilities. Profound Radio Silence.

Groaning, Eik looked down at his hands. His nails had grown to points as well. But what part of this was supposed to be Omnicide?

As curiosity warred with worry in his chest, a sleek, crystalline katana appeared in his hand. Without much thought to it, he swung the blade in a downward slash, the motion beginning high above his shoulder and ending at his feet.

Everything a couple of kilometers in front of him was annihilated to completion, dust and debris torn up as if by an underground explosive detonation of ruinous proportions. Material quickly reached the zenith of the atmosphere before losing upward acceleration. It fell back down like rain around him, forming sand banks in the area.

A few waves of his hand miraculously cleared away the dust, leaving only a gaping abyss in the earth too deep to see the bottom of. He was reminded of the demonstration Chop had given of his power some time ago. What the old man had done could have contained a city.

Hands on hips, Eik studied the devastation he had wrought. Yeah, a city definitely fit in there as well. And he hadn’t even been trying his best. Maybe growing those damned feathers wasn’t such a bad thing after all.

And his physical capabilities weren’t the only thing affected by his transformation. He could feel it.

At a thought, thick, glistening Profound Toxin poured out of him and pooled at his feet. Within his spirit had appeared something that almost felt like a switch.

He flipped it with a flex of his will. And immediately began to sink.

Like a terrifyingly potent acid, Profound Toxin was eating away at the earth. Yes, he had certainly always been able to destroy inorganic matter with Accelerant, and he had been able to contaminate some things, like liquids, with toxin in order to make it deadly.

And sure, it could swallow some things, like blood, dust, and grime, but this was on a completely different level.

It offered an entirely new aspect of power. This was erasure of matter. It probably wouldn’t be able to just outright destroy the Lord of the Moon like nothing—after all, Profound Toxin had been waging war against the guy for eons—but this was a game changer nonetheless.

It was a weapon capable of punching through the Lord of the Moon’s defenses. That filthy, undulating, parasitic swarm he was capable of deploying would be toast now.

“Is this why you’ve been such a choleric little baby? Because we didn’t have this against the six-armed asshole last time?”

Enraged nodding.

“Well, you should have given it to me earlier, then. It’s not like I would have said no.”

Wiggling and squiggling offered some vague hints.

“I needed to cross this threshold first?”

Yes!

“Okay, but, if you don’t mind me asking… Why did we have to go with feathers?”

Esoteric hand-waving and unintelligible gestures were what he got in response.

“I’m not catching what you’re throwing here, buddy. Can you try that again, but a little less crazy?”

It did not get better after that. Only fussier.

“All right, I’ve had enough of this! I’m coming in there,” Eik muttered and headed to Toxin.

***

“I’m you?” Eik exclaimed.

“No. You have just… taken on some aspects that I used to have,” Profound Toxin replied.

They were sitting on the porch of the house the Worldbreaker had built with Eik’s home as inspiration. Quite a lot had been added to the place, both to the house itself as well as to the garden.

Tall, vibrantly colored flowers swayed in the wind all around the garden. The toxic entity didn’t seem to care nearly as much about order in the garden as Eik did. Maybe that was one point where they still differed.

Two hanging chairs were suspended from the ceiling of the porch pent roof, creaking slightly as they swayed under their weights. Eik didn’t have anything like this on his porch, but damn if he wasn’t going to. They looked great too.

Wait. Did this ancient, cosmic dominator also happen to have a talent for design and decoration?

“Used to have? What are you talking about?” Eik asked with a deep frown. “You’re the avatar of Toxin. You were created by the world itself, weren’t you?”

The gooey facsimile of Eik’s own face stared back at him, as expressionless as always. “Not… correct.”

“What? You’re the one who told me! I didn’t just make this story up in my head.”

“You’re always… making shit up on the spot.”

It was Eik’s time to stare. Roasted by a blob of snot. “So Toxin didn’t create you?”

“It… totally did.”

“Okay, now you’re just fucking with me, aren’t you?”

A moment’s pause. “Yes.”

Eik sighed. “All right, you got me.”

“Ha… ha ha.” The laughter was so painfully staccato that Eik couldn’t help but curl his bare toes against the wooden floor.

“What then? What’s the story?”

“I was both created by Toxin and… not created by Toxin.”

Eik massaged the bridge of his nose with a groan. “Okay…”

A shadow of a smile touched its semi-translucent lips. “I was created by Toxin, but before that I lived here. I… grew up here.”
