Waking up after a long slumber within a Dungeon World was very much unlike anything he had ever experienced before. It wasn't the fact that he was in a dangerous place itself, but rather the fact that he had woken up more sore than he could imagine. Everything hurt. His toes and fingers. Jaw, eyebrows, forehead, and even his neck felt a knick or two. That wasn’t mentioning areas he couldn’t believe could become sore, like his armpits, hip, and more.

He struggled to get off the hammock, groaning and moaning with every single movement. Worse was that every step he took sent a shock of dull pain through his entire body. Unable to do anything else, William summoned himself a couple of cups of water and downed them without hesitation.
