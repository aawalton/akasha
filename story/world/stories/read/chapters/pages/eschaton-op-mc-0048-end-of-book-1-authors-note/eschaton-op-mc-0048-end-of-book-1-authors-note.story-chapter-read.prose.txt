Hello! If you're reading this and haven't read the last chapter, Chapter 47 - Devoured Dreams, you probably should. Anyway.

So the posting schedule is going to change. Right now I'm doing 1 a day because I actually had completed Book 1 before this launch. (This is actually a relaunch of Eschaton) Book 2 is not complete and I don't believe that I could write 1 chapter a day at the quality I want. So for now, we're going down to 2 a week. I know that's a big step down, but 4-5k of polished words a week is really quite difficult. I'm hoping that someday I'll be able to go up to 3 a week, but 2 a week is more comfortable for me at this stage and so that's where we'll start from. I am sorry about this, but that's just how it is.

I'll post Mondays and Fridays, so the first chapter of Book 2 will go live tomorrow, have no fear. Now onto my thoughts on Book 1.

Book 1 is the first novel I have ever written, but it will not be last. Unfortunately, given it is the first novel I have written, it is quite heavily flawed in many areas. Many of you have (very kindly!) caught misspellings or incorrect words or the like. I think the overall structure was probably alright but could have been better. Eschaton actually started off as a side project I just really wanted to write before it became a main project. The first 10,000 words were written in like three days. In total, the first posted draft of Eschaton was 100,400 words over 47 chapters written in almost exactly 5 months. Which, honestly, is incredible. It did come at a cost. I gave myself Repetitive Stress Injury in both my wrists, thought mainly my right one. I couldn't write for nearly two months and I had to buy a very expensive ergonomic keyboard to be able to continue to use a computer at all. (An Iris SE from Keebio, if you're curious. If you want an ergonomic board I recommend them very heavily, their customer support is goated) Only my massive backlog and rather slow posting schedule at the time saved me from delaying chapters.

I am much better now, and I've written Book 2 at more or less the same-ish or better pace than Book 1, which is nice. I'm trying to write a better Book 2, but it's very hard at times. Writing is a lonely and difficult profession, and I'm far from a professional. Writing into what feels like the void at times hurts a lot, especially when it feels like I'm writing absolute peak. But your collective support and nice words and TFTCs are all very encouraging. When I decided on my relaunch, I truly believed that I could hit main RS, and that required alot of self-delusion. Which recently collapsed at the same time I had to reorder the beginning of book 2 entirely, and that sucked big time. Though Main RS is almost truly out of reach now, that's fine. For my first web serial, Eschaton has been wildly successful. Though it'll never be picked up by any publisher (which would be very cool) for a variety of reasons, that's alright. There are people who read Eschaton, and I can honestly say that's very awesome. So here's to Book 1! I hope you all liked the ending (as I'm writing this the chapter has yet to go live so I have no idea! I'm only extremely nervous, it's fine), and I hope you're all excited for Eschaton's Book 2! See you tomorrow!
