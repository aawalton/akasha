It had been a long time since Xavier had dissected a beast.

He vividly remembered using Dismantle on the beasts he’d slain when fighting against the Endless Horde, all those years ago, early in the Tower of Champions. He’d used it many times since then, but hardly at all lately.

Xavier knelt by the corpse of the Gorgotlin he’d summoned back out of his Storage Ring, peering curiously at its body. This was a created beast. Something he’d known, considering he was inside a Void Dungeon. But it made him realise he hadn’t encountered a great many created beasts.

He wondered how it might function differently to a normal beast.

Then there was the fact it wasn’t from his home universe, or even his corner of the multiverse. It would have many differences.

The first thing he’d noticed about these created beasts was that they lacked a soul. He’d noticed that well before he’d killed one of them. It hadn’t been worth noting at the time. Then there was the fact that they only had one core.

Is it like the Reality Core, I wonder? Cultivators only have one core, after all. Well, technically they have two if you include the soul. Then there are some Denizens who only have one visible core, the others hidden inside them, hard to find until they’re more empowered.

He could sense the energy inside that core easily enough now the thing was dead, and while it was similar to Reality Energy, there were some acute differences.

Xavier created a bone scalpel and began slicing through the beast’s fur-covered flesh, pushing hard to get past the powerful protective layer around it—a layer that was still strong despite the thing being dead, which meant it wasn’t some skill or spell that had made it that way.

This beast already had wounds, of course, from when he’d killed it. But he wanted a more direct line to its core. The moment he opened the beast up, the stench it produced increased three-fold, and it was already pretty damned bad. He compartmentalised his sense of smell, having another part of his mind take over that process, and continued cutting into the beast.

As he opened the energy channels, the energy within leaked out into the air, floating idly, getting pushed around like it was nothing more than a mote of dust. He touched a hand to the energy, focusing the full might of his Immortal Soul lens on it.

The likeness to Reality Energy was strong. Wherever this Void Dungeon had come from, there was a good chance it was similar to the place where Xavier’s Reality Energy came from—not that Xavier knew anything about that particular corner of the multiverse.

Maybe I’ll pay it a visit sometime in the future. Learn from a true Cultivator…

Xavier tried to see if he could take the energy into his body. Maybe there was a chance he could create another core, like he had with the Reality Core.

Walking a third path of advancement might not be such a bad thing.

But the energy wouldn’t enter his body. In that way, it didn’t act as Reality Energy did, because Xavier had been able to have trillions upon trillions of Denizens in his empire take on Reality Energy in this exact same way, and he was far more powerful and experienced than any of his citizens had been.

He sighed. Well, he supposed that would have been a little too easy.

Xavier made his way to the core. Cores didn’t often survive the death of a Denizen or beast, not back in his universe. A core was held together by the cycling of energy, even if unconsciously, and when that cycling stopped… it didn’t take long for a core to disintegrate and for all its energy to simply disappear as it leaked out into the world.

It was kind of tragic, he supposed.

This core wasn’t like a Denizen core or a Reality Core, however. Like the beast, it was artificial. More artificial than the cores he was used to dealing with. It reminded him a little of the Personal System he’d just had installed into his mind. It was a sphere, similar in shape and size to that of a crystal ball.

Energy didn’t spark or crackle within it. It flowed, swirling around like there was a whirlpool trapped inside. The more he examined the core, the more he noticed the strangeness of it. There was some kind of spatial distortion within, showing that it was likely far bigger on the inside.

Xavier cocked his head to the left, peering at the core. It was cold to the touch. Odd. He figured it would have been warm.

There was more he could do to examine it himself, and he figured he had all the time in the world in this place what with the ancient Wanderer, Derrida, having assured him that no time would pass back home while he was here, but he figured it was time to use the Personal System to identify this thing.

Void Dungeon Mini Core

This item is found inside beasts created by the Void Dungeon. All naturally occurring dungeons of the same type produce beasts with Dungeon Mini Cores. Dungeon Mini Cores are connected to the dungeon itself. The spatial distortion you can sense acts similar to that of a portal, connecting it to the main Void Dungeon Core.

The Dungeon Mini Core doesn’t merely produce power, it also acts as the beast’s brain. Instructions are fed to the beast through the Dungeon Mini Core.

Xavier blinked. A Void Dungeon Mini Core? From all he knew about dungeons, he’d never heard of nor encountered anything like this. He smiled. It was nice to find something new. Nice to come across something completely novel.

Can I use the Dungeon Energy inside for myself? Convert it into Celestial Energy?

Yes. Though you do not currently have the requisite skills to perform the task.

What happens if I pierce the Void Dungeon Mini Core to get to the energy inside? If it’s connected to the main Void Dungeon Core, could I somehow siphon energy from the dungeon itself?

If the sphere is penetrated, the Void Dungeon will cut off the connection to its main core.

Xavier frowned. That wasn’t optimal, but he supposed it made sense. He wouldn’t be the first person to think of something like that, after all.

The Void Dungeon was beginning to feel more like the nest of some hivemind beast, and he supposed it technically was. The Void Dungeon-created beasts, though they would be significantly more varied, would be a lot like the Arakashinai drones, in that they were controlled by the dungeon itself just as the drones had been controlled by the Arakashinai Queen.

Except, this was more. The energy was connected to the main Void Dungeon Core. It made him very curious if he would somehow be able to use that to his advantage.

Is there a way to circumvent the disconnection?

This information is unknown to me.

Xavier made a hmmm noise. He couldn’t help but notice the wording the Personal System had used—that the information was unknown to it, not that it wasn’t out there somewhere. Perhaps this was something Derrida knew… but if she hadn’t given that information to the Personal System, she was unlikely to simply offer it up if he asked. Not that he knew how to contact the woman.

She’s probably watching me, just like she was watching me the entire time I was back in my home universe. I remember being able to feel her, though I doubt I’d be able to feel her watching now…

What happens if I place the Void Dungeon Mini Core in my Storage Ring? Some spatial devices couldn’t be placed inside a Storage Ring, and some could. Sometimes, a device could be placed inside a Storage Ring but doing so would damage it.

But the moment he asked the question he realised how foolish it was. He didn’t know where his mind was at to miss that—he had already placed the Void Dungeon Mini Core inside his Storage Ring, when it had still been inside the corpse of the Gorgotlin.

Inside your Storage Ring, the Void Dungeon Mini Core will be preserved. Energy cannot enter it or leave it. The Void Dungeon will not be able to pull the energy within it back into the main core.

Xavier focused his Immortal Soul lens on the Void Dungeon Mini Core again. There was a noticeable difference in how much energy the core now held. It must have lost roughly 5 percent of its energy since the last time he’d examined it—and that wasn’t very long ago.

The instant he noticed this, Xavier placed the Void Dungeon Mini Core back inside his Storage Ring. He knelt at the Gorgotlin corpse, touched a hand to it, and placed the created beast back in his Storage Ring as well. Then he stood and glanced around.

It was still quiet in the Dark Tunnel. According to the Personal System, nothing would attack him until he moved on, and though he still felt wary he was inclined to believe the Personal System’s assertion.

He summoned another of the Gorgotlin corpses and used the same bone scalpel to extract the Void Dungeon Mini Core from this one as well, placing it back inside his Storage Ring. He did the same from the last three corpses, getting faster at the process each time—the faster he was, the less energy the cores lost while outside his storage device.

He could do more before moving on. He could try to figure out how to use the energy inside the cores. That would help him replenish far more energy than simply killing one of these beasts did, assuming he was able to convert it into raw Celestial Energy, but he wanted to move forward through the dungeon, not linger here for much longer.

There would be time to experiment with these cores later.

He had a Void Dungeon to explore.

Xavier created a bone spear as he walked down the Dark Tunnel, heading deeper into the dungeon. He pushed out his spiritual sense, using the full force of his Immortal Soul lens. The Personal System had told him he could sense more than he realised here. That he hadn’t reached his full potential at his current level of power. Xavier found that hard to believe. Roln had made a great many adaptions to his neural network, reinforcing every single skill he had. His ability to sense energy should have been completely maxed out. So what exactly could he sense that he didn’t realise he could sense?

He started to walk more slowly, even though he was eager for another encounter. He let his mind enter a familiar meditative state. One that he could slip into in an instant. A calm fell over him like a heated blanket in the middle of a particularly cold winter day.

It was still strange, not having Roln. It made him realise how much he’d come to rely on the mental construct. Not just for information or companionship, but for the refinements it constantly bestowed upon him. Those refinements to his neural network were a godsend. They made it possible for him to do so much. Xavier could execute those refinements himself—he’d done a few already, between extractions of the Void Dungeon Mini Cores—but he was nowhere near as efficient and skilled at it as Roln. Roln could make adjustments almost instantly. For Xavier, it took far more effort and concentration, even if it only really took a few seconds to do inside his mind. It wasn’t something he could do while in the middle of a fight like he could with Roln. Not yet.

I’m going to have to teach myself how to do that…

His mind was wandering. He reined it in, forcing his focus on one thing and one thing only—his spiritual sense. He sunk into his Immortal Soul lens, and rather than rely on refinements Roln might have made, or refinements he could find for himself, Xavier simply used his instincts.

His instincts told him there was something out there. Something just beyond the strange, artificial darkness. Something that was watching him.

And it was far more powerful than the Gorgotlin he’d just faced.
