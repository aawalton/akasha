The sun dipped low on the horizon as they crested the final dune before Grimroost. The town’s stone walls glowed in the amber light of sunset, casting long shadows across the desert floor. In stark contrast, the ominous silhouette of the dragon loomed behind them, its hulking body backlit by the fiery sky, a living nightmare etched against the heavens.

Ada’s breath hitched as she clung to the saddle. Her wide, pink eyes stared unblinkingly at the monstrous figure. For once, her usual stream of complaints faltered, replaced by wide-eyed terror. "You… you don’t think it followed us, do you?" she whispered, her voice trembling as if afraid the dragon might hear her.

Sigurd didn’t answer right away. His gaze remained fixed on the massive creature, his jaw tightening with grim determination. He adjusted his grip on the reins, his knuckles whitening. "No," he finally said, his voice low and steady, "I don’t think it followed us. I know it did."

A deafening roar split the air, sending vibrations through the ground. Ada yelped, nearly losing her grip. "Well, that’s just fantastic! Maybe next time, we can try not leading a fire-breathing behemoth back to a populated area. Just a suggestion!"

Sigurd cast her a side glance, his brow twitching with irritation. "If you’re going to yell, at least make it something useful."

Ada threw her arms up in mock surrender, her voice dripping with sarcasm. "Oh, sure! Let me just pull out my handy ‘Dragon-Slaying for Dummies’ guide! Wait, I forgot it in my nonexistent dragon-proof workshop!"

Sigurd gave a short, humorless chuckle, spurring the horse forward. "Keep that wit handy. You’ll need it when we’re face-to-face with that thing."

Another roar echoed behind them, louder and closer. Ada’s fake bravado faltered as she gripped Sigurd’s armor. "Face-to-face? Oh no, no, no. I’m strictly a background player here. Support role, moral encouragement, sarcastic commentary. You’re the hero!"

Sigurd smirked, his eyes narrowing as the dragon’s shadow began to stretch across Grimroost. "Guess it’s time to play the part, then."
