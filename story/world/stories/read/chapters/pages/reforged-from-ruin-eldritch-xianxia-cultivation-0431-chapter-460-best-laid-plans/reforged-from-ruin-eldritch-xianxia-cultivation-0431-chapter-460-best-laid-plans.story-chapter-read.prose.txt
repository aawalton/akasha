The man who Is Not There can wipe out anything of Raika, so long as it self-identifies as Raika or is similar enough to other parts of her.

We Are What We Eat.

She ate a Feng.

She’s not sure what other plans the gestalt has beyond that, it’s too hard to remember right now, but it matters less because it worked.

Between her Truth and the likelihood that whatever used to be the Raika-That-Was would also attack anything truly Feng on sight, she gambled. Between impossibilities, she guessed and decided to believe in certain possibilities.

She ate the Feng. She Is what she Eats. The Feng is strong enough to fight back. If the Feng becomes weakened enough, damaged enough, her Truth might overcome Warrior Realm ontology.

By the time she’d thrown it in here, she’d already eaten a good chunk of it. Gender, self-image, name.

The thing in the dark blinks first.

The beast she only vaguely recognizes snarls something, but as she watches, she sees its form blending into the shadows, moving just a little bit more gracefully than before.
