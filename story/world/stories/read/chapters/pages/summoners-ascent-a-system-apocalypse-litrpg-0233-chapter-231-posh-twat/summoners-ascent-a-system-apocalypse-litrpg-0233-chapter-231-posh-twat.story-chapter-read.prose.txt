Ashton focused on the small key in his hand. He had been trying to figure out or make some kind of connection with this thing for the past hour. It was really all he did when waiting for Millie to find him. But he couldn't remember anything related to this in the slightest.

After all, a wooden key? What kind of lock could this fit into? It didn't look carved, either; it was more like a branch miraculously took on this shape with all its complex small details. The description that the Pen of Insight had written down for him wasn't particularly helpful, either.

Key of the Old Woods

Grade - Unknown

Material - Oak

A small key that was borne to protect the entrance to the Old Woods.

The corresponding door can be found within the most ancient oak of the forest.

Ashton had no idea what this was supposed to mean. What were the 'Old Woods', and how was he supposed to find the 'most ancient oak of the forest'? There wasn't any particularly large tree that he knew about hidden in Drakewood. Unless it wasn't even talking about Drakewood in the first place, in that case it would take a while for him to figure this out anyway.

Of course, he knew why this information was so straightforward, because technically, this was a very simple object. Sure, the 'Unknown' grade was a little concerning, but otherwise it was just a key without any particularly special abilities. Except, of course, the way that it bent the veil around itself. Which wasn't even mentioned in the description, so it wasn't even a noteworthy enough of a reason.

Ashton sighed and stashed the key away again, giving it to the Backpacker so that they could put it back into the magic-sealing chest.

He leaned back a little and expanded his channelling range outward again. Millie was wandering around the university courtyard. She had made her way outside a little bit ago but wasn't quite able to pinpoint Ashton yet. Well, maybe his hiding spot was also a little unfair.

But even so, it didn't take long until Millie literally walked past below him. A few steps later, she stopped, and turned back around. Confused, she stretched out her hand and tried to figure out what was going on.

"Ashton? Where are you? Did you bury yourself underground?" the girl asked, until her hand finally slammed against the large tree in the centre of the courtyard.

"Close," Ashton said from above. He was sitting on one of the tree's branches, just relaxing a bit, taking in some of the fresh air out here.

Ashton dropped down and smiled at Madeleine. "Thank you so much for your help."

"Hm? Oh, yeah, of course, it was no issue at all," she replied, shaking her head. "But, uhm... how exactly did she do it? Is the mask some kind of a trick?"

Ashton shook his head immediately. "Of course it's not a trick. It's what Is aid before. She's been using her sixth sense to find me." He squatted down and helped Millie take the mask off again, this time putting it back onto his own eyes. "The fact she was able to learn how to do this so fast is pretty special. I know I never hid too far away, but only relying on a sense she never knew she really had is pretty hard. So good job, kiddo, you passed with flying colours."

Millie beamed up at Ashton with an excited grin.

"Yes! I knew it! But okay, it's my turn," Millie said, excitedly looking around.

"Alright. What handicaps do you want to give me this time?"

Millie thought about it for a moment. She glanced up at Ashton, and grinned broadly.

"All of them!"

"All of them?" Ashton repeated. "What does that mean?"

"You get all the handicaps. No hearing, no smelling, no wisp, nothing!"

Ashton smiled. "Oh? The wisp was a given, but I don't get anything now?"

"Mm-hmm!"

"Well, alright," Ashton replied, and clicked his fingers. The wisp disappeared and the staff curled back up to make up for the absence. Ashton then, with the help of the undine, plugged up his ears with water and solidified it, to try and block out as much sound as possible. He did the same with his nose, not letting in any sense.

This was actually surprisingly good training for himself, too. As long as he tried his best not to rely on his memory for knowing where everything was, Ashton could fully focus on sensing the ambient mana with his own natural sixth sense and figure out where he was walking.

Having a rough image of his surrounding when he didn't have the wisp with him wasn't exactly a bad idea, and he had been slacking on his practice.

Again, the rules were simple, and to make it more 'fair', Ashton was given a few handicaps. For one, while Ashton couldn't hide too far away from Millie, Millie was allowed to hide wherever she wanted to on the whole campus. Of course, Madeleine was still there to keep an eye on her, and was nice enough to keep Millie out of restricted or dangerous areas.

And, since Millie's senses were restricted, Ashton's had to be as well. Plus, in this small 'competition', every second that Ashton took to find Millie counted as ten seconds. So basically, while Millie had thirty minutes to find Ashton, he only had three to find her. Not that he needed that long, anyway.

Though, this time might be a bit different. If he didn't have any of his senses, finding Millie might be a bit rough.

The girl tapped Ashton's hand to let him know they were starting, and then ran off with Madeleine following behind. Of course, while it was a good way to get the conversation started, at this point Madeleine wasn't doing this because she was attracted to Ashton. But rather, she must have seen the worth that Ashton had when he gave her the potion recipes and instructions.

Considering the strictness with which people worked here, there was no way that, under normal circumstances, Madeleine would just goof off and help Ashton and Millie out in playing hide and seek, but she must know that Ashton would give her something to make that 'wasted' time worthwhile. Which was exactly what would happen.

Especially now that he was going to get a bunch of new herbs and materials for potion-making, Ashton was going to give her some of his leftovers with instructions how to best use them. Ashton only picked the best of the best, and from what he spotted as he walked through the university, it was most likely better than anything people had brought here so far.

Of course, they didn't know anything about how to pick out the materials with the most magic within them. If your senses were established enough, you could slowly learn the trick to it, but otherwise, just relying on the plant's physical condition wasn't enough.

Ashton focused on the arcane pocket watch. Just by keeping track of the flow of mana within it, he was able to figure out what time it was, even without using wisp-sight. Five minutes had passed now, plenty of time for Millie to find a good spot.

And then, Ashton started to walk forward. He tried to block out his memory of the courtyard as much as he could, so that he would instead rely on his senses to navigate. He stretched out his sixth sense, trying to focus on the minute bits of mana around him.

Ashton sent out pulses of his own mana, basically trying to mimic the wisp-sight in his own limited fashion. It wasn't perfect in any sense, however. The most that Ashton could do was roughly sense the location of other people around him. He could somewhat tell his surrounding, but it was hard, not even being able to rely on the sound bouncing around.

So, how was Ashton supposed to find Millie like this? It was simple. He knew Millie's mana-pattern pretty well. Since he had injected his own mana into her to help with her mana-manipulation, he knew more about her mana than she did herself. And while he couldn't sense things super precisely from a distance, he could at least tell a few details of the mana pattern around him.

And most people weren't exactly great at controlling their mana, and usually let some of it leak out. It was a completely natural thing, and even many mages struggled to keep it all reined in.

Even Ashton didn't bother with it most of the time, unless he was specifically trying to suppress his presence, and in the case of this game, even pushed it out even further so that Millie had an easier time sensing him. Not that he was sure that was how Millie actually sensed other people, but it was worth a try anyway.

And well, sometimes, folk simply left behind a small trail of their mana that particularly sensitive people were able to pick up on. And Ashton was one of those particularly sensitive people.

It was why it wasn't exactly hard to find the area where Millie was hiding. She had gone into the library. This was sort of the last place Ashton wanted to go right now, but he–

The Backpacker appeared in front of Ashton. They tugged on his cloak, and motioned for him to take the ice out of his ears.

The moment he did, he understood what the issue was.

"I told you to go and do your job, Madeleine!" Quincy's voice echoed through the otherwise so quiet space. "This isn't the time to play stupid games! The world is ending, and you think it's okay for you to spend the day playing hide-and-seek? Get back to work already!"

Ashton extended his senses. He called the wisp back immediately and extended his channelling range. And what he saw made Ashton's blood boil.

Quincy was standing there, yelling at Madeleine, while holding onto Millie's arm. She was trying to pull herself loose, and was clearly in a panic, but she couldn't do anything against an adult man.

Ashton clicked his fingers and called on the Helping Hand. The two gloves shot forward immediately, grabbing Quincy's hand and peeling the fingers off Millie's arm.

Quincy looked forward, startled. He let go of Millie's arm, but the Helping Hand wasn't letting go. They were pushing back Quincy's fingers and hand.

"What are you doing?! Let go of me!" Quincy yelled out, but Ashton just silently approached him.

Millie was shaking, and immediately ran over to Ashton. She was practically hiding in his cloak.

"Who do you think you are?! You come in here, throw your weight around, and think you own this place? You better show me some– urgh, some fucking respect!" Quincy yelled, though this time, his voice was clearly soaked in pain.

But Ashton wasn't focused on him. He squatted down, and checked on Millie's arm. She flinched when he ran his fingers over her wrist.

"Does it hurt here?"

Millie slowly nodded. "It's... it's really red..."

It seemed swollen.

Ashton slowly turned to Quincy, and channelled the Helping Hand. With a swift motion, Ashton broke Quincy's wrist, just as he had done with Millie.

The gloves finally let go of him, but Quincy just stood there in disbelief, holding his arm in pain. "What the– what the hell is wrong with you? I was your parents' friend, how could you–"

Ashton picked Millie up from the ground, using the Helping Hand to keep her upright since she couldn't hold onto him properly right now. And then, he turned around and walked away, though not without leaving Quincy with a last message.

"My parents thought you were a posh twat."
