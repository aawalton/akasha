Jade’s plate of snacks was empty, dissolved away as if it had never been. Ferris settled down to meditate next to the chained slime.

He’d never gone in and out of his soulspace so often in a single day, but was getting faster each time.

Returning to the slimespace’s Digest River, Ferris confirmed that the level of liquid was once again higher.

A shimmer of light caught his eye, and Ferris moved closer to the mouth of the river. There was a cloudy spot floating in the river, shimmering with an iridescent light. It stood out from the green acid around it like a bright hazy cloud, the color reminded Ferris of… the magical berry.

Developing the soulspace was essential to growing stronger, and everything Ferris wanted to build would require resources. Gathering qi was just the beginning, he would also need precious gems, potent medical herbs, rare metals, and anything with magic. There were many different ways to absorb resources from the real world into the spirit.

With a bit of luck, Jade’s Consume Pool would help get him started.

That hazy cloud was much larger than the tiny berry, and clearly still magical. At least, it sparkled. That had to mean something, right?

If the Consume Pool somehow enlarged resources, that would be an amazing boon, although Ferris didn’t know if size in the soul meant the same thing as real life. There may also be some downsides to the ability, like the fact that everything was dissolved. Perhaps the quality was affected by the size change?

The combination with the Digest River was also a strong synergy, but maybe resources eaten by the Consume Pool were restricted in other ways? The Grow ability suggested that Ferris could find other uses for the food, if he collected it before it fell into the pool of acid.

Ferris wondered if Jade could eat the hazy cloud, or maybe bring some of it to the surface. What would eating something twice do? Would it appear again over the Consume pool? Maybe if they swam through it, some of that glittering haze would stick to the slime’s outer body…

Picking up on the idea, Jade was excited to try. Really, the slime would jump at any idea that involved eating. Jade began slowly rolling along the riverbed through the acid.

As Ferris watched, he noticed movement from downstream. A large silver fish swam up the river and darted through the hazy magical cloud, eating some of it.

“Thief!” Ferris yelled angrily, unable to do anything from the side of the river.

Jade, picking up on his emotion, began swimming up towards the fish. Unfortunately, they were not a very good swimmer. Jade tried to expand and contract to move through the water, but the result was a slow rocking motion moving forward sporadically. Ferris tried to call Jade back, but by then the fish had responded.

A bit shocked, Ferris could only watch as the silver fish elegantly circled the slime. Then, with a flash of light reflected off its scales, the fish darted into attack. It was too big for Jade to wrap around and moving too fast to grab. The fish rammed through the slime’s body, swallowing the core along the way.

Ferris felt a shock of blinding pain through the bond, and Jade’s body slowly disappeared into the acid pool. He fell to his knees, clutching at his heart as an earthquake shook the dome.

Taking a deep breath, Ferris tried to focus through the pain. It will be fine, he told himself, get yourself together.

In the real world, this would have been fatal for both of them, but in the soul, it was merely painful. Jade should be fine, although they would need some time to recover.

The entire slimespace shook for a couple seconds, the trauma of Jade losing a mental body somehow echoing all around. The grey ants ran for cover, disappearing into the yellow spires. Even the birds appeared distressed, retreating to their roost in the tallest tower.

After a couple agonizing minutes, Ferris was able to move again. He’d died before in the soulspace, but that time he had quickly lost consciousness. This lingering agony was new, like a piece of his heart had been ripped out, the empty space throbbing with each breath.

Beyond the pain, a creeping worry gnawed at his thoughts. Had he made a mistake? He knew slimes were weak, but Jade had died in a single hit. Even worse, it’d happened to what looked like a basic fish. How could he delve against monsters like this?

No, he thought, this just shows what I can improve. Ferris, with his missing leg, would have been as helpless as Jade. Slimes may start weak, but he believed in their potential. It wasn’t Jade’s fault Ferris hadn’t developed the strength they needed. They just needed a plan and some hard work.

Standing back up, Ferris shook his head and focused on what he could do. He wanted to harvest the qi chunks from the acid river. Especially knowing there were creatures that could steal them. But he had no way to get them. Jade would be unconscious all night, and Ferris had nothing that could survive the acid. In any case, the silver fish had left after eating the rest of the magical berry cloud.

Briefly, Ferris gazed outside the dome. Green acid flowed hazily all around, the glowing light from the dome only shined so far, dropping off into darkness. If the silver fish existed, what other creatures were out there? Demons inside the soulspace were personal to the cultivator, but how did that work for a familiar bond? Was the silver fish Ferris’ demon, or Jade’s? The grey ants, birds, and clams ignored Jade, but the fish attacked on sight. It was hard to imagine the joyful slime with heart demons, but maybe that was naive…

For now, Ferris was trapped in the giant bubble, but someday he would explore the slimespace more fully. It would be good practice for the rifts back home.

Returning to the top of the second mid-sized tower, with its black rock ground and Core Tree atop a hill of dark green crystal, there was something here Ferris could do. He picked the remaining mature fruit, closed his eyes, and slowly ate it. This fruit was a bit tart, almost sour.

This was another ability for Jade, instead of Ferris. While the first seed had the Grow ability, this one could create an ability called Jump. Ferris chuckled, remembering poor Jade desperately trying to hop high enough to reach the fruit. If they planted this seed and cultivated the ability, Jade would be able to easily jump high enough.

Like the Grow ability, Jump was very simple in its requirements. The seed just needed to be planted within the dome and watered with liquid qi. Surprisingly, the ability would grow as a tree. That was odd for an ability, despite them being seeds. Ferris had no idea how a tree would help Jade jump, but there should be some abstract relationship to the ability’s effect.

Curiously, once again some information Ferris learned from eating the fruit was specific to the Consume Pool. While Grow could be accelerated with any food, Jump would only benefit from certain kinds: specifically muscles of animals. If Ferris developed other abilities that could help, would the fruit tell him more? Would the fruit have informed him that muscles could help grow Jump faster if he didn’t have the Consume Pool?

Unlike Grow, he didn’t think the Jump ability was particularly strong for a slime. Grow took the slime’s strong point and made more of it, while Jump gave a simpler improvement. The enhanced mobility was definitely helpful, and it could let Jade approach enemies quicker… but then the slime would just die or be flung off. Perhaps Jump would be better as part of a synergy, but even developing one ability was expensive. He needed a solid ability to build a fighting style around, and Jump wasn’t it.

Grow would be a better choice than Jump, but maybe not better than the third fruit.

However, he’d noticed something unexpected about both. Uncle Winton had given Ferris a lot of advice for how to evaluate potential abilities, and these were a bit non-standard.

Usually, eating the fruit would give instructions on how to build a structure or provide the correct conditions. It was expected for a new cultivator to take about a month of hard work to get one ability working, which made it important to choose the correct ones to focus on. Even if the ability was good, it might not be worth the time and effort.

Both Grow and now Jump could be considered much slower than that. He’d gotten the impression that unless he dumped massive amounts of qi or additional resources on them, they would take far longer than a month to develop. Perhaps even half a year before even seeing slight benefits.

Despite this drawback, neither required any other work. None of the thousands of hours of physical labor his uncle had warned him about.

Really, for Ferris’ situation, a slow ability that didn’t require his attention was perfect. He could focus on the research project with Elder Harper while letting the abilities develop in the background. Neither of them would be bad to have. It may be a problem if Grow got too large, although Ferris thought it would be years before he would need to worry about that.

The only problem was qi. They could both develop slowly without a lot of work, but each needed daily watering of liquid qi. Ferris wasn’t sure what would happen if he stopped feeding them, but it risked destroying the seed.

Also, if he went this route he wouldn’t see the true power of either ability for years. Only by focusing on them with everything he had could he accelerate their development to anything truly impactful in a fight.

Still, Ferris liked the idea of starting their slow development. Long term, the extra size and mobility would help almost any fighting style.

To help offset the additional qi cost, he had Jade’s Digest River. Most new cultivators wouldn’t have a familiar with a qi gathering ability, so he should be better off in that regard. The book on familiar bonds hadn’t mentioned this as an advantage, but he thought it would be a great one. At least, once he figured out how to get the Digest River’s qi chunks out of the acid.

He decided to plant both seeds immediately, then wait to see what the next few potential abilities were. If nothing else appealed to him, he could funnel more resources into Grow.

Speaking of which, he didn’t think the last fruit on the tree was mature. It wasn’t as colorful or large as the other two had been. It felt safer to wait and see if it grew bigger.

Ferris made a quick trip to his Qi Pool to fill his bucket with liquid qi and crafted a shovel. The Qi Pool had shrunk a lot today, from making a pedestal for the core, forming the bucket, watering the tree, constructing the shovel, and now nourishment for the two ability seeds. An entire week of work, gone in a day. Somehow, the Qi Pool was smaller than when he had arrived at Scalefort, although Ferris was showing growth in other places.

He started digging at an empty spot back in the slimespace, near the south edge of the dome. Some abilities needed to be connected to others to work properly, but not a passive like Jump. Anything that created an effect in the real world would also need a meridian, but internal body enhancements usually didn’t need it.

The swarms of ants were strangely reclusive, still hiding in the yellow spires from earlier. The birds were also missing, giving an eerily empty feeling.

As he dug up a small hole in the green dirt, Ferris wondered what would happen if he planted the Jump seed in his soulspace, instead of in the slimespace. Would Ferris get the Jump ability, or would Jade still get it? If Jade did get it, would the ability only work when they were touching? Eating the fruit hadn’t provided answers to these questions, so Ferris would have to decide what was worth testing for himself. This was another advantage martial traditions helped with; they usually had generations of tests and experiments to share specific to the bonded weapon.

Oddly, the color of the dirt quickly changed from green to brown just below the surface. He wasn’t sure if the color meant anything; just another mystery to investigate when he had time. Maybe he could dig deeper and see if it changed again, or try various spots to check the layer depth of the change.

Ferris planted the Jump seed, covered it back up with dirt, and then watered it with half the qi in the bucket. He made sure to note the location, so that he could continue feeding it liquid qi each day.

For the Grow seed, Ferris approached the edge of the dome. This magical, glowing transparent wall was all that separated Ferris from an ocean of acid outside. It was hard to tell exactly, but the wall appeared to be thicker than the length of Ferris’ arm.

Tentatively, Ferris placed a hand on the dome’s surface. There was some flexibility if Ferris pressed hard, but there was no way to press his hand into the wall. He had sort of wondered if it would let things through like Jade’s outer layer, but no such luck. It wasn’t hard as a rock, but still solid enough to hold its form.

Looking up at the massive dome, he felt there was no way it would be structurally stable in real life. Only magical soul shenanigans could allow it to exist here. Perhaps once Ferris understood how it worked, he could recreate it. Just because he currently lacked knowledge, didn’t mean he always would.

While he couldn’t press his hand directly into the wall, the shovel could cut it. Carefully, Ferris carved out a hole in the wall, just deep enough to stick a hand inside. He’d intentionally sloped it slightly down as he dug. Ferris placed the Grow seed in, then back filled it most of the way with dirt from the ground. Finally, he watered it with the rest of the qi from the bucket—the slope ensuring that the liquid qi trickled down to the seed.

Done with the seeds, Ferris looked around for what to do next. While he wanted to learn more about the slimespace, that would likely be more productive after Jade recovered. He also still needed to do his nightly cultivation routine.

After traveling back to his soulspace, he started by cutting back the white chains growing from the oath statue. It brought to mind his consideration of how to protect himself from any subversive influence. If the bonded core and oath statue could affect his thoughts, he would need to isolate them from the rest of his soulspace… somehow.

Perhaps he would never let these chains dig it. Would that affect the oath? Would it wither and die, like a plant with no roots? He’d have to watch it closely for any problems.

Collecting qi chunks from his Air Passage went quickly. After Ferris finished that last chore, he stopped cultivating to get some sleep. He’d only accomplished one of his three goals, unable to collect qi chunks from the river or find any clues to the Adaptation ability, but he would just have to keep trying tomorrow.

Back in the real world, Ferris checked on Jade. The slime was unresponsive but otherwise looked fine. There shouldn’t be any long-term consequences to losing a mental body. Jade wouldn’t be able to enter the soulspace for a couple days, but should wake up fine by tomorrow.

That wouldn’t always be the case. Some dangers in the soulspace could cause permanent damage, especially when attempting to advance tiers, but losing a mental body should be fine. Ferris had died once in the soulspace as well, with no longer term effects.

Sleep did not come easily to Ferris that night. He had finally bonded a core, getting one step closer to his dream. He made sure Jade was fully secured to the dresser, unable to reach his bed, but a giddy excitement kept making him sit up to check on his familiar. Thoughts of the two of them raced through his head: challenging rifts and fighting monsters. Sleep was a long time coming, but inevitably arrived.
