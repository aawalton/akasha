*** When Primordial Unleashed was first conceived, it was supposed to be a Young Adult oriented Progression Fantasy adventure with an Epic Fantasy background.

I cooked too hard.

My passions in military tactics, strategy games, mythology and the Roman Legions all took over. What was intended to be a quick and easy project intending to get me started in self-publishing fiction turned into a huge passion project, with a fully-fledged world, pantheon, and a huge cast of characters, all of whom demanded to come alive on the page, with their own character arcs, believable interactions, quirks, thoughts and feelings.

At last, I have acheived that.

I am a perfectionist. I can never say 'it's enough' until it's done. That is my one biggest weakness in this industry. I'm hoping that it shall become my strength.

I have invested what little savings I had in an editor, and I am now betting a lot on Legion of the Firesworn's modest success. Please, if you can spare another moment of your time to give the new fiction page a follow, that would be wonderful!

Though there are dozens of little changes, only a few are essential to know for anyone who has read the original first book. They are:

Regarding the Coven...

Skippii does not seek them out in hopes of an alliance. He instead trains his combined tactics with his Companeight openly, to the audience of the Cohort. This gains the attention of many, and acclaim, and proves that he is indispensible for the fight to come.

And it cuts down on an unecessary anti-climactic beat with the whole sneaking up on the Coven camp and the chat with Kylinissa. All cut. He and Kylinissa still have a one-to-one conversation though, however it is about their homes, upbringings, hopes etc.

Regarding the mid-way twist with the nightmares that Skippii experiences...

No longer do they seem random and unexplained.

During his first encounter with the heretic magus at the farmstead, Skippii is infected with a heretical seed - a becon of sorts, which the heretics at Nerithon use to infuse him with dark visions. He has these visions for days, worsening to the point where he sleeps seperate from his companions, afraid of exploding intoa fireball in the night. He confides in Kylinissa and Orsin, and each advise him caution.

However, one such night, the visions become real; Cosmipox grips Skippii, seeking to kill him from afar. Cor surges to Skippii's aid and the invocation is repelled, but at a cost: Skippii has rampaged through camp while blinded by darkness, setting fire to tents and legionnaires. None are killed, though dozens are injured, and many supplies destroyed.

This entire re-write justifies the twist much better, in a more grounded way. The result of Skippii's outburst is more severe.

Later, when he chooses...

... to desert the Legion, this is on the back of the overt threat of decimation of Tonnage VI by the Coven, and the execution of Custos Maritor. However, if Skippii is to avoid being a martyr, and desert and thus tarnish his name, the Coven claim not to seek recompence for his and the Tonnage's transgressions.

Again, a much tighter, more clear reason for his motivations.

In Lore

Eirene tells that in the aftermath of the War of Heavens, the Pantheon was tasked with binding the Primordials. However, ever since the Second War of Heavens, those bonds have been neglected. The Gods are much weakened by war among their own kind, leading to the Primordials gaining strength and thus resurging. This explains how Skippii's power has come to be now, and hints at what to expect of the future.

Repulsed, Hespera retreated, eclipsing herself behind Chrysaetos. Up into the heavens the sickly form rose, and into Chrysaetos’ welcoming light. A bell knelled in Hespera’s heart. She knew then that the Pantheon was no more. The alliance of Gods was tarnished. What allies had she remaining? What new enemies amongst her kin?

Aequentia quivered at her back, dice gripped in their hands.

“Hush,” she whispered. “Do not shut your eyes, little one. The truth will find us in the end, no matter its burden. Think now, roll your dice. What new reality is this? What new Realm shall be wrought?”

There below, within thin brick walls, slept the Primordial child. A mist of ice concealed his aura. Hespera peered with a gentle gaze, and the mist parted—Oyaltun giving her blessing. Hespera watched the mortal sleep. So fragile was his life, yet so volatile. Assessing his heart, she discovered much that she had not guessed.

“What indeed of alliances old and new?”
