"I see you did not mention your defeat?" Jaq commented, arching his left eyebrow.

"I don't think we need to spend a lot of time on things like that," Beth said with a tiny frown, waving her hand in front of her face as she tried to laugh it off.

"I wanna see this statue stage ya mentioned," Baelvyr said. "Then, we're gonna look at that infiltration stage in detail."

"Can't we just, you know, leave that for some other time?" Beth asked. "It's already been so long and-"

"Girlie, it ain't even been two full hours yet," Baelvyr cut her off, switching on the recording of stage twenty-five. They proceeded to watch a highly sped-up version of the stage, Beth noting that with their increased stats they were even able to process a video played at multiple times normal speed as if it were just running at a standard rate. Baelvyr paused a couple times to point out some mistakes, though he did comment they had done a rather good job of handling this stage. Jaq threw in a few snide comments, but even his snarky remarks still pointed out flaws in their techniques or teamwork.

They moved on after a handful of minutes to the last parts of the review, starting with their failed infiltration stage. Jaq was more serious here, but no less harsh as he critiqued them on their performance, in particular their planning. He lectured them greatly on their information gathering operations in particular, as he pointed out information was the basis of all actions, and their poor efforts in gathering and analyzing information about the count's forces was key to their loss.

"Alright," Baelvyr said. "Now that we got that shit outta the way, let's get to it."

He stood up and ejected the flash drive, handing it back to Beth before walking over to the training area. Jaq followed him and stopped to the side, shoulders square, back straight, and hands clasped at the small of his back in an 'at-ease' stance. Baelvyr then gestured to the training stage, and Beth and Blood quickly took the hint, hopping up onto the stage and turning to face the two.

"Alright, show me what ya've learned," Baelvyr said, summoning a group of opponents for them to fight. The next hour turned into a living hell, Baelvyr and Jaq pushing the two far harder than they ever had, summoning ever more and ever more powerful opponents for them to face before Jaq himself stepped into the arena. Baelvyr just crossed his massive arms over his enormous gut with a stony expression while Beth quirked an eyebrow. Two long knives appeared in Jaq's hands, made of some type of golden metal so reflective it practically glowed, with each being thin as a hair.

Jaq stepped forward almost lazily, looking like he was going out for a simple stroll, but he was on top of the two girls in an instant. It took everything they had to stop him, as the blades he held turned into a storm of attacks, a dozen slashes targeting both of them simultaneously. After a few seconds Beth gave up trying to use finesse and called upon her Presence, nearly getting gutted in the process as Jaq was not staggered by her summoning of the crown for even a microsecond. She launched forward at her maximum speed, burning all of her buffs and smashed apart the sword lights Jaq had made with the knives, her fist empowered with so much mana it glowed faintly through her gauntlet that was rocketing toward his chest. At the same moment, Blood attacked with her new claws and Blood Queen's Claws, firing out a storm of energy claws so thick they practically formed a wall as they approached Jaq.

The commissary officer sneered, moving his arms languidly but producing a shocking effect. He struck Beth's arm nine times in a fraction of a second, shoving her immensely powerful attack far off course while seemingly using no strength himself. At the same time, he slashed behind himself in a complex pattern, shattering Blood's massive attack by precisely hitting the weak points amongst the energy claws. He swiped with his knives again after deflecting both attacks, Beth only barely able to block his strikes while Blood darted out of the path of the blades. Beth still suffered several cuts on her arms and one across her right shoulder that sliced through the mana steel scales, leather, and her skin all as effortlessly as a hot knife through butter.

She ignored the slight pain of the wounds as she pushed herself harder, determined to land a hit that would wipe the smug look off the Enforcer's face. She moved even faster than she had during the fight against the stone statue boss, to the point where somebody at level fifty would have barely seen her as a blur. Realistically, she knew that she was still barely a child compared to Jaq, in multiple ways considering his power, age, and experience, but that didn't discourage her at all. She punched like the piston of a top-of-the-line engine, hammering away at Jaq's defense with a hundred fist strikes a second. Blood continued circling at the same time, slashing at Jaq constantly as she moved with the flow of battle, all three of them allowing Beth to dictate the pacing and movement.

Jaq's defense was impeccable, however, and Beth's fists never even got close to him. She changed her tactics as every blow she struck was slid away with a graceful flick of his wrist, her greatsword appearing in her hand before she swung it with a fully charged Monstrous Blow. The air tore apart as the blade passed with a shriek, Jaq having to put a little more effort in to block this than her previous hits. A little more effort still wasn't much effort, however, and he easily toyed with her even with her blade out, not much impressed by her combined use of fists, feet, and sword to attack. He eventually seemed to have seen enough, his body flickering for a second before disappearing from their sight, Beth only realizing several seconds later that both her and Blood had been cut to ribbons, almost literally.

Thankfully, they were using one of the training stages and, when Jaq reappeared beside Baelvyr outside the ring, their bodies and equipment were fully restored to the moment before the spar started. Beth's sword vanished into her ring with a flourishing twist of her wrist, and she hopped down from the stage, ignoring the two instructors as she went over to the corner of the room to retrieve drinks, petulantly only getting enough for her and Blood. Her snub was somewhat ruined as Jaq simply made a canteen appear from his storage item while giving her a somewhat comical side-eye.

"What did we learn?" Baelvyr rumbled the question as they drank.

"He's a cheater," replied Blood.

"He's an asshole, picking on us," Beth snorted, rolling her eyes.

"If you weren't so weak, you might have a chance," Jaq replied coldly.

"What's your Daggers at, anyway," Beth replied, ignoring the jibe.

"Grandmaster[9]," he replied calmly.

"Yeah, bullshit," Beth snorted. "We're not even a full year into our training and leveling and we're supposed to fight Grandmasters? I don't care how cracked we're supposed to be, that's stupid."

"Ya can just meet people like that," Baelvyr snorted. "There're people who are just as young an' green as ya and already have amazing abilities. It's called the 'Limitless Path' for a damn reason, despite that everybody always seems to forget that."

"We did actually see at least two people like that at the Trial, now that you mention it," Beth said. "There was a woman who had her spear skill at least at Expert, and then there was a guy there that I'm pretty sure was a Master with the sword, even though he couldn't have been older than thirty, at most."

"Yep," Baelvyr nodded. "There ain't no limit to what ya can do if ya really try."

"There are some limits," Jaq butted in with a scoff. "Don't throw yourself off the roof just because you get it in your head that you'll be able to fly by believing it."

"The two of you telling us two different things at once isn't very helpful," Blood said caustically, both of her ears twitching.

"Right, it's like this," Baelvyr said, holding a hand up to stop the rest from continuing. "Ya can do basically anything on the Path, right? But here's the deal; ya gotta have the knowledge and understandin' to do it. Not only that, but some things are also sometimes just beyond ya; can't get everythin' for free. I've seen people do things ya wouldn't believe, but some of them have paid a lot for it. Includin' all the way up to sacrificin' their life for that crazy thing. There's always some kinda balance that ya gotta keep in mind."

"So, let me sum this up," Beth said, right hand on her hip while her left index finger was tapping her chin. "There are gonna be people out there who are just so good that their skills, levels, whatever, are way higher than they 'should' be at their age or experience level. And basically, anybody can do it, but if you try too hard and don't have the knowledge, skill, or strong enough body to handle it, you could face serious consequences, all the way up to death."

"I guess it takes one blockhead to be able to translate another's words," Jaq commented acerbically.

"Enough," Baelvyr grumbled. He then refocused them on the task at hand, going over Beth and Blood's gains from the Trial and making several comments and notes before saying, "Yer doin' well so far, but we've got a lot more to work on. I'll see both of ya tomorrow for more of this."

"Rome wasn't built in a day," Beth quipped with a sigh.

"And come see me to restock your supplies," Jaq said. "I also want to look at your current equipment before you run off anywhere actually dangerous."

After Jaq finished speaking, he disappeared with a bang, seemingly done with his babysitting efforts for the day. Baelvyr was never as flashy, though with his pillar-like legs he didn't need to be, ambling out the door and down the hall and still disappearing in a blink. Beth and Blood trailed after; having returned in the middle of the day, they made their way to the lounge now that it was the evening. They wanted to see if they could catch the rest of their group there or if they would need to just meet up with Sabs in their suite for now and catch the other girls later.

They were somewhat surprised to find the lounge exceptionally lively, at least, by the standards they had been used to. The place was packed with seemingly new recruits, Beth only recognizing a single face in the crowd, that being of one of the women who had joined shortly before they left for the Trial. Neither Sabs nor her sisters were in the lounge, though both Lyrissa and John were present, meaning it was rather unlikely the other girls were training today, or they had all finished and left already. Beth didn't stay in the room, leading Blood out the door and down the hallway to the front lobby, stopping in front of Tazeen when they got there.

"Beth," the stoic elf said with a very slight nod.

"Tazeen, good to see you," Beth said. There was no one else currently in the lobby, but a young man walked in from outside and moved to the head to the back as they spoke. Beth assumed the place was really starting to pick up now that the changes had had almost half a year to percolate through society. "We need to get Blood an Emblem, and I wanted to talk about upgrading mine."

"Certainly. Let's take care of young Miss Blood's Emblem first, then we can speak on yours," Tazeen replied with another nod. He then walked through the process of signing Blood up, the wolf now being an official member on her own. Beth thought it was important that she had the full package now that she had a human form, especially since there were times the two of them weren't together, and Blood would be better served being a full member herself. Beth also made sure Blood had a working key for the room before they did anything else.

"Now, as to your other request," Tazeen said after Blood had been sorted out. "As you may recall from our discussion before you left for the Trial, we covered the requirements for upgrading to a Silver Emblem."

"Right, then I might suggest that as your first task," Tazeen said. "I would recommend you start that right away. At your current level of strength, completing three missions will not be especially difficult nor time-consuming, so I suggest you start studying immediately. That will, by far, consume the bulk of your time leading up to your qualification for Silver."

"Just to confirm, do we have any missions here yet? Or will I need to go somewhere else to get those missions?" Beth asked.

"Do not fret, arrangements can be made," Tazeen answered smoothly.

"Right, well then, I guess studying is priority one," Beth said with a sigh. "Thanks for the help, Tazeen. We'll come back once I've worked my way through that list you recommended."

"It's no trouble. Simply let one of us know if you have questions," he said.

Beth then led the way back through the halls and into the arena before they made their way down to the basement. They approached the familiar door, and Beth quickly fished out her key from her pouch, swiping it over the reader before the door beeped and slid open. She sighed as she stepped inside before grumbling a little as Blood hip-checked her out of the way so she could saunter inside. As she closed the door behind her, she heard a voice saying, "Beth?" in a bit of a tremulous manner, turning to see Sabs sticking her head out of the kitchenette.

"I'm back," Beth said with a big smile.

"And who, uh, is this?" Sabs asked, walking into the living room cautiously as she looked at Blood.

"Blood," said Beth.

"Wait, this is Blood," Sabs gasped, taking a step towards the strange woman and leaning forward. Blood suddenly took a step back and there was a brief moment of twisting in space and the massive red wolf stood there, her armored vest still equipped.
