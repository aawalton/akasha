This is…

I stared at myself in the mirror with wide eyes. I was wearing a long flowing black dress with golden leaf-shaped accents to the side. No cleavage was shown as it went all the way to my neck before going to an open back. I had a necklace and earrings, both golden, that contrasted with my eyes. And my hair was slowly being combed behind me by none other than Elasia.

I couldn’t help but look at my figure as a whole. I looked so… fancy. It was such a strange sight, but one that made me feel slightly giddy and nervous deep down. My hair was being slowly tied into a bun, one held by golden accessories that resembled chains.

And soon enough, Elasia finished as she stood behind me.

“What do you think?”

I simply looked at my whole body. I felt like royalty. “This is…”

And she placed her hands on my shoulders. “Look at you, you’re so beautiful.” Elasia got closer as she looked at me from behind. “You have the same eyes as me, the same stature, you have Lumina’s complexion and her affinity.”

Suddenly, I felt a bit awkward as Elasia pressed herself against me.

“You really are our daughter, even if not bound by blood.” She met my gaze in the mirror with a small smile. “To think it all started from an off-handed comment, but after Lumina was revived and things happened, we ended up having multiple discussions. Discussions about having a family, and ultimately, we chose you⁠—” she shook her head. “No, you chose us in spite of how fast things went.”

I paused as my heart rushed. “It’s really not a big deal, I was just…”

“We were all not in the right headspace, I understand. Perhaps we sought happiness too quickly and expedited the process. All this time, unlike Lumina, I felt unsure.” She looked down. “I felt unsure about whether I considered you my actual daughter or something else.”

“That is…” A bad feeling came within me, but before I could say anything Elasia continued.

“But after watching you fight the Emperor I am sure of it. After all, I was more than ready to kill that man even if the System made me pay a heavy price.” She closed her eyes. “Seeing you get hurt was painful. I felt more emotions during those moments than any other time. And that is when I realized that…”

And her eyes met mine as she sincerely smiled.

“You are my and Lumina’s child. Our pride and joy.” And she hugged me as my feelings went haywire. “We love you Blair.”

I bit my lip as I struggled not to cry. Was it even right to say it back? It had only been months since I even met them, but at that moment, I couldn’t help myself as I squeaked.

“I love you both too, thank you for supporting me.”

And then, I ugly-cried.

I didn’t know how long it took, but Elasia helped me wipe my tears. Her face seemed slightly red too as she smiled at me.

“We can’t take too long. You are the main event after all.”

I nodded. “Thank you.”

I cleaned my face once more, and Elasia helped me fix up my hair before we set out. She flicked her fingers and we instantly found ourselves underground, inside of the mountain. It was dark. We were in a tunnel, and in spite of my senses I couldn’t feel anything.

“Are you ready?” Elasia turned to me.

Taking a breath, I nodded. “Yeah.”

And at that moment, I saw light. My surroundings unfolded like a flower as I saw red petals, and then, I saw it. A grand hall of wood with hanging chandeliers. Dark plants decorated the venue with beautiful and exotic flowers, and I was standing on a gigantic crimson flower. I heard noises in our surroundings as petals of different plants slowly and constantly rained.

I could see tables, people, hundreds of them, and all of them took notice of me pretty much instantly as they began to chatter amongst themselves. And before us, stood Lumina, with carefully combed white hair, a matching white dress and ruby eyes, all while walking towards me with a kind smile.

“Congratulations, Blair. You’ve achieved everything.” She extended her hands with a sparkling box. “I have a gift for you.”

“A gift…?” I received the box with some confusion as I slowly opened the lid only to pause.

The System spoke in my mind.

[Mutated Osthus Erinea. Lvl. 200]

I could see tentacles wriggling inside but they were mostly unresponsive. I blinked.

“This is.”

“It’s the same Osthus Erinea that saved your life. I resurrected it.” Lumina scratched her cheek. “Though it's very weakened and it also requires you to heal it by giving it your mana. It’s going to take a very long time before it's back to full health. But you seemed to care a lot about it.” She seemed a bit awkward.

I shook my head. “Thank you, this means a lot.”

Then I turned back to look at the box.

“Thank you.”

And I placed the lid over it. The plant was inert, it was probably best to let it rest. I held the box within my arms as I turned to look all around the hall. I could see the skeletal ceilings, tall arches made of wood and combed plants.

The tables had plenty of food, and people seemed to be enjoying themselves. The hall expanded forward, clearly leading towards a podium, but before we could do anything, a person approached us.

A familiar old human. It was none other than the Light Maker.

“I saw your battle. Congratulations Blair.”

“Ah, thank you.”

He nodded at me and walked off. Lumina nudged me from the side. “You have lots of fans now. Everyone saw the fight against the Emperor.”

I blinked. “They… did?”

“Yeah, I sort of decided to show everyone in the city…” Elasia scratched her cheek.

“Oh.” I nodded slowly, taking in the atmosphere once more. “That makes more sense.”

“Anyway, are you ready to claim the planet?” Lumina asked with mild concern. “I can understand that such an event is a bit… weird.”

I shook my head. “Why wouldn’t I be ready? Both of my parents are here.” I turned to the end of the hall. “Leon, Sun, Glim; all of my family is here.”

Lumina smiled. “Then let’s get this started.”

She took the box from my hands and clicked her fingers, instantly vanishing along with Elasia, appearing a moment later at the end of the hall along with Leon and the others. Sun was there too with a small bowtie going around his neck.

Then, the light changed as the edges of the hall darkened, giving me a straight line of light towards the end of the hall where there was a podium of plants.

I took a breath and began to walk forward under the curious gazes of everyone.
