The AoE spell, Seal of Expanding Detonations, propagated and finished off all the other non-dog-headed flying serpents completely, leaving only seven of the lesser serpents left at all. These had significant accrued wounds, however, and they thankfully didn’t have any relevant regeneration effects, by all indications.

The giant yellow serpent was viciously scoured by the AoE as well, burning it and causing numerous deep wounds as it was made to flop on the stone floor, its muscles and guts on display and oozing green ichor.

Samantha also had her Array spell up, and she chose to divert it to one of the dog-headed serpents that was less wounded, figuring it would be useless against the greater serpent. As the magic dart hit the flying serpent and caught it on fire, it screeched and careened to the stone floor, stunning it as all but one other had been, by the accrued Major Wounds dealt. At least, this was her guess about what she was seeing at the moment.

Preferring that Azure be freed up from the serpent, Samantha used her Haste action to hit the giant yellow beastie with a Seal of Devastation, taking advantage of Azure’s distance created from her evasion to keep her clear of the 2-meter radius.

The serpent had no potential for avoiding it, and the seal was etched directly on its skin and then detonated.

There was a titanic spray of green goo and gore as the beastie was completely eviscerated. Samantha avoided getting sprayed, but once again, Azure wasn’t so lucky.

“Oh, come the fuck on, Samantha!” Azure yelled in annoyance, soaked in both red and green gore, and it obviously wasn’t too pleasant considering that it was smoking from off of her.

“Sorry!” Samantha called back, though she remained pristinely untouched and felt rather giddy about that. It was likely short-lived, as she steeled herself for what she had to do next — move in and utilize melee against the seven-headed serpent, instead of wasting ranged spells that weren’t getting the job done. She brandished her staff, Foolmaker, and flew like the wind.

Her charge into melee opened her up to a quicker response, however. The seven-headed serpent sprayed from all seven heads, in another gout that was fairly unavoidable. On the other hand, it was directional…

Samantha kept diving forward, picking a beneficial angle, and conjured a body-sized mirror, using [Reflect Force], which quickly blocked one attack and reflected it. This functioned to block even more of the attack layers, as acid spray immediately hit more acid spray and was arrested, falling free directly downward.

The mirror shield was quickly disintegrated by criss-crossing acid spray, but Samantha had her Ardioq magic shield ready, catching more of the spray. She couldn’t avoid all of it, and it splashed over her legs, ripping through first one wound negation and then the other, as provided by Trilogy Fusion Soul.

That was it, though. She was clear, and moving in simultaneously with the gore-soaked Azure, who’d had to dodge the outer edges of the spray zone herself — which she’d done successfully, at least.

Samantha activated [Spellfyr: Charged Strike] and went for the central trunk of the beastie. She wasn’t sure what to expect, such as whether or not the serpent would try to eat her staff, but [Charged Strike] would go off on any sort of parry, at least.

The serpent did not try to bite the staff, either unable to trigger what ability it had before, or wanting nothing to do with the staff in particular. Regardless, it moved and slid away with surprising speed, deftly on track to avoid the strike and deliberately moving its heads around protectively, as if to say, ‘even if you do hit me, it’ll just be one head.’

Samantha shrugged internally and let the staff go spinning out of her hand to maneuver telekinetically via Foolmaker’s Magi’s Rapport trait. She stacked the deck further by cheating completely, with [Alter Fate], guaranteeing a successful hit right where she wanted.

The staff whipped around the serpent’s guard like a lightning bolt and slammed into its trunk, as its many eyes seemed to widen in shock at the impossible maneuvering.

The impact was a cacophonous explosion into its body, wracking it and driving deep into its core structure with pneumanic annihilation. It should have been disintegrated, but some last-ditch protective effect saved it and mitigated the wound.

Bah! Of course. Good for the goose, good for the gander, I guess.

Its many mouths were wide open, unleashing hisses of stress and alarm, if not quite pain.

Azure moved in on the opposite side, taking full advantage of both its focus on Samantha and its immediately wounded and weakened state, to slice with her spear three times, causing deep, bleeding gouges. That was likely a good thing, to counter its annoying regeneration effects, keeping it weakened and slowed.

One singular, flying dog-headed serpent was coming for Samantha’s back, but her chosen way of perceiving the world made it fairly difficult to go unnoticed, especially in the spirit world. Samantha simply used her second ‘due up’ pot shot from Sizzling Array to shoot it in the face, ending its charge prematurely as it was promptly stunned by the impact, like its brothers.

Samantha’s Haste action was up, her superior speed once again giving her the greater opportunity compared to her foes. The great thing about [Spellfyr: Charged Strike] compared to [Unleash] was that there was no cooldown between uses: she could keep using it. Taking a gamble that a standard strike would end the battle then and there, she activated the SP effect and went for what was hopefully a kill strike, levitating her staff without a need for really getting in close, though proximity did help utilize her skill more effectively.

By the most ridiculous of narrow margins, the seven-headed serpent detected the strike and interposed a head with startling quickness, parrying the staff by a feather’s breadth. It promptly disintegrated down to the root in a way that felt… final. The serpent’s other heads veritably snarled in outrage.

Oh. Well, then.

The serpent leapt at her as if off a spring, its many heads imminently snapping for her in a great tangle.

Samantha tracked them all with the cool and calm veteran precision born of many death-defying battles. She dodged one and was ready for the second waiting to munch her, kicking off of it with a foot slammed into its snout. Yet again, there was an interposed third head opening its maw, but she arrested her flight and rolled into a ball right over its head. The next, she avoided simply by using the great serpent’s own neck, flying around underneath. The fifth was on point, but did not see the shield until too late, and it found itself clamping its fangs onto the tough magic substance of minotaur artifice, Ardioq.

The sixth seemed unavoidable with her movements and the many blocked routes of the tangle of necks and heads. Samantha didn’t move at all — instead, her staff came and parried it by smashing it in the face, closing its mouth for it.

Samantha finally slipped away as the serpent fell from the limits of its leap, twisting and contorting itself in preparation for defense.

Untouched, undamaged, free of gore, clean dress and hair billowing in the torrents of the air, Samantha maneuvered her staff into play, activating [Spellfyr: Charged Strike] and sending it ruthlessly in pursuit of her foe, now more exposed than ever. Despite that she imagined she had an advantage, she nonetheless added an extra two SP, and on top of it, [Alter Fate: Hoping For Daylight].

The falling serpent attempted to parry again with a head, but it was hopelessly outmatched in speed and precision, and Samantha drove the staff right into its midsection like a spear. The explosive charge of energy enveloped it, and its whole shape could briefly be seen as if made of pneumanic energy alone.

Then, the multiheaded serpent disintegrated into dust.

Her staff and shield returning to her vicinity, Samantha watched as the remaining flying dog-headed serpents recovered and came flying for her. She smiled as she lifted a hand and readied three more AoE castings in succession. With three snaps of her fingers, seemingly endless explosions rang out like a symphony of destruction, and the serpents were finally blown apart and left to smoke the rest of the way to nonexistence.

And that brings an end to our fun, tough little bastards though you might’ve been.

Hands held behind her back, Samantha floated down to the door, where Azure waited, glaring up at her and slinging acidic goo off of herself. “An extremely hot display,” Azure declared, “though you left me extremely not in the mood with all these monster guts.”

Samantha chuckled. “There’s a first time for everything, it seems. What a shame.”

Azure rolled her eyes and sighed in defeat. “I lied. Wanna do it right here? Let me just clean up with a cantrip right quick, and-”

Samantha rolled her eyes inside their lids and sighed. “Stay focused, Blueberry! I know you’re just speaking from nerves right now. What might be contained in this temple scares as much as it intrigues, eh?”

Azure put her hands on her hips as she looked over at the obsidian door, staring at it for a long few moments. “Yeah, pretty much.”
