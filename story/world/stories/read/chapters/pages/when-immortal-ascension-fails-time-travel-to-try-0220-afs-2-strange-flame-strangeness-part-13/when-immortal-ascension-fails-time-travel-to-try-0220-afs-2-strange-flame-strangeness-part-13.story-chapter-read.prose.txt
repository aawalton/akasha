“Fairy Lin, are you sure this will work? Your strange flame looks…”

Using it in the pill would allow me to carve my final symbols into my Golden Core as long as I boosted the energy of the pill. To do that, I had to follow the 5 elements.

“I’d have to be some kind of impossible genius to read and understand all of those during the past few days.”

With a gesture, the Enchanting Golden Orchid flew into the cauldron and started to melt from its delicate petals. It also added a metal element to the pot.

Next was the Flat Armed Cactus. Even though it was a desert plant, it had a water attribute. “And speaking of students. I need to come up with a Daoist name for your sister, that is, if she’s still interested in learning from me.”

I was considering something along the lines of Winter to go with Spring but I’d have to think more about it.

After counting 27 breaths, I cut off the tips from the Vibrant Purple Stem and threw the stalk into the pot.

“Not today!”

I swiftly returned the flame and used a hand seal to normalize the temperatures. Then I put in the last ingredients after warming them by having them fly by the side of the cauldron first.

It was like her not being in the room with me while I created pills aggravated the laws of this universe.

Well, like fuck would I allow that to stop me.

Ah shit… he saw that, didn’t he? Well, whatever. If he could pretend that didn’t happen, so could I.
