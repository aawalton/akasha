Yes, I'm making a video game, which is one of the reasons why I've been so stressed lately.

Regarding Torchbearer, I have about 75K words done, and am 60% through the current arc. As you might imagine, writing has been a bit slow leading up to the launch of the Kickstarter preview, but as always, the story is stuck in my head and keeps me up at night.

I've recently done an interview with Andrew Rowe, who is an absolute sweetheart, and he was very encouraging about the story, which helped. As I mentioned before, I'm one of the only people in my writer's group who hasn't seen a lot of success on RR (let alone financial success) with writing, so sometimes, the impostor syndrome is strong with this one. I keep reading all these stories (currently bingeing The Last Horizon, which is bloody excellent) that do what I want to do, but manage to pull it off way better. That's hard on the mindset, sometimes, and in my desire to do better, I stagnate, go back, and over-edit, which I then have to undo. It's a dumb, long process, but I'm learning, and I think that you will like the next book a lot, once I am done with it.

That being said: Timeframes! As always, I can't make any promises, but at the current rate, I hope to be done and start posting again in a couple of months. I'll do two chapters a week until I've finished posting everything to complete book 2, then go back into my hole to write some more. I'm excited for book 2, as I mentioned, but book 3 is the one I wanted to write from the very beginning, and I hope you guys will enjoy it. I already got the rough outline done, and it's looking good so far!

So, thanks for your attention, time, and patience, and I hope to see you again soon!

P.S. If the game does absurdly well, I might add a Torchbearer Cameo! :D
