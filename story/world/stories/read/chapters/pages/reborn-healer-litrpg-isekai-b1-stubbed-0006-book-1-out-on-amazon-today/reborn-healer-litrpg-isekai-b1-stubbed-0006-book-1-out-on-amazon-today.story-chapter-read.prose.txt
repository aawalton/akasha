Legendary healers can save cities from the brink of death with a word, but warriors can destroy them just as quickly. Ren will become both.

Reincarnated into a world of magic and monsters, Ren gets a chance to live again. In a world where everyone awakens a single magical core, Ren discovers something impossible inside his soul: two. One a mana-bound healer, the other a warrior that wields the shadows.

But power comes at a cost. Ren’s unique abilities come with powerful enemies and strange mysteries. Guilds, demons, and kingdoms scheme as an era of change comes to the new world, dragging him into a bloody mess.

Fortunately, it turns out he thrives with his life on the line. Ren will walk through fire and drown in poison if that’s what it takes to grow his power.

What doesn’t kill Ren makes him stronger, and healers are very hard to kill.
