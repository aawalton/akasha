[ARE YOU CERTAIN IT IS NECESSARY TO WAIT UNTIL WE ARE SO FAR AWAY, DR. MAYHEW?]

“It isn’t necessary, but I’d prefer it,” Kyle said, using another blast of Repel to launch himself airborne in an arc, the towering tree in view.  “Besides, I thought you’d like to see the tree.”

[I VERY MUCH LOOK FORWARD TO IT, DR. MAYHEW.  I AM ALSO VERY INTERESTED IN HEARING WHAT SKILL OPTIONS YOU HAVE AVAILABLE FOR YOUR SECOND CLASS.]

“Well, then your curiosity can be satisfied all at once,” Kyle replied, launching himself once again.

Short as it was, the battle for Er’Mithren had resulted in Kyle pushing the boundaries of the World Tree Tender class in more dynamic ways than he had before.  While alone on Kertan Six, he spent much of his time in slow study and experimentation.  The results were impressive creations, such as the prosthetic arms that he tested against the Scourge, but they didn’t compare with the sheer volume of expenditure and creation he’d used to support the Resistance troops.  Moreover, the levels of fine control he’d had to exercise pushed in him some uniquely challenging ways.  Most interesting had been his creation of the wall of baobab trees and the support structure for the tower, each of which had likely netted him more than a full level on their own.

The nagging sensation within his Center told him that there was a skill selection waiting, which meant nine total levels in the World Tree Tender class, at least.  Given the relatively small gain in overall power he felt, he suspected that he still hadn’t gained any more in the Unbroken Survivor class, and given that he hadn’t been in any real danger since the fight with the Scourge, that tracked.

Still, Kyle wanted to make it to the massive tree before entering meditation.  It stuck out to him the first time he visited, and he wanted to get some time to study it undisturbed.  Given everything that needed to be sorted out in Er’Mithren, he felt that making himself scarce sooner than later was the wisest course of action.  There was little to be gained from staying, so he broke down the baobab forest with Parasitic Resonance, used Healing Impetus on those with more serious injuries, then absorbed the chaddlings before taking off.

As he approached his target, he could feel the gentle thrum of mana coursing through it, courtesy of Auric Perception.  The aura the tree gave off wasn’t quite C Grade, but it was close.  Furthermore, both he and C.H.A.D.D. felt the telltale fluctuations of some type of natural treasure, though even with his senses and the drone’s scans, they couldn’t quite pinpoint its location in the canopy.

[SHOULD WE GO AFTER IT NOW, DR. MAYHEW?]

“I thought you wanted to know what skill I’d choose?” Kyle teased back.

[I WANT BOTH,] the drone replied, impatience coming through Pack Leader’s Instinct.  Even Phaegos seemed somewhat interested in the tree, though the glutton was still working though all the mana it drained from the Scourge.

“Meditate first, then explore the tree,” Kyle promised, entering meditation.

He didn’t bother reviewing all of his attributes, just focusing on the only change.  The World Tree Tender class did reach Level 40.  With a quick allocation of his free attributes toward Willpower, he focused on the indistinct orbs representing his penultimate E Grade skill selection for the class.  His first two had been lackluster, and he sincerely hoped that this time would be more interesting.  As he focused his attention on the first of the shadowy gray orbs, he wasn’t disappointed.

Ingrain

Kyle saw an image of himself, or rather, the World Tree Seed he fused with, extending roots down into the ground.  At the same time, roots from within the seed delved into the space Between.  He saw himself sitting motionless for what felt like months, then years, in some type of meditation.  Yet, as he watched, he felt his power increasing.

I’m gaining levels, Kyle realized with a start.  The drawbacks of the skill were pretty obvious.  He couldn’t move, the leveling speed was slow, and it didn’t appear to have any application in a combat situation or to assist with his other projects.  At the same time, he’d never heard of a skill that would allow the user to outright grow in strength.

He had an instinctual feeling that it wouldn’t grant levels to his Unbroken Survivor class, and even more, that using it would actually decrease his Path Synergy.  Still, it could let him push the World Tree Tender to ever-higher power without any real effort, particularly if he waited to use the skill until he progressed to mid-tier C Grade.  He could theoretically set up for a few decades and emerge with his World Tree Tender class at peak D Grade.  Of course, with entirely lacking foundations and fundamentals.

There was no way he was going to choose the skill, but the sheer novelty made him excited to see what else was on offer.  And if the other skills are terrible…  Without letting himself dwell any further, he moved on to the next option.

Meld

This time, the vision showed Kyle in a forest, near a large tree.  He pressed his palm against it, and then slowly sank into the tree’s bark.  A moment later, he exited from a different part of the tree.  Next, he was in a grassland, and he reached down to the grasses, which were then absorbed into him.  He had the sense that each time he used the skill he would archive aspects of the plants he touched within the World Tree Seed, while also gaining a modest boost to his own mana regeneration while melded.

The skill was definitely niche, but had some potent applications.  First, when traveling to different places with unique plants, he wouldn’t need to necessarily drain them with Parasitic Resonance.  A quick use of Meld would help him add more of the essences to the World Tree Seed.  Secondly, depending on where he was, the ability to disappear into a network of trees or other large plants could prove useful.  Before the first meeting of his Tribunal, he wouldn’t have thought he’d need to hide from the Collective, but those were considerations he’d need to take more seriously if he was left out in the cold.

Keeping it in mind, Kyle turned his attention to his final option.

Graft

Kyle smiled as the vision started, already feeling confident that he found the next skill he was going to take.  True to his expectations, he saw himself using the skill to combine different plants together, allowing them to exhibit characteristics of one another.  To a degree, C.H.A.D.D. did something similar when the chaddlings were created, though each of those pieces were distinct, and just worked together.

Graft, on the other hand, would allow him to have plants that took on the characteristics of one another.  With enough time and mana, he was confident that they could not only enhance what they’d already created, but open the door to entirely new possibilities.  To a very large extent, Kyle had been using the World Tree Tender class like a pseudo-Crafter class, and this skill was the representation of that take.  That’s not to say that he wasn’t still putting large amounts of energy into the World Tree Seed, or using the associated skills for other things, but this boost to his capabilities aligned extremely well with his own vision for the future.

Still, there were three interesting options this time around, Kyle thought, reminding himself that this was still an E Grade class, after all.  His options back as a Healer at Level 40 weren’t nearly this interesting, although Haste had become the foundation for his absolutely incredible boosting skill in Genesis Engine.  By the time he reached late-tier C Grade, he expected that the upgrades to Graft would also forge it into something incredible.

Exiting meditation, Kyle felt the excitement from C.H.A.D.D., and didn’t try to hide his own.

[IS THE SKILL REALLY THAT HELPFUL, DR. MAYHEW?]

“I think it will be,” Kyle said, taking the time to explain what he understood of it.

[WE WILL NEED TO DEDICATE QUITE A BIT OF TIME TO TESTING.  I WILL BEGIN MAKING A LIST.]

“Please do.  And while you’re at it, keep an eye out for any reports of strange plant life.  I think that we’re going to want to start prioritizing anything with unique properties that we could start blending into a super-plant.”

[I WILL SEARCH THE RECORDS, DR. MAYHEW.  ONCE WE GET PAST YOUR TRIBUNAL AND THINGS SETTLE DOWN, WE WILL HAVE QUITE A BIT OF TRAVEL AHEAD OF US.]

“I don’t mind that at all, C.H.A.D.D.  I really don’t think that we’ll lose our Collective citizenship at this point, and the dust will settle soon.  Hell, even if we get booted from the Courier’s Guild, we could make a living supporting agricultural interests and do just fine.”

Kyle meant it, too.  Most C Grades that weren’t in the Guilds served as executives in larger organizations, or even ran their own.  There would be no shortage of groups looking to snap up a free agent like Kyle, particularly with his unique talents.  Of course, Jarberry hadn’t invested so much in the duo to watch them leave the Courier’s Guild.  He expected his impish patron would continue to push for their continued membership, keeping them close in the process.

Kyle stretched as he stood, looking up at the canopy of the massive tree.  “Ultimately, that can wait for a little while.  Let’s see what this tree is all about, shall we?”

As it turned out, the tree was all about being extremely large.  It was subtle, but with Identify, Kyle got hints that the space around the tree was compressed slightly, making the already massive plant appear smaller than it truly was from a distance.  The other thing Kyle noticed that raised his guard a bit was that there were no other living creatures around the tree, at least that he and C.H.A.D.D. could perceive.  There weren’t even any stranglevine pods around it, indicating that even the Scourge kept its distance.

As they traversed back and forth across the colossal limbs, Kyle continued to get a sense of something else vaguely familiar, though he struggled to place it.  Finally, as they crested the top of the tree, it started to click in his mind.  Though it didn’t have an effect on him, the tree’s aura felt vaguely similar to D’Oillelat’s.

“C.H.A.D.D., this thing is a variant species of some type; one that seems to impact other creatures here.”

[I WAS BEGINNING TO SUSPECT SOMETHING SIMILAR AS WELL.  THERE ARE OTHER CONCEPTS AT PLAY HERE AS WELL.  IF WE CAN FIND THE NATURAL TREASURE, IT MAY HELP CLARIFY THE TREE’S ABILITIES.]

Kyle nodded, surveying the city-sized crown of vibrant leaves atop the tree.  Something caught his eye, perhaps just a trick of the light, but he felt a pull from the World Tree Seed, and trusted his gut.  As he drew nearer to the center of the tree, the spatial compression magnified.  Each step he took, the tree grew larger, until Kyle and C.H.A.D.D. finally found what they were looking for.

There, just beneath the canopy of leaves, a single limb of the tree rose, not the rich brown of the others, but instead a crystalline blue.  It was only a few meters long, and looked impossibly frail; a ridiculous sight compared to the other oversized limbs.  On the small branch, a single emerald leaf hung, perfectly perched near the top.  Other diminutive buds grew on it, though none were larger than the nail on Kyle’s thumb.

As he stared at the branch and leaf, he felt a gentle pulse from the World Tree Seed.  As if on command, the leaf gently fell from the branch, drifting straight toward Kyle and C.H.A.D.D.  Without thinking, he reached out with his hand to catch it, the cool, smooth surface feeling as much like stone as it did like a plant.  He wasn’t sure how long he gazed at the leaf, the gentle mana and intent radiating through the emerald leaf into his skin.

There was a concept in the leaf that resonated with both the World Tree Seed and his path, and he felt that he was on the cusp of understanding it.  Minutes stretched to hours as he stared at his reflection, refracted back to him in the facets of the strange leaf.  And then, finally, it came to him.  This tree, this leaf, they’re timeless.  Ageless.  Enduring.

In that moment, a gentle golden glow radiated from the World Tree Seed.  As much on instinct as anything else, Kyle took the leaf and pressed it against his left shoulder, watching it absorb into the World Tree Seed.  He could feel it resonating with the strange space within the seed, causing the energy from some of the other treasures C.H.A.D.D. absorbed to stir as well.

[HOW DO YOU INTEND TO USE THAT TREASURE, DR. MAYHEW?] C.H.A.D.D. asked as they stood inside the strange sanctuary beneath the leaves.

“I’m not entirely sure, C.H.A.D.D.  But either way, I’m glad we came here.  For now, I think we’re best served studying it and understanding the concepts it uses.  Besides, we have plenty of other things to experiment with.”
