Alaric was nearly impaled by the massive fangs — each as long as his body — of the giant sandworm as it swallowed him whole. The only reason he survived was that he wedged himself between the bodies of the large desert chameleons that were devoured alongside him.

The massive lizards shrieked in agony as the fangs pierced their bodies. In turn, they crushed against Alaric from all sides. He was out of mana, but the power of the root chakra energy still surged through his body.

With a roar, Alaric heaved against the chameleons’ flesh, fighting to avoid being crushed. Do I have to escape the rift like this? Without his mana, his options were limited. But he refused to surrender.

He forced one arm through the tangle of bodies, seized a fang lining the worm’s mouth, and pulled with all his strength. Inch by inch, he dragged himself out of the writhing mass.

The giant worm continued to move, occasionally opening its mouth. Brief glimpses of light revealed that it was no longer underground. He waited for the right moment to escape. For now, he clung to one of the fangs to avoid being swallowed further.Alaric glanced back. The heap of dead chameleons was slowly pulled deeper inside by the worm’s powerful muscle contractions. Its entire body was lined with fangs tipped in black ichor, undoubtedly some type of poison.

He wiped the sweat from his brow. Phew! I managed to survive that. Alaric was eager to get out of this hell hole as soon as possible. The air reeked of rotting flesh, and the worm’s slimy saliva made his skin crawl.

Unwilling to wait any longer, Alaric pulled on the fang with all his might and tore it from its roots. The giant worm wailed in pain, its mouth yawning open.

“Did you like that? I’m just getting started,” Alaric taunted as he drove the fang into the worm’s soft flesh. It thrashed wildly, nearly throwing him off balance, but he kept stabbing the fang deeper into its mouth, again and again.Suddenly, he felt the worm plummeting toward the ground. It’s trying to burrow again. I have to get out now. Alaric sprinted toward the mouth but stopped short.

It was high above the ground, at least a few hundred feet in the air. He scanned the vast expanse of dunes and rocky cliffs. In the distance, a few pyramids shimmered in the rays of the golden sun.

Making up his mind, he leaped from the worm’s mouth and rolled to the side just as the giant beast plunged into the sand once more.

Alaric was battered, exhausted, and pain racked his body. The [Vital Mana Flow] skill had failed him long ago when his mana was depleted. Yet, he felt exhilarated to have survived the encounter.

After a while, a trickle of mana regenerated, and he activated [Mana Pulse] for a few seconds to survey his surroundings. Just as he suspected, several more chameleons were slowly stalking toward his location.

And, as if that weren’t enough, faint tremors rippled through the ground, growing steadily stronger as the giant sandworm tunneled closer, seeking revenge.

There was little he could do at the moment. Before leaving The Howling Expanse, he locked eyes with each chameleon as they materialized before him. “Just you wait.” He grinned. “I’ll be back for round two.”

Moreover, he realized that fully exploring and conquering The Howling Expanse would take much longer. The area was far more expansive than he had initially anticipated, judging from his brief time there.

Alaric estimated that he hadn’t even uncovered five percent of the entire region. A grin spread across his face as he sensed someone approaching.

Alaric studied the small vial of crimson liquid as it gleamed under the afternoon sun. It was the Troll's Blood Elixir.

Just as pills were designed to reforge the body up to a certain grade, elixirs were formulated to dramatically accelerate the body reforging process. However, they didn’t guarantee the grade one could achieve. The actual grade depended on the cultivator’s innate talent.

Alaric downed the Troll's Blood Elixir in one swift gulp. The thick liquid tasted sweet like honey but left a cloying, syrupy aftertaste. He funneled mana into his stomach and envisioned the potent energy of the elixir gradually dissolving within.

Once his belly radiated warmth, he directed this heat to spread throughout his body, reaching the farthest corners of his limbs. Gradually, the warmth intensified into searing heat. Alaric gritted his teeth and visualized the scorching energy merging with his blood.

A fiery sensation surged through his arteries and veins, as if his blood were boiling. When the pain reached its peak, Alaric’s consciousness was abruptly pulled from his body, and he felt himself plummeting into an endless void.

All awareness of his physical form vanished, leaving only the intricate network of blood vessels and the blood coursing through them. He could sense the flow, viscosity, and temperature of his blood with startling clarity.

Not only sense but also see, smell, taste, and hear every detail. Blood was the reservoir of life energy. And now, his awareness was completely attuned to this life energy pulsing through his circulatory system.

He descended deeper and deeper until the red blood expanded and unraveled into distinct components — plasma, red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets.

Alaric observed how each component of blood was distinctly different and how they interacted with mana and the vital energy flowing through his veins.

Many of the components were perfectly preserved, while others were broken, damaged, or decaying — impurities that needed to be purged from his body. He also noticed countless microtears lining his circulatory system.

He had no idea how long he lingered in this state. Once he finished gathering all the insight, he flooded his circulatory system with mana and noticed how effortlessly his blood absorbed it. Purging the old blood cells and mending the microtears felt far easier now.

His skin flushed pink, and steam rose from his body as he infused more mana into his blood. Yet, the excruciating pain was greatly diminished. After exhausting all his mana, he checked his blood reforging progress.

Blood Reforging:

Reforging Progress - 2%
