Naomi sensed the aura spike a split second before her friends. They were in a courtyard in the L-ReF compound, surrounded by a collection of large, parked vehicles and a loose crowd of people hurrying in every direction. The alarm had brought everyone to alert, with all eyes scanning for danger. Naomi forewent using her mundane senses, instead reaching out with her magical ones. One whiff of that powerful, malevolent aura was enough for her to begin shouting a warning. It was too late, the others were facing the wrong direction. The demonic presence bore down on them like a lightning bolt, aiming straight for--

"Jade!"

Naomi threw herself into her friend, knocking her aside just as a shadowy figure slammed into them both. Something tore through the bard's side, completely bypassing her armor's defensive field and sending waves of agony ripping through her. She screamed, clutching at where the tip of a glowing, obsidian blade emerged from her abdomen. She stared down at it in utter shock, unable to process the sight of the long, dark dagger skewering her though the back. Long weeks of hard training kicked in, forcing her body to move even as her mind struggled to keep up with events. She twisted away from the demonic creature pressing against her back, trying to wrench herself free from her grip.

She wasn't in time.

The blade buried in Naomi's back pulsed, sending an explosive wave of dark energy through the wound. The blast of destructive force unleashed into her body would have torn a normal human apart. As it was, Naomi's enhanced fortitude kept her in one piece… barely. There was a geyser of blood, a flash of blinding pain, and she knew no more.

Jade rolled to her feet, recovering from the tumble just in time to see a demonic woman wrench a midnight dagger from Naomi's back. The weapon left a smoking hole the size of Jade's fist in the bard, so complete that she could see right through her and out the other side. Jade was dimly aware of an explosion of blood and of her friends rushing to confront the new enemy, but her mind couldn't focus. Naomi's pained scream echoed in her mind, and she couldn't tear her eyes away as her friend fell to the ground, unmoving.

No…

The sound of her name snapped her back to the present. The demon was charging her, blade poised to strike. She had blurry, indistinct features that were hard to focus on. Jade got the impression of dark skin, horns, and long, white hair before the demon was on her, bringing her malevolent weapon down in a slashing arc. Jade blinked aside, dodging the attack and lashing out with a kick to the demon's side. It didn't do any real damage, but it did knock her off balance long enough for Jade to assess the situation. Rage threatened to consume her, burning through her veins like hot magma, but she held it at bay with everything she had. She couldn't afford to lose focus. First, she pulled up the creature's stats.

Or she tried to. An error message appeared, informing her that she was out of the Labyrinth's primary coverage range. She swore, blinking away the message and ripping both daggers free from their sheaths. Her cloak was next, dropped to the ground to reveal her succubus armor. She was in her human form, but her abilities would still provide some protection. Luis and Fenrin had followed up on Jade's attack and were keeping the demon busy. She took that opportunity to glance back at Naomi. The bard was groaning and stirring weakly, lying in a rapidly expanding pool of her own blood.

"Siora!" Jade shouted, but the elf was already dropping to her knees at Naomi's side, heedless of the blood.

"I'm… I'm on it!" Siora sounded shaken, but her hands glowed with golden-green light as she began channeling a healing spell.

Please, let it be enough…

She couldn't spare any further thoughts on worry, not while her friends were fighting for their lives. She didn't know who this was, and after what she'd done, she wouldn't live long enough for that to matter. Leaving her friend in the elf's capable care, Jade dashed through a crowd of panicked people and scrambling soldiers, trying to catch up to the fight. In the short time she'd been preoccupied, the clash between Luis, Fenrin, and the unknown demon had moved away from the main courtyard and into the doorway of a large warehouse that dominated one side of the L-ReF compound. Tara joined Jade as she drew near the battle, panels of white composite armor shimmering into existence around her lithe frame as she flicked her visor over her eyes.

"Jade, more of them!"

For the first time, Jade realized that she could hear the chatter of gunfire coming from the other side of the courtyard. A quick glance revealed soldiers converging on a large, glowing portal that definitely hadn't been there before the alarms started ringing. Demonic forms, some familiar and some not, were engaging a small group of Wayfarers and soldiers in a battle that seemed concentrated around a cluster of small storage buildings at the opposite end of the courtyard.

"We can help after we take care of this one!" Jade said, daggers appearing in her hands. Although she couldn't pull up the demon's info, her magical senses detected an aura strength that far surpassed her own. A monster like this would tear through regular soldiers and low level Wayfarers like tissue paper. They had to keep her contained.

As soon as she drew near enough Jade channeled mana into her legs, blinking forward and appearing behind the demonic woman just in time to bury her daggers in the small of her back. The creature's form blurred right as she struck, and Jade's blades passed straight through her opponent as if she were made of smoke. The unexpected lack of resistance left Jade off balance and she stumbled, leaving herself wide open. Only a timely blast from Tara's pulsecaster and one of Luis's bubble shields saved her from being eviscerated by a counterstrike executed with such speed, the demon's dagger all but disappeared. As it was, the tip of the obsidian dagger nicked her side, scoring a shallow cut in Jade's exposed flesh. Jade leapt away, resetting her stance and regrouping with her companions. She was about to launch another attack when, belatedly, her mind caught up with what had just happened.

My barrier didn't deflect that!

Her armor's wards usually intercepted the first couple of hits she took in a fight, with attacks inflicting wounds only while they were drained and recharging. Something about this demon's weapon, or one of her powers, let her ignore, or at least be less effected by, such protections. Jade's second of stunned surprise cost her the initiative. The demon dove into their midst just as Jade shouted a warning to her friends, landing between them and sending out a wave of force that sent the four of them stumbling away. Jade was not so easily disoriented, though, and she recovered in time to dodge another follow-up strike, parry a second, and counter a third. Again, her attacks passed straight through the woman's insubstantial form, having little effect.

"You'll have to do better than that." The demon said in a cool, condescending voice. "This is the group that caused us so much trouble?"

She was interrupted by one of Luis's force waves slamming into her back. It was followed closely by twin blasts of fire: One from Tara and the other launched from Fenrin's bow. These had a more satisfying effect on their enemy, sending her stumbling into Jade and leaving her open for an aura-enhanced slash across the chest. This time, Jade's mana-infused strike landed, burning through her fog-like body like mist under a rising sun. They weren't major wounds, but the snarl of pain they elicited satisfied Jade for the moment.

"I don't know who you are, and I don't care." Jade spat, circling back around the demon to rejoin her friends. The fight had taken them into the large warehouse. The building was rapidly emptying as the few workers present so late in the day fled the conflict. Outside, the sound of gunfire was joined by several loud explosions. Jade barely noticed, so focused on the object of her simmering fury. "I'm going to tear you apart."

The demon's only response was a slight smile.

The fight that followed was a desperate one. With Siora caring for Naomi, their group lacked any means of healing other than the potions they'd had on them at its start. That, combined with the demon's ability to partially ignore their armor's wards, put them in a very dangerous situation. Vylith attacked from the shadows, blinking from one patch of darkness to the next in a rapid sequence of teleports that made her nearly impossible to keep track of. Her attacks were like striking cobras, unseen and possessing blinding speed, their force barely blunted by their magical protections. Only their superior numbers and ample experience with acting as a team prevented the four of them from being torn apart in seconds, but it was a near thing.

Fenrin fought from the air as much as possible, leveraging his wind abilities to both keep him away from treacherous shadows and maintain the best sightlines possible in a building stacked high with large containers. Luis stayed with Tara and Jade, projecting shields, launching clinging tethers at their opponent, and interposing himself between them and the demon as often as he could. Every stroke of his sword left behind trailing threads of lambent light that soon formed a web of crackling energy, preventing them from being attacked from all sides.

Concious of its increased effect on her, Jade stayed away from the radiant energy and focused on dropping as many rune traps as her cooldowns would allow, trying to pin down their enemy for long enough to land a decisive blow. This was interspersed with brief but violent clashes of blade against blade as Jade fought to stay ahead of the assassin's dagger, relying on her ranged hail blade talent and her extended aura blades to keep her distance. She refrained from manifesting her incandescent aspect, though. That, she held in reserve, waiting for the right moment to land a critical blow that could turn the fight around.

Tara, unfortunately, was at a disadvantage. The tek'kalla was best suited to fighting a large number of weaker enemies rather than one tough enemy, and all the more so if that powerful foe was also fast. Unable to safely deploy her wide area attacks, she focused on using her magical senses and suit sensors to pinpoint the demon's movements. She kept up a constant barrage of weak but numerous bolts of fire, as much to keep her companions updated on their foe's whereabouts as to deal significant damage. Fighting like this, Jade and her friends landed a few solid blows on their enemy, but nothing decisive. For every strike they landed on her, she returned two or more in kind. That rate of exchange wouldn't have been a problem if their defenses were effective or they had healers but, as it was, they were steadily losing ground.

Finally, one of Jade's rune traps triggered. A tangle of whirling chains erupted from the now-blazing sigil, wrapping around Vylith and anchoring her to the ground. The magical bindings appeared unaffected by their subject's insubstantial nature, and the wraith demon wasted a critical second trying to simply phase out of the trap. It only took her a moment to realize her mistake and begin slicing at the impediment, hacking through solid chains like they were made of smoke, but it was exactly what Jade had been waiting for. Her aura surged, bursting with incandescent energy as she blinked behind the demon, shaping her mana into a pair of radiant blades projecting from her daggers. Her form shifted in an instant, wings and horns bursting from her body. The enhanced strength and agility of her demon form lent power to Jade's sudden attack, and she struck with an unexpected burst of speed.

She missed. The wraith demon slashed through the last of the chains just in time to twist away from the attack, shifting inside Jade's guard with practiced ease. Jade suddenly found herself face to face with the creature, staring into the dark, empty pools of her eyes. They drew her in, sending her spiraling into a world of frigid darkness. Jade didn't react as Vythis calmly raised her empty hand and plunged into her chest, aimed straight for her heart. The wraith demon's ghostly fingers had barely phased into her chest when she suddenly jerked away, snarling in pain. Jade experienced the distinctly unnerving sensation of something grasping not at her flesh, but at her spirit. It felt as though the demon had been about to rip her conciousness straight out of her body.

What the hell?!

The sudden shock shattered the spell and Jade blinked, realizing that she'd been standing stock still for several seconds. Luis had positioned himself in front of her, firing off more arcs of force as he shouted something over his shoulder at her. She heard the words but was having a hard time processing them. Whatever the demon had done to her mind was still in the process of fading, but she was aware enough to take in the situation. Tara and Fenrin were blasting away at Vylith and actually looked to have her on the defensive. The demon's dagger was nowhere to be seen. Instead, she clutched at her hand, which looked blackened and burned. As if seared by…

My aura!

Jade's concentration had lapsed, but she manifested her mana around her once again. This time, she highlighted its inner radiance, channeling the angelic essence until her own skin began to prickle from the dangerous energy.

"What are you?!" Vythis hissed, transfixed by the halo of light emanating from Jade's demonic visage. Before Jade could respond, Fenrin sent a lightning arrow straight through her chest. The arrow phased straight through her without much of an effect, but the burst of electricity it sent through her form was another matter. The wraith demon shuddered and screeched, retreating towards the warehouse entrance. Jade took off after her at a dead sprint, ignoring Luis's attempt to stop her.

"I'm not letting her get away!" Jade snarled, tucking her wings behind and putting on a burst of speed.

"No, Jade, look out!"

Too late, she realized what he meant. She emerged from one of the aisles of stacked crates, hot on the tail of her wounded quarry… and right into the sights of the two squads of L-ReF soldiers in black fatigues that had finally arrived to contain this new threat. The first thing they saw was two demons charging straight at them, and that was all they needed to know. An instant later, the air around Jade was filled with a hailstorm of bullets.
