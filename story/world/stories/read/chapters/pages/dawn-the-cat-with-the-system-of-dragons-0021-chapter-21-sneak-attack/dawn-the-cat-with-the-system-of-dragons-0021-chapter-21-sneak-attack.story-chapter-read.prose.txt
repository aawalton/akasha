It was morning. Unlike any other day, the beginning of this day was special, but not in a good way. The cyclone was making everyone’s life difficult. It started in the early morning and caused a huge disaster in the forest. Sylvane was also affected by it.

In the end, adventurers were dispatched to create a barrier to cover the entire town, just in case the cyclone started expanding.

Thankfully, that never happened. In fact, the cyclone was getting smaller and the wind currents were getting weaker.

Currently, adventurers were standing not far from the corpse of a dead wyvern, outside the border of Sylvane.

This wyvern was killed by Dawn when it was about to crush her with its jaw.

If anyone other than the adventurers were to look at the corpse, they would not be able to recognize what creature it was. The corpse looked like a scattered pile of thick skin, scales, organs, and bones. Blood was everywhere in the vicinity.

Seeing the wyvern getting killed instantly was shocking for everyone.

While everyone was looking at the cyclone and waiting for the next command, Dawn was standing on a tree branch, looking at the adventurers while hiding.

Aegis was currently controlling Dawn’s body. Its goal was to take the elemental core from the wyvern’s body. But it didn’t want to get attacked again, so observing the current situation was necessary.

“Elenora, how did you befriend such a strong evolved cat?” Hasper asked Elenora calmly. All the nearby adventurers were listening. They also wanted to know the secret to taming a strong beast. If they could get a beast companion like that, it would make their life easier.

“Huuh…” Hearing the question, Elenora wanted to start scolding everyone, but stopped, thinking this was not the right time.

She knew about the guardian level monster that had taken Dawn under its protection. So she could also imagine how badly the situation would escalate if the black cat died at the adventurers’ hands. She wanted to say it, but then she stopped. Informing them about everything would only increase her troubles.

In the end, she continued saying without informing anything else. “When I met it, it was just like any other street animal. You would not believe how badly injured it was after fighting with multiple dogs. My daughter attacked the dogs with magic, and then I healed the black cat. Since then, I have been feeding that cat. It must have evolved while staying in the forest.”

“Your daughter attacked the dogs, you healed the cat, and then it evolved!” Hasper had many questions about this, but didn’t ask about them, thinking it would seem like an official investigation. “I guess you had a lucky encounter. Everyone these days wants a strong beast companion.”

(You wouldn’t be so envious if you knew about the monster that is protecting the cat and what it did to the cat.) Hearing Hasper’s reply, Elenora thought that he was envious and sighed internally. “I guess it was a lucky encounter.”

Suddenly, Elenora noticed the black cat coming back. She was sure that the cat would not come back for a while. But even after being attacked, it still came back and was walking towards the scattered body parts. Then the black cat entered the bloody mess while sniffing.

Elenora was shocked to see this. She didn't understand what it was doing. But then she noticed something different about Dawn. This time, Dawn didn’t have her extensions like before, but her eyes were still glowing.

Aegis needed a constant source of energy to maintain the claw extensions. Since the cyclone was getting weak and the elemental density in the surrounding area was not as high as it was inside the cyclone, Aegis didn’t bother to maintain the extensions. Otherwise, it would have drained Dawn’s small energy reserve. Even the core that Dawn had eaten earlier didn’t have any energy left.

All the adventurers in the vicinity were bewildered. Many of them were prepared to attack, many were standing in defensive positions, and many were simply waiting for the next order from Hasper.

All of them knew what the cat could do, so no one dared to attack this time.

“Elenora, are you sure the cat is safe? I mean, look at its eyes, it seems like it is prepared for an attack.” Hasper asked Elenora just to be sure. Looking at the cat, he was not sure whether to trust Elenora or not. Clearly, the cat was using elemental energy in its eyes. This was a danger sign for Hasper.

“...Yes.” (Probably) "Don't worry about it. See, the cat doesn’t have the big glowing claws anymore.” Elenora didn't say 'probably' out loud. It was better to say positive things about the cat. If the adventurers attacked again and the cat died, they would have to incur the wrath of the monster.

Moreover, the black cat's attention was towards the corpse, and it was totally ignoring the adventurers as if they didn't exist. So both Hasper and Elenora were relieved.

After looking for some time inside the scattered pile of body parts, Dawn finally took out an elemental core with its mouth and started walking towards the adventurers.

A few of the adventurers flinched, noticing the cat walking towards them.

The black cat stopped near Elenora's legs and mewed while holding the core with its mouth. Dawn was covered in blood from head to toe. Elenora didn't want to be near it because of the blood.

Elenora kept looking at it, bewildered. “Dawn, what are you doing?” (It wants to give me the core?)

Aegis then moved behind Elenora and jumped on her shoulder.

Elenora was smiling from outside and having chaos from inside. “Ahh… Nice…” (Of course, it wants to use me as a platform. Huuh….)

Then, Aegis started resting Dawn's whole body over Elenora’s shoulder. Aegis' goal was to wait until the cyclone was over and see what humans were talking about. It was the best spot to gather information.

All the adventurers kept looking at Elenora and the cat. “I also want to get a strong beast companion.”

“Agreed. I want one too.”

“Then get prepared to get your clothes bloody just like hers.” One of the adventurers said while looking at the black cat covered in blood, resting on Elenora’s shoulder. There was nothing Elenora could do. She could only sigh.

And just like that, an hour passed. The cyclone was finally over. The adventurers didn’t need to put up a barrier. In the end, it never expanded to the town.

Aegis didn’t get any useful information. The only thing Aegis got to know was that the adventurers would start investigating the cause of the cyclone and why it was created with a high concentration of elemental energy. For them, it was concerning.

To Aegis, it was a given that this would happen sooner or later.

Aegis then jumped from Elenora’s shoulder and ran into the forest undergrowth.

“Wasn’t that cat yours? Why did it run off inside the forest?” Hasper asked.

“The cat comes every morning and evening. The rest of the time, it stays inside the forest. I guess it was waiting for the cyclone to be over,” Elenora replied while looking in the direction where Dawn had just disappeared.

(You mean it is just an evolved wild cat that comes to you for food?) Hasper thought this and didn’t say it out loud.

Inside the forest, Aegis was running fast through the undergrowth, straight towards the big tree. Since all the animals ran away, there was no need to look for nearby predators. To get more cores, it was necessary to secure the wyvern’s core that was in Dawn’s mouth. And the best place to secure it was Dawn's den.

The tree had removed its roots from the den since the elemental pressure was not there anymore. When Aegis entered the den, Dawn noticed her babies were alright. But all of them were mewling in distress. Only the adopted baby was silently twitching. Probably it was also distressed.

Dawn wanted to stay with them for a while.

Aegis moved forward, put the core in the ditch, and then went back out of the den without even waiting for the babies to calm down. To Aegis, getting the core was more important, as the wild animals would soon start coming back, and they would take the core.

Aegis has to move fast because of this. And the worst part was that Aegis didn’t know the location of the wyvern corpse.

Dawn didn’t understand any of this. To her, moving away from her babies was unacceptable.

But Dawn didn't know how to take back control. When Dawn tried to move her body, nothing happened. Her body kept moving automatically. Dawn didn't want this.

After trying for a while, Dawn finally snapped. She started trying to move her body desperately, but it still kept moving according to Aegis.

Then Dawn finally lost her patience. She kept trying to control her body.

Being a cat, Dawn’s thought process was very simple and instinctive. She kept trying to move her body. If she had a complex thought process like humans or dragons, Dawn would have commanded Aegis to give back the body’s control without any delay.

Aegis kept taking advantage of this and kept doing whatever was necessary for Dawn to evolve. Taking care of its real user, the infant dragon, was the priority. And forcefully evolving Dawn was crucial for protecting the dragon baby.

After a while, Aegis heard a loud roar from afar and stopped running. It twitched Dawn’s ears, smelled the air, and then, after determining the direction, started running towards it.

After reaching near the destination, Aegis finally saw what was happening. A wyvern was fighting with a lion and three lionesses. Dawn got scared looking at the fierce battle.

Aegis wanted to attack, but there was a huge problem. There was no dense elemental energy in the surrounding area, and the elemental core that Dawn ate inside the cyclone also had no elemental energy left.

Without a dense elemental energy source, Aegis couldn’t use its full power. Dawn didn’t have a vast energy reserve.

More than half of its energy was used up when Dawn obliterated the wyvern, and then when the adventurers attacked Dawn, elemental energy was used to create a barrier, which wasted most of the remaining elemental energy, as Dawn dashed away. The barrier was not needed in the end.

After thinking for a bit, Aegis decided to observe them and wait for them to tire themselves out and kill each other. This may allow Aegis to snatch the elemental core and run away when any of them starts having a meal after killing its opponent.

Aegis noticed that the wyvern mostly used its claws and jaws to attack, and used its wings to flap and dodge the incoming group attack. Then, after a while, Aegis got a smell of blood.

Clearly, the battle between the wyvern and the lion and lionesses was not concluded. None of them had major injuries. This was very suspicious.

Aegis started looking here and there and finally understood what exactly had happened.

A wyvern was sitting far away from the battle area, and another wyvern was lying down there. Its body was filled with blood.

Seeing this, Aegis concluded that two wyverns must be protecting the heavily injured or the presumably dead wyvern.

Now, there was no way Aegis could get any core. This was the worst possible outcome. The wyvern would surely kill Dawn the moment Aegis tried to snatch the core. The wyvern was waiting there. It was not tired, it was not injured. If all the predators were attacking each other, the story would be different.

Then Aegis started descending slowly from the tree and started heading back to Dawn’s den.

Suddenly, an idea struck. Aegis looked back. The wyvern was still trying to push back the evolved lion and lionesses. The wyvern standing a bit away was guarding another lying wyvern. The standing wyvern was looking at the battle going on.

Aegis started walking slowly while crouching down. It was Dawn’s hunting position. Aegis was circling around the standing wyvern from a distance.

After some time, Dawn reached a spot behind the wyvern, which was standing guard over its lying peer while watching the battle. The battle was about to be concluded. The standing wyvern was intensely focused on the fight, trying not to miss anything. Aegis took this chance.

The standing wyvern was a bit far away. Aegis started moving toward the wyvern from behind. Then, after closing the distance a bit, Aegis climbed the nearest tree behind the wyvern.

Then, after reaching high enough, Aegis jumped toward the wyvern’s head. The aim was to take out both eyes first.

Aegis used most of the elemental energy in Dawn’s claws, and as soon as Dawn’s claws reached both of the wyvern’s eyes, Aegis pushed out all the elemental energy through the claws, like a sudden wave, slashing the wyvern’s eyes along with its skull.

Dawn didn’t have much energy, so the attack was not devastating. But it was dangerous enough to slash the skull along with both eyes.

“Rooooaaaaar.” The wyvern roared in agony. It was not killed as expected, but it had two deep wounds on its skull and was blind.

Immediately after attacking, Aegis jumped from its head to its back to avoid getting attacked with its claws, then jumped down from its body and dashed away as fast as possible.

The wyvern started thrashing around, rolling on the ground, and moving away.

Hearing the loud roar, the wyvern, which was fighting the lion group, got distracted. It was not expecting its peer to roar loudly in distress.

It glanced behind, and the lion attacked its neck immediately. All the lionesses jumped on its back and held it in place. After a few seconds, the battle concluded.

The wyvern died by the lion group. This was not supposed to happen. The wyvern was expecting support during the fight’s climax, but everything turned for the worse instantly. The support never came.

It was because of Aegis.

The thrashing wyvern moved a bit away while the other group’s fight concluded. Aegis started running towards the lying wyvern. It was clearly dead. Aegis concluded that the two wyverns must be its companions and were trying to protect the dead wyvern’s body when its blood attracted the group of evolved lions.

There was a lot of blood on the ground below the dead wyvern. The wyvern’s back was slashed from its neck to its tail. Aegis remembered attacking a wyvern’s back when Dawn was in the cyclone.

Aegis moved to its chest and used the remaining Elemental energy to create an invisible elemental wave to attack just above the wyvern’s heart, to make a way and take out the core.

Because of the armor-like skin, it was necessary to use all the remaining energy in Dawn’s body. Though the energy finished, it was worth it.

Aegis inserted Dawn’s claws into the chest, took out the core, held it with Dawn’s jaw, and started dashing.

Aegis glanced behind while running away to see whether Dawn was being chased or not. But there was no one.

The wyvern was still thrashing, and lions were now moving towards it. They clearly finished their first prey.

Seeing this, Aegis stopped immediately.

One wyvern was dead, one was thrashing, all the lions were extremely injured and tired, and Dawn had one Elemental core which belonged to the wyvern that was killed in the cyclone.

Aegis formulated another daring plan.

The lion and the lionesses were currently trying to take control of the thrashing wyvern. Because of its rapid and erratic movement with its huge body, the lion group was having a hard time controlling it, especially with their injured and tired bodies.

Aegis started waiting for the lions to finish what they were doing and tire themselves out even more.

Since the thrashing wyvern was only injured in its eyes and was not tired much, it had lots of energy to spare. It would have killed the lions had its vision been intact. But that was not possible anymore.

It struggled a lot to scare away the tired lions. It even attacked the lions blindly, but nothing worked. In the end, the lions won after a lot of struggle.

As soon as both the wyverns died, the lions, without even waiting to secure their prey, sat on the ground to recover enough stamina to secure the hunt.

Aegis immediately made Dawn eat the Elemental core, which was in her mouth, and enhanced Dawn’s body.

After enough enhancement, Aegis started sprinting.

The lion group noticed this and stood up.

“Roooooaaaaaaarrrrrr”

Aegis ignored this and came just in front of the lion’s mouth. The lion tried to bite Dawn with a sudden movement, but Aegis dodged by jumping to the left and used its claws to slash the lion’s neck.

With a single point release of a burst of Elemental energy, the lion got beheaded, and the lioness, which was just beside, also got sliced in half from its torso.

Two lionesses were shocked seeing this. They were having a hard time processing what they had just witnessed. Two of their companions were dead just like that! It was unbelievable.

Aegis immediately jumped towards the two lionesses, which were above the wyvern’s body. They froze the moment they saw that they were the target.

One tried to attack, but Aegis moved fast and jumped on its forehead. The system used the enhanced claws to pierce its neck. The blood started gushing like a fountain.

Aegis took out its claws and instantly noticed an open jaw that was about to bite and crush Dawn’s body. It was the lioness that froze seeing its one hit two kills.

Aegis had no time to move. It immediately created enlarged, blue -colored claw extensions and slashed the jaws. The lioness’s face was cut into five slices from the front.

Every predator died.

Now, Dawn had very little energy left. There was no cyclone, so one wyvern’s core was barely enough to enhance its whole body and attack with elemental wave and claw extensions.

Aegis used the remaining energy to pierce the chest of every dead beast and take out the Elemental core.

Aegis got a total of six Elemental cores. Two of them were from wyverns, which were high-level, and four were from lions, which were equivalent to one wyvern.

Aegis understood that if it had not attacked the wyverns, then all the lions would have died. Though they were strong, it was taking everything for them to defeat one wyvern. Even if they had won, the wyvern that was guarding the dead wyvern would have surely killed them.

Aegis essentially took the opportunity of their weakness and their distraction.

Aegis held all the cores in Dawn’s mouth and started darting towards her den.
