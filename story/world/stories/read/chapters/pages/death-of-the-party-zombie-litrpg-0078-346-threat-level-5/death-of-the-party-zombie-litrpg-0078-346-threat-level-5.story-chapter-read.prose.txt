Sally opened her eyes to the ruddy hues of Hell. She raised a hand to her throbbing head as her stomach gurgled. Someone reached down to help her off the floor, and she took a moment to realize it was a familiar face.

“Ah, Lana.” The zombie grinned. “I just had quite the dream.”

A bandana held back the woman’s dark curly hair, and she wore an odd scanning device over her eye. After grimacing for a moment, she shook her head. “I can never tell how serious you’re being.”

Sally glanced around, and reality settled in. “Rats. That big, powerful Radoch killed me, huh? A little mean, but a back and forth is expected.” She looked down at her hands, confused as to why she had fingers instead of vines.

“I’d love to talk more, but we’re preparing to return to the surface.” Lana gave her a grim smile. “Second life. All or nothing.”

“Just throw me to the wolves, and I’ll come back with wolf brains in my tum.” Sally wavered slightly. “But… if I’m here, who is holding the big bug back? Fangs? Archie?”

“No. Archie is dead. Chuck is fighting it.”

Most people would think twice about turning themselves into an intangible being. Fewer still would devote their existence to running an entire planet. Of those remaining, many would cling to their power, not pass it around for the good of all.

In this moment, Chuck wished he had been a bit more of a selfish tyrant.

His shielding aura brightened to the point of almost breaking as it split the Hive King’s attack against it. The remaining energy struck the ground behind him, vaporizing every zombie and Radoch in the way, before scarring the dirt for fifty feet.

The roach leader didn’t even want to kill him, he could sense that. Wound him enough to collect, sure. The lack of monologue or desire to engage in useless chatter was an annoying confirmation that he was serious and focused. No doubt he was angry that his main ship was obliterated. Collection and potential way off Sanctuary all in one.

Archie dying in the process wasn’t part of the original plan. While the army had a non-zero amount of resurrection items, it was difficult to reach the wizard’s body with it being behind enemy lines in a deep crater filling with seawater.

A worthwhile sacrifice, to take out so many important assets, along with one very important piece of technology.

The King darted down from the sky, exploding against the ground. Chuck had switched his main form with a nearby projection, avoiding the damaging attack. A wry smile crossed his face.

With a flash of blue, Sally appeared behind the Hive King and chomped down. He moved at the last second, so she only managed to crunch into his thick chitinous shoulder.

As the scraps of the Radoch army on the Isles tried to gather themselves to join their brethren on the front lines, hundreds of portals opened up around them. Hell unleashed not only every Adventurer that had died, but every demon and dark denizen of that secretive area.

The invaders were now surrounded, pushed from every side.

With a growl, the Hive King flipped forward, tossing Sally off of his shoulder. As she bounced across the ground to her feet, he lashed forward with a fiery lance.

A metallic clang rang out across the battlefield, as the strike ended several feet before the zombie. Theo strained against the King, his coffin holding back the pointed end of the lance.

“Is this +5 Threat bothering you, m’lady?”

“That high, even for you?” Sally brought out Deathforged and her dagger. “Take this seriously now. That is an order.”

“Understood.”

Theo snapped forward instantaneously, moving past the lance so that his lance jabbed at the King. As the plated roach slid backward twenty feet, a cone of lesser fighters died from the shockwave. Theo moved again, as if teleporting into his strike, repeating the killing blast and sending the lead Radoch into the air. The air burst again as he appeared for a third strike, but the Hive King caught him around the neck.

Foul wings buzzed in the air, keeping the pair aloft. Golden energy glowed around the Radoch, burning at Theo’s skin. Before he could strike at the held vampire, the King twisted to deflect a sniper shot. Arcane cards fluttered around him to obscure where the shot came from.

“Irritating fleshbags,” he bellowed. With the flick of his arm, he sent the vampire tumbling toward the ground as he tried to locate the other Outsiders.

Sally dodged the attack of a Commander, allowing her zombie to be split in two so she could sever off the offender’s arm with Skeleton Key. She cast Life of the Party and A Score to Settle, followed by Summon Giant Zombie.

Chuck appeared beside her, flickering as he eyed the ongoing melee. “Archie wanted to give you this.”

The Hive King slammed into the earth beside the magician and swung at him. Rather than hitting Max, the lance shattered the door standing in place where he had been. The ruined door slowly swung open as it were on hinges, revealing a cannon behind it - which promptly blew confetti over the enraged Radoch.

Sally slipped the skull-motif ring onto her finger. “You know, I was expecting some kinda deus ex machina device to get rid of the stubborn bug.”

“You put a weapon and armor piece in the provided slots,” Chuck explained, ignoring her. “It summons five undead of the class or profession of what you stuck in. Wizard gear, wizards. Get it?”

“Should have forewarned me before I did spring cleaning.” She spun her dagger around before it vanished into the ring. After a quick glance through her Inventory, she added a cloak that increased the dodge chance and movement speed of any allied undead nearby.

Five circles of green light bloomed around as tall wights appeared, each draped in long black shadows and holding a Skeleton Key.

Another sniper shot rang across the battlefield, killing several elites before destroying a large weapon platform. There were very few weaker Radochs left, and any attention that should be going toward the Hive King kept getting split between attacking Commanders and odd devices trying to kill them.

Big Joe wrestled with a massive toad-like monster. The other Dangers slowly pushed in through the flank of slow-moving cannons and energy throwers. Wolf tackled the monster side of the fleet from the other flank, aided by the members of Sanctuary flooding the back lines.

The Outsiders struggled to gain space in the middle of the battle, getting barely any time to fend off the Hive King for a couple of attacks before being swamped by Commanders and other elites.

What was even worse was they hadn’t even damaged him.

“Feels like he is draining our power or something.” Theo sidestepped the swing of a blade. “I can’t access my Aspect. Ability limiter somewhere?”

Before he could look around for a culprit, the Hive King appeared beside him. Theo vanished and bit down into the Radoch’s neck, but failed to get purchase. Instead, the King slammed his weapon into the ground, creating a blast that blew up everything in a fifteen-foot radius. The vampire staggered back, dazed. It was only by instinct he dodged away and avoided being decapitated.

But the pointed tip of the lance did cut through his throat.

He grabbed at the wound as blood pulsed through his fingers. Anti-healing blast, he thought. It was a coin toss whether the debuff faded before he bled out or not. Too ironic a way to go. With a one-handed grip on his coffin-mace, he deflected the next two follow-up attacks, but the bleeding hadn’t stopped.

Max appeared beside him and clicked his fingers, casting his Demonic Domain.

Blood-slick mud became wooden boards of rich brown. Stage lights rose up into the air, illuminating the created stage and several rows of seats. Zombies and Radochs alike crowded the audience, with the King himself at the forefront.

The magician twirled his hat into his hands. “For my first trick, let me pick out someone from the audience.”

A spotlight ran across the restrained masses, back and forth before stopping at the Hive King. The sound of gunfire came from backstage, passing out through Max’s held hat and detonating on the Radoch leader.

Oil Slick, Fire Vulnerability, Greater Slow, and Fireball detonated from the magic bullet, causing the audience to erupt not in cheer, but in flames.

“Things are really heatting…” Max began before the King twisted and broke through one side of his restraints. “Ah, impatience is a sin.”

He instead brought an arcane card into his hand and pooled energy into it. From purple to lilac, and finally white. It grew in size until it was as big as a poster. As he readied it to throw into the burning crowd, the illusion of his Domain started to flicker.

The Hive King burst forward instantly, putting the end of his lance into the magician’s throat before continuing out the back of his head. Muddied field replaced the stage instantly, and the lights overhead faded to grim daylight.

Max appeared a dozen feet away and launched his card.

While his aim was normally critically precise, the King moved fast. Sparks and gore flew into the air as one of the Radoch’s upper arms dropped from his body. The card continued on through the battlefield, bisecting several elites before the energy powering it dissipated.

Red-hot anger flooded the Hive King’s eyes. He took a step forward before a bullet pinged off the side of his head, barely leaving a mark. White lights blinked into life across a gauntlet on his lower right arm as he gave the direction of the gunshot a glance.

Several miles off, where the rest of the battle raged, several metallic structures whirred and moved in place. Detonating brief shockwaves, each shot two orbs of green light into the air - ten in total. These balls of energy traveled straight up for several seconds before whizzing off almost horizontally.

Theo fought back against three Commanders at once, gradually becoming dizzy. He winced as bright green light blinded half of the battlefield. Ten distinct explosions blew heated air and the smell of charcoal across the fighting. Ren hadn’t been able to dodge them all.

It wasn’t the only death - he could tell. But an Outsider stung the most.

Sally felt it too and ground her sharp teeth together. They had made such a leap to gain an advantage over the bugs, but the threat was still stacked against them. She imagined that the win condition was for them to all use their Aspect forms, but with something blocking them, they were at a loss.

She watched as Max transformed into his demon form and flew at the King to get revenge. Hundreds of cards spiraled around him, turning purple before clouding the Radoch. The King didn’t move, but instead attacked into the cards, sweeping a horizontal strike with his lance that sent out a long arc of energy. Max couldn’t fly high enough to dodge it, and it severed his legs off. The Hive King was in the air to meet the magician already, and a blast removed the man’s head.

Chuck was off trying to bring back Archie. She hadn’t seen Hunter since she came back from Hell. Everyone else was tied up in fights more their caliber. Theo was…

She turned to see the vampire sluggishly beating back Radochs. Her arm went out, but something stopped her from using her healing threads. A curse? She glared at the Hive King. It was clear now that his armor and affixed trophies gave him dozens of overpowered auras and abilities.

//Sally: Feed.

Theo vanished from his fight and appeared behind her, immediately sinking his fangs into her neck.

“Drain me if you need,” she grunted. “The walking curio shop has gathered every cursed trinket known. We need a way to break him.”

The vampire moved his mouth away. Although the bleeding from his neck had slowed, he wasn’t healing yet. “Perchance lend me your dagger, just this once?”

She sighed. “Really, after dropping the perchance on a gal? I guess it’s a special occasion, so just this once.”

As soon as she handed Skeleton Key over to Theo, an odd light brightened his eyes. A pink glow ran down the short blade - and he was off.

One simple incision was all that was needed to fell a Radoch, and he darted between elites and Commanders on the path to the Hive King, increasing in speed until he reached the leader.

Sally beamed with glee.

Right up until the Hive King caught Theo mid-attack, and bent his head clean backward.
