Time blurred for Luke. He was consumed with killing the green slimes for countless hours, collecting points, resting his soul, which still ached. Every battle melted into the next; moments flashed by as he continued to adapt to the way the slimes fought. His hands grew harder through every gash of acid spilling on him, learning how to shift the slime’s weight around.

Countering after every move, deflecting, and using his arm as leverage. Luke continued to battle on, consuming fewer and fewer points on restore until he felt like he couldn’t go on or until his hunger consumed him.

Looking down, Luke saw how the clothes he had arrived in had become dirty, scorched, and eaten away by the acid, leaving him with rags hanging from his body.

Luke walked over to the familiar pedestal, its tall, reflective surface, without a single scratch from all the previous battles, completely restored from the explosion.

His body was also cleared of any wounds after his continuous restores. But it couldn’t remove the calluses that formed along his palm, but he felt ready, clenching his fist, enjoying the baked feeling of his raw hand.

But the ground couldn’t be spoken of the same way, with most of it still being uneven and still being littered with crevices and manholes.

But today was the moment when he would take on floor 3. His soul was no longer under the effect of fatigue as the marks had been restored to his golden, shining luster, leaving the crimson color behind. Fighting all his previous battles with only his natural body.

It was finally time to put his soul under stress again to compete against another enemy, wondering if he was the only person who was currently using souls to fight. Knowing well that if his soul collapsed, he would no longer be the same person. He might as well be just an empty shell for whatever takes over.

But with his soul back in perfect condition, while even being stronger than it was, he felt like he had a chance against this slime if he gave it everything he had. Pressing his hand on the pedestal, he willed the next floor to begin.

This time, a shift in the atmosphere caught his attention compared to all the previous ones; it actually felt hotter than before.

The surrounding temperature was usually perfectly the same at any given moment, being a mix of warmth that wanted nothing more from him than to embrace it, but it actually turned almost into a blistering heat that he hadn’t felt in a while.

As if it were summer, with the sun's rays beaming directly into his skin, the surrounding energy only grew before it died down to reveal what stood before him on the platform. It was a burning orb of fire. Or more likely a crimson red ball shaped like all the previous slimes, being even smaller than the green one.

It completely broke his previous vision of the slime getting bigger and bigger as the floors continued onward, clearly breaking the stereotype he had thought of. But he guessed it was fine for now as it’s not like he could predict what was ever going to pop up from these different floors.

But he was alarmed to see the slime only grew hotter as it stood there, creating lumps of smoke as it rose from the burning soil that was scattered across the platform. It was even reaching the point where he felt a wave of heat brushing past his face, especially from where it stood, acting as a sun’s replacement.

Now, how the hell was I supposed to fight this, the other being as hard as steel, and this one being hotter than a blast oven.

He was just fighting green slimes a short while ago; at least that’s how it seemed, he thought. But it could be well over weeks now, but that wasn’t the focus at this moment.

Luke stared at the slime, his eyes gaping, trying to discern anything that could be used to his advantage. The branches would be totally useless at this point, even if he had any left; just the heat of that slime alone would have set the whole branch up in flames.

Soul energy once again had to be used to fight this thing, literally being impossible without it or without him having his attributes higher. But maybe putting those points in the constitution a while ago had at least built up some resistance to what he might be facing right now.

Luke tried to look at the slime as the heat itself was affecting his vision by causing heat distortion around it. Barely discerning the small, crystal-shaped ball that hovered in the middle of the slime, maybe he had just found its core. But reaching it was a different problem; there was only so much he could do without waiting any longer.

The souls vanished from the sky as the 3 souls appeared in his soul space, forcing them once more to rotate at a considerable speed with the best of capabilities. Causing his body to shudder, with the reactivation of the soul energy seeping into his body.

The power felt like a drug as his cells screamed for more of it, but he resisted the temptations, being scared of the drawback it held. The energy gathered in his chest, he supplied a slow flow to his legs as he flexed them, sprinting his way towards the slime.

Creating a small sound burst as the feet kicked up dirt as he darted to the slime's side, ready to throw a haymaker straight towards its core, but not without the slime retaliating with a wall of flames appearing out of nowhere.

Shifting his body, he barely managed to dodge, only getting his forearm grazed by the blazing heat, backing away from the flame.

Most of his right forearm had become torched as the flame charred his skin, proving his constitution did almost nothing against those flames.

Now this was going to be harder than I thought.

He stood some distance away from the slime; he walked around it, looking for any way to breach its defenses. Without warning, the slime gathered a short burst of energy that shot towards Luke like a bullet, barely managing to duck under a flaming ball, getting most of his hair completely burnt off.

Tears almost welled up in his eyes as all the effort of growing his hair out had been completely thrown away; a scorched scalp was what remained.

Now I’m really jealous of people who can tolerate heat.

Knowing this thing could shoot out literal flames came as a surprise, being the first slime he had fought that could shoot out projectiles so quickly.

Now, this was a completely different fight than it should have been; not being able to get close really limited what he could do. Without much thought, Luke rushed towards it, but this time, his whole body was filled with soul energy. This put an immense strain not only on his soul but also on the mental strength it took to manage all this energy.

His body itched with excitement as an unbearable headache began, arriving right in front of the slime again, before it launched a flaming wall at him, but it wasn’t too tall for him being able to jump over. But what lay before his eyes beyond the wall was 4 hovering projectiles lying right next to the slime in mid-air.

They launched like missiles straight at his head, the mass of energy barely missing him as he advanced while dodging as it streamed past his face.

Charing his face without it even touching him, but he wasn’t just gonna let this end. Getting right up before the slime, he sent a punch empowered with the maximum amount of energy he could muster. Launching the slime across the air before it landed a considerable distance away.

But the fight wasn’t over as his fist missed the core; its body had warped around his fist; it was going to take more than this just to kill the slime.

His face scrunched up at the thought of this being a long fight; his body already started aching due to strain.

Not only that, his whole right fist had been charred even worse than he would have thought, barely able to clench his own fist as the outer skin felt just like crust, like a brick. He really hoped he wouldn’t lose his arm. At that point, he might as well be a dead man.

Luke made some more distance to get some control of his arm; he almost lost complete feeling in it. Shaking his arm out, Luke realized the slime hadn't conjured another set of projectiles while he was backing up.

Maybe I do have a chance after all, he thought.

The energy shifted inside his body as he gained control of the orbiting souls as best as he could before he advanced forward.
