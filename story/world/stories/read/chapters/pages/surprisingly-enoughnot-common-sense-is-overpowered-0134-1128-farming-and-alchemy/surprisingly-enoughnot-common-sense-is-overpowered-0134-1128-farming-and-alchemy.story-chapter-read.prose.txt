The lecture continued for another hour, with Lin Yue answering questions about troublesome spiritual herbs and sharing his experiences cultivating Second-Tier medicines.

Some of the discussions went beyond Ning's current understanding, but he still managed to pick up several useful points. Whenever Lin Yue addressed a problem he'd encountered himself, Ning paid close attention and compared the elder's methods with what he'd learned from Old Zhou and his own experiments.

Eventually, Lin Yue brought the gathering to a close.

“Well, I think that's enough for today. Thank you all for coming, and I hope some of what I've shared will prove useful in your own fields.”

The farmers rose and cupped their hands.

“Thank you for your guidance, Elder Lin Yue!”

“We've benefited greatly from your lecture!”

Lin Yue returned their greetings, only to find himself surrounded by farmers eager to ask more questions.

Ning watched the scene with amusement.

Well, that's one way to become popular. Master could never.

Sui Feng stretched beside him.

“Well, that was certainly worth attending.”

“Indeed.” Ning nodded. “I learned quite a bit.”

“Haha! I told you Elder Lin Yue's lectures were worth listening to.”

They joined Old Fang and the others, who were still enthusiastically discussing the demonstration.

“That Spring Returns to Ten Thousand Things really is something else,” one of the older farmers remarked. “If I possessed such a supernatural power, I'd probably spend every waking moment in my fields.”

Old Fang snorted. “You already spend every waking moment in your fields.”

“That's different! If I had that ability, I'd actually have something to show for it!”

The others burst into laughter, and Ning couldn't help smiling as well.

After chatting for a while, Old Fang suggested they pay their respects to Lin Yue before leaving. He still had some seeds to exchange, while several of the others wanted to visit the remaining stalls before the gathering ended.

They approached the platform, where Lin Yue greeted Old Fang warmly and accepted the group's thanks.

Ning also cupped his hands. “Thank you for your lecture, Elder Lin Yue.”

Despite the competition between Ling Xin and Lin Yue for cultivation resources, Ning had to admit that the man was the real deal. He could understand why Lin Yue was considered comparable to Ling Xin.

Seriously, all these people had hax.

“You're welcome,” Lin Yue replied with a pleasant smile.

Just as they were about to leave, the burly farmer from earlier suddenly stepped forward.

“Elder Lin Yue! There's something I've been meaning to ask!”

Ning recognized the man's enthusiastic expression and immediately felt a bad premonition.

“What if we planted spiritual grain according to the flow of spiritual energy?” the burly farmer continued. “In circles, spirals, or even the shape of a Spirit Gathering Formation! Could we turn an entire field into a functioning formation?”

Ning froze.

Oh no. Not this again.

Old Fang covered his face. “Didn't I tell you to grow a decent field first?”

“Hahaha! Of course! But I thought I'd ask Elder Lin Yue!”

The elder considered the question for a moment before chuckling.

“An interesting idea. Some farmers already arrange their crops according to the flow of spiritual energy, though maintaining an entire field as a formation would be difficult. Plants grow and change, after all. Still, it might be possible under the right conditions.”

“See! I knew it!” the burly farmer exclaimed triumphantly.

“Don't encourage him!” Old Fang retorted, drawing laughter from the surrounding farmers.

Ning, meanwhile, was seriously considering slipping away before the conversation went any further.

At least nobody had mentioned where the idea came from.

“Speaking of which!” The burly farmer suddenly turned toward him. “Junior Brother Ning, that Wandering Mind Pill of yours really is something! I've never had so many ideas in a single afternoon!”

You just had to say it, didn't you?

“Oh?” Lin Yue raised his eyebrows. “Wandering Mind Pill?”

“Hahaha! Yes! Junior Brother Ning refined it himself!”

Old Fang shook his head, while Sui Feng struggled to suppress his laughter.

Lin Yue looked at Ning with renewed interest.

“You're Ji Ning, aren't you?”

For a moment, Ning experienced a strange sense of déjà vu.

Seriously, what am I, Mr. Worldwide? Why does everyone suddenly know who I am?
