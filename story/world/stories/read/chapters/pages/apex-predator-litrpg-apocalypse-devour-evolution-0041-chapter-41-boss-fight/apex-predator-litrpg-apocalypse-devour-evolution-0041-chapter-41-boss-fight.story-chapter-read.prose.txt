Homass, King of Grimoids, stood up from his throne.

The wood groaned. The platform shook under the weight of the beast. Don had seen big enemies before. Hell, he'd fought the bears. But this thing had weight behind it. The long limbs were relaxed, but ready.

The king was truly a monster. Like a semi-truck crossed with a swamp ape. Even slouched, it scraped ten feet. Its belly sagged over a leather belt of bones, and its limbs were wrapped in bronze rings, each one bigger than Don’s head.

And then there was the club.

Seven feet long, knotted and splintered, and reinforced with iron bands. Homass picked it up with one hand and dragged it across the floor.

Don felt a tremble running up from his spine. This was going to be a true limit test. He could tell the king had stats to match his, and clearly a wealth of fighting experience.

The tremble enveloped his whole body. But it wasn’t fear, no. It was excitement. Don was eager to play.

“Let’s go, big boy.”

The king roared. Not a scream. Not a battle cry. Something deeper. Like a furnace exhaling. The club came up.

Don didn’t wait.

He pushed off the floor with all the speed Simian Physique gave him. A blur across the broken tiles. His claws came up, angled for the king’s side—

Homass reacted.

Fast. Too fast.

The club slammed down where Don had been a half-second earlier. Wood exploded. Don rolled, twisted, and came up at the king’s flank. He raked his claws across the thick hide. The cuts didn’t go deep, but blood welled instantly. The creature grunted, annoyed more than pained.

Good.

Don kept moving. He kept circling and darting in. He had to use his speed against the king’s size. If he let Homass plant his feet and get a solid swing in, Don was in trouble. He needed to keep the king moving at his chosen pace.

Don ducked another swing, leapt to the side and kicked off a broken pillar. Mid-air, he pulled a spear from the floor with his mind and launched it point-first.

It struck the king’s shoulder.

Homass staggered. Just a half-step. But that was all Don needed.

He closed in, claws flashing. He slashed twice, aimed high. Aimed for the neck.

The first strike drew blood. The second skidded off a metal collar Don hadn’t seen. Homass snarled and backhanded with the club. Don ducked and drove a knee into the creature’s side. It was like hitting a meat wall, but he felt ribs buckle under the blow.

Homass roared again. Then it stomped.

The floor beneath Don cracked.

Don stumbled, footing thrown. That was all the king needed.

The club swept in low. Don barely twisted away, but not fast enough.

[Health at 72%]

[Debuff inflicted: Fractured bones]

The blow caught him, but only the tip. Still, the only thing that managed to mitigate the damage was that Don had managed to block just in time. He flew into a column and rolled to the floor, gasping. He was already on his feet. No time to feel pain. No time to think. Move or die.

Now his arms were fractured. He needed to fix that immediately. He kept moving and with his Telekinesis, he took a healing potion and chugged it as he dodged another swing. But now his pace was off. The king was leading this dance.

And it was on him now.

Don ran. Not out of fear. Out of strategy.

He dropped low, rolled under a swing, and using telekinesis, pulled a brazier over behind him. Coals scattered across the floor. Homass barreled through them like they weren’t even hot.

Don flicked a blowpipe dart up with Telekinesis, snapped it into the king’s cheek. It embedded, shallow. Didn’t even make him blink.

Homass lunged.

Don dashed aside, slid under the swing, and slashed at the tendons in the king’s wrist. The claws bit deep this time. The club dropped with a crash as Homass bellowed and reeled back. The beast was bleeding now. Thick green blood poured across the floor.

Don didn’t let up. He charged again, claws flashing. A full combo. Torso, ribs, thigh. Every hit landed. Homass roared and swung with his bare fists. Don ducked, spun, and tried to go for the knees—

Too slow.

Homass pivoted and shoulder-checked him like a freight train.

[Health at 42%]

Don slammed into the wall and tasted blood.

He pushed up, vision swimming.

Okay. Okay. He still had this.

He raised both hands. Pulled three broken spearheads from his satchels with his mind. He sent them flying.

The king threw up an arm. One embedded in his forearm. Another in his chest. The last missed. It didn't even flinch. It charged.

Don managed to use his Telekinesis to pull the club into an off-angle, which slowed the king’s advance. Don dodged it to the side and sliced at the monster’s muscular calf.

Don surged forward before the king could reset his stance. He ducked under the wide swing of Homass’s club and rammed his claws deep into the beast’s side. The metal tips bit through hide, meat, and something rubbery underneath. Homass howled and twisted, but Don didn’t let go.

He twisted his whole body and yanked sideways, tearing a gouge open across the king’s flank. Blood sprayed in thick ropes. The king reeled, off-balance.

Don didn’t give him a second.

He darted low, slammed a foot into the inside of the creature’s knee. Homass dropped to one side with a snarl, forced down onto a knee.

Don spun around and launched a spearhead with his mind. It struck the king clean in the open mouth. A tooth flew loose. Homass gagged, roaring in rage and blood.

Don closed the gap and vaulted onto the king’s shoulder. He climbed like a wildcat, claws digging into muscle for handholds, and grabbed the brass ring around the creature’s neck.

He yanked it tight. Homass thrashed, but Don clung on, wrapped around the beast’s shoulders and sank his claws into its back. With his free hand he started slashing into the side of the king’s face. One swipe opened the cheek to the bone. Another tore through an ear.

The king tried to shake him off, but Don gripped tighter, driving his claws into the creature’s throat, sawing into tendon.

Then Homass stood with a roar and leapt backward.

Don realized what was about to happen a split second too late.

The king slammed his entire body backwards into a support pillar, crushing Don between meat and wood.

[Health at 18%]

The breath left Don’s lungs in a single shocked exhale. His spine cracked against the wood. His arms fell limp. He slid off the king’s back and hit the ground on his knees, seeing double.

His body screamed in protest.

His legs barely worked.

He tasted copper.

Something was seriously broken. There was so much pain. With his bleary vision he saw the king reading a coup de grace.

Resolve and defiance flared up in Don’s mind, clarifying it. In his disorientation, he gathered energy with his psychic sense and flared it outwards in a concentrated wave.

The tables near Don exploded. The king was thrown on its back, the club scattering several yards behind it.

Don coughed up blood. That had spent most of his Energy. Cool trick though. If he lived through this, he would practice it.

However, Homass was no small fry. The wave had only surprised it and thrown it back. No real damage was done.

Same couldn’t be said for Don. He wasn’t even on his last legs. He couldn’t feel his legs. All he could feel was pain.

Homass walked over to him and grabbed Don by the neck. The monster squeezed, and Don felt like his eyes would explode out of their sockets. His and throat were on fire when he tried to struggle a breath in. Don felt a faint crunch as his windpipe was crushed.

[Health at 3%]

[Debuffs: Severe internal damage]

Caution: Lethal damage received!

[Estimated time until incapacitation: 7 seconds]

But Don didn’t feel the light dimming. He felt something else.

A massive surge of strength and energy. It filled his limbs like a lightbulb.

Berserk mode activated:

When dropped below 10% Health, go into berserk mode, increasing all stats by 35%. Resistance to damage increased while in berserk mode by 60% After berserk mode expires, all stats reduced by 90% for a duration

Don drew in a breath. It was the sweetest thing he ever felt. The king’s brows furrowed and it squeezed in harder, a brutal grin on its face.

Don wouldn’t let it. He flicked out a claw and cut into the hand. It felt like air.

There was a spray of blood and Don dropped on the floor, along with Homass’ right hand.

Don spat out blood and lunged while the king bellowed.
