Funny how with all my intellectual superiority complex from back in the day, I never really twigged to how fuckin' forgetful I was.

I think it's because in general, my brain remembers things when I'm presented with some kind of reminder. It doesn't have to be a big thing, either. Like, walking into class used to be enough to remind me not only that we'd had homework the night before, but everything we'd studied that I intended to use to bullshit my way through the assignment. Once in a while that even saved my ass, since I'd be able to spit all kinds of facts at the teacher, talk about everything I'd 'written' as I claimed some stupid thing like 'the printer in the library didn't work' or 'my internet went out so I couldn't email it to you'.

I mean, I'd forget again the moment I walked out of class, until something brought it back to mind, but that's just, I dunno, presentation.

Not sure how I feel about the fact that here and now I don't think I ever remember having 'homework'. Independent study time, yeah, and assignments in some classes, but nothing like 'homework'. Then again, with classes stretching from dawn to dusk except a couple hours for lunch, there wasn't much 'home' to do work in. If I imagine memory like a warehouse full of information, mine is plenty big, and has no problem retaining things and keeping them in reasonably good condition. It's the organizational system that's not only shitty, but seems to actively fight against any attempt to make it less so. I dunno if that's due to my trauma, or something inherent to me, or, fuck, something that happened because my mom got lit when I was still bean sized and she didn't know I was there.
