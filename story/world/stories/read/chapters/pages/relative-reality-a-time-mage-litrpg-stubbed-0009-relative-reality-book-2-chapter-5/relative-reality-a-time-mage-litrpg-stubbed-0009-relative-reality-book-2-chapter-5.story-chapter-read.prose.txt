Dawn sat in the viewing room, an old friend beside him. The illusion in front of them was showing Helja and her team as they sprinted through an emergence.

“An interesting tactic,” Dawn said.

Dawn’s friend leaned back, hands interlocked.

“It could work against them, if they haven’t prepared for the entities to… ahh, and they have. Very interesting indeed.”

“What do you think of her? She is powerful for her tier, is she not?”

“Time will tell, dear friend, as with all things. That fire, it’s a result of your old companion if I’m not mistaken.”

Dawn nodded.

“It is.”

“And does it share the same properties?”

“I believe so.”

“Then I want her team to be one of those that we attempt to employ for our current problem.”

“Is that wise?”

They watched as a fiery tornado that had more substance than it should tore apart a number of creatures, scorching their remains as they flew through the air.

“I believe they will do just fine.”

“Very well. We should keep an eye on Rowan as well, he is the one I was telling you about. A Displaced Soul who somehow managed to create not one but two astral breaches at Tier two. Not normal ones either. He also managed to close one of them before it created an emergence.”

Dawn’s friend cocked an eyebrow, essentially an exclamation of surprise coming from him.

“Interesting indeed. Was it your doing to associate him with your successor?”

“Not at all, some other hand than mine guided them together. Enough about them though, let us look at the next.”

Dawn waved his hand and the illusion changed to another team. This one was led by Hendrik and focused more on defense followed by bursts of extreme violence.

“We should also attempt to recruit this team. They have an interesting tactic that I think will work well against our enemy…”

___

Rowan punched the creature in front of him as hard as he could, flesh and bones giving way beneath his fist. The monkey-snake hybrid dropped dead, the last in this room.

They had been fighting nearly nonstop for the past hour, or what felt like an hour for Rowan. He didn’t exactly follow the normal time stream like other people did. They were definitely approaching the end of the emergence now, with the enemies getting harder and harder to kill by the room.

Melody touched a hand to her two Afterimages, releasing yet another burst wave of health, stamina and mana recovery. Taking only a second to breathe in and out, Arne led the charge into the next room.

Helja had to immediately drop out of the sky as birds began to dive bomb them from the treetops, savage claws extended to grab onto and tear apart their team. At a word, they gathered together and Helja used her innate ability Air Magic to create a dome of swirling wind and fire around them, then froze it solid with Corporeal Air.

Corporeal Air - Body, Soul - Passive - Stone of Power

Tier 1 Effect:

Your air based effects can impart significantly more physical force.

Tier 2 Effect:

Your air based effects can be created with sharper dimensions.

Tier 3 Effect:

You may solidify your air based effects, freezing them in place.

Birds began to slam into the dome, each with a sickening crunch as their claws or legs snapped. Many broke straight through leaving a fiery streak behind as they descended upon the team. Arne was first to act, controlling his roots so that they rose up to swat at the birds, while Rowan patiently waited for a chance to do anything. He was starting to feel frustrated at his inability to reach Tier three before the tournament, having gotten tantalisingly close.

Soul facet - Tier two - 84%

Mind facet - Tier two - 81%

Body facet - Tier two - 92%

With another week or two of training time, he was sure he could have gotten there. Still, Helja carried the team in this fight as blades and blasts of air tore through the bare few fragile birds that broke through the dome. With Arne dealing with the rest and Melody playing her songs, Rowan had nothing to do other than stand by Elise, who was similarly unnecessary in this fight.

The room was cleared soon enough, and they pushed on to the next.

Room after room passed, until they arrived at the final one at last. Inside stood the anchor entity, a horrific creature made of bones, wood and general forest debris. It was a shapeless blob, with flailing limbs.

Arne went in first, of course, already attempting to grab and hold down some of the limbs with his roots. Helja rose up and began cutting into the creature with sharp blades of air and fire, while Elise vanished to do her thing.

Feeling somewhat at a loss again, Rowan waited beside Melody as she began to create her Afterimages, knowing that more than anything else, they would be most important to the fight. The mass let out a screech, the sound tearing through Rowan’s mind with a sharp pain, driving him to the ground. Elise, Arne and Melody were all unaffected by the attack, but Helja also dropped from the air and fell to her knees in pain.

Roots were torn as the creature's limbs broke free, and hit Arne hard enough to push him back a step. This fight would be a waiting game, as Song of Power and Elise’s various curses and triggered abilities grew stronger. As such, Rowan positioned himself near Arne and began to draw the attention of the blob, redirecting the attacks or simply letting them phase through his Reality Guard. Thanks to Arne’s Root-Bound Blessing, Rowan’s armguards now slowly repaired over time.

Root-Bound Blessing - Mind - Blessing - Stone of Gifting

Tier 1 Effect:

Gift an ally with enhanced toughness that lasts for eight hours.Tier 2 Effect:

The enhanced toughness applies to the ally’s summons and summoned items.

Tier 3 Effect:

This blessing slowly repairs damaged summons and summoned items.

With their enhanced durability and repair, Rowan was able to alternate between dodging attacks, blocking with his armour, shifting them aside with Twisting Mist, and simply letting them pass through him. Fracture remained absorbed for now, empowering Rowan’s abilities and sharing his senses. Through this he saw another screech coming, and signalled to Helja before backing off.

Pain ripped through his mind once more, debilitating but not painful enough to injure.

The time finally arrived for Elise to appear from the shadows, sending a coursing curse through the blob with two lightning quick strikes. She delivered Debilitating Strike with the attacks as well, causing the previously coordinated limbs of the creature into a spasm. Seeing his time come, Arne activated Fiery Form once more and began laying into the creature with powerful strikes of his axe, cleaving it into pieces. Helja followed up with her own blades, leveraging Arne’s deep cuts to slice into the innards of the creature. The twitching mass tried to pull itself together, lengths of leaf litter and bone grabbing onto separate pieces.

Arne used his flaming roots as wedges, levering the mass apart while Helja used Air Blast empowered by The Dawn Eagle to coat swathes of it with fire. The twitching eventually stopped, and with it the illusion of the emergence slowly faded away.

Coming back to reality, Rowan unstrapped himself from the alcove. Across from them, Anya and her team were already out of the alcoves but with a sullen look on their faces.

Curious, Rowan walked over to them.

“You were out first? Why aren’t you happy?”

Anya let out a deep sigh.

“We beat the anchor entity, but I died a few seconds before we managed it. We’ll receive a penalty now I guess, whatever that means, and you guys woke up less than a minute after we did. You’ll probably get first now.”

“Sorry to hear it, I’m a little surprised nobody else has finished yet actually.”

“Well, let’s go watch them shall we?”

Rowan gestured for her to lead the way, and their two teams went back into the viewing room. Only Lylc was in there, watching Felir and his team take on their anchor entity.

Watching the fight play out, Rowan had to admit to himself again that he was impressed by the progress Felir had made as an adventurer. Since Dawn had been cracking down on the nobility of Lyel, it appeared that Felir was significantly less sure of his place in the world and working quite hard to make one on his own.

The anchor entity went down without much of a fight, and the illusion for that team vanished. Last of the two teams were Hendrik and Norok’s teams. Hendrik wasn’t even at the anchor entity yet, while Norok and his team were struggling to take theirs down. They eventually killed it, one of Norok’s team members receiving a severe injury that their team’s healer took care of. With that, the test was done as Hendrik’s team, coming last, were brought out of the Illusorum.

If indeed Anya was set to second place by penalty, that would mean that they would need to fight Hendrik’s team in the next round. The outcome of that fight would be disastrous for Anya, and the expression on her face told Rowan that she knew it. If that were the case, Norok and Felir’s teams would also be fighting, while Rowan and his team would get to rest and watch the next round out.

With everyone assembled, they learned that it would indeed be the case.

“First to finish, with a ten minute penalty for losing a team member, Anya’s team are second. Second to finish but moving up to first place is Helja’s team, with Felir, Norok and Hendrik’s teams placing third, fourth and fifth in that order. For our next round of one versus one matchups, Anya and Hendrik’s teams will be competing against each other, and Felir and Norok’s teams against each other,” Instructor Sky announced, “While Helja and her team will be resting in preparation for our fourth and final round, a three team free for all.”

Rowan grinned at Helja.

“See? Arne was absolutely right that we could win!”

Helja allowed herself one of her now rare brilliant smiles.

“Yes Rowan, he was right. Let us just focus on how we are going to win our final match, alright?”

“Let’s just take the win,” Rowan muttered under his breath, fully aware Helja would hear it just fine.

Still, he sat back in his seat as the other four teams filed out of the viewing room. With the two matchups, he favoured Hendrik and Felir’s teams winning. Anya and her blitz style tactics would just not work against Hendrik very well. In fact, it was exactly the type of strategy that Hendrik would excel against, which made Rowan wonder if they had come last on purpose for that very reason.

With the other two, their teams were similarly built but Norok was simply outclassed. Felir himself was somewhat skilled, but his team was without a doubt some of the most skilled adventurers in the course. Rowan wasn’t even sure that his own team would win that fight.

The two separate battles began, and events spiralled quickly.

Anya did as her team always did, went on a full out offensive. Hendrik’s team was quickly beaten into a shell where they suffered the onslaught. Meanwhile, the other fight started off on a more even footing. Matching blow to blow, Norok’s team held their own for a short while before making a mistake. One mistake cascaded into two, and before anybody knew what was happening, the team was levered apart and taken down one by one. It was such a quick defeat that by the time Norok’s entire team was out, the other fight hadn’t even really begun.

Rowan could see Hendrik and his team holding firm within their shell, the desperation on Anya’s face as the outcome of their battle sped along the exact path that she had seen happen to the previous team to fight Hendrik. Nothing they did could get through, and Rowan found himself wishing that it had been his own team in that arena fighting Hendrik. They had a plan to deal with the defensive strategy, one that very well might work in a one versus one fight. In a three team free for all, he wasn’t so sure about it. Felir might just wait for them to pull apart Hendrik only to jump in and finish the fight when they were at their weakest.

Still, there was nothing to be done. Hendrik finally unleashed the power gained over the course of their hardened defence against Anya, and his team surged forward. Much like before, what appeared to be a one sided fight turned on its heel and Anya’s team was sent into full retreat. One member died, then another. Without a healer, they fell within the minute.

Rowan’s prediction came true, with Hendrik and Felir coming out on top of their fights in relatively short order. The final players were set, and the battle to be the winner of this tournament would soon take place. Rowan only hoped that they could beat the others and come out on top, that their hard training regimen would pay off.
