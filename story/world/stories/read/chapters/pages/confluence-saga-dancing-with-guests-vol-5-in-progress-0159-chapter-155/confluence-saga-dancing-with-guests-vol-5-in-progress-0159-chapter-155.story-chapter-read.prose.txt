Depths maximums per pond.

Popular opinion is that the Depths are bottomless. That once you start down a path you can continue as long as you can keep advancing. This may be true for some paths but most are limited by the Pond they are connected to.

Some are limited by the Wall, constrained to never provide progression beyond that point, even should the Awakened be capable. Of course travel to another Pond, and from there to another path, solves the issue, but only when such travel is possible.

The limits always seem to fall at a multiple of ten. The density spikes surpassing the capabilities of the Pond and preventing further descent.

The true challenge is to find any path that continues past the 50th level. They exist, there is clear evidence of this, but no verifiable records have ever been located.

Of course verifiable records of Awakened who have reached that level are also near non-existent.

Fay sighed as she stepped through the portal. This was the first time she'd actually wanted to interact at least slightly with the Doorkeeper, to see what she could discover from its responses. However the fact that both Briana and Sandra were with her made that impossible.

If anything went wrong and Briana was affected she'd never forgive herself. If anything happened to Sandra… she wasn't her sister, not the way Anya was, but she was indirectly. Of course a slight scare might help make her stay calmer and not be rushing off to-

"Sandra! We're supposed to wait here."

"It's a field, a space to wander while you wait. You said they were waiting for you last time, that they were here when you arrived. They're not here so we should find them."

"You agreed you would listen," Fay said. "You told your mother you would behave and do as were told."

"Fine," Sandra huffed before coming back over to join them. "But where are they?"

"They'll be here," Fay answered as she took a proper look around.

During her last family visit she'd been too distracted by the encounter with the Doorkeeper to pay attention to the surroundings. They weren't the only group waiting, the distance between each just enough to give the illusion of privacy, anyone yelling, anyone acting as extravagantly as Sandra, would be obvious, while normal conversations would be inaudible.

"Fay!"

She smiled at the sound of Anya's voice, her eyes catching sight of Anya, Seb, Gwen and Cara all approaching. She hadn't expected the Fae to be showing up to greet them, especially not walking so closely together. She thought she'd seen a couple Fae a few groups over, why weren't they going to see what must be Gwen's parents?

"Sorry we weren't here waiting," Anya said as they got closer. "I'll explain later."

"Anya!" Sandra said before rushing over. "Mother wasn't going to let me visit. Said I hadn't given her enough warning even though I told her months ago. Then that I wouldn't have any guards because they wouldn't be able to come along, even though the guards would be lower level than her so they wouldn't have been any protection. When are you going to authorize more work on the Holding, I've finished sorting Yshe's workshop and she's having me work on sorting the one next to it, apparently Sam and I will need to share it but he doesn't have to go through it, he has other things he's working on. And-"

"Sandra," Anya said with a laugh. "There's three days. You don't have to say everything before even saying hello."

"Hello," Sandra said before continuing. "Hello to your friends too. Do they like coffee and bacon? Fay said they had good coffee but that the coffee in your room wasn't nearly as good, you're not going to make me drink the bad coffee are you?"

"Sandra," Briana said. "I said I would look at their coffee machine if they let me and see what can be done."

"This is your sister?" Gwen asked.

"Yes," Anya said. "Sandra is my sister. Briana is Fay's girlfriend. And this is Gwen and Cara."

"Her eyes are like yours, not the color but the effect," Briana said.

"They are!" Sandra said. "Does that mean she-"

"We think we're cousins," Anya said.

Fay said nothing, keeping her eyes on the pair of fae, especially on Cara. She still remembered how reactive the woman had been until it had been made clear what the relationship was. This time however she seemed calm, content to stay a half step back and let everyone say hello. The only indication that she wasn't completely at ease was the way she kept looking towards the Fae couple, now a trio.

"You don't have family visiting?" Sandra asked.

"We do," Gwen said after only the slightest hesitation. "Lord Percivus needs to talk to them before we say hello though."

"Bern is waiting too," Cara muttered.

"You can go wait too," Seb said before holding up a hand. "I know, we agreed to go with you."

"What are we doing for sleeping arrangements this time?" Fay asked. "You only had the one spare room so I suppose Bri and I will take the quarters so Sandra can't get into trouble on her own?"

"Hey!" Sandra said before exhaling. "But I don't want to stay somewhere alone."

"Maybe you all stay in the quarters? Then no one has to stay alone?" Anya said.

Fay started to answer when she felt Seb reaching out to form a band. She accepted, enjoying the reconnection with the two before forcing herself to stay calm at their words.

Gwen and Cara are using the spare room. Bern, her brother, forced issues. They are our Guests.

Fay let them know she understood even as the band dissolved. She was aware enough of what being a Guest and a Host meant to the fae that she hadn't asked any further questions, not even if they knew what they were saying. She could tell they did.

The grouping as they had approached made more sense now. Gwen and Cara were being protected, not overtly, but were part of their group, staying close and deferring to the situation.

"That works," she answered. "As long as you don't mind us arriving at your dorm early so that we can see about improving the coffee before breakfast. Unless you have training?"

"It was made clear to all of us that we should enjoy the time with our families," Seb said.

"He's approaching," Cara said, her hair flickering oddly despite no change in lighting.

"Gwen," the third of the trio she had seen said as he came into earshot. "I have let them know my view and stance on the situation. They wish to speak with both of you, as well as Morgan."

"At the same time?" Gwen asked.

"That is your choice. Bern was already approaching as I stepped away."

"Anya, Seb," Gwen said. "Will you join us?"

"I was going to show them where the guest quarters were," Seb said. "After?"

"I am willing to escort them," the Fae said.

"Thank you Lord Percivus," Seb said. "These are Sandra, Anya's sister. Fay, our found sister and her girlfriend Briana."

"Greetings. I am Lord Percivus," he said before gesturing across the field. "If you would?"

Fay hesitated, looking between Anya and Seb until they both nodded. She hadn't told them what she'd found out about their teacher. She hadn't explained why the Fae were such a secret, why they were a complete unknown for most of Awakened Humanity. The peace between them had been fragile more than once, had been agreed to due to both sides seeing the necessity of it. This man was part of the necessity, and yet here he stood, willing to escort them the way any other teacher might.

She trusted them, she trusted the promises she knew bound Seb's mentor. That didn't make her feel nearly as safe as she felt it should. On the other hand his history meant he knew things, had experienced things, was aware of things she had no way of exploring.

"Of course," she said. "It would be an honor to be escorted by the Silver Bladesinger."

She saw Gwen's mouth open slightly, Cara's stance shift at the words. She knew the words were a gamble, but they were not explicitly forbidden.

"Your sister's words have bite," Percivus said with a chuckle. "A bite that cannot be discussed openly, unless certain secrets are already shared?"

"They are not," she answered, already wondering what it would take to apologize to Bri when she had to tell her she couldn't explain. "Considering the family ties they may need to be, at least with those present and perhaps Anya's mother."

"I shall consider your words. I believe there is precedent."

With that Lord Percivus set off, clearly expecting them to follow him, to be shown the way to their quarters.
