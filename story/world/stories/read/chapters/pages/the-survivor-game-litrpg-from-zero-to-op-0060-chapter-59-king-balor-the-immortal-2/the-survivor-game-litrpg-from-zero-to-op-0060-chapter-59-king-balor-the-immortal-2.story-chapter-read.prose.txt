Roaring more in anger than from physical torture, he found himself dragged deeper into that maelstrom of fiery torment. Every inch of his skin was a burning torch, his very blood boiling under that wave of primordial fire.

Until a detonation of dazzling energy ripped through the fire vortex from the inside, releasing a shockwave so powerful that it even dispersed the eternal flames in their infernal dance!

The crimson sphere pulsed dangerously like a mad heartbeat, expanding with each swirling cycle of particles until it reached terrifying dimensions. The heat it emitted was so intense that even Balor began to sweat magma from the pores of his armor.

"BEHOLD IN TERROR THE OVERDRIVE!" he bellowed, his voice hollowed by agony but etched with absolute determination. "OBLIVION!"

Marshmallow maintained the discharge with a groan of inhuman effort, his body reduced to little more than a blazing furnace as the Overdrive Oblivion sucked away the last remnants of his strength. Yet his iron will burn more intensely than the explosion, a fire that is impossible to extinguish.

Yet his eyes glowed with one last spark of triumph, fixed on the smoking crater where Balor once reigned.

With a guttural groan, Marshmallow fell to his knees, life ebbing from him like the last echo of that titanic explosion. But a fierce grin continued to ripple across his disfigured lips, marred by burns and wounds.

"I told you..." he managed to mutter before darkness swallowed him. "You were just... an insect..."

Then he collapsed to the ground, exhausted yet victorious, the smoking crater symbolizing his overwhelming supremacy even in the abyss of oblivion. King Balor the Immortal was no more... incinerated by the very fury of the Overdrive.

You therefore have a choice, mortal: you can return to the veiled woman to deliver the Amulet as proof of your endeavor... or you can cross the ultimate portal, and challenge the flames that dissolve everything."
