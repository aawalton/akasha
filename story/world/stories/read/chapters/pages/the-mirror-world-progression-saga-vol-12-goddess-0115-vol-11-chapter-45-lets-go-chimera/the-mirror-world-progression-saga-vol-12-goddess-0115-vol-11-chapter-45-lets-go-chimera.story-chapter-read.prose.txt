Four enormous bear-traps on chains and anchored posts appeared around the chimera, the constructs animated and chomping down on it immediately, as its multiple heads screamed in offense. Though the pressure was intense, the blades only barely dug in and drew blood. It was enough to hold, however. The beast attempted to keep going but was arrested by the chain after one huge jerk that threatened multiple posts and fully dragged the further two sideways through the ground.

As if this wasn’t enough, a great pillar of blue energy slammed down, squashing the chimera into the ground with devastating force, once more arresting its momentum and causing at least a few crunchy sounds. Despite this, it definitely wasn’t incapacitated. Its lion head roared.

Briefly checking on Othenia, Ashawein caught sight of her, no worse for wear, hiding under the cover of trees and watching.

How noble of her!

Just as the great hammer-pillar rose back up, Ashawein unleashed a torrent of magical destruction on the beast. The chimera’s powerful aura seemed to activate and flare up, absorbing the magic.

“Stick with physical effects!” Othenia called. “It may seem counterintuitive, but nothing else is going to work!”

“Could’ve mentioned it before,” Ashawein muttered under her breath, but called back, “Gotcha, thanks!”

Actually, I don’t think Ashawein is strong at battle magic anyway.

Considering the matter and delving into ‘who she was as Ashawein,’ Samantha realized she should probably transform back to giant form. Ashawein preferred beating things to hell with a size-changing studded warclub of sorts, which Samantha could use similarly to her staff-and-scepter. Wound negations did exist on this world, which seemed to be very ‘late’ in the mix — the warclub stripped them, dealing a lesser wound on a gradient depending on how many it had to remove.

As Ashawein changed back and summoned her warclub, the chimera tried to surge forward again before the hammer came down. Simultaneously, its two secondary heads did things: the dragon-cobra opened its mouth and sprayed out a glittering cloud that surrounded the immediate area around the massive body of the beast.

It attempted to both dismiss and dispel all persistent effects. Instead of that happening, the circle absorbed the ‘damage,’ bursting in a flash of light and dissipating. The spells remained.

The vulture head, meanwhile, over-extended itself from the trapped state of the rest of the body. On a stretching neck, it came for Ashawein at great speed and snapped at her with its nasty-looking beak.

Ashawein, still disoriented, narrowly parried this with the club, knocking the beak away with her prodigious strength and stumbling to the side. Samantha realized she’d done something without thinking about it, because it was pulled from her predecessor’s abilities: she’d ‘sacrificed’ ensuing active action Effective Levels, to add them to her defense.

Hmm. Potentially useful. It’s only the first strike, after all, with ensuing attacks unpenalized.

She moved in close for melee, now in her toughened giant form and brandishing her club. She went with a basic feint, knowing she was under penalties, and relying on the difference in ‘guile’ levels between herself and the beast, to hopefully make it flinch. She swung her club in a wide, destructive arc, telegraphed and potentially nasty, though she would pull it and pivot at the end.

The chimera did flinch, jerking itself sideways, though still forward enough to keep worrying the posts anchoring it to the ground, digging them up that much more. It was also setting itself up for a counterattack, it seemed.

Fortunately, the great pillar of force came down again, smashing it into the ground and putting a temporary damper on its plans.

Ashawein had only so many targets to strike while it was pinned, as the pillar blocked access to the snake head. It was either the lion head, the head or neck of the vulture head, or the front legs, primarily. If she had a cutting weapon, she’d probably go for the exposed neck.

As it was, she went for a crippling strike on its trapped, pinned leg, determining the lion head would take more than one strike to take out. Ashawein wasn’t the Goddess of Fate yet, so guaranteeing a kill shot or knockout wasn’t feasible.

Pivoting from her swing, she transferred it into a thrust, and on instinct, made the club grow and extend, adding reach and power before impact.

The precision attack could not be avoided and hit the primary joint dead on with a force that could shatter boulders. There was a crunching sound as it was shattered and totally separated the bone, making the flesh of the leg basically floppy and useless in the middle. Moreover, the beast let out a hideous moan of pain.

When the pillar came up again, the chimera’s lion head seemed to quickly go into a frenzy, its eyes reddened and bloodshot. It jerked harder than ever, growling and drooling from its mouth. Two posts were ripped out of the ground, and the chains were dragged forward as the beast was half free of its binds. Worse, it suddenly had plenty of ‘give’ with which to strike at Ashawein.

It tried to bite her, and was in a good position to do so, but its missing leverage with the broken leg hindered it, and Ashawein narrowly dodged the fangs.

She could not evade the taloned paw that came after, as the chimera sacrificed its face in the dirt to get at her. The paw immediately slammed into her and carried through, the claw raking her even as she was sent tumbling into the dirt.

Owie. Fuck me, I’m not as fast in this life! I’ve been debuffed here! I need to remember that.

Nonetheless, the force of that strike would’ve sent Samantha through trees just as the gnoll form had been blasted, yet as Ashawein, she just took a tumble and rolled right back onto her feet, her sheer mass, strength, and force absorbing and stopping much of the effect. Ablative defenses made the wounds negligible, though she did have superficial cuts in her adamant skin.

So quick and powerful was her recovery that when she spun around, she was able to once again trade her offense to parry the snapping beak of the vulture, knocking it into the dirt this time, which made it screech in frustration.

Her eyes flashed to the dragon-cobra head, worried about a third attack, but the head had turned upward, and it spat a gout of green venom at the force pillar. It veritably exploded on contact, turning to a strange green-blue steam that blanketed the forest and stung the skin and eyes. In any case, the force pillar was now gone.

At least it's hindered, both by the ruined leg and the bear traps. But this may be my best opportunity to unleash on it!

While the chimera was still face-planted, Ashawein moved in to punish it, aiming to unleash multiple strikes on the exposed and vulnerable lion head. She sold out totally this time, fully intent on abandoning defense; she pushed for speed, for fury, for cramming in as many strikes as possible. Some ability triggered, aiding her effort.

The club slammed directly on the top of the skull for the first hit, the chimera with no recourse to stop it. The crack was nasty and rebounded her weapon upward. She used the weapon’s resizing effect to pivot into a quick thrust right into its squeezed shut eye, which caused a great burst of gore before she felt the butt end stopped by bone. As the beast writhed and screamed, once more, she resized the weapon to pull it out quicker, and thrust it again into its other eye, this time with waning momentum, but still dealing a grievous wound.

She did not let up, when the chimera remained slow to respond, stunned and in agony. Ashawein spun the club around and went for what might be a finishing blow to the jaw…

Just as the end of the club was nearing impact, the vulture head came snapping from behind, closing right over her body. Her carry-through still clipped the lion’s jaw and jerked both of their bodies just enough to spoil the vulture’s chomp, which ended up enclosing just at the tip end.

Nonetheless, the beak’s pressure was terrible upon impact, stealing Ashawein’s breath and making her see stars. She felt a gut-wrenching feeling as she was taken off her feet and going for a spin in the air, apparently in a chaotic whirl.

Just barely, she got her bearings enough to see the ground coming up — specifically an unfortunate rocky section — and Ashawein, with a fierce cry, ripped her muscles as hard as she could in a twist, to spoil what was an attempted slam by the vulture's head and leveraging neck.

The vulture's head was twisted slightly just before impact, and they both hit the stone hard, shattering it.
