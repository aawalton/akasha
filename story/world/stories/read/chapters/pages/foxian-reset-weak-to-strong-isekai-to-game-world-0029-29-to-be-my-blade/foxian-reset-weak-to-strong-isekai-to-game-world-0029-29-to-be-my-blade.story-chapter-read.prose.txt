The two journeyed back to Restoria, the city empty and void of players. The silence of the city, the darkness of the streets, and calming lights burning in the darkness gave off a slight ambient vibe. Kae had never seen it void of players, so this added a unique charm to the castle city she'd spent so long in.

"Tuffy!"

Kae called the little guy out. He bounced out of a void of smoke and into her arms. Calmly, she found a nice place in a small park nearby. There, the fox sat under a tree and held the fluffy little guy in her arms. From a distance, Tuffy looked to be a doll for cuddling with.

"Take a seat, Palona."

Kae urged her, patting the grass next to her. But the Knight crossed her arms and lowered her gaze.

"Aren't we going to look for Rexia, Loredom?"

After a moment of contemplation, Kae's eyes softened.

"No."

She looked at the night streets and the collection of beautiful stars above her. Everything was so peaceful that she didn't dare break the serene atmosphere.

"I think we should at least relax and enjoy the life we have for a moment. I mean, we just survived a death match with a high-level Fallen Angel. We shouldn't be alive, Palona."

She rubbed Tuffy's tail and put her chin on his soft head. It felt like a pillow, perfect to sleep on.

"We should cherish the calm moments a bit, don't you think?"

Palona shook her head in defiance. With everything calm, finding her sister would be the best course of action for them. The last thing she wanted was more chaos to spread as her sister was still hidden away somewhere in this vast world...

But the Foxian looked exhausted to her, even though they didn't need to eat or sleep. Just seeing Kae's eyes fall slightly reminded her of when she put all her energy into fighting off Perry.

"Do you need to sleep, Kae?"

The fox shook her head, slowly brushing her chin on her Tuffy toy.

"No, but I wish I could dream again. So, instead, I'm just going to imagine that I'm asleep, if only for a little bit while the servers are down."

The fox fell over and curled herself up to her Tuffy. She wrapped her tail around her body, isolating herself as if to find a bit of peace after the madness she endured. Palona didn't find this productive at all. Honestly, she was a little annoyed that the one person who claimed to have dealt with Rexia was lazing around.

"I'm going to look for my sister. I can't stand by after seeing the people of Chalaco suffer like that. You can stay here and... try to dream."

"I will. Because if I keep pushing myself... I'll burn myself out. Then I'll begin to hate this world again."

"Hm?"

"I don't want to hate Keostar anymore, Palona. I want to understand it better and become a part of it. I... don't want to get into the cycle that I fell into for ten years."

Palona leaned up against the tree Kae was under. Looking down at the Foxian in this position, Kae reminded her of a cat, curled up in a little ball on the grass. In a way, it was worlds different than the combat-ready strategist she witnessed hours ago.

"What do you mean by that, Kae? That you don't want to hate Keostar anymore?"

The fox opened one eye.

"I've played this game every day for ten years, Palona. When I woke up, I was logged in. The only time I stopped playing was when it shut down like this. Anyone who does something like that will see all the things they hate about the world they forced themselves into."

Kae turned over and laid on her back, looking up at the stars. She held Tuffy tight, as if he were going to float away if she let him go.

"In the Chalaco desert, I was able to feel the sand and heat. I saw the anguish of the people around me... because I wasn't just an observer anymore."

The soft-spoken girl looked past the Knight to the heavens above. Her eyes were full of the wonderful stars above.

"As we traveled back to Restoria, I asked myself something..."

Kae tilted her head back to Palona, who had been watching her silently.

"What if I stopped feeling this way about Keostar? What if I start treating this wonderfully crafted world as nothing but a game to optimize again?"

Palona let the thought linger in the air as she listened intently to the veteran fox. This was a rare side to Kae, one she only saw briefly in the Thieves' Guild.

"That answer was simple; Enjoy the quiet moments that this world offers. Let the grass touch my face as I lie in the grass. Live in the world I want to love again."

She kicked her feet like a child excited about a new toy, then calmed down and closed her eyes. Once more, Kae curled into a ball on the grass like a tuckered-out kitten, holding her Tuffy tight.

"So, you can find Rexia, Palona. I'll speak to her whenever she decides to search me out to mess with me. But after fighting Raha, I want to enjoy the world I fought hard to be a part of... for just a little longer."

Despite Kae's outright rejection, Palona couldn't help but smile softly at her true words. It was like a little gear within her was wound up, turning off her stoic, icy gaze as she thought about the silliness of it all.

"You really are strange."

However, this world was worth fighting for, and every moment she wasn't doing that... Palona couldn't thank it for all the love it gave her in the past...

"We love this world differently, don't we, Kae Loredom?"

Palona whispered under her breath.

"Hm?"

"No, it's nothing. I'll be back later. Please, enjoy your rest, Kae Loredom."

With that, the Knight walked off into the void of the empty city streets of Restoria. Her boots clanked, echoing down the cobblestone. Kae's words lingered in her mind, and she understood them to some level.

Walking around aimlessly would be pointless, though. So, when she got to a spot in the city, far away from Kae, she decided to try something else. Now that she was alone by the grand fountain at the center of town, likely the only person within this vast world aside from the insane fox...

She stopped herself, lifted her head to the sky, and sang a song that echoed in her chest for all the years she'd known her beloved sister. A hymn that Rexia would play to soothe the minds of her beloved people on her golden harp. The loneliness in Palona's Godlike voice resonated in the empty world...

And before she realized it...

"You called?"

The Goddess that she'd been searching for appeared before her. In the vast, empty streets, they stood alone. Two seeming Goddesses connected in a world of mortals.

"It's fascinating that you, of all people, know that holy song. But you never forgot it when I first played it for you, all those years ago. Did you, Palona?"

Palona awed at Rexia like she was a fragile gem. One that if she touched, she'd shatter and disappear forever. But the Goddess put both hands behind her back and walked around the fountain, feet still floating slightly in the air.

"I'm glad you're safe after that incident in the desert. But remember, if you mess up and get killed, I can't help you, Princess. You will die."

Palona put both hands on her hips and leaned forward. The shock of seeing her sister had worn off fast with that comment.

"Princess? Don't call me that. And do you know what me and that psychopath, Kae Loredom, went through?"

She kicked her feet and giggled as she floated in the air. The moon illuminated behind her as she howled like a hyena.

"Yes, I watched it all! I'm so proud of you, Palona!"

The Knight clenched her fist and bit back at her sister.

"Are you a part of this madness, Rexia?!"

"Maybe, why do you ask, Pri- I mean, Palona?"

"Because your people are suffering! They are feeling the pains of this world changing! Nothing is right about Keostar!"

Palona's voice peaked, the trembling rage directed at the Goddess. But Rexia wasn't moved by her anger. Actually, she kept that high and mighty smirk as she answered back softly.

"And you are my blade to stop their suffering, are you not, Elf Knight Princess?"

The Elf stopped; her anger subsided almost instantly, recalling what she promised to the people in Chalaco.

"Huh?"

Confused, she looked back at Rexia in a different, softer light.

"Didn't you swear to be the sword for these people? Didn't you promise them that I didn't abandon them?"

Palona lowered her head. How she wished to shout back, but instead...

"Yes, I am. I promised to be your blade."

The Goddess flew to her Elf and held her close in her arms. But Palona couldn't feel her warmth, as if she were a spirit, visible but lingering, not bound to the world she protected and loved.

"Don't worry, Keostar will thrive, by the people within. So, keep fighting, precious little Princess."

The lingering wetness on her cheek took the Elf by surprise. Palona tried to hold her tight but Goddess vanished in the open air. Once again, she found herself alone in the world. Rexia was gone, and she couldn't feel her presence anymore.

"I will continue to be your sword, if that's what you wish, sister."

After a long while, the Elf found the fox once again. She was breathing softly, holding Tuffy in her arms like he was a plush toy found in a doll shop. Instead of fighting it any longer, Palona lied next to her and rested her head on the grass.

"Hm?"

Kae stirred.

"You're right, it might be for the best to rest, Kae. That way we don't burn ourselves out."

Palona said. But Kae wasn't convinced. She sounded like a robot, stoic, emotionless, and a tad upset.

"Hm? What changed your mind?"

The Elf shrugged as she closed her eyes.

"..."

"I see. Well, you can sleep on my tail. It's fluffy enough for a pillow."

"T-Thanks, I guess..."

Silently, Palona gazed into the sky, the stars twinkling above them. Their world was created by the person with whom she spoke only moments ago. Every gentle breeze of wind was blessed to be by the woman she admired.

"Rexia, of course I'd be your blade if you're in so much pain you hide it from me with a smile."

It was time to rest. So, Palona closed her eyes and fell into a deep false sleep, trying her best to remember the times when she watched her beloved sister from afar, admiring all the love and kindness she gave to the world below.
