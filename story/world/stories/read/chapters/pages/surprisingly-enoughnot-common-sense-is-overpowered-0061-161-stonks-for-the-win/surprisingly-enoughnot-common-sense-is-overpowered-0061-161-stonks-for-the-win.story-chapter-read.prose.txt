The competition was slowly coming to an end as more disciples accumulated their second loss and were eliminated. By this point, only two competitors remained undefeated: Xiao Fan and Qiu Han.

Since disciples with similar records were generally paired together, it was only natural for the two of them to meet now. Even if the competition would technically continue afterward, this was already beginning to feel like the decisive match.

“I didn’t expect the final undefeated contender to be someone like you,” Qiu Han said, his tone carrying its usual condescension even though he didn't seem to notice it himself.

Xiao Fan’s expression darkened. “What do you mean by someone like me?”

“I mean that among everyone who has made it this far, you are still the only one with a low-grade spiritual root.”

Considering Qiu Han’s beliefs, the answer was hardly surprising. If there were a way to measure what he considered innate superiority, spiritual roots would undoubtedly be near the top of the list, and by that standard, someone like Xiao Fan should never have made it this far.

“Tsk, you talk too much. I’ll defeat you right here, right now.”

The moment Xiao Fan said that, the surrounding stands erupted.

“The trash has lost his mind! He wants to challenge Senior Brother Qiu!”

And they were calling him trash again.

“He thinks he can defeat Qiu Han just because he’s currently ranked second? He’s too arrogant!”

Ning genuinely didn’t understand how that was arrogant.

“He has eyes but can’t see Mount Tai!”

A classic.

“It was indeed surprising that you defeated the other high-grade spiritual root disciples,” Qiu Han continued calmly, ignoring the noise around them, “but defeating me is something you cannot possibly accomplish. I will give you a chance to step down.”

He spoke as though the result had already been decided, giving Ning a strange sense of déjà vu. Hadn't Zhou Sheng said something rather similar before fighting Xiao Fan?

Xiao Fan merely shook his head and grinned before settling into a fighting stance. “I won’t give up.”

His Fifth Stage cultivation spread openly across the arena, immediately drawing another round of disbelief from the spectators.

“The Fifth Stage of Qi Condensation! He’s still only at the Fifth Stage!”

“The trash is finished. I bet he won’t last more than three moves!”

“Three moves? You’re overestimating him. He won’t even last one!”

By now, Ning had grown used to the crowd somehow forgetting everything Xiao Fan had already accomplished whenever he faced someone stronger. He was far more interested in seeing how Xiao Fan intended to deal with the enormous gap between the Fifth and Seventh Stages of Qi Condensation.

Qiu Han regarded him calmly. “You understand how strong I am?"

“I understand it,” Xiao Fan replied. "But I will win."

A faint smile appeared on Qiu Han’s face. “You won’t.”

The presiding elder lowered his sleeve.

“Begin.”

Qiu Han vanished.

[Supernatural Power: Quick, Doubles all movement speed]

The distance between them disappeared almost instantly as Qiu Han appeared directly in front of Xiao Fan and drove a palm toward his sternum.

Boom!

Xiao Fan was blasted backward, his heels scraping across the stone as he struggled to regain his balance, but Qiu Han was already following. Another activation of Quick brought him straight back into striking range, where an elbow came toward Xiao Fan’s shoulder, followed by a short punch to the abdomen and a knee aimed at his ribs.

Every movement was compact and efficient, and Qiu Han’s Seventh Stage spiritual sense made escaping even more difficult, allowing him to track Xiao Fan the moment he tried to dodge.

From the stands, Ning narrowed his eyes. This was the ugly part about fighting someone above your cultivation level. Qiu Han was already stronger and faster, while spiritual sense gave him another enormous advantage at close range.

Xiao Fan planted his feet and retaliated with Heaven-Shattering Fist, pouring condensed qi into his arm as he punched directly into Qiu Han’s guard.

Qiu Han caught the fist with one hand.

His arm trembled slightly under the impact, but the metallic sheen spreading across his skin absorbed most of the force. Before Xiao Fan could pull away, Qiu Han twisted his wrist, dragged him off balance, and followed with a straight punch across the face.

"Effort alone cannot overcome talent."

Xiao Fan turned his head at the last moment, but the strike still split his lip and sent blood scattering across the platform.

“Give up your futile persistence. Defeating someone above your cultivation requires talent, and with your talent, you cannot match me.”

Ning almost face-palmed.

This guy is practically raising his own flag.

Xiao Fan retreated several steps, and as he steadied himself, Ning noticed the old ring on his finger giving off a faint glow.

Then Xiao Fan grinned.

“So only someone with talent can defeat a person above their cultivation?”

Spiritual qi suddenly began gathering around him.

Ning immediately sat straighter.

It took the crowd another moment to understand what was happening.

“Impossible!”

“He’s trying to advance to the Sixth Stage?”

“How can he attempt a breakthrough just like that? With his spiritual root, there should be a bottleneck!”

Breaking through in the middle of a fight wasn't unheard of, but it was rare enough that most cultivators would never attempt it willingly.

Xiao Fan made the situation even more ridiculous. With his low-grade spiritual root, the bottleneck should have been more difficult to overcome, and breakthroughs under pressure usually happened in genuine life-and-death situations where someone had already been pushed to their limit.

Yet Xiao Fan had apparently decided that halfway through a tournament match was as good a time as any.

“Interesting.”

Qiu Han made no attempt to interrupt him and simply watched as the surrounding spiritual qi gathered faster.

“If the gap between us is too big, then all I need to do is close it, right?” Xiao Fan asked with a grin.

It was ridiculously simple logic.

Qiu Han stepped back. “Break through, then. If you can.”

There wasn't even a hint of concern in his voice. Apparently, he genuinely believed Xiao Fan reaching the Sixth Stage wouldn't change the result.

As spiritual qi churned around Xiao Fan and rushed into his body, Ning couldn't help staring at the scene.

It was moments like this that made him remember he really was living inside what seemed to be a xianxia novel.

Ning couldn't tell whether Xiao Fan had carefully judged Qiu Han's personality and concluded that he wouldn't interfere with the breakthrough, or whether he had simply decided to gamble on it. Either way, Ning would never have tried something this reckless himself.

Breaking through required concentration. Whenever Ning approached a bottleneck, he prepared beforehand, settled his condition, and made sure there was as little chance of interruption as possible. Xiao Fan, meanwhile, was attempting the same thing in the middle of an arena while his Seventh Stage opponent politely waited for him to finish powering up.

Ridiculous.

Qi surged through Xiao Fan’s meridians as he forced his way against the bottleneck, and after several tense moments, the final barrier gave way.

His aura surged outward.

Sixth Stage of Qi Condensation.

The golden light in Xiao Fan’s eyes deepened as his newly strengthened qi settled, and the crowd erupted almost immediately.

“He actually advanced!”

“In the middle of battle!”

Qiu Han, however, looked completely unconcerned as he stepped forward again.

“Now,” he said calmly, "you'll understand the difference between us."

Quick activated and his palm arrived in front of Xiao Fan almost immediately.

This time, Xiao Fan reacted in time.

His Pure Eyes turned fully golden, allowing him to catch Qiu Han’s movement just early enough to shift his body and redirect part of the incoming force. The palm still sent him several steps backward, but compared to the opening exchange, the difference was obvious.

Qiu Han paused briefly. “An ocular technique that enhances perception. It’s rare for me to encounter two opponents with such techniques in the same competition.”

His gaze lingered on Xiao Fan’s golden pupils.

Meanwhile, Ning stared.

What the hell is with this difference?

They were both using Pure Eyes, yet Ning had spent a considerable amount of time and resources cultivating his to its current level, while Xiao Fan's eyes had apparently decided that a faint glow wasn't dramatic enough and had gone straight to gold.

Protagonists really did get all the good special effects.

“Well,” Xiao Fan said, “consider this payback for that guy as well.”

His qi surged as he unleashed Heaven-Shattering Fist, and Qiu Han answered with a punch of his own.

Their fists collided hard enough to fracture the stone beneath their feet, and for the first time, Qiu Han was pushed back half a step.

Then his expression changed.

Qiu Han activated Quick and rushed forward, using the sudden acceleration to add even more force to his attack.

“Metallic Dragon’s Consecutive Fist!”

A barrage of strikes followed. Xiao Fan's golden Pure Eyes allowed him to deflect some attacks and retaliate against others, though several still found their mark.

Ning watched with growing interest. Qiu Han was already using Quick far more frequently than he had against most of his previous opponents, and his increasingly aggressive attacks suggested that Xiao Fan was beginning to get under his skin.

“It must be because this match means a lot to him,” Zhang Feng said from beside him.

Ning glanced over. “Hm?”

“You might not know this, but most clans have a main branch and several side branches. Qiu Han came from one of the side branches, and his family apparently wasn't highly regarded. If he had been an ordinary child, he probably would've spent his life handling minor affairs for the main family.”

Zhang Feng lowered his voice slightly before continuing.

“It was his spiritual root and supernatural ability that changed everything. Once his talent became apparent, the clan started treating him differently, and eventually he gained a status comparable to the heirs of the main branch.”

“No wonder he keeps talking about innate strength,” Ning muttered.

For Qiu Han, being blessed at birth had genuinely changed his fate. Without his spiritual root and Quick, he might have remained an insignificant member of a side branch for the rest of his life.

Xiao Fan's experience had been almost the exact opposite. His low-grade spiritual root had cost him his position as heir and turned him into an object of ridicule.

Looking at the two of them now, Ning finally understood why Qiu Han seemed to care so much about this particular fight.

In the arena below, Qiu Han's expression grew serious as he prepared to reveal his strongest technique.

“Earth-Grade Enhancement Technique,” he said evenly. “Crimson Ascendant.”

The sigil behind Qiu Han pulsed as Crimson Ascendant took effect, compressing the qi throughout his body while a faint red glow spread across his limbs.

Ning's attention immediately sharpened. He had never personally seen the technique before, but according to the information he'd gathered, Crimson Ascendant was an enhancement art that strengthened the user's body while increasing the amount of force they could produce.

Combined with Quick, Qiu Han could use the sudden acceleration to put far more power behind a body that had already been strengthened enough to withstand the resulting force.

Qiu Han demonstrated that almost immediately.

Quick carried him straight into Xiao Fan's guard, his crimson-enhanced palm striking with enough force to smash him into the platform and fracture the stone beneath him.

Before Xiao Fan could recover, Qiu Han was upon him again, following with a relentless series of punches and elbows that drove him farther across the arena.

"You see?" Qiu Han said as Xiao Fan staggered away from another blow. "When gold is refined, it becomes purer. Iron only cracks."

Xiao Fan coughed up a mouthful of blood, planted his feet, and slowly straightened as his breathing changed. Faint golden patterns began appearing beneath his torn robes, spreading across his skin while the qi within his body settled into a completely different circulation.

"Heavenly Lotus Body."

A translucent lotus appeared behind him giving off an air of etherality.

Ning's eyes widened. The Heavenly Lotus Body was reputed to be the strongest body-refining art within the Pure Qi Sect, and merely beginning its cultivation required the essence of a Heavenly Lotus, a spiritual plant that was said to be extinct.

Qiu Han clearly recognized it as well.

"Heavenly Lotus Body?" His eyes sharpened before a faint smile appeared. "This should be interesting."

"I hope you can still speak so calmly after I beat you."

The golden patterns across Xiao Fan's body brightened as he stepped forward and threw another Heaven-Shattering Fist. When the punch collided with Qiu Han's crimson-enhanced arm, both of them were forced backward.

Ning narrowed his eyes. Unlike Jade Skin and Icy Meridians, which strengthened specific parts of his body as he progressed through its stages, the Heavenly Lotus Body provided much broader reinforcement.

Xiao Fan's strength, speed, and durability had all increased noticeably, and together with whatever he had gained from his sudden breakthrough, it was enough for him to contend with Qiu Han directly.

As expected. He had another trump card.

As far as Ning knew, however, the Heavenly Lotus Body alone shouldn't have completely erased the difference between them, which meant something else was probably contributing to Xiao Fan's sudden increase in strength. He could worry about figuring that out later.

Qiu Han attacked again, but this time, Xiao Fan's golden Pure Eyes caught his approach, allowing him to meet the incoming palm with a straight punch.

Boom!

Both were thrown backward, yet neither hesitated before charging forward again. Fists and palms collided across the platform as the two exchanged increasingly powerful blows, neither willing to surrender ground.

As the exchange continued, Qiu Han began chaining Quick together more aggressively, attacking from one direction before accelerating into another. Crimson Ascendant allowed his strengthened body to withstand the tremendous strain, turning each burst of speed into a devastating attack.

Ning leaned forward.

He had experienced Quick firsthand, but Qiu Han hadn't pushed it nearly this hard during their match. Watching him now, Ning could see the interaction between the two techniques much more clearly.

Quick supplied the acceleration, while Crimson Ascendant allowed Qiu Han to withstand the resulting force. Unfortunately for him, the combination also made the weakness Ning had discovered during their earlier fight more pronounced.

The faster Qiu Han moved, the harder it became for him to stop or change direction.

Normally, this hardly mattered. Most opponents were defeated before they could exploit such a small opening, and even Ning had only managed it once by tricking him with Magical Spear.

Against Xiao Fan, however, Qiu Han was being forced to use Quick repeatedly, making those brief moments of lost control increasingly apparent.

Qiu Han attacked again, striking with enough force to drive Xiao Fan into the platform despite his attempt to block with both arms.

A crack came from Xiao Fan's ribs as the impact sent him to the ground, but the golden patterns of the Heavenly Lotus Body brightened beneath his torn robes and helped absorb enough of the force to keep the injury from ending the match.

By now, even Qiu Han was beginning to breathe harder. Repeatedly using Quick while maintaining Crimson Ascendant was clearly consuming a lot of spiritual energy, yet Xiao Fan continued forcing himself back to his feet no matter how many attacks got through.

"Why won't you fall?" Qiu Han demanded, the first clear trace of frustration entering his voice. "Give up! You have no chance of defeating me!"

Xiao Fan slowly straightened.

"Give up...?"

The words reminded him of something Ning had told him before the match.

"Quick is terrifying, but chaining those bursts together has a weakness. The faster he accelerates, the harder it becomes for him to stop. That's why I managed to catch him with my final attack. Once he commits to a movement, changing it becomes much harder. If you get an opportunity, that may be your best chance."

At the time, Xiao Fan had only understood Ning's explanation in theory. Now, after enduring Qiu Han's relentless assault, he could finally recognize the opening for himself.

When Qiu Han attacked again, Xiao Fan shifted aside at the last moment, his golden Pure Eyes catching the brief instant when his opponent struggled to bring his momentum under control.

There.

The opening was small, but Qiu Han's increasingly aggressive attacks were making it easier to recognize. All Xiao Fan needed was an opportunity to exploit it.

Another attack struck Xiao Fan and forced him backward, but he remained standing, his Pure Eyes following Qiu Han even as blood ran from the corner of his mouth.

Across from him, Qiu Han's frustration continued to build.

"So be it. I'll finish this once and for all!"

Crimson Ascendant surged as he pushed the technique even further, and Quick activated with more force than before. Qiu Han disappeared and came down toward Xiao Fan an instant later with both palms, putting nearly all of his accumulated momentum behind the attack.

Xiao Fan stepped forward.

The movement was small enough that Qiu Han still tried to correct his attack, but doing so in the middle of such an aggressive burst pulled his balance slightly off-center. After enduring the entire assault and watching for this exact moment, Xiao Fan finally had the opening he needed.

"Heaven-Shattering Fist: Consecutive Flash!"

He surged forward and drove the first punch into Qiu Han's chest before he could completely regain his footing.

Crack!

A fracture appeared across the crimson sigil behind Qiu Han as the circulation of Crimson Ascendant was disrupted, and before he could stabilize it, Xiao Fan's second punch was already coming, followed closely by the third.

Xiao Fan stayed close and continued attacking, refusing to give Qiu Han enough room to rebuild the momentum that had allowed him to dominate the fight.

Each successive punch disrupted Crimson Ascendant and drove Qiu Han farther backward, denying him the space and footing needed to make proper use of Quick.

For the first time since the match began, Qiu Han was losing ground.

"How can this be?" His earlier composure finally broke. "Gold remains gold! You cannot defeat me!"

He activated Quick again with far more urgency than before, accelerated too aggressively, and carried himself farther than intended. The already unstable crimson sigil behind him shuddered as its circulation slipped further out of rhythm.

Xiao Fan's golden Pure Eyes caught the opening immediately, and he gathered what remained of his qi into his right arm.

"Heaven-Shattering Fist!"

The punch struck Qiu Han squarely in the chest.

Thud!

Qiu Han was thrown backward across the platform, crashing through broken stone before finally coming to a stop near the edge of the arena. The crimson sigil behind him flickered several times before disappearing altogether.

Xiao Fan remained standing on the other side, though barely, while Qiu Han tried to rise and only made it halfway before his trembling legs gave out beneath him.

The presiding elder waited long enough to confirm that he couldn't continue.

"Winner, Xiao Fan."

Xiao Fan immediately collapsed face-first onto the platform.

For a moment, nobody reacted, and then the stands erupted.

"Impossible!"

"How can this trash beat Senior Brother Qiu?!"

They were still calling him trash.

Ning genuinely had to admire the dedication at this point. Xiao Fan had entered the competition as a Fifth Stage cultivator, defeated several of the strongest disciples in his generation, broken through during a match, and then beaten the only Seventh Stage cultivator in the tournament.

Apparently, none of that was enough to earn a promotion from trash.

A few spectators were even slapping themselves as if checking whether they were dreaming, while Xiao Fan remained face-down on the ruined platform, completely unconscious and utterly unconcerned about the chaos he'd caused.

Ning watched him for a moment.

One of the universal rules of shounen protagonists seemed to be that after an intense battle, they had to immediately pass out. Otherwise, there was simply no explaining this level of disregard for personal safety.

Xiao Fan had publicly offended an elder not long ago, and now he was lying unconscious in the middle of an arena without the slightest ability to protect himself. Thankfully, Zhou Chen was nowhere to be seen; Ning wouldn't have put it past that hotheaded fool to try something if he were here.

Then again, protagonists tended to have the survival instincts of a deer sprinting straight into headlights, which was to say, none at all. Somehow, things usually worked out for them anyway.

Ning's attention eventually returned to Qiu Han as the elders moved onto the platform. Giving Xiao Fan everything he had learned during their earlier match had paid off better than expected, since the weakness Ning had only managed to exploit once had ultimately become the key to Xiao Fan's victory.

As the saying went, when you moved faster than light, you ended up in darkness.

Or something like that.

More importantly, Xiao Fan had actually won against overwhelming odds, which meant Ning's gamble had paid off as well.

He was going to be rich.
