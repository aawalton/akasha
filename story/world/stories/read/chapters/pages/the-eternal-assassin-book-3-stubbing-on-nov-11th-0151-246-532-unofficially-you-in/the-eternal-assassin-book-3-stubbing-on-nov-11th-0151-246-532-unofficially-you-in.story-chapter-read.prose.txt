Ignoring the aggravated people behind him shouting at him to get down, Asher stared at the trio of realm wanderers doing their best to ignore the crowd of people hovering just off palace grounds. It wasn’t against the law for people to enter the grounds in front of the palace by any means, but again, most people simply didn’t want to even risk angering anyone with untold power at their fingertips. Even if High Prince Donvath had a reputation for being a fair and popular ruler, other nobles often visited the capital and stayed at the royal palace during their trips, and there was no telling what those people thought about more common folk getting in their way.

One of the three realm wanderers quickly noticed him hovering a bit above the rest of the crowd, and their eyes widened. The man who’d spotted him was tall and muscular, with a shaved head and neatly trimmed beard. He wore heavy leathers with metal plates covering most of his vitals and had a spear secured on his back. As soon as he spotted Asher, he grinned and motioned for him to come forward.

Admittedly curious, Asher stopped levitating, dropping back down into the crowd. Slipping into the astral, he walked straight through the crowd, returning to the material realm the moment he was free. He heard a handful of gasps from those behind him as he suddenly appeared out of nowhere, and the bald realm wanderer’s grin only grew wider as he walked forward.

“That’s some trick you’ve got there!” the man laughed, stopping before him and snapping his fingers. The noise of the crowd vanished the moment he did so, and Asher blinked as his aura finally made more sense. The realm wanderer’s aura was like a silent pool of smoke drifting around him, feeling as though it absorbed any and all sound.

“Sound element?” Asher asked, turning to look over his shoulder. He knew he accomplished much of the same using his Spatial Lock half the time, but whenever he used his skill, there was always at least a near-invisible bubble to be seen. As far as he could tell, the realm wanderer’s skill was completely invisible.

“Sound, Smoke, and Light,” the man nodded, putting a fist over his chest in some form of salute. “I’m Mako, captain of the realm wanderer squad for the Eight Crests. It’s good to finally meet the man behind the myth.”

“What myth?” Asher asked, growing increasingly curious as they spoke. The other two realm wanderers seemed content to sit back and let their captain do the talking, as they made no effort to come forward and join them.

“That the God of Death has come to this realm in order to purge it of evil,” Mako laughed, shaking his head. “Though I think some of the stories we’ve heard about The Reaper might have been a tad exaggerated. Did you actually kill an entire gang of bloodthirsty criminals in their own base, all on your own?”

“No, I had quite a lot of help for that one,” Asher said, scratching the back of his head. “Though I did take out their gang leader. And convinced her second in command to join my team.”

“Nine hells, that’s even more impressive if you ask me,” Mako grinned. The realm wanderer seemed to have a near-permanent smile on his face. Though rather than one like Moxy’s demonic grin she wore whenever she was fighting, Mako’s looked like he was just happy to be part of the conversation. “Anyway, you’re probably wondering why we’re even here in the first place.”

“The thought certainly crossed my mind,” Asher admitted. “Realm wanderer business?”

“I mean, yes, but that’s all our business!” Mako laughed. “No, we’re here for a couple of reasons. The fourth member of our team, a wizard who specializes in traveling between realms, is currently off chatting with your High Prince. Without her, we’re pretty much just a group of fancy warriors with quite a good bit of useless knowledge!”

“You guys can’t travel to different realms on your own?” Asher asked, blinking at Mako’s honesty.

“Nope! We’re not Spatial element holders, and we can’t mimic skills with our tritessence like a wizard can. Lanra, our wizard, handles the transportation, and we handle the mission. We make a pretty solid team.”

“I guess I never thought too hard about all the details of a realm wanderer team,” Asher admitted. “I sort of assumed you had artifacts or something to help with that.”

“Well now I have to ask,” Mako said, raising an eyebrow as he looked Asher up and down. “Can you travel to the different realms?”

“I’m still developing my skills, but somewhat,” he admitted, not seeing any reason to lie to Mako. It was his first time meeting actual realm wanderers, and Mako’s aura didn’t seem to have anything concerning in it. Not to mention, he just plain liked the man. “At the moment, I can only reach the astral realm and the wild realm. Though I did fight against a monster I’m pretty certain was from the abyss at one point that a summoner brought to the material realm.”

“Hey, that’s two more realms than I can go to!” Mako laughed, before his face grew serious. “And the abyss, huh? Nasty place, that. Even we try to avoid spending too long in there whenever possible. You said someone summoned a monster from there? I didn’t even know something like that was possible.”

“You and me both,” Asher said. “So what realm wanderer business do you have with Donvath?”

“Already on a first name basis with your High Prince, are you?” Mako chuckled. “That’s probably not going to make Lanra’s job any easier. Like I said, we’re here for a few things, but our primary mission is one you’ll probably hear about soon enough. We wanted to see about recruiting you.”

“I wondered if that was it. You’re not actually the first country to try and do so.”

“Yeah, we heard about the Hojon empire. I can’t believe you met with Groln of all people. He’s a legend, even in our country! I mean, hard not to be when you’re walking around with a Demon element!”

“I didn’t realize he was such a big name,” Asher admitted. “He seemed nice enough.”

“You haven’t seen him fight,” Mako said, shaking his head as his eyes went elsewhere. “We once worked together to take out a mass of lesser demons a wanna-be cult had summoned. It was… well, both awe-inspiring and the scariest thing I’ve ever seen. Word to the wise, try not to get on his bad side if you can help it.”

“I sort of figured that one out on my own,” Asher said, thinking back to the monstrous man who had harnessed the power of demons for himself. “Either way, if you guys came here to try and recruit me, shouldn’t I be the one meeting with your wizard? Why is she talking to Donvath?”

“That’s politics,” Mako shrugged. “We wouldn’t want to anger any of our neighbors by trying to snatch one of their national assets out from under them without warning. The Hojon empire doesn’t share a border with the Noala Kingdom, and even if they did, they’re one of the three superpowers on our continent. They can pretty much do whatever they want, which is probably why Groln didn’t bother with the pleasantries.”

“Doesn’t that mean you shouldn't have brought any of this up before Lanra and Donvath finished talking?”

“I was never one for politics myself,” Mako grinned, winking at him. “If your High Prince brings out any of his famous truthseekers, maybe just don’t say anything? If you wouldn’t mind?”

“I’ll see what I can do,” Asher said, returning Mako’s grin. The longer he spoke with the realm wanderer, the more he came to like the man. “You’re actually the first person from the Eight Crests I’ve spoken to. Do you guys have your own truthseekers?”

“Not exactly. We’ve got our searchers, which are elite warriors trained to awaken and properly use the Mind element. They can pull out even the smallest of secrets from within someone’s subconscious, so it’s next to impossible to pull one over on them.”

“Wait, the Mind element?” Asher started, staring at Mako. “Isn’t that forbidden?”

“Not in the Eight Crests!” Mako grinned, hooking a thumb over his shoulder. “In fact, Jameston back there has a Mind element himself, and it’s critical to our ability to work together as a team. He’s got this one skill, Mind Meld, which lets us telepathically speak to one another. Never venture into another realm without it.”

“That does sound useful…” Asher said slowly, peering at Jameston from afar. The man in question wore a similar outfit to Mako, but rather than a spear, his weapon of choice appeared to be twin crossbows holstered on either hip. His aura was like an intangible octopus stretching out of his head, the tentacles casually drifting about as if in search of other minds to touch. Asher shuddered despite himself, and Mako must have noticed.

“It’s really not that big of a deal,” he said, waving back at his teammate. “To be honest, I always thought the concept of ‘forbidden’ elements as rather silly. Why is the ability to mentally attack someone and put them into a coma any more dangerous than the ability to hurl a horse-sized fireball at someone and burn them to a crisp? All elements are incredibly dangerous, it’s just about how one uses them. Oh, and just for the record, we’ve got one or two forbidden ones you guys don’t. Like Bone, for example. You get one infamous serial killer who had the skill to take over people’s skeletons and bam, forbidden element.”

“To be fair, that sounds like a pretty solid reason,” Asher said, trying not to imagine what it would be like for someone to forcibly control his very skeleton.

“Bah, that was like, six hundred years ago. But getting elements taken off the forbidden list is a whole thing, and I certainly won’t pretend like I know how it works. Again, politics. Not my forte. Anyway, we’re getting off topic again! You asked why we were here, and I only partially answered. Yes, trying to recruit you was our primary goal, but Lanra was also being super secretive about needing to chat with your High Prince for another reason. No clue what that was all about, but she made us wait out here. She’s never forced us to stay away from a meeting, which means it’s got to be something really interesting.”

“Out here?” Asher asked, gesturing around them at the palace grounds. “Isn’t this a bit far from the meeting room?”

“Sound element, remember?” Mako grinned, tapping his ear. “Unfortunately, Lanra knows the range of my skills, and also knows I would absolutely try and listen in on her secret meeting. Your High Prince probably has his office warded against things like that anyway, but Lanra doesn’t like to take chances when she doesn’t have to.”

“Makes sense,” Asher said, deciding he probably shouldn’t mention the purpose of the meeting either if their own teammate was keeping them in the dark. The meeting could only have something to do with Donvath spreading the word to the different rulers about Zygrul and his Void element. Donvath had made it quite clear that they were keeping this as top secret as possible, so Asher figured the realm wanderers probably wouldn’t know about it until the last possible moment.

“I know I’m not officially allowed to proposition you to join us, but even if you don’t accept the offer I totally didn’t make, you should come by and join us for a mission one of these days,” Mako offered. “I’d love to see how an actual Spatial element holder functions on the other realms, and who knows, maybe we can help push your still-developing skills in the right direction to ensure you can travel to whatever realm you want.”

“Thank you, I’ll probably take you up on that offer just out of curiosity,” Asher said, giving Mako one last smile before sensing Samantha and Brian touching down on solid ground outside the city. “If you don’t mind, I need to run. I’ve already promised I’d spend my day hanging out with someone, and they just finished what they were doing.”

“By all means, don’t let me keep you!” Mako said, snapping his fingers again and summoning sound back to their ears. Giving him a quick wave, the realm wanderer grinned one last time. “Hope we see you again sometime!”

Waving back, Asher focused on Samantha and triggered Recall, watching as the world shifted around him and he teleported. He couldn’t help but laugh at the final image of both shock and excitement on Mako’s face as he warped away right in front of him.
