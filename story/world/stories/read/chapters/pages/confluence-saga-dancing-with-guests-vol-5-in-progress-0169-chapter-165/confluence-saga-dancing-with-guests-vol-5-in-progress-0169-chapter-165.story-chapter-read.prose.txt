Fay stared across her desk at her visitor. She hadn't expected to get a message from Valene the morning after getting back from Scattered Creek, she especially hadn't expected it to be a request to meet with her, at Holding Syl, at her earliest convenience, ideally today.

Valene didn't come see her, she went to see him. Valene didn't reach out to her for appointments, he told her he wanted to talk. OverSeers didn't ask Seers, even Senior Seers who no longer worked for the Council, if something was convenient. Valene did ask her if she had time to work on projects for him, but that was paid work.

"Sir?"

"It's been a while since you called me that."

"I'm trying to figure out why we're here."

"This is your office? Not someone else's?"

"Yes?"

"Then where else would we be meeting?"

"In the past you've always had me come to you."

"In the past I wasn't trying to keep our conversation primarily between us."

Fay frowned. Very few of their conversations in his office had ever had anyone else present, and when they did it was usually his assistant bringing them something. There wasn't anyone to keep their conversations from unless…

"You're not really here, are you."

She caught Valene's brief smile before it turned into a frown.

"No, I'm not. I've been delayed while doing a personal inspection of local Awakened society. Deep Earth is easy to keep track of, the more feudal Holdings, especially those that partially integrate with Unawakened society are much harder to get accurate information on."

"I could have gotten you a report, I have the-"

"If you have one ready I'll owe you a debt. Otherwise I'll have to spend a few hours actually looking around."

OverSeers didn't spend time looking around, that was the work of Seers. Anyone looking would know he was up to something, would know he was creating a plausible explanation for his absence and nothing more.

"No one will believe you were doing an inspection."

"They don't have to believe me. The record has to reflect it."

"Sir," she took a deep breath before starting again. She could do this. "Valene. Why are you here."

"Your self-directed Technique."

"My..?"

"Your search query that didn't stop. It was noticed."

"So it actually did answer twice? I saw the responses, but couldn't figure out why."

"It triggered an alert. It also sent me a snippet that shows that Clyde Velli has miraculously returned from the Depths, just barely past the wall."

"It… Alert?" she said before realizing what else he had said. "Clyde? And it sent you information? When?"

"Less than a week ago."

"That was after…"

"Fay. You aren't in any trouble but I need you to answer this honestly. What is a Doorkeeper?"

A Doorkeeper? He- She wasn't supposed to know about that, she'd found the records by accident. He was asking her to be honest, telling her she wasn't in trouble. But knowing about the Doorkeeper Constructs was…

"A Seer Construct that is charged with monitoring a location. One that requires maintenance performed by authorized Seers who are trained in the appropriate techniques to ensure that the… oh."

"I won't ask how you found out, or when. It doesn't matter," he said and seemed to relax into his chair. "Your knowing that much, even if you didn't realize you knew, does help explain."

"But I don't have the training or knowledge. I don't-"

"Fay," Valene said before she could finish explaining herself. "The initial Doorkeeper creators knew less than you do. They didn't have any of the advantages you seem to be thinking about."

"You mean that any self-directed technique..?"

"No. Not any, not every. There are specific protocols built into the Doorkeeper techniques to ensure they serve their purpose, to constrain their capabilities to exactly what they need. They also need to be tied to appropriate power and growth sources or they will ultimately break down, if long past their expected completion."

"So my search will vanish?"

"By now it has. If it hasn't then you've skipped a few steps. The fact that it sent me something means you skipped others."

"What now? Do I learn to maintain Doorkeepers?"

She saw Valene stare at her before laughing.

"No. Absolutely not. You'd have to rejoin the Council, move into restricted housing, learn a set of very specific skills that would do you absolutely no good except for in service to creation you get assigned to."

"Specific skills?"

"Each one is in some way unique. They mostly maintain themselves, manage themselves, update themselves. The Seers who maintain them are almost more servant and janitor than anything else."

"Then what. You didn't come here to tell me all this in private. You would have called me to your office, told me to avoid repeating whatever I did and been done."

"You never used to speak to me like that."

"You never were the visitor."

"You're right. You've been cleared to access three sets of information and I'll tell Yshe to expect to need to come visit you in private."

"Yshe? She's not a Seer."

"Yshe used to be an Architect, nearly Senior," Valene said, continuing before she could even start to question the revelation. "She still remembers things that come with that, which hopefully includes how to provide you with a means to integrate your techniques with the power system and more importantly with the Seed."

He'd mentioned power so the first part made sense. If she wasn't maintaining the technique herself it would need to be fueled somehow. And a Doorkeeper was a Construct, not just a Technique. Tying it to the Seed though? Why would she…

"Growth source," she said.

"Yes. Although you will want to talk to your family before you take that step."

"And the information?"

"Clarifications on how to intentionally create self-directed techniques. Every Seer has done it slightly differently, but supposedly the advice helps. The Doorkeeper Protocols. You don't want them sniffing around because you did the wrong thing, but also you will want to determine which to incorporate yourself."

"That's only two, you said three."

"The differences between Fae and Human technique patterns, as recorded by Seers from both sides. Simple things are mostly identical. More complex techniques are built on different premises. As far as everyone knows anyone can use either style, or both, it just matters how you were taught. Awakened do tend to favour one or the other, use it more naturally."

"What does that have to do with self-directed techniques and Doorkeepers?"

"Different approaches to the same techniques? Different ways of making something work? I'm sure you'll find a benefit to that knowledge."

Fay started to nod then caught herself. He'd warned her that there were Fae at the school, so he knew Anya was. But did he realize how close she was getting to some of them? That there would be lasting changes?

"You are allowed to speak of these things with Anya and Seb," Valene said. "In the interest of the growth of their Holding."

"She's made friends with one of her cousins," Fay said. "And Lord Percivus gave his approval for Briana, Sandra, and Sovereign Lys to learn of her heritage."

"Good that she's made fri-"

Valene froze mid sentence and she both saw and heard his chair distort under his hands.

"Percivus? Why would he be… Seb. Of course they'd request help with training a Bladesinger and of course He'd sent Percivus, they would have woken him up to approve requesting training for the Royals."

He stood, sighing as he saw the damage he'd caused her chair.

"I'll cover the replacement when paying for the next project. I have to get going. If you speak to Percivus during your visits, tell him I say hello."

Fay stared at the empty doorway. Valene was gone before he'd even finished speaking.

She wasn't in trouble. She hadn't done anything wrong. She was being given access to information that would help her recreate what she'd done by accident and turn it into something even more useful.

Valene knew Lord Percivus, not just by name but seemed to have interacted with him before. Valene approved of her actions. Yshe had been an Architect? Close to becoming a Senior Architect? But Yshe was barely past the Wall. Yes she was an old friend of Valene's but Fay had always assumed that meant they'd crossed paths at some point. Yes, she'd been aware that Yshe had been Broken, but she'd never guessed it was to that degree.

Her eyes dropped to her desk as a sigh turned in to a smile. She had research to do.
