I stood, looking out a window my father had looked out, his father had looked out, even his father too had looked out. This place was the personal meeting hall of the king since our family first came to rule this land. It was built by the first of our line, and would continue even after I was gone. This was the right place for what came now, the place my father and his father had chosen too.

“You wanted to see me father?” a voice behind me called, my son, my only son, no longer a child, not quite the man he could be yet.

“Yes, it is nearly time for me to retire. With that in mind it is time for you to begin to take on my duties. Are you ready?”

Each king endeavored to train and hand over power to his replacement before he died. This meant that when he did die, the king didn't die, the former king did, with the new one ensconced well. Sons didn't fight sons in battles of succession, for the son who would be king was king before succession needed to happen.
