Inside the forest of Hylas, there was a small den under the roots of a huge sentient tree. This den currently belongs to the black cat named Dawn.

She was residing there with her five kittens and an unknown infant creature. Dawn didn't know what it was, and only adopted this baby out of pity.

This baby creature was an infant lightning dragon. Until now, his eyes have only opened partially due to having undeveloped vision, and he only woke up when he was distressed or was starved. But now he suddenly woke up with his eyes wide open in shock. For the first time, the baby dragon was able to open his eyes properly.

“MIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEEOOOOOOOOOOW.” It was a loud, high-pitched meowing sound. So, being a baby, this sound was hurting his ears.

Along with him, the other babies who were there with him also became shocked, and they even started mewling loudly.

At first, the dragon baby didn't understand what was happening, so he looked at the source of the loud, painful scream and then got even more shocked.

In front of him, a huge black cat was holding its head with its front paws and rolling on the ground in immense pain. Its eyes and head were glowing with blue light.

This black cat was Dawn. She was inside her den. Due to her scream, everything became chaotic.

“Meeeeooow.eeeeooow.eeeooow.eeooow.” She was screaming nonstop and kept rolling on the ground.

(What the....) The baby dragon got scared seeing the huge cat rolling violently.

Then suddenly the cat stopped rolling and stayed there as if it was unconscious.

The cat's eyes and head kept glowing with blue light. The light from inside sometimes became dim purple and sometimes bright blue, especially in the joints. The light looked purple because of the blood flow, so its head looked especially weird and somewhat beautiful.

Under the skin, the blue light was coming from the gaps of the joints between the skull bones. And it seemed like the bones were disjointed while creating a gap due to being pushed from inside. Although the skin held the bones together, it was noticeable enough due to the blue light coming from inside its head.

He had never seen such a big animal with such a weird, glowing head.

Then he put his front legs on the edge of the ditch and lowered his head, and kept peeking at Dawn while trying to hide in the ditch and observe what was happening.

Suddenly, he felt something touching his sides. When he turned his head, he saw all five kittens beside him. Immediately, he tried to jump away, but his undeveloped motor skills hindered him from doing that, and moreover, he felt so heavy that he was not even able to stand up. He was only able to wiggle, turn his body, and drag himself with great effort.

Until now, he was so panicked by the screaming sound and so immersed in the weird cat’s head that he never noticed the kittens lying with him. When he was not able to jump away, he looked at the big kittens in panic.

He tried for a while to move away. But being pushed from both sides made his attempt useless. It took him a while to realize that those beings were not harmful and were only pushing him from the sides.

He realized another thing. The baby kittens were distressed, and two of them were looking at the black cat with a horrified expression, and three of the kittens were trying to shove their head into each other’s bellies for some reason.

(Whaaat??? …. Where… am… I…???) He was extremely confused now. He had never seen creatures like that.

When he determined that the big kittens were not harmful, he turned his head towards the big cat again. He felt like it was the most important subject in this enclosed space, and he felt that to know what was happening, he needed to keep his eyes on the big black cat.

Its head was still glowing with blue light coming out from the skull's bone joints. He kept looking with a bewildered expression. Then, after a while, he started hearing a faint, continuous cracking sound from the big black cat. He never noticed this earlier.

The cat was lying on the ground, but sometimes its body was moving slightly, and sometimes it just stayed there as if nothing was happening. But something was definitely happening to it.

The baby dragon was sure of it because he noticed glowing lights sometimes coming from the body.

It was as if after each cracking sound, the body part glowed for a few seconds. And then a cracking sound came from another body part, and that part glowed for a few seconds. This process kept happening.

(Is that sound from the bones? But it doesn't seem like it.)

After a while, the cracking sound stopped, and the cat’s head stopped glowing as well.

Then the cat stood up and started moving towards the opening of the enclosed space. Seeing this, he was relieved.

Outside, the cat started jumping at a weird angle and started doing stunts. The baby dragon, seeing this, got even more confused.

Just a while ago, the cat was screaming, then its body started glowing with a crackling sound, and now, the big cat was running here and there, jumping around in weird positions. Although the opening was small and he was not able to see everything clearly, the opening was enough to notice what the cat was doing outside.

The cat kept coming in front of the enclosed space and going somewhere while jumping. He didn’t know what to think anymore.

He was finally relieved.

He wanted to figure out what was happening, so he tried to remember the past few days.

“!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” Immediately, his face paled from remembering a few things.

It was not the first time that he was with these kittens and that black cat. He remembered a few blurry visions of himself being with the cat and these kittens.

He remembered how he was being licked endlessly with the cat’s huge, rough tongue, only to feel relieved. He remembered how the kittens shoved him aside when he was trying to get his meal from the big cat’s nipples. He remembered how he fought to push the kittens to get a drinking spot.

He never thought that he, a human, would someday try to push aside a few extremely huge and oversized kittens to get milk from a cat, and be happy about it.

Cringe filled his belly thinking of all these things. He didn’t want to believe all these things.

(This must be a dream… hmm… yup, this must be an endless dream.) He immediately moved his head above in the air, and then hit the ground as hard as he could with his head.

But instead, he hit his jaw. It was hurting. He wanted to bang his head on the ground, but for some reason, his jaw came in the middle and hit the ground instead.

(muuuuu…) It was painful. He held his jaw with his front limbs and stayed until the pain subsided.

Surely, it was not a dream. (What is happening?)

Everything around him was weird. He was in a damp, enclosed space, with only one opening. He was sitting with five kittens, and one big black cat was playing outside the space.

And the most weird thing was the big cat was taking care of him like its kittens.

He didn't know what to think anymore.

(What have I gotten myself into…. Huuuh) “muuuu….” While thinking, he sighed and produced a weird sound.

“!!! … muuuu…” (Was that my sound!!!) “muuu… !!!” He didn’t notice the change in his vocals earlier. Now that he was thinking about many things, he finally noticed it. He only sighed but the sound was very different.

(Aaaaaaa) “Muuu…”

(Aaaaaaaaa!) “Muuu…!”

(Abcdefg!!) “Muu uuuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu…!!”

(ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOP!!!) “Muuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu uuu…!!!”

(WHAAAT THE HELLL!!! THAT'S MY SOUND!!!!!!) “iiiuuuoooouuuiiiiuooouuuu!!! uiiiiiuuuuooouu uuuuu!!!”

(I’M DIIIISABLED NOW!!! I CAN'T SPEAK ANYMORE!!!) “uuuuoooouuuuuouuuoooo!!! yiiiiiiooooooiiiiooooooooiooooo!!!” The baby dragon finally started getting his situation perfectly, according to him. He didn’t know how wrong his assumption was.

Until now, he was looking straight at the kittens, the enclosed space, and the big black cat. Due to all the chaos around him, he didn't notice the changes.

He turned his body and started looking at the ceiling, trying to take in the information.

(Fine… everything is fine… I’m still alive… I’ll go to the doctor after dealing with these creatures.) He calmed himself down.

While getting sad, he touched his neck, looking at his body. He was not interested in the cat and its kittens anymore. Now there was another series of shocks waiting for him.

He finally noticed his body.

He had white fur all over his hands, legs, and belly. His body shape was also different. (Am I wearing a costume…)

This was not even a little bit shocking when compared to his earlier reaction.

The baby then started rubbing the fur on one hand with the other hand. Then he touched his belly with both hands. Those were not hands. Those looked more like paws. And his fur was very soft and silky.

“iiuuuuu” (Hmmm… it’s high quality, I must say… Whoever made me wear this costume must be an excellent creator.)

(There must be an invisible zipper somewhere.) He started using his front paws to look for the invisible zipper to open the costume. But while using his paws, he noticed another weird thing.

His vision was very clear. He was NOT seeing through the holes in the costume. And it didn’t feel like a costume at all.

All costumes are hot from the inside, and people inside always sweat excessively. But he didn’t feel like that at all.

He felt like it was his real skin.

He didn’t want to believe what his instincts were telling him.

He touched his belly again with his paws and rubbed his belly fur. Then he moved his paw in front of his face and tried to move his fingers.

Strangely, instead of his fingers, his whole front limb moved along with his claws.

He then moved his other paw in front of his face and tried to move his fingers.

Again, instead of fingers, his whole paw moved along with his claws.

He was not able to move the small animal's fingers, and instead, his paws moved every time. His claws also moved a bit.

He finally started getting the situation he was in.

“Eeeuuuuuuuu” (So I have become a cat… Okay.)

Until now, he had had enough of being shocked. He didn’t react much as before.

Only looked at the ceiling after moving his head. His head started aching.

(But how did I become a cat? . . . Wait… What was I even doing before?)
