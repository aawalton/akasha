Chapter 201 / B3-46: Divine Grace

The crowd parted for Joe, as if they knew that he needed to be up front. Someone may have made an announcement before he arrived, or it could have been a subtle impression given by the goddess herself. Joe knew the gods did not walk among the world or “speak” directly to people … well, maybe they did to Mercy Suku, but not to normal folks. Even so, they could make their presence known at times. Mairree, for instance, was notorious for amplifying gatherings into blow-out parties. Joe could imagine that if Murccee wanted him at the front of the crowd, she would have no trouble gently nudging folks out of his way.

When he reached the paved area between the muralled wall and the Mercy’s little home, he saw that the flagstones had already been prepared for the ceremony. The stones had been painted with five large interlacing circles painted with a different hue: amber, green, blue, white, and a dusty mauve. Joe had spent enough time around Mercy Suku to know these five colors represented the tenets of Murrcee.

Amber was for compassion, her primary principle. White denoted charity, while blue was the one Joe best fit, wellness and healing. Green was for community. One could provide care to strangers, but within a community, that care is redoubled many times over. The last, and in some ways the most vital, was mauve’s love. The obligation to offer support to those in need is noble, but to do so out of a true love for your fellow beings was holiness in the eyes of the goddess.

Joe felt a sense of welcoming from the circle, and after passing Mojo to Kenda, he stepped fully through the crowd. Each ring was pushed out in one direction, so at every 72 degrees there was a clear section of the band exposed from the other colors. Joe moved to the blue arc and stood upon it. He felt the gentlest brush of the divine being’s greeting, nothing as overpowering as channeling Mercy Suku’s resuscitation spell or witnessing her presence as he did during the healing of Runkbadok. This felt like a friendly wave from across the room, not even a spoken ‘hi’. Even so, the goddess’s regard sent warm tingles pulsing through his body. Joe felt himself relax, basking in the sensation.

He was not alone for long. A few seconds after he had stepped onto the blue ring, others joined him in the middle. A man and a woman in baking aprons took places on the white strand. Joe wondered if they gave out bread to their neighbors, thereby fulfilling charity.

A grandmotherly woman stepped slowly onto the amber. With [Witchsight], Joe saw that her advanced age was slowing her movements, but there was no sign of any inflammation in her joints, proof that even the saint’s constant aura could not forestall time indefinitely. Joe noted her name as he scanned her health. The matron, Durva Reez, threw him a warm smile of greeting when she spotted his attention.

Joe was pleasantly surprised by the man who strode onto the green arc of community. A now fully upright Runkbadok, waved to Joe. Gone was any sign he had ever had his hump. His gait was smooth, and his aura flushed with health, no longer straining against the mass that had him curled over for so many years. His colorful attire was speckled with the very colors on which they were standing, letting Joe know just who it was who painted the ring under his feet. As the artist who crafted the incredible design of the worship wall, it made perfect sense to have Runk reflect community.

A young mother, carrying a newborn babe, stepped onto the mauve ring. Just looking at her, everyone in the gathering could recognize the joyous and protective love she had for the infant in her arms. Joe knew Burth was the actual deity of motherhood and that Murrcee’s love was more about general love than individual love. Even so, to characterize the aspect of love, one could not find a more obvious example.

A moment after the five symbols of Murrcee’s tenets were in place, the cloth hanging covering the little home’s door parted, releasing a giant of a man, easing his way out of the small aperture. When Margen stood upright, Joe could see countless small prayers had been painted onto his skin. What was even cooler was that all of these short blessings had been written by different hands, and each one held a small spark of divinity. In [Witchsight], the once-lost founder looked like a Christmas tree, twinkling with specks of many different colors. Before Joe had arrived, Mercy Suku must have enlisted those gathered here to begin the process of literally wishing Margen well.

Soon, it was time for the five chosen aspects to add their blessings. Margen walked up to Runk, and the painter took the inked writing brush from the saint. With a deft sweep of strokes, he painted a few words across the big man’s palm. The mother wrote on his other palm. Margen turned his hands over and received a benediction from the bakers and grandmother across the back of each hand.

Lastly, it was Joe’s turn. With both hands front and back ritually taken, he had no idea where or what he should write. The pair stepped up to him, and Joe swallowed nervously until the warmth of Murrcee washed through him once again. There was a comfort in that sensation that seemed to say,  ‘Have faith in yourself. If you believe in us, there is no wrong way to show it.’

Joe’s eyes swept over the towering figure in front of him, [Witchsight] lingering passively behind his normal vision. It was thanks to the combined perceptions that Joe saw where his blessing was most needed. On Margen’s shoulder, just under the skin, remained a deep, black knot of corruption in the shape of a handprint. This had been the spot Joe had had to work hardest on when he had been healing the swordsman. It was the spot where the Erlking had infected the hero with his corruptive touch. It was the core of the curse ravaging Margen’s body.

He also noted that no one else had written across this blighted patch of skin. Even with most of Margen looking healthy to normal vision, the flesh over his trapezius was darker and grayer than the rest of him.

Plucking the brush from the Mercy’s delicate claws, Joe wrote the word, ‘Begone’ to the foul sickness. He hated that twisted malignancy. With the very core of his existence, he wanted to see it destroyed.

As the brush’s bristles lifted from the general’s skin, Joe felt what equated to a sigh from Murrcee herself, while Suku gently shook her head.

‘Oops,’ he thought. ‘I guess there actually is a wrong sentiment after all.’

Murrcee was not about eradicating disease or wounds. She was about giving health and comfort. In [Witchsight], amongst the gentle flickers of the other blessings, Joe’s prayer burned with a jarring, angry, red hue. Totally out of place.

He closed his eyes and began to calm himself. After drawing in a deep breath, he let the air out slowly, centering his emotions. His eyes opened, and Joe swept his thumb across the back two-thirds of the hostile command, smearing out the ‘gone’. Below the remaining ‘Be’, he added, with the utmost sincerity, the word ‘Well’.

“Be well, Margen,” he wished out loud.

“Much better,” Mercy Suku praised before leading Margen into the center of the rings.

The ceremony began with Murrcee’s benediction.

May this day bring you comfort.     Hurt abated, and sorrow consoled.     Deliver joy to the joyless.     Succor to the mournful.     And balm to the infirm.     Let us be joined, healed, and kind.     Bless us, Murrcee.     As we bless each other.

The entire gathering took up this litany, repeating over and over. Joe noted that some clever vocalists were staggering their prayers, turning the psalm into a roundel, giving the collective prayer depth and beauty.

A light began to fill Margen’s body. At first, Joe only saw it magically, but after a few rounds of the benediction, it was clearly apparent to everyone. This warm glow was beginning to overwhelm his [Witchsight]. There was such a depth of bountiful power within that emanation that Joe had to shut down his perception skill to prevent himself from being overloaded by Murrcee’s attention once again.

Even though the grace was so potent it made Joe stagger, he couldn’t help but sneak peeks. The saint of Cliffside marshaled Murrcee’s bounty, managing a level of potency that was so far beyond Joe’s comprehension, it was unbelievable to witness. When Joe worked to purge Margen’s corruption, it felt like a fight. Joe’s spell drove out the cursed afflictions, attacking the nodes of blackened taint. Even the wildness had gotten involved at times, helping Joe battle through the stench and the malevolence inside the founder.

Suku and Murrcee did not fight the [Mythic Mummy Rot]. Together, they simply erased it. They didn’t drive it out as Joe had tried to do, since that would never completely heal Margen. Instead, wherever the saint and her goddess focused just became healthy, as if the taint had never occurred in the first place. Starting from the soles of his feet, this wave of cleansing worked inward and upward. When the sweeping edge reached his chest, it seemed to spin and fold; one branch passed to his fingers and up his arm, while the other side swept through the man’s head. The two halves met at his shoulder, and the source of the blight. Joe expected to see the closing waves smash the curse’s core, and yet once again, he was not thinking like a Murrceeian. There was no attack. The deepest blot of rot was simply replaced by healthy flesh.

Feeling a bit let down by this anticlimactic conclusion to Margen’s plight, it dawned on Joe that there was a fundamental difference between himself and the Mercy. Up until the last days of his life, Joe had fought against the illness that had killed him. That mentality had not vanished when he came to Illuminaria. He saw wounds and disease as problems that he had to remedy. Mercy Suku saw wellness as something she needed to provide. Either way, it amounted to the patient getting better, but there was a subtle difference.

With this moment of clarity, Joe felt a touch of praise from the deity. He could feel the goddess smile on him, which Hawking translated into a system-based bounty.

Boon: Your self-reflection has been acknowledged by Murrcee.  Your conduct has awarded you the following Boon:

[Benevolent Boost] I am with you. Gain a significant enhancement to any relevant skills for one action you attempt in the benefit of another. The greater the impact this endeavor has on others, the more significant the enhancement will be. One use.

Joe felt a tingling sensation on his arm. He spun his hand, and sure enough, beside the glittering [Mark of Death] and the shifting flower of the [Sign Spring’s Asperiant], was a watercolor-looking circle made up of the five colors at his feet, bound by a ring of Murrcee’s pale tan.

“What is it with you guys and tattoos?” he whispered. The sign was not as dynamic as the other two, but the soft colored ball was by far the most comforting. It also meant that Joe now had two boosts he could use to break the fey curses: this new mark and the [Elixir of the Arch-Sage].

Letting his arm relax, Joe wondered how the new mark would affect Kenda’s bets. While it was definitely something, it didn’t feel like it rose to the level of another miracle.

He let those thoughts fall away for now and rejoined the chorus, thanking Murrcee for her benevolence.
