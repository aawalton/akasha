Though, it is definitely getting a lot harder to level. No more killing a single monster and getting three levels. Now I am lucky to get a single level from a dozen monsters several levels above me.

“You okay, bro?” Someone says and I turn to see the healer looking over me.

“I’ve seen better days.” I say, which is a complete understatement. Seven broken bones, far too many gashes over my body and so much blood loss that I am not sure how I am walking.

“Fair enough, that last fight was brutal. Barely scraped by with the tips of my nails.” He says and I feel one of his inspection skills run over me. “Damn man, how are you still conscious? You’ve got internal bleeding in eight places."

I laugh, and cough up a bit of blood in the process. After clearing my throat, I shrug. “I’ve got way too much vitality, I think.” Speaking of stats, mine have grown so much, and it has been a while since I checked them. So much so that I have forty six points banked up, which I split between endurance and strength. All of my stats are now above a hundred, and a couple hit the two hundred mark. It is weird to think that, at least numerically, I am like, ten times stronger than when I started. Of course, that doesn't exactly translate into reality. Realistically, it's probably more like five times stronger–in pure physical ability, anyways. Though I am sure that I am not making full use of all of my newfound power. Perhaps if I spent some time training, I would actually be able to realize that full increase.

I have noticed that most of my attacks and really everything I do lack technique. Not just in the actual hard system stuff, but just like, how I swing my hammer and such. Ever since I got the ability to see stamina, I have noticed that everyone seems to do it so smoothly.

For me, it is like I am forcing the energy through my body with sheer will. Which is most certainly not the way to do it. “Hey, you wouldn't happen to be willing to teach me how to properly manipulate stamina?” I ask Mark, who is tending to his own wounds not far away. “I can do it, but I am certain that I have got it all wrong.”

Mark looks at me like I am a talking goose. “What do you mean? You just do it. The more you do, the easier it gets I guess. Why do you think you are doing it wrong?”

“Hmm, well it feels like my entire body doesn't want to let the energy pass through I guess. Like I am just manhandling it, and it kinda hurts if I do it too much.”

“Interesting, don’t think I've ever heard of that problem.” Mark truly does seem confused. Just what am I getting wrong? “Most kids can do it easily enough, it's as natural as breathing.”

Oh, I see. That does make sense. I am not from this world, thus I did not grow up inherently using stamina for stuff. Everyone that was born in this world started off with stamina, mana, and all sorts of other stuff. So to them, not using stamina would be harder than using it.

Which is a sharp contrast with me. I instinctively do not use it, which must be why it is harder for me. Heck, it took me quite a while just to figure out how to swing my hammer properly, and there is no way I could use some of the fancier techniques I have seen people using.

My Anvil Strike is a weird technique from what I can tell. There is literally no finesse to it, it is purely smacking something as hard as I can. Which is fair, when one is trying to forge metals that really do not like to bend and such. But flesh isn't as hard to break, usually.

In other words, it is overkill. My new body knows this instinctively, at a fundamental level. Thus it is trying to resist me using stamina like it is just a force multiplier.

So I just need to learn to work with my body and stuff. Problem is, how the fuck does one do that? I will have to experiment eventually with it. But, the middle of combat is not the best time to be changing up my fighting style so drastically. So I shall add it to my ever growing list of ‘problems for later.’

I realize I spaced out a bit and go to respond to Mark, only to find everyone has gathered around a campfire. Seems I spaced out for so long that they just started eating without me. Which is fair enough, it is probably time for me to go separate ways anyways. I did get the sleep I wanted, after all.

The healer guy seems to have fixed me up pretty good, and my health is almost replenished from my nap. So I stand up and stretch, and then head back off into the city. I let Dovey out as well, and he does the whole scouting thing.
