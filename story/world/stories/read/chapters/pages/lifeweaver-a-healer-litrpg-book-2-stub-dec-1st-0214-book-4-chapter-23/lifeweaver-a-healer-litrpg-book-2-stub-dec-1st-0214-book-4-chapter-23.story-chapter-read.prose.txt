It was another trap, because of course it was. Dot had been placed in what amounted to a coffin, a room so small it just about fit three people lying on top of each other. The room was filled with gas and crackled with electricity that drained mana when touched. Spells on the walls reinforce them, and somewhere in there, more mana-draining items did their thing. The moment they appeared, Luke took it all in through Weaver's Perception, finding a large group of Singers surrounding the space. Dot was fine, placed into some sort of hibernation. All this was just as Luke had anticipated.

Knowing they were heading into a trap, they had taken precautions. In Luke's case, that meant drawing even more infernal mana into himself, along with his normal, untinted mana. Together, they filled him to bursting, and his meta-heart was brimming with healing mana. Appearing in that room zapped mana out of him, but far from all of it at once, and they hadn't found a way to drain infernal mana yet. Big mistake on their part. Extending threads in all directions, Luke used them to destroy the spells on the walls even as he broke down the walls themselves. Emerging in an enormous hall full of Singers, he released Smudge and pulled Ray out of the confined space.

"Let's go!" Luke roared, jumping off and into the closest group of Singers.

There had to be hundreds of them, but the hall wasn't crowded, and the high ceiling lent itself to maneuverability. Sensing incredible strength in some Singers in the crowd, and the way they'd all gathered, Luke figured this was the Singers' stand. They intended to beat him here and either kill him or take him prisoner again. A sword sliced into Luke's back, opening up a deep wound that healed itself before the blade had even finished its arc, flesh closing behind the metal. Ray appeared and killed the Singer with a well-placed cut across his throat, and Luke threw himself at the Singers in front, extending even more spellweaves. The trap had taken some of his mana, but there was plenty left, and with so many walking and talking batteries around, he was in no danger of running out.

Luke dodged under the swing of an axe and lashed out, going low. Infernal threads of mana wrapped around the axe-wielding Singer's legs. Activating Weaver's Whip was enough to take them off. The heat of the threads cauterized the stumps as Luke extended yet another thread, inserting it into the Singer's meta-heart. Moving on to the next pair of opponents, a female mage type and one who fought with katars, like Zelaui, he kept the thread in the first Singer, draining it of mana.

A katar thrust so fast he couldn't follow it opened up Luke's side, but he returned the favor with an elbow in the Singer's face and a thread around his neck. Twisting, the head didn't quite come off, but it was a near thing. Pushing off with threads of mana against the floor, he rocketed toward the mage, who cried out and raised her arms. A spellweave for a shield of some sort formed in front of her, but Luke cut through it and landed on her. When he stood, the mage Singer's meta-heart in his hand, he saw more and more of them coming, and they weren't about to strike at him one by one. Smudge and Ray were fighting too, taking some of the pressure off, but they needed to thin the herd a little if they didn't want to be overwhelmed. More Singers poured into the hall from doors at all four corners, adding to their numbers.

Judging the distance between the many Singers, Luke figured they were close enough. Healing the katar wound in his side while taking mana from those closest to him, he activated The Healer's Moment. This was going to be a long fight, perhaps even long enough that he would be able to use the skill twice. No use holding on to your best weapon and never using it. As time froze, he used another costly skill, Cascading Threads, and threads emerged from those connected to Luke, jumping into the Singers next to them, then again and again. With so many Singers present, it was a strain to keep it going, but he held on, convinced he needed to lessen their numbers to stand a chance. Some Singers were strong enough to push the thread invading them away, even with time frozen, but Luke just went around them, not bothering to force the issue. The stronger Singers would get extra attention later once their numbers had gone down. Infernal mana kept rising and attempting to follow along the threads, taking them over. Keeping them in check was a balancing act of pain and frustration, but he managed it in the end.

Having reached his limit and with time running out, Luke sliced and heaved. When time resumed, over a hundred Singers were dead on their feet. Panting, he watched them crumple to the floor, revealing those who'd been powerful enough to keep his threads at bay. They all looked at him, except for Smudge, who spun around in confused circles, all of his opponents having fallen. What Luke saw in their eyes was not anger or fear, but what he interpreted as grudging respect.

"Don't just stand there!" Ray shouted as he flung one of the many knives he'd gathered from dead enemies at a hulking female Singer, who, in turn, equipped a shield, blocked the attack, then unequipped it in one smooth motion. Seeing the same Singer charge toward him, Ray disappeared into stealth.

Luke: "Hey, we should probably get out of here."

Ray: "You think?"

Luke: "Well, yeah. We can't just stick around and fight forever."

Ray: "My tone doesn't carry well over messages. I meant to say was, 'ya thunk?!'"

Ray: "Obviously, we have to leave."

Luke: "Right."

Ray: "Can you, like, sense the portals with your spidey-skill?"

A short female Singer fired at Luke with something that looked an awful lot like an energy blaster gun, and the shots were too quick to even consider dodging. They burned through Luke's shoulder, stomach, and left knee like they were nothing and continued until they slammed into the opposite wall. As Luke fell to his remaining knee with a yelp of surprise, more than pain, two more Singers closed in, both wielding short swords in one hand and daggers in the other.

Luke: "Not when I'm almost dead."

Ready for their approach, Luke used Weaver's Renewal on himself to recover from the blasts, even as he pushed to his feet and leapt over their heads with the help of a set of threads of mana. It wasn't quite the flowing move he'd hoped, and one reached straight up with their dagger, burying it in Luke's gut. That's what you get when you try to be fancy.

Ray: "Let's get out of here first, then."

Ray: "What about Dot?"

Caught by the Singers, Luke endured several more cuts into his flesh, avoiding a couple by deflecting them with threads as he reached into them. Both Singers were strong enough to push back, but this time, he didn't need to focus on another hundred opponents. Asserting his will, gaining access to them was like pushing open a heavy door with his shoulder. Once inside, the fight was over. Skilled swordsmen they may be, but that was little help once a Lifeweaver had his claws in them. Healing himself with their mana while looting their weapons, Luke got to his feet. The nightshirt was in tatters, and he ripped it off and used the fabric to wipe his face before discarding it.

Luke: "Dot made her choices."

Ray: "They won't be very nice to her."

More shots from the female Singer followed, but this time Luke was ready for them. Judging the timing by having the attacks rip into his flesh turned him into a quick study, and Weavestep carried him forward, to the left, and out of harm's way. She fired again and again, Luke dodging his way toward Dot, irritation growing with every step. Smudge ran, avoiding attacks from several other Singers, and threw himself into a lunge that ended in the female Singer's throat. Shaking, he ripped her apart without making a sound.

The floor beneath Luke's feet exploded upward. It tore his feet apart and threw him forward and onto the box with Dot. Without time to do it right, he used Weaver's Renewal again to bring his feet back, wincing at the pain. Opening a portal for her to walk through was out of the question, and jumping with her would leave Ray alone and in danger he wouldn't walk away from. Another blast rocked the box to the side, and Dot fell out and on top of him, still unconscious. Just looking at her pissed him off, and in the end, he did something that he hadn't tried before. It was something he'd considered the possibility of. For obvious reasons, he hadn't been able to find the time to test his theory, not until that moment. No matter what, it was best to remove Dot from the possibility of Relian taking over her.

Luke grabbed Dot with a thread of mana and more or less forced the weave to appear through will alone. The weave looked fuzzy and out of focus, and almost seemed insulted at the way he approached it, but, like he'd told Ray, this wasn't the time for meditation. Instead, he reached for an echo thread connecting Dot to another person on Earth. That done, he... well... 'threw her' wasn't quite the right way of looking at it, but it was the best description he could think of. Pulling her from the physical realm and into the metaphysical was like flexing a muscle that hadn't seen enough use, and the weave protested by breaking his insides. Still, Dot went, and he thought she would end up on Earth. It was either that or she'd stay floating in the weave forever.

Not having to look at her or consider her safety was a step in the right direction, and Luke got up, slicing through an ice spell to destroy it before it could land. Ice exploded downward rather than in his face, and he winced as he stepped on an already melting piece. First, they'd taken his robe and shirt, and now his shoes? Unforgivable.
