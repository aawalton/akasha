Ferris, after his third consecutive night of staying up late, was not looking forward to another full day of refining spirit gems. He had to drag himself out of bed that morning.

Jade, on the other hand, was as chipper as ever, excited for breakfast. The slime did sleep, but Ferris wondered if Jade needed as much as he did. Slime biology was… weird.

Breakfast was a large yellow tart fruit, sliced in half and drizzled with a sweet white sauce. The inside of the fruit was soft enough to scoop out with a spoon, and the rind acted as a natural bowl. Jade helped clean up again, this time simply eating everyone’s ‘bowl’ whole.

At the Ox building, Ferris was presented with a cloudy blue gem to refine first. He grabbed his bone spear, but decided to leave the crossbow behind, as it would be awkward to carry more than one weapon while using the pickaxe.

Inside the refinement device, he had to slog through the water alone once again, Jade waiting on the wall above. It was slightly frustrating each time he had to search around in the water picking up the black rock pollution. The work was much easier with Jade eating everything that fell.

The morning passed in a haze, after the blue gem was finished Ferris and Jade worked on a yellow.

For their lunch, Mother Lily had ground up the meat of some kind of beast, mixed it with chopped greens and a honey sauce, then steamed it all in a bun. It was filling, and Jade loved it, but a bit sweet for his taste. As he ate, Ferris considered how to start building the Assimilate Object ability. He knew it wouldn’t be quick or easy—he’d been warned his first couple of abilities could take months to construct.

He would need a clear space in the slimespace for it. Eventually, he imagined it as a mighty fortress, so he wanted plenty of room around it. Most of the slimespace had the yellow spires in the way…

After today, Ferris would have six merit points with Clan Hollowfield. He needed twelve for Acid Resistance, but he could use six for one of the jade slips on spiritual techniques that Gilbert at the library had recommended. Mental Materials would probably help him with large construction, like Assimilate Object needed.

But… Ferris was unable to delve with just one leg. Learning Acid Reconstruction would likely take far longer than anything else, so he wanted to prioritize starting progress towards the healing ability. That meant purchasing Acid Resistance first, and getting to work studying Jade’s healing with Serena. His next appointment with her was tomorrow, and they should finally be able to start.

Constructing the Acid Resistance ability might also need information from the basic technical manuals Gilbert recommended, but that was the beauty of having Serena’s help. He could just ask her if he got stuck.

With the merit points unavailable, Ferris was left planning how to build Assimilate Object without help. It would need some material for the frame’s structure, a rope construct, a lot of qi, and an object representing the artificial organ—both in the real world and the soulspace.

Finished with lunch, Ferris and Jade started on their third gem of the day: a cloudy red. Inside the refinement device, the spirit gem made it as hot as ever. Watching Jade bounce around merrily somehow made the sweltering heat worse.

About a third of the way through the red spirit gem, Ferris was taking a break when he felt a spike of excitement from Jade. The slime had been rolling around, meticulously mapping out the area around Ferris, when they found something. Jade, as usual upon encountering anything new, started to consume it. But then, it started moving!

There was a high-pitched screech. As if triggering an ambush, movement burst all around Ferris. Small red lizards—about half the size of Jade—jumped out of hiding, running around on top of the crystals surrounding them. Jade had caught one and was struggling to keep it from escaping.

Ferris had set the pickaxe down to rest, so his arms were free. He pulled the bone spear from where he had strapped it to his back as three of the red lizards started climbing up his legs. They had sharp claws that sliced through his clothes and pierced his skin as they scurried up.

He tried to throw them off by twisting around, but they clung too tightly. One bit his neck, a row of sharp teeth digging in painfully, while the other two crawled over his chest. He dropped the spear, in pain and desperation. Screaming, he grabbed the one on his neck with both hands and ripped it off, its teeth tearing small gashes in his skin as he broke it free.

Ferris squeezed the beast as hard as he could, until he felt something break inside it. He reached for the next one. While he was struggling to get a good grip, two more crawled up his legs. He finally got a good hold on his target, crushing it like he had the first.

For each lizard Ferris could get ahold of, more hopped on. Individually, they couldn’t do much damage unless they got their teeth latched in, so Ferris started guarding his neck. The beasts seemed to go for it instinctively.

Eventually he fell over, and the fight continued on the ground—kicking and screaming. Ferris wasn’t sure how long this lasted. The struggle was chaotic and painful, rolling around in the dirt. Then, he went to grab the next one, only to find there weren’t any more.

Getting back on his feet, Ferris looked around. There were still some red shapes running around atop and behind the crystals around them, but Jade was rolling protectively in front of him. The slime was currently consuming one while circling Ferris in a defensive pattern.

Over the bond, Jade told Ferris what happened. Jade hadn’t been able to see, as Ferris wasn’t watching, but they knew his location to come help.

Apparently, the small lizards were unable to reach Jade’s core, giving the slime a large advantage. The small, sharp claws and piercing teeth did nothing to Jade, although they were much faster than the slime. Jade had taken to defending Ferris, grabbing any lizard they could. Many had gotten by anyway, but not enough to kill him.

Ferris quickly picked up his bone spear and checked his wounds. His clothes were torn to shreds, and he had a thousand tiny punctures in his skin from the lizard’s claws. Worst of all were the gashes from where he had to pull their teeth out, although thankfully there were fewer of those. He was bleeding pretty badly, but was still able to move.

The small red lizards were fast, but apparently liked to stay around the red crystals. One of them darted out, only for Jade to roll up to meet it. The thing screeched and easily avoided the slime, running back out of sight.

On guard, Ferris quickly grabbed the pickaxe and retreated. Ferris and Jade kept moving until they were back on top of the wall. Only then did Ferris take some time to try to stop the worst of his bleeding.

His shirt was barely hanging on. Ferris would like to say he used it as bandages, but it was closer to a ball of rags he pressed up against his neck until the worst of the bleeding stopped. Thankfully, the cuts weren’t deep, just numerous.

After catching his breath, Ferris and Jade left through the gate of the refinement device, waking up back in the room at the Ox building. His mental body would heal faster with Ferris not cultivating, and even better if he could sleep.

The lizards had all attacked together, but Ferris wasn’t sure if they had been intentionally waiting in ambush. They might’ve just reacted when Jade found one. Either way, they made this gem much more difficult to refine, and proved Theodore’s warning to be true.

Individually, they weren’t much of a threat. Even as a swarm, they hadn’t been able to hurt Ferris enough to kill him. Thankfully, their jaws didn’t seem strong enough to rip chunks out of him. Maybe if they had more time, but Jade had helped scare off the horde.

His spear hadn’t helped at all once they got on him, reminding Ferris that he still needed to make a backup weapon. Some armor would’ve helped as well. It was just so hard to find the qi to spare for it…

Jade had also shown the advantage that slimes have over anything smaller. This was why the Grow ability could be so good. For instance, if Jade was larger than the frog demons the slime could take on any number of them. Enemies with longer reach like the chain demons would still be a problem. Ferris wasn’t swayed from focusing on Assimilate Object first, but he did look forward to Grow in the future.

Ferris and Jade spent about an hour recovering before returning to the refinement device. When they walked through the gate, Ferris checked his wounds. They had all scabbed over, and it hurt to stretch the skin as he moved. His shirt was still gone, but his ragged pants were better than nothing.

Ferris grabbed the pickaxe, his bone spear, and the sack before heading back down the wall. Previously, he had been leaving the sack behind, as Jade was disposing of the black rock for him, but now he wanted to collect any corpses he could.

Carefully, they approached where the red lizards had appeared. The area had calmed down, and Ferris wasn’t able to see any movement from afar. Curiously, he saw the corpses still lying on the ground. In the soulspace, the bodies would be long gone—likely taken by other demons. This place existed spiritually like the soulspace, but there was a difference in feel. Ferris’ soul was intrinsically tied to his life, while the refinement device was missing that spark.

Circling around, Ferris spotted one of the lizards, hiding on the side of a crystal. It blended in, easy to miss if you weren’t looking for it.

Raising his spear, Ferris made a quick jab, skewering it against the crystal. The lizard made a high-pitched screech as it died, and several more of the things moved around nearby. Thankfully, the lizards weren’t as numerous as before, and Ferris was able to back away from them before any crawled up onto him.

Slowly, Ferris and Jade picked off as many of the lizards as they could find. They collected the bodies in the sack—at least the ones that Jade didn’t eat. It took a while, but they swept through the entire formation of crystals until they couldn’t find any more. Most of the lizards had been in the same area, but they did find a couple further out.

After clearing out as many as possible, Ferris and Jade had to return to refining the gem. It went slower, and a couple more lizards surprised them, but individually the beasts weren’t much of a threat. Ferris ached all over, but was able to push through it.

After the gem was cleared, Ferris made a quick trip to drop off the red lizard corpses at his Heartfire before getting his final gem for the day. It had taken twice as long as usual to refine the red gem, so he definitely wouldn’t finish work early.

As Ferris turned the clear red gem into the attendant, the man glanced at him briefly, but then returned to recording the gem and retrieving a new one. It was just a glance… but Ferris wondered if the man had known there were creatures in the red gem. Was he waiting to see if Ferris would say he couldn’t refine the last gem today? Or maybe he just noticed this one had taken longer than usual.

Either way, the attendant soon returned with a cloudy, vivid green gem. Inside the refinement device, Ferris and Jade found the same grassy field growing up around the crystals. Jade’s insistence on eating away every blade of grass worked in Ferris’ favor. It was slow, but clearing the grass prevented anything from sneaking up on them while they worked. In the end, they refined the green gem without any issue.

Sore and exhausted, Ferris carried Jade back to Lily House. He was happy to be done with the work for the week. He didn’t mind so much, but couldn’t help but think that it was three full days of hard labor he could have spent on his own soulspace, instead of for Hollowfield. The merit points were nice, giving access to techniques and information that would speed up his cultivation… but he would rather purchase them with resources from delving.

At Lily House, they arrived just in time for dinner. It was back to bird meat, skewered alongside peppers and roasted. In contrast to the usual jovial mood, tonight was decidedly unpleasant.

“I just feel like they are making everything harder on purpose,” Brix complained, tearing a piece of meat off her skewer, “Cyrus’ test was so much easier.”

“Cyrus is bonded to a book, one of the most well studied and understood bonds there is,” Karl replied, a bit helplessly. “They know the power and breadth of his abilities. He has all of the required utility to participate and support the group.”

Brix and Cyrus had tested to join Hollowfield clan delving groups today. Apparently, Cyrus had passed, but Brix had failed.

“What happened?” Ferris asked. Yesterday, Brix had been quite confident.

Brix growled a little, but answered the question.

“I passed the rift magic and obstacle course with no problems. My defensive ability was rated as acceptable, but they failed me on offence. Which is stupid, I can reduce a building to ash.” Brix punctuated the last with a fist slamming into the table.

Cyrus sighed, and added, “Yes, but that ability is overkill for anything smaller. They want something that you can use more often, with less damage.”

“The problem,” Brix lifted her metal box of a bonded weapon up onto the table, “is that once I start pumping out the concoction, it all has to go. I have a plan to change the ability to be able to release it in short bursts, keeping the alchemical process going…” Brix looked up, hopelessly, “but I need more resources first, which I need to delve for, which I can’t do until I pass this stupid test.” She slammed her fist into the table with each of the last three words.

“What did Elder Tarr say?” Ferris asked, but then received a glare from Brix and a smile from Cyrus. The Elder had agreed to help mentor Brix. If she were stuck, he should be able to suggest a way forward. Ferris’ contract with Elder Harper didn’t include any such mentorship, as he preferred to rely on his uncle. Brix was the only Adept in her family, so it worked out better for her. Their retainer contracts each had different advantages, and likely different commitments attached.

“Elder Tarr,” Brix wasn’t quite as respectful with the man’s name as she should be, “wants me to focus on learning alchemy. He thinks I should spend months saving up the merit to purchase resources to expand my ability.”

“Back home,” Ferris said, a bit unsure, “new cultivators are encouraged to delve as soon as possible.”

“Exactly!” Brix said, pointing at Ferris. “That’s how it should be.”

Karl shook his head. “The risk of death for new delvers is staggering. Clan Hollowfield has a large enough support structure to ease its members in gently, and the clan’s track record speaks for itself.”

“That’s why this is stupid,” Brix said, “they don’t need to protect me. They shouldn’t get in my way. The examiner agreed my attack is powerful, just costly to use for every fight. Why can’t they just let me deal with the price?”

Ferris hesitated, not quite as ready to dismiss Karl’s words. They felt… uncomfortable.

He could see how Brix focusing on alchemy would help her, but wasn’t it slowing down her advancement? Delving gave benefits in the soul that would accelerate everything else she did, all the stories agreed. Everyone back home talked about how important it was.

Ferris’ own work refining spirit gems wasn’t giving him anything other than merit. He wouldn’t call it ‘easing into delving,’ as Karl had said.

Rifts were dangerous, but so were cultivators. Brix’s ability sounded deadly. Why should the clan care if it was inefficient? It would get Brix into the rifts, fighting. Growing.

I don’t understand why they’d hold her back, he thought. It’s not like Brix is like… Ferris scowled. Like me.

Karl didn’t press the point, appearing to decide it wasn’t worth arguing. Brix took the rest of her anger out on the food, then stormed off.

Cyrus… well, Ferris wasn’t able to read him. Was he happy he’d passed? Sad his friend hadn’t? Both? Whatever it was, his expression had turned neutral.

Ferris finished his food, then grabbed Jade to make a retreat from the meal. However, Mother Lily pulled him aside on his way out.

“Ferris, dear, some men dropped off your delivery today.”

This perked up his attitude. It must be the extra food he’d ordered for Jade.

It was time to give the qi production a boost.
