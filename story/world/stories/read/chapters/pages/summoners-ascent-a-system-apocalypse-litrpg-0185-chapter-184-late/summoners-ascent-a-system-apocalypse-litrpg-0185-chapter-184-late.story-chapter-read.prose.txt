Ashton turned one more time as he walked down the road, waving at Clementine. Well, he was too far away to see if she was really still standing there, but he figured she was, knowing her.

The conversation they had just now went into a lot of detail. Clementine had always been a little intense whenever she got curious about something, and once Ashton gave her the green light, she really didn't hesitate at all anymore. Even if it was a topic that way maybe a little awkward at first considering their... history.

But still, in a really weird way, it was nice to just openly talk about his past like that. It was the same when he spoke to Sam the other day. Even though it was weird, and he didn't want to think too deeply about details of his relationships with those in Lumia, it was nice to reminisce. To think about the parts that made things bearable, and at times even... sweet. And especially after talking to the Mask Maker spirit, and now that he was mostly free from terrible reign of the Summoner, it was weirdly nice to reminisce at times.

Yet, for now, the past wasn't what Ashton should be focusing on. He should be facing forward, at the 'now' and that which was yet to come. He still had plenty of time to get lost in his memories in the future. It wasn't like his memories were something he had to work hard to protect.

Ashton started back up into a fairy-magic enhanced run. Just like before, there were more of the strengthened monsters, but now that it was just around sundown, the nocturnal ones were starting to come out as well. There was definitely a much greater variety of those at this point.

Ashton continued to take those down, trying to collect more information on the new guys that were spread through town. He of course tagged them all with the Pen of Insight as well. There was a lot that the construct would have to jot down later. Hopefully it was enough to finally push it over the edge to level 10.

After a while, without too many issues, Ashton made it back to the gates of the primary school.

Just like the last time he was here, they had been vastly improved. These were seriously just solid wooden structures that felt hard to break down even for Ashton.

Once again, Ashton knocked on the heavy gate.

"Who's there?" Someone asked through a thin slit in the door. It was barely enough to peek through.

"Ashton Garling. Open up."

"Are you here to see one of the children?"

Ashton raised a brow, confused. "Dude, are you new here? I'm the guy that–" he stopped himself, slightly grumbling. "Yes, I'm here to see a child. Millicent Lacy. I'm her guardian."

The man on the other side of the door hesitated. "Millicent Lacy..." He seemed to be flipping through something, before soon slamming that same thing shut. "You're not in the book."

"Excuse me?"

"You're not in the book, you're not listed as a relative of Millicent. Please come back with proof that you–"

"Jesus fucking christ..." Ashton groaned loudly, interrupting the man. He sighed lightly and took a step back.

There were multiple along the gate and walls for people to look through at different vantage points, and they were just wide enough. Ashton positioned himself right and then threw his barbell toward one of the slits like a spear, and lodged it right in there.

"Wh-What are you–" the man stuttered. Ashton would have rolled his eyes if he could.

He ran up and jumped against the door, kicking himself off it to reach the barbell. Pulling himself up, Ashton swung over and forward with the right momentum and tore the staff back out of the slit, leaping high enough that he could easily hold onto the spiked top edge of the wooden wall. But well, there were plenty of places for him to just push his fingers between without hurting himself.

With one arm, Ashton pulled himself over and soon landed on the other side of the gate.

"Won't even add me to the fucking guest list after saving this place multiple times, fucking hell," Ashton whispered out a slight grumble as he walked forward. But soon, the gatekeeper ran up to him with a weapon, a simple spear, in hand that he pointed at Ashton's face.

"What do you think you're doing?"

With a sigh, Ashton walked past the man. "Don't embarrass yourself."

"H-Hey, I told you to stop!" nervous, the young man rushed forward, before a voice yelled out from outside where Ashton could see.

"Lad! You're finally back! Your friend came by the other day and said you didn't show any signs of waking up, but who'd have thought that you would oversleep by this much, ya lazy fuck." Rory approached as he laughed into his beard. As he came closer, he did look at the gatekeeper though, raising his brow confused. "What's going on? Why are you pointing that at Ashton?"

The gatekeeper was confused. "I, uh... He wasn't on the list, and, uhm..."

Rory let out a long sigh. "Right, sorry. That would be my fault, then," he said, in a tone that let Ashton know that it very much was not in fact Rory's fault. "This 'ere is Ashton. Do not ever stop him from coming in here, alright?"

The gatekeeper slowly nodded his head. "Right... sorry, I uhm... sorry..."

With his tail between his legs, he turned and walked over to the gate, as Ashton was pushed by his back away from here. "What was that about?" He asked.

"Don't even get me started, lad... While ye were sleeping, some folk from the university came back. This time, they were the right folk for the job. Almost all the kids decided to join their siblings, beside, well..."

"Joey."

"Mm. And when her sister heard that, she got very pissy. We were fightin' about it for a while... Oh, uh, verbally I mean. Not like you do, lad."

"What's with the sudden dig at me?"

"Hm?"

"Don't worry about it. So Joey didn't want to go, her sister got mad. What does that have to do with that kid over there acting as the guard?"

Rory began to grumble. "When it was clear that Joey wouldn't be joining her sister, they went a different route. They said that if Joey didn't want to come, this place should be protected 'as well as possible'. The next day, here we suddenly have a bunch of 'em, these guys that they were clearly trying to pawn off on us. They're kind of..."

Ashton thought back to the spear that the gatekeeper was holding. It wasn't all that high quality. At least, it wasn't the sort of item that someone that put actual effort into levelling up would have.

"Useless?"

"Your words, not mine. But... yes. We're trying our best to find a place for them, but it's not easy. Joey's sister convinced all the others that it was a good idea to have these guys here, so I can't kick them out to go back to the university on my own, either."

Letting out a long sigh, Ashton made a quick guess. "So basically, they're trying to mess with you guys so that you give in and actually hand over Joey to them?"

"Hm? No, no... probably not, right?" Rory replied, not having seen it from that angle at all before now. Though, after thinking about it for a few moments, his expression didn't seem like he could completely dismiss that idea.

"Well, whatever. Where's Millie? I want to apologise to her for being late before we talk about anything else."

"About that, lad... ya know, the book that the kid at the gate had was something where we wrote down all the names of known relatives of the kids. Just to filter people out a bit more easily. We originally had you written down for Millie, but..."

"She was mad at me for being gone so long and had me struck from the record again?"

Rory nodded. "That about sums it up."

Ashton stopped and thought for a second. He figured that she might be angry at him, but to this degree?

"Well, where is she, then? I need to talk to her."

"It's dinner, so I hope she'll be in the gym."

The way Rory said that didn't exactly fill Ashton with confidence that she would be there, but it was still the first place they should look. That trio was known for acting out a lot anyway, so it wouldn't be surprising if they were either gone, or some kind of trouble was centred around them within the gym.

As they walked through the open doors into the gymnasium, where now lots of fold-up tables had been set up so people could eat, it was clear that things had gone the way of the latter of those two options.

"Sean! How could you?!" one of the teachers yelled out. Ashton remembered her voice, she was one of the ones that was whispering about being scared of him the last time he was here. "You can't do this kind of thing!"

"Argh, I didn't even use a skill! And he's the one that started it!" Sean barked back. "He's been messing with us for days!"

"That doesn't mean you can just hit him, Sean! Come with me!"

Despite being in the middle of the gym, the yelling was continuing without wanting to slow down. Clearly, while Ashton was gone, things had kept escalating in the worst direction. No wonder Rory had that look on his face right now. He had been here as the school's custodian, not as an educator. Knowing how to deal with children in this sort of situation was hard. Then again, nobody knew how to help children through the end of the world.

That was with the exception of Ashton.

He walked forward through the gym, and it didn't take long for people to notice him, and the room became more quiet beside the yelling that was still happening around a particular table.

There was a child being held by a parent, holding a tissue to his nose to deal with a nosebleed. A few steps behind Sean, though, Joey was cleaning up some shards from a plate that had been broken. Next to her, Millie was just sinking into her seat, as if she was hoping to go invisible if she just kept going with this.

She was covered in what were clearly the remains of her dinner, from head to toe. And while that teacher was busy yelling at Sean, Millie was left to deal with that mess on her own.

Ashton walked past that kid with the nosebleed, and even the teacher and Sean. Though, he did ruffle up Sean's hair a bit. Ashton was happy that he defended Millie like that, even if it was maybe a little aggressive. At least he didn't use a skill, if his words could be believed.

Instead, the person that Ashton aimed for was Millie.

He squatted down next to her and pushed the table a bit to the side, before starting to clean her up. He put the chunks of vegetables stuck in the folds of her clothes into his hand, and used his cloak to wipe up some of the mess on her arms and face, and to hopefully dry her clothes a little.

"Are you hurt?" Ashton asked, but Millie just shook her head.

But as she did, Poppy jumped over to her shoulder and pointed at Millie's cheek. Concentrating his channelling range as much as he could, Ashton tried to inspect her face more closely. There was some blood dripping from a small cut on her cheek.

Ashton pushed his hand into one of his cloak's pockets, where he was keeping some really simple first aid supplies for situations where the Backpacker couldn't come out.

He cleaned her cheek a bit more and then put a band-aid on her.

By that point, the room had really grown dead silent. Ashton looked at Millie and smiled.

"I'm sorry I'm late."
