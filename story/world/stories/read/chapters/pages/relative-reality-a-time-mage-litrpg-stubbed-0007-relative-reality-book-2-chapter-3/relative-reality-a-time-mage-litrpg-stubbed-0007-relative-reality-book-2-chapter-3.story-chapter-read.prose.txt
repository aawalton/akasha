Time fled quickly before Rowan, even Relativity unable to entirely stop its endless march forwards. Before he knew it, the day of the tournament had arrived.

The large arena on the Birkston Adventurer Academy grounds that usually served as the training area for all of the adventurers in the academy had been closed for a few days while renovations had been going on. Rowan wasn’t sure why they had been undergoing renovations when the tournament was to be hosted within the illusory chamber, but that had also been closed off for testing during this time.

That meant that Rowan and his team had mostly practiced on one of the lawns, which had begun attracting small groups of early training academy go-ers. Rowan wasn’t particularly fond of being gawked at, but neither did he let it distract him.

All of that was in the past now however, as Rowan and his team stood before the doors to the illusory chamber.

Of the original thirteen different teams in their year, that number had reduced down to ten in total. This was partially due to the joining of both Melody and Elise to Rowan, Helja and Arne, but had also been caused by the leaving of a number of students due to Dawn’s cleansing of the nobility. Two teams had needed to join up, forming a single, larger team that now stood a much better chance of winning.

Teams were paired off in the first stage of the tournament, with five separate direct combats to take place. After that, the five remaining teams would engage in a timed test of skills. The first place team from that competition would move straight to the next stage, with the four remaining teams engaging in yet another one on one direct combat. The last three remaining teams would then fight in a three team combat, with the first team with all members incapacitated coming third, then the next being second and finally, the only remaining team coming first.

All of that was to take place in a single day.

It was to be an utterly exhausting day, with the team who came first in the timed test having a significant advantage. In that same timed test, the team that placed fifth would then have to fight the team who placed second in the next stage. It was here that Helja was looking to find an easy advantage.

“If we just give up the timed test to come fifth, we’ll be just as rested as the first team that has spent a significant amount of energy to come first!” Helja said.

“But we will be give up a test. It will look not good for any who watch,” Arne rebutted.

“Or, they will see our intelligence in the strategy.”

“Or lacking spirit. There is no honour in this way.”

“Honour means nothing.”

“Maybe,” Melody carefully said, ”we should consider a strategy somewhere in the middle? Aim for fourth, so that we only need to fight the third team. That way we can conserve energy on the timed challenge, and still have an easier time on the next? Also, we won’t look like we’re trying to cheat the system.”

“And why not should we aim for placing first? In this way we will maintain both honour, and gain the rest over the others,” Arne said.

Helja rolled her eyes.

“Because it is a timed test, Arne. We are not the fastest team out there, we have a slow build up.”

“... Why not?” Rowan asked.

“Why not what?”

“Why are we not the fastest team?”

“Because we rely a lot on Melody’s slowly building power to achieve our end goals.”

Rowan hummed in thought.

“Okay, what if we didn’t?”

“If we didn’t rely on Melody’s abilities as a be all end all for our tactics… I guess we could be fast.”

“Then let’s keep that in mind. Right now, I think we need to make it through the first round.”

The first two teams to fight were Felir’s team versus one that Rowan hadn’t really had much interaction with. He recognised the members, but couldn’t recall their names.

It became immediately obvious why there had been renovations going on in both the illusory chamber and training arena over the course of the past week. The illusory chamber had been expanded to have a much larger number of alcoves to accommodate all members from at least five separate teams at once. In addition, the small space with a viewing screen had been expanded as well, allowing for a large number of people to watch the ongoing fights. It was in this space that Rowan and his team sat.

Both Felir and his father, Minister Enon, had somehow managed to avoid Dawn’s cleansing nova so far. Instead of pulling out of his training to go help his father with his work as one of the city’s nobility, Felir had doubled down on the adventuring life. Rowan hated to admit it, but the guy had obviously put in a hell of a lot of work. His team had been training with him as a weight up until recently, and with Felir’s renewed efforts, they were suddenly free from that weight and stronger than ever. This became evident when they beat down the opposing team in minutes.

The team Rowan was worried about facing, led by Hendrik, went next. They were thankfully not fighting them, and Rowan watched as they calmly and slowly waited out their enemy in an unbreakable defensive shell. In opposition to the previous fight’s quick and explosive ending, this fight took a long, long time. Nothing really happened as Hendrik and his team grew stronger. In fact, the start of the fight was the most eventful. Soon enough, their opponents couldn’t hurt them at all.

This trend continued up until the moment Hendrik and his team broke their defensive shell. Their opponents went from a full out assault into a desperate retreat immediately, losing within seconds.

That was the kind of tactic that Rowan and his team were not looking forward to facing. The sheer defensive capability of the team followed by the savage assault they unleashed would overwhelm even Arne’s defensive prowess.

“Did you see that?” Helja whispered excitedly.

“Yeah, looked terrifying to fight. Why?” Rowan asked.

“I think there is a weakness to their strategy. Have you noticed that they don’t really appear to have many defensive powers for a team that has that as their central strategy? The amount of powerful attacks they have is inconsistent to what we’re seeing.”

“I don’t see where this is going.”

“It’s one ability, probably belonging to Hendrik if I am seeing right. They also tend to break their defensive strategy after the same amount of time every single fight. I noticed that during the emergence, but seeing it here has solidified it in my mind.”

Rowan hummed.

“So the ability is on a timer? They can’t stay in there forever. That doesn’t change the fact that they come out as strong as ever.”

“Yes, but have you noticed that how powerful they are when they finally do break out seems to be directly comparable to how strong the thing attacking them is?”

Rowan’s eyes widened.

“They’re gaining strength from absorbing attacks somehow. The stronger their enemy, the stronger they become.”

Helja nodded emphatically.

“Which means all we need to do to fight them is…”

“Nothing,” Rowan finished.

The time for Rowan’s team to fight came soon enough. Their opponent was a middling team consisting of four members, one less than Rowan’s own team. A numbers advantage was good, but had also been taken into account by the Academy. The Illusory Chamber usually created an exact one-to-one replica illusion of the person in the alcoves. For this tournament, teams with less members than their opposition received an increase to their strength for the test. It was small, in this case an increase of about twenty-five percent to make up for the one less member they had.

This team had two people who fought with swords, Dagen and Rorik, and two healer types. One of them, Soril, was focused more on the cleansing of conditions like Rowan’s ability Titan’s Burden and Elise’s curses. The other, Vin, was closer to the traditional type of healer.

For some teams this matchup may have been a bigger issue, but not so for Rowan and his team.

The match began with a call from the Adjuticator. Elise vanished into the shadows while Rowan sped up and dashed to the side. Arne stood front and centre before Dagen and Rorik, while Helja and Melody set up behind him. Melody began her Song of Power, immediately breaking it and leaving behind an Afterimage to begin her next song. Helja began drawing out the ritual magic to cast Tornado while Arne focused on spreading out roots from his feet.

Rowan’s job was to try get around the swordsmen to get to the healers. If they were to win this fight, one of the two would need to be taken out first. Noticing Rowan’s movement, Rorik pulled back and positioned himself between Rowan and the two healers. Melody broke her next song, the Song of Peace, and began the Song of Mending. With their setup finished, Helja began her plan by using Gasping Breath on Rorik.

Gasping Breath - Mind - Affliction - Stone of the Ocean

Tier 1 Effect:

You may attempt to pull the air from the body of an enemy. They may resist, initiating a struggle of the Mind facet. Constant concentration is required to maintain this effect.

Tier 2 Effect:

While the struggle of the Mind facet continues, the enemy's body is slowly drained of air.

Tier 3 Effect:

This effect additionally drains a very small amount of mana from the target, granting that mana to you.

Seeing his opponent stumble, Rowan followed her up by accelerating with Relativity to reach Rorik near instantly. He grabbed Rorik’s arm, twisted behind him and began channelling Titan’s Burden into the man.

Rowan might have been quick, but so was Soril. Noticing that they had begun assaulting Rorik with debuffs, she began to cleanse them one by one. Helja and Rowan both continued to lay on the abilities, though they disappeared as quickly as they arrived.

Their plan hinged around Soril remaining focused on Rorik, as while all this had been happening, Elise had used Soul Curse on Vin.

Soul Curse - Body, Soul - Affliction - Triggered Ability - Stone of Corruption

Tier 1 Effect:

You may curse a target, causing a portion of damage dealt by your next attack to be copied against the targets soul, after which the curse is dispelled

Tier 2 Effect:

This ability is now triggered by your next two attacks before being dispelled

Tier 3 Effect:

This curse becomes undetectable.

As Rorik struggled to break free from the bombardment of afflictions, Arne and Dagen were squaring off. With the help of Vin, Arne could not harm Dagen, but neither could he hurt Arne. They were locked in an intense combat, sword clashing against root as Helja continued to create the complex ritual that would cast Tornado.

The battle descended into an immediate lockdown, as Rorik tried to get out of Rowan’s hold and Dagen tried to get through Arne to attack Melody and Helja. Rowan’s mana steadily drained as Rorik pulled away, his effort made even harder by Melody’s Song of Peace. The strength and healing granted by her other songs continued to grow steadily more powerful, and Rowan had an easier time holding Rorik still. Unfortunately, his mana ran out first. Or so he let Rorik think.

Rorik pulled away from Rowan and used his spinning backstep to bring his sword down in a savage slash that Rowan warded away with his forearm guard. Helja unleashed her Tornado at the same time that Vin, who also used a similar form of rune magic to Helja, let out a massive healing spell to counteract it. Melody used Afterimage Consumption on both of her Afterimages at the same moment, releasing a powerful burst of each effect. Elise, with her power massively increased, appeared before Vin at the moment she cast the healing spell. With two quick strikes that each punched through some kind of magical barrier, one to the heart and the other up through the jaw, Vin dropped dead courtesy of Elise’s Shadow Dagger.

Shadow Dagger - Body - Summoned Item - Stone of Death

Tier 1 Effect:

You may summon a dagger which enhances the effects of your triggered abilities

Tier 2 Effect:

Abilities triggered using this weapon have increased effect depending on how long they have been active before being triggered.

Tier 3 Effect:

Abilities triggered using this weapon have significantly increased effect if triggered at the moment the target casts or uses an ability. This effect scales based on the amount of mana being used by the ability being cast by the target.

With her Soul Curse empowered by Melody’s song and all three tiers of effects of her own dagger, strengthened again by the time the team had spent delaying while it built in power, Elise all but ended the fight right there.With the continued healing from Melody’s Song of Mending and Soril running low on mana due to cleansing Titan’s Burden and Gasping Breath, the fight came to a quick end after that. Helja’s Tornado empowered by the temporary burst of power and turned into a fiery nightmare by The Dawn Eagle melted away the other members.

As the illusion of the battle melted around them and reality asserted itself once more, Rowan couldn’t help but think, not for the first time, that Elise was utterly terrifying.
