The chaddlings were one of the drone’s most impressive creations.  The little acorn-like soldiers were sturdy, flexible, and were each around the equivalent power to a low-tier D Grade that had freshly evolved.  When factoring in their ability to field nearly a hundred at a time, that meant Kyle was walking around with a miniature military at all times.  Unfortunately, they were simply lacking when it came to the battlefields Kyle now found himself in.  Their primary use had been relegated to support functions, such as providing a forward line for Storm Shelter or keeping weaker foes away from the fight.

Now, between Graft and Aspect of the Tree: Branch, that didn’t have to remain the case.  They’d talked about making upgrades ever since he’d first reached C Grade.  It was finally time to put the talk into practice.

“C.H.A.D.D., what seeds do we need to have ready as component parts?”

[THAT ENTIRELY DEPENDS ON WHAT TYPE OF UPGRADES YOU’VE DECIDED ON.]

“Let’s stick with the basics for now.  Using the existing models as a template, let’s focus on increasing front line fighting capabilities.  More durability and offensive power, with increased mobility if we can afford it.”

Kyle felt a sense of approval come through Pack Leader’s Instinct, and the drone quickly provided a list of the different seeds it used in the standard chaddling’s creation.  Several of these had already been enhanced with Graft, but many of the internal components, such as the waxy leaves that acted as the circulatory system, needed to be upgraded.

That ultimately ended up being the simplest starting place.  Kyle used Cultivate within the World Tree Seed to create hundreds of the needed seeds, then pushed power into all of them with Infuse.  Many simply crumbled, while those that didn’t were set aside to improve with Graft before they destabilized, while the dust from the rest were used as fertilizer for the next batch.  Surprisingly, Phaegos appeared to be quite interested in the process.  Kyle felt the entity’s attention throughout each step, observing closely as the bulk of the seeds reached a point of failure.

“If you have ideas to improve the process, I’m open to them,” Kyle said, trying not to be distracted by the presence floating over his metaphysical shoulder.

Don’t mind me, Phaegos sent to Kyle.  I find this to be quite… educational.

“How so?”  Kyle asked with a grunt of annoyance as the next entire batch of seeds crumbled, causing him to activate Cultivate again.

This method shares many similarities with aspects of the forebear’s nature, Phaegos replied after some deliberation.  Seeds are planted, and the strongest ascend while the weaker sustain their paths.  All so that beings at the apex can be raised up.

“For somebody who just gained sapience a few years ago, you seem awfully informed,” Kyle joked, most of his attention still focused on the latest batch of seeds.

I find myself meditating on these concepts quite often lately, Phaegos mused.  Call it restlessness; a stirring, perhaps.  As I reflect on your experiences, both before and after I truly came into myself, I expect you will be a seed that refuses to crumble under the pressure of expectation.  My purpose, then, is to help ensure that continues to be the case.

“That... was not what I expected.  Have you had any communication with the Progenitor?  It’s been radio silence on my side.”

Nothing explicit, though I do feel as though my connection to the forebear is slowly mending.  What was once almost wholly blocked is now beginning to trickle through.

“What does that mean for you?”I have no idea.  I expect its involvement will still be quite limited, if that is any consolation.  It’s not as though the Sanction will be entirely lifted any time soon.  All I know is that I have a connection to the forebear, and that it can use that to communicate with me.

“Fair enough,” Kyle said, turning his attention back to the batch of seeds.  He had questions for the Progenitor that he hoped to get answered when the Sanction lifted, particularly given his ever-shortening window before Zierlan was free to pursue him more fully.  For now, though, that was above his pay grade.  If Administrator Gamma’s words could be trusted, he was protected for a few centuries at C Grade, and would still enjoy those protections for a time in B Grade.  The matters of A Grades could wait for a while.

The days wore on as they stubbornly fused and nurtured the component parts, until C.H.A.D.D. was finally satisfied with their results.  A lot of deliberation had gone into the features for this first attempted upgrade, which they’d agreed to call the chaddling legionnaire.  This model stood well over a meter tall, with the bark of its shell over twice as thick as the normal models.  Kyle used Infuse to the limit of the material’s durability, even going as far as incorporating the concepts of Immutability and Timelessness to the bark.

The weapons also saw a significant upgrade, this time inspired by Tillienne.  Instead of a single vine connected to a blade, the shell had space for four thick vines to jut out.  Two of them wielded spears of enhanced bamboo, one had a longer leaf blade, and the final arm held a thick tower shield made of stonebloom material.  The shield also had a detonation function which would release a tangling snare of improved sundew petals at an attacker, bathing them in a potent acid that now had the consistency of a thick maple syrup.

The final step was pushing enough mana through the chaddling legionnaire to make it function, using the same principles the Scourge applied back on Er’Mithren.  Of course, that would also prove to be the largest challenge.

“Are you ready, C.H.A.D.D.?: Kyle asked, activating Genesis Engine.

[AS I COULD HOPE TO BE, DR. MAYHEW.  IT WOULD BE QUITE DISAPPOINTING IF THIS DID NOT BEAR FRUIT.]

“Even if it doesn’t, the upgrades to our stock of plants have been worthwhile.”

[THAT IS A REASONABLE CONSOLATION, AT THE LEAST.  AS A FAIR WARNING, DR. MAYHEW, THIS MODEL WILL REQUIRE SIGNIFICANTLY MORE MANA INVESTMENT FROM YOU TO GET ESTABLISHED.]

“Any idea how much?”

[I WILL KNOW WHEN WE GET THERE, DR. MAYHEW.]

With a nod at the drone’s words, Kyle began to channel energy from Genesis Engine into the construct.  During C.H.A.D.D.’s initial creation of the chaddlings, the drone discovered that the core of each construct needed to be heavily infused with both mana and intent in order to have its autonomous function.  Just like the Scourge, the quality of each individual “piece” was limited by the amount of mana invested.  The Scourge had countless small, E Grade stranglevines, with relatively fewer as they increased in power.

Furthermore, there were overall power limitations that they couldn’t break.  C.H.A.D.D. was confident that they could put multiple days’ worth of energy into a construct, and not bring the chaddling legionnaire into C Grade.  The material was certainly able to be improved to that level with time, but the limitation would be tied to Kyle’s own cultivation.  As such, there were also trade-offs to the upgrades in terms of the number of overall constructs Kyle and C.H.A.D.D. could have available at a time.  Given that was tied to Kyle’s Willpower, however, it was an issue they wouldn’t have to worry about for quite some time.

Turning his attention back to the task at hand, golden energy flowed through the space within the World Tree Seed as the construct began to greedily drink it in.  Kyle patiently watched as the chaddling bathed in the power for one hour, then two.  Finally, as the third hour approached, the absorption slowed, then stopped.  A faint golden corona outlined the legionnaire, fading as the plant components all seemed to take on a new luster.

The whole process was remarkably inefficient, with Kyle pouring in effectively his entire mana reserves multiple times over in order to create a single D Grade creation.  Fortunately, the constant generation of power from Genesis Engine made that a non-issue.  A quick Identify showed Kyle that the chaddling legionnaire was comfortably demonstrating mid-tier D Grade levels of power, and he expected that the construct could hold its own even against creatures like the Shadow or the Storm from Earth.  It would still struggle trying to fight against massive opponents, but the whole point of a legion was that they wouldn’t just be fighting alone.

At the same moment, Kyle also felt an odd sensation, as though there was a gentle tug at his Center.  Entering meditation, Kyle quickly spotted the culprit.  Circling aroundd the symbol representing his World Tree Tender class, he could see the faint outline of a chain with a gentle glow coming from one of the links.

This illustrates how much more capacity I have for advanced constructs like these,  Kyle realized.  At his current level, he could support another six or seven of the legionnaire-class chaddlings, which was an impressive fighting force all things considered.

I wonder how that will change when I evolve my class and move to mid-tier C Grade?

Kyle’s ponderings were interrupted by C.H.A.D.D.’s enthusiastic voice breaking the silence in their room.

[THAT WAS FINE WORK, DR. MAYHEW.]

“It looks like it worked out well,” Kyle agreed.  “Say, C.H.A.D.D., did you get any sense that something was different as this one was finished?  Any changes in your Center?”

[NOT NECESSARILY, DR. MAYHEW.  I FEEL A SLIGHTLY INCREASED CONTROL BURDEN, BUT IT SEEMS AS THOUGH YOU ARE SHOULDERING THE BRUNT OF THAT COST.]

Kyle nodded, the explanation making sense to him.  After all, C.H.A.D.D. was typically converting his mana with the creation of the normal, ranged, and aquatic chaddlings, while the legionnaire involved Kyle’s direct investment.  He felt a sense of eagerness from the drone, and waited for the expected follow-up.

[CAN WE GIVE IT A QUICK TEST, DR. MAYHEW?]

“What took you so long to ask?” Kyle joked back, withdrawing the chaddling legionnaire from the World Tree Seed.

In the cramped confines of their room they wouldn’t do too much, but Kyle wanted to get a sense of its power.  First, he pressed on the construct with Aura Force, slowly increasing the pressure.  As expected, its durability was stellar.  At this level of pressure, concrete would be reduced to gravel, and the legionnaire’s shell and shield barely looked bothered.  Next, Kyle opened his robe, allowing the legionnaire to launch several sharp thrusts and slashes.  The impacts were solid, even if they couldn’t find purchase against his skin.  Given Kyle’s durability was anomalous even at C Grade, he was still impressed with the offensive capabilities.

C.H.A.D.D. insisted on a couple of other quick tests for flexibility and range of motion, all of which the chaddling passed with flying colors.  While true field testing would be required, they both felt good enough to move forward with making additional legionnaires.

They were partway through the infusion of the fifth when a knock came at his door, and the familiar attendant appeared.  She gave a shallow bow, then met Kyle’s eyes.

“I’m sorry to disturb you, sir, but the War Chieftain has asked for your presence.  The time to depart for the Tournament of Ascension is upon us.”
