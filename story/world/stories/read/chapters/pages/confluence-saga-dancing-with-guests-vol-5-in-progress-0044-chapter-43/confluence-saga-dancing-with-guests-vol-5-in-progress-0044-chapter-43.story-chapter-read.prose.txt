Weregild as a cost standard.

Various societies, in particular Unawakened ones, use some form of currency to determine costs. The WidePond as a whole does the same, by establishing costs based on Energy Levels. Downward conversion is easy while upward conversion of Energy is more challenging. Six months of Energy distribution into a standard meditation room is one Weregild of that level of Energy. The choice of 6 months is a mystery, perhaps it was meant to be a year originally but no one remembers.

What you get for a given level Weregild varies, there is no oversight so prices often become negotiations, at least when the affected parties have their own views on value.
