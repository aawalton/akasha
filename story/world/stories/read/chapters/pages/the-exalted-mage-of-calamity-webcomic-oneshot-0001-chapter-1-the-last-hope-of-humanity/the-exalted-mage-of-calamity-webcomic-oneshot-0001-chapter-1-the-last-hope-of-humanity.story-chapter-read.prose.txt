Near the orchard line

Behind her, the demon leaned forward. Its claws scraped the earth as it lunged, growling.

A breath later

There.

A pair of children.

Present day - 3 years later - Ruins of Annesheim.

Veronica Everwells.

Her lightly tanned skin, once glowing with vitality, was now streaked with soot and blood. Short, pointed ears peeked through her tangled, ash-caked hair. A chip cracked from her arm and fell.

Not quite alive. Not quite dead.

Medusa’s Curse. A rare affliction caused by imbalance. It resulted from being born with two mana cores. None of those who suffered through it lived past infancy.

Except for Veronica.

She had been thirty-one when she reached the 10th-tier, a feat no one else in recorded history had matched. To the world, Veronica was nothing short of a prodigy, a living testament of hard work triumphing over fate. They called her a miracle, a genius, proof that curses could be defied.

But genius was only what she let them see. Few understood what her brilliance demanded, or the price that befell her with every spell cast.

Each incantation hastened her petrification, grinding years away as stone crept further up her veins. She hid the truth from the world beneath large robes. Even her elven blood could not shield her from the rapid decay that followed; her body continued to wither, her years spent faster than she wanted. And the pain… it was something she wished she hadn’t become accustomed to.

Vitality simply wasn’t something she was blessed with in her lifetime.

Nor was peace.

Six months. That’s all it took. From the celebratory heights of her 10th-tier ascension came the invasion of the entire demon realm.

Now thirty-four, she had spent three years at war. The last year, she spent it alone, now, as humanity’s last vestige.

It was especially hard when she lost the allies closest to her. Not only were they her friends, but they also handled what she normally couldn’t. Her job was just to kill demons. Simple, and easy.

At least, it should have been.
