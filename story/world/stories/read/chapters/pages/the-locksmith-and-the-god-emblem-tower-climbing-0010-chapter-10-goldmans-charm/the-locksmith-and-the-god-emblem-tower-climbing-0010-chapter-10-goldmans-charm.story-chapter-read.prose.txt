After Harry released the two men using his Emblem, they returned together to find Prism and Brun. They didn't fully trust the Locksmith, but for now the man seemed to be on their side. Harry and Madeline had told them about the deal they made with Brun, and though Goldman seemed to buy it, Daniel didn't.

The barely naked Goldman, of almost Herculean proportions, had the Compass Emblem on the Circle format, which rivaled Harry's own Emblem versatility.

"So you can change the flow of things?" He asked Goldman, "You're the leader of the group, aren't you?"

Still only in his underwear, the man waited a few moments before answering, "I don't believe in leadership... but yes, I do most of the talking. The Compass Emblem allows me to control the poles of any item. I just can't do it to myself, which explains why they stripped me from my gear..."

Still not fully understanding the scope of his abilities, Harry refrained from asking more, especially with the uncomfortable gaze of Brun's father, Daniel Laskow. What kind of Emblem could that madman possess? Maybe his body turned him into a meat grinder, or perhaps he could summon bloody axes. Either way, the Locksmith didn't want to find out.

"Locksmith, correct?" Goldman continued the conversation, "A strange name, if I may. But the theme matches with your abilities. I guess "The Doorman" Would be a nicer fit, though."

"The Doorman was already taken... But I kinda like the nickname," Harry replied, dodging Daniel's muderous gaze. "Besides, it would be too much of a hassle to regain my authority in the underground."

"Authority...?" Madeline chuckled once again. "Yeah, right..."

"So you're a thief." The viking said, still in his underwear, which, surprisingly, did not lower the man's presence. "Those usually don't last long. Especially here."

...Is this a threat? Harry added inwardly as he gulped hard. Daniel's voice was timbering and blocky, almost as if he were always furious at the world. "I have the, um, Door Emblem. It allows me to create doors wherever I'm looking, up to a certain distance, and create passages between them that quickly disappear after the door closes. I can control the door's properties, too..."

Although the Locksmith wasn't comfortable in telling them about his Emblem, it seemed the smart choice to gain their confidence.

"How interesting!" With a radiant smile, he added, "I'm sure Prism saw you as a valuable asset and correctly determined you would be of great help! I mean, we're out of the cell, right?"

I think it's more out of lack of choice, really... Harry thought with an embarrassed smile.

Still feeling Daniel's gaze on his neck, he glanced to meet the man's gaze. To earn Brun's respect, he had to push the old woman to the ground, so perhaps his father needed the same treatment. Although I can't push him without injuring myself. I guess a verbal approach will have to work.

A malicious smile was painted on the Viking's face as soon as Harry looked at him, and with a tugging motion of his hand, the Locksmith knew he might be in danger. When he turned forward, he saw an arrow coming towards him. Instinctively, he dodged the arrow and saw another one to his right, coming from the other side of the corridor. He created one of his doors to block the arrow, and when he realized it, another one was coming from the left.

What the hell? he thought to himself as he realized it was Daniel controlling the arrows. Brun's father was clearly amused watching Harry dodge his arrows while the others had already jumped aside, surprised.

Everything happened so fast that not a word was spoken. And after the third arrow that Harry had deflected with one of his doors, he decided to advance on the man, who had fallen a little behind as soon as he began orchestrating his arrows.

In a fraction of a second, Harry appeared in front of him, his blood boiling. Daniel, taking a quick step back, understood a little of how Harry moved. In a subtle movement, he prepared a strong punch to his left, and like a trained hunter, anticipated that the Locksmith would appear there, and as soon enough, Harry appeared through another door.

Dodging a terrifying punch by a hair's breadth, he leapt as graciously as a runaway rabbit and used a thick door as a platform to launch himself even higher, like an acrobat. Perched atop a door that refused to open, Harry stared at Daniel, confused.

"Do not be afraid." Said the man in his underwear. "I just wanted to see if you have what it takes to hang out with us, nothing more."

Under the disappointed gazes of Goldman and Madeline, he continued with his malicious grin.

"You really are Brun's father, aren't you?" Now calmer, knowing the man's intentions, he decided to continue his game and give him a taste of his own medicine. He looked to the end of the corridor from where they were coming and imagined an arrow could come from there. In a swift movement, he jumped from his doorway and caught the arrow in mid-air, his hand screaming in pain from the graze.

Harry, still in the air, threw an arrow through a passage on his right and jumped on top of the man. When Daniel tried to grab his neck, he generated a door exactly the size of his hand. What happened was the man grabbing his own head and pulling it through a door that opened behind him. Irritated, he growled or grunted — it could have been either one — and, taking his arm out of one of the passages, grabbed the door that then closed in front of him and crushed it to Harry’s surprise.

Wait, did he really growl like a fucking bear? Harry thought, his eyes wide.

Having dodged his grasp, he hung from a door like a pull up bar and took off his hood. Dodging the various arrows that circled him, he realized he was being pushed into a corner from which he couldn’t escape. A fight with a man as heavy as this certainly wouldn’t end well. He then attached his hood to Daniel’s head, leaving him blind.

In a furious attack, the man broke all the dozens of small doors around him like a gorilla. Realizing his lack of options, he needed to end the fight quickly. While the man was hooded, he grabbed another arrow in the air and broke it, for some reason. Now with one whole arrow and another broken in half, he leapt at the man, who, even without vision, landed a grab on Harry’s neck with his immense strength.

The arrow in Harry’s hand then slipped out of his hand and stopped beside Daniel, who now had removed the hood and was on the floor.

"I think this ends here." The man said with his bloodshot eyes, "I can feel all my arrows."

"Even this one?" Harry spat out some words when suddenly he pulled the broken half of the arrowhead he had snapped and pointed it at the man’s neck like a knife.

Confused, the man quickly understood what had happened. He could feel the presence of his arrows, so he knew where Harry was when he jumped, even without vision. What he hadn’t noticed was that, at some point, the Locksmith broke the arrow, ending his connection with the item.

Harry imagined that Daniel would have some control over his arrows the same way he had about his doors, so he made a backup. What he didn’t know was that the presence acted almost like another sense for the viking.

"...So it was when I got distracted..." He whispered to himself while looking at the young man in front of him and gave an approving smile. Harry returned the smile and adjusted his glasses.

After a few excruciating moments of the Viking's piercing gaze, he released him and picked up one of his arrows, throwing it to the other side of the corridor, where it vanished into the darkness.

As Harry coughed violently on the floor, he looked up and saw Daniel, now with a smile similar to Goldman's. With a hand extended to the young man, all his doors had already disappeared. Harry accepted the hand and stood up.

"Fuck... You and your daughter really need to work on your introductions better..." Harry said in a hoarse voice, "Don't tell me your father is also alive inside the Tower."

Helping him to his feet, the man let out a warm, lively laugh, similar to Goldman's, "Unfortunately not, small but not-so-weak man."

No need for the 'small' part... Harry added inwardly as Madeline handed him his cloak and tapped his shoulders.

"...Why didn't you help?" Harry asked.

"Really?" Madeline replied with a smile. "You think I'd interfere with a fight from a literal viking from old days? Yeah, not my thing. But you fought well. I mean it."

And she did mean it. Harry's instincts and quick thinking were well appreciated in the fight and Madeline could see that. She wished she could help, but what would happen if Goldman turned in her? Even with her Brush, the gentle giant could squish them both like grapes.

"Was this necessary?" Goldman said, his worried smile fading as soon as he saw that Harry was alright, "If you do that to everyone who shows up, no one will want to join us."

"I just needed to see if he had what he needed," Daniel said, "I've lost too many men to others' lack of guts. I want my daughter to be in good hands if I'm no longer around."

And at that moment Harry felt melancholic and noticed the protective look with which Daniel gazed down the corridor; was this what it meant to be a father?

"So that's your Emblem?" he asked Daniel, "Manipulating arrows?"

"I can summon and pull arrows as I please," He answered proudly. "So neither you nor I have ever been in real danger. Your Emblem is quite interesting indeed, Harry."

"Call me Locksmith," he said, smiling. "Harry is only for people who haven't tried to kill me."

"Understood, Locksmith." Daniel said, smiling and turning to continue towards the group.

"...How did you guys end up here, anyway?" He wanted to ask this in the first place, but felt intimidated.

"Long story—"

"Goldman is an idiot." Daniel cut him off, leaving Goldman with an embarrassed smile.

"We, um..." He tried to explain, "I thought a more diplomatic approach would suffice. Safe to say it... did not. As soon as we were brought to the church, Daniel simply blacked out from a knock in the head. The object that hit him was big and, before I could see who threw it, I also fell. Probably the act of an Emblem User. That was, um... a couple cycles ago."

"If I could've seen the asshole that did it..." Daniel mumbled to himself.

"And how did they lock you in here?"

"They forced C-Coins on us inside that cell. Some hooded figures then made us take off our clothes and... Yeah, that's it. I guess because they fear I could use my Emblem to escape or something. They've been doing this every Cycle restart. I suppose they are fearful we could hurt their flame."

After a few minutes, when everyone was more relaxed, Harry realized they were getting close, so he decided to ask one last question while they were quiet.

"How many floors does the Tower have? Do you know?" he asked Goldman, but left it open for Daniel to answer if he wanted.

"More than five..." Goldman said, more seriously. "But from what I've heard, the Shepherd has already been to the top."

"But why is he down here, then?" Harry asked.

Maybe, if he met the enigmatic figure, he could talk to him. He could've found the God Emblem and, for some reason, decided to return. Perhaps his real wish was to stay in the Tower, forever.

"He told stories about reverse mountains and floating islands. Aye, in my opinion, the bloke is crazier than a castaway in a summer day. Don't get your hopes up, Locksmith."

The journey so far has been smooth, but I feel that's about to change... Harry thought to himself as he imagined the possible dangers of the floors above.

They kept walking until they saw some familiar lights. A face was missing, however, to match the lights.

"Where’s Vela?" Harry was the first to ask, running ahead, "Didn’t you find him?"

"We believe he’s up there." Prism said, "Or maybe..."

Maybe what? Harry almost said, but held back. He knew what it meant. Vela, with his Candle Emblem, was potentially the biggest heavy hitter. But what haunted Harry the most was what they could think the former monk could bring. With their similar devotions to fire, perhaps Vela was in for a much grim ending.

If the cultists didn’t kill us, then they still see some use in our abilities... They're trying to convert us? Harry thought of possible explanations that didn’t involve Vela’s death.

"My god, dad!" Brun got close to Daniel, "Where are your clothes? Prism, go get some clothes back there. We found everyone’s items near the hallway."

Unconsciously, Harry touched his journal once again; it was still there. Nice...

"Oh, it's nothing..." Daniel said with a soft voice, "They just left us like this to stop Goldman from being able to get us out of here, but they underestimated us once again! HAHAHA!"

Do you mean they underestimated me, right? The Locksmith added inwardly. I was the one who took you out.

"Their actions represent fear or respect, and at this moment, I’ll take either." Goldman said with a hand on his waist.

Now in a slightly uncomfortable environment due to the two half-naked men in the place, Harry said looking at Daniel. "...Why do you have six fingers?"

In their previous fight, Harry had felt something extra around his neck, but in the heat of battle, chose to ignore it. Maybe that explained the strength in his grip.

Now under the looks of his companions, the man visibly shrank and answered with a frown. "What do you want me to say? You want me to tell you my entire genetic code?"

"You know what a genetic code is?" Prism asked, surprised.

"Yes. It is many lines that are in red liquid and contain your information."

"You know about genetic code but suddenly blood is just a 'red liquid'?"

"No. No, I was just curious..." Harry answered Daniel while scratching his chin, "I didn’t know you were so insecure about that..."

"The insecure one here is you with your poorly made beard you leper hobo!—"

And in the same moment Brun held his father’s hand, stopping him from pulling an arrow behind Harry.

"There’s no way I look that much like a beggar!"

He looked at everyone while they also averted their eyes. His face, visibly shrinking, moved to Goldman, who kept an embarrassed smile.

"I think it’s because of the hair, Harry. You really need a bath." Goldman said, trying to soften it, "If it serves you anything, you look like a street professor!"

"...It really doesn't."
