Collapsing from exhaustion was normal, but it was strange that it had already happened twice in this very hall.

That was Ning’s first thought upon seeing the familiar white ceiling.

His second was that the bed was quite comfy, and his third was that his master’s head looked quite big from this close.

“Congratulations. The pill was successful. You’re a girl now,” Ling Xin said seriously.

Hm? Hmm?!

Ning must have heard something wrong. His head was still foggy.

Then Ling Yinran began scolding Ling Xin. “Aunt, Junior Brother is still recovering. Please don’t joke with him.”

“Haha. But did you see the look on his face? It’s rare to see him panic like that.”

Her previously stern persona was nowhere to be seen. As someone who took joy in making all kinds of strange pills, it was no surprise that she had a mischievous streak.

“So… that was just a joke, right?” Ning finally asked.

Don’t ask him why he was so worried. There was a gender-changing pill in this world called the Yin-Yang Rotation pill. He had every reason to be concerned.

“Yes, yes. Anyway, can you feel the qi in your acupoint? Any pain or discomfort?” Ling Xin asked, becoming serious again.

“No, I feel fine.”

Ning could sense the newly opened acupoint in his body, along with the faint trace of alchemical qi residing within it.

[Medicine King Technique: Starting (Acupoint Opening)]

“That’s good. We managed to succeed in a single attempt.” Ling Xin smiled.

“Also, Master, I do have a question. Why didn’t you tell me about this beforehand? I could have been more mentally prepared.”

Ning couldn’t help but complain. It had hurt like hell, and he would have taken a pain-suppressing pill if he had known.

“T-this…” Ling Xin blinked. “I just wanted to give you a surprise.”

Surprise?

Yes, that was certainly a surprise and a very memorable one.

“She procrastinated until the end and only remembered this morning that it was your first alchemy session. That’s why I had to go and get the Acupoint Opening Pill from Grandmaster,” Ling Yinran said, exposing her aunt.

“No wonder it all felt so sudden.”

Ning glanced at Ling Xin, who seemed to wilt.

“Enough of that. Let’s talk about the Medicine King Technique.” Ling Xin forcefully changed the topic. “You’ve now completed the acupoint opening stage. From here on, you need to nourish the acupoint with alchemy qi every day."

Ning knew Ling Xin was avoiding responsibility, but he didn’t have the energy to care about it right now. “So I have to perform alchemy every day?”

“Yes. The amount of alchemy qi you obtain depends on the quality and rank of the pill you refine. Your Fasting Pill is very basic, so you’ll only get a trace each time,” Ling Xin explained.

“Don’t worry too much, Junior Brother. It may seem burdensome, but the benefits are amazing,” Ling Yinran encouraged, ever the reliable senior sister.

Ning nodded.

“During the nourishing stage, you can use alchemy qi to slightly enhance the effects of pills, allowing you to absorb their medicinal power more efficiently. Once you reach the next stage, you can use accumulated alchemy qi to assist with breakthroughs.” Ling Xin raised two fingers. “Those are the two most fundamental uses of the Medicine King Technique. The later stages develop further from them.”

“This sounds amazing.”

Ning could immediately see the value of this technique. His cultivation was about to go brr…

“Of course it is. This is one of the four major techniques brought here by our founder.” Ling Xin looked proud. “You know the founder came from another region, right? These techniques originated there. Considering he was a Nascent Soul patriarch, anything he considered worth passing down wouldn’t be ordinary.”

Ling Xin had grown up in the sect and clearly had a strong sense of belonging.

“Master, since the Medicine King Technique is so amazing, what are the other three techniques?” Ning asked curiously.

“Well, normally such things aren’t discussed openly, but since you’ve learned the Medicine King Technique, you can be considered a true member of our lineage.” Ling Xin began counting them off. “The first is the Beast King Technique, which allows a beast master and their contracted beast to synchronize aspects of their cultivation.”

“The second is the Talisman King Technique. Its practitioners refine a natal talisman and eventually absorb it into their body.”

“And the last is the Weapon King Technique. It allows a cultivator to establish a deeper connection with a weapon and nourish it with their own qi, gradually improving its quality. Of course, people often call it the Sword King Technique nowadays, since that’s the version practiced by our current sect leader.”

“Damn!”

That was the only word Ning could manage after hearing all of that. Every single one sounded like a cheat. Given the founder’s cultivation level, it made sense that techniques of this caliber would have caught his attention.

“Aunt, wasn’t there also a rumor about a secret fifth technique?” Ling Yinran asked curiously.

“Hm. There was such a rumor, but I don’t think it’s true.” Ling Xin shook her head. “Founder Li Changsou wasn’t only a cultivation genius. He was highly accomplished in every art corresponding to the four techniques. If there really were a fifth technique belonging to the same series, there should have been some record of the art he practiced alongside it.”

“I see.”

Ning and Ling Yinran nodded.

“Of course, it’s also possible that even our genius founder couldn’t practice the fifth technique, so no record of it exists.” Ling Xin smiled. “But that’s obviously impossible, right?”

“…Right.”

Ning could recognize a flag when he saw one.

This was definitely a flag.

...

For the next few hours, Ning focused on memorizing the technique manual. At this point, he was practically a pro at this kind of stuff.

After reading through the Medicine King Technique manual, Ning could only say that he was lucky to have such a good master.

The main requirement for cultivating the technique was to open an acupoint early enough to nourish it with the “primordial” alchemical qi produced during one’s first successful refinement.

Only now did he realize that the pill his master had given him was a top-grade second-tier pill, something he wouldn’t be able to obtain even if he sold himself. Its sole function was to help open an acupoint without damaging the body while allowing it to gradually adapt to the new pathway.

But oh boy, was that a difficult process.

From what he had read, Ning knew that the bodies of cultivators were said to possess “infinite” possibilities. By following specific techniques, one could transform the body or open things like the Eight Extraordinary Meridians, the one hundred and twenty-eight star-shaped acupoints, or whatever else the cultivation method required.

Since the ultimate goal of these kinds of cultivation paths seemed to be replacing the universe with one’s own body, Ning had already grown used to the strangeness of this human body of his.

All these transformations required fuel, and high-grade fuel at that. The Acupoint Opening Pill was exactly that, and considering Ling Xin hadn’t said anything about compensation or payment, Ning knew this was simply his master taking care of him.

“Now, my main task is to accumulate as much alchemy qi as possible.”

Ning understood that his cultivation path had broadened even further with this technique. He now had the possibility of reaching the eighth stage of Qi Condensation, and perhaps even going beyond that, within the given deadline.

Ning was grateful to have someone like Ling Xin as his master. He would have to find ways to take better care of her in return, even if there wasn’t much he could do for now.

After cleaning up, Ning walked back toward his hut, greeting the occasional spiritual farmer along the way. His reputation had improved quite a bit ever since he attended that last gathering.

It would have been awkward to promote himself as an alchemist on his own, but with the unwitting help of Lao San, he had managed to establish himself properly.

“Is this what they call doing a good thing with bad intentions? Or a reward after surviving a tribulation?”

Ning’s mind, as always, wandered to strange places.

Fenghuang was perched on a tree in Ning’s field. Hearing someone approach, he swiftly flew forward, only to stop after recognizing Ning.

“Thank you for looking after the field.”

Ning reached out and petted the raven.

He had gradually come to realize the benefits of having a beast companion. Now that his field had reached the first grade, the accompanying troubles had also increased.

With Fenghuang around, weeds and pests could be burned away, allowing Ning to focus on other tasks. Plus, it was good to have a companion by his side in daily life, no matter how stoic the raven was.

“We should try to obtain the Beast King Technique in the future,” Ning thought as he absentmindedly stroked Fenghuang’s feathers.

With the Beast King Technique, he could synchronize with Fenghuang and accelerate both of their cultivation speeds. Of course, that was a long-term goal. Given his current identity and position, he had no leverage to obtain a core technique from another lineage.

If he hadn’t gained a Foundation Establishment master like Ling Xin, someone directly connected to a Purple Mansion elder, he wouldn’t have even caught a glimpse of the Medicine King Technique.

...

It was bright and early when Ning began his daily work tending to the farm. With the growing demands of alchemy, he had already planted a large batch of basic spiritual grain, and at his current skill level, producing high-quality grain was no longer difficult.

The problem was that it still took three months to mature.

Ning had a solution: the Spring Breeze Seedling Technique.

He had already read through the technique manual, which, like most spiritual farming techniques, was written in the aggressively poetic style that seemed to be a mandatory requirement for anyone transcribing cultivation manuals.

“As spring wind stirs the sleeping earth, so does this technique rouse the vital essence within the seed. The practitioner becomes the season itself, determining growth and prosperity.”

Ning translated this internally.

It makes plants grow faster.

Much better.

He ignored the rest of the poetry and focused on how the technique actually worked. Plants went through different phases of growth, and the Spring Breeze Seedling Technique supplied additional spiritual qi at the right point in that cycle. If the user got the timing right, the plant would use that qi to grow faster; if they got it wrong, there was a good chance of damaging the plant instead.

“This is why it requires Great Accomplishment in Withering and Flourishing,” Ning muttered. “You need to know when the plant actually wants the qi.”

That was something he had plenty of experience with. After two years of managing Dragon Qi Grass, Frozen Breath Grain, Serpent Flowers, and various other crops, Ning had become quite familiar with the rhythms of spiritual plants. Some eagerly absorbed qi while growing, some needed time to stabilize, and others simply wanted to be left alone for a while.

Ning crouched beside the basic spiritual grain and placed a hand against the soil. The manual called the first step Breath Reading, which made it sound much more impressive than it was. He simply needed to determine where the plant currently was in its growth cycle.

Closing his eyes, Ning guided a faint trace of qi into the roots and quickly felt the response. The grain was in a period of active growth, readily accepting his qi as spiritual energy moved steadily through its roots.

Then Ning paused.

“…Hm.”

Something had felt different.

He tried again, paying closer attention this time, and soon noticed what had caught his attention. Through the Withering and Flourishing Technique, Ning had always been able to tell whether a plant was actively growing, but now he could sense something more within that growth.

There was a series of faint pulse.

Ning remained still and focused on the interval between them. The rhythm was very clear, almost as though he could tell exactly where the grain currently was within its growth cycle.

He quickly realized what was happening.

Extreme Space-Time Sensitivity.

The spatial side of his talent had shown itself fairly early. Ning had always possessed an unusually good sense of distance, trajectories, and movement, while the time side had mostly manifested as an unusually accurate awareness of how much time had passed, sometimes to an annoying degree.

That was beginning to change.

“So the growth cycle counts too…”

Ning focused on the plant again. A living thing grew according to its own rhythm, and that growth was ultimately a process occurring over time. With the Spring Breeze Seedling Technique giving him something specific to focus on, his sensitivity seemed to be picking up on that rhythm.

How interesting.

Ning filed the discovery away for later and returned his attention to farming.

He formed the first seal and slowly released spiritual qi from his palm. The Spring Breeze Seedling Technique spread that qi across the entire root network instead of concentrating it into a single point.

Basically, it was a sprinkler.

The qi spread through the soil and reached the grain. For a moment, nothing happened, but soon Ning felt the rhythm change as the plant drew in his qi and its growth gradually accelerated.

There it is.

Ning finally understood what the manual meant by the spring wind metaphor. The technique worked by supplying additional qi while the plant was already growing, matching the pace at which it could naturally absorb it and encouraging the process along.

The poetry had actually been useful for once.

Ning maintained the technique until a familiar notification appeared.

[Spring Breeze Seedling Technique: Starting (1/100)]

He had officially learned it.

Over the next two hours, Ning tested the technique on three different plots: the basic spiritual grain, a section of Dragon Qi Grass that was running slightly behind the rest of its batch, and the Serpent Flowers.

The Dragon Qi Grass was easy to work with. Its growth was stable, and the additional qi integrated without much trouble. The Serpent Flowers required more attention, as their response to the supplementary qi was different from the grain and Dragon Qi Grass.

Ning adjusted the amount and timing several times, using what he could sense through the Withering and Flourishing Technique to match the qi pulses to their growth rhythm. It took a few attempts before he found the right pace, but once he did, the technique began working smoothly.

That was probably the real value of practicing on different plants.

After two hours, Ning checked his progress.

[Spring Breeze Seedling Technique: Starting (5/100)]

It was slower than his early progress with the Withering and Flourishing Technique, but he had also killed two plants while learning that one, so Ning was perfectly willing to trade some speed for fewer casualties.

He leaned back against the courtyard wall and looked over his fields. At Mastery, the Spring Breeze Seedling Technique could accelerate the growth of first-tier plants by roughly thirty percent, which was already useful on its own. Now that Ning had started learning alchemy, however, the benefits extended beyond simply harvesting his crops faster.

Better crops meant better ingredients, which would make his refinements easier and help him produce more alchemy qi. In turn, that alchemy qi could be used to nourish his acupoint and assist his cultivation.

It’s all coming together, Ning thought with a small smile.

Being optimistic was also part of maintaining a healthy cultivation mindset.

With the Spring Breeze Seedling Technique added to his repertoire, Ning was satisfied with his farming techniques for the time being. Anything significantly better, such as advancing the Small Cloud Rain Technique into the Large Cloud Rain Technique, was still Foundation Building territory and therefore not something he needed to worry about yet.

That meant he finally had some room to focus elsewhere, and there was one thing in particular he had been putting off for long enough.

The spear.

His bow was progressing well, especially after developing the Flame Spiral Arrow with Fenghuang, and Shadow Steps gave him plenty of mobility in battle. The storage bag trick could also serve as an emergency defense if things went badly, but his close combat still needed work.

The Falling Leaf Spear Technique had served him well, but it was ultimately a basic spear art. Ning wanted something more substantial now, preferably a technique that could take advantage of his physical cultivation and remain useful as his cultivation increased.

He made a mental note to visit the Scripture Pavilion soon.

Alas, there was still much work to be done.
