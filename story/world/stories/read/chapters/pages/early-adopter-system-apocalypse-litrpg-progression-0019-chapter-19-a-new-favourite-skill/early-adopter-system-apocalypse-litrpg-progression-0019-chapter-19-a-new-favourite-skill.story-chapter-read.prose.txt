The moment the Skill Book dissolved she felt a form of energy entering her body, then seeping deeper. Into the part of her she was only newly aware of. The place where the ‘she’ in the real world connected to the ‘she’ inside of herself. Then she could follow the connection no longer.

Scarlet queried the Guide about her new Skill.

[Inspect – Tier 2: Provides an overview of a target’s properties, general condition, and immediately relevant qualities. Results scale with user knowledge, target resistance, and relative power.]

Okay, time to put the Skill to the test. She Inspected the Inspect Skill.

Scarlet couldn’t believe it actually worked.

[Inspect – Tier 2: Active. A skill that provides an overview of a target’s properties, general condition, and immediately relevant qualities. It is a System-assisted appraisal skill. One of the most common T2 advancements in the ‘Observe’ skill tree. Results scale with user knowledge, target resistance, and relative power.]

“Well now,” Scarlet breathed, finally beginning to shake off the numbness that had overwhelmed her in the aftermath of the battle and revelations. There was a lot to unpack there.

The good thing was that this ‘artificial’ Skill assimilation hadn’t done any lasting damage. As far as she could perceive. She was just as intact as she had been before the book had dissolved into her.

Speaking of books, before she got lost in testing out her new Skill, Scarlet needed to finish receiving her loot.

[Tome – Knowledge: A System-supported artifact enabling direct assimilation of information into retrievable memory. Depth, clarity, and integration are determined by the tome’s quality and the recipient’s capacity and compatibility]

So, degrees of assimilation were variable based on the individual. It made sense. Adding a cup of water to an ocean wouldn’t do much to change the ocean while adding a cup of water to another cup of water would double the volume.

Scarlet had received ‘Initiate Guide to Butchering, Harvesting, and Scavenging’. Concepts she was familiar with, though not proficient at.

[Would you like to Retrieve Knowledge Tome: Initiate Guide to Butchering, Harvesting, and Scavenging]

[Yes]

Much like the Skill Book, the Tome was somehow both a book and not a book at all. There was a different off-ness to the Tome that she couldn’t quite explain.

[Assimilate Knowledge Tome: Initiate Guide to Butchering, Harvesting, and Scavenging?][Yes | No]

She ignored the prompt, Inspecting the tome in her hands.

[Knowledge Tome: Initiate Guide to Butchering, Harvesting, and ScavengingTier: 0Condition: PerfectDetails: Contains practical instruction in the processing of many System-recognised organic materials, including butchering, resource extraction, and scavengingEffect:

Limitations:

“Oh, Inspect. What a treasure you are,” she murmured, marveling at the details in the item description.

The only real questions Scarlet had were: just how valuable was this tome, and how valuable was it for her specifically.

Looking around her at all the bodies she had yet to process, her eyes fell on the bisected corpse of the shadow bat. It was gross, yes, but it was also alien to her. Also, she had no real knowledge of creatures and plants with magical properties.

She refused to leave behind useful materials. Whether they came from foraging or fallen enemies. Especially now that she had a handy-dandy storage bag.

‘One should never let a victory go to waste,’ she sent to Maus, receiving confirmation and delight in return.

[Assimilate Knowledge Tome: Initiate Guide to Butchering, Harvesting, and Scavenging?][Yes | No]

[Yes]

Her knees buckled, her stomach roiled, and she felt a pressure in her head that was so overwhelming that she thought her eyes might pop out of her skull. It was a fight to hold in her groan of misery, but one she focused on to get her through the seconds that felt like hours. Then as quickly as it came, the sensation receded, leaving her with a lingering headache as she apologised to Maus.

Poor little fellow had nearly been thrown off when she’d doubled over.

After settling her bond, and her stomach, she scanned around the yard again, only this time, when she looked at the bodies, she had a vague sense of ‘knowing’ something. Like that feeling of having a word on the tip of your tongue, only this was knowledge. Somehow, she just knew that if she began to actively meditate on the concepts within the Tome, she’d gain a deeper understanding. Once she began to make use of that knowledge the information would assimilate faster.

If nothing else, the Tome alone would help her finish collecting her battle loot, considering she’d have to harvest it herself.

She shifted her attention to the nearest carcass and inspected it.

[Roidcoon Corpse – Level 3Tier: 0Condition: DamagedDetails: The corpse of a Tier 0 creature. Harvestable materials include pelt, bones, and some meat and organs]

Interesting. She wondered if perhaps her new harvesting knowledge was helping Inspect give her more information.

She Inspected the rest of the roidcoon corpses and received similar information, until she got to the final one. The one that had blasted them with that sonic attack. On top of the usual information about levels and harvestable pelts she saw something new.

[…Harvestable materials include pelt, bones, some organs, and a kinetic mana shard.]

Just what was a kinetic mana shard? She queried the Guide.

[A mana shard is a partially formed mana crystal or mana core. They are produced naturally within creatures or areas of high mana density. Shards may contain either attuned or unattuned mana.]

She turned to the shadow bat to see if she found something similar in the only other creature she’d fought that had used a Skill.

[Shadow Bat Corpse – Level 5Tier: 0Condition: DamagedDetails: The corpse of a Tier 0 creature. Harvestable materials include pelt, bones, some organs, and a kinetic/shadow mana shard.]

Okay, this she had to see. She needed to use Inspect on it, and that meant she had to find it. She trudged to the detached garage where she kept her father’s hunting kit – the ass knew his stuff – her cleaning supplies, and extra nitrile gloves. It also had a conveniently placed sink where a certain nasty mouse could receive another good scrub.

Once she was back out under the floodlights it took her little time to begin the harvesting process, and considerably more effort and willpower to systematically butcher the creature according to the information from the Tome.

Impatience would get her nothing but waste. If she had to collect her own loot, she was damn well getting every last usable claw and tendon these nightmare creatures had to offer.

On a positive note, she found that all the parts she collected had a close enough ‘signature’ that her new storage bag let her put it all in one slot. Also, watching things appear and disappear into the bag was both fascinating and deeply unnerving. She’d discovered she didn’t even need to place items into the opening. So long as she made contact and willed it into the bag, whatever she had been touching would just, vanish.

She looked down at the slightly damp Maus, who was staying warm inside her shirt, and thought of all the mischief they would get up to once she figured out how to share access with him.

Eventually, she pulled out the shard. It looked a bit like a translucent silver crystal with some red and gold iridescent facets depending on which way she turned it under the lights. It was roughly the size of a peach pit, but relative to the size of the animal, that was substantial.

She used Inspect.

[Kinetic Mana Shard:Tier: 0Condition: PerfectDetails: A shard containing kinetic mana formed within a Tier 0 creature.]

The process of butchering the shadow bat took much longer. Not because the skin was tougher, in fact it was the opposite. Unlike the roidcoon whose mana seemed to mostly concentrate in the area around the shard, the shadow bat had mana suffusing it.

Scarlet was quite glad she’d decided to assimilate the Tome because she would have absolutely butchered the shadow bat harvest otherwise. Pun absolutely intended. Once all the usable pieces of the bat were neatly stored away in her handy-dandy storage bag, Scarlet was left with what appeared to be a grey and silver, translucent crystal about the size of a cherry stone.

[Shadow/Kinetic Mana Shard:Tier: 0Condition: PerfectDetails: A shard containing both shadow and kinetic mana formed within a Tier 0 creature.]

Scarlet had so many questions, about dual attunement and whether attunement itself was unique to mana. She could feel the mana in the air, and it was not homogenous.

Around the tree line, most of the mana had a very verdant feel to it. If she had to name it, she’d call it something like nature mana? The mana closer to the corpses felt more like decay. It wasn’t unpleasant, just unnerving. The tolling of a bell in an empty town.

Unlike the mana, the butchery was unpleasant. However, she’d need to acclimate quickly if she wanted to do more than just survive. The System might tolerate hesitation, but she'd never seen it reward it.
