Chapter 136

Mayhem At The Ceremony

One would think that the easiest time to strike people would be during a ceremony. Especially when a ceremony is open to the public and involves leadership and high-ranking members from all the different countries of the world. For the most part, you’d be right that those involved in the ceremony are themselves vulnerable to a great many forms of attack, as their focus is on precision, on being in the right spot at the right time.

Fortunately, when there is a ceremony of great importance, if there is competent leadership, then they will have security on hand to help protect the ceremony goers. That is definitely the case here as I can barely keep up with where we are going and what we are doing. Being members of the host country, I am right at the forefront of our little procession. But fortunately, Madame Bloom is right beside me, with two of the aunties that I fought a duel against last night bringing up the rear. Flag bearers, carrying the colors of the Empire race ahead, seeming to need to go up and prepare the arrival area for us. Even with the protection of our healer’s veils and garb, I can feel their eyes piercing into me from behind, as an almost palpable feel of malevolence radiates from them and into the mental daggers they are piercing into my spine.

Following Madame Bloom, the process is easier, for she purposefully slows herself. Each step measured and rhythmic. A pause after each step, allowing those behind us to fall into lock step beside and behind her. The veil I’m wearing is also nice, as it lets me see what she is doing, and allows me the ability to mimic her movements without being obvious about it, at least I hope it isn’t obvious.

As the oldest Healer of the Empire, and likely one of the oldest Healers in general, she would be the obvious choice to follow for any Healer-related ceremony. The entire time we are moving, I try to understand what we are doing, but it is odd, as the stage containing the condemned is right next to us, and there is a long walkway that goes past the stage, followed by an upward spire of steps that stands several layers above the stage containing the condemned. My first thought would be that we would go to the stage, attempt to heal those on the stage, and then go up the spiraling tower of steps to watch the others try as we fail. But that is not the case.

Again, I am glad I am not leading this ceremony as we go to the stage, and then Madame Bloom holds up her right hand, palm inward as she covers half her face. I copy the gesture, using my own right hand for the same process shielding my eyes from the condemned, as we all continue to walk forward. After a few paces and just as we are forced to turn to begin going up the spiraling staircases we drop our hands and begin walking upwards. A spire that is clearly raised and locked into place by various metal poles sliding into latches. A marvel of engineering, or a sign that this world has a lot more tricks than I continue to give it credit for. While I mentally think the world is less developed than my past life because it doesn’t have super computers and modern technology for healing, I see that it does have more fundamentals when it comes to engineering principles. Glancing behind me, I can see the other Healers, a hundred or more in total, doing the same. Each raising their right hand in a sign of denial before walking past and beginning to walk along the staircase.

Once on the steps, Madame Bloom slows down even more, her steps more deliberate and forced as she climbs each step with the purpose of an elderly person who does not want to be the one who falls face first during such a ceremony. Seeing her forceful strides, I do the same, making sure that I am not the novice who makes her debut appearance as the falling healer. With that, we make our way upwards.

At about the hallway point, when we are literally stuck on a death trap. A death trap where we are stuck a few dozen feet up in the air, where we can only go up, or force our way out and through a horde of healers. That is of course is when I first hear it, the sounds that something is off. It begins with whistles off at the far exits of the gathered mass of citizens who all came out to witness this public execution.

Being so high up, I instinctively found myself reaching out for the familiar comfort that comes from the ground, only to realize that we are on a mostly wooden construct. That there are metal pots and frames under the wooden planks we are walking on, and then open air. Basically, nothing that would help comfort me at this time. That is when I fully realize how much of a death trap this whole ceremony is, and how vulnerable we all are up here. If we panicked, if we fought to get away, there would be chaos beneath.

Of course, that is when we finally got word to the whistles. How we began to get a form to the looming threat that had taken the air out of a nearly violent and awakened audience and caused the entire crowd to go silent as they all looked on for a threat.

Then the words of one of the guards rang out, but his words were cut through by a gust of wind that hit me at just that time. The wind, along with my veil over my ears made the cry from the far end of the courtyard almost impossible to make out.

“NEC..! NECR.. ASSa..N!”

That said, I had a guess as to what was happening as I raised my head and almost instinctively turned left, to try to look behind us, towards the source of the cry. That is when Madame Bloom made a quick hand sign, her palm down and waggling violently in a don’t get distracted gesture. Seeing her gesture, I turn back to her, only to see that she is continuing to walk forward. Seeing the act, I shake my head and begin moving to keep in lock-step with her. Finally, after a dozen paces we are up and turned around, facing the opposite way we were a moment ago. That is when I get a view of the mayhem that is happening behind us.

There are royal guards, and what could only be described as secret service members coming to life from the crowd and attacking intruders. One point in particular stands out to me, the glowing golden form of a man with golden eyes and a long glowing golden serpentine tongue who moves with a grace and speed that I instantly recognize as my master Mr. Yi. While I had only seen him change forms the one time, on Saturday, it was enough for me to link his new form to his fighting form and make the mental connection. It didn’t hurt that today was Tuesday, somehow just four days removed from that incident. An incident that cleared me of any lingering obligations to Madame Bloom, while all but locking in her last commitment to me, in our contract as Master and Apprentice.

Seeing Mr. Yi in action, I can only register the movements for a second, before we are forced to continue to move. Madame Bloom never slows down or stops her relentless pace forward. Instead, she keeps the same methodical pace forward, each step deliberate, and each step held at a rhythm that all but forces everyone else to fall in line behind her. This is good, as it forces me to turn slowly, as our bodies continue to make the turns up and around the raised spire.

My mind gets distracted by the steps, as I make sure to find the next correct foothold, and then I look up and see that more people are moving and reacting. From this height and vantage point, the crowd looks like a flat sea of people. A sea that is now rippling and seeming to move as one ocean wave, that moves and ripples based on the stimuli around it. While multiple sets of elite guards are moving, the crowd seems to be one wrong move away from complete panic.

Yet, there is one calm spot that draws my eye. In the rippling waves of the crowd below, there is one calm spot that seems to stand still, while the world goes crazy around it. Looking at that one calm spot, I look out and feel my heart stop beating for a second, as I swear, even over this vast distance, with so many people fighting and reacting to threats, there in the heart of that sea are three people standing still. My mind instantly makes each of the three out, Markan on the left, Lucia in the middle, and lovely Luna on the right. It is only once I stop to see that Luna is correct that I realize part of why she is so still is that she is staring back at me. And for a moment, I feel that connection, that pulse of realization when two sets of eyes meet and you realize someone important is there watching you, and only you, that caused my heart to stop for a moment. Not in fear, well not entirely in fear, but in recognition that someone is there, and watching out for me. Feeling strengthened by her stare, I pause for only a second, before my heart seems to start up again, and I continue marching at Madame Bloom’s slow measured pace up the spiral staircase.

Once done with the outer ring, it becomes harder to see the crowd. While each layer of the spiral is higher than the last, I find our bodies moving further and further inward, causing a row of black clad healers to obstruct our view. Which is good, as our movement and commitment to the ceremony, knowing that guards are there to protect us, helps to keep the sea of panicked audience members still.

We go up, until we are met with a small standing box. The flag bearers that had raced ahead were performing the finishing touches on the final stand. There is a chain with posts that have been erected around the top. There are also two sets of large metal cylinders at the top that were standing upright at one point, as the flag bearers placed the ceremonial flags straight up at first, before locking the flags into place with a latch, then lowered the flags to hang at an angle. Both ceremonial flags of the country now waved prominently at 45-degree angles. Next to them is the flag of the United Empires, a symbol of all the empires that have gathered here today to witness this ceremony. The flag of the United Empires also serves as a testament to the power of the warriors protecting our citizens from this apparent violence going on below us.

I am not certain if I approve of us forcing this ceremony to continue, but I also see it from the other side. If we stopped a ceremony at the merest hint of opposition, then we would not be able to have any celebrations. That said, I do know that if the crowd was allowed to flee, it would cause more damage to the citizens, and likely expose more of them to the threats going on around us. Now that we are at the top, just when it is clear that I am locked here at the top of this death spiral, that is when the final few members of the crowd break free from the crowd of onlookers and rush towards the rows of healers.

“For the Scr—” the protestors cry out, their words getting caught in the wind, and being cut off by a violent punch to the gut by one of the guards near us.

Yet, that doesn’t seem to be the end, for down below, my senses pulse as I instantly recognize danger nearby. Taking a moment to activate my Divine Sight, I see something that sends chills down my spine, for now that my sight is enhanced by my bloodline power to see ghosts, and now all forms of supernatural, I see black wriggling masses of shadow below.

Once again, I wonder about the effectiveness of forcing a ceremony to go on, even while active threats are being dealt with. But then I see the moment of horror, as those same wriggling shadows, each one covering a Death Eater in a protective layer of shadow, swim forward, and lunge at the healers who neither have a country or lineage to protect them. These are the weakest healers, and the ones that instantly begin to panic and run away from the charging protesters. Not that they can see the threats looming in their shadows but because the guards that should be protecting them are already engaged.

Seeing that we are seconds from disaster, that’s when I decide I am done standing around, waiting for someone to save me. That is when I make my move.
