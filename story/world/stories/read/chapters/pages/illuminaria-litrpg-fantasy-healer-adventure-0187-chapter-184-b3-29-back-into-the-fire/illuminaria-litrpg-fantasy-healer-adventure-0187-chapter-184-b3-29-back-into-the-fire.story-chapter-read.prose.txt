184 / B3-29: Back into the Fire

“Alright, are you ready for Phase Two, Joe?” Myllo asked, still staring in amazement at the recovered founder, while Joe gave the shug monkey as much love as he could. The korrigan flapped an arm out toward Joe, pushing toward him a piece of white fabric. “Take this and tie it around your forehead.”

The guild leader was holding the long, white scarf Zaebos had been wearing.

[Sash of Sacred Sanctuary] (Item: Apparel: Back/Head/Torso/Waist - Epic): This item improves your resistance levels by one increment. The type of resistance that is enhanced depends on which body slot the item is placed in.

• If the item is worn on your back, your Magical Resistances are enhanced.• If the item is worn on your head, your Mental Resistances are enhanced. • If the item is worn on your Torso, your Physical Damage Resistances are enhanced.• If the item is worn on your waist, your Physical Condition Resistances are enhanced.

Restrictions: Level 15, and this item cannot be worn by beings with any of the following natures: Baleful, Desecrated, Fiendish, or Unhallowed.

“With that on your noggin, your major mentals jump up to massive. And your stun resistance skill will function as if it had at least ten more ranks. Now, don’t you go and lose that. That’s an heirloom we borrowed from Lady Blackgrove. We’re gonna need that back.”

“And you stuck it on Zaebos?” Joe questioned.

“Made the most sense. Companions tend to get double mental resistances, so they can’t be turned against their masters. That’s why we were all so surprised when you were able to send Z after Muck,” Myllo recalled with a grin. “The sash ramped her resistences further. Also, hyenas don’t have thumbs, so no potions for them. Which reminds me. Stun will be your biggest danger. I have eight [Lucid Juice] vials for you. All we have left.”

The korrigan’s gaze finally shifted off the Swordmaster being treated by Mortalius. As he handed over a pouch of small potions, Myllo looked up at Joe’s shoulder. “Can I safely assume you are going with him, Yuk?” The swarm of mostly scarabs fluttered into the air for a second before resettling back down across Joe. “We thought as much. Ok. Here are some [Beads of Dispelling]. If Joe gets stunned or some other control spell gets in his way, pop one of those to get rid of it.”

“Gooooottttttt iiiiiiitttttt,” the chorus of bugs articulated in their wobbling voice. The small, round magic pearls vanished into the chitinous throng.

“Last and by no means least, this goes around your neck.” He held out a crimson, crystalline collar. Joe took it and realized he could see through the choker. It was fabricated entirely from magic. Joe began to look at the formation patterns woven into the item and was again hit with a wave of jealousy. Vexor’s work was so precise and delicate that Joe felt like a kindergartener with his current level of warding aptitude. Seeing Myllo’s impatient expression, Joe clipped the neckband around his throat.

“You know you still haven’t explained what the heck it is we are doing, Myllo,” Joe stated as he settled the ruby collar in place.

“I’m getting there,” the expedition captain began before being interrupted.

“Twas my idear,” twanged a familiar smug-sounding hunter. Joe looked back to see Graymuck had somehow snuck right up behind him. He was mildly surprised, but if the banderling had been a real threat, Joe was sure Yuk would have warned him.

The archer handed Joe the [Hide of the Hunter]. It looked and smelled like Muck had even expended a cleaning charm on the once rot-covered pelt. Giving the archer a thankful nod, Joe donned the furs, configuring it into the combat shape of hauberk and hood.

“Can’t wait ta see yer face,” Graymuck gloated, as the hide settled around Joe.

“Don’t help, Muck,” Myllo huffed. “OK. The plan is simple. In a few seconds, you and two passengers are going to charge up the last set of stairs and into the upper hall. Vexor is sure that there is a spatial field, meaning it’s going to be much bigger than the actual top of the ziggurat. When you get close enough, you unload and smack that collar,” Myllo stated, miming a punch to his throat. “Vex hung a teleportation spell in there, just waiting for you to trigger it.”

“Why are you being so cagey?” Joe asked, scowling at the short team leader. “Two passengers? Yuk and … ?”

“Me,” piped a tiny voice. Joe looked down to see the trembling little mousling by his foot. Joe's heart immediately went out to the minuscule murine guilder. Taylyn’s all-black eyes were wide with fear, and her two-foot frame was quivering continuously.

Joe looked up to ask why they wanted him to further traumatize this terrified little lady, but his words were lost when his gaze fell on the huge, tusky grin plastered over the banderling’s face.

“Dis is gonna be EPIC!” the archer hooted.

Joe’s focus cycled between the beastmaster's massive smile, Myllo’s look of consternation, and Taylyn's clear terror. The russet-bearded man approached the tiny woman and began talking quietly with her. He finally understood why Myllo was being so evasive; the guild captain clearly had reservations of his own about sending Taylyn into the fray.

Feeling awkward, Joe stepped back to give the pair more space, only to have the archer’s long fingers grip his shoulder and pull them to the other side of the pavilion. The tusked hunter tapped his pebbly blue-gray skin for Yuk. A moment later, Joe heard the gangly warrior’s voice twang in his head.

‘Heya, Bug Boy,’ Graymuck sent telepathically. ‘Been a while since I had one o’ yer ticks. Let’s catch up after this brouhaha is settled. Now Joe, from the look on yer face, ya still seem confused. Didna no one tell ya about our apocalyptic lil’ lassie there?’

‘I heard she can create explosions,’ Joe sent back, getting a mental raspberry in return from the Hellion.

‘That's like saying Z’s just a dogo. She don’t pop off firecrackers, bucko. Yer sweetheart says Bomber Girl could level a mountain if’n she needed to. She ain’t done it yet, but I shore wanna see it when she does.’

‘Kendell said that?’ Joe questioned. ‘Is she really that powerful at level twenty?’

Yuk's shaky mental voice answered before Graymuck could. ‘We heard Tezeno say that the guild house gets jumpy seers coming in all the time, telling us how we’re harboring Fort Coral’s doom.’

‘Harsh,’ Joe replied through the mental channel.

‘Yeah. Actually, they sometimes say the same crap about us, too. Kendell told us Taylyn has a Perfect affinity for {Vortex} magic. That’s {Chaos} and {Space} combined. {Space} is one of the most potent affinities. And {Chaos} is by far the hardest to control. What a combination to be able to use at max power.’

“That’s why ar girl’s a nervous wreck all da time,” Graymuck drawled, quietly but out loud. “A true prophet once purdicted her power would be the thing that kills her, so she scared ta death a usin’ it. But she also is a gods-damned wonder. Got control like Broon’s got blowflies. I seen her once shatter a single bottle an not even nick nothing else.”

“So, the plan is to blow the top off the pyramid, breaking the loom? Geez, what is it with explosions today?” Joe bemoaned. “This will be the fourth one. It better not be worse than our first one?”

“Guarantee ya; this one’ll be bigga. MUCH, much bigga. You just make sure that when Tay says so, you hit that collar Vexa made ya,” the banderling advised, as he grabbed the cambion sorcerer’s shoulders and gave them a good shake. Vexor had been sitting at a workbench beside where Joe, Graymuck, and Yuk had been standing, manipulating some insanely complex magical array. “You wanna be as far away as that port’ll take ya,” the archer proclaimed.

“Is that why the Hellions are not going? Too hard to get everyone out at once,” Joe questioned the somewhat-crazed archer.

“Thar might be that too, but the real reason is we need ya focused on one thing; just get in, drop, and get out. More folks be’in mind-whapped with ya would just make yer job harder.”

“Why go up into the hall at all, then?” Joe asked. “Why not just place the charge on the stairs and blow the lid off the place from a reasonably safe spot?”

“I can answer that one,” Vexor chimed in, scowling as he fixed the error the Graymuck’s jostling had introduced into his floating ruby formulas. “The Erlking Hall is a bound domain. That means the fey lord will have almost complete control over that space. The only chance you will have to make this work will be to drop the explosion core either too close or too unexpectedly for the Erlking to shunt it away quickly enough. Even reaching him, or the loom, will be a challenge since he will be able to warp the space around you. You have to move in unpredictable ways if you want to make any headway through a fey’s domain.”

“But if we put a big enough bomb on the floor below his …” Joe started to ask.

“He will just move the whole top floor into liminal space and reform it after the blast,” Vex professed. “Taylyn’s abilities have no subtlety to them. That much power will be easily detectable as soon as it is primed to detonate. Your hide, being soulbound, has your cloaking ability. Our hope is that it will hide her long enough to get the job done.”

“But you don’t know for sure? This is just a gamble?”

“What ain’t bucko?” Graymuck countered.

“Fair enough. So we drop the nuke point blank, break the collar, hoping the teleport is faster than the bomb and the bomb is faster than the Erlking. Okay. You sure you wanna be part of this insanity, bud?” Joe checked, looking at the scarabs clinging to the hide.

‘We’re all in, Joe.’

“I’d pay ta see that show ifn I could,” the acher sighed. “Guess I’ll just have to find a good spot ta watch it from out here.”

Since the mousekin needed to be both screened and protected by the heavily resistant pelt, Joe spent a minute of concentration making a good spot for her. He shaped a pouch for her to sit in on the inside of the shaggy morphic armor. It was like what a kangaroo would have for her joey. He added a section of leather warded with Thickhide over the compartment. The extra shielding should repel claws and arrows.

He then used his last charge to cleanse himself and his armor. Graymuck had freshened the hide, but the rest of Joe was far too rank for a guest. Joe could not subject the sweet-looking gentlemouse to that.

“We should get going, Myllo,” Joe suggested as he finished his preparations. “The longer we wait, the more undead the loom is going to respawn.”

“You’re right. OK. Keep her safe, son. And you make sure you teleport out of there as soon as Taylyn gives you the go.”

“Are you ready, Ma'am?” Joe asked, extending his hand to pick up the small guilder.

“Ma’am? I’m not that old, Joe,” she chided softly, stepping into his grip.

“Sorry.” Joe stood, embarrassed, until he noticed the first small smile he had seen on her face since she had arrived. “You’re teasing me, aren't you?”

“Yes, I am. A break in the tension ... something I sorely need.” Opening his hairy coat, he let her step inside. “My, isn’t this snug?”

When she settled, Joe nodded his readiness to Myllo.

“You hanging on tight, Bud?”

‘We’re set. Connecting Taylyn into the mind-link now. Later, Muck,’ the swarmling stated as he swapped the archer out and the mouseling into the [Parasitic Bond].

Taylyn Dale has joined your party

“The gate is ready,” the red-horned genius remarked. “A spatial warp will cling to you when you pass through the portal. The warp will be so tightly confined, even in his domain, the Erlking won’t be able to move or expel you. He will still be able to shift away from you, though, so don’t let this turn into a chase.”

“Go get 'em!” the gangly archer chortled, whacking Joe on the back.

He downed one of the [Lucid Juice] draughts, flared [Hunter’s Pursuit], and kicked off the ground, sending the three of them hurling through Vexor’s rift.
