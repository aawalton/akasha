Demon Hat has now finished!

It's odd to write those words, because this represents the first series I've written to completion. It will certainly not be the last. It also doesn't really seem like an ending due to the vast editing I've been doing with my publisher and on my own - which will be continuing for the next month at least before the KU launch of book 2. I've even got the notion that there's at least 3/4 extra chapters I could slot into book 4 before the end (even though its already the largest entry in the series by far!).

The amount of fun I've had bringing Ethan's story to life is reward in itself, and has taught me a great deal about serialization, LitRPG, and genre expectations in general. Demon Hat has been my own way of interrogating many of these tropes, and the result has been a series that has left me satisfied with what I've been able to do in this genre. I hope its given you something to think about.

What’s next?

At the moment, I'm fully committed to Demon's Amazon launches, but also working on my next writing projects. I have returned to my last novel series, Reborn as a Fantasy General, which you can read here: https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/83787/reborn-as-a-fantasy-general-army-building-isekai

Fantasy General is a Military Fantasy Isekai series entirely separate from Demon Hat. For the foreseeable future, this is the genre I will be focused on.
