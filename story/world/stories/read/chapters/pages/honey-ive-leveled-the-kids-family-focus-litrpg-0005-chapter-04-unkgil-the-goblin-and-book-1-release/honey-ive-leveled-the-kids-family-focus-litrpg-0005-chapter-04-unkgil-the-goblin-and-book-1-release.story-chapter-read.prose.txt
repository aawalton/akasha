The goblin cried so hard that he hiccupped. A few hearts floated above the monster’s head, making the entire scene emotionally confusing. “Not if you befriend every mob! This isn’t how we play the game,” Naiad grumbled, annoyed at being patted like a little kid.

Triangle still cowered by Grandma, who rubbed his back. The screams from deeper in the mine bothered him.

Grandma clicked her tongue. “We follow rules all day in our lives. I loved being chaos in a video game because it didn’t matter. Right Triangle?”

“Chaos!” he said in a deep voice, the one he used to mock Dad. He could flip the switch from scared to humor in a second, just like Unkgil could flip his status from mob to NPC.

“Chaos is creation.” Unkgil said back. “Need Unkgil’s tools in back. Unkgil wants Grandma with yous to defend home. Unkgil’s wife moved here week past. City.” He paused, looked back, bit his tongue, and then kicked with his leg. “City swing leg past week. Worst time.”
