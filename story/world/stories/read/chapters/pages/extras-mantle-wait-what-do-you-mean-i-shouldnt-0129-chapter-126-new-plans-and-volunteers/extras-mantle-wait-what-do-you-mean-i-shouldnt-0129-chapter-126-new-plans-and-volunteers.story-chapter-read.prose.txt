An hour.

That's how long the briefing had dragged on after Mathew dropped the question: "What's the plan?"

Heck, if he knew. Life had been going so chaotic that Jin had started freestyling plans, and they'd seemed to work so far. But that didn't mean he didn't have ideas—he had plenty of those, scattered across his brain like shrapnel—and Mathew wasn't wrong to nail down the details.

The problem was his grip on Sovereign's Indifference kept slipping.

He didn’t know what, but there was something stuck inside him, and it was growing bigger every second, pressing against his ribs like a balloon slowly inflating. Jin could feel it expanding with each breath, each heartbeat, and the grey calm that usually filtered everything was starting to crack around the edges.

Still, he'd managed to get through an hour of laying out the crucial primary and secondary target locations. All the cult outposts he remembered from harvested memories. The trojan he'd installed in the core ritual, which got Illiana all excited and talkative until Mathew's glare and Jin's deadpan stare shut her down.

They'd gone through everything he knew about the ritual. Disruption strategies. Timelines. Points of failure.

And then the two most important questions.

First: just how many resources did he have on hand?

Jin didn't actually know the answer, so he'd simply dumped piles of spatial storage artifacts onto the table his ring held.

Each of the spatial storages was filled to the brim with everything they'd looted from raids. He'd barely had time to go through a portion of them.

By the time the tally reached a hundred storage artifacts, the people in the room were left dumbfounded.

And even more so when he mentioned this was a small cache. The real storage was with Joe.

Obviously, he didn't dump any high Epic-grade or truly important items he had found. But the rest? They could make do with that, after all, they are in this together now.

The second question: how would he get people rank-boosted?

Jin had given a very short answer, "It’s all related to the quest and his mantle’ and refused to elaborate further.

That single hour felt like six, with Jin getting more and more uncomfortable. Asking Angel only revealed it was related to the harvest, which was about as helpful as getting hot chocolate in the current situation.

And so, having said his part, he got up and walked out into the corridor with Rudy falling half a step behind.

The grey calm was still holding, but barely. Pain was building again, pushing against the mental walls Jin had erected. His essence channels felt like someone had taken sandpaper to them, and there was a persistent burning sensation deep in his chest that Angel kept flagging as "concerning but not immediately fatal."

Great. That's reassuring.

« Your sarcasm is noted. »

You are having way too much fun, Angel.

Rudy was giving him that look again—the one that said he wanted to say something but wasn't sure if he should, and Jin stopped walking, letting Sovereign's Indifference drop just enough to meet his friend's eyes properly.

"I'm fine." He said.

"You're not fine," Rudy said immediately. "You look like you're about to—"

"I'm fine enough," Jin corrected. He rolled his shoulders. "Rather, you should check on Reyana. Then Joe. Make sure she's actually resting. And as for Joe, he can do whatever the hell he wants."

Rudy frowned. "Jin—"

"NO, I'm serious." Jin kept his voice level. "We've finally gotten a little breathing room from all the chaos. Use it, because we're going to be very, very busy in the next couple of days."

He reached out, placing his hand on Rudy's shoulder. "That means you, too, dummy."

Jin could see the troubled expression on his friend's face. "What is it?"

Rudy sighed and looked at him. "I was almost useless back there."

Jin blinked. "What? No, you weren't."

"You heard me." Rudy's hands clenched into fists at his sides. "Both you and Reyana were down there fighting for your lives while I was stuck—"

"Controlling the chaos," Jin finished for him. His voice went flat. Serious. "Rudy, look at me."

Rudy met his gaze reluctantly.

"We're fighting a war," Jin said. "And just us? We're not enough to win. We need our people. We need morale, and we need hope." He squeezed Rudy's shoulder once. "If it weren't for you and your father, we wouldn't have gotten things in order so fast. The bastion would still be tearing itself apart."

"But—"

"No buts." Jin shook his head. "You did exactly what needed to be done. And besides..."

He paused, making sure Rudy was actually listening.

"I'm almost useless in combat for a long, long while. My essence channels are scorched. My body's still processing the harvest. Which means it'll be all on you and Reyana to lead the attacks when they come."

Jin met Rudy's eyes directly. "Can I lean on you for that?"

Something shifted in Rudy's expression. The uncertainty faded, replaced by the stubborn determination Jin had seen a hundred times before.

Rudy straightened, bumping his fist against his chest. "Yes."

Jin allowed himself a small smile. "Good. Now go check on—"

The door opened again.

Jin turned, watching as four figures emerged from the briefing room. Veric with his ever-present cigarette, Vera trailing slightly behind, Elenor looking exhausted but determined, and Hans bringing up the rear.

All three—Vera, Elenor, Hans, except Veric, of course—snapped into crisp salutes the moment they saw Jin.

Jin stared at them. Then sighed. "I'm not military. You can be at ease."

They didn't move.

"Sir, you are our new leader," Hans said, keeping his salute. "Proper respect is a must."

"Well, whatever floats your boat," Jin muttered.

Veric chuckled, smoke curling from his lips. "Told you he'd hate it."

"Shut up."

Just then, a notification materialized in Jin's vision.

« FIRST MILESTONE OF HARVESTED POWER REACHED. 15% COMPLETE. BEGIN CONSOLIDATION? »

Okay? What's this, Angel?

« You need to find a safe room and enter a meditative state. I cannot process any more power before we consolidate this portion. You have seven minutes. »

Seven minutes?! Wow, very reliable.

« The absorption rate is accelerating. If we don't consolidate now, the next milestone will hit while you're still unstable. That would be... bad. »

Bad?

« Ka-boom kinda bad. »

Rudy must have caught the scowl on Jin's face because his concern immediately returned. "Jin? You okay?"

"Fine," he lied. He turned to address the group. "I need some meditation and probably lots of rest to recover. But before that—"

He fixed his attention on Veric. "We need to begin. You'll need help, so take whoever you need. If I remember correctly, Elenor's a people person. Have her be the front of the whole operation along with the twins."

"That sounds good." Veric nodded sagely while Elenor frowned.

"Wait, what—" she started, but Jin cut her off.

"Have her dress into something presentable and parade her around until she's fully recovered. That'll teach her not to go alone into ambush sites." Jin said, turning to Hans. "Thoughts?"

Hans snapped to attention. "That sounds wonderful, sir. She does have a tendency to disregard her superiors."

"What?!" Elenor's voice went up an octave.

Jin nodded. "Then get her started. There should be plenty of stuff in what I gave you before." He turned to Vera. "You were a mind specialist, right?"

Vera straightened. "Yes, sir."

"Are you a mage?"

She nodded.

"I'm also a mage!" Elenor perked up, then quickly added, "Sir."

"Great, that’s good to know, but you have your task," Jin's attention stayed on Vera. "Can you cast a sorcery if I give you the incantation and the address?"

"Yes, sir, but... I only know some simple, one-verse sorceries."

"That won't be an issue. This is just a two-verse sorcery, but with your mind specialty, it should be a breeze." Jin said. "I'll have the sorcery delivered to you in an essence crystal sometime soon, along with instructions."

He then turned to Veric, who straightened slightly, cigarette pausing halfway to his mouth. "So am I to begin the recruitment?"

"Yes," Jin glanced at the others. "But Veric, I want you to keep Vera by your side and have people tested with the sorcery I'll give her. Test in batches of thirty. What I'm looking for right now is anyone with hints of magical potential. Also, ask Illiana to craft an essence and rune assessment orb to verify aptitude. Start with volunteers, but if people are being difficult..." He paused. "Persuade them."

"Persuade," Veric repeated, smile widening. "I like how that can mean so many things."

"Don't break anyone who might be useful," Jin said flatly. "We need numbers, but we also need people who can actually think more than they can fight."

Vera spoke up, her voice quiet. "What's the target number?"

"As many as possible," Jin said. "But realistically? For this task, I'm looking for thousands."

"What?"

"Thousands? Sir?"

Even Veric had a troubled expression while Rudy was chuckling by his side, mumbling something Jin caught: "Typical Jin."

"Yes, thousands. But also remember, I don't care about their power levels. Just that they have magical potential and aren't below fifteen or above sixty in age." Jin said. "Leave the rest to me. You'll see the results in a week or so, but for the first couple of days, I want those with the highest aptitude or anyone with prior experience."

"Yes, sir!" Hans whistled low. "That's... ambitious."

"That's necessary," Jin's voice went cold. "We have twenty-five days before we all die. Let’s say nineteen to be safe before the cult initiates its ritual. And when that happens, anyone not Order IV will die. Simply die."

Silence settled over the corridor.

Elenor broke it. "Where do we start? And what do I wear?"

"Lower levels," Veric said immediately. "The civilian sectors. Lots of people, lots of desperation, and just enough organization that we can actually process them efficiently."

"Hans, stay with them. Your task is to keep order and single out anyone with potential to join as a combatant."

"Understood." Hans saluted again—Jin didn't bother correcting him this time. "We'll begin immediately."

"Good, also take the doctor Maya with you," Jin could feel the seven-minute timer ticking down in his head. Six minutes now. Maybe five and a half. "Go. I'll check progress later."

The group dispersed, Veric already pulling out his data-slate to coordinate with the twins, Vera and Elenor falling into step behind him, Hans moving in the opposite direction toward the lower-level access lifts.

Rudy lingered. "You sure you're—"

"Go," Jin said. "Check on Reyana. That's an order."

Rudy hesitated, then nodded. "Fine. But if you collapse and die, I'm dragging your corpse back to life just to kill you again."

"Sure."

Jin waited until Rudy had rounded the corner before allowing himself to sag slightly against the wall.

Five minutes.

He pushed off, forcing his legs to move. His temporary assigned quarters weren't far—just two corridors and a lift down—he could make it.

« Four minutes and thirty seconds. »

Not helping, Angel.

« Wasn't trying to help. Was providing accurate time information. Because I've found you are actually beyond any help. »

Haha... made me laugh.

« Point proven. »

Shut up.

Jin made it to the lift, slapped the call button, and watched the floor indicator crawl upward with agonizing slowness.

The doors finally opened. Jin stepped inside, hit the button for his floor, and leaned against the wall as the lift descended.

« Three minutes. »

I'm aware.

His room's door recognized him immediately, sliding open without requiring manual input. Jin stumbled inside, letting the door close behind him before making his way to the bathroom, discarding all his clothes, and dropping into the cramped bathtub.

“Ahhh, the water feels so good.”

« Two minutes. »

“Alright, Angel. Walk me through this.”

« Sit. Cross-legged position. Hands on knees. »

Jin settled into position, palms resting on his knees.

« Breathe. Slow. Controlled. Let the Eternal Sovereign's Path guide the flow. »

"Start the consolidation."

« Confirmed. Beginning power consolidation sequence. Brace yourself... this is going to feel a little rough. »

Jin closed his eyes and fell into the breathing pattern he'd drilled a thousand times. In through the nose, hold, out through the mouth. Each cycle pulled essence through his channels, and with each pull, the harvest responded.

Power flooded through his essence channels like liquid fire, but it wasn't painful—not exactly—both the Origin Code and Angel were smoothing the process, filtering the raw energy into something his body could process.

But the power still hit like a wave. His First Star spun faster, pulling the torrent through its filter, breaking down the foreign power into something his own.

His skin prickled as every nerve lit up with the foreign power trying to carve new pathways through his body.

Not happening.

Jin tightened his focus, guiding the flow. The Eternal Sovereign's Path wasn't just a breathing technique; it was a framework, a pattern that forced chaotic essence into structured channels.

His second star joined the rotation, and the cryo affinity also kicked in, and suddenly, the burning heat of Trish's power cooled to something manageable. Ice-blue essence threaded through the red-gold chaos, tempering it, shaping it.

« Consolidation at 30%... 45%... 60%... »

« Careful. Don't force it. Let it settle naturally. »

Easy for you to say.

The pressure built. His channels strained, walls stretching thin under the load. For a moment, Jin thought he'd miscalculated—thought maybe the harvest was too much, too fast—

His Third Star flared to life.

The three points of his constellation aligned, and suddenly everything snapped into place. The chaotic influx stopped fighting him and started flowing with him, following the pattern his Stars had carved.

Jin felt his essence heart pulse. Once. Twice. Each beat pushed more power through his system, reinforcing channels, widening pathways, building capacity.

Then something clicked.

The essence stopped resisting and started cooperating.

His Second Star layered the influx with cryo affinity, tempering the heat. His Third Star wove everything together into a cohesive pattern.

And Jin felt it… the barrier between Order III and Order IV.

It was right there.

Close enough to touch.

He reached for it—

« No. »

Angel's voice was sharp.

« You're not ready. The foundation isn't stable yet. Push through now, and you'll cripple your future growth. »

But—

« Trust me. »

Jin exhaled slowly and pulled back. The barrier remained tantalizingly close, but he let it be.

Fine. We wait.

The consolidation continued, and power settled into his channels, reinforcing them, widening them, making space for more. His essence heart pulsed stronger. His stats crept upward.

« Status stabilizing. Absorption rate normalized. You're clear. »

Jin opened his eyes. The room looked the same, but he felt different. Stronger and more centered. Like he'd crossed some invisible threshold, even without breaking through to Order IV.

Angel, what changed? Because I definitely feel changed.

« Significantly better. Essence channel integrity restored to 70%. Bloodline integration is progressing smoothly. Harvest absorption rate has stabilized at 3% per hour. Initiating the second round of harvest at the next milestone: 20% of the harvest. »

Jin stood slowly, rolling his shoulders and flexing his hands. Everything felt... different. Stronger. More solid.
