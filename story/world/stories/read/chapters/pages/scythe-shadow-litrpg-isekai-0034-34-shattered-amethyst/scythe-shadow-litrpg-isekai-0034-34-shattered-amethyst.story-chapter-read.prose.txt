The sea lapped up against my feet as sparks sizzled across my body, reveling in the proximity to a strong conductor.

My Health and Volition both blinked in my interface. Barely any left.

I closed my eyes.

I'm sorry, Avery…

I tilted my head to the side, tears forming. Waiting for the final blow. When I opened my eyes, I noticed my scythe sticking blade-first out of the sand nearby. I didn't even realize I'd dropped it. It still glowed purple with the Amethyst Will of that third Unchained.

Zeus stepped into view. "I'm really gonna enjoy this."

If only I could've relinquished that Will during battle, I could've refreshed my Volition. I could've stood a chance… I—

Wait. I don't need to relinquish it. I need to advance with it! But I don't know how…

Zeus lifted his hand, a ball of lightning forming between his fingers.

Calliope said something about advancing rank, but it was right after I'd relinquished my first Will. My mind had been swimming, my body a wreck.

I searched my memory as Zeus' ball of lightning grew larger. I told my memory to get its ass in gear.

Then I had it! 'You have to bind a higher, compatible Will to your own.' Okay. Not helpful. What does that even mean?

An idea came to me. I remembered when I'd first arrived in Penumbra. When I'd first met Zeus. I'd worried that he stole my Will with his scythe.

I looked down at my chest where my Will resided.

I looked over at the scythe containing an Astral Affinity Amethyst Will.

Is it really that simple?

I had nothing to lose.

I stretched a hand out to my scythe, calling it back. The blade twirled through the air, landing perfectly in my grasp.

Zeus brought down his electric strike.

I shoved the glowing purple blade of my scythe…

Into my own chest.

…

The world seemed to move in slow motion for a heartbeat. An amethyst crystalline dome sparkled into existence around me. Zeus's lightning blasted against it, sparks flying in all directions. Zeus ricocheted backward, flying head over heels in the air.

The melted air smelled strongly of ozone.

I didn't see anything else. Blackness overcame my vision.

[Advancement to Amethyst Rank commencing…]

Holy shit. I did it. I actually did it.

I suddenly stood in blackness, a vast void that I imagined the space between galaxies to look like. Not at all like the setting of normal Will relinquishing, which had been lit with a billion stars.

Instead, fuzzy purple haze filled the air and purple waves of minuscule dust-like particles splashed around me. I didn't remember opening my eyes, yet I could see all this.

Emotions brushed against my memory as I waded through the black and the waves flowed into me.

My heart tingled with warmth, like how it felt the time Avery had 'accidentally' touched her hand to mine as we walked side-by-side before we started dating.

Then I felt anger. Sadness. I didn't know why.

The waves undulated faster, targeting me from all directions.

I suddenly wanted to hurt something. Someone. Tears of uncontrollable fear rolled down my cheeks, but it wasn't my own fear. I didn't even no the source of it.

There was no escape, no reprieve from the powerful feelings bubbling inside me, like a schizophrenic nightmare. I squeezed my eyes shut.

Then it was all gone.

Instead, I was running through the rain at night. My body moved by its own volition. I had no control, like I was watching a movie in first person.

A women with an umbrella chased after me, giggling. It wasn't Avery, but it wasn't anyone else I knew either. Was this a vision of the future? I couldn't imagine a scenario where I was with anyone other than Avery. Maybe a peek into an alternate timeline? If I'd made different choices, would this be a scene from that life?

I felt genuine love for this woman, even though I'd never seen her in my life. It warred against the confusion within me like two hurricanes colliding. How was this even possible?

It was almost as if I was experiencing part of someone else's soul—future me? Alternate me? Whoever it was.

And why is this appearing to me now? Calliope said the rank transition would be overwhelming, but this was nothing like what I imagined.

I reached out a hand to the mystery woman as I stepped off a curb into a street. She held her hand to mine, an engagement ring on her left ring finger. Bright light twinkled through the diamond.

A brighter light glared into my eyes from the other direction.

The blaring of a horn.

Then the world flashed abruptly black.

But right now, I needed a skill I could rely on. Not one I'd need to learn to use with my life on the line. And a new skill might not matter anyway if I couldn't keep up with Zeus' Aero Shift.

It felt impossibly painful to pass up a new skill, but I didn't have much of a choice this time.

The world of Penumbra blinked back into focus. I remained within the crystalline structure, my Health and Volition fully recovered.

My sense of smell returned first. The salt air of the ocean.

Hearing next. The slashing of a blade against my protective shell as Zeus hammered away at the dome with his scythe without leaving so much as a scratch.

Rage practically poured from his eyes.

I didn't know how long I'd been gone, but it couldn't have been too long or he'd have given up. The protective shell probably wouldn't hold up much longer now that I was awake, so I formulated a quick plan.

If it worked, this battle could be over in three seconds.

I Aero Shifted through the amethyst shield. The world blurred into lines of motion around me. It felt like I had no control, yet somehow more at the same time, moving at twice the speed I had previously, careening in a tight arc around Zeus. I ended up directly behind him.

Surprisingly, the amethyst shield remained completely in tact in the sand where I'd been, even though I'd Shifted away. Zeus' next strike shattered the crystalline structure though, catching him off guard. He stumbled forward. I Shifted after him, grabbing his face with my carapace hand.

His eyes went wide between my massive armored fingers. He tried to Aero Shift away, but I held tight, matching his movement with an Aero Shifted of my own, never releasing him.

Then I cast Life Drain.

The dark mist entered his eyes, nose, ears, mouth and instantly stained red.

"Die, asshole!" I yelled.

Zeus screamed like a baby. He scraped his fingernails against my hand in pure panicked agony. His shrieks morphed to cries as he tried to hit me.

I held on.

I drained his life until there was nothing left.

His cloak whirled around him, wrapping his body into a tight ball, then imploded to nothing. Back to Vitria.
