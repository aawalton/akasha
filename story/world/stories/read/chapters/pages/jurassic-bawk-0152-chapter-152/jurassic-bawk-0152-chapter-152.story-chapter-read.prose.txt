“The System turned me from a slavering idiot into the sophisticated and intelligent creature that you see before you. I would rather die than go back to the way that I was before,” Phatagin screamed, his voice hoarse with anger.

The purple-robed giant chuckled.

“Well, there has been plenty of death today already, and there will be more to come before the day is finished.  And I have been patient, so patient with you, slaves of the System.  I have offered you mercy, offered you a chance to cast off your chains, but you have spat upon your one chance for survival.  So be it.  My goodwill has reached its limits, and now that the System can no longer empower you or gorge itself upon your deaths, I may now dispose of you at my pleasure.”

“The only thing you’re doing to dispose of is your own carcass,” Elidi the Sun Queen snarled as she strained to bring her power to bear against the intruder.  But her solar beam was weakening.  Charlemagne could almost look directly at it without scorching his retinas.

“How eloquent,” the four-armed giant deadpanned as he pointed the thin stick in his upper right hand toward the Sun Queen.  Even from a distance, the rooster felt a powerful tug on the mana swirling around him as the tip of the stick began to glow.  Mana swirled around the tip, gathering in luminous torrents that spiraled tighter with every passing moment.  The currents wound upon themselves, compressing into a point that seemed to hungrily devour every scrap of mana that reached it.  The glow that surrounded the stick grew in intensity with every passing moment as the sheer concentration of mana began to make Charlemagne’s beak ache.

But all that mana had to come from somewhere.  The barrier wall that was protecting the four-armed giant diminished as the magical energy was redirected into the wand.

“Hurry, let’s push through!” Phasima cried as she resumed her attack, targeting the weak spot that her sister had created previously.

“There’s no time,” the Sun Queen cried as she began to marshal her own strength.  “He’s going to switch to offense, be ready!”

Charlemagne watched, conflicted as to what the best strategy would be.  The amount of mana that the invader casually wielded was vast beyond measure: even he would have struggled to control that much power, much less defend against it.  If he were to be targeted directly by whatever attack was forthcoming, he would instantly die.

But, on the other hand, if he could get close enough, he might be able to disrupt his enemy’s control over the attack, ruining it before it was even launched.  Then he would steal as much of the mana as he could before his foe could recover.  But that would require him to leave the balcony, and he felt a modicum of safety from being perched above the fray.

“Perchance is there anything you can do to aid them, Charlemagne,” Phatagin asked hopefully.  “My own powers seem to have diminished greatly due to the System lockout.  The only part of me that remains unaffected is my Core.  Well, perhaps unaffected is not the right word, it seems to be changing into something…different.  I must take the opportunity to study this phenomenon.”

Teem, who was standing near, heard the pangolin’s plea as well.

“Teem does not believe that his aid would be productive in this encounter,” the strange Knight answered.  “Teem is continuing to monitor the situation for any changes.”

Charlemagne looked at his Squire, who appeared even more pathetic than usual.  Was this what he wanted to do, just stand around while the powerful beings fought and hope for scraps?  Phasima had jumped into the fight with no hesitation.  It didn’t matter if her motivation was to protect her flock, or if it was completely selfish, she would grow stronger or die.  Wasn’t that what Charlemagne had decided he would do for as long as it was possible: seize power with his own beak, heedless of the cost?

The rooster knew the answer before he had even finished questioning himself.  It was clear the moment he vaulted over the railing that he would always choose the latter option.  But his advance did not remain unnoticed.  The intruder turned his head toward the newest addition to the fight, his two small, beady eyes gleaming with battle lust.

“Ah ah ah, little bird,” the giant chided.  “It’s not yet your turn.  Or at least, it wasn’t until you came running to me.  You truly do not understand the power that you have turned down.  Now die!”

With a flick of his wrist, the intruder pointed the stick at Charlemagne and unleashed a beam of pure energy so powerful that it completely overwhelmed the rooster’s mana sense.  In the face of certain death, Charlemagne could only stare, his body seemingly frozen, as the blast approached.  Even his Ignition Core went silent, as if it had given in to the inevitable.  There was no time to think.  There was no time to even feel fear.  Oblivion awaited.

A gentle push sent him out of the beam’s path, mostly.  His left wing and a good chunk of his torso were still within the attack’s enormous circumference.  The strangely unqualifiable energy passed straight through him.  For a moment, the rooster thought he had escaped unscathed.  Then the shockwave hit, sending him flying into the far corner of the throne room.  At the last moment, he attempted to flap his wings to right himself, only to find that he was flapping one rather than two appendages.  Then he slammed into the wall, knocking the air from his still-functional lung as well as the remnants of the other.  His vision swam as he collapsed into a smoking heap onto the hard floor.  It was so nice and cool there, the rooster thought he might stay there a while.

“Charlemagne!” Phasima cried.  But the Vessarian didn’t have time to worry about the rooster, as the giant’s gaze was drawn by the exclamation.

“That is one tough bird.  I think I shall have some fun with it later, as long as it does not expire before the rest of you do,” the invader laughed.  “But of course, the purging of the System and its defenders must come first.”

The wand now turned toward the two Vessarian sisters.  Phasima was already hurling a jet of fiery mana straight into the shield that protected the four-armed intruder, and the Sun Queen now added her immense power as a foil against the inevitable beam attack.  The air around Elidi crackled dangerously as energy began to swirl around her hands.

“Despair and begone, wielders of lesser magics,” the purple-robed giant intoned as he unleashed the power of his wand again.  The entire throne room shook as another beam of energy blasted out of the tip of the magical artifact.  The blinding white light it emitted seemed to drain the entire universe of color, save for the two Vessarians in its path.  Against the overwhelming power contained within the beam, even the Sun Queen appeared as nothing more than a small yellow dot.

That is, until she unfurled her power once again.

Though her respite was short, the Vessarian had not been idle.  Her solar beam attack lanced out, spiraling around the fiery attack of her sister to form a helix not unlike a striped peppermint stick.  The combined assault slammed into the onrushing energy beam and halted it in its tracks.  But only for a moment.  Then the invader’s attack began to overwhelm the sisters’ combined beams.

Seeing that their charge was now in mortal danger, the Elite Royal Guards, who had so far stood mute as the scene unfolded, sprang into action once again despite the Sun Queen’s orders for them to stand down.

“For the Sun Queen!” they shouted in unison as they charged.

“No!” Elidi screamed as the group approached the dueling energy beams.  But she was not obeyed.  As one, the remaining guards lowered their shields and attempted to muscle back the giant’s energy beam with sheer strength.

“Charlemagne, get up and help them!  Please!” Phasima yelled, sparing a moment to turn her attention away from her own plight and examine the rooster’s injuries.  Although he remained grievously wounded, she was certain that he would recover without difficulty due to his ridiculous healing ability.

But if the rooster heard his companion, he gave no indication.  Nor would it have mattered if he had: the Elite Royal Guards lasted mere seconds before the strange energy worked its way through their bodies, vaporizing them from the inside out.  Even their armor and weapons were completely gone in the aftermath.

“I’ll kill you for that!” the Sun Queen shouted as she redoubled her efforts.  Her beam sputtered wildly for a moment before surging out even stronger than before as she let out a wild, wordless cry of exertion.  The renewed attack once again halted the colossal beam of energy, sending streamers of raw power crackling throughout the throne room.  Up in the balcony, Teem and Teem were struck by a stray bolt, sending them crashing into the hallway, unconscious.  Charlemagne was lashed repeatedly by the chaotic energy, his body convulsing with each blow.  Somehow, Phatagin remained untouched despite his entire focus seemingly having turned inwards.

“I had thought perhaps that you would survive until the System lockout was complete, but I suppose that this is your limit,” the four-armed giant scoffed.  “It is a pity: my presence is sorely required elsewhere.  I could have delegated this assault to a subordinate.”

“Tell that to the corpses of your comrades that lie dead at your feet,” the Sun Queen spat.  “Perhaps you should have left some of them at home if you find that we are such easy prey.”

The intruder laughed and held his two lower arms out wide, gesturing to the carnage all around him.

“This was their holy purpose: to die in the service of ridding this entire dimension of the System’s taint.  You who serve only for your own edification could never understand true conviction, whose wielders do not shirk from laying down their lives for the smallest of gains.  Each and every one of them accompanied me knowing that they would not return.  Can you say the same of your companions?  Were you not enticed here with promises of personal gain?”

“You’re insane!” Elidi shouted back.  “Only a sick or mad creature would willingly seek out its own end.  And there is nothing wrong with wanting to grow stronger, or seeking novelty or comfort.”

“Perhaps I am mad.  But it matters little,” the giant laughed.  “So long as I have the strength to put down the dogs of the System, I will trade every last scrap of my sanity!”

With that, the invader let out a cry.  His beam attack widened as the chamber itself began to shake with the sheer amount of power being unleashed, and the Vessarians’ attack was pushed back once again.

“Charlemagne!  We need help!  Please get up and heal yourself!” Phasima cried in despair as Elidi once again began to flag, unable to sustain her maximum output despite the solar energy flooding into her from above.  As the seconds ticked by, the enemy’s attack began to gain more and more momentum.  The sisters were out of time.

“I love you,” the Sun Queen shouted as the enemy’s beam finally reached her and Phasima.

“And I love you!” Phasima called back as she let her flames die out.  Then, without hesitation, she plunged her left arm deep into her chest and brought it swiftly back out again.  In her hand was a jewel-like object that shone with a soft light that slowly shifted colors.

It was her Manifold Core.

“Phasima!” the Sun Queen screamed as she watched in horror while her sister mutilated herself.

“Forgive me, sister, it was the only way,” Phasima gasped as she positioned herself in front of Elidi.  Then, she thrust her Manifold Core into her sister’s chest.

The Sun Queen screamed.
