New Story: Gunwitch

Last Friday I launched a new story called Gunwitch. It's a LitRPG about a girl who becomes the first [Gunwitch]. Tone is lighter than Bladeweaver (after chapter 13). It’s already on Rising Stars main, and the early response has been great.

Bladeweaver Status

I hired a dev editor for Bladeweaver. After going through the story, the conclusion was that a massive rewrite is needed. Since it’s 260k words and I’m also actively writing Gunwitch, this is going to take a while. I don't know how long the rewrite will take or when I'll be able to continue posting new chapters.

I want to be transparent about this: I'm committed to making Bladeweaver the best story it can be, but it's going to be a significant undertaking. I appreciate your patience.

In the meantime, if you’re looking for something new to read, I think you’ll enjoy Gunwitch. It’s been (and still is) a lot of fun to write.
