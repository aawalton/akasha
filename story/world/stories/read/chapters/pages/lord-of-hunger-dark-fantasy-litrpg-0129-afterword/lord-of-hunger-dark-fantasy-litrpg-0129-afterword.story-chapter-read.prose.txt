All that being said, I made a lot of mistakes.

I’m very glad you all enjoyed this story, but I can do a lot better.

I learned a lot just by writing and posting here on RR and Patreon. About the craft of long form storytelling, and the business of it. I’ve met a bunch of cool authors who have offered their expertise.

Most importantly. I met you guys.

Your comments on every chapter kept me going strong. Even the critical ones. Seeing engagement on my work was and is an awesome feeling, I think I’m hooked on it.

One thing is for certain. This is what I’m doing now. Telling stories is the coolest job I’ll ever get to do, so I’m stickin with it.

Unfortunately that means I have to go dark for a little while. One lesson I learned the hard way about writing for RoyalRoad and Patreon is that one should be over-prepared.

Dear readers, when I dropped this story in mid October I was barely prepared.

I feel like I stumbled across the finish line on this one.

But I did make it to the top three on RS (All).

The numbers are real good all around, especially for a rookie. Especially for a first story.
