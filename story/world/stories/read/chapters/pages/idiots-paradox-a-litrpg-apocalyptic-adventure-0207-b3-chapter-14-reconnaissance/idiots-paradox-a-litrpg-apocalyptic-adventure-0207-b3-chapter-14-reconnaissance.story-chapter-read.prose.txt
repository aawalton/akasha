Logan beamed from ear-to-ear as he walked over to his discarded dagger. Although he hadn’t tested his new armour yet, the material continued to shimmer unless he activated the camouflage effect. It wasn’t glaring, it wasn’t as if he were walking around with sparkling armour… and yet the shimmer clearly indicated the material was System sourced and high quality.

The difference between the sandstone and this new exoskeleton was night and day. It would take a real battle to assess the strength of the material, but if he were a betting man, he’d say it could withstand most pressure points. That meant that not only did he have [Regenerate] to heal him in case of an injury, but now, he had armour to prevent that injury from happening in the first place.

He had no illusions that his armour would stand up against opponents like Pied, but against the queen serpent? It would give him protection against her claws and her sharp hide. He hadn’t forgotten that he’d lost his hand in the encounter in the tunnel. Hell, she’d used her body as a weapon.

As Logan reached the Sabáktes dagger, he crouched down and studied it.

[Sabáktes Dagger. AA grade. This dagger belonged to The Destroyer, the ruler of the first System integrated world. The Sabáktes Dagger is alive.]

The thing resembled a short sword rather than a dagger, the width at least four inches across. Nothing close to his massive sword. It would be handy in a close-quarters fight, but still, it was puzzling as hell. Its blade was black, black like his new armour. If the grubby handle weren’t so dirty, it would blend in with the rest of his outfit.

The System rated it AA grade. Logan knew it was valuable. And yet, at this point, it was a valuable dud.

Oh well. He wasn’t going to leave it behind, and the only way he’d know if it was any use to him was in the heat of battle. With a frown, Logan deployed [Mimicry Armour] to—

Hold on.

He’d used up all the metal in the pouch. How could he manipulate his armour if he didn’t have additional material to work…

To work…

…with.

In front of his eyes, grains of metal flew off his chest, multiplying like hatching flies. His mouth parting in shock, Logan almost didn’t have the presence of mind to use [Mimicry Armour] to capture the buggers before they fell to the ground.

With a blink, he raised the new grains into the air, forming them into another mini storm cloud. Holy shit, this metal was turning into a goldmine! If it never ran out, did that mean that he could use it for other things? Logan’s breathing sped up, his nerves on fire with excitement. A wall! He could craft a wall around the complex! He could craft barriers, frigging bridges, fucking hell. There were too many possibilities to count. He could craft items for his store, and on and on…

Logan’s mouth went dry.

But for now, he needed to win a war.

Snatching the grains of metal from the air, he forced them to fly to his thigh, and then crafted a scabbard, something that wouldn’t be too cumbersome, and yet sturdy enough to hold the dagger.

“I need more metal.”

Before his eyes, black grains shot out of his armour, multiplying, allowing Logan to lift them into the air. He could get used to this. Taking the additional material, Logan formed his helmet, leaving his faceplate off for now. Then he crafted his backpack, making it sturdy and flexible, yet comfortable for Ernie. After having crafted it before at Rattlesnake Island, this time it was easy. Logan didn’t know if Ernie would want to hitch a ride, but it was better safe than sorry. The little bugger had been temperamental lately.

With a fond grin, Logan picked up the Sabáktes dagger and inserted it into his new scabbard. The blade fit like a glove, leaving only the top of the grubby handle sticking out. Not the best solution—he’d much rather have it inside his spatial storage collar—but the best he could do in a pinch.

Breaking into a jog, Logan burst through his property and over to Jack’s. He slowed down as he reached Jack’s yard, skidding to a more normal walk as he caught sight of kids drilling with wooden sticks out front.

Wouldn’t want to scare them.

With a gentle hand, Chase adjusted a child’s grip on his wooden stick, urging him to hold it higher. Chase’s face was clean shaven, his salt and pepper hair tidy. The man must have finally taken a shower. Around them, what had to be the children’s parents sat in a circle in collapsable camping chairs and cheered them on. Christ, this reminded him of a soccer match. In a way, Logan was glad they could get a semblance of normality back.

The kids were various ages, from a toddler who stumbled around the clearing holding one of the pool noodles Logan had nabbed from the neighbour’s property, whacking a large stuffed animal that resembled a penguin, to a boy and girl who looked Sawyer’s age.

Brooke was leading the training of the older teenagers, her hair in a high ponytail, a shorter strand standing straight up from her hair tie. She wore shorts and a white tank top, her arms full of raw cuts and scabs. Her sword was strapped to her back, and it bounced as she maneuvered around the drilling kids, her focus on making sure they didn’t cause injuries to each other.

Logan edged around the crowd, and when Chase and Brooke glanced his way, gestured with his chin for them to pull to the side.

“All right, everyone!” Brooke said, clapping her hands and raising her voice. “Let’s take five. Go to your parents, get hydrated.”

So far, Logan had given his [Idiot’s Vassal] title to both Lara and Emily, but he was debating whether it made sense to give it to Chase and Brooke. Although he’d only met them recently, they’d done a remarkable job of keeping everyone organized. These drills were yet another example that they had good heads on their shoulders. The only reason he’d been hesitant until now was that the title gave them a lot of power, power to indiscriminately add other people to his community.

He could give it to Jack and Tasha, but their recent undead transformations made him hesitant. An [Idiot’s Vassal] title holder was supposed to help expand his community, and how would Tasha and Jack get anyone to trust them considering that one scan with [Identify] would make people run for the hills just on principle?

Logan pulled up the description of the [Idiot’s Vassal] title:

[This title grants up to five users a permanent stat bonus of 50% to all attributes. In addition, Idiot’s Vassals can act as administrators, with the ability to accept other users into your lordship community. This title can be taken away at the lord’s discretion.]

If he gave this to Emily, why not give it to both Chase and Brooke? If it didn’t work out, he could always revoke the title and give it to someone else.

“New gear?” Emily asked, scanning him up and down.

“An upgrade,” said Logan with a grin.

Chase’s mouth grew pinched. “How is it that you walk around with these upgrades while us mere mortals struggle for scraps? I’m convinced the System must have favorites. How else could you have advanced so far?”

Logan held back a frown. It hadn’t been a walk in the park. And if anything, he’d had to deal with the glitchy AI minion, who had been the opposite of helpful. In a way, Brooke and Chase were lucky that they had dealt with the regular System rather than becoming a System ‘favorite.’

Brooke elbowed Chase in the side. Chase scowled but then studied Logan’s expression and swallowed. “Hey man, I know what you did for us. If you want fancy new gear, who am I to begrudge you? I’m just running my mouth.”

Brooke elbowed him again.

Chase coughed. “If you want to know the truth, it’s jealousy. You saved our lives, gave us weapons, food and shelter. But do you know how far away I am from the 1%? We’re not talking single digits here. I’m going to need massive growth to get anywhere close to surviving in a year.”

Logan scanned them both with [Idiot’s Inspect]:

[Chase Evans: Level 32. A human being.]

[Highest Stat: Perception. Characteristics: A marksman. Hidden name: Buckerfield.]

[Brooke Fisher: Level 34. A human being.]

[Highest Stat: Agility. Characteristics: An analytical mind. Hidden name: Brooke.]

Not good, but not pathetic either.

Logan peered at the kids and their parents. They were in earshot. “This way,” he said, leading them closer to the beach. “I won’t begrudge you a bit of complaining. I wouldn’t be much of a leader if the people in my community were afraid to express their opinions.” That was his last job. The company cared so much about profit that they never wanted to hear from anyone with a dissenting opinion. That caused stagnation and unhappy people.

It was all well and good to build a community, but if the people hated the person who led it, then what was the point? “Besides,” said Logan, “you know I’m trying to make the one percent a non-issue.”

“Right,” said Chase. “Your quest. You won’t begrudge a man for hedging his bets, though, would you? I’m rooting for you! We all are.” Even though they’d moved away from the parents, Chase lowered his voice. “Yet, I know the realities of our situation. It’s a crapshoot. A quest that needs you to reduce so much carbon it’s impossible. I’m convinced the System gave it to you for shits and giggles, to get you distracted from leveling everyone else up. A fruitless quest.”

Logan frowned. “I can complete the quest. With your help, with everyone’s. Humanity can do amazing things if everyone works together. It’s real. Trust me. And if you don’t believe that, think of this. There’s a reason I have a bounty on my head.”

Brooke looked furtive.

Logan gave her a brittle smile. “Don’t pretend you don’t know about it. It must be obvious that I got it from an alien species. And what they told me happened to their own world falls in line with my quest. We can save everyone here, save the kids, save yourself. We just need to work together to come to a solution.”

Brooke flicked her ponytail and smoothed her bangs. “Don’t listen to this downer,” she said, rolling her eyes at Chase. “I’ll help you with whatever you need. After what you did for us, we’d be stupid to not follow you wherever you led.”

Logan had been on the fence about giving them the [Idiot’s Vassal] title, but that just cemented it. He needed people who cared about the group, cared about making themselves strong, but who were also willing to work together so they could save the world.

“The first step is to make you more powerful. Chase, you want to get into the one percent, then I’ll help you.” Logan looked towards the shoreline, his eyes drifting to the flashes of Ernie’s tentacles as he conducted drills. “Ernie and I and his minions will be preoccupied for the day, maybe longer than a day. We’ll be fighting the thing that infected Tasha and Jack, the monster who warped the green beans into zombie incubators. While Ernie’s minions are gone, I’ll need you to protect the property. And I need you to be strong.”

With a blink, Logan deployed [Dominion Building].

[Please select the following actions:

Logan mentally hovered over the second option and then held out his hand. When he’d tried this with Emily, Logan had been confused about how it worked. He’d thought he’d need to input her name, only to realize that out of billions of people, there had to be more than a few ‘Emily Bastiones’ out there. Instead, all he needed to do was hold out his hand after activating the [Idiot’s Vassal] title option and the System did the rest.

“Hold my hand.”

Brooke gave his armour-covered glove a dubious look. “Why?”

“Just trust me.”

She shrugged. “Well, I trusted you with everything else.”

[Selection confirmed. Idiot’s Vassal title granted!]

Brooke gasped, her eyes widening. Reflexively, she tightened her grip on his hand while her legs wobbled. In front of his eyes, her muscles seemed to flex, her toes twitching. Looking through Logan as if she didn’t see him, her gaze became unfocused.

That had to be the wisdom and intelligence upgrades. Logan knew they did a number on your body.

With a swallow, she dropped his hand and backed up, her face white with shock. “You… how could you…? How is that possible?”

Logan smiled. “That should help you get closer to the one percent.” He nodded at Chase. “Clasp my hand.”

Logan knew that giving Chase and Brooke a 50% boost to their attributes wouldn’t make them into powerhouses, but at least they would be stronger than before. Still, even if everyone had the advantage of the array and the tiger guard dog to protect them in Logan’s absence, it might be a good idea to leave a few of Ernie’s minions behind just in case. Being part of Logan’s community gave everyone the ability to call for help if needed, but what if they called for help while Logan was in the middle of battle with the queen serpent?

Logan walked onto the dock, giving the tiger guard dog a wide birth as he surveyed Ernie’s army. The top of Ernie’s pointy helm dipped up and down, breaking the surface as he waved his tentacles, lifting one and then the other. Was that…? Ernie was blasting the ends of each tentacle with different colors, waving them through the air like he was in a bizarre disco dance.

Around him, the undead octopi swarmed, and the fins of the undead sturgeons rose through the surface like sharks. Pale fish flapped, splashing everything with water, while the Rattuspede let out click, click, click noises as it slapped its tail, generating a wave that crashed against the dock. Around the shore, Ernie’s undead ducks, looking like bleached penguins, waddled through the wet sand, making pathetic, sad warbles, their red eyes full of yearning.

And that’s what he could see above the surface. If Ernie’s army was over 500 strong, there had to be a ton of minions below.

Logan rested his hands on his hips. “It’s time to go,” he sent to Ernie with his mental voice.

Ernie’s tentacles jerked in the water. With a twirl, he zoomed through the lake, leaving a confused army in his wake. Shooting himself out of the lake with a surge of his tentacles, flying like a dolphin, he landed on top of the dock.

“Do you mean it?” said Ernie. “Do you truly?” His eyes were alight, his mouth parted. Drops of water trailed down his metal helm, landing in a puddle underneath his tentacles. “It’s time for slaughter? It’s time for carnage?”

“Yeah, buddy. It’s time to go to war.”

Ernie glowed with happiness, his pale Liche cheeks flushing red. Crawling forward, he rested his tentacle against Logan’s leg and turned his head, surveying his minions, who had bobbed to the surface and were waiting patiently for a command. “We will drown them in a cauldron of my own making, a cauldron of blood. All shall whimper in fear and awe as they behold my mighty, mighty army. We will tear the queen serpent from limb to limb, shedding her hide and wearing it like a robe of triumph. And then we will trample on her bones and fashion a throne to rival all.”

Logan wrinkled his nose. “Er, sure, buddy.”

Ernie snapped out of his slaughter trance and poked Logan in the leg. “What’s this? Where is the dull and ugly brown? Now you shimmer, shimmer under the sun!” With a bob of his head, Ernie activated his own [Mimicry] skill and became black as night, mirroring Logan. “I like it, yes, yes, yes. It is serious, it is mighty!”

Logan snorted. “The queen serpent will also be able to see us from a mile away.” With a blink, Logan activated his camouflage effect and made himself invisible. “We need to approach her… how did you say? We need to be stealthy. At least at first. I want to get an idea of what we’re going into, do a little reconnaissance. I can transport your whole army… say a mile a way from her lair? Do you think that’s far enough? You’re the expert.”

“Her lair is deep, deep at the bottom. As long as you transport my minions to the surface, it will still be a stealthy, stealthy mission.”

“Fair enough.” Logan looked towards the shore. “Do you mind if we leave your ducks behind to help patrol the community? I’m worried about Lara and the kids and everyone else while we’re away.”

Ernie lowered his voice into a mutter. “They are not so good at underwater attacks, at least not yet! Yes, they can stay.” Ernie’s eyes flared and the ducks came to attention, squawking. They flapped their white wings as if wanting to launch into the air and then panted, their chests inflating as if they were overheated.

“There, there,” Ernie crooned. “You are the most useful of the lot, you know! The most important, with such a super secret mission. A mission that I could trust with no other!”

Ernie activated his camouflage effect, turning himself invisible, leaving his hovering helm. A helm that bobbed in the air like it was worn by a ghost. Logan held back a smile and held out his arm. “You want to take a ride in the pouch, then?”

“Do you have to ask?” said Ernie, jumping onto his arm and sliming over to his shoulder and into his backpack. “You are the best mighty steed.”

Then Ernie whispered from inside the backpack, “Just don’t tell Larry.”
