The moment the blue fire consumed the root, it spread like wildfire. The flames surged along the tangled network of vines surrounding Zuri, jumping from tendril to tendril with unnatural speed.

Zuri's eyes widened, a flicker of realization crossing her face before the fire reached her. The moment it touched her, the ward amulet at her chest flared to life, its protective magic shielding her from the heat. Even so, she staggered as the force of the flames sent her back a step.

I got a hit in, Adrian thought. But one wouldn’t be if he wanted to secure his victory before she could counter.

One more hit should do it.

"Heh, if you say so."
