While she had confidence in her friend's strength, weird as it was, Penny wasn't about to let him get hurt when she could swoop in and help. Strength in numbers and all that.

Worm, worm, worm!

Take this in lieu of salt!

Lightning crackled between Penny's hands. This time, she had chosen to be the catalyst herself, since that lent itself to infusing her will into the attack. It slammed into the void worm as expected, but the effect was anything but. Rather than going in blind and striking at the void, she had focused the attack on the Source inside it. There was a lot of the stuff, far more than she had ever seen, and the key to defeating these types was to destabilize it. That would lead to the creature's own void eating the Source and killing it. Penny's lightning hit the Source channels, but it wasn't strong enough to break them down. The worm's attention shifted from Curtis to Penny, and she felt its will seize the lightning. The force of nature that was supposed to be faster than anyone's reflexes, the power that destroyed in the blink of an eye before disappearing, her power, stayed present by their opponent's sheer force of will.

That same will slammed into her through the bolt of lightning, and all of a sudden, it was as if the world around her had gone dark, and she stood in the void before this thing's true form. It was massive. Falzyxi. That was its name, and it had come to feed. The hunger made her own stomach twist, the all-consuming need to eat endlessly. It was strong enough to rub off on her to the point that it became difficult to focus. She felt herself falling, but there was nothing she could do, not against this being. It wasn't a worm, not really. It was a nightmare, and that nightmare had come to end all life on Earth. Who did she think she was, trying to step in between this godlike creature and its meal?

No. This was wrong. She had to fight it. The will pressing down on her was like a mountain, but that didn't mean she was helpless.

Tempest, come to me.

A stiff breeze broke through the nothingness. It startled the void worm, and Penny seized on its surprise, pushing back while reaching in and targeting its Source. Destroying it from this position was futile, of course, but it was nothing more than a diversion because she needed one to get out. The mirage in her mind shattered, and she found herself hovering just above the ground. She had been far above, and slamming into the cobblestone from such a height meant death. It was not the wind carrying her, and she had to look around to see what had saved her. Priyah, beautiful Priyah, stood with one hand raised way back on the roof she shared with Ray. The distance was too great for her, should have been too great, but she had done it. Penny let the wind sweep in and grab her.

The void worm had far more Source stored, and she just knew this wasn't all of it. Feed, little one.
