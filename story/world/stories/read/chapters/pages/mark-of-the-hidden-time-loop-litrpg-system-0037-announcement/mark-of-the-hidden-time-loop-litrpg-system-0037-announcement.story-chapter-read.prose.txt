Hello everyone,

It has been a while, I have finished my exams and hopefully passed. I was planning to continue Mark of the Hidden when I returned, albeit with a less frequent schedule, however, when looking over the story, I realized, there were so many mistakes I made and a lot of places where I could have done a much better job. It was my first time writing an Epic Fantasy and I'm glad I gave it a try as it helped me grow as a writer. I still love the concept of MOTH and I will one day come back to it, hopefully as a better writer, but for now, this story will unfortunately be on an indefinite hiatus.

I'm sorry to everyone who was hoping it would return soon.

Although, I will be releasing the Patreon chapters to Royal Road over the next few days so you can read it if you like, I know some readers were interested in the Magic System, the advanced chapters is where the LitRPG aspect is at.

Now onto some good news!

Whilst I was studying for my exams, I couldn't help but do some writing on the side, I decided to go back to my bread and butter and write another xianxia webnovel. I hope you all enjoy it, it has all the things I like about from my other novel Demonic Sect Elder and much more! I learned from my mistakes with MOTH and DSE so I hope it's a better novel than both of them!

Check it out if you're interested :)

Here is the synopsis:

Cultivation is a Game [Xianxia LitRPG Isekai]

Kill. Die. Respawn.

Kai woke up in a Xianxia world...with a LitRPG system.

But hey, at least cultivation works like a game.

Too bad the world itself is brutally real.

Now, it's up to him to leverage his gaming skills, conquer deadly quests, and survive the ruthless environment to become the ultimate cultivator.

What you can expect:

• Progression

• Character Development

• Comedy

• No Harem/Romance
