“Do you know the value of my Skill, Fisher Emperor?” Han Jianguo asked. He began circling.

“Canceling other people’s Skills has a pretty obvious value,” James said, staying in place and only following Jianguo’s movements with his head. His monsters, disconnected from James’s body, moved to respond to Jianguo’s new location with slow, steady repositioning so that they faced him at all times.

“It sounds obvious, yes,” Jianguo agreed, giving James a small, tight smile. “But it only works because of how reliant people became on the Skill aspect of the System as soon as it appeared. Just like people growing reliant on their technology over time, before the System, for transportation, information, reproduction, even the basic duty of thought.”

“Hmm.”

“Almost everything that we want to do with Skills could be done with our bodies and Mana using a conscious application of technique and effort. People are mostly lazy. I myself am not. That is to say… when I use Skill Nullification, it doesn’t affect my personal combat efficiency much at all.” He grinned.

“Maybe that says something about the quality of Skills you’ve gathered, rather than just the laziness of other people,” James replied, deliberately calm and cool, though his heart was pounding in his chest. His mind couldn’t help but think back to the decisions he’d made, wondering whether he was truly taking the right approach to this fight. There was also just a little pang of regret as a doubt he had quietly suppressed resurfaced, wondering if there was some way he could have prevented the Panther Queen’s death. That had been back in the melee, so there was no point in thinking about it now. But still…

Stop it! he ordered himself. Jianguo might be emulating a Skill right now, something to do with rattling you with words. That’s something humans can easily do without magic. You have experienced things like that before. More than once. Before the System, you weren’t this easily shaken. Maybe you have gotten a bit lazy. Use this fight as an opportunity to work on it. Get better.

“I don’t gather Skills, Fisher Emperor,” Jianguo replied. “I earn them. With blood and sweat.”

“You’re really determined to make me sound lazy, aren’t you?” James said, raising an eyebrow. Jianguo had positioned himself to James’s left flank now, though James’s monsters had all repositioned so that Jianguo could not easily approach James without getting dogpiled by them.

“No, but you have the Cannibalism Talent, right? That’s how you’ve accumulated so many different powers?”

James nodded. It was no true secret. Many of the people at the Summit, in the melee, and in this Tournament, certainly had that Talent.

“It’s a way of earning Skills, Talents, Titles, and stats by defeating people,” he said. “And killing them.”

“You know, in our homeland, Zhang Chāo and I have only ever encountered that Talent among criminals. It was strange and maddening to me when I recognized that it was common among Rulers. But Chāo pointed out that the gap between a person society labels as bad and an ordinary politician is simply whether you’re good at getting away with things. I do think, though, that it shows a willingness to take shortcuts.”

“Guilty,” James replied. “We can keep going back and forth about this all day, if you like. I’m not using any Skills with an unknown duration. Skill Nullification has to be running down some resource of yours. But go ahead and keep talking.”

“I told you before,” Jianguo said, smiling. “I can continue using this Skill for more than long enough. The time constraint is not a concern for me in fights like these.”

“If you say so.”

In that case, I won’t give him time to figure out a strategy for dealing with me and all my monsters.

Get him, James ordered.

The creatures responded in an instant. The four closest to Han Jianguo lunged toward him, leaping overhead or at his chest height. Jianguo naturally dove for the only open area, knee level, but that brought him to a vulnerable position relative to James himself.

The Fisher Emperor charged and aimed vicious kicks at Jianguo’s head, shoulders, and neck, and the other man had to just lie there and take it for a few seconds, shielding his neck and the back of his head from the worst of the blows with his arms.

“So this. is Skill. Nullification. Huh?” Every other word was punctuated by a kick, breaking up the sentence. “Doesn’t seem. Very helpful. One on—”

Jianguo grabbed James’s ankle and pulled him down, and the monsters that had been gathered all around dove onto Jianguo’s body attacked with fangs, claws, serrated limbs, tentacles, bony skulls, every natural weapon they had available to them.

James knew to a near certainty that Jianguo would have been injected with venom by at least one of his creatures, probably more than one.

At that moment, though, it didn’t matter.

Jianguo rained down blows on James’s stomach and chest, and the force behind them was terrible. James tried to block with his arms, but in physical stats, he was clearly outclassed. The punches found their way around his forearms and onto his ribs or sides. The Fisher Emperor felt where bruises were forming and recognized that if this went on for too long, he would quickly suffer broken bones.

This was despite the fact that the monsters were doing everything they could to hold Jianguo back, wrapping limbs around his arms and stabbing him as best as they could with pointed appendages.

Finally, Doppelganger, which had been hanging back on the sidelines waiting for an opportunity to make itself useful, threw itself onto Jianguo’s back and began clawing at the man’s eyes and slowly constricting an arm around Jianguo’s neck. Having someone of a similar size, strength, and build to James on his back, choking him out and trying to gouge one of his eyes, forced Jianguo to finally roll off of James. The Emperor got a few seconds to take a breath while Jianguo tried to fight off the monsters.

All of them dogpiled him, and it quickly became impossible for James to see Jianguo’s face.

James pushed himself to his feet, cracked his neck, opened his magic satchel, and drew out his best weapons: the two Bonded Oni Axes he had gained when he beat Carol’s Dungeon weeks prior. The Battle of Orlando had not seen him gain any worthy replacement, and the axes were rather special.

He took a few deep breaths, got a firm grip on each ax, and looked down at the pile of writhing flesh that covered Han Jianguo.

Is it just me, or is he starting to get up under there?

As James watched, he saw a few of the monsters fall off of Jianguo in pieces. James’s opponent was taking a while to get control of the situation, but he was far from down for the count.

That showed James really couldn’t just leave it to the creatures. At least not yet.

Get off of him, except his arms, legs, and head, he commanded.

In a fraction of a second, the beasts parted, leaving Jianguo’s midsection open for a blow. The man had been wearing armor, but it was in tatters by now, with big chunks of it missing. Like James, it seemed Jianguo had been having trouble acquiring equipment that was well suited to his personal level of power and the strength of the opponents he was now facing.

James swung the axes down, one after another, swinging with wild abandon since he had no Skill to guide him. He just pretended that he was chopping down a tree. Each blow left a large gouge in the armor and a shallower, smaller gouge in Jianguo’s flesh. The bone and meat that made up James’s opponent was so far beyond human at this point that even magical axes could not do much to him.

But as long as they were leaving cuts, that meant the damage would add up over time.

Then the wounds started to close up.

James frowned until he realized that his own injuries didn’t hurt as much as they had before either. His own accelerated healing came from Boundless Vitality of the Hydra, a Talent rather than a Skill.

I’ll just have to see which one of us heals faster.

Jianguo pushed himself up more quickly now that his body was less encumbered, and James took the opportunity to slam the axes down onto his back twice more before Jianguo had risen completely. It didn’t seem to slow the Taiwanese down at all.

But James continued doing it until Jianguo had risen so nearly to his full height. He didn’t stop swinging until Jianguo blocked one with the armored back of his arm. Jianguo raised his face and met James’s eyes, and the Emperor smiled.

He pulled his axes back and ordered the monsters to get off of Jianguo for a moment.

Let him get into a fighting position before you attack him again. I want to see what he’s got.

Jianguo took his own magic satchel from his pocket, opened it, and drew out two hook swords.

Then he lunged at James, swinging the hooked blades in opposite directions at his throat.

As Jianguo stepped forward, James shifted his axes, raising one in front of himself to block the two swords and swinging the other one up at Jianguo’s abdomen.

As James’s weapons drew closer to clashing with Jianguo’s swords and armor, Jianguo pulled back and pivoted—or tried to. He looked down as he fell, the Great Intestinal Worm wrapped firmly around his ankle.

James swung one ax down at Jianguo’s unprotected side, where he had already hacked a wider gap into the armor. The blade was inches away from slamming into a gap between his ribs when Jianguo opened his mouth.

“Back!”

The sound of it hit James’s body like a slap. At the same moment that he staggered back, he realized that his Skills were suddenly working again. He tried to use Dominion—and then he could feel the sense of weakness hit him again.

Jianguo had deactivated and reactivated Skill Nullification inside of a second.

That’s awfully convenient for him. He must have practiced to get that trick just right. That could be a much bigger problem than I was expecting.

James found his footing again and raised his axes. Jianguo, from his crouched position, had taken a firm grip of the Great Intestinal Worm, and as James’s other creatures dove onto him, he ripped the monster’s head off.

The Bone Spider sunk its fangs into Jianguo’s exposed neck, injecting more venom. James was tempted to rush in and lend his axes to the attack, but all he could think about for a moment was whether the pause and unpause that Jianguo had just demonstrated would provide a decisive advantage.

No, it would be a real problem if he had some special healing Skill, but he hasn’t demonstrated that yet. If he does have a Skill like that, why would he have bothered using a Skill to make me fall back? It doesn’t make sense. It would have been better for him to inflict some wound on me while he had me close, then turn Skills back on and heal himself. Either his healing Skills are really slow and can’t be used that way, or he was worried the wound would be too severe to heal. Either way, I’m good.

James charged in. He still had his plan in place.

Jianguo crushed the Bone Spider’s head in one hand, and the Emperor just smiled. His Bonded Oni Axes swung through the air in parallel arcs, two semicircular blurs in the air moving with all the acceleration the Emperor could give them.

James’s opponent blocked with his two hook swords, ignoring the monsters that were climbing onto his back, stabbing and biting him in the broken parts of his armor, and—in the case of Doppelganger—grabbing him around the neck again. The strength of James’s blows barely managed to push Jianguo back, despite the fact that James had built momentum as he moved while Jianguo was still in mid-crouch.

At the same moment, however, James’s Blood Slime finally managed to hit at one of the places where Jianguo was still bleeding, and as it attempted to make entry through the wound, James saw Jianguo’s facial expression twist into one of pain.

Then he felt it. Jianguo had lifted Skill Nullification again, and James felt all his passive abilities come back into effect.

The Emperor had anticipated this moment, and he reacted instantly rather than trying to focus on what Jianguo was using it for.

“Dominion.”
