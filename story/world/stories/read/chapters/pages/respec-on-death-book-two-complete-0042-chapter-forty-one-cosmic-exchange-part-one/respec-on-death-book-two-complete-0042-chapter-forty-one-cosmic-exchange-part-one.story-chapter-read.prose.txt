I stare at the yes for a few more moments, thinking back on the last month. I’ve grown a lot stronger since I gained Respec on Death. Far stronger than I had hoped. Healers don’t usually make it over twenty. Burnout is very common, both in mentality and mana channels. Too often healers are presented with things that they cannot fix, yet try anyway.

I’m not a healer anymore, but I’m also not as sour about it anymore. This class, Witherbrand, it’s really good. Even at level fourteen I basically soloed a boss of a yellow gate. Something that even the WHA guys wouldn’t have been able to do. Not the ones we had at least.

Wow, there’s weird fanfare, a dark mist instead of the usual colorful ones. I feel a strange sensation that passes after a moment.

[ Scion of Chaos ]

What the hell does Scion mean? It’s like an heir to something right? Cortez wasn’t wrong, I need to read more. I’ll ask her when she shows up. She should have been here already, I wonder what’s taking her so long. Maybe I should message her again after I check what Scion of Chaos is.

[ Subclass – Scion of Chaos ]

[ Scion of Chaos: After a lifetime of studying the source of power, the Scion of Chaos discovered how to harness and convert it. Their skills specialize in directing the flow of chaos. ]

What? What does that even mean? That’s so fucking ambiguous. Whatever it is though, it’s probably strong. Witherbrand is broken, it stands to reason this will be too. I move as fast as I can through the skill menu, trying to find the new base class skill. There it is…

[ Chaos Conversion ]

Okay… I click it.

[ Skill: Chaos Conversion ]

[ Usage: Allows the user to harness Charges in all their forms and convert them into Chaos Charges. ]

[ Effect: Exchanges any type of Charge into Chaos Charges. ]

[ Activation: Intention. ]

[ Cost: None. ]

[ Unique Passive: Access to the Chaos Emporium. ]

Okay, I don’t get it. Like, I do, but I don’t. Really wish I could scratch my chin right about now. Being bedridden sucks. Positive thoughts, at least I have time to explore everything finally, that long weekend I was looking for.

So, I’m going to guess here, but converting Charges must be stuff like Wither Charges. Which means there are other classes that use their own version of Charges. Which kind of makes sense since Subclasses generally are the connection point of several branches of other classes. I wonder what other classes use charges though.

Right now, it looks like its only skill converts Charges into Chaos Charges. Which means, I won’t know how broken it is until I get another class skill. Looking at the unique passive, I wonder if I spend the Chaos Charges at this Chaos Emporium place. Yet another place to buy or sell things it would seem. That reminds me, I still haven’t checked the Cosmic Exchange. Mwangi was pretty adamant that I should check it. If he’s right, healing skill might be for sale there. That’s a big guess though. There’s no evidence to support that theory, besides the fact that the custom RoD menu has a list of my legacy skills with synergy. We don’t actually know what that means though.

A lot to process. No point overthinking it, I’ll find out soon enough, hopefully.

Opening my messages section, I find Cortez in there. No response to my earlier messages. I wonder if she’s okay. Has me worried. I’ll ask Tran… no, on second thought, I’ll ask Barlow if he’s seen her.

[ SPC Novak: Hey bro, you seen Cortez? She hasn’t shown up yet. ]

As I wait for him, I look through the rest of my messages, there are quite a few from Trevanius. I’ve been avoiding opening them. Not much I can do about Cactus Cleric until I get out of this bed. Some things are better squashed in person. I get a chime, Barlow’s replied already.

[ SPC Barlow: Haven’t seen her, she missed formation. Want me to check with Lt? ]

I write him a painstakingly slow message back. Saying that I’d do it. Thankfully, Dorliac replies as quickly as she usually does.

[ 1LT Dorliac: What do you mean she hasn’t shown up?

I’ll check with SSG Mwangi. Sit tight. I’ll send a replacement for her. ]

Yeah, sitting tight… about all I can manage.

An hour later I finally hear the door opening to my room, I can tell from the gait and the blurry outline that it’s Barlow. Where the fuck is Cortez?

“Well, I know I’m not who you wanted to see, but I’m here,” he says, sitting down on a seat next to the bed, “I brought a book, if you want me to read it?”

I turn to look at his blurry outline again.

“I was thinking we could start in Genesis, it’s my favorite scripture.”

“Pass… hard… pass…” I groan.

“Look, I know it’s hard to believe when life kicks you in the nuts. But I saw you praying on the plane, you don’t have to hide it, be proud.”

“Goddess…”

Barlow pauses.

“Oh, I guess I should have realized that.”

I let out a sigh of relief. There’s nothing wrong with being religious. I have nothing against it personally. But the thought of being muddled through a sermon by one of my peers, all while being strapped to a bed I can’t escape yet… well, that sounds painful.

“Never too late though, you know…”

“Pass…”

“Okay, worth a shot.”

There’s a heavy silence between us now. He’s not mad though, he’ll try again, always does. While he reads, I still want to check out the Cosmic Exchange. I check my messages tab, still no message back from Dorliac. Weird. Alright, time to pull up the menu.
