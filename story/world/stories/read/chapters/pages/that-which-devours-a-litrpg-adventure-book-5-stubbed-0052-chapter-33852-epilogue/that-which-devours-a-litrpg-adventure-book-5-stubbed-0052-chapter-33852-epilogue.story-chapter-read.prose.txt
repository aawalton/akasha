Noseen, somewhere with Nessetra…

“What did you do with my favorite neighbor?” she asked, as I landed on a leaf.

Somehow she knew I was here, even in my tiniest form.

“She decided to do something very selfless and brave,” I said. “She was dumb.”

“She did what she had to do,” I amended. “I just don’t like it.” Alex needed to be smart and strong right now. I needed to believe she could do what she needed to.

“It’s one of the reasons she is a favorite of mine,” the Knowledge-Bearer said, as she floated on the surface of the water.

“I connected another world to the nexus point, and to my surprise a connection to one of the megacities appeared. You wouldn’t know anything about that, would you?” I asked as carefully as I could. The Knowledge-Bearer wasn’t someone even I wanted to upset.

Nessetra started laughing. A sweet sound that made the very air shimmer.

I waited.

Her laughter eventually faded.

“Alex might have done something else very selfless and brave, that those who protect knowledge take very seriously.” She paused, and bowed her head toward me. “She has more supporters on her adventure than you might expect.”

I buzzed lightly to myself, wondering what Alex could possibly have pulled off with the Creator. We didn’t have time to talk about it before I had to go back to protect the eggs. Even now, I needed to go.

Something urged me to give her something in return.

“I am guarding the last eggs of Apochas,” I said, in the quietest voice possible. “The Great Devourers will fly again.”

Her eyes locked onto me, and for a split moment I stood in front of one of the Ancients.

Then, she bowed deeply to me. “I will visit, when they have their wings. I loved the great flyers.”

Someone sprinted down the path in our direction, and Nessetra straightened up, but didn’t vanish.

I lifted off the leaf and slowly headed up the path.

“Nessetra!” called out the Pathfinder, as she passed right by me. “We now have access to a Rank 3 world…”

I slowed down a little, thinking of the eggs I was protecting on the very planet she spoke of.

“Gassamer. Alex made me its Leader,” she said, in a fast voice. “I plan to keep it as wild as possible, with a small settlement for those who like their comforts.”

“So, you would accept civilized beasts from here who want to grow?” Nessetra asked.

“Of course! That’s why I wanted to talk to you, to see if you knew anyone who wanted to join me. Dengu is coming, and hopes to raise his young there. Strange plans to go full messenger. I need someone a little more neutral for my team.”

“I have just the beast in mind,” she said, as I flew away.

Alex still lived. That was what mattered.

Alex, on top of the colony ship deep in space, an unknown time later…

I sat cross legged on the top of the ship as it moved slowly through the darkness of space. A thin shield surrounded the entire thing, almost like a bubble of time stasis. Whenever I looked at it, the same thing popped up.

[Sentient life protection protocol in place.]

At least as long as I touched the dang thing, I could direct where it went. Day after day I aimed for a star on my map. Lakeside.

That, and the tablet from the Library of the Cosmos, had saved me. Still, the days ticked by slowly. Time turned into a figment of my imagination. Nothing changed quickly enough to register the passage of time, even at the insane speeds we were moving.

Other strange beings flew between planetary objects and paid no attention to us. Sometimes they were devourers, other times I didn’t have a clue what they were. The tablet gave me info when I looked up what they might be, and sometimes didn’t. For once I became a decent student, with nothing else to do.

I popped the tablet back into my inventory and closed my eyes. This was going to be a long trip. It already was.

A single peach remained in my inventory, and I still didn’t know when it had appeared. At some point before I ended up here, it had just poofed into being. I didn’t dare take it out, not while in space. Still, all my insight said was that it was a perfectly ripe peach. It was sitting there, teasing me.

Something or someone very powerful knew I really wanted one, and somehow it had ended up in my inventory. A perfect treat. I didn’t worry about it. I tried to ignore it. Instead, I marveled over how it would taste when I arrived at Lakeside Landing.

It helped my mind rest.

A notification woke me.

Doc had taken over leadership of Lakeside Landing.

I smiled to myself, happy for Abby. Hopefully, she and dad didn’t wait until I arrived to get married. They deserved to be happy. I no longer wanted to rush leveling. For once, I was okay moving at a much slower timeline.
