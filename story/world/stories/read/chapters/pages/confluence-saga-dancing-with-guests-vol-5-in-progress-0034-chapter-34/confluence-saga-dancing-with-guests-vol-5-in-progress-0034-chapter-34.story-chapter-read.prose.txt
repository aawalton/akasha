All Paths are equal.

The Seers tell us this. It is both completely true and utterly false.

All Paths have equal potential is closer to the truth. There are no options that prevent success. There are no Archetypes or Specializations that cannot become powerhouses.

In the end you get from your Path what you put into it, because your Path is a reflection of yourself.

By the time Phelann calmed down Seb and Anya had switched places once more to let him take a better look at his options. He was fairly certain he had made up his mind about which Archetype to select however he was interrupted before he could explain his reasoning.

"I apologize for my outburst," Phelann said. "The first of those Notes is the most common and even it is considered a rare boon. I'm not sure I've heard of anyone getting the third note personally."

"But what do they mean? Why are they so shocking?" Seb asked. Rarity was probably a good thing based on her reaction, but maybe she'd be able to tell them why Valene felt so dangerous.

"I can't tell you much. By the time I could you'll likely be able to figure out what I'm not saying. The first note relates to why I would feel dangerous if Valene hadn't warned me how to treat you. The third note means you felt something from him that was much more, something you wouldn't learn of unless you're a Seer until much later. Seers end up learning certain details early because we have to be able to understand what other Awakened experience when they ask questions," she answered.

"So my recognizing you and Yshe as dangerous ties to something past level 9. And Valene was more dangerous because he's somewhere higher still you can't talk about?" he clarified.

"Yes. And the second note means that there was no planning or intent behind you finding out. Some Awakened families intentionally trigger the first note for the benefits, but it must be truly accidental for the second. And as the third says, you must not be harmed. The difference in Energy State between Valene and you is enough that most demonstrations an Awakened would recognize would usually kill them."

Seb froze and felt Anya tense beside him. "Just how much danger was I in?"

"None?" Phelann answered hesitantly. "Valene wasn't doing anything, you simply noticed something you should not have been capable of. That I shouldn't be capable of."

"And my getting the notes?" Anya asked.

"You were communicating over band when it happened?" At their nods she continued. "You're close and you were sharing awareness in a way. You felt it from him. Delayed recognition is extremely unusual, but it is based on your recognition. Seb knew it was what it was, his notes gave you context."

"Which is, in my opinion, completely ridiculous. I don't know how you managed to do what you did, but I would suggest never letting anyone know you have the third note, even the second might get you attention you won't like."

Seb frowned. "Why would they be a problem?"

"Their benefits. Choose your Archetype, or let Lady Lysanya take your place. You'll be able to see then."

Seb went to move away only to feel Anya's hand on his arm, holding him in place. "I'm not choosing before you have your Archetype. We work together."

He smiled then looked back over the list one last time.

"I'm going to choose Stalker," he explained. " SpellBlade sounds like a version of Monk that uses weapons. Energyst, other than maybe Energy Barrier, would be very vulnerable if actually attacked."

Seb could feel Anya's relief without looking at her. Apparently she'd had some concerns about his choice or vulnerability as well.

I would protect you Seb, but if they got past me.

I know, and I don't want you to feel forced to.

You- she sent, frustration leaking through. It's what I would want to do.

Decision made Seb selected the Archetype and the feed changed, showing new choices.
