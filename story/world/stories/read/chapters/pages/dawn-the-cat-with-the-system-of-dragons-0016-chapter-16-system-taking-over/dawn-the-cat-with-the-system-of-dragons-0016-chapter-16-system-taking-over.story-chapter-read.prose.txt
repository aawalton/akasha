Dawn was sprinting through the undergrowth of the dense forest. She was happy and excited. She was this energetic after a long time.

For the last couple of days, she was struggling with various things without even getting a proper rest. She was continuously pushing forward without her full strength.

More annoying than that, the blue stats screen hindered her vision and reduced her hunting success rate. She was not sure how to go on with it.

Fortunately, when she went to the town, Elenora not only gave her food, she healed her, and she even obliterated her nemesis, the stats screen. Dawn was happy about this development.

Now the sun was up. In the morning, sunlight made a beautiful environment in the forest. Multiple beams of sunlight were entering through the dense leaves, removing the night’s darkness. Thick fog in the forest was getting thinner. Visibility was getting better.

While running, Dawn realised the atmosphere in the forest had changed slightly. Beams of sunlight stopped entering the forest. The sky became dark. Air speed went up. The rustling sound of tree leaves became louder. Tree tops started bending due to air pressure.

Thankfully, she was in the dense forest, so the air was not affecting her as much as the beings who were currently outside the forest. The forest was too dense for the air to penetrate properly.

She didn’t think much about it and kept running towards her den.

Suddenly, the black cat sensed that the barometric pressure had dropped drastically. The temperature dropped rapidly. She could feel strong cool air entering the forest from above. She could also hear the sound of strong wind in the distance. Her exceptional sense of hearing and smell, along with her sensitive inner ear and whiskers, helped her catch these changes immediately. She knew what was happening to the weather.

Being a cat, her vision was not as good as humans', but her natural abilities helped her sense these changes in the atmosphere immediately. It was a clear sign of a huge storm, so she didn’t have the time to think about what to do.

She was worried about her babies. She wanted to reach her den as soon as possible. She started sprinting with everything she had. She was so tense that she decided to take the risk, and she started running straight through the territories of other predators.

Previously, she had never taken such a risk, and always travelled through the border area. But now the situation had changed. It required drastic measures.

But then, she saw many animals running towards her. Deer, wolves, rabbits, rats, monkeys, and every other animal were running towards her straight. Seeing this, she got scared and climbed a tree immediately.

Then every animal started passing by the tree where Dawn had climbed just now. None of the animals cared about her.

Actually, none of the animals cared about any predators or prey nearby. All of them were running for their lives. They were running in the opposite direction. Dawn was the only one who wanted to run towards her den. Something bad was clearly happening there.

Since the animals were not interested in her, she decided to ignore the running animals and started sprinting through the chaos, straight towards her den.

After a few minutes, she was closer to her destination. It was only a few minutes away. But there was a huge problem. It was not allowing her to move forward properly.

The air pressure was directly hitting her face, pushing her in the opposite direction. It was too much for her to handle. Her grip on the ground started loosening. The air was so strong that it was getting hard to move forward. Even walking became hard.

In the end, she had to take cover with a thick tree trunk to protect herself from being pushed away by the wind. Now, she was even more scared. Her babies were in the den. She was not sure whether they would be protected by her enclosed den.

“BAAAAAM” … “BAAAAAAAAAM”

Suddenly, she heard a loud noise from above. She turned her head immediately and saw a huge uprooted tree being supported by thick branches of the tree where she was taking cover. It would soon fall on her.

Seeing this, her eyes widened in shock. She immediately tried to run away. But it was of no use. As soon as she stepped outside the tree’s cover, the air current pushed her so much that she lost her balance immediately and started rolling on the ground due to fierce air pressure.

While being pushed and rolling on the ground, she got slammed into a thick tree truck. The impact made her vision blurry immediately. She hit her head and it started bleeding.

[Notification]

[Health decreased by 325]

As soon as she hit the tree, a small blue box popped up in front of her and disappeared after a second.

She was lying down and was being supported by the tree roots. The strong wind current was hitting her directly and pushing her against the tree trunk where she had just been slammed.

Suddenly, a voice rang in her head. It was speaking in a dragon language. A blue screen also appeared out of nowhere in front of her face. It covered her vision again.

[Temporary user, do you need my assistance? Deny with answering ‘NO’ if you don’t need my assistance.]

[NO]

Dawn didn’t understand anything the voice said to her. Moreover, she was disoriented due to being hit on her head. Her consciousness was coming in and going out. She was bleeding slightly from her head, and it affected her a lot. Her vision was slowly greying out.

All she was seeing was a blurry landscape and a blurry blue dim light hovering in front of her face. Also, she was not in a state to react to the voice. With every second, her vision was coming in and going out even more.

Suddenly, the voice rang inside her head again.

[User, since you didn’t answer NO, it means you need my assistance… Observing the circumstances, I can assist you by taking full control of your body. But to proceed with this, I need your consent. Since the situation is getting dangerous, please answer within 2 seconds if you don’t need my assistance.]

[NO]

Dawn didn’t understand what was happening. The Aegis system used this to its advantage.

The system knew that its temporary user was not intelligent enough to handle the system. So it decided to ask the questions in a way that gave it permission without needing the user to understand the language or make decisions.

It was a loophole only a system like Aegis could use.

It was not life-threatening yet, so System had to take a roundabout way to assist its user. Had it been life threatening, the system wouldn’t have asked before taking action.

[Since you didn’t deny needing assistance. I’m proceeding with it and taking over the body temporarily.] As soon as Dawn heard this voice, she started feeling different.

It felt as if she was suddenly getting healed by someone, but there was no one nearby. The feeling was similar to when Elenora was healing her wounds.

Her wounded head stopped aching. Bleeding stopped entirely. Her body became energized. And the most noticeable thing was, she was not feeling anxious anymore.

In fact, she was calm, calmer than ever, as if nothing was happening around her.

Along with this, her natural vision also became normal, just like before.

After a few seconds, she started feeling her strength rising. Her strength rose so much that the unbearable air pressure that she was feeling earlier became bearable. At first, she thought that the air pressure had reduced. But seeing her surroundings, she knew that was not the case.

Then, suddenly, her eye color changed. Her golden iris became sky blue. Her vision got clearer. Her natural vision, which was limited to mid-range, became so sharp that she was now able to identify objects from a long distance.

And the first thing she noticed was a huge towering cyclone in the middle of the forest. It was directly in front of her. It was towards her destination.

The cyclone’s suction power was so much that all the nearby objects were getting swept away and flying inside the cyclone. Animals, monsters, rocks, trees, nothing was being spared by it.

Everything close to the cyclone got sucked up inside, and everything outside its suction range was being pushed with pure air pressure, as if someone was intentionally making the cyclone work like this.

The big sentient tree, under whose roots Dawn was residing, was not visible anymore. The cyclone covered it from all sides. Her babies were clearly in danger. It was possible that her babies might have been swept away already.

Even after seeing all this, Dawn was calm. She knew what she needed to do. She wanted to find her babies. Even if she was able to save only one of her babies, it was worth the try.

She was about to take action, but she was not able to move for some reason. Then her head turned towards the ground. She started looking at her legs. She was no longer able to control her body. It was now being controlled by Aegis System.

Suddenly, all of the claws got covered with a blue transparent layer. Those looked like a weaponised extension and were significantly longer and sharper than her natural claws..

Then she stood up, used her claws on the ground to create hooks, and bolted forward. She started sprinting with full speed as if the air pressure was nothing to her anymore.

Dawn felt as if her body was moving on its own. How did she get extensions like claws? She didn’t know. How was she sprinting even in harsh air currents? She didn’t know. All she knew was that she was now moving towards the cyclone, without any hindrance.

Seeing this, Dawn stopped trying to control her body. She understood that what was happening was beneficial to her babies.

After a few minutes, she reached near the cyclone. Again, the wind became too much to handle. Wind direction changed entirely. The wind pressure stopped pushing her away. Instead, it was trying to pull her into the air.

Then her body started moving differently. She started moving, using her long claws to hold herself down on the ground. The Aegis system was using everything it had in its arsenal to enter the cyclone safely.

Dawn could feel that she was getting weaker. She could feel her energy getting drained. What she didn’t know was that Aegis system was doing something which drained Dawn’s elemental energy rapidly. It allowed them to stay on the ground. Claws were not enough. Those only provided support to move forward.

Thankfully, nothing happened, and after a minute, Dawn reached near the entrance of her den. But there was something weird. The entrance of her den was covered with thick tree roots.
