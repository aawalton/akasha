If I can duplicate enough items to sell, then I could fuse a lot more weapons.

I’ve done enough to make a competent build, so I want to invest the points I have into something that will benefit future loops. It’s what makes the most sense.

I’ll need to have a real talk with her about this.

About everything.

Even if I die, she needs to push on.

If I can manage to relay the information in a way that doesn’t violate the censorship that the scale imposes on visions of the past.

Hopefully its evolutions are similar to Elementalist.
