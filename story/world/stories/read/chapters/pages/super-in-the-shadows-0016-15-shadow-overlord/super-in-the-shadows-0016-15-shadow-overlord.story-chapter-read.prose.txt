He didn’t know how to explain it, but he felt it all. His mount's muscles coiling and exploding with power, its paws tapping the ground beneath, and even the quaking of its existence.

He was ONE with the wolf, as if an extension of his body.

That’s when everything changed. He didn’t know why, but riding the Ink Wolf became as easy as breathing. And from that point onward, he was free to focus on attacking.

His Razorpear slashed the air and— SPLATTER!

His Razorpear slashed the air and— SPLATTER!

His Razorpear slashed the air and— SPLATTER!

….

Each of his strikes meant death.

But killing wasn’t the only thing he did. With his soles, he tapped his companion’s sides, ordering it where to go, ordering them all where to go. They moved as one, as a pack.

This feeling of being one, a sense of belonging, and that crazy speed.... it felt liberating and so goddamn amazing! He even found himself ignoring the system’s constant affinity messages.

He didn’t care about any of it. He existed solely to ride, stab goblins, and deflect their strikes. He, who had started as the worst luggage ever, had turned into a protector.

“An arrow volley? Cool, but we ain’t there!”

“Trying to stab us? Not on my watch!”

“Where are you even poking?!”

…

He kept attacking, killing endless goblins, all the while running from the orcs.

He’d swing his Razorpear constantly. In fact, he did it so many times that he felt like his arms would fall off, yet he kept going even then, his heart and mind aflame.

Perhaps it was his near-death experience, but he was buzzing.

Right now, he felt more than ALIVE!

Blood stained him from head to toe, even his teeth turned blackish from the creature’s blood. But just as he was reveling in the slaughter, he suddenly felt incredibly bad.

Something was wrong, hella wrong, but what?! He hadn’t fallen off in forever; goblins were dying by the hundreds, his troops were doing good, and the Orcs were despairing as—

FUCK! The Orcs, it was the goddamn orcs!

They stood in the back as they screamed for the goblins to kill them, yet only now did he finally realize. The Orcs, there were fewer of them than before?!

This place was sealed off, and they sure as hell hadn’t left, which could only mean one thing., “TURN BACK, NOW!” Jack screamed, but it was already too late.

Orcs, a shitload of Orcs, all appeared at once.

Orcs weren’t as fast as the wolves. Orcs were also easy to spot, and they had been avoiding them all along, but those orcs had used the sea of goblins to crawl to them sneakily!

"Grrahnk..." They screamed as they attacked them with bone clubs bigger than he was. No, actually, those weren’t clubs, but spines, spines of their previous victims, no doubt.

The wolves tried escaping. They didn’t succeed.

A single strike and they were reduced to paste, their shadow cores shattered. And that’s when the deviousness of the trap revealed itself. They were completely surrounded!

It wasn’t a first for them, but orcs were something else entirely. They were taller and way more dangerous. They couldn’t simply be dodged and toyed with like the goblins.

Damn. Goddammit. How had he even missed that?!

Had he grown overconfident? Had he lowered his guard? No, he had simply been way too busy attacking and defending to notice. His inexperience was about to cost them dearly.

He hurriedly turned toward Shadow. Could he help?!

No, he was still locked in a duel with the Shadow Abomination. The thing had been made with Ink Wolves' bodies, and it clearly resisted Shadow’s attacks. They were on their own.

What should he do?! What if he had the wolves throw him out of the orc encirclement? As long as he managed to leave it, they might go after him and forget the wolves.

After all, HE was their only target.

It seemed they had misunderstood. They wrongly believed that he was the summoner. Then again, he couldn't blame them since wolves and Shadow all protected him.

Yes, first get out, and then he could deal with everything else. As long as he survived and they kept misunderstanding, there would be hope. As long as— that’s when he froze.

“Are you kidding me?! Don’t tell me they thought of it?!”

He stared outside the orc encirclement, shellshocked. There, tons of goblins awaited, but there was something very specific about them. THEY WERE ALL ARCHERS!

That’s when he saw the evil glint in the Orc’s eyes, almost taunting him.

‘Go on. Try and run, but they’ll shoot you down.’

He wasn’t like the Ink Wolves. They may not care too much about arrows, but he DID. Without their cover, a single well-placed volley would turn him into a bloody porcupine.

Then it got worse, the encirclement closing.

60 meters

40 meters

20 meters

….

They were now right upon them.

Jack could smell their fetid breath and sense their eagerness as they came over. And as they engaged the wolves, there was absolutely no room for any counter from the latter.

SMASH! SMASH! SMASH! SMASH! SMASH! SMASH! Every smash meant one more dead Ink Wolf. In a matter of minutes, there were only a handful left, all huddled around him.

Even now, they only cared about his safety.

“Awoooo! Awoooo!! Awoooo!”

They powerfully howled even in the face of death. If their sacrifice could buy him a few instants of life, they’d do it, every death pulling on his heartstrings.

Those weren’t Ink Wolves dying. They were his new friends and family. It had only been a few days, but the bond they now shared went far beyond the mission.

“I’m sorry. I’m so sorry.” He apologized, yet they didn’t care. They knew he had done his best. His best hadn’t been enough, but so was life. They wouldn’t hold it against him.

The few remaining hugged him for the last time, their fur brushing against him.

“You guys. Thank you for everything.”

It soothed his bleeding heart and—

[Ding! Affinity With Ink Wolves Increased!]

[Ding! Affinity With Ink Wolves Increased!]

[Ding! Affinity With Ink Wolves Increased!]

…

‘Shut up, annoying system.’

He didn’t need a message to know it. He simply enjoyed the moment, knowing this would be their very last stand. He and the wolves shared a meaningful glance, almost laughing.

One. No matter what, they’d get one. They’d instill fear in the orcs if that was the last thing they did. They’d teach them the savagery of a wolf and how sharp their claws were!

As more Affinity BS messages resounded, they charged as one.

Jack used every last bit of energy he still had. He dodged the first few ginormous bone clubs by some miracle. Then he struck with all he had left. His vision turned red: kill, kill, KILL!!

He’d show them the pride of an Ink Wolf!

He struck faster than bloody lightning.

Then he kept going even faster!

Overflowing with adrenaline, he kept attacking. This was merely the last speck of a dying life, but at that precise moment, he felt so very much alive! He could feel it all.

He felt the White Plane pulsating under him. He felt the ambient Mana colliding with his fur as he dashed. He felt the confusion and fear in the orcs' eyes as he reached them.

He especially felt his fangs sink into gross flesh.

It was ecstatic! The feeling of his fangs tearing the skin, crushing the meat underneath, and then the blissful warm stream that flowed out of his prey. Right now, he was the hunter!

But even that wasn’t enough. He swung his Razorspear, burying it to the hilt into his enemies, his claws soon following the same path as they dug through the flesh and—

That’s when he noticed something was strange…

As he gazed at his hands, all he saw was shadowy darkness. Also, since when did he have fangs and claws? But the system chimed in just as he was utterly confused.

[Ding! Reached Max Affinity!]

[New Ability Unlocked Shadow Meld!]

[Wolf or Human, all are Merely Shadows!]

Seriously?! It seemed like this was an ability of the Ink Wolves, and one had now fused with him?! He had charged as one with the wolves, but it had ended up being quite literal, too.

No wonder the orcs had frozen in confusion!

His current state was a weird human-wolf humanoid hybrid. It almost looked like he was wearing magical armor all over his body, which even gave him claws and fangs.

Three Ink wolves by the side also stared wide-eyed.

They had long accepted the Human as part of their pack, but they never had expected that! Still, that didn’t stop them from reacting surprisingly fast, reaching Jack.

And just as he was liberating his spear from an orc corpse…

“AWOOOOOOOOOOO!”

A powerful howl resounded, and then they struck! Their momentum was so great that even the mighty orcs couldn’t help but take a step back. Except, they had never been the target.

Jack suddenly felt a powerful force as he was catapulted into the air. As the world spun, he saw brave wolves howling him goodbye, with their tails wagging.

Their happiness didn’t stop even as the orcs massacred them.

“Woooooooo!”

He understood it all. They wished him safe travel. They also asked their brother to protect him, the shadow armor on his body rippling in reaction. They died with large grins.

Jack should have felt relief, but all that came were tears.

All of them. They were all DEAD.

But that was precisely why he had to live. By now, the orcs were screaming at the goblins to shoot his ass, and soon the sky darkened with arrows as if an extinction event.

The white plane disappeared, and only darkness was left. He did his best to protect himself with his weapon. The Ink Wolf Armor did the rest, and then he landed safely on the other side.

The orcs bellowed in anguish, but he was out of their encirclement.

He was now free to run once more. Except he wouldn’t. Not only did he not run, but he headed deeper into the sea of goblins, his body soon disappearing amidst them.

A human’s technique and knowledge.

An Ink wolf’s regenerative powers.

He carved a path of blood.

He kept going all the way to his target. In the middle, Shadow and the abomination were still explosively fighting, both growing weaker, with lots of Shadow Cores destroyed.

But amidst a world-shattering fight, a certain abomination stilled. It had just seen something that terrorized it to its core. It was a system message.

[A Bloodthirsty Human is Targeting You!]

[A Bloodthirsty Human is Targeting You!]

[A Bloodthirsty Human is Targeting You!]

It turned its grotesque head in shock… just in time to see Jack right next to it?! He was done with fleeing. He was done with people dying for him. He was done with it all.

The creature was already wounded. Shadow was there to assist, too. The Shadow Meld’s state also greatly buffed him, but none of it mattered. It was his eyes that traumatized it.

Those were the eyes of a grim reaper. As a primal fear took hold of the creature, he dived right into its maw! Then he started crushing core after core.

At some point, the abomination tried to swallow him. Shadow stopped it.

At some point, it tried escaping. Shadow stopped it again.

At some point, it begged for mercy. Jack killed it.

[Successfully Defeated Shadow Overlord!]

He didn’t revel in the kill. He came out of the behemoth’s corpse, the shadow around him rippling in elation, and instead walked straight toward the army in the distance.

The main army had still been skirmishing, but it suddenly stopped as all eyes landed on a certain human casually strolling their way.

Even now, his body was full of the grime from the behemoth. And as he gazed their way, what they felt wasn’t dread but fatality, one confirmed by the system.

[A Bloodthirsty Human is Targeting You!]

[A Bloodthirsty Human is Targeting You!]

[A Bloodthirsty Human is Targeting You!]

…

They didn’t stick around for more.

They turned tail and ran. The rest was like a dream, two main armies retreating into five portals as fast as their legs could carry them and not daring to return.

It happened way too fast, so fast that he wouldn’t have believed it real if not for the corpses and the new ability he had somehow unlocked.

‘Hundreds of wolves died, most of them to save me.’

‘What’s the point of it all? They came and killed.’

‘Now they’re gone just like that?’

The change from the heated battlefield to the quiet post-battle atmosphere was so stark that he almost doubted his sanity. But amidst his shock were lots of grateful friends.

“Human, you saved us! Without you, who knows how it would have gone? You made them flee with one look, how?! You all, did you see it?!”

As Shadow chimed in, the Ink Wolves howled in agreement.

But a second later, one raised a mischievous paw as it wagged its tongue. “Woo! Woo! Woo!” It wanted to know, if they ever told the story, how should they call him?

Jack, Jack Bright? Human? It didn’t do him justice. He deserved the coolest title!

For an instant, he remained motionless. He had never really thought about it. He was merely a student and hadn’t expected to become an otherworldly general out of nowhere.

Yet, he couldn’t help but give a derisive grin as he decided upon the most ironic name ever. They wanted a nickname? He had the perfect one for them. He chuckled.

“Let’s just say there’s a new Shadow Overlord in town…”

[Ding! New Title Obtained Shadow Overlord!]

[Title Only Active In the White Plane!]

[Increased Overall Recognition!]

‘How neat!’

But that wasn’t what made him smile the most. With this fight, he had understood things that would change their future: things about himself, the wolves, and even Shadow.

He couldn't stop the ginormous grin adorning his face at that moment.

He had the feeling he’d be going home VERY soon…
