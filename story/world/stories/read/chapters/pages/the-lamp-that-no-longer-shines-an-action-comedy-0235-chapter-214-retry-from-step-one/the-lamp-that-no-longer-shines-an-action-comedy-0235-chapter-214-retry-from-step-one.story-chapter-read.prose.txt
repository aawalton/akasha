Two lifetimes of accumulated composure held the line beautifully everywhere except her knees, which had achieved the consistency of warm dessert.

A gloved hand closed over hers on the broom handle.

Victoria had drawn up alongside, silver hair torn into a halo, pale eyes level. She did not say anything. At this altitude the wind ate speech before it cleared the teeth.
