After I finished writing Barbarian Cultivator, I mentioned in the Afterword that I would be coming out with the next thing in a few months.

A few months have gone by since then, so I thought it would be good to keep you up to speed with what's going on with me.

In short, a bunch of good but unexpected stuff.

The first thing was that I landed a very good job, possibly the best job I've ever landed.

I've had to move cities to take the job, and as of this update I'm finished with the first leg of on-the-job training and am working regular shifts.

The second good thing is that this job happens to have ample stretches of downtime. When it gets quiet, in my neighboring workstations, out come the phones, tablets, Steam Decks and Switch 2s.

I plan to write. I've already started, actually.

I can't say how long it will be until my next story is ready for RoyalRoad, but work on it has begun. Let's tentatively say it'll be ready sometime in the first quarter of 2027.

Thank you all, again, for reading and enjoying my work.

It'll keep getting better!
