Months of calm, of dealing with students the way we always have. Months of days spent ensuring that the current batch doesn't do anything foolish. Because someone always does.

Two months ago I had to step in when a team didn't bother to check down one of the crevices and was blindsided by a full wave of reinforcements right when they started to relax. Last month Sigrid had to shove a student out of the way when an Energyst didn't bother confirming everyone was clear. I know the others have stories too, they all tend to blend together though.

On a normal Dive there would have been serious injuries, possibly fatal ones. The fact that they made it this far isn't proof that they'll make it to graduation. Let alone keep going.

Sigrid thinks at least half the mistakes we stop are our own fault, that they know we're nearby and take risks they wouldn't otherwise. I think half is too few.

She's not looking forward to next week. I'm curious if they figured out how to work together in the months they've been gone. It would be fast, but it wouldn't be impossible.

If they have I might be able to convince Master Leo to let them push the way Mithrax is going to. Would be a good excuse to ask for assistance, a second set of eyes to help monitor the far tunnels and ensure the lists are still accurate.

I'm trying to remember the last time two teams got approved to set up temporary encampments rather than range from basecamp every morning.

Fay watched Seb walk away before turning to catch Cara staring at her.

"Cara?"

"You're not hurt? I wasn't fast enough to…"

"I think I was far enough away, and high enough level that I wasn't at risk. Seb knew that."

"Good," Cara muttered then repeated more firmly. "Good. That you weren't at risk I mean. That you were safe."

"What is it?"

"I was too slow. I was out of place."

Fay frowned and looked down at the handprint on the table.

"No one could have predicted. It wasn't an attack. You weren't-"

"I knew." Cara said sharply enough to make Fay look up and see a glow in her eyes. "Seb knew before I did but we both knew something was going to happen. We should have done something."

"You did. You were ready if anything more happened. I wasn't in any actual danger and Sandra is fine."

"I didn't need to protect Gwen, I knew she was safe, what she was moving to do. I…"

"You were free to choose?"

She stepped closer, resting her hand on Cara's shoulder.

"You did the right thing. I think you reacted properly. You were there before I even started reacting, Gwen was there before I could have gotten up."

"But Seb…"

"Cheated? Took advantage of his Gifts to act in a way that Lys noticed should have been impossible?"

She gave Cara the lightest nudge towards the door.

"I'm sure they're waiting for us, or at least for you."

She caught the flicker of distraction, the moment where Cara was probably talking over band. If she had to guess they were likely all communicating, all aware of one another's thoughts and plans even as they acted, they'd all shown those signs before the incident.

"They said to meet them at the dorm."

"Off you go then, I'll-"

"Both of us. Something about wanting all the Fay? All the Fae?"

Fay tried not to laugh. She must not have done a good job because Cara turned back to look at her.

"Fay?"

"I'm Fay, you and Gwen are Fae. So we're all…"

"Did Seb come up with that?" Cara said with a sigh.

"I did, first time I met you both. I told them they were replacing Fay with Fae while they were sipping their coffee."

"You didn't…"

"The mess was worth it."

She saw Cara grinning as they made their way back to the dorms. They didn't want to leave her behind. They wanted her to be there, be with them, even if only for a short time.

"She'll probably be sore tomorrow," Gwen said

"I'll be in my room. Meditating," Lys said.

Sandra started to sit up, only for Gwen to stop her.

"You need to rest."

Rest? Anya sent.

She's not a Combat Path, Gwen answered. I doubt she'll recover as quickly, or be as used to dealing with injuries.

She had combat training before Awakening, Anya sent. And probably for at least a few months in her Husk.

I'd be telling you you should rest if you had the opportunity.

Anya closed the door once Gwen had stepped out of the room.

She actually made that joke? Gwen sent.

She was smug about it, Seb answered as they stepped outside to see him waiting for them. I'm surprised we didn't think of it first.

It's a bad joke, Anya sent, turning to head back to their dorm.

She still disagrees, Cara sent.

Anya felt Fay's thoughts join theirs as they neared the dorm, noticing her through Seb's awareness a second later.

You just don't like that you choked on your coffee, Fay sent.

Fay…

Anya couldn't actually disagree. She was more upset about her own reactions than she was about the joke itself. Fay had gotten them both and Seb had brought it up while she was still worrying about her sister.

I'm glad you weren't hurt, she sent as they all started up the stairs.

It's strange, Fay answered. That sort of manifestation isn't common, even if she's having trouble integrating her Affinity. Unless it was manifesting for years before she recognized it and managed to advance.

It's possible, Anya sent. No one at the Holding would have recognized it if it had. She never let her emotions get the best of her in public.

Tomas might know, Seb sent.

I can ask after I go home, Fay said. We can't do anything more for her.

Hopefully she figures something out, Anya sent before settling comfortably on the floor.

She felt Seb slip his arm around her as he joined her, Cara and Gwen sitting nearly close enough to touch across from them. She saw Fay hesitate before taking her own place to the side, looking significantly less comfortable on the floor.

Sorry we don't have chairs, Anya sent. Don't have them in the Depths so we got used to-

It's fine, Fay answered. It's just different.

I hate her, Gwen sent, her emotions starting to crack. She… she…

It isn't your fault, Cara sent. You did exactly what we planned.

What exactly happened? Seb asked.

Anya felt the uncertainty through the band. It took her a moment to realize it was as much Fay's as it was Gwen's. She'd been part of their band before, but only ever with just the two of them, she'd never had to deal with the Fae as well.

Fay, she sent. You're family. No one thinks you shouldn't be here.

You helped us both, Cara sent. Helped us understand our Gifts, helped us be ourselves.

My mother, Gwen sent. She called us traitors, said I was no daughter-

She what!?

Anya flinched at Fay's reaction.

She refused to accept that Gwen might have had reasons for her actions, Cara sent. Bern was exactly as outraged as we all expected, blamed us for every single slight he could think of, including Lord Percivus's refusal to rank him first.

My father didn't seem happy, Gwen added. Although maybe slightly relieved?

He wasn't upset, not until your mother started yelling. I almost thought he was going to yell at her, and Bern.

He did stop her from saying something, Gwen sent. Or at least she looked at him and decided not to.

Traitor though? Fay asked.

Emerald eyes, Cara answered. There's a history and she is very upset by whatever it is.

Gwen's emotions seemed to hiccup in the band, for a moment turning both angry and pained before she looked at Cara and started laughing.

When you said no to Bern…

He looked more shocked then after Seb nearly punched him that first duel.

And then he tried again, as if screaming would have more effect.

What? Anya asked.

I think they heard you mutter that you'd come to your senses the moment you had a good night's sleep in this dorm, Gwen sent.

That would explain them saying I was a traitor too.

My parents? Twenty-six I think?

Ten years then.

What do you mean? Gwen asked.

Twelve if you end up at the slow side of the elite curve.

Fay? Anya asked.

You should all be level 24 by then, maybe even 25. Even if they're Combat Path they aren't elite from what you've said.

They all felt the moment Gwen realized what Fay meant. A moment later the rest of them caught up and Anya asked the obvious question.

You mean if they force the issue, challenge them?

It's barbaric, Fay said. Something only a Combat Path would do. But sometimes the answer to an obstacle is to smash it with a rock until all that's left is bacon.

By the time they'd finished telling Gwen and Cara about Fay's trip into the Depths to find them the troubles of the day had been set aside. By the time they stopped laughing Fay had promised to demonstrate her technique on a training dummy so that Cara could better understand how to defend family from giant pigs.

By the time they realized how late it had gotten none of them cared that they'd missed dinner. Cara had undone Gwen's hair and was in the process of re-braiding it, while Gwen refused to look at anyone. Fay had given up pretending to be comfortable and was leaning against the wall. The band still felt different, still carried the occasional flare of uncertainty, but no one seemed to mind.

Tomorrow would likely bring new challenges, new frustrations. Next week would bring another attempt at working as a unit in the Depths, at figuring out Fay's project. But none of that mattered, not tonight.
