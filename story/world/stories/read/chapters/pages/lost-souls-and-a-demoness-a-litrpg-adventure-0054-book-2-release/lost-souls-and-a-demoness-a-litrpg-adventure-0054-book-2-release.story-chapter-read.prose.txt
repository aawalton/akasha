I'm happy to announce that book 2 of Lost Souls and a Demoness is now live on Kindle and Audible!

Link: https://www.royalroad.com/amazon/B0FPDL5ZZF?maas=&ref=dbs_m_mng_rwt_calw_tkin_1

I spent a good amount of time tweaking things and making minor adjustments, plus a truck load of grammatical fixes, and I think the end result turned out quite well! I highly recommend checking it out when you get the chance (although I am, of course, biased). Personally, I think book 2 is a lot stronger than book 1, both in terms of the narrative and general pacing/structure. I've learned a lot about how to organize and write a story throughout the whole process of creating Lost Souls and a Demoness, and I hope that each entry ends up being stronger than the one before.

Thanks to all of you for being my supporters on this journey! We're nearing the end of the series with book 3, but it won't be the end of my stories. I hope you'll stick around to see what comes next, I've been working on outlining my second series for a while and I really enjoy the premise!

An extra thanks to everyone who takes the time to leave a rating or review on book 2! Having a couple of early reviews makes a big difference for an indie author like myself. If you want to support the series, that's one of the best ways you can do it.
