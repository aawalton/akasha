Chapter 278

“Permission to teleport you?”

Travis nodded, and after Michael snapped his fingers, the two found themselves in a small, mostly empty room. The presence of the Domain told Travis that this was one of the Sites, but it wasn’t immediately apparent which one of them it was.

“We are in the control tower, aren’t we?” he asked, pretty sure he knew the answer already.

“Yup,” Michael said. Ever since the incident, despite its tragic consequences, he had been acting cheery and almost carefree. “Floor -30. Only you can access it.”

He told Travis the proper procedure to get here. Normally, Icarus would take care of it, but there were several backup methods in case the AI wasn’t available. They were complex enough that Travis had no doubts Michael was only pretending to be carefree, while instead he was putting a lot of serious care into everything that he did. The company was still being restructured following his promotion to CEO and David’s step back, but things were moving along quickly.

“Then you press this button, which scans your biometrics, your magic and your connection to the Origin System and… there.”

A swirl of magic thick enough to daze even Travis preceded the opening of a small opaque portal. It was not only mana that he felt, but also more powerful energies all the way up to the Renegade tier. When he asked about them, Michael grinned.

“It’s the Origin System,” he said, as if it was supposed to be enough to explain the situation. “Come.”

He vanished inside the strange portal, and Travis followed. The transition was not as seamless as it usually was with his portals, instead leaving him feeling like he had just walked into another place that was slightly… off, in a way he couldn’t quite describe.

As soon as the disorientation was gone, he understood just why he had been feeling that way.

“Holy shit,” he whispered, eyebrows shooting up while his mouth hung open. It was a face he rarely made, but even though he was aware of how he looked, he just did not have the strength to school his expression.

All around him, through transparent walls in a room full of holograms and screens, he could see wonders beyond his ability to describe using mere words.

“Where are we?” he asked, even though he knew the answer.

“Travis Tyrell,” Michael announced with a grand gesture. “Welcome to the inner space and the heart of the Origin System.”

It took a moment for Travis to process what he had just heard. Knowing one thing and hearing it from the man himself was rather different. Especially with the strange feeling that had accompanied it, almost as if the revelation that he was in the heart of the System itself had strengthened his already present connection with it. He felt it change, like it was doing a massive update.

“While your System reboots, let me give you a quick tour of the place,” Michael said. There was a hint of a grin on his face, and his eyes were playful and full of life.

“Is this what you have been working on for the last three days?” Travis asked.

“More than just three days,” Michael said. He held up a finger. “Don’t worry. I haven't been using the dungeon’s time dilation. It’s just that all this has been brewing for quite some time now. From even before the whole idea of a System came together, this feels like the culmination of everything we have been trying to achieve. The Huntington incident only made me take that final step.”

Travis approached one of the walls. A thick white line of data cut through the darkness right in front of him, while behind it more and more lines were arranged in all sorts of geometric shapes and forms. Sometimes they met, forming clusters and things Michael explained were called data rooms. There was an instance of Icarus in each one of them, sometimes many of them.

To Travis, they looked like a vast web of… neurons.

“They are,” Michael said. “And yes, I can read the minds of anyone inside my Domains, especially here where my rule is absolute.”

“I know that already,” Travis said. “It just felt strange. Like you were waiting for me to finish thinking, like you already knew what I was going to think before even I did.”

Michael’s face twisted into a complicated mix of emotions that lasted for a fraction of a second before vanishing. “I sort of do. That’s why I try not to focus on it, it’s uncanny. I might have accepted a great deal about myself, my place in the world, and all of this…” he looked around. “But some changes must be gradual. I already accepted the fact that I have long lost my humanity. The things you see all around, which you aptly called neurons, they are me. Some of them are a representation of my actual neurons, others are a representation of the computronium data cubes scattered around the world, surrounded by miniature Domain emitters, while others only exist here. They all come together and form the strange thing that is me and Icarus and the System, three entities in one.”

Travis made a face. “Sounds familiar,” he said.

Michael chuckled. “Funny, isn’t it? I’m still debating whether to tell it to the pope or not. I’m sure he would make a face I won’t ever forget. Better than even yours right now.”

“Please,” Travis said. He could already feel the headache coming. “Do inform me if you do.”

“That’s why we are here, in this room. I present to you the Central Dashboard.”

Michael waited as Travis gingerly approached the myriad of holograms and screens. He began to manipulate them, summoning more of them and dismissing others, skimming over the data, trying his best to take everything in all at once and failing.

“What is this?” he asked in the end. There was sweat on his forehead, and his heart thumped in his chest.

“The thing you’ve always wanted. Admin access to the Origin System.”

“But… this… satellite data, surveillance feeds, live biometrics from all the operators…”

“It’s just the surface,” Michael said. “The Origin System is expanding as we speak. Which, by the way, since it and I and icarus are the same thing, means that we are expanding as we speak. Through this Central Dashboard you will be able to see everything that I see, but it goes beyond that. You will be able to influence reality using all the tools of the Origin System–from issuing Quests to manipulating the very building blocks of the world and the people in it–making you the most powerful being on the planet after Icarus and myself. More powerful than David. More powerful than Johanne. At least, while you are inside a Domain.”

“I… need a moment.”

Travis breathed, feeling his head spin. This… this was the coronation of everything he had ever wanted. He knew the price he was going to pay for it, and he would gladly pay one a million times greater without batting an eye.

“You are basically giving me the keys to the kingdom,” he said, words transforming his simple hunch into stone-solid reality. In this strange space, it was more than possible to do that and more.

“I trust you completely. In exchange,” Michael began. Travis listened, heart in his throat. “I expect you to work your ass off. We are back to our original agreement.”

“You do whatever the fuck you want and I clean up your messes?”

Michael laughed. “I think I have more than earned it, haven’t I?”

“Just like with the President. I had a whole speech prepared. He was not going to walk out of it unscathed.”

“I was sure you had one. Was it long?”

“Oh yes.”

Michael shrugged. “What can I say? This is your life now. Do you accept it?”

“Fuck yes, brother.”

With a swift motion, he approached Michael and held out his arm.

They clasped arms. Not a handshake, but something much deeper.

When they separated, Michael nodded at him. “Back at you, brother. Now, let me tell you about the latest changes to the Origin System. Other than its capabilities as a management system, which you will explore with Icarus, I am beginning to work on its ability to function as a complete magic system that lets us retain control, and influence its users directly.”

Travis nodded as Michael spoke.

“The first thing we did was add XP to the System and made everything revolve around it. Every user starts with a bare-bones System that only gives them quests and rewards XP. Their first quest will unlock the shop, which is a built-in way to spend this XP to get stuff in exchange and unlock the rest of the System. For instance, if a user wanted a [Fireball] skill, the System would offer various choices. Then, after the choice, a new line would form from the user to the inner space, and then back out to the Candle Light vaults, connecting to the skill stone, or the card or the tome or whatever. I also found a way to emulate stats and other things we cannot influence directly just yet, using passive skills. Let’s say someone wants to increase their Reflexes stats, the System just hooks him up with a passive [Reflexes] skill and modulates its output to match the increase the user bought with his XP. For now, XP is just a made-up number that is arbitrarily associated with the user but, do you remember a while ago when you caught me opening and closing doors at random all around Site 00?”

“Yes?”

“Do you remember what I was doing?”

“You were trying to gather Unity energy,” Travis said as his eyes lit up.

“Touchè. Unity energy is a kind of energy that is generated from actions of significance. So, not only would it be very abundant because all actions generate Unity Energy, unlike regular magic which is only generated when enough of it gathers together in particular circumstances like the storm. With it, the System would gain the ability to do a lot of new and cool stuff. Remember, the System can only use magic up to the sum total of the magic of its users, this inner space here, and everything it can get from outside connections like the Candle Light vaults. Eventually, I hope to expand the outside connections to the whole volume of space controlled by Domains–but I am having trouble linking the Domains, the System and the spiral black hole.”

“That’s how you have been able to use Renegade Energy, is it not? Through the Origin System?”

“Yes, but it came from the coins in the inner space and not from the users, nothing really new. We have several hundred Operators plus the President hooked up to the System, but save for a few of them, they are all too weak to amount to more than a trickle of magic compared to my current stash of coins. By default, the System only takes their extra regeneration, which would otherwise go to waste, but save for me nobody can spontaneously generate energy just yet. Even worse, people outside an Area of Influence don’t have ambient magic to absorb, and we need to sell them coins in exchange for XP. Only the dungeon farming expeditions and the spiral are keeping us topped up. Even my own regeneration is not really keeping up, something I want to rectify soon.”

“Or you could just sit around and relax,” Travis said.

“I know you are joking, but this is a serious matter. The System’s core is here, in the inner space. I must be ahead of everyone all the time, or the strain would be too much and it could all fall apart. Or people could find a way to break the System. The good thing is that I can use the System as a new way to grow now.”

“And now the XP makes sense,” Travis said. “You could use something like quests to make people do stuff so that you can harvest more magic, and eventually even Unity energy from them. The same energy could then be used, through the System, to power them up through the shop! This removes talent from the equation.”

“You get it. And the cool part is that you will be able to use Quests to influence the world through your Admin access. The only limit I'm putting on it is that you will only be able to use your admin rights while physically inside the Central Dashboard. Outside, you will have access to special privileges, along with David and Johanne at the current moment. This means that you will be able to channel the Origin System to get to a level of power that is very close to mine for a short period of time. Please don’t abuse it, because we are running with limited resources.”

“You don’t have to tell me. I was a businessman before all this, remember?” he said.

“Good. In fact, please use the quests to encourage people to grow their own power rather than having to rely on System power. It will make them feel good about having something that they will think is really theirs, earned through their own efforts, while in the end it won’t make a speck of difference. Anyone with the Origin System is ours, permanently, completely, their magic and all, no matter whether they believe their power comes from them or from the System.”

“Brother, this is insane,” Travis had to sit, his legs giving out on him. “You really do trust me with all of this?”

“It’s your idea, taken to the extreme. And it relies on a lot of things we still don’t have, hence why the System is still in early alpha. Now, it’s time for me to go grind in the dungeon.”

“Can I stay? Or will you being in the dungeon influence this space?”

“You can stay, there should be no issue. The inner space is like a separate dimension. The exit portal will remain open and you can come and go as you please.”

“It’s not a liability for you, is it?”

“You should see the datascript lining the room in the control tower and, look around. Look below.”

“The spiral,” Travis said.

“This is the closest room to the spiral’s event horizon of all the inner space. Anything goes wrong, this data room is the first thing to go. Don’t worry, it won’t break the System, but it will kill any intruder for sure. This is on top of all the other security measures. Ah, before I forget. You can wander around if you want, but don’t EVER leave the boundary of my aura. Don’t touch the purple clouds and don’t, for any reason, venture inside of them even if your life is at stake. Not even I know what hides there.”

“Alright. Don’t worry. So you will be in the dungeon doing things and fighting monsters, while I will be here like nothing is happening?”

“Yes, you might see the outside world slow down a bit if I go into the deeper floors, but that’s it. You won’t notice anything else.”

“What about the taint?”

“You will be able to monitor its progression by looking at the data lines. Do you see that tar-like substance corrupting them? It always starts from the bottom, from the black hole. It won’t reach this room, it’s isolated.”

“So I'll be able to check on the status of your taint.”

“Yes. But Infy will give me more taint serum after beating a floor if I need it. It requires some rule-bending, but nothing she can’t handle. Now, anything else? An Icarus instance will always be available if you want them to help you out with something, you just need to call them.”

There were a million questions in Travis’s mind, of course. Starting from what would the taint do to the System itself if it managed to infect enough of the strange trinity being that was Michael, Icarus and it, all the way to what he should do now that he had admin access.

He knew that Michael could read his mind. The man, however, simply looked at him and waited for him to speak.

“That should be all, thank you Michael. Have fun delving.”

As Travis sat on the strangely comfortable floating chair in front of the bulk of the screens, Michael turned around one last time.

“Have fun spying on the President. By the way, if you want to wake him up early just initialise the System and trigger the welcome message. It should be fun to see his reaction.”
