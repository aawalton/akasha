The Grand Frame decided to grant him a new Soul Glyph now that he had absorbed a great deal of divine energy, so to speak.

[ Achievement Recognized: Time Breaker

You have manifested the living past itself.

Rank up from Empyrean [38] to Empyrean [44].

Reward: Soul Glyph of Time evolved to Soul Glyph of Eternity. ]

There. He had done it. His Soul Glyph of Time was now the Soul Glyph of Eternity. Now, all that was left was manipulating time itself just as the Grand Frame had claimed.

“Ryland,” Xyke warned. “You’re drawing in too much divine energy from the surroundings. It’s breaking apart.”

She was right. Now that Ryland’s Soul Glyph of Time had decided to evolve, it was causing this whole region of reality to warp and shift continuously. That added another layer of stress to the decomposition that Xyke was worried about.

That said, Ryland was succeeding in his actual goal. The essence of the past that the Order mages had brought here was coming to life before him, glowing brighter and brighter every passing moment. With a little more effort, he would be able to recreate it in reality, and then he’d be able to draw connections to it.

Giant cracks thundered through the space, shattering through everything.

Ryland and Xyke both stared up with great alarm, watching the divinity of the region they were in strip away to nothing. The sight was impossible. All the divine energy had simply ripped away from the “walls” of the pseudo-Realm, revealing… that it was no Realm to begin with.

“This… this is a Wound Corridor’s interior!” Xyke said. “And an enormous one.”

The alarm bells ringing in Ryland’s head only grew louder in intensity. They had been tricked. The Flourinus Order hadn’t led them to a different Realm or region of sorts. They had just hollowed out the natural Wound Corridor, increasing it in size and width until it was big enough to be disguised as a Realm.

And now, the Order was returning to finish this.

“What’s this?” Valorous said. His body was different, older and lankier, but his voice remained that same annoying grate. “Trying to come up with the essence of our Incarnation?”

“Perhaps we should lend a hand?” said another Order mage. A powerful one. Ryland frowned. That thin figure, that rough-hewn face, that all suggested the speaker was Dexterous, but it might not be him. They had ways of changing their bodies, after all.

But it didn’t matter. There were a few others as well, but Ryland paid them no attention. For all of them had decided to gather their magic together and unleash their stolen essence.

The Incarnation Ryland had been seeking.

A spell circle bloomed in the sky far above, the Order mages casting magic creating the main points within the formation. It was barely an instant before a meteor shower of white bolts pelted down upon them.

“[Eternal Bastion],” Ryland incanted hurriedly.

Sadly, the spell didn’t have much effect.

The hammering bolts flashed down with so much power, they were shading the entire region of the Wound Corridor with burning daylight. They speared through Ryland’s barrier and quickly flashed into the gathering of essence Ryland had been recreating through time.

“They’re occupying a different Plane,” Xyke said, her words coming out quickly. “The bastards came well prepared.”

Of course they had. Ryland had proof in how they hadn’t waited for a single second before unleashing their spell, which had taken into account whatever counterspells they’d need to succeed against Ryland.

For just a second, Ryland entertained the idea of rising to meet the Order mages head-on. They had chosen to appear out from the Overrealm. This was a great opportunity to finally capture them and find out what they were doing with Acrypha.

“Forget it!” Valorous shouted down from above. “You have no room to fight us. Face your real—”

“Take care of the Incarnation, Xyke,” Ryland said. He didn’t wait for the Glyphmaster’s response. “[Translocate].”

The cast took him straight to the Wound Corridor’s ceiling. Sadly, the Order was wise to his proclivities, so no sooner had he arrived there than the mages started disappearing in much the same way they had arrived. This essentially had them melding into the walls of the Wound Corridor, sinking into and becoming part of the twisted structure.

“How silly of you to assume you can spare any time for us,” Valorous’s disembodied voice said. He was already one again with the edge of reality, seemingly beyond Ryland’s ability to attack.

Seemingly.

“How foolish of you to doubt me,” Ryland said. “[Mana Burn].”

He had appeared right near the very top of the entire Wound Corridor, where he had then thrust his hand into edge of reality. Then his mana had emerged. Like an eruption gurgling out from the depths of the earth, a violent surge of mana ripped off Ryland before injecting into the Wound Corridor with powerful spikes.

A chorus of shrieks followed, cries of pain and agony ricocheting through the entire realm. Sadly, it didn’t last long. The Order had already fled to wherever they had come from. Likely somewhere deep within the Overrealm.

“I can’t hold him for much longer, Ryland!” Xyke warned.

Her words echoed up hundreds of armspans, buoyed by the power inherent within an Incarnation. Or from one of her many Glyphs. But Ryland cast [Translocate] yet again to return to his original location, for Xyke wasn’t the kind to fight against any foe head on as he had learned to do over the centuries.

“Do you know which Incarnation this is?” Ryland asked, trying to figure out the amorphous creature writhing to violent life from within that overly dense combination of essence.

“Growth,” Xyke said with absolute certainty. “That’s the Incarnation of Growth.”

Hmm. That probably wasn’t good. Even for Ryland.

Silently sending forth an apology, he actually gave voice to his spell. “[Dragongod’s Endbreath].”

A colossal rift allowed the colossal maw of the Dragongod to burst into the Wound Corridor. It opened wide, before spewing forth a stream of energy that could have passed for a solar flare. The whole region turned ruddy white as the stream collided with the Incarnation.

It failed to end the threat before it could grow out of control. Ryland was already too late, likely due to his attack on the Order itself.

And because he hadn’t known this was the Incarnation of Growth.

Thick threads of fleshy matter exploded away from the point of contact, shooting out as fast as lightning as they collided with the different sections of the Wound Corridor. Enormous as [Dragongod’s Endbreath] was, it couldn’t cover the entire breadth of the Corridor, so there was room for a sentient being capable of evading to escape to. Partially, if nothing else.

Now, the Incarnation was targeting one of Ryland’s weaknesses—multi-targeting. There were too many pieces of it to take them out all at once.

He attempted it, nevertheless. “[Eye of the Storm].”

By creating rifts across the face of a furious storm, Ryland could send forth an avalanche of lightning all at the same time. Or as close to it as was possible. A hundred bolts flew out from the series of rifts, striking the flesh threads from the broken Incarnation.

Not fast enough. Yet again. A few of the bits and pieces survived, moving almost as fast as the bolts Ryland had fired out.

Then they coalesced together to quickly form the Incarnation in truth.

Its form was partially skinless flesh, partially blistering light. Arms rippled out, bones jutted here and there, more limbs grew haphazardly around its thick head and thicker torso, jets of light shooting out of its shrieking body as more essence than it could control was stuffed into a form that shouldn’t have even existed in this timestream to begin with.

Basically, they were recreating an abomination of an Incarnation. A violation of whatever state it had once been in now forced to return in this horrific living atrocity.

“[Endless Evolution],” Ryland incanted. “[Everlife Spectre]. [Translocate].”

The goal was simple—make the Incarnation stop existing in its abhorrent state. To that end, Ryland needed to grab the essence in a much more concrete manner, to pull out the very manasoul if possible without simply relying on mindless mass destruction.

But the problem was that the Incarnation was, well, an Incarnation.

As soon as Ryland appeared behind his target, the newly formed being had already turned to face him. Its idea of growth was pressing on Ryland, who had unleashed his own Incarnation form already, but was still being pushed back by the sheer virulence of his opponent’s aura.

Growth wasn’t simply limited to the Incarnation’s biological form, after all. The very air was growing too, churning into a gale where its very particles were rapidly becoming unstably huge.

Ryland slammed his fist forward but missed. The growing air was pushing him back.

His opponent shrieked at him. Mindless. The Order had reduced an Incarnation into a mindless being. Ryland could only scowl.

Just before he was attacked, an Encapsulated Glyph of Repulsive Dispersion created a hole in the growing air. There! Xyke had created an opening.

Ryland flashed forward, hand outstretched to grab his target’s torso and stop it. But when he was a hairsbreadth from making contact, the Incarnation’s stomach simply deformed, turning into a maw. It crunched down on Ryland’s arm, but not before he had cast another [Translocate] to attack from a different angle.

But the maws kept appearing, his opponent reacting just as fast if not even faster than he was.

“[Void Seal],” Ryland finally cast, somewhat in exasperation. An orb of darkness appeared around the offending Incarnation, finally keeping it at bay for now. “[Mindscape].”

The next spell was to ensure he got a proper breather. It would have been ideal to include Xyke in the process as well, but unfortunately, that would be difficult.

But in the less-than-two-minutes that Ryland possessed, he quickly came up with a plan of action. The main point was that letting the Incarnation of Growth get even a miniscule opportunity to express himself would create a disaster. Not one that Ryland couldn’t tame, but one he’d rather do without.

So, he focused on what he needed to do as time returned to normal.

“Xyke!” Ryland warned.

His fellow Incarnation was already on it.

“I have it contained,” she said, her voice a forced calm.

The trapped being was already applying its innate power to [Void Seal]. The orb of darkness keeping it locked down was expanding with blinding speed.

Until it met the Glyphs Xyke had cast to keep space itself contained.

The stalemate wasn’t going to last long. Incarnations were capable of overcoming each other’s powers, and the sphere of darkness was starting to crack with violent fracturing noises. Overflowing spikes of bone and blood lit up like lava were already jetting out of the trap.

To think, Ryland usually considered [Void Seal] a perfect cage for anyone who was incapable of casting spatial magic.

Yet, when it came to an Incarnation, that failed. Ryland wouldn’t be surprised if the Incarnation was causing space itself to grow at a pace that the universe itself couldn’t withstand, causing a rupture in reality itself. Ryland was intimately familiar with such incidents.

“I can contain it one more time,” Xyke said.

“Good,” Ryland said. “Then I will ensure that you get that opportunity.”

The Incarnation chose that moment to explode out of the darkness sphere. Ryland had seen his [Void Seal] shatter before, but never in such a fashion. Space had broken out of the enclosed region, but not before it had somehow fused with the being trapped within it. Now, aside from bulging flesh and bloodied bone, the Incarnation had light and space cladding its form.

He really did embody the idea of growth. Even when it came to horror, it was growing.

It was a good thing Ryland had already prepared a slew of spells to deal with the issue once and for all.

“[Continental Step],” Ryland incanted. “[Broken Realm].”

The first one opened another massive rift, where a country-sized cliff with a face of burning magma descended upon the Incarnation. One reason Ryland relied on the spell was that his target was growing. As such, his speed was reducing. Evasion was getting more difficult.

It crashed in, and the Incarnation screamed. But where its speed had slowed thanks to its rising mass, that same increase in mass meant it wasn’t squashed.

Instead, growth started affecting the foot of the Magma Giant that Ryland had called in. Stones were popping and shooting out. The particles of the rock, solid or molten, were expanding and bursting out from the gigantic foot. It wouldn’t be long before the Incarnation somehow managed to incorporate the rocks and magma into itself as well.

Thankfully, [Broken Realm] stopped it from getting too crazy. The idea of growth could only be applied to one type of element or vector at any given time. It was impossible to target both rocks and space itself simultaneously. That was what kept the Incarnation of Growth at bay.

That was what allowed Ryland to cast the next spell.

“[Erupt—”

It turned out Ryland had perhaps underestimated the half-formed Incarnation standing against him.

Somehow, the malformed being used space itself as a springboard, launching its mass at Ryland with blood and blistering light wreathing the lance of spearing bones jutting out from its body. A burning halo streamed around the being like it was turning into a comet.

Ryland was forced to shift his spell in real time. “[Awakened Avatar]. [Animus Aegis].”

The collision didn’t leave a mark, but only thanks to the barrier spells he created in no time at all. Not only that, but the Incarnation of Growth bounced off Ryland as well, thanks in part to the sudden bloom of Glyphs from Xyke. She was glowing now too, her Incarnation form loose as she appeared as an ethereal dragonkin with wings of ghostly flames.

Ryland would have given her a look of gratitude, but now was the time to take advantage. Once the Incarnation stopped spewing its furious stream of energy, of course.

As soon as he had bounced off and discovered that assaulting directly was making no headway against Ryland, the Incarnation had shifted to a new tactic. A volcanic eruption of energy shot out with a shrieking fury.

Sadly, for the Incarnation of Growth, he still wasn’t as strong as he really ought to be. This was nothing compared to a spell like [Dragongod’s Endbreath]. The stream of energy simply ricocheted off Ryland’s Incarnation form empowered by his multiple barriers, tearing off bits and pieces but nothing at all like what Mavar had managed. So, Ryland countered.

“[Great Arsenal: God’s Spears],” he incanted.

Since there was a ton of divine energy here, why not make use of it?

Rifts cracked open all around, more gold than black thanks to the divine energy filling everything to the brim. There was so much power warping the very air itself that the whole of the Wound Corridor started bowing inwards.

But the bigger effect was, quite obviously, the thundering golden spears even longer than battleships bursting out of the rifts.

They stabbed forward with blinding speed, their spearheads twisting to form a drilling funnel with a cyclone stream trailing their charge. Each spear carved through space itself to leave behind a warp in reality that would catch and end anyone who managed to somehow escape the thrusts.

The Incarnation responded with shocking speed. Literally. As space rippled behind the Incarnation of Growth like a trampoline to send him shooting forward, cracks thundered through everything.

Several of the Gods’ Spears missed thanks to the Incarnation’s speed, and a few managed to graze its meteor-like form. But none managed to land a direct blow.

Until Ryland disappeared with another [Translocate].

Another rift had opened behind him, but the gigantic spear jutting out from its depths had been obscured by Ryland’s Incarnation form. At the very last moment, Ryland had vanished, leaving the spear free to charge forward. Straight into the onrushing Incarnation of Growth. His own momentum had him spiking himself onto the spear crackling with divine energy.

The shriek from the Incarnation made Ryland wince. Poor thing was out of control and had to be put down, but that didn’t mean he deserved this.

The Order had ripped the Incarnation from his vessel and turned him monstrous.

With the Incarnation now stopped, at least momentarily, all the other huge spears swiftly shifted before stabbing into their target from a wide range of angles. It couldn’t dodge. The shrieking just amplified to an even worse degree now that the Incarnation was caught.

“Xyke,” Ryland said.

She was already acting. Glyphs were trapping the Incarnation. Enormous Encapsulated Glyphs were quickly overtaking the trapped Incarnation’s position, forming right through the divine spears and crackling energy. Ryland was trying to read them, to see if he could add his own spell circles to assist Xyke, though she normally wouldn’t need any sort of help.

It was enough for now, though, as the Grand Frame decided to confirm.

[ Achievement Recognized: Incarnation Trapper

You’ve trapped an Incarnation, despite being only Empyrean-ranked.

Rank up from Empyrean [44] to Empyrean [63].

Reward: 75 Merge Prints. ]

Now that was an odd reward. Merge Prints. Ryland couldn’t recall ever receiving those, though he did know of them.

His musings were interrupted when more cracks appeared. More divine rifts, none of which Ryland had called upon. A flood of divine energy entered the Wound Corridor, and with it came a new interruption.

It seemed the Flourinus Order had called in a Great God as reinforcements.
