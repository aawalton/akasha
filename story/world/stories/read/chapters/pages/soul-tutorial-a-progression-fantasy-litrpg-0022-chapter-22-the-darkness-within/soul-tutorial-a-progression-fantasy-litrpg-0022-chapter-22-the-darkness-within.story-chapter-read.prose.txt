Luke woke up with a stiff face, completely numb on the side he slept on. But what Luke’s attention was drawn to was his own soul as he finally could peer inside his soul chamber, and what lay before his eyes was just disastrous.

If this were all real, he really questioned how he was alive right now, even if he didn’t understand how bad the current state of his soul actually is. It was still black, dark, and foul, as a quarter of his soul was currently stained even after he hadn’t used soul energy for days.

Crimson red completely replaced the golden color within the thread veins. He desperately needed a solution and frankly made his way to the pedestal, stumbling and sweating profusely. He genuinely questioned what had happened to him.

“No, no, no, how can this be happening!” he shouted.

Could I never use soul energy again.. would my soul even be alright?

His mind was filled with thoughts of death.

“If I tried to push myself anymore with soul energy…would this be it for me?” He mumbled, head down, staring at his feet.

Is this just a world that wants to see me die?

Again, he had to change. This reality needed him to change. He held back his tears, clenching his teeth, trying to find any information or anything that could be done.

Even if it was just false hope, he needed some assurance as his sweaty palm came into contact with the pedestal. Anything new had changed, and the work on his face had disappeared as he forced himself awake instantly.

“Why am I going to the damn pedestal? It’s useless!” Luke said while scratching his chest.

His primary source of power had been cut away from him. His hand trembled.

“I can’t lose soul energy, at least.. not right now.” He said, while wiping away the tears that streamed down his face.

His chance of survival disappeared right before his eyes when he looked at the state of his soul.

If finding out information here was useless, he could at least try to see if using souls had any changes to it. With his soul chamber open, maybe it would still be possible to harbor a single soul and see what happens.

With furrowed eyes, he forced the soul out from above, leading it towards his soul chamber. The soul stood alongside his own; the best he could do currently was a single soul. Starting slowly as it wobbled into place.

Suddenly, his body contorted as streams of red tears flowed out of his eyes, turning them bloodshot. He still forced it to continue as his chest inflated, only to deflate the moment soul energy floated through his body.

But he stopped it the very moment he confirmed he could use soul energy, but that wasn’t without consequences; his body felt like it was being eaten by fire ants.

Screaming his heart out, he fell on the floor while convulsing, his limbs twisted in weird ways. The pain came from within, as his very cells felt like they were being vanquished, but that was all in his head. His soul shook, but the darkness didn’t grow any bigger, and he felt like he was losing himself in all that.

Suddenly, peace was reclaimed as the pain disappeared as quickly as it came, and his soul stayed completely the same since he saw it.

He slowly got up to feel nothing but tiredness. His face darkened as he tried to figure out what had happened.

The pain was clearly from his soul fatigue, but why had the pain disappeared? He could only be thankful he didn’t need to go through the torment anymore; eyes still bloodshot with residue of blood on his cheeks, he could only take it with a grain of salt and just fight more to place his mind in a better place.

Time passed swiftly with no issues, as not a single elite had popped up since that time, and he clearly had killed hundreds of slimes at this point, but half of all those points gained were spent on restore.

His stats still looked bare, as not a single attribute had made it to double digits, with the highest only being one away. His eyes peered into nothingness as he stood there, leaning over the pedestal. He might as well try to make his soul resonance stronger to see if that would change anything.

But he still distributed his points as he did before, with most of it all going into the constitution. He needed defence over everything if he wanted a firm body to handle the strain the souls applied to him.

Constitution: 9 → 10 [19 points]

He looked over the rest of his attributes and decided it was finally time to have everything leveled up. He didn’t have as much as he did with his last attribute gain, but he still had 55. Sensory and intellect were falling behind. His points disappeared as he upgraded both once.

Sensory: 0 → 1 [1 point]

Intellect: 0 → 1 [1 point]

So, with nothing more than getting used to his improved body, he gradually tried to wait out whether anything would change within his soul chamber if he didn’t use soul energy.

He watched as the slime was disintegrated by his fist, all dying without much improvement on the way he fights, with the upgraded attributes that provided no problems. Minutes turned into hours, hours turned into days. Before he knew it, he was fighting a metal slime for fun as his fist glided along its metallic shell, echoing out as he pulled back his fist. Taking a step back, he launched his leg towards the solid slime.

Crashing into it, cracking the silver luster of its body, apparently ending the slime as it disintegrated before his very eyes. Luke walked over towards the pedestal, laying his hand on it as the screen displayed itself right before his eyes.

His attributes were portrayed right before his face. With physique and soul resonance being level 10, his highest stat, constitution, stood at 12. With his lowest attribute, agility, being level 4. All that time of just continued fighting has changed him into a robotic slime-killing machine as his bored gaze landed on floor 3.

His heart quickened at the thought of facing off on floor 3 again, causing his right hand to twitch.

The memories of those flames burning him away, which almost completely paralyzed his other hand. Grasping his fist at the thought of getting revenge, he readied his heart as he willed for floor 2 to repeat itself. He still needed to go through some more training. Not being able to control his body to the best of his abilities just yet.

I can’t wait to beat this slime into pulverized gel

As the energy shifted around him, Luke peered inside his chamber to see how the progress was going, only to be disappointed with the foul darkness still occupying mostly the same space, with only his veins not being as blood red as before.

But that didn’t really change the effect the darkness already had on him. But he usually just endured the swift events of pain from using the souls most of the time.

Luke pulled up the veil once more to keep his attributes in mind for when he defeats this thing.
