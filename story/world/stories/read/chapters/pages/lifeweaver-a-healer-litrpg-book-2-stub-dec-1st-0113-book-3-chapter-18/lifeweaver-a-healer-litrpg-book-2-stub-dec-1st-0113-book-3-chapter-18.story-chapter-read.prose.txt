Screams of anguish and death erupted from behind what could only be described as a demon. With fire for eyes, a maw that opened wide in something resembling a grin, horns jutting up and back, and skin the color of blood, it stepped through the opening it had torn through the air itself. As the monster emerged into the large hall, veins of bright orange appeared, radiating outward from the center of its chest, giving it a fearsome glow.

Simean, Lesser Demon of The Fallen Realm. Level 35.

Simean spoke, its words far clearer than Luke expected from that monstrous mouth. “Sister Leander.”

“BEGONE, FOUL DEMON!” Clementine shouted, her cheeks red and her eyes betraying the fear that must be simmering inside her.

The white light that had decimated the lesser undead surrounded the demon in an instant, but it just stood there, a grin plastered across its face. “I am no undead thrall, Sister Leander. Come with me, and I shall show you pleasure and joy beyond your comprehension.”

“GOD WILL SMITE YOU, DEMON!”

“Not much of a Holy Whisperer,” Ray said, having appeared next to Luke. “More of a Holy Shouter.”

“That thing will be difficult,” Luke said.

Ray nodded and disappeared again, his next words drifting through the empty air. “I’ll be careful, Mom.”

“This is a big one!” Nick shouted, destroying the last of the undead by bashing it with his shield.

Curtis, Hannah, and Nick raced toward the demon, trying to reach it before the thing decided to focus more on Clementine, their healer. Well, one of their healers. Luke followed, leaving George, Clementine, and Ariel behind. The latter seemed to have taken an interest in the robed figures who had worked together to either summon or call for the beast now descending the stairs, but he got the sense she knew how to take care of herself.

Curtis was first in line to engage Simean, but Nick showed impressive speed and overtook his older brother to get the first strike in. He threw himself at their opponent with a war cry that echoed off the walls, and his sword took on a glowing quality that extended well beyond the point of the weapon. The slash flew out with impressive speed, taking even the demon off guard, and it withdrew its hand with a wince as the skin opened up to reveal more of the orange light beneath.

A roar erupted from Simean, and it pulled back the arm to shield it from another attack. A feint, it turned out. With nothing more than a slight shift of its weight, Simean struck out with one massive punch aimed at Nick’s head. It was too quick for Luke to follow, even at that distance, but Nick saw it coming and ducked under. He followed through and thrust the blade upward into the demon’s arm, giving it another wound.

That was when the screaming began.

Some of that orange blood landed on Nick. It sizzled and burned, like liquid fire, tearing through his armor. The smell of burnt hair reached Luke as he raced forward, but he didn’t have a chance to get there in time.

In that haze of pain, Nick didn’t react in time, and another blow from the demon found its mark. It slammed into Nick with an impact great enough that the Knight almost seemed to disappear into thin air, before slamming into the stone wall to the demon’s right.

A gasp of pain escaped him, and when Nick rebounded off and fell to the ground in a heap, cracks in the stone showed just how much force had been behind that one attack. This thing was strong, and it wasn’t even the boss.

Clementine’s white glow surrounded Nick even before he hit the ground, so Luke let her deal with the man’s injuries. Better he kept going to support the others, if they wanted to bring it to its knees before it sent the rest of the group flying.

“Humans always believe themselves stronger than any opponent,” Simean said, his voice reverberating through the chamber. “See what little good resistance did for the Lilineese, sister! Not even your so-called God can protect you here in this conquered realm!”

“SILENCE, DEMON!” Clementine shouted without taking her eyes off Nick, who was getting to his feet, looking a little worse for wear.

Curtis reached the demon, slid under a swing from the monster, and got to one knee as he thrust his now re-formed sword forward and up. The dark tip of his weapon almost reached. Almost. Simean, light on his feet, stepped back and slid to the side before lifting a foot for a forward kick that Curtis had no hope of dodging.

A shiver-inducing crunch rang out as he remained in place, the heel of the demon's foot well past where his head had been. Rather than fall, however, Curtis's headless body continued forward, darkness streaming from his neck. The large sword disappeared, and the Reaver found his mark in the demon's side, sinking two smaller blades of darkness into its flesh.

Simean raised his head to bellow, but as he did so, Dirk appeared in the air at the end of a jumping arc that took him right in front of the monster. The short sword he favored slammed into the demon's exposed neck as another Dirk appeared, doing the same, and then another.

When Simean raised an arm to swat the Mirror Rogue off, Hannah's shadow tentacles reached up to restrain him. They held for a mere moment before the monster's incredible strength tore them apart, but it was enough for Dirk to re-enter stealth.

Simean was about to launch another attack, but stopped and let out a shriek of pain. Fiery blood poured from between the monster's legs, and Ray cried out. A few sizzling drops floated in the air, revealing the Rogue's location. Even with the onslaught of attacks, Simean was far from finished, and the monster's inhuman roars took on a new inflection. Anger, rather than pain. It had never been afraid of them, and that hadn't changed. Now, it was pissed off.

Just as Nick was about to rejoin the fight, Simean touched two hands to its chest, and some sort of runic letters appeared. Luke couldn't read what they said, but he got a sense of them, and they were wrong, profoundly incompatible with anything human. Suffering lived within that script, and even as the demon's blood poured out of it to cover its skin, Luke knew that something far worse was about to happen.

Simean's voice rang out in the chamber, coming from all directions and none. "See my true form and despair!"

With that, the demon's body, hidden by the burning glow of its own blood, began to transform.

The small group of humans scattered as the beast fell down on its knees, its burning blood flying in all directions. Not even Curtis's darkness or Hannah's shadows seemed immune to it, because even they showed clear signs of pain when touched. Clementine kept up her healing, which was all well and good for the minor burns the group sustained, allowing Luke to keep at the ready if something more serious happened. Simean's form got down to all fours and even with all that burning, orange-glowing blood coming off it, Luke saw when it opened its mouth and began puking up even more of the stuff, creating a small patch of it on the ground that grew and grew to cover the entire floor around the monster.

"That will be a pain," Curtis grunted, retreating to Luke's position.

"Ray!" Luke shouted, pointing to the stairs, where the robed figures were now in the process of casting another spell. "Ariel is heading for them! Go help her!"

Ray took one look at the slow-building lake of fire and nodded with seldom-seen enthusiasm. "Good idea!"

"Is it growing?" Nick asked. He looked unsure of how to approach the problem at hand. Getting close enough to strike at the monster now meant a high risk of burning.

Also, it was indeed growing. Not larger, perhaps, but the flowing glow of orange bulged outward along its side. With a splash of fiery death, new limbs emerged. That wasn't all. Cracking and popping sounds from its back kicked off another process of transformation, one where it folded up on itself. Shrieking, Simean's head emerged from the cocoon of fire to show its new form. It looked almost like a twisted version of a centaur with its four legs and two arms, but more than anything, the demon looked far too pleased with itself.

"Maybe we should have attacked it while it was transforming," Dirk said, appearing right next to the group.

"You think?" Hannah asked, her tone dripping with sarcasm.

George kept his eyes on the monster as he walked up to Nick. "Its mind is too strong. Not much I can do here."

Nick nodded. "See if you can help Ariel."

"She doesn't need help," George said, but stepped aside to go around the now receding lake of fire.

Just as he said that, the robed figures on the stairs began to let out wails, arms reaching out to hold their heads or chests.

"What's going on with them?" Luke asked.

"Ariel," Curtis said.

Luke pointed. "But she's nowhere near them."

"She doesn't have to be."

"Here it comes!" Hannah shouted.

Simean, now with all of its disgusting, flesh-like, and glittering body showing, let out a roar as it charged forward. Everyone readied themselves, but soon found that they were not the target. Rather than attack Nick, Curtis, or Hannah, Simean bounded to the side and jumped, soaring through the air in a trajectory that would see the monster crush Clementine under its massive bulk.
