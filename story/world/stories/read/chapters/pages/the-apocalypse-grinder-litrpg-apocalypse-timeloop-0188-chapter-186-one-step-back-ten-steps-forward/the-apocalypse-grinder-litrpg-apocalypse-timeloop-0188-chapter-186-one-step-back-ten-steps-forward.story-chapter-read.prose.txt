The hammer crashed into the four crossed arms of the Vatronid abomination a second time. Ronan’s ruined forearms were like wet spaghetti, barely holding the weapon straight. Only through sheer grit was he able to deliver the blow.

Still, his foe could not prevent the assault. A third and fourth hammer blow smashed into it, with the fourth finally breaking through its guard. The four arms were bent and broken beyond recognition, giving way to the fifth strike that blew a hole in its chest.

The critical hit was devastating, sending chunks of grey flesh flying and breaking hard tissue inside the Vatronid’s body. Ronan had no idea what it was made of, but he was destroying it.

His mana and stamina were far from being depleted. His huge stat gain meant his resource pools were far from what they’d once been. A sixth, seventh, and eighth hammer blow smashed into the Vatronid’s chest. Chunks of grey flesh were torn and battered with every strike.

No matter how much he struck his foe, the yellow orbs did not flinch in their gaze. He felt the four arms repairing themselves even as he continued to pour himself into the skill. A ninth blow struck so hard that it tore all the way through the creature’s body and thudded into the trunk of the tree behind it. The hammer bounced off, almost destabilising Ronan. He refused to fall, not done with his relentless assault.

The tenth hammer blow literally exploded the Vatronid’s torso. Ronan was hopeful, wishing he might hear the chime of victory. It never came.

Instead, as his arm reared back for an eleventh strike, he felt something shoot out and wrap around his legs and arms. His arm pushed against the restraints, but failed to finish the swing. Glancing to the side, he saw tendrils of grey flesh entangling his body from foot to neck.

The lower half of the Vatronid had morphed into biological chains that kept him locked in place. The upper half collapsed on itself, gluing to the trunk of the tree and then compressing inwards. Ronan tried to cast a lightning strike, but before the mana even left his heart, the clump of grey flesh formed a needle that blasted through his forehead and pierced his brain.

He remained conscious for about six seconds, but he lost control of his body after two. The last thing he felt was the Vatronid’s strange flesh expanding through his skull and prodding at his brain.

The iteration analysis brought him another windfall. The most chronos merits he’d ever earned from a single iteration, along with a huge upswing in all of his multipliers. In his next iteration he felt he could tear the Vatronid fusion creature apart with ease, no matter if it was formed from seven or seventeen of the odd grey aliens.

That would be a problem to worry about when he faced it, however, as right now he had a lot on his to-do list. The ancestral grounds of the Chronos Clan unfurled before him in the endless white void, the Temporal Obelisk calling to him as though it were his hearth. In that moment, it felt like returning home.

For a moment Ronan basked in the infinity of it all. The white space stretched into endlessness, no physical dimensions to speak of. The night sky had a certain majesty, but this was a different experience entirely.

He wondered who had created the Temporal Obelisk. Had they been through the same struggles as him? Endless deaths and continued improvement? Watching the same people dying over and over again and not being able to change their fate in any meaningful way?

He enjoyed growing stronger. He enjoyed fighting. That last battle against the Vatronid had been the most alive he’d felt since… Well, forever. When the needle pierced his brain, it hurt like hell. Ronan had been disappointed in himself for losing, but it only made the flames of his ambition burn brighter and fiercer.

The moment of reflection ended and he realised he’d been compressing himself. The mass that made him up here floated apart, the tension he’d felt slipping away as he approached the obelisk. The towering obsidian megalith seemed to be the source of many of his abilities, and even now it was supposed to be ‘awakening,’ whatever that entailed. Hopefully more powers for him.

He made contact with the obelisk, bringing up the chronos enhancements menu. He had a tough decision ahead of him, and while he had received a windfall of merits, they were a finite resource that was hard to obtain. Spending them carelessly would only make his future iterations more of a struggle.

Either he spent all 9 of them right now and greatly enhanced his capability to grow, hoping that would allow him to push on and earn far more merits than he otherwise could, or he saved up to earn 4 more merits and get himself a second sub-class.

Both options were valid routes to getting more powerful. A second sub-class felt like it would be really powerful. Sorcerer was only an uncommon class, but already it was proving to be a great assistance in his quest for strength. From the get-go he had class skills allowing him to practice magic, as well as increased stat gain at levels 0 through 10.

This would be a tough choice to make.
