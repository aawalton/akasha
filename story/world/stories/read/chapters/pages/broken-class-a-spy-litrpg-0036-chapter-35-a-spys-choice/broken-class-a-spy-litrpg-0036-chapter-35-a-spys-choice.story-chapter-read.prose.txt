A notification blazed across Arthur's vision, so urgent it bypassed the 'mute' setting he kept perpetually active. It wasn't a solid block obstructing his view, but a translucent overlay that pulsed with a simple message: The Great Hunt was over. He was the victor. And a choice awaited. The sleeping Howler he’d been moving toward faded from his focus. This was more important. He abandoned the pursuit and searched for a safe place, eventually settling in the hollow of a great tree.

The weight of the choice ahead settled on him. This wasn't just loot; it was a strategic decision that could define his future here, and he couldn't afford a mistake. For the first time, a rare flicker of uncertainty crossed his mind. Drogan was an arrogant meathead, but he was an ancient arrogant meathead. He might know something about these System rewards.

"Drogan," he projected mentally, "I've won the Hunt. There's a selection of rewards. I could use your counsel."

A booming, self-satisfied laugh echoed in his skull. "FINALLY! You seek the wisdom of your master! Show me these trinkets! I shall guide your feeble hand to true power!"

Arthur sighed. Instant regret.

With a mental command, he brought the notification to the forefront. The overlay solidified into a holographic screen.

[GREAT HUNT VICTOR'S REWARD]

The screen filled with a collage of beautifully rendered images. Arthur focused on the first, a gleaming longsword wreathed in a crimson aura.

"YES!" Drogan roared. "That one! A blade that sings with blood! A proper weapon for a warrior! Choose it, boy, and we shall carve a path of glory through this world!"

Arthur willed for the details, ignoring the phantom cheerleader in his head.

A snowball of death, perfect for someone like Drogan. But it was also a declaration of war, announcing its presence with every glowing swing. It thrived on open conflict and offered no advantage in stealth. Useless for me, he thought, and swiped.

"WHAT ARE YOU DOING?!" Drogan bellowed. "THAT WAS THE ONE!"

The image shifted to a suit of dark plate armor.

"Ah, well," Drogan conceded, his tone slightly mollified. "A warrior needs his shell. This is also an acceptable choice. A man who cannot take a hit is no man at all."

Safety. A get-out-of-jail-free card for a mistake he never planned to make. But it was a reactive defense, a single-use safety net. His training was built on avoiding the fatal blow, not surviving it. Relying on the Aegis would breed complacency. He swiped again.

"Are you mad?!" Drogan shouted. "You'd forsake both sword and shield? What's next, a pointy hat?"

A sleek, black dagger appeared.

Drogan groaned. "A toothpick. A glorified letter opener. Are you planning to assassinate field mice? Pathetic."

It was a tool for espionage, perfect for neutralizing a guard or creating an escape window. But its venom was limited, and only effective on E-grade foes. It was a good option, but it felt... limiting.

The carousel turned again, revealing a pair of supple leather boots.

"Boots," Drogan said flatly. "You're considering footwear over a weapon that gorges on life force. My legacy is a comedy."

The tactical applications were obvious. He could scale the walls of Copperpine at night, bypassing the gates entirely. The boots were a top contender, a near-perfect tool for infiltration. Still, it would be foolish to choose without seeing everything. He filed them away as the leading candidate and moved on.

Next, an ornate staff.

"Oh, for the love of... it is a pointy hat," Drogan despaired.

Arthur focused on the description.

The staff was a conduit for projecting power outward. Since he couldn't yet cast anything externally, its primary function was useless. The passive recovery was a nice bonus, but it couldn't compare to consuming monster cores. He swiped one last time.

The final item rotated into view. It wasn't a simple oval of porcelain. It was a thing of ancient, intimidating beauty. Forged from a dark, stone-like material, its surface was a labyrinth of swirling, arcane carvings.

Arthur focused on it, intrigued.

Arthur froze. His breath caught. The other items were tools for fighting, for surviving. This... this was different. This was a key. A skeleton key that could unlock any door, any city, any identity. It was a spy’s wildest dream made manifest.

A slow, dangerous smile spread across his lips. The sword was power. The armor was safety. But the mask? The mask was freedom.

"Don't you dare," Drogan whispered, his voice a low, dangerous growl. "Don't you dare choose that... that piece of costume jewelry."

Arthur's finger reached out and tapped the image of the mask.

[Selection Confirmed: Mask of Infinite Faces] [Thank you for your participation. The Great Hunt is now officially over.]

The screen dissolved. The air grew heavy, and motes of light swirled, coalescing and darkening until a dark, stone mask floated down into his hands.

"YOU FOOL!" Drogan's voice boomed in his mind, dripping with the raw, undiluted fury of a spurned B-Grade. "YOU CHOSE A TRINKET OVER A TRUE WEAPON? THE AEGIS COULD HAVE SAVED YOUR LIFE! THE CRIMSON OATH WOULD HAVE SUNG IN YOUR HANDS! WHAT IS THAT?!"

Arthur truly regretted asking the remnant for advice, but he dismissed the rage in Drogan's voice. His focus entirely consumed by the sheer, intoxicating possibility now resting in his hands.
