The library still had that wonderful smell of old books that Ashton remembered from when he went here. He couldn't quite appreciate it back then, but now that he was experiencing it through his new lens, it was really quite wonderful. He almost felt nostalgic for his time here, though it wasn't like he actually spent a lot of time in the library when he studied here.

He did remember being here a few times when his parents visited the university, sporadically giving lectures here and there even after they technically retired. They would usually stop by all over the place, and often just relaxed here in the library for a while. Especially in the summers. For some reason, this place was always far colder compared to the any other building at this university. It was just that kind of massive space that was hard to heat up all the way.

Ashton also remembered that there was one person that particularly liked to meet with his parents here, a certain old man that Ashton had only spoken to directly a handful of times after his parents passed.

That same man was now sitting at the table in front of Ashton. He was just middle-aged; when Ashton started studying here, he had just started as the Vice-Chancellor. Back when Ashton met him for the first time, the Vice-Chancellor was just a relatively new Professor who was trying to make a name for himself in the classical literature department.

"So it really was you," the Vice-Chancellor stood up and adjusted the glasses on his face. "I'm Quincy Aberdeen. It's nice to finally meet you. You may not know, but I was quite friendly with your parents."

The man stretched out his hand forward. If Ashton still had eyes, he would be rolling them right now. But instead, he just shook Quincy's hand.

"I know. We've actually met a few times before now," Ashton replied with a slight smile. "I studied here up until a few years ago. Plus, we met at my parents' funerals."

Quincy stared at Ashton for a few moments, a little dazed as though he wasn't prepared for this turn of events. Clearly, he had absolutely no recollection of having met Ashton.

"Right, now I recall," Quincy lied, but Ashton didn't really care enough to call him out on it. He wasn't here for pleasantries anyway.

"Mm-hmm. Anyway, I've brought a few things from Calibur. Just tell me where to put them, and—"

Quincy's eyes widened. "Yes! Right, there were a few things that we were yet owed. I'm guessing you're bringing some of the notes today? Just go ahead and give them to me now, please."

Ashton shook his head. "I'm bringing all the things you were 'owed' I guess." He didn't like the way that Quincy was talking, but he figured that it didn't really matter.

He channelled the Backpacker and quickly selected all of the items he got from Sam to bring here. It was mostly foodstuffs or items harvested from different monsters. The spirit quickly appeared and started pulling everything out of their bag, simply putting it down on the ground of the library. Quincy watched the entire process in confusion.

He only started speaking at the end, when the Backpacker put the paper notes down on the table in front of the Vice-Chancellor.

"I... I had heard that you had access to certain 'special' abilities, but this is very unexpected," Quincy explained, placing a hand over his mouth. Ashton noticed that Millie flinched lightly right then. "Say, that bag, you don't coincidentally have any intention of selling it to me, do you?"

Ashton scoffed. "Doesn't work that way. The bag belongs to the Backpacker, and the Backpacker can literally only exist in this world because of me."

Still curious, Quincy looked at the Backpacker more closely. "Oh? How exactly does that work?"

"I create a bridge between myself and another realm that allows my summons to step over and exist within this material plane for a while, using an artificially created body. It takes energy for that body to exist here and for the summon to exert its power, which is created through specialised offerings to each individual summon I have contracted," Ashton said absent-mindedly with a slight yawn. He didn't mind explaining this stuff; it wasn't like Quincy could replicate it anyway.

"I see... And how exactly do you form that bridge?"

"Magic."

"Magic?"

"Yes, magic."

Quincy was seemingly getting a bit frustrated. Seriously? Even with all of this, he still didn't believe in magic?

"Fucking hell," Ashton muttered under his breath, clicking his finger. Some water flowed out from the bottle in his pocket, forming the Undine's body. Ashton took control of some more water and quickly started moving it around Quincy's head. It distracted him for a few moments, and Ashton figured that it was enough to at least satisfy him enough so that they could get to the main business. But the entire time, Quincy kept catching glances at the Undine, and Millie grabbed Ashton's cloak again, clutching it tightly.

"Real magic..? I figure it makes sense for it to actually exist now. How wonderful," Quincy said, and quickly turned to Ashton. "Young man, do you not think that this power should be explored further? That we should study what we can do with such magic?"

This time, he didn't even need Millie's reaction to feel creeped out. The tone of Quincy's voice was one that he had heard many times before. It was greed for something that was beyond them. A desperate grasping at a power that wasn't meant for them, but that they would try to do anything to acquire anyway.

"Sure. But you can do that without me," Ashton sighed. "Sooner or later, someone here will figure out magic, and then you can study it all you want. Don't involve me in that shit."

Quincy looked at Ashton confused, but then tried to just laugh it off. "Not quite the academic, are you? Sorry, I thought you'd be more like your parents."

"Excuse me?"

"Oh, I'm sorry, I just thought you'd be more academically inclined, considering your parents' intellectual prowess."

Ashton kept up his smile, scoffing lightly. Was this guy trying to subtly call him stupid? People like this always tried to make themselves feel like their other's superior any way they could. It was almost cute.

"If you say so," Ashton responded. "Anyway, I've got a few more things to talk about. First, about the zone with the Ancient Constructs."

"Ah! Yes, of course, I also wanted to speak about that," Quincy replied, nodding. He glanced up at the crown on Ashton's head. "That is the item that you stole from the church, right?"

"The item that I 'stole'? Sorry, but this is mine," Ashton pointed out, shaking his head.

But Quincy didn't quite let up. "I did hear that you're quite possessive of it, but you have to understand; we laid official claim to that area days ago in the community... so technically, it is theft."

"Technically? You're talking about technicalities here?" Ashton laughed loudly, shaking his head as he stepped closer to Quincy. "I don't give a shit about any of that. You don't get to claim any area as your own beside maybe this campus. We're all fighting to survive together, so why are you trying to do something like that?"

Quincy sighed, shaking his head. "But please, Ashton, I'm just doing my due diligence. We're all just trying to survive all of this, aren't we? It's one thing to deal with those mindless monsters that will chase you at the cost of their own lives, but those 'Ancient Constructs'? They were built with a purpose. They protect their area, and then don't continue chasing. They aren't here to kill, they're here to protect."

Ashton silently continued to listen to Quincy speak. This guy was annoying, but this part of what he was saying wasn't nonsense, at least. And seeing that Ashton was quiet, Quincy felt much more empowered to continue on.

"Now, what if we would be able to take control of these Constructs? What if we could turn them against the monsters, and make them protect us instead? And I believe that this crown you're wearing is the key to that. Don't you understand? The constructs have been protecting it since they appeared. And I simply believe that we here, as academics, are the only ones qualified to really study that crown and figure out the connection between it and the constructs," Quincy explained, quickly walking back over to the table. He picked up a notebook and held it toward Ashton. Of course, he couldn't see it, but Quincy apparently didn't understand, or believe, that Ashton was actually blind.

"Here, this is our analysis of the constructs thus far. We believe that these constructs have a religious purpose, which is why they took over a church. And that crown you're wearing is most likely some religious effigy; some important artefact that they're bound to protect. Don't you think that if we can harness that deep piety ingrained in their very being, we can turn this around?"

You know, construction, protection, even housework apparently. The reason they took over the church doesn't have anything to do with them, but with us," Ashton replied, taking out his looking glass to try and take a look at Quincy's notes. "I remember that church being used for tons of weddings. The people that made the constructs may have had similar spaces, but that's irrelevant, because this crown is from after their time. It was tradition in a certain tribe to make these for their spouse-to-be, and so when the Ancient Constructs were given the task to deliver this crown here, they chose to place it into a church where loads of weddings are held."

"On the fact that I'm said spouse this crown was made for." The response was blunt, and he knew that this would lead to more questions, but Ashton didn't really care. He sighed as he took in the notes. "Yeah, basically all of this is wrong."

"... A handful."

But they're running on magic, so it would need to be done by someone that can control their mana precisely enough, and a person like that might be hard to find for you guys. Also, I don't think that a normal tamer could control an Ancient Construct, but it might be worth a try?" Ashton pointed out. "There are some really small and relatively weak units, you can probably trap one and use it to test things out on it."
