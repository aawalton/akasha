The sound of fighting was nearly deafening. Across Sanctuary, blood soaked the ground. Smoke danced between ruined buildings. The clash of metal and screech of magical explosions were never-ending. As more Radochs landed, the defenders became more entrenched and hard-pressed.

Sally slipped between her zombies as the battle raged on. Now on solid ground, she felt a lot more comfortable pushing back against the bugs. Or at least holding her ground.

The site of the secret she had to defend was a temple near one of Sanctuary's great lakes. Thanks to the deep water, the Radochs had limited avenues of attack. Of course, it had turned into a messy brawl either way. Her zombies were a little weaker than the generic troops, but she was able to keep up numbers enough to win the war of attrition if given enough time.

That factor was… certainly less of a guarantee.

She stood at the temple doorway atop the corpse of an elite roach. Her dwarven retinue formed two groups, half each on the stairs to her left and right. The resulting channel straight in front of her was a dense wall of zombies that spilled out onto the battlefield like foul water. While much shorter than the Radochs, the skeleton’s armor and proficient weaponry kept the bugs at bay, providing enough resistance to funnel the pressing force toward the horde.

Sally worked on regulating her energy expenditure. From her minor perch, she could fling Mortis Bombs or use elemental scrolls to weaken the bugs so her pals could drag them down. Occasionally she’d weave in some heals or buffs, or push back against the seemingly endless assault with one of her new abilities.

Eventually, the piling roach bodies made it difficult to maintain their position. The dwarves fell back gradually, close to the temple entrance. New zombies struggled to find purchase on the ground full of the dead.

“If only we could break their anti-zombification trait,” she grumbled. A floating skull of green flames bloomed above the open tome floating beside her.

Before she could throw it out into the crowd, something caught her eye.

Against the thrashing fray, arcane-heated air, and general battle malaise, it was difficult to make out at first. A dark shape was approaching from the rear of the Radoch forces. Whatever it was, it was large.

“Steel yourselves,” she commanded. “Siege unit incoming.”

As her Mortis Bomb hit the bugs ahead, the dwarves using ranged weapons swapped to shields and spears to assist the melee-wielding skeletons in front.

The approaching monster was giant, but had some striking similarities to the rank-and-file roaches it was barreling through. It was like the difference between a human and an ogre. Heavy chitin plating covered its wide shoulders as it ran using its lower set of arms. The upper limbs were more like blunt crab claws. Riding atop this monstrosity was a much smaller Radoch.

He was glowing red and looked to be charging up some kind of ability as he guided the larger steed with reins.

Sally pulled a face and raised her crossbow. “This better not be another bug who can nullify my powers.”

More than that, she hoped it wouldn’t be a fight that pushed her to use her Aspect form. Reports had come in that tens of thousands more Radoch were landing around Sanctuary. The dwindling defenders were outnumbered, and that was compounding the more time passed. She needed to keep as much fighting energy in reserve as possible.

The bolt left her weapon and arced over the throng of fighters too distracted by their struggle to move out of the way as the juggernaut plowed toward them. With a puff of smoke, the projectile exploded against the thick exoskeleton of the massive roach, doing no damage to it.

Now she could see that the one on top was forming a spear of red energy, readying it for when the steed had crossed the short remaining distance between them.

Weapon stowed, she held her hand out toward the looming pair.

The tank-like bug trampled Radoch and zombies alike before rearing up for a last burst of speed to meet Sally.

A sharp-toothed grin spread across her face.

Cast earlier in the battle, Summon Specter wrapped its ghostly form around the leg of the great beast. Unable to resist, it stumbled briefly before becoming stunned for five seconds.

The ground behind it erupted, sending corpses raining through the air as Summon Zombie Giant brought Big Joe out. The hulking undead immediately clasped his hands together and lifted them high into the air, ready to crush down on the Radoch.

Struggling to rouse his ride, the powerful bug turned to face his new aggressor, red lance held high above his head. The air buzzed as Big Joe struck a force-field surrounding the Radoch, which slowed the descent of his hands. As the shielding broke at the same time as the lance struck the zombies, an explosion flashed between the pair, blinding those still battling around them.

Sally appeared within the smoke, slashing through the neck of the smaller Radoch before driving her dagger down into the head of the large one. White threads moved to heal Big Joe’s hands as the giant looked solemn at the damage he had sustained.

“Almost a challenge.” Sally wobbled as the chitinous platform sank down to the ground. “Not that I’m about to give them tips, but..” Her eyes went over the battlefield. From this position, she could see much further and…

The sea of roaches was unending. She thought she had been halfway through a few hundred of them. There were easily two thousand, probably more past her line of sight.

Were they migrating toward her, or was the secret in the temple really this important?

Either way, she was going to need a lot more zombies.
