Eik didn’t bother to interfere with the touch of the Ak’ki as it reported his level rewards. He didn’t obscure it from the Lord’s eyes either.

Considering the fact that the Lord of the Moon was one of the legendary Primal Entities—beings that, eons ago, were so unimaginably powerful that nature itself felt it necessary to step in and establish a system through which other lifeforms could gain a fighting chance—who had only just returned to the world after an untold number of years, Eik doubted that he would even really understand what he was looking at if he saw it.

But most importantly, Eik needed to see it himself.

Living Manifestation getting close to level 300 was great and would probably give him something nice, but the skill he was really looking forward to was Monarch’s Will hitting its capstone level. The skill seemed to govern utility and general expansion of Profound Toxin and it had been an indispensable part of his rise to power since the moment he first unlocked it.

[Acquired Monarch’s Will — Lv. 399]

Just one more.

Why were they coming so slowly? It was excruciating. It had never been quite as clear as with most of his other skills exactly what triggered level gains for Monarch’s Will.

Obviously, it would be to use the skill in high risk situations, but as an auxiliary skill that mostly boosted or influenced Profound Toxin, its use was in Profound Toxin. One of the most direct uses was in Tyrant’s Pressure, which allowed the properties of Profound Toxin to flow into and mix with Eik’s aura and manifest the titanic avatar of the Monarch.

But with the strength he possessed now, that ability was constantly active during a fight like this, and while the avatar of the Monarch might not be physically manifest, that was simply a choice on Eik’s part.

Then he would just have to wait a little longer.

Azure crystal blade buried deeply in his neck, the Lord’s arms were firmly around Eik in a bear hug.

Swarming parasites ate away at Eik insides through the terrible wound that refused to close, sustaining the fiend’s life, while Profound Toxin circulated through the Lord’s system in ever-increasing quantities, bringing him closer to a death that even his parasites couldn’t prevent with every moment.

Reaching for Extinction Canvas for a third time, Eik constructed a prison no more than four meters in diameter. While the Extinction Zone beneath whirred like an engine, Apocalypse Canvas right above them pelted the Lord with thousands of crystals.

“Do you perhaps think this is enough to defeat me?” the Lord wondered out loud. Eik tried to break free of his grasp, but anything he did only caused the monster to squeeze harder. Whether it was because of the parasites invading his flesh or due to some other ability the Lord hadn’t revealed, Eik couldn’t say, but something was preventing Toxic Liquefaction from activating. “You are nothing but a pebble facing a mountain. You face a mountain named Xuqubpalanth—”

About a fourth of the way through the name, which was way too long, by the way, the effects of the translation seemed to fail, leaving Eik’s ears and brain incapable of comprehending the sounds uttered.

Fine. Big Fat Pile of Garbage suited him better anyway.

And as haughty and vicious as the Lord of the Moon’s words were, the tremble in his voice and the weakness in his limbs were a testament to the toll Profound Toxin had already taken on him. The only reason why Eik couldn’t break out of his feeble embrace was because he was even more injured himself.

Four more rods pierced Eik. Two through the chest, one through the hip, and one through his thigh.

“Die,” the ancient Worldbreaker snarled.

Before he could speak, blood poured out of Eik’s mouth, splattering across the Lord’s back. He felt his consciousness slipping as the drain on his energy intensified. It was a struggle just to get two words out. “Fuck… you…”

The Lord snorted derisively. “A futile waste of bre—”

In the next instant, the Lord felt the outermost layer of his black sphere crack under an unknown but massive pressure that seemed to rupture it inwardly from all sides. Briefly, he sent energy surging into the construct of darkness, but he acted too late, and occupied as he was with the human, the reinforcement just didn’t cut it.

The moment it shattered, he hardly had time to realize it before the second sphere was struck by the same force. Due to its smaller size, this second one was denser and stronger, and he had better time to bolster it.

Literally pressed against the fiend’s body, Eik noticed the shift of energy immediately and went all in on the opening created by that brief moment of distraction. Sprouting a razor-sharp, slender spike from each of his palms, he slammed his hands flat into the sides of the Lord’s head.

The discombobulating maneuver struck the fiend just below the crescent protrusion, spikes piercing deeply as his skull was sandwiched.

Eik didn’t hesitate to Accelerate both spikes inside the fiend’s head, dealing massive damage and forcing the Worldbreaker to waste precious energy on regeneration.

And then he did it again. Keeping his hands in place, he grew new spikes into the wounds already opened by the first pair. It was devastating.

For just a moment, the Lord of the Moon seemed to lose consciousness as his grip on Eik’s body slackened and he began to slump over. But Eik wasn’t doing so well either, with parasites swarming his wounds faster than he could eliminate them.

In that moment of unconsciousness, the second barrier of darkness shattered and the massive force outside finally met the last and densest of them. Cracks appeared immediately.

But the Lord had already regained his senses, his extreme regenerative ability disallowing loss of consciousness, even after an otherwise fatal head injury. Seeing even the third sphere about to shatter, the Lord sought to evacuate rather than reinforce the structure, but Eik was ready for him.

He drove slender rods of crystal through the fiend’s body and attached each side to the barrier wall of Extinction Canvas. It was only due to the continuous, full-body weakening by Profound Toxin that Eik could pierce the Worldbreaker’s flesh so easily at all. It was a testament to the effects of his toxin, even if the parasites were constantly working against it.

Eik smirked in the monster’s face. “Sorry, but I can’t let you leave right now, mate. I invited a friend over for a bite to eat, you see. Wouldn’t want to be rude.”

Reinforcing his hands with pitch black energy, the Lord destroyed the rods as if they were made of glass. With the same movements, he sent devastating attacks after Eik as well.

Deep wounds bit into the human’s body despite his attempts to dodge and block, but at this range, they were simply too powerful to be dealt with properly. Two wounds managed to pierce him cleanly through the torso, coming out of his back, while another carved up his side brutally, severing flesh, skin, and tendon from his hip and in to his bellybutton.

Eik gasped a breathless curse at the pain.

He had already reinforced the walls of the innermost Extinction Canvas with several meters of hyper durable crystal, so even the Lord’s direct hits could not win against the speed of reformation of the azure structure considering the state he was in.

Eik clenched his teeth. Just a little more.

As a crystalline hand axe snapped into existence in his hand, Eik tensed his muscles to the point of causing the air inside the small prison to shriek. “Just give up and let it happen, you fucking…” he snarled, bringing the axe up from hip height in a vicious horizontal swing. “asshole!”

What caused the Lord of the Moon to react in earnest was the color of the weapon. Sickly, swirling purple.

He caught it with a hand enveloped in a sinister flow and successfully absorbed the strike, but with the many small, open wound all over his armored body, he could not prevent the purple liquid from invading his body as the axe melted, even with his parasitic swarm.

The Lord resented the fact that this battle between Worldbreakers had devolved into little more than a slogging match. It was crude and disgraceful.

How in the world had this human gained so much power in such a short time? It made little sense.

This couldn’t be allowed to continue. Although he had wanted to avoid it, it would seem that more… precarious measures had to be taken. “You are showing more promise than I had expec—”

“Is it impossible for you to struggle with your mouth closed?” Eik barked, cutting off the lord.

In that same moment, the final, inner sphere of blackness shattered and Spaghetti Jones massive maw suddenly appeared, already surrounding them on all sides.

The Lord jerked as he reacted. Why hadn’t he been able to perceive that enormous creature so near? His senses should have been lighting up like a wildfire.

And more importantly, how the hell was it moving around like this? He was absolutely certain that he had sliced it in half. Had it really regenerated that swiftly? He had felt its vital signs fade.

Eik smirked at the expression on the fiend’s face. Even with his alien features, he was too easy to read. “Just so you know, he will come back endlessly. This world will reform him even if you were to reduce him to motes.”

Already swallowed up, the Lord of the Moon didn’t hear much after the first few words.

Spaghetti Jones dove deeply into the earth. As deeply as he could manage. It came as no surprise when the Lord destroyed Spaghetti Jones’ body once more—this time from the inside. He was too powerful to be contained, but even in the short amount of time he had been confined, more toxin had been loaded into his system.

His death was drawing nearer.

But Eik’s own condition was no better. He had killed off the parasites consuming his energy, but he couldn’t take much more.

Hovering in the air above Toxin, he watched the hole the serpent had dug, waiting for the Lord to reemerge, more than ten thousand crystal shards floating around him, Apocalypse Canvas in the sky spitting out more endlessly. Native Profound Toxic beasts gathered around, merging with his summons as they prepared to meet the enemy.

When a subterranean boom announced the serpent’s death, Eik waved a hand and caused the beast to melt into liquid. The Lord would have to wade through that as he climbed out.

Less than a second later, Eik felt an energy shift from below, his instincts suddenly screaming at him to flee. As he watched, thousands of titanic pillars of pure darkness tore through the earth and pierced the heavens above.

The sinister energy pouring out of them set off warning bells in Eik’s head. Reflexively, Omnicide and Backflow resurged intensely as more grade 7 potion was pumped through his system.

That parasitic carapace had not been his final form.

Eik couldn’t predict what kind of being would come back out, but he knew it would be bad news.

Something tickled his forearm.
