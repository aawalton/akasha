Healing bones took more mana than Luke wanted to spend, but this opponent fought a lot like Hiroki. Weavestep took care of that. Once in range, threads of mana were about to snake their way into the Singer's mana channels, but found that he had none. In fact, this one wasn't Integrated at all. Unfortunately, that meant the staff was just a mundane one, but he still pocketed it. A couple of Integrated Singers stood in their way near the door at the opposite side of the large hall from where the gunmen entered. While the vehicles turning and coming their way weren’t the same as the ones back on Earth, there were similarities one couldn't ignore.

Luke pointed. "Tanks!"

Luke: "How do we get in?"

Luke slapped the palm of his hand against the cold, windowless wall. "Wish I could do that."

"You can teleport," Ray said as he searched through a bunch of papers on a table.

"A map. Orders. Anything, really."

Everything was in the Singer language, meaning Luke couldn't understand a thing. Instead, he looked around the spacious but bare room. "Isn't it kind of strange?"

"The tables, the chairs. That cot over there. There's even the smell of coffee in the air. Or something like it. It's all just so... human."

Walking over there, Luke understood what Ray wanted him to see. While it held similarities to what you would expect from a human kitchen, with appliances, running water, and the like, all of them held small differences you wouldn't see on Earth, at least not things Luke had seen. The faucet had four knobs to turn on the water. A small, cracket monster core sat in a contraption next to the stove, and he guessed it gave the whole thing power, since he didn't see a single electrical outlet anywhere. Similar, but different.

"Here," Ray said, pulling out a sheet of paper. Or maybe it wasn't paper, but it sure looked like it.

Luke walked over and peered down at it. "You can read that?"
