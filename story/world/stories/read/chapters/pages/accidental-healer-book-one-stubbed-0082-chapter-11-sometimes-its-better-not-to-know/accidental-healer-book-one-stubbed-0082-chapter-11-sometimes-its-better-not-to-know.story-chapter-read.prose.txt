Foundation class? Was that what the rest of the universe called your first class?

It was Stacy again.

She was muttering something, but I couldn't quite make it out.

She’s crying?

“Someone...help me.”

“Zortana, the fusion needs a great amount of fuel to remain stable. You’ll need to consume the flesh of the ones you kill, or it will dissolve.”

“Although this woman has asked for your aid, she may change her mind. It is critical that this does not happen. For the descent to take full effect, she must submit entirely to your control.”

“Hello?”

Stacy looked down at her hand like she didn’t even recognize it.

With the help of Zortana she was able to survive the chaos spawn.

I was still new to the universe and didn’t know the rules yet, but that seemed like a much better way than whatever this was.

They struggled to find dungeons but completed them when they could. Every time they killed, they ate.

Regret.

The red-haired giant. Only this time, he wasn’t a giant. He was floating in the air beside me, hand clasped behind his back in a gold embroidered green tunic.

Is this still part of the memory? When did this man get here, and how was he on earth?
