Without hesitation, Rusty tapped his metallic finger against the glowing option for ‘Vengeance Continues II’. The summoning circle reacted quickly, and he stepped onto it after selecting all the weapons and items he was taking with him. Now that he had multiple loadouts and his storage space had increased, he was feeling much better about being a summon. While he still could not take everything with him, he had access to more of his true capabilities.

“I wonder how Teral is doing… He should have been fine with that ‘Enchanted Steel Heavy Warrior’ upgrade I gave him.”

Rusty was excited to see one of his more interesting summoners. The young elf was in an interesting world, one that was not nearly as guarded as the one Gwen was in. There was no one like a teacher there to save them, so more things depended on him. He always appreciated a challenge.

“Should I use the Goliath form first? If I’m getting summoned, then he might be in trouble again.”

Usually, when he was summoned, he was up against at least one opponent, but that was not always the case. Sometimes, after appearing, he had time to strategize and prepare before the main scenario played out.

“I wonder what it will be… Here I go!”

With that said, his body vanished from the summoning chamber, and soon everything went white. His vision returned shortly after, revealing a dimly lit stone hallway. The air smelled strongly of sulfur and burning oil, but there were also plenty of other sounds coming from nearby.

‘The sounds of battle?’

What he was hearing reminded him of the previous siege of that baron’s castle, as well as the time the orcs had attacked. It quickly became clear that he had been summoned in the middle of a large-scale battle. When he turned around, he saw him. His old summoner, Teral.

The Teral standing before him looked a bit older. His hair had been cut short, and he even had a few scars on his hands and one on his cheek. It was clear that some time had passed since their last meeting, perhaps even half a year, just like it had with Gwen.

However, this was not the time to comment on his summoner’s more manly appearance. Before Rusty could say anything, a spear tip came at him from behind.

*Clink*

The spear bounced off his back. His current body had reached a stage where it could no longer be damaged by D-rank weapons. The attack had come from a man wearing the usual guardsman uniform. He wore good quality half plate armor and carried a well-maintained spear. This was not just some random slave trader like before. This was something bigger.

“Rusty… It’s really you!”

Teral gasped in surprise as Rusty materialized before him. His summoners never knew when he would appear, so his surprise was understandable. It seemed they were in some kind of dungeon, one where the cells were filled with elves who looked like they were on their deathbeds.

“Let’s talk later. Who do you want me to defeat?”

Rusty asked while gripping his halberd with both hands. He swung it into the spearman who had just attacked him, sending the soldier flying into the damp stone wall with a loud crunch. The man collapsed, his steel chestplate badly crushed by the blow.

Teral let out a breath he seemed to have been holding for minutes, though his expression quickly hardened once more. He gripped something that looked like a mage staff, his fighting stance far more polished and confident than it had been during their previous battles together.

The area was filled with other living armors, but with Rusty now here, a large amount of mana had to be reserved to maintain his form. This left Teral with only enough mana to support the lesser armors rather than the upgraded ones.

“The warden is on the upper floor.”

Teral said urgently, pointing toward a spiraling staircase at the end of the corridor.

“We managed to breach the lower dungeon, but they sealed the exit above. If we do not clear a path soon, the reinforcements will flood down and slaughter everyone in these cells.”

‘A warden?’

He knew what the word meant, and now that he looked around, he noticed the rows of cages surrounding them. This was a prison, and all of the prisoners were elves. Most of them were in terrible condition.

Rusty glanced across the rows of rusted iron bars. Emaciated figures huddled in the shadows, their sunken eyes reflecting a faint glimmer of hope at the sight of him, or perhaps at Teral’s arrival. His summoner had grown significantly since their last meeting, carrying himself more like a proper leader than the frightened boy Rusty had rescued after the tragic events in his village.

“Warden? Sounds a bit stronger. Sounds like someone I should handle!”

There was not much time to ask what was happening, but the situation was fairly obvious. The last time he had left this summoning world, the elves had conquered the castle of a human baron. That victory had probably given the elven people the motivation they needed to fight back and slowly reclaim the territory they had lost.

Rusty did not waste another second. The better his performance, the better the rewards. He now also understood how the summoning system worked, and maintaining a good relationship with his summoners was key. There was always a chance to gain more points. Any of the prisoners here could also become new allies in the future.

More importantly, he wanted to see what this world had in store for him. He tightened his grip around his long weapon and charged forward in the direction Teral had pointed.

“Lethira, I’ll leave the prisoners to you. Now that Rusty is here…”

“You don’t need to say anything more. I’ll get them out!”

The huntress from the previous summoning mission was here as well. She was already working on the prison doors, inserting keys she had seemingly taken from one of the guards.

Rusty charged down the stone spiral staircase, his metallic boots shaking the old, decrepit floor tiles with every step. Two human guards stationed above raised heavy wooden shields to block the narrow passage, but against Rusty, their formation was useless.

He did not even bother to swing his weapon. Instead, he leaned into his stride and slammed his shoulder into the lead guard, crushing the wooden shield into splinters. The force sent both men tumbling backward down the steps, where they crashed into the soldiers behind them as they tried to climb the staircase.

“We’re going down... Is this a dungeon with floors?”

He wondered about it, as there were no windows here for him to peek through, but there was no opportunity to ask. Teral, his summoner, was far more focused on the current battle than on small talk.

A few of the lesser living armors under his control continued clearing out the guards that Rusty had bulldozed through. Soon, after racing through a rather large corridor, they arrived before an enormous door. It was not the sort of door Rusty expected to lead to someone called a warden.

“Are you sure this is the right place? Shouldn’t a warden be in some kind of office?”

“Um... There isn’t enough time. We need to get rid of the Warden, otherwise we won’t be able to open the main door. The opening mechanism should be in there.”

The young man explained as his constructs pushed the large door open. Rusty had a premonition about what was waiting on the other side, so he made sure to position himself between the entrance and Teral. He was now the main Vanguard, and it was his duty to protect him.

“Hah! You walked right into my trap, you filthy elf!”

The moment they stepped into the chamber, a voice resounded from somewhere within. Rusty immediately searched for its source, but there was no person to be seen. Only a disembodied voice echoed through the room, as if some form of magic were transmitting it from elsewhere.

It was an obvious trap, one Rusty had expected. Moments later, the doors slammed shut behind them, wedging one of the lesser living armor constructs between them and snapping it in half.

“Stupid elves! Did you really think you could storm my correctional facility without paying the price?”

The Warden’s voice echoed through strange boxes mounted in the corners of the chamber. Unfamiliar symbols covered their surfaces, glowing red and pulsating in strange harmony with the sound.

Rusty stared at them for a moment, but quickly looked away when something began rising from the ground.

“What’s that...?”

He asked, but Teral did not seem to hear him. Instead, the young man bit his lower lip, seemingly already expecting what was about to appear.

“They really were conducting those terrible experiments here...”

A platform slowly rose from the floor. At its center stood a large glass container filled with a semitransparent liquid. Something was floating inside it. Something large and unnatural.

“What is that thing...?”

Rusty had no idea what he was up against. The creature did not look human in the slightest, nor did it resemble any natural beast from this world.

Suspended within the bubbling container and submerged in thick amber fluid was a twisted mass of mismatched limbs and rootlike appendages. However, when Rusty activated his mana sense, he noticed something familiar.

The creature's mana felt similar to that of the elves from the new village. It was clearly radiating plant-based mana, much like the Wood Elves he had interacted with before.

Teral staggered back, his face turning pale as he recognized the tragic origin of the creature. The Warden had not merely imprisoned their kin. He had been harvesting their bodies and mana, fusing them together with wild beasts to create an obedient abomination.

A loud hiss pierced the air as the glass cylinder cracked from top to bottom. Amber liquid poured across the stone floor, steaming as it touched the cold ground. The creature spilled forward, landing on its twisted limbs with a wet, heavy thud. Its multiple eyes snapped open all at once.

It was quite the strange sight. Eyes were everywhere, covering its arms, legs, and all over its body. To make it even more disturbing, all the eyes seemed to be sobbing. A liquid resembling tears streamed from them, yet there were no mouths through which it could release a cry. It simply sobbed in silence while staring at both Rusty and Teral. Then, without warning, it attacked.

“Leave this to me, Teral!”

Rusty shouted and quickly activated his loadout, changing into his Goliath form. His newly improved body, made from the most superior, high-grade twilight steel, appeared before them. It was slightly larger than the old model, more pristine, and better balanced. Holding a massive tower shield and his heavy halberd, he braced himself as the silent horror lunged.

The abomination moved with erratic, frightening speed. Dozens of pale arms and whiplike vines lashed out simultaneously, slamming against the face of Rusty’s tower shield with thunderous force. Stone cracked beneath his metallic feet as he absorbed the impact, but the upgraded Goliath frame did not yield. Tears from the creature’s weeping eyes dripped onto his armor, sizzling as they struck. They were highly acidic, yet the refined twilight steel remained completely undamaged.

“Teral, stay behind me... or better yet, maybe find an exit and find where that warden is!”

He shouted as he exchanged a few blows with the massive beast. The other party had seen them enter the room, and there also seemed to have been a shadow moving somewhere above them. There was a chance that whoever had triggered the trap was still nearby.

Teral nodded without a second thought. Last time, Rusty had been treated like some kind of special spirit, and their conversations had been rather awkward. Now, however, things seemed to be different. That made fighting this thing a lot easier, especially since his summoner did not panic and simply started moving on his own. It also seemed that he had learned some new tricks.

Just like with Gwen, it was clear that his summoners were not limited to living armors. Teral also seemed to be capable of creating other magical spells. This time, after chanting for a moment, he produced a strange creature, an eyeball with small batlike wings.

This strange summon was not alone. Three more appeared, and they immediately began to float upward. There was something above them, a hidden observation balcony constructed from some kind of dark metal and glass, suspended near the vaulted ceiling. A figure wearing dark robes stood there, watching the unfolding carnage while looking at something that was displaying an image of Rusty’s battle.

“I see him, Rusty! I see the warden!”

“Okay, so you try to get him, and I’ll take care of this thing...”

Rusty responded while his shield continued to be pummeled by the creature. The beast was strong, but it had an obvious weak point that he could exploit.

A sword appeared in his arm, instantly igniting with bright red flames. He plunged it into the monster, which, like any plant-based creature, immediately began to burn.

‘That storage upgrade really helps.’

He had placed a few magical items into the storage area to supplement his arsenal. He was planning to upgrade it even further so he could carry more disposable weaponry later on, but for now, this flame sword was more than enough.

The crimson flames devoured the creature’s outer layer of twisted vines and rotting flesh. Once the magical flames activated, and despite having no mouth, a strange scream of agony escaped the monster, but Rusty did not stop. His halberd swung forward, chopping away at its limbs. Whenever one was severed, it flopped about like a snake before crumbling into nothing but dust.

‘Hm, this thing… isn’t it a bit similar to that monster…?’

He recalled the baron he had faced before. This creature was about the same level as that thing, but now that his body had advanced to the pinnacle of D-rank, it stood no chance against him.

With one final, sweeping cleave of his flaming blade, Rusty severed the central core of the weeping creature. For some reason, it had multiple cores hidden within its body, but only one of them generated a proper mana signal.

The crimson fire surged inward, burning away the remaining vine tendrils and dissolving the mournful eyes into bright cinders. The abomination collapsed into a silent pile of ash, completely vanquished before it could land another blow against his twilight steel frame.

Without pausing to admire the lingering flames, Rusty turned his attention toward the vaulted ceiling. There, he spotted his summoner, Teral, already climbing upward by using his constructs as a ladder.

‘He really did change…’

Rusty was surprised. The boy who could barely handle himself was no longer there. What stood before him was a young man who knew how to act and use his surroundings to his advantage.

‘Let’s see what this mission will be about, then.’

He thought to himself as he shifted into his wraith form. He quickly jumped onto the wall and began climbing upward. He was not sure how strong this Warden was, and while Teral had become more proactive, he still did not seem capable of properly assessing the situation just yet.
