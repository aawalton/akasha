"Everything," Guide said, as if tasting the word. He had appeared right in the middle of Luke's experiments with Source, the power inside void creatures, monster cores, the weave, and, he imagined, most of everything else.

"Source is inside the void beings. They get it from eating the weave, and we take it from them."

Guide looked thoughtful. "Source is an apt name for it, I'd say."

"What do you call it?" Luke asked.

"It has many names."

"Fair enough. What about our meta-hearts?" Luke considered his own question, then stepped past Guide to reach the now chewed-on kick-bike to insert a meta-heart into the slot for the monster core. The damn thing powered on.

"Your curiosity gives you credit," Guide said from behind Luke. "Most of us need to be told about these things."

Luke spun around and pointed at the meta-heart powered kick-bike. "Now hold on a moment. That works. Does this mean mana is the same as Source?"

"Was that meant as a philosophical question?" Guide asked.

The comment reminded Luke of the Integrated they had just lost, and he grimaced. "No. Let's stay practical."

"Mana is an aspect of source," Guide confirmed. "It is not the same, but it also is. Same as stamina and health. Source is the system, and it powers the system."

"And you brought it here, to Earth."

Guide nodded again. "That is correct."

"Where does it come from?"

"We don't know."

Luke scoffed. "How is that possible?"

"We have records detailing several Source generators, but not where they came from, what they looked like, or how they worked. There are no more than hints telling us what happened to them. This is beyond history, so far back it can't be counted in years."

Luke considered for a moment and drew a mental line through the various truths he had learned these last couple of days. "It's the void, isn't it? You said you have some information about where those generators went. The void took them, didn't it?"

Guide gave him another thoughtful look before answering. "We are now outside the line of what I can share with you. You can learn more later, if your interest holds."

Luke gave him an exasperated look. "You have to be able to give me something."

"I can tell you this," Guide began, choosing his words with careful consideration. "The Source generators are long gone. Their disappearance pre-dates the appearance of the void. Some, we know were destroyed. Others left this realm, and a few have been lost to time."

"If the void and the bastards in it eat the system to gather Source," Luke began, before stopping a moment to think. "I mean... If we don't lose much to them, and you said we siphon Source from them. Shouldn't that mean they're getting weaker? If they're not, that proves they have a generator. Doesn't it?"

"There are theories that point us in that direction," Guide agreed. "But your thinking that we don't sacrifice in our fight against them is in error. The void claims much."

"Yeah, sorry. What I meant to say was that we should look into sending people out to find the Source generator."

Guide gave a thin smile. "We have. Many."

"And?"

"None have returned."

Luke sighed. "Great..."

"A threat approaches," Guide said, getting a faraway look on his face.

"Teach me how to siphon Falzyxi's Source," Luke said.

"That is impossible."

Luke held up the core with Source taken from Smudge. "It's not."

"Improbable, then," Guide corrected himself. "The abomination is young and did not struggle. What is coming to Earth is ancient."

"But you're still not going to help."

Guide gave Luke a level look. "I am unable to. The Greater System is in turmoil with attacks on many new minor systems such as your own. Earth has an uncharacteristically high number of Integrated able to sense the underlying system and even traverse it, like you. Whatever aid The Greater System has available is in use elsewhere."

"So we're on our own," Luke said.

"You are."

"And you said we can't siphon Source from it."

"Not in any meaningful way."

Luke raised his hands in exasperation. "Then how are we supposed to beat it?"

Guide looked tired then, like he hadn't slept in a long, long time, and just remembered some past event, perhaps one just like this. "You need to join together. All of you. Together, you might stand a chance."

"Together?" Luke asked. "The others aren't ready. One guy appeared and died in an instant. The void claimed him and turned him into one of those things!"

Shock rocked Guide's expression. "What?"

"Like I said. The guy turned into void, pretty much, except for his meta-heart and mana channels. He transformed into one of the void creatures, sort of, the beginning of one, anyway."

"They're recruiting," Guide breathed.

"Huh?"

"They cannot force a person to become one of them, but they can offer. It's not something they have done during the last many ages."

"Does this mean we're beating them?" Luke asked. "And why would anyone join?"

"Power, for the most part. Not wanting to embrace what comes after this life is another. Some are fearful."

Luke blinked. "Hold on... What comes after?"

"I need to relay this information to The Council," Guide said.

"Wait!"

This time, Guide waited.

"What about fusion?"

"...What?" Guide asked.

"Back when this all started, you said Earth's integration started because we solved fusion. Does this have something to do with Source?"

It took Guide a moment to catch up, but then he laughed. The sound shocked Luke, who didn't think the entity in front of him was even able to express emotions to that extent. Seeing joy on that face was strange, to say the least.

"It was an inside joke among my kind," Guide said, gathering himself from the outburst. "Nothing you did on Earth triggered the integration. That was all me."

"Oh."

"And for the record, you didn't actually solve fusion."

Luke shook his head, unsure what to make of it. "I don't care. What about taking Source into ourselves?"

Guide grew very still. "You'll die."

"No matter what?"

"You are curious and inquisitive. Those are traits we need, but the high survivability you have reached with your version of the class you chose lends itself to a disregard for safety."

"...That's fair."

"But I need you to listen to me now. Do not attempt to take Source into yourself. There is only one instance where that does not result in instant death for anyone foolish enough to try, and even that instance could be argued whether you are still truly alive or not."

"What's that instance?"

"That is a discussion for later. Perhaps," Guide said. "Source is, in its core form, incompatible with life. It's used to create mana, health, even experience, but that is after careful manipulation and extensive dilution. Don't do it. Do not take Source into your meta-heart."

"So what you're saying is," Luke began with a grin on his face.

Guide was not amused. "Don't."

"I was just kidding. I won't."

"Do not do it, Luke Quinn."

"I said I won't!"

Guide gave him a long look, as if to confirm Luke was serious, then nodded and disappeared. A whisper sounded right by Luke's ear. "Don't."

"Say don't again, and I'll do it just to spite you!"

Perhaps it was just as well he hadn't told Guide he had been moments away from doing just that. The power of Source was enticing. Being able to harness it would let him go toe to toe with the scariest void beings and crush them with ease. That, however, wasn't in the cards, at least not for the time being. Luke would heed Guide's warning, at least for the moment. Talking with Earth's steward had revealed a lot, but sifting through and digesting the answers would take some time. Time he didn't have at the present moment. The void approached, and they still needed a way to beat the more developed void creatures, not to speak of Falzyxi itself.

Guide told Luke that siphoning a void creature's Source with any success was an improbable task. Improbable was not impossible. Taking a whisper of Source from Smudge hadn't been much of a challenge, but, as luck would have it, a more fitting opponent had just arrived.

Luke: "Let me take that one."

Dimitrios: "Yes. I'm resting. Don't wake me if you can help it."

Moving his consciousness into the metaphysical realm to traverse the weave, Luke soon stood face to face with a single void creature. It glanced up from what it was doing, gnawing on the end seam of Earth's weave, then ignored Luke in favor of its feeding.

"Do you have a name?" Luke asked.

His words didn't register in the dim eyes of the tentacled blob of death, and he hadn't expected them to. Getting its name, if it even knew it had one, wasn't the reason for asking either. He just wanted to know if there was any amount of awareness inside the creature. While most of it was made up of void, it did have slivers of Source inside. It was the least amount Luke had ever seen, but it was there. Something about it sparked recognition. Luke narrowed his eyes.

The lines of Source were thin, but long. A concentration of the stuff was centered in the middle, kind of like a meta-heart. All in all, it looked more like a person's mana channels than a void being's Source. Or a person who had just turned into a void creature.

"Philosopher?" Luke asked, still not getting an answer. He didn't need one. The moment the thought crossed his mind, Luke knew he was right. They had killed the others, meaning there was no one to bring their new ally into the fold. With nothing else to guide it, the creature before Luke, who had up until just hours earlier been an Integrated, reverted to its base instinct: to feed. It didn't even have the awareness to react to Luke's presence. The creature right outside the weave, almost close enough for him to reach, evoked a pang of sadness. But, more than anything, it made Luke angry. Oh, so angry.
