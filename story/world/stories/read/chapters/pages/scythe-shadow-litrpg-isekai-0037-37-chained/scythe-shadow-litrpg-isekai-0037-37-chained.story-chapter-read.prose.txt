It didn't take long to find and slay a Marked Sin nearby. The long part was hauling the body back to the destroyed village, especially with Nuzzle riding the thing's head, chirping out orders like a chariot driver.

When I returned, Silas appeared more awake. He'd moved from the spot I left him and was kneeling beside the corpse of a young Penumbran girl. Lines from streaks of tears cut through the ash on his cheeks, dry now. He'd been here a while.

"You knew her?" I asked, dragging the Sin and dropping it nearby.

"I thought I was gonna marry her someday," he said, his voice still raspy. He appeared maybe thirteen or fourteen. Too young to be having such thoughts. Though, this place seemed to age your mind.

My thoughts flashed back to Avery. When would I see her again? This would be her, sitting by my bedside soon, if not already. It hurt my soul.

"I knew everyone here," Silas added. "My father was the mayor of this village."

"Your father?"

"Not my real father. My adopted father. Here in Penumbra. He's a Reaper too. Hopefully he phased out when the danger came."

I hadn't realized there were formal settlements like this with semi-structured leadership. Had it just been Silas and his father lording over a group of Penumbrans, playing at kings? Silas didn't seem the type in my brief interactions so far.

"We were just trying to make a life, you know?" Silas said, his voice quivering. If he'd had any tears left they would've come now. "Live in peace."

I looked around at the ruined village. "You should harvest this Will and get out of here."

Silas did as I suggested and we both relinquished our stored Wills in the pillar outside the fallen town wall.

The Amber Will didn't boost my stats as much as when I'd been an Amber myself. If I wanted to keep progressing, I'd need to leave the Meadow in search of higher stage Sins.

I exited the circle of frost around the pillar and joined Silas, who'd stood off to the side watching, looking much more energetic with his replenished Volition.

"You're the Death Reaper everyone's been talking about, aren't you?" he asked.

Word travels fast…

"What makes you say that?"

He nodded to my scythe. "Saw the skull on your blade. And the symbol on your cloak. They say you're gonna end sin forever."

"They say that, huh?"

He nodded vigorously. "To be honest… that made me a little sad. I didn't want to leave this place. But now…"

I stared at the boy, wondering what his life in Vitria must be like if he preferred the Umbral Plane.

"You should phase out," I said. "Refresh your Health, then meet up with your father when you return."

Silas shook his head. "If I phase out now I'll just return here. I'm never coming back again. Father and I have an arrangement to meet up in Meadowvale if we're separated."

"Best of luck then." I started walking toward the nearest hill to get a lay of the land. The forest I'd come from and the sea were west—behind me. I'd need to go east to get to the next zone. Jungle I think.

"Wait!' Silas called. "I need to repay you somehow. What will you do now?"

"I'm investigating the Unchained for Valciferous," I called back.

"I saw where they came from," Silas said.

I stopped and turned back to the boy. "You saw where the Unchained came from?"

He nodded. "I was out harvesting ore. On my way back I saw them exit a cave." He scanned the horizon and pointed south. "That way. That's why I tried to warn the village, but they were already at the walls by the time I got here."

"Thank you, Silas." I started walking the direction he indicated and was surprised to find him jog up alongside me. Nuzzle hopped onto his head, looking like a giant hat.

"I'll show you where."

"You don't need to do that. Just this information is repayment enough."

"You saved my life. There's never enough repayment for that. And besides, if you're investigating the Unchained, I want in."

"You want revenge," I hedged.

"Hell yes, I do."

I nodded. "Alright then. Show me the way."

The 'cave' Silas brought me to was nestled among several steep hills covered in jutting stone. I could see how the area was good for mining. From a distance, nothing appeared out of the ordinary. Up close, striations and grooves in the stone around the edges made it apparent the opening was anything but natural.

An Unchained Sin couldn't have done this. It's too intentional.

"Was this always here?" I asked.

"Never saw it until today."

I examined the opening suspiciously. Something was off here, but I couldn't figure out what.

"What are we waiting for?" Silas asked, striding toward the cave's mouth, scythe in hand.

I grabbed his wrist. "You're staying out here."

"Like Hell I am! Screw that."

"Keep your voice down. Your Health is still low, remember? And we don't know what's in there."

"I'm coming with you. Besides I recovered a bit with a snack while you were relinquishing your Will."

My face must have betrayed my surprise because Silas grinned.

"What? You thought I was just gonna prance around the Meadow with hardly any Health?"

"I forgot there were items that could recover Health."

"You sure you're the Death Reaper? Seems like you don't know too much."

Talk about being forward. Fucking kids. "I'm still kind of new here," I said.

"Oh. How many months?"

"Months? How about a couple of days…"

His mouth dropped open. "You get right to it, don't you?"

"Wasn't given much choice, but yeah."

"In that case, you definitely need me to come with you."

I sighed. "Fine. But you stay behind and you listen to me. I say quiet, you shut up. I say run, you bolt. Understood?"

"Yeah, I got it. You may wanna tell your lizard the same thing though."

"What?"

I turned to see Nuzzle peering into the darkness ahead, a devilish look in his eyes. Then he whistled, the echoes singing off the walls deep into the cave system.

"Nuzzle," I hissed. "Get over here."

The lizard obliged, trotting along, tongue out, practically smiling as if he'd just earned a medal.

I patted him on the head. "Can you keep guard out here, buddy? Whistle down to us like that if anyone is coming?"

Nuzzle sat his bum down in the dirt and licked his eye.

"Good boy."

I entered the cave cautiously, Silas behind me. My senses were on high alert for another Unchained. Every little sound we made bounced off the stone walls. The deeper we went, the darker it became. Blindly, I steadied myself against the wall, fingers pushing against the hard stone. Still grooved, unnatural—as was the ground, which bothered my bare feet.

Eventually, Silas took a lantern from his inventory and I instructed him to keep the flame as low as possible. We made our way deeper at a barely perceptible decline, down a straight path with no offshoots. It made the travel easy, but left me more concerned the deeper we went. If something caught us, there'd be no escaping, no hiding. Just a race for the exit or a fight.

After nearly fifteen minutes of walking, a soft glow lit the way ahead around the first curve in the tunnel we'd come across. Silas put away his lantern without prompting. I eased forward, just enough to peer around the corner.

The tunnel opened into a circular cavern the size of four or five decent sized homes, lit by several torches along the walls. Large cages lined the cavern, equally spaced out. There had to be twenty of them. Maybe more.

What the fuck is going on here?

I felt Silas looking over my shoulder. He breathed out, heavy. Angry.

I held up a hand, hoping he'd take the hint and stay calm.

Two Reapers patrolled among the cages. Emerald and Amethyst. If my jump in stats and skill during my fight with Zeus was any indication, I had no hope defeating either of them. Silas would be torn apart in seconds.

But they were the least of my concerns.

Half the cages here housed prisoners. Sins. Unchained Sins. Each with the glowing broken skull hovering above their heads. Most were Amber, but there were several Amethyst and one Emerald as well.

They should've been rattling at their bars. Howling, trying to rampage against their captors. Instead they sat in their cells. Awake, but subdued, with familiar metal collars around their necks and chains attached to the floor.

A new light moving closer caught my eye in the continuation of our tunnel which ran tangential to the cavern. I pulled my head back and put a finger to my lips. Silas and I held our breaths. I prayed whoever was coming would stop here and not continue on to our side.

Someone called forth. A female voice. "Daddy's coming home and he's brought a new baby."

"The cradle is ready to receive," someone replied. Probably one of the guards.

I didn't dare look, but I was too curious to leave.

Armored footsteps. Two pairs, maybe? And a third, heavier, more beast-like.

"Cradle C-2 prepped," the same guard said.

The footsteps sounded like they were heading into the array of cages. I risked the slighted peek around the corner.

Two new Reapers guided a large bear-moth-human hybrid into the cavern. The Sin was Unchained, Emerald. The Reapers were a female Emerald—the one who'd spoken—and… something else. His cloak was gray with a large symbol on the back not unlike my own, but also different.

Gray?

What did that mean? I tried to remember the ranks Calliope had taught me. Gray would be Iron, right?

The highest rank. Even above Lancelot.

We need to get out of here.

I wanted to leave, but my legs wouldn't move.

"This'll be the last one before Meadowfall," the female Emerald said.

The Iron Reaper stepped over to another set of cages as the two guards locked the bear-like Sin in its prison. "I'll re-up the rest. No mistakes."

He held up a hand and threads like fishing line spiraled from his fingertips, then speared into each of the caged Sins through their Wills. There was no reaction from the Sins. The threads retracted and the Iron Reaper walked away, heading back toward the tunnel he'd come from.

I pulled my head back out of view.

"What number is that?" one of the guards asked.

"Two-hundred-thirty four," the Iron said.

"All at once… That's fucking nuts."

"Just be ready for the signal," he replied.

The female Emerald exchanged more words with the guards, mostly pleasantries and jokes.

I turned, ushering Silas to start our trek back out, but caught one more line I hadn't wanted to hear.

"Our contact from the Meadow should be here soon."

From the Meadow.

My eyes met Silas' and were probably just as wide.

We need to get the Hell out.

Now.
