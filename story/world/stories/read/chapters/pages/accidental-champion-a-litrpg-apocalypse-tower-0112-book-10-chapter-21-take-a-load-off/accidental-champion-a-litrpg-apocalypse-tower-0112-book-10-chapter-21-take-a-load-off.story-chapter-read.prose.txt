Xavier left the bones swords in the beast’s head and flew over it—the ceiling barely high enough for him to manage the feat—then landed on the ground behind it, two more swords grown and grasped in his hands, ready to face the final beast.

He was still reeling from what had just happened. Killing the beast had given him at least an extra 20 percent boost to the Personal System Integration, which meant the energy the Personal System needed for its installation hadn’t come from his cores.

His mind worked rapidly. He split off a part and occupied one of his mental spaces, puzzling out exactly what had happened. Outside his mind it was as though time had stopped—though it hadn’t, of course.

The beasts he was facing were fast. Damned fast. And though Xavier could speed up time like this inside his head, that didn’t mean he could benefit from that inside his body. Not in the same way, at least. He was only this fast inside his mental space. He could use this space for deep thought and inform the main part of his consciousness currently commanding his body of what he discovered, but he couldn’t command his body from here…

He supposed the differences were slight, but he noticed them.

Xavier played back the memory of slaying the beast, starting from when he severed the string of energy that had connected them. That string of energy was dangerous, and Xavier was glad he’d used his Soul Energy to deal with it, but he also couldn’t help but be worried about just how much Soul Energy it had taken.

These two beasts would by no means be the only things the Void Dungeon threw at him. He couldn’t go using 10 percent of his Soul Energy reserve every time he fought one. He’d run out. Fast.

For the moment, he put that worry aside and instead focused on what had happened when he’d thrust his two swords into the Void Dungeon beast and energy had pulsed so brightly he’d thought it was going to be an explosion.

The energy hadn’t exploded. It had surged through his channels instead. In his memory, using his Immortal Soul lens, he focused his spiritual sense on that energy.

As he’d suspected, it was Celestial Energy. Raw Celestial Energy.

Inside his mental space, Xavier was pacing back and forth across a carpeted room, his boots making barely a sound. He tapped a finger on his chin.

He was thinking about his encounters with enemies inside the void. Every encounter he’d had with an enemy while he’d been inside the void had been very close to his home universe. The System had sometimes been able to send him a notification while he was there, and sometimes it hadn’t been able to at all. The void was somewhere the System had minimal influence over. Somewhere it struggled to reach.

Which meant its rules weren’t always in place inside the void—but maybe they had been when he’d slain the True Dragon. All two thousand of them. The dragons hadn’t been inside the void, either. They’d been inside System-governed universes. Only the Xaviers had been outside it.

He had slain a Void Being with the other Xaviers as well, but even that had been close enough to his host universe that the System might have governed what that gave him. Or perhaps slaying a Void Being was different to slaying a normal beast.

Or a dungeon beast… because are these lion-octopus-nightmare Void Dungeon created things normal beasts at all?

Inside his own universe (and any other universe the System that governed his corner of the multiverse controlled) when a Denizen slayed an enemy—beast, spirit, or another Denizen—they received Spirit Energy and Mastery Points for the kill.

But Spirit Energy was a construct, something created by the System. Or, at least, something created out of Celestial Energy. Perhaps some being had created it long before his System had. He didn’t know, and that really didn’t matter.

But it made sense that before the System had been created, slaying an enemy wouldn’t give one Mastery Points. And it followed that it wouldn’t give them Spirit Energy either, because Spirit Energy wasn’t universal.

Universal… would it be more accurate to say multiversal?

He shook his head, not meaning to get sidetracked.

Now, he wasn’t close to his home universe. He had an inkling he wasn’t close to any universe, this deep inside the void. Which meant things had quite possibly returned to their natural, pre-System state, meaning that when an enemy was slain, raw Celestial Energy was gained.

Not that he had any confirmation that that was how things used to be.

Even if they hadn’t been, this was very useful information. There was no ambient Celestial Energy in the air. In this place, he couldn’t replenish his core reserves though cultivation. Not in the traditional sense.

He couldn’t restore it through killing these Void Dungeon beasts, either. Not while the Personal System was still installing. But, once the integration had completed, things would be different. The energy gained from killing these things would come into him.

He could cultivate energy directly from the death of his enemies.

Xavier grinned, sharing this thinking with the main part of his consciousness. He wouldn’t have to worry so much about just how much energy he lost, though Soul Energy might be a difficult thing to restore in this place, his other energies shouldn’t be.

Inside his body, he tightened then loosened his grip on the second set of two bone swords he’d grown since coming to this place and pounced on the remaining lion-octopus-nightmare beast, still benefiting from the burning of Reality Energy. The energy flowed through every part of him, strengthening everything. The blades sunk deep into the second beast’s skull, killing it instantly just as he’d killed the first.

Personal System Integration has reached 55%.

More than halfway.

He was just taking a cleansing breath, looking down at the dead Void Dungeon beasts and wondering if something could be done with their corpses, when another screech sounded from down the tunnel, this one much angrier than either of the beasts he’d just slain.

Then two more screeches sounded. It was clear they weren’t all coming from the same beasts.

Xavier put a foot on the skull of the beast he’d just slain, gripped his two bone swords, and pulled them clean out from where they’d been lodged. He swung them from side to side in a completely unnecessary, yet flashy, movement. The grin on his face widened. He could still smell the disgusting fluid that had gotten onto him when he’d slammed a bone spear into the second beast, but it no longer bothered him.

Because right now it smelled like victory.

Personal System Integration has reached 56%.

Xavier tore forward, his wings propelling him farther down the dark tunnel just as the three beasts emerged from the eerie, unnatural blackness ahead. He crashed into the first one, sending it and him both tumbling across the ground. The lion-octopus-nightmare beast’s tentacle-like limbs tried to come at him, spikes growing from them, but he slashed each of them off before they could reach him. And that wasn’t all he did.

He gutted the beast, sliding one of the swords straight along its underside in a wide arc. By the time the dragonkin and the Void Dungeon beast stopped tumbling along the tunnel, only one of them was able to get up off the stone floor.

The other was dead.

Another influx of energy poured through his channels up to his brain.

Personal System Integration has reached 76%.

The other two beasts screeched with tremendous rage, and he knew what was coming next. The spikes they’d grown from the ends of the tentacle-like limbs glowed. In a fraction of a second, they would be launched straight at him. A dozen spikes. As fast as they were, they would be impossible to dodge.

So Xavier didn’t try.

He stood his ground. As the glowing spikes shot toward him, he initiated one of the many spell patterns he had ready inside his body, no longer as worried about the energy expenditure.

And I’ve clearly already attracted beasts.

The spell helped him phase straight out of reality. A dozen spikes slammed into the stone wall behind him, though they weren’t powerful enough to lodge themselves in the stone, and they fell to the ground with a clatter.

Xavier felt… invigorated. God, it felt good to be fighting like this. Using his hands. Using his weapons. Not just pointing all the energy he could gather together from thousands of galaxies at an enemy.

That was of course far more powerful than what he was doing right now, but this felt different. Better. The more of his energy and spells he used, the less threat these things posed to him, and, if he were honest, these things weren’t a match for him at all.

Even so, it felt like a challenge. Like he was pushing his body. And this was only the very start of the dungeon. There would be far worse enemies to face than these…

He flashed forward, straight at another of the lion-octopus-nightmare beasts, carving through it with devastating brutality until it too was dead at his feet.

Personal System Integration has reached 96%.

Only one more.

It didn’t take long for him to take care of the final of the five Void Dungeon beasts. He was breathing heavily, staring down at it, as another notification popped up—the text in his vision and the sound in his mind.

Personal System Integration has reached 100%

Prepare for full installation…

This should only take a few minutes.

Grab a coffee.

Take a load off.

Slay your enemies.

Xavier frowned at the last few lines of the message. Then he frowned harder when something like elevator music started playing in his mind.

What the…?

Xavier sighed. He’d been excited to find out just what this thing would do, but he supposed he could wait a few more minutes. Not that he really had a choice.

He glanced down the dark tunnel, straining his ears.

He couldn’t hear anything, but that didn’t mean there wouldn’t be more of these things streaming down the tunnel real soon.

The last influx of energy he’d received had been more than enough to finish the installation, which meant not all of it had fed straight toward his brain. The rest of the raw Celestial Energy had been left behind. Xavier felt it cycling around his channels. The feeling was a good one. He directed much of the energy toward his Reality Core, then toward the other cores he’d used energy from.

It wasn’t enough to restore everything he’d used. He would still need to be a little careful with his energy usage here. But with the Personal System already having used what it needed for its integration… well, he no longer thought not being able to cultivate normally here was a serious problem.

In fact, the energy he received from these beasts was fairly significant, especially if he could slay them in greater numbers.

One step at a time. I’ve only killed five of these things, after all. Getting cocky now would be a mistake.

He bent down, touching a hand to the nearest Void Dungeon beast, depositing its corpse into his Storage Ring. Something told him these things would come in handy. He gathered up the other four corpses, constantly keeping an ear out for another screech. When he’d gathered the last, less than a minute had passed.

The elevator music was still playing.

Xavier glanced down the tunnel again. He couldn’t help but want to head down it. To explore more of this dungeon. He was sure there would be much to see. Many beasts to face. Far more sinister things than the ones he’d just slain.

But he would wait until the Personal System finally finished.

Having nothing better to do, Xavier sat cross legged on the ground and began to meditate. He left his eyes open—he couldn’t trust his spiritual sense to pick up on much of anything down here—and focused on his energy channels and Reality Core.

He’d neglected his Cultivator path for a good while, not having done much of anything to advance past Tier 3. He’d been so focused on… everything else. Yet here, burning Reality Energy had been a great boon, instantly turning the tide of the fight in his favour. There were many other ways he could have turned the tide, he was sure, but this one had been the most natural.

I’ve been neglecting both my paths of advancement. Derrida was right. There’s nothing stopping me from pushing to A Grade, even S Grade. There’s nothing stopping me from pushing to Tier 4, 5, and beyond. I’ve been holding back. All my reasons for doing so make complete sense—because of course they do—but that doesn’t mean those reasons are right. If I’m going to defeat the Interdimensional Dragon, I can’t let myself neglect any path of power.

As he became lost in thought, the notification finally came.

Personal System installation complete.

Welcome to the Multiverse, Xavier Collins.
