Chapter 384

Before Eik could make his own move, a blade crescent was launched from the streets of Gimleh.

Traveling at hyper sonic speeds, the wave of pure, cutting energy reached the Lord in an instant, but the fiend still batted it away with a hand enveloped in impenetrable darkness.

Two seconds later, another wave followed, but this one was not so easily deflected and actually sent the Lord reeling back at the impact. Chop must have finally taken his potion.

Unwilling to let the old geezer have all the fun, Eik rocketed into the air, hurling three javelins at the Lord in quick succession, immediately followed up by hundreds of streaks of liquid Profound Toxin that would swirl about the beast’s body and harassing him until he the moment where they could invade his system.

Initiating his own offense, the Lord hurled an inky bolt of squirming parasites at Eik. More than a dozen layers of crystal shells caught and slowed the momentum of the attack as they shattered one by one, but it wasn’t enough to actually stop it.

However, instead of continuing through the barriers, the bolt burst and the parasites were flung out in all directions and sent raining toward the city.

The burst knocked Eik toward the ground and as he fell, he reached up and manipulated a gigantic floating sphere of crystal into existence, which secured the parasites before they could scatter into the city. It followed his fist as he clenched tightly, shrinking it to a hundredth of its original size.

With a thought, he flooded the inside of it with liquid Profound Toxin and Accelerated it to detonate, erasing the parasites entirely.

The moment he landed on the ground, Eik reached for Movement Boost and turned it up to max, Backflow revving up in earnest as well. All that was left under his feet when he leaped for the Lord was a crater of dust and debris, the buildings around his launch point blown to pieces.

Already spinning into a vicious back fist, the Lord met him as he whirled into existence behind him. Eik saw it coming and put up his arms, reinforced with thick crystal, but it shattered on impact and the remaining force sent him flying, shock wave splitting the sky.

A conjured plate and cushion caught his fall before Accelerating and sending him back up. In a moment of magical timing, Chop the Blade arrived on the Lord’s opposite side, creating a pincer move as his blade sliced the air in two.

But the hostile Worldbreaker wasn’t fazed and unleashed a pulse of aura just powerful enough to throw Eik and Chop off balance, followed immediately up by a swarm of parasites.

Vigorous, blue flames erupted along the swordsman’s crystal blade as each of his masterful strokes obliterated millions of the black parasites. Chop must have been working busily as well these past days and weeks. Those flames were clearly a trait of the sword skill granted to him by the Pristine Blade Saint title he had gotten when he had been initiated into the court of the Supreme Divinity of Toxin.

Eik dealt with his own share in the same way that he had previously—trapping the parasites within a solid sphere and detonating a liquid inside. Within that sphere he also caught the Lord himself.

The lightning quick Accelerant rocked the fiend just enough to allow Chop’s powerful slashes to continue through the swarm of parasites and into the Lord. For gashes opened along his side, shedding a viscous, pale ichor that resembled white paint.

Several streaks of Profound Toxin zipped for the wounds, but only a couple managed to plunge inside before the rest were driven back by a forceful expulsion of aura.

And so the long path to victory could begin.

Already beginning to heal, one of the beast’s four arms lashed out as he howled in anger, catching Chop solidly in the face, crushing his nasal bone with a nauseating crunch, and sending him plummeting into the jungle where he disappeared in an explosion of broken trees and upheaved earth.

Eik cursed under his breath and went for an upward swing while the enemy was distracted, a short axe manifesting in his hand even as carried out the attack. The Lord avoided in narrowly by twisting his body unnaturally, and lunging for Eik in return as he whirled back.

It was by a hair’s breadth that Eik ducked by detonating a chunk of crystal at his own temple and was sent into a sideways spin, a horrific ringing in his ear. Going with the flow, the axe descended again, this time aiming for the Lord’s shin, but instead of a clean hit Eik’s fingers were broken by a devastating kick that caused the weapon to be flung from his weapon and into free fall over the wilderness.

Before he could react further, another kick hammered into the back of his head and shot him the same way as Chop.

Even as he fell, Eik turned in the air and caught of glimpse of the Lord moving rapidly toward Gimleh, all four of his arms raised as if to beckon in something. And to Eik’s horror, on the distant horizon, a blanket of pure black rose up and rapidly made its way across the sky toward the city.

“Like hell you will!” Eik snarled and maneuvered back up toward the Lord, Chop emerging from the hole he’d excavated in the same moment, nose healed.

Not even deigning to look at the two X-rankers, the Lord brought down one arm decisively, a wicked grin plastered on his lips.

“No!” Eik shouted. “Chop, block it!”

Altering his course with an air step, the old man practically teleported in front of the energy blast, instantly deploying the same defensive technique he had used against Eik during their spar. A glowing image of the sword master’s blade appeared in front of him, the previously golden color now shifted into a deep azure—a testament to the old geezer’s continued advancements down the path of the Pristine Blade Saint.

Spiderweb fractures surged down the length of the bright blade as it nearly shattered under the force of that single casual offensive. Chop grunted under the weight, struggling to stay in place, and when the second blast struck the practically destroyed defensive sword, there was little else he could do but tank it with his body.

The old man obliterated at least a few blocks of a major residential area of Gimleh when he was hurled into the city by the sheer force of Lord’s attack. By the time the third energy blast aiming to cause indescribable devastation was launched, Eik had already thrown up a column of crystal shells to take the brunt of the attack, but they didn’t have his own personal power behind them in the same way as Chop’s defensive blade did and the attack leveled half a district of Gimleh as a result.

As the final arm came down, Eik saw no option but to throw himself in front of it.

The strength of the strike robbed his lungs of air completely and sent memories of their first fight flashing through his mind. There wasn’t a shred of doubt in his mind that the Lord had managed to increase his power since their last meeting. Absorption of the miasma collected from the infested worlds must be only one of multiple methods he used to grow stronger.

Only a sequence of toxic chunks Accelerated in rapid sequence in his back prevented him from crashing into Gimleh and wreaking even more havoc than Chop had done.

“Wh-Why do you… struggle so?” the Lord rasped as his hands went back to directing the blanket of darkness as it swept closer to Gimleh, twirling and moving like a conductor. His voice traveled like the wind. “I have not come to cause you harm.”

Now stable in the air, Eik glowered, seeing no reason to even utter a reply to that.

When the white, toothy grin widened even further, it stood out much more starkly on the Lord’s jet black face than it did on his servants. “So my lies are… of little use here, hmm?”

“Nobody would fall for your shit, bitch,” Eik muttered, the massive, ten meter long war hammer manifesting in his hand. More than twenty streaks of Profound Toxin still swirled about in the air. This would become a battle of attrition.

A disgusting giggle that belonged in a horror movie more than anywhere else echoed in Eik’s ears. “The universe is… filled to the brim with fools.”

Eik would have liked to reject that notion, but it was undeniably true. And he was most certainly one of them himself. Not in the sense that he would fall for some kind of sweet, eons-old prophecy of a divine rebirth of the Unified Mass, but he wasn’t about to describe himself with words such as wise or intelligent.

Beneath all of the extreme strength, all-encompassing influence, and disgusting wealth, he was very much just some guy. He had no delusion about that. He hadn’t even been powerful or important for a majority of his life.

But just because he was kind of right, didn’t mean the Lord of the Moon wasn’t a total asshole.

“Now… I believe your suffering has been delayed long enough,” the Lord said. Abruptly, the speed of the encroaching darkness increased many times over. It was eating its way through the borders of the Apocalypse Canvas.

Inhaling sharply, Eik hurled the enormous hammer at the Worldbreaker as two shrieking Death Stars quickly formed in his palms. While the Lord sidestepped the flying hammer, each of the deadly spheres were launched deep into the blanket at different points to maximize effect and avoid mutual overlap.

From one instant to the next, the sheet of pitch that had been floating above at approximately the same height as Eik’s Apocalypse Canvas, collided with the wilderness below, two ruinous discharges of energy dispersing the black and revealing a blinding, glittering blue underneath.

“Enough.” Moving suddenly, the Lord exploded forward, the broken blanket of black reforming behind him as it continued moving in on the city. It was a matter of time, especially now that the fiend was making a move in earnest.

They had been exchanging sporadic back and forths until now, each party trying to get a read of the other’s current strength, but it would seem that the Lord had decided to put an end to the dance and get serious.

Eik could never let that happen. “Chop, stop him! He’s going for the city!”

“I don’t need you to order me... around!” the old man shouted in the same moment that he appeared before the Lord, his crystal blade brought down in a decisive downward slash.

A rod of pure darkness was suddenly in the beast’s hands, gripped in both right arms at the same time. It looked like what had pierced Eik’s chest during their first fight. The Lord grunted at the force of the attack but overpowered Chop nonetheless, batting away the energy and sending it crackling through the air.

Flowing smoothly through his forms, Chop moved seamlessly into another attack, but as he was bringing his blade in from the side, the Lord drove the black rod through the man’s shoulder. The only indication of pain from Chop was a clenching of his teeth, but the damage was enough to rob his slash of power.

Eik was ready directly beneath them with a ferocious upward thrust with a crystalline spear, but before he could see it through, he was assaulted.

With no telegraphing, a roiling wave of parasites swarmed over Eik’s body as his spear pierced the Lord through the shin and melted into the wound. Ignoring the wound, the Lord of the Moon was already moving again.

Continuing the battle here was too dangerous. His family and friends would have been moved to a different world by now, but he had made a vow to every person living here, and he wasn’t about to break it.

“Chop, you make sure every last spawn is dead here, you hear!” Eik shouted through the swarming parasites. “Prioritize swift elimination!”

“What are you talking about?” the X-ranker answered, clenching his teeth as he struggled to remove the rod from his chest.

Eik huffed. “It’s a simple case of delegation of responsibility.”

Without a moment of hesitation, he triggered Movement Boost and exploded after the fiend, Omnicide surging through his system.

Feeling his approach, the monster spun around to bat away his attack, but rather than an attack, Eik simply slapped the Lord’s hand, activating an ability in the same moment.

Admittance to the Realm.
