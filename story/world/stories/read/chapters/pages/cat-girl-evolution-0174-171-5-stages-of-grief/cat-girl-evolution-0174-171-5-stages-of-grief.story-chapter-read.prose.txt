Metztli and Kai fly almost straight to me. I have to Blink just a little bit to the side to intercept them properly. I catch Metzli in a giant mana hand, then create a few smaller mana hands to grab her horns, and neck in case she destroys the big one. Kai is still dangling there looking scared.

“You dumbass!” I shout at him. “What the hell do you think you’re doing?”

Kai doesn’t respond because Metztli is crushing his windpipe.

“Drop me or he dies,” says Metztli, grinning at me.

“I don’t give shit if he dies,” I say. “Just get it over with already if you’re gonna do it.”

“I’ll really do it.”

“I know, so hurry up and get it over with already.”

Metztli narrows her eyes in suspicion, and for some reason, seems to let up on the pressure.

“What are you planning?”

I glance over at Kai, who looks back at me. His eyes are full of fear, and he seems to be begging me to save him. Dumbass. If he didn’t want to die, he shouldn’t have dived in like that.

“I’m planning to kill you,” I say, looking back at Metztli. “And that fuckhead is in the way.”

“Right…” she says. “Fine, then. I never needed you to drop me anyway.”

She swings her arm down, shattering the mana hand gripping her, then reaches up to break the ones holding her horns. I conjure a half dozen new ones to grab her and keep her from falling, but she’s just too fast. Even with only one arm, she’s destroying the hands faster than I can make them. I need to do this now.

I Blink right in front of her and slightly above her and rake my claws across her face, using Spatial Slice. It’s full power, but it barely does any damage. It doesn’t even draw blood. It definitely pisses her off, though, and a second later, her free arm shoots up, straight at me.

It’s too fast to dodge. If she had been aiming for my head, I would have just died. But I kept my head just out of reach, hoping she wouldn’t do anything super mana-based, and it worked. Her hand sinks into my stomach, rising higher through my innards until it reaches my heart. With Food Sense, I can see her hand grasping my beating heart, and she pulls me down, smiling.

“Idiot,” she hisses.

I cough blood on her face, then smile back. As I do, right around the place where her arm entered, my stomach splits open further. Teeth form around the edge of the split, and right around her arm, clamping down on it, and forming a giant mouth. The teeth are made of the demon bone that I stole, so they’re strong enough to pierce Metztli’s skin, but not quite strong enough to do any more than that.

“What the–?” she says.

She crushes my heart, and tries to pull her hand out, but all she does is pull me closer. My body feels cold, and the pain is immense, but I just barely have the presence of mind to open my mouth and bite. I manage to latch onto her horn, and she tries to push me away, but my head stays attached to her.

She flails around, trying to get her arm out of my torso and my horn out of her mouth, but I never let go. A new heart is already forming out of reach of her trapped hand. All I need to now is… It’s…

My vision goes dark for a split second, then comes back hazy. Someone shouts something—my name, maybe—but I don’t hear it. Everything’s so cold. The voice feels so far away. And this thing in my mouth is awfully tasty…

“Irina!”

Abby’s voice suddenly snaps my mind back into focus, just in time for me to see Metztli drop Kai and swing her other hand at my neck. Time seems to slow as it rushes toward me, and I rapidly go through the five stages of grief.

Maybe I can survive this. Maybe she somehow won’t behead me. It’s not that bad. I just need to raise a shield to block it.

My mana is moving too slow. Fuck! I’m fucked.

Maybe I can survive a beheading, though. I heard severed heads were still alive for a few seconds. Maybe I can grow a new body before I fully die.

But then where does that leave me? I can’t manipulate flesh that quickly, and it’s not like Metztli will just sit around watch me heal. She’ll just destroy my head next.

I guess this is it, then. It’s over. I had a good run. Too bad I didn’t get to kill Metztli, but this should be enough, right? They separated her from the main body, so they should be able to seal her away. That might actually be a fate worse than death. Too bad I never got to finish the rest of the Delicacies, though.

Then, suddenly, I feel mana rushing in from somewhere far away. It coalesced right next to my neck between it and Metztli’s hand. The barrier isn’t strong enough to stop the attack, but it slows it down. And it doesn’t even slow it down that much, but it ends up being enough.

Her hand cuts through half my neck, but stops when it reaches my spine. My neck still breaks, of course, but it doesn’t get severed. And with the breaking of the neck, I lose all sensation in my body, including the pain. Suddenly, my head feels clearer. I remember what I need to do.

Teeth form around the torn part of my neck, clamping down on Metztli’s wrist. She tries to pull away, but she can’t. She tries grabbing for my spine, but I grow the flesh on my neck out like an ugly tumor, pulling her hand out of reach. The severed artery reforms around the mouth that now holds her hand, and the damaged windpipe gets repaired. My spine starts healing, and I start Devouring the two trapped limbs to recover faster.

In my stomach, my organs start to rearrange themselves. The new heart is not in the right place, but the veins and arteries are shifted around so they still attach properly, and it soon starts beating again. The organs she tore through also get rerouted, and new flesh forms around her arm like a second throat, forming a flesh wall between her arm and the rest of my innards.

And for the final step of the plan, the maw on my back unfolds like a flower, and starts reversing, wrapping around both of us. The torn muscles still attacked the vertebrate Metztli’s body grows from whip around, trying to attack me. They shatter my legs, but my neck isn’t healed yet, so I don’t even feel it. They try to attack the edges of the maw, but I keep it out of reach. Soon, the sun gets blotted out, and the maw closes around us both, and I start Devouring.

“Stop!” she screams. “What are you doing? You’re going to kill us both!”

“No,” I say, voice still muffled from biting down on her horn. “Just you.”

Devour starts tearing us apart. My clothes disintegrate, and flakes of blood and skin start pealing off, pulled toward the “throat” of the maw, which is still attached to my back. As soon as they disappear, though, they are restored, my own flesh constantly recycled to heal me. Metztli gets torn up too, but more slowly. It doesn’t matter. I’m healing, and she’s not.

The mana hands holding us both up also disintegrate, and we start falling, but only for a few seconds before we land on a mana barrier, holding us up high and out of reach of the earth that Metztli would be able to use to attack from the outside. She tries gathering mana, probably to blow me apart from the inside, but whenever she does, the mana just ends up getting Devoured before she can use it. The only mana I can’t reach is the mana inside her body, but with her arms trapped, and no legs, she can’t really do anything with it.

“No, no, no, no, NO!” she screams. “HOW?! What are you doing!?”

She tries pulling her arms out, and fails once again. She tries spreading her arms out to tear me in two, but she doesn’t have the leverage, and my body is just strong enough to resist it. My neck is restored enough that I can feel it, and it hurts like hell, but I hold out, strengthen my body to resist it.

She tries pushing her arms together to crush me instead, which works slightly better, but I recover as fast as she breaks me. I recover in a fucked up, mutated way, but I keep all the essentials intact, and I strengthen everything with the bone that Devour is absorbing from the severed vertebrae.

Metzli screams at me for a while, and then after a little while, she starts bargaining with me.

“What do you want?” she asks. “Money? Power? Men? Women? Animals? I can make you stronger. I can help you get what you want. We don’t have to be enemies.”

I finally bite off the horn, which is brittle from Devour doing its work. I chew a few times, then swallow. It’s quite delicious. Kind of like a good bone broth. And it makes me want more, just like all demon flesh, but for some reason, the urge isn’t quite as strong. Maybe because I’m also eating my own flesh, which is balancing it out somehow? I don’t really get it.

“You ate my frogs,” I say.

“Seriously!?” she exclaims. “That’s why you’re doing this?!”

“Next time, maybe don’t be such a petty bitch.”

She gapes at me throwing her own words back at her, and she looks like she’s about to get really angry, but stops herself.

“You’re right,” she says. “That was wrong of me. I shouldn’t have done it. I’m sorry.”

“Are you?”

“I am.”

“I heard drow can’t feel emotions like that.”

“Well it’s not. That’s just what they say. They’re scared of us. Of what we can do. Yeah, we sometimes associate with demons. So what? That doesn’t mean we are demons! We’re people too! We don’t deserve this!

“So please. Don’t do this. We can help each other. We don’t need to fight.”

“Hmmm, let me think about it,” I say.

“We can–”

“I said let me think about it.”

Anger flashes across her face, but then disappears a second later, turning into a downright pleasant smile. Or it would be pleasant if the skin wasn’t peeling off her face and her teeth weren’t so vampire-like.

“Are you actually thinking of letting her go.”
