Dear Diary,

Pretty slow couple days.

Not boring in any way whatsoever. Just slow.

I think I've mentioned it before that people underestimate boredom, but I think I may have been mistaken.

Don't get me wrong, boring can be okay. When I've hit an absolutely overwhelmed point, managed to fix all the critical shit, and am lying there in a pool of sweat, or possibly less savory bodily fluids, I find boredom to be a pleasant relief. That's not to say boredom is always good. When I'm waiting for shit to go down, and nothing at all is happening, that's bad boredom. Mostly because I can't shut my brain off and go to sleep, let everything go into rest and recovery mode, but I can't do shit to distract myself either. I just gotta be on, but, like, 'on standby', for unsustainable lengths of time.

To put it another way, I hate waiting.

But the past couple haven't been like that. First the Autumn Equinox Revel, which was one big block party in eight different locations. Kinda interesting thing that I didn't think to mention the other day, but there were a couple... Plays, I guess. I wouldn't call any of them Shakespeare, and from what I saw they had a lot more in common with community theater than Broadway, even in New Amsterdam where I discovered my Temple is, in fact, on Broadway. But that's not to say they weren't entertaining. I watched, I laughed, I clapped, I dispensed thanks and hugs and congratulations on making it through the whole show without screwing up.

I got a few invites to come see, uh, adult vignettes by some of those same performers. Yeah, apparently... No, I'm getting ahead of myself.
