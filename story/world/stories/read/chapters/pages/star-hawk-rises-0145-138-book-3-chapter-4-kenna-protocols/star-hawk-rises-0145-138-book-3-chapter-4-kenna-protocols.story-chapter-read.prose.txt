I had sat thinking about the earth as I watched it getting closer as the ship flew towards the orbital station. I suddenly let out a soft giggle as I considered going down to the planet and taming it. However, I was distracted by a thought.

”system, who was the third party that intervened?”

The Federation's Emperor. He spoke to the Twiddles Clan leaders in a private closed server and when the discussion was concluded the Twiddles clan apologised and withdrew. Not a single clansman has come near a Mackenzie owned star system since.

“R… really?” I stammered as I felt my mouth was somewhere near the floor.

The system didn’t respond, and I was quickly pulled from my shock when the alert that the orbital space station was attempting to hail me finally broke through my skull, registering that I was getting close. A moment later, I accepted the call.

“Blossoming Terranova orbital station to the unknown approaching vessel, please identify yourself. Be advised you are in a class-two restricted star system,” a young sounding male voice said.

“Dinnae be daft, boy. Ye can see plain as day my ship’s called the Young Blood. Now, hurry yersel’. Clear me a hangar bay for an auld family member comin’ home out o’ the storm.” I tried not to laugh as I decided to use my old accent from home for a change, instead of continuing to use the American one that I had gotten used to.

“Negative, the Young Blood is not a family registered ship, please identify yourself. Your Scottish accent is very good, though, I have had a few come through over the years with terrible imitations.”

“Very well, I shall identify myself as simply ‘the forgotten.’ I gave a smug smile as I mentally patted myself on the back, but I knew that callsign would be absolutely meaningless as I had just made it up.

“Negative, the Forgotten is not a registered callsign or identity, please identify yourself. Be advised, this is your last chance before the station systems activate the station defence protocols.

”Oh forget this, Blossom activate protocols mommy is home” I snapped at the communications channel. I had started to feel a little irritated by his threat.

“Please identify mommy” came a female voice I recognised as belonging to the AI Blossom.

“How could mommy be identified as anything other than mommy?” I replied, trying not to giggle.

“Mommy could be daddy, but then daddy would be mommy… wouldn’t he?”

“What is going on?” Came the young man's voice, but I ignored him and replied to blossom.

“Mommy is lost in the void, daddy is home by the fire, won't you let mommy in from the darkness?”

“Seriously… wait, what the hell? My systems are being overridden, is this your doing? Are you hacking the station!” His voice was cut off as Blossom took over the communication channel.

“Welcome home, My Lady, long may you rain as the guild mistress of the Blossoming Terranova guild! All hail, Kenna Mackenzie is home.”

The next second, a route appeared on my navigation systems, directing me to a large VIP priority hangar. I turned the ship slightly and headed towards it with a big smile on my face.

I wasn't surprised that armed men were waiting at the edge of the hangar when I came in to land. I was less surprised when lockdown was activated, clamping my ship in place, and I felt insulted when only twelve armed and armoured soldiers approached the ship with someone in a uniform I didn’t recognise.

I pulled the helmet off and slid out of the pilot's chair before walking off the bridge without saying a word. I had a scowl on my face. Moments later, Itshu Biyu appeared next to me with a concerned look on her face as she watched me out of the corner of her eye.

“Ever had that feeling like you have come home but someone else is living in your house, and they want to arrest you for being there?” I asked.

“No, can’t say I have ever been welcomed home like that” she replied now giving me an odd look.

“You're about to.” I said as I stepped into the airlock, letting the door cycle closed. I was slightly surprised to find my disciple and four of the disciples from the law department standing in the airlock with me and Itshu Biyu, who had followed me in.

The moment I passed through the other side, I took three steps, then I simply stepped off the moving staircase that had been rolled up beside the door and dropped to the ground. I didn’t smile, but I felt a strong urge to when the other six simply landed next to me, having stepped off the top like I had.

I couldn't keep the slight upward lip twitch when I saw the soldiers hesitate for a moment. The uniformed person, who I could now see, was a man with a captain's insignia, and a name tag that read ‘Williams.’ Continued to walk towards me like he had seen it all before.

Williams stopped half a dozen feet from me, before glancing at a holographic slate hovering a few inches from his open palm, then looked at me. With a smile that oozed confidence in his own authority, he asked.

“Can you please identify yourselves?”

“I am sure the system already has identified me for you” I stated as I stared back at him.

“People have legally changed their names in the past.” He replied without missing a beat.

I raised an eyebrow as I tried to figure out why people would pretend to be me before slowly turning my head to look at the armed escort Williams had following him.

“So you need me to identify myself beyond a shadow of a doubt, tell me Captain Williams do you know blossom?” I finally said to him.

“Everyone knows Blossom, she has been the AI running this orbital station for the past five hundred years.” He snapped back at me

I grinned as I glanced at the ceiling briefly before turning to look the captain in the eye again. “Blossom, where is my baby?” I suddenly asked.

The captain looked confused for a moment before rolling his eyes and replying. “Blossom can only be spoken to—” he snapped his mouth shut as his eyes widened, when blossom spoke over him.

“Baby is in the Blossoming Terranova orbital station museum. It has been maintained and cleaned almost religiously for two thousand years.” Blossom's voice said from speakers around the hangar.

“That's not possible, Blossom only replies to people at enquiry terminals.” He stammered.

I smiled as I stared at him for a moment longer then took a step forward until I was standing a foot from the captain, with each step I spoke a word. “I am Kenna Rowan Mackenzie!”

“So, others have made that claim.” He replied shakily as the holographic slate in his palm shattered into light, his hand dropping to his side.

I smiled before I spoke to blossom again. “Blossom, can you identify me?”

“I have already identified you during our communications, now that I can clearly see you, I have no doubts. Welcome home, Guild Mistress Kenna Mackenzie.”

“That’s not possible, Kenna Mackenzie sacrificed herself for the clan over two thousand years ago! Clan Mistress Clara was there, Kenna is dead!” He screamed.

“Technically Kenna Mackenzie deployed from the Lost Hawk ship in a life pod, it is possible she survived, was rescued by the enemy ship, and has finally returned to us.” Blossom said with a mocking tone in her voice.

“The galactic federation declared her dead!” He screamed back at her.

“Governments have made mistakes in the past,” I replied without blinking

I pivoted a moment later, moving to step past him, but as I did the soldiers shifted like they were not sure if they should stop me or not. They glanced between the captain and me. I watched their hands tighten on the guns, but they didn’t raise them.

“Wait!” He shouted as he turned to face me and opened his hand palm up, letting his holographic slate appear above it again. “Call Clan Mistress Clara Mackenzie” he said while looking at me smugly.

I watched the little screen change to a green phone that shook occasionally. It rang six times before the phone vanished and Clara’s head appeared over his palm, looking up at him.

“Captain Williams? Why are you calling me?” She asked.

“Clan Mistress, I have rung you under the Kenna protocols.” He replied without looking down at her, the smug look still on his face as he stared at me.

Clara didn’t seem to notice, as she wasn't actually looking at him either. I saw a small hand appear momentarily as it waved in dismissal. “I dissolved those protocols a century ago, it's time to accept that Auntie Kenna is lost, I will not be able to ever show her my inventions again. Now, if that is all, captain, good day.”

”Blossom verified this one.” He said calmly.

I saw her head snap up to stare at him as a look of shock crossed her face. “What did you say?”

Captain Williams moved a finger to his palm then flicked it towards me, turning the holographic screen around, so Clara could see me. The moment her eyes landed on me, I simply smiled and gave a small wave.

“Hey Clara, sounds like you're doing well.” I said.

“What the fuck… wait, forget that. First, don’t just stroll in here after two thousand years and just hey me. Second, where the fuck have you been?!” She exploded.

I was about to respond when I paused for a moment, then turned to Itshu Biyu. “Hey, why have we been gone for two thousand years? I’m pretty sure I have only experienced one year.”

I caught the shocked and frozen look on the captain's face that had appeared the moment Clara started yelling, only for her face to match his after I had asked Itshu Biyu my question.

“When we travel through the void between dimensions cultivators follow a path, every cultivator sees or feels a path that is often unique to their bloodline, for instance I know of a cultivator that sees a dirt path in the middle of an endless grass field. Others, such as yourself, may feel wind currents that you follow.” She calmly said before the captain recovered.

“Wait! Are you confirming this is Kenna Mackenzie?” He suddenly asked, his eyes locked on the back of Clara’s head.

Clara hadn’t recovered yet and Itshu Biyu and I were ignoring him, so his question went unanswered. However, Itshu Biyu had passed at his interruption and I had to wave my hand, dismissing him before she continued.

“It is a common belief that if you deviate from this path, you will be lost in the void, likely never to be found again. However, while it is rare, a ship has returned thousands of years later having only experienced moments. It is believed that the path holds time between the dimensions, where the void is out of time. The reason I was not worried when you blew off the path like a rocket is that all the ships that have come back have a common connection. They were going too fast.” She said calmly.

“So we flew blindly through the void and lucked out on hitting a dimension that I happened to know, and we are so late due to a time dilation effect?” I asked, deciding to clarify.

“If you want to explain it simply, in a barbaric way, then yes.” Itshu Biyu replied.

I smiled as I turned back to Clara to finally answer her question. “I’m late because it seems I flew through a time dilation bubble. On the bright side, I'm home now.”

“You're home now,” she echoed before tears started streaming down her face and a huge smile spread across it. “Yes! You are home now… Welcome home Auntie!”
