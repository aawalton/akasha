The warble of his essential soul and being was so violent and jarring that he dropped his book and slid down from his chair to the floor. He felt scoured and gutted, stripped of something precious within him. He awoke as if from fainting, in a cold sweat on the floor, with people yelling in the background.

No. No, she cannot already be at this level! It can’t be true!

But of course she was. Matrazere had been destroyed — already unthinkable power on display.

Whatever had just happened, it was almost certainly a World Spell effect. He sent a query to Central mentally, but there was no response — they were all ‘busy,’ which was unheard of, but they’d been heavily depleted by desertion, so it was hardly a stretch.

Greizer pulled himself off the floor — with more effort than should’ve been necessary — to sit in the chair, blinking, panting, feeling an odd sense of being… diminished, yet also queerly freed. But he knew intrinsically that this was not a freedom he desired as a venerated, empowered elder.

It hit him rather suddenly, just what it was missing from him: the Oaths of the Sage, one of the most closely guarded secrets of the order, known and given only to the greatest, elder devotees… they were just gone, totally stripped out of his spirit and mind.

Impossible.

He went through the ‘thought check’ that verified he could not do such things as pray to deities, or-

A shaking hand came to his mouth as he realized the restrictions weren’t there, and he’d just committed a ‘minor’ sin by praying to a long-dead deity, which would actually be impossible for a sanctified, separated elder like him.

A host of other benefits had gone with it, as the positive parts of the trade that all Sages other than Inquisitors accepted without reservation, being a part of their long, long education. They weren’t generally seen as restrictive.

Suddenly, he felt weak in a way he never had before and was having trouble breathing.

I’ve aged. The Oath’s lifespan benefits…

They were only relevant past a certain mature age, but he’d long passed that.

A feeling came to him, like hell itself had risen and overtaken them, and the order he’d dedicated his entire life to was doomed. Was the World Spell that governed it supplanted? It’s permanent, whatever it is. Only a fool would believe otherwise.
