Wow, when was the last time Chloe managed to beat a guy who actually gave out experience? She couldn’t really remember, and a part of her didn’t care too much at this point. Because what mattered was she totally beat up an Administrator, well, with a lot of help from Sierra too of course, but a win was a win!

Not only did she get a whole bunch of levels at once, Chloe also got a lot of Trait Points too from beating up Administrator P! And she hasn't even nibbled on his brain yet. Well, okay, maybe nibble was a bit of an understatement. She was totally going to stuff that brain in her mouth like it was the last thing she was gonna do.

Well, just like old times, Chloe needed to do a bit of prep work before she could get the brain to be ready to eat. She first got to work separating Administrator P's head from the rest of his body with her bare hands, and after that, a little bit of elbow grease was all she needed to crack that skull open and get to the goodies inside.

"Um… Chloe?"

Chloe turned her head and saw that Sierra looked super uncomfortable, which was totally fair. But like, she wasn't planning on leaving without eating this brain first, so she'd have to work something out.

"Sierra, could you like, just look away for a little bit?" Chloe asked.

Sirra looked at the head Chloe was holding, winced a little, but eventually nodded. "Uh, just tell me when it's safe to look, okay?"

"Kay, sure thing!"

With that, the wolf girl turned her back against Chloe and covered her ears with her hands. Chloe thought that was maybe a bit more dramatic than she expected, but whatever, she had a brain to eat.

When Chloe ate the brain, she felt a rush spread through her entire body, sending a wave of pure satisfaction rushing through her. Ohhh, man. The taste of a brain after sooo long was just totally unbeatable! It was like she was eating buttery lobster, a perfectly juicy steak, and a whole bunch of other fancy, delicious foods all at once. Every bite was an absolute treat, with that oh-so-good flavor she had been missing for ages. Man… that's the stuff.

Chloe licked her fingers clean after she was done. That was a really good brain.

Congratulations on your promotion to Administrator! This promotion grants you expanded access to organizational resources, restricted information systems, and internal databases, alongside the authority to modify system settings, manage access permissions, and approve routine operational requests. Your new position also carries additional responsibilities, including maintaining accurate records, and…

This might be the first time Chloe didn't fully read the description of a trait, because the list of responsibilities was so long it started to make her want to doze off somewhere. And to be honest, this trait was super lame, why would additional responsibilities be considered part of a privilege? That just didn't make sense to Chloe.

Kinda wonder if this trait actually does anything, but ugh, it looks sooo boring. Chloe thought.

The good news was, however, that there was a bunch of other Traits Chloe could buy with her stockpile of Trait Points. She had twenty in total to spend, and she planned on using a lot of them, or all of them, at the same time. Because uh, she wasn't sure if she'd get another chance to spend these points if more dangerous administrators showed up.

Chloe scrolled through the list of traits she could buy, and to be honest, she wasn't sure if she was supposed to see so many options to choose from. Normally all the traits would fit in a single screen, but Chloe kept scrolling and kept seeing more and more traits. She could buy Traits ranging from funnier sounding ones like Jelly Bones and Unblinking Eyes to much cooler ones like Divine Strength and Lord of Flames.

But, even though there were all those traits to choose from. Eventually, Chloe found one that looked like the best of the bunch, and probably because it was the most expensive of the bunch.

[Immortal Soul (Cost: 20 Trait Points)]

Your soul is an eternal existence that cannot be erased, destroyed, or removed from reality by any means. You are now immune to most attacks that can control, alter, corrupt, or destroy your mind and soul. Additionally, if your physical body is completely destroyed, your soul will remain intact, allowing you to move without a physical form as a ghost.

Well, protection from being erased from reality might be a bit excessive, but considering the fact that Z had an attack that could do exactly that, there was always the possibility that someone else in Divinity Inc. could hold the same kind of power. So, having this trait as insurance just in case the worst happened, seemed like a pretty smart choice. And hey, it looked like she could even become a ghost if things really went south.

[Trait Purchased: Immortal Soul]

[Trait Points Remaining: 0]

Anyway, with that Trait purchased, Chloe decided it would be a good time to check on Sierra and figure out what they were going to do next. "Hey, Sierra, you can look back now! I'm done."

Sierra hesitantly approached as she glanced between Chloe and their surroundings. "Um... Chloe? So, what are we going to do now?"

"Hmmmm... honestly? I'm not really sure yet." She shrugged, then flashed Sierra a smile. "But hey, I do know someone who might be able to help us out!"

"O-Oh? Who?"

"Cloaky, let Mr. White out!"

The moment those words left her mouth, her cloak stirred.

The cloak began to expand, growing far beyond what its size should have allowed. The fabric twisted, creating a gaping opening within its folds, and from that the inside of Cloaky came the sound of hooves striking the ground.

A moment later, a skeletal horse emerged from within the cloak. Bluish flames flickered along its exposed bones, dancing around its powerful legs without consuming them. Despite its rather intimidating appearance, the horse carried itself with an almost regal grace, its skeletal head turning toward Chloe as though it had been waiting a very long time to see her again.

"MR. WHITE!"

Chloe immediately threw her arms around the horse's bony neck, hugging it. "Ahhh, it's so good to have you back! You have NO idea how much I've missed you!"

Mr. White responded with a low, rumbling sound that was somewhere between a whinny and a supernatural growl. He lowered his skull and gently nuzzled against Chloe, his bluish flames flickering a little more brightly as though he were just as happy to see her.

Chloe giggled and hugged him tighter. "Ahahaha! Yeah, yeah, I missed you too!"

A short distance away, Sierra watched the reunion with wide eyes. She hesitated before taking a small step forward, then another. Her gaze wandered nervously over Mr. White's exposed ribs and flaming skull, and she clasped her hands together a little more tightly.

"U-Um... hello again, Mr. White, it's been a while"

The horse turned its skull toward her, and Sierra froze. But before she could decide whether she ought to step back, Mr. White leaned down toward her. His movements were slow and deliberate, giving her plenty of time to react. Then, he extended his long skull and gave her a little boop right on the top of her head.

"Eh?" Sierra muttered out loud.

Chloe, meanwhile, burst into laughter. "Oh my gosh, he likes you!"

"R-Really...?" Sierra asked timidly, her cheeks coloring as she tentatively reached out to touch Mr. White's bony muzzle.

Mr. White leaned into Sierra's hand, his muzzle pressing affectionately against her palm. Of course he did! He could tell that Sierra was a nice girl, and Mr. White had excellent taste in friends. Unfortunately, as much as Chloe would've loved to spend the rest of the day watching Sierra pet her favorite bony boy, they did have places to be.

With a hop, Chloe swung herself onto Mr. White's back. "C'mon, Sierra! Up you go!"

Sierra approached hesitantly, eyeing the skeletal horse. Climbing onto a flaming skeleton was apparently a little more intimidating than she'd anticipated. But eventually, she grabbed hold of Mr. White's back and tried to pull herself up… and this didn't work out quite the way anybody hoped. So, Sierra tried again, and that also didn't work.

Chloe watched for a moment, because this was almost too adorable. But, I should probably help Sierra out by now.

She leaned down and offered Sierra both hands, guiding her foot into place before giving her a firm tug. After a brief scramble, Sierra finally managed to haul herself onto Mr. White's back, settling behind Chloe with a relieved sigh.

"There! See? Easy peasy!" Chloe said, giving her a reassuring pat.

Sierra didn't look like she was entirely convinced that easy peasy was the right term for what she'd just experienced, but she wasn't about to argue. Instead, she carefully wrapped her arms around Chloe's waist.  "Um... Chloe? Where are we going?"

Chloe shrugged. Honestly, she hadn't given it much thought beyond the general idea of finding someone who could help them.

"Hey, Mr. White. Can you sense someone really powerful nearby? Someone who feels kinda like me, except way stronger? That's my mom, by the way."

Mr. White began surveying their surroundings, his skull turning from side to side as the blue flames flickering around his bones brightened. After like, a couple fo minutes, he turned his head and nodded.

"Great! Let's go, Mr. White! Take us to Mom!"

As Chloe said that, she pointed dramatically toward the distance. Mr. White promptly turned in the opposite direction and galloped away.
