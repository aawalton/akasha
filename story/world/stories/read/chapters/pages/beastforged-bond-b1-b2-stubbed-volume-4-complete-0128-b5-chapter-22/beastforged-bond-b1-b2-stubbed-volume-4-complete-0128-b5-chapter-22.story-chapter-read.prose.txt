Breathing Tissue and Respiring Flesh. Both were fairly common Body Tempering techniques among the Mikoniv and the reason their race could advance through the ranks in the first place. Unlike humans with Worlds or the Caldera with their spirit seas, the Mikoniv had no way to store ether. Not only that, they didn’t have the equivalent of the weave either.

Instead, the Mikoniv created nodes inside their bodies. The maritime race lived in oceans flowing with water-attuned ether. To move swiftly through water, they learned to control water ether from a young age, allowing them to escape the greatest aquatic threats. It was a necessity, and they took advantage of it.

Their ether control increased drastically until one day, one of their prodigies did something that was both mad and prodigious. He was frail, but his control of ether was even greater than that of those decades older than him, so he used what he was best at to improve his greatest weakness. He forced mana into his limbs, something many had died trying to do.

The scrolls did not go into detail, but the amount of detail revolving around possible failures and problems that could arise if the user’s ether control was insufficient told me all I had to know.

Focusing back on the scrolls lying before me on the bed, I frowned.

The Mikoniv created nodes inside their bodies to strengthen their physiques, although the strengthening part didn’t happen just like that. If anything, the nodes were merely the first step in a long list of steps, for they only absorbed or distributed ether.

It required a set of different nodes to create a system that allowed the Mikoniv to absorb ether into their bodies and distribute it precisely into their flesh and bones, strengthening them physically. There were a lot of conditions and restrictions, much information that could be useful to the Mikoniv, whose anatomy appeared to differ quite a lot depending on where they came from, but the specifics were not all that important to me.

I didn’t have to know how many nodes a Mikoniv needed to advance past their ‘mortal’ shell or how to safely remove a Stage-1 node. While the part about upgrading nodes sounded interesting, I didn’t need that knowledge just yet.

What I needed was an in-depth understanding of Stage-1 nodes. That was the bare minimum if I wanted to create one inside myself as well.

There was a little more curious information about the Mikoniv, but it mostly revolved around Body Tempering up to the Adept Rank. After that, they seemed to do something else, which appeared to be the reason they could live on land as well. That too could come in handy if I ever wanted to reverse the effect and learn how to live in an ocean, though I highly doubted it would ever come that far.

The days flew by without any results.

Ether was too unstable to be forced into a static form. Deliberately condensing ether inside the weave, compressing it again and again with brute force until the formless radiance thickened into luminous liquid was possible, and I was already fairly good at it, but that wasn’t enough yet.

Locating key intersections in the weave was time-consuming, because some intersections didn’t appear to be anything special until I realized belatedly that I actually used them the most. Interestingly enough, the intersections were tougher than the weave’s regular channels. The ether flow naturally slowed and branched smoothly there, creating the perfect place to start with. And that I did – or tried to.

Once the liquefied ether settled near the key intersections, the real process started. First, I pulled fire-attuned ether out of a Blazing Gate and guided it toward the intersection. Split into multiple streams, I circulated them in impossibly fine spirals, forcing the liquid ether to rotate in place. First slowly, then faster as the cycles intensified, the liquid ether spread outward.

As the rotation reached its peak, the outer edge of the liquid abruptly stabilized. A membrane formed around it, impossibly thin, translucent, and luminous. It resembled one of the ancient coffee filters my parents had used when we still lived in the Bastion’s poor, ether-scarce sector.

Within the membrane, the liquefied ether never stopped moving. It endlessly circulated as the filter refined everything that passed through it. Turbulent ether smoothed and concentrated as soon as it was pulled into the filter, and ambient ether was drawn closer, pressing against my skin, seeping into my body in an attempt to reach the filter.

While not a node and most definitely not what I initially planned to form, it was…something. It worked similarly to the Stage-1 Gathering Node I wanted to create, yet it was both more and less than that at the same time. The gathering effect was nowhere near the efficacy promised by the scrolls, while the filtering process was something that should have required a different node entirely.

Once I removed the fire-attuned ether from the filter, it quickly became apparent that the liquid ether filter wouldn’t last long. The membrane was already falling apart, and I didn’t doubt for a second that it wouldn’t last another twenty-four hours.

For my tenth attempt, the first of which succeeded, that wasn’t good. It also used the ether stored within an Unblemished Gate. All of it had been compressed to form the ether filter, which now slowly absorbed ether and filtered it into the weave.
