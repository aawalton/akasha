Days blended into weeks, which turned into months as Kyle and C.H.A.D.D. continued their deliveries.  The War Chieftains were spread across the planet, and the ancestral paths he was forced to walk didn’t always follow the most direct routes between the larger villages.  Along the way, he also stopped to help many of the smaller settlements along the paths that had been adversely impacted by the presence of the hodags.

Even the presence of a handful of D Grade hodags was enough to cause severe damage to the towns without a C Grade present, and Kyle found himself providing medical aid to injured guards and helping to regrow vital farmland and infrastructure.  It felt good making a difference for these smaller populations, and as news of his work spread, the smaller settlements met him with anticipation and excitement as he journeyed across the planet.

The same could not be said, unfortunately, for the War Chieftains.  Once the aftermath of his encounter with the C Grade hodag variant was discovered, the derision he received from Svynar was no more.  Instead, he was treated as every bit the dangerous creature he’d proven to be.  War Chieftains met him in their full combat equipment, often accompanied by a retinue of their finest D Grade guards.  Interactions were quick, brusque, and Kyle was sent on his way, often not even provided an evening’s accommodation.  It would have been easy to be upset by the development, given the contributions he was making to orcish society, but he didn’t fault them.  After all, he’d killed a lot of hodags.

The creatures were ubiquitous, and the more he encountered, the more confident Kyle was that they hadn’t arrived by mistake.  Most commonly, he’d encounter a nest with a handful of D Grades and one or two C Grades, though the most challenging environments along the ancestral path each had a powerful variant.  Unfortunately for the powerful monsters, none of the other variants countered him the same way the first had.

One of the creatures had aquatic adaptations, living inside a large lake that the ancestral path crossed.  While it was effective in the water, he was able to easily get it tangled up through propagating infused kelp and seaweed.  Once its movements were bound, it lacked the defensive means to stand up to Parasitic Resonance and fell in short order.

Another interesting one appeared in the middle of a shaded wood, using odd concepts related to shadows and stealth.  Unfortunately for it, between C.H.A.D.D.’s C Grade scanning capabilities, Synaptic Barbs, and Kyle’s own domain with Aura Force, the creature never had an opportunity to launch its surprise attack, even with much of its aura suppressed.  This one was nearly bisected by a combination from an upgraded Model: Shrike, and finished off mercifully with Parasitic Resonance.

An area made up of magnetically floating platforms had a hodag with two sets of large, bat-like wings that allowed it to fly nimbly through the air and navigate the floating platforms with ease.  Coupled with its ability to launch focused blasts of air, Kyle could have seen it being a very troublesome opponent for somebody who was trying to follow the path the traditional way.  However, he could fly.  Chasing the beast down in the air proved to be a good test of his finer control with Aura Force, one he passed without too much trouble.  Once he disabled the wings on one side, the fight was all but finished.

The final variant he’d encountered was in a volcanic zone, and was likely the best counter to him outside the first.  The beast had strong adaptations to fire and heat, which created an oppressive aura that burned away anything that got close.  Even Parasitic Resonance would be overwhelmed after only short connections, forcing Kyle to rely on a select few fire-resistant plants and compressed attacks from Aura Force to gain the upper hand.  If this creature shared the durability of the first he’d encountered, it would have been a match Kyle didn’t think he could win.  With its relatively lacking defenses, their fight turned into a battle of attrition that scorched and shattered the mountainside, with Kyle coming out on top.

Alongside his fights came levels, and even with the diminishing returns he’d reached Level 75 in his World Tree Tender class, and was Level 59 in Unbroken Survivor.  With only one level to go before being able to evolve World Tree Tender to D Grade and get his next skill upgrades, he’d been confident in the next couple of hodag encounters pushing him beyond the threshold.  However, none came.  Usually, nests dotted the paths toward all the villages, but this one was suspiciously empty.  Particularly as the last village he had to visit before returning to Duroc was Mangalica’s.

“You’re sure you don’t spot anything on your sensors, C.H.A.D.D.?” Kyle asked as they crested a frozen crag, looking down at the large village nestled in the foothills.

[FOR THE TWELFTH TIME, DR. MAYHEW, YES.  THERE ARE CERTAINLY SOME OTHER CREATURES NEARBY, BUT NOTHING THAT APPEARS HOSTILE, AND I CAN CONFIDENTLY SAY THAT NONE ARE HODAGS.]

“I’m sorry,” Kyle said with a sigh, watching his breath turn to white mist in the air.  “It’s just been oddly quiet the last week or so.”

[IT HAS BEEN ODD,] the drone agreed.  [YOU STILL SUSPECT THE HODAGS ARE RELATED TO THIS MANGALICA?]

“I do,” Kyle said with a nod.  “The timing felt way too coincidental for the hodags to start attacking all these settlements shortly after Mangalica took power, and the fact that there aren’t any wild nests near his territory makes it even more likely.”  He gestured back to the frozen peaks before continuing.  “Hell, I was half-expecting to fight a variant somewhere in the mountains along this path.  The environment was certainly as dangerous as any of the other zones that created variants.”

[I DO NOT DISAGREE WITH YOU, DR. MAYHEW.  HOWEVER, I WOULD ENCOURAGE YOU TO KEEP AN OPEN MIND TO OTHER POSSIBILITIES AS WELL.]

“What else could it be?” Kyle asked.

[IF REPORTS OF THIS MANGALICA’S STRENGTH ARE TO BE BELIEVED, WOULD YOU NOT AGREE THAT THEY COULD HAVE HANDLED ANY HODAGS WITHIN THEIR TERRITORY DIRECTLY?]

Kyle paused, considering C.H.A.D.D.’s idea.  It made sense, and it bothered him that his mind hadn’t gone there at all.  Sometimes it’s easier to try and find a villain, I suppose.

“We’ll see what we find when we get to the village,” he finally said, beginning their descent from the frigid peaks.

As they did, he double checked his nav bracelet, which still didn’t have any updates.  The auction for Kertan Six was today, and Marcus was one of the final contenders.  It looked as though there weren’t too many interested parties, but he wouldn’t know the outcome until after it was all said and done.  Communicating from the auction table was strictly prohibited by the Collective, as passing information inside or outside could lead to an unfair advantage.  They’d just have to wait for an update from Marcus, and do their best to focus in the meantime.

Though Kyle could have simply flown down the mountainside to reach the village, he chose to walk, showing deliberate respect to the traditions that Mangalica had supposedly flaunted during their rise to power.  Once down the mountain, he walked the winding path between fenced-off areas of farmland, with the orcs tending their livestock and crops pausing to look at him as he walked by.  He gave nods of acknowledgement, but continued on, only stopping when two D Grade guards crossed their halberds, blocking his path.

Kyle raised an eyebrow at them, as the larger of the two stepped forward.  “You must be the Courier.  By order of the War Chieftain, you are not permitted entrance to the village.”

“I have a delivery to make, in accordance with the ancient orcish customs,” Kyle said evenly, meeting the orc’s eyes.  He didn’t flex his aura, but based on the look of concern on both the guards’ faces, he knew his reputation preceded him.

“Those are our orders, sir,” the second guard said, unable to fully control a quiver in his voice.

“And I have my orders,” Kyle replied cooly.  “I’m not looking for trouble, but I do have a job to finish.  If I’m not allowed inside the city, is the War Chieftain willing to meet me out here?”

“That’s…” the second guard began, before the first interrupted.

“Asking him to make an appearance outside the gates is highly irregular,” the first guard managed.

“No more irregular than telling a Courier he can’t complete his delivery to set up the Tournament of Ascension,” Kyle countered.

“Nevertheless, sir, we can’t let you pass,” the second guard said, seemingly finding his resolve.

Kyle sighed, raising his hand.  “Then I guess there’s nothing to be done.”

The two orc guards visibly relaxed, only to have their feet swept out from under them with a wave of force, while immense pressure pinned them firmly to the ground.

“What are you…” the second guard struggled to say as Kyle walked past him.

“I’m making my delivery,” Kyle said, not letting up on the pressure until he was almost out of Aura Force’s range.  He heard a general alarm being sounded, and ignored it.  He was going to finish his job, and see exactly what kind of person this Mangalica was.

A few other D Grade guards attempted to intercept him on his way to the central palace, but a quick use of Aura Force subdued each before they could do much.  As he was on the cusp of mid-tier C Grade now, the low and mid-tier D Grades just didn’t have the personal power needed to make him stop.

Finally, as he approached the palace C.H.A.D.D. flashed a warning, just as he felt a spike of power through Auric Perception.  The front door flew open, and the largest orc Kyle had ever seen stepped through the open doors.  The four meter tall C Grade resembled a wild boar as much as anything else, with thick dark bristles covering its exposed head, arms, and legs.  Four massive tusks jutted out of its lower jaw, and it carried a colossal blade a meter wide and three long.

Beady red eyes fixed on Kyle as its aura flared, and the blade’s tip was leveled at him as mana surged around the orc.  It spoke, its voice deep and resonant, carrying with it an undertone of barely-contained violence.

“You have some nerve coming into my city uninvited, Courier.”

The last word was spoken with a sneer, which Kyle promptly ignored.

“You must be Mangalica,” Kyle said.  “As a point of fact, I was invited, at least according to the statutes set forth by the council of War Chieftains some odd centuries back.  That’s the whole reason I’m here, after all.  You’re welcome to challenge those customs after the tournament, but for now I have a delivery to make.”

With that, he withdrew the final scroll from his pack and tossed it to the orc.  With a flash of movement, the scroll was sliced in half by the massive blade, a piece landing on either side of his feet.

“I have no need for your scroll,” Mangalica snarled.  “And no need for your petty tournament.”

“That’s your decision to make,” Kyle said with a shrug.  “I’ve done my part.  Thank you for your hospitality.”

Kyle could see the muscles tensing beneath Mangalica’s thick hide, and almost expected their interaction to devolve to a fight right then and there.  He’d naturally already used Identify on the orc, and to no surprise he was specialized in physical combat, with a surprising amount of Willpower and Perception for somebody so otherwise physically oriented.  Still, it was an encounter he felt comfortable escaping, if nothing else.

Fortunately, Mangalica lowered his blade a moment later, giving a violent gesture with his snout toward the village door.  “Begone.  And if you know what’s good for you, leave this planet.  If we meet again, the stones will run crimson with your blood.”

Not needing any further prompting, Kyle left.  He kept Synaptic Barbs and Auric Perception trained on the monstrous orc until he was outside the city limits, then took off toward Duroc’s village at full speed.  He’d need to debrief with his friend and see how Duroc wanted to proceed, because things were getting dangerous fast.

He felt C.H.A.D.D. puzzling through a problem as he took to the sky, but before he could ask the drone any questions he was interrupted by a notification on his nav bracelet.

Kyle,

We won the auction.  Some details to work out on the process of acquisition, but it’s ours.  Once you wrap up that job, come see me and we’ll get a plan together for the Earthlings.

Marcus
