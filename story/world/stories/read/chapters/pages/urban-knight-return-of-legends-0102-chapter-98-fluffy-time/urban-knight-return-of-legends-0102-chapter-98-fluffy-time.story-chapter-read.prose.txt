After gathering what seemed like average harpy materials—with nothing out of the ordinary popping out—everyone regrouped at the center of the clearing. With the flying menace completely vanquished, it seemed that no other beast would interrupt them until they left the area, giving them enough time to take a short but well-needed rest.

As they talked about the last fight, Ascalon peered at his friends intently, scratching his chin thoughtfully. Both Lyra and Lilithra needed to train their upper torsos a bit more in order to properly wield their weapons. Their arms needed some more muscle, and they would benefit from strengthening their core too—in the end, maybe they just needed more training in general.

“Keep your weapons at the ready, Lilithra, Lyra.” Ascalon suddenly spoke, garnering the group's attention. Selorien smirked mischievously, perhaps realizing his intentions, while the two girls briefly exchanged glances.

“Huh… alright!” Lilithra quickly replied, summoning back the weapon from her inventory. She grabbed it tight before resting it against her shoulder, slightly grimacing. The weight of the halberd was probably hurting her a bit, but she had followed Ascalon’s request without a second thought, which made him happy.

“Ow…” Lyra winced as her huge scythe appeared, yet didn’t complain either. She rested the base against the ground, and hugged the large handle against her chest. She wouldn’t benefit from simply grabbing onto it like a pole, but Ascalon decided to cut her a bit of slack since she was the physically weakest among them. She would get enough exercise once they started walking, anyway!

After that, they kept chatting for a couple more minutes, before finally deciding to continue their dungeon delving. They set out once more for the distant peaks, climbing through a more stable, yet arguably more strenuous, path.

It didn’t take long for Lyra to start heaving, the massive sight proving to be quite the cumbersome equipment for her frail, little arms. Still, she did her best to carry it, trying to not drag it on the ground—perhaps, she had noticed what Ascalon wanted from her. The knight would encourage her by patting her head, which seemed to, somehow, fuel her with energy to keep going.

At the same time, Lilithra was taking a more serious approach, switching the halberd from hand to hand every couple steps, perhaps trying to replicate weight-lifting. Sweat glistened on her skin, and soft panting could be heard every time she shifted the weapon’s weight, but she wasn’t complaining in the slightest—she was doing her best!

They kept marching on with determined steps, and soon, the temperature around them began to drop. Gradually, the surroundings started to change from the rocky, rough cliffs to a pleasant, snowy white. Scattering snowflakes and a cold gust of wind greeted them as they finally arrived at their destination.

“We’re here!” Lilithra declared with joy in her voice, raising her arms high, albeit with some difficulty. The halberd’s cloth danced slightly over the chilly environment, while Lyra used her scythe as a crutch as she let out a deep sigh. Then, she started to fiddle with her network interface, a more appropriate set of clothing appearing over her shivering body.

She now wore a big, puffy pastel coat, thick white cotton pants, a pink muffler around her neck, and a cute, pink wool cap that had the shape of her cat ears at the top. She now looked more chubby and rounder, accentuating her adorableness by a huge margin!

At the sight, Lilithra and Selorien followed suit! Lilithra changed into thick, black pants, with red gloves covering her hands, a set of earmuffs, and a pink, oversized hoodie that looked comfy and warm. Ascalon remembered she had worn similar clothes the last time they had been there. Seeing her face partly hidden by her hood brought him a sense of nostalgia, causing him to smile gently.

Selorien wore a black, puffy jacket, baggier blue pants, and a gray wool cap with what seemed like bear ears poking out from the top. Ascalon had the feeling that the elf had worn something similar the last time, too, but the ears were likely different.

Lilithra giggled at the sight as she rested the huge halberd against her shoulder, before casting a glance to her surroundings. Everyone stared in different directions as the wind coldly caressed their cheeks—except for Lyra, who tugged shyly at Ascalon’s shirt.

“Uhm, aren’t you cold, Ascalon?” She timidly asked, looking up at the knight with her big, brown eyes. Ascalon chuckled as he patted her head once more, ruffling her wool cap slightly.

“I have trained my body to withstand high and low temperatures—though I admit I prefer the cold.” Ascalon explained as he reminisced about their time in Starugnia. Compared to the harsh desert heat, the chilling atmosphere was rather relaxing!

Lyra nodded in understanding at his words, her cheeks mildly flushed by the cold. Lilithra placed a hand on her shoulder, smiling reassuringly as she spoke.

“One can’t help but worry about Ascalon, huh?” She said in a gentle voice, gazing at the knight. Ascalon simply chuckled, with Selorien smirking at his side. It was indeed amusing that they would always worry about him, yet he relished these moments.

While Lyra giggled softly at Lilithra’s remark, Ascalon perked up with interest, his eye focusing on the white snowscape. The rest quickly caught on, the air around them shifting to a more serious, prepared demeanor.

“They’re coming?” Selorien asked as he concentrated on the distance, his elven sight catching glimpses of fast-moving figures.

“Yes—wolves.” Ascalon replied with a gleeful grin plastered on his face, his eye sparkling with eagerness and excitement. Lyra side-eyed him and tilted her head in confusion, unaware of what the knight was currently feeling.

In summary: dogs! Dogs were coming! And Ascalon couldn’t be any more happier!

As he rejoiced inwardly, the iconic howl of the beasts reverberated around them, as many four-legged shapes began to emerge from behind trees and snow mounds. It was quite the big pack—perhaps bordering the thirties–their furtive, intelligent eyes firmly glued to the group.

Surprisingly enough, a few of the wolves flinched at the sight of Ascalon—perhaps survivors from their last encounter. But still, something was amiss. The knight squinted as he looked at every wolf, one by one, noticing most of them seemed to be injured. Some were limping, while others had streaks of blood covering their fur.

It didn’t take long before the rest also noticed the creatures’ current state, causing Lilithra to speak up with curiosity.

“Why are they hurt…?” She asked as she tilted her head, frowning in both confusion and concern. Ascalon let out a thoughtful ‘hmm’ as he expanded his senses, his aura reaching further and further. Then, he snapped to attention, his brows furrowing as he replied.

“It seems there is some territorial conflict going on.” Ascalon said with a worried tone, his gaze shifting toward his friends. “I can sense a battle between wolves and other beasts in that direction.” He added, pointing past the injured canines.

It seemed that these wolves had approached to stop them from interrupting the fight, or perhaps in order to protect their companions from an unexpected new enemy. Ascalon pondered the matter briefly, before Lilithra raised her voice in surprise.

“It’s just like the scientists said! They’re like ‘normal’ animals… of course they would fight over territory, too!” She stated with utmost certainty, puffing out her chest in proudness. Lyra let out an impressed ‘oh~’ as she gazed at Lilitha and then at the wolves.

They had told Lyra before about their conversation with the scientists—when they retold to her their test-taking adventures—but it was probably the first time Lyra could witness something like that firsthand. After all, most adventurers probably didn’t pay any mind to the dungeon monsters’ behaviour, focusing only on slaying them.

Suddenly, everyone’s train of thought was interrupted by a loud yawn coming from Selorien, which garnered both his friends and the wolves attention.

“Uh, sorry…” The elf smirked as he scratched his head with the hilt of one of his daggers, his carefree demeanor easing up the tension slightly.

“So, uh, what do we do?” Selorien asked, casting a bored glance toward the beasts. Some of the wolves were growling and glaring at them, but were clearly reluctant to engage in a fight—most likely, they were worried about their brethren.

Lilithra’s lips curved downward in a saddened gesture, while Lyra simply fidgeted with her scythe, awaiting her friends’ decision. Ascalon groaned as he tilted his head, feeling conflicted about the whole ordeal.

On one hand, he wanted his friends to fight and gain experience—both literally and figuratively—and knew they would benefit from battling the wolves again. On the other hand, he loved dogs, plain and simple! But he wasn’t only worried about that, but also intrigued by the couple of presences fighting the rest of the pack.

They were most likely snow-dwelling beasts, and bigger than the wolves by far. Judging by how many canines were already injured, the battle was clearly leaning in the unknown creature’s favor. With a final groan, Ascalon faced his friends as he spoke up.

“Can we help them, leader?” He asked with a serious expression, his words directed directly at Lilithra. She was caught off-guard by Ascalon’s sudden request, jolting in place as her eyes opened wide.

“Helping… the wolves?” She said in a confused yet slightly curious tone, garnering thoughtful ‘hmms’ from both Lyra and Selorien.

“Hey… why not?” The elf asked with a playful smile spreading across his cheeks, his elven eyes darting across the landscape. Lyra nodded shyly, though resolutely, a soft voice escaping her lips.

“I would like to help them!” She exclaimed with eagerness, perhaps feeling excited about Ascalon’s proposal. It was not everyday that they would decide something so out of the ordinary, after all. Lilithra nodded as he crossed her arms, hugging the halberd against her chest.

“Hmm… seems we’re all in agreement! Alright!” She chirped with clear elation in her voice, her eyes sparkling with resolution. While it may have sounded like a strange choice, she wasn’t too keen on attacking dogs either! Even if they were quite the dangerous ‘dogs.’

Ascalon sighed softly, smiling at his friends with relief, and glad his idea hadn’t been rejected. Still, he could’ve seen that coming if only he had thought about it a bit more—they were his friends, and he knew they would be on-board with that kind of plan anyway!

If anything, their kindness was one of their biggest traits.

As his chest filled with pleasant feelings, Lilithra cleared her throat, her gaze resting on Ascalon. “So, uhm… h—how do we help them?” She asked with clear nervousness, her eyes shifting toward the more-than-unfriendly pack of wolves.

“I don’t think they will simply let us pass.” Selorien added, causing Ascalon to realize what they meant, while Lyra nodded shyly at their side. That was indeed a problem!

While their feelings were in the right place, how could they convey them to the wolves? Perhaps there could be a way!

“Let me handle this!” Ascalon declared with confidence, walking toward the wolves. The canines showed their fangs and slowly surrounded the knight, while his friends raised their guards just in case. Under their expectant gazes, Ascalon sighed softly, and then spoke up.

“Come at me, good boys and girls!” He called out with a firm voice, using his [Heroic Challenge] skill to taunt the whole pack. Unable to resist the urge to attack, the wolves lunged at him with bloodshot eyes and unbridled fury.

Ascalon fell to the ground under their relentless assault, and the beasts swarmed him in a matter of seconds! At the sight, his friends gasped in a mix of shock and panic.

“Ascalon!” Lilithra cried out in worry and anguish, prepared to jump in and help him. But Selorien stopped her, placing a hand before her. She looked at him with a frown, ready to scold him, but the elf’s dead-pan expression made her hesitate.

“Look at him, Lil…” Selorien began, prompting Lilithra to raise an eyebrow in suspicion. She looked at Ascalon, tilting her head.

“He’s… he’s enjoying it?!” Lyra suddenly blurted out, and everyone focused on Ascalon’s face amidst the multitude of wolves surrounding him.

It was an expression of pure, unadulterated bliss, only achievable by being surrounded by fluffy, cute canine creatures.

Their worry and panic disappeared immediately, and the three of them sighed in unison, resigned.
