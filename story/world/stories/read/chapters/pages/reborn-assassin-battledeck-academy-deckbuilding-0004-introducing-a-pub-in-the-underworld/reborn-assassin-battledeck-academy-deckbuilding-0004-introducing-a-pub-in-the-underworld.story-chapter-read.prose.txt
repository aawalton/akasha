Yesterday, I released a very special book to the masses (the horror!) in print, ebook, KU, and audible.

It's called A Pub in the Underworld, and it is about a warrior named Sylas who dies and is given a deed to a pub in... you guessed it, the underworld.

The pub comes with a magical cat, and as our hero settles in, a new message comes to him that changes the cozy nature of the afterlife in just a few words:

[You have 89 days until the invasion...]

What follows is a slice-of-life fantasy LitRPG adventure as Sylas navigates the Underworld, grows his power through ale sells and demon-hunting, and tries to solve the invasion message.

I have enjoyed posting to Royal Road and in the future, I'll post new stories here first. A Pub in the Underworld was written before I got back into it, but I think some of you may enjoy it. This one is very different from Reborn Assassin, mainly because I like challenging myself. I've written between 70-90 books at this point (really!), so I try to do things my own way, but also in a way that I think the audience will enjoy.

The final reason this launch is so important to me is because it's through Podium, and if it does well, it will be in bookstores this fall.

Yes, A GoblinWrangler (or in this case, Harmon Cooper), in bookstores. This is why I'm telling you about it! Otherwise I wouldn't interrupt the Reborn Assassin story (which continues tomorrow with a fun chapter, trust me).
