Holy.

Fuck.

Another Unchained Sin careened down the dune behind the first, sliding through Allium's scattered bloody remains. I didn’t even take a second to see what it was before I started sprinting in the opposite direction.

Nuzzle squealed in alarm as I snatched him up and carried him under my arm.

Only to be met by a third—a fucking third—Unchained Sin.

It stumbled on the dune to my left and rolled the rest of the way down. Some sort of kangaroo-humanoid hybrid with an Amber aura. It appeared to have one animal leg, one human, which made its movements awkward and slow. A metal collar and chain hung from around its neck.

The chains were broken. Literal chains… That was something I hadn't seen on any other Unchained so far.

I passed by the beast, giving it a wide birth, as it stumbled to get up.

I had to make it to the lighthouse. Maybe I could find safe haven within. It wasn’t that far away… Okay it was pretty damn far, but it was my only option.

I risked a glance back. The gorilla and other soul were still occupied, tossing pieces of Allium in all directions. One of them breathed out flames, but I couldn't tell which. I held down the sick roiling in my stomach.

The gorilla had a blue Will. I hadn't had time to examine it, but blue was Sapphire. That… That's Jericho's rank. I tried to remember what Calliope had said about the Will rankings. How high above me was that? Doesn't matter. I'm so screwed.

My leg caught on something and I slammed into the ground face first. Rough grains of sand filled my mouth and nose. I rolled over and coughed, but whatever snared my leg still had it pinned. An inhuman orange hand of shadow and mist protruded from the sand like a zombie bursting from its grave, long fingers wrapped around my ankle. I kicked at it with little effect. It only squeezed harder, cutting of the circulation to my foot, nerves going numb.

The kangaroo hybrid limped closer, a human hand held up in a vice-like grip, mimicking the shadow that trapped me.

It was only a few feet away. Behind it, the other rampaging souls appeared to take notice. One breathed a cone of weird yellow fire into the air, but the gorilla's hand wrapped around its neck cutting off the flame.

They started wrestling with each other. A limb flew into the air and landed in the water with a splash. The gorilla was clearly going to win that tussle. And it wouldn't take long…

Fuck.

Luckily, I had a trick of my own.

I used Aero Shift. My body accelerated forward, leaving the shadow hand gripping nothing but air. Distance—that’s all I needed. I Aero Shifted again, using precious Volition.

I scrambled to my feet and ran toward the carved stairs in the cliff side. Another shadow hand exploded from the ground before me, blocking the path. I summoned my scythe and swung it at the phantom limb in one smooth motion.

It did nothing. Instead, the hand swiped at me. I rolled to the side. Nuzzle squeaked as I lost hold of him and he tumbled back toward the kangaroo monster.

It lifted a large foot over the lizard casting a shadow down over him.

"Nuzzle! No!" I shouted.

The foot slammed down, making a crater as sand burst into the air from the impact.

Another scream was caught in my throat. First Allium… And now…

The Sin yelped in pain, spinning around. And there was Nuzzle, teeth clenching onto its tail. Alive!

I Aero Shifted forward and sliced the kangaroo across the chest. Blood sprayed as it flailed backwards. Not dead, but at least injured.

I snatched up Nuzzle again and made for the stairs. In the distance the gorilla let out a shriek of victory.

Stone steps flew beneath me, three at a time, the rough, sharp edges cut at my bare feet, but I didn’t care. I couldn’t care.

The stairs were terrifyingly narrow, but the anxiety of being that close to falling was overshadowed by urgency.

I climbed the stairs faster than I’d ever moved in my life, yet the top of the cliff seemed miles away. The sound of limbs scratching on stone was never far behind me.

Nor was the unearthly roar of the gorilla monstrosity.

It reminded me too much of the night I became a Reaper.

Heart already pounding in my chest, my breath quickened, nearly hyperventilating.

I slipped, toes hitting an uneven stair the wrong way, and fell to a knee. My shin split on the edge of a stone. I rolled to the side, reflexively grabbing it in pain, rolling far too close to the precipitous drop.

The kangaroo hobbled into view behind me. It whined and tried to run toward me in an awkward stumble. The shadow hand grabbed me again, but I Aero Shifted a short distance away being sure to conserve Volition. Just enough to escape its clutches.

Nuzzle jumped to my shoulder to hiss and spit at the creature as I continued running up the stairs.

I gained ground on the Sin quickly, avoiding another costly fall. Finally, we reached the top of the cliff.

The lighthouse was right there. I ran to it, slamming against the side. I'd hug the damn thing if I could get my arms around it. The door was… no where to be seen. My hands searched the rough surface. Nothing hidden.

Nuzzle tried licking the wall, as if that might open a secret passage. Obviously, it didn't.

I rounded the remaining circumference.

Nothing.

There was no door.

Why is there no fucking door!?

I made it back to the stairs, then circled it again to make sure I didn't miss anything.

You've gotta be kidding me…

By the time I returned to where I started, the kangaroo Sin had reached the top. It limped toward me. Then paused, peering down the way it'd come.

A black hand the size of my entire body reached over the edge of the cliff. A second hand followed, both matted with sticky blood. The gorilla hybrid Sin pulled itself onto the ledge, Sapphire aura pulsing madly. It stood twice my height, a disturbingly human face spattered with gore resting on broad, muscular shoulders. It also had a thick metal collar around its neck, broken links of chain dangling onto its chest. I hadn’t noticed before, but strange gray fins jutted out from the beast’s forearms and the top of its head. Gills opened and closed on its chest, dripping red liquid. It smiled, and the jaw expanded, revealing at least seven rows of serrated teeth.

The kangaroo hopped toward me, squealing as if crazed. As if it needed me for itself or it would die.

It didn't make it very far.

A single backhanded swipe from the gorilla sent it cartwheeling off the cliff and out of view.

Apparently, it had no intention of sharing this victory.

That would be me next… or more likely, looking like poor Allium smeared all against the side of this lighthouse.

What happens to a Reaper when their Health goes to zero but they can't phase out to Vitria?

I didn't want to find out.
