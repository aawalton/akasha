Charlemagne's consciousness flickered as he flitted through vast stretches of the empty space that filled the void between universes. On occasion, he drifted close enough to a particular reality to snatch a glimpse of distant worlds. A flock of deep space leviathans gamboled through emerald and gold nebulae, straining the remnants of dead stars like krill. Liquid planets composed almost entirely of caustic chemicals slowly grew through accretion until their acids were neutralized by the influx of raw stone and metal. Scattered tribes that knew nothing of their shared billion-year history fought each other within megastructures that caged the very stars themselves. Charlemagne ignored them all: he was too busy looking through the good news that the Squiggles had brought him.

Charlemagne quickly ruled out the bottom two options, having no need of anyone to save him nor requiring any weapons. The thought that he could perhaps eat the weapon crossed his mind, but his recent experience with a certain indigestible cudgel made him leery of making an attempt. That left the Skill Upgrade Token as well as the Genetic Refinement Token.

After a good bit of thought, at least in his opinion, the rooster ruled out the Genetic Refinement Token. The possibility of losing his ability to create offspring made the Token too risky.

Having eliminated the other three options, Charlemagne quickly accepted the Skill Upgrade Token. He knew exactly what Skill he was going to use it on, too: his Ignition Core. After all, the Squiggles had promised him that the Token could upgrade any Skill, so why not use it on his best one?

Charlemagne willed the Token to upgrade his Ignition Core and waited for the process to begin. Although it felt like it was already on the cusp of evolving naturally, Charlemagne did not want to wait. Gaining his original Skill, Mana Core, had saved his life against the microscopic plasmodium that had almost taken over his body. Its evolution to Ember Core had given him the strength needed to conquer two Dungeons and stop a meteor from devastating the Earth. And, of course, he would never have survived his most recent battle without his newest evolution: Ignition Core.

Although Charlemagne was pleased with his increased levels and the Attribute gains that came with them, he was just as happy to see that his Core had finally broken past the level 20 mark. The round magical organ, a tangible testament to the Skill that created and sustained it, had been polished to near perfection by the deluge of radiant mana that the rooster had used to expunge the leader of the anti-System invaders from existence. It was no wonder that the Skill had jumped all the way to level 25. Hopefully, it would stay all the way up there once his Ignition Core evolved again.

The rooster’s anticipation climaxed as a pleasant warmth blossomed deep inside his chest. The upgrade had commenced. Charlemagne lost himself in the sensation, which made him both happy and drowsy at the same time. In fact, the effect dulled his senses so much that he forgot to observe the process using his mana-based senses.

It felt like much later that the warmth slowly faded and the Squiggles arrived to congratulate him on his latest acquisition.

The rooster read the message several times, growing more enraged with each repetition. The Squiggles had lied to him!

Charlemagne did not care about the odds. He bawked at the Squiggles not to bother him with such trifles.

Charlemagne took a few moments to compose himself. As he did, the calming warmth once again began to mount inside him. This time, he held it in check and demanded that the Squiggles show him his Skills from highest to lowest level.

Even Charlemagne had to admit that the list was fairly unimpressive. He had not given much thought to deliberately acquiring or leveling his Skills, except for his Ignition Core and the Skills that had fused with it. That being said, gaining five levels to his Core Skill was more like gaining 50 or more Skill levels at once, since so many Skills had merged with it. And these were the Skills that he used most often; in fact, Mana Reinforcement ran continuously, allowing the rooster to maintain his unnatural bulk, strength, and toughness.

Still, he needed to pick a Skill that hadn’t fused with his Ignition Core, which meant that he had a few strong contenders for the next best Skill to upgrade. Starting at the highest leveled option, Charlemagne thought about what upgrading Meditation might do.

The Skill was a relic from his first Class, Zen Barbarian, and, while it was high level, its effects seemed to overlap more and more with his other Skills and Special Abilities. In particular, the combination of his Ignition Core and Any Mana of Mine largely obviated the need for a separate Skill to help regenerate and utilize mana. So, while it was number two on the list, the rooster gave it a pass.

The second Skill he considered was Molten Rage. While re-reading the Skill’s description, Charlemagne realized that it was the only reason that he had survived absorbing the insane amount of solar energy inside the Sun Queen’s throne room. Essentially, the Skill had saved his life by creating a positive feedback loop, granting him enough toughness to survive the intense radiance, which allowed his internal temperature to go even higher, thus making him tougher again.

Charlemagne put Molten Rage into the “maybe” pile and checked out the next Skill on the list: Pain Resistance. Charlemagne almost immediately chose the Skill out of reflex, but held himself back. Even before the Skill, he had simply powered through pain when necessary. While he certainly didn’t enjoy being hurt, the rooster realized that it was better to be alive and in pain than to be dead. Perhaps when he no longer had any defensive Skills to upgrade, Charlemagne would find a way to evolve his Pain Resistance, but it would not be today.

Moving on, the rooster found Advanced Fowl Arts, a Skill that had become more and more neglected as his fighting style moved away from using his razor-sharp beak and slashing talons to take down foes. Although he still used his natural weapons on occasion, larger or stronger foes required different tactics. However, on the plus side, the rooster found grim joy in getting up close and personal with his foes, which made him loath to remove Advanced Fowl Arts from consideration. He put the Skill into the “maybe” pile, but below Molten Rage.

The next Skill was the first true defensive Skill among the list, and a good one at that. Inferno’s Embrace, which had emerged from the triad of Veins of Lava, Ember Shield, and Flame Physique, complemented a number of other Skills. It worked well with Ignition Core, which was a major plus, allowing the rooster to channel more mana faster and with fewer drawbacks. It also synergized well with Molten Rage by raising the rooster’s base body temperature. Not only did it work well with Charlemagne’s Skills, but it also played nice with his Special Abilities, helping to augment his self-healing, develop the rooster’s mana channels, and control the external temperature around his body. The last was incredibly important to Charlemagne, as it directly impacted his ability to procreate.

After taking a few moments to consider and finding no better contenders, Charlemagne demanded that the Squiggles upgrade Inferno’s Embrace. Instantly, the warm feeling in his chest faded. It was replaced by a growing cold that was somehow both biting and numbing at the same time. The rooster began to shiver as his temperature plummeted, shaking so violently that his feet began to knock into each other. His heartbeat slowed to a crawl while his Ignition Core sputtered anemically.

Ice began to form around the rooster’s eyes, nostrils, and mouth as trace amounts of moisture froze in the nothingness that surrounded Charlemagne. Dimly he felt his Fop’s Raiment, consisting of a top hat, monocle, and vest, transform to protect him from the unforgiving void. Unfortunately, it did nothing to replace the heat that the rooster had already lost. Charlemagne’s eyes grew heavy as his body slowly shut down.

But before he completely lost consciousness, the icy claws gripping the rooster’s innermost parts slowly lost ground against a new surge of heat that welled up from each and every cell in the rooster’s body. Excess thermal energy was shunted to his Ignition Core where it was stored, magically multiplied, and then fed back through Charlemagne’s system as both heat and mana.
