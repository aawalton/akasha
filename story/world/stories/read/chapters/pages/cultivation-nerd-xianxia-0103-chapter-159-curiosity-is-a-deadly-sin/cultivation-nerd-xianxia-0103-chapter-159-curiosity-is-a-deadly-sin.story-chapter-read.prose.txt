I couldn't help but think about Hu Jin and the highly lethal poison technique he used. Many historical figures used poison as a weapon to varying degrees of lethality. I didn't know much about Hu Jin, but Ye An hated him for stealing something. If he was a thief, I doubted he had a righteous master.
