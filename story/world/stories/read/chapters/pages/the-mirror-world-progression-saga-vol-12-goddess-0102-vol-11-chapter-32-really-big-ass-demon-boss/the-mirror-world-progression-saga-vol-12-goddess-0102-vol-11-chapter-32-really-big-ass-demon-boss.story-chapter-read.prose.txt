“Right…” Samantha flew up to place herself among the rest of the flyers as the giant demon came through the gate, though it seemed to mostly be taking its time.

It paused as it looked around, its head moving sideways and its eyes rotating independently. In a soft voice that nonetheless shook the air all around as a vibration, it said, “Calrenazzod. We’d almost forgotten the name.” Its mouth moved as it spoke, which was quite disturbing to watch. “Not much longer, now, is it?” Its eyes focused on Samantha.

Samantha thought she understood the question and shook her head. “The final battles are being fought.” She shrugged and smiled, looking at the demon hopefully. “I don’t suppose you might choose my side? Be friends? Not fight?”

The giant demon had all six eyes blink. “Sides.” It seemed to taste the word. “We are bound as we are bound.” It lifted a taloned claw to point down at Glae and the ritual. “Noble that it is, this interferes with what we are bound to protect. We must stop it.”

Samantha sighed. “And I can’t let you do that. We.”

It dropped its hand. “You see, then. You are no different.”

“I do this by choice and believe in it.”

The demon smiled a pointy-toothed smile that was no doubt a great horror on the world, though Samantha found it somehow endearing. “We envy this perspective.”

“You know what’s being done. You’re tied to this world, aren’t you? Affected by the curse?”

“Indeed, but it matters not. Shall we begin?”

Samantha felt many people behind her tense. “You’re suggesting doing this honorably?”

“Of course. A showdown is only fitting. The first to flinch in hostility?”

In the gestalt, Samantha sent out a warning to be ready after she spoke. I’m not letting this thing trick me with some flinchless action. “Agreed.”

Immediately, Samantha struck — which perfectly abided by the letter of the agreement. With nothing behind it, she hit it right in the face with [Spellfyr: Unleash], forming a two-meter wide beam, though against this colossal thing, that wasn’t its whole face, but maybe enough for two eyes.

On a dime, its face turned into a wall of flesh and eyes that blocked the blast and quickly turned to ash, but it was revealed as an extra sacrificial growth, as the demon was still whole behind it.

Crap!

“Trust is earned, it seems,” the demon muttered, as it gestured with a hand and made a sign, fast in the execution but then holding it for an extended period. With the sharpness of Samantha’s spiritual vision, she also noticed the hand had very intricate, ostensibly invisible runes etched all over, and curling up to the forearm. These runes, something unique and alien which she could not fully fathom, had powered the effect, making them more visible while they did.

Briefly, she noted signs that the other hands had their own runes, and each was wildly different in patterns.

Below, a massive AoE bloomed in a dome shape, of some sizzling, destructive effect, fuming and gaseous. The Antimages that were free coordinated to generate an equally dome-like protective bubble in response, that attempted to absorb it… and failed. It collapsed, likely because it was better against arcane magic.

A blast of disintegrating, acidic gas propagated and ate into all matter within the radius. Though everyone had gotten some measure of boost from the Antimagi efforts, Servitors vanished, and men died. The stone pillars all around the ritual became pockmarked by damage, and many of them collapsed, falling to the earth and sending cascades of dust. Grass and weeds were dissolved completely, leaving only dirt behind.

Damn it, damn it, damn it! I made the wrong choice, somehow, didn’t I? I should’ve tried something else! People are dead now. Please tell me that didn’t also-

Samantha held her breath as she felt a hiccup in the ritual, the energies disrupted and warbling. However, the cage of protection around Glae remained, and he seemed utterly unharmed. The ritual continued, straining and likely slowed down.

She had little time to release her breath in relief, however, as the threat remained very imminent and, though the ritual was far along, she actually had no clue how much longer it needed. Long enough, now, that the demon was going to have more chances to fuck things up, she was certain.

Good thing we had a chat, after all. I wasn’t even thinking about it, but it was quite the stall job, all things considered.

“Interestingly stubborn,” the demon muttered to itself, one eye looking down at Glae’s cage.

Maybe it thought it didn’t need to rush because of that destructive trump card. Probably thought this shit would be over in one shot.

She was about to shout orders to reinforce the stones somehow, but a minotaur was already on it, kneeling and putting a gauntleted hand to the earth as he chanted some command word, and an artifact activation triggered from the gauntlet. All around, granite grew up and encased both the pillars and the assorted fighters protectively, reinforcing.

Samantha focused on her next attack, even as it was loosely simultaneous with a host of combatants striking at the demon. She knew it was keeping at least one of its eyes on her.

She utilized [Conjure] to make a giant metal spike and drove it at the beast’s torso. But she paid only half a mind to this, as it was actually a feint timed with the blasts and ranged strikes of everyone else. Indeed, a slurry of walls formed, blocking the more dangerous attacks, as it otherwise tanked any AoEs, without any discernible harm dealt.

Her real target was the hand, and her attack was a rather unavoidable destructive cone effect via [Deific Pneumamancy: Pneumanic Plasma]. It spewed with enough area to engulf the demon’s whole arm.

No wall formed thankfully, all of its focus in that regard directed elsewhere — for that matter, it actually didn’t seem concerned about the attack, likely thinking itself immune to such corrosives and burning.

The fiery plasma, effectively ‘super-napalm,’ stuck to the demon and burned its flesh, scouring it deeply. One of its eyes looked down at its hand, apparently in genuine surprise.

Ha! Even this fucking thing isn’t immune to pneuma effects.

Less encouraging, the hand didn’t seem crippled by it, despite being injured.

And I don’t have the shaman haste buff anymore due to the short durat-

She felt the shaman’s buff reapply on her, speeding her up again.

Oh, hell yes! Smart froggy is smart! Man, am I glad she didn’t die! Or was it he? Bah, no time!

Able to cram in extra attacks as the demon was slowly adjusting and constantly getting pelted by others, Samantha moved her staff in for a series of rapid strikes, charging it with SP to make use of [Charged Strike]. The demon flicked its hand away swiftly on the first strike, narrowly avoiding the attack.

It looked as if it was going to use that hand again soon, by the way the fingers were curling and preparing, and the ebb of focusing energy from the runic structures.

I can’t let it throw that shit again, or likely something worse, perhaps more targeted. This has to connect, damn it!

As she telekinetically spun the staff for another strike at the hand, she made use of [Alter Fate], forcing success. Immediately, she felt resistance from the demon, in which it did something that destabilized Fate, altered things, and negated the automatic success.

Of fucking course!

However, with the bonus, the attack was still just enough to hit, and the staff cracked dead-on to its already damaged, burning hand. Raw destruction bloomed in a fiery, pneumanic explosion, and the hand was completely splattered into gore, right along with the demon’s entire forearm. Pieces of blackened bone went cascading in all directions and rained down on the battlefield.

Cheers rang out from the flyers on Samantha’s side to see the colossal being finally take a wound.

An eye of the demon rotated to look at the ruined hand. Nonetheless, Samantha noted the flesh was beginning to grow back out from its arm, stitching back together. “Oh. Very sound strategy, that. Congratulations.” It didn’t sound sarcastic. oddly. One of its other hands lifted and began making another, different, though equally strange sign, fingers contorted and twisted enough to make mortals wince. “Perhaps this will do the trick, then. If you’re curious, it’s called ‘Ilukferias Twin Rings,’ though the one word seems to have lost its meaning. A shame.”

As the demon spoke, two circles of intense, dark energy bloomed, one immediately around Glae’s cage and the protectors there, and one perhaps ten meters above it in the air. In a flash, it formed two huge masses of pulsating dark viscera, evil-looking cat-like eyes, and long tentacles ending in various nasty weaponry, ready to immediately strike out both in the center and in all directions in radius around them.

Idly, the demon said, “The second ring’s distance could’ve been raised into orbit if desired. Fascinating, isn’t it?”

Samantha replied, “Thanks, I hate it!”
