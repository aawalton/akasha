Hello there, everyone!

Well, here we are!

After nearly two years, over a million words written, and a release schedule that has been, to put it mildly, rather unpredictable (sorry about that, lol), we've finally reached another milestone!

Book 1 of Re:Birth is officially available on Amazon, Kindle Unlimited, and Audible!

You guys know how these things go. Every author who makes one of these announcements inevitably goes, "When I first started posting this story, I had no idea it would take off like this," yadda yadda yadda. And I swear, in my case, that's (mostly) true!

I mean, obviously, I was expecting to become the greatest author to ever live, write the number one story in the world, and achieve such ridiculous success that Harry Potter and The Lord of the Rings would pale in comparison.

You know, perfectly reasonable expectations for someone posting their first(ish) story on Royal Road.

But hey, almost two years later, we're sitting at over 10,500 followers, which will no doubt shrink a little after the stub. (All love, folks. I... I get it.)

And you know what? That's a pretty damn good place to be, too.

Jokes aside, I'm genuinely grateful that so many of you decided to give this story a chance, and even more grateful that you've stuck around for so long.

It all started on Royal Road and I don't think I can overstate how important this platform has been throughout this journey.

One of my favourite things about writing a web serial is getting to see people react to a story as it unfolds. Sometimes you'll spend weeks planning a particular scene, only for someone to notice a tiny detail you barely thought anyone would remember. Other times, you'll read a theory so elaborate that you start wondering whether the reader knows something you don't. And, of course, sometimes someone points out a plot hole and you have to sit there wondering how on earth you missed it. Fun times, lol.

For real though, thank you all so much for your continued support. It is a big part of why Re:Birth has come this far, and I hope you continue to enjoy it, whether it's here, on Patreon, or on Amazon.

Now, I promised myself I wouldn't say anything cheesy or negative in this announcement, so I won't!

Other than the fact that I am, uh, very stressed, lol. Like, ridiculously stressed. I've managed to accumulate a frankly impressive debt of sleep over the past few days, entirely through my own doing, mind you.
