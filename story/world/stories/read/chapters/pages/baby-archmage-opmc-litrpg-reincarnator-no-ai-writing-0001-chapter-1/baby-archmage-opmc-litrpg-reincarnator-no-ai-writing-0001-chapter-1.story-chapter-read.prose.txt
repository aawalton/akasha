The first thing I saw was a white, bright light. There was pain and everything was just too much. The whole world came at me all at once. Sounds, lights, sensations. Apparently, I was also naked and covered in blood and who knows what else.

Naturally, I cried like a little baby.

“There, there,” a gentle voice said. I was wrapped in something soft and cradled in giant arms. I looked up and saw a beautiful young woman with black hair, olive skin and a tired, but happy expression.

“He looks healthy and well, Ramona,” a wizened voice said.

“Ramona!” a panicked young man said. “How are you feeling? Do you need anything?”

“Just come close, Jonaka,” Ramona said. “Hold us.”

I felt a weight shift and an absolutely enormous hand laid on my torso. Ramona started humming some song and rocking gently. I still screamed, but started to strangely calm down. After the initial shock of getting shoved into the world, I was starting to feel comfortable. Really icky, though. There was some sticky film stuck on my cheek.

I got a hold of myself. I was… alive? I was me? What in the hell was going on?

I remembered vaguely what had happened. I had been on a weekend trip with my family. We had been driving towards the family cabin when a truck slid out of control on an icy road and barreled through my dad’s pick up.

It had been over so fast, I barely had time to feel fear. Just a flash of white and then everything had gone black.

I vaguely remember being asleep. I remembered talking to someone… someone old. They had asked me what I’d do differently if I had another chance. I didn’t remember my answer, but it did seem to have interesting consequences.

For one thing, I was a baby being cradled by my mother. That raised questions. The conclusion was clear. I had been reincarnated. Why? How?

“Amazing…” the young man muttered. “I can’t believe we made this.”

I felt Ramona nudge her husband. I looked up and saw her give him a wry, but loving smile. “I made this. You were just there for the ride.”

I craned my head and saw my father. A handsome man with a friendly open face and blonde hair. He noticed me and grinned.

“Hey there little fellow! Don’t listen to mama. I made you. And I’ll take care of you.”

This felt… weird. But nice. I still remembered my old life. But at the same time it was vague, like a dream. I felt an inexplicable connection with my new parents. Even though I still had the mind of someone in their mid twenties, I also felt like a baby. The emotions of the new body were mine. The fear, the confusion, and the unconditional love for my parents.

Ah crap, I thought. I’m going to be a really weird child.

Father wiggled a thumb in front of my face. I indulged him and grabbed it, which made him tear up.

“I am glad your healing wasn’t required, Elder Piraka,” Ramona— mother said. “But will you bless him before you leave?”

“Of course,” the old man said.

An old, bearded man wrapped in a green robe approached me. He was holding a staff of gnarled wood that curled around an amber gemstone at the tip.

One thing was sure— this room didn’t look like modern Earth. Everything was too archaic. Wooden furniture, handmade clay bowls with water, crude towels made of hemp or some other coarse fiber. And the old guy was dressed up like a swamp wizard. Mother had called him elder.

Oh no. I got reincarnated into a cult. A superstitious hippie cult.

Elder Piraka waved his staff and the amber started to glow. Now, that got my attention.

“By the spirits in the sea and the land, and the holy words that guide us, let this child be blessed. May his levels be many and his skills be of use to himself and the tribe.”

Levels? Skills?

The candlelights illuminating the room flickered and a green and golden mist started emanating from the staff’s amber. Elder Piraka performed a handsign and the mist enveloped me. Even though my vision was blurry, a clear rectangle with words appeared in the corner of my vision.

[Minor Blessing of the Tribe] acquired.

Description: 5% increase in Skill Experience for all Skills

Duration: 720 days

Holy crap.

Okay, not a hippie cult. Definitely something else. There was only one logical solution. I had gone insane. I hadn’t really died, but was in a coma dream.

I let out a cry and my mother reached to kiss me on the forehead. That calmed me down.

I let my parents whisper sweet nothings to each other, and saw Elder Piraka cast a gentle smile at us before he left.

I tried to make sense of the situation.

Whether this was a dream, hallucination, simulation or an actual reincarnation, it didn’t really matter, did it? I had to assume it was real. Okay, so I was in another world. A world with magic and apparently skills and levels. This system box in the corner of my vision must have been the ‘holy words’ that the elder had mentioned. And I had been reincarnated into all of this with a mature mind.

A new chance… I mused.

I cracked open an eye and saw my parents smiling at me. They looked so happy. I smiled back at them, which brought tears to my father’s eyes again. These were clearly good and loving people.

I wondered what kind of a place I was in. This hut that we were in was certainly austere. The clothes my parents wore were simple. We were not rich. But it was warm inside, and there was fresh clean water in a barrel in the corner next to a full barrel of some green tubers.

It was enough.

I was excited about a new life! I hadn’t had much going on in my previous one, truth be told… That would change here. I wanted to apply myself. I wondered what I should do with this blessing that was given to me.

My thoughts immediately returned to the glowing amber and the iridescent mist of green and gold.

Magic…

An explosive excitement blossomed inside me.

This world had magic!

I had always loved stories with magic. The wonder, mastery and power expressed through it. Learning magic, wouldn’t that be something?

My mind kept replaying the spell Elder Piraka had cast. I couldn’t contain my excitement.

Naturally, I started crying. It was all I could do to express myself. My mother mistook it for hunger and offered her breast to me. Instinctively I grabbed it.

Let me tell you holy crap, there’s nothing like the first meal of your life.

That warm milk was like the ambrosia of the gods. I drank my fill and fell contently into my mothers arms again. She smiled fondly at me and stroked my cheek.

“My baby boy…” she whispered.

I smiled at her.

“Babies can’t smile, right?” my father asked. “Not right away?”

“I don’t know, but that is a beautiful smile,” mother said. “Maybe he is special.”

“Of course he is,” father said. “You made him.”

My mother pushed her head against my father’s broad chest. “No wonder I fell for you. Such a sweet talker.”

My father grinned evilly. “And now you’re stuck with me and my son. FOREVER!”

He laughed theatrically.

My mother booped my nose softly. “Don’t worry about papa. He is a dumbass. Lets hope you inherited my wits.”

“And looks,” my father said.

Mother gave him a tired smile. “I must look awful.”

“Never,” he said and leaned for a kiss.

I let them bask in their after-baby glory. I was really happy for them and hoped I wouldn’t be too weird a child. I don’t think it was possible for me to be whatever they expected. I had a matured mind with memories of another world. Hell, I could understand what they were saying, which was passing strange.

Having said all that, I felt a genuine affection towards my parents. I could see from the way they acted that they were good and caring people. I felt lucky. I had never been a religious person, but I did hazily recall talking to someone between life and death. If that person had decided the circumstances of my reincarnation, I could only be grateful.

I didn’t know enough to make decisions yet. For all I knew reincarnation was normal in this crazy world with System notifications. Whether it was or not, I would do my best to be a good child and love my parents.

But I also wanted to find my own way. I had some baggage from my last life, which I felt I had completely wasted.

I didn’t know what I had agreed with the person between life and death, but I felt one thing strongly. I needed to spend this life well. It was a growing urge inside me.

As my mother cradled me, my thoughts drifted from the happy warm feelings to the regrets of my last life, and wonder at having been reborn. It was rather chaotic, but in all of that my thoughts kept returning to the spell cast by Elder Piraka. I recalled the glowing amber. Magic. There was magic in this world. Who hadn’t fantasized about being able to cast magic. The act of imposing your will on laws of reality themselves. I had felt so powerless and weak in my last life. I spent time with those emotions and mixed into them was the promise of magic. It would be the answer I had been searching for in my previous life.

I wanted to learn more. How had the elder cast magic? Did you need Skills? A staff? It would be a while before I could start asking questions. I needed to figure something out. A plan! I needed a plan!

First, I would need to…. need to….

Damn it…

I fell asleep and dreamt of magic.

The first days were spent in a stupor. When I was awake I was fed, washed, clothed and held. It was nice. I mostly stayed with my mother in bed. She sang to me in a soft voice and I was filled with unconditional love.

Slowly my vision became clearer, I managed to exert some level of motor control and my thoughts weren’t interrupted by constant naps.

Which meant I could apply myself.

I knew what I wanted. I wanted magic. But I doubted there were any magic schools for newborn babies. There wasn’t much of anything for babies, and frankly not being able to move, talk or do anything was boring. My options were very limited. But I wanted to test the System and its skills.

So what could I do?

Well in my past life, I had dabbled with meditation. Mostly just the mindfulness stuff to ground myself and decompress my thoughts and emotions. But meditation was also associated with all kinds of esoteric stuff right? Who knew what it could do in a world where magic existed!

Even if it didn’t immediately transform me into a transcendental baby-god, it would likely improve my powers of concentration and give me clarity of mind. At the very least it would prepare my mind by teaching the focus I assumed was needed to learn magic.

Most importantly, it was something I could do right now.

So I closed my eyes in my mother’s arms. She must have thought I fell asleep, because she stopped singing. I fell into a breathing rhythm and focused my mind. I wasn’t sure what I was looking for, but this felt right.

It took some practice. My baby mind was mushy and chaotic. And most annoyingly I kept falling asleep! Stupid baby body, stop interrupting my quest for greatness.

I kept trying and gradually it got easier. My bouts of meditation got longer, and my mind was becoming clearer. Then one time, right after mother had fed me, I fell into a state of flow. I lost myself and only focused on breathing. Time stopped mattering as I passed through boredom and loss of form. It was a blissful state of connection with myself and the world. I just breathed it in and held that state. For how long, who knows? And finally, in that beautiful emptiness of inner space, a voice called me back into the world. But not the voice of my mother. Something otherworldly.

[New Skill Obtained: Meditation (uncommon)!]

Description: Allows the user to enter a meditative state, improving mental clarity, concentration, and control over their thoughts. Higher levels improve the depth and stability of meditation.

[Meditation level 1!]

[Attribute Unlocked: Perception]

Perception: Ability to perceive, distinguish and process sensory information, including special information when using the appropriate Skills.

[Attribute Unlocked: Focus]

Focus: Ability to concentrate, resist distraction, maintain complex mental tasks and divide attention between simultaneous activities.

[+1 Perception]

[+1 Focus]

Yes! My hard work had paid off. I had gotten my first Skill. What were the implications? Who knew, but the sense of victory felt awesome. I had applied myself and gotten rewarded. The attributes were subtle, but I could feel them. My mind felt ever so slightly clearer and my vision got ever so slightly less blurry. I was really happy the System actually gave me an explanation of the Attributes.

I studied the notification further. I had gotten an Uncommon Skill. So that meant there were rankings to Skills? I wish I knew the scale.

I had tried interacting with the system by asking it questions and using command words, like you would to a computer program. But nothing had worked. I would just have to figure things out for now and ask questions from the adults when I was older.

I turned my attention back to the notifications, because they were far from being done.

[Achievement Obtained: First Skill!]

For unlocking your first Skill, you get a passive perk related to the Skill.

[Reward: Perk — Contemplative]

Description: All Skill Experience related to Meditation boosted by 10%

Achievements? That’s neat. So doing impressive stuff gave you extra rewards? I liked that. I wondered what skills were related to Meditation. Would magic skills be?

When I looked at my final notification, my eyes went wide and my whole body started to tremble. I was pretty sure I had gotten something huge.

[World First Achievement Obtained: An Awakened Mind!]

You are the first sapient being to deliberately acquire a Skill within the first month of life. Reward adjusted to match unlocked Skill.

[Reward: Special Perk — Sage]

I looked at the reward description of the Sage Perk.

Oh shit.
