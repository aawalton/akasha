Footsteps echoed outside the room, followed by a familiar, frantic voice. "KiAera! You're awake!" Amber's large ratite frame barreled through the door, nearly knocking it off its hinges. Her feathers were ruffled, and her puffy red eyes were filled with relief.

"Amber…" KiAera managed a weak smile. "Missed me?"

Amber didn't respond with her usual playful sass. Instead, she rushed to KiAera's side, nuzzling her gently. "You scared us! Don't ever do that again!"

"I'll try not to," KiAera replied with a gentle laugh. She glanced at the doorway, where Scarbol, Tailza, and a few of the kobolbos peeked in hesitantly. Among them were the lean plant forms of Saptal and Sapshorla—the two sapkins—along with the crystal mouse monsters known as the osseods at their feet. "Come on in, guys. I'm not a ghost."

Saptal and Sapshorla entered the room with their wooden frames, their shawls swaying with the rhythm of each step taken by their root-like feet pressing down on the soft carpet below.

"Oh, sunlights! You're awake! That's great!" Sapshorla exclaimed, her voice muffled by the leaf cascading over her face. She hastily tucked the leaf away between her fingers, revealing her smile and flushed cheeks.

"Yep, indeed! We've missed you, KiAera," Saptal added, his branch-like hand reaching to pat Sapshorla on the shoulder. "It's great to see yer sprouted back to yer feet."

"Miss KiAera!" Tailza, the kobold girl, nearly tackled her in a hug. "W-We thought—"she sniffled as her voice broke off. "We thought you were gone for good!"

Scarbol cleared his throat, approaching with a more composed expression, though his eyes betrayed his relief. "You gave us quite a scare, KiAera. Glad to see you're still in one piece. We all are." He gestured to the others.

KiAera smiled weakly. "I'm sorry I worried all of you. I didn't mean to... collapse like that."

Saptal the Sapkin stepped up, shaking his head. "What matters is that yer alive." His gaze flicked to the egg beside a slumbering Big Osseod. "And that… kiddo. It's what drained you, isn't it?”

"Not just the egg," KiAera admitted. "I overestimated myself. But Liozel is part of our family now. It's going to be hard to believe but..."

She began to explain the events that transpired between herself and the Liorex egg. However, while she addressed the monsters before her, she kept the Faux Nym naming mechanism to herself—for the monsters' sanity. Her intention was genuine to not create a ripple of panic throughout the crew. The crew may believe that simply saying their name would cause her crash again. So it was better to leave that part out, for now.

She also shared her concerns about how the egg was still unhatched, yet it was already able to communicate with her telepathically.

"LEELEE SPEAKS?!" Amber's eyes widened with absolute delight.

The kobolbos exchanged glances, their expressions uncertain but trusting. Among them, Tailza spoke with a timid voice. "C-Can we… help somehow?"

The question made her smile. "You already are. Just… stick with me, okay?"

Her expression grew grim as a more serious matter struck her. Two days remained until the necessary crystal quota needed to be met, and she had already been out for an entire day.

If the dungeon expedition was to be carried out, she needed to head out very soon—for her crew's sake, for the kobolbos, and for Scarbol. Without those crystals—if Mina and Boggorc kept their word—she recognized that the likelihood of a skirmish against the two was very high. This was not the time to take a backseat.

"Listen, everyone. I understand I've been gone and all of you have been worried, but we have a job to do. We're behind on our crystal quota, and if we don't meet it, we risk a confrontation with Mina and Boggorc. I can't let that happen. Yet, there's too many unknowns to who we're dealing with."

Of course, she refused to risk her fellow monsters. However, only gathering crystals for the adversaries was a risk in itself. She needed to take every precaution possible.

Amber shuffled her feet, feathers rustling as she tilted her head. "What do you propose, KiAera? You just woke up, and you're talking about heading back out? You need to rest! LeeLee egg would be devastated if KiAera hurt!"

Scarbol stepped closer, frowning. "And what do you suggest? Sending a small team out while you recover? Or do you really think you can manage the dungeon in your condition? You know how dangerous it is out there, especially if we haven't mapped the area thoroughly."

KiAera gave Scarbol a bit of credit for his insight. Her lips curled into a grin. "You're right, but… don't worry about me. Actually, I've got a few plans up my sleeve that might just do the trick."

"Ya got plans? And what's that, KiAera?" asked Saptal.

"We will expedite our training in the Monster Domain next door. While gathering crystals for those two in exchange for a wisp of peace is ideal, we should also focus on strengthening ourselves."

"It's necessary for us to drill ourselves now and then shift our focus toward dungeon expeditions. That way, we can maximize our resources and train simultaneously. We'll start now while the sun is rising. So, let's get to it, folks!"

The monsters cried out, causing the whole room to shake violently. KiAera grinned as she patted the Liorex egg and Big Osseod. "We'll be back."

Okay... please come back when you're done, Big Sis, the egg replied.

"I promise," she embraced the egg before turning to the other monsters. "Let's head out!"

While her crew departed from the room, KiAera noted the absence of the Nexperbax triplets. Had they left while she was out of commision? Or they had simply been too cautious to approach, fearing her condition?

Whatever had happened, she needed to significantly expand her focus on managing her crew, and she deemed the Monster Domain a major key to their success. Being the only evolved monster was a detriment to both the crew and herself, and if she faltered again, they would be in a precarious position.
