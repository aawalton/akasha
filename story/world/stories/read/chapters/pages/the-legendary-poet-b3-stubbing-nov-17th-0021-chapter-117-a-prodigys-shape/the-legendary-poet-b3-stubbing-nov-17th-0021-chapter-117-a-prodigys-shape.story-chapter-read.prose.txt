A Draft of Grass and Sun.

The frame opened onto a plain that was almost finished.

A Draft of Root and Hollow.

The frame opened onto a downward.

The wing was a single long descending corridor that the dungeon had not bothered to finish drawing the sides of. The walls were roots, vast and pale, and where the roots ended, the corridor ended too, opening into nothing on either side.
