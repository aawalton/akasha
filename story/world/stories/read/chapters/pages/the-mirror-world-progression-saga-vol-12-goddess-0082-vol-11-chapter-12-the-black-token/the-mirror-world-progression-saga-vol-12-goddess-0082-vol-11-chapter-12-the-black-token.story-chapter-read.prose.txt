Ugh! We wouldn’t even be in this situation if I hadn’t gone for that hug way back when, on the first meeting with Goldie.

Goldie was quite effective in her own realm to be able to ambush Samantha like that. She hadn’t so much moved fast as altered her realm’s reality, ‘skipping’ the actual motion directly to the grappled state. Probably not too surprising that a snake entity had a trick for that if she had a preference for ‘constriction.’

Goldie sighed prettily and dabbed at her eyes, apparently emotional about things. Ignoring the sullen scowls directed at her, she said, “To be more definitive, Goddess, your chances are very encouraging for these final hours to go your way. By no means can it be called absolute, but so many checkpoints have been hit! It’s really quite surprising and thrilling! Naturally, we have good cause to be hopeful. Stay sharp, take nothing for granted, and keep going forward, hmm?”

Azure, meanwhile, held up the dark coin. “What else do we need to know about this thing? Can we activate it here, what will happen, where will it take us, what should we expect?”

Goldie blinked at all of the questions. “I certainly don’t remember all of that!” She squinted thoughtfully at the coin and touched a finger to her lips. “Hmm. I know it is a realm-to-realm coin, and there are not even standard exits from this place, you see, so it is best to use that from here, when you’re ready, and do hold hands with your lover when you do. It’ll take you both to another realm where I’ve no doubt arranged a means for containing and viewing your likely final vision, or something like that. I do know that it can’t reveal anything before your arrival, because I’m certain I never knew how you came to be here.” She shrugged and smiled with her eyes closed. “Nothing else whatsoever is coming to me!”

Why do I have a feeling that’s a really bad thing?

Giggling to herself, she settled back down to nap through the rest of time. While she’d like to see what happened at the end, she couldn’t bother poor Serkars’srek again, to be her eyes and ears abroad. He was owed his final rest, too.

In a disorienting rematerialization, Samantha found herself in a very strange realm. It was like being inside a floating, swaying mirage, like being caught in the image of a bubble’s surface, and the bubble was ready to burst. She was aware of Limbo all around on the periphery, bordering it. In fact, as they materialized, the whole damn place was being crafted for them on a dime, through the coin.

It was fascinating and powerful magic being unveiled, and only her lightning senses of time, combined with her knowledge of pneuma, combined with her focus on Third Eye perception, allowed her to see this incredible sequence unravel itself into expanding complexity, like a puzzle box unfurling and inverting from smallness to largeness.

Within the foggy void of Limbo, this realm was inherently unstable and would need to collapse within hours. Probably sooner, when they triggered it by trying to leave. She could also tell her realm was cut off, or at least, it was strongly inhibited.

Best to do our business here quickly. The collapse wouldn’t hurt us, I don’t think, but it’ll make it inaccessible, likely forever.

When more physical associations caught up with the raw spiritual perceptions, a desert-like environment of flat, white sand could be seen stretching right out of the borders of the fog. In the center, ahead, was a stone structure, composed of two square platforms stacked on each other, as if the base of a pyramid, and then a temple top, or perhaps a tomb or mausoleum, based on its appearance. It was mostly white marble with gold highlights, and a black door, with the look of being obsidian like the coin.

Through her spiritual perceptions, she sensed something similar to other places they’d been: beyond the doorway, something connected to Azure was waiting.

Once Azure took stock of the wavering environment, she scowled. “I feel almost seasick from this shit. I don’t like the look of this at all, Chestnut. We had better prepare for anything…” She began casting something.

“Wait!” Samantha held up a hand in warning. “Cancel.”

Azure did so, allowing the spell to abort before anything occurred. Her eyes flickered around. “Okay. Why?”

“Just hold on…” Samantha kept studying the environment. “I need to analyze more carefully, here.”

She focused her abilities in concert, namely [Deific Pneumamancy], [Spirit Sight], and [Sense Magic], peering long and deeply into the realm, which as a temporary, unowned realm, was significantly more blatant in discernible details. Beneath the thin, unstable surface, the realm’s rules were evident, its principles exposed entirely to her. The System translated this into hard information.

Cardinal Rule #1:

Exclusive, contained. This realm is only stable for those who entered and innate denizens. Any other entities entering will immediately collapse it, and it will wholly and completely vanish. If anyone who entered leaves, it will also vanish.

Outgoing and incoming communications are very difficult to achieve and will threaten collapse if successful. If the realm does not collapse, at the very least, instability will increase.

Upon collapse, innate denizens vanish, but entering subjects are unharmed.

Cardinal Rule #2:

Unstable. Even without inappropriate invaders, the realm is continually unraveling. Its outer environment will diminish over the course of a few hours, and then the realm will collapse entirely within minutes, wholly and completely vanishing.

Cardinal Rule #3:

Neither here nor there. This realm is incredibly difficult to find from the outside, as is everything within it, and when it vanishes, no trace will be left within minutes.

Realm Rule (#4):

Guardians with teeth. Even though the entire realm may collapse, guardians in this realm can spiritually harm or kill unwanted intruders.

“Okay, good to know,” Samantha muttered. Briefly, she sent the rules mentally to Azure for review. “We can’t summon ‘entities’ here. And we can expect trouble unless the guardians give us a pass or something.”

Azure rolled her eyes. “Wonderful. Can I unleash Deathsting, at least? If necessary.”

“He’s already here. His wyvern form is just that: a differing form. Shapeshifting isn’t disallowed.”

“Shall we start on protections, then?”

“Yeah… but don’t bother with physical shit. This is too close to baseline Limbo to have any varying forms of attack. All spirit.”

Azure shrugged. “My physicality translates.” She swept a hand to indicate her crystalline body.

“Sure, but standard wound negations are useless. I suppose you don’t need them. At least I have Trilogy Fusion Soul for a couple of uses here… Anyway, let’s get crackin’, baby!”

They buffed up with everything useful and lasting for at least around fifteen minutes of duration. Samantha did one thing technically at ten minutes of duration, but doubled with [Alter Spell: Prolong]: her Sizzling Array spell, which caused numerous color-changing balls to float above her head. She stuck with the (Pneuma) secondary effect, as the least likely to bump into resistances in addition to the nasty secondary burning effects.

Naturally, Azure made sure to scatter and bring along a small horde of levitating mirrors, should they need them.

The duo floated over the stone, heading for the temple at the center. They saw immediate signs of something foreboding in sigil-like carvings embedded flat in the stone. They were slightly varying dragon-like pictures, or perhaps more accurately, winged serpents. Their heads varied, some of them dog-like, some more ape-like, and many bug-like, with some reminiscent of dragonflies. In any case, there were at least dozens of them, making triangles.

None are cat-like, thankfully. I don’t hate cats, but I do hate cat-things.

She considered pre-emptively marring the sigils, but something told her that would spell immediate violence, and she wasn’t sure it couldn’t be avoided yet.

When they approached the door, they saw two much larger, more elaborate sigils to either side of the door. One of them was a long, coiled yellow snake with a very dragon-like head, and the other was a seven-headed red serpent, otherwise quite snake-like.

Once they got within meters of the door, all of the sigils came to life at once in a great combination of flaring magics. The giant ones by the door hissed in warning, and answering hisses and other noises came from all around. There were a few bark-like sounds in the mix.

Samantha stopped, because she recognized the meaning: a command to halt. Immediately, she gave a hissing sound in greeting, raising her hands slightly but not threateningly and bowing her head respectfully. She invoked [Ascendant: Snake Friend] as she continued with hisses, “Hey! We’d like to slither on past that door, there, if it’s all the same. I’m a friend to all snakes, and Goldie sent me here.”

The two snake entities stared at her and flicked their tongues, perhaps a bit taken aback by her unusual language proficiency.
