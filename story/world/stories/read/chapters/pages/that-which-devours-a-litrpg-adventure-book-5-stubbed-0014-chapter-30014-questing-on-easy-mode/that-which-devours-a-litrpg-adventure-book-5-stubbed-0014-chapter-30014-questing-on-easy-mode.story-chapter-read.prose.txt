Alex and Lenna, portal hopping…

Landing on the other side wasn’t bad, though for some reason I almost felt damp. The connection to Strange led off to one side.

Lenna appeared next to me, and we both paused, glancing around.

The courtyard reminded me of the landing area for the Academy. Stone covered the ground, and there were high walls that blocked the view. A small archway led to a pathway, where some buildings stood nearby.

As soon as we approached the archway, the sound picked up.

Lots of footsteps of people, and voices, filled the air like an almost physical presence. Creatures and beings of many shapes and sizes moved past the arch, down the road. Most headed in one direction, like shift workers leaving a factory. We joined the crowd, which seemed to be headed where we needed to go. Before long, the path between the two buildings opened up to a much bigger courtyard.

Restaurants with balconies stretching up several floors, and shops offering a wide variety of goods, lined the area, but the biggest distractions were the portals. One stood tall, and many people passed through it. That had to be the one to the megacity. This almost reminded me of the outskirts of a city, a neighborhood.

People commuted to work in the much bigger city on the other side of that tall portal.

The other portal sat dark, lonely, and unused. Instead of the bright white color, it appeared as a pitch black, with only little flecks of light.

As soon as I noticed it, I paused, but then someone behind me almost hit my shoulder. I stepped out of the flow of traffic, and Lenna followed.

“This is amazing,” she whispered, glancing around at all of the buildings.

It reminded me of the overcrowded cities back on earth, where you could barely breath, yet with a ton less people and cleaner air.

Very few of the people were anywhere near level 200, and I didn’t feel threatened at all.

The bond with Strange led through the bright white portal that everyone else was using. Yet, I kept staring at the black one.

“Don’t get mesmerized by it,” said a soft voice. It came from a very tall humanoid with catlike features and metal spikes attached to the backs of their paws. “That leads to darkness, and to the Great Ones.”

[City Guard, Bean, Spike Smash, Level 182, Prey, Unknown.]

Lena’s lips moved, but no sound came from her.

“I appreciate the advice,” I said, grabbing her hand.

“Just doing my job,” they said with a nod. “This must be your first time here. Once you head over to the megacity, stay on your guard. Thieves and pickpockets are a thing. Everyone is civilized, but…” He shrugged.

“First visit, and hopefully we’re just passing through,” I added, wondering what the rest of their job entailed.

Bean chuckled, and smacked me on the shoulder. “Good luck with that. People always say that, visiting the big one, then end up losing a year or two just exploring. I mean, the weapons are great, along with the libraries… but the food! The food is worth it.”

“Libraries,” said Lenna, suddenly paying attention to our conversation.

“Ah, she speaks. I haven’t seen one of your kind in a long time, though they usually flit through without us seeing. Usually closer to your level.” They pointed at me. “I love libraries. The megacity has so many, and most are open to the public. Some are only open to different guilds, or professions, but the majority are open to all, and there’s plenty to read. Give me a good book, some noodles, and a sunny place to relax, and I am a happy cat.”

“I mean, wouldn’t that be a good day for almost anyone?” I asked, thinking of some tasty noodles. My stomach growled a little.

“I like you! Tell you what, head to Tails Noodle shop, and tell them that Beany sent you.” They gave me another pat on the shoulder, then something across the way drew their attention. They darted off, just as Lenna started to speak.

She pressed her lips together. “So, noodles?”

“Just a quick stop, I promise.” My stomach growled again. Maybe slightly longer than we wanted, but I could always stock up on the noodles.

Lenna let go of my hand and stepped back into the flow of people. I joined her, but stayed close by her side, not wanting to get separated.

Heat brushed over me as we crossed the portal, with a hint of whispered voices in the air. This courtyard rivaled anything I’d seen in my travels so far.

At least five different portals sat in a half circle, with bridges leading people down into an open area. An archway then let people into the city proper.

Buildings stretched in all directions. Some were made of metal and glass, with others carved into massive trees or out of stone. Clouds danced overhead, and something flicked just beyond them.

A hum buzzed in the background, along with the scent of flowers, motor oil, and sweat. It made me shake my head.

Near the archway leading into the massive sprawl of a city stood a screen, with various locations marked on it.

Lenna paused in front of it, searching.

I walked right up to it and touched the panel. A small popup appeared, and I typed in Tails Noodle. It popped up on a much smaller map right in front of me, along with a notification.

[Do you want directions to Tails Noodle?]

Yes, please.

“This tech is a lot like what we used back before…” I said, suddenly glancing around and noticing more things that appeared like they could have come from Earth, including the massive towers that looked like skyscrapers.

“Oh, is that a library?” she asked, touching a spot near Tail Noodles. It popped an information card up. “It is! I’ve heard of them, and my people have a small one with a collection of crystals. Once I saw a book, but we have few of them. Not enough for rooms and rooms of them, like the tails say.”
