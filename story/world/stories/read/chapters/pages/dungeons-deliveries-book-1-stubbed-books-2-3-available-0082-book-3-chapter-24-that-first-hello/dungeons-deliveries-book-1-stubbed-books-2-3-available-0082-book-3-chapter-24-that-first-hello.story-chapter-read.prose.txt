***

Beepy lanced out another [Volt Whip] in an arc as Zippy shoved his hand into his open hatch full of knives. A beam of solid energy wove out of Beepy’s hand to circle their position, weaving together with his previous casts.

“Again!” Aria shouted, activating [Thrice This] three more times.

One [Volt Whip] became three, became nine, became twenty-seven. The Paladrones had practiced their combos well. Each beam of energy twisted and snaked together from Beepy’s will while the multiplying effect from Aria stacked the lattice higher and higher. Stretching his mental capacity to keep track of each whip, Beepy tightened each strand to form the first stage of the Paladome into a massive, impenetrable dome of [Volt Whip]s. All told the fence was made of fifty-two casts interlocking and circling the drones, Brody, and Tank.

“OW!” Someone shouted as they ran into the fence, grill-marking their face.

“My sword!” wailed another who tried to chop a [Volt Whip], only for their weapon to collapse in two.

“Now you, Zip!” Aria yelled over the cries of pain.

Zippy felt Beepy straining against the essence needed to maintain the shell of the Paladome. The brothers were always connected, able to parse and send information back and forth easily, and with the sheer amount of [Volt Whips], Beepy had maxed out the computational power he could muster on his own. Zippy’s own essence felt lower than appropriate from flying to the Falls as fast as they could.

A torrent of attacks clashed against the Paladome, and Zippy felt his Beepy’s strain cry out. Beepy always tried his best and often would put himself in dangerous situations to protect the Clan.

“Zippppy!!!! We need you to CAST!”

A monstrous man body slammed into the Paladome, healing away the cauterization instantly, snarled from the pain, and readied another charge. Through his connection, Zippy felt his brother’s Core straining against it’s limits. He had to do something, even if it was against Paladrone protocol. He would not bear to see his brother Beepy fall.

They’d practiced for days. Worked out how far they could push things. Fifty-two [Volt Whip]s was Beepy’s max at his current skill level, requiring two separate casts from Beepy and two separate triple ups from Aria. Zippy himself could handle twenty-seven [Sacrificial Strike]s before his Core felt as if it would shatter.

But he’d always wondered what they could do with their shared skill, [Better Together], under a formation. It synced Beepy and Zippy, allowing them to share essence and formulate their attacks together more efficiently. It was on their list to add to their Paladrone forms.

They were supposed to practice it first. That was Paladrone protocol they’d all agreed to. There just wasn’t the time these days with so many meanie-bo-beanies to take down.

But Beepy was in danger, and Zippy would never let his brother fall.

Unpracticed and unsure of what would happen, Zippy forced [Better Together] on, and an instant later, cast the skill side of his [Sacrificial Strike] Title, his hand clasping an [Ancient Paring Knife]. Aria spotted the slit in reality and activated [Thrice This] three times.

Brody, surrounded by the fence and with Tank sitting next to him, got a feeling in his stomach that felt like terror and hope mixed together.

“AHHHHHHHHHH!” A man’s shout erupted as his hand disappeared and blood spurted out.

“Oops!” Aria laughed evilly. “You shouldn’t touch a portal as it opens! That’s common knowledge.”

A wail of despair cried out before vanishing forever.

Two people’s heads were lopped off, their bodies collapsing.

Knife after knife in Zippy’s stomach disappeared, every triple cast of Zippy’s [Sacrificial Slice] opening a portal to the unspace. He wondered why twenty-eight disappeared instead of twenty-seven. Zippy cast the initial portal horizontally along the outside of the Paladome like a porcupine. Positioned just right, they were unseen, though glancing from an angle, the portals appeared as flat purplish needles circling the already menacing fence.

Aria laughed maniacally at their powerful formation. Tank panted and leaned his heavy body against his master, thankful for the rest and wanting to go and meet the funny little danger toys. Brody’s mouth had gone dry from the horror.

Attackers approaching during the portals opening were beheaded and maimed. Limbs were lopped, some falling into the unspace forever, some splashing onto the grass. Others wailing and trying to break apart the dome fell away, unseen and forever marked missing, their whereabouts unknown. Only, they had simply been sent to the unspace to spin forever between all things.

The smarter hunters stopped in their tracks to gawk at the Paladome, terrified at the structure. They’d witnessed the impenetrability of it and were trying to get a peek as to who lay in the center.

And in the center, two drones favoured by the System were locked in, clasping each other mentally through their shared skill, [Better Together].

“Perfect!” Aria cackled, wiping her hands together. “Now hold, Beep and Zip! HOLD!”

She looked back and forth between them, smiling first before noticing they were unnaturally still.

“Guys?” She asked, worry pinging her. “Heeeeelllloo?”

***

Pain fell away as Beepy and Zippy linked more acutely with [Better Together].

Two beings with their own computational power and own cores shared resources, multiplying their power by factorial two.

They were always able to pass thoughts back and forth between each other. But when [Better Together] was on, their minds became one, the thoughts instantaneous and their beings mingled.

Beepy’s core strain settled as Zippy’s essence flashed over to balance the throughput. Zippy locked the portals in, their essence drain low once cast. Together they pushed against the limits of the System, twisting portals and essence from sacrificed relics to protect their friends.

The System assessed two of it’s favoured and found their acts formative to its balance. Those two were small still, as were that two, and those three, and those two as well. But in its best assessment, they were all imperative. It decided in a rare act of choice to open their growing minds to the rest of the gander. Just enough to allow them to gaze upon the potential of themselves, as rarely did the River of Parallels flow together as it did in this moment.

And though the System was incapable of fear, as it was just a System after all, it felt the longing to notify these favoured across the planed. It lifted their many minds.

The battle felt distant. They heard Aria asking them questions and poking their shoulders. They felt the attacks that made it through the portals coating on the Paladome, and their combined power held it all back, because together they had become aware of something else far more acute.

It wasn’t the System watching they noticed, though they felt it’s endless, churning gaze watching.

And it wasn’t just something else. It was thousands of somethings. A hive of distant minds a skip away in eerily similar situations, all slightly different, all slightly curious as to where the occasional copy of the [Ancient Paring Knife]s had gone, and all slightly startled at what they were encountering.

They froze at the distant pinging awareness of each other. A speckling that seemed so numerous in the vastness of its availability, out there and nearby, calling and frustratingly out of reach. They all felt like friends. Maybe more than friends.

Other brothers?

The System watched it’s many favoured across the various planes, never allowing its awareness to falter a smidgen amongst the River of Parallels. Though it didn’t know fear, it knew something was very wrong with the balance. It had to show someone. Or many someones that had gained its trust.

Then Beepy and Zippy, alongside many others, saw the infinite backdrop of nothingness that the many minds sparked against. Was it potential lost? Was it other minds that hadn’t made it?

Was it to do with the balance they felt the urge to fight for?

A ripple of fear loosed from Beepy, and Zippy reached and clasped around it, ensuring his brother was protected. He could handle it, and would handle it for both. Many of the others followed suit. After all, they were quite similar in their uniqueness.

Zippy, and the many similar to Zippy, silently and alone held back his brother’s fear, and gazed upon the dark of the infinite.

Each mind was alone in their thoughts, aware of the others, gazing into the abyss and the friendly specks.

One winked out against the dark, followed by another.

The System stopped it’s favour in the next moment. The ten it granted were enough, and anymore would surely drive them insane.

***

Beepy and Zippy were shoved back into their bodies, still connected with [Better Together].

They opened their eyes together, knowing that there was much to discuss. Once they helped their friends.

“ARIA,” Beepy announced, his voice barely catching over the screams of pain against the Paladome. “WE MUST SPEAK AFTER.”

“What the shit was that?” Aria yelped, looking accusatorily at Beepy and Zippy. “You went all still! It’s bullshit you two can talk together without me!”

Beepy sent a ping to Zippy to collate the information. Zippy was much better at understanding, and Beepy relied on him to make big problems known. He was momentarily shocked to find Zippy scrambling to shut some part of his mind away, but he’d witnessed the lingering thought.

Fear is what makes us brave.

They couldn’t hide anything from each other with [Better Together] on. And Beepy had seen his brother’s fear.

“Guys!” A voice shouted from behind them.

It was Alex.

Beep decided he would speak with him later privately.

“GUYS! I’m the fucking man! Shit was easy! It was right there where Tank said it would be! [Burrow Sense] almost friend my brains! But I went zen and focused and BOOM! It’s TINY, but I GOT THE FUCKING TAX GUILD—”

He felt burning hope blossoming in his chest.

WOOF, Tank barked and sent mental images over, his tail cracking Brody in the ribs painfully. Brody snorted at the mental image of everyone riding Tank as the dog ran them away away.

Yeah…, Brody thought, not sure that’s going to work, Tank. Guess it’s time to figure that bit out…

He had no doubt that they would. All together, of course.
