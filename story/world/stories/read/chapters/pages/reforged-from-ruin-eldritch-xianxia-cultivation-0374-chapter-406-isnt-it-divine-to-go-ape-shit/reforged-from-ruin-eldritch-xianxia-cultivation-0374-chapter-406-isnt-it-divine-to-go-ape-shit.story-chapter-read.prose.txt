In spite of everything, it almost reminds her of old times. Being strong in unexpected ways, being hard to detect due to weird curses and “spells” she casts on herself, finding a way to surprise and undercut those who would try to destroy her. Or who get in her way. Or who treated her in a way that she felt better suited them to get the shit kicked out of them.

It’s weird, acknowledging that she’s a hypocrite. For most of her life, she’s been dealing with the unfair, unjust bullshit of those with power. Whether that’s been bureaucracy holding her down, violence and violation from more individuals, or the sort of institutional restriction and consequence that an Empire can enforce, she’s been trapped by the ideas others have for what she should be, what should be allowed, for as long as she can remember.

And yet, when she has a bit of power, does she not act the same way? Does she not reach out and bend the world to her will? Does she not stare down others who would try to do things she finds unjust, or wrong, and act against them?

Yes. Yes she does.

It’s a messy thought. People have been grappling for millenia, trying to figure out what it means to have justice, what it means to enforce one’s will over another, what it means to try to shape the world rather than accept it. Kai Ra’s sure that there are quite a few schools of thought and quite a few philosophers who would be ready and willing to grapple about it some more.

In the moment, however, that falls to the wayside.

She has the opportunity to fuck up something that does not work and is bad. She has the opportunity to punish those who would try to harm her son, who would take and use him for their own ends. She has the chance to fuck some shit up that has earned it.
