Sora stood in the middle of the forest, his breathing steady, the air around him thick with mana. The early morning sunlight filtered through the trees, casting shifting patterns of light on the ground beneath him. He had been continuously channeling all his Martial Titan skills since he'd read the skills description, flooding his muscles and bones with mana, reinforcing them as much as possible. He could feel the subtle changes within his body. His bones were hardening, his muscles becoming denser and more responsive. But for Sora, it wasn't enough. He wanted more.

He absorbed mana at a crazed rate, pulling from the abundant ambient energy around him, compressing it within his body to speed up the forging process. It was something most players couldn't do, not at the pace he was pushing. But his supreme skill, his [Mana Evolution], had evolved his mana control and quality to a level where absorbing and refining mana was second nature. Every breath, every beat of his heart, was in sync with the energy moving inside his body.

An hour passed like this—Sora's body constantly absorbing and refining mana, driving the [Bone forging] and [Muscle forging] skills to their limits. Yet, after an hour of effort, something changed.

He stopped, feeling a sudden block. No matter how much he tried to push the mana further, to guide it into his bones and muscles, his body simply refused to absorb more. It was as if he'd hit some kind of invisible barrier. He attempted to force it for a moment longer, but his body resisted.

Sora took a deep breath, stepping back and letting the tension ease from his frame. "I've hit a limit," he muttered to himself. His mind raced with questions. Limits?

'This is interesting. How do I break this limit now?' he thought to himself, before remembering the [Limit Breaker] skill he had. When he understood what he needed to do to push his body further, he started accumulating Titan Energy. To create such energy, he had to filter the mana he had, and store part of it inside his dantian.

After a few hours, the energy reached its peak. He couldn’t store any more.

He closed his eyes and focused inward, taking a look at the pool of Titan Energy he had been refining. The dense energy remained still within him, waiting for the moment he would need it.

"So, it's time..." he whispered to himself, excitement building. He could finally break through the first of his body's limits.

‘This will take me beyond the current cap my body level has, and open the door to even greater strength.’ Sora was excited at the prospect of bridging the gap between his body and energy levels.

He stood and braced himself, pulling the Titan Energy from deep within his dantian and circulating it throughout his body. The instant the energy moved, a searing pain shot through him, like molten lava coursing through his veins. His muscles tensed and his bones felt as if they were cracking under the immense pressure. Sweat beaded on his forehead, and his body trembled.

He clenched his fists, feeling the energy slamming into his bones and muscles, trying to break through the invisible barrier that had held him back.

"This is... intense," he gritted through clenched teeth as he started feeling pain all over his body.

Sora felt the pain increase, but he chose to ignore it.

He couldn't help but think, ‘Wouldn’t it have been much more dangerous if I hadn't already tempered my body with the training gear every time I could? I’m also used to this level of pain.’ Because his leveling speed rose with each reset, the settings of his training gear were always pushed to the extreme.

Everytime his accumulated Titan Energy failed to break the wall of his limits, a shockwave spread through his body. But Sora had something else working in his favor. Each shockwave was met with mana that defended Sora’s organs, blood and vessels.

He understood that with such a method, even with the guidance of the class, there was no guarantee of success in breaking through the limits.

Sora's [Mana Evolution] helped him control his Martial Titan’s skill more precisely and efficiently than others. As it surged through his body, Sora adjusted its flow, increasing his success rate by each passing second.

Slowly but surely, the pain began to subside. His body was adapting to the immense pressure, and the cycles of energy ramming into it.

Though it felt like hours, minutes passed before he heard a crack. The limit within his body shattered, releasing a flood of energy that rushed through him like a tsunami.

His bones and muscles seemed to expand and contract at once, welcoming their new potential. The pain soon vanished, and was replaced by a sense of pleasure.

"That... was amazing," Sora muttered, clenching and unclenching his fists.

His muscles felt denser, stronger, more alive. Even his bones felt different—more solid, more resilient. He flexed his arm, marveling at the feeling of power.

‘I’ve done it. I have broken through my limits for the first time.’

Sora sat back down on the ground, letting the adrenaline drain from his body. His clothes were soaked with sweat, and blood.

Hiyoko, perched comfortably on his shoulder, chirped softly, its head tilted curiously as it watched Sora. The little bird's feathers fluffed up as if sensing the change in Sora's aura. It hopped slightly, nudging Sora's neck with its beak.

Sora chuckled, gently rubbing Hiyoko's tiny head with a finger. "You're just as curious as I am, huh?" he said with a smile. "Let's see what I can do now. Wanna spar with me?"

He wanted to test his physical strength, to see how far this new class had taken him. He could feel the difference, but he wasn’t able to gauge it. The only way to measure it was through combat.

"Let's find something big," he muttered when Hiyoko shook her head. Hiyoko chirped happily when it felt Sora move.

He activated his [Monster radar], scanning the area for something challenging. He wasn't looking for an easy fight.

‘I’m level 38, so I might as well look for a level 45 or 46 monster.’ He thought, before finding a level forty-five beast.

‘Perfect.’

The target was a Grathorn, a hulking creature resembling a rhinoceros, but twice the size and covered in jagged, stone-like armor. Its head was adorned with two massive horns, which it used to kill its enemies. It was known for its overwhelming physical strength and its endurance.

‘It’s probably stronger than me, but this is what I wanted,’ Sora thought as he unequipped his training gear.

As he made his way toward the Grathorn, Sora deliberately blocked off his mana flow. No mana would reinforce his body, no skills would assist him—except for Titan Regeneration, which he kept active to heal any damage his body took.

When the Grathorn spotted him, it let out a growl. Its massive hooves dug into the ground as it was ready to charge. Ready for a fight, Sora braced for the impact.

He stood his ground, waiting until the last possible second. Just as the beast's horns were about to impale him, he leaped to the side, barely dodging the attack.

The Grathorn stopped rushing, turned to face its enemy again. However, Sora dashed forward, and threw a punch with all his strength into the side of the creature. His fist connected with the creature's stone-like armor, and a shockwave ran up his arm from the impact.

‘What the ?!’ Sora’s fist almost broke, and he jumped back, holding his hand in pain.

But the Grathorn seemed to be in pain as well. It staggered slightly, the force of Sora's punch disrupting its balance, but it quickly recovered. Before Sora could move out of the way, the beast swung its massive head, and slammed into Sora with one of its horns. The blow sent him flying, crashing into the ground several meters away.

Sora groaned, pain shooting through his side. He felt a rib – or two– crack. His body screamed from pain, but Titan Regeneration kicked in, and he could feel his bones already starting to knit themselves back together.

The beast charged again and Sora stood up despite the pain.

He dodged another charge, feeling the wind whip past him as the beast's horns missed him by inches. He retaliated with another punch, this time aiming for the beast's legs. The blow was powerful, and it stumbled.

But Sora could combo it, it was back up. The monster lashed out with its front leg, catching Sora in the air and sending him flying. This time, he felt more bones breaking—his forearm, and a couple more ribs. He gasped, struggling to breathe as his body trembled in pain. Blood dripped from his mouth, but he refused to stop.

Titan Regeneration worked tirelessly, slowly repairing the damage, but it wasn't immediate.

‘This damn regen skill is too slow. Won’t I die before it kicks in?’ He complained inwardly, but didn’t give up. ‘Let’s see how much my body can take…’

As the beast lunged one more time, Sora planted his feet and thrust both hands out, catching the Grathorn's horns with all his might and accompanying it to redirect its power to counterattack.

The impact was brutal, but Sora held firm. His arms screamed in pain, some of his bones cracking under the pressure, but he managed to lift the beast and smash it on its back.

The Grathorn howled in pain, its massive body collapsing to the ground. Before it could move, Sora started punching its weak points with all his strength. One punch after another, he beat it down. It resisted, and managed to kick Sora away a few times, but he came back angrier each time.

After a few minutes, Sora stood over the beast, breathing heavily, blood trickling from the corners of his mouth. His entire body was battered and bruised, but he had done it. He had taken down a level 45 beast with nothing but his raw physical strength.

He fell to one knee, exhausted.

After winning, Titan Regeneration accelerated, quickly healing the worst of his injuries. Hiyoko jumped down from a nearby tree, landing softly on his shoulder.

"Not bad, huh?" Sora muttered, wincing as his body continued to heal. Hiyoko chirped, seemingly disappointed with Sora's performance, though the little bird's eyes showed concern for his injuries.

“I didn’t use mana! It was impressive, trust me!”  Sora wiped the sweat from his brow, glancing down at the fallen Grathorn. If he had amplified his body with mana, if he had used his items, his skills, he could've probably killed it in one blow.

‘[Martial Titan]... This class isn’t bad.’
