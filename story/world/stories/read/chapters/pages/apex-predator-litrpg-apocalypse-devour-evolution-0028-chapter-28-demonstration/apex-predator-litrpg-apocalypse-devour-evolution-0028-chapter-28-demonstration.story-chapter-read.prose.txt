The colosseum was silent. There was no crowd. No fanfare or an over-eager announcer asking whether people were ready to rumble. Just wind across the sand.

Don leaned forward on the couch, elbows on knees, watching the two Evolutors in the arena. He felt excitement bubbling in his chest. He would finally get to see how high level creatures fought.

The two combatants stood still at first. The Reaper—black and broad and almost armored in muscle—snorted once and lowered its head like a bull. Thick cables of toxin-tubing flexed along its sides. The Dominator hovered gently two feet above the ground, shrouded in that pale psychic bubble.

Mal’asha didn’t speak. She simply watched with a pleased smile on her wide mouth.

Then the Reaper moved.

It was shockingly fast. Blink and you’ll miss it. Don flinched despite himself.

“Oh…” Mal’asha said off-handedly. “I forgot your Mental is very low. I will slow down the feed.”

The video slowed down by at least half. But the Reaper still moved in a blur. The beast tore forward, claws carving into the stone with each step. The ground shook slightly with each stride. A faint hiss followed in its wake, and Don realized it was exuding some kind of  green gas. It started filling the room. Whatever it was, the Dominator didn’t wait to find out.

White lightning snapped across the arena as the Dominator shot backward through the air like it had been slingshotted. No wings. No visible thrust. No acceleration. It just moved. Telekinesis.

The Reaper didn’t stop. It rose on its hindlegs and revealed a cluster of green spines in its underbelly. It roared and the spines started shooting out.

The venomous darts filled the air in a fusillade as the Dominator dodged around, pulling into dives and dashing in the air behind buildings. The quills penetrated the buildings, throwing rubble and dust as they exploded through them.

Most of them the Dominator dodged, but some hit the bubble protecting it. It held for now. But before the rubble from the buildings could fall on the ground, they were gathered up by an invisible hand, and crushed into small pebbles. The pebbled shot back at the Reaper and it had to stop its attack.

The green fumes were now everywhere, filling up the arena up to ten feet in the air.

The Dominator’s bubble shimmered faintly, then collapsed into nothing. Don noticed that the creature was holding its shoulder. One of the quills had struck. The shoulder had turned black and there were green lines like crawling veins protruding from the impact point.

The Dominator didn’t hesitate. It revealed thin white claws and cut off its arm. Immediately one started regrowing.

Then it turned to its enemy and raised a hand.

The Reaper was now sprinting forward. Charging at the Dominator with all of its hulking body. It jumped through the green gas and tried to slam the Dominator out of the air.

The Dominator clenched its fist.

The Reaper fell out of the air like a rock. With tremendous force, it crashed into a half-crumbled building. The poison cloud burst upward. The Dominator flew higher, but it had clearly gotten a whiff of the gas. It was coughing. Don raised an eyebrow.

“How did it do that?” he asked. “It just stopped the Reaper mid-air.”

“Telekinetic field,” Mal’asha said. “The Dominator tethered the Reaper and ground together.”

“Nice,” Don nodded. “But looks like the poison got to it.”

Mal’asha pursed her lips. “The fight’s not over yet.

The Reaper was already up. It didn’t roar. It didn’t posture. It just launched forward again, this time cutting sideways to dodge another spray of rubble fanning out at breakneck speed at it.

The Dominator flinched, like it sensed something. It pulled a wall from the ground between itself and the Reaper. A bolt of kinetic force cracked across the arena. The Reaper had spread out beetle-like wings from its back and was now airborne. It was now polluting the air in swift stripes as it dashed around.

There was nowhere for the Dominator to run. It charged energy around its body, and soon something shimmering, like white lightning crackled around it.

Then the Dominator moved.

Even with halved speed, Don’s eyes couldn’t keep up. The Dominator basically vanished and reappeared, blinking through space, leaving afterimages behind.

But the Reaper matched its speed, hurling through the air like a wrecking ball.

They collided. Don sat forward.

The Reaper’s claws tore forward in a savage arc. The Dominator didn’t block. Instead, it phased slightly sideways. Not teleporting. Just… bending space. The claw passed through nothing. Then the Dominator was inside the Reaper’s guard. It touched its palm to the black beast’s chest and pushed.

The sound of a bullet resounded in the air. A shockwave followed and the Reaper crashed into the side of the colosseum.

It smashed into a stone pillar and cracked it clean in half. The pillar collapsed in a spray of dust and rock. The Reaper lay still for just a moment, then got up. Slowly. Something green oozed from its ribs. It moved weakly. Then its back suddenly grew ablaze with green flames and it zipped at the Dominator with breakneck speed.

The Dominator was still flying overhead, watching its foe. The Reaper was too fast even for it. The shimmering bubble and high speed that turned the violent charge into a glancing blow saved the Dominator.

“So strong,” Don muttered.

“He is,” Mal’asha said, nodding. “This was a berserk ability, much like yours.

“The poison is doing work on the Dominator,” Don noted. “It’s slowing down.”

“The fight ends soon,” Mal’asha said.

Then it happened fast.

The Reaper charged again in the air, this time lower. It didn’t aim for the Dominator’s chest. It aimed for the ground just below.

It surged, claws-first, smashing through the earth with sheer weight. A shockwave rippled through the arena. Rubble and poisonous gas flew in arcs in the air towards the Dominator. The white Evolutor floated upward to dodge—

—and the Reaper was already above it.

A faint shimmer in the air. No jump pads. No tricks. Just raw muscle. The Reaper had launched itself off the collapsing terrain like a spring trap.

The Dominator raised both hands. The bubble pulsed. Force crackled in the air between them.

Then the Reaper struck.

Its claws hit the shield and the world shook.

A ripple of white-blue light burst out from the point of contact. The shield held. Barely. Cracks ran along its surface like spiderwebs. The Reaper kept pressing. It snarled, toxic gas venting from the tubes along its neck. The Dominator’s eyes flared with white light. The barrier reformed in an instant and pushed outward, sending the Reaper tumbling away.

Don caught his breath.

“That thing is a freight train,” he said.

Mal’asha smiled. “And the other is a scalpel.”

The Dominator didn’t pursue. Instead, it rose higher, floating at the edge of the arena like a phantom. It was bleeding and poisoned, and clearly on its last legs. Its hands traced symbols in the air. Purple and white glyphs appeared like stars on the nightsky. They hovered, glowing faintly. Don couldn’t read them, but he felt the pressure build.

The Reaper landed back on all fours and launched forward again.

This time, the Dominator didn’t move.

It let the Reaper come.

Then the symbols snapped shut.

The Reaper’s body locked mid-air. All four limbs froze in place like a statue. Then the pressure increased. Don could see the black muscle strain against the unseen force. The Reaper writhed. Fought.

Then the Dominator made a fist.

There was a sound. Wet. Sharp. Something between a bone breaking and a powerline snapping.

But the Dominator wasn’t done. From its forehead, an eye opened. Large, unblinking, black as space. There was a sucking sound before the whole arena erupted in purple and white light. A laser beam shot out from the Dominator and struck the crumpled up Reaper, raining pure destruction on it.

It collapsed on the ground, a smoking, twitching mess.

“So that’s it?” Don asked.

“Not quite…” Mal’asha said. “The Reapers are… durable.”

Don looked at the twitching mess that was the Reaper, and he gaped in shock.

Despite the absolute devastation of its body, the Reaper was smiling. A monstrous, animal grin.

Then the Reaper coughed and hacked.  The tubes on its sides unhinged from its body and started pulsing out a dark green mass on the ground.

The Dominator was on the ground, breathing heavily and leaning against a pile of concrete. It coughed up something gray, that Don assumed was blood. It was on its last legs. It struggled towards the Reaper as it kept pouring the green goop on the ground.

The Dominator attempted to throw some rocks at it, but it mostly missed. But it was preparing new glyphs around it that grew stronger and brighter.

As the toxin tubes were exhausted, Don noticed that the Reaper’s body had basically turned into crumpled paper. A thin, lifeless husk. But the green mass on the ground twitched, boiled and bubbled until it started to take shape and a new Reaper with an intact body was standing again.

“Holy shit,” Don whispered.

“Yes…” Mal’asha said. “It lost a few levels doing that, but it's a good trick.”

The new Reaper rose on its hindlegs and charged something in its chest. Out of its mouth burst out a giant glob of shining green acid. It flew at the Dominator like a bullet. The moment it hit the ground near the Dominator, it exploded in a splash of corrosive green. The Dominator vanished inside it.

Don leaned in.

The acid mist hung in the air like fog. No movement. Just sizzling and hissing.

Then something cut through the green. A streak of purple and white energy surrounded by floating glyphs.

The Dominator shot out of the cloud like a bullet, trailing smoke. One shoulder was blackened. Burned. But the eyes were bright with white light.

It raised both hands. The ground beneath the Reaper cracked.

Then—without warning—the Reaper exploded upward again.

The Dominator caught it mid-flight.

They spun in the air, a flurry of limbs and claws. The Reaper thrashed, poison venting. The Dominator screamed—not in pain, but effort—and then pushed one hand against the Reaper’s forehead.

The Reaper convulsed.

Its limbs locked up completely straight.

The pair of combatants crashed into the ground, sending rubble and dust everywhere. When it settled, Don saw the Reaper on the ground, and the Dominator on top of it, still holding its head.

“Neural overload,” Mal’asha said calmly. “Too much psychic feedback. Muscles lock. Systems reboot. Insects do it. Beasts do it. Evolutors do it too. When the Dominator has to get close and personal, that is how it fights. A poison of its own, if you will.”

Don watched as the Reaper twitched once. The Dominator was still holding it, lifting it off the ground from its face.

The Dominator snapped the fingers of its free hand. The Reaper’s body exploded.

The explosion however was contained into a sphere, like a bubble to avoid the mess from spreading. The bubble then compressed into the size of a tennis ball and the Dominator swallowed it, and soon its body started to heal.

The arena faded into black. The screen shut off. Silence returned.

Don exhaled.

“That was insane.”

Mal’asha turned her head slowly to Don, as she lounged on the sofa, resting her porcelain cheek against one hand. A pleased grin spread across her too-wide mouth.

“Yes… Now then, Don Damon. It’s time you choose. What manner of monster will you become?”
