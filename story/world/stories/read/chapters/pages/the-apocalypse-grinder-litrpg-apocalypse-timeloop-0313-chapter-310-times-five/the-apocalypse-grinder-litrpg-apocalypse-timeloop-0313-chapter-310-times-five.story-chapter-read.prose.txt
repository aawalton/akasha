The new control mastery made it a total of five merged masteries, which Ronan believed might unlock a new class evolution for Perfectionist other than the basic path which simply improved the mastery gain bonuses without much else to offer. If it offered an easier time merging masteries or something equally powerful he was sure to see a big upgrade to his combat prowess.

That was a useful class evolution. The stat points weren’t that high, but his two sub-classes had that covered more than enough. To be honest Ronan would have taken the evolution even without the mastery gain bonuses purely for the first buff it offered. Having a list of every single mastery that existed was an absurd boon. That he also got an increase to his mastery gain, and also gained new ways to merge masteries, if he was reading the last bonus correctly, was just the icing on the cake.

Ronan went through the relatively painless class evolution and immediately pulled up the list to have a look at what he had missed out on and what else he could incorporate into his style. There were likely to be a near-infinite number of masteries he would never have a use for, but there were sure to be many obvious ones he’d missed out on during his fairly limited number of iterations. Maybe calling over a hundred lives a limiting amount of time is a bit unfair on people who only get one? He shrugged. It wasn’t his problem to cater to the feelings of mortals. He just wanted to get stronger so he could overcome the system.
