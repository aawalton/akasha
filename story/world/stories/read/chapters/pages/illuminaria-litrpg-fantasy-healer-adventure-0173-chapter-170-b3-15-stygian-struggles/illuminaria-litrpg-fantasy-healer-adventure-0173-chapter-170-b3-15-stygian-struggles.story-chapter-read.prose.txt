Chapter 170 / 15: Stygian Struggles

‘Yuk, get back here!’ Joe thought as loudly as he could through their private connection. He received back an oddly fragmented reply, as if Yuk was too spread out to form complete sentences. He felt a sense of affirmation and a single flash of information.

Mournful Thrall: Level 27: Undead (Dirge Mummy): Controller: Spirit

The broken image of the abomination looked like someone had bound a putrid, rotting werebear in oily, broken wrappings. It swayed and shambled behind the faster wights, slowing but inexorably drawing closer. Its manner held a purposeful malevolence that surpassed the rabid madness of the wolf or boar wights.

Looking across the vast cavern with her [Deeprealm Goggles], Kendell relayed what she was seeing. “It looks like the wights are outpacing those big things. We should move back into the hallway. When the first wave gets here, don’t hold back. We should try to clear these lesser ghasts before those big guys reach us. Then we can figure out how bad the big guys are. It’s either that or we run back up the way we came down and look for another path.”

As Joe explained what Yuk had shared with him, the connection to the group reestablished itself. Enough of the raggamuffin was back.

“There are more of the boars. One of them alone almost broke my barrier. We can’t count on a full defensive wall,” Tezeno pronounced.

“I’ll stay up front with you,” the axewoman agreed as she drew out more vials of the sacred oil she had brought. “Joe, see if you can use your [Halefires] to split their mob apart. Try to limit how many we face at once. Yuk, what you got that’ll help us?”

‘[Biting Vortex],’ the disembodied voice replied. ‘We’ll keep it churning right in front of you two. Burns through a lot of stamina. Only keep it up for ten seconds at a time. Nothing’ll get to the wall without getting chewed up some first.’

“Perfect. If anyone has any objections or suggestions, now's the time.”

“I’ll place a [Tracking Mark] on the closest mummy,” Hah’roo offered. “If it looks like it will reach us before we clear the ghouls, I can place a [Gale Wall] between us. They will be able to push through it, but the spell should give us a few seconds to disengage and retreat.”

With light and walls around them again, any last traces of terror vanished. Joe was still apprehensive; they were facing a far larger and higher-level force without the Hellions than they had with the a-team. Still, if they could keep the advantages they had in the small fight, he was confident he could keep them all alive while his allies tore the enemy down.

Granted, the first strike was Joe’s. As the mass of ghoulish runners reached them, he unloaded two fully charged [Grit Razors] before stepping back behind Tezeno and Kendell. Hawking spooled dozens of damaged, maimed, and killed alerts past the corner of Joe’s vision.

Those who survived the spray of obsidian shards ran into Yuk’s effect. A ten-foot cloud of buzzing, vicious critters filled the air just outside Tezeno’s barricade. It was impossible to distinguish the individual insects, but their effect was undeniable. Joe watched as the attackers’ ghastly flesh was stripped away, in places, down to the bone.

He placed a pair of [Halefires] in the middle of the pack of wights, causing those in the rear of the throng to halt and draw back, while those trapped between the phoenix-powered flames and the force wall were being taken apart by the combined effects of Yuk’s vortex, Joe’s undead burning auras, and the team’s direct attacks.

Joe had no time for his curses or more [Grit Razors]. Dozens of mental attacks were hammering into his team. Dual-casting [Purge] took all of his focus. The only thing he spared effort for was to use [Efferous Endurance] on Tez and Yuk, and [Swap Stamina] on himself since the [Halefires] were eating through massive amounts of mana as they burned into the horde of undead ghasts.

Weakened by the dual [Halefires] and ravaged by Yuk’s swarming insects, the wights were falling to the combined strikes from Hah’roo, Kenda, and Wen. The boarish chargers received the women’s attention first, to keep the brutes from cracking through their primary defense.

“Hah’roo,” the archon barked. “How far out are the Mournful Thralls?”

“Hundred yards,” the galing answered as she sent her rope dart drilling into the head of the ghoul trying to reach Kendell. “At their pace, maybe two minutes.”

“We need to clear these,” Kendell called. “If anyone has a one-use AoE they haven't told me about, now’s the time to use it.”

“Your world has grenades?” Joe couldn’t help but exclaim.

“Yeah,” Wen retorted. “Her name is Taylyn, but she’s up there. Not down here with us.”

“Focus, people,” the skill-savant urged. “Does anyone have a big hit stashed away?”

‘We do,’ uttered the voice in their heads, ‘but if we use it, we’ll be out of commission for quite a while. At least half an hour.’

“Don’t! We need your front-end damage, and scouting,” the trainer commanded. When no one else spoke up, Kenda sighed. “Ok, we found a weakness in our group. We lack area attacks. Nothing to do about it now but …”

“Save the buts, Kenda,” Tez interrupted. “You and I should go on offense for a minute and a quarter. Let’s clear this line. Joe, keep our heads clear.”

With those words, Tezeno shifted his stance and began battering the undead in front of him, through the field, which began to wobble as the archon lifted his full focus from it. Using both his shield and a truncheon in his other hand, he pounded the attacking ghouls. The party could hear bones shattering under the sentinel’s hammering blows. The archon took a few hits he normally would have blocked, but they made only a slight dent in the man’s massive health pool, which Joe refilled a second later.

Kendell’s style focused more on riposting the attacks. Her long-handled axes shunted aside clawed strikes, while they hooked arms and necks. After yanking the enemy off balance, she followed through with vicious cuts of her own.

The all-out assault chewed through the pack. In far less time than Tezeno had allotted, the team was in the process of destroying the last few wights in reach. There were more of the fleet-footed dead racing across the grotto from further away. Not to mention the more ponderous Mourners. Still, it looked like they had a moment to heal and cleanse before any more of the enemy would be able to close with them.

Or they would have, until Yuk announced a warning. ‘We have a problem, guys! There are more coming down the passage behind us. A lot more! Maybe a minute away.’

Wen held up a hand and cocked her head, listening to something only her supernatural perception was able to perceive. Hah'roo exhaled a deep breath and stopped to listen as well. A second later, they both gave the group grave-looking nods, confirming the discorporated tatterdemalion’s warning.

They did not have enough team members to hold on two fronts. They’d be surrounded and overwhelmed in no time.

“Looks like we are going to have to get pulled out,” Kendall groaned. “Tez, what does the beacon look like?”

“I checked it when we stopped. It’s not great. There is a spark, but barely. It will take a while for him to get the signal. Too long in my opinion. Better we head back up. How many do you guys hear? Can we push through them?”

“Doubtful,” Earcellwen replied in a rushed voice. “There sounds like a lot of them.”

“The winds speak of dozens,” Hah’roo confirmed.

‘That’s our guess, too. Well over twenty.’

“Too many for most of us to break through before we are overrun from behind,” Hah’roo expressed. “I vote we run for one of those,” she stated, pointing across the cavern. On the far wall were cave openings, now visible thanks to the [Halefires] burning in the middle of the giant chasm. “There are stronger currents of air across the cavern from us. I do not believe we can hold here. Better a gamble than a sure defeat. Once there, we find either a passage to the surface or a spot to hold long enough for the sorcerer to fetch us.”

“Anyone have a better idea? If not, we need to go now,” Tezeno droned, somehoow finding a way to stress the word ‘now’ even with his emotionless tone.

No one did.

Hah’roo nodded to the archon as Joe exploded the last ghoul with his amber paw and witch curse. “Stay on my heels,” she ordered, adding, “Speed boost, Wen.”

Earcellwen activated her [Spirit of Stags] aura, and Joe felt a strong breeze at their backs, pushing them forward even faster. The five of them dashed across the rocky floor while Yuk rode along, spread out pretty much across everyone.

‘We are outrunning my [Questing Breath],’ Hah’roo thought to the sprinting group. ‘I can’t tell what’s in front of us. Does anyone have a reason to pick one of those openings over another one? Otherwise, I’m just going to pick one without any wights in the way.’

‘Ramp! A little to the left of where we are headed,’ Yuk thought. ‘It feels quiet.’

The galeling adjusted her course. The new route took them slightly closer to the shambling dark mummies, but the bigger danger was the ghouls that were closing on the party, even with the pair of acceleration buffs.

Hah’roo bound up the ramp. Wen and Joe, as well as Yuk by default, were right on her tail. Kenda was not quite as fast, but she was still considerably quicker than the sentinel. Tezeno’s build was not designed for speed.

As the others were nearing the opening at the top of the inclined slope, Joe hung back.

“Go, you idiot,” the archon shouted, oblivious to the pack of ghouls that were almost on top of him.

“Hand!” Joe shouted, reaching behind him like a relay racer.

As soon as he had a grip on his friend, Joe teleported them both as far up the ramp as the medallion would carry them. Tezeno, having more momentum than Joe at that moment, ran ahead, but [Hunter’s Pursuit] put Joe right on his heels a second later.

“Ok, that was a good plan,” the guardian admitted.

“Thanks,” Joe huffed out loud before thinking to the group. ‘Hope you’re right, Yuks. If there are ghouls in here, we are in trouble.’

‘We’re sure. The bugs know there are a lot of cracks and crevasses the dead might be able to crawl through, but we’re positive there are no dead in there now.’

‘Good enough for me,’ Joe sent back, as he and the archon crossed through the opening, into the tunnel beyond.

Yuk had not been wrong about the layout. Unlike the smooth, solid hallways they had been traveling so far, this was a natural fissure. It twisted back and forth, with openings in both walls. There were large cracks in both the ceiling and the floor. Thankfully, the lower ones had been filled in with small rocks and gravel, making a path they could follow without having to jump over the ditches.

‘The way ahead has many more pathways, Hah’roo.’ Yuk thought to them all.

‘I was sensing the same thing with my [Questing Breath], Yuk. We’d get hit from all sides if we tried to hold there. This might be the best we are going to get. How long will we need, Kendell?’

‘A few minutes at least. Tez, you set up defenses while I start the beacon.’

The archon tossed Kenda the ruby-hued object and then gulped down a pair of potions to refill his mana and stamina. When a new barrier was erected, Joe dropped a pair of [Halefires] at the opening of the tunnel. He resummoned his own shield and coated it with the werewolf pelt; it had been easier to run without it.

As ready as they could be, the two exchanged worried glances before moving to cover the opening, side by side.
