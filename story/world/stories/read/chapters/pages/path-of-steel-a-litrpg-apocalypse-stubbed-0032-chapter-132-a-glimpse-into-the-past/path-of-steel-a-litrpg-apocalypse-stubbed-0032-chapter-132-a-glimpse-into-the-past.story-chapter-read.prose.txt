“Again.”

Gary flicked his spear. It took everything he had to keep the drops of aura on its head from fusing. They splashed out, a fan of miniature thrusts, but he lost control as they left the range of his aura field. They sparked. Lightning arced from one drop to the next, their energies mixed, and in the end, they collapsed as a half-baked net of electricity.

“No!” Colorful said. “Focus on the concepts, Gary! Empty your mind. Don’t let lightning take over.”

Gary didn’t reply. He sat on the grass and grabbed his head with both hands. Colorful hesitated.

“Perhaps we were too hopeful,” he said. “I’ve pushed you past your limits, yet the third song eludes you… It is not your fault. You’ve tried hard enough. At this point, it’s just fate…”

“There’s still a day and a half, Master,” Gary said, raising bloodshot eyes. He wobbled to his feet. “I can do it.”

“Sit down, my boy. You’re so tired it’s impossible to make progress.”

“But I have to.”

“You have to do what maximizes your chance of survival. Right now, that’s rest.”

“Resting is resigning.”

“Resting feels like resigning. It’s not. If you don’t rest now, you’ll be a shell tomorrow, and if you lose, you’ll have to face Sui Duang in the afternoon. Winning will get you an extra half day of training.”

Gary considered losing on purpose just so he’d be spared the half day of torture. He shut out that thought. I must live. Whatever it takes. If I fail, they got away with it.

“Your mind was wandering,” Colorful said.

“It wasn’t.”

“Don’t lie to me, disciple. What are you thinking?”

Gary sat better. “Those things you said with Elder Sui… Was it true?”

Colorful’s expression didn’t change. However, his aura fluctuated. For someone like Gary to catch the aura fluctuations of someone so much stronger than him revealed the storm in his master’s heart.

“Everyone has lived a life, disciple,” he said. “I once challenged a man to a Duel of Honor. A brilliant man. Less than a century old and he was already a topper, but alas, he was a piece of shit. I lost. I was ordered to keep my life. That’s all.”

“Who was he, Master? What did he do to you?” Gary asked.

Colorful’s aura grew messier. Endless pain sang in it. Helplessness. Exhaustion. “His name is Sui Yuhuang: the successor of the Sui family, and after Kyle Mercy’s passing, the successor of the entire Heaven Reaching clan… Don’t concern yourself with me, disciple. Focus on your own opponent.”

“Can I ask you one more thing?”

“You may.”

“The reason you made me your disciple… Was it because I became enemies with Keng Sui and the Sui family?”

Colorful gave a bitter smile. “That was part of the reason. I’m sorry, disciple. When I saw you striving for righteousness against overwhelmingly powerful opponents, when you refused to bend the knee, I saw myself in you. I remembered what it was like to live… But, for what it matters, harming the Sui wasn’t the main reason I chose you. I just liked you and wanted to help you. I was hoping that, at least like this, this nameless old man could contribute to the world again…”

“I understand,” Gary said, standing up. “That’s all I needed to hear, Master. I’m here. Let’s show them what we can do.”

Colorful chuckled. “Fight for yourself, disciple, not for me. But, fine. Let’s train a little more, and then you rest.”

“Yes, Master!”

Gary dove into practice. His mental exhaustion remained, however, and soon he realized he was achieving nothing. With a heavy heart, he took off.

“We still have a day, disciple,” Colorful said. “If you can learn at least the third song, I might have a way to help you…but getting there is something you can only do yourself!”

“Yes, Master,” Gary said. He departed the manor and stumbled towards his cabin. Soon, however, he got a message.

Chloe: I found something you need to see.

Gary: I’m busy.

Chloe: Oh, trust me, you’re not. I’m at your cabin. Where are you?

Gary: On my way.

She waited by the path before the cabin area. It was the first time Gary saw her in her clan attire—she wore long, dark robes embossed with stars, and also the telltale wizard hat. She seemed like a Hogwarts disciple, but somehow, she pulled it off. Her brown hair fit snugly under the hat, and her spirited eyes certainly played the part of a curious wizard.

Her expression, however, was odd.

“What happened?” Gary asked, dropping his voice to a whisper. “Did the inspector catch anyone?”

“Not yet, thankfully. It’s nothing like that. Where can we get some privacy?”

“My cabin.”

He led her in, Chloe drawing some looks in the male section of the cabins. As soon as they entered, she took two dark pieces of fabric and covered the windows.

“What are you doing?” Gary asked.

“Just watch,” she replied. When the room was dark, she placed a device on the table and clicked a button. It hummed to life. A projection rose from it, clear and lifelike.

“Did you get this from the tournament presenter?” Gary asked.

“No, Gary. He was using a vibration; this is a magical device.”

“What are we watching?”

Chloe hesitated, her finger hovering over a button on the disk-like device.

“My clan has a repository of recordings from all over the tower. After the argument of your Elders in the morning, I looked it up. I didn’t expect to find anything, but apparently, a certain someone didn’t care about publicity…”

Gary’s eyes widened. “Do you mean…”

“I’ve already watched it. You should as well,” she said, clicking the button.

The device hummed again. The empty projection was replaced by a plaza where two men performed a ritual. There were throngs of people around them, and the projection seemed like it was being recorded from somewhere in the crowd. Cheers echoed. The atmosphere was festive.

“The successor is born! Blessings for a thousand years!” someone close to the recording exclaimed.

“We rise again! Bless the Sui! Bless the clan!” another replied. As the recording turned a little, Gary realized these weren’t audience members, but ceremonial announcers scattered around the edges of the plaza. They occasionally shouted stuff like that.

“It’s been so long…” an old audience member whispered close to the recording. Gary saw tears in her elderly eyes.

The voices of the two men in the plaza were lost in the clamor, but suddenly, something changed. A powerful gale. Lanterns were ripped off the ground, clothing flew, people gasped and fell on their knees. The sky turned darker—stormier. Rain fell.

“SUI YUHUANG!” an angry voice yelled, and the projection, now shaking, turned towards the sky. A single man stood there. His voice had been enough to recognize him, but even if it hadn’t, his multi-colored clothes would do the same.

“That’s my master?” Gary exclaimed. Colorful stood in the middle of the sky, alone above a massive crowd. He seemed younger than he currently was, though not young, but it was hard to tell because his face was distant and warped in rage.

“Who dares!?” one of the men in the plaza thundered. His voice stopped the wind and rain, protecting everyone in the crowd.

“SUI YUHUANG! COME UP HERE IMMEDIATELY!”

“Insolent! You use the name of my clan’s successor? Who are you?” the previous man replied.

“COME NOW OR I WILL SLAUGHTER EVERYONE!” Colorful screamed. He seemed completely beside himself. A thunder strike fell from above, crashing into an aura shield conjured over the plaza. The shield cracked.

As the speaking man was about to retort, his companion raised a hand. He was young and handsome. His hair was long, and his features square, unlike most of the Sui. He seemed like a hero, while his calmness in the face of Colorful breathed trust in the hearts of his people. Gary heard sighs of relief.

“I’m here,” the man said calmly. “Who are you, and what business do you have with me?”

“Who am I? You dare ask who am I? You raped my daughter, you son of a bitch!”

“Holy shit,” Gary said.

Gasps came from across the plaza. Sui Yuhuang raised a calming hand. “You must be Great Envoy Colorful,” he said. “You misunderstand.”

“I misunderstand? I MISUNDERSTAND!?”

Lightning and rain descended. Colorful whipped up a storm. The plaza was protected, but outside it, houses collapsed. People screamed. Sui Yuhuang took to the sky, spreading his hands to command the rain, shooting up to choke the clouds. The lightning stopped.

“Step down,” Sui Yuhuang growled. “You show up unannounced, you interrupt my crowning ritual, you slander me, you break our property and terrorize our people. I tried to give you respect, but you’re going too far, Colorful!”

“Going too far? I’m the one going too far? Hahaha!” Colorful laughed with sorrow. “I’m here to challenge you to a Duel of Honor, Sui Yuhuang! If you accept, I am going to kill you and wash away her blood with yours! If you don’t accept, I will slaughter your clan until you all kill me!”

“You spout bullshit. I did no such thing. If you truly believed it, you’d have gone to the Enforcement Hall.”

Colorful laughed again, even more crazily this time. “As if they can touch you, Sui Yuhuang! Come up here this instant or face family extermination!”

The second man from the plaza, who looked like an older version of Sui Yuhuang, also took to the sky and whispered something to him. Sui Yuhuang shook his head. He flew up. The recording followed him.

Then, it all blurred.

“They cut out the high-level battles. It messes with our growth, apparently,” Chloe apologized.

Gary was about to reply when the recording returned. It showed the same plaza as before, only now, a crater covered its center. Colorful lay in that crater, a bloody mess. Sui Yuhuang floated over him, red spear in hand, dripping crimson. He was also injured, but not as heavily.

There was also a new person. A well-built, blond man, seemingly in his forties.

“The clan cannot afford this loss. Heed my decree,” the blond man declared. “Sui Yuhuang, the accusations against you will be investigated. Colorful, your personal wealth will be used to reimburse the Sui family for the damage you caused. If it is not enough, the wealth of your closest relatives will be used as well. You must surrender your spear to Sui Yuhuang, and from today henceforth, you lose your honor. You may never act against the Sui family, directly or indirectly. You may keep your title, but neither you nor anyone can use your name. That person was killed today. What remains is Colorful, a nameless topper, a tool for the clan as honor demands. Moreover, as I forbade Sui Yuhuang from taking your life, you are also forbidden. You must live on. We’ll find you a suitable assignment where you can rest until the clan needs your strength. If you desire to abandon your life, wait until you can give it for the clan which raised you.”

The blond man’s words were so harsh that even the person recording recoiled. Gary felt bile rising in his throat. However, the blond man’s face was also awash with sorrow. He spoke with self-hatred, as if he, too, had no choice in the matter. Sui Yuhuang was calm.

“Thank you, Clan Head,” he said, cupping his hands.

Colorful curled up on the ground. His face could not be seen. Two men ripped the colorful spear from his hands, and the projection fizzled out, leaving Gary in a dark room.

“Holy shit,” he muttered, eyes shaking.

“Yeah. It was a big deal when it happened,” Chloe said. “I hope it wasn’t a mistake to show you.”

“I honestly don’t know, Chloe… I had no idea…”

“I’m sorry.”

Gary shook his head.

“Sui Yuhuang was declared completely innocent. Colorful’s daughter had lied to him. The baby was an illicit lover’s, whose name she never revealed," Chloe said.

“I’m sure that’s completely true,” Gary said.

“You never know.”

“I do know, Chloe. Master Colorful is a good man, and the Sui are notorious assholes. Sui Yuhuang did the crime.”

“If you say so.”

“Yes, I do say so.”

He stared her in the eyes but found only coldness. A moment later, her gaze softened. “Okay, I’m sorry. Sui Yuhuang was completely guilty, but as Colorful said in the recording, who could touch him? The tower is a cruel place.”

“I thought my master had just lost his spear, but this is on a whole different level…” Gary said. “Can you imagine losing to your daughter’s rapist and having to give up your revenge, your spear, and your name? I don’t understand why he still lives for the clan that betrayed him.”

“I don’t either…” Chloe said. “It must be horrible.”

“Yeah.”

“Well, I just wanted to show you this. I figured information is good to have.”

“I don’t think it will matter, but thanks. I really need to sleep now.”

“I’ll be going. Good luck tomorrow. I’ll be rooting for you!”

When Gary lay on his bed, somehow, sleep didn’t take him immediately. His head throbbed, but so did his heart. He felt deep, unfettered, uncontrollable rage building up inside him.

Will they do the same to me when I demand revenge? he wondered. Will they force me to give it up? Tell everyone I’m crazy?

The thought made him want to get up and break something. He patiently bottled his rage, saving it for when he’d need it.

I can’t touch that level yet, but one day I will. I’ll make it right. For now, Sui Duang… No matter what it takes, no matter what I have to sacrifice, I swear… I will kill you.
