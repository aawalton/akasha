I was fighting against the force and current of the river. It was dragging me down, but at the same time it was pushing me forward. Thankfully, I had gained a bit of fluffiness, so I was able to float rather comfortably.

Still, I didn’t want to be at the mercy of this stupid river, and I also wanted to see what sort of creature pushed me inside!

Maybe I should have seen it coming; this was a creepy place…

As the river churned, I was able to move closer to the shore, slowly but surely. Although it did feel more like it was guiding me toward the shore rather than me being able to get there.

It was rather annoying…

When I almost got there, the river sped up, and I got spat out, leaving me damp and confused.

What the fuck was that all about?

Unexplainable, but the hidden realm within the hidden realm seemed to have a mind of its own.

After drying myself off, I looked around to get familiar with the new area.

It was pretty far away despite the short amount of time that had passed, and I was already a few levels lower than where I should have been.

That sort of worked out for me, but I couldn’t help but wonder how many fruits and other plants I’d missed out on…

Maybe I could return after, but for the moment, it was clear that this realm wanted me to go somewhere, which was strange.

Making my way forward past some green twisting vines that almost seemed alive, hanging from trees that looked like they were about to jump me, I advanced.

My Divine Sense was turned up to eleven as I was careful not to get taken by surprise again. Back there I was also on full alert, yet I still got hit, so whatever it was couldn’t have been normal.

That aside, I moved through the creepy forest, and it wasn’t long before it became less dense. Almost as if I’d arrived at its end, or beginning.

Either way, when I passed through a giant wall of plants, I entered a completely different area.

It was… Well, it wasn’t lush green, but the cave’s ceiling lowered significantly, and the Yin Qi concentration was even thicker than before.

Taking a deep breath, I could feel my Ghost Qi Cultivation Technique flaring up, begging me to use it and to absorb the energy.

But I wasn’t going to do that, not now nor ever. I would get rid of this technique’s effect on my body as soon as I could!

Nevertheless, I kept going forward. The new area had a lot of vegetation, and it was illuminated by another type of Qi Fruit, but the light was more purple than blue.

As expected, it was called Qi Fruit, but the Qi inside was so dense it would probably be able to reinvigorate a Foundation Establishment Cultivator. It was clearly some very good stuff, so naturally I didn’t hesitate to collect as many of them as I could on the way.

Much to my surprise and excitement, the points went up by a staggering amount. Not only were these fruits better, but their bounty was also immense!

I supposed it made sense; nobody had ever been here before. Honestly, discovering this place in the first place should have made me the winner by default; how rude of the hidden realm not to do that for me!

Anywho, I kept walking, since there wasn’t much point in flying, and my flying abilities were also a little altered due to my new flufiness.

As I did, I noticed a large root sticking out of the ground, and when I saw its name, I almost couldn’t believe my eyes.

[Purple Ginseng]

The hair was a little purple upon closer inspection, and I could feel my heart beating wildly.

It was one of the ingredients needed for Yo Na’s elixir!

I’d gotten to digging immediately, and soon created a large, three-foot-deep hole and just as wide.

The purple ginseng was huge, almost the same size as my base form! It must have been at least more than five hundred years!

What a great find!

Since I had a lot more space in my bag of holding, I carefully placed a wing over the now free-moving ginseng and pulled it inside.

In permanent stasis, there was no way for it to die or go bad!

As I did that, hundreds, no, thousands of red dots appeared out of the place where it once stood, and they all rushed toward my forehead.

Almost immediately, the giant clusters on my forehead combined, forming the largest cluster I’d ever seen, and it didn’t stop there.

Another, equally giant cluster appeared right next to it, the two of them shrinking to accommodate a medium-sized cluster.

Just how many points was that? Heavens!

Giddy with excitement, I found myself walking faster. Not only did I get far more ginseng than I needed, but also a ton of points!

I was the richest bird around, hahaha!

As I kept walking, I kept finding plants of all sorts. Ginsengs, treasured berries that could be used in creating at least five different types of healing elixirs, many herbs that could be used for pills, and even some forging materials I had no use for.

It was like walking five steps forward would reward me with a treasure, and many, many points.

I was in the ninth cloud!

To the point that I’d almost forgotten I was in a secret realm…

Almost.

I was reminded of that when I got a sneaking suspicion that the red glowing fruit somewhere to the side wasn’t free for the taking like the rest.

I could feel a very rich and powerful fire affinity oozing off of it, and I was hungry for it.

However, considering its location, it was clear that the place had to be protected by something.

Even though it wasn’t the thing I was looking for… I really wanted it. And… it was only a Qi Condensation challenge; there was no way it would be difficult to grab, right?

Oh, how wrong I was…

Not long after I settled down, a giant ape with red fur and a yellow belly walked in with a lot of Spirit Beasts, most dead, but some alive.

It stopped in front of the fruit and dropped all of them next to the plant.

In no time, the corpses got eaten by the ground, and the fruit glowed with an even more powerful light than before.

The few live Spirit Beasts didn’t dare move at first, not under the ape’s pressure, but when the ground began to swallow them too, they made futile attempts at leaving.

Unfortunately, it was far too late for them. The fruit glowed with a sadistic pleasure as the beasts cried out for pity, but the ape only stared at them with no expression on its face.

When I saw its level, though… I was pretty animated.

[Alpha Fire Ape, Lvl. 65]

A level sixty-five beast! Wasn’t that equal to the Golden Core Realm? What the hell was it doing here? Was this hidden realm within a hidden realm fucking with me?

Was Elder Su?

Was this Spirit Beast even real, or was it just another illusion created by the realm? I didn’t understand anymore…

However, that fruit… It was calling for me. I had to have it!

There was some hesitation in my thoughts, but I steeled my nerves and made my mind up. It was just a stupid ape!

The first thing I did was summon twenty-four Myriad Qi Needles and shoot them all at the ape. If it was a fake like the rest, then it should die within moments, and I would get the fruit for free.

But… Reality was often disappointing. The needles did hit the ape, but it didn’t even notice them.

What was it at, the third, fourth evolution? I was nothing but an ant in comparison, and it also happened to be a fire-type Spirit Beast, so my main attacks weren’t useful at all.

Shifting through my skills, I decided to take the physical approach for a probing hit.

Activating all my stealth skills, I snuck around and behind the thing, its giant red ass almost making me barf.

I would need to use all the Overeater reserves as well, and I had just the right idea for it.

Maybe…

I prepared myself and waited for the ape to get distracted. In the meantime, I started experimenting.

I pushed a lot of stamina into my body, using Strengthened Constitution as a guide. The more stamina I had, the stronger I would get. That was the idea, at least. I didn’t know if it would work, but I wanted to try.

Sure enough, the results were there. Pushing more stamina into my body did increase my strength, but the issue was that it used a lot of it, and it also used much more Qi in return. And for that…

I had my fluff!

Using the excess energy and Qi Conversion, I was able to push more and more strength into my body.

As the Qi went down, it immediately filled back up to one hundred percent thanks to the huge amount of energy reserves I had.

My body shrank gradually as I watched the ape, and it was also getting hotter and hotter due to the stored potential.

Soon…

I whispered to myself, and after a few minutes, I could not convert any more fluff into Qi to then feed into Strengthened Constitution.

But I got another surprise just as I was about to act.

[Congratulations!]

[Skill: Strengthened Constitution (Expert) has become Strengthened Constitution (Master)!]

Amazing, simply amazing!

With that rank up, I was able to continue the process and massively increase my already massive strength once again.

It only took a few minutes to reach the maximum of what I could do, and I knew then that it was time to act.

Holding my heart in my throat, I stalked the ape, but all it was doing was staring intently at the fruit.

However, I wasn’t about to let my guard down just because it didn’t seem like it was paying attention to anything. Even if I was as stealthy as I could be, that was still a level sixty-five beast we were talking about.

But it didn’t mean the situation was hopeless. I didn’t have a lot of time to wait, though. I quickly crossed off some skills I knew wouldn’t work, such as Paralyzing Screech. That thing was far stronger than me, and I wasn’t even sure this plan of mine would be able to put a dent in its health.

It was probably in the hundreds of thousands range, but the situation was not completely hopeless.

I wasn’t a normal Spirit Beast either!

Finally, it let out a yawn, and I acted, moving so fast that I’d almost stumbled on my own feet.

But that didn’t matter. As a blur, I approached the beast and attacked with a savage combo!

Iron Claws paired with Ember Step and Flaming Talons jumped at the beast’s eyes, while I surrounded myself in Four Calamity Lotus Flame. I felt the passive Assassination skill activate, dealing increased damage to the unaware Spirit Beast.

Dragons made out of the same element appeared, borrowing Dragon Flame and Lightning Dragon to create this new, balanced, and powerful elemental dragon.

My Qi reserves instantly plummeted, but Qi Conversion filled me back up while increasing my agility.

My talons connected with its face just as the dragons wrapped and coiled around it. The smell of burning fur immediately filled the air.

As the creature finally reacted, I pushed off of its face, leaving three painful-looking marks, bleeding and burning with the Four Calamity Lotus Flame.

Right as it let out a pained scream, I turned with Aerial Redirection and used a Four Calamity Lotus Flame-infused Blade Wing, aimed at the thing’s neck.

My powerful feathers made contact, but the beast had already expected something like that, so I had no choice but to fall back as the skin around its neck hardened.

Tsk.

Clicking my tongue inwardly, I used a modified Elemental Wave and decreased the thing’s fire resistances, specifically against my Four Calamity Lotus Flame. It let out an agonised scream, but I still had plenty of stamina and Qi, as well as many reserves of fluff left.

Just in case, I summoned twelve needles and waited for the right moment to use them. It wouldn’t be quick, but I had to keep my moment.

With a forward Side Step, I combined it with Ember Step and forced a Four Calamity Lotus Flame wall right under its groin. Yet more agonising screams rang out, but I wasn’t about to let it rest.

Quickly taking to the skies, I flew high above while the dragons restricted the ape’s movements.

It wouldn’t last long, I knew it, so with all my leftover Qi and fluffiness, I let it rain!

Pyro Breath activated, spitting out a large cloud of Four Calamity Lotus Flame, covering the ape and the fruit!

Oh no!
