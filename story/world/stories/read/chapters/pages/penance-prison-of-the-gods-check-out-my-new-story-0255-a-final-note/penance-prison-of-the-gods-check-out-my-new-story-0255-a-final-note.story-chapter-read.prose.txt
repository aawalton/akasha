Howdy,As of today, Penance is on abandoned, I will not write anything else in this series, and that makes me sad. Besides the fact that I am waiting on the SLP lawsuit, I just don't think I'll ever have motivation to write this series again. Every time I think about this book, my thoughts immediately go to the awful situation ive found myself in. Anyway, I have a new book I'd like you to consider reading. I have been working on this story for almost a year at this point. It's just one of those ideas that I couldn't get out of my head. I love fantasy. It's my favorite genre, but one thing that I never get to see enough of is the quality of life magic that every character takes for granted. It got me thinking about how I could tell a story about a mage who made it his mission to focus on that kind of magic. It had to be a lazy mage, why else focus on it? I had also just finished binging the excellent Stubborn Skill grinder stuck in a time loop and an idea clicked into place. Lazy Loops. The laziest mage ever stuck in a time loop.

I know its a sideways genre (factory to Academy Scifi to fantasy) but I think this is my best work yet. and hope yall will give this a read. here's the cover, and the blurb:

Lazy Loops: An Idle LitRPG [Op to archmage]
