Non-Combat Pathers in the depths.

First of all, no.

If you aren't a Combat Pather you don't belong there unless your Awakening Gifts somehow make you useful to a team. Or you're part of a collection team that is there looking for those resources that combat teams ignore.

But if you're a collection team you already know all this. You're there to do a job, you're there to show up once the Divers do their thing. If you're there because of your Gifts and don't have a Combat Path, then I hope your contract is excellent.

Before you ask Healers count as Combat Paths when they're with a team. They're there and expected to contribute directly, they aren't there as auxiliary support crew.

If you're thinking about going into the Depths for any other reason? Go back to my first answer. Don't.

~ * ~

They didn't go back into the Depths for almost two months. Seb knew they'd only missed out on a handful of visits, maybe a week's worth of days to make up for, but that wasn't the point. They'd spent an extra month they hadn't planned for.

In the end Fay told them it was time for them to head back to Diving. Anya had been antsy for a couple weeks but had bit her tongue and said nothing despite the deadline growing closer by the day. The boar had been something they hadn't expected, something they maybe could have gotten past sooner on their own, but not without the risk of extreme injury being higher than either would accept.

Most of the first month was recovery. Almost all of it was spent in the Shallows as family, in the house or out taking things easy. She didn't ask what happened, she didn't talk about the Depths. She even seemed hesitant to head over to Kaf-Fay the first few days. Not because she didn't want to see Briana, that much Seb was sure of.

Fay would fall asleep on the couch, curling up to cuddle against Briana while they all spent the afternoon or evening together. Briana wouldn't go into details about how their absence had affected Fay, didn't share specifics about anything that happened, but she was more protective than Seb could remember.

Alex complained once, extremely formally, about how Fay pulled rank. He did it when Fay wasn't there, when she couldn't say anything. The next morning they all had breakfast together and he apologized to her for the complaint, then had to explain that he had complained. Fay refused to forgive him until he forgave her. Sandra stole the bacon they were trying to use as a shared peace offering and got banned from breakfast for the rest of the week.

The second month felt more normal. Everyone seemed calmer, less worried about someone panicking. Everyone knew about the pig, had found some excuse to make a joke about it. Everyone knew how instrumental Fay was, especially Briana.

They told Fay about the rest of their Dive. About the yellow goblins they had found on the level. How they were human sized yet fewer in number. The overall challenge was the same, they weren't any more dangerous as a group, simply as individuals.

The only thing they hadn't been ready for was the roamer. It hadn't been there when they arrived, it settled in at the landing at some point and they only found out when they went to leave. It was gone now, even Fay was certain the odds of another showing up were all but non-existent.

Fay saw them off, she stepped through the portal with them just long enough to say good luck then ran back into the Holding. They saw the effort it took, she didn't try to hide that she was terrified. She'd done it before out of desperation, this time she'd done it to show herself she could.

~ * ~

They were done. They'd made it.

She's in her office, Seb sent as they stepped through the portal and back into the Holding.

"Fay!" Anya called out.

The sound wasn't loud, he'd heard her yell louder on several occasions in the last couple days. The Energy that rippled out with the words, that wove through the hallways and demanded the sound be heard did all the work.

She'd figured out a lot about her Affinity in the last year. About how to draw attention, to make her words carry to her target without needing to shout. She still did yell, still made sure every other possible target, even those she wasn't focusing on, heard her and paid attention, but she didn't need to.

"Anya?" Fay said as she came racing into the room.

"We're done!"

"You're-"

"Level 15." Anya clarified before Fay could actually ask what she'd meant.

He saw Fay start to narrow her eyes, to think through what that meant, whether they'd managed.

"A few months to spare," Seb said. "If you could send a note to her Godparents the first chance you-"

"Alex!" Fay yelled, loud enough to set all three wincing at the echo.

Alex came running, confusion and worry on his face until he saw them.

"Send a message to the Keepers. Let them know their Goddaughter satisfied the timeline."

"Of course," he said. "Congratulations?"

"Thank you," Seb said with a nod.

After Alex left Fay turned back to them both.

"Supper? You're a day early but I'm sure Briana will be happy to celebrate."

Up to you love, he sent.

"Everyone was invited tomorrow, right?" Anya asked then continued when Fay nodded. "Then don't change anything. We can do something small in the house."

"If you're sure."

"Fay," Anya said before Seb felt all their shared exhaustion hit over the bond. "I want to celebrate. But first I want a bath and to curl up with Seb and do nothing. If you want to join us you can."

Fay started to blush and he couldn't help but laugh.

"On the couch," he said. "We didn't push as hard as when we passed the wall. Or when we kept the band in our dreams, but we came close."

"I knew that," Fay said quickly before glaring at Anya. "You just never suggest joining, and you're always wanting time with Seb to unwind."

Seb wasn't sure if he blushed first or Anya, but he was sure Anya was the one who had to bury her head in his chest in an attempt to hide. Fay wasn't wrong, Anya had said what she'd said and meant nothing more than time with family.

~ * ~

Anya glared at her little sister across the dinner table before turning to Fay.

"What's the soonest?"

"One week if we assume everyone can make plans last minute. Three if we want to be sure no one has to say no."

"I don't want to agree with the bacon stealing brat," Anya said with a smirk. "But sooner is better. Ask the ones most likely to be busy for a week, if they can we just tell the other handful that they are changing their plans."

She saw Sandra go from frowning at her words to cheering at the plan to frowning once more.

"What is it Sandra?"

"I'm not a brat."

"You steal bacon," Seb said.

"But if it's next week I won't be able to find a da-"

"To what..?" Anya asked, drawing the question out as far as the sounds would let her as she cut her off.

"To find someone to…" Sandra began again then nodded. "Right, no extra guests."

Be nice, Seb sent.

"Sis," Anya said, trying to follow his advice. "Is there someone you want to ask?"

"What? No. That's the thing, there isn't anyone and a week isn't very long and I wanted someone to dance with me and compliment my dress and tell me how much I-"

"You wanted to get all the attention at my wedding."

Our wedding.

Our wedding, but she's my sister being selfish and-

-young. She just wants to feel special.

Anya started to answer when Seb sent memories flickering through their shared awareness. Of Sandra flitting between partners the entire night at her birthday party, of never letting anyone dance with her long enough for it to mean anything.

Anya took a deep breath before pushing the last of the dessert towards Sandra.

"I get it. But this is still Seb and my's night."

Sandra didn't say anything, she took the dessert and smiled.

Are you feeling okay? Seb sent.

Yes. I let her have the dessert because you were right, I wasn't being nice.

Love you.

Love you too, soon-to-be husband.

She felt Seb's thoughts stutter. She felt him hesitate in a way she'd rarely felt but savored every time she did. She'd said something he hadn't expected, something that he appreciated and wouldn't forget.

One more week, she was sure of it.
