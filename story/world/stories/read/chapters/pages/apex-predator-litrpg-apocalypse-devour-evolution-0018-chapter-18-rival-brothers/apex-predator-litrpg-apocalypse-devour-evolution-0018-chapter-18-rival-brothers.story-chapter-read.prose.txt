“You,” Don said and immediately flicked his claws out. The guy had already seen him use Devour so the cat was out of the bag. “You’re the Hunter.”

The Hunter let out a low chuckle. “Yes, the cattle calls me that. But I dislike it when you do it. You should call me by my name, Jacques. After all, we have a special connection.”

Don measured the distance between himself and the Hunter. The bow was slung on his back and on his belt was a long knife, definitely not part of the tutorial starter pack.

“The kind of connection where you tried to kill me?”

“Ah,” Jacques said and smiled apologetically. “That was before I realized you were clearly special.”

This was getting weird. The skinny guy had a strange gleam in his eyes. Yet his movements were measured and subtle. Don used Identify and found he was indeed dealing with someone dangerous.

[Blood Hunter, Level 10]

“Interesting class you got there,” Don said. “How many humans did you have to kill for it?”

“I have killed… twenty? Maybe twenty five. Ah, but who counts these things? This is not important. What I find even more interesting is that you have no class at all.”

Jacques looked at the drained bear with its grayed fur and sunken features. “Yet you possess the strangest abilities. Of this, I would learn more.”

Don said nothing. He just watched Jacques. The man was seemingly relaxed. That meant he had to have a movement ability or some kind of defensive measure. Don could cross the five meters between them in seconds and rip a human to shreds with ease.

“Not talkative? This I understand,” Jacques said and took a step closer. Don drew his right arm back. Jacques clicked his tongue and nodded, taking the step back. He nodded at the Blackmaw Grizzly.

“I saw most of the fight. You bled it out. Smart. Efficient. Most of the cattle flail and panic until they are torn to pieces. Even I have trouble killing these. I shall adopt your tactics…”

“What the fuck do you want?” Don growled, growing tired of the Hunter’s nonchalance.

“You have been chosen by our matron,” Jacques said and tried to step closer again, pressed his hand into a fist and controlled himself. “I wish to know why.”

“Our… matron?”

“One of the many deities of this Multiverse,” Jacques said in a feverish tone. He spoke fast. “She is the goddess of all hunters and predators. The Eternal Hungerer, The Endless Maw. And she chose me, just as she has chosen you….Why she has not contacted you is beyond my understanding, but… I am here.”

Of course this nutjob has a god…

Don had a lot of questions. He let himself relax just a bit. He was still ready for an attack, but it seemed like this weirdo was not immediately dangerous. He just wanted… attention?”

Still. This is a murderer. I need to be alert.

“Uh great…” Don said. “I’d rather just get back to killing the monsters, so…”

“It is exquisite, isn’t it?” Jacques asked and a slow grin spread on his face. “Taking the life of another. Conquering and dominating their very being. It is so pure, so sublime… We never had this chance in the old world.”

“Look. Shit needs to be killed,” Don said. “The forest will be safer and I’ll get stronger. It’s not pretty, but it has to be done.”

“You lie.”

Don only scoffed. Well, the crackpot was kind of right. It felt amazing beating the odds and taking these beasts down. Accumulating power and seeing yourself visibly progress. Jacques was right… Don never did have this chance in the old world.”

“We are cut from the same cloth. We are both beloved of the matron. That is why… I must see who is superior.”

That was enough. Don pushed off the ground and lunged at the Hunter. He dodged back, and jumped away, sliding his knife from its sheath.

“Please, do not be so hasty. I do not wish to kill you. Not right now. It wouldn’t be good sport.”

“Sport? You think there’s sport to this? It’s survival. Didn’t you listen to the System? If we don’t get strong, we’ll get fucked. After the tutorial is over, we are going to fight another planet. And you want to play games?”

“I am not interested in wars between humans and aliens,” Jacques said. “My immediate interest lies in you. I would like to challenge you to see who truly deserves to be matron’s beloved.”

“Not interested.”

“Ah, then let us make it interesting. You see I have already managed to kill a boss guarding a System Point Dispenser.”

Okay. That got Don’s attention.

“It was weak, and the dispenser is limited. But I see you are eyeing the Blazeclaw, powerful boss, larger dispenser than the Earth Wyrm, I fought last night.”

“Get to the point.”

“Ah, so brash. I do appreciate your efficiency. You waste no time, unlike the cattle, which bleets and cowers. This is the gist. We shall see who kills Blazeclaw first. The loser will have to give their System Points to the victor.”

“Huh…” Don said. “Now that is interesting. How many do you have?”

Jacques grinned. “I have killed the cattle. They yield many. I have more than you.”

Don chuckled. But he left his crouch and retracted his claws. “You’re a sick bastard, you know that.”

“Hah, no,” Jacques said. “I am perfect fit. I am the ultimate hunter. I am the one who belongs in this world. But this I must prove, for the matron favors you. I have an inkling why, but I must see for myself. Come, let us push each other towards greatness.”

“Fine,” Don said. “I’ll compete. What are the rules?”

“No rules. Only rule here is that of the jungle,” Jacques said. “Whoever kills the boss first, shall receive the others points. Then the victor shall choose the next challenge.”

“You really like games, huh?”

“You have to admit,” Jacques said. “This is good. It will push you forward. The alternative is that we fight and one of us dies.”

“I feel like that’s going to happen sooner or later.”

Jacques laughed. “Then let it be later! First, let us have some fun.”

“And how are we going to find each other in this endless forest?” Don asked.

“I shall find you. If you have already killed ze Blazeclaw, I shall honor my word and find you. If I kill it before you, I will come for you.”

“Huh,” Don said. “Alright. I’m in you crazy fuck. But no killing other humans to level up.”

“Ah, ah,” Jacques said and wagged a finger. “I said no rules. If you beat me, you will dictate next challenge.”

“You’re stacking the deck. You’re higher level than me,” Don said.

“Yes…” Jacques said. “I have leveraged my strengths… Yet, you are stronger than me, no? It vexes me. I believe this is why our matron likes you.”

“Does she approve of this challenge?” Don asked. “This… deity of yours?”

“Yes…she suggested it.”

“Fine, then,” Don said. “Now leave me alone. I’ll get started on the grind. May the best man win.”

“Yes… By the end of this, we shall know who is superior. Now, I shall go find more cattle.”

Then Jacques bowed his head and vanished into the foliage. That bastard had a pretty high Stealth skill, because as soon as he was out of sight, the rustling muffled instantly.

Don wasn’t sure what the hell had that just been, but he had figured it was better to accept. Friends close and enemies closer? Something like that. It wasn’t a good idea to let this crazy bastard roam the woods wantonly. He had apparently already been in this guy’s crosshairs, so he might as well return the favor.

He kills people for fun. I’ll have to put him down.

Some grim and primal part of Don’s mind also noted that Jacques would yield a lot of System Points. In this new world, Don would take every advantage offered. And if he had to compete for this one with a wackjob murderer. Hey, that was just another day in the new reality.

“Time to go back to the grind. I’ll grind these bears until I get an Evolution Trait.”

As he finished that thought, to his amusement the System pinged a notification.

[Special Quest assigned: Battle of Prodigies 1/3]

Description: Be the first to defeat Blazeclaw (0/1)

Don chuckled. “I guess it’s on.”
