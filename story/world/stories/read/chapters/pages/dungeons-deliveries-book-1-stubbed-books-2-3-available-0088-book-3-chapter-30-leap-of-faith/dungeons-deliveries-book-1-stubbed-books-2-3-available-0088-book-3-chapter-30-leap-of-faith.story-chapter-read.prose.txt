It’s more like a pull, Emilio said to Alex. As if I’m being tricked by a simple promise of a treat. But it keeps working. And I want all these things I’m being…taken towards.

The shock of Emilio’s reveal had worn off. First, he’d been amazed. Then, he’d been slightly annoyed Emilio hadn’t trusted him fully. Alex had quickly gotten over that. He understood how difficult it could be to open up others. It was almost more difficult with those you actually cared about. And now, it felt oddly normal to chat with his cat through his mind.

He scratched Emilio behind his ear, lying on his back atop the roof, stared at the stars, and enjoyed the warm loaf snuggled next to him.

“That’s got to be tough, I’ll give you that.” Alex agreed. “But can’t you just read what the System is saying from the notifications?”

A throaty, quick noise flitted through his mind, making Alex grin. It was nice to finally hear Emilio’s laugh.

Of course, how easy that would be. If I could read. I must have missed that class at school.

“Oop.” Alex winced. “Sorry, dude. Forgot you’re not a human for a sec. Maybe you can learn? I can teach you.”

Emilio stiffened for a moment under his hand, and Alex felt his presence pull briefly from his mind. But then the full presence was back.

Yes, I would like that. But please do not speak with others about this.

“Your secret’s safe with me, buddy. We’ll add ‘teach Emilio to read books,’ to our to-do list. It’s not like there’s anything major on there. Figure out what to do with the Tax Guild Stone. Help you secure your power. Sorry, what was it again? You wanted to rule the region of Toronto and then Algonquin? Ah, but first we need to destroy the Beast of Blightwood. That’s just your list. Never mind all the other stuff I have to do.”

The deep, quick vibrations started from Emilio’s, and Alex held it in as long as he could. Emilio quickly lost control, and began meowing, belly laughing with purrs, and filling his head with deep chuckles. Alex’s own laughter poured out all at once, joining his cat in giggling about how ridiculous their lives had gotten.

Both settled down, and Emilio’s deep purrs of comfort started up again.

“What a vent session.” Alex shook his head. “What else is there? Dating? We haven’t talked about that. You know my situation with Snu. What about you? Any lucky cats catch your eye?”

He didn’t expect anything. Alex hadn’t ever seen Emilio with another cat. So, he was surprised when Emilio turned his big green eyes around and curled his lip in an approximation of a smirk. It was all sharp teeth on one side.

“Are you smirking?” Alex asked. “I don’t think it works on you, man. I’m sorry to be the one to tell you.”

I think it makes me look dashing, Emilio answered. And yes. One has certainly caught my eye.

“Ooh lala, Emilio,” Alex sat up. “This is serious then? Well, what are they like? When do I get to meet them? You know they’ll have to get my approval, right? That’s just how this works. What’s their name? Fur colour? I’ll bet you like blondes. Or is it beige? Can cats be blonde?”

Well, truthfully, I don’t know her name yet. But she is special. Her scales are quite…, Emilio practically shook, her scales are quite something.

Alex was confused. “Wait, what? Did I hear that right? Scales? A cat with scales?”

Emilio somehow managed to raise an eyebrow.

She is a dragon. A very special dragon.

Alex closed his mouth, his mind immediately trying to figure out how that might mechanically work. Emilio was large, but he’d read about dragons online. They were rare and odd, completely unlike what people thought they’d be like before the System came. And they were big.

“You know what?” Alex said. “I’m not one to judge. I’m dating a Dungeon Boss. But you haven’t don’t know her name? Means you two haven’t met. This isn’t one of those System pulling things you’ve been getting, right?”

Not at all. Emilio chuckled. This is all of my own volition. And I was going to meet her, but someone important needed to be helped first.

“What, who?”

Someone who ran from their hurt.

“Ah.” Alex nodded, then scooped Emilio up to hug him. “You’re a big sap, you know what?”

Emilio allowed Alex to hug him for a moment, laughing, before gently squirming out and sitting in front of him.

“Well, you’ve got a plan then?” Alex asked. “If she’s a special dragon. Surely you know how you’re going to impress her?

Oh, yes. Emilio answered. In fact, she is waiting to meet me right now. I have prepared a gift, but I will be there when she receives it.

“Aren’t you suave. You’ve sent her a message? A sign in the sky?”

I sent a hundred gnomes.

“That’s basically a love letter!” Alex laughed. “So, what are we waiting for? Let’s go meet her right now?”

Emilio swung his tail nervously. You would like come?

“Of course I bloody would like to come. Don’t want me there? I promise I won’t embarrass you.”

You never embarrass me. But what if she doesn’t like me as I like her?

Alex scootched over to Emilio. “Hey, don’t think like that. First, you gotta’ meet here. Right now, you’re all eyeballs. And you’re the best, Emil. If she doesn’t like you, that’s her loss. Obviously, she is going to be into you. You’re you. And you got her a gift. Girls love gifts.”

It is a special gift. Emilio agreed. One moment, let me check that all is well.

Alex was about to respond, but he felt the connection to Emilio’s mind pull back and then expand out. His cat was still there, still with him, and still smacking his tail on the roof, but suddenly it was as if his mind occupied the very air in the sky. Then, the full force of Emilio was back.

The gnomes have created a giant bath of mud for her, Emilio said. She seems…as if she waits.

“Impatient and ready to meet you, I’ll bet.” Alex winked. “Look, I’m feeling way better. You know how calm I would have been all this time if we could have just been chatting like this with you? Fricken best kind of connection. So, it’s your turn, big man. Let’s go to the Allen Gardens and meet her?”

She is at our base in Muskoka, not Allen Gardens. It is becoming of her might. Danger taints the air. A perfect place for a gift for a future queen.

“And you’re a romantic. Bah, Emilio. This sounds formal!”

Yes. She deserves all the formality.

“Then butter me up and call me a biscuit, I’m in. Do I need to change? I’ve only fought for my life, sprinted all day, and ran up a tower. I’m a bit ripe.”

Your natural scent is much more appropriate than that evil mist you prefer.

“Hey, you keep your lady happy, and I’ll keep mine happy.”

Emilio swooshed his tail and glared at Alex. He didn’t need the connection to figure out what the cat was getting at.

“Uh, can I bring a date? Please?”

A date is not an appropriate place for an apology.

Alex scratched his head. He hadn’t been the kindest to Snu during his panic. Not the worst, either, but he needed to check in and say sorry.

“Yeah…I’ll apologize first. Promise.”

Emilio perked up, flicking his tail and strutting away towards the passed-out Pimple Pete. The content creator was still face down, passed out, snoring, and from Alex’s estimation, severely dehydrated. At least he could see Pete breathing. He figured Emilio’s silence was approval and chased after him.

“You’re sure he’s not going to die, right? The dude is annoying, but not evil or anything.”

He will live. He will also come with us to Muskoka. He is important.

“Ah, think he’s got some answers or something that will help us?”

Emilio turned his head around, looking thoughtful. No. I brought him here because of those…feelings I get from the System.

“Spooky.” Alex crouched down and gently rustled the sleeping Pete to no success. “Maybe zap him again?”

I fear he may soil himself if I continue.

They’d tried multiple times to wake Pete. Nothing had worked.

“Fair point.” Alex stood up and stretched as he turned to face the city cast under the night sky. He wondered why he wasn’t tired.

With the connection open as it is now, we can share some amount of essence. Being physically close strengthens this. Emilio answered his unspoken question and flicked his tail. Now that Alex spoke can better, he knew it for smugness.

“We’re so badass. You as the bottomless pit of cat essence. Me as your handsome sidekick.” Alex sniffed, walking to the edge of the roof for what he believed to be the last time. “Alright, apologize, date, dragon. Mind flying me to Snu’s?”

Emilio came over and sat next to Alex. The pair stared out at the world below. Through their bond, he felt the cat mulling something over, as if he wanted to be honest but was afraid to be.

“Just say it, Emil.” Alex said. “I can handle it.”

I sense you capable of so much. Emilio said, making sure to lean on Alex. You are my human for a reason. Do you doubt my decision?

Alex raised his eyebrows. “Think I’m holding myself back? Damn, dude. I try pretty hard.”

You take leaps of faith for others. But rarely for yourself. I think of every place as a perch. And there are always higher perches to reach.

“Or to jump off of.” Alex said, suddenly feeling an urge and idea form together.

Emilio must of picked up on what he was thinking, as the cat laughed softly in his mind.

Yes, this is most humorous.

“We’ll work on your punchlines, too. Adding it to the to-do list.” Alex said as he pulled out his phone. He sent a quick text to Snu and slotted it back in his pocket.

Alex: I’m coming over. And I’m sorry.

His nerves flared as he looked down with new eyes. The city looked massive, but so far down below everything appeared doll-house-like. He was really going to do it, and even if what he was planning didn’t work, he figured he could survive.

“Running jump?”

Of course. I would expect nothing less.

Alex backed up a few paces, feeling his heart hammering in his chest. He cast one final smug look of his own at Emilio.

I know what you are thinking. Please, do not do it.

“You’re special, Emilio, but everybody gets the guns. See you at Snu’s in ten.”

Alex took off in a flash, sparking [Relative Goggler] as he ran toward the edge of the CN tower. The weight of sunglasses appeared onto his face. The end came quick, and he leapt with all his might off the skyscraper. He pulled his knees and arched, flipping backwards towards the tower. He’d aimed perfectly, and in the brief second where he was upside down and facing Emilio, he fired a double barrel boom at his cat and winked. Then, he fell swan dived headfirst towards the ground.

“WU-WU-WU-WOMBO COMBO!” He cackled, activating [Illusory Copy] to bring Brody there.

Alex just hoped his clone would also see the fun to the start of his apology. And that the glasses he’d pulled out with his skill were cool.

Brody popped out, looking utterly confused, and that’s when Alex received a System notification.
