By the time Ethan walked back out into the street, the modest festivities were already ongoing. It wasn’t anything loud or intense, just a general good mood on the main street of New Dawn, with groups chatting and eating whatever they managed to get their hands on. A few had taken it upon themselves to start offering snacks and drinks, with at least two of them having managed to recover some large griddles from the abandoned shopping malls. The scent of roasted meats was pervasive, and it seemed to be mixing well with liquor and wine—which seemed to have handled the world changing well enough.

Of course, his presence was quickly noted and as he made his way toward the building Amanda had pointed out to him earlier, he got his fair share of shoulder taps and cheers. A large drumstick of some roasted fowl ended up in his hand, along with a mug full to the brim and a spirit that nearly burned his nose and by the time he made it to his destination, Marcello and Audrey were grinning at him, cups in their own hands as well.

“Oh look at this. Our local celebrity’s here,” the mole-man teased. None of his spellforms were out, but Ethan still felt like the man had somehow grown…stouter. For a lack of a better word. And same for Audrey, while her shield and armor wasn’t out, she radiated a certain air of fortitude that almost felt like a challenge to him. And while she was as tall as he was, somehow, she felt larger.

Were they projecting their own Manifestation at him, or was Ethan growing more receptive to them?

“I’m sure the list will update soon enough,” Ethan began, pushing his musings aside as he came to a stop next to the duo. “Gonna have to work hard to keep my spot. But I’d love to see more of us up there, so.”

The two nodded, a glimmer in their eyes, and he took a sip that he nearly immediately spat. Grimacing, he looked down at his mug. “The hell is this stuff?”

“I’m pretty sure the fumes could strip wood. It’s not bad though,” Marcello said, swishing what he had in his mug before he downed it one shot. Ethan just handed him his mug, and Marcello took it happily while Ethan turned to Audrey. “How’s Amelie? Liz?”

“They’re both fine. Amelie’s already out on assignment, dealing with a rift and Liz is helping around. She’s mobile.”

Ethan smiled, relieved. Amelie had recovered pretty fast from her over-exhaustion from dealing with the poison Liz had been afflicted with. But the latter had needed a few days. From what he had heard and seen, the toxin had ravaged both her body and spirit, making any use of mana excruciating and forcing her to bed for nearly 48 hours, even after Amelie said the toxin’s out of her system. He’d gone to check on her and back then, she was still in bed, slipping in and out of sleep, so it’s good that she was out and about.

“That’s good to hear,” he said, eyeing the drumstick in his hand. Then taking a bite, he closed his eyes and thanked his good fortune that somehow, someone had finally managed to put their hands on some salt and spices.

“So what brings you around?” asked Audrey after a few quiet seconds.

Ethan told her what they just discussed with Amanda, between him coordinating with them as well as potentially helping out with training on occasion, which he felt was received pretty well.

“That’s awesome!” said Marcello, teeth bared in a wide smile. “We’re still looking for a good spot to settle in and have some spars, but I bet there’s plenty to learn there.”

Audrey nodded, but he could see her examining him as Marcello spoke and when he finished, she continued. “Once you come back, I’m sure we’ll have something set up. For now, I’m assuming you’re heading out and want to know if something’s worth your attention?”

Ethan blinked, then grinned. “Yup. I’ve gotta go talk to Ember, then work on some stuff. Anything I need to know about the rifts?”

Marcello grimaced, rubbing the back of his neck, and Audrey sobered up. Nodding back toward the wide door behind her, she turned. “There is. Come take a look.”

Following after the duo, Ethan walked into a surprisingly wide space with multiple recovered desks strewn with notes and papers. In the center of the room, a large recovered map of the east coast was hung on a bulletin board, with multiple pins dotting their surroundings. Most were blue, but Ethan spied a handful of yellow ones and a couple of red ones, the latter of which already had a post-it notes with names on them, as well as details about the monsters leaking out of the rift.

“We’re still making do with what we find, but so far, it’s all under control,” Audrey said, which made Imani turn toward them from the stack of papers she had been sifting through.

[Human - Level 83 (II)]

Ethan almost raised a brow, but he kept his surprise out of his face. He hadn’t seen the healer ever since he had met her. Back when he had rescued those captives from the eldritch-possessed leader a few days back. But now he guessed her absence made sense. At least, he supposed, as he hadn’t been particularly paying attention to her coming and going, just that he hadn’t seen her in a few days. It seemed that just like him, she had kept up with her leveling. And she wasn’t the only familiar face around. Taylor was here as well, the girl with the horns who he had also met on the same occasion, and so was Marc who he had first met in Dwayne’s company. Aaron, the coach, was here too, and the latter two were updating the map with some of their findings, adding some details about a nearby rift that was just located before moving away from the board.

Ethan realized there was a line in front of it, and once the two moved away, another familiar figure stepped forward. The mage from a couple of days ago—Megan—silently removed a pin from the map, ignoring some of the cold stares she was getting from the folks of Wyldrock before placing her name under another rift. With that done, she headed back toward a nearby desk, where two people he didn’t know were waiting for her. They looked down on the rift details she had brought with her, then silently headed toward the door, nodding at him before passing by, which he returned, though a little uncomfortably.

I kinda did go off on her… and the mood’s a little awkward around the new people, he thought. He considered stopping them for a second, maybe say something, but that wouldn’t do. They were the new addition, and he felt that a little frostiness was understandable. If it lasted for long or if it devolved he’d say something, but for now, it was fine and frankly, if he was in her place and her people, he’d be doing the exact same thing she was. Work hard and earn their spot.

But he’d apologize. Maybe when he came back. He couldn’t expect the people of Wyldrock to let go of their anger if he didn’t lead by example. After all, they were all on the same side.

The rest of the visit to the new headquarters of the task force went on without issues. There were no severe emergencies that needed his attention, and even if there had been, there were enough high levels to deal with most. But that didn’t mean that there were no majorly concerning news. Apparently, the formation of rifts was accelerating, or at least, they were finding them faster than they were clearing them, and reviewing his chat with Ember the day prior, Ethan knew what it meant, and he repeated what he had been told.

They were days from the first formation of a tier-3 rift to appear, which meant a wave of tier-2 monsters and tier-3 bosses, and Ethan would be lying if he said he wasn’t looking forward to the new rifts. Thankfully, the task force leaders took the news in stride, Imani having already suspected the ramp up was near considering the phenomenon she had noted.

So, with his meeting with the task force settled, he headed out and without wasting any time, he flew up, leaving another wave of cheers behind as he headed to Wyldrock. He was to have a quick chat with Ember, cajole her into giving the high council some of her attention, and then it would be time to see what the rift token had in store.

“I wonder what it’s going to be like,” he said, wind whistling past his ears.
