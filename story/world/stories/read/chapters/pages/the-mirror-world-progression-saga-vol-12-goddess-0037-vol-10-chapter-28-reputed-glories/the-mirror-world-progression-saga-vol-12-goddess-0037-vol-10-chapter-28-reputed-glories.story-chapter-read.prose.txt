Samantha found Azure sitting at the top of the tower on the balcony, overlooking the sea and the developing island spread across from it, now dotted with all manner of buildings, almost the entirety of the former ruins now rebuilt or replaced. It made for an interesting hodgepodge of old and new. The bay was now filled with ships going to and fro, and one didn’t need to look far to see fishing vessels taking advantage of the prolific attraction of wildlife to such absurd mana concentrations as existed around Itha Ranon.

Azure had a basket of yellow obles in her lap — spherical little sweet fruits, something between grapes and an indefinable new berry in taste. She popped them in her mouth, chewed happily, and spat the seeds through the balcony wall divides, her tail behind her swishing back and forth on the floor in a manner that communicated contentment.

I envy her ability to not give a shit here and there and just relax. I’d probably never even bother without her. A bad influence that is a very good influence.

Samantha sat down quietly and helped herself to a few obles out of the basket. They were wonderful, both crisp and sweet. She didn’t bother spitting the seed, considering her gullet annihilated most everything equally.

“So,” Samantha began, finally, “I heard back from Crow. Seems we’ve already been doing something related to the quest to acquire the First Page. Lucky us.”

Azure looked over and raised an eyebrow as she popped another oble in her mouth in a languid manner. “Oh? Do tell.”

Samantha related what Crow had about the First Page, and the necessities of carving out slices of the founding stones from ten special locations, with one extra ‘wrong’ locale.

Azure listened and nodded along, giving a huff when the spiel was over. “Hidden because men were greedy for it, eh? Sounds about right. I guess it begs the question whether we should keep it.”

Samantha sighed. “Seems like a bad play, even ignoring that it would piss off Glae. The Consolidator was certain it would be involved in an act of mercy. At least it reduces my worry about what Glae is up to. Whatever way he hopes to stick it to the Sage with this, it’s looking more like a positive.”

“Sure you don’t wanna be a monkey wrench in Fate for once?” Azure wiggled her eyebrows.

Samantha gave her a wry grin. “Tempting, but no. I’m sure I don’t. Can’t, practically.”

Azure took a perfectly good oble and tossed it over the balcony. “Can’t is not in our vocabulary.”

“Wow. Such a terrible rebel, throwing away expensive fruit morsels.”

Azure grinned impishly. “Perhaps our clothing articles next?”

Samantha rolled her eyes in exasperation. “So, what do you think about the special locations? How easy will it be?”

Azure’s face turned a bit sour. “Digging. Dirt and mud aren’t really my favorite substances. By far, the easiest means will be with earth element specialists. They’re high in demand with construction needed everywhere, but this shouldn’t take too much of their time. Even less if you transport them directly.” After a pause, eating another oble, she added, “Leave it to us to organize. We’ll have you spend only a short time doing the teleportation business, where necessary.”

“Great! Thanks. And what about the eleventh extra?”

“We’ll figure it out. I’ll inspect each foundation myself — with the help of the specialists. We’ll keep the cuts separated and labeled. Perhaps it will come to me. We’re missing, what, two locations?”

“With the lich’s place, it’s nine locations we’ve found, but his doesn’t count into this, so that’s eight. We need eleven. Three more. One will be Matrazere, I’m sure.”

Azure stared at her.

“What?” Samantha asked.

“Am I missing something? Why not bridge there?! We’d be under the place!”

Samantha scoffed. “You think this shit is going to be that easy, babe? Hell no. I might as well try to bridge to the Capital. Even if I saw that it was possible, I’d assume it was a trap.”

“Well, you need to scope it out, anyway. For that thing the hot gorgon mentioned. Right?”

“You think she’s hot, do you?” A deliberately warning tone.

Azure showed no signs of caring about the warning. She popped another oble in her mouth and munched it — with extra smugness. “I do.” Samantha only scowled at her, so Azure said, “Don’t pout and pretend you don’t! Curious how you forget our tendencies in other lives. I honor your preferences, but I will never be blind, and I know you aren’t, either.”

Samantha sputtered, simultaneously feeling heat in her cheeks at what she was referencing. “Y-you- Ff! I absolu-! I… Mmmph.” Seldom were they… completely exclusive in other lives, regardless of the degree. Especially when it came to ‘sharing’ those of mutual attraction. She had rather unwanted memories from past lives on that fact. To her, it felt like a ‘phase’ she’d grown out of. The oddity of her technically being far too young to grow out of wild sexual experiences made the whole thing that much more uncomfortable.

Samantha sighed and gave up with a toss of her hands. “Fine! I can acknowledge that Endless Templar is a smoking hot physical specimen. Which was made abundantly clear when she unfairly stood around completely nude in front of us. Happy now?”

Azure gave a very smug nod. “Very.”

Snorting once, Samantha continued, “Anyway, sure. I’ll scope it out. But that place is locked tight from every angle spiritually.”

“If nothing else, it’ll be another dimension of intel on it.”

“True.”

A wonderful gust of wind came suddenly, creating a pause as they enjoyed it. Samantha pretended not to notice Azure peeking and admiring her billowing hair. Something about that gave her special, subtle fuzzy feelings.

“So, what’s next on your itinerary, Chestnut?” Azure finally asked.

Samantha considered it for a moment. “I feel like I need to withdraw for a few. All the spellslinging has me eager to meditate and act on the inspiration. Aside from that, there’s experience waiting around in designing custom spells. Hopefully, I can put together something tangible in the time I have. I won’t spend forever, regardless, and move right on to scoping out Matrazere, I suppose.”

Azure nodded. “I’ll be available, but otherwise starting organization on this whole digging project. Hopefully, each other location we’ve already found is one with these founding stones. Otherwise, we’ll have even more locations to find.”

“Sounds like a plan!” Samantha gave her betrothed a quick kiss, and they parted ways.

She indeed had a big goal of research during downtime before Matrazere: finally completing that damned high-level ‘machinegun’ type spell she’d been tinkering with for ages. She’d almost screwed herself not having it for the lich fight, but at least now she could focus on making it pretty damned high level. Level 13 for a custom spell was easily as good as 14 or even 15, considering her ability to add costs for improved effects. Just adding FE alone added a level to the power, pretty much.

Arrays, like her existing spell, were the best method for rapid-fire on Calrenazzod, unless she wanted to go the actual summoning route. The levels she was approaching, though… summons that weren’t special, like Wire Jammer — which was deeply resistant to being de-summoned — were very prone to de-summoning effects and even dispels. The higher levels only made it worse, as various damaging AoEs would tack on de-summoning as a secondary effect.

It was a very standard thing. Level 12 Unmake Magic wiped standard summons automatically if they had no protections. Level 14 had Banishing Burst, which was a combo searing damage effect and banishment of summons, and even Servitors. In battle, all of her Servitors needed special resistances due to that, variations of it, and other banishment effects, despite being immune to de-summoning.

She’d need to go with another Array, she felt, but found something very awesome within the high-level potentiality very quickly — focusing on attacks increased rate-of-fire, but she could add in lesser automation! The Array could fire on its own after the initial casting. Add yet another layer of attacks to her blistering arsenal of DPS potential?

Don’t mind if I do, baby! Nyehehehe! Ozzy will be so happy!

She wanted a hardcore Goddess of Magic feel to the Array, so they’d be little cackling balls of energy hovering around her. At these levels, the wounding had a maximum of Major Wounds, which was as much as she could expect from rapid-fire. It was miles better than only Minor Wounds, with even one hit capable of stunning an opponent and dooming them to get battered by the rest, which could keep upgrading wounds and be quite lethal.

With dumping a bunch of FE into the equation, she could get up to one attack per second, which meant up to seven every five seconds due to her innate Haste effect. That was insane! Of course, she’d rarely be doing that when she could drop something like [Spellfyr: Unleash] in two seconds as the ‘kill-dog’ effect. But it was still great, and gave her tremendous modularity in combat. Unsurprisingly, the automated rate-of-fire was much more limited: less accuracy, and two attacks, basically ‘one attack every two seconds,’ or three every five if focused on one subject and not interrupted somehow. But whatever — free real estate!

She worked like a mad scientist in her research area at Itha Ranon, able to stack all manner of bonuses to the timeframe these days, only further amplified by the ridiculous benefits of being at a mana nexus and having accrued the absolute most expensive books, materials, and tools. On top of that, she had a lot of recent inspiration from a ridiculous mage-duel, lending earned credence to the efforts.

Crackling explosions paired with cackles of laughter resounded repeatedly from her research chambers, and the servants steered well-clear of those brightly flashing halls for the time that the mistress was in. It was rare, of late, and the gossip went that the goddess aimed to crush the opposition in one stroke with the deadly magic she was conspiring within.

‘She’d turn the very sun into a laser and fry Matrazere to ashes!’

‘No, she’d conjure a golden dragon that breathed transmutative magic, and turn it into a glittering palace! The War Wizards would be turned into crystal golems to serve her!’

‘No, she’d break the wards and all things within would crumble apart… including clothes, leaving them all naked and red-faced!’

That servant probably had a fetish.

In any case, something far more practical, level-appropriate, and down-to-Earth was being designed — but certainly with all the gusto and pride of one creating the reputed fortress-destroying horror.
