Music was coming from somewhere below them as well, just adding to the fantastical view.

There were floating islands that seemed to be held in place with blue arcane circles. The beautiful morning sun, clear blue sky, and immaculately clean city made for a breathtaking sight.

Kesa took a deep breath, "Wow...we really are here, aren't we?"

"Yeah, look!" Dark pointed.

A floating whale came in, utterly dwarfing a blue whale from Earth many times over, casually flapping a series of 3 fins per side. It was equipped with a whole series of buildings on its back, a beautiful porch that wrapped around the sides. The whale 'docked' at a floating island as if it were a huge delivery platform, its docks reaching out to latch onto it.

"This is amazing, but we need to get going before we get caught!" Zedra said. He walked to the other side of the wall to find a drop nearly twice as far as the one they had just climbed, straight down to a sparkling blue lake. The clear sky and overhead sun reflected off the mirror-like lake, and soft ripples showed the nature of the wind. The drop was so far that it would have killed most people, water landing or not. But this was a gaming world, and huge drops into water were commonplace.

"Yeah, so Atticus...this part seems like a bit much, but we are at full health, and as long as you jump from the circle and don't hit a bird, you will be just fine," Sky said with a terrifying smile.

Atticus took a few seconds before he realized that they indeed meant to jump off the wall, "You want me too..."

"It's really easy!" Zedra encouraged. He remembered being on this wall with old characters, looking down at this lake on the computer screen. It was as nostalgic as it was beautiful.

"Are you insane!? I knew this was a bad idea, you guys are going to get me killed!" Atticus said, anxiety rising.

"It's really going to be alright, you see that right there," Zedra nodded to a glowing circle.

"A blue circle?!"

"Exactly! It's a guaranteed jump that grants experience if you complete it, but you can do flips and stuff to get more. This is a quick way to get to level 2 on the way out of the city. All you have to do is at least three flips or spins on the way down, after you activate the circle, of course," Zedra said matter-of-factly.

"Yeah, watch, I'll go first!" Sky said. She walked up to the circle, "Yup, I got the pop-up the second I entered, 'Would you like to take the Wall's Dive Test?' Yes, I would!" She put the staff on her back sling and prepared herself.

With two steps and maximum effort, Sky jumped off the wall, vaulting her legs over her head like a handless cart wheel. With the grace of a professional diver, she did 7 spinning flips with perfect form and landed in a dive.

Atticus watched in amazement at the high dive, something that would have set records or gone viral back on Earth if they managed to survive.

"10 out of 10!" Dark cheered.

"Oh yeah, she's probably the highest level in the game now...this jump is huge when you do it the first time!" Zedra said with a shake of his head. "How about you next Kesa?"

Kesa flipped her purple hair and fixed her deep sea blue eyes on her husband, "I guess I am ready."

She gracefully stepped off the edge after activating the circle. It was just as graceful, if less intense, with fewer flips involved. She landed feet first and swam up to the surface to join Sky a few seconds later. They looked like tiny dots floating in the beautiful water below.

"She did three flips, just enough to hit the second level..." Dark said. Then he also followed suit, throwing his head forward to do a quick front flip, followed by a series of them on the way down, easily leveling up with the experience from the little event.

"Atticus, there is no way to hit anything on the way down, but if you do at least 3 flips or spins, you get enough experience to hit level 2. How are you feeling?" Zedra asked.

"Terrified, but I guess I would rather land in water than go back the way we came..." Atticus said, stepping up to the circle and accepting the quest.

"You got this!"

"Uhh....AHHHHHHHH!!!" Atticus stepped off, slowly flipping twice and spinning once, while screaming the entire way down. A huge splash, nearly as big as Dark's, was the result. It was completely uncoordinated for a species as graceful as a kainen, and his health went down 15% instead of the standard 10.

"Not bad! Now...it's my turn!" Zedra activated the test, stepped back, and dove off into a spinning series of flips, managing twelve before he impacted the surface.

Down in the water, the party had nothing but smiles as they waited for Zedra.

"Oh. I see! The level up heals you!" Atticus said with a toothy smile to match the rest of the party. The pain and fear of the jump were instantly over as the group swam towards the shore. The beautiful water was warm and comfortable, even smelling clean and fresh. A side effect of the ambient magic.

Kesa was swimming faster than anyone could run on account of her racial bonus as a myrim. Her movement speed was wildly improved as she took off in the direction of the nearby shore.

"Whoa! Go Kesa!" Sky yelled from her position, treading water.

"I knew she would love that Myrim race, but she doesn't get her full mermaid form until 50," Dark said while watching his wife happily. The huge dark goliath treaded water slowly, but he didn't have any trouble staying afloat, even while wearing armor. Another aspect of the game is transferring over to this new life.

Kesa popped out of the water with a spin and landed on the shore, waiting for the rest to catch up. She had her staff in hand and a smirk firmly in place as she spoke, "Yeah, I can get used to this."

"That was awesome, Kes!" Dark said.

"I didn't get a skill point for that level, did I?" Atticus asked, searching his HUD for any notifications.

"Level three for the first skill point! You get 20 attribute points per level in the first tier, but I don't know the specifics for your race and class, which is what decides the stat distribution. Do you know which skill distribution Lightning Enhancers need Zed?" Sky asked.

"As long as I hear the skill descriptions, I can easily tell you what you need. I can't remember the exact names...It should be easy to figure out, though," Zedra said, thinking about it as they trudged along.

"Great, we have another level of XP to worry about before our first skill choice, let's get to it!" Dark said.

Zedra took off and found their first target, striking it on the rump to initiate combat.

“Of course it’s a boar…” Sky said sarcastically.

Just like that, the first combat the party experienced took place. The second it got stabbed, the thing squealed like mad, and the small floating health bar ticked down nearly 20%.

Then it ran after Zedra, who vaulted past Dark. He pulled his wooden shield and iron sword out. The boar used a [Charge Attack], blowing back Dark Hero.

“OW! Damn this thing is strong!" Dark yelled as he centered himself again.

The boar took off, circling around as Kesa laughed at her husband before turning and launching a [Mana Bolt].

It hit, followed immediately by Sky's [Mana Bolt], which decided to crit, bringing the boar down to 4% HP.

"Take it down, Atticus!" Sky yelled, ignoring the fact that she was supposed to go last instead of the kainen.

"R-right.." Atticus held the wooden scepter out like a rifle. Finally, a blue bolt flew off and dropped the boar as it turned back for another attack at Dark.

"Hah! See? Easy as pie!" Sky said with a smile.

"Yeah, that wasn't so bad!" Atticus said, the adrenaline slowly receding.

"Incoming! And you didn’t follow the attack order, Sky!" Zedra said, having pulled the next group of boars.

“Oops!”

Two boars were headed right at Dark, and he was able to get his shield up in time, stopping the charge of the lead boar. [Mana Bolt]s flew in from all sides, but stayed focused on one target at a time. The cooldowns synced, and the party just followed each other’s lead, while Zedra and Dark attacked with dagger and sword.

After the group of boars was cleared, the party exchanged adrenaline-filled gazes, grins appearing on all their faces.

They were already addicted.
