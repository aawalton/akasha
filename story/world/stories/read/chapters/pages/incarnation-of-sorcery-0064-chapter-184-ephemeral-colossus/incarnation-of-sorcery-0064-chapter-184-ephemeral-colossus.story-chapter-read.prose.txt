“What do you need an Incarnation for, sir?” Viren asked the next day.

They were to start the next phase of preparing the Inscription. Technically, one of the last phases. After this was over, after Ryland had gathered what he was looking for, he could consider things more or less done for the time being.

And then he could concentrate on getting Acrypha back.

“What I need is a bit of a Pure Manifestation that isn’t just myself,” Ryland said. “I need the energy of an Incarnation, the pure will and intent at a Conceptual level one can provide.”

That didn’t seem to fully answer Viren, which was a fair take. Ryland hadn’t properly explained everything. But he trusted Viren would slowly understand as time went on and Ryland took the necessary steps. It would just be significantly easier to show Viren instead of just trying to explain via words.

So, before he talked about it all, he first started searching for an Ephemeral Colossus.

“The problem with finding one of them is that they are rather elusive creatures,” Ryland said. “They often exist in half-Planes, which are transient Realms that sometimes can and sometimes cannot be reached easily, even with [Translocate].”

“How are you going to find them then, sir?” Viren asked.

“By hunting down the traces their passage leave behind and seeking them that way.”

“Like a frontiersman!”

“Yes, more or less.” Ryland was about to move and continue with his tasks, but one small detail bothered him. “Tell me, Viren. Do you miss Arcoryx City? Or the academy?”

Viren needed some time to think about it, which Ryland obviously didn’t mind as deliberated answers were always better. Yet he had thought the boy’s answer would have been more forthright. He was assuming Viren missed everything back home and was forcing himself to remain with Ryland because of Acrypha.

“I’d be lying if I said I didn’t miss the academy to some extent, sir,” Viren said. “But I don’t mind, really. I’m seeing and learning so much more than the academy could have ever taught me, and while I miss my friends, I’m sure I’ll be getting back to them before long.”

He gave Ryland a bright smile. A genuine one. It set Ryland’s heart at ease. He kept wondering if allowing Viren to accompany him had been the right decision or not, but so long as Viren himself never regretted it, then Ryland had nothing to fear either.

While Viren made Ryland’s work sound somewhat easy, it was anything but. By the time he found a trace that he could actually follow, it had taken him over two whole days, and he was now slightly concerned about time. At this rate, he was hoping Acrypha hadn’t been harmed by the Flourinus Order due to the value her connection with the Living Mana brought to them.

“[Translocate],” Ryland cast, after ensuring Viren was ready and layered with a great deal of protections. [Endless Evolution] and [Wings of Salvation] had granted Ryland aerial mobility, but he needed a different method for Viren. “[Translocate].”

The second rift spell came once they reached their destination, where a series of smaller rents in the air had little rocks and chunks of earth jutting out to form steps in the middle of the air. Viren would be able to use those to, quite literally, walk across the sky.

“We should come upon an Ephemeral Colossus soon enough, Viren,” Ryland said. “Please be prepared.”

Viren gave him a tight nod. “I’m ready, sir. This Colossus won’t know what hit it.”

“Well, I’m hoping we won’t have to fight or do anything aggressive. Depending on the being’s mood, we might be able to finish here with just a conversation.”

It was quite obvious that Viren very much doubted that, but he didn’t voice his doubts. He just nodded and put his faith in Ryland, something that was almost palpably obvious.

“Where exactly are we, sir?” Viren asked.

Ryland supposed this was where he explained what he had mentioned earlier about the Ephemeral Colossi existing in partial Realms. “This is a Transient Realm, Viren. They exist sometimes alongside our cosmos, sometimes along other Planes of existence such as the Overrealm.”

“That sounds complicated. How does it do that?”

“Explaining that would unfortunately require a whole course on spatial magic and understanding the related Conceptual Glyphs. But suffice it to say that many of the Conceptual Glyphs related to spacetime, such as the Glyphs of Dimension, Space, Time, and so on, are all warped and twisted.”

“What could allow something like that to happen?”

“Well, the presence of the Ephemeral Colossus, mostly.”

“Oh, right.”

Ryland got the impression that Viren was looking for something a little more in-depth than that. Maybe he did miss his academic instruction, which was what they really needed here to understand the magical intricacies taking place that could allow such situations to come about.

“This specific Transient Realm likely existed in multiple Planes at the same time,” Ryland said. “Going by how stitched-together everything here is.”

“That’s true…” Viren said.

The world they had entered through Ryland’s rift was one where mountains poked out through the clouds in the purple sky, where endless waterfalls slammed down to the shifting ground far below. A world where trees floated in mid-air and grew like hairballs, where the land had formed spires and lightless pits in equal measure.

Forget Viren, this was probably one of the most alien locations Ryland had ever seen.

They forged through the area, navigating through the strange landscape and its stranger features. Viren was gawking at everything, perhaps even forgetting what exactly they had come here to look for in the first place, but of course, Ryland had it well in mind.

And when it arrived, even Viren could no longer ignore it.

“Is that it, sir?” Viren asked, pointing to the distant glow. “That red and gold light?”

It was not only outshining anything else in the entire Transient Realm so far, it also just seemed alien when compared to anything else. The light had a strange solidness to it, as though it was more an aura than anything else.

“I believe so,” Ryland said. “While I don’t believe we’ve found our exact quarry yet, I think that’s the start of the main trail.”

They followed the actual trail quite quickly after that. The air condensed with more of that aura, and more forms of energy started blasting themselves against Ryland’s barriers. Compressed waves, constant blasts, tiny bolts naked to the invisible eye but still palpable in how the barriers shook and rippled.

The longer they travelled, the warier Ryland grew. Something had happened here. Something that didn’t foretell anything good.

Eventually, they discovered just what was wrong.

Where their final site should have contained the Ephemeral Colossus, there was just a yawning pit. A pit where only one thing was visible—the Colossus’s corpse.

Viren didn’t say anything. He just stared at the destruction far below.

Ryland’s mouth curled. The Ephemeral Colossus was a gigantic humanoid with glowing skin, a giant with stubby limbs and no head. But where its face should have been embedded into its chest, there was nothing but a gory, bleeding hole, energy turning into ash under whatever cursed spell had taken root here.

The being’s skin would normally have been as bright as a diamond under the sun. But now, it had turned dark as old ash, slowly breaking apart here and there in little sandy dunes.

Someone had thoroughly murdered the Ephemeral Colossus.

Viren seemed to understand this was no natural death, going by his next words. “Who did this, sir?”

Ryland held back a scowl. “That is what we need to discover…”

True to his word, Ryland bent to investigating the Colossus’s corpse next. He proceeded to cast a bevy of spells that would reveal what had happened to the body. Most of these centred around his Soul Glyph of Time. [Rewind] came up multiple times, replaying what had happened with so much clarity, Viren actually flinched when they saw the final scene.

“Impossible…” he muttered.

The holographic visual before them showed the Ephemeral Colossus in all its glory. It stood a few dozen armspans tall, gleaming and glowing with a power that made it pulse with lifegiving vigour. When looking at it, one could be forgiven for thinking that such a creature could never be pulled down. That such a being would never die.

A terribly wrong assumption. The proof of that lay right before Ryland.

The Colossus’s last moments were in tragic pain. Several bolts of magic fired in from above. Ryland inspected it closely, ignoring what happened to the Colossus, though he saw Viren wince and even look away a couple of times when the battle turned gruesome.

It wasn’t difficult finding the origin of the attacks. Ryland expanded the bounded region of [Rewind] to reveal powerful mages from high above pelting the Colossus with destructive magic. Closer inspection revealed unsurprising culprits. The Ephemeral Colossus Ryland was after, the entity he was hoping to assist, had been assaulted and killed by the Flourinus Order.

Ryland knew them well from the insignia on their cloaks. There was no point in looking at their figures and forms. Some could shapeshift. Others changed bodies, much like he did. Others might just have evolved. Who knew. The possibilities were endless.

Yet, if there was one thing about the Order, it was that not all of them cared to hide their identities.

“Are those the mages who attacked Vyrd, sir?” Viren asked. He probably recalled them well from the time they had tried unleashing a Calamity in Androvia. They had obviously been active on Rhel too, but Viren hadn’t been directly involved with those incidents as much.

“Yes,” Ryland said. “That is them.”

“Why would they do this? Did they come here seeking the same thing you did?”

“Ephemeral Colossi are repositories of enormous mana. So much so that they often house Pure Manifestations of mana, sometimes even other Incarnations. That’s just how they are born. As such, I was hoping I’d find what I sought here. Either the Order wanted to steal it before me, or they suspected I would need this and acted pre-emptively. You see the real issue, Viren?”

The boy thought for a moment before his eyes widened. “You said they were stuck in the Overrealm, sir. That means either this place can reach the Overrealm, or—”

“Or they have a way of moving in and out of the Overrealm.”

Ryland didn’t think it was merely a cast of [Wound Corridor]. Otherwise, if it was that simple, Ryland would have been able to enter and leave the Overrealm at will with his rifts. No, there was something else going on.

Most likely, these were Order operatives who had never entered the Plane of the Great Gods to begin with. They had remained outside to carry out their masters’ commands. It wasn’t that the Order could freely travel between Planes. Rather, they just had a way of transmitting their intentions or otherwise communicating with those not inside the Overrealm.

“I’m sorry, sir,” Viren said mournfully. “If these Order bastards could come here, then there’s no telling where else they might have gone.”

Ryland sighed, then focused back on his task. “It’s alright, Viren. Take heart. It’s unlikely that they could have repeated this incident. And there is more to be gleaned from this. Plus, the visit here is far from a waste. We’ve been witnessing the vital essence ejected from the Colossus all throughout our little trek in this Transient Realm.”

He went on to take care of matters here. Well, the preliminary matters. With similar magic as to what he had used to collect some of the accretion stream matter, Ryland gathered up a good chunk of the essence of the fallen Colossus.

This was mostly gathering the crimson aura floating all over the place into a newly-constructed pocket Realm, closing the pocket Realm with a Tier [25] Subglyph of Sealing, then Inscribing the Glyph into a sketchbook where Viren noted down the Glyphs he was learning.

“Don’t worry, sir,” he said. “I’ll get this back to the Inscription on Rhel and place it right on top of the last open site.”

Ryland had already shown him where all the ingredients were supposed to go, so he was familiar with the workings of it. With another [Translocate], he returned Viren to Rhel. Now, he could focus on seeing just what had happened to the Ephemeral Colossus.

And perhaps, if he was fortunate, he might just find a trace of an Incarnation as well.

If there was one thing Viren was proud of about himself, it was his growing capacity to accept and understand absolute hogwash.

Maybe it was a skill anyone would master after sticking around someone like Ryland for as long as Viren had. After all, his presence just seemed to cause more and more unbelievable and impossible incidents to pile up without giving anyone any room to take it all in. Viren was almost suffocating under the barrage of incredulity he had been forced to witness.

And it wasn’t just the crazy things he kept seeing. It was everything he was learning too. Planes of existence that went beyond “mere” Realms? Transient Realms that could supposedly exist nowhere and everywhere all at once.

What was next? A pocket Realm that could hold a whole castle fitting inside one’s palm?

Honestly, he wouldn’t even be surprised if Ryland already had something of the sort. He had displayed that he possessed a pocket Realm just as a personal inventory. He had also proven that he could create his own pocket Realms without a great deal of effort.

All that said, Viren was ultimately glad to be back on Rhel. What had happened to that poor Ephemeral Colossus was a tragedy. But at the same time, he had stood witness to the destruction of those Astral Raiders. It was starting to feel like he was spectating two sides of a war he didn’t quite understand just yet.

Which was bad, because that meant he was caught up in it too, didn’t it?

Viren sighed and headed over to the site Ryland had shown him a while back. Not a lot of time had passed since they had left. It was only the afternoon now.

A rumble travelled through the earth. Viren’s heartrate immediately picked up pace, and his eyes began darting around to find the source of—

Cracks split the air, like distant thunder. Viren stared straight up, calling on his manasoul at the same time instinctively. His eyes widened. The sky was turning dark. A storm of literal lava was burning through the air.

He cursed. Just as Ryland had foretold, they were under attack!
