“Void?” I ask, tilting my head, and Inu nods.

It lets me play around more with the storage of [Reservoir], and any gravity or nullification based effects will pair nicely with [Resistance]. I got a lot of emotion-based options, but I felt like I didn’t need any help in that. I know I’m kind, I don’t need the Tower to assure me.”

That makes me smile, just a little. I’m glad that she’s not too down about our most recent fight anymore. Usually, I’d have expected Inu to feel the aftereffects of killing someone who was defenselessly scrabbling on the ground for a little longer… but she’s a tough girl, and has only gotten tougher.

I do wonder, though… one of my epitaph offerings was called vicious void. Did she get this because of me? Of course, almost instantly, she notices my thoughts and turns to me, tilting her head with a smile.

“If you beat yourself up over this, I’m going to strangle you,” she threatens with a smile, and I can’t suppress a snort of amusement.

“Not beating myself up,” I say sagely. I try to cross my arms, then remember that I only have one, and the attempt looks a little silly, so I simply lay my hand on the stump of my shoulder.

Inu nods at that, then smiles. “Better not be.”

For a long moment, a companionable silence hangs in the room. We’ve all made our choices, and I can see that everyone seems reasonably happy with theirs. They’re a whole spectrum of flashy, but I’m still happy with what I picked. It’ll let me improve faster. And given how things seem structured, I’m pretty sure I can gain more authorities later.

“You’re smiling again,” Inu notes, and I nod.

“We’ll have a spot ready for them,” Inu finishes with a smile. “That sounds good to me.”

Opal groaned slightly. “Dar’s gonna be real upset if I leave them again.”

“You wanna spend another month on the floor that stinks like rot?” I ask quietly.

They blink. “Point taken,” they say, then sigh softly. “So we climb?”

“Yeah, we climb,” I confirm, with everyone’s agreement.

And with that, we set off to meet the others.

With everything settled, there’s just one more member of our party left to talk to. I glance down at my shadow, and hold out my hand.

Kuro crawls out from that darkness, the cloak whose form they usually take unravelling into their preferred shape - a large centipede, though with a somewhat cutesified appearance. Their long body weaves around my arm, finding purchase with strong legs, as they look at me.

“So, buddy, can you come with us?” I ask quietly. Kuro nods at me, eagerly agreeing.

[The Creeping Darkness informs you that it has instilled a part of its authority in your companion. Additionally, due to your current situation, the Tower will generally consider Kuro and you as a single entity.]

I look up at the ceiling, trying to stare through it to see the eyes, and give a small snicker. What a weird constellation, so attached to seeing me succeed, just because I was a little nice to one shadow critter.

With a small sigh, I direct Kuro to scamper up further, so I can pet them with my single arm. Then, a moment later, they dissolve into smokey darkness, turning back into a cloak that hides my stump. “Well then,” I say out loud, for everyone. “I need one last recharge on my headphones, and we’ll be off.”

Bay laughs for a brief moment at the request, but still obliges. I give the others some time to say their goodbyes, letting them hug and chat and make their small promises of safety. Sitting by the side, I watch it all unfold, the way they interact. A small sigh leaves my lips, and I smile.

It’s nice, seeing my friends happy. It’s nice, going on adventures with them. And I’ll make sure there will be many, many more such adventures.
