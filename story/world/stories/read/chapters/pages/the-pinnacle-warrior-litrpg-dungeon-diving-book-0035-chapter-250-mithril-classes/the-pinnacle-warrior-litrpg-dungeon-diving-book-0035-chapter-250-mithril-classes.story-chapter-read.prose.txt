“That’s it!”

Benedict’s excited words were a shining beacon of hope for Astrid.

It was now the tenth day since Gabriel guided them to the lower parts of the Dungeon proper to test themselves against Mithril tier monsters, irregular and otherwise. Each day passed in a blur, and under normal circumstances, she would have loved every moment. The constantly changing landscapes and monsters to face fought to demand her attention, and the constant exploration of what each monster was capable of had been an incessant challenge. She’d fought lamias and arachne, drakes, chimeras, and a hydra. Each of these were something she’d dreamed of as a kid, but with the civil war rearing its head behind her, Astrid couldn’t bring herself to care about these little exciting things. Instead, she could only concern herself with how much time had passed.

Was her mother fighting right now? Was her father tending to the wounded with his talismans? Was Ahna being forced to carry her son and flee from enemies? And, if all these things were happening, could Astrid have stopped them from happening by being there?

Benedict had now reached the peak of level 90, and was the penultimate one to do so. Astrid herself was only a couple hundred experience away, with the actual numerical discrepancies between each party member's experience count varying by the day, depending on who had dealt the killing blow, who managed to do something that would give them experience outside of killing something, and so on. After taking a month just to study and experiment with spells, Skandr had stepped solidly into the lead and had reached the peak of level 90 less than half an hour before. Muti, then Felix, followed, and now that Benedict was done, Astrid was the last one, with a single fight separating her from her own options being presented to her.

“I saw some sludge monstrosity over there,” Benedict said, chuckling and waving over to the left. “You can kill it, and then finally we'll all take a look at our options.”

“I’ll do it for you,” Muti said, grinning wide.

In fact, Muti didn’t wait for an answer before she disappeared, her body flashing with shadow. Astrid reactivated Body Surge and followed along, finding the monster that Benedict had actually caught sight of. It wasn’t a sludge monster or a slime, thankfully. Those were, at best, difficult to kill. Instead, it was a stone elemental that had taken the form of a type of mole, except it was about person-sized.

With the two's burst Skills being pushed to their maximum, they were able to deal with the high Steel tier monster very quickly. When the kill notification flashed in Astrid’s vision, she didn’t care about that, but she did smile widely at seeing the level-up notification.

She and Muti shared excited smiles with each other as they hurried back to the rest of the party. Almost as a testament to how much time they had spent together, Gabriel stood visibly nearby, and he allowed himself a small smile at the obvious results of Astrid and Muti's hunt.

“Ordinarily,” Gabriel said, interrupting the party, “I would get us all out of the Dungeon proper before you look at your evolution options. After all, most people aren’t in a whole lot of a hurry and prefer to be able to take some time to look at what options are available. However, there’s a good chance that you’ll receive quests that have you getting into fights with things around here.”

“So you’re saying for us to do what we were already doing,” Benedict asked, his tone dry.

Instead of giving a verbal answer, Gabriel just smiled, and Benedict shook his head, waving off the older man.

Then, without any further conversation between the five of them, the Wanderers looked at their evolution options. Astrid felt her stomach twist and, despite the nervousness and expectations, started to read.

Astrid swallowed around a sudden dryness in her throat. So long as it was of extraordinary rarity, of course. But to see this Class, see what it was offering to her, was more than enough to make her tongue suddenly feel like cotton in her mouth. And all of that was simply from reading the description, before she actually thought about what any of it meant.

She wanted to just move onto the next, to see what everything else was, especially since a part of her mentally revolted against her class title becoming “berserker“. Regardless, she focused on the other offerings from the class. The only skill that was even remotely familiar to her was that of the equipment, skill, and she still needed to bring it up to see what the changes in her auxiliary skills would be with this evolution.

Immortal Berserker’s Equipment (Mithril): A compound Skill comprising of: Blunt Weapon’s Mastery (Mithril),  Shield Wielding (Mithril), Steady Load (Mithril). Grants the Skill holder the ability to doff and don their equipment in an instant for a Minute mana cost.

Astrid let out a long, slow breath. The upgrade from Lunt weapons mastery to Malay weapons mastery gave, so far she could tell, the same damage bonus that her previous skill would have, but now she would be able to wield bladed weapons with just as much skill as she used her hammer. That would be really nice, allowing her to have a deeper storage of weapons, just in case whatever she preferred to use wasn’t able to be used effectively against her next enemy. That was amazing. Regardless, this was the most boring of all of the skills that this potential class would give her, and she moved her attention to the next one. Without thinking too much about it, she opened the description of what apparently was the keystone skill of this class.

Berserker’s Immortality (Mithril): This Skill increases the holder’s passive rate of mana, health, and stamina recovery by 300%. For a Severe Stamina cost per two minutes, the Skill’s holder can activate the Skill to increase the Skill holder’s mana, health, and stamina recovery by an additional 600% and increase the Skill holder’s Power, Alacrity, and Fortitude by 150%. The breath flew from Astrid’s lungs. She felt her eyes widen as she found a place to sit to try to understand what this Skill would do. Passively, she would recover four times as fast as her attributes alone would give her. Then, of course, there was Boon of Indomitability, which she was fairly certain was multiplicative with the boost to her passive recovery rate as given by Physique. It was hard to tell, exactly. Regardless, that would mean that she would visibly heal from wounds without spending charges, and the Skill’s active state would become even more insane.

Giving her the same boost to her attributes as Body Surge already did, but more to Fortitude and making her do more damage the longer the fight went on was crazy. It’d be a difficult balancing act to make sure she wasn’t going to kill herself by running out of stamina and collapsing in the middle of the fight, but when she learned how to use it correctly, the Skill would let her become basically a Boss all by herself. Then, there would be the addition to her attributes, and the words “negative effects” weren’t limited. Could that also apply to all the damage she took? But the next part talked about damage reduction, but was phrased differently…

She shook her head and, with a sigh that tried to convince her that she couldn’t say no to the Class, she looked at the next Skill granted by merging two of her existing ones.

Murderous Rage (Mithril): For a Moderate stamina and a Minor health cost per minute, grants every one of the Skill holder’s attacks a 200% increase in damage. During the duration of Murderous Rage, the Skill holder suffers from Heavy reduction in rationality.

Astrid shook her head. Just like the attributes, which were so totally focused on the physical three, this Skill was focused on continuing to focus entirely on the continued doubling and tripling down on becoming a killing machine. She almost couldn’t think about the Skill as she mindlessly looked at the other two this evolution would offer her.

Furious Fists (Mithril): Every one of the Skill holder’s melee attacks is strengthened by 70%

Brutal Fury (Mithril): The Skill holder’s mana, without any instruction from the Skill holder, will passively serve to worsen every wound they deal and inhibit the wounded from healing.

Yes, this was exactly the kind of Class that would lead the Barbarians. The kind of Class that she’d have had nightmares about as a child. If she was dropped in an enemy formation, so long as she wasn’t killed in the first handful of seconds, she would be able to rip through them all and come out stronger than she went in. The last two Skills were interesting, the first being a plain strengthening Skill and the second being a reminder of how the Barbarian people looked down on mana.

The Class wasn’t exactly what she was looking for, but it was immensely powerful. Everything worked together to paint a very obvious picture, and she could see herself able to carve through battlefields, whether they were filled with people or monsters, with impunity. Having looked at the Class in its entirety, she moved on to the next.

While everything that the Class gave was amazing, it lacked the excitement of the Berserker Class. Astrid couldn’t help but laugh at herself having the opinion as she reread “the echoes of your hammer ringing over the horizon are the surest death knell”. The Immortal Hammermaster was, so far as she could tell, a direct upgrade to Immortal Champion. It was a somewhat balanced distribution of attributes, though it leaned more directly into physicality, proportionally, than Immortal Champion had. That didn’t matter, and, in fact, Astrid preferred it that way, given how much she depended on her party to cover her weaknesses.

Even though she felt the overwhelming difference between Berserker and Hammermaster, Astrid remained, at best, leery of the first Class. Switching Smite and Spectral Graviton for Murderous Rage was a big sacrifice.
