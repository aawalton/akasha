"Okay, so here's the chores you need to do for the day. Feed the chickens, clean all the dishes, draw water from the well, and weed the fields. You also need to clean the house, the chicken coop, and get the sauna going during this week," Dad listed Callie's chores the next morning.

She paled. While she expected an increase in chores, drawing water from the well was very tiring for someone as small as she was. Not to mention cleaning the coop and starting the sauna. It was true that they could all probably use a good wash, but even if this summer wasn't exactly the warmest, with a rather chilly wind blowing from the north, she didn't feel like sweating in the sauna.

"Father, isn't that a bit much? Especially drawing water. It's too heavy a job for Callie," Callen protested.

"She can always use the smaller bucket and make more trips," Dad pointed out.

Callie wanted to protest, but kept her mouth shut. She had already decided to accept her punishment with open arms. As long as Wiggles remained safe, Callie would haul the heavy buckets the whole day if she needed to.

"She still needs to do her apprenticeship at Granny Ethel's," Callen pointed out.

"I'm not picky. You can do it in the evening if you can't manage in the morning. As long as it is done by the end of the day, I don't care," Dad said.

"I… I'll get it done," Callie said, clenching her little fists.

"Good. Callen, don't forget about your own chores before training. Stables need a thorough cleaning, as does Gale," Dad said firmly.

Callen sighed, but didn't dare to object to Dad. With the tasks handed out, Dad headed to the smithy. Callen threw a piteous glance at Callie.

"You know, I could help you draw at least some of the water," he offered.

"No, I'm alright," Callie said stubbornly. She was still a bit mad at him for trying to tell everyone about Wiggles. "I will manage."

Determined, Callie headed to the storage room where she poured three cups of chicken feed into the bowl. They didn't need as much during the summer as the chickens managed to forage a good deal on their own. Not to mention that Callie would be giving them softer weeds to peck later on.

"Callie, are you sure?" Callen asked again.

"Yes! I wouldn't want to scare you since I touch monsters," Callie snapped, just to make sure that Callen knew what she was mad about.

He winced and opened his mouth to say something, but she was already out the door. Deep in her heart, she felt like she was being a bit harsh. Her brother had only tried to protect her. But imagining villagers trying to hunt down Wiggles quickly reignited her anger. He had even called him a monster!

Maybe Brother should meet Wiggles. Perhaps he would understand that Wiggles is no monster if he saw him.

That seemed like a rather good idea in Callie's opinion. Although she had no clue how to do it. With as many chores as she had, she was unlikely to get a chance to meet Wiggles. All she could do was console herself with the thought that he was safe and continue her work.

Chickens were eagerly clucking at Callie's arrival, gathering around her at the expectation of food. The rooster watched over his chickens diligently. Callie poured the food into the special wooden feeding troughs and proceeded to their nests to search for eggs.

Callie had found four eggs, noticing the fifth one in the last nest, she reached out for it. But then a familiar greenish tendril extended toward her from underneath the nest.

"Wiggles?!" Callie exclaimed, noticing the mireling hiding in the corner. He had flattened himself so close to the wall that his translucent, green slime nearly blended in with the wall. Even his core seemed to have gone a much darker shade of red to blend in.

The mireling wiggled joyously and touched her hand gently with his tendril. As if to answer, Callie's mark glowed ever so faintly.

"What are you doing here, Wiggles?" Callie asked, but couldn't help but smile.

Wanted to see friend.

Callie blinked. Had she just heard a voice? A gentle, soft voice that reminded her of a young boy.

"What?" She gaped at Wiggles, not quite believing her… ears?

Wanted to see friend.

The voice repeated, but it wasn't the sound she was hearing. The voice was talking directly in her head.

"Wiggles? You're talking to me? In my head?" Callie asked in disbelief.

Yes. Probably.

"What?! But how is that possible?"

Don't know.

The way Wiggles spoke was rather simplistic, but the fact that he could speak was amazing in its own right. Could other arcane beasts do it too? Did they choose not to because they didn't trust people? Or could Wiggles speak to her now because of the petal that grew on her? She felt like some sort of magic was involved.

"It's dangerous here, Wiggles. If my parents saw you…" Callie shuddered to imagine such a scenario.

Hide.

Wiggles withdrew his tendril and shrank down, pressing himself as close to the corner as possible. It was actually amazing how small he could make himself, leaving only a small layer of slime around his core.

"I didn't know you could get so small," Callie called out in fascination.

Can grow big!

The next moment Wiggles expanded, growing large enough to reach her knee. The rooster clucked threateningly, but Callie gently patted him away, assuring that nobody would hurt his hens.

"That's amazing, Wiggles!" Callie clapped happily.

Grow bigger if more slime.

"More slime?"

Eat more, more slime.

Callie didn't know what to do with all this wealth of knowledge Wiggles was giving her. It was no wonder that mirelings seemed ever-present, yet uncatchable at the same time. They were fast, could change their size, and choose whether their slime was acidic or safe to touch.

She had so many questions for Wiggles. She wanted to grab paper and write it all down. But the clucking of the rooster reminded her that she had a ton of chores to do.

"I wish I could play with you, Wiggles. But I have chores to do," Callie sighed.

Cho-res?

"Those are things you have to do. Like feeding the chicken and bringing water inside from the well."

Why? Eat and play.

Callie scratched her head. Wiggles likely didn't understand the concept of chores. Mirelings had no chores. They could do whatever they wanted.

"I'll play with you once I am done, okay? Dad would be really mad at me if I didn't finish the chores," Callie tried to explain, rather unsuccessfully.

Who? Mean human who push friend? Acid!

Callie hurriedly shook her head. "No! No! Those girls aren't my dad. Dad would never push me. And you can't burn people with acid!"

Why not?

"Because then they will come and hunt you," Callie warned. "And it's not a nice thing to do."

Not nice…

Callie realized that Wiggles didn't understand the concept of nicest or the danger the hunters posed. If Wiggles were to walk around burning people with acid… Well, needless to say, no amount of chores Callie did would save him.

She crouched down and said to Wiggles, trying to approach the matter from an angle little mireling could understand. "Please, do not burn people. I would need to do more chores and wouldn't have any time to play."

Wiggles wobbled his slime.

No! Burn not. So friend play.

"Thank you, Wiggles! Now let me get the water from the well. Maybe if I am fast enough, we can play a little," Callie said and rushed out of the coop.

But to her horror, Wiggles followed right after her, making the chickens cluck nervously, and the rooster puff out his feathers angrily as the mireling got a little too close.

"No, Wiggles, you can't come with me. What if someone sees you?" Callie said.

Hide.

"You'll hide?"

Hide.

That didn't fully satisfy Callie. What if Dad came back home for something? Would Wiggles be able to hide in time? What if the neighbor stopped by, or Aurelia decided to harass Callie in her own home? Okay, never mind the last one. Even she wouldn't dare to do that, especially with Callen around.

Which reminded Callie. This was the perfect opportunity. She could show her brother that Wiggles was completely harmless. Never mind the acid and Wiggles threatening to burn more people. That would be something she would work on. Surely, the little mireling would understand that using acid slime on people was bad.

"Remember that you can't burn people with acid. Okay?" Callie warned.

Yes!

His confirmation calmed Callie a little, and she hurried to the well, followed by Wiggles. The mireling was quite curious about her drawing the water. Callie also warned Wiggles not to crawl inside it because it was very deep, and he could end up stuck.

Climb out.

The mireling slid onto the edge of the well and then crawled inside. To Callie's surprise, he could easily stick to the moist wall, hanging on much like a snail would.

"Wow, you can even do that! I'm not sure if I'll be able to remember all the amazing things you can do to write them down," Callie chuckled.

Wri-te?

Callie was once again stumped. How could she explain writing to a mireling who had no concept of writing or letters whatsoever? Her thoughts were interrupted by a shocked gasp from behind her.

For a second, she feared Dad had returned and saw Wiggles, but thankfully, it was Callen.

"Wh-What is that?" He pointed at Wiggles's tendril, which was sticking out of the well.

"That's Wiggles! Wiggles, this is my brother, Callen," Callie said, and the mireling poked itself gingerly over the edge of the well. Although he didn't reveal himself fully and withdrew his tendril.

Brother? Friend's kin?

"Yes, he is my kin," Callie nodded in confirmation, then turned to Callen. "Did you hear that, brother? Wiggles can speak!"

Callen's face filled with confusion and concern. "Speak? Callie, what are you talking about? It's just the two of us talking."

"No, Wiggles can talk too. Wiggles, say something," Callie urged the mireling.

Hello.

"See, he said hello," Callie smiled.

Callen's face grew deeply concerned. "C-Callie, it didn't say anything. It's a mon—an arcane beast. They can't talk."

Callie's heart sank. Could it be that she was the only one who could understand Wiggles? Then it was undoubtedly the work of the magic mark. Although the expression Callen was making scared Callie. He looked at her like other villagers had looked at her. Like she was different. An outcast.

"Brother—"

She didn't get to say anything when they both heard shouts coming from the front of the house. "Aurelwynns! Anyone at home?!"

"Hide!" Callie whispered to Wiggles.

But the mireling was already ahead of her, disappearing into the well. Callen threw a quick look in his direction. Seeing the mireling gone, Callen took Callie's hand. "Let's go see what's going on. It sounds urgent."

Her brother smiled, but it wasn't his genuine smile. She could tell this was forced as the same strange look still sat in his eyes. Did her brother believe that she was crazy now? If that were the case, she wasn't sure what she would do…

The man at their house turned out to be one of the hunters. The one with whom Mom usually went on her hunts. But Mom herself was nowhere to be seen.

"Where's Mom?" Callie asked, a nasty feeling building in her gut.

The hunter, seeing Callie, hesitated, rubbing the back of his neck nervously.

"Where's Mom?" Callen demanded, his hand tightening its hold on Callie's.

"Your mother got injured by the monsters. She's currently at Granny Ethel's. I thought I should let you two know."

At that moment, it felt like Callie's whole world came crashing down. Her mom… Her beloved mom was injured…

Without any more words, Callen and Callie both sprinted towards the healer's hut.
