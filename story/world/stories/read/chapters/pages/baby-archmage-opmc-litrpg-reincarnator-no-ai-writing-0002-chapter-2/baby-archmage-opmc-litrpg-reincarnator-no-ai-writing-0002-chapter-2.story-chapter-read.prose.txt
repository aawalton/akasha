I didn’t know what removing restrictions from Mind and Magic related Skills meant, but it couldn’t be bad either.

I looked at the Mythic Skill. Being able to see magic sounded just what I needed. Was that something rare and special, since the ability came from a Mythic Skill?

I would need to learn how to activate this Skill and test it, but first I finished reading the notifications.

Six attribute points per level? That was crazy! I had gotten two from leveling up Meditation. Mythic was clearly higher value than Uncommon. Now that I had two data points, I could make a hypothesis. Was this world really using video game logic? Well hell, wasn’t that obvious with the System being a thing? Was it so implausible that the rarity system was also using the same logic.

I applied my magnificent powers of meditation to string together an idea. Uncommon gave two attribute points. Whatever rarity was below it, be it basic or common, would likely give one. Mythic was several tiers above it. Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic?

Made sense to me. The difference in growth between a Mythic and an Uncommon Skill was kind of huge. I wondered if there was a balancing system. Otherwise wouldn’t you just want to collect high tier skills? Maybe they were harder to level or something.

Damn it! I wanted to learn more and not just guess.

Either way, I was going to be getting massive gains in my Perception Attribute, and I was looking forward to seeing what kind of an effect that would have on me.

Mana Flow was certainly an interesting Attribute. It was just what I had been hoping for, and if my baby face obeyed me, I’d have had the widest grin. Mana Sight was clearly an amazing Skill, and I was now one step closer to learning magic.

I tried activating it through the System. It took some fiddling, like trying to find the right frequency for a radio station, but I finally managed. But to my great disappointment, I barely saw anything. What I saw was the equivalent of faintly flashing lights; the kind you see when you hit your head. Was that mana? I reached out to one such shining particle and my hand passed through it.

Maybe I needed higher Perception to see the mana? That was an idea worth trying. Given the amazing cascade of rewards the System had given me, I was more than ready to grind my Perception higher! I would put my Sage Perk to work and power level Meditation until I could see the mana.

The following weeks I spent basking in the love of my parents and meditating. Mother and father clearly loved each other, and from what I could gather we lived in a small island community. The cabin smelled earthy with a hint of fish. Father smelled of sweat and fish whenever he came home from work. I applied my magnificent powers of Perception and concluded that he worked with fish. I got used to the smell fast, and even learned to like it.

The meditation got intense. I was convinced I just needed a few levels and I could access Mana Sight. Did I have any proof of that? Absolutely not. But it was motivating, so I just went with it. For the first time in my existence I applied myself whole-heartedly. It was amazing, and I enjoyed the budding pride that was starting to emerge in my mind. The only problem was the preposterous amount of sleep my baby body required.. It meant that my practice was constantly interrupted, but I was making steady progress.

Damn straight. I tried to meditate on the effects of the Meditation level. They were subtle, but evident. I was a baby who couldn’t move! If I didn’t accept boredom, there would be suffering even Buddha couldn’t save me from.

The fear of losing myself and the fear of formlessness were the big hurdles. The fear of nothingness of the abyss. I knew it could not hurt me intellectually, but it was of little help when you felt like you were going to die when you pushed further into the deep state of meditation.

I failed multiple times, before I learned to relax into it. I still vaguely remembered my death. That feeling of letting go of life and losing yourself. It helped me with the practice, but it was difficult.

As time passed, memories of my previous life were getting hazier, as if a dream I was forgetting. I didn’t remember my name anymore, or the family I lost. But I did remember the most distinct feature of my past life though.

Passiveness.

Not necessarily lack of action. I remembered playing video games with friends. I remembered going to school and working part time. I remembered asking a girl out.

But I also remembered being stuck.

It was a passiveness of spirit. Sure, I had been doing stuff, but if I was honest with myself, I had just been going through the motions.

At some point in my life, I had stopped moving forward. I had been stuck in a job I didn’t like. With hobbies I no longer cared about. I remembered the sensation of gradually, sneakily, my world getting smaller and smaller. It came to a point where I was just wasting time on my sofa, vaguely dissatisfied with every waking moment as I scrolled through internet nonsense to waste another day.

I still remembered that taste of despair.

I would not allow myself to live like that again. As monotone as being a baby was, I found immense joy in improving my Skills and applying myself and getting rewarded for it. I wanted more of it. I wanted it all. I couldn’t wait to start using Mana Sight and begin my journey into magic.

I started to use meditation for something different. Instead of letting go of form, I decided to focus on form. I focused on the feelings of my last life and the disgust I felt.

I resolved to live this life differently. I would take joy in life. I would strive for things. I would respect myself and work hard. I focused on who I wanted to become in this life.

Someone worthy. Someone who took zest in life. I would not compromise, cower and certainly not fall into the gray pit of indifference that I had suffered in.

I would chase magic, and never apologize for it.

This I promised myself. For the first time I was awake longer than before. I must have held this state of mind for hours.
