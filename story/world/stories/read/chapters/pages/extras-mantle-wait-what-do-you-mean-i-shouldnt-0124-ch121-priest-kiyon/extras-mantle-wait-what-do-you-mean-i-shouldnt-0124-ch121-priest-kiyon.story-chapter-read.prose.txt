The floors below the fortieth had no lights—not even the emergency lights—and the corridors stretched into darkness, thick with dust.

He moved through the abandoned corridor, his fingers brushing against the wall as he walked, coming away coated in grime that hadn't been disturbed in what felt like years.

Veric was right. These lower levels are untouched.

« It appears so, judging from the complete lack of active power systems, » Angel confirmed in the back of his mind. « But there's something else. I'm not detecting any ambient essence flow in these floors either. It's like... static. Dead air. »

"I can guess why," Jin muttered, scanning the corridor ahead with his mystic eyes.

Only two exits from the corridor he was in. Both were sealed by thick and tall blast doors.

He reached out curiously with his darkness, trying to phase through the door the way he'd slipped through floors throughout his descent.

Nothing happened.

His shadows refused to penetrate beyond the blast door's surface, sliding off the metal like water off oiled steel. Even when he fired a test shot at the door—low-power, just checking—the bullet sparked off without leaving a mark.

At least he could still phase through the floor. That was what mattered. The Order IV cultist was down there somewhere in the depths, and he could figure out these blast doors later once the primary threat was neutralized.

And so he dropped through.

The descent was a slow process—falling through the forty-first floor, forty-second, forty-third—each one the same as the last. Dust, darkness, sealed doors, and the oppressive weight of abandonment pressing down from all sides.

He'd slid the now-cracked veil of the shared horizon mask back over his face somewhere around the forty-third level.

The mask, even cracked, kept its basic function.

Which was a good thing, too, because the dust situation was getting worse with each floor he descended, disturbed by his passage and billowing up in choking clouds that had already triggered one uncontrolled coughing fit that cost him several minutes.

The deeper he went, the worse everything became.

First, the dust. Then the cobwebs stretched across corridors in thick curtains.

He finally reached what he counted as the forty-ninth level and immediately felt the change.

Angel?

« On it. Scanning now. »

Jin stayed hidden in his conjured shadows—even in total darkness, wrapping himself in skill-conjured darkness provided better concealment against detection abilities—and took in his surroundings with careful attention to detail.

This floor was clean and well-maintained. He'd expected the cultists to tidy up their base of operations, so the cleanliness itself didn't surprise him.

What did surprise him was the ambient essence.

Unlike the stale, static atmosphere on the floors above, the air here practically hummed with… twisted power, like something left to rot.

Jin cut off his passive essence gathering before Angel could even warn him.

« Good instinct, that ambient mana is heavily tainted. Drawing it in would compromise your channels. »

Yes. And there's something else.

Blood.

To produce a stench this pervasive, the cultists must have killed hundreds of civilians down here.

Jin clenched his teeth, reeling back the anger that wanted to surge forward.

« Breathe, lean into the Sovereign's indifference. You'll need the clarity. »

Not yet. Jin tightened his grip on Iron Howl, feeling the gun's weight settle in his palm like an old friend. I need these emotions first.

« Oh. For the soul… »

Jin nodded, even though Angel didn't need the physical confirmation.

He pulled up Iron Howl's status with a thought, reviewing the weapon's current state.

Jin dismissed the panel and kept moving, letting the anger at what he'd found down here—the blood, the corruption, the casual cruelty—seep into the gun through their bond.

Fuel for later.

« I think we've found the ritual site, » Angel said. « Multiple large-scale essence disturbances ahead. The signature matches… they're draining something and redirecting it elsewhere. »

Understood.

« Also—and I can't stress this enough—do not attempt passive essence gathering during the upcoming fight. At all. Rely exclusively on essence-stealing effects from your skills and equipment. »

Jin raised an eyebrow behind the mask. Going into a fight against an Order IV entity without the ability to passively regenerate essence would significantly limit his options.

He'd have to either commit to aggressive plays right from the start, holding back no reserves, or play very safely.

Are you certain?

« Yes. Your Eternal Sovereign star can synthesize corrupted essence into usable power, but the conversion takes time. If you try to draw in ambient mana during active combat, it'll clog your channels faster than the star can process it. You'll choke on your own power intake. »

Great start.

« I'm confident this is a deliberate ritual effect the enemy has established. Which means they'll be expecting us and that they are not taking any chances. »

Jin exhaled slowly, already running tactical adjustments.

I already assumed they'd be prepared after managing to purge the Doom mark.

He took one more deep breath—letting the last dregs of anger and righteous fury charge into Iron Howl's resonance—and started walking.

The corridor ended in a ruined junction where three passages converged into a larger space, and the man waiting at the far end was staring directly at Jin with a seething intensity that suggested he'd been expecting company.

Male, judging from the face and patchy beard. Eyes locked on Jin's position despite the darkness and concealment.

Definitely expected.

The full wave of Order IV presence hit Jin like a speeding truck, but he didn’t waver.

He let the last flicker of emotion—the anger, the determination, the icy hatred for what these cultists had done—tap into Iron Howl through their bond, charging the weapon with intent.

Then he reached for the Sovereign's Indifference and let it wash over him.

The world drained to grey, and the crushing weight of the Order IV aura trying to worm into his mind and break his will faded into background pressure that he could simply... account for.

Still there. Still dangerous.

Just not overwhelming anymore.

There was the physical pressure too—like a massive boulder dropped across his shoulders—but that was bearable now that he had his own aura surging to retaliate.

He'd stood up to Vellakin's aura. To Nourma's killing intent. To Trish's cheerful violence.

Compared to those experiences, this priest was strong.

But not peak-realm strong.

Difficult, yes. Dangerous, absolutely.

But doable without catastrophic costs.

Jin twisted his neck, joints popping, and tightened his grip on Iron Howl while pulling Essence's Edge into his off-hand.

The cultist’s expression was carefully blank—no visible emotion on the surface—but Jin's eyes caught the truth burning behind those crimson irises.

Rage. Anger. Hatred.

The robes were ridiculous. Long and flowing in that theatrical villain way, dark black fabric with red-gold threaded designs.

The man also had the most ridiculous amount of jewelry and ornaments he had ever seen on someone.

All of them couldn’t be artifacts since there was a limit on how many one could keep without them interfering with each other, but he was damn sure each and every piece was enchanted for sure.

The man's aura once again pressed against Jin's like a mountain leaning into a knife's edge.

Angel.

« Already working on it. He has formidable mental defenses. I can break through given time, but remember… this is a dedicated mind mage. Expect psychic attacks integrated into every other action. »

Let’s go meet them.

Jin stepped forward into the hall, and one by one, flames ignited along the wall-mounted torches.

Only then did Jin's conscious mind register that he'd been seeing the cultist through his mystic eyes in total darkness this entire time.

That was not good; he had given away that he could see in the dark just fine to the enemy.

But maybe—

Maybe I can spin this.

A part of Jin’s mind raced through various possibilities while the other stayed fixed on the cultist.

Let the mask "slip off" during the fight. Act like I can't see in the dark without it. If he tries darkness-based attacks…

"Welcome!" the man said, his voice cutting through the hall with surprising clarity.

He clapped once.

With a series of grinding metallic groans, blast doors slammed shut behind him.

Trapped.

"Welcome, usurper!" The man said, his voice echoing in the halls as he remained standing in the same spot, his arms now clasped behind his back. "Welcome! I've been waiting ever since you used that magnificent skill on us."

Jin stayed silent and directed his willpower toward his mind star.

He remembered that this was a mind mage and no doubt he would try to worm his way into his mind at some point in the battle, and so his hold over the sovereign’s indifference as a shield was a must.

"Oh? Not much of a talker, are you?" The cultist tilted his head with exaggerated curiosity. "Such a pity. I do so enjoy conversation before the screaming begins."

"Listen well, O Masked usurper—a puny mortal who dared disturb our divine mission." The man's tone shifted.

Jin felt the change immediately.

The cultist's expression morphed from blank to something approaching pity mixed with absolute superiority.

Which he was sure came from when the man’s aura brushed against his. No doubt finding out that Jin was only an Order III, and a newly ascended one at that.

The cultist's lips curved into a smile.

"By the decree of Hierarch Malachar, his excellency, I am the one who proclaims the Darkened One's commands to the unenlightened masses."

A halo of slow-circling glyphs—made of blood and darkness—formed behind the cultist’s head like a mockery of divine blessings.

The man spread his arms wide and levitated, rising several inches off the stone floor with a casual ease.

"I am Priest Kiyon," the cultist declared. "Remember this moment well, usurper. This will be the last thing you experience before your mind becomes mine to reshape."

In response, Jin only raised Iron Howl at the cultist.

Priest Kiyon's smile widened, and the expression never reached those glowing crimson eyes.

"You killed my people," Kiyon continued, his voice dropping to a murmur, and like a whip, the words sliced against Jin's mind. "You killed Orodain and Mareth... both of them had the potential to ascend to lordhood within the next few decades, and you... you..."

The pressure intensified with each word.

"...killed them." The priest said. "They could have elevated my standing in the eyes of my lord. Increased my authority. Advanced my position within the hierarchy."

Kiyon's expression shifted to something that might have been genuine sorrow if it wasn't so obviously performative.

"But no matter. These setbacks are temporary. You... you will replace what was lost. Your mind will be broken and remade. Your skills harvested. Your potential redirected toward proper—"

BANG.

Jin squeezed the trigger.

The first round was simple kinetic force wrapped in a basic targeting sorcery through Spellshot Synthesis.

It was nothing fancy, just a test of the priest's reflexes and defensive priorities.

The bullet screamed down the corridor and impacted approximately one foot away from Kiyon's actual body, stopped cold by an invisible barrier that flared briefly into visibility on contact.

Shields and multilayered ones at that.

"Impatience!" Kiyon snarled, his composure cracking slightly. "Utter disregard for your superiors! You will learn respect before I hollow out your skull."

He raised one hand in a sharp gesture.

Shadows boiled up from the floor beneath his feet, pooling like liquid darkness before taking shape.

BANG. BANG. BANG.

Jin fired three more rounds in quick succession.

The priest ignored the incoming fire and continued his work, pulling more shadows upward with deliberate theatrical movements.

All three bullets struck the barrier at slightly different angles and shattered against the shields.

Feedback from Iron Howl came back muted.

«An estimated forty to fifty percent damage negation across all attack vectors just from the passive rank bonus. »

« This number would only increase further if he actively reinforced his aura and defenses. You'll need leverage to punch through that kind of defense. »

Right.

Jin adjusted his expectations by two notches.

"Allow me to demonstrate proper technique," Kiyon murmured, as he swept his raised hand sideways in a theatrical arc.

The pooling shadows beneath him separated into twin orbs that elongated and spun before launching toward Jin as streams of a dozen razor-edged blades.

Jin stepped into motion as the first wave closed the distance.

Zephyr Striders flared, lifting him an inch off the ground as he blurred forward.

He swiped Essence's Edge through the air, relying on its essence manipulation boost and conjuring multiple square barriers made from his [Essence Armament] skill and positioning them directly in his path.

These constructs were formed from a sliver of his essence, and he wasn't expecting them to actually stop the incoming attacks.

They were sacrificial. Designed solely to collide with Kiyon's blades and provide kinetic charges for his [Thermokinetic Engine] to drink.

He also reached inward and activated [Entropy Zone] for the first time since harvesting it from Vellakin, directing Angel to assign an echo to moderate the skill's output.

The flimsy constructs he'd sent forward collided with the first wave of shadow blades and shattered exactly as expected, obliterated by superior force.

"Tch." Jin made sure to audibly click his tongue—a small sound of apparent frustration—as he surged his aura outward and wove it tightly around his body in additional defensive layers.

He dropped low and slid under the leading edge of the blade storm, letting the glide effect of his boots carry him forward.

For the attacks he couldn't dodge cleanly, he poured more essence into proper Armament barriers and used them to deflect rather than block, redirecting the blades' trajectories away from vital areas.

The feedback from the impacts rattled his teeth, but the Engine opened its maws wide and drank, which he redistributed all over his body.

His body burned as he came up from the slide with Iron Howl already leveled at Kiyon's position.

Two more quick, dense essence shots to test patterns and preferences.

BANG. BANG.

One aimed at the center mass. One offset slightly to the left, forcing a choice.

Kiyon shifted right to avoid the second shot, his levitation carrying him laterally.

« Bias noted »

"You are... interesting," Kiyon said, his lips curling into an expression that wasn't quite amusement. "Most mortals are screaming by now. Begging for mercy, they know won't come. You are quiet. Cold."

His crimson eyes narrowed, and a dark violet glow started intensifying in them, matching the halo behind his head.

"Let us see what makes you that way and discover what allows you to stand before divinity without breaking."

The priest raised his other hand, and the air thickened. The darkness under Jin’s boots turned viscous, tugging at his ankles, trying to glue him to the floor.

« You can brute-force through with the Engine’s support. »

Increase the regulator.

REGULATOR: 8% → 25%

Jin felt the engine burn hotter and diverted more of its output through his body.

He ripped his feet free of the grasping shadows with raw force.

Kiyon watched him break free, head tilted slightly in that exaggeratedly curious pose.

"You move well. For your rank. With some very interesting skill choices."

A prickling sensation ran along the back of Jin's skull, which he realized was a warning that came half a second too late.

Jin's awareness tried to tilt sideways, the corridor warping at the edges of his perception as an alien presence stabbed directly into the inner sky of his mind like a needle piercing fabric.

His world narrowed for an instant, focus forced inward as hostile intent crashed against his mental sphere.

He staggered back, and his mind star unleashed a furious roar.

His mantle surged, and silver chains the size of buildings rose in his mind, coiling around the intruding intent.

The Eternal Sovereign’s star pulsed once, cold and absolute, and the psychic spear’s structure began to unravel.

Jin seized those threads and shredded them, pulling the attack apart.

« No admittance,» Angel's voice echoed through the shared mental space.

The psychic spear snapped, and the Mantle of Harvest's chains didn't stop at defense.

They pulled, following the broken connection back toward its source like a predator tracking wounded prey.

"Harvest," Jin said softly. He had a weak tether to the man’s soul now, but he held himself from doing a proper harvest. He needed the cultist at his lowest.

Out in the physical corridor, Priest Kiyon's eyes widened with genuine shock.

He jerked as if physically struck, his head snapping backward hard enough that Jin heard vertebrae crack. One hand flew to his temple, fingers digging into his own skull as psychic backlash burned through him.

There.

Jin didn't waste the opening.

«O fire that devours the unwary... let your hunger consume what stands before me.»

He pulled the trigger.

BANG.

Iron Howl roared.

The flaming bullet left the barrel with a thundering hiss, its cruel flames licking the air.

It hit the remnants of Kiyon’s conjured darkness wall, punched through, and slammed into the priest’s shields, detonating in a wide, flaming explosion.

He absorbed more from the impacts and used it to propel himself forward, sending cracks through the floor in the process by using it as a launching pad.

BANG. BANG. BANG.

He kept firing—Iron Howl kicking like a cannon in his hands with each shot—trying to establish tempo and overwhelm the priest's shield before Kiyon could recover from the psychic backlash.

Unfortunately, the hall was way too big for creating a bullet storm.

No matter.

BANG. BANG. BANG.

The bullets, each with an essence-stealing sorcery imbued in them, landed on the shields.

The flames roared, and so did Priest Kiyon. His aura surged and manifested into white flames around him that devoured all the red flames and his bullets.

Lord flames…

But he was too slow.

Ordnance: Thundergrave Penetrator.

All of them except his [Helldiver’s Mark] shield, which flared just in time to catch the attack, just as it hit him square in the chest.

Tick. Tick. Tick.

"NO!—You... ARRRHGH—"
