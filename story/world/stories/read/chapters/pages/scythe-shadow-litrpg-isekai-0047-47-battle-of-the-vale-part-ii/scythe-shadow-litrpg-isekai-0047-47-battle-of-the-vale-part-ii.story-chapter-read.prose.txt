The eagle Sin's carcass unravelled beneath me as I fell from the sky with it.

I'd hoped to somehow commandeer the bird's wings to glide down to a gentle landing. A fool's hope that quickly dwindled into an impossibility. Feathers flicked off in bunches. Skin and cartilage shriveled until there was nothing left but decaying bones.

The Sin's Emerald Will flung out from within the rib cage and disappeared behind me. I Aero Shifted back, scrambling my arms through the air as if I could swim after the treasure, but is was gone. Nothing more than a green speck in the distance. My Volition was too low for a Shift that far.

Air continued to rush by me and I plummeted, nearing terminal velocity. I couldn't help but picture Avery's face as the ground loomed larger and larger below.

She'd talked about booking a skydiving adventure on our honeymoon. I'd rejected it, surprised the idea even sparked her interest. It just didn't seem like her. We'd booked a reef snorkeling event instead.

I understood the appeal now, even as my potential doom approached. Adrenaline pumping through my veins, the sensation of utter freedom.

I imagined her falling with me, hair flowing behind her, parachute pack mounted other back, ready to catch and guide the fall into a gently landing. I reached out and grabbed her hands, holding them tight. Wishing her imaginary parachute could carry us both.

But I had no such safety line.

I could make out individual cobblestones in the street below at this point. The muscles throughout my body tensed involuntarily, bracing for impact. My Volition blinked at the top of my vision. Enough left for one last miracle.

Every time I'd used Aero Shift it propelled my momentum through the action with me. When I Aero Shifted with my horse during the battle at the Meridian we'd continued right on as if nothing happened—until we got caught by the Reaper's chain skill, of course. But that meant if I Aero Shifted to the ground now, at this speed I'd just smash into it and explode like a stolen pumpkin on Halloween.

But I could change direction.

At the last possible second, I Aero Shifted horizontal, avoiding the impending collision, instead launching myself parallel to the ground. My legs pinwheeled beneath me, trying to transition into a smooth run, but I was traveling far too fast. I protected my head with my arms as I lost control. My body tumbled like a rag doll, head over heels, face into stone. I broke through a wooden fence, destroyed several bushes, a bench, and a lamp post. Eventually, I crashed to a halt against a half-standing wall.

Bruises ached, scrapes and scratches bled profusely. My left shoulder was dislocated and one of my knees shattered. Health down to 20%.

But I was alive…

I stood, then immediately collapsed, the pain unmanageable. If an enemy Reaper of Sin found me in this state, I was as good as dead, no matter what rank they were. Luckily, I had a few items that could help now.

I summoned the stethoscope I'd earned while hunting Reapers on my return trip from the tunnel.

[Stethoscope

Effect: Heals three major injuries]

I used it to repair my knee, shoulder, and likely concussion. A few of the Health recovery items I'd procured took care fo the rest and I was good as new. Of course, now my item supply was dwindling. Any damage from the fights to come would have to be healed another way.

My surroundings were a mess. This area of the city had been destroyed too. The vast majority of residents in Meadowvale were Penumbrans unable to effectively defend themselves against Sins, let alone Unchained on a rampage, just like in the outlying villages. Sure, there was the city guard, like Eugene, but they dealt more with petty crime, maintaining order, and alerting Reapers for bigger issues. A spear would do little against a ball of fire or rock spike. And the Reapers that were here were mostly Amber.

I could see areas here, deeper into the city, where guards and Reapers had taken a stand. Groups of bodies in disjointed formations lay in the streets. Dormant Wills of defeated Sins and Reapers were scattered among them. Apparently, no one had time to harvest them in the heat of battle.

With my Second Sight, I could easily tell which were Sins and which were not. I harvested all of them, leaving the Reaper ones—I didn't know what would happen if I tried to relinquish the Will of a human, but I didn't want to find out. The idea felt cannibalistic.

My mind flashed back to the horror of watching that Reaper harvest the Will of that Reaper at the city gate. That would never be me. No matter the situation. No matter how desperate. These were real people, with real lives back in Vitria like me. Family, friends, aspirations. A line had to be drawn somewhere and this was it.

I scoured the abandoned streets, a veritable graveyard from the battles than ensued, finding more and more Wills from defeated Sins. Most were Amber—two Amethyst though, and I harvested them all. No Emeralds to make up for the one I'd lost, unfortunately.

From the black spotlight rising into the sky nearby, I could tell a pillar was only a few streets over. I approached cautiously with the rainbow of Wills trapped in my scythe. Nine total, one of which was a Death Affinity and two Astral. Not a bad haul for minimal work. Though, the work had been escaping that fucking eagle. And the price would be coming soon.

I looked up at the tower still standing. My heart told me to go to the colosseum to help with anyone still holding out a defense, but my instincts said there was something important about this structure. It'd be my next destination.

First, I needed to relinquish these Wills. To my surprise, the pillar was undefended. I entered the circle of frost and slid my scythe into the stone.

Within the starry void of the pillar I cast up the variety of Wills I'd harvested, enhancing nearly all my stats, including a large increase in my Volition. The first of the two Astral Affinity Wills earned me an augmentation to Aero Shift that allowed me to have finer control over whether I brought an object with me into the Shift or not. In that first Arena battle against Kaiju, I'd grabbed his head and bashed it against the stone using Aero Shift because it wasn't Shifting with me, but when I upgraded previously I'd overwritten that capability with the conflicting ability to carry objects with me in the Shift, which helped me bring my horse past the line of Reapers at the Meridian. This new option gave me the control to do both at my choosing.

With the second Astral Affinity Will I selected a new skill. I got lucky that it was Amethyst rank, which gave me access to several new options. Throughout my fights so far, I noticed one crucial thing that constantly put me at a disadvantage—I lacked firepower. Hopefully this will solve that.

[Affinity: Astral

Foundational Skill: Prismatic Bow

Skill Type: Summon

Effect: Summons a bow that can launch starlight arrows at foes from a distance

Status: Available.]

The Death Affinity Will was a little more of a difficult choice. With how rare they were, I didn't want to make a choice I'd regret. I still didn't have access to the Volition Drain skill, but I could get the Decay skill to debuff my opponents stats. Or would it be better to just focus on enhancing Life Drain?

Why can't I test drive some of these? That'd make this so much easier.

I focused on the different stars twinkling in the dark sky, idly scanning through my options, annoyed at having to make the decision with so little knowledge of the skills. One caught my eye. I'd really just been focusing on what I could actual acquire to this point, so I'd never looked at them all since I couldn't see the effects until I had access anyway.

[Affinity: Death

Skill: Threads of Fate

Skill Type: Unique

Effect: Rank Locked

Status: Rank Locked.]

I'd never seen anything else described as a 'Unique Skill.' I made a mental note to check back on this one every time I ranked up.

In the end, I decided that powering up my primary skills would be better than having a bunch of weaker ones. Jack off all trades, master of none and all that. I focused on the Life Drain augmentation.

[Affinity: Death

Skill Upgrade: Life Drain

Skill Type: Drain

Effect: Steals Health from target and replenishes own by 20% (+10%) of amount stolen (at a 10% discount to Volition)

Status: Available.]

Nothing wrong with more for less. It would at least help my Volition consumption problems a little.

I closed my eyes and felt the platform fall away beneath me.

I returned to Penumbra slightly dizzy, still adjusting to the enhancements the stat boosts provided my body. This was the largest lump of improvements I'd had in one go and I could sense the difference. Aside from the first Will I relinquished, the changes had felt gradual, barely noticeable, like learning a language—you don't really feel like you're making progress until you look back and compare to where you started.

But this time… This time it was obvious, like when I'd used the Syringe item to boost my stats against Zeus.

My muscles tingled, eager to test their new strength. I wiggled my fingers, watching them react to my instructions almost before I thought them, moving as if I'd spent a lifetime as a master pianist.

So when I felt the prick of a scythe's blade against my back, my body reacted before my mind could even register what was happening.

I spun and grabbed the shaft of the weapon in one fluid motion, wrenching it from the Reaper's hands and threw it away.

Three others stood around me, frost growing on their cloaks, fog clouding from their breaths. Two Amethysts and two Ambers total.

The first—one of the Amethysts—called back his scythe. It whirled into his hand. "Death Reaper… Pirhani wants to speak to you."

"Well that's convenient," I said. "I wanna speak to her too."

And with that, I called forth my new and improved Life Drain.
