I mean, all of a culture is part of its history, but I'm talking about challenges overcome, triumphs achieved, the kind of stuff that when you learn about it you get a sense of pride in your people. I heard that bit about 'if you're studying history and feel pride, you're probably not studying history', but frankly, I think that's missing an important phrase that turns it from important cautionary statement to purest bullshit.

That being 'nothing but'. If you feel nothing but pride? You're not looking at history, you're looking at propaganda. But any group of people is gonna have things one or more of their people did that they can feel proud of. If the people has been around long enough, there's probably gonna be some things they're ashamed of. Given that people are people, there are gonna be some things that fill them with deep and abiding sorrow. Sometimes the shame and sorrow come from the same piece of history. Fuck, sometimes the pride and sorrow do, too.

Then the liberation of the camps, where Eisenhower insisted they be photographed, that the politicians who considered hitching the American star to the Nazi swastika look at what happened. Shame, pride, sorrow, all from the same war.

Yeah, anyhow, every groups gonna have moments of legit pride. They're also gonna have cultural traumas. I think I am in a unique position... Nah, nah, I'm really not in a unique position to realize that 'trauma' is not something you want to base your identity on. Yeah, it has an effect. Yeah, there's even gonna be occasional pride in surviving that trauma. But when it goes past that it can wind up twisted. Just like with a person, trauma is shit you need to heal. Not just survive. Because if you're constantly reminding yourself of trauma, basing your entire fuckin' identity on it, you're just retraumatizing yourself over and over and over again. I dunno, but I'm pretty sure that goes for cultures as well as individuals.

Before I'm misunderstood, that's for after the trauma is over and done and in the past. Not while that shit is ongoing. Not while Malcom X's knife is still in your collective backs, and not while the dude who stabbed you is standing there with the bloody knife in his hand, wanting props for pulling it out, threatening to stab you again if you don't thank him for his 'kind' act.
