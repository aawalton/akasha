Seriously, I still don't have true multitasking going on, which means I need to trust my subordinates. I guess maybe that means at some point, once everything we've set out to deal with is dealt with, I'll be able to retire. Because I won't stop being a Goddess, but I might be able to step back from being ready to intervene on a moment to moment basis.

Just gave myself a bit of a chill below the waist, in the weirdest good way I've had in a while.

At any rate, spent yesterday teaching Math and playing stenographer for the negotiations. Okay, that's not quite right. Saffron does specifically ask me to weigh in on things that have either taken a long time to hammer out or that everyone's agreed on almost immediately. Funny, but Redbeard actually called her on that, or rather on my silence.

Okay, what he said when I nodded and said something like 'Sounds good' was, "I find it odd you haven't been more active in our negotiations."

I thought about it for a second, then decided just to be honest. "Yeah, I'm a Goddess."

He pursed his lips for a moment. "This does not explain your detachment from the proceedings."

"I thought you'd prefer that?"

He shrugged at that. "I have concerns."

"About?"

"You saying later that you are not bound by these agreements because you didn't fully participate in making them."

My temper flared a little at that, but I kept it in enough that nobody noticed. Nobody flinched at any rate. When I had my shit together, I sighed and said, "nah, I'm not gonna do you like that. I make an agreement, I stick to it, see it through, even if I don't like it after. I mean, so long as everybody else holds up their end, or didn't pull some shady shit while we were bargaining."

"Such as?"

I shrugged. "If you guys wanted payment in advance, and we gave it to you, and then you guys had new elections for Captain aboard your ships, and said the new Captains weren't bound by the agreement, I might get a little annoyed."

"How annoyed?"

I thought about that, my head tilting. "Hold your ships still while I take the payment back, then maybe slap each of you and the new Captains upside the head for pulling a fast one annoyed."

John snorted. "My wife has done worse to our children."

That set Red off a little. "So you'd treat us like children?"

"I mean, in terms of relative power level, you are." I nodded to Saffron and Tallulah. "I love these two, but even there I could literally pick them up and send them home if I had to." Before he could respond, I decided that made my point. "I think I said this before, but in case I didn't that's kinda why I'm holding back in the negotiations. I'm gonna try to keep things moving forward if I need to, and Saffron's getting me to okay anything she thinks I need to, but I've got ultimate veto power if I need to use it."

"So why are we negotiating with them instead of with you?"

I snorted. "Because you guys might each be smarter than me, and you're definitely more well informed about the details of the situation." While he was still blinking at me calling him smarter than me, I added, "every smart leader I've met has known how to find experts, listen to them, and even put them in charge of their area of expertise. In this case," I nodded to Saffron and Tallulah, "diplomacy," I shifted to my left and nodded to Tallulah and Swanson, "naval and maritime shit," then waved a hand at the Captains, "which you guys know as well. You're also way more knowledgeable about what your people will accept. Which brings up the question of whether you're gonna put these contracts to a vote?"

"With our crews?" asked Anne. I nodded, and she turned to the others. "I will, and it might take some convincing, possibly even amendments to our Articles, but I'm confident they'll all agree."

Red snorted. "My men will agree, because their Articles say they will." When the other two looked at him, he shrugged and said, "I have authority to negotiate with other ships and ports outside the Thames." He nodded to Swanson. "He's a ship captain."

Swanson just chuckled. "Actually no. These three are, though."

When the Pirate Captains looked a little aghast, Saffron cleared her throat. "He's right. He's an Admiral. His proper focus is the fleet itself. Tallulah and I each Captain one of the Tentacles, although my First Mate and Executive Officer do most of the work, make most of the decisions."

Yeah, my Kitten didn't take that lying down. "We take no pay as Captains. It is a holy calling for us. But our crews are well paid, as are our officers."
