A devilish smile slit across Zeus' lips and he squeezed my shoulder tighter until his fingertips pressed into bone.

“I’m so sorry you had to face a scythe in the arena,” Sibyl said, oblivious to the pain coursing through my arm. “It doesn’t happen often. One person died a few months back though.”

I cringed. So Valciferous wasn't exaggerating as part of his sales pitch.

“Oh, you didn’t know?” Zeus peeled his hand away from my shoulder and put it to his mouth in feigned surprise. The skin still burned where he’d touched, even through my cloak and shirt. “Still as clueless as ever, I see."

"Actually, I do know, Zeus. Nice of you to jump to conclusions though."

“Was it scary?” Sibyl asked, clearly not grasping the tense subtext of the conversation. She’d dropped the card and held her hands clutched together by her chin.

Zeus slapped my back, sending a subtle jolt of electricity through it.

“Of course not!” he announced. “Nothing scares, the great Death Reaper. Ain’t that right?” He called forth his scythe and held it up near my chest. “Not even a little scythe.”

Sweat formed in my armpits.

He’s toying with you. He’s not a real threat.

Zeus wouldn’t do anything with all these people about. Right? He wasn't crazy. Right?

Sibyl looked around, not sure what to do either. A few others had stopped to stare at the scene.

Eugene cleared his throat and stepped forward, knuckles white on his spear. “This is a non-combat area. Please put away your weapon and leave the premises.”

The scythe retracted and Zeus grinned, raising his hands in the air. “I’m joking of course! Just a little fun between old friends, ain’t that right, Zephyr?”

I didn’t respond.

“What? Still bitter?” Zeus ran fingers through his curly golden hair.

"I see you're still an Amber," I countered. "Thought you'd be halfway to the Spire by now."

His face scrunched angrily. "Everyone knows you need to get stronger before you advance ranks to maximize your potential."

I cupped my hand around my ear. "What's that? I can't hear you over the sound of excuses."

His expression darkened. “You don't want to make an enemy of me."

"You already did that for yourself. Go cuddle with knives." It probably wasn't the smartest idea to antagonize him, but it was oh so satisfying.

Surprisingly, Zeus smiled wide. "Ah, well. I tried to be courteous. Thought we could be friends, you know? It's not to be, I suppose. I’ll be seeing you, I’m sure.”

He waved, little sparks dancing between his fingertips.

“Well, he’s just delightful, isn’t he?” Sibyl said after he disappeared out the colosseum arches.

He's a problem, is what he is. Unpredictable and explosive. Not a good combination.

"Anyway… I have your other rewards ready. Because it was an intro brawl the rewards aren't that good. If you register for the real circuit you can make much more.” She turned a key in a cabinet below the counter and pulled out a small bag of coins and a rolled piece of paper sealed with wax. “Here you are. 10 dien and one free item.”

I knew 10 dien was basically nothing. Even the meals at the tavern cost more than that. The armor sets I'd seen out in the market cost well over 100. But the item could be interesting.

[Notice of Resignation

Effect: Unknown]

"Thanks. Do you know where I can find an appraiser? It says the effect is unknown."

Sibyl picked it up and examined it. "Whoops! My bad. I totally forgot to do that. Luckily I have the Appraisal skill too."

"It's okay, you were a little busy before I got back." I winked at Eugene. Both their cheeks glowed red.

She put a hand over the paper, a light glow emanated from underneath. "All set!"

I re-examined the item.

[Notice of Resignation

Effect: Instantly teleports the user to the nearest lantern]

That seemed useful, especially if I found myself in an unfavorable battle.

“I can appraise any other items you have too,” Sibyl said.

“That’d be helpful.” I brought forth the Silver Engagement Ring and 5cc Syringe.

Sibyl repeated the appraisal process and slid them back to me.

[Silver Engagement Ring

Effect: Creates a small, impenetrable shield that will protect user and surrounding area for 12 seconds]

[Syringe - 5cc

Effect: Small boost to Strength and Stamina for 30 seconds]

These both seemed great too, especially the Engagement Ring. I could see how these helped a Reaper hit above their rank, but could also become overwhelming if I had too many at once trying to remember each effect and summon the correct item for the situation in the heat of battle. If I got a new item from each Sin killed, that would be a lot.

"Do you mind showing me how to use these?" I asked. "Jericho sure as hell isn't gonna teach me."

“The Jericho?” Sibyl asked, eyes wide. “Isn’t he the best?”

I groaned. “Why does everyone keep asking me that..?”

She shrugged. "He's kind of a minor celebrity in the Meadow. You don't find many mid ranks sticking around these parts unless they're visiting the city for some reason or another."

Huh. I never considered that. It was odd that Jericho travelled out and about in the Meadow zone where all the Sins were far below his rank.

"But sure, I'll teach you. There are various activation methods unique to different types of items. Some are fairly obvious. For example, you put on rings. You stick needles into yourself or others and inject the contents, balls can typically be thrown as explosives that detonate on impact, and so on."

I picked up the ring and held it over my left ring finger where my wedding ring would eventually be.

Assuming that would still happen.

The thought sobered my mood.

Of course it would still happen. Being a Death Reaper—the Death Reaper—wouldn't change that.

Then hands clutched onto my shoulders from behind.

I flinched.

Not again…

My scythe began crawling down my arm and forming in my hand.

“I knew you could do it!” Calliope shouted from on my back.

I hear the familiar chirp of Nuzzle as his feet pattered onto my shoulder.

I relaxed, allowing my weapon to dissolve back into place.

Nuzzle snuggled up against my neck, squishing his cheek against mine. I rubbed a finger affectionately under his chin.

Calliope released me and turned my body around to face her and Jericho. They were both smiling wide.

I returned the gesture. "How'd you know I was in the arena for the battle?"

"We were waiting for you outside the colosseum for a while," Jericho said. "Eventually, we heard the announcer introduce you from out there." He turned to my new acquaintances. "Hi Eugene. Sibyl."

They waved in reply.

"I saw you enjoying the new skill I helped you acquire," Calliope said. "You're welcome!"

"Yeah. Thanks for that."

"A Death Affinity." Jericho shook his head and placed a hand on my shoulder. "All this time I've been carting around a celebrity. Nay! Mentoring a celebrity! I had no idea. I didn't even think to ask about your Affinities."

"Well, it was probably one of the questions to ask on your stupid notecards."

"I'll have you know those notecards have come in very handy! Ask any new Amber I've assisted."

"Hey Calliope? Are the notecards handy?"

"I have literally no idea what you guys are talking about. Notecards? I don't—Oh… OH! Are those what I…?"

"Yeah, never heard of those. Sounds useless though."

"Have it your way," Jericho said. "See if I help either of you ever again."

"Well, I wanted to cover up my five measly chest hairs, remember?"

"Not the shirt, dumbass." She grabbed the cloak hanging from my back and pulled it around the front so I could see. "The cloak!"

I hadn't even noticed. Valciferous. That fucking assgod.

Adorned on the back of my cloak, taking up nearly the entire length of the fabric, were a pair of golden crown-like antlers. A veritable blinking arrow saying 'Hey! I'm with the faceless god thing!'

Jericho gasped audibly. "You swore an oath to a god?"

"He promised to return me to Vitria permanently if I played Death Reaper. He's got me started out on a few small issues."

That's fair, but… "Ah, shit." What else didn't he tell me? Was I being played here?

Oh no… Oh, fucking no. You didn't…

My shadow… My one portal home.

Was gone.
