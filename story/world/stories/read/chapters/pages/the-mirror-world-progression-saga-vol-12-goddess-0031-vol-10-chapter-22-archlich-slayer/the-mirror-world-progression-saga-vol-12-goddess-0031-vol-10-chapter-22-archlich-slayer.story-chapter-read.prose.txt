The sarcophagus took five annihilation blasts to actually disintegrate it. On the fourth, it broke apart, and Samantha felt the mystical alignment of the place shift even more extremely than it had when the lich had perished. Much as Lovie suggested, she still felt the vast power above, rippling with the effects but far from cleansed. It was a well thousands of years in filling, and it would only drain over centuries, at best. She’d simply removed the focus, effectively the ‘proper routing’ that kept filling the well — along with the significant source that was the Consolidator.

With little else to do, they left. Samantha dematerialized and shifted over to Itha Ranon, and Azure soon followed her. Once the head had been transported out, the quest was completed.

The quest ‘Unknown 01’ is complete. 12(9) exp gained in [Goddess], 20(10) exp gained in [Magineer]. 33 Maximum MP gained. [Arcane Breach] class skill upgraded to Grand Master. [Slayer] upgraded.

Level 11 Goddess Obtained. +20,000 Maximum FE added.

The Special Trait [Oppression of Divinity] added. [Inspire Ally] consolidated into [Assurance] as an Enhancement and improved.

Assurance, Dazzling Spectrum, Faith Energy Utilization, Grant Prayer (Illusion, Fate, Light, Pneuma, Magic), Mirror Sense, Mirror Walk, Sheath of Light, and The Unseen Animus upgraded.

Standing on the steps up to the tower, Samantha paused as the level up came in, smiling grandly.

Azure turned and shared in the glee. She laughed and flung her hand. “Well? Go on! Don’t let me hold you up. Review, darling, review!”

Samantha returned the laugh. “I feel like I’m usually on my throne for this. Here I am, leveling up when I’m just walking into the next fucking thing.”

“Yeah, and you don’t even need to walk!”

Samantha laughed again. “How primitive! Remind me not to be seen doing such peasant things in the future.”

Azure smirked and quite suddenly scooped her up in a princess carry, as Samantha squawked and giggled in response. Azure declared dramatically, “I shan’t allow Her Majesty to shame her heavenly soles with this dirty world again! Oh, never again, I swear on my honor! Allow me to be your throne, oh Queen of Grace!”

Laughing, shaking her head — and rolling her eyes for good measure — Samantha allowed herself to be indulged as she reviewed her various level upgrades.

Well, first she wanted to see the glory of a ‘complete’ Arcane Breach skill, something she’d developed progressively through various schemes and accomplishments, crowned with the achievement of defeating the Consolidator. She must’ve been constantly breaching the lich’s Spell Resistance to gain it from the quest.

Arcane Breach (Grand Master)

Cancels out up to 5 Effective Levels of arcane Magical Resistance OR any form of (Warding) against your arcane spells. If both persist on a target and are relevant to your effect, Arcane Breach will reduce the highest of the two.

Look at that glorious beauty of a max skill. The promised +5! Mmn-mm-mm. It takes my very breath away! Anyway, on to my other ‘prize’ from the ordeal…

Archlich Slayer (The Consolidator): You’ve defeated a legendary, level 20 undead spellcaster in direct, convincing fashion. Other liches and high-level intelligent undead may sense this in your aura and think twice about tangling with you, if they can avoid it. To a lesser degree, this can impress spellcasters. Double the potential intimidation/awe bonus if at all susceptible.

This does not apply to upgraded Minor Wounds.

Against these same types of foes (regardless of level), your Effective Caster Level for dispels, protection breaching type spells or effects, and direct countermagic effects are also at +2 Effective Levels.

“Sweet!” Samantha exclaimed in glee. “Archlich Slayer! Puts me closer to dealing with the strongest Dominion creeps, I think. I’m punching higher and higher, babe!”

Azure, still walking up the steps with Samantha in her arms, nodded along sagely to this. “I got something for helping, even. Congrats on taking down your most powerful foe yet. I think?”

“Yeah, probably. I only kinda defeated Endless Templar, and she was probably holding back.”

“Mmn, I dunno about that. She was also in her element…”

“No need to defend my honor when you’re already carrying me.” Samantha kicked her feet and curled her arms inward as she smiled, all in deliberately excessive cuteness. “Don’t overdo it, love!”

“You, lecturing me, on overdoing things!?” Azure scoffed.

Smiling shamelessly, Samantha moved on in her review. New ability time!

Oppression of Divinity

(Fate & Magic Synergy) After dealing at least a Minor Wound on any non-deific target, automatically apply the permanent (Curse) type status effect, Oppression. There is no contest to resist.

Oppression

-1 Effective Levels to all forms of defense and resistance, -2 Effective Levels to resist (Fear) and other morale-type effects, and all [Luck], [Fortune], or [Fate] Effective Level bonuses are reduced to 0. This stacks up to two times, doubling the EL penalties.

This can be removed only by the granting of a prayer by you or another deity. In the latter case, they must succeed in a Will contest with you, or it is not removed.

Another harsh curse effect. Opens the door to whittling someone down more easily once getting any decent hit on them.

Next up was the consolidation of [Inspire Ally], which she’d used here and there over time. It was bundled into [Assurance], which also got a slight stat boost, from +1 to +2, on one element.

Assurance

Power (Divine; Aura; Light, Fate, Magic, Abjurer, ‘Major Battle Victory’ Synergy). Your simple presence gives your allies powerful, supernatural faith in victory, and particularly in resisting the enemy.

This affects all within a radius of 5 meters per deity level, centered on you. Add +1 Effective Levels to any Active Defenses, +2 Effective Levels to resist Knockback or any Stunning, Unconsciousness, Knockdown, or Pain effect, and reduce any Wounding effects by -2 Effective Levels. These bonuses do not apply to you.

Inspire Ally (Bard Synergy): You may encourage and bolster any ally that can hear you speak at least one line of inspiration, in whatever form you desire, so long as it has an auditory component. This is not instantaneous but can be combined with other actions if feasible.

This adds 2 Effective Level to the next attack the ally makes, or potentially to another immediately imminent action that is subject to raw motivational inspiration. It cannot aid any form of long-term task nor something that requires calm, mental focus. For 30 seconds (1 minute if a performing Bard), the ally also has advantage on any ties qualifying for the bonus (i.e., turns ties into full success).

The temp bonus weakens by -1 if used within the same hour, on the same person, and disappears if used so twice.

In addition, add +3 Effective Levels to any courage or morale-based level comparisons, such as resisting [Fear] effects. If already under such an effect, the ally may re-compare, potentially breaking its hold.

Samantha grunted. “Huh. So, improved and infinite inspiration, but the sexiest bonus can’t be abused too much on the same person.”

Azure, now near the top of the steps, frowned. “I thought it could be abused.”

“Not my version, anyway. Guess that’s the trade-off for infinite use. Can hit many allies over time. Aside from that, my usage was only twice a day, before, so I technically didn’t lose anything.”

Samantha moved on to [Faith Energy Utilization], which had gained a new enhancement.

Mana Conversion (Magic & Pneuma Synergy): With a minimum of one minute of concentration, you may convert 1000 FE into either 100 temporary MP or 1 permanent MP. You may do this a maximum number of times per day equal to Maximum Faith Energy / 10,000, rounded down (current uses: 14).

Concentration time after the first minute-long conversion is twenty seconds per additional conversion.

“Ooh!” Samantha exclaimed, thrusting up a fist. “Another FE sink! Not gonna say no to that! Not at that time rate, anyway.”
