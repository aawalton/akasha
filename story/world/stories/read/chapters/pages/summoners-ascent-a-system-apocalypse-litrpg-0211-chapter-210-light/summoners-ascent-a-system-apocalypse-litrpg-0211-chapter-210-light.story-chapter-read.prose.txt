The creature in front of Ashton made his skin crawl. All the fear that had been imbued into his very soul by the Summoner was surfacing, and he had no idea how to fight back against it in this moment.

But his body still knew instinctively to do one thing. Despite his hands shaking, and his legs getting weak, he still jumped in front of Oka to shield them. The creature that was looming and staring at them from around the corner was clearly powerful beyond belief, Ashton could tell that much from a glance.

"Th-This is what you ran from?" he asked, and Oka slowly gripped his shaking arm.

"Ye-yes, but also... no..?" Oka whispered. They were scared, but not nearly as terrified as Ashton was. They were used to having numerous strong monsters around them, so to them, this was just another instance of that. Sure, this was stronger than anything they had ever seen, but the part that terrified Oka much more was Ashton's reaction.

They started trying to pull him away, slowly moving backward. "That's not the same one... but it's similar..."

Ashton felt a pit form in his stomach. There were more of these things?

"Fuck, fuck, fuck," Ashton spun his staff around and let his internal energy flow into it. At this point, the creature started to run at him, but Ashton struck at the walls and ceiling in quick succession, causing them to collapse. The moment the rubble started to fall, he turned and pushed Oka forward.

The two ran through the maze-like ruins, trying to get back to the entrance. There was no part of Ashton that wanted to face that creature. Rather, he would do everything in his power to make sure that he wouldn't need to.

There were other things that the Summoner wanted; things that Ashton could leverage for some kind of advantage.

They didn't have to face this creature. It was fine, it was okay. Ashton could get Oka out of here. He could. He had to. He just had to. Ashton ran, and jumped, and even carried Oka for some time.

But then, when Ashton turned around the corner with that long hallway supposed to lead back to that storage room, he found something else. A different, shorter hallway, that turned around a corner. Confused, Ashton ran down the hallway, thinking that maybe he was mistaken, but then he spotted a part of the ruins that he knew for a fact wasn't here. It was physically impossible for him to have reached this place by running in this direction.

"We're stuck in another barrier," Ashton said with a pale face. He spread out his mana, trying to sense what exactly was going on. The mana around him was distorted and changed into a complex pattern with aspects that were telltale signs of barriers.

"Fuuuck! Shit, bloody goddamn fucking pieces of–" Ashton dug his nails into his hair and started walking in circles. He couldn't destroy the barrier from here. Earlier, he was at the edge of the barrier and could directly destroy it, but this time, he was already caught directly within a barrier that distorted physical space.

"Oka, I'm sorry, I–I fucked up! We should have never come here, we have to–" Ashton stared at his spouse, grabbing their arms. He expected them to be angry or disgusted by him—emotions they had literally never shown toward him even once—but instead, they were just looking down at him with a smile.

"It's okay, we will be alright. We always figure out how to get out of trouble somehow, don't we?" Oka pointed out, with a confidence in their face that Ashton couldn't quite pinpoint the source of.

But meanwhile, he was just staring at them. They didn't know. They had no idea that the creature that was surely hunting them within these ruins right now was a being that shouldn't exist in this—or any other—world that Ashton knew of.

At the same time... they were right. Maybe Ashton could figure something out. It didn't seem like that creature was as powerful as the Summoner, or nearly as intelligent. It was more like a beast than anything else.

If that was the case, then maybe the two of them were at least able to avoid it.

"See? You're already thinking of something." Oka placed their hand onto Ashton's cheek. He slowly nodded.

"Yeah, and it's deeply reckless, since I have no idea what this item even is, but I can tell that it's incredibly powerful," Ashton said, staring down the hallway. If it was powering a spatial barrier like this, then it had to be that strong. "If we can take it away and stop it from powering the barrier, I can instead divert the energy to destroy the barrier right away and get us out of here. Probably. Maybe."

Oka nodded. "Perfect."

They turned around, looking down the hallway. "In that case, let's try our best to get to the central chamber with the item."

"Alright," Ashton sighed, still feeling anxious. "But now that we know there's a spatial barrier around, it might be harder to find that place after all."

"That's okay. There's always a way through. The people that built this place would have needed to navigate through here as well, no?"

Ashton thought about it. "Right... in that case, maybe... I can figure out some path if I inspect the mana pattern."

"And meanwhile I will continue guiding us along the hints of a path scattered around."

And just like that, the two of them started running again. This maze, which had seemed relatively easy to traverse just earlier, had now suddenly become a completely jumbled mess. But luckily, it seemed like they weren't the only ones that fell victim to the barrier. Otherwise, the creature would have already caught up to them.

They did encounter it a few times here and there, but by blocking the pathways they ran down, they were able to at least buy some time without having to actually fight that thing.

And then, they reached the 'central chamber'. It was almost identical to what Oka guessed it to be. Well, they had never been wrong about this before, either. It was a truly massive space with numerous magical sigils surrounding a large obelisk in the middle. Ashton could feel immense power streaming from there, and was almost too nervous to approach it.

But right then, behind them, that creature let out a loud screech. It must have followed the trail that the two inadvertently left behind to get here as well. Otherwise, Ashton doubted that this place would be as relatively pristine as the rest of the ruins were.

There were far less traces of destruction down here, though it was still truly dilapidated and fit the word 'ruin' perfectly well.

"It should be right in there," Oka said, looking at the obelisk. Ashton nodded and quickly approached it, letting his energy flow into his staff. With a pointed attack right at the centre of the pitch black obelisk, Ashton was able to make it crack open and reveal part of that which was hidden within it.

And it was something that words could not quite describe. The only thing that Ashton was able to see was the light streaming out through the cracks, and that alone was already overwhelming.

He pushed his fingers into the crack, trying to tear the obelisk open further just to see even a tiny bit more of whatever that thing was behind it. The skin on his fingertips was blistering and peeling off the moment he touched the stone, but he didn't care.

"Ashton? What's going on?"

Only Oka's voice was able to snap him out of it. He turned around, and the moment he looked away from the light, he could feel his mind clear again.

"I-I don't know what's in there, but we need to get away from it," Ashton replied. That massive amount of energy escaping from the obelisk now was already making the barrier crumble into pieces, so they should be able to escape. As long as neither of them looked at that light again, it should be fine.

"Wait, Ashton, your hands!" Oka grabbed his arms, staring down at them. They were bloody messes, dripping down onto the ground. It hurt, but it wasn't a pain that Ashton couldn't deal with. He had experienced worse.

Actually, somehow, in a twisted way... the injuries caused by that light actually felt quite nice.

"It's okay," Ashton replied, slowly standing up. "I'll heal it later, it's fine. For now, let's just–"

Though he said this, it was too late. The creature had already caught up to them. And even if his hands didn't hurt, he wasn't able to fully close them around his staff right now. He couldn't fight, or defend himself and Oka against it in any way.

The only thing there was, was one of the combat-potions that he had brought. Maybe one of them was enough to daze that creature. Ashton pushed his hand into his bag, and shakily pulled out one of the vials. He clutched it, ready to throw it at the creature, but when it actually ran forward... it just ran straight past them.

The only thing reflected in those beads it called eyes was the obelisk and the light shining from within.

The creature jumped at the obelisk, and immediately began to tear into it. Just like with Ashton, its body was being torn apart by the light, but it didn't seem to care. It just seemed to get even more eager the more its body was being destroyed.

It didn't take long until the obelisk was destroyed enough that the creature could crawl inside. This was perfect. Everything was working out! That thing would kill itself, and Ashton and Oka could run away.

But before they could act on that, a crack appeared in space.

Ashton recognised it immediately, and he could feel his heart stop in his chest.

A hand pushed out through the crack, and grabbed the creature by its hind-appendages. The hand pulled the creature out of the obelisk. By now, it was nothing more than a half-molten mess made up of black ooze clinging onto the remains of a frame that terrified Ashton so much just half an hour ago.

But now, there was something even scarier, as the Summoner stepped through the rift they created in the air.
