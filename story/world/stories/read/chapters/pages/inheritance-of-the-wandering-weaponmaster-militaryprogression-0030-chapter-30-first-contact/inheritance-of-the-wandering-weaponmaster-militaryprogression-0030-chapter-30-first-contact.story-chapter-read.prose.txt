Ezra held his shield up high, locked in with Georg on his right and Troy on his left. Georg stood in the center, and his shield was in front of the soldiers on his left and right, Ezra’s edge being behind Georg’s and in front of Troy’s. They practiced this particular formation so that he didn’t struggle to march forward, but his fear spiked as the sounds of battle washed over him and furious, desperate claws and weapons started scraping at his shield.

“Strike!” Georg commanded as his shield lowered just enough to be able to see, and his spear took a goblin in the eye. It squealed and went down, and Georg pulled back as he struck again at the next closest monster. On his left, Troy did the same, though his spear didn’t kill on the first hit. Ezra swallowed down his nerves as a goblin came close enough to be hit, and thrust forward as hard as he could. He’d remembered Petr's instruction, that he needed to make sure to pierce through the twisted creatures’ natural defenses.

His hand slipped up the haft of his spear, he hadn’t held it tight enough for the hit. The point of the spear hit the left breast of the goblin, its tightly corded muscles slicing open while the tip bounced off of a rib and knocked the goblin back.

“Fuck,” Ezra gasped as he readjusted his grip on the spear to make sure he could maintain a safe distance from the enemies. The same goblin he’d already hit recovered and swiped its club at the head of Ezra’s spear. His next attack was knocked wide and the goblin ran forward, leaping at Ezra’s face.

“Only strike when you can kill,“ Georg commanded as his spear took the goblin in the side of the neck. It fell with a gurgle, deep purple blood spraying onto Ezra’s boots. “Look alive!”

“Yes, sir,” Ezra replied as he held his spear at his side and ready to strike. He hadn’t had to think about ensuring it was a deadly blow while practicing. With his heart thundering in his chest and his hands shaking, he prepared for the next monster that pressed forward. This time, it wasn’t just one goblin, it was three that threw themselves at the line as an arrow whipped overhead with so much force that it made the wind whip Ezra‘s armor’s fastening straps. Basil, then, was doing his job.

Georg and Troy‘s spears distracted two of the three goblins while the third held its sword and danced forward only to jump back when Ezra’s spear shot towards it. He pulled his weapon back, just in time as the goblin rushed forward and smashed its sword into his shield. Ezra grunted as the blow forced his shield to the side, and the goblin lunged forward into that tiny gap. It stabbed forward into the gap, trying to catch him as its hands scrabbled between the shields.

As a sword swung madly for his legs, Ezra twisted with his whole body and shoved the edge of his shield forward into Georg’s. Between two shield brims reinforced with metal, the goblin’s arm snapped, and it squealed in pain as it thrashed and pulled back. Its sword, trapped between the shields, raised up as it tried to stab Ezra in the face, but he tightened his grip on his spear even more as he raised his spear high and slammed it into the goblin's open mouth.

The mouth, too wide for a human and made for ripping flesh, was the right size to allow the spear to pierce straight through and out the back of its head. Ezra’s stomach turned as its pained streaks became gurgles and it fell to the ground.

“Good,” Georg barked as his spear shot out on the other side, supporting Amy. Ezra took comfort in that reassurance as another goblin came forward. This one carried a thicker cudgel, fifty centimeters long and ten centimeters thick, and it also carried a primitive shield. It grabbed another one of its companions and shoved it forward towards Ezra, and as he stabbed towards the unarmored one wielding another blade, the one with a shield threw itself towards the soldiers.

As its companion took a spear to the chest, this one cracking bones but not quite killing it, the one with a cudgel hustled forward, set its stance, and slammed the weapon into the bottom of Ezra’s shield. This time, it knocked the shield towards Georg, and Ezra wasn’t ready to hold himself steady.

He stumbled slightly, accidentally opening a gap of maybe twenty centimeters between himself and Troy. The shielded one lunged into that gap, literally jumping forward as Troy and Ezra slammed their shields back together. Then, without hesitating, the goblin turned the weapon onto Ezra. He tried to protect himself, but couldn’t do so without sacrificing the integrity of the shield wall. The club slammed into his elbow and ribs, and Ezra screamed in pain. His arm went mostly numb, and he only barely managed to keep his shield up and the goblin screeched in excitement.

Its cudgel swung again, this time towards Ezra‘s knee, and he had to choose between sacrificing the shield wall and taking the hit. His eyes widened, unsure of what to do, when an arrow suddenly appeared in the goblin‘s eye. It spun and fell, dead, and Ezra looked back to the approaching enemies. His arm screamed in protest, and he pulled some of the aura from his inner world to try to strengthen his arm. It took the worst of the bite of the pain away, but the equipment still shook in his hand as he walked, unsteady.

“That was a rough spot,” Troy said. “If there’s too many of them, you just need to trust in the rest of us. Good job.”

“Advance ten steps,“ Georg commanded with a brief lull in the fighting on their side. Ezra didn’t have enough time to look up and down the line, instead seeing the next squad of goblins approaching. He took three deep breaths to steady himself, reset his grip on the spear, and followed the commands from Georg. Those ten steps brought them closer to the next fight, these ones holding crude spears and not giving the human soldiers any time to rest.

Even before they reached the line with the spears, a hail of stones the size of fists sailed towards the shield wall, and Ezra ducked his head behind the shield. Three struck his shield while the sound of cracking wood and bones filled the air. He heard somebody on his right groan as they were hit, but there were no changing commands afterwards, so he assumed nobody was seriously wounded.

Poking his head over the edge of the shield, Ezra saw that the spear-wielding goblins were only a pace away, and he cursed himself.

Stay focused, Jormun barked. This is the battlefield.

Ezra didn’t say anything, simply holding his weapon at the ready as he was forced to accept the goblin charge. Two spears hit his shield, fortunately, on either side, so they didn’t rip open his grip or guard. Instead, the shield slammed against Ezra‘s shoulder, making his hurt elbow scream in pain. He pushed more of his aura into his elbow, but already he was feeling its effects weaken as his inner world’s reserves were reduced.

One of the goblins stumbled as its spear cracked in its attack, and Ezra stabbed at its eye. Instead of accepting the blow, the creature fell on its back and rolled away, being stepped on two or three times by its companion as it did so. Though he noticed it popping back up, Ezra could only focus on the other goblin that raised its spear and stabbed towards his legs. He lowered his shield just enough to make sure the weapon didn’t catch his thigh, and it slid past him as the goblin got much closer than it anticipated.

Ezra stabbed again, this time catching the goblin in the eye. It didn’t die, though it stumbled back, and his second stab caught it in the sternum. Bone crunched as a spear pierced through the monster's hide and ribcage, and it fell down, twitching.

The battle continued like this, Ezra taking more scrapes as they went. One of the spears caught him in the calf, and every step with his right leg burned though the wound wasn’t too serious. Another stone had sailed high and landed on his right shoulder, the armor there keeping the hit from hurting him too much. Even so, he struggled to strike as accurately now with his neck aching so much. He didn’t fall back though, Georg and Troy both keeping him much safer than he deserved.

Once more, he allowed a goblin to broach his defenses as it ran in on his right. It drew a line of blood from Georg’s left leg before Ezra could strike it with his spear.

“Don’t worry about that,” the lieutenant commanded, “focus on what’s ahead.”

It was just as well that he said that, because that was when the stronger goblinoids, and even the Dragonkin appeared. One orc captain and two bugbears accompanied the next squad, and when two arrows flew towards the orc, it raised a massive shield and caught both. When its face was revealed after it lowered the shield, it grinned widely. Its dark green skin was mostly covered by thick furs, only its legs from its knees down and its arms from the elbows down, uncovered. Even so, both had straps of leather wrapped around them as further armor. Two thick tusks jutted out of its mouth, and it started running forward to break the line. It was well over two meters tall, and carried a metal-banded club which whipped through the air as it approached.

The bugbears both had no armor or clothing on, thick matted fur covering their bodies. Though not quite as tall as the orc, they still towered over even Boyd at about two meters tall, and their arms were so long that they touched their knees. They loped forward with an animalistic gait as the orc raised its shield against two more arrows.

“Helen! Artemis! Fall in! Leah, retreat and support,” Georg commanded as he braced himself to take the orc’s charge. Ezra fought to contain his anxiety as another company of monsters approached, and then he saw the Dragonkin behind them and his heart thrummed in fear.

Though he’d never seen one, it was immediately obvious what the Dragonkin was. Though it was built like a man, nobody could think it was one. It was just as tall as the orc, though where the green-skinned goblinoid was huge and fat, the Dragonkin was broad-shouldered and powerful. Its build communicated power and was covered with thick, armorlike scales. The black scales along its shoulders and back transitioned to a lighter color on its throat and belly, and it carried no weapon. Instead, both hands had claws and embers rose from its snout. Its face was long and lined with massive fangs, and it roared in challenge as it stood there, watching the orc and bugbears begin the attack.

“Hold the line!” Georg commanded. “Chloe will be here soon!”

And until then, Ezra needed to stay alive.
