Arthur’s mana ticked up… and up… and finally hit full.

The moment it did, he funneled it to his battered face. He braced for pain and got something else entirely. Cool, refreshing pressure spread over his gums. A few seconds later… pop.

His missing teeth were back.

“Oh,” Arthur whispered. “Oh, hell yes.”

Teeth. Regrown. Just like that.

Who needed dentists when you had mana?

Mana Heal skill increased by 2 levels.Mana Heal now at Level 3.

Arthur grinned. Now with a full, if slightly crooked smile. “Guess I’m leveling up my face first.”

He checked his mana pool. 80/180. Right. Healing was effective… and mana-expensive. A mental note was filed: no casual faceplants going forward.

He scrolled through the message console to make sure he didn’t miss anything during his head-meets-wall experiment.

One entry made his eyebrows lift.

You’ve acquired a new skill: Mana Body Empower your physical attributes using mana. Enhances Strength, Agility, Dexterity, and Constitution. Effectiveness scales with current stats and mana applied.

Arthur’s eyes sparkled. “Oh, this is the one.”

Finally, some shonen nonsense he could get behind. Magic muscle mode.

He didn’t forget the last time he tried to move fast. His nose had opinions about that. So, unlike his previous trial-by-wall, he decided to practice first. Controlled. Safe. No acrobatics until he could at least trust his balance.

Unfortunately, his stomach had other opinions.

It snarled like it was drafting angry letters to the gods of starvation. Arthur grimaced as his thoughts wandered, uninvited, to the idea of frying up some Nexus Beast steak.

Yeah, no thanks.

That thing had been corrupted by the Corruptor. If it could kill an A-grade, he wasn't about to end up as the punchline of “guy eats magic flesh and dies screaming.”

Moss it is.

He knelt, scraped a handful off the cavern wall, and shoved it into his mouth. It tasted like moss. Because it was moss. Still moss. Always moss.

Drogan wheezed into his thoughts. “What the hell are you chewing on now?”

“Food,” Arthur mumbled, his mouth half-green.

“You’re not a goat, you idiot! That’s moss!”

“It's moss with fiber,” Arthur corrected.

“You’re supposed to be my protégé, not some malnourished lawnmower! I need you on mana-rich meat, not grass clippings!”

Arthur shrugged. “Well, unless this cavern comes with a buffet, I’ll stick with the house salad.”

Drogan cursed in six languages and then sighed. “You’d better kill that monster soon. There’s food outside. Civilization. Real calories.”

“Working on it,” Arthur muttered. “But before I go die, do skill levels actually do anything?”

“YES,” Drogan roared. “Are you even listening when I talk?!”

Arthur remained calm. Poker face. It worked on bureaucrats. It worked on condescending remnants too.

“Leveling up a skill makes you more efficient. Lower mana costs. Better effects. Some even evolve after a threshold.”

Arthur nodded and got back to training.

The next few hours passed with a monotonous loop: Mana Sight, Mana Body, occasionally Mana Heal. Meditation between cycles. Practice, recover, repeat.

Results:

Not bad for a guy who’d been living off glorified wall-scum.

Arthur had learned to extract every drop of mana the moss had to offer. Whatever reconstruction the System had done to his body, it seemed to run more on magic than nutrients anyway. Moss wasn’t much of a meal, but it still offered more mana than just breathing in the ambient air. Barely food, but undeniably fuel.

But his body was the bottleneck now. His stats were too low to fully exploit Mana Body, and he knew it. If he wanted real power, he’d need real food, actual training, and… well, probably not Drogan’s version of leg day, which most likely involved getting kicked into a volcano.

His stomach groaned again - long, loud, and deeply offended.

“I get it,” Arthur muttered. “You hate moss. Noted.”

Eventually, his gains plateaued. He couldn’t get more out of the cave without new inputs or targets. That brought him back to Exhibit A: Chimera - the thing standing between him and the only exit from the cavern.

Drogan’s voice returned. “If I have to watch you slap yourself with mana for one more hour, I swear I’ll evaporate from boredom.”

Arthur stood, brushed moss off his shirt, and made his way to the cavern mouth.

He activated Mana Sight and scanned the outside.

And there it was, lounging behind a boulder like it owned the damn mountain. The chimera monster. Still E-rank. Still ugly. Still digesting his parents, probably.

A wave of cold anger passed through him.

But he forced it down.

Rage was a shortcut to stupid. And stupid got you killed.

Instead, he studied. Watched. Waited.

New skill acquired: Identify Use mana to analyze monsters, artifacts, potions, and magical items. Accuracy increases with Intelligence and skill level.

Arthur focused on the beast.

Chimera Rank: E Classification: Uncommon Status: Resting

Internally, Drogan groaned. “Of course it’s an Uncommon E-rank. Just your luck. You really are cursed.”

Arthur snorted. “You’re helpful.”

“If I hadn’t already seen you get a Legendary Class from nothing, I’d forge your gravestone right now. But if you die, try to make it heroic.”

“Go forth,” Drogan declared solemnly, “and set your heart ablaze!”

Arthur blinked. “That’s dramatic.”

“It’s called inspiration. Now go get your ass kicked.”

Instead of going full kamikaze like Drogan clearly wanted, Arthur picked up a rock, crept out of the crevice, and lobbed it squarely at the chimera’s snoozing head.

It thunked.

The beast growled and stirred.

Fur bristling, fangs gleaming, hate radiating off it like smoke. It saw Arthur.

Arthur waved at it.

The monster charged.

But Arthur wasn’t standing his ground. He bolted for the crevice, the one place the beast couldn’t reach him.

Claws swiped at him and he ducked. Just barely. The monster tried to wedge a paw through the crevice entrance, roaring.

Arthur grabbed the limb and willed.

The storage ring flared with light. The limb vanished. Then the rest of the monster followed, sucked into stasis like a furry screaming jigsaw puzzle.

Gone.

Silence.

“…You didn’t just do that,” Drogan muttered.

Arthur checked the ring mentally. Yep. Chimera: captured.

He slumped to the ground in relief.

“That… was bold,” Drogan said slowly. “I thought you’d get torn in half for sure. Honestly? I’m impressed.”

Arthur blinked. “Wait, was that… respect?”

Drogan huffed. “Don’t make it weird.”

Arthur smiled faintly. “Noted.”

He had caught a monster instead of killing it.

Smart. Safe. Controlled.

And, for the first time since waking up in this world, Arthur finally had the upper hand.

He stood, dusted off his clothes, and looked past the crevice.

There was a whole world out there.

Time to explore it.

Time to grow.

Time to break the game.
