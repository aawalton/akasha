Carian didn’t know what the screech came from, let alone Hayden’s plan, so she was bewildered when her wings returned and stammered, “T-The mountain,” with a pale face.

“Grade?” He laughed. “If that thing’s a colossus, that A6 we felled in Mora was a…” He searched for a good analogy but found none, so he blurted, "Tadpole. A frog. Whatever! It’s the mountain! That purple mountain—that was the colossus. The entire mountain was a fucking colossus!”

“Oh my God…” Carian whispered. “Where’s Hayden?”

“Hayden’s running toward it!”

A booming quake rattled in the distance, and they saw pebbles shift on the ground around them. Dust fell off rocks. Trees swayed. The colossus then released another chorus of ear-shattering roars, all overlapping like screeching cicadas. The group covered their ears, hearts pounding.

“He’s running toward that?” Carian screamed. “We need to go!”

The group sprinted up the mountain at full speed, reaching the clifftop in the blink of an eye. When they got to the top and saw their vantage point, the situation became clear.

He was leading us here… she thought. But why?

Carian looked down the mountain and watched Hayden running in a bubble barrier with two others. The Bubble spirit was valuable because it stabilized the user and kept them off the ground, ensuring that the barrier wasn’t blown away, only to damage the user. For that reason, the trio ran over the quakes as if they didn’t exist.

As the group moved, Sickles flew to intercept them, trying to protect Hayden as he ran past the colossus, but a balding man shot Living Arrows to ward them off as they ran unhindered.

Where the hell are they going? she wondered. And where’s the rest of his team?

Suddenly, Caro yelled, “What are we doing? We need to save our team!”

“What?” Carian muttered.

“What do you mean, What?” Caro snapped. “Hayden clearly led up to this ridge so we could attack that fuckin’ thing! And as much as I don’t want to play into that fucker’s plan, we don’t have a choice. If we don’t lead it away, our entire team’s gonna die.”

“Forget the team, we need to protect Hayden,” Sylvia, the leader of the team that showed up to meet up with them, said. “That’s the role—don’t forget it.”

An intense stare-off between the two teams broke out. Carian considered it and said, “Yes, we’re protecting him—and that means we have to lead the colossus away. It was clearly hiding the treasure, right? Otherwise, he wouldn’t have led us here to lead it away.”

Sylvia tongued her canine. “True.” She closed her eyes. “Let’s split up. If you can follow Hayden over the quakes, follow me. If you can’t, lure the beasts away. That’s final. Now wait for the signal!”

Sylvia lifted her hands, and massive boulders from the mountain floated in the air, coalescing into a meteor capable of leaving a fifty-meter crater on level ground. She clearly didn’t hold back; everyone could tell by the heavy sweat sticking to her skin. Raising her hand to the heavens, she swiped her hand down, and the massive meteor slammed into the colossus from the south, making it stagger and fall onto three legs.

The colossus screeched, staring at them with hatred in its eyes.

As it stood and approached, Sylvia lifted her hand again. “Ready your spells!” Forty elite rifters prepared their spirits.

“Wait for it!” Sylvia yelled. The colossus shook the cliff as it approached, moving an eighth of a mile with every step. “Wait for it!” The rifters shook and trembled as the fifteen-hundred-foot beast towered over them, casting dark shadows of the clifftop they stood on. “Wait for it!”

It released a devastating screech and charged.

“Now!”

Forty high-ranking rifters used their spirits in unison. One created a bow with a golden energy arrow so large that it could destroy a warship. It hit the creature in one of its eyes. It let out a war cry and charged, only to be stopped when Carian clenched her fist and a massive black sphere formed over its body, increasing gravity tenfold. It wasn’t much, but Calid, who was now free to move, hit it with a hundred-meter wind blade that was visible even from two miles away.

It slammed into the creature’s leg. It fell to a knee, triggering a massive earthquake that caused the cliff to crumble. Three rifters shook and fell off the mountain, but the rest stayed put, releasing an overwhelming barrage of wind and ice and thunder that streaked across the sky like a firework show.

“Now split up!” Sylvia screamed. She jumped off the mountain with an A-rank in her arms, landing on solid air and running with the Airwalk spirit. Her wings flew down the mountain, picking up Calid and his heavy hitters.

This was the work of an elite team’s leader—not a reserve.

Sylvia was decisive, bold, and effective, but Carian also felt uneasy about her taking an elite force to chase after Hayden. There would certainly be a confrontation—and she felt like that was a mistake.
