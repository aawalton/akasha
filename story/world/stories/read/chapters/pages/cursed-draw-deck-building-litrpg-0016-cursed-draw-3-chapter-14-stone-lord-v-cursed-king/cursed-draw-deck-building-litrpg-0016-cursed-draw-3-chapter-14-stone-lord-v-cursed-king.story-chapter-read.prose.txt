Alley repressed a wince as Vern Euchenic began his turn. While there might be no in-game reason to hide one’s emotions from your opponent, he had always been taught to both ignore the expressions of his enemy as they might be used to deceive, and display as few as he could himself. Unless, of course, he was actively trying to deceive his opponent. It was sound advice and had served Alley more than once before.

Still, even if he was able to remain stony -faced about it, Alley was a little frustrated that the old man across the arena had already begun getting the machinery of his Deck moving. There was nothing that made him more frustrated by his own massive library than seeing an opponent draw exactly what they needed on turn one.

“Strike It Rich!” Vern declared activating the spell which made his eyes glow blue the same way Alley’s had when he entered into in the pocket reality that contained his Cards.

After about fifteen seconds, the glow dulled and the floating image of a new Card appeared from the torc on the white bearded man’s thick arm and flew up to join its brethren in Vern’s hand. Alley couldn’t see what it was, but he did know it had to be a Relic, and thought he had a decent idea which it would be.

“Terracotta Warriors!”

Alley once again kept his face neutral, but internally, he was swearing up a storm. The old man from The Shattered Archipelago seemed to have hit just about everything he needed to get rolling in his opening hand. Alley had watched him play two matches and taken a look at a portion of his Library with the Dead Man’s Hand, so he knew what he was dealing with pretty intimately.

That, at least, was one advantage of using a Cursed Deck: you could watch Alley play four or five matches and still be caught off guard by what you were up against. Alley, on the other hand, knew he was dealing with a Relic-heavy swarm.

There was no question in Alley’s mind that the next Card played was going to be a Relic of some sort, and if he had to guess, knife to his throat, it was going to be Shard of The Living M-

“Shard of The Living Mountain!”

The huge chunk of crystal Manifested in a flare of blue light. The crystal itself was  oddly cube-shaped as though it had been cut but roughly, and was about three times the size of Vern. The rough cube was a deep ocean blue, with inky dark sections that occasionally pulsed with pinpricks of light, making them look almost like the night sky.

At the same time, two of the gemlike Vials that floated in tight circles around Vern’s arm vanished. Once they were gone, the effect Alley had been waiting for was activated. Terracotta Warriors; Alley had seen them when he was looking through the top five Cards of Vern’s Library. Those Cards, including the ante that Alley selected, had all been shuffled back into the Library. So either Vern had been extremely lucky on the draw, or he ran multiple copies of Terracotta Warriors. Given Alley had seen him use this exact same opening set of plays in one of the matches he‘d watched, he was all but certain there were three copies of the statue men in the man’s Library. Probably three copies of Strike It Rich, for that matter.

In spite of the Creature Card’s name being Terracotta Warriors, there was actually only one. The warrior in question was a clay statue of a  lightly  armored  man with a sword in one hand and a topknot haircut. Above the statue hovered its stats like with any Manifested Creature. The Terracotta Warrior pulsed with the slate grey colored energy of Earth Resonance. The energy flowed down out of the one thousand Power Creature into the earth of the arena beneath it.

A few seconds after the Resonance flowed out of the soldier statue, a pair of similar clay arms burst from the ground beside it, and over the course of another ten or twenty seconds, another Terracotta Warrior pulled itself out of the ground and came to stand with military style uniformity next to the original Terracotta Warrior.

Once the clay soldiers took their place, the Power above both Terracotta Warriors grew by three hundred, leaving them both at thirteen hundred.

Alley had expected all of this, but it was still a significant hurdle to get over. While neither Terracotta Warrior could stand up to his Brutish Zombie, especially with it protected and empowered by the Bone Weaved Spell. In fact, since the Terracotta Warriors only had one Vitality each, as long as Bone Weaved remained in play, they wouldn’t be able to attack Brutish Zombie at all, but a stalemate was far from ideal. Especially as the Terracotta Warriors were only going to keep growing in number and Power.

Vern ended his Creation Phase and redrew back up to a hand size of five. He considered his options for a moment and then ended his turn without discarding. Of course, Alley knew the end wasn’t quite where the white-bearded man’s turn would stop.  The huge crystalline cube that was the Relic Shard of The Living Mountain activated.  First, the blue of the cube’s crystal structure glowed brightly, and then a beam of Resonance that was blue tinged with grey around the edges fired in a beam from the cube into Vern’s Dealer.

A moment later, the image of a Card back hovered over Vern before chunks of crystal began growing over the image at an accelerated rate. Within a few seconds, the Image fell to the ground as though weighed down by the crystal nodes. Where the Card fell a wall made of the same Crystal rose that was about six feet tall, and as many feet across.

Which is, of course, when the ability possessed by both Terracotta Warriors is activated again. Slate grey Resonance pulsed through the pair of them, and then into the ground. Like before, two pairs of clay arms punched up from the ground, and pulled the complete form of two Terracotta Warriors pulled themselves out of the earth to join the two that were already on the field.

‘This is getting out of hand.’  Alley thought, whilst still doing his best not to react. The block of now four Terracotta Warriors still couldn’t force their way through Bone Weaved, but their Power was continuing to grow with each addition to their number.  At this point, the four of them each had nineteen hundred power, but if Alley couldn’t clear any of the creatures out soon, he would be staring down eight of the clay warriors next turn, meaning they would have...He did a quick count in his head, three thousand one hundred Power each, and that was assuming Vern didn’t play another Relic manually.
