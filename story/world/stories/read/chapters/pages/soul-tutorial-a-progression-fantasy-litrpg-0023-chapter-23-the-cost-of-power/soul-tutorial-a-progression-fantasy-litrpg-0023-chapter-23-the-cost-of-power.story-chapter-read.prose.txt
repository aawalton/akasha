A crack echoed out; his leg mushed into paste, even with soul energy flooding into it. However, with his broken leg. Luke quickly pulled the energy from his crippled leg into his arm, putting it out in front of his face, stopping himself from crashing face-first into the ground. Pushing off the ground with his right arm. He tried his best to balance on his only leg while the other hung beside it.

Luke’s face grimaced; his eyes darted around to find the slime before it could make its way around. He noticed how the ground shook from the right, quickly glancing over. Without waiting another second, he quickly rotated the energy, condensing a heavy amount of it towards his legs as he rushed towards it.

As he approached, he reduced the intensity of his energy and pooled it into his right arm; his arm changed color along with the continued flow of energy. Standing directly before it, he brought his arm down with a slam. The slime attempted to retreat, but its efforts were futile, leaving it exposed to his approaching fist. Without giving the slime time to react, he slammed it with more soul energy pooling from his chest, ignoring the accompanying pain.

As the slime deteriorated into particles that floated away, leaving bright souls in the sky.

This victory signifies his continued sweep of silver slimes. Luke decided he could finally start putting his body to work more as he continued to fight more silver slimes. As Elites might be the only difficult thing on the floor, hopping his way over to the pedestal, every jump made him really regret why he had lost focus during that fight.

The only price coming from this is the loss of points and the pain that is linked with the injury.

Wounds had accumulated on his body as he tried his best to keep gaining more points. Investing those points into the constitution really started showing its effects, and physique upgrades made more than just a visual difference. Not using nearly as much soul energy as he once did before for the same effect.

With soul energy only acting as a boost that grows along with his attributes as he gets stronger. Finding it very effective to keep a balance in stats.

But he couldn’t really tell the effects of sensory since it only reached level 4 compared to his other various stat points.

Even after those few days of training, there were moments he really regretted. Getting his body crushed repeatedly by silver slimes. Even one that crashed into him and broke 3 ribs. Only by the tenth fight with the silver slimes could he finally gain a grasp of how he could maneuver around and defend himself. This helped him build new ways to use soul energy and gain stability with his attributes.

He shivered at the thought of all the pain. But there was still so much that needed to be done, even if it was to help cleanse his soul. Finding no solution to the problem as of yet, and his soul still comprises of darkness that wants to eat away at him.

Only regretting his decision to push himself more than he should in that moment when fighting the fire slime, still figuring out why he had rushed towards it so soon.

Leaving now wouldn’t save his mother, as he was still weak and unaware of what was happening on Earth.

Luke could only clench his fist, hopping towards the pedestal.

The price showed up before his eyes; he could only glare at it while making amends with the outcome.

Restore: 25 points

He could only bite his lip while his points whisked away.

39 points → 14 points

But his stats weren’t the only thing that had changed; the points needed for treatment had risen, with even some light injuries costing upwards of 10 points. But it wasn’t much to him these days, as few as 5 blue slimes would garner him that many points. Unless it was a large injury like the last one, which almost tanked his whole points backlog.

Without trying to tire himself out too much before he began experimenting again, he took the souls he currently had and sent them back into the sky with a bit of their energy left. If he could take the souls, what’s stopping him from returning where they belong? But they had dimmed significantly, amounting to nothing more than dwarf stars in the sky.

The pedestal lay before him, currently figuring out how to proceed from now on. quietly staring at the fire slime floor. Tremors shot through his arm at the thought of being burnt alive. He couldn’t just push to floor 4 without getting a handle on the problem on floor 3.

Finding himself in a particular situation, he could only push forward and brace for anything. Before he proceeded with engaging with the flaming slime and the mysterious floor 4, he thought over his choices one more time and found this time better than ever to test what he always wanted to do.

Willing the floor to occur again, the energy shifted the very atmosphere as a sense of heaviness almost made Luke's knee buckle, but it was just all in his imagination.

The grey-silver luster of the slime stood before him, as daunting as ever. With his reflection shining through, he caught himself. A disastrous mess of a boy. Laid bare before his very eyes. Left arm removed almost up to his shoulder with a stump of pink flesh caved inward.

A layer of loose skin covered a barely visible layer of what looked like abs. His appearance astounded him. He caught himself by coughing, relishing his past self. Only hoping he doesn’t turn out worse than he already has.

The slime still stood there like an immobile piece of steel, but Luke already knew the truth behind all that. Quickly shifting his weight to the soles of his feet, he swiftly made his way towards that sack of metal. Without the accumulation of energy, he still swung his right fist with enough force to crush the creature.

The slime moved back at the last second. Luke pivoted and backed away, scared of getting his body crushed.

Luke grabbed the 3 weakened souls out of the sky once again, forcing them inside his core, generating a slight flow of energy as it rotated with a slow start that quickly started picking up speed. With a good pace of the souls, he reached deeper than he ever had before, orbiting the spherical souls around his own, creating a circle as they blurred. With a grunt, he rushed the energy from his chest towards his right hand.

Time slowed down; energy pulsed through his arm. The first attempt at projecting the energy shattered immediately.

Without a second thought, he tried again, turning the energy in a more effective way. It was barely visible before it exploded into harmless sparks.

Blood trickled down his nose as he pushed the energy even further, without a care in the world about his well-being.

“Come on!” He screamed at the top of his lungs.

By the third try, a translucent blade of green soul energy shot out, which was a foot long. That was flickering.

Without wasting another moment, with all the power he had left, he shifted towards the slime as it rolled towards him at the energy that was currently warping the surrounding air. The same blade that resembled the one from when he first fought a blue slime in a berserk state.

At the point of impact, the blade held true. The sharp tip of the energy blade sliced deeply into the silver slime as the weight of the slime combined into a stronger force, splitting its core in half. Particles were all that remained of the slime. The slime helped with the current effect of the attack. Moving his hand toward his stump, he grabbed it as it convulsed with the worst pain he could ever imagine. Barely maintaining consciousness, the pain snapped out of existence as if it had never been there.
