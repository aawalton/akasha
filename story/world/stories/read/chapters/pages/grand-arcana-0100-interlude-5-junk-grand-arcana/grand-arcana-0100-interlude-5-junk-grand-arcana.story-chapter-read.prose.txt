JOKER (Grand Arcana)

(Rarity - Epic+)

JOKER: has the unique ability to transform into any card of the GRAND ARCANA.

The change is permanent. Only skills, attributes, and bonuses of the new skill will apply.

CRYING PROPHET (Grand Arcana)

(Rarity - Restricted)

CHANGE: All current skills, items, spells, statistics, and bonuses are lost. The owner is taken back to the day before the GRAND ARCANA trials with all memories intact.

SERPENT BLADE (Grand Arcana)

(Rarity - Epic)

PARALYSIS: cover item or self with paralytic liquid.

LIVING WEAPON: A child of the BLADE SERPENT, the SERPENT BLADE has sentience and intelligence similar to that of a standard human. Similar to a divine weapon, it is unbreakable and can be Awakened for further abilities.

QUICK STRIKE: strike attacks performed with the SERPENT BLADE are three times faster.

GNAW: the SERPENT BLADE grows when consuming flesh or most living organic matter.

BLOOD DRAIN: draws blood from any wound it creates in the form of blood strands.

VENOM STRIKE: strikes performed with the SERPENT BLADE inflict poison.

MINOR SERPENT FORM: take the form of a serpent at will.
