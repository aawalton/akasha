“And, it appears a lot of the Celestial clans are drifting from the set rules, I believe, widespread culls may be necessary. Having said that, there are also three clans that seem to be moving to support the pilots' clan, they have not broken any of the rules yet. However, Yutra Hastr is throwing a tantrum but has selected a new planet to seed after his precious experiment on that blue and green planet the locals called earth was disrupted.” Archie quickly reported.

Yue had gone to bed shortly after the revelation, though she had struggled to fall asleep, sleep did eventually claim her weary eyes. However, when she woke up, it was Akemi shoving her out of bed and screaming for her to wake up.

“Ouch” I groaned as I rolled over to rub the sore spot that had met the floor and rudely woken me. “What the hell?” I asked, feeling the injustice of the rude awakening from such a good dream.

“Get up, you're going to miss the alchemy exam! The door will open any minute, and your lard ass is too heavy to drag you to the shower.” Akemi screamed at her.

That got me up, my adrenaline kicking in better than anything else would have to get me up and going. I quickly rushed off to the showers to get myself clean, and dressed, did my hair, and ran out the bedrooms to find only a couple of people waiting by the door instead of everyone. Akemi had a cheeky grin on her face as she stood behind a couch watching me carefully.

“Okay, I might have exaggerated a little bit,” she admitted, sheepishly.

I felt my eyes narrow as I considered the many ways I wanted to make the insufferable rich girl pay for this little prank. Before slowly tilting my head and watching her with a new light as an idea came to me.

“What?” She suddenly asked, looking slightly nervous.

“I just thought of a way you can make it up to me” I said triumphantly.

“We don't have time for that.” She said, throwing a heap of innuendo onto the sentence, making me frown confused for a second before blushing when my mind finally reached the gutter.

“What the hell are you talking about!” I screeched as I covered my face.

“Nothing, what are you talking about?” She replied, while trying to act innocent.

“Your family lives in this city, right?” I asked, deciding to quickly move on and completely ignore the embarrassing moment.

“You know they do,” she replied, my lip twitching up slightly as I saw her frown.

“Then have your family look after mine, it would be nice knowing they weren’t living in poverty while I was away at a fancy sect.” I suggested, knowing I was asking for a lot but deciding to try my luck anyway.

Akemi merely rolled her shoulders, as if this were nothing of consequence, before giving me a casual nod. “Sure, I have a shop that isn’t making much money, and it won't hurt me or my family if I get rid of it.” She said offhandedly, her gaze drifting to a slim bangle on her wrist.

For a few moments, she ran her finger along its edge and tapped it lightly. Then she looked back up at me. “There. I have transferred ownership of the shop to your father, it’s not much, but it should be enough to keep a small clan out of poverty.” She shrugged, as if she’d just offered me a spare coin rather than a business.

My mouth fell open. I just stared at her, struggling to process what she’d said. Not able to understand why or how anyone would just give a business away like that. Even after several seconds, I still couldn't wrap my head around it.

“What?” She asked suddenly, a hint of concern flickering across her face. Then she frowned. “Is it not enough? Are you from a medium-sized clan?” Her eyes dropped back to the bangle.

“Wait!” I hissed, trying not to raise my voice as I rushed forward. I grabbed her hands, pressing them away from the bangle before she could give away anything else. “Rich… Akemi, that’s way too generous. My family isn’t part of any clan. We’re just a small household. Anything that could keep even a small clan out of poverty is already beyond generous. We can’t accept it.”

“Oh?” Her lips curved into a faint smile. “Then consider it my contribution to the founding of a new small clan! You can’t refuse. I’ve already transferred the ownership, and will not accept it back,” She declared.

Her gaze softened. When she spoke again, her voice carried a quiet sincerity. “I am from a large clan, daughter of the clan head. Everyone who’s ever spoken to me wanted something from me or my father. You’re the first person who’s ever treated me like an equal, like a real person. So I won’t let my best friend waste time worrying about her family when she should be throwing herself wholeheartedly into cultivating immortality.”

I was about to argue or make more excuses to refuse when she spoke again with a serious tone as she turned to indicate that we had been talking long enough for everyone to gather and the door to open. “Now let's go and show everyone the alchemy prodigy that you are!”

Ashley was laying back on a comfortable chair enjoying the peace and quiet, while the pilot, flew their new Corvette class ship that they had unanimously decided to name ‘The Kenna’ in honour of the amazing clansmen that got them all into space and giving them the opportunity to make more money than they ever dreamed they would.

Her peace and quiet was suddenly interrupted by someone screaming and turning up the volume of the 3D wall holoscreen. Causing her to sit up properly and look at the screen.

“I can't believe it, Pete, is the emperor truly dead?” A blond woman sitting behind a desk asked as she stared at the large man with wide eyes. Who nodded back at her.

“It is true, my sources in the emperor’s city are screaming in panic as they try to buy their way off the planet. According to the information they are selling so they can afford the inflated ticket prices, the emperor is dead. Oh my, his entire clan is gone, not even the children were spared. Wait, that’s not all, I have a few sources around the universe reporting the death of entire celestial clans. As of this moment, Kim, less than a dozen Celestial clans still stand.” He replied, his face looking a little pale as he stared at a holoscreen in the desk.

They both sat there silently staring at the holoscreens in the desk for a few moments, reading something, before Kim suddenly spoke without even looking up at the audience.

“Pete, what will this mean for the universe?”

“I am not sure,” he responded as his gaze slowly moved away from the desk to look at us. “But one thing is for sure, the universe will never be the same again.”

“That’s a serious understatement,” one of the guys near me said as he shook his head.

“I don't care about that, what I want to know is what this means for us?” A woman on the other side of the room shouted at the screen.

The screen, of course, didn’t respond to her question and the two on it, went on to report events around the universe as they unfolded, including the death or disappearance of a few more clans. They did name each clan, but the ones I was listening for were never mentioned. I found it strange that the clan that had attacked our solar system was not wiped out. However, I was also relieved not to hear anything about the clans that had come to our aide.

The news soon ended, and the program moved on to sports. I quickly started to ignore the noise as I laid back to resume my relaxation. After a moment, I realised that once I got up, I would need to check the contract board to see how this news would affect our income.

I managed to lay there for five more minutes before the worry got to me, and I groaned in annoyance before getting to my feet and walking to the captain's quarters. I turned on my holoscreens and navigated to the contract boards.

I quickly noted that all the small time contracts were still there, just a lot less of them as any that had been put up by the now dead clans had been shifted into a new category that listed them as ‘limited time contracts’ and came with a warning that they needed to be completed quickly before the situation evolved beyond the scope of the contract, if it did then the contract would become voided, as the contractor would be unable to finance any changes.

The larger contracts that I could accept with my Corvette Class Ship had also changed dramatically, and new ones were being listed, they were requesting ships to defend places for a certain amount of time. I quickly came to understand that anyone still alive and powerful enough to list a contract was turtling and trying to draw as much firepower towards them to defend them from whatever was powerful enough to take out so many untouchable clans.

“Perhaps it's time for a vacation.” I whispered to the empty room.
