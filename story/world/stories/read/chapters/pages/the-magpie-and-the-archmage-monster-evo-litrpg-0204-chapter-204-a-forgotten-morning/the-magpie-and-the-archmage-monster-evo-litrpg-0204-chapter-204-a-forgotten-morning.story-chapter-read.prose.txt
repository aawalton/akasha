Collecting the glimmerstones and treasure was easy. There weren't even as many glimmerstones in the dragon as one would have expected. Obviously, that was because the real prize was the glimmercore.

Unfortunately, getting to it was far from easy, as it was located near the dragon's heart, hidden behind thick scales and the ribcage. It took a while to get to it, but eventually she did.

Everyone marveled at the beautiful glowing azure orb.

"That's so pretty," Cee commented.

"What does it do?" Caspian asked, unable to look away from the treasure.

"Let's see..." Renir muttered, pressing his hand against the orb with a soft clang.

[Glimmercore of Azure Dragon]

[Contains the following trait:]

[Tempest Wings - the storms and hurricanes no longer hinder your flight but rather enhance it. Navigating through storms is also easier. While this trait is being actively used, your stamina consumption is doubled.]

"Mine!" Liora said, snatching the glimmercore for herself.

"I don't think anyone would have argued otherwise," Cee said, but then a calculating glint appeared in her eyes. "But since we're on the topic of glimmercores, how about we give the shark glimmercore to Smokey. I bet that scorching sand attack would be a great addition to his move pool."

They all looked at the derpy wyvern who was currently buried shoulder deep in the dragon's carcass.

"Are you sure he can handle it? I do not want to get caught in that scorching sand," Liora said.

"I'll train him properly with the skill. C'mon! It would be better to use it rather than let it sit in your inventory. And I don't think anyone else could utilize that skill properly, given that we are rather sensitive to heat."

Liora thought about it for a moment, then nodded. Although her magpie instincts just wished she could keep both glimmercores in her hoard, it would also improve the overall strength of their team.

"How do I absorb it?" Liora asked.

"You just hold it firmly and think about absorbing it," Renir explained.

Liora held the beautiful azure orb and thought about acquiring the trait within it. A minute later, she received a notification.

[New trait learned - Tempest Wings.]

It wasn't so easy for Smokey and Cee, since getting the wyvern away from his meal was already a chore and a half. So Liora moved on and decided to upgrade Glimmerlust while she was at it.

[Glimmerlust (Rank 2) variants available]

[Mana Hoard — Rewards your desire to hoard glimmerstones. At [Basic] level, it provides you with a 0.05% power and speed bonus to all your skills per glimmerstone, and increases your mana regeneration speed by 0.01% per glimmerstone.]

Liora read the option aloud.

"That sounds pretty good," Cee said, as she tried to force Smokey to hold the glimmercore. "Like a direct upgrade, but now it includes mana regeneration. It's never bad to take these direct upgrade options."

Renir hummed with approval. "Should you take this option, your mana regeneration will vastly increase. You likely won't have to worry about running out of mana."

"Yes, but that's not very useful in this place," Liora countered.

Renir raised one of his gem fingers in protest, but quickly lowered it, grumbling under his breath. "We have to think of long-term benefits."

"I think for the time being, we need to think of maximum short-term benefit. If grinding dragons is not an option, then acquiring the best skills and traits is the way to go," Liora said sagely.

"Does that mean you're going to buy a skill?" Renir rubbed his hands together.

"Caw! I never said that!"

"What other options do you have?" Cee said, attempting to divert the topic.

[Corvid Court — Attract other magpies, crows and corvid-kin to yourself. The larger the horde, the more corvids you can attract. They will gather information, steal on your behalf and even fight enemies in exchange for a small portion of your hoard.]

Everyone stared at each other for a moment.

"Does this mean you can pretty much send other birds to attack the enemies? If that's the case, then you must take it. You could just have a horde of crows peck the enemies to death. With large enough numbers, nothing could stand in our way," Caspian cheered.

"It does sound amazing, but I'm not sure if I want to be surrounded by crows and magpies at all times. Frankly, one is enough," Cee said.

"Hey! What's that supposed to mean?" Liora huffed, then shook her head. "In either case, I will not take this upgrade."

"Why not?" Renir asked.

"Didn't you listen? They will want a small portion of my horde in exchange. If it's just one crow, then fine, but what if I attract like a hundred or a thousand of them? It is a trait, so I can't turn it off either."

"Yeah, that could be problematic…" Renir agreed. "Let's move on."

[Appraiser's Eye — You instantly know the value, origin, and any secret abilities of any item which you have touched for more than 5 seconds.]

"This is great! I want this!" Liora said.

"Hold up! You definitely don't need this. I can appraise and uncover the secrets of most items already," Renir said. "Although I do admit that if you didn't have me around, it would be the best option to pick."

Liora paused for a second, then muttered. "There's no telling if you're going to remain by my side forever…"

"What?" Renir scuttled closer, not having heard her properly.

Cee gave her a pitying glance. She must have heard her, but decided not to comment on it.

"Nothing! I was saying that there's another option available."

[A secret upgrade available thanks to your draconic race.]

[Draconic Avarice — Your hoard becomes a wellspring of your power. While resting atop or being in contact with your hoard—your nest, your cave, or wherever your hoard is kept—you regenerate health, mana, and stamina at an increased rate. Rate increases with trait level and the size of the hoard.]

There was a moment of silence as everyone was likely thinking the same thing as Liora. This was without a doubt the best option out of all, even if Liora would lose the boost Glimmerlust currently provided. The more glimmerstones Liora had, the faster she would recover.

"Before you take this option," Caspian said, looking thoughtful. "I have to wonder whether you need to be physically touching the hoard. It wouldn't be very convenient to always have to dump your glimmerstones on the battlefield. Many of them could be lost."

"I don't think that's the case…" Renir said thoughtfully. "I believe Liora has stumbled upon an anomaly in the system."

"An exploit!" Liora said, laughing. "Chak-chak-chak!"

"What do you mean?" Caspian asked.

"Monsters normally aren't supposed to have an inventory," Renir explained. "I mean, just look at the dragon who used to live in this cave. His hoard was hidden here with the dragon himself guarding it. Do you really think that dragons, being the greedy creatures that they are, would leave their treasure lying about if they had a different option?"

"No way," Cee said.

"Exactly! Inventory is supposed to be unique to humans. But Liora and Cee both have managed to exploit that. They are human enough, yet they possess the strength of a monster. No offense, Liora dear. Just a scientific term of your current physical being, not your mental one," Renir clarified.

Liora shrugged, trying to act nonchalant, but after the outburst she had earlier, the word "monster" did sting a bit.

"So, I believe that if Liora were to take this skill, she would be able to utilize it without ever needing to withdraw anything," Renir said. "She would be constantly recovering at an accelerated rate."

"What if not? It's a risk," Caspian pointed out.

[Would you like to upgrade Glimmerlust to Rank 2 trait <Draconic Avarice>?]

Yes!

[Your trait has been upgraded.]

"I suppose it is… You could take the Mana Hoard upgrade. It would be the safer option," Renir said.

"I already upgraded it to Draconic Avarice," Liora said matter-of-factly.

Caspian groaned, Cee shrugged as if she had been expecting that, and Renir looked like he wanted to say something, but ultimately just shook his head.

"I guess there's no going back now. I would love to check whether it worked, but with the mountain sucking up all the mana, I don't see a way to do it. Maybe you can fly around for a bit to see if you feel more tired?"

"We could make a small cut and see how fast it heals," Cee suggested.

"No!" Renir instantly protested. "There's no need to inflict actual pain to check something like that. Maybe you can try using Wind Blast. It won't work, but your mana might still be drained."

Liora flapped her wings, still in her magpie form, and thought of using Wind Blast. Nothing happened, as Renir had said, but Liora did feel her mana drain away the same way as it always did when she used the skill. Liora checked her status.

[Status]

Health: Healthy

Mana: Flowing

She stared at the status screen for a while, and about a minute later, her mana status changed to Surging, indicating that it was full.

"It recovered really fast! It must be thanks to the new trait," Liora said.

"Excellent. That way we don't need Caspian to be that close anymore. He'll be able to focus on more serious injuries and buffs," Renir announced. "We just need to cover another weakness of yours, and after that, I'm going to spend the remaining evening teaching Cee sigils."

"Sigils! Yay, finally!" Cee did the spider equivalent of a clap, although her legs were so thin that no real sound came from it. "It's been too long."

"Hey, I only recently acquired proper fingers. I will teach you a whole bunch of useful sigils. I think they will be our trump card in these mountains. But first…" Renir turned towards Liora with a glint in his eyes.

"Nu-uh! Nope. I will not." Liora hopped back a little.

"Liora, darling," he said, a smile forming on his gem face. While beautiful thanks to the shimmering, it was also horrifying.

"I will not spend any glimmerstones on—"

"Liora, my dearest, loveliest, most beautiful magpie!" He stepped even closer, flaunting his beautiful crystal structure in exactly the kind of way that made him shimmer the most.

Liora couldn't resist, and the pile of compliments was also affecting her, as much as she hated to admit. She was the most beautiful magpie, but when was the last time somebody had actually properly complemented her?

"N-No… I…"

"Liora, you do know that I couldn't do anything without you. You are truly the best bird in the entire world, no, the whole universe. If only you could open that skill shop and search for physical long-range skills. I'm sure it's no problem for a beautiful and wise magpie such as yourself," Renir delivered the finishing blow.

"Screw you, Renir! You can't do this!" Liora complained but opened the skill shop, filtering out the long-range physical attacks.

[Long range physical skills available for purchase]

Feather Volley – 50 glimmerstones

Regurgitated Payload – 60 glimmerstones

"I wonder what Regurgitated Payload does," Caspian smirked.

"Oh, hell no! I am buying Feather Volley!" Liora announced angrily.

[Would you like to buy the skill <Feather Volley> for 50 glimmerstones?]

Yes!

[Skill <Feather Volley> has been purchased.]

[Feather Volley – Shed and launch razor-sharp feathers at the enemy. Range: 15 meters.]

"Wait… What did I just do?" Liora asked, looking at Renir.

He smiled. "Good girl, Liora. I knew you would see reason."

"M-My glimmerstones… You've cheated me!"

He came and gave Liora a reassuring pat. "It's necessary. You need a long-range attack in these mountains. Ideally, I would love you to purchase a trait as well."

"No way! They were so expensive!" Liora shook her head.

"Yeah, I figured that would be too much. Rest for the day. Maybe I can figure out a way to unlock a trait naturally. I'm going to teach Cee some sigils in the meantime," he said.

Liora narrowed her eyes at him, wondering if he was just trying to come up with a new way to fool her into purchasing a trait. But he really turned his focus to Cee, who seemed quite eager to learn.

She watched them for a moment, while she preened her feathers, cleaning off the gore, blood, and wyvern slobber. But her eyes soon closed, and before long she fell asleep.

It wasn't restful sleep.

Liora saw glimpses of her past life. She had been having dreams of her past life ever since Lucien messed with her head, but they were usually very blurry, and she forgot them as soon as she woke.

This was stronger, although the scenes she saw were mundane.

She was at home, making breakfast. Some waffles with blueberries. She tried to focus on the process of making the waffles, but even in this dream she was acutely aware of the presence with her.

Somebody was with her in the kitchen, sipping a cup of coffee and waiting for her to finish. He occasionally would say something, but his words and voice was muddled. She couldn't hear him properly, and Liora tried not to focus on it. Tried not to think too hard about this memory.

It was just a memory. Nothing else. It wasn't important anymore.

But as her dream self finished cooking, she turned towards the man, putting the freshly cooked waffles in front of him. She felt a smile form on her lips, as he too smiled.

It was bizarre, because even in this dream she couldn't recall his face. It was all blurry. But it was without a doubt the same man who had died protecting her.

Fuck… I was trying not to remember my past life this whole time. How am I supposed to do that now?
