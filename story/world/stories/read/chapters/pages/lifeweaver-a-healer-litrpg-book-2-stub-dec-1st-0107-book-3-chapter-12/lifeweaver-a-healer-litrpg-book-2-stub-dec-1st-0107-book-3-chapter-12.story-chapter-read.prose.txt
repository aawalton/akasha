The private jail of Integrated Solutions Group in New York looked much the same as the one in Chicago. A hallway stretched out before them, filled with small cells housing various Integrated and, Luke was sure, other people, Alan, and by extension, the guild, wanted removed.

"Where are they?" Luke asked.

Peter pointed deeper into the corridor. "Just over here. I would appreciate it if you asked them to remain calm as you exit."

"Sure," Luke said, eyeing the other man. "You some sort of higher-up in the guild, then?"

"You could say that."

"I meant what I said up there. If you hurt Dot in any way, shape, or form..."

Peter gestured for them to turn left when the corridor branched. "We would do no such thing. There will be a full audit to ascertain the extent of Mr. Schmidt's illegal actions. You can rest assured that we will not kill anyone to get our hands on their class. From what my associates are telling me over our communication channels, Dr. Marlene has retired to her apartment. After she is rested enough, we will recommend she see a therapist to deal with," he glanced up at Luke's still blood-covered face, "today's unpleasantness."

"Good."

Ray frowned. "The gem blocking stuff. That was Alan?"

"That was part of a skill. I don't know much more than that. We are unsure how he went from a Commander to his new class, Master, but it provided him with a host of abilities we will be just as glad to be rid of."

"I can see that," Luke said, stopping in front of a door Peter indicated, glancing in through the window. "This is it?"

The floor, ceiling, and walls all glowed with bright, white and cold lights, strong enough that no shadows appeared.

"It is," Peter said, unlocking the door by swiping a card in a reader. He input a code and held up his thumb to a fingerprint scanner.

Luke pulled the door open and stepped inside. "Hannah?"

"No," Curtis said, getting off the floor, where he'd been sitting pressed into the corner to the right of the wall, unseen from the window. "About time. He's dead, I take it? You look like you slept inside a dead animal."

"I know," Luke said. "And yes. He's dead, I mean."

Ray chuckled, but settled down after a look from Curtis. "How did they capture you anyway?"

"That Alan asshole," Curtis said as the group moved over to the next door, releasing Hannah.

She looked as pissed off as Curtis sounded, and glared at Peter, who kept his eyes on the ground. A good choice, by all accounts. "That bastard is evil!" She turned to Luke. "You look terrible, Luke."

"Yeah..."

"Was evil," Ray corrected.

Her eyes shot to Luke, who nodded.

"Good," she said, pressing her palms to the sides of her head. "Fucking with our minds like that is pure evil. Never again."

"That's how they caught you?" Luke asked.

Curtis nodded. "The whole not killing people didn't make it easier, and when that asshole returned without you, everything went to shit in a hurry."

"But your ears. I saw the blood."

Hannah glared at Peter. "They turned the whole place into a dungeon somehow..."

"...and healed us," Curtis grunted.

"That's pretty smart," Ray said, nodding in recognition.

Curtis and Hannah both turned their glares at him, and he held up his hands in surrender. "Just saying!"

"Well, it's over," Luke said.

"No Dot?" Hannah asked.

He shook his head. "No. Not yet, anyway. Maybe she'll come around."

"What now?" Ray asked as they got to walking back out of the building, Peter in tow. "Can we finally get a proper group together and plow through dungeons?"

Luke drew in a deep breath, considered for a moment, then nodded. "I think that might just be the thing for us, if you're all into that idea." He looked over to Curtis and Hannah, who nodded. "It's settled then. Guide reminded me that's what we should be doing."

They all stopped dead in their tracks. Hannah was the first to speak. "You met Guide?"

"He sort of saved me from being eaten by the void."

"What happened?" Ray asked, leaning in.

Luke noted Peter standing very still, even holding his breath, hoping they didn't tell him to get lost before speaking. That reminded Luke that he needed some information from the man, too, so he figured sharing some first wouldn't hurt. "Alan tricked me into following through a portal to a dead version of Earth, then trapped me there. My first idea was to escape through the void."

"Stupid," Hannah said, with everyone nodding.

"I'm not going to argue against that," Luke said with a sigh. "Guide turned up and pulled me away from crossing over and probably dying." That summoned an image of Smudge in his mind, and he shook his head. "Almost certainly dying. Told me some stuff about the void trying to eat Earth and every other system, and also some interesting tidbits about Relian."

Curtis sniffed the air. "Can't wait to hear it, but maybe we should get you a shower first."

"I could use one too," Hannah complained.

Ray lifted his shirt and leaned forward a little, smelling himself. "I'm good."

"Sure you are," Curtis said, turning to Peter. "Did you want something else?"

"No, no," he told Curtis. "Please send my regards to your brother." Turning to Luke, he added. "Let's keep our lines of communication open, yes? I'm sure we can find ways to collaborate going forward."

"We'll see."

"Of course, of course. We'll at least keep you updated on the process surrounding Mr. Schmidt's demise and the legal repercussions of that, if any."

With that, Peter turned and walked away at a brisk pace, looking like he would start sprinting as soon as he was out of sight.

"Weird guy," Ray said.

Hannah pulled a hand through her dark, shadow-filled hair. "Weird how."

"Just weird."

Since he heard no siren sounds in the distance, Luke didn't feel any sort of rush. Calm spread through him. Alan had paid for all he'd done, and Dot considered Luke a monster for it, meaning he'd lost her. Not the outcome he'd hoped for, but it was an outcome, nonetheless. An ending, or perhaps a beginning. When he searched his feelings, a beginning felt like it fit better. He was in a new city, one that seemed to have a massive portal problem, and a sort of directive from Guide telling him to grow stronger. It was uncomplicated, almost free, in a way. Luke could breathe, anxieties melting away.

"How does that Peter guy know your brother, by the way?" Ray asked Curtis, taking Luke out of his train of thought.

Curtis shrugged. "The guild knows about other guilds, I guess."

"Your brother is in a guild?" Hannah asked. "Which one?"

"My little brother is in Phoenix Phalanx. Well, he's the leader of it, I guess."

"You guess he's the leader?" Ray asked, clearly taken aback.

"He is," Curtis said. "And he's a little shithead." He stopped himself and sighed. "Sorry, force of habit. He's not that bad anymore. A real pain when growing up, but that was a long time ago."

"So your little brother is the guildmaster of the most well-recognized guild in the city? One of only five guilds in the US? Can you introduce us?" Ray asked.

"Why?"

"Maybe he knows people who would fit in well with us?" Ray asked.

Curtis looked suspicious. "More people?"

"We'll need a full party," Hannah said. "And maybe some alternates."

Luke scratched his cheek, coming away with dried blood under his fingernail. "Might not be such a bad idea. If Phoenix Phalanx is so well regarded, maybe they do have people we could use. Alan didn't like them, so I'm sure I will."

"Such a contrarian," Ray sighed. "But I agree."

"That won't work," Curtis said.

Luke watched a small cluster of people hurry past, looking at him like he was a demon, and supposed he must look the part. "Maybe we should get off the street. You mentioned something about a shower and a couch?"

"Why won't it work?" Ray persisted.

Hannah jumped in. "They don't have members to spare."

"How do you know?" Ray asked, shaking his head as he gestured to Luke. "It's a well-known guild. They must have tons of people who'd jump at the chance to work with the most famous Lifeweaver in the universe?"

"There are six of them," Curtis said.

Ray blinked. "Say what now?"

"Only six of them," Hannah confirmed. "One party."

"About that shower?" Luke asked and found himself ignored.

"I'm not sure if that's stupid or awesome," Ray muttered. "Why just the six of them? Why make a guild out of that few?"

Curtis shrugged again. "Ian is a little on the grandiose side. Once he found out there were guilds, I knew it was only a matter of time."

"That's your brother?" Hannah asked.

"Yup."

Her eyes widened. "No way! Not the Phoenix Phalanx from Atlantic? He's Ian The Sneak?"

Curtis looked tired all of a sudden.

"Ian The Sneak?" Ray asked.

Luke shook his head, unsure of what was going on. "What's Atlantic? Atlanta?"

"You're one of those people, aren't you?" Curtis asked Hannah. "Yes, it's him."

She let out a squeak of joy, a strange sound coming from her. "Now you have to introduce us! I'm a huge fan of his stories!"

Seeing their confused looks, Curtis gave a deep sigh and then explained. "Phoenix Phalanx is the name of my brother's guild in a super old game. It was kind of a big deal, I think. Never played the game."

"What game?" Luke asked.

"Ultima Online!" Hannah shouted, the shadows in her hair wriggling with excitement. "The first and last good MMO!"

Ray made a face of disgust. "I've heard of Tibia. Is it like that? My first MMO was EverQuest."

The death glare she gave Ray looked like it could've turned a lesser man to ash. "No. UO is nothing like Tibia."

"Guys?" Luke asked, his entire body starting to itch from the dried blood. "About that shower?"
