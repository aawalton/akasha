Chapter 105

Splashing Slimes

There was a manic glee on Madame Voyou’s face, the type often used for when someone realizes they are getting paid to do something they would gladly do for free. A taboo that society would normally look down upon, but given this exact set of circumstances, they got to not only act on their deepest desires but also share their boundless enthusiasm with the world.

In this case, Madame Voyou got to play her violin, her notes were powerful and vibrantly crisp, each note causing the slime before her to ripple and crack its outer defensive layer. A layer caused by chemicals, but apparently able to be hardened by the living slime underneath who attempted to hide its body, its gelatinous core from everything else.

Unlike what I had expected, there was no floating core, no dense concentration of amino acids that were used to direct the protective movements of the slime. Instead, there were just floating black dots throughout that my mind associated with nuclei. A decentralized nerve cluster that could group together or break off and form individual globs of slime.

“Hahaha, dance for me!” Madame Voyou hissed out, her voice low enough so that only I could hear, but emphatic enough that the few boys in the front row who were close enough to hear her seemed to tremble with fear. Seeing what she was doing, I felt I got a sense of what was happening and raised my violin under my chin and prepared to join, then I did.

At first, I met her notes and cadence one for one, causing the clusters of slime on my end to vibrate at the same frequency. While this vibration was enough to clearly break apart any lingering shielding caused by the slime being exposed to acids and bases, it did little to affect the overall slime. Instead, the broken chunks crumbled inward, and seemed to be dissolved by the slime, which the slime then used to add to its overall size. Though the slime’s size was a lot smaller as if mass had been burned away somehow in this conversion process. Then without notice Madame Voyou stopped, and just as I was about to stop, she quickly raised her hand and gave a rolling measure letting me know to continue.

I just nodded and continued playing the same chaotic melody that was performed by Madame Voyou, but I added a few variations, speeding up the rhythm to see the slime vibrate faster, going up a pitch to cause the ripples in the slime to spike out in jagged patterns. While I first felt that Madame Voyou was a bit too enthusiastic, I had to admit that this process did seem to be fun for no explainable reason that I could tell.

“Now you can see how music can break apart protective layering, of the slime. In this case, we caused the slime to move so much that its entire defense is now gone. Now this is where you either need a magic user, or a second musician, or just one singularly talented musician. To be clear, you don’t need much more assistance, once you have the slime to this point,” Madame Voyou began and pointed at the slime that I was now causing to dance and shrink away at my constantly changing notes and tempo.

“With what I am about to show you, I will demonstrate why Bards are so sought after in the military, and why a good Bard will always have a place in a military unit,” there she was with that sales pitch again, trying to get everyone to go down the bardic path. Though I had to admit that this was fun.

“As a bard, you aren’t expected to fight, don’t have to join in at calisthenics as often. It is not your job to set up tents and the like. Instead, all you really need to do is to be able to prove you can clear a singular path through slimes. Again, if you can use magic in your music, then that is a plus and you will have an even easier time finding a permanent place in the military that doesn’t involve direct fighting.”

With that comment, I could see a lot of the students’ eyes brighten up at the statement. Seeing their reaction, it was clear that they all feared being in the military. I did too, as I realized that the time to join for service would be soon. Had I not already had my Class chosen for me, I might be in their same shoes, trying to realize what I wanted to do with my life. Rationalizing with myself that I was willing to sell my time for a future that wouldn’t be so bleak.

Even while playing the notes, my mind goes back to the realization that people who don’t join the Military or civil services in some way shape or form almost always get a Common class, making their lifespans infinitely shorter, along with limiting how much they can grow in the future. A slow, but subtle hint that unless you join society, you will be ground to dust. One that every student, regardless of peerage must be feeling, especially as we get closer to our graduations.

Perhaps I am thinking too much, letting my own thoughts and frustration at the state of the world that I am in bleed into my thoughts, which eventually trickles down into my playing, but I feel a deep impotent rage kick in. A rage formed from this world’s willingness to let hundreds of people die quick meaningless deaths, if they don’t appear to contribute to the war machine of the state. How everyone is seemingly expendable, until they do something for the military, or the government as a whole.

Madame Voyou is talking, but I mentally tune her out. I can’t listen to her not-so-subtly push her want for everyone to be a Bard on the students of this class. Mentally, I know she does this out of love. That she wants these students to survive past the age of forty with having at least an Uncommon rarity class, that allows people to have a regular lifespan. I also realize how hypocritical it is for me to be angered on their behalf, knowing that I have been granted an exceptional Class. A Class that I would willingly pass on, but I doubt anyone would have the qualifications needed to pass my class onto for quite some time. That is when I dig deep, and rather than complaining about a problem that seems too big to control, I instead let music be my outlet.

I am just one person. Yes, I can speak out and protest. But protesting just to say life is too hard is meaningless. Instead, I need to think rationally. Deconstruct the problems I see to their base roots, identify why these problems are handled as they are, then try to fix them. Only once I come up with a clear rational solution that works will my rage be meaningful. Only then will I be able to fix the problems that are so numerous before me. Only then with this rage that is burning deep inside me finally have an outlet, and only then can I truly relax.

Only after a few minutes of this building rage, growing within this teenage body that has more hormones than I can shake a stick at all rising and bubbling to the surface, do I realize that the room is quiet. Well not entirely quiet, as I can still hear the notes of the music I am playing, a variant of the cords from a violent protest song in my life. A song that spoke of rage at the injustices of the world, all converted to the crisp notes of the violin. My mind instantly making the bridge between notes used for guitar riffs and bass rhythms and merging both instruments into one harmonious mixture. My fingers moving so quickly and deftly that I can and do get the pace for both instruments to overlap with each other. Somehow the strings on the violin and my bow all manage to maintain their integrity, a sign of just how special this exact violin was that Madame Bloom had offered me.

Only at this point do I realize that I have been in the zone again, something that seems to come naturally to me while here in Madame Voyou’s classroom. Last time, I felt the sensation of singing. This time, I feel the resonance of my own violin, the way the notes echo around the classroom and reverberate back to me. My ears easily picking up the two distinct patterns of notes being played over top of one another. One the notes for the guitar, which are alternated one for one with the deeper bass notes, then back. My fingers are moving and it would almost be painful, if it wasn’t for the fact that I felt like I was finally releasing my rage. Releasing that pent-up frustration of not being able to do anything, of wanting to change the world, but not quite realizing how or what should be changed to make it better.

With this song, with music, I feel that impotent fire within me being focused and harnessed in a way that is impossible to express otherwise. I also feel that deep burning pain growing inside of me. This is similar to the pain I felt while at the arena, the one that came when I pushed myself too far. The one where it was only after seeing the attack heading towards Luna’s back that I forced myself beyond the threshold, beyond where I normally would have stopped. With that sobering thought, I realized that while I was experiencing pain, nothing truly terrible would happen. That if I continued through, it would be fine. The burning in the fingers of my left hand were nothing, just a minor trial to get through. If anything truly terrible happened, I could always heal myself later. But for now, I needed to get through this, to take all my anger and burn it away in this one constructive way, and be done with it, be done with the rage. That feeling of inadequacy that always seems to find you, regardless of how many lifetimes you feel like you have lived through. Even when my right arm burns, I consider it the good type of burning, the one you get from a really good exercise routine. While I had done more playing yesterday, today’s felt particularly potent. Maybe it was doing such a workout in consecutive days, or maybe it was my own body feeling the strain of too much repetitive movements too quickly, regardless of the reason I could feel that this was different. Maybe because I had more passion behind my movements.

Then I felt it, a sensation akin to glass shattering, or what sounded like glass shattering. Only after a second did I realize that the crisp sound of glass shattering was not inside me but coming from the outside. Pausing, I opened my eyes, my fingers going through the last few chaotic notes.

That’s when I felt the splash of a warm, but instantly cooling liquid on my face and hands. Then as if drawn out from a trance, I saw what could only be described as a mess. Deep blue soapy suds were everywhere, along with what appeared to be glass shards and fragments.

I looked around, seeing that the glass jar before me was completely broken and shattered, with a few remaining bubbles bursting and dissolving before me.

After that, I looked further ahead and saw that the case of slimes that had been in glass jars at the beginning of the class now had many that were broken and shattered, while others had pulses of slimes in various states of expansion and bubbling.

That’s when I turned to the right, towards a seemingly frozen Madame Voyou, who just stared at me with wide eyed wonder. Her outfit was now covered by blue bursting splotches of slime. Slime that seemed like they would act worse than grease stains on clothing.

Finally I dropped my suddenly tired arms that felt like they were made of granite inside. Then watched and tried to understand what exactly had happened.

I wasn’t alone, as it seemed everyone suddenly had questions. Questions I didn’t have the answer for.

“What was that?”
