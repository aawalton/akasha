Maybe it would be possible to reach Tier-3 and procure his first Legendary Card. That would change everything. It would give him access to the necessary power and potential to kill the gods.

As expected from Zayn’s strongest Spirit Soul.

Zayn watched the Roc Spirit for a few seconds as it shot up and dived down again. It didn’t pay much attention to the injuries it sustained or the Soul Power each dive drained. The Woodwalker Deer Spirit could solve that issue for the Roc.

It devoured the Demons alive and converted their bodies into nutrition for its body or fuel for searing hot azure flames.

The Colossus Pyro Slime wasn’t fast, but it was hard to damage due to its extremely high magical and physical resistance. Most wounds regenerated rapidly as well. The Slime transformed a portion of the Demons it devoured into Soul Power, further expanding its size before splitting up temporarily, spreading its terror in multiple directions.

Zayn watched his main Spirit Souls spreading chaos and terror, but he was also very satisfied with the damage inflicted by the Demon Spirits. Even the weakest Lesser Demon Spirit managed to kill two members of its kin before its Soul Power was consumed. That was all that mattered.
