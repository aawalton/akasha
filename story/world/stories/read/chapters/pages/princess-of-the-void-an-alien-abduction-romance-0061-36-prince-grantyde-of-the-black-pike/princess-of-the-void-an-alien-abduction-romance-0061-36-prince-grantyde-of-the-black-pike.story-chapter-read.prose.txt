Grant swallows. Prince Grant. He doesn’t even know what it means, barely understands its import. But the removal of that Consort is a block removed from the base of his self-image that topples it. You are royalty, Grant Hyde. Not just the husband of royalty. You are royalty. The college dropout son of a washed-up, dead-end outlaw motorcycle jagoff. You are a Prince.

The feeling is so overwhelming he doesn’t know if it’s good or bad. It just Is.
