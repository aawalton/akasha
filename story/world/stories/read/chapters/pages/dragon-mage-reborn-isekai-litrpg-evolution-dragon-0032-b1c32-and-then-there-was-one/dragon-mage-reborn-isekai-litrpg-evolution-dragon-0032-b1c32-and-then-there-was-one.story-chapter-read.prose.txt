[Spider Queen, Level: 60]

Scorch! I ripped out a spicy flame breath for the bastard and he immediately winced. When that symbiote-looking crap melted off of me, I turned to Nolan and helped him out too. Between those pincers was a circular maw lined with rings of needle-like teeth that rotated independently, creating a perpetual grinding motion. Above this horrific mouth, four piercing white eyes, two on each side of its gross head.

The creature’s head had barbed antennae that twitched and probed the air, probably sensing our every movement. This wasn’t just some minion—this was Arachnea’s personal guardian, a nasty abomination that had likely defeated countless adventurers before us.

“Freya, move!” Nolan’s voice snapped me from my horrified trance just as the centipede lunged. I darted sideways, feeling the rush of air as its massive head crashed into the spot where I’d been standing. The creature’s segmented body contorted with annoying flexibility, curling around to track me again. [Omachra Centipede, Level: 50]
