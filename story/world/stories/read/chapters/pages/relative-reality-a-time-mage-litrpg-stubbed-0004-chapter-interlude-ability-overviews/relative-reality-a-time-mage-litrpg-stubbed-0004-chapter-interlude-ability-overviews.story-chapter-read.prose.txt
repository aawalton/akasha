Elise

Race: Death-Tal

Innate Trait - One With Shadow

Can hide in shadows and resist shadow based attacks

Power Affinity: Stealth.

Element Affinity: Shadow.

Innate Abilities:

Innate Ability - Soul Reap

Allows the gathering of information from killing something with a soul.

Innate Ability - Strength In Darkness

Grants benefits such as resistance and healing when outside of direct light.

Innate Ability - Connected In Thought

Can read minds, speak into minds and resist both effects naturally.

Essence Abilities

Shadow Dagger - Body - Summoned Item - Stone of Death

A dagger that grants benefits to triggered attacks based on how long they have been active, or if the enemy is casting at the time.

Soul Curse - Body, Soul - Affliction - Triggered Ability - Stone of Corruption

An undetectable curse that deals damage when an attack is made.

Debilitating Strike - Mind, Body - Triggered Ability - Stone of Pain

Causes debilitating effects to the mind and body.

Cloak of Darkness - Body - Summoned Item - Stone of Cloth

Creates a concealing cloak made of darkness.

Silence - Body, Mind, Soul - Area Effect - Stone of Quiet

Silences sound in an area.

Elusive Repositioning - Soul - Blessing - Stone of the Tactician

Team-wide invisibility with speed and dexterity increase and a one-time burst of damage to the next attack.

All-Consuming Darkness - Mind, Soul - Passive - Triggered Ability - Stone of Defense

Damage mitigation while in darkness.

Rowan

Race: Displaced Soul

Innate Trait - World Guide

You can interact with items and people to learn basic information about them.

You have access to a personal map.

Power Affinity: Utility and Affliction.

Element Affinity: Astral.

Innate Abilities:

Innate Ability - Warped Space

An extradimensional storage space

Innate Ability - Flickerstep

Allows instantaneous movement every 3-5 steps

Innate Ability - Timeless Body

More resistant to diseases, poisons and the effects of time and time magic

Essence Abilities

Relativity - Body, Mind, Soul - Affliction - Blessing - Stone of Reality

Slow or speed up a persons stream of time

Reality Guard - Body - Summoned Item - Stone of Armour

Magical armour that makes attacks pass through it

Rewind - Body, Mind, Soul - Activated Ability - Triggered Ability - Stone of Renewal

Turn back time when activated, automatic activation on death

Titan’s Burden - Body, Soul - Affliction - Debuff - Stone of the Sky

Increase the gravity for a target

Twisting Mist - Mind - Affliction - Debuff - Passive - Stone of Mist

Blur vision of you and bend space to make attacks miss

Reality Command - Body, Mind, Soul - Summon Familiar - Stone of Command

Summon Fracture, a being from the Astral

Melody

Race: Erendel Human

Innate Trait - Human Aptitude

You’re able to adapt better to your environment

You have minorly increased resistance to most attacks or environmental hazards, as well as minorly increased damage dealt of all types.

Power Affinity: Support.

Element Affinity: Sun.

Innate Abilities:

Innate Ability - Afterimage

Creates afterimages that continue songs

Innate Ability - Sun Strengthened

Become stronger in the sun

Innate Ability - Musical Mind

Increased mental resistance when playing music and better memory for songs

Essence Abilities

Violin of the Sun - Mind, Body - Summoned Item - Stone of Music

Channels songs and decreases their mana cost.

Song of Power - Soul - Channelled Ability - Stone of Enhancement

Increases strength of abilities and reduces cost of abilities

Song of Peace - Soul, Mind - Channelled Ability - Stone of Calm

Reduces targets will to fight

Song of Mending - Body - Channelled Ability - Stone of Health

Overtime healing, targeting worst injuries first

Song of Rest - Body, Mind, Soul - Channelled Ability - Stone of Home

Regenerates stamina and mana over time, free to cast outside of combat

Afterimage Consumption - Body, Soul - Active Ability - Stone of Absorption

Consumes afterimages to create a burst of power, the effect of which is based on which song the afterimage is playing.

Helja of Dawn

Race: Avian of the Dawn Eagle

Innate Trait - Born to the Wind

You fall significantly slower than others.

You have increased resistance to air based attacks or environmental hazards, as well as increased damage dealt by any air based attacks.

Power Affinity: Utility.

Element Affinity: Air.

Innate Abilities:

Innate Ability - Air Magic

Control wind and imbue it with force. It also affects the soul.

Innate Ability - Flight

Limited flight at no cost, quick flight at increasing mana costs

Innate Ability - Wind Sense

Smell, touch or hear things with the wind.

Essence Abilities

Wind Rune Enhancement - Mind, Soul - Body Enhancement - Stone of Magic

Increased mana pool, mana regeneration when under open sky, and magic strength when off the ground. Rune magic enhances strength of abilities

Air Blast - Body - Attack - Stone of Air

Create a focused or broad blast of air from any direction at a target.

Gasping Breath - Mind - Affliction - Stone of the Ocean

Drain mana and air from a target, regaining that mana for yourself

Tornado - Body, Mind, Soul - Area Effect - Stone of Devastation

Create a powerful tornado that grows in strength over time

Wind Shear - Body - Attack - Stone of Sharpness

Create a blade of air capable of cutting magical constructs

Corporeal Air - Body, Soul - Passive - Stone of Power

Air based effects have more force, sharper dimensions and can be frozen in place

The Dawn Eagle - Body, Mind, Soul - Summon Familiar - Stone of the Dawn

Summon the dawn eagle that empowers your magical effects against undead and imbues them with fire.

Arne of Forest

Race: Avian of the Forest Owl

Innate Trait - Born to the Forest

You move through forested areas with ease.

You have increased resistance to plant based attacks or environmental hazards, as well as increased damage dealt by any plant based attacks.

Power Affinity: Defense.

Element Affinity: Plant.

Innate Abilities:

Innate Ability - Call of the Roots

Grow, control and grant sapience to roots

Innate Ability - Earthbound

Bind yourself to the ground, becoming unmoveable

Innate Ability - Deeply Rooted Mind

Have a strengthened mind which is harder to read or affect with powers.

Essence Abilities

Root Guard - Body - Summoned Item - Stone of the Root

Summon a root shield that adapts to damage and grabs weapons and limbs

Fiery Form - Body, Mind, Soul - Avatar Form - Stone of Fire

Assume a fiery form that alters abilities to become aggressive

Toughened Roots - Body - Passive - Stone of Stone

Roots are tougher, damage weapons and grant you mana when attacked

Forest Awakening - Mind, Soul - Temporary Summon - Stone of Sapience

Summon forest guardians that count as roots

Root-Bound Blessing - Mind - Blessing - Stone of Gifting

Gives allies and their summons enhanced toughness and slow repair

Form of the Deep - Body, Mind, Soul - Avatar Form - Stone of Water

Assume a watery form that changes abilities to grant healing effects
