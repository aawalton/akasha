Moxy’s definitely not trained in how to feed shards into artifacts directly from her core, but the ring was already fully charged, which means she should have a good couple of hours before it wears off. I think our best bet is to bring her back to the capital and hand her off to Donvath and his healers while we finish things up here.”

There was no telling what would happen if whatever toxin had infected Moxy got out and began spreading throughout the capital, and they were all sort of covered in poisonous blood. Once she’d been transferred to their care, Asher and Samantha Recalled back to the monster’s corpse.

“Thank the realms you had the foresight to give her that ring,” Samantha said, shuddering as she no doubt imagined the alternative.

The closer one got to the inner building, the denser the bugs became. “They stop projectiles from getting close to the building, and the distance is still too much for me to cover from within the astral realm… What do you think? I guess we can try just running through them and hoping that Polly’s potion is strong enough to keep them off us, but I don’t know.”

“Even if the potion killed them all, we’d drown in insect corpses before reaching the building,” Samantha pointed out. “I don’t know…”

It wasn’t until Samantha wondered out loud if Polly could create a potion to shrink them down and allow them to try to crawl like a bug themselves through the swarm that Asher had a breakthrough.

“I know how to get through,” he said, shaking his head as he realized what a stupid idea it was.

“You, uh, might want to look away.” Ignoring Samantha’s concerned expression, Asher pulled another one of Polly’s bug repellant potions out of his rift, upending the bottle over his left hand and thoroughly dousing his fingers with the entire contents of the bottle. As soon as he finished, he pulled out Crescent.

“Getting through the sea of bugs without them attacking us,” he said, surprised to find that the pain in his severed wrist was a lot less than he’d expected. He wasn’t sure if it was because Crescent made such a fine cut or because of his Complete Resilience, but either way, he was thankful for it. “Watch.”

Tossing his hand onto the ground, Asher focused on the severed appendage, experimentally trying to flex his fingers despite the fact that there was nothing connecting him to his hand beyond his own tritessence.

Remote Control - lvl 3

Sure enough, his hand moved just as easily as it would have if it were still attached to his arm, and Asher laughed as he managed to get his hand to stand on his fingers and scramble around like a freaky spider. As he darted his hand back and forth across the ground, Samantha’s face turned a slight shade of green.

“That is… really concerning.”

“Hey, I didn’t choose my blended skill, but I’ll work with what I have,” Asher said, focusing on the sea of bugs before him as he began marching his hand forward.

Thankfully, his plan worked perfectly. The bugs had to have been trained only to attack people or fast-moving projectiles, as they didn’t seem all that interested in a single severed hand, and the sheer amount of Polly’s bug repellent he’d doused it in ensured that any bug that got close died almost instantly. Despite losing sight of his hand the moment it entered the swarm of bugs, Asher somehow knew precisely where it was. He could even sense the surrounding area stretching out a few feet out from his hand as well, just like he could with an Eternal Mark. It took him a couple of minutes to cross the space between them and the inner building, but in no time at all, his hand was literally knocking on the front door.

Unlike the clinical workshop design of the King of Death or the bare cave filled with equipment that The Lost Lady worked out of, Jormon’s choice of interior design was a bit different. Similar to how the walls making up his fort were pure wood, so was the majority of his fort’s interior.
