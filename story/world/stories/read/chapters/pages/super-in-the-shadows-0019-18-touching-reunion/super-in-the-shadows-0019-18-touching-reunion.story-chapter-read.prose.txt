Jack clutched his head in pain.

It WANTED it, and he could feel it. This thing wanted him to help it take revenge upon the world. It wanted to bend him to its will, and it offered him all the power in the world for it.

His consciousness was getting hammered by dark thoughts. Kill! Destroy! Obliterate! Get revenge! Except, it was wrong; it was all wrong. It was missing…. CHEESE!

Just as he struggled, thousands of voices rose in protest.

They went, “Woo!” “Woo!” “Woo!” and even “WOO!”. Those were honestly the most inspiring and insulting calls Jack had heard in his life. After all, the wolves all resented the dark being.

What a cheap bastard! Its promises were even cheaper!

Immortality, they had it already! Infinite power? They were already following the Greatest General! As for revenge, they had sent the Goblins, Orcs, and the shitty worms packing already!

They were so fucking done with revenge that they all sneered!

Inside Jack’s mind was an earth-shattering scene as Shadow Wolf after Shadow Wolf appeared, all with the same haughty, disdainful expression toward that wind.

It was too much, their voices drowning it.

The discordant wind stopped. What had been a terrifying shout was quickly squashed into a whisper, and then nothingness. As for Jack, he stared right back at those glowing eyes.

“You promise a lot, but I doubt you can even deliver.”

“You think you’re some hot shot? Think again!”

“Not interested. Go fish, you idiot!”

SHATTER!

SHATTER!

SHATTER!

It all vanished: the wind, the dark thoughts, and the creature.

Jack was left shaking with remnant dread. Whatever creature was in there, it was VERY powerful for its consciousness to breach Grimdark’s boundaries, even while locked up!

Still, he’d be damned if he became a vessel for some mad entity! He shivered as he thanked god for the Ink Wolves. Without them, that would have probably been one hard fight.

Good thing their silly minds were just built different.

And as he heaved a sigh of relief—

“JACK?! Are you alright?!”

Lilly’s worried voice woke him, her face an inch of his as she gently shook him, trying to make him regain consciousness, and only once he gave her a reassuring smile did she stop.

“I’ll be fine, don’t worry. Just need a good night’s rest…”

“Ah, sorry for going overboard with your Quirk…”

“All good. Let’s go home, shall we?”

The return was uneventful.

A few minutes walk and the two of them were back home, with Jack making sure the door was locked a couple dozen times, even knowing how silly he was being.

Then, he slipped into the most comfortable clothes, wished Lilly goodnight, and plugged in his phone, his mind still buzzing with the earlier events, suddenly feeling pressured.

Wolves were great, but he didn’t fully know how to use his Quirk, which was a problem. Sadly, it wasn’t as if he could ask anyone. He remembered the System messages.

[Hidden Path: Became a Contractor!]

[Congrats on a New beginning!]

[Path of the Shadow!]

Hidden path and all, this would probably be hard to learn even at Legendary-U. Still, he wasn’t out of options yet. Some facilities focused on identifying unknown Quirks.

They would only give a vague idea, but it would probably help a ton in his case. He should probably check that first thing in the morning, right? Maybe play hooky from school a bit more.

He could probably claim he was still “recuperating”.

The training instructor would back him up.

But just as he had finally decided…

< Ding! New Message! >

< Ding! New Message! >

< Ding! New Message! >

…

It was his phone. It had finally charged enough for his messages to appear. On there, a familiar Shark avatar kept popping out over and over again with messages.

“JACK, I’M COMING BACK!”

“SEE YOU AT SCHOOL TOMORROW!”

“WHY?! WHY AREN’T YOU HERE? I’M FINALLY HALF-NOT GROUNDED ANYMORE. I WAS SURE WE COULD PARTY TOGETHER AND EXPLORE THE ENTIRE SCHOOL!”

“HOLY SHIT. YOU WENT TO TRAIN AGAIN?! WERE YOU A LEMMING IN ANOTHER LIFE?! HOW SUICIDAL ARE YOU?! DON’T WORRY. I’LL BE THERE WHEN YOU COME BACK!”

“ABORT! WORRY, I REPEAT, DO WORRY! HURRY A SHITLOAD! WE GOTTA PICK OUR CLASSES! GET BACK TO SCHOOL ASAP! DAMN AT THIS RATE THEY’LL PUT US IN A SEWING CLASS. DON’T DO THIS TO ME. I CAN’T HANDLE NEEDLES!”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The more he read, the more Jack chuckled.

Just picturing Sharky sewing made him smile, not because he couldn’t do it, but because he’d definitely eat the needle by mistake! And with this, he changed his mind.

“Forget Quirk analysis for now. Tomorrow’s… Thursday, September 18. Yep, might as well lock in the classes I want. I’ll go during the weekend instead, no rush.”

Hopefully, it wouldn’t be too late to get his first picks. There was one class in particular that he ABSOLUTELY wanted: Runic Magic, aka what Devil-Guy had been doing day one.

The whole thing sounded so fucking amazing!

Was it because it was powerful? No, but it didn’t require a Quirk! It only took Mana, and there were workarounds for that, albeit incredibly costly ones.

In any case, he grinned, already picturing seeing Sharky again.

He was already expecting the next day…
