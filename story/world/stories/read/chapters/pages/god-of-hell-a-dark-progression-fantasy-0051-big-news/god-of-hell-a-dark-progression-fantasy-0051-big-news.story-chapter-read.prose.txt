Dreaming, Scheming, Screaming. Unauthorized usage: this narrative is on Amazon without the author's consent. Report any sightings.Emma was never a particularly productive member of society, but she at least had her own little place in the world. That all ended when she died in the heroic attempt to get out of her shower, slipping and cracking her head open. When she woke up, she was in some mystical space between worlds. Clearly, hallucinating. But if her lot in life is now to imagine some Isekai power fantasy while drooling in a coma ward then that's just fine by her, all she needs is the chance to figure out her weird new powers, make the asshole severed head she's stuck with actually cooperate, and put together the harem of hot women she's entitled to. And, of course, keep herself from being employed, by any means necessary.

After all, this is an isekai, right? The world is all in her head, right? Everything is catered to her and her alone!

...Right?
