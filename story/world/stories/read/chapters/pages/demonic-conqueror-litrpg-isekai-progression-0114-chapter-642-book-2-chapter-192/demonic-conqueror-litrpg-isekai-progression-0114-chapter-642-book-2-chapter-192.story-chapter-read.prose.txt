It was an odd feeling, to be invited into a subterranean lair when he'd always needed to sneak his way in before.Simon and his Boon-Bearers were quiet as they followed Lady Aurelia to the basement underneath her castle. They couldn't discuss what they'd learned with Demons around, and even if they could, they probably would've needed some time to think anyway.Soon, they had reached the bottom floor. Two humans awaited them below.That in itself wasn't surprising. After Malruth's den of nightmares, Simon had steeled himself to expect more horrors from the Demon Lords he targeted. Finding some humans in cages, half-starved and mutilated, wouldn't have been out of the question.What he hadn't expected was for them to be hale and hearty, sitting at a table as they treated themselves to gourmet meals."There you are, Lady Aurelia," one of the humans said, with a sliver of irritation. He was dressed in flashy, expensive garments that instantly marked him as upper-class. "You've kept us waiting for too long.""Indeed," the other human agreed. "We are more than ready to witness this wondrous new development you've been acting so coy about." She was dressed in clothing equally as elaborate, but the style seemed different from what the man was wearing.Actually, both their styles seemed different from any type of clothing Simon had encountered in Valtia, even the nobility."Your patience is appreciated. It shall be rewarded – that, I guarantee." Aurelia put on a voice that Simon interpreted as 'customer service'. She was exhibiting cordial respect towards her guests, but not warmth or familiarity. After she'd established something of a rapport with the transmigrator and his Boon-Bearers, the contrast was easy to hear.
