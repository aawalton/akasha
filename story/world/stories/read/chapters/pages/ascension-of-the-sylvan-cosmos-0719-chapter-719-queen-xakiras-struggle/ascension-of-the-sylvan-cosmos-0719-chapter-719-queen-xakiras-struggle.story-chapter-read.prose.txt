Queen Xakira, as the strongest female hornet, naturally had endless suitors. With a mindset leaning toward reproduction, there were not that many conventional ties between couples. They had some warnings here and there as old habits resurfaced, but nothing worth punishing. Moreover, since they corrected it the moment it was flagged, no one pointed fingers or found them underhanded.

Queen Xakira looked at everyone present with her compound eyes without the need to move her head. Her gaze lingered on Xalis and Ignatia for a little while. She, not afraid or wanting to make herself look like a weak party, took the lead.

Xakira’s eyes flashed with surprise. Xalis was one of her few children who didn’t really care about the throne. She was someone free and unfettered, acting as she pleased. That’s why she was roaming the galaxy with her group of pirates. Sometimes, Xalis would be the savior, and other times, she would become a thief.

Xalis naturally understood that, but she didn’t continue, just shaking her head and becoming silent once more.

Ignatia jumped into the conversation at this time, smoothing things over. “While what Xalis said might be an exaggeration, I do believe that Lady Heavenly Willow’s existence can uplift us to become a fierce competitor.”

Flor blinked, slightly confused. ‘Why was I targeted by this Queen so suddenly?’

The reason Flor and I support her is, simply, because she is our child. That’s beside the point, though. We won’t be biased just because of that.”

A small white lie. Ignatia would, of course, always be extremely biased toward her cuddly big girl. Whoever dared to offend her baby would be met by her billions of ants.

Queen Xakira’s advisors frowned. They had been listening silently, but they were unable to follow the Ant Queen’s thoughts. For them, and almost any other Insectoid, Humanoid Ant Queens were arrogant, selfish, brutal, and with an insatiable desire to conquer and swallow everything with their endless armies of ants.

While Queen Xakira could tell that Ignatia was a very young Humanoid Ant Queen, she would never underestimate her. With a single wave of Ignatia’s hand, millions of ants would throw themselves at whoever she pointed at, killing them through sheer number advantage.

Even a Level 200 individual would need to tread with caution when facing a <First Ascension> Humanoid Ant Queen.
