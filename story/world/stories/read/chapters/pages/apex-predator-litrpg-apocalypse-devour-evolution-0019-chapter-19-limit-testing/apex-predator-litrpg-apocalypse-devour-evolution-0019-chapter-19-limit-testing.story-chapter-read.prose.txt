Don did not waste time. He quickly found another bear and started running it down. The bear was level ten, and was significantly faster and stronger than the last one, but with his superior tactics, he managed to kill it in a few intense minutes. The larger and stronger bear got a few swipes in and opened a nasty gash in Don’s shoulder, which made the kill more challenging.

Don actually had to wait a bit for the blood loss to kick in and properly weaken the beast. After delivering the coup de grace, Don breathed in the victory.

[You have slain [Blackmaw Grizzly – lvl 10] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 79 System Points earned]

Don promptly Devoured the beast and stacked up a Blackmaw Grizzly Evolutionary Trait and some Evolution Points. He wanted to waste no time. There was absolutely no way he was going to give up all his System Points. And he’d be damned if lost to a creep like this Jacques.

Don pushed on. He kept killing bears using his hit and run tactics. While he ran away from one, he actually managed to attract another bear. Don didn’t want to take risks, and he immediately climbed a tree and jumped from tree to tree until the bears were confused. While he was perched, he watched the bear he initially attacked to get weaker and weaker due to blood loss. When it collapsed, Don jumped down and finished it.

[You have slain [Blackmaw Grizzly – lvl 9] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 64 System Points earned]

Shortly after he tracked down the other bear. It was closeby, and clearly still looking for Don. Learning from his mistake, Don started to run in a wide circle, instead of a straight line. It was slightly harder, but safer in case he got unlucky and ran into a really high level bear.

Levels really made a difference. Don supposed it made sense. He was definitely stronger with each level. And as far as he knew the monsters were mutated beasts from earth. As he sat down to catch his breath after a third kill, he thought of Baxter. He really hoped he had either died immediately when the apocalypse started or that his amazing german shepherd was safe somewhere.

Heh, Baxter is sure to be a boss-monster. Nobody’s going to mess with him.

Don shook his head and took a swig of his water flask. This was no time to get sentimental. He needed to keep focused, keep competing, keep killing, so that he could beat Jacques.

Don got up and breathed in the rich forest air. His beard had started to grow out and his body was dirty all over. Yet, he didn’t seem to mind. It felt natural. Pathogens didn’t seem to be a thing for Don. He hadn’t taken an animal bite for a while, but he did recall them causing an infection. Devour kept him hale and healthy. He was lucky to have it, and he knew he needed to leverage it to beat Jacques. That bastard was killing humans, and the System was rewarding him with great amounts of experience.

Don would not stoop to savagery. But if he wanted to keep riding his high horse, he needed to do something drastic. Don wasn’t sure what level he needed to be to beat the level 15 boss, but he figured at least level 12. Jacques would get there first at this rate, having already two levels on him.

Don found another bear. Unfortunately the bear also found him. Until this moment, Don had leveraged his Stealth skill with every kill and managed to ambush the bears, getting a crippling blow on one of their back legs. No such luck this time. The bear rose on its hindlegs and roared. Don considered escaping. It was the wise thing to do. But maybe it was time to loosen up a little. Get a little creative. So he can behind a tree and waited for the bear to come. It did and tried to take a swipe. Don dodged and slammed his shoulder into the paw, pinning it against the tree. He grabbed one of the massive paws and pulled it down, while his free hand impaled the bear’s wrist with his black claws. Blood spurted and the bear struggled. But it couldn’t wrench the paw free from the awkward angle, and with Don pressing his whole body against it. The tree trunk was thick and Don curled up so that the Bear couldn’t get a proper swing in with its other paw.

The bear did the logical thing. It tried to bite Don’s face. Don grinned. It was exactly what he had been waiting for. As the monstrous jaws closed in on him, Don released the bear’s paw and jammed his other clawed hand deep into the bear’s throat. The jaws locked down and Don grunted in pain as he felt the bone in his arm snap.

[Health at 74%][Debuff inflicted: Moderate Bleeding, Fractured Bones]

The bear’s eyes went wide with shock. It tried to rip itself free, but now with one claw pinning its arm into the tree, and another one lodged into its throat, it was weakening severely. In its desperation, the bear thrashed and bucked, slamming its body against the tree. Don’s arm twisted and he gasped as the snapped bone pushed through the flesh of his arm. The bear was about to wrench free, Don could feel it. He pushed the arm further in the bear’s mouth.

He roared like a beast. Filled with fury, pain and an unyielding, absolute resolve to win, he reared his head back and snapped it on the bear’s snout. He felt a satisfying crunch. The bear reeled and Don managed to pull the bear into himself with his wrecked arm. He headbutted once. Twice. Again and again. He was only seeing red and feeling crunch and squish under the weight of his blows. Don didn’t stop slamming his head against the bear until a notification pulled him out of his rampage.

[You have slain [Blackmaw Grizzly – lvl 9] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 70 System Points earned]

[’DING!’ Race: [Evolutor (spawn)] has reached level 9 – Stat points allocated, +2 Free Attribute Point]

The bear slumped on itself and Don managed to pull his twisted arm from its mouth. Only the claws were intact. Rest of his hand and arm looked like raw hamburger meat. The snapped bones jutted out of completely wrong angles and he felt nausea overcoming him. The pain was exquisite. But on some level he enjoyed it. This was the price he had paid to take down anothe mighty foe.

Don breathed in the sensation. Defeating strong foes like this was the most exciting feeling he had ever felt. He hoped he could keep doing this forever. The pain and the struggle were a cheap price to pay for the high slaying such a monstrous bear gave him.

Sure, the System had yanked the human population out of their lives and put them in a hostile environment. Sure, it had turned men into savages and wiped out civilization that humankind had been building for thousands of years.

Don looked at the mangled corpse of the bear. It was a majestic beast. Easily seven or eight hundred pounds.

Maybe the System took a lot. But it also gave. I still can’t believe I can do this. Look at what I can do. I can fight off a monstrous bear, alone with no weapons!

Don was happy to pay the price. It wasn’t like he had cared for modern civilization that much anyway. Seemed like his want for a hot shower had lessened significantly because of the level ups or something else.

In fact, Don had never felt so healthy, so happy and so goddamn alive as he felt in the tutorial.

I guess I’m a bit of a wackjob too. I love this. I truly, genuinely love this.

But the pain was insistent and immediate. While it was definitely the reason Don could appreciate this new harsh reality that strangely fit him, it was also telling him to stop daydreaming. The nausea was mounting and Don was seeing stars. The System told him he would die of blood loss in fifteen minutes if he didn’t do anything.

In his mind Don thanked the bear for the great fight and used Devour.

[45 Evolution Points acquired!]

[Evolution trait Blackmaw Grizzly: Evolved!]

“Yes!” Don exclaimed. “Finally.”

He was fairly sure that at this point acquiring each Evolution Trait was harder than the last. That was fine. Don was ready for the grind. Getting a level up and an Evolution Trait would increase his ability to keep killing dramatically.

Don opened up the notification. His eyes flashed open.

“Oh… This is a good one.”
