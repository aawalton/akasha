"Fool!" a voice shouted from above.

A silver armored knight hung by one arm from a ledge at the top of the lighthouse. His feet rested against the face of the structure like some expert rock climber resting as they scaled a vertical cliff.

He pushed off and dropped, landing on a knee between the gorilla-shark monstrosity and me with a thunk and the clang of his metal plate mail. The ground beneath him cracked on impact and his Diamond cape fluffed to fall lightly to his back.

This wasn't just any random Reaper.

It was fucking Lancelot.

He peered back at me, his face unreadable—glued in that perpetual stoic frown—and lowered the visor on his helm. Then he charged at the gorilla, massive white scythe assembling in his outstretched hand as he ran.

The beast met him head-on. Or, would have… Except Lancelot was no longer there. In a whiz of nearly invisible motion, the Diamond knight was behind the enraged soul.

Aero Shift. He used Aero Shift! Except his was infinitely faster than mine. He'd even curved in an arc as he did it rather than a short, straight line. I could practically see the ghost of his movement burned into the air. Damn…

The gorilla collapsed forward, both legs completely severed at the shins, and landed on its chest. The only sign that Lancelot had even touched the foe was a single drip of blood that fell from the tip of his white blade.

Unfazed, the gorilla continued toward me, giant hands ripping up swatches of grass as it crawled, legless, like Anakin's desperate crawl up the volcanic rock after Obi-Wan defeated him. Except, the gorilla was faster. Much faster. And its legs were slowly regrowing from the bloody stumps.

I took several paces back, but the lighthouse stopped me from going any further. Nuzzle leapt from my shoulder and scurried up the wall to safety.

"Deserter," I mumbled. Not that we were truly in danger, now that Lancelot was here.

I examined the Sin, wanting to see what it was before Lancelot finished it off.

[Identity: Sin

Rank: Sapphire

Affinity: Life]

Life. That was Calliope's second Affinity. The one she said was what allowed her to bond with Allium. And it was the Affinity that killed him…

The Diamond knight walked casually behind the monster, tiptoeing around the trail of blood it left to ensure his pristine armor was unblemished. He raised his hand. A shimmer of yellow light like stars being born condensed in the air above the approaching beast, then coalesced into a giant sword. It fell straight through the gorilla's back with an audible shwing.

The Sapphire aura around the Sin faded.

Without a word, Lancelot proceeded to harvest the Will from his defeated foe. He knelt down beside the beast and rested a hand on its shoulder. He whispered words I couldn't hear and drew a symbol with his thumb on the back of the gorilla's head, just below the protruding shark fin. Then he slowly sliced his scythe into the center of its back.

I watched through the Second Sight of my dark eye as cracks formed on the dormant orb and the blue light flowed into Lancelot's scythe.

The body of the once impressive gorilla-shark-human hybrid rapidly mummified and disintegrated into bits of ancient flesh and bone. Where the stomach would have been, a horse head remained. Eyes still wide. Mouth open in an eternal scream, teeth exposed and shattered.

I had to turn away. Swallow the bile in my throat.

Allium… Fucking Allium… Why'd he run that direction? Why couldn't he have just stayed with me. Stupid creature. Stupid fucking creature. Tears pooled in my eyes. I didn't even know why. I had no personal connection with the horse. But just seeing it so… so hopelessly, brutally dead…

"Do you seek death?" a thick, heavy voice asked from behind.

I couldn't turn to face him, then I'd have to see Allium again.

"Unchained can kill Reapers. They have a sense for the Will. If you let them, they will break it and you will die. You must know this."

I didn't actually. Once again the victim of Jericho and Calliope's wonderful teaching skills. It wouldn't have made a difference for me anyway though…

"Why didn't you phase out?" Lancelot demanded.

"I can't."

A long pause followed.

"But you're not marked."

"I made a deal with a god."

I could feel him examining the symbol on my cloak, the lack of shadows beneath me.

"Then you're even more of a fool than I thought."

"Yeah. I get that a lot…"

He stepped up beside me, armor clanking, and put a hand on my shoulder. "I'm sorry about your horse."

I winced. Calliope is going to murder me.

Lancelot grunted and started to walk away.

"Wait," I said. This Reaper rarely spoke with anyone, yet here he was. With me. "You're not just gonna leave me here, are you?"

He shrugged. "Not my problem. This was already a mistake."

"What if another high rank Unchained attacks?"

"Not my problem," he repeated and continued walking.

"Just one question, at least. Please."

The Diamond knight paused, but said nothing.

I stared at Lancelot's back, unmoving. He hadn't left, but he hadn't said he'd answer my question either. This man was the second-highest rank Reaper according to Calliope. He knew basically everything there was to know about Penumbra. If anyone could help me, he could.

I took a chance.

"For this deal…" I started.

Lancelot immediately began walking away.

"No, please," I pleaded. "I just need to know how I can advance faster. That's all. I won't ever speak to you again."

The giant knight growled. Then, he whirled around on me, his entire bulk looming over me, more terrifying than any Sin. He brought his dark eye inches from my face. The Diamond iris shimmered bright, expanding to almost block his pupil entirely. The numerous scars littering his face threatened to burst. He examined me for a long moment.

I didn't dare move.

"I know who you are, Zephyr." He said the words with such intensity I almost flinched. I'd never told him my name. "I thought you'd be different. First Death Affinity Reaper in ages. You could've changed everything. Instead you want shortcuts. Sign deals with the devils."

"Hey, I'm not trying change a thing. I just want to get home. I'll do what I have to." The scent of his breath washed over my face. He was still inches from me.

"These…" He paused, gritting his teeth as if he wanted to say something, but couldn't find the right words. "These… Sins." He stopped again, reconsidering his words and backed away, allowing his body to relax. "Do you know the original use of the scythe in the Vitrial Plane?"

I nodded, perplexed and fascinated by his reaction.

"Harvesting crops. Gardening." He laughed alarmingly loud, and gazed back up into the sky where I imagined the sun would be if one existed here. His tone had completely shifted from aggressive to thoughtful and subdued. "It is no tool of death, but cultivation. We slaughter when we should shepherd."

Did he say shepherd? As in Shepherd shepherd? I suddenly felt uncomfortable at his words. What was he saying? Is he sympathizing with the monsters? The Sins?

"It's ironic, isn't it?" he said. "That the symbol of the scythe and its true purpose are at such odds? How do you think that happened?"

I just stared at him, my mind whirling.

"No matter. I've overstayed. And I didn't answer your question yet. I don't think I will." He rested a hand on my shoulder. "Instead, I leave you with this… Think long and hard about who you want to be, not who the gods of this forsaken place want you to be. Farewell, Zephyr."

He bent his knees, one leg forward, one back as if he were about to sprint, then exploded into the air. In seconds, he was nothing more than a pinprick against the blue sky arching away and into the distance.

I sat back against the lighthouse, watching, dumbfounded, as he disappeared from view.

Every second he spoke, my suspicions were confirmed further. I could barely comprehend it.

Lancelot. Diamond ranked Lancelot… is the enemy.

I was so historically fucked.
