The streaks of aura cut deep into Gary. Blood flew—lots and lots of blood. A few streaks missed him and pierced the wall against which he was backed. Cracks spread around deep, long, finger-wide holes. The streaks that hit Gary penetrated him fully and crashed into the wall and floor, not as concentrated as before. Stone chunks flew everywhere. Sand rose. The ripples pulled at the sand, raising it further, creating a tall cloud with the red tint of Gary’s blood. Even more blood stained the sand outside the cloud—so far it had been spilled.

Elder Sui fought to restrain his smile. It would be unbecoming. Finally, however, he couldn’t hold it. Well done, Duang! Not only did you not shame us, but you also earned us face. The sure-kill technique I taught you was demonstrated beautifully… Truly, you are an excellent disciple.

“The lethality of this technique is well-known, and it struck cleanly! Gary Steel didn’t even have his spear! What a convincing demonstration of the Sui family’s military might and history of martial arts! After this, anyone looking to defy them should think twice, because the fierce Sui Duang will only continue to grow! The winner of this duel is him, the water genius, Sui Duang!”

Many in the crowd cheered. Most disciples stayed silent. Alicia held her mouth with trembling hands, tears pouring freely. Cog shut his eyes and made a silent promise for revenge. Caramel hugged Alicia, while Pop Ear placed a hand on her shoulder. “It’s going to be okay,” he told her.

On the VIP stands, hushed conversations erupted everywhere. “It’s a shame. He was talented, but alas, a dead genius is no genius,” said the Branch Head with the very tall hat.

Elder Sui’s smile widened. And now, it’s time for the main act, he thought, gripping the item in his pocket. “A talented man fell today. It’s really such a shame,” he said aloud. “He never stood a chance against my Duang, but if his master hadn’t doomed him, he might have shown a better fight. Truly, the apple never falls far from the apple tree, and incompetence is hereditary.”

Everyone in the VIP stands and beyond it heard his words, including Colorful, but he pretended he hadn’t. There was nothing he could say. I’m sorry, disciple… The technique I taught you didn’t work. This is my failure, too… Perhaps I really did doom you… He sighed deeply, opening his eyes to look at the ceiling. Goodbye, Gary. I pray your next life is luckier.

Sui Duang himself, still on the arena sands, turned and bowed towards the VIP seats. “Thank you for your guidance, Master,” he said. “Let me apologize to everyone, too. I’m sure you expected a better match, but with greater strength and better guidance, there was only so much I could hold back.”

That’s right, Duang. Well said! Elder Sui thought, the edges of his lips curving up. “Actually,” he said, drawing all eyes. He slowly reached for his pocket. Then, he paused. “Hmm?” he said. His eyes widened. Everyone in the arena, who had been looking at him, followed his gaze.

Why is everyone looking at me? Sui Duang wondered. Wait, no. They’re looking…behind me!?

He whirled in place. His eyes went wide as saucers, and his jaw dropped. “Wait a moment, everyone!” Rollo Frollo said. “It appears the duel isn’t over yet!”

A trembling form could be seen in the clearing sand cloud.

Tattered clothes. Blood covering him and dripping from his fingers. Shallow, rugged breaths. Bloodshot eyes that refused to close. Gary Steel stood there, in front of a wall full of holes, grievously injured but alive.

Impossible! Elder Sui thought. He was hit by Meat Grinder! How can he still be standing? Don’t tell me all the attacks missed his vital spots!

Did I miss his vital spots? Sui Duang wondered. No, that’s impossible. I can see the punctures. His lungs were hit, and so was the side of his throat. How is he standing? Without realizing it, he took a step back.

“Gary!” Alicia yelled, but most of the audience could only smile sadly. Gary stood, but he didn’t look like he could even walk, let alone fight.

“He’s got his spear, too! The spirit flew it to his hand while we weren’t looking!” Rollo Frollo said.

Sui Duang regained his composure. “I commend you for your resolve,” he said. “You probably hope for mercy, but I must apologize. Our differences are unbridgeable. If I let you live, I would only be inviting trouble for my future self and loved ones.”

Gary chuckled wetly. He spat two mouthfuls of blood on the already red sands beneath him, then spoke in a raspy voice. “I don’t want your mercy…” he said.

“Then why do you stand? You can’t possibly expect to win,” Sui Duang said.

“I don’t want to win… I stand… because I want you… to lose.” His voice clogged up, and he spat a third mouthful of blood. “I will not let you get away. My planet… You will all… die. I… will kill you.”

“Woah, he’s muttering incoherently!” Rollo Frollo said. “Gary Steel managed to stand through pure force of will. He might not even be conscious right now! With those injuries at the Birth of Aura realm, who could be?”

Colorful’s eyes were shaking. He stared at Gary, not daring to believe. Please, disciple, don’t do this to my old heart. You can rest now. It’s alright. However, he didn’t speak any of those words. Instead, he yelled, “Gary!”

The entire arena turned at him.

“Make him pay!” Colorful shouted.

There came tense silence. Elder Sui burst into laughter. “Did you go senile? Make who pay? Your disciple’s a walking corpse! A breeze could take him down, let alone my Duang!”

Colorful didn’t reply. He stared at Gary. You have no hope, disciple… But if you insist to fight, then as your master, I must stand by you and support you until the end! Show them who you are, Gary Steel!

“Gary Steel! Gary Steel!” a lone voice chanted. It was Cog’s. After staring for a moment, Alicia, Caramel, Pop Ear, and Helen mustered their courage. “Gary Steel! Gary Steel!” they screamed with all the power in their lungs.

Their emotions were contagious. On a distant ledge of the audience stands, a bunch of people nodded at each other, then unfurled the twenty-foot banner they’d just rolled up. “Baldinator! Baldinator!” they yelled.

“Gary Steel! Gary Steel!”

“Baldinator! Baldinator!”

More and more people were drawn into the chanting. Soon, the entire stadium screamed Gary’s name more passionately than any other time, shaking the massive building to its foundations. The shaking was even noticed on the few floors above the arena, making people stare oddly at the ground.

Elder Sui tsked. “Idiots,” he muttered. “Why cheer for the loser?”

“Don’t get your hopes up, Gary Steel. That’s just their goodbye. You fought well. In honor of that, I’ll give you a warrior’s death,” Sui Duang said.

“Gary! Gary, are you there?” Goldie yelled in Gary’s mind.

“I’m here…” he replied.

“Please don’t leave me, Gary! You must win! I don’t want you to die! I want to climb the tower with you!”

He gave a blood-soaked grin. “I’m almost dead, Goldie. I only got up so I could die standing… But now, with all those idiots chanting my name, how could I let them down? I really have to fight again…”

“That’s right, Gary! You can’t give up! As long as you’re breathing, you must fight! Otherwise, who’s going to climb the tower with me? I can’t make the Network pay alone!”

Gary’s grin turned emotional. He raised a shaky finger to pet the spear in his hands. “You’re a good spear, Goldie. I’m sure you’ll find another wielder.”

“Don’t say that! Don’t give up, Gary, please, please. I’ll let you call me Goldie and whatever you want, and I’ll be the best spear there ever was, just please don’t die. Don’t stop. I’M BEGGING YOU, GARY, YOU MUST WIN!”

Sui Duang couldn’t hear that conversation. He only saw Gary grin, blood soiling his white teeth, then rub a finger on his spear. “I hope you said your goodbyes,” he said, lowering his voice to a whisper. “You don’t deserve a warrior’s death, Gary Steel. I should let you bleed out for disrespecting me, but the crowd must be satisfied. Farewell.”

He whirled his spear crazily, carving up the air. The slashes became ripples. The ripple realm appeared, quickly enveloping Gary. His already vanishing strength was further reduced. He could barely feel his hands and couldn’t even see what was coming, but he fell into a thrusting stance.

Gary had entered a peculiar state where he felt more focused than he’d ever been. The fourth song became clear. He didn’t know why he’d ever struggled. Right now, he felt he could use it directly, without going through the first three songs, but charging it up was way too slow.

“Fourth—,” he said, interrupted by his coughing blood.

“Meat Grinder,” Sui Duang said, slashing around again. Streaks of aura flew everywhere. The ripples redirected them. They focused on Gary, who moved neither his hands nor body to defend. Perhaps he couldn’t. He remained locked in his thrusting stance.

People averted their eyes. This time, Colorful didn’t. Elder Sui wore a gloating smile. The aura strikes pierced into the undefended Gary, penetrating his legs and arms, his torso, his head, his neck. He became a sieve. Sui Duang hadn’t aimed them downward this time, leaving the sand undisturbed so everyone could see Gary’s fall. The slashes flew into the wall behind him and doubled the number of holes in it.

Gary was half flesh half hole. He was practically see-through. Yet, throughout the maiming, he hadn’t screamed. He hadn’t moved. The strikes had been so sharp they hadn’t even pushed him back.

“It is done,” Sui Duang said.

“Incredible! Even in death, he refused to fall! He died standing! What a man!” Rollo Frollo yelled.

Sui Duang passed his aura field over Gary, finding no life functions. All that remained was the lightning around his spear, which he’d never dispersed. He shook his head and turned around.

“...song…”

A tiny whisper echoed. “Hmm?” Sui Duang said, turning back towards Gary. Sparks of black lightning played around the spear. Had the energy increased? Blood flowed from Gary’s half-open lips, as they moved ever so slightly.

“...lightning.”

Sui Duang’s eyes widened. “No!” he yelled, jumping aside. The Golden Bolt accelerated instantly. It didn’t need Gary’s muscles to move, just electrical charges—the charges that Gary had endured the full Meat Grinder to build. Though Sui Duang had seen the strike coming, they were almost face-to-face. The spear tore through the left side of his waist, obliterating it before flying in the distance. A massive hole was left on Sui Duang’s body, and as the black lightning erupted on the wound, his eyes widened to the extreme, and all his muscles seized. His hair fell like feathers.

In that moment, the same thought went through everyone’s stunned minds: the words that Gary had said before. I don’t want to win. I want you to lose.

“The son of a bitch. He actually meant it,” Pop Ear muttered.

Colorful’s eyes were shaking. What a disciple… I never should have let him fight. I should have taken him away. I’m unworthy, so unworthy. I cannot protect anyone! Tears came to his elderly eyes. Well done, Gary Steel… Well done… You showed this old man how it’s supposed to be done…

“He’s still not dead!” Elder Sui yelled. “Duang! Finish him and eat this!” Reaching into his robes, he threw out a green glowing pellet. Endless life energy filled the air around it.

“A Second Life Pill!” Rollo Frollo gasped. “That’s expensive medicine that can completely heal Sui Duang’s injuries!”

Under everyone’s gazes, the pill fell towards the arena and landed on the energy wall. “Elder Feng! My disciple clearly won. Deactivate the wall this instant!” Elder Sui yelled, but Elder Feng—the gray-haired, middle-aged woman—turned a hard gaze at him.

“The wall is attuned to their life force,” she said. “Gary Steel lives. When he does not, the wall will naturally deactivate.”

“What a bunch of bullshit!” Elder Sui said, swishing his sleeves. “You are letting a disciple die out of stubbornness! If you won’t take down your wall, watch me do it!”

A burst of fiery blue aura emerged from his sleeves. Before it could reach the energy wall, however, a multi-colored form got in its way. The blast of aura shattered. Colorful floated over the arena, pouring the full force of his aura onto Elder Sui’s head. He keeled over.

“Interfere again, and I will kill you,” Colorful said. Silence covered the arena. Everyone held their breaths and looked down, at the sands.

Half of Sui Duang’s waist was missing. He bled profusely, but he wasn’t unconscious. His hands spasmed. He tried to move, to finish off Gary, but he was paralyzed. Too much lightning had entered his body. No! he thought. Not like this!

Suddenly, Gary fell forward. Sui Duang’s heart soared. In this state, just the impact against the sand would dislodge all of Gary’s organs, and he would die. Even if not, Sui Duang suddenly realized he was panicking for no reason. Though the pain was terrible, he wouldn’t die immediately. Gary Steel would die first, one thousand percent, and then Elder Sui could feed him the pill.

Gary fell.

And as he fell, he grabbed onto the paralyzed Sui Duang’s hands. He managed to hold himself upright. The two were face-to-face, but the state of Gary’s face birthed nightmares in Sui Duang’s mind. How can he stand? he wondered again. He’s using me to support himself, but it’s pointless. Soon, the paralysis will wear off, and I will end him!

As he looked at Gary’s empty eyes, however, something shone inside them. Sui Duang expected to see unwillingness. He saw mockery.

What!?

“Lightning,” Gary whispered, so low that even Sui Duang could barely hear him. All of Gary’s remaining aura was pushed out. He couldn’t form even the most basic technique at this point, but he didn’t need to. He just vibrated the lightning in his body. It filled him—and, through his hands, it found a new conduit. It spread into Sui Duang’s body, arcing from one of Gary’s hands to the other, crossing Sui Duang’s arms and torso. The larger man seized again, stiff as a rock. More and more lightning entered his body, flowing by itself. Gary didn’t need to do anything anymore. He was barely conscious.

Smoke rose from Sui Duang’s wounds and ears. It rose from the top of his now bald head. From his eyes. He wanted to scream in pain, but paralyzed as he was, he couldn’t. Water conducted electricity. He was fried alive. If this went on, he’d just die!

“Stop that!” Elder Sui yelled.

“The duel isn’t over,” Elder Feng said.

“My disciple surrenders! He lost! In the name of the Sui family, let him keep his life!” Elder Sui commanded. In his mind, he added another sentence. This genius was entrusted to me. If he dies, I will be blamed!

“Does your disciple accept this, Vice Head Colorful?” Elder Feng asked.

“Do it, Colorful, or your disciple will die too!” Elder Sui said.

Colorful, however, only shook his head. “How could I betray my disciple’s resolve? I’m sorry, but at this point, their differences are unbridgeable. If he let Sui Duang live, he would only be inviting trouble for his future self and loved ones.”

Elder Sui shook from rage. These were Sui Duang’s previous words flung back at him. “Gary Steel! Can you hear me, Gary Steel? You won! Let go of Duang, and I will give you anything you want!”

If Gary could hear him, he showed no signs of it. His hands held on. Sui Duang specialized in Vitality—despite the constant electrocution, he survived, but that was more curse than blessing. His flesh was black and flaking off. His organs were showing. His eyes had melted in their sockets. The pain he was experiencing must have been torturous, but he couldn’t even scream.

“Gary Steel! Let go this instant, I command you!” Elder Sui yelled.

“A talented man fell today. It’s really such a shame,” Colorful said, shaking his head. “He never stood a chance against Gary, but if his master hadn’t doomed him, he might have shown a better fight. Truly, the apple never falls far from the apple tree, and incompetence is hereditary.”

This was, once again, Elder Sui’s own words thrown back at him. He grew so angry his face turned red and shivering. Blood rose to his mouth, and he almost spat it out before swallowing it back down. “Colorful!” he roared.

“What?” Colorful replied innocently.

“Look! The wall dissipated!” Rollo Frollo yelled. Indeed, everyone saw the energy wall over the arena fizzle out. “One of the two died. Who was it?”

Everyone held their breath. Gary was still a mess, and Sui Duang still smoked, but there was no telling who was alive from this distance. The Second Life pill that Elder Sui had previously thrown fell between the two.

Finally, Gary’s grip slipped. He collapsed. Sui Duang remained standing.

“Gary!” his friends screamed.

And then, out of some miracle, Gary’s fingers moved. Inch by inch, he crawled on the sand, leaving a thick smear of blood behind him. His mouth came over the pill. He couldn’t swallow it, but even putting it in his mouth activated some of its potency. His body began to heal. A gulping sound later—and many bloody coughs—the healing accelerated. A green glow engulfed him as Gary’s body reknit itself. The skin and fresh and organs regrew, new blood was birthed, and the wounds closed by itself.

“Would you look at that,” Colorful said. “A penniless old man like myself could never have a Second Life pill. I must thank you for taking care of my disciple, Elder Sui. To revive your disciple’s killer with such expensive medicine, your generosity is truly admirable.”

“The pill tossed by Elder Sui ended up healing Gary Steel! He was shouting threats before, but he accidentally saved him himself!” Rollo Frollo shouted.

Elder Sui realized what had happened, and finally, he couldn’t hold it back. A ball of black blood flew out of his mouth. “Colorful! Gary Steel!” he roared like a beast. “If I don’t have revenge for what you did today, my name won’t be Sui Deng!”

“I’m right here. You can try anything you want,” Colorful said casually, floating in the middle of the vast arena. Elder Sui shook with rage. He was red as a tomato.

On the sands beneath, Gary slowly stood. He flexed his hands, finding them whole. A deep exhaustion permeated him, but he was…alive?

“Well, that’s surprising,” he said. A golden snake flew out of the spear and fell onto his face. “Oomph!”

“Gary!” Goldie yelled, rubbing all over him. “You’re okay! I’m so glad!”

“Yeah, somehow,” Gary said. He glanced at Sui Duang’s blackened corpse. He’d died in agony. Then, Gary swiped his hand, and a wind of aura took apart Sui Duang’s body, spreading him as ash over the sand, leaving his family no corpse to bury.

“How dare you!” Elder Sui exploded. “This isn’t over! If you think your disciple is leaving this arena alive, Colorful, you’re deeply mistaken!”

“Oh yeah? Go on then, give it a shot,” Colorful said, standing between the furious Elder and his disciple.

“Hahaha, fool! I won’t even have to do it myself! Senior Inspector Whitefang—look what I have here!”

He fished a booklet from his robes and held it high. “This was found in the laboratory rented by Asim El’Shenawy, one of Gary Steel’s close friends! I have a signed testimony from the lab overseer! With this, his guilt is confirmed, as I said from the start!”

Whitefang didn’t expect to be called up. “What was that?” he said, rising from his seat.

“Evidence!” Elder Sui tossed him the booklet, which crossed the dozen feet to Whitefang under the eyes of everyone in the arena. Rollo Frollo didn’t dare comment anymore.

“Deng…” the Branch Head said. He stood up, hesitated for a moment, then finally sat back down. He sighed helplessly.

Whitefang grabbed the booklet and flipped through it. Meanwhile, Elder Sui kept speaking. “Everyone must have heard of the Muscle Sap ring operating on this floor. We found traces of it in our clan, and one of our main suspects was Gary Steel. We almost eliminated him, but I found new evidence! One of my trusted enforcers did a background check on him and found that one of his close friends was a Witch Coven disciple renting an expensive private lab—how suspicious for a drafter!

“When we raided it a few days ago, not only did we find evidence of manufacturing Muscle Sap, but also this booklet containing the signature writing of the Cult of the Broken Compass—the same people who were dealing the sap and have now fled the city. Gary’s friend, Asim El’Shenawy, is connected to the cult—completely reinstating Gary Steel as our prime suspect!”

Gary was absolutely not in the state of mind to think about these things. He’d just survived a grueling battle by the skin of his teeth. All he could think was, Fuck, we’re screwed.

A message appeared in his interface.

Colorful: Are you guilty?

Gary hesitated for a moment. However, if anyone could save him right now, that was Master Colorful.

Gary: Yes.

Colorful: Okay. Don’t move or say anything. Let me handle this. Today, even if I have to fight through the entire clan, I swear that I will get you out of here.

Gary’s heart warmed up.
