“I’d rather not lose,” James said quietly. “But it’s better to fight and lose than to be seen running away.”

The Martial Emperor let a slow smile spread across his lips.

“There’s the spirit I saw in your other matches, Fisher Emperor. Fight me, then. Show me everything you have. I’ll give you two minutes. If you can’t bring me to my knees in that time, I’ll take you down in one swift stroke.”

James simply nodded, then gathered a ton of Mana around himself. If Abaturov claimed to be immune to magic, James would make him prove it. Perhaps he was just immune to lightning. Physical blows had shown no potential to do damage thus far, so it made sense to test his magic a little further.

He used Complete Illusion Magic and began casting again immediately. Abaturov didn’t seem at all affected by the illusions. James couldn’t feel him shaking them off. It was just as if James hadn’t done anything.

All right, maybe it’s really true. Doesn’t matter, didn’t cost me much.

He converted the Mana around his body now into fire.

Flame Strike!

The Martial Emperor didn’t even flinch as the punch crumpled against his chin. James felt his knuckles shatter, though there was no pain associated with that injury for him. He jumped back as his hand recovered on its own, while Abaturov continued to remain standing in place.

If he’s literally going to do nothing, maybe I should just flood this place with aura and bury him in a swamp. Or charge the biggest magical attack I can.

But he sensed that the Martial Emperor had a comparable aura to himself. If James used Dominion again, after having used it twice already—and having endured such a vigorous aura tug of war with Chāo—he would be spent. It was unlikely to overcome his fellow Emperor’s power, and it might leave James completely helpless if it failed.

The magic option seemed even worse. None of his magic-enhanced attacks seemed to be doing anything more than a non-magic-infused blow of a similar type. The only magic he knew that might penetrate the Martial Emperor’s defenses was Soul Magic. But even that might not work, if his claim to be immune to magic held any truth.

If it did work, James would have turned this otherwise honorable contest into a death match. And if it didn’t, he would have attempted to murder an opponent who wasn’t an actual enemy, before the eyes of the entire world.

So that was out.

All right. What if I try to combine all of my less magic-reliant Skills into a single form of attack?

He activated Invisibility and watched the Martial Emperor’s eyes, which remained fixed on where James had been even as the Fisher Emperor slipped to the side.

Full Body Control. Proficient Silk Fabrication. Precision Gravity Control. Predator’s Venomous Armaments.

James produced spikes of bone dipped in the deadliest venom he could produce from his shoulders, arms, kneecaps, chest, and fists. The thicket of bone that covered him by the time he finished was almost an organic armor. It took ten seconds or so.

He generated a web of silk behind him, so thin as to be nearly as invisible as he was, yet stronger than steel. He used Precision Gravity Control to pressure the Martial Emperor and also to make his own body heavier so that his attack would strike with more weight.

Then James turned toward the Martial Emperor and took a step forward, activating two more Skills at once as he did.

Warp Step! Instant Acceleration!

Rather than just using Warp Step this time to teleport from one place to another, James used Instant Acceleration as well to get the maximum momentum he could in charging from his starting position into close range with the Martial Emperor. It was the same combination that he believed the Vorpal Wyvern had used to blitz attack him when he and that monster fought, before he acquired its Warp Movement Talent and these related Skills.

[Warp Step: With a single step, a single flap, a single jump, a single motion, move to any location within sight range in one movement. Consumes Mana and a minor amount of Stamina. Mana expenditure and results are dependent on distance and clarity of intent.]

[Instant Acceleration: Transform Mana into immediate acceleration in any direction you choose. Permits you to transcend the natural limits of your own Agility and move in ways that would otherwise be physically impossible.]

James had never used Instant Acceleration in combat, let alone using both Skills together, but he was desperate to find a way to attack that the Martial Emperor could not easily counter.

As James suddenly appeared beside Abaturov, his spear-like fist barrelling toward the Martial Emperor’s head, Abaturov reacted with actual caution for the first time in the fight. Clearly seeing through the Fisher Emperor’s Invisibility, he grabbed James’s arm, both hands around the wrists, carefully avoiding impaling himself on the venomous spikes that jutted out sharply everywhere, and grunted with effort as the point of the spine blades on the fist drew closer and closer to his eye.

Finally, Abaturov ducked down and managed to avoid being blinded by James’s Skill, the fist zipping past the top of his head like a stray bullet.

James tried to knee his abdomen, but Abaturov took that one without seeming to have the wind knocked out of him. He did kick James away, though. Even as he rolled away, the Fisher Emperor was pleased. He counted that kick as a sort of admission.

The Martial Emperor had told James to show him everything he had for two minutes. That, and the fact that he was now blocking, dodging, and counter attacking when he hadn’t before, told James that his attacks were beginning to feel threatening to his fellow Emperor.

A smile crept over James’s lips.

“Don’t get cocky,” Abaturov said.

But James was in motion before Abaturov could finish the thought, deactivating Invisibility and using Warp Step at the same time to zip back into close range and smash his venom-bladed fists into the Martial Emperor’s neck.

Again, Abaturov was able to avoid it, this time sidestepping, grabbing James’s wrist, and flipping him onto the ground face-first.

James kicked off the ground. The Martial Emperor released him almost in the same instant, flitting backward, and James activated his trump card as he moved through the air.

Wrath of the Predator.

Despite never having employed the Skill in a real fight since creating it with Skill Fusion, James remembered exactly what it did. He maintained a careful awareness of what each of the Skills he kept in his status was capable of.

[Wrath of the Predator: The wrath of a true beast is fearsome to behold. Your wrath is no less deadly. When cornered, or when seeking to finish a foe, the Predator can unleash his secret weapon. Wrath of the Predator lasts up to five minutes, during which time each attack by the Predator is more powerful than the last, and the Predator’s rage fuels blows that exceed the parameters of his normal physical and magical power. Consumes up to 25% of Stamina with each use, proportionate to duration of Wrath of the Predator. Duration is fully under the Predator’s control. If used for longer than one minute, Predator is required to wait for a period of time—exact duration dependent on resources consumed during use—before using the Skill again, or suffer potentially significant backlash. Consumes Mana dependent on the method of combat engaged in. May cause severe strain to the body due to exceeding normal limits.]

James let loose a bestial roar as he let rage took control of his body. The Martial Emperor shuddered slightly at the sound and accompanying pressure. And James recognized that his voice had contained a hint of the power of his Hypnotic Shriek in its wild, ear-shattering tones.

That’s right, tremble, he thought, baring his teeth in a savage grin.

A moment later, Abaturov steadied himself, and at the sight of that, James took more of himself back under his own, disciplined power.

Let’s go.

With a cold, controlled anger, more than a hot rage, James swung at the Martial Emperor, venom-bladed appendages stabbing and slashing at every exposed inch of skin, any place where Abaturov’s guard seemed weak, any opening of any kind that his predatory instincts could find.

At first, the Martial Emperor gave ground tactically, managing James’s offensive strikes as well as he had before.

But the nature of Wrath of the Predator was to grow more ferocious and violent over time. As the blows he had to dodge or parry grew stronger, quicker, and fiercer, Abaturov began to breathe heavily and to take the occasional hit from the bladed points. They left only shallow holes in his rugged skin, but those wounds slowly added up. James knew that he was injecting venom with each of these attacks, and he sensed that Abaturov was aware of it too.

James just had to keep pushing, and he would wear the Martial Emperor down.

At one point, James threw a full force roundhouse kick, and Abaturov intercepted it with his elbow. James felt his knee shatter, but he still continued fighting. His leg could hold itself together. That was the impossible might of his healing power.

The Fisher Emperor pushed Abaturov to the very edge of the ring, a triumphant grin beginning to spread on James’s face. He could only use Wrath of the Predator for a total of five minutes at a time, but the Skill had clearly pushed his opponent to his limits already.

That was when the Emperor blinked and said something James had not been expecting.

“Time’s up.”

His whole posture changed, and James sensed that the fight had shifted in an instant.

Rather than trying to dart back, James struck harder and faster than ever, jabs at his opponent’s chest that forced the Martial Emperor to take his first steps outside of the fighting stage.

But that only made Abaturov smile.

“You put up a good showing,” he said in a very low voice. “Now I’ll reveal my own special trick.”

The blood that had been streaming from the various wounds James had inflicted—too much blood for the shallow holes that those attacks had made, he recognized now—suddenly levitated from the surface of Abaturov’s clothing, from the little puddles and trace droplets that were spread over the area of the ring where they had been fighting.

That’s going to completely purge my venom from his body, isn’t it? James thought uneasily at noticing blood coming out of the Martial Emperor’s body. Then he gaped slightly as he realized that there was more going on.

This was a strange sort of attack.

The blood all swirled together and massed around Abaturov’s fist, and it seemed to congeal into a solid, almost crystalline form in front of his knuckles.

James swallowed, tried to use Warp Step to move back and get distance—but he wasn’t quite quick enough. The fist struck his head impossibly quickly, and he felt his jaw shatter under it. His neck snapped back so hard that James knew his spine was broken in places from the impact.

As James fell backward, he saw, in Abaturov’s grin, something that had not been there before. Small, pointed fangs. The Martial Emperor was a Vampire. That was his secret. Something neither James nor, as far as he knew, any of Abaturov’s other opponents had noticed or taken advantage of. That explained his impossible physical power and his ability to manipulate blood, if not his apparent immunity to magic.

I just need to try Solar Ray, he thought. That will reverse this.

He tried to pull the solar energy to his body and harness the energy that had already been inside him.

Before he could do anything else, a rain of hardened-blood-encased blows rained down on his face, chest, neck, and sides more quickly than he could react. He felt his head crack like an eggshell, blood and brains beginning to leak out, ribs smashing to pieces again, spine fracturing. Then James knew nothing.
