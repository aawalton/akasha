Kyle felt the first disruption to DeRosa’s aura, but dismissed it as he carefully carved away the tendrils of corruption nearest to Skippy’s heart.  The stubborn fungi latched on to Skippy’s vital energy, and Healing Impetus had provided them with an even greater feast.  It took absolute focus and precision to only strip away the corrupted flesh, but he’d finally cleared it out.  Just in time, as a moment later the cavern shook once more.  The oppressive domain that DeRosa maintained shattered, and Kyle’s heart soared in triumph.  Then, C.H.A.D.D. spoke.

[DR. MAYHEW, MS. TILLIENNE IS IN GRAVE DANGER.]

The drone projected an image of Tillienne, highlighting a growing web of corruption expanding from a point in her lower-left shoulder.  And it was spreading fast.  Sparing one more look at Skippy’s unconscious form, Kyle sealed him within a cocoon of vines, then exploded out of the makeshift shelter.  Without DeRosa’s amplified domain pressing down on him, Kyle expanded Aura Force and the Devourer’s Domain to their full potential.  He saw Tillienne, standing amidst the ruined pieces of the amplification pillar, opposing a fungal horror with its back turned to him.

Kyle didn’t need to see the creature’s face to know that this was what DeRosa had become.  Anger burned in his chest at the sight, memories of his lost friends coming unbidden to mind.  Anger, and regret.  If Kyle had only put DeRosa out of commission when he had the chance back on Earth, none of this would have happened.  His naivety cost thousands upon thousands of lives.  And now, it was time to bring the madman to his end.

With a surge of Willpower, he smashed down with Aura Force.  The writhing fungal tendrils were pounded into pulp as a violet barrier emerged around the creature, blocking the impact.  A second, then third wave of force slammed into the barrier, leaving spiderweb cracks all across it.  At the same time, Kyle created a small barrier around the now-unconscious body of Tillienne, shifting her away from the battlefield.  She’d need attention quickly, but Kyle knew that this wasn’t a fight he would win if he was distracted.

The creature within the violet barrier turned to face him, malevolent eyes locking on to his.

Once again, a Mayhew has taken it upon themselves to ruin my day, the voice rasped.  It raised a clawed hand toward him, releasing a condensed beam of mana.  Kyle flew beneath it, not slowing his approach as he continued to focus on his domain skills.  DeRosa’s voice was still recognizable despite the changes, reminding Kyle of the first time he’d heard it through a communication array so many years ago.  Back then, Kyle had been so impotent, forced to escape Nierburg.  He’d truly thought that he could earn his way back into DeRosa’s good graces, given time.  How foolish he’d been.

DeRosa’s voice rang out once again, a sneer in his tone.  Either way, it matters little.  Your story will end here.

As he spoke, a cloud of noxious yellow spores began to rain from the ceiling of the cavern, filling it with acidic mist.  As it closed in on Kyle, he finally pushed Devourer’s Domain, seizing control of the area around him.  The spores descending toward Kyle blackened and withered, falling harmlessly to the ground.  Simultaneously, Devourer’s Domain ripped away the mana empowering the violet barrier.  It wasn’t enough to entirely dispel the defensive skill, though the follow-up blast from Aura Force managed to shatter what remained.

DeRosa rocked back, not having taken any meaningful damage, but clearly surprised by the combination.  It was the opening Kyle was looking for.  Model: Shrike immediately appeared on Kyle’s left arm, and he launched the initial crescent wave of energy horizontally, right at the top of Derosa’s legs.  The monster was surprisingly nimble, managing to leap over the first swing.  Not altogether unexpected, and a second blade of mana was already flying at an angle, ready to cleave DeRosa in two.  Another barrier of violet energy materialized around the fungal abomination, but Devourer’s Domain was already greedily drawing the mana out, weakening the shield just before the blade struck.

The mana barrier shattered as the energy blade cut deeply into DeRosa’s torso, nearly bisecting him.  Kyle felt a rush of triumph, only to be forced to dodge as another concentrated beam of mana lanced toward him from DeRosa’s claw-tipped hand.  By the time Kyle refocused, the grievous wound from Model: Shrike was already healed, writhing fungal tendrils knitting the body back together as though nothing happened.

You truly have no idea what you’re up against, do you?  DeRosa taunted, releasing a cascade of shimmering, violet orbs.

Kyle pushed to disperse them with Devourer’s Domain, but found them far more difficult to influence than he expected.  The moment cost him, and the orbs fired more concentrated lances of mana directly at Kyle, forcing him back slightly as he avoided the beams.  That bought DeRosa enough time to land back on the cavern’s floor, where his thick legs seemed to meld into the fungal mass.

I have ascended; become something that none other from our old world ever could have fathomed.  I tasted perfection, perfect harmony, and allowed others to participate in my utopia.

Another lance of purple light flew toward him, just as C.H.A.D.D. warned Kyle of approaching projectiles from behind and a blade of stone rising from below.  A shockwave of force ripped out from Kyle in every direction, disrupting the attacks.  The projectiles behind him exploded in a cloud of spores, which were once again immediately neutralized by Devourer’s Domain.  Kyle felt a phantom itch in his left shoulder, which he pushed away.  Soon, he thought.  His special preparations for this fight were ready, but he’d only get one shot.

And yet you fools couldn’t see the vision.  At every turn, you sought to undermine my life’s work.  You and your simpleton colleagues cost me years of effort and planning, and for what?  To die here, on this rock, with nothing else to show for it?  You couldn’t have simply stayed away?

Kyle gritted his teeth, flying toward DeRosa once again.  The madman skated across the fungal ground with astonishing speed, keeping respectable distance with a constant barrage of ranged attacks.  For his part, Kyle made it a point to dodge or block everything he could without relying on bigger skills.  He knew Tillienne was on a timer, just as he understood that both she and Skippy were weaknesses DeRosa would gladly take advantage of.  Taking any pressure off of him to treat one could give the fungal horror an opening to attack the other.

Even as it was, Kyle had to reposition both Skippy and Tillienne several times as DeRosa launched lances of energy their direction, causing Kyle to take several glancing blows.  Each of the beams had enough power to break through his skin, even with Genesis Engine active, and brought with them pockets of the anti-healing spores.  Under ordinary circumstances, it would have been a strong countermeasure to somebody like Kyle.  Now that he expected it, however, he simply focused on Devourer’s Domain and destroyed the spores before they had a chance to find purchase in his body.  DeRosa seemed to notice this, and adapted accordingly.  Soon, his attacks no longer carried the spores, instead packing more pure destructive energy.

Why do you insist on being such a thorn in my side?  DeRosa roared.  Answer me, Mayhew!  The voice was louder now, shaking the fungal walls as DeRosa continued to bombard Kyle with mana lances and explosions.

But Kyle didn’t respond.  The rage still burned hot in his chest, but he pushed it down as best he could.  He simply kept chasing, slowly gaining on DeRosa as his plan came together.  At the surface, DeRosa was coming out slightly ahead in their exchanges.  The fungal abomination constantly put up barriers that blocked Kyle’s blasts of force, and replenished them whenever Devourer’s Domain stripped them away.  Meanwhile, Kyle was slowly accumulating damage as he took glancing blows from DeRosa’s bombardment.

However, that equilibrium was about to change.  Through the course of their fight, Devourer’s Domain slowly gained influence within the cavern.  Phaegos had not been idle since reviewing the skill options with Kyle, and had been refining its ability to incorporate what it learned from seeing both Reaper’s Resonance and Resonant Creation.  Every place they passed, he left small traces of conceptual Death lying dormant as his mana gradually infused the area, just waiting for his command.  The walls and floor formed a web of Kyle’s design, and the moment Victor crossed back over one of the lines, Kyle struck.

The whole cavern shuddered and slumped as the walls and floor immediately rotted, Kyle no longer holding anything back as he flooded the area with the deadly concept.  DeRosa was halted in his tracks, the high-speed movement technique he was using being entirely broken as the cavern began to rot.  DeRosa immediately moved his energy to put up another barrier, but by now Kyle was familiar with how the defensive skill worked.

Kyle winced in pain as the conceptual Death flooded out of him and into DeRosa, disrupting the barrier and causing one of the abomination’s arms to rot away entirely.  The moment it happened, Kyle activated Aura Force, this time pulling DeRosa directly toward him as he brought out his special prosthesis.

Just before DeRosa was dragged into range, his body seemed to explode with violet light.  A vortex of energy surrounded him, managing to counter Aura Force as everything within a dozen meters of DeRosa was pushed back.  The cavern’s ceiling was torn open even further by the massive release of energy, and soon Victor was floating in the eye of a tornado of mana, sharp stones and fungal remains swirling around him in a helix.

I never expected you would have pushed me this far, DeRosa’s voice rasped out above the thunderous din of his skill.  I’d hoped to use your body as the foundation for my rebuilding, but it appears that will not be an option.  Now, die.

The moment he finished speaking, hundreds of stony spears were sent flying directly toward Kyle and the unconscious bodies of Skippy and Tillienne.
