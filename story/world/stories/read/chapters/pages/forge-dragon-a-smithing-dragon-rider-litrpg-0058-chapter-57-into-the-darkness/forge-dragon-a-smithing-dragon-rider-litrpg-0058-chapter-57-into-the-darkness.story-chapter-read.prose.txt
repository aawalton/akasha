“You selfish bitch! I was going to kill you before you went crazy, only now father will commend me for it.”

Caleb heard Mettius’ curse as he entered the darkness. The fight had begun with witty banter on his part to keep his conversation based buff active, but as it had raged on it had devolved into insults.

“May he reign forever,” Cassandra said in a mocking tone.

The voice came from all around in the darkness.

Caleb was blind but used his metal and wind sense to get a vague idea where Mettius was. He stood in the center of the darkness, spinning around and disturbing the air enough for Caleb to see his whole body, while his pauldrons and gloves pinpointed the locations of his shoulders and hands.

Through these senses he could tell that Mettius was fully on the defensive. He stood hunched forward, hands waving about rapidly and spinning around in all directions. Caleb made his way toward Mettius through the darkness until suddenly the darkness was gone and he found himself in an extremely dim bubble a meter across with Mettius in the center.

The other man looked haggard. Potion vials littered the floor beneath him and he was bleeding from dozens of slashes all over.

“Caleb!” Mettius said, surprised at his sudden appeared. He recovered quickly and commanded, “Focus on your mana pool. Press it out as far as you can and make push out any of Cassandra’s magic!”

Caleb understood immediately what this bubble free of darkness was. He turned his senses to his mana pool and saw that Cassandra’s mana had intruded into his own. He pushed and after a brief contest of wills he won out and the darkness around him vanished. Then he pushed his mana pool outward, expanding it from the half meter diameter it had been to a circle a full three meters across.

This brief exchange gave Caleb insight into the resistance he’d been feeling when trying to move metal on other people. His mana had to fight through their pool, and it became a contest of willpower—a contest Caleb could win against anyone his level, but it still cost him some of his power to do so.

“About time” Mettius said, and Caleb felt as Mettius’ mana pool—now within his own—collapsed to its own normal half meter diameter.

The effect on Mettius’ condition was immediate. He stood taller and immediately spun around to cast a Shield at an encroaching spike of darkness.

Caleb had seen the spike appearing, but it had moved so fast he’d have been unable to dodge it.

“I can keep us alive if you can find her and kill her,” Mettius said, in words only Caleb could hear as he used to his skill to taunt her.

“So brave of you to walk into the darkness to die with my dear little brother,” Cassandra said, ignoring Mettius’ taunt. “Your steel affinity might have been enough to deal with poor Rodger, but it won’t help you here. I’m above such paltry metals.”

Caleb sent out his senses and found no metal in the immediate area he could confidently say was Cassandra and for a moment he feared she was right. Right up until the next attack came. Another spike manifested in the darkness to Caleb’s left, and he saw it in time to push off the door hinges of a building within range to narrowly push himself out of the spikes path. He’d been tipped off by its appearance by the sudden sense of a variety of both mundanely and magically dense metals—none of which had any steel in them.

As soon as the spike vanished, so did the sense of the metal. Mettius fired a force bolt after the spike but if it hit, Caleb couldn’t tell.

“You’re a quick one,” she said playfully. “But I’m sure that frost druid knows that already.” [PL1]

She doesn’t know I have a general metal affinity, Caleb realized. How rare are these that she never even considered it? I can use this.

“I need metal,” Caleb said. “She has to be out there in the darkness somewhere. Follow me and keep me from getting spiked.”

Caleb activated his Iron Vortex, taking a mana potion offered to him by Mettius, and begun to spin the wind around him. Cassandra appeared again, but this time Mettius, enhanced with his haste, was able to block it for Caleb. Arrows picked up off the ground began to spin around him but he doubted he’d be so lucky as to hit Cassandra with one.

He walked carefully down the street, looking for more arrows and other metal debris from the battle. When he got near buildings, he ripped the muntins out of the glass, joining them to his storm. Some of the metal pieces meant to give the windows support took their glass with them and he had glass shards join his vortex of death. As he ran building up his arsenal, he searched the rooftops with his senses. He briefly thought he saw her in his wind sense, up on roofs, but as he got closer realized it was only another dark archer occupied in battling Kelia.

Caleb longed to help her by ripping the arrows from his quiver, but he couldn’t risk Cassandra realizing he had a means to find her.

Caleb continued to walk briskly down the streets of the village with Mettius in tow, Cassandra’s attacks becoming less frequent as she had to dodge the debris of his vortex, but each strike came quicker and they began to get through their defenses. Mettius began timing his counter attacks better and Caleb sensed he was making contacts through his metal sense, but some rare metal covered Cassandra’s body and the bolts of magic that hit it vanished into the metal. Through his sense he could tell that the magic was empowering her, but he didn’t dare tell Mettius less he tip his hand. Each man gained another dozen cuts before she finally made her mistake.

After a strike, she appeared on his senses just above him on a nearby roof. He stopped his run and used Jump, sailing high into the air up out of the darkness. The surprise was evident on Cassandra’s face as Caleb appeared before her on top of a two-story building, but he reacted quickly. Tendrils of darkness shot up after Caleb to pull him back down.

Caleb used both Imperiums [PL2] in tandem to pull Cassandra toward him. A gust of with appeared behind her with the force of a hurricane while he pulled on all the gold, silver, and magical metals armoring her body. Her surprise widened as she realized what was happening and he felt as her will began to actively fight against his own. Despite her efforts, she couldn’t cancel out the force entirely and she was pulled toward the edge just as Caleb was pulled toward her.

He drew his sword and slashed down at her. She tried to draw her own rapier as soon as she saw him appear above the shadow bubble, but the sudden force of Caleb’s magic took her by surprise and her blade was ripped from her hand.

The blade came at Caleb under the full force of his Imperium and pierced him straight through his unarmored leg. He could have deflected it but it would have risked him losing his focus on what he needed to do.

Caleb reached Cassandra on his downward arc, just as she began to fall over the edge of the building. At the last instant he released his imperium and put all his stamina and mana into an Arcane Power Attack. He didn’t know what her armor was made of, but he knew that all metal could break with enough force.

His spatha reinforced with his Imperium under his own control, crashed into her back across her golden cuirass. His blade met the magically enhanced armor and he had to fight with the full force of his will for his blade to keep some semblance of an edge. Through the combined might of his willpower and skill, he tore through the armor, the blade continuing through her body beneath, cutting her in two.

Her darkness fell apart, folding into itself revealing Mettius down below. Reacting quickly thanks to his Haste spell, Mettius fired a bolt of force at his falling sister, the bolt entering from the bottom of her torso and ending her a heartbeat before she would have died from being cut in half.

Caleb felt the essence flood from her corpse into himself, and then something inside him clicked.
