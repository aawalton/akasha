Dear Diary,

I think I may have to subcontract the execution of these fuckin' Spartans. Either that or resign myself to the fact that when I catch them, I'm dumping a bunch of guilt on my plate that I'll need to work through.

Seriously, I'm still not fully copacetic with what happened with those Spartans and that one Athenian over in Europa. Yeah, they definitely deserved death, and I was the one there willing and able to carry out the execution. Maybe they even deserved a painful death, although as I've mentioned before, there's a kind of, I dunno, trauma karma that happens to the person deliberately handing out that kind of hurt.

Strange, I feel worse about those Spartans than I do about Diana. I guess part of that is because at the end of the day I Revived her, with another big part being how she basically told me she deserved it. I think some of it, though, is that I wasn't pissed at her when I did it. Everything I did was calm, collected, and fully controlled. At this point, I don't even really feel guilty about it. Horrified, traumatized, yes, but not guilty. I'm not sure I ever really felt guilty, really.

That's something I've got a big problem with, maybe. Then again, I'm pretty sure I'm not the only one. When I get all up in my feels, I can't separate them. They're overwhelming, and I can't even identify them, let alone process them. That's when I lash out. Well, okay, 'lash out' isn't the only thing that happens. I'm pretty sure I've had similar situations with positive emotions, like on our wedding night. Yeah, some of what we did was just fun, but other stuff was kinda over the top. I wasn't angry at all at Marie, and I definitely don't feel guilty, but I fuckin' cracked the solid stone of the mountain granulating my Murder Mittens pelvis. She wanted it, and we both got the kind of over the top climaxes out of that shit poets write about as life changing.

I mean, she hadn't birthed kittens before and now she has, so that's kind of a life change, but it's not like she hadn't adopted five kids plus Isnomi and Ria. She was definitely already a mom.

But swinging back from my daily diversion, I'm thinking I feel bad about the Spartans not because I killed them, because they deserved that shit, and I may be one of the few people on the planet who can reliably execute Spartans. Not even really because of how I did it, even though how I did it was especially horrifying. But like I said, maybe people intending to rape as a systematic cultural thing deserve some horrific shit happening to them.

No, I think the biggest single reason why I feel bad about what happened there, what happened at the Battle of the Walls, fuck, even what happened with the Mother of Water Panthers, was all about losing control. About making what I did a thoughtless reaction instead of a thoughtful, justified reaction. I mean, my old ROTC teachers talked about acting rather than reacting, and I guess I'd like to do more acting, but that's what I do on Fridays, whether that's communing with my Worshippers or teaching Math to kids. But that's neither here nor there, the thing I'm trying to keep centered in my brain is the difference between doing awful things to people who desperately deserve it because I'm the one with the power to do so, and that makes it my responsibility, and doing awful things to people who desperately deserve it because I'm too pissed too think straight.

Yeah, I fobbed Loviatar off on the crew of the Black Dragon, but then that was kind of a sudden bout of fear and anger. I don't think I would have gone over the top with her; I'd have just killed her fast and hard. On the other hand, what I did to Lyssa was...

Okay, now that I'm thinking about it with something like a clear head, or at least a head free of irrational self-hatred, I realize that I wanted to do all that to myself, but I really can't. Suicide is not only not an option for me, what with my existence powering the growth of what might be the first step out of dystopia here and now, it's also not something I really want to do. Yeah, I kinda tried it while I was down there hiding at the bottom of the M-Space ocean. But just because I hit a level of self-hatred where I wanted to end myself doesn't mean that when I'm not at that low I think it's a good idea. Shit, I don't even really want to. I've got too much in life that ranges between 'good', like all the food my Wifiest of Wifes makes, to great, like the adult fun time my partners and I indulge in on the regular, to awesomesauce dipped in pure raw serotonin, like time spent with any or all of the fam just vibing.

Weird, I think right now the cortisol is flowing, but much like Lyssa I've got an external target for the hate, and even at my most levelheaded they still deserve death or worse.
