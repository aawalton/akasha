Luke stood there ominously, staring at the pedestal, still deciding on what his next plans would be.

He tried to scratch his chin instinctively with his left hand. Oh, he forgot it was gone; it was about time he took it easy. All that fighting had gotten to him way too much. Having spent a short time here, but gained more scars in the last week than in his entire lifetime, almost dying three to four times.

He struggled to recall the tranquility he had possessed a short time prior. Staring off into the distance, where his only source of entertainment was those souls in the sky.

Wondering the effects of not having a left arm would do to him

I can’t do a one-arm pushup, he thought while sighing.

He could at least try it. Gently descending to the floor, he aimed to avoid making his injury worse.

Maybe this isn’t the smartest thing I could be doing with my time.

In the last fight, he hadn’t even cared one bit about pain.

Once in a stable pushup stance with a broad base, he prepared himself, attempting to bend his elbows and gradually lower himself. But that quickly failed.

As he landed face-first into the dirt. He sat back up with utter disappointment in himself. What could he possibly do?

He still needed to be healed before he could do anything dangerous. The head injury kept bleeding heavily, more so because he had moments before crashed his face into the ground.

He was too tired to fight, but not exhausted enough to fall asleep on the rough ground. Luke peered around, eyes blank, utter desolation, as if life lacked any meaning.

Looking back at his arm, which had become paler, worried about dying from blood loss, trying his best to wipe the blood away as it stuck to him like glue.

Despite his utter weariness, which would have moved others to tears, he remained resolute.

He walked back to the pedestal. Placing his hand on the pedestal, the screen popped up once more. He willed it to restart floor 0, as the familiar energy condensed again into the green slime.

Luke stared at it profusely.

I can't catch a break for even a second.

He waddled his way over towards it, trying his best not to lose balance, finding it even worse to balance with the loss of his arm and the state of the island.

The fight with the Elite Slime left the island littered with pits and cracks that formed segments, leaving the island almost disconnected from different areas, which annoyed him.

Shifting his way over, staying out of the slime's range, not gaining attention just yet. He would need to fight just in the usual old fashion way once more.

This time, knowing for a fact he wouldn’t be able to gather any of the soul energy he had used multiple times, only concerned Luke more.

His mind was considerably clearer than it had been against that blue slime. He just had to win enough fights just to heal himself. After that, he would take the time to rest.

He needed to get ready for the upcoming fight on floor 2, hoping the monster on floor 2 was just a slime. So him versus a slime would have given him a higher chance of winning.

One thing he noticed between the green and blue slime was they still had the same attack patterns so it didn’t even matter what kind of special ability the other one would have compared to the rest of the slimes he fought, except the elite one of course. He was ready for whatever monster would spawn.

Plus that he really wanted to live, so he had to struggle. But the main reason he truly wanted to win was to gain freedom from this place.

He wanted to go back to his own world, back to his original ways of lazing around.

But he is still weak at this point. But enough with all that waiting. He wiped away the blood from his eyes and forehead while rushing towards the green slime. It noticed him and spun around and slightly twitched, telling Luke that it was ready to launch itself towards him. He swiftly dodged it to the right as the slime blitzed past him.

The second the slime landed again on the floor, Luke was already crawling to his feet and lunging at the slime, trying to be as swift as possible.

But Luke was still chubby around the edges, so he was still quite slow, and this allowed the slime to pounce at him once again. Luke realized this and tried to dash to his left, away from it. But the slime nicked him in the stump, causing it to sizzle quickly on contact.

Taking a second to think of a better way, one thing he did realize was that blue slimes were less acidic.

Luke quickly tried to dispatch as much acid that landed on his stump. The slime had just landed from attacking him. Luke was swift enough to get to his feet and stood there like a fortress. Taking the initiative this time, he charged towards the slime with his increased physique and bodied slam it, which this time was actually quite effective and pushed the slime backwards a few steps.

Luke swore he had just seen the slime giggle at him. Luke looked confused at the slime.

Did that slime just giggle at me? With gaping eyes, he didn’t even care if he was in pain; he just wanted to end that slime. He clenched his only hand and decided if that slime wanted to laugh at him being fat, then it could die.

Gathering up the courage, he rushed once more to the slime, as the slime actually tried to dodge him this time, but Luke wouldn’t allow that; he leapt as far as he could. To belly flop into the slime, but only caused his stomach to be scorched by the acidic fluid.

The smell of burnt flesh entered his nose again, as it reminded him of the first time he fought one of these. Painful as it was, he still pounded on it, using whatever strength he could muster.

The slime slowly flattened as his weight kept it locked to the ground, beating away at it until its core cracked, and the slime disappeared into particles.

Luke collapsed onto the ground, breathing heavily, turning over onto his back. He still had a scorched belly, but it was manageable compared to his first encounter.

He could have easily defended one of these slimes if he was once back at his peak condition. The wound on his forehead didn’t seem to have gotten worse, which was something that pleased him.

He continued just lying there, taking a breather, knowing that he now had 3 points. Which put him two more away from a restore. Luke, even though in pain, made his way back to the pillar, his mind drifting around as he willed for another slime to spawn.

Even though this would be tedious, he still had to get the points. It took him more time to kill another 2 slimes because of his clumsy mistakes. He ended up getting another injury on his left leg and his lower back.

Tears welled up in his eyes whenever he moved his back with the strain and corroded flesh.

The slime really did a number on me. He thought overwhelmed with exhaustion.

He could barely breathe right, along with a constant pain that arose in his gut every time he tried breathing. He really wished he had taken a longer break between those fights.

But he achieved it, though. The points needed for a restore filled him with so much excitement that he felt like dancing his way towards the pedestal.

But he took his time while walking towards it with a big slime. But the bloody forehead made him look as if he were suffering.

He staggered onto the pedestal, treating it like his only friend.

“If it hadn’t been for your pedestal, I would have been long dead,” Luke said, while the light blue veil with white strands appeared.

He saw the status screen again and spent all his points to heal himself. He finally felt rejuvenated, as his wounds healed rapidly, with muscle fibers being built back up. His leg wound featured burn marks, which revealed some of his bones. Recovered almost instantly, though his left arm was still missing. His stump, however, looked considerably better.

He rested near the pedestal. A wave of happiness washed over him. He wished he could have seen others again or interacted with people before he went completely crazy.

Luke knew he was slowly losing his sanity, staring back into the sky which had held 2 souls before, now. It was now at a generous 8, being the only light in the darkness. He still didn’t quite get why he could see so well in an area that seemed to have had no sunlight or whatever. But he didn’t care. He had made it out alive, and that was complete enough.
