Chapter 173 / 18: Wonders of Witch Sight

Having repaired ribs in the past, Joe decided to tackle those first. Lacking Hah’roo’s nimble fingers to guide the bones into place, Joe made do with [Morphic Form]. By using the transformative skill to shape his muscles, he wedged the cracks together and began filling them with the bone cement that would heal the breaks.

The eye was going to be trickier. Doc. Reevadah’s warning echoed through his head. If he messed this up, the healing might cause his immune system to reject the new eye. That would be bad, but even worse, it could cause his body to attack the other eye, too, fully blinding him.

Joe weighed the pros and cons.

Trying to find his way out of here with just Yuk was going to be hard enough, let alone trying to do it half blind. The rejection of his eye would not be immediate, so as long as he was not stuck down here for too long, that worst-case scenario should not be an issue. He was certain Mercy Suku could fix his vision if he screwed up; the only question was, would it be expensive? Possibly going into debt later, as opposed to being seriously visually impaired now, made for a pretty easy choice.

He was going to try to fix the eye.

The first step was to look at the problem. Turning his awareness inward, he focused on the ruined orb, only to be amazed at just how much more information [Witch Sight] provided him over [Assess Wounds]. With the basic skill, Joe had merely seen localized auras of red for injury, green for poison, gray for weakness, black for corruption, blue for health, and so on. Now, not only could he trace issues through his body, but he could also understand far more than simple shades of hue.

For instance, the major reason he could not fully heal himself was not the still-mending bones or his mangled eye, but the creeping black taint that was trying to spread through his system. Wherever one of the wight’s claws had pierced his skin, necrotic corruption was burrowing away from the site of the wound.

Following the inky tendrils from each wound showed Joe how his system was fighting back. The spread flickered as it was actively being attacked by his primal magic. Joe’s habit of instinctively self-healing had developed into a vigorous passive regeneration. [Witch Sight] revealed how {Life} infused mana was constantly cycling through his body. It wasn’t the same as a conscious use of [Healer’s Hand], but Joe could tell the necrotic blight was not spreading as quickly as it should have with someone else. The putrefaction was slowly overpowering the regeneration, but the keyword there was slowly. Joe knew he could get ahead of the taint.

After a minute of breaking down and pushing out the undead corruption, Joe was finally ready to tackle his eye. Again, his new vision provided him with an in-depth understanding of the issue. He studied his good eye, detecting a thin barrier of a type of tissue that Joe couldn’t find anywhere else in his body. His enhanced passive regeneration didn’t reach his eyes either. There were healing ‘cells’ in his eyes that felt different than the ones everywhere else. Joe didn’t actually see them as cells, more like motes of {Life} energy, but ‘cell’ was an easy term from his Earth-based understanding of anatomy. For some reason, the eye immunity cells reminded him of Tezeno, a thought that made him chuckle. They gave off a more stoic, stiff impression than the wild healing cells running throughout the rest of his body.

He carefully pushed his healing through the wounded socket, focusing only on the Tezeno cells. They got to work. Unlike how Glaidlbea handled the problem, the ripped flesh was not discarded but recycled. The tattered lens was merged back together, and a gooey substance was created to inflate the orb.

This work was slower than any heal he had done so far, for a couple of reasons. One, Joe was being super careful. Two, he could not throw his whole healing potential at the problem; he had to stick to the Tezeno cells and nothing else. After a while, Joe closed his good eye and could finally make out some vague, blurry shapes and colors. It was a bit like looking through a block of foggy ice, but it was something.

‘We hate to bother you, Joe. But we have company coming,’ Yuk nudged through the [Parasitic Bond].

Joe hopped to his feet but almost pitched right back over. His balance was way off due to the weirdness of his vision. It was like he was looking through a rearview mirror that had been set for someone else. He was seeing double, and the blurry, overlapping images were looking at completely different angles.

‘You ok?’

‘Yeah, but I’m going to have to tie something over my bad eye or else I won’t be able to fight. Trying to hold the eye closed through a brawl is going to be as distracting as the double vision.’

‘No time. Here they come!’ Yuk warned.

He shut his bad eye and saw four undead forms bounding down the dark passageway. Joe didn't even have a [Halefire] up, since he was trying to stay unnoticed. The only light came from the dim [Glowstone] hovering overhead.

“Shit!” he swore. The timing of these wandering monsters couldn’t have been much worse.

Joe winked his right eye closed and summoned a stone to begin breaking it down for [Grit Razor]. He only had time for a partial charge, but he should be able to hit all of them.

As the stone crumbled and the dead closed the gap, Joe felt the wildness rise up. It did not want to fight impaired. It wanted payback for the eye. Contrary to all his careful work, the primal spirit shoved healing into the eye, rounding out its deformed shape and smoothing the lens.

The lid popped open, and Joe found he had perfect vision once more, but at what cost?

‘What the fuck!’ he swore at his wild side. ‘We are going to talk about this later.’

‘Talk about what?’ the confused swarm boy asked.

‘Sorry. Was talking to myself … sort of. I’ll explain it later.’

Firing [Grit Razor] at knee height, Joe caused the attackers to stumble. In quick succession, he fired off [Rattlebones] at the closest ghast and [Vulture’s Beak Mark] at the next closest. The marked wolfish dead was in bad enough shape now that a [Dispel Noxa] and a slashing claw dropped it.

A small tornado of biting insects whirled around the remaining wights, giving Joe time to drop [Halefires] and get his shield active and covered.

‘Damn it. I could have just covered the eye with the pelt, you idiot. I had plenty of time for that,’ Joe grumbled, this time just to himself, saving Yuk from any further confusion.

The ghasts completely ignored the living swarm. Maybe they couldn’t find his broken presence, or whatever made up the raggamuffin, was nothing they cared to corrupt. Either way, it made Joe’s life easier, not having to worry about cleansing others. The Dirge Wights had three primary threats: their physical attacks, their mental powers, and their corruption. Of all of these, their mind assaults were the most dangerous for anyone else. Not for Joe. As long as he did not get overwhelmed by too many at once, he had a good chance of winning these smaller skirmishes.

Joe had worked hard to train up his fighting skills, even though he knew he was a long way from the level of his combatant-savvy teammates, like Hah’roo, Tez, and Kenda. He had only been training for a few weeks; to be honest, he’d only been in Illuminaria for a month. By this point, though, he felt he had a grasp of the basics, maybe green belt level. So while Joe spelled and clawed his way through the remaining three ghasts, he took several wounds, though he contained those hits to nonvital areas that a quick heal could erase.

Between the initial [Grit Razor], the undead burning effect of the [Halefires], and Yuk’s [Biting Vortex], the three wights were already in bad shape. He blocked one set of claws with his hide-bound [Grimoiriguard], and dual cast vulture mark onto the other two, dropping their health by thirteen percent of their max health pool. Ducking his head to keep the claws off his face, Joe popped the pair with a double shot of his rot-blasting curse, [Dispel Noxa].

The last one, Joe let the wildness loose on. Hexes and shields were put aside as the battle shifted fully to claws. Joe’s shaggy forearms blocked the ghast’s talons, but its withered skin had no such stopping power against the [Morphic Form] enhanced strikes that laid them open. After the fourth exchange of blows, the wight no longer had claws to battle with as both of its arms now ended at their elbows. When it tried to bite him, the wildness tore its head off.

As the last body fell, Joe shook off the wildness’s fervor. He had work to do. After driving out the corruption, he turned his focus to his force-fixed eyeball. Sure enough, he found inflammation already growing within the altered orb. Using his previous analogy, wild immunity cells were inside the barrier, and they were not at all happy with what they were finding. There were still a decent number of the stiff ‘Tezeno’ cells, but they were being torn apart by the more aggressive motes of primal healing.

Again, [Witch Sight] was the key to salvaging this mess. Joe could target the primal cells and send them to the new claw wounds that crisscrossed his legs and ribs. Once they were out, Joe energized the Tez cells, encouraging them to split, reproduce, and spread. The process was far more painstakingly difficult than the initial repair, but after fifteen minutes of herding bits of {Life} energy, the eye was finally free of inflammation. The new organ was not exactly like his undamaged eye, but it was anatomically stable, and he could see clearly out of it. At the moment, that was good enough.

Sliding back down the wall, Joe let out a deep, weary sigh. He had armor to mend and layers of undead splatter to clean off, but he wanted just two minutes to collect himself. In a distracted, semi-exhausted state, he leaned back against the rough stone of the tunnel wall and watched Yuk gather every insect in the area to reform a new swarm-base body for themselves.
