Paladin of Vengeance

It is said that those who seek vengeance should first dig two graves. Asgar's readying a whole damned field.

Asgar was a farmer. Not exactly a life of luxury, but he was happy enough­—big dumb lad, quiet little life. Then soldiers from his own kingdom burned his village to the ground.

Swearing an oath of vengeance, Asgar is empowered with a class by God himself, in a world where only the richest and luckiest of noble scions are so blessed. His target is the kingdom’s most famous Knight, so joining the army seems the obvious first step on his path of revenge.

Fate has other plans. The Golden Lance is no simple Knight, and Asgar is shown to be more ignorant of the world than he ever realised. The Pits to Hell are opening, the Ardent Church is fighting a hidden war with itself, and the nobles and crown scheme incessantly.

To claim his vengeance, the son of a humble turnip farmer will have to grow into a Paladin of Vengeance, and fight through all three to reach his goal.

What to expect:

- A fast-paced revenge story

- Light litRPG mechanics in a skill and class-based levelling system

- Grimdark/noblebright worldbuilding, with grounded characters striving to fix a brutal world

- A fair bit of swearing
