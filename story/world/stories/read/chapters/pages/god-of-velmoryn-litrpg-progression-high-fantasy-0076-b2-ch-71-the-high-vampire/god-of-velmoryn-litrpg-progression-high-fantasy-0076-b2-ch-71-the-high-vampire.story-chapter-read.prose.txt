“Theia?” A rough voice came from the darkness as the woman entered the hall, built from obsidian stone. “Why have you come?”

Is she scared of her own master?

I had no reason to believe they were a kind or welcoming race, but I had assumed the one she called “master” would be someone she did not fear.

“I have a request, master,” she said carefully. “I wanted to ask if you knew anything about the Mistress…”

Even after raising my rank, this damn Window can’t see through darkness…

Theia’s breath caught for a moment, and she glanced toward the door.

“If you know nothing, then I’ll leave, master. My friend is waiting outside, and I don’t want to keep him…”

The door slammed shut with a loud bang. The darkness swallowed everything.

Theia screamed, but her voice was cut off, as if something had silenced her.

I could see nothing in the darkness. Nor could I sense anything.

The silence and darkness lasted only a fraction of a second before the door burst open.

Crimson and gold light flooded the hall as Aurelion spread his wings. His eyes swept across the room, searching for the source of the voice and for where Theia had been taken.

Deeper inside, on a small stone platform, Theia lay sprawled, a figure cloaked in a dark veil pressing her down. I sensed no mana from him. He looked like a tall, thin figure wrapped in a cloak.

“Who dared to disrupt my meal…”

Aurelion grabbed Theia, pulling her close. He checked her condition quickly, and only after confirming she was merely unconscious did he turn back to the vampire.

Whatever… it doesn’t matter anyway.
