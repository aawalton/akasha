Going down the emergency stairs turned out to be more, uh… exciting than Chloe had expected. Not exciting in the fun way, either. There was lightning everywhere, and the stairs themselves had stopped behaving like stairs a while ago. Some sections stretched impossibly far into the distance, while others seemed to shrink until they were barely wider than Chloe's foot. At one point, she was sure that she had seen a flight of stairs loop completely upside down.

Reality had apparently decided that the normal rules were more of a suggestion. The walls twisted around while doors appeared where there definitely hadn't been doors before, only to vanish a second later. There was even a time when Chloe saw herself running past on another section of the staircase, which was a little weird considering she was fairly certain she wasn't running anywhere. And also, there could only be one Chloe in the world anyway, her! Well, she did have some classmates before who were also named Chloe, but they weren't as special.

Then the floor tilted sideways and Chloe lost track of which direction was supposed to be up. Through all of it, Sierra clung to her like her life depended on it. Which, to be fair, it probably did to at least some extent.

Sierra had both arms wrapped tightly around Chloe, her face buried against Chloe's shoulder while Chloe stumbled from one shifting piece of staircase to another. Every time another crack split through the air or lightning exploded nearby, Sierra's grip tightened another notch, and Chloe didn't really blame her.

She must have been through a lot. Chloe thought.

Luckily, Chloe was pretty sure she was doing a good job of pretending she wasn't terrified herself. But after what felt like a really really long time, the chaos finally seemed to reach a breaking point. There was a sudden flash of white. Chloe felt herself falling, so she grabbed Sierra.

Sierra grabbed onto her just as tightly, and the two of them fell down onto something solid, leaving Chloe sprawled there for a few seconds as she stared blankly at the ceiling and waited for literally anything else to go wrong. But, nothing exploded, there was no more lightning, the walls weren't doing that super creepy reality-bending thing anymore, and the floor was thankfully staying where it was supposed to be, so Chloe slowly lifted her head and looked around with a completely confused expression, because honestly, she had absolutely no idea where they had even ended up.

They were definitely not in the stairwell anymore, because somehow Chloe and Sierra had ended up in this absolutely massive underground tunnel, with the thing stretching so far into the darkness in both directions that Chloe couldn't even tell where it ended. The ceiling was really high up, and thick concrete walls reinforced with huge metal supports covered in faded warning signs and weird glowing lines that Chloe was pretty sure were probably important somehow; dim white lights ran along the walls every few meters, flickering just enough to make everything feel even creepier.

"Okay," Chloe muttered. "I think we're probably somewhere."

She pushed herself upright and immediately turned toward Sierra. "Also, are you okay?"

Sierra was still clinging to her. But when Chloe moved, Sierra finally realized that they were no longer falling through a reality-shaped nightmare. She slowly loosened her grip and looked around, her ears twitching nervously as she took in their surroundings. "I-I think so..."

Chloe studied her carefully, checking for any obvious injuries. Aside from being covered in dust and looking thoroughly shaken, Sierra seemed fine. "Okie."

After that, Chloe slowly turned in a circle, taking another look at the enormous tunnel surrounding them, and boy, there was a whole lot of nothing that she could see. Maybe there was a metaphor for this kind of thing somewhere, but there were probably better times to think about fancy language stuff.

Chloe glanced at Sierra, who was also looking around. "Okay, so we have absolutely no idea where we are."

Sierra nodded. "Yeah, I can't smell anything much here either except for rust and metal."

Chloe considered this for a moment before cupping her hands around her mouth and shouting, "Hello!"

Her voice rang through the tunnel, bouncing from wall to wall. The echo traveled farther than Chloe expected, growing fainter as it disappeared into the darkness before eventually returning from somewhere behind them. Chloe blinked and lowered her hands. "Wow. That goes pretty far."

"Uh, maybe we should just start walking and see where we end up." Sierra suggested.

"Sure, beats just standing around here anyway." Chloe said.

Chloe looked around again, searching for anything that might give them some clue about where they were supposed to go. Unfortunately, the tunnel wasn't exactly covered in helpful signs pointing toward an exit or anything like that.

With that, the two of them began walking. Their footsteps echoed as they made their way farther into the tunnel. Chloe tried to keep an eye out for anything that might give them some clue about where they had ended up, occasionally glancing around.

Sierra, meanwhile, seemed considerably less interested in exploring their surroundings. She kept looking over her shoulder, and at first Chloe didn't think much of it. After Sierra had done it several more times, however, Chloe finally glanced at her. "You know, there's probably nothing behind us."

"I know."

"But you keep checking anyway."

Sierra's ears twitched nervously. "I know."

Chloe shrugged and turned forward again. "You know what? Do what you have to do."

They continued deeper into the tunnel, and although nothing seemed to be happening, then even those sounds seemed to fade. Sierra slowed beside her. "Chloe… Did you hear something?"

"Uh, I don't think so?"

Chloe actually hadn't heard anything at first, but after Sierra stopped speaking, Chloe listened more carefully, and eventually did listen to the sound of footsteps.

Someone was walking toward them. Sierra moved closer, her hand finding Chloe's arm. The footsteps continued to approach, their echoes growing louder as the stranger came nearer. Chloe felt a little spooked, and she found herself wondering whether they had finally stumbled across one of the other Administrators that Z had warned them about.

Then the footsteps suddenly stopped, leaving Chloe and Sierra standing there in the middle of the tunnel. Chloe wasn't really sure whether they should keep moving or just wait to see what happened next, but before she could decide, a strange crackling sound came from somewhere ahead and a flash of pale light suddenly lit up the darkness, revealing a figure standing a couple of feet, and wow, he was seriously tall. The guy was dressed almost entirely in black with a long coat, while dark gloves covered his hands.

The stranger stood so completely still that Chloe might have honestly mistaken him for some kind of creepy statue if not for the faint electricity crawling around his feet, but what really caught her attention was the bizarre mask covering his face, which looked exactly like the head of one of those old-fashioned plague doctors with its long, curved beak, giving him this whole mysterious, creepy-supervillain vibe that Chloe couldn't help thinking looked kind of awesome despite how unsettling it was.

"Okay, he's super creepy, but you've gotta admit that mask looks really cool." Chloe said, glancing at Sierra.

Sierra stared at her. "That's what you're worried about?"

Chloe shrugged. "Well, I can be worried about a lot of stuff."

Another flash of crackling light lit up the tunnel, briefly revealing the stranger in greater detail before plunging him back into shadow. He just kinda stood there, watching them, and for some reason, Chloe found that much more unsettling than if he'd simply started attacking them.

Chloe narrowed her eyes as she looked at the stranger more carefully. Now that she had a better look at him, something seemed... off. He certainly looked intimidating from a distance, but the closer Chloe looked, the more she noticed that he didn't exactly seem to be in the best condition. His long black cloak was torn in several places, with holes exposing the clothing underneath, and parts of the fabric looked as though they had been scorched.

Then Chloe noticed something even stranger: one of his legs was twitching. It was subtle, almost as though he were trying to hide it, but every few seconds the leg gave a small, involuntary jerk before becoming still again.

"Uh... are you alright?" Chloe asked the man.

The plague doctor guy straightened slightly, apparently offended by the question. "Hmph. I am more than alright."

Chloe nodded. "Cool, so, who are you?"

The masked man drew himself up to his full height, despite the occasional twitch in his leg, and his voice took on a strangely formal tone. "I am Administrator P, and I will be honored to kill Ishgria's daughter."

He turned his masked face toward Sierra. "And... whatever this is."

Chloe's expression immediately soured. "Hey! First of all, Sierra has a name. You can't just call her whatever this is!"

Sierra's ears flattened as she shifted uncomfortably beside Chloe, but Chloe remained focused on the strange Administrator. "And also, how do you even know I'm Mom's daughter?"

The question left her mouth before she had a chance to think about it. Chloe stopped. She slowly blinked. Oh.

That was... probably not the smartest question she'd ever asked. If Administrator P already knew she was Ishgria's daughter, then asking how he knew had basically confirmed it for him. Chloe stared at him, silently wishing she could reach into the last ten seconds and pull the words back out of the air. Well, there wasn't much she could do about that now. "Never mind."

She immediately shifted her stance and raised her fists, apparently deciding that the best way to recover from accidentally revealing information was to simply stop talking. "Forget I said anything. Because I'm still going to beat you up."

Administrator P suddenly sprang backward. Chloe barely had time to wonder what he was doing before the shadows around his body began to move.

At first, she thought the strange flickering lights in the tunnel were playing tricks on her eyes, but the darkness gathered around him far too deliberately for that. Black wisps curled up from the floor and wrapped themselves around his legs, then climbed higher, twisting around his torso like living smoke. The shadows thickened until they completely obscured his body, swallowing the torn cloak and plague-doctor mask beneath a churning mass of darkness.

Chloe took an instinctive step backward as something cracked inside the swirling shadows. Administrator P's silhouette suddenly jerked upward as his body began to change. His shoulders widened, his arms stretched and twisted into enormous, angular limbs, and the shape of his torso elongated as dark feathers began bursting through the swirling darkness. His legs bent at impossible angles before reshaping themselves into powerful, birdlike limbs, while his cloak seemed to dissolve into a mass of black plumage. The transformation continued until the figure towering before Chloe barely resembled the man from moments earlier.

What emerged from the shadows was a gigantic birdlike creature. Its body was covered in dark feathers, and its long neck rose high above Chloe's head before ending in an angular beak. Massive wings unfolded from its back, scraping against the surrounding walls as it stretched to its full height. Oh, and the now transformed Administrator P also had a big black spear now too, so that was also terrifying.

Before Chloe could think about what the heck she was actually fighting, she stepped in front of Sierra, spreading her arms slightly to put herself between her friend and the enormous creature. It probably wasn't going to help much.

The monster was several times her size, and Chloe had a growing suspicion that its spear could probably cut her in half if it really wanted to. Still, she wasn't about to let Sierra stand in front of it.

Okay, Chloe, things are totally going to be fine.
