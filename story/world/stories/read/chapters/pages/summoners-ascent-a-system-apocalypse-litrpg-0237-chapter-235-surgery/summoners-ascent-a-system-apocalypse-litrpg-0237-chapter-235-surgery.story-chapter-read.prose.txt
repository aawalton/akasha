The mass of raw flesh and body parts that Ashton had prepared for the Spawn of Akrazoth disappeared, and he could feel a wave of relief come over him. It was like that feeling of refreshment you had when you stepped out of your house in the morning into a chilly yet sunny day.

Ashton felt his 'Spirit' increase considerably and smiled, happy to have resolved this issue he had been worried about for a while now. Frankly, turning the Spawn of Akrazoth into a reliable ally would be great. At least, now Ashton could define exactly what he would give to them, even if they might still try to get greedy and get their hands on even more. Well, if they kept doing a good job, Ashton might reward them and hand out a few more things.

But most importantly, he would now be able to go back and fix Millie's wrist without any lasting damage or even a trace of a surgery. And if Ashton got seriously injured now, he wouldn't have to worry about having even more important body parts taken from him.

Of course, there were other things that the Spawn was useful for, but they were things that were relatively low-priority. For example, the Spawn could extract magic stones and other important materials from slain monsters for Ashton now, hand them to the Backpacker, and just let Ashton worry about fighting. That alone would be a huge time-saver.

During the occasions where Ashton was under pressure, he had to leave behind a lot of valuable things because there was simply not enough time. But now, that would change.

Though, most importantly, Ashton had to run back to Calibur. He had wasted more than enough time on this. The potion that Ashton had given to Millie would have helped with the pain, but she still couldn't do anything right now beside just sit there and be coddled, and Millie wasn't exactly the type of child to enjoy that.

Poppy gave him another speed-boost and Ashton pushed himself forward even more with the Sylph's help, quickly rushing down the road. He used the sylph to help slow him down as well, by creating strong gusts at an angle to help his normal turning.

Ashton even avoided most monsters as much as he could. There were more important things right now. It didn't take long until he got back to Calibur, stepping into the camp by opening the gate on his own, once again startling the gatekeeper.

"Could you stop doing that?" they asked with a groan, but Ashton just smiled at them and passed by. He rushed up the stairs and made his way back to Sam's office, where the little girl Ashton was looking for was still sitting on the chair where Ashton had left her.

Besides her, Sam, Jacky, and Paul were here, watching and trying to entertain her.

"Huh? You're back, already?" Sam looked down at his watch and back at Ashton confused. "I was expecting to have to wait until later tonight... I was actually concerned this would mean you couldn't go to help out Clem."

Ashton shook his head, "No, of course not. I keep all my promises to the best of my ability."

He squatted down in front of Millie. "Including the one to protect you, and I'm sorry that I wasn't able to prevent this injury, but it's going to be fixed soon, okay?"

"... Okay," she replied, hesitantly.

"You don't need to worry. I've changed my contract with the Spawn of Akrazoth, so we can fix your wrist really easily, alright? It'll be over before you know it," Ashton said, as the Spawn slowly came crawling out of the mark on Ashton's arm. This time, it didn't hurt. It wasn't comfortable, but it wasn't painful, at least.

Millie flinched slightly when she saw the small demon. She might not have seen them directly, but she knew that this was the one that had cut off her arm, and that had taken Ashton's eyes from him. The others in the room didn't help, either.

"What the fuck is that?" Jacky let out, startled, taking a step back. She bumped into some of the boxes in the corner behind her.

"Jesus bloody fuck, you could've warned us first, man," Sam said in a similarly shocked tone. Paul couldn't even say anything, but it was clear that he was just as taken aback by the Spawn's appearance. And well, Ashton got their reaction. His other summons were pretty cute, and though he believed that the Spawn of Akrazoth had some charm as well, he knew that for most people it looked, well, disgusting. Crawling out of a small mark in Ashton's skin didn't help much with that, either.

But Ashton just ignored those three; this was about Millie. "Do you need my mask?"

Millie thought about it for a moment, but in the end shook her head. "No... I'm okay. I want to watch."

Ashton raised his brow. "Are you sure? You don't have to."

"I'm sure."

"Well, alright," Ashton said with a proud smile. The Spawn of Akrazoth climbed from Ashton's hand onto Millie's arm, and started to pull off the bandages first and foremost.

With a small stinger placed into one of its 'fingers', the demon numbed the area first and then got started. The flesh seemed to melt off the fingers that made up the demon's body and the bones underneath had taken on the form of different surgical tools.

Immediately, the demon got started and cut into Millie's wrist. Ashton was holding her arm to make sure she didn't accidentally move, though that wasn't enough to stop the surgery anyway.

And the moment Millie's arm was cut open, the Spawn pushed its fingers into the gap. Though she said she wanted to watch, Millie pressed her eyes shut. Probably not because she was disgusted, but rather, she seemed to have issues with the sensation.

The Spawn's venom anesthetized the patient, but it only removed pain. You could still feel everything else that was going on, and having something writhe inside of your skin wasn't exactly a pleasant sensation.

But even so, it didn't take the Spawn long to finish. It set all the bones properly in place, and even did something called 'bone stitching'. Something that wasn't normally possible, but through the demon's power, simply worked.

That way, all the tiny splinters were pulled back into their place and would solidly sit there until the potion made them fuse back together. Technically, everything was so tightly pulled together that Millie should be able to move her hand without any worries, but it was of course still a bit weaker for now until the potion did its job.

The Spawn closed up Millie's wrist again, tapped her arm, and then walked back over to Ashton who held out his own arm.

Immediately, the Spawn pushed its stinger into him, but instead of injecting him with venom, blood was pulled from Ashton. This was what Ashton was giving the demon as payment for this right now, and it seemed satisfied enough, even if he could tell that it wanted to get its fingers on more if at all possible. But it did as it was asked and just moved back into the mark on Ashton's arm.

"So? All better?" Ashton asked, and Millie slowly nodded.

"I think so..?" she replied, opening and closing her fist, which she hadn't been able to properly do earlier. Ashton quickly applied another bandage with some more of a healing potion on it, and instructed Millie to be careful for now. With how clean the Spawn's work was, this wound would be almost perfectly healed by the time Millie woke up tomorrow morning.
