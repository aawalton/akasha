Shuren watched as Su Kang left the balcony, while Celes continued to observe him.

Those two… I should’ve followed my gut instinct. As soon as I wrap up this duel and avenge the honour of my cousin, I’ll look further into what’s going on.

By now, even Shuren had an inkling. With the rise of demonic activities in the vicinity, the strange tension in the sect, the change of the situation with the Unhallowed Monolith he had last found, and even the words of Lixi during their encounter.

He would be a fool to think nothing was going on.

And if even he knew, then the sect must’ve already been acting in the shadows. The game amongst the higher ups must’ve long started.

Little did he know the thin strings had long wrapped around his limbs.

I’ll have to gather information later on. For now, I’ll continue to gain merits and increase my influence through success and opportunities.

He knew the white-haired girl was looking down on him, but Shuren scoffed while paying attention to the elders who decided to appear as overseers. He already knew why they had made an appearance.

It was time for them to probe Shuren. Now that he was getting even closer to Path Foundation, he had the opportunity of becoming a Core Disciple.

Core Disciples, the ones with the most resources poured into them; second to the Grand Disciple. Especially if they achieved it at a young age, the Seven Clouds would allow them to become personal disciples of the Elders. Of course, this was also an opportunity where the Elders would competitively vie for a suitable successor, or a rather useful personal assistant.

Elders would also, in return, gain resources and a higher status for nurturing a Core Disciple, especially if they were a student as an Inner Disciple. Initially, the elders were not interested in a silly duel, especially with a magnanimous disparity between both contestants.

The chattering and murmurs of the crowd grew quiet when cultivators flying in the sky began to appear. They were none other than the elders, the only one who could fly without the need of a flying nimbus.

Elder Spiritweaver, the oldest elder in the Seven Clouds Sect, sat on his flying nimbus, coloured a dark grey streaked with gold, shaped like a throne. His eyes were almost hidden by his wrinkled and deep eyebrows, and though his body looked decrepit, it held immense power, emanating the authority of a wise elder.

“This student of mine has a lot of potential. To think he’s able to infuse Wind Qi without even a catalyst before Path Foundation, directly gaining comprehension in the Wind Path. Even most Stage 12 Qi Condensation cultivators struggle to do such a thing. Most take years even to reach such a result,” Elder Spiritweaver laughed heartily,

He faced towards the black-haired elder with sharp eyebrows at his side. “Swordfall, you already chose a potential Core Disciple, so don’t think you can come here to swipe another one.” With confidence, Elder Spiritweaver probed. He was sure that his student, Shuren, would be more inclined under his tutelage.

He needed someone with high battle prowess for him to delve deeper into his new plan.

Elder Swordfall barely paid attention to Shuren, almost looking at him with a cold gaze before looking away. He didn’t sit, but rather stood strikingly atop his cloud, with his hands behind his back. “He wouldn’t have much compatibility with me. I mainly teach bladed weapons, not blunt weapons. But, it is good to see what he can do with both the staff and his minor attainment in the Wind Path,” he explained himself, easing the pressure on Elder Spiritweaver.

Elder Spiritweaver hid his confusion. Why the hell is he even here then? I doubt it’s just to spend the time out of boredom.

Elder Harmony also showed herself, letting her brown hair flow on top of her pristine white robes. She sat on a simple, cushioned red chair as she held a cup of tea, with the fragrance of flowers drifting from it. She didn’t say much, but instead harshly complained in her mind.

If only that stupid kid listened to his mum, I’d be the one reaping benefits, I mean, it would be with my help that he would reach this far. I could’ve used this to get even more fundings for my research. Curses! Whatever, she specifically asked me to keep progress on her oldest kid. She sure is a worrywart over this little arrogant battle maniac. Who the hell does he even get it from anyway?

She took a sip of her tea in a bid to retain her neutral expression. Unfortunately, most of her disciples weren’t well-versed in the arts of battle, at least compared to the average disciple. If Shuren had joined her side, she could’ve made a lot more use of him, and even save resources on various missives.

Elders didn’t get free reign on whatever they wanted to make disciples do. There was a hierarchy amongst elders as well, and they needed to provide results to gain more rewards from the deep coffers of their Sect Leader. Of course, all of this was on top of the various benefits they gained by default, which was roughly the same as any other average sect they would’ve been in, so it was a carrot on a stick to ensure all elders worked to improve the sect's disciples.

And Elder Harmony herself had now become the main person in custody of healing Liu Wei. The only reason the Hall of Punishment hadn’t taken action yet was when they discovered the duel had spread to such an extent that it became an anticipated show amongst the sect. After some representatives from the Hall of Punishments gained the details from Shuren and Tez, they decided to let the duel occur, and only handle it afterward.

Still, even with the rumours, after the duel Tez would be taken for questioning, and his punishment would wait depending on the official declaration of Wei’s medical state. Crippling a fellow student was grounds for expulsion at Seven Clouds. This was a vital rule the Sect Leader had upheld no matter what, regardless of backing and status.

The Sect Leader had specifically drilled into the minds of all of his new elders that he did not want any potentials to be uprooted before they got to bloom. Even the most underrated, unknown worthless cultivator could upheave the world itself, and he had seen it enough times. Besides, only he was qualified enough to tell any force in the world off for daring to use their influence in his domain.

Tez had tied both of his shoes tightly, before setting off. This time, he had long abandoned the Evening Clouds robes, and wore his usual leather and metal wayfarer equipment. It had been the most suitable attire for when he travelled for a year to reach Cloud City.

He took a deep breath, dulling his anxiety somewhat. He already received a pep talk from his close friend, Fatty Chen, and told him to watch outside amongst the spectators. And he made sure to tell his other friend, Arene, to take care of Sarya for the meantime.

With a standard cardio stretch, Tez finally readied himself. “Now then, I’ll have to depend on you, my partner.” Within his hands, a rusty and worn out sheath manifested itself, landing perfectly in his palm. Strapping it to his side, he moved out. And from the musty small room, he squinted his eyes at the scorching sun and its light from midday.

Its sunlight was far stronger than the one he was used to.

In the end, I still never figured out why this duel ended up becoming the show of the entire sect. Did that young master from the Liu Clan really went out of his way to make it such a big commotion?

Either way, Tez didn’t really care. He had only one goal in mind today.

Everything else would fall in place after.

Elder Kuveiros had ended up appearing at the last minute, though for an entirely different reason. He ended up glancing at Tez, before focusing in on the sheathed weapon. “That weapon… It’s quite similar to a Spiritude Armament, but it’s not from this current era… Then it must be the prototype we based our technology off of. I’ll need to examine that thing later on, lest it’s the remnants of a living artifact.”

The crowd watched with anticipation as the second opponent had finally appeared. The duel had some interesting elements for them to enjoy; a nobody orphan with a Bronze Talisoul and an antiquated weapon, and a descendent of the Liu Clan on the cusp of becoming a Path Foundation and a Core Disciple.

Tez stared down Shuren with a blank gaze, but likewise Shuren did the same. He couldn’t gauge the attitude of his opponent, and for once held some shock that Shuren seemed to take him a bit too serious.

This is not good. I’m banking everything on him taking me as lightly as possible. Why is he acting so serious now like some seasoned swordsman?!

As he continued to walk, his dirty leather shoes left imprints on the floor, with some of the dirt and dust shifting around it. The heat berated down on Tez’s heavily geared body, but he had long gotten used to it. He wouldn’t even sweat in these temperatures anymore.

Finally, when he was a few steps away from Shuren, he stopped. They were both silent, almost at eye level, with Shuren a touch taller, but their intentions were obvious to everyone, let alone the two themselves.

But Shuren was the first to break the silence before the overseer of the duel appeared.

“You’ve broken through to Stage 9, haven’t you?”

Shit! How did he realise when I concealed my aura as much as possible?! At most, he should expect me to be at the peak of Stage 7!

That was when a thought he never would’ve expected treaded at the back of Tez’s mind.

Have I been relying too much on the System? I’m still far too ignorant, there must be ways for others to ignore the measures I’ve placed using it as well…

“You have a good eye. How many people beneath your level have you beaten to the point of figuring out these simple schemes?” Tez retorted back with hostile banter, refusing to allow shock or surprise to appear on his face.

But he didn’t expect Shuren to ignore his blatant provocation.

“You’re quite good. Almost as if you have someone, or something, backing you up,” Shuren casually commented.

And though the scorching heat wasn’t enough to cause Tez to sweat, a trickle went down from his forehead.

He’s acting too different, nothing like I expected. He isn’t looking down on me with arrogance, but treating me like a serious opponent. I shouldn’t have thought of him as some caricature of a young master, this world doesn’t run on these idiotic rules. Can I even complete my goal in lasting a hundred hits?

Doubt pervaded Tez’s mind as he thought. He had slowly gotten used to the ways of the world, and slowly his old notions were long abandoned.

Shuren continued. “Do you enjoy using that?”

“Using what?”

“That System.”

Tez forgot to breathe.

He turned pale, feeling even more pressure from the man in front of him.

“The hell are you on ab-”

“Play dumb if you wish. It’ll be wise for you to not hold back. Show me the power you’ve gained from this System.” Shuren dared to step forward, this time with a malicious smile as he stared down at Tez.

“I want to see if you’ve made it this far because you’re strong, or if you’ve only made it because your master chained you like a good dog, walking your path for you.”

There was no point in Tez bluffing anymore, or pretending to be ignorant.

Is he an otherworlder like me?! No, it isn’t that. He’s just using his own wits to figure it out! Have I been giving away that many hints?! Shit, if this spreads, then I may as well be dead!

An old man, a head taller than the two, appeared between them. He wore a simple sky blue robe, and held a hand on his large white beard. His appearance didn’t come with any fanfare, but rather as if he was always part of the world. But both Tez and Shuren only noticed him just a breath ago.

As if he just manifested his existence into the world itself in a wave of clouds.

“I will be overseeing this little duel. Tez, Shuren, both of you have grown rapidly over the past year. It is time to settle the karma you two have sewn, ever since the very first day of the Evening Clouds Examination battle. Release your limits and break through! And know what it means to be as seamless as the clouds themselves!” Grand Elder Wanshang had said with an overbearing but serious tone.

And with that announcement, the entire Duelling Colosseum went into a loud commotion.

He smiled as he said the next statement, pointless as it was. “If anyone wishes to forfeit, or wishes to change the conditions of the duel, now is the chance.”

Shuren finally expressed some emotion in his eyes. When Tez saw those cold blue eyes boring a hole in him, he knew what it meant. Shuren was declaring with just his gaze, telling Tez to not back down from the duel now. To not be the coward that ran away in the face of hundreds of disciples.

And Tez felt the wrath of someone that wished to avenge his dear cousin.

“Very well. As part of the agreement, Shuren, I will be restricting your cultivation level to Stage 9 of the Qi Condensation Realm. Once you fall on your knees, it is Tez’s victory,” Grand Elder Wanshang announced, using the archaic term for their cultivation level.

“Grand Elder Wanshang, I’d like to change the condition of the duel,” Shuren cut off the elder, beckoning dead silence amongst the crowd.

Tez finally broke his stoic expression, staring at Shuren with wide eyes.

Is this arrogant bastard for real?!

Shuren gave a mocking smile to Tez. Finally, it was an expression befitting of the image Tez thought of Shuren. “For this duel, seal my cultivation level to Stage 7.”

The commotion amongst the crowd was halted for a moment, and only the laughter of Elder Wanshang was present as he witnessed the arrogance of Shuren.

“Your request is accepted, young man.”

Su Kang watched from on high, a deep smile as he recalled back the memories of the older brother he had once followed around.

Celes didn’t react, as she watched the colour within his soul remain stagnant. As always. “What a façade.”

But there was only one thing Tez could say, and he could only sum it up with a crass term only he would know.

“What the fuck.”
