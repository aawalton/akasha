A guard did indeed take us into the castle! Honestly, I was beginning to be tired of how these guards all looked the same, but I ignored it for the sake of keeping my mind sane. It all felt so artificial, yet I couldn't help marveling at how the castle itself looked. It was marvelous! From the outside, it was pretty rough, but from the inside? Absolutely amazing.

I knew from the knowledge implanted into my mind that castles weren't something that humans occupied in the modern era anymore, but I kind of didn't understand why. It was so much cooler than living in a normal house or, heck, living in a flat shared with several dozen other families. Humans were weird.

"The dungeon is located in the basement," the guard said as we walked. Other guards passed us, but we didn't see any other players or any other folks that didn't wear a suit of armor. Strange. I would have expected at least accountants to be in. Oh my god. Was this one of the Crusades that I've learned about? Would they charge at the fake gods if they got the opportunity?

And so we walked around the paintings, through the castle halls, lower and lower. We took down some spiral stairs, about which Crudia giggled while walking downwards, and then suddenly we found ourselves in front of a giant door. To our surprise, we weren't the first ones here. No, in front of me stood two other players, looking at the door intently, talking to each other as they didn't notice us. Guards stood next to them as well.

I used [Analyze] immediately, hoping that they weren't our enemies.

Their windows opened up, and I suppressed a sigh as I saw one of them being higher level than me. The name rang a bell too. It was one of the top ten as well.

Player - Lia

Race - Common Raven

Level - 25

Class - Feathersting

The other one wasn't one of the top ten, but that didn't make them any less impressive.

Player - Aura

Race - Secretary Bird

Level - 24

Class - None

I looked at Mark, wondering about the class. "What the fuck does Feathersting mean?"

"I haven't got the slightest idea, but I think they just noticed us, so you'll have a chance to ask them!" Mark smirked and started hovering towards them.

Their guides… They were not here with us. I wondered whether they selected a similar class like Talisha, one that left them in their guild's base.

Lia, the stronger player with the class, looked a bit dull, to be honest. She was completely clad in black clothing, wearing some kind of silk that had the shape of a feathers on its ends. I mean, I got what it was meant to represent, but black, of all colors?! How many people would I need to teach about the beauty of colors before they understood?

As if that wasn't enough, the other one, the secretary bird, was wearing completely white clothing instead of the weird feathery robe; however, she was wearing a shirt and white pants. Her legs were a bit longer than I thought they should have been, and I noted that both of them had feathers growing on their neck and on the edge of their faces, though the face itself remained human, just like mine.

Another strange thing, however, was that Aura, the secretary bird, had orange skin around her eyes.

White & orange and black…

These people didn't deserve to walk the same earth as I did.

I was about to complain, but Crudia was the first to speak before I could even let out a word. "Hello, you two! How are you doing? Are you going into the dungeon as well?"

The two looked between each other then back at us and nodded.

"It appears so. Hello there, Crudia. I guess meetings are unnecessary?" Lia said, smiling shyly.

"We all know each other's names, but hi there, guide! I haven't seen mine since the beginning of the floor. Do you have any idea where all of the other guys went?" Aura completed.

"And what do I get for the information?" He smirked, his cockiness returning like a wave of water after a drought.

"My guide disappeared as well," Crudia said, and reached out her hand to add them to her friends list. "We'll tell you. Just—"

"We won't tell them, Crudia. We don't know if they are even friends, and she is in the top 10." Mark pointed at Lia.

"So why do you have to be so rude, man?" Lia scoffed and then pointed her finger at me. "He's in the top 10 as well!"

"Of course I am! Only awesome people get there." I smiled.

"Well, anyway, it was nice meeting you." Lia rolled her eyes. "But we'll be going into the dungeon first. Nothing better than seeing how to get to the next floor, isn't there? Bye bye." She waved her arm and reached to open the door. I quickly stumbled forward, closing the distance in seconds with [Rush], and then grabbed her wrist.

"Hey, what is it? Let go, man!" she said, not summoning her weapon but looking at me clearly irritated.

"Maybe we haven't stepped out on the right foot?" Aura commented, placing her hand on Lia's shoulder.

I didn’t know why I wanted to stop them from getting themselves killed. I knew that technically it would have been better for us if they did. But I wasn’t… I couldn’t think that way. Us players… other players were all we had. And these just haven’t had the luck to meet someone who would tell them it’s probably suicide.

"Maybe. Listen, you two, our guide said that going there alone will most likely mean that we'll end up dead. Let's talk about this for a second, all right? At least you're not trying to kill me outright like that goddamn Xila!"

"Xila! Another of the top ten! Well, somebody is well connected!" Lia smirked, some of her mirth returning before she nodded as I let go. "Let us talk then. Probably not here though."

"Why not here? We could talk here. We wanted to inspect the dungeon anyway."

"Didn't you just forbid us from entering?"

"Maybe, but I haven't explained it well, okay? Don't blame me."

"Okay, calm down now, kids. Let's sit in a circle and plan out. Also, give each other a handshake so we can communicate."

And so we did. They popped up on my selection screen, and already I could see that I could invite them to our guild. I didn't do so, however; who knew if these two were mentally okay or whether they'd be another Xila.

"Okay, so… our guides. Please, do you have any idea what happened to them? I quite liked my guide," Aura said, sadness emanating from her voice.

"Yeah, I do. They probably selected a class that made them material again. Once you are in a guild house, you'll be able to see them and talk to them again. You'll just need to buy one."

"Or join ours!" Crudia added.

"We will talk about that. Anyway, you will need a party if you want to go into the dungeon. I mean, we planned on going with just the two of them as well, but it'll be safer if you scout out what's in there with the four of you. No deep delves though. We’re just checking it out"

Wait, was Mark actually asking them to join our party without asking me? This little... well, I understood where he was coming from though; couldn't really blame the guy. The last floor was kind of crazy, especially towards the end, and I didn't feel like repeating that either.

"How is the class?" I asked Lia, doing my damn best to suppress the excitement in my voice. "Does it make you a lot stronger, or just a little, or how is it?"

I shouldn't have asked. The satisfied, cocky smirk she gave me in return was enough to tell me how happy she was, and that in turn made me want a class even more. Who cared that I would get it with just one more kill? I wanted it now, this very moment.

"Oh, it's amazing. I honestly don't know what to tell you. Before, I had a few skills that were useful, but this one added some more and already is expanding how I can fight. Also, it just fits me so well. I dunno. I think my guide was right; my decision about what to select was really on point."

Oh right, she didn't have her guide to order her what to select. Enviable.

"Okay, then that totally didn't make me envious. Well, then how do you two fight? Close range?"

"No," both of them said in unison.

"How the hell do you fight if you are both long ranged?" Mark asked, confused. "Don't you need someone to tank for you to distract your enemies?"

"There's never been a reason for that. We are both fast on our feet, and we kill everything that comes our way before it reaches us," Aura said, looking us over. The worst part was she didn't even sound cocky or anything; she just said it as a matter of fact.

I thought of my shields and whether I would want something like this, but no, there was something magical about smashing it into the body of my enemies. Doing that from a long range seemed boring, to be honest.

"Okay then, well, the two of us are close range. We also had two more guys in our party, but they are on another island. We got split as the second floor began."

"Oh, but you'll like them a lot! Bryga is really awesome and strong, and Dusk is so calm, and his guide is also really nice."

"Okay, okay, they get it." Mark hushed her with his hand and looked at the two newbies again. "I don't like doing this so suddenly, but I think you two could join our guild and share our space. You will need to pull your weight, of course, and pay for your own enhancements as well. It's not that we are doing charity work; we are doing cooperation. Does that sound good?" He offered his blue hand.

Lia looked at him, smirked, looked at Aura, and I saw their eyes glaze over as they texted with each other. Finally, they looked back and took the hand, passing through Mark's form, of course.

"Very well then, can you invite them, Shrimpie?"

I did so and added them to our party. They immediately changed color on my mini-map.

"Okay, so, dungeon exploration. This is a legendary dungeon, as you can see. We will need to go inside, check how strong the enemies here are, and preferably not engage with any of them.” He tapped his arm absentmindedly. “After that, we will try to get the three of you to kill one small enemy, whatever life form you can find, so you can level up to 25 and select a class."

"Okay, okay, Mister Planner, I'm tiring of this; let's just go!" I stood up and started walking towards the door.

"Wait, we didn't discuss a formation!" Mark began, but I ignored him. The drake outside kicked our ass, and the drake before killed poor Fink. I wasn't in the best mood already, and I wanted to punch something that would be weaker or stronger than me; whatever I punched, I'd be pretty happy, actually. As long as it didn’t fly.

The door swung open.

The rest of the players followed suit as I stepped inside, the air around me changing and suddenly blackness, covering my eyes.

The same void that I saw at the beginning of each floor, but this time it felt different. I looked around, tried to search on my mini-map, yet there was nobody. There was no system. This time I felt my body, though; I couldn't turn my head to look, but I felt it.

"Hello there, Shrimpie. Finally we can talk."

A voice suddenly resonated from all of the directions around me, from me as well, as if the voice came from the middle of my body. It felt strange. I felt a shiver running down my spine.

The worst part was, however, that I recognized that damned fucking voice. It was the one voicing the system!
