Archie, who was sporting a massive three-fingered punch indent across the right pauldron of his armor, drove his fist square into the sternum of the Cragspew Ravager with a bone-rattling crack. The blow sank deep, splitting along the fracture lines he’d already carved into its natural cragstone plating. Its chest armor, once unbroken stone, now bore eight distinct fist-shaped craters – the last strike embedding itself deeper than the rest.

The ravager let out a bellow that shook through its barrel chest, a sound halfway between a grinding avalanche and a beast’s roar in response to the sheer force behind Archie’s punch that ripped its towering frame off balance and sent it flying into the air. For a creature that weighed well over a ton and a half, it had no strength to halt its own momentum, crashing through trunks and breaking limbs with its sheer weight.

As the ravager struck the ground, its impact resonated through the forest floor, dirt spraying upwards as its impact carved a crater into the ground. The shockwave set leaves quivering on the canopy, and loose branches dropped like rain. Archie skidded to a stop halfway in his charge, boots scraping through the soil as he stood upright and watched.

The behemoth of a beast twitched for a few seconds before it began to struggle itself up. Its arms – three of them, each ending in four serrated, claw-like fingers – thrust themselves into the earth with a rending shriek like steel spikes being driven into stone. The earth itself moved as though responding to some unspoken command. Chunks of stone and clods of dirt climbed up its body, plate upon plate, filling in the wound Archie had made within a matter of seconds.

The once shattered carapace leveled out to look unshattered, plated over again in slabs of living earth.

Archie tilted his head, blinking. “Huh.” He let his hands fall loose at his sides, his tone carrying genuine surprise. “Guess you’ve got a self-repair feature too… neat. I wonder how that could work in tandem with my Self-Repair rune.”

The ravager flexed its claws, hissing as stone and dirt transmuted into excess cragstone and poured from the gaps in its joints. As it glowered at Archie, it let loose another growl that rumbled from its chest.

Archie smirked faintly as he activated Vital Sight to see if its self-repairing ability also had an effect on its vital energy. “I guess it doesn’t,” Archie mused to himself as he witnessed its vital energy was at half capacity in comparison to when he first caught sight of it.

“I guess that makes sense since it's not an elemental golem.” Archie rolled his right shoulder as he activated the Self-Repair tune around the dent in his pauldron and in quadrupole the time it took for the ravager to repair its armor, Archie’s pauldron was back in tip top shape.

“You’re patching your armor back up for me…” He grinned as his armor was in tip-top shape. “Which means I get to crack it all over again – without having to worry about damaging your core. Of which I believe it’ll provide good service to me as either a way for me to enhance my Self-Repair rune or something I can create a self-repairing punching bag with.”

Activating Savage Charge and coupling with the boost he was gaining for having Adrenaline Rush at ten percent, the forest around him blurred. The 300 meters between him and the Ravager vanished in an instant, and as a result, everything in the path between him and the Ravager tore up and flew into the air.

His fist drove upward into the underside of the Ravager’s skull with a crack that echoed within a kilometer-wide radius.

The force behind Archie’s punch snapped the ravager’s head back like it had been hit with a battering ram. Before its head even finished reeling back, Archie took notice that its throat was semi-ballooned, and not a second later, a volley of jagged cragstone erupted skyward, streaking towards the treeline above them before arcing downward.

Archie’s first instinct was to plant his feet and weather it, but after having dealt with the ravager’s horde before, he chose to back away. He disengaged in a hard backpedal, skidding across dirt and rock before launching onto a boulder fifty meters back.

The moment the stones finished their descent and touched ground, they detonated with incredible explosive force that left massive three-fingered indents wherever they struck. Thinner trees that were struck by them split down the middle, while larger ones were decorated in three finger gouge marks, and the varying-sized stones and boulders around them were met with similar fates.

“Figuring out how to isolate that ability and the ability to self-repair ability it has when forging is gonna be tricky to do considering how I’ve never had to deal with a creature having multiple abilities like that,” Archie muttered with narrowed eyes as the Ravager’s throat was beginning to swell once again.

With no time to waste, he raised his left arm and activated Thornscourge Expansion. Vines burst from his forearm before darting and snapping out until they wrapped around a boulder that was seven times his size and probably his weight as well. And with a sharp flex of his muscles and a surge of nature mana, he turned the vines as taut as steel cables.

With a grunt, he heaved it forward through the air just as the Ravager let loose another barrage.

The moment the first volley touched the boulder, it went to rubble, annihilated under the sheer concussive force of the detonations – just as Archie intended it to be.

As a plume of pulverized stone erupted outward in all directions, a choking cloud of dirt and dust swallowed Archie and a small part of the forest around him.

Hidden inside the smokescreen, he bent his knees and leaped vertically into the air, and only when he was a hundred meters into the air did he flip himself to face the ground and conjure a mana platform beneath both his feet as he reached the apex of his leap.

The Ravager’s head swung left and right, eyes locked onto the rubble cloud, waiting for Archie to either emerge or become apparent to him, its throat still bulging with the promise of another barrage.

Not once did it ever gain the idea that the one who killed its horde had the ability to vanish and leap into the air, which worked in Archie’s favor, as whenever the Cragspew Ravagers spewed out cragstone, their eyes instinctively get covered with a hardened eyelid that protects them from stray rock and dust for a brief period before and after spewing.

Archie smirked from above as he activated Savage Charge the second he felt gravity call his name and lunged at the head of the ravager.

The mana-fueled burst snapped him downward like a falling comet, his body cutting through the dust cloud and locking onto the beast’s crown. The Ravager stiffened, instincts flaring in the split-second before impact, its head whipping side to side in a desperate attempt to find the source of the killing intent pressing down on it. But it was blind, its lids crusted over with the natural armor it had sheathed itself in.

What greeted it instead was the full weight of Hyper Impact after having stored enough energy from killing other Cragspew Ravagers and punching the one in front of him, chained with the power generated by Adrenaline Rush, all of it funneled into Archie’s right gauntlet-clad fist.

The blow struck the back of its head with a thunderous crack, and not one moment later, Archie felt the ravager’s skull give out and cave inwards like brittle clay, and as such, his fist went straight through it fully out from the ravager’s right eye socket without any tangible resistance.

Blood, brain matter, and shards of bone jetted violently from the ravager’s skull in a grisly spray as the beast let out a strangled, half-born bellow. It tried to lurch forward, one massive claw scraping across the dirt to steady itself, but with its brain turned to paste, it could no longer send any signals to move its limbs.

As a result, Archie’s right gauntlet-clad arm was covered, and his upper face and headdress were covered in the ravager’s brain, blood, and bone.

Archie wrenched his arm free from the ruined cavity with a wet rip, the motion pulling the Ravager’s massive frame off balance. Momentum carried through its staggering form until the beast toppled, crashing onto its back with a seismic thud that shook the ground for dozens of meters.

“Ah, crap,” Archie muttered, grimacing as he glanced down at his right arm. The socket had popped on the pull, his shoulder sagging awkwardly as his arm flopped uselessly like dead weight. Blood and brain clung to his gauntlet in thick strings. He gave it a half-hearted shake, flinging off clumps of gore that painted the dirt, then, with a sharp twist of his torso, he jammed his shoulder back into place with a loud, ugly pop.

Only then did he finally glance at the glowing System prompts flooding his vision—notifications he’d ignored since first stumbling into the Ravager horde.

*[Cragspew Ravager Lv 103] has been slain – XP has been given.*…*[Cragspew Ravager Lv 108] has been slain – XP has been given.*

*Your Class has reached Lv 102 – Points allocated, +10 Free Points.*…*Your Class has reached Lv 104 – Points allocated, +10 Free Points.**Your Race has reached Lv 101 – Points allocated, +3 Free Points.**Your Race has reached Lv 102 – Points allocated, +3 Free Points.*

“About damn time,” Archie muttered, flexing his re-seated arm to test the joint before crouching down beside the Ravager’s corpse and deactivating Vital Sight as he saw the vital energy in its body disappear and remain solely inside its core.

With his left hand, he grabbed hold of its jagged carapace and, with a thought, sent the massive body vanishing into the depths of his Spatial Storage Cube and grouped with the other corpses and herbs he’d already tossed inside it.

He let out a loud exhale in an attempt to let his adrenaline begin to taper out as he activated Forgesmith’s Flame on himself and cleansed himself from the grime and blood that covered him, but he stopped short of doing so as he caught sight of something.

His gaze snapped to the treeline as he caught a flicker of movement going across the periphery of his left eye’s vision, causing his smirk to fade away.

Either someone or something incredibly fast just went past him, or… he was being spied on.

His eyes narrowed as, from atop a branch nearby, he saw a couple flakes of blood seemingly hovering in mid air and slowly beginning to arc downwards as if it were water on a screen.

[?]

Archie’s eyes narrowed as his eyes shifted to a darker red color, indicating that Vital Sight had just been reactivated and saw a humanoid vital energy outline, indicating that he was currently being watched by some humanoid, a female humanoid.

And if he was right on his assumption, he was being recorded on some sort of camera, judging by the way the person was holding up the device in their hands.

But all of that paled in detail to him in importance due to one thing: her sparks of Vitality, everyone’s was unique.

Loosening his tensed stance, Archie remained staring at the three hovering drops of blood and addressed the person spying on him. “Why didn’t you say you were also from Universe 72 when you saved me from that cave in?” He asked.

A brief moment of silence passed between the two of them before, high above in the tree’s a female figure came into view with blue skin and holding up a black camera with a large scope. “I knew I got too close,” the woman said with a click of her tongue.

“Ya really should try and not make your fights be so messy,” she complained as she brushed her hair backwards with one hand, unintentionally revealing to Archie that she hadn’t had two metal chips behind her ears anymore.
