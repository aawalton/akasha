“Little Rixia. Don’t waste your brother Hei Xin’s offer.” Lixi walked forth with a hand resting in his robe.

“That’s right. Hurry and go. We’re counting on you,” Shuren urged, barely convincing Aerestia to leave.

She held her staff with both hands, and her body trembled with fear.

Fear of her comrades dying.

But she had to send a message. That a Rank 3 was about to kill their sect disciples.

If she was fast enough, Shuren and Ming would surely survive.

“Don’t die, you two. Please.” With those words, Aerestia left.

“Hah, don’t worry so much, or those frowns will be on your head forever.” Ming walked next to Shuren, gazing at his aura. “How long can you sustain it for?”

“Three minutes.” Shuren stared at Lixi, but continued to talk. “Ming. Keep your sword arm free at the last second. You’ll know what I mean.”

“Am I finally going to see it?”

“If you mess up, then sure.” Shuren likewise started moving.

“Colour me intrigued.” Lixi took his time, and the snake went back in his robes. He didn’t want to use that much strength.

Otherwise, it would end all too quickly.

“Then the timer is three minutes. I have high expectations for your trump card, Hei Xin.” Lixi held out his left arm, while his right still rested in his robes. “I’ll only use my left arm. Come, I’ll allow you the first five moves.”

Shuren got a sense of deja vu. Is this what he sounded like to others?

He’s just baiting. But let’s test out how sturdy his Talisbody is.

“Then we’ll take you up on that offer!” Shuren ran forth, and Ming followed parallel to him.

They would go for a pincer attack.

Shuren and Ming had gone on so many battle missives together, they were basically in tune with each other when it came to combat.

Shuren even believed that if the day came they were both capable of mind transmission, they still wouldn’t need it.

Shuren used Staff Purge, stabbing at Lixi’s forearm.

It landed on his skin.

Without even a single scratch.

Shuren backed off, controlling his stance. That was one of my strongest moves. And it did nothing whatsoever. But, I believe he’s using Qi to defend himself alongside his Talisbody. We have to force up a single opening!

Ming sliced towards Lixi’s neck, but a small snake appeared from his robe to deflect it with its head. His hands reverberated as if he smashed into heavy iron.

“What’s with that look? I did say I would only use a single hand.” Lixi grinned mischievously at Ming, enjoying the reaction of his opponents.

Shuren stepped back, focusing Qi in his Ruyi Staff. It had grown with him throughout the past year.

As a Spiritude Grade weapon, the more Shuren spent time using his staff, the stronger and more durable his staff would get. He had a feeling if it was just any other staff, it would’ve shattered on impact if he used Staff Purge.

Let alone the flexibility of the weapon itself, not just externally but as a conduit facilitating Shuren’s flow of Qi. It was as close as it could get to being an extension of his arm.

But in this battle, all of that didn’t matter.

The difference between him and Lixi was too much.

Shuren and Ming attacked once more, still not even making Lixi even budge.

This time, Lixi used his index finger to stop Shuren’s Ruyi Staff.

And he let the sword cut against his neck, only for it to bounce off.

“One more move left,” Lixi announced, anticipating more from his opponents. From his dear close friends.

Both Shuren and Ming backed away by a dozen metres, giving themself a moment to convene.

“I’ll take the lead. You’ll know what to do,” Shuren said, readying his weapon once more.

Ming acknowledged, focusing all his Qi in his blade. In his thoughts, there was nothing but the blade. Reaching zen, all he had to do was cut.

Lixi fidgeted with the snake on his shoulder. The swordsman… The air around him is different. He’s using quite the mental technique. It’s extremely high quality. Just what is his background?

Mental techniques were one of the best techniques to learn as a cultivator.

Why? Because mindset was the strongest asset on the path of cultivation.

Boosting and changing your mindset would make it easier for you to comprehend the mystical arts of cultivation. To understand Qi, and walk on the Grand Paths.

So all of the most powerful factions would often perfect their own beginner Mental Technique to teach to all their disciples.

Strength was nothing without a powerful mind.

And even Lixi could sense the difference in Ming when he employed the Liu Clan’s Mortal Severing technique.

Shuren stopped himself right in front of Lixi, but instead of attacking, he stabbed his weapon deep in the ground.

Lixi lowered his eyes. “Heh. Hei Xin, you think of some interesting stuff. Until you land the next move, I cannot attack.”

Shuren smirked back, feeding into Lixi’s boredom. The defiance of a Rank 1 against a Rank 3, flailing for his life while doing everything he can in his mind to find a way to survive. He had to play around like this.

Right before he bent the Ruyi Staff down to destroy the earth, Ming sliced a circle around Lixi.

Debris rose from the ground, flying everywhere. Lixi himself flew high, standing on a perfectly cut platform courtesy of Ming. Shuren didn’t stop, stabbing the earth again to cause a rain of stones and rocks to cover the sky, while dust clouds stifled the air.

They were trying to cause as much of a disturbance as possible to get that one single opening.

Even if Lixi was a Rank 3, he would have to analyse and take note of every rock in the vicinity that Ming and Shuren used as a cover to attack from. As long as the action caused Lixi to use more thought processing power to defend, it would give them that slight advantage.

Lixi had to admit. The teamwork of this pair was astonishing. And he thought back to the only person worthy of being his partner.

I wonder. If me and Reizou were this in tune in battle, just how unstoppable would we be? I doubt even the strongest Rising Paragons from The Great Nine would be able to compare to us. Hahaha. Did I actually consider Reizou as a comrade?!

Lixi’s hair and clothes danced wildly in the sky, and soon he felt the sense of vertigo as gravity brought him back down.

This time, to a canyon filled with a pandemonium of dirt.

Shuren and Ming jumped and danced around the falling rocks in the sky, finding an opening to deal with Lixi.

Shuren continued to charge up Qi in the Ruyi Staff, to prepare for a long ranged attack. He would strike the moment Ming moved.

Shuren saw the glint of steel blitz past the rocks towards Lixi.

And he raised his arm with his staff in a javelin stance.

And threw it with all his might.

But not towards Lixi.

His Ruyi Staff flew towards the top of the canyons, destroying a portion at the top.

Bringing down an avalanche of boulders.

Ming raised his blade, only to fly past Lixi and not attack.

Now Lixi had to deal with the flying rocks, falling boulders, and the combined attacks of Ming and Shuren.

His expectations only grew. They don’t have mind transmission at their level. This teamwork… It increases their battle strength to an absurd amount. If they were on my level… Aether Ul-Regen truly is a monster, making disciples like these. She was right to fear that madman.

Shuren recalled his weapon, once more dancing around to aim for Lixi.

This time, Ming aimed for the neck.

If he could just nick even the jugular vein, it would be more than enough.

And Shuren threw his staff, ignoring the searing pain in his arm. It felt like his arm had exploded like a cannon, hot silver air steaming from his skin.

With the approaching bursts of attack from Shuren and Ming, Lixi only smiled.

First, Ming neared his neck. Lixi only needed to increase his defences around that region.

But, mid swing, Ming sliced towards Lixi’s arm instead. And still kept the inertia without losing force.

Lixi almost considered it a feint before realising it.

He grabbed the blade with his entire hand, rather than a finger this time, stopping Ming on the spot.

In that same split second, the whizzing of the staff aimed at Lixi roared, reaching to pierce through his torso.

Lixi stared at Shuren, giving him a devious smile. As if telling him to watch.

Shuren held his left hand to stabilise his right arm, barely controlling the string of Qi that reached to his arm. Commanding in his mind to detonate the Qi in the staff, it doubled its speed.

Grabbing Ming by the neck like a cut, Lixi used him as a human shield. Taunting at Shuren in the meantime, gazing him straight in the eye.

To show him how a demonic cultivator fights.

Shuren didn’t even have time to react, let alone yell out.

The staff pierced straight through Ming’s stomach, and Lixi disappeared from his sight.

Where did he go?! How?!

The shadow beneath Shuren’s foot trembled.

And he felt an ominous presence press down on his shoulder.

“You were quite close. If you had shortened the interval between his and your attack, you might have made it.”

Teleportation?!

Shuren used every vestige of power in his body to turn around, in an attempt to fight back.

“It’s about time I also play around, don’t you think?”

He was smacked away like a rag doll, falling to the ground.

Just one casual wave of Lixi’s palm was enough to throw Shuren that far.

Before Shuren could even land, Lixi had already appeared beneath his landing point, kicking him around like he was playing a game, juggling a ball.

Cracks reverberated inside his body. Blood boiled and rolled around his body as his internal organs shook.

He saw Ming stand up already, with his blade ready. He didn’t let the wound in his stomach faze him.

Controlling the Qi around his body, Shuren forced his body to a standstill, landing on his feet.

But when he tried to recall his weapon, it didn’t budge.

It was spinning around in Lixi’s hand!

“Quite the toy. It’s not a Zeal Artifact, yet it has a similar resonance. Who crafted this? I might just visit to make one of my own.”

Shuren would never tell him the answer. Saying it was his father was as good as killing him.

And he remembered times of the past.

If his father was at his peak, he could kill this demonic cultivator with a single slash of his sword.

If I don’t stake it all, then it’s all going to be a waste!

Lixi teleported once again, this time appearing before Ming. In that same moment, he slammed the Ruyi Staff into Ming’s body, making him fly all the way across the canyon.

There was no point in Shuren yelling out in fear of Ming’s injuries. They had to continue fighting, without regard to their life.

For that one moment, Shuren and Ming exchanged glances as he flew with a battered body.

They didn’t talk. They didn’t even nod their heads or make a signal.

But Ming saw the cold shine of silver in Shuren’s eyes.

He knew what to do next.

The rocks and boulders had fallen, the dust clouds still stifling the surroundings. The beautiful cold morning had long been ruined by the dirt and blood in the canyon.

Shuren raised both his fists in a battle stance, glaring at Lixi.

In the shadow of one of the boulders, Lixi appeared, now closer to Shuren.

“It’s a shame I don’t use the staff. Hei Xin. I would like to make a proposition for you.”

Shuren almost faltered from confusion, but he retained his guard up. For all he knew, it may have been a ruse.

“Come with me. It’d be quite interesting if you joined our sect.”

Shuren laughed.

“I didn’t know your vocation was a jester.” Shuren ran, taking the initiative to do his next and final move.

He had to put his all into it.

Everything.

Lixi threw the Ruyi Staff to the side, with both his hands out to deal with Shuren. “Haha, I wouldn’t dare joke on this matter.”

Amongst the dust clouds, cold metal sliced through at a frightening speed. Towards Shuren.

Right before he reached a step before Lixi, Shuren waved his left hand out.

And Ming’s sword flew right into Shuren’s hand.

His Qi pulsed, burning his body. His soul churned, yearning for the blade. His mind roared, in defiance of his fate!

One swing! He had to put everything into it!

Even at the cost of his life!

For the first time, Lixi narrowed his eyes.

Something’s changed. His Aura… It reminds me of Reizou!
